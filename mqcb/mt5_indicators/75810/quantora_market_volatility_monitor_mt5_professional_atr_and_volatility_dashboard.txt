Quantora Market Volatility Monitor MT5 is a real-time volatility analysis indicator for MetaTrader 5.
It measures current market activity using ATR and compares it with a historical average to show whether volatility is low, normal, high, or extreme. This helps traders quickly understand current conditions before making decisions.
The dashboard also displays daily high, low, and range, along with the ratio between daily movement and ATR. Live market data such as bid, ask, and spread are included for a complete view of current conditions.
All thresholds and ATR settings can be adjusted, allowing the indicator to adapt to different trading styles and markets.
The interface is simple, updates automatically, and works on all symbols and timeframes.
This tool is designed for market monitoring and analysis only. It does not generate trading signals or manage trades.
