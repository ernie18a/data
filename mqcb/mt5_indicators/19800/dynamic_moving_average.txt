VolMA draws a moving average, the period of which is calculated by RSI, which is based on tick volume data.
The indicator has four input parameters:
RSI Vol Period
- the period of RSI calculated on volume data;
MA Period
- the period of the dynamic Moving Average;
MA Method
- Moving Average calculation method;
Applied price
- Moving Average calculation price.
