WPR HL - Williams’ Percent Range indicator calculated by (High-Low)
The indicator has three input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Overbought
- overbought level
Oversold
- oversold level
Calculation:
WPR_HL =-100.0 * (Max-HL) / (Max-Min)
where:
HL = High - Low
Max, Min are the highest and lowest prices within the Period interval
