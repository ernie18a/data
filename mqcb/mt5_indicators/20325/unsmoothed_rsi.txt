The indicator shows a line of the unsmoothed RSI in a separate chart window.
It has four input parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
Applied price
- the calculation price;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level.
Interpretation of the indicator is similar to the standard RSI.
