This indicator shows the "ultimate support/resistance" levels of current trend. Trend changes is shown with color rectangles, trend direction is shown with colored dots. The direction of trend is determined by dynamics of
moving average
, the support/resistance levels depend on current volatility and calculated using the
Average True Range
(ATR) indicator.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
CodeBase at mql4.com
26.04.2008.
