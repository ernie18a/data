The indicator shows a cloud within a channel formed by two МАs, EMA and LWMA
It has five input parameters:
First LWMA period
- calculation period of the first LWMA
Second LWMA period
- calculation period of the second LWMA
First EMA period
- calculation period of the first EMA
Second EMA period
- calculation period of the second EMA
Applied price
- price used for calculations
