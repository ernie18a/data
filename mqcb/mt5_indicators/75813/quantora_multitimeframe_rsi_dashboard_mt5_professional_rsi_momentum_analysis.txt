Quantora Multi-Timeframe RSI Dashboard MT5 is a multi-timeframe momentum analysis indicator for MetaTrader 5.
It analyzes RSI across seven timeframes — from M1 to D1 — and classifies each one as overbought, bullish, neutral, bearish, or oversold. This makes it easy to see how momentum differs across short, medium, and higher timeframes without switching charts.
All readings are combined into an average RSI and a clear overall momentum bias, giving a quick summary of market conditions.
The dashboard also shows how many timeframes fall into each category, helping to understand whether momentum is aligned or mixed.
RSI settings and levels can be adjusted to fit different strategies. The interface is clean, updates automatically, and works on all symbols and timeframes.
This tool is designed for analysis only. It does not generate trading signals or manage trades.
