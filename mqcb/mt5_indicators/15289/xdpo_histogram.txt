The simplest oscillator based on the difference between the price and its Moving Average. The oscillator is smoothed and displayed as a color histogram.
The indicator is calculated by the following formula:
XDPO(i) = MA(Close(i) - MA(i, N),M)
where N — MA period, M — indicator smoothing period
The indicator uses the classes of SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
on 01.09.2008.
