Spread Monitor — a simple information indicator for MetaTrader 5.
Features:
Displays the current spread in pips
Displays Bid and Ask
Colour coding: green / yellow / red
Supports Russian and English
Customisable thresholds and colours
Does not overload the chart — just a single text panel
Settings:
Language — language selection (Russian / English)
X_Offset, Y_Offset — panel position
MidThreshold, HighThreshold — spread thresholds in points
ColorLow, ColorMid, ColorHigh — display colours
FontSize — font size
Why it is needed:
Traders use it to monitor the spread before entering a trade. It is particularly useful during news events and periods of low liquidity.
