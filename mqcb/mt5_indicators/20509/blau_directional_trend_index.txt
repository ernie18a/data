The William Blau Directional Trend Index indicator shows the William Blau directional trend indexes.
It has six input parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
First smoothing period
- the initial smoothin period;
Second smoothing period
- the secondary smoothing period;
Third smoothing period
- the resulting smoothing period;
Upper level
- the overbought level;
Lower level
- the oversold level.
