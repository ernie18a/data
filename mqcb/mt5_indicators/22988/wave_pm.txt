The indicator has twelve input parameters:

- fast line calculation period
Fast bands shift
- fast line shift
Fast bands deviation
- fast line deviation
Slow bands period
- slow line calculation period
Slow bands shift
- slow line shift
Slow bands deviation
- slow line deviation
Chars
- fast and slow oscillators calculation period
Extreme top level
- extreme upper level value
Top level
- upper level value
Central level
- central level value
Bottom level
- lower level value
Extreme bottom level
