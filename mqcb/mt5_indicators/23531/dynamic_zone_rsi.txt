Oscillating indicator Dynamic Zone RSI is
Relative Strength Index
with dynamic overbought/oversold zones based on
Bollinger Bands
.
It has six adjustable parameters:
RSI period
- RSI calculation period
Bands period
- Bollinger Bands calculation period
Deviation
- Bollinger Bands deviation
Show intersection arrows
- show the labels of RSI crossing the lines of zones
Allow midline intersections
- allow controlling the intersections with the midline
Use alerts
- use alers as soon as RSI intersects the lines of zones
