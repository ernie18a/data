The indicator displays the ratio of two ATRs with different periods.
It is calculated by the following formula:
ATRR = (ATR1/ATR2)*100
It has two parameters:
ATR1 Period
- ATR1 calculation period;
ATR2 Period
- ATR2 calculation period.
