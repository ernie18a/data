Aroon Horn oscillator is based on
Aroon
indicator. The indicator displays a trend direction filtering minor fluctuations.
It features two adjustable parameters
Period
- Aroon calculation period
Filter
- filter size
Calculation:
AH = 10.0 * (Up-Dn) / Period
where:
Up = 100.0 * (Period-MaxBar+index) / Period
Dn = 100.0 * (Period-MinBar+index) / Period
MaxBar, MinBar - High and Low prices index in the Period range
index - current bar index
A bullish trend is displayed in
green
A bearish trend is displayed in
red
Sorted out values are displayed in
gray
