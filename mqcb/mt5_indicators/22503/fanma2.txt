Fan Moving Averages with Average line is a moving average fan in the form of an oscillator in a separate window with the average line.
Oscillator values oscillate with the +1/-1 range
It has eight adjustable parameters:
First MA period
- first MA calculation period
MA method
- calculation method
Applied price
MA count
- number of MAs in the fan
Incremental method
- method of incrementing MA period values:
Addition
Multiplication
Coefficient of increment
- increment ratio
Smoothing period
- middle line smoothing period
Smoothing method
- middle line calculation method
