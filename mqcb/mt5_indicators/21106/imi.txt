Oscillator Intraday Momentum Index displays the ratio between the directed change in the price and the total change in the prices over the specified period.
There are three adjustable parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level.
Calculation:
IMI = 100*U /(U+D)
where:
U = SMA(Up, Period),
D = SMA(Down, Period)
If Close > Open:
Up = Close - Open
otherwise:
Up = 0
If Close < Open:
Down = Open - Close
otherwise:
Down = 0
