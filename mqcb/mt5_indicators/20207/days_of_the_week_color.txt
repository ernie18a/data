A modification of
Days of the week
- now every day of the week is marked with a separate color:
The
DRAW_COLOR_HISTOGRAM
style requires
two buffers
(a buffer of values
HistogramBuffer
and a color buffer
HistogramColors
):

Management of histogram colors is implemented by assigning an individual
color index
to each index of the
HistogramColors
buffer:
switch
