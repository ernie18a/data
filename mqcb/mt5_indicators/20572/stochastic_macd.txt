The Stochastic MACD oscillator shows in a separate window the stochastic MACD.
It has six input parameters:
MACD Fast MA
- fast MA to calculate MACD;
MACD Slow MA
- slow MA to calculate MACD;
MACD Signal period
- the signal period of the MACD line;
Stoch period
- the period of calculating the stochastic;
Method
- calculation method;
Applied price
- price used for calculations.
