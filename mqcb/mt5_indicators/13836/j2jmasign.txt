Semaphore signal indicator triggering when the
J2JMA
moving average changes direction.
The indicator uses the CJJMA class from SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library (copy it to
<terminal_data_folder>
\MQL5\Include). The use of the class was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
