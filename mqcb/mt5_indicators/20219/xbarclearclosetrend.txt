A trend state indicator. It is a signal modification of the
trend indicator
of the same name.
It has only one input parameter:
Period
- indicator calculation period.
The indicator displays color marks on a chart indicating the trend state. Six types of marks are possible:
Strongly up
- a strong uptrend;
Up
- an uptrend;
Uncertainly up
- an uncertain uptrend (rollback/consolidation after an uptrend);
Strongly down
- a strong downtrend;
Down
- a downtrend;
Uncertainly down
- an uncertain downtrend (rollback/consolidation after an uptrend).
