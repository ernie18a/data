The
SVS_Trend
indicator with the possibility to change the indicator timeframe in its input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
The indicator requires the compiled
SVS_Trend.ex5
indicator file. Add it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
Fig.1. Indicator Dtm_HTF.Support_and_Resistance_HTF.
