The simplest moving-like indicator. The formula for the calculation:
PLdot = SMA(Period=3, (High[bar] + Low[bar] + Close[bar]) / 3)
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
on 31.05.2008.
