The custom NHNL_Divergence indicator (based on the
NHNL
indicator) draws the HL index line in a separate window and displays found divergences in the indicator window and on the price chart.
The NHNL indicator is
not required
for the operation of NHNL_Divergence.
The indicator has three input parameters:
Period
- the period for calculating High/Low indices of the NHNL indicator;
Bullish color
- the color of bullish signal arrows and lines;
Bearish color
- the color of bearish signal arrows and lines;
