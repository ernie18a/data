The indicator displays the crossing points of RSI (
Relative Strength Index
) indicators belonging to various periods. Eight RSI indicators are used in total (two indicators per one crossing). The indicator signals must be compared with readings of some other trend indicator.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base
at mql4.com 17.07.2009.
