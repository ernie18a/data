A simple indicator that calculates rounded volatility of a financial asset. Volatility is calculated in points based on the maximum and minimum prices.
The volatility is calculated using a simple MA as the sum of the maximum prices for the averaging period minus the sum of minimal prices for the same period. The resulting value is converted into points and rounded according to the coordinate grid step defined by the
StartLevel
and
LevelsStep
input values.
