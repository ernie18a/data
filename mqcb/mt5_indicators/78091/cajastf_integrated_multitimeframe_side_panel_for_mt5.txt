CajasTF is a lightweight MT5 indicator that displays a real-time mini-chart in a right-side panel, allowing you to monitor any timeframe (M1 to MN1) on a single screen. It automatically matches your chart’s color palette, features an independent price scale, candle countdown timer, live OHLC metrics, and auto-shift margin so your active trading candles are never hidden.
CajasTF
is a lightweight, visual indicator for MetaTrader 5 that renders a
secondary mini-chart directly on the right side of your main chart
, allowing you to monitor real-time price action across
any higher or lower timeframe
without switching tabs or opening additional windows.
Engineered with
native visual integration
, the panel automatically inherits your chart’s existing color palette (background, bullish/bearish candles, wicks, dojis, and Bid line), delivering a clean, modern, and uncluttered trading workspace.
Key Features
Instant Multi-Timeframe (MTF) View:
Select any timeframe (from M1 up to MN1) and view the last
N
candles of that period directly on your working chart.
The active candle updates tick-by-tick in real-time as it forms.
Smart Palette & Automatic Color Matching:
Detects and adapts instantly to your chart template: background, grid, labels, candle bodies, and wick outlines. If you switch themes (light/dark mode), the panel updates seamlessly.
Independent Price Scale:
Features its own dedicated vertical scale with a 5-level grid, ensuring the mini-chart proportions remain optimal regardless of the zoom level on your main chart.
Intelligent Chart Auto-Shift:
Automatically adjusts the right margin (
chart shift
) so the panel never covers active price action. When the indicator is removed, your original shift settings are automatically restored.
Real-Time Market Data & Metrics:
Bar Countdown Timer:
Accurate timer showing the time remaining until the active candle closes.
Current Price Line:
Live visual Bid price marker projected onto the panel's scale.
OHLC Data Footer:
Live Open, High, Low, Close metrics and current candle percentage change (% gain/loss).
