Arixis Market Regime Map is a market-state classification indicator for MetaTrader 5 designed to answer a simple but important question:
What type of market am I trading right now?
Instead of generating Buy or Sell signals, the indicator analyzes price behavior and classifies the current market into four primary regimes:
TREND UP
TREND DOWN
RANGE
TRANSITION
The classification is based on a combination of quantitative measurements including price efficiency, normalized directional slope, volatility, market structure and agreement across multiple analysis horizons.
One of the core measurements is the
Efficiency Ratio
, which compares the net displacement of price with the total distance travelled during the same period. This helps distinguish directional movement from noisy or ranging price action.
Arixis Market Regime Map combines this information with directional and structural measurements to produce a transparent assessment of the current market regime.

Regime Strength
The indicator also calculates a
Regime Strength
value representing how strongly the available quantitative evidence supports the detected market state.
Regime Strength is
not a probability of future price movement
and should not be interpreted as a prediction that price will rise or fall.
Designed for analysis, not prediction
Arixis Market Regime Map does not attempt to predict the next candle or guarantee future market direction.
Its purpose is to provide a structured and quantitative description of the
current market environment
.
This can be useful for:
discretionary traders
algorithmic traders
Expert Advisor developers
strategy research
regime filtering
identifying transitions between trending and ranging conditions
understanding when the behavior of a strategy changes across different market environments
For example, a trend-following system may behave differently during a RANGE regime, while a mean-reversion strategy may react differently during strong directional expansion.
Transparent quantitative approach
The indicator is built around interpretable mathematical measurements rather than opaque Buy/Sell signals.
The objective is not to tell the trader what trade to take, but to provide a clearer representation of the market structure in which a trading decision or algorithm is operating.
