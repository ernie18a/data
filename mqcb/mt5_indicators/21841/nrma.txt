NRMA -
an indicator by
Konstantin Kopyrkin
. It is one of the many implementations of the NRTR indicator (Nick Rypock Trailing Reverse
).
The indicator displays NRMA and trailing stop positioning marks - NRTR.
There are four input parameters:
K
- coefficient of NRTR points shift from the price and direction change
Smooth
- NRMA smoothing factor
Fast
- minimal smoothing period
Sharp
- degree of dynamism
The indicator is used to determine entry points and trailing stop positions.
