Quantora Market Status Dashboard MT5 is an indicator that provides a clear and structured overview of current market conditions directly on the chart. It does not open or manage trades and is designed purely for analysis.
The tool combines multiple key metrics such as spread, volatility, volume activity, and daily range into a single interface. It helps you quickly understand how active the market is by showing real-time data along with simple classifications. You can see whether conditions are normal, quiet, or highly volatile without checking multiple indicators separately.
It also tracks the current daily range and compares it with the average daily range, giving a quick view of how much movement has already taken place. This makes it easier to judge whether the market still has room to move or is already extended.
In addition, the dashboard monitors major trading sessions including Sydney, Tokyo, London, and New York. It shows which sessions are currently active, detects overlaps, and provides a general view of global market activity throughout the day.
All information is displayed in a clean and easy-to-read dashboard, allowing you to follow market conditions in real time without leaving the chart.
Suitable for traders who want a more complete and practical overview of market activity in a single place.
