Bezier-weighted Moving average.
The indicator has three input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Sensitivity
Applied price
Calculation:
BMA = SPC
where:
SPC - Sum(Price*Coeff) in the Period range
Coeff = (Period! / (i! * (Period-i)!)) * Sensitivity^i * (1-Sensitivity)^(Period-i)
Price - Applied price
N! - factorial
