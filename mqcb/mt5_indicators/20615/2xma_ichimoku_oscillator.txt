An oscillator based on the difference of two smoothed, different-period Tenkan-Sen lines in form of a colored histogram.
For the indicator to work, there must be indicator
XMA_Ichimoku.ex5
in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
The indicator uses the SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The classes were described in detail in the
Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers
article.
