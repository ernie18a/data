The
XCCX
indicator implemented as a sequence of candlesticks. Candlesticks appear as a result of relevant price timeseries processed by the XCCX algorithm.
In many situations, such approach may be more informative for the purpose of analysis.
This indicator requires the compiled indicator file XCCX.ex5. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
