Indicator NMA (Navel Moving Average) - a moving average calculated by the formula:
NMA = MA(P, Period, Method)
where:
P = (5*Close + 2*Open + High + Low)/9.0
It has two parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
Method
- calculation method.
