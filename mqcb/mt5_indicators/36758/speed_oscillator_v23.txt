Information:
Trade Value
depends on
Price Positioning,
the shape of which can be described also by MA Speed and Price-to-MA Distance.  This indicator provides:
Speed Data as a Histogram, scaled with the historical average.
Distance Data as a Line, in percentage points.
Use:
The two properties are provided together in a single indicator more so for ease of use, than their immediate compatibility. On the surface, the indicator makes it easy to distinguish price
pressure
on trending Moving average.
