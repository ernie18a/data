MA Trend trend oscillator displays a trend based on the analysis of a specified set of moving averages with calculated periods within the specified range.
There are five inputs:
Period begin
- MA initial calculation period
Period step
- МА calculation period increment step
Count
- number of calculated МАs
Method
- МА calculation method
Applied price
- МА calculation price
Calculation:
MATrend = Sum(S) / Count
where:
If Applied price > MA
S = 1
If Applied price < MA
S = -1
MA - MovingAverage(Applied price,Period begin+Period step,Method)
