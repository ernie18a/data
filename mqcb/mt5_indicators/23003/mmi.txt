Market Meanness Index indicator is a business activity index. It displays the market state - trend or random (chaotic).
The indicator has three input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Random boundings
- level, above which the market has no any obvious trend and is quite chaotic
Trend
- level, below which the market is subject to trends
