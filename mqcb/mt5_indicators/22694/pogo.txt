Oscillating indiator POGO displays the mean bar size over a given period.
It has two adjustable parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Method
- calculation method
Calculations:
POGO = AvgRAW
where:
RAW = Open - Close
AvgRAW = MA(RAW, Period, Method)
