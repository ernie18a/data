Theory:
The indicator that is using self adjusting filter (published and described here :
Filtered Price
) but applied to one of the 4 types of averages :
simple moving average (SMA)
exponential moving average (EMA)
smoothed moving average (SMMA)
linear weighted moving average (LWMA)
Filter size is the "size" of self adjusted deviations and can be fractional.
Usage:
You can use the color change as a signal.
