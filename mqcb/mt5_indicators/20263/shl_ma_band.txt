The indicator is a variation of Bollinger Bands and Envelopes, based on two smoothed moving averages calculated by the Linear Regression method (LSMA
Linear regression MA
).
This is a version of the
HL_MA_Band
indicator with an additional smoothing.
It has two parameters:
Period
- LSMA calculation period;
Method
- LSMA smoothing method.
