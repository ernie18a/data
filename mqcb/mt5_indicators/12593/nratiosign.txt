A semaphore signal indicator that uses an algorithm based on crossovers of oversold and overbought levels from the
NRatio
indicator.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to
<terminal_data_folder>
\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
