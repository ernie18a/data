Arixis Backtest Robustness Analyzer
Arixis Backtest Robustness Analyzer is a free and open-source analytical utility for MetaTrader 5 designed to examine historical trading performance beyond headline statistics such as Profit Factor, net profit and win rate.
The analyzer works with closed trading history and provides additional information about how historical results were produced.
Main features
Net result, gross profit and gross loss
Profit Factor and win rate
Average and median trade
Best/worst trade analysis
Top 1, 3, 5 and 10 trade dependency
Historical sensitivity after removing extreme trades
Winning and losing streak analysis
Reconstructed closed-equity drawdown
Underwater periods and recovery information
Performance by year, month, weekday and hour
Separate BUY and SELL statistics
Trade-result percentiles and standard deviation
Sample-size information
Optional CSV export
Profit Dependency
The Dependency section recalculates the historical result after removing the strongest observations. This can help reveal whether historical performance was broadly distributed or concentrated in a small number of trades.
If total net profit is zero or negative, dependency percentages are displayed as N/A because expressing the strongest trades as a percentage of net profit could be misleading. The analyzer still displays the resulting net value after those trades are removed.
Historical Sensitivity
The analyzer also performs the inverse experiment by removing the worst historical trades and periods.
This does not modify the original trading history and should not be interpreted as an improved backtest. It is simply a sensitivity analysis showing how extreme observations influenced the historical result.
Drawdown
The utility reconstructs a closed-equity curve from completed historical trades and analyzes drawdown depth, underwater periods and recovery.
Closed-equity drawdown is not the same as intratrade floating-equity drawdown. Floating losses that occurred while positions were still open cannot necessarily be reconstructed from completed trading history alone.
Time Analysis
Historical performance can be examined by year, month, weekday and hour. The number of observations is displayed together with the statistics so that small samples remain visible.
BUY / SELL Analysis
BUY and SELL operations are displayed separately without automatically ranking either direction.
Distribution
The analyzer displays the mean, median, standard deviation and several percentiles of historical trade results.
Filters
The utility supports date-range, symbol and Magic Number filtering.
Read-only operation
Arixis Backtest Robustness Analyzer is completely read-only. It does not open, close or modify trades and does not interfere with other Expert Advisors.
Important
All measurements describe historical observations only. They do not predict future profitability and should not be interpreted as future risk limits.
The complete MQL5 source code is provided for educational and research purposes.
