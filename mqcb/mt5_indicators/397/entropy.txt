The entropy is the measure of the disorder of the system. The
entropy
is calculated using the
Maximum Entropy Method
.
If we try to evaluate it according to its indicative attributes without paying much attention to its code, we will find out that it is an ordinary unnormalized
oscillator
and it is possible to apply to it all appropriate technical analysis methods. The indicator was presented in two variants - as a line (Entropy.mq5) and as a multicolored histogram (ColorEntropy.mq5).
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
5.07.2008.
