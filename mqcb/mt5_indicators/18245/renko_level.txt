The indicator displays the Renko bars on the MetaTrader 5 chart.
The calculation of the nearest border is as follows:
For the current price three price levels are calculated:
price_ceil
price_round
price_floor
In this case, the distance between "price_ceil" and "price_round" and the distance between "price_round" and "price_floor" is equal to the size of the "brick". The size of the brick is set in the input parameter "
InpSize
".
