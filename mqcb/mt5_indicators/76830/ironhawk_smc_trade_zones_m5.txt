IronHawk SMC Trade Zones M5 is a chart indicator designed to identify structured trading opportunities on closed M5 candles.
The indicator combines confirmed swing points, liquidity sweeps, BOS and CHoCH events, displacement, Fair Value Gaps, Order Blocks, RSI, MACD and ATR-based risk measurements.
It does not open, modify or close trades. Entry, Stop Loss, TP1 and TP2 are displayed as proposed levels for analysis and manual decision-making.
Main features:
• Closed-bar and non-repainting calculations
• Bullish and bearish market-structure detection
• Liquidity sweep, trend-continuation and momentum-reversal paths
• Automatic selection between STOP and LIMIT entry proposals
• ATR-based Stop Loss and target calculation
• TP1 and TP2 visualization
• Market regime and structural bias display
• Setup states: ARMED, ACTIVE, TP, SL, EXPIRED, INVALIDATED and AMBIGUOUS
• Five recent completed setups displayed with outcome colors
• Clickable setup history that navigates directly to the original signal candle
• Optional terminal, push and email alerts
• Optional CSV audit journal
Retail-frequency mode:
Organic SMC setups always have priority. If fewer than two opportunities have appeared during a complete broker day, the indicator can add scheduled retail signals after 09:00 and 14:00 server time.
These additional signals use a closed-bar majority vote from EMA 20/50, MACD and RSI. They are clearly identified as retail fallback setups and use configurable ATR-based risk levels.
The daily count represents displayed opportunities. It does not guarantee that an entry will be triggered or that a setup will be profitable.
Important settings:
• HistoryBars controls the amount of M5 history scanned
• MinimumDailySignals controls the daily retail-frequency objective
• RetailFirstSignalHour and RetailSecondSignalHour use broker server time
• RetailStopATR controls fallback Stop Loss distance
• RetailTargetR controls the proposed TP2 risk multiple
• MaximumDisplayedSetups controls the number of zones kept on the chart
• RecentSetupCount controls the number of completed setups listed
• Alert and CSV options are disabled or configurable from the indicator inputs
The indicator must be attached to an M5 chart. Test it on a demo account before using its indications in real trading.
Community feedback is welcome, especially regarding the possible addition of native MQL5 Economic Calendar warnings and news filters in a future version.
