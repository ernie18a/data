An indicator of trend power with eight states. A growing trend is light blue and blue, the falling is red and yellow. Dark color indicates a strong trend, light color - a weak one.
For the correct operation of the StepChoppy_v2 indicator, place the compiled files of
StepRSI
and
StepMA_Line
in folder terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Indicators.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
on 22.02.2008.
