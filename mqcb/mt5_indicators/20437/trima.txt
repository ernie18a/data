Triangular Moving Average.
Calculation:
TriMA[i] = (MVA(i,N) + MVA(i-1,N) + … + MVA(i-N+1,N))/N
where
MVA(i,N) – a simple moving average (SMA),
N=(Period+1)/2
The indicator has two input parameters:
Period
- period;
Applied price
- price used for calculations.
