The indicator draws a Moving Average, as well as bullish and bearish areas built using Bollinger Bands calculated based on this Moving Average.
It has five input parameters:
Period of MA calculation
- MA calculation period;
Method of MA calculation
- MA calculation method;
Applied price of MA
- price used for the MA calculation;
Period of BB calculation
- Bollinger Bands calculation period;
Deviation of BB
- Bollinger Bands deviation.
