Parabolic SAR made as an oscillator.
It has three configurable parameters:
SAR step
- step of Parabolic SAR
SAR maximum
- maximum Parabolic SAR
Smoothing Period
- smoothing period
Calculations:
SARH = SMA(CloseSAR, Smoothing Period)
where:
CloseSAR = Close - SAR
SAR - Parabolic SAR(SAR step, SAR maximum)
