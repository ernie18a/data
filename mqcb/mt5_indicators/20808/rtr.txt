Oscillator RTR (Range To Range) shows the ratio of two ATRs, a fast one and a slow one.
It has two input parameters:
Fast ATR Period
- fast ATR period;
Slow ATR Period
- slow ATR period.
Calculations:
RTR = 100 * ATR(Fast ATR Period) / ATR(Slow ATR Period)
