The system is based on two indicators -
MACD
and
EMA
. When both indicators move upwards, a candlestick is colored in green, when they move downwards, it is colored in red. In case of multidirectional movement, it turnes blue.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
17.06.2010.
