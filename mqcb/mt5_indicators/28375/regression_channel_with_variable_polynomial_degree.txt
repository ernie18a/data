i-Regr:
Polynomial Regression Channel consists of two parallel lines, equidistant up and down from the line of polynomial regression trend. The distance between frame of the channel and regression line equals to the value of maximum close price deviation from the regression line.
e-Regr:
(work in progress) MetaTrader Expert Advisor e-Regr based on Regression Channel MetaTrader Indicator. Trade signals: If price lower than under line - Buy, If price bigger than upper line - Sell, TakeProfit by average line.
