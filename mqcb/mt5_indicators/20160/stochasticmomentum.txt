Two indicators in one: Momentum and Stochastic:
If smoothing is applied, it is an Average Stochastic Momentum (ASM);
If not, it is a Stochastic Momentum (SM).
The indicator has eight configurable parameters:
Period
- period of Stochastic;
Signal
- period of the signal line;
Method
- signal line calculation method;
Smooth
- enable or disable smoothing (Yes/No);
First MA period
- the period for the calculation of the initial smoothing MA;
First MA method
- the method of the calculation of the initial smoothing MA;
Second MA period
- the period for the calculation of the second smoothing MA;
Second MA method
- the method of the calculation of the second smoothing MA.
