The smoothed Commodity Channel Index oscillator with the color indication of overbought/oversold level breakouts and of the histogram direction. Depending on the value of the indicator's input parameter:
input
ENUM_COLOR_MODE Mode=ENUM_LEVELS;
//Breakout indication method
There are two variants of histogram coloring:
Breakout of overbought/oversold levels;
Change of direction.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was described in detail in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
