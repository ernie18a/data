The indicator shows values of
Repulse
in a separate window, searches for divergences between the indicator lines and the price, and displays these divergences. You can choose the Repulse indicator line to search divergences for.
The indicator has three input parameters:
Repulse period
- Repulse oscillator calculation period;
Signal period
- Repulse signal line calculation period;
Repulse line
- Repulse indicator line to search divergences for:
Main
- oscillator line;
Signal
- signal line.
The Repulse indicator is
not required
for a proper operation, since all calculations are performed inside the indicator.
