Like
Deviation from MA
, the indicator
displays values of maximum deviation between MA and the price. However, unlike
Deviation from MA
,
it uses a colored histogram in a separate window.
Frames are used to detect maximum values (as when searching for fractals).
It has four inputs:
Period
- МА calculation period
Method
- МА calculation method
Applied price
- МА calculation price
Frames
- size of one frame, within which the search for a maximum deviation is performed
Detected maximum deviations within a frame are marked with colored histogram columns:
maximum deviation
downwards from МА is marked in blue
maximum deviation upwards
from МА is marked in red.
