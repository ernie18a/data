This indicator is based on the Nadaraya-Watson method using Gaussian smoothing to build a dynamic envelope around price. The upper and lower bands adjust with market movement, while arrows are displayed when price crosses beyond either side of the envelope. The calculation is designed so that historical signals remain fixed once they have been calculated.
Main Features
Non-repainting calculation
Nadaraya-Watson Gaussian smoothing
Dynamic upper and lower envelope bands
Clear upper and lower band crossing signals
Solid colored signal arrows
Adjustable bandwidth
Adjustable envelope multiplier
Selectable applied price
Customizable band and arrow colors
Works on all symbols and timeframes
Lightweight chart display
No external indicators required
