A simple oscillator based on the difference between values of two MAs either of different periods (Fast MA - Slow MA) or of the same one but with a "lag" of a few bars (set in the
iPeriod
variable).
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
The indicator has originally been written in MQL4 and was first published in the
Code Base
on 21.07.2014.
