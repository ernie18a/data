Oscillator Volatility Ratio2 is the second version of indicator
Volatility Ratio
.
If the values exceed 2, the current trend reversal probability is high.
It has three adjustable parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Threshold
- the threshold level
Show alerts
- show data on exceeding the threshold level
Calculations:
VR = TR / EMATR
where:
TR = Max(Max(High-Low, Abs(High-PrevClose)), Abs(PrevClose-Low))
EMATR = EMA(TR, Period)
