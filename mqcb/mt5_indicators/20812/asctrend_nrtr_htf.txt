Indicator
ASCtrend_NRTR
with the timeframe selection option available in input parameters, alerting and sending e-mails and push-messages when the indicator changes its color:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// The indicator chart period (timeframe)
For the indicator to operate, the
ASCtrend_NRTR.ex5
indicator must be added to the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
