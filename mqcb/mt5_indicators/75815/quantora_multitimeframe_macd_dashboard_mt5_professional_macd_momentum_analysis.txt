Quantora Multi-Timeframe MACD Dashboard MT5 is a multi-timeframe momentum and trend analysis indicator for MetaTrader 5.
It analyzes MACD across seven timeframes — from M1 to D1 — and shows the main, signal, and histogram values for each one. Every timeframe is classified from strong bearish to strong bullish, giving a clear view of momentum conditions.
All results are combined into a total momentum score and an overall MACD bias, making it easy to understand whether market direction is aligned or mixed across timeframes.
The dashboard also tracks how many timeframes are bullish, bearish, or neutral, along with positive and negative histogram conditions.
MACD settings can be adjusted to fit different trading styles. The interface is clean, updates automatically, and works on all symbols and timeframes.
This tool is designed for analysis only. It does not generate trading signals or manage trades.
