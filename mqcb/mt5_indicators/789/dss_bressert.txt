The Double Smoothed Stochastics indicator was proposed by William Blau and Walter Bressert. The calculation of DSS values is similar to
Stochastic
indicator, the difference is the use of double exponentially smoothing.
The interpretation of DSS values is the same as for
Stochastic
- the values above 80 indicates the overbought state of the market, the values below 20 indicates the oversold market state.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
08.08.2008.
