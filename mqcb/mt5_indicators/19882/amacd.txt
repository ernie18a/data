The indicator shows MACD calculated based on AMA.
The indicator has eight configurable parameters:
Period of the Fast AMA
;
Fast EMA of the Fast AMA
- the period of the fast EMA in the fast AMA settings;
Slow EMA of the Fast AMA
- the period of the slow EMA in the fast AMA settings;
Period of the Slow AMA
;
Fast EMA of the Slow AMA
- the period of the fast EMA in the slow AMA settings;
Slow EMA of the Slow AMA
- the period of the slow EMA in the slow AMA settings;
The signal line period
- period for the signal line of AMACD;
Applied price
- AMACD calculation price.
