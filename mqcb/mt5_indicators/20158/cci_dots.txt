The indicator adds signal marks to a price chart when the
CCI
indicator crosses its zero line. If CCI crosses the zero line upwards, a dot is added below the candlestick, if downwards the dot is above the candlestick.
The indicator has three input parameters:
CCI period
- CCI indicator parameter;
CCI applied price
- price used for CCI calculation;
MA period
- period of the moving average for the calculation of the distance from the candlestick's High or Low to placing signal marks.
