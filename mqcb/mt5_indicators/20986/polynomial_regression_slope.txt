Indicator Polynomial Regression Slope draws a
regression curve
as a histogram that fits best with the prices between the initial and end points of the price.
There are three adjustable parameters:
Period
- calculation period (regression line length in bars);
Power
- defines the "strength" of the polynomial to be calculated;
Applied price
- price used for calculations.
