Basics:
The trend direction and force was developed long time ago by Pyotr
Wojdyło for metastock trading platform. Since then we have made multiple versions for
MetaTrader
(both
MetaTrader 4 and
MetaTrader 5).
This version:
This indicator is using double smoothed EMA (DSEMA) for smoothing result in order to produce less false signals.
Usage:
You can use the color change as signals for trend change or for breakouts.
