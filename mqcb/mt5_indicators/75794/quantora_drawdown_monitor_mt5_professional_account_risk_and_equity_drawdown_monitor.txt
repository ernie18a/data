Quantora Drawdown Monitor MT5 is an indicator that tracks account drawdown and risk in real time. It does not open or manage trades and is designed for monitoring and analysis.
The tool compares balance and equity to show current drawdown in both percentage and account currency. It also tracks peak equity and calculates equity drawdown, giving a clearer view of how the account is performing over time.
It includes maximum drawdown tracking and a daily performance summary with daily profit, loss, and drawdown values. Floating profit or loss and the number of open positions are also displayed.
Risk levels are automatically classified based on configurable thresholds, helping you quickly understand whether the account condition is low, moderate, high, or critical. A simple visual bar supports quick monitoring.
Suitable for traders who want a clear and practical way to track drawdown and overall account risk.
