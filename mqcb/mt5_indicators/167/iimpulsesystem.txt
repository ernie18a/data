The indicator is based on the Elder's Impluse system, it plots candles with different colors, dependent on the
OsMa
(12,26,9) and
Moving Average
(EMA 13) indicators:
If OsMa and EMA grows up, the candle color is green;
If OsMa and EMA falls down, the candle color is red;
For all other cases the candle color is grey;
