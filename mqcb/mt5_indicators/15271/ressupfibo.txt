The indicator displays a price momentum for a symbol accompanied by Fibo retracement markup. Pink and blue lines are displayed on a chart, as well as Fibo retracement markup indicating a market entry direction. Sell if the price breaks through the lower line and buy if it pierces the upper one. The indicator works best on H1 and higher. Support/resistance lines are calculated as local extreme values per the number of bars defined by
iPeriod
input parameter.
input
uint
iPeriod=
24
;
//price extremums searching period
The countdown for searching extreme values starts from the bar set in
SignalBar
.
input
uint
SignalBar=
1
;
//bar index for searching extremums
