Witold Wozniak
The indicator is inspired by John Ehlers' article
"Using The Fisher Transform"
published in November 2002 in the
"Technical Analysis Of Stock & Commodities"
magazine.
It generates buy and sell signals. The simplest trading system for working with the indicator is completely equivalent to working with
Stochastic Oscillator
.
See also the article
"Applying The Fisher Transform and Inverse Fisher Transform to Market Analysis in MetaTrader 5"
.
