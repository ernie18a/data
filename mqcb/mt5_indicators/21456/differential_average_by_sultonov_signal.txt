Differential_Average_By_Sultonov_Signal indicator shows information on the current trend using the values of the
Differential_Average_By_Sultonov
indicator with a fixed timeframe.
When receiving signals from the color cloud, the color is shown. Colored points appear on the lines when the bar of the relevant timeframe changes.
The indicator requires compiled
Differential_Average_By_Sultonov.ex5
indicator in the <terminal_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
