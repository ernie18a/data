The
Volume_Weighted_MA
indicator implemented as a sequence of candlesticks. Candlesticks appear as a result of relevant price timeseries processed by the Volume_Weighted_MA indicator algorithm.
In many situations, such approach may be more informative for the purpose of analysis.
