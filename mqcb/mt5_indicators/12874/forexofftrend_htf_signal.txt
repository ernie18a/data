The ForexOFFTrend_HTF_Signal indicator shows a trend direction or a signal generated by the
ForexOFFTrendSign
indicator at the chosen bar as a graphical object with colored trend indication or deal direction. It also triggers alerts and plays audio signals.
If the trend continues at the selected bar, the indicator displays a right arrow. Its color corresponds to the trend direction. If the trend has changed at the selected bar, the indicator displays a diagonal arrow. Its color and direction correspond to the deal direction.

If several ForexOFFTrend_HTF_Signal indicators are to be used on one chart, each of them should have its own Symbols_Sirname (indicator labels names) string variable value.
This indicator requires the compiled indicator file ForexOFFTrendSign.mq5. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators\.
