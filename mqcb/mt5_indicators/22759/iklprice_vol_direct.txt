Indicator
i-KlPrice_Vol
that shows the direction of moving the histogram bars as colored labels on such bars.
The indicator uses the classes of SmoothAlgorithms.mqh (to be copied to terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in article
Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers
