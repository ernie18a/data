It allows setting any price and calculation method for the central line. The channel lines are calculated based on ATR.
It has five input parameters:
Number of the periods to smooth the center line
- central line calculation period;
Number of periods to smooth deviation
- period for calculating the channel lines;
Factor which is used to apply the deviation
- cloud size;
The center line applied price
- price used for calculating the central line;
The center line smoothing method
- smoothing method.
