The
XFisher_org_v1_Vol_Supr
indicator with additional signals displayed as round dots, which indicate zero line breakout by the indicator line.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes is described in details in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
