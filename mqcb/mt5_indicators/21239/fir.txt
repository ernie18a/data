Indicator FIR is a symmetrically weighted filter (Finite Impulse Response Filter).
It has one input parameter:
Applied price
- price used for calculations.
Calculations:
FIR = (Price(0) + 2*Price(1) + 2*Price(2) + Price(3)) / 6
where:
Price = SMA(1, Applied price)
