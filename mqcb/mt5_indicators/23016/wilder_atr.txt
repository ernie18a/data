Wilder ATR is an average true range by
J. Welles Wilder
It has two adjustable parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Method
- the method of smoothing
Calculations:
Wilder ATR = MA(TR, Period, Method)
where:
TR (True Range) = Maximum(HL, HC, LC)
HL = High-Low
HC = Abs(High-PrevClose)
LC = Abs(Low-PrevClose)
