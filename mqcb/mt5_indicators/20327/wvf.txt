The volatility index oscillator (VIX).
It has two parameters:
Period
- calculation period (default 22)
Drawing style
:
Line - a colored line
Histogram - a colored histogram
The original calculation formula:
WVF = [Highest (Close,22) - Low) / (Highest(Close,22)] * 100
