A trading system based on the signals of the
ColorXDerivative
indicator. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is a change in the oscillator direction.
For the EA to operate correctly, the compiled file of indicator
ColorXDerivative.ex5
must be in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
In the texts below, the default inputs of the EA are used. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
