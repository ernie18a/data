Working with pending orders Buy Stop and Sell Stop. A channel of two indicators iMA (Moving Average, MA).
Input parameters
Take Profit
- Take Profit;
Trailing Stop
- trailing;
Trailing Step
- trailing step;
Lots
- lot size is set manually (a NECESSARY CONDITION:
Risk
must be equal to zero!);
Risk
- lot size is calculated automatically, in risk percents per trade (a NECESSARY CONDITION: "Lots" must be equal to zero!);
Difference
- offsets from the channel borders;
Expert Every Tick
- enable/disable working on every tick;
MA PRICE_HIGH and PRICE_LOW: averaging period
- indicators averaging period;
MA PRICE_HIGH and PRICE_LOW: horizontal shift
- indicators horizontal shift;
MA PRICE_HIGH: smoothing type
- averaging type for indicator calculated by High prices;
MA PRICE_LOW: smoothing type
- averaging type for indicator calculated by Low prices;
magic number
- unique identifier for the EA.
Trading idea: Forming a channel of two indicators iMA (Moving Average, MA) - one based on prices PRICE_HIGH and another one on PRICE_LOW. Pending Buy Stop and Sell Stop orders are placed at the channel boarders. The calculation is on the break-through (catching a strong movement).
The EA can work in modes: On every tick and only when a new bar appears. The mode is defined by parameter
Expert Every Tick
