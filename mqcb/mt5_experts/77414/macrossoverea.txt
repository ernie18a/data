MA Crossover EA — an expert advisor based on the crossover of moving averages
The expert advisor opens trades when two moving averages (Fast and Slow) cross. It works on any instrument and timeframe.
Features:
Opens a BUY trade when the Fast MA crosses from bottom to top
Opens a SELL position when the Fast MA crosses from top to bottom
Setting Stop Loss and Take Profit
Only one position at a time
Trading only on a new bar (optional)
Customisable parameters
Parameters:
Lot — lot size
FastMAPeriod, SlowMAPeriod — MA periods
StopLoss, TakeProfit — levels in pips
MagicNumber — magic number
TradeOnNewBar — trade only on bar close
Recommendations:
Before using this expert advisor on a live account, test it in the Strategy Tester and optimise the parameters for your specific instrument.
