The Exp_Bezier Expert Advisor is based on the change in the direction of a universal
Bezier
curve. A signal for a transaction forms when a bar is closing, provided that the curve direction has changed.
Proper operation of the generated Expert Advisor requires the compiled indicator file Bezier.ex5 to be placed in terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Indicators.
