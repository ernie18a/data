Two independent trading systems using indicators
SilverTrend_V2
and
ColorJFatl_Digit
within one EA. Signals to open trades by trading systems are formed when a bar is closing, if the trend has changed, displayed through changing the color of any of those two indicators.
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled files of indicators
SilverTrend_V2.ex5
and
ColorJFatl_Digit.ex5
must be in the
<terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators
folder.
The default EA inputs were used in tests below. Stop Loss and Take Profit were not used in testing.
