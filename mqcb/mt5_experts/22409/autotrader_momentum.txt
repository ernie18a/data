The EA works on netting and hedging accounts. Trading signals are checked only when a new bar appears, while trailing works constantly. When opening a position, a stop loss (
Stop Loss
) and take profit (
Take Profit
) are placed immediately.
To disable stop loss, take profit or trailing, simply set
Stop Loss
,
Take Profit
or
Trailing Stop
to "0.0".
