Usage notes
Run the EA in Strategy Tester first. Brick size is expressed in price units, not points. Virtual SL/TP require the EA and terminal to remain running; they are not broker-side protective orders. Exits may be delayed by closed sessions, connection loss or execution restrictions. Price gaps can cause losses beyond the nominal stop.
Trade requests are checked against symbol trading sessions. Renko/momentum history starts again after EA reinitialization. Other positions or active orders on the symbol block new entries. Use one instance per symbol and a unique Magic.
The historical result is not evidence of a persistent trading edge and is not a promise of future profitability.
