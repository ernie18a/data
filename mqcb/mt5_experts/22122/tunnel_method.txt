Trading is based on three iMAs (Moving Average, MA). Two different indicators are compared for BUY and SELL. The necessary condition is a distance between indicators (
Indentation between indicators
) on a compared bar: the compared indicators should be separated from each other at the distance equal to
Indentation between indicators
/2.
You can work on any timeframe. OnTick sends signals for verification with the
Pause
interval.
