A trading system based on the signals of indicator
FineTuningMACandle
. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is change in candle direction maintaining change of its color.
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled
FineTuningMACandle.ex5
indicator file should be available in the
<terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators
folder.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below.
