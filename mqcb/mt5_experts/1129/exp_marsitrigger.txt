The Exp_MaRsi-Trigger Expert Advisor realized the trading system based on the
MaRsi-Trigger
indicator of three states of the market. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is change in trend direction fixed by the indicator. In order to get trading signals to speed the work of the Expert Advisor, the MaRsi-Trigger indicator is not used in the Expert Advisor itself. For these purposes the code is implemented on the basis of the initial technical indicators and the ColorMaRsi-Trigger indicator is activated only for visualization when testing the trading system.
