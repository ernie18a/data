This Expert Advisor is
purely
a
diagnostic tool
: it does not open any trades, but analyses and ‘captures a snapshot’ of the environment in which MetaTrader 5 is running.
Once launched on the chart, it collects all the broker’s technical specifications and displays them in three ways:
The chart panel
at the top left of the chart (with data updated in real time).
Terminal log (the
‘Experts’ tab at the bottom).
CSV file
(saved in the MQL5/Files folder on your PC).
What information does it extract?
The EA reads dozens of vital parameters in real time, grouping them by category:
Time Zone (Crucial):
Calculates the broker’s server time, your PC’s time and the exact difference between the server time and UTC (Greenwich) time.
Trading Costs:
Extracts the swap calculation method, the long and short swap values in real currency (per 1 lot), and the current spread.
Symbol Rules:
Digits (decimals), contract size, minimum/maximum/step volume, Stop Levels (minimum distance for placing Stop Loss and Take Profit orders) and Freeze Level.
Execution and Connection:
Ping in milliseconds (latency), connection status, order filling modes accepted by the broker (FOK, IOC) and margin calculation method.
Account:
Broker name, server, account currency, leverage and equity.
