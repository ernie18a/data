The Expert Advisor Exp_AdaptiveCyberCycle is built on the base of indicator
Adaptive Cyber Cycle
. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is crossing of indicator and its signal line. In input parameters of the Expert Advisor is possibility to use the expert Advisor with the same indicators
Adaptive CG Oscillator
,
CyberCycle
and
Adaptive RVI
.
Place
AdaptiveCyberCycle.ex5, CyberCycle.ex5, AdaptiveRVI.ex5, AdaptiveCGOscillator.ex5 and
CyclePeriod.ex5
complied files to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
