The Exp_ColorTrend_CF trading system is based on change of the trend direction displayed by the
ColorTrend_CF
indicator. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is change in cloud color of the indicator.
Place ColorTrend_CF.ex5 compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
