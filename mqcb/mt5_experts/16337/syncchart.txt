Synchronization of the bars displayed on all open charts of the terminal. Synchronization of charts if performed by timer. No DLL!
Additional features:
option to synchronize vertical lines
disable synchronization when autotrading is disabled in the terminal;
synchronization of chart type (bars/candlesticks/line chart), scale, shifting.
