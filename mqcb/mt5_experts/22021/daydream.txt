The EA searches for the minimum and maximum price for
Channel
bars starting with bar #1. If the Close price of bar #0 is less than the minimum price, open BUY position, if Close price of bar #0 exceeds the maximum price, open SELL.
Take profit (
Virtual Take Profit
) is virtual here - positions are closed at the market price when reaching a virtual take profit.
