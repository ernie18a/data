How it works
- Classic fixed-size Renko is built internally from BID ticks.
- Reversal uses the classic two-brick rule.
- SuperTrend ATR and bands are calculated from completed Renko bricks, not time candles.
- A trade is allowed when the completed Renko brick direction agrees with the Renko
SuperTrend direction.
- Positions close by price target, stop, SuperTrend flip, or maximum holding time.
- A short Renko cooldown can be used before re-entry.
- Only one position on the symbol is allowed.
The EA is standalone. No external indicator, DLL, custom symbol or offline chart is required.
