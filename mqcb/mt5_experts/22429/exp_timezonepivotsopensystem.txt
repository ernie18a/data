The trading system based on the signals of the
TimeZonePivotsOpenSystem
indicator. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is a channel breakthrough.
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled
TimeZonePivotsOpenSystem.ex5
indicator file should be available in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
The EA works on timeframes not higher than H1:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
InpInd_Timeframe=
PERIOD_H1
;
// TimeZonePivotsOpenSystem indicator timeframe
