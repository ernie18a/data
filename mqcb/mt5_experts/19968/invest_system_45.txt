The Expert Advisor checks two parameters: the profitability of the last closed trade and the account balance.
Attention: works only on "EURUSD"!
Input Parameters
Stop Loss (in pips)
- Stop Loss value;
Take Profit (in pips)
- Take Profit value;
magic number
- unique identifier for the EA.
