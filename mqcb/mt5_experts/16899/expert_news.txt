The Expert Advisor trades on the market jumps using no indicators.
The idea behind the EA is that stop orders move on the set distance from the current price discretely in time.
If the price moves sharply to one side, the Expert Advisor simply has no time to move the order and therefore, it becomes a market one.
The order trailing is activated afterwards.
