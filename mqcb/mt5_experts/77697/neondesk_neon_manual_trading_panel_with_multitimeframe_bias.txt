NeonDesk is a manual trading panel for MetaTrader 5. It does not trade automatically -- every position opens only when you press BUY or SELL, and every action is confirmed before it is sent.
What it shows you: a multi-timeframe bias read (M1, M30, H4, D1) so you can see at a glance whether the market is leaning bullish, bearish or mixed; a live candle countdown timer for a timeframe you choose yourself (M1 up to D1), independent of your chart's own period; current Bid/Ask and the candle's Open/High/Low/Close; and an ATR-based Stop-Loss/Take-Profit suggestion for information only -- it never overrides your own settings.
What you control: lot size, Stop-Loss and Take-Profit, entered and edited directly as real price levels matching your chart's own price format; an independent trailing stop set in pips that only ever moves your stop-loss in your favour, never backwards; and one-click BUY, SELL and CLOSE ALL buttons, isolated to this panel's own trades via a dedicated magic number, so it never touches positions opened by any other EA or by you manually.
Built-in safety: a risk disclaimer must be accepted once when the panel is attached, reminding you that BUY/SELL/CLOSE ALL use real funds and that you are responsible for your own Stop-Loss/Take-Profit decisions. Every BUY/SELL asks for confirmation first, showing the exact symbol, lot size, entry, SL and TP before anything is sent, then reports whether the order succeeded. The panel refuses an order outright if your Stop-Loss or Take-Profit is on the wrong side of the entry price, or closer than your broker's minimum stop distance allows. If you already have an open trade on the same symbol, it warns you first, showing your combined exposure and a note on how your account's margin mode (netting/hedging) will treat the new trade.
The panel is fully draggable and resizable, fades to a low opacity when your mouse is elsewhere on the chart, and uses a dark neon colour scheme so it stays legible without cluttering your chart.
