An educational example of a breakout Expert Advisor that works with pending orders. It shows how to build a daily price range from M1 bars, place a pair of stop orders, delete the other order after a fill and restore its state after a terminal restart.
Strategy
After the range ends (e.g. 00:00–07:00 server time) the EA calculates its High and Low from M1 bars and places one set of orders per day:
BuyStop = High + Shift + spread; SL = Low − ShiftSL; TP = entry + RR × (entry − SL),
SellStop = Low − Shift; SL = High + ShiftSL + spread; TP = entry − RR × (SL − entry).
When one order is filled, the other is deleted. Unfilled orders are deleted at the cancel time; an open position can optionally be closed at a set time.
If the price is already beyond a breakout level (gap), that side is skipped. Ranges narrower than the minimum or wider than the maximum width are skipped for the day.
After a restart the EA checks today's orders in the history, so it does not place a second set on the same day.

Inputs
Range start / Range end (HH:MM, server time) – 00:00 / 07:00
Delete pending orders at (HH:MM) – 20:00
Close open position at CloseTime – on; Close time – 22:00
Min / Max range width – in points, 0 = no limit
Entry shift from High/Low – 20 points; Stop Loss shift beyond the range – 20 points
TP RR – 1.0 (1:1); Lot (fixed) – 0.10
Magic number – 5030; Max slippage – 10 points
Trading days – Monday to Friday (each can be switched off)
Max spread – 30 points (0 = off)
Important
This is an educational example, not a trading system. It works only on demo accounts and in the Strategy Tester – on a live account it shows a message and stops.
