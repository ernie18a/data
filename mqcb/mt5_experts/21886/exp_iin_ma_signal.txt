A trading system based on the signals of the
Iin_MA_Signal
indicator. A signal is formed at the close of a bar if a color icon of the indicator has appeared.
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled
Iin_MA_Signal.ex5
indicator file should be available in the
<terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators
folder.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
