Portfolio Allocation Planner
Before configuring a basket, see which target weights fit your broker's minimum lots. This free open-source tool turns an explicit hypothetical notional budget and percentages into a chart dashboard. It shows the minimum-lot notional, lots rounded down, calculated allocation, target shortfall and status of each row. Recalculate on demand, hover for details, paginate longer baskets and optionally export the displayed snapshot to CSV.
Native MT5 snapshot: hypothetical 5,000 USD notional budget, weights 50/30/20, quote age limit = 0 (historical quotes permitted). Page 1/3: US500 rounded down to 0.3 lot.
Install and use
Copy PortfolioAllocationPlanner.mq5 to MQL5/Experts and compile with F7 in MetaEditor.
Select the exact broker symbols in Market Watch and wait for quotes.
Attach the utility to a separate chart without a trading EA. It is an interface EA with no trading function.
Enter InpBudget, the explicit InpBudgetCurrency, comma-separated InpSymbols and matching InpWeightsPct. Use a decimal point; total weights must be greater than zero and at most 100%. Weights are not normalized. Empty symbols uses the chart symbol with one weight.
Click Recalculate for a new snapshot; press F7 to change inputs. Choose PAP_FR or PAP_EN for the panel language.
CSV export is off by default. Enable InpAllowCSVExport and click Export CSV to create a uniquely named UTF-8 semicolon-separated file in MQL5/Files.
InpPanelMaxWidth caps the panel width (default 1100 pixels, minimum 580 pixels). The panel also adapts to the chart window; longer portfolios use pages when available height is limited.
What the results mean
Long-side notional is Ask × contract size × an explicit currency conversion rate. Volume is rounded down on the broker lot-step grid, capped by the per-order maximum and static directional limit. Below-minimum targets stay at zero lots. The minimum notional budget estimate is the largest minimum-lot notional / target weight among resolved positive rows. It is not a recommended account deposit; unresolved rows make the basket incomplete, and the estimate does not guarantee exact weights or accepted orders.
The broker profit currency determines the native notional currency. For a different budget currency, supply exact already selected direct/inverse Forex symbol names in InpFXSymbols. Q/B uses Ask and B/Q uses 1/Bid. The route is verified from base/profit currency metadata; names and cross rates are never guessed. Quotes older than InpMaxQuoteAgeSeconds (120 by default) are unavailable; 0 explicitly permits historical estimates.
Supported calculation modes: Forex, Forex No Leverage, CFD, CFD Index, CFD Leverage, Exchange Stocks and Exchange Stocks MOEX. Futures, bonds, options and custom/synthetic symbols are unsupported. Notional is neither margin nor maximum loss. Existing exposure, session availability, funds, commission, swap, slippage and liquidity are outside scope. Static directional caps do not account for existing positions.
No orders are sent and no account number, balance, equity or positions are read. Only uniquely prefixed objects belonging to this tool are drawn and removed. Chart settings, other chart objects, Market Watch selection and terminal globals are unchanged. Files are written only after CSV opt-in and an explicit export click. No DLL, WebRequest or external library. Original utility by Fabien Martino; MIT license is included in the source.
