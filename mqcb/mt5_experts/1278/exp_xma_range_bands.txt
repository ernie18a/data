Trading system using the
XMA_Range_Bands
indicator of the universal Keltner channel. A signal to perform a deal is formed at bar closing, when the price is inside of the channel boundary.
Place XMA_Range_Bands
.ex5
compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
