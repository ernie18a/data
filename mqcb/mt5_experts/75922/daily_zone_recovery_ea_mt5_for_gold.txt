Daily Zone Recovery
— is a trading Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5 that trades from the previous day's highs and lows. It uses three independent strategies: breakout with return to the level, level test followed by breakout, and the "test-pullback-retest" scenario.
The Expert Advisor features an ATR volatility filter, spread control, individual settings for timeframes, zones, Take Profit and Stop Loss for each strategy. If needed, grid management of positions with a fixed lot and series closure at the average price is available.
