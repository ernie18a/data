The trading system based on the signals of the
XWPR_Histogram_Vol_Direct
indicator. Trading signals are formed when a bar closes if the histogram direction has changed (which is displayed by five-pointed stars changing their color).
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled
XWPR_Histogram_Vol_Direct.ex5
indicator file should be available in the
<terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators
folder.
