Quantora Margin Calculator MT5 is a utility that estimates the margin required before opening a trade. It does not open trades or generate signals, and is designed to help evaluate margin usage in advance.
The tool calculates required margin based on symbol, lot size, trade direction, and price. It also shows how the trade would affect free margin and overall margin level, helping you understand the impact before execution.
It includes margin usage percentage and a simple safety classification to indicate risk level. Symbol specifications such as contract size and volume limits are handled automatically.
Suitable for traders who want a clear view of margin requirements and account exposure before placing a trade.
