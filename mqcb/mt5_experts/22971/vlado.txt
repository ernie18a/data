The EA applies
WPR Custom
custom indicator, which allows correct display of WPR levels (Value Level #1 and WPR: Value Level #2) during a visual test
The EA only operates when a new bar appears. The EA opens only one position at a time, therefore it is able to work both on hedging and netting accounts. BUY trading signals are formed when the indicator on the
Bar current
bar is below the
WPR: Value Level #2
level; SELL signals when the indicator on the
Bar current
bar is above the
WPR: Value Level #1
level.
BUY open signal is also SELL close one and vice versa.
