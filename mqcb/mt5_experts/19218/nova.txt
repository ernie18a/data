Input Parameters
N-seconds ago
- the number of seconds to record price movement.
Step (in pips)
- step value.
Lots
- position volume.
Stop Loss (in pips)
- Stop Loss value.
Take Profit (in pips)
- Take Profit value.
Coefficient, if the stop-loss is triggered
- lot increase ratio after the stop loss.
The Expert Advisor analyzes the last '
N-seconds ago
' seconds and compares with the current price.
The Expert Advisor monitors triggering of Stop Loss and Take Profit by using the excellent enumeration
ENUM_DEAL_REASON
:
Identifier
Description
***
DEAL_REASON_SL
The deal was executed as a result of Stop Loss activation
DEAL_REASON_TP
The deal was executed as a result of Take Profit activation
***
If Stop Loss has triggered, the lot of the next position will be increased in accordance with the '
Coefficient, if the stop-loss is triggered
' parameter.
Position Opening Rules
BUY
- bar #1 must be bullish (Close > Open) and the current price must be higher than the price '
N-seconds ago
' by '
Step
'.
SELL
- bar #1 must be bearish (Close < Open) and the current price must be lower than the price '
N-seconds ago
' by '
Step
'.
