The EA only operates when a new bar appears: It compares the tick volumes of the first and the second bar. Block for making decisions:

if
(array_volume[
1
]>array_volume[
2
])
     {
      ClosePositions(
POSITION_TYPE_SELL
);
      OpenBuy();
     }
if
(array_volume[
1
]<array_volume[
2
])
     {
      ClosePositions(
POSITION_TYPE_BUY
);
      OpenSell();
     }
If the tick volume of the first bar is
LARGER
than that of the second bar, then you should CLOSE your Sell positions and open a Buy position;
If the tick volume of the first bar is
SMALLER
than that of the second bar, then you should CLOSE your Buy positions and open a Sell position;
