USDX Trader builds a
synthetic US Dollar Index
from the currency pairs already in your Market Watch, shows it on your chart as a normal symbol, and lets you
buy or sell the index as a basket of the underlying pairs
— with take-profit and stop-loss measured on the
index itself
, not on any single pair.
It is made for traders whose broker
does not offer a USDX / DXY symbol
.
Why this matters if your broker does not offer USDX
The US Dollar Index is the standard yardstick for broad dollar strength. Many retail brokers do not list it at all, or list only a futures-based CFD with wide spreads, limited hours or contract rolls. Without it, traders often have to watch the index on a separate website and trade on another platform.
Problem without a USDX symbol
What USDX Trader does
No dollar-strength chart inside MT5
Creates a custom symbol with live prices and history, so all timeframes, indicators and drawing tools work on it
You judge USD pairs one at a time (EURUSD, GBPUSD…) and mix in the other currency's news
Shows one combined USD reading built from six or more pairs
You can watch the dollar but cannot trade it as one position
Opens and closes the whole basket with one click, sized by index weights
SL and TP on one pair do not match your view of "the dollar"
Virtual TP/SL are checked against the index level, and the whole basket closes together
Broker symbol names differ ( EURUSD.m , EURUSDpro …)
Finds the correct symbols automatically (or uses your suffix)

this is a

Lot sizing
For the geometric formula, each leg's exposure is weight × size × Basket unit in account currency and is converted to lots with the symbol's tick value. With the default basket unit of 100,000,
size 1.0 ≈ 100,000 of exposure
, and a 1% move in the index is worth about 1,000 in account currency.
Small legs (SEK, CHF, SGD) need a larger size to stay accurate. If a leg's ideal volume is below the broker's minimum lot, the EA uses the minimum lot, shows the ideal and actual volumes and the percentage difference, and
asks for your confirmation
. The basket will then not match the target weights exactly.
Strategy Tester and automatic mode
Custom symbols cannot be created in the Strategy Tester, so in the tester the EA
computes the index virtually
from the leg symbols (it selects them automatically). In the tester there are no dialogs and no panel clicks, so an
optional automatic mode
is provided:

The automatic logic is a simple demonstration: fast/slow EMA crossover on closed index bars (default H1, 12/26). It takes a first position after the warm-up and reverses on each crossover, using the virtual TP/SL.
It is not an optimised or recommended strategy.
If a leg symbol is missing in the tester, the EA drops it and renormalises the remaining weights so the test can run.

Limitations
Not the official index.
Values come from your broker's prices. Expect small differences from the published ICE index and from DX futures (feed, spread, futures basis, bid vs mid).
High and low of older bars are approximations.
Only the last N bars (default 20) are rebuilt from real ticks; older history is built from M1 bars.
Multi-leg execution.
Each leg pays spread, may be slippage-affected and carries its own swap. A leg that fails to open triggers a rollback of the other legs.
Virtual TP/SL.
Levels are checked by the EA while it is running. If the terminal is offline or the connection is lost,
nothing closes the basket
. There are no server-side SL/TP orders.
Gaps and fast markets
can fill beyond the TP/SL level.
One basket at a time
per magic number. On netting accounts, other positions in the same pairs will merge with the basket's legs.
Small sizes
distort the hedge ratio because of minimum lot sizes.
The synthetic symbol is
not tradable directly
(trading on it is disabled); trades are placed on the underlying pairs.
The automatic mode is a demonstration and is not optimised.
