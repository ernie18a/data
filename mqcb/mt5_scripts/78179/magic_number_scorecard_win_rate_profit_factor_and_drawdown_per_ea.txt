Every Expert Advisor marks its trades with a magic number. Magic Number Scorecard groups the closed trades of the account by magic number, so you get one score line per EA (magic 0 = manual trades). It is a quick way to see which EA really earns, and which one only looks busy.
For each magic number
trades, wins, losses and win rate
gross profit, gross loss and profit factor
net profit and average trade
best and worst trade
maximum drawdown of its closed-trade equity curve
symbols traded
The table is printed in the Experts journal, shown on the chart, and written to a CSV file in MQL5\Files that opens in Excel. The rows are sorted by net profit.
How the trades are counted
A trade is a closed position. All of its deals are added up: profit, swap, commission (entry and exit) and fee. A position closed in parts counts as one trade.
The owner of a position is the magic number of its opening deal, so an EA trade that you close by hand still counts for that EA.
Positions that are still open, fully or partly, are left out.
