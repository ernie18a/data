The "Pending orders UP" script sets a grid of orders above the current price.
Input Parameters
Gap for pending orders UP from the current price (in pips)
- the distance from the current price to place the first pending order.
Step between orders UP (in pips)
- distance between pending orders.
Type of pending orders UP
- the type of the pending order (can be either Buy Stop or Sell Limit).
UP quantity
- the number of orders to be placed.
Lots
- the volume of each order.
Stop Loss (in pips)
- Stop Loss value.
Take Profit (in pips)
- Take Profit value.
The script ensures the fastest possible sending of trade requests to place pending orders due to the use of the
asynchronous mode of trading operations
:

true
