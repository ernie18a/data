Theory:
Lets consider how does it work on EURUSD. Imagine that we have two synthetic pairs
EURUSDx
and
EURUSDy
.
They have similar dynamics, so if we open two opposite positions on these pairs we will have a hedged position.
Open: BUY
EURUSDx
and SELL
EURUSDy
. After some time we close these positions: SELL
EURUSDx
and BUY
EURUSDy
.
Profit:
Profit = (BIDx - ASKx) + (BIDy - ASKy) = (BIDx - ASKy) + (BIDy - ASKx)
In the experssion presented above we know the value of the first bracket (BUY
EURUSDx
and SELL
EURUSDy
).
The value of the second bracket is known after positions close (SELL
EURUSDx
and BUY
EURUSDy
)
There are several cases with positive Profit values. One of them is:
At open:
BIDx > ASKy
,
At close:
BIDy > ASKx
.
