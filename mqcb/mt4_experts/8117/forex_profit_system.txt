Trading Strategy Algorithm:
1. Open a one-hour chart for EURUSD, lots 0.1;
2. Draw indicators: one Parabolic SAR and three Exponential Moving Averages with the periods of (10, 25, 50).
Buying signal:
EMA with the period of 10 intersects EMAs 25 and 50 from bottom to top, Parabolic SAR being below the price
.
Selling signal:
EMA with the period of 10 intersects EMAs 25 and 50 from top to bottom
,
Parabolic SAR being above the price
.
Exiting Trades:
1. By StopLoss;
2. By TakeProfit
;
3. When the ЕМА10 line turns back (if there is a
Buy position, then close in the point where
ЕМА10 peak is formed, or trough - for Sell positions
), provided the minimum profit is available. At the same time, the value of StopLoss and TakeProfit for buying and selling trades will be different.
