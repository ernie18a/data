The Dealers Trade v 7.74 EA is based on the algorithm of multi-lot scalping. The EA has been repeatedly tested on a live account.
The direction for opening positions is selected based on the standard MACD. The EA subsequently opens positions, with every following one 1,5 or 2 times greater than the previous. The parameter is selected in the settings. The interval between the opened positions in points can be set in the EA parameters. When a positive result is reached on the last (closure by TP or by trailing SL) position, largest by the volume, the order batch is closed, regardless of the profitability of the previously opened positions.
As a rule, the profit of the last order covers the loss (if any) of the previously opened orders and makes profit in general. The EA can work on any time frame, it is advisable to choose the parameters for each currency pair. The report on real testing is attached.
