Quantora Trailing Stop Manager MT4 is an automatic stop loss management utility for MetaTrader 4 designed to help protect profits on open trades.
It monitors active positions in real time and adjusts the stop loss as price moves in your favor. Once a defined profit level is reached, the trailing system activates and follows the market using user-defined distance and step settings. This allows trades to stay open during favorable conditions while gradually securing gains.
The tool includes flexible controls for how trailing is applied. You can define when trailing starts, how far the stop loss should follow price, and how often it should be updated. A minimum profit condition ensures that trailing only begins when a trade has reached a meaningful level of profit.
Filtering options allow you to manage all trades, only manual trades, or positions with a specific Magic Number. It also supports both current symbol and all symbols, making it suitable for single-strategy or multi-strategy accounts. Buy and sell positions can be handled together without manual intervention.
A built-in dashboard provides a clear overview of account status, active trades, floating profit or loss, and current trailing settings. You can start or stop the manager at any time, or instantly apply trailing logic using the one-click function.
The utility is lightweight, updates automatically, and is designed for stable performance across different brokers and market conditions.
This tool is intended for trade management only. It does not open trades or generate trading signals, and trailing results depend on market conditions and execution.
