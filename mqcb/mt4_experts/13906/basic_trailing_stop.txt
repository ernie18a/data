Add a trailing stop to your entry signals. Simply put in your entry conditions so that the
LongRule()
function returns "1" when long conditions are met and the
ShortRule()
function returns "-1" when the short conditions are met. You can set the trailing stop distance, in pips, as an external input.
Trailing stop will adjust at each tick.
Recommendations:
Will work on 4 and 5 digit brokers.
Always test on demo account first.
Does not include exit logic besides Trailing Stop.
