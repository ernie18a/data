Internal Bar Strength (IBS)
- is the indicator developed by Volker Knapp.
Calculation:
It is calculated as the moving average of the values of the internal bars strength that represent the ratio
(Close-Low)/(High-Low) * 100%
for each of them.
How to use:
The period that is equal to five bars is usually used. The crossing of 60% level means the overbuying and the crossing of 40% level means overselling, and so they are the signals for selling and buying respectively.
Input Parameters:
extern int Per=5;
