NRMA is the famous indicator by Konstantin Kopyrkin, it produced numerous realizations of the indicator NRTR. In this variant we see a moving average (line) and trailing stops NRTR (points).
Access to other intermediate Omega indicators (NRTR_Oscillator, NRatio, NRTR_DT) is available throug calls of
iCustom
(NULL,0,"NRMA",parameters,
index_number
, shift).
More detailed information is on the
author's website
