This indicator uses Bollinger Bands lines to detect markets trend.
It's a very useful tool for manual trading.
Parameters
BarsCount
- How many bars used to show the line.
BBperiod
- Period of Bollinger Bands.
BBdeviations
- Deviations of Bollinger Bands.
ATRperiod
- Period of ATR.
UseATRfilter
- Use ATR for filter.
AlertON
- Pop up an alert.
ShowArrows
- Show arrows in case of any trend change.
