Elastic Volume Weighted Moving Average (
EVWMA
), a natural replacement for the standard moving average.
Differences of the EVWMA
from the standard МА:
1. The
EVWMA
does not have an averaging period, instead it uses the volume to determine the period of the average component.
2. The
EVWMA
considers the information on volume (or time) in a natural and logical manner.
3. The
EVWMA
can be obtained as an approximation to the statistical estimation, and thus has a mathematical basis.
