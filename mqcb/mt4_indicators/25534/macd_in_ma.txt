MACD in MA is an indicator to change color in iMA if iMACD is upper of zero (uptrend) or lower of zero (downtrend).
It helps any trader to see the trend of pair.
Parameters
MA_Periods
— Period for iMA.
MA_Method
— Method for iMA.
MACD_FastEMA
— Fast EMA for MACD.
MACD_SlowEMA
— Slow EMA for MACD.
MACD_SignalSMA
— SMA signal for MACD.
MACD_ApliedPrice
— Price used for MACD.
BarsCount
— How many bars will show the line.
ReverseSignals
— Reverse up trend to down trend signals.
