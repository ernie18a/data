This indicator calculates correlation between MAs of different periods.
Lines:
Black
/
Red
dots - Shows Open/Close signals (Above chart = BUY, below = SELL)
Silver Line
- Trend corelation (100 = All MAs are UP trend, -100 = All MAs are DOWN trend)
Blue Line
- Sort Degree (100 = All MAs are ASCENDING, -100 = All MAs are DESCENDING)
Blue
Dashed
Line
- Rough Sort Degree between MAcheck, Higher MAs and Lower MAs (100 = All Higher MAs are above MAcheck and all Lower MAs are below MAcheck, -100 = Higher MAs < MAcheck < Lower MAs; Higher and Lower MAs don't have to be sorted)
