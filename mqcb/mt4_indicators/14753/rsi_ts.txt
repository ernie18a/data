The RSI indicator implemented as a histogram with a designation of the overbought and oversold levels.
Description of the indicator settings:
InpRSIPeriod — Averaging period for the RSI calculation.
OverboughtLevels — the upper level of the overbought zone.
OversoldLevels — the lower level of the oversold zone.
