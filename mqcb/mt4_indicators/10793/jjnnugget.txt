This indicator gives
BUY or SELL signals
and
entry level
. Use it on M5 or above.
IMPORTANT! >>
DON'T
open new trade
IMMEDIATELY
when the status (BUY or SELL) changes, because it may vary! Wait until the opening of the next candle.
(But if you like the high risk and the higher profit,
in a strong trend
you can enter immediately when the status changes in the same direction of the strong trend.)
It can be useful to use in conjunction with a filter (for example, as
JJN-BigTrend
on higher timeframe), i.e. sell only in overall downtrend, buy only in overall uptrend.
Test it on
demo
for the
first
time.
Use PosX and PosY to place the indicator to the desired location.
