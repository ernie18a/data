This indicator shows the
last candle
(High-Low) of all timeframes (M1,M5 ... D1) together with the actual price.
Colors show whether the bar is
bullish
or
bearish
or
neutral
(Doji).
High and low levels are displayable for each timeframe.
Maybe useful for
scalping
and
breakout
systems.
Color setting is variable.
Please, give a little feedback if you find it useful. Thanks!
