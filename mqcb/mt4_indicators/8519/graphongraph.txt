The indicator is intended for displaying the charts of two currency pairs in a single window.
Input parameters:
symbol
- the name of the currency pair that is to be added to the main chart of the other currency pair.
timeFrame
- the TF of the currency piar that is to be added to the main chart of the other currency pair.
price
- the price used for drawing the chart of the currency pair that is to be added. It can take the following values:
Open
,
Close,
Median
