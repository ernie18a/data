Psychology is overrated. You should develop an edge that even your emotion cannot f*** up. Second, price can lie. Volume cannot. With price you are guessing the interaction between market participants. With volume you are visualizing it. The informational advantage you get from order flow is unfair. Third, don't put a profit cap
on your best trading sessions. If your model, for example, is trend following like mine and you have a super trending day, you cannot decide to stop for the day because you had too much profit. Because from statistical analysis of the distribution of my profit, I saw that my profit is concentrated in a small number of really good sessions.
