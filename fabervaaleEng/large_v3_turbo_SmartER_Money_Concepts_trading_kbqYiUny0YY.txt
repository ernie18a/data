from an order flow perspective can be at least three things. First, a failed auction. Aggressive buyers push price outside of the range but are not ready to pay premium prices so there is a dry up in volume and sellers take control of the price. Option two, a stop run.
Stop orders are above a high and they enter the market all at once, creating a so-called book sweeping. Now the path of list resistance is the bid or the buy side of the book, so sellers will have an easy time to push price downwards. The third option is an absorption.
So buyers are aggressive outside of the range, but they are being absorbed by a wall of passive sellers. And as soon as they stop buying, sellers get in control. So one pattern in price action can be three radically different things in order flow. But it doesn't end here. A price action traders will take a fair value gap from a week to the other one.
