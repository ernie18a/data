i-KlPrice_Vol_Direct
indicator with the timeframe selection option available in input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// The indicator chart period (timeframe)
For the indicator to work, indicator
i-KlPrice_Vol_Direct.ex5
must be in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
