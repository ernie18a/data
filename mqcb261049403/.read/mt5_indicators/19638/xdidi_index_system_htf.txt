Two
Didi Needles
indicators from different timeframes on one chart. The indicator of a larger timeframe is displayed as a color trend cloud, which determines trend. The second indicator of a smaller timeframe draws small colored dots, which show trend direction, and a large colored dot when trend reverses.
The indicator requires the compiled SXDidi_Index.ex5 indicator file. Add it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
