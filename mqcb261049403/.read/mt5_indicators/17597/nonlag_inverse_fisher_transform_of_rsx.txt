Inverse fisher transform version of nonlag smoothed RSX.
Since RSX is already a "smoother than RSI" RSI, if you wish to turn the nonlag smoothing off, then simple set the nonlag smoothing period to <=1.
This version is extended (compared to MetaTrader 4 version
NonLag Inverse Fisher Transform of RSX
) with a set of 22 price types and a bit different look and feel (using the MetaTrader 5 possibilities).
