The CronexAC_Signal indicator shows information on the current trend using the values of the
CronexAC
indicator with a fixed timeframe.
The source of a signals is the mutual arrangement of the main and signal lines of the indicator. The indicator displays the color of the cloud of the source CronexAC indicator. Color stars on the line occur when the bar of the corresponding timeframe changes.
The indicator requires CronexAC.mq5 indicator file. Place it to terminal_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
