An indicator that shows buy and sell signals of three indicators (iSAR, iRSI, and iMA) on nine timeframes with full settings.
The indicator uses the class of library
GetFontName.mqh
(to be copied to the terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Include).
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
on June 10, 2008.
