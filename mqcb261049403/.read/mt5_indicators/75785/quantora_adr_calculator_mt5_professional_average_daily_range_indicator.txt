Quantora ADR Calculator MT5 is an indicator that measures the average daily price range based on historical data. It does not open or manage trades and is intended for analysis.
The tool calculates the Average Daily Range over a selected period and compares it with the current day’s movement. It shows how much of the expected range has been used and how much may remain.
It also provides basic daily levels such as high, low, and projected range, along with a simple progress display to track daily volatility.
Suitable for traders who want a quick view of market range and daily movement.
