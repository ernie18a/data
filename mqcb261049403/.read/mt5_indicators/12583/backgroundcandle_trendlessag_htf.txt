The indicator draws candlesticks of a larger timeframe as color filled rectangles using
DRAW_FILLING
buffers. Rectangles are color filled according to the
TrendlessAG
indicator values.
Depending on location of
TrendlessAG
relative to the zero line, the candlestick body is painted in blue or orange, and shadows are painted in light blue or light pink.
This indicator requires the the compiled indicator file TrendlessAG.mq5. Place it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators\.
