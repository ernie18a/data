Completed Bar Trend Regime Dashboard is a lightweight MetaTrader 5 indicator that summarizes market structure from completed candles. It combines EMA structure and slope, ADX/DI strength, higher-timeframe confirmation, ATR volatility, breakout position and spread context in one panel.
The indicator is designed as a decision-support tool. A bullish or bearish regime requires agreement between the signal timeframe and the higher timeframe. If the conditions are not aligned, the panel reports a range or transition instead of forcing a directional label.
How to read the panel
EMA stack
shows the fast/slow relationship and the completed close relative to the filter EMA.
Fast EMA slope
shows whether the short-term average is rising, falling or flat.
ADX and DI
show trend strength and directional pressure.
Higher-timeframe alignment
confirms or rejects the signal-timeframe direction.
ATR and body/ATR
put the completed candle in current volatility context.
Breakout
shows whether the completed close is outside or inside the recent range.
Spread/ATR
compares current spread with recent volatility.
Suggested US500 setup
Start with a US500 H4 chart, H4 signal timeframe and D1 higher-timeframe confirmation. Read the dashboard after the H4 candle closes. The defaults are only a starting point; all timeframes, EMA periods, ADX threshold, ATR period and breakout lookback are adjustable.
Inputs
Signal timeframe / Higher timeframe
control the two analysis periods.
Fast, slow and filter EMA periods
define trend structure.
ADX period / Minimum ADX
define the trend-strength requirement.
ATR period / Minimum body in ATR
define volatility and candle-body context.
Breakout lookback
defines the recent completed-bar range.
Dashboard corner, offsets and font size
control panel placement.
All regime decisions use shift 1, so the current forming candle is excluded. The indicator does not open or manage trades, use DLLs, send network requests or collect account data. It is an analysis tool, not a guarantee of future results.
