Buff Dormeier's Moving Average was presented in Buff Dormeier's article "Buff Up Your Moving Averages".
It has one input parameter:
Period
- calculation period.
Calculations:
Buff = AvgVC / AvgVol
where:
AvgVC = SMA(VC, Period)
AvgVol = SMA(Volume, Period)
VC = Volume*Close
