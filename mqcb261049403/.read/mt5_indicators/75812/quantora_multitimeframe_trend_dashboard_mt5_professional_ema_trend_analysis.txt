Quantora Multi-Timeframe Trend Dashboard MT5 is a multi-timeframe trend analysis indicator for MetaTrader 5.
It analyzes seven timeframes at the same time — from M1 to D1 — and classifies each one as bullish, bearish, or neutral using an EMA-based model. This allows traders to quickly see how different timeframes are aligned without switching between charts.
Each timeframe contributes to an overall trend score, which is summarized into a clear market bias ranging from strong bearish to strong bullish. This helps identify whether the market structure is consistent or mixed across timeframes.
The dashboard also shows basic market data such as bid, ask, and spread, and updates automatically in real time.
EMA periods can be adjusted, making the indicator flexible for different trading styles.
This tool is designed for market analysis only. It does not generate signals or manage trades.
