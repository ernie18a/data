The indicator creates fractals from another larger timeframe on a current chart based on
Fine_Fractals
indicator data. There is a possibility to change the timeframe of calculated fractals in the indicator input parameters.
Place compiled
Fine_Fractals
indicator file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
