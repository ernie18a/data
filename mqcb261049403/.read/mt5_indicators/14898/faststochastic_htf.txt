The
FastStochastic
stochastic oscillator with the noise reduction, and the timeframe selection option available in input parameters.
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
The indicator requires FastStochastic.mq5 indicator file. Place it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
