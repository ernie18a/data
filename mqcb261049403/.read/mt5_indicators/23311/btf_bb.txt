Bigger Time Frame Bollinger Bands displays Bolinger Bands indicator data of a higher timeframe on the current one.
The indicator has nine adjustable parameters:
BB period
- Bollinger Bands calculation period
BB deviation
- Bollinger Bands deviation value
BB applied price
- Bollinger Bands calculation price
BB timeframe
- displayed Bollinger Bands timeframe
Drawing mode
- indicator drawing type
Steps
- as a ladder
Slope
- inclined lines connecting two neighboring bars of the calculation timeframe
Show middle lines
- show the Bollinger Bands middle line (Yes/No)
Show alerts
- whether to enable alerts (Yes/No)
Send mails
- whether to notify by email (Yes/No)
Send push-notifications
- whether to send push notifications (Yes/No)
Signals are generated when the current Close price moves beyond the upper and lower Bollinger Bands
