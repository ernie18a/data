INTRODUCTION :
Have you notice that the market really moves during a specific time of the day ?  Let's not forget that when the market really moves we can actually make some decent profit. So if you would like to know the best time to trade the market, this is the script that will identify these times for you.
For day traders, this is one of the important thing to consider in order to improve our strategy by calculating on average how much an asset moves, in which it will allow us to identify the best TIME during the day to trade when the market moves the most as well
