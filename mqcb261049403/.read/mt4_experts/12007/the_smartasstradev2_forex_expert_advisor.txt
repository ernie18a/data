The SmartAssTrade-V2 Forex Expert Advisor (EA) is a new update for
SmartAssTrade EA Version 1
(old version) in Code Base MQL4.
SmartAssTrade-V2 EA Only working on M30 Timeframes or PERIOD_M30.
If used on other timeframes may be lost.
