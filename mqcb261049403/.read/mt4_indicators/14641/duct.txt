The indicator plots a channel based on fractals.
Description of the indicator settings:
Time_Frame — period of the chart for finding the fractals and the points of control of the channel. At = 0 means the current chart, on which the MQL4 program is running.
Needed_Bars
— The number of bars in history for the indicator calculation, at = 0 all bars are calculated.
Clamping_Bars
— limit of the number of bars for the search of fractals, at = 0 calculated automatically.
Koeff_Flat
— The coefficient to determine the flat.
collir
—
the color of the indicator lines.
