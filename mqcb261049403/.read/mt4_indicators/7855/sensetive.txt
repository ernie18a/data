The SeNSetiVe indicator.
The indicator displayed the market movement direction when the zero line is crossed. When the crossing is from below - buy, when it is from above - sell.
Variables
:
SPerioD
- Period (100-200), at lower values (<100) the indicator line will generate a lot of false signals. The optimum value for the hourly charts is 160-180. For smaller - 100-160.
SFactoR
- (from 1 to 10) - sensitivity of the market line to rapid (sometimes false) turns of the trend line, optimal value (5-7)
ShiFt
- shift relative to the zero line.
