Introduction:
Calculation of pivot and similar levels based on time zones. If you want to modify the colors, please scroll down to line 200 and below (where it says "Calculate Levels") and change the colors. Valid color names can be obtained by placing the cursor on a color name (e.g. somewhere in the word "Orange" and pressing F1).
Time-Zone Inputs:
LocalTimeZone
: TimeZone for which MT4 shows your local time, e.g. 1 or 2 for Europe (GMT+1 or GMT+2 daylight savings time). Use zero for no adjustment. The MetaQuotes demo server uses GMT +2.
DestTimeZone
: TimeZone for the session from which to calculate the levels (e.g. 1 or 2 for the European session (without or with daylight savings time). Use zero for GMT
Example
: If your MetaTrader server is located in the EST (Eastern Standard Time, GMT-5) zone and you want to calculate the levels for the London trading session (European time in summer GMT+1), then enter -5 for LocalTimeZone, 1 for Dest TimeZone.
Please understand that the LocalTimeZone setting depends on the time on your MetaTrader charts (for example, the demo server from MetaQuotes always located in CDT (+2) or CET (+1)), no matter what the clock on your wall says.
If in doubt, leave everything to zero.
