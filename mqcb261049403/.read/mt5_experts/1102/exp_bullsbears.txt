The Expert Advisor Exp_BullsBears based on the signals taken from the
BullsBears
semaphore signal trend indicator. A signal to perform a deal is formed at bar closing when the indicator reverses and its color changes.
Place BullsBears.ex5 compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
