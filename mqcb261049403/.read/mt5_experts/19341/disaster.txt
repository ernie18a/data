The trading strategy is based on the observation, which shows that if the price crosses the MA chart and moves a certain distance, the price is likely to continue its movement in the same direction.
The EA uses pending Buy Stop and Sell Stop orders. Pending orders are modified to follow the current price at the specified
Distance from MA for setting a pending order
. An action is performed every
Timeout
seconds. The EA only trails pending orders. Once an order triggers and turns into a position, the position will no longer be modified.
Input Parameters
Stop Loss
Take Profit
Trailing Step
Distance from MA for setting a pending order
- minimum distance between the current price and the MA(590) indicator
Timeout
magic number
- unique Expert Advisor number.
