is a free, fully open EA for the German 40 index on H1. It buys in the evening session after a recent narrow-range bar, when volatility is calm and volume is normal, and closes everything by 23:00 server time. Four conditions, long only, one trade at a time, a stop loss on every trade.

The strategy in more detail
A signal needs all four conditions on the bar that just closed:
Volatility is not spiking. The last 10 hourly returns are less than 1.6 times as volatile as the last 100.
Volume is at least normal. The bar’s tick volume is not below its 50-bar average.
A quiet regime. The average tick volume of the last 252 bars is below a set level.
A recent coil. One of the last 13 bars was an NR7 bar, the narrowest of its last 7.
These came out of my automated research. I don’t claim a theory for why they work, so the code is here for you to test and challenge.
Trade management:
it buys at the open of the next bar with a stop at 3.5 ATR and a target at 4.5 ATR. Everything is closed at 23:00 server time, or one minute before your broker closes GER40 if that’s earlier.
When it trades:
Monday to Friday, in the evening session after the Frankfurt cash close while Wall Street is open. The times assume the common MetaTrader server time of GMT+2 in winter and GMT+3 in summer:
