The EA uses iSAR (Parabolic SAR) indicator signals on the
Bar current
bar from the
Work timeframe
. The EA opens only one position at a time, therefore it is able to work both on hedging and netting accounts.
An open signal is also a signal to close an opposite position. If an opposite position is present, it is closed, and a new position is opened in a signal direction.
