The simplest Expert Advisor based on WPR
. Sells at downward crossing of an oscillator overbought level, buys at an upward crossing of an oscillator oversold level. The entry signal is formed when a bar is closing if the level is crossed.
Note that the TradeAlgorithms.mqh library file allows using Expert Advisors with brokers who offer
nonzero spread
and the option of setting Stop Loss and Take Profit
together with position opening
. You can download more variants of the library at the following link:
Trade Algorithms
.
