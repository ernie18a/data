An educational example of a mean-reversion Expert Advisor based on Bollinger Bands. It shows how to combine two indicators, limit trading to selected hours and manage exits based on the current indicator value.
Strategy
BUY: bar 2 closed below the lower band and bar 1 closed back inside the bands (optionally: RSI on bar 2 below the lower level). SELL – the other way round (upper band, RSI above the upper level).
Exit: when the price touches the current middle band (BUY: Bid ≥ middle, SELL: Ask ≤ middle) or at Stop Loss.
Trading hours limit only opening new positions (overnight ranges such as 22:00–06:00 are allowed); closing always works. Optionally, positions can be closed outside trading hours.
Maximum number of open positions at once – a parameter (1 by default).
What the code demonstrates
reading two indicators through handles and
CopyBuffer
with a check of the number of copied values,
an optional filter that can be switched on and off,
time windows in server time, including ranges that cross midnight,
free margin, lot and stop level checks, spread filter, order retries.
Inputs
TimeFrame – timeframe of the signal (current chart by default)
Bollinger Bands period / deviation / applied price – 20 / 2.0 / Close
Use RSI filter – off by default; RSI period 14, lower level 30 (BUY below), upper level 70 (SELL above)
Trading start / end (HH:MM, server time) – 00:00 / 00:00 (start = end → all day)
Close position outside trading hours – off
Lot (fixed) – 0.10; Stop Loss – in points, 0 = none (300)
Max open positions at once – 1
Magic number – 5020; Max slippage – 10 points; Max spread – 30 points (0 = off)
Important
This is an educational example, not a trading system. It works only on demo accounts and in the Strategy Tester – on a live account it shows a message and stops.
