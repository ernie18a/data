The Exp_3rdGenerationXMA Expert Advisor is based on the change in the
3rdGenXMA
moving average direction. A signal for a transaction forms when a bar is closing, provided that the MA direction has changed.
Proper operation of the generated Expert Advisor requires the compiled indicator file 3rdGenXMA.ex5 to be placed in terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Indicators.
