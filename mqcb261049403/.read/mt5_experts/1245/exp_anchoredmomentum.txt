Trading system using the
AnchoredMomentum
indicator. A signal to perform a deal is formed at bar closing when there is breakthrough of high or low level indicator. Exit from the deal is performed either by a pending order, or by changing color of the indicator from pink to green or vice versa.
Place
AnchoredMomentum.ex5
compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
