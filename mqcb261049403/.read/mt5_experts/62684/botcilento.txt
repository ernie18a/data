Smart Grid Management

Supports Martingale or Arithmetic lot sizing

Risk Safeguards
Auto-stop at daily loss limits
Drawdown-based emergency closure
Position age expiration
Spread/slippage filters
Unique Features
Optional "hold through reversals" mode
Stagnation detection (no movement)
Real-time dashboard with profit metrics
