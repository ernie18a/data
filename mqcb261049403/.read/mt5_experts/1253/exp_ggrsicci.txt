Trading system using the
GG-RSI-CCI
signal indicator. A signal to perform a deal is formed at bar closing when the bar changes color from red to green or blue. Exit from the deals is performed either by a pending order, or by bar color changing.
Place
GG-RSI-CCI.ex5
compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
