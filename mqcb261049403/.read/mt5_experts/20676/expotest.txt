The EA uses the indications of SAR for making trading decisions. If the Ask price is higher than the indicator values, we assume buying; if it is lower, then selling.
Settings
TimeFrames
- selecting the chart timeframe for signals from the indicator;
SL
- stop loss level;
TP
- take profit level;
Volume
- volume of orders; if it is 0, the volume will be defined automatically according to the Risk setting;
Risk
- risk setting in percents; it runs at Volume = 0;
MAGIC
- identifier of the EA's orders;
Slippage
- the maximum allowed slippage;
step
- price changing step - acceleration factor, for indicator SAR;
maximum
- maximum step, for indicator SAR.
