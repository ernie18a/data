The algorithm of trading strategy:
1. Open a H1 chart of EURUSD, lots 0,1.
2. Draw Parabolic SAR and three Exponential Moving Averages with periods 10, 25, 50.
A Buy signal:
ЕMA with the period of 10 crosses ЕМА 25 and 50 from bottom upwards, while Parabolic SAR is below the price
.
A Sell signal:
ЕMA with the period of 10 crosses ЕМА 25 and 50 from top downwards, while Parabolic SAR is above the price
.
Exit:
1. By Stop Loss
2. By
Take Profit
3. When the ЕМА 10 line turns the opposite direction (if we have a
Buy
position, close where the ЕМА10 top was formed, for
Sell
- where bottom is formed
), with a minimum profit. The values of Stop Loss and Take Profit for buy and sell trades will be different.
