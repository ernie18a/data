An Expert Advisor based on cluster indicator
CCFp (Complex Common Frames percent)
Stop Loss, Take Profit, and Trailing levels in true points. For instance, parameter 500 means 0.00500 for a five-digit symbol and 0.0500 for a four-digit one.
Inputs
Lots
- volume of the position to be opened
Stop Loss
- stop loss
Take Profit
- take profit
Trailing Stop
- trailing
Trailing Step
- trailing step
Step
- step between the indicator values
Close opposite positions
- closing opposite positions
magic number
- unique identifier for the EA.
Default parameters for EURUSD,H1: In one case, parameter "Close opposite positions" is equal to true; in the other case, it is equal to false
