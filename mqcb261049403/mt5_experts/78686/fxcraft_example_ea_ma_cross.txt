An educational example of a simple trend-following Expert Advisor. It shows how to structure a trading robot in MQL5: signal on closed bars, order execution with checks and retries, and safe handling of a terminal restart.
Strategy
A BUY signal appears when the fast MA crosses above the slow MA on closed bars (fast ≤ slow on bar 2 and fast > slow on bar 1); SELL – the other way round. The signal is checked once, at the opening of a new bar.
After start the EA does not open a trade immediately – it waits for the first new crossover.
One position at a time. An opposite signal closes the position and opens the opposite one.
If opening fails (spread, requote), the EA retries on the next ticks, but only until the end of the signal bar.
What the code demonstrates
free margin check ( OrderCalcMargin ) before every order,
lot normalized to the symbol's minimum, maximum and step,
SL/TP widened to the broker's minimum distance ( SYMBOL_TRADE_STOPS_LEVEL ),
spread filter, retries on requotes and price changes, indicator handles released in
OnDeinit ,
messages in English or Polish, depending on the terminal language.
Inputs
TimeFrame – timeframe of the signal (current chart by default)
Fast MA period / Slow MA period – 10 / 30 (slow must be greater than fast)
MA method / Applied price – EMA / Close
Lot (fixed) – 0.10
Stop Loss / Take Profit – in points, 0 = none (300 / 600)
Magic number – 5010
Max slippage – in points (10)
Max spread – in points, 0 = off (30)
Important
This is an educational example, not a trading system. It works only on demo accounts and in the Strategy Tester – on a live account it shows a message and stops.
