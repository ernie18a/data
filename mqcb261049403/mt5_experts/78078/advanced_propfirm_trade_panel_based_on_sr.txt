What it is
This is a trade-management panel, not a signal robot. On a live or demo account it never opens a trade by itself. You decide when to trade, and the panel does the work around the trade:
It finds support and resistance levels and suggests stop losses and targets.
It sizes the lot from your risk percentage.
It checks every order against your daily and maximum drawdown limits and against leverage and margin rules.
It draws everything as draggable lines on the chart.
It is built for traders on funded or challenge accounts, where one oversized trade can end the account. It also works on ordinary accounts.
This is custom-built according to my liking. I am of the opinion that it is just as good if not better than the expensive trade panels on the market!

Key features
1. Prop-firm drawdown guard
Daily drawdown and maximum drawdown limits in %, plus a safety buffer.
The starting balance is detected automatically or can be entered by hand.
The remaining allowance is shown live, with colour bars.
When a limit is reached, the EA closes positions and pending orders and locks trading until the next day, or permanently for the maximum drawdown.
The day boundary can follow 17:00 New York time, with daylight saving handled automatically, or the broker's server midnight.
Optional profit target and optional maximum daily profit, each with its own lock.
2. Hidden Support/Resistance finder
Levels are calculated from price pivots and are not drawn on the chart. They feed the suggestions and the grid planner only.

3. Live trading tab
Buy and Sell buttons, with SL and TP lines you drag by their handles (small coloured tabs at the right edge of each line).
One-click suggestions for the three nearest targets and stops.
Lot size from your risk % of the starting balance, or a fixed lot.
The spread is added to the stop-loss distance, so a stop is never placed inside the spread.
Live display of risk, reward, risk-to-reward and risk as a percentage.
4. Pending order tab
Three S/R-based entry suggestions with SL and TP.
Choose limit orders only, stop orders only, or both.
Draggable entry, SL and TP lines.
An identical pending order is never placed twice.
5. Grid tab
Plans a grid of 2 to 8 orders along S/R levels, with a master stop beyond the outer zone.
Optional lot increase per level, set in the settings.
Automatic or manual first take profit, and a take profit for the whole grid in points or currency.
Optional breakeven.
Every pending grid order carries a take profit at the open price of the first order, so profit and stop are held by the broker server even if the EA or VPS goes offline. This can be switched off.
The preview and levels refresh every minute, or when you press the Re-suggest button.
When a grid is open in one direction, a note says so and the preview for another grid in that direction is hidden. The opposite direction stays available, and a toggle enables a second grid in the same direction.
Extend Grid
moves the stop to the next strong S/R zone and adds orders up to the remaining daily allowance, which includes the day's profit. It needs two clicks, so you see the plan on the chart before confirming.
6. Hedge-aware risk model
Risk is judged on the whole account, not trade by trade.
Open opposite positions offset each other. A new order that reduces the worst-case loss is never blocked.
Pending orders count only their possible loss and never earn hedge credit, because they may never fill.
Each order is checked against the remaining daily allowance, and the panel shows the account risk before and after the order.

9. Interface
Dark and light themes. The chart colours follow the theme: colour on near-black in dark mode, colour on white in light mode. Your trade-level and trade-history settings are left as they are.

Strategy Tester
On a live or demo account the panel never trades automatically. In the Strategy Tester,
automatic trading is on by default
so the EA can be validated and back-tested. It opens a trade when a bar touches a strong S/R level and closes back on the right side of it. It uses the same risk, hedge and margin checks as a manual order. Turn InpTestAutoTrade off to use the panel by hand in visual mode. A non-visual test skips the panel for speed.

How to use it
Attach the EA to a chart and allow algorithmic trading. Adjust the settings as per your prop firm (Defaults are for FXIFY).
On the MAIN tab, check your daily and maximum drawdown and the starting balance. Choose Aggressive or Conservative levels.
On LIVE, GRID or PEND, pick Buy or Sell and drag the lines by their handles if you want to adjust them.
Read the risk, margin and account-risk rows, then place the order.
Important notes
S/R levels are statistical suggestions, not predictions.
The hedge model assumes price moves in one direction between now and the stops. A whipsaw that hits stops on both sides can lose more than the model shows.
Margin figures are estimates built from your settings and the broker's data. Prop firms and brokers have different rules and can change them, so set the values to your own firm's current terms.
Grids, and especially grids with increasing lots, can produce large losses.
