BUY position opening rule:
Tenkan-sen on bar #1 < Kijun-sen on bar #0
AND
Tenkan-sen on bar #0 >= Kijun-sen on bar #0
AND
Close of bar #0 > Senkou Span B on bar #0
The following rule is applied for opening SELL positions (the difference from the BUY rule is
marked with color
):
Tenkan-sen on bar #1
>
Kijun-sen on bar #0
AND
Tenkan-sen on bar #0
<
= Kijun-sen on bar #0
AND
Close of bar #0
<
Senkou Span B on bar #0.
Test on EURUSD,D1:
