News Lines – Economic Calendar Lines & Event Panel
Version 1.30 · Expert Advisor (utility, does not trade) · MetaTrader 5
1. Overview
News Lines
puts the economic calendar where you actually look: on the price chart. Every scheduled release appears as a vertical line at its exact time, coloured by importance. Hover over a line to see the event names, forecast, previous and actual values. A compact panel lists what is coming in the next hours and what was released in the past hours, and can be moved, resized, collapsed and scrolled.
The product is a
utility
. It does
not
open, modify or close any trades and does not require trading permissions.

2. How it works
Step
What happens
1
The EA loads events from the selected source (MT5 calendar or Forex Factory feed).
2
Events are filtered by impact level and by the active group (currencies + keywords).
3
Lines are created for the visible chart range plus one screen-width on each side, so scrolling stays filled. The range is recalculated shortly after you scroll or zoom.
4
The panel shows events within the configured window around the current server time.
5
The data is refreshed automatically (default every 15 minutes) so new forecast and actual values appear.
If more than the maximum number of lines would be drawn (default 1000), the lowest-impact lines are dropped first.

9. Frequently asked questions
Question
Answer
Does it trade?
No. It only displays information.
Why can't I see lines to the right of the last bar?
Enable
Chart Shift
or scroll the chart to open empty space on the right. On higher timeframes the empty area covers more days.
The lines show no tooltip.
Set
Lines selectable
to true.
The footer says the Forex Factory download failed.
Add the feed address to
Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → Allow WebRequest for listed URL
. The footer text shows the exact error.
The MT5 calendar shows no data.
The terminal must be connected to the broker. Some brokers provide limited calendar data.
Labels are hidden behind candles.
Labels are drawn in the chart background so that the panel is always above them.
Can I use it together with a trading EA?
Only one EA can run per chart. Attach News Lines to a second chart of the same symbol.
Times look different from my local time.
Chart lines use broker server time. The panel can show local or server time (input
Panel time zone
).
How do I reset the panel position?
Set
Ignore remembered state/position
to true.
