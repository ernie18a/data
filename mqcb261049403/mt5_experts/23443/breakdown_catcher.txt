The EA works with pending Buy Stop and Sell Stop orders.
Buy Stop is placed at the High price of bar #1 + "
Indent
", Sell Stop is placed at the Low price of bar #1 - "
Indent
".
Program Parameters
Stop Loss
, in pips (1.00045-1.00055=1 pips) - Stop Loss
Take Profit
, in pips (1.00045-1.00055=1 pips) - Take Profit
Trailing Stop
(min distance from price to Stop Loss, in pips - Trailing
Trailing Step
, in pips (1.00045-1.00055=1 pips) - Trailing step
Indent
, in pips (1.00045-1.00055=1 pips) - Indent from the High and Low of bar #1
Allowable spread
, in points (1.00045-1.00055=10 points) - the largest spread permitted
Money management
: Lot OR Risk - volume calculation type (fixed lot size or risk % per trade)
The value for "Money management"
- volume or percentage
Print log
magic number
