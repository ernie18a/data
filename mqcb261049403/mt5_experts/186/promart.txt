An Expert Advisor opens position using two
MACD
indicators. It reverts the position in the case of loss. It uses a martingale with the limited number of doublings. The doublings can be disabled.
Input parameters:
DML- amount of money, used for 1 lot;
Ud - number of doublings. It starts from 0 - no doublings;
Stop - stop loss in points;
Tp - take profit in points;
Slipage - slippage in points;
MACD1Fast - Fast
ЕМА
of MACD, used for the entry;
MACD1Slow - Slow ЕМА;
MACD2Fast - Fast ЕМА of MACD, used to determine the trend;
MACD2Slow - Slow ЕМА.
