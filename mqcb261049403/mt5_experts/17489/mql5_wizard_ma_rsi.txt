The
"MQL5 Wizard MA RSI"
Expert Advisor has been generated using the
MQL5 Wizard
based on the signals of the
trend MA (Moving Average) indicator
and of the
RSI (Relative Strength Index) oscillator
.
The Expert Advisor only works at
moment of new bar emergence

false
