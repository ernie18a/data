The MQL5 Wizard MACD Parabolic SAR Expert Advisor has been created using the
MQL5 Wizard
, on the basis of
MACD
(Moving Average Convergence/Divergence) signals and the
Parabolic SAR
trend indicator.
The Expert Advisor only works at the
moment of new bar emergence
:

ulong

756
;
//
bool
Expert_EveryTick        =
false
;
