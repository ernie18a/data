Overview:
BreakRevertPro combines statistical analysis with robust validation features, implementing both breakout and mean reversion strategies with built-in testing safeguards.
Key Features:
Statistical trade identification using Weibull, Poisson, and Exponential distributions
Validation-optimized design with automatic safety trade mechanism
Smart position sizing with special handling for precious metals
Multi-timeframe analysis (M1, M15, H1) for comprehensive market assessment
Dynamic stop loss and take profit validation
Automatic detection of validation environments
Technical Highlights:
Integrated validator class ensuring broker compliance
Conservative risk management with multiple margin safety checks
Adaptive execution based on market conditions
Persistent data storage for continuous strategy refinement
Designed for reliable performance in both live trading and validation testing environments.
