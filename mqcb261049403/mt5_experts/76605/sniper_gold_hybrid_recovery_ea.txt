The EA uses RSI filtering, ATR based recovery spacing, market structure checks, basket level profit management, crash pause protection, and optional news filtering. It is designed to manage recovery entries with configurable risk controls and dashboard monitoring.
Main features:
Fixed lot or balance based compounding lot calculation
ATR based adaptive recovery distance
RSI entry filter with normal, high ATR, and extreme ATR modes
Structure checks using LL/LH and support conditions
Basket profit target and recovery basket trailing
Smart trimming option for basket management
Spread, slippage, equity loss, and crash movement protections
News filter using MQL5 calendar, CSV file, or both
On chart dashboard with current recovery and status information
Fully configurable inputs including magic number, lot settings, ATR/RSI settings, recovery distance, target profit, trailing, news filter, and dashboard position
Recommended chart:
XAUUSD, M15
