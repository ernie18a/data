It trades on the market volatility.
Inputs
Lots
- position volume;
Multiplier relative to current volatility
- a multiplier relative the current volatility;
Period for the calculation of volatility in bars
- a period in bars for calculating the volatility;
Volatility smoothing Mode
- averaging type: 0 - simple moving average, 1 - linear-weighted;
Volatility calculation Mode
- the type of calculating the volatility: 1-open/close, 0-High/Low;
Stop loss as a percentage of volatility
- Stop Loss in percents of volatility (from 0 to 1);
magic number
- unique identifier for the EA.
Testing for all symbols on H1 in the "Every tick" mode from 2017.01.01 to 2018.05.08.
