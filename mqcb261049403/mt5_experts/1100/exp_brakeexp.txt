The Expert Advisor Exp_BrakeExp based on the signals taken from the
BrakeExp
semaphore, signal, trend indicator. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if there is reversal of indicator by changing color and occurrence of a big color dot.
Place BrakeExp.ex5 compiled file to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
