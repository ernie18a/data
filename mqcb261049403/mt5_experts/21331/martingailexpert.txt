A simple Martingale. If no positions are opened, the entry is by indicator iStochastic (Stochastic Oscillator).
Inputs
Lots
- initial lot size to open a position
Multiplicator
- lot size increasing factor
Step
(in pips) - step
Profit factor
(in pips) - profit level
Stochastic: K-period (number of bars for calculations)
Stochastic: D-period (period of first smoothing)
Stochastic: final smoothing
Stochastic: type of smoothing
Stochastic: calculation method
Stochastic: BUY level
- indicator level to open a BUY position
Stochastic: SELL level
- indicator level to open a SELL position
magic number
- a unique identifier of your positions
EURUSD,M30
