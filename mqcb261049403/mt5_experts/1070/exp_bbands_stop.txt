The Exp_BBands_Stop Expert Advisor is based on the signals obtained from the
BBands_Stop_v1
signal indicator. A signal for a transaction forms when a bar is closing, if the indicator changes its position and color.
Proper operation of the generated Expert Advisor requires the compiled indicator file BBands_Stop_v1.ex5 to be placed in terminal_data_directory\MQL5\Indicators.
