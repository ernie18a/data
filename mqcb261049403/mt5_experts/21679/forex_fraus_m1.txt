How it works
The EA only operates (opens new positions) when a new bar emerges.
The volume of the opened position is equal to
Lots
.
Stop Loss
,
Take Profit
and
Trailing Stop
can be disabled by setting the appropriate parameter to "0.0".
The EA checks the value of
iWPR
(Williams' Percent Range, %R) at the #0 bar. The EA sells in the overbought zone and buys in the oversold zone. If the time period control is enabled (
Use Timecontrol
== true), the time interval is configured from
Start hour
to
End hour.
When a signal emerges, opposite positions can be closed: this can be done by setting
Use close opposite positions
= true.
