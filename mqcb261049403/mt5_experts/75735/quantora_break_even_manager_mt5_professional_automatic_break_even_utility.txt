Quantora Break Even Manager MT5 is a utility that automatically protects open trades by moving stop loss to the break even level once a defined profit is reached. It does not open trades or generate signals.
The tool monitors positions continuously and applies break even based on your settings. You can define the trigger level and optionally set an offset to lock in a small profit. It supports managing manual trades, all positions, or trades filtered by magic number, with separate control for buy and sell orders.
It also includes symbol filtering and a simple dashboard to track account and trade status. A one-click option allows you to apply break even instantly to eligible trades.
Suitable for traders who want a simple and reliable way to secure profits automatically.
