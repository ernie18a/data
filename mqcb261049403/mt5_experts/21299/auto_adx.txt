The EA uses indicator iADX (Average Directional Movement Index, ADX), places Stop Loss and Take Profit, and can enable trailing of your positions. Two main parameters are used: "
ADX Smoothing Period
" and "
ADX Level
".
The resting three parameters:
Reverse
- if it is "true", the EA closes a position opposite to the signal.
Lots
- defining the lot size manually, the constant lot size
Risk
- lot size is calculated dynamically, in risk percents per trade
Upon optimizing for EURUSD,H1 (Attention! The code attached to the CodeBase contains NON-OPTIMIZED parameters!!!)
