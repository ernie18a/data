WaveMaster Pro EA
is the natural extension of the WaveMaster Oscilloscope — a clean, professional bridge that turns visual market intelligence into disciplined, automated execution.
Every signal computed by the oscilloscope (PROBE direction, ANOMALY energy, RSI rhythm, MOMENTUM velocity, machine learning probability, and the combined Signal Score) is exported into structured buffers. WaveMaster Pro EA reads those buffers tick-by-tick and translates them into precise trading decisions — no guessing, no manual clicking, no missed moves.
It is designed the way a professional execution engine should be:
transparent, disciplined, and fully under your control.
What Makes WaveMaster Pro EA Different
Plug-and-Trade Architecture
— auto-detects the oscilloscope indicator on first launch and attaches it visibly to your chart, no manual setup needed.
Signal-Aware, Not Signal-Spam
— rising-edge detection ensures one clean trade per signal event, not repeated entries on the same move.
Adaptive Risk Engine
— fixed lot or percentage-based risk, calculated from the actual stop-loss distance, not a rough approximation.
Dynamic SL / TP
— choose between fixed-point distances or ATR-based adaptive levels that breathe with volatility.
Break-Even Automation
— protects profit the moment a trade reaches your threshold, with configurable buffer.
Intelligent Trailing Stop
— follows price only in your favor, never retreats, always respects the break-even floor.
Full Session & Spread Filters
— trade only when the market is right: your hours, your spread limits.
Daily Guardrails
— maximum daily trades and maximum daily drawdown auto-pause trading before you blow your rules.
Real-Time Control Panel
— a draggable, one-click panel with AutoTrade toggle and Close-All button directly on your chart.
Backtest-Ready
— fully compatible with MT5 Strategy Tester for pre-deployment validation and parameter tuning.
Multi-Broker Symbol Compatible
— works on any broker, any symbol suffix, with no manual path configuration.
100% Source Transparency
— the EA logic is clean, readable, and open — no hidden modules, no obfuscated black boxes.
