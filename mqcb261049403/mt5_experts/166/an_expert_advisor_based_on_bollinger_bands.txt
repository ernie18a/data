This Expert Advisor is based on Bollinger Bands. It uses trend-following strategy (DEMA) and
Bollinger Bands ®
indicator.
It shows a stable profit in Strategy Tester during last 17 years (EURUSD M30).
The Strategy:
Open long position:
DEМА is growing up and white (bull) candle crossed the Lower Bollinger Band from below to above.
Open short position:
DEМА is falling down and black (bear) candle crossed the Upper Bollinger Band from above to below.
Close long position:
Black candle crossed the Upper Bollinger Band from above to below.
Close short position:
White (bull) candle crossed the Lower Bollinger Band from below to above.
