The Expert Advisor searches for N identical candlesticks in a row. It buys on bullish candlesticks and sells on bearish ones. The EA checks whether the account is netting or hedging. The
CPositionInfo
,
CTrade
,
CSymbolInfo
, and
CAccountInfo
trade classes are used in this code.
