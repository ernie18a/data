The EA checks entry opportunities only when a new bar appears, while trailing works on each tick. When checking a signal, there is another verification apart from comparing the values of the main and signal line on the current and previous bar: the main line value is converted into points and compared to the
MACD open level
parameter. When a BUY open signal arrives, close SELL positions, and vice versa.
