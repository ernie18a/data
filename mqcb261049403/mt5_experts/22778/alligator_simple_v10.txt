The EA based on iAlligator (Alligator) indicator. It can work both on hedging and netting accounts, since it always holds only one position in the market at a time. If
Every tick
is
true
, the EA will work at each tick. If
false
(default), then the possibility of opening a position will only be checked as soon as a new bar appears.
Market entry condition
BUY:  Lips#1 > Teeth#1 && Teeth#1 > Jaws#1
SELL:  Lips#1 < Teeth#1 && Teeth#1 < Jaws#1
