The Exp_LaguerreFilter Expert Advisor with the entry at the intersection of the fast and the slow lines of the
LaguerreFilter
indicator. The signal is formed when a bar is closing if the oscillator has crossed its signal line.
This Expert Advisor requires the compiled indicator file LaguerreFilter.ex5 in order to run. Place it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
Default Expert Advisor's input parameters have been used during the tests shown below. Stop Loss and Take Profit have not been used during the tests.
