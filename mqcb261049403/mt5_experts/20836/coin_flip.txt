An EA without using any custom indicators; positions are opened pseudorandomly. The EA can show the current statistics in two languages:
Trailing modifies the Stop Loss of open positions. In case of loss, the lots increase using Martingale. How to monitor losses: In
OnTradeTransaction
, we catch transaction
DEAL_REASON_SL
- the sign of the Stop Loss triggered:
