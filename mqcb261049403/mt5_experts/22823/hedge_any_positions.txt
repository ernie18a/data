The EA works on hedge accounts only!
The EA tracks all positions regardless of the symbol or magic number.
As soon as a position (opened by another EA or manually) features a loss exceeding or equal to
Losing
, an opposite position is opened. The volume of the opened position is calculated considering
Lot coefficient
.
For each position (opened by another EA or manually), the "Hedge any positions" EA opens an opposite position ONLY ONCE.
Please note that the EA simply sets the opposite position once without Stop Loss and Take Profit.
