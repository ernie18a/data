An EA based on indicators iMA (Moving Average, MA) and iStdDev (Standard Deviation, StdDev)
The EA has an interesting parameter to manage the number of positions:
Number of positions in one direction
- the highest number of positions currently opened in one direction.
Moreover, the options are available: Reverse of trading signal (
Reverse
) and forcedly closing opposite positions (
Close opposite
) at receiving a trading signal.
In the picture above, the optimization results are shown for the forward period at EURUSD, M5.
