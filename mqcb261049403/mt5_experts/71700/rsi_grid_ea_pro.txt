This Expert Advisor is designed to assist traders by combining
RSI-based signal logic
with a
grid trading system
to manage positions dynamically.
The EA opens trades based on RSI levels and continues building positions using a grid approach when the market moves against the initial trade. To help reduce drawdown, it includes an
overlap function
, which intelligently closes combinations of profitable and losing trades when specific conditions are met.
Features:
Adjustable lot sizing (fixed, balance-based, or risk-based)
Grid step and multiplier control
Trailing stop and breakeven management
Manual trading panel (Buy/Sell buttons)
Time filter for controlled trading sessions
This EA is highly customizable, allowing traders to adjust risk according to their strategy. However, due to the nature of grid trading, proper risk management is essential.
Important Notes:
Works only on
Hedging accounts
Not compatible with
Netting accounts
Recommended to test on a
demo account first
Grid strategies can carry
high risk in trending markets
