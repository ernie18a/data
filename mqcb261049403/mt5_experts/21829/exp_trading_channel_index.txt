A trading system based on the
Trading_Channel_Index
oscillator signals. A trading signal is formed at the bar close time, if the indicator enters the oversold or overbought area (which is displayed by a change in the indicator color).
For the generated EA to operate correctly, the compiled
Trading_Channel_Index.ex5
indicator file must be available under the
<terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators
folder.
