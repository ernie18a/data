Long: short position AND current price − entry price ≥ hedge distance
Short: long position AND entry price − current price ≥ hedge distance
Calculation: total open-position profit = sum(position profit + swap across open positions)
Exit: long, total open-position profit ≥ profit threshold OR total open-position profit ≤ loss threshold OR open position count ≥ position threshold
Exit: short, total open-position profit ≥ profit threshold OR total open-position profit ≤ loss threshold OR open position count ≥ position threshold
