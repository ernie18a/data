Calculation: quality-adjusted move(minimum move, quality) = minimum move × (1 − clamp(quality, quality lower bound, quality upper bound) / quality scale)
Long: At the first tick of each new chart bar, no open position AND for every i from 0 through long past-candle count − 1, low[i] ≥ close[0] AND high[i] − high[long past-candle count] < quality-adjusted move(long minimum move in price units, long trend quality) AND close[0] ≤ high[long past-candle count] − long minimum move in price units AND RSI(close, long RSI lookback) on the last completed chart bar < long RSI threshold
Short: At the first tick of each new chart bar, no open position AND for every i from 0 through short past-candle count − 1, high[i] ≤ close[0] AND low[short past-candle count] − low[i] < quality-adjusted move(short minimum move in price units, short trend quality) AND close[0] ≥ low[short past-candle count] + short minimum move in price units AND RSI(close, short RSI lookback) on the last completed chart bar > short RSI threshold
