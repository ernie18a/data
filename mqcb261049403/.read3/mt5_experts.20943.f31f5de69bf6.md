Calculation: TrendColor(lookback, risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T, n) = on bars of T, carry the trend from the oldest bar (initially neutral); for each bar set it bearish if close[n] < L+(H-L)*(risk-base-risk)/risk-scale, then bullish if close[n] > H-(H-L)*(risk-base-risk)/risk-scale, where H=max(high[n..n+lookback-1]) and L=min(close[n], low[n..n+lookback-1]); output 0 for bullish trend with open[n]<=close[n], 1 for bullish trend with open[n]>close[n], 3 for bearish trend with open[n]<close[n], or 4 for bearish trend with open[n]>=close[n]; n=0 is forming and n>0 is completed. The EA samples T1/T2 values on chart ticks when the corresponding T bar opens for nonzero offsets, or every chart tick at offset zero; T3 is the chart timeframe for alert rules.
Long: long entry enabled AND TrendColor(long lookback, long risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T1, long signal offset)<2 AND TrendColor(long lookback, long risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T1, long signal offset+1)>1
Short: short entry enabled AND TrendColor(short lookback, short risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T2, short signal offset)>2 AND TrendColor(short lookback, short risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T2, short signal offset+1)>0
Exit: long, long exit enabled AND TrendColor(long lookback, long risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T1, long signal offset)>2
Exit: short, short exit enabled AND TrendColor(short lookback, short risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T2, short signal offset)<2
Long: TrendColor(indicator lookback, indicator risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T3, indicator signal offset+1)>1 AND TrendColor(indicator lookback, indicator risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T3, indicator signal offset)<2
Short: TrendColor(indicator lookback, indicator risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T3, indicator signal offset+1)<3 AND TrendColor(indicator lookback, indicator risk, risk-base, risk-scale, T3, indicator signal offset)>2
