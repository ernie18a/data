Calculation: Fractal Force Index on T1 = selected volume × (MA(selected price, adaptive lookback, MA method) − prior bar’s MA using that bar’s adaptive lookback); adaptive lookback = round(base speed/[2×(2−FDI)]); FDI = 1 + ln(2×path length)/ln(2×(fractal lookback−1)), or 0 when path length is 0; path length is 0 when the selected-price range in the fractal-lookback window is 0, otherwise it is the sum over adjacent bars in that window of sqrt((difference between normalized selected prices)^2 + 1/fractal lookback^2), where normalized selected price = (selected price−window low)/(window high−window low); applied-price and volume-type settings select the price and volume inputs.
Long: (direction mode is normal AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset] > upper threshold AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset+1] <= upper threshold) OR (direction mode is reversed AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset] < lower threshold AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset+1] >= lower threshold); values are from T1 bars, with the offset bar forming when the offset is zero and completed otherwise; evaluate on each chart update at zero offset and on each new T1 bar otherwise.
Short: (direction mode is normal AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset] < lower threshold AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset+1] >= lower threshold) OR (direction mode is reversed AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset] > upper threshold AND Fractal Force Index(T1)[signal offset+1] <= upper threshold); values are from T1 bars, with the offset bar forming when the offset is zero and completed otherwise; evaluate on each chart update at zero offset and on each new T1 bar otherwise.
Exit: long, (stop-loss is configured AND low <= entry price − max(stop-loss distance, minimum permitted stop distance)) OR (take-profit is configured AND high >= entry price + max(take-profit distance, minimum permitted stop distance))
Exit: short, (stop-loss is configured AND high >= entry price + max(stop-loss distance, minimum permitted stop distance)) OR (take-profit is configured AND low <= entry price − max(take-profit distance, minimum permitted stop distance))
