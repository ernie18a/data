Long: A pending, unsuperseded Heikin-Ashi flip from bearish (Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open) to bullish (Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open) is within its configured trigger window, and the last completed chart-bar close >= EMA(close, channel-center lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) − ATR(channel-width lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) × channel multiplier; a disabled trigger-window limit means no expiry.
Short: A pending, unsuperseded Heikin-Ashi flip from bullish (Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open) to bearish (Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open) is within its configured trigger window, and the last completed chart-bar close <= EMA(close, channel-center lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) + ATR(channel-width lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) × channel multiplier; a disabled trigger-window limit means no expiry.
Calculation: Long initial stop = lowest low of the stop lookback completed chart bars minus stop buffer (swing method), or entry minus ATR(stop ATR lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) × stop ATR multiplier (ATR method).
Calculation: Short initial stop = highest high of the stop lookback completed chart bars plus stop buffer (swing method), or entry plus ATR(stop ATR lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) × stop ATR multiplier (ATR method).
Calculation: Long target = entry + (entry − long initial stop) × target multiple; short target = entry − (short initial stop − entry) × target multiple.
Calculation: Active long stop = long initial stop if initial stops are enabled and break-even has not triggered; entry + lock buffer after price reaches entry + (long target − entry) × break-even trigger fraction if break-even is enabled; otherwise none.
Calculation: Active short stop = short initial stop if initial stops are enabled and break-even has not triggered; entry − lock buffer after price reaches entry − (entry − short target) × break-even trigger fraction if break-even is enabled; otherwise none.
Exit: long, low <= active long stop OR high >= long target OR a bearish Heikin-Ashi flip when opposite-signal exits are enabled.
Exit: short, high >= active short stop OR low <= short target OR a bullish Heikin-Ashi flip when opposite-signal exits are enabled.
