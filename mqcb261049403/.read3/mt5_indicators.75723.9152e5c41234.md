Calculation: Volume percentile = 100 × count of positive volumes <= current volume / count of positive volumes over the volume lookback chart bars, including the forming bar; use the score midpoint if the effective lookback is below the minimum history or no positive volumes exist; when the volume filter is disabled, use the score midpoint
Calculation: T1 Parkinson-volatility percentile = 100 × count of valid rolling T1 Parkinson volatilities <= Parkinson volatility over the volatility period of last completed T1 bars / count of valid rolling T1 Parkinson volatilities; calculate rolling volatilities on T1 bars over the volatility period for each of the volatility lookback most recent T1 bars, including the T1 bar still forming
Calculation: MTF confirmation = true when T1 is the chart timeframe; otherwise false when the volatility regime is LOW and T1 Parkinson-volatility percentile > elevated-volatility threshold, or when the volatility regime is HIGH or EXTREME and T1 Parkinson-volatility percentile < low-volatility threshold; true otherwise
Calculation: Probability score = clamp(score midpoint + sign(close - MA(close, MA lookback)) × min(abs(close - MA(close, MA lookback)) / ATR(volatility period) × trend weight, trend cap) + sign(close - close[momentum lookback]) × min(abs(close - close[momentum lookback]) / ATR(volatility period) × momentum weight, momentum cap) + (volume percentile - score midpoint) × volume weight + (MTF enabled ? (MTF confirmation ? MTF bonus : -MTF bonus) : 0) + the score adjustment for the current volatility regime, minimum score, maximum score); use zero for each ATR-based term when ATR is not positive; use separate score-adjustment parameters for LOW, NORMAL, ELEVATED, HIGH, and EXTREME regimes
Long: close > MA(close, MA lookback) OR (volatility regime is LOW AND (MTF disabled OR MTF confirmation) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume percentile > low-volume threshold)) OR Probability score >= score midpoint + neutral band
Short: close < MA(close, MA lookback) OR (volatility regime is LOW AND (MTF disabled OR MTF confirmation) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume percentile > low-volume threshold)) OR Probability score <= score midpoint - neutral band
