Calculation: elapsed whole minutes = floor((current datetime − entry datetime) / 1 minute)
Calculation: elapsed-time limit in minutes = configured minute limit + 60 × configured hour limit
Exit: long, long side enabled AND (specific-time closing disabled OR (current hour >= GMT-adjusted close hour AND current minute >= GMT-adjusted close minute)) AND ((profit closing enabled AND (elapsed-time closing disabled OR elapsed whole minutes >= elapsed-time limit in minutes) AND close − entry price >= profit limit × instrument point size) OR (loss closing enabled AND elapsed whole minutes > elapsed-time limit in minutes AND close − entry price <= loss limit × instrument point size))
Exit: short, short side enabled AND (specific-time closing disabled OR (current hour >= GMT-adjusted close hour AND current minute >= GMT-adjusted close minute)) AND (elapsed-time closing disabled OR elapsed whole minutes >= elapsed-time limit in minutes) AND ((profit closing enabled AND entry price − close >= profit limit × instrument point size) OR (loss closing enabled AND entry price − close <= loss limit × instrument point size))
