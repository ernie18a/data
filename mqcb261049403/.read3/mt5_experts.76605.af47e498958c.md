Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) / SMA(ATR(ATR lookback), ATR average lookback, T1, last completed bar), or the neutral fallback ratio if either value is nonpositive
Calculation: Buy RSI threshold = normal RSI threshold if ATR or its average is nonpositive; otherwise extreme ATR RSI threshold if ATR ratio >= extreme ATR multiplier, high ATR RSI threshold if ATR ratio >= high ATR multiplier, else normal RSI threshold
Calculation: Recent swing low = low of the most recent qualifying T1 bar within the structure lookback, starting at least swing depth bars after the last completed bar; its low is <= each low of the swing-depth bars on both sides
Calculation: Prior swing low = low of the nearest qualifying swing-low bar at least swing depth plus one bars older than the recent swing low and within the structure lookback
Calculation: Recent swing high = high of the most recent qualifying T1 bar within the structure lookback, starting at least swing depth bars after the last completed bar; its high is >= each high of the swing-depth bars on both sides
Calculation: Prior swing high = high of the nearest qualifying swing-high bar at least swing depth plus one bars older than the recent swing high and within the structure lookback
Calculation: Bearish structure = recent swing low < prior swing low AND recent swing high < prior swing high
Calculation: Near LL support = current bid <= recent swing low + ATR(T1, last completed bar) * support-zone ATR multiplier OR (low of the last completed T1 bar < recent swing low - ATR(T1, last completed bar) * sweep ATR multiplier AND close of the last completed T1 bar > recent swing low)
Calculation: Bullish reversal = RSI(RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) > RSI(RSI lookback, T1, preceding completed bar) OR (close of the last completed T1 bar > low + candle-range fraction * (high - low) AND (close > open OR min(open, close) - low >= candle-wick range fraction * (high - low) OR min(open, close) - low >= candle-wick/body multiple * abs(close - open)))
Calculation: Equity drawdown percent = max(0, 100 * (balance - equity) / balance), or zero if balance is nonpositive
Calculation: Emergency recovery = emergency recovery enabled AND open position count >= emergency minimum position count AND equity drawdown percent >= emergency drawdown threshold AND current ask <= lowest buy entry price - ATR(T1, last completed bar) * emergency ATR multiplier
Calculation: Recovery quality score = clamp(score floor, score cap, RSI score weight when RSI <= buy RSI threshold + deeper RSI score weight when RSI <= buy RSI threshold - RSI strengthening offset + structure score weight when bearish structure + support score weight when near LL support + reversal score weight when bullish reversal + high ATR score weight when ATR ratio >= high ATR multiplier + emergency score weight when emergency recovery; when rescue mode, also add rescue confluence weight when bearish structure AND near LL support AND bullish reversal, add rescue drawdown weight when equity drawdown percent >= emergency drawdown threshold, subtract rescue support penalty when not near LL support, and subtract rescue reversal penalty when not bullish reversal)
Calculation: Recovery distance = max(ATR(T1, last completed bar) * recovery ATR distance multiplier * (1 + (open position count - one) * recovery depth step), minimum second-trade distance when one position is open, minimum third-trade distance when two are open, or minimum deep-recovery distance when more are open); during the post-crash wide-gap interval, also max with the crash recovery minimum distance
Calculation: News blocked = selected calendar and/or CSV source contains a relevant event within the configured before/after window; relevance uses the configured currency filter, or in automatic mode a currency in the instrument name, USD for a gold instrument, or ALL; when high-impact-only is enabled, require high calendar importance or a CSV impact containing high or red
Calculation: Crash active = a sampled absolute bid move divided by price point and elapsed seconds exceeds the crash-speed threshold, remaining active until the crash pause interval after the latest detection expires
Calculation: Lot multiplier = max(one, normalized base lot / reference lot unit)
Long: on a new T1 bar, no robot positions are open AND spread <= spread limit AND NOT news blocked AND NOT (crash active AND first-entry crash block enabled) AND RSI(RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) <= buy RSI threshold AND recovery quality score with emergency recovery false and rescue mode false >= normal recovery score threshold AND (bearish structure required setting is false OR bearish structure) AND (LL support required setting is false OR near LL support) AND (bullish reversal required setting is false OR bullish reversal)
Long: on a new T1 bar, at least one robot position is open AND spread <= spread limit AND NOT news blocked AND (automatic recovery depth enabled OR open position count < manual recovery trade limit) AND ATR(T1, last completed bar) > zero AND current ask <= lowest buy entry price - recovery distance AND RSI(RSI lookback, T1, last completed bar) <= buy RSI threshold AND recovery quality score with emergency recovery as calculated and rescue mode when open position count plus one >= rescue start trade >= rescue recovery score threshold in rescue mode or normal recovery score threshold otherwise AND (emergency recovery OR (bearish structure required setting is false OR bearish structure) AND (LL support required setting is false OR near LL support) AND (bullish reversal required setting is false OR bullish reversal))
Exit: long, basket profit reaches the basket target and subsequently falls by at least the trailing step from its peak since reaching that target
Exit: long, an individual position's profit plus swap >= trim profit target * lot multiplier
