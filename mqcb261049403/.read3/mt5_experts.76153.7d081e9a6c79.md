Calculation: opening-range high = max(high, T2 bars from session open through the T2 bar containing range end, inclusive; calculated on chart bars T1, including that T2 bar while it is forming)
Calculation: opening-range low = min(low, T2 bars from session open through the T2 bar containing range end, inclusive; calculated on chart bars T1, including that T2 bar while it is forming)
Long: long trades enabled AND no trade has yet been taken this session AND datetime is within the trading window after the opening range AND opening-range high > 0 AND opening-range low > 0 AND opening-range high > opening-range low AND close(T1, last completed chart bar) > opening-range high + breakout buffer
Short: short trades enabled AND no trade has yet been taken this session AND datetime is within the trading window after the opening range AND opening-range high > 0 AND opening-range low > 0 AND opening-range high > opening-range low AND close(T1, last completed chart bar) < opening-range low - breakout buffer
