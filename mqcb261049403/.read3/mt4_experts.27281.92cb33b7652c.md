Calculation: LevelHigh = high of the most recent T1 bar in the backward scan of up to one day whose opening hour is the level-setting hour, including its current value if still forming
Calculation: LevelLow = low of the same T1 bar as LevelHigh
Calculation: MeanClose = arithmetic mean of T1 closes from the current, possibly forming, bar through the LevelHigh bar, inclusive
Calculation: RecheckClose = close of the most recent T1 bar in the backward scan of up to one day whose opening hour is the recheck hour, using its value when evaluated (possibly still forming)
Calculation: BuyBarFilter = all-bars check disabled OR every T1 close from the current, possibly forming, bar through the LevelHigh bar is at or below LevelHigh
Calculation: SellBarFilter = all-bars check disabled OR every T1 close from the current, possibly forming, bar through the LevelHigh bar is at or above LevelLow
Calculation: BuyRecheckFilter = recheck disabled OR NOT (MeanClose > RecheckClose AND RecheckClose < close(T1, last completed bar))
Calculation: SellRecheckFilter = recheck disabled OR NOT (MeanClose < RecheckClose AND RecheckClose > close(T1, last completed bar))
Long: on a new T1 bar opening at the order-check hour, (contrarian mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelLow − minimum level distance AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelLow − maximum level distance AND BuyBarFilter AND BuyRecheckFilter) OR (trend-following mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelHigh + minimum level distance AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelHigh + maximum level distance AND SellBarFilter AND SellRecheckFilter)
Short: on a new T1 bar opening at the order-check hour, (contrarian mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelHigh + minimum level distance AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelHigh + maximum level distance AND SellBarFilter AND SellRecheckFilter) OR (trend-following mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelLow − minimum level distance AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelLow − maximum level distance AND BuyBarFilter AND BuyRecheckFilter)
Exit: long, (scheduled-time exit enabled AND current hour >= scheduled close hour) OR (signal-close enabled AND new T1 bar AND ((contrarian mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelLow + minimum level distance) OR (trend-following mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelHigh − minimum level distance)))
Exit: short, (scheduled-time exit enabled AND current hour >= scheduled close hour) OR (signal-close enabled AND new T1 bar AND ((contrarian mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) < LevelHigh − minimum level distance) OR (trend-following mode AND close(T1, last completed bar) > LevelLow + minimum level distance)))
