Calculation: Swing high(T, strength) = a completed bar's high exceeds the highs of the strength bars immediately before and after it
Calculation: Swing low(T, strength) = a completed bar's low is below the lows of the strength bars immediately before and after it
Calculation: Liquidity zone midpoints = on each new T1 bar, form buy-side levels from the previous completed T7 bar's high followed by up to the liquidity-swing cap most recent completed T3, T4, T5, and T6 swing highs within their separate lookbacks; form sell-side levels the same way from the previous completed T7 bar's low and swing lows, using the shared liquidity pivot strength; compute matching distance as the greater of ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times the ATR matching multiplier and minimum price increment times its multiplier when ATR matching is enabled and ATR is positive, otherwise use the fixed matching distance; process each side's levels in source order, matching each level against other current same-side levels within matching distance and replacing it with their mean; rank by number of matches, preserving source order for ties; retain up to the zone-count parameter, skipping levels within the uniqueness fraction times matching distance of a retained midpoint; source pivots use completed bars on their own timeframes
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close(T1, last completed bar) > open(T1, last completed bar) AND (displacement filter disabled OR (absolute body >= ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times displacement body multiplier AND close(T1, last completed bar) > high(T1, preceding bar))
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close(T1, last completed bar) < open(T1, last completed bar) AND (displacement filter disabled OR (absolute body >= ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times displacement body multiplier AND close(T1, last completed bar) < low(T1, preceding bar))
Calculation: Sell reversal setup = a buy-side zone midpoint has high(T1, last completed bar) > midpoint AND close(T1, last completed bar) < midpoint when the sweep filter is enabled, close(T1, last completed bar) < midpoint when the close filter is enabled, and bearish displacement when the displacement filter is enabled
Calculation: Buy reversal setup = a sell-side zone midpoint has low(T1, last completed bar) < midpoint AND close(T1, last completed bar) > midpoint when the sweep filter is enabled, close(T1, last completed bar) > midpoint when the close filter is enabled, and bullish displacement when the displacement filter is enabled
Calculation: Long stop = the most recent T2 swing low found in the stop-search range beginning at the stop-start offset, using the M1 pivot-strength parameter; its pivot comparison may include the still-forming T2 bar
Calculation: Short stop = the most recent T2 swing high found in the stop-search range beginning at the stop-start offset, using the M1 pivot-strength parameter; its pivot comparison may include the still-forming T2 bar
Calculation: Long TP1 = the nearest-price T2 swing high above entry among the target-candidate cap most recent eligible swings within the target-search range, using the M1 pivot-strength parameter and completed T2 bars, or entry plus absolute distance from entry to long stop times the fallback reward multiple if none exists
Calculation: Short TP1 = the nearest-price T2 swing low below entry among the target-candidate cap most recent eligible swings within the target-search range, using the M1 pivot-strength parameter and completed T2 bars, or entry minus absolute distance from entry to short stop times the fallback reward multiple if none exists
Long: long stop > 0 AND long stop < close(T1, last completed bar) AND absolute distance from close(T1, last completed bar) to long stop > minimum price increment times the minimum-risk increment parameter AND ((NOT sell reversal setup AND buy reversal setup) OR (NOT sell reversal setup AND NOT buy reversal setup AND breakout enabled AND a buy-side zone midpoint has close(T1, last completed bar) > midpoint AND close(T1, preceding bar) <= midpoint AND bullish displacement))
Short: short stop > 0 AND short stop > close(T1, last completed bar) AND absolute distance from close(T1, last completed bar) to short stop > minimum price increment times the minimum-risk increment parameter AND (sell reversal setup OR (NOT sell reversal setup AND NOT buy reversal setup AND breakout enabled AND a sell-side zone midpoint has close(T1, last completed bar) < midpoint AND close(T1, preceding bar) >= midpoint AND bearish displacement))
Exit: long, close(T1, still-forming bar) <= stored long stop OR close(T1, still-forming bar) >= stored long TP1
Exit: short, close(T1, still-forming bar) >= stored short stop OR close(T1, still-forming bar) <= stored short TP1
