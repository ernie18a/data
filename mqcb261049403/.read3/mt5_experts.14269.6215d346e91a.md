Long: SuperTrend(CCI lookback, CCI level, T1 bars, last completed signal bar) has an up signal.
Short: SuperTrend(CCI lookback, CCI level, T1 bars, last completed signal bar) has a down signal.
Exit: short, the signal bar has no up or down signal AND at least one earlier completed T1 bar has an up signal.
Exit: long, the signal bar has no up or down signal AND the most recent earlier completed T1 bar with an up or down signal has a down signal.
