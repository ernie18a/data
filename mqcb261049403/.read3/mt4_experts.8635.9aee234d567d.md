Calculation: Pattern score(w1,w2,w3,w4,p,c,centerA,m2,m3) = v if abs(v)>c, otherwise 0; v=(w1-centerA)+((w2-centerA)*AO(T2,p)+(w3-centerA)*AO(T2,m2*p)+(w4-centerA)*AO(T2,m3*p))/AO(T2,0). Read T2 values on chart bars; shift 0 is forming, and the other shifts are p, m2*p, and m3*p bars ago. Each use has separate w1-w4, p, and c parameters.
Calculation: Price score(w1,w2,w3,w4,p,centerB,m2,m3,m4) = (w1-centerB)*(close[0]-open[p])+(w2-centerB)*(open[p]-open[m2*p])+(w3-centerB)*(open[m2*p]-open[m3*p])+(w4-centerB)*(open[m3*p]-open[m4*p]); close[0] is the forming chart bar. This use has its own w1-w4 and p parameters.
Calculation: AO change = AO(T1, latest completed bar) - AO(T1, bar preceding the latest completed bar), read on chart bars from T1.
Long: long-entry enabled AND (((price-score filter bypass selected OR Price score > 0) AND Pattern score(long-base parameters)>0 AND AO change>0) OR (priority mode selects gated overlay AND Pattern score(overlay-gate parameters)>0 AND Pattern score(overlay-long parameters)>0) OR (priority mode selects long overlay AND Pattern score(overlay-long parameters)>0))
Short: short-entry enabled AND (((price-score filter bypass selected OR Price score<0) AND Pattern score(short-base parameters)>0 AND AO change<0) OR (priority mode selects gated overlay AND Pattern score(overlay-gate parameters)<=0 AND Pattern score(overlay-short parameters)>0) OR (priority mode selects short overlay AND Pattern score(overlay-short parameters)>0))
Exit: long, day-of-week equals exit-day parameter AND hour is at least exit-hour parameter
Exit: short, day-of-week equals exit-day parameter AND hour is at least exit-hour parameter
Exit: long, close is greater than current stop-loss level plus (trailing multiplier times current stop distance plus spread) times point size AND (price-score filter bypass selected OR Price score<0) AND Pattern score(short-base parameters)>0 AND AO change<0
Exit: short, close is less than current stop-loss level minus (trailing multiplier times current stop distance plus spread) times point size AND (price-score filter bypass selected OR Price score>0) AND Pattern score(long-base parameters)>0 AND AO change>0
