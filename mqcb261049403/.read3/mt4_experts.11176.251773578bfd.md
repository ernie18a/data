Calculation: SMA trend direction = 1 when SMA(close, trend lookback)[0] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[1] and SMA(close, trend lookback)[1] > SMA(close, trend lookback)[2]; -1 when both comparisons are <; otherwise 0. These are chart-timeframe bars, with [0] still forming.
Calculation: SMA deviation for an instrument at bar i = close[i] - SMA(close, correlation lookback)[i], using chart-timeframe bars including the still-forming bar.
Calculation: deviation correlation between the base and a hedge = sum of paired SMA deviations over i from 0 to correlation lookback - 1 divided by the square root of the product of each instrument's summed squared SMA deviations over the same bars.
Calculation: a correlation threshold passes when the threshold is positive and deviation correlation >= the threshold, or the threshold is negative and deviation correlation <= the threshold.
Calculation: projected basket swap = base leg long swap rate × base leg lots + the hedge leg's swap rate for its selected side × hedge leg lots.
Long: base leg, trading enabled and SMA trend direction = 1 and at least one of: hedge 1 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 1; hedge 2 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 2; hedge 3 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 3.
Short: base leg, trading enabled and SMA trend direction = -1 and at least one of: hedge 1 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 1; hedge 2 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 2; hedge 3 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 3.
Long: hedge 1, trading enabled and hedge 1 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 1 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 1 follows the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 1 opposes the base leg)).
Short: hedge 1, trading enabled and hedge 1 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 1 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 1 opposes the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 1 follows the base leg)).
Long: hedge 2, trading enabled and hedge 2 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 2 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 2 follows the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 2 opposes the base leg)).
Short: hedge 2, trading enabled and hedge 2 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 2 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 2 opposes the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 2 follows the base leg)).
Long: hedge 3, trading enabled and hedge 3 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 3 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 3 follows the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 3 opposes the base leg)).
Short: hedge 3, trading enabled and hedge 3 enabled and configured and its base-hedge deviation correlation passes correlation threshold 3 and ((SMA trend direction = 1 and hedge 3 opposes the base leg) or (SMA trend direction = -1 and hedge 3 follows the base leg)).
Exit: long, for each hedge basket, projected basket swap > 0 and total open trading profit > profit target, or projected basket swap <= 0 and total open trading profit plus swap > profit target.
Exit: short, for each hedge basket, projected basket swap > 0 and total open trading profit > profit target, or projected basket swap <= 0 and total open trading profit plus swap > profit target.
