Calculation: Candidate trailing level = on each chart bar, using the selected method: for a long, the nearest completed T1 bar low or confirmed lower fractal whose offset-adjusted level is at least the broker minimum stop distance below close; for a short, the nearest completed T1 bar high or confirmed upper fractal whose offset-adjusted level is at least that distance above close; or, for ATR, close minus ATR(lookback, T1, bar still forming) and offset for a long, and close plus ATR(lookback, T1, bar still forming) and offset for a short; or Parabolic SAR(step, maximum, T1, bar still forming) minus offset for a long or plus offset for a short, set to zero if it is within the broker minimum stop distance; or MA(applied price, averaging method, lookback, T1, bar still forming) minus offset for a long or plus offset for a short, set to zero if it is within that distance; or close plus the percentage of unrealized gain for a long or minus it for a short; or close minus offset for a long or plus it for a short. T1 values are sampled on chart bars; the offset and distances are in instrument points.
Calculation: Active trailing stop = for a long, the greater of the prior active stop and candidate trailing level, updated only when the candidate is at least the activation profit above the reference entry price, improves the prior stop loss by at least the movement step, and is at least the broker minimum stop distance below close; for a short, the lesser of the prior active stop and candidate trailing level, updated only when the candidate is at least the activation profit below the reference entry price, the prior stop loss is absent or the candidate improves it by at least the movement step, and the candidate is at least the broker minimum stop distance above close. The reference entry price is the position entry price or, when trailing from breakeven is enabled, the volume-weighted average entry price of same-side positions. The active stop starts absent; activation profit and movement step are in instrument points.
Exit: long, active trailing stop exists AND low <= active trailing stop
Exit: short, active trailing stop exists AND high >= active trailing stop
