Calculation: Skyscraper trend = computed on T1 bars; seed the oldest calculated bar with neutral direction and both boundaries at close. For each next bar, let upper ATR extreme = highest(ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), extreme-window lookback) and lower ATR extreme = lowest(ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback), extreme-window lookback); step = floor(0.5 * factor * (upper ATR extreme + lower ATR extreme) / minimum price increment) * minimum price increment. If the prior direction is bearish, candidate upper boundary = low + 2 * step and candidate lower boundary = high - 2 * step; otherwise, candidate upper boundary = close + 2 * step and candidate lower boundary = close - 2 * step. Carry the prior direction, set it bullish if close > prior upper boundary, then set it bearish if close < prior lower boundary. If the resulting direction is bullish, set the lower boundary to max(candidate lower boundary, prior lower boundary) and the upper boundary to the candidate upper boundary; otherwise, set the upper boundary to min(candidate upper boundary, prior upper boundary) and the lower boundary to the candidate lower boundary. The selected T1 bar may be still forming or completed, as chosen by the bar-selection parameter.
Long: on chart bars, Skyscraper trend on the selected T1 bar is bullish
Short: on chart bars, Skyscraper trend on the selected T1 bar is neutral or bearish
