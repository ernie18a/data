Calculation: Adaptive step line(x, step) = on the first bar set trend to short and upper/lower bands to x + 2 × step and x − 2 × step; on each later bar set candidate bands to x ± 2 × step, carry the prior trend, set trend to long if x exceeds the prior upper band, then to short if x is below the prior lower band, raise the candidate lower band to the prior lower band when trend is long and it is smaller, lower the candidate upper band to the prior upper band when trend is short and it is larger, and output candidate lower band + step when trend is long or candidate upper band − step when trend is short
Long: Adaptive step line(Stochastic(high, low, close, stochastic parameters), fast step) > Adaptive step line(Stochastic(high, low, close, stochastic parameters), slow step)
Short: Adaptive step line(Stochastic(high, low, close, stochastic parameters), fast step) < Adaptive step line(Stochastic(high, low, close, stochastic parameters), slow step)
