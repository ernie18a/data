Long: On chart bars, MACD line = EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, longer lookback) is above its signal, SMA(MACD line, signal lookback), on the forming bar and is rising from the previous completed chart bar; OR it is rising and was at or below its signal on the previous completed chart bar while it is at or above its signal on the forming bar.
Short: On chart bars, the MACD line is below its signal on the forming bar and is falling from the previous completed chart bar; OR it is falling and was at or above its signal on the previous completed chart bar while it is at or below its signal on the forming bar.
Long: For each of the six configured EMA/CCI timeframe pairs (Eᵢ, Cᵢ), EMA(close, EMA lookback, Eᵢ, forming bar) > EMA(close, EMA lookback, Eᵢ, previous completed bar) AND CCI(close, CCI lookbackᵢ, Cᵢ, forming bar) > CCI threshold AND CCI(close, CCI lookbackᵢ, Cᵢ, forming bar) > CCI(close, CCI lookbackᵢ, Cᵢ, previous completed bar); OR the EMA is falling, the forming CCI is below the threshold, and the forming CCI is at least its previous completed value. Values are sampled on chart bars.
Short: For each of the six configured EMA/CCI timeframe pairs (Eᵢ, Cᵢ), EMA(close, EMA lookback, Eᵢ, forming bar) > EMA(close, EMA lookback, Eᵢ, previous completed bar) AND CCI(close, CCI lookbackᵢ, Cᵢ, forming bar) > CCI threshold AND the forming CCI is at most its previous completed value; OR the EMA is falling, the forming CCI is below the threshold, and the forming CCI is below its previous completed value. Values are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Long-status count = count of the six configured EMA/CCI timeframe pairs satisfying the per-pair Long condition.
Calculation: Short-status count = count of the six configured EMA/CCI timeframe pairs satisfying the per-pair Short condition.
Calculation: Wait-status count = six minus the Long-status count minus the Short-status count.
Long: Long-status count > Short-status count AND Wait-status count < wait-count threshold.
Short: Short-status count > Long-status count AND Wait-status count < wait-count threshold.
Calculation: Stochastic-up count = count of the five configured stochastic timeframes where Stochastic main (K lookback, D lookback, slowing, SMA, low/high, forming bar) > Stochastic signal with the same inputs; values are sampled on chart bars from each timeframe's forming bar.
Calculation: Stochastic-down count = count of the five configured stochastic timeframes where Stochastic main (K lookback, D lookback, slowing, SMA, low/high, forming bar) < Stochastic signal with the same inputs; values are sampled on chart bars from each timeframe's forming bar.
Calculation: OSMA-rising count = count of the five configured OSMA timeframes where OSMA(shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, signal lookback, forming bar) > OSMA with the same inputs on the previous completed bar; values are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: OSMA-falling count = count of the five configured OSMA timeframes where OSMA(shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, signal lookback, forming bar) < OSMA with the same inputs on the previous completed bar; values are sampled on chart bars.
Long: close(T1, forming bar) > close(T1, previous completed bar) AND Stochastic-up count > Stochastic-down count AND OSMA-rising count > OSMA-falling count; values are sampled on chart bars.
Short: close(T1, forming bar) < close(T1, previous completed bar) AND Stochastic-up count < Stochastic-down count AND OSMA-rising count < OSMA-falling count; values are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Currency-strength up count = count across a currency's seven related exchange rates and four configured timeframe values where the forming close is above the previous completed close when the currency is the base, or at most the previous completed close when it is the quote; values are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Currency-strength down count = count across a currency's seven related exchange rates and four configured timeframe values where the forming close is at most the previous completed close when the currency is the base, or above the previous completed close when it is the quote; values are sampled on chart bars.
Long: For each currency-strength measure, Currency-strength up count >= Currency-strength down count.
Short: For each currency-strength measure, Currency-strength up count < Currency-strength down count.
