Calculation: Recent volatility volume(side) = on chart bars taking T1 values, when the volatility lookback is positive, sum volume on completed T1 bars at shifts i where 1 <= i < volatility lookback and on the current forming T1 bar when the candle body exceeds the body threshold and its direction is bullish for long or bearish for short, with directions reversed when the body threshold parameter is negative; when the lookback is negative, use one shared value initialized to zero, replace it with current T1 volume plus a baseline whenever the current forming bar's absolute body is at least the absolute body threshold, and otherwise retain its previous value, where the baseline is volume at shift |lookback| if the absolute difference between close at shift max(1, |lookback|-1) and open at shift |lookback| is at least the absolute body threshold, otherwise zero; when the lookback is zero, use current T1 volume if its absolute body is at least the absolute body threshold, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Older volatility volume(side) = on chart bars taking T1 values, when the volatility lookback is positive, sum volume on completed T1 bars at shifts i where volatility lookback <= i < 2 times the lookback, for candles whose body exceeds the body threshold in the bullish direction for long or bearish direction for short, with directions reversed when the body threshold parameter is negative; when the lookback is negative, sum volume on completed T1 bars at shifts i where |lookback| <= i < 2 times |lookback| and absolute body exceeds the absolute body threshold, identically for both sides; when the lookback is zero, use the initialized older-volume baseline.
