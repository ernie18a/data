Long: MACD(close, long-entry fast, slow, and signal lookbacks, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > MACD(close, same lookbacks, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars) AND MACD(close, same lookbacks, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < 0 AND Stochastic(high, low, close, long-entry stochastic parameters A, T4, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < long-entry stochastic threshold AND Stochastic(high, low, close, long-entry stochastic parameters B, T4, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > Stochastic(high, low, close, long-entry stochastic parameters C, T4, forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND close(T3, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > high(T3, last completed bar sampled on chart bars)
Short: MACD(close, short-entry fast, slow, and signal lookbacks, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < MACD(close, same lookbacks, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars) AND MACD(close, same lookbacks, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars) > 0 AND Stochastic(high, low, close, short-entry parameters, T2, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > short-entry stochastic threshold AND close(T3, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < low(T3, last completed bar sampled on chart bars)

Calculation: Short favorable extreme = lowest low since entry
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop distance in points OR (long trailing distance in points > 0 AND Long favorable extreme - entry price > long trailing distance in points AND low <= Long favorable extreme - long trailing distance in points)
