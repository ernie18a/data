Long: close crossed above EMA(high, EMA lookback, EMA shift) on the last two completed chart bars AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the current forming chart bar > long RSI threshold AND hour(datetime) >= start-hour parameter AND hour(datetime) < end-hour parameter AND no long position is open
Short: close crossed below EMA(low, EMA lookback, EMA shift) on the last two completed chart bars AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) on the current forming chart bar < short RSI threshold AND hour(datetime) >= start-hour parameter AND hour(datetime) < end-hour parameter AND no short position is open
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR hour(datetime) >= end-hour parameter OR (close crossed above EMA(high, EMA lookback, EMA shift) on the last two completed chart bars AND a short position is open)
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR hour(datetime) >= end-hour parameter OR (close crossed below EMA(low, EMA lookback, EMA shift) on the last two completed chart bars AND a long position is open)
