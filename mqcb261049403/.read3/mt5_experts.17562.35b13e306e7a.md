Calculation: adjusted point = instrument point size × (10 for 3- or 5-digit quotes, otherwise 1)
Calculation: highest high since entry = Highest(high since entry)
Calculation: lowest low since entry = Lowest(low since entry)
Long: On each chart tick, using last completed bars from T1 (shorter timeframe) and T2 (longer timeframe), close(T1) < SMMA(median price, T1 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T1) AND close(T2) < SMMA(median price, T2 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T2) AND SMMA(median price, T1 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T1) - close(T1) <= proximity parameter × adjusted point AND Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) < oversold level AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) < oversold level AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high, two bars ago) < Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) > Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high)
Short: On each chart tick, using last completed bars from T1 (shorter timeframe) and T2 (longer timeframe), close(T1) > SMMA(median price, T1 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T1) AND close(T2) > SMMA(median price, T2 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T2) AND close(T1) - SMMA(median price, T1 MA lookback, shared MA plot shift, T1) <= proximity parameter × adjusted point AND Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) > overbought level AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) > overbought level AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high, two bars ago) > Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) AND Stochastic main line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high) < Stochastic signal line(T1, K lookback, shared D/slowing lookback, SMA, low/high)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance × adjusted point OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance × adjusted point OR highest high since entry > entry price + trailing distance × adjusted point AND low <= highest high since entry - trailing distance × adjusted point
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance × adjusted point OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance × adjusted point OR lowest low since entry < entry price - trailing distance × adjusted point AND high >= lowest low since entry + trailing distance × adjusted point
