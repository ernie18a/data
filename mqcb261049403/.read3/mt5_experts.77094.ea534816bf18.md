Calculation: Long stop distance = entry price - (sweep bar low - SL buffer ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar at entry))
Calculation: Short stop distance = (sweep bar high + SL buffer ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar at entry)) - entry price
Long: At sweep detection, the most recent unswept FractalLow(low, swing lookback bars on each side) level is swept by a completed chart bar with low < level - sweep ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND close > level; the trend filter is disabled or, sampled on chart bars, the forming T1 close > EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, forming bar); the session filter is disabled or the sweep occurs within its server-time window (hour >= start and < end when start <= end, otherwise hour >= start or < end); then within max confirmation bars, a later completed chart bar has close > max(sweep bar open, sweep bar close) AND high - low >= confirmation range ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND current spread points <= maximum spread points AND long stop distance >= minimum SL-to-ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND long stop distance >= minimum SL-to-spread ratio × current spread in price units
Short: At sweep detection, the most recent unswept FractalHigh(high, swing lookback bars on each side) level is swept by a completed chart bar with high > level + sweep ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND close < level; the trend filter is disabled or, sampled on chart bars, the forming T1 close < EMA(close, trend EMA lookback, T1, forming bar); the session filter is disabled or the sweep occurs within its server-time window (hour >= start and < end when start <= end, otherwise hour >= start or < end); then within max confirmation bars, a later completed chart bar has close < min(sweep bar open, sweep bar close) AND high - low >= confirmation range ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND current spread points <= maximum spread points AND short stop distance >= minimum SL-to-ATR multiplier × ATR(ATR period, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND short stop distance >= minimum SL-to-spread ratio × current spread in price units
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop distance OR high >= entry price + reward ratio × long stop distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short stop distance OR low <= entry price - reward ratio × short stop distance
