Calculation: Previous-period high and low = high and low of the last completed T1 bar
Calculation: Average range = mean(high − low, average-range lookback, T1, last completed bars)
Calculation: For strategy i, high band = [previous-period high − previous-period high × zone_i / 200, previous-period high + previous-period high × zone_i / 200]; low band = [previous-period low − previous-period high × zone_i / 200, previous-period low + previous-period high × zone_i / 200]
Calculation: Average entry price for a strategy and position side = sum(entry price × volume) / sum(volume) for its open positions
Calculation: Latest entry price for a strategy and position side = entry price of its most recently opened position
Long: On each new T5 bar, spread ≤ spread limit AND (ATR filter disabled OR yesterday’s T1 high-low range / average range × 100 is between the minimum and maximum range percentages) AND (latest completed T2 close is in strategy 1’s high band AND, within the preceding one-T1-period lookback of completed T2 bars, the nearest earlier close outside that band is above it AND a high from that close through the bar before the latest completed T2 bar is ≥ the high band’s upper edge × (1 + distance_1 / 100) OR, applying the latest completed T3 close to strategy 2’s stages that reset on each new T1 bar and after a signal, a close ≤ strategy 2’s low band upper edge is followed by a close ≥ strategy 2’s high band upper edge × (1 + distance_2 / 100) OR, applying the latest completed T4 close to strategy 3’s stages that reset on each new T1 bar and after a signal, a close ≤ strategy 3’s low band upper edge is followed by a close ≥ that edge × (1 + distance_3 / 100), followed by a close within strategy 3’s low band)
Short: On each new T5 bar, spread ≤ spread limit AND (ATR filter disabled OR yesterday’s T1 high-low range / average range × 100 is between the minimum and maximum range percentages) AND (latest completed T2 close is in strategy 1’s low band AND, within the preceding one-T1-period lookback of completed T2 bars, the nearest earlier close outside that band is below it AND a low from that close through the bar before the latest completed T2 bar is ≤ the low band’s lower edge × (1 − distance_1 / 100) OR, applying the latest completed T3 close to strategy 2’s stages that reset on each new T1 bar and after a signal, a close ≥ strategy 2’s high band lower edge is followed by a close ≤ strategy 2’s low band lower edge × (1 − distance_2 / 100) OR, applying the latest completed T4 close to strategy 3’s stages that reset on each new T1 bar and after a signal, a close ≥ strategy 3’s high band lower edge is followed by a close ≤ that edge × (1 − distance_3 / 100), followed by a close within strategy 3’s high band)
Long: On each new T5 bar, strategy i’s grid is enabled, a long position for strategy i is open, and bid ≤ latest entry price for that position side − latest entry price × grid_step_i / 100, for i = 1, 2, or 3
Short: On each new T5 bar, strategy i’s grid is enabled, a short position for strategy i is open, and ask ≥ latest entry price for that position side + latest entry price × grid_step_i / 100, for i = 1, 2, or 3
Exit: long, for strategy 1, 2, or 3, bid ≤ average entry price × (1 − stop_i / 100) OR bid ≥ average entry price × (1 + target_i / 100)
Exit: short, for strategy 1, 2, or 3, ask ≥ average entry price × (1 + stop_i / 100) OR ask ≤ average entry price × (1 − target_i / 100)
