Calculation: High pivots(I) = for each I in RSI(close, RSI lookback), MACD main(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback), and Stochastic main(high, low, close, %K lookback, %D smoothing, slowing), pair I(i) with high(i) on chart bars i from pivot radius D through lookback B minus D minus one; keep bars whose high strictly exceeds highs at i±1 through i±D and exceeds the peak-average multiplier times the mean high at available bars i±1 through i±2D, clipped to the last B chart bars; index 0 is the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Low pivots(I) = for each I in RSI(close, RSI lookback), MACD main(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback), and Stochastic main(high, low, close, %K lookback, %D smoothing, slowing), pair I(i) with low(i) on chart bars i from pivot radius D through lookback B minus D minus one; keep bars whose low strictly undercuts lows at i±1 through i±D and is below the trough-average multiplier times the mean low at available bars i±1 through i±2D, clipped to the last B chart bars; index 0 is the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Divergence strength(p_new, p_old, I_new, I_old) = min(abs(p_new-p_old)/max(p_new,p_old) + abs(I_new-I_old)/max(abs(I_new),abs(I_old)), strength cap).
Calculation: Bullish divergence(I) = the two most recent low pivots exist, newer pivot price < older pivot price, I at newer pivot > I at older pivot, and divergence strength >= divergence threshold.
Calculation: Bearish divergence(I) = NOT Bullish divergence(I) AND the two most recent high pivots exist, newer pivot price > older pivot price, I at newer pivot < I at older pivot, and divergence strength >= divergence threshold.
Long: long entries enabled AND at least confirmation threshold of RSI, MACD main, and Stochastic main have bullish divergence AND (trend filter disabled OR previous completed chart-bar close > EMA(close, trend lookback) on the previous completed chart bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR previous completed chart-bar volume > volume multiplier × mean volume of the previous N completed chart bars, including that bar).
Short: short entries enabled AND fewer than confirmation threshold of RSI, MACD main, and Stochastic main have bullish divergence AND at least confirmation threshold of them have bearish divergence AND (trend filter disabled OR previous completed chart-bar close < EMA(close, trend lookback) on the previous completed chart bar) AND (volume filter disabled OR previous completed chart-bar volume > volume multiplier × mean volume of the previous N completed chart bars, including that bar).
Exit: long, stop-loss distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance in price units OR take-profit distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance in price units.
Exit: short, stop-loss distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance in price units OR take-profit distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance in price units.
