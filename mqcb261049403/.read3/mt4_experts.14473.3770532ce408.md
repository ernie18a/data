Calculation: Long trailing stop = close − trailing distance when (profit-only trailing is disabled OR close − entry price > trailing distance) AND prior long trailing stop < close − trailing distance − trailing step + one point; otherwise prior long trailing stop; initially entry price − initial stop-loss distance when the stop-loss parameter is positive, otherwise zero
Calculation: Short trailing stop = close + trailing distance when (profit-only trailing is disabled OR entry price − close > trailing distance) AND (prior short trailing stop > close + trailing distance + trailing step − one point OR prior short trailing stop = zero); otherwise prior short trailing stop; initially entry price + initial stop-loss distance when the stop-loss parameter is positive, otherwise zero
Long: close crosses above Envelopes lower band(close, lookback, deviation, current forming chart bar) AND (time control is disabled OR (start hour < stop hour AND terminal hour >= start hour AND terminal hour < stop hour) OR (start hour > stop hour AND (terminal hour >= start hour OR terminal hour < stop hour)))
Short: close crosses below Envelopes upper band(close, lookback, deviation, current forming chart bar) AND (time control is disabled OR (start hour < stop hour AND terminal hour >= start hour AND terminal hour < stop hour) OR (start hour > stop hour AND (terminal hour >= start hour OR terminal hour < stop hour)))
Exit: Long, close crosses below Envelopes upper band(close, lookback, deviation, current forming chart bar) OR (Long trailing stop > zero AND low <= Long trailing stop) OR (take-profit parameter > zero AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance)
Exit: Short, close crosses above Envelopes lower band(close, lookback, deviation, current forming chart bar) OR (Short trailing stop > zero AND high >= Short trailing stop) OR (take-profit parameter > zero AND low <= entry price − take-profit distance)
