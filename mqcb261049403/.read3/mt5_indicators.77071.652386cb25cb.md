Calculation: Quality score = clamp(liquidity points + sweep-depth points + MSS points + displacement points + FVG points + retest points + volume points, score lower-bound parameter, score upper-bound parameter); sweep-depth points use separate base and deep-sweep point parameters, selecting deep-sweep points when frozen sweep penetration / ATR at sweep >= deep-sweep threshold; displacement points are none unless the MSS candle body points in the setup direction, then use separate base and enhanced point parameters, selecting enhanced points when abs(close − open) / ATR at MSS >= enhanced-body multiplier × minimum-displacement ATR, otherwise base points when that ratio >= minimum-displacement ATR; FVG points use separate base and enhanced point parameters, selecting enhanced points when FVG size / ATR at sweep >= FVG-score threshold; volume points use a separate bonus-point parameter when Volume Z-Score(volume at MSS, prior T1 volumes over volume lookback) >= volume-score threshold, otherwise no points; Volume Z-Score is (current volume − mean of prior volumes) / population standard deviation of prior volumes, and is zero when the prior sample has fewer than the minimum-sample-bars parameter or zero standard deviation.
Calculation: Risk distance = max(abs(entry price − buffered frozen sweep extreme), minimum-risk ATR × ATR(T1, ATR lookback) at sweep); buffered frozen sweep extreme is the lowest low from a low sweep through MSS minus stop-buffer ATR × ATR at sweep for long, or the highest high from a high sweep through MSS plus stop-buffer ATR × ATR at sweep for short.
Calculation: Opposing liquidity room = distance from entry price to the opposing extreme of the swept session range; use range high for long and range low for short.
Calculation: TP2 distance = min(TP2 risk multiple × risk distance, opposing liquidity room).
