Calculation: Step stochastic lines and state = Calculate on bars of T1, including its bar still forming. Let p be the median of MA(selected price source, filter period, average type), h, and l; h and l are MA(high, filter period, average type) and MA(low, filter period, average type) when range smoothing is enabled, otherwise high and low. MA is unfiltered when the filter period is at most one. Let u=max(p,h,l) and d=min(p,h,l). A is the sum over available k from 0 to ATR lookback−1 of max(u[k],d[k+1])−min(d[k],u[k+1]), divided by ATR lookback. Let amin and amax be the minimum and maximum of A over the current and previous range-window bars. Set qlow=fast multiplier×amin, qmid=fast multiplier×0.5×slow multiplier×(amax+amin), and qhigh=fast multiplier×amax. For each q, raw lower and upper bands are p−2q and p+2q. On each later bar, trend is up if p exceeds the previous raw upper band, down if p is below the previous raw lower band, and otherwise carries its prior value. Carry forward the prior trailing bands; in an up trend set the lower band to max(raw lower band, prior lower band) and line to lower band+q; in a down trend set the upper band to min(raw upper band, prior upper band) and line to upper band−q. The lower line is 100×(line(qlow)−(line(qhigh)−qhigh))/(2×qhigh); the middle line is 100×(line(qmid)−(line(qhigh)−qhigh))/(2×qhigh), or both are zero when qhigh is zero. Before the range-window warmup, state is neutral. State is bullish when lower line exceeds middle line, bearish when lower line is below middle line, and otherwise carries its prior value. Chart bars use the state of their containing T1 bar.
