Calculation: SNR levels = on completed bars of T1 (longest) through T4 (shortest), within the scan lookback, add resistance at (close of an older bullish candle + open of the next bearish candle)/2, support at the same midpoint for an older bearish candle followed by a bullish candle, bullish body-gap support at the midpoint between the older body high and next body low when the latter is higher, and bearish body-gap resistance at the midpoint between the older body low and next body high when the latter is lower; merge same-side levels within the equal-level tolerance, retaining the first price and combining gap flags.
Calculation: Confluence cluster = group same-side levels when the new level is within the confluence-zone distance of the cluster's representative level; update the representative level to the mean of its previous value and the new level, track the cluster's minimum and maximum levels, and count each distinct triggered layer once.
Calculation: Level rejection(side, level) = for long, low[1] <= level + touch buffer AND close[1] > level AND close[1] > open[1]; for short, high[1] >= level - touch buffer AND close[1] < level AND close[1] < open[1].
Calculation: Liquidity sweep(side) = for long, low[1] < the minimum low in the swing window ending two chart bars ago minus sweep buffer AND close[1] > that minimum; for short, high[1] > the maximum high in that window plus sweep buffer AND close[1] < that maximum.
Calculation: Engulfing(side) = bullish regular engulfing, where close[2] < open[2], close[1] > open[1], close[1] >= open[2], and open[1] <= close[2], OR bullish hidden engulfing, where close[1] > open[1], low[1] < low[2], and close[1] > (high[2] + low[2])/2; bearish engulfing uses the reversed regular conditions OR close[1] < open[1], high[1] > high[2], and close[1] < (high[2] + low[2])/2.
Calculation: MISS(side, level) = at least the minimum miss count in the MISS lookback window beginning two chart bars ago, where a short miss has high < level - touch buffer and distance from level <= the MISS proximity multiple of touch buffer, or a long miss has low > level + touch buffer and distance from level <= that proximity.
Calculation: Trendline confluence(side, level) = find the two most recent strict swing highs for short or swing lows for long in the trendline scan window, with each pivot exceeding or undercutting neighboring highs or lows by the swing-confirmation lookback; project the line through them to chart bar 1 and require its value within twice the touch buffer of close[1] or level.
Calculation: QML(side) = compare the highest high and lowest low in two equal-length, non-overlapping chart-bar windows ending before bar 1; short requires the nearer-window high to exceed the older-window high AND close[1] below the older-window low; long requires the nearer-window low below the older-window low AND close[1] above the older-window high.
Calculation: Breakout-retest(side, level) = a chart bar in the breakout lookback window opened on one side of level and closed across it, AND bar 1 retests within the touch buffer and closes beyond level on the breakout side.
Calculation: CRT(side) = on bars of T6, using completed bars only, long requires low[1] < low[2] - sweep buffer AND low[2] < close[1] < high[2]; short requires high[1] > high[2] + sweep buffer AND low[2] < close[1] < high[2].
Calculation: Triggered layers at a cluster = touch rejection; touch rejection AND liquidity sweep; touch rejection AND MISS; touch rejection AND engulfing; a body-gap level AND touch rejection AND engulfing; touch rejection AND trendline confluence; touch rejection AND QML; touch rejection AND QML AND engulfing; breakout-retest AND trendline confluence; touch rejection AND CRT.
Calculation: PD position = clamp((current bid/ask midpoint - lowest low)/(highest high - lowest low), 0, 1), using the PD lookback of completed bars on T5; use 0.50 if the range is invalid.
Calculation: Narrow range = highest high minus lowest low over the narrow-range lookback of completed chart bars, including bar 1, is at most the range-width threshold. Range edge = for long, current bid/ask midpoint <= range low + range width times the range-edge fraction; for short, midpoint >= range high - range width times that fraction.
Calculation: Expansion = highest high minus lowest low over the expansion lookback of completed chart bars is at least the expansion-range threshold OR abs(close[1] - open[1]) is at least the expansion-body threshold.
Calculation: Recent break-hold = using the prior chart range from bars 2 through the break-hold lookback, at least one bar in the break-hold validity window has body at least the break-hold body threshold and closes beyond the range by the break-hold buffer while its opposite extreme remains within that buffer of the range boundary; long uses close above the prior high and low >= prior high - buffer, short uses close below the prior low and high <= prior low + buffer.
Calculation: Momentum confirmations = breakout when close[1] exceeds the momentum prior-range high plus momentum buffer for long or is below the prior-range low minus buffer for short; displacement when abs(close[1] - open[1]) meets the momentum body threshold; FVG when low[1] > high[3] for long or high[1] < low[3] for short; hold when low[1] >= prior-range high - momentum buffer for long or high[1] <= prior-range low + buffer for short; HTF pressure when close[1] minus close at the momentum lookback on T7 is at least the momentum move threshold for long or at most its negative for short. T7 values are taken from completed T7 bars on chart bars.
Calculation: HTF alignment = close[1] minus close at the separate long-cycle lookback on T8 meets its long-cycle move threshold AND the same completed-bar change on the shorter T9 meets its separate shorter-cycle threshold for long; for short, the T9 change is at most its negative threshold OR the T8 change meets its positive threshold. Values come from completed bars on T8 and T9 on chart bars.
Calculation: Strong-trend break(side) = on chart bars, close[1] breaks the high or low of the strong-trend prior range, excluding bar 1, by the strong-trend break buffer AND abs(close[1] - open[1]) meets the strong-trend body threshold AND HTF alignment; long also requires PD position <= the strong-trend buy threshold.
