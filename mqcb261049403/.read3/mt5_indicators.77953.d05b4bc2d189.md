Calculation: session high = maximum high of chart bars whose datetimes fall within each enabled configured session instance, finalized when that instance ends
Calculation: session low = minimum low of chart bars whose datetimes fall within each enabled configured session instance, finalized when that instance ends
Short: on a closed chart bar, high > the most recent completed session high for that session AND close < that session high AND the level is active (the next instance has not started, no prior bar since completion with high above the level and close at or above it when close-break cancellation is enabled, and no prior sweep when first-sweep-only is enabled)
Long: on a closed chart bar, low < the most recent completed session low for that session AND close > that session low AND the level is active (the next instance has not started, no prior bar since completion with low below the level and close at or below it when close-break cancellation is enabled, and no prior sweep when first-sweep-only is enabled)
