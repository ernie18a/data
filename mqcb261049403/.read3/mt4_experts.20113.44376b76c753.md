Calculation: highest entry price(side) = max(entry price of open positions on side)
Calculation: lowest entry price(side) = min(entry price of open positions on side)
Long: on each new chart bar, long trading is enabled AND MA(method, applied price, shorter lookback, short MA shift, T1, current forming bar) > MA(method, applied price, longer lookback, long MA shift, T2, current forming bar) AND (no open short position OR hedging is enabled OR close-all-at-MA-crossover is enabled) AND (no open long position OR (averaging up is enabled AND close > highest entry price(long) × (1 + step size percentage / 100)) OR (averaging down is enabled AND close < lowest entry price(long) × (1 − step size percentage × averaging-down multiplier / 100)))
Short: on each new chart bar, short trading is enabled AND MA(method, applied price, longer lookback, long MA shift, T2, current forming bar) > MA(method, applied price, shorter lookback, short MA shift, T1, current forming bar) AND (no open long position OR hedging is enabled OR close-all-at-MA-crossover is enabled) AND (no open short position OR (averaging up is enabled AND close < lowest entry price(short) × (1 − step size percentage / 100)) OR (averaging down is enabled AND close > highest entry price(short) × (1 + step size percentage × averaging-down multiplier / 100)))
Exit: long, short trading is disabled AND close-all-at-MA-crossover is enabled AND MA(method, applied price, longer lookback, long MA shift, T2, current forming bar) > MA(method, applied price, shorter lookback, short MA shift, T1, current forming bar)
Exit: short, long trading is disabled AND close-all-at-MA-crossover is enabled AND MA(method, applied price, shorter lookback, short MA shift, T1, current forming bar) > MA(method, applied price, longer lookback, long MA shift, T2, current forming bar)
