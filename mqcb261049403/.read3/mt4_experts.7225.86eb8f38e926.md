Calculation: WPR swing direction(T1) = +1 when the latest chart high at a WPR swing-high index minus the previous chart high at a WPR swing-high index is at least the ATR multiplier times ATR(T1, ATR period, latest swing-high shift) AND the latest chart low at a WPR swing-low index is above the previous chart low at a WPR swing-low index; -1 when the previous chart low at a WPR swing-low index minus the latest chart low at a WPR swing-low index is at least the ATR multiplier times ATR(T1, ATR period, latest swing-low shift) AND the previous chart high at a WPR swing-high index is above the latest chart high at a WPR swing-high index; otherwise 0. Find the two latest indices of each kind by scanning T1 bars from the forming bar toward older bars: a swing high is the highest T1 high from the previous swing index through the next crossing at or below the lower Williams %R threshold after Williams %R reaches the upper threshold; a swing low is the lowest T1 low from the previous swing index through the next crossing at or above the upper threshold after Williams %R reaches the lower threshold. The upper and lower thresholds are respectively minus the WPR threshold and the WPR threshold minus the Williams %R range; use the separate WPR period and threshold parameters. The final high and low comparisons use chart bars at those indices; ATR uses T1 bars.
Calculation: Aggregate trend score(T1) = the arithmetic mean of WPR swing direction(T1), NRTR_GATOR(first parameter, second parameter, third parameter, output buffer 7, T1 forming bar), and NRTR_GATOR(first parameter, second parameter, third parameter, output buffer 6, T1 last completed bar). Use the same three separate NRTR_GATOR parameters across calls and timeframes. T2 and T3 are higher timeframes than T1, using separate timeframe-step parameters, with T3 farther above T1 than T2.
Calculation: TP/SL ratio(T1) = when aggregate trend score(T1) equals 1, set target distance to the greater of the two latest T1 swing highs minus the greater of the two latest T1 swing lows, and stop distance to close minus the greater swing low; otherwise set target distance to the lesser of the two latest T1 swing highs minus the lesser of the two latest T1 swing lows, and stop distance to the lesser swing high minus close. If stop distance is nonpositive and target distance exceeds the minimum-target-in-spreads parameter times spread in price units, the ratio is the fallback-ratio parameter; if stop distance is zero in all other cases, the ratio is undefined; otherwise it is (target distance minus spread in price units) divided by stop distance.
Long: WPR swing direction(T1) = 1 AND (aggregate trend score(T1) × aggregate trend score(T2) = 1 OR aggregate trend score(T1) × aggregate trend score(T3) = 1) AND TP/SL ratio(T1) >= TP/SL threshold.
Short: WPR swing direction(T1) = -1 AND (aggregate trend score(T1) × aggregate trend score(T2) = 1 OR aggregate trend score(T1) × aggregate trend score(T3) = 1) AND TP/SL ratio(T1) >= TP/SL threshold.
