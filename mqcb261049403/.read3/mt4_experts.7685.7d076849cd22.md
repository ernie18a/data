Calculation: Up-fractal trigger = if Williams up fractal at the configured center lag and side lookback is present on completed chart bars, then low[0] - fractal offset * minimum tick size, else zero
Calculation: Down-fractal trigger = if Williams down fractal at the configured center lag and side lookback is present on completed chart bars, then high[0] + fractal offset * minimum tick size, else zero
Calculation: Pivot = arithmetic mean of highest high, lowest low, and close at the selected lag on chart bars; on the first calculation, the range starts at round(minutes since midnight / chart-period minutes), ends at start plus the initial range-end offset, and close is taken at start; thereafter, the range starts at the configured lag of the latest completed bar and ends at the configured range-end lag, with close taken at a separately configured lag
Calculation: Long position count = count of open long positions in the traded instrument
Calculation: Short position count = count of open short positions in the traded instrument
Calculation: Total open position count = long position count + short position count
Calculation: Long open profit = sum of open long-position profit in the traded instrument
Calculation: Short open profit = sum of open short-position profit in the traded instrument
Calculation: Rounded combined open profit = round(long open profit + short open profit)
