Calculation: VWAP = VWAP(close, volume, rolling lookback)
Calculation: Weighted standard deviation = sqrt(sum(volume * (close - VWAP)^2, rolling lookback) / sum(volume, rolling lookback))
Calculation: High velocity = elapsed time across the velocity sample is greater than zero and less than the velocity limit
Calculation: Candle ratios = body ratio abs(close - open)/(high - low); upper wick ratio (high - max(open, close))/(high - low); lower wick ratio (min(open, close) - low)/(high - low); close location (close - low)/(high - low)
Calculation: Local score = min(maximum score, weighted average of directionally aligned enabled timeframe fraction; clamped direction-adjusted deviation Z/normalization; clamped sweep depth/max(ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times depth ratio, fallback depth); clamped snapback/minimum snapback; velocity score; fraction of applicable candle structure checks passed; ATR quality; spread quality; and session quality, using separate parameters for each factor weight and normalizing by the sum of weights. ATR quality is zero for nonpositive ATR, scaled below the minimum ATR, reduced above an enabled maximum ATR, and otherwise one; spread quality is clamped one minus spread/maximum spread when the maximum is positive, otherwise one; session quality is higher during its configured hour interval and lower outside it.
Calculation: External model confidence = rounded weighted sum of (snapback at least minimum snapback and high velocity), (direction-adjusted deviation Z above its threshold), (at least one of the T4 or T3 last completed closes aligned with the trade side relative to its moving average), and (directional T1 last completed candle wick ratio at least its threshold), with separate parameters for the weights, thresholds, and response precision
Calculation: Initial stop distance = fixed stop distance or ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times stop multiplier
Calculation: Initial target distance = fixed target distance or ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) times target multiplier
Calculation: Long protective stop = the highest of entry price minus initial stop distance, entry price after the break-even trigger, and the highest price since entry minus trailing distance after the trailing trigger and step conditions are met
Calculation: Short protective stop = the lowest of entry price plus initial stop distance, entry price after the break-even trigger, and the lowest price since entry plus trailing distance after the trailing trigger and step conditions are met
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus the greater of initial target distance and VWAP at entry minus entry price when the VWAP target is enabled and above entry; otherwise entry price plus initial target distance
Calculation: Short target = entry price minus the greater of initial target distance and entry price minus VWAP at entry when the VWAP target is enabled and below entry; otherwise entry price minus initial target distance
Long: During an active lower-band sweep, price first falls below VWAP minus deviation multiplier times weighted standard deviation while high velocity; then price reaches the lowest price since that breach plus minimum snapback and optional extra movement, before the sweep resets; spread is within its maximum; time is within a configured session and outside any enabled rollover exclusion; ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) is at least its minimum and no greater than its enabled maximum; every enabled T4, T3, and T2 last completed close is above its respective moving average; if enabled, the last completed T2 candle is bullish; the last completed T1 lower wick ratio is at least its minimum, its body ratio is at least its minimum when that filter is enabled, it is bullish when candle bias is required, and its close location is at least its minimum when close location is required; and the bridge permits BUY (disabled bridge, local mode with local score at least its minimum, web mode with a BUY verdict and confidence at least its minimum or an allowed local fallback, or hybrid mode with matching BUY verdicts, local score at least its minimum, and confidence at least its minimum; unavailable web response uses local fallback only when fallback is not block)
Short: During an active upper-band sweep, price first rises above VWAP plus deviation multiplier times weighted standard deviation while high velocity; then price reaches the highest price since that breach minus minimum snapback and optional extra movement, before the sweep resets; spread is within its maximum; time is within a configured session and outside any enabled rollover exclusion; ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) is at least its minimum and no greater than its enabled maximum; every enabled T4, T3, and T2 last completed close is below its respective moving average; if enabled, the last completed T2 candle is bearish; the last completed T1 upper wick ratio is at least its minimum, its body ratio is at least its minimum when that filter is enabled, it is bearish when candle bias is required, and its close location is no greater than one minus its minimum when close location is required; and the bridge permits SELL (disabled bridge, local mode with local score at least its minimum, web mode with a SELL verdict and confidence at least its minimum or an allowed local fallback, or hybrid mode with matching SELL verdicts, local score at least its minimum, and confidence at least its minimum; unavailable web response uses local fallback only when fallback is not block)
Long: External model returns BUY when external model confidence is at least its decision threshold
Short: External model returns SELL when external model confidence is at least its decision threshold
Exit: long, low <= long protective stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short protective stop OR low <= short target
