Calculation: HPF = Hodrick–Prescott filter of the selected applied price, using the filter lookback and smoothing parameter, fitted on T1 bars including the forming bar; map its values to chart bars.
Long: The HPF curve fitted for the current T1 bar is nondecreasing across the absolute trend length when that length is nonzero, or its endpoint is at least the previous T1 bar’s endpoint when the length is zero; AND (the previous T1 bar was Long-marked OR (the current endpoint is at least the previous T1 bar’s endpoint AND the selected applied price is at least the current endpoint)).
Short: The HPF curve fitted for the current T1 bar is nonincreasing across the absolute trend length when that length is nonzero, or its endpoint is at most the previous T1 bar’s endpoint when the length is zero; AND (the previous T1 bar was Short-marked OR (the current endpoint is at most the previous T1 bar’s endpoint AND the selected applied price is at most the current endpoint)).
