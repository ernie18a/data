Calculation: Rounded price = round(current chart-bar close, rounding precision)
Calculation: Long level = Rounded price + level offset
Calculation: Short level = Rounded price - level offset
Calculation: Long take-profit price = entry price + take-profit distance + extension distance if, at entry, RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T2 current-forming bar) is above the lower target-RAVI bound and below the upper target-RAVI bound and rises strictly from each preceding completed T2 bar through the current-forming T2 bar over the target-trend lookback; otherwise entry price + take-profit distance; while open, replace it with entry price + revised target distance if current close < entry price, any pair in the trailing T3 scan separated by the pattern lag has high at the later bar - low at the earlier bar > trailing pattern distance and close at the earlier bar < open at the later bar, and the current take-profit price > entry price + trailing eligibility distance
Calculation: Short take-profit price = entry price - take-profit distance - extension distance if, at entry, RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T2 current-forming bar) is below the negative lower target-RAVI bound and above the negative upper target-RAVI bound and falls strictly from each preceding completed T2 bar through the current-forming T2 bar over the target-trend lookback; otherwise entry price - take-profit distance; while open, replace it with entry price - revised target distance if current close > entry price, any pair in the trailing T3 scan separated by the pattern lag has high at the earlier bar - low at the later bar > trailing pattern distance and close at the earlier bar > open at the later bar, and the current take-profit price < entry price - trailing eligibility distance
Long: On chart bars using T1, T2, T3, and T4 values, with lag zero forming and positive lags completed: (RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T1 current-forming bar) < 0 OR any high in the bonus completed-T1 lookback > Long level + bonus excursion) AND T3 last completed close > Long level AND T1 current-forming low and last completed low > Rounded price + core buffer AND preceding completed T1 low > Rounded price AND no high in the spike T3 lookback > Long level + spike distance AND (RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T2 current-forming bar) >= -RAVI cutoff OR no pair in the entry-pattern T3 scan separated by the pattern lag has high at the later bar - low at the earlier bar > entry-pattern distance AND open at the later bar > close at the earlier bar) AND any high in the cross T1 lookback > Long level + cross excursion AND any high in the consolidation T4 lookback >= Long level + consolidation distance
Short: On chart bars using T1, T2, T3, and T4 values, with lag zero forming and positive lags completed: (RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T1 current-forming bar) > 0 OR any low in the bonus completed-T1 lookback < Short level - bonus excursion) AND T3 last completed close < Short level AND T1 current-forming high and last completed high < Rounded price - core buffer AND preceding completed T1 high < Rounded price AND no low in the spike T3 lookback < Short level - spike distance AND (RAVI(open, shorter SMA lookback, longer SMA lookback, T2 current-forming bar) <= RAVI cutoff OR no pair in the entry-pattern T3 scan separated by the pattern lag has high at the earlier bar - low at the later bar > entry-pattern distance AND close at the earlier bar > open at the later bar) AND any low in the cross T1 lookback < Short level - cross excursion AND any low in the consolidation T4 lookback <= Short level - consolidation distance
Exit: Long, chart-bar low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR chart-bar high >= Long take-profit price
Exit: Short, chart-bar high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR chart-bar low <= Short take-profit price
