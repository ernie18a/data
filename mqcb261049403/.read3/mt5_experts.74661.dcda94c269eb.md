Calculation: Rounded %D = round(Stochastic %D(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, smoothing method, price field), instrument decimal places minus precision adjustment)
Long: test mode enabled AND no open positions AND Stochastic %D(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, smoothing method, price field) crosses above (test midpoint - offset)
Short: test mode enabled AND no open positions AND Stochastic %D(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, smoothing method, price field) crosses below (test midpoint + offset)
