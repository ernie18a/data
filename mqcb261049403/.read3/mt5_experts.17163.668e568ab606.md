Short: (flat position AND close[1] < open[1] AND high[1] > SMA(close, lookback, current forming chart bar) AND Chaikin Oscillator(shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, tick volume, last completed chart bar) > opening threshold) OR (long position AND close[1] < open[1] AND high[1] > SMA(close, lookback, current forming chart bar) AND Chaikin Oscillator(shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, tick volume, last completed chart bar) > locking threshold)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
