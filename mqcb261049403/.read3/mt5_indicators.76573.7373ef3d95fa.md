Long: close > latest confirmed PivotHigh(high, lookback), where the pivot high is strictly above the lookback newer highs and at least as high as the lookback older highs, AND that pivot has not previously been exceeded by a close since confirmation; evaluate on the last completed chart bar when closed-bar confirmation is enabled, otherwise on the forming chart bar
Short: close < latest confirmed PivotLow(low, lookback), where the pivot low is strictly below the lookback newer lows and at most as low as the lookback older lows, AND that pivot has not previously been fallen below by a close since confirmation; evaluate on the last completed chart bar when closed-bar confirmation is enabled, otherwise on the forming chart bar
