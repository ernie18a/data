Calculation: RSI stop line(step) = RSI(close, RSI lookback) on each T1 bar; initialize upper and lower bands to RSI ± band-width multiplier × step, retain the prior direction and bands, switch up when RSI exceeds the prior upper band and down when RSI falls below the prior lower band, otherwise retain direction; in an uptrend set lower band to max(current lower band, prior lower band) and output lower band + offset multiplier × step; in a downtrend set upper band to min(current upper band, prior upper band) and output upper band − offset multiplier × step. Fast and slow use separate step parameters, with fast step < slow step.
Long: RSI stop line(fast step, T1, previous completed bar) > RSI stop line(slow step, T1, previous completed bar) AND RSI stop line(fast step, T1, current bar at close) <= RSI stop line(slow step, T1, current bar at close)
Short: RSI stop line(fast step, T1, previous completed bar) < RSI stop line(slow step, T1, previous completed bar) AND RSI stop line(fast step, T1, current bar at close) >= RSI stop line(slow step, T1, current bar at close)
Exit: short, RSI stop line(fast step, T1, previous completed bar) > RSI stop line(slow step, T1, previous completed bar)
Exit: long, RSI stop line(fast step, T1, previous completed bar) < RSI stop line(slow step, T1, previous completed bar)
