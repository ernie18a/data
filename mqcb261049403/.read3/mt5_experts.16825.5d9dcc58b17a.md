Calculation: T4 volume totals = for positive lookback, sum tick volume of rising or falling bars over the current and recent window and over the immediately preceding equal-length window; the recent window includes the forming bar. For negative lookback, sum tick volume where absolute close-open body is at least the body-size threshold in the recent window and greater than the threshold in the preceding window. For zero lookback, use the current-bar volume proxy and its initialized reference.
Long: (CCI limit > 0 AND CCI(median price, lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) > 0 AND CCI(median price, lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) < CCI limit) OR (CCI limit <= 0 AND CCI(median price, lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) < CCI limit), AND low of the current forming bar of T1 > low of the previous completed bar of T1, AND low of the current forming bar of T2 > low of the previous completed bar of T2, AND low of the current forming bar of T3 > low of the previous completed bar of T3, AND current chart-bar close > high[1], AND high[2] > high[1], AND high[3] > high[2], AND ((lookback > 0 AND recent up-volume > preceding up-volume AND preceding up-volume > 0) OR (lookback < 0 AND recent threshold-qualified volume > preceding threshold-qualified volume AND preceding threshold-qualified volume > 0) OR (lookback = 0 AND current-bar volume proxy > initialized reference AND initialized reference > 0))
Short: CCI limit > 0 AND CCI(median price, lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) < 0 AND CCI(median price, lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) > -CCI limit, AND high of the current forming bar of T1 < high of the previous completed bar of T1, AND high of the current forming bar of T2 < high of the previous completed bar of T2, AND high of the current forming bar of T3 < high of the previous completed bar of T3, AND current chart-bar close < low[1], AND low[2] < low[1], AND low[3] < low[2], AND ((lookback > 0 AND recent down-volume > preceding down-volume AND preceding down-volume > 0) OR (lookback < 0 AND recent threshold-qualified volume > preceding threshold-qualified volume AND preceding threshold-qualified volume > 0) OR (lookback = 0 AND current-bar volume proxy > initialized reference AND initialized reference > 0))
Exit: long, (holding-time limit > 0 AND floor(elapsed seconds / 60) > holding-time limit) OR (Friday cutoff hour > 0 AND datetime is Friday AND hour > Friday cutoff hour)
Exit: short, (holding-time limit > 0 AND floor(elapsed seconds / 60) > holding-time limit) OR (Friday cutoff hour > 0 AND datetime is Friday AND hour > Friday cutoff hour)
Exit: long, reduction interval > 0 AND current price > position take-profit price - floor(floor(elapsed seconds / 60) / reduction interval) * pip size
Exit: short, reduction interval > 0 AND current price < position take-profit price + floor(floor(elapsed seconds / 60) / reduction interval) * pip size
