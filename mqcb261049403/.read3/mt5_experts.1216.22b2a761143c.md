Calculation: Upper rounding channel = MA(close, MA lookback, T1, bar) + range; range is ATR(ATR lookback, T1, bar) × channel multiplier at the start of a flat-MA run and is retained during that run; when MA changes, carry the prior upper value only if continuity is enabled, otherwise it is undefined
Calculation: Lower rounding channel = MA(close, MA lookback, T1, bar) − range; when MA changes, carry the prior lower value only if continuity is enabled, otherwise it is undefined
Long: close(T1, last completed bar) > upper rounding channel(T1, last completed bar) AND ((buy permission enabled AND upper rounding channel(T1, bar still forming) = 0) OR close(T1, bar still forming) ≤ upper rounding channel(T1, bar still forming))
Short: close(T1, last completed bar) < lower rounding channel(T1, last completed bar) AND ((sell permission enabled AND lower rounding channel(T1, bar still forming) = 0) OR close(T1, bar still forming) ≥ lower rounding channel(T1, bar still forming))
Exit: short, sell-close permission enabled AND close(T1, last completed bar) > upper rounding channel(T1, last completed bar) AND NOT ((buy permission enabled AND upper rounding channel(T1, bar still forming) = 0) OR close(T1, bar still forming) ≤ upper rounding channel(T1, bar still forming))
Exit: long, buy-close permission enabled AND close(T1, last completed bar) < lower rounding channel(T1, last completed bar) AND NOT ((sell permission enabled AND lower rounding channel(T1, bar still forming) = 0) OR close(T1, bar still forming) ≥ lower rounding channel(T1, bar still forming))
