Calculation: StepMA stochastic difference = for each T1 bar, compute weighted range as the sum over the range lookback of (1 + (lookback − bars ago)/lookback) × abs(high − low), divided by the lookback; take its running minimum and maximum from the first bar with sufficient history; calculate minimum, maximum, and midpoint step widths by rounding the step multiplier times the respective running range to the nearest price point; for each width, initialize upper and lower bands as low + 2 × width and high − 2 × width in high/low mode, or close + 2 × width and close − 2 × width in close mode, with trend zero; on each bar set trend up if close exceeds the previous upper band and down if close is below the previous lower band, otherwise retain trend; in an up trend, do not let the lower band fall below its previous value, and set the line to lower band + width; in a down trend, do not let the upper band rise above its previous value, and set the line to upper band − width; use the minimum, maximum, and midpoint widths for their respective lines; let r = minimum line + maximum width − (maximum line − maximum width), replacing zero r with 0.00000001; subtract the six-decimal rounded value of (midpoint line − (maximum line − maximum width))/r from the six-decimal rounded value of (minimum line − (maximum line − maximum width))/r
