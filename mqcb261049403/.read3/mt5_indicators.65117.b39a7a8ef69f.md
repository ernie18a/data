Calculation: squeeze = Keltner upper band using the Bollinger middle band as its basis, ATR(Keltner lookback), and the Keltner width parameter (or the Bollinger width parameter when the Keltner width is nonpositive) > Bollinger upper band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger width), on chart bars
Long: NOT squeeze AND (close > Bollinger upper band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger width) OR close > Bollinger middle band(close, Bollinger lookback)), on chart bars
Short: NOT squeeze AND (close < Bollinger lower band(close, Bollinger lookback, Bollinger width) OR close <= Bollinger middle band(close, Bollinger lookback)), on chart bars
Calculation: centered trigger = the selected trigger type's Stochastic main line(T1, %K lookback, %D lookback, slowing, SMA, close/close) minus its neutral-level parameter; CCI(close, CCI lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; MACD main(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; Momentum(close, Momentum lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; Williams %R(Williams %R lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; ADX(ADX lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter; or DeMarker(DeMarker lookback, T1) minus its neutral-level parameter, each on T1 bars with separate lookback and neutral-level parameters for each trigger type
Long: centered trigger is positive on chart bars using the corresponding T1 value, including the forming T1 bar
Short: centered trigger is nonpositive on chart bars using the corresponding T1 value, including the forming T1 bar
Long: zero-cross alerts are enabled AND centered trigger crosses above the above-alert threshold on the trigger-selected T1 bar (current or previous), evaluated on chart bars
Short: zero-cross alerts are enabled AND centered trigger crosses below the below-alert threshold on the trigger-selected T1 bar (current or previous), evaluated on chart bars
