Calculation: MA difference = SMA((high + low) / 2, shorter lookback, T1 bar) - EMA((high + low) / 2, longer lookback, T1 bar)
Long: On chart bars, MA difference on the containing T1 bar still forming > MA difference on the preceding completed T1 bar
Short: On chart bars, MA difference on the containing T1 bar still forming < MA difference on the preceding completed T1 bar
