Calculation: Grid volatility = mean(high - low, grid volatility lookback, T1 last completed bars), sampled on chart bars from T1 values
Calculation: Stop volatility = mean(high - low, stop volatility lookback, T1 last completed bars), sampled on chart bars from T1 values
Calculation: Grid step(side, count) = side-specific manual step when positive; otherwise, for the first add, grid volatility × volatility step multiplier when volatility-based steps are enabled or the fixed initial step otherwise; for later adds, the gap between the two most recent same-side entry prices, optionally multiplied by the dynamic step multiplier from its start count onward
Calculation: Take-profit price(side, stage) = entry price for individual targets or lot-weighted average entry price for basket-average targets, adjusted by the target distance; the first entry uses the fixed initial target distance, later entries use the previous target distance, optionally multiplied by the dynamic target multiplier from its start stage onward and floored at the minimum target distance; in basket-average mode, the distance is zero from the break-even stage onward when enabled
Long: No long basket is open AND (CCI entry filter is disabled OR CCI(close, lookback) on chart bars is above zero on the last completed bar and below zero on the preceding completed bar) AND long entries are permitted AND (critical-level filter is disabled OR price is at or above the long critical level)
Short: No short basket is open AND (CCI entry filter is disabled OR CCI(close, lookback) on chart bars is below zero on the last completed bar and above zero on the preceding completed bar) AND short entries are permitted AND (critical-level filter is disabled OR price is at or below the short critical level)
Long: A long basket is open AND its count is below the configured long maximum AND low < last long entry price - Grid step(long, count) AND the projected same-side margin plus absolute floating profit is at or below the risk limit AND applicable long-entry and critical-level filters permit the add
Short: A short basket is open AND its count is below the configured short maximum AND high > last short entry price + Grid step(short, count) AND the projected same-side margin plus absolute floating profit is at or below the risk limit AND applicable short-entry and critical-level filters permit the add
Exit: Long, high >= Take-profit price(long, stage)
Exit: Short, low <= Take-profit price(short, stage)
Exit: Long, abs(close[1] - close[N]) > Stop volatility × volatility threshold AND (close[1] < close[N] OR volatility-stop mode closes all sides)
Exit: Short, abs(close[1] - close[N]) > Stop volatility × volatility threshold AND (close[1] > close[N] OR volatility-stop mode closes all sides)
Exit: Long, critical-level stop is enabled AND price < long critical level AND critical-level stop mode closes the side
Exit: Short, critical-level stop is enabled AND price > short critical level AND critical-level stop mode closes the side
