Calculation: TrendDirection = bullish if close > EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, forming bar) + upper band OR close is within the upper and lower bands and EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, forming bar)[0] - EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, forming bar)[2] > slope threshold; bearish if close < that EMA - lower band OR close is within the bands and the EMA difference < -slope threshold; otherwise neutral; T1 values are sampled on chart bars.
Calculation: Trending = abs(EMA(close, shorter chart lookback, forming bar) - EMA(close, longer chart lookback, forming bar)) > separation threshold AND ATR(chart lookback, forming bar) > volatility multiplier × mean(ATR(chart lookback), current and two previous chart bars); otherwise Ranging.
Calculation: FibRetracement = highest(high, swing lookback, chart bars) - (highest(high, swing lookback, chart bars) - lowest(low, swing lookback, chart bars)) × retracement parameter / 100.
Calculation: StrongSRLevel(price, side) = the first active support or resistance level, ordered by descending strength, whose strength meets the minimum and whose distance from price is within the zone buffer; levels come from local low or high pivots in the S/R lookback, same-side pivots within the zone buffer are grouped, strength is touch count multiplied by an age weight, and only the strongest levels up to the configured limit are retained.
Calculation: ParallelChannel = linear regression of chart-bar highs and lows over the channel lookback; the channel is active when both absolute correlations exceed the correlation threshold and the absolute slope difference is below the parallelism threshold; channel support and resistance are the low and high regression intercepts.
Calculation: BasketAverageEntryPrice = sum(entry price × position volume) / sum(position volume).
Calculation: LongTrailingStop = max(BasketAverageEntryPrice, highest price since basket opened - trailing distance); ShortTrailingStop = min(BasketAverageEntryPrice, lowest price since basket opened + trailing distance).
Long: enhanced entry enabled AND ranging AND channel trading enabled AND ParallelChannel active AND abs(close - channel support) <= zone buffer AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) < oversold threshold).
Short: enhanced entry enabled AND ranging AND channel trading enabled AND ParallelChannel active AND abs(close - channel resistance) <= zone buffer AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) > overbought threshold).
Long: enhanced entry enabled AND trending AND bullish TrendDirection AND StrongSRLevel(close, support) exists AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) < oversold threshold).
Short: enhanced entry enabled AND trending AND bearish TrendDirection AND StrongSRLevel(close, resistance) exists AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) > overbought threshold).
Long: enhanced entry enabled AND StrongSRLevel(close, resistance) exists AND breakout distance above that resistance is between the minimum and maximum breakout distances AND TrendDirection is not bearish AND (momentum filter disabled OR ATR(chart lookback, forming bar) >= minimum volatility) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) <= upper breakout RSI limit).
Short: enhanced entry enabled AND StrongSRLevel(close, support) exists AND breakout distance below that support is between the minimum and maximum breakout distances AND TrendDirection is not bullish AND (momentum filter disabled OR ATR(chart lookback, forming bar) >= minimum volatility) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) >= lower breakout RSI limit).
Long: enhanced entry disabled AND ranging AND bullish TrendDirection AND abs(close - FibRetracement(third retracement parameter)) <= Fibonacci entry distance AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) < oversold threshold).
Short: enhanced entry disabled AND ranging AND bearish TrendDirection AND abs(close - FibRetracement(third retracement parameter)) <= Fibonacci entry distance AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback, forming chart bar) > overbought threshold).
Long: simple entry enabled AND bullish TrendDirection AND ATR(chart lookback, forming bar) <= maximum ATR.
Short: simple entry enabled AND bearish TrendDirection AND ATR(chart lookback, forming bar) <= maximum ATR.
Long: Fibonacci grid enabled AND basket side is long AND FibRetracement(each of the four separate retracement parameters) < close.
Short: Fibonacci grid enabled AND basket side is short AND FibRetracement(each of the four separate retracement parameters) > close.
Exit: long, close - BasketAverageEntryPrice >= take-profit distance.
Exit: short, BasketAverageEntryPrice - close >= take-profit distance.
Exit: long, trailing enabled AND breakeven has activated after profit reached the breakeven trigger AND close <= LongTrailingStop.
Exit: short, trailing enabled AND breakeven has activated after profit reached the breakeven trigger AND close >= ShortTrailingStop.
