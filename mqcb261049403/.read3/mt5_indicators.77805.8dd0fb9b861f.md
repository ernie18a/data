Calculation: ChartEMAAlignmentScore(direction) = count of adjacent aligned pairs in five chart-bar EMA(close, separate lookbacks ordered shortest to longest), with shorter EMA above the next for long and below the next for short
Calculation: HigherTimeframeTrendBias(T) = up when EMA(close, shorter lookback, T, last completed bar) > EMA(close, middle lookback, T, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T, last completed bar); down for the reverse ordering; neutral otherwise
Calculation: CandleMeasures(bar) = range high − low; body absolute(close − open); lower wick min(open, close) − low; upper wick high − max(open, close)
Calculation: TouchPrice(direction) = min(current and preceding chart-bar lows) for long; max(current and preceding chart-bar highs) for short
Calculation: PriceLevelSet = at each closed chart bar, valid round levels based on floor(close / round interval) × round interval plus integer offsets from the configured lower to upper offset bounds, with round interval equal to a separate interval parameter × pip size; confirmed chart-bar swing highs and lows within a separate lookback, with candidates at least the separate swing-strength number of bars old, highs strictly above preceding strength highs and at least following strength highs, and lows strictly below preceding strength lows and at most following strength lows; the three longest chart-bar EMAs; upper and lower Bollinger Bands(close, separate period and deviation parameters); on each enabled T1, T2, and T3, the three higher-timeframe EMAs and the configured number of latest confirmed swing highs and lows using a shared higher-timeframe swing-strength parameter; on T2, four Fibonacci levels using separate increasing retracement fractions from the highest high and lowest low of the configured number of last completed T2 bars, only when their range is at least a separate minimum ATR multiple of current chart-bar ATR, subtracting fractions of the range from the high if the low occurred earlier than the high and otherwise adding them to the low, with the latest occurrence used for tied extrema; and the high and low of the last completed T4 bar. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed bars as of the current chart-bar close; unavailable levels are omitted. Lower Bollinger levels are long-side, upper Bollinger levels short-side, and other levels neutral.
Calculation: DistinctKindsNearPrice(price, side) = number of distinct categories in PriceLevelSet within the configured zone-width ATR multiple of price, counting only neutral levels or levels assigned to side
Calculation: HigherTimeframeKindsNearPrice(price, side) = number of distinct higher-timeframe categories in PriceLevelSet within the configured zone-width ATR multiple of price, counting only neutral levels or levels assigned to side; categories are higher-timeframe EMAs, higher-timeframe swings, T2 Fibonacci levels, and T4 high/low
Calculation: SpreadInPrice = bar spread points × instrument point size, using current quoted spread for the latest chart bar when its bar spread is nonpositive
Calculation: StopDistanceBeforeSpread(direction) = max(configured minimum-stop ATR fraction × chart-bar ATR, absolute(current close − TouchPrice(direction)) + separate stop-buffer ATR fraction × chart-bar ATR)
Calculation: TotalRiskDistance(direction) = StopDistanceBeforeSpread(direction) + SpreadInPrice
Calculation: AdjustedTarget(direction) = nearest PriceLevelSet price in the trade direction from current close, farther away than the zone width and no farther than the configured maximum target ATR multiple × chart-bar ATR, whose level side is neutral or opposite the trade direction and which has at least the configured target-kind count under DistinctKindsNearPrice(price, opposite direction); move that price toward current close by a separate target-buffer ATR fraction × chart-bar ATR; undefined if no such price exists
Calculation: NetReward(direction) = distance from current close to AdjustedTarget(direction) measured in the trade direction − SpreadInPrice
Long: On each closed chart bar with valid chart-bar ATR, current high − low is at least the configured minimum candle-range ATR multiple × ATR(chart bars, configured lookback); the session filter is disabled or the chart-bar opening hour in broker-server time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive session (wrapping across midnight when start is at or after end); no long or short signal occurred in the preceding configured number of chart bars; the trend filter is disabled or ChartEMAAlignmentScore(long) is at least the configured minimum score; (current-bar range > 0 and current lower wick ≥ the configured hammer/star body-wick multiple × current body and ≥ the configured minimum dominant-wick range fraction × current range and current upper wick ≤ the configured maximum opposite-wick range fraction × current range) OR (current close > current open and preceding close < preceding open and current close ≥ preceding open and current open ≤ preceding close) OR (the same hammer wick conditions hold on the preceding bar and current close > current open and current close > preceding close); and the MTF filter is off or, for each enabled T2 and T3, its last completed bar is available or missing-data blocking is disabled, and each available bar has a bias that is not down in no-counter mode or is up in aligned mode; and DistinctKindsNearPrice(TouchPrice(long), long) and HigherTimeframeKindsNearPrice(TouchPrice(long), long) meet their separate configured minimums; StopDistanceBeforeSpread(long) ≤ the configured maximum-stop ATR multiple × chart-bar ATR; the spread-ratio filter is disabled or SpreadInPrice ≤ its configured maximum fraction × TotalRiskDistance(long); AdjustedTarget(long) is defined; NetReward(long) > 0; and NetReward(long) / TotalRiskDistance(long) ≥ the configured minimum reward/risk ratio
Short: On each closed chart bar with valid chart-bar ATR, current high − low is at least the configured minimum candle-range ATR multiple × ATR(chart bars, configured lookback); the session filter is disabled or the chart-bar opening hour in broker-server time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive session (wrapping across midnight when start is at or after end); no long or short signal occurred in the preceding configured number of chart bars; the trend filter is disabled or ChartEMAAlignmentScore(short) is at least the configured minimum score; (current-bar range > 0 and current upper wick ≥ the configured hammer/star body-wick multiple × current body and ≥ the configured minimum dominant-wick range fraction × current range and current lower wick ≤ the configured maximum opposite-wick range fraction × current range) OR (current close < current open and preceding close > preceding open and current close ≤ preceding open and current open ≥ preceding close) OR (the same shooting-star wick conditions hold on the preceding bar and current close < current open and current close < preceding close); and the MTF filter is off or, for each enabled T2 and T3, its last completed bar is available or missing-data blocking is disabled, and each available bar has a bias that is not up in no-counter mode or is down in aligned mode; and DistinctKindsNearPrice(TouchPrice(short), short) and HigherTimeframeKindsNearPrice(TouchPrice(short), short) meet their separate configured minimums; StopDistanceBeforeSpread(short) ≤ the configured maximum-stop ATR multiple × chart-bar ATR; the spread-ratio filter is disabled or SpreadInPrice ≤ its configured maximum fraction × TotalRiskDistance(short); AdjustedTarget(short) is defined; NetReward(short) > 0; NetReward(short) / TotalRiskDistance(short) ≥ the configured minimum reward/risk ratio; and the complete Long condition is false on this bar
