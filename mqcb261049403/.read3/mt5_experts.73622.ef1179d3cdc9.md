Calculation: Volatility ratio = 0.5 if fewer than volatility lookback + 1 T1 bars are available or the population standard deviation of the returns is zero; otherwise abs(mean(returns) / population standard deviation(returns)), where return[i] = ln(close(T1, forming bar, i)) - ln(close(T1, forming bar, i+1)) for i = 0 through volatility lookback - 1
Calculation: Signal strength = the sum of each enabled module's contribution: RSI weight × adaptive RSI coefficient × (RSI(close, T1, RSI lookback, forming bar) - 50) / 50; ADX weight × adaptive ADX coefficient × (ADX(high, low, close, T1, ADX lookback, forming bar) - 20) / 30; moving average weight × adaptive moving average coefficient × (SMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, forming bar) - SMA(close, longer lookback, T1, forming bar)) / price point; volatility weight × adaptive volatility coefficient × volatility ratio; and state weight × adaptive state coefficient × (random integer from 0 through state count - 1 - state count / 2) / state count
Long: Signal strength > signal threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio >= volatility threshold)
Short: Signal strength < -signal threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio >= volatility threshold)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × stop-loss multiplier OR high >= entry price + max(ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × take-profit multiplier, ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × minimum take-profit multiplier)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × stop-loss multiplier OR low <= entry price - max(ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × take-profit multiplier, ATR(T1, ATR lookback, forming bar at entry) × minimum take-profit multiplier)
