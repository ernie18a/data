Calculation: Sentiment Zone Oscillator = MA(sign(filtered price - filtered price[1]), SZO lookback, SZO method), where filtered price = MA(selected OHLC-derived price, price-filter lookback, price-filter method)
Calculation: Effective level lookback = configured level lookback when greater than one bar; otherwise SZO lookback
Calculation: Rolling quantile(x,n,p) = linearly interpolated quantile of the current and previous x values at rank (n-1)×p/100, treating unavailable earlier values as zero
Calculation: Upper level(x,n,p) = Rolling quantile(x,n,p) for percentile levels; otherwise Lowest(x,n) + p×(Highest(x,n)-Lowest(x,n))/100
Calculation: Lower level(x,n,p) = Rolling quantile(x,n,p) for percentile levels; otherwise Lowest(x,n) + p×(Highest(x,n)-Lowest(x,n))/100
Calculation: Middle level(x,n,p_up,p_down) = Rolling quantile(x,n,(p_up+p_down)/2) for percentile levels; otherwise (Upper level(x,n,p_up)+Lower level(x,n,p_down))/2
Long: (color mode selects level thresholds AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator > Upper level(Sentiment Zone Oscillator, effective level lookback, upper percentile)) OR (color mode selects midpoint AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator > Middle level(Sentiment Zone Oscillator, effective level lookback, upper percentile, lower percentile)) OR (color mode selects slope AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator > Sentiment Zone Oscillator[1])
Short: (color mode selects level thresholds AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator < Lower level(Sentiment Zone Oscillator, effective level lookback, lower percentile)) OR (color mode selects midpoint AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator < Middle level(Sentiment Zone Oscillator, effective level lookback, upper percentile, lower percentile)) OR (color mode selects slope AND Sentiment Zone Oscillator < Sentiment Zone Oscillator[1])
