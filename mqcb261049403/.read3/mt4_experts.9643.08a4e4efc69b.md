Long: SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, current forming bar) - SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, current forming bar) >= one point AND SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 2 bars ago) - SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 2 bars ago) >= one point AND SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 5 bars ago) - SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 5 bars ago) >= one point AND previous chart bar close > previous chart bar open; short lookback < long lookback
Short: SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, current forming bar) - SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, current forming bar) >= one point AND SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 2 bars ago) - SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 2 bars ago) >= one point AND SMA(close, short lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 5 bars ago) - SMA(close, long lookback, MA shift parameter, T1, 5 bars ago) >= one point AND previous chart bar close < previous chart bar open; short lookback < long lookback
Exit: long position, low <= entry price - stop-loss parameter × point OR high >= entry price + take-profit parameter × point
Exit: short position, high >= entry price + stop-loss parameter × point OR low <= entry price - take-profit parameter × point
