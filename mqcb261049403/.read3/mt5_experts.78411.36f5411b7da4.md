Long: On the first completed T1 bar after the longer-lookback EMA warm-up, EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed bar), OR on a later completed T1 bar the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA and the latest earlier completed T1 bar with unequal EMAs had the shorter-lookback EMA below the longer-lookback EMA
Short: On the first completed T1 bar after the longer-lookback EMA warm-up, EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, last completed bar), OR on a later completed T1 bar the shorter-lookback EMA is below the longer-lookback EMA and the latest earlier completed T1 bar with unequal EMAs had the shorter-lookback EMA above the longer-lookback EMA
Exit: long, (take-profit distance > 0 AND current index bid - entry price >= take-profit distance) OR (stop-loss distance > 0 AND entry price - current index bid >= stop-loss distance)
Exit: short, (take-profit distance > 0 AND entry price - current index ask >= take-profit distance) OR (stop-loss distance > 0 AND current index ask - entry price >= stop-loss distance)
