Calculation: HA direction = compare (open[0]+close[0])/2 with (open[1]+high[1]+low[1]+close[1])/4 on T1 bars; shift 0 is still forming and shift 1 is the preceding bar. The calculation is read at each chart tick; direction is bullish when the first value is lower, bearish when it is higher, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend = at each chart tick, let F[i]=SMA(typical price, shorter MA lookback, T1, shift i) and S[i]=SMMA(median price, longer MA lookback, T1, shift i), where the shorter MA lookback is less than the longer one and shift 0 is still forming. Initialize max and min to F[1] and their indices to 0; scan i from n down to 0, replacing max/min and their indices only on strict greater/lesser values, where n is the short scan-count parameter. Set d=F[0]-S[0], dprev=F[1]-S[1], up=false, down=false. Scan j downward from m-1 to 0 on the first chart tick after T1's bar count increases, otherwise from n-1 to 0, where m is the long scan-count parameter and m>n. If max index>min index and F[j]>min, set up and clear down; if max index>min index and d<dprev, set down and clear up; if max index<min index and F[j]<max, set down and clear up; if max index<min index and d>dprev, set up and clear down. Return up, down, or neutral.
Long: HA direction is bullish AND Trend is up AND no long position is open AND (a short position is open OR the current hour differs from the latest recorded close hour).
Short: HA direction is bearish AND Trend is down AND no short position is open AND (a long position is open OR the current hour differs from the latest recorded close hour).
Exit: long, Trend is down AND open position profit plus swap and commission is greater than zero AND trailing stops are disabled.
Exit: short, Trend is up AND open position profit plus swap and commission is greater than zero AND trailing stops are disabled.
