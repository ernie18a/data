Calculation: scaled Williams %R(lookback, timeframe, bar) = 100 - ABS(Williams %R(lookback, timeframe, bar))
Calculation: profit distance = if automatic take profit and no minimum take profit, automatic target distance - spread - (small adjustment if spread <= spread cutoff, otherwise large adjustment); else if automatic take profit, minimum target distance; else 0
Long: on chart bars, at least K of the five comparisons OsMA(shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) > OsMA(shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, last completed bar) through T5 are true AND at least K of the five comparisons SMA(close, moving-average lookback, T1, forming bar) > SMA(close, moving-average lookback, T1, last completed bar) through T5 are true AND scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, forming bar) < upper threshold AND scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, forming bar) > scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, lookback, T4, forming bar) < upper threshold AND RSI(close, lookback, T4, forming bar) > RSI(close, lookback, T4, last completed bar) AND minute(datetime) modulo entry interval > entry delay; all T1–T5 values are sampled on chart bars
Short: on chart bars, at least K of the five comparisons OsMA(shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) < OsMA(shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, last completed bar) through T5 are true AND at least K of the five comparisons SMA(close, moving-average lookback, T1, forming bar) < SMA(close, moving-average lookback, T1, last completed bar) through T5 are true AND scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, forming bar) > lower threshold AND scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, forming bar) < scaled Williams %R(lookback, T4, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, lookback, T4, forming bar) > lower threshold AND RSI(close, lookback, T4, forming bar) < RSI(close, lookback, T4, last completed bar) AND minute(datetime) modulo entry interval > entry delay; all T1–T5 values are sampled on chart bars
Exit: long, close on current chart bar > entry price + profit distance AND high on current chart bar - close on current chart bar > long price-distance threshold
Exit: long, close on current chart bar > entry price + profit distance AND close on current chart bar < entry price - long price-distance threshold
Exit: short, close on current chart bar < entry price - profit distance AND close on current chart bar - low on current chart bar > short price-distance threshold
Exit: short, close on current chart bar < entry price - profit distance AND close on current chart bar > entry price + short price-distance threshold
