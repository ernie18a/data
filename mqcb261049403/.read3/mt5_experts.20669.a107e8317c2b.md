Calculation: Indicator direction = rising when the indicator value on a T1 bar is greater than its previous T1 bar, falling when lower, and unchanged when equal
Long: Indicator direction on the last completed T1 bar is rising AND indicator direction on the preceding completed T1 bar is not rising
Short: Indicator direction on the last completed T1 bar is falling AND indicator direction on the preceding completed T1 bar is not falling
Exit: short, indicator direction on the last completed T1 bar is rising AND indicator direction on the preceding completed T1 bar is rising
Exit: long, indicator direction on the last completed T1 bar is falling AND indicator direction on the preceding completed T1 bar is falling
