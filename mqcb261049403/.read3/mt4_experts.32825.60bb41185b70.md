Calculation: Relative price displacement = ((current chart-bar close - close of last completed T1 bar) / close of last completed T1 bar) * scale factor, calculated on chart bars
Calculation: Same-side average entry price = lot-weighted mean of open entry prices on that side
Long: (no long position AND long trading enabled AND previous chart-bar close < previous chart-bar open AND relative price displacement < -threshold) OR (a long position exists AND number of long positions < maximum position count AND current close <= most recent long entry price - grid spacing)
Short: (no short position AND short trading enabled AND previous chart-bar close > previous chart-bar open AND relative price displacement > threshold) OR (a short position exists AND number of short positions < maximum position count AND current close >= most recent short entry price + grid spacing)
Exit: Long, low <= most recent long entry price - stop-loss distance OR (high >= same-side average entry price + first-position target distance when one long position exists) OR (high >= same-side average entry price + multi-position target distance when more than one long position exists)
Exit: Short, high >= most recent short entry price + stop-loss distance OR (low <= same-side average entry price - first-position target distance when one short position exists) OR (low <= same-side average entry price - multi-position target distance when more than one short position exists)
