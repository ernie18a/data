Calculation: Candle range = high - low, or the minimum price increment when high = low
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close - open) / Candle range
Long: On the selected chart signal bar (current forming or last completed, according to entry mode), volume >= mean volume of the preceding volume-lookback chart bars * the aggressive volume threshold (use the alternate volume threshold when the chart timeframe is at least T1) AND Candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND Candle range >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum volatility multiplier AND close > EMA(close, long lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < upper RSI threshold AND close > high[1] + ATR(ATR lookback) * breakout buffer multiplier AND close > open AND, using chart bars and the current forming T2 bar, close(T2) > EMA(close, long lookback, T2, current forming bar)
Short: On the selected chart signal bar (current forming or last completed, according to entry mode), volume >= mean volume of the preceding volume-lookback chart bars * the aggressive volume threshold (use the alternate volume threshold when the chart timeframe is at least T1) AND Candle body ratio >= minimum body ratio AND Candle range >= ATR(ATR lookback) * minimum volatility multiplier AND close < EMA(close, long lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > lower RSI threshold AND close < low[1] - ATR(ATR lookback) * breakout buffer multiplier AND close < open AND, using chart bars and the current forming T2 bar, close(T2) < EMA(close, long lookback, T2, current forming bar)
