Calculation: NRTR direction = direction of the latest bullish or bearish trigger that passes the flat-distance condition, initially neutral
Calculation: prior bullish trigger low = low of the latest bullish trigger that passes the flat-distance condition, initially zero
Calculation: prior bearish trigger high = high of the latest bearish trigger that passes the flat-distance condition, initially zero
Long: SMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses above SMA(close, longer lookback) AND (RSI period disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI threshold) AND (confirmation disabled OR current high reaches the confirmation distance above the low of the pending bullish crossover bar) AND absolute(current signal-bar low - prior bullish trigger low) >= flat distance AND NRTR direction before the trigger is short or neutral; evaluate on chart bars, or on T1 bars including the still-forming bar, with T1 results mapped to chart bars
Short: SMA(close, shorter lookback) crosses below SMA(close, longer lookback) AND (RSI period disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI threshold) AND (confirmation disabled OR current low reaches the confirmation distance below the high of the pending bearish crossover bar AND current open >= current close) AND absolute(current signal-bar high - prior bearish trigger high) >= flat distance AND NRTR direction before the trigger is long or neutral; evaluate on chart bars, or on T1 bars including the still-forming bar, with T1 results mapped to chart bars
