Calculation: WATR bands = on each calculation bar, let uptrend mean the previous bar's upper band is defined and its close is above that band; let offset = K × point size + M × ATR(K). If (uptrend and close < open) or (!uptrend and close > open), carry both bands forward; otherwise, if uptrend, set upper band to max(previous upper band, close − offset) and lower band undefined; otherwise set lower band to min(previous lower band, close + offset) and upper band undefined. Then, if uptrend and close < upper band, set lower band to close + offset and upper band undefined; if !uptrend and close > lower band, set upper band to close − offset and lower band undefined. An undefined previous band imposes no max or min bound.
Long: WATR upper band on the current chart bar is defined AND WATR upper band on the previous chart bar is undefined
Short: WATR lower band on the current chart bar is defined AND WATR lower band on the previous chart bar is undefined
Long: on chart bars, WATR upper band on the mapped, still-forming T1 bar is defined AND its upper band on the preceding T1 bar is undefined
Short: on chart bars, WATR lower band on the mapped, still-forming T1 bar is defined AND its lower band on the preceding T1 bar is undefined
