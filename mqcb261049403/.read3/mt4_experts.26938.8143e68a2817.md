Calculation: CompositeRate(i) = one third of the sum, over scales s = 1, intermediate scale, and longer scale, of [LWMA(typical, s × support lookback, chart timeframe, bar i) + gain × (LWMA(typical, s × rate lookback, chart timeframe, bar i) − LWMA(typical, s × rate lookback, chart timeframe, bar i + s × rate span))], for completed chart bars i; 1 < intermediate scale < longer scale
Long: CompositeRate(averaging span − 1) < mean(CompositeRate(i), i = 1 to averaging span − 1) AND abs(current bid − close[2]) > distance parameter × point size AND, on chart bars reading the still-forming T2 bar, LWMA(typical, shorter lookback, T2) > LWMA(typical, longer lookback, T2) AND low[2] < high[1] AND, on chart bars reading T1, RSI(close, lookback, T1, still-forming bar) > RSI(close, lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Short: CompositeRate(averaging span − 1) > mean(CompositeRate(i), i = 1 to averaging span − 1) AND abs(current ask − close[1]) > distance parameter × point size AND, on chart bars reading the still-forming T2 bar, LWMA(typical, shorter lookback, T2) < LWMA(typical, longer lookback, T2) AND low[1] < high[2] AND, on chart bars reading T1, RSI(close, lookback, T1, still-forming bar) < RSI(close, lookback, T1, last completed bar)
