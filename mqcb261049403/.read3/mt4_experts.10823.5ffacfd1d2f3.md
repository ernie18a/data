Calculation: upper breakout level = the first of High(T4, current forming bar)+ATR(ATR lookback, T4, current forming bar)+breakout buffer if it is above current price+breakout buffer, High(T5, current forming bar)+ATR(ATR lookback, T5, current forming bar)+breakout buffer if it is above current price+breakout buffer, or current price+anchor distance+breakout buffer; T4<T5. Breakout buffer is delta distance plus spread.
Calculation: lower breakout level = the first of Low(T4, current forming bar)-ATR(ATR lookback, T4, current forming bar)-breakout buffer if it is below current price-breakout buffer, Low(T5, current forming bar)-ATR(ATR lookback, T5, current forming bar)-breakout buffer if it is below current price-breakout buffer, or current price-anchor distance-breakout buffer; T4<T5. Breakout buffer is delta distance plus spread.
Calculation: Pivot, R1, R2, R3, S1, S2, S3 = floor Pivot Points(range high, range low, close(T6, x[2])), where tHour=hour(datetime(T6,x[2])), range high=max(high(T6,x[tHour]), highest high from T6 bars x[2] through x[tHour+2]), and range low=min(low(T6,x[tHour]), lowest low from T6 bars x[2] through x[tHour+2]); use last completed T6 bars.
Calculation: account gain mode = total open net P/L >= normalized minimum lot amount AND (total losing net P/L is zero OR total winning net P/L > absolute total losing net P/L).
Calculation: long trailing stop = when account gain mode is true and the highest price since entry has reached entry+trail-start, entry+max(0,floor((highest price since entry-entry-trail-start)/trail-amount)-1)*trail-amount, if trail-amount is positive; if trail-amount is zero, entry.
Calculation: short trailing stop = when account gain mode is true and the lowest price since entry has reached entry-trail-start, entry-max(0,floor((entry-lowest price since entry-trail-start)/trail-amount)-1)*trail-amount, if trail-amount is positive; if trail-amount is zero, entry.
Long: RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T1, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) > upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) > upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T3, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) > upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T1, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) < lower RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T2, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) < lower RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T3, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) < lower RSI threshold AND EMA(weighted close, short EMA lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) < EMA(weighted close, long EMA lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars); T1<T2<T3 and short EMA lookback<long EMA lookback.
Short: RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T1, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) < upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) < upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T3, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) < upper RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T1, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) > lower RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T2, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) > lower RSI threshold AND RSI(weighted close, RSI lookback, T3, x[lag parameter], sampled on chart bars) > lower RSI threshold AND EMA(weighted close, short EMA lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars) > EMA(weighted close, long EMA lookback, T2, current bar still forming, sampled on chart bars); T1<T2<T3 and short EMA lookback<long EMA lookback.

Short: low <= lower breakout level.

Short: total open net P/L < -account balance*risk percentage parameter AND (total open long volume > total open short volume OR total losing long volume > total losing short volume).
Long: emergency risk block is active AND losing long and short volumes are both positive and equal AND losing long net P/L > losing short net P/L.
Short: emergency risk block is active AND losing long and short volumes are both positive and equal AND losing short net P/L > losing long net P/L.
Exit: long, low <= entry-stop-loss distance when stop-loss distance is positive OR position age >= maximum holding duration and position net P/L < 0 OR account gain mode is true and low <= the higher of entry-stop-loss distance and the active long trailing stop.
Exit: short, high >= entry+stop-loss distance when stop-loss distance is positive OR position age >= maximum holding duration and position net P/L < 0 OR account gain mode is true and high >= the lower of entry+stop-loss distance and the active short trailing stop.
Exit: long, total open net P/L < 0 AND the position belongs to a basket with one winning position and at least two losing positions, each losing position's absolute net P/L is less than the winner's net P/L, and the absolute x-factor times the combined losing net P/L is less than the winner's net P/L.
Exit: short, total open net P/L < 0 AND the position belongs to a basket with one winning position and at least two losing positions, each losing position's absolute net P/L is less than the winner's net P/L, and the absolute x-factor times the combined losing net P/L is less than the winner's net P/L.
