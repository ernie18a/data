Calculation: PriceChannel_Stop reversal signals = on T1 bars, upper stop = rolling highest high over the channel lookback − risk fraction × (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), and lower stop = rolling lowest low + risk fraction × the same range; trend turns up when close > prior upper stop and down when close < prior lower stop; in an uptrend, carry the lower stop forward as the greater of its current and prior values; in a downtrend, carry the upper stop forward as the lesser of its current and prior values; emit a bullish signal on a downtrend-to-uptrend change and a bearish signal on an uptrend-to-downtrend change when signal points are enabled, using the selected signal bar, which is the last completed T1 bar
Long: long entries enabled AND bullish PriceChannel_Stop reversal signal
Short: short entries enabled AND bearish PriceChannel_Stop reversal signal
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR bearish PriceChannel_Stop reversal signal AND long exits enabled AND short entries disabled
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance OR bullish PriceChannel_Stop reversal signal AND short exits enabled AND long entries disabled
