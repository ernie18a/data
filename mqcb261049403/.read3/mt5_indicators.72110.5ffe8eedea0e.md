Calculation: Extended pivot lookback = sensitivity lookback + max(minimum pivot extension, floor(sensitivity lookback / pivot-extension divisor))
Calculation: High pivot = highest(high, sensitivity lookback) > highest(high, extended pivot lookback) AND (major-level filter disabled OR high - lowest(low, sensitivity lookback) > ATR(key-level ATR period) * key-level depth)
Calculation: Low pivot = lowest(low, sensitivity lookback) < lowest(low, extended pivot lookback) AND (major-level filter disabled OR highest(high, sensitivity lookback) - low > ATR(key-level ATR period) * key-level depth)
Calculation: Volume filter passes = surge requirement disabled OR current volume > surge factor * SMA(volume, sample depth including current bar)
Calculation: Post-loss pause = base pause otherwise; when loss scaling is enabled, floor(minimum of maximum cooldown and base pause * (1 + loss amount / (simulated position size * cooldown loss fraction)))
Calculation: Signal eligibility = extended pivot lookback bars available AND bars since prior signal >= signal spacing AND current-session signal count < session limit AND current-session simulated PnL > session loss limit AND consecutive losses < streak limit AND loss cooldown elapsed
Long: Signal eligibility AND Volume filter passes AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI length) < cold threshold on at least one of the current or preceding cold-memory bars) AND ((signal mode is reversal AND SuperTrend(selected OHLC price basis, ATR period, band multiplier, RMA-or-EMA ATR smoothing) changes from bearish to bullish AND (fresh-pivot requirement disabled OR a low pivot occurred during the bearish trend before the change)) OR (signal mode is breakout AND Low pivot AND SuperTrend trend is bearish))
Short: Signal eligibility AND Volume filter passes AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI length) > hot threshold on at least one of the current or preceding hot-memory bars) AND ((signal mode is reversal AND SuperTrend(selected OHLC price basis, ATR period, band multiplier, RMA-or-EMA ATR smoothing) changes from bullish to bearish AND (fresh-pivot requirement disabled OR a high pivot occurred during the bullish trend before the change)) OR (signal mode is breakout AND High pivot AND SuperTrend trend is bullish))
