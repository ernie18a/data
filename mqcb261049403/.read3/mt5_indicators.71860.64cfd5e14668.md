Calculation: Long score = sum the EMA-alignment weight when EMA(close, fast lookback) > EMA(close, slow lookback) and their absolute gap > ATR(ATR lookback) × EMA-separation multiple; the trend-position weight when close > EMA(close, trend lookback) + ATR × trend-distance multiple; the RSI-momentum weight when RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI midpoint, < long RSI-zone ceiling, and > its previous chart-bar value; the MACD-expansion weight when the current MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) histogram > its previous chart-bar value; the VWAP-position weight when close > (high + low + close) / 3; the volume-surge weight when volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback, including the current chart bar) × volume-surge multiple; and the ADX-direction weight when ADX(ADX lookback) > ADX threshold and +DI(ADX lookback) > -DI(ADX lookback). Only when HTF bias is enabled, also sum the HTF-alignment weight when EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, current T1 bar) > EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, current T1 bar). These values are evaluated on chart bars using the current bar; the live latest bar is still forming. HTF values are used on chart bars and the current T1 bar is still forming.
Calculation: Short score = sum the EMA-alignment weight when EMA(close, fast lookback) < EMA(close, slow lookback) and their absolute gap > ATR(ATR lookback) × EMA-separation multiple; the trend-position weight when close < EMA(close, trend lookback) - ATR × trend-distance multiple; the RSI-momentum weight when RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI midpoint, > short RSI-zone floor, and < its previous chart-bar value; the MACD-expansion weight when the current MACD(close, MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback) histogram < its previous chart-bar value; the VWAP-position weight when close < (high + low + close) / 3; the volume-surge weight when volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback, including the current chart bar) × volume-surge multiple; and the ADX-direction weight when ADX(ADX lookback) > ADX threshold and -DI(ADX lookback) > +DI(ADX lookback). Only when HTF bias is enabled, also sum the HTF-alignment weight when EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, current T1 bar) < EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, current T1 bar). These values are evaluated on chart bars using the current bar; the live latest bar is still forming. HTF values are used on chart bars and the current T1 bar is still forming.
Calculation: Grade-filter pass = true when all grades are selected; score >= A-grade threshold when A+ and A are selected; score >= A+-grade threshold when only A+ is selected.
Long: EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses above EMA(close, slow lookback) AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) + ATR(ATR lookback) × trend-distance multiple AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < long entry RSI ceiling AND abs(close - open) > ATR × candle-body multiple AND abs(close - EMA(close, fast lookback)) < ATR × maximum-extension multiple AND (HTF bias disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, current T1 bar) >= EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, current T1 bar)) AND Long score >= minimum score AND Grade-filter pass AND (C-grade hiding disabled OR Long score >= C-grade threshold) AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous signal AND the most recent signal was not long or that long trade has since exited at its stop.
Short: EMA(close, fast lookback) crosses below EMA(close, slow lookback) AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) - ATR(ATR lookback) × trend-distance multiple AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > short entry RSI floor AND abs(close - open) > ATR × candle-body multiple AND abs(close - EMA(close, fast lookback)) < ATR × maximum-extension multiple AND (HTF bias disabled OR EMA(close, fast lookback, T1, current T1 bar) <= EMA(close, slow lookback, T1, current T1 bar)) AND Short score >= minimum score AND Grade-filter pass AND (C-grade hiding disabled OR Short score >= C-grade threshold) AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous signal AND the most recent signal was not short or that short trade has since exited at its stop.
Calculation: Long initial stop = entry price - ATR on the entry chart bar × flat-stop ATR multiple when structure-based stops are disabled; otherwise use the lowest low across the entry bar and the preceding swing-lookback chart bars minus ATR on the entry bar × stop-buffer ATR multiple, unless entry price minus that level is less than ATR on the entry bar × minimum stop-distance multiple, in which case use entry price - ATR on the entry bar × flat-stop ATR multiple.
Calculation: Short initial stop = entry price + ATR on the entry chart bar × flat-stop ATR multiple when structure-based stops are disabled; otherwise use the highest high across the entry bar and the preceding swing-lookback chart bars plus ATR on the entry bar × stop-buffer ATR multiple, unless that level minus entry price is less than ATR on the entry bar × minimum stop-distance multiple, in which case use entry price + ATR on the entry bar × flat-stop ATR multiple.
Calculation: Long target levels = entry price + abs(entry price - Long initial stop) × each of the separate target risk-reward parameters.
Calculation: Short target levels = entry price - abs(entry price - Short initial stop) × each of the separate target risk-reward parameters.
Calculation: Long active stop at the start of a chart bar = Long initial stop when trailing is disabled or no target was reached on an earlier bar; with trailing enabled, entry price after target 1, target 1 after target 2, and target 2 after target 3, using the highest target reached since entry.
Calculation: Short active stop at the start of a chart bar = Short initial stop when trailing is disabled or no target was reached on an earlier bar; with trailing enabled, entry price after target 1, target 1 after target 2, and target 2 after target 3, using the highest target reached since entry.
Exit: Long, low <= Long active stop
Exit: Short, high >= Short active stop
