Calculation: Buy level = high of the third setup bar in the scan of current chart bars, including the forming bar, from newest to oldest. For each candidate first bar whose low is below the next older bar’s low, the second bar is the first older non-inside bar with high above the first bar’s high; an inside bar has high below the immediately newer bar’s high and low above its low. The third bar is the first older non-inside bar after the second with high above the second bar’s high. During the second-bar search, a low below the first bar’s low rejects the candidate; during the third-bar search, a low below the first bar’s low also rejects it, and when the breakout-only setting is on, a high above the highest high from the current bar through the first bar rejects it. Resume the pivot scan after a rejected candidate; if a search ends without finding the second or third bar, there is no level.
Calculation: Sell level = low of the third setup bar in the scan of current chart bars, including the forming bar, from newest to oldest. For each candidate first bar whose high is above the next older bar’s high, the second bar is the first older non-inside bar with low below the first bar’s low; an inside bar has high below the immediately newer bar’s high and low above its low. The third bar is the first older non-inside bar after the second with low below the second bar’s low. During the second-bar search, a high above the first bar’s high rejects the candidate; during the third-bar search, a high above the first bar’s high also rejects it, and when the breakout-only setting is on, a low below the lowest low from the current bar through the first bar rejects it. Resume the pivot scan after a rejected candidate; if a search ends without finding the second or third bar, there is no level.

Short: lowest low of chart bars more recent than the third bar defining Sell level < Sell level
