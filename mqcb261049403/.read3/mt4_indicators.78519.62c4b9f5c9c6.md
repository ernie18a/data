Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a low or high (or close when wick pivots are disabled) strictly below or above each of the preceding pivot-span bars and no higher or lower than each of the following pivot-span bars; available after those following bars are present
Calculation: Eligible prior pivot = a confirmed pivot of the matching price side among the most recent maximum-pivots pivots, more than the minimum separation and no more than the maximum reach before the signal bar
Calculation: Divergence endpoint = the signal bar when live-bar comparison is enabled, otherwise the preceding bar; in the latter mode the signal bar's oscillator OR close must rise for bullish or fall for bearish divergence relative to the endpoint. Run pivot, oscillator, and signal calculations on bars aggregated to the analyzed timeframe T1, not on chart bars sampling T1; closed columns use completed T1 bars and the current preview uses the still-forming T1 bar
Calculation: Clear divergence path = every intervening oscillator value and close is on or above its respective straight line between the prior pivot and endpoint for bullish divergence, or on or below for bearish divergence
Calculation: Regular divergence = bullish when the oscillator at the endpoint is higher than at an eligible prior pivot low while the endpoint low (or close when wick pivots are disabled) is below that pivot's low (or close); bearish when the oscillator is lower than at an eligible prior pivot high while the endpoint high (or close) is above that pivot's high (or close); require a clear divergence path
Calculation: Hidden divergence = bullish when the oscillator at the endpoint is lower than at an eligible prior pivot low while the endpoint low (or close when wick pivots are disabled) is above that pivot's low (or close); bearish when the oscillator is higher than at an eligible prior pivot high while the endpoint high (or close) is below that pivot's high (or close); require a clear divergence path
Calculation: Divergence count = count each enabled oscillator, enabled regular or hidden type, and bullish or bearish side combination satisfying those divergences once; oscillators are MACD(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback), MACD histogram(close, shorter MACD lookback, longer MACD lookback, signal lookback), RSI(close, RSI lookback), SMA(Stochastic %K(high, low, close, stochastic lookback), stochastic smoothing lookback), CCI(close, CCI lookback), Momentum(close, momentum lookback), OBV(close, volume), VWMA(close, volume, shorter VW-MACD lookback) - VWMA(close, volume, longer VW-MACD lookback), CMF(high, low, close, volume, CMF lookback), and MFI(close, volume, MFI lookback)
Long: Divergence count is at least the minimum divergence count AND at least one enabled oscillator has an enabled bullish regular or hidden divergence
Short: Divergence count is at least the minimum divergence count AND at least one enabled oscillator has an enabled bearish regular or hidden divergence
