Calculation: RSI value = RSI(close, RSI lookback, T1, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T1; bar shift selects a forming or completed T1 bar, and T1 is the chart timeframe when no separate RSI timeframe is set
Calculation: Stochastic %K and %D = Stochastic(high, low, close, %K lookback, %D lookback, slowing lookback, T2, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T2; bar shift selects a forming or completed T2 bar, and T2 is the chart timeframe when no separate stochastic timeframe is set
Calculation: +DI and -DI = +DI(low, ADX lookback, T3, bar shift) and -DI(low, ADX lookback, T3, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T3; bar shift selects a forming or completed T3 bar, and T3 is the chart timeframe when no separate DI timeframe is set
Calculation: ADX main line = ADX(low, ADX lookback, T4, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T4; bar shift selects a forming or completed T4 bar, and T4 is the chart timeframe when no separate ADX main timeframe is set
Calculation: Parabolic SAR = Parabolic SAR(SAR step, SAR maximum, T5, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T5; bar shift selects a forming or completed T5 bar
Calculation: Moving average = Moving Average(selected price, MA period, MA line shift, MA method, T6, bar shift), sampled on chart bars from T6; bar shift selects a forming or completed T6 bar
Long: at least one of the RSI, stochastic, or DI filters is enabled AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI value < lower RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (stochastic threshold filter disabled AND %K > %D) OR (stochastic threshold filter enabled AND %K > %D AND %K > upper stochastic threshold)) AND (DI filter disabled OR +DI > -DI) AND (ADX main filter disabled OR ADX main line > ADX main threshold) AND (SAR filter disabled OR Parabolic SAR > chart close at bar shift) AND (moving average filter disabled OR Moving average < chart close at bar shift)
Short: at least one of the RSI, stochastic, or DI filters is enabled AND NOT (RSI filter enabled AND stochastic filter enabled AND DI filter enabled) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI value > upper RSI threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR (stochastic threshold filter disabled AND %K <= %D) OR (stochastic threshold filter enabled AND %K < %D AND %K < lower stochastic threshold)) AND (DI filter disabled OR -DI >= +DI) AND (ADX main filter disabled OR ADX main line > ADX main threshold) AND (SAR filter disabled OR Parabolic SAR <= chart close at bar shift) AND (moving average filter disabled OR Moving average >= chart close at bar shift)
Short: RSI filter enabled AND stochastic filter enabled AND DI filter enabled AND (SAR filter enabled OR moving average filter enabled) AND RSI value < lower RSI threshold AND ((stochastic threshold filter disabled AND %K > %D) OR (stochastic threshold filter enabled AND %K > %D AND %K > upper stochastic threshold)) AND +DI > -DI AND (ADX main filter disabled OR ADX main line > ADX main threshold) AND (SAR filter disabled OR Parabolic SAR <= chart close at bar shift) AND (moving average filter disabled OR Moving average >= chart close at bar shift)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss parameter × point size OR high >= entry price + take-profit parameter × point size
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss parameter × point size OR low <= entry price - take-profit parameter × point size
