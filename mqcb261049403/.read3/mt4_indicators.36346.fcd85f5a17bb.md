Calculation: T1 flow midpoint = (T1 open + T1 close) / 2, using the T1 candle still forming and sampled on chart bars
Calculation: upper ATR band = T1 flow midpoint + ATR(lookback on chart bars) × multiplier
Calculation: lower ATR band = T1 flow midpoint - ATR(lookback on chart bars) × multiplier
Long: On chart bars, close crosses above T1 flow midpoint
Short: On chart bars, close crosses below T1 flow midpoint
Long: On chart bars, close > upper ATR band AND T1 close != T1 open
Short: On chart bars, close < lower ATR band AND T1 close != T1 open
