Calculation: Oscillators = the individually enabled MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback), MACD histogram(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback), RSI(close, lookback), Stochastic(high, low, close, lookback, smoothing lookback), CCI(close, lookback), Momentum(close, lookback), OBV(close, volume), VWMACD = VWMA(close, volume, fast lookback) - VWMA(close, volume, slow lookback), Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, lookback), and MFI(close, volume, lookback); MACD fast and slow lookbacks are shared by its line and histogram, fast is shorter than slow, VWMACD has separate fast and slow lookbacks with fast shorter than slow, and all other lookbacks are separate parameters.
Calculation: MFI(close, volume, lookback) = 100 - 100 / (1 + positive flow / negative flow), where positive flow sums volume[k] * close[k] when close[k] > close[k-1] over the lookback and negative flow sums volume[k] * close[k] when close[k] < close[k-1]; if negative flow is zero and positive flow is positive, MFI = 100.
Calculation: Divergence(oscillator, side, kind, endpoint) = a comparison between the oscillator and a same-side pivot among the latest pivot-limit pivots no more than reach-limit bars before the endpoint and more than gap-parameter bars before it; pivots use the configured source (close, or high for tops and low for bottoms), span-parameter bars on each side, with a top strictly above the preceding bars and at least as high as the following bars, or a bottom strictly below the preceding bars and at most as low as the following bars, and become eligible once the following bars have been observed. Bullish regular means endpoint price probe < pivot low and oscillator(endpoint) > oscillator(pivot); bullish hidden means endpoint price probe > pivot low and oscillator(endpoint) < oscillator(pivot); bearish regular means endpoint price probe > pivot high and oscillator(endpoint) < oscillator(pivot); bearish hidden means endpoint price probe < pivot high and oscillator(endpoint) > oscillator(pivot). For wicks, the endpoint probe is low for bullish and high for bearish; otherwise it is close. Every intervening bar's oscillator and close must be at or above their respective straight lines from pivot to endpoint for bullish divergences, or at or below them for bearish divergences.
Calculation: Divergences(evaluation bar) = all Divergence(oscillator, side, kind, endpoint) results for Oscillators and the configured divergence kinds (regular, hidden, or both); with confirmation enabled, endpoint is the preceding chart bar and the evaluation bar must be closed, with either that oscillator or close rising from endpoint to evaluation bar for bullish divergences, or falling for bearish divergences; with confirmation disabled, endpoint is the current forming chart bar and there is no next-bar test. Count each oscillator, side, and kind result separately.
Long: count(Divergences(evaluation bar)) >= minimum-count parameter AND at least one member of Divergences(evaluation bar) has bullish side.
Short: count(Divergences(evaluation bar)) >= minimum-count parameter AND at least one member of Divergences(evaluation bar) has bearish side.
