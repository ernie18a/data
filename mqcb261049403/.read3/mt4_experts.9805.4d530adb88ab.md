Calculation: Long stop candidate = selected method: nearest preceding completed chart-bar low minus offset, or nearest preceding lower Fractal minus offset, each within its search lookback and below the current forming-bar close by at least the minimum stop distance; current forming-bar close minus (offset − Velocity(current forming chart bar, lookback) + Velocity(previous completed chart bar, lookback)) × multiplier when Velocity is rising; Parabolic SAR(current forming chart bar, acceleration, maximum) minus offset when current forming-bar close minus minimum stop distance exceeds SAR; or current forming-bar close minus trailing distance
Calculation: Short stop candidate = selected method: nearest preceding completed chart-bar high plus offset, or nearest preceding upper Fractal plus offset, each within its search lookback and above the current forming-bar close by at least the minimum stop distance; current forming-bar close plus (offset + Velocity(previous completed chart bar, lookback) − Velocity(current forming chart bar, lookback)) × multiplier when Velocity is falling; Parabolic SAR(current forming chart bar, acceleration, maximum) plus offset when current forming-bar close plus minimum stop distance is below SAR; or current forming-bar close plus trailing distance
Exit: Long, long stop candidate > current stop loss + step AND (general breakeven enabled AND long stop candidate >= volume-weighted average entry price of matching long positions OR general breakeven disabled AND (profit-only disabled OR long stop candidate >= entry price)) AND (no-loss-only disabled OR current stop loss < entry price) AND (stop-loss-only disabled OR current stop loss != 0)
Exit: Short, short stop candidate != 0 AND (short stop candidate < current stop loss − step OR current stop loss = 0) AND (general breakeven enabled AND short stop candidate <= volume-weighted average entry price of matching short positions OR general breakeven disabled AND (profit-only disabled OR short stop candidate <= entry price)) AND (no-loss-only disabled OR current stop loss > entry price) AND (stop-loss-only disabled OR current stop loss != 0)
