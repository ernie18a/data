Calculation: CurrencyStrength(C) = mean of RSI(exchange-rate close, RSI lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) when C is the base currency and 100 - RSI(exchange-rate close, RSI lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) when C is the quote currency, across the other seven currencies in {EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, USD, CAD, CHF, JPY}
Long: abs(Bid - close[1]) > gap parameter × point size AND LWMA(typical price, fast lookback, chart bars, forming bar) > LWMA(typical price, slow lookback, chart bars, forming bar) AND low[2] < high[1] AND ((base currency is EUR and quote currency is any other currency in {EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, USD, CAD, CHF, JPY}, or base is GBP and quote is in {AUD, NZD, USD, CAD, CHF, JPY}, or base is AUD and quote is in {NZD, CAD, CHF, JPY}, or base is USD and quote is in {CHF, CAD, JPY}) AND CurrencyStrength(base) is greater than each other currency's strength AND CurrencyStrength(quote) is less than each other currency's strength OR base is GBP and quote is USD and CurrencyStrength(AUD) is greater than each other currency's strength and CurrencyStrength(USD) is less than each other currency's strength)
Short: abs(Ask - close[1]) > gap parameter × point size AND LWMA(typical price, fast lookback, chart bars, forming bar) > LWMA(typical price, slow lookback, chart bars, forming bar) AND low[1] < high[2]
Calculation: Long stop = the maximum of entry price minus stop-loss distance, entry price plus lock distance after gain exceeds the lock trigger, each Bid minus trailing distance while gain exceeds the trail trigger and candle trailing is disabled, and each candle-trail candidate since entry; a candle-trail candidate is the lowest low of the latest X chart bars including the forming bar minus candle offset, evaluated on each new chart bar when candle trailing is enabled
Calculation: Short stop = the minimum of entry price plus stop-loss distance, entry price minus lock distance after gain exceeds the lock trigger, each Ask plus trailing distance while gain exceeds the trail trigger and candle trailing is disabled, and each candle-trail candidate since entry; a candle-trail candidate is the highest high of the latest X chart bars including the forming bar plus candle offset, evaluated on each new chart bar when candle trailing is enabled
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus take-profit distance plus trailing distance for each candle-trail stop increase since entry
Calculation: Short target = entry price minus take-profit distance minus trailing distance for each candle-trail stop decrease since entry
Exit: long, Bid <= long stop OR Bid >= long target
Exit: short, Ask >= short stop OR Ask <= short target
