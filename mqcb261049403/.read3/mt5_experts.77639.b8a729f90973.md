Calculation: Confirmed swing high = the latest completed chart-bar high at least as high as each of the configured number of completed bars on both sides
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = the latest completed chart-bar low at most as low as each of the configured number of completed bars on both sides
Long: While no setup is pending, a completed chart bar has low < the latest unused confirmed swing low - sweep ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) AND close > that swing low AND no unused confirmed swing high is swept on the same bar; if the hour filter is enabled, the sweep occurs inside its configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window; if the trend filter is enabled, at the sweep evaluation the current T1 close > EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, current forming bar). Consume the swept low even if the trend filter rejects it. Enter on the first of the next configured confirmation bars whose close > max(sweep-bar open, sweep-bar close), only if that bar's high-low range >= confirmation-range ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar); a first close-through with insufficient range consumes the setup.
Short: While no setup is pending, a completed chart bar has high > the latest unused confirmed swing high + sweep ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) AND close < that swing high; if the hour filter is enabled, the sweep occurs inside its configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive window; if the trend filter is enabled, at the sweep evaluation the current T1 close < EMA(close, trend lookback, T1, current forming bar). Consume the swept high even if the trend filter rejects it. Enter on the first of the next configured confirmation bars whose close < min(sweep-bar open, sweep-bar close), only if that bar's high-low range >= confirmation-range ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar); a first close-through with insufficient range consumes the setup.
Calculation: Long initial risk = max(entry price - (sweep low - stop-buffer ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar)), minimum broker stop gap)
Calculation: Short initial risk = max((sweep high + stop-buffer ATR multiple x ATR(lookback, chart bars, last completed bar)) - entry price, minimum broker stop gap)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long initial risk before a successful break-even stop move OR low <= entry price + break-even offset x long initial risk after that move OR high >= entry price + reward multiple x long initial risk
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short initial risk before a successful break-even stop move OR high >= entry price - break-even offset x short initial risk after that move OR low <= entry price - reward multiple x short initial risk
