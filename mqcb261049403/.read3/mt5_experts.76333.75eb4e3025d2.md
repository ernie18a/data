Calculation: Opening-range high = MAX(high of completed T1 bars within the configured opening interval)
Calculation: Opening-range low = MIN(low of completed T1 bars within the configured opening interval)
Calculation: Long initial stop = opening-range low - entry/stop buffer when range-based stops are selected; otherwise entry price - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * stop ATR multiple
Calculation: Short initial stop = opening-range high + entry/stop buffer when range-based stops are selected; otherwise entry price + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * stop ATR multiple
Calculation: Long take profit = entry price + (entry price - long initial stop) * target reward-risk ratio
Calculation: Short take profit = entry price - (short initial stop - entry price) * target reward-risk ratio
Calculation: Long protective stop = when trailing is enabled and the current T1 close is at least (entry price - current long stop) * trailing activation ratio above entry price, max(current long stop, current T1 close - ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * trailing distance); otherwise, when breakeven is enabled and the current T1 close is at least (entry price - current long stop) * breakeven activation ratio above entry price, max(current long stop, entry price + one minimum price increment); otherwise current long stop
Calculation: Short protective stop = when trailing is enabled and the current T1 close is at least (current short stop - entry price) * trailing activation ratio below entry price, min(current short stop, current T1 close + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * trailing distance); otherwise, when breakeven is enabled and the current T1 close is at least (current short stop - entry price) * breakeven activation ratio below entry price, min(current short stop, entry price - one minimum price increment); otherwise current short stop
Long: high[1] > opening-range high AND the opening interval contains at least the minimum range bars AND opening-range high - opening-range low >= ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * minimum range-size ATR multiple AND spread <= maximum spread AND current time is after the opening interval and before the trade cutoff AND trades today < daily trade limit AND no long order is armed and no long trade has fired today
Short: low[1] < opening-range low AND the opening interval contains at least the minimum range bars AND opening-range high - opening-range low >= ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar) * minimum range-size ATR multiple AND spread <= maximum spread AND current time is after the opening interval and before the trade cutoff AND trades today < daily trade limit AND no short order is armed and no short trade has fired today
Exit: Long, low <= long protective stop OR high >= long take profit OR T1 bars held >= maximum holding bars OR current day is Saturday or Sunday OR (current day is Friday AND current time >= configured Friday close) OR current time >= configured session close
Exit: Short, high >= short protective stop OR low <= short take profit OR T1 bars held >= maximum holding bars OR current day is Saturday or Sunday OR (current day is Friday AND current time >= configured Friday close) OR current time >= configured session close
