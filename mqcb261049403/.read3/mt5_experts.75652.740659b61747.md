Calculation: MA bias(T) = +1 when EMA(close, short lookback, T, last completed bar) > EMA(close, long lookback, T, last completed bar), -1 when <, otherwise 0; use the same short and long lookback parameters on every series.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = 0 if MA biases on T2 and T3 conflict or both equal 0; otherwise use the nonzero bias. Both use last completed bars.
Calculation: Structure valid(s) = true if the high-low range over the structural lookback of completed T1 bars is zero; otherwise let p = (last completed T1 close - lowest low in that range) / (highest high in that range - lowest low in that range). For long, valid if p = lower range-location boundary or p >= upper boundary; for short, valid if p <= lower boundary or p = upper boundary.
Calculation: Volatility valid = true if the mean ATR over the ATR-averaging lookback of completed T1 bars is <= 0; otherwise true when ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) / that mean is between the lower and upper volatility-ratio parameters, inclusive.
Calculation: London open = UTC hour is within the configurable opening duration after London start and before London end. London-New York overlap = both sessions are enabled and UTC hour is within both session ranges.
Calculation: Session valid = UTC time is not after the configurable Friday cutoff AND ((overlap is not required AND London session is enabled and active OR New York session is enabled and active) OR (overlap is required AND (London-New York overlap OR (London open is allowed AND London open AND London session is active))).
Calculation: News blocked = manual news pause OR external news red flag OR (calendar news enabled AND an eligible USD calendar event falls between current UTC time minus the post-event window and current UTC time plus the pre-event window); eligible events are high importance, plus moderate importance when moderate-news blocking is enabled.
Calculation: Effective spread cap = max(general spread cap, asset-specific spread cap) when the asset-specific cap is positive; otherwise use the general spread cap.
Calculation: Day drawdown percent = 100 * (equity at the start of the current UTC day - current equity) / max(equity at the start of the current UTC day, 1).
Calculation: Entry score(s) = clamp(base score + (higher-timeframe bias = s ? HTF-aligned adjustment : higher-timeframe bias = 0 ? HTF-flat adjustment : HTF-opposed adjustment) + (external macro filter disabled ? 0 : external T2 MA bias = s ? macro-aligned adjustment : external T2 MA bias = 0 ? macro-flat adjustment : macro-opposed adjustment) + (Volatility valid ? volatility-pass adjustment : volatility-fail adjustment) + (spread points < tight-spread fraction * effective spread cap ? tight-spread adjustment : ordinary-spread adjustment) + (London-New York overlap ? overlap adjustment : (London open allowed AND London open ? higher-timeframe bias = s ? London-aligned adjustment : London-opposed adjustment : ordinary-session adjustment)) + news-clear adjustment + (Structure valid(s) ? structure-pass adjustment : structure-fail adjustment), score floor, score ceiling). The external T2 MA bias uses the EUR/USD exchange-rate close and the same short and long lookback parameters on its last completed T2 bar.
Calculation: R multiple = favorable mark-price movement from entry price / initial stop distance; favorable movement is mark price minus entry price for long positions and entry price minus mark price for short positions.
Long: on each new T1 bar, (EMA(close, short lookback, T1, last completed bar) crosses above EMA(close, long lookback, T1, last completed bar) OR (the short EMA is above the long EMA AND last completed T1 close > close at the momentum lookback AND last completed T1 high >= highest high from the intervening completed T1 bars AND last completed T1 close > preceding completed T1 close)) AND Session valid AND NOT News blocked AND spread points <= Effective spread cap AND Day drawdown percent < daily-loss limit AND (floating-drawdown limit <= 0 OR Day drawdown percent < floating-drawdown limit) AND consecutive nonpositive closed trades today < consecutive-loss limit AND trades opened today < daily-trade limit AND open strategy positions < position limit AND UTC minute of hour >= hour-open exclusion parameter AND ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) > 0 AND (overlap is not required OR London open is not allowed OR NOT (London open AND NOT London-New York overlap) OR higher-timeframe bias = +1) AND (hard quality filters disabled OR ((higher-timeframe bias = 0 OR higher-timeframe bias = +1) AND Volatility valid AND Structure valid(long))) AND Entry score(+1) >= minimum score AND stop distance > 0 AND (target distance - spread price distance) / stop distance >= minimum net reward-to-risk; stop distance = ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) * stop-loss multiplier; target distance = that ATR times the runner target multiplier when runners are enabled and Entry score(+1) >= runner-score threshold, otherwise times the regular target multiplier.
Short: on each new T1 bar, (EMA(close, short lookback, T1, last completed bar) crosses below EMA(close, long lookback, T1, last completed bar) OR (the short EMA is below the long EMA AND last completed T1 close < close at the momentum lookback AND last completed T1 low <= lowest low from the intervening completed T1 bars AND last completed T1 close < preceding completed T1 close)) AND Session valid AND NOT News blocked AND spread points <= Effective spread cap AND Day drawdown percent < daily-loss limit AND (floating-drawdown limit <= 0 OR Day drawdown percent < floating-drawdown limit) AND consecutive nonpositive closed trades today < consecutive-loss limit AND trades opened today < daily-trade limit AND open strategy positions < position limit AND UTC minute of hour >= hour-open exclusion parameter AND ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) > 0 AND (overlap is not required OR London open is not allowed OR NOT (London open AND NOT London-New York overlap) OR higher-timeframe bias = -1) AND (hard quality filters disabled OR ((higher-timeframe bias = 0 OR higher-timeframe bias = -1) AND Volatility valid AND Structure valid(short))) AND Entry score(-1) >= minimum score AND stop distance > 0 AND (target distance - spread price distance) / stop distance >= minimum net reward-to-risk; stop distance = ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) * stop-loss multiplier; target distance = that ATR times the runner target multiplier when runners are enabled and Entry score(-1) >= runner-score threshold, otherwise times the regular target multiplier.
Exit: long, mark price <= entry price - ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar at entry) * stop-loss multiplier OR mark price >= entry price + ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar at entry) * (runner target multiplier when runners are enabled and entry score >= runner-score threshold, otherwise regular target multiplier) OR (elapsed whole minutes >= runner maximum duration for a runner, otherwise regular maximum duration, when that duration is positive) OR (not a runner AND green-close enabled AND elapsed seconds >= minimum green hold AND favorable movement > 0 AND R multiple >= green-close R threshold AND (green-close money threshold <= 0 OR floating profit plus swap >= green-close money threshold)) OR (not a runner AND bank-profit duration > 0 AND elapsed whole minutes >= bank-profit duration AND R multiple >= bank-profit R threshold) OR (news flattening enabled AND News blocked).
Exit: short, mark price >= entry price + ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar at entry) * stop-loss multiplier OR mark price <= entry price - ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar at entry) * (runner target multiplier when runners are enabled and entry score >= runner-score threshold, otherwise regular target multiplier) OR (elapsed whole minutes >= runner maximum duration for a runner, otherwise regular maximum duration, when that duration is positive) OR (not a runner AND green-close enabled AND elapsed seconds >= minimum green hold AND favorable movement > 0 AND R multiple >= green-close R threshold AND (green-close money threshold <= 0 OR floating profit plus swap >= green-close money threshold)) OR (not a runner AND bank-profit duration > 0 AND elapsed whole minutes >= bank-profit duration AND R multiple >= bank-profit R threshold) OR (news flattening enabled AND News blocked).
