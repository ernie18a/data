Long: On each chart bar, Parabolic SAR(high, low, step, maximum) for the chart timeframe and every enabled timeframe in the successively longer prefix T1–T3 is below low, AND on the previous chart bar Parabolic SAR for at least one of those timeframes is above high[1]; higher-timeframe values are sampled on chart bars from the timeframe bar containing each chart bar, including the still-forming bar.
