Calculation: Price distance(p) = abs(open - p)
Calculation: Missed-bar count = initialize to zero; on chart bars with Price distance(last entry) > P_indent, reset to zero if Price distance(first entry) < P_skip3_min; otherwise increment if Price distance(first entry) >= P_skip3_min and Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip3_max, or if Price distance(first entry) > P_skip3_max and P_skip3_max < Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip6_max; reset to zero when the count exceeds the applicable skip-count parameter
Long: open < MA(applied price, lookback, horizontal shift, method, T1, bar still forming), sampled on each chart bar AND (no position is open OR (Price distance(last entry) > P_indent AND (Price distance(first entry) < P_skip3_min OR (Price distance(first entry) >= P_skip3_min AND Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip3_max AND Missed-bar count > P_skip3_count) OR (Price distance(first entry) > P_skip3_max AND P_skip3_max < Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip6_max AND Missed-bar count > P_skip6_count))))
Short: open > MA(applied price, lookback, horizontal shift, method, T1, bar still forming), sampled on each chart bar AND (no position is open OR (Price distance(last entry) > P_indent AND (Price distance(first entry) < P_skip3_min OR (Price distance(first entry) >= P_skip3_min AND Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip3_max AND Missed-bar count > P_skip3_count) OR (Price distance(first entry) > P_skip3_max AND P_skip3_max < Price distance(last entry) <= P_skip6_max AND Missed-bar count > P_skip6_count))))
Exit: long, account net profit (profit + commission + swap) >= P_profit_target
Exit: short, account net profit (profit + commission + swap) >= P_profit_target
