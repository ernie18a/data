Long: open position count for this strategy < maximum-trades parameter AND abs(current bid - close[2]) > retracement parameter × point size AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1 forming bar sampled on chart bars) > LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1 forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND low[2] < high[1] AND standard-deviation-channel endpoint price 1 < endpoint price 2
Short: open position count for this strategy < maximum-trades parameter AND abs(current ask - close[2]) > retracement parameter × point size AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1 forming bar sampled on chart bars) < LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1 forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND low[1] < high[2] AND standard-deviation-channel endpoint price 1 > endpoint price 2
Calculation: shorter EMA group aligned = all six EMAs of typical price at distinct, successively increasing shorter-group lookbacks on T2's forming bar sampled on chart bars are equal
Calculation: longer EMA group aligned = all six EMAs of typical price at distinct, successively increasing longer-group lookbacks on T2's forming bar sampled on chart bars are equal; every longer-group lookback exceeds every shorter-group lookback
Exit: long, shorter EMA group aligned OR longer EMA group aligned OR close[1] <= lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, deviation parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar)
Exit: short, shorter EMA group aligned OR longer EMA group aligned OR close[1] <= lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, deviation parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar)
