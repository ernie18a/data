On each new bar, allow a new position only while the open-trade count is below its cap. Enter long when the fast weighted moving average of typical price is above the slow one; the selected higher-timeframe closes show a sustained decline, with the current close below nearly all of the older closes in the lookback; the second-prior low is below the prior high; and the bid is farther from the second-prior close than a configured candle-based distance. Enter short on the reverse higher-timeframe close pattern and moving-average ordering, when the prior low is below the second-prior high and the ask is farther from the prior close than that distance. Attach a stop loss and take profit to each entry. Close the instrument's positions when their combined profit reaches a configured cash target, or when a profit-trailing rule gives back its allowed amount from the best combined profit. Optionally close the basket at a balance-based profit or loss limit. Also close positions when their combined loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account's running equity high-water mark; an optional money-based loss rule can apply an additional multiplier.
