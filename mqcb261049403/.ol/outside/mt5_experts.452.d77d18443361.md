For each target instrument, calculate the stochastic main-minus-signal spread on it and two related instruments using the same settings, on synchronized bars. Treat positive spreads as upward and negative spreads as downward. Reconcile the three directions as follows: if two are upward and the third downward, infer the third upward only when the magnitude of the other upward spread exceeds the opposing spread; apply the symmetric rule for two downward spreads. Then propagate a direction to a zero or not-opposing member when the other two agree, including inferring the target direction when both related instruments agree and the target does not oppose them. Buy the target when all three directions are upward; sell it when all three are downward. Close a target short when its own spread is positive, and close a target long when its own spread is negative. Evaluate signals from the last completed bar; open only when no position already exists for that target.
