Before opening trades, require that trading hours are allowed, no monitored-currency economic event is within the configured halt window, account exposure is below its limit, and position, margin, and order-count risk checks pass. During the halt window, make no new entries; if configured, close the system’s open positions before the event. Close the basket when its net profit or loss reaches the configured percentage of account balance. When these external-data and account-risk gates pass, direction is determined by the traded instrument’s own price and indicator signals, so no external-data rule selects long versus short.
