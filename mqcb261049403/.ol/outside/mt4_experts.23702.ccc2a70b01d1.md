On each new bar, allow entries only while the position limit and margin checks permit. Go long when the prior bar dips below and closes above a moving average, the fast average is above the slow average, the prior close is sufficiently far from the current price, and the preceding-bar range condition is met. Go short when the prior bar rises above and closes below the moving average, the fast average is above the slow average, price is sufficiently far from the prior close, and the corresponding range condition is met. Attach protective stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry. Exit or reduce risk by closing positions when aggregate losses exceed an equity-based threshold, when configured aggregate profit targets or profit drawdown limits are reached, or by moving stops to breakeven and trailing them using recent candle extrema or favorable price movement.
