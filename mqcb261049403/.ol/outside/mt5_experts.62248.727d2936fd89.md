At each decision, manage only positions belonging to this strategy on the traded instrument. If any positions are open, first close all positions when their combined unrealized profit in pips reaches the profit target, or when combined monetary profit reaches its target, according to the selected target type. Otherwise, if combined pips reach the configured loss threshold and loss closing is enabled, close either the oldest position on each open side or every position, according to the selected loss mode. Otherwise, if hedge mode is enabled and combined monetary loss reaches the configured percentage of account balance, open a position on the side with less total volume, sized to offset the volume difference times the hedge multiplier; do this only once until the hedge is balanced or all positions are gone.

If no higher-priority action applies, open a first position on each enabled side that currently has none. For subsequent grid entries, compare the current ask for buys or bid for sells with that side’s most recent entry price. In trend-following mode, add a buy after price rises by the side’s grid interval and a sell after price falls by that interval. In contrarian mode, add a buy after price falls by the interval and a sell after it rises by the interval. The interval is the base spacing for static progression, base spacing times the number of positions on that side for geometric progression, or base spacing times the greater of one and two raised to one less than that count for exponential progression. Only open grid entries when spread is within its configured maximum and the open-position limit is not reached; the hedge action is not subject to these opening limits.
