On each new chart bar, if fewer than the allowed number of positions are open, consider entries. Go long when the current bid is farther from the close two bars earlier than the configured retracement distance, the low two bars earlier is below the prior bar’s high, and on the selected higher timeframe the previous bar’s low touched or fell below at least one Ichimoku line (conversion, base, or either cloud boundary) while the current bar opened above that same line. Go short when the current ask is farther from that earlier close than the retracement distance, the prior bar’s low is below the high two bars earlier, and the higher-timeframe previous bar’s high touched or exceeded at least one of those lines while the current bar opened below that same line. Close long positions when the previous chart bar closes exactly at the upper band of a 20-period, two-deviation Bollinger Channel; close short positions when it closes exactly at the lower band. Also close positions when enabled aggregate profit targets or money-based trailing and drawdown rules are reached; move stops past entry after the configured favorable price movement.
