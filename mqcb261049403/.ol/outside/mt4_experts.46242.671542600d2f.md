During the allowed trading hours, derive a range from the highest high and lowest low across the current and prior weekly bars. Divide it into evenly spaced levels; place a sell limit at each level above the current bid and a buy limit at each level at or below it. Once more than one order has executed and the pending-order count is two below the configured grid size, place a new limit at the penultimate executed price, choosing sell above the bid and buy otherwise. Optionally reset pending orders daily. Close the strategy’s market positions when account-wide floating profit reaches the configured percentage of balance, then reset pending orders. If configured, close all of the strategy’s positions and pending orders outside the trading hours.
