When the base instrument’s moving average rises across three consecutive readings, signal a base long; when it falls across them, signal a base short. For each enabled hedge, calculate the rolling correlation between the base and hedge instruments using each instrument’s price deviation from its own moving average. Open the base position and corresponding hedge only when that hedge’s correlation meets its configured positive or negative threshold, trading only if the base position and hedge position are not already open. Set each hedge to follow the base direction or take the opposite direction according to its configuration. Close each hedge group when its profit exceeds its target: use price profit alone when the estimated combined swap is positive, otherwise include swap in the profit total.
