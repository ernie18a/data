Compare the executable cross rate implied by EUR/USD and GBP/USD with the executable EUR/GBP rate, including all three pairs’ spreads and the round-trip commission cost. When the implied rate is sufficiently above EUR/GBP to cover those costs, close any opposing basket; if no positions remain, sell EUR/USD, buy GBP/USD, and buy EUR/GBP. When the implied rate is sufficiently below EUR/GBP to cover those costs, close any opposing basket; if no positions remain, buy EUR/USD, sell GBP/USD, and sell EUR/GBP.
