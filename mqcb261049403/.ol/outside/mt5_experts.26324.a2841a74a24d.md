On each tick, open a trade in the configured initial direction if there is no closed trade history; otherwise open in the opposite direction of the most recently closed trade. Use the starting position size initially and after a profitable or breakeven close; after a loss, multiply the most recently closed trade’s size by the configured multiplier. Set existing trades’ stop loss and take profit to the same configured distance from their entry, tightening stops only. New trades are opened without attached stop loss or take profit.
