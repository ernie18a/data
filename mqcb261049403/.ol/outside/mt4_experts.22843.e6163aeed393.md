On a new bar, open a long when progressively longer moving averages are strictly stacked from shortest to longest, a fast/slow moving-average trend filter is bullish, the recent candle ranges overlap, and price is sufficiently far from an earlier close. Open a short under the inverse moving-average alignment and bearish trend filter, when the corresponding recent candle ranges overlap and price is sufficiently far from an earlier close. Limit entries to the configured maximum number of open trades. Close positions at their attached stop-loss or take-profit; tighten stops as favorable movement grows, move stops beyond breakeven after sufficient favorable movement, and close positions when configured money or percentage profit targets are reached or account-level drawdown limits are breached.
