Generate a long signal when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, or when they are bullishly ordered, recent momentum is positive, and price breaks above the recent high. Generate a short signal using the inverse conditions: bearish EMA cross, or bearish ordering with negative momentum and a break below the recent low. Score the signal using higher-timeframe EMA bias, the EUR/USD EMA bias as a gold macro proxy, volatility relative to its recent average, spread quality, session quality, news status, and price location within the recent range. Trade only if the score meets the configured minimum and any enabled hard quality filters pass; a strong score selects runner management. Size positions from equity risk and stop distance, reducing size when the macro bias opposes the signal. Set an ATR-based stop and target, requiring the expected reward relative to risk to remain adequate after spread cost. For open trades, close at the stop, target, or time limit; optionally close a non-runner once it has remained profitable long enough and reached both the required risk multiple and net profit. Optionally bank qualifying early profit, take staged partial profits, move the stop to breakeven after sufficient favorable movement, and trail it by an ATR-based distance. Flatten open positions when a news block is active if configured.
