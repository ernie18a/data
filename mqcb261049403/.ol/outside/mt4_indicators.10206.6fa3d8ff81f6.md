For each monitored currency pair, independently display directional readings from five indicators; do not combine them into a trade entry, exit, or position. Moving averages: bullish when the short average is above the medium average, with an extreme bullish reading if the medium average is also above the long average; bearish when the short average is below the medium average, with an extreme bearish reading if the medium average is also below the long average. Otherwise the short-versus-medium comparison sets the simple direction. Stochastic: above the midpoint is bullish and below it bearish; readings above the upper threshold or below the lower threshold are extreme. CCI: positive is bullish and negative bearish; values beyond the positive or negative outer threshold are extreme. RSI: above the midpoint is bullish and below it bearish; values above the upper band or below the lower band are extreme. MACD: an above-zero main line below its signal line is simple bullish, and an above-zero main line above its signal is extreme bullish; a below-zero main line above its signal is simple bearish, and a below-zero main line below its signal is extreme bearish. Leave readings unassigned where the source's strict comparisons do not match, including equality boundaries.
