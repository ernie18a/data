For the traded instrument, open or add a long after consecutive bullish candles with sufficient tick volume (and, if enabled, sufficiently large bodies relative to ATR), provided the optional RSI filter is in its permitted bullish range, the higher-timeframe trend filter is bullish, ADX confirms strong bullish direction, and price is inside the market-profile value area but outside the point-of-control avoidance zone. Add only when price has moved down far enough from the lowest existing long entry; widen this spacing as the long grid grows using Fibonacci-like ratios. Open or add a short under the mirrored conditions: consecutive bearish candles with sufficient volume and optional ATR body confirmation, RSI in its permitted bearish range, bearish higher-timeframe trend, strong bearish ADX direction, acceptable value-area location away from the point of control, and sufficient upward movement from the highest existing short entry. Grid size may increase with the number of same-direction positions, subject to a cap. Open a counter-direction hedge when price moves adversely by the hedge trigger distance from its saved reference price, if hedge capacity remains and the hedge-specific trend and ADX filters agree with the hedge direction. Exit or reduce positions when individual stop-loss or take-profit levels are reached; trail enabled stops or targets, and move basket targets toward the volume-weighted average entry plus a small profit offset. Close configured groups of positions when their aggregate profit reaches its target, close profitable positions when their profit threshold is met if enabled, and close profitable hedges at their hedge-profit threshold, optionally reducing their size. When a directional or total position limit is reached, optionally close the oldest eligible position according to its profit or breakeven category.
