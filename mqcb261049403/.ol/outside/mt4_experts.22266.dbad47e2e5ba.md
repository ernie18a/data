On each new bar, consider entries only while below the configured trade limit. Buy when either the chart-timeframe RSI or CCI shows the coded bullish divergence (the value on the prior bar exceeds a scanned older value while the prior bar’s high is below that scanned bar’s high), the fast moving average is above the slow one, the specified adjacent candles overlap, monthly RSI is rising, and the current bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago. Sell when either indicator shows the coded bearish divergence (the prior-bar value is below a scanned older value while the prior bar’s high is above that scanned bar’s high), the fast average is below the slow one, the specified adjacent candles overlap, monthly RSI is falling, and the current ask is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago. Attach the configured stop loss and take profit to each entry. Close the instrument’s basket when its aggregate profit reaches the configured balance-based profit threshold or loss limit; also close it when the enabled equity stop is exceeded. Optional exits close the basket at a configured money profit, or after aggregate profit has risen and then fallen by the configured trailing amount. Move a long stop above entry or a short stop below entry once the position has moved favorably by the configured trigger distance.
