On a new bar, if the trade limit and risk checks permit, open long when price has moved sufficiently from a recent close, both MACD readings are positive, the fast moving average is below the slow one, a recent-bar range condition holds, and the monthly RSI is rising. Open short under the corresponding conditions when price has moved sufficiently from a recent close, both MACD readings are negative, the same moving-average and range conditions hold, and monthly RSI is falling. Close positions at their stop loss or take profit; optional account-level rules also close trades when aggregate profit reaches a money or balance percentage target, drawdown exceeds an equity-based limit, or a money trailing-profit threshold is breached. Move stops into profit after a favorable move and trail stops using recent candle extremes or price movement.
