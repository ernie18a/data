For each currency, calculate its strength from the closes of currency pairs containing it, orienting pair prices so increases represent strength in that currency, then compare the strength with its moving average. In trend mode, mark the currency green when strength is above its average and red when below; in counter-trend mode, reverse those colors. For a traded pair, when the base- and quote-currency indicators show the same color in the same mode, buy on green and sell on red. No exit rule is specified.
