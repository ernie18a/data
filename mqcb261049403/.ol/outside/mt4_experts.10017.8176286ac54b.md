On the current day, select an upcoming economic news event whose currency matches one of the traded instrument’s currencies and whose importance is enabled for trading. During the configured pre-event window, if no trade or pending order is already open for that instrument and event, place a buy stop above the recent one-minute high and a sell stop below the recent one-minute low, with configured entry offsets; attach stop-loss and take-profit levels. Before the event, update pending orders to follow the latest calculated levels until the modification cutoff. After the event, if one stop order triggers, cancel the opposite pending order when one-cancels-the-other is enabled. Delete any untriggered pending orders after the post-event wait expires. For open trades, optionally move the stop to breakeven after the configured profit threshold and trail it once the configured trailing distance is reached.
