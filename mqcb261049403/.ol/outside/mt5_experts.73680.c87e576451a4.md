On each completed M5 bar, consider entries only during the enabled Asia, Europe, or US sessions, with acceptable spread and no active loss-streak or balance-drawdown pause. Build support and resistance zones from higher-timeframe candle bodies and gaps. Around each zone, group same-direction confirmations: rejection at the zone; sweep and reclaim of a recent swing; repeated near-misses; engulfing; engulfing at a gap zone; trendline confluence; QML reversal; QML plus engulfing; breakout and retest with trendline confluence; or an H4 range sweep and reclaim. A reaction trade requires at least five distinct confirmations matching one of the enabled confirmation combinations (supersets qualify). Reject reaction trades in normal conditions and range-lock conditions; apply the premium/discount filter, and in a narrow range require an entry near the matching range edge unless a recent range break-and-hold qualifies.

A separate momentum route may trade only during expansion or a recent break-and-hold. Require at least three of: directional close beyond the prior range, displacement candle, direction-matching fair-value gap, hold outside the range, or aligned H1 directional movement; a fair-value gap is mandatory. Apply the configured directional group rules: in expansion, require the range break, displacement, fair-value gap, and H1 pressure together, and allow only the selected proven confirmation groups; in break-and-hold, require the first three momentum conditions and an allowed confirmation group. If both routes qualify, use the momentum route.

An independent strong-trend route buys a close above the recent M5 range with displacement, rising D1 and H4 movement, and price in the lower portion of the H1 range. It can enter immediately. A qualifying buy also arms a temporary continuation setup: while armed, buy after a bounded pullback that retests the broken range or taps the bullish gap midpoint, then closes bullish with displacement; price must be discounted or satisfy that retest condition. Strong-trend sells require a close below the range, displacement, and either falling H4 movement or sufficiently strong rising D1 movement, but the configured sell entry and sell pullback routes are disabled.

For valid entries, place a structural stop beyond the relevant zone, swing, or signal candle; reject stops outside the allowed distance. Target the nearest directional prior-day/week or recent-swing liquidity level if it offers the required reward multiple, otherwise use the configured reward projection. Close at stop or target; after the configured favorable move, take a partial profit and move the remaining stop to approximately breakeven.
