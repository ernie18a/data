Mirror each newly opened master position on the same symbol when its spread is within the configured limit and no copy already exists. Use the master direction unless reverse copying is enabled, in which case use the opposite direction. Size the copy by matching the master volume, applying a configured multiplier, or using a configured fixed volume; clamp and round it to the symbol’s permitted volume increments. Preserve the master’s stop-loss and take-profit distances from its entry at the copied entry, reversing those distances when copying the opposite direction. On master modifications, update the copied stops and targets, reflecting them for reverse copies; for match-volume or multiplier sizing, reduce the copied volume if the new target is smaller, but do not increase it. Close the copied position when the master position closes.
