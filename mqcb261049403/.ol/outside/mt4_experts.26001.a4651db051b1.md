On each new bar, consider entries only while the open-trade count is below its cap and margin is sufficient. A long setup occurs when the prior candle’s upper wick is at least half its full range, the fast typical-price weighted moving average exceeds the slow one on the selected higher timeframe, and the current bid is farther from the close two bars ago than a configured price-distance threshold. A short setup occurs when the prior candle’s lower wick is at least half its range, the fast average is below the slow one, and the current ask is farther from the prior bar’s close than that threshold. In live trading, additionally require the recent high on the high-fractal trend line to exceed its older high and the recent low on the low-fractal line not to be below its older low for a long; require the high-line condition to fail and the recent low to be below its older low for a short. In testing, omit these trend-line filters. Open qualifying positions with protective stop-loss and take-profit levels. Close positions when an enabled basket profit target in money or as a percentage of balance is reached; an enabled money-based trailing rule also closes the basket after profit retreats from its peak by the configured money stop, and closes losing positions when loss exceeds the equity-risk threshold adjusted by a loss multiplier. When enabled, move a profitable long’s stop above entry or a profitable short’s stop below entry after its profit threshold is reached.
