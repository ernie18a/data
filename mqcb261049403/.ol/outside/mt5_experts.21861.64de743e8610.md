For each day, calculate the high and low of the price range between the configured start and end times. On each completed bar, compare its close with these range boundaries. Enter long on the first close above the upper boundary after the preceding bar was not above it; enter short on the first close below the lower boundary after the preceding bar was not below it. Close a short when a completed bar is above the upper boundary, and close a long when a completed bar is below the lower boundary. Close any open position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit. Attach the configured stop-loss and take-profit levels when entering.
