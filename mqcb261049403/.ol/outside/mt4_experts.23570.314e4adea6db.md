On each new bar, allow entries only during the configured trading hours and when trading is permitted. For a fresh position, require the prior bar’s monthly MACD main line to be above its signal line and a confirmed upper fractal to satisfy both: higher-timeframe Ichimoku conversion line above the base line, the low two bars back below the fractal, and the previous bar’s open above it; then go long. The coded short condition is unreachable: it requires MACD main above its signal for the entry check, then below it for a short, so it never opens a short. After a losing trade, if the most recent loss is later than the most recent win, use the same entry signal to open in that signal’s direction with an increased position size. Close the strategy’s positions when their combined profit reaches a balance-based profit target; when combined loss reaches a balance-based hard-loss limit; or when combined loss reaches the configured loss limit and at least three positions are open. Also close positions if strategy profit falls below zero and its loss exceeds a configured fraction of the running account-equity high.
