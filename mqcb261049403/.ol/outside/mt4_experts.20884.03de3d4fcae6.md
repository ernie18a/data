All versions use the traded instrument’s own prices for their signals; account data also gates entries and some exits. On a new bar, the only reachable initial signal is short: the previous bar’s fast simple moving average is below its slow simple moving average, and the previous completed monthly MACD main line is nonzero and above its signal line. The apparent long branch is unreachable because its moving-average condition contradicts the enclosing condition. Open only when the instrument has no open or pending order, trading is allowed, sufficient history is available, and the account’s projected free margin and stop-out test permit the order. Existing positions can trigger additional same-direction orders after an adverse price move, subject to margin checks.

Where enabled, the equity stop runs on a new bar: close this strategy’s market positions on the instrument when their combined gross loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account equity reference. On each evaluation, that reference becomes the previous sampled equity if the stored reference is below it; otherwise it becomes current equity. Reset the reference to current equity first if the strategy has no market positions.

In the versions with a balance-based profit target, on each tick accumulate gross profit for the instrument’s open market orders in descending order-list position, starting at the invalid position immediately after the last order and stopping before position zero. After each step, if the running sum reaches the configured fraction of account balance, close all market orders and delete all pending orders on the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

In the version with basket protection, on each tick sum profit, swap and commission for this strategy identifier’s market positions on the instrument. If any exist, close them when their net profit reaches a configured fraction of account balance, their net loss reaches a hard-stop fraction of balance, or their net loss reaches a separate configured fraction of balance while the basket has at least the specified number of trades. When any of these conditions triggers, skip the rest of that tick.
