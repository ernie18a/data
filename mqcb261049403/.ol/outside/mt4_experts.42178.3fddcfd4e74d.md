For each open trade within the selected symbol scope, set any enabled protective stop or target that is missing, measuring its distance from the entry price and respecting the instrument’s minimum stop distance. For a long, place the stop below entry and target above; for a short, place the stop above entry and target below. If trailing protection is enabled, it takes precedence over break-even: once favorable movement exceeds its activation threshold, advance the stop in price-step increments, offset it from the stepped price, and only tighten the existing stop. If configured, constrain the trailing stop to the break-even level. Otherwise, if break-even protection is enabled, once favorable movement reaches its threshold and the existing stop has not reached the required level, move the stop to entry plus an offset for a long or entry minus an offset for a short, only tightening it. In hidden-stop mode, close a long when bid reaches its hidden target or falls to its hidden stop; close a short when ask reaches its hidden target or rises to its hidden stop. There is no logic to initiate long or short positions.
