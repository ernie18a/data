On each new bar, consider entries only when trading is allowed, sufficient history and margin are available, and the open-trade limit has not been reached. The shared pattern gate requires a lower fractal somewhere in the recent lookback and a bullish candle whose body exceeds the preceding bearish candle’s body. Open a long when that gate passes, price has moved sufficiently from an earlier close, the fast moving average is above the slow one, the recent candle lows/highs satisfy the stated overlap condition, and monthly RSI is rising. Open a short when the same pattern gate passes, price has moved sufficiently from an earlier close, the fast moving average is below the slow one, the recent candle lows/highs satisfy its overlap condition, and monthly RSI is falling. Attach a stop loss and take profit to each entry. Close the EA’s basket when its profit reaches a balance-based target, when its loss reaches a hard balance-based limit, or when its loss reaches a smaller balance-based limit while at least three trades are open. Optional money management can close the basket at a specified total profit, trail a positive basket’s peak profit and close after a specified pullback, or close a losing basket when its loss exceeds a threshold relative to the account’s recent equity high. An equity-risk condition can also close the symbol’s trades when their combined loss exceeds a fraction of the equity high.
