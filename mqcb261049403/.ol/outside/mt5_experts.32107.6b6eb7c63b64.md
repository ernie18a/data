Evaluate up to eight configured indicator-based rules on each update. A rule may trigger when an indicator is nonempty; has the selected positive or negative sign; crosses another indicator or a level in the selected direction; is above, below, equal to, or unequal to another indicator or level; or when a configured expression evaluates with the selected sign or truth value. For crossings, compare current values with the previous bar’s values and trigger only on a transition across the comparator. When a rule triggers, perform its configured action: open long, open short, close long, close short, close both, combine an entry with closing the opposite side, or request a stop-loss or take-profit modification. Apply only enabled rules; indicator and expression inputs, comparisons, directions, and actions are configurable.
