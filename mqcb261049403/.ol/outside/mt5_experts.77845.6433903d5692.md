For each account position or pending order whose trade identifier matches a configured source pattern, create and maintain a linked mirror on the same instrument. For an open position, take the opposite direction and use the source volume; swap its stop-loss and take-profit price levels. For pending orders, keep the entry price and invert the order type: buy limit to sell stop, buy stop to sell limit, sell limit to buy stop, and sell stop to buy limit; skip stop-limit orders. Keep the mirror’s volume, protective levels, pending price, and expiration synchronized with source changes. Mirror partial reductions in source volume with corresponding reductions in the mirror. When the source position closes, close its mirror; when a source pending order is removed or filled, remove its still-pending mirror. Never reopen a mirror that was already closed.
