For each configured instrument, allow new entries only during its configured trading hours and after any post-failure waiting period. If there is no active trade, open in the configured starting direction at the initial position size. Close an active trade when price reaches its configured profit or loss threshold; when trailing is enabled and the profit target is at least as large as the loss threshold, instead update the stop in the favorable direction, either from the start or after price has moved favorably by the loss distance, according to the configured trailing mode. After a trade closes profitably or at break-even, open another trade in the same direction at the initial size. After a loss, open in the opposite direction with the previous size multiplied by the configured growth factor, rounded down to the permitted size increment; once the configured reversal limit is reached, use the initial size again. If an increased-size reversal cannot be opened, retry only after the configured waiting period.
