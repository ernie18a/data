For the selected currency trio, compare the third pair’s market price with the cross rate implied by the first two pairs, using executable bid/ask sides: generally, implied ask = first ask ÷ second bid and implied bid = first bid ÷ second ask; when both legs share a quote currency such as JPY, use first ask ÷ second ask and first bid ÷ second bid. Add the three pairs’ spread and commission costs, plus the configured minimum arbitrage buffer, to form the entry threshold. If implied ask exceeds the third pair’s ask by more than this threshold, close any opposing trio positions and, when flat, sell the first pair and buy the second and third. If implied bid is below the third pair’s bid by more than the threshold, close any opposing trio positions and, when flat, buy the first pair and sell the second and third. Close the strategy’s positions once they exceed the configured maximum holding time. Only evaluate entries when market data and account balance are valid, equity is at least half the balance, free margin covers all three trades, and the daily trade limit has not been reached.
