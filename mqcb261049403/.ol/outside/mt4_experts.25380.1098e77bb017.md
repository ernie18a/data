On each new chart candle, open a long if the fast weighted average is below the slow weighted average, the recent candle ranges overlap, price is sufficiently far from a recent close, and the MACD main line is above its signal line on the chart and the configured higher timeframe. Open a short under the same average and distance conditions when the alternate recent candle ranges overlap and MACD is below its signal line on both timeframes. Allow entries only while below the position limit; attach stop loss and take profit. Close the instrument’s open positions when enabled aggregate-profit or balance-percentage targets are reached, when aggregate losses exceed the configured equity-risk threshold, or when the enabled money-based trailing exit is triggered. Manage open positions with the enabled break-even and price- or candle-based trailing stops.
