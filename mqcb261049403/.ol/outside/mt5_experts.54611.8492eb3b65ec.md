Trade only on enabled months, weekdays, and times, and only when no position is already open. Calculate the current daily high-low range as a percentage of the daily high; enter only when it is above the minimum and below the maximum allowed range. Divide the daily range to form upper and lower breakout zones, using a tighter zone when the range reaches the configured midpoint. Buy when the previous candle closes above the upper zone, the current candle opens at or above that close, the current bid remains above the zone, and the previous candle’s range passes the spread filter. Sell symmetrically when the previous candle closes below the lower zone, the current candle opens at or below that close, the ask remains below the zone, and the same spread filter passes. Optionally reverse a profitable day’s last position when price closes inside the zones and the daily range exceeds its reversal threshold: reverse a short to long or a long to short.

Close positions at the configured closing time; optionally close a losing position after its time limit. Close positions and stop new trading when daily or weekly realized profit or open profit crosses its configured gain or loss limit, when account equity reaches its protected floor based on starting capital and cumulative results, or when the overall profit target is reached. Limit entries after a configured count of daily losing trades, adjusting that count upward after qualifying profitable prior days. Optionally take partial profits or reduce positions at configured open-profit thresholds. Attach a take-profit and choose the stop from the daily range, beyond the prior signal candle, or at a fixed distance, in that priority order when enabled; widen a fixed-distance stop when the daily range is elevated but still below the no-trade maximum. Trail stops on profitable positions, moving them with price after the profit distance is reached.
