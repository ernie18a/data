On a new bar, open a long if the fast weighted moving average is above the slow one on both the chart timeframe and the hourly timeframe, the prior candle-range condition and price-distance filter are met, and MACD is above its signal line on both timeframes. Open a short if both moving-average comparisons and both MACD comparisons are reversed and the corresponding candle-range and price-distance filters are met. Respect the maximum open-trade limit and trade-eligibility checks. Close positions at their fixed stop-loss or take-profit; move stops to lock in profit after a position advances sufficiently, and trail stops as favorable price movement grows. Close the symbol's positions when enabled aggregate profit targets are reached, or when aggregate loss exceeds the configured equity-risk threshold.
