On each new chart bar, if there is capacity for another position, consider entries as follows. For a long, require the current bid to be farther from the close two bars back than a configured retracement distance; a recent-bar setup must show the short, medium, and long weighted moving averages of typical price ordered upward, with a scanned bar’s low at or below the short average and above the long average, and the ask above the highest high of the recent lookback. Also require the fast weighted moving average to exceed the slow one, the low two bars back to be below the prior bar’s high, and monthly RSI to be higher than its previous reading. For a short, apply the mirrored conditions: price-distance filter, the three averages ordered downward, a scanned bar’s high at or above the short average and below the long average, bid below the recent lowest low, fast average below slow, the prior bar’s low below the high two bars back, and monthly RSI lower than its previous reading. Open at most one position per bar, with a stop loss and take profit set at configured price distances.

For open positions, close the symbol’s positions when aggregate open profit is negative beyond a configured fraction of the tracked equity peak, if the equity stop is enabled. If enabled, close symbol positions when their aggregate profit reaches a configured money target or percentage of account balance. A configured per-position trailing stop moves the stop in the profitable direction after price advances by its activation distance; break-even management moves stops after a configured favorable move. The money-trailing routine also attempts to close positions on its configured profit giveback or loss threshold. The documented trend-based exit toggle has no operative effect in the code.
