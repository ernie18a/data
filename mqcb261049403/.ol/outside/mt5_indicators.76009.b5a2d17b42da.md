Trend riding: Go long in an established uptrend or short in an established downtrend when momentum confirms and sentiment agrees; enter on a pullback to a fast or middle trend average. Avoid entry when trend stage signals reversal risk or momentum diverges. Stay flat around imminent high impact news and when the higher timeframe is sideways or at reversal risk. Breakouts: After a volatility squeeze, go long or short only on a confirmed channel breakout, in its direction; do not chase a mere test. Fade: After price breaks above a range then returns inside, go short; after it breaks below then returns inside, go long. News: Near an important event, either stay flat or place buy and sell stop orders beyond the pre-event range, canceling the opposite order after one triggers. Carry: Prefer the direction with positive overnight carry only when the trend agrees; avoid holding through reversal risk. For trend entries, place the stop beyond a relevant trend average or opposite channel edge; for breakouts, inside the channel or near a Bollinger Band; for fades, beyond the failed breakout extreme. Fade targets are the range midpoint or opposite Bollinger Band.
