For a currency pair, calculate each currency’s strength as the compounded relative change across its seven quoted pairs, inverting pair changes when that currency is the quote currency. Compare base-currency strength minus quote-currency strength with the instrument’s ATR-scaled threshold. Go long when the difference crosses upward through the positive threshold and the prior signal state is not long; go short when it crosses downward through the negative threshold and the prior state is not short. A new opposite signal reverses the direction; no separate exit signal is defined.
