For each configured currency pair, open a minimum-size long position initially. If an open position’s loss exceeds a threshold proportional to its tracked size, close it and reverse its direction; open the reversed position when no position is open and free margin is sufficient. If its profit exceeds a threshold proportional to tracked size, add a minimum-size position in the same direction and increase tracked size. Close a position when its profit exceeds a separate fixed threshold. If account-wide floating loss or profit exceeds its respective limit, close all positions and reset tracked sizes; trading can then resume.
