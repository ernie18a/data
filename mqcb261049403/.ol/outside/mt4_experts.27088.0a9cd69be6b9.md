For open positions, close the managed basket when its combined profit, including costs, reaches a configured profit threshold based on account balance or a configured loss limit based on account balance. If enabled, also close positions when their floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account equity high-water mark. Optional monetary controls close the basket at a profit target or after profit retreats by a configured amount from its peak. The entry signals use only the traded instrument’s price data, so they do not meet the stated external-data criterion.
