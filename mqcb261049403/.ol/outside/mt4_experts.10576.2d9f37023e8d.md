When flat, follow the selected trend mode: in the original version, compare the current price with a linear weighted moving average of closing prices on a configurable timeframe; buy above it and sell below it. In the updated version, compare the current price with an exponential moving average on the chart timeframe; buy above and sell below. A forced direction can instead open a long or short whenever flat. While positions are open, add another position in the same direction when the latest position has moved against the trade by the configured distance, subject to the configured trade limit and stage sizing. Close all positions in that direction when any reaches the configured favorable profit distance. In the updated version, when moving-average reversal handling is enabled, close long positions and switch to short mode when the prior close is below the moving average; close short positions and switch to long mode when it is above. Alternatively, when stop handling is enabled, close all positions in the latest position's direction if its adverse move reaches the configured stop distance at the specified trade count. Optional controls can close all positions, close only positions above a profit threshold, or delete pending stop orders.
