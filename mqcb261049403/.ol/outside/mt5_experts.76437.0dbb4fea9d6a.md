Periodically calculate current-day drawdown as the percentage decline from the day’s starting balance, reconstructed from current balance and the day’s realized profits, commissions, and swaps, to current account equity. If drawdown reaches the hard limit, close all open positions and cancel all pending orders, then stop further risk actions for the day. Otherwise, if it reaches the soft limit, periodically partially close the open position with the greatest floating loss, including swap; if the calculated partial volume is below the instrument’s minimum, close the whole position. Take no action when there is no losing position.
