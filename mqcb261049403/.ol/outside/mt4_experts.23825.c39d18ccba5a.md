On each tick, for open positions in the current instrument, close the EA’s basket when its net profit reaches a balance-based profit target, when its loss reaches a balance-based hard-loss limit, or when its loss reaches the configured loss limit and the basket contains at least three trades. Also close the basket when enabled money-based take-profit or trailing-risk rules trigger: close after aggregate profit reaches its money target; while profit is positive but below that target, close on a retracement from the recorded peak by the configured money stop; while aggregate profit is negative, close when its loss exceeds the configured equity-based risk threshold. Close matching long positions if the previous bar’s close exactly equals the upper Bollinger Band, and matching short positions if it exactly equals the lower band. Long and short entries are initiated by manual button clicks, with no data-based entry signal.
