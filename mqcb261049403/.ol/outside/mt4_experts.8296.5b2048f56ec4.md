Identify confirmed swing highs and lows as local extrema. For a short signal, connect a more recent swing high to the next older, higher swing high and extend the descending resistance line forward. Signal short when a bar closes above that line and at least one breakout qualifier holds: the prior close is below the close two bars earlier; the bar opens above the line; or twice the prior close minus its low is below the line. For a long signal, connect a more recent swing low to the next older, lower swing low and extend the ascending support line forward. Signal long when a bar closes below that line and at least one qualifier holds: the prior close is above the close two bars earlier; the bar opens below the line; or twice the prior close minus its high is above the line. Invalidate a short breakout if a later bar makes a low below the lowest low between the two swing highs, or closes below that low plus 23.6% of the rise to the more recent swing high. Invalidate a long breakout if a later bar makes a high above the highest high between the two swing lows, or closes above that high minus 23.6% of the fall to the more recent swing low. Open a position in the signal direction when flat; an opposing signal closes the open position. An optional reversal setting swaps long and short signals. Signals may be acted on at the current bar or the preceding completed bar, according to the configured execution timing.
