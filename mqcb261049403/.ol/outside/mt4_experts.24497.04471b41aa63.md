Open a long position when the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the MACD main line is above its signal line on the designated higher timeframe, and the low from two bars ago is below the previous bar’s high. Open a short when the fast average is below the slow one, the MACD main line is below its signal line, and the previous bar’s low is below the high from two bars ago. Limit entries to the configured maximum number of open positions and apply the configured protective stop and profit target. For open positions, move the stop beyond breakeven after a favorable move reaches its configured trigger. Optional account-level exits close positions when their combined profit reaches a configured cash or balance-based target, or when the money-based trailing rule is breached. The exit routine also closes either direction if either short or long exponential moving-average groups become fully equal, or the previous close is at or below the lower Bollinger Band.
