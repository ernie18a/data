Open a long only when the rate signal is bullish: form a rate estimate from weighted moving averages on the chart timeframe and two higher timeframes, average those estimates over the lookback, and treat the latest estimate being below that average as bullish. Also require the fast typical-price weighted moving average to exceed the slow one, the current monthly RSI to exceed its prior value, the specified recent candle low to be below the adjacent prior candle high, and the live bid to be farther from the close two candles back than the configured candle-distance threshold. Open a short on the bearish rate signal (latest estimate above its lookback average) when the fast average is below the slow one, monthly RSI is falling, the specified recent low is below the adjacent prior high, and the live ask is farther from the prior candle close than that threshold. Entries are gated by the open-trade limit and available margin. When enabled, exit positions if the relevant group of short-period or long-period exponential moving averages becomes exactly aligned, or if the prior close is at or below the lower Bollinger band; this same exit test is applied to longs and shorts. Also close positions under enabled aggregate profit-target, profit-pullback, or account-equity drawdown rules. Manage open trades with price-based stop-loss, take-profit, trailing-stop, and optional break-even stop adjustments.
