On each new bar, consider a short entry when the bearish candle-state check passes (as implemented, it always passes), at least one of the previous three stochastic readings is below its lower threshold, the fast weighted moving average of typical price is below the slow one, the prior-bar low is below the high from two bars ago, and the current ask is farther from the prior close than the configured retracement distance. No long entries are generated because the candle-state calculation never produces its bullish result. Apply the configured position-count, margin, and trade-validity checks before entry, with a stop loss and take profit. Close the symbol’s positions when the enabled account-risk limit is exceeded, an enabled aggregate profit target is reached, or the enabled money-based trailing rule gives back its allowed amount from the tracked favorable profit level. Also move stops to lock in a configured gain after price advances sufficiently in favor of an open position.
