On the traded instrument and current chart timeframe, enter long when the previous completed close was at or below the prior value of an exponential moving average of highs and the latest completed close is above its corresponding value, provided the current RSI is above its lower threshold, no long position is open, and the current hour is within the allowed trading window. Enter short when the previous completed close was at or above the prior value of an exponential moving average of lows and the latest completed close is below its corresponding value, provided current RSI is below its upper threshold, no short position is open, and the current hour is within the allowed window. Attach a fixed stop loss and take profit to each entry. At or after the window's end, close all positions. At a downward crossing, the code attempts to close short positions if any long position exists; at an upward crossing, it attempts to close long positions if any short position exists.
