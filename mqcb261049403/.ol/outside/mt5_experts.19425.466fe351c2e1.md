On each completed bar, calculate the selected price series and a trailing reversal level. Carry the prior level when price is unchanged. If current and prior prices are both below the prior level, set the new level to the lower of that level and current price increased by a proportional buffer; if both are above, set it to the higher of that level and current price decreased by the buffer. Otherwise set it below current price when current price is above the prior level, or above it when below. Generate a long signal when current price crosses above the new level while prior price was below the prior level; generate a short signal on the inverse crossing. A long signal closes shorts and may open a long; a short signal closes longs and may open a short. Open only when no position exists for the instrument and within the configured trading interval. Outside that interval, close all positions in the instrument. Optional stop-loss and take-profit orders can also close positions.
