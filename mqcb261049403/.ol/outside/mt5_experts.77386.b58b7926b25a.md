For each position within the configured symbol and trade filters, never open positions or close one whose floating profit, including swap and commission, is nonnegative. After the configured minimum holding period, evaluate enabled exit rules in order and close the losing position when the first applies: adverse price movement from entry exceeds a configured multiple of ATR; the position has reached its maximum age; on the last closed bar, the fast EMA is below the slow EMA for a long or above it for a short; closed-bar RSI is below the configured threshold for a long or above it for a short; or floating loss exceeds the configured monetary cap. In observe-only mode, record qualifying exits without closing positions.
