During permitted trading hours, close the strategy’s open positions outside those hours and on Friday. Use the traded instrument’s MACD main line: buy when it rises from the previous bar, or sell when it falls, subject to the corresponding side being enabled; an optional reverse setting swaps the direction. Open the first position immediately when a signal occurs. Add positions in the signal direction only after price moves against the most recent entry by a fixed distance, up to the trade limit; increase position size with each addition. Set an initial stop loss and take profit when configured. Once a position has moved favorably by the trailing threshold plus the entry spacing, move its stop to trail price and adjust its take profit. When the position count reaches the protection threshold, set a shared take profit based on the most favorable entry price for that direction. Account protection thresholds can stop further entries; the configured account-profit and account-drawdown checks do not themselves close positions in the shown code.
