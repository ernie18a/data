When flat, use the prior two completed hourly bars of a 14-period stochastic oscillator with 3-period smoothing: go long when its main line crosses above its signal line while below the lower extreme, and go short when it crosses below while above the upper extreme. Keep that direction for subsequent entries. Add another position only after price moves adversely from the most recent entry by a configured spacing; increase each added position’s size geometrically. Once the position count reaches a configured threshold, place the next entry in the opposite direction. Cap the number of open positions. Set take-profit orders and update the open positions’ targets when adding a position; close positions when their targets are reached. Apply configured stop-loss or account-level exit protections when enabled.
