During the London placement session, consider new entries only when the recent candle range exceeds the trading-timeframe ATR, volatility is within its normal range, the pre-London H1 range is valid and within an acceptable fraction of average daily range, and the spread is acceptable. For a long, require the Parabolic SAR to switch from above price to below it, MACD to be bullish, and Stochastic not to be overbought; place a buy stop above the latest SAR swing high with an ATR-based buffer, and only above the Asian range high. For a short, require the opposite SAR switch, bearish MACD, and Stochastic not to be oversold; place a sell stop below the latest SAR swing low with a buffer, and only below the Asian range low. Set the stop beyond the opposite SAR swing with its distance constrained relative to average daily range, and set the profit target as a multiple of that stop distance. Close a long on a bearish MACD crossover and a short on a bullish crossover. As price moves favorably, trail the stop and move it toward or beyond breakeven. At the daily account profit or loss limit, cancel pending orders and close positions; also cancel pending orders and close positions near the New York close or the instrument's session close.
