On each new 15-minute candle, consider long entries only; never open shorts. Block new entries during the configured spread, crash pause, or relevant high-impact news window. For a first long, require RSI below a volatility-adjusted oversold threshold (lower in elevated ATR regimes), a sequence of lower swing lows and lower swing highs, price near the latest swing-low support or sweeping below it and closing back above, and either a bullish rejection candle or rising RSI. For recovery longs, additionally require price to fall below the lowest existing buy by an ATR-scaled gap that widens with basket depth and after a crash; apply a manual depth limit when enabled. Require the same RSI, structure, support, and reversal checks, with a higher quality threshold for rescue entries; permit the configured deep-emergency exception only after sufficient drawdown and a larger ATR decline, while still requiring the quality threshold. Close the basket if equity falls below its balance-based loss limit, if profit reaches its target and then gives back the configured trailing amount, or, for multi-position baskets, if recovery-trailing profit gives back its allowance while remaining positive. When trimming is enabled and at least three positions are open, close the most profitable and most losing positions together if the winner and their combined profit meet their thresholds. News and account equity are external conditions; the directional entry signals use the traded instrument's own price and indicators.
