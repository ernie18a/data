This is a user-directed trade manager, not an autonomous entry signal. On request, it can open a market buy or sell on the current instrument. For matching open positions, close those with positive floating profit or those with negative floating profit; close all positions and delete pending orders; or partially close eligible positions and protect the remainder at entry plus a favorable lock distance. It can remove stop losses or apply a favorable locked stop to positions that have moved far enough in profit. When enabled, basket protection waits until filtered positions' combined profit reaches a trigger, calculates the price that would preserve the requested basket profit using the positions' net exposure, then moves their stops to that level when it improves protection. Other enabled stop management trails stops behind favorable price movement after a profit threshold, locks a per-position gain after sufficient favorable movement, or advances a stop in favorable increments as price moves by matching increments from its initial stop (or entry if no stop exists). Scalp positions are trailed similarly after favorable movement reaches a start threshold. Apply configured symbol, direction, magic, and comment filters where relevant; preserve take-profit levels when changing stops.
