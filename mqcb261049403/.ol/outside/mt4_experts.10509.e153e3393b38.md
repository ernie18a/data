After collecting at least five observations, go long when the relative-vigor main line exceeds its signal line by more than the adaptive midpoint threshold, their gap exceeds a minimum safety threshold, and both the traded instrument’s ask and close and a basket of five related currencies’ ask and close are higher than their respective earlier readings. Require the channel trend to be bullish: the current close has crossed above the prior upper volatility band, or the carried channel direction is bullish. Go short under the inverse conditions: the signal line exceeds the main line by more than the adaptive threshold, the gap clears the safety threshold, the instrument’s bid and close and the currency basket’s bid and close are lower than earlier readings, and the channel trend is bearish after a close crosses below the prior lower band or carries a bearish direction. Close an open long or short when its profit reaches a positive profit limit or its loss reaches a negative loss limit. When a new directional signal is acted on, first attempt to close the existing position.
