Across multiple price-sampling intervals, encode the recent sequence of adjacent price rises and falls as a pattern. For each pattern and risk distance, track simulated long outcomes from its occurrence: count a win if price rises by that distance first, and a loss if it falls by that distance first, with older results gradually downweighted. After sufficient observations, go long when the win share exceeds a threshold; go short when the loss share exceeds it. Limit repeat entries with a time cooldown. Close an opposing position when the new signal is materially stronger than the last recorded signal in that direction; also close a profitable position when the corresponding directional outcome share exceeds a lower threshold and the pattern has sufficient observations. Set protective stop and profit target from the risk distance, and trail stops as price moves favorably.
