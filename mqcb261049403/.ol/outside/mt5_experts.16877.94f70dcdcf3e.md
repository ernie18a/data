On each new bar, compare the short-term and long-term moving averages on the current and previous bars. Treat the trend as bullish when the short-term average is above the long-term average on both bars, bearish when it is below on both, and neutral otherwise. Record the first confirmed bullish or bearish trend without entering a trade. On a later confirmed change from bearish to bullish, close any short position and enter long; on a change from bullish to bearish, close any long position and enter short. Enter only when no position is open, and after a position exits for any reason, wait for the next confirmed trend change before re-entering.
