Form every three-currency combination from the selected currencies, preserving their supplied strength order. For each ring of pairs (A/B, A/C, B/C), the plus basket buys A/B, sells A/C, and buys B/C; the minus basket takes the opposite positions. In normal operation, open the selected basket or baskets across all three pairs when a ring has no positions. Optionally add another complete basket after its floating result reaches the configured adverse-loss step and the required time has elapsed; scale the step by the configured progression and respect group, order, spread, session, and trading limits. Close a basket when its aggregate floating profit reaches its configured target, or, if loss closing is enabled, when its loss reaches the configured limit; closing may apply to a whole basket or its oldest positions according to the selected mode. In automatic stepping mode, use the opposite basket's loss level to trigger a new basket and close a profitable basket when the opposing basket reaches its step condition; close both baskets when their combined profit reaches the basket target. A close-and-stop mode closes all positions immediately; a profit-and-stop mode waits for profitable closure before stopping. Apply configured side selection and operation mode throughout.
