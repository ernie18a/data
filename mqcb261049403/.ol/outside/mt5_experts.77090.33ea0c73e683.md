On a user’s Buy or Sell command, if trading is not blocked and the risk-sized order volume is valid, open that direction at the current market quote. Size volume so the distance from the quote to the stop, taken as the absolute distance between the user-set entry and stop lines, risks the configured share of account balance; place the stop that distance from the live quote and the take profit at the configured reward-to-risk multiple. The take-profit line itself does not determine the order. For managed positions, once favorable movement reaches the break-even trigger, move the stop to entry plus an offset for longs or entry minus an offset for shorts, only if that improves protection. Once favorable movement reaches the trailing start, move the stop to the current price minus the trailing distance for longs or plus it for shorts, only if it improves protection. If enabled, when equity loss from the day’s starting balance reaches the daily limit, or equity drawdown from the highest recorded balance reaches the drawdown limit, close all managed positions and block new orders until the next trading day. The stop distance may be initialized from current-period ATR times a multiplier when reset is requested; otherwise use a fixed default distance.
