Calculate NZD strength from contemporaneous closes of seven NZD currency pairs: negate pairs where NZD is the quote currency, add pairs where it is the base currency, scale the NZD/JPY close to account for its quote-unit convention, then average the signed values. Compare this strength with its moving average. In trend mode, above-average strength signals long NZD and below-average signals short NZD; in counter-trend mode, reverse those signals. For a traded pair with NZD as its base currency, use the NZD direction directly; with NZD as quote currency, invert it. The indicator represents long with green and short with red.
