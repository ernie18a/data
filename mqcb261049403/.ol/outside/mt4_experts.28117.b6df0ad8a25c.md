Convert each configured indicator into bullish, bearish, or neutral signals using its selected output rule. Enter long when the primary confirmation, optional second confirmation, exit filter, and baseline all agree bullish; apply any enabled extra filter and volume direction, and require the prior close to satisfy the baseline distance filter unless that filter is disabled. Enter short under the corresponding bearish conditions, with volume direction respected. A fresh qualifying signal is normally required; while continuation is enabled, a prior qualifying trade can permit continued entries while baseline conditions persist. Close a long if any required confirmation, exit filter, or baseline ceases to be bullish, or if an enabled extra filter fails; close a short on the corresponding bearish failures. Manage protective exits using volatility-scaled stop loss and take profit, with optional staged exits, break-even protection, and trailing stops.
