On each tick, compare BTC/USD’s bid price with ETH/USD’s. If the difference exceeds a positive threshold, and neither instrument has an open position, open a long BTC/USD position and a short ETH/USD position. If it falls below the corresponding negative threshold, under the same no-position condition, open a short BTC/USD position and a long ETH/USD position. In either case, skip entry if either instrument’s short-term ATR exceeds a volatility limit. Pause new entries when account drawdown reaches its limit, resuming only once equity recovers to a specified fraction of balance. Close all open positions together when their combined profit reaches a target.
