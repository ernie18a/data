On each new bar, if below the trade limit, go long when a higher-timeframe candle pulls back to a rising short weighted moving average (short average above the long average, candle low touches or crosses the short average while its open is above it), the chart-timeframe short weighted moving average is above the long one, the prior chart candles satisfy the specified low/high overlap, and bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago. Go short on the mirrored higher-timeframe pullback in a falling trend, with the chart short average below the long one, the specified prior-candle overlap, and ask sufficiently far from the previous close. Attach fixed-distance stop-loss and take-profit levels. Close the instrument’s trades when enabled aggregate profit reaches a cash or balance-percentage target, when aggregate losses exceed a configured fraction of the account equity high-water mark, or when a profitable basket retraces by a cash amount from its peak; also close losing baskets when their drawdown exceeds a scaled equity-risk threshold. Move a position’s stop beyond entry by a fixed offset after favorable movement reaches a threshold.
