Place paired buy-stop and sell-stop orders around price: use the nearest qualifying support and resistance formed by recent higher-timeframe highs/lows expanded by ATR, or fall back to offsets from the current quotes; at the scheduled time, use the prior daily high and low as the breakout boundaries. If only one side’s pending order remains, place the opposite-side hedge order. In trend mode, allow a hedge sell only when the bearish signal is present without the bullish signal, and a hedge buy only when the bullish signal is present without the bearish signal. The bullish signal requires RSI across three short-to-medium timeframes to have risen from below its midpoint to above its upper threshold, with the slow weighted EMA above the fast EMA; the bearish signal requires those RSI readings to have fallen from above the midpoint to below its upper threshold, with the slow EMA below the fast EMA. When aggregate open-trade profit falls below the allowed balance-based loss limit, delete pending orders and open a market position opposite the net exposure to hedge it; while blocked, suppress further entries in a direction when existing trades in that direction are losing. Close the basket after it reaches the profit target and then retreats from its recorded peak by the defined profit giveback; in trend mode, close the basket when the target is first reached, retaining trades whose stops have moved into profit. Apply an initial protective stop where configured, move stops to breakeven after the configured favorable move, and trail them in steps as price continues favorably. For losing trades without a take-profit, set a take-profit after the maximum holding period.
