When the configured time control permits execution, inspect each order matching the configured symbol scope and magic-number filter. If the open-price range filter is disabled with both bounds unset, or enabled with both bounds positive and the order’s open price strictly between them, act according to order type and enabled direction: delete buy-limit and buy-stop pending orders, delete sell-limit and sell-stop pending orders, close open long positions at the bid, and close open short positions at the ask. With time control enabled, execute only within the configured time interval, including intervals that cross midnight; equal start and stop times permit no execution. An enabled range filter with invalid bounds permits no action. The logic only exits or deletes orders; it does not open positions or trade options.
