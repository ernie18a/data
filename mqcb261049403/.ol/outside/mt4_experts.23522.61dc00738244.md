On each new bar, when no position is open, enter a long if the long-term moving average is above the still longer-term moving average, the candle two bars ago touched or pierced the lower Bollinger Band and the previous candle closed back above it, the fast moving average is below the slower entry moving average, and momentum’s distance from its neutral level is below the buy threshold on any of the last three closed bars. Enter a short if the long-term moving average is below the still longer-term moving average, the candle two bars ago touched or pierced the upper band and the previous candle closed back below it, the fast moving average is below the slower entry moving average, and momentum’s distance from neutral is below the sell threshold on any of the last three closed bars. Set protective stop-loss and take-profit levels at entry. Exit longs when the previous close exactly equals the upper Bollinger Band; exit shorts when it exactly equals the lower band. Also move a profitable position’s stop beyond breakeven after a profit trigger, and trail the stop once profit exceeds its trigger.
