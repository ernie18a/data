On a user-triggered buy action, open long positions only if the monthly MACD main line is above its signal line. On a user-triggered sell action, open short positions only if the monthly MACD main line is below its signal line. A user-triggered close action closes all positions on the traded instrument. For open positions, close longs when the previous bar’s close equals the upper Bollinger Band and close shorts when it equals the lower band. Close the EA’s positions on the instrument when their basket profit, including swap and commission, reaches its profit target; when basket loss reaches the hard loss limit; or when basket loss reaches the ordinary loss limit and at least three positions are open. Also close the EA’s positions when their profit-only loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s equity high. Optional money-based management closes all instrument positions at a cash profit target, trails a peak cash profit by closing when profit falls by a configured amount, and closes a losing basket when its loss exceeds an equity-based limit scaled by a loss factor. Individual positions also have stop-loss and take-profit levels; profitable positions may have their stops advanced to lock in gains, and stops trail price after sufficient favorable movement.
