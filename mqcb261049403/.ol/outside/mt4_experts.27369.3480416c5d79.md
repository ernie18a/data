For each enabled couple of two currency pairs formed from three currencies, open an initial matched hedge on each pair: one side buys or sells both pairs according to their currency overlap, and the opposite side takes the inverse positions. Respect the configured allowed sides. When adding to losing positions is enabled, add another matched set on a side when that side’s combined open profit, including trading costs, falls to or below its configured adverse-move threshold; widen the threshold according to the configured step progression. Allow entries only when trading is enabled, the market and spread meet configured limits, and order capacity permits. Close positions when configured profit targets are reached, using the selected per-side, whole-couple basket, or hybrid profit rule. If loss closing is enabled, close a whole side at its loss limit, or close its oldest matched set after the configured minimum grid depth and loss condition; otherwise do not close for loss. A stop mode may prohibit new entries while waiting for profit exits, or close all positions immediately, according to the selected operating mode.
