When the user activates the chart’s sell button, open a short position on the chart instrument. On each tick, manage open EURUSD short positions in newest-to-oldest order: while processing the first seven positions, if their average net profit exceeds a lower configured threshold, close the most profitable and least profitable eligible positions. For positions from the eighth onward, if the average net profit across the open EURUSD shorts exceeds a higher configured threshold, close the most profitable and least profitable eligible positions. Recheck these conditions as the loop processes positions.
