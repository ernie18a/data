For each bar, compare its opening price with the highest high and lowest low over a forward-looking horizon. Let the upward excursion be the highest high minus the open, and the downward excursion be the open minus the lowest low. In the first signal mode, normalize the imbalance between these excursions to a range from −1 to +1: emit buy when it exceeds a positive threshold, sell when it falls below the corresponding negative threshold, and otherwise emit no signal. In the second mode, emit buy when the upward excursion exceeds the take-profit distance while the downward excursion remains below the stop-loss distance; emit sell when the downward excursion exceeds the take-profit distance while the upward excursion remains below the stop-loss distance; otherwise emit no signal. On a buy signal, close an existing short and open a long; on a sell signal, close an existing long and open a short. A neutral signal does not close a position.
