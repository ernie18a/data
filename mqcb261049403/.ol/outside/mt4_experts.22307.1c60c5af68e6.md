On each new chart bar, if the number of open positions on the instrument is below the configured cap and the current bar’s tick volume is no greater than one, consider a long only when all of these hold: either the recent lower channel boundary is at or above the previous low on the mapped higher timeframe and below that bar’s open, or the channel midpoint is at or below that low and above that open; the fast weighted moving average of typical price is above the slow one; the low two bars ago is below the previous bar’s high; and the current bid is farther from the close two bars ago than the configured retracement distance. Open with a stop below and target above the entry. The short-entry branch is unreachable because the long branch returns before control can reach it, so the strategy opens no shorts. Close the instrument’s positions when their aggregate loss exceeds the equity-based risk limit, or, when enabled, when their aggregate profit reaches the money target or their money trailing rule triggers. When enabled, close the strategy’s basket once its net profit reaches the balance-based profit threshold or its net loss reaches the balance-based loss limit. Move a long stop above entry or a short stop below entry after its favorable price move reaches the configured break-even trigger. The fixed stop and target also close positions when price reaches them.
