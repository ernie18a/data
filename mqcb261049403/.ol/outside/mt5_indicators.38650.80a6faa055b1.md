For each configured currency pair, measure the current bid’s position as a percentage of that period’s high-low range. Assign a higher discrete strength score as the bid moves through progressively higher levels of the range. For each currency, average its scores when it is the base currency and the inverse scores when it is the quote currency. Compare the traded pair’s base and quote averages: signal long when the base is stronger, short when the quote is stronger, and wait when they are equal.
