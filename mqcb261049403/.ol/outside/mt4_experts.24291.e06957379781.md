When enabled, close all positions in the traded instrument if their aggregate loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s tracked equity high-water mark. When money-based take profit is enabled, close the instrument’s positions when aggregate profit reaches a configured cash target; when percentage-based take profit is enabled, close them when aggregate profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. When money trailing is enabled, track aggregate position profit: after profit is positive but below its target, close all positions if profit falls by the configured cash drawdown from its tracked peak; while aggregate profit is below zero and below its target, close all positions when the configured equity-relative loss limit, adjusted by a configured multiplier, is exceeded. Entry signals and price-based stop management use only the traded instrument’s own prices.
