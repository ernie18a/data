On each new bar, compare the fast and slow moving averages on the traded instrument’s chart timeframe and compare the monthly MACD main line with its signal line on the prior completed bar. When the fast average is below the slow average and MACD main is above signal, open or continue a short position; when the fast average is above the slow average and MACD main is below signal, open or continue a long position. If a position already exists, add in the same direction when price moves adversely by the configured grid distance from the most adverse entry, increasing position size by the configured multiplier, up to the trade limit. Close the matching side when its trade limit is reached if configured. Close the instrument’s basket when its profit reaches the configured target, when its loss reaches the hard account-protection threshold, or when its configured loss limit is reached with at least three open trades. Also close the basket when the equity-loss threshold is exceeded, when enabled money-profit target is reached, or when enabled money trailing gives back its configured amount from the tracked peak profit.
