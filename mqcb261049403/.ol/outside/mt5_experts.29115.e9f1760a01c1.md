Form groups of two currency pairs from each selected trio, with currencies ordered from stronger to weaker. For each group, define a plus basket and its inverse minus basket: if the pairs share the same base currency or the same quote currency, buy the first and sell the second for plus, and reverse both for minus; otherwise buy both for plus and sell both for minus. At startup, open the enabled basket sides across eligible groups, subject to trading-session, spread, order-count, margin, and group limits. In manual grid mode, add a complete two-pair basket on a side when that side has at least one complete basket, its combined floating profit is at or below its next-loss level, and the minimum interval since its previous addition has elapsed. Scale the next-loss level by basket count using the selected static, triangular, or exponential progression. In automatic mode, when one side has no positions and the opposite side still has a complete basket, open a basket on the empty side, subject to limits. Close rules depend on the selected mode: close a profitable side when its combined floating profit reaches its cost-adjusted target; optionally close a losing whole side when its loss reaches its cost-adjusted loss threshold, or close its oldest pair of orders when that side has more orders than the opposite side and reaches the threshold. In both-sides profit mode, also close the smaller side when the opposite side's loss reaches its next-addition level, and close both sides when their combined profit reaches the larger side target. Hybrid profit modes instead aggregate specified related groups and close a side across those groups when their combined profit reaches the largest applicable group target. Apply configured tick delays before profit or loss closures. Ignore any rules disabled by the selected operating mode or side settings.
