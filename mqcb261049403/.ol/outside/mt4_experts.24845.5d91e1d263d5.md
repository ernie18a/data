On each new bar, consider a new position only while the number of open positions on the instrument is below its limit and account free margin is sufficient. For a long, require the faster weighted moving average of typical price to be above the slower one, the current bid to differ from the close two bars ago by more than a configured retracement distance, and the low two bars ago to be below the prior bar’s high. In live trading, also require the open two bars ago to be above the older Fibonacci level and either the close two bars ago to be below the newer level or the previous close to be at or below it. For a short, reverse the moving-average direction and price-distance test, require the prior bar’s low to be below the high two bars ago, and in live trading require the open two bars ago below the older Fibonacci level and either the close two bars ago above the newer level or the previous close at or above it. The Fibonacci levels come from the recent range; ignore this filter in testing. Open qualifying positions with protective stop-loss and take-profit levels.

Close all positions on the instrument if their combined loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s tracked equity peak. Optional profit controls close positions when combined profit reaches a cash or balance-percentage target, or when a profitable basket retreats by a configured cash amount. A separate money-based loss control closes the basket when its loss exceeds a scaled equity-risk limit. For individual positions, when enabled, move the stop beyond entry after a configured favorable price move. Trailing stops, when enabled, adjust stops using recent candle highs or lows on a new bar, or trail price by a configured distance after a favorable move.
