On each bar, calculate fast and slow simple moving averages of closing prices. Open a long when the fast average moves from at or below the slow average on the previous bar to above it on the current bar; open a short when it moves from above to at or below. Open a new position only when there are no existing positions. When enabled, close any short on an upward crossover and any long on a downward crossover. Apply configured stop-loss and take-profit levels, move stops to break-even after the configured profit threshold, and trail stops after their configured activation threshold.
