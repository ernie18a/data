Place a buy-stop grid when the configured buy entry is enabled, there are no open positions, the pending-order limit is not reached, and price has moved sufficiently below the entry while the two latest completed trigger-timeframe candles remain near or above it. Place a sell-stop grid under the mirrored conditions: price is sufficiently above the configured sell entry while the two latest candles remain near or below it. Space grid orders by a configured gap. A separate condition-based grid mode checks daily price ranges and multi-timeframe candle direction, but its coded range comparisons are contradictory, so it cannot trigger as written.

For open positions, add a configured fixed stop when none exists; move the stop beyond entry after a favorable move reaches the break-even trigger, then trail it as price advances further. Close all positions when combined floating profit reaches the configured loss limit. Begin equity-profit protection after profit reaches its activation threshold, set a floor near the configured break-even profit, raise that floor as profit grows, and close all positions when profit falls back to the protected floor.
