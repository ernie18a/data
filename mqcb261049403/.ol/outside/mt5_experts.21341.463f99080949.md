Manage all open positions as a group; do not open trades. When time-window trading is enabled, define whether the current time falls within the configured start/end interval, including intervals that cross midnight. If the time is inside the interval, aggregate account profit is positive, and no global stop has been triggered, leave positions open. Otherwise, if no stop is already active, trigger a global stop when the absolute aggregate account profit reaches or exceeds the configured loss threshold as a percentage of account balance, or strictly exceeds it when measured in deposit currency. Close every open position whenever the global stop is active or the current time is outside the enabled trading interval. Clear the stop state once no positions remain.
