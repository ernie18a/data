Build a pair-strength oscillator from the difference between the two currencies’ aggregate strength, where each currency’s strength reflects the short-versus-long moving-average movement across its component currency pairs. Identify a clearly visible fractal and an auxiliary reference peak, then draw a signal line through them. A crossing of the oscillator and signal line is an aggressive signal; buy the pair when the fractal is above zero and the crossing occurs below zero. No short or exit rule is specified.
