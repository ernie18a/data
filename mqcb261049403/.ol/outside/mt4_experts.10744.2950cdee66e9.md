On each eligible time update, maintain a six-step history of the channel indicator’s buy and sell signals; add progressively smaller weights to the corresponding directional score, with the newest history step weighted most. Also score trend comparisons across the traded instrument and a basket of related instruments: use RSI, ask, and close series to favor increases for buying and decreases for selling; use bid series to favor decreases for selling. Compare the current observation with the prior seven observations, the prior observation with the next five older observations, and the observation two steps back with the next three older observations. For each comparison, use descending weights from strongest for the nearest comparison to neutral and then increasingly negative for farther comparisons; award the weight when the series moves in the favored direction. Current traded-instrument RSI increases only the buy score; current basket RSI decreases count twice toward the sell score. Enter only when the required observation history and allowed trading time are available, the current channel direction confirms the side, the channel signal-line value differs from at least one of the prior three main-line values, its gap from the main line exceeds a configured safety threshold, and the corresponding score meets the configured minimum and strictly exceeds the opposing score. Close an open trade when its profit reaches the configured profit target or its loss reaches the configured loss limit. Before opening a qualifying opposite-side trade, close the existing position; otherwise leave the position open.
