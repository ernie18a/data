On each new bar, compare a fast and a slow moving average on the traded instrument and compare the monthly MACD main line with its signal line. Open a long when the fast average is below the slow average and MACD main is above its signal; open a short when the fast average is above the slow average and MACD main is below its signal. Start only when no position is open. Place a take-profit in the trade direction and a stop-loss beyond the adverse recovery zone. If price moves against the initial trade by the recovery-zone distance from its entry, open an opposite trade; thereafter, alternate long and short recovery trades when price crosses the applicable recovery boundary around the initial entry, increasing each new trade's size according to the configured progression. Close the basket when an individual position reaches its take-profit or stop-loss. If enabled, also close the basket when its combined profit reaches a money target or a percentage of account balance; once combined profit reaches a separate money threshold, close the basket if profit falls by the configured giveback amount.
