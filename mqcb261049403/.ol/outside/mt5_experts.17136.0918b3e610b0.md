On each new bar, evaluate each enabled MACD configuration using completed-bar values. Sell when the third-prior MACD is positive or the latest MACD is negative, provided both have magnitudes at least a configured multiple of the intervening MACD’s magnitude. Buy when the third-prior MACD is negative or the latest MACD is positive, under the same magnitude condition. Place protective stops beyond recent highs for sells or recent lows for buys, and set targets at successively more-extreme recent lows for sells or highs for buys. For profitable positions, partially close buys when the prior close is above a short-term moving average or the prior high exceeds the midpoint of a longer-term simple and exponential moving average; partially close sells when the prior close is below the short-term average or the prior low falls below that midpoint.
