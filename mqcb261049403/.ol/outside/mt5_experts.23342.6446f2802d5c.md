Calculate a Spearman rank correlation from a rolling window of the traded instrument’s prices, then classify it as negative, neutral, positive, or beyond either configured extreme threshold. On the selected signal bar, compare its class with the preceding bar. In zero-cross mode, open long when the class moves into positive territory and open short when it moves into negative territory; close shorts in positive territory and longs in negative territory. In extreme-zone entry mode, open long when entering the upper extreme and short when entering the lower extreme; close shorts in positive territory and longs in negative territory. In extreme-zone entry and exit mode, use those same extreme-zone entries and close the opposite position upon entering the corresponding extreme. Apply the configured long/short enable switches. If weekly time filtering is enabled, only trade within the configured weekly interval; close all positions on the instrument outside it.
