For each currency, calculate its relative strength from the average of the seven relevant pair closing prices, reversing pairs where the currency is quoted and scaling yen pairs consistently. Compare that strength with its moving average. In trend mode, mark the currency favorable when strength is above its average if it is the traded pair’s base currency, and below its average if it is the quote currency; reverse these comparisons in counter-trend mode. For a traded pair, signal long when both currency indicators are favorable and signal short when both are unfavorable. The indicator defines no exit rule.
