On each new bar, open a long if the open position count is below its limit, the slow stochastic signal is below the fast stochastic main line, RSI is above its midpoint, a fast typical-price weighted moving average is above a slower one, the recent-bar low/high relationship is satisfied, and the current bid is farther from the specified prior close than a configured candle-distance threshold. Open a short under the mirrored conditions: slow stochastic above fast, RSI below its midpoint, fast average below slow, the mirrored low/high relationship, and sufficient distance from the corresponding prior close. Set protective stop and profit target in opposite directions, with target distance derived from stop distance and the configured reward-to-risk ratio; require valid trade volume, margin, and stop levels. Close the instrument's positions when their combined loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account's tracked equity high, or when an enabled money or balance-percentage profit target is reached. An enabled money-based trailing rule closes the basket after profit retraces from its tracked peak by the configured cash amount; its loss-side rule closes when basket loss exceeds the equity-risk threshold adjusted by a configured multiplier. Move a long position's stop above entry after price advances by the configured trigger distance, and move a short position's stop below entry after an equivalent favorable decline, using the configured offset.
