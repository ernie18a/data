Enter long when a fast weighted moving average is above a slower simple moving average, the stochastic oscillator is rising, RSI is above its cutoff, and MACD is positive. Enter short when those conditions are reversed: the fast average is below the slow average, stochastic is falling, RSI is below its cutoff, and MACD is negative. Only open new positions during the permitted trading hours. Close a short when a long signal occurs; close a long when the long signal is absent. Apply a stop loss and take profit to each new position.
