On each new bar, estimate the relative strength of USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY, AUD, CAD, and NZD from moving-average ratios of the seven USD currency pairs, using each pair’s ratio in the appropriate direction for its currencies. For every ordered currency pair, signal when their strength difference crosses above a configurable threshold: it was at or below the threshold on the prior bar, is above it now, the stronger currency’s strength rose, and the weaker currency’s strength fell. Trade the signal as long the stronger currency and short the weaker: for USD pairs, buy or sell the corresponding pair according to its quote/base orientation; for two non-USD currencies, buy the stronger currency’s USD pair when it is stronger than USD, and sell the weaker currency’s USD pair when USD is stronger than it. If enabled, close opposite positions in the selected instruments before opening the signal positions. Apply configured stop loss and take profit; trail stops as price moves favorably.
