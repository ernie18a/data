On each new bar, scan the supported currency pairs and timeframes. For each, identify the latest two swing highs and lows from Williams %R turning points. Signal long when the latest high and low are both higher than their predecessors and the rise in highs exceeds an ATR-based threshold; signal short when both are lower and the fall in lows exceeds that threshold. Also derive trend directions from the Gator and NRTR indicators. Consider a pair and timeframe only when the three trend directions agree and that same direction is fully aligned with at least one of the specified higher timeframes. Estimate the trade’s target from the prior swing range and its stop from the current price to the opposing swing; reject candidates whose spread-adjusted target-to-stop ratio is below a minimum. Among eligible candidates with sufficient margin, select one candidate and trade in the swing-based signal direction, provided there is not already an open trade in that direction on the pair. Size the position by account risk relative to stop distance, subject to margin and maximum-size limits. Set the target and initial stop using the swing levels, with ATR-based minimum distances. For open trades, tighten the stop along a time-based parabolic path until it reaches entry; after the trade has remained open beyond the breakeven period, trail the stop at an ATR-based distance from current price.
