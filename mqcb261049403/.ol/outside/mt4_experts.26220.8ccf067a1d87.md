On each tick, inspect the latest completed candle. In the two-sided strategy, open a long if the candle closes above its open and its body meets or exceeds the configured minimum, provided no long is already open for that strategy; open a short if it closes below its open and its body meets or exceeds the configured minimum, provided no short is already open for that strategy. In the long-only strategy, open a long if the candle closes above its open and its body exceeds the configured minimum, provided no trade is already open for that strategy. The strategies do not otherwise signal exits.
