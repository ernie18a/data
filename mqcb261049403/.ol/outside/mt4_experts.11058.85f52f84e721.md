Allow one position at a time; open a new position only when no position is already open. The default entry rule is a moving-average crossover: go long when the short-period average of median price crosses above the long-period average, and short when it crosses below. For an optional oscillator mode, go long when the current oscillator is positive while its two preceding readings are negative and falling; go short when it is negative while those readings are positive and rising. For an optional swing-breakout mode, go long when the ask breaks above the upper trendline drawn from recent swing highs, or short when the bid breaks below the lower trendline drawn from recent swing lows; clear the breakout latch and pending entry signals when price returns between the lines. Close a profitable long when the active oscillator or moving-average spread turns negative, and close a profitable short when it turns positive. The swing-breakout mode has no indicator-based exit rule; positions use protective stop and target orders.
