For each currency, calculate its strength as the average of its position within the current bar’s high–low range across its seven currency pairs, inverting the position when the currency is the quote currency. Use the selected daily, weekly, or monthly period. When a currency reaches the configurable high threshold, pair it with the weakest other currency; when it reaches the low threshold, pair it with the strongest other currency. The candidate pair is a suggestion, not an automatically placed trade; buy or sell direction is not specified. Re-arm a currency’s alert after its strength returns between reset thresholds. In smart-alert mode, issue the alert only after a matching pair’s price movement meets a volume-based trigger.
