Manage existing market positions matching the selected instrument scope and trade identifier. For each buy, derive a candidate stop from one of: the nearest prior bar low, nearest eligible lower fractal, a favorable rise in a velocity indicator, a valid Parabolic SAR value, or a fixed distance below the current bid. For each sell, use the mirrored rule: prior bar high, nearest eligible upper fractal, favorable fall in velocity, valid SAR above price, or a fixed distance above the ask. Apply the configured offset and respect the broker’s minimum stop distance. If portfolio break-even protection is enabled, require the candidate stop to be at or beyond the volume-weighted average entry price of all matching positions in that direction. Optional filters can restrict updates to individually profitable positions, positions whose existing stop is still on the loss side of entry, or positions that already have a stop. Modify a buy’s stop only when the candidate is higher than its current stop by at least the configured step; modify a sell’s only when it is lower by at least that step, or when no stop exists.
