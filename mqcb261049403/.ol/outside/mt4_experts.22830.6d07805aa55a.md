On each new bar, allow entries only while below the configured trade limit and after basic trading checks. For a long signal, require the fast moving average above the slow one, a bullish ADX/DI breakout or DI crossover signal, rising momentum, and recent volume exceeding either of two earlier bars. Breakout signals require ADX or +DI above the breakout threshold; when the DI trend filter is enabled, +DI must exceed -DI by a minimum gap. Crossover signals require +DI to cross above -DI, ADX above its minimum level, and the same moving-average, momentum, and volume confirmations; when breakout confirmation is enabled, also apply the coded breakout threshold condition. The crossover path requires rising ADX only when that option is enabled. Also require the recent-bar price-pattern and price-distance filters before opening a long.

For a short signal, require the fast moving average below the slow one, a bearish ADX/DI breakout or DI crossover signal, falling momentum, and the same volume confirmation. Breakout signals require ADX above the breakout threshold or -DI below it; when the DI trend filter is enabled, -DI must exceed +DI by a minimum gap. Crossover signals require +DI to cross below -DI, ADX above its minimum level, and the same moving-average, momentum, and volume confirmations, subject to the coded breakout-confirmation condition. The crossover path requires rising ADX only when that option is enabled. Also require the recent-bar price-pattern and price-distance filters before opening a short.

Close all positions for the instrument when enabled aggregate profit targets are reached, when the enabled profit trail gives back its configured amount from its recorded favorable profit, or when aggregate losses exceed the configured equity-risk threshold (with the additional loss multiplier in the money-trailing rule). Move a long stop above entry after price advances by its trigger distance, and move a short stop below entry after the corresponding favorable advance. Position sizing may increase after consecutive losing closed trades; apply volume, margin, stop, and take-profit checks before entry.
