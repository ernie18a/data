Use the Parabolic SAR calculated from the traded instrument’s price bars. Generate a bullish signal when SAR is below the current bar’s close and was above the previous bar’s close; generate a bearish signal for the reverse. With normal direction enabled, a bullish signal closes an existing short and may open a long, while a bearish signal closes an existing long and may open a short. With reverse direction enabled, swap the entry directions and close an existing position matching the signal’s normal direction. Open a position only when no orders are open and the configured intrabar movement gates pass; set protective stop-loss and take-profit levels on entry, and optionally trail the stop as price moves favorably.
