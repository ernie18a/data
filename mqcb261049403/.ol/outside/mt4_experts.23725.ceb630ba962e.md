On each new bar, the main strategy may open a trade if the number of its open trades is below the configured limit. Buy when the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the short-term exponential average is above the short-term weighted average, the prior low is below the preceding high, and the two preceding candles form the specified reversal: the older candle is bearish, the newer one bullish, and the newer body is larger. Also require the current bid to be farther from the older candle’s close than the configured retracement distance. Sell on the inverse moving-average alignment and candle pattern, with the newer bearish body smaller than the older bullish body; require the prior low below the preceding high and the ask farther from the newer candle’s close than the configured distance. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit levels.

In the ECN variant, entries are allowed only when no trade is open. Buy when the short exponential average is above the short weighted average, the prior low is below the preceding high, the candle pattern is bearish then bullish with the newer body larger, and monthly momentum confirmation holds: monthly MACD is above its signal while positive or negative, or monthly RSI has risen. Sell on the inverse averages and candle pattern, with monthly MACD below its signal while positive or negative, or monthly RSI has fallen. Attach protective stop and target levels. Close a buy if the prior close equals the upper band, and close a sell if it equals the lower band.

Manage open trades by moving stops beyond entry after a configured favorable move, and trail stops behind recent candle lows for buys or highs for sells (or by a fixed distance when candle trailing is disabled). Close the strategy’s basket when its combined profit reaches a configured balance-based gain or loss limit, when an enabled aggregate cash-profit target is reached, or when an enabled equity drawdown limit is exceeded. The main strategy also closes a profitable basket if its running profit falls by the configured cash trailing amount from its peak.
