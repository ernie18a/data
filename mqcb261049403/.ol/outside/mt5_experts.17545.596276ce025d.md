On each new bar during the configured trading window, evaluate six independent MACD pattern rules on the traded instrument; place a long or short when a rule's staged conditions complete. The rules detect: (1) a retreat from a positive MACD extreme with a local-peak reversal for shorts, or recovery from a negative extreme with a local-trough reversal for longs; (2) a threshold-qualified MACD turn across zero followed by a local reversal; (3) a multi-stage sequence of successively qualified MACD peaks for shorts or troughs for longs; (4) a second, weaker MACD peak above a positive threshold for shorts, or second, weaker trough below a negative threshold for longs; (5) recovery from an extreme followed by a threshold-crossing reversal, with the bearish sequence for shorts and bullish sequence for longs; and (6) a MACD threshold excursion tracked over a required number of bars, followed by a return across the threshold, with mirrored rules for long and short. Use prior closed-bar MACD values and maintain each pattern's staging state between bars. Attach a stop beyond the recent price extreme over a lookback window and a target at a successive prior-window price extreme in the trade's favorable direction. For profitable open positions, partially close a first portion when the prior close crosses the relevant shorter EMA in the trade's favorable direction, then close another portion when the prior high (long) or low (short) passes the midpoint of a long SMA and EMA. After a closed trade, restore base size after a gain and double size after a loss.
