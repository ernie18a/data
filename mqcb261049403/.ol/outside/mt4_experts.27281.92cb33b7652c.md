At a configured daily evaluation time, use a configured timeframe’s bar from a configured earlier hour to set the reference high and low. Evaluate the previous completed bar: if it closes below the reference low by at least a configured minimum but less than a configured maximum distance, signal a buy; if it closes above the reference high by at least the minimum but less than the maximum, signal a sell. A configurable mode either follows these breakout directions or reverses them. Optionally reject entries if any intervening close crossed the opposite reference boundary, or if a close sampled at a configured recheck time conflicts with the average close over the reference period and its recent direction. Open only when spread is acceptable, the same direction has no open position, and the configured position limit permits it. Optionally close positions on the configured daily cutoff or when the signal rules indicate exit; apply configured profit targets, stop losses, and optional break-even or trailing-stop exits.
