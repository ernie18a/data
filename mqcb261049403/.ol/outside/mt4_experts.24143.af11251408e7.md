On each tick, if there are no open positions on the chart instrument, require its current-timeframe ATR to be below a configured ceiling, a long-period stochastic main line to be below a configured upper threshold, and both a very-long-period and a shorter-period stochastic main line to cross downward through a configured lower threshold. When all conditions hold, open a market short; after it succeeds, place a configured grid of pending short orders at progressively higher prices, with take-profit and expiry settings. If one or more positions are open, calculate their average floating profit in pips; when it reaches a configured target, close all open positions on the instrument and delete its pending orders.
