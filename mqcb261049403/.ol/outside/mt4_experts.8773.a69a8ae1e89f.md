When trading is enabled and account risk controls permit a trade, use the last completed bar on the signal timeframe. Go long when its close is above the midpoint of its high-low range and the current ask is above its high plus the spread. Go short when its close is below that midpoint and the current bid is below its low. Set the stop beyond the signal bar’s high for a long or low for a short, offset by a configurable multiple of that bar’s range; set the target from the entry quote by a configurable multiple of the same range. Submit only if stop and target distances satisfy the broker’s minimum stop distance. Allow at most one open position from this strategy. Size each order from the account’s per-trade risk allowance and stop distance, rounded down to the permitted lot increment and bounded by broker limits; skip it if the size is below minimum or available margin is insufficient. For an open position, when profitable, move its stop using a configurable multiple of the last completed bar’s range on a separate trailing timeframe: below the current bid for a long and above the current ask for a short. Trail only when broker stop and freeze constraints are met, the target remains valid, and the proposed stop improves on the current stop by at least the spread-based step. No separate signal-based position exit is defined; positions exit through their stop or target.
