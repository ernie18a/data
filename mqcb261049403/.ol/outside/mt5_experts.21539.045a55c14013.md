On each completed signal bar, form synthetic candles from smoothed open, high, low, and close: use the lagged smoothed open, the highest smoothed high and lowest smoothed low across the lookback, and the smoothed close. Calculate a moving average and deviation bands from the selected synthetic-candle price. Open long when a candle closes above the upper band after the prior candle was not in an upper-band breakout state. Open short when it closes below the lower band after the prior candle was not in a lower-band breakout state. Close a short when the current candle state is bullish or bullish-breakout; close a long when it is bearish or bearish-breakout. Also close any position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit.
