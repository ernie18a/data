On each tick, determine whether the current time is inside the configured trading window, including windows that cross midnight. If time restriction is enabled and the time is outside that window, close every open position. Otherwise, when aggregate floating profit is negative and no global take-profit stop is active, take no action. In other cases, activate a persistent global stop when the absolute aggregate floating profit reaches or exceeds the configured threshold measured as a percentage of account balance, or exceeds the configured threshold measured in deposit currency. While that stop is active, close every open position; clear it once no positions remain. Close long positions by selling and short positions by buying.
