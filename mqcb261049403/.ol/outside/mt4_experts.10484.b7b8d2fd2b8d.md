A user-placed buy or sell command line triggers a market entry in that direction. A pending buy or sell line places an order at its price; classify it as a stop or limit according to whether that price is above or below the current market quote for the intended direction. Set stop loss and take profit at configured distances from entry, unless explicitly specified or disabled. While a position is open, move its stop to trail the market once the configured profit threshold is reached. Close the position when its stop loss or take profit is reached; deleting its main line also requests closure when that behavior is enabled.
