When the user activates the close control, inspect open orders and apply the configured scope, optionally filtering by order comment. Close matching market orders: all, buys only, sells only, a selected instrument (defaulting to the chart instrument), a selected identifier, or a specific ticket. Delete matching pending orders when the scope selects pending orders, optionally restricted by instrument and identifier; a combined scope closes market orders and deletes pending orders. Use the appropriate current market side to close buys or sells.
