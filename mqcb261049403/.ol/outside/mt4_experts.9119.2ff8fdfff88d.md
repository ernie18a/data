An optional external EUR-strength filter sums, across EURUSD, EURGBP, EURCHF, EURJPY, EURCAD, and EURAUD, each pair’s current moving average minus its lagged moving average, scaled by that pair’s quote precision. A positive sum confirms long entries; a negative sum confirms short entries; zero confirms neither. When enabled, this confirmation must agree with the other active entry conditions, including the selected directional model where applicable. The calculated USD index does not affect decisions.
