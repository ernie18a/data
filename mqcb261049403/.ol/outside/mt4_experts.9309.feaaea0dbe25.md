On the chart’s timeframe, evaluate two candle patterns over a configurable recent lookback. For pattern A, require the older of the two most recent completed candles to contain the newer candle’s full high-low range; signal bullish if that older candle is the lookback’s lowest low, or bearish if it is the lookback’s highest high. For pattern B, signal bullish when the newest completed candle contains the prior candle’s range and is the lookback’s lowest low; signal bearish when its high is at least the prior candle’s high, its low is higher, and it is the lookback’s highest high. For each pattern independently, act only when no position or pending order for that pattern is open. Either directional signal places both a buy stop above the newest candle’s high and a sell stop below its low, each with a small price buffer. Set each order’s stop loss beyond the older candle’s opposite extreme and its take profit a configured distance beyond its entry. Expire pending orders after a configured duration. Remove pending buy stops when a sell position exists, and pending sell stops when a buy position exists. For open positions, move the stop to a small gain beyond entry once price has advanced by a configured trigger distance. Also trail stops behind favorable price movement, using separate distances for buys and sells and updating only after a configured step; trailing may be restricted to profitable positions. Positions exit at their stop loss or take profit.
