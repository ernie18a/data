For each traded currency pair, place a buy stop above resistance and a sell stop below support, adding spread and a small buffer. Define resistance as the current bar’s high plus ATR and support as its low minus ATR, using either H1/H4 or H4/D1 levels according to the selected aggressiveness mode; if neither level is sufficiently beyond the market, use a fixed offset from the quote. At the configured daily time, use the previous daily high and low as the entry boundaries. Place a missing side when no pending entries exist; if only one side is pending, add the opposite stop as a hedge, sized according to the net open position. In trend mode, allow a sell hedge only when RSI across M5, M15 and M30 is above its upper threshold now and below neutral on earlier readings, with the slow EMA above the fast EMA; allow a buy hedge only for the inverse RSI pattern and EMA ordering. Close positions after aggregate profit reaches the minimum-lot value: in non-trend mode, track the peak and close all positions and pending orders after profit falls by one tenth of that value; in trend mode, close at the threshold while retaining positions protected by a favorable trailing stop, then reset when no positions remain. Apply an initial stop loss when configured; move stops to break-even after a favorable move reaches the trailing-start distance, then advance them in fixed trailing increments. If aggregate profit falls below the configured percentage loss limit, delete pending orders and open a market position opposite the net lot imbalance; suppress further entries in a direction while the corresponding older position is losing, and restore that direction when it is no longer losing.
