For each currency, calculate its strength from the closing prices of the seven pairs containing it, then compare that strength with its moving average. In trend mode, signal stronger when strength is above its average and weaker when below; reverse these signals in counter-trend mode. Reverse the signal orientation for a currency quoted in a pair rather than base. For a traded pair, act when both currencies’ indicators show the same direction: buy when both indicate green/strong and sell when both indicate red/weak.
