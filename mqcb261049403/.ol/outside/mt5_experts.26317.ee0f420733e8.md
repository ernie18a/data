On each tick, calculate RSI on closing prices. Buy when the latest completed bar’s RSI is below the oversold threshold and the preceding bar’s RSI was at or above it. Sell when the latest completed bar’s RSI is at or above the overbought threshold and the preceding bar’s RSI was below it. On a buy signal, close any matching short positions; on a sell signal, close any matching long positions. Open a long or short only when no matching positions are open. Apply configured stop loss and take profit to trades.
