On each new bar, open a long when trade count is below its cap, price is sufficiently displaced from a recent close, the higher-timeframe fast weighted moving average is above the slow one, the specified recent candle low is below the prior candle high, the chart’s short-to-long exponential averages are strictly ascending, and the latest completed low touches or falls below the middle average. Open a short when trade count is below its cap, price is sufficiently displaced from a recent close, the higher-timeframe fast weighted moving average is below the slow one, the specified recent candle low is below the earlier candle high, the chart’s short-to-long exponential averages are strictly descending, and the latest completed high touches or exceeds the middle average. For each entry, attach a protective stop and target at configured distances. Close the strategy’s open basket when its net profit reaches a balance-based profit threshold or its net loss reaches a balance-based loss limit. Also close positions when the symbol’s floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s running equity peak, when aggregate position profit reaches a configured cash target, or when the money-based trailing rule gives back its allowed amount from its tracked profit peak. Move stops to lock in profit after a position gains a configured distance; trail stops by recent candle extremes with a configured offset, or by price distance when candle trailing is disabled.
