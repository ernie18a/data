For assets in a shared- currency instrument cluster, derive each asset’s relative value or change from the cluster’s quotes. Use any of these signal rules: on a crossing between two asset lines, buy the asset whose line is rising and sell the one whose line is falling; on a zero-line crossing, buy an asset crossing upward and sell one crossing downward; buy on an upward breakout beyond the outer positive deviation band and sell on a downward breakout beyond the outer negative band; after an asset reaches an outer deviation band, buy when it retraces inward through the smaller positive band or sell when it retraces inward through the smaller negative band; or buy or sell in the direction indicated by a peak in relative movement speed. No exit condition is specified.
