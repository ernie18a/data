Block new entries during enabled economic-news windows, using the selected calendar source and optionally limiting events to currencies in the traded pair. The window spans each event’s configured lead and lag period; classify overlapping events by highest severity. If configured for that severity, close open positions during the window. Independently, close positions when the selected scope’s profit or loss crosses its configured money or equity-percentage threshold. Entry signals themselves come only from the traded instrument’s candle patterns, so they are outside this framework’s external-data decision logic.
