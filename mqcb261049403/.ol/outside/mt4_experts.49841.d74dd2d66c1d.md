During allowed trading hours, choose one of two automatic entry modes. In RSI mode, when there is no open position on a side and that side is enabled, open short if a close-based relative-strength measure on the selected timeframe reaches the upper threshold; open long if it reaches the lower threshold. In fixed-distance mode, periodically set a long trigger above the current ask and a short trigger below the current bid; open when price reaches the respective trigger, provided that side is enabled and has no open position. For either mode, add to an existing long when ask falls at least one grid step below its latest entry, and add to an existing short when bid rises at least one grid step above its latest entry; grid spacing may grow with the number of same-side positions. Close a side's basket when its configured take-profit or stop-loss boundary is reached, when a trailing stop or breakeven step is crossed, or when a user-initiated close is requested. Once the position count reaches the overlap threshold, identify the most profitable and most unprofitable open positions and close both when price reaches a favorable level that offsets their combined result by the required minimum profit.
