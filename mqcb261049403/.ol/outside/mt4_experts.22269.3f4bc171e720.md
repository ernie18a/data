On each new bar, allow entries only while the open-trade count is below its configured cap and basic history, margin, and order checks pass. On the higher timeframe associated with the chart, require a recent confirmed fractal. For a long, require the fast weighted moving average of typical price to be above the slow one, the prior candle to have an upper wick at least half its range, a recent chart-bar low to be below the following bar’s high, and the current bid to be farther from the close two bars back than the configured retracement distance. For a short, require the moving averages in the opposite order, a lower wick at least half the prior candle’s range, the corresponding recent low/high relationship in the opposite bar order, and the current ask to exceed that same distance from the close two bars back. Close the instrument’s positions when their combined loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high, or when the enabled basket-protection rule sees combined net profit reach its balance-based profit or loss boundary.
