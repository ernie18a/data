On each new bar, open a position only when none is open. Go long when the close is above its moving average by a minimum margin and the MACD main line rises across several consecutive samples around an upward zero crossing. Go short when the close is below its moving average by that margin and the MACD main line falls across several samples around a downward zero crossing. Set a stop loss and take profit relative to the entry price. If break-even protection is enabled, move the stop to the entry price or a specified favorable offset when permitted by the broker. If end-of-day closing is enabled, close open positions at the configured hour, which differs on Fridays.
