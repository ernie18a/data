When no trade is active, and the spread passes its limit and the news filter permits trading, go long if RSI is oversold and the current price is above both the instrument’s short-term and higher-timeframe moving averages. Go short if RSI is overbought and price is below both averages. Set the initial stop loss and take profit at ATR-based distances, and size the position from account balance and risk allowance. While a position is active, adjust its stop according to profit distance measured in ATR units, using progressively wider ATR-based trailing distances as profit grows. No separate indicator-based exit signal is defined.
