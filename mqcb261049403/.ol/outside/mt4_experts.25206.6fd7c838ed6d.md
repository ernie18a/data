On a BUY button click, open long positions when the monthly MACD main line is above its signal line; on a SELL button click, open short positions when it is below. A CLOSE button click closes all positions on the chart instrument. For open positions, close the basket when its net profit reaches a configured cash target, when a configured profit trail retraces by its cash stop amount, or when configured loss protection is breached. The loss protection closes the basket if its loss exceeds a set fraction of the account equity high; a separate basket rule closes at the hard loss limit, or at the smaller configured loss limit once at least three positions are open. Also close longs when the previous candle closes exactly at the upper Bollinger Band, and shorts when it closes exactly at the lower band.
