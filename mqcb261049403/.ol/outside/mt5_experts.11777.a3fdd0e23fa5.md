When the correlation between the two instruments reaches a configured target band, and trading is enabled for the current weekday, open a position in each instrument. In manual mode, use the configured buy or sell side for each. In automatic mode, infer direction from the active market session and each symbol’s currency: buy when a session currency is the symbol’s base currency, sell when it is the quote currency. For similar (paired) instruments, take opposite sides; for reflected (mirror) instruments, take the same side. Do not open automatically when neither instrument matches the active session currencies. Close the two positions when their combined floating profit reaches its target. If either position closes by its stop loss or take profit first, wait for the other position to close, then reset.
