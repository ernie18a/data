On a new bar, if the open-position count is below its cap, open a long when the chart-timeframe EMAs are ordered upward, the higher-timeframe stochastic main line is above its signal line, price is sufficiently far from a recent close, the higher-timeframe fast weighted moving average is above its slow one, the recent candle range pattern qualifies, the chart RSI exceeds its buy threshold, and monthly RSI is rising. Open a short under the mirrored downward-trend conditions: EMAs ordered downward, stochastic main below signal, sufficient price-to-close distance, fast weighted average below slow, the corresponding candle range pattern qualifies, chart RSI is below its sell threshold, and monthly RSI is falling. Attach protective stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry.

For open positions, close all trades when aggregate floating loss exceeds the configured fraction of the running account-equity peak. Close trades when aggregate profit reaches the configured money or balance-percentage target. The money-based trailing rule also closes trades when positive aggregate profit has fallen by the configured money drawdown from its peak; when aggregate loss exceeds the equity-risk threshold adjusted by a loss multiplier, close trades under the corresponding loss drawdown condition. Move stops into profit after a position gains the configured distance, and trail stops using recent candle extremes or a fixed distance once the relevant trigger is reached.
