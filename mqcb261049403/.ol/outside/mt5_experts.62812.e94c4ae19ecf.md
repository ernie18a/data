On each new market tick, close the managed positions when an enabled account-wide profit target or loss limit is reached, or when the position count reaches its threshold and the configured equity condition is met. Also close positions when a new entry brings the count exactly to the threshold, subject to that equity condition. Otherwise, if the position count is below the threshold and the daily entry limit allows, open a randomly chosen long or short when the market is open, the trade cooldown has elapsed, and price is far enough from existing positions. If hedging is enabled and a position has moved against its entry by the configured distance, open one opposite-direction hedge for it, provided the daily entry limit allows and it has not already been hedged. Optionally attach a stop based on ATR or a fixed price distance and a take-profit based on the configured reward-to-risk ratio; move the stop to entry after the configured favorable move.
