Build a directional forecast from recent price pressure, volume imbalance, tick activity, detected flow-pattern direction, and prior-candle trend; use a historical regression model when sufficient history is available. Combine that forecast with pressure, normalized volume imbalance, and signed pattern confidence into a decision score, adjusted by the historical pressure/volume-imbalance correlation when enabled. A sufficiently positive score gives bullish bias; a sufficiently negative score gives bearish bias; otherwise bias is neutral. Convert the score to conviction. Recommend entering long or short only when forecast confidence and conviction exceed their configured gates, bias is directional, and expected-move-to-dynamic-risk-threshold ratio exceeds its minimum; otherwise wait. Raise the conviction gate in high volatility, lower it in low volatility, and lower it when the historical regression fit is strong. If the entry gates fail but forecast confidence is above a fallback level, recommend prepare; otherwise wait. The program does not define an exit signal or execute trades.
