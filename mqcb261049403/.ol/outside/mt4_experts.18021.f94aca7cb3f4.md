During the permitted trading session, and only when current ATR exceeds its prior value, place at most one pending order per direction when the same Heiken Ashi signal appears on both the daily and chart timeframes. A long signal requires the current candle to be bullish with its open equal to the indicator’s first price buffer, while the preceding candle is bullish but its open differs from that buffer. A short signal uses the corresponding bearish conditions, with the same equality and inequality checks. Place buy-limit and sell-limit orders offset from the current market price; attach configured stop-loss and take-profit levels when enabled. At the start of a new weekly bar, close positions and delete pending orders for this strategy if weekly volume is at or below the trigger. Trading is disabled for the listed excluded symbols.
