For the configurable version, evaluate signals once per new bar. A long signal requires every enabled condition to be bullish: the selected fractal checks on the current and hourly charts indicate bullishness; the selected RSI is below its midpoint by the configured margin; the selected stochastic reading is below its midpoint by its margin; the hourly EMA is rising; the hourly parabolic SAR is below the current bar’s open; the prior bar’s low is above a lower channel boundary derived from recent highs, lows, and closes; and a configurable weighted score of recent high and low changes is positive. A short signal requires the corresponding bearish conditions: bearish fractal checks, RSI and stochastic above their midpoints by their margins, falling hourly EMA, SAR above the current open, prior bar’s high below the upper channel boundary, and a negative weighted score. Ignore disabled filters. Open a trade only when no matching trade is already open; apply its configured stop loss and take profit. If opposite-signal closing is enabled, close a long on a short signal or a short on a long signal. Optionally trail the stop, subject to the configured profit-trailing rule.

For the second version, a long requires bullish fractal indications on both the current and hourly charts, daily RSI below its midpoint, and one current-chart parabolic SAR below the other. A short requires bearish fractal indications on both charts, daily RSI above its midpoint, and the SAR ordering reversed. Open only when no matching trade is already open, with its configured stop loss and take profit. Optionally close an open trade on the opposite signal and trail its stop after the configured profit condition is met.
