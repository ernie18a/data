Build a synthetic dollar index from the selected currency-pair basket. On each completed index bar, compare fast and slow exponential moving averages: after warm-up, take the direction indicated by their ordering; thereafter, go long when the fast average crosses above the slow average and short when it crosses below. If a basket is open in the opposite direction, close it, then open the signaled direction once flat; keep an existing basket when its direction matches the signal. Close a complete long basket when the index’s bid reaches its take-profit gain or stop-loss loss from entry; for a short basket, use the entry price minus the index’s ask. These exits are disabled when their respective levels are set to zero.
