On each new bar, consider entries only when the open-trade count is below its cap and free margin is sufficient. Go long when the previous bar’s RSI and MACD main value each exceed their current values, the second-previous low is below the previous high, the fast weighted moving average is above the slow one, and the current bid is farther from the second-previous close than the configured candle-distance threshold. In live trading, also require both drawn fractal trend lines to slope upward; in testing, omit this filter. Go short on the mirrored conditions: previous RSI and MACD are each below their current values, the previous low is below the second-previous high, the fast average is below the slow one, and the ask is farther from the previous close than the distance threshold. In live trading, also require both trend lines to slope downward. Attach stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry.

Exit or reduce risk on existing positions through attached stop-loss and take-profit orders; move stops to lock in a configured gain after a position reaches its break-even trigger; and trail stops using recent candle extremes, or by a fixed distance after the trailing trigger when candle trailing is disabled. Close all positions when the configured account-equity drawdown limit is exceeded, when the enabled account-currency or balance-percentage profit target is reached, or when the money-based trailing logic detects a configured retracement from its profit threshold.
