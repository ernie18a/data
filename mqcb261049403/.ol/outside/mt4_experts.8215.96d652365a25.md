On the traded instrument’s chart timeframe, calculate a 100-period stochastic signal line using 3-period slowing, 3-period signal smoothing, and SMMA; also calculate a 100-period Williams %R. Go long when the stochastic signal line is below 3 and Williams %R is below −99.9. Go short when the stochastic signal line is above 97 and Williams %R is above −0.1. A long signal closes open shorts, and a short signal closes open longs. Open a position in the signaled direction when the entry is permitted.
