Calculation: FRASMA = For each bar, calculate the fractal dimension index (FDI) over a lookback: normalize each input price by the lookback high-low range, sum the square root of each consecutive normalized-price change squared plus 1/lookback², then FDI = 1 + (ln(sum) + ln 2)/ln(2 × (lookback − 1)); use FDI = 0 if the sum is zero. Set smoothing length to round(normal speed × (1/(2 − FDI))/2), clamped to at least 1, and take the mean of that many input prices ending at the bar. Use the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) and its last completed bars; the signal uses the selected completed bar and the immediately preceding completed bar.
Long: FRASMA on the selected bar > FRASMA on the preceding bar AND FRASMA on the preceding bar <= FRASMA two bars earlier
Short: FRASMA on the selected bar < FRASMA on the preceding bar AND FRASMA on the preceding bar >= FRASMA two bars earlier
Exit: long, FRASMA on the selected bar < FRASMA on the preceding bar AND FRASMA on the preceding bar >= FRASMA two bars earlier
Exit: short, FRASMA on the selected bar > FRASMA on the preceding bar AND FRASMA on the preceding bar <= FRASMA two bars earlier
