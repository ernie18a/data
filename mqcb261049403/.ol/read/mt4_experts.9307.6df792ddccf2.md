Calculation: CCI = Commodity Channel Index on chart-timeframe bars, using the last completed bar
Calculation: Current directional volume = volume of the current, still-forming lower-timeframe bar plus volumes of earlier completed lower-timeframe bars in the short lookback that close above their open by more than the threshold (up) or below their open by more than the threshold (down)
Calculation: Prior directional volume = volume of completed lower-timeframe bars in the preceding lookback, equal in length to the short lookback, that close above their open by more than the threshold (up) or below their open by more than the threshold (down)
Long: CCI > 0 AND CCI < upper CCI threshold AND current higher-timeframe low > last completed higher-timeframe low on each of three successively lower higher timeframes AND current chart bar price > high of the last completed chart bar AND current up directional volume > prior up directional volume AND prior up directional volume > 0 AND high of the bar two chart bars ago > high of the last completed chart bar AND high of the bar three chart bars ago > high of the bar two chart bars ago AND spread <= maximum spread AND weekday is not Friday AND open-position limit is not reached AND minimum order interval has elapsed
Short: CCI < 0 AND CCI > negative upper CCI threshold AND current higher-timeframe high < last completed higher-timeframe high on each of three successively lower higher timeframes AND current chart bar price < low of the last completed chart bar AND current down directional volume > prior down directional volume AND prior down directional volume > 0 AND low of the bar two chart bars ago < low of the last completed chart bar AND low of the bar three chart bars ago < low of the bar two chart bars ago AND spread <= maximum spread AND weekday is not Friday AND open-position limit is not reached AND minimum order interval has elapsed
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR price reaches the trailing stop OR position age exceeds maximum lifetime OR (weekday is Friday AND hour exceeds Friday close hour) OR price exceeds take-profit price minus one pip per complete reduction interval of position age
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR price reaches the trailing stop OR position age exceeds maximum lifetime OR (weekday is Friday AND hour exceeds Friday close hour) OR price falls below take-profit price plus one pip per complete reduction interval of position age
