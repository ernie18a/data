Calculation: Selected indicator direction = On its selected timeframe bar mapped to the chart-bar timestamp (which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe), compare that bar with the preceding bar; use the mapped bar even if it is still forming. Moving average: bullish if current MA > previous MA + 1 pip; bearish if current MA < previous MA − 1 pip. MACD, OsMA, stochastic, RSI, and RVI: bullish when the main line crosses above its signal line; bearish when it crosses below. RSI signal line = simple average of RSI over the signal lookback; OsMA signal line = simple average of OsMA over the signal lookback. CCI: bullish when crossing upward through any of −100, 0, or 100; bearish when crossing downward through any of those levels. ADX: when ADX < 25, bullish when +DI crosses above −DI; bearish when +DI crosses below −DI. Bollinger Bands: bullish when the bar's low is below the lower band and its close is above the lower band, or its low is below the middle band and its close is above the middle band; bearish when its high is above the upper band and its close is below the upper band, or its high is above the middle band and its close is below the middle band. If both selections use the same indicator, they use the same indicator settings.
Long: First selected indicator is bullish AND (second indicator is not selected OR second selected indicator is bullish)
Short: First selected indicator is bearish AND (second indicator is not selected OR second selected indicator is bearish)
