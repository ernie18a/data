Calculation: ASCTrend long marker = long-side price level marked by the ASCTrend signal indicator on the current chart bar (forming bar); ASCTrend short marker = short-side price level marked by that indicator on the current chart bar (forming bar)
Calculation: BrainTrend long marker = long-side price level marked by the BrainTrend signal indicator on the current chart bar (forming bar); BrainTrend short marker = short-side price level marked by that indicator on the current chart bar (forming bar)
Calculation: Standard deviation = standard deviation of median prices over a lookback, using the current chart bar
Long: time is within the permitted trading hours AND no position is open AND ((standard deviation exceeds its threshold AND ASCTrend long marker is positive AND below the current bar low AND price is above the central pivot level AND current volume minus previous bar volume meets its threshold) OR (ASCTrend long marker and BrainTrend long marker are positive and both below the current bar low AND current volume minus previous bar volume exceeds its threshold))
Short: time is within the permitted trading hours AND no position is open AND ((standard deviation exceeds its threshold AND ASCTrend short marker is positive AND above the current bar high AND price is below the central pivot level AND current volume minus previous bar volume meets its threshold) OR (ASCTrend short marker and BrainTrend short marker are positive and both above the current bar high AND current volume minus previous bar volume exceeds its threshold))
Exit: long, opposite short-entry condition OR at a minute boundary both the current and last completed chart bar have no ASCTrend long marker and position age is within its time limit OR price reaches the long stop or target
Exit: short, opposite long-entry condition OR at a minute boundary both the current and last completed chart bar have no ASCTrend short marker and position age is within its time limit OR price reaches the short stop or target
