Calculation: RBVI = 100 × positive / (positive + negative), or 100 when negative is zero; each component is smoothed recursively using the selected volume times ATR, with positive and negative changes assigned to their respective components. The ATR and RBVI use the same period. On the chart timeframe, the current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe RBVI = the same RBVI calculated on bars of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; use the higher-timeframe bar containing the chart bar, which may still be forming.
Long: Chart-timeframe RBVI < lower threshold
Long: Higher-timeframe RBVI < lower threshold
Short: Chart-timeframe RBVI > upper threshold
Short: Higher-timeframe RBVI > upper threshold
