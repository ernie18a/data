Calculation: ADX and directional indicators = ADX, +DI, and −DI on the configured work timeframe’s selected bar; that timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and the selected bar may be forming or completed depending on its configured offset.
Calculation: Grid spacing(x) = Delta 2 + Plus Delta × x / 2, for each grid level x from 1 through Max Lines.
Long: No position is open AND within the configured working-hour window AND ((ADX < flat threshold AND +DI > −DI) OR (ADX > flat threshold AND +DI > −DI))
Long: A pending buy order is triggered at its price level: after a flat-regime long, either Ask − (Delta 1 + x × Grid spacing(x)) [buy limit]; after a flat-regime short, Ask − x × Grid spacing(x) [buy limit]; after a trend-regime long, Ask + x × Grid spacing(x) [buy stop]; or after a trend-regime short, Ask + (Delta 1 + x × Grid spacing(x)) [buy stop].
Short: No position is open AND within the configured working-hour window AND ((ADX < flat threshold AND +DI < −DI) OR (ADX > flat threshold AND +DI < −DI))
Short: A pending sell order is triggered at its price level: after a flat-regime long, either Bid − Delta 1 [sell stop] or Bid + x × Grid spacing(x) [sell limit]; after a flat-regime short, Bid + (Delta 1 + x × Grid spacing(x)) [sell limit]; after a trend-regime long, either Bid − Delta 1 [sell stop] or Bid − (Delta 1 + x × Grid spacing(x)) [sell stop]; after a trend-regime short, Bid − x × Grid spacing(x) [sell stop].
Exit: Long or short, combined open-position profit including commission and swap > profit target OR (loss limit is enabled AND combined open-position profit including commission and swap < loss limit.
