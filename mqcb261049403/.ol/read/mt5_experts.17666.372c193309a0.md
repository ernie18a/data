Calculation: CG oscillator = −[sum over the lookback of (bar index + 1) × (high + low)/2] ÷ [sum over the lookback of (high + low)/2] + (lookback + 1)/2, with bar index 0 for the newest bar
Calculation: CG trigger = the CG oscillator value on the preceding bar
Long: CG oscillator on the higher-timeframe trend series > its trigger AND CG oscillator on the lower-timeframe entry series was ≤ its trigger on the preceding completed bar and is > its trigger on the latest completed bar
Short: CG oscillator on the higher-timeframe trend series < its trigger AND CG oscillator on the lower-timeframe entry series was ≥ its trigger on the preceding completed bar and is < its trigger on the latest completed bar
Exit: long, CG oscillator on the higher-timeframe trend series < its trigger OR CG oscillator on the lower-timeframe entry series < its trigger
Exit: short, CG oscillator on the higher-timeframe trend series > its trigger OR CG oscillator on the lower-timeframe entry series > its trigger
