Long: current price >= high of the last completed bar on the configured breakdown timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) + indent AND shorter-lookback moving average > longer-lookback moving average (both on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar) AND current time is within the configured trading window, inclusive, AND open long count < configured maximum AND no long has already opened in the current breakdown-timeframe bar
Short: current price <= low of the last completed bar on the configured breakdown timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) - indent AND shorter-lookback moving average < longer-lookback moving average (both on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar) AND current time is within the configured trading window, inclusive, AND open short count < configured maximum AND no short has already opened in the current breakdown-timeframe bar
Calculation: trailing stop = for a long, current price - trailing distance; for a short, current price + trailing distance; update only when favorable price movement exceeds trailing distance + step and the existing stop is below current price - (trailing distance + step) for a long, or above current price + (trailing distance + step) or unset for a short
Exit: long, current price <= initial stop-loss price or current price <= trailing stop or current price >= take-profit price
Exit: short, current price >= initial stop-loss price or current price >= trailing stop or current price <= take-profit price
