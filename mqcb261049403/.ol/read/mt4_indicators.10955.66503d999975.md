Calculation: Stretch = average over a lookback of the smaller of the bar’s absolute open-to-high and absolute open-to-low distances, using higher-timeframe bars completed before the current higher-timeframe bar
Long: high >= current higher-timeframe open + Stretch
Short: low <= current higher-timeframe open - Stretch
Exit: long, low <= current higher-timeframe open - Stretch
Exit: short, high >= current higher-timeframe open + Stretch
