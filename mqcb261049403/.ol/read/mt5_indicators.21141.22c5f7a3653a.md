Calculation: X-period candle open = selected moving average of chart-bar open from period−1 chart bars ago
Calculation: X-period candle close = selected moving average of current chart-bar close
Calculation: Higher-timeframe X-period candle open = selected moving average of higher-timeframe bar open from period−1 higher-timeframe bars ago, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Higher-timeframe X-period candle close = selected moving average of the higher-timeframe bar close still forming
Long: X-period candle close > X-period candle open OR Higher-timeframe X-period candle close > Higher-timeframe X-period candle open
Short: X-period candle close < X-period candle open OR Higher-timeframe X-period candle close < Higher-timeframe X-period candle open
