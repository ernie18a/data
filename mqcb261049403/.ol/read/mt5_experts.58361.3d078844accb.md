Calculation: Liquidity level = highest high or lowest low over the lookback, including the current chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Market structure shift = bullish when current chart-timeframe close > prior bar high AND prior bar close < the bar-before-prior open; bearish when current close < prior bar low AND prior bar close > the bar-before-prior open.
Calculation: Fair value gap = scan chart-timeframe bars from the current bar through the ninth prior bar; select the first bullish gap where the older bar's high < the newer bar's low AND the middle bar's close > the older bar's high, or bearish gap where the older bar's low > the newer bar's high AND the middle bar's close < the older bar's low. Gap bounds are the two non-overlapping prices.
Calculation: 2022-model inducement = a liquidity sweep opposite the trade bias: for long, current close > liquidity level AND prior high >= liquidity level; for short, current close < liquidity level AND prior low <= liquidity level.
Calculation: OTE entry reference = fair value gap low + 0.618 × (gap high − gap low).
Calculation: Protective stop = lowest low of the current and prior eight chart-timeframe bars minus one point for long; highest high of those bars plus one point for short.
Calculation: Risk distance = absolute difference between OTE entry reference and protective stop. Target 1, target 2, and target 3 are respectively 1, 2, and 3 risk distances from the OTE entry reference in the trade direction.
Long: higher-timeframe current ask > 200-period SMA on the last completed bar AND that SMA > its value on the currently forming higher-timeframe bar AND ((within the configured Silver Bullet time window AND current close < lowest-low liquidity level AND prior low <= liquidity level) OR (2022-model inducement AND bullish market structure shift)) AND a fair value gap exists AND current close is within its bounds AND current bar range <= configured threshold × 14-period ATR on the currently forming chart-timeframe bar.
Short: higher-timeframe current ask < 200-period SMA on the last completed bar AND that SMA < its value on the currently forming higher-timeframe bar AND ((within the configured Silver Bullet time window AND current close > highest-high liquidity level AND prior high >= liquidity level) OR (2022-model inducement AND bearish market structure shift)) AND a fair value gap exists AND current close is within its bounds AND current bar range <= configured threshold × 14-period ATR on the currently forming chart-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, current price <= protective stop OR current price >= target 1 OR current price >= target 2 OR current price >= target 3.
Exit: short, current price >= protective stop OR current price <= target 1 OR current price <= target 2 OR current price <= target 3.
