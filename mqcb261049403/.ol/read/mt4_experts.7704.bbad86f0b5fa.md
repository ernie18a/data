Calculation: Simple moving average of close = mean close over a long lookback on a lower timeframe, using the bar still forming
Long: no open or pending orders AND close < simple moving average − entry distance
Short: no open or pending orders AND close > simple moving average + entry distance
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
