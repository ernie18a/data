Calculation: Tipu Trend buy/sell signal = the Tipu Trend indicator's buy/sell output
Calculation: Tipu Trend range = the Tipu Trend indicator's range output
Calculation: Tipu Stops = the indicator's swing-low stop for long entries and swing-high stop for short entries
Long: higher-timeframe Tipu Trend buy signal on the bar still forming AND lower-timeframe Tipu Trend buy signal on the last completed bar AND higher-timeframe Tipu Trend is not in range AND chart-timeframe Tipu Stops long stop is valid AND (close − long stop) ≤ maximum entry risk
Short: higher-timeframe Tipu Trend sell signal on the bar still forming AND lower-timeframe Tipu Trend sell signal on the last completed bar AND higher-timeframe Tipu Trend is not in range AND chart-timeframe Tipu Stops short stop is valid AND (short stop − close) ≤ maximum entry risk
Exit: long, valid Short condition OR bar low ≤ active stop-loss OR bar high ≥ take-profit
Exit: short, valid Long condition OR bar high ≥ active stop-loss OR bar low ≤ take-profit
