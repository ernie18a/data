Calculation: Divergence inputs = At the start of each chart bar, use the MACD main line, rounded to six decimals, from the chart timeframe or a configurable higher or lower timeframe. Index bars backward from the forming bar at 0; use the last completed bar at 1. The MACD uses a shorter and a longer close EMA. The price-extremum search includes the forming bar, using its price as known at the start of the chart bar. Evaluate divergence only when the last completed divergence-timeframe bar has changed.
Calculation: Divergence pairs = For a bullish pair, require MACD[1], MACD[2], MACD[3], and MACD[4] below a negative threshold, and MACD[2] strictly below MACD[1], MACD[3], and MACD[4]. For a bearish pair, reverse all inequalities and use a positive threshold. Find the first low, or for bearish the first high, scanning backward from the forming bar over a configurable recent search range, that is strictly lower, or higher for bearish, than each of a configurable number of immediately older bars. Scan older MACD bars backward, starting beyond a configurable minimum separation: an older candidate centered at index k must have its MACD and the two values on each side beyond the same threshold, and its center must be strictly lower, or higher for bearish, than each of those four values. Process candidates in order; stop when a candidate is not strictly lower, or higher for bearish, than the preceding candidate, or when the configurable candidate limit is exceeded. For each candidate, find the first price low or high satisfying the same price-extremum test, scanning backward from the newer edge of its five-bar MACD window through a configurable search range. If a price search finds no extremum, use zero for the recent price and retain the preceding candidate's price, initially zero, for the older price. Each processed candidate supplies its MACD and price together with MACD[2] and the recent price.
Calculation: Stochastic range test = Use the low-high stochastic main line with simple moving-average smoothing, rounded to two decimals. Starting at one bar older than a configurable shift from the forming stochastic-timeframe bar, inspect a configurable number of consecutive older bars. The oldest inspected value must be within the inclusive configurable bounds and the number outside those bounds must not exceed the configurable exception count. If the bounds are reversed, swap them. The stochastic timeframe is the chart timeframe or a configurable higher or lower timeframe; the inspected values are completed bars.
Long: At least one bullish divergence pair has older MACD > recent MACD AND older low < recent low, OR older MACD < recent MACD AND older low > recent low.
Short: At least one bearish divergence pair has older MACD > recent MACD AND older high < recent high, OR older MACD < recent MACD AND older high > recent high.
Long: A bullish divergence pair satisfies either comparison above AND the last completed low-high stochastic main line > its simple moving-average signal line AND the stochastic range test is within the lower range.
Short: A bearish divergence pair satisfies either comparison above AND the last completed low-high stochastic main line < its simple moving-average signal line AND the stochastic range test is within the upper range.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus a fixed stop distance OR high >= entry price plus a fixed target distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus a fixed stop distance OR low <= entry price minus a fixed target distance.
Exit: long, high >= the upper Bollinger Band fixed at entry from the last completed band-timeframe bar OR low <= entry price minus a configurable fraction of the absolute distance from entry price to that band. The band timeframe is the chart timeframe or a configurable higher or lower timeframe.
Exit: short, low <= the lower Bollinger Band fixed at entry from the last completed band-timeframe bar OR high >= entry price plus a configurable fraction of the absolute distance from entry price to that band. The band timeframe is the chart timeframe or a configurable higher or lower timeframe.
