Calculation: OsMA = MACD line minus its signal line, where the MACD line is the fast EMA minus the slow EMA and the signal line is an EMA of the MACD line
Long: OsMA 3 bars ago > OsMA 2 bars ago AND OsMA 2 bars ago < OsMA 1 bar ago AND OsMA 1 bar ago < OsMA on the current, forming bar
Short: OsMA 3 bars ago < OsMA 2 bars ago AND OsMA 2 bars ago > OsMA 1 bar ago AND OsMA 1 bar ago > OsMA on the current, forming bar
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
