Calculation: Bollinger Stop trend and levels = Calculate Bollinger upper and lower bands from close prices. Carry forward the trend, initially neutral, and the prior bands. Set the trend bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band, bearish when close falls below the prior lower band, and otherwise retain the trend. In a bullish trend, clamp the lower band so it cannot decrease; in a bearish trend, clamp the upper band so it cannot increase. Extend the clamped band by an offset equal to a configurable factor times the band width: add it to the upper band and subtract it from the lower band. In a bullish trend, the lower extended band is the active bullish stop; clamp it so it cannot decrease. In a bearish trend, the upper extended band is the active bearish stop; clamp it so it cannot increase. An active stop is its level on a bar where that trend is active, and absent otherwise.
Long: The active bullish stop exists on the last completed bar of the selected indicator timeframe AND the active bearish stop exists on the immediately preceding completed bar of that timeframe.
Short: The active bearish stop exists on the last completed bar of the selected indicator timeframe AND the active bullish stop exists on the immediately preceding completed bar of that timeframe.
Exit: short, The active bullish stop exists on the last completed bar of the selected indicator timeframe AND the active bearish stop exists on the immediately preceding completed bar of that timeframe.
Exit: long, The active bearish stop exists on the last completed bar of the selected indicator timeframe AND the active bullish stop exists on the immediately preceding completed bar of that timeframe.
