Calculation: Smoothed momentum = Kaufman adaptive moving average of selected applied price minus that price from a longer lookback; use a shorter fast smoothing period than the slow smoothing period. Calculate on chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe smoothed momentum = the same calculation on higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Long: Smoothed momentum > 0 OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum > 0
Short: Smoothed momentum < 0 OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum < 0
