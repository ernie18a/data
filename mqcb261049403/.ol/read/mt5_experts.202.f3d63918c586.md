Calculation: Median price = (high + low) / 2
Long: Fast EMA < slow EMA on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND fast EMA > slow EMA on the preceding completed chart-timeframe bar AND RSI of median price < oversold level on the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Short: Fast EMA > slow EMA on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND fast EMA < slow EMA on the preceding completed chart-timeframe bar AND RSI of median price > overbought level on the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
