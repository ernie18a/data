Calculation: Trigger 1 RSI lookback is shorter than Trigger 2 RSI lookback.
Calculation: Trigger 2 asymmetric RSI bands = mean of the lookback RSI values ± entry-sigma multiplier times the standard deviation on that side of the mean (values at or above the mean for the upper band; below it for the lower band); outer limits use a larger multiplier. Clamp lower bands and limits to at least 5 and upper bands and limits to at most 95. Use the same lookback length for the entry bands and outer limits. Chart-timeframe and higher-timeframe samples use the last completed bars; the highest-timeframe samples include the bar still forming.
Long: (Trigger 1 enabled AND higher-timeframe RSI < its oversold threshold AND chart-timeframe RSI < its oversold threshold AND highest-timeframe RSI < its oversold threshold AND each RSI > its corresponding oversold outer limit AND chart-timeframe RSI change (last completed RSI minus the preceding completed RSI) > its lower delta limit AND chart-timeframe stochastic main line < its oversold threshold) OR (Trigger 2 enabled AND higher-timeframe RSI < its asymmetric lower band AND chart-timeframe RSI < its asymmetric lower band AND highest-timeframe RSI < its asymmetric lower band AND each RSI > its corresponding asymmetric lower outer limit AND chart-timeframe RSI change > its lower delta limit AND chart-timeframe stochastic main line < its oversold threshold); for the applicable trigger, higher-timeframe Bollinger-band width > its minimum, higher-timeframe ATR < its maximum, chart-timeframe Bollinger-band width < its maximum, current hour is within the configured entry window, and it is not past the Friday cutoff. RSI and stochastic decisions use last completed chart-timeframe bars, last completed higher-timeframe bars, and the highest-timeframe bar still forming for RSI; Bollinger-band widths and ATR use last completed bars.
Short: (Trigger 1 enabled AND higher-timeframe RSI > its overbought threshold AND chart-timeframe RSI > its overbought threshold AND highest-timeframe RSI > its overbought threshold AND each RSI < its corresponding overbought outer limit AND chart-timeframe RSI change < the negation of its lower delta limit AND chart-timeframe stochastic main line > its overbought threshold) OR (Trigger 2 enabled AND higher-timeframe RSI > its asymmetric upper band AND chart-timeframe RSI > its asymmetric upper band AND highest-timeframe RSI > its asymmetric upper band AND each RSI < its corresponding asymmetric upper outer limit AND chart-timeframe RSI change < the negation of its lower delta limit AND chart-timeframe stochastic main line > its overbought threshold); for the applicable trigger, higher-timeframe Bollinger-band width > its minimum, higher-timeframe ATR < its maximum, chart-timeframe Bollinger-band width < its maximum, current hour is within the configured entry window, and it is not past the Friday cutoff. RSI and stochastic decisions use last completed chart-timeframe bars, last completed higher-timeframe bars, and the highest-timeframe bar still forming for RSI; Bollinger-band widths and ATR use last completed bars.
Exit: long, bar low ≤ entry price − configured stop-loss distance OR bar high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit distance.
Exit: short, bar high ≥ entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR bar low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit distance.
