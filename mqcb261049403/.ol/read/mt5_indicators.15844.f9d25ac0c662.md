Calculation: Volume-weighted moving average = sum of the selected price times its bar volume over the lookback, divided by the sum of bar volumes over that lookback
Calculation: Standard deviation of moving-average changes = population standard deviation of the moving-average changes over the lookback
Long: current moving-average change > lower trend-strength coefficient × standard deviation of moving-average changes
Short: current moving-average change < −(lower trend-strength coefficient × standard deviation of moving-average changes)
