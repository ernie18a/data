Calculation: Normalized EMA distance = absolute(close − EMA) / ATR, calculated on the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) using completed bars.
Long: No position is open AND long trading is enabled AND ADX on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > ADX threshold AND ADX on that bar > ADX on the preceding completed bar AND normalized EMA distance on the last completed bar < pullback threshold AND normalized EMA distance on the preceding completed bar ≥ pullback threshold AND +DI on the last completed bar > −DI on that bar.
Short: No position is open AND short trading is enabled AND ADX on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > ADX threshold AND ADX on that bar > ADX on the preceding completed bar AND normalized EMA distance on the last completed bar < pullback threshold AND normalized EMA distance on the preceding completed bar ≥ pullback threshold AND −DI on the last completed bar > +DI on that bar.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − (ATR on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar at entry × stop multiplier) OR high ≥ entry price + (ATR on that bar × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio).
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + (ATR on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar at entry × stop multiplier) OR low ≤ entry price − (ATR on that bar × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio).
