Calculation: Fast average = short-period EMA of median price (high + low) / 2; use the current forming chart bar and the preceding completed chart bar.
Calculation: Smoothed averages = successively longer-period weighted moving averages of the fast average, then of the prior smoothed average, then of that result; use the current forming chart bar and the preceding completed chart bar.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = middle, upper, and lower bands around median price; use the current forming chart bar and the preceding completed chart bar.
Calculation: MACD direction = bullish when the MACD line and MACD histogram are both rising; bearish when both are falling. The MACD line is the difference between long-period and short-period exponential averages of close, with the short period shorter than the long period; the signal line is a simple average of the MACD line. Use the current forming bar and preceding completed bar on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4; use the current forming chart bar and preceding completed chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish trend = any of: (fast average >= its prior value AND first smoothed average > its prior value AND (first smoothed average − second smoothed average) >= its prior value AND chart open <= fast average); (fast average >= its prior value AND first smoothed average > second smoothed average AND second smoothed average > Bollinger middle AND Bollinger middle > its prior value AND chart open <= fast average); (fast average > its prior value AND fast average > Bollinger upper AND (fast average − first smoothed average) > its prior value AND (fast average − Bollinger upper) > its prior value AND chart open > fast average); (third smoothed average > its prior value AND Bollinger middle > its prior value AND Bollinger middle > third smoothed average AND fast average > Bollinger middle AND fast average > its prior value); (fast average >= its prior value AND (Bollinger middle − third smoothed average) > its prior value AND Bollinger middle < third smoothed average AND fast average > third smoothed average).
Calculation: Bearish trend = any of: (fast average <= its prior value AND first smoothed average < its prior value AND (first smoothed average − second smoothed average) <= its prior value AND chart open >= fast average); (fast average <= its prior value AND first smoothed average < second smoothed average AND second smoothed average < Bollinger middle AND Bollinger middle < its prior value AND chart open >= fast average); (fast average < its prior value AND fast average < Bollinger lower AND (fast average − first smoothed average) < its prior value AND (fast average − Bollinger lower) < its prior value AND chart open < fast average); (third smoothed average < its prior value AND Bollinger middle < its prior value AND Bollinger middle < third smoothed average AND fast average < Bollinger middle AND fast average < its prior value); (fast average <= its prior value AND (Bollinger middle − third smoothed average) < its prior value AND Bollinger middle > third smoothed average AND fast average < third smoothed average).
Calculation: Bullish exit trend = the fast average minus a short-period weighted moving average of the fast average is rising, and the fast average is at or above that weighted moving average; use current and preceding chart bars.
Calculation: Bearish exit trend = the fast average minus a short-period weighted moving average of the fast average is falling, and the fast average is at or below that weighted moving average; use current and preceding chart bars.
Long: MACD direction is bullish AND fast average > first smoothed average AND bullish trend AND current Heikin-Ashi close > prior Heikin-Ashi close AND chart close > fast average AND chart close > chart open AND chart high <= fast average + configured target distance.
Short: MACD direction is bearish AND fast average < first smoothed average AND bearish trend AND current Heikin-Ashi close < prior Heikin-Ashi close AND chart close < fast average AND chart close < chart open AND chart low >= fast average − configured target distance.
Exit: Long, market price <= the attached stop-loss OR market price >= the attached take-profit OR a short-entry signal occurs while redemption is enabled OR (automatic minimum take-profit is enabled AND market price − entry price exceeds the configured minimum-profit distance AND (chart high − chart close exceeds the configured reversal threshold OR chart close < entry price − the configured reversal threshold)) OR (automatic take-profit without a minimum is enabled AND bearish exit trend AND market price − entry price exceeds half the configured target distance).
Exit: Short, market price >= the attached stop-loss OR market price <= the attached take-profit OR a long-entry signal occurs while redemption is enabled OR (automatic minimum take-profit is enabled AND entry price − market price exceeds the configured minimum-profit distance AND (chart close − chart low exceeds the configured reversal threshold OR chart close > entry price + the configured reversal threshold)) OR (automatic take-profit without a minimum is enabled AND bullish exit trend AND entry price − market price exceeds half the configured target distance).
