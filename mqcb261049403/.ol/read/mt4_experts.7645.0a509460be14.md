Calculation: EMA of typical price = exponential moving average of typical price
Long: position side is flat or short AND chart-bar open < EMA − point AND ask < EMA AND previous completed bar’s EMA < current forming bar’s EMA
Short: position side is flat or long AND chart-bar open > EMA + point AND bid > EMA AND previous completed bar’s EMA > current forming bar’s EMA
Exit: short, chart-bar open < EMA − point AND ask < EMA AND previous completed bar’s EMA < current forming bar’s EMA
Exit: long, chart-bar open > EMA + point AND bid > EMA AND previous completed bar’s EMA > current forming bar’s EMA
