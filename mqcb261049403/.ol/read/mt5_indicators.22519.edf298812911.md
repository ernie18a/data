Calculation: BullsBearsEyes volume oscillator = multiply selected volume by 100 × CU/(CU+CD) − 50, where CU is the sum of positive adjacent differences in the four smoothed values L0–L3, and CD is the sum of the absolute values of negative adjacent differences. Set CU/(CU+CD) to 0 when the denominator is 0. For each chart-timeframe bar, let B = Bulls Power + Bears Power, where Bulls Power = high − EMA(close, lookback) and Bears Power = low − EMA(close, lookback). Recursively calculate L0 = (1 − smoothing factor) × B + smoothing factor × prior L0; L1 = −smoothing factor × L0 + prior L0 + smoothing factor × prior L1; L2 = −smoothing factor × L1 + prior L1 + smoothing factor × prior L2; L3 = −smoothing factor × L2 + prior L2 + smoothing factor × prior L3. Initialize prior values to 0. The EMA lookback and oscillator period are the same parameter. For the higher-timeframe variant, calculate on higher-timeframe bars and use the bar still forming.
Long: BullsBearsEyes volume oscillator > upper inner level × selected volume
Short: BullsBearsEyes volume oscillator < lower inner level × selected volume
