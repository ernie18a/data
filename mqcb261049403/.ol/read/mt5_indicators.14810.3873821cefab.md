Calculation: Bounce Strength Index = average over a lookback of the sum of floor and ceiling bounce strengths; for each bar in a shorter lookback, each strength is its close-to-low or high-to-close distance, respectively, multiplied by a range-position ratio and optional volume weight, then divided by the sum of that bar’s rolling high-low ranges and multiplied by 100. The range-position ratio is 1 at the corresponding rolling extreme; otherwise it is the distance from that extreme to the bar’s low or high divided by the rolling range. Include floor strength when the close is at least the previous close minus 20% of the bar range; include negative ceiling strength when the close is at most the previous close plus 20% of the bar range. The rolling range lookback is longer than the bounce-strength lookback. Optional volume weight is selected volume divided by its maximum over the rolling range lookback.
Long: Bounce Strength Index > Bounce Strength Index on the previous bar
Short: Bounce Strength Index < Bounce Strength Index on the previous bar
