Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed daily bar’s high and low and the close of the last completed chart-timeframe bar at the session boundary: P=(high+low+close)/3; R1=2P−low; S1=2P−high; R2=P+(high−low); S2=P−(high−low); R3=2P+high−2low; S3=2P−2high+low; M5=(R2+R3)/2; M4=(R1+R2)/2; M3=(P+R1)/2; M2=(P+S1)/2; M1=(S1+S2)/2; M0=(S2+S3)/2
Calculation: Pivot targets = in the interval containing the current price, the lower and upper bounds are the short and long targets; for the interval M5–R3, the code instead assigns S3 as the short target and M0 as the long target
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average; compare the shorter close-based EMA with the longer open-based EMA, both using the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: ADX and directional indicators = standard ADX, +DI, and −DI; use the forming chart-timeframe bar for current values and the last completed chart-timeframe bar for prior values
Calculation: OsMA = standard moving-average convergence/divergence oscillator histogram; compare the forming chart-timeframe bar with the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: Trailing stop = after favorable movement exceeds the trailing distance, ratchet the long stop up to current bid minus that distance, or the short stop down to current ask plus that distance
Long: No open orders AND long target minus ask exceeds the entry-distance threshold AND ADX exceeds its threshold AND current +DI > prior +DI AND current +DI > current −DI AND shorter close-based EMA exceeds longer open-based EMA by at least the point-distance threshold AND forming-bar OsMA > prior-bar OsMA
Short: No open orders AND bid minus short target exceeds the entry-distance threshold AND ADX exceeds its threshold AND current −DI > prior −DI AND current +DI < current −DI AND longer open-based EMA exceeds shorter close-based EMA by at least the point-distance threshold AND forming-bar OsMA < prior-bar OsMA
Exit: long, bid <= active stop (initially entry price minus the stop-loss distance; ratcheted by the trailing-stop rule) OR bid >= long target
Exit: short, ask >= active stop (initially entry price plus the stop-loss distance; ratcheted by the trailing-stop rule) OR ask <= short target
