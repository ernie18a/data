Calculation: Previous-day range = previous completed higher-timeframe daily bar high − low
Calculation: H3 = previous completed daily close + range × m3; L3 = previous completed daily close − range × m3
Calculation: H4 = previous completed daily close + range × m4; L4 = previous completed daily close − range × m4
Calculation: H5 = (previous completed daily high ÷ previous completed daily low) × previous completed daily close; L5 = 2 × previous completed daily close − H5
Long: low ≤ L3 OR close > H4
Short: high ≥ H3 OR close < L4
Exit: long, high ≥ H5
Exit: short, low ≤ L5
