Calculation: RAVI = (short-lookback simple moving average of bar open − long-lookback simple moving average of bar open) / long-lookback simple moving average of bar open × 100; the long lookback is longer than the short lookback.
Short: RAVI on the shorter of the two higher-than-chart timeframes, using the bar still forming, is greater than 0 AND ((RAVI on the longer higher-than-chart timeframe, using the bar still forming, is greater than its value on the last completed bar AND the last completed bar’s RAVI is less than the RAVI two bars ago AND the RAVI two bars ago is less than the RAVI three bars ago) OR (RAVI on the longer higher-than-chart timeframe, using the bar still forming, is less than its value on the last completed bar AND the last completed bar’s RAVI is greater than the RAVI two bars ago AND the RAVI two bars ago is greater than the RAVI three bars ago)).
Exit: short, high is greater than or equal to entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR low is less than or equal to entry price minus the take-profit distance
