Calculation: Tick Volume Index = smooth₃(100 × (U₂ − D₂) / (U₂ + D₂)), where U₂ and D₂ are U and D each smoothed first with lookback 1 and then with lookback 2; U = (volume + (close − open) / point size) / 2; D = volume − U. Apply the same smoothing method at all three stages; lookbacks 1, 2, and 3 are independently configurable.
Long: Tick Volume Index on the higher indicator timeframe, using completed bars, is lower on the previous completed bar than on the bar before it AND higher on the latest completed bar than on the previous completed bar.
Short: Tick Volume Index on the higher indicator timeframe, using completed bars, is higher on the previous completed bar than on the bar before it AND lower on the latest completed bar than on the previous completed bar.
Exit: Short, Tick Volume Index on the higher indicator timeframe, using completed bars, is lower on the previous completed bar than on the bar before it AND higher on the latest completed bar than on the previous completed bar.
Exit: Long, Tick Volume Index on the higher indicator timeframe, using completed bars, is higher on the previous completed bar than on the bar before it AND lower on the latest completed bar than on the previous completed bar.
