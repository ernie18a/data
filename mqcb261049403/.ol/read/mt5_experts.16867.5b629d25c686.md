Calculation: Williams %R = -100 × (highest high − close) / (highest high − lowest low) over the configured lookback, calculated from completed bars on the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe)
Long: (trend mode is direct AND previous completed Williams %R ≤ oversold level AND latest completed Williams %R > oversold level) OR (trend mode is against signals AND previous completed Williams %R ≥ overbought level AND latest completed Williams %R < overbought level)
Short: (trend mode is direct AND previous completed Williams %R ≥ overbought level AND latest completed Williams %R < overbought level) OR (trend mode is against signals AND previous completed Williams %R ≤ oversold level AND latest completed Williams %R > oversold level)
Exit: long position, (trend mode is direct AND previous completed Williams %R ≥ overbought level AND latest completed Williams %R < overbought level) OR (trend mode is against signals AND previous completed Williams %R ≤ oversold level AND latest completed Williams %R > oversold level)
Exit: short position, (trend mode is direct AND previous completed Williams %R ≤ oversold level AND latest completed Williams %R > oversold level) OR (trend mode is against signals AND previous completed Williams %R ≥ overbought level AND latest completed Williams %R < overbought level)
