Calculation: Fast moving average = moving average of close using the short lookback
Calculation: Slow moving average = moving average of close using the longer lookback
Calculation: Recent high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured lookback, including the forming signal-timeframe bar
Calculation: Williams %R = Williams %R on the configured filter timeframe; current value uses its forming bar and prior value uses the configured historical shift
Calculation: Stochastic = main-line stochastic on the signal timeframe, using the configured historical shift
Long: Within the configured trading hours AND (selected signal mode is 0 AND prior slow moving average < current slow moving average AND prior fast moving average < prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average > current slow moving average OR selected signal mode is 1 OR 2 AND prior fast moving average < prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average > current slow moving average OR selected signal mode is 3 AND prior fast moving average < prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average > current slow moving average AND current price > both moving averages AND recent low < both moving averages OR selected signal mode is 4 AND the mode 3 conditions AND prior Williams %R < oversold threshold OR selected signal mode is 5 AND the mode 3 conditions AND prior Williams %R < oversold-exit threshold AND current Williams %R > oversold-exit threshold OR selected signal mode is 6 AND the mode 3 conditions AND Stochastic < oversold threshold) AND (CounterTrend disabled OR the corresponding short signal is true)
Short: Within the configured trading hours AND (selected signal mode is 0 AND prior slow moving average > current slow moving average AND prior fast moving average > prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average < current slow moving average OR selected signal mode is 1 OR 2 AND prior fast moving average > prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average < current slow moving average OR selected signal mode is 3 AND prior fast moving average > prior slow moving average AND current fast moving average < current slow moving average AND current price < both moving averages AND recent high > both moving averages OR selected signal mode is 4 AND the mode 3 conditions AND prior Williams %R > overbought threshold OR selected signal mode is 5 AND the mode 3 conditions AND prior Williams %R > overbought-exit threshold AND current Williams %R < overbought-exit threshold OR selected signal mode is 6 AND the mode 3 conditions AND Stochastic > overbought threshold) AND (CounterTrend disabled OR the corresponding long signal is true)
Exit: Long, configured stop loss is reached OR configured take profit is reached OR an opposite short signal occurs while reverse-close is enabled
Exit: Short, configured stop loss is reached OR configured take profit is reached OR an opposite long signal occurs while reverse-close is enabled
