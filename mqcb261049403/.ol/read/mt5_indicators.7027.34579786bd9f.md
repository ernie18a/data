Calculation: NonLagMA = Apply the selected moving average to the selected price, then take its weighted sum over the current and older bars using cosine weights w(k)=g(k)cos(πt(k)), normalized by the sum of weights and scaled by (1+deviation/100); let P be the lookback, Cycle=4, Phase=P−1, L=4P+Phase, t(k)=k/(Phase−1) for k≤Phase−1 and 1+(k−Phase+1)(2Cycle−1)/(Cycle·P−1) otherwise, and g(k)=1 when t(k)≤0.5 or 1/(3πt(k)+1) otherwise. If the absolute change from the previous NonLagMA is less than the static filter in price units, carry forward the previous value. It may be calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; higher-timeframe values use the bar still forming.
Long: The most recent NonLagMA change whose absolute size exceeds the static filter is positive
Short: The most recent NonLagMA change whose absolute size exceeds the static filter is negative
