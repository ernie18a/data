Calculation: Completed session high/low = maximum high and minimum low, respectively, across bars whose datetime hour is within the configured session window; freeze these levels when the session ends.
Long: Outside the session, a bar after a recorded breach where a prior bar's low fell below the completed session low by more than the penetration buffer, and no more than the allowed number of bars have elapsed since that breach, has close > completed session low. Record at most one breach at a time; if one bar breaches both sides, record the high-side breach first.
Short: Outside the session, a bar after a recorded breach where a prior bar's high rose above the completed session high by more than the penetration buffer, and no more than the allowed number of bars have elapsed since that breach, has close < completed session high. Record at most one breach at a time; if one bar breaches both sides, record the high-side breach first.
