Calculation: take-profit distance = stop-loss distance × selected risk-benefit multiple
Long: MACD signal line on the current forming bar of the strategy timeframe > 0 AND MACD signal line on the preceding bar < 0
Short: MACD signal line on the current forming bar of the strategy timeframe < 0 AND MACD signal line on the preceding bar > 0
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
