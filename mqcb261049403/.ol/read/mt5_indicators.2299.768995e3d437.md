Calculation: Asymmetric stochastic = For each bar, over the slowing lookback, sum closes and sum selected highs and lows: each high is the maximum over a K lookback and each low the minimum over a K lookback. Use the short K lookback for highs and long for lows when the prior smoothed stochastic is above the overbought threshold; reverse them when it is below the oversold threshold; initially use the short lookback for both. If the summed high-low range is less than sensitivity times the slowing lookback, use that floor as the range and lower the summed low by half the difference. Compute 100 × (summed closes − adjusted low) / adjusted range.
Calculation: Signal line = smoothed asymmetric stochastic.
Long: Asymmetric stochastic on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar > 50 OR Asymmetric stochastic on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar > its signal line
Short: Asymmetric stochastic on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar < 50 OR Asymmetric stochastic on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar < its signal line
