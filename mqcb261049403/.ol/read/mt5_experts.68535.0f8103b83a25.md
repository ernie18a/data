Calculation: bullish pivot = a bar whose high is at least as high as every high in the 5 bars before and after it; bearish pivot = a bar whose low is at least as low as every low in those bars. Use the most recent pivot of each type, scanning back from 5 to 99 completed chart bars.
Calculation: Fibonacci buy-limit price = pivot high − (pivot high − pivot low) × retracement fraction; Fibonacci sell-limit price = pivot low + (pivot high − pivot low) × retracement fraction.
Calculation: structure stop = pivot low − price-point buffer for long positions; pivot high + price-point buffer for short positions.
Calculation: take-profit price = entry price + (entry price − stop price) × risk-reward multiple for long positions; entry price − (stop price − entry price) × risk-reward multiple for short positions.
Calculation: market-structure prediction = ONNX model class and probability, using completed chart-bar momentum change, distances from the recent 50-bar high and low, relative tick volume, candle body strength (each price measure normalized by ATR), and hour of day.
Long: predicted class is 2 or 3 AND its probability exceeds the confidence threshold AND current price is above the 50-period SMA of completed chart bars AND no position or pending order exists AND both pivots exist AND Fibonacci buy-limit price is below current ask.
Short: predicted class is 0 or 1 AND its probability exceeds the confidence threshold AND current price is below the 50-period SMA of completed chart bars AND no position or pending order exists AND both pivots exist AND Fibonacci sell-limit price is above current bid.
Exit: long, price is at or below stop price OR at or above take-profit price; once favorable price movement is at least the trailing activation fraction of the entry-to-take-profit distance, raise stop to entry price + 0.3 × favorable price movement.
Exit: short, price is at or above stop price OR at or below take-profit price; once favorable price movement is at least the trailing activation fraction of the entry-to-take-profit distance, lower stop to entry price − 0.3 × favorable price movement.
