Calculation: Smoothed stochastic lines = Apply the same configurable moving-average method, length, and phase independently to the main and signal lines of a slow stochastic oscillator. The oscillator has configurable lookback, slowing, signal period, averaging method, and high/low or close/close price range. Calculate separate, independently parameterized lines for long and short decisions on a configurable timeframe that may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower. Use a configurable signal bar: the last completed bar or the still-forming bar; the previous value is from the immediately preceding bar of that timeframe.
Long: Long smoothed stochastic main line on the previous signal-timeframe bar <= its signal line on that bar AND long smoothed stochastic main line on the signal bar > its signal line on that bar
Short: Short smoothed stochastic main line on the previous signal-timeframe bar >= its signal line on that bar AND short smoothed stochastic main line on the signal bar < its signal line on that bar
Exit: long, long smoothed stochastic main line on the signal bar < its signal line on that bar OR low <= entry price - long stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + long take-profit distance
Exit: short, short smoothed stochastic main line on the signal bar > its signal line on that bar OR high >= entry price + short stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - short take-profit distance
