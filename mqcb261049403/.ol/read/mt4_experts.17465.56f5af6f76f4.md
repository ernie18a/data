Calculation: ADR = average true range on completed daily bars; ATR = average true range on completed chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: ATR variation = 0.5 × (short-lookback daily ATR / longer-lookback daily ATR − 1) + 0.5 × (short-lookback chart-timeframe ATR / longer-lookback chart-timeframe ATR − 1)
Calculation: Asian range = highest high and lowest low of the configured number of completed H1 bars before London open
Calculation: PSAR swing high/low = most recent completed-bar PSAR flip above/below price, respectively
Calculation: Entry price = PSAR swing high + ATR buffer for long; PSAR swing low − ATR buffer for short
Calculation: Stop price = opposite PSAR swing, buffered by ADR, with entry-to-stop distance clamped to a minimum and maximum fraction of ADR; target price = entry price ± stop distance × target factor
Long: completed chart-timeframe bar high − low > ATR AND absolute ATR variation ≤ maximum AND within London order-placement window AND Asian range size is between its minimum and maximum ADR fractions AND spread ≤ maximum ADR fraction AND PSAR on the last completed chart-timeframe bar < prior close AND PSAR on the preceding completed chart-timeframe bar > last completed close AND MACD main > signal on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND Stochastic ≤ upper bound AND entry price > Asian range high
Short: completed chart-timeframe bar high − low > ATR AND absolute ATR variation ≤ maximum AND within London order-placement window AND Asian range size is between its minimum and maximum ADR fractions AND spread ≤ maximum ADR fraction AND PSAR on the last completed chart-timeframe bar > prior close AND PSAR on the preceding completed chart-timeframe bar < last completed close AND MACD main < signal on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND Stochastic ≥ lower bound AND entry price < Asian range low
Exit: long, MACD main crosses below signal between the two most recent completed chart-timeframe bars OR price reaches stop price OR price reaches target price OR New York close or symbol session close is reached
Exit: short, MACD main crosses above signal between the two most recent completed chart-timeframe bars OR price reaches stop price OR price reaches target price OR New York close or symbol session close is reached
