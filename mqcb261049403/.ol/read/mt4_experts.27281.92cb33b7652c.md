Calculation: Session high and low = high and low of the most recent signal-timeframe bar whose opening hour matches the configured level hour.
Calculation: Mean close = arithmetic mean of closes from the current signal-timeframe bar through the session-level bar, inclusive.
Long: At the configured signal hour, the previous completed signal-timeframe close is below the session low minus the minimum distance and above the session low minus the maximum distance, and no close from the current signal-timeframe bar through the session-level bar is above the session high, and NOT (mean close > the close of the bar opening at the configured recheck hour AND that recheck close < the immediately preceding signal-timeframe close) [contrary mode]; OR at the configured signal hour, the previous completed signal-timeframe close is above the session high plus the minimum distance and below the session high plus the maximum distance, and no close from the current signal-timeframe bar through the session-level bar is below the session low, and NOT (mean close < the close of the bar opening at the configured recheck hour AND that recheck close > the immediately preceding signal-timeframe close) [according-trend mode].
Short: At the configured signal hour, the previous completed signal-timeframe close is above the session high plus the minimum distance and below the session high plus the maximum distance, and no close from the current signal-timeframe bar through the session-level bar is below the session low, and NOT (mean close < the close of the bar opening at the configured recheck hour AND that recheck close > the immediately preceding signal-timeframe close) [contrary mode]; OR at the configured signal hour, the previous completed signal-timeframe close is below the session low minus the minimum distance and above the session low minus the maximum distance, and no close from the current signal-timeframe bar through the session-level bar is above the session high, and NOT (mean close > the close of the bar opening at the configured recheck hour AND that recheck close < the immediately preceding signal-timeframe close) [according-trend mode].
Exit: Long, at or after the configured close hour OR (when signal-based closing is enabled) the previous completed signal-timeframe close is above the session low plus the minimum distance [contrary mode] OR below the session high minus the minimum distance [according-trend mode] OR price reaches the configured take-profit or stop-loss distance from entry.
Exit: Short, at or after the configured close hour OR (when signal-based closing is enabled) the previous completed signal-timeframe close is below the session high minus the minimum distance [contrary mode] OR above the session low plus the minimum distance [according-trend mode] OR price reaches the configured take-profit or stop-loss distance from entry.

Signal-timeframe bars may be the chart timeframe or a configured higher or lower timeframe. The session level and recheck bars are completed bars; the current signal-timeframe bar is still forming and its close is used in the mean close and intervening-close checks. Distances are configured price distances.
