Calculation: Chart-timeframe swing markers = On each chart bar, compare its high and low with the highest high and lowest low of the preceding N bars. A high-only breakout sets the swing direction upward; a low-only breakout sets it downward; a breakout of both reverses the prior direction. When direction changes upward, mark the lowest low tracked during the preceding downswing; when it changes downward, mark the highest high tracked during the preceding upswing. While direction persists, update its tracked extreme to the highest high (upward) or lowest low (downward).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing markers = Apply the same swing-marker calculation to bars of a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe, using the same N-bar lookback. Map its markers onto chart bars while the higher-timeframe bar is still forming.
Long: An upward marker is present on the chart timeframe OR on the higher timeframe
Short: A downward marker is present on the chart timeframe OR on the higher timeframe
