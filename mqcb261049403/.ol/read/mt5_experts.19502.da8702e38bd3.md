Calculation: CMO = 100 × (sum of positive changes in the moving average of applied price over the lookback − sum of absolute negative changes) ÷ sum of absolute moving-average changes; calculate on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using completed bars.
Long: CMO on the earlier of the two most recent completed indicator-timeframe bars ≤ 0 AND CMO on the later bar > 0
Short: CMO on the earlier of the two most recent completed indicator-timeframe bars ≥ 0 AND CMO on the later bar < 0
Exit: Long, CMO on the most recent completed indicator-timeframe bar < 0
Exit: Short, CMO on the most recent completed indicator-timeframe bar > 0
