Calculation: Pivot point = (previous completed higher-timeframe bar high + low + close) / 3; use the chart timeframe when it is the higher timeframe
Calculation: Resistance = 2 × Pivot point − previous completed higher-timeframe bar low
Calculation: Support = 2 × Pivot point − previous completed higher-timeframe bar high
Long: On the first chart bar of a day, open ≥ previous completed higher-timeframe bar high OR (open < Resistance AND Resistance − open ≥ open − Support)
Short: On the first chart bar of a day, open ≤ previous completed higher-timeframe bar low OR (open > Resistance AND open < previous completed higher-timeframe bar high) OR (open < Resistance AND Resistance − open < open − Support)
