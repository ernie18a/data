Calculation: H3 and H4 = previous completed daily bar close plus its high-low range multiplied by separate parameters, with H4's multiplier larger than H3's; L3 and L4 = previous completed daily bar close minus its high-low range multiplied by separate parameters, with L4's multiplier larger than L3's. Use the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe when chart is intraday).
Calculation: H5 = previous completed daily bar close × (high / low); L5 = 2 × previous completed daily bar close − H5. Use the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe when chart is intraday).
Long: close ≤ L3 OR close > H4
Short: close ≥ H3 OR close < L4
Exit: long, close ≥ H5
Exit: short, close ≤ L5
