Calculation: Reversal distance = average absolute high–low range over a lookback, divided by a decimal scaling factor.
Calculation: NRTR state = In an uptrend, update the peak to the greater of the prior peak and current high, then set the threshold to the greater of the prior threshold and peak × (1 − reversal distance); if current high is below the threshold, switch to downtrend and reset peak to current high and threshold to peak × (1 + reversal distance). In a downtrend, update the trough to the lesser of the prior trough and current low, then set the threshold to the lesser of the prior threshold and trough × (1 + reversal distance); if current low is above the threshold, switch to uptrend and reset trough to current low and threshold to trough × (1 − reversal distance). Initialize the neutral state’s peak and trough from the close.
Long: On the higher-timeframe bar still forming, the prior state is downtrend AND current low is above the NRTR threshold.
Short: On the higher-timeframe bar still forming, the prior state is uptrend AND current high is below the NRTR threshold.
