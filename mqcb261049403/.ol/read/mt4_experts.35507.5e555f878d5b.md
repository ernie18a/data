Calculation: Prior maximum range = maximum high minus low among the configured number of chart bars immediately preceding the signal bar.
Long: At the open of a new chart bar, no long position is open AND the last completed bar's shorter-period close EMA is greater than its longer-period close EMA for each of two independently configured EMA pairs AND its open is greater than a separately configured close EMA AND prior maximum range is greater than the minimum range AND its close minus open is at least prior maximum range multiplied by the impulse-strength parameter AND its close is at least its open AND its high minus close is at most the configured fraction of its high-low range AND its open minus low is at least the configured fraction of its high-low range AND (minimum candle range is disabled OR its high-low range is at least the minimum candle range) AND (maximum candle range is disabled OR its high-low range is at most the maximum candle range) AND its low is at most a separately configured close EMA.
Short: At the open of a new chart bar, no short position is open AND the last completed bar's shorter-period close EMA is less than its longer-period close EMA for each of two independently configured EMA pairs AND its open is less than a separately configured close EMA AND prior maximum range is greater than the minimum range AND its open minus close is at least prior maximum range multiplied by the impulse-strength parameter AND its close is less than its open AND its high minus open is at least the configured fraction of its high-low range AND its close minus low is at most the configured fraction of its high-low range AND (minimum candle range is disabled OR its high-low range is at least the minimum candle range) AND (maximum candle range is disabled OR its high-low range is at most the maximum candle range) AND its high is at least a separately configured close EMA.
Exit: long, (stop-loss distance is positive AND bar low is at most entry price minus stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance is positive AND bar high is at least entry price plus take-profit distance).
Exit: short, (stop-loss distance is positive AND bar high is at least entry price plus stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance is positive AND bar low is at most entry price minus take-profit distance).
