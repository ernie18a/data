Calculation: Session pivot = (previous session high + previous session low + 2 × previous session close) / 4
Calculation: Buy zone = session pivot + ATR on a lower timeframe; sell zone = session pivot − ATR on a lower timeframe
Calculation: First buy target = session pivot + ATR on a lower timeframe + ATR on a lower timeframe with a longer bar interval; first sell target = session pivot − ATR on those same two timeframes
Calculation: Bullish power = percentage of bullish votes among the moving-average, CCI, MACD, directional-index, Bulls Power, Bears Power, stochastic, RSI, Force Index, momentum, and DeMarker comparisons, summed across seven timeframes spanning lower and higher than the chart. Moving-average lookbacks are short, medium, and long in that order. A vote is bullish when: each EMA rises from its previous value; CCI is above zero; MACD main is above signal; +DI is above −DI; Bulls Power or Bears Power is above zero; stochastic main is at least signal; RSI is at least 50; Force Index is at least zero; momentum is at least its neutral level; or DeMarker is at least its previous value. Use the bar mapped to the chart-bar timestamp on each timeframe, including a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Bearish power = 100 − bullish power
Calculation: Analysis bullish percentage = bullish votes / all directional votes × 100, using RSI direction (current versus two bars earlier), RSI fast/slow crossing, RSI level, stochastic direction (current versus two bars earlier), stochastic crossing, and stochastic level. RSI and stochastic levels are bullish above 50 through 70, bearish from 30 through 50, and otherwise contribute no level vote. A fast RSI crossing is bullish when fast RSI is above slow RSI now and was below it two bars earlier; bearish for the reverse. A stochastic crossing is counted bullish when either current or previous stochastic main/signal comparison indicates a crossing. Use chart-timeframe bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Analysis bearish percentage = 100 − analysis bullish percentage
