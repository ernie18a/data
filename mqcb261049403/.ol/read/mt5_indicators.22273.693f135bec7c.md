Calculation: Skyscraper lower and upper bands = On the selected timeframe (same as or higher than the chart timeframe), calculate each bar’s average high-low range over a lookback; track its running maximum and minimum; set step to half their sum times a sensitivity factor, rounded down to a whole price point. In High/Low mode, candidate lower band = high − 2 steps and candidate upper band = low + 2 steps; in Close mode, use close − 2 steps and close + 2 steps. Set trend up if close exceeds the prior upper band, down if it falls below the prior lower band, otherwise retain prior trend. In an uptrend, the lower band is the maximum of its candidate and prior lower band; in a downtrend, the upper band is the minimum of its candidate and prior upper band. Use the selected timeframe’s bar corresponding to each chart bar, including the bar still forming.
Long: Previous bar has a lower band AND current bar has an upper band
Short: Previous bar has an upper band AND current bar has a lower band
