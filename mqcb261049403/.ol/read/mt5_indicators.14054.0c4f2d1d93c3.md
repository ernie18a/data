Calculation: Composite DeMarker = weighted sum of DeMarker readings at five progressively longer lookbacks; the second-shortest and middle-lookback readings both use the second-shortest reading’s weight.
Calculation: Smoothed Composite DeMarker = recursive average of the Composite DeMarker, using the same smoothing parameter at every bar.
Long: Composite DeMarker > Smoothed Composite DeMarker
Short: Composite DeMarker < Smoothed Composite DeMarker
