Calculation: BrakeExp direction = Start bullish on the oldest bar with its low as the starting price. For each later bar, track the highest high and lowest low since the last direction change. While bullish, compare the starting price plus an increasing exponential offset with the bar's low; when the offset line is above the low, switch to bearish and reset the starting price to the tracked highest high. While bearish, compare the starting price minus the increasing exponential offset with the bar's high; when the offset line is below the high, switch to bullish and reset the starting price to the tracked lowest low. The offset grows exponentially with the number of bars since the last direction change; its growth rate and price scale are parameters. Evaluate on the signal timeframe using the last completed bar; its relation to the chart timeframe depends on the configured signal timeframe.
Long: BrakeExp direction changes from bearish on the prior signal-timeframe bar to bullish on the current signal-timeframe bar
Short: BrakeExp direction changes from bullish on the prior signal-timeframe bar to bearish on the current signal-timeframe bar
Exit: long, BrakeExp direction is bearish on the current signal-timeframe bar
Exit: short, BrakeExp direction is bullish on the current signal-timeframe bar
