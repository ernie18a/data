Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = candle 1 high < candle 3 low; gap bounds are candle 1 high to candle 3 low.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = candle 1 low > candle 3 high; gap bounds are candle 3 high to candle 1 low.
Calculation: Gap fill percentage = greatest penetration into the gap by any later bar, divided by gap width, times 100; penetration is measured from the gap’s near edge and capped at full width.
Long: On the chart timeframe, candle 1 high < candle 3 low AND (candle 3 low − candle 1 high) ≥ minimum gap size; candle 3 may still be forming. OR, on a selected scan timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), candle 1 high < candle 3 low AND gap width ≥ minimum gap size AND gap fill percentage < fill threshold AND the gap ranks among the configured maximum number of most recent unfilled gaps for that timeframe; the latest bar may still be forming.
Short: On the chart timeframe, candle 1 low > candle 3 high AND (candle 1 low − candle 3 high) ≥ minimum gap size; candle 3 may still be forming. OR, on a selected scan timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), candle 1 low > candle 3 high AND gap width ≥ minimum gap size AND gap fill percentage < fill threshold AND the gap ranks among the configured maximum number of most recent unfilled gaps for that timeframe; the latest bar may still be forming.
