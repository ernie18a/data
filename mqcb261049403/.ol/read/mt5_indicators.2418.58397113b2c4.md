Calculation: Price and Volume Trend = cumulative sum of bar volume multiplied by the selected price’s percentage change from the prior bar; calculate on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar when the higher-timeframe version is used.
Calculation: Signal line = a moving average of Price and Volume Trend, using the selected smoothing method and lookback on the same timeframe and bar state as Price and Volume Trend.
Long: Price and Volume Trend > Signal line
Short: Price and Volume Trend < Signal line
