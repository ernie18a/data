Calculation: EMA envelope = EMA of close with upper and lower bands offset from the EMA by a configurable percentage; calculate it on the configured signal timeframe, using the last completed bar for closed-bar signals or the bar still forming for live signals.
Long: (signal bar low ≤ lower band OR, on startup, a recent completed bar low ≤ its lower band AND current ask ≤ lower band + configured tolerance) AND spread ≤ maximum AND buy entries enabled AND no existing long position AND (current long position count < maximum AND (no long position exists OR current ask is at least the configured grid distance below the newest long entry)); if both bands are reached, choose long when the lower-band excursion is larger, or when excursions are nearly equal and signal bar close ≥ open.
Short: (signal bar high ≥ upper band OR, on startup, a recent completed bar high ≥ its upper band AND current bid ≥ upper band − configured tolerance) AND spread ≤ maximum AND sell entries enabled AND no existing short position AND (current short position count < maximum AND (no short position exists OR current bid is at least the configured grid distance above the newest short entry)); if both bands are reached, choose short when the upper-band excursion is larger, or when excursions are nearly equal and signal bar close < open.
Exit: long, current bid is at least the configured single-trade take-profit distance above the entry price, when exactly one long grid level is open.
Exit: short, current ask is at least the configured single-trade take-profit distance below the entry price, when exactly one short grid level is open.
