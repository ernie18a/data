Calculation: Daily pivot levels = From the last completed bar of the higher D1 timeframe: P=(high+low+close)/3; R1=2P−low; S1=2P−high; R2=P+(R1−S1); S2=P−(R1−S1); R3=high+2(P−low); S3=low−2(high−P).
Long: Previous completed chart-bar close ≤ P AND latest completed chart-bar close > P AND flat.
Short: Previous completed chart-bar close ≥ P AND latest completed chart-bar close < P AND flat.
Exit: Long, low ≤ selected stop level OR high ≥ selected target level OR intraday-only mode is enabled AND datetime hour = 23.
Exit: Short, high ≥ selected stop level OR low ≤ selected target level OR intraday-only mode is enabled AND datetime hour = 23.
