Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is greater than the highs of the preceding and following lookback bars; confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the preceding and following lookback bars. Confirm only after the following lookback bars have closed.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = the price zone from the high two bars earlier to the current bar’s low when the current low is above that earlier high; bearish FVG = the zone from the current bar’s high to the low two bars earlier when the current high is below that earlier low. Use the newest unmitigated gap in the sweep-to-break sequence, within the configured sequence lookback.
Calculation: Order block = the most recent opposite-direction candle within the configured lookback before a structure break; its zone is its full high-low range. Reject if price closed beyond the zone before the break or it was revisited at least twice. Use only as a fallback when no qualifying FVG exists and fallback is enabled.
Calculation: Market regime = low volatility when current ATR is below its longer-lookback ATR average times a factor; otherwise ranging when recent high-low width divided by ATR is below a threshold and the absolute fast/slow EMA separation divided by ATR is below a threshold; otherwise trending when fast EMA is above slow EMA and MACD main is above signal for bullish bias, or both are below for bearish bias; otherwise reversal.
Long: On a closed bar, close > latest unswept, unbroken confirmed swing high + ATR break buffer AND either a valid unused low sweep occurred within sweep memory (bar low < swing low and close > swing low), or this is a non-CHoCH break in a trending regime, or this is a break in a reversal regime; bullish candle body / ATR meets the path’s displacement minimum; regime is neither low volatility nor ranging; a fresh bullish FVG exists or an allowed fresh order-block fallback exists; for continuation or reversal paths, RSI > 50 AND MACD main > MACD signal; the entry zone midpoint is within the configured ATR distance of the break close, or body / ATR meets the stronger-body threshold and momentum is aligned (then use a stop entry beyond the break bar high by an ATR buffer); risk is within the configured maximum ATR; spread is within its configured fraction of risk; nearest unswept, unbroken opposing swing liquidity is absent or has sufficient target room; final target room meets its minimum; and quality score meets its threshold. Arm the setup at the zone midpoint for a limit entry, or at the stop-entry level; enter when a later bar touches the limit price or crosses the stop-entry level. A scheduled fallback may also arm a long stop entry when the daily opportunity count is below its target, the configured server-time slot has arrived, and a majority of fast/slow EMA, MACD, and RSI votes bullish; require spread within its risk fraction. Invalidate pending setups if close reaches/passes the stop, an opposite CHoCH occurs, the pending window expires, or a stop-entry zone is fully traversed.
Short: On a closed bar, close < latest unswept, unbroken confirmed swing low - ATR break buffer AND either a valid unused high sweep occurred within sweep memory (bar high > swing high and close < swing high), or this is a non-CHoCH break in a trending regime, or this is a break in a reversal regime; bearish candle body / ATR meets the path’s displacement minimum; regime is neither low volatility nor ranging; a fresh bearish FVG exists or an allowed fresh order-block fallback exists; for continuation or reversal paths, RSI < 50 AND MACD main < MACD signal; the entry zone midpoint is within the configured ATR distance of the break close, or body / ATR meets the stronger-body threshold and momentum is aligned (then use a stop entry beyond the break bar low by an ATR buffer); risk is within the configured maximum ATR; spread is within its configured fraction of risk; nearest unswept, unbroken opposing swing liquidity is absent or has sufficient target room; final target room meets its minimum; and quality score meets its threshold. Arm the setup at the zone midpoint for a limit entry, or at the stop-entry level; enter when a later bar touches the limit price or crosses the stop-entry level. A scheduled fallback may also arm a short stop entry when the daily opportunity count is below its target, the configured server-time slot has arrived, and a majority of fast/slow EMA, MACD, and RSI votes bearish; require spread within its risk fraction. Invalidate pending setups if close reaches/passes the stop, an opposite CHoCH occurs, the pending window expires, or a stop-entry zone is fully traversed.
Exit: Long, bar low <= stop price OR bar high >= second target OR active setup age exceeds its expiry.
Exit: Short, bar high >= stop price OR bar low <= second target OR active setup age exceeds its expiry.
