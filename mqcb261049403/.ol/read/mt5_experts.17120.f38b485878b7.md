Calculation: Fractal MFI = For each bar, calculate the fractal dimension over a lookback of E bars of the selected applied-price series: normalize each price to the lookback low/high range, sum the distances between consecutive normalized prices using √(difference² + 1/E²), then FDI = 1 + (ln(sum) + ln 2)/ln(2(E−1)). Set Hurst = 2 − FDI and the MFI lookback = round(normal speed / (2 × Hurst)). Calculate MFI over that lookback by comparing consecutive selected prices: sum volume × current price for rises as positive flow, and for falls as negative flow; MFI = 100 − 100/(1 + positive flow/negative flow), or 100 when negative flow is zero. Use the indicator timeframe, whether higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and its last two completed bars.
Long: (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≤ oversold threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar > oversold threshold) OR (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≥ overbought threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar < overbought threshold)
Short: (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≥ overbought threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar < overbought threshold) OR (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≤ oversold threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar > oversold threshold)
Exit: Long, (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≥ overbought threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar < overbought threshold) OR (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≤ oversold threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar > oversold threshold)
Exit: Short, (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≤ oversold threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar > oversold threshold) OR (Fractal MFI on the older completed bar ≥ overbought threshold AND Fractal MFI on the newer completed bar < overbought threshold)
