Calculation: Asian range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured Asian session, used only after that session completes
Calculation: Previous day high/low = highest high / lowest low among chart bars in the previous calendar day
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is strictly greater than the highs of the N preceding bars and greater than or equal to the highs of the N following bars; confirm after those N following bars close
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is strictly lower than the lows of the N preceding bars and less than or equal to the lows of the N following bars; confirm after those N following bars close
Long: Confirmed swing low OR current bar price > Asian range high OR current bar price > previous day high
Short: Confirmed swing high OR current bar price < Asian range low OR current bar price < previous day low
