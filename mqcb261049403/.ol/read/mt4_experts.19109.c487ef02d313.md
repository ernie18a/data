Calculation: Correlation = Pearson correlation between the two symbols’ close prices over the lookback on the selected correlation timeframe, including its current still-forming bar; that timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Overlay deviation = (Pair 1 close − adjusted Pair 2 close) / Pair 1 point size, using the current chart-timeframe bar and the last 400 bars including the current bar. Adjusted Pair 2 close = Pair 2 range midpoint + (Pair 2 close − Pair 2 range midpoint) × (Pair 1 range / signed Pair 2 range). Each range is highest high − lowest low; Pair 2’s signed range is positive for positively correlated pairs and negative for negatively correlated pairs.
Calculation: ATR ratio = Pair 2’s average high−low range in points divided by Pair 1’s average high−low range in points, over valid bars on the selected ATR timeframe, using completed bars only. The ATR timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: ATR ratio > 0 AND ((Correlation > correlation threshold AND Overlay deviation < −overlay threshold) OR (Correlation < −correlation threshold AND Overlay deviation < −overlay threshold))
Short: ATR ratio > 0 AND ((Correlation > correlation threshold AND Overlay deviation > overlay threshold) OR (Correlation < −correlation threshold AND Overlay deviation > overlay threshold))
Exit: long or short, (points-based take profit is enabled AND combined floating points ≥ points target) OR (USD-based take profit is enabled AND combined floating USD profit, including commission and swap, ≥ USD target)
