Calculation: Force Index = bar volume × (current bar’s moving average of applied price − preceding bar’s same moving average)
Calculation: Short-term Force Index = shorter-period moving average of Force Index
Calculation: Long-term Force Index = longer-period moving average of Force Index
Long: Short-term Force Index crosses below zero AND Long-term Force Index is rising
Short: Short-term Force Index crosses above zero AND Long-term Force Index is falling
