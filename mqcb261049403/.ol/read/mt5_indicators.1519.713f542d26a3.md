Calculation: Bull strength = Apply the selected smoothing method with the long lookback to raw bull force, then with the shorter smoothing lookback; raw bull force is the positive one-bar change in the pre-smoothed selected price, or its distance above the lowest low over the evaluation lookback in Stochastic mode. In DMI mode, compare the positive change in pre-smoothed selected price with the positive decline in pre-smoothed low, keeping only the larger force (zero on a tie). Divide force by the instrument point size.
Calculation: Bear strength = Apply the selected smoothing method with the long lookback to raw bear force, then with the shorter smoothing lookback; raw bear force is the magnitude of the negative one-bar change in pre-smoothed selected price, or the distance below the highest high over the evaluation lookback in Stochastic mode. In DMI mode, compare the positive decline in pre-smoothed low with the positive change in pre-smoothed selected price, keeping only the larger force (zero on a tie). Divide force by the instrument point size.
Calculation: Bull signal = Apply the selected smoothing method to bull strength over the signal lookback; when the signal lookback is one, use the previous bar's bull strength.
Calculation: Bear signal = Apply the selected smoothing method to bear strength over the signal lookback; when the signal lookback is one, use the previous bar's bear strength.
Long: Bull strength > Bull signal AND Bull strength > Bear strength AND (Bear strength <= Bear signal OR Bear strength = 0)
Short: Bear strength > Bear signal AND Bear strength > Bull strength AND (Bull strength <= Bull signal OR Bull strength = 0)
