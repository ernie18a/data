Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of the configured price, calculated on the configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the corresponding bar, including while it is still forming
Calculation: RSI moving average = configured moving-average method applied to the RSI series, with a longer lookback than the RSI period and scaled by the configured-timeframe-to-chart-timeframe ratio
Long: RSI > RSI moving average
Short: RSI <= RSI moving average
