Calculation: BrainTrend2 trend state = bullish or bearish state maintained by a trailing threshold: initialize bullish when the close two bars back exceeds the close one bar back; otherwise initialize bearish. On each bar, calculate true range as the maximum of high−low, |high−previous close|, and |low−previous close|; calculate ATR as the weighted average of the current and preceding true ranges, with the greatest weight on the current bar. Multiply ATR by the threshold factor. In bullish state, switch to bearish if low falls below the trailing level minus the threshold; in bearish state, switch to bullish if high rises above the trailing level plus the threshold. While bullish, raise the trailing level to each higher low; while bearish, lower it to each lower high. Use the selected BrainTrend2 timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe itself); compare the selected signal bar with its immediately preceding bar, both completed.
Calculation: Double LWMA line = a linearly weighted moving average of the selected applied price, followed by a second linearly weighted moving average of that result using the same lookback. The applied price is selected from OHLC, median, typical, weighted close, open-close average, OHLC average, TrendFollow price, or DeMark price. Use the selected AbsolutelyNoLag LWMA timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe itself); compare the selected signal bar with its immediately preceding bar, both completed.
