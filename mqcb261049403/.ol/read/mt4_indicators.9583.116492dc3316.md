Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = four close-based EMAs with successively longer lookbacks, evaluated using the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Upper channel = highest high among the shorter-lookback chart-timeframe bars completed before the current bar, plus a buffer.
Calculation: Lower channel = lowest low among the same shorter-lookback chart-timeframe bars, minus a buffer.
Long: Shortest-lookback EMA > next EMA > next EMA > longest-lookback EMA AND close > upper channel
Short: Shortest-lookback EMA < next EMA < next EMA < longest-lookback EMA AND close < lower channel
