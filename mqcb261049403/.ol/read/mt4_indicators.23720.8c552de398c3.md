Calculation: Selected pivot levels = use the configured pivot formula on the prior completed bar of the configured pivot timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe): Standard: PP=(H+L+C)/3; R1=2PP−L; S1=2PP−H; R2=PP+H−L; S2=PP−H+L; R3=R1+H−L; S3=L−2(H−PP); R4=R3+R2−R1; S4=S3−(S1−S2); midpoint levels are the averages of adjacent levels. Fibonacci: PP=(H+L+C)/3; Rn=PP+k(H−L), Sn=PP−k(H−L), for k=0.382, 0.618, 0.786, 1, 1.382, 1.618, 2. Camarilla: range=H−L; R1–R4=C+(1.1/12, 1.1/6, 1.1/4, 1.1/2)×range; S1–S4=C−the same offsets; PP=(R4+S4)/2; R5=(H/L)×C; S5=2C−R5. Woodie: PP=(H+L+2×O)/4, where H, L, C are from the prior completed pivot-timeframe bar and O is the open of the still-forming bar on that timeframe; R1=2PP−L; R2=PP+H−L; S1=2PP−H; S2=PP−H+L.
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = upper and lower bands on the configured Bollinger timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using its still-forming bar.
Long: Price crosses upward through any enabled selected pivot level OR a completed bar on the configured bounce timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe) spans a support or pivot level and closes above it (when candle-color filtering is enabled, the bar must close above its open); if the Bollinger filter is enabled, price > upper Bollinger Band OR price < lower Bollinger Band.
Short: Price crosses downward through any enabled selected pivot level OR a completed bar on the configured bounce timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe) spans a resistance or pivot level and closes below it (when candle-color filtering is enabled, the bar must close below its open) OR price first crosses and closes above a resistance level and subsequently closes below it; if the Bollinger filter is enabled, price > upper Bollinger Band OR price < lower Bollinger Band.
