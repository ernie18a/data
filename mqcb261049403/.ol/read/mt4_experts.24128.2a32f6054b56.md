Calculation: Aroon Up = 100 × (lookback − bars since the highest high) ÷ lookback; Aroon Down = 100 × (lookback − bars since the lowest low) ÷ lookback, using completed chart-timeframe bars
Long: Aroon Up > Aroon Down on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND Aroon Up ≤ Aroon Down on the preceding completed bar AND Williams %R < −(100 − entry threshold)
Short: Aroon Up < Aroon Down on the last completed chart-timeframe bar AND Aroon Up ≥ Aroon Down on the preceding completed bar AND Williams %R > −entry threshold
Exit: long, Williams %R ≥ −close threshold
Exit: short, Williams %R ≤ −(100 − close threshold)
