Calculation: Fast and slow StepWPR lines = Normalize Williams %R to 0–100 by adding 100. For each line, use its own step size; the fast step size is shorter than the slow step size. On each bar, form candidate bounds WPR + 2 × step and WPR − 2 × step. Set the trend up if WPR exceeds the prior upper candidate bound, or down if WPR falls below the prior lower candidate bound. While trending up, do not let the lower bound fall below its prior value; while trending down, do not let the upper bound rise above its prior value. The line is the lower bound + step while up, and the upper bound − step while down. Initialize bounds to positive and negative infinity and trend to neutral. Calculate on chart-timeframe bars; the higher-timeframe version uses the same or a higher timeframe and, when higher, its still-forming bar.
Long: Fast StepWPR line > slow StepWPR line
Short: Fast StepWPR line <= slow StepWPR line
