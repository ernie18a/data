Calculation: TVI = EMA of length u of 100 × (double EMA of length s of up ticks − double EMA of length s of down ticks) / (double EMA of length s of up ticks + double EMA of length s of down ticks), where up ticks = (volume + (close − open) / price point) / 2 and down ticks = volume − up ticks; each double EMA applies an EMA of length r followed by an EMA of length s
Long: (TVI[selected bar] > TVI[previous bar] AND TVI[selected bar] < levelDOWN) OR (TVI[selected bar] > TVI[previous bar] AND TVI[previous bar] ≥ TVI[bar before previous]) OR (TVI[selected bar] > TVI[previous bar] AND TVI[previous bar] ≥ TVI[bar before previous] AND TVI[selected bar] < levelDOWN)
