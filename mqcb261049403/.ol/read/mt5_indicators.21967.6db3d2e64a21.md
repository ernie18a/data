Calculation: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI = apply the selected smoothing method to CCI multiplied by volume.
Calculation: Smoothed volume = apply the same smoothing method and parameters to volume.
Long: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI > upper level 1 × Smoothed volume OR Smoothed volume-weighted CCI > its value on the previous chart bar
Short: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI < lower level 1 × Smoothed volume OR Smoothed volume-weighted CCI < its value on the previous chart bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe smoothed volume-weighted CCI = the same calculation on a higher timeframe, sampled from its still-forming bar and held between higher-timeframe bar openings.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe smoothed volume = the same calculation on a higher timeframe, sampled from its still-forming bar and held between higher-timeframe bar openings.
Long: Higher-timeframe smoothed volume-weighted CCI > upper level 1 × Higher-timeframe smoothed volume OR Higher-timeframe smoothed volume-weighted CCI > its value on the previous chart bar
Short: Higher-timeframe smoothed volume-weighted CCI < lower level 1 × Higher-timeframe smoothed volume OR Higher-timeframe smoothed volume-weighted CCI < its value on the previous chart bar
