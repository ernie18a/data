Calculation: two-bar daily average range = average of high minus low for the last two completed daily bars on the chart timeframe
Calculation: initial stop distance = rounded two-bar daily average range minus the pipsing target and slippage
Calculation: pipsing target = at least one tenth of the rounded two-bar daily average range
Long: no open positions AND price reaches the current price plus the initial stop distance OR price reaches the entry price of an existing short position at its next larger leverage level
Short: no open positions AND price reaches the current price minus the initial stop distance OR price reaches the entry price of an existing long position at its next larger leverage level
Exit: long, price is more than the pipsing target plus slippage above the entry price
Exit: short, price is more than the pipsing target plus slippage below the entry price
