Calculation: JFATL = apply the fixed 39-tap FATL weighted-sum filter to the selected bar price, smooth it with a Jurik Moving Average, then round to the configured price increment.
Calculation: JFATL trend state = +1 when JFATL rises, -1 when it falls, otherwise carry the previous state.
Long: on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured), the last completed bar's JFATL trend state = +1 AND the preceding completed bar's state < +1 AND current time is within the configured trading interval.
Short: on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured), the last completed bar's JFATL trend state = -1 AND the preceding completed bar's state > -1 AND current time is within the configured trading interval.
Exit: long, on the configured indicator timeframe's last completed bar, JFATL trend state = -1 AND the preceding completed bar's state > -1 OR low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR current time is outside the configured trading interval.
Exit: short, on the configured indicator timeframe's last completed bar, JFATL trend state = +1 AND the preceding completed bar's state < +1 OR high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance OR current time is outside the configured trading interval.
