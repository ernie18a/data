Calculation: Fine-tuned candle open and close = For each OHLC price, compute a weighted average over the current bar and preceding bars. The configurable weight at lag i is proportional to [s1 + (i/(N−1))^r1(1−s1)] × [s2 + (1−i/(N−1))^r2(1−s2)] × [s3 + |1−2i/(N−1)|^r3(1−s3)], normalized so the weights sum to 1. Replace the calculated open with the preceding candle’s calculated close when the absolute difference between the raw bar’s open and close is within a configurable gap threshold. On the chart timeframe, use the current bar, which may still be forming. In the higher-timeframe version, calculate on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar, and carry that value across chart-timeframe bars until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Fine-tuned candle close > fine-tuned candle open
Short: Fine-tuned candle close < fine-tuned candle open
