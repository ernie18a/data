Calculation: Bullish AC signal = On a configured higher timeframe, using its current forming bar and three preceding completed bars, either (AC[1] > AC[2] AND AC[2] > AC[3] AND AC[0] < 0 AND AC[0] > AC[1]) OR (AC[0] > AC[1] AND AC[1] > AC[2] AND AC[0] > 0)
Calculation: Bearish AC signal = On a configured higher timeframe, using its current forming bar and three preceding completed bars, either (AC[1] < AC[2] AND AC[2] < AC[3] AND AC[0] > 0 AND AC[0] < AC[1]) OR (AC[0] < AC[1] AND AC[1] < AC[2] AND AC[0] < 0)
Calculation: Bullish score = 12.5 × (number of the five SMAs whose value on the current forming bar of this configured higher timeframe is greater than its value on the previous completed bar + 3 if the bullish AC signal is true, otherwise 0); the five SMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest
Calculation: Bearish score = 12.5 × (number of the five SMAs whose value on the current forming bar of this configured higher timeframe is less than its value on the previous completed bar + 3 if the bearish AC signal is true, otherwise 0); the five SMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest
Long: At least one of the five SMAs is rising on any configured higher timeframe OR the bullish AC signal is true on any configured higher timeframe OR the bullish score is greater than 50 on all three configured higher timeframes
Short: At least one of the five SMAs is falling on any configured higher timeframe OR the bearish AC signal is true on any configured higher timeframe OR the bearish score is greater than 50 on all three configured higher timeframes
