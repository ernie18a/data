Calculation: WAMI = Apply a 1-bar simple moving average to the configured applied price, take its change from the prior bar, then smooth that change successively with the configured first, second, and third moving averages; use the third result on the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Signal line = Smooth the third moving-average result with the configured signal moving average on the same higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: WAMI > Signal line
Short: WAMI < Signal line
