Calculation: Chaikin Volatility Stochastic = 100 × the linearly weighted moving average of the normalized smoothed high-low range: (current smoothed range − lowest smoothed range over the stochastic lookback) ÷ (highest − lowest over that lookback). The range is smoothed high − low; the weighted average uses a shorter lookback than the range normalization. On the higher timeframe, calculate from its still-forming bar and carry the sampled value forward on chart bars until the next higher-timeframe boundary.
Long: chart-timeframe Chaikin Volatility Stochastic > its previous chart-bar value OR higher-timeframe Chaikin Volatility Stochastic > its previous higher-timeframe value
Short: chart-timeframe Chaikin Volatility Stochastic < its previous chart-bar value OR higher-timeframe Chaikin Volatility Stochastic < its previous higher-timeframe value
