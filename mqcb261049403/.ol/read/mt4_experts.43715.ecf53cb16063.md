Calculation: Average volume = sum of volume over the last 50 completed chart-timeframe bars divided by 49
Long: On a new chart-timeframe bar, the forming bar’s close < the last completed bar’s close AND the forming bar’s CCI < −level AND the last completed bar’s volume < average volume
Short: On a new chart-timeframe bar, the forming bar’s close > the last completed bar’s close AND the forming bar’s CCI > level AND the last completed bar’s volume < average volume
Exit: long, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop-loss multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × take-profit multiple
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop-loss multiple OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry × take-profit multiple
