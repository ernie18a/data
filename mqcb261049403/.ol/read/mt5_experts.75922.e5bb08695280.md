Calculation: Previous-day high and low = high and low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart).
Calculation: Daily zones = for each strategy, zone half-width = previous-day high × that strategy’s zone-width parameter ÷ 200; high zone = previous-day high ± half-width; low zone = previous-day low ± half-width.
Calculation: Yesterday-range filter = yesterday’s high-low range is between the configured minimum and maximum percentages of the arithmetic mean of daily high-low ranges over the configured lookback, using completed daily bars (higher timeframe than the chart).
Long: Yesterday-range filter passes AND (last completed signal-timeframe close (higher timeframe than the chart) is within the high zone AND the most recent earlier completed signal-timeframe close outside that zone is above it AND a high from that close through the intervening completed signal-timeframe bars reached at least high-zone top × (1 + breakout-distance parameter) within the preceding one day of signal-timeframe bars OR, since the start of the current day, a completed signal-timeframe close was at or below the low-zone top and a later completed signal-timeframe close reached at least high-zone top × (1 + breakout-distance parameter) OR, since the start of the current day, a completed signal-timeframe close was at or below the low-zone top, a later close reached at least low-zone top × (1 + pullback-distance parameter), and a still later close was within the low zone OR a long position exists and the bar low is at or below the last long entry price × (1 − grid-step parameter)).
Short: Yesterday-range filter passes AND (last completed signal-timeframe close (higher timeframe than the chart) is within the low zone AND the most recent earlier completed signal-timeframe close outside that zone is below it AND a low from that close through the intervening completed signal-timeframe bars reached at most low-zone bottom × (1 − breakout-distance parameter) within the preceding one day of signal-timeframe bars OR, since the start of the current day, a completed signal-timeframe close was at or above the high-zone bottom and a later completed signal-timeframe close reached at most low-zone bottom × (1 − breakout-distance parameter) OR, since the start of the current day, a completed signal-timeframe close was at or above the high-zone bottom, a later close reached at most high-zone bottom × (1 − pullback-distance parameter), and a still later close was within the high zone OR a short position exists and the bar high is at or above the last short entry price × (1 + grid-step parameter)).
Exit: Long, bar low ≤ weighted average long entry price × (1 − stop-loss parameter) OR bar high ≥ weighted average long entry price × (1 + take-profit parameter)
Exit: Short, bar high ≥ weighted average short entry price × (1 + stop-loss parameter) OR bar low ≤ weighted average short entry price × (1 − take-profit parameter)
