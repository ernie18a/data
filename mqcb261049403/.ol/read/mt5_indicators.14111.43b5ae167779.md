Calculation: BullsBearsEyes = 100 × CU/(CU+CD), or 0 when CU+CD=0; for each bar, let P be the selected-period EMA of close, Bulls Power = high−P, and Bears Power = low−P. L0=(1−gamma)×(Bears Power+Bulls Power)+gamma×previous L0; L1=−gamma×L0+previous L0+gamma×previous L1; L2=−gamma×L1+previous L1+gamma×previous L2; L3=−gamma×L2+previous L2+gamma×previous L3. CU=sum of max(0, L0−L1), max(0, L1−L2), max(0, L2−L3); CD=sum of max(0, L1−L0), max(0, L2−L1), max(0, L3−L2).
