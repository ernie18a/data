Calculation: VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price ((high + low + close) / 3), reset at the selected daily, weekly, or monthly anchor; use chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Upper/lower bands = VWAP plus/minus the configured multiplier times the standard deviation of chart-bar typical prices from the current VWAP within the current anchor period, including the bar still forming
Long: close < VWAP OR close <= lower band
Short: close > VWAP OR close >= upper band
