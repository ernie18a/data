Calculation: Fast-smoothed MFI = MFI smoothed with a shorter lookback; calculated on chart-timeframe bars or, when using the higher-timeframe variant, on its still-forming bars.
Calculation: Slow-smoothed MFI = Fast-smoothed MFI smoothed with a longer lookback than the fast smoothing.
Long: Fast-smoothed MFI > Slow-smoothed MFI
Short: Fast-smoothed MFI < Slow-smoothed MFI
