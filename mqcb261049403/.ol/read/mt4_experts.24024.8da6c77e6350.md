Calculation: Fast and slow MACD lines = MACD main line with shorter and longer fast/slow lookbacks, respectively; both use the same signal lookback. Evaluate on the chart timeframe using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Fast and slow weighted moving averages = linearly weighted moving averages of typical price, with shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively. Evaluate on the chart timeframe using the bar still forming.
Calculation: Monthly RSI = RSI of monthly closing prices. Compare the bar still forming with the last completed monthly bar.
Long: abs(current bid - close from two chart bars ago) > retracement distance AND fast MACD > 0 AND slow MACD > 0 AND fast weighted moving average < slow weighted moving average AND low from two chart bars ago < high of the last completed chart bar AND monthly RSI on the forming bar > monthly RSI on the last completed bar
Short: abs(current ask - close of the last completed chart bar) > retracement distance AND fast MACD < 0 AND slow MACD < 0 AND fast weighted moving average < slow weighted moving average AND low of the last completed chart bar < high from two chart bars ago AND monthly RSI on the forming bar < monthly RSI on the last completed bar
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR (favorable price move from entry > break-even trigger distance AND low <= entry price + break-even offset) OR low <= lowest low across the forming chart bar and the preceding trailing-lookback bars minus trailing padding
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (favorable price move from entry > break-even trigger distance AND high >= entry price - break-even offset) OR high >= highest high across the trailing-lookback bars beginning two chart bars before the forming bar plus trailing padding
