Calculation: Median-price simple moving averages = simple moving average of (high + low)/2; compare a shorter lookback with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Stochastic main and signal lines = stochastic oscillator main and signal lines; compare values on the stated bars
Calculation: Adaptive step oscillator = 10 times the normalized difference between the adaptive minimum and midpoint step lines, with step sizes derived from weighted recent high-low ranges
Calculation: RSI of EMA changes = RSI calculated from changes in a short-period close-price EMA; compare its latest value with its prior value
Calculation: Fast and slow close-price simple moving averages = simple moving averages with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Short and long close-price exponential moving averages = exponential moving averages with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: Short close-price linear-weighted moving average = linear-weighted moving average using the short EMA lookback
Calculation: Typical-price simple moving average = simple moving average of (high + low + close)/3
Calculation: Smoothed median-price moving average = smoothed moving average of (high + low)/2
Calculation: AO = 5-period simple moving average of median price minus 34-period simple moving average of median price
Calculation: Adaptive AO thresholds = 0.7 times the maximum and minimum AO values over the available history
Calculation: RSI-change crossover = whether the fast close-price simple moving average crossed the slow one between the last two completed chart bars
