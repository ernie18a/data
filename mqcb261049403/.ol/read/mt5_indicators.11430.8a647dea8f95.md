Calculation: AMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of the selected price
Calculation: WMA = linearly weighted moving average of the selected price
Long: On the chart timeframe, or on a higher timeframe using its current forming bar and prior completed bar, previous-bar AMA > previous-bar WMA AND current-bar AMA < current-bar WMA
Short: On the chart timeframe, or on a higher timeframe using its current forming bar and prior completed bar, previous-bar AMA < previous-bar WMA AND current-bar AMA > current-bar WMA
