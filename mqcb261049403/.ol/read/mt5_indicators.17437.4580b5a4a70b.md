Calculation: Smoothed momentum = the selected bar price minus that price from the lookback bars ago, smoothed over the shorter lookback and multiplied by that lookback.
Calculation: TDI = absolute smoothed momentum minus (twice the longer-lookback smoothed absolute momentum minus the current absolute momentum); the longer lookback is twice the shorter lookback. Both calculations use the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Long: Higher-timeframe TDI > higher-timeframe smoothed momentum
Short: Higher-timeframe TDI < higher-timeframe smoothed momentum
