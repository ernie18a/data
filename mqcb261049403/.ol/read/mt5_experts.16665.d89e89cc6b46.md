Calculation: X2MA = Apply a configurable moving average to the applied price, then apply a second configurable moving average to that result.
Calculation: JFATL = Apply JMA smoothing to the 39-bar FATL weighted sum of the applied price.
Long: X2MA is rising on the last completed higher-than-chart trend bar AND JFATL is rising on the last completed lower-than-chart entry bar AND JFATL was not rising on the preceding completed entry bar.
Short: X2MA is falling on the last completed higher-than-chart trend bar AND JFATL is falling on the last completed lower-than-chart entry bar AND JFATL was not falling on the preceding completed entry bar.
Exit: long, X2MA is falling on the last completed higher-than-chart trend bar OR JFATL is rising on the last completed lower-than-chart entry bar.
Exit: short, X2MA is rising on the last completed higher-than-chart trend bar OR JFATL is falling on the last completed lower-than-chart entry bar.
