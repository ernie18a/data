Calculation: MACD divergence components = On a higher timeframe, index bars backward from the bar still forming as index 0. MACD is the close-based short-period EMA minus the long-period EMA, rounded to six decimal places. For a bullish case use lows, a negative threshold, and minima; for a bearish case use highs, a positive threshold, and maxima. The recent MACD extreme is at index 2: indices 1 through 4 must all be beyond the threshold, and index 2 must be strictly more extreme than indices 1, 3, and 4. The recent price extreme is the first index from 0 through 15 whose price is strictly more extreme than each of the next four older prices; use zero if none is found. Scan older MACD candidates in increasing index i, starting where i > 10: indices i through i-4 must all be beyond the threshold, and index i-2 must be strictly more extreme than the other four. Accept the first candidate and then only candidates strictly more extreme than the preceding accepted candidate; stop at the first candidate that fails this progression. For each accepted candidate, use the first price extreme from indices i-4 through i+15, defined by comparison with the next four older prices; retain the previous candidate's price extreme, initially zero, if none is found. Compare at most the first 18 accepted candidates.
Long: On a new completed higher-timeframe MACD bar, the recent MACD minimum and an accepted older MACD minimum exist AND ((older MACD > recent MACD AND older price low < recent price low) OR (older MACD < recent MACD AND older price low > recent price low)) AND the last completed higher-timeframe stochastic main line > its signal line AND the stochastic main line on the preceding higher-timeframe bar is between the lower and upper long thresholds inclusive.
Short: On a new completed higher-timeframe MACD bar, the recent MACD maximum and an accepted older MACD maximum exist AND ((older MACD > recent MACD AND older price high < recent price high) OR (older MACD < recent MACD AND older price high > recent price high)) AND the last completed higher-timeframe stochastic main line < its signal line AND the stochastic main line on the preceding higher-timeframe bar is between the lower and upper short thresholds inclusive.
Long: The long divergence condition above AND the last completed higher-timeframe short-period EMA of close > the long-period EMA of close AND the last completed higher-timeframe close on the MACD timeframe < the last completed EMA on the longer EMA timeframe, using the same short EMA period.
Short: The short divergence condition above AND the last completed higher-timeframe short-period EMA of close < the long-period EMA of close AND the last completed higher-timeframe close on the MACD timeframe > the last completed EMA on the longer EMA timeframe, using the same short EMA period.
Exit: long, last completed higher-timeframe RSI of close >= upper RSI threshold OR low <= entry price - fixed stop distance OR high >= entry price + fixed profit distance.
Exit: short, last completed higher-timeframe RSI of close <= lower RSI threshold OR high >= entry price + fixed stop distance OR low <= entry price - fixed profit distance.
