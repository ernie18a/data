Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively greater/less than the highs/lows of the depth more-recent bars and at least as great/less than those of the depth older bars; use fully confirmed bars on analysis timeframe 1, which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Active swing = latest leg between alternating confirmed swing points; bullish if its newest point is a high, bearish if a low. Previous swing = immediately preceding leg. Use the last completed bar’s close on analysis timeframe 1.
Long: Active swing is bullish OR (previous swing unavailable AND active swing is bullish) OR (active swing high > previous swing high AND active swing low > previous swing low AND close > active swing high) OR (active swing high < previous swing high AND active swing low < previous swing low AND close > active swing high) OR (NOT (active swing high > previous swing high AND active swing low > previous swing low) AND NOT (active swing high < previous swing high AND active swing low < previous swing low) AND close > active swing high)
Short: Active swing is bearish OR (active swing high > previous swing high AND active swing low > previous swing low AND close < active swing low) OR (active swing high < previous swing high AND active swing low < previous swing low AND close <= active swing high) OR (NOT (active swing high > previous swing high AND active swing low > previous swing low) AND NOT (active swing high < previous swing high AND active swing low < previous swing low) AND close < active swing low)
