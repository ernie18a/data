Calculation: Fast VAMA = sum(applied price × volume) / sum(volume) over a shorter lookback than the slow VAMA
Calculation: Slow VAMA = sum(applied price × volume) / sum(volume) over a longer lookback than the fast VAMA
Calculation: MACD = fast VAMA − slow VAMA
Calculation: Signal line = sum(MACD × volume) / sum(volume) over the signal lookback
Long: MACD > Signal line
Short: MACD < Signal line
