Calculation: Support = lowest low over the last N completed bars on the configured analysis timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe)
Calculation: Resistance = highest high over the last N completed bars on the configured analysis timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe)
Long: Open of the still-forming analysis-timeframe bar > EMA of close on that timeframe AND close > Resistance
Short: Open of the still-forming analysis-timeframe bar < EMA of close on that timeframe AND close < Support
Exit: Long, close < Support AND close > entry price
Exit: Short, close > Resistance AND close < entry price
