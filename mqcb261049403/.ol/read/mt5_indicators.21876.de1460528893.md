Calculation: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI = apply the selected volume (tick or real) to the CCI of the selected price on each chart-timeframe bar, then smooth the product with the selected moving-average method and smoothing parameters.
Calculation: Smoothed volume = smooth the selected volume (tick or real) on each chart-timeframe bar using the same moving-average method and smoothing parameters.
Calculation: Dynamic overbought and oversold levels = the configured level multipliers times smoothed volume.
Long: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI < dynamic oversold level 1
Short: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI > dynamic overbought level 1
