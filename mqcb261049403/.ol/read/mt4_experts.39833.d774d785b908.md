Calculation: Long SMMA = smoothed moving average of median price with the longer of the two MA lookbacks, on a lower timeframe than the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming and an older completed bar for its change.
Calculation: Short SMMA = smoothed moving average of median price with the shorter of the two MA lookbacks, on a lower timeframe than the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming and an older completed bar for its change.
Calculation: Long SMMA change = Long SMMA on the forming lower-timeframe bar − Long SMMA on an older completed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: ADX and +DI/−DI = ADX main and directional indicators calculated from median price on the chart timeframe; compare the forming bar with an older completed bar using the same lookback.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Long: ADX main ≥ threshold AND ((|Long SMMA change| is between a minimum and maximum threshold AND |chart close − Long SMMA| > a threshold AND Long SMMA < Short SMMA AND Short SMMA is rising AND chart close < Short SMMA) OR (Long SMMA < Short SMMA AND current +DI > threshold AND older +DI < older −DI AND chart close < Short SMMA) OR (|Long SMMA change| > the maximum threshold AND Long SMMA > Short SMMA AND Short SMMA is falling) OR (current bar range > a threshold times the previous bar range AND open < close AND close < (high + low) / 2)) AND no existing short position AND no existing long position within a threshold times ATR of the current price.
Short: ADX main ≥ threshold AND ((|Long SMMA change| is between a minimum and maximum threshold AND |chart close − Long SMMA| > a threshold AND Long SMMA > Short SMMA AND Short SMMA is falling AND chart close > Short SMMA) OR (Long SMMA > Short SMMA AND current −DI > threshold AND older −DI < current −DI AND chart close > Short SMMA) OR (|Long SMMA change| > the maximum threshold AND Long SMMA < Short SMMA AND Short SMMA is rising)).
