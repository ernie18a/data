Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close prices; evaluate on completed chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: ATR = average true range; evaluate on the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Short: RSI on the preceding completed bar < upper threshold AND RSI on the latest completed bar >= upper threshold AND signal bar hour >= session start AND signal bar hour < session end
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop ATR multiple × ATR recorded at entry OR low <= entry price − take-profit ATR multiple × ATR recorded at entry OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars
