Calculation: Bullish setup = fast moving average crosses from below to above the slow moving average AND the RSI filter is disabled or RSI > 50; its confirmation level is the crossover bar’s low, and it triggers when current high − confirmation level ≥ confirmation threshold. A zero confirmation threshold triggers on the crossover bar.
Calculation: Bearish setup = fast moving average crosses from above to below the slow moving average AND the RSI filter is disabled or RSI < 50; its confirmation level is the crossover bar’s high, and it triggers when confirmation level − current low ≥ confirmation threshold AND current open ≥ current close. A zero confirmation threshold triggers on the crossover bar if the open/close condition holds.
Calculation: Long anchor = low of the most recent bar on which a bullish setup triggered; initialize to 0. Short anchor = high of the most recent bar on which a bearish setup triggered; initialize to 0. Update the corresponding anchor whenever that setup triggers, even if no signal is marked.
Calculation: Trend state = direction of the most recently triggered setup; initialize neutral. Each triggered setup updates the state to its direction, even if no signal is marked.
Long: Bullish setup triggers AND (current low ≤ long anchor − flat threshold OR current low ≥ long anchor + flat threshold) AND prior trend state is neutral or short.
Short: Bearish setup triggers AND (current high ≤ short anchor − flat threshold OR current high ≥ short anchor + flat threshold) AND prior trend state is neutral or long.
