Calculation: Bullish Flat Trend mark = FlatTrend V2 light-green or lime mark; bearish mark = salmon or red mark. Trigger marks use the chart timeframe; filter marks use higher timeframes. All marks are from completed bars: current means the last completed bar, previous means the preceding completed bar.
Calculation: ADX = Average Directional Index on the chart timeframe, calculated from close prices.
Calculation: +DI and -DI = positive and negative Directional Indicators on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Juice = close-price standard deviation using an EMA and the selected lookback, minus the selected level multiplied by price-point size.
Long: no open position AND Juice > 0 AND current ADX >= previous ADX AND current ADX >= its threshold AND current +DI >= current -DI AND current +DI >= previous +DI AND (((previous trigger mark is bearish) AND (current trigger mark is bullish) AND (current first-filter mark is bullish) AND (current second-filter mark is lime)) OR ((current trigger mark is bullish) AND (current first-filter mark is bullish) AND (previous second-filter mark is bearish) AND (current second-filter mark is bullish)) OR ((current trigger mark is bullish) AND (previous first-filter mark is bearish) AND (current first-filter mark is bullish) AND (current second-filter mark is bullish)))
Short: no open position AND Juice > 0 AND current ADX >= previous ADX AND current ADX >= its threshold AND current -DI >= current +DI AND current -DI >= previous -DI AND (((previous trigger mark is bullish) AND (current trigger mark is bearish) AND (current first-filter mark is bearish) AND (current second-filter mark is bearish)) OR ((current trigger mark is bearish) AND (current first-filter mark is bearish) AND (previous second-filter mark is bullish) AND (current second-filter mark is bearish)) OR ((current trigger mark is bearish) AND (previous first-filter mark is bullish) AND (current first-filter mark is bearish) AND (current second-filter mark is bearish)))
Exit: long, current trigger mark is red AND previous trigger mark is lime.
Exit: short, current trigger mark is lime AND previous trigger mark is red.
