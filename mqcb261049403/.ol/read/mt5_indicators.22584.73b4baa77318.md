Calculation: Volume-weighted smoothed Bulls/Bears oscillator = apply the selected moving average to volume × (100 × CU/(CU+CD) − 50), using 0 for the ratio when CU+CD=0; CU is the sum of positive differences across L0−L1, L1−L2, and L2−L3, and CD is the sum of the absolute negative differences. L0=(1−γ)×(Bulls Power+Bears Power)+γ×prior L0; L1=−γ×L0+prior L0+γ×prior L1; L2=−γ×L1+prior L1+γ×prior L2; L3=−γ×L2+prior L2+γ×prior L3. Bulls Power is high minus the exponential moving average of close; Bears Power is low minus that average. Use the selected volume series and the same configured moving-average method and length as the oscillator. 
Long: Volume-weighted smoothed Bulls/Bears oscillator > its value on the prior chart bar
Short: Volume-weighted smoothed Bulls/Bears oscillator < its value on the prior chart bar
