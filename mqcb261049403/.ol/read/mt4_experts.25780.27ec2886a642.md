Long: (14-period Money Flow Index on the higher timeframe is <= 30 on any of the last 3 completed bars) AND (short-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming, > long-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming) AND (low 2 chart bars ago < high 1 chart bar ago) AND (absolute difference between current chart bar open and close 2 chart bars ago > retracement distance)
Short: (14-period Money Flow Index on the higher timeframe is >= 70 on any of the last 3 completed bars) AND (short-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming, < long-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming) AND (low 1 chart bar ago < high 2 chart bars ago) AND (absolute difference between current chart bar open and close 1 chart bar ago > retracement distance)
Exit: long, price <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance OR price <= active long stop
Exit: short, price >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price - take-profit distance OR price >= active short stop
