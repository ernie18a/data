Short: position side = flat AND Ichimoku Kijun-sen < Senkou Span A < Senkou Span B < Chikou Span AND current close on the Ichimoku timeframe < Kijun-sen AND previous +DI on the configured ADX timeframe < the lower threshold AND current +DI on that timeframe > the higher threshold AND |Kijun-sen − Tenkan-sen| / minimum price point > the configured separation threshold
Exit: long, bar low ≤ entry price − configured stop-loss distance OR bar high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: short, bar high ≥ entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR bar low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit distance
Calculation lines: The Ichimoku values and close use the still-forming bar on the separate Ichimoku timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe depending on configuration. ADX +DI uses the current still-forming bar and the previous completed bar on its configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe depending on configuration. The lower and higher thresholds are distinct, with the former lower than the latter.
