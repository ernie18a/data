Calculation: Heikin-Ashi open = prior Heikin-Ashi open and close average; Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4. Initialize the oldest required Heikin-Ashi open to that bar’s open.
Calculation: TTM-Trend state = for each bar, classify as long-signal if (Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open AND open <= close) OR (Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open AND open > close); classify as short-signal if (Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open AND open <= close) OR (Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open AND open > close); otherwise neutral. If both Heikin-Ashi open and close lie within the high-low range of any of the preceding lookback bars’ Heikin-Ashi open and close, carry forward that preceding bar’s state instead. Use bars from the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; evaluate the last completed bar and compare it with the preceding completed bar.
Long: (last completed TTM-Trend state is long-signal AND preceding completed state is not long-signal AND no position is open) OR (long position is open AND number of additions is below its limit AND current price minus the last deal price exceeds the addition spacing)
Short: (last completed TTM-Trend state is short-signal AND preceding completed state is not short-signal AND no position is open) OR (short position is open AND number of additions is below its limit AND last deal price minus current price exceeds the addition spacing)
Exit: Long position, last completed TTM-Trend state is short-signal OR price reaches the stop-loss or take-profit distance from the applicable entry price
Exit: Short position, last completed TTM-Trend state is long-signal OR price reaches the stop-loss or take-profit distance from the applicable entry price
