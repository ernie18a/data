Calculation: Daily percentage change of a currency pair = (current price − open of its higher-timeframe bar still forming) / that bar’s open × 100, rounded to 2 decimals
Calculation: Currency strength = signed sum of the pair daily percentage changes listed below, divided by the stated count, rounded to 2 decimals: EUR = (EURJPY + EURCAD + EURGBP + EURCHF + EURAUD + EURUSD + EURNZD) / 7; USD = (USDJPY + USDCAD − AUDUSD + USDCHF − GBPUSD − EURUSD − NZDUSD) / 7; JPY = −(USDJPY + EURJPY + AUDJPY + CHFJPY + GBPJPY + CADJPY + NZDJPY) / 7; CAD = (CADCHF + CADJPY − GBPCAD − AUDCAD − EURCAD − USDCAD) / 6; AUD = (AUDUSD + AUDNZD + AUDCAD + AUDCHF + AUDJPY − EURAUD − GBPAUD) / 7; NZD = (NZDUSD + NZDJPY − EURNZD − AUDNZD − GBPNZD) / 5; GBP = (GBPUSD − EURGBP + GBPCHF + GBPAUD + GBPCAD + GBPJPY + GBPNZD) / 7; CHF = (CHFJPY − USDCHF − EURCHF − AUDCHF − GBPCHF − CADCHF) / 6
Long: absolute difference between base-currency strength and quote-currency strength exceeds the configured threshold AND base-currency strength exceeds quote-currency strength AND current price exceeds the open of its higher-timeframe bar still forming
Short: absolute difference between base-currency strength and quote-currency strength exceeds the configured threshold AND base-currency strength is below quote-currency strength AND current price is below the open of its higher-timeframe bar still forming
Exit: long, price reaches the configured take-profit level above entry price OR the configured stop-loss level below entry price
Exit: short, price reaches the configured take-profit level below entry price OR the configured stop-loss level above entry price
