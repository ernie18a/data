Calculation: ATR-based trend boundaries = On the chart timeframe, or on a selected higher timeframe using its still-forming bar, calculate ATR and the rolling maximum and minimum ATR over the same lookback; set step to sensitivity × their average. Each bar’s bullish candidate floor is High − 2 × step and bearish candidate ceiling is Low + 2 × step (or Close ± 2 × step when using close mode). Switch bullish when Close exceeds the prior bearish ceiling and bearish when Close falls below the prior bullish floor; while bullish, ratchet the floor upward with the maximum of its candidate and prior floor, and while bearish, ratchet the ceiling downward with the minimum of its candidate and prior ceiling.
Long: prior bar has an active bearish ceiling AND current bar has an active bullish floor
Short: prior bar has an active bullish floor AND current bar has an active bearish ceiling
