Calculation: Market phase = trending when the absolute difference between the 10-period and longer-period EMAs on the chart timeframe exceeds a threshold and current ATR exceeds twice the average of current and previous two ATR values; otherwise ranging.
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when current price exceeds the higher-timeframe EMA by a threshold, bearish when it is below by a threshold; otherwise use the EMA change over the current and second-previous higher-timeframe bars if it exceeds a slope threshold, and neutral otherwise. Use the higher timeframe’s bar still forming.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement = highest high minus (highest high minus lowest low) multiplied by the retracement fraction, using the configured lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Strong support and resistance = swing lows and highs, respectively, found using three bars on either side within the configured chart-timeframe lookback; merge levels within the configured zone buffer, score by touch count and age, and retain the strongest levels. A qualifying level meets the configured minimum strength.
Calculation: Parallel regression channel = linear-regression lines fitted separately to highs and lows over the same chart-timeframe lookback; valid when both absolute correlations exceed the configured threshold and the slope difference is below a threshold. The lines’ intercepts are channel resistance and support.
Calculation: Breakout confirmation = after a qualifying resistance break or support breakdown, price remains beyond the level by at least half the minimum breakout distance for the configured number of chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Market phase is ranging AND valid channel AND price is within the configured zone buffer of channel support AND RSI < 40
Long: Market phase is trending AND trend direction is bullish AND price is within the configured zone buffer of a qualifying support AND RSI < 40
Long: A qualifying resistance breakout has distance between the configured minimum and maximum breakout distances AND momentum score meets its configured threshold AND volume exceeds its configured multiple of the average over the preceding lookback AND breakout confidence meets its configured threshold AND breakout confirmation holds AND trend is not bearish AND ATR meets the minimum volatility threshold AND (RSI filtering is disabled OR RSI <= 80) AND current hour is outside the excluded overnight interval
Long: Simple-entry option is enabled AND trend direction is bullish AND ATR is below its configured maximum
Long: Enhanced-entry option is disabled AND market phase is not trending AND trend direction is bullish AND price is within the configured entry distance of the 61.8% Fibonacci retracement AND (RSI filtering is disabled OR RSI < 40)
Short: Market phase is ranging AND valid channel AND price is within the configured zone buffer of channel resistance AND RSI > 60
Short: Market phase is trending AND trend direction is bearish AND price is within the configured zone buffer of a qualifying resistance AND RSI > 60
Short: A qualifying support breakdown has distance between the configured minimum and maximum breakout distances AND momentum score meets its configured threshold AND volume exceeds its configured multiple of the average over the preceding lookback AND breakout confidence meets its configured threshold AND breakout confirmation holds AND trend is not bullish AND ATR meets the minimum volatility threshold AND (RSI filtering is disabled OR RSI >= 20) AND current hour is outside the excluded overnight interval
Short: Simple-entry option is enabled AND trend direction is bearish AND ATR is below its configured maximum
Short: Enhanced-entry option is disabled AND market phase is not trending AND trend direction is bearish AND price is within the configured entry distance of the 61.8% Fibonacci retracement AND (RSI filtering is disabled OR RSI > 60)
Exit: Long, price reaches the configured take-profit distance above the basket average entry price OR trailing is enabled AND favorable price has reached the breakeven trigger AND price falls to or below the trailing level, which is the greater of the basket average entry price and the best price since entry minus the configured trailing distance
Exit: Short, price reaches the configured take-profit distance below the basket average entry price OR trailing is enabled AND favorable price has reached the breakeven trigger AND price rises to or above the trailing level, which is the lesser of the basket average entry price and the best price since entry plus the configured trailing distance
