Calculation: Adaptive Renko direction = On the configurable indicator timeframe (which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe), choose each bar’s high and low from either its high/low or its close; initialize upper and lower rails to the chosen price and brick size to max(multiplier × initial high-low range, minimum brick size). On each bar, set volatility brick size to max(multiplier × ATR or standard deviation, minimum brick size). If the chosen high exceeds upper rail + current brick size, raise the upper rail by floor((high − upper rail) / current brick size) × current brick size, then set brick size to volatility brick size and lower rail to upper rail − brick size. Next, if the chosen low is below lower rail − current brick size, lower the lower rail by floor((lower rail − low) / current brick size) × current brick size, then set brick size to volatility brick size and upper rail to lower rail + brick size. Set direction bullish if the upper rail rose from the preceding chronological bar; otherwise set it bearish if the lower rail fell; otherwise retain the previous direction. The initial direction is neutral. Evaluate the selected signal bar at its configurable shift (shift zero is still forming; positive shifts are completed); its preceding chronological bar is one bar older.
Long: Adaptive Renko direction on the selected signal bar is bullish AND Adaptive Renko direction on the preceding chronological bar is bearish
Short: Adaptive Renko direction on the selected signal bar is bearish AND Adaptive Renko direction on the preceding chronological bar is bullish
Exit: long, Adaptive Renko direction on the selected signal bar is bearish OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, Adaptive Renko direction on the selected signal bar is bullish OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
