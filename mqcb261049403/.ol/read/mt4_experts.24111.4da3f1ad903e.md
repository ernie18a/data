Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = linear weighted moving averages of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar; the fast average has the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Short EMA group = exponential moving averages of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar, with shorter lookbacks than the long EMA group.
Calculation: Long EMA group = exponential moving averages of typical price on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar, with longer lookbacks than the short EMA group.
Long: ADX on the chart timeframe, last completed bar > threshold AND +DI on the chart timeframe, last completed bar < -DI on the chart timeframe, last completed bar AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND low two bars ago < high one bar ago AND absolute difference between current price and close two bars ago > distance threshold AND (Strategy 1 selected OR (Strategy 2 selected AND close one bar ago >= lower Bollinger Band AND slow stochastic >= threshold AND fast stochastic >= threshold)).
Short: ADX on the chart timeframe, last completed bar > threshold AND +DI on the chart timeframe, last completed bar < -DI on the chart timeframe, last completed bar AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND low one bar ago < high two bars ago AND absolute difference between current price and close one bar ago > distance threshold AND (Strategy 1 selected OR (Strategy 2 selected AND close one bar ago <= upper Bollinger Band AND slow stochastic >= threshold AND fast stochastic <= threshold)).
Exit: long, price <= entry price minus stop distance OR price >= entry price plus take-profit distance OR all EMAs in the short EMA group are equal OR all EMAs in the long EMA group are equal OR close one bar ago <= lower Bollinger Band.
Exit: short, price >= entry price plus stop distance OR price <= entry price minus take-profit distance OR all EMAs in the short EMA group are equal OR all EMAs in the long EMA group are equal OR close one bar ago <= lower Bollinger Band.
