Calculation: Center line = triangular-weighted average of the applied price over a symmetric window of 2H+1 bars, with weight H+1 at the center and weights decreasing by 1 per bar toward either edge; use the available bars at history edges. Channel timeframe = chart timeframe or a selectable higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe, use the mapped bar, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Upper band = center line + deviation multiplier × square root of upper variance; lower band = center line − deviation multiplier × square root of lower variance. For each bar, let d = applied price − center line and N = 2H+1. Seed the variance on the oldest available bar with d² on the matching side and 0 on the other; thereafter update the matching side as (prior variance × (N−1) + d²)/N and the other side as prior variance × (N−1)/N.
Long: Previous bar low < its lower band AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND current bar close > current bar open AND (upper band − lower band)/abs(center line) × 100 ≥ minimum band width percentage when the width filter is enabled AND previous bar tick volume ≥ average tick volume over the preceding lookback × volume multiplier when the volume filter is enabled AND no long or short signal occurred within the configured preceding cooldown bars when cooldown is enabled.
