Calculation: Currency strength = mean of seven RSI readings for that currency against the other seven currencies; use RSI for the currency when it is the pair base and 100 − RSI when it is the quote, on the chart timeframe using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on the chart timeframe using the bar still forming; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Long: The traded pair’s base currency has the highest strength among the eight currencies AND its quote currency has the lowest strength among the eight currencies AND the absolute difference between the current bar close and prior bar close exceeds the retracement distance AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND low two bars ago < high one bar ago.
Short: The traded pair’s base currency has the lowest strength among the eight currencies AND its quote currency has the highest strength among the eight currencies AND the absolute difference between the current bar close and prior bar close exceeds the retracement distance AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND low one bar ago < high two bars ago.
Exit: Long, price ≤ entry price − stop distance OR price ≥ entry price + target distance OR price ≤ entry price + break-even offset after price has risen above entry price + break-even trigger.
Exit: Short, price ≥ entry price + stop distance OR price ≤ entry price − target distance OR price ≥ entry price − break-even offset after price has fallen below entry price − break-even trigger.
