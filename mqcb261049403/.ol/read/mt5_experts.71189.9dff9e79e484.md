Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA AND abs(fast EMA - slow EMA) >= ATR × trend-strength multiplier AND latest completed close < both EMAs AND latest completed close < breakout low + ATR × breakout-buffer multiplier AND previous completed close >= breakout low AND RSI is within the configured short range AND latest completed close < previous completed close AND (higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA, when enabled) AND entry time is within the configured weekday session and before the configured Friday cutoff
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = fast and slow EMAs calculated from the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
