Long: fast moving average > slow moving average AND within the configured trading schedule AND long trading is enabled AND (no open long position OR averaging up is enabled AND current price > highest long entry price × (1 + averaging step) OR averaging down is enabled AND current price < lowest long entry price × (1 − averaging step × averaging-down multiplier))
Short: fast moving average < slow moving average AND within the configured trading schedule AND short trading is enabled AND (no open short position OR averaging up is enabled AND current price < lowest short entry price × (1 − averaging step) OR averaging down is enabled AND current price > highest short entry price × (1 + averaging step × averaging-down multiplier))
Exit: long, fast moving average < slow moving average when close-all-at-crossover is enabled OR low <= stop-loss price OR high >= take-profit price
Exit: short, fast moving average > slow moving average when close-all-at-crossover is enabled OR high >= stop-loss price OR low <= take-profit price
For each moving average, use its configured timeframe, which may be higher, lower, or the same as the chart timeframe; use the current bar, which may still be forming. The fast moving-average period is shorter than the slow period.
