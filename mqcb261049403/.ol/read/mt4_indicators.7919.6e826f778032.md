Calculation: Up score on each of three successively higher timeframes = 12.5 × (number of five simple moving averages with increasing lookbacks whose value on the still-forming bar is greater than on the previous completed bar + 3 if the Accelerator Oscillator is bullish, otherwise 0). For the oscillator, bullish means (value[1] > value[2] AND value[2] > value[3] AND value[0] < 0 AND value[0] > value[1]) OR (value[0] > value[1] AND value[1] > value[2] AND value[0] > 0); value[0] is on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Down score on each of the same three higher timeframes = 12.5 × (number of the same five simple moving averages whose value on the still-forming bar is less than on the previous completed bar + 3 if the Accelerator Oscillator is bearish, otherwise 0). Bearish means (value[1] < value[2] AND value[2] < value[3] AND value[0] > 0 AND value[0] < value[1]) OR (value[0] < value[1] AND value[1] < value[2] AND value[0] < 0); value[0] is on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Up score > 50 on each of the three higher timeframes
Short: Down score > 50 on each of the three higher timeframes
