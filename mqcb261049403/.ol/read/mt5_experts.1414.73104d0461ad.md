Calculation: Step stochastic fast and slow lines = On the configured indicator timeframe, use ATR-based stepped bands. Track cumulative ATR minimum and maximum; form step widths from these extremes using the fast and slow step parameters. For each width, set raw bands to close ± 2 × step width; maintain an up/down trend that switches up when close exceeds the prior upper band and down when close falls below the prior lower band. In an uptrend, carry the lower band forward if the raw lower band is lower; in a downtrend, carry the upper band forward if the raw upper band is higher. The stepped line is the carried lower band plus step width in an uptrend, or carried upper band minus step width in a downtrend. Use the fast parameter for the minimum- and maximum-ATR widths; use the fast and slow parameters for the midpoint width. Normalize the fast line from the minimum-width stepped line and the slow line from the midpoint-width stepped line against the maximum-width stepped line ± its step width, scaling to 0–100. Use the last completed indicator-timeframe bars; the indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured.
