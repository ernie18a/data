Calculation: Volatility = moving average of close − moving average of median price, using the volatility and zone lookbacks, respectively, on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Upper volatility zone = moving average of high − moving average of median price, using the zone lookback on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Lower volatility zone = moving average of low − moving average of median price, using the zone lookback on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Smoothed signed volume = moving average of signed tick volume using the volume lookback on the chart timeframe; signed tick volume is positive when close > open and negative otherwise
Long: Volatility > upper volatility zone OR smoothed signed volume > 0
Short: Volatility < lower volatility zone OR smoothed signed volume < 0
