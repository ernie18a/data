Calculation: Entry stop = the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over a lookback ending at the last completed chart bar, fixed at entry.
Long: No long position is open AND an older, longer row of consecutive chart bars has open − close >= a minimum body size AND a newer, shorter row of consecutive chart bars ending at the last completed bar has close − open >= a minimum body size AND the last completed chart bar's close > the highest high over a lookback ending at the preceding bar AND the last completed chart bar's high-low stochastic main line > its signal line AND the high-low stochastic main line is between a lower and an upper threshold inclusive on every bar of a lookback ending at the second most recent completed chart bar. Use SMA smoothing and round stochastic values to two decimal places; the stochastic used for the threshold test has independently configurable parameters.
Short: No short position is open AND an older, longer row of consecutive chart bars has close − open >= a minimum body size AND a newer, shorter row of consecutive chart bars ending at the last completed bar has open − close >= a minimum body size AND the last completed chart bar's close < the lowest low over a lookback ending at the preceding bar AND the last completed chart bar's high-low stochastic main line < its signal line AND the high-low stochastic main line is between a lower and an upper threshold inclusive on every bar of a lookback ending at the second most recent completed chart bar. Use SMA smoothing and round stochastic values to two decimal places; the stochastic used for the threshold test has independently configurable parameters.
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= entry price + a configured multiple of the distance from entry price to entry stop.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= entry price − a configured multiple of the distance from entry price to entry stop.
