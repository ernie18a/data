Calculation: Moving-average gap = shorter-period moving average of close minus longer-period moving average of close, using the same selected averaging method on the signal timeframe. The current signal-timeframe bar may still be forming; prior bars are completed. The signal timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: During the configured trading-hour window, the moving-average gap is positive now and was negative on the prior bar OR the gap was zero on the prior bar and negative two bars ago OR the gap is positive now and on the prior bar, the gap two bars ago is greater than the prior-bar gap, the current gap is at least the prior-bar gap, and the prior-bar gap is within the configured bounce tolerance of zero.
Short: During the configured trading-hour window, the moving-average gap is negative now and was positive on the prior bar OR the gap was zero on the prior bar and positive two bars ago OR the gap is negative now and on the prior bar, the gap two bars ago is less than the prior-bar gap, the current gap is at most the prior-bar gap, and the absolute prior-bar gap is within the configured bounce tolerance of zero.
