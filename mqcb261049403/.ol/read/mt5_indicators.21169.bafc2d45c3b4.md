Calculation: Volume Flow Oscillator = the exponential moving average over the smoothing period of (calculation period × the calculation-period simple moving average of signed capped volume ÷ the previous bar’s calculation-period simple moving average of volume); use the unsmoothed value when the smoothing period is 1. For each bar, typical price = (high + low + close) ÷ 3; log change = ln(typical price ÷ previous bar’s typical price); threshold = deviation coefficient × 30-bar standard deviation of log changes × close; capped volume = min(volume, previous bar’s calculation-period average volume × volume coefficient); signed capped volume = capped volume if typical price change > threshold, −capped volume if typical price change < −threshold, otherwise 0.
Long: Volume Flow Oscillator > 0
Short: Volume Flow Oscillator < 0
