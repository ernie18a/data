Calculation: EMV = change in median price × current median price ÷ (2 × volume); if volume is zero, EMV = 0
Calculation: Smoothed EMV = EMV smoothed on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Signal line = Smoothed EMV smoothed on the chart timeframe with a shorter lookback than the first smoothing
Long: Smoothed EMV > 0 OR Smoothed EMV > Signal line
Short: Smoothed EMV < 0 OR Smoothed EMV < Signal line
