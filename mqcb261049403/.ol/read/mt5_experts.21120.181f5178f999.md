Calculation: Fibonacci levels = highest high and lowest low of the last N daily bars, including the still-forming daily bar; 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, and 61.8% levels are measured from the low toward the high.
Calculation: Moving average = moving average of chart-timeframe prices; use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Resistance and support = levels from the Resistance & Support indicator on the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar.
Long: close > moving average AND close > Fibonacci 23.6% level AND close < Fibonacci 61.8% level AND close > resistance
Long: close > moving average AND close < resistance AND close < Fibonacci 0% level
Long: close < moving average AND close < resistance AND close < Fibonacci 0% level
Short: close > moving average AND close > Fibonacci 23.6% level AND close < resistance
Short: close < moving average AND close > Fibonacci 23.6% level AND close < Fibonacci 61.8% level AND close < support
Short: close < moving average AND close > Fibonacci 23.6% level AND close < resistance
Exit: long, close − entry price > trailing stop distance + trailing step AND close − entry price − trailing stop distance > trailing step
Exit: short, entry price − close > trailing stop distance + trailing step AND entry price − close − trailing stop distance > trailing step
