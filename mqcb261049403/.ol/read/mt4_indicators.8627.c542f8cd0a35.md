Calculation: Prior-day range = prior completed day's high minus low
Calculation: Long entry, stop, and three targets = Fibonacci retracement/extension levels derived from the prior completed day's high and low
Calculation: Short entry, stop, and three targets = Fibonacci retracement/extension levels derived from the prior completed day's low and high
Calculation: Target risk/reward ratio = absolute distance from entry level to target divided by absolute distance from entry level to stop
Long: close > current day's opening price AND each of the three long target risk/reward ratios > the minimum threshold
Short: close < current day's opening price AND each of the three short target risk/reward ratios > the minimum threshold
