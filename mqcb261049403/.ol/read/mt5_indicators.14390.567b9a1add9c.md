Calculation: RMACD = short-lookback Relative Vigor Index minus longer-lookback TRVI signal line, using the selected volume series
Calculation: Signal Line = smoothed RMACD
Long: RMACD on the last completed chart bar < Signal Line on the previous chart bar AND RMACD on the previous chart bar > Signal Line on the chart bar before that
Short: RMACD on the last completed chart bar > Signal Line on the previous chart bar AND RMACD on the previous chart bar < Signal Line on the chart bar before that
