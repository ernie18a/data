Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = the candidate high, at the configured confirmation offset, is >= the highs 2 and 3 bars older and > the highs 1 and 2 bars newer.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = the candidate low, at the configured confirmation offset, is <= the lows 2 and 3 bars older and < the lows 1 and 2 bars newer.
Calculation: Minimum level spacing = (contract step + precision) × minimum price increment; precision offset = precision × minimum price increment.
Calculation: Resistance level = confirmed pivot high + precision offset when the pivot is at least one minimum level spacing above the previous resistance level or below it; otherwise carry forward the previous resistance level. Support level = confirmed pivot low − precision offset when the pivot is at least one minimum level spacing below the previous support level or above it; otherwise carry forward the previous support level. When sourced from another timeframe, use the higher-timeframe indicator value mapped to the current chart bar; that bar may still be forming, while the pivot inputs are completed bars.
Long: Confirmed pivot low exists AND (confirmed pivot low − previous support level >= minimum level spacing OR confirmed pivot low < previous support level).
Short: Confirmed pivot high exists AND (previous resistance level − confirmed pivot high >= minimum level spacing OR confirmed pivot high < previous resistance level).
