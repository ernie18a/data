Calculation: Aroon oscillator = 100 × (bars since the lowest selected low − bars since the highest selected high) over a lookback; include the current bar and prior bars, and divide by the lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = rolling quantiles of the Aroon oscillator over the same lookback; use a higher quantile for the upper band and a lower quantile for the lower band.
Long: Aroon oscillator > upper band
Short: Aroon oscillator < lower band
