Calculation: Bullish signal = On the selected indicator bar, either (1) close > moving average AND low < moving average; (2) MACD crosses above its signal line; (3) OsMA crosses above its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; (4) stochastic %K crosses above %D; (5) RSI crosses above its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; (6) CCI crosses upward through any of −100, 0, or 100; (7) RVI crosses above its signal line; (8) ADX < 25 AND +DI crosses above −DI; (9) TRIX crosses above its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; or (10) low < lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band, OR low < middle Bollinger Band AND close > middle Bollinger Band. For all selected-indicator calculations, use the bar on the configured timeframe corresponding to the chart bar; that timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and the bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Bearish signal = On the selected indicator bar, either (1) close < moving average AND high > moving average; (2) MACD crosses below its signal line; (3) OsMA crosses below its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; (4) stochastic %K crosses below %D; (5) RSI crosses below its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; (6) CCI crosses downward through any of −100, 0, or 100; (7) RVI crosses below its signal line; (8) ADX < 25 AND +DI crosses below −DI; (9) TRIX crosses below its simple moving average over a shorter lookback; or (10) high > upper Bollinger Band AND close < upper Bollinger Band, OR high > middle Bollinger Band AND close < middle Bollinger Band. For all selected-indicator calculations, use the bar on the configured timeframe corresponding to the chart bar; that timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and the bar may still be forming.
Long: The first selected indicator has a bullish signal AND (the second indicator is disabled OR the second selected indicator has a bullish signal).
Short: The first selected indicator has a bearish signal AND (the second indicator is disabled OR the second selected indicator has a bearish signal).
