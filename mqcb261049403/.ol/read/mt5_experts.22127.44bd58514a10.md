Calculation: In-range percentage = 100 × (number of the last N chart-timeframe bars, including the current forming bar, where |Chaikin Oscillator| ≤ flat level) / N
Long: current forming chart-timeframe Chaikin Oscillator ≥ open level AND in-range percentage > percentage threshold
Short: current forming chart-timeframe Chaikin Oscillator ≤ −open level AND in-range percentage > percentage threshold
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
