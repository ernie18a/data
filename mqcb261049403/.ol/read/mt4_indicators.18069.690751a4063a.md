Calculation: Accumulated RSI = sum of consecutive RSI values ending on the current bar.
Calculation: Upper and lower levels = either the selected upper and lower quantiles of accumulated RSI over a lookback, or floating levels derived from its lookback high and low using selected upper and lower percentages.
Calculation: Mid level = midpoint of the upper and lower levels, or the selected middle quantile of accumulated RSI, according to the selected level method.
Long: Accumulated RSI > upper level OR Accumulated RSI > mid level OR Accumulated RSI > previous bar's accumulated RSI
Short: Accumulated RSI < lower level OR Accumulated RSI < mid level OR Accumulated RSI < previous bar's accumulated RSI
