Long: Higher-timeframe forming-bar close < 5-period SMA of open on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND no existing position on the symbol
Short: Higher-timeframe forming-bar close > 4-period SMA of open on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND higher-timeframe forming-bar close >= 5-period SMA of open on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND no existing position on the symbol
Exit: Long, low <= long stop-loss price OR high >= long take-profit price
Exit: Short, high >= short stop-loss price OR low <= short take-profit price
