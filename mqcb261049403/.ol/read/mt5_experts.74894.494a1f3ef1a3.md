Calculation: Supertrend trend = bullish when the close is above the lower band and bearish when below the upper band; otherwise retain the prior trend. Bands use midpoint (high + low) / 2 ± ATR × multiplier. For each bar, the lower band is max(current basic lower band, prior lower band) when prior close > prior lower band, otherwise the current basic lower band; the upper band is min(current basic upper band, prior upper band) when prior close < prior upper band, otherwise the current basic upper band. A bearish trend turns bullish when close > prior upper band; a bullish trend turns bearish when close < prior lower band.
Long: Chart-timeframe Supertrend trend on the last completed bar is bullish AND (there are no existing long positions OR higher-timeframe Supertrend trend on its last completed bar is bullish).
Short: Chart-timeframe Supertrend trend on the last completed bar is bearish AND (there are no existing short positions OR higher-timeframe Supertrend trend on its last completed bar is bearish).
Exit: Long positions, long-basket floating profit ≥ take-profit threshold OR total floating profit ≤ global loss threshold.
Exit: Short positions, short-basket floating profit ≥ take-profit threshold OR total floating profit ≤ global loss threshold.
