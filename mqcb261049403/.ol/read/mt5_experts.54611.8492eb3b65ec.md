Calculation: Daily range % = (current high − current low) / current high × 100, using the still-forming daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart when the chart is below daily).
Calculation: Upper zone = current daily high − (current daily high − current daily low) / zone divisor; lower zone = current daily low + (current daily high − current daily low) / zone divisor. Use 4 as the divisor when daily range % is at least the configured minimum and at least the midpoint between the configured minimum and maximum; otherwise use the configured zone divisor. Daily-bar values are from the still-forming daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart when the chart is below daily).
Long: Daily range % > configured minimum AND daily range % < configured maximum AND prior completed chart-bar close > upper zone AND current chart-bar open >= prior completed chart-bar close AND current chart-bar price > upper zone AND configured month, weekday, and trading-time filters allow trading
Long: Daily realized profit > 0 AND daily range % > configured minimum / 0.75 AND prior completed chart-bar close is above lower zone AND below upper zone AND last position side is short AND configured month, weekday, and trading-time filters allow trading
Short: Daily range % > configured minimum AND daily range % < configured maximum AND prior completed chart-bar close < lower zone AND current chart-bar open <= prior completed chart-bar close AND current chart-bar price < lower zone AND configured month, weekday, and trading-time filters allow trading
Short: Daily realized profit > 0 AND daily range % > configured minimum / 0.75 AND prior completed chart-bar close is below upper zone AND above lower zone AND last position side is long AND configured month, weekday, and trading-time filters allow trading
Exit: Long, current price <= stop-loss price OR current price >= take-profit price OR current time >= configured close time
Exit: Short, current price >= stop-loss price OR current price <= take-profit price OR current time >= configured close time
