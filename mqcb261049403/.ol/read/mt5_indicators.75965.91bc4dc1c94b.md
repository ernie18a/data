Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is strictly above/below the highs/lows of the specified number of neighboring bars on each side; use only completed bars with all neighbors completed.
Calculation: Buy-side and sell-side liquidity zones = previous completed daily high/low plus up to six most recent confirmed swing highs/lows from each analyzed timeframe; cluster same-side levels within a matching distance based on execution-timeframe ATR, average each level with all matching levels, then select up to two strongest non-overlapping clusters per side. A zone's midpoint is its level; buy-side zones come from highs and sell-side zones from lows.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = bullish execution-timeframe bar with body at least a parameterized fraction of execution-timeframe ATR and close above the preceding bar's high. Bearish displacement = bearish bar with body at least that fraction of ATR and close below the preceding bar's low.
Calculation: Long stop = most recent confirmed swing low on a lower timeframe; short stop = most recent confirmed swing high on a lower timeframe.
Calculation: First target = nearest confirmed swing high above entry for a long, or swing low below entry for a short, on a lower timeframe; if none exists, use one unit of entry-to-stop risk in the trade direction.
Long: (execution-timeframe low < sell-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND execution-timeframe close > sell-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND bullish displacement) OR (execution-timeframe close > buy-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND preceding execution-timeframe close <= buy-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND bullish displacement); AND long stop < entry.
Short: (execution-timeframe high > buy-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND execution-timeframe close < buy-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND bearish displacement) OR (execution-timeframe close < sell-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND preceding execution-timeframe close >= sell-side liquidity-zone midpoint AND bearish displacement); AND short stop > entry.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= first target
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= first target
