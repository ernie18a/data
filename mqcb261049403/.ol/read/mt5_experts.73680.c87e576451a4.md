Calculation: SNR levels = on higher-timeframe bars, support at the midpoint of a bearish candle’s close and the next bullish candle’s open; resistance at the midpoint of a bullish candle’s close and the next bearish candle’s open. Also include bullish body gaps as support and bearish body gaps as resistance. Use completed bars; discard a level if a later completed bar’s body breaks it.
Calculation: Recent swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over the longer chart-timeframe lookback, excluding the latest completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish rejection at support = latest completed chart-timeframe bar low reaches the support level within the touch tolerance, and closes above the level and above its open. Bearish rejection at resistance = its high reaches resistance within the touch tolerance, and closes below the level and below its open.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = bullish when the latest completed chart-timeframe low breaks the recent swing low by the sweep tolerance and closes back above it; bearish when its high breaks the recent swing high by the tolerance and closes back below it.
Calculation: Engulfing confirmation = regular engulfing of the prior chart-timeframe candle, or hidden bullish engulfing (bullish close, lower low, close above the prior candle’s midpoint) or hidden bearish engulfing (bearish close, higher high, close below the prior candle’s midpoint).
Calculation: MISS = at least two of the seven chart-timeframe bars before the latest completed bar have highs just below resistance (bearish) or lows just above support (bullish), within the touch tolerance multiplied by five.
Calculation: Trendline confluence = project a line through the two most recent confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs for bearish setups or swing lows for bullish setups; true when the projection at the latest completed bar is within twice the touch tolerance of its close or the SNR level.
Calculation: QML pattern = bearish when the highest high in the nearer 15-bar chart-timeframe window exceeds the highest high in the older 15-bar window and the latest completed close is below the older window’s lowest low; bullish when the nearer lowest low is below the older lowest low and the latest completed close is above the older highest high. Both windows exclude the latest completed bar.
Calculation: Breakout-retest = a completed chart-timeframe candle within the prior breakout lookback crossed an SNR level by its open and close, and the latest completed candle retests the level within the touch tolerance and closes beyond it in the breakout direction.
Calculation: CRT = on the higher H4 timeframe, the latest completed bar sweeps the preceding completed bar’s high (bearish) or low (bullish), then closes back within that preceding bar’s range.
Calculation: Reaction layers = bullish or bearish SNR rejection (L1); rejection AND liquidity sweep (L2); rejection AND MISS (L3); rejection AND engulfing (L4); rejection AND engulfing at a GAP SNR level (L5); rejection AND trendline confluence (L6); rejection AND QML (L7); rejection AND QML AND engulfing (L8); breakout-retest AND trendline confluence (L9); rejection AND CRT (L10). A cluster combines same-direction layers at SNR levels within the confluence-zone tolerance.
Calculation: Whitelisted reaction cluster = any cluster containing L1+L4+L5+L7+L8, L1+L3+L4+L5+L10, L1+L3+L4+L6+L9, L1+L3+L6+L9+L10, L1+L2+L3+L4+L10, or L1+L3+L4+L7+L8.
Calculation: Momentum confirmations = range break (latest completed chart-timeframe close beyond the prior range by the break buffer), displacement (absolute candle body meets the displacement threshold), direction-matched FVG (bullish latest low above the high three bars back; bearish latest high below the low three bars back), hold outside the prior range, and directional movement across the longer H1 close lookback. The H1 bars are higher timeframe and completed.
Calculation: Strong-trend confirmations = chart-timeframe close breaks the prior range, latest completed candle body meets the displacement threshold, and higher-timeframe alignment. For longs, completed D1 and H4 close movements meet their respective upward thresholds; for shorts, completed H4 movement meets its downward threshold OR completed D1 movement meets its upward threshold.
Long: A bullish reaction cluster contains at least five distinct layers and a whitelisted reaction cluster, OR it has at least three momentum confirmations including the direction-matched FVG and passes the momentum layer/state rules; AND the state is not NORMAL, reaction is not in RANGE_LOCK, the higher-timeframe premium/discount position is within the long limit (unless an eligible recent range break-hold overrides it), and the cluster is at a narrow-range edge or has a recent break-hold; AND its DOL or fallback target gives at least the minimum risk/reward. Alternatively, a bullish strong-trend setup has all strong-trend confirmations and its higher-timeframe range position is within the long limit. Alternatively, after such a bullish strong-trend setup arms a pullback, the latest completed chart-timeframe candle has a pullback within the allowed size, retests the break level or taps the bullish FVG midpoint and closes above it, is bullish with sufficient body, and is either in discount or has that retest/FVG tap; the arm remains valid and below its entry limit.
Short: A bearish reaction cluster contains at least five distinct layers and a whitelisted reaction cluster, OR it has at least three momentum confirmations including the direction-matched FVG and passes the momentum layer/state rules; AND the state is not NORMAL, reaction is not in RANGE_LOCK, the higher-timeframe premium/discount position is within the short limit (unless an eligible recent range break-hold overrides it), and the cluster is at a narrow-range edge or has a recent break-hold; AND its DOL or fallback target gives at least the minimum risk/reward.
Exit: long, price reaches the stop or target; close the configured partial portion when favorable movement reaches the configured R multiple, then move the stop to entry plus the configured offset.
Exit: short, price reaches the stop or target; close the configured partial portion when favorable movement reaches the configured R multiple, then move the stop to entry minus the configured offset.
