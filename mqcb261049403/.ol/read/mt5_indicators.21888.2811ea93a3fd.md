Calculation: Volume-weighted smoothed CCI = selected moving average of CCI (using the selected applied price) × selected volume.
Calculation: Smoothed volume = selected moving average of selected volume, using the same method and lookback as the volume-weighted CCI.
Long: Volume-weighted smoothed CCI > positive inner threshold × smoothed volume; on the higher timeframe, use the bar still forming.
Short: Volume-weighted smoothed CCI < negative inner threshold × smoothed volume; on the higher timeframe, use the bar still forming.
