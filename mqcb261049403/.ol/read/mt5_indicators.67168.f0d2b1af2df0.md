Calculation: Previous bar high = high of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous bar low = low of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Long stop level = previous bar low + (previous bar high − previous bar low) × long stop ratio
Calculation: Long target level = previous bar low + (previous bar high − previous bar low) × long target ratio
Calculation: Short stop level = previous bar low + (previous bar high − previous bar low) × short stop ratio
Calculation: Short target level = previous bar low − (previous bar high − previous bar low) × absolute value of short target ratio
Long: close > previous bar high
Short: close < previous bar low
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop level OR high ≥ long target level
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop level OR low ≤ short target level
