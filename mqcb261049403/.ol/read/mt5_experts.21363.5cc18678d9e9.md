Calculation: BrainTrend state = On each bar, true range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close)); weighted ATR = 2 × sum of the last N true ranges weighted N for newest through 1 for oldest, divided by 7 × 8; threshold = 0.7 × weighted ATR. Initialize bullish if the close two bars back is greater than the previous close, otherwise bearish, with reference extreme equal to the previous close. While bullish, switch bearish when low < reference extreme − threshold and set the extreme to high; while bearish, switch bullish when high > reference extreme + threshold and set the extreme to low. Otherwise, update the bullish extreme to the greater of itself and low, or the bearish extreme to the lesser of itself and high. Use last completed bars on the configured indicator timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe as configured).
Calculation: No-lag weighted moving average = linear weighted moving average of the linear weighted moving average of the configured applied price, using the same lookback for both; the newest bar has the greatest weight. Use last completed bars on the configured indicator timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe as configured).
Long: BrainTrend state on the last completed bar is bullish AND its state on the preceding completed bar is bearish OR No-lag weighted moving average is rising on the last completed bar AND was not rising on the preceding completed bar.
Short: BrainTrend state on the last completed bar is bearish AND its state on the preceding completed bar is bullish OR No-lag weighted moving average is falling on the last completed bar AND was not falling on the preceding completed bar.
Exit: long, BrainTrend state on the last completed bar is bearish OR No-lag weighted moving average is rising on the last completed bar OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, BrainTrend state on the last completed bar is bullish OR No-lag weighted moving average is falling on the last completed bar OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance.
