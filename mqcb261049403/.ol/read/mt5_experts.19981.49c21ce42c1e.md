Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = fast-period moving average of the accumulation/distribution line minus its slow-period moving average, using tick volume; fast period is shorter than slow period
Long: (no open positions AND previous completed chart bar closed above its open AND previous completed bar low < current, still-forming chart-bar moving average AND previous completed chart-bar Chaikin Oscillator < lower opening threshold) OR (exactly one open short position AND previous completed chart bar closed above its open AND previous completed bar low < current, still-forming chart-bar moving average AND previous completed chart-bar Chaikin Oscillator < lower hedging threshold)
Short: (no open positions AND previous completed chart bar closed below its open AND previous completed bar high > current, still-forming chart-bar moving average AND previous completed chart-bar Chaikin Oscillator > upper opening threshold) OR (exactly one open long position AND previous completed chart bar closed below its open AND previous completed bar high > current, still-forming chart-bar moving average AND previous completed chart-bar Chaikin Oscillator > upper hedging threshold)
Exit: Long, price <= stop-loss level OR price >= take-profit level; initially the stop-loss level is entry price minus the stop distance, and take-profit level is entry price plus the target distance; move the stop to entry after favorable movement exceeds the breakeven distance, then trail it at price minus the trailing distance when favorable movement exceeds trailing distance plus breakeven distance
Exit: Short, price >= stop-loss level OR price <= take-profit level; initially the stop-loss level is entry price plus the stop distance, and take-profit level is entry price minus the target distance; move the stop to entry after favorable movement exceeds the breakeven distance, then trail it at price plus the trailing distance when favorable movement exceeds trailing distance plus breakeven distance
