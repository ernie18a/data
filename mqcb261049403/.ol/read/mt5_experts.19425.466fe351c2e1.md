Calculation: Applied price = the configured price series derived from OHLC.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on the signal timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed bar: initialize to applied price × (1 + configured offset); thereafter carry forward the prior stop if current applied price equals it; if current and prior applied prices are both below the prior stop, use the lower of the prior stop and current applied price × (1 + offset); if both are above, use the higher of the prior stop and current applied price × (1 − offset); otherwise use current applied price × (1 − offset) if it is above the prior stop, or current applied price × (1 + offset) if it is below.
Long: Current applied price > current trailing stop AND prior applied price < prior trailing stop AND prior trailing stop is nonzero AND current time is within the configured trading interval (when time filtering is enabled).
Short: Current applied price < current trailing stop AND prior applied price > prior trailing stop AND prior trailing stop is nonzero AND current time is within the configured trading interval (when time filtering is enabled).
Exit: Long, current applied price < current trailing stop AND prior applied price > prior trailing stop AND prior trailing stop is nonzero OR current time is outside the configured trading interval (when time filtering is enabled) OR price reaches the configured stop loss or take profit.
Exit: Short, current applied price > current trailing stop AND prior applied price < prior trailing stop AND prior trailing stop is nonzero OR current time is outside the configured trading interval (when time filtering is enabled) OR price reaches the configured stop loss or take profit.
