Calculation: Grid level = the lowest grid price, rounded down from the current price to a grid-size multiple, plus successive grid-size increments; use the configured low or high boundary as the starting level when provided.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the base target distance; higher profit ranks add one or two grid spacings to that distance.
Calculation: Exponential moving average = exponentially weighted average of close prices over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Simple moving average = arithmetic average of close prices over the configured lookback.
Calculation: MACD = fast exponential moving average minus slow exponential moving average; compare its main line with zero.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR = the parabolic stop-and-reverse value.
Calculation: Stochastic main and signal lines = the stochastic oscillator and its smoothed signal line.
Long: A grid level is above the current ask AND long trading is enabled AND (the level is above the EMA filter, if enabled) AND (the MACD main line is positive on each of the three latest bars, if MACD filtering is enabled) AND (the weekly close is above weekly Parabolic SAR, if SAR filtering is enabled) AND (if MA-group filtering is enabled, the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly descending by lookback, the shortest-minus-middle average exceeds a threshold, and, if stochastic filtering is enabled, the stochastic main line exceeds its signal line by a threshold) AND (the level is above ask and breakout entries are enabled OR the level is at or below ask and countertrend entries are enabled).
Long: A grid level is at or below the current ask AND long trading is enabled AND (the level is above the EMA filter, if enabled) AND (the MACD main line is positive on each of the three latest bars, if MACD filtering is enabled) AND (the weekly close is above weekly Parabolic SAR, if SAR filtering is enabled) AND (if MA-group filtering is enabled, the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly descending by lookback, the shortest-minus-middle average exceeds a threshold, and, if stochastic filtering is enabled, the stochastic main line exceeds its signal line by a threshold) AND (the level is above ask and breakout entries are enabled OR the level is at or below ask and countertrend entries are enabled).
Short: A grid level is below the current bid AND short trading is enabled AND (the level is below the EMA filter, if enabled) AND (the MACD main line is negative on each of the three latest bars, if MACD filtering is enabled) AND (the weekly close is below weekly Parabolic SAR, if SAR filtering is enabled) AND (if MA-group filtering is enabled, the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly ascending by lookback, the middle-minus-shortest average exceeds a threshold, and, if stochastic filtering is enabled, the stochastic signal line exceeds the main line by a threshold) AND (the level is below bid and breakout entries are enabled OR the level is at or above bid and countertrend entries are enabled).
Short: A grid level is at or above the current bid AND short trading is enabled AND (the level is below the EMA filter, if enabled) AND (the MACD main line is negative on each of the three latest bars, if MACD filtering is enabled) AND (the weekly close is below weekly Parabolic SAR, if SAR filtering is enabled) AND (if MA-group filtering is enabled, the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly ascending by lookback, the middle-minus-shortest average exceeds a threshold, and, if stochastic filtering is enabled, the stochastic signal line exceeds the main line by a threshold) AND (the level is below bid and breakout entries are enabled OR the level is at or above bid and countertrend entries are enabled).
Exit: Long, current price reaches or exceeds the position's take-profit price OR reaches or falls below its stop-loss price OR (MACD filtering and close-on-cross are enabled and the MACD main line was negative two completed bars ago and is positive on both the latest completed and forming bars) OR (MA-group filtering and close-opposing-positions are enabled and the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly ascending by lookback and the middle-minus-shortest average exceeds a threshold).
Exit: Short, current price reaches or falls below the position's take-profit price OR reaches or exceeds its stop-loss price OR (MACD filtering and close-on-cross are enabled and the MACD main line was positive two completed bars ago and is negative on both the latest completed and forming bars) OR (MA-group filtering and close-opposing-positions are enabled and the short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple moving averages are strictly descending by lookback and the shortest-minus-middle average exceeds a threshold).
