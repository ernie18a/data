Calculation: EMA slope = current EMA of open prices minus the previous completed bar’s EMA, using the chart timeframe; the current EMA may use the bar still forming.
Long: no long position is open AND EMA slope is between the minimum and maximum entry slopes, inclusive AND current minute-of-hour > begin-minute threshold AND current bar close <= open AND close <= low + entry allowance AND every absolute difference between consecutive opens among the latest six chart bars is < single-jump threshold AND every absolute difference between opens two bars apart among the latest six chart bars is < double-jump threshold
Short: no short position is open AND EMA slope is between the negative maximum and negative minimum entry slopes, inclusive AND current minute-of-hour > begin-minute threshold AND current bar close >= open AND close >= high - entry allowance AND every absolute difference between consecutive opens among the latest six chart bars is < single-jump threshold AND every absolute difference between opens two bars apart among the latest six chart bars is < double-jump threshold
Exit: Long, current bar close <= entry reference price - smart-stop distance AND current minute-of-hour > end-minute threshold AND current bar close >= open AND close >= high - exit allowance OR previous completed bar volume <= minimum volume OR current bar low <= entry price - emergency-stop distance OR current bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, current bar close >= entry reference price + smart-stop distance AND current minute-of-hour > end-minute threshold AND current bar close <= open AND close <= low + exit allowance OR previous completed bar volume <= minimum volume OR current bar high >= entry price + emergency-stop distance OR current bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance
