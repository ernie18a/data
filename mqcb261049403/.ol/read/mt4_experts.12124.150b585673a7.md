Short: On the chart timeframe, the Stochastic main line crossed below its signal line from the last completed bar to the bar still forming; both lines on the forming bar are above the upper threshold; the long-term moving average on the bar two bars ago is above its value on the bar still forming; and price is above that moving average by more than the distance threshold. Enter while that marked short setup is within its timeout, if the short-term moving average on a lower timeframe is falling faster than the speed threshold between its value two bars ago and its value on the bar still forming, the forming-bar Stochastic signal remains above the upper threshold, ATR is at or above the volatility minimum, and the time is within the trading session.
Long: On the chart timeframe, the Stochastic main line crossed above its signal line from the last completed bar to the bar still forming; both lines on the forming bar are below the lower threshold; the long-term moving average on the bar two bars ago is below its value on the bar still forming; and price is below that moving average by more than the distance threshold. Enter while that marked long setup is within its timeout, if the short-term moving average on a lower timeframe is rising faster than the speed threshold between its value two bars ago and its value on the bar still forming, the forming-bar Stochastic signal remains below the lower threshold, ATR is at or above the volatility minimum, and the time is within the trading session.
Exit: long, price reaches the stop loss below entry price OR reaches the take profit above entry price; the take-profit distance is the low-volatility distance when chart-timeframe ATR on the bar still forming is below the volatility threshold, otherwise the high-volatility distance.
Exit: short, price reaches the stop loss above entry price OR reaches the take profit below entry price; the take-profit distance is the low-volatility distance when chart-timeframe ATR on the bar still forming is below the volatility threshold, otherwise the high-volatility distance.
