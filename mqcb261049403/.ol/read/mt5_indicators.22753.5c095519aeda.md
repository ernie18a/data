Calculation: AD resistance line = On the chart timeframe, find local AD maxima (middle value greater than both neighboring values) among bars more than the unchecked-bars count and no farther back than the bars-for-calculation lookback. Let the newest maximum be the right endpoint and the highest maximum the initial left anchor. Require the endpoint to be newer and the AD value at the left anchor’s bar to exceed the endpoint value. Scan maxima from that left anchor toward the endpoint; whenever a maximum is above the line projected from the current anchor to the endpoint, move the left anchor to that maximum. The resulting line is the straight line through the final left anchor and the endpoint.
Calculation: AD support line = Use the same lookback and unchecked-bars exclusion to find local AD minima (middle value below both neighbors). Let the newest minimum be the right endpoint and the lowest minimum the initial left anchor. Require the endpoint to be newer and the AD value at the left anchor’s bar to be below the endpoint value. Scan minima from that left anchor toward the endpoint; whenever a minimum is below the line projected from the current anchor to the endpoint, move the left anchor to that minimum. The resulting line is the straight line through the final left anchor and the endpoint.
Long: AD at the last completed bar is above the AD resistance line there AND (AD at the bar two bars back is at or below the line there OR AD at the bar three bars back is at or below the line there) AND (CCI filtering is off OR CCI at the last completed bar is at or below the lower filter level AND CCI two bars back is below the lower filter level)
Short: AD at the last completed bar is below the AD support line there AND (AD at the bar two bars back is at or above the line there OR AD at the bar three bars back is at or above the line there) AND (CCI filtering is off OR CCI at the last completed bar is at or above the upper filter level AND CCI two bars back is above the upper filter level)
