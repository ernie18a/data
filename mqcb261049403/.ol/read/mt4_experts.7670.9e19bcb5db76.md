Calculation: RSI pivot high = RSI at a completed lower-timeframe bar whose value is at least the RSI at the next older bar and greater than RSI at the next newer bar; RSI pivot low = RSI at a completed lower-timeframe bar whose value is at most the RSI at the next older bar and less than RSI at the next newer bar
Calculation: Upper RSI line = straight line through the most recent RSI pivot high above 40 and the next older pivot high above 60 that is higher than the recent one; invalidate the pair if an intervening RSI pivot low is below 40. Evaluate the line at the current and previous lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Lower RSI line = straight line through the most recent RSI pivot low below 60 and the next older pivot low below 40 that is lower than the recent one; invalidate the pair if an intervening RSI pivot high is above 60. Evaluate the line at the current and previous lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: RFTL = output buffer 0 of the RFTL indicator on the chart timeframe, sampled from its last completed bar.
Long: lower RSI line is valid AND the older lower-line pivot has a greater past-bar index than the older upper-line pivot AND RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar > lower RSI line at the current bar AND RSI on the prior completed lower-timeframe bar < lower RSI line at the previous bar AND RFTL < previous completed chart-timeframe close AND RSI on the prior completed lower-timeframe bar < 50 AND RSI on the still-forming lower-timeframe bar < 55 AND no long position is open
Short: upper RSI line is valid AND the older upper-line pivot has a greater past-bar index than the older lower-line pivot AND RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar < upper RSI line at the current bar AND RSI on the prior completed lower-timeframe bar > upper RSI line at the previous bar AND RFTL > previous completed chart-timeframe close AND RSI on the prior completed lower-timeframe bar > 50 AND RSI on the still-forming lower-timeframe bar > 47 AND no short position is open
Exit: long, RSI on the still-forming lower-timeframe bar > 70 OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, RSI on the still-forming lower-timeframe bar < 30 OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
