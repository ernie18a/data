Calculation: Long score = weighted sum: fast EMA > slow EMA and their gap exceeds the ATR separation threshold; close exceeds trend EMA by the ATR trend buffer; RSI is in the bullish momentum zone and rising; MACD histogram is rising; close > (high + low + close) / 3; tick volume exceeds its recent average threshold; ADX exceeds its trend threshold and +DI > -DI; add the HTF alignment weight when enabled and higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA
Calculation: Short score = weighted sum: fast EMA < slow EMA and their gap exceeds the ATR separation threshold; close is below trend EMA by the ATR trend buffer; RSI is in the bearish momentum zone and falling; MACD histogram is falling; close < (high + low + close) / 3; tick volume exceeds its recent average threshold; ADX exceeds its trend threshold and -DI > +DI; add the HTF alignment weight when enabled and higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA
Calculation: Structure stop = for a long, lowest low over the swing lookback minus an ATR buffer; for a short, highest high over the swing lookback plus an ATR buffer. If stop distance is below the minimum ATR distance, use the ATR-based stop instead.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop; after each successive profit target is reached, move it to entry, then the prior profit target. Profit targets are entry price plus (long) or minus (short) initial risk multiplied by the configured risk/reward levels.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close > trend EMA plus ATR trend buffer AND RSI < its upper entry limit AND candle body exceeds the ATR body threshold AND absolute distance from close to fast EMA is below the ATR extension limit AND HTF fast EMA is not below HTF slow EMA (when enabled) AND long score >= configured minimum score AND long score passes the configured grade filter AND last signal direction is not long AND cooldown has elapsed
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < trend EMA minus ATR trend buffer AND RSI > its lower entry limit AND candle body exceeds the ATR body threshold AND absolute distance from close to fast EMA is below the ATR extension limit AND HTF fast EMA is not above HTF slow EMA (when enabled) AND short score >= configured minimum score AND short score passes the configured grade filter AND last signal direction is not short AND cooldown has elapsed
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop OR short-entry condition
Exit: short, high >= trailing stop OR long-entry condition
