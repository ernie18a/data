Calculation: AML = Adaptive Market Level: for each bar, let R1 and R2 be the high-low range of the current and preceding fractal-length windows, each divided by the fractal length; let R3 be the range of their combined 2×fractal-length window, divided by 2×fractal length. Set dimension to (ln(R1+R2)−ln(R3))/ln(2) when the ranges are positive, otherwise 0. Set alpha to clamp(exp(−lag×(dimension−1)), 0.01, 1). Smooth the weighted price (high+low+2×open+2×close)/6 using alpha and the prior smoothed value. Update AML to the smoothed value when it differs from the value lag bars earlier by at least lag²×point; otherwise carry forward the prior AML.
Calculation: Skyscraper trend = ATR-based trend state: over the lookback bars, take the maximum and minimum ATR; step = integer(0.5×sensitivity×(maximum ATR+minimum ATR)/point). In High/Low mode, candidate upper and lower levels are low+2×step×point and high−2×step×point. Compare close with the prior state levels: close above the prior upper level sets trend up; close below the prior lower level sets trend down; otherwise retain the trend. In an up trend, raise the upper level to at least the candidate upper level; in a down trend, lower the lower level to at most the candidate lower level. Initialize both state levels to close. Use the indicator's configured timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe if different) and its last completed bar for signals.
Long: AML is rising on the last completed bar and was not rising on the preceding completed bar, OR Skyscraper trend changes from down to up on the last completed bar.
Short: AML is falling on the last completed bar and was not falling on the preceding completed bar, OR Skyscraper trend changes from up to down on the last completed bar.
Exit: long, AML is falling on the last completed bar OR Skyscraper trend is down on the last completed bar.
Exit: short, AML is rising on the last completed bar OR Skyscraper trend is up on the last completed bar.
