Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of close over the parameter lookback on the chart timeframe; use the value at bar 2 (completed).
Calculation: Hammer = on completed bar 1, midpoint of high and low is below SMA at bar 2; the lower of open and close is above high minus one third of the high-low range; close is below bar 2 close; and open is below bar 2 open.
Calculation: Hanging Man = on completed bar 1, midpoint of high and low is above SMA at bar 2; open is above zero; close is above high minus one third of the high-low range; close is above bar 2 close; and open is above bar 2 open.
Long: Hammer AND MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar < 40
Short: Hanging Man AND MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar > 60
Exit: short, (MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar > 30 AND MFI on the prior completed bar < 30) OR (MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar > 70 AND MFI on the prior completed bar < 70)
Exit: long, (MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar > 70 AND MFI on the prior completed bar < 70) OR (MFI on the last completed chart-timeframe bar < 30 AND MFI on the prior completed bar > 30)
