Calculation: MACD = moving average of close with a shorter lookback minus moving average of close with a longer lookback.
Calculation: MACD momentum = MACD minus MACD from the momentum lookback ago.
Calculation: TSI = 100 × the longer-lookback smoothing of the shorter-lookback smoothing of MACD momentum, divided by the same nested smoothing of absolute MACD momentum.
Calculation: TSI signal = moving-average smoothing of TSI.
Long: TSI > TSI signal (calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar)
Short: TSI < TSI signal (calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar)
