Calculation: Market Profile = On the chart timeframe, bin each bar's close into price levels within 5 points; the Point of Control is the level with the most closes. Starting at that level, expand the Value Area toward the nearer next price level until it contains the configured percentage of closes.
Long: Each of the configured number of last completed chart-timeframe bars has close > open and volume >= minimum volume AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, each bar's absolute close-open body > configured ATR multiple of ATR calculated on the chart timeframe AND the long-position count and total-position count are below their configured limits AND (higher-timeframe close > moving average on the latest still-forming higher-timeframe bar OR, when MACD trend mode is selected, MACD > signal line on that bar) AND chart-timeframe ADX on the still-forming bar > threshold and +DI > -DI AND current price is inside the Market Profile Value Area and farther than the configured avoidance distance from the Point of Control AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, chart-timeframe RSI on the still-forming bar > 50 and < overbought level AND either there is no existing long position or current ask is more than the configured grid step below the lowest long entry price.
Short: Each of the configured number of last completed chart-timeframe bars has close < open and volume >= minimum volume AND, if the ATR filter is enabled, each bar's absolute close-open body > configured ATR multiple of ATR calculated on the chart timeframe AND the short-position count and total-position count are below their configured limits AND (higher-timeframe close < moving average on the latest still-forming higher-timeframe bar OR, when MACD trend mode is selected, MACD < signal line on that bar) AND chart-timeframe ADX on the still-forming bar > threshold and -DI > +DI AND current price is inside the Market Profile Value Area and farther than the configured avoidance distance from the Point of Control AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, chart-timeframe RSI on the still-forming bar < 50 and > oversold level AND either there is no existing short position or current bid is more than the configured grid step above the highest short entry price.
Exit: Long, price <= entry price - configured stop distance OR price >= entry price + configured target distance OR, for a long basket, price >= volume-weighted average entry price + configured breakeven offset.
Exit: Short, price >= entry price + configured stop distance OR price <= entry price - configured target distance OR, for a short basket, price <= volume-weighted average entry price - configured breakeven offset.
