Calculation: Short moving average = short-lookback simple moving average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3, on the selected signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; uses the bar still forming.
Calculation: Long moving average = longer-lookback smoothed moving average of median price (high + low) / 2, on the same selected signal timeframe; uses the bar still forming.
Calculation: StepUpDown direction = compare the bars of the highest and lowest short moving averages over its lookback, and compare the current and previous short-minus-long average differences; process the lookback from oldest to newest, setting bullish when the highest-average bar is later than the lowest-average bar and the current short average is above the lookback low, or when the highest-average bar is earlier and the current difference is greater than the previous difference; set bearish when the highest-average bar is later and the current difference is less than the previous difference, or when the highest-average bar is earlier and the current short average is below the lookback high. Later applicable conditions override earlier ones; the current bar is still forming.
Calculation: ATR stop direction = for each bar, compute a lower candidate as the maximum of high minus an ATR multiple over a lookback, and an upper candidate as the minimum of low plus that ATR multiple; carry forward the prior direction, switching bullish when close exceeds the prior upper stop and bearish when close falls below the prior lower stop. In a bullish direction, ratchet the lower stop upward; in a bearish direction, ratchet the upper stop downward. Compare the prior and current directions to detect flips. ATR and OHLC use the selected signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; the current bar is still forming.
Long: ATR stop direction flips bullish AND StepUpDown direction is bullish AND (flat OR short position with opposite-signal closing enabled).
Short: ATR stop direction flips bearish AND StepUpDown direction is bearish AND (flat OR long position with opposite-signal closing enabled).
Exit: long, opposite short-entry signal with opposite-signal closing enabled OR StepUpDown direction is bearish AND position is profitable.
Exit: short, opposite long-entry signal with opposite-signal closing enabled OR StepUpDown direction is bullish AND position is profitable.
