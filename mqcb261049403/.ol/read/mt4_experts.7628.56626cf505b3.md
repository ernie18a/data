Calculation: Previous-day pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Local momentum = average close of the last 3 chart bars minus average close of the last 9 chart bars, multiplied by 1000; include the chart bar still forming.
Long: Position side = flat AND ((previous completed chart bar open < previous-day pivot AND previous completed chart bar close > previous-day pivot AND previous bar close - open > minimum body threshold AND current forming bar close > previous bar close AND local momentum > 0 AND current forming bar close < current forming bar high) OR (current Donchian channel width > previous-day high - low AND previous chart bar Donchian channel width < previous-day high - low AND current upper Donchian boundary > previous upper boundary AND current lower Donchian boundary >= previous lower boundary AND local momentum > 0 AND current forming bar close is below its high by at least the entry offset)).
Short: Position side = flat AND ((previous completed chart bar open > previous-day pivot AND previous completed chart bar close < previous-day pivot AND previous bar open - close > minimum body threshold AND current forming bar close < previous bar close AND local momentum < 0 AND current forming bar close > current forming bar low) OR (current Donchian channel width > previous-day high - low AND previous chart bar Donchian channel width < previous-day high - low AND current lower Donchian boundary < previous lower boundary AND current upper Donchian boundary <= previous upper boundary AND local momentum > 0 AND current forming bar close is above its low by at least the entry offset)).
Exit: Long, current low <= active stop OR current high >= entry price + target distance OR elapsed chart time is >= 3 bars and < 4 bars AND previous completed chart close < entry price - failure threshold.
Exit: Short, current high >= active stop OR current low <= entry price - target distance OR elapsed chart time is >= 3 bars and < 4 bars AND previous completed chart close > entry price + failure threshold.
