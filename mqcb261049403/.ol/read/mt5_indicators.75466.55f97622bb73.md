Calculation: Swing pivot = a bar whose high (low) is at least as high (low) as the corresponding values in the configured number of bars before and after it; confirm only after those later bars close. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Calculation: Structural trend = direction of the latest close beyond an unbroken pivot high or low; bullish above a pivot high, bearish below a pivot low, otherwise retain the prior direction. For internal structure, allow a bullish break only when swing trend is neutral or bullish, and a bearish break only when swing trend is neutral or bearish.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = within the configured window after an internal pivot, high exceeds a pivot high and close returns below it (buy-side sweep), or low falls below a pivot low and close returns above it (sell-side sweep). Use the latest sweep direction.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when the current bar's low exceeds the high two bars earlier; bearish when its high is below the low two bars earlier. A gap is fresh until price reaches its far boundary. Use completed chart-timeframe bars; an optional higher-timeframe gap uses higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Order block = the highest-volume opposite-direction candle between a structural pivot and its break (or the last such candle if volume weighting is off); discard ranges smaller than the configured ATR fraction. A bullish block is mitigated after a later close below its low; a bearish block after a later close above its high. ATR is the mean true range over the configured lookback, using completed chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: HTF bias = structural trend from confirmed pivots with a shorter lookback, calculated separately on each of three higher timeframes using each timeframe's last completed bar.
Calculation: Chart bias = direction of the more recent internal or swing structural break, falling back to the other structure's trend if the more recent one is neutral.
Calculation: Discount / premium = close below / above the midpoint of the latest swing high-low range.
Calculation: Delta direction = sign of the sum over the configured number of recent completed chart bars of candle-anatomy buy-minus-sell pressure, weighted by volume; for bullish candles buy pressure is body plus lower wick and sell pressure is upper wick, and for bearish candles sell pressure is body plus upper wick and buy pressure is lower wick.
Calculation: Equal-high/low sweep = latest swing high or low is exceeded by the current closed bar's high or low, respectively, where the latest two same-side swing pivots are within the configured ATR tolerance and separated by the configured minimum bars.
Calculation: Entry zone tap = close is within one ATR of a fresh bullish/bearish order block on the corresponding side of price, or within one ATR of a fresh fair value gap of that direction. Use the nearest qualifying zone.
Calculation: HTF alignment = bullish when all three higher-timeframe biases are bullish; bearish when all three are bearish. In non-strict mode, require the master higher-timeframe bias in that direction and no opposing bias among the three.
Calculation: Setup score = start at zero; add 25 for strict HTF alignment, otherwise 12 for at least two same-direction HTF biases and none opposing; add 15 for matching discount (long) or premium (short), 15 for matching recent sweep, 15 for matching CHoCH after that sweep, 15 for matching order-block tap, 10 for matching FVG tap, 5 for matching equal-low/high sweep, and 10 for matching delta direction. Clamp to 0–100.
Long: close exceeds an unbroken swing or internal pivot high (bullish BOS or CHoCH).
Long: low falls below an internal pivot low and close returns above it within the configured sweep window.
Long: swing pivot high exceeds the preceding swing pivot high, or swing pivot low exceeds the preceding swing pivot low.
Long: bullish HTF alignment and bullish chart bias AND a sell-side sweep and subsequent bullish internal CHoCH within the configured sequence window AND a fresh bullish order-block or fair-value-gap tap AND close is not above the swing-range midpoint AND long setup score meets the configured threshold AND long score is at least the short score.
Short: close falls below an unbroken swing or internal pivot low (bearish BOS or CHoCH).
Short: high exceeds an internal pivot high and close returns below it within the configured sweep window.
Short: swing pivot high is below the preceding swing pivot high, or swing pivot low is below the preceding swing pivot low.
Short: bearish HTF alignment and bearish chart bias AND a buy-side sweep and subsequent bearish internal CHoCH within the configured sequence window AND a fresh bearish order-block or fair-value-gap tap AND close is not below the swing-range midpoint AND short setup score meets the configured threshold AND short score exceeds the long score.
