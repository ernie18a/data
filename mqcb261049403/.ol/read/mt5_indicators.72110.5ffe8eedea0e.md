Calculation: SuperTrend trend = bullish when price crosses above its trailing upper band; bearish when price crosses below its trailing lower band; bands are the selected price basis plus/minus ATR times the band multiplier, with the lower band carried forward while price remains above it and the upper band carried forward while price remains below it
Calculation: New swing high = highest high over the shorter lookback exceeds the highest high over the longer lookback; new swing low = lowest low over the shorter lookback is below the lowest low over the longer lookback. The longer lookback is the shorter lookback plus a confirmation lookback derived from it
Long: (reversal mode AND (SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish OR, when fresh pivot is required, a new swing low formed while bearish)) OR (breakout mode AND new swing low AND SuperTrend is bearish); AND (RSI touched below the cold threshold in its lookback window, if the RSI filter is enabled); AND (current volume exceeds the surge factor times average volume over the recent sample, if volume surge is required); AND (if major levels only is enabled, the swing range exceeds the key-level ATR threshold); AND (minimum bar spacing since the previous signal has elapsed)
Short: (reversal mode AND (SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish OR, when fresh pivot is required, a new swing high formed while bullish)) OR (breakout mode AND new swing high AND SuperTrend is bullish); AND (RSI touched above the hot threshold in its lookback window, if the RSI filter is enabled); AND (current volume exceeds the surge factor times average volume over the recent sample, if volume surge is required); AND (if major levels only is enabled, the swing range exceeds the key-level ATR threshold); AND (minimum bar spacing since the previous signal has elapsed)
