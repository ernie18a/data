Calculation: MACD = short-period EMA of close minus long-period EMA of close; the signal line is an EMA of MACD
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD minus its signal line
Calculation: RSI = Wilder-smoothed relative strength of close gains to losses
Calculation: Stochastic = short-period close location within the high-low range, smoothed by a shorter-period average
Calculation: CCI = close minus its moving average, divided by mean absolute deviation times a constant
Calculation: Momentum = close minus close at a shorter lookback
Calculation: OBV = cumulative tick volume signed by close movement
Calculation: Volume-weighted MACD = short-period volume-weighted average close minus long-period volume-weighted average close
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = volume-weighted close location within each bar's high-low range, summed over a lookback and divided by total volume
Calculation: Money Flow Index = relative ratio of rising to falling close-times-tick-volume flows
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = selected price source (close, or high for highs and low for lows) is greater/less than the values over a span of bars on both sides; equal values are allowed only on the right side
Calculation: Clear divergence path = at every intervening bar, both close and oscillator remain on the non-crossing side of the straight line between the pivot and signal endpoints
Long: at least the required minimum number of enabled oscillators has a regular divergence (signal oscillator higher than at a prior confirmed pivot low while selected price source is lower than at that pivot) OR a hidden divergence (signal oscillator lower than at a prior confirmed pivot low while selected price source is higher than at that pivot), with a clear divergence path, using a recent pivot within the configured pivot-count and bar-distance limits and more than the minimum separation
Short: at least the required minimum number of enabled oscillators has a regular divergence (signal oscillator lower than at a prior confirmed pivot high while selected price source is higher than at that pivot) OR a hidden divergence (signal oscillator higher than at a prior confirmed pivot high while selected price source is lower than at that pivot), with a clear divergence path, using a recent pivot within the configured pivot-count and bar-distance limits and more than the minimum separation
