Calculation: Blau smoothed stochastic = 100 × the three-stage smoothed value of (selected price − midpoint of the rolling high/low range) ÷ the three-stage smoothed value of half that range; use the same smoother and stage parameters for numerator and denominator. The range lookback is shorter than the first smoothing lookback; the second and third smoothing lookbacks are shorter than the first. The selected price is close by default.
Long: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0 AND chart-timeframe oscillator > its previous value OR, on the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, the oscillator on any of the seven higher timeframes, using its still-forming bar, > its previous value
Short: Chart-timeframe oscillator < 0 AND chart-timeframe oscillator < its previous value OR, on the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe bar, the oscillator on any of the seven higher timeframes, using its still-forming bar, < its previous value
