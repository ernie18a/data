Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized as (open + close) / 2. Use completed chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Upper channel band = EMA(close, longer-term channel lookback) + ATR(channel width lookback) × multiplier; lower channel band = EMA(close, longer-term channel lookback) − ATR(channel width lookback) × multiplier. Use completed chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open on the latest completed bar AND Heikin-Ashi close <= Heikin-Ashi open on the preceding completed bar within the unexpired signal window AND close >= lower channel band AND signal-bar tick volume >= minimum ratio × average tick volume over a longer lookback AND ATR <= maximum allowed ATR AND current time is within the allowed session and not past the Friday cutoff AND no position is open.
Short: Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open on the latest completed bar AND Heikin-Ashi close >= Heikin-Ashi open on the preceding completed bar within the unexpired signal window AND close <= upper channel band AND signal-bar tick volume >= minimum ratio × average tick volume over a longer lookback AND ATR <= maximum allowed ATR AND current time is within the allowed session and not past the Friday cutoff AND no position is open.
Exit: Long, Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open on the latest completed bar AND Heikin-Ashi close >= Heikin-Ashi open on the preceding completed bar OR low <= initial stop OR high >= take-profit OR (price has reached the break-even trigger and low <= entry price + break-even lock).
Exit: Short, Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open on the latest completed bar AND Heikin-Ashi close <= Heikin-Ashi open on the preceding completed bar OR high >= initial stop OR low <= take-profit OR (price has reached the break-even trigger and high >= entry price − break-even lock).
