Exit: Long, net position is losing AND age in minutes ≥ grace period AND (adverse move > ATR × multiple OR age in minutes ≥ time limit OR fast EMA < slow EMA on the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (its relation to the chart timeframe is unspecified) OR RSI < buy exit level on the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (its relation to the chart timeframe is unspecified))
