Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = linearly weighted moving averages of typical price ((high + low + close) / 3) on a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Long: Fast moving average > slow moving average AND previous bar low < the bar before it high AND the absolute difference between the current bar open and the close two bars ago exceeds the candle retrace distance AND fewer than the maximum number of positions are open AND (in live trading) the older of the two Fibonacci channel anchor opens < the newer anchor open.
Short: Fast moving average < slow moving average AND previous bar low < the bar before it high AND the absolute difference between the current bar open and the previous bar close exceeds the candle retrace distance AND fewer than the maximum number of positions are open AND (in live trading) the older of the two Fibonacci channel anchor opens > the newer anchor open.
Exit: Long, bar low <= entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR bar high >= entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, bar high >= entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR bar low <= entry price minus the take-profit distance.
