Calculation: Asymmetric stochastic = For each bar, sum the closes across the slowing window; sum the highest highs over the short or long lookback and the lowest lows over the opposite lookback across that window. Divide 100 × (close sum − adjusted low sum) by the adjusted range. If the range is below the sensitivity threshold, use the threshold as the range and lower the low sum by half the shortfall. The short lookback is shorter than the long lookback. After each bar, use short highs and long lows when the prior signal line is above the overbought threshold; use long highs and short lows when it is below the oversold threshold. Initially use short lookbacks for both.
Calculation: Signal line = configured smoothing of the asymmetric stochastic.
Long: Asymmetric stochastic on the last completed signal-timeframe bar > signal line on that bar AND asymmetric stochastic on the preceding completed signal-timeframe bar < signal line on that bar
Short: Asymmetric stochastic on the last completed signal-timeframe bar < signal line on that bar AND asymmetric stochastic on the preceding completed signal-timeframe bar > signal line on that bar
Exit: Short, asymmetric stochastic on the last completed signal-timeframe bar > signal line on that bar AND asymmetric stochastic on the preceding completed signal-timeframe bar < signal line on that bar
Exit: Long, asymmetric stochastic on the last completed signal-timeframe bar < signal line on that bar AND asymmetric stochastic on the preceding completed signal-timeframe bar > signal line on that bar
