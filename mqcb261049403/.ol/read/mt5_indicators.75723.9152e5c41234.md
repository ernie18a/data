Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentile rank of current chart-bar volatility among the current and previous lookback bars, then average it with the preceding smoothed percentile values over the smoothing lookback; use the selected volatility method (ATR, Parkinson high-low volatility, or close-to-close log-return volatility). The current chart bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Volatility regime = classify the smoothed volatility percentile as low, normal, elevated, high, or extreme by the ordered low, elevated, and extreme thresholds, with the highest percentile range classified as extreme; change the regime only after the candidate regime persists for the required consecutive chart bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe volatility percentile = percentile rank of Parkinson high-low volatility on a higher timeframe; calculate the current comparison volatility from completed higher-timeframe bars, while the ranking window includes the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = true unless the chart regime is low and the higher-timeframe volatility percentile exceeds the elevated threshold, or the chart regime is high/extreme and the higher-timeframe volatility percentile is below the low threshold.
Calculation: Volume percentile = percentile rank of the current chart-bar volume among the current and preceding volume-lookback chart bars; the current chart bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Trend direction = +1 when current chart-bar close is above its EMA, -1 when below, and 0 when equal; the current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Normalized trend strength = absolute difference between current chart-bar close and its EMA divided by current ATR; the current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Normalized momentum = current chart-bar close minus the close a short lookback earlier, divided by current ATR; the current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Direction score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of a neutral midpoint, trend direction times a capped weighted normalized trend strength, the sign of normalized momentum times a capped weighted absolute normalized momentum, a weighted volume-percentile adjustment from its midpoint, a positive or negative higher-timeframe-agreement adjustment when that filter is enabled, and a regime adjustment favoring low/normal volatility and penalizing high/extreme volatility.
Long: low volatility regime AND higher-timeframe agreement AND (volume filter disabled OR volume percentile above the low-volume threshold) AND current chart-bar close above its EMA OR direction score at least the upper directional threshold.
Short: low volatility regime AND higher-timeframe agreement AND (volume filter disabled OR volume percentile above the low-volume threshold) AND current chart-bar close below its EMA OR direction score at most the lower directional threshold.
