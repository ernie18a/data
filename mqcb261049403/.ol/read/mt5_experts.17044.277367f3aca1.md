Calculation: Price-position direction = On the configured strategy timeframe, average a 26-period smoothed moving average of median price and a 20-period simple moving average of median price. Find the most recent bar where open is at or below this average and close is above it, or open is at or above it and close is below it; use that bar’s low in the first case and high in the second as the direction level. Direction is up when the current close is above that level and down when it is below.
Calculation: StepUpDown direction = On the configured strategy timeframe, compare a 2-period simple moving average of typical price with a 30-period smoothed moving average of median price. Over the latest 10 bars, find the indices of the fast average’s maximum and minimum, initializing both extrema to the fast average at index 1. Compare the current fast-minus-slow difference with the previous bar’s difference. The direction is up if the maximum index is greater than the minimum index and either the fast average exceeds the minimum or the current difference exceeds the previous difference; it is down if the maximum index is greater than the minimum index and the current difference is below the previous difference, or if the maximum index is less than the minimum index and either the fast average is below the maximum or the current difference is below the previous difference. Use the last applicable direction from the scan; otherwise direction is neutral. Use current and still-forming strategy-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Change from prior completed daily close = (close − prior completed daily close) / prior completed daily close × 100. The comparison uses the current, still-forming strategy-timeframe close and the previous strategy-timeframe close; the daily close is from the last completed daily bar.
Long: Price-position direction is up AND StepUpDown direction is up AND current strategy-timeframe close > current strategy-timeframe open AND current strategy-timeframe low < current 2-period simple moving average of typical price AND that moving average > its value on the previous completed strategy-timeframe bar AND current change from prior completed daily close > previous strategy-timeframe change from prior completed daily close.
Short: Price-position direction is down AND StepUpDown direction is down AND current strategy-timeframe close < current strategy-timeframe open AND current strategy-timeframe high > current 2-period simple moving average of typical price AND that moving average < its value on the previous completed strategy-timeframe bar AND current change from prior completed daily close < previous strategy-timeframe change from prior completed daily close.
Exit: Long, StepUpDown direction is down AND open-position profit > 0.
Exit: Short, StepUpDown direction is up AND open-position profit > 0.
Exit: Long, opposite short-entry signal.
Exit: Short, opposite long-entry signal.
