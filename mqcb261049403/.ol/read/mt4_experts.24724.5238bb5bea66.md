Calculation: Fast moving average = linearly weighted moving average of typical price (high + low + close) over a shorter lookback on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Slow moving average = linearly weighted moving average of typical price (high + low + close) over a longer lookback than the fast moving average on the chart timeframe, using the bar still forming
Long: absolute difference between current bar open and close two completed chart bars ago > retracement distance AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND 14-period RSI on the higher timeframe, using the bar still forming, > 14-period RSI on the higher timeframe, using the last completed bar AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed upper fractal highs > the older one
Short: absolute difference between current bar open and close one completed chart bar ago > retracement distance AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND low one completed chart bar ago < high two completed chart bars ago AND 14-period RSI on the higher timeframe, using the bar still forming, < 14-period RSI on the higher timeframe, using the last completed bar AND the latest of the two most recent confirmed upper fractal highs < the older one
Exit: long, price <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, price >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price - take-profit distance
