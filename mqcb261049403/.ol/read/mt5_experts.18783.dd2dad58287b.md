Calculation: Average range = mean of high − low over the current and 9 preceding bars on the indicator timeframe.
Calculation: Scaled Williams %R = 100 − |Williams %R|, using a 4-bar lookback if any of the 6 tests |close[j+3] − close[j]| ≥ 4.6 × Average range (j = 0…5) is true; otherwise use a risk-dependent lookback. If the lookback high equals the lookback low, carry forward the prior value.
Long: On the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, and either the Scaled Williams %R is above its upper threshold while its most recent preceding value outside the threshold band is below the lower threshold, or that oscillator condition occurred on the immediately preceding bar. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback. Upper and lower thresholds are risk-dependent; evaluate the configured risk values.
Short: On the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast EMA crosses below the slow EMA, and either the Scaled Williams %R is below its lower threshold while its most recent preceding value outside the threshold band is above the upper threshold, or that oscillator condition occurred on the immediately preceding bar. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback. Upper and lower thresholds are risk-dependent; evaluate the configured risk values.
Exit: long, a short-entry signal occurs.
Exit: short, a long-entry signal occurs.
