Calculation: Adaptive channel = On the indicator timeframe, calculate a moving average of close and the standard deviation of close around that average over the same lookback. Initialize the centerline to the moving average; thereafter, let d be the prior centerline minus the current moving average and s the standard deviation, and set the centerline to prior centerline + k × (current moving average − prior centerline), where k = 0 if d² ≤ s², otherwise k = 1 − s²/d². Add the channel half-width to the centerline for the upper boundary and subtract it for the lower boundary; round the upper boundary up and the lower boundary down to the configured price increment. Compare each close with boundaries shifted backward by the configured bar shift.
Long: On the last completed indicator-timeframe bar (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), close > upper boundary AND the previous completed bar's close ≤ its upper boundary.
Short: On the last completed indicator-timeframe bar (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), close < lower boundary AND the previous completed bar's close ≥ its lower boundary.
Exit: long, close < lower boundary on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar.
Exit: short, close > upper boundary on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar.
