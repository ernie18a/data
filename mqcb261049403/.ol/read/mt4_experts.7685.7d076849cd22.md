Calculation: Upper-fractal marker = low of the forming chart bar minus a small point offset when a chart bar’s high is greater than the highs of the two preceding and two following completed chart bars; otherwise 0
Calculation: Lower-fractal marker = high of the forming chart bar plus a small point offset when a chart bar’s low is lower than the lows of the two preceding and two following completed chart bars; otherwise 0
Calculation: Daily pivot = (high + low + close of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar) / 3
Long: (upper-fractal marker > 1 AND volume of the last completed chart bar > volume limit AND volume of the last completed chart bar > volume of the preceding completed chart bar AND chart-timeframe OsMA of the last completed bar < OsMA of the forming bar AND OsMA of the last completed bar >= 0 AND OsMA of the forming bar > 0 AND close of the forming chart bar < daily pivot AND no open positions) OR (long-side open profit < loss threshold AND hedging/locking enabled AND open position count <= 1) OR (hedging enabled AND hedge not already placed AND open position count <= 2 AND delay since last trade elapsed AND (long-side open profit >= profit threshold OR short-side open profit < loss threshold))
Short: (lower-fractal marker > 1 AND volume of the last completed chart bar > volume limit AND volume of the last completed chart bar > the preceding completed chart bar AND chart-timeframe OsMA of the last completed bar > OsMA of the forming bar AND OsMA of the last completed bar <= 0 AND OsMA of the forming bar < 0 AND close of the forming chart bar > daily pivot AND no open positions) OR (short-side open profit < loss threshold AND hedging/locking enabled AND open position count <= 1) OR (hedging enabled AND hedge not already placed AND open position count <= 2 AND delay since last trade elapsed AND (short-side open profit >= profit threshold OR long-side open profit < loss threshold))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
Exit: all positions, trailing stop is enabled AND (long-side open profit < hedge loss limit OR short-side open profit < hedge loss limit)
Exit: long, trailing stop is enabled AND long-side open profit < negative profit threshold AND short-side open profit > profit threshold AND open position count >= 2 AND hedging enabled AND a base-size long position is open
Exit: short, trailing stop is enabled AND short-side open profit < negative profit threshold AND long-side open profit > profit threshold AND open position count >= 2 AND hedging enabled AND a base-size short position is open
Exit: all positions, trailing stop is disabled AND ((total open profit >= profit threshold AND open position count = 1) OR (total open profit > profit threshold AND open position count = 2 AND locking disabled) OR (total open profit >= profit threshold AND open position count = 3 AND locking enabled))
