Calculation: QWMA = weighted average of the current and prior input values, with lag k weighted by (period − k)^speed for k = 0 through period − 1
Calculation: QWMA MACD = short-period QWMA of selected price − long-period QWMA of selected price
Calculation: MACD signal = QWMA of QWMA MACD using a period shorter than the long-period QWMA and longer than the short-period QWMA
Long: QWMA MACD > MACD signal (calculation timeframe is the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; on a higher timeframe, use the bar still forming)
Short: QWMA MACD < MACD signal (calculation timeframe is the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; on a higher timeframe, use the bar still forming)
