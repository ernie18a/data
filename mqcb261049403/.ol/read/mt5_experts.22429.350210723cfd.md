Calculation: Daily channel = open of the first bar of each day at the configured start hour, plus or minus a configured price offset; calculated on the indicator timeframe using completed bars.
Long: On the indicator timeframe, the latest completed bar closes above the upper channel AND the preceding completed bar closes at or below the upper channel.
Short: On the indicator timeframe, the latest completed bar closes below the lower channel OR the preceding completed bar closes below the lower channel and the latest completed bar closes at or above the lower channel.
Exit: Short, latest completed indicator-timeframe bar closes at or above the lower channel.
Exit: Long, latest completed indicator-timeframe bar closes at or below the upper channel.
