Calculation: Previous-day pivot levels = P=(previous calendar day's high+low+close)/3; D=high−low; R1/R2/R3=P+D×(short/medium/long pivot-range ratios); S1/S2/S3=P−D×(short/medium/long pivot-range ratios)
Calculation: Fib levels = the configured percentage points between adjacent pivot levels; use the long-side and short-side levels at each zone as defined below. The outer levels extend beyond R3 and S3 by the configured percentage of the R2–R3 range.
Long: trading is enabled and within the configured start-to-finish window AND (R2 < price < R3 AND price reaches the long level between R2 and R3 OR R1 < price < R2 AND price reaches the long level between R1 and R2 AND long orders are enabled for this zone OR P < price < R1 AND price reaches the long level between P and R1 OR S1 < price < P AND price reaches the long level between S1 and P OR S2 < price < S1 AND price reaches the long level between S1 and S2 AND long orders are enabled for this zone OR S3 < price < S2 AND price reaches the long level between S2 and S3 OR price > R3 AND price reaches the outer level above R3)
Short: trading is enabled and within the configured start-to-finish window AND (R2 < price < R3 AND price reaches the short level between R2 and R3 OR R1 < price < R2 AND price reaches the short level between R1 and R2 AND short orders are enabled for this zone OR P < price < R1 AND price reaches the short level between P and R1 OR S1 < price < P AND price reaches the short level between S1 and P OR S2 < price < S1 AND price reaches the short level between S1 and S2 AND short orders are enabled for this zone OR S3 < price < S2 AND price reaches the short level between S2 and S3 OR price < S3 AND price reaches the outer level below S3)
Exit: long, price reaches the order's take-profit or stop-loss, or datetime is later than the configured close-all time
Exit: short, price reaches the order's take-profit or stop-loss, or datetime is later than the configured close-all time
