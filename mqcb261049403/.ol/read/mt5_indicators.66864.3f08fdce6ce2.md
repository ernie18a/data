Calculation: Estimated buy and sell volume = for a bar with positive range, assign the candle body’s volume to buys if close > open or sells if close < open; split the remaining wick volume equally between buys and sells. For a doji, split volume equally. Use volume, substituting 1 when it is zero.
Calculation: Net Delta = EMA(estimated buy volume) − EMA(estimated sell volume).
Long: Price makes a lower low AND Net Delta makes a higher low.
Short: Price makes a higher high AND Net Delta makes a lower high.
