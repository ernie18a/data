Calculation: Percentage distance from the forming daily high = (forming daily close / forming daily high − 1) × 100; use the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Percentage distance from the forming daily low = (forming daily close / forming daily low − 1) × 100; use the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Volume-weighted average entry price = sum(entry price × position volume) / sum(position volume) for positions on the same side.
Long: No open short positions AND ((no open long positions AND percentage distance from the forming daily high ≥ −threshold AND the last completed chart-timeframe bar closed above its open) OR (at least one open long position AND current ask < the lowest long entry price − (base spacing + spacing increment × number of open symbol positions) + current spread))
Short: No open long positions AND ((no open short positions AND percentage distance from the forming daily low ≤ threshold AND the last completed chart-timeframe bar closed below its open) OR (at least one open short position AND current bid > the highest short entry price + (base spacing + spacing increment × number of open symbol positions) + current spread))
Exit: Long, current price ≥ entry price + current spread + take-profit distance when there is one open long position OR current price ≥ volume-weighted average entry price + profit offset when there are at least two open long positions
Exit: Short, current price ≤ entry price − current spread − take-profit distance when there is one open short position OR current price ≤ volume-weighted average entry price − profit offset when there are at least two open short positions
