Calculation: MACD histogram = the shorter-lookback EMA minus the longer-lookback EMA, minus a moving average of that difference (signal line); the signal line uses a configurable moving-average method.
Long: MACD histogram on the chart timeframe > its previous bar
Short: MACD histogram on the chart timeframe < its previous bar
Long: MACD histogram on the selected timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the bar aligned to the chart bar, which may still be forming) > its previous bar
Short: MACD histogram on the selected timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the bar aligned to the chart bar, which may still be forming) < its previous bar
