Calculation: Fast/slow weighted moving averages = linearly weighted averages of typical price (high + low + close) / 3 on the chart timeframe, both using the same lookback type with the fast lookback shorter than the slow; use the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe weighted moving averages = the same fast and slow linearly weighted averages of typical price on a higher timeframe; use the bar two shifts back (completed), with the fast lookback shorter than the slow.
Calculation: Trigger distance = retracement-bar parameter × minimum price increment.
Long: Open positions < position limit AND fast chart average > slow chart average AND fast higher-timeframe average > slow higher-timeframe average AND low[2] < high[1] AND |current bar open − close[2]| > trigger distance AND MACD main[1] > MACD signal[1] AND MACD main[1] ≠ 0 AND higher-timeframe MACD main[1] > higher-timeframe MACD signal[1] AND higher-timeframe MACD main[1] ≠ 0. Use completed chart bars for chart averages, MACD, and OHLC references; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for higher-timeframe MACD.
Short: Open positions < position limit AND fast chart average < slow chart average AND fast higher-timeframe average < slow higher-timeframe average AND low[1] < high[2] AND |current bar open − close[1]| > trigger distance AND MACD main[1] < MACD signal[1] AND MACD main[1] ≠ 0 AND higher-timeframe MACD main[1] < higher-timeframe MACD signal[1] AND higher-timeframe MACD main[1] ≠ 0. Use completed chart bars for chart averages, MACD, and OHLC references; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for higher-timeframe MACD.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below the active stop-loss OR high reaches or exceeds take-profit; after favorable movement exceeds the trailing-stop distance, raise the stop-loss to the greater of its current level and the highest price since entry minus the trailing-stop distance; after favorable movement exceeds the break-even trigger, move the stop-loss to entry price plus the break-even offset when that raises it.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds the active stop-loss OR low reaches or falls below take-profit; after favorable movement exceeds the trailing-stop distance, lower the stop-loss to the lesser of its current level and the lowest price since entry plus the trailing-stop distance; after favorable movement exceeds the break-even trigger, move the stop-loss to entry price minus the break-even offset when that lowers it.
