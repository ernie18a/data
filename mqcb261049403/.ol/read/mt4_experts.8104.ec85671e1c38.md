Calculation: Fast moving average = short-term EMA of close on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Slow moving average = long-term, shifted SMA of open on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Crossover price = average of the fast and slow moving-average values on the two completed bars spanning the crossover
Calculation: Rolling high/low = highest high/lowest low across the configured recent completed chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Upper/lower envelope = SMA of close shifted above/below its average by the configured percentage; use the slower and faster higher-timeframe envelopes on their current forming bars
Long: fast moving average crossed above slow moving average on one of the two most recent completed bars AND current ask is at least the crossover price plus the configured trigger distance AND the cross is within the configured entry window AND RSI of typical price on the fixed RSI timeframe, using its current forming bar, is above its upper threshold AND current ask is above the rolling high AND current ask is above both upper envelopes AND blackout is inactive AND no trade is open or pending
Short: fast moving average crossed below slow moving average on one of the two most recent completed bars AND current bid is at most the crossover price minus the configured trigger distance AND the cross is within the configured entry window AND RSI of typical price on the fixed RSI timeframe, using its current forming bar, is below its lower threshold AND current bid is below the rolling low AND current bid is below both lower envelopes AND blackout is inactive AND no trade is open or pending
Exit: long, current price is at or below the initial stop (low of the configured earlier completed chart-timeframe bar) or the ratcheted trailing stop, OR at or above the take-profit price
Exit: short, current price is at or above the initial stop (high of the configured earlier completed chart-timeframe bar) or the ratcheted trailing stop, OR at or below the take-profit price
