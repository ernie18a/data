Calculation: Confirmed swing high = A bar whose high is at least the highs of the preceding swing-confirmation lookback bars and strictly greater than the highs of the following bars of the same lookback; it becomes available after those following bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = A bar whose low is at most the lows of the preceding swing-confirmation lookback bars and strictly lower than the lows of the following bars of the same lookback; it becomes available after those following bars complete.
Calculation: Resistance and support levels = Confirmed swing highs and lows, respectively, stored newest first; discard the oldest levels when their age exceeds the barrier-memory lookback, while retaining at least one level.
Calculation: Zone thickness = Recent rolling high-to-low range multiplied by the zone-tolerance fraction.
Calculation: Session VWAP = Cumulative typical price, (high + low + close) / 3, weighted by volume since the start of the calendar day, divided by cumulative volume since that start.
Calculation: SMA = Mean of closing prices over its lookback.
Calculation: SMMA = Seed with the mean closing price over its lookback, then update as (previous SMMA × (lookback − 1) + current close) / lookback.
Long: On a completed bar, resistance-level count ≥ required-touch count AND close > open AND close > the highest high of the preceding swing-confirmation lookback bars AND open ≤ the selected resistance barrier AND close > the selected confirmation filter (session VWAP, SMA, or SMMA; omit this condition when the filter is disabled) AND the scan of newest-first resistance levels below close passes more levels than the required-touch count AND at least the required-touch count of those scanned levels lie within one zone thickness below the selected resistance barrier AND the preceding-bar high ceiling < the selected resistance barrier. The selected resistance barrier is the highest price among the scanned levels; stop the scan at the first level at or above close.
Short: On a completed bar, support-level count ≥ required-touch count AND close < open AND close < the lowest low of the preceding swing-confirmation lookback bars AND open ≥ the selected support barrier AND close < the selected confirmation filter (session VWAP, SMA, or SMMA; omit this condition when the filter is disabled) AND the scan of newest-first support levels above close passes more levels than the required-touch count AND at least the required-touch count of those scanned levels lie within one zone thickness above the selected support barrier AND the preceding-bar low floor > the selected support barrier. The selected support barrier is the lowest price among the scanned levels; stop the scan at the first level at or below close.
