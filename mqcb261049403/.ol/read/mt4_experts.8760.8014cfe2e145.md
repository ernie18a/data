Long: At least one entry signal is enabled, and every enabled reproducible entry signal is long: RSI on the configured chart or higher timeframe rises between the last two completed bars and remains below its upper threshold; the moving average on a higher timeframe rises between the last two completed bars and is above the current chart bar's still-forming close.
Short: At least one entry signal is enabled, and every enabled reproducible entry signal is short: RSI on the configured chart or higher timeframe falls between the last two completed bars and remains above its lower threshold; the moving average on a higher timeframe falls between the last two completed bars and is below the current chart bar's still-forming close.
Exit: long, an enabled exit signal indicates short: RSI falls between the last two completed bars and is above its lower threshold, or the higher-timeframe moving average falls between the last two completed bars and is below the current chart bar's still-forming close; or price reaches the stop-loss or take-profit level measured from entry price.
Exit: short, an enabled exit signal indicates long: RSI rises between the last two completed bars and is below its upper threshold, or the higher-timeframe moving average rises between the last two completed bars and is above the current chart bar's still-forming close; or price reaches the stop-loss or take-profit level measured from entry price.
