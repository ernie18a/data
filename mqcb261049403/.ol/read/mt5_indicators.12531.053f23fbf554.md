For each bar, the absolute-change value is |high − high two bars ago| + |low − low two bars ago|. The conditional value is high − high two bars ago + low − low two bars ago when both of these are false: (high two bars ago < close seven bars ago AND high two bars ago < close eight bars ago AND high < high five bars ago AND high < high six bars ago); (low two bars ago > close seven bars ago AND low two bars ago > close eight bars ago AND low > low five bars ago AND low > low six bars ago). Otherwise it is zero.
