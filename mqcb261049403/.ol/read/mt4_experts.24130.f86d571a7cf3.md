Calculation: Average bar range = moving average of high − low over a long lookback of completed chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Maximum prior candle body = maximum absolute close − open over a recent lookback of completed chart-timeframe bars before the last completed bar
Long: (last completed close > last completed open AND last completed ATR > average bar range × configured multiple) OR (close two bars ago < both Ichimoku Senkou Span A and B calculated on the last completed chart bar AND last completed close > both spans) OR (last completed close > last completed open AND absolute last completed close − open > maximum prior candle body × configured multiple)
Short: (last completed close < last completed open AND last completed ATR > average bar range × configured multiple) OR (close two bars ago > both Ichimoku Senkou Span A and B calculated on the last completed chart bar AND last completed close < both spans) OR (last completed close < last completed open AND absolute last completed close − open > maximum prior candle body × configured multiple)
Exit: long, low reaches configured stop-loss below entry price OR close two bars ago > Parabolic SAR two bars ago AND last completed close < Parabolic SAR on the last completed bar
Exit: short, high reaches configured stop-loss above entry price OR close two bars ago < Parabolic SAR two bars ago AND last completed close > Parabolic SAR on the last completed bar
