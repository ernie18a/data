Calculation: Price pivot high/low = a local high/low over a configurable symmetric span of bars; it must be strictly higher/lower than bars to its left and may tie bars to its right. Use either close or high/low as the pivot source.
Calculation: VWMACD = shorter-lookback volume-weighted moving average of close minus longer-lookback volume-weighted moving average of close; use volume.
Long: For at least the configured minimum number of oscillator-divergence matches on the signal bar, an enabled oscillator has either (a) regular bullish divergence: oscillator at the endpoint > oscillator at a prior price pivot low AND endpoint price probe < pivot low price, or (b) hidden bullish divergence: oscillator at endpoint < oscillator at a prior price pivot low AND endpoint price probe > pivot low price; AND no intervening bar crosses the straight line between endpoints on either that oscillator or close; AND, when next-bar confirmation is enabled, the following completed bar has oscillator > endpoint oscillator OR close > endpoint close. Search the newest qualifying pivot lows among the configured number of recent lows within the configured lookback, excluding pivots inside the configured minimum separation. The endpoint price probe is close for close-based pivots, otherwise low.
Short: For at least the configured minimum number of oscillator-divergence matches on the signal bar, an enabled oscillator has either (a) regular bearish divergence: oscillator at the endpoint < oscillator at a prior price pivot high AND endpoint price probe > pivot high price, or (b) hidden bearish divergence: oscillator at endpoint > oscillator at a prior price pivot high AND endpoint price probe < pivot high price; AND no intervening bar crosses the straight line between endpoints on either that oscillator or close; AND, when next-bar confirmation is enabled, the following completed bar has oscillator < endpoint oscillator OR close < endpoint close. Search the newest qualifying pivot highs among the configured number of recent highs within the configured lookback, excluding pivots inside the configured minimum separation. The endpoint price probe is close for close-based pivots, otherwise high.
