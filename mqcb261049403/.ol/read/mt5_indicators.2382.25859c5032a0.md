Calculation: SuperTrend = midpoint (high + low) / 2 with upper and lower bands offset by a multiplier times ATR; trend switches up when close exceeds the previous upper band and down when close falls below the previous lower band, otherwise it retains the prior trend. In an uptrend, trail the lower band upward using the greater of its current basic value and previous value; in a downtrend, trail the upper band downward using the lesser of its current basic value and previous value. The SuperTrend value is the lower band in an uptrend and the upper band in a downtrend. Calculate on the chart timeframe or another timeframe as specified below.
Long: low < chart-timeframe SuperTrend AND close > chart-timeframe SuperTrend OR close > SuperTrend on a selected higher or lower timeframe's still-forming bar OR (close = that SuperTrend AND previous chart-timeframe close > SuperTrend on the previous bar of that timeframe)
Short: high > chart-timeframe SuperTrend AND close < chart-timeframe SuperTrend OR close < SuperTrend on a selected higher or lower timeframe's still-forming bar OR (close = that SuperTrend AND previous chart-timeframe close < SuperTrend on the previous bar of that timeframe)
