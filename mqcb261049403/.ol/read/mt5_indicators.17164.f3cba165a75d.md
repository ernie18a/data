Calculation: Step stochastic pair = On the chart timeframe, or on a higher configured timeframe using its still-forming bar, calculate a filtered selected price and high/low series (the high/low may be unfiltered). For each bar, average over the ATR lookback the ranges max(current high, prior low) − min(current low, prior high); find the minimum and maximum of this average over the rolling window. Set minimum, middle, and maximum step sizes to fast multiplier × minimum ATR, fast multiplier × slow multiplier × (minimum ATR + maximum ATR) / 2, and fast multiplier × maximum ATR. For each step size independently, form candidate bands at price ± 2 × step size. Carry forward its trend state, changing it to up when price exceeds the prior upper band and down when price falls below the prior lower band. In an up state, prevent the lower band from decreasing; in a down state, prevent the upper band from increasing. Its step line is the lower band + step size when up, or upper band − step size when down. The fast and slow stochastic lines are respectively 100 × (minimum-step line − (maximum-step line − maximum step size)) / (2 × maximum step size) and the same expression using the middle-step line. Set direction bullish when fast > slow, bearish when fast < slow, and otherwise retain the prior direction.
Long: direction = bullish
Short: direction = bearish
