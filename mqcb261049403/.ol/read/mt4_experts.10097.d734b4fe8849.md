Calculation: Reference level = current bid rounded to two decimal places, shifted by a configurable offset; use configurable distance thresholds around this level.
Calculation: RAVI = 100 × (short-period SMA of open − long-period SMA of open) / long-period SMA of open; the short period is shorter than the long period.
Calculation: Long score = add configurable positive weights when (a) higher-timeframe RAVI is below zero, (b) either of the two most recent completed higher-timeframe highs exceeds the reference level by a large distance, or (c) current bid is below the reference level, the last completed lower-timeframe close is above it, and the lows of the current and two most recent higher-timeframe bars stay above their specified level bounds. Subtract a configurable penalty for each of the recent lower-timeframe bars whose high exceeds the upper bound; for each qualifying pattern across the recent lower-timeframe bars where a bar's high minus a bar three bars later's low exceeds a distance threshold and the earlier bar's close is below the later bar's open while current higher-timeframe RAVI is below a negative threshold; if no high in the recent higher-timeframe bars exceeds a second upper bound; and if all of the recent lower-timeframe highs are below a third upper bound.
Calculation: Short score = add configurable positive weights when (a) higher-timeframe RAVI is above zero, (b) either of the two most recent completed higher-timeframe lows is below the reference level by a large distance, or (c) current bid is above the reference level, the last completed lower-timeframe close is below it, and the highs of the current and two most recent higher-timeframe bars stay below their specified level bounds. Subtract a configurable penalty for each of the recent lower-timeframe bars whose low falls below the lower bound; for each qualifying pattern across the recent lower-timeframe bars where a bar's high minus a bar three bars later's low exceeds a distance threshold and the earlier bar's close is above the later bar's open while current higher-timeframe RAVI is above a positive threshold; if no low in the recent higher-timeframe bars falls below a second lower bound; and if all of the recent lower-timeframe lows are above a third lower bound.
Long: Long score >= configurable entry threshold
Short: Short score >= configurable entry threshold
Exit: long, price <= stop-loss level OR price >= take-profit level
Exit: short, price >= stop-loss level OR price <= take-profit level
