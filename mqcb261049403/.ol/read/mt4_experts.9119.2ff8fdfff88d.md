Calculation: Perceptron score = sum, for i=1..4, of (weight_i × difference_i) + threshold offset; each difference is the current-bar LWMA of close minus the LWMA of weighted close at p, then the differences between weighted-close LWMAs at p, 2p, 3p, and 4p. Long, short, and supervisor scores use their own parameters; each score uses one MA period and successive lookback offsets p, 2p, 3p, 4p.
Calculation: Price range retracement levels = over a lookback of completed chart bars, resistance is highest high − 30% of (highest high − lowest low), and support is lowest low + 30% of that range.
Calculation: Moving-average range retracement levels = over a lookback of completed chart bars, find the highest and lowest values of the same-period LWMA of weighted close; resistance is highest − 30% of the range, and support is lowest + 30% of the range.
