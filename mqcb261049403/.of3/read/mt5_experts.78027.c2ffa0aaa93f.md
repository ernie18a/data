Calculation: time-of-day relative tick volume = current chart-bar tick volume / mean(tick volume of the most recent lookback prior trading days at the same time of day); defined only when at least the minimum observation count is available
Long: on the last completed chart bar, its opening day is enabled AND (chart timeframe is at least T1 OR its opening hour is within the configured inclusive start-to-end hour window) AND ((chart timeframe is shorter than T1 AND time-of-day relative tick volume < relative-volume threshold) OR (chart timeframe is T1 or longer AND time-of-day relative tick volume < max(relative-volume threshold, floor threshold))) AND (close - SMA(close, lookback)) / sample standard deviation(close, same lookback) > threshold AND MACD histogram(close, shorter EMA lookback, longer EMA lookback, signal EMA lookback) / ATR(lookback) < threshold
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop multiple × ATR(lookback) from the completed signal bar OR high >= entry price + target multiple × ATR(same lookback) from the completed signal bar OR held chart bars >= maximum hold bars OR current server time-of-day >= configured flat time
