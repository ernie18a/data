Calculation: Currency strength = the mean of each currency’s scores across its configured currency-pair basket; for each pair, score the base currency with the bid-ratio score and the quote currency with the maximum score minus that score. The bid ratio is (currency-pair bid − window low) / (window high − window low), or zero when the window high equals its low; if the bid is unavailable, use the T1 close at the shift parameter. Map the ratio to the highest score level whose paired ascending cutoff it meets, or zero below the lowest cutoff. With smoothing enabled, compute ratios for nested T1 windows of lengths 1 through the window-length parameter, starting at the shift parameter, and average them; with triangular weighting enabled, weight window k by (window-length + 1 − k) / (window-length × (window-length + 1) / 2), otherwise weight equally. With smoothing disabled, use the ratio for one window of the full window length. Recalculate on each timer evaluation; shift zero includes the still-forming T1 bar, and positive shifts use completed bars.
Long: base-currency strength > upper threshold AND quote-currency strength < lower threshold
Short: base-currency strength < lower threshold AND quote-currency strength > upper threshold
Calculation: Stop or target distance(multiplier) = multiplier × ATR(ATR lookback, T2, two bars ago) in ATR mode, or multiplier × instrument point size in point mode.
Exit: long, Takeprofit > 0 AND high >= entry price + Stop or target distance(Takeprofit) OR Stoploss > 0 AND low <= entry price − Stop or target distance(Stoploss)
Exit: short, Takeprofit > 0 AND low <= entry price − Stop or target distance(Takeprofit) OR Stoploss > 0 AND high >= entry price + Stop or target distance(Stoploss)
