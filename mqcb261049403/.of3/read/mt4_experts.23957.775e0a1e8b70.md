Calculation: Fractal breakout direction = scan chart bars from the fractal scan-lookback bar toward the forming bar; at each bar, return up if an upper Fractal exists, the current close is at or above it, and at least one of the preceding fractal comparison-window highs is below it; otherwise return down if a lower Fractal exists, the current close is at or below it, and at least one of the preceding fractal comparison-window lows is above it; if both Fractals exist and neither condition returns a direction, return none; otherwise continue scanning
Calculation: Renko direction = scan chart bars from the retracement-lookback bar toward the forming bar; at each bar, return up if current close minus that bar's low is at least the threshold, otherwise return down if that bar's high minus current close is at least the threshold, otherwise continue; the threshold is ATR with a separate lookback parameter on the forming chart bar when ATR mode is enabled, or box-size parameter × pip unit when it is disabled
Calculation: pip unit = tick size × 10 for instruments quoted with 3 or 5 decimal digits; otherwise tick size
Long: Alligator Lips (SMMA of median price, shortest lookback and smallest forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) > Alligator Teeth (SMMA of median price, intermediate lookback and forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND Alligator Teeth > Alligator Jaw (SMMA of median price, longest lookback and largest forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND Fractal breakout direction = up AND Renko direction = up
Short: Alligator Lips (SMMA of median price, shortest lookback and smallest forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) < Alligator Teeth (SMMA of median price, intermediate lookback and forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND Alligator Teeth < Alligator Jaw (SMMA of median price, longest lookback and largest forward-shift parameter, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND Fractal breakout direction = down AND Renko direction = down
Exit: long, all six EMAs of typical price with separate, strictly increasing short-group lookback parameters are equal (T1 forming bar values sampled on chart bars) OR all six EMAs of typical price with separate, strictly increasing long-group lookback parameters are equal (T1 forming bar values sampled on chart bars) OR close of the last completed chart bar <= lower Bollinger Band (separate lookback and deviation parameters, last completed chart bar)
Exit: short, all six EMAs of typical price with separate, strictly increasing short-group lookback parameters are equal (T1 forming bar values sampled on chart bars) OR all six EMAs of typical price with separate, strictly increasing long-group lookback parameters are equal (T1 forming bar values sampled on chart bars) OR close of the last completed chart bar <= lower Bollinger Band (separate lookback and deviation parameters, last completed chart bar)
