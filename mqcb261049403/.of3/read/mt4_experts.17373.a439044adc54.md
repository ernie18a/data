Long: ((prior-bar direction is selected AND close[1] > open[1]) OR (EMA alignment is selected AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, last completed chart bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, last completed chart bar) AND (middle EMA is disabled OR EMA(close, intermediate lookback, last completed chart bar) < EMA(close, shorter lookback, last completed chart bar)))) AND (volume lookback is disabled OR SMA(tick volume, volume lookback, chart bars including the forming bar) >= minimum average volume) AND current ask <= upper price limit AND current bid >= lower price limit, OR current ask < lower price limit, OR a short position exists AND short entry price - current bid >= grid distance for the current grid level, capped at the final configured grid level
Short: ((prior-bar direction is selected AND close[1] < open[1]) OR (EMA alignment is selected AND EMA(close, shorter lookback, last completed chart bar) < EMA(close, longer lookback, last completed chart bar) AND (middle EMA is disabled OR EMA(close, intermediate lookback, last completed chart bar) > EMA(close, shorter lookback, last completed chart bar)))) AND (volume lookback is disabled OR SMA(tick volume, volume lookback, chart bars including the forming bar) >= minimum average volume) AND current ask <= upper price limit AND current bid >= lower price limit, OR current bid > upper price limit, OR a long position exists AND current ask - long entry price >= grid distance for the current grid level, capped at the final configured grid level
Calculation: Basket profit = integer part of combined open profit in points
Calculation: Peak basket profit = highest basket profit since the current basket began
Exit: long or short, basket profit > trailing-distance parameter - trailing-step parameter AND peak basket profit - basket profit >= trailing-step parameter
