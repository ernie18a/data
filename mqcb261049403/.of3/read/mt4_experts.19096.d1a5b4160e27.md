Calculation: Timing = clamp(center × (1 + D/A), 0, 2 × center), or center when A ≤ tolerance or warm-up is incomplete; P = price scale × (high + low + close)/3 on T1, delta = P − P[1], C(x) = (1 + lead) × EMA(x, alpha) − lead × EMA(EMA(x, alpha), alpha), D = C(C(C(delta))), A = C(C(C(abs(delta)))); use the same alpha and lead in all stages and for D and A, recompute on each chart update over the trailing lookback of T1 bars from oldest to newest with zero-initialized EMA states, use the forming T1 bar for [0] and completed T1 bars for [1] and [2], and return center until the six-step warm-up completes, restarting it after five consecutive zero price changes
Calculation: Volatility = ((abs(high[1] − low[0]) + abs(high[0] − low[1]) + abs(close[1] − close[0])) / point) × volume[0] / elapsed seconds since the forming T1 bar opened; recompute on each chart update using the forming T1 bar [0] and completed T1 bar [1]
Long: Timing[1] ≤ Timing[2] AND Timing[0] > Timing[1] AND elapsed seconds since the forming T1 bar opened > 0 AND Volatility > volatility threshold AND spread ≤ spread limit AND StdDev(close, deviation lookback, SMA, T1, forming bar) ≤ deviation limit
Short: Timing[1] ≥ Timing[2] AND Timing[0] < Timing[1] AND elapsed seconds since the forming T1 bar opened > 0 AND Volatility > volatility threshold AND spread ≤ spread limit AND StdDev(close, deviation lookback, SMA, T1, forming bar) ≤ deviation limit
Calculation: Long stop = for an initial entry, entry price − (entry spread + 2 × trail distance) × point; for a profit-based add-on entry, unset until rounded open profit in points reaches trail distance + truncated commission + spread limit, then set to bid − trail distance × point; thereafter raise only when the new stop is at least one point higher
Calculation: Short stop = for an initial entry, entry price + (entry spread + 2 × trail distance) × point; for a profit-based add-on entry, unset until rounded open profit in points reaches trail distance + truncated commission + spread limit, then set to ask + trail distance × point; thereafter lower only when the new stop is at least one point lower
Exit: Long, low ≤ Long stop
Exit: Short, high ≥ Short stop
