Calculation: MA order state = down only when the moving averages of close, weighted, typical, median, and open prices, in that order, are strictly increasing in value from close to open; up otherwise. Use one MA method and lookback for all five prices, on chart bars using the still-forming T1 bar.
Calculation: Perceptron score = sum of four components multiplied by their respective weight inputs minus a shared weight-center parameter. A shared component-mode parameter selects close[nᵢ] − open[nᵢ₊₁], Stochastic(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, EMA, Low/High, main, T1)[nᵢ] − a stochastic-center parameter, or CCI(applied-price parameter, lookback, T1)[nᵢ]. Use five separately parameterized, increasing bar offsets n₁ through n₅. Evaluate on chart bars using completed T1 bars.
Calculation: Accelerator direction score = warp × AC[n₁] + scale × (deviation − deviation-center parameter) × AC[n₂] + (amplitude − amplitude-center parameter) × AC[n₃] + scale × distortion × AC[n₄]. Warp, deviation, amplitude, distortion, and the four increasing bar offsets are separate parameters. Evaluate on chart bars using completed T1 bars.
Calculation: CCI trail level = initialize to the positive CCI entry threshold minus one trail increment for a long, or plus one trail increment for a short; scan completed T1 bars backward from the latest completed bar through the latest bar of the most recent entry signal, raising the level by one trail increment for each long-side bar where CCI(Typical, lookback, T1) > level + activation-multiple parameter × trail increment, or lowering it by one trail increment for each short-side bar where CCI(Typical, lookback, T1) < level − activation-multiple parameter × trail increment.
