Calculation: MACD contribution(T1) = MACD weight(T1) × (sign(MACD main(MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback, MACD applied price, T1, last completed bar)) + sign(MACD main(...) - MACD signal(MACD fast lookback, MACD slow lookback, MACD signal lookback, MACD applied price, T1, last completed bar)))/2
Calculation: AO contribution(T1) = AO weight(T1) × (sign(AO(T1, last completed bar)) + sign(AO(T1, last completed bar) - AO(T1, preceding completed bar)))/2
Calculation: OsMA contribution(T1) = OsMA weight(T1) × sign(OsMA(OsMA fast lookback, OsMA slow lookback, OsMA signal lookback, OsMA applied price, T1, last completed bar))
Calculation: WPR contribution(T1) = WPR weight(T1) × (−1 if WPR(WPR lookback, T1, last completed bar) < overbought level and WPR(WPR lookback, T1, preceding completed bar) >= overbought level; otherwise +1 if WPR(WPR lookback, T1, last completed bar) > oversold level and WPR(WPR lookback, T1, preceding completed bar) <= oversold level; otherwise 0)
Calculation: Stochastic contribution(T1) = stochastic weight(T1) × (−1 if %K(last completed T1 bar) < overbought level and %K(preceding completed T1 bar) >= overbought level; otherwise +1 if %K(last completed T1 bar) > oversold level and %K(preceding completed T1 bar) <= oversold level; otherwise 0; plus sign(%K(last completed T1 bar) - %D(last completed T1 bar)))/2, where %K and %D use separate %K lookback, %D lookback, slowing, method, and applied price parameters
Calculation: total signal = sum of MACD, AO, OsMA, WPR, and stochastic contributions over every configured indicator/timeframe pair; evaluated on chart bars using each T1's last completed bar
Calculation: active total signal = latest nonzero total signal since position entry
Long: total signal > 0 AND no long position is open
Short: total signal < 0 AND no short position is open
Exit: long, (dynamic stop/target mode disabled AND ((stop-loss distance enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance))) OR (dynamic stop/target mode enabled AND ((stop-loss distance enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance × active total signal × signal multiplier) OR (take-profit distance enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance × active total signal × signal multiplier)))
Exit: short, (dynamic stop/target mode disabled AND ((stop-loss distance enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance))) OR (dynamic stop/target mode enabled AND ((stop-loss distance enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance × (−active total signal) × signal multiplier) OR (take-profit distance enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance × (−active total signal) × signal multiplier)))
