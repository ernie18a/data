Calculation: Histogram = selected Stochastic main(high, low, close, lookback, separate smoothing lookbacks) centered on its midpoint; CCI(close, lookback); RSI(close, lookback) centered on its midpoint; MACD main(close, shorter and longer lookbacks); Momentum(close, lookback) centered on its neutral level; Williams %R(high, low, close, lookback) centered on its midpoint; ADX(high, low, close, lookback); or DeMarker(high, low, lookback) centered on its midpoint, using the T1 bar mapped to each chart bar, which may still be forming
Calculation: SqueezeRatio = Bollinger deviation parameter × StdDev(close, Bollinger lookback) / (Keltner factor parameter × ATR(Keltner lookback)), using the T1 bar mapped to each chart bar, which may still be forming
Long: (Histogram above its neutral level AND SqueezeRatio at least the squeeze threshold) OR (zero-cross alerts enabled AND Histogram crosses above the alert-above level on the configured current or previous completed T1 bar)
Short: (Histogram below its neutral level AND SqueezeRatio at least the squeeze threshold) OR (zero-cross alerts enabled AND Histogram crosses below the alert-below level on the configured current or previous completed T1 bar)
