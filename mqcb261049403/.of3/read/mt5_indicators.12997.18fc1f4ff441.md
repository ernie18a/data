Calculation: Smoothed weighted score = the configured smoother, using its method, length, and phase, of the sum of seven terms: MA weight × sign(close − MA(close, MA lookback)); MACD weight × sign(MACD(fast, slow, signal lookbacks)[0] − MACD(fast, slow, signal lookbacks)[1]); OsMA weight × sign(OsMA(fast, slow, signal lookbacks)[0] − OsMA threshold); CCI weight × sign(CCI(CCI lookback)[0] − CCI threshold); Momentum weight × sign(Momentum(Momentum lookback)[0] − Momentum threshold); RSI weight × sign(RSI(RSI lookback)[0] − RSI threshold); ADX weight × sign(+DI(ADX lookback)[0] − −DI(ADX lookback)[0]). Each sign is +1 for a positive difference, −1 for a negative difference, and 0 for equality; each term is zero unless its weight is positive. The seven weights are separate parameters; MACD and OsMA have separate fast, slow, and signal lookbacks, with fast shorter than slow. Compute on T1 bars; the wrapper samples the configured signal-bar offset on chart bars, where offset zero uses the still-forming T1 bar and a positive offset uses a completed bar.
Calculation: Latest turn direction = direction of the most recent turn in the smoothed weighted score on the selected T1 bar: a trough, where score[2] > score[1] AND score[1] < score[0], is long; a peak, where score[2] < score[1] AND score[1] > score[0], is short. The direction persists until the opposite turn.
Long: Latest turn direction is long
Short: Latest turn direction is short
