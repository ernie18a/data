Long: On chart bars, CCI(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) < -threshold AND MACD main crosses above MACD signal between the second-last and last completed T2 bars AND MACD main on the last completed T2 bar < 0
Short: On chart bars, CCI(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) > threshold AND MACD main crosses below MACD signal between the second-last and last completed T2 bars AND MACD main on the last completed T2 bar > 0
Exit: long, current price - breakeven distance > entry price AND entry price > stop-loss price AND (close(T2, last completed bar) - open(T2, last completed bar) > candle-body factor * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar) OR (MACD main(T2, last completed bar) < MACD main(T2, 2 bars ago) AND current price - entry price > profit threshold))
Exit: short, current price + breakeven distance < entry price AND entry price < stop-loss price AND (open(T2, last completed bar) - close(T2, last completed bar) > candle-body factor * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar) OR (MACD main(T2, last completed bar) > MACD main(T2, 2 bars ago) AND entry price - current price > profit threshold))
