Calculation: Chande Exit bands = (upper, lower), where upper = Highest(high, range lookback bars beginning shift bars ago) − ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier, and lower = Lowest(low, same range window) + ATR(high, low, close, ATR lookback) × ATR multiplier
Calculation: Chande Exit arrows = computed chronologically on T1 bars. Initialize direction to neutral and prior first and second rails to zero. When direction is nonnegative, set the first and second rails to (lower, upper) if close < upper, otherwise (upper, lower); on historical bars, change direction to negative if close < upper. When direction is negative, set the rails to (upper, lower) if close > lower, otherwise (lower, upper); on historical bars, change direction to positive if close > lower. Do not change direction on the forming bar. Buy arrow when prior second rail ≤ prior first rail and current second rail > current first rail; sell arrow when prior second rail ≥ prior first rail and current second rail < current first rail. Update prior rails after each bar. Read the arrow on the T1 bar selected by the signal-bar offset, which may be forming or completed according to that offset.
Long: long-entry permission is enabled AND a buy arrow occurs on the T1 bar selected by the signal-bar offset
Short: short-entry permission is enabled AND a sell arrow occurs on the T1 bar selected by the signal-bar offset
Exit: long, long-exit permission is enabled AND (a sell arrow occurs on the T1 bar selected by the signal-bar offset OR (no current arrow triggers either enabled exit AND ((long-entry permission and long-exit permission are enabled) OR (short-entry permission and short-exit permission are enabled)) AND a sell arrow occurs on any T1 bar older than the selected signal bar))
Exit: short, short-exit permission is enabled AND (a buy arrow occurs on the T1 bar selected by the signal-bar offset OR (no current arrow triggers either enabled exit AND ((long-entry permission and long-exit permission are enabled) OR (short-entry permission and short-exit permission are enabled)) AND a buy arrow occurs on any T1 bar older than the selected signal bar))
