Long: BuyMAcount > long-count threshold OR RSI(close, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > buy threshold OR Stochastic signal(close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing lookback, SMA, close/close, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > buy threshold OR CCI(typical price, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > buy threshold OR MACD main(close, fast lookback < slow lookback, signal lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > neutral threshold OR Momentum(close, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > neutral threshold
Short: BuyMAcount <= short-count threshold OR RSI(close, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) <= sell threshold OR Stochastic signal(close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing lookback, SMA, close/close, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < sell threshold OR CCI(typical price, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) <= sell threshold OR MACD main(close, fast lookback < slow lookback, signal lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) < neutral threshold OR Momentum(close, lookback, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < neutral threshold
