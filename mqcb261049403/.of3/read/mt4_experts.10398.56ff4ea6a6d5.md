Calculation: M = MACD main(close, fast lookback shorter than slow lookback, signal lookback, T2, current forming bar sampled on chart bars); M[1] is the last completed T2 bar and M[2] the preceding completed T2 bar
Calculation: E = EMA(close, EMA lookback, T1, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: SARvalue = ParabolicSAR(high, low, step, maximum, T3, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: S1 = SMA(close, shortest of five separate ordered lookbacks, T4, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: S2 = SMA(close, next-shortest lookback, T4, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: S3 = SMA(close, middle lookback, T4, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: S4 = SMA(close, next-longest lookback, T4, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: S5 = SMA(close, longest lookback, T4, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: StochK = Stochastic %K(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, SMA smoothing, low/high, T1, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: StochSignal = Stochastic %D(K lookback, D lookback, slowing, SMA smoothing, low/high, T1, current forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Long: MACD mode selected AND SAR mode not selected AND moving-average-group mode not selected AND M > 0 AND M[1] > 0 AND M[2] > 0 AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price > E)
Short: MACD mode selected AND SAR mode not selected AND moving-average-group mode not selected AND M < 0 AND M[1] < 0 AND M[2] < 0 AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price < E)
Exit: long, MACD mode selected AND close-on-transition enabled AND ((M > 0 AND M[1] > 0 AND M[2] < 0) OR (M < 0 AND M[1] < 0 AND M[2] > 0))
Exit: short, MACD mode selected AND close-on-transition enabled AND ((M > 0 AND M[1] > 0 AND M[2] < 0) OR (M < 0 AND M[1] < 0 AND M[2] > 0))
Long: SAR mode selected AND moving-average-group mode not selected AND SARvalue < close(T3, current forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price > E)
Short: SAR mode selected AND moving-average-group mode not selected AND SARvalue > close(T3, current forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price < E)
Long: moving-average-group mode selected AND S1 > S2 AND S2 > S3 AND S3 > S4 AND S4 > S5 AND S1 - S3 > divergence threshold AND (stochastic filter disabled OR StochK - StochSignal > stochastic spread threshold) AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price > E)
Short: moving-average-group mode selected AND S1 < S2 AND S2 < S3 AND S3 < S4 AND S4 < S5 AND S3 - S1 > divergence threshold AND (stochastic filter disabled OR StochSignal - StochK > stochastic spread threshold) AND (EMA restriction disabled OR entry price < E)
