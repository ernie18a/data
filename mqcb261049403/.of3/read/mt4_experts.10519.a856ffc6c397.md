Calculation: average candle range = mean(high - low, lookback, T1, last completed bars)
Calculation: effective minimum distance = configured minimum distance, or average candle range divided by the average-range divisor in average-candle mode
Calculation: trailing stop = for long, the greater of the initial stop and the highest close since activation minus trailing distance; for short, the lesser of the initial stop and the lowest close since activation plus trailing distance; activation occurs when close - entry price exceeds trailing distance
Long: high(T1, current forming bar) - open(T1, current forming bar) <= opposite-move allowance AND effective minimum distance <= close - open(T1, current forming bar) <= effective minimum distance * entry-distance multiplier
Short: high(T1, current forming bar) - open(T1, current forming bar) > opposite-move allowance AND open(T1, current forming bar) - low(T1, current forming bar) <= opposite-move allowance AND effective minimum distance <= open(T1, current forming bar) - close <= effective minimum distance * entry-distance multiplier
Exit: long, close >= open(T1, bars ago equal to the position's age in T2 bars) OR close < entry price - loss-distance multiplier * max(average candle range, effective minimum distance) while the position's age in T2 bars is zero OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + max(take-profit distance, minimum permitted stop distance) OR low <= trailing stop AND trailing distance is enabled AND (virtual trailing is off OR close - entry price > minimum profit)
Exit: short, close <= open(T1, bars ago equal to the position's age in T2 bars) OR close - entry price > loss-distance multiplier * max(average candle range, effective minimum distance) while the position's age in T2 bars is zero OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - max(take-profit distance, minimum permitted stop distance) OR high >= trailing stop AND trailing distance is enabled AND (virtual trailing is off OR entry price - close > minimum profit) OR position age in T2 bars >= lifetime
