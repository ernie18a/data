Calculation: RangeHigh = Highest(high) over chart bars in the current server day with hour(datetime) >= session-start-hour and hour(datetime) < session-end-hour; lock after session end
Calculation: RangeLow = Lowest(low) over chart bars in the current server day with hour(datetime) >= session-start-hour and hour(datetime) < session-end-hour; lock after session end
Calculation: RangeATR = ATR(ATR-lookback) on the chart bar before the first chart bar with hour(datetime) >= session-end-hour
Calculation: RangeWidth = RangeHigh - RangeLow
Calculation: EntryPrice = open of the chart bar after the confirmation bar
Calculation: LongStop = low of the sweep bar - stop-buffer-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) on the confirmation bar
Calculation: LongRisk = EntryPrice - LongStop
Calculation: LongTarget = EntryPrice + reward-ratio parameter × LongRisk
Calculation: ShortStop = high of the sweep bar + stop-buffer-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) on the confirmation bar
Calculation: ShortRisk = ShortStop - EntryPrice
Calculation: ShortTarget = EntryPrice - reward-ratio parameter × ShortRisk
Long: a valid range exists (session contains at least minimum-range-bars chart bars, RangeATR > 0, and minimum-range-ATR parameter × RangeATR <= RangeWidth <= maximum-range-ATR parameter × RangeATR) AND a closed chart bar in the trade window has low < RangeLow - minimum-sweep-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) and close > RangeLow, while the low side has not previously been swept or closed through that day AND no other sweep is pending AND the number of confirmed signals that day is below the maximum-signals-per-day parameter AND any previous simulated trade's exit bar precedes this bar AND, if enabled, close(T1, last completed bar as of the sweep bar close) > EMA(close, trend-EMA-lookback, T1, last completed bar as of the sweep bar close) AND within the confirmation-bars parameter subsequent closed chart bars, before hour(datetime) reaches the trade-end-hour parameter, a bar closes above the sweep-bar high with no intervening close below the sweep-bar low AND minimum-stop-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) <= LongRisk <= maximum-stop-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) on the confirmation bar AND no unconsumed upper-side sweep on the same bar passes its optional trend filter
Short: a valid range exists (session contains at least minimum-range-bars chart bars, RangeATR > 0, and minimum-range-ATR parameter × RangeATR <= RangeWidth <= maximum-range-ATR parameter × RangeATR) AND a closed chart bar in the trade window has high > RangeHigh + minimum-sweep-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) and close < RangeHigh, while the high side has not previously been swept or closed through that day AND no other sweep is pending AND the number of confirmed signals that day is below the maximum-signals-per-day parameter AND any previous simulated trade's exit bar precedes this bar AND, if enabled, close(T1, last completed bar as of the sweep bar close) <= EMA(close, trend-EMA-lookback, T1, last completed bar as of the sweep bar close) AND within the confirmation-bars parameter subsequent closed chart bars, before hour(datetime) reaches the trade-end-hour parameter, a bar closes below the sweep-bar low with no intervening close above the sweep-bar high AND minimum-stop-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) <= ShortRisk <= maximum-stop-ATR parameter × ATR(ATR-lookback) on the confirmation bar
Exit: long, (after the entry bar AND (server day differs from the entry day OR hour(datetime) >= day-close-hour parameter)) OR ((entry bar OR (same server day as entry AND hour(datetime) < day-close-hour parameter)) AND (low <= LongStop OR (low > LongStop AND high >= LongTarget)))
Exit: short, (after the entry bar AND (server day differs from the entry day OR hour(datetime) >= day-close-hour parameter)) OR ((entry bar OR (same server day as entry AND hour(datetime) < day-close-hour parameter)) AND (high >= ShortStop OR (high < ShortStop AND low <= ShortTarget)))
