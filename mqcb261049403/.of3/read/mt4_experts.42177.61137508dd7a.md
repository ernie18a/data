Calculation: first-order cumulative closed pips = sum(abs(exit price - entry price) / pip size) for historical order1 trades and the first untagged trade encountered scanning history backward
Calculation: grid break-even price = (sum(long entry price * lot size) - sum(short entry price * lot size)) / (sum(long lot size) - sum(short lot size))
Calculation: grid target distance = the total-profit target parameter for the current order count / current order count
Long: (no open first order AND the latest closed first order was long and closed at take profit AND first-order cumulative closed pips < first-order cumulative target AND abs(close - latest first-order take-profit price) >= renewal offset) OR (an open long order numbered n, where n is 1–14 and the first order may be unnumbered, AND no order numbered n+1 is open AND maximum order count > n AND low <= its entry price - grid spacing)
Short: (no open first order AND the latest closed first order was short and closed at take profit AND first-order cumulative closed pips < first-order cumulative target AND abs(close - latest first-order take-profit price) >= renewal offset) OR (an open short order numbered n, where n is 1–14 and the first order may be unnumbered, AND no order numbered n+1 is open AND maximum order count > n AND high >= its entry price + grid spacing)
Exit: long, (one open first order AND high >= its entry price + initial first-order take-profit distance) OR (2–3 open orders AND first order AND high >= its entry price + first-order partial take-profit distance) OR (2–3 open orders AND second order AND high >= its entry price + second-order take-profit distance) OR (3 open orders AND third order AND high >= its entry price + third-order take-profit distance) OR (4–15 open orders AND long orders outnumber short orders AND high >= grid break-even price + grid target distance) OR (cycle floating loss > account balance * allowed risk percentage)
Exit: short, (one open first order AND low <= its entry price - initial first-order take-profit distance) OR (2–3 open orders AND first order AND low <= its entry price - first-order partial take-profit distance) OR (2–3 open orders AND second order AND low <= its entry price - second-order take-profit distance) OR (3 open orders AND third order AND low <= its entry price - third-order take-profit distance) OR (4–15 open orders AND short orders >= long orders AND low <= grid break-even price - grid target distance) OR (cycle floating loss > account balance * allowed risk percentage)
