Calculation: adaptive Williams %R = Williams %R(applied price, round(base period / (2 * (2 - FDI))), high, low), calculated on T1 bars using the last completed bar; FDI = 1 + (ln(sum over the previous fractal-lookback bars of sqrt((z[i] - z[i-1])^2 + 1/fractal-lookback^2)) + ln 2) / ln(2 * (fractal-lookback - 1)), where z is applied price normalized to its fractal-lookback minimum and maximum; FDI is zero when that price range is zero, and Williams %R holds its previous value when its high-low range is zero
Long: long entries enabled AND ((signal mode follows oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses above the oversold level) OR (signal mode opposes oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses below the overbought level))
Short: short entries enabled AND ((signal mode follows oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses below the overbought level) OR (signal mode opposes oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses above the oversold level))
Exit: short, short positions may close AND long entries disabled AND ((signal mode follows oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses above the oversold level) OR (signal mode opposes oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses below the overbought level))
Exit: long, long positions may close AND short entries disabled AND ((signal mode follows oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses below the overbought level) OR (signal mode opposes oscillator signals AND adaptive Williams %R crosses above the oversold level))
