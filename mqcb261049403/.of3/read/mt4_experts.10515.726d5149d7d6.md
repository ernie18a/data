Long: SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[0] > SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[0] AND SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[1] > SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[1] AND SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[2] < SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[2]
Short: SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[0] < SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[0] AND SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[1] < SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[1] AND SMA((high + low) / 2, long-term lookback, long-term horizontal shift)[2] > SMA((high + low) / 2, short-term lookback, short-term horizontal shift)[2]
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
