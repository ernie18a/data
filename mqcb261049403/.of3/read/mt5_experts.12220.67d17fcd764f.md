Calculation: weighted average entry price = volume-weighted mean of open position entry prices
Calculation: last-position loss in points = round(max(0, (entry price - close) / point size)) for long positions or round(max(0, (close - entry price) / point size)) for short positions
Calculation: adaptive loss threshold in points = clamp(round((highest(high, lookback, prior completed chart bars) - lowest(low, same bars)) / (range divisor × point size)), floor(base point range / range divisor), round(base point range × range divisor))
Long: no open positions AND RSI(close, lookback, last completed chart bar) > lower RSI threshold AND close[1] > close[2]
Short: no open positions AND RSI(close, lookback, last completed chart bar) < upper RSI threshold AND close[1] <= close[2]
Long: current direction is long AND open position count < position limit AND last-position loss in points >= adaptive loss threshold
Short: current direction is short AND open position count < position limit AND last-position loss in points >= adaptive loss threshold
Calculation: long trailing stop = max(previous long trailing stop, close - trailing distance in price) when round((close - weighted average entry price) / point size) >= activation profit
Calculation: short trailing stop = max(previous short trailing stop, close + trailing distance in price) when round((weighted average entry price - close) / point size) >= activation profit
Exit: Long, high >= weighted average entry price + take-profit distance in price OR (classic stop enabled AND trailing stop disabled AND low <= weighted average entry price - stop distance in price) OR (trailing stop enabled AND classic stop disabled AND long trailing stop is set AND low <= long trailing stop) OR (CCI exit enabled AND CCI(close, lookback, last completed chart bar) < -CCI exit level) OR (time exit enabled AND count of T1 bars from first position entry through the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar, evaluated on chart bars >= holding limit) OR (equity exit enabled AND account equity >= equity target)
Exit: Short, low <= weighted average entry price - take-profit distance in price OR (classic stop enabled AND trailing stop disabled AND high >= weighted average entry price + stop distance in price) OR (trailing stop enabled AND classic stop disabled AND short trailing stop is set AND high >= short trailing stop) OR (CCI exit enabled AND CCI(close, lookback, last completed chart bar) > CCI exit level) OR (time exit enabled AND count of T1 bars from first position entry through the current, possibly still-forming T1 bar, evaluated on chart bars >= holding limit) OR (equity exit enabled AND account equity >= equity target)
