Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than the highs of the preceding swing-left lookback bars and greater than or equal to the highs of the following swing-right lookback bars; confirm after the following bars close on T1.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar low less than the lows of the preceding swing-left lookback bars and less than or equal to the lows of the following swing-right lookback bars; confirm after the following bars close on T1.
Calculation: Usable bullish sweep = within the sweep-memory lookback, a T1 bar low below the last unswept, unbroken confirmed swing low and close above that swing low; its stop extreme is the lowest qualifying low while unused. Usable bearish sweep = the inverse at a confirmed swing high, with the highest qualifying high as its stop extreme.
Calculation: Fresh FVG(direction, origin, break) = the newest unmitigated gap from origin through break: bullish when low[x] > high[x+2], zone [high[x+2], low[x]]; bearish when high[x] < low[x+2], zone [high[x], low[x+2]].
Calculation: Order block(direction, origin, break) = the zone and visit count of the nearest preceding candle with a body opposite the break direction, searched within the order-block lookback and after origin; reject it if an intervening close passes its far edge or distinct returns into the zone reach the maximum allowed visits.
Calculation: Setup zone(direction) = the positive-width intersection of the fresh FVG and order block when both overlap; otherwise the fresh FVG if present, or the order block if fresh-order-block fallback is enabled.
Calculation: Regime = LOW VOLATILITY if ATR is below its rolling mean times the low-volatility factor; otherwise RANGING if the high-low range over the range lookback divided by ATR is below the range-width threshold and the absolute fast-slow EMA difference divided by ATR is below the EMA-separation threshold; otherwise TRENDING if the updated structural bias, fast/slow EMA order, and MACD main/signal order agree; otherwise REVERSAL. Evaluate on each completed T1 bar, updating bias from that bar's swing break first.
Calculation: Setup entry(direction) = setup-zone midpoint when its distance from close is at most the limit-distance ATR threshold; otherwise, if body ATR reaches the stop-entry body threshold and momentum is directionally aligned, long high plus the stop-entry buffer ATR or short low minus that buffer.
Calculation: Setup stop(direction) = usable sweep extreme minus the stop-buffer ATR for a long or plus it for a short; without a usable sweep, setup-zone low minus the buffer ATR for a long or setup-zone high plus it for a short.
Calculation: Nearest opposing liquidity(direction, entry) = nearest confirmed swing high above a long entry or confirmed swing low below a short entry that is neither broken nor swept.
Calculation: Target2(direction, entry, risk) = the nearer of entry plus/minus the target-R multiple times risk and nearest opposing liquidity when present, further capped toward entry by the optional instrument-qualified fixed target.
Calculation: Setup score = the capped sum of base score, fresh-FVG bonus, overlapping-order-block bonus (unvisited or visited once), path bonus (sweep, continuation, or momentum break), momentum-alignment bonus, and environment-alignment bonus; environment is aligned when regime is neither LOW VOLATILITY nor RANGING and the spread-risk filter passes.
