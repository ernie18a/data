Calculation: Alternating pivots = chronological T1 pivot highs whose high strictly exceeds the highs of each of the preceding and following pivot-strength bars and, only when a bar is not a pivot high, pivot lows whose low strictly undercuts the lows of each of those bars; collapse each consecutive run of highs to its highest high and each run of lows to its lowest low, keeping the earliest pivot on a tie.
Calculation: Pivot-line slope(a,b) = (b.price-a.price)/(b.bar-a.bar)
Calculation: Pivot-line value(a,b,x) = a.price + Pivot-line slope(a,b)*(x-a.bar)
Calculation: Pivot-line intersection bar(a,b,c,d) = (a.price-c.price-Pivot-line slope(a,b)*a.bar+Pivot-line slope(c,d)*c.bar)/(Pivot-line slope(c,d)-Pivot-line slope(a,b))
Calculation: Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) = max(p1.price,p2.price,p3.price,p4.price,p5.price)-min(p1.price,p2.price,p3.price,p4.price,p5.price)
Calculation: Convergence ratio(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) = abs(Pivot-line slope(p1,p3)-Pivot-line slope(p2,p4))*(p5.bar-p1.bar)/Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5)
Long: On T1 bars using last completed bars, p1 through p5 are the latest five Alternating pivots in low-high-low-high-low order AND p3.price < p1.price AND p4.price < p2.price AND p5.bar-p1.bar is between minimum pattern width and maximum pattern width inclusive AND Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) > the instrument's minimum price increment AND abs(Pivot-line slope(p1,p3)-Pivot-line slope(p2,p4)) is at least the parallel-line tolerance AND Pivot-line intersection bar(p1,p3,p2,p4) > p5.bar AND Convergence ratio(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) >= minimum convergence AND abs(p5.price-Pivot-line value(p1,p3,p5.bar))/Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) <= line tolerance AND no T1 bar k from p5.bar through the latest completed bar has high[k] >= Pivot-line value(p1,p4,k)
Short: On T1 bars using last completed bars, p1 through p5 are the latest five Alternating pivots in high-low-high-low-high order AND p3.price > p1.price AND p4.price > p2.price AND p5.bar-p1.bar is between minimum pattern width and maximum pattern width inclusive AND Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) > the instrument's minimum price increment AND abs(Pivot-line slope(p1,p3)-Pivot-line slope(p2,p4)) is at least the parallel-line tolerance AND Pivot-line intersection bar(p1,p3,p2,p4) > p5.bar AND Convergence ratio(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) >= minimum convergence AND abs(p5.price-Pivot-line value(p1,p3,p5.bar))/Five-pivot height(p1,p2,p3,p4,p5) <= line tolerance AND no T1 bar k from p5.bar through the latest completed bar has low[k] <= Pivot-line value(p1,p4,k)
