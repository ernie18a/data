Calculation: Range-filter ATR = ATR(ADR lookback, T1 bars, last completed T1 bar)
Calculation: Breakout ATR = ATR(ADR lookback × trading period, chart bars, last completed chart bar)
Calculation: Asian range size = highest(high, range lookback, T2 bars before London open) − lowest(low, range lookback, T2 bars before London open)
Calculation: PSAR = Parabolic SAR(symbol-specific step parameter, maximum-acceleration parameter, chart bars)
Calculation: MACD lines = MACD(close, signal lookback converted from hours to chart bars, fast lookback rounded from signal lookback × fast multiplier, slow lookback rounded from signal lookback × slow multiplier and longer than fast lookback, chart bars)
Calculation: Stochastic main = Stochastic main(%K lookback = trading period, %D smoothing = slowing = rounded(trading period × smoothing multiplier), SMA, low/high, chart bars)
Calculation: PSAR swing high = most recent PSAR value within max(search-floor parameter, search-multiplier parameter × trading period) completed chart bars where PSAR is above close and PSAR on the preceding bar is below its close
Calculation: PSAR swing low = most recent PSAR value within max(search-floor parameter, search-multiplier parameter × trading period) completed chart bars where PSAR is below close and PSAR on the preceding bar is above its close
Long: PSAR[1] < close[2] AND PSAR[2] > close[1] AND MACD main[1] > MACD signal[1] AND Stochastic main[1] <= upper stochastic bound AND Asian range size >= lower range fraction × Range-filter ATR AND Asian range size <= upper range fraction × Range-filter ATR AND spread <= maximum-spread fraction × Range-filter ATR AND PSAR swing high and PSAR swing low exist AND PSAR swing high + breakout-buffer fraction × Breakout ATR > highest(high, range lookback, T2 bars before London open) AND current time is before New York close AND current time is outside the session-close lead-time of an active symbol session
Short: PSAR[1] > close[2] AND PSAR[2] < close[1] AND MACD main[1] < MACD signal[1] AND Stochastic main[1] >= lower stochastic bound AND Asian range size >= lower range fraction × Range-filter ATR AND Asian range size <= upper range fraction × Range-filter ATR AND spread <= maximum-spread fraction × Range-filter ATR AND PSAR swing high and PSAR swing low exist AND PSAR swing low − breakout-buffer fraction × Breakout ATR < lowest(low, range lookback, T2 bars before London open) AND current time is before New York close AND current time is outside the session-close lead-time of an active symbol session
Exit: long, (MACD main[2] > MACD signal[2] AND MACD main[1] < MACD signal[1]) OR current time is at or after New York close OR current time is within the session-close lead-time of an active symbol session OR daily profit target reached OR daily loss limit reached
Exit: short, (MACD main[2] < MACD signal[2] AND MACD main[1] > MACD signal[1]) OR current time is at or after New York close OR current time is within the session-close lead-time of an active symbol session OR daily profit target reached OR daily loss limit reached
