Calculation: trend direction = process bars chronologically; initialize direction and next reversal as bullish, and the tracked-low and tracked-high to separate seed parameters; on each bar calculate highest(high, lookback), lowest(low, lookback), MA(high, lookback, MA method), and MA(low, lookback, MA method); if the next reversal is bearish, set tracked-low to max(lowest low, tracked-low), then if MA(high) < tracked-low AND close < low[1], set direction bearish, next reversal bullish, and tracked-high to highest high; next, if the next reversal is bullish, set tracked-high to min(highest high, tracked-high), then if MA(low) > tracked-high AND close > high[1], set direction bullish, next reversal bearish, and tracked-low to lowest low; otherwise retain the prior direction and trackers. The bullish reversal check follows the bearish reversal check on the same bar.
Long: trend direction on the current chart bar, including while it is forming, = bullish
Short: trend direction on the current chart bar, including while it is forming, = bearish
Long: trend direction calculated on still-forming T1 bars and copied onto chart bars at the first chart bar at or after each T1 period begins, then held through the rest of that T1 period = bullish
Short: trend direction calculated on still-forming T1 bars and copied onto chart bars at the first chart bar at or after each T1 period begins, then held through the rest of that T1 period = bearish
