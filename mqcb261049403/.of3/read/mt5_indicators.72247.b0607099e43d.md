Calculation: TQI = weighted mean of Kaufman ER(close, ER lookback), volume regime, structure alignment, and momentum persistence, using their separate weights; volume regime is volume Z-score mapped from its lower to upper bound and clamped to [0,1], or, when volume is unavailable, ATR divided by its longer-lookback ATR mean mapped from its lower to upper bound and clamped to [0,1]; structure alignment is the absolute normalized position of the selected price source within the rolling high-low range; momentum persistence is the fraction of recent chart bars whose close-to-close movement agrees with the prior trend; calculate on chart bars, using the current bar and last completed bars for lookbacks
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend direction = calculate on chart bars; effective ATR is ATR(ATR lookback) times the configured ER base plus ER scale times Kaufman ER(close, ER lookback), or ATR(ATR lookback) when efficiency weighting is disabled; the symmetric multiplier is base multiplier times the configured legacy ER adaptation factor times (1 minus quality influence plus quality influence times (quality base plus quality scale times (1 minus TQI) to the quality curve), with TQI set to its neutral value when disabled; apply separate active-band tightening and passive-band widening factors based on asymmetry strength and TQI, then smooth both multipliers with the configured multiplier smoothing coefficient when enabled; form lower and upper raw bands from the selected price source minus or plus the respective multiplier times effective ATR, and trail the lower band to the greater of its raw and previous values when previous close is above the previous lower band, otherwise reset it to raw; trail the upper band to the lesser of its raw and previous values when previous close is below the previous upper band, otherwise reset it to raw; initialize trend bullish; on a confirmed bar, flip bearish to bullish when close exceeds the current upper band or when character-flip detection and TQI are enabled, prior TQI exceeds the character-flip high threshold, current TQI is below its low threshold, trend age meets its minimum, and close exceeds the selected price source; flip bullish to bearish when close is below the current lower band or under the same character-flip conditions when close is below the selected price source
Long: Adaptive SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND current chart bar is closed AND warm-up is complete
Short: Adaptive SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND current chart bar is closed AND warm-up is complete
Exit: Long, on a later confirmed chart bar, low is at or below min(entry price, last confirmed pivot low if available, otherwise entry-bar low) minus stop ATR multiplier times effective ATR at entry, OR high is at or above entry price plus the largest of the three target R-multiples times the distance from entry to stop, OR bars since entry reach the trade timeout
Exit: Short, on a later confirmed chart bar, high is at or above max(entry price, last confirmed pivot high if available, otherwise entry-bar high) plus stop ATR multiplier times effective ATR at entry, OR low is at or below entry price minus the largest of the three target R-multiples times the distance from entry to stop, OR bars since entry reach the trade timeout
