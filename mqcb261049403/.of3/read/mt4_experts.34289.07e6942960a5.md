Long: At the open of a chart bar whose datetime day-of-month differs from the preceding chart bar AND MACD histogram(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, SMA signal lookback) falls from the preceding completed chart bar to the last completed chart bar, with fast lookback shorter than slow lookback
Short: At the open of a chart bar whose datetime day-of-month differs from the preceding chart bar AND MACD histogram(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, SMA signal lookback) rises from the preceding completed chart bar to the last completed chart bar, with fast lookback shorter than slow lookback
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - min(stop-loss distance, trailing-stop distance) OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR (high >= entry price + trailing-stop distance AND low <= highest(high since entry) - trailing-stop distance)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + min(stop-loss distance, trailing-stop distance) OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (low <= entry price - trailing-stop distance AND high >= lowest(low since entry) + trailing-stop distance)
