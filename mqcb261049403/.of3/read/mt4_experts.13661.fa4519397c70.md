Calculation: bullish signal = close on the current bar > Parabolic SAR(acceleration, maximum, current bar) AND close[1] < Parabolic SAR(acceleration, maximum, previous completed bar) AND SMA(close, shorter trend lookback, current bar) > SMA(close, longer trend lookback, current bar) AND close on the current bar > SMA(close, price-filter lookback, current bar)
Calculation: bearish signal = close on the current bar < Parabolic SAR(acceleration, maximum, current bar) AND close[1] > Parabolic SAR(acceleration, maximum, previous completed bar) AND SMA(close, shorter trend lookback, current bar) < SMA(close, longer trend lookback, current bar) AND close on the current bar < SMA(close, price-filter lookback, current bar)
Calculation: stop-loss distance = min(max(if automatic stop-loss is selected then abs(entry price − Parabolic SAR(acceleration, maximum, current bar)) × stop-loss multiplier else fixed stop-loss distance, minimum stop-loss distance), maximum stop-loss distance)
Long: ((normal signal mapping AND bullish signal) OR (inverted signal mapping AND bearish signal)) AND stop-loss distance > minimum stop-loss distance
Short: ((normal signal mapping AND bearish signal) OR (inverted signal mapping AND bullish signal)) AND stop-loss distance > minimum stop-loss distance
