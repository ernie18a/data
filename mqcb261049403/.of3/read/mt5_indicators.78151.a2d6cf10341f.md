Calculation: Zone thickness = (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low over the capped lookback ending on the completed chart bar) × zone tolerance parameter
Calculation: Resistance levels = confirmed swing-high prices using SwingStrength confirmation bars
Calculation: Support levels = confirmed swing-low prices using SwingStrength confirmation bars
Long: On a completed chart bar, close > open AND let R be the highest resistance level below close AND open ≤ R AND close > R AND highest high of the preceding SwingStrength bars < R AND more than RequiredTouches resistance levels are below close AND at least RequiredTouches resistance levels are between R − zone thickness and R AND (confirmation filter is disabled OR session VWAP is selected and close > session VWAP OR SMA is selected and close > SMA(close, filter lookback) OR SMMA is selected and close > SMMA(close, filter lookback))
Short: On a completed chart bar, close < open AND let S be the lowest support level above close AND open ≥ S AND close < S AND lowest low of the preceding SwingStrength bars > S AND more than RequiredTouches support levels are above close AND at least RequiredTouches support levels are between S and S + zone thickness AND (confirmation filter is disabled OR session VWAP is selected and close < session VWAP OR SMA is selected and close < SMA(close, filter lookback) OR SMMA is selected and close < SMMA(close, filter lookback))
