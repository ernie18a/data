Calculation: PairScore = the bucket rank, using ordered percentage-band cutoffs, of the percentage of a pair bar’s range below its close; the other currency’s score is the complement to the maximum rank.
Calculation: CurrencyStrength(currency,T) = the mean PairScore for that currency across enabled pairs containing it; evaluate from last completed T1 bars and the still-forming T2 bars on each chart update, where T1 is short-term and T2 is long-term.
Calculation: RecoveryFactor = (highest account balance since initialization / current account balance)^recovery exponent when balance is positive, otherwise 1.
Long: pair enabled AND chart bar count > warm-up-bar parameter AND (news filter disabled OR (news upcoming AND time until news > pre-news interval) OR (news past AND time since news > post-news interval)) AND ((swing hour window: swing start < swing end AND hour(datetime) is between them inclusive, OR swing start > swing end AND (hour(datetime) >= swing start OR hour(datetime) <= swing end)) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND at least one of base or quote is among the WeakCurrencies strongest by T2 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the LongCurrencies weakest by T2 strength AND CurrencyStrength(base,T1) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T1) AND at least one of base or quote is among the ShortCurrencies strongest by T1 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the ShortCurrencies weakest by T1 strength AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift bars ago) > RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift plus RSI separation bars ago) AND (CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > swing threshold OR CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) > swing threshold)) OR (scalping hour window: scalping start < scalping end AND hour(datetime) is between them inclusive, OR scalping start > scalping end AND (hour(datetime) >= scalping start OR hour(datetime) <= scalping end)) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T1) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T1) AND at least one of base or quote is among the ScalpingShortCurrencies strongest by T1 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the ScalpingShortCurrencies weakest by T1 strength AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift bars ago) < RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift plus RSI separation bars ago) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < scalping threshold AND CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) < scalping threshold); RSI uses completed T3 bars sampled on chart updates.
Short: pair enabled AND chart bar count > warm-up-bar parameter AND (news filter disabled OR (news upcoming AND time until news > pre-news interval) OR (news past AND time since news > post-news interval)) AND ((swing hour window: swing start < swing end AND hour(datetime) is between them inclusive, OR swing start > swing end AND (hour(datetime) >= swing start OR hour(datetime) <= swing end)) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND at least one of base or quote is among the WeakCurrencies strongest by T2 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the LongCurrencies weakest by T2 strength AND CurrencyStrength(base,T1) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T1) AND at least one of base or quote is among the ShortCurrencies strongest by T1 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the ShortCurrencies weakest by T1 strength AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift bars ago) < RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift plus RSI separation bars ago) AND (CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > swing threshold OR CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) > swing threshold)) OR (scalping hour window: scalping start < scalping end AND hour(datetime) is between them inclusive, OR scalping start > scalping end AND (hour(datetime) >= scalping start OR hour(datetime) <= scalping end)) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T1) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T1) AND at least one of base or quote is among the ScalpingShortCurrencies strongest by T1 strength AND at least one of base or quote is among the ScalpingShortCurrencies weakest by T1 strength AND RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift bars ago) > RSI(close, RSI lookback, T3, RSI shift plus RSI separation bars ago) AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < scalping threshold AND CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) < scalping threshold); RSI uses completed T3 bars sampled on chart updates.
Exit: long, (position age > profit-close interval AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0 AND close > entry price + min-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback,T3,ATR shift bars ago) × RecoveryFactor) OR (open trade count <= starter-trade limit AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0 AND close > entry price + start-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback,T3,ATR shift bars ago) × RecoveryFactor) OR (position age > long-hold interval AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) < CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0); ATR uses completed T3 bars sampled on chart updates.
Exit: short, (position age > profit-close interval AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0 AND close < entry price − min-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback,T3,ATR shift bars ago) × RecoveryFactor) OR (open trade count <= starter-trade limit AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0 AND close < entry price − start-profit ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback,T3,ATR shift bars ago) × RecoveryFactor) OR (position age > long-hold interval AND CurrencyStrength(base,T2) > CurrencyStrength(quote,T2) AND position profit > 0); ATR uses completed T3 bars sampled on chart updates.
