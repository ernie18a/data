Long: On chart bars using values from the last completed T1 bars, (weekday(datetime) != Monday OR hour(datetime) >= Monday-hour parameter) AND (weekday(datetime) != Friday OR hour(datetime) <= Friday-hour parameter) AND (if weekday(datetime) = Monday then close > max(high of the last completed T1 bar, high of the preceding T1 bar) else close > high of the last completed T1 bar)
Short: On chart bars using values from the last completed T1 bars, (weekday(datetime) != Monday OR hour(datetime) >= Monday-hour parameter) AND (weekday(datetime) != Friday OR hour(datetime) <= Friday-hour parameter) AND (if weekday(datetime) = Monday then close < min(low of the last completed T1 bar, low of the preceding T1 bar) else close < low of the last completed T1 bar)
Calculation: long trailing stop = max(entry price - stop-loss distance, highest high since entry - trailing distance), active after highest high since entry exceeds entry price by the trailing activation distance; trailing distance and activation distance are derived from the trailing-stop parameter
Calculation: short trailing stop = min(entry price + stop-loss distance, lowest low since entry + trailing distance), active after entry price exceeds lowest low since entry by the trailing activation distance; trailing distance and activation distance are derived from the trailing-stop parameter
Exit: long, close <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR close >= entry price + take-profit distance OR (trailing-stop parameter > 0 AND close <= long trailing stop)
Exit: short, close >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR close <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (trailing-stop parameter > 0 AND close >= short trailing stop)
