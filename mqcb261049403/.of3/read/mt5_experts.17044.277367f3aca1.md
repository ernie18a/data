Calculation: CrossLevel = on T1 bars, the low of the most recent bar whose open is at or below and close above the mean of SMA(median price, shorter cross lookback, T1, that bar) and SMMA(median price, longer cross lookback, T1, that bar), or the high of the most recent bar whose open is at or above and close below that mean; include the forming bar and search the history lookback.
Calculation: CurrentRelativeChange = round(100 * (close(T1, forming bar) - close(T2, last completed bar)) / close(T2, last completed bar), precision).
Calculation: PriorRelativeChange = round(100 * (close(T1, last completed bar) - close(T2, last completed bar)) / close(T2, last completed bar), precision).
Calculation: TrendState = let F be SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1) and S be SMMA(median price, slow lookback, T1), with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback; initialize maximum and minimum to F[1] and their indices to 0, then scan offsets from the short offset down to 0, updating each extreme and its index only on a strict new extreme. Let D=F[0]-S[0] and Dprev=F[1]-S[1]. Scan F at offsets 0 through the long history length minus 1 if the T1 bar count changed since the previous call, otherwise offsets 0 through the short offset minus 1. If the maximum index exceeds the minimum index, return down when D<Dprev, else up when any scanned F exceeds the minimum; if the maximum index is less than the minimum index, return up when D>Dprev, else down when any scanned F is below the maximum; otherwise return neutral.
Long: on T1 bars, close(T1, forming bar) > CrossLevel AND TrendState = up AND close(T1, forming bar) > open(T1, forming bar) AND low(T1, forming bar) < SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, forming bar) AND SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, forming bar) > SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND CurrentRelativeChange > PriorRelativeChange AND no long position is open AND (a short position is open OR hour(current datetime) differs from hour of the most recent matching trade close within the recent-history duration, using hour zero if none).
Short: on T1 bars, close(T1, forming bar) < CrossLevel AND TrendState = down AND close(T1, forming bar) < open(T1, forming bar) AND high(T1, forming bar) > SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, forming bar) AND SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, forming bar) < SMA(typical price, fast lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND CurrentRelativeChange < PriorRelativeChange AND no short position is open AND (a long position is open OR hour(current datetime) differs from hour of the most recent matching trade close within the recent-history duration, using hour zero if none).
Exit: long, TrendState = down AND current position profit plus swap and commission > 0.
Exit: short, TrendState = up AND current position profit plus swap and commission > 0.
