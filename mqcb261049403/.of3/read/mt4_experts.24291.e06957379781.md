Calculation: Candle body[n] = abs(open[n] - close[n])
Calculation: Fractal touch = scan upper and lower Williams Fractals from the oldest bar in the fractal lookback through the current chart bar; return true if close equals a nonzero fractal value, otherwise return false at the first bar containing both an upper and lower fractal
Long: on a new chart bar, Fractal touch AND Candle body[3] > Candle body[2] AND Candle body[3] > Candle body[4] AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1, current forming bar) > LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1, current forming bar) AND low[2] < high[1] AND abs(close - close[2]) > retrace parameter × point size
Short: on a new chart bar, Fractal touch AND Candle body[3] > Candle body[2] AND Candle body[3] > Candle body[4] AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1, current forming bar) < LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1, current forming bar) AND low[1] < high[2] AND abs(close - close[1]) > retrace parameter × point size
Exit: long, low <= max(entry price - stop-loss distance, highest high since entry - trailing distance when trailing is enabled and the favorable move exceeds the trailing distance, entry price + moved-stop offset when break-even is enabled and the favorable move exceeds the moved-stop trigger) OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= min(entry price + stop-loss distance, lowest low since entry + trailing distance when trailing is enabled and the favorable move exceeds the trailing distance, entry price - moved-stop offset when break-even is enabled and the favorable move exceeds the moved-stop trigger) OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
