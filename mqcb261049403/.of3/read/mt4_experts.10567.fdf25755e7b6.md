Calculation: startup volatility count = count of the latest volatility-lookback T1 bars, sampled once at the first check after initialization and including the T1 bar still forming, whose high > (open[1] + close[1])/2 of the previous completed chart bar at initialization and whose low < that midpoint
Long: SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[1] > SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[2] AND SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[3] < SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[4] AND RSI(median price, RSI lookback)[1] <= upper RSI bound AND close[1] > open[1] AND startup volatility count < volatility cutoff AND fewer than the position cap of this strategy's positions are open AND no strategy long position is open
Short: SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[1] < SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[2] AND SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[3] > SMMA(median price, shorter lookback)[4] AND RSI(median price, RSI lookback)[1] >= lower RSI bound AND close[1] < open[1] AND startup volatility count < volatility cutoff AND fewer than the position cap of this strategy's positions are open AND no strategy short position is open
Calculation: long virtual stop = entry price - trail distance initially; on each check that has not exited, while reduced trailing is inactive, raise the stop to close - trail distance if close > entry price and that candidate exceeds the stop; whenever close >= entry price + trail distance and close - reduced trail distance exceeds the stop, set the stop to close - reduced trail distance and activate reduced trailing, whether or not it was already active
Calculation: short virtual stop = entry price + trail distance initially; on each check that has not exited, while reduced trailing is inactive, lower the stop to close + trail distance if close < entry price and that candidate is below the stop; whenever close <= entry price - trail distance and close + reduced trail distance is below the stop, set the stop to close + reduced trail distance and activate reduced trailing, whether or not it was already active
Exit: long, close <= long virtual stop
Exit: short, close >= short virtual stop
