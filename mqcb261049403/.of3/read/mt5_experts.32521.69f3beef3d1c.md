Long: no long position is open AND free margin covers the initial order's required margin AND close(T1, forming bar) > TEMA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar)
Short: no short position is open AND free margin covers the initial order's required margin AND close(T1, forming bar) < TEMA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar)
Long: adverse-move averaging is selected AND at least one long position is open AND long position count is below the order-count limit AND close <= lowest long entry price - grid multiplier × ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)
Short: adverse-move averaging is selected AND at least one short position is open AND short position count is below the order-count limit AND close >= highest short entry price + grid multiplier × ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)
Long: favorable-move averaging is selected AND at least one long position is open AND long position count is below the order-count limit AND close >= highest long entry price + grid multiplier × ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)
Short: favorable-move averaging is selected AND at least one short position is open AND short position count is below the order-count limit AND close <= lowest short entry price - grid multiplier × ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)
Calculation: Long stop = first long entry price - stop-loss distance when enabled; with at most one long position or averaging disabled, set it to entry price + trailing distance when close - entry price > trailing distance + activation offset and the stop is absent or below entry price, then raise it by trailing step when the stop is above entry price and close - stop > trailing step + step offset; with favorable-move averaging at the order-count limit, anchor it at lowest long entry price + trailing-start distance, set effective trailing distance and step to the greater of their configured distances and the broker minimum stop distance, skip updates when effective distance < effective step, set the stop to anchor + effective distance when close - anchor > effective distance + effective step and the stop is absent or at/below anchor, then raise it by effective distance when stop > anchor and close - stop > effective distance + effective step + activation offset
Calculation: Short stop = first short entry price + stop-loss distance when enabled; with at most one short position or averaging disabled, set it to entry price - trailing distance when entry price - close > trailing distance + activation offset and the stop is absent or above entry price, then lower it by trailing step when the stop is below entry price and stop - close > trailing step + step offset; with favorable-move averaging at the order-count limit, anchor it at highest short entry price - trailing-start distance, set effective trailing distance and step to the greater of their configured distances and the broker minimum stop distance, skip updates when effective distance < effective step, set the stop to anchor - effective distance when anchor - close > effective distance + effective step and the stop is absent or at/above anchor, then lower it by effective distance when stop < anchor and stop - close > effective distance + effective step + activation offset
Exit: long, close <= long stop when present OR close >= first long entry price + take-profit distance when enabled
Exit: short, close >= short stop when present OR close <= first short entry price - take-profit distance when enabled
Exit: long, more than one long position is open AND combined floating profit including swap and commission >= 0
Exit: short, more than one short position is open AND combined floating profit including swap and commission >= 0
