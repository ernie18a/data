Calculation: P&F columns = map prices to floor(round(price / point size) / box size) levels; initialize from the close at the start of available history; use chart-bar closes or highs and lows as the selected source; extend an X column to higher levels or an O column to lower levels, otherwise reverse after the opposite extreme moves by the reversal-box parameter; record the first chart bar each new X column exceeds the preceding X-column top or each new O column falls below the preceding O-column bottom; use completed chart bars when closed-bars mode is enabled, otherwise include the forming bar; box size is fixed points or ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar) times a multiplier, rounded to the nearest increment in the nice-size ladder, retaining the prior box while relative change in raw ATR points is below the hysteresis threshold
Long: a new X column's top exceeds the top of the preceding X column
Short: a new O column's bottom is below the bottom of the preceding O column
Long: a new X column's top exceeds the top of the preceding X column AND (higher-low filter disabled OR the preceding O column's bottom is above the prior O column's bottom) AND (EMA filter disabled OR breakout-bar close > EMA(close, lookback) on chart bars at the breakout bar) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(lookback) on chart bars at the breakout bar >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR the breakout chart bar's open hour is within the configured session, start inclusive and end exclusive, wrapping midnight, when the chart interval is within the session filter's intraday cutoff) AND (flip-only filter disabled OR direction differs from the most recent earlier effective signal)
Short: a new O column's bottom is below the bottom of the preceding O column AND (lower-high filter disabled OR the preceding X column's top is below the prior X column's top) AND (EMA filter disabled OR breakout-bar close < EMA(close, lookback) on chart bars at the breakout bar) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX(lookback) on chart bars at the breakout bar >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR the breakout chart bar's open hour is within the configured session, start inclusive and end exclusive, wrapping midnight, when the chart interval is within the session filter's intraday cutoff) AND (flip-only filter disabled OR direction differs from the most recent earlier effective signal)
