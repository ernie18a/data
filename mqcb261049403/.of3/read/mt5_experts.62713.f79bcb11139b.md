Calculation: Market profile = on chart bars over the profile lookback, including the forming bar, assign each close to the first price level within the grouping distance (using that close as the level price), count bars per level, set POC to the level with the greatest count, then expand from POC by adding the nearer adjacent level (lower on ties) until included counts reach the configured fraction of total; value-area bounds are the lowest and highest included levels
Calculation: Grid distance(k) = base grid step × (Fibonacci ratio indexed by same-side position count k, capped at the maximum grid ratio, when Fibonacci grid is enabled; otherwise 1) × tick size
Long: (for every one of the previous N completed chart bars: close > open AND volume >= minimum volume AND (ATR filter disabled OR abs(close - open) > ATR multiplier × ATR(lookback, that completed chart bar))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > RSI midpoint AND RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) < upper RSI threshold)) AND (main trend filter disabled OR (configured trend method is MA AND close(T1, forming bar) > MA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) OR configured trend method is MACD AND MACD(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) > MACD signal(T1, forming bar))) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > ADX threshold AND +DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > -DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar))) AND (market profile disabled OR (value-area restriction disabled OR close is within the profile value-area bounds) AND (POC avoidance disabled OR abs(close - POC) > POC avoidance distance)) AND (long position count < long position limit OR long position limit is disabled) AND (total main position count < total position limit OR total position limit is disabled) AND (long position count = 0 OR lowest long entry price - close > Grid distance(long position count))
Short: (for every one of the previous N completed chart bars: close < open AND volume >= minimum volume AND (ATR filter disabled OR abs(close - open) > ATR multiplier × ATR(lookback, that completed chart bar))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR (RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) < RSI midpoint AND RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > lower RSI threshold)) AND (main trend filter disabled OR (configured trend method is MA AND close(T1, forming bar) < MA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) OR configured trend method is MACD AND MACD(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) < MACD signal(T1, forming bar))) AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > ADX threshold AND -DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > +DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar))) AND (market profile disabled OR (value-area restriction disabled OR close is within the profile value-area bounds) AND (POC avoidance disabled OR abs(close - POC) > POC avoidance distance)) AND (short position count < short position limit OR short position limit is disabled) AND (total main position count < total position limit OR total position limit is disabled) AND (short position count = 0 OR close - highest short entry price > Grid distance(short position count))
Long: hedging enabled AND (maximum hedge count disabled OR hedge position count < maximum hedge count) AND elapsed time since hedge snapshot >= hedge check interval AND a short position exists AND close - stored ask-price snapshot >= hedge activation distance AND (hedge trend filter disabled OR (configured trend method is MA AND close(T1, forming bar) < MA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) OR configured trend method is MACD AND MACD(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) < MACD signal(T1, forming bar))) AND (hedge ADX filter disabled OR main ADX filter disabled OR (ADX(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > ADX threshold AND -DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > +DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar)))
Short: hedging enabled AND (maximum hedge count disabled OR hedge position count < maximum hedge count) AND elapsed time since hedge snapshot >= hedge check interval AND a long position exists AND stored bid-price snapshot - close >= hedge activation distance AND (hedge trend filter disabled OR (configured trend method is MA AND close(T1, forming bar) > MA(close, lookback, T1, forming bar) OR configured trend method is MACD AND MACD(close, fast EMA lookback, slow EMA lookback, signal lookback, T1, forming bar) > MACD signal(T1, forming bar))) AND (hedge ADX filter disabled OR main ADX filter disabled OR (ADX(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > ADX threshold AND +DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar) > -DI(lookback, chart timeframe, forming bar)))
Exit: Long, stop-loss enabled AND (low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR trailing stop enabled AND low <= highest high since entry - trailing-stop distance) OR take-profit enabled AND trailing take-profit disabled AND (high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR long position count >= breakeven position count AND high >= volume-weighted average long entry price + breakeven offset)
