Calculation: D(O, side, kind) is true if any eligible prior pivot p forms the selected divergence with oscillator O at endpoint e. O is one enabled series among MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback), MACD histogram(close, the same MACD lookbacks), RSI(close, lookback), Stochastic(high, low, close, lookback parameters), CCI((high+low+close)/3, lookback), Momentum(close, lookback), OBV(close, volume), volume-weighted MACD(close, volume, lookback parameters), Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, lookback), and Money Flow Index(high, low, close, volume, lookback). A high pivot uses high in wick mode and close otherwise; it is strictly above the preceding span bars and at least as high as the following span bars. A low pivot uses low in wick mode and close otherwise; it is strictly below the preceding span bars and at most as low as the following span bars. P uses high for bearish pivots and low for bullish pivots in wick mode, and close otherwise. p is among the most recent maximum-pivot-count confirmed pivots of the matching side, with distance from p to e greater than the minimum pivot separation and no greater than maximum reach. For bullish regular divergence, O[e]>O[p] AND P[p]>P[e]; for bullish hidden divergence, O[e]<O[p] AND P[e]>P[p]; for bearish regular divergence, O[e]<O[p] AND P[e]>P[p]; for bearish hidden divergence, O[e]>O[p] AND P[e]<P[p]. Between p and e, every close and O value is on or above its straight-line interpolation for bullish divergence, or on or below it for bearish divergence. When confirmation is off, e is the current forming bar. When confirmation is on, e is the preceding completed bar and the current bar must have close[current]>close[e] OR O[current]>O[e] for bullish divergence, or close[current]<close[e] OR O[current]<O[e] for bearish divergence.
Calculation: D count = the number of true D(O, side, kind) results across enabled oscillator series, both sides, and selected divergence kinds; each oscillator, side, and kind contributes at most one.
Long: D count is at least the minimum divergence count AND at least one enabled oscillator series has a bullish D for a selected divergence kind.
Short: D count is at least the minimum divergence count AND at least one enabled oscillator series has a bearish D for a selected divergence kind.
