Calculation: Step size(bars, lookback, sensitivity, fixed-step, average-mode) = sensitivity × fixed-step when fixed sizing is enabled; otherwise round(0.5 × sensitivity × (running maximum + running minimum of the range averages) ÷ point size), where each range average is the sum of (high − low) × weight over the lookback divided by the lookback, weight is 1 in equal-weight mode or 1 + (lookback − bars ago) ÷ lookback in linear-weight mode, and the running extrema include all processed bars.
Calculation: StepMA NRTR direction(bars, step size, price-mode) = initialize direction to neutral, upper level to low + 2 × step size × point size, and lower level to high − 2 × step size × point size; on each bar retain the prior direction, set it bullish if close > prior upper level, then bearish if close < prior lower level; in high/low mode set raw levels to low + 2 × step size × point size and high − 2 × step size × point size, otherwise set them to close ± 2 × step size × point size; if bullish, set the lower level to max(raw lower, prior lower) and the upper level to raw upper, otherwise set the upper level to min(raw upper, prior upper) and the lower level to raw lower.
