Calculation: M = MACD(applied price, shorter lookback, longer lookback, common smoothing method, common phase)
Calculation: S = moving average(M, signal lookback, signal smoothing method, signal phase)
Long: M > 0
Short: M < 0
Long: M > S
Short: M < S
Long: On chart bars, M from the T1 bar still forming, sampled at its first chart bar and held through that T1 bar, is greater than 0
Short: On chart bars, M from the T1 bar still forming, sampled at its first chart bar and held through that T1 bar, is less than 0
Long: On chart bars, M > S using values from the T1 bar still forming, sampled at its first chart bar and held through that T1 bar
Short: On chart bars, M < S using values from the T1 bar still forming, sampled at its first chart bar and held through that T1 bar
