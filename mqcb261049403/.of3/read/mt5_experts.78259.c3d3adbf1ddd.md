Calculation: SessionOpen = open of the first T2 bar at or after session start, using that bar while it is still forming
Calculation: SessionStretch(L) = mean over the preceding L sessions of min(session high − session open, session open − session low), using T2 bars from each session start through its configured exit time
Calculation: EntryOffset = SessionStretch(stretch lookback) or ATR(T1, ATR lookback, last completed bar) × ATR multiple, as selected by the offset mode
Calculation: NarrowestRange(n, T1, last completed bar) = latest high − low is strictly less than the high − low of each of the preceding n − 1 bars
Calculation: WidestRange(n, T1, last completed bar) = latest high − low is strictly greater than the high − low of each of the preceding n − 1 bars
Calculation: MultiBarRange(m, T1, last completed bar) = highest high − lowest low across m consecutive bars
Calculation: ContractionFilterPasses = contraction filter disabled OR (short lookback narrow-range filter selected AND NarrowestRange(short lookback)) OR (long lookback narrow-range filter selected AND NarrowestRange(long lookback)) OR (inside-day-plus-short-lookback filter selected AND latest high ≤ preceding high AND latest low ≥ preceding low AND NarrowestRange(short lookback)) OR (multi-bar narrow-range filter selected AND the latest MultiBarRange(m) is strictly less than every such range in the preceding comparison lookback) OR (any-pattern filter selected AND at least one of those four patterns holds)
Calculation: WideSkipPasses = wide skip disabled OR (short lookback wide-range skip selected AND NOT WidestRange(short lookback)) OR (long lookback wide-range skip selected AND NOT WidestRange(long lookback))
Long: session start ≤ datetime < session start + entry-window duration AND high of the still-forming T2 bar ≥ SessionOpen + EntryOffset AND ContractionFilterPasses AND WideSkipPasses AND (Friday skip disabled OR weekday is not Friday) AND neither entry level was reached by T2 bars from session start until the levels were prepared
Short: session start ≤ datetime < session start + entry-window duration AND low of the still-forming T2 bar ≤ SessionOpen − EntryOffset AND ContractionFilterPasses AND WideSkipPasses AND (Friday skip disabled OR weekday is not Friday) AND neither entry level was reached by T2 bars from session start until the levels were prepared
Exit: long, low of the still-forming T2 bar ≤ SessionOpen − EntryOffset OR (take-profit multiple > 0 AND high of the still-forming T2 bar ≥ SessionOpen + EntryOffset + take-profit multiple × EntryOffset) OR datetime ≥ the earlier of session start + exit duration and server-day end − server-day buffer
Exit: short, high of the still-forming T2 bar ≥ SessionOpen + EntryOffset OR (take-profit multiple > 0 AND low of the still-forming T2 bar ≤ SessionOpen − EntryOffset − take-profit multiple × EntryOffset) OR datetime ≥ the earlier of session start + exit duration and server-day end − server-day buffer
