Calculation: Signal(line,n) = Close[start+n] for a main-chart line; otherwise the indicator in the line's pane at start_indicator+n: RSI(close, shared indicator lookback), CCI(close, shared indicator lookback), Williams %R(shared indicator lookback), Momentum(close, shared indicator lookback), Force Index(close, volume, shared indicator lookback), DeMarker(shared indicator lookback), ATR(shared indicator lookback), OBV(close, volume), or MFI(shared indicator lookback), all on chart bars.
Calculation: LineValue(line,shift) = trendline value at shift + endpoint-derived price change per bar * fraction of the current chart bar elapsed.
Calculation: CrossesAbove(line) = Signal(line,0) > LineValue(line,start) for the one-touch cross method, or Signal(line,0) > LineValue(line,start) AND Signal(line,1) < LineValue(line,start) for the two-bar cross method.
Calculation: CrossesBelow(line) = Signal(line,0) < LineValue(line,start) for the one-touch cross method, or Signal(line,0) < LineValue(line,start) AND Signal(line,1) > LineValue(line,start) for the two-bar cross method.
Long: CrossesAbove(any BUYSTOP line) OR CrossesBelow(any BUYLIMIT line)
Short: CrossesBelow(any SELLSTOP line) OR CrossesAbove(any SELLLIMIT line)
Exit: long, CrossesBelow(any SLBUY or SLALLBUY line) OR CrossesAbove(any TPBUY or TPALLBUY line) OR (the opening line specifies a positive stop-loss distance AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance * point size) OR (no positive stop-loss distance is specified AND a nonzero SLINITBUY level was set at entry AND low <= that level) OR (the opening line specifies a positive take-profit distance AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance * point size) OR (no positive take-profit distance is specified AND a nonzero TPINITBUY level was set at entry AND high >= that level)
Exit: short, CrossesAbove(any SLSELL or SLALLSELL line) OR CrossesBelow(any TPSELL or TPALLSELL line) OR (the opening line specifies a positive stop-loss distance AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance * point size) OR (no positive stop-loss distance is specified AND a nonzero SLINITSELL level was set at entry AND high >= that level) OR (the opening line specifies a positive take-profit distance AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance * point size) OR (no positive take-profit distance is specified AND a nonzero TPINITSELL level was set at entry AND low <= that level)
