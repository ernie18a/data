Calculation: crossover level = the sum of the short EMA and long SMA values on the two completed chart bars spanning the cross, divided by four; EMA uses close and the shorter lookback parameter, SMA uses open and the longer lookback parameter, and the SMA shift parameter exceeds the EMA shift parameter
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback parameter, EMA shift parameter) crosses above SMA(open, longer lookback parameter, SMA shift parameter) within the cross-recency parameter in completed chart bars AND current time is no later than the crossover-bar open plus the opportunity-window parameter times chart-bar duration AND current price >= crossover level + trigger-distance parameter in instrument points AND RSI(typical price, RSI lookback parameter, T1 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) > upper RSI-threshold parameter AND current price > highest(high, high/low lookback-duration parameter, completed chart bars) AND current price > upper SMA Envelopes(close, shorter T2 lookback, larger T2 shift and deviation parameters, T2 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) AND current price > upper SMA Envelopes(close, longer T3 lookback, smaller T3 shift and deviation parameters, T3 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) AND no EA order for this instrument is open or pending AND no post-entry blackout is active
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback parameter, EMA shift parameter) crosses below SMA(open, longer lookback parameter, SMA shift parameter) within the cross-recency parameter in completed chart bars AND current time is no later than the crossover-bar open plus the opportunity-window parameter times chart-bar duration AND current price <= crossover level - trigger-distance parameter in instrument points AND RSI(typical price, RSI lookback parameter, T1 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) < lower RSI-threshold parameter AND current price < lowest(low, high/low lookback-duration parameter, completed chart bars) AND current price < lower SMA Envelopes(close, shorter T2 lookback, larger T2 shift and deviation parameters, T2 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) AND current price < lower SMA Envelopes(close, longer T3 lookback, smaller T3 shift and deviation parameters, T3 forming bar, sampled on chart bars) AND no EA order for this instrument is open or pending AND no post-entry blackout is active; upper RSI-threshold parameter exceeds lower RSI-threshold parameter
Exit: long, price <= low at the stop-lookback parameter chart bars before entry, or, when trailing is configured, price <= the greater of that entry stop and highest high since entry minus the trailing-distance parameter in instrument points, or price >= entry price plus either configured take-profit distance parameter in instrument points; the farther take-profit distance exceeds the nearer one
Exit: short, price >= high at the stop-lookback parameter chart bars before entry, or, when trailing is configured, price >= the lesser of that entry stop and lowest low since entry plus the trailing-distance parameter in instrument points, or price <= entry price minus either configured take-profit distance parameter in instrument points; the farther take-profit distance exceeds the nearer one
