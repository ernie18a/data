Calculation: OpenValue(buffer index, opening parameters, shift) = opening custom-indicator buffer on T1 at shift, sampled each chart tick; shift 0 is forming, shift 1 is last completed, larger shifts are older completed bars
Calculation: CloseValue(buffer index, closing parameters, shift) = closing custom-indicator buffer on T1 at shift, sampled each chart tick; shift 0 is forming, shift 1 is last completed, larger shifts are older completed bars; closing parameters may be copied from opening parameters
Calculation: OpeningLongSignal = (arrow-buffer method selected AND OpenValue(buy-arrow index, opening parameters, opening shift) is nonempty and nonzero) OR (main/signal method selected AND OpenValue(main index, opening parameters, opening shift) > OpenValue(signal index, opening parameters, opening shift) AND OpenValue(main index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) <= OpenValue(signal index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1)) OR (single-line level method selected AND OpenValue(line index, opening parameters, opening shift) > buy level AND OpenValue(line index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) <= buy level) OR (three-value method selected AND OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift) > OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) AND OpenValue(line index 2, opening parameters, opening shift + 2) > OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift + 1)) OR (two-color method selected AND OpenValue(uptrend buffer index, opening parameters, opening shift) is nonempty and nonzero)
Calculation: OpeningShortSignal = (arrow-buffer method selected AND OpenValue(sell-arrow index, opening parameters, opening shift) is nonempty and nonzero) OR (main/signal method selected AND OpenValue(main index, opening parameters, opening shift) < OpenValue(signal index, opening parameters, opening shift) AND OpenValue(main index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) >= OpenValue(signal index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1)) OR (single-line level method selected AND OpenValue(line index, opening parameters, opening shift) < sell level AND OpenValue(line index, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) >= sell level) OR (three-value method selected AND OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift) < OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift + 1) AND OpenValue(line index 2, opening parameters, opening shift + 2) < OpenValue(line index 1, opening parameters, opening shift + 1)) OR (two-color method selected AND OpenValue(downtrend buffer index, opening parameters, opening shift) is nonempty and nonzero)
Long: OpeningLongSignal AND NOT OpeningShortSignal
Short: OpeningShortSignal AND NOT OpeningLongSignal
Exit: long, (stop-loss enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance) OR (separate indicator close selected AND ((arrow-buffer method selected AND CloseValue(close-buy-arrow index, closing parameters, closing shift) is nonempty and nonzero) OR (main/signal method selected AND CloseValue(main index, closing parameters, closing shift) < CloseValue(signal index, closing parameters, closing shift) AND CloseValue(main index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) >= CloseValue(signal index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1)) OR (single-line level method selected AND CloseValue(line index, closing parameters, closing shift) < close-buy level AND CloseValue(line index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) >= close-buy level) OR (three-value method selected AND CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift) < CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) AND CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 2) < CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 1)) OR (two-color method selected AND CloseValue(downtrend buffer index, closing parameters, closing shift) is nonempty and nonzero)) AND (profit check disabled OR close >= entry price + minimum profit) AND (stop-profit check disabled OR stop-loss absent OR stop-loss price - entry price < minimum stop-profit)
Exit: short, (stop-loss enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance) OR (separate indicator close selected AND ((arrow-buffer method selected AND CloseValue(close-sell-arrow index, closing parameters, closing shift) is nonempty and nonzero) OR (main/signal method selected AND CloseValue(main index, closing parameters, closing shift) > CloseValue(signal index, closing parameters, closing shift) AND CloseValue(main index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) <= CloseValue(signal index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1)) OR (single-line level method selected AND CloseValue(line index, closing parameters, closing shift) > close-sell level AND CloseValue(line index, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) <= close-sell level) OR (three-value method selected AND CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift) > CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 1) AND CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 2) > CloseValue(line index 1, closing parameters, closing shift + 1)) OR (two-color method selected AND CloseValue(uptrend buffer index, closing parameters, closing shift) is nonempty and nonzero)) AND (profit check disabled OR close <= entry price - minimum profit) AND (stop-profit check disabled OR stop-loss absent OR entry price - stop-loss price < minimum stop-profit)
