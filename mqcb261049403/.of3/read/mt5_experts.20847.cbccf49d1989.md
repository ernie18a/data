Long: open < SMA(close, lookback, shift, current forming chart bar) AND close > SMA(close, lookback, shift, current forming chart bar)
Short: open > SMA(close, lookback, shift, current forming chart bar) AND close < SMA(close, lookback, shift, current forming chart bar)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
