Calculation: Selected-day OHLC = OHLC aggregated from completed T1 bars beginning with the bar after the most recent earlier completed T1 bar whose hour matches the current forming T1 bar, within the boundary-search lookback, through the latest completed T1 bar
Calculation: Protective distance = max(stop ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, T2, last completed bar at entry), broker minimum stop distance, stop-distance floor in points × point size)
Long: On each new T2 bar, no account position is open AND weekday of the latest completed T1 bar equals the selected weekday AND (ATR filter is disabled OR selected-day high − selected-day low ≥ minimum range ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND ((direction mode is continuation AND selected-day close > selected-day open) OR (direction mode is reversal AND selected-day close ≤ selected-day open))
Short: On each new T2 bar, no account position is open AND weekday of the latest completed T1 bar equals the selected weekday AND (ATR filter is disabled OR selected-day high − selected-day low ≥ minimum range ATR multiple × ATR(ATR lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND ((direction mode is continuation AND selected-day close ≤ selected-day open) OR (direction mode is reversal AND selected-day close > selected-day open))
Exit: long, (stop ATR multiple > 0 AND low ≤ entry price − protective distance) OR (stop ATR multiple > 0 AND take-profit multiple > 0 AND high ≥ entry price + protective distance × take-profit multiple) OR datetime hour ≥ configured close hour
Exit: short, (stop ATR multiple > 0 AND high ≥ entry price + protective distance) OR (stop ATR multiple > 0 AND take-profit multiple > 0 AND low ≤ entry price − protective distance × take-profit multiple) OR datetime hour ≥ configured close hour
