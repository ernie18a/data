Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = the current pivot candidate is an RSI pivot low: RSI(close, RSI lookback) is lower than RSI(close, RSI lookback) on each of the preceding left pivot lookback chart bars and following right pivot lookback chart bars; low at the candidate < low at the preceding RSI pivot low AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the candidate > RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the preceding RSI pivot low. When the candidate is right pivot lookback bars ago, the last following bar is the current forming chart bar.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = the current pivot candidate is an RSI pivot high: RSI(close, RSI lookback) is higher than RSI(close, RSI lookback) on each of the preceding left pivot lookback chart bars and following right pivot lookback chart bars; high at the candidate > high at the preceding RSI pivot high AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the candidate < RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the preceding RSI pivot high. When the candidate is right pivot lookback bars ago, the last following bar is the current forming chart bar.
Calculation: Current chart signed volume = if current chart close > current chart open then current chart volume else negative current chart volume
Long: Bullish RSI divergence
Short: Bearish RSI divergence
Long: Bullish RSI divergence AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the current pivot candidate <= long RSI threshold AND (candidate pivot is more than right pivot lookback bars ago OR (candidate pivot is right pivot lookback bars ago AND, on chart bars, current chart close >= open of the current forming T1 bar AND current chart signed volume >= 0))
Short: Bearish RSI divergence AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) at the current pivot candidate >= short RSI threshold AND (candidate pivot is more than right pivot lookback bars ago OR (candidate pivot is right pivot lookback bars ago AND, on chart bars, current chart close < open of the current forming T1 bar AND current chart signed volume <= 0))
