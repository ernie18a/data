Calculation: adjusted Williams %R = 100 - ABS(Williams %R(lookback, T4, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation))
Calculation: automatic target distance = minimum automatic target distance when the minimum target is enabled; otherwise Bollinger Band Width(median price, band lookback, band deviation, T5, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation) divided by the narrow-width, middle-width, or wide-width divisor when the width is respectively <= the narrow-width threshold, > the narrow-width threshold and <= the wide-width threshold, or > the wide-width threshold
Long: OsMA(weighted close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation) > OsMA(weighted close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, previous bar) AND the same OsMA comparison holds on T2 and T3 AND at least the long minimum count of T1, T2, and T3 have SMA(close, common lookback, timeframe, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation) > SMA(close, common lookback, timeframe, previous bar) AND adjusted Williams %R < long Williams %R threshold AND day of week is a weekday AND NOT (hour = restricted hour AND minute < opening cutoff) AND minute modulo entry-check cycle > intra-cycle delay
Short: OsMA(weighted close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation) < OsMA(weighted close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, previous bar) AND the same OsMA comparison holds on T2 and T3 AND at least the short minimum count of T1, T2, and T3 have SMA(close, common lookback, timeframe, still-forming bar sampled on each chart evaluation) <= SMA(close, common lookback, timeframe, previous bar) AND adjusted Williams %R > short Williams %R threshold AND day of week is a weekday AND NOT (hour = restricted hour AND minute < opening cutoff) AND minute modulo entry-check cycle > intra-cycle delay
Exit: long, low <= entry price - manual stop distance when automatic stop loss is disabled OR low <= Low(T4, still-forming bar at stop placement) - automatic stop buffer when automatic stop loss is enabled OR high >= entry price + manual target distance when automatic take profit is disabled OR close > entry price + automatic target distance AND (high - close > long retracement buffer OR close < entry price - long retracement buffer) when automatic take profit is enabled
Exit: short, high >= entry price + manual stop distance when automatic stop loss is disabled OR high >= High(T4, still-forming bar at stop placement) + automatic stop buffer when automatic stop loss is enabled OR low <= entry price - manual target distance when automatic take profit is disabled OR entry price - close > automatic target distance AND (close - low > short retracement buffer OR close > entry price + short retracement buffer) when automatic take profit is enabled
