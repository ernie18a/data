Calculation: Smoothed HA close(method, lookback, phase) = (MA(open, lookback, method, phase) + MA(high, lookback, method, phase) + MA(low, lookback, method, phase) + MA(close, lookback, method, phase)) / 4
Calculation: Smoothed HA open(method, lookback, phase) = (previous Smoothed HA open(method, lookback, phase) + previous Smoothed HA close(method, lookback, phase)) / 2
Calculation: Smoothed HA trend(method, lookback, phase) = 1 if Smoothed HA open(method, lookback, phase) < Smoothed HA close(method, lookback, phase), -1 if Smoothed HA open(method, lookback, phase) > Smoothed HA close(method, lookback, phase), otherwise previous Smoothed HA trend(method, lookback, phase)
Long: previous Smoothed HA trend(chart smoothing method, chart smoothing lookback, chart smoothing phase) <= 0 AND Smoothed HA open(chart smoothing method, chart smoothing lookback, chart smoothing phase) < Smoothed HA close(chart smoothing method, chart smoothing lookback, chart smoothing phase)
