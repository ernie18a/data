Calculation: short P&L = sum of profit, swap, and commission for open non-hedge short orders
Calculation: hedge P&L = sum of profit, swap, and commission for open hedge orders
Calculation: pair P&L = long P&L + short P&L + hedge P&L
Calculation: all-pair P&L = sum of pair P&L across traded pairs
Calculation: side step multiplier = side order count in static progression; sum of indices from zero through side order count in geometric progression; or sum of max(one, exponential base raised to index minus one) over those indices in exponential progression
Calculation: side transaction-cost allowance = current spread × side lots × tick value × point-size multiplier + sum of absolute commission and swap for open orders on that side
Calculation: side loss-add level = negative of (side lots × configured loss-add step × side step multiplier × tick value + side transaction-cost allowance)
Calculation: initial side profit-close level = oldest side order size × side step multiplier × configured profit target per lot × tick value in automatic loss-add mode during normal operation; otherwise side lots × configured profit target per lot × tick value

Calculation: hybrid or advanced basket profit-close level = side lots × configured profit target per lot × tick value in automatic loss-add mode during normal operation; otherwise the initial side profit-close level
Calculation: newest side order P&L = profit, swap, and commission for the most recently opened non-hedge order on that side
Calculation: oldest side order P&L = profit, swap, and commission for the earliest opened non-hedge order on that side
Calculation: profitable side-order P&L = sum of P&L for open non-hedge orders on that side with positive P&L
Calculation: earliest side-group P&L = sum of P&L for the earliest rounded fraction of side orders, using the configured early-group divisor
