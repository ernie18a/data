Calculation: Upper entry boundary = highest(high, lookback) minus (base factor minus entry offset) percent of (highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback)).
Calculation: Lower entry boundary = lowest(low, lookback) plus (base factor minus entry offset) percent of (highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback)).
Calculation: Upper exit boundary = highest(high, lookback) minus (base factor minus exit offset) percent of (highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback)).
Calculation: Lower exit boundary = lowest(low, lookback) plus (base factor minus exit offset) percent of (highest(high, lookback) minus lowest(low, lookback)).
Calculation: Channel state = bullish if selected applied price is above the upper entry boundary, bearish if it is below the lower entry boundary, otherwise the prior channel state; calculate on chart bars or on T1 bars using the still-forming T1 bar and map T1 values to chart bars. Indexed values refer to bars of the calculation timeframe.
Long: (channel state is bullish AND channel state[1] is bearish) OR (re-entry enabled AND channel state is bullish AND channel state[1] is not bearish AND selected applied price is above the upper entry boundary AND selected applied price[1] is at or below upper entry boundary[1]) OR (alerts enabled AND channel state[1] is bullish AND channel state[2] is bearish) OR (alerts enabled AND re-entry enabled AND channel state[1] is bullish AND channel state[2] is bullish AND selected applied price[1] is above upper entry boundary[1] AND selected applied price[2] is at or below upper entry boundary[2]).
Short: (channel state is bearish AND channel state[1] is bullish) OR (re-entry enabled AND channel state is bearish AND channel state[1] is not bullish AND selected applied price is below the lower entry boundary AND selected applied price[1] is at or above lower entry boundary[1]) OR (alerts enabled AND channel state[1] is bearish AND channel state[2] is bullish) OR (alerts enabled AND re-entry enabled AND channel state[1] is bearish AND channel state[2] is bearish AND selected applied price[1] is below lower entry boundary[1] AND selected applied price[2] is at or above lower entry boundary[2]).
Exit: long, (exit mode enabled AND channel state is bullish AND selected applied price is below the upper exit boundary AND selected applied price[1] is at or above upper exit boundary[1]) OR (alerts enabled AND exit mode enabled AND channel state[1] is bullish AND selected applied price[1] is below upper entry boundary[1] AND selected applied price[2] is at or above upper entry boundary[2]).
Exit: short, (exit mode enabled AND channel state is bearish AND selected applied price is above the lower exit boundary AND selected applied price[1] is at or below lower exit boundary[1]) OR (alerts enabled AND exit mode enabled AND channel state[1] is bearish AND selected applied price[1] is above lower entry boundary[1] AND selected applied price[2] is at or below lower entry boundary[2]).
