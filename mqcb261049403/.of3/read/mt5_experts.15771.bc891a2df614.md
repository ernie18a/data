Calculation: Long signal = Williams %R(lookback, T1) crosses above the oversold level on the selected T1 bar; offset one is the last completed bar, offset zero is the forming bar, and positive offsets are completed bars. For a nonzero offset, evaluate on T1 bar updates; for zero, evaluate on chart ticks using T1 values.
Calculation: Short signal = Williams %R(lookback, T1) crosses below the overbought level on the selected T1 bar; offset one is the last completed bar, offset zero is the forming bar, and positive offsets are completed bars. For a nonzero offset, evaluate on T1 bar updates; for zero, evaluate on chart ticks using T1 values.
Long: Long signal
Short: Short signal
Exit: long, (stop-loss points > 0 AND low <= entry price - stop-loss points × point size) OR (take-profit points > 0 AND high >= entry price + take-profit points × point size) OR (long-close permission AND ((long-entry permission AND long-close permission) OR (short-entry permission AND short-close permission)) AND NOT (Long signal AND short-close permission OR Short signal AND long-close permission) AND any Short signal on a T1 bar older than the selected signal bar)
Exit: short, (stop-loss points > 0 AND high >= entry price + stop-loss points × point size) OR (take-profit points > 0 AND low <= entry price - take-profit points × point size) OR (short-close permission AND ((long-entry permission AND long-close permission) OR (short-entry permission AND short-close permission)) AND NOT (Long signal AND short-close permission OR Short signal AND long-close permission) AND any Long signal on a T1 bar older than the selected signal bar)
