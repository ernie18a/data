Calculation: Short close EMA current = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, forming bar), read on each tick
Calculation: Short close EMA previous = EMA(close, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar), read on each tick
Calculation: Short open EMA current = EMA(open, shorter lookback, T1, forming bar), read on each tick
Calculation: Short open EMA previous = EMA(open, shorter lookback, T1, last completed bar), read on each tick
Calculation: Long close EMA = EMA(close, longer lookback, T1, forming bar), read on each tick
Calculation: Narrow upper envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, narrow deviation, upper, T1, forming bar), where narrow deviation < medium deviation < wide deviation, read on each tick
Calculation: Narrow lower envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, narrow deviation, lower, T1, forming bar)
Calculation: Medium upper envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, medium deviation, upper, T1, forming bar)
Calculation: Medium lower envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, medium deviation, lower, T1, forming bar)
Calculation: Wide upper envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, wide deviation, upper, T1, forming bar)
Calculation: Wide lower envelope = Envelopes(EMA, close, longer lookback, wide deviation, lower, T1, forming bar)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initialize from the buy stop loss if set; on each tick, when buy trailing distance > 0 and current price - entry price > buy trailing distance + trailing step and the active stop is unset or less than current price - buy trailing distance - trailing step, set it to current price - buy trailing distance
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initialize from the sell stop loss if set; on each tick, when sell trailing distance > 0 and entry price - current price > sell trailing distance + trailing step and (the active stop is unset or greater than current price + sell trailing distance + trailing step), set it to current price + sell trailing distance
Long: (Short close EMA current > Wide lower envelope AND Short close EMA previous < Wide lower envelope) OR (Short close EMA current > Medium lower envelope AND Short close EMA previous < Medium lower envelope) OR (Short close EMA current < Narrow lower envelope AND Short close EMA previous < Narrow lower envelope) OR (Short close EMA current > Long close EMA AND Short close EMA previous < Long close EMA) OR (Short close EMA current > Narrow upper envelope AND Short close EMA previous < Narrow upper envelope) OR (Short close EMA current > Medium upper envelope AND Short close EMA previous < Medium upper envelope)
Short: (Short open EMA current < Wide upper envelope AND Short open EMA previous > Wide upper envelope) OR (Short open EMA current < Medium upper envelope AND Short open EMA previous > Medium upper envelope) OR (Short open EMA current < Narrow upper envelope AND Short open EMA previous > Narrow upper envelope) OR (Short open EMA current < Long close EMA AND Short open EMA previous > Long close EMA) OR (Short open EMA current < Narrow lower envelope AND Short open EMA previous > Narrow lower envelope) OR (Short open EMA current < Medium lower envelope AND Short open EMA previous > Medium lower envelope)
Exit: long, (buy stop-loss distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - buy stop-loss distance) OR (buy take-profit distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + buy take-profit distance) OR (long trailing stop is set AND low <= long trailing stop)
Exit: short, (sell stop-loss distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + sell stop-loss distance) OR (sell take-profit distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - sell take-profit distance) OR (short trailing stop is set AND high >= short trailing stop)
