Calculation: chart fast MA = EMA(typical price, shorter chart lookback, current chart bar)
Calculation: chart slow MA = LWMA(typical price, longer chart lookback, current chart bar)
Calculation: T2 fast MA = LWMA(typical price, shorter T2 lookback, T2 current bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: T2 slow MA = LWMA(typical price, longer T2 lookback, T2 current bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: T1 MACD main = MACD main(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: T1 MACD signal = MACD signal(close, shorter fast lookback, longer slow lookback, signal lookback, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: T1 RSI current = RSI(close, lookback, T1, current bar sampled on chart bars)
Calculation: T1 RSI prior = RSI(close, same lookback, T1, last completed bar sampled on chart bars)
Long: chart fast MA > chart slow MA AND low[2] < high[1] AND close[1] > open[1] AND open[2] > close[2] AND close[1] - open[1] > open[2] - close[2] AND ((T1 MACD main != 0 AND T1 MACD main > T1 MACD signal) OR T1 RSI current > T1 RSI prior OR (T2 fast MA > T2 slow MA AND abs(current bid - close[2]) > retracement lookback × point size))
Short: chart fast MA < chart slow MA AND low[1] < high[2] AND open[1] > close[1] AND close[2] > open[2] AND open[1] - close[1] < close[2] - open[2] AND ((T1 MACD main != 0 AND T1 MACD main < T1 MACD signal) OR T1 RSI current < T1 RSI prior OR (T2 fast MA < T2 slow MA AND abs(current ask - close[1]) > retracement lookback × point size))
Exit: long, close[1] = upper Bollinger Band(close, lookback, deviation, last completed chart bar)
Exit: short, close[1] = lower Bollinger Band(close, lookback, deviation, last completed chart bar)
