Calculation: Stop distance = static stop points × point size when dynamic stops are disabled; otherwise stop multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: Target distance = static target points × point size when dynamic stops are disabled; otherwise target multiplier × ATR(lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: Break-even padding = padding points when padding points ≤ trigger points; otherwise zero
Calculation: Long score(level) = total signed point movement from non-overlapping simulations over the optimization lookback, divided by instrument tick value / order lot size and rounded to two decimals; enter at chart close when the selected indicator on T1 crosses above level using completed T1 bars at matching chart-bar shifts, then exit at the first later chart close below entry − optimization-time stop distance or above entry + optimization-time target distance; omit trades reaching neither exit
Calculation: Short score(level) = total signed point movement from non-overlapping simulations over the optimization lookback, divided by instrument tick value / order lot size and rounded to two decimals; enter at chart close when the selected indicator on T1 crosses below level using completed T1 bars at matching chart-bar shifts, then exit at the first later chart close above entry + optimization-time stop distance or below entry − optimization-time target distance; omit trades reaching neither exit
Calculation: Best long level and score = candidate level between the lower and upper bounds with the greatest positive Long score, choosing the highest level on ties
Calculation: Best short level and score = candidate level between the lower and upper bounds with the greatest positive Short score, choosing the lowest level on ties
Long: (reverse enabled AND Best short score > Best long score OR reverse disabled AND Best long score > Best short score) AND (aggressive mode enabled OR (selected indicator(T1, last completed bar) > Best long level AND selected indicator(T1, previous completed bar) < Best long level))
Short: (reverse enabled AND Best long score > Best short score OR reverse disabled AND Best short score > Best long score) AND (aggressive mode enabled OR (selected indicator(T1, last completed bar) < Best short level AND selected indicator(T1, previous completed bar) > Best short level))
Exit: long, low <= entry − stop distance OR high >= entry + target distance OR (break-even enabled AND price has exceeded entry + trigger points × point size AND low <= entry + Break-even padding × point size)
Exit: short, high >= entry + stop distance OR low <= entry − target distance OR (break-even enabled AND price has fallen below entry − trigger points × point size AND high >= entry − Break-even padding × point size)
