Calculation: Upper signal = at least one indicator is enabled, and every enabled RSI(applied price, lookback, T_RSI, configured bar shift) is above its upper level, every enabled Stochastic main and signal (K lookback, D lookback, slowing, method, price field, T_STOCH, configured bar shift) is above its upper level, every enabled close is above its upper Bollinger Band(applied price, lookback, deviation, band displacement, T_BB, configured bar shift), and every enabled moving-average condition is met: in trend mode, close is above both the shorter-lookback and longer-lookback moving averages in single-timeframe mode or above the moving average in multi-timeframe mode; in countertrend mode, close is below them. Evaluate on each chart evaluation using the bar at each indicator's configured shift on its timeframe; the selected bar may still be forming. Use separate configured timeframes for indicators where applicable, the shared selected T1 in single-timeframe mode, and require the conditions on every selected T1 through Tn in multi-timeframe mode.
Calculation: Lower signal = at least one indicator is enabled, and every enabled RSI(applied price, lookback, T_RSI, configured bar shift) is below its lower level, every enabled Stochastic main and signal (K lookback, D lookback, slowing, method, price field, T_STOCH, configured bar shift) is below its lower level, every enabled close is below its lower Bollinger Band(applied price, lookback, deviation, band displacement, T_BB, configured bar shift), and every enabled moving-average condition is met: in trend mode, close is below both the shorter-lookback and longer-lookback moving averages in single-timeframe mode or below the moving average in multi-timeframe mode; in countertrend mode, close is above them. Evaluate on each chart evaluation using the bar at each indicator's configured shift on its timeframe; the selected bar may still be forming. Use separate configured timeframes for indicators where applicable, the shared selected T1 in single-timeframe mode, and require the conditions on every selected T1 through Tn in multi-timeframe mode.
Calculation: Effective trailing gap = maximum of the configured trailing gap, spread plus broker offset, and minimum stop distance plus broker offset.
Long: (trend mode AND Upper signal AND inverse disabled) OR (countertrend mode AND Lower signal AND inverse disabled) OR (trend mode AND Lower signal AND inverse enabled) OR (countertrend mode AND Upper signal AND inverse enabled)
Short: (countertrend mode AND Upper signal AND inverse disabled) OR (trend mode AND Lower signal AND inverse disabled) OR (countertrend mode AND Lower signal AND inverse enabled) OR (trend mode AND Upper signal AND inverse enabled)
Exit: long, virtual take-profit enabled AND close >= entry price + target distance
Exit: short, virtual take-profit enabled AND close <= entry price - target distance
Exit: long, virtual stop-loss enabled AND close <= entry price - stop distance
Exit: short, virtual stop-loss enabled AND close >= entry price + stop distance
Exit: long, virtual trailing enabled AND the highest high since entry > entry price + trailing start + effective trailing gap AND close <= highest high since entry - effective trailing gap
Exit: short, virtual trailing enabled AND the lowest low since entry < entry price - trailing start - effective trailing gap AND close >= lowest low since entry + effective trailing gap
Exit: long, exact bar index of the position's open time in its candle timeframe >= candle-count threshold
Exit: short, exact bar index of the position's open time in its candle timeframe >= candle-count threshold
