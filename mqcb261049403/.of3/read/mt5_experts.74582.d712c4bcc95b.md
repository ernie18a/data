Calculation: KCI = sum of the absolute differences between every pair of z-scores of net close change divided by path length, close minus mean close, population standard deviation of close, and net close change; net close change = close - close[shorter lookback], path length = positive offset parameter + sum of absolute consecutive close changes over the shorter lookback, mean and standard deviation use close over the shorter lookback, and each z-score standardizes its component over the longer lookback using population standard deviation, or equals 0 when that deviation is 0.
Long: KCI[2] < KCI[1] AND KCI[2] < KCI[3] AND KCI[2] <= compression threshold AND volume[2] × (close[2] - close[3])² < volume[1] × (close[1] - close[2])² AND close[2] < close[3] AND Williams %R(forming chart bar, lookback) <= long WPR threshold AND close[0] >= MA(close, lookback)[0]
