Calculation: Average range = SMA(high - low, range lookback, T1, last completed bar)
Calculation: S/R levels = cluster pivot highs and lows on T1 bars, where pivots are extrema against pivot-strength bars on each side; pivot checks use the forming T1 bar and average range uses completed T1 bars; cluster sorted pivot prices within cluster tolerance × average range, set each level to the cluster mean and its zone to the cluster price bounds, require minimum touches, and rank by recency-weighted pivot count; in conservative mode increase strength, lookback, tolerance, and minimum touches by their respective adjustment parameters, keep the strongest specified percentage, and enforce the specified minimum spacing; retain the highest-ranked levels up to the maximum level count
Calculation: Long stop = min(signal-bar low, selected S/R zone low) - stop buffer; if entry price - stop is below minimum stop distance, use entry price - minimum stop distance
Calculation: Short stop = max(signal-bar high, selected S/R zone high) + stop buffer; if stop - entry price is below minimum stop distance, use entry price + minimum stop distance
Calculation: Long target = nearest S/R level above entry price at least max(minimum stop distance, risk × effective minimum reward/risk) away; use entry price + risk × effective minimum reward/risk if none qualifies or the selected level is farther than risk × maximum target-risk multiple
Calculation: Short target = nearest S/R level below entry price at least max(minimum stop distance, risk × effective minimum reward/risk) away; use entry price - risk × effective minimum reward/risk if none qualifies or the selected level is farther than risk × maximum target-risk multiple
Long: on each new T1 bar, no EA position is open AND the last completed T1 bar is bullish AND, for the lowest qualifying S/R level L, its low is at most L + touch tolerance × average range and at least L - maximum excursion × average range, and its close is above L
Short: on each new T1 bar, no EA position is open AND the last completed T1 bar is bearish AND, for the lowest qualifying S/R level L, its high is at least L - touch tolerance × average range and at most L + maximum excursion × average range, and its close is below L
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR equity <= starting balance - maximum drawdown amount + maximum drawdown buffer OR day-start reference - equity >= daily drawdown amount × (1 - daily buffer percentage) OR enabled pass target is reached OR enabled daily profit target is reached OR (basket trailing is disabled AND scoped floating profit >= basket target) OR (basket trailing is enabled and armed AND scoped floating profit <= peak scoped floating profit - trail distance) OR (grid AND grid take-profit target is reached)
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR equity <= starting balance - maximum drawdown amount + maximum drawdown buffer OR day-start reference - equity >= daily drawdown amount × (1 - daily buffer percentage) OR enabled pass target is reached OR enabled daily profit target is reached OR (basket trailing is disabled AND scoped floating profit >= basket target) OR (basket trailing is enabled and armed AND scoped floating profit <= peak scoped floating profit - trail distance) OR (grid AND grid take-profit target is reached)
