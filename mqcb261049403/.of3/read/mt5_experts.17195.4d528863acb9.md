Calculation: crossover level = mean(EMA(close, shorter lookback)[c], EMA(close, shorter lookback)[c+1], SMA(open, longer lookback, shift parameter)[c], SMA(open, longer lookback, shift parameter)[c+1]), using completed chart bars, where c is the most recent completed bar on the crossed-to side
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback, chart timeframe, completed bars) crosses above SMA(open, longer lookback, chart timeframe, completed bars, shift parameter) within the cross-lookback parameter of completed chart-bar pairs AND current time is within the trigger-validity parameter after that crossover AND current Ask >= crossover level + trigger-distance parameter AND RSI(typical price, lookback parameter, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) > upper RSI-threshold parameter AND current Ask > Highest(high, range-lookback parameter, chart timeframe, completed bars) AND current Ask > upper Envelopes(close, shorter lookback, larger shift, larger deviation, T2, forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND current Ask > upper Envelopes(close, longer lookback, smaller shift, smaller deviation, T3, forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback, chart timeframe, completed bars) crosses below SMA(open, longer lookback, chart timeframe, completed bars, shift parameter) within the cross-lookback parameter of completed chart-bar pairs AND current time is within the trigger-validity parameter after that crossover AND current Bid <= crossover level - trigger-distance parameter AND RSI(typical price, lookback parameter, T1, forming bar sampled on chart bars) < lower RSI-threshold parameter AND current Bid < Lowest(low, range-lookback parameter, chart timeframe, completed bars) AND current Bid < lower Envelopes(close, shorter lookback, larger shift, larger deviation, T2, forming bar sampled on chart bars) AND current Bid < lower Envelopes(close, longer lookback, smaller shift, smaller deviation, T3, forming bar sampled on chart bars)
Exit: long, current price <= low[stop-lookback parameter] at entry OR current price <= highest price observed since entry - trailing-distance parameter
Exit: long, current price >= entry price + nearer take-profit parameter
Exit: long, current price >= entry price + farther take-profit parameter
Exit: short, current price >= high[stop-lookback parameter] at entry OR current price >= lowest price observed since entry + trailing-distance parameter
Exit: short, current price <= entry price - nearer take-profit parameter
Exit: short, current price <= entry price - farther take-profit parameter
