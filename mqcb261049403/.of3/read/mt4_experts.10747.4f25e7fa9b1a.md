Calculation: Upward AMA dot = AMA(close, efficiency-ratio lookback, fast smoothing lookback, slow smoothing lookback, exponent) rises by more than the dot-threshold parameter from the preceding chart bar
Calculation: Downward AMA dot = AMA(close, efficiency-ratio lookback, fast smoothing lookback, slow smoothing lookback, exponent) falls by more than the dot-threshold parameter from the preceding chart bar
