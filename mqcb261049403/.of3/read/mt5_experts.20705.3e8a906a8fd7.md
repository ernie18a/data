Calculation: Asymmetric RSI level(series, multiplier, side) = max(lower-clamp parameter, mean(series) − multiplier × downside deviation) for lower, or min(upper-clamp parameter, mean(series) + multiplier × upside deviation) for upper; downside and upside deviations are population standard deviations about mean(series) for values below and at or above the mean, respectively, using the overall population standard deviation for an empty subset; when the sample count is below the minimum-sample parameter, mean is the first sample and both deviations equal the fallback-factor parameter times the first sample.
Calculation: Entry window(hour, start, duration) = hour inclusively between start and (start + duration) modulo one day, wrapping across midnight; if the endpoints are equal, hour must equal start.
