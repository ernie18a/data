Calculation: Instrument net basket P/L = sum of profit, commission, and swap for all open buy and sell positions on the instrument
Calculation: Strategy gross P/L = sum of profit for all open buy and sell positions belonging to the strategy on the instrument
Calculation: Basket trail reference = zero when no tracked strategy trades are open; while instrument net basket P/L is positive and below the money take-profit target, update to the greater of the prior reference and instrument net basket P/L; while instrument net basket P/L is negative, update to its absolute value when that value is no greater than the absolute prior reference, otherwise retain the prior reference
Calculation: Tracked equity reference = current account equity when no tracked strategy trades are open; otherwise, previous tick's equity if the prior reference is below previous tick's equity, and current account equity otherwise
Long: SMA(close, shorter lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) > SMA(close, longer lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) AND MACD main(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 last completed bar sampled on chart bars) < MACD signal(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 last completed bar sampled on chart bars) AND no open orders on the instrument
Short: SMA(close, shorter lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) < SMA(close, longer lookback, chart bars, last completed bar) AND MACD main(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 last completed bar sampled on chart bars) > MACD signal(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 last completed bar sampled on chart bars) AND no open orders on the instrument
Long: on a new chart bar, at least one tracked long position is open AND no tracked short position is open AND current bid <= the lowest tracked long entry price - current spread - entry spacing
Short: on a new chart bar, at least one tracked short position is open AND no tracked long position is open AND current ask >= the highest tracked short entry price + current spread + entry spacing
Exit: long, money take-profit is enabled AND its threshold is nonzero AND sum of profit for all open buy and sell positions on the instrument >= the money take-profit threshold
Exit: short, money take-profit is enabled AND its threshold is nonzero AND sum of profit for all open buy and sell positions on the instrument >= the money take-profit threshold
Exit: long, money trailing is enabled AND instrument net basket P/L > 0 AND instrument net basket P/L < the money take-profit target AND instrument net basket P/L <= basket trail reference - the money stop amount
Exit: short, money trailing is enabled AND instrument net basket P/L > 0 AND instrument net basket P/L < the money take-profit target AND instrument net basket P/L <= basket trail reference - the money stop amount
Exit: long, money trailing is enabled AND instrument net basket P/L < 0 AND instrument net basket P/L < the money take-profit target AND strategy gross P/L < 0 AND abs(strategy gross P/L) > equity-risk percentage × tracked equity reference × loss multiplier AND sum of absolute profit-plus-commission-plus-swap for each open buy or sell position on the instrument >= abs(basket trail reference - the money stop amount)
Exit: short, money trailing is enabled AND instrument net basket P/L < 0 AND instrument net basket P/L < the money take-profit target AND strategy gross P/L < 0 AND abs(strategy gross P/L) > equity-risk percentage × tracked equity reference × loss multiplier AND sum of absolute profit-plus-commission-plus-swap for each open buy or sell position on the instrument >= abs(basket trail reference - the money stop amount)
Exit: long, instrument net basket P/L <= -account balance × hard-stop percentage / 100 OR (instrument net basket P/L <= -account balance × maximum-loss percentage / 100 AND open instrument trade count >= minimum trade count)
Exit: short, instrument net basket P/L <= -account balance × hard-stop percentage / 100 OR (instrument net basket P/L <= -account balance × maximum-loss percentage / 100 AND open instrument trade count >= minimum trade count)
Exit: long, close-at-maximum-orders is enabled AND (tracked long position count = maximum trades OR open instrument order count > maximum trades)
Exit: short, close-at-maximum-orders is enabled AND (tracked short position count = maximum trades OR open instrument order count > maximum trades)
