Calculation: StepWPR(step) = a trailing line of x = Williams %R(lookback) + 100; initialize trend up; each bar set candidate bands to x ± 2 × step, switch trend up when x exceeds the prior upper band and down when x falls below the prior lower band, ratchet the lower band upward in an uptrend and the upper band downward in a downtrend, and output lower band + step in an uptrend or upper band − step in a downtrend
Long: On chart bars, the shorter-step StepWPR on the current forming T1 bar is above the longer-step StepWPR and the shorter-step StepWPR on the last completed T1 bar is at or below the longer-step StepWPR, and long entries are enabled
Short: On chart bars, the shorter-step StepWPR on the current forming T1 bar is below the longer-step StepWPR and the shorter-step StepWPR on the last completed T1 bar is at or above the longer-step StepWPR, and short entries are enabled
Exit: long, long closes are enabled AND the shorter-step StepWPR on the current forming T1 bar is below the longer-step StepWPR AND (the shorter-step StepWPR on the last completed T1 bar is below the longer-step StepWPR OR short entries are disabled)
Exit: short, short closes are enabled AND the shorter-step StepWPR on the current forming T1 bar is above the longer-step StepWPR AND (the shorter-step StepWPR on the last completed T1 bar is above the longer-step StepWPR OR long entries are disabled)
