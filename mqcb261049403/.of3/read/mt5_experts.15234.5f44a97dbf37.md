Calculation: Pending long-confirmation low = low at the latest bullish MA cross whose RSI filter passed while confirmation was enabled, until a long signal occurs or a qualifying bearish MA cross supersedes it
Calculation: Pending short-confirmation high = high at the latest bearish MA cross whose RSI filter passed while confirmation was enabled, until a short signal occurs or a qualifying bullish MA cross supersedes it
Calculation: Previous long-signal low = low at the previous long signal on T1, initialized to zero
Calculation: Previous short-signal high = high at the previous short signal on T1, initialized to zero
Long: On indicator bars of T1, using the bar at the signal-offset parameter (forming at offset zero, completed at positive offsets), (MA(close, shorter lookback, MA method, MA shift) crosses above MA(close, longer lookback, MA method, MA shift) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) > 50) AND confirmation distance is zero OR pending long-confirmation low is defined AND high >= pending long-confirmation low + confirmation distance) AND abs(low - previous long-signal low) >= flat distance
Short: On indicator bars of T1, using the bar at the signal-offset parameter (forming at offset zero, completed at positive offsets), (MA(close, shorter lookback, MA method, MA shift) crosses below MA(close, longer lookback, MA method, MA shift) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(close, RSI lookback) < 50) AND confirmation distance is zero OR pending short-confirmation high is defined AND low <= pending short-confirmation high - confirmation distance AND open >= close) AND abs(high - previous short-signal high) >= flat distance
