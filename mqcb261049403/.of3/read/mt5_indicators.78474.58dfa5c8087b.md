Calculation: Channel = a live box bounded by highest(high) and lowest(low) from the bar after a squeeze begins (the falling squeeze measure crosses above the rising squeeze measure) through the bar it ends (the rising squeeze measure crosses above the falling squeeze measure), if its duration exceeds the minimum squeeze duration, its squeeze range is valid, and nested channels are enabled or the box does not overlap a live box; a breakout makes the box inactive, and channel checks run newest first, skipping the next older live box after each breakout
Long: For an eligible live channel, (body-midpoint breakout enabled AND (open+close)/2 > channel top) OR (body-midpoint breakout disabled AND close > channel top)
Short: For an eligible live channel, (body-midpoint breakout enabled AND (open+close)/2 < channel bottom) OR (body-midpoint breakout disabled AND close < channel bottom)
