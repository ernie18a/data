Calculation: Color(T1 bar) = 1 if the sum over the lookback of consecutive T1 close changes (+1 when close is greater than the previous close, −1 otherwise) is greater than 0, else 0
Long: long-entry permission is on AND ((mode assigns the 1-to-0 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 1 AND Color(T1, latest completed bar) = 0) OR (mode assigns the 0-to-1 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 0 AND Color(T1, latest completed bar) = 1))
Short: short-entry permission is on AND ((mode assigns the 1-to-0 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 0 AND Color(T1, latest completed bar) = 1) OR (mode assigns the 0-to-1 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 1 AND Color(T1, latest completed bar) = 0))
Exit: long, long-exit permission is on AND ((mode assigns the 1-to-0 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 0) OR (mode assigns the 0-to-1 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 1))
Exit: short, short-exit permission is on AND ((mode assigns the 1-to-0 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 1) OR (mode assigns the 0-to-1 color transition to long AND Color(T1, prior completed bar) = 0))
