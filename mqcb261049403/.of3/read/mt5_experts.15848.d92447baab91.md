Calculation: Long trailing candidate = on each chart bar, use the selected method: the latest valid low(T1,n) − offset among preceding completed T1 bars within the candle scan depth, where candidate < close − d; or the latest valid low(T1,n) − offset within the fractal scan depth, where n is at least two completed T1 bars ago, low[n] < low[n+1], low[n] < low[n−1], low[n] < low[n+2], and candidate < close − d; or close − ATR(ATR lookback,T1,last completed bar) − offset; or Parabolic SAR(SAR step,SAR maximum,T1,last completed bar) − offset if candidate ≤ close − d; or Moving Average(applied price,MA lookback,MA method,T1,last completed bar) − offset if candidate ≤ close − d; or e + (close − e) × profit percentage / 100; or close − offset. For virtual trailing, d is zero and e is the volume-weighted average long entry price; otherwise d is the instrument's minimum stop distance and e is the position entry price. These calculations use T1 values on chart bars.
Calculation: Short trailing candidate = on each chart bar, use the selected method: the latest valid high(T1,n) + offset among preceding completed T1 bars within the candle scan depth, where candidate > close + d; or the latest valid high(T1,n) + offset within the fractal scan depth, where n is at least two completed T1 bars ago, high[n] > high[n+1], high[n] > high[n−1], high[n] > high[n+2], and candidate > close + d; or close + ATR(ATR lookback,T1,last completed bar) + offset; or Parabolic SAR(SAR step,SAR maximum,T1,last completed bar) + offset if candidate ≥ close + d; or Moving Average(applied price,MA lookback,MA method,T1,last completed bar) + offset if candidate ≥ close + d; or e − (e − close) × profit percentage / 100; or close + offset. For virtual trailing, d is zero and e is the volume-weighted average short entry price; otherwise d is the instrument's minimum stop distance and e is the position entry price. These calculations use T1 values on chart bars.
Calculation: Long virtual stop = the running maximum of long trailing candidates that exceed the volume-weighted average long entry price by the start distance; initially unset.
Calculation: Short virtual stop = the running minimum of short trailing candidates that are below the volume-weighted average short entry price by the start distance; initially unset.
Calculation: Long broker stop = the prior stop, replaced by the long trailing candidate when candidate > prior stop + step distance and candidate ≥ position entry price + start distance; initially the position's existing stop.
Calculation: Short broker stop = the prior stop, replaced by the short trailing candidate when (prior stop is unset or candidate < prior stop − step distance) and candidate ≤ position entry price − start distance; initially the position's existing stop.
Exit: long, low ≤ long virtual stop
Exit: long, low ≤ long broker stop
Exit: short, high ≥ short virtual stop
Exit: short, high ≥ short broker stop
