Calculation: Monday candle = (open of first completed T2 bar for Monday, maximum high, minimum low, close of last completed T2 bar for Monday)
Long: on each new T1 bar, last completed T2 bar's weekday is Monday AND Monday close <= Monday open AND (ATR filter is disabled OR Monday high - Monday low >= minimum range multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar))
Short: on each new T1 bar, last completed T2 bar's weekday is Monday AND Monday close > Monday open AND (ATR filter is disabled OR Monday high - Monday low >= minimum range multiplier * ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar))
Exit: long, (stop multiplier > 0 AND low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar at entry) * stop multiplier) OR (stop multiplier > 0 AND reward/risk multiplier > 0 AND high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar at entry) * stop multiplier * reward/risk multiplier) OR hour(datetime) >= close hour
Exit: short, (stop multiplier > 0 AND high >= entry price + ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar at entry) * stop multiplier) OR (stop multiplier > 0 AND reward/risk multiplier > 0 AND low <= entry price - ATR(ATR lookback, T1, last completed bar at entry) * stop multiplier * reward/risk multiplier) OR hour(datetime) >= close hour
