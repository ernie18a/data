Calculation: fractal direction(frame, shift) = bullish when the upper Fractals buffer is zero and the lower buffer is nonzero; bearish when the lower buffer is zero and the upper buffer is nonzero; neutral otherwise. Read the bar selected by shift in frame on chart bars; it is forming when shift selects the current frame bar and completed otherwise.
Calculation: channel range = Highest(High, channel lookback, chart bars beginning at the previous completed bar) - Lowest(Low, channel lookback, chart bars beginning at the previous completed bar)
Calculation: perceptron score = (weight A - weight center) × (High[first recent offset] - High[first older offset]) + (weight B - weight center) × (High[second recent offset] - High[second older offset]) + (weight C - weight center) × (Low[first recent offset] - Low[first older offset]) + (weight D - weight center) × (Low[second recent offset] - Low[second older offset]), using chart bars
