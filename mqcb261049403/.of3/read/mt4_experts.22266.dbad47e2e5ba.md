Calculation: CCI divergence = scan chart-bar offsets from divergence lookback down through the current bar; at each offset, return bullish if CCI(typical price, CCI lookback, nearer lag) > CCI(typical price, CCI lookback, offset) AND high[nearer lag] < high[offset], otherwise return bearish if CCI(typical price, CCI lookback, nearer lag) < CCI(typical price, CCI lookback, offset) AND high[nearer lag] > high[offset]; otherwise no signal
Calculation: RSI divergence = scan chart-bar offsets from divergence lookback down through the current bar; at each offset, return bullish if RSI(typical price, divergence RSI lookback, nearer lag) > RSI(typical price, divergence RSI lookback, offset) AND high[nearer lag] < high[offset], otherwise return bearish if RSI(typical price, divergence RSI lookback, nearer lag) < RSI(typical price, divergence RSI lookback, offset) AND high[nearer lag] > high[offset]; otherwise no signal
Long: abs(bid - close[farther lag]) > divergence lookback × point size AND (CCI divergence is bullish OR RSI divergence is bullish) AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1, current/forming bar) > LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1, current/forming bar) on chart bars AND low[farther lag] < high[nearer lag] AND RSI(close, T2 RSI lookback, T2 current/forming bar) > RSI(close, T2 RSI lookback, T2 last completed bar) on chart bars
Short: abs(ask - close[farther lag]) > divergence lookback × point size AND (CCI divergence is bearish OR RSI divergence is bearish) AND LWMA(typical price, shorter lookback, T1, current/forming bar) < LWMA(typical price, longer lookback, T1, current/forming bar) on chart bars AND low[nearer lag] < high[farther lag] AND RSI(close, T2 RSI lookback, T2 current/forming bar) < RSI(close, T2 RSI lookback, T2 last completed bar) on chart bars
Calculation: Long initial stop = bid when entering − stop-loss distance
Calculation: Long target = bid when entering + take-profit distance
Calculation: Long protective stop = max(long initial stop, entry price + break-even offset after break-even is enabled and price rises above its trigger, highest high since entry − trailing distance after price rises above the trailing distance)
Exit: long, bid <= long protective stop OR bid >= long target
Calculation: Short initial stop = ask when entering + stop-loss distance
Calculation: Short target = ask when entering − take-profit distance
Calculation: Short protective stop = min(short initial stop, entry price − break-even offset after break-even is enabled and price falls below its trigger, lowest low since entry + trailing distance after price falls below the trailing distance)
Exit: short, ask >= short protective stop OR ask <= short target
