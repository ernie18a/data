Calculation: Support 1 = 2 × Pivot − high and Resistance 1 = 2 × Pivot − low; for levels n from 2 through 6, Support n = low + (n − 1) × (Pivot − high) and Resistance n = high + (n − 1) × (Pivot − low), using the same T1 bar.
Calculation: Strong support = midpoint(Support 1, Support 2); strong resistance = midpoint(Resistance 1, Resistance 2).
Calculation: Four intermediate levels = Support 1 + f × (Resistance 1 − Support 1), using four separate increasing fraction parameters.
