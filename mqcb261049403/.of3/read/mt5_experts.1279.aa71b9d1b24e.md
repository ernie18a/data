Calculation: asymmetric stochastic = 100 × (sum of close over the slowing bars − adjusted minimum) / adjusted range; sum the maxima and minima across those bars using the shorter lookback for both initially, switch to shorter-max/longer-min when the previous value exceeds overbought, switch to longer-max/shorter-min when it falls below oversold, and otherwise retain the prior pair; use close maxima/minima or high maxima and low minima according to the range-source parameter; if the raw range is less than sensitivity × slowing, use that threshold as the adjusted range and lower the minimum by half the difference; if the adjusted range is zero, return −2
Long: long-entry permission enabled AND asymmetric stochastic on T1 bars at the last completed bar < its signal line AND asymmetric stochastic on T1 bars at the preceding completed bar > its signal line
Short: short-entry permission enabled AND asymmetric stochastic on T1 bars at the last completed bar > its signal line AND asymmetric stochastic on T1 bars at the preceding completed bar < its signal line
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
