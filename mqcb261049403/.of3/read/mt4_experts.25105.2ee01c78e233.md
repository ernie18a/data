Calculation: mean open pips = mean of (close - entry price) in pips for each running long and (entry price - close) in pips for each running short in the strategy group for the chart instrument
Short: on each new chart bar, Williams %R(high, low, close, entry WPR lookback) on the still-forming chart bar > entry threshold AND MACD(close, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, main line) on the still-forming chart bar > MACD threshold AND Stochastic(high, low, entry K lookback, entry D lookback, entry slowing, SMA, low/high, main line) on the still-forming chart bar < entry threshold AND Stochastic(high, low, entry K lookback, entry D lookback, entry slowing, SMA, low/high, main line)[1] < entry threshold
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (mean open pips < first profit threshold AND Williams %R(high, low, close, exit WPR lookback) on the still-forming chart bar < exit threshold) OR (mean open pips > second profit threshold AND Stochastic(high, low, exit K lookback, exit D lookback, exit slowing, SMA, low/high, main line) on the still-forming chart bar < exit threshold)
