Long: on each chart bar, LWMA(typical price, short lookback, T1, last completed bar) > LWMA(typical price, middle lookback, T1, last completed bar) > LWMA(typical price, long lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) > LWMA(typical price, middle lookback, chart, last completed bar) > LWMA(typical price, long lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND low[1] <= LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND close > LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND low[2] < high[1] AND abs(close - close[2]) > retracement parameter × point size; short, middle, and long lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest and shared across T1 and chart.
Short: on each chart bar, LWMA(typical price, short lookback, T1, last completed bar) < LWMA(typical price, middle lookback, T1, last completed bar) < LWMA(typical price, long lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) < LWMA(typical price, middle lookback, chart, last completed bar) < LWMA(typical price, long lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND high[1] >= LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND close < LWMA(typical price, short lookback, chart, last completed bar) AND low[1] < high[2] AND abs(close - close[1]) > retracement parameter × point size; short, middle, and long lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest and shared across T1 and chart.
Calculation: Renko threshold = ATR(ATR lookback, forming chart bar) in ATR mode; otherwise, box-size parameter × pip size.
Long: in Renko mode, the first bar in an oldest-to-current scan of the retracement lookback for which close - low or high - close reaches the Renko threshold satisfies close - low >= the Renko threshold.
Short: in Renko mode, the first bar in an oldest-to-current scan of the retracement lookback for which close - low or high - close reaches the Renko threshold satisfies high - close >= the Renko threshold AND close - low < the Renko threshold.
Long: the first upper fractal in an oldest-to-current scan of the fractal lookback for which high[1] < its level OR high[2] < its level OR high[3] < its level has close >= its level.
Short: the first lower fractal in an oldest-to-current scan of the fractal lookback for which low[1] > its level OR low[2] > its level OR low[3] > its level has close <= its level.
Exit: long, close <= entry price - stop-loss distance in pips OR close >= entry price + take-profit distance in pips.
Exit: short, close >= entry price + stop-loss distance in pips OR close <= entry price - take-profit distance in pips.
