Long: (second branch enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR MA(applied price, filter lookback, smoothing type, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) > MA(applied price, filter lookback, smoothing type, T1 bar[n+1] sampled on chart bars) AND RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n+1] < second low threshold AND RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n] > RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n+1] AND RSI(close, T1 RSI lookback, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) < second high threshold AND MA(applied price, longer MA lookback, smoothing type)[n] > MA(applied price, longer MA lookback, smoothing type)[n+1]) OR (first branch enabled AND RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n+1] < first low threshold AND RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n] > RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n+1] AND RSI(close, T1 RSI lookback, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) < first high threshold AND MA(applied price, shorter MA lookback, smoothing type)[n] > MA(applied price, shorter MA lookback, smoothing type)[n+1])
Short: (second branch enabled AND (trend filter disabled OR MA(applied price, filter lookback, smoothing type, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) < MA(applied price, filter lookback, smoothing type, T1 bar[n+1] sampled on chart bars) AND RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n+1] > second high threshold AND RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n] < RSI(close, longer RSI lookback)[n+1] AND RSI(close, T1 RSI lookback, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) > second low threshold AND MA(applied price, longer MA lookback, smoothing type)[n] < MA(applied price, longer MA lookback, smoothing type)[n+1]) OR (first branch enabled AND RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n+1] > first high threshold AND RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n] < RSI(close, shorter RSI lookback)[n+1] AND RSI(close, T1 RSI lookback, T1 bar[n] sampled on chart bars, forming when n=0) > first low threshold AND MA(applied price, shorter MA lookback, smoothing type)[n] < MA(applied price, shorter MA lookback, smoothing type)[n+1])
Calculation: Long protective stop = initially 0 if stop-loss multiple is zero, otherwise entry price − ATR(shorter MA lookback, chart bar at entry) × stop-loss multiple; on each price update, set to close − trailing distance if trailing distance > 0 AND close − entry price > trailing distance + trailing step AND previous stop < close − trailing distance − trailing step, otherwise retain previous stop
Calculation: Short protective stop = initially 0 if stop-loss multiple is zero, otherwise entry price + ATR(shorter MA lookback, chart bar at entry) × stop-loss multiple; on each price update, set to close + trailing distance if trailing distance > 0 AND entry price − close > trailing distance + trailing step AND (previous stop > close + trailing distance + trailing step OR previous stop = 0), otherwise retain previous stop
Exit: long, protective stop > 0 AND low <= protective stop OR take-profit multiple > 0 AND high >= entry price + ATR(shorter MA lookback, chart bar at entry) × take-profit multiple
Exit: short, protective stop > 0 AND high >= protective stop OR low <= entry price − ATR(shorter MA lookback, chart bar at entry) × stop-loss multiple
