Calculation: Bullish MACD divergence = on each new chart bar, use the MACD main line of close with separate bullish fast EMA, slow EMA, and signal SMA lookbacks on completed chart bars; require a recent candidate window below negative threshold with its center a strict local minimum, then scan older candidate windows from the configured minimum offset toward older bars, requiring each window below negative threshold and its center a strict local minimum; accept the first older candidate and then only successively more negative candidates; pair each accepted candidate with the first low in its configured search interval that is below the lows of the configured number of next older bars, and pair the recent candidate with the first low found by the same pivot test in its own interval, including the still-forming chart bar; use zero when no qualifying low is found; signal when candidate depth is within its maximum and either older MACD is greater than recent MACD while older low is lower than recent low, or older MACD is lower than recent MACD while older low is greater than recent low; stop at the first signal or first older candidate that is not more negative.
Calculation: Bearish MACD divergence = on each new chart bar, use the MACD main line of close with separate bearish fast EMA, slow EMA, and signal SMA lookbacks on completed chart bars; require a recent candidate window above positive threshold with its center a strict local maximum, then scan older candidate windows from the configured minimum offset toward older bars, requiring each window above positive threshold and its center a strict local maximum; accept the first older candidate and then only successively more positive candidates; pair each accepted candidate with the first high in its configured search interval that is above the highs of the configured number of next older bars, and pair the recent candidate with the first high found by the same pivot test in its own interval, including the still-forming chart bar; use zero when no qualifying high is found; signal when candidate depth is within its maximum and either older MACD is greater than recent MACD while older high is lower than recent high, or older MACD is lower than recent MACD while older high is greater than recent high; stop at the first signal or first older candidate that is not more positive.
Long: no open position AND RSI(close, long RSI lookback) on the last completed chart bar < long RSI threshold AND Bullish MACD divergence
Short: no open position AND RSI(close, short RSI lookback) on the last completed chart bar > short RSI threshold AND Bearish MACD divergence
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop-loss distance in pips OR high >= entry price + long take-profit distance in pips
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short stop-loss distance in pips OR low <= entry price - short take-profit distance in pips
