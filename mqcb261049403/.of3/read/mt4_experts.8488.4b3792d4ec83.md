Calculation: normalized deviation product = Σ(xy)/√(Σx²Σy²), where x and y are each instrument’s T1 close minus its same-bar SMA(close, N, T1), summed over the N T1 bars through the bar still forming; N is the chart timeframe duration in minutes; evaluate on chart bars using T1 values; defined only when both sums of squares are positive.
Long: EMA(close, shorter lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) > EMA(close, longer lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, bar still forming) ≤ its threshold AND normalized deviation product < 0.
Short: EMA(close, shorter lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) < EMA(close, longer lookback, chart timeframe, last completed bar) AND RSI(close, lookback, chart timeframe, bar still forming) ≥ its threshold AND normalized deviation product < 0.
