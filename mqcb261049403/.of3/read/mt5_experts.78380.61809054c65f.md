Calculation: entry ATR = ATR(ATR lookback, last completed chart bar before entry)
Long: flat AND the last completed chart bar opens within the configured signal-hour window on an enabled weekday AND sample SD(close / close[1] - 1, shorter volatility lookback) / sample SD(close / close[1] - 1, longer volatility lookback) < volatility ratio threshold AND (volume - SMA(volume, volume z-score lookback)) / sample SD(volume, volume z-score lookback) > minimum volume z-score AND (quiet-regime volume filter disabled OR SMA(volume, quiet-regime lookback) < quiet-regime volume ceiling) AND at least one of the most recent coil-recency lookback bars has a high - low range no greater than the range of each of its preceding six bars
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop multiplier * entry ATR OR high >= entry price + target multiplier * entry ATR OR bars since entry >= maximum holding bars OR server time reaches the earlier of the configured flat time and broker session end minus the close buffer
