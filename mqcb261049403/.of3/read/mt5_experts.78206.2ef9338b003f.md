Calculation: Bullish first-order signal = on each chart update, using the last completed T1 bar (and preceding completed T1 bar for crosses), MA(close, shorter lookback, method, T1) crosses above MA(close, longer lookback, method, T1) when the MA signal is selected, or RSI(close, lookback, T1) <= buy threshold when the RSI signal is selected, or T1 close < Bollinger lower band(close, lookback, deviation, T1) when the Bollinger signal is selected, or Stochastic %K(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) <= oversold threshold AND Stochastic %K(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) > Stochastic %D(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) when the Stochastic signal is selected
Calculation: Bearish first-order signal = on each chart update, using the last completed T1 bar (and preceding completed T1 bar for crosses), MA(close, shorter lookback, method, T1) crosses below MA(close, longer lookback, method, T1) when the MA signal is selected, or RSI(close, lookback, T1) >= sell threshold when the RSI signal is selected, or T1 close > Bollinger upper band(close, lookback, deviation, T1) when the Bollinger signal is selected, or Stochastic %K(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) >= overbought threshold AND Stochastic %K(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) < Stochastic %D(high, low, close, K lookback, D lookback, slowing, T1) when the Stochastic signal is selected
Calculation: First-order time eligibility = session filter disabled OR (start hour <= end hour AND current hour >= start hour AND current hour < end hour) OR (start hour > end hour AND (current hour >= start hour OR current hour < end hour)), AND Friday cutoff disabled OR current datetime is before the Friday cutoff
Calculation: Grid step(count) = (fixed step in points × point size when fixed mode is selected, otherwise ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar) × ATR multiplier) × step expansion multiplier^max(0, count − 1); evaluated on each chart update using the last completed T2 bar
Calculation: Adverse entry extreme(side) = minimum open price among positions on the long side, or maximum open price among positions on the short side
Calculation: Basket average entry price(side) = sum(position open price × position volume) / sum(position volume) for positions on that side
Calculation: Basket profit(side) = sum of current position profit plus swap for positions on that side
Calculation: Peak basket profit(side) = running maximum basket profit while basket profit is at least the trailing activation threshold; reset when that side has no positions
Long: Long direction enabled AND ((no long positions AND first-order time eligible AND (signal filter disabled OR (signal reversal disabled AND bullish first-order signal) OR (signal reversal enabled AND bearish first-order signal))) OR (long position count >= 1 AND long position count < maximum levels per side AND close <= adverse entry extreme(long) − grid step(long position count))) AND (minimum margin filter disabled OR margin level <= 0 OR margin level >= minimum margin level)
Short: Short direction enabled AND ((no short positions AND first-order time eligible AND (signal filter disabled OR (signal reversal disabled AND bearish first-order signal) OR (signal reversal enabled AND bullish first-order signal))) OR (short position count >= 1 AND short position count < maximum levels per side AND close >= adverse entry extreme(short) + grid step(short position count))) AND (minimum margin filter disabled OR margin level <= 0 OR margin level >= minimum margin level)
Exit: long, basket profit(long) <= −basket stop-loss amount when basket stop-loss is enabled
Exit: short, basket profit(short) <= −basket stop-loss amount when basket stop-loss is enabled
Exit: long, trailing take profit enabled AND basket profit(long) >= trailing activation threshold AND basket profit(long) <= peak basket profit(long) − trailing pullback amount
Exit: short, trailing take profit enabled AND basket profit(short) >= trailing activation threshold AND basket profit(short) <= peak basket profit(short) − trailing pullback amount
Exit: long, trailing take profit disabled AND money take profit enabled AND basket profit(long) >= money take-profit threshold
Exit: short, trailing take profit disabled AND money take profit enabled AND basket profit(short) >= money take-profit threshold
Exit: long, points take profit enabled AND close >= basket average entry price(long) + take-profit points × point size
Exit: short, points take profit enabled AND close <= basket average entry price(short) − take-profit points × point size
Exit: long, Friday close enabled AND current datetime is Friday at or after the configured close hour
Exit: short, Friday close enabled AND current datetime is Friday at or after the configured close hour
