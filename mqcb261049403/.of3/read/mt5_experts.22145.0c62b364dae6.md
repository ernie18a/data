Long: high of the current forming chart bar >= high of the last completed T1 bar + breakout distance AND (fast lookback = 0 OR slow lookback = 0 OR MA(fast applied price, fast lookback, fast shift, smoothing method, chart bars, current forming bar) > MA(slow applied price, slow lookback, slow shift, smoothing method, chart bars, current forming bar))
Short: low of the current forming chart bar <= low of the last completed T1 bar - breakout distance AND (fast lookback = 0 OR slow lookback = 0 OR MA(fast applied price, fast lookback, fast shift, smoothing method, chart bars, current forming bar) < MA(slow applied price, slow lookback, slow shift, smoothing method, chart bars, current forming bar))
Exit: long, (stop-loss distance > 0 AND low of the current forming chart bar <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND high of the current forming chart bar >= entry price + take-profit distance)
Exit: short, (stop-loss distance > 0 AND high of the current forming chart bar >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND low of the current forming chart bar <= entry price - take-profit distance)
