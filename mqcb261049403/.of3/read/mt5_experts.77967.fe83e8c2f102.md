Calculation: Opening-range high = maximum high of completed T1 bars in the configured opening-range interval
Calculation: Opening-range low = minimum low of completed T1 bars in the configured opening-range interval
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening-range high - opening-range low
Calculation: Stop distance = opening-range size * stop-distance fraction + 2 * entry buffer
Long: high >= opening-range high + entry buffer AND datetime is at or after the configured opening-range end and before the configured entry cutoff AND long trading is enabled AND no trade has already been taken that session day AND (weekday is not Friday OR Friday trading is enabled) AND opening-range size > 0 AND (minimum range-to-ATR filter is disabled OR opening-range size >= minimum range-to-ATR multiple * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND (maximum range-to-ATR filter is disabled OR opening-range size <= maximum range-to-ATR multiple * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar))
Short: low <= opening-range low - entry buffer AND datetime is at or after the configured opening-range end and before the configured entry cutoff AND short trading is enabled AND no trade has already been taken that session day AND (weekday is not Friday OR Friday trading is enabled) AND opening-range size > 0 AND (minimum range-to-ATR filter is disabled OR opening-range size >= minimum range-to-ATR multiple * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar)) AND (maximum range-to-ATR filter is disabled OR opening-range size <= maximum range-to-ATR multiple * ATR(lookback, T2, last completed bar))
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR (take-profit multiple is enabled AND high >= entry price + stop distance * take-profit multiple) OR datetime >= configured close time OR position entry datetime < current session day start
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR (take-profit multiple is enabled AND low <= entry price - stop distance * take-profit multiple) OR datetime >= configured close time OR position entry datetime < current session day start
