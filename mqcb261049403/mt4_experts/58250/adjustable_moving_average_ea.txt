📈 Adjustable Moving Average EA for MT4
This Expert Advisor trades based on the crossover of two customizable moving averages. It’s designed for traders who want full control over trade direction, entry logic, and risk settings.
⚙️ Input Parameters Explained:
FastPeriod
: Period of the fast moving average.
SlowPeriod
: Period of the slow moving average.
MAPriceType
: Price used in MA calculation (e.g., Close, Open, High, Low).
SL_Points
: Stop Loss in points. Set to 0 to disable.
TP_Points
: Take Profit in points. Set to 0 to disable.
TrailStopPips
: Enables a trailing stop after the trade is in profit.
EntryMode
: Choose between Long-only, Short-only, or Both directions.
FixedLot
: Lot size used for each trade.
MaxSlippage
: Maximum allowed slippage in points.
TradeComment
: Adds a comment to each trade for identification in history.
