An indicator that draws the
XPeriodCandle
candlestick sizes in points.
The candlesticks having their body sizes increasing by trend are drawn in bright colors. The candlesticks having their body sizes decreasing by trend are drawn in pale colors.
The indicator uses the
SmoothAlgorithms.mqh
library classes. Working with them was described in details in article
Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers
.
