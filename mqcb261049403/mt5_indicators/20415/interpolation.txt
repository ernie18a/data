The indicator draws on a price chart a line, constructed by the method of polynomial interpolation between two node lines.
It has four input parameters:
Length
- the initial distance between two node lines (in bars);
Power
- the sensitivity parameter (the number of line calculation iterations);
Applied price
- the calculation price;
Drawing style of node line
- choose the style for the node lines.
