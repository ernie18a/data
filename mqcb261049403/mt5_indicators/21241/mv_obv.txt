Unlike standard On Balance Volume calculated on Close prices only, and indicator MultiVote OBV is calculated on HLC prices, which resulted in its higher sensitivity.
The indicator has no configurable parameters. Negative values are drawn in
red
, positive ones in
green
.
Calculations:
MVOBV = PrevMVOBV + TotalVote * Volume
where:
TotalVote = HighVote + LowVote + CloseVote
If High > PrevHigh:
HighVote = 1
If High < PrevHigh:
HighVote = -1
If Low > PrevLow:
LowVote = 1
If Low < PrevLow:
LowVote = -1
If Close > PrevClose:
CloseVote = 1
If Close < PrevClose:
CloseVote = -1
