Simple Anchored VWAP for MetaTrader 5
Anchored VWAP (AVWAP)
calculates the volume-weighted average price (fair value benchmark) starting from a specific, user-defined point in time rather than an arbitrary midnight session reset.
Popularized by Brian Shannon, Anchored VWAP enables traders to track the true break-even benchmark price of institutional participants from key market catalysts:
Major economic news releases (CPI, Non-Farm Payrolls, Rate Decisions)
Key swing highs and swing lows
Session opens (London, New York, Tokyo)
Weekly and Monthly opens
Features
Custom Anchor Date/Time:
Anchor calculation to any historical date/time.
Auto Volume Detection:
Automatically detects whether your broker provides Tick Volume (Forex) or Real Volume (Futures, Indices, Stocks, Crypto).
Applied Price Flexibility:
Calculate based on Typical Price (H+L+C)/3, Close, Median, or Weighted price.
Standard Deviation Bands:
Optional Upper and Lower volatility bands (+/- 1 SD or custom multiplier) to identify statistical overbought/oversold extremes.
Zero Memory Leaks:
Fully optimized OnCalculate routine suitable for backtesting and multi-timeframe analysis.
Input Parameters
InpAnchorTime:
Anchor Date and Time (e.g. 2026.10.01 00:00).
InpAppliedPrice:
Price source (Default: PRICE_TYPICAL).
InpVolumeType:
VOL_AUTO (auto-detect), VOL_TICK, or VOL_REAL.
InpShowBands:
Enable/Disable volatility standard deviation bands.
InpBandMultiplier:
Standard deviation multiplier (Default: 1.0).
