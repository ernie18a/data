An indicator for trend following trading implemented in the form of a colored cloud. Open a position when the color changes. Monotonic convergence of the lines indicates the fading trend.
This indicator is actually a combination of the bull and bear power indicators, one of which is upside down.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (must be copied to the
terminal_data_folder
\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
Originally this indicator has been written in MQL4 and was first published in the
Code Base
on 20.03.2012.
