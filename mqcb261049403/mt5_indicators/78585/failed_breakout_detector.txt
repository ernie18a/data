Failed Breakout Detector marks the breakouts of the recent trading range and, more important, the breakouts that fail. It is a small price action tool: it does not predict anything, it only shows where the traders who chased a breakout got trapped.
US500 M5: a failed breakout down (green arrow) at 17:00, the trapped sellers fuel the rally that follows. Blue dots mark the breakout bars.
How it reads the chart
The range is the highest high and the lowest low of the last N bars (default 12).
A bar that closes outside that range, by at least a small ATR-based buffer, is a breakout. It is marked with a small blue dot.
If one of the next K bars (default 3) closes back inside the range, the breakout has failed. A failed breakout up is marked with a red arrow above the bar, a failed breakout down with a green arrow below the bar.
If price holds outside the range for K bars, the breakout is confirmed and nothing more is drawn.
A label in the chart corner counts the failed breakouts of the current day (up / down).
Why failed breakouts matter
Traders who bought a breakout up and then see a bar close back inside the range are trapped. They have to get out, and their selling often starts a move the other way. The same holds for a failed breakout down. On a range day the failed breakouts are the places to fade the edges; on a trend day they are rare, which is a useful piece of information on its own.
Everything is calculated on closed bars only, so nothing repaints. The range is filtered by its height (between 0.5 and 4 ATR by default), so very tight and very wide ranges are ignored. Only one breakout is tracked at a time: the next breakout is looked for once the previous one has failed or has been confirmed.
DE40 M5: two failed breakouts down (17:00 and 17:50) at the low of the afternoon, then the rally to the day high. The label counts the failed breakouts of the day.

Buffers
(for an Expert Advisor via iCustom; the value is 0 where there is no signal)
0 – breakout up (price of the dot), 1 – breakout down
2 – failed breakout up (bearish), 3 – failed breakout down (bullish)
4 – signal: +1 failed breakout down (bullish), -1 failed breakout up (bearish), 0 nothing
5 – number of failed breakouts on the bar's day, so far
