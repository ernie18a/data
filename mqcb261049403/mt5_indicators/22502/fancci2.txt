Fan CCI with Average line is a CCI fan in the form of an oscillator in a separate window with the average line.
Oscillator values oscillate with the +1/-1 range
There are seven configurable parameters:
First CCI period
- first CCI calculation period
Applied price
CCI count
- number of CCIs in the fan
Incremental method
- method of incrementing CCI period values:
Addition
Multiplication
Coefficient of increment
- increment ratio
Smoothing period
- middle line smoothing period
Smoothing method
- middle line calculation method
