Trend indicator, based on crossover of pivot level and its median.
Pivot Level is calculated by formula:
PL=(MAX+MIN+CLOSE)/3
where:
PL - Pivot level;
MAX - maximum price (n bars);
MIN - minimum price (n bars);
CLOSE - close price.
