"Average change price" displays the average change in the price value.
It has three input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Method
- calculation method
Applied price
- price used for calculations
Calculation:
Average change = AvgAC
AC = Price - PrevPrice
Price - Applied price
AvgAC - MA(AC, Period, Method)
