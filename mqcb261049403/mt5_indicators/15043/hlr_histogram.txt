The
HighestLowestRange (HLR)
indicator implemented as a histogram with a color indication of entering the overbought and oversold zones. The first bar upon entering the overbought and oversold zones has a brighter color if preceded by an opposite trend.
Originally the indicator has been written in MQL4 and was first published in the
Code Base at mql4.com
on 25.01.2007.
