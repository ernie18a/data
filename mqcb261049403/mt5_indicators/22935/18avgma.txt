Average of 18 Moving Averages shows the average value of 18 different moving averages.
It has two input parameters:
Period
- calculation period of each MA used in calculation
Applied price
- price used for the calculation of each MA
Calculation:
MA = (MA1 + MA2 + MA3 + ... + MA18) / 18.0
Moving averages used in the calculation:
Simple
Exponential
Smoothed
Linear-Weighted
Wilder Exponential
Sine-Weighted
Triangular
Least Square
Hull MA by Alan Hull
Zero-Lag Exponential
Instantaneous Trendline by J.Ehlers
Moving Median
Geometric Mean
Regularized EMA by Chris Satchwell
Integral of Linear Regression Slope
Combination of
LSMA
and
ILRS
Triangular MA generalized by J.Ehlers
Volume-Weighted
