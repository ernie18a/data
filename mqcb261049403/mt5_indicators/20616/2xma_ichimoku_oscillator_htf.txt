Indicator
2XMA_Ichimoku_Oscillator
with the timeframe selection option available in its input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
For the indicator to work, there must be indicators
2XMA_Ichimoku_Oscillator.ex5
and
XMA_Ichimoku.ex5
in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
