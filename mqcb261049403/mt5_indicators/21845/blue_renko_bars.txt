An indicator for constructing Renko bars in the chart sub-window.
Indicator Properties
Brick Size
- brick size in pips;
Show Wicks
- if true, bar shadows will be drawn If false, shadows will not be displayed;
Use full history
- if true, entire available history of the trading instrument is used for the indicator calculation.
