The
LSMA_Angle_
indicator implemented as a sequence of candlesticks. Candlesticks appear as a result of relevant price timeseries processed by the LSMA_Angle_ algorithm.
In many situations, such approach may be more informative for the purpose of analysis.
This indicator requires the compiled indicator file LSMA_Angle_.ex5. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
