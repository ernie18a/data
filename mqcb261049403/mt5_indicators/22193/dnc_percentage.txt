DNC Percentage oscillator displays a relative price position inside the
Donchian channel
in a separate window.
It has two configurable parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Analyze current bar
- consider the value of the current bar in calculations (Yes/No)
Calculation:
Percentage = ((Close - Bottom) / (Top - Bottom)) * 100.0
where:
Top    = Max(High, Period)
Bottom = Min(Low, Period)
