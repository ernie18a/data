Key Features:
Channel Length (n1):
Default 10, adjusts sensitivity of price movements.
Average Length (n2):
Default 21, smooths the WT1 line for clearer signals.
Cross Signals:
Generates notifications when WT1 crosses WT2, indicating potential buy (upward cross) or sell (downward cross) opportunities.
Ideal for traders seeking reliable trend-following and reversal signals across various timeframes and currency pairs.
