The indicator uses the classes of library SmoothAlgorithms.mqh (to be copied to <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in article
Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations without Using Additional Buffers
.
For the indicator to work, the
XFisher_org_1.ex5
indicator must be added to the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
