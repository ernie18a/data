The Slow Stochastic oscillator shows in a separate window a stochastic calculated as follows:
SSD[i] = SMA(FSK,PeriodD)
SSDS[i] = SMA(SSD,Slowing)
where:
FSK[i] = (min / max) * 100.0,
SSD[i] = SMA(FSK,PeriodD),
min = close[i] - low[BL],
max = high[BH] - low[BL],
BH is the highest price index within the range from PeriodK to i,
BL is the lowest price index within the range from PeriodK to i,
if max = 0,
FSK[i] = 50.0
The indicator has five input parameters:
Period K
- the period of calculating line K;
Period D
- the period of calculating line D;
Slowing
- the slowing period;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level.
