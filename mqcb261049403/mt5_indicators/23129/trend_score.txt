Trend Score indicator by Tushar S. Chande was described in the article "Rating Trend Strength" in S&C magazine (September 1993).
It displays both the power and direction of a trend.
There are five inputs:
Price method
- applied prices
Open/Close
- current Open and Close
Close/Close
- past and current Close
Period method
- use the range in calculation
Use period
Do not use period
Overbought
- overbought level
Oversold
- oversold level
