The Trend Force oscillator indicator.
The indicator has three input parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
ATR's period
- ATR parameter;
ATR's coefficient
- ATR parameter.
Calculation:
Trend Force[i] = Up[i]-Dn[i]
where
Up[i]
- the highest value (MaxPrice-ATR_Coeff*ATR) in the range from (i-Period+1) to i;
Dn[i]
- the lowest value (MinPrice+ATR_Coeff*ATR) in the range from (i-Period+1) to i;
MaxPrice
,
MinPrice
- the highest and the lowest price in the range from (i-ATR_Period+1) to i;
ATR
- Average True Range value with the period ATR_Period.
