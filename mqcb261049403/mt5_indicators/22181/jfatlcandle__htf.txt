JFatlCandle_HTF
indicator with the timeframe selection option available in input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
The indicator is presented in two versions:
JFatlCandle_HTF for displaying in additional window;
JFatlCandle__HTF for displaying in the main window.
The indicator requires
JFatl.ex5
and
JFatlCandle.ex5
indicators for operation. Place them in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
