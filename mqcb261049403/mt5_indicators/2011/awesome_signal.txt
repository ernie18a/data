Bill Williams' Awesome Oscillator with the possibility of fine-tuning and an additional signal line in the form of a colored cloud.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (must be copied to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
Originally this indicator has been written in MQL4 and was first published in the
Code Base
on 03.01.2008.
