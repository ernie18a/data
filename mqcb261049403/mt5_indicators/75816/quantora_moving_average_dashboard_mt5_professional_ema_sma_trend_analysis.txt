Quantora Moving Average Dashboard MT5 is a trend analysis indicator for MetaTrader 5 that brings multiple moving averages into one simple dashboard.
It tracks EMA 20, 50, 100, 200 and SMA 50, 200, showing how price is positioned relative to these levels. This helps identify short, medium, and long-term trend structure at a glance.
The indicator combines moving average alignment into a score and an overall market bias, making it easy to see whether conditions are bullish, bearish, or neutral.
It also displays basic market data such as bid, ask, and spread, and updates automatically in real time.
All periods can be adjusted to match different trading styles, and the indicator works on any symbol and timeframe.
This tool is designed for analysis only. It does not open or manage trades.
