The idea behind it
Most trend/range filters (ADX, choppiness index, efficiency ratio) look at how far price has traveled relative to how much ground it covered. This indicator takes a different angle borrowed from
fractal geometry
: a price curve that moves cleanly in one direction has a
low fractal dimension
, close to that of a straight line (1.0), while a curve that whipsaws back and forth fills more "space" and has a
higher fractal dimension
, approaching 2.0. The calculation is a simplified version of
Katz's fractal dimension
method: over a rolling window of
InpFractalPeriod
bars, it sums the total path length walked by price (the sum of bar-to-bar absolute changes) and compares that to the maximum displacement from the window's starting price. A tight ratio between those two numbers (the price traveled far with little backtracking) produces a dimension near 1.0; a loose ratio (price traveled a long path but ended up close to where it started) pushes the dimension toward 2.0.
The raw per-bar value is noisy, so it is smoothed with an EMA (
InpSmoothPeriod
) before being plotted. The indicator is designed to sit in a separate subwindow, with the line colored against two configurable thresholds: below
InpTrendThreshold
the market is classified as trending, above
InpChopThreshold
it is classified as choppy. It works on any symbol and timeframe, but behaves most consistently on liquid instruments and intraday timeframes such as
EURUSD
on an
M15 chart
, where enough bars accumulate inside a session to make the rolling window meaningful.
How to interpret it
Fractal Dimension line
— the single plotted line, bounded between 1.0 and 2.0. Falling values mean price is moving more efficiently in one direction; rising values mean price is covering ground without making net progress.
Katz-style calculation
— for each bar, the indicator measures the total absolute price path over the lookback window and the maximum distance from the window's first price, then derives the dimension from the logarithmic ratio of those two quantities. EMA smoothing (InpSmoothPeriod) removes bar-to-bar jitter without materially lagging genuine regime changes.
Color / zone logic
— the line is drawn in one of three states:
trending
(value at or below InpTrendThreshold),
choppy
(value at or above InpChopThreshold), or
neutral/transition
(between the two). Crossing from the choppy zone down through the neutral band into the trending zone is the earliest signal that a directional move is taking hold.
Practical usage
— use the trending zone as a filter to enable trend-following entries (breakouts, moving-average crosses, momentum signals) and suppress them in the choppy zone, where mean-reversion or range-trading approaches tend to perform better. It is a regime filter, not a standalone entry signal, and works best paired with a directional tool that tells you which way to trade once the regime is confirmed trending.
External variables (inputs)
Input
Default
Purpose
InpFractalPeriod

InpSmoothPeriod
5
EMA smoothing period applied to the raw fractal dimension values to reduce bar-to-bar noise.
InpTrendThreshold
1.40
Smoothed FD value at or below this level is classified as a trending market and colored accordingly.
InpChopThreshold
1.65
Smoothed FD value at or above this level is classified as a choppy/ranging market and colored accordingly.

PRICE_CLOSE
Price series used in the fractal dimension calculation (close, open, high, low, median, typical, or weighted).
Recommended use
Add the indicator to its own subwindow and leave the two threshold levels (1.40 / 1.65) visible as dotted reference lines so the current regime is obvious at a glance. On intraday charts, pair it with a directional trend tool (moving average slope, MACD, or a breakout system) and only act on that tool's signals while the Fractal Dimension line is in or crossing into the trending zone; stand aside or switch to a range-trading approach while it sits in the choppy zone. Because the calculation depends on the lookback window, avoid very short InpFractalPeriod values (below 15-20) on noisy symbols, since the dimension estimate becomes unstable with too few points; widen the window on faster timeframes and narrow it on slower ones to keep the signal responsive without being erratic.
