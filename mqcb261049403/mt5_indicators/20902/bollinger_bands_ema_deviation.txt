Bollinger Bands consist of a
N
-period Moving Average (MA), an Upper Band at
K
times an
N
-period standard deviation above the Moving Average (MA +
Kσ
), and a Lower Band at
K
times an
N
-period standard deviation below the Moving Average (MA −
Kσ
).
This version does not use standard deviation for Upper and Lower Bands, but uses the EMA deviation. That makes it faster in the response to market volatility.
