ATR Dual displays data from two indicators ATR with different averaging periods.
Averaging periods are set in parameters
ATR "Master" averaging period
and
ATR "Slave" averaging period
.
By default,
ATR "Master" averaging period
is 14 and displayed as a dotted line, while the
ATR "Slave" averaging period
is 1 and displayed as a histogram.
How to interpret the indicator data at the current bar: If histogram
"Slave"
has started approaching the
"Master"
line, it means that the volatility on the current bar differs strongly from the mean value over
ATR "Master" averaging period
periods.
