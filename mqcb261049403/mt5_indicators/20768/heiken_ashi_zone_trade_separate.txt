Heiken Ashi Zone Trade - Separate indicator is based on 3 indicators:
Heiken Ashi.
Accelerator Oscillator.
Awesome Oscillator.
The combined states and values of the 3 are producing zones and trade signals.
This version of
Heiken Ashi Zone Trade
indicator is made to display values in a separate window - that way it will not interfere with the other on-chart indicators and can be easier to read the states in some cases.
