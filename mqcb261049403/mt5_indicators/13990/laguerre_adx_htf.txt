The
Laguerre_PlusDi
and
Laguerre_MinusDi
indicators in a single window in the form of a colored cloud with the timeframe selection option available in the input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
The indicator requires Laguerre_PlusDi.mq5 and Laguerre_MinusDi.mq5 indicator files. Place them in terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
