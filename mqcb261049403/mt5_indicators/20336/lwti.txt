An indicator of Larry Williams' COT index (
LWPI
), with a different calculation formula
It has one input parameter:
Period
- calculation period
Calculation:
MovingAvg(Close - Close[Period], bars used in average)/MovingAvg(Range,bars used in average)*50 + 50
