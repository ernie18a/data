The indicator allows customizing the day beginning hour, the vertical shift of candlesticks, as well as colors of bullish, bearish and doji candlesticks.
It has five input parameters:
Hour of begin day
- day beginning hour;
Candles vertical shift
- vertical shift for the candlesticks;
Color of bullish candle
- select color for bullish candlesticks;
Color of bearish candle
- select color for bearish candlesticks;
Color of doji candle
- select color for doji candlesticks.
