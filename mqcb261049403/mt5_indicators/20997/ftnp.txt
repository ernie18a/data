An oscillator using Fischer Transform of normalized prices.
Normalizing price flows by Fischer Transform allows you to detect the peak oscillations of the indicator line, which indicate the trend resersal points.
The indicator has two input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Applied price
- calculation price
Calculation:
Fisher = 0.5*(Log((1+V) / (1-V)) + PrevFisher)
Trigger = PrevFisher
where:
V = (2/3) * ((Price - MinPrice) / (MaxPrice - MinPrice) - 0.5 + PrevV)
MinPrice, MaxPrice - the minimum and the maximum prices within the Period range Period
Log - natural logarithm
