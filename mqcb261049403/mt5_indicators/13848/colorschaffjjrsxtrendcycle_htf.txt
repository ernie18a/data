The
ColorSchaffJJRSXTrendCycle
indicator with the timeframe selection option available in the input parameters:
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
The indicator requires JJRSX.mq5 and ColorSchaffJJRSXTrendCycle.mq5 indicator files. Place them in terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators.
