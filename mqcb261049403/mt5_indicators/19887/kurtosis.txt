A trend oscillator. It is calculated by the following formula:
SMA(3) of EMA (66) of ( Momentum[period] - Momentum[period-1] )
It has four configurable parameters:
Period of momentum
- momentum calculation period;
Primary smoothing period
- EMA smoothing period (66);
Secondary smoothing period
- SMA smoothing period (3);
Applied price
- price used for calculating the momentum.
