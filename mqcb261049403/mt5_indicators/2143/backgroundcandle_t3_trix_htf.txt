The indicator draws candlesticks of a larger timeframe as color filled rectangles using
DRAW_FILLING
buffers.
Rectangles are color filled in accordance with the direction of the
T3_TRIX
histogram or the colored cloud. Depending on trend direction the candlestick body is painted in blue or brown, shadows are painted in light blue or yellow.
The indicator requires the compiled indicator file T3_TRIX.mq5 for operation. Place it to terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Indicators\.
