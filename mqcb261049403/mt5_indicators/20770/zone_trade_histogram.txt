The indicator was described by B. Williams as fourth dimension - Trading Zone:
If the current bars of AC and AO are
trending up
, it shows that the zone is
up
.
If the current bars of АС and АО
down
, it shows that the zone is
down
.
If the bars of AC and AO are differently directed then the bar is colored
grey
.
This version of
Zone Trade
indicator is made to show as histogram in separate window.
