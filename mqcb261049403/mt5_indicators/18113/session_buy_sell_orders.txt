The "
Session Buy Sell Orders
" indicator shows the maximum and minimum numbers of the following parameters in the form of a histogram:
SYMBOL_SESSION_BUY_ORDERS —
"the total number of current buy orders"
SYMBOL_SESSION_SELL_ORDERS
— "the total number of current sell orders".
The indicator drawn using two buffers with the DRAW_HISTOGRAM drawing style.
