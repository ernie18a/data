Quantora Drawdown Monitor MT5 is a risk and drawdown tracking indicator for MetaTrader 5.
It monitors account balance and equity in real time and presents key risk data in a clear dashboard. Traders can quickly see current drawdown, daily drawdown, floating P/L, daily result, margin level, and open positions without manual calculations.
The indicator also tracks peak equity and shows peak drawdown, helping to understand how far the account has moved from its highest point during the session.
Configurable warning and danger levels provide a simple visual indication of risk. The dashboard automatically classifies the current condition as low, warning, or danger based on drawdown percentage.
Symbol filtering and optional Magic Number filtering allow focused monitoring for specific strategies or Expert Advisors.
The interface is lightweight, updates automatically, and works on all timeframes.
This tool is designed for monitoring and analysis only. It does not open or manage trades.
