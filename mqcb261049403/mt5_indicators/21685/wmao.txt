WMAO (Wilder's Moving Average Oscillator) - an oscillator based on
Welles Wilder's Moving Average
.
The indicator has three input parameters:
Fast WMA period
- fast WMA calculation period;
Slow WMA period
- slow WMA calculation period;
WMA applied price
- price used for the calculation of both WMAs.
Calculations:
WMAO = FastWMA - SlowWMA
where:
FastWMA = WMA(WMA applied price, Fast WMA period)
SlowWMA = WMA(WMA applied price, Slow WMA period)
WMA = Wilders Moving Average
