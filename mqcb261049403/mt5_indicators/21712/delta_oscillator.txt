The Delta Oscillator indicator displays the difference in the
Delta
indicator lines as a histogram.
The indicator has no input parameters.
Calculations:
OSC = Price - Delta
where:
Delta = Price + Diff
Diff = Log10(PrevPrice / Price)
Price = (Open + High + Low + Close) / 4.0
