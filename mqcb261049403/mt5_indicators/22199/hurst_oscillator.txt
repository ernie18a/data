Hurst oscillator. Two indicators: Hurst oscillator and
Hurst bands
are described in Jim Hurst's "The Magic of Stock Transaction Timing" book
The indicator has two input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Smoothing
- smoothing period
Calculation:
HO = MA - CMA[Period/2-1]
where:
CMA = SMA(PRICE_MEDIAN, Period)
MA  = SMA(FlowValue, Smoothing)
If Close > PrevClose
FlowValue = High
If Close < PrevClose
FlowValue = Low
If Close == PrevClose
FlowValue = (High+Low)/2
