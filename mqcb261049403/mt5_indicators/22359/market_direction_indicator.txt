Market Direction Indicator
by Donald Lambert was described in the article "The Market Direction Indicator Anticipating Moving Average Crossovers" of "Stocks & Commodities" magazine.
The indicator has five input parameters:
Fast MA period
- fast MA calculation period
Slow MA period
- slow MA calculation period
Cutoff in points
- "cutoff area" - filter of minor price fluctuations (in points)
Show below zero
- display negative indicator values in the area below zero (oscillator)
Applied price
Rising indicator values mean a bearish trend, while falling ones mean a bullish trend, the values included into the +/- Cutoff range are considered neutral.
