'Three timeframes RSI Average' displays in a separate window three Relative Strength Index indicator of the specified timeframes, as well as their average value.
It has ten input parameters:
RSI period
- RSI calculation period
Overbought
- overbought level
Oversold
- oversold level
Drawing mode
- indicator drawing type
Steps
- as a ladder
Slope
- sloping lines
First RSI timeframe
- the timeframe of the first RSI
Second
RSI
timeframe
- the timeframe of the second RSI
Third
RSI
timeframe
- the timeframe of the third RSI
Show first
RSI
- show the first RSI indicator
Show second
RSI
- show the second RSI indicator
Show third
RSI
- show the third RSI indicator
