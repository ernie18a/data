RSI of MA is an exponentially smoothed RSI based on exponential moving average data.
It has six input parameters:
Source EMA period
- initial EMA calculation period
Source applied price
- initial EMA calculation price
RSI period
- RSI calculation period
EMA of RSI period
- smoothing EMA period
Overbought
- overbought level
Oversold
- oversold level
Calculation:
RSIofMA = EMA(RSI, EMA of RSI period)
where:
RSI - RSI(SrcMA, RSI period)
SrcMA - EMA(Source applied price, Source EMA period)
