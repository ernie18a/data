An oscillator indicator. Shows the ratio of two moving averages constructed by the
linear regression
method.
There indicator has three adjustable parameters:
Period LSMA first
- period of the first LSMA;
Period LSMA second
- period of the second LSMA;
Applied price
- price used for the calculation of the two LSMAs.
