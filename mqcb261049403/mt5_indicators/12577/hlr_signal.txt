The HLR_Signal indicator displays information on the current trend in a separate narrow window as a two-colored straight line, and information on signals as colored stars. It uses the values generated by the
HLR
indicator with a fixed timeframe.
The indicator requires HLR.mq5 indicator file. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
