NR (Narrow Range Bar) pattern indicator by Toby Crabel with the ability to display WR (Wide Range Bar) patterns as well
It has six input parameters:
NR Method
- patterns type:
2NR
3NR
4NR
8NR
Custom
Sample (NR Custom)
- number of samples (if NR Method = Custom)
Period (NR Custom)
- calculation period (if NR Method = Custom)
Show wide range bar
- display Wide Range Bar patterns (red labels on a bar's High)
Show narrow range bar
- display Narrow Range Bar patterns (green labels on a bar's Low)
Exclude current bar
- exclude the current bar from search (Yes/No)
