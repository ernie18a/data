Adaptive Exponentially Smoothed Moving Average, based on
StdDev
indicator.
The indicator is similar to
EMA_ATR_VA
, but based on
StdDev
, instead of
ATR
indicator.
Input parameters:
STDPeriod
- Period of StdDev indicator;
EMAPeriods
- Period of EMA indicator;
Sensitivity
- Sensitivityот (from -100 to 100);
Price
- Applied price.
