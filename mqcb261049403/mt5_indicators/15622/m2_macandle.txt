M2_MA
implemented as a sequence of candlesticks. Candlesticks appear as a result of relevant price timeseries processed by M2_MA indicator algorithm.
In many situations, such approach may be more informative for the purpose of analysis.
The indicator requires M2_MA.mq5 indicator file. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (must be copied to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
