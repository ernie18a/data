A semaphore signal indicator using the
Commodity Chanel Index
oscillator.
Signals to enter a position are shown on the chart as corresponding colored arrows. Signals to exit a position are shown as blue diamonds or opposing arrows.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in the
Code Base at mql4.com
on 10.02.2008.
