Indicator Heikin-Ashi Difference calculates and displays as an oscillator the difference between the Open and Close prices of Heikin-Ashi candlesticks, a signal line being added. It can be used in combination with
haOpen
.
It has two parameters:
Signal period - signal line calculation period;
Signal Method - signal line calculation method.
Calculation:
HA Difference = (HAClose - HAOpen) / Point
Signal = MA(HA Difference, Period, Method)
where:
HAOpen = (PrevHAOpen + PrevHAClose) / 2.0
HAClose = (Open + High + Low + Close) / 4.0
HAClose
- Heikin-Ashi candlestick closing price;
HAOpen
- Heikin-Ashi candlestick opening price;
PrevHAClose
- closing price of the previous Heikin-Ashi candlestick;
PrevHAOpen
- opening price of the previous Heikin-Ashi candlestick;
MA
- moving average with Signal period and Signal Method, based on the value of HA Difference.
