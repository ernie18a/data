SSS (Special Slow Stochastic) has an additional smoothed (slow) line based on the Stochastic signal line.
It has six input parameters:
%K period
- the %K period of the standard Stochastic;
%D period
- the %D period of the standard Stochastic;
Slowing
- the slowing parameter of the standard Stochastic;
Period Special Slow
- the period of the additional smoothing line;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level.
