Moving Average of Oscillator (OsMA) is the difference between the oscillator and oscillator smoothing.
In this case, the base line of
Moving Average Convergence/Divergence
(MACD) is used as the oscillator, and the signal line is used as the smoothing.
Moving Average of Oscillator
Calculation:
OSMA = MACD - SIGNAL
