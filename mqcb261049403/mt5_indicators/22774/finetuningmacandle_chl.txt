FineTuningMACandle
indicator with two colored channels based on standard deviations from High and Low of the indicator candles. For a normal display of the indicator, check the "Chart on foreground" checkbox in the chart properties.

Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations without Using Additional Buffers
