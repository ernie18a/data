This indicator is a multi-timeframe Bill Williams AO (
Accelerator Oscillator
) with adjustable parameters.
It has five adjustable parameters:
AO Fast MA period
-
Awesome Oscillator's
fast SMA calculation period
AO Slow MA period
-
Awesome Oscillator's
slow SMA calculation period
AC MA period
- SMA period to calculate accelerating/slowing
AC timeframe
- timeframe, from which the AC data will be displayed on the current chart
Drawing mode
- drawing mode
Steps
- by steps
Slope
- by sloping lines
