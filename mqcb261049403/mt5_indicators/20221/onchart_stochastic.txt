The indicator draws on a price chart a stochastic with overbought/oversold zones.
The indicator has eight configurable parameters:
%K period
- main line calculation period;
%D period
- signal line calculation period;
Slowing
- slowing period;
Smoothing method
;
Price field
- price used for the indicator calculation;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level;
ATR period
.
The ATR period affects the strength of the general impact of the price on stochastic lines.
