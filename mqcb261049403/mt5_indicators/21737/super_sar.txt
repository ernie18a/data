Super SAR is a signal indicator based on
SuperTrend
and
iSAR
.
It has six input parameters:
SAR step
- Parabolic SAR step;
SAR max
- the maximum value of Parabolic SAR;
ATR Period
- ATR period for the SuperTrend calculation;
Multiplier
- SuperTrend line multiplier (to set it closer/farther from the median price);
Swing
- alteration of signals (Yes/No);
Show SAR
- whether to display Parabolic SAR (Yes/No).
Green
/
red
arrows (position entry) appear after a change in the SAR position, provided there is confirmation from SuperTrend.
Gray
arrows (do not enter position/open position can be closed) appear after a change in the SAR direction, if there is no confirmation from SuperTrend.
If Swing (Yes) is used, arrows (only up/down) appear when both criteria are met, taking into account the previous signal direction.
The SuperTrend indicator is not required for operation - its values are calculated by the indicator.
