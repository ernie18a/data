Box size:
fixed (points) or ATR-based, so it adapts to the symbol and timeframe. The reversal is configurable (default 3 boxes). The price source is Close or High/Low.
Pattern detection:
double and triple top breakout, double and triple bottom breakdown, bullish and bearish catapult, and triangle. Names are shown on the chart.
BUY/SELL signals
on breakouts, with optional filters: higher low / lower high, EMA trend filter, ADX minimum, trading session, and signal only on direction change.
Info panel
on the main chart, with Full, Compact and Minimal modes. It shows the signal, entry, SL, TP, risk/reward, lot size from the risk percentage, spread, ATR, and warnings for an oversized SL, a late entry or a wide spread. It also shows the P&F structure.
Support/resistance:
R1/R2/S1/S2 lines and labels drawn on the main chart from the P&F columns.
Alerts:
popup, sound, push and e-mail, for signals, patterns or new columns.
Signal buffer
for use in EAs: +1 BUY, -1 SELL, 0 none.
Notes:
the “closed bars only” option keeps signals stable, with no repainting on closed bars. Colors, panel size and text scale are all customizable.
