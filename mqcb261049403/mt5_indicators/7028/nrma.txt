Well-known indicator of Constantine Kopyrkin which generated a great number of NRTR indicator implementation. NRMA moving average (line) and trailing stops NRTR (circular-shaped dots) are implemented in this variant.
For details, see
the author's site
.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (must be copied to the
terminal_data_folder
\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
This indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
CodeBase
on August 19, 2006.
