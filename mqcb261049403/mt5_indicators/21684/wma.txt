The Wilder's Smoothing Average (WMA) indicator.
It has two input parameters:
Period
- calculation period;
Applied price
- price used for calculations.
Calculations:
WMA = (Applied price - PrevWMA) * k + PrevWMA
where:
k = 1/Period
