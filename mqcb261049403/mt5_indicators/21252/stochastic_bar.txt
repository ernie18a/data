A simple indicator based on standard stochastic. Along with the Main and Signal lines of standard stochastic, it also draws a color histogram between these two lines. Histogram color depends on the lines positioning against each other and against the overbought/oversold lines.
It has seven configurable parameters:
%K period
- stochastic K line period;
%D period
- stochastic D line period;
Slowing
- stochastic slowing period;
Method
- sotchastic calculation method;
Price field
- stochastic calculation price field;
Overbought
- overbought level;
Oversold
- oversold level.
Colors:
K > D and K < Overbought -
green
;
K > D and K > Overbought -
light green
;
K < D and K > Oversold -
red
;
K < D and K < Oversold -
orange
.
