This indicator displays day price change in percentage for four selected financial institutes.
The indicator was first implemented in MQL4 and published in
Code Base at mql4.com
on 31.05.2009.
The indicator uses the class of library
GetFontName.mqh
(should be copied to the terminal_data_folder\MQL5\Include).
