The ZeroLAG MA indicator is a Moving Average with zero lag. This indicator was introduced in Technical Analysis of Stocks and Commodities magazine, April 2000.
Formula:
ZeroLAG MA(i) = 2*MA(Price, P1, i) - MA(MA( Price, P1, i), P2, i)
where:
MA — Moving Average;
Price — applied price;
P1 — period of Moving Average for first smoothing;
P2 — period of Moving Average for second smoothing.
Indicator uses the СMoving_Average class of the SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library. Working with that class was described in details in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
Originally this indicator has been written in MQL4 and was first published in the

on 25.10.2006.
