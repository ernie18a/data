Price Action Volumetric Order Blocks MT5
An open-source market structure and order block visualisation tool for MetaTrader 5.
The indicator identifies confirmed swing points, BOS/MSB structure events and active bullish or bearish order blocks. It also estimates the balance between buying and selling activity within each block and summarises active zones in a compact chart panel.
Key features
Confirmed market-structure pivots and BOS/MSB levels.
Bullish and bearish order blocks projected across the chart.
Estimated buy/sell participation within each active zone.
Compact dashboard showing price range, buy/sell percentages and volume.
Configurable colours, panel position, number of zones and visual options.
Deterministic removal of chart objects when the indicator is removed.
Volume interpretation
MT5 does not provide centralised buy/sell volume for every market. The indicator therefore estimates participation from the available bar and tick-volume data. Where actual volume is unavailable, tick volume is used as a fallback. These values are analytical estimates, not exchange order-flow data.
Historical behaviour
Swing points are displayed only after the selected confirmation bars have closed. BOS/MSB events and order blocks are based on confirmed structure. This prevents unconfirmed movements within the current bar from being presented as completed structural events.

Compatibility and use
Designed for MetaTrader 5 and suitable for use on symbols and timeframes with sufficient price history. It is a visual analysis tool; it does not place or manage trades.
