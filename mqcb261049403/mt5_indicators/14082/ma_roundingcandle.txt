The
MA_Rounding
indicator implemented as a sequence of candlesticks. Candlesticks appear as a result of relevant price timeseries processed by the MA_Rounding algorithm.
In many situations, such approach may be more informative for the purpose of analysis.
This indicator requires the compiled indicator file MA_Rounding.ex5. Place it in <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Indicators.
