Custom ATR. The indicator values are calculated based on the current day's High-Low and current day's High - previous day's Close or current day's Low - previous day's Close.
There are three adjustable parameters:
Period of indicator
- indicator calculation period;
Use volume
- use volumes in calculations. If the parameter is set to "yes", then the current day's volume is added to the calculated minimum difference;
Type of indicator
- indicator drawing type, Line/Histogram.
