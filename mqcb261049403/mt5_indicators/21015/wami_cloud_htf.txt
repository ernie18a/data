Indicator
WAMI
allowing changing the timeframe of the indicator in its input parameters, the space between the oscillator and its signal line being filled in color, according to the trend direction
input
ENUM_TIMEFRAMES
TimeFrame=
PERIOD_H4
;
// Indicator chart period (timeframe)
For the indicator to operate, indicator
WAMI.ex5
must be in the <terminal_data_directory>\MQL5\Indicators folder.
