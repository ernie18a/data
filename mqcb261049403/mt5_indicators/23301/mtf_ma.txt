The Multi-timeframe Moving Average indicator.
It has five input parameters:
Period
- calculation period
Method
- calculation method
Applied price
- price used for calculations
Timeframe
- calculation timeframe
Drawing mode
- line drawing mode
Steps
- as a ladder
Slope
- inclined lines, which connect the values of neighboring bars of the calculation timeframe
