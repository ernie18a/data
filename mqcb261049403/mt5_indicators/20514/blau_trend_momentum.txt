The William Blau Trend Momentum indicator shows the William Blau trend momentum.
It has one input parameter:
Period
- calculation period.
Calculations:
Trend Momentum[i] = (H - L)
where:
H = High[i] - High[i+Period],
If (H<0) H=0,
L = Low[i+Period] - Low[i],
If (L<0) L=0
