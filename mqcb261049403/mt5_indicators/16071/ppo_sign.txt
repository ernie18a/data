Semaphore signal indicator based on the
relative smoothed rate of price change
.
The indicator uses SmoothAlgorithms.mqh library classes (copy it to <terminal_data_folder>\MQL5\Include). The use of the classes was thoroughly described in the article
"Averaging Price Series for Intermediate Calculations Without Using Additional Buffers"
.
Originally this indicator has been written in MQL4 and was first published in the
Code Base
on 08.03.2008.
