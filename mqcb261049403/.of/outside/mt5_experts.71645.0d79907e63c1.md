When the news filter is enabled, use the economic calendar to identify events for configured currencies and impact levels. Halt new entries and grid additions during the configured interval before and after each event. If pre-news closing is enabled, close the strategy’s positions and cancel its unfrozen pending orders when that interval is reached before the event; do this once per event. The web news parser supplies no events. Independently, if the strategy’s floating profit or loss, including swap and commission, reaches an enabled profit or loss threshold expressed as a percentage of account balance, close its positions and cancel its unfrozen pending orders. Block new trades when the strategy’s floating profit or loss divided by account equity falls to or below the negative exposure limit, or when free margin is insufficient.
