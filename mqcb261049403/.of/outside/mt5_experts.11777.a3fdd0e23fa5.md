Trade two configured instruments. At startup, and after both previous positions have been closed, latch an entry signal when the current value of a custom correlation indicator for the two instruments lies within the configured tolerance of the configured target. The signal remains latched until both orders open, except that automatic mode clears it when neither instrument has a session currency. Attempt entry on enabled GMT weekdays; disabled weekday settings also match Sunday because they are stored as zero.

Directions can be configured manually. If either direction is automatic, recompute both directions from GMT sessions: AUD and NZD at 19:00–05:59, JPY at 06:00–08:59, EUR, CHF and GBP at 09:00–15:59, and USD and CAD at 16:00–21:59. Sessions overlap at 19:00–21:59. For each instrument, mark up if a session currency appears at the start of its symbol and down if it appears at position four. Use the first instrument's direction if exactly one of its marks is set; otherwise use the second instrument's marks. Up means buy and down means sell; if both marks on the selected instrument are set, down takes precedence. In paired mode give the other instrument the opposite direction; in mirror mode give it the same direction. If neither selected mark supplies a direction, retain the previous directions. An order is a buy when its direction text has exactly three characters, and a sell otherwise.

Open both positions with configured sizes, individual stop losses and take profits, retrying each up to the configured limit. Once both orders succeed, monitor their combined estimated profit. When it reaches the configured target, send opposite orders for both instruments, with configured sizes and retry limits. If either position instead hits its stop loss or take profit, wait for the other to hit its own stop loss or take profit before allowing another entry.
