On a user-triggered buy, open long if the previous completed monthly MACD main value is nonzero and above its signal line. On a user-triggered sell, open short if it is nonzero and below its signal line. For either position direction, close when the short- or long-term exponential moving average group becomes exactly equal within itself, or when the previous close is at or below the lower Bollinger Band on the selected higher timeframe. Also close the managed basket when its net profit reaches a balance-relative target, when its loss reaches a hard balance-relative limit, or when its loss reaches a softer balance-relative limit while the basket contains at least three trades. A separate equity drawdown condition closes the managed positions after losses exceed a balance-equity-relative threshold. The chart cycle lines do not generate automatic entries.
