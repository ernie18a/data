For trend riding, enter long on a pullback to the fast or middle exponential moving average when the fast, middle and slow averages are strictly ordered upward, their fast-to-middle gap is widening, both the MACD histogram and stochastic main line are rising, and the technical sentiment score is bullish or stronger. Enter short under the symmetric downward conditions, treating unchanged momentum readings as non-rising, with bearish or stronger sentiment. Skip entries ahead of a high-impact economic event affecting either currency. Alternatively, shortly before such an event, stay flat or place a buy stop above and a sell stop below the pre-news range; after one triggers, cancel the other. These are discretionary alternative methods, not a single combined rule.
