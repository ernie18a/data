Trade long only. On each tick, manage the basket before considering entries: close all managed positions if account equity falls below a configured fraction of balance, then block new trades for a configured period. Otherwise, trail basket profit after it reaches a configured target and close on the configured giveback. For baskets with multiple positions, optionally use a separate lower profit trail that closes only while profit remains positive. Optionally, when enough positions are open, close the most profitable and least profitable positions together if the winner and their combined profit meet configured thresholds. Scale profit thresholds with the base lot size.

Evaluate entries at most once per new trading-instrument candle, subject to the equity-stop pause, spread limit, and an optional news blackout. The blackout uses calendar events, a CSV schedule, or both; filter by configured impact and relevant currencies, and block from a configured time before until a configured time after an event. Automatic currency relevance includes currencies in the symbol, USD for gold, and events marked for all currencies. A rapid absolute price move activates a timed crash state: optionally block first entries during it and optionally widen recovery spacing for a separate post-crash period.

For a first entry or recovery buy, require the last closed candle's RSI to be at or below a threshold selected by the ratio of current ATR to its historical average. Score the setup using configurable weights for RSI below the threshold and further below it, a pattern of lower swing lows and lower swing highs, price near the latest swing low or a sweep below it followed by a close back above it, bullish candle rejection or rising RSI, and elevated ATR. Require a configured score and, when enabled, each structure, support, and reversal condition. Define swing points by comparison with neighboring candles; define support proximity and sweep distance using ATR, and candle rejection using close location and lower-wick size.

For recovery, also require a managed buy and an ask price below its lowest entry by an ATR-based distance that increases with basket depth and is bounded below by depth-specific and optional post-crash minimum distances. Respect the optional manual position limit. At the configured rescue depth, use a higher score threshold and adjust the score for combined structure, support and reversal, account drawdown, and missing support or reversal. An optional emergency recovery qualifies only after a minimum basket depth, sufficient account drawdown, and an additional ATR-based price decline; it adds to the score and waives the individual structure, support, and reversal requirements, but not the RSI, distance, or score requirements. Require sufficient free margin for every buy. Size entries using configurable fixed or balance-based lots, optional depth-based increases, or basket-exposure-based rescue sizing, subject to configured and broker volume limits.
