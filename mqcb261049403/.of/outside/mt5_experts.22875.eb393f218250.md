While running on the designated exchange, use the selected reference price—either the traded instrument’s book price or its mark or index price—to quote both sides. At each successive level, place a buy limit below the reference and a sell limit above it, with the distance increasing by level. Size buy quotes according to remaining capacity to reach the target long exposure, and sell quotes according to remaining capacity to reach the target short exposure, accounting for the current position and limiting each quote to the configured per-level size. Remove quotes on a side when no capacity remains; otherwise place missing quotes and adjust existing ones when their desired size changes or their price drifts sufficiently from target.
