On the designated exchange, use the bid of a selected reference: the traded instrument, its mark-price instrument, or its index-price instrument. Maintain buy and sell limit orders at each configured level, respectively below and above the reference by a configured fraction of the reference multiplied by the level number; round prices to the nearest tradable tick. Start the total buy quantity at the per-level quantity times the number of levels minus the signed position, and the total sell quantity at that same product plus the signed position. Process levels from farthest to nearest. At each level, set each side's quantity to the remaining total capped at the per-level quantity and floored at the instrument's minimum volume, then subtract that quantity from its remaining total. Place missing orders, cancel orders whose quantities differ from the targets, and reprice orders when their price deviation from target divided by the reference exceeds a configured threshold. Filled buy and sell orders can open, increase, reduce, or reverse the position.
