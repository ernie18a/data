On each new chart bar, allow entries only if the history and account checks pass and the number of open positions is below its limit. For a long entry, the first qualifying higher-timeframe fractal found in a backward scan must be an upper fractal, with that timeframe’s previous candle opening below the fractal and below its 20-period linearly weighted moving average. Also require the chart’s fast linearly weighted moving average of typical price to be above its slow counterpart, the second-previous chart low to be below the previous chart high, and the current bid to be farther from the second-previous close than a configured threshold. For a short entry, require the scan to signal a lower fractal, the higher-timeframe previous candle opening above the upper-fractal value and above its 20-period linearly weighted moving average, the chart fast average below the slow average, the previous chart low below the second-previous chart high, and the current ask farther from the previous close than a configured threshold. Open in the signaled direction with configured stop-loss and take-profit distances.

Close positions when an enabled aggregate currency-profit target or balance-percentage target is reached. Close all positions when aggregate loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s running equity high; an additional loss exit applies while aggregate profit is negative and below the currency target, once loss exceeds that risk threshold scaled by a configured factor. While positions are open, trail stops using either recent chart lows for longs and highs for shorts, or market price after a configured favorable move. When a position has moved favorably by its break-even trigger, move its stop beyond its entry by a configured offset. The enabled monetary trailing logic also closes the basket when its accumulated profit retraces by the configured currency loss amount from its tracked profit level.
