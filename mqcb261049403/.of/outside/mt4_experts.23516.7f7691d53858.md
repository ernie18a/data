Evaluate once per new chart bar. Use account-wide balance, equity, free margin and stop-out rules alongside the traded instrument’s data.

For entries, require sufficient free margin, a permissible post-trade margin level, valid stop levels, trading permission, enough price history and fewer than the allowed number of strategy positions. Size the order from a base volume multiplied by an exponent for each open strategy position; after more than one consecutive losing historical trade on the instrument, optionally replace this with a fraction of free margin. Conform volume to broker limits.

Scan higher-timeframe fractals from oldest to newest within the lookback. Return a long signal on the first upper fractal above the previous higher-timeframe open when that open is below the current higher-timeframe typical-price linear weighted moving average. Otherwise, return a short signal on the first lower fractal when that open is above both the upper-fractal value at the same bar and that moving average. Check the upper case first; if neither case matches and both fractals exist at that bar, stop without a signal. For a long, also require the current fast typical-price linear weighted moving average above the slow one, the second-previous chart low below the previous high, and the absolute distance from bid to the second-previous close above a threshold. For a short, require the fast average below the slow one, the previous low below the second-previous high, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close above a threshold. Open with fixed stop-loss and take-profit distances.

Optionally close all strategy positions on the instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of account equity. The equity reference resets to current equity when no strategy positions are open; otherwise, on each calculation it becomes the previous sampled equity if the prior reference is below that previous equity, and current equity otherwise. Equity is sampled only when this reference is calculated.

Optionally close all positions and delete pending orders on the instrument when its summed floating profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. The sum scans order indices from the total down to one, so it excludes index zero, and the threshold is tested during the scan.

If money trailing is enabled and the instrument’s net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the money profit threshold, first require the strategy positions’ floating loss to exceed a configured percentage of the equity reference times a loss multiplier. Then scan instrument orders from the total index down to one, accumulating absolute net profits; close all instrument positions and delete pending orders once the accumulation reaches the configured money-loss threshold. This trailing calculation resets its reference on every call.
