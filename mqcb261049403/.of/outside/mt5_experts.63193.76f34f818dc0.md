Trade the current instrument using account-wide equity, balance and margin as well as its own market data. On each new analysis bar, skip entries if trading is halted, account equity is below the minimum, account-wide drawdown from balance or from the day's peak equity reaches its enabled limit, or the periodically updated loss from start-of-day equity reaches its enabled limit. The daily loss limit is the lesser of a balance percentage and the greater of an absolute amount and an ATR-based amount. A halt prevents normal daily reset processing; the timer can clear halts caused by the daily loss limit or repeated margin failures.

When entry is permitted, require valid indicators and, if enabled, a minimum absolute ratio of the mean to the population standard deviation of recent log returns. Draw a random discrete state and sum enabled, weighted components: centered and scaled RSI, scaled ADX, the fast-minus-slow simple moving average difference in price points, the return ratio, and the centered random state. Buy if the sum exceeds the positive decision threshold; sell if it falls below the negative threshold; otherwise do nothing. Cache indicator values once per analysis bar. Adapt the component weights together after recognized trade events according to the fraction of recent signed outcomes that are positive, where a profitable buy or unprofitable sell counts positive; bound the weights.

Require the configured interval since the last successful trade, valid ATR-based stop and target distances, sufficient free margin and compliance with the account-equity-based position margin limit, subject to the enabled minimum-lot micro-account exception. Size the position from balance, stop distance and volatility-adjusted risk, with micro-account and available-margin adjustments. Repeated insufficient-margin failures can halt trading. Existing positions do not prevent another entry.

Place a stop loss and take profit at configured ATR distances from entry. If enabled, trail the stop after a configured favorable ATR move, using a distance adjusted by the return ratio and current random state; only improve the stop when broker distance and minimum-change checks pass. If the enabled hard-stop threshold for account-wide drawdown from balance is reached, close all positions in the current instrument and remove the adviser. These per-tick exit controls are skipped while trading is halted or equity is below the minimum.
