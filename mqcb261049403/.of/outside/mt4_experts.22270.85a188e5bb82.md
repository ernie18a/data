On each new bar, allow entries only below the configured trade limit. Require a Bollinger Band expansion relative to a comparison bar: the current band width, normalized by its middle line, must exceed the comparison width by the configured factor. For a long, also require MACD main above its signal, the fast typical-price moving average above the slow one, the older low below the more recent high, and the current bid sufficiently far from the older close. For a short, require MACD main below its signal, the fast average below the slow one, the more recent low below the older high, and the current ask sufficiently far from the more recent close. Close longs when the previous close equals the upper Bollinger Band; close shorts when it equals the lower band. Also close positions when configured profit targets, trailing-loss rules, break-even rules, or the equity drawdown limit are triggered.
