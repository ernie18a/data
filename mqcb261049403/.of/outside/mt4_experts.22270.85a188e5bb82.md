At each new bar, if the optional equity stop is enabled, close all strategy positions on the traded instrument when their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference by setting it to current equity when there are no strategy positions, then setting it to the previous observed equity if the stored reference is below that previous equity, or to current equity otherwise. If the optional percentage profit target is enabled, close all positions and delete pending orders on the instrument when the running sum of their floating profits reaches the configured percentage of account balance; the summation skips the order at position zero. If money trailing is enabled and the instrument's summed net profit is negative and below its money target, also close all instrument orders when the strategy's floating loss exceeds the configured equity-risk percentage multiplied by the equity reference and loss multiplier, and the running sum of absolute net profits reaches the absolute difference between the stored trailing-profit reference and the configured money stop. This calculation also skips the order at position zero.
