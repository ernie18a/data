Select an ordered currency trio: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, EUR/GBP; GBP/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/JPY; EUR/GBP, GBP/CHF, EUR/CHF; AUD/USD, USD/NZD, AUD/NZD; EUR/CAD, CAD/JPY, EUR/JPY; or USD/SGD, EUR/USD, EUR/SGD. Periodically refresh each pair's cached bid, ask, and spread when either price changes by at least that pair's point size. Skip the entire cycle, including exits, if equity is below the configured fraction of balance, any pair's data is invalid, balance is nonpositive, estimated free margin is insufficient for all three trades, or the configured trade-cycle limit has been reached in the rolling daily counting period. Otherwise, close strategy positions older than the configured maximum duration. For the GBP/USD–USD/JPY–GBP/JPY and EUR/CAD–CAD/JPY–EUR/JPY trios, calculate the first pair's ask divided by the second pair's ask and the first pair's bid divided by the second pair's bid. For every other trio, divide the first ask by the second bid and the first bid by the second ask. Round both ratios to the third pair's price precision. Set the hurdle to the third pair's point size plus the sum of all three spreads and the sum of each pair's configured commission multiplied by its point size, rounding each component and the total to the third pair's precision; add the configured trio-specific minimum discrepancy, also rounded to that precision. If the ask ratio minus the third pair's ask strictly exceeds the hurdle, close strategy-owned longs in the first pair and shorts in the other two; if the entire account then has no open positions, sequentially sell the first pair, buy the second, and buy the third. Independently, if the bid ratio minus the third pair's bid is strictly below the negative hurdle, close strategy-owned shorts in the first pair and longs in the other two; if the entire account then has no open positions, sequentially buy the first pair, sell the second, and sell the third. Use the same balance-derived volume for all legs, rounded and constrained against each pair's volume rules in sequence. Stop submitting legs when one fails, leaving any already opened legs in place; count a trade cycle only when all three submissions succeed.
