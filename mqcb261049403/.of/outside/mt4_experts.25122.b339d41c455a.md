Evaluate short entries once whenever a newly completed EUR/USD bar is detected on the chart timeframe, including the initial evaluation. Using the traded instrument’s current bar, open a short when Williams’ Percent Range exceeds an upper threshold, the close-based MACD main line exceeds a positive threshold, and the stochastic main line is strictly below its upper threshold after being strictly above it on the previous bar. Use simple-average smoothing and high/low prices for the stochastic. Allow additional shorts while positions remain open.
After entry evaluation, check exits on every tick. If a single strategy-managed short exists, close it when average open-position profit exceeds its threshold and current Williams’ Percent Range is below a lower threshold. If multiple strategy-managed shorts exist, close all shorts when average open-position profit exceeds a separate threshold and a separately configured stochastic main line is below its lower threshold. Calculate average profit without volume weighting across all strategy-managed positions on the traded instrument, using entry minus current bid for shorts and current ask minus entry for longs, expressed in pips. Give each short a fixed-distance take-profit and no stop-loss.
