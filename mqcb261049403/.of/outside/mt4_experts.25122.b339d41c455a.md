On each new bar of EUR/USD on the chart timeframe, evaluate the current chart instrument: open a short when its long-lookback Williams %R is above its upper cutoff, its MACD main line is above its positive cutoff, and its fast stochastic crosses downward through its upper cutoff. For existing shorts on the current instrument: when exactly one is open, close it if its average open-trade profit in pips exceeds the lower profit threshold and long-lookback Williams %R falls below its lower cutoff. When more than one short is open, close them if average open-trade profit exceeds the higher profit threshold and a slow stochastic is below its lower cutoff. No long entries are made.
