For the traded currency pair, calculate a strength score for each currency from its exchange rates against the other seven currencies. On the chart timeframe, divide each pair's current linearly weighted moving average of close by its value a configurable number of bars earlier. Invert this ratio when the currency being scored is the quote currency. Multiply the seven resulting ratios and subtract one. When calculating the USD and CAD scores, use the USD/CHF moving average in place of USD/CAD, retaining the orientation assigned to USD/CAD. On each completed bar, subtract the quote currency's score from the base currency's score. Signal long when this difference rises above the current bar's ATR multiplied by a configurable coefficient, having been at or below that same threshold on the preceding bar, provided the last signal was not long. Signal short when it falls below the negative of that threshold, having been at or above that same negative threshold on the preceding bar, provided the last signal was not short. Retain the last signal direction between signals. No exit rule is specified.
