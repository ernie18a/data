On each new execution-timeframe bar, consider the traded instrument’s price-action entry signals only during enabled trading sessions. Determine session membership from the configured clock; the US session may shift with the New York daylight-saving calendar. Also require an acceptable spread, the configured chart timeframe and sufficient available account margin. Pause new entries for a configured number of bars when account balance drawdown from its tracked peak reaches its limit, or when the strategy records the configured number of consecutive losing exit deals. Reset the losing streak after a profitable exit deal; a zero-result deal leaves it unchanged. The drawdown guard can reset its tracked peak when a pause begins. Outside these gates, enter long or short according to the instrument’s own price-action signals; these external conditions do not themselves choose a direction or close a position.
