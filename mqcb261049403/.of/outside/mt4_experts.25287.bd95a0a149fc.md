On each new chart bar, open a long if the open-trade count is below its limit, the current bid is more than the configured retracement distance from the close two bars ago, the fast typical-price weighted moving average on the selected higher timeframe is above the slow one, and the low two bars ago is below the prior bar’s high. In live trading, also require both projected ZigZag trend lines to be lower at the prior bar than two bars back, and require that the high at the bar associated with the second high pivot is not below the high at the first high pivot. In testing, omit this trend-line filter.

Open a short if the trade count is below its limit, the current ask is more than the retracement distance from the close two bars ago, the fast higher-timeframe weighted moving average is below the slow one, and the prior bar’s low is below the high two bars ago. In live trading, also require both projected trend lines to be higher at the prior bar than two bars back; in testing, omit this filter.

Close the symbol’s positions when enabled money or balance-percentage profit targets are reached. Close positions when combined floating profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s tracked equity peak. The money-based trailing rule also closes positions after positive combined profit has peaked and then fallen by the configured currency amount. For individual positions, trail stops using recent candle lows for longs and highs for shorts when candle trailing is enabled; otherwise trail by price after the configured favorable move. Move a long stop above entry after the configured favorable move; move a short stop below entry after that move.
