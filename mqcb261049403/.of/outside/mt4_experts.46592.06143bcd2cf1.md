Monitor the managed trades’ combined net profit against account-balance-based profit and loss thresholds. When either threshold is crossed, close all managed trades. If the loss threshold is crossed while the loss remains below a broader account-balance-based limit, add the loss to the profit target, widen the loss threshold to that broader limit, and enter recovery mode; otherwise reset both thresholds to their initial account-balance-based levels and leave recovery mode. The breakout entries use only the traded instrument’s own candles, so they are not included.
