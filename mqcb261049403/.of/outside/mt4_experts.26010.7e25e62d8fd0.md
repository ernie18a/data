On each new chart bar, open a long when the trade limit allows, price has moved far enough from the referenced recent close, the fast weighted moving average on the selected higher timeframe is above the slow one, the referenced prior-bar low is below the next bar’s high, and the current monthly RSI is above its previous value. Open a short when the trade limit allows, price has moved far enough from its referenced recent close, the fast moving average is below the slow one, the referenced prior-bar low is below the next bar’s high, and monthly RSI is falling. In live trading, require the two fractal trend lines to slope in opposite directions for either entry; in testing, omit this filter. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry.

For open trades, move the stop into profit after price advances past the configured break-even trigger. Trail stops either from recent candle extremes, with a configured offset, or by a fixed distance after the configured profit trigger, according to the selected trailing mode. When enabled, close the symbol’s trades at the configured aggregate money-profit or balance-percentage target, or close them if aggregate loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s recent equity peak.
