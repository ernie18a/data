On each tick, iterate over open long and short trades in the strategy's trade group across all instruments, from newest to oldest. For each encountered trade, calculate the average net monetary profit of all currently open trades in that group across all instruments, including commission and swap. Round each trade's net profit and the resulting average to two decimal places. If the average exceeds a profit threshold, close the trade with the highest net monetary profit, then close the remaining trade with the lowest net monetary profit. Recalculate the average for each subsequent iteration, as the set of open trades may have changed.
