On Friday, attempt to close all strategy positions in the traded instrument and do not open new ones. On other days, block new trades when the enabled account-equity target is exceeded and there are no account orders, or when equity exceeds the target plus an additional margin; also block them when enabled account-balance protection is breached. If enabled basket protection applies and strategy position profit exceeds its threshold, stop processing for that tick without closing positions. Otherwise, select a long when the instrument's current MACD main value exceeds its previous value and the long side is enabled; select a short when it is lower and the short side is enabled. Optionally reverse the selected direction after applying the side filters. Open only below the position limit and with sufficient free margin: open immediately if there are no strategy positions; otherwise require the current ask to be sufficiently below the remembered entry price for a long, or the current bid sufficiently above it for a short. Set initial stop-loss and take-profit levels when enabled, trail profitable positions, and optionally reset basket take-profits using the lowest long entry or highest short entry once the protection position count is reached. The account profit stop-loss condition only logs a message and does not close or block trades.
