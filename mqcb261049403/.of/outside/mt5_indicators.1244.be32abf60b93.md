For each of two instruments, synchronize price histories, carrying the previous price forward across missing bars. Calculate a fast and a slow moving average of the selected price, and subtract the slow average from the fast average. Scale the first difference by a fixed factor and the second by that factor times the ratio of the instruments' current opening prices. Negate either instrument's result if its correlation is configured as reversed. Optionally multiply the second result by the volatility-balanced lot ratio, calculated as the first instrument's ATR times its per-lot value per unit of price change divided by the corresponding product for the second instrument. Measure divergence as the absolute difference between the two resulting lines. After a very strong divergence, when that difference starts decreasing, sell the instrument with the upper line and simultaneously buy the instrument with the lower line. Close both positions when the lines cross. The source does not define a threshold for 'very strong' divergence.
