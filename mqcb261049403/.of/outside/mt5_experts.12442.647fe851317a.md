Periodically sample the external instrument’s quote and timestamp. When no strategy position is active, find the latest sample at or before the configured lookback time and compare it with the newest sample. If either quote is zero, do not enter. If the newest quote is strictly more than the configured threshold above the historical quote, buy the traded instrument; if it is strictly more than the threshold below it, sell. Otherwise do nothing. Allow entry only while the configured position count has not been reached; a negative limit permits unlimited entries. On entry, set fixed-distance stop loss and take profit and record the elapsed time between the two external quote samples. Close the position once that elapsed time has passed since entry. Reset the sample history when the external session is reported closed or the current weekday is disabled; also reset it on Sunday. When the session is reported closed, reset the entry count and suppress subsequent samples equal to the recorded closing quote until a different quote appears. These resets do not themselves prevent the timer from continuing to process quotes and trading logic.
