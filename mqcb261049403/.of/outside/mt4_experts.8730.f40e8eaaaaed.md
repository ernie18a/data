Optional account protections close all positions and delete all pending orders on the traded instrument, regardless of strategy identifier, then stop processing for the current tick: (1) account equity is at or below a configured fraction of account balance; or (2) account balance exceeds a configured profit target. Optionally, when the strategy's market-position count on the traded instrument reaches a configured minimum, sum profit plus swap and lots across all open orders on all instruments. Close the traded instrument's positions and delete its pending orders if the sum of profit plus swap reaches a configured profit threshold multiplied by total lots; alternatively, when the position count reaches the configured maximum, do so if that sum falls to or below the negative of a configured loss threshold multiplied by total lots. These two aggregate protections are independently enabled. Optionally suppress new orders on the traded instrument whenever any open or pending order exists on another instrument.
