Form every enabled three-currency ring from currencies ordered strongest to weakest. For currencies A, B, C in that order, one side buys A/B, sells A/C, and buys B/C; the opposite side reverses all three trades. In normal operation, establish each configured side and replace missing initial legs, subject to trading session, combined spread, order limit, margin, and instrument availability checks.

Track each side's combined floating profit including swap, position count, lots, and costs across its three instruments. In manual grid mode, add another three-leg set to a side when its combined profit falls to or below its loss step, the minimum time has elapsed, and the group limit permits it. With basket profit closing, add only to a side whose position count is at least the opposite side's. The loss step is the negative sum across legs of total lots times the configured step and a group-count progression multiplier; multiply each leg by its tick value unless fair sizing is enabled. Subtract the side's aggregate spread value and absolute swap. The progression multiplier is the number of sets, the sum of integers through that number, or the sum of successive powers of two starting with one, according to the selected progression.

For each side, calculate the profit target as the sum of total lots times the configured profit target, weighted by tick value unless fair sizing is enabled, less aggregate spread value and absolute swap. Floor it at the sum of the first position's lots on each leg times the profit target and tick value. Calculate the loss threshold as the negative sum of average lots per position on each leg times the configured loss target, similarly tick-value weighted unless fair sizing is enabled, less aggregate spread value and absolute swap.

After the configured confirmation delay, close all positions on a side when its positive combined profit reaches its target, or, in basket mode, close both sides when their combined positive profit reaches the larger side target. If loss closing is enabled, close the entire side when its negative combined profit reaches its loss threshold, or close the first position on each leg only when that side has more positions than the opposite side. Continue an initiated whole-side or basket closure until complete.

In automatic grid mode with both sides enabled, when one side has been closed while the other remains, open a new set on the missing side if the remaining side is below its group limit. Also close a profitable side with no more positions than the opposite side when the opposite side's combined profit reaches its loss step, after the profit-close confirmation delay. Basket profit closing also applies in this mode.

When position counts differ across the three legs of a side, optionally restore matching counts by opening missing legs, or close first positions on excess legs if the order limit prevents opening. Standby suppresses normal trading; close-and-stop mode permits existing positions to be managed without initial entries; immediate-close mode closes all positions.
