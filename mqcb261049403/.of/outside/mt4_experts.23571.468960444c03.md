Evaluate once per new bar. Account-wide free margin gates entries: require sufficient margin for the base size and for the proposed order under the broker's stop-out rules. Subject to the position limit, history and stop-distance checks, go long when the previous bar's slow stochastic signal is below its fast stochastic main line, previous RSI is above neutral, the current fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous high, and the bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago. Otherwise, go short when those indicator comparisons reverse, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, and the ask is sufficiently far from the previous close. Long signals take priority. Set a fixed stop and a profit target based on the risk-reward ratio.

When enabled, close this strategy's positions on the instrument if their combined floating profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured fraction of account equity. The equity reference is updated when this test runs: reset it to current equity if no strategy positions exist; then use previous equity if it exceeds the reference, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity as previous equity.

When enabled, close all orders on the instrument if the running sum of their market-order profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. The sum iterates open-order indices from the total down to one, excluding index zero, and checks the threshold after each iteration.

When money trailing is enabled, a negative instrument-wide net profit can also trigger closure of all instrument orders if the strategy's gross floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the equity reference multiplied by a loss factor, and the running sum of absolute individual net profits reaches the configured trailing-loss threshold relative to the stored trailing value. This loop also excludes order index zero.
