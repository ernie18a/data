On each new bar, manage exits first, then consider entries. Use account free margin and broker margin checks to gate entries. Subject to the position limit and valid order conditions, go long when the completed-bar, typical-price linear weighted moving averages are strictly ordered from highest to lowest as their periods increase, the current fast average exceeds the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds a configured threshold. Go short when the average ordering and fast/slow comparison are reversed, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds the threshold. Give the long signal priority. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit levels; optionally move stops toward profit and trail them as price moves favorably. Optionally close the strategy's positions on the instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of account equity as calculated by the program. Optionally close all positions and delete pending orders on the instrument when their summed floating profit reaches a money target or a percentage of account balance. The profit summation for these targets skips the order at position zero. An optional money-trailing routine also skips that order: while total net profit is positive but below its configured ceiling, it closes all instrument orders if a running partial sum falls at least the configured money distance below that total; while total net profit is negative and the strategy's floating loss exceeds an equity-based threshold multiplied by a configured factor, it closes all instrument orders if the running sum of absolute individual net profits reaches the configured money distance. These running sums and the comparison peak reset on every bar.
