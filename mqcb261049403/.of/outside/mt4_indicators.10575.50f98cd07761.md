On each evaluation, compare the selected candle’s open and close across the major currency pairs involving EUR, GBP, and USD, translating each pair’s direction into the strength of the relevant currency. Signal a buy of EUR/USD when EUR is bullish against every compared currency and USD is bearish against every compared currency; signal a sell when those conditions are reversed. Apply the same rule to GBP/USD using GBP and USD strength. The strength scores accumulate across evaluations rather than resetting, and each signal type is alerted at most once per chart bar. No exit rule is defined.
