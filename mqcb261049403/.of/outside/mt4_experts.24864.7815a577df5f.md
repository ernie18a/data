On each new chart bar, open a long if the fast typical-price weighted moving average exceeds the slow one on the selected higher timeframe, the prior-bar range condition is met, and current price is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago. Open a short under the reverse moving-average ordering when the corresponding prior-bar range and price-distance conditions are met. Limit entries to the configured maximum number of open trades. In live trading, require the drawn Fibonacci channel’s first price coordinate to be below its second for longs and above its second for shorts; omit this filter in testing. Attach price-based stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry. Close all symbol positions when an enabled account-equity drawdown limit is breached, or when an enabled aggregate profit target in money or as a share of account balance is reached. The enabled aggregate money trailing logic also closes positions when its profit/loss conditions and configured money thresholds are met. For open trades, move the stop beyond breakeven after favorable movement reaches its trigger, and trail stops using recent candle lows for longs or highs for shorts (or by a fixed price distance when candle trailing is disabled).
