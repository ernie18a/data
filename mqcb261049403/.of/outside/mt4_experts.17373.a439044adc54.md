When no positions are open, start a cycle only during the configured trading session and when average recent tick volume meets its minimum. Choose the initial direction from either the previous candle’s body (up means long, down means short) or the ordering of fast, medium, and slow exponential moving averages: with fast above slow, go long only if medium is below fast; with fast below slow, go short only if medium is above fast; otherwise wait. Suppress entries in the middle price range; buy below the configured lower boundary and sell above the configured upper boundary. For each open position, set a fixed stop-loss and take-profit. During a cycle, if price moves adversely from the most recently opened position by the next configured grid distance, open a position in the opposite direction, using the next configured grid size. Close all cycle positions when their combined favorable price movement reaches the trailing threshold and then falls from its recorded peak by the configured trail step. Use the grid and trailing rules only while positions are open.
