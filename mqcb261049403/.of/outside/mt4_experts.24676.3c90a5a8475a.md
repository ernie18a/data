On each eligible bar, allow new entries only when the open-trade count is below its configured maximum and free margin is sufficient. Compare fast and slow linear weighted moving averages of typical price on a higher timeframe selected from the chart timeframe. Buy when the fast average is above the slow average, the prior-bar low is below the latest completed bar’s high, and the current bid is farther from the close two bars ago than a configurable retracement distance. In live trading, also require the two price coordinates of the chart’s Andrews Pitchfork to slope upward; in tester mode, omit this filter. Sell when the fast average is below the slow average and the latest completed bar’s low is below the high two bars ago; in live trading also require the Pitchfork coordinates to slope downward, while omitting that filter in tester mode. Set initial stop loss and take profit at configurable distances from entry. Size positions from a base lot amount multiplied by a configurable exponent raised to the number of existing trades, with an optional increase after consecutive losses. Close all positions for the instrument when an enabled account-equity drawdown limit is exceeded, or when enabled aggregate profit targets in money or as a percentage of balance are reached. An enabled money-based trailing rule closes the instrument’s positions when aggregate profit falls by a configured amount from its favorable level, and applies a loss-based close rule when aggregate loss exceeds a configured equity-risk threshold adjusted by a loss multiplier. For existing positions, move the stop beyond entry after favorable movement reaches a configured threshold; trail stops either behind recent candle extremes on each new bar or by a fixed distance after a configured favorable move, according to the selected trailing mode.
