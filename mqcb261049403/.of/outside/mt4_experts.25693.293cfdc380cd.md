On each new bar, consider entries only if the open trade count is below its limit and basic history, margin, and order checks pass. Go long when the higher-timeframe fast weighted moving average is above the slow one; the prior candles meet the long-side low/high comparison; four chart-timeframe exponential moving averages, ordered from shortest to longest, are strictly descending in value; price is farther from the specified earlier close than the candle-based threshold; and either the monthly MACD main line is above its signal line or monthly RSI is rising. Go short under the mirrored conditions: fast weighted average below slow, the short-side candle comparison holds, the four exponential averages are strictly ascending, price clears the corresponding distance threshold, and either monthly MACD is below its signal or monthly RSI is falling. Open entries with a stop loss and take profit on the traded instrument. Close positions when an enabled account-equity drawdown rule is breached, or when an enabled money-based or percentage-based profit rule triggers. Manage open positions by moving stops to lock in gains after a favorable move, trailing stops by recent candle highs or lows (or by price movement when candle trailing is disabled), and applying the configured money-based group trailing and loss exits.
