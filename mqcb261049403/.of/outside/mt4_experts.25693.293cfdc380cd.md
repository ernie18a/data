Evaluate once per new chart bar. Entry requires sufficient account free margin, broker margin and stop-distance checks, enough price history, a valid configured profit target, and fewer than the allowed number of strategy positions. Buy when the higher-timeframe fast typical-price weighted moving average exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous high, four previous-bar typical-price exponential averages are strictly ordered shortest above longest, the bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, and either the previous monthly MACD main line is nonzero and above its signal or current monthly RSI exceeds the previous monthly RSI. Otherwise sell when the moving-average comparisons reverse, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, the ask is sufficiently far from the previous close, and either the previous monthly MACD main line is nonzero and below its signal or current monthly RSI is below the previous monthly RSI. Give a buy signal priority even if its order fails validation. Apply configured initial stops and profit targets.

If enabled, close strategy positions on the instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account-equity reference. The reference is updated when checked: reset it to current equity when there are no strategy positions, then use previous checked equity if that exceeds the reference; otherwise use current equity. If enabled, close all instrument orders when the running sum of their floating profits reaches a configured fraction of account balance. This calculation scans order positions from the total count down to one, omitting position zero, and checks the threshold after each iteration.

If money trailing is enabled and the similarly scanned net instrument profit is negative and below its configured profit ceiling, and the strategy's floating loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-based risk threshold, accumulate absolute net profit per scanned instrument position; close all instrument orders when that running total reaches the configured money-loss amount.
