For each time period, count the advances and declines among available instruments containing the selected base currency, reversing the advance/decline direction for quoted pairs. Divide advances minus declines by their total, then subtract a slower smoothed version of this breadth measure from a faster smoothed version and cumulatively sum the result. Derive floating upper and lower thresholds from the recent index range, with their midpoint as the center threshold. Use the selected coloring rule to generate directional states: bullish above the upper threshold, bearish below the lower threshold, or neutral between them; alternatively, bullish/bearish states follow the index’s slope or its position above/below the midpoint. Treat changes in these states as signals for the traded instrument: bullish for long, bearish for short, and neutral or an opposing state to exit.
