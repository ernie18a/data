For a proposed trade, estimate its usual size from the account balance, desired risk, stop distance, and instrument value per point. Compare the traded instrument with every open position using the Pearson correlation of recent bar returns. Treat a position as adding risk when its correlation, adjusted for whether its direction matches or opposes the proposed trade, meets the positive correlation threshold; sum its stop-loss risk when that risk can be calculated. Treat strongly offsetting positions as hedges and exclude them from the basket risk. Set the remaining basket budget to the maximum allowed basket risk minus the summed correlated risk. Recommend zero size if no budget remains; otherwise size the trade to the lesser of its usual risk and the remaining budget, then normalize to the instrument's allowed trade increments. Positions with unknown correlation or risk cannot contribute a calculable amount to the sum.
