Calculate signals on the selected timeframe using the traded instrument’s prices and the quoted decimal precision of EUR/USD. For each bar, calculate the average high-minus-low range over the configured lookback, including that bar, and divide it by 10 raised to the power of EUR/USD decimal digits minus the traded instrument’s decimal digits minus the configured precision adjustment. Initialize the trend as neutral and both the tracked price and trailing level to the first processed close. In a neutral or rising trend, raise the tracked price to the highest close seen and raise the trailing level to the greater of its previous value and the tracked price multiplied by one minus the scaled range. If the close falls below that level, reset the tracked price to the close, set the level to the close multiplied by one plus the scaled range, and switch to a falling trend. In a falling trend, lower the tracked price to the lowest close seen and lower the trailing level to the lesser of its previous value and the tracked price multiplied by one plus the scaled range. If the close rises above that level, reset the tracked price to the close, set the level to the close multiplied by one minus the scaled range, and switch to a rising trend. Signal long only on a transition from falling to rising, and short only on a transition from rising to falling. A transition from the initial neutral state produces no signal.
