Evaluate once at the first tick of each new chart bar. Account-level balance, equity and free margin affect the following decisions.

Close all market positions and delete pending orders on the traded instrument, regardless of strategy identifier, if enabled percentage profit-taking is reached: sum the floating profits of that instrument's market orders from the last order down through order index 1, excluding index 0, and compare the sum with the configured percentage of account balance.

If enabled, close this strategy's market positions on the instrument when their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds the configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference when evaluated: if there are no strategy positions, first set it to current equity; then use previous equity if the reference exceeds neither previous equity nor the initial comparison's opposite condition—specifically, if the reference is less than previous equity, set it to previous equity; otherwise set it to current equity. Save current equity as previous equity. Continue processing the bar after closing.

If enabled monetary trailing is evaluated while the instrument's net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured monetary profit threshold, and this strategy's floating loss exceeds the configured percentage of the equity reference multiplied by the configured loss factor, scan orders from the last index through index 1. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each instrument market order; close all instrument orders when that running sum reaches the configured monetary loss distance. This equity reference uses the same update rule above. The positive-profit branch depends only on the instrument's orders.

New entries require sufficient account free margin and a successful post-trade margin and stop-out check, as well as valid stop levels and trading permission. On an eligible bar, compare fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on the selected higher timeframe. Buy when the fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance. Sell when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. Require fewer than the configured maximum strategy positions and sufficient chart history. In live trading, also require the most recent upper fractal to be below the third most recent upper fractal and the most recent lower fractal to be above the third most recent lower fractal; omit this filter in testing. Attempt at most one entry per bar. The higher timeframe is selected from the chart timeframe according to the strategy's configured timeframe mapping.
