Evaluate once per new chart candle. Open a long when the open-position count is below its configured cap, the fast linear-weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one on the mapped higher timeframe, the prior candle lows/highs satisfy the long-side overlap condition (low two candles ago < high one candle ago), and the current bid is farther than the configured retracement distance from the close two candles ago. Open a short under the same position cap when the fast average is below the slow one, the short-side candle overlap condition holds (low one candle ago < high two candles ago), and the current ask is farther than the retracement distance from the close one candle ago. In live trading, both entries additionally require converging fractal trend lines: the older upper-line point is above its newer point and the older lower-line point is below its newer point. In testing, omit this trend-line filter. Set initial stop loss and take profit at configured price distances. Close all positions when enabled aggregate profit targets in currency or as a share of account balance are reached; when enabled equity protection is breached by aggregate losses relative to the tracked equity high; or when enabled money-based profit/loss trailing protection triggers. For individual positions, optionally move the stop beyond breakeven after a configured favorable move, then trail it by either a configured distance from price or beyond recent candle highs/lows. The separate moving-average/band exit routine is not invoked.
