Open a long when, on a higher timeframe, a recent low reaches or falls below the lower Bollinger band, the latest completed candle’s high reaches the middle band, and the fast weighted moving average is above the slow one; also require the chart’s recent candle structure and current-price distance to satisfy the entry trigger. Open a short under the mirrored conditions: a recent high reaches or exceeds the upper band, the latest completed candle’s low reaches the middle band, the fast average is below the slow one, and the chart’s candle structure and price-distance trigger are satisfied. Limit entries to the configured maximum number of open trades and available margin. Exit positions at their attached stop loss or take profit; close the instrument’s positions when an enabled account-equity drawdown limit or profit target is reached, or when the money-based profit trailing rule closes the basket. Move a profitable position’s stop beyond its entry after the break-even trigger, and trail its stop as price moves further in its favor.
