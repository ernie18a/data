Evaluate once per new chart bar. When enabled, close all positions on the traded instrument and delete its pending orders if the running sum of its position profits reaches a configured percentage of account balance. The scan runs backward from the order count through index 1, checking the threshold after each selected order; index 0 is omitted.

When enabled, close the strategy's positions on the instrument if their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of a tracked account-equity value. On each evaluation of that value, first set it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then, if it is below the previously recorded equity, set it to that previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity. Finally record current equity as the previous equity.

When monetary trailing is enabled, an additional loss exit uses that same equity calculation: if the instrument's scanned net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured profit ceiling, and the strategy's floating loss exceeds the configured equity-risk percentage multiplied by a loss factor, scan orders backward from index 1, accumulating the absolute net profit of each instrument position. Close all instrument positions and delete its pending orders when that running sum reaches the configured monetary loss threshold.

New entries also require sufficient account free margin and post-trade margin under the broker's stop-out rules, valid stops, trading permission, sufficient chart history, and fewer than the configured maximum number of strategy positions. On the mapped higher timeframe, compare recent completed bars with the latest completed bar's Bollinger bands; on the chart timeframe compare current fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price. Go long if any of three earlier higher-timeframe lows touches or falls below the lower band, the latest completed higher-timeframe high reaches the middle band, the fast average exceeds the slow average, the chart's second-previous low is below its previous high, and the absolute distance from bid to the second-previous close exceeds the configured threshold. Otherwise go short if any of those earlier higher-timeframe highs touches or exceeds the upper band, the latest completed higher-timeframe low reaches the middle band, the fast average is below the slow average, the previous chart low is below the second-previous high, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds the configured threshold. A long signal takes priority and ends entry evaluation even if its order cannot be placed.
