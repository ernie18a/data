On each of six timeframes from intraday to daily, mark bullish when MACD momentum is rising, a stochastic-cross-derived buy stop level exists, and both Heikin-Ashi open and close are above it. Mark bearish when MACD momentum is falling, a stochastic-cross-derived sell stop level exists, and both Heikin-Ashi open and close are below it. Derive the stop levels from recent price extrema around stochastic crosses and update them as price crosses those levels. Generate a buy signal when a new shortest-timeframe bullish level appears and at least three timeframes are bullish; use the highest bullish level as the stop and the lowest bearish level as the target. Generate a sell signal symmetrically when a new shortest-timeframe bearish level appears and at least three timeframes are bearish; use the lowest bearish level as the stop and the highest bullish level as the target. Require entry-to-stop distance to exceed 2.4 spreads and entry-to-target distance to exceed 3 spreads. No exit rule or option decision is present.
