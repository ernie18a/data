Once per new bar, if no position is open: go long when the previous closed bar’s RSI is below the configured oversold threshold and MACD forms a negative local trough, with price and an earlier local low showing either regular bullish divergence (price makes a higher low while MACD makes a lower trough) or hidden bullish divergence (price makes a lower low while MACD makes a higher trough). Go short when RSI is above the configured overbought threshold and MACD forms a positive local peak, with price and an earlier local high showing either regular bearish divergence (price makes a lower high while MACD makes a higher peak) or hidden bearish divergence (price makes a higher high while MACD makes a lower peak). Otherwise, take no trade. Trades use configured stop-loss and take-profit levels; no separate signal-based exit rule is present.
