When there are no open positions or pending orders, evaluate the prior completed bar’s EURUSD indicators. Place a buy-stop entry when daily RSI is below its configured ceiling, hourly RSI is below its configured ceiling, and the longer-period hourly moving average is above the shorter-period one. Place a sell-stop entry when daily RSI is above its configured floor, hourly RSI is above its configured floor, and the longer-period hourly moving average is above the shorter-period one. The two published versions use different RSI periods, thresholds, and moving-average periods; one version also places an opposite-side stop order alongside each qualifying entry as a reversal order.
