On the first tick of each bar, evaluate exits before entries. For the traded symbol, optionally close and delete all its orders when a running sum of open-position profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. The sum scans order indices from the total down to 1, excluding index 0, and checks the threshold after each iteration.

Optionally close this strategy's positions on the symbol when their combined unrealized profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured fraction of a tracked account-equity amount. On each call to the equity tracker, reset the tracked amount to current equity if the strategy has no open positions; then set it to the previous equity if that is higher than the tracked amount, otherwise set it to current equity. Save current equity as previous equity. The tracker is called only when its preceding loss conditions pass.

If monetary trailing is enabled, there is at least one strategy position, total scanned symbol profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured profit ceiling, and the strategy's negative profit exceeds the configured equity-risk fraction times a loss multiplier, scan symbol orders from index total down to 1. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position. Maintain a shared trailing value: replace it with the absolute total scanned profit when that amount is no greater than the existing value. Close and delete all symbol orders if the running absolute sum reaches the absolute difference between that value and the configured money-stop distance. The same trailing value is updated to the maximum total scanned profit during the positive-profit trailing branch and reset when no strategy positions remain.

For entry, require sufficient bars, a valid configured take-profit distance, free margin above a configured minimum, fewer than the maximum number of strategy positions, broker-permitted volume, stop levels and trading, and sufficient post-trade margin relative to account equity and the broker's stop-out rule. Size starts at base volume multiplied by a configured exponent raised to the number of strategy positions; after more than one losing historical trade on the symbol since its latest profitable trade, replace it with a configured fraction of free margin, then clamp and round it to broker volume limits.

Buy when both completed-bar stochastic readings are at or below the buy threshold, the current fast linearly weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured point distance. Otherwise sell when both stochastic readings are at or above the sell threshold, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. A qualifying buy branch ends entry evaluation even if order placement fails. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit distances to entries.
