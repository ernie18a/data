Evaluate once per new bar. For both strategies, require a configurable run of bearish candles followed by a configurable run of bullish candles; each candle must meet the configured minimum body size, and body sizes must also follow the configured increasing or decreasing pattern when selected. Require the bearish-entry filters: the previous closed candle’s close is below the lowest low in the configured historical window, and the stochastic main line is below its signal line on the previous closed bar. Require the stochastic main line to stay within the configured lower and upper bounds for every bar in the configured recent closed-bar window. If all filters pass and there is no existing short position in the traded instrument, open a short. For the bullish strategy, use instead a close above the highest high in the configured historical window and a stochastic main line above its signal line; with the same row and range filters, open a long only when no long position exists. No separate signal-based exit is defined.
