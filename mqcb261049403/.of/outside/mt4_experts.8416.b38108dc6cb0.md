When no buy position is open, open a long if the smoothed Stochastic value is rising and its previous value is above the buy threshold. When no sell position is open, open a short if the value is falling and its previous value is below the sell threshold. For an existing long basket, add a long after price falls by the configured spacing from the most recently processed long entry; for a short basket, add a short after price rises by that spacing from the most recently processed short entry. Increase each added position's size by a fixed multiplier, subject to the basket's total size limit; spacing may be fixed or increase with the number of positions. Close all positions in a basket when its combined profit is positive and price reaches the basket target, set from the most recently processed entry price and adjusted by the position count.
