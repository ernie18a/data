On each completed signal bar, compare two smoothed rate-of-change series computed from the traded instrument’s closing prices. Open long when the faster-period series crosses from at or below the slower-period series to above it; close short positions while the faster-period series is above the slower-period series. Open short when it crosses from at or above to below; close long positions while it is below. Gate new entries to the configured trading-time window, and close any open position when that window is closed.
