When configured to aggregate across instruments, use the combined net profit, including commission and swap, and combined long and short exposure of the selected orders to manage each traded instrument. Otherwise, apply the same rules to the current instrument only.

If combined profit reaches a threshold, start tracking it. In the non-trending mode of both versions, close the selected positions and cancel their pending orders when profit falls by a specified amount from the previous tracked value, provided profit still meets the threshold. In the newer version's trending mode, close immediately on reaching the threshold, except positions whose stop is above their entry price; cancel pending orders. Reset the gain state when no positions or profit remain.

If combined loss exceeds a specified fraction of account balance, cancel selected pending orders and, once per emergency, open a market position on the current instrument opposite the combined net lot imbalance, sized to offset that imbalance, subject to available margin. Thereafter, for current-instrument orders no newer than the hedge, disable new entries in an order's direction when that order is losing and re-enable them when it is not; process orders sequentially. The older version assesses this by raw order profit, the newer by profit including costs.

In the older version, also sum net profit on current-instrument positions opened after the hedge and net profit on losing current-instrument orders. Close selected positions and cancel pending orders when the former is positive and exceeds the absolute latter; reset the emergency and entry permissions. Apply the specified trailing stop to those later positions.
