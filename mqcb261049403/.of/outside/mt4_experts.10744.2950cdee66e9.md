On each tick, record the configured reference instrument’s bid, ask, previous-bar close and current RSI, along with the arithmetic means of those prices across the reference currency basket. Also record the chart instrument’s current RVI main and signal lines. Maintain a bounded history of these snapshots. The basket RSI is calculated but is not stored in ordinary snapshots; it is stored only in the newest slot when the full history is reorganized. Preserve these stored values rather than substituting the calculated average.

Read the global channel signals produced by the companion indicator. That indicator carries its previous direction forward, changes it to long when the current close exceeds the previous upper ATR channel, and changes it to short when the close falls below the previous lower ATR channel. In a long direction, the lower channel cannot decrease; in a short direction, the upper channel cannot increase. Publish the resulting direction as mutually exclusive long and short signals. Retain the previous signals in a fixed-depth history: the most recent prior pair is copied correctly, but subsequent sell-history slots are copied from buy-history slots, and the third slot is also overwritten from the second when the second is populated.

Build long and short scores from the historical channel signals, giving more weight to recent slots. Add weighted comparisons of snapshot values: compare each of the latest several snapshots with selected older snapshots, rewarding recent increases for the long score and decreases for the short score, with weights declining from positive through zero to negative as the comparison interval grows. Apply this pattern to the basket RSI, reference RSI, basket and reference previous-bar closes, basket and reference asks for long, and basket and reference bids for short. For the latest reference-RSI comparisons contributing to the short score, use the basket RSI instead; this duplicates the corresponding basket-RSI short contribution.

Once the required history exists and local time access is enabled, require the absolute current RVI main-to-signal difference to exceed its threshold and the current signal line to differ from at least one of the recent recorded main lines. If at least one score meets its threshold, select long when its score is strictly higher and the current channel signal is exclusively long; select short symmetrically. A tie or an unmet condition leaves the prior pending direction unchanged. Time access is enabled when local hour, minute and second fall within their allowed ranges; an out-of-range second disables it, while an out-of-range hour or minute alone leaves its previous state unchanged.

When the total number of open orders is below the position limit and sufficient history exists, open the pending direction on the chart instrument and clear it. Independently, inspect the first open order and close a market position when its monetary profit reaches the profit threshold or its loss reaches the loss threshold. A pending direction does not itself close an existing position.
