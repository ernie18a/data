This EA manages existing trades and pending orders; it does not open trades. When enabled, if account equity exceeds a target calculated from balance, close every open trade and delete every pending order, then reset the target from the updated balance. For each buy or sell, optional hard-stop logic places a fixed stop on trades without a stop-loss; the buy stop is below entry, while the sell stop is calculated as the configured distance above zero. Optional hidden-stop logic closes a trade without a stop-loss or with a stop on the loss side when the quote crosses the configured adverse distance from entry. Optional dynamic-stop logic uses the previous bar’s open as its reference: for a buy, place a stop below that open if it is below entry; for a sell, place a stop above it if it is above entry. The hidden dynamic variant instead closes the trade when the quote crosses that adverse threshold. Optional trailing logic activates when net trade profit exceeds a balance-based threshold with a minimum floor, then moves the stop as price advances, maintaining a positive buffer that accounts for the configured trail distance, guaranteed-profit distance, and spread. Optional pending-order trailing moves buy-stop orders down toward the current ask and sell-stop orders up toward the current bid when they are farther away than the configured distance plus the market’s minimum stop distance and spread. No option decisions are present.
