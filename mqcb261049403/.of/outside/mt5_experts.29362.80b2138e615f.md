On each tick, calculate strength for each currency from a fixed basket of exchange rates: GBPUSD, USDCHF, EURUSD, USDJPY, USDCAD, NZDUSD, AUDUSD, AUDNZD, AUDCAD, AUDCHF, AUDJPY, CADJPY, CHFJPY, EURGBP, EURAUD, EURCHF, EURJPY, EURNZD, EURCAD, GBPCHF, GBPAUD, GBPCAD, GBPJPY, GBPNZD and NZDJPY. Use the unprefixed pair names for this calculation. For each pair, locate its current bid within the high-low range over a configurable number of candles and historical shift; use the shifted close if the bid is unavailable. Optionally smooth by averaging the ratios for all nested lookback lengths up to that number, with either equal weights or decreasing triangular weights for longer lookbacks. Treat division by zero as zero. Convert the ratio into a discrete score using configurable ascending cutoffs and score levels. Give the base currency that score and the quote currency the complementary score relative to the maximum score; average each currency's scores across its basket pairs.

Evaluate these traded pairs in order: USDJPY, USDCAD, AUDUSD, USDCHF, GBPUSD, EURUSD, NZDUSD, EURJPY, EURCAD, EURGBP, EURCHF, EURAUD, EURNZD, AUDNZD, AUDCAD, AUDCHF, AUDJPY, CHFJPY, GBPCHF, GBPAUD, GBPCAD, GBPJPY, CADJPY, NZDJPY, GBPNZD, CADCHF. Buy a pair when its base strength is strictly above the upper threshold and its quote strength strictly below the lower threshold; sell when the opposite holds. Enter only during the configured time window and an open broker session, with valid quotes, sufficient margin and valid volume, fewer than the configured maximum strategy positions, no open position of any strategy on that pair, and no strategy deal on that pair today. Process signals sequentially, so earlier entries can consume the position limit.

If enabled, close all strategy positions when today's realized strategy profit including swap and commission plus current strategy floating profit including swap and blocked commission reaches the configured target, expressed either as money or as a percentage of account balance. Signals are still evaluated afterward.
