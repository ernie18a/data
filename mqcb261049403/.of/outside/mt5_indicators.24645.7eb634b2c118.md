Use the signal line of a stochastic oscillator calculated for the selected instrument and timeframe. When it crosses upward into the overbought zone, emit a directional signal only if the current candle’s body engulfs the previous candle’s body: a bullish engulfing pattern signals long; a bearish engulfing pattern signals short. Apply the same candle rule when the signal line crosses downward into the oversold zone. Ignore crossings in the opposite direction and crossings without an engulfing pattern.
