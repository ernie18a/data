On each eligible new trading bar, derive a long signal from a bullish fast/slow EMA crossover, or from a bullish EMA trend with positive momentum and a breakout above recent highs. Derive a short signal symmetrically; give the long signal priority if both occur. Use closed bars for price and trend inputs.

Score the signal using the traded instrument’s hourly and four-hour EMA biases, volatility relative to its recent average, spread, session, and position within its recent price range. Treat conflicting higher-timeframe biases as neutral. Optionally add or subtract configured score weights according to whether the hourly EMA bias of an external currency pair agrees or disagrees with the signal; neutral external bias has no effect. Clamp the score to its configured range. Optionally veto signals opposed by the higher-timeframe bias, with unsuitable volatility, or with unsuitable price structure. Outside session overlap, an allowed London-open exception requires higher-timeframe alignment. Enter in the signal direction only if the score and spread-adjusted reward-to-risk pass their thresholds and the other session, spread, account-risk, position-count, trading-permission and order-validity gates pass. An opposing external currency bias also reduces position size, subject to minimum-lot and margin rules.

Block new entries during a manual or bridge news pause or a configured window around qualifying USD economic calendar events. When configured, close all strategy positions during such a news block outside testing. A bridge command can also close a specified position or all strategy positions, or request a long or short market trade; manual market trades are blocked by news and constrained by valid size, margin and stop placement.

Set ATR-based stops and targets, with a wider target for high-scoring runner trades. Manage positions with configured time limits, profit-taking, partial exits, breakeven and ATR trailing stops; runners use separate settings. In testing, manage exits only with the attached stops, targets and time limit.
