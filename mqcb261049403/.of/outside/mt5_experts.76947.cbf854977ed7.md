For positions within the configured symbol and strategy scope, calculate account equity loss from the balance at the start of each trading day and drawdown from the highest observed equity. When either enabled limit is reached, exit the scoped positions if breach closing is enabled; otherwise only report the breach. Trigger each daily breach once per day. After a total drawdown breach, re-arm when drawdown recovers to at most half its trigger level, or after the cooldown by resetting the equity peak to current equity. The system opens no positions.
