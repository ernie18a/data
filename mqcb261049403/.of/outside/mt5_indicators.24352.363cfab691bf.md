For the traded instrument, count advances and declines among available instruments containing the selected base currency, reversing direction for pairs where that currency is quoted. Smooth the advance-minus-decline balance at fast and slow rates and subtract the slower result from the faster. Derive upper and lower thresholds from the oscillator’s recent range. Treat a move above the upper threshold as bullish and a move below the lower threshold as bearish; when it returns between the thresholds, treat it as neutral and exit any directional position.
