On a manual buy request, go long when the traded instrument’s monthly MACD main line is above its signal line; on a manual sell request, go short when it is below. A manual close request closes both sides. Close the strategy’s open basket when its net profit reaches a configured target, when its loss reaches a configured hard limit, or when it reaches a configured loss limit with at least three positions open. Also close the basket when its profit, after rising above zero, falls by a configured amount from its peak; when cumulative loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s equity high-water mark; or when cumulative profit reaches a configured cash target.
