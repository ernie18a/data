On each completed bar of the chosen signal timeframe, build a smoothed candle: use the oldest smoothed open in the rolling lookback, the highest smoothed high, the lowest smoothed low, and the current smoothed close; extend the high or low if needed to contain the body. If the body lies entirely above the candle’s range midpoint, open long and close any short. If it lies entirely below the midpoint, open short and close any long. If the body crosses the midpoint, open neither direction; close a short when the candle is bullish, and close a long when it is bearish. Close any open position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit.
