On each tick, if enabled, sum the traded instrument's strategy-owned open positions' profit, swap and commission. If the sum reaches the configured profit or loss percentage of account balance, close those positions and skip the rest of the tick. On each new bar, if enabled and the strategy-owned positions' combined unrealized profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds the configured percentage of account equity, close those positions. The equity reference is updated using current and previous account equity as in the source: reset it to current equity when there are no strategy-owned positions; then use previous equity if it exceeds the reference, otherwise use current equity. If money trailing is enabled and the traded instrument's summed net position profit is negative, a loss exceeding a configured multiple of that equity-based limit enables a further money-loss check that can close all orders for the instrument. New entries also require sufficient account free margin and compliance with the broker's account stop-out limits.
