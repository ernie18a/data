Continuously sum the profit and swap of all open orders. Once the total exceeds a configurable minimum-profit threshold, set a floor at the current total multiplied by one minus a configurable percentage. Raise the floor whenever the total profit reaches a new level that implies a higher floor. When total profit falls below the floor, close all open buy and sell orders across symbols, retrying until none remain. Reset the trailing state after all orders are closed. No long or short entry decisions are made.
