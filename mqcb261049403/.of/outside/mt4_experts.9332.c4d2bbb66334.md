On every tick, calculate currency strength on the chart timeframe for the last two completed bars. For each non-USD currency, calculate two composite averages of its USD-pair price: sum moving averages using the configured method and price, with periods equal to the respective configured base period multiplied by successive factors. Starting at the chart timeframe, use the suffix of the supported timeframe sequence from one minute through monthly; start the factor at 1 and, after each included timeframe, add the corresponding increment from 5, 3, 2, 2, 4, 6, 4, 4. For each currency, express its value in USD as the composite average for a currency/USD pair, the reciprocal for a USD/currency pair, and 1 for USD. Its strength is the sum, over every other currency, of (its fast USD value divided by the other's fast USD value) divided by (its slow USD value divided by the other's slow USD value), minus 1. If required composite averages are unavailable or zero, do not calculate that bar.

Find the strongest and weakest currencies on the most recently completed bar, resolving ties in the order USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY, AUD, CAD, NZD. A strongest-currency entry is eligible only if some currency had strictly greater strength than the current strongest on the preceding bar. A weakest-currency entry is eligible only if some currency had strictly lower strength than the current weakest on the preceding bar. For each side independently, if no order with that side's identifier exists and its entry is eligible, trade the selected currency against USD: buy the strongest or sell the weakest, reversing the order direction when USD is the pair's base currency. Open no trade when the selected currency is USD. Attach a fixed-distance stop loss and no take profit.

After considering entries, close each identified strongest-side order if its traded non-USD currency is no longer the strongest, and close each identified weakest-side order if its traded non-USD currency is no longer the weakest. Stop losses can also close positions.
