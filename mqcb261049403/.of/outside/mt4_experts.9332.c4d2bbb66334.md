On each evaluation, rank the eight currency-strength readings derived from fast-versus-slow moving-average comparisons across major currency pairs, using the last completed bar. For the strongest and weakest currencies, enter only when that currency was not already the corresponding extreme on the preceding completed bar, and only if no position for that role is open. For the strongest, buy EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, or NZDUSD when the strong currency is the base currency; sell USDCHF, USDJPY, or USDCAD when it is the quote currency. For the weakest, take the inverse direction in the corresponding pair. Do not open a trade when USD is the selected extreme. Close a role’s open position when its currency is no longer the current strongest or weakest, respectively. Trades have a stop loss and no take profit.
