Read an external control command and supplied stop-loss and take-profit prices. For a buy or sell command, enter in the requested direction only if there are no open orders, trading is enabled, the trading pause has elapsed, account equity remains above the peak-equity reserve threshold, and the requested levels satisfy the broker's minimum distances. Size the order from account equity, the configured risk fraction and stop distance, rounded down to permitted lot increments and capped at the maximum; reject it if the size is below the minimum or free margin is insufficient. Buy at the ask and sell at the bid. Attach the supplied stop-loss and take-profit. On an external close command, attempt to close the selected open order. On an external modification command, update the selected order's supplied stop or target if the new levels meet broker distance restrictions; do not allow a long stop to decrease or a short stop to increase. Clear each command after processing.
