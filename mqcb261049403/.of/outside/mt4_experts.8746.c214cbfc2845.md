When no positions or pending orders exist, place buy-stop and sell-stop entries symmetrically around the current price, forming a breakout corridor. After either entry fills, remove the old pending order and place a new buy-stop above and sell-stop below the latest filled entry, each one channel-width away. Repeat this after each fill, increasing the next order size by the initial lot increment when arithmetic lot addition is enabled. Close the instrument’s open positions when their combined unrealized profit exceeds its profit target. Also close positions and cancel pending orders across all participating instruments when their combined unrealized profit, measured in each instrument’s points, exceeds the shared profit target.
