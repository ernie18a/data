For all open positions across instruments with the same strategy identifier, sum their unweighted price movements in each instrument's points: current bid minus entry price for longs, and entry price minus current ask for shorts. While the current instrument has an active position, if this sum strictly exceeds the configured aggregate profit threshold, set a shared close-all signal. On receiving that signal, each strategy instance closes its own instrument's long and short positions and deletes its pending orders. Keep the signal active until no orders with that identifier remain across instruments.
