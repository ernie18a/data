Evaluate on the first tick of each new bar. When enabled, sum the traded instrument’s open strategy-position profit, swap, and commission. If the basket reaches a configured profit or loss percentage of account balance, close those positions and stop processing for that bar. When enabled, close those positions if their combined floating profit, excluding swap and commission, is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured percentage of the account-equity reference. Update that reference using current and previous account equity as follows: reset it to current equity when there are no open strategy trades; then, if the stored reference is below previous equity, set it to previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity; finally store current equity as previous equity. An optional money-trailing rule can also remove all orders on the instrument when its sampled net floating loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-risk threshold and its accumulating absolute position losses reach the configured trailing-loss distance from the stored profit reference. In the second martingale mode, scan account-wide order history, including other instruments, remembering the direction of each selected gross-losing order. While scanning, for each losing closed strategy trade on the current instrument before the first profitable one in reverse close-time order, attempt a trade opposite the remembered losing direction, provided no order exists on the instrument and margin requirements are met. The account history scan and its order selection affect which losing direction is remembered.
