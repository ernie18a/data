On each new bar, open a long if the open-trade count is below its cap, current price is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, a weighted moving average of typical price on the mapped higher timeframe is above the slower one, the prior bar’s low is below the bar before it’s high, and current monthly RSI is above the previous monthly RSI. In live trading, also require the recent-fractal high line to slope upward, the recent-fractal low line to slope downward, and the main trend line to slope downward; omit these line filters in testing. Open a short under the corresponding conditions: price sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, the fast average below the slow one, the prior bar’s low below the bar before it’s high, and current monthly RSI below its previous value. In live trading, require the same two fractal-line orientations and an upward-sloping main trend line; omit these line filters in testing. Attach a stop loss and take profit to each entry. Close positions when an enabled aggregate money or balance-percentage profit target is reached, when the equity-loss limit is exceeded, or when the enabled aggregate money trailing rule triggers. Move stops to lock in a gain after a position moves favorably by its break-even threshold; trail stops using recent candle highs or lows when candle trailing is enabled, otherwise trail by price after the trailing threshold. The defined moving-average/Bollinger exit routine is not called by the tick handler, so it does not govern exits.
