Close the instrument’s open basket when its combined realized and floating trade profit reaches an enabled cash target or a target percentage of account balance. Some versions also close the basket after profit has reached a threshold and then retreats by a specified cash amount. If enabled, close the basket when its loss exceeds a specified fraction of the account’s highest tracked equity. In the capital-protection version, close at a balance-based profit target, at a hard balance-based loss limit, or at a separate balance-based loss limit once the basket contains at least three trades. Entry signals are not based on external data.
