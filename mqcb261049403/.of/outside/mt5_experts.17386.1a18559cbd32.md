On an enabled trading day, when there is no open position and no trade has already closed during the current selected candle, generate a buy signal if the current candle closes above its Parabolic SAR while the previous candle closed below its SAR, the faster EMA is above the slower EMA, and the current ask is above a third EMA. Generate a sell signal on the inverse conditions, using the current bid below the third EMA. Optionally reverse the resulting direction. Open only if the stop distance passes the configured minimum and maximum checks and the order volume is valid. Set the stop loss from the SAR distance or a fixed distance, and the take profit from a multiple of the stop distance or a fixed distance. Close an open position on the opposite signal when enabled, accounting for any signal reversal. As a position-management exit rule, move the stop to lock in a small gain after a profit threshold, then trail it behind price after a further threshold. If the prior closed trade lost money and martingale sizing is enabled, increase the next trade's risk; otherwise use the normal risk-based size.
