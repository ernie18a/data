On every tick, if basket protection is enabled and there are open strategy positions on the traded instrument, sum their profit, swap, and commission. Close those positions when the sum reaches the configured profit percentage of account balance or falls to the negative of the configured loss percentage; then skip the rest of that tick.

On each new bar, if the equity stop is enabled and the strategy's open positions on the instrument have a combined trading loss greater than the configured percentage of the tracked account equity, close those positions. Update tracked equity when evaluating this condition: reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then use previous equity if it exceeds the stored value, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity as previous equity.

If money trailing is enabled, there are strategy positions, the instrument's net profit including swap and commission is negative and below the configured profit threshold, and the strategy's trading loss exceeds the equity-risk percentage of tracked account equity multiplied by the configured loss factor, evaluate the loss trail. Iterate the instrument's orders in descending position index, starting at the total order count and stopping at index one. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each selected market position. If the absolute total net profit is no greater than the absolute stored trail value, set that value to the absolute total net profit. When the accumulated amount reaches the absolute difference between the stored trail value and the configured money stop, close all market positions and delete pending orders on the instrument. Reset the stored trail value when no strategy positions remain.
