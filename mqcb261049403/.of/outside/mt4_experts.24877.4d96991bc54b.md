When the open-position limit permits a new trade, use the configured higher-timeframe moving averages as a trend filter. Go long when the fast average is above the slow average, the current quote is sufficiently far from a recent candle close, and the recent candle ranges overlap; in live trading, also require the chart’s Gann line to slope upward. Go short under the mirrored conditions: fast average below slow, sufficient quote-to-close distance, overlapping recent ranges, and a downward Gann line in live trading. In backtests, omit the Gann-line filter. Close positions through their stop-loss or take-profit, enabled portfolio profit or drawdown controls, or trailing and break-even stop adjustments.
