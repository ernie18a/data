On the first tick of each new bar, apply these enabled account-dependent exit rules in order:
1. If the cumulative floating profit of the current instrument's market orders reaches a configured percentage of account balance, close all its market orders and delete its pending orders. The profit scan runs backward from the order count through index 1, omitting index 0, and checks the threshold after each selected order.
2. For the money-trailing loss rule, if the scanned net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured money-profit threshold, and the strategy's current-instrument floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the tracked account equity multiplied by a loss factor, scan the same orders backward, accumulating the absolute net profit of each current-instrument market order. Once this total reaches the configured money-loss amount, close all current-instrument market orders and delete its pending orders.
3. If the equity stop is enabled and the strategy's current-instrument floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of tracked account equity, close its current-instrument market positions. For this calculation, when there are no such positions first set tracked equity to current equity; then set it to the previous sampled equity if the tracked value is lower than that previous value, otherwise set it to current equity. Finally record current equity as the previous sample. Update this state whenever either loss rule evaluates its equity threshold.
