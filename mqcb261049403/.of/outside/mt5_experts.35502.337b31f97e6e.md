Evaluate once per new bar, using the last completed candle. For a long entry, require no existing long position, the fast EMA above the medium EMA and the medium EMA above the slow EMA, and the candle’s open above the medium EMA. The candle must be bullish; its upper wick must be no larger than its configured share of the full range, its lower wick must meet the configured minimum share, and its total range must be within configured size limits. Its close-to-open rise must be at least the configured impulse multiple of the largest high-low range among the preceding quiet bars, with that range above the configured minimum. Also require the candle’s low at or below the fast EMA, then buy with preset fixed-distance stop loss and take profit. For a short entry, reverse the EMA ordering and open-versus-medium-EMA comparison; require no existing short position, a bearish candle, upper wick meeting its configured minimum share and lower wick no larger than its configured maximum share, size within configured limits, and a close-to-open fall meeting the same quiet-range impulse test. Also require the candle’s high at or above the fast EMA, then sell with preset fixed-distance stop loss and take profit. Close positions when their stop loss or take profit is reached.
