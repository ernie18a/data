On the configured price series and timeframe, calculate eight successive exponential smoothings, combine them with alternating binomial weights to form a pentuple exponential average, then round it to the configured price increment. Color each bar by the average’s change from the prior bar: rising, falling, or unchanged (carry forward the prior color). On each signal update, open a long when the selected bar is rising and the preceding bar was not; close that long when the selected bar is falling. Open a short when the selected bar is falling and the preceding bar was not; close that short when the selected bar is rising. Long and short signals use independently configurable settings. Also close a position when its holding time exceeds its configured limit. Apply each direction’s configured enable switches; fixed stop loss and take profit may also close positions.
