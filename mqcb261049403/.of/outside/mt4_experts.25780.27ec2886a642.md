On each new bar, open a long if the active-trade limit is not reached, at least one of the latest three higher-timeframe money-flow readings is oversold, the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the specified recent-low/high relationship holds, and price is farther than a configured threshold from the referenced close. Open a short under the mirrored conditions: at least one of the latest three money-flow readings is overbought, the fast average is below the slow one, the opposite recent-candle relationship holds, and price is sufficiently far from its referenced close. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry. Close all positions when enabled aggregate profit reaches its money or balance-relative target, or when enabled floating losses exceed a configured fraction of the account’s equity peak. Manage open positions with enabled candle-based or distance-based trailing stops and move stops beyond entry after a configured favorable move.
