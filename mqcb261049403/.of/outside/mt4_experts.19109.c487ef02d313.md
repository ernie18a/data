At initialization and once daily at the designated recalculation hour, compute Pearson correlations of aligned historical closing prices for distinct Market Watch symbols. Retain pairs whose correlation magnitude strictly exceeds the threshold but is strictly below perfect correlation, subject to the implementation's removal of duplicate or overlapping pairs and invalid pair identifiers or volatility ratios. Compute each pair's size ratio as the second symbol's average historical high-low range in points divided by the first symbol's, using bars valid for both.

For each retained pair with no existing order on either symbol under its pair identifier, find each symbol's recent high, low and midpoint on the chart timeframe. Map the second symbol's current close onto the first symbol's range: subtract the second midpoint, multiply by the first range divided by the second range, and add the first midpoint. Reverse the sign of the second range for negative correlation. Divide the first close minus this mapped close by the first symbol's point size. If the result is below the negative overlay threshold, buy the first symbol and sell the second for positive correlation, or buy both for negative correlation. If it is above the positive threshold, sell the first and buy the second for positive correlation, or sell both for negative correlation. Otherwise do nothing.

Enter only when the total order count is below its limit, used margin is less than free margin, and both spreads are below their limit. Size the second leg with a free-margin- and leverage-based base lot, bounded by the reference instrument's lot limits; size the first leg by multiplying that lot by the volatility ratio. Submit the first leg and submit the other only if the first succeeds; for the sell-first-symbol/buy-second-symbol signal, submit the second symbol's buy first. An existing single leg prevents a new entry on later checks.

For each tracked order identifier, close all its positions when either enabled group take-profit condition is met: summed net profit in account currency reaches its threshold, or the sum of each order's net profit divided by the current base lot and that symbol's tick value, with each contribution truncated to an integer, reaches the points threshold. Net profit includes commission and swap. Continue monitoring identifiers of existing orders even if their pairs no longer qualify after recalculation.
