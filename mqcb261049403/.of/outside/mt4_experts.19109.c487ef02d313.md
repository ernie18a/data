For each distinct instrument pair, trade only when their price correlation is strongly positive or negative, excluding perfect correlation. Build an overlay from their recent price ranges: align the second instrument’s range with the first for positive correlation, and invert it for negative correlation; scale its displacement from its range midpoint by the ratio of the instruments’ range widths. Compare the first instrument’s current price with this overlay, expressed in the first instrument’s points. If it is sufficiently below the overlay, buy the first instrument and sell the second when correlation is positive, or buy both when negative. If sufficiently above, sell the first and buy the second when correlation is positive, or sell both when negative. Open a pair only when neither instrument already has an open position for that pair. Close all positions in the pair when their combined floating profit reaches the configured take-profit in points or currency.
