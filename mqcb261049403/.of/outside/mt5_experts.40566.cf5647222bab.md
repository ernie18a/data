On each tick, calculate the traded instrument’s total floating result by summing each open position’s profit, swap, and the account-wide blocked commission, then round the total to two decimal places. The account-wide commission is added once per position. Scale the configured monetary thresholds by the configured order size relative to the reference lot size. Close all positions in the traded instrument if the total result is at or below the enabled maximum-loss threshold. When the result reaches the profit trigger, initialize a profit-stop level at the configured break-even amount. If the result exceeds that level by more than twice the trailing distance, raise the level to the result minus the trailing distance. Close all positions in the instrument when the positive result falls to or below the profit-stop level, provided either the level has risen above the initial break-even amount and the result remains above that amount, or the level still equals the initial amount and the result is at or below it. Remove the profit-stop level when no positions remain.
