Manage eligible trades on symbols containing XAU or GOLD, plus EURUSD and USDJPY; by default, manage manual trades, with optional inclusion of a specified EA magic number. Do not generate entries. For an unprotected buy, set a stop below the lowest low of recent closed bars on the structure timeframe, buffered by ATR; for a sell, set it above the highest high, buffered by ATR. If structure data is unavailable or gives an invalid distance, use an ATR-based fallback, then clamp the distance to configured ATR-based minimum and maximum bounds. Apply the same protection to eligible pending orders, using their entry price as the reference. Optionally set a take profit at a configured multiple of the initial risk. For open positions, when favorable movement reaches a configured multiple of the current entry-to-stop risk, move the stop beyond entry by a configured risk fraction. Once movement reaches the symbol profile's trailing threshold, trail the stop behind the current exit-side price by a profile-specific ATR distance; only move stops in the direction that protects more profit. Estimate net profit from gross profit, swap, and commission. After net profit reaches its activation level, close at market if net profit falls by the allowed amount from its tracked peak, and move the stop to lock the configured dollar profit when that improves protection. Skip modifications when spread exceeds its configured limit; observe broker stop-distance constraints. Use closed-bar ATR and swing data.
