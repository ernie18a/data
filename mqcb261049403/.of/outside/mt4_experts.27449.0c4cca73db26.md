Use account-wide balance, equity and free margin alongside the traded instrument. On every tick, if basket protection is enabled and the strategy has open positions in the instrument, sum their profit, swap and commission. Close those positions and stop processing the tick when the sum reaches either a configured profit percentage or a negative loss percentage of account balance.

If money trailing is enabled and the strategy has positions, calculate the instrument's net open profit across all strategies, but omit the order at position zero. Reset the stored trailing value when the strategy has no positions. When this profit is positive and below the configured money target, scan the same orders in reverse position order, accumulating net profit; update the stored value to the greater of itself and total profit, and close all instrument orders if the running sum falls at least the configured money distance below it. When total net profit is negative and below the target, and the strategy's gross instrument loss exceeds a configured fraction of account equity multiplied by a loss factor, scan those orders in the same order, accumulating absolute net profits. If the absolute total profit is no greater than the absolute stored value, replace the stored value with the absolute total profit. Close all instrument orders when the running sum reaches the absolute difference between that stored value and the configured money distance.

Once per new bar, if equity protection is enabled and the strategy's gross instrument loss exceeds a configured percentage of its equity reference, close its instrument positions. Each time the equity reference is requested, set it to current account equity if there are no strategy positions; then set it to previous account equity if its current value is less than previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity. Finally store current equity as previous equity. Continue evaluating entries after an equity-stop closure.

Allow entries only with sufficient chart history, a valid configured profit target, free margin above the configured base-lot requirement, fewer than the configured maximum strategy positions, sufficient margin after the proposed trade, valid broker stop distances and trading permission. On the designated higher timeframe, compare fast and slow linear-weighted averages of typical price. On the chart timeframe, compare the current and previous MACD main and signal lines and the current and previous close-price exponential trend average. Enter long when the higher-timeframe fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured threshold, MACD is negative and above its signal after being below it on the previous bar, its absolute magnitude exceeds the configured threshold, and the trend average is rising. Enter short when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds the configured threshold, MACD is positive and below its signal after being above it on the previous bar, its magnitude exceeds the configured threshold, and the trend average is falling. Check long first and stop entry processing if its signal occurs, even if its order cannot be placed. Place configured stop-loss and take-profit levels on accepted entries.
