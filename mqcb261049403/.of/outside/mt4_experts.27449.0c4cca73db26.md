Close all open positions in the traded instrument when the basket’s net profit reaches a balance-based profit target or its net loss reaches a balance-based loss limit. Also close the basket if its floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s recorded equity high. Optional money-based exits close the basket at a cash profit target, or after positive basket profit has peaked, if profit falls by a configured cash amount; another optional rule closes it when the cash loss exceeds an equity-high-based limit adjusted by a multiplier.
