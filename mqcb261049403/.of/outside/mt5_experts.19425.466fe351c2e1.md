When time-based trading is enabled and sufficient indicator history is available, use the current server hour and minute to determine whether trading is allowed. If the start hour is earlier than the end hour, allow trading from the start minute through the intervening hours and until, but excluding, the end minute. If the hours are equal, allow trading from the start minute until, but excluding, the end minute. If the start hour is later, allow trading when the hour is at or after the start hour and the minute is at or after the start minute, or when the hour is before the end hour, or when it is the end hour and the minute is before the end minute. Outside the allowed interval, close all positions in the traded instrument and prohibit new entries. When time-based trading is disabled, impose no time restriction.
