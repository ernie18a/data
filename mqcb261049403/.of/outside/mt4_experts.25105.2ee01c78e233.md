On the first tick after a new EURUSD bar is detected, evaluate the traded chart’s current bar. Open a short if its Williams %R is above an upper threshold, its close-based MACD main line is above a threshold, and its stochastic main line is below an upper threshold while the previous bar’s value was strictly above it. The EURUSD bar-time gate uses data outside the traded instrument only when the strategy is attached to another symbol.
