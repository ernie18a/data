For each direction, calculate a volatility-scaled trailing reversal level from that direction’s configured price series: average the high-low ranges over its lookback, then scale that average by a power of ten determined by the quote precision of EURUSD relative to the traded instrument (and the configured precision adjustment). In an uptrend, track the highest close and set the level below it by the scaled range; switch to downtrend when a close falls below the level. In a downtrend, track the lowest close and set the level above it; switch to uptrend when a close rises above the level. On the selected signal bar, open long on a transition to uptrend and close long whenever the downtrend level is present. Open short on a transition to downtrend and close short whenever the uptrend level is present. Long and short use independently configured series; each entry and exit can be enabled or disabled separately.
