Run independent long and short systems on the traded instrument, each with its own configurable timeframe, lookback, scaling adjustment and signal-bar offset. Both use EURUSD's quote precision in their calculations. For each system, initialize a neutral trend and set both the running extreme and trailing level to the oldest calculated close. Process bars chronologically. On each bar, calculate the average high-minus-low range over the lookback ending at that bar, then divide it by 10 raised to the power of EURUSD's decimal digits minus the traded instrument's decimal digits minus the scaling adjustment. In a neutral or rising trend, update the extreme to the greater of its prior value and the close, and the trailing level to the greater of its prior value and the extreme multiplied by one minus the scaled range. If the close is below that level, set the extreme to the close, the level to the close multiplied by one plus the scaled range, and the trend to falling. In a falling trend, update the extreme to the lesser of its prior value and the close, and the trailing level to the lesser of its prior value and the extreme multiplied by one plus the scaled range. If the close is above that level, set the extreme to the close, the level to the close multiplied by one minus the scaled range, and the trend to rising. Recalculate the forming bar from the last completed bar's state. At the chosen signal bar, the long system opens long on a transition from falling to rising and exits long whenever its trend is falling. The short system opens short on a transition from rising to falling and exits short whenever its trend is rising. Evaluate each system on its new bar, or on every tick when using the forming bar. Entry and signal-based exit permissions are independently configurable. Process exits before entries. Each system manages positions under its own identifier, enters only if it has no position, and permits at most one entry per signal timeframe bar. Attach independently configurable fixed-distance stop-loss and take-profit exits to each entry when enabled.
