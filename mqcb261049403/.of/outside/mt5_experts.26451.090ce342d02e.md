On each tick, find the highest high and lowest low across the configured number of completed bars on the traded instrument’s current timeframe, excluding the forming bar. Convert their difference to points. If the range width is within the configured maximum, enter long when the current bid is at or above the range high, or short when it is below the range low. Enter only when there are no open positions in either direction for this instrument and strategy identifier. Apply the configured stop loss and take profit distances to open positions, tightening existing levels only; these levels provide the exit conditions. There is no separate signal to close a position.
