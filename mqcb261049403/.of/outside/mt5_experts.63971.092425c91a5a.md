On each new bar, evaluate the latest completed candle against the prior candle and the candle before that. Ignore patterns below a configurable minimum range or whose body exceeds a configurable fraction of their range. Require the body to lie within the prior candle’s range. Go long when the candle’s low extends below the prior low and is no higher than the earlier low, its body midpoint lies near the prior range’s lower edge, and its lower wick is sufficiently longer than its upper wick. Go short on the mirrored pattern: the high extends above the prior high and is at least the earlier high, the body midpoint lies near the prior range’s upper edge, and the upper wick is sufficiently longer than the lower wick. Enter at most once per direction per bar, and do not reopen in the same direction on the bar where that direction’s position was last closed. Signals do not close opposite positions. Stops and targets are configurable; the stop may be placed beyond the signal candle’s shadow, and an optional trailing method may update stops.
