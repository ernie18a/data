Use a configurable signal instrument and timeframe, which may differ from the traded instrument. Process its bars chronologically. Initialize the trend as neutral and both the tracked price and trailing level to the initial close. For each bar, calculate the average high-low range over a configurable lookback, including that bar, and divide it by 10 raised to the difference between EUR/USD price precision and the signal instrument's price precision, adjusted by a configurable exponent. In a neutral or rising trend, raise the tracked price to the greater of itself and the current high, and raise the trailing level to the greater of itself and the tracked price multiplied by one minus the scaled range. If the current high falls below that level, set the tracked price to the current high, set the level to that price multiplied by one plus the scaled range, and change the trend to falling. In a falling trend, lower the tracked price to the lesser of itself and the current low, and lower the trailing level to the lesser of itself and the tracked price multiplied by one plus the scaled range. If the current low rises above that level, set the tracked price to the current low, set the level to that price multiplied by one minus the scaled range, and change the trend to rising. Signal long on a falling-to-rising transition and short on a rising-to-falling transition. Otherwise retain the last trend; there is no separate exit signal. Recalculate an unfinished bar from the state at the preceding completed bar.
