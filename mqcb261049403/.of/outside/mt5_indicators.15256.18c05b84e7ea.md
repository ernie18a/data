Calculate a reversal threshold from the instrument’s recent average high–low range. In an uptrend, track the highest high and raise a trailing threshold beneath it; if a bar’s high falls below that threshold, switch to a downtrend. In a downtrend, track the lowest low and lower a threshold above it; if a bar’s low rises above that threshold, switch to an uptrend. Signal long on a down-to-up reversal and short on an up-to-down reversal. Otherwise, indicate the continuing trend; no exit rule is defined.
