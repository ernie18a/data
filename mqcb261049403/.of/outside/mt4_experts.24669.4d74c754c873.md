On each new bar, allow entries only while below the trade limit and after basic history, margin, and order-validity checks. For a long, require the faster linear weighted moving average to be above the slower one on the selected higher timeframe, the second prior candle’s low to be below the previous candle’s high, and the current bid to be farther from the second prior close than the configured retracement distance. In live trading, also require the Gann Fan’s second price coordinate to be above its first; in testing, omit this filter. For a short, require the faster average below the slower one, the previous candle’s low below the second prior candle’s high, and the ask farther from the previous close than the retracement distance. In live trading, also require the Gann Fan’s second price coordinate below its first; in testing, omit this filter. Open each position with protective stop-loss and take-profit levels. Manage open positions by moving stops to lock in profit after the configured favorable move, and trail stops using recent candle extremes when candle trailing is enabled. Close all positions for the traded symbol when the configured cash or balance-percentage profit target is reached, or when the basket loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s equity peak. Constituents of the equity-risk check are the traded symbol’s open positions.
