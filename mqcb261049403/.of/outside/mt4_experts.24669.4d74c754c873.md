On each new bar, apply these account-dependent rules:

- If enabled, close all positions and delete pending orders for the instrument when the running sum of market-order profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. Traverse open orders from the total count down to position 1, so position 0 is omitted; check the threshold after each selection.
- If enabled, close this strategy's positions on the instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of the equity reference. Update that reference only when evaluating a negative combined profit: if there are no strategy positions, first set it to current account equity; then set it to the previous recorded equity if the reference is below that previous value, otherwise set it to current equity. Record current equity for the next update.
- If money trailing is enabled and the instrument's net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured money profit threshold, also require the strategy's floating loss to exceed the configured equity-risk percentage multiplied by the loss multiplier. Then traverse orders from the total count down to position 1, accumulating absolute net profit for market orders on the instrument. Close all its positions and delete its pending orders once that accumulation reaches the configured money loss threshold.
- Before opening a position, require account free margin to exceed the configured base-lot margin requirement, and require the proposed trade to pass the account's free-margin and stop-out checks. Subject to those checks and broker constraints, enter long when the faster typical-price linear weighted moving average on the selected higher timeframe exceeds the slower one, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured point distance. Enter short when the moving-average relationship is reversed, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. Require fewer than the configured maximum strategy positions. In live trading additionally require the previous bar's open to exceed the older fan-anchor open for a long, or be below it for a short; omit this filter in testing. A qualifying long branch ends entry evaluation even if its order is rejected.
