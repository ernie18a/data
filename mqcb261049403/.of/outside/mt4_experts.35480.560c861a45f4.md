On each new bar, if no position in that strategy’s direction is open, enter long when the latest completed candle is bullish, its body is within the configured size bounds, its close is above the high of the candle before it, and that earlier candle is bearish. Enter short under the mirrored conditions: the latest completed candle is bearish with a body within the configured bounds, its close is below the earlier candle’s low, and the earlier candle is bullish. Attach configured fixed-distance stop-loss and take-profit levels. Make no entry when the conditions fail.
