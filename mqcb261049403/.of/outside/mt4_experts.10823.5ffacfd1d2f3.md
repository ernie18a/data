For each instrument, trade only during the permitted hours. On each 15-minute update, compute RSI on 5-, 15-, and 30-minute data at the current bar and seven bars earlier, plus fast and slow weighted-price EMAs on 15-minute data. Enter long when all three current RSI readings exceed 55, all three earlier readings are below 50, and the slow EMA is above the fast EMA. Enter short for the inverse: all current readings below 55, all earlier readings above 50, and the slow EMA below the fast EMA. Do not take the trend entry when both directions qualify. Limit these additional market entries by the configured order cap.

Maintain breakout stop orders when a direction is allowed and no order of that direction is pending. Set the sell stop below the lower of the current price and the relevant support estimates, and the buy stop above the higher of the current price and the relevant resistance estimates. Each estimate is a selected higher-timeframe bar extreme expanded by ATR, a price offset, and spread; use H1/H4 in aggressive mode or H4/D1 otherwise, falling back to a fixed distance from price when neither estimate is suitably beyond the market. At the daily reset time, clear pending orders and instead place stops beyond the previous daily high and low, adjusted for distance and broker stop constraints. If only one side is pending, add the opposite-side stop when that direction is allowed. Gate new orders by time, margin, and order-count conditions.

The code also detects a sharp increase in recent M15 range and assigns a stop-entry price based on the direction of the preceding M15 candle. However, it submits the corresponding limit-price variable, which remains zero; as written, this branch does not place those volatility orders.

For risk recovery, when aggregate open profit falls below the configured loss limit relative to account balance, delete pending orders and open a market hedge on the current instrument to offset the net open buy or sell volume. While this emergency state persists, block new entries in directions with losing positions; if losing buy and sell volumes match, add volume on the side with the less-negative aggregate loss.

Close losing positions in baskets when a winning position can cover a selected set of losing positions after applying the hedge multiplier. When aggregate profit is positive and winning positions outweigh losses, close the whole managed basket after a profitable position has aged enough and its price movement relative to recent M15 extremes falls within the configured range. If aggregate profit falls back by the configured amount from its prior peak while winners do not outweigh losses, close positions and reset direction blocks. When aggregate profit reaches the minimum profit target, treat the account as in gain: move profitable positions' stops to break-even and then trail them in increments. For losing positions old enough for pivot management, move their take-profit toward the opposing pivot resistance/support level when price has crossed a support/resistance by at least the configured offset. Close losing positions once they exceed the maximum holding age, when that limit is enabled. A forced-close condition closes all managed market positions and removes pending orders.
