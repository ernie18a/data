The strategy can manage multiple instruments together. When cross-instrument management is enabled, calculate aggregate net profit and counts of open longs and shorts across the selected orders; otherwise use the current instrument. Net profit includes commissions and swap. Pending-order counts remain specific to the current instrument.

Use the aggregate long and short counts to size new hedges, and allow a trend-triggered market order only when their absolute count difference is within the configured per-instrument limit. The trend signal itself uses only the current instrument’s RSI and moving averages.

When aggregate net profit is negative, examine profitable and losing selected positions across the managed instruments. Close one winner together with at least two losers when the winner’s profit exceeds a configured multiple of their combined loss. Test losers in order, starting with each eligible loser and adding eligible earlier losers, reducing the examined prefix until a qualifying group is found.

When aggregate profit reaches the configured minimum, compare gross winning and losing profits across all account orders, even if the managed scope is narrower. If the account-wide winners do not outweigh the losers and aggregate profit has fallen sufficiently from its last recorded value, close the selected positions. If winners do outweigh losers, enable breakeven and stepped trailing stops on selected positions; also close the selected positions when an aged, profitable position meets the configured recent-price-range exit condition. Reset directional permissions after these basket exits.

If aggregate loss exceeds a configured fraction of account balance, cancel selected pending orders and hedge the current instrument’s net exposure with an opposite market order. In the later version, balance all current-instrument positions on first entering this state; while blocked, if losing longs and shorts have equal nonzero lots, open a position in the direction with the smaller aggregate loss. In the earlier version, the balancing scan uses losing positions and attempts a hedge during the scan. Directional permissions for subsequent pending orders are also affected by the profit or loss of positions examined in the blocked state.
