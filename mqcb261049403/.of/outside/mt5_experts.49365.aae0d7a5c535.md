On each tick, sum the unrealized profit of every open position across all instruments, excluding swap. If an enabled positive profit threshold is met or exceeded, or an enabled negative loss threshold is met or breached, close all open positions across all instruments. On a button command, close all positions, only positions whose profit plus swap is positive, only those whose profit plus swap is negative, only long positions, or only short positions, according to the selected button. For each requested close, proceed only if the current price is farther than the instrument's freeze distance from both its take-profit and stop-loss prices; retry as configured.
