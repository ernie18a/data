On each tick, if enabled, sum the net profit, including swap and commission, of this strategy's open positions on the instrument. Close them all and skip the rest of the tick when that sum reaches either a profit target or a loss limit expressed as a percentage of account balance.

On each new bar, if the equity stop is enabled and the strategy's gross open loss on the instrument exceeds a configured percentage of account equity, close its positions. The equity reference is stateful: reset it to current equity when no strategy positions are open; then use the previous observed equity if it exceeds the stored reference, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity for the next observation. Continue evaluating entries after this closure.

Evaluate entries only on a new bar, with sufficient history, a valid configured take-profit distance, fewer than the maximum strategy positions, and sufficient account free margin. Use the current values of fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on the designated higher timeframe, and short, middle, and long exponential moving averages of median price on the chart timeframe. For the short and long averages use the second completed bar; for the middle average use the latest completed bar.

Enter long when the absolute distance from bid to the second completed bar's close exceeds the configured threshold, the higher-timeframe fast average is above the slow average, the second completed bar's low is below the latest completed bar's high, the three chart-timeframe averages ascend from short to long, and the latest completed bar's low is at or below the middle average. Otherwise enter short when the absolute distance from ask to the latest completed bar's close exceeds the threshold, the higher-timeframe fast average is below the slow average, the latest completed bar's low is below the second completed bar's high, the three chart-timeframe averages descend from short to long, and the latest completed bar's high is at or above the middle average. Entries also require sufficient projected margin, valid stop and target distances, and trading permission.

If money trailing is enabled, its loss-side exit also depends on account equity: when the instrument's selected positions have negative net profit and the strategy's gross loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-risk threshold, accumulate the absolute net profits of the selected instrument positions in reverse order and close all instrument orders when the accumulated amount reaches the configured money-loss threshold adjusted by the trailing state. The selection loop starts at the order count and stops before index zero.
