When basket breakeven management is enabled, consider filtered positions on the current instrument. Sum their floating profits, optionally including swaps and entry commissions. When processing a long after a short has already been counted, subtract its spread cost if the selected reference is ask; when processing a short after a long has already been counted, subtract its spread cost if the reference is bid. If this total reaches the configured trigger and net long volume is nonzero, calculate the price that would protect the configured account-currency profit: divide the difference between target and current basket profit by the absolute product of net volume and loss-side value per unit of price movement; add the result to bid for a net long basket, or subtract it from ask for a net short basket. For instruments requiring profit-currency conversion, calculate that value using the ask of a profit-currency/account-currency forex pair, or the reciprocal of the bid of the inverse pair; if neither pair exists, use a conversion factor of one. Set each filtered position's stop to the resulting basket price, adjusting for spread on longs when ask is the reference and shorts exist, or on shorts when bid is the reference and longs exist. Only tighten stops that satisfy broker distance constraints; the position exits if its stop is subsequently hit.
