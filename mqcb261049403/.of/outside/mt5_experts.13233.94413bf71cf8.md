On each order-book update, remove pending limit orders that are crossed or no longer positioned one minimum price step inside the current best quote; also remove an order when it is the sole displayed volume at its best price and the adjacent book-price gap exceeds one step. If a position is open and no pending orders remain, place an opposite-side limit order for twice the open position’s volume to close and reverse it. If flat with no pending orders, place buy and sell limit orders only when the bid–ask spread reaches the configured threshold; price them one minimum step inside the current best bid and ask, respectively.
