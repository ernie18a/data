On each completed bar, compare fast and slow smoothed values of the selected price series. If the fast value exceeds the slow value by at least the configured threshold, mark the trend bullish; if it falls below by at least that threshold, mark it bearish; otherwise there is no directional signal. When a bullish mark appears after any non-bullish or absent mark, close any short and open a long if enabled. When a bearish mark appears after any non-bearish or absent mark, close any long and open a short if enabled. Close any open position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit, if time-based exits are enabled. Open a new position only when no position is already open on the instrument.
