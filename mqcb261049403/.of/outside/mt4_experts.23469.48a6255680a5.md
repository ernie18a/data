On each new bar, allow entries only while the configured position limit and account trading checks permit. For a long entry, require the higher-timeframe Ichimoku conversion line to be above its baseline, the chart-timeframe fast weighted moving average of typical price to be above the slow one, the prior two-bar low to be below the prior bar’s high, and the current bid to be farther from the close two bars ago than a configured price-distance threshold. For a short entry, require the Ichimoku conversion line below its baseline, the fast moving average below the slow one, the prior bar’s low below the two-bars-ago high, and the current ask farther from the prior bar’s close than that threshold. Attach configured fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry. Close all positions in the instrument if their combined loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high; optionally also close all when combined profit reaches a configured cash amount or percentage of account balance. When money trailing is enabled, track favorable basket profit and close positions after a configured drawdown from its peak; apply the configured equity-risk threshold and loss multiplier to basket losses. For individual positions, once price moves favorably by a configured distance, move the stop beyond entry by a configured offset if the existing stop is still behind entry.
