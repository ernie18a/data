On the first tick of each new chart bar, manage exits and then consider entries. Account-wide balance, equity and free margin affect decisions.

For entries, require sufficient chart history, a valid configured profit target, free margin above the configured base-lot requirement, and fewer than the maximum number of this strategy's open positions on the instrument. Use the previous completed bar's Ichimoku conversion and base lines on a higher timeframe: map the chart timeframe to 15-minute, 30-minute, hourly, 4-hour, daily, weekly or monthly respectively for 1-minute through daily charts; weekly and monthly charts use the chart timeframe. Compare current-bar fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on the chart timeframe. Go long when the conversion line is above the base line, the fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from the bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured point distance. Otherwise go short when both indicator comparisons are reversed, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from the ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. Subject orders to trade permission, margin and broker stop-distance checks. Size starts at the base lot multiplied by a configured exponent raised to the number of strategy positions; after more than one consecutive losing historical trade on the instrument, replace that size with a configured fraction of account free margin, then clamp and round it to broker volume rules. Attach configured stop-loss and take-profit distances.

If enabled, close all strategy positions on the instrument when their combined floating profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference when this check runs: reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions, then set it to the previous observed equity if the reference is below that previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity; finally remember current equity.

If enabled, close all instrument positions and delete its pending orders when the sampled sum of instrument position profits reaches a configured money target or a configured percentage of account balance. These profit scans iterate from the order count down to index one, omitting index zero; the percentage check is applied to the running sum.

If enabled, the money trailing rule resets its stored reference when there are no strategy positions. Otherwise, sum instrument position profit including commission and swap using the same index-one cutoff. When that sum is positive but below the configured profit ceiling, scan the same orders, update the stored reference to the greater of itself and the total, and close all instrument orders if the running profit falls at least the configured money distance below that reference. When the total is negative and below the ceiling, first require the strategy positions' loss to exceed the configured equity-risk threshold multiplied by the configured loss factor; then scan the same orders, accumulating absolute net profits, lower the stored reference to the absolute total if it is no greater than the absolute stored reference, and close all instrument orders if the running absolute sum reaches the absolute difference between that reference and the configured money distance.
