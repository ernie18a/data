On each tick, check total floating account profit against the configured loss and profit limits. In percentage mode, divide total floating profit by account balance and convert to a percentage; trigger an exit when the loss percentage is greater than or equal to the loss limit or the profit percentage is greater than or equal to the profit limit. In deposit-currency mode, trigger an exit when the loss or profit strictly exceeds its respective limit. Once triggered, attempt to close every open long and short position across all instruments, repeating on subsequent ticks until no positions remain. Then reset the exit state and resume monitoring.
