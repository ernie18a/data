On each new bar, consider entries only while below the open-trade limit. Go long when the fast weighted moving average on the selected higher timeframe is above the slow one, the previous two candles satisfy the coded low/high overlap condition, and the current bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars back. In live trading, also require the chart’s recent fractal trend lines to satisfy the long-side geometry filter: the lower line’s price span is smaller than the upper line’s, the recent upper-line high exceeds its older high, and the recent lower-line high is below its older high. Go short when the fast average is below the slow one, the adjacent candles satisfy the corresponding coded overlap condition, and the ask is sufficiently far from the prior candle’s close. In live trading, also require the short-side fractal-line filter: the lower line’s span exceeds the upper line’s, and highs at the recent line endpoints are below highs at their older endpoints. In testing, omit both trend-line filters. Attach a protective stop and profit target to each entry. For open trades, move the stop after favorable movement reaches the configured threshold, then trail it using recent candle extremes (or price distance when candle trailing is disabled). Close the symbol’s trades when an enabled aggregate profit target in money or as a share of balance is reached, or when aggregate losses exceed the configured equity-risk threshold. A separate exit routine based on moving-average alignment and a lower volatility band is defined but never called, so it does not affect decisions.
