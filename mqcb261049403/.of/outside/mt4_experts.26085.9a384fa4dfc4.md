On each new bar, optionally close all orders for the traded symbol when its accumulated open-position profit reaches a configured fraction of account balance. Sum positions in reverse order starting at the order count and stopping before index zero; check the threshold after each addition.

Optionally close the strategy's positions on the symbol when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of account equity. For this calculation, maintain an equity reference and previous equity: reset the reference to current equity when there are no strategy positions; then, if the reference is below previous equity, replace it with previous equity, otherwise replace it with current equity. Update previous equity on each calculation.

For the optional money-based trailing exit, sum symbol positions' profit including commission and swap using the same reverse iteration that skips index zero. When this sum is negative and below the configured profit ceiling, and the strategy's floating loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-based risk threshold, accumulate the absolute net profit of those positions in reverse order. Close all symbol orders if that running total reaches the configured money-loss amount. The reference used by this calculation resets to zero on every bar.

Entries require sufficient account free margin and a successful projected margin and stop-out check, as well as valid broker stop levels and trading permission. Subject to the configured position limit and minimum chart history, compare fast and slow linear-weighted moving averages of typical price on the designated higher timeframe. Buy when the fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance. Sell when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds the configured distance.

In live trading, add a fractal filter using the latest and third-latest upper fractal bars and the latest and third-latest lower fractal bars. For a buy, the absolute change in lows between the lower-fractal bars must be smaller than that between the upper-fractal bars; the high at the latest upper fractal must exceed the high at the older upper fractal, and the high at the latest lower fractal must be below the high at the older lower fractal. For a sell, reverse the absolute-change comparison and require the high at each latest fractal bar to be below the high at its corresponding older fractal bar. Testing omits this filter.
