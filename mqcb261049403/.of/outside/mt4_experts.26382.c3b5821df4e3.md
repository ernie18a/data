In advanced profit-closing mode, compare the net floating profit and loss of all traded pairs, including hedge positions. Select the most profitable pair and the most losing pair. If both pairs have open long and short positions, and their combined net result reaches the largest applicable profit target across their four directional baskets, close all positions on both pairs. Otherwise, if the combined floating result across all pairs reaches the larger directional profit target of the most losing pair, close all positions across all pairs. Directional profit targets are based on the pair's lots, tick value and configured profit target; in automatic-step mode they are recalculated using total directional lots for these cross-pair checks. Advanced mode falls back to hybrid mode when fewer than the required number of pairs are traded.
