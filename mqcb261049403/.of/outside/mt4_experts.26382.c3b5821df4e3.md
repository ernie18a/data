For each selected currency pair, evaluate on a new bar when trading is enabled, within the allowed time window, and the spread is acceptable. In normal operation, open an initial long and short when neither side has orders. In grid mode, add to a side when its open positions’ combined floating loss reaches the next adverse-price step; increase step spacing and position size according to the configured progression, subject to order and lot limits. In automatic-step mode, when one side has been fully closed while the other remains open, open a position on the now-empty side. Close positions when the configured per-side or basket profit target is reached; hybrid and advanced modes may close the less populated side first, then close a basket or coordinate closes across the most profitable and most losing pairs. If loss closing is enabled, close a whole side or its oldest position when its loss reaches the configured limit. Optionally close all positions for a pair when combined loss reaches the account-equity threshold. Emergency management may partially close profitable positions, open an opposite hedge when order imbalance reaches its trigger, close the hedge with selected positions after recovery, and reduce the more populated side when its order count reaches a further trigger. Standby mode prevents new entries; close-and-stop mode waits for all positions to reach a profit exit and then stops; immediate-close mode closes all positions.
