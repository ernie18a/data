Form couples of two currency pairs sharing one currency, using a user-ordered currency list. For each enabled couple, define one side as long the first pair and short the second when their shared currency occupies the same position in both symbols; otherwise make both legs long. The opposite side reverses both trades. Open the configured side or both sides when trading is permitted.

For each side, sum floating profit and swap across both legs. Derive its grid-loss level from the total lots on each leg, a configurable step, and a progression based on the number of existing two-leg groups; use either tick-value weighting or fair-lot weighting, then subtract estimated spread cost and absolute swap. In manual grid mode, add a two-leg group when side profit falls to or below that level, subject to the configured time and group limits. In automatic mode with both sides enabled, reopen a missing side while the opposite side remains open.

For profit exits, calculate each side's target from its leg volumes, the configured profit target and, unless fair-lot weighting is selected, each leg's tick value; subtract estimated spread cost and absolute swap, but floor it at the initial-leg target weighted by tick value. Depending on the selected mode, close either a profitable side reaching its target, or both sides when their combined profit reaches the larger side target. In automatic grid mode with the combined exit, also close a profitable side with no more orders than the opposite side when the opposite side reaches its grid-loss level. Apply the configured tick confirmation delay.

Alternatively, aggregate same-side profit across predefined overlapping collections of couples sharing a pair or a common currency; close every included side when the collection's positive profit reaches the largest individual side target. The combined hybrid mode applies both collection types.

If loss exits are enabled, calculate the side's loss threshold from each leg's average position size and the configured loss target, with optional tick-value weighting, then subtract estimated spread cost and absolute swap. After the configured tick delay below that threshold, close either the whole losing side or, if it has more orders than the opposite side, its oldest position on each leg. Optional order reconciliation opens missing legs or closes excess oldest positions when the order limit prevents opening. Respect configured trading sessions, spread and order limits, and operation modes, including immediate close-all and stop.
