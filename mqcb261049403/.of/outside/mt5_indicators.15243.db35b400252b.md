Maintain a trend state and an extreme closing price since the last reversal. Compute a reversal distance from the average high–low range over a configurable lookback, scaled by the difference in quote precision between the traded instrument and EURUSD, with an adjustable precision offset. In an uptrend, raise the extreme to any higher close and place the reversal threshold below it by that distance; if a close falls below the threshold, switch to a downtrend and reset the extreme to that close. In a downtrend, lower the extreme to any lower close and place the threshold above it; if a close rises above the threshold, switch to an uptrend and reset the extreme. A switch to uptrend signals long; a switch to downtrend signals short, reversing the prior direction.
