Never open positions. On each new position, close it if the account’s daily loss lock is active or if the open-position cap is exceeded, closing the newest position. Otherwise, add a protective stop if missing; then reduce volume to the amount whose stop-loss risk fits the configured per-trade risk budget, closing the position if even the minimum volume exceeds that budget. Track closed and floating account P/L for the day; when their sum reaches the daily loss limit, lock trading until the next server day and optionally close all guarded positions. Apply these rules to all positions or only the selected instrument, according to scope settings.
