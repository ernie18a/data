Use configurable entry signals that each return long, short, or neutral. The cross-market signal comes from an external indicator comparing the opening-bar movements of several configurable currency pairs, with configurable positive or inverse relationships. The sentiment signal comes from an external sentiment indicator: its buy output signals long and its sell output signals short; if both are nonzero, short takes precedence. The indicator implementations are absent, so their outputs cannot be reconstructed from the supplied code alone.

Other optional entry signals use the traded instrument: a rising completed-bar RSI below an upper threshold signals long, and a falling RSI above a lower threshold signals short; a rising moving average above the current close signals long, and a falling moving average below the current close signals short. A separate external indicator can also supply a directional signal. Require at least one enabled signal and unanimity among all enabled signals. Count repeated unanimous observations, resetting the opposite count and optionally resetting both counts on a nonunanimous observation. Enter in the confirmed direction after the configured number of observations, subject to configured time, trend, pullback, position-count, and order-spacing restrictions. Enabling the temporary-trend entry filter prevents every entry because that filter always returns a blocking result. If an enabled directional exit signal currently opposes a proposed entry, reject the entry.

For exits, optionally close a position when the sentiment signal or another enabled directional signal opposes it. The cross-market exit instead closes a long when the external exit output equals the external movement sum plus one, and closes a short when it equals that sum minus one. These exits may be gated by configurable additional trend confirmation: aggregate the enabled trend filters and directional signals, require unanimity for a directional trend, and use the configured policy for matching, opposing, or absent trends. With sentiment included in that confirmation, its external direction output can also prohibit changing a position. The same confirmation can gate timed exits and changes to stops or profit targets. The calculations inside the external indicators are unavailable.
