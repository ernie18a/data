On each eligible evaluation, combine the enabled directional signals by summing their votes: buy when every enabled signal votes up, sell when every enabled signal votes down, and otherwise do not enter. Require the same direction for the configured number of consecutive evaluations; when reset-on-interruption is enabled, clear the count on a neutral or incomplete consensus. Signals are: higher-timeframe RSI rising while below its upper bound votes up, and falling while above its lower bound votes down; a higher-timeframe moving average rising with current price below it votes up, while falling with current price above it votes down; cross-currency open-bar correlation and Quantum indicators supply their directional votes; sentiment supplies an up or down vote when its corresponding buy or sell output is nonzero. Optional entry filters can block trading by time, restrict entries to a detected main or temporary trend, or defer entry until a specified price peak is reached. Apply configured trade-count and per-direction limits before entry.

For open positions, enabled countertrend exit signals close a long when they indicate down and a short when they indicate up, subject to trend-confirmation filters that can protect positions aligned with the detected trend. The correlation exit closes when its exit reading moves one step beyond the current aggregate movement in the adverse direction. Other optional exits close after a holding-time limit, at a specified Friday time, or after enough recrosses of the position’s midpoint and a subsequent retracement. Trend-management rules may extend a take-profit in the favorable trend, reduce it when a countertrend is detected, or move the stop as price advances; a detected adverse main trend can activate a trailing stop. A sufficiently strong short-term tick trend can close an opposing position when its stop is near.
