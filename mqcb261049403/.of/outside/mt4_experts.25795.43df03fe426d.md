On the hourly timeframe, go long when the previous completed bar’s GBP/USD MACD and USD/CHF MACD are both positive, GBP/USD MACD is below USD/CHF MACD, and on the latest completed bar GBP/USD MACD is above USD/CHF MACD. Go short when both prior values are negative, GBP/USD MACD is above USD/CHF MACD, and on the latest completed bar GBP/USD MACD is below USD/CHF MACD. Open at most one position at a time. There are no signal-based exit rules.
