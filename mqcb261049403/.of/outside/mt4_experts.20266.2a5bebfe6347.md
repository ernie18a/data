When the optional percentage profit target is enabled, compare the running sum of open market-order profit on the traded instrument with a configured percentage of account balance. Scan open orders from the position equal to the order count down to position one, skipping unsuccessful selections; after each selection, close all market orders and delete all pending orders on that instrument if the running sum reaches the target.

In versions II and III, on every tick, if a historical order for the traded instrument and strategy identifier closed within a configured recent interval, or the traded instrument's current bid or ask exactly equals that historical order's stop-loss or take-profit, close every open market position across the account, including positions in other instruments. In version I, when its recovery routine runs, close any open market position whose take-profit exactly equals the traded instrument's current ask or whose stop-loss exactly equals its current bid, regardless of that position's instrument.
