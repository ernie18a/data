Evaluate once per new bar, and open trades only when there is no existing trade in either direction on the instrument. For a long entry, require the current close to be at least the minimum range above the high of the anchor bar immediately before the lookback window; every bar in the window must have a low at or above the current close, and none may have a high exceeding the anchor high by the quality-adjusted fraction of the minimum range. Also require the previous completed bar’s RSI to be below the oversold threshold. For a short entry, require the current close to be at least the minimum range below the anchor bar’s low; every bar in the window must have a high at or below the current close, and none may have a low below the anchor low by the quality-adjusted fraction of the minimum range. Also require the previous completed bar’s RSI to be above the overbought threshold. Set a fixed-distance stop and a profit target whose distance is a configurable percentage of the stop distance; close at either level. Close trades after their configured one-day lifetime as well.
