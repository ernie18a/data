At the start of each trading-timeframe candle, while the instrument is available and its broker session is open, calculate the strategy-wide daily result across all instruments and managed timeframes. Sum realized profit, swap, and commission on managed orders closed since the traded instrument's current daily bar began, and add the same components for all open managed positions. Divide by account balance plus credit minus the realized result to obtain the daily return; skip the limit check if that denominator is nonpositive. If an enabled profit limit is reached or exceeded, or an enabled loss limit is reached or exceeded by the negative daily return, cancel the traded instrument's pending orders and close its positions for the current timeframe. Skip orders or positions within the broker's freeze zone.
