This is an exit-only, time-based rule that never opens positions. On every tick, compare the current server time with a fixed deadline datetime set in advance. If the server time is later than the deadline and any positions are open on the account, exit all of them. Close long positions by selling their full volume at the bid. Close short positions by buying their full volume at the ask. Allow a small maximum price slippage. Repeat on each subsequent tick until no positions remain. If the deadline has not passed, or there are no open positions, do nothing.
