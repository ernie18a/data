For each of EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/CHF, and USD/JPY, compare a faster smoothed moving average with a slower one. Sum the four signed differences. When the sum is positive, open a long position on a pair only if its own faster average is above its slower average; when the sum is negative, open a short only if its own faster average is below its slower average. Open at most one market position per pair. Close all open positions when account equity reaches either a configured gain or loss threshold relative to the starting balance.
