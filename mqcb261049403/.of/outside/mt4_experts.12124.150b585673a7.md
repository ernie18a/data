On initialization, close profitable existing strategy orders on the traded instrument; leave losing orders open. On each tick, process trades only when free margin exceeds its limit. Block new entries if a high-impact calendar event whose country code appears in the instrument symbol is within the configured time window before or after the current time. Also require the total number of strategy orders to be below its limit, the absolute difference between the current minute-of-hour and the last entry minute-of-hour to meet the minimum pause, volatility to meet its minimum, and the current hour to be within the trading session. The spread check uses an unassigned value initialized to zero.

For a short setup, record the current minute-of-hour when the slow moving average is falling, both stochastic lines are above the upper threshold, the main line crosses strictly below the signal line, and the normalized distance from bid to the slow average exceeds its threshold. Enter short when a recorded setup exists within the timeout measured by absolute minute-of-hour difference, the fast moving average's normalized change is sufficiently negative, the distance condition still holds, the stochastic signal remains above the upper threshold, and the same-direction order count is below its limit. For a long setup, use a rising slow average, both stochastic lines below the lower threshold, a strict upward main-line crossing, and normalized distance below its threshold; enter when the corresponding setup is within the timeout, the fast average's change is sufficiently positive, the distance condition still holds, the signal remains below the lower threshold, and the same-direction count is below its limit. Evaluate the short and long paths in that order, and clear a setup when attempting its entry.

Place a fixed-distance stop loss and a take profit on each entry; choose the take-profit distance according to whether entry-time volatility is below the configured threshold. The additional active profit-close conditions require volatility to be simultaneously above and below that threshold, so they never trigger.
