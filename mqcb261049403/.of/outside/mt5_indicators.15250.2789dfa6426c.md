On a configurable timeframe, calculate the mean high-minus-low range over a configurable number of bars, including the current bar. Initialize the trend as neutral and both the tracked price and trailing level to the first processed bar’s close. For each bar in chronological order: when the trend is neutral or upward, raise the tracked price to the greater of itself and the bar’s high, then raise the trailing level to the greater of itself and tracked price multiplied by one minus the distance factor. If the bar’s high is below that level, set the tracked price to the high, reset the level to that price multiplied by one plus the distance factor, and switch to a downward trend. When the trend is downward, lower the tracked price to the lesser of itself and the bar’s low, then lower the trailing level to the lesser of itself and tracked price multiplied by one plus the distance factor. If the bar’s low is above that level, set the tracked price to the low, reset the level to that price multiplied by one minus the distance factor, and switch to an upward trend.
