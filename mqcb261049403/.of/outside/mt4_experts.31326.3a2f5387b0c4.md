Use the selected economic calendar to identify enabled low-, medium-, high-impact and nonfarm payroll events. Optionally restrict events to either currency in the traded symbol. Apply the configured time-zone offset and each event category’s before-and-after window. If windows overlap, use the highest-priority active category: nonfarm payrolls, high, medium, then low. During an active window, block all new trades and other trade management; close all positions for this strategy on the instrument if closing is enabled for that category. The Investing.com feed filters disabled impact categories before identifying nonfarm payroll events.

Outside news windows, when trading is allowed and this strategy has more than one open position on the instrument, optionally close all those positions if total account floating profit divided by account equity exceeds the configured profit percentage or falls below the negative loss percentage. Account floating profit includes positions outside the traded instrument.
