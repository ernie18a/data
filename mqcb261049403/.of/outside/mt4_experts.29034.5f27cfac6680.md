Form a ring for every non-skipped combination of three currencies in the configured order: A/B, A/C and B/C. One side buys A/B, sells A/C and buys B/C; the opposite side reverses all three trades. In normal operation, open any missing initial legs for each enabled side, subject to market session, combined three-pair spread, order-limit and margin checks. Maintain each ring independently.

For each side, calculate net floating profit including swap and commission, total lots and order count per leg, and the number of complete three-leg groups. Calculate profit, loss and additional-entry thresholds by summing configured per-lot amounts across the legs; use tick-value weighting unless fair-lot sizing is enabled, and subtract estimated spread costs and absolute commission plus swap. For the loss threshold use average lots per order on each leg. For an additional-entry threshold, multiply by a progression based on the number of groups: linear, cumulative arithmetic, or cumulative powers of two.

In manual grid mode, add the same three-leg side when its aggregate profit falls to or below its additional-entry threshold, the configured waiting time and group limit permit it, and its leg counts are consistent. In basket-profit mode, add only when that side has at least as many orders as the opposite side. In automatic grid mode with both sides enabled, when one side has been closed and the other remains, open a new group on the closed side, subject to the group limit.

For profit exits, choose the configured policy: close an entire side when its positive aggregate profit reaches its side threshold; close both sides when their positive combined profit reaches the larger side threshold; or close each leg independently when its profit reaches its leg threshold. Whole-side and basket triggers require the configured consecutive evaluations. In automatic grid mode, also close a profitable side with no more orders than its opposite when the opposite side falls to its additional-entry threshold, after the configured delay.

If loss exits are enabled, close an entire losing side when its aggregate profit reaches its loss threshold after the configured delay, or close the oldest order on each leg only when that side has more orders than the opposite side. Continue an initiated whole-side or basket close until its orders are gone. If leg counts become inconsistent, open missing legs to match the largest count, or close oldest excess legs to match the smallest count when the order limit prevents opening. Standby suspends trading; close-and-stop prevents new initial entries while managing existing positions; immediate-close mode closes all ring orders.
