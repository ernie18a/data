For each configured triple of currencies, trade its three cross-pairs in the fixed sequence A/B, A/C, B/C. The initial “plus” basket buys A/B, sells A/C, and buys B/C; the “minus” basket takes the opposite positions. Open the enabled basket sides when empty, without a directional price signal. If grid trading is enabled, add another full three-pair basket on a side when its combined floating P&L reaches the configured adverse-loss threshold, subject to the configured time interval and group limits; apply the selected lot and step progression. In automatic recovery mode, when one side closes, open the opposite side if the other side remains open. Close positions according to the selected policy: close a whole side when its combined P&L reaches its configured profit target; optionally close a whole losing side or its oldest tickets when the configured loss threshold is reached; close both sides when their combined P&L reaches the basket target; or close each pair and side independently at its profit target. In automatic recovery mode, also close a profitable side with fewer orders when the other side’s loss reaches its next-grid threshold. A stop-and-close operation exits all positions. Apply configured spread, session, order-count, and market-availability gates to new entries.
