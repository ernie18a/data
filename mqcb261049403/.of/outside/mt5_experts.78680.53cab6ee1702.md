At each configured server-time daily reset, set the daily baseline to account balance and release any lock whose expiry has passed. If no baseline is saved for the current day, reconstruct it as current balance minus the net profit, swap, commission, and fees from closed buy and sell deals since the reset; exclude non-trading deals. Compare the baseline with current account equity. When the decline reaches the configured daily loss limit, lock trading and repeatedly close all open positions, optionally filtering by magic number, on each processing pass. Keep closing positions while locked, including newly opened ones, until the lock expires at the next daily reset. A manual unlock clears the lock and sets the baseline to current equity.
