On each new bar, compute a strength score for each of EUR, GBP, AUD, NZD, USD, CAD, CHF, and JPY from their seven major currency crosses. For each cross, measure price change over a lookback (the variants use open versus prior close, close-to-close rate of change, RSI, or the ratio of current to older linear-weighted moving averages); add the measure to the base currency and subtract it from the quote currency, averaging where applicable. Rank the scores. Consider a long when the chart pair’s base currency is strongest and its quote currency weakest; consider a short when the base is weakest and quote strongest. The implemented pair checks cover EUR crosses, GBP crosses, selected AUD crosses, and USD crosses; the AUD/USD check mistakenly tests GBP/USD, and other major crosses are not covered. An entry also requires the chart’s fast weighted moving average to be above the slow one for a long or below it for a short, a sufficiently large distance between the current quote and a recent close, and the respective candle-range condition. In the RSI/LWMA variant, a long additionally requires momentum below its neutral level and sufficient deviation from neutral in one of the recent momentum readings. Limit entries by the configured open-trade cap. Close all matching positions when their combined loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high. The LWMA variant also closes a long when positive MACD crosses below its signal line above a minimum magnitude, and closes a short on the symmetric negative-side cross.
