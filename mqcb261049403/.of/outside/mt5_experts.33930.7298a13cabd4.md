For each open position within the selected symbol scope, measure unrealized movement in points from its entry price, using the bid to evaluate longs and the ask to evaluate shorts. In server-stop mode, set configured stop-loss and take-profit levels relative to entry, adjusted for the broker’s minimum stop distance, but only when the corresponding levels are missing. In hidden-stop mode, close a long when its gain reaches the configured take-profit distance or its loss reaches the configured stop-loss distance; apply the inverse conditions to a short. When profit locking is enabled and movement reaches its trigger, move the stop to a configured amount of profit if the current stop has not already protected profit. Once beyond the lock trigger plus the trailing distance, trail the stop; alternatively, when profit locking is disabled, trail whenever movement exceeds the trailing distance. Trailing can keep a fixed distance from market, keep a reduced distance based on the step, or advance the stop incrementally; never loosen a stop. In strategy-test mode, if there are no qualifying open positions, attempt a small long in one major currency pair and a small short in another, subject to available margin and valid volume.
