On each completed bar of the selected signal timeframe, calculate a fixed weighted filter of the selected OHLC-derived price, smooth it with a lag-reducing moving average, and round the result to the instrument’s price precision. Assign an upward state when the rounded value rises and a downward state when it falls; when unchanged, retain the prior state. On a transition into the upward state, open a long if enabled and close any short. On a transition into the downward state, open a short if enabled and close any long. Restrict new entries to the configured daily time window when time filtering is enabled; close all positions in the instrument outside that window. Signal-based exits apply regardless of the entry window.
