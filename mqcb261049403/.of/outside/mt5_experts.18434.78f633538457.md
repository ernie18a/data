On each completed bar of the configured timeframe, derive a candle price: use the midpoint of close and low for a down candle, the midpoint of close and high for an up candle, and close for a doji. Smooth this series with the configured moving-average method and settings. When the smoothed value rises from the prior bar, open long if enabled and close any short if enabled; when it falls, open short if enabled and close any long if enabled. Trade entries are allowed only inside the configured time window. If time-window enforcement is enabled, close all positions in the traded instrument outside that window.
