For each aligned bar, compare the stochastic oscillator’s main line with its signal line for the traded instrument and two related instruments: a positive difference is bullish, a negative difference bearish, and zero neutral. If the traded instrument and one related instrument agree, classify the other related instrument in that direction when it is neutral; if it opposes them, do so only when the agreeing related instrument’s absolute difference is larger than the opposing instrument’s. If both related instruments agree while the traded instrument is neutral, classify the traded instrument in their direction. Signal long when all three are bullish and short when all three are bearish. A bullish reading on the traded instrument marks an exit from a short; a bearish reading marks an exit from a long.
