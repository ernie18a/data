On each completed signal-timeframe bar, compare the close with the prior channel state: the upper boundary is the highest high in a fixed lookback window offset into older bars, and the lower boundary is the lowest low in its corresponding window. Open long when the close is above the upper boundary and the preceding bar was not; close any short on that bullish signal. Open short when the close is below the lower boundary and the preceding bar was not; close any long on that bearish signal. Open only when no position is already open for the instrument. Close any position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit.
