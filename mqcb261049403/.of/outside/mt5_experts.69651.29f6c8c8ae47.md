Trade only when platform and instrument allow trading, account equity and margin are sufficient, spreads and volatility are within permitted bounds, market activity is adequate, and the time/volatility context passes the session filter. Detect a bullish or bearish impulse on a completed candle when its volume exceeds an adaptive multiple of recent average volume, its body and close location show directional strength, its range expands versus the prior candle, and price is on the matching side of a moving average whose slope agrees with the direction. Adapt volume and body-strength thresholds to session activity and volatility. Track the impulse while it remains valid and within its time window. Enter in the impulse direction on any of: a break of the tracked impulse high or low; a retracement into the specified middle portion of the tracked range accompanied by a directionally confirming candle or break of the prior candle's extreme; or a directional break of the prior candle's extreme with current volume above a fraction of recent average volume. For a long, place a volatility-scaled stop below entry and target above entry; reverse these for a short. As price advances, optionally move the stop to entry after sufficient progress toward the target and/or trail it at a volatility-scaled distance, only tightening the stop and keeping it beyond entry. Exit when the stop or target is reached.
