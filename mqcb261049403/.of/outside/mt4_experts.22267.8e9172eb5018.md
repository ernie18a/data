On each new bar, derive standard Fibonacci retracement levels from the previous candle on a higher timeframe selected for the chart timeframe. Consider a long when a close among the prior three bars is above a level and the immediately preceding bar’s low reaches or crosses it; also require the preceding two bars’ low/high structure to qualify, price to have moved sufficiently far from the close two bars back, and a fast typical-price weighted moving average to be above a slower one. Consider a short when a close among the prior three bars is below a level and the preceding bar’s high reaches or crosses it; for the lowest level, the code instead checks whether the preceding bar closes at or above that level. Also require the specified low/high structure, sufficient price distance from the close two bars back, and the fast average below the slow average. Open trades with stop-loss and take-profit orders, subject to the configured maximum open-trade count and trade feasibility checks. Close the strategy’s basket when its combined floating profit, swap, and commission reaches a balance-relative profit target or loss limit. Other active exits: close the symbol’s trades when floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the tracked equity high; optionally close all symbol trades at a configured cash profit; and optionally trail the basket’s cash profit, closing after a pullback from its peak, or close a losing basket when its loss crosses the equity-risk threshold adjusted by a multiplier. Move individual stops to protect profit after a trade gains a configured distance. Standard stop-loss and take-profit orders also close trades when hit.
