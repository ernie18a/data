All price indicators use the traded instrument. Account free margin gates entries: require it to exceed a multiple of the base order size, and require sufficient margin after the proposed trade. Also require enough price history, a valid configured profit target, fewer open strategy trades than the limit, valid order levels, and permission to trade.

Compare fast and slow linear-weighted moving averages of typical price on a higher timeframe, using the current higher-timeframe bar. Map the chart timeframe to the higher timeframe as follows: minute to 15-minute, 5-minute to 30-minute, 15-minute to hourly, 30-minute to 4-hourly, hourly to daily, 4-hourly to weekly, and daily to monthly; otherwise use the current timeframe. Go long when the fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from the current bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured point distance. Go short when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from the current ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. In live trading additionally require the earlier channel-anchor open to be below the later anchor for a long, or above it for a short; the anchors are opens at the configured channel length minus one and minus thirty bars ago. Ignore this filter in testing. A qualifying long branch returns even if its live filter or order checks fail.

If enabled, exit all strategy positions on the instrument when their combined unrealized profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured percentage of the account equity reference. The reference is reset to current equity when no strategy positions are open; then it becomes the previous tick's equity if that exceeds the stored reference, otherwise current equity. Update the previous equity each time the reference is calculated. Entry checks can run again on the same tick after an exit.

If enabled, exit all orders on the instrument when the running sum of open market-order profits reaches a configured percentage of account balance. This calculation scans order positions from the total count down to one, excluding position zero, and checks the partial sum during the scan.

If the money-based trailing feature is enabled and the scanned net profit including commission and swap is negative and below its configured profit threshold, first require the strategy positions' combined unrealized loss to exceed a configured multiple of the equity-risk threshold. Then scan the same orders from the total count down to one, accumulating absolute net profits; remove all instrument orders once that accumulation reaches the configured money-loss amount.
