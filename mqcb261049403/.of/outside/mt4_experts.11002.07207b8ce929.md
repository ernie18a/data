For each open position that passes the enabled magic-number and symbol filters, and whose side is enabled, consider closing it only after any enabled minimum holding-time and scheduled-time gates are met. The scheduled-time gate passes when both the current hour and minute are at least the configured close hour and minute after applying the GMT offset. Close a long when its bid-based gain reaches the configured profit threshold, or, if loss closing is enabled, when its bid-based result reaches the configured loss threshold and it has been held longer than the minimum duration. Close a short when its ask-based gain reaches the configured profit threshold, or, if loss closing is enabled, when its ask-based result reaches the configured loss threshold. Thresholds are measured as price distance using the instrument’s point size.
