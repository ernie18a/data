On startup, open a short position. While positions are open, add positions in the same direction whenever price moves adversely from the latest grid position by the configured spacing, stopping at the configured order cap. Give the first three positions individual take-profit levels; once four or more positions are open, set a shared take-profit beyond the volume-weighted break-even price by the configured basket target. After a first position closes at take profit, reopen in its direction when the cumulative pip profit of the relevant first-position history is below its target and price has moved the configured offset beyond that prior take-profit. Close all managed positions if their combined floating loss exceeds the configured fraction of account balance.
