On every tick, if basket protection is enabled, sum profit, swap, and commission for this strategy's open positions in the traded instrument. If at least one exists and the sum reaches the configured profit percentage of account balance or falls to the negative loss percentage of account balance, close those positions and stop processing the tick.

If monetary trailing is enabled and at least one strategy position exists, calculate the traded instrument's net open profit, including commission and swap, over order-list positions from the total count down to index 1. If that total is negative and the strategy's gross open loss on the instrument exceeds a configured fraction of the account equity reference multiplied by a loss multiplier, scan the same order-list positions in descending order. For matching instrument orders, accumulate the absolute net profit of market positions; whenever the absolute total net profit is no greater than the absolute stored trailing value, replace that value with the absolute total net profit. If the running accumulation reaches the absolute difference between the stored trailing value and the configured monetary stop, close all market positions and delete pending orders on the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

Once per new chart bar, if equity protection is enabled and the strategy's gross open loss on the instrument exceeds a configured fraction of the account equity reference, close the strategy's positions on the instrument.

Update the equity reference only when a rule calls for it: if there are no strategy positions on the instrument, first set the reference to current account equity. Then, if the reference is below the previously recorded equity, set it to that previous equity; otherwise set it to current equity. Finally record current equity as the previous equity.
