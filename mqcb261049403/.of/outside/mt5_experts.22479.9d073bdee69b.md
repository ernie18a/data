At the configured daily start hour, anchor upper and lower thresholds to that bar’s open, offset equally above and below. On each completed bar, classify its close: above the upper threshold, below the lower threshold, or between them. Enter long when the bar moves into the above-upper state from any other state; close shorts whenever the close is above the upper threshold. Enter short when the bar moves into the below-lower state from any other state; close longs whenever the close is below the lower threshold.
