On each tick, operate only on daily or longer charts, when spread is below the allowed maximum and the last error is not insufficient funds. Consider closed orders with the strategy identifier across all instruments. If the most recent losing order closed after the most recent winning order, and no matching position is open on the current instrument, sell when that losing order was a buy and the current ask is sufficiently below today's open; buy when it was a sell and the current bid is sufficiently above today's open. Size the new position as the last recorded winning or losing order's size plus an equity-based increment. Separately, when flat, after the configured hour and at most once per chart bar, buy if the bid is sufficiently above today's open and current bar volume exceeds its threshold, or sell if the ask is sufficiently below today's open and volume exceeds its threshold. Mark the bar as processed even if no order is placed. After entries, close matching shorts if the bid is slightly above today's open, and close matching longs if the ask is slightly below it. Entry and exit distances for the loss-reversal and open-crossing rules use a digit-dependent integer multiplier, which becomes zero for instruments quoted with two or four decimal places. Positions also have a fixed-distance take profit and optional profit-triggered stop adjustments.
