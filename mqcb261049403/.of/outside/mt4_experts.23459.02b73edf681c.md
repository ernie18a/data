On each new bar, when no position from this strategy is open, inspect the most recently closed strategy trade. If it lost, open a position opposite to its direction, increasing its size according to the configured martingale rule. Otherwise, open long when DeMarker is above its midpoint, the low from two bars earlier is below the prior bar’s high, and the MACD main line is above its signal line. The coded short condition is unreachable, so no indicator-based short is opened. Attach price-based stop-loss and take-profit levels to new positions. Close the strategy’s basket when its profit reaches a balance-relative target, its loss reaches an emergency balance-relative limit, or its configured loss limit is reached while multiple positions are open. Also close positions when the equity-risk stop is exceeded, when the enabled money profit target is met, or when positive basket profit retreats by the configured trailing amount from its peak.
