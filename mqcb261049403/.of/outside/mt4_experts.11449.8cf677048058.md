When the current candle’s body exceeds a configured minimum, go long if it is bullish, price is above its moving average, and price is above the upper Keltner band; go short if it is bearish, price is below its moving average, and price is below the lower band. The moving-average confirmation may be disabled or require the close, entire candle body, or full candle range to lie on the trade’s side of the average. The Keltner bands are centered on a simple moving average of typical price and offset by the average high-low range. Enter only when trading is permitted, no trade or pending order is active, and average spread including commission is within its configured limit. On a newly closed trade, a qualifying signal may also place a stop entry in the signal direction, which is adjusted to remain a configured distance from market and canceled if its moving-average confirmation fails. For open positions, apply the configured initial stop loss and take profit when enabled; move the stop to breakeven with its configured offset after sufficient favorable movement, and trail the stop behind price by the configured distance. There is no separate signal-based exit.
