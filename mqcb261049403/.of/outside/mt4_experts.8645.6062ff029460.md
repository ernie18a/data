On each new bar, detect a local peak or trough in the traded instrument’s MACD main line over the three preceding completed bars. A peak signals a short; a trough signals a long. In training mode, enter in the signaled direction and, if configured, close open orders in the opposite direction. Record separate long and short training sets from all historical account orders of each type, including orders on other instruments. For each order, use the traded instrument’s five specified simple moving-average ratios from the bar preceding its opening and label the outcome profitable if its order profit is nonnegative. In classification mode, on either signal, optionally close all profitable open account orders, then compute the same ratios on the latest completed bar. Find the nearest historical vectors by Euclidean distance in the training set for the signaled direction; the fraction labeled profitable is the score. Enter in the signaled direction if the score meets its entry threshold. Independently, if inversion is enabled and the score is at or below its inverse threshold, enter in the opposite direction. Apply configured stop-loss and take-profit levels to each entry.
