Evaluate once at the first tick of each new chart bar. Account-level data affects entries and exits.

Enter only if there is sufficient free margin, the proposed trade passes broker margin and stop-level checks, trading is allowed, the minimum history and configured take-profit requirements are met, and the number of this strategy's open positions on the instrument is below the limit. Go long when the absolute distance between bid and the close two bars ago exceeds the configured threshold, the current fast linear-weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one, and the current monthly RSI exceeds its previous monthly value. Go short when the absolute distance between ask and the previous close exceeds the threshold, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, and current monthly RSI is below its previous value. In live trading additionally require the newest fractal high to exceed the third newest for a long, or be below it for a short; omit this filter in testing. A qualifying long branch takes precedence and prevents a short attempt even if the long order is rejected. Size positions from the configured base and an exponent of the current position count; after more than one consecutive historical loss on this instrument, optionally replace that size with a fraction of account free margin, then conform it to broker volume limits.

If enabled, close this strategy's positions on the instrument when their combined floating profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds the configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference when checked: reset it to current equity if no strategy positions exist; then, if the reference is below the previous observed equity, set it to that previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity; finally record current equity as the previous equity. Trading may resume on the same bar after a closure.

If enabled, close all positions and delete pending orders on the instrument when the running sum of floating profit reaches the configured percentage of account balance. This calculation iterates open orders from the highest index down to index one, excludes index zero, and checks the threshold after each selected order.

If monetary trailing is enabled, the strategy's combined floating loss exceeds the equity-reference risk threshold multiplied by the configured loss multiplier, and the separately calculated instrument-wide net profit including commission and swap is negative and below the configured monetary profit threshold, iterate instrument orders from the highest index down to index one. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position; close all instrument positions and delete its pending orders as soon as this accumulated loss reaches the configured monetary stop amount. The instrument-wide net-profit calculation also excludes order index zero.
