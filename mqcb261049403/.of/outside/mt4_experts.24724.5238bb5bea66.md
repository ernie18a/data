On each new chart bar, open a long if the current bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, the chart-timeframe fast linear weighted moving average of typical price is above the slow one, and monthly RSI has risen since its prior bar. In live trading, also require the drawn trend line to rise; in testing, ignore this filter. Open a short if the current ask is sufficiently far from the prior bar’s close, the fast average is below the slow one, the prior low is below the high two bars ago, and monthly RSI has fallen; in live trading, also require the trend line to fall. Apply these entry rules only when below the configured position limit and other order and margin checks pass. Attach a stop loss and take profit to each entry. Close positions when their stop or target is reached; optionally close all when the symbol’s combined profit reaches a configured money or balance-percentage target, when profitable basket gains retrace by a configured money amount, or when combined losses exceed a configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high. Move stops to lock in gains after a position advances sufficiently, and trail stops using recent candle extremes or a price-distance rule, as configured.
