On each tick, form a directional signal when the current candle’s absolute open-to-price body exceeds a configured minimum and price is on the same side of a moving average as the candle direction: signal long for a rising candle above the average, and short for a falling candle below it. If the Keltner filter is enabled, require price above the upper channel for a long or below the lower channel for a short; the channel uses a simple average of typical price as its center and the average high-low range as its width. If the tick-based RSI filter is enabled, additionally require its smoothed ratio of upward to downward tick movement to be above its midpoint for a long or below it for a short, with price on the corresponding side of the moving average and the current candle moving in that direction. Otherwise give no signal. Open a market position only when there are no existing strategy orders and average spread including commission is within its configured limit. A closed strategy position can also trigger a pending stop order in the current signal direction, subject to any configured candle-versus-average entry filter; pending stops follow price and are removed when that filter fails. Manage open positions with configured stop loss, take profit, break-even, and trailing-stop rules.
