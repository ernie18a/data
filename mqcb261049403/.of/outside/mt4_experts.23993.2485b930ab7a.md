On every tick, if basket protection is enabled, sum net profit including swap and commission for open market trades on the current instrument with the strategy identifier. Close those trades and stop processing the tick if their profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance, their loss reaches a separate hard-stop percentage of balance, or their loss reaches a configured percentage of balance while at least a specified number of trades are open.

On each new bar, if the equity stop is enabled, close the strategy's market trades on the current instrument when their combined profit excluding swap and commission is negative and its magnitude exceeds a configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference by resetting it to current equity when no strategy trades are open, then using previous equity if the stored reference is below previous equity, otherwise current equity; store current equity as previous equity.

If money trailing is enabled and the current instrument's net open-market profit is negative and below the configured money-profit threshold, apply an additional loss exit only when the strategy's loss excluding swap and commission exceeds the equity-stop threshold multiplied by a configured factor. Iterate the current instrument's orders backward starting at the position equal to the total order count and excluding position zero. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market order; update the stored trailing value to the absolute total net loss when that loss is no greater than the stored value. Close all current-instrument market orders and delete its pending orders when the accumulated absolute amounts reach the absolute difference between the stored value and the configured money stop.
