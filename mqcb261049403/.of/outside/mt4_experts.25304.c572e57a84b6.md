Evaluate once per new chart bar. Account-wide balance, equity and free margin affect decisions on the traded instrument.

For entry, require enough history, fewer than the configured maximum of this strategy's open positions, sufficient account free margin, acceptable projected margin relative to the broker's stop-out level, valid stops and permission to trade. Use the current fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on the configured higher timeframe. Go long when the fast average is above the slow average, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured threshold. Go short when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds that threshold. In live trading, additionally require the latest fractal high to exceed the preceding fractal high and the latest fractal low to be below the preceding fractal low. For a long, also require the latest fractal high to exceed the older fractal high used by the longer line and the timestamp of the latest fractal low to exceed the older low's price; reverse both of these last comparisons for a short. In testing, omit the fractal filters. Set each entry's configured price stop and target.

When enabled, close this strategy's positions on the instrument if their combined floating profit is negative and its magnitude exceeds the configured fraction of the account equity reference. That reference is updated from current and previous account equity as in the source, rather than being a conventional running peak. Separately, when enabled, close all orders on the instrument if the selected positions' accumulated profit reaches a configured fraction of account balance. The source's accumulation loop skips the first order position and checks the threshold during accumulation.

When money trailing is enabled, its loss branch can also close all instrument orders: require negative combined net profit below the configured money target and this strategy's floating loss to exceed the equity-based threshold multiplied by the loss factor; then close if the accumulated absolute net losses reach the tracked money threshold. Its accumulation likewise skips the first order position.
