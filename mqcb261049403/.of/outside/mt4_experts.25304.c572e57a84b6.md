On each new bar, consider entries only while the open-trade limit and account margin allow. Use a higher-timeframe comparison of a fast and slow linear weighted moving average of typical price as the trend filter. For a long, require the fast average above the slow average, the recent candle low below the preceding candle high, and the current bid sufficiently far from the close two bars earlier. For a short, require the fast average below the slow average, the preceding candle low below the candle high two bars earlier, and the current ask sufficiently far from the preceding close. In live trading, additionally require the chart’s fractal-based trend lines to pass the corresponding directional slope checks; in testing, omit this line filter. Attach a protective stop and profit target to each entry. Close the symbol’s positions when the aggregate loss exceeds the configured equity-risk threshold; optional account-currency or balance-percentage profit targets and aggregate profit/loss trailing rules can also close them. Move stops to breakeven after sufficient favorable movement, and trail stops by recent candle extremes or by price after the chosen activation distance.
