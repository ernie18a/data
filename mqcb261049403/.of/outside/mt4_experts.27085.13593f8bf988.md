When there are no open trades on the current instrument, open a market long. Track outcomes in configurable sets of trades: after a set consisting entirely of losses, increase the next lot size by a configurable scale factor; after a set consisting entirely of wins, reset it to the baseline lot size; after any mixed set, keep the lot size unchanged. Set the baseline lot size from account equity divided by a configurable sizing divisor. Attach the configured stop-loss and take-profit distances. In the equity-control variant, when account balance exceeds the stored equity reference plus a configurable increment, close open trades, reset the set counters, recalculate the baseline lot size from equity, and advance the reference to equity plus the increment. No short entries.
