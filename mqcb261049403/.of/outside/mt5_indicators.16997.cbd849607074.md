For each instrument in a configured portfolio, synchronize a rolling window of bar opening prices across all instruments, filling missing bars with the next available value. Transform each series either to mean-centered log prices or to percentage change from its fixed initial price. Run PCA on the resulting matrix and select the configured principal component. Align its overall sign with the previously selected vector: invert it if no corresponding nonzero coefficients share a sign, or if the sum of absolute coefficient differences from the previous vector exceeds the sum of absolute coefficient sums. Otherwise retain its sign. Recalculate the vector as the window advances until the configured freeze point, then reuse it. For each instrument, go long when its selected coefficient is positive and short when it is negative. No action for a zero coefficient or exit rule is specified.
