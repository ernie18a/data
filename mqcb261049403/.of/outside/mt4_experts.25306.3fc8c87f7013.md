On every tick, if basket protection is enabled, sum profit, swap, and commission for this strategy's open positions on the traded instrument. If the sum reaches the configured profit or loss percentage of account balance, close those positions and skip the rest of the tick.

If money trailing is enabled and the strategy has open positions, sum net profit across all positions on the instrument. While that sum is negative and below the configured money target, if the strategy's gross instrument loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-risk threshold, scan open orders backward, excluding the first order. Accumulate the absolute net profit of instrument positions, update the stored trailing value when the absolute total loss is no greater than that value, and close all instrument orders when the accumulated amount reaches the absolute difference between the stored value and the configured money stop.

On each new bar, if equity protection is enabled, close the strategy's instrument positions when their combined gross profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the equity reference. The reference is updated when checked: reset it to current account equity if there are no strategy positions; then set it to previous account equity if that is higher than the reference, otherwise set it to current equity. Store current equity as previous equity. Continue evaluating entries after attempting closure.

Entries require enough history, a valid configured profit target, free margin above a configured minimum, fewer than the maximum strategy positions, sufficient post-trade margin under the broker's stop-out rules, valid stop and target distances, and trading permission. On a new bar, go long when the current bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, the fast higher-timeframe linearly weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one, and the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high. Otherwise, go short when the bid is sufficiently far from the previous close, the fast average is below the slow one, and the previous low is below the high two bars ago. In live trading, both entries additionally require that, separately for upper and lower fractals, the price of the third most recent fractal minus the most recent fractal is no greater than a configured distance. Testing omits this filter. Attach configured price-based stop loss and take profit.
