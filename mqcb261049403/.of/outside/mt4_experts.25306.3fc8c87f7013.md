On each new bar, open a long if the faster higher-timeframe weighted moving average is above the slower one, the current price is sufficiently far from the close two bars earlier, and the low two bars ago is below the prior bar’s high. Open a short if the faster average is below the slower one, price is sufficiently far from the prior bar’s close, and the prior bar’s low is below the high two bars ago. In live trading, require both chart trend lines drawn through recent fractal highs and lows to meet the configured slope filter; omit this filter in testing. Limit entries by the maximum open-trade count. Close the symbol’s basket when its profit reaches the configured money target, when the configured basket profit trail retraces by its money stop, when losses exceed the equity-risk threshold, or when basket profit reaches the configured percentage gain or loss limit. Manage individual positions with their stop-loss and take-profit orders, move stops beyond entry after the configured favorable move, and trail stops according to the configured price or candle rule.
