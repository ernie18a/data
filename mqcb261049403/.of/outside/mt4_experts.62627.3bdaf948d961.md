When automatic trading is enabled and sentiment data is available, evaluate once on each new chart candle, provided there are no open or pending orders anywhere in the account. Convert the instrument's retail sentiment ratios to integers. Go long if the retail short ratio is at or above a configured threshold and the current candle's close-based short-period simple moving average is above the long-period simple moving average. Go short if the retail long ratio is at or above the threshold and the short-period average is below the long-period average. Otherwise take no action. No exit rule is specified.
