Evaluate once per new bar. Use configurable thresholds and periods throughout.

For entries, require enough chart history, a valid configured take-profit distance, sufficient account free margin, fewer than the allowed number of open strategy positions on the instrument, and broker approval of volume, margin, stops and trading. Scan recent fractals from oldest to newest: accept if bid or ask exactly equals an upper or lower fractal; stop scanning and reject if a candle has both fractals before a match is found. Also require the absolute body of the second completed candle to be smaller than that of the third, and the third to be larger than the fourth. Go long if the current fast linear-weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one, the second completed candle's low is below the latest completed candle's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the second completed close exceeds the configured distance. Otherwise go short if the fast average is below the slow one, the latest completed low is below the second completed high, and the absolute distance from ask to the latest completed close exceeds that distance. A qualifying long setup prevents a short attempt even if its order fails.

When enabled, close all strategy positions on the instrument if their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the tracked account equity. Update tracked equity when this check is reached: reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then use previous equity if tracked equity is below previous equity, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity as previous equity.

When enabled, close all instrument orders if accumulated floating profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance. Accumulate in descending order-position order, starting at the total order count and ending at position one, and check the threshold after each iteration.

When money trailing is enabled and strategy positions exist, sum instrument market-order profit including commission and swap over that same position range. If the sum is negative and below the configured money-profit threshold, and strategy floating loss exceeds a configured multiple of the equity-risk threshold, accumulate the absolute net profit of each instrument market order in that range. At each instrument order, replace the saved trailing value with the absolute total loss if it is no greater than the absolute saved value; close all instrument orders when the accumulated absolute profits reach the absolute difference between that saved value and the configured money-loss distance. Reset the saved value when no strategy positions remain.
