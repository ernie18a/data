For each traded pair, calculate its midpoint and a synthetic midpoint from the related currency pairs. Compare the synthetic midpoint with the traded pair’s midpoint, and compare the synthetic quote time (the earlier time of its component quotes) with the traded pair’s quote time. When the synthetic price is above the traded price by more than a configurable deviation and the synthetic quote time is later, buy the traded pair if it has no open position. When the synthetic price is below by more than that deviation and the synthetic quote time is earlier, sell if it has no open position. On either signal, close an opposite-direction position only if its net profit is positive. Open trades only when free margin is sufficient.
