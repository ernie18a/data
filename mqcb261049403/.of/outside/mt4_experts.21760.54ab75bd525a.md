Evaluate entries once per new chart candle. If the number of open trades is below the configured limit, consider a long when price is sufficiently far from the close two candles ago; at least one of the closes four, three, or two candles ago is at or below the latest detected fractal level and the previous candle closes above it; the Renko-style movement over the configured lookback reaches the box-size threshold upward; and the low two candles ago is below the previous candle’s high. Consider a short under the mirrored conditions: price is sufficiently far from the close two candles ago; at least one of those three earlier closes is at or above the latest fractal level and the previous candle closes below it; Renko-style movement reaches the threshold downward. Open each position with its configured stop loss and take profit. Close positions when their stop loss or take profit is reached; optionally move the stop to lock in profit after a favorable move, trail it after a larger favorable move, close all positions when their combined loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s equity peak, or close all when combined profit reaches a configured money or balance-percentage target. A separate money-based trailing rule can close the group after profit falls by a configured amount from its peak.
