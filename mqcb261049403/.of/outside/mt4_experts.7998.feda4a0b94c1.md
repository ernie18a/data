Across the configured instruments, track the strategy's aggregate point profit for the current day: add realized price changes on closed trades and unrealized price changes on open trades, using bid for longs and ask for shorts. Also count today's closed trades whose close price equals their take-profit price. If either aggregate reaches its configured threshold, close all open long and short positions, delete all pending orders, and disable new trading for all configured instruments until the daily reset. The aggregate includes the traded instrument and the other configured instruments.
