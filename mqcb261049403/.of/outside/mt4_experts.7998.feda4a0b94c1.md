For each traded instrument, calculate the prior day’s high, low, and close, then derive a central pivot, range-based support and resistance levels, and configurable fractional levels between them. During the configured trading window, place pending orders when price lies between adjacent pivot levels: in most bands, place orders on both sides of fractional levels to trade a move back across the band, with targets at the paired fractional level or band boundary. In the first-to-second resistance and support bands, allow the configured direction scheme to select buys, sells, or both. If price moves beyond the outer resistance or support, place a breakout stop order beyond that level, targeting a further extension and using the next inner level as a stop; after placing this outer-band order, stop placing further orders for that instrument until the daily reset. Choose a stop order when the entry lies beyond the current market in the order’s direction, otherwise choose a limit order. Avoid duplicating an active order for the same strategy level. Close positions at their stop or target. Track daily realized and unrealized profit in points and winning trades: when an instrument reaches its daily profit or win-count limit, close its positions, remove its pending orders, and stop trading it for the day; when the combined instruments reach an overall daily profit or win-count limit, close all positions, remove pending orders, and stop trading for the day. At the end of the order-placement window, remove pending orders; at the configured forced-close time, close all positions. Reset daily trading restrictions at the next start of the trading window.
