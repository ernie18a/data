On each tick, if enabled, sum net profit including swap and commission for this strategy’s open positions in the traded instrument. If it reaches a configured percentage of account balance in profit or loss, close those positions and skip the rest of the tick. On each new bar, if enabled, close the strategy’s positions when their combined unrealized price profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the account-equity reference. Update that reference when evaluated: reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then use the previous observed equity if it exceeds the reference, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity as the previous observation. Entry may still be evaluated after this exit. If money trailing is enabled, and the same strategy profit is negative and exceeds a separately multiplied equity-based loss threshold, scan the instrument’s open orders from the highest index down to index 1, accumulating absolute net profit per market order. Close all orders in the instrument when the accumulation reaches the absolute difference between the stored trailing value and the configured money stop; while scanning, replace the stored value with the absolute total net profit if that total is no greater than the stored value. Reset the stored value when there are no strategy positions. Entry is evaluated once per new bar only if account free margin exceeds a configured multiple of the base position size, there is sufficient margin for the order, and the strategy position limit is not reached. For each of RSI and CCI, scan from the configured oldest bar toward the current bar and use the first mismatch: bullish if the last completed bar’s indicator is higher while its high is lower; bearish if the indicator is lower while its high is higher. Go long if either indicator is bullish, the current fast typical-price weighted moving average exceeds the slow one, the current monthly close-price RSI exceeds its previous value, the previous bar’s high exceeds the high of two bars ago, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured threshold. Go short under the opposite indicator, moving-average and monthly-RSI conditions, provided the previous bar’s low is below the high two bars ago and the corresponding ask-to-close distance exceeds the threshold. Prefer the long condition if both apply. Require sufficient history, valid configured take-profit distance, valid broker stop levels and trading permission. Attach configured price stop-loss and take-profit levels.
