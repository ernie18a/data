At each new bar, if the percentage profit target is enabled and nonzero, scan open orders from the highest position index down to index one, accumulating gross profit of market orders on the traded instrument. Whenever the running total reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market orders and delete all pending orders on that instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

If money trailing is enabled and at least one market position on the instrument belongs to this strategy, calculate net profit including commission and swap for market orders on the instrument using the same index range. When that total is negative and below the configured profit ceiling, and the strategy's gross market-position loss on the instrument exceeds a configured fraction of account equity multiplied by the loss factor, scan the same orders again. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market order on the instrument. At each instrument order, reduce the stored trailing reference to the absolute aggregate net profit if that is no greater than the reference; close all orders on the instrument if the accumulated absolute net profit is at least the absolute difference between the reference and the configured money stop. Reset the reference to zero when no strategy market positions remain; during positive-profit trailing, update it to the greatest observed aggregate net profit. For the equity reference, when there are no strategy positions initialize it to current equity; then use previous equity if it exceeds the stored reference, otherwise use current equity, and save current equity as previous equity.
