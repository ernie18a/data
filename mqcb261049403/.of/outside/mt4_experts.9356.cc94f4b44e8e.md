For each ordered currency pair, construct every available direct, inverse, and two-leg cross-rate representation. Calculate executable bids and asks from the underlying quotes, using bids for sales and asks for purchases. Compare every pair of representations, but trade only combinations on a configured allowlist. If one representation's bid exceeds the other's ask by more than the configured minimum price difference, sell the higher-bid representation and buy the lower-ask representation. Express each synthetic position as the corresponding real currency-pair trades, with quantities converted at executable exchange rates to balance the currency exposures. Do not repeat a trade while that combination is already held in the same direction. When the opposite arbitrage exceeds the threshold, reverse the combination: trade enough to offset its previous exposure and establish the configured exposure in the new direction. No exit is triggered solely because the arbitrage disappears. Aggregate the resulting trades by real instrument; where opposing positions are not permitted, close them before opening new positions, subject to lot-size constraints.
