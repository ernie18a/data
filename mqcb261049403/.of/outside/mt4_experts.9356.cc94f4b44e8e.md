For each underlying currency pair that can be quoted in at least two ways from available market pairs, derive each route’s executable bid and ask, accounting for inversions and cross rates. Only compare route pairs enabled in the trading configuration. If one route’s bid exceeds the other route’s ask by more than the configured minimum arbitrage gap, sell the higher-bid route and buy the lower-ask route; if the reverse inequality holds by that gap, sell the reverse higher-bid route and buy the reverse lower-ask route. Translate each synthetic buy or sell into the corresponding real-pair orders, forming a hedge. Avoid reopening a route-pair opportunity in a direction already represented by its tracked position. There is no independent price-based exit rule; positions are adjusted through subsequent arbitrage trades.
