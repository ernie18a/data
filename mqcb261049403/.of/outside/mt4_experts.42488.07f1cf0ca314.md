On each new candle, track which Donchian channel boundary was touched: a touch below the lower boundary arms a long setup, while a touch above the upper boundary arms a short setup; touching the opposite boundary switches the armed direction. Enter long when a long setup is armed and the prior candle closes above the candle before it opened while its low extends below that candle’s low. Enter short when a short setup is armed and the prior candle closes below the candle before it while its high extends above that candle’s high. Open an initial position only when there are no open orders. If a position exists, add in its direction whenever price moves adversely from the initial entry by another ATR-scaled grid interval, increasing each added order’s size geometrically. Close all orders when combined open profit exceeds a fraction of total invested value or when combined open loss exceeds a large fraction of account balance; also close all orders when the program shuts down.
