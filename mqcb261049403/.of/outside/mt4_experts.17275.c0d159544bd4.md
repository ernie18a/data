Trade the yen cross using a major currency pair and USD/JPY as references. On each new trading-chart bar, evaluate the last completed signal-timeframe bar only if the number of open strategy positions is below the configured limit. For a long signal, every enabled reference test must be bullish on USD/JPY and bullish on the major pair when its configured orientation is left, or bearish when it is right. For a short signal, reverse every comparison. Enabled tests are: the latest close versus either the close a configured number of bars back or the highest high/lowest low of that many preceding bars; RSI versus its midpoint; CCI versus zero; RVI main versus signal; and close versus moving average. All comparisons are strict. A lookback of at most one disables the price test. Apply the trading-chart candle filter too: averaging requires a bearish previous candle for long or bullish for short; pyramiding requires the reverse; the combined mode accepts either. On a signal, optionally close all opposite strategy positions, then open in the signal direction if hedging is allowed or no opposite position remains. Entry also requires spread within the configured limit. Repeat entries on later bars while conditions persist and capacity remains.
