On each new signal bar, consider a trade only while below the open-position limit. Use the selected major currency pair and USD/JPY as external signals for the traded yen cross. With the major pair oriented in its normal direction, a long signal requires both pairs to break above their respective prior lookback highs (or have closes above their respective lookback closes); a short requires both to break below their prior lows (or closes). With the major pair orientation reversed, a long requires the major pair to break below its prior lows/closes while USD/JPY breaks above its highs/closes; a short requires the opposite. If price lookback is disabled, these price conditions impose no direction filter. Apply every enabled indicator as an additional required filter: RSI and CCI must be above their midlines on USD/JPY and agree with the selected major-pair orientation for a long, with the inverse for a short; RVI must have its main line above its signal line on USD/JPY and agree with the major-pair orientation for a long, with the inverse for a short; price must be above its moving average on USD/JPY and agree with the major-pair orientation for a long, with the inverse for a short. Optionally restrict entries by the traded instrument's previous candle: averaging permits longs after a down candle and shorts after an up candle; pyramiding permits longs after an up candle and shorts after a down candle. When a qualified long signal occurs, close existing shorts if opposite-signal closing is enabled, then open a long if hedging is allowed or no short remains. Apply the inverse for a qualified short signal.
