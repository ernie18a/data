Use scheduled economic-calendar events to control trading. For events affecting a currency in the traded symbol and enabled by the impact filter, block ordinary trading during the configured pre-event and post-event blackout. In the configured pre-release setup window, if daily news risk and news-position limits allow, a matching event has no active setup, suitable support and resistance levels bracket price within the allowed range, and both breakout orders meet the minimum risk-to-reward requirement, place a buy stop above resistance and a sell stop below support. Set each stop beyond the opposite boundary and its target beyond the breakout by a multiple of the support-resistance range. When either order fills, cancel the other; expire or cancel remaining orders after the event window.
