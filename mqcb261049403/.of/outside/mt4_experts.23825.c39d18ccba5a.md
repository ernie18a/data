On each tick, if basket protection is enabled, sum net floating profit including swap and commission for open market positions on the chart instrument with the base strategy identifier. If any exist, close those positions when the sum reaches a profit target based on account balance, reaches an emergency loss limit based on account balance, or reaches a second balance-based loss limit with at least a specified number of positions. When triggered, skip other tick processing even if a close fails.

If money trailing is enabled and at least one open market position has the base strategy identifier, sum net floating profit across all market positions on the chart instrument. When that sum is negative and below the money profit threshold, and the base-identifier positions' gross floating profit is negative with its absolute value greater than a configured fraction of account equity multiplied by a loss factor, scan chart-instrument orders from the position after the last down to position one. Accumulate absolute net profit of market positions; during the scan, lower the stored trailing value to the absolute total net profit if that is no greater than the stored value. If the accumulated amount reaches the absolute difference between the stored value and the money stop distance, close all chart-instrument market positions and delete its pending orders. The account equity reference is updated on each applicable check: use the previous tick's recorded equity if it exceeds the stored reference; otherwise use current equity, then record current equity.
