The system does not choose new entries: the trader places buy-stop and sell-stop orders manually. While a position is open, periodically trail its stop loss using recent confirmed fractals: for a long, place it below the nearest lower fractal and raise it only; for a short, place it above the nearest upper fractal and lower it only. If no suitable fractal is found within the search window, use the low (long) or high (short) of the third recent candle as the stop reference, with a small price buffer. When take-profit adjustment is enabled and a position is profitable but has no take profit, find a more distant upper fractal for a long or lower fractal for a short and set the target near that level, accounting for spread; do not move the target closer to the current price. If configured, opening a position triggers deletion of pending orders, either across the terminal or only for the traded instrument.
