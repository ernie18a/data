For positions on an instrument other than EURUSD, use EURUSD's quoted decimal precision to scale the configured stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing-stop distances: multiply them by 10 when EURUSD has five decimal places; otherwise leave them unchanged. On entry, place the stop loss and take profit at the scaled distances from the entry quote, on the appropriate sides for the position. If trailing is enabled, use the greater of the scaled trailing distance and the traded instrument's minimum stop distance. Move a long position's stop up to the current bid minus that distance, or a short position's stop down to the current ask plus that distance, only when it improves the existing stop; also set a short stop if none exists. Exit when the stop loss or take profit is reached.
