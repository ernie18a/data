On the supported exchange, continuously quote buy and sell limit orders on the traded instrument using the selected reference bid: the instrument's own bid, its mark price, or its index price derived from other exchanges. At each configured level, set the buy price below and the sell price above the reference by the level number times a configurable fraction of the reference; shift both prices by another configurable fraction and round to the nearest tick. Allocate buy capacity as total configured level capacity minus current signed position plus target position; allocate sell capacity as total capacity plus current position minus target position. Starting with the outermost level, allocate each side up to the per-level capacity, placing no order when remaining capacity is below the minimum tradable volume. Maintain one order per side per level: create missing orders, cancel orders whose volume differs or whose allocated volume is zero, and amend prices when their relative deviation from the desired price exceeds the configured side-specific threshold. Fills can increase, reduce, or reverse the position.
