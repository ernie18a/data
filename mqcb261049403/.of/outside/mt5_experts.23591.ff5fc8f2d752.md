Use the bid of a selected reference price feed (the traded market, mark price, or index price) as the quoting anchor. At each configured level, place a buy limit below and a sell limit above the anchor, with successive levels farther away by equal increments; shift all levels together by a configurable offset. Allocate buy-side quote volume according to remaining capacity to reach the target position, reduced by current signed inventory; allocate sell-side volume according to remaining capacity to move toward the target from the opposite direction. Cap each level at the per-level size, and omit a side when its remaining volume is below the instrument minimum. Keep quotes aligned with the calculated prices and volumes: place missing quotes, adjust existing quotes when their volume differs or their price has moved sufficiently, and remove quotes when that side has no eligible volume.
