Monitor the combined floating profit and loss, including swap and commission, of the strategy's positions across all instruments. Close all strategy positions and delete their pending orders when the combined loss reaches a configured fixed amount or a configured percentage of account balance. In later versions, optionally stop new trading; otherwise continue. Also in later versions, close all strategy positions and pending orders when account equity reaches a target set from the balance at initialization or after a risk or profit close, plus a fixed amount or percentage of that balance. Optionally stop new trading; otherwise reset the target and continue.

When the news filter is enabled, block new entries during the configured window around qualifying calendar events for either currency of the checked pair. Ignore medium-impact, low-impact and holiday events, events without a fixed time, and titles containing 'Speaks'. High-impact mode requires a high-impact event. The named-event mode has no effect in the supplied code because its event-name list is never populated. The second version checks the chart pair; the Plus version checks the selected trading pair. During a qualifying news window, close all strategy positions and delete pending orders if their combined floating profit and loss is nonzero and lies within the configured symmetric loss/profit bounds, inclusive.
