Close all managed positions when their aggregate floating loss reaches a configured fixed-money or balance-percentage limit. In later versions, also close all positions when account equity reaches a configured fixed-money or balance-percentage profit target. When news filtering is enabled, pause new entries around qualifying high-impact or selected news events; during that window, close all positions if aggregate floating P/L is nonzero and within the configured loss/profit band. Long and short entries otherwise use indicators calculated from the traded symbol itself.
