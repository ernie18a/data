On each eligible new tick, sum unrealized profit and swap across all open positions and compare the total, or its percentage of account balance, with the enabled profit and loss limits. Also count all account positions. Close positions on the chart instrument, or on all instruments if configured, when a profit or loss limit is reached, or when the position count reaches the configured threshold and the optional equity-above-balance condition holds. On an entry transaction, also close those positions if the total count equals the threshold and, when required, equity is at least balance. Skip the next tick after a close attempt.

Otherwise, if the total account position count is below the threshold and the daily limit on entry deals bearing the configured identifier has not been reached, attempt a randomly chosen long or short on the chart instrument. Require a weekday, the minimum time since the last successful random entry, sufficient distance from every existing position on that instrument, valid market data, valid order placement and sufficient margin. An optional initialization entry uses the same entry checks when there are no account positions.

If hedging is enabled, no basket close was triggered and the same daily limit has not been reached, inspect each account position. When its own instrument has moved adversely from entry by the configured distance, open an opposite position on that instrument, subject to market and margin checks, unless that position has already been hedged. Record successful hedges so each original position is hedged at most once.
