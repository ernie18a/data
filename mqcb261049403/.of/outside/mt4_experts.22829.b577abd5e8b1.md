On each new candle, if the open position count is below its configured limit, enter long when the stochastic oscillator crosses upward through its oversold threshold, the parabolic stop-and-reversal value is below the prior candle’s open, the fast weighted moving average of typical price is above the slow one, the low two candles ago is below the prior candle’s high, and the current bid is farther than a configured distance from the close two candles ago. Enter short when the oscillator crosses downward through its overbought threshold, the parabolic value is above the prior candle’s open, the fast average is below the slow one, the prior candle’s low is below the high two candles ago, and the current ask is farther than that distance from the prior candle’s close. If enabled, close the traded symbol’s positions when its aggregate loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high.
