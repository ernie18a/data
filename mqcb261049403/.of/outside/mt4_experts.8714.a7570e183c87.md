Trade the designated instrument using its daily indicators and the attached chart’s candles. The chart can be a different instrument. On the first tick of each chart bar, classify the market as trending when the previous daily vertical horizontal filter meets its threshold; otherwise classify it as ranging. Compute that filter as the absolute difference between the high at the highest close and the low at the lowest close over its lookback, divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes.

Using completed chart candles, define a bullish engulfing as a down candle followed by an up candle with a larger body; define its bearish counterpart symmetrically. Define a morning star as a down candle, a smaller-bodied middle candle, and an up candle whose body is also larger than the middle body and whose close is above the first candle’s body midpoint. Define an evening star symmetrically. A doji has a body smaller than its high-low range divided by the configured divisor.

First, close all short orders with the strategy identifier, across instruments, on a bullish engulfing or morning star, or when the market is trending and the previous daily OsMA is positive, or when ranging and the previous daily stochastic main line is at least its signal line. Close all similarly identified long orders on a bearish engulfing or evening star, or when trending and OsMA is negative, or when ranging and stochastic main is at most its signal. Delete corresponding pending orders too.

After closures, do not enter if the designated instrument’s open-position count has reached the limit for the current regime. Otherwise require previous daily close standard deviation above its threshold. Enter long when the latest chart candle is up, is not a doji, and has no evening star or bearish engulfing; additionally require positive and rising daily OsMA in a trending market, or stochastic main above signal in a ranging market. Enter short under the symmetric candle and indicator conditions. Apply the configured stop loss, take profit, and trailing stop.
