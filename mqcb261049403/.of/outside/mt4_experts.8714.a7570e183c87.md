On each new daily bar, classify the market as trending when the vertical-horizontal filter is above its threshold; otherwise treat it as ranging. Open a long only when the prior candle is bullish, is neither a doji nor an evening star nor a bearish engulfing pattern, and volatility exceeds its minimum: in a trending market, the MACD histogram must be positive and rising; in a ranging market, the stochastic main line must be above its signal line. Open a short under the mirrored conditions: the prior candle is bearish and is neither a doji nor a morning star nor a bullish engulfing pattern; in a trend, the histogram must be negative and falling; in a range, the stochastic main line must be below its signal line. Limit new entries when the existing position count reaches the regime’s order cap. Close shorts on a bullish engulfing pattern or morning star, when the trend histogram turns positive, or in a range when stochastic main is at or above signal. Close longs on a bearish engulfing pattern or evening star, when the trend histogram turns negative, or in a range when stochastic main is at or below signal. Apply protective stop-loss, take-profit, and trailing-stop exits. Use moving-average alignment across daily and monthly data only to scale position size, not to choose direction.
