On a configurable timeframe, calculate a trailing trend from the traded instrument’s bars, using EURUSD’s quoted decimal precision as an external input. For each bar, divide the mean high-minus-low range over a configurable lookback by 10 raised to (EURUSD decimal digits minus traded-instrument decimal digits minus a configurable adjustment). Initialize the trend as neutral and both the tracked extreme and trailing level to the first calculated close. Process bars chronologically: in a neutral or rising trend, raise the extreme to the greater of itself and the close, and raise the trailing level to the greater of itself and the extreme multiplied by one minus the scaled range. If the close is below that level, switch to a falling trend, set the extreme to the close, and set the level to the close multiplied by one plus the scaled range. In a falling trend, lower the extreme to the lesser of itself and the close, and lower the level to the lesser of itself and the extreme multiplied by one plus the scaled range. If the close is above that level, switch to a rising trend, set the extreme to the close, and set the level to the close multiplied by one minus the scaled range.

At the configured signal-bar offset, a falling-to-rising transition signals a long entry and a short exit; a rising-to-falling transition signals a short entry and a long exit. Also exit a short whenever the selected bar is in a rising trend if both short entry and short exit are enabled; symmetrically, exit a long in a falling trend if both long entry and long exit are enabled. Respect separate entry and exit enable switches. Process exits before entries, and enter only when no position exists. Evaluate on each tick for the current bar, otherwise on a new indicator-timeframe bar. Limit repeated entries in the same direction until the end of the signal’s current timeframe bar. Attach configurable stop-loss and take-profit distances to entries when enabled.
