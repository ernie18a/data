Trade EUR/USD long, EUR/GBP short, and GBP/USD short when those pairs have no open position. Use a price grid whose step is the larger of a fraction of the target price, the broker’s minimum stop distance, and the spread. Give new positions a take-profit beyond the entry; increase the target distance for the initial position. Add one minimum trade unit in the same direction each time price moves adversely by another grid step, subject to per-pair size, currency exposure, account risk, and margin limits. After the risk cutoff, restrict additions to the minimum unit. When price retraces toward the target by the grid schedule, reduce an open position by one unit; leave the final unit to close at its take-profit. If account margin is sufficiently high, exposure and risk limits may be bypassed.

Track currency exposure across open positions and identify the largest losing and winning positions. When the worst loser is negative and larger than one unit, reduce it by one unit if the scaled loss would leave the balance above its prior protected level; during a drawdown response, prioritize reducing the worst loser. Raise the protected balance after a successful reduction. If the best winner’s profit exceeds the worst loss, or total open profit is positive, move the best winner’s take-profit closer to the market, provided the revised target satisfies broker stop-distance rules.
