On each new bar, determine a bullish state when the Alligator’s current lips are above its jaws, its teeth are below its jaws, and the prior bar’s lips were below its jaws; also require five same-period EMAs, applied respectively to close, weighted price, typical price, median price, and open, to be strictly descending in that order. Determine a bearish state using the mirrored Alligator relationships and require those five EMAs to be strictly ascending. If an optional hour filter is enabled, require the current hour to fall strictly between its configured start and end. Open long only when the state becomes bullish after previously being bearish, and open short only when it becomes bearish after previously being bullish, subject to the configured open-order limit. Track the last non-neutral state across bars. For an open long, raise its stop to the prior-bar value of a smoothed moving average of close when that value is above entry and below the current ask; for an open short, lower its stop to that average when it is below entry and above the current bid. Update a stop only when it tightens the existing stop. These trailing stops provide the exit condition.
