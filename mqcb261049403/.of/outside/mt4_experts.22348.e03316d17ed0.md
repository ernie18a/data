Close all positions on the traded instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s running equity high-water mark. Optionally close them when combined profit reaches a configured cash target or a target proportional to account balance; also apply a money-based trailing exit to combined position profit, closing after it falls by a configured amount from its peak. Long and short entries depend only on that instrument’s own price history, so they do not meet the stated cross-instrument criterion.
