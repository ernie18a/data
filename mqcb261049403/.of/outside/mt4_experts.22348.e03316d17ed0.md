On the first tick of each new chart bar, manage exits before considering entries. An enabled account-equity stop closes all market positions on the current instrument with the strategy identifier when their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the equity reference. Update that reference when evaluating the stop: reset it to current account equity if there are no such positions; then use the previous recorded equity if it exceeds the reference, otherwise use current equity, and record current equity for the next evaluation.

An optional percentage profit target uses account balance: iterate open orders backward starting at the order count and ending at index one, summing floating profit for market positions on the current instrument regardless of strategy identifier. Whenever the running sum reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on that instrument. This iteration omits order index zero.

An optional money-trailing loss exit also uses account equity. If there is at least one strategy market position and the similarly indexed sum of current-instrument net profit, including commission and swap, is negative and below the configured profit threshold, check whether the strategy positions' combined floating loss exceeds the configured equity-risk percentage multiplied by a loss factor and the equity reference. If so, iterate the same order indices backward, accumulating the absolute net profit of each current-instrument market position. For each current-instrument order, replace the stored trailing value with the absolute total net profit when that absolute total is no greater than the stored value; close all positions and delete all pending orders on the instrument when the running absolute sum reaches the absolute difference between the stored trailing value and the configured money-stop distance. Reset the stored value to zero when there are no strategy market positions.

Entries also depend on account free margin. Require enough chart history, a valid configured profit target, fewer than the allowed number of strategy market positions, sufficient free margin, and broker validation of volume, margin, protective levels, and trading permission. Size starts at the configured base size multiplied by a configured exponent raised to the number of strategy positions. If loss-based sizing is enabled and more than one losing historical order on the instrument is encountered before the most recent profitable one, replace that size with a configured fraction of free margin; clamp and round to broker volume constraints.

For direction, scan progressively longer historical windows beginning at bar three. At each window, first identify an upward breakout if the previous bar's open is above the window's highest high and any of bars two through four has a high below that maximum; otherwise identify a downward breakout if the previous open is below the window's lowest low and any of those bars has a low above that minimum. Stop at the first identified direction. Open long if it is upward, the absolute difference between current bid and the close two bars ago exceeds the configured point distance, and the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high. Otherwise open short if it is downward, the absolute difference between current ask and that close exceeds the distance, and the previous low is below the high two bars ago. Attach configured price-based stop loss and take profit.
