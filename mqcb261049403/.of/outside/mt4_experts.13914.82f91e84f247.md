Long-only averaging strategy. Open the initial position when the stochastic oscillator crosses upward through its oversold threshold, provided the current close is between configured price bounds. After a position exists, add one equal-sized lot when the current close falls below the next stored level, remains above the lower price bound, and is above the exponential moving average calculated on the configured timeframe. Set each subsequent level lower than the prior level by the configured percentage of the current close. For open long positions, move the stop upward to just below the current bid once the gain exceeds the configured trail activation distance, but only if this tightens the existing stop. When scheduled closing is enabled, close long positions during the configured closing window if their profit exceeds the configured threshold. No short-entry rule is present.
