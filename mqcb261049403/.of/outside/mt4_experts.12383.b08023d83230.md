For each enabled currency pair, calculate currency strength on a short and a long timeframe. For every enabled cross, locate its selected candle close within its high-low range, map that percentage through an increasing stepwise score table, and assign the complementary score to the quote currency. Average each currency’s scores across its enabled crosses, then rank all currencies separately on each timeframe. Retain the previous cross score when its candle range is zero.

For an enabled and tradable pair, allow signals only during the applicable signal’s trading hours and, when the news filter is enabled, outside the configured windows before and after the calendar’s next or most recent event. The news restriction applies to all pairs regardless of event currency or impact.

Swing signal: require the base-versus-quote strength ordering to agree on both timeframes. On each timeframe, at least one currency in the pair must belong to the configured strongest group and at least one to the configured weakest group; the group sizes can differ by timeframe. Also require at least one long-timeframe currency strength to exceed the swing threshold. If the base is stronger on both timeframes and the pair’s recent RSI has fallen, signal short. If the base is weaker on both and RSI has risen, signal long.

Scalping signal: require the pair to contain a currency in each of the configured strongest and weakest short-timeframe groups, and require both long-timeframe currency strengths to be below the scalping threshold. If the base is stronger on the short timeframe and RSI has risen, signal short. If the base is weaker and RSI has fallen, signal long.

Open at most one position per pair after its cooldown, subject to available margin, startup and global order spacing, the account trade limit, and the requirement that every existing strategy trade has been open beyond the profit-wait period and is losing, unless the account has fewer than the starter trade count. Use the higher trade limit when average position age exceeds the long holding period. If signals conflict, long takes precedence; scan pairs in their fixed order and open at most one per pass.

For a profitable position, when long-timeframe currency strength reverses against its direction, exit after the profit-wait period if its favorable price move exceeds the configured ATR-based minimum, or after the longer holding period regardless of that move. A position without a take-profit may also exit at the larger ATR-based profit target under the configured starter-position limit. These exits operate only when the account has at most the starter number of trades or its average position age exceeds the long holding period. For losing positions without a take-profit, place a recovery take-profit at the long-timeframe candle’s high for a long or low for a short when the configured adverse ATR move and price-placement conditions hold; adjust an existing recovery target toward the entry within the configured ATR bound.

Close all strategy positions when aggregate profitable-position gains exceed the configured multiple of aggregate losses and charges and the account has more than the starter number of trades. Also queue all profitable strategy positions and the least negative losing position older than the profit-wait period for closure when their aggregate gains exceed the configured multiple of that loss.
