On trading days, optionally close all open positions using an account-wide daily profit target and trailing profit threshold. Initialize the target from the highest recorded account balance and increase it by a fixed growth factor at each new trading day. The remaining daily goal is the target balance minus the current account balance. Derive a risk-sensitivity score from weighted recent closed-trade expectancy and its range over recent history. When positions exist, the remaining goal is positive, and current account profit reaches a sensitivity-adjusted fraction of that goal, update a trailing threshold to the greater of its previous value and a fixed multiple of current profit minus the remaining goal. Close all positions if current profit falls to or below that threshold. Reset the threshold whenever the entry conditions cease to hold or no positions remain. If automatic closing is disabled, recommend closing instead. Separately, recommend closing all positions and resting when the account stress score reaches its critical lower limit; stress combines account drawdown, equity, margin level, current profit, and the account's per-trade profit target.
