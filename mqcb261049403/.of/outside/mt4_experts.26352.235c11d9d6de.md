For each enabled currency and each enabled, available pair associated with it, use scheduled economic calendar events for that currency. Exclude holidays, events without a definite time, impacts below the configured minimum, and optionally speeches. During the currency's configured daily session, activate trading from the configured time before the next qualifying event through the configured time after the most recent qualifying event. Equal session start and end mean all day; sessions may cross midnight. Calendar times are compared with broker time adjusted by the configured offset. Forecast and previous values do not determine direction.

When the event window is active and the currency-pair group has no market position, place any missing buy stop above ask and sell stop below bid at the configured distance. If one-trade-per-event is enabled, require more than the combined before-and-after window to have elapsed since the last closed market trade's opening time. Either pending order can trigger according to price. Optionally cancel remaining pending orders when a position exists, and cancel pending orders or close positions when the event window ends. Optionally reposition pending orders during the window, normally only before the event, as price moves.

In basket mode, aggregate open positions and pending orders by event currency across its pairs, including their lots and net profit, commission and swap. Once at least one position is open and, if configured, no pending orders remain, close the currency's positions when aggregate net profit reaches the configured profit per total lot or its loss reaches the configured loss per total lot.

Optional recovery after a losing closed trade opens an opposite market position when that currency-pair group has neither positions nor pending orders, during the event window or optionally afterward. Optional replacement after a loss recreates the missing opposite stop when exactly one stop remains, no position is open, and price is within the configured distance of that remaining stop; it may run after the event if enabled. After a nonnegative result, replacement mode can instead cancel a lone remaining stop.
