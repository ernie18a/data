For each enabled currency and its selected, available pairs, filter scheduled economic events by currency, minimum impact, and configured exclusions. During the allowed daily session, activate trading from the configured lead time before a qualifying event through the configured delay after it; the event instant also qualifies. When active, if no market position exists and the per-event re-entry rule permits, place both a buy stop above and a sell stop below the current price, unless that side already has a pending order. A triggered stop opens a position in that direction. Optional management can trail stops or move them to break even, close positions outside the event window, or close a currency’s basket when aggregate profit or loss reaches its configured threshold. In recovery mode, after a losing trade with no open or pending orders, open a market trade opposite the last trade, using an increased size. In replacement mode, after a losing trade, replace the sole remaining pending order with an opposite-side stop when price approaches it; after a non-losing trade, optionally delete the untriggered counterpart. In manual mode, a user’s currency-level open command starts the same two-sided pending-order setup; close and delete commands act on that currency’s positions and pending orders.
