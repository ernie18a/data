On each new bar, subject to sufficient price history, the configured trade limit, free-margin and account stop-out checks, valid broker stop distances, and trading permission, evaluate entries in order. Buy if the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance, the current bar opens above the current EMA of highs, the current fast linear-weighted typical-price average exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the current monthly RSI exceeds the previous monthly RSI. Otherwise, sell if the absolute distance from ask to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance, the current bar opens below the current EMA of lows, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the current monthly RSI is below the previous monthly RSI. A qualifying buy signal prevents a sell attempt on that bar even if the buy order fails.

On each new bar, if the optional equity stop is enabled and the strategy's open-position profit on this instrument is negative and its absolute loss exceeds the configured fraction of the equity reference, close its positions on the instrument. Update that reference when checked: reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then, if the stored reference is below the previously sampled equity, use that previous equity, otherwise use current equity; finally store current equity as the previous sample.

On every tick, if the optional percentage profit target is enabled, scan open orders in descending position order, starting at the total order count and stopping before position zero. Accumulate profit from market positions on this instrument; whenever the running sum reaches the configured fraction of account balance, close all market positions and delete pending orders on this instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

On every tick, if money trailing is enabled, sum profit, commission and swap for market positions on the instrument using the same descending scan that excludes position zero. If this net sum is negative and below the configured money-profit threshold, and the strategy's instrument profit is negative with absolute loss exceeding the configured equity-risk fraction times the equity reference and loss multiplier, scan the same orders again, accumulating the absolute net profit of each market position on the instrument. Close all positions and delete pending orders on the instrument when that running sum reaches the configured money-loss threshold.
