On each new chart bar, first manage exits, then consider entries. An optional equity stop closes this strategy's positions on the traded instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of an account-equity reference. The reference is updated from current and previous account equity, and is reset to current equity when no strategy positions are open. An optional profit target closes all orders on the instrument when the accumulated floating profit of its market positions reaches a configured percentage of account balance. An optional money-trailing loss rule can also close all instrument orders when the strategy's floating loss exceeds an equity-based threshold multiplied by a loss factor and the accumulated absolute per-position net losses reach the money-trailing threshold. The profit calculations used by the latter two rules iterate order positions from the total count down to one, excluding position zero.

For entry, require sufficient account free margin, sufficient margin after the proposed trade, an acceptable account stop-out level, a position count below the configured limit, sufficient chart history, valid protective levels, and trading permission. Compare current fast and slow linearly weighted moving averages of typical price on a configured higher timeframe. Go long when the fast average is above the slow average, the low two completed bars ago is below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds a configured point distance. Go short when the fast average is below the slow average, the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds that distance. In live trading, additionally require both the latest fractal high and latest fractal low to be higher than their respective third-most-recent fractals for a long, or both to be lower for a short; omit this filter in testing. Place the configured stop loss and take profit with each entry.
