When opening a position, set its stop-loss distance from account risk: take the smaller of balance and equity, multiply by the configured risk percentage, divide by position size, tick value per point, and the leverage-based risk factor; add the spread and convert points to price. Apply the minimum stop distance. Place the stop below the entry for a long and above it for a short; place the take-profit on the opposite side at the configured multiple of that distance. Exit when either level is reached.
