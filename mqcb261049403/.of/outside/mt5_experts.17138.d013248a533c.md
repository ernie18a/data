On each evaluation, derive a bullish or bearish reversal from ATR-based trailing bands: switch bullish when the close rises above the prior upper band, and bearish when it falls below the prior lower band. Confirm direction with a trend signal from a fast simple average of typical price and a slow smoothed average of median price; this signal uses the fast average’s recent high/low ordering and its position or changing spread relative to the slow average. Enter long only when both signals are bullish, and short only when both are bearish. If an opposite position exists, either close it when the new signal appears or retain it according to the configured opposite-signal policy. When trailing stops are disabled, close a profitable long if the trend signal turns bearish, or a profitable short if it turns bullish. Apply the configured position and recent-close timing restrictions to entries.
