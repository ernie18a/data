Only in the strategy tester, when demonstration trading is enabled, evaluate on each tick. If any position belonging to the demonstration is open, close it once its holding period has elapsed; otherwise wait. Do not open another position on that tick. When no such position exists, wait until the interval since the last opening attempt has elapsed and the chart instrument permits full trading. Alternate opening attempts between long and short, starting with long. Attempt a minimum-size market trade only if the relevant quote and minimum volume are positive, margin can be calculated, and free margin meets the required margin buffer. Record the attempt time and advance the direction even if the order fails. Use no stop loss or take profit.
