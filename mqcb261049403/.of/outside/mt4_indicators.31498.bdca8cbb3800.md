On the chart timeframe, build a USD index as a scaled, weighted geometric product of EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, USD/CAD, USD/SEK and USD/CHF; EUR/USD and GBP/USD have negative exponents and the others positive. Build a EUR index similarly from EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY, EUR/CHF and EUR/SEK, all with positive exponents. Use the selected price type and configurable index scales and weights. For each index, subtract its slow moving average from its fast moving average, using the configured periods and moving-average method. When the current bar's difference crosses above zero from a nonpositive value on the preceding bar, signal buying that index's currency; when it crosses below zero from a nonnegative value, signal selling that currency. Suppress repeated signals in the same direction until an opposite crossing occurs. Signals are generated only when the chart signal display and at least one alert method are enabled; each index's oscillator is calculated only when its display is enabled. No exit rule is specified.
