For the selected symbols and order identifier, sum the floating profit, swap and commission of open long and short positions. Measure this either in account currency or as a percentage of account balance. When enabled, close all eligible positions if aggregate profit reaches the take-profit threshold or falls to the stop-loss threshold. For trailing aggregate profit, activate when profit reaches the activation threshold; thereafter maintain a nondecreasing exit level equal to the highest observed profit less the trailing distance, and close all eligible positions when profit falls to or below that level. Reset the trailing activation when no monitored positions remain. Evaluate these exits on each tick or at the start of the configured bar period. Because the selected positions can span multiple instruments, one instrument's exit can be driven by profit on the others.
