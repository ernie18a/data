On each new bar, go long when the fast exponential moving average crosses above the slower simple moving average, confirmed by the completed bar and still holding above; go short on the confirmed reverse cross. For either entry, anchor a Fibonacci range from the crossover bar’s close to the prior wave’s low for a long or high for a short. Set the stop at the shallow retracement level, constrained by the maximum permitted price distance. Set the target from the extension levels, adjusting it to account for the previous day’s high or low and enforcing a minimum distance from entry. Open only during permitted trading days and hours, below the open-position limit and with sufficient margin. Close losing positions when the fast average crosses the intermediate simple moving average against the position; keep profitable positions open for their stop or target.
