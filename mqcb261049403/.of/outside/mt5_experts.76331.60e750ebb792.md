Trade only during enabled sessions, outside rollover, with acceptable spread and M1 volatility, after the tick buffer is ready. A fast move beyond the volume-weighted price deviation band starts a sweep: an upper sweep tracks its high and may trigger a short when price snaps back far enough; a lower sweep tracks its low and may trigger a long after an equivalent rebound. Require available enabled timeframe trends to align with the trade, the enabled prior M5 candle setup to pass, and the enabled prior M1 candle to show directional rejection (short: upper wick; long: lower wick, with configured body, candle direction, and close-location checks). Apply position limits, cooldown, and spacing rules. Then score alignment across H1/M15/M5, directional deviation and sweep depth, snapback, tick speed, M1 structure, ATR regime, spread, and session quality. In local mode, allow the candidate direction only when its score meets the adaptive minimum. In web mode, require a BUY or SELL response with sufficient confidence; if unavailable, apply the configured fallback, which either blocks or uses the local verdict. In hybrid mode, require the web and local verdicts to agree on the candidate direction and both confidence and score to meet their adaptive minima; otherwise apply the fallback. The included example web service scores a candidate from sufficient snapback plus high velocity, strong direction-adjusted deviation, either H1 or M15 alignment, and the matching M1 rejection wick, and returns that direction only when its confidence clears its threshold. For open positions, move the stop to entry after sufficient favorable movement, then trail it as favorable movement continues, updating only in the protective direction and subject to broker distance and step constraints; stop execution closes the position. No separate signal-based exit is defined.
