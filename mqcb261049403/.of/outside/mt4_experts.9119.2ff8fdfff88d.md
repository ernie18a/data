An optional cross-instrument filter measures euro strength on the traded chart’s timeframe. For each of EURUSD, EURGBP, EURCHF, EURJPY, EURCAD and EURAUD, subtract the moving average at a configurable earlier bar from its current moving average, and scale the difference by ten raised to that pair’s price precision. Sum the six scaled differences. A positive sum confirms a long; a negative sum confirms a short; zero confirms neither. This confirmation is applied in the same direction for every traded instrument. When the filter is enabled, it must agree with every other enabled indicator filter before an indicator-based entry is allowed. In indicator-only mode, test long first, then short. In the long-only and short-only model modes, the corresponding model signal must also agree; in the supervised model mode, a positive supervisor permits only a confirmed long, while a nonpositive supervisor permits a confirmed short. Evaluate entries on a new bar when trading and new orders are allowed and the configured trading hours permit it; do not open another strategy position in a direction already held. The calculated USD index does not affect any decision.
