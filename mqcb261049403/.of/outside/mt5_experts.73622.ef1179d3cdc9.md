On each new analysis bar, if trading is not halted and account drawdown and daily loss limits permit, calculate a weighted signal from the traded instrument’s RSI, ADX, fast-minus-slow simple moving averages, and volatility, plus a randomly selected internal state. Each component can be enabled separately. Normalize RSI and ADX around their respective baselines, express the moving-average difference in price points, and measure volatility as the absolute mean of recent log returns divided by their standard deviation. Optionally reject entries below a volatility threshold. Go long when the combined signal exceeds the positive threshold, short when it falls below the negative threshold, and otherwise do nothing. Require the configured interval since the last successful entry and valid stops, margin, and position size.

The signal weights depend on account-wide deal history, including deals on other instruments. On every trade event, use the latest account deal: record its buy/sell direction with its sign preserved for positive profit and reversed otherwise. Calculate the fraction of positive nonzero records in the recent window, adjust every weight equally according to its deviation from the neutral success rate, and clamp the weights to their allowed range. Load saved weights on initialization and save them on shutdown.

Place instrument-ATR-based stop loss and take profit orders and optionally tighten the stop after a configured profit, using ATR, volatility, and the current internal state. If the optional hard stop is enabled and account-wide equity drawdown from balance reaches its threshold, close all positions in the traded instrument and remove the strategy. This hard-stop check runs only while trading is not halted.
