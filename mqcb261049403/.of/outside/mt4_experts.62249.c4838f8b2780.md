If hedging is enabled and its loss threshold is positive, compare the combined floating profit and loss of this strategy’s open positions, including swap and commission, with account balance. When the combined result is negative and its absolute value as a percentage of balance reaches the threshold, enter hedge mode and open a position opposite the larger total position volume. Size it as the difference between sell and buy volumes times the configured hedge multiplier, subject to the existing lot cap and broker volume and margin constraints. If volumes are equal, enter hedge mode without opening a position. Do this only if hedge mode is not already active. Reset hedge mode when no positions remain; also activate it when each side has more than one position and total buy and sell volumes are equal. Profit and loss exit signals take priority over hedging. The hedge order bypasses the normal spread and order-count checks. When the completed-bar option is enabled, tick processing returns without trading.
