Maintain separate buy and sell grids according to the configured side selection. If an enabled side has no open orders, open its first order immediately. For each enabled side with existing orders, add another order when price moves from that side’s most recent entry by the configured grid distance: buys trigger upward and sells downward in with-trend mode, and the reverse in counter-trend mode. The distance is fixed or scales with the number of orders on that side, according to the selected step mode. Before adding grid orders, close all open positions when their combined unrealized profit reaches the configured pip-based or currency-based target. If loss closing is enabled and combined position pips reach its loss threshold, close either the earliest position on each side or all positions, as configured. If hedge mode is enabled, combined unrealized loss is negative, its absolute size reaches the configured share of account balance, and a hedge has not already been placed, open a position on the side with lower total lots to offset the lot imbalance. Open orders only when spread is within its configured limit and the open-order cap has not been reached; apply these gates to grid entries, while exits and hedge orders are handled separately.
