On each tick, scan the configured currency pairs and entry timeframes. On a new bar, classify each pair's trend from its two latest Williams %R swing highs and lows: bullish when the latest high exceeds the preceding high by an ATR-based threshold and the latest low is higher; bearish when the latest low is below the preceding low by an ATR-based threshold and the latest high is lower. The swing positions come from the pair and timeframe being evaluated, but these high and low comparisons use the chart instrument's price arrays. Average this classification with the two directional outputs of the required external trend indicator. A candidate qualifies when this average and the average on either of two specified higher timeframes have a product of one. For bullish candidates, use the higher of the two swing highs as target and the higher swing low as support; for bearish candidates, use the lower swing low as target and the lower swing high as support. Calculate reward as the target-to-support distance less spread and risk as the bid-to-support distance in the trade direction. If risk is nonpositive and reward before spread exceeds a spread-based minimum, assign a fixed high score; otherwise divide reward by risk, aborting the scan if risk is zero. Retain candidates meeting the reward-to-risk threshold. Scan them in pair and timeframe order, considering only those for which minimum-lot margin is available. The selection compares each score with the previously selected candidate's list index, then stores the current index; it does not select the highest score reliably. If the total open-order limit permits and no order already exists in the selected pair and direction, enter in the swing-trend direction, subject to margin and minimum lot size. Set the initial stop and target distances to at least an ATR-based minimum. On new bars, manage open orders with a time-based parabolic stop that moves toward breakeven; after breakeven and a configured holding period, trail by an ATR-based distance. Exit when the stop or target is hit. The indicator's calculations are not supplied, so its directional outputs are required inputs.
