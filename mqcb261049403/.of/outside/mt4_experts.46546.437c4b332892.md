On each new bar, go long when price is above the slow EMA and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, ADX is below its ceiling, and within the recent three-bar window there is a retracement toward the slow EMA (closer than the candle range and neighboring bars), an upward break of the fast EMA that occurred before the retracement, and a stochastic main-line crossing up through the oversold threshold. Go short under the mirrored bearish conditions, with a downward fast-EMA break before the retracement and the stochastic main line crossing down through the overbought threshold. Require the same direction's signal to be more than three bars apart, and respect configured order limits and trade feasibility checks. For hedging, in the pro-trend mode add a buy after price falls sufficiently below the latest buy entry, and a sell after price rises sufficiently above the latest sell entry. In contra-trend mode, after a sufficiently adverse move from the latest order, open the opposite side if it is the only open order; otherwise add to the same side. When trailing-stop management is enabled, activate a protective stop after a position moves favorably by the configured activation distance and move it as price advances. In the alternate close mode, close managed orders when total account floating profit becomes positive.
