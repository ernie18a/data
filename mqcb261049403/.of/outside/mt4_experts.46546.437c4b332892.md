At each configured management interval, use the total number of open and pending orders across the account. In immediate-close mode, if that count is not exactly one and account equity exceeds account balance, close all orders on the traded instrument that match the configured order-ownership filter. In the alternative closing mode, or when the account-wide count is exactly one, manage stops instead. After closing or stop management, in counter-trend hedge mode, find the matching order on the traded instrument with the highest ticket. If its entry price and volume are positive and price has moved adversely from its entry by at least the configured distance, open an opposite-direction position when the previously captured account-wide order count equals one; otherwise open a position in the same direction. Use ask to measure an adverse move from a buy entry and bid for a sell entry. Opening also requires valid trade volume, sufficient account balance and free margin.
