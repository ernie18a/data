Manage all account positions except those with a configured excluded identifier. On each market tick and periodic timer check, update account risk and enforce exits. At the configured daily server-time reset, record equity, clear the breach flag, and update the highest reset-time balance. Track peak equity continuously. Flag a breach when equity has fallen from reset-time equity by at least the configured fraction of initial balance, or equity is at or below the maximum-loss floor. Calculate that floor as initial balance, peak equity, or the greater of initial balance and highest reset-time balance, according to the selected mode, minus the configured fraction of initial balance. On the first breach, if enabled, close all managed positions and cancel all pending orders; if continued blocking is enabled, close managed positions while the breach flag remains set. When the combined notional of managed positions exceeds an enabled total cap, close positions newest first until the remaining total fits; calculate each notional as volume times current price times tick value divided by tick size, treating unavailable tick data as zero. If enabled, close all managed positions outside the configured server-hour trading window, at or after the configured Friday closing hour, or during the configured interval around a high-impact calendar event for a watched currency. Recheck the news calendar at most once per cache interval.
