At each check, reset the daily equity baseline and clear the breach state at the configured daily reset. Track peak equity and the highest balance at a reset when the selected maximum-loss rule requires them. If the equity drawdown from the daily baseline reaches its configured limit, or equity reaches the floor defined by the selected static, peak-equity, or highest-reset-balance rule, mark a breach; optionally close all managed positions and cancel pending orders. After a breach, close any new managed positions until the next reset when blocking is enabled. Close individual positions that exceed configured volume or account-currency notional caps. If combined notional exceeds its cap, close the newest positions until it fits. Close positions that remain without a stop after the allowed grace period. For a position whose stop risks more than its configured account-currency risk cap, move the stop to the maximum-risk distance from entry; close the position if that stop can no longer be placed because the market has crossed it. Close positions outside the configured trading window, after the configured Friday cutoff, or during the configured window around high-impact news in watched currencies. Ignore excluded positions. Best-day profit statistics are informational and do not affect trading decisions.
