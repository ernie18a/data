Evaluate once at the start of each new chart bar. Optional account-based exits: close all positions on the current instrument and delete its pending orders if their summed floating profit reaches a configured money target, or if the running sum reaches a configured percentage of account balance. Both profit scans start at the order count and stop at index 1, so they omit index 0. If enabled, close this strategy's positions on the instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of account equity; the equity reference is updated using current and previous equity rather than a persistent maximum. The enabled money-trailing routine also closes all instrument orders based on partial sums of position profit, commission and swap: in profit, when a partial sum falls a configured amount below the total; in loss, after the strategy's loss exceeds an equity-based threshold multiplied by a configured factor, when the partial sum of absolute position results reaches the configured money-loss amount. Its scans also omit order index 0.

For entries, require sufficient account free margin, broker margin and stop-distance checks, sufficient chart history, and fewer than the configured maximum number of this strategy's open positions. On the current instrument, go long when the absolute difference between bid and the close two bars ago exceeds a configured distance, the current fast linear-weighted moving average of typical price exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, the previous bar's RSI exceeds a configured buy threshold, and the current monthly RSI exceeds the preceding monthly RSI. Go short when the absolute difference between ask and the previous close exceeds the distance, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, the previous RSI is below a configured sell threshold, and the current monthly RSI is below the preceding monthly RSI. In live trading additionally require the previous high below the open of the configured anchor bar and current ask above it for a long, or the previous low above that anchor open and current ask below it for a short. Testing omits this filter. Evaluate the long condition first and skip the short evaluation whenever the long conditions are met, even if the live filter or order checks prevent entry. Use configured stop-loss and take-profit distances; position size grows with the number of open strategy positions and can instead depend on free margin after consecutive historical losses.
