For each bar, let A and B be the closes of the two component instruments, X the close of their cross, and T the traded instrument’s close. Compute three adjustments: 3×(A×B−X), 3×(A−X/B), and 3×(B−X/A). If reversal is enabled, negate each adjustment. Add T to each and average the three results to get a reference value. When the reference changes from at or below T to above T, buy; when it changes from at or above T to below T, sell. No separate exit rule is specified.
