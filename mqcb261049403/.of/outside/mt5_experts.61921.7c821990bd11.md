For the selected traded instrument, define the price unit as the chart instrument's point size, multiplied by ten if the broker's EUR/USD quote has five decimal places; otherwise use the chart point unchanged. Thus, when either reference instrument differs from the traded instrument, outside instrument metadata affects these decisions.

For each enabled strategy position with virtual exits, close a long when bid reaches its entry price plus the configured profit distance or minus the configured loss distance; close a short when ask reaches entry minus the profit distance or plus the loss distance. Convert each distance to price using the price unit, and raise it to at least the traded instrument's broker stop/freeze distance. Check these exits on every tick, including in standby and outside the permitted trading session.

For signal-driven entries, reject a new order if the traded instrument's ask-minus-bid spread divided by the price unit exceeds the enabled spread limit. When adding to an existing same-direction strategy position, require the configured time interval and, according to the enabled grid mode, a favorable or adverse price move from the relevant extreme existing entry price by at least the configured distance multiplied by the price unit.
