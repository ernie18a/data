On each new bar, evaluate each enabled strategy independently using its configured periods, thresholds, and pattern choices. For Accelerator and Awesome oscillators, compare current and prior values with symmetric positive/negative entry or exit thresholds; the selected pattern combines threshold location or crossing with whether the oscillator is rising or falling to signal long, short, or close. For the directional movement index, require ADX to exceed its strength threshold, then use whether ADX is rising or falling, which directional line is greater, and the selected directional-line threshold to signal long or short; exit patterns compare ADX and directional lines against configured thresholds. For DeMarker and Money Flow Index, use the selected pattern of crossing or being above or below complementary upper and lower oscillator thresholds to signal long, short, or close. For the Bollinger and Force Index strategy, an optional band-width filter gates a prior candle closing wholly beyond a Bollinger band; combine that result with a selected two-bar Force Index threshold-and-slope pattern, requiring both entry conditions. Close a position when either configured Bollinger or Force Index exit condition occurs. For the MACD and Stochastic strategy, combine a selected MACD pattern (main line versus signal line, threshold location or crossing, and line slopes) with a selected Stochastic pattern (K versus D, threshold location or crossing, and line slopes); require both for entry and close when either configured exit pattern occurs. Close opposite-side positions when a strategy signals entry in the reverse direction. Apply configured price-based stop-loss and take-profit exits. Only execute signals when operation, market-session, spread, order-limit, and any configured grid and spacing rules allow; multiple enabled strategies may operate together or be restricted to one strategy at a time.
