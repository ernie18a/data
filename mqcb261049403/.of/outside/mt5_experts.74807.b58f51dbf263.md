Monitor the source account’s open positions, treating positions already open at startup as a baseline. For each subsequently detected new position, open the same instrument and direction on the receiving account, or the opposite direction if reverse copying is enabled. Skip an open if it is already mapped to a copied position, the instrument has no valid bid or ask, or its spread exceeds the configured limit. Size the copy using the source volume, a configured multiple, or a fixed volume; clamp it to the instrument’s volume limits and round to its volume step. Set initial stop-loss and take-profit at the source levels’ offsets from the source entry, applied to the receiving entry; negate those offsets when reversing. When a source position changes, update the mapped position’s stop-loss and take-profit to the source levels, or reflect each positive level around the receiving entry when reversing. In source-volume and multiplier modes, partially close the receiving position if the new target volume is smaller; do not increase it. When a source position closes, close its mapped receiving position.
