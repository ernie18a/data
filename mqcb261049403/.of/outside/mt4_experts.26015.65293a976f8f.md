On each new bar, if the number of open trades is below its limit, consider a long when the fast typical-price weighted moving average on the selected higher timeframe is above the slow one, the prior candle ranges overlap (the low two bars ago is below the previous high), and the current bid is farther from the close two bars ago than a configured retracement distance. In backtests, enter directly when these conditions hold. In live trading, also require the lower fractal trend line to be sufficiently flat, the ask to be above its recent endpoint, and the latest relevant high-fractal comparison to pass. Consider a short under the mirrored moving-average, candle-overlap, and price-distance conditions; in live trading also require the upper fractal trend line to be sufficiently flat and the bid below its recent endpoint. Attach fixed stop-loss and take-profit levels to entries. Close the strategy’s open basket when its net profit reaches a balance-based profit threshold or its loss reaches a balance-based loss limit. Also close the basket when its loss exceeds a configured fraction of the highest tracked equity, or when an enabled money-based take-profit or trailing-profit rule is reached. For open trades, move the stop beyond entry after sufficient favorable movement; optional trailing-stop rules may further tighten protection.
