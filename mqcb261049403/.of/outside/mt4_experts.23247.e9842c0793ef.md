Once per new bar, evaluate these account-dependent exits in order:
1. If percentage-based profit taking is enabled, accumulate the gross profit of market positions on the traded instrument in descending order-position order, starting at the total order count and stopping at index one. After each iteration, if the accumulated profit reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.
2. If money trailing is enabled, the same index-one-and-up subset of instrument market positions has negative total net profit, and the strategy's gross instrument loss exceeds the configured fraction of the equity reference multiplied by the loss multiplier, accumulate the absolute net profit of each selected instrument market position in descending order. When that running total reaches the configured money-loss threshold, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.
3. If the equity stop is enabled and the strategy's gross instrument loss exceeds the configured fraction of the equity reference, close its market positions on the instrument.

Net profit includes commission and swap; gross profit excludes them. Each time the equity reference is requested, first set it to current account equity if the strategy has no open market positions on the instrument. Then, if the reference is below the previously recorded account equity, replace it with that previous equity; otherwise replace it with current equity. Record current equity as the previous equity. The reference and previous equity start at zero. Continue evaluating the remaining new-bar logic after an exit attempt.
