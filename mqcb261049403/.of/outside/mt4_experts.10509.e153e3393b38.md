On ticks within the configured local-time windows, record a snapshot of the reference instrument’s bid, ask and previous bar close; the arithmetic mean of those same prices across a configured currency basket; and the chart instrument’s current RVI main and signal lines. Keep a rolling history and wait until the configured minimum number of snapshots is available.

Update a channel signal on each eligible tick: advance a bar index, calculate that bar’s high-low midpoint plus and minus a multiple of the current ATR, and compare the chart’s current close with the preceding calculated upper and lower bands. A close above the preceding upper band sets a buy signal; a close below the preceding lower band sets a sell signal. When a direction is set, trail its corresponding band against the preceding band. If neither comparison succeeds, retain the previous buy/sell signal.

Set a long instruction when the RVI main is above its signal by more than a configured fraction of their current absolute difference, that difference exceeds a safety threshold, the basket’s current mean ask and previous-bar close both exceed their values a configured number of snapshots earlier, the reference instrument’s current ask and previous-bar close both exceed their values another configured number of snapshots earlier, and the channel signal is buy. Set a short instruction using the opposite RVI and price inequalities, the basket and reference bids instead of asks, and a sell channel signal. If neither condition holds, retain any pending instruction.

On every tick, close the first selected open market order if its profit reaches the configured profit target or its loss reaches the configured loss limit. During an eligible time window, if the total number of orders is below the configured limit and enough snapshots exist, execute any pending instruction on the chart instrument and clear it. Before sending the order, apply the same profit/loss closure check to the first selected order.
