On each new chart bar, allow entries only when daily ADX is below a trend-strength threshold and no same-instrument position is open. Enter long when the chart-timeframe fast simple moving average crosses above the slow average and daily RSI is below a lower threshold. Enter short when the fast average crosses below the slow average and daily RSI is above the corresponding upper threshold. Close a long when its gain reaches a price-relative profit target, price falls below the lowest low of a recent lookback, or the fast average crosses below the slow average. Close a short when its gain reaches the price-relative target, price rises above the highest high of the lookback, or the fast average crosses above the slow average. After an open trade exceeds a price-relative profit threshold, set a protective stop a fixed distance beyond the entry price in the profitable direction.
