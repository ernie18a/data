At each day’s start, reset the daily protection state. Calculate the account’s daily result by summing profit, swap, and commission for all market trades closed since that day began and all currently open market trades. If the enabled daily loss limit is reached or exceeded, lock trade management and, if configured, close all currently open trades matching the management scope and filters. Otherwise, if the enabled daily profit target is reached or exceeded, do the same. The tool does not open long or short positions.
