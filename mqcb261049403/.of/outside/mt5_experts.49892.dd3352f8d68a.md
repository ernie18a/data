When copying is enabled, monitor the selected source account for new and removed orders. For each new source order, place the corresponding order on the mapped instrument if that destination order type is enabled. Normally preserve its direction and order type; in reverse mode map buy to sell, sell to buy, buy limit to sell stop, sell limit to buy stop, buy stop to sell limit, and sell stop to buy limit. For market orders, if the optional deviation filter is enabled, open only when the absolute difference between the destination bid-ask midpoint and the source entry price, measured in points, is at least the configured threshold. Pending orders use the source entry price and bypass that filter. Optionally copy the source stop loss and take profit; in reverse mode exchange their roles. Propagate subsequent enabled stop-loss and take-profit changes and source entry-price changes to the matching copied order.

When a source order disappears, close its matching copied market position if closing that destination direction is enabled, or delete its matching pending order. Also reconcile copied market positions against the source's current ticket list and close those whose source ticket is absent, subject to the destination-direction close setting. Identify copied orders by source ticket and mapped instrument.
