On the master account, publish trade events when positions or pending orders are opened, closed, or modified. On the slave, for each matching master event, act only when copying is enabled for that event type and direction. Open market trades in the master’s direction, or the opposite direction when reverse copying is enabled; if deviation filtering is enabled, open only when the absolute difference between the slave’s current midpoint and the master’s entry price meets or exceeds the configured threshold. Copy pending orders only when their corresponding order type is enabled; in reverse mode, invert the order type and trade direction. Close or delete the corresponding slave order on a master close only when closing that direction is enabled, accounting for reverse mode. Apply master stop-loss and take-profit changes only when copying the respective protection is enabled. Match copied trades by symbol and their master trade identifier.
