Initialize a neutral trend and set both the tracked price and trailing level to the initial close. For each bar, calculate the mean high-low range over the configured lookback, including that bar, and divide it by ten raised to the difference between the reference currency pair's price digits and the source instrument's price digits, less the configured digit adjustment. In a neutral or rising trend, raise the tracked price to the maximum of itself and the close, and raise the trailing level to the maximum of itself and tracked price times one minus the scaled range. If the close falls strictly below that level, set the tracked price to the close, reset the level to the close times one plus the scaled range, and switch to a falling trend. In a falling trend, lower the tracked price to the minimum of itself and the close, and lower the trailing level to the minimum of itself and tracked price times one plus the scaled range. If the close rises strictly above that level, set the tracked price to the close, reset the level to the close times one minus the scaled range, and switch to a rising trend. Signal long only on a transition from falling to rising, and short only on a transition from rising to falling; the first transition from neutral produces no signal.
