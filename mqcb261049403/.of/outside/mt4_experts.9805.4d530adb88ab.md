When configured to manage all instruments, trail existing market positions using the chart instrument’s broker minimum stop distance even for positions in other instruments. First raise the selected trailing setting to at least that minimum; this can also change which trailing method is selected. For each eligible position, calculate a proposed stop from its own instrument: a fixed distance from bid for longs or ask for shorts; the nearest prior candle low or high or fractal beyond the chart instrument’s minimum distance, shifted by an offset; a velocity-based distance when velocity is rising for longs or falling for shorts; or the current parabolic SAR shifted by an offset, if the SAR is beyond that minimum distance. In group breakeven mode, require the proposed stop to be at least the lot-weighted average entry price of same-direction positions in that instrument for longs, or at most that average for shorts. Otherwise, if profit-only mode is enabled, require the stop to be at least the position’s entry for longs or at most its entry for shorts. Respect the options to manage only positions with an existing stop and to skip positions whose existing stop has reached breakeven. Move a long stop only upward by more than the configured step, and a short stop only downward by more than the step or when it has no stop; reject a zero short stop. The resulting stop exits the position if triggered.
