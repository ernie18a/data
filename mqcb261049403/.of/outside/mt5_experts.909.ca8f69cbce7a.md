When the user selects a synthetic currency pair and clicks Buy or Sell, open two component positions; there is no automatic entry or exit signal. The available constructions are: EURUSD = EURGBP × GBPUSD; GBPUSD = EURUSD ÷ EURGBP; USDCHF = EURCHF ÷ EURUSD; USDJPY = EURJPY ÷ EURUSD; USDCAD = EURCAD ÷ EURUSD; AUDUSD = EURUSD ÷ EURAUD; EURGBP = EURUSD ÷ GBPUSD; EURAUD = EURUSD ÷ AUDUSD; EURCHF = EURUSD × USDCHF; EURJPY = EURUSD × USDJPY; GBPJPY = GBPUSD × USDJPY; AUDJPY = AUDUSD × USDJPY; GBPCHF = GBPUSD × USDCHF. For a product construction, buy both legs to go long and sell both to go short. For a quotient construction, buy the numerator leg and sell the denominator leg to go long; reverse both to go short. Size the second leg from the entered first-leg volume, the synthetic ask for a long or bid for a short, and both legs’ tick values and point sizes. Open the first leg, then the adjusted second leg; the interface has no exit action.
