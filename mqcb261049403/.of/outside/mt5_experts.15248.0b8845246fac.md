On the configured timeframe, calculate a trend from the traded instrument's bars, scaling its average high-low range by 10 raised to the difference between EURUSD's price precision, the traded instrument's price precision, and a configurable adjustment. Initialize the trend as neutral and both the tracked extreme and trailing level to the starting close. For a neutral or rising trend, raise the extreme to the bar's high if higher, and raise the trailing level to extreme times one minus the scaled range if higher. If the high falls below that level, switch to a falling trend, set the extreme to the high, and set the level to extreme times one plus the scaled range. For a falling trend, lower the extreme to the bar's low if lower, and lower the level to extreme times one plus the scaled range if lower. If the low rises above that level, switch to a rising trend, set the extreme to the low, and set the level to extreme times one minus the scaled range. Process bars chronologically, preserving the last completed bar's state when recalculating the current bar. At the configured signal bar, a switch from falling to rising signals a long entry and short exit; a switch from rising to falling signals a short entry and long exit. A rising trend also signals a short exit when both short entry and short exit are enabled; a falling trend similarly signals a long exit when both long entry and long exit are enabled. Respect the separate entry and exit enable settings. Evaluate on each new indicator-timeframe bar, or every tick when using the current bar. Close positions before opening, open only when flat, and prevent another entry in the same direction until the end of the signal's current timeframe bar. Apply configured stop-loss and take-profit distances to entries.
