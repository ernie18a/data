On each eligible entry or add-on check, block trading when short-term one-minute standard deviation exceeds its configured ceiling. Also respect the configured calendar blackout, spread ceiling, and margin/order-capacity constraints. Compute a bounded momentum oscillator from recent one-minute typical prices: pass signed price changes and their absolute values through matching cascades of exponential smoothing, take the ratio of smoothed signed movement to smoothed absolute movement, and map it to a 0–100 scale. Buy when the oscillator turns upward from its prior reading after that reading was no higher than the one before it; sell when it turns downward after its prior reading was no lower than the one before it. Require a volatility outbreak: multiply a composite of recent one-minute high/low and close changes, measured in points, by tick volume per elapsed second in the current minute, and require the result to exceed its configured threshold. Open in the indicated direction only when both timing and outbreak conditions hold. For an existing position, once its favorable movement reaches the trailing activation distance, move its stop to remain the configured trailing distance from market; add another position in the same direction when favorable movement reaches the next configured pyramid interval and the entry conditions still hold. Close positions when their stops are hit; after pyramiding has begun, close all positions if account drawdown exceeds its configured equity limit.
