On each new bar, go long when the current bid is at or above both moving averages from their respective timeframes, and the previous completed bar closes above the lower Bollinger Band while the bar before it closed at or below that band, with the bid still at or above the lower band. Close short positions when this signal occurs, then open two long positions: set their stop below the latest ZigZag trough; give the first a take-profit a configured fraction of the entry-to-stop distance above entry. Go short when the bid is at or below both moving averages, and the previous completed bar closes below the upper Bollinger Band while the bar before it closed at or above that band, with the bid still at or below the upper band. Close long positions when this signal occurs, then open two short positions: set their stop above the latest ZigZag peak; give the first a take-profit a configured fraction of the entry-to-stop distance below entry. For open positions, move the stop to lock in a configured profit after price advances sufficiently, and trail it as price continues favorably; no separate indicator-based exit signal is used.
