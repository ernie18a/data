For each enabled traded instrument, use its stochastic oscillator and those of two related instruments on the same timeframe. On each new bar, use the last completed bar, provided all three series are synchronized and have sufficient history. For each instrument, round the stochastic main line minus its signal line to four decimal places; mark it up if positive and down if negative. Zero initially has neither mark.

Apply these rules in order, adding marks without removing existing ones: If the traded instrument and one related instrument are up while the other related instrument is down, also mark the latter up when the agreeing related instrument has the larger absolute difference. Apply the symmetric rule for down. Next, if the traded instrument and one related instrument are up and the other related instrument is not down, mark the other up; apply the symmetric rule for down. Finally, if both related instruments are up and the traded instrument is not down, mark it up; apply the symmetric rule for down.

Close a short when the traded instrument is marked up, and close a long when it is marked down. Then open a long if all three are marked up, or a short if all three are marked down, provided no position already exists in the traded instrument. Process long before short. Keep an unfilled entry signal active and retry it until it succeeds or the next bar replaces it. Attach the configured stop loss to new positions.
