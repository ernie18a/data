Long and short entries are user-triggered; there is no automatic entry rule based on outside data. For open positions, monitor account equity and daily realized plus floating P/L. Close all positions and pending orders and lock trading if equity reaches the configured maximum-drawdown stop, the buffered daily-drawdown stop, or the configured total-profit target; the daily drawdown lock and daily-profit lock last until the next trading day, while the maximum-drawdown and total-profit locks persist. Close everything and lock until the next trading day when daily profit reaches its configured cap. Optionally close positions in the selected scope when their combined floating profit reaches a target; if basket trailing is enabled, arm at that target and close when profit falls by the configured trail distance from its peak. Manual unlock may be overridden immediately if a drawdown limit remains breached.
