Monitor account-wide equity and trading-day reference balance or equity. Close all positions and cancel all pending orders when equity reaches the buffered maximum-drawdown floor, or when the decline from the trading-day reference reaches the buffered daily-drawdown limit. Lock trading permanently after maximum drawdown and until the next trading day after daily drawdown; while locked, continue closing positions and cancelling orders. The daily reference is the configured choice of day-start balance, day-start equity, or the higher of the two.

If enabled, close all positions and cancel all pending orders when account equity reaches the total profit target relative to starting balance, or when equity exceeds the trading-day reference by the daily profit target. Lock trading permanently for the total target and until the next trading day for the daily target. Disable the total profit target during automatic strategy testing.

If the basket target is enabled, sum floating profit and swap across positions in the selected account, symbol, or strategy scope. On reaching the target, close those positions and cancel grid pending orders in that scope. If trailing is enabled, reaching the target instead arms a trailing exit: track the peak scoped floating profit and close when it falls by the configured trailing distance. Basket exits do not lock trading.
