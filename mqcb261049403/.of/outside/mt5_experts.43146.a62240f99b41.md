Evaluate entry conditions once per completed chart bar, and open a trade only when no position is already open on the instrument. For a long: detect bullish MACD divergence on the five-minute chart by pairing a recent negative MACD trough with a price low and comparing it with an earlier trough and low; signal when the two measures move in opposite directions. Require the hourly stochastic main line to be above its signal line and within its lower band, the daily fast exponential moving average to be above the daily slow exponential moving average, and the last completed five-minute close to be below the daily fast average. For a short, apply the bearish counterparts: a positive MACD peak paired with a price high shows divergence, the hourly stochastic main line is below its signal line and within its upper band, the daily fast average is below the slow average, and the last completed five-minute close is above the daily fast average. Close long positions when their stop loss or take profit is hit, or when five-minute RSI reaches its upper exit threshold; close short positions on their stop loss or take profit, or when RSI reaches its lower exit threshold.
