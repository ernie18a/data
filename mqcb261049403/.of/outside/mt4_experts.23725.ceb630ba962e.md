Account-based exits:
- In the ECN version, if percentage profit-taking is enabled and its threshold is nonzero, scan open orders from the highest position index down to index 1, accumulating floating profit of market positions on the traded instrument. After each scan step, if the accumulated profit reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on that instrument, regardless of identifier. Index 0 is omitted.
- In the other version, if basket protection is enabled, sum profit, swap and commission for open market positions on the instrument with the strategy's identifier. If at least one exists and the sum reaches the configured profit percentage of account balance or falls to the negative of the configured loss percentage, close those positions and stop processing that tick.
- On each new bar, if the equity stop is enabled and the floating profit of the instrument's identified market positions is negative and its magnitude exceeds the configured percentage of the equity reference, close those positions. Update the reference when checked: if there are no identified trades, first set it to current equity; then, if the reference is below the previously recorded equity, replace it with that previous equity, otherwise replace it with current equity; finally record current equity as previous equity.
- In that same version, when money trailing is enabled, its loss branch can also close all orders on the instrument. It applies when the instrument's scanned net profit including swap and commission is negative and below the configured money target, and the identified positions' floating loss exceeds the configured percentage of the equity reference multiplied by the loss factor. Scan orders from the highest index down to index 1, omitting index 0; accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position on the instrument. During the scan, if the absolute total net profit is no greater than the absolute stored trailing reference, replace that reference with the absolute total; close all instrument positions and delete its pending orders when the accumulated absolute amount reaches the absolute difference between the reference and the configured money stop. Reset the trailing reference when there are no identified market positions.
