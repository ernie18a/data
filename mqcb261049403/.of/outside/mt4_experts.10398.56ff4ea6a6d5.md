On each grid update, define a ladder of price levels across the configured range, using the market price as the anchor when no range boundary is set. At each level, consider long orders only when long trading is allowed and short orders only when short trading is allowed. Optionally restrict longs to levels above the hourly 60-period EMA and shorts to levels below it. Place breakout orders above market for longs and below market for shorts, and countertrend orders below market for longs and above market for shorts, according to which modes are enabled. Do not exceed the per-side position limit; skip a level and profit target rank when a same-side order or position already exists nearby. Each enabled rank uses a distinct take-profit distance; optional stop-losses may be attached. Optional direction filters: allow longs when the four-hour MACD has stayed above zero for two bars, shorts when it has stayed below zero; on a zero crossing cancel pending orders and optionally close open positions. Alternatively, weekly close above the Parabolic SAR allows longs and below it allows shorts. A moving-average group allows longs when short-to-long moving averages are strictly ordered upward and sufficiently spread, and shorts when ordered downward and sufficiently spread; an optional stochastic separation must confirm the direction. Otherwise, the group allows neither side. On group direction changes, cancel opposing pending orders and optionally close opposing positions. Optionally close all positions and pending orders when account equity rises or falls by the configured percentage from its saved reference, then update that reference; optionally stop further trading after either event. Optionally trail open-position stops once price has moved sufficiently in profit, moving the stop behind current price. Optionally delete pending orders that have moved too far from market.
