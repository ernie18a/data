Open a long when the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, price has moved sufficiently from a recent candle close, a recent candle range condition is met, the prior short-period RSI is above the buy threshold, and monthly RSI is rising. In live trading, also require both recent-fractal trend lines to slope upward. Open a short under the mirrored conditions: fast average below slow, the short-period RSI below the sell threshold, monthly RSI falling, and, in live trading, both trend lines sloping downward. Limit entries by the configured maximum open-trade count. Close all positions on the instrument when its combined floating loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high.
