On the first tick of each chart bar, apply account-based controls and evaluate entries. Account equity and free margin can reflect other instruments.

If the equity stop is enabled, close this strategy's positions in the traded instrument when their combined floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds the configured fraction of the equity reference. The reference is updated on each call: reset it to current equity when there are no strategy positions, then use previous equity if it exceeds the stored reference; otherwise use current equity. Save current equity as previous equity.

If the percentage profit target is enabled, compare accumulated floating profit from the traded instrument's market orders with the configured percentage of account balance. Scan open-order positions from the total count down to one, skipping position zero; as soon as the accumulated profit reaches the target, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

If money trailing is enabled and the instrument's net floating profit including commission and swap is negative and below its configured profit ceiling, also require the strategy's floating loss to exceed the configured equity-risk fraction multiplied by a loss factor and the equity reference. Then scan the same order positions, accumulating absolute net profit for the instrument's market orders; close all its orders when that accumulation reaches the configured money-loss amount. The reference profit in this loss branch starts at zero on every call.

For entries, require sufficient account free margin, adequate price history, a valid configured profit target, fewer than the maximum strategy positions, and broker permission, margin and stop-level checks. Go long when the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance, the current fast linear-weighted typical-price average exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high, the previous bar's chart-timeframe RSI exceeds its buy threshold, and current monthly RSI exceeds the previous monthly RSI. Go short when the absolute distance from ask to the previous close exceeds the distance, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, the previous chart-timeframe RSI is below its sell threshold, and current monthly RSI is below the previous monthly RSI. In live trading additionally require both the third-most-recent fractal high and third-most-recent fractal low to exceed their respective most-recent fractals for a long, or both to be below them for a short. Omit this fractal filter in testing. Evaluate long first; once its signal conditions are met, do not evaluate short on that bar, even if the live filter or order checks prevent entry.
