For the current instrument and strategy identifier, sum open market positions' profit including swap and commission. On every tick, if basket protection is enabled and at least one such position exists, close all of them when basket profit reaches a configured percentage of account balance, falls to a hard loss percentage of balance, or falls to a configured loss percentage of balance while the basket has at least the configured minimum number of trades. After a closing condition triggers, do no further processing on that tick.

If the equity stop is enabled, once per new bar sum the same positions' profit excluding swap and commission. Close them if that sum is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the account equity reference. Update that reference whenever it is requested: if there are no matching market positions, first set it to current account equity; then set it to previous equity if the reference is below previous equity, otherwise set it to current equity; finally store current equity as previous equity.

If money trailing is enabled, there is at least one matching strategy position, and the net profit including swap and commission of all open market positions on the instrument is negative but below the configured profit ceiling, compare the matching strategy positions' loss excluding swap and commission with the configured percentage of the equity reference multiplied by the configured loss index. If it exceeds that threshold, scan the instrument's open orders backward, starting at the order count and ending at index one. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position encountered. Maintain a persistent trailing reference: if the absolute total instrument net profit is no greater than the absolute reference, set the reference to that absolute total. After each instrument order, close all positions and delete all pending orders on the instrument if the accumulated absolute profit is at least the absolute difference between the reference and the configured money stop. Reset the reference to zero whenever no matching strategy market positions remain.
