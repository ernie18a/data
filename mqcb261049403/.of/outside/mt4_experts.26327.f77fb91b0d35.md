On each changed quote, manage this instrument's strategy orders. Stop trading if the broker's minimum stop or freeze distance exceeds the allowed limit. If the optional UTC session filter is enabled, allow entries only from the start time inclusive to the end time exclusive, including overnight sessions; equal start and end times allow no session. Outside the session, optionally close market positions and/or cancel pending orders as configured.

Measure current spread or the configured rolling average, add the configured or automatically observed commission, and compare with the maximum allowed cost. If existing strategy orders are present and cost is too high, either close all positions and cancel pending orders, or multiply stop, target, and modification distances by a configured factor and block new entries. With no existing orders, this cost check does not block entry. Require sufficient free margin for the larger of the buy and sell margin requirements.

When entries are allowed, maintain one market long and one market short. If neither exists, open both. Otherwise, replace a missing side immediately when the replacement distance is zero. For a positive replacement distance, replace a missing long when its last historical close minus current ask, or a missing short when current bid minus its last historical close, reaches the positive threshold or falls to its negative threshold multiplied by the safety factor. For a negative replacement distance, replace when that signed distance falls to the threshold or rises to its negation multiplied by the safety factor. Use the last matching historical order encountered, or zero distance if none exists.

Set each long's stop below bid and optional target above ask; set each short's stop above ask and optional target below bid. Respect the broker's minimum distance. After a configured number of relevant quote changes, reset both levels around current prices whenever either level's distance deviates from its configured distance by at least the modification step; initialize missing stops immediately. With virtual levels, close a long at its stop or target using bid and a short using ask, but only while both sides are open. Broker-held stops and targets execute normally.

Optionally, while both market sides exist, place a buy limit below bid and a sell limit above ask by the stop distance plus a configured offset. Whenever a side's levels are modified, move its corresponding pending order to that side's stop plus the outward offset.
