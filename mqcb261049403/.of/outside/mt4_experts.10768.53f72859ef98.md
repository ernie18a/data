For each configured instrument, optionally aggregate managed positions across all instruments when calculating total profit, long and short position counts, and long and short volume. Use net profit including commission and swap. Pending-order counts remain specific to the instrument being processed.

When aggregate profit reaches a target, either close managed positions and cancel pending orders immediately in trend mode, or track profit on successive qualifying checks and close them if it falls by a fixed amount from the previous qualifying check. In trend mode, leave any position whose stop price is above its entry price open, including shorts. Reset the recovery state when the remaining positions and profit reach zero.

If aggregate loss exceeds a configured fraction of account balance, enter recovery once, cancel managed pending orders, and open a market position in the current instrument opposite the aggregate excess volume, sized to offset that excess. During recovery, inspect older positions on the current instrument: disable new orders in a position's direction when its net profit is negative, and enable them when it is nonnegative; process positions in order, so the last inspected position of a direction determines its permission.

Maintain missing buy-stop and sell-stop orders for each instrument, subject to directional permission and margin requirements. Derive their levels from that instrument's recent high or low plus or minus volatility and a buffer, falling back to a wider timeframe or a fixed distance; at the configured time use the previous day's high and low instead. Size orders from the absolute difference between aggregate long and short position counts, using a base size when the counts match. In trend mode, when exactly one side is pending, permit the missing side only when the corresponding trend signal is present: compare current and earlier momentum readings across several short timeframes and fast versus slow averages. These trend restrictions persist until reset or changed by recovery processing.
