Evaluate once per new bar. When the percentage profit target is enabled and nonzero, scan the instrument's open orders from position count down to position one, excluding position zero. Accumulate market-order profit without commission or swap; after each position, if the cumulative profit reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier. When the equity stop is enabled, sum the profit without commission or swap of this strategy's market orders on the instrument. If the sum is negative and its absolute value exceeds the configured percentage of the equity reference, close those strategy orders. Update the equity reference whenever it is needed: if there are no strategy market orders, first set it to current account equity; then, if it is below the equity recorded on the previous update, replace it with that previous equity, otherwise replace it with current equity; finally record current equity for the next update. When money trailing is enabled, first sum profit including commission and swap for the instrument's market orders at positions count down to one. If that sum is negative, below the configured money profit threshold, and the strategy's summed market-order profit is negative with absolute value exceeding the equity-risk threshold multiplied by the configured loss factor, scan those positions again, accumulating the absolute net profit of each market order. Once the accumulation reaches the configured money loss amount, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument.
