On each new completed bar of the selected timeframe, compare its close with the Parabolic SAR and compare the preceding bar’s close with its SAR. When price crosses from below SAR to above it, open long and close any short; when it crosses from above SAR to below it, open short and close any long. Open only when no position is already open on the instrument, subject to the direction’s entry being enabled. Close an open position when price is on the opposite side of SAR (longs when close is below SAR; shorts when close is above SAR). Also close any position once its holding time exceeds the configured limit, if timed exits are enabled.
