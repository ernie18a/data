On each new bar, compare the MACD main lines for GBP/USD and USD/CHF on the hourly timeframe. If both lines were above zero on the prior bar and GBP/USD’s line crosses from below USD/CHF’s line to above it, go long. If both were below zero and GBP/USD’s line crosses from above USD/CHF’s line to below it, go short. When reverse mode is enabled, swap the long and short signals. There is no standalone exit signal; if configured, close positions opposite to a new signal before opening in its direction.
