At each new bar of the traded instrument, compare the main MACD lines calculated from hourly closes of GBPUSD and USDCHF, using configurable MACD periods. Go long when both lines were positive on the previous completed hourly bar, GBPUSD's line was strictly below USDCHF's then, and GBPUSD's current hourly line is strictly above USDCHF's. Go short when both previous lines were negative, GBPUSD's line was strictly above USDCHF's then, and its current line is strictly below USDCHF's. Optionally reverse both signals. Optionally close strategy-owned positions opposite to the resulting signal before opening; if only one position is allowed, ignore the entry while any strategy-owned position on the traded instrument remains open.
