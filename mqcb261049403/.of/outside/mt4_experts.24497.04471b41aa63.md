When enabled, close all positions and delete pending orders for the traded instrument if the running sum of its open positions' profits reaches a configured percentage of account balance. Scan orders from the last index down to index 1, checking the running sum after each order.

When money trailing is enabled and at least one strategy position is open, track a shared profit reference, resetting it to zero when no strategy positions remain. Sum the instrument's open-position profit including commission and swap, again scanning from the last index down to index 1. While that total is positive and below the configured profit threshold, update the reference to the greatest total observed; during a second scan, close all instrument positions and delete its pending orders if the running profit sum falls at least the configured money distance below the reference.

While that total is negative and below the configured profit threshold, apply the loss exit only if the strategy's instrument positions have negative combined profit, excluding commission and swap, whose magnitude exceeds a configured percentage of an account-equity reference multiplied by a configured factor. On each such evaluation, set the equity reference to the previous account equity if it exceeds the existing reference; otherwise set it to current equity, then store current equity as previous equity. During a second scan of instrument orders, accumulate absolute position profits including commission and swap. If the absolute total profit is no greater than the absolute shared profit reference, replace the reference with the absolute total profit. Close all instrument positions and delete pending orders when the accumulated absolute profits reach the absolute difference between that reference and the configured money distance.
