When eligible to trade, identify a bullish setup if trend strength exceeds its configured threshold, positive directional movement exceeds negative directional movement, and the Alligator lines are ordered from fastest to slowest above one another. Identify a bearish setup when trend strength exceeds the threshold, negative directional movement exceeds positive, and the Alligator lines are ordered in the reverse direction. In indicator-only mode, enter long on a bullish setup and short on a bearish setup. In AI-confirmation mode, first require a setup, then analyze entry-, intermediate-, and higher-timeframe charts; enter only when the AI returns the matching direction and meets the configured confidence minimum. Skip entries when a position is already open or when account risk limits, permitted trading days or hours, a relevant high-impact news window, or the spread filter blocks trading. Close open positions at the configured Friday close time, when the standard-account equity profit or loss boundary is reached, or when a prop or funded account reaches its daily loss, total drawdown, or daily profit limit. Manage open positions with the configured trailing stop and staged partial exits at their risk-reward milestones.
