At the start of a cycle, record the account balance. While account-wide equity minus that balance remains strictly between a configured loss limit and profit target, place a grid of sell limits above the current bid and buy limits below the current ask. The first order on each side is one configured spacing away; subsequent orders are spaced from that side's first accepted order, with 15 levels per side. Place each level once per cycle. When the equity difference reaches or exceeds the profit target or reaches or falls below the loss limit, delete buy and sell limit orders and close open positions across the account. Once no orders remain, reset the cycle and record a new baseline on the next tick.
