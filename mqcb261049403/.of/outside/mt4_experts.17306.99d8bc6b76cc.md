On each tick, if there is at least one open or pending order on the chart instrument with the strategy's identifier, compare its current candle price with the previous candle: below the previous low, close all long positions with that identifier across all instruments; otherwise, above the previous high, close all short positions with that identifier across all instruments. Then recount orders on the chart instrument. If none remain, buy it when its current price is above the previous high, or sell it when its current price is below the previous low, provided there is sufficient free margin.
