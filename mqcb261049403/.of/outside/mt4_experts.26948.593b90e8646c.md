On each new bar, if there are no open strategy positions, buy when the short-period simple moving average is below the long-period average; sell when it is above. After a closed trade loses, switch entry strategy and open a recovery trade in the new strategy’s direction with an increased lot size. The alternate strategy buys when the current ask is above the lower envelope band; otherwise it sells when the ask is below the upper band. Close the strategy’s open basket when its profit reaches a configured balance-based target, or when its loss reaches a configured balance-based limit and at least three trades are open; also close at the hard loss threshold. Close positions when aggregate profit reaches a configured money target, when the money-based trailing condition is met, or when aggregate loss exceeds the configured fraction of the recent account-equity peak.
