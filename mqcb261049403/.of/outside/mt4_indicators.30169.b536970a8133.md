For each currency, calculate relative strength from the closes of its seven major currency pairs, reversing the sign for pairs where it is quoted; compare that strength with its exponential moving average. In trend mode, mark the currency green when strength is above average if it is the traded pair’s base currency, or below average if it is the quote currency; mark it red for the opposite conditions. In counter-trend mode, reverse those color assignments. When the base and quote currency indicators use the same mode and show the same color, go long the pair if both are green and short it if both are red. No exit condition is specified.
