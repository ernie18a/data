Manage each configured instrument independently on each chart tick. If both a long and a short are tracked, take no action. For an open long, use bid; for an open short, use ask. With trailing disabled, or when the profit target is smaller than the stop distance, close when price reaches either the fixed profit target or the fixed stop distance from entry. Otherwise, maintain a trailing stop at the configured distance from current price, updating it only when the improvement exceeds the spread; in break-even mode, permit updates only after profit exceeds the stop distance or no stop exists. While trailing, do not apply the virtual profit target.

After an exit, or when no position is tracked, open a new trade only within the configured server-hour window if enabled, and outside the shared blocked time period. Start in the configured direction at the initial size. After a nonnegative result, trade again in the same direction at the initial size; after a loss, reverse direction and multiply the previous size, rounding down to the volume step. Reset to the initial size if this exceeds the configured maximum progression size. Before opening, check remaining account free margin; if insufficient, retry with initial size and abandon the entry if that also has insufficient margin. If a reversal entry fails, block new entries for all instruments for the remainder of the configured time period. Position exits continue to be managed during blocked periods and outside trading hours.
