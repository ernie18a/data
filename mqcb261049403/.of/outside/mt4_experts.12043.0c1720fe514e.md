For each open buy or sell position, if stop-loss setting is enabled and the position has no stop loss, set one relative to its entry price: below entry for a buy and above entry for a sell. Choose the distance either as a fixed pip distance or as a multiple of ATR for the selected timeframe and period. If take-profit setting is enabled and the position has no take profit, set one relative to entry: above entry for a buy and below entry for a sell. Choose its distance either as a fixed pip distance or as a multiple of ATR. Do not open or close positions.
