On each new bar, allow entries only while the number of open positions on the instrument is below the configured limit. For a long entry, require the absolute distance between the current bid and the close two bars ago to exceed a candle-retrace threshold, the fast typical-price weighted moving average to be above the slow one on a higher timeframe, and the low two bars ago to be below the previous bar’s high. In live trading, also require the Fibonacci arc’s shift at the current ask to be less than the previous bar’s open; omit this arc filter in backtests. For a short entry, require the absolute distance between the current ask and the close two bars ago to exceed the same threshold, the fast average to be below the slow one, and the previous bar’s low to be below the high two bars ago. In live trading, also require the arc shift at the current ask to be greater than the previous bar’s open; omit it in backtests. Open positions with a stop loss and take profit. When enabled, close all instrument positions if their combined loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account’s peak equity; optional money or balance-percentage profit targets also close all instrument positions when reached. Move stops to lock in a configured gain after a position has moved sufficiently into profit, and trail stops using recent candle extremes when candle trailing is enabled, otherwise trail by a fixed distance after a profit threshold.
