On each new chart bar, first apply account-dependent exits. If enabled, close this strategy's positions in the current instrument when their combined floating loss exceeds a configured percentage of the tracked account equity. The tracked equity is updated on each call using current and previous account equity, rather than a persistent all-time high. If the account-balance-based profit target is enabled, scan current-instrument orders from the last index down to index 1, accumulating market-order profit; whenever the running sum reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument. If money trailing is enabled and the scanned net profit is negative but below its configured profit ceiling, and this strategy's instrument loss exceeds the equity-risk threshold multiplied by a configured factor, scan the same orders and close all instrument orders when the running sum of absolute net profits reaches the configured money-loss amount.

After exits, consider a new position only with enough account free margin, sufficient history, a valid configured profit target, and fewer than the maximum strategy positions. Scan higher-timeframe fractals from the oldest bar in the configured window toward the present; the first upper fractal gives a short signal, otherwise the first lower fractal gives a long signal. If both occur on that bar, the upper fractal takes precedence. The apparent candle-pattern checks do not gate these signals. For a long, also require the current fast linear-weighted moving average of typical price above the slow one, the low two bars ago below the previous bar's high, and the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago above the configured distance. For a short, require the fast average below the slow one, the previous low below the high two bars ago, and the absolute distance from ask to the previous close above that distance. Submit the qualifying order only if its volume, account margin and stop-out checks, stop distances, and trading permissions pass. A qualifying long branch ends the bar's processing even if submission fails.
