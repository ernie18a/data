On each new bar, permit entries only while the position count is below its cap. Scan recent candles on a selected higher timeframe from oldest to newest: the first candle with an upper fractal signals short; if none is found before a lower fractal, that signals long. The additional candle-pattern checks around each fractal do not gate the signal in the implemented logic. Enter long only when that signal is long, a fast weighted moving average is above a slow one, the chart’s second-prior low is below its prior high, and current bid is farther from the second-prior close than the configured candle-distance threshold. Enter short only when the signal is short, the fast average is below the slow one, the prior low is below the second-prior high, and current ask is farther from the prior close than that threshold. Attach a protective stop and profit target to each entry. For open positions, move stops to lock in gains after a favorable move, trail stops as price advances, and close positions when enabled aggregate money or percentage profit targets are reached. Close all positions when aggregate loss exceeds the configured fraction of the account’s recent equity high; an additional money-trailing rule can close the basket after profit pulls back from its peak.
