On the one-minute chart, consider entries only on enabled weekdays when the spread is within its configured limit, the seasonal and Bollinger-band volatility filters allow trading, no strategy order is open, and the strategy is unlocked. For a long, require the stochastic main line to be oversold and above its signal line, the faster exponential moving average on a higher timeframe to be above the slower one, and the RSI to be deeply oversold; the code also requires both quote prices to be below the RSI reading. For a short, require the stochastic main line to be overbought and above its signal line, the faster higher-timeframe average below the slower one, and RSI deeply overbought; both quote prices must be above the RSI reading. After a long entry, lock further entries until RSI reaches an overbought reset level. Adjust the long position's stop and target when RSI recovers above a threshold; close it if RSI falls to an even more extreme oversold level, and, if reversal is enabled, open a short with protective stop and target. After a short entry, lock entries until RSI reaches an oversold reset level. Adjust its stop and target when RSI falls below a threshold; close it if RSI rises to an even more extreme overbought level, and, if reversal is enabled, open a long with protective stop and target. Near a position's target, modify its stop and target again to keep the position protected.
