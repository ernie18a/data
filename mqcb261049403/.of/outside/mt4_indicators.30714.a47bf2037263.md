On the chart timeframe, construct a USD index and a EUR index as separately scaled, geometrically weighted products of their respective currency-basket prices, using a configurable price representation. For each index, subtract a slow moving average from a fast moving average using the same configurable averaging method. When the current oscillator crosses from at or below zero to above zero, signal buying that index's currency; when it crosses from at or above zero to below zero, signal selling it. Evaluate the current, potentially incomplete bar against the preceding bar. Emit a new signal for an index only when its latest crossing direction differs from its previously alerted direction. The two currency signals are independent; no rule combines them or specifies an exit. Signals require the corresponding index calculation and chart signal display to be enabled, and at least one alert channel to be enabled.
