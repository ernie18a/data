Calculate a momentum oscillator as the difference between fast and slow moving averages whose periods adapt independently to the market’s estimated cycle. Compare it with adaptive upper, lower, and middle levels. In level mode, classify momentum as bullish above the upper level, bearish below the lower level, and neutral otherwise; in middle-line mode, classify it as bullish above the middle level and bearish below; in slope mode, classify it as bullish when rising and bearish when falling. A change to bullish signals long, a change to bearish signals short, and a change from either directional state to neutral signals an exit or retracement.
