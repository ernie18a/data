On the first tick of each bar, apply account-based exits before considering entries. If enabled, close the strategy's positions on the instrument when their summed floating profit is negative and its absolute value exceeds a configured percentage of the account-equity reference. Each time that reference is requested, reset it to current equity if there are no strategy positions; then replace it with previous equity if the reference is below previous equity, otherwise replace it with current equity, and record current equity as previous equity.

If the percentage profit target is enabled and nonzero, sum floating profit for the instrument's market orders in reverse order, starting at the total order count and stopping at index one. After each selected order, if the running sum reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument, regardless of strategy identifier.

If money trailing is enabled, the instrument's summed net floating profit is negative and below its configured profit threshold, and the strategy's summed floating loss exceeds the configured equity-risk percentage of the equity reference multiplied by the loss index, scan orders in the same index range. Accumulate absolute net profit for each selected market order on the instrument; whenever that running total reaches the configured money-loss amount, close all market orders and delete all pending orders for the instrument.

Entry is evaluated after exits, subject to sufficient history, the configured take-profit minimum, and free margin exceeding a configured multiple of base position size. The number of strategy positions must be below its limit. Buy when the absolute distance from bid to the close two bars ago exceeds the configured distance, the fast higher-timeframe linearly weighted moving average of typical price is above the slow one, and the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high. Sell when the corresponding ask-to-close distance exceeds the threshold, the fast average is below the slow one, and the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago. In live trading, additionally require the standard-deviation channel's earlier endpoint price to be below its later endpoint price for a buy, or above it for a sell; omit this filter in testing. The higher timeframe is selected from the chart timeframe. A qualifying buy branch takes precedence and ends entry evaluation even if its live channel filter or order checks fail.

Before submitting an entry, calculate the configured position size from the number of strategy positions; after more than one consecutive historical loss on the instrument, replace it with a configured fraction of free margin when that feature is enabled. Clamp and round size to the broker's volume rules. Submit only if account margin and stop-out checks, broker stop-distance checks, and trading permission pass.
