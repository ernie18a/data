Read an external command and its supplied stop-loss and take-profit prices on each cycle. A buy or sell command requests a long or short position, respectively; a close command requests an exit. Clear the command after processing it. There is no automatic market-based signal.

For an entry, require no open or pending orders anywhere in the account. Use the traded instrument's ask for a buy and bid for a sell. After rounding prices to instrument precision, require the take-profit distance from entry and the stop-loss distance from entry less the quoted spread each to be at least the broker's minimum stop distance, on the appropriate side of the entry. Permit entry only when trading is enabled, both configured timeframes are valid, the pause after initialization or a trade operation has elapsed, and current equity minus peak equity adjusted for the account reserve is at least current equity times the per-order risk fraction. Also require trading permission, a risk-sized order meeting the broker's minimum lot, and sufficient free margin. Calculate size from the equity risk budget and entry-to-stop distance using the instrument's tick value and margin specifications; round down to the permitted lot increment and cap at the maximum lot. Submit the requested direction with the supplied stop and target.

For a close command, if any account order exists, attempt to close the currently selected order at its close price for its full size. Selection scans orders in reverse and retains the last matching strategy identifier; if none matches, the last scanned order remains selected. This close attempt is independent of the entry gates. Submitted stop-loss and take-profit orders can also exit the position when their prices are reached.
