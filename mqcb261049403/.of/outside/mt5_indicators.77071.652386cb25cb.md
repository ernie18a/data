For each instrument, use closed bars and completed session ranges. During the London scan, use the completed Asian range; during the New York scan, use the completed London range. Start a candidate when price first sweeps beyond a range high or low by at least the configured ATR-scaled minimum, with the previous close on or inside that boundary. A high sweep sets short direction; a low sweep sets long direction. Require a confirmed pre-sweep swing level in the trade direction. Within the configured wait, require a directional candle to close beyond that swing level, with body displacement meeting the ATR-scaled minimum. Then require a strict three-candle fair-value gap in that direction, formed on the shift candle or within the allowed follow-up window. Invalidate if price breaches the frozen sweep extreme after the shift. After the gap forms, require a later closed bar to overlap its zone within the configured ATR tolerance and close beyond the zone midpoint in the trade direction; the gap-forming bar cannot count as its own retest. Score the completed sequence for session context, sweep depth, structure shift, displacement, gap quality, retest, and optionally elevated tick volume; require the score to meet the configured viability threshold. For a valid long, project entry at the retest close, stop below the sweep extreme with an ATR buffer, and targets above entry; for a short, reverse those placements. Ensure risk is within the configured ATR bounds, opposing session liquidity is in the profitable direction, and capped distance to that liquidity meets the minimum risk/reward requirement while leaving the first target before the second. Otherwise, issue no trade projection. The indicator reports projections only; it does not place or manage trades.
