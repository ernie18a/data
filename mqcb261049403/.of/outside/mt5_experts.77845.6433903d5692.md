Monitor positions and pending orders from selected other traders or strategies on the same account, excluding the framework’s own mirrors. Select sources by configurable identifier patterns. For each eligible source, create one mirror on the same instrument, either in the same direction or in the opposite direction. In inverse mode, map buy limit to sell stop, buy stop to sell limit, sell limit to buy stop, and sell stop to buy limit at the same entry price; skip stop-limit orders. Pending-order mirroring is optional.

Optionally copy protective levels: in inverse mode swap the source’s take-profit and stop-loss levels; otherwise retain them. Synchronize subsequent level changes and pending-order price and expiration changes. If partial-close synchronization is enabled and size is based on the source, reduce the mirror when the source shrinks. Close a mirror position when its source position disappears, and delete a mirror pending order when its source disappears. Do not reopen a mirror that has already closed independently. Existing sources at startup may optionally be ignored.

Use the configured same, fixed, or proportional position size, adjusted to the instrument’s permitted volume. Defer actions when the market is closed or broker restrictions prevent them, and retry failed actions. If a market entry with protective levels fails, retry without them and subsequently synchronize them.
