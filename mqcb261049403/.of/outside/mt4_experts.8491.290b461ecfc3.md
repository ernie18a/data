For each scheduled high-importance economic news event, wait for actual data. Compare it with the forecast when available; otherwise compare it with the previous value. If actual is higher, buy the associated currency against its mapped counterpart for EUR, GBP, AUD, or NZD events, and sell the mapped pair for USD, CHF, CAD, or JPY events. Reverse those directions when actual is lower. Make no trade on equality or if the required comparison values are unavailable. Trade each event at most once. Set position size according to the absolute percentage difference between actual and the comparison value, increasing size with the difference. Close positions at their stop or target, or after the configured holding period when timed closure is enabled.
