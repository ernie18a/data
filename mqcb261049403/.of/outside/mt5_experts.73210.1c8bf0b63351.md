At each new trading-timeframe bar while the instrument's broker session is open, calculate the strategy's aggregate realized result across all instruments since this instrument's daily bar opened, including profit, swap and commission, and add the floating profit and swap of all strategy positions. Divide that total by account balance plus credit minus the realized result. If the resulting return reaches an enabled daily profit limit, or its negative reaches an enabled daily loss limit, cancel pending orders and close positions for the current instrument and timeframe. Skip orders and positions inside the broker's freeze zone; retry on a later bar.
