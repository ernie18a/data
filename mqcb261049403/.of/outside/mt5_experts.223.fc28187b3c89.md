When the user enables the portfolio close rule, aggregate open-position profit and a point measure across all symbols. The point measure adds each short position’s opening price minus its current ask, and each long position’s current ask minus its opening price, with prices converted to points. In the selected mode, compare aggregate profit in account currency, aggregate profit as a percentage of account balance, or aggregate points against the configured upper and lower thresholds. If a nonzero upper threshold is met or exceeded, or a nonzero lower threshold is met or undershot, close all open positions and delete all pending orders.
