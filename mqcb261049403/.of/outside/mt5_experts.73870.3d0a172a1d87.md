At each configured daily reset, record the account’s balance or equity as the day-start reference and release any prior daily lock. Measure loss as the greater of zero and day-start reference minus current account equity. When loss reaches a configurable fraction of a daily loss limit (the limit may be a configurable percentage of the reference or a fixed monetary amount), close all account positions and cancel pending orders, then keep the account flat until the next reset. If flat enforcement is disabled, retry closing only for a limited period after the trigger. A manual lock also closes all positions and cancels pending orders; manual close-all does so without locking. After a manual unlock, do not trigger again until loss falls below a recovery threshold set as a fraction of the trigger.
