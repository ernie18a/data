Open a long when the Alligator lines are ordered upward, price breaks above a recent upper fractal, and the retracement test shows an upward move of at least the configured fixed range or volatility-based range. Open a short when the lines are ordered downward, price breaks below a recent lower fractal, and the retracement test shows a downward move of at least that range. Limit entries to the configured maximum number of open trades. Close positions at their initial stop loss or take profit; optionally move the stop beyond entry after a favorable move, trail it behind recent candle extremes or current price, and close all positions when the configured aggregate profit target, profit-percentage target, trailing-profit giveback, or account-equity loss limit is reached.
