On each tick and timer event, calculate daily account-wide P/L as the sum of profit, swap, and commission for all open market trades plus market trades closed since the start of the current server day. Scan closed history backward and stop at the first order closed before that day. If an enabled, positive daily loss limit is reached or exceeded, lock protection and stop automatic management; if closing on loss is enabled, close all matching open market trades. Otherwise, if an enabled, positive daily profit target is reached or exceeded, apply the same lock and, if closing on profit is enabled, close all matching open market trades. Loss protection takes precedence. Matching trades are restricted by the configured symbol scope, allowed directions, and manual, all-trades, or specific trade-identifier filter; the P/L calculation is not restricted by these filters. Protection is checked even when management is stopped. At the start of a new server day, clear the protection lock but do not automatically restart management. There are no entry decisions.
