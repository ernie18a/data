Form every two-pair combination sharing one currency from the selected, strength-ordered currencies, excluding configured skips. For pairs with the same base or the same quote, one side buys the first and sells the second; otherwise it buys both. The opposite side reverses both trades. Initially open each enabled side without a price signal.

For each side, calculate combined floating profit including swap and commission. Calculate each threshold from both legs' total lots and tick values, subtracting the combined spread value and absolute swap-plus-commission costs. The profit threshold uses a positive configured target; the loss threshold uses a negative configured target. The next-entry threshold uses a negative configured step multiplied by a progression based on the number of two-leg layers: linear, cumulative arithmetic, or cumulative powers of two.

In manual grid mode, add a two-leg layer when that side's combined profit reaches its next-entry loss threshold. Except in separate-side profit mode, add only if that side has at least as many orders as the opposite side. In automatic grid mode, when the losing side reaches its next-entry threshold, close the profitable opposite side if it has no more orders than the losing side; after the close, reopen the missing side. Require the configured confirmation count before closing.

In separate-side profit mode, close all of a side when its positive combined profit reaches its profit threshold. In basket profit mode, close both sides when their positive combined profit reaches the higher side profit threshold. Confirm profit conditions for the configured number of evaluations. Optionally close a losing side at its loss threshold after confirmation, either entirely or by closing its oldest order on each leg once the configured minimum layer count is reached.

In hybrid profit mode, compare the most profitable and least profitable eligible couples, excluding the first couple. With no loss grid, compare each side separately and close both selected sides when their combined profit reaches the higher of their profit thresholds. With a loss grid, compare whole-couple profits and close both couples when their combined profit reaches the higher side profit threshold of the least profitable couple.

Only open when trading and session controls permit, combined pair spread is acceptable, margin and order limits permit, and no relevant close is in progress. Repair unequal leg counts by opening missing orders, or close excess oldest orders if the order limit prevents repair. A configured immediate-stop mode closes all positions.
