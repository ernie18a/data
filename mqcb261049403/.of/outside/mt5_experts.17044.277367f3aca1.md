On the selected chart timeframe, form a reference line by averaging a smoothed median-price moving average and a simple median-price moving average. Find the latest candle that crosses this line: an upward cross is marked at that candle’s low, and a downward cross at its high. Classify price as bullish when the current close is above the marked price, bearish when below. Independently determine trend direction from a fast typical-price moving average and a slower smoothed median-price moving average, using their recent relative highs and lows and whether their spread is widening or narrowing. Go long only when price and trend are bullish, the current candle closes above its open, its low is below the fast average, that average is rising, and the current close’s percentage distance above the previous daily close is greater than the prior candle’s. Go short under the mirrored conditions: bearish price and trend, a down candle whose high is above the fast average, a falling fast average, and a decreasing percentage distance from the previous daily close. Open a signal when there is no position in that direction and the most recent close was not in the current hour. If an opposite position exists, either open the new direction and close the opposite position when opposite-signal closing is enabled, or open the new direction while retaining the opposite position when disabled. When trailing stops are disabled, close a profitable position if the trend reverses. When enabled, trail the stop with the market and adjust the take-profit accordingly; initial trades also receive protective stop-loss and take-profit levels.
