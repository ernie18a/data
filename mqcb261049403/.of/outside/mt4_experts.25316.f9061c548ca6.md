Open a long when the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the second-previous bar’s low is below the previous bar’s high, and the current bid is farther from the second-previous close than the configured retracement threshold. Open a short under the same candle-overlap condition when the fast average is below the slow one and the current ask is farther from the previous close than that threshold. In live trading, also require the drawn trend-line slope filters: for a long, both white lines slope upward and the blue line slopes downward; for a short, both white lines slope downward and the yellow line slopes upward. In testing, omit these line filters. Manage open positions with their attached stop-loss and take-profit; move stops to lock in gains after a favorable move, and trail stops using recent candle extremes or price movement. Close positions when enabled account-currency or balance-percentage profit targets are reached, when the monetary trailing rule gives back its allowed amount from peak profit, or when the configured equity drawdown limit is exceeded.
