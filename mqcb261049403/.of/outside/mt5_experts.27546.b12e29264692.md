On each new bar during the configured trading window, open a buy when the entry RSI is above its threshold and rising, provided the open-buy limit, minimum price spacing from the previous entry, and margin checks allow it. Close all buys when all configured exit signals hold: RSI is below its threshold, the stochastic signal is below its overbought level, and the previous bar’s high is above the upper Bollinger Band. Outside the trading window, close buy and sell baskets only when their combined profit is positive. No short entries or options trades are defined.
