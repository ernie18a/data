On each new bar, use the chart instrument’s data to manage all positions sharing the configured identifier, including positions on other instruments. Outside the configured trading hours, if the chart instrument’s combined position profit plus swap is positive, close all such long and short positions. During trading hours, if the chart instrument has an open long, close all such long positions when every selected exit condition is true; an empty selection is true. The independently selectable exit conditions are: the current simple moving average is above the current exponential moving average; the current longer exponential moving average is above each of the preceding bars’ highs over a configured lookback; the previous bar’s high is above its upper Bollinger band; current negative directional movement exceeds positive directional movement; the current stochastic signal is below its threshold; current Williams %R is below its threshold; current MACD main is below its signal; current Ichimoku span B is above span A; and current RSI is below its threshold. Indicator periods, thresholds, and the selection of conditions are configurable. Evaluate the exit after any new entry on that bar.
