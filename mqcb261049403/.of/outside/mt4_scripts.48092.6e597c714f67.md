For each open position, skip it unless it is profitable, meets the enabled symbol, side, identifier, and comment filters, and has reached the configured minimum profit in points. Round the configured offset from the entry price to the instrument’s tick size. For a long position, when the bid reaches or exceeds entry plus the offset and the existing stop is below that level, move the stop to entry plus the offset. For a short position, when the ask reaches or falls below entry minus the offset and the existing stop is above that level or absent, move the stop to entry minus the offset.
