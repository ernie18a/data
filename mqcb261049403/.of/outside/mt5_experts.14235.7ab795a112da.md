During permitted weekday trading sessions, when flat, trade only if the latest trade volume is within the configured range and the bid–ask spread is below its limit. If the latest trade price is at least a configured deviation above the best ask, place a buy limit at the ask; if it is at least that deviation below the best bid, place a sell limit at the bid. For an open long, exit when trading is no longer permitted or when the stored entry reference is below the current bid, or both the stored entry reference and the previously recorded trade price are at or below the bid. For an open short, exit when trading is no longer permitted or when the stored entry reference is above the current ask, or both the stored entry reference and previously recorded trade price are at or above the ask. Submit exits as opposite-side limit orders at the current best quote; cancel a pending entry or exit if that quote changes before the position changes.
