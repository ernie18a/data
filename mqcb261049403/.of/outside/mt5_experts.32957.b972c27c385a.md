Use a configurable economic calendar source and optionally restrict events to either currency in the traded symbol. For each enabled low, medium, high, or nonfarm-payroll category, block all trading and routine position management during its configured interval before and after an event. If intervals overlap, use the highest-priority category: nonfarm payrolls, high, medium, then low. When that category's close setting is enabled, close the strategy's positions in the traded instrument. For the Investing calendar, events are first filtered by their enabled impact category, including nonfarm-payroll events.

When account-wide closing is selected and trading is not blocked by news, compare total account floating profit with configured money thresholds or with thresholds expressed as a percentage of account equity. If the loss threshold is exceeded and the strategy has at least one position in the traded instrument, close all account positions. If the profit threshold is exceeded and the strategy has more than one position in the traded instrument, close all account positions.
