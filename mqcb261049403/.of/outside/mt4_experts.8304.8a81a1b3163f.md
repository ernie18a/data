On each new bar, derive a slow trend from major swing pivots and track the latest minor swing pivot. In an uptrend, signal long when a weighted candle price rises at least one spread above the latest minor swing low; in a downtrend, signal short when it falls at least one spread below the latest minor swing high. Cancel the pending signal if price returns to the pivot. Use these signals from a higher timeframe for entries; an optional reverse mode swaps long and short. Allow entries only during the daily entry window, subject to the open-order cap. Close open positions when an opposite entry signal appears, optionally subject to a holding-time rule. The separate close-indicator output is read but does not affect trading decisions.
