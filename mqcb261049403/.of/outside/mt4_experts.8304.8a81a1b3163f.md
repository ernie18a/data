On each new chart bar, calculate signals from the chart instrument on a configured higher timeframe. For each of a short and a long lookback, scan historical bars from old to new for rolling lows and highs. Accept a new extreme only when it differs from the preceding rolling extreme and the bar's corresponding low or high is within the current spread of it. Within a configured backstep, remove earlier, less extreme candidates. Consolidate consecutive same-side candidates by retaining the most extreme. In the consolidation pass, process long-lookback candidates only on bars that have a short-lookback candidate; when comparing consecutive long-lookback lows for removal, compare the previous long low with the current short low. A long-lookback low sets the trend up and a long-lookback high sets it down. For each bar use (5 × close + 2 × open + high + low) / 9 as its representative price. After a short-lookback low, issue a buy signal on the first later non-pivot bar whose representative price rises at least one current spread above that pivot's representative price while the long trend is up. After a short-lookback high, issue a sell signal on the first later non-pivot bar whose representative price falls at least one spread below that pivot's representative price while the long trend is down. Remove an issued signal if price subsequently returns to or beyond its pivot price; reset signal eligibility at each new short pivot. Read these signals at the configured historical offset. After any entry attempts, inspect the first open account order, regardless of instrument. If it is a buy and the sell signal remains, or it is a sell and the buy signal remains, attempt to close every open market order across all instruments. Depending on the configured closing mode, either close immediately or only when the order's opening time maps to a bar older than the current bar on the configured timeframe. Entry attempts can clear signal flags before this exit test: in normal mode a buy attempt clears the buy signal and a sell attempt clears the sell signal; in reverse mode a buy-signal attempt opens a sell and clears the sell signal, while a sell-signal attempt opens a buy and clears the buy signal. Entries are attempted only while the daily entry flag is set and the total account order count is below the configured limit; an attempt clears the daily flag, which is reset during the configured early-day window.
