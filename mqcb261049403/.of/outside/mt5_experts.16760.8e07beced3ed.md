On each tick, if liquidation is not already active, do nothing when total account floating profit is negative. Otherwise, activate liquidation when either the absolute total floating profit as a percentage of account balance is at least the configured percentage threshold, or, in currency mode, the absolute total floating profit strictly exceeds the configured currency threshold. Once activated, close every open long and short position across all instruments, retrying on subsequent ticks regardless of current profit until no positions remain. Then reset liquidation mode.
