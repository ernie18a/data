Use the traded instrument’s current and previous bars to detect a parabolic SAR crossing: a move from above the previous close to below the current close signals long; the opposite signals short. An optional reverse setting swaps the directions. Scale the configured entry thresholds, stop loss, take profit, and trailing distance by ten when EURUSD has five decimal digits; otherwise leave them unchanged. Before closing anything, check whether any account order has the strategy’s identifier; if so, block new entries for this tick. On a signal, optionally close the first account order if its direction opposes the intended trade, regardless of its instrument or identifier. For a potential long, calculate and truncate to an integer the current bid minus the current bar’s low multiplied by the traded instrument’s point size. For a potential short, similarly truncate the current bar’s high minus the current ask multiplied by point size. Enter only if the relevant result is greater than the scaled minimum threshold and no greater than the scaled maximum threshold, unless the maximum threshold is zero, which disables that upper bound. Attach the scaled stop loss and take profit to each new trade. If trailing is enabled, apply it to every market order on the traded instrument: use the greater of the scaled trailing distance and the broker’s minimum stop distance, and move the stop only in a favorable direction relative to the current bid for longs or ask for shorts.
