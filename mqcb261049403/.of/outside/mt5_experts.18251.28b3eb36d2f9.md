On each tick, calculate current smoothed moving averages of median price for USDJPY using the configured shorter and longer periods, timeframe, and shift. The initialization assigns every moving-average handle to USDJPY, so this same comparison drives all four pairs. For each of EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF, and USDJPY with no position belonging to this strategy, go long if the shorter average is strictly above the longer average, or short if it is strictly below. Do nothing on equality.

Before trading, calculate a profit distance from the average high minus the average low over the configured number of completed four-hour bars of the chart instrument, converted to its adjusted points and floored at the configured minimum. If the required history or chart point size is unavailable, skip the tick.

If at least one of the four pairs has exactly one strategy position at the start of the tick, check all strategy positions in these pairs. Exit a long when its bid is strictly more than the profit distance above its entry, or a short when its ask is strictly more than that distance below its entry; convert the distance using each position's adjusted point size. Also exit all such positions if account equity minus balance reaches the configured profit percentage of balance or falls to the negative of the configured loss percentage of balance. Entry checks occur before these exits, using position counts captured at the start of the tick.
