For each of EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCHF, and USDJPY, open a position only when none is already open: buy when the sum of the four pairs’ short-period smoothed median-price moving averages exceeds the sum of their long-period averages and that pair’s short-period average exceeds its long-period average; sell when the aggregate comparison and that pair’s comparison are both reversed. Close an open buy when its bid is sufficiently above its entry price, or an open sell when its ask is sufficiently below entry; the favorable-move threshold is based on the average high-minus-low range of the most recent 50 completed four-hour bars of the chart’s symbol, with a minimum floor. Also close all positions in these four pairs when account equity rises above, or falls below, the current balance by the configured percentage threshold.
