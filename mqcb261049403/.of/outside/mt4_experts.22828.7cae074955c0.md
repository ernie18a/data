On each new bar, evaluate these enabled exits in order:
1. Percentage profit target: Iterate the open-order list backward from the position after its last valid index down to index 1. Accumulate gross profit from market positions on the traded instrument, regardless of strategy identifier. After each iteration, if the accumulated profit reaches the configured percentage of account balance, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on that instrument.
2. Money-trailing loss exit: Sum profit, commission, and swap for market positions on the instrument using the same order-list indices. If this sum is negative and the strategy's gross profit on the instrument is negative with absolute loss greater than a configured fraction of the account-equity reference multiplied by a loss factor, iterate those indices again. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position on the instrument; once it reaches the configured money-loss amount, close all market positions and delete all pending orders on the instrument.
3. Equity stop: If the strategy's gross profit on the instrument is negative and its absolute loss exceeds a configured fraction of the account-equity reference, close all of that strategy's market positions on the instrument.

Whenever the equity reference is requested, first set it to current account equity if the strategy has no market positions on the instrument. Then, if the reference is below the previously recorded equity, replace it with that previous equity; otherwise replace it with current equity. Finally record current equity as the previous equity. The previous equity and reference start at zero.
