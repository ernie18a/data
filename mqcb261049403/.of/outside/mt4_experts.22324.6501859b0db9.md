On every tick, if basket protection is enabled, sum net profit including swap and commission for this strategy's open positions in the traded instrument. Close those positions and stop processing the tick when profit reaches a configured fraction of account balance, loss reaches an emergency fraction of balance, or loss reaches a configured fraction of balance with at least a configured number of positions.

If money trailing is enabled and the strategy has open positions, calculate net profit across all market positions in the instrument. In the loss branch, when that total is negative and the strategy's gross loss exceeds a configured fraction of its tracked account equity multiplied by a loss factor, iterate the instrument's orders from the end while skipping the first order. Accumulate the absolute net profit of each market position; update the stored trailing value to the absolute total loss when that loss is no greater than the stored value. Close all instrument orders once the accumulated amount reaches the absolute difference between the stored value and the configured money stop.

On each new bar, if the equity stop is enabled and the strategy's gross loss exceeds a configured fraction of tracked account equity, close its positions in the instrument. The equity tracker resets to current equity when there are no strategy positions; otherwise, if its stored value is below the previously observed equity, replace it with that previous equity, and in all other cases replace it with current equity. Then store current equity as the previous observation. Processing can continue to a new entry after an equity-stop closure.

Entries require sufficient history, trading permission, sufficient account free margin, fewer than the configured maximum strategy positions, valid stop distances, and sufficient margin after the proposed trade. On the new bar, the previous candle must be bullish with a larger body than the preceding bearish candle. Scan fractals from the oldest bar in the configured lookback toward the present; the signal is valid only if a lower fractal is encountered before a bar containing both upper and lower fractals. Buy if the bid is sufficiently far from the close two bars ago, the current fast linear-weighted typical-price average exceeds the slow one, the low two bars ago is below the previous high, and current monthly RSI exceeds the prior monthly RSI. Otherwise sell if the corresponding ask distance is sufficient, the fast average is below the slow one, the previous low is below the high two bars ago, and current monthly RSI is below the prior monthly RSI. Attach configured price-based stop-loss and take-profit levels.
