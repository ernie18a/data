On each new trading-timeframe bar, use the latest completed Bollinger-timeframe candle. Buy when it opens below the lower Bollinger band and closes above it, the daily DEMA is rising across its latest three values, and the long-period EMA on the Bollinger timeframe rose from its prior completed value. Sell when the candle opens above the upper Bollinger band and closes below it, the daily DEMA is falling across its latest three values, and that EMA fell from its prior completed value. Close open long positions when a candle opens above the upper band and closes below it; close open short positions when a candle opens below the lower band and closes above it.
