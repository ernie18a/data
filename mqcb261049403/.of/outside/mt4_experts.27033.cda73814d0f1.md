On each new bar, calculate each of eight major currencies’ strength as the average of its RSI readings against the other seven, complementing pair readings when the currency is quoted. Mark a currency strongest or weakest only when its score is strictly greater or less than every other score. For a charted pair, allow a long only when its base is strongest and quote weakest, the fast typical-price weighted moving average is above the slow one, the current bid is farther from the prior close than a candle-range threshold, and the second-prior low is below the prior high. Allow a short when the current ask is farther from the prior close than that threshold, the same fast-above-slow condition holds, and the prior low is below the second-prior high; the short entry does not require a currency-strength signal. Close the EA’s symbol basket when its combined profit reaches a balance-based profit target or loss limit, or when its loss exceeds a fraction of the running peak equity. Optional money management also closes the symbol’s trades at a money profit target or after profit retreats from its recorded peak by a money amount; in a losing basket it closes after the configured equity-relative loss condition and loss retreat condition are met.
