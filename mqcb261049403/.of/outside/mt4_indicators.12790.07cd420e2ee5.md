For each supported traded pair, calculate the same selected indicator on three pairs at the selected timeframe: the traded pair and the following two pairs, in order: EURUSD: EURJPY, USDJPY; EURJPY: EURUSD, USDJPY; GBPUSD: GBPJPY, USDJPY; GBPJPY: GBPUSD, USDJPY; AUDUSD: AUDJPY, USDJPY; USDCAD: CADJPY, USDJPY; USDCHF: CHFJPY, USDJPY; USDJPY: EURUSD, EURJPY. Use the main line for MACD, stochastic, and RVI. Center stochastic and RSI on their midpoint and DeMarker on its midpoint; leave CCI and RVI unchanged. Scale MACD values for JPY-quoted pairs by dividing by 100. Call the three resulting values A, B, and C in the order listed. For a USD-quoted traded pair, compute base strength as (A+B)/2 and quote strength as (-A+C)/2. For a JPY-quoted pair other than USDJPY, use (A+B)/2 and (-A-C)/2. For USDJPY, use (A-B)/2 and (-A-C)/2. For a USD-base pair other than USDJPY, use (A+C)/2 and (-A+B)/2. When alerts are enabled after initialization, signal long when base strength is strictly above quote strength and was strictly below it on the preceding bar; signal short for the opposite strict crossover. Emit at most one signal per current chart bar. No exit condition is specified.
