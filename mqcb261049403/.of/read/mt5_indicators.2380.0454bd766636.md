Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period smoothing of RSI of a selected bar price using a selected smoothing method.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period smoothing of the fast line using the same smoothing method.
Long: Chart-timeframe fast line > chart-timeframe slow line.
Short: Chart-timeframe fast line < chart-timeframe slow line.
Long: Higher-timeframe fast line > higher-timeframe slow line OR higher-timeframe fast line = higher-timeframe slow line AND the most recent unequal comparison of those lines on a preceding higher-timeframe bar was fast line > slow line; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Short: Higher-timeframe fast line < higher-timeframe slow line OR higher-timeframe fast line = higher-timeframe slow line AND the most recent unequal comparison of those lines on a preceding higher-timeframe bar was fast line < slow line; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
