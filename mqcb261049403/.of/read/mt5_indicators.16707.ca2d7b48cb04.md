Calculation: Rounded RSI = RSI of the selected bar price, rounded toward the RSI scale midpoint to a selectable increment; RSI at the midpoint remains unchanged.
Long: Chart-timeframe rounded RSI > upper threshold.
Short: Chart-timeframe rounded RSI < lower threshold.
Long: Rounded RSI of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > upper threshold.
Short: Rounded RSI of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < lower threshold.
