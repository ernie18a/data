Calculation: MACD = fast EMA of close minus slow EMA of close; its signal-line value is not used.
Calculation: Stochastic main line = the main line of the stochastic oscillator; Stochastic signal line = its signal line.
Calculation: MACD trough pair = On the configured MACD timeframe, the current trough is MACD[2] below −threshold, with MACD[1], MACD[3], and MACD[4] above MACD[2], and all MACD[1..4] below −threshold. Find the first current price trough at shift k=0..15 where Low[k] is below Low[k+1..k+4]. Search older shifts i from 6 onward for a trough centered at i−2, requiring i>10, MACD[i−2] below MACD[i−3], MACD[i−4], MACD[i], and MACD[i−1], and MACD[i..i−4] below −threshold. Accept the first such older trough, then only successively lower MACD troughs; for each, find the first price trough at j=i−4..i+15 where Low[j] is below Low[j+1..j+4]. Accept a pair only at depth ≤18 and when (older MACD trough > current MACD trough AND older price trough < current price trough) OR (older MACD trough < current MACD trough AND older price trough > current price trough).
Calculation: MACD peak pair = On the configured MACD timeframe, the current peak is MACD[2] above threshold, with MACD[1], MACD[3], and MACD[4] below MACD[2], and all MACD[1..4] above threshold. Find the first current price peak at shift k=0..15 where High[k] is above High[k+1..k+4]. Search older shifts i from 6 onward for a peak centered at i−2, requiring i>10, MACD[i−2] above MACD[i−3], MACD[i−4], MACD[i], and MACD[i−1], and MACD[i..i−4] above threshold. Accept the first such older peak, then only successively higher MACD peaks; for each, find the first price peak at j=i−4..i+15 where High[j] is above High[j+1..j+4]. Accept a pair only at depth ≤18 and when (older MACD peak > current MACD peak AND older price peak < current price peak) OR (older MACD peak < current MACD peak AND older price peak > current price peak).
Long: MACD trough pair AND Stochastic main line > Stochastic signal line AND the stochastic main line is within its configured lower and upper bounds over the configured lookback, allowing the configured number of exceptions.
Short: MACD peak pair AND Stochastic main line < Stochastic signal line AND the stochastic main line is within its configured lower and upper bounds over the configured lookback, allowing the configured number of exceptions.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − configured stop distance OR high >= entry price + configured target distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR low <= entry price − configured target distance.
Exit: long, low <= 2 × entry price − upper Bollinger band sampled at entry OR high >= upper Bollinger band sampled at entry.
Exit: short, high >= 2 × entry price − lower Bollinger band sampled at entry OR low <= lower Bollinger band sampled at entry.
