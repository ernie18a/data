Calculation: Oscillators = enabled MACD line, MACD histogram, Wilder RSI, smoothed stochastic %K, close-based CCI, close momentum as a difference, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, and close-based Money Flow Index; use volume. MACD and volume-weighted MACD subtract the longer average from the shorter average; the MACD histogram subtracts the EMA signal from the MACD line. Smoothed stochastic is the SMA of raw %K. Close-based CCI uses the mean absolute deviation of close; close-based Money Flow Index uses close times volume. Chaikin Money Flow is unavailable if any bar in its window has zero range. An oscillator value is unavailable when its required inputs or denominator are unavailable.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a low or high of the selected source, close or low/high respectively, strictly below or above every value in the preceding pivot-span bars and below or above or equal to every value in the following pivot-span bars. A pivot becomes available after the following bars have elapsed.
Calculation: Divergence candidates = for each enabled oscillator and enabled divergence kind, compare the endpoint with each of the most recent eligible confirmed pivots of the corresponding side, newest first, within the maximum pivot count and maximum bar distance, and farther from the decision bar than the minimum pivot distance. The endpoint is the decision bar when confirmation is off and the preceding bar when confirmation is on. A candidate requires available oscillator values at both endpoints and, at every intervening bar, both oscillator and close at or above their respective straight lines joining their endpoint values for bullish candidates, or at or below those lines for bearish candidates. The price comparison uses the selected pivot source, but the path always uses close. With confirmation on, that oscillator or close must also move in the candidate's direction from the endpoint to the decision bar. Count at most one candidate per oscillator and divergence kind, using the first qualifying pivot; the total count includes both sides and all enabled kinds.
Long: Total divergence candidate count >= minimum count AND at least one bullish candidate has either endpoint price < pivot low AND endpoint oscillator > oscillator at pivot, or endpoint price > pivot low AND endpoint oscillator < oscillator at pivot.
Short: Total divergence candidate count >= minimum count AND at least one bearish candidate has either endpoint price > pivot high AND endpoint oscillator < oscillator at pivot, or endpoint price < pivot high AND endpoint oscillator > oscillator at pivot.
