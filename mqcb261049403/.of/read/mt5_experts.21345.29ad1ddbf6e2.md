Calculation: Pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the previous completed chart bar
Long: AO on the current, still-forming chart bar > 0 AND CCI on the current, still-forming chart bar >= 0 AND current bar close > Pivot AND (AO on the previous completed chart bar < 0 OR CCI on the previous completed chart bar <= 0 OR close of the last completed bar on the higher timeframe < Pivot) AND absolute open-price differences for each consecutive pair among the current and previous five chart bars < the single-bar jump threshold AND absolute open-price differences for each pair two chart bars apart among those bars < the two-bar jump threshold
Short: AO on the current, still-forming chart bar > 0 AND CCI on the current, still-forming chart bar >= 0 AND current bar close > Pivot AND (AO on the previous completed chart bar < 0 OR CCI on the previous completed chart bar <= 0 OR close of the last completed bar on the higher timeframe < Pivot) AND absolute open-price differences for each consecutive pair among the current and previous five chart bars < the single-bar jump threshold AND absolute open-price differences for each pair two chart bars apart among those bars < the two-bar jump threshold
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR (highest price since entry - entry price > trailing-stop distance + trailing-step distance AND low <= highest price since entry - trailing-stop distance)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR (entry price - lowest price since entry > trailing-stop distance + trailing-step distance AND high >= lowest price since entry + trailing-stop distance)
