Calculation: Fine-tuned candle color = compute weighted open and close on indicator-timeframe bars (the indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe). For each lookback bar h, where x=h/(N−1) and h=0 is the current bar, calculate weight w[h] as the product of (shift1 + x^rank1 × (1−shift1)), (shift2 + (1−x)^rank2 × (1−shift2)), and (shift3 + (1−|2x−1|)^rank3 × (1−shift3)); normalize weights to sum to 1. Weighted open and close are the sums of each bar's open and close multiplied by its normalized weight. If the absolute difference between the current bar's raw open and close is at most the configured gap, replace weighted open with the previous indicator candle's close. Color is 2 if adjusted open < weighted close, 0 if adjusted open > weighted close, and 1 if equal. Apply this to the signal bar and the immediately older bar on the indicator timeframe; the signal bar is completed when its configured shift is at least 1, and still forming when the shift is 0.
Long: signal-bar color = 2 AND older-bar color ≠ 2
Short: signal-bar color = 0 AND older-bar color ≠ 0
Exit: long, signal-bar color = 0 OR low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, signal-bar color = 2 OR high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
