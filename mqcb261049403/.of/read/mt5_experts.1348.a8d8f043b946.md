Calculation: Momentum = selected price minus the selected price a lookback ago.
Calculation: Adaptive momentum = Kaufman adaptive moving average of momentum, using an efficiency-ratio lookback, a shorter fast smoothing period, a longer slow smoothing period, and a configurable smoothing-constant exponent. Efficiency ratio is the absolute momentum change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar momentum changes over that lookback plus a small positive constant. The smoothing constant is (slow EMA coefficient + efficiency ratio × (fast EMA coefficient − slow EMA coefficient)) raised to the exponent. Initialize the average with the previous bar's momentum at the first bar with sufficient history, then update it as previous average + smoothing constant × (current momentum − previous average).
Long: Chart-timeframe adaptive momentum > 0 AND chart-timeframe adaptive momentum > its previous value.
Short: Chart-timeframe adaptive momentum < 0 AND chart-timeframe adaptive momentum < its previous value.
Long: The last completed adaptive momentum on the selected indicator timeframe > its previous value AND that previous value < the value before it. The indicator timeframe may be the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe, or a lower timeframe.
Short: The last completed adaptive momentum on the selected indicator timeframe < its previous value AND that previous value > the value before it. The indicator timeframe may be the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe, or a lower timeframe.
Exit: long, the last completed adaptive momentum on the selected indicator timeframe < its previous value OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the last completed adaptive momentum on the selected indicator timeframe > its previous value OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
