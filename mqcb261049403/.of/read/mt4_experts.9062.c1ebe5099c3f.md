Calculation: RSX = Apply three successive stages to both the change in 100 × (high + low) and its absolute value. Each stage updates a = (1 − α) × a + α × input, then b = α × a + (1 − α) × b, and outputs 1.5 × a − 0.5 × b; α = 3 / (length + 2), and all stage states start at zero. After the warm-up of max(length − 1, 5) bars containing a price change, RSX is 50 × (1 + signed output / absolute output), clipped to [0, 100], when the absolute output exceeds a small positive tolerance; otherwise it is 50.
Calculation: Short-timeframe RSX and long-timeframe RSX = RSX of the last completed bars of two timeframes, with the long timeframe higher than the short timeframe. Each timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long stop = At entry, entry price minus a stop distance, or the lowest low over a lookback including the forming long-timeframe bar, or the low of a specified earlier long-timeframe bar. For either bar-based mode, use entry price minus the stop distance if the selected low is closer to entry than the minimum buffer.
Calculation: Short stop = At entry, entry price plus a stop distance, or the highest high over a lookback including the forming long-timeframe bar, or the high of a specified earlier long-timeframe bar. For either bar-based mode, use entry price plus the stop distance if the selected high is closer to entry than the minimum buffer.
Calculation: Long target = At entry, entry price plus a target distance, or the lowest low over a lookback including the forming long-timeframe bar, or the low of a specified earlier long-timeframe bar. For either bar-based mode, use entry price plus the target distance if the selected low is less than entry price plus the minimum buffer.
Calculation: Short target = At entry, entry price minus a target distance, or the highest high over a lookback including the forming long-timeframe bar, or the high of a specified earlier long-timeframe bar. For either bar-based mode, use entry price minus the target distance if the selected high is greater than entry price minus the minimum buffer.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = At each chart bar open, select open minus a trailing distance, or the lowest low over a chart-bar lookback including the forming bar, or the low of a specified earlier chart bar. When open is above entry price and the selected level is below open by more than the minimum buffer, plus the trailing minimum distance for either bar-based mode, raise the existing stop to the selected level if it is higher; otherwise retain the existing stop.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = At each chart bar open, select open plus a trailing distance, or the highest high over a chart-bar lookback including the forming bar, or the high of a specified earlier chart bar. When open is below entry price and the selected level is above open by more than the minimum buffer, plus the trailing minimum distance for either bar-based mode, lower the existing stop to the selected level if it is lower; otherwise retain the existing stop.
Long: Long-timeframe RSX > long buy threshold AND short-timeframe RSX < short buy threshold.
Short: Long-timeframe RSX < long sell threshold AND short-timeframe RSX > short sell threshold.
Long: Long-timeframe RSX > long buy threshold AND short-timeframe RSX < short buy threshold AND long-timeframe RSX > its value at an earlier completed long-timeframe bar.
Short: Long-timeframe RSX < long sell threshold AND short-timeframe RSX > short sell threshold AND long-timeframe RSX < its value at an earlier completed long-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, long-timeframe RSX < long sell threshold AND short-timeframe RSX > short sell threshold.
Exit: short, long-timeframe RSX > long buy threshold AND short-timeframe RSX < short buy threshold.
Exit: long, long-timeframe RSX < long sell threshold AND short-timeframe RSX > short sell threshold AND long-timeframe RSX < its value at an earlier completed long-timeframe bar.
Exit: short, long-timeframe RSX > long buy threshold AND short-timeframe RSX < short buy threshold AND long-timeframe RSX > its value at an earlier completed long-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, low <= enabled long stop OR high >= enabled long target OR low <= enabled long trailing stop.
Exit: short, high >= enabled short stop OR low <= enabled short target OR high >= enabled short trailing stop.
