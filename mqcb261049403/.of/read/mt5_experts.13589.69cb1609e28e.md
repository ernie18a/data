Long: On the chart timeframe, or on a configurable higher or lower timeframe using a selected completed bar or the bar still forming, the shorter-period moving average on the preceding bar < the longer-period moving average on the preceding bar AND the shorter-period moving average on the selected bar > the longer-period moving average on the selected bar AND the selected bar's close > its open.
Short: On the chart timeframe, or on a configurable higher or lower timeframe using a selected completed bar or the bar still forming, the shorter-period moving average on the preceding bar > the longer-period moving average on the preceding bar AND the shorter-period moving average on the selected bar < the longer-period moving average on the selected bar AND the selected bar's close < its open.
Exit: long, the most recent long or short signal at or before the selected signal bar is short OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the most recent long or short signal at or before the selected signal bar is long OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
