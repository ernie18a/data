Calculation: Trailing stop = entry price minus the stop distance for a long, or entry price plus the stop distance for a short. If trailing is enabled, increase the long stop by one trailing step when price - stop > stop distance + trailing step; decrease the short stop by one trailing step when stop - price > stop distance + trailing step.

Short: Trend state = bearish.
