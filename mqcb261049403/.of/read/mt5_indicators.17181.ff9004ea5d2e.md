Calculation: Input prices = three independently selected prices from open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, trend-biased extreme price, or the same prices calculated from Heikin Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise; trend-biased extreme price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Sort the three selected prices on each bar into low, middle, and high.
Calculation: Recursive smoothed stochastic = on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe, calculate 100 × (middle − rolling minimum low)/(rolling maximum high − rolling minimum low), using 0 when the denominator is 0. Smooth with a selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average. Apply the same rolling normalization to the smoothed result using its own rolling minimum and maximum, then smooth again with the same method and period. Repeat the whole calculation for a selected depth, using the preceding result as low, middle, and high. Both normalizations and every recursion use the same lookback. Use available history for incomplete lookbacks; initialize exponential averages with the first input and smoothed averages with their second input.
Calculation: Upper, lower, and middle levels = either fixed upper and lower levels and their midpoint; or the rolling stochastic minimum plus the upper or lower percentage of its rolling range and their midpoint; or the upper and lower rolling stochastic quantiles and the quantile at the midpoint of their percentages. Quantiles sort the lookback values and linearly interpolate at index (lookback − 1) × percentage/100, padding unavailable history with zeros.
Calculation: Directional state = in slope mode, up when the stochastic rises, down when it falls, and otherwise the previous state; in middle-level mode, up above the middle level, down below it, and neutral at equality; in outer-level mode, up above the upper level, down below the lower level, neutral strictly between them, and otherwise the previous state. The initial state is neutral.
Long: Directional state = up
Short: Directional state = down
