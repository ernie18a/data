Calculation: Bounce strength index = For each bar, find the highest high H and lowest low L over a range lookback including that bar, and set R = max(H − L, minimum price increment). Set weight W to 1 when volume weighting is disabled; otherwise set W to the bar's volume divided by the maximum volume over the same range lookback, or 1 if that maximum is zero. Set floor contribution to (close − low) × (1 if low = L, otherwise (H − low) / R) × W when close >= previous close − 0.2 × (high − low), otherwise 0. Set ceiling contribution to −(high − close) × (1 if high = H, otherwise (high − L) / R) × W when close <= previous close + 0.2 × (high − low), otherwise 0. Sum the floor and ceiling contributions over a slowing lookback, divide by the sum of R over that lookback, and multiply by 100. The index is the simple moving average of that result over an averaging lookback. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe, use its bar still forming at the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe interval and hold that value until the next interval. Compare the index with its previous bar on the timeframe on which it is calculated.
Long: Bounce strength index > previous bounce strength index
