Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over a lookback ending at the evaluated bar.
Long: Chart bar close > chart-timeframe range midpoint.
Short: Chart bar close < chart-timeframe range midpoint.
Long: For each independently selected higher or lower timeframe, the selected bar's close > its timeframe's range midpoint. The selected bar may be the still-forming bar or a completed bar at a configurable lag.
Short: For each independently selected higher or lower timeframe, the selected bar's close < its timeframe's range midpoint. The selected bar may be the still-forming bar or a completed bar at a configurable lag.
