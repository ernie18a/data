Calculation: Volume-weighted oscillator = moving average of ((Williams %R + 50) × volume), using the same smoothing method and length as the moving average of volume.
Calculation: Direction = rising when the volume-weighted oscillator is greater than its previous bar, falling when it is less, and otherwise the previous direction.
Long: Volume-weighted oscillator > positive threshold × moving average of volume OR direction = rising.
Short: Volume-weighted oscillator < negative threshold × moving average of volume OR direction = falling.
Long: The same long condition calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Short: The same short condition calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
