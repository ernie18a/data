Calculation: Applied price = configurable selection of open, high, low, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, close, high if close>open and low if close<open and close otherwise, or (high+close)/2 if close>open and (low+close)/2 if close<open and close otherwise.
Calculation: Forecast oscillator = 100*(applied price-W)/W, where W = 6/(L*(L+1))*sum over i=1..L of (i-(L+1)/3)*applied price at bar t-L+i, and L is the lookback length.
Calculation: Signal = -b^3*E6+3*(b^2+b^3)*E5-3*(2*b^2+b+b^3)*E4+(1+3*b+3*b^2+b^3)*E3, where E1 through E6 are six successive exponential smoothings of the forecast oscillator, each updated as E = alpha*input+(1-alpha)*previous E, alpha=2/(1+(1+0.5*(T-1))), T is the smoothing length, b is a smoothing parameter, and all six previous E values start at zero when calculation begins at bar index L+2.
Long: On the just-completed chart bar, forecast oscillator > previous bar's signal AND previous bar's forecast oscillator <= signal two bars ago AND signal < 0.
Short: On the just-completed chart bar, forecast oscillator < previous bar's signal AND previous bar's forecast oscillator >= signal two bars ago AND signal > 0.
