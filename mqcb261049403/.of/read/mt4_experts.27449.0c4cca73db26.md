Calculation: MACD signal line = simple moving average of the MACD main line.
Calculation: Break-even stop = entry price plus the stop offset for a long, or entry price minus the stop offset for a short; activate it after price moves favorably from entry by more than the activation distance.
Long: At the opening of a new chart bar, the shorter-period typical-price linear weighted moving average is above the longer-period typical-price linear weighted moving average on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the low two chart bars ago is below the high of the last completed chart bar AND the absolute difference between the current open and the close two chart bars ago exceeds the entry-distance threshold AND the current MACD main line is below zero and above its signal line AND the previous MACD main line is below its signal line AND the absolute current MACD main line exceeds the MACD entry threshold AND the current close-price exponential moving average is above its previous value.
Short: At the opening of a new chart bar, the shorter-period typical-price linear weighted moving average is below the longer-period typical-price linear weighted moving average on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the low of the last completed chart bar is below the high two chart bars ago AND the absolute difference between the current open and the close of the last completed chart bar exceeds the entry-distance threshold AND the current MACD main line is above the MACD entry threshold and below its signal line AND the previous MACD main line is above its signal line AND the current close-price exponential moving average is below its previous value.
Exit: long, price is at or below entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR price is at or above entry price plus the take-profit distance OR the break-even stop is enabled and active AND price is at or below the break-even stop.
Exit: short, price is at or above entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR price is at or below entry price minus the take-profit distance OR the break-even stop is enabled and active AND price is at or above the break-even stop.
