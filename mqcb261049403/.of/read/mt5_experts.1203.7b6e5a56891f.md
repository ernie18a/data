Calculation: MESA adaptive averages = Apply the following recurrence to close prices on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe or the same as it. Select a completed indicator bar or the still-forming bar as the signal bar; the preceding value is from the immediately preceding indicator bar. For a series x, define H(x,y)t = (0.0962*x[t] + 0.5769*x[t-2] - 0.5769*x[t-4] - 0.0962*x[t-6])*(0.075*y[t-1] + 0.54). Set smoothed price = (4*close[t] + 3*close[t-1] + 2*close[t-2] + close[t-3])/10; detrender = H(smoothed price, period); Q1 = H(detrender, period); I1 = detrender[t-3]; jI = H(I1, I1); jQ = H(Q1, Q1). Set I2 = 0.2*(I1-jQ) + 0.8*I2[t-1] and Q2 = 0.2*(Q1+jI) + 0.8*Q2[t-1]. Set real = 0.2*(I2[t]*I2[t-1]+Q2[t]*Q2[t-1]) + 0.8*real[t-1], and imaginary = 0.2*(I2[t]*Q2[t-1]-Q2[t]*I2[t-1]) + 0.8*imaginary[t-1]. Set raw period = 6.285714/atan(imaginary/real) when both are nonzero and the arctangent is nonzero; otherwise set it to 6.285714. Bound raw period first to 0.67–1.50 times the previous period, then to 6–50; period = 0.2*raw period + 0.8*period[t-1]. Phase = 57.27272987*atan(Q1/I1) if I1 is nonzero, otherwise 57.27272987. Alpha = max(slow limit, fast limit/max(1, phase[t-1]-phase[t])), with fast limit greater than slow limit. MAMA = alpha*close + (1-alpha)*MAMA[t-1]; FAMA = 0.5*alpha*MAMA + (1-0.5*alpha)*FAMA[t-1]. Initialize intermediate histories to zero and both averages to the close immediately before the first calculation bar.
Long: MAMA on the signal bar > FAMA on the signal bar AND MAMA on the preceding indicator bar < FAMA on the preceding indicator bar.
Short: MAMA on the signal bar < FAMA on the signal bar AND MAMA on the preceding indicator bar > FAMA on the preceding indicator bar.
Exit: long, MAMA on the signal bar < FAMA on the signal bar OR low <= entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, MAMA on the signal bar > FAMA on the signal bar OR high >= entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
