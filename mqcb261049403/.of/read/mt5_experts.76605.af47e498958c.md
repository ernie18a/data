Calculation: ATR regime = last completed bar's ATR divided by the mean ATR over a lookback ending on that bar.
Calculation: RSI limit = extreme-volatility RSI threshold if ATR regime >= extreme-volatility threshold, otherwise high-volatility RSI threshold if ATR regime >= high-volatility threshold, otherwise normal RSI threshold. The extreme-volatility ATR threshold is higher than the high-volatility threshold; its RSI threshold is lower than the high-volatility RSI threshold, which is lower than the normal RSI threshold.
Calculation: Swing low or high = a bar whose low or high is respectively no greater or no less than those of every bar within the swing depth on either side. Search completed bars within the structure lookback, starting beyond the swing depth. The older of each pair must be separated from the newer by at least the swing depth plus one bar.
Calculation: Bearish structure = the newest swing low is below the preceding swing low AND the newest swing high is below the preceding swing high.
Calculation: Support condition = current bar open <= newest swing low + ATR multiplied by a support-zone factor OR the last completed bar's low < newest swing low - ATR multiplied by a sweep factor AND its close > newest swing low.
Calculation: Reversal condition = last completed bar's close is above its range midpoint AND (its close > its open OR its lower wick >= its range multiplied by a wick factor OR its lower wick >= its absolute body multiplied by a body factor) OR last completed bar's RSI > preceding bar's RSI.
Calculation: Entry quality score = the sum of configured weights for each true condition: RSI <= RSI limit; RSI <= RSI limit minus an RSI margin; bearish structure; support condition; reversal condition; ATR regime >= high-volatility threshold. Clamp the sum to the configured score bounds. RSI and ATR use the last completed bar.
Long: position count = zero AND last completed bar's RSI <= RSI limit AND entry quality score >= entry score threshold AND (bearish structure required = false OR bearish structure = true) AND (support required = false OR support condition = true) AND (reversal required = false OR reversal condition = true)
