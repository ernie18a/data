Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a completed chart-timeframe bar whose low is no higher than the lows of the specified number of bars on either side
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a completed chart-timeframe bar whose high is no lower than the highs of the specified number of bars on either side
Calculation: Bearish structure = latest confirmed swing low < preceding confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing high < preceding confirmed swing high
Calculation: Near last swing-low support = decision price is within an ATR-based zone above the latest confirmed swing low OR the previous completed bar's low is below that swing low by an ATR-based margin and its close is above the swing low
Calculation: Bullish rejection = previous completed bar closes above its open and in the upper half of its range, OR its lower wick is at least a configured fraction of its range or body, and it closes in the upper half of its range
Calculation: Rising RSI = RSI on the most recent completed bar > RSI on the preceding completed bar
Calculation: Average ATR = mean ATR across a longer lookback than the ATR calculation's own period
Calculation: Dynamic buy RSI threshold = extreme-volatility threshold when ATR >= average ATR × extreme-volatility multiplier; otherwise high-volatility threshold when ATR >= average ATR × high-volatility multiplier; otherwise normal threshold
Calculation: Recovery quality score = weighted score from RSI at/below the dynamic threshold, RSI further below that threshold, bearish structure, near support, bullish rejection or rising RSI, high ATR regime, and emergency recovery eligibility; rescue mode adds bonuses for structure with support and reversal and for sufficient drawdown, and penalties for lacking support or reversal
Calculation: Adaptive recovery distance = ATR × base spacing × (1 + number of prior recovery entries × spacing increment), floored at the applicable minimum spacing for the next entry
Long: RSI on the most recent completed chart-timeframe bar <= dynamic buy RSI threshold AND recovery quality score >= normal minimum AND (bearish structure if required) AND (near last swing-low support if required) AND (bullish rejection OR rising RSI if reversal is required)
Long: A long position is open AND decision price <= lowest long entry price - adaptive recovery distance AND RSI on the most recent completed chart-timeframe bar <= dynamic buy RSI threshold AND recovery quality score >= the rescue minimum when rescue mode applies, otherwise >= the normal minimum AND (emergency recovery is eligible OR each required bearish-structure, support, and bullish-reversal condition holds)
Exit: long, basket net profit has reached the target and current basket net profit <= its peak since reaching the target - trailing step
Exit: long, at least two positions are open AND recovery basket net profit has reached its trail-start level AND current recovery basket net profit > 0 AND current recovery basket net profit <= its peak since reaching that level - allowed giveback
Exit: long, at least three positions are open AND the most profitable position's profit >= minimum winner profit AND the combined profit of the most profitable and most losing positions >= trim target; close those two positions
