Calculation: Bull Aroon = round to the nearest integer 100 × (1 − (age of the most recent highest high in the lookback + 0.5) / lookback), where age is measured in bars and the current bar has age zero.
Calculation: Bear Aroon = round to the nearest integer 100 × (1 − (age of the most recent lowest low in the lookback + 0.5) / lookback), using the same lookback as Bull Aroon.
Calculation: Aroon values use either the chart bar or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Higher-timeframe values are copied to chart bars within that higher-timeframe bar and can repaint as it forms.
Long: Bull Aroon > Bear Aroon AND Bull Aroon >= the midpoint of the Aroon scale.
Short: Bull Aroon < Bear Aroon AND Bear Aroon >= the midpoint of the Aroon scale.
