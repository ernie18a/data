Calculation: Weighted RVI = weighted sum of five Relative Vigor Index main lines with progressively longer lookbacks; the second and third lines share the same weight.
Calculation: Smoothed weighted RVI = weighted RVI divided by the smoothing period plus the previous smoothed weighted RVI multiplied by (smoothing period minus 1) divided by the smoothing period; initialize it to the first weighted RVI divided by the smoothing period.
Long: Weighted RVI > smoothed weighted RVI.
Short: Weighted RVI < smoothed weighted RVI.
Long: Higher-timeframe weighted RVI > higher-timeframe smoothed weighted RVI, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Short: Higher-timeframe weighted RVI < higher-timeframe smoothed weighted RVI, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
