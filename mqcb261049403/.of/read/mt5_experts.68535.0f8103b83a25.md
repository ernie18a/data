Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of close on the chart timeframe, last completed bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe, last completed bar.
Calculation: PivotHigh = high of the most recent bar whose high is >= the highs of N bars on each side (the right-side neighbors may include the still-forming bar). PivotLow = low of the most recent bar whose low is <= the lows of N bars on each side (same neighbor rule). Both are searched on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Signal = output of a pre-trained 4-class market-structure classifier (label index 0..3 plus the probability of the predicted label, called confidence). It runs once per new bar on the last completed chart-timeframe bar. Its 6 inputs are: (close - close k bars earlier)/ATR; (highest high over a long lookback - close)/ATR; (close - lowest low over the same long lookback)/ATR; tick volume / mean tick volume over a lookback shorter than the swing lookback; (close - open)/ATR; hour of day of the bar.
Calculation: Range = PivotHigh - PivotLow.
Calculation: LongEntry = PivotHigh - fib_ratio*Range. LongSL = PivotLow - small buffer. LongTP = LongEntry + RR*(LongEntry - LongSL).
Calculation: ShortEntry = PivotLow + fib_ratio*Range. ShortSL = PivotHigh + small buffer. ShortTP = ShortEntry - RR*(ShortSL - ShortEntry).
Long: on a new bar, AND no open position and no pending order, AND PivotHigh and PivotLow both exist, AND Signal confidence >= threshold, AND Signal label is 2 or 3, AND current price > SMA, AND LongEntry < current ask. Then place a buy limit at LongEntry with LongSL and LongTP. The order is cancelled if unfilled after a fixed expiry time.
Short: on a new bar, AND no open position and no pending order, AND PivotHigh and PivotLow both exist, AND Signal confidence >= threshold, AND Signal label is 0 or 1, AND current price <= SMA, AND ShortEntry > current bid. Then place a sell limit at ShortEntry with ShortSL and ShortTP. The order is cancelled if unfilled after a fixed expiry time.
Exit: long, low <= current stop OR high >= LongTP. The stop starts at LongSL. Once (price - entry) >= trail_fraction*(LongTP - entry), the stop is raised to entry + trail_factor*(price - entry), where trail_factor is below 1. The stop only ratchets upward.
Exit: short, high >= current stop OR low <= ShortTP. The stop starts at ShortSL. Once (entry - price) >= trail_fraction*(entry - ShortTP), the stop is lowered to entry - trail_factor*(entry - price), where trail_factor is below 1. The stop only ratchets downward.
