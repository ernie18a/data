Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over a lookback including the current bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, compare the still-forming higher-timeframe bar's close with its range midpoint; record bullish if above, bearish if below, and retain the higher-timeframe bar's existing direction if equal, initialized bearish. Hold the recorded direction until the next higher-timeframe bar begins.
Long: Chart-timeframe close > chart-timeframe range midpoint.
Short: Chart-timeframe close < chart-timeframe range midpoint.
Long: Higher-timeframe direction = bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe direction = bearish.
