Calculation: Digital oscillator = fast finite-impulse-response weighted sum of the selected bar price minus slow finite-impulse-response weighted sum of the same price, using separate coefficient sets for current and past bars.
Calculation: Signal = moving average of the digital oscillator using the selected smoothing method.
Long: (chart-timeframe digital oscillator > 0 AND chart-timeframe digital oscillator != its previous-bar value) OR chart-timeframe digital oscillator > its previous-bar signal OR (higher-timeframe digital oscillator > 0 AND higher-timeframe digital oscillator != its previous-bar value) OR higher-timeframe digital oscillator > its current-bar signal.
Short: (chart-timeframe digital oscillator < 0 AND chart-timeframe digital oscillator != its previous-bar value) OR chart-timeframe digital oscillator < its previous-bar signal OR (higher-timeframe digital oscillator < 0 AND higher-timeframe digital oscillator != its previous-bar value) OR higher-timeframe digital oscillator < its current-bar signal.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = values of the higher-timeframe bar still forming, sampled on its first chart bar and held until the next higher-timeframe bar; previous-bar references are to the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
