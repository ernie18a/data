Calculation: Fibonacci levels = the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, plus the standard Fibonacci retracement levels and midpoint between them.
Calculation: Current-bar typical-price moving average = linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close) / 3, including the chart bar still forming at its open.
Long: At the open of a new chart bar, a close within a lookback of completed bars ending before the latest completed bar > any Fibonacci level AND the latest completed bar's low <= that same level AND the preceding bar's low < the latest completed bar's high AND the absolute difference between the current open and the preceding bar's close > a price-distance threshold AND the shorter-period current-bar typical-price moving average > the longer-period current-bar typical-price moving average.
Short: At the open of a new chart bar, a close within a lookback of completed bars ending before the latest completed bar < any Fibonacci level AND the latest completed bar's high >= that same level, except that for the lowest level its close >= that level AND the latest completed bar's low < the preceding bar's high AND the absolute difference between the current open and the preceding bar's close > a price-distance threshold AND the shorter-period current-bar typical-price moving average < the longer-period current-bar typical-price moving average AND the long condition is false.
Exit: long, price <= entry price minus a stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price plus a take-profit distance OR the highest price since entry > entry price plus a break-even activation distance AND price <= entry price plus a break-even stop distance.
Exit: short, price >= entry price plus a stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price minus a take-profit distance OR the lowest price since entry < entry price minus a break-even activation distance AND price >= entry price minus a break-even stop distance.
