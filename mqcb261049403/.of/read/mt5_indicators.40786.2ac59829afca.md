Candidate power is the squared amplitude of the Ehlers in-phase/quadrature resonator applied to the smoothed high-pass midpoint price.
Calculation: Restoring Pull line = volume × (2π / dominant cycle period)²; its signal line is a simple moving average over a shorter lookback.
