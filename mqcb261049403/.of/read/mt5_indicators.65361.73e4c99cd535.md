Calculation: Opening-range high and low = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose timestamps fall within the configured daily opening interval; retain these levels until the next opening interval begins.
Calculation: Breakout state = false at the start of each opening interval; outside the interval, set it to true when either breakout condition below occurs, then set it to false after either retest condition below occurs or after (previous close > opening-range high AND current close < opening-range high) OR (previous close < opening-range low AND current close > opening-range low).
Long: Outside the opening interval AND low two bars ago < opening-range high AND close two bars ago > opening-range high AND previous low > opening-range high AND previous close > opening-range high AND current close > previous low AND current low > opening-range high.
Short: Outside the opening interval AND high two bars ago > opening-range low AND close two bars ago < opening-range low AND previous high < opening-range low AND previous close < opening-range low AND current close < previous high AND current high < opening-range low.
Long: Outside the opening interval AND breakout state is true AND previous close > opening-range high AND current low <= opening-range high AND current close >= opening-range high.
Short: Outside the opening interval AND breakout state is true AND previous close < opening-range low AND current high >= opening-range low AND current close <= opening-range low.
