Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA pair = short- and long-lookback close EMAs on the higher timeframe. During a historical scan, use the higher-timeframe bar whose offset from the latest available bar equals the chart bar's offset from the latest chart bar; if unavailable, use the latest higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming. When the higher timeframe is disabled, use the chart-timeframe EMA pair instead.
Calculation: Directional score = sum of weights for satisfied conditions: short EMA above/below long EMA with their absolute gap greater than an ATR multiple; close above/below the trend EMA by an ATR multiple; RSI above/below its midpoint and below/above the respective momentum-zone boundary and rising/falling from the previous bar; MACD histogram rising/falling from the previous bar; close above/below the current bar's (high + low + close) / 3; volume greater than a multiple of its moving average including the current bar; ADX above a threshold AND positive/negative DI greater than the other DI; enabled higher-timeframe short EMA above/below its long EMA. Use above, rising and positive DI for the long score, and below, falling and negative DI for the short score. The first three weights are equal, the MACD and ADX weights are equal, the price and volume weights are equal, and the higher-timeframe weight is the largest.
Calculation: Entry eligibility = score at least the minimum score AND score at least the selected grade threshold, if a grade threshold is enabled AND score at least the C-grade cutoff, if C grades are hidden AND bars since the last entry at least the cooldown, unless no entry exists AND the last entry direction differs from this direction, unless a stop has since been hit.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry close minus/plus an ATR multiple for a long/short, or, if structure stops are enabled, the lowest low/highest high over the entry bar and a lookback of preceding bars minus/plus an ATR buffer, limited to at least a minimum ATR distance from entry. Fix the stop and ATR at entry.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus/minus risk distance times each of three increasing reward-to-risk parameters for long/short, where risk distance is the absolute difference between entry and initial stop.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until a target has been reached; if trailing is enabled, move it to entry after the first target, to the first target after the second, and to the second target after the third. A target is reached when a later bar's high/low reaches or exceeds/falls below it for a long/short. Test the stop using its value before the current bar's target updates.
Long: On a completed chart bar, short EMA crosses above long EMA AND close is above the trend EMA plus an ATR multiple AND RSI is below an upper limit AND absolute candle body exceeds an ATR multiple AND absolute distance between close and short EMA is below an ATR multiple AND the higher-timeframe short EMA is at least its long EMA AND long entry eligibility holds.
Short: On a completed chart bar, short EMA crosses below long EMA AND close is below the trend EMA minus an ATR multiple AND RSI is above a lower limit AND absolute candle body exceeds an ATR multiple AND absolute distance between close and short EMA is below an ATR multiple AND the higher-timeframe short EMA is at most its long EMA AND short entry eligibility holds.
Long: On the current chart bar, short EMA is above long EMA AND close is above the trend EMA plus an ATR multiple.
Short: On the current chart bar, short EMA is below long EMA AND close is below the trend EMA minus an ATR multiple.
Long: On the current chart bar, the higher-timeframe short EMA is above the higher-timeframe long EMA.
Short: On the current chart bar, the higher-timeframe short EMA is below the higher-timeframe long EMA.
Exit: long, a short entry signal occurs OR a bar after entry has low at or below the active stop.
Exit: short, a long entry signal occurs OR a bar after entry has high at or above the active stop.
