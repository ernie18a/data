Calculation: Entry MACD = fast EMA minus slow EMA of median price; use its main line on the chart timeframe, evaluated on completed bars.
Calculation: Trend MACD = fast EMA minus slow EMA of median price; use its main line on the chart timeframe, evaluated on completed bars.
Long: No open position AND entry MACD 3 bars ago > entry MACD 2 bars ago AND entry MACD 2 bars ago < entry MACD on the last completed bar AND trend MACD on the last completed bar > trend MACD 2 bars ago.
Short: No open position AND entry MACD 3 bars ago < entry MACD 2 bars ago AND entry MACD 2 bars ago > entry MACD on the last completed bar AND trend MACD on the last completed bar < trend MACD 2 bars ago.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
