Calculation: Let D1 and D2 be the last completed and preceding chart-timeframe bars; let H1 and H2 be the last completed and preceding lower-timeframe bars. O, H, L, C and V denote open, high, low, close and volume; R = D1.H - D1.L. Use separate configurable thresholds for range fractions, body sizes, reversal distances and target offsets.
Calculation: Up-pattern(X1, X2) = (X1.O <= X1.L + lower fraction * R AND X1.C >= X1.L + upper fraction * R AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.L < X2.L AND X1.C > X1.O AND X1.C > X1.L + failure fraction * R AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.C > X1.O AND X1.C - X1.O <= body threshold AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.L < X2.L AND X1.H > X2.H AND X1.C >= X2.H AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (abs(X1.C - X2.O) < reversal threshold AND X2.O > X2.C).
Calculation: Down-pattern(X1, X2) = (X1.C <= X1.L + lower fraction * R AND X1.O >= X1.L + upper fraction * R AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.H > X2.H AND X1.C < X1.O AND X1.C < X1.H - failure fraction * R AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.C < X1.O AND X1.O - X1.C <= body threshold AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (X1.L < X2.L AND X1.H > X2.H AND X1.C <= X2.L AND X1.V > X2.V) OR (abs(X1.C - X2.O) < reversal threshold AND X2.O < X2.C).
Calculation: Up-failure(H1, H2) = the Up-pattern alternatives excluding its first alternative. Down-failure(H1, H2) = the Down-pattern alternatives excluding its first alternative. Use separate body and reversal thresholds for chart and lower timeframes; the lower-timeframe patterns still use R from D1.
Calculation: Weak-down = H1.V < H2.V AND ((H1.C <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.O >= H1.L + upper fraction * R) OR (H1.C <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.O >= H1.L + middle fraction * R AND H1.O <= H1.L + upper fraction * R) OR (H1.H > H2.H AND H1.C < H1.O AND H1.C < H1.H - failure fraction * R) OR (H1.C < H1.O AND H1.O - H1.C <= lower-timeframe body threshold) OR (H1.L < H2.L AND H1.H > H2.H AND H1.C <= H2.L)). Weak-up is the corresponding upward mirror: H1.V < H2.V AND ((H1.O <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.C >= H1.L + upper fraction * R) OR (H1.C >= H1.L + upper fraction * R AND H1.O <= H1.L + middle fraction * R AND H1.O >= H1.L + lower fraction * R) OR (H1.L < H2.L AND H1.C > H1.O AND H1.C > H1.L + failure fraction * R) OR (H1.C > H1.O AND H1.C - H1.O <= lower-timeframe body threshold) OR (H1.L < H2.L AND H1.H > H2.H AND H1.C >= H2.H)).
Calculation: Strong-down = H1.O >= H1.L + upper fraction * R AND H1.C <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.V > H2.V. Strong-up = H1.V > H2.V AND ((H1.O <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.C >= H1.L + upper fraction * R) OR (H1.C >= H1.L + upper fraction * R AND H1.O <= H1.L + middle fraction * R AND H1.O >= H1.L + lower fraction * R) OR (H1.C <= H1.L + lower fraction * R AND H1.O >= H1.L + middle fraction * R AND H1.O <= H1.L + upper fraction * R)).
Calculation: Strength markings use completed chart-timeframe bars and, when the chart is the shorter timeframe, completed higher-timeframe bars. For either timeframe, a buying-strength bar has volume greater than its preceding bar AND ((close > open AND close > midpoint of high and low) OR (close < open AND close > midpoint) OR (close = open AND close != midpoint)). A selling-strength bar has volume greater than its preceding bar AND ((close < open AND close < midpoint) OR (close > open AND close < midpoint) OR (close = open AND close < midpoint)).
Calculation: Target levels are closes of strength bars within a configurable lookback, processed oldest to newest. An up-close buying-strength bar removes older selling levels at or below its close; a down-close buying-strength bar or a doji buying-strength bar below its midpoint removes older buying levels at or above its close. A down-close selling-strength bar or a doji selling-strength bar removes older buying levels at or above its close; an up-close selling-strength bar removes older selling levels at or below its close. On the higher timeframe, remove affected levels only when older than a separate shorter lookback; retain affected newer levels. At entry, the long target is the nearest retained selling level above entry plus a minimum distance, or entry plus that distance if none exists; the short target is the nearest retained buying level below entry minus that distance, or entry minus that distance if none exists.
Long: A completed strength-marking bar satisfies buying strength.
Short: A completed strength-marking bar satisfies selling strength.
Long: Position is flat AND Up-pattern(D1, D2) AND NOT Down-pattern(D1, D2) AND Up-failure(H1, H2) AND NOT Down-pattern(H1, H2).
Short: Position is flat AND Down-pattern(D1, D2) AND Down-failure(H1, H2).
Exit: long, current chart-bar close > long target - target offset.
Exit: short, current chart-bar close < short target + target offset.
Exit: long, at the start of a new chart bar AND NOT Weak-down AND NOT Strong-down AND Down-pattern(H1, H2); exit partially.
Exit: short, at the start of a new chart bar AND NOT Weak-up AND NOT Strong-down AND Up-pattern(H1, H2) AND NOT Down-pattern(H1, H2) AND NOT Strong-up; exit partially.
