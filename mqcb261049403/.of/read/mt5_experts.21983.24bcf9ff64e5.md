Calculation: Long stop = initial stop at entry price minus stop-loss distance, if enabled; otherwise unset. If trailing is enabled, whenever price minus entry price > trailing distance plus trailing step AND the stop is unset or stop < price minus trailing distance minus trailing step, set the stop to price minus trailing distance.
Calculation: Short stop = initial stop at entry price plus stop-loss distance, if enabled; otherwise unset. If trailing is enabled, whenever entry price minus price > trailing distance plus trailing step AND the stop is unset or stop > price plus trailing distance plus trailing step, set the stop to price plus trailing distance.
Long: Time of day is within the inclusive trading window AND current price >= last completed breakout-timeframe bar high plus indent AND (either moving-average period is zero OR forming chart-bar shorter-period moving average > forming chart-bar longer-period moving average) AND open long count < maximum per direction AND no long has been opened during the current breakout-timeframe bar. The breakout timeframe is configurable as higher or lower than the chart timeframe; each moving average has its own price input and horizontal shift, and both use the same smoothing method.
Short: Time of day is within the inclusive trading window AND current price <= last completed breakout-timeframe bar low minus indent AND (either moving-average period is zero OR forming chart-bar shorter-period moving average < forming chart-bar longer-period moving average) AND open short count < maximum per direction AND no short has been opened during the current breakout-timeframe bar. The breakout timeframe is configurable as higher or lower than the chart timeframe; each moving average has its own price input and horizontal shift, and both use the same smoothing method.
Exit: long, long stop is set AND current price <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND current price >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, short stop is set AND current price >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND current price <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
