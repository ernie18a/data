Calculation: Buy score = count of these seven comparisons that are true between the current forming signal-timeframe bar and its preceding completed bar: open > preceding open; high > preceding high; low > preceding low; high + low > preceding high + preceding low; close > preceding close; high + low + close > preceding high + preceding low + preceding close; high + low + 2 × close > preceding high + preceding low + 2 × preceding close. The signal timeframe can be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Sell score = seven minus the buy score.
Long: Buy score > 0
Short: Sell score > 0
Long: Buy score > sell score
Short: Sell score > buy score
Exit: long, sell score > buy score
Exit: short, buy score > sell score
