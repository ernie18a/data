Calculation: high-low midpoint = (high + low) / 2
Calculation: typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: close-weighted price = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4
Long: On the signal timeframe, at least four of the current forming bar's open, high, low, close, high-low midpoint, typical price, and close-weighted price are greater than their corresponding values on the preceding completed bar; and the position is flat or short
Short: On the signal timeframe, at least four of the current forming bar's open, high, low, close, high-low midpoint, typical price, and close-weighted price are less than or equal to their corresponding values on the preceding completed bar; and the position is flat or long
Exit: Long, at least four of the current forming bar's open, high, low, close, high-low midpoint, typical price, and close-weighted price are less than or equal to their corresponding values on the preceding completed bar
Exit: Short, at least four of the current forming bar's open, high, low, close, high-low midpoint, typical price, and close-weighted price are greater than their corresponding values on the preceding completed bar
