Calculation: Price source = independently select each of two sources from open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (open+close)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, trend-biased price, or extreme trend-biased price, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high+close)/2 when close>open and (low+close)/2 otherwise; extreme trend-biased price is high when close>open, low when close<open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Adaptive filter = for each input series independently, initialize the output to the first input. On each subsequent bar, let change be the absolute difference between the current input and the previous filtered output; let mean change be the sum of available changes over the filter lookback divided by that full lookback; let deviation be the square root of the sum over that lookback of (each change minus its corresponding mean change) squared, divided by the full lookback. Output the previous filtered output if change is less than the filter multiplier times deviation; otherwise output the current input. Disable the filter when its multiplier is nonpositive.
Calculation: Aroon oscillator = optionally filter each selected price source independently, then assign their maximum to the high series and their minimum to the low series on each bar. Over the current bar and the specified number of preceding bars, find the age in bars of the highest high-series value and the age of the lowest low-series value, choosing the most recent occurrence on ties. Oscillator=100*(age of lowest-age of highest)/specified number of preceding bars. Optionally apply the adaptive filter independently to the oscillator.
Calculation: Quantile bands = sort the current oscillator and preceding oscillator values over a specified lookback, padding unavailable early values with zeros. For each band, use its specified quantile: linearly interpolate between sorted values at zero-based index (lookback-1)*quantile. The upper band uses a higher quantile than the lower band.
Calculation: Evaluation timeframe = the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. For a higher timeframe, calculate the oscillator and bands using the bar still forming; use the resulting band comparison without interpolation.
Long: The evaluation-timeframe bar is not its first bar AND Aroon oscillator>upper quantile band.
Short: The evaluation-timeframe bar is not its first bar AND Aroon oscillator<lower quantile band.
