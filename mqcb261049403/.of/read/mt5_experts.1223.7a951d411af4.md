Calculation: Trend bands and direction = On the chart timeframe, or on a configurable higher or lower timeframe, calculate a linear weighted moving average of highs and an equal-length linear weighted moving average of lows, and ATR. For each bar, set upper band = high average × (1 + vertical shift percentage) + ATR × sensitivity and lower band = low average × (1 − vertical shift percentage) − ATR × sensitivity. Initialize previous bands and direction to zero. Set direction to long if close > previous upper band, then set it to short if close < previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous direction. If direction is long, replace the lower band with max(lower band, previous lower band); if short, replace the upper band with min(upper band, previous upper band). Carry both resulting bands and direction forward. For the EA, use a configurable signal-bar offset on the indicator timeframe: the bar still forming or a completed bar.
Long: direction = long AND previous direction != long
Short: direction = short AND previous direction != short
Exit: long, direction = short OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, direction = long OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
