Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of close, open, high, low, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, high if close>open and low if close<open and close otherwise, (high+close)/2 if close>open and (low+close)/2 if close<open and close otherwise, or (high+low+close+X)/2-(high+low)/2 where X is low if close<open, high if close>open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Smoothed Fisher = for each bar, let H and L be the highest high and lowest low over the lookback including that bar; if H=L, increase H by the minimum price increment. Set v=(selected price-L)/(H-L)-0.5+0.67*previous v, with initial v=0. Set r=(1+v)/(1-v), replacing r with 1 if r<0.0000001; set Fisher=0.5*ln(r)+0.5*previous Fisher, with initial Fisher=0. Smooth Fisher using the selected moving-average method and its parameters.
Long: Smoothed Fisher on the chart timeframe > its previous bar value OR smoothed Fisher on a higher timeframe, sampled from the still-forming higher-timeframe bar at the first chart bar of that period and held until the next period, > its preceding higher-timeframe bar value.
Short: Smoothed Fisher on the chart timeframe < its previous bar value OR smoothed Fisher on a higher timeframe, sampled from the still-forming higher-timeframe bar at the first chart bar of that period and held until the next period, < its preceding higher-timeframe bar value.
