Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-period Hull moving average of close minus longer-period Hull moving average of close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe oscillator state = oscillator calculated on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar and compared with its preceding completed bar. Map the state to chart bars in that higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe oscillator on the preceding chart bar > 0 AND higher-timeframe oscillator on the preceding chart bar > its preceding higher-timeframe value AND (higher-timeframe oscillator on the current chart bar = 0 OR higher-timeframe oscillator on the current chart bar = its preceding higher-timeframe value).
Short: (Higher-timeframe oscillator on the preceding chart bar = 0 OR higher-timeframe oscillator on the preceding chart bar = its preceding higher-timeframe value) AND higher-timeframe oscillator on the current chart bar > 0 AND higher-timeframe oscillator on the current chart bar > its preceding higher-timeframe value.
