Calculation: Sampled RSI and moving averages = For each selected chart or higher timeframe, calculate short-lookback and long-lookback RSI and shorter-period and longer-period moving averages on their respective selectable applied prices and moving-average methods. At the first chart bar of each selected-timeframe bar, sample the indicators from that same bar, which is still forming in live calculation, and hold the sampled values until the next selected-timeframe bar. Historical calculation uses the final values of that same selected-timeframe bar.
Long: For any selected timeframe, sampled short-lookback RSI > sampled long-lookback RSI AND sampled shorter-period moving average >= sampled longer-period moving average OR sampled short-lookback RSI >= sampled long-lookback RSI AND sampled shorter-period moving average > sampled longer-period moving average.
Short: For any selected timeframe, sampled short-lookback RSI < sampled long-lookback RSI AND sampled shorter-period moving average <= sampled longer-period moving average OR sampled short-lookback RSI <= sampled long-lookback RSI AND sampled shorter-period moving average < sampled longer-period moving average.
