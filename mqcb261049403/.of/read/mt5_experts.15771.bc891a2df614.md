Calculation: Prior direction = direction of the most recent Williams %R crossing before the signal bar: bullish when %R crosses above the lower threshold, bearish when %R crosses below the upper threshold; if both occur on the same bar, choose bullish. Evaluate %R on the indicator timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar as the signal bar.
Long: Williams %R on the signal bar > lower threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar <= lower threshold
Short: Williams %R on the signal bar < upper threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar >= upper threshold
Exit: long, Williams %R on the signal bar < upper threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar >= upper threshold OR neither crossing occurs on the signal bar AND prior direction = bearish OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, Williams %R on the signal bar > lower threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar <= lower threshold OR neither crossing occurs on the signal bar AND prior direction = bullish OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
