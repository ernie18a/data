Calculation: Price = current close as the available proxy for Bid and Ask; historical OHLC values are taken from the corresponding aggregated bars.
Calculation: Timeframes A, B, C, and D are ordered from shortest to longest; their relationship to the chart timeframe is unspecified by the source. Index 0 denotes the bar still forming, and positive indices denote completed bars, with index 1 the last completed bar.
Calculation: Trend on a timeframe = 100 × (short-period SMA of opens − long-period SMA of opens) / long-period SMA of opens. The same short and long periods are used for C and D.
Calculation: Upper level = Price rounded to a parameterized decimal precision + entry offset; lower level = Price rounded to the same precision − entry offset.
Calculation: Long target distance = extended profit distance if, when the order is placed, forming D trend is between its positive bounds and is greater than the last completed D trend, which is greater than each successively preceding completed D trend over the specified lookback; otherwise, the standard profit distance.
Calculation: Short target distance = extended profit distance if, when the order is placed, forming D trend is between its negative bounds and is less than the last completed D trend, which is less than each successively preceding completed D trend over the specified lookback; otherwise, the standard profit distance.
Long: datetime is outside the prohibited weekday and hour interval AND Price < upper level AND A close[1] > upper level AND C low[0] and C low[1] > upper level − entry offset + low buffer AND C low[2] > upper level − entry offset AND (forming C trend < 0 OR at least one of C high[1] and C high[2] > upper level + large rise distance) AND every A high over the specified lookback including bar 0 <= upper level + high exclusion distance AND no A index x in the specified lookback including 0 has A high[x + bar offset] − A low[x] > reversal distance AND A open[x + bar offset] > A close[x] AND forming D trend < negative trend threshold AND at least one C high over the specified lookback including bar 0 > upper level + intermediate rise distance AND at least one B high over the specified lookback including bar 0 >= upper level + small rise distance.
Short: datetime is outside the prohibited weekday and hour interval AND Price > lower level AND A close[1] < lower level AND C high[0] and C high[1] < lower level + entry offset − high buffer AND C high[2] < lower level + entry offset AND (forming C trend > 0 OR at least one of C low[1] and C low[2] < lower level − large rise distance) AND every A low over the specified lookback including bar 0 >= lower level − high exclusion distance AND no A index x in the specified lookback including 0 has A high[x] − A low[x + bar offset] > reversal distance AND A close[x] > A open[x + bar offset] AND forming D trend > positive trend threshold AND at least one C low over the specified lookback including bar 0 < lower level − intermediate rise distance AND at least one B low over the specified lookback including bar 0 <= lower level − small rise distance.
Exit: long, Price <= entry price − stop distance OR Price >= entry price + long target distance OR trailing adjustment is enabled AND long target distance > trailing eligibility distance AND, since entry, Price < entry price AND at least one A index x in the specified lookback including 0 has A high[x + bar offset] − A low[x] > trailing reversal distance AND A close[x] < A open[x + bar offset] AND Price >= entry price + reduced target distance.
Exit: short, Price >= entry price + stop distance OR Price <= entry price − short target distance OR trailing adjustment is enabled AND short target distance > trailing eligibility distance AND, since entry, Price > entry price AND at least one A index x in the specified lookback including 0 has A high[x] − A low[x + bar offset] > trailing reversal distance AND A close[x] > A open[x + bar offset] AND Price <= entry price − reduced target distance.
