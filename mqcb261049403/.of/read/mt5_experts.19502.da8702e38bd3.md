Calculation: Smoothed-price CMO = 100 × (sum of positive changes in a moving average of the selected price over its lookback minus sum of absolute negative changes over the same lookback) / (sum of positive changes plus sum of absolute negative changes); use zero when the denominator is zero. The moving average and CMO use the same lookback. Calculate independently for long and short decisions, with separately configurable parameters and timeframes. A timeframe may be the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; use the last completed bar by default, or a configurable earlier completed bar or the still-forming bar.
Long: Long smoothed-price CMO on the selected bar > 0 AND its value on the preceding bar <= 0
Short: Short smoothed-price CMO on the selected bar < 0 AND its value on the preceding bar >= 0
Exit: long, long smoothed-price CMO on the selected bar < 0 OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, short smoothed-price CMO on the selected bar > 0 OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
