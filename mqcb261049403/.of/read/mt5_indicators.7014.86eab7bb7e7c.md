Calculation: CCI = Commodity Channel Index of the selected applied price on the chart timeframe, or the selected higher timeframe in the higher-timeframe version; for a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Fast line = the selected smoothing method applied to CCI with a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Slow line = the same smoothing method applied to the fast line with a longer lookback.
Long: Fast line > Slow line
Short: Fast line < Slow line
