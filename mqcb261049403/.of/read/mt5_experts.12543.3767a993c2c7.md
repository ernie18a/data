Calculation: Trend state = On the indicator timeframe, initialize state to long, next-switch flag to 0, running low to 0, and running high to 9999999. For each bar in chronological order, calculate the lowest low and highest high over the same lookback, including that bar, and moving averages of high and low using that lookback and the same averaging method. If the flag is 1, set running low to the greater of itself and the lowest low; if the high moving average is below running low AND close is below the previous bar's low, set state to short, flag to 0, and running high to the highest high. Then, if the flag is 0, set running high to the lesser of itself and the highest high; if the low moving average is above running high AND close is above the previous bar's high, set state to long, flag to 1, and running low to the lowest low. The indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the same; evaluate the selected signal bar, which may be the last completed bar or the bar still forming, and its preceding indicator bar.
Long: Selected signal bar's trend state is long AND preceding indicator bar's trend state is short
Short: Selected signal bar's trend state is short AND preceding indicator bar's trend state is long
Exit: long, selected signal bar's trend state is short OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, selected signal bar's trend state is long OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
