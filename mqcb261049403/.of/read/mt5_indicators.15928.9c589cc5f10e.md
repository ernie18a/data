Calculation: Shifted upper channel = highest high over a lookback ending a configurable number of bars before the evaluated bar, inclusive.
Calculation: Shifted lower channel = lowest low over the same lookback ending the same number of bars before the evaluated bar, inclusive.
Long: Chart bar close > chart-timeframe shifted upper channel.
Short: Chart bar close < chart-timeframe shifted lower channel.
Long: Forming higher-timeframe bar close > higher-timeframe shifted upper channel.
Short: Forming higher-timeframe bar close < higher-timeframe shifted lower channel.
