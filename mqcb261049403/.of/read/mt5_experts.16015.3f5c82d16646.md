Calculation: Adaptive line = recursively smooth the close using a configurable moving-average method and lookback; on each bar, move the prior line value toward the smoothed close by a fraction equal to 1 minus (the standard deviation of close around the smoothed close over that same lookback, divided by the absolute distance from prior line to smoothed close) squared; use a zero fraction when that distance is zero or less than the standard deviation. Initialize the prior line to the smoothed close. Use the configurable indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) and its last completed bar.
Calculation: Line-change volatility = population standard deviation of the latest lookback of one-bar changes in the Adaptive line.
Long: Adaptive line two bars ago > Adaptive line one bar ago AND Adaptive line one bar ago < Adaptive line on the signal bar OR Adaptive line change on the signal bar > the lower volatility threshold, where the threshold is Line-change volatility multiplied by the lower of two threshold factors
Short: Adaptive line two bars ago < Adaptive line one bar ago AND Adaptive line one bar ago > Adaptive line on the signal bar OR Adaptive line change on the signal bar < negative the lower volatility threshold, where the threshold is Line-change volatility multiplied by the lower of two threshold factors
Exit: long, Adaptive line one bar ago > Adaptive line on the signal bar OR Adaptive line change on the signal bar < negative the lower volatility threshold
Exit: short, Adaptive line one bar ago < Adaptive line on the signal bar OR Adaptive line change on the signal bar > the lower volatility threshold
