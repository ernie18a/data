Calculation: Supertrend = On the calculation timeframe, let the raw upper and lower bands be (high + low) / 2 plus and minus an ATR multiplier times ATR. Starting with zero-initialized bands and trend, calculate from the first bar with ATR available: set trend to bullish if close > previous upper band, otherwise bearish if close < previous lower band, otherwise retain the previous trend. In a bullish trend, set the lower band to the greater of its raw value and its previous value, except on a change from bearish to bullish, when it is the raw value. In a bearish trend, set the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and its previous value, except on a change from bullish to bearish, when it is the raw value. The other band remains at its raw value. The Supertrend value is the lower band when bullish and the upper band when bearish. On a trend change, the preceding Supertrend value is overwritten with the value from two bars earlier.
Calculation: Other-timeframe Supertrend = Supertrend calculated independently on each selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using its bar still forming and the same ATR parameters as the chart-timeframe calculation. Its previous value is from the preceding bar of that timeframe.
Long: Chart-timeframe Supertrend trend is bullish.
Short: Chart-timeframe Supertrend trend is bearish.
Long: Low < chart-timeframe Supertrend AND close > chart-timeframe Supertrend.
Short: High > chart-timeframe Supertrend AND close < chart-timeframe Supertrend.
Long: Chart close > other-timeframe Supertrend OR (chart close = other-timeframe Supertrend AND previous chart close > previous other-timeframe Supertrend).
Short: Chart close < other-timeframe Supertrend OR (chart close = other-timeframe Supertrend AND previous chart close < previous other-timeframe Supertrend).
