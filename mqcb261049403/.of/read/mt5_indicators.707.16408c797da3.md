Calculation: Upper fractal = a standard upper Bill Williams fractal OR, if increased sensitivity is enabled, a bar whose high is greater than the preceding and following bar highs AND whose low is greater than or equal to the following bar low OR whose high is greater than the second following bar high. Evaluate the added sensitivity condition as high greater than both adjacent highs AND (low greater than or equal to the following low OR high greater than the second following high).
Calculation: Lower fractal = a standard lower Bill Williams fractal OR, if increased sensitivity is enabled, a bar whose low is less than both adjacent lows AND (high less than or equal to the following high OR low less than the second following low).
Calculation: Fractals are evaluated on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe. The second bar after the candidate can still be forming, so the result can change. If flat shifting is enabled, move an upper fractal to the earliest consecutive preceding bar with the same high, or a lower fractal to the earliest consecutive preceding bar with the same low. For a higher-timeframe fractal, assign it retrospectively to the first chart bar in the fractal's higher-timeframe bar whose high reaches the upper fractal price or whose low reaches the lower fractal price, respectively.
Long: a lower fractal is present.
Short: an upper fractal is present.
