Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = the selected moving-average method applied to close; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: Confirmation distance = the configured price distance required to confirm a moving-average cross.
Calculation: Prior long reference low = low of the last bar on which the long-signal routine ran, initially 0; update it whenever that routine's flat-distance condition passes.
Calculation: Prior short reference high = high of the last bar on which the short-signal routine ran, initially 0; update it whenever that routine's flat-distance condition passes.
Long: Previous bar fast moving average <= previous bar slow moving average AND current bar fast moving average > current bar slow moving average AND (RSI filter disabled OR current RSI > 50) AND (confirmation distance is zero OR a pending bullish cross exists and current high - low of its cross bar >= confirmation distance and current close <= current high) AND (current low >= prior long reference low + flat threshold OR current low <= prior long reference low - flat threshold) AND current direction <= 0; calculate on the chart timeframe or the selected higher timeframe's bar aligned to the chart bar, using the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
Short: Previous bar fast moving average >= previous bar slow moving average AND current bar fast moving average < current bar slow moving average AND (RSI filter disabled OR current RSI < 50) AND (confirmation distance is zero OR a pending bearish cross exists and high of its cross bar - current low >= confirmation distance and current open >= current close) AND (current high >= prior short reference high + flat threshold OR current high <= prior short reference high - flat threshold) AND current direction >= 0; calculate on the chart timeframe or the selected higher timeframe's bar aligned to the chart bar, using the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
