Calculation: Directional state = Initialize state, bullish reference low, bearish reference high, and both pending levels to zero. Process bars chronologically using configurable short- and long-period moving averages of close, with the same method and shift. On a strict upward crossover, if the optional close RSI filter is disabled or RSI is above its midpoint, immediately perform the bullish update when the confirmation distance is zero; otherwise store the current low as the pending bullish level and clear the pending bearish level. On a strict downward crossover, if the RSI filter is disabled or RSI is below its midpoint, immediately perform the bearish update when the confirmation distance is zero; otherwise store the current high as the pending bearish level and clear the pending bullish level. After processing crossovers, if a pending bullish level is nonzero and high minus that level is at least the confirmation distance and close is at or below high, perform the bullish update and clear that pending level. If a pending bearish level is nonzero and that level minus low is at least the confirmation distance and open is at or above close, perform the bearish update and clear that pending level. A bullish update sets the state to positive and the bullish reference to the current low only if the absolute difference between that low and the previous bullish reference is at least the flat-distance parameter. A bearish update sets the state to negative and the bearish reference to the current high only if the absolute difference between that high and the previous bearish reference is at least the flat-distance parameter. Otherwise retain the state and reference. Calculations may use the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for the higher-timeframe version use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and carry its result across chart bars until the next higher-timeframe bar.
