Calculation: Laguerre oscillator = On the chart timeframe for indicator markings, or on a configurable higher, lower, or equal indicator timeframe for trading signals, initialize four stages to the first available close used for calculation. For each bar, using previous-bar stages p0 through p3 and smoothing parameter g, compute s0=(1-g)*close+g*p0, s1=-g*s0+p0+g*p1, s2=-g*s1+p1+g*p2, and s3=-g*s2+p2+g*p3. Let U=sum(max(si-s(i+1),0)) and D=sum(max(s(i+1)-si,0)) for i=0,1,2. The oscillator is 100*U/(U+D) when U+D is nonzero; otherwise it is 100 times its previous value, initialized to zero.
Calculation: Direction state = Classify each oscillator value as above the upper level, above the middle level through the upper level, from the lower level through the middle level, or below the lower level. Starting after the indicator warmup with neutral state, set the state to +1 when the current value is above the upper level or moves into the upper-middle region from either lower region or into the lower-middle region from below the lower level. Set it to -1 when the current value is below the lower level or moves into the lower-middle region from either upper region or into the upper-middle region from above the upper level. Otherwise retain the previous state. The upper, middle, and lower levels are ordered thresholds.
Long: chart-timeframe direction state = +1
Short: chart-timeframe direction state = -1
Long: indicator-timeframe direction state on the selected bar = +1 AND direction state on its preceding bar = -1; the selected bar is the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal-bar setting
Short: indicator-timeframe direction state on the selected bar = -1 AND direction state on its preceding bar = +1; the selected bar is the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal-bar setting
Exit: long, indicator-timeframe direction state on the selected bar = -1; the selected bar is the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal-bar setting
Exit: short, indicator-timeframe direction state on the selected bar = +1; the selected bar is the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal-bar setting
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
