Calculation: Volume-weighted oscillator = smooth (100 × CU / (CU + CD) − 50) × volume, using a selectable moving-average method and period; use −50 × volume when CU + CD = 0. For each bar, let X = high + low − 2 × EMA(close), where the EMA uses the oscillator lookback. Starting with L0 = L1 = L2 = L3 = 0, update L0 = (1 − gamma) × X + gamma × previous L0, L1 = −gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1, L2 = −gamma × L1 + previous L1 + gamma × previous L2, and L3 = −gamma × L2 + previous L2 + gamma × previous L3. CU is the sum of positive differences L0 − L1, L1 − L2, and L2 − L3; CD is the sum of their absolute negative differences. Apply the same smoothing method and period separately to volume to obtain smoothed volume. The oscillator and smoothed volume may use chart bars or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Volume-weighted oscillator > positive threshold × smoothed volume
Short: Volume-weighted oscillator < negative threshold × smoothed volume
