Calculation: Price path efficiency = (close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over N bars; calculated on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Kinetic displacement = current close − mean close over N chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Energy dispersion = population standard deviation of closes over N chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Phase velocity = current close − close N bars ago.
Calculation: KCI compression score = sum of the six pairwise absolute differences among the rolling Z-scores of price path efficiency, kinetic displacement, energy dispersion, and phase velocity; each Z-score uses the longer lookback, and all four components use the same shorter lookback.
Long: KCI compression score two bars ago < score one bar ago AND KCI compression score two bars ago < score three bars ago AND KCI compression score two bars ago ≤ compression threshold AND tick volume two bars ago × (close two bars ago − close three bars ago)² < tick volume one bar ago × (close one bar ago − close two bars ago)² AND close two bars ago < close three bars ago AND Williams %R on the last completed chart-timeframe bar ≤ oversold threshold AND energy dispersion on the last completed chart-timeframe bar ≠ 0.
Short: KCI compression score two bars ago < score one bar ago AND KCI compression score two bars ago < score three bars ago AND KCI compression score two bars ago ≤ compression threshold AND tick volume two bars ago × (close two bars ago − close three bars ago)² < tick volume one bar ago × (close one bar ago − close two bars ago)² AND close two bars ago ≥ close three bars ago AND Williams %R on the last completed chart-timeframe bar ≥ overbought threshold AND energy dispersion on the last completed chart-timeframe bar ≠ 0.
Exit: long, price ≤ entry price − energy dispersion at entry × stop-loss multiplier OR price ≥ entry price + energy dispersion at entry × take-profit multiplier OR price ≤ active trailing stop.
Exit: short, price ≥ entry price + energy dispersion at entry × stop-loss multiplier OR price ≤ entry price − energy dispersion at entry × take-profit multiplier OR price ≥ active trailing stop.
