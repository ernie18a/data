Calculation: Signal line = smooth the weighted sum of 10 moving averages of the same applied price, using progressively longer lookbacks and their respective weights.
Calculation: Signal-line change = current signal line − previous bar’s signal line.
Calculation: Change standard deviation = population standard deviation of the signal-line changes over a lookback.
Long: Signal line > previous bar’s signal line OR signal-line change > lower deviation threshold
Short: Signal line < previous bar’s signal line OR signal-line change < −lower deviation threshold
