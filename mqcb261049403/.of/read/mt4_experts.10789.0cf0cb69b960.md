Calculation: avg_entry = size-weighted average entry price of all open same-side positions; each add-on position's size = base size x max(1, number of positions open at that moment)
Long: (no open position AND on the higher-timeframe bar still forming, (close / high - 1) x 100 >= -threshold, i.e. close is within threshold percent below that bar's high, AND last completed chart-timeframe bar close > open) OR (position side is long AND no short open AND price < lowest long entry price - fixed distance)
Short: (no open position AND on the higher-timeframe bar still forming, (close / low - 1) x 100 <= threshold, i.e. close is within threshold percent above that bar's low, AND last completed chart-timeframe bar close < open) OR (position side is short AND no long open AND price > highest short entry price + fixed distance)
Exit: long, (one open long position AND high >= entry price + fixed profit distance) OR (two or more open long positions AND high >= avg_entry + small offset); close all long positions
Exit: short, (one open short position AND low <= entry price - fixed profit distance) OR (two or more open short positions AND low <= avg_entry - small offset); close all short positions
