Calculation: GMMA = multiple exponential moving averages grouped into shorter-term green, medium-term red, and longer-term yellow lines
Calculation: MACD = MACD main line
Calculation: Daily pivot = (previous completed higher-timeframe daily high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: First resistance = 2 × daily pivot − previous completed higher-timeframe daily low
Calculation: Second resistance = daily pivot + (previous completed higher-timeframe daily high − low)
Calculation: First support = 2 × daily pivot − previous completed higher-timeframe daily high
Calculation: Second support = daily pivot − (previous completed higher-timeframe daily high − low)
Calculation: Volatility channel = simple moving average of highs and simple moving average of lows, using the same lookback
Long: Short-term GMMA lines are above medium-term lines AND the GMMA lines are rising AND more than half of the GMMA lines are rising AND the Laguerre line crosses above its lower threshold after having been below it while the completed bar closed below the longest-term GMMA line and its low was below the shortest-term GMMA line AND the short EMA crosses above the long EMA after having been below it AND MACD is above zero
Short: Short-term GMMA lines are below medium-term lines AND the GMMA lines are falling AND more than half of the GMMA lines are falling AND the Laguerre line crosses below its upper threshold after having been above it while the completed bar closed above the longest-term GMMA line and its high was above the shortest-term GMMA line AND the short EMA crosses below the long EMA after having been above it AND MACD is below zero
Exit: Long, position is profitable AND price is above first resistance; after this partial exit, the red HMA direction line appears OR price is above second resistance OR the completed bar opens below the volatility channel's lower line
Exit: Short, position is profitable AND price is below first support; after this partial exit, the blue HMA direction line appears OR price is below second support OR the completed bar opens above the volatility channel's upper line
