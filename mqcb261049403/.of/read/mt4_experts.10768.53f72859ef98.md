Calculation: Upper entry level = the first buffered resistance above price from the nearer or farther higher-timeframe bar, otherwise price plus the anchor distance and buffers; resistance is that bar’s high plus its ATR.
Calculation: Lower entry level = the first buffered support below price from the nearer or farther higher-timeframe bar, otherwise price minus the anchor distance and buffers; support is that bar’s low minus its ATR.
Calculation: Bullish condition = current RSI is above the upper threshold on each of three lower timeframes AND prior RSI is below the midpoint on each of them AND the longer-lookback EMA is above the shorter-lookback EMA on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish condition = current RSI is below the upper threshold on each of three lower timeframes AND prior RSI is above the midpoint on each of them AND the longer-lookback EMA is below the shorter-lookback EMA on the chart timeframe.
Long: price >= upper entry level
Short: price <= lower entry level
Exit: long, price <= active stop-loss
Exit: short, price >= active stop-loss
