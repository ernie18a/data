Calculation: Fractal Weight Oscillator = the configured smoothing of the weighted mean of Fractal RSI, Fractal MFI, Fractal WPR + 100, and 100 × Fractal DeMarker
Long: (configured signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; latest completed bar > oversold level AND previous completed bar ≤ oversold level) OR (Trend is against signals AND latest completed bar > overbought level AND previous completed bar ≤ overbought level)
Short: (configured signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; latest completed bar < overbought level AND previous completed bar ≥ overbought level) OR (Trend is against signals AND latest completed bar < oversold level AND previous completed bar ≥ oversold level)
Exit: long, (Trend is with signals AND latest completed bar < overbought level AND previous completed bar ≥ overbought level) OR (Trend is against signals AND latest completed bar > oversold level AND previous completed bar ≤ oversold level)
Exit: short, (Trend is with signals AND latest completed bar > oversold level AND previous completed bar ≤ oversold level) OR (Trend is against signals AND latest completed bar < overbought level AND previous completed bar ≥ overbought level)
