Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the lower-timeframe bars from the session start through the bar containing the opening-range end, inclusive; include that bar as still forming at the opening-range end and freeze the range then. If the range timeframe equals the chart timeframe, use the corresponding chart bars.
Long: Current bar start time >= opening-range end AND current bar start time <= trading-window end AND trades entered this session < session trade limit AND opening-range low > 0 AND opening-range high > opening-range low AND previous chart bar close > opening-range high + breakout buffer.
Short: Current bar start time >= opening-range end AND current bar start time <= trading-window end AND trades entered this session < session trade limit AND opening-range low > 0 AND opening-range high > opening-range low AND previous chart bar close < opening-range low - breakout buffer.
Exit: long, bar low <= opening-range low at entry - stop buffer OR bar high >= entry price + reward-to-risk ratio * (entry price - (opening-range low at entry - stop buffer)).
Exit: short, bar high >= opening-range high at entry + stop buffer OR bar low <= entry price - reward-to-risk ratio * ((opening-range high at entry + stop buffer) - entry price).
