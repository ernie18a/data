Calculation: Trend = compare the chart price with the EMA of close on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar: bullish if price > EMA × an upper multiplier; otherwise bearish if price < EMA × a lower multiplier; otherwise bullish if the difference between that EMA and its value two higher-timeframe bars earlier > a positive slope threshold, bearish if the difference < the negative slope threshold, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Trending = absolute difference between the shorter-period and longer-period chart-timeframe EMAs > a price-distance threshold AND current ATR > a multiplier × the average ATR over a short lookback including the current bar. Ranging = Trending is false.
Calculation: Support and resistance levels = scan chart-timeframe highs and lows within a lookback, including the current bar. A swing high is strictly greater than the highs on both sides within a shorter lookback; a swing low is strictly lower than the corresponding lows. Group same-type swings within a price buffer of the first level in each group, counting touches; support comes from lows and resistance from highs. Strength is touch count multiplied by an age factor. Retain the strongest levels and refresh them periodically.
Calculation: Channel = linear regressions of chart-timeframe highs and lows over the same lookback, indexed from the current bar backward. The upper and lower boundaries are the respective regression intercepts. A channel is valid when the absolute correlations of both regressions exceed a threshold AND the absolute difference between their slopes is below a threshold. Refresh it periodically.
Calculation: Retracement level = highest high over the swing lookback, including the current bar, minus a retracement fraction multiplied by the difference between that highest high and the lowest low over the same lookback.
Long: Ranging AND channel is valid AND absolute distance from price to the lower channel boundary <= channel buffer AND RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: Ranging AND channel is valid AND absolute distance from price to the upper channel boundary <= channel buffer AND RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: Trending AND trend is bullish AND absolute distance from price to a support level <= zone buffer AND support strength >= minimum strength AND RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: Trending AND trend is bearish AND absolute distance from price to a resistance level <= zone buffer AND resistance strength >= minimum strength AND RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: Resistance strength >= minimum strength AND price − resistance is between minimum and maximum breakout distances inclusive AND price − resistance <= zone buffer AND trend is not bearish AND ATR >= minimum volatility AND RSI <= upper extreme threshold.
Short: Support strength >= minimum strength AND support − price is between minimum and maximum breakout distances inclusive AND support − price <= zone buffer AND trend is not bullish AND ATR >= minimum volatility AND RSI >= lower extreme threshold.
Long: Ranging AND trend is bullish AND absolute distance from price to the selected retracement level <= entry distance AND RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: Ranging AND trend is bearish AND absolute distance from price to the selected retracement level <= entry distance AND RSI > overbought threshold.
Long: Trend is bullish AND ATR <= maximum volatility.
Short: Trend is bearish AND ATR <= maximum volatility.
Exit: long, price − entry price >= take-profit distance.
Exit: short, entry price − price >= take-profit distance.
Exit: long, price has risen at least the breakeven activation distance above entry price AND price <= the greater of entry price and the highest price since entry minus trailing distance.
Exit: short, price has fallen at least the breakeven activation distance below entry price AND price >= the lesser of entry price and the lowest price since entry plus trailing distance.
