Calculation: Volume-weighted CCI close = CCI of close multiplied by volume.
Calculation: Volume-weighted CCI open = previous bar's volume-weighted CCI close if the absolute difference between the current bar's open and close is at most a gap threshold; otherwise, CCI of open multiplied by current volume. Both CCIs use the same lookback.
Long: Volume-weighted CCI close >= volume-weighted CCI open.
Short: Volume-weighted CCI close < volume-weighted CCI open.
