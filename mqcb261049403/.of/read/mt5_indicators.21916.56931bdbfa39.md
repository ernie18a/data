Calculation: Volume-weighted CCI candle = CCI applied separately to the bar’s open, high, low, and close, each multiplied by the selected bar volume; candle high and low are the maximum and minimum of those four values. Candle open is the previous bar’s volume-weighted CCI close when the bar’s price open-close distance is within the configured gap, otherwise it is the current bar’s volume-weighted CCI open. Upper and lower channel levels are the configured high and low levels multiplied by bar volume.
Long: Volume-weighted CCI candle close > volume-weighted CCI candle open OR (volume-weighted CCI candle close = volume-weighted CCI candle open AND volume-weighted CCI candle close >= lower channel level)
Short: Volume-weighted CCI candle close < volume-weighted CCI candle open OR (volume-weighted CCI candle close = volume-weighted CCI candle open AND volume-weighted CCI candle close < lower channel level)
