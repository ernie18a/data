Calculation: Regression slope = least-squares slope of weighted price (high + low + 2 × close) / 4 over a chart-bar lookback including the current bar, with time increasing from oldest to newest.
Long: Regression slope > 0.
Short: Regression slope < 0.
Long: The most recent highest high is at least as recent as the most recent lowest low over a chart-bar lookback including the current bar.
Short: The most recent highest high is older than the most recent lowest low over a chart-bar lookback including the current bar.
