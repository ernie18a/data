Calculation: Fisher center-of-gravity oscillator = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, let n be the lookback and p[i] the midpoint of high and low i bars ago. Compute CG = -sum((i+1)*p[i], i=0..n-1)/sum(p[i], i=0..n-1) + (n+1)/2; use 0 if the denominator is 0. Normalize CG to (CG - lowest CG)/(highest CG - lowest CG) over the latest n bars, using 0 if the range is 0. Let v be the weighted average of the current and preceding three normalized values with weights 4, 3, 2, 1, divided by 10. The oscillator is 0.5*ln((1+1.98*(v-0.5))/(1-1.98*(v-0.5))). Initialize unavailable CG and normalized values to 0. At the first chart bar of each oscillator-timeframe bar, evaluate the still-forming oscillator-timeframe bar and hold that comparison until the next oscillator-timeframe bar.
