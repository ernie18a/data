Calculation: Weighted momentum oscillator = For five momentum calculations with successively longer lookbacks, calculate momentum as 100 times the selected applied price divided by its price at the corresponding lookback. Sum the five momentum values using configurable weights, with the second and third momentum values sharing the second weight. Smooth this sum recursively as S = sum / first smoothing period + previous S * (first smoothing period - 1) / first smoothing period. Then smooth the difference recursively as oscillator = (sum - S) / second smoothing period + previous oscillator * (second smoothing period - 1) / second smoothing period. Initialize S to the first sum divided by the first smoothing period and the oscillator to (first sum - S) divided by the second smoothing period.
Long: Chart-timeframe weighted momentum oscillator is not zero AND chart-timeframe weighted momentum oscillator > its previous-bar value
Short: Chart-timeframe weighted momentum oscillator is not zero AND chart-timeframe weighted momentum oscillator < its previous-bar value
Long: For any configured higher or lower timeframe, the weighted momentum oscillator on its last completed bar is not zero AND is greater than its value on the preceding bar
Short: For any configured higher or lower timeframe, the weighted momentum oscillator on its last completed bar is not zero AND is less than its value on the preceding bar
