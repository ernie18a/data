Calculation: Double exponential moving average (DEMA) = 2 × EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close)); at each evaluation, initialize both EMA recursions with the close at a fixed history horizon and recalculate through the current bar.
Long: A new chart-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed bar of a higher timeframe opens below its lower Bollinger Band and closes above that band AND the DEMA of a second, higher timeframe is greater on its forming bar than on its last completed bar AND its last completed DEMA is greater than its preceding DEMA AND the first higher timeframe's close EMA on its last completed bar is greater than on the preceding bar.
Short: A new chart-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed bar of a higher timeframe opens above its upper Bollinger Band and closes below that band AND the DEMA of a second, higher timeframe is less on its forming bar than on its last completed bar AND its last completed DEMA is less than its preceding DEMA AND the first higher timeframe's close EMA on its last completed bar is less than on the preceding bar.
Exit: long, a new chart-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed bar of the Bollinger Band higher timeframe opens above its upper band and closes below that band.
Exit: short, a new chart-timeframe bar begins AND the last completed bar of the Bollinger Band higher timeframe opens below its lower band and closes above that band.
