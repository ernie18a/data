Calculation: Trading Channel Index = smooth((selected price − moving average of selected price) / (coefficient × moving average of the absolute difference between selected price and that moving average)); the center and deviation averages use the same smoothing method and lookback, and the final smoothing uses its separately configured method and lookback.
Long: On the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, the last completed bar’s Trading Channel Index > upper threshold AND the previous completed bar’s Trading Channel Index <= upper threshold.
Short: On the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, the last completed bar’s Trading Channel Index < lower threshold AND the previous completed bar’s Trading Channel Index >= lower threshold.
Exit: long, on the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, the last completed bar’s Trading Channel Index < lower threshold AND the previous completed bar’s Trading Channel Index >= lower threshold OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, on the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, the last completed bar’s Trading Channel Index > upper threshold AND the previous completed bar’s Trading Channel Index <= upper threshold OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
