Calculation: Liquidity pools = enabled previous-day high and low from the last completed daily bar, and enabled current-day session high and low from chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session, available only after the session ends and when the chart timeframe is no greater than the configured maximum. The daily timeframe is higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe according to their durations. Search at most the configured number of preceding chart bars for the session range.
Calculation: Opposite swing = the most recent eligible swing before the sweep, searched within a configured lookback. A swing high is strictly higher than each of the configured number of older neighboring highs and at least as high as each of the same number of newer neighboring highs; a swing low is strictly lower than the older neighboring lows and at most equal to the newer neighboring lows. All neighboring bars must precede the sweep bar.
Calculation: Sweep state = maintain one active state per direction. On each closed bar, first search for a new sweep only if that direction has no active state; check the previous-day pool before the session pool. Save the sweep bar, its extreme, and its opposite swing. Then invalidate the state if the current bar exceeds the saved extreme or its age exceeds the configured maximum; otherwise test for confirmation, including on the sweep bar. Clear the state after confirmation.
Calculation: Relative volume = sweep bar volume divided by the average volume of the configured number of preceding bars; use zero when the average is zero.
Calculation: Stop = the confirmed sweep low minus a configured price buffer for a long, or the confirmed sweep high plus that buffer for a short. Risk = entry price minus stop for a long, or stop minus entry price for a short. Target = entry price plus a configured multiple of risk for a long, or entry price minus that multiple for a short.
Long: No active long sweep state AND a valid opposite swing high exists AND low < an enabled low pool AND close > that pool AND (minimum relative volume <= 0 OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume) AND ATR > 0.
Short: No active short sweep state AND a valid opposite swing low exists AND high > an enabled high pool AND close < that pool AND (minimum relative volume <= 0 OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume) AND ATR > 0.
Long: Active long sweep state remains valid AND close > saved swing high AND high - low >= configured multiple of ATR AND ATR > 0.
Short: Active short sweep state remains valid AND close < saved swing low AND high - low >= configured multiple of ATR AND ATR > 0.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target.
