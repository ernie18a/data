Calculation: Force Index = volume × (N-period moving average of applied price on the current bar − the same moving average on the previous bar)
Calculation: Short-term Force Index average = moving average of Force Index with a shorter lookback
Calculation: Long-term Force Index average = moving average of Force Index with a longer lookback than the short-term average
Long: Short-term Force Index average < 0 AND current long-term Force Index average > its previous value OR Force Index reaches a new peak
Short: Short-term Force Index average > 0 AND current long-term Force Index average < its previous value OR Force Index reaches a new trough
