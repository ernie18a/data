Calculation: Applied price = selected close, open, high, low, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, trend-follow price, smoothed trend-follow price, or DeMark price. Trend-follow price is high if close>open, low if close<open, otherwise close. Smoothed trend-follow price is (high+close)/2 if close>open, (low+close)/2 if close<open, otherwise close. DeMark price is (X-low+X-high)/2, where X is (high+low+close+low)/2 if close<open, (high+low+close+high)/2 if close>open, otherwise (high+low+2*close)/2.
Calculation: Fisher = calculate on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; use its last completed bar by default, or its still-forming bar when configured. For each indicator bar, H is the highest high and L the lowest low over the lookback including that bar; if H=L, increase H by a minimum price increment parameter. Let W=(applied price-L)/(H-L), V=clamp(W-0.5+0.67*previous V,-999,999), and R=(1+V)/(1-V); replace R with 1 if R<0.0000001. Fisher=0.5*ln(R)+0.5*previous Fisher. Initialize previous V and Fisher to zero before the first calculated bar.
Long: Fisher > lower threshold AND previous Fisher <= lower threshold
Short: Fisher < upper threshold AND previous Fisher >= upper threshold
Exit: long, the most recent Fisher threshold crossing is below the upper threshold OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, the most recent Fisher threshold crossing is above the lower threshold OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
