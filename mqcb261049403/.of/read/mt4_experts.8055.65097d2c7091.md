Calculation: Shifted oscillator = short-period simple moving average of median price minus long-period simple moving average of median price; both averages are shifted backward by their respective shift parameters, with the long-period average shifted farther.
Calculation: Fractal zigzag level = on the higher timeframe, find confirmed upper and lower fractals using bars available through the forming bar. Remove repeated equal fractal values and, for adjacent candidates of the same type, remove the less extreme older candidate. Construct alternating high and low pivots, replacing the latest pivot when a more extreme same-type candidate appears before an opposite-type candidate. At the last completed bar, carry forward the most recent nonzero pivot value.
Long: Shifted oscillator on the chart timeframe > its previous-bar value OR shifted oscillator on the chart timeframe = its previous-bar value AND its most recent unequal change was upward.
Short: Shifted oscillator on the chart timeframe < its previous-bar value OR shifted oscillator on the chart timeframe = its previous-bar value AND its most recent unequal change was downward.
Short: Higher-timeframe forming-bar open > higher-timeframe last-completed-bar low minus a price offset AND chart-timeframe close < higher-timeframe last-completed-bar low minus the price offset AND chart-timeframe close > higher-timeframe fractal zigzag level AND chart-timeframe shifted oscillator < 0.
