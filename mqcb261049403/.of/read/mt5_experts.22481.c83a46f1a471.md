Calculation: Histogram = moving average of (Williams %R + 50) × volume, calculated on the chart timeframe or an independently selected higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed indicator-timeframe bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting.
Calculation: Histogram direction = up when the histogram is greater than its previous indicator-timeframe value, down when it is less, and otherwise the previous direction.
Long: Histogram direction = up.
Short: Histogram direction = down.
Exit: long, histogram direction = down OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, histogram direction = up OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
