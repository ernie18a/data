Calculation: Candle Stochastic Index = 100 × the third-stage smoothed momentum divided by the third-stage smoothed range. Momentum is the configured applied-price series minus the other configured applied-price series from XLength bars earlier; range is the highest high minus the lowest low over XLength bars. Smooth momentum and range separately in three successive stages using the same smoothing method, with respective lookbacks XLength1, XLength2, and XLength3.
Long: Candle Stochastic Index on the current forming higher-timeframe bar > 0 AND Candle Stochastic Index on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar < 0
Short: Candle Stochastic Index on the current forming higher-timeframe bar < 0 AND Candle Stochastic Index on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar > 0
