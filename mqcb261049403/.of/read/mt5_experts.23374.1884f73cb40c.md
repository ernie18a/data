Calculation: Adaptive Renko trend = On each indicator-timeframe bar, use close as both the high and low, or use the bar’s high and low, according to the price mode. Set brick size to the greater of a multiplier times ATR or close-price standard deviation (the selected volatility measure), and the minimum brick width. If the selected high exceeds the upper bound plus the prior brick size, advance the upper bound by the whole number of prior bricks crossed, set the brick size to the current brick size, and set the lower bound to upper bound minus brick size. If the selected low falls below the lower bound minus the prior brick size, move the lower bound down by the whole number of prior bricks crossed, set the brick size to the current brick size, and set the upper bound to lower bound plus brick size. Trend becomes bullish when the upper bound rises, bearish when the lower bound falls, and otherwise retains its prior state. Evaluate signals on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar.
Long: Adaptive Renko trend changes from bearish on the preceding bar to bullish on the signal bar.
Short: Adaptive Renko trend changes from bullish on the preceding bar to bearish on the signal bar.
Exit: Long, Adaptive Renko trend is bearish on the signal bar.
Exit: Short, Adaptive Renko trend is bullish on the signal bar.
