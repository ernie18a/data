Calculation: Skyscraper state and bounds = On the first eligible bar, initialize the previous lower and upper bounds to that bar's close and the previous state to neutral. On each bar, calculate ATR and the maximum and minimum ATR over a lookback including the current bar. Set step to the price increment multiplied by the integer part of sensitivity × (maximum ATR + minimum ATR) / (2 × price increment). Set the candidate upper bound to low + 2 × step and the candidate lower bound to high − 2 × step; alternatively, use close in place of both low and high. Starting with the previous state, set the state to up if close > previous upper bound, then set it to down if close < previous lower bound. If the state is up, set the lower bound to max(candidate lower bound, previous lower bound) and the upper bound to the candidate upper bound. Otherwise, set the upper bound to min(candidate upper bound, previous upper bound) and the lower bound to the candidate lower bound. Carry the state and both bounds forward. The ATR period and the ATR-extrema lookback are independently parameterized.

Short: Chart-timeframe Skyscraper state is not up AND previous chart-timeframe Skyscraper state = up
Long: Higher-timeframe Skyscraper state = up, evaluated on the selected higher-timeframe bar, which may be the still-forming bar or a completed bar at a configurable offset
Short: Higher-timeframe Skyscraper state is not up, evaluated on the selected higher-timeframe bar, which may be the still-forming bar or a completed bar at a configurable offset
