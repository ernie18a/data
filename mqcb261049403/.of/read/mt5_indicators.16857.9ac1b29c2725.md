Calculation: ZigZag pivots = Process chart bars chronologically. For each bar, find the lowest low and highest high over a lookback including that bar. For each side, discard the rolling extreme if it equals that side's previous rolling extreme; otherwise update the previous rolling extreme. If the current low is within a deviation threshold of a new rolling low, remove higher low candidates within the preceding backstep window; record a low candidate only if the current low equals the rolling low. Apply the symmetric rules to highs. Starting with no pivot, record any candidate high and candidate low on the first qualifying bar; if both occur, seek a high next. After a low pivot, replace it with a lower low candidate that has no simultaneous high candidate, or record a high candidate with no simultaneous low candidate and then seek a low. After a high pivot, replace it with a higher high candidate that has no simultaneous low candidate, or record a low candidate with no simultaneous high candidate and then seek a high. Ignore simultaneous high and low candidates after initialization. Recalculate recent pivots as new bars arrive; the latest pivots can move or disappear.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot marks = Apply the same ZigZag pivot calculation to a higher timeframe using its bar still forming. Place each higher-timeframe high pivot on the first chart bar within or after that higher-timeframe bar whose high is at least the pivot price, and each low pivot on the first such chart bar whose low is at most the pivot price. Update or remove marks when their underlying pivots change.
Long: A ZigZag high pivot is present OR a higher-timeframe high pivot mark is present.
Short: A ZigZag low pivot is present OR a higher-timeframe low pivot mark is present.
