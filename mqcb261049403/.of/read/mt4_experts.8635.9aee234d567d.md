Calculation: Hourly X, Y, and Z AO scores = for each score, (w1−50) + [(w2−50)×AO(p) + (w3−50)×AO(2p) + (w4−50)×AO(3p)] / AO(0); return 0 when the absolute raw score is at most its threshold. AO is the Awesome Oscillator. Use the higher, hourly timeframe: AO(0) is the bar still forming; AO(p), AO(2p), and AO(3p) are completed bars. X, Y, and Z use independently parameterized lookbacks; no ordering is specified.
Calculation: Chart-bar Z price score = (z1−50)×(current chart-bar close−open pz chart bars ago) + (z2−50)×(open pz bars ago−open 2×pz bars ago) + (z3−50)×(open 2×pz bars ago−open 3×pz bars ago) + (z4−50)×(open 3×pz bars ago−open 4×pz bars ago). Use the current chart bar, which is still forming.
Long: Z price score > 0 AND hourly X AO score > 0 AND not Monday before 02:00 AND not Friday at or after 18:00 AND fewer than 2 open orders total AND fewer than 1 open long order
Short: Z price score < 0 AND hourly Y AO score > 0 AND not Monday before 02:00 AND not Friday at or after 18:00 AND fewer than 2 open orders total AND fewer than 1 open short order
Exit: Long, Friday at or after 22:00 OR price reaches the long stop-loss or take-profit OR (price > stop-loss + 2×initial stop distance + spread AND chart-bar Z price score < 0 AND hourly Y AO score > 0 AND four-hour AO(1)−AO(2) < 0)
Exit: Short, Friday at or after 22:00 OR price reaches the short stop-loss or take-profit OR (price < stop-loss − 2×initial stop distance − spread AND chart-bar Z price score > 0 AND hourly X AO score > 0 AND four-hour AO(1)−AO(2) > 0)
