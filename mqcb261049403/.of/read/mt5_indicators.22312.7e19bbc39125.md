Calculation: Skyscraper trend = On each chart-timeframe bar, calculate ATR and set step to the sensitivity factor times the mean of the highest and lowest ATR over a lookback, rounded down to a price increment. Using either High/Low or Close as the price source, calculate raw lower rail = source high − 2 × step and raw upper rail = source low + 2 × step (when using Close, use Close for both). Set trend bullish when Close > the prior bar’s raw upper rail; bearish when Close < the prior bar’s raw lower rail; otherwise retain the prior trend. In bullish trend, raise the lower rail to at least its prior value; in bearish trend, lower the upper rail to at most its prior value. Initialize trend neutral and both prior rails to Close.
Long: Prior bar’s Skyscraper trend is bearish AND current bar’s Skyscraper trend is bullish.
Short: Prior bar’s Skyscraper trend is bullish AND current bar’s Skyscraper trend is bearish.
