Calculation: Confirmed pivot = on a selected timeframe, a high is a pivot if it is strictly higher than each of the specified number of newer completed highs and at least as high as each of the same number of older highs; a low is a pivot if it is strictly lower than each newer completed low and at most as low as each older low. Search a configurable lookback, excluding the bar still forming. Order pivots newest first, placing a high before a low when both occur on the same bar.
Calculation: Swing = starting at a configurable offset in the sequence of alternating pivot legs, pair the newer pivot with the first older pivot of the opposite type; the next leg starts at that older pivot. The swing high and low are the respective pivot prices and the swing is valid only if high > low. The previous swing is the next older leg. Recalculate swings at the start of each chart bar. Each selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or equal to it; use completed bars only.
Long: A valid swing on any selected timeframe has its high pivot newer in the pivot sequence than its low pivot.
Short: A valid swing on any selected timeframe has its low pivot newer in the pivot sequence than its high pivot.
Long: The current and previous swings on the primary selected timeframe are valid AND (the last completed primary-timeframe close > current swing high OR (current swing high > previous swing high AND current swing low > previous swing low AND last completed primary-timeframe close >= current swing low)).
Short: The current and previous swings on the primary selected timeframe are valid AND (the last completed primary-timeframe close < current swing low OR (current swing high < previous swing high AND current swing low < previous swing low AND last completed primary-timeframe close <= current swing high)).
