Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus stop-loss distance; at each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled AND open minus entry price > trailing distance plus trailing step AND stop < open minus trailing distance minus trailing step, set stop to open minus trailing distance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus stop-loss distance; at each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled AND entry price minus open > trailing distance plus trailing step AND stop > open plus trailing distance plus trailing step, set stop to open plus trailing distance.
Long: Fast moving average > normal moving average AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND fast Parabolic SAR < previous chart-bar close AND normal Parabolic SAR < previous chart-bar close. Use the last completed bar of each indicator's own timeframe; indicator timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and fast, normal, and slow use successively longer timeframes and moving-average lookbacks.
Short: Fast moving average < normal moving average AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND fast Parabolic SAR > previous chart-bar close AND normal Parabolic SAR > previous chart-bar close. Use the last completed bar of each indicator's own timeframe; indicator timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and fast, normal, and slow use successively longer timeframes and moving-average lookbacks.
Exit: long, Short condition OR stop loss enabled AND low <= long stop OR take profit enabled AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, Long condition OR stop loss enabled AND high >= short stop OR take profit enabled AND low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
