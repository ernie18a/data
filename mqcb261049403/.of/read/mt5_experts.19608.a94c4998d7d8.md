Calculation: Shifted moving average = moving average of the selected price, shifted forward by the configured number of indicator-timeframe bars; use the current forming bar when its timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR = Parabolic SAR on its independently selected timeframe; use the current forming bar when that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance for a long position, or entry price plus that distance for a short position; if stop loss is disabled, initially unset. When trailing is enabled, update it on each observation: for a long, if close - entry price > trailing distance + trailing step AND the stop is unset or stop < close - trailing distance - trailing step, set stop = close - trailing distance; for a short, if entry price - close > trailing distance + trailing step AND the stop is unset or stop > close + trailing distance + trailing step, set stop = close + trailing distance.
Long: Position is flat AND (Parabolic SAR < shifted moving average OR chart close at a bars-ago offset equal to the moving-average shift < shifted moving average)
Short: Position is flat AND Parabolic SAR >= shifted moving average AND chart close at a bars-ago offset equal to the moving-average shift >= shifted moving average AND (Parabolic SAR > shifted moving average OR chart close at that offset > shifted moving average)
Exit: long, stop is set AND low <= trailing stop OR take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, stop is set AND high >= trailing stop OR take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance
