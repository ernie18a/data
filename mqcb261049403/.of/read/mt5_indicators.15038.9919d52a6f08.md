Calculation: GFilter = fourth-order Gaussian recursive filter of the configured OHLC applied-price series P: α = −β + √(β² + 2β), β = [1 − cos(2π/N)] / [1.414^(2/3) − 1]; Gₜ = α⁴Pₜ + 4(1−α)Gₜ₋₁ − 6(1−α)²Gₜ₋₂ + 4(1−α)³Gₜ₋₃ − (1−α)⁴Gₜ₋₄, where N is the filter lookback. Compute on chart bars and on higher-timeframe bars; the higher-timeframe value uses the bar still forming.
