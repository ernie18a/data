Calculation: Input price = a selectable source: open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, high if close > open else low if close < open else close, or (high + close)/2 if close > open else (low + close)/2 if close < open else close.
Calculation: Gaussian filter = a four-pole recursive filter of input price. For lookback p, let w = 2π/p, beta = (1 − cos(w))/(1.414^(2/3) − 1), alpha = −beta + sqrt(beta² + 2 × beta), and filter[t] = alpha⁴ × input price[t] + 4 × (1 − alpha) × filter[t−1] − 6 × (1 − alpha)² × filter[t−2] + 4 × (1 − alpha)³ × filter[t−3] − (1 − alpha)⁴ × filter[t−4]. Start at bar p−1, indexing from zero, and seed the preceding four filter values with their respective input prices. Calculate either on chart bars or on higher-timeframe bars; for the higher-timeframe version, sample the still-forming higher-timeframe bar at the first chart bar in that higher-timeframe period and carry the sampled values forward until the next period.
Long: Gaussian filter[t] >= Gaussian filter[t−1].
Short: Gaussian filter[t] < Gaussian filter[t−1].
Long: Gaussian filter[t] − Gaussian filter[t−1] > Gaussian filter[t−1] − Gaussian filter[t−2].
Short: Gaussian filter[t] − Gaussian filter[t−1] < Gaussian filter[t−1] − Gaussian filter[t−2].
