Calculation: Signal bars = bars of a configurable timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar and its predecessor.
Calculation: First-entry time filter = current datetime is before the configured Friday closing hour on Friday, or the Friday close is disabled; AND the current hour is within the configured session, including an overnight session, or the session filter is disabled.
Long: No long position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is moving-average crossover AND ((signal reversal is off AND the shorter moving average on signal bars was <= the longer moving average on the preceding bar AND is > it on the last completed bar) OR (signal reversal is on AND the shorter moving average was >= the longer moving average AND is < it)).
Short: No short position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is moving-average crossover AND ((signal reversal is off AND the shorter moving average on signal bars was >= the longer moving average on the preceding bar AND is < it on the last completed bar) OR (signal reversal is on AND the shorter moving average was <= the longer moving average AND is > it)).
Long: No long position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is RSI AND ((signal reversal is off AND RSI on the last completed signal bar <= the lower threshold AND RSI < the upper threshold) OR (signal reversal is on AND RSI on that bar >= the upper threshold)).
Short: No short position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is RSI AND ((signal reversal is off AND RSI on the last completed signal bar >= the upper threshold) OR (signal reversal is on AND RSI on that bar <= the lower threshold AND RSI < the upper threshold)).
Long: No long position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is Bollinger Bands AND ((signal reversal is off AND the last completed signal bar's close < its lower band AND close <= its upper band) OR (signal reversal is on AND close > its upper band)).
Short: No short position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is Bollinger Bands AND ((signal reversal is off AND the last completed signal bar's close > its upper band) OR (signal reversal is on AND close < its lower band AND close <= its upper band)).
Long: No long position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is stochastic AND ((signal reversal is off AND stochastic %K on the last completed signal bar <= the lower threshold AND %K > %D) OR (signal reversal is on AND %K >= the upper threshold AND %K < %D)).
Short: No short position AND first-entry time filter passes AND the selected signal is stochastic AND ((signal reversal is off AND stochastic %K on the last completed signal bar >= the upper threshold AND %K < %D) OR (signal reversal is on AND %K <= the lower threshold AND %K > %D)).
Exit: long, Friday close is enabled AND the current datetime is Friday at or after the configured closing hour.
Exit: short, Friday close is enabled AND the current datetime is Friday at or after the configured closing hour.
