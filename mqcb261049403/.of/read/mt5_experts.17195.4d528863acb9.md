Calculation: Cross price = average of the fast and slow moving-average values on the two bars that form the crossover
Calculation: Recent range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding 120 seconds worth of completed chart bars, starting with the last completed bar
Long: The short-term EMA crosses above the longer-term SMA on either of the last two completed chart bars AND ask price >= cross price + trigger distance AND current time <= crossover time + 2 chart-bar durations AND H1 RSI on the bar still forming > upper threshold AND ask price > recent range high AND ask price > upper D1 Envelopes on the bar still forming AND ask price > upper H4 Envelopes on the bar still forming AND blackout inactive
Short: The short-term EMA crosses below the longer-term SMA on either of the last two completed chart bars AND bid price <= cross price - trigger distance AND current time <= crossover time + 2 chart-bar durations AND H1 RSI on the bar still forming < lower threshold AND bid price < recent range low AND bid price < lower D1 Envelopes on the bar still forming AND bid price < lower H4 Envelopes on the bar still forming AND blackout inactive
Exit: Long, price <= stop loss set to the low four chart bars ago or price >= entry price + take-profit distance; while open, raise the stop loss to bid minus trailing distance whenever that is above the current stop loss
Exit: Short, price >= stop loss set to the high four chart bars ago or price <= entry price - take-profit distance; while open, lower the stop loss to ask plus trailing distance whenever that is below the current stop loss
