Calculation: Signal bars = chart bars or bars aggregated to a configurable higher or lower timeframe; for another timeframe use the last completed bar. All offsets below refer to signal bars, with t as the evaluated bar.
Calculation: Weighted close W(p,t) = a × close[t] + (1 − a) × close[t−1], where a = 2 / (p + 1). Scan a parameterized grid of short and longer periods in ascending longer-period order, then ascending short-period order; retain only the first pair with a strict crossover at t.
Calculation: Mean range R[t] = mean of high − low over a fixed lookback ending at t.
Calculation: Oscillator O[t] = 100 + Williams %R at t, using an integer period obtained by truncating a base period derived from a risk parameter, except use a shorter override period when, within a shorter lookback ending at t, the absolute close change over a fixed lag is at least a multiple of R[t]. If the Williams %R range is zero, reuse its previous value, initialized at its neutral value. Use only the first risk parameter in its configured range. The lower and upper oscillator thresholds are respectively a base lower threshold minus risk and a base upper threshold plus risk.
Calculation: Prior extreme at t = the first earlier O value outside the inclusive lower-to-upper threshold interval, skipping all intervening values inside that interval; if no such value exists, neither oscillator condition is true.
Calculation: Up crossover at t = W(short,t−1) < W(long,t−1) AND W(short,t) > W(long,t) for the first crossover pair. Down crossover is defined by reversing both inequalities.
Calculation: Up oscillator event at t = O[t] > upper threshold AND prior extreme < lower threshold AND low[t] − a positive multiple of R[t] > 0. Down oscillator event at t = O[t] < lower threshold AND prior extreme > upper threshold AND high[t] + a positive multiple of R[t] > 0.
Calculation: Up candidate at t = (up crossover at t AND (up oscillator event at t OR at t−1)) OR (up crossover at t−1 AND up oscillator event at t). Down candidate is defined equivalently with down crossover and down oscillator event.
Long: Up candidate AND down candidate = false AND low − a positive multiple of R ≠ 0.
Short: Down candidate AND up candidate = false AND high + a positive multiple of R ≠ 0.
Exit: long, most recent long or short signal is short OR low ≤ entry price − enabled stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, most recent long or short signal is long OR high ≥ entry price + enabled stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − enabled take-profit distance.
