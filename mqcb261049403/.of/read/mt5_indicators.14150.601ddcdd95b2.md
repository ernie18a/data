Calculation: FatlMacd = a smoothed difference between the selected bar price and the 39-tap FATL digital filter of that price, divided by the minimum price increment; compare each value with the prior bar’s value. The smoothing method, period, phase, and selected price are configurable.
Long: FatlMacd > 0 AND FatlMacd > its prior-bar value
Short: FatlMacd < 0 AND FatlMacd < its prior-bar value
