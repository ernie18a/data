Calculation: F(short, k) and F(long, k) = Force Index using volume and, respectively, a shorter and a longer moving-average period, at k bars before the signal bar. The signal bar is the last completed bar of a higher timeframe for the trading algorithm, or the current chart bar for the indicator; open[k] and close[k] use the same timeframe and indexing.
Long: close[3] < open[3] AND close[2] > open[2] AND close[1] < open[1] AND F(short,4) > F(short,3) AND F(short,3) < F(short,2) AND F(short,2) > F(short,1) AND F(long,4) > F(long,3) AND F(long,3) < F(long,2) AND F(long,2) > F(long,1) AND ((F(short,3) > F(short,1) AND F(long,3) < F(long,1)) OR (F(short,3) < F(short,1) AND F(long,3) > F(long,1)))
Short: close[3] > open[3] AND close[2] < open[2] AND close[1] > open[1] AND F(short,4) < F(short,3) AND F(short,3) > F(short,2) AND F(short,2) < F(short,1) AND F(long,4) < F(long,3) AND F(long,3) > F(long,2) AND F(long,2) < F(long,1) AND ((F(short,3) > F(short,1) AND F(long,3) < F(long,1)) OR (F(short,3) < F(short,1) AND F(long,3) > F(long,1)))
Exit: long, the most recent higher-timeframe signal satisfies the Short condition OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, the most recent higher-timeframe signal satisfies the Long condition OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
