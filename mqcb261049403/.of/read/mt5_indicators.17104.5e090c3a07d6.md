Calculation: Selected price = a configurable OHLC price, median price, typical price, weighted close, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price. Trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise; its extreme variant is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi open starts at (open + close)/2 and subsequently equals the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close midpoint; Heikin-Ashi close is the OHLC average, high is the maximum of high and Heikin-Ashi open and close, and low is their minimum.
Calculation: Recursive CCI = CCI of the selected price smoothed by a configurable simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average, followed by a configurable number of additional CCI applications to the preceding CCI series, all using the same CCI lookback. At the start of each CCI series, divide sums of available observations and absolute deviations by the full lookback; return zero when the deviation is zero. Initialize the moving average from available observations; the smoothed moving average starts its recursive update on the third bar.
Calculation: Upper, lower, and middle levels = either the rolling minimum of recursive CCI plus configurable upper, lower, and midpoint fractions of its rolling range, or rolling recursive CCI quantiles at configurable upper and lower percentiles and their midpoint percentile. For quantiles, pad missing initial observations with zeros and linearly interpolate between sorted observations. The upper percentile exceeds the lower percentile.
Calculation: Direction state = a configurable level, middle, or slope mode. In level mode, set up when recursive CCI > upper level, down when recursive CCI < lower level, and neutral when recursive CCI < upper level AND recursive CCI > lower level; retain the previous state at equality. In middle mode, set up when recursive CCI > middle level and down when recursive CCI < middle level; retain the previous state at equality. In slope mode, set up when recursive CCI > previous recursive CCI and down when recursive CCI < previous recursive CCI; retain the previous state at equality. Initialize the state as neutral. Use slope mode when the level lookback is disabled or has only one bar. Calculate the state on the chart timeframe or a configurable higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use the bar still forming.
Long: Direction state = up
Short: Direction state = down
