Calculation: BrainTrend state = on the selected signal timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), calculate true range from high, low, and previous close; calculate a linearly weighted average of recent true ranges; multiply by a sensitivity factor to get a band width. Maintain a state and extreme: switch bullish to bearish when low falls below the extreme minus the band width, and bearish to bullish when high rises above the extreme plus the band width; while bullish, raise the extreme to each higher low, and while bearish, lower it to each lower high. Use completed bars.
Calculation: Double-smoothed LWMA = apply a linearly weighted moving average to the selected price, then apply a second linearly weighted moving average of the same lookback to the first result; use completed bars on the selected signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Double-smoothed candle open and close = apply the selected first moving-average method and lookback, then the selected second method and lookback, to each series in sequence; use completed bars on the selected signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. For candle color, replace the smoothed open with the previous bar's smoothed close when the absolute raw open-close difference is no greater than the gap threshold.
Long: previous completed BrainTrend state is bearish AND current completed BrainTrend state is bullish OR previous completed Double-smoothed LWMA is falling or flat AND current completed Double-smoothed LWMA is rising OR previous completed double-smoothed candle color is bearish or neutral AND current completed double-smoothed candle color is bullish.
Short: previous completed BrainTrend state is bullish AND current completed BrainTrend state is bearish OR previous completed Double-smoothed LWMA is rising or flat AND current completed Double-smoothed LWMA is falling OR previous completed double-smoothed candle color is bullish or neutral AND current completed double-smoothed candle color is bearish.
Exit: Long, previous completed BrainTrend state is bearish AND current completed BrainTrend state is bullish OR previous completed Double-smoothed LWMA is rising OR previous completed double-smoothed candle color is bullish OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, previous completed BrainTrend state is bullish AND current completed BrainTrend state is bearish OR previous completed Double-smoothed LWMA is falling OR previous completed double-smoothed candle color is bearish OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
