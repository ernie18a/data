Calculation: Channel state = On each chart bar, or on each higher-timeframe bar, calculate ATR and either a moving average of the selected price for both upper and lower bases, or moving averages of high and low for the upper and lower bases. Set the upper candidate to the upper base plus an ATR multiple and the lower candidate to the lower base minus the same ATR multiple. Initialize the previous upper barrier to zero, previous lower barrier to 999999999.9, and previous trend to zero. Set the trend to the previous trend; set it to positive if the bar's high, or optionally the upper base, exceeds the previous upper barrier; then set it to negative if the bar's low, or optionally the lower base, is below the previous lower barrier. In a positive trend, set the lower barrier to the maximum of its candidate and previous lower barrier; otherwise use its candidate. In a negative trend, set the upper barrier to the minimum of its candidate and previous upper barrier; otherwise use its candidate. Save the barriers and trend for the next bar.
Calculation: Displayed stop = In a positive trend, subtract an ATR-based offset from the lower barrier and, if the previous bar's positive-trend stop is nonzero, take the maximum of the result and that stop. In a negative trend, add the same ATR-based offset to the upper barrier and, if the previous bar's negative-trend stop is nonzero, take the minimum of the result and that stop. A stop for the inactive trend is zero.
Long: Channel trend is positive AND previous channel trend is not positive AND displayed stop is nonzero. For the higher-timeframe version, evaluate the higher-timeframe bar still forming and apply the decision only on the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe bar; historical calculation assigns the completed higher-timeframe bar's decision to that first chart bar.
Short: Channel trend is negative AND previous channel trend is not negative AND displayed stop is nonzero. For the higher-timeframe version, evaluate the higher-timeframe bar still forming and apply the decision only on the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe bar; historical calculation assigns the completed higher-timeframe bar's decision to that first chart bar.
