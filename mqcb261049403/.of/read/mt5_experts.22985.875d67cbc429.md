Calculation: Aggressiveness = normalize the simple moving average of absolute changes in close over a lookback to its minimum and maximum over a separate interpolation lookback, using 100 × (value − minimum) / (maximum − minimum); return 1000000 when maximum equals minimum.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, the current price is above the open of the forming higher-timeframe direction bar AND the RSI of the forming higher-timeframe bar is below its upper threshold AND aggressiveness on the forming higher-timeframe bar is below its threshold AND no long position is open AND the current time is before the Friday cutoff or the current day is not Friday.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, the current price is below the open of the forming higher-timeframe direction bar AND the RSI of the forming higher-timeframe bar is above its lower threshold AND aggressiveness on the forming higher-timeframe bar is below its threshold AND no short position is open AND the current time is before the Friday cutoff or the current day is not Friday.
Exit: long, the initial long position's high reaches or exceeds its entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the initial short position's low reaches or falls below its entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
