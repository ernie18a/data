Calculation: Normalized average absolute close change = 100 × (A − rolling minimum of A) / (rolling maximum of A − rolling minimum of A), where A is the moving average of absolute one-bar close changes; use the configured lookback lengths, with the normalization window including the current bar and prior bars. If the rolling maximum equals the rolling minimum, the value is 1,000,000.
Long: On a new entry-check bar, not Friday after the cutoff, no open long position, close > open of the configured direction-timeframe bar (still forming), RSI on the configured RSI timeframe (still forming) < 70, and normalized average absolute close change on the configured first-entry indicator timeframe (still forming) < its threshold OR an open long position exists, long-position count < its maximum, EMA on the configured moving-average timeframe (still forming) < close, last long entry price − close ≥ the required add-on distance, and normalized average absolute close change on the configured add-on indicator timeframe (still forming) < its threshold OR the same add-on conditions hold with the normalized average absolute close change ≥ its threshold and the alternate add-on distance applies.
Short: On a new entry-check bar, not Friday after the cutoff, no open short position, close < open of the configured direction-timeframe bar (still forming), RSI on the configured RSI timeframe (still forming) > 30, and normalized average absolute close change on the configured first-entry indicator timeframe (still forming) < its threshold OR an open short position exists, short-position count < its maximum, EMA on the configured moving-average timeframe (still forming) > close, close − last short entry price ≥ the required add-on distance, and normalized average absolute close change on the configured add-on indicator timeframe (still forming) < its threshold OR the same add-on conditions hold with the normalized average absolute close change ≥ its threshold and the alternate add-on distance applies.
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance.
