Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: MACD histogram = (fast EMA − slow EMA) − the signal EMA of (fast EMA − slow EMA).
Long: EMA(current bar) > EMA(previous bar) AND MACD histogram(current bar) > MACD histogram(previous bar) AND MACD histogram(current bar) > 0
Short: EMA(current bar) < EMA(previous bar) AND MACD histogram(current bar) < MACD histogram(previous bar) AND MACD histogram(current bar) < 0
