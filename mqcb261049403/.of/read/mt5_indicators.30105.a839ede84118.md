Calculation: For each chart bar, body, range, upper wick, and lower wick are respectively abs(close - open), abs(high - low), abs(high - max(open, close)), and abs(min(open, close) - low), each divided by a configurable price unit and rounded to a configurable decimal precision.
Calculation: A bullish bar has close > open AND is not a doji; a bearish bar has close < open AND is not a doji. A doji has body <= a configurable threshold AND upper wick > body AND lower wick > body.
Calculation: Bar A is the last completed chart bar and bar B is the preceding chart bar. Wick-to-body, dominant-to-opposite-wick, long momentum body, and short momentum range ratios are configurable parameters.
Long: A is bullish AND B is bearish AND ((A lower wick > wick-to-body ratio * A body AND A lower wick > dominant-to-opposite-wick ratio * A upper wick) OR (A upper wick > wick-to-body ratio * A body AND A upper wick > dominant-to-opposite-wick ratio * A lower wick) OR (A range > B range AND A body > B body AND A high > B high AND A low < B low) OR (A range < B range AND A body < B body AND A high < B high AND A low > B low) OR (A body < A lower wick AND A body < A upper wick AND A lower wick = A upper wick))
