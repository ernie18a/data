Calculation: Long active stop = the highest of the initial stop (entry price minus stop distance), the breakeven stop (entry price plus offset after favorable price movement exceeds its trigger), and the candle trail (lowest low of the last X chart-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar, minus a buffer); raise the stop only
Calculation: Short active stop = the lowest of the initial stop (entry price plus stop distance), the breakeven stop (entry price minus offset after favorable price movement exceeds its trigger), and the candle trail (highest high of the last X chart-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar, plus a buffer); lower the stop only
Long: absolute distance between current-bar open and previous close > entry gap threshold AND previous bar low < fast linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar close > fast linear weighted moving average of typical price AND fast linear weighted moving average of typical price > slow linear weighted moving average of typical price AND bar before previous low < previous bar high
Short: absolute distance between current-bar open and previous close > entry gap threshold AND previous bar high > fast linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar close < fast linear weighted moving average of typical price AND fast linear weighted moving average of typical price > slow linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar low < bar before previous high
Exit: long, current bar low <= long active stop OR current bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, current bar high >= short active stop OR current bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance
