Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = linearly weighted moving averages of typical price, with the fast average using a shorter lookback; evaluate both on the chart timeframe using the current bar at its open.
Calculation: Long stop = initial entry price minus a stop distance; when candle trailing is enabled, raise it to the lowest low over the trailing lookback, including the current bar at its open, minus a candle distance whenever that level is higher. Otherwise, when price minus entry price exceeds a trailing activation distance, raise it to price minus a trailing distance if higher. When break-even is enabled and price minus entry price exceeds its activation distance and the stop is below entry price, move the stop to entry price plus a break-even offset.
Calculation: Short stop = initial entry price plus a stop distance; when candle trailing is enabled, lower it to the highest high over the trailing lookback, including the current bar at its open, plus a candle distance whenever that level is lower. Otherwise, when entry price minus price exceeds a trailing activation distance, lower it to price plus a trailing distance if lower. When break-even is enabled and entry price minus price exceeds its activation distance and the stop is above entry price, move the stop to entry price minus a break-even offset.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus a target distance, increased by the trailing distance each time candle trailing raises the long stop.
Calculation: Short target = entry price minus a target distance, decreased by the trailing distance each time candle trailing lowers the short stop.
Long: At the current bar open, the absolute difference between open and previous close exceeds a distance threshold AND previous low < current fast moving average AND previous close > current fast moving average AND current fast moving average > current slow moving average AND low two bars ago < previous high.
Short: At the current bar open, the absolute difference between open and previous close exceeds a distance threshold AND previous high > current fast moving average AND previous close < current fast moving average AND current fast moving average > current slow moving average AND previous low < high two bars ago.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
