Calculation: Reference bars = bars of the trend timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar and an older completed bar at the trend lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance; if trailing is enabled and price minus entry price exceeds the trailing distance, raise the stop to price minus the trailing distance whenever that is higher than the existing stop.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance; if trailing is enabled, price plus entry price is less than the trailing distance, and the existing stop is greater than price plus the trailing distance, lower the stop to price plus the trailing distance.
Long: current time is the start of a reference bar AND current hour equals the configured trading hour AND no position is open AND no trade has been opened on the current chart bar AND older reference-bar close > last completed reference-bar close
Short: current time is the start of a reference bar AND current hour equals the configured trading hour AND no position is open AND no trade has been opened on the current chart bar AND older reference-bar close <= last completed reference-bar close
Exit: long, position age > maximum position age OR enabled stop exists AND price <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND price >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, position age > maximum position age OR enabled stop exists AND price >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND price <= entry price minus take-profit distance
