Long: short-period SMA of close minus long-period SMA of close on the forming chart bar ≥ one minimum price increment AND long-period SMA minus short-period SMA at bar 2 ≥ one minimum price increment AND long-period SMA minus short-period SMA at bar 5 ≥ one minimum price increment AND previous completed bar close > open AND current time is within the trading-hour interval AND no position is open
Short: long-period SMA of close minus short-period SMA of close on the forming chart bar ≥ one minimum price increment AND short-period SMA minus long-period SMA at bar 2 ≥ one minimum price increment AND short-period SMA minus long-period SMA at bar 5 ≥ one minimum price increment AND previous completed bar close < open AND current time is within the trading-hour interval AND no position is open
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
