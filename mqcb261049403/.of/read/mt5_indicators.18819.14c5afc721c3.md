Calculation: Force Trend = Starting at the bar with index one greater than the lookback length, with prior force and prior indicator initialized to zero, calculate the highest high and lowest low over the lookback including the current bar. If their difference is nonzero, force = 0.66 * (((high + low) / 2 - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) - 0.5) + 0.67 * prior force; otherwise force = 0.67 * prior force - 0.33. Clamp force to [-0.999, 0.999]. Indicator = 0.5 * ln((1 + force) / (1 - force)) + 0.5 * prior indicator. The direction is long when indicator > 0, short when indicator < 0, and otherwise retains the preceding direction, initially long.
Long: Chart-timeframe Force Trend direction = long OR forming higher-timeframe Force Trend direction = long.
Short: Chart-timeframe Force Trend direction = short OR forming higher-timeframe Force Trend direction = short.
