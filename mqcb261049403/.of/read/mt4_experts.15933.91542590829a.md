Calculation: Directional scores = For each configured timeframe, which may be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe, compare the bar still forming with the preceding completed bar for open, high, low, close, (high + low) / 2, (high + low + close) / 3, and (high + low + 2 × close) / 4. Count each strictly higher value as a long vote and every other value as a short vote. Each score is the integer-truncated percentage of its votes out of all votes across the timeframes.
Long: Long score > opening threshold
Short: Short score > opening threshold
Exit: long, long score < closing threshold
Exit: short, short score < closing threshold
