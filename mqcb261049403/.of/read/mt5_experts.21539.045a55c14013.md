Calculation: Smoothed channel candle = smooth open, high, low, and close with the same smoother and smoothing parameters; synthetic open and close are the smoothed open and close, and synthetic high and low are the rolling maximum of smoothed highs and minimum of smoothed lows over a lookback, expanded to include synthetic open and close. Use bars on the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); evaluate signals on completed bars.
Calculation: Bollinger upper/lower bands = moving average of the selected synthetic candle price plus/minus a deviation multiplier times its population standard deviation around that moving average, using the same lookback for both.
Long: On the latest completed indicator-timeframe bar, synthetic open <= synthetic close AND synthetic close > upper band AND the previous completed bar was not a bullish breakout (synthetic open <= synthetic close AND synthetic close > upper band).
Short: On the latest completed indicator-timeframe bar, synthetic open > synthetic close AND synthetic close < lower band AND the previous completed bar was not a bearish breakout (synthetic open > synthetic close AND synthetic close < lower band).
Exit: long, synthetic open > synthetic close OR low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance OR elapsed time since entry > holding limit
Exit: short, synthetic open <= synthetic close OR high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance OR elapsed time since entry > holding limit
