Calculation: Bid open = current chart bar open; ask open = bid open + spread parameter.
Calculation: Active stop and target = initial stop and target set at entry from the entry-bar bid open for a long or ask open for a short, offset by the respective stop-loss and take-profit distances. At each new chart bar, first move the stop to entry price plus a configured offset for a long, or entry price minus that offset for a short, if break-even is enabled, the corresponding open-quote profit exceeds its activation distance, and the existing stop is still beyond entry. Then, if candle trailing is enabled, move the long stop up to the lowest low of the configured number of chart bars including the current forming bar minus a distance, or the short stop down to the highest high of those bars plus a distance, only when that improves the stop; whenever this happens, increase the long target or decrease the short target by the trailing amount. If candle trailing is disabled, instead move the long stop up to bid open minus the trailing amount when bid open minus entry price exceeds the trailing activation distance, or move the short stop down to ask open plus the trailing amount when entry price minus ask open exceeds that distance, only when that improves the stop.
Long: At a new chart bar, MFI on any of the last three completed higher-timeframe bars <= the oversold threshold AND the shorter-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price on the current forming chart bar > the longer-period one AND the low two chart bars ago < the previous chart bar's high AND absolute(bid open - close two chart bars ago) > the entry distance.
Short: At a new chart bar, MFI on any of the last three completed higher-timeframe bars >= the overbought threshold AND the shorter-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price on the current forming chart bar < the longer-period one AND the previous chart bar's low < the high two chart bars ago AND absolute(ask open - previous chart bar's close) > the entry distance.
Exit: long, bid price <= active stop OR bid price >= active target.
Exit: short, ask price >= active stop OR ask price <= active target.
