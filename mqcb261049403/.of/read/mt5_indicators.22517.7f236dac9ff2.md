Calculation: Adaptive Renko trend = Choose close for both high price and low price, or use high and low respectively. Starting at the first bar after the volatility lookback, initialize upper level to high price, lower level to low price, brick size to max(multiplier × (high price − low price), minimum brick size), and trend to neutral. On each bar, save the previous upper and lower levels and calculate volatility size as max(multiplier × ATR or close-price standard deviation, minimum brick size). If high price > upper level + brick size, increase the upper level by floor((high price − upper level) / brick size) × brick size, set brick size to volatility size, and set lower level to upper level − brick size. Then, using the updated levels and brick size, if low price < lower level − brick size, decrease the lower level by floor((lower level − low price) / brick size) × brick size, set brick size to volatility size, and set upper level to lower level + brick size. If the upper level > its previous value, set trend to positive; then, if the lower level < its previous value, set trend to negative. Otherwise retain the previous trend. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
