Calculation: Previous-period high = high of the last completed bar on the selected analysis timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous-period low = low of the last completed bar on the selected analysis timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe
Long: current bar high >= previous-period high
Short: current bar low <= previous-period low
Exit: Long, current bar low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR current bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, current bar high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR current bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance
