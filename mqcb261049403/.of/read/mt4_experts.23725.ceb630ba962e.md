Calculation: MACD main = shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close; MACD signal = simple moving average of MACD main.
Long: At the opening of a new chart bar, the forming chart-bar EMA of typical price is greater than the longer-period linear-weighted MA of typical price, low of the second-last completed chart bar is less than high of the last completed chart bar, the second-last completed chart bar closes below its open, the last completed chart bar closes above its open, the last completed chart bar's body is larger than the second-last completed chart bar's body, AND ((the last completed higher-timeframe MACD main is nonzero AND greater than its signal) OR the forming higher-timeframe RSI of close is greater than the last completed higher-timeframe RSI of close).
Short: At the opening of a new chart bar, the forming chart-bar EMA of typical price is less than the longer-period linear-weighted MA of typical price, low of the last completed chart bar is less than high of the second-last completed chart bar, the second-last completed chart bar closes above its open, the last completed chart bar closes below its open, the last completed chart bar's body is smaller than the second-last completed chart bar's body, AND ((the last completed higher-timeframe MACD main is nonzero AND less than its signal) OR the forming higher-timeframe RSI of close is less than the last completed higher-timeframe RSI of close).
Exit: long, close of the last completed chart bar equals its upper Bollinger Band.
Exit: short, close of the last completed chart bar equals its lower Bollinger Band.
