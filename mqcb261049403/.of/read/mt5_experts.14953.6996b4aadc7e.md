Short: Williams %R on the last completed or still-forming indicator bar, as configured, is below the oversold threshold.
Long: Williams %R on the selected indicator bar is above the overbought threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar is at or below the overbought threshold AND position side is flat.
Short: Williams %R on the selected indicator bar is below the oversold threshold AND Williams %R on the preceding indicator bar is at or above the oversold threshold AND position side is flat.
Exit: short, Williams %R on the selected indicator bar is above the overbought threshold.
Exit: long, Williams %R on the selected indicator bar is below the oversold threshold.
Exit: long, low is at or below entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high is at or above entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high is at or above entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low is at or below entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
