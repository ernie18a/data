Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period moving average of DeMarker; the averaging method is selectable.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period moving average of the fast line, using the same averaging method.
Long: Chart-timeframe fast line >= slow line.
Short: Chart-timeframe fast line < slow line.
Long: Fast line >= slow line on a higher timeframe or the chart timeframe, sampled from the bar still forming at its opening and held until the next bar of that timeframe opens.
Short: Fast line < slow line on a higher timeframe or the chart timeframe, sampled from the bar still forming at its opening and held until the next bar of that timeframe opens.
