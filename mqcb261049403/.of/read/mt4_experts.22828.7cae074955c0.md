Calculation: Typical-price LWMA(n) = weighted moving average of (high + low + close) / 3 over n bars, with the greatest weight on the most recent bar
Long: On the first tick of a new chart bar, absolute distance between its open and the close two bars ago exceeds the retracement threshold AND the short-lookback chart-timeframe LWMA on the forming bar is above the longer-lookback chart-timeframe LWMA on the forming bar AND low two bars ago is below high one bar ago AND the forming higher-timeframe RSI(14) is above the previous completed higher-timeframe RSI(14) AND there is a bar i from 3 through 19 bars ago where the chart-timeframe 8-bar LWMA > 13-bar LWMA > 21-bar LWMA on bar i AND low[i] is at or above the 8-bar LWMA and above the 21-bar LWMA on bar i AND current bar open > highest high of the five completed bars immediately before the current bar
Short: On the first tick of a new chart bar, absolute distance between its open and the close two bars ago exceeds the retracement threshold AND the short-lookback chart-timeframe LWMA on the forming bar is below the longer-lookback chart-timeframe LWMA on the forming bar AND low one bar ago is below high two bars ago AND the forming higher-timeframe RSI(14) is below the previous completed higher-timeframe RSI(14) AND there is a bar i from 3 through 19 bars ago where the chart-timeframe 8-bar LWMA < 13-bar LWMA < 21-bar LWMA on bar i AND high[i] is at or below the 8-bar LWMA and below the 21-bar LWMA on bar i AND current bar open < lowest low of the five completed bars immediately before the current bar
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus the initial stop distance OR high >= entry price plus the take-profit distance OR low <= the trailing stop (highest high since entry minus the trailing distance, activated after a favorable move greater than that distance) OR low <= the moved stop (entry price plus the break-even offset, activated after a favorable move exceeds its trigger distance)
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus the initial stop distance OR low <= entry price minus the take-profit distance OR high >= the trailing stop (lowest low since entry plus the trailing distance, activated after a favorable move greater than that distance) OR high >= the moved stop (entry price minus the break-even offset, activated after a favorable move exceeds its trigger distance)
