Calculation: Synthetic candle = smooth open, high, low, and close independently with the same selected moving-average method and parameters; its open is the smoothed open at the start of a rolling candle lookback, its close is the current smoothed close, its high is the maximum of the rolling smoothed highs, synthetic open, and synthetic close, and its low is the minimum of the rolling smoothed lows, synthetic open, and synthetic close.
Calculation: Band input = the selected applied price of the synthetic candle, using the existing middle-band buffer value for the current bar in place of synthetic low. The selectable applied prices are open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted close, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, high if close > open else low if close < open else close, (high + close)/2 if close > open else (low + close)/2 if close < open else close, and DeMark price. The middle-band buffer is initially zero for an uncalculated bar.
Calculation: Bands = a separately parameterized moving average of band input plus or minus a deviation multiplier times the square root of the mean squared distance of band inputs over the band lookback from the current middle band. Compare the synthetic close with the band at the configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Timeframe = calculate the synthetic candle and bands either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Synthetic open <= synthetic close.
Short: Synthetic open > synthetic close.
