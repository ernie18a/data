Calculation: Bullish MACD divergence = On each new chart bar, require MACD main-line values at shifts 1–4 all below the negative divergence threshold, with shift 2 below shifts 1, 3, and 4. Let the recent price low be the first low at shifts 0–15 that is below each of the next four older lows. Scan older MACD troughs with center at shift i−2 for i>10, requiring values at shifts i through i−4 all below the negative threshold and the center below the other four. Keep successive older troughs only while each is lower than the previously kept trough; stop at the first that fails this rule. For each kept trough, let its price low be the first low pivot in shifts i−4 through i+15, where a pivot is below each of the next four older lows. The divergence is true if a kept trough at depth ≤18 has either a higher MACD value and lower price low than the recent trough and recent price low, or a lower MACD value and higher price low. Use its own MACD periods and threshold.
Calculation: Bearish MACD divergence = On each new chart bar, require MACD main-line values at shifts 1–4 all above the divergence threshold, with shift 2 above shifts 1, 3, and 4. Let the recent price high be the first high at shifts 0–15 that is above each of the next four older highs. Scan older MACD peaks with center at shift i−2 for i>10, requiring values at shifts i through i−4 all above the threshold and the center above the other four. Keep successive older peaks only while each is higher than the previously kept peak; stop at the first that fails this rule. For each kept peak, let its price high be the first high pivot in shifts i−4 through i+15, where a pivot is above each of the next four older highs. The divergence is true if a kept peak at depth ≤18 has either a higher MACD value and lower price high than the recent peak and recent price high, or a lower MACD value and higher price high. Use its own MACD periods and threshold.
Long: RSI of close on the last completed chart bar < oversold threshold AND bullish MACD divergence
Short: RSI of close on the last completed chart bar > overbought threshold AND bearish MACD divergence
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
