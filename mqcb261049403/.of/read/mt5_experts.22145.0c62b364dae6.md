Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the stop-loss distance if enabled; when trailing is enabled, update the stop to current price minus the trailing distance whenever current price minus entry price exceeds trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is absent or below current price minus trailing distance minus trailing step.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the stop-loss distance if enabled; when trailing is enabled, update the stop to current price plus the trailing distance whenever entry price minus current price exceeds trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is absent or above current price plus trailing distance plus trailing step.
Long: Current price >= last completed breakout-timeframe bar high plus breakout offset AND time of day is within the trading window AND (moving-average filter is disabled OR the shorter-period chart-timeframe moving average > the longer-period chart-timeframe moving average). The breakout timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; the moving averages use the still-forming chart bar and their respective configured horizontal shifts and price inputs.
Short: Current price <= last completed breakout-timeframe bar low minus breakout offset AND time of day is within the trading window AND (moving-average filter is disabled OR the shorter-period chart-timeframe moving average < the longer-period chart-timeframe moving average). The breakout timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; the moving averages use the still-forming chart bar and their respective configured horizontal shifts and price inputs.
Exit: Long, current price <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND current price >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, current price >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND current price <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
