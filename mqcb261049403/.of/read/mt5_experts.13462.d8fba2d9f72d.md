Calculation: MFI trend cycle = On the selected indicator timeframe, subtract longer-lookback Money Flow Index from shorter-lookback Money Flow Index. Normalize this difference over a rolling cycle lookback to a 0–100 stochastic; if its range is zero, use the previous smoothed first stochastic. Smooth it recursively as the average of its current value and its previous smoothed value. Normalize the smoothed first stochastic over the same cycle lookback to a -100–100 stochastic; if its range is zero, use the previous cycle value. Smooth this second stochastic recursively as the average of its current value and its previous smoothed value. Initialize rolling buffers and the previous cycle value to zero, and use the first raw stochastic values without smoothing. Use volume for MFI. The indicator timeframe may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; evaluate the last completed indicator bar, or the still-forming bar when configured, and its preceding indicator bar.
Long: Current MFI trend cycle > upper threshold AND previous MFI trend cycle <= upper threshold.
Short: Current MFI trend cycle < lower threshold AND previous MFI trend cycle >= lower threshold.
Exit: long, MFI trend cycle < lower threshold OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, MFI trend cycle > upper threshold OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
