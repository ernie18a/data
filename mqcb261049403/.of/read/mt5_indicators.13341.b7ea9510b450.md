Calculation: Zero-lag momentum OSMA = recursively smooth the difference between a weighted sum of five close-based momentum rates and that sum recursively smoothed; each momentum rate is 100 × close / close at its lookback, the lookbacks increase from shortest to longest, and the third rate uses the second rate’s weight. Recursive smoothing with length N is current input / N + previous smoothed value × (N − 1) / N; the difference is smoothed with a second length.
Long: Zero-lag momentum OSMA > 0 OR, at the opening of a higher-timeframe bar, OSMA on that higher timeframe (current forming bar) > its value on the previous higher-timeframe bar
Short: Zero-lag momentum OSMA < 0 OR, at the opening of a higher-timeframe bar, OSMA on that higher timeframe (current forming bar) < its value on the previous higher-timeframe bar
