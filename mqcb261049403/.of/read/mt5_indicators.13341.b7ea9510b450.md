Calculation: Oscillator = For five momentum lookbacks ordered from shortest to longest, calculate each momentum as 100 × applied price / applied price at its lookback, and sum the weighted momentums, using the second momentum's weight for the third momentum as well. Smooth the sum recursively with coefficient 1 / first smoothing period; subtract this smoothed value from the sum, then smooth the difference recursively with coefficient 1 / second smoothing period. Initialize the first smoothed value as the initial sum / first smoothing period and the second as the initial difference / second smoothing period.
Long: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0 AND oscillator > previous oscillator OR chart-timeframe oscillator < 0 AND oscillator > previous oscillator
Short: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0 AND oscillator < previous oscillator OR chart-timeframe oscillator < 0 AND oscillator < previous oscillator
Long: At the start of a higher-timeframe bar, its still-forming oscillator >= previous higher-timeframe oscillator OR its oscillator = 0; retain this decision until the next higher-timeframe bar starts
Short: At the start of a higher-timeframe bar, its still-forming oscillator < previous higher-timeframe oscillator AND its oscillator != 0; retain this decision until the next higher-timeframe bar starts
