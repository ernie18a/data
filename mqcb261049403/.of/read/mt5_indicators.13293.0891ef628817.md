Calculation: Weighted TRIX oscillator = Sum five TRIX values of the applied price with successively longer periods, each multiplied by its weight; use the second period's weight for the third period as well. Smooth the sum recursively with smoothing factor 1 divided by the first smoothing period. Subtract this smoothed sum from the original sum, then smooth the difference recursively with smoothing factor 1 divided by the second smoothing period. Initialize the first smoother to the initial sum divided by its smoothing period and the second to the initial difference divided by its smoothing period.
Calculation: Sampled trend = At the start of each bar on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, compare that timeframe's still-forming weighted TRIX oscillator with its previous bar's oscillator; hold the resulting comparison until the next bar of that timeframe starts.
Long: Chart-timeframe weighted TRIX oscillator > its previous-bar value
Short: Chart-timeframe weighted TRIX oscillator < its previous-bar value
Long: Sampled trend oscillator >= its previous-bar value OR sampled trend oscillator = 0
Short: Sampled trend oscillator < its previous-bar value AND sampled trend oscillator != 0
