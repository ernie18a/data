Calculation: Slow MA distance = truncate toward zero ((slow close-based moving average - current price) / price increment).
Calculation: Fast MA speed = truncate toward zero (scale factor × (current fast close-based moving average - fast moving average two bars ago) / price increment); use the forming bar of a lower timeframe, with a shorter MA lookback than the slow MA.
Calculation: Long setup = slow moving average two bars ago < current slow moving average AND stochastic main and signal are both below the lower threshold AND previous stochastic main < previous stochastic signal AND current stochastic main > current stochastic signal AND slow MA distance < distance threshold AND ATR >= minimum volatility threshold AND hour is within the inclusive trading session; use the forming chart-timeframe bar and the preceding completed bar for stochastic.
Calculation: Short setup = slow moving average two bars ago > current slow moving average AND stochastic main and signal are both above the upper threshold AND previous stochastic main > previous stochastic signal AND current stochastic main < current stochastic signal AND slow MA distance > distance threshold AND ATR >= minimum volatility threshold AND hour is within the inclusive trading session; use the forming chart-timeframe bar and the preceding completed bar for stochastic.
Long: Fast MA speed > positive speed threshold AND a long setup has occurred since the last long entry AND absolute difference between the minute-of-hour of the most recent long setup and the current minute-of-hour <= setup timeout AND slow MA distance < distance threshold AND current stochastic signal < lower threshold AND ATR >= minimum volatility threshold AND hour is within the inclusive trading session.
Short: Fast MA speed < negative speed threshold AND a short setup has occurred since the last short entry AND absolute difference between the minute-of-hour of the most recent short setup and the current minute-of-hour <= setup timeout AND slow MA distance > distance threshold AND current stochastic signal > upper threshold AND ATR >= minimum volatility threshold AND hour is within the inclusive trading session.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + profit distance selected at entry using the longer-lookback ATR: the lower-volatility profit distance if ATR < volatility threshold, otherwise the normal profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - profit distance selected at entry using the longer-lookback ATR: the lower-volatility profit distance if ATR < volatility threshold, otherwise the normal profit distance.
