Calculation: Trailing trend = initialize trend to 0 and extreme and stop to the close of the first calculable bar. For each bar, let distance be the mean of high minus low over a lookback including that bar, divided by a configurable price-scale factor. If the previous trend is nonnegative, set extreme to the maximum of the previous extreme and current high, and stop to the maximum of the previous stop and extreme × (1 − distance); if current high < stop, set extreme to current high, stop to extreme × (1 + distance), and trend to −1. Otherwise, set extreme to the minimum of the previous extreme and current low, and stop to the minimum of the previous stop and extreme × (1 + distance); if current low > stop, set extreme to current low, stop to extreme × (1 − distance), and trend to +1. When no reversal occurs, retain the previous trend. Calculate this on the chart timeframe and separately on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Displayed higher-timeframe trend = initially 0; update to +1 when the higher-timeframe trailing trend changes from negative to positive and its low minus a positive multiple of ATR is nonzero; update to −1 when the higher-timeframe trailing trend changes from positive to negative and its high plus the same multiple of ATR is nonzero; otherwise retain its previous value.
Long: Chart-timeframe trailing trend changes from negative to positive AND low minus a positive multiple of ATR is nonzero.
Short: Chart-timeframe trailing trend changes from positive to negative AND high plus the same multiple of ATR is nonzero.
Long: Displayed higher-timeframe trend > 0.
Short: Displayed higher-timeframe trend < 0.
