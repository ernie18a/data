Calculation: Bullish signal = current close > current Parabolic SAR AND previous close < previous Parabolic SAR AND current shorter-period close SMA > current longer-period close SMA AND current close > current shortest-period close SMA.
Calculation: Bearish signal = current close < current Parabolic SAR AND previous close > previous Parabolic SAR AND current shorter-period close SMA < current longer-period close SMA AND current close < current shortest-period close SMA.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = fixed stop distance when automatic stop is disabled; otherwise the absolute difference between entry price and entry-bar Parabolic SAR multiplied by the stop multiplier. Clamp the result to the minimum and maximum stop distances.
Calculation: Profit target distance = fixed target distance when automatic target is disabled; otherwise initial stop distance multiplied by the target multiplier.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus initial stop distance at entry; thereafter, when close minus entry price > the breakeven trigger, raise it to at least entry price plus the breakeven offset, and when close minus entry price > the trailing trigger, raise it to at least close minus the trailing distance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus initial stop distance at entry; thereafter, when entry price minus close > the breakeven trigger, lower it to at most entry price minus the breakeven offset, and when entry price minus close > the trailing trigger, lower it to at most close plus the trailing distance.
Long: No open position AND initial stop distance > minimum stop distance AND ((reverse entry disabled AND bullish signal) OR (reverse entry enabled AND bearish signal)) AND (weekday filter disabled OR current weekday is enabled).
Short: No open position AND initial stop distance > minimum stop distance AND ((reverse entry disabled AND bearish signal) OR (reverse entry enabled AND bullish signal)) AND (weekday filter disabled OR current weekday is enabled).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + profit target distance OR (opposite-signal exit enabled AND ((reverse entry disabled AND bearish signal) OR (reverse entry enabled AND bullish signal))).
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - profit target distance OR (opposite-signal exit enabled AND ((reverse entry disabled AND bullish signal) OR (reverse entry enabled AND bearish signal))).
