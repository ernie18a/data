Calculation: Volatility = choose ATR; or the square root of the sum of squared log(high/low) over the volatility period divided by 4 × ln(2) × period, omitting invalid ranges from the sum; or the root mean square of valid close-to-close log returns over the period.
Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentage of positive, valid volatility values in the lookback ending at the current bar that are less than or equal to current volatility. Smooth by averaging the current raw percentile with the preceding smoothing-period-minus-one smoothed percentiles. Carry the previous value forward when current volatility is invalid.
Calculation: Regime = classify the smoothed volatility percentile as low when at or below the low threshold, normal when above low and below elevated, elevated when at or above elevated and below high, high when at or above high and below extreme, and extreme otherwise. Retain the previous regime until the new classification persists for the specified number of consecutive bars.
Calculation: Volume percentile = percentage of positive volumes in the lookback ending at the current bar that are less than or equal to current volume; use the percentile midpoint if no positive volumes exist.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe percentile = percentage of Parkinson volatility values in the higher-timeframe lookback that are less than or equal to the Parkinson volatility of the last completed higher-timeframe bar. The lookback includes the higher-timeframe bar still forming; each Parkinson value uses its own bar and preceding bars, dividing by the number of valid positive high/low pairs. Use the same volatility period and percentile lookback as the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = true when the filter is disabled or no higher timeframe is available; otherwise false when the chart regime is low and higher-timeframe percentile is above the elevated threshold, or when the chart regime is high or extreme and higher-timeframe percentile is below the low threshold; true otherwise. Treat insufficient higher-timeframe data as agreement.
Calculation: Directional score = clamp to the percentile range the sum of the percentile midpoint; sign(close − EMA) × min(abs(close − EMA) / ATR × trend coefficient, trend cap); sign(close − close at the momentum lookback) × min(abs(close − close at the momentum lookback) / ATR × momentum coefficient, momentum cap); (volume percentile − percentile midpoint) × volume coefficient; an agreement bonus or disagreement penalty when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled; and a regime-specific adjustment. Use the same coefficient for trend and momentum. Use zero for ATR-normalized terms when ATR is nonpositive, and use the percentile midpoint for volume percentile when the volume filter is disabled.
Long: regime is low AND higher-timeframe agreement is true AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume percentile > low-volume threshold) AND close > EMA.
Short: regime is low AND higher-timeframe agreement is true AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume percentile > low-volume threshold) AND close < EMA.
Long: directional score >= percentile midpoint + neutral-band width.
Short: directional score <= percentile midpoint − neutral-band width.
Long: close > EMA.
Short: close < EMA.
Long: ATR > 0 AND close > close at the momentum lookback.
Short: ATR > 0 AND close < close at the momentum lookback.
