Calculation: Bollinger Bands = bands around a moving average of opening prices, using a lookback and deviation multiplier
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index calculated from opening prices
Long: RSI is above 50 on the chart timeframe and each higher timeframe AND the selected chart bar's high reaches or exceeds the upper Bollinger Band AND Parabolic SAR is below the current low on the chart timeframe and each higher timeframe
Short: RSI is below 50 on the chart timeframe and each higher timeframe AND the selected chart bar's low reaches or falls below the lower Bollinger Band AND Parabolic SAR is above the current high on the chart timeframe and each higher timeframe
Exit: long, bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR bar low <= entry price - initial-stop distance
Exit: short, bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR bar high >= entry price + initial-stop distance
