Calculation: Displaced EMA = EMA of close displayed at a bar after shifting the EMA line by its displacement; a negative displacement places an EMA value earlier and a positive displacement places it later.
Long: On the shortest lower timeframe, the short-period EMA with negative displacement >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the short-period EMA with positive displacement >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA >= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA >= the positive-displacement short-period EMA; AND on the longer lower timeframe, the negative-displacement short-period EMA >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA = the positive-displacement short-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA >= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA >= the positive-displacement short-period EMA; AND on the higher timeframe, the negative-displacement short-period EMA >= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA >= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA >= the positive-displacement short-period EMA; AND the short-entry condition is false. Each comparison uses the bar still forming, or alternatively the last completed bar in every timeframe.
Short: On the shortest lower timeframe, the short-period EMA with negative displacement <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the short-period EMA with positive displacement <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA <= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA <= the positive-displacement short-period EMA; AND on the longer lower timeframe, the negative-displacement short-period EMA <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA <= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA <= the positive-displacement short-period EMA; AND on the higher timeframe, the negative-displacement short-period EMA <= the undisplaced long-period EMA AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA <= the undisplaced short-period EMA AND the undisplaced short-period EMA <= the positive-displacement short-period EMA. Each comparison uses the bar still forming, or alternatively the last completed bar in every timeframe.
Exit: long, the negative-displacement short-period EMA on the shortest lower timeframe <= the undisplaced short-period EMA on that timeframe AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA on the longer lower timeframe <= the undisplaced short-period EMA on the higher timeframe AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA on the higher timeframe <= the undisplaced short-period EMA on that timeframe. Each comparison uses the bar still forming, or alternatively the last completed bar in every timeframe.
Exit: short, the negative-displacement short-period EMA on the longer lower timeframe >= the undisplaced short-period EMA on the shortest lower timeframe AND the negative-displacement short-period EMA on the higher timeframe >= the undisplaced short-period EMA on the longer lower timeframe. Each comparison uses the bar still forming, or alternatively the last completed bar in every timeframe.
Exit: long, price <= entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price plus the take-profit distance OR the highest price since entry > entry price plus the trailing-stop distance AND price <= the highest price since entry minus the trailing-stop distance.
Exit: short, price >= entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price minus the take-profit distance OR the lowest price since entry < entry price minus the trailing-stop distance AND price >= the lowest price since entry plus the trailing-stop distance.
