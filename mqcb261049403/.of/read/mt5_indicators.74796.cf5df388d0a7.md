Calculation: Evaluation bar = the last completed chart bar when closed-bar evaluation is enabled; otherwise the chart bar still forming. All indicator values and structure confirmation use data through the evaluation bar.
Calculation: Effective indicator periods = configured periods, unless adaptive periods are enabled and usable history is shorter than the slow EMA period. Usable history is the number of chart bars minus the evaluation-bar shift minus 5. In that case, multiply each period by clamp(usable history / configured slow EMA period, 0.05, 1), round to the nearest integer, and apply minimums of 3, 5, and 10 to the fast, medium, and slow EMA periods; 5 to ADX, RSI, and ATR periods; and 3, 6, and 3 to the fast, slow, and signal MACD periods. If the resulting fast MACD period is at least the slow period, set it to max(3, slow period - 2).
Calculation: Structure component = 1 if the most recent two confirmed swing highs and lows are both rising; -1 if both are falling; otherwise 0. A swing high or low is strictly higher or lower than each of its two preceding and two following bars, all available through the evaluation bar. Search the configured lookback capped at usable history; use 0 if fewer than two highs or two lows are found or the capped lookback is less than 20.
Calculation: Signed score = 100 times the weighted sum of these components divided by the sum of their weights, using 100 as the denominator weight sum if that sum is not positive: trend = ((1 if fast close EMA > medium close EMA else -1) + (1 if medium close EMA > slow close EMA else -1)) / 2; ADX = (1 if +DI > -DI else -1) × clamp(ADX / 50, 0, 1); RSI = clamp((close RSI - 50) / 50, -1, 1); MACD = clamp((MACD main line - MACD signal line) / ATR, -1, 1); structure = structure component. The fast EMA period is shorter than the medium EMA period, which is shorter than the slow EMA period; the fast MACD period is shorter than the slow MACD period.
Calculation: Final score = clamp(abs(signed score) × volume multiplier, 0, 100). Volume multiplier = clamp(0.85 + 0.15 × (evaluation-bar volume / average volume of the preceding lookback-minus-one bars - 1), 0.85, 1.15); use 1 if the volume history is unavailable or its average is not positive. The volume lookback includes the evaluation bar and is at least 2.
Long: Signed score > 0 AND final score >= minimum score AND ATR > 0 AND evaluation-bar close > 0 AND sufficient history is available.
Short: Signed score < 0 AND final score >= minimum score AND ATR > 0 AND evaluation-bar close > 0 AND sufficient history is available.
