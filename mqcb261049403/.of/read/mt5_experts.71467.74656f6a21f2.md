Calculation: Bullish gap = a completed bar's low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; lower boundary = that earlier high and upper boundary = that low.
Calculation: Bearish gap = a completed bar's high is below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; lower boundary = that high and upper boundary = that earlier low.
Calculation: Tracked gaps = scan completed bars within the lookback at each new chart bar, adding gaps in chronological order unless a gap with the same middle-bar time and direction is already tracked; remove the oldest tracked gap when capacity is reached. Before checking entries, mark a bullish gap filled if the forming chart bar's low <= its upper boundary, or a bearish gap filled if the forming chart bar's high >= its lower boundary. A gap remains filled until removed; mark it traded after an entry and do not reuse it while tracked.
Calculation: Stop distance = either a fixed price distance or a multiplier of ATR from the last completed chart bar; take-profit distance is independently chosen by the same two modes, with its own fixed distance or ATR multiplier.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus stop distance at entry; short stop = entry price plus stop distance at entry. If break-even is enabled and the new chart bar's open reaches entry price plus the current break-even trigger distance for a long, or entry price minus that distance for a short, while the stop remains on the loss side of entry, move the stop to entry plus a buffer for a long or entry minus a buffer for a short. If trailing is enabled, at each new chart bar move the long stop up to open minus the current trailing distance, or the short stop down to open plus the current trailing distance, only when it improves the stop. Break-even trigger and trailing distances are each either fixed price distances or separate multiples of ATR from the last completed chart bar, according to the stop-distance mode. Apply break-even before trailing.
Long: At a new chart bar, an enabled tracked bullish gap is neither filled nor traded AND the last completed bar's low <= the gap's upper boundary AND its low >= lower boundary minus gap width AND (confirmation is disabled OR its close > its open AND its close >= the lower boundary) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR the new bar's open > the shorter-period EMA > the longer-period EMA, both using the forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR the forming chart bar's ADX >= the minimum strength AND DI+ > DI-) AND (session filter is disabled OR the new bar's hour >= session start AND < session end).
Short: At a new chart bar, an enabled tracked bearish gap is neither filled nor traded AND the last completed bar's high >= the gap's lower boundary AND its high <= upper boundary plus gap width AND (confirmation is disabled OR its close < its open AND its close <= the upper boundary) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR the new bar's open < the shorter-period EMA < the longer-period EMA, both using the forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR the forming chart bar's ADX >= the minimum strength AND DI- > DI+) AND (session filter is disabled OR the new bar's hour >= session start AND < session end).
Exit: long, price <= long stop OR price >= entry price plus take-profit distance at entry.
Exit: short, price >= short stop OR price <= entry price minus take-profit distance at entry.
Exit: long, partial exit enabled AND at a new chart bar open >= entry price plus either half the take-profit distance at entry or a risk-reward multiplier times the absolute difference between entry price and the current stop.
Exit: short, partial exit enabled AND at a new chart bar open <= entry price minus either half the take-profit distance at entry or a risk-reward multiplier times the absolute difference between entry price and the current stop.
