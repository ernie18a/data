Calculation: Bearish FVG = a three-bar pattern where the newest completed bar's high is below the low two bars earlier; zone = [newest high, that earlier low]
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback period, using the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Long: An unfilled, untraded bullish FVG exists AND the last completed chart bar's low is at or below the zone's upper boundary and at or above its lower boundary minus the gap size AND (confirmation is disabled OR that bar closes above its open and at or above the zone's lower boundary) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR current price is above the fast EMA and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, both from the still-forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX on the still-forming chart-timeframe bar is at or above its threshold and DI+ is above DI−)
Short: An unfilled, untraded bearish FVG exists AND the last completed chart bar's high is at or above the zone's lower boundary and at or below its upper boundary plus the gap size AND (confirmation is disabled OR that bar closes below its open and at or below the zone's upper boundary) AND (EMA filter is disabled OR current price is below the fast EMA and the fast EMA is below the slow EMA, both from the still-forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX on the still-forming chart-timeframe bar is at or above its threshold and DI− is above DI+)
Exit: Long, current price is at or below the stop-loss or at or above the take-profit; move the stop to entry plus a buffer when current price is at or above entry plus the trigger distance and the existing stop is below entry; move the trailing stop to current price minus the trail distance when that is above the existing stop; partially close when current price reaches halfway from entry to take-profit or the configured risk-to-reward level
Exit: Short, current price is at or above the stop-loss or at or below the take-profit; move the stop to entry minus a buffer when current price is at or below entry minus the trigger distance and the existing stop is above entry or absent; move the trailing stop to current price plus the trail distance when that is below the existing stop or the stop is absent; partially close when current price reaches halfway from entry to take-profit or the configured risk-to-reward level
