Calculation: Chart pivot = a high or low that is at least as extreme as every corresponding high or low in an equal-length window on each side; it is confirmed after the right-hand window closes. Internal pivots use a shorter window than swing pivots.
Calculation: Structure trend = starting neutral, set the trend bullish when close exceeds the latest confirmed, unbroken pivot high, or bearish when close falls below the latest confirmed, unbroken pivot low; consume the broken pivot and retain the trend until the next break. Calculate internal and swing trends separately. An internal break is permitted for markings and order blocks when the swing trend is not opposite, unless the confluence filter is disabled; internal trend still changes on an unpermitted break. A change of character is a permitted break opposite the preceding internal trend.
Calculation: Chart bias = the direction of the most recent internal or swing break, preferring internal on a tie; use the nonneutral trend if the selected trend is neutral. Rebuild structure over the configured history window on each closed chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = structure trend over a fixed history of higher-timeframe bars through the last completed bar, using pivots strictly higher or lower than every corresponding neighboring value. Calculate it independently on three higher timeframes, with the longest as master.
Calculation: Directional alignment = either all three higher-timeframe biases match the direction, or, when strict alignment is disabled, the master matches and neither other bias is opposite.
Calculation: Sweep = the first bar within a configured window after a confirmed internal pivot's bar whose low is below that pivot low and close is above it, or whose high is above that pivot high and close is below it. The latest sweep is the sweep with the greatest bar index; retain the first encountered on a tie. Sweep detection searches from the pivot bar even when the sweep predates pivot confirmation.
Calculation: Order block = on a permitted structure break, select an opposite-body candle from the latest opposite pivot through the break bar, or from the broken pivot if no opposite pivot exists; choose the greatest-volume candle, preferring the latest on ties, or choose the latest opposite candle if volume selection is disabled. Its high-low range must be at least a configured fraction of the current simple-average true-range ATR. It is bullish on an upward break and bearish on a downward break. It is mitigated if any later close since its candle is below its low for bullish or above its high for bearish. Select the unmitigated directional block with midpoint nearest close, subject to its midpoint being no more than a configured ATR distance above close for bullish or below close for bearish; a tap means close lies within its bounds expanded by a configured ATR distance.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when a bar's low exceeds the high two bars earlier, bearish when its high is below the low two bars earlier; the bounds are those two prices. Require the gap size to meet an optional threshold based on chart ATR for chart-timeframe gaps or on mean high-low range over the fetched history for higher-timeframe gaps. Higher-timeframe gaps include the still-forming bar. A bullish gap is mitigated when a chart low from the bar after the gap's middle-bar time reaches its lower bound; a bearish gap is mitigated when a chart high reaches its upper bound. A tap means close lies within the bounds of an unmitigated directional gap expanded by a configured chart ATR distance.
Calculation: Equal-level sweep = two consecutive recorded swing pivots of the same type are separated by at least a configured number of bars and differ by at most a configured ATR tolerance, AND the current high exceeds the later high pivot or the current low falls below the later low pivot.
Calculation: Directional sequence = the latest sweep has the direction and is within the configured window, and the latest permitted internal change of character has the direction, is no earlier than the latest sweep, and is within that window. Each requirement can be disabled independently; the change-of-character timing still references the latest sweep index when the sweep requirement is disabled.
Calculation: Directional score = clamp to a bounded percentage the sum of configured weights for directional alignment, close in the favorable half of the latest swing high-low midpoint, a recent directional sweep, a recent directional change of character no earlier than the latest sweep, a directional order-block tap, a directional fair-value-gap tap, a same-direction equal-level sweep, and optional same-direction order-flow proxy; subtract a location penalty for close in the unfavorable half. If alignment fails, award a smaller alignment weight when at least two higher-timeframe biases match and none is opposite. The order-flow proxy sums volume times (buy pressure minus sell pressure) divided by total pressure over a configured number of closed bars ending before the current bar; for close at or above open, buy pressure is body plus lower wick and sell pressure is upper wick, otherwise buy pressure is lower wick and sell pressure is body plus upper wick. Its direction is the sign of the sum.
Long: close exceeds an unbroken internal or swing pivot high AND the internal confluence filter permits the break when applicable.
Short: close falls below an unbroken internal or swing pivot low AND the internal confluence filter permits the break when applicable.
Long: a newly confirmed swing high or low is greater than the preceding swing pivot of the same type OR no preceding swing pivot of that type exists.
Short: a newly confirmed swing high or low is less than or equal to the preceding swing pivot of the same type.
Long: a bullish sweep occurs.
Short: a bearish sweep occurs.
Long: swing trend is bullish.
Short: swing trend is bearish.
Long: chart bias is bullish.
Short: chart bias is bearish.
Long: a higher-timeframe bias is bullish.
Short: a higher-timeframe bias is bearish.
Long: close is below the midpoint of the latest swing high and low.
Short: close is above the midpoint of the latest swing high and low.
Long: an unmitigated bullish order block or bullish fair value gap exists.
Short: an unmitigated bearish order block or bearish fair value gap exists.
Long: chart bias is bullish AND bullish higher-timeframe alignment holds AND each enabled directional-sequence requirement holds AND a bullish order-block or fair-value-gap tap holds if the entry-zone requirement is enabled AND close is not above the swing midpoint AND bullish score is at least the configured threshold AND bullish score is at least bearish score.
Short: chart bias is bearish AND bearish higher-timeframe alignment holds AND each enabled directional-sequence requirement holds AND a bearish order-block or fair-value-gap tap holds if the entry-zone requirement is enabled AND close is not below the swing midpoint AND bearish score is at least the configured threshold AND bearish score is greater than bullish score.
