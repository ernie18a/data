Calculation: StrongestVoid = state updated on every bar in this order. (1) Void formation: AvgRange = mean of (high-low) over the prior N bars, excluding the current bar. AvgVol = mean of volume over the same prior N bars. If AvgRange>0 AND AvgVol>0 AND (high-low) > AvgRange*k1 (k1>1) AND volume < AvgVol*k2 (k2<1), register a zone with zone high=bar high, zone low=bar low, score=100, direction=bullish if close>open, otherwise bearish. If the number of zones is at its maximum, replace the zone with the lowest score only if that score is below 100, otherwise discard the new zone. (2) Decay, applied to every zone with score>0, including a zone registered on this bar: overlap = min(bar high, zone high) - max(bar low, zone low). If overlap>0, score = max(0, score - min(100, overlap/(zone high-zone low)*decay*10)), where decay is a positive rate. (3) StrongestVoid = the zone with the highest score among zones with score>0, with ties going to the most recently created zone. If no zone has score>0, there is no StrongestVoid.
Long: StrongestVoid exists (its score>0) AND its direction is bullish
Short: StrongestVoid exists (its score>0) AND its direction is bearish
