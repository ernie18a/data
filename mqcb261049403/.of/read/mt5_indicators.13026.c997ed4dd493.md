Calculation: Bulls-bears oscillator = On the selected timeframe, let x = high + low − 2 × EMA(close). Starting all four stages at zero, update each bar using smoothing coefficient g: L0 = (1 − g) × x + g × previous L0; L1 = −g × L0 + previous L0 + g × previous L1; L2 = −g × L1 + previous L1 + g × previous L2; L3 = −g × L2 + previous L2 + g × previous L3. Let U = sum of max(Li − L(i+1), 0) and D = sum of max(L(i+1) − Li, 0) for i = 0, 1, 2. The oscillator is U / (U + D), or zero when U + D = zero.
Long: For any selected timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, the oscillator sampled from its still-forming bar at the start of that bar is greater than the upper threshold; retain that decision until the next bar of that timeframe.
Short: For any selected timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, the oscillator sampled from its still-forming bar at the start of that bar is less than the lower threshold; retain that decision until the next bar of that timeframe.
