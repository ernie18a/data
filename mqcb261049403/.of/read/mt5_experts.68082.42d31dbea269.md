Calculation: OpeningRange = for each New York local calendar day, RangeHigh = highest high and RangeLow = lowest low of the lower-timeframe signal bars (lower than the 4-hour-scale window, aggregated from the data) that fall inside a fixed-length window starting at New York local midnight. It becomes valid when the window ends and stays valid until the next day's window starts. No signals are evaluated while the window is forming.
Calculation: BelowRun = the run of consecutive completed signal bars, ending at the bar before the latest completed bar and going backward, whose close is below RangeLow. Its length is the bar count and LongStop = the lowest low in the run.
Calculation: AboveRun = the run of consecutive completed signal bars, ending at the bar before the latest completed bar and going backward, whose close is above RangeHigh. Its length is the bar count and ShortStop = the highest high in the run.
Long: OpeningRange is valid AND no open position AND latest completed signal bar close > RangeLow AND previous signal bar close < RangeLow AND BelowRun length < a maximum length limit (so a long excursion below the range invalidates the signal); entry at the current price after that bar closes.
Short: OpeningRange is valid AND no open position AND latest completed signal bar close < RangeHigh AND previous signal bar close > RangeHigh AND AboveRun length < a maximum length limit (so a long excursion above the range invalidates the signal); entry at the current price after that bar closes.
Exit: long, price <= LongStop (stop loss) OR price >= entry + K*(entry - LongStop) (take profit, with K a fixed positive reward multiple of the stop distance)
Exit: short, price >= ShortStop (stop loss) OR price <= entry - K*(ShortStop - entry) (take profit, with K a fixed positive reward multiple of the stop distance)
