Calculation: Selected price = choose open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, or (open+high+low+close)/4; alternatively choose high if close>open, low if close<open, otherwise close; or (high+close)/2 if close>open, (low+close)/2 if close<open, otherwise close; or (2*r-low-high)/2 where r=(high+low+close+x)/2 and x is low if close<open, high if close>open, otherwise close.
Calculation: Trend and countertrend lines = on the configurable indicator timeframe, initialize both to the selected price of the first calculated bar. On each subsequent bar, update the trend line to its previous value plus (selected price minus its previous value) divided by the smoothing coefficient if the selected price is strictly above both the previous trend line and previous selected price, or strictly below both; otherwise retain its previous value. Update the countertrend line by the same formula using its own previous value if the selected price is strictly above that value and below the previous selected price, or strictly below that value and above the previous selected price; otherwise retain its previous value. The indicator timeframe can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting, and compare with the immediately preceding indicator bar.
Calculation: Trading interval membership = if start hour<end hour, (hour=start hour AND minute>=start minute) OR (hour>start hour AND hour<end hour) OR (hour=end hour AND minute<end minute); if start hour=end hour, hour=start hour AND minute>=start minute AND minute<end minute; if start hour>end hour, (hour>=start hour AND minute>=start minute) OR hour<end hour OR (hour=end hour AND minute<end minute).
Long: Trend line>countertrend line AND previous trend line<=previous countertrend line AND (time filter disabled OR datetime is inside trading interval).
Short: Trend line<countertrend line AND previous trend line>=previous countertrend line AND (time filter disabled OR datetime is inside trading interval).
Exit: long, trend line<countertrend line OR (time filter enabled AND datetime is outside trading interval) OR (stop loss enabled AND low<=entry price minus stop distance) OR (take profit enabled AND high>=entry price plus profit distance).
Exit: short, trend line>countertrend line OR (time filter enabled AND datetime is outside trading interval) OR (stop loss enabled AND high>=entry price plus stop distance) OR (take profit enabled AND low<=entry price minus profit distance).
