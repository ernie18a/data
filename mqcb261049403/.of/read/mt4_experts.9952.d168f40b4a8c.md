Calculation: Price = chart-bar close, used as the available approximation of the current bid and ask.
Calculation: Bar indexing = index 0 is the bar still forming; positive indices count completed bars backward. The chart timeframe is the shortest timeframe. The higher timeframes, from shorter to longer, are the intermediate, trend, and slow timeframes.
Calculation: Trend oscillator = 100 × (short-period SMA of open − long-period SMA of open) / long-period SMA of open. Calculate it on the forming trend-timeframe and slow-timeframe bars; use completed slow-timeframe bars when referring to earlier values.
Calculation: Long level = price rounded to a parameterized decimal precision plus a level offset. Short level = price rounded to the same precision minus the same offset. Recalculate each level on each chart bar.
Calculation: Long setup = price < long level AND the last completed chart-bar close > long level AND each of the forming and preceding completed trend-timeframe lows > long level − level offset + a buffer AND the trend-timeframe low before them > long level − level offset.
Calculation: Short setup = price > short level AND the last completed chart-bar close < short level AND each of the forming and preceding completed trend-timeframe highs < short level + level offset − a buffer AND the trend-timeframe high before them < short level + level offset.
Calculation: Long exclusions = any chart-bar high in a short lookback including the forming bar > long level + a large distance OR the slow-timeframe oscillator < a negative threshold AND any chart-bar pair in a longer lookback has the older high − the newer low > a movement threshold AND the older open > the newer close OR every trend-timeframe high in a lookback including the forming bar <= long level + a medium distance OR every intermediate-timeframe high in a lookback including the forming bar < long level + a small distance. Each chart-bar pair is separated by a fixed bar offset. The large distance > medium distance > small distance.
Calculation: Short exclusions = any chart-bar low in a short lookback including the forming bar < short level − a large distance OR the slow-timeframe oscillator > a positive threshold AND any chart-bar pair in a longer lookback has the newer high − the older low > a movement threshold AND the newer close > the older open OR every trend-timeframe low in a lookback including the forming bar >= short level − a medium distance OR every intermediate-timeframe low in a lookback including the forming bar > short level − a small distance. Each chart-bar pair uses the same separation as the long exclusions.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + a profit distance, increased by an additional profit distance if, when the entry order was placed, the forming slow-timeframe oscillator was between two positive thresholds and greater than each preceding value in a sequence of completed slow-timeframe bars, with each value in that sequence greater than the one before it. If optional target adjustment is enabled and price < entry price AND a chart-bar pair in the longer lookback has older high − newer low > an adjustment movement threshold AND older open > newer close AND the current target > entry price + an adjustment threshold, change the target to entry price + a smaller adjustment distance.
Calculation: Short target = entry price − a profit distance, decreased by an additional profit distance if, when the entry order was placed, the forming slow-timeframe oscillator was between two negative thresholds and less than each preceding value in a sequence of completed slow-timeframe bars, with each value in that sequence less than the one before it. If optional target adjustment is enabled and price > entry price AND a chart-bar pair in the longer lookback has newer high − older low > an adjustment movement threshold AND newer close > older open AND the current target < entry price − an adjustment threshold, change the target to entry price − a smaller adjustment distance.
Long: The weekday and hour are outside the excluded early-week interval AND long setup AND (forming trend-timeframe oscillator < zero OR either of the two most recent completed trend-timeframe highs > long level + a reach distance) AND none of the long exclusions AND a buy limit at the setup price − a limit offset is reached before its expiration interval.
Short: The weekday and hour are outside the excluded early-week interval AND short setup AND (forming trend-timeframe oscillator > zero OR either of the two most recent completed trend-timeframe lows < short level − a reach distance) AND none of the short exclusions AND a sell limit at the setup price + a limit offset is reached before its expiration interval.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= short target.
