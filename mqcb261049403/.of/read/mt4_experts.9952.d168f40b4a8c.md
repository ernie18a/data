Calculation: Level = current price rounded to 2 decimal places plus (long) or minus (short) an offset
Calculation: RAVI = 100 × (short-lookback simple moving average of open prices − long-lookback simple moving average of open prices) / long-lookback simple moving average of open prices; short lookback is shorter than long. Use the current forming bar on the higher timeframe.
Calculation: Long score = +10 if higher-timeframe RAVI < 0; +7 if either of the two latest completed higher-timeframe highs > Level + offset; +45 if current price < Level AND the previous completed chart-timeframe close > Level AND the current and two latest completed higher-timeframe lows > Level − offset AND the second latest completed higher-timeframe low > Level − offset; −50 for each of the 12 latest chart-timeframe highs > Level + offset; −50 for each of the 31 latest chart-timeframe patterns where a high from three bars later minus the low is > offset, the open three bars later > the low bar's close, and current forming daily RAVI < threshold; −50 if none of the 15 latest higher-timeframe highs > Level + offset; −50 if all of the 8 latest higher-timeframe highs < Level + offset.
Calculation: Short score = +10 if higher-timeframe RAVI > 0; +7 if either of the two latest completed higher-timeframe lows < Level − offset; +45 if current price > Level AND the previous completed chart-timeframe close < Level AND the current and two latest completed higher-timeframe highs < Level + offset AND the second latest completed higher-timeframe high < Level + offset; −50 for each of the 12 latest chart-timeframe lows < Level − offset; −50 for each of the 31 latest chart-timeframe patterns where the high minus the low three bars later is > offset, the close is greater than the open three bars later, and current forming daily RAVI > threshold; −50 if none of the 15 latest higher-timeframe lows < Level − offset; −50 if all of the 8 latest higher-timeframe lows > Level − offset.
Long: Long score ≥ threshold
Short: Short score ≥ threshold
Exit: long, low ≤ stop-loss price OR high ≥ take-profit price
Exit: short, high ≥ stop-loss price OR low ≤ take-profit price
