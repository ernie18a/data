Calculation: Selected price = independently chosen high-side and low-side price, each selected from open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted close, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, trend-biased extreme, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price. Trend-biased price is the average of high and close when close > open, otherwise the average of low and close. Trend-biased extreme is high when close > open, low when close < open, otherwise close.
Calculation: Filtered value = optionally filter each selected price and/or the oscillator independently. For each series, let change be the absolute difference between the current input and the previous filtered value, and let average change be the sum of the current and preceding changes over the filter lookback divided by that lookback. The threshold is a filter multiplier times the square root of the sum, over the filter lookback, of squared differences between each bar's change and its own average change, divided by the lookback. If the current change is less than the threshold, retain the previous filtered value; otherwise use the current input. The first filtered value equals its input; unavailable initial changes contribute zero.
Calculation: Aroon oscillator = after optional price filtering, swap the two selected prices whenever the low-side price exceeds the high-side price. Over the current bar and the preceding oscillator-lookback bars, find the ages of the highest high-side price and lowest low-side price, choosing the most recent occurrence on ties. The oscillator is 100 times (age of lowest minus age of highest) divided by the oscillator lookback, optionally filtered as described above. Use available bars at the start of the series. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Oscillator quantile = sort the current oscillator and preceding oscillator values over the quantile lookback, padding unavailable initial values with zero. At the chosen quantile, linearly interpolate between adjacent sorted values at index (lookback minus 1) times the quantile fraction. The upper quantile uses a higher quantile fraction than the lower quantile and both use the same lookback.
Long: Aroon oscillator > upper oscillator quantile
Short: Aroon oscillator < lower oscillator quantile
