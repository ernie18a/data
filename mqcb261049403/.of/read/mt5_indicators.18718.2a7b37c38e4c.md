Calculation: Trend state and bands = On each calculation timeframe, initialize the trend state to neutral and both previous bands to the close of the first eligible bar, after moving average and ATR warm-up. For each bar, set the upper band to the moving average of the selected price plus an ATR multiple and the lower band to the moving average minus the same ATR multiple. Start with the previous trend state; set it to up if the high exceeds the previous upper band, then set it to down if the low is below the previous lower band. In an up state, raise the lower band to at least its previous value; in a down state, lower the upper band to at most its previous value. Carry the resulting state and bands forward. The moving average method, price, lookback, ATR lookback, and ATR multiple are parameters.
Long: Chart-timeframe trend state is up.
Short: Chart-timeframe trend state is down.
Long: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the still-forming higher-timeframe trend state is up; retain this decision through the remaining chart bars of that higher-timeframe bar.
Short: On the first chart bar of a higher-timeframe bar, the still-forming higher-timeframe trend state is down; retain this decision through the remaining chart bars of that higher-timeframe bar.
