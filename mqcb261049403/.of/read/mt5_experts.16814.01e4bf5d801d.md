Calculation: EMA spread = 100 × (short-lookback EMA of close − long-lookback EMA of close) / long-lookback EMA of close; use hourly and daily bars, reading the current forming bar for index 0 and completed bars for higher indices
Calculation: Entry reference level = current bid rounded to a price precision + a parameterized offset for long entries, or − that offset for short entries
Long: Long score ≥ its threshold, where the score starts at 0 and adds 10 if the current hourly EMA spread is below 0; adds 7 if either of the two latest completed hourly highs exceeds the entry reference level plus a parameterized rise; adds 45 if current bid is below the entry reference level, the latest completed minute close is above it, the current and two latest completed hourly lows are above it minus the offset plus a parameterized buffer; subtracts 50 for each of the 12 most recent minute bars whose high exceeds the reference level plus a parameterized upper threshold; subtracts 50 if any of the 31 tested minute-bar pairs has a rise from the low at index x to the open at index x+3 above a parameterized range, the earlier open exceeds the later close, and the current daily EMA spread is below a parameterized negative threshold; subtracts 50 if none of the 15 hourly bars at indices 0–14 has a high above the reference level plus a parameterized threshold; and subtracts 50 if all of the eight most recent 30-minute highs are below the reference level plus a parameterized threshold. Use aggregated minute, hourly, and 30-minute bars; indices 0 are forming and positive indices are completed. Restrict to times other than Monday hours up to a parameterized cutoff.
Short: Short score ≥ its threshold, where the score starts at 0 and adds 10 if the current hourly EMA spread is above 0; adds 7 if either of the two latest completed hourly lows is below the entry reference level minus a parameterized fall; adds 45 if current bid is above the entry reference level, the latest completed minute close is below it, the current and two latest completed hourly highs are below it plus the offset minus a parameterized buffer; subtracts 50 for each of the 12 most recent minute bars whose low is below the reference level minus a parameterized lower threshold; subtracts 50 if any of the 31 tested minute-bar pairs has a fall from the high at index x to the low at index x+3 above a parameterized range, the later close exceeds the earlier open, and the current daily EMA spread is above a parameterized positive threshold; subtracts 50 if none of the 15 hourly bars at indices 0–14 has a low below the reference level minus a parameterized threshold; and subtracts 50 if all of the eight most recent 30-minute lows are above the reference level minus a parameterized threshold. Use aggregated minute, hourly, and 30-minute bars; indices 0 are forming and positive indices are completed. Restrict to times other than Monday hours up to a parameterized cutoff.
