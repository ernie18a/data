Calculation: Input price = a selectable open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or trend-biased extreme, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open, otherwise (low + close)/2. Trend-biased extreme is high when close > open, low when close < open, otherwise close.
Calculation: T3 = the weighted sum of the third through sixth successive exponential smoothings of the input price: (1 + 3a + 3a² + a³)E3 + (-3a - 6a² - 3a³)E4 + (3a² + 3a³)E5 - a³E6, where a is the configurable volume factor. Each smoothing starts at the first input price and uses alpha = 2/(1 + period) for the original method or 2/(2 + (period - 1)/2) for the modified method. For period <= 1, all smoothings remain at their initial value. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Floating levels = the minimum T3 over the configurable lookback including the current bar plus the range between the maximum and minimum T3 over that lookback multiplied by a configurable fraction. Use separate upper and lower fractions, with the upper greater than the lower. The middle level is their average. Calculate on the same timeframe as T3.
Calculation: Oscillator = T3 minus the middle floating level, calculated on the same timeframe as T3.
Long: In outer-level mode, T3 > upper floating level; OR in middle-level mode, T3 > middle floating level; OR in T3-slope mode, T3 > previous T3; OR in oscillator-slope mode, oscillator > previous oscillator.
Short: In outer-level mode, T3 < lower floating level; OR in middle-level mode, T3 < middle floating level; OR in T3-slope mode, T3 < previous T3; OR in oscillator-slope mode, oscillator < previous oscillator.
