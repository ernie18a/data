Calculation: Upper floating level = the rolling low of T3 plus the rolling T3 range multiplied by the upper level percentage, minus the midpoint of the upper and lower floating levels
Calculation: Lower floating level = the rolling low of T3 plus the rolling T3 range multiplied by the lower level percentage, minus the midpoint of the upper and lower floating levels
Long: T3 > upper floating level OR T3 oscillator > 0 OR T3 > previous bar's T3 OR T3 oscillator > previous bar's T3 oscillator
