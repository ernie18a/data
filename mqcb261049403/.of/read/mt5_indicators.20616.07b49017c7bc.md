Calculation: Ichimoku XMA = smooth the midpoint between the high of the bar with the highest configured OHLC price over the upper lookback and the low of the bar with the lowest configured OHLC price over the lower lookback.
Calculation: 2XMA Ichimoku oscillator = shorter-lookback Ichimoku XMA minus longer-lookback Ichimoku XMA; on a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar.
Long: 2XMA Ichimoku oscillator > 0
Short: 2XMA Ichimoku oscillator < 0
