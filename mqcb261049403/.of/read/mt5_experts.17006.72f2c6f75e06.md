Calculation: Fractal WPR = Williams %R over a dynamic lookback. Let FD be 1 + (ln(L) + ln(2)) / ln(2 × (e_period − 1)), where L is the sum over consecutive values in the e_period applied-price bars of sqrt((difference in each value’s position within that window’s high-low range)^2 + 1/e_period^2). The dynamic lookback is round(normal_speed / (2 × (2 − FD))). Williams %R = −100 × (highest high − applied price) / (highest high − lowest low) over that lookback.
Long: Fractal WPR on the lower-timeframe last completed bar > the oversold level AND Fractal WPR on the preceding completed bar <= the oversold level
Short: Fractal WPR on the lower-timeframe last completed bar < the overbought level AND Fractal WPR on the preceding completed bar >= the overbought level
Exit: Long, Fractal WPR on the lower-timeframe last completed bar < the overbought level AND Fractal WPR on the preceding completed bar >= the overbought level OR low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance
Exit: Short, Fractal WPR on the lower-timeframe last completed bar > the oversold level AND Fractal WPR on the preceding completed bar <= the oversold level OR high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price − target distance
