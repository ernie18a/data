Calculation: Applied price = selected open, high, low, close, median price, typical price, weighted close, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, trend-following price, or DeMark price. The first trend-following price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise; the second is (high + close)/2 when close > open, (low + close)/2 when close < open, and close otherwise. For DeMark price, let X = (high + low + close + low)/2 when close < open, (high + low + close + high)/2 when close > open, and (high + low + 2 * close)/2 otherwise; price = (2 * X - low - high)/2.
Calculation: Fractal Williams %R = on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe, use the selected signal bar: the still-forming bar or a completed bar at a configurable lag. For each indicator bar, let n be the fractal lookback, p the applied price, M = max(0, highest p over n bars), and m = min(9999999999, lowest p over n bars). If M > m, normalize each of those n prices as q = (p - m)/(M - m), and let L be the sum over adjacent normalized prices of sqrt((q_current - q_previous)^2 + 1/n^2); otherwise L = 0. Let D = 1 + ln(2 * L)/ln(2 * (n - 1)) if L > 0, otherwise D = 0. Set the Williams %R lookback to round(normal speed / (2 * (2 - D))). Using that many bars including the indicator bar, let H = max(0, highest high) and B = min(9999999999, lowest low). The value is -100 * (H - p)/(H - B); if H = B, use the previous completed value, initially -50. A value is unavailable when the bar index is less than the lookback.
Long: In direct mode, previous Fractal Williams %R <= oversold level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R > oversold level; OR in inverse mode, previous Fractal Williams %R >= overbought level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R < overbought level.
Short: In direct mode, previous Fractal Williams %R >= overbought level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R < overbought level; OR in inverse mode, previous Fractal Williams %R <= oversold level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R > oversold level.
Exit: long, direct mode AND previous Fractal Williams %R >= overbought level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R < overbought level OR inverse mode AND previous Fractal Williams %R <= oversold level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R > oversold level OR low <= entry price - enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, direct mode AND previous Fractal Williams %R <= oversold level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R > oversold level OR inverse mode AND previous Fractal Williams %R >= overbought level AND selected-bar Fractal Williams %R < overbought level OR high >= entry price + enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - enabled take-profit distance.
