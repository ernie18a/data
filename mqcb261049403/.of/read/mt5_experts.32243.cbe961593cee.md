Long: Initial trading is enabled AND there is no long position AND CCI on its independently selected same, higher, or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, is below the negative CCI threshold AND RSI on its independently selected same, higher, or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, is below the RSI threshold.
Short: Initial trading is enabled AND there is no short position AND CCI on its independently selected same, higher, or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, is above the positive CCI threshold AND RSI on its independently selected same, higher, or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, is above the RSI maximum minus the RSI threshold.
