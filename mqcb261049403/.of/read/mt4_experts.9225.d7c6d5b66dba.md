Calculation: Fractal = Williams 5-bar fractal; upper if the bar's high is above the highs of the 2 bars on each side, lower if the bar's low is below the lows of the 2 bars on each side
Calculation: FractalDir(tf, n) = +1 if the bar n bars back on tf (0 = forming bar) has a lower fractal and no upper fractal; -1 if it has an upper fractal and no lower fractal; else 0
Calculation: BasicLong = FractalDir(chart TF, n1) > 0 AND FractalDir(higher TF, n2) > 0 AND RSI(higher TF, applied to open price, forming bar) < 50 - L AND StochasticMain(slow, 3/3 SMA smoothing, low/high, chart TF, forming bar) < 50 - M (L, M >= 0)
Calculation: BasicShort = FractalDir(chart TF, n3) < 0 AND FractalDir(higher TF, n4) < 0 AND RSI(higher TF, applied to open price, forming bar) > 50 + L2 AND StochasticMain(slow, 3/3 SMA smoothing, low/high, chart TF, forming bar) > 50 + M2 (L2, M2 >= 0; long and short parameters are independent; BasicLong takes priority if both are true)
Long: no open position AND BasicLong AND ParabolicSAR(smaller acceleration step, chart TF, forming bar) >= ParabolicSAR(larger acceleration step, same max step, chart TF, forming bar) AND EMA(close, higher TF) of last completed bar > EMA of the bar before it AND ParabolicSAR(higher TF, forming bar) < open of current chart bar AND low[1] > min + k*(max - min) AND PerceptronSum > 0
Short: no open position AND BasicShort AND ParabolicSAR(smaller step, chart TF, forming bar) <= ParabolicSAR(larger step, chart TF, forming bar) AND EMA(close, higher TF) of last completed bar < EMA of the bar before it AND ParabolicSAR(higher TF, forming bar) > open of current chart bar AND high[1] < max - k*(max - min) AND low[1] <= min + k*(max - min) AND PerceptronSum < 0
Calculation: max/min = highest high / lowest low of the N completed bars ending at bar 1; k is a fraction of the range; PerceptronSum = w1*(high[1]-high[7]) + w2*(high[4]-high[11]) + w3*(low[1]-low[7]) + w4*(low[4]-low[11]), where w1..w4 are fixed signed weights (any sign) and [n] = n bars back from the forming bar
Exit: long, BasicShort is true (opposite signal)
Exit: short, BasicLong is true (opposite signal)
Exit: long, price <= entry - stop distance OR price >= entry + take-profit distance OR price <= trailing stop, where the trailing stop = highest (price - trail distance) since profit (price - entry) first exceeded an activation distance >= trail distance
Exit: short, price >= entry + stop distance OR price <= entry - take-profit distance OR price >= trailing stop, where the trailing stop = lowest (price + trail distance) since profit (entry - price) first exceeded an activation distance >= trail distance
