Calculation: Bullish fractal state = an upper fractal is absent and a lower fractal exists at the selected bar; bearish fractal state = a lower fractal is absent and an upper fractal exists. Fractals are evaluated at configurable shifts on the chart timeframe and on a higher timeframe, using completed bars.
Calculation: RSI = RSI of bar open prices, evaluated on a higher timeframe’s bar still forming; the v2.1 signal uses a longer RSI period than the final-version signal.
Calculation: Stochastic = main line of the stochastic oscillator, evaluated on the chart timeframe’s bar still forming.
Calculation: EMA direction = the higher-timeframe EMA on the last completed bar minus the EMA on the preceding completed bar.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR position = parabolic SAR on a higher-timeframe bar still forming compared with the chart-timeframe bar’s open, which is still forming.
Calculation: Channel boundaries = highest high and lowest low over a configurable lookback of completed chart-timeframe bars; channel pivot = average of the closes of the three most recent completed chart-timeframe bars. Long channel boundary = lowest low + k × (highest high − lowest low); short channel boundary = highest high − k × (highest high − lowest low).
Calculation: Weighted high-low momentum score = four configurable weights, each offset by 50, multiplied respectively by (high[1] − high[7]), (high[4] − high[11]), (low[1] − low[7]), and (low[4] − low[11]), then summed; all bars are completed chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Final-version buy enabled AND (fractal filter disabled OR bullish fractal state on the chart timeframe AND on a higher timeframe) AND (RSI filter disabled OR higher-timeframe RSI < 50 − configurable threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR chart-timeframe stochastic main line < 50 − configurable threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA direction > 0) AND (SAR filter disabled OR higher-timeframe parabolic SAR < chart-timeframe open) AND (channel filter disabled OR low[1] > long channel boundary) AND (momentum filter disabled OR weighted high-low momentum score > 0).
Long: OR, v2.1 signal, bullish fractal state on the chart timeframe AND on a higher timeframe AND higher-timeframe RSI < 50 AND the first of two differently configured chart-timeframe parabolic SAR values >= the second; RSI and SAR bars are still forming.
Short: Final-version sell enabled AND (fractal filter disabled OR bearish fractal state on the chart timeframe AND on a higher timeframe) AND (RSI filter disabled OR higher-timeframe RSI > 50 + configurable threshold) AND (stochastic filter disabled OR chart-timeframe stochastic main line > 50 + configurable threshold) AND (EMA filter disabled OR EMA direction < 0) AND (SAR filter disabled OR higher-timeframe parabolic SAR > chart-timeframe open) AND (channel filter disabled OR high[1] < short channel boundary) AND (momentum filter disabled OR weighted high-low momentum score < 0).
Short: OR, v2.1 signal, bearish fractal state on the chart timeframe AND on a higher timeframe AND higher-timeframe RSI > 50 AND the first of two differently configured chart-timeframe parabolic SAR values <= the second; RSI and SAR bars are still forming.
Exit: Long, close on opposite short signal when opposite-signal closing is enabled, or when price reaches entry price + take-profit distance or entry price − stop-loss distance, or when price reaches the active trailing stop. For profit trailing, begin moving the stop only after profit exceeds the configured trailing distance in the final version, or twice that distance in v2.1; when profit trailing is disabled, move it without that threshold. Move the long stop upward when it is below bid − trailing distance.
Exit: Short, close on opposite long signal when opposite-signal closing is enabled, or when price reaches entry price − take-profit distance or entry price + stop-loss distance, or when price reaches the active trailing stop. For profit trailing, begin moving the stop only after profit exceeds the configured trailing distance in the final version, or twice that distance in v2.1; when profit trailing is disabled, move it without that threshold. Move the short stop downward when it is above ask + trailing distance or unset.
