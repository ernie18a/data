Calculation: Spectrally smoothed RVI lines = Calculate the standard Relative Vigor Index main and signal lines on a configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. At each evaluation, include the indicator bar still forming and take a window of 2^N + 1 values ending at that bar. For each line separately, apply a type-I discrete cosine transform, retain a configurable number of lowest-frequency coefficients, set the remaining coefficients to zero, and apply the inverse transform. Use the resulting values for both the selected signal bar and its preceding bar from this same transformed window.
Calculation: Signal bar = A configurable bar offset on the indicator timeframe; it may select the still-forming bar or a completed bar. For a completed signal bar, evaluate when a new indicator-timeframe bar begins; for the still-forming signal bar, evaluate as it updates.
Long: Smoothed RVI main line on the signal bar > smoothed RVI signal line on the signal bar AND smoothed RVI main line on the preceding bar <= smoothed RVI signal line on the preceding bar AND position is flat.
Short: Smoothed RVI main line on the signal bar < smoothed RVI signal line on the signal bar AND smoothed RVI main line on the preceding bar >= smoothed RVI signal line on the preceding bar AND position is flat.
Exit: long, smoothed RVI main line on the signal bar < smoothed RVI signal line on the signal bar OR low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance.
Exit: short, smoothed RVI main line on the signal bar > smoothed RVI signal line on the signal bar OR high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance.
