Calculation: Fractal high/low = a Williams fractal on the chart timeframe, evaluated using the bar still forming; compare the fractal bar with bars on both sides.
Calculation: Long stop and target = initially the entry price minus the stop distance and plus the target distance. At each new chart bar, optionally raise the stop to the lowest low over a lookback including the forming bar minus a padding distance; whenever this raises the stop, also increase the target by a trailing distance. Alternatively, once the open exceeds the entry price by an activation distance, raise the stop to the open minus a trailing distance. Optionally, once the open exceeds the entry price by a break-even activation distance and the stop is below entry, raise the stop to entry plus a break-even offset.
Calculation: Short stop and target = initially the entry price plus the stop distance and minus the target distance. At each new chart bar, optionally lower the stop to the highest high over a lookback including the forming bar plus a padding distance; whenever this lowers the stop, also decrease the target by a trailing distance. Alternatively, once the open is below the entry price by an activation distance, lower the stop to the open plus a trailing distance. Optionally, once the open is below the entry price by a break-even activation distance and the stop is above entry, lower the stop to entry minus a break-even offset.
Long: At the open of a new chart bar, absolute difference between open and the close two bars ago > a distance threshold AND shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the higher timeframe's forming bar AND low two bars ago < previous bar's high AND RSI of close on a higher timeframe's forming bar > RSI on that timeframe's last completed bar.
Short: At the open of a new chart bar, absolute difference between open and the previous bar's close > a distance threshold AND shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price < longer-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the higher timeframe's forming bar AND previous bar's low < high two bars ago AND RSI of close on a higher timeframe's forming bar < RSI on that timeframe's last completed bar.
Long: A fractal low is confirmed.
Short: A fractal high is confirmed.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
Exit: long, all six short-period exponential moving averages of typical price are equal OR all six longer-period exponential moving averages of typical price are equal OR the previous close <= the previous lower Bollinger Band.
Exit: short, all six short-period exponential moving averages of typical price are equal OR all six longer-period exponential moving averages of typical price are equal OR the previous close <= the previous lower Bollinger Band.
