Long: At the start of a chart bar, position side = flat AND the last completed Heikin-Ashi close >= its open AND (that close = its open OR the preceding Heikin-Ashi close <= its open) AND the last completed Heikin-Ashi open > a moving average of close AND the last completed close > the preceding bar's high AND the preceding bar's close < its open AND the last completed shorter-period moving average of close > the longer-period moving average of close AND each of the two RSI filters passes
Short: At the start of a chart bar, position side = flat AND the last completed Heikin-Ashi close <= its open AND (that close = its open OR the preceding Heikin-Ashi close >= its open) AND the last completed Heikin-Ashi open < a moving average of close AND the last completed close < the preceding bar's low AND the preceding bar's close > its open AND the last completed shorter-period moving average of close < the longer-period moving average of close AND each of the two RSI filters passes
Calculation: RSI filter = RSI of close, rounded to two decimal places, is within inclusive lower and upper bounds on the oldest bar of a parameterized consecutive window AND the count of values outside those bounds in that window <= the allowed exception count; the window starts a parameterized number of bars before the last completed chart bar, and the shorter-period RSI uses an older starting bar and a longer window than the longer-period RSI
Exit: long, low <= entry price - fixed stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + fixed take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + fixed stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - fixed take-profit distance
