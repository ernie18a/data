Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period moving average of RSI of the selected bar price.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period moving average of the fast line, using the same smoothing method.
Long: Fast line > slow line on the chart timeframe OR fast line > slow line on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Short: Fast line < slow line on the chart timeframe OR fast line < slow line on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
