Calculation: Rising OBV trend = each successive OBV peak and trough is higher than the preceding peak and trough
Calculation: Falling OBV trend = each successive OBV peak and trough is lower than the preceding peak and trough
Calculation: Doubtful OBV trend = OBV moves sideways without making successive highs and lows
Long: OBV changes to a rising trend
Short: OBV changes to a falling trend
Exit: Long, OBV changes to a falling trend OR a doubtful OBV trend persists longer than its persistence threshold
Exit: Short, OBV changes to a rising trend OR a doubtful OBV trend persists longer than its persistence threshold
