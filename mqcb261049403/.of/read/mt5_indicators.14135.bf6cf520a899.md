Calculation: Selected price = the selected one of open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, or one of these: high if close > open, low if close < open, otherwise close; (high + close)/2 if close > open, (low + close)/2 if close < open, otherwise close; or (close + x)/2, where x is low if close < open, high if close > open, otherwise close.
Calculation: Digital filter = sum of selected prices at the current bar and successive earlier bars multiplied respectively by [0.24470985659780, 0.23139774006970, 0.20613796947320, 0.17166230340640, 0.13146907903600, 0.08950387549560, 0.04960091651250, 0.01502270569607, -0.01188033734430, -0.02989873856137, -0.03898967104900, -0.04014113626390, -0.03511968085800, -0.02611613850342, -0.01539056955666, -0.00495353651394, 0.00368588764825, 0.00963614049782, 0.01265138888314, 0.01307496106868, 0.01169702291063, 0.00974841844086, 0.00898900012545, -0.00649745721156].
Calculation: Reference close = close of the bar that is round(seconds elapsed since midnight at the current bar's opening time / bar duration in seconds) + 1 bars before the current bar.
Calculation: Trigger = reference close plus a configurable price-distance threshold if the digital filter >= reference close; otherwise reference close minus that threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe filter and trigger = digital filter and trigger calculated on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming as sampled at the opening of that higher-timeframe bar and held until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Digital filter > trigger OR higher-timeframe filter > higher-timeframe trigger
Short: Digital filter < trigger OR higher-timeframe filter < higher-timeframe trigger
