Calculation: Cycle filter = For midpoint price p=(high+low)/2 and smoothing parameter a, let s[t]=(p[t]+2p[t-1]+2p[t-2]+p[t-3])/6 and c[t]=(1-a/2)^2*(s[t]-2s[t-1]+s[t-2])+2*(1-a)*c[t-1]-(1-a)^2*c[t-2]; initialize c with (p[t]-2p[t-1]+p[t-2])/4 and unavailable history with zero.
Calculation: Dominant cycle period = Apply the cycle filter to midpoint prices, using its initialization for the first six bars. Let q[t]=(0.0962*c[t]+0.5769*c[t-2]-0.5769*c[t-4]-0.0962*c[t-6])*(0.5+0.08*i[t-1]), r[t]=c[t-3], and d[t]=(r[t]/q[t]-r[t-1]/q[t-1])/(1+r[t]*r[t-1]/(q[t]*q[t-1])) when both q values are nonzero; otherwise retain the value in the current five-bar circular-buffer slot. Clamp d to [0.1,1.1], take its five-bar median m, set dc=2*pi/m+0.5, i[t]=0.67*i[t-1]+0.33*dc, and period[t]=0.85*period[t-1]+0.15*i[t]. Initialize i and period to 1 and buffers to zero.
Calculation: Sine and lead sine = Apply the cycle filter separately, initializing it through the eighth bar. For n=min(floor(dominant cycle period), number of preceding bars), calculate R=sum from j=0 to n-1 of sin(2*pi*j/n)*c[t-j] and I=sum from j=0 to n-1 of cos(2*pi*j/n)*c[t-j]. In degrees, phase=atan(R/I) when abs(I)>0.001, otherwise phase=90 if R>=0 and -90 otherwise; add 90, add 180 if I<0, and subtract 360 if phase>315. Sine=sin(phase); lead sine=sin(phase+45), except set lead sine to sin(45) when phase=180 and previous lead sine>0, or to sin(225) when phase=0 and previous lead sine<0. Initialize the filter buffers to zero and start its calculation at the fourth bar.
Calculation: Slow and fast sine pairs = Calculate the sine and lead sine independently on a higher timeframe and on the chart timeframe, respectively, using the last completed bar of each; the preceding fast values are from the preceding completed chart bar.
Long: Slow lead sine > slow sine AND preceding fast lead sine <= preceding fast sine AND fast lead sine > fast sine.
Short: Slow lead sine < slow sine AND preceding fast lead sine >= preceding fast sine AND fast lead sine < fast sine.
Exit: long, slow lead sine < slow sine OR fast lead sine < fast sine OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, slow lead sine > slow sine OR fast lead sine > fast sine OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
