Calculation: Moving average = a selected moving average of a selected price derived from OHLC, calculated on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar. The selectable types are simple, exponential, double smoothed exponential, double exponential, triple exponential, smoothed, linear weighted, parabolic weighted, Alexander, volume weighted, Hull, triangular, sine weighted, linear regression value, IE/2, non-lag, zero-lag exponential, and leader exponential moving averages. Use volume for volume weighting.
Calculation: Double smoothed exponential moving average = apply an EMA twice, each with smoothing factor 2/(1+sqrt(length)).
Calculation: Parabolic weighted moving average = weighted mean of prices at offsets k from the current bar, with weight (length-k)^2.
Calculation: Alexander moving average = weighted mean of prices at offsets k from the current bar, with weight length-k-2; use price itself when length < 4.
Calculation: IE/2 = ((sum(price)/length + slope) + (sum(price)+slope*sum(k))/length)/2, where slope = (length*sum(k*price)-sum(k)*sum(price))/(sum(k)^2-length*sum(k^2)); sums use offsets k from the current bar over the lookback.
Calculation: Leader exponential moving average = EMA(price) + EMA(price - EMA(price)), with the same length for all EMAs.
Calculation: Non-lag moving average = sum(weight[k]*price[k])/sum(weight[k]) over length*4+length-1 offsets, where phase=length-1, t=k/(phase-1) when k<=phase-1 and otherwise t=1+(k-phase+1)*7/(4*length-1), and weight[k]=cos(pi*t) multiplied by 1 when t<=0.5 and otherwise by 1/(3*pi*t+1). Use the current price when length<3 or fewer than 3 prior bars exist; unavailable historical prices contribute zero.
Long: Moving average > its previous bar's value OR moving average = its previous bar's value AND the previous bar's direction is long.
Short: Moving average < its previous bar's value OR moving average = its previous bar's value AND the previous bar's direction is short.
