Calculation: Current price = close of the still-forming chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Latest swing high and low = the most recent confirmed upper and lower five-bar fractal prices.
Calculation: Triangle midpoint = midpoint of the high and low of the most recent bar containing either fractal.
Calculation: Rising 45° line = line projected upward from the low of the most recent fractal bar, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Falling 315° line = line projected downward from the high of the most recent fractal bar, evaluated at the current bar.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = for each ratio r in {0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 1, 1.618, 2, 2.618}, if current price is below the latest swing low, level(r) = swing high − (swing high − swing low) × r; otherwise level(r) = swing low + (swing high − swing low) × r.
Long: (swing low < current price < swing high AND (level(0.382) < current price < level(0.5) OR level(0.5) < current price < level(0.618))) OR (current price > swing high AND level(1) < current price < level(1.618)) OR (current price < swing low AND level(2.618) < current price < level(2)) OR (current price > swing high AND level(2) < current price < level(2.618)) OR (current price > triangle midpoint AND current price > rising 45° line AND current price > falling 315° line AND rising 45° line is nonzero AND (swing low < current price < swing high AND level(0.5) < current price < level(0.618) OR current price > swing high AND level(1) < current price < level(1.618) OR current price < swing low AND level(2.618) < current price < level(2)))
Short: (swing low < current price < swing high AND level(0.382) < current price < level(0.5)) OR (current price < swing low AND level(1.618) < current price < level(1)) OR (current price > swing high AND level(2) < current price < level(2.618)) OR (current price < triangle midpoint AND current price < falling 315° line AND current price < rising 45° line AND falling 315° line is nonzero AND (swing low < current price < swing high AND level(0.382) < current price < level(0.5) OR current price < swing low AND level(1.618) < current price < level(1) OR current price > swing high AND level(2) < current price < level(2.618)))
