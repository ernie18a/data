Calculation: Asian range = highest high minus lowest low across completed chart-timeframe bars from the Asian session start through its end
Calculation: Long entry price = Asian session high + ATR-based buffer
Calculation: Short entry price = Asian session low − ATR-based buffer
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR-based stop distance, fixed when pending orders are placed
Long: during the London entry window AND Asian range is between the minimum and maximum fractions of Daily ATR AND bar high ≥ long entry price
Short: during the London entry window AND Asian range is between the minimum and maximum fractions of Daily ATR AND bar low ≤ short entry price
Exit: long, bar low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR bar high ≥ entry price + reward multiple × stop distance
Exit: short, bar high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR bar low ≤ entry price − reward multiple × stop distance
