Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times are within the same-day range window, including both endpoints; lock the range when the window ends, using the bar still forming at that time if its opening time is the end of the window.
Calculation: Placement snapshot = at the first opportunity after the range is locked and within the breakout window, record chart-timeframe ATR from the bar still forming and ATR from the higher timeframe bar still forming; attempt placement only once per day.
Calculation: Breakout levels = locked range high plus a positive multiple of snapshot chart ATR for the long level, and locked range low minus the same buffer for the short level; keep both levels fixed until the breakout window ends.
Calculation: Stop distance = a positive multiple of snapshot chart ATR; keep it fixed for the trade.
Long: Placement snapshot higher-timeframe ATR > 0 AND snapshot chart ATR > 0 AND locked range width >= a lower fraction of snapshot higher-timeframe ATR AND locked range width <= a higher fraction of snapshot higher-timeframe ATR AND price at placement < long breakout level AND time >= breakout window start AND time < breakout window end AND high >= long breakout level AND no opposite breakout order has filled that day.
Short: Placement snapshot higher-timeframe ATR > 0 AND snapshot chart ATR > 0 AND locked range width >= a lower fraction of snapshot higher-timeframe ATR AND locked range width <= a higher fraction of snapshot higher-timeframe ATR AND price at placement > short breakout level AND time >= breakout window start AND time < breakout window end AND low <= short breakout level AND no opposite breakout order has filled that day.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop distance OR high >= entry price plus a positive reward multiple of stop distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop distance OR low <= entry price minus a positive reward multiple of stop distance.
