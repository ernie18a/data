Calculation: MACD main line = shorter-lookback EMA of close minus longer-lookback EMA of close; MACD signal line = moving average of the MACD main line
Long: On lower-timeframe bars still forming, MACD main line is above its signal line on both the shorter- and longer-timeframe pair; OR on higher-timeframe bars still forming, (MACD main line is below its signal line on the shorter timeframe, above on the middle timeframe, and above on the longer timeframe) OR (above on the shorter timeframe, below on the middle timeframe, and above on the longer timeframe) OR (above on both shorter and middle timeframes, and below on the longer timeframe) OR (above on all three timeframes)
Short: On lower-timeframe bars still forming, MACD main line is below its signal line on both the shorter- and longer-timeframe pair; OR on higher-timeframe bars still forming, (MACD main line is above its signal line on the shorter timeframe, below on the middle timeframe, and below on the longer timeframe) OR (below on both shorter and middle timeframes, and above on the longer timeframe) OR (below on the shorter timeframe, above on the middle timeframe, and below on the longer timeframe) OR (below on all three timeframes)
