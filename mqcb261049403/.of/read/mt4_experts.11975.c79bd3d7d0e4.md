Calculation: Moving averages = moving averages of close on a configurable timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming. Compare the current values with values at the same configurable earlier-bar offset. The fast average has a shorter lookback than the slow average.
Long: Hour of bar open <= configured start hour AND hour of bar open < configured end hour AND ((normal direction enabled AND earlier fast average < earlier slow average AND current fast average > current slow average AND configured slow-average trend condition is satisfied) OR (countertrend enabled AND earlier fast average > earlier slow average AND current fast average < current slow average AND configured slow-average trend condition is satisfied)). The bullish crossover's selectable trend condition is earlier slow average < current slow average, earlier slow average > current slow average, or no trend restriction; the bearish crossover's selectable trend condition is earlier slow average > current slow average or no trend restriction.
Short: Hour of bar open <= configured start hour AND hour of bar open < configured end hour AND ((normal direction enabled AND earlier fast average > earlier slow average AND current fast average < current slow average AND configured slow-average trend condition is satisfied) OR (countertrend enabled AND earlier fast average < earlier slow average AND current fast average > current slow average AND configured slow-average trend condition is satisfied)). The bullish crossover's selectable trend condition is earlier slow average < current slow average, earlier slow average > current slow average, or no trend restriction; the bearish crossover's selectable trend condition is earlier slow average > current slow average or no trend restriction.
Exit: long, fixed stop enabled AND low <= entry price minus stop distance OR take profit enabled AND high >= entry price plus profit distance.
Exit: short, fixed stop enabled AND high >= entry price plus stop distance OR take profit enabled AND low <= entry price minus profit distance.
