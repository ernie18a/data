Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a closed bar whose high/low is at least as high/low as every high/low in an equal lookback on each side; confirm it when the final right-side bar closes. Track the latest confirmed swing of each type and mark it unused when confirmed. Update swings only while flat.
Calculation: Pending sweep = while flat and no sweep is pending, a closed bar sweeps the latest unused swing high if its high is greater than the swing high plus an ATR multiple and its close is below the swing high; it sweeps the latest unused swing low if its low is less than the swing low minus an ATR multiple and its close is above the swing low. Mark the swept swing used even if the optional trend filter rejects the sweep. If both sweeps occur on the same bar, process only the high sweep. When the optional time-window filter is enabled, the sweep must occur within the configured window. When the optional trend filter is enabled, retain a high sweep only if the higher-timeframe still-forming bar's close is below its EMA, and a low sweep only if that close is above its EMA; if the EMA is unavailable, allow the sweep. Store the sweep bar's body high and low and its high or low as the sweep extreme. A pending sweep expires after a configured number of subsequent bars; ignore new sweeps while it is pending. The first subsequent close through the appropriate body boundary consumes the pending sweep even if another entry condition fails.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a long, the sweep low minus an ATR multiple; for a short, the sweep high plus an ATR multiple. Use ATR from the confirmation bar. Initial risk = the distance from entry price to initial stop.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until the optional break-even trigger is reached, then entry price plus a fixed fraction of initial risk for a long or entry price minus that fraction for a short. The trigger is reached when favorable price movement from entry is at least a configured multiple of initial risk.
Long: Position is flat AND a low sweep is pending AND the current closed bar is within the allowed confirmation window AND its close is above the sweep body high AND its high minus low is at least a configured multiple of its ATR AND entry price minus initial stop is at least a configured multiple of that ATR.
Short: Position is flat AND a high sweep is pending AND the current closed bar is within the allowed confirmation window AND its close is below the sweep body low AND its high minus low is at least a configured multiple of its ATR AND initial stop minus entry price is at least a configured multiple of that ATR.
Exit: Long, price is at or below the active stop OR price is at or above entry price plus a configured multiple of initial risk.
Exit: Short, price is at or above the active stop OR price is at or below entry price minus a configured multiple of initial risk.
