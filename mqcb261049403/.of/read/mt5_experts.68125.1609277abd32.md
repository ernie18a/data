Calculation: Fair value gap = for three consecutive completed chart bars, a bullish gap has oldest high < newest low and boundaries oldest high and newest low; a bearish gap has oldest low > newest high and boundaries newest high and oldest low. Gap size is upper boundary minus lower boundary; midpoint is their average. Retain each gap until its first midpoint touch or reset. A bullish gap resets when the forming chart bar opens below its lower boundary and its close rises above its upper boundary; a bearish gap resets when the forming chart bar opens above its upper boundary and its close falls below its lower boundary. Check reset before touch.
Calculation: Midpoint touch = an active gap's midpoint is between the forming chart bar's low and high, inclusive; optionally use the forming bar of a lower timeframe for this range. Only the first touch of each gap counts.
Calculation: Trend filter = optionally require gap size >= a separate minimum and, for a bullish gap, forming chart close > previous completed-bar close EMA > forming-bar close EMA, or, for a bearish gap, forming chart close < previous completed-bar close EMA < forming-bar close EMA.
Calculation: Liquidity level = a previously recorded swing high or low, equal high or low, or the high or low of a last completed higher-timeframe day or week. A swing high or low is strictly higher or lower than every bar on both sides over the same lookback. Equal levels are same-side swing levels separated by less than a price tolerance. A level is swept once, when the forming chart high exceeds a high level or the forming chart low falls below a low level.
Calculation: Liquidity target = at entry, select the first nonzero level in the session-specific priority order: associated session high, associated session low, last completed higher-timeframe day high, day low, week high, then week low. In the later session-target variant, select the associated session high if the forming chart close is below it and the high is nonzero; otherwise select the nonzero session low. Session highs and lows are accumulated from forming chart-bar highs and lows while their session is active, without resetting between sessions.
Long: New bullish fair value gap size >= minimum gap size.
Short: New bearish fair value gap size >= minimum gap size.
Long: First midpoint touch of a bullish gap AND touch-bar close > midpoint, OR first midpoint touch of a bearish gap AND touch-bar close >= midpoint.
Short: First midpoint touch of a bullish gap AND touch-bar close <= midpoint, OR first midpoint touch of a bearish gap AND touch-bar close < midpoint.
Long: First midpoint touch AND forming chart close > midpoint AND optional trend filter, optionally restricted to a configured trading window AND a nonzero liquidity target AND absolute distance from forming chart close to target >= minimum distance.
Short: First midpoint touch AND forming chart close < midpoint AND optional trend filter, optionally restricted to a configured trading window AND a nonzero liquidity target AND absolute distance from forming chart close to target >= minimum distance.
Long: First sweep of a low liquidity level AND absolute distance between forming chart close and the level >= minimum distance AND optional trading-session filter.
Short: First sweep of a high liquidity level AND absolute distance between forming chart close and the level >= minimum distance AND optional trading-session filter.
Exit: long, forming chart close >= entry reference close + target fraction × absolute distance from entry reference close to liquidity target AND first sweep of any liquidity level; the earlier target fraction triggers a partial exit and the later, larger fraction triggers a full exit after the earlier target has been reached.
Exit: short, forming chart close <= entry reference close - target fraction × absolute distance from entry reference close to liquidity target AND first sweep of any liquidity level; the earlier target fraction triggers a partial exit and the later, larger fraction triggers a full exit after the earlier target has been reached.
Exit: long, forming chart close >= entry reference close + target fraction × absolute distance from entry reference close to liquidity target.
Exit: short, forming chart close <= entry reference close - target fraction × absolute distance from entry reference close to liquidity target.
