Calculation: Volume-weighted price = Sum of [((2 × close + high + low) / 4) × volume] over Average bars ÷ Sum of volume over the same bars
Calculation: RSV = (Volume-weighted price − its lowest value over Periods bars) ÷ max(its highest value − its lowest value over Periods bars, minimum price increment) × 100
Calculation: First smoothed RSV = linear weighted moving average of RSV over Average bars
Calculation: Second smoothed RSV = linear weighted moving average of the First smoothed RSV over the same Average bars
Long: First smoothed RSV ≥ Second smoothed RSV
Short: First smoothed RSV < Second smoothed RSV
