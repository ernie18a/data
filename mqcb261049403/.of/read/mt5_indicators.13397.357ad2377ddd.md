Calculation: Normalized deviation oscillator = 100 × smoothed deviation / smoothed absolute deviation, where deviation is the selected bar price minus its moving average; smooth the deviation and its absolute value with the same second moving-average method and parameters. Set the oscillator to zero when the smoothed deviation is zero.
Calculation: Stepped oscillator line = for each bar and step size, start with upper = oscillator + 2 × step and lower = oscillator − 2 × step. Set trend to positive if the oscillator exceeds the previous upper, negative if it is below the previous lower, and otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is positive, replace lower with the greater of lower and the previous lower; if negative, replace upper with the lesser of upper and the previous upper. The line is lower + step for positive trend and upper − step for negative trend. Initialize the previous upper to negative infinity, previous lower to positive infinity, and trend to zero. Calculate independently with a smaller fast step and a larger slow step.
Calculation: Sampled stepped oscillator lines = calculate the fast and slow lines on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, and hold those sampled values until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Fast stepped oscillator line > slow stepped oscillator line OR fast sampled stepped oscillator line > slow sampled stepped oscillator line
Short: Fast stepped oscillator line < slow stepped oscillator line OR fast sampled stepped oscillator line < slow sampled stepped oscillator line
