Calculation: Volume-weighted oscillator = For each bar, let x = high + low − 2 × EMA(close, lookback). Starting with L0 = L1 = L2 = L3 = 0, update simultaneously from their previous values: L0 = (1 − smoothing factor) × x + smoothing factor × previous L0; L1 = −smoothing factor × L0 + previous L0 + smoothing factor × previous L1; L2 = −smoothing factor × L1 + previous L1 + smoothing factor × previous L2; L3 = −smoothing factor × L2 + previous L2 + smoothing factor × previous L3. Let U be the sum of max(L0 − L1, 0), max(L1 − L2, 0), and max(L2 − L3, 0), and D the sum of max(L1 − L0, 0), max(L2 − L1, 0), and max(L3 − L2, 0). The oscillator is (100 × U / (U + D) − 50) × volume; use −50 × volume when U + D = 0. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Volume-weighted oscillator > positive threshold × volume of the oscillator's bar.
Short: Volume-weighted oscillator < negative threshold × volume of the oscillator's bar.
