Calculation: H3 = previous completed daily close + 1.1 × (previous completed daily high − previous completed daily low) / 4
Calculation: H4 = previous completed daily close + 1.1 × (previous completed daily high − previous completed daily low) / 2
Calculation: L3 = previous completed daily close − 1.1 × (previous completed daily high − previous completed daily low) / 4
Calculation: L4 = previous completed daily close − 1.1 × (previous completed daily high − previous completed daily low) / 2
Calculation: H5 = previous completed daily high / previous completed daily low × previous completed daily close
Calculation: L5 = 2 × previous completed daily close − H5
Long: close ≤ L3 OR close > H4
Short: close ≥ H3 OR close < L4
Exit: long, close ≥ H5
Exit: short, close ≤ L5
