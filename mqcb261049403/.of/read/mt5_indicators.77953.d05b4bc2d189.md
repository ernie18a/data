Calculation: Session range = the highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening timestamps, converted to GMT using the configured offset, fall within an enabled session's configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval; sessions spanning midnight belong to the day on which they start.
Calculation: Active session level = the high or low of the latest completed session of each enabled session type, available on bars outside that session until its next instance starts. Track high and low independently. If first-sweep-only is enabled, deactivate a level after its first sweep. If cancel-on-break is enabled, deactivate the high after a bar has high > session high AND close >= session high, and deactivate the low after a bar has low < session low AND close <= session low. Apply deactivation after evaluating the current bar.
Long: Closed bar low < any active session low AND close > that same session low.
Short: Closed bar high > any active session high AND close < that same session high.
