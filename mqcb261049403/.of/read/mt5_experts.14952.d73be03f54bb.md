Long: Money Flow Index on the selected indicator timeframe is above the upper threshold AND its previous bar's Money Flow Index is at or below the upper threshold.
Short: Money Flow Index on the selected indicator timeframe is below the lower threshold AND its previous bar's Money Flow Index is at or above the lower threshold.
Exit: short, Money Flow Index on the selected indicator timeframe is above the upper threshold.
Exit: long, Money Flow Index on the selected indicator timeframe is below the lower threshold.
Exit: long, low is at or below entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR high is at or above entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high is at or above entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR low is at or below entry price minus the take-profit distance.
Calculation: Selected indicator timeframe Money Flow Index = Money Flow Index using volume on a configurable timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use a configurable signal bar, including either the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
