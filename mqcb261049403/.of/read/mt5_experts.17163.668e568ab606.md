Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = EMA of the Accumulation/Distribution line over a shorter period minus EMA of the same line over a longer period, where the Accumulation/Distribution line is the cumulative sum of ((close-low)-(high-close))/(high-low)*volume
Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of close, including the currently forming bar (evaluated at its open, so its close equals its open)
Long: no open position AND last completed bar close > its open AND last completed bar low < SMA AND Chaikin Oscillator of the last completed bar < -entry threshold
Short: no open position AND last completed bar close < its open AND last completed bar high > SMA AND Chaikin Oscillator of the last completed bar > entry threshold
Exit: long, last completed bar close < its open AND last completed bar high > SMA AND Chaikin Oscillator of the last completed bar > exit threshold (exit threshold larger than entry threshold) OR price <= entry price - fixed stop distance OR price >= entry price + fixed take-profit distance (take-profit distance larger than stop distance)
Exit: short, last completed bar close > its open AND last completed bar low < SMA AND Chaikin Oscillator of the last completed bar < -exit threshold (exit threshold larger than entry threshold) OR price >= entry price + fixed stop distance OR price <= entry price - fixed take-profit distance (take-profit distance larger than stop distance)
