Calculation: SessionWindow = bars whose timestamp hour falls in a fixed daily hour range [start, end), which may wrap past midnight
Calculation: SessionHigh, SessionLow = highest high and lowest low of the bars in the most recently completed SessionWindow (fixed once the window ends, until the next window completes)
Calculation: Breach = state set on a bar outside SessionWindow (with SessionHigh/SessionLow defined and no breach active): up-breach if high > SessionHigh + buffer, otherwise down-breach if low < SessionLow - buffer; the bar that sets it is the breach bar. The state stays active on following bars until a signal fires, until bars since the breach bar reach the reversal limit without a signal, or until a new SessionWindow completes; it is not evaluated on SessionWindow bars. A new breach can only be set on a later bar than the one where a prior breach ended.
Long: current bar is outside SessionWindow AND a down-breach is active AND the current bar is after the breach bar AND close > SessionLow AND (bars since breach bar) <= reversal limit
Short: current bar is outside SessionWindow AND an up-breach is active AND the current bar is after the breach bar AND close < SessionHigh AND (bars since breach bar) <= reversal limit
