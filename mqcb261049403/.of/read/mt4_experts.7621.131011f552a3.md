Calculation: Daily Pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed daily bar, on a higher timeframe than the chart
Calculation: True Range = the greatest of current high − current low, |current high − previous close|, and |current low − previous close|
Calculation: Average True Range = average True Range over a lookback; use four distinct lookbacks ordered shortest to longest, each longer than the one-bar True Range
Long: current 3-period EMA of close > current 3-period EMA of open AND previous 3-period EMA of open <= Daily Pivot AND current 3-period EMA of open > Daily Pivot AND current Heiken Ashi close > current Heiken Ashi open AND (current True Range > previous True Range OR previous True Range > True Range two bars ago) AND at least one of the four Average True Ranges is greater on the current bar than on the previous bar
Short: current 3-period EMA of close < current 3-period EMA of open AND previous 3-period EMA of open >= Daily Pivot AND current 3-period EMA of open < Daily Pivot AND current Heiken Ashi close < current Heiken Ashi open AND (current True Range > previous True Range OR previous True Range > True Range two bars ago) AND at least one of the four Average True Ranges is greater on the current bar than on the previous bar
Exit: long, previous 3-period EMA of open >= Daily Pivot AND current 3-period EMA of open < Daily Pivot AND current Heiken Ashi close < current Heiken Ashi open AND (current True Range > previous True Range OR previous True Range > True Range two bars ago) AND at least one of the four Average True Ranges is greater on the current bar than on the previous bar AND current 3-period EMA of close < current 3-period EMA of open
Exit: short, previous 3-period EMA of open <= Daily Pivot AND current 3-period EMA of open > Daily Pivot AND current Heiken Ashi close > current Heiken Ashi open AND (current True Range > previous True Range OR previous True Range > True Range two bars ago) AND at least one of the four Average True Ranges is greater on the current bar than on the previous bar AND current 3-period EMA of close > current 3-period EMA of open
