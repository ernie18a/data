Calculation: Pivot = (previous completed pivot-timeframe high + low + close) / 3; use the pivot associated with the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar. The pivot timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Indicator bars = the still-forming bar and preceding completed bars of the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: Previous open EMA < current pivot AND current open EMA > current pivot AND current close EMA > current open EMA AND current Heiken Ashi close > current Heiken Ashi open AND (current true range > previous true range OR previous true range > true range two bars ago) AND at least one of four ATRs with different, successively longer lookbacks is greater than its previous value.
Short: Previous open EMA > current pivot AND current open EMA < current pivot AND current close EMA < current open EMA AND current Heiken Ashi close < current Heiken Ashi open AND (current true range > previous true range OR previous true range > true range two bars ago) AND at least one of four ATRs with different, successively longer lookbacks is greater than its previous value.
Exit: long, previous open EMA >= current pivot AND current open EMA < current pivot AND current close EMA < current open EMA AND current Heiken Ashi close < current Heiken Ashi open AND (current true range > previous true range OR previous true range > true range two bars ago) AND at least one of four ATRs with different, successively longer lookbacks is greater than its previous value.
Exit: short, previous open EMA <= current pivot AND current open EMA > current pivot AND current close EMA > current open EMA AND current Heiken Ashi close > current Heiken Ashi open AND (current true range > previous true range OR previous true range > true range two bars ago) AND at least one of four ATRs with different, successively longer lookbacks is greater than its previous value.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
