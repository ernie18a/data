Calculation: Selected average = a simple, exponential, smoothed, linear-weighted, sine-weighted, Hull or T3 moving average, linear regression value, or rolling quantile of a selected price series; the available price series are open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, and (high + low + 2 × close)/4. The triangular average option uses the linear-weighted moving average. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: Multicolor mode is enabled AND the selected average is above its preceding value, or equal to it after its most recent unequal change was upward.
Short: Multicolor mode is enabled AND the selected average is below its preceding value, or equal to it after its most recent unequal change was downward.
