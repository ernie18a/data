Calculation: Direction histogram = one selected oscillator: stochastic main line minus 50; close-based CCI; close-based RSI minus 50; close-based MACD main line using a shorter and a longer EMA; close-based momentum minus 100; Williams %R plus 50; ADX main line; or DeMarker minus 0.5. Calculate it on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: Direction histogram > 0 AND Bollinger standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of close >= Keltner ATR multiplier × ATR, calculated on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of the same higher timeframe as the direction histogram.
Short: Direction histogram < 0 AND Bollinger standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of close >= Keltner ATR multiplier × ATR, calculated on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of the same higher timeframe as the direction histogram.
