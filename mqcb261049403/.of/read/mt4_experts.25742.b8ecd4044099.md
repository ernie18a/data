Long: absolute difference between the current bar’s opening price and the previous bar’s close exceeds a threshold AND the previous bar’s low is below the fast LWMA of typical price AND the previous bar’s close is above that fast LWMA AND the fast LWMA is above the slower LWMA AND the previous bar’s low is below the previous bar’s high AND the RSI of close on the second-last completed chart bar is below 50 AND the RSI of close on the last completed chart bar is at least 50
Short: absolute difference between the current bar’s opening price and the previous bar’s close exceeds a threshold AND the previous bar’s high is above the fast LWMA of typical price AND the previous bar’s close is below that fast LWMA AND the fast LWMA is above the slower LWMA AND the previous bar’s low is below the high two bars ago AND the RSI of close on the second-last completed chart bar is above 50 AND the RSI of close on the last completed chart bar is at most 50
Exit: Long, price <= stop loss OR price >= take profit
Exit: Short, price >= stop loss OR price <= take profit
