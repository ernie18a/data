Calculation: Input price = a selected bar price: open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, high when close > open and low when close < open and close otherwise, (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 when close < open and close otherwise, or Demark price defined by r = (high + low + close + (low when close < open, high when close > open, otherwise close))/2 and price = r - (high + low)/2.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = smooth 100 × clamp(smooth(input price change) / smooth(abs(input price change)), -1, 1) with a separately selected smoothing method and period; the numerator and denominator use the same method and parameters. Smoothing methods are SMA, EMA, SMMA, linear weighted MA, Jurik moving average, parabolic regression moving average, Tillson T3, VIDYA, Kaufman adaptive moving average, or the following ultralinear average: let a = 3/(period + 2), b = 1 - a; recursively update f1 = b × f1 + a × input, f2 = a × f1 + b × f2, f3 = b × f3 + a × (1.5 × f1 - 0.5 × f2), f4 = a × f3 + b × f4, f5 = b × f5 + a × (1.5 × f3 - 0.5 × f4), f6 = a × f5 + b × f6; output 1.5 × f5 - 0.5 × f6, initializing all six states to the first input. The oscillator is undefined when the smoothed absolute change is zero.
Calculation: Oscillator change = smoothed oscillator minus its previous bar value; threshold = a multiplier times the population standard deviation of oscillator changes over a lookback including the current change. Round the change and threshold separately to the selected price precision plus two decimal places before comparing them with each other.
Calculation: Calculate the oscillator either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using the higher-timeframe bar still forming. For the higher-timeframe version, the two smoothing periods and their auxiliary parameters are equal, although their smoothing methods may differ.
Long: Smoothed oscillator > previous bar's smoothed oscillator.
Short: Smoothed oscillator < previous bar's smoothed oscillator.
Long: Rounded oscillator change > rounded threshold.
Short: Rounded oscillator change < negative rounded threshold.
