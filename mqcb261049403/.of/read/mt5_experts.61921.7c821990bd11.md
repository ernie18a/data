Calculation: Force index = simple moving average of (close minus previous close) multiplied by volume; its lookback equals the Bollinger Bands lookback.
Calculation: Signed oscillator pattern = for current value x, previous value p and positive threshold L, one of: x>L AND p>L AND x>p; x>L AND p<L; x>L AND p>L AND x<p; x<L AND p>L; x<-L AND p<-L AND x<p; x<-L AND p>-L; x<-L AND p<-L AND x>p; x>-L AND p<-L. Use a separately selectable pattern for each decision.
Calculation: MACD pattern = for main line m, signal line s, their preceding values mp and sp, and positive threshold L, one of: m>L AND mp>L AND m>s AND m>mp AND s>sp; m>L AND mp>L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s>sp; m>L AND mp>L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s<sp; m<L AND mp>L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s>sp; m<L AND mp>L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s<sp; m>L AND mp<L AND m>s AND m>mp AND s>sp; m<-L AND mp<-L AND m>s AND m>mp AND s>sp; m<-L AND mp<-L AND m>s AND m>mp AND s<sp; m<-L AND mp<-L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s<sp; m>-L AND mp<-L AND m>s AND m>mp AND s>sp; m<-L AND mp>-L AND m<s AND m<mp AND s<sp. Use a separately selectable pattern for each decision.
Calculation: Stochastic pattern = for stochastic main line k, signal line d, their preceding values kp and dp, and upper threshold U with lower threshold 100-U, one of: k>U AND kp>U; k<U AND kp>U; k>U AND kp<U; k<100-U AND kp<100-U; k>100-U AND kp<100-U; k<100-U AND kp>100-U. For the first and fourth alternatives, additionally select one of all eight combinations of k>d or k<d, k>kp or k<kp, and d>dp or d<dp. For the second and sixth alternatives, additionally select one of: k>d AND k<kp AND d>dp; k<d AND k<kp AND d>dp; k<d AND k<kp AND d<dp. For the third and fifth alternatives, additionally select one of: k>d AND k>kp AND d>dp; k>d AND k>kp AND d<dp; k<d AND k>kp AND d<dp. Use a separately selectable pattern for each decision.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, the accelerator oscillator or awesome oscillator on the forming bar and the last completed bar satisfies the signed oscillator pattern.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, the negatives of the accelerator oscillator or awesome oscillator on the forming bar and the last completed bar satisfy the signed oscillator pattern.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, forming-bar ADX exceeds its threshold AND ADX differs from its last completed value AND either +DI>-DI AND +DI exceeds its threshold, or -DI>+DI AND -DI exceeds its threshold; select the DI direction and whether ADX rises or falls.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, forming-bar ADX exceeds its threshold AND ADX differs from its last completed value AND either +DI>-DI AND +DI exceeds its threshold, or -DI>+DI AND -DI exceeds its threshold; use the opposite DI direction to the corresponding long decision and the same selected ADX slope.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, DeMarker or money flow index on the forming bar is strictly across either an upper threshold or its complementary lower threshold from its value on the last completed bar; select the threshold and crossing direction.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, DeMarker or money flow index on the forming bar crosses strictly in the opposite direction across the complementary threshold from its value on the last completed bar.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, the last completed bar's high is below its lower Bollinger Band OR its low is above its upper Bollinger Band, with the selected band direction AND an optional band-width threshold filter AND an optional signed oscillator pattern on the force index of the last two completed bars.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, the last completed bar's low is above its upper Bollinger Band OR its high is below its lower Bollinger Band, with the opposite band direction to the corresponding long decision AND the same optional band-width filter AND an optional signed oscillator pattern on the negatives of the force index of the last two completed bars.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, the forming-bar MACD and its preceding values satisfy a selected MACD pattern AND the forming-bar stochastic and its preceding values satisfy a selected stochastic pattern; either indicator condition may be disabled.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, the negatives of the forming-bar MACD main and signal lines and their preceding values satisfy the selected MACD pattern AND the forming-bar stochastic main and signal lines and their preceding values, each subtracted from 100, satisfy the selected stochastic pattern; either indicator condition may be disabled.
Exit: long, an opposite entry decision occurs.
Exit: short, an opposite entry decision occurs.
Exit: long, the relevant strategy's accelerator oscillator or awesome oscillator satisfies a separately selected signed oscillator pattern using the forming and last completed bars.
Exit: short, the negatives of the relevant strategy's accelerator oscillator or awesome oscillator satisfy a separately selected signed oscillator pattern using the forming and last completed bars.
Exit: long, forming-bar ADX exceeds its exit threshold AND either +DI>-DI AND ADX minus +DI exceeds a difference threshold, or -DI>+DI AND ADX minus -DI exceeds it, or +DI>-DI AND +DI minus ADX exceeds it, or -DI>+DI AND -DI minus ADX exceeds it; select one alternative.
Exit: short, forming-bar ADX exceeds its exit threshold AND the corresponding long exit alternative holds with +DI and -DI exchanged.
Exit: long, DeMarker or money flow index on the forming bar crosses strictly over or under either an upper exit threshold or its complementary lower threshold from its last completed value.
Exit: short, DeMarker or money flow index on the forming bar crosses strictly in the opposite direction across the complementary exit threshold from its last completed value.
Exit: long, the last completed bar's high is below its lower Bollinger Band OR its low is above its upper Bollinger Band, with the selected band direction AND the selected nonzero band-width threshold filter, OR the force index of the last two completed bars satisfies a separately selected signed oscillator pattern.
Exit: short, the last completed bar's low is above its upper Bollinger Band OR its high is below its lower Bollinger Band, with the opposite band direction AND the selected nonzero band-width threshold filter, OR the negatives of the force index of the last two completed bars satisfy a separately selected signed oscillator pattern.
Exit: long, the forming-bar MACD and its preceding values satisfy a separately selected MACD pattern OR the forming-bar stochastic and its preceding values satisfy a separately selected stochastic pattern.
Exit: short, the negatives of the forming-bar MACD main and signal lines and their preceding values satisfy the selected exit MACD pattern OR the forming-bar stochastic main and signal lines and their preceding values, each subtracted from 100, satisfy the selected exit stochastic pattern.
Exit: long, price reaches entry price plus the strategy's take-profit distance OR price reaches entry price minus its stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, price reaches entry price minus the strategy's take-profit distance OR price reaches entry price plus its stop-loss distance.
