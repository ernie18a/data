Calculation: Channel upper = the high of the higher-timeframe bar whose offset equals the offset of the highest high among the preceding chart bars in the channel lookback; offset zero is the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Channel lower = the low of the higher-timeframe bar whose offset equals the offset of the lowest low among the preceding chart bars in the channel lookback; offset zero is the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Channel middle = (channel upper + channel lower) / 2.
Long: (channel lower >= the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low AND channel lower < its open OR channel middle <= its low AND channel middle > its open) AND short-term linear-weighted moving average of typical price > long-term linear-weighted moving average of typical price AND the second-last completed chart bar's low < the last completed chart bar's high AND absolute difference between the current chart bar's open and the second-last completed chart bar's close > a price-distance threshold.
Short: (channel upper <= the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high AND channel upper > its open OR channel middle >= its high AND channel middle > its open) AND short-term linear-weighted moving average of typical price < long-term linear-weighted moving average of typical price AND the last completed chart bar's low < the second-last completed chart bar's high AND absolute difference between the current chart bar's open and the second-last completed chart bar's close > a price-distance threshold.
