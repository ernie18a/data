Calculation: Ultra-linear smoothing of series x at length L = apply three successive stages; each stage updates a = (1-k)*a + k*x and then b = k*a + (1-k)*b, and outputs 1.5*a - 0.5*b as the next stage's input, with k = 3/(L+2) and each stage's a and b initialized to its first input.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = Jurik moving average of the ratio of ultra-linear-smoothed price change to ultra-linear-smoothed absolute price change, using the same oscillator length for both smoothing operations; clamp the ratio to [-1, 1] before the Jurik moving average and leave it undefined when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Fast composite = weighted sum of five smoothed oscillators with progressively longer oscillator lengths and shared price source, Jurik moving-average length and phase; the second and third oscillators use the same weight, while the other oscillators have separate weights.
Calculation: Slow composite = recursively smoothed fast composite: current fast composite divided by smoothing length plus previous slow composite multiplied by (smoothing length minus one) divided by smoothing length; initialize the slow composite to the first fast composite divided by smoothing length.
Long: Fast composite > slow composite on the chart bar OR fast composite > slow composite on the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, evaluated separately for each configured timeframe.
Short: Fast composite < slow composite on the chart bar OR fast composite < slow composite on the last completed bar of a higher or lower timeframe, evaluated separately for each configured timeframe.
