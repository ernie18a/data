Calculation: Step oscillator = On the selected higher timeframe, use the still-forming bar in real time; historically, the final value of each higher-timeframe bar is assigned from its first chart-timeframe bar onward. The chart timeframe may also be selected. Compute ATR and its running maximum and minimum from the first processed bar. Set three step sizes to scale A times the running minimum ATR, scale A times the running maximum ATR, and scale A times scale B times the mean of the running minimum and maximum ATR. For each step size, maintain upper and lower bands and a trend. Initialize both bands to the first processed close; initialize the minimum-ATR trend down and the other two trends up. On each bar, set the trend up if close exceeds the previous upper band, down if close is below the previous lower band, and otherwise retain it. Set provisional bands to close plus and minus twice the current step size. In an uptrend, replace the lower band with the maximum of its provisional and previous values; in a downtrend, replace the upper band with the minimum of its provisional and previous values. The corresponding line is lower band plus step size in an uptrend, or upper band minus step size in a downtrend. Let the lower and upper normalization bounds be the maximum-ATR line minus and plus its step size. The fast oscillator is the minimum-ATR line's position between those bounds; the slow oscillator is the middle-step line's position between those bounds. Multiply both positions by 100.
Long: Fast oscillator > slow oscillator
Short: Fast oscillator < slow oscillator
