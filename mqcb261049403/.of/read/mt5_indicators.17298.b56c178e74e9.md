Calculation: Selected price = a chosen open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint price, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC; alternatively, use trend-biased price: (high + close) / 2 if close > open, otherwise (low + close) / 2, or extreme trend-biased price: high if close > open, low if close < open, otherwise close.
Calculation: Normalized MACD = (shorter-period EMA of selected price − longer-period EMA of selected price) / population standard deviation of selected price over a lookback equal to the sum of the two EMA periods; if the standard deviation is zero, use the undivided EMA difference. Seed each EMA with its first input and use all available bars when fewer than the standard-deviation lookback are available. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Signal = EMA of normalized MACD, seeded with its first input and calculated on the same timeframe as normalized MACD.
Long: Normalized MACD > signal OR normalized MACD = signal AND the most recent unequal normalized MACD was > its signal.
Short: Normalized MACD < signal OR normalized MACD = signal AND the most recent unequal normalized MACD was < its signal.
