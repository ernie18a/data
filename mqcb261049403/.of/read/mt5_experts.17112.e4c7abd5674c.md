Calculation: Heiken Ashi direction = bullish when Heiken Ashi close > Heiken Ashi open, bearish when close < open; Heiken Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4, and Heiken Ashi open = (previous Heiken Ashi open + previous Heiken Ashi close) / 2. Use the current, still-forming bar and its previous bar on the configured calculation timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = a shorter-lookback simple moving average of typical price and a longer-lookback smoothed moving average of median price, respectively. Use the current, still-forming bars on the configured calculation timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: StepUpDown direction = over a short lookback of the fast moving average, find the indices of its highest and lowest values (index 0 is the current bar). If the highest-value index is greater than the lowest-value index, set direction up when any scanned fast-MA value is above the lookback minimum, and set direction down when the current fast-minus-slow spread is less than the previous bar's spread. If the highest-value index is less than the lowest-value index, set direction down when any scanned fast-MA value is below the lookback maximum, and set direction up when the current spread is greater than the previous bar's spread. When multiple conditions apply, process the down/up spread condition after the fast-MA condition; the last condition that applies determines direction. Use the current, still-forming bars and preceding bars on the configured calculation timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Heiken Ashi direction is bullish AND StepUpDown direction is up AND no long position is open.
Short: Heiken Ashi direction is bearish AND StepUpDown direction is down AND no short position is open.
Exit: Long, Heiken Ashi direction is bearish AND StepUpDown direction is down AND close-by-opposite-signal is enabled OR position profit is positive AND StepUpDown direction is down while trailing stops are disabled.
Exit: Short, Heiken Ashi direction is bullish AND StepUpDown direction is up AND close-by-opposite-signal is enabled OR position profit is positive AND StepUpDown direction is up while trailing stops are disabled.
