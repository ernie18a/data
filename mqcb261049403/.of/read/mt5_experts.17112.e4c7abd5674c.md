Calculation: Step direction = On the chart timeframe, including the forming bar, calculate a shorter-period SMA of typical price and a longer-period SMMA of median price. Index bars backward from the forming bar at index 0. Initialize the maximum and minimum to the SMA at index 1 and both extrema indices to 0. Scan a lookback from oldest to newest through index 0, updating each extreme and its index only on a strict inequality. Let the current and previous gaps be the SMA minus the SMMA at indices 0 and 1. Initialize direction to 0, then scan the lookback from oldest to newest; on the first evaluation of a new chart bar, scan a longer history instead. At each scanned index, apply these assignments in order: if the maximum index is greater than the minimum index and the scanned SMA is greater than the minimum, set direction to 1; if the maximum index is greater than the minimum index and the current gap is less than the previous gap, set direction to -1; if the maximum index is less than the minimum index and the scanned SMA is less than the maximum, set direction to -1; if the maximum index is less than the minimum index and the current gap is greater than the previous gap, set direction to 1.
Calculation: Candle difference = On the chart timeframe, copy a history ending with the forming bar in oldest-to-newest order. On the first evaluation of a new chart bar, use a longer history; otherwise use the two most recent bars. Subtract the average of the oldest copied bar's open and close from the average of the next copied bar's open, high, low, and close.
Long: Step direction = 1 AND candle difference > 0
Short: Step direction = -1 AND candle difference < 0
Exit: long, opposite-signal closing enabled AND step direction = -1 AND candle difference < 0
Exit: short, opposite-signal closing enabled AND step direction = 1 AND candle difference > 0
