Calculation: Signal price = opening price of the new chart bar, used as the available bar-data proxy for the contemporaneous bid.
Calculation: Initial long stop = most recent nonzero ZigZag low on a completed chart bar at entry, minus a price offset; initial short stop = most recent nonzero ZigZag high on a completed chart bar at entry, plus a price offset.
Calculation: First-position target = entry price plus, for a long, or minus, for a short, a fraction of the distance between entry price and initial stop, rounded down to whole price points; a zero distance disables the target. The second position has no target.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, subsequently moved only in the profitable direction. For a long, when price exceeds the active stop by more than trailing distance plus trailing step, move the stop to price minus trailing distance; for a short, when price falls below the active stop by more than trailing distance plus trailing step, move it to price plus trailing distance. If the optional profit-lock rule is enabled, when long price reaches entry plus profit-lock trigger plus profit-lock offset, raise the stop to at least entry plus profit-lock offset; when short price reaches entry minus profit-lock trigger minus profit-lock offset, lower the stop to at most entry minus profit-lock offset. Update the trailing stop before applying the profit-lock rule.
Long: At the start of a chart bar, signal price >= moving average on the still-forming longer higher-timeframe bar AND signal price >= moving average on the still-forming shorter higher-timeframe bar AND last completed chart-bar close >= lower Bollinger Band of that same bar AND preceding chart-bar close <= that same lower Bollinger Band AND signal price >= that same lower Bollinger Band.
Short: At the start of a chart bar, signal price <= moving average on the still-forming longer higher-timeframe bar AND signal price <= moving average on the still-forming shorter higher-timeframe bar AND last completed chart-bar close <= upper Bollinger Band of that same bar AND preceding chart-bar close >= that same upper Bollinger Band AND signal price <= that same upper Bollinger Band.
Exit: long, first position AND short-entry condition.
Exit: short, first position AND long-entry condition.
Exit: long, price <= active stop OR first position AND price >= first-position target when the target is enabled.
Exit: short, price >= active stop OR first position AND price <= first-position target when the target is enabled.
