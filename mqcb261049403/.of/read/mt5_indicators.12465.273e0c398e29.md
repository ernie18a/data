Calculation: Selected-bar data = for each selected indicator, use the chart bar if its timeframe is the chart timeframe; otherwise use the higher- or lower-timeframe bar containing the chart bar's opening time. The selected bar is still forming at that time; historical calculations use its final values. Previous values refer to the preceding bar of that indicator's timeframe.
Long: For each selected indicator, either moving average is selected AND low < moving average AND close > moving average; OR MACD, stochastic or RVI is selected AND its main line > its signal line AND its previous main line < its previous signal line; OR OsMA, RSI or TRIX is selected AND its value > its simple moving average AND its previous value < its previous simple moving average; OR CCI is selected AND CCI > any of the negative threshold, zero or positive threshold AND previous CCI <= the same level; OR ADX is selected AND ADX < its threshold AND +DI > -DI AND previous +DI < previous -DI; OR Bollinger Bands is selected AND (low < lower band AND close > lower band OR low < middle band AND close > middle band) AND NOT (high > upper band AND close < upper band OR high > middle band AND close < middle band).
Short: For each selected indicator, either moving average is selected AND high > moving average AND close < moving average; OR MACD, stochastic or RVI is selected AND its main line < its signal line AND its previous main line > its previous signal line; OR OsMA, RSI or TRIX is selected AND its value < its simple moving average AND its previous value > its previous simple moving average; OR CCI is selected AND CCI < any of the negative threshold, zero or positive threshold AND previous CCI >= the same level; OR ADX is selected AND ADX < its threshold AND +DI < -DI AND previous +DI > previous -DI; OR Bollinger Bands is selected AND (high > upper band AND close < upper band OR high > middle band AND close < middle band).
