Calculation: Shifted EMA = EMA of chart-timeframe close, shifted forward by a common number of bars; evaluate the current-bar value at the bar open.
Calculation: Boolean comparison value = 1 if the comparison is true, otherwise 0.
Long: No open position AND previous-bar short-period shifted EMA < previous-bar medium-period shifted EMA AND previous-bar medium-period shifted EMA < previous-bar long-period shifted EMA AND current-bar medium-period shifted EMA < current-bar short-period shifted EMA AND current-bar short-period shifted EMA < current-bar long-period shifted EMA.
Short: No open position AND previous-bar short-period shifted EMA > previous-bar medium-period shifted EMA AND previous-bar medium-period shifted EMA > previous-bar long-period shifted EMA AND current-bar medium-period shifted EMA > current-bar short-period shifted EMA AND current-bar short-period shifted EMA > current-bar long-period shifted EMA.
Exit: Long OR short, Boolean comparison value of (previous-bar short-period shifted EMA > previous-bar medium-period shifted EMA) > previous-bar long-period shifted EMA AND Boolean comparison value of (current-bar long-period shifted EMA < current-bar short-period shifted EMA) < current-bar medium-period shifted EMA.
