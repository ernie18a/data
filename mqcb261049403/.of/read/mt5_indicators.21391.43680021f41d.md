Calculation: Upper channel = DEMA of high plus a price offset; lower channel = DEMA of low minus the same price offset, using the same lookback.
Long: Chart close > upper channel from a specified number of chart bars ago AND chart close >= lower channel from the same number of chart bars ago.
Short: Chart close < lower channel from a specified number of chart bars ago.
Long: Forming higher-timeframe close > upper channel calculated on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND forming higher-timeframe close >= lower channel calculated on that bar.
Short: Forming higher-timeframe close < lower channel calculated on the forming higher-timeframe bar.
