Calculation: True Strength Index (TSI) = 100 × EMA(EMA(close − prior-bar close, longer lookback), shorter lookback) ÷ EMA(EMA(|close − prior-bar close|, longer lookback), shorter lookback)
Long: TSI > prior-bar TSI AND prior-bar TSI ≤ two-bars-prior TSI OR TSI > 0 AND prior-bar TSI ≤ 0
Short: TSI < prior-bar TSI AND prior-bar TSI ≥ two-bars-prior TSI OR TSI < 0 AND prior-bar TSI ≥ 0
