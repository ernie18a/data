Long: On the indicator timeframe, the Jurik moving average of close crosses above the Jurik moving average of open, with both using the same length and phase; use the last completed bar or, if configured, the forming bar. The indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: On the same indicator timeframe and selected bar, the Jurik moving average of close crosses below the Jurik moving average of open, with both using the same length and phase.
Exit: long, the most recent crossover of the Jurik moving average of close and the Jurik moving average of open on the selected indicator bar or earlier bars is downward OR low <= entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the most recent crossover of the Jurik moving average of close and the Jurik moving average of open on the selected indicator bar or earlier bars is upward OR high >= entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the take-profit distance.
