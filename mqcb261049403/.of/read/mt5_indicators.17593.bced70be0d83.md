Calculation: NRTR trend = Start with trend 0 and price and trail equal to the close of the first processed bar. On each bar, let range factor equal the mean high-minus-low range over a lookback including the current bar, multiplied by a configurable scale. If the prior trend is nonnegative, set price to the greater of prior price and current close and trail to the greater of prior trail and price × (1 − range factor); if close < trail, set price to close, trail to close × (1 + range factor), and trend to −1. Otherwise retain the prior trend. If the prior trend is negative, set price to the lesser of prior price and current close and trail to the lesser of prior trail and price × (1 + range factor); if close > trail, set price to close, trail to close × (1 − range factor), and trend to +1. Otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: Trend extreme = For each positive-trend run, track its highest high; for each negative-trend run, track its lowest low. A new extreme replaces the previous marked extreme of that run. Calculate separately on the chart timeframe and on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; place a higher-timeframe extreme on the chart bar where that high or low occurred.
Long: Chart-timeframe NRTR trend > 0 AND (previous trend ≤ 0 OR current high > previous highest high in the positive-trend run)
Short: Chart-timeframe NRTR trend < 0 AND (previous trend ≥ 0 OR current low < previous lowest low in the negative-trend run)
Long: Still-forming higher-timeframe NRTR trend > 0 AND (previous higher-timeframe trend ≤ 0 OR current higher-timeframe high > previous highest high in the positive-trend run)
Short: Still-forming higher-timeframe NRTR trend < 0 AND (previous higher-timeframe trend ≥ 0 OR current higher-timeframe low < previous lowest low in the negative-trend run)
