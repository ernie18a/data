Calculation: Fast and slow fractal pivots = a pivot high whose high is at least the highs of the preceding lookback bars and greater than the following lookback bars, or a pivot low whose low is at most the preceding lows and less than the following lows; the slow lookback is longer than the fast lookback. Confirm pivots only after the following bars have completed.
Calculation: ATR bands around fast pivots = bands based on the pivot bar’s ATR and a configurable width: around a high, the upper boundary is above the pivot and the lower boundary is below it; around a low, the lower boundary is below the pivot and the upper boundary is above it. A pivot also marked by a slow pivot is strong; otherwise it is weak. Discard a weak band when price first breaks it. For a strong band, a first break switches its support/resistance side and clears its prior test count; discard it on a second break before a later qualifying retest. Count retests only when at least ten bars separate them. Merge overlapping surviving bands.
Calculation: Support and resistance zones = classify each surviving band as support when it is below the latest reference close, or resistance when it is above; if it straddles that close, use the first older close outside the band to determine the side (below means resistance, above means support).
Long: Current close >= lower boundary and current close < upper boundary of any support zone
Short: Current close >= lower boundary and current close < upper boundary of any resistance zone
