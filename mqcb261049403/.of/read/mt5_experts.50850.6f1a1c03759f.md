Calculation: Sell TP level = midpoint of the last completed bar of the configured calculation timeframe (if different from the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower) minus 0.618 × (high − low)
Long: previous completed chart bar low < Sell TP level AND close > Sell TP level
Short: previous completed chart bar low > Sell TP level AND close < Sell TP level
Exit: Long, low <= signal bar close − fixed point distance OR high >= signal bar close + fixed point distance
Exit: Short, high >= signal bar close + fixed point distance OR low <= signal bar close − fixed point distance
