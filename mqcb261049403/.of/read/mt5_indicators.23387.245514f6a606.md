Calculation: Trend state and trailing lines = Process chart bars chronologically after the moving averages, optional RSI, and ATR are available. Initialize trend, pending-up level, pending-down level, last accepted low, last accepted high, and both trailing lines to zero. On each bar, start both current trailing lines at zero. An upward crossover requires the shorter-period close moving average to be strictly below the longer-period close moving average on the previous bar and strictly above it on the current bar, with RSI filtering disabled OR current close RSI above its midpoint. A downward crossover uses the opposite strict comparisons and RSI below its midpoint. On an upward crossover, if the confirmation distance is zero, attempt an upward update; otherwise set the pending-up level to the current low and clear the pending-down level. On a downward crossover, if the confirmation distance is zero, attempt a downward update; otherwise set the pending-down level to the current high and clear the pending-up level. Then, if the pending-up level is nonzero AND current high minus that level is at least the confirmation distance AND close is at most high, attempt an upward update and clear the pending-up level. Then, if the pending-down level is nonzero AND that level minus current low is at least the confirmation distance AND open is at least close, attempt a downward update and clear the pending-down level. An upward update is accepted if low is at least last accepted low plus the flat distance OR low is at most last accepted low minus the flat distance; on acceptance, record a long reversal if trend was nonpositive, set last accepted low to low, and set trend positive. A downward update is accepted if high is at least last accepted high plus the flat distance OR high is at most last accepted high minus the flat distance; on acceptance, record a short reversal if trend was nonnegative, set last accepted high to high, and set trend negative. After these updates, if trend is positive, set the upward line to low minus an ATR multiple on a long reversal and copy the previous downward line; otherwise set the upward line to the maximum of low minus that ATR multiple and the previous upward line. If trend is negative, set the downward line to high plus the ATR multiple on a short reversal and copy the previous upward line; otherwise set the downward line to the minimum of high plus that ATR multiple and the previous downward line. Confirmation and flat distances are price-unit parameters; the moving averages share a method and shift.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = Apply the trend-state calculation to a higher timeframe using its bar still forming. At the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, set direction long if the upward line is nonzero, then set it short if the downward line is nonzero; if both lines are zero, retain the previous direction. Retain that direction on subsequent chart bars until the next higher-timeframe bar begins.
Long: Long reversal recorded
Long: Chart-timeframe upward trailing line is nonzero
Long: Higher-timeframe direction is long
Short: Short reversal recorded
Short: Chart-timeframe downward trailing line is nonzero
Short: Higher-timeframe direction is short
