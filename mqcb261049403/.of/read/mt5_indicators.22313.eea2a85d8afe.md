Calculation: Skyscraper trend = On each bar, calculate ATR, then take its highest and lowest values over a separate lookback ending at that bar. Set step to the sensitivity factor times their average, rounded down to a configurable price increment. Set upper candidate to low + 2 × step and lower candidate to high − 2 × step, or use close in place of both high and low. Initialize the previous upper and lower levels to the first eligible bar's close and the previous trend to neutral. Set the trend to bullish if close > previous upper level; then set it to bearish if close < previous lower level; otherwise retain the previous trend. In a bullish trend, set the lower level to the maximum of its candidate and previous lower level; otherwise set the upper level to the minimum of its candidate and previous upper level. Set the other level to its candidate. Treat neutral as bearish for marking purposes.
Long: Skyscraper trend is bullish AND previous bar's Skyscraper trend is bearish or neutral.
Short: Skyscraper trend is bearish AND previous bar's Skyscraper trend is bullish.
Long: Skyscraper trend is bullish on a configurable higher or lower timeframe, using a configurable bar offset from either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar.
Short: Skyscraper trend is bearish or neutral on a configurable higher or lower timeframe, using a configurable bar offset from either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar.
