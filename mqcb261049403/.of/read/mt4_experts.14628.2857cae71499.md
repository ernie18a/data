Calculation: RSI deviation bands = For each timeframe, calculate the mean of a lookback of RSI values. The upper deviation is the square root of the mean squared distance from that mean among RSI values at or above the mean; the lower deviation uses values below the mean. If either group is empty, use the population standard deviation of all values for that group's deviation. If the lookback contains fewer than three values, use its first RSI value as the mean and a fraction of that value as both deviations. The inner and outer lower bands are the mean minus their respective deviation multipliers times the lower deviation, floored at a lower bound; the inner and outer upper bands are the mean plus their respective multipliers times the upper deviation, capped at an upper bound. The outer multiplier is larger than the inner multiplier. Include the RSI being tested in the lookback.
