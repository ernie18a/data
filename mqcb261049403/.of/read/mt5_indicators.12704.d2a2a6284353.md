Calculation: Cycle period = On the higher timeframe, use the still-forming bar. Let price be (high + low) / 2 and smoothed price be the weighted average of the current and preceding three prices with weights 1, 2, 2, 1. For the first six bars, cycle = (price - 2 × prior price + price two bars ago) / 4; thereafter cycle = (1 - alpha / 2)² × (smoothed price - 2 × prior smoothed price + smoothed price two bars ago) + 2 × (1 - alpha) × prior cycle - (1 - alpha)² × cycle two bars ago. Let Q = (0.0962 × cycle + 0.5769 × cycle two bars ago - 0.5769 × cycle four bars ago - 0.0962 × cycle six bars ago) × (0.5 + 0.08 × prior instantaneous period), and I = cycle three bars ago. When current and prior Q are nonzero, phase change = (I / Q - prior I / prior Q) / (1 + I × prior I / (Q × prior Q)); otherwise retain the phase-change value from five bars ago. Clamp phase change to [0.1, 1.1]. Let median phase change be the median of the latest five phase changes, and dominant cycle = 6.28318 / median phase change + 0.5, or 15 if the median is zero. Instantaneous period = 0.67 × prior instantaneous period + 0.33 × dominant cycle. Cycle period = 0.85 × prior cycle period + 0.15 × instantaneous period. Initialize both periods to 1 and historical arrays to zero.
Calculation: Adaptive relative vigor = On the higher timeframe, use the still-forming bar. Set lookback to the floor of (4 × current cycle period + 3 × prior cycle period + 2 × cycle period two bars ago + cycle period three bars ago) / 20, capped at the number of preceding available bars. For each bar, smooth close - open and high - low separately using weights 1, 2, 2, 1 on the current and preceding three bars. Adaptive relative vigor is the sum of smoothed close - open over the lookback divided by the sum of smoothed high - low over the same lookback; it is undefined when the denominator is zero.
Long: Adaptive relative vigor > its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Adaptive relative vigor < its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
