Calculation: Lower consolidation bounds = On the lower timeframe, scan candidate bars from current to oldest where the current bar is still forming; for each candidate, take the highest high and lowest low over a fixed lookback beginning at that candidate. Keep the first pair whose width is at most the configured consolidation width and whose bounds are both below the chart price by at least that width. If none qualifies, center bounds of that width around the chart price rounded down to a whole price increment, offset below it.
Calculation: Upper consolidation bounds = On the lower timeframe, scan candidate bars from current to oldest where the current bar is still forming; for each candidate, take the highest high and lowest low over a fixed lookback beginning at that candidate. Keep the first pair whose width is at most the configured consolidation width and whose bounds are both above the chart price by at least that width. If none qualifies, center bounds of that width around the chart price rounded down to a whole price increment, offset above it.
Long: (chart close − entry buffer > lower consolidation upper bound AND lower-timeframe forming close − entry buffer > forming open AND previous completed lower-timeframe low < lower consolidation upper bound) OR (chart close − entry buffer > upper consolidation upper bound AND lower-timeframe forming close − entry buffer > forming open AND previous completed lower-timeframe low < upper consolidation upper bound)
Short: (chart close + entry buffer < lower consolidation lower bound AND lower-timeframe forming close + entry buffer < forming open AND previous completed lower-timeframe high > lower consolidation lower bound) OR (chart close + entry buffer < upper consolidation lower bound AND lower-timeframe forming close + entry buffer < forming open AND previous completed lower-timeframe high > upper consolidation lower bound)
Exit: long, chart low ≤ active stop OR chart high ≥ active target
Exit: short, chart high ≥ active stop OR chart low ≤ active target
