Calculation: price = close of the current forming chart bar. LTF = timeframe lower than the chart timeframe. LTFf = the forming LTF bar. LTFc = the last completed LTF bar.
Calculation: LowerBox (LowerTop, LowerBot) = scan LTF bars from i=0 (forming) toward older bars. Take the first i where LTFf close - range > high of LTF bar i. For that i, let hi = highest high and lo = lowest low over a fixed-length window of LTF bars starting at bar i and going back in time. Require hi - lo <= range AND price - range > hi. Then LowerTop = hi and LowerBot = lo. If no i qualifies, take the round figure just below (price - offsetL), floor-rounded to a coarse price step, and set LowerTop = figure + range/2 and LowerBot = figure - range/2. Recompute only when price > LowerTop + offsetL OR price < LowerBot - offsetL (also periodically, and when the position count falls).
Calculation: UpperBox (UpperTop, UpperBot) = mirror image. Scan LTF bars from i=0. Take the first i where LTFf close + range < low of LTF bar i. Let hi and lo be the window high and low as above. Require hi - lo <= range AND price + range < lo. Then UpperTop = hi and UpperBot = lo. If no i qualifies, take the round figure just above (price + offsetU), floor-rounded, and set UpperTop = figure + range/2 and UpperBot = figure - range/2. Recompute when price > UpperTop + offsetU OR price < UpperBot - offsetU, with offsetU much smaller than offsetL.
Calculation: TP distance = for a long from LowerTop or a short from UpperBot, max((2/3)*(UpperBot - LowerTop), range). For a long from UpperTop or a short from LowerBot, 2*range.
Calculation: trailing stop = initially entry -/+ a very wide fixed distance (long: below entry, short: above entry). Long: once price - trail > entry, stop = max(stop, price - trail), updated only if it improves by at least a small minimum step. Short: mirror image, i.e. once price + trail < entry, stop = min(stop, price + trail).
Long: [for L in {LowerTop, UpperTop}: price - distance > L AND LTFf close - distance > LTFf open AND LTFc low < L] AND (no open long OR exactly one open long with |price - its entry| > range) AND the cooldown since the last trade operation (open or close) has elapsed
Short: [for L in {LowerBot, UpperBot}: price + distance < L AND LTFf close + distance < LTFf open AND LTFc high > L] AND (no open short OR exactly one open short with |price - its entry| > range) AND the cooldown since the last trade operation (open or close) has elapsed
Exit: long, price >= entry + TP distance OR price <= trailing stop
Exit: short, price <= entry - TP distance OR price >= trailing stop
