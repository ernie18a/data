Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = Process chart bars chronologically within the selected history limit. At each bar, confirm a swing high from the bar a swing-strength number of bars earlier if its high is at least every high in the preceding swing-strength bars and strictly greater than every high in the following swing-strength bars; confirm a swing low symmetrically using at most and strictly less. Prepend each confirmed level to its direction's newest-first list. On adding a level, remove levels older than the barrier-memory lookback from that list, oldest first, while retaining at least one level.
Calculation: Zone thickness = The highest high minus the lowest low over the current bar and preceding bars in a lookback whose length is the smaller of the bar's zero-based chronological index and a maximum lookback, but at least one bar, multiplied by a tolerance fraction.
Calculation: Resistance barrier and touches = Scan confirmed swing highs newest first until the first level greater than or equal to the current close. The resistance barrier is the maximum scanned level, initialized to the newest level. Count scanned levels between the barrier minus zone thickness and the barrier, inclusive. Require the scanned prefix to contain more levels than the required touch count.
Calculation: Support barrier and touches = Scan confirmed swing lows newest first until the first level less than or equal to the current close. The support barrier is the minimum scanned level, initialized to the newest level. Count scanned levels between the barrier and the barrier plus zone thickness, inclusive. Require the scanned prefix to contain more levels than the required touch count.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = The cumulative sum of typical price multiplied by volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each calendar day; it is unavailable when cumulative volume is zero.
Long: The chart bar is completed AND close > open AND close > the highest high of the preceding swing-strength bars AND open <= resistance barrier AND the highest high of the preceding swing-strength bars < resistance barrier AND resistance touches >= required touch count AND (confirmation is disabled OR close > the selected available filter: daily VWAP, close SMA, or close SMMA).
Short: The chart bar is completed AND close < open AND close < the lowest low of the preceding swing-strength bars AND open >= support barrier AND the lowest low of the preceding swing-strength bars > support barrier AND support touches >= required touch count AND (confirmation is disabled OR close < the selected available filter: daily VWAP, close SMA, or close SMMA).
