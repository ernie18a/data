Calculation: Candidate trailing stop = selected method: for long, the most recent completed trailing-timeframe bar low minus offset, or the most recent low satisfying low[i] < low[i+1], low[i] < low[i-1], and low[i] < low[i+2] minus offset, or current price minus previous completed ATR minus offset, or previous completed Parabolic SAR minus offset, or previous completed moving average minus offset, or entry price plus the configured fraction of favorable price movement, or current price minus offset; for short, use the corresponding bar high plus offset, high[i] > high[i+1], high[i] > high[i-1], and high[i] > high[i+2] plus offset, current price plus ATR and offset, Parabolic SAR plus offset, moving average plus offset, entry price minus the configured fraction of favorable movement, or current price plus offset. Indicator and bar calculations use the configured trailing timeframe, which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; they use completed bars. ATR, Parabolic SAR, and moving average use their configured lookback and method. Reject Parabolic SAR or moving-average candidates that violate the broker's minimum stop distance; bar-low and bar-high candidates must also satisfy that distance.
Calculation: Active long stop = in normal mode, replace the existing stop with the candidate only if it is more than one configured step above the existing stop and at least the configured start distance above entry; in virtual mode, retain the highest candidate only if it is above the position break-even price by the configured start distance and above the previously retained stop. Reset when there are no long positions.
Calculation: Active short stop = in normal mode, replace the existing stop with the candidate only if it is more than one configured step below the existing stop (or no stop exists) and at least the configured start distance below entry; in virtual mode, retain the lowest candidate only if it is below the position break-even price by the configured start distance and below the previously retained stop (or no stop exists). Reset when there are no short positions.
Exit: Long, close <= active long stop
Exit: Short, close >= active short stop
