Calculation: SuperTrend direction = On each completed bar of the indicator timeframe (lower than the chart timeframe), calculate CCI on typical price (high + low + close) / 3 and add 70. Compare this value with the configurable level. Above the level, activate the up rail at low minus the timeframe-specific point offset; retain the previous up rail if the previous rail state is up and the bar is bearish, the candidate rail is below the previous up rail, or the current high is below the previous high. Below the level, activate the down rail at high plus the offset; retain the previous down rail if the previous rail state is down and the bar is bullish, the candidate rail is above the previous down rail, or the current low is above the previous low. On an upward crossing, carry the previous down rail into the up rail; on a downward crossing, carry the previous up rail into the down rail. A bullish flip occurs when the previous down rail and current up rail are nonzero; a bearish flip occurs when the previous up rail and current down rail are nonzero. Evaluate flips on the selected signal bar after it has completed.
Long: SuperTrend direction flips from bearish to bullish.
Short: SuperTrend direction flips from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, the down rail is nonzero on the selected completed signal bar.
Exit: short, the up rail is nonzero on the selected completed signal bar.
