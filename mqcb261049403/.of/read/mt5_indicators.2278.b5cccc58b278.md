Calculation: Volatility-pivot stop = recursively update the prior stop using the current close and delta: keep the prior stop if the close equals it; if the prior and current closes are both below it, use the lower of the prior stop and current close + delta; if both are above it, use the higher of the prior stop and current close − delta; otherwise use current close − delta when the current close is above the prior stop, or current close + delta when below. Delta is either a fixed price distance or a factor times the EMA-smoothed ATR. Seed the first prior stop with the preceding close. In the higher-timeframe variant, calculate from the higher timeframe’s still-forming bar and map signals to the first chart bar of its interval.
Long: close > volatility-pivot stop AND previous close <= previous volatility-pivot stop
Short: close < volatility-pivot stop AND previous close >= previous volatility-pivot stop
