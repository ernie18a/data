Calculation: Fast stepped Williams %R line = Apply the stepped Williams %R calculation below using a shorter step parameter.
Calculation: Slow stepped Williams %R line = Apply the stepped Williams %R calculation below using a longer step parameter.
Calculation: Stepped Williams %R calculation = On each bar, let x = 100 + Williams %R and let S be the step parameter. Set candidate upper and lower bounds to x + 2S and x - 2S. Set the trend upward if x exceeds the prior upper bound, or downward if x is below the prior lower bound. In an upward trend, clamp the candidate lower bound to at least the prior lower bound; in a downward trend, clamp the candidate upper bound to at most the prior upper bound. The line equals the resulting lower bound + S in an upward trend, or the resulting upper bound - S in a downward trend. Initialize prior bounds to negative and positive infinity, respectively.
Long: On the indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast line on the completed bar immediately before the last completed bar is greater than the slow line on that bar AND the fast line on the last completed bar is less than or equal to the slow line on that bar.
Short: On the indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast line on the completed bar immediately before the last completed bar is less than the slow line on that bar AND the fast line on the last completed bar is greater than or equal to the slow line on that bar.
Exit: Long, on the indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast line on the completed bar immediately before the last completed bar is less than the slow line on that bar AND the fast line on the last completed bar is greater than or equal to the slow line on that bar OR the bar low is at or below entry price minus the configured stop-loss distance OR the bar high is at or above entry price plus the configured take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, on the indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the fast line on the completed bar immediately before the last completed bar is greater than the slow line on that bar AND the fast line on the last completed bar is less than or equal to the slow line on that bar OR the bar high is at or above entry price plus the configured stop-loss distance OR the bar low is at or below entry price minus the configured take-profit distance.
