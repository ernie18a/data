Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-lookback EMAs = exponential moving averages of close, with the short lookback shorter than the medium lookback and the medium shorter than the long; use the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Quiet-bar maximum range = maximum of high − low over the configured number of completed bars immediately before the breakout bar.
Long: Short-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA AND medium-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA AND breakout-bar open > medium-lookback EMA AND quiet-bar maximum range > minimum quiet-range threshold AND breakout-bar close − open >= quiet-bar maximum range × impulse-strength multiplier AND breakout-bar close > breakout-bar open AND breakout-bar close >= breakout-bar high − (breakout-bar high − breakout-bar low) × configured upper-wick percentage AND breakout-bar open >= breakout-bar low + (breakout-bar high − breakout-bar low) × configured lower-wick percentage AND breakout-bar range is within the configured candle-size bounds AND breakout-bar low <= short-lookback EMA AND no open long position for this system and instrument.
Short: Short-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA AND medium-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA AND breakout-bar open < medium-lookback EMA AND quiet-bar maximum range > minimum quiet-range threshold AND breakout-bar open − close >= quiet-bar maximum range × impulse-strength multiplier AND breakout-bar close < breakout-bar open AND breakout-bar open <= breakout-bar high − (breakout-bar high − breakout-bar low) × configured upper-wick percentage AND breakout-bar close <= breakout-bar low + (breakout-bar high − breakout-bar low) × configured lower-wick percentage AND breakout-bar range is within the configured candle-size bounds AND breakout-bar high >= short-lookback EMA AND no open short position for this system and instrument.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − configured stop distance OR high >= entry price + configured target distance.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR low <= entry price − configured target distance.
