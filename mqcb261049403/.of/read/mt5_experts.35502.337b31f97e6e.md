Calculation: Quiet range = maximum high minus low of each of the configured number of chart bars immediately preceding the last completed chart bar.
Long: Position side is not long AND, on the last completed chart bar, shorter-period close EMA > longer-period close EMA for each of two independently parameterized EMA pairs AND open > a separately parameterized close EMA AND quiet range > minimum quiet range AND close - open >= quiet range × impulse strength AND close >= open AND high - close <= maximum upper-wick fraction × (high - low) AND open - low >= minimum lower-wick fraction × (high - low) AND (minimum bar range <= 0 OR high - low >= minimum bar range) AND (maximum bar range <= 0 OR high - low <= maximum bar range) AND low <= a separately parameterized close EMA.
Short: Position side is not short AND, on the last completed chart bar, shorter-period close EMA < longer-period close EMA for each of two independently parameterized EMA pairs AND open < a separately parameterized close EMA AND quiet range > minimum quiet range AND open - close >= quiet range × impulse strength AND close < open AND high - open >= minimum upper-wick fraction × (high - low) AND close - low <= maximum lower-wick fraction × (high - low) AND (minimum bar range <= 0 OR high - low >= minimum bar range) AND (maximum bar range <= 0 OR high - low <= maximum bar range) AND high >= a separately parameterized close EMA.
Exit: long, (stop-loss distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance).
Exit: short, (stop-loss distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance).
