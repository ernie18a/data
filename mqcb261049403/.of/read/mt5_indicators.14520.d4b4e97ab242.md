Calculation: Step line = For each chart bar, let D be DeMarker multiplied by 100 and s be the step size. Initialize the previous lower bound to positive infinity, the previous upper bound to negative infinity, and the trend to 0. Set lower = D - 2s and upper = D + 2s. If D > the previous upper bound, set trend to 1; if D < the previous lower bound, set trend to -1; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is 1, set lower to the maximum of lower and the previous lower bound and the step line to lower + s. If trend is -1, set upper to the minimum of upper and the previous upper bound and the step line to upper - s. Retain the resulting bounds and trend for the next bar; the step line is unavailable while trend is 0. Calculate a fast and a slow step line using the same DeMarker and a smaller step size for the fast line.
Long: Fast step line > slow step line
Short: Fast step line < slow step line
