Calculation: Stochastic momentum = 100 times the three-stage smoothed value of (selected price minus the midpoint of the lookback highest high and lowest low), divided by the three-stage smoothed value of half that high-low range; use zero when the denominator is zero. Apply the same smoothing method and corresponding stage lengths to both series; the first smoothing length is longer than the second, and the second is longer than the third.
Calculation: Signal line = smoothing of stochastic momentum using the same method and length as the third smoothing stage.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = the most recent nonzero comparison of stochastic momentum on a higher timeframe or the chart timeframe with its preceding bar, sampled at the start of each bar of that timeframe; use the bar still forming and retain the previous trend when the values are equal.
Long: Stochastic momentum > signal line OR (stochastic momentum > 0 AND stochastic momentum != previous bar's stochastic momentum) OR higher-timeframe trend > 0
Short: Stochastic momentum < signal line OR (stochastic momentum < 0 AND stochastic momentum != previous bar's stochastic momentum) OR higher-timeframe trend < 0
