Calculation: Power = a configurable moving average of ((high + low) / 2 minus a separately configured moving average of a configurable OHLC-derived price); the two averages have independent methods and lookbacks. Calculate on the chart timeframe for the indicator markings and on a configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, for the trading decisions. Trading decisions use a configurable signal bar: the last completed indicator-timeframe bar, an earlier completed bar, or the still-forming bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe Power > its previous-bar value OR signal-bar Power > its previous-bar value AND previous-bar Power <= its own previous-bar value
Short: Chart-timeframe Power < its previous-bar value OR signal-bar Power < its previous-bar value AND previous-bar Power >= its own previous-bar value
Exit: long, signal-bar Power < its previous-bar value OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, signal-bar Power > its previous-bar value OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
