Calculation: Adaptive average = For a selected OHLC-derived price series, let the efficiency lookback be N, the fast moving-average period be shorter than the slow period, and each smoothing constant be 2 divided by its period plus 1. Starting at bar N+2, initialize the preceding adaptive average to the preceding price. At each bar, efficiency equals the absolute price change over N bars divided by the sum of the absolute one-bar price changes over those N bars plus a small positive price increment. The adaptive average equals its preceding value plus (efficiency times the difference between the fast and slow smoothing constants plus the slow smoothing constant) raised to a configurable exponent, multiplied by the difference between the current price and the preceding adaptive average.
Calculation: Filtered adaptive-average change = Round the current one-bar change in the adaptive average to a configurable price precision; the threshold is the population standard deviation of the latest N one-bar adaptive-average changes multiplied by a configurable factor and rounded to the same precision. Evaluate signals only from bar 2N+6 onward.
Long: Chart-timeframe filtered adaptive-average change > threshold AND adaptive average != 0
