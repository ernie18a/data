Calculation: Signal RSI = RSI of a configurable OHLC-derived price on a configurable timeframe: chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed bar, a configurable earlier completed bar, or the still-forming bar when configured. Compare it with the RSI of the immediately preceding bar.
Long: position side = flat AND ((mode = direct AND previous signal RSI <= lower threshold AND signal RSI > lower threshold) OR (mode = against AND previous signal RSI >= upper threshold AND signal RSI < upper threshold))
Short: position side = flat AND ((mode = direct AND previous signal RSI >= upper threshold AND signal RSI < upper threshold) OR (mode = against AND previous signal RSI <= lower threshold AND signal RSI > lower threshold))
Exit: long, (mode = direct AND previous signal RSI >= upper threshold AND signal RSI < upper threshold) OR (mode = against AND previous signal RSI <= lower threshold AND signal RSI > lower threshold) OR (stop-loss distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance)
Exit: short, (mode = direct AND previous signal RSI <= lower threshold AND signal RSI > lower threshold) OR (mode = against AND previous signal RSI >= upper threshold AND signal RSI < upper threshold) OR (stop-loss distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take-profit distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance)
