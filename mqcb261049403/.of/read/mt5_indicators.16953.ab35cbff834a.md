Calculation: Input price = a selected OHLC price, median price, typical price, weighted price, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or the equivalent price calculated from Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open and (low + close) / 2 otherwise; its extreme variant is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Smoothed price = the input price, optionally smoothed with a selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average. For the linear-weighted method, weight the current input and previous moving-average outputs by descending weights over the lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator = a selected RSI, Cutler's RSI, Ehlers' smoothed RSI, Harris' RSI, Rapid RSI, RSX, or Slow RSI of the smoothed price. Calculate it on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Rapid RSI = 100 × sum of positive price changes / sum of absolute price changes over the lookback; use zero when the denominator is zero. Slow RSI is a smoothed moving average of Rapid RSI, initialized at 50. Ehlers' smoothed RSI is Rapid RSI applied to (current price + 2 × previous price + price two bars ago) / 4, using the unsmoothed price for the first three bars. Harris' RSI = 100 − 100 / (1 + average positive change / average absolute nonpositive change), where each average uses the count of its own class and a zero change is nonpositive.
Calculation: Upper and lower levels = selected fixed levels, or the rolling oscillator minimum plus a selected upper or lower percentage of its rolling range. The middle level is the midpoint of the fixed levels, or the midpoint of the rolling oscillator minimum and maximum when floating levels are selected.
Long: Oscillator > previous oscillator in slope mode OR oscillator > middle level in middle-level mode OR oscillator > upper level in outer-level mode
Short: Oscillator < previous oscillator in slope mode OR oscillator < middle level in middle-level mode OR oscillator < lower level in outer-level mode
