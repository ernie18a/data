Calculation: MACD = fast EMA of close minus slow EMA of close; signal = SMA of MACD. Use the last completed and preceding completed bars of the shortest indicator timeframe.
Calculation: Alligator = smoothed moving averages of median price, with separate periods and forward shifts for jaw, teeth, and lips; jaw has the longest period and shift, teeth intermediate, and lips the shortest. Use the last completed bar of the longest indicator timeframe.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry price minus the stop-loss distance for a long or plus it for a short. At each chart-bar open, if trailing is enabled and price has moved favorably from entry by more than the trailing distance plus the trailing step, move the stop to the open minus the trailing distance for a long or plus it for a short, only if this improves the existing stop by more than the trailing step.
Long: At a new chart-bar open, position side = flat AND (time control disabled OR current hour is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval) AND MACD on its last completed bar < signal AND MACD on its preceding completed bar > signal AND RSI on the last completed bar of an intermediate indicator timeframe > RSI midpoint AND Alligator jaw > teeth AND teeth > lips.
Short: At a new chart-bar open, position side = flat AND (time control disabled OR current hour is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval) AND MACD on its last completed bar > signal AND MACD on its preceding completed bar < signal AND RSI on the last completed bar of an intermediate indicator timeframe < RSI midpoint AND Alligator lips > teeth AND teeth > jaw.
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= trailing stop OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
