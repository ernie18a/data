Calculation: Price pivot = a low or high of the selected source, confirmed after an equal span of bars on each side; the pivot must be strictly lower or higher than every source value on its left and at least as low or high as every source value on its right. The source is either close for both sides or low for lows and high for highs.
Calculation: Oscillators = MACD line; MACD histogram with an EMA signal; Wilder RSI of close; SMA-smoothed stochastic %K; CCI calculated from close; momentum as close minus its lookback close; OBV using volume; volume-weighted MACD as the shorter-lookback volume-weighted moving average of close minus the longer-lookback one; Chaikin Money Flow; and Money Flow Index calculated from close and volume. Seed each EMA with its initial SMA. An oscillator value is unavailable when its required window is incomplete or its denominator is zero; RSI and Money Flow Index equal their maximum when their loss or negative flow is zero and their gain or positive flow is positive.
Calculation: Divergence endpoint = the decision bar when confirmation is disabled, or the previous bar when confirmation is enabled. In confirmation mode, the decision bar must have either oscillator or close moving in the divergence direction relative to the previous bar.
Calculation: Eligible pivot = a confirmed price pivot on the corresponding side, among the most recent specified number of pivots, whose distance from the decision bar is greater than a minimum and no greater than a maximum.
Calculation: Clear divergence path = every intermediate oscillator value and close between pivot and endpoint is at or above its respective straight line between the pivot and endpoint for bullish divergence, or at or below it for bearish divergence; missing oscillator values are ignored in this intermediate check.
Calculation: Divergence count = the number of enabled oscillator and enabled divergence-type combinations found on the decision bar across both directions; each combination counts at most once, using its most recent eligible pivot that satisfies its comparisons and clear path.
Long: Divergence count >= minimum count AND an enabled oscillator has an eligible low pivot with a clear divergence path AND, at the endpoint, (oscillator > oscillator at pivot AND selected low source < pivot source value OR oscillator < oscillator at pivot AND selected low source > pivot source value) AND, if confirmation is enabled, (oscillator > previous oscillator OR close > previous close).
Short: Divergence count >= minimum count AND an enabled oscillator has an eligible high pivot with a clear divergence path AND, at the endpoint, (oscillator < oscillator at pivot AND selected high source > pivot source value OR oscillator > oscillator at pivot AND selected high source < pivot source value) AND, if confirmation is enabled, (oscillator < previous oscillator OR close < previous close).
