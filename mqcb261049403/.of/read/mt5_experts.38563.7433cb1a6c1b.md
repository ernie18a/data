Calculation: Bullish candle row = configured number of consecutive candles with close > open and body size at least the configured minimum; in increasing or decreasing mode, each newer body must be respectively larger or smaller than the immediately older body. Use the configured timeframe and bar offsets; offset zero is still forming, positive offsets are completed bars.
Calculation: Bearish candle row = configured number of consecutive candles with open > close and body size at least the configured minimum; in increasing or decreasing mode, each newer body must be respectively larger or smaller than the immediately older body. Use the configured timeframe and bar offsets; offset zero is still forming, positive offsets are completed bars.
Calculation: Long stop level = lowest low from bar 1 through the configured endpoint on the selected timeframe, using completed bars.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price - long stop level) × configured percentage.
Calculation: Short stop level = highest high from bar 1 through the configured endpoint on the selected timeframe, using completed bars.
Calculation: Short target = entry price - (short stop level - entry price) × configured percentage.
Long: no existing long position AND bearish candle row at its configured offset AND bullish candle row at its configured offset AND completed close on the selected timeframe > highest high from bars 2 through the configured endpoint on that timeframe AND completed stochastic main line > completed stochastic signal line AND each of the configured number of completed stochastic main-line values is between the configured lower and upper levels, inclusive
Short: no existing short position AND bearish candle row at its configured offset AND bullish candle row at its configured offset AND completed close on the selected timeframe < lowest low from bars 2 through the configured endpoint on that timeframe AND completed stochastic main line < completed stochastic signal line AND each of the configured number of completed stochastic main-line values is between the configured lower and upper levels, inclusive
Exit: long, bar low ≤ long stop level OR bar high ≥ long target
Exit: short, bar high ≥ short stop level OR bar low ≤ short target
