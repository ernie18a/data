Calculation: Squeeze oscillator = one selected indicator: Stochastic main line minus 50, CCI of close, RSI of close minus 50, MACD main line of close, Momentum of close minus 100, Williams %R plus 50, ADX main line, or DeMarker minus 0.5. Calculate it on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: Bollinger upper band >= Bollinger middle band + ATR multiplied by the Keltner width multiplier AND close > Bollinger middle band.
Short: Bollinger upper band >= Bollinger middle band + ATR multiplied by the Keltner width multiplier AND close <= Bollinger middle band.
Long: Bollinger standard deviation of close multiplied by the Bollinger width multiplier >= ATR multiplied by the Keltner width multiplier AND squeeze oscillator > 0; calculate the standard deviation and ATR on the same timeframe and bar as the oscillator, with independently selectable lookbacks.
Short: Bollinger standard deviation of close multiplied by the Bollinger width multiplier >= ATR multiplied by the Keltner width multiplier AND squeeze oscillator < 0; calculate the standard deviation and ATR on the same timeframe and bar as the oscillator, with independently selectable lookbacks.
