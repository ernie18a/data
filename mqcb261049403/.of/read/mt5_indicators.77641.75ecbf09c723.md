Calculation: Asian high = highest high during the Asian session, locked when the session ends
Calculation: Asian low = lowest low during the Asian session, locked when the session ends
Long: valid Asian range (at least four session bars and range width within its minimum and maximum ATR bounds) AND trade window AND low < Asian low − minimum sweep distance in ATRs AND close > Asian low AND that low side has not previously swept or closed through AND, within the confirmation-bar limit, a later close > sweep-bar high AND entry-to-stop risk is within its minimum and maximum ATR bounds AND (if the trend filter is enabled) the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA; at most the permitted number of signals per day
Short: valid Asian range (at least four session bars and range width within its minimum and maximum ATR bounds) AND trade window AND high > Asian high + minimum sweep distance in ATRs AND close < Asian high AND that high side has not previously swept or closed through AND, within the confirmation-bar limit, a later close < sweep-bar low AND entry-to-stop risk is within its minimum and maximum ATR bounds AND (if the trend filter is enabled) the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA; at most the permitted number of signals per day
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR day-close time (exit at that bar's open)
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR day-close time (exit at that bar's open)
