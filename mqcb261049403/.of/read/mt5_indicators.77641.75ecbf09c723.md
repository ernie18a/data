Calculation: Session range = maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured session on the same calendar day; lock it on the first bar after the session. A range is valid when its bar count meets the minimum and its width is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of the preceding chart bar's positive ATR, inclusive.
Calculation: Sweep state = after a valid range is locked, examine bars opening within the trade window while the daily signal limit has not been reached and no signal is active. Each range side is available once per day: mark it used when its sweep occurs or when a bar closes beyond that side. Examine the high side first. A high-side sweep requires high > range high + sweep-distance multiplier × current ATR AND close < range high; a low-side sweep requires low < range low − sweep-distance multiplier × current ATR AND close > range low. ATR must be positive. If the optional trend filter is enabled, accept a high-side sweep only when the last completed higher-timeframe close at the sweep bar's close is <= its EMA, and a low-side sweep only when that close is > its EMA. An accepted sweep becomes pending; while pending, do not examine other sweeps. Starting with the next bar, discard the pending sweep if its age exceeds the confirmation limit, its opening time reaches the trade-window end, or its close is beyond the sweep extreme in the sweep direction. Reset pending state and side availability each calendar day.
Calculation: Long stop = pending low-side sweep low − stop-buffer multiplier × ATR on the confirmation bar; long risk = next bar's open − long stop; long target = entry price + reward multiplier × long risk.
Calculation: Short stop = pending high-side sweep high + stop-buffer multiplier × ATR on the confirmation bar; short risk = short stop − next bar's open; short target = entry price − reward multiplier × short risk.
Long: A low-side sweep is pending AND confirmation-bar close > sweep-bar high AND confirmation-bar ATR > 0 AND long risk is between the minimum and maximum ATR multiples, inclusive; enter at the next bar's open.
Short: A high-side sweep is pending AND confirmation-bar close < sweep-bar low AND confirmation-bar ATR > 0 AND short risk is between the minimum and maximum ATR multiples, inclusive; enter at the next bar's open.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR, after the entry bar, the bar's calendar day differs from the entry day OR its opening time reaches the configured day-close time; use the bar's open for a day or time exit, otherwise use the stop before the target if both are reached.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR, after the entry bar, the bar's calendar day differs from the entry day OR its opening time reaches the configured day-close time; use the bar's open for a day or time exit, otherwise use the stop before the target if both are reached.
