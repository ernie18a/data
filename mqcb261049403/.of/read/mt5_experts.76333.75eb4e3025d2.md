Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the completed chart bars whose start times fall within the configured opening-range interval on the current day; require at least two bars.
Calculation: Initial stop = opening-range low minus a price buffer for a long, or opening-range high plus the buffer for a short; alternatively, entry price minus an ATR multiple for a long, or entry price plus an ATR multiple for a short. Use the last completed chart-bar ATR when the order is placed.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus an initial-risk multiple for a long, or entry price minus an initial-risk multiple for a short; initial risk is the distance between the order's entry price and initial stop.
Long: Opening range is formed AND its width is at least a multiple of the last completed chart-bar ATR at formation AND current time is before the entry cutoff AND the day is a weekday AND the session and Friday closing times have not been reached AND today's entry count is below its limit AND no long order is pending AND no long entry has occurred today AND the last completed chart-bar high is above the opening-range high; then the high reaches the opening-range high plus a price buffer before the entry cutoff.
Short: Opening range is formed AND its width is at least a multiple of the last completed chart-bar ATR at formation AND current time is before the entry cutoff AND the day is a weekday AND the session and Friday closing times have not been reached AND today's entry count is below its limit AND no short order is pending AND no short entry has occurred today AND the last completed chart-bar low is below the opening-range low; then the low reaches the opening-range low minus a price buffer before the entry cutoff.
Exit: long, low is at or below the initial stop OR high is at or above the profit target.
Exit: short, high is at or above the initial stop OR low is at or below the profit target.
Exit: long or short, the day is a weekend OR current time is at or after the session closing time OR the day is Friday AND current time is at or after the Friday closing time.
Exit: long or short, opening range is formed and valid AND bars held is at least the holding limit AND (current time is at or after the entry cutoff OR today's entry count is below its limit).
