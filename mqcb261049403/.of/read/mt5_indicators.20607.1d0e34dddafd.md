Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = a configurable moving average of 100 × DeMarker × Money Flow Index / Momentum; use 100 as the unsmoothed value when Momentum is zero. Calculate Money Flow Index with volume and Momentum with a configurable applied price.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe slope = compare the smoothed oscillator on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar with its value on the preceding higher-timeframe bar; sample this comparison on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carry the result forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed oscillator > its previous bar's value.
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed oscillator < its previous bar's value.
Long: Higher-timeframe slope = rising.
Short: Higher-timeframe slope = falling.
Long: Higher-timeframe slope on a configurable chart-bar lag = rising AND higher-timeframe slope on the preceding chart bar = flat.
Short: Higher-timeframe slope on a configurable chart-bar lag = flat AND higher-timeframe slope on the preceding chart bar = rising.
