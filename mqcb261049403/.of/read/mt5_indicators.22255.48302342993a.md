Calculation: Histogram = apply the configured moving average to volume × (100 × DeMarker − 50), using the chart timeframe; evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: Smoothed volume = apply the same moving average, with the same parameters, to volume on the chart timeframe and completed bars.
Calculation: Threshold zones = compare the histogram with each configured level multiplied by smoothed volume; classify it as upper extreme, upper, middle, lower, or lower extreme, in that order. Upper extreme means above the higher upper threshold; upper means above the lower upper threshold but not upper extreme; lower extreme means below the lower lower threshold; lower means below the higher lower threshold but not lower extreme; otherwise middle.
Calculation: Histogram direction = rising when the histogram is greater than its prior value, falling when less, and otherwise unchanged from its prior direction.
Long: prior histogram ≤ 0 AND current histogram > 0 OR prior histogram direction is not rising AND current direction is rising OR prior threshold zone is middle, lower, or lower extreme AND current zone is upper OR prior zone is upper or any lower zone AND current zone is upper extreme OR prior zone is lower AND current zone is middle, upper, or upper extreme OR prior zone is lower extreme AND current zone is lower, middle, upper, or upper extreme
Short: prior histogram ≥ 0 AND current histogram < 0 OR prior histogram direction is not falling AND current direction is falling OR prior threshold zone is upper extreme, upper, or middle AND current zone is lower OR prior zone is upper extreme, upper, lower, or middle AND current zone is lower extreme OR prior zone is upper AND current zone is middle, lower, or lower extreme OR prior zone is upper extreme AND current zone is upper, middle, lower, or lower extreme
