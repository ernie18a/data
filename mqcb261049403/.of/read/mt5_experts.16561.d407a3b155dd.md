Long: Money Flow Index on the chart timeframe, or on a configurable higher or lower timeframe using its last completed bar or its still-forming bar, is at or above the upper threshold AND (the slowdown filter is disabled OR the absolute difference between that Money Flow Index and its previous-bar value is below the slowdown threshold).
Short: Money Flow Index on the same timeframe and bar selection is at or below the lower threshold AND (the slowdown filter is disabled OR the absolute difference between that Money Flow Index and its previous-bar value is below the slowdown threshold).
Exit: long, the current Money Flow Index satisfies the short condition OR neither current directional condition is satisfied AND the most recent earlier directional condition was short OR low is at or below entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high is at or above entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the current Money Flow Index satisfies the long condition OR neither current directional condition is satisfied AND the most recent earlier directional condition was long OR high is at or above entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low is at or below entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
