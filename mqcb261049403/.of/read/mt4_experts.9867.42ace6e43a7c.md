Calculation: Pos(n) = close of the bar n bars ago - (high + low)/2 of that bar, on the chart timeframe; n=1 is the last completed bar
Calculation: Level = close of the last completed bar rounded to a fixed number of decimals (round-number grid)
Calculation: W = weighted sum of four differences, using the last completed bar and bars p and 2p ago: (high[1]-high[p]), (high[p]-high[2p]), (low[1]-low[p]), (low[p]-low[2p]), with user-set signed weights
Long: Pos(a) > Pos(b) AND Pos(c) > m1*Pos(d) AND Pos(e) > m2*Pos(f) (a is a more recent bar than b; d is older than c; f is older than e; m1, m2 > 0; this set of offsets and multipliers is independent of the Short set) AND close[2] < Level AND close[1] > Level AND (optional filters, each may be off: hour of bar time within an allowed window; volume[1] > threshold; daily-timeframe CCI of the still-forming daily bar > 0; W > 0; ATR(period ap) of the last completed chart bar > ATR(same period ap) of the bar cs bars ago; (highest high - lowest low) over the last MinimumBars completed bars < maximum range)
Short: Pos(a) > Pos(b) AND Pos(c) > m1*Pos(d) AND Pos(e) > m2*Pos(f) (same structure as Long but with its own independent offsets and multipliers) AND close[2] > Level AND close[1] < Level AND (optional filters, each may be off: hour of bar time within an allowed window; volume[1] > threshold; daily-timeframe CCI of the still-forming daily bar < 0; W < 0; ATR(period ap) of the last completed chart bar > ATR(same period ap) of the bar cs bars ago; (highest high - lowest low) over the last MinimumBars completed bars < maximum range)
Exit: long, low <= stop level OR high >= entry price + fixed target distance (stop level starts at entry price - fixed stop distance; optional trailing: whenever price > stop level + 2*stop distance, stop level = price - stop distance)
Exit: short, high >= stop level OR low <= entry price - fixed target distance (stop level starts at entry price + fixed stop distance; optional trailing: whenever price < stop level - 2*stop distance, stop level = price + stop distance)
Exit: long, (optional) the Short pattern condition holds (Pos conditions AND close[2] > Level AND close[1] < Level, without the other filters)
Exit: short, (optional) the Long pattern condition holds (Pos conditions AND close[2] < Level AND close[1] > Level, without the other filters)
