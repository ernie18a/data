Calculation: Source indicator = the selected MACD main line, RSI minus 50, CCI, RVI main line, DeMarker minus 0.5, or percent range index minus 50. MACD is the shorter-period moving average of the selected price minus the longer-period moving average. Calculate the selected indicator on the chart timeframe or on the bar containing the chart bar's opening time in a selected higher or lower timeframe; a higher-timeframe bar is still forming, and the selected lower-timeframe bar is the first within the chart bar.
Calculation: Percent range index = 100 times (the average selected price over a slowing lookback minus the range low) divided by (the range high minus the range low), clamped to [0, 100]; use 50 when the range is zero. Over the range lookback, choose the range high and low as either the highest high and lowest low, the highest and lowest closes, or respectively the average of the highest high, highest low and highest close and the average of the lowest high, lowest low and lowest close.
Calculation: Signal = moving average of the source indicator over a signal lookback, using the selected averaging method. For exponential averaging, use current source times 2/(lookback+1) plus the previous stored exponential value times (1-2/(lookback+1)); initialize the stored value to zero. For smoothed averaging, use (previous stored sum minus previous stored sum/lookback plus current source)/lookback; initialize the stored sum to zero. Update the stored exponential value or sum only when the selected timeframe bar index changes; the sum is the sum of source values over the signal lookback.
Calculation: Oscillator = source indicator minus signal.
Long: Oscillator >= previous chart bar's oscillator.
Short: Oscillator < previous chart bar's oscillator.
