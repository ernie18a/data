Calculation: normalized deviation = 100 × smoothed signed deviation from a smoothed applied price ÷ smoothed absolute deviation; signed deviation = applied price − smoothed applied price, and absolute deviation = its absolute value. Both deviation series use the same smoothing method and lookback.
Calculation: fast step line and slow step line = for each line, form upper and lower bounds at normalized deviation ± twice its step size. Set trend up when normalized deviation exceeds the prior upper bound, down when it falls below the prior lower bound, otherwise retain prior trend. In an uptrend, raise the lower bound to the greater of its candidate value and prior lower bound; in a downtrend, lower the upper bound to the lesser of its candidate value and prior upper bound. The line is the lower bound plus one step size in an uptrend, or the upper bound minus one step size in a downtrend. The fast step size is smaller than the slow step size. For the higher-timeframe variant, use the selected timeframe’s bar containing the chart bar, including while that bar is still forming; the selected timeframe may equal or exceed the chart timeframe.
Long: fast step line > slow step line
Short: fast step line < slow step line
