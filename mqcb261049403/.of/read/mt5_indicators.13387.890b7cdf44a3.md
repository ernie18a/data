Calculation: Oscillator = Let price be a selected OHLC-derived price and deviation be price minus its moving average. Smooth deviation and its absolute value separately using the same smoothing method and parameters. The oscillator is 100 times the smoothed deviation divided by the smoothed absolute deviation; use zero when the smoothed deviation is zero.
Calculation: Step line = Calculate two independent lines from the oscillator, using a smaller step for the fast line and a larger step for the slow line. For each line, initialize the upper band to a large negative number, the lower band to a large positive number, and the trend to zero. On each bar, set candidate upper and lower bands to oscillator plus and minus twice the step. Set trend to positive if the oscillator exceeds the previous upper band, then set it to negative if the oscillator is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is positive, replace the lower band with the greater of its candidate and previous value; if negative, replace the upper band with the lesser of its candidate and previous value. The line is the lower band plus the step for a positive trend, or the upper band minus the step for a negative trend. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use the bar still forming.
Long: Fast step line > slow step line
Short: Fast step line < slow step line
