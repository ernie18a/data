Calculation: Session open = open of the first available bar at or after the configured session start, provided its delay does not exceed the allowed delay; session start is configured in server time or converted from London local time using the applicable daylight-saving offsets.
Calculation: Stretch = mean over the configured number of preceding valid sessions of min(session high − session open, session open − session low), where each session runs from its start until the configured session exit time; exclude sessions with insufficient bars or whose first bar exceeds the allowed opening delay.
Calculation: Offset = either stretch or a positive multiple of ATR on the last completed daily bar. Daily bars may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Upper level = session open + offset; lower level = session open − offset.
Calculation: Contraction filter = the selected alternative: no filter; the last completed non-Sunday daily range is strictly less than every range in a shorter lookback; the same condition using a longer lookback; the shorter-lookback condition AND the last completed non-Sunday daily high <= the preceding non-Sunday daily high AND its low >= the preceding low; the combined high-low range of the most recent group of completed non-Sunday daily bars is strictly less than that of every preceding overlapping group in a longer reference lookback; or any of these contraction conditions. Daily bars may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Wide-day filter = no filter, or the last completed non-Sunday daily range is not strictly greater than every preceding range in the selected shorter or longer lookback. Daily bars may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Time >= session start AND time < entry-window end AND session open is available AND offset > 0 AND contraction filter passes AND wide-day filter passes AND (Friday skipping is disabled OR day of week is not Friday) AND session high before orders are armed < upper level AND session low before orders are armed > lower level AND session entry count < maximum AND no position is open AND high after orders are armed >= upper level.
Short: Time >= session start AND time < entry-window end AND session open is available AND offset > 0 AND contraction filter passes AND wide-day filter passes AND (Friday skipping is disabled OR day of week is not Friday) AND session high before orders are armed < upper level AND session low before orders are armed > lower level AND session entry count < maximum AND no position is open AND low after orders are armed <= lower level.
Exit: Long, low <= lower level OR (take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit multiple × offset) OR time >= the earlier of the configured session exit time and the configured cutoff before server-day end.
Exit: Short, high >= upper level OR (take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price − take-profit multiple × offset) OR time >= the earlier of the configured session exit time and the configured cutoff before server-day end.
