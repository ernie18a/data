Calculation: Applied price = a selectable OHLC price: open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, or (open + high + low + close)/4; alternatively, use high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise; or use (high + close)/2 when close > open, (low + close)/2 when close < open, and close otherwise; or use (high + low + close + X)/2 − (high + low)/2, where X is low when close < open, high when close > open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Recursive level and direction = calculate chronologically from the selected applied price on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar. Initialize the level to the applied price of the first calculated bar and each bar's direction to bullish. For each bar let P be its applied price, Q the applied price a configurable lookback earlier, and R the preceding bar's level. If P = R, the level is R and the bar's direction remains unchanged. Otherwise, if Q > R AND P > R, the level is max(R, P × a discount factor) and direction is bullish; if Q < R AND P < R, the level is min(R, P × a premium factor) and direction is bearish; otherwise, if P > R, the level is P × the discount factor and direction is bullish; otherwise, the level is P × the premium factor and direction is bearish. Recalculate a forming bar from the preceding completed bar's level.
Long: Direction = bullish
Short: Direction = bearish
Exit: long, direction = bearish OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, direction = bullish OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
