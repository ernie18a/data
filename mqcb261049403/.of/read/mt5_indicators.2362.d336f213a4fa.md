Long: On the chart timeframe, each of a consecutive lookback of bars has a higher high AND higher low than its preceding bar OR the most recent qualifying run at or before the selected higher-timeframe bar is a run in which each bar has a higher high AND higher low than its preceding bar
Short: On the chart timeframe, each of a consecutive lookback of bars has a lower high AND lower low than its preceding bar OR the most recent qualifying run at or before the selected higher-timeframe bar is a run in which each bar has a lower high AND lower low than its preceding bar
Calculation: Selected higher-timeframe bar = the still-forming bar or a selected completed bar; a qualifying run is a consecutive lookback of higher-timeframe bars in which every bar has both a higher high and higher low than its preceding bar or every bar has both a lower high and lower low than its preceding bar
