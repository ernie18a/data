Calculation: Asymmetric oscillator = On the selected indicator timeframe, use either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar. Initialize both the high and low lookbacks to the shorter lookback. For each bar, sum the closes over the slowing window, and separately sum, for each bar in that window, the highest high over the high lookback and the lowest low over the low lookback; optionally use closes for both extrema. Let range = sum of highs - sum of lows. If range < sensitivity in price units, subtract half of (sensitivity - range) from the sum of lows and set range to sensitivity. The oscillator is 100 × (sum of closes - adjusted sum of lows) / range; if range is zero, it is -2. Smooth the oscillator with the selected moving-average method to obtain its signal line. After calculating each bar, set the high lookback to shorter and low lookback to longer if the preceding bar's signal line is above the upper threshold; then set the high lookback to longer and low lookback to shorter if that signal line is below the lower threshold. Otherwise retain the lookbacks. The indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Oscillator on the selected indicator bar > its signal line AND oscillator on the preceding indicator bar < its signal line.
Short: Oscillator on the selected indicator bar < its signal line AND oscillator on the preceding indicator bar > its signal line.
Exit: long, oscillator on the selected indicator bar < its signal line OR low <= entry price - enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, oscillator on the selected indicator bar > its signal line OR high >= entry price + enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - enabled take-profit distance.
