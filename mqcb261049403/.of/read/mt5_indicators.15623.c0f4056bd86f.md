Calculation: Selected price = choose close, open, high, low, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, high if close > open and low if close < open and close otherwise, or (high + close)/2 if close > open and (low + close)/2 if close < open and close otherwise.
Calculation: Smoothed composite = apply a chosen moving-average method to (5 × EMA − LWMA)/4 of the selected price. For smoothing length N, the EMA length is floor((N + 3)/N) and the LWMA length is floor((N + 5)/N). Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe. For a higher timeframe, sample its still-forming bar on the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe bar; historical recalculation instead uses that higher-timeframe bar's completed value.
Long: The chart bar is the first bar of the selected timeframe AND its smoothed composite > the smoothed composite sampled on the preceding selected-timeframe bar.
Short: The chart bar is the first bar of the selected timeframe AND its smoothed composite < the smoothed composite sampled on the preceding selected-timeframe bar.
