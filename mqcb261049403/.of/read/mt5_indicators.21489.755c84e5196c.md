Calculation: Upper level = highest high over a lookback window beginning a configurable number of bars before the evaluated bar.
Calculation: Lower level = lowest low over an independently configured lookback window beginning an independently configurable number of bars before the evaluated bar.
Long: Chart-bar close > chart-timeframe upper level AND chart-bar close >= chart-timeframe lower level.
Short: Chart-bar close < chart-timeframe lower level.
Long: Still-forming higher-timeframe bar close > its upper level AND still-forming higher-timeframe bar close >= its lower level.
Short: Still-forming higher-timeframe bar close < its lower level.
