Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over a lookback ending at the current bar.
Calculation: Sampled higher-timeframe close and range midpoint = the close and range midpoint of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, sampled on its first chart bar and held until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart close > chart range midpoint OR sampled higher-timeframe close > sampled higher-timeframe range midpoint.
Short: Chart close < chart range midpoint OR sampled higher-timeframe close < sampled higher-timeframe range midpoint.
