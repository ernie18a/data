Calculation: StepMA stochastic histogram = Calculate three StepMA trend lines using short, long, and midpoint adaptive step sizes. For each bar, compute weighted average true range from high-low ranges over a lookback, weighting the current bar by 2 and reducing weights linearly to 1 for the oldest bar; track its running minimum and maximum, and set the short and long step sizes to those extrema times a multiplier, with the midpoint step size their average times the multiplier. For each step size, maintain upper and lower bands at close ± 2 steps and a trend state: set the state to +1 when close exceeds the prior upper band, or -1 when close falls below the prior lower band; in a positive state, keep the lower band from falling, and in a negative state, keep the upper band from rising. The trend line is the lower band plus one step in a positive state, or the upper band minus one step in a negative state. Normalize the short and midpoint trend lines between (long trend line − long step) and (short trend line + long step); the histogram is the short-line normalized value minus the midpoint-line normalized value. Use the higher-timeframe still-forming bar.
