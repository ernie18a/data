Calculation: Step oscillator = For each bar, calculate the weighted range as the sum of (high − low) for the current and preceding lookback bars, with weights decreasing linearly from 2 for the current bar by 1/lookback per bar, divided by the lookback. Track the running minimum and maximum weighted ranges, initialized to the first weighted range. Form three step sizes by multiplying the running minimum, running maximum, and their average by a multiplier and rounding each to the nearest price increment. For each step size, maintain an upper band, lower band, and trend. Initialize the bands to the first close, or to the first low and high respectively in high-low mode, and initialize the trend to zero. On each bar set the candidate upper band to close + 2 × step size and the candidate lower band to close − 2 × step size; in high-low mode use low for the upper band and high for the lower band. Set the trend to positive if close exceeds the previous upper band, then set it to negative if close is below the previous lower band; otherwise retain the prior trend. In a positive trend, replace the candidate lower band with the greater of it and the previous lower band; in a negative trend, replace the candidate upper band with the lesser of it and the previous upper band. The step line is lower band + step size for a positive trend and upper band − step size for a negative trend. Let the lower normalization bound be the maximum-range step line minus the maximum step size, and the upper bound be the minimum-range step line plus the maximum step size. If their difference is zero, substitute a small positive denominator. Normalize the minimum-range and average-range step lines separately by subtracting the lower bound and dividing by the denominator, rounding each result to six decimal places; the oscillator is the first normalized value minus the second. Calculate once on the chart timeframe and once on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
