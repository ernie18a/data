Calculation: Applied price = a selected one of close, open, high, low, median price, typical price, weighted close, (open + close) / 2, (open + high + low + close) / 4, high if close > open otherwise low if close < open otherwise close, (high + close) / 2 if close > open otherwise (low + close) / 2 if close < open otherwise close, or (high + low + 2 * close) / 2 - (high + low) / 2 with the second close replaced by low when close < open or by high when close > open.
Calculation: Fast and slow averages = independently selected moving averages of applied price, with a shorter and a longer smoothing length respectively; choices are SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, Jurik moving average, ultra-linear average, quadratic regression moving average, Tillson T3, VIDYA, or Kaufman adaptive moving average.
Calculation: Ultra-linear average = apply three successive stages to the input; each stage calculates two successive EMAs with smoothing factor 3 / (length + 2), then outputs 1.5 times the first EMA minus 0.5 times the second; initialize each EMA to the first input.
