Calculation: TrailStop(long) = once price - entry > trail distance, stop is set to price - trail distance; it is moved up only when the new level exceeds the current stop by more than a step (the step is smaller than the trail distance); TrailStop(short) is the mirror: once entry - price > trail distance, stop = price + trail distance, moved down only when the new level is below the current stop by more than the step
Long: during the current bar, price >= previous bar high + small spread buffer AND current bar open < previous bar high AND current bar open > previous bar low AND (previous bar high - current price at order placement) >= min distance AND (current price at order placement - previous bar low) >= min distance
Short: during the current bar, price <= previous bar low - small spread buffer AND current bar open > previous bar low AND current bar open < previous bar high AND (previous bar high - current price at order placement) >= min distance AND (current price at order placement - previous bar low) >= min distance
Exit: long, price <= entry - fixed stop distance OR (fixed take-profit mode AND price >= entry + fixed take-profit distance) OR (trailing mode AND price <= TrailStop(long))
Exit: short, price >= entry + fixed stop distance OR (fixed take-profit mode AND price <= entry - fixed take-profit distance) OR (trailing mode AND price >= TrailStop(short))
