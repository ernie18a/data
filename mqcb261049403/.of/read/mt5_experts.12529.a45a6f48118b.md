Calculation: Range Expansion Index = For each indicator-timeframe bar i, let dH = high[i] - high[i-2] and dL = low[i] - low[i-2]. Set the numerator contribution to zero if (high[i-2] < close[i-7] AND high[i-2] < close[i-8] AND high[i] < high[i-5] AND high[i] < high[i-6]) OR (low[i-2] > close[i-7] AND low[i-2] > close[i-8] AND low[i] > low[i-5] AND low[i] > low[i-6]); otherwise set it to dH + dL. Divide the sum of numerator contributions over the lookback by the sum of abs(dH) + abs(dL) over the same lookback and multiply by 100; use zero if the denominator is zero. The indicator timeframe is configurable relative to the chart timeframe and may be higher or lower; use the last completed indicator bar by default, or the still-forming bar if configured, and compare it with its preceding indicator bar.
Long: Previous Range Expansion Index <= lower threshold AND current Range Expansion Index > lower threshold
Short: Previous Range Expansion Index >= upper threshold AND current Range Expansion Index < upper threshold
Exit: long, Range Expansion Index <= lower threshold OR Range Expansion Index < upper threshold OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, Range Expansion Index > lower threshold OR Range Expansion Index >= upper threshold OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
