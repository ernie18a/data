Calculation: Pivot levels = From the preceding completed bar on the configured pivot timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), let H, L, C be its high, low, close, and let R=H−L. Standard: PP=(H+L+C)/3; R1=2PP−L, S1=2PP−H; R2=PP+R, S2=PP−R; R3=R1+R, S3=L−2(H−PP); R4=R3+R2−R1, S4=S3−(S1−S2); midpoint levels are averages of adjacent levels. Fibonacci: PP=(H+L+C)/3; each Rn=PP+kR and Sn=PP−kR, for k∈{0.382,0.618,0.786,1,1.382,1.618,2}. Camarilla: R1–R4=C+(1.1R)/{12,6,4,2}; S1–S4=C−(1.1R)/{12,6,4,2}; PP=(R4+S4)/2; R5=HC/L, S5=2C−R5. Woodie: PP=(H+L+2O)/4, where O is the open of the current pivot-timeframe bar; R1=2PP−L, R2=PP+R, S1=2PP−H, S2=PP−R.
Long: For any enabled pivot level, close crosses from at or below to above the level OR the last completed bar on the configured bounce timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) has open below the level, high at or above it, low at or below it, and close above it; for resistance levels require a bearish close, for support and central pivot levels require a bullish close, when the candle-color filter is enabled; if the Bollinger filter is enabled, price is above the upper band or below the lower band on its configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using the still-forming bar.
Short: For any enabled pivot level, close crosses from at or above to below the level OR the last completed bar on the configured bounce timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe) has open above the level, high at or above it, low at or below it, and close below it; for resistance and central pivot levels require a bearish close, for support levels require a bearish close, when the candle-color filter is enabled; if the Bollinger filter is enabled, price is above the upper band or below the lower band on its configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using the still-forming bar.
