Calculation: Pivot levels = Standard, Fibonacci, Woodie, or Camarilla pivot levels calculated from the last completed pivot-timeframe bar; the pivot timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Woodie uses the open of the forming pivot-timeframe bar. For Standard, R3 = R1 + previous high - previous low, S3 = previous low - 2 × (previous high - PP), R4 = R3 + R2 - R1, and S4 = S3 - S1 + S2; optional intermediate levels are midpoints of adjacent levels. For Camarilla, PP = previous close, R5 = previous high / previous low × previous close, and S5 = 2 × previous close - R5.
Calculation: Selected crossed level = a pivot level within the inclusive high-low range of the last completed bounce-timeframe bar whose open differs from that level. The bounce timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. If several levels qualify, select the highest for a bullish bar with positive levels; otherwise select the last qualifying level in the source order: Standard PP, S1, S2, S3, R1, R2, R3, R4, S4, then resistance midpoints descending and support midpoints ascending; Camarilla PP, S1 through S4, R1 through R5, S5; Woodie PP, S1, S2, R1, R2; Fibonacci PP, resistance levels ascending, support levels ascending.
Calculation: Pending bounce = a stored resistance level when a completed bounce-timeframe bar crosses the selected resistance and closes above it, or a stored support level when it crosses the selected support and closes below it. Reset the pending bounce at the start of each pivot-timeframe bar, when a newly selected crossed level has an index different from the pending direction code, or when the qualifying return close occurs. The direction code is one for resistance and two for support; level indices are zero-based in the source order. A new pending bounce can be set only when none remains.
Long: The selected crossed level is support or PP AND the completed bounce-timeframe close is above that level AND no bounce is pending AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close is above open AND the level is enabled AND the configured number of bounce-timeframe bars has elapsed since its previous bounce alert.
Long: A support bounce is pending AND the completed bounce-timeframe close is above the stored level AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close is above open AND the stored level is enabled AND the configured number of bounce-timeframe bars has elapsed since its previous bounce alert.
Short: The selected crossed level is resistance or PP AND the completed bounce-timeframe close is below that level AND no bounce is pending AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close is below open AND the level is enabled AND the configured number of bounce-timeframe bars has elapsed since its previous bounce alert.
Short: A resistance bounce is pending AND the completed bounce-timeframe close is below the stored level AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close is below open AND the stored level is enabled AND the configured number of bounce-timeframe bars has elapsed since its previous bounce alert.
