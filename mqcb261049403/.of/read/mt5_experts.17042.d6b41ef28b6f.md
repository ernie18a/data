Calculation: Fractal Force Index = selected volume × (current dynamic-period moving average of applied price − preceding moving-average value). The period is rounded from normal speed ÷ [2 × (2 − fractal dimension)], where fractal dimension = 1 + [ln(path length) + ln 2] ÷ ln[2 × (lookback − 1)]; path length is the sum across the lookback of √(squared change between consecutive prices normalized to the lookback high-low range + 1/lookback²). Use the configured moving-average method, applied price, and volume type. Calculate on the configured signal timeframe, which is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe; use completed bars.
Long: In direct mode, the Fractal Force Index crosses above the upper threshold; OR, in countertrend mode, it crosses below the lower threshold.
Short: In direct mode, the Fractal Force Index crosses below the lower threshold; OR, in countertrend mode, it crosses above the upper threshold.
Exit: long, in direct mode the Fractal Force Index crosses below the lower threshold; OR, in countertrend mode it crosses above the upper threshold.
Exit: short, in direct mode the Fractal Force Index crosses above the upper threshold; OR, in countertrend mode it crosses below the lower threshold.
