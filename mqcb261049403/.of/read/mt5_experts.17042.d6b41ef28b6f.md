Calculation: Adaptive force = volume × (current adaptive moving average − previous bar's adaptive moving average). For each bar, select an OHLC-derived price and a fractal lookback n. Let maximum = max(0, highest selected price over n bars), minimum = min(9999999999, lowest selected price over n bars), and normalized price = (selected price − minimum) / (maximum − minimum). If maximum > minimum, path length is the sum over adjacent pairs in the n-bar window of sqrt((normalized price difference)^2 + 1/n^2); otherwise path length is zero. Fractal dimension = 1 + ln(2 × path length)/ln(2 × (n − 1)) when path length > 0, otherwise zero. Adaptive period = round(normal speed / (2 × (2 − fractal dimension))). The adaptive moving average is the selected SMA, linearly weighted MA, EMA, or smoothed MA of selected price using that bar's adaptive period. For EMA and smoothed MA, seed with the price one adaptive period before the bar and apply the recurrence over the following adaptive-period bars. Calculate each bar's adaptive moving average with its own adaptive period. Use chart-timeframe bars or higher- or lower-timeframe bars; compare the selected bar and its predecessor, with the selected bar either the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
Long: No open position AND ((direct mode AND adaptive force crosses above the upper threshold) OR (reverse mode AND adaptive force crosses below the lower threshold)).
Short: No open position AND ((direct mode AND adaptive force crosses below the lower threshold) OR (reverse mode AND adaptive force crosses above the upper threshold)).
Exit: long, (direct mode AND adaptive force crosses below the lower threshold) OR (reverse mode AND adaptive force crosses above the upper threshold) OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, (direct mode AND adaptive force crosses above the upper threshold) OR (reverse mode AND adaptive force crosses below the lower threshold) OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
