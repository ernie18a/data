Calculation: ADR = ATR on the higher D1 timeframe, using the last completed D1 bar.
Calculation: Long-lookback ATR = ATR on the chart timeframe with a longer lookback than the short-lookback ATR; short-lookback ATR uses the trading-period lookback.
Calculation: ATR variation = 0.5 × (one-day ATR / ADR − 1) + 0.5 × (short-lookback ATR / long-lookback ATR − 1), using last completed bars.
Calculation: Asian range = highest high and lowest low of a parameterized number of completed H1 bars immediately before the London open; H1 is higher than M15/M30 charts and the same timeframe as H1 charts.
Calculation: PSAR swing high = the most recent completed-bar PSAR flip from below price to above price; PSAR swing low = the most recent completed-bar flip from above price to below price.
Calculation: Long entry price = PSAR swing high + an ATR-scaled buffer. Short entry price = PSAR swing low − an ATR-scaled buffer. ATR and PSAR use the chart timeframe and completed bars.
Calculation: Initial stop = opposite PSAR swing, offset outward by an ADR-scaled buffer, with its distance from entry clamped between ADR-scaled minimum and maximum distances. Take profit = entry-to-stop distance multiplied by a parameterized factor.
Long: PSAR on the last completed chart bar < close two chart bars ago AND PSAR two chart bars ago > close on the last completed chart bar AND MACD main > signal on the last completed chart bar AND Stochastic ≤ its upper threshold AND last completed chart-bar high − low > long-lookback ATR AND ABS(ATR variation) ≤ its threshold AND Asian range size is between its ADR-scaled minimum and maximum AND current time is within the London order-placement window AND long entry price > Asian range high.
Short: PSAR on the last completed chart bar > close two chart bars ago AND PSAR two chart bars ago < close on the last completed chart bar AND MACD main < signal on the last completed chart bar AND Stochastic ≥ its lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar high − low > long-lookback ATR AND ABS(ATR variation) ≤ its threshold AND Asian range size is between its ADR-scaled minimum and maximum AND current time is within the London order-placement window AND short entry price < Asian range low.
Exit: long, MACD main crosses below signal between the two most recent completed chart bars OR price reaches the active stop OR price reaches take profit OR current time is at or after the New York close or within the configured interval before the symbol session close.
Exit: short, MACD main crosses above signal between the two most recent completed chart bars OR price reaches the active stop OR price reaches take profit OR current time is at or after the New York close or within the configured interval before the symbol session close.
