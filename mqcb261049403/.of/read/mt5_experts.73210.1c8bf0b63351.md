Calculation: Reference range = ATR on the higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Volatility variation = ((shorter-lookback ATR on the higher timeframe / reference range - 1) + (shorter-lookback ATR on the chart timeframe / longer-lookback ATR on the chart timeframe - 1)) / 2; use the last completed bar of each timeframe.
Calculation: Pre-session range = highest high and lowest low of a fixed number of completed bars immediately before the London open, on a timeframe higher than or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: PSAR swing high = the most recent PSAR value within the search lookback where PSAR is above the corresponding close and the preceding PSAR is below its corresponding close; PSAR swing low = the most recent PSAR value within that lookback where PSAR is below the corresponding close and the preceding PSAR is above its corresponding close. Both swings must exist.
Calculation: Long trigger = PSAR swing high plus a fraction of the longer-lookback chart-timeframe ATR; short trigger = PSAR swing low minus a fraction of that ATR. Calculate triggers at setup and retain them until hit or canceled at the session exit.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = absolute difference between the trigger and the opposite PSAR swing, adjusted away from the trigger by a fraction of the reference range, clamped between minimum and maximum fractions of the reference range. Calculate it at setup. The profit target distance is a multiple of this stop distance.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry price minus the initial stop distance for a long, or plus it for a short. At each chart-bar open, once price has moved favorably from entry by the trailing-start fraction of the initial stop distance, calculate a candidate stop at current price minus that distance times the break-even fraction for a long, or plus it for a short; if that candidate remains beyond entry on the loss side, use current price minus the full initial stop distance for a long, or plus it for a short. Update the stop only if the candidate is more favorable than the existing stop.
Long: At a chart-bar open within the London placement window, PSAR on the last completed bar < close two bars ago AND PSAR two bars ago > close on the last completed bar AND MACD main > MACD signal on the last completed bar AND close-based stochastic main on the last completed bar <= upper threshold AND last completed bar high - low > longer-lookback chart-timeframe ATR AND absolute volatility variation <= threshold AND pre-session range size >= minimum fraction of reference range AND pre-session range size <= maximum fraction of reference range AND long trigger > pre-session range high AND long trigger > current price AND opposite PSAR swing minus its reference-range buffer < long trigger AND subsequent price >= long trigger.
Short: At a chart-bar open within the London placement window, PSAR on the last completed bar > close two bars ago AND PSAR two bars ago < close on the last completed bar AND MACD main < MACD signal on the last completed bar AND close-based stochastic main on the last completed bar >= lower threshold AND last completed bar high - low > longer-lookback chart-timeframe ATR AND absolute volatility variation <= threshold AND pre-session range size >= minimum fraction of reference range AND pre-session range size <= maximum fraction of reference range AND short trigger < pre-session range low AND short trigger < current price AND opposite PSAR swing plus its reference-range buffer > short trigger AND subsequent price <= short trigger.
Exit: long, MACD main two completed bars ago > MACD signal two completed bars ago AND MACD main on the last completed bar < MACD signal on the last completed bar.
Exit: short, MACD main two completed bars ago < MACD signal two completed bars ago AND MACD main on the last completed bar > MACD signal on the last completed bar.
Exit: long, price <= trailing stop OR price >= entry price + profit target distance.
Exit: short, price >= trailing stop OR price <= entry price - profit target distance.
Exit: long, current time >= New York session close.
Exit: short, current time >= New York session close.
