Calculation: Smoothed log-price momentum = apply the same selected moving-average method and length twice in succession to the natural logarithm of the selected bar price; subtract its value a momentum-lookback number of bars ago from its current value, then smooth the difference with the same moving-average method and a separately configured length. Calculate on a configurable higher or lower timeframe, using the last completed bar or the bar still forming as configured; compare consecutive bars of that timeframe.
Long: Smoothed log-price momentum on the signal bar > its value on the preceding bar AND its value on the preceding bar < its value two bars before the signal bar.
Short: Smoothed log-price momentum on the signal bar < its value on the preceding bar AND its value on the preceding bar > its value two bars before the signal bar.
Exit: long, smoothed log-price momentum on the signal bar < its value on the preceding bar OR low <= entry price minus the configured stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the configured take-profit distance.
Exit: short, smoothed log-price momentum on the signal bar > its value on the preceding bar OR high >= entry price plus the configured stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the configured take-profit distance.
