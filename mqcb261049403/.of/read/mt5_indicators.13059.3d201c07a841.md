Calculation: Smoothed-price CMO = 100 × (sum of positive changes in a moving average of the selected price − sum of absolute negative changes in that moving average) / sum of absolute changes in that moving average, over a lookback equal to the moving average period; use 0 when the denominator is 0.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed-price CMO > 0
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed-price CMO < 0
Long: Smoothed-price CMO of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > 0
Short: Smoothed-price CMO of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < 0
