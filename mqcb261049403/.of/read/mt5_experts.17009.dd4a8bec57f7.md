Calculation: shorter and longer SMAs = simple moving averages of bar opens on the chart timeframe, using the last completed bar; the shorter SMA has a shorter lookback than the longer SMA
Long: no open position AND last completed bar close < longer SMA
Short: no open position AND last completed bar close > shorter SMA
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
