Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-period moving average of median price minus longer-period moving average of median price; the two averages may use different smoothing methods.
Long: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0 AND oscillator > previous oscillator OR chart-timeframe oscillator < 0 AND oscillator > previous oscillator
Short: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0 AND oscillator < previous oscillator OR chart-timeframe oscillator < 0 AND oscillator < previous oscillator
Long: On the higher timeframe, the last completed oscillator > the preceding oscillator AND the oscillator before that > the preceding oscillator
Short: On the higher timeframe, the last completed oscillator < the preceding oscillator AND the oscillator before that < the preceding oscillator
Exit: long, last completed higher-timeframe oscillator < preceding higher-timeframe oscillator OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, last completed higher-timeframe oscillator > preceding higher-timeframe oscillator OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
