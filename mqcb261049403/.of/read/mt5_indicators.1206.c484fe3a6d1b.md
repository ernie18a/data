Calculation: True RVI = weighted sum over the lookback of four-bar weighted sums of volume × (close − open), divided by the same weighted sums of volume × (high − low); the four-bar weights are 1, 8, 8, 1, and the lookback weights decrease from longest to shortest lag.
Calculation: RMACD = short-lookback Relative Vigor Index − long-lookback True RVI.
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving-average smoothing of RMACD.
Long: RMACD on the latest completed bar < signal line on the preceding bar AND RMACD on the preceding bar > signal line on the bar before it.
Short: RMACD on the latest completed bar > signal line on the preceding bar AND RMACD on the preceding bar < signal line on the bar before it.
