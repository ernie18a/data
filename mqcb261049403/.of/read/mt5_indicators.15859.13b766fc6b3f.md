Calculation: Rounded volume-weighted moving average = round the volume-weighted average of a selected bar price over a lookback, plus a price offset, to a configurable price increment. The selected price is open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2*close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, high if close > open else low if close < open else close, (high + close)/2 if close > open else (low + close)/2 if close < open else close, or (high + low + close + X)/2 - (high + low)/2 where X is low if close < open, high if close > open, and close otherwise.
Long: The most recent nonzero change in the rounded volume-weighted moving average is positive.
Short: The most recent nonzero change in the rounded volume-weighted moving average is negative.
