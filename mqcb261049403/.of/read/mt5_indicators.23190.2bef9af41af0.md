Calculation: Input price = a selected OHLC-derived price: open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, or a conditional price. The conditional price is high, low, or close when close is respectively above, below, or equal to open; alternatively (high+close)/2, (low+close)/2, or close under the same respective conditions; alternatively (high+low+close+X)/2-(high+low)/2, where X is low, high, or close when close is respectively below, above, or equal to open.
Calculation: Volume-weighted Fisher = for each bar, let H and L be the highest high and lowest low over a lookback including the current bar, v = (input price-L)/(H-L)-0.5+0.67*previous v, initialized to zero, and r = (1+v)/(1-v); replace r with 1 if r < 0.0000001. Fisher = 0.5*ln(r)+0.5*previous Fisher, initialized to zero. Apply the selected moving-average method and parameters to Fisher*volume, then multiply by 100. Apply the same method and parameters to volume to obtain smoothed volume.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = calculate volume-weighted Fisher and smoothed volume on the higher timeframe using its bar still forming at the start of each higher-timeframe period, and hold those values throughout that period. The previous Fisher value in the comparisons is the preceding higher-timeframe bar's volume-weighted Fisher. The same calculations and comparisons can instead be made directly on the chart timeframe.
Long: Volume-weighted Fisher > its previous bar's value.
Short: Volume-weighted Fisher < its previous bar's value.
Long: Volume-weighted Fisher > upper threshold * smoothed volume AND volume-weighted Fisher >= its previous bar's value.
Short: Volume-weighted Fisher < lower threshold * smoothed volume AND volume-weighted Fisher <= its previous bar's value.
