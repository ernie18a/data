Calculation: Oscillator candle = for each of open, high, low and close separately, smooth the bar-to-bar change and its absolute value with the same selected moving-average method and parameters; divide the smoothed change by the smoothed absolute change, clamp the result to [-1, 1], smooth it with a second selected moving-average method and multiply by 100. The oscillator high is the maximum of the resulting high, open and close; the oscillator low is the minimum of the resulting low, open and close. The oscillator is undefined when the smoothed absolute change is zero.
Calculation: Upper and lower boundaries = either the selected upper and lower levels, respectively, or the SMA of oscillator close plus and minus a selected multiplier times the SMA of oscillator high minus oscillator low, using the same lookback for both SMAs. Select one boundary mode for each evaluation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe variant = calculate the oscillator candle and boundaries on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; sample the resulting values at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carry them forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: oscillator close > upper boundary OR (oscillator close <= upper boundary AND oscillator close >= lower boundary AND oscillator close > oscillator open)
Short: (oscillator close <= upper boundary AND oscillator close < lower boundary) OR (oscillator close <= upper boundary AND oscillator close >= lower boundary AND oscillator close < oscillator open)
