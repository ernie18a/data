Calculation: Centered smoothed money flow index = For each bar, sum volume × applied price for price increases and decreases over a lookback, using the newer bar’s volume and price; calculate 100 × positive flow / (positive flow + negative flow) − 50, then smooth it. If calculated on a higher timeframe, use that timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Long: In level mode, centered smoothed money flow index > middle level OR, in direction mode, centered smoothed money flow index > middle level AND greater than its prior bar OR centered smoothed money flow index < middle level AND greater than its prior bar
Short: In level mode, centered smoothed money flow index < middle level OR, in direction mode, centered smoothed money flow index > middle level AND less than its prior bar OR centered smoothed money flow index < middle level AND less than its prior bar
