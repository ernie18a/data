Calculation: Selected price = a selectable open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (open+close)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, trend-biased price, or trend-biased extreme, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high+close)/2 when close>open and (low+close)/2 otherwise; trend-biased extreme is high when close>open, low when close<open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: RSX = initialize to 50; for each subsequent bar, separately apply three successive stages to selected-price change and its absolute value. At each stage, a=K*input+(1-K)*previous a, b=K*a+(1-K)*previous b, output=1.5*a-0.5*b, with all a and b initialized to zero and K=3/(period+2). RSX=clamp(50*(1+final signed output/max(final absolute-change output, smallest positive floating-point number)), 0, 100).
Calculation: Nonlag inverse Fisher RSX = tanh(nonlag average of 0.1*(RSX-50)). For smoothing length L, use the unsmoothed input if L<5 or fewer than three preceding bars exist. Otherwise let P=L-1, N=4*L+P, t=k/(P-1) for k<=P-1 and t=1+(k-P+1)*7/(4*L-1) otherwise, for integer k from 0 to N-1. Weight w[k]=cos(pi*t) when t<=0.5 and cos(pi*t)/(3*pi*t+1) otherwise. The average is the sum of w[k]*input[k bars ago] over available bars divided by the sum of all N weights; if that denominator is not positive, use zero. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Nonlag inverse Fisher RSX > upper level.
Short: Nonlag inverse Fisher RSX < lower level.
