Calculation: Resistance = highest high over a lookback ending at a configurable bar offset on the selected timeframe, rounded to a configurable price increment. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or equal to it; offset zero uses the still-forming bar, while a positive offset uses completed bars.
Calculation: Support = lowest low over the same lookback and bar offset as resistance, rounded to the same price increment.
Long: Close crosses above resistance.
Short: Close crosses below support.
