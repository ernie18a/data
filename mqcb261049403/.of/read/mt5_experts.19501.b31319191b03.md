Short: ATR on the signal bar > ATR on the preceding bar AND ATR on the preceding bar < ATR two bars before the signal bar AND signal-bar close < signal-bar open
Exit: long, the most recent signal through the selected signal bar is short OR low <= entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance
Exit: short, the most recent signal through the selected signal bar is long OR high >= entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance
Calculation: Signal bar = a selected bar on the chart timeframe or on a selectable higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed bar by default, with a selectable older completed bar or the still-forming bar as alternatives.
