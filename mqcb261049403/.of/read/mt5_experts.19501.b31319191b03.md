Calculation: ATR local-minimum reversal = On the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed signal bar: ATR on the signal bar > ATR on the immediately preceding bar AND ATR on the immediately preceding bar < ATR two bars before the signal bar
Long: ATR local-minimum reversal AND close > open
Short: ATR local-minimum reversal AND close < open
Exit: long, ATR local-minimum reversal AND close < open
Exit: short, ATR local-minimum reversal AND close > open
