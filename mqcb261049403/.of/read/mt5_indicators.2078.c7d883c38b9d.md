Calculation: Adaptive Renko trend = On the first eligible bar after the volatility lookback, initialize upper and lower levels to close, or to high and low respectively if using the high/low price mode; initialize brick size to the greater of the volatility multiplier times their difference and the minimum brick size, and trend to neutral. On each bar, let high-price and low-price both be close in close-price mode, or high and low in high/low mode. Let new brick size be the greater of the volatility multiplier times current ATR or close standard deviation and the minimum brick size. If high-price > upper level + brick size, increase the upper level by floor((high-price - upper level) / brick size) times brick size, set brick size to new brick size, and set lower level to upper level - brick size. Then, if low-price < lower level - brick size, decrease the lower level by floor((lower level - low-price) / brick size) times brick size, set brick size to new brick size, and set upper level to lower level + brick size. If the upper level > its preceding-bar value, set trend to bullish; if the lower level < its preceding-bar value, set trend to bearish, overriding the bullish update. Otherwise retain the previous trend. Treat both preceding-bar levels as zero on the first eligible bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Adaptive Renko trend = Adaptive Renko trend calculated on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, and carried forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Adaptive Renko trend = bullish OR higher-timeframe Adaptive Renko trend = bullish
Short: Adaptive Renko trend = bearish OR higher-timeframe Adaptive Renko trend = bearish
