Calculation: Trailing trend = On the chart timeframe and, separately, on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar, maintain a trend, extreme price, and trailing level. Initialize trend to neutral and both prices to the close of the first eligible bar. On each bar, set distance to the mean high-minus-low over the lookback including that bar, multiplied by a price-scale parameter. If the prior trend is neutral or positive, set extreme price to the maximum of its prior value and close, and trailing level to the maximum of its prior value and extreme price × (1 − distance); if close is below that level, set extreme price to close, trailing level to close × (1 + distance), and trend to negative. If the prior trend is negative, set extreme price to the minimum of its prior value and close, and trailing level to the minimum of its prior value and extreme price × (1 + distance); if close is above that level, set extreme price to close, trailing level to close × (1 − distance), and trend to positive. Otherwise retain the prior trend. Recalculate the forming bar from the last completed bar's state.
Calculation: Up transition = Prior trailing trend < 0 AND current trailing trend > 0 AND low − 3/8 × ATR != 0.
Calculation: Down transition = Prior trailing trend > 0 AND current trailing trend < 0 AND high + 3/8 × ATR != 0.
Calculation: Displayed trend = The direction of the most recent up or down transition on the higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar; initially neutral.
Long: Chart-timeframe up transition OR higher-timeframe displayed trend > 0.
Short: Chart-timeframe down transition OR higher-timeframe displayed trend < 0.
