Calculation: Price inputs = three independently selected prices per bar, each chosen from open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, (open+close)/2, (high+close)/2 if close>open else (low+close)/2, or high if close>open else low if close<open else close. Each choice may instead use Heikin-Ashi OHLC in the same formulas. Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = for each bar take the maximum and minimum, respectively, of its three price inputs; over a common lookback, sort each resulting series ascending and calculate its quantile by linear interpolation at zero-based index (lookback-1)*quantile percentage/100. The upper band's quantile percentage is higher than the lower band's. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Band direction = up if the band is greater than its previous bar's value, down if less, and otherwise its previous direction; initially neutral. In higher-timeframe mode, compare with the previous higher-timeframe bar and carry its direction forward when equal.
Long: Upper band direction is up OR lower band direction is up.
Short: Upper band direction is down OR lower band direction is down.
