Calculation: Bar indexing = 1 is the last completed chart bar, 2 is the bar before it, and so on; evaluate entries at the open of each new chart bar. All price buffers, thresholds, lookbacks, counts, session hours, and risk multiples are parameters.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe levels = scan several higher timeframes, using completed bars only. For each adjacent older and newer pair, a bearish-to-bullish pair creates support at the average of the older close and newer open; a bullish-to-bearish pair creates resistance at the same average. A newer body entirely above the older body creates gap support at the midpoint between the two nearest body edges; a newer body entirely below creates gap resistance analogously. Merge same-side levels within a tolerance in scan order, retaining the first price and marking it as a gap if any merged level is a gap.
Calculation: Directional rejection = for support, bar 1 low <= level + touch buffer AND close > level AND close > open; for resistance, bar 1 high >= level - touch buffer AND close < level AND close < open.
Calculation: Directional sweep = for support, bar 1 low < the minimum low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars minus a buffer AND close > that minimum; for resistance, bar 1 high > the maximum high of those bars plus the buffer AND close < that maximum. The lookback starts at bar 2.
Calculation: Directional near misses = for support, count preceding bars starting at bar 2 whose lows are > level + touch buffer and <= level + a larger miss-distance threshold; for resistance, count bars whose highs are < level - touch buffer and >= level - that threshold.
Calculation: Directional engulfing = bullish if bar 2 is bearish and bar 1 is bullish with its body enclosing bar 2's body, OR bar 1 is bullish, its low < bar 2 low, and its close > bar 2's high-low midpoint; bearish is the inverse with highs and lows exchanged.
Calculation: Directional trendline proximity = find the two most recent strict pivot lows for support or pivot highs for resistance in a chart-bar search window starting several bars before bar 1, requiring each pivot to exceed all neighboring highs or be below all neighboring lows over a pivot lookback on both sides. Project their straight line to bar 1 using bar indices; proximity holds if its distance from bar 1 close OR the level is <= a buffer.
Calculation: Directional QML = compare adjacent equal-length older and newer chart-bar windows ending before bar 1: bullish if the newer minimum low < the older minimum low AND bar 1 close > the older maximum high; bearish if the newer maximum high > the older maximum high AND bar 1 close < the older minimum low.
Calculation: Directional breakout-retest = within a preceding chart-bar search window, an older bar opens below and closes above support AND bar 1 low <= level + touch buffer AND bar 1 close > level; for resistance, an older bar opens above and closes below it AND bar 1 high >= level - touch buffer AND bar 1 close < level.
Calculation: Directional higher-timeframe sweep = using the last completed bar and its predecessor on a higher timeframe, bullish if the latest low < the preceding low minus a buffer AND its close is strictly between the preceding low and high; bearish if its high > the preceding high plus the buffer AND its close is strictly between those levels.
Calculation: Setup layers = for each support or resistance level, respectively form directional layers: A = rejection; B = rejection AND sweep; C = rejection AND near-miss count >= a threshold; D = rejection AND engulfing; E = gap level AND rejection AND engulfing; F = rejection AND trendline proximity; G = rejection AND QML; H = rejection AND QML AND engulfing; I = breakout-retest AND trendline proximity; J = rejection AND higher-timeframe sweep. Group same-direction triggered layers whose level is within a zone buffer of a cluster's current representative, processing layers in the stated order and levels in scan order; update the representative to the average of its previous value and each added level, retain the minimum and maximum levels, and count each layer once.
Calculation: Recent range = maximum high and minimum low over a chart-bar lookback including bar 1; a narrow range has positive width <= a threshold. Expansion holds when a separate recent-range width >= its threshold OR the absolute body of bar 1 >= its threshold.
Calculation: Break-hold = compare each of the most recent validity-window chart bars with the same prior range calculated from a lookback beginning at bar 2: bullish if its absolute body >= a threshold AND its close > prior high + buffer AND its low >= prior high - buffer; bearish if its close < prior low - buffer AND its high <= prior low + buffer. The directional state is break-hold if any such bar qualifies, otherwise expansion if expansion holds, otherwise narrow-range if the range is narrow, otherwise normal.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range position = clamp (current chart-bar open - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low) to the interval from zero to one, using a higher-timeframe lookback ending at its last completed bar; use the midpoint if the range is invalid.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = compare the last completed close with an older close on each of two higher timeframes, one higher than the other. Bullish alignment requires both increases >= their respective thresholds. Bearish alignment requires the lower of the two higher timeframes to decrease by its threshold OR the higher to increase by its threshold; an alternative setting requires both to decrease by their thresholds.
Calculation: Momentum confirmations = directional prior-range breakout by bar 1 close beyond a buffer; bar 1 absolute body >= a threshold; bullish bar 1 low > bar 3 high or bearish bar 1 high < bar 3 low; bar 1 low holds above the prior high minus a buffer for bullish or its high holds below the prior low plus a buffer for bearish; and the change between the last completed higher-timeframe close and an older close exceeds a directional threshold. The prior chart range starts at bar 2.
Calculation: Reaction qualification = cluster layer count >= a threshold AND its layer set contains an enabled combination: A+D+E+G+H, A+C+D+E+J, A+C+D+F+I, A+C+F+I+J, A+B+C+D+J, or A+C+D+G+H. Exact matching may replace subset matching. Its state must be break-hold or expansion, and either break-hold holds OR higher-timeframe range position is <= the long threshold for support or >= the short threshold for resistance.
Calculation: Momentum qualification = state is expansion or break-hold AND momentum confirmation count >= a threshold AND the directional fair-value gap confirmation holds AND any configured hold requirement holds. In expansion, require breakout, displacement, gap, and higher-timeframe pressure; permit only exact layer sets A+G+J or A+G for resistance, or A+F, A+F+I, or A for support. In break-hold, require breakout, displacement, and gap, and require either higher-timeframe pressure or an exact layer set of A, A+G, A+D+E+G+H, A+D+E+G+H+J, A+C+F+I+J, or A+B+C+D+E+J. Exclude exact A+J in all states. Support momentum additionally requires bullish higher-timeframe alignment.
Calculation: Strong-trend breakout = bar 1 close breaks the prior chart-range high plus a buffer for bullish or low minus a buffer for bearish, its absolute body >= a threshold, and directional higher-timeframe alignment holds. Optionally require the directional fair-value gap. Bullish also requires higher-timeframe range position <= a threshold; an optional bearish filter requires it >= a threshold.
Calculation: Armed pullback = on a strong-trend breakout, save its prior-range boundary, breakout-bar high or low, and, if present, the midpoint of its fair-value gap. For a bounded number of subsequent chart bars and entries, update the saved high to the highest high since a bullish breakout or the saved low to the lowest low since a bearish breakout. A bullish pullback requires saved high minus bar 1 low within configured bounds, bar 1 bullish body >= a threshold, and either bar 1 low <= saved boundary + retest buffer AND close > boundary, OR low <= saved gap midpoint + gap buffer AND close > midpoint. The bearish version reverses highs, lows, and inequalities. A fresh breakout resets the corresponding arm.
Calculation: Initial stop = for a support cluster, choose the highest of cluster minimum level, minimum low in a preceding chart-bar lookback starting at bar 2, and bar 1 low, each minus a stop buffer, provided it is below entry; for a resistance cluster choose the lowest corresponding buffered maximum level, maximum high, and bar 1 high above entry. For strong-trend and armed-pullback trades, use the lower of bar 1 low and the preceding-lookback low minus the buffer for long, or the higher of bar 1 high and the preceding-lookback high plus the buffer for short. Enforce a minimum entry-to-stop distance and reject distances above the applicable maximum, with a separate expansion maximum for clusters and a separate maximum for trend trades.
Calculation: Target = select the nearest price beyond entry in the trade direction among the highs and lows of the last completed bars on two higher timeframes, the chart-timeframe recent high and low, and a further higher-timeframe recent high and low; recent extrema use lookbacks starting at bar 2 on their respective timeframes. Use that price if its distance from entry divided by initial risk meets the minimum reward-to-risk threshold; otherwise use entry plus or minus initial risk times the largest configured fallback reward multiple. An optional fixed reward multiple replaces this target for cluster trades.
Long: A support cluster qualifies for reaction OR momentum AND its initial stop distance and target reward-to-risk satisfy their limits AND, during a narrow range, the recent range-zone trade count remains below its limit.
Short: A resistance cluster qualifies for reaction OR momentum AND its initial stop distance and target reward-to-risk satisfy their limits AND, during a narrow range, the recent range-zone trade count remains below its limit.
Long: Bullish strong-trend breakout AND initial stop distance <= the trend-trade maximum.
Short: Bearish strong-trend breakout AND bar 1 high <= prior range low + breakout buffer AND immediate bearish trend entries are enabled AND initial stop distance <= the trend-trade maximum.
Long: Bullish armed pullback AND initial stop distance <= the trend-trade maximum.
Short: Bearish armed pullback AND bearish pullback entries are enabled AND initial stop distance <= the trend-trade maximum.
Long: Bar 1 absolute body >= the displacement threshold AND bar 1 close > open.
Short: Bar 1 absolute body >= the displacement threshold AND bar 1 close <= open.
Exit: long, price <= initial stop OR price >= target.
Exit: short, price >= initial stop OR price <= target.
Exit: long, price >= entry + initial risk times the partial-profit multiple AND a partial exit has not already occurred; close the configured fraction and, if enabled, move the remaining stop to entry plus the configured risk multiple and price offset.
Exit: short, price <= entry - initial risk times the partial-profit multiple AND a partial exit has not already occurred; close the configured fraction and, if enabled, move the remaining stop to entry minus the configured risk multiple and price offset.
Exit: long, after the partial exit and stop adjustment, price <= adjusted stop.
Exit: short, after the partial exit and stop adjustment, price >= adjusted stop.
