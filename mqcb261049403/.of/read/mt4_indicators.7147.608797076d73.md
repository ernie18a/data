Short: For a selected chart, higher, or lower timeframe, each shorter-period close EMA is less than or equal to the next longer-period close EMA AND at least one is strictly less; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming at the chart bar's close, or the last completed lower-timeframe bar within the chart bar.
