Calculation: RSI step line = For each bar, calculate RSI of the selected price. For each of two step sizes, fast smaller than slow, initialize the previous upper bound to negative infinity, previous lower bound to positive infinity, trend to zero, and line to zero. Set upper bound = RSI + 2 × step size and lower bound = RSI − 2 × step size. Set trend to +1 if RSI > previous upper bound, or to −1 if RSI < previous lower bound; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend = +1, set lower bound to the greater of its current and previous values and line = lower bound + step size. If trend = −1, set upper bound to the lesser of its current and previous values and line = upper bound − step size. Carry the bounds, trend, and line forward to the next bar. Calculate both lines on the chart timeframe and separately on a higher timeframe using the selected higher-timeframe bar, which may be still forming or completed.
