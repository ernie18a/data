Calculation: Relative Strength Index = RSI of typical price; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: Entry CCI = Commodity Channel Index of typical price with a shorter lookback than Trend CCI.
Calculation: Trend CCI = Commodity Channel Index of typical price with a longer lookback than Entry CCI.
Long: On any configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), Stochastic %K on the last completed bar > Stochastic %D on the last completed bar OR short-lookback RSI on the still-forming bar > long-lookback RSI on the last completed bar OR Entry CCI on the last completed bar > 0 AND Entry CCI on the last completed bar <= Entry CCI on the prior completed bar OR Entry CCI on the last completed bar < 0 AND Entry CCI on the last completed bar >= Entry CCI on the prior completed bar OR Trend CCI on the last completed bar > 0 AND Trend CCI on the last completed bar <= Trend CCI on the prior completed bar OR Trend CCI on the last completed bar < 0 AND Trend CCI on the last completed bar >= Trend CCI on the prior completed bar.
Short: On any configured timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), Stochastic %K on the last completed bar < Stochastic %D on the last completed bar OR long-lookback RSI on the last completed bar > short-lookback RSI on the still-forming bar OR Entry CCI on the last completed bar > 0 AND Entry CCI on the last completed bar > Entry CCI on the prior completed bar OR Entry CCI on the last completed bar < 0 AND Entry CCI on the last completed bar < Entry CCI on the prior completed bar OR Trend CCI on the last completed bar > 0 AND Trend CCI on the last completed bar > Trend CCI on the prior completed bar OR Trend CCI on the last completed bar < 0 AND Trend CCI on the last completed bar < Trend CCI on the prior completed bar.
