Calculation: TSI of WPR = 100 × twice-smoothed WPR momentum / twice-smoothed absolute WPR momentum; use zero when the denominator is zero, then replace zero with a small positive value. Smooth both series using the same selected method, first with a shorter smoothing length and then with a longer smoothing length.
Calculation: Signal line = the TSI of WPR smoothed using the same method and a longer smoothing length than either TSI smoothing length; replace zero with a small positive value.
Long: TSI of WPR > signal line.
Short: TSI of WPR < signal line.
Long: TSI of WPR computed on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > its signal line computed on the same bar.
