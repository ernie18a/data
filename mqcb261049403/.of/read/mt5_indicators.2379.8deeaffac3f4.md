Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period moving average of Money Flow Index using volume, calculated on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period moving average of the fast line, using the same averaging method and timeframe.
Long: Fast line > slow line OR fast line = slow line AND the most recent unequal fast and slow lines on the higher timeframe had fast line > slow line.
Short: Fast line < slow line OR fast line = slow line AND the most recent unequal fast and slow lines on the higher timeframe had fast line < slow line.
