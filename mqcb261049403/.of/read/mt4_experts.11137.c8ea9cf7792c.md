Calculation: Weighted price = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Calculation: Automatic profit distance = Bollinger Band width on weighted price, using the bar still forming on a separate timeframe, divided by one of three configured divisors selected according to two ordered width thresholds. The separate timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: On each of three distinct timeframes, the OsMA of weighted price on the bar still forming > its value on the last completed bar AND on at least two of those timeframes the simple moving average of close > the simple moving average of open on the bar still forming AND Williams %R on the bar still forming on a separate timeframe < an upper threshold AND weekday is Monday through Friday AND time is outside the configured interval after the start of the day AND elapsed time within the configured entry cycle > the configured delay AND no long position is open AND either hedging is enabled or no position is open AND no entry has occurred since the latest entry-cycle reset.
Short: On each of three distinct timeframes, the OsMA of weighted price on the bar still forming < its value on the last completed bar AND on at least two of those timeframes the simple moving average of close <= the simple moving average of open on the bar still forming AND Williams %R on the bar still forming on a separate timeframe > a lower threshold AND weekday is Monday through Friday AND time is outside the configured interval after the start of the day AND elapsed time within the configured entry cycle > the configured delay AND no short position is open AND either hedging is enabled or no position is open AND no entry has occurred since the latest entry-cycle reset.
Exit: long, automatic take profit is enabled AND close > entry price + either the configured minimum profit distance or the automatic profit distance AND high - close > the configured reversal distance.
Exit: long, manual take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + the configured take-profit distance.
Exit: long, manual stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price - the configured stop-loss distance.
Exit: long, automatic stop loss is enabled AND low <= the low of the bar still forming on a separate timeframe at entry - the configured stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, manual stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price + the configured stop-loss distance.
