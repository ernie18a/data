Calculation: Stepped channel = For each bar, calculate simple moving averages of high, low, and close over the same smoothing period, called H, L, and C. Let B be the volatility estimate multiplied by a scale parameter. Update the stepped high to H if H - B exceeds its previous value, to H + B if H + B is below its previous value, and otherwise retain its previous value. Update the stepped low to L if L + B is below its previous value, to L - B if L - B exceeds its previous value, and otherwise retain its previous value. Update the stepped close to C - B/2 if C - B/2 exceeds its previous value, to C + B/2 if C + B/2 is below its previous value, and otherwise retain its previous value. The upper, lower, and median channel values are simple moving averages over the same smoothing period of stepped high + B/2, stepped low - B/2, and (stepped high + stepped low + 2 × stepped close)/4, respectively. Initialize stepped high and low from the corresponding bar prices and stepped close to zero.
Calculation: Close dispersion = Square root of the mean, over a dispersion lookback, of squared differences between each close and the current bar's smoothed moving average of close over that lookback.
Calculation: Bounce strength = For each bar k in a bounce lookback ending at the current bar i, let spread be high[k] - low[k], and let R be the current upper channel minus the current lower channel, expressed in price points. Let G(x) be zero when x or R is zero, and otherwise exp(-b × c^x), where b and c are Gompertz parameters and x is expressed in price points. With volume weighting disabled, weight is 1. With it enabled and the volume baseline at k positive, weight is min(1, sqrt(volume[k]/volume baseline[k])) when volume[k] is positive; when volume[k] is zero and the previous volume is positive, use min(1, sqrt(volume[k-1]/volume baseline[k-1])); otherwise weight is 1. Sum (close[k] - low[k]) × G(median channel[k] - low[k]) × weight for bars where close[k] >= close[k-1] - spread × a spread-fraction parameter to obtain P. Sum (high[k] - close[k]) × G(high[k] - median channel[k]) × weight for bars where close[k] <= close[k-1] + spread × the same spread-fraction parameter to obtain N. Bounce strength is (P - N) × abs((P - N)/(P + N)) when P + N is nonzero, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Histogram bounce strength = Bounce strength using a stepped channel whose volatility estimate is the simple moving average of close dispersion over a volatility lookback and whose volume baseline is the simple moving average of volume over that same lookback. The dispersion lookback is shorter than the volatility lookback.
Calculation: Histogram main = Simple moving average of histogram bounce strength over a fast averaging period.
Calculation: Histogram slow = Simple moving average of histogram main over a separate slow averaging period.
Calculation: Trend-line bounce strength = Bounce strength using a stepped channel whose volatility estimate is the sum of close dispersion over the oldest portion of a longer volatility window, through the bar at the start of a shorter volatility window, divided by the shorter volatility period. Its volume baseline is the sum of volume over that same portion divided by the shorter volatility period. The dispersion lookback is shorter than either volatility window.
Calculation: Trend line = Linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close)/3 plus the simple moving average of trend-line bounce strength over a fast averaging period.
Long: Histogram main > 0 AND histogram main > histogram slow
Short: Histogram main < 0 AND histogram main < histogram slow
Long: Trend line > max(current linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close)/3, previous linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close)/3) AND trend line > previous trend line
Short: Trend line < min(current linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close)/3, previous linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close)/3) AND trend line < previous trend line
