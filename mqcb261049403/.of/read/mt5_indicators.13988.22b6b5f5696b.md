Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3; typical-price LWMA = linearly weighted moving average of typical price.
Calculation: BSI = (P − N) × |(P − N) / (P + N)|, or 0 if P + N = 0. Across the BSI lookback, P sums close−low for bars not down by more than 20% of their range, weighted by the Gompertz ratio of the bar's distance from the lower side to the channel width; N sums high−close for bars not up by more than 20% of their range, weighted by the Gompertz ratio of the bar's distance from the upper side to the channel width. Apply the tick-volume square-root weight, capped at 1, to each contribution when volume weighting is enabled. The channel width and median are moving averages of the upper and lower step-channel lines and the median step-channel line. Each step channel uses moving-average high, low, and close; a volatility-scaled step that moves each prior high, low, and close line only when the corresponding moving average crosses its prior line by more than the volatility offset; then upper/lower lines offset the high/low lines by half the offset, and the median is the average of high line, low line, and twice the close line. Volatility is the moving average of rolling standard deviations of close.
Calculation: Main = typical-price LWMA + the simple moving average of BSI; its BSI lookback is shorter than the LWMA lookback.
Calculation: Slow median = the wider, slower step-channel median line, using a longer volatility lookback and larger volatility scale than the channel used for BSI.
Long: Main > max(current typical-price LWMA, previous typical-price LWMA) AND Main > previous Main AND (previous state is uptrend OR (previous state is not downtrend AND slow median now > slow median three bars ago)).
Short: Main < min(current typical-price LWMA, previous typical-price LWMA) AND Main < previous Main AND (previous state is downtrend OR (previous state is not uptrend AND slow median now < slow median three bars ago)).
Exit: long, neither the Long nor Short condition is true.
Exit: short, neither the Long nor Short condition is true.
