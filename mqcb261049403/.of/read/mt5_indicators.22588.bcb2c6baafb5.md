Calculation: Oscillator = Let power = high + low − 2 × EMA(close). Starting with L0 = L1 = L2 = L3 = 0, update in order: L0 = (1 − gamma) × power + gamma × previous L0; L1 = −gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1; L2 = −gamma × L1 + previous L1 + gamma × previous L2; L3 = −gamma × L2 + previous L2 + gamma × previous L3. Let up = sum of max(L0 − L1, 0), max(L1 − L2, 0), and max(L2 − L3, 0); down = sum of max(L1 − L0, 0), max(L2 − L1, 0), and max(L3 − L2, 0). The oscillator is the selected moving average of volume × (100 × up / (up + down) − 50), using zero for the ratio when up + down = 0.
Calculation: Zone = 0 if oscillator > upper outer level × moving average of volume; otherwise 1 if oscillator > upper inner level × moving average of volume; otherwise 4 if oscillator < lower outer level × moving average of volume; otherwise 3 if oscillator < lower inner level × moving average of volume; otherwise 2. Use the same moving average method and parameters as for the oscillator. The upper outer level is above the upper inner level, and the lower outer level is below the lower inner level.
Calculation: Direction = 1 when oscillator > previous oscillator, −1 when oscillator < previous oscillator, and otherwise the previous direction.
Long: Direction = 1 OR (previous oscillator <= 0 AND oscillator > 0) OR (previous zone > 1 AND zone = 1) OR (previous zone > 0 AND zone = 0) OR (previous zone = 3 AND zone < 3) OR (previous zone = 4 AND zone < 4)
Short: Direction = −1 OR (previous oscillator >= 0 AND oscillator < 0) OR (previous zone < 3 AND zone = 3) OR (previous zone < 4 AND zone = 4) OR (previous zone = 1 AND zone > 1) OR (previous zone = 0 AND zone > 0)
