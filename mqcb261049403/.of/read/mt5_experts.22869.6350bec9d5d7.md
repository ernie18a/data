Calculation: Volume-normalized oscillator = smooth(volume × score) / smooth(volume), where score = 100 × (P − (moving average of P over the longer lookback − smoothed high−low range over the shorter lookback)) / (2 × smoothed high−low range) − 50; P is the selected bar price, and use 1 when the smoothed range is zero. Both averages and smoothing use completed bars on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; the price lookback is longer than the range lookback, and the numerator and denominator use the same smoothing lookback.
Long: current oscillator > strong overbought level AND previous completed oscillator <= strong overbought level OR current oscillator > weak overbought level AND current oscillator <= strong overbought level AND previous completed oscillator <= weak overbought level
Short: current oscillator < strong oversold level AND previous completed oscillator >= strong oversold level OR current oscillator < weak oversold level AND current oscillator >= strong oversold level AND previous completed oscillator >= weak oversold level
Exit: long, current oscillator < weak oversold level AND previous completed oscillator >= weak oversold level OR current oscillator < strong oversold level AND previous completed oscillator >= strong oversold level OR bar low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, current oscillator > weak overbought level AND previous completed oscillator <= weak overbought level OR current oscillator > strong overbought level AND previous completed oscillator <= strong overbought level OR bar high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR bar low <= entry price − take-profit distance
