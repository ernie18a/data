Calculation: Oscillator and bands = On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, use close as price, a longer-lookback SMA of price as the center, and a shorter-lookback Jurik moving average of high minus low as the range. Set the lower band to center minus range; if range is zero, use one only as the divisor. Multiply (100 × (price − lower band) / (2 × range) − 50) by volume, then apply a Jurik moving average. Apply a Jurik moving average with the same smoothing parameters to volume. Multiply that smoothed volume by two positive thresholds and two negative thresholds to obtain the outer upper, inner upper, inner lower, and outer lower bands, respectively.
Calculation: Zone = For each higher-timeframe bar, assign 4 if the oscillator is above the outer upper band; otherwise 3 if above the inner upper band; otherwise 0 if below the outer lower band; otherwise 1 if below the inner lower band; otherwise 2.
Long: (Last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 4 AND preceding higher-timeframe bar zone < 4) OR (last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 3 AND preceding higher-timeframe bar zone < 3)
Short: (Last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 0 AND preceding higher-timeframe bar zone > 0) OR (last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 1 AND preceding higher-timeframe bar zone > 1)
Exit: long, last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 0 OR last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 1 OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 3 OR last completed higher-timeframe bar zone = 4 OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
