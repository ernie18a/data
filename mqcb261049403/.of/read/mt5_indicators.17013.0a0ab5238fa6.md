Calculation: Selected price = a selectable open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint price, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi close is (open + high + low + close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open is the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close midpoint, initialized to (open + close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high and low are the maximum and minimum of high, low, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close.
Calculation: Velocity = the selected price's weighted mean over the velocity lookback with weights (lookback - bar age)^2 minus its weighted mean with weights (lookback - bar age), divided by the selected normalization divisor. The divisor is selectable as 1, the population or sample standard deviation of selected price over the velocity lookback, or the sum of true ranges over that lookback divided by the full lookback length; the first bar's true range is high + low. If the divisor is zero, leave the difference undivided. Use available bars when history is shorter than the lookback. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Floating level = the minimum velocity over the floating-level lookback plus a selectable fraction of the difference between the maximum and minimum velocity over that lookback, including the current bar. The upper fraction is higher than the lower fraction; the middle fraction is halfway between the minimum and maximum. Use available bars when history is shorter than the lookback.
Long: Velocity > upper floating level, OR velocity > middle floating level, OR velocity > previous velocity, according to the selected coloring mode.
Short: Velocity < lower floating level, OR velocity < middle floating level, OR velocity < previous velocity, according to the selected coloring mode.
