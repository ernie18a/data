Calculation: Step trend = On either chart bars or higher-timeframe bars, process bars chronologically, starting with the first bar having at least lookback plus two older bars. For each bar, calculate a step as either a constant step size times a sensitivity factor, or the rounded-to-nearest-integer value of sensitivity factor times the mean of the highest and lowest volatility values observed since processing began, divided by the price-point size. Volatility is the sum of high minus low over the lookback divided by the lookback; optionally multiply each range by 1 + its age-adjusted rank divided by the lookback, where the oldest rank is 1 and the current rank is the lookback. Convert the step to price units by multiplying by the price-point size. Skip bars whose step is zero. On the first processed bar initialize trend to zero, upper boundary to low plus twice the step, and lower boundary to high minus twice the step. On each processed bar set candidate upper and lower boundaries to either close plus and minus twice the step, respectively, or low plus twice the step and high minus twice the step, respectively. Set trend to positive if close exceeds the previous upper boundary; then set it to negative if close is below the previous lower boundary; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is positive, replace the candidate lower boundary with the maximum of itself and the previous lower boundary; otherwise replace the candidate upper boundary with the minimum of itself and the previous upper boundary. Save both candidates as the boundaries for the next processed bar. For the higher-timeframe variant, use the higher-timeframe bar still forming and evaluate its signal only on the first chart bar in that higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Step trend is positive AND previous processed Step trend is negative.
Short: Step trend is negative AND previous processed Step trend is positive.
