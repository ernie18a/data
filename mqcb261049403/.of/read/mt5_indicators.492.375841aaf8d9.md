Calculation: Daily pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed daily bar, where daily is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Daily resistance 2 = daily pivot + high - low of the last completed daily bar, where daily is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Daily support 2 = daily pivot - high + low of the last completed daily bar, where daily is higher than the chart timeframe
Long: open of the daily bar still forming, where daily is higher than the chart timeframe, > daily pivot
Short: open of the daily bar still forming, where daily is higher than the chart timeframe, < daily pivot
Exit: long, high >= daily resistance 2
Exit: short, low <= daily support 2
