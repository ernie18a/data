Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period moving average of the Accelerator Oscillator; use a selectable smoothing method.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period moving average of the fast line, using the same smoothing method.
Long: On the chart timeframe, fast line > slow line.
Short: On the chart timeframe, fast line < slow line.
Long: On a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's fast line > slow line AND the preceding bar's fast line <= slow line.
Short: On a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's fast line < slow line AND the preceding bar's fast line >= slow line.
Exit: long, on a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's fast line < slow line.
Exit: short, on a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's fast line > slow line.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
