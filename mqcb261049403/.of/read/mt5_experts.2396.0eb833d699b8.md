Calculation: Slow line = smooth the Fast line on the chart timeframe using a longer lookback; use completed bars.
Long: Fast line on the latest completed bar > Slow line on the latest completed bar AND Fast line on the prior completed bar <= Slow line on the prior completed bar
Short: Fast line on the latest completed bar < Slow line on the latest completed bar AND Fast line on the prior completed bar >= Slow line on the prior completed bar

Exit: long, Fast line on the latest completed bar < Slow line on the latest completed bar
