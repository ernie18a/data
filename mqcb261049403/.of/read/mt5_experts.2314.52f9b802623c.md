Long: On the configured signal timeframe, for each of N consecutive bars starting with the last completed bar, the bar’s high is greater than the immediately older bar’s high AND its low is greater than the immediately older bar’s low
Short: On the configured signal timeframe, for each of N consecutive bars starting with the last completed bar, the bar’s high is less than the immediately older bar’s high AND its low is less than the immediately older bar’s low
Exit: long, the most recent signal is Short
Exit: short, the most recent signal is Long
