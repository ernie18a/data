Calculation: StochMain = 100 * SUM(close - LowestLow(K lookback), s) / SUM(HighestHigh(K lookback) - LowestLow(K lookback), s), where s is a short smoothing window (the slowing period) and the lookback uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: TrailStop = a stored stop price. Fixed profit levels L1<L2<L3 are distances from entry, and lock offset C is a constant smaller than L1. For a long position, on each evaluation where price >= entry + L1, let Lk be the highest level with price >= entry + Lk, and set TrailStop = price - (Lk - C), overwriting the previous value. For a short position, on each evaluation where price <= entry - L1, let Lk be the highest level with price <= entry - Lk, and set TrailStop = price + (Lk + C), overwriting the previous value. If no level is reached, TrailStop keeps its last value, or stays unset if it has never been set.
Long: no open position AND DeMarker(previous bar) < lower_threshold AND DeMarker(latest bar) >= lower_threshold AND StochMain(previous bar) < stoch_lower_threshold AND StochMain(latest bar) >= stoch_lower_threshold. Both indicators use the chart timeframe. Latest bar is the bar still forming, previous bar is the last completed bar.
Short: no open position AND DeMarker(previous bar) > upper_threshold AND DeMarker(latest bar) <= upper_threshold AND StochMain(previous bar) > stoch_upper_threshold AND StochMain(latest bar) <= stoch_upper_threshold. Both indicators use the chart timeframe. Latest bar is the bar still forming, previous bar is the last completed bar.
Exit: long, price <= entry - stop_distance OR price >= entry + take_distance OR (TrailStop is set AND price <= TrailStop). take_distance > stop_distance, and take_distance > L3. Price is the sell-side price (bid).
Exit: short, price >= entry + stop_distance OR price <= entry - take_distance OR (TrailStop is set AND price >= TrailStop). take_distance > stop_distance, and take_distance > L3. Price is the buy-side price (ask).
