Calculation: Normalized deviation = let price be the selected OHLC-derived price, deviation = price − Jurik moving average of price, and numerator and denominator be independently calculated T3 moving averages of deviation and absolute deviation with the same parameters; normalized deviation = 100 × numerator / denominator if numerator ≠ 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Step line = for each bar and step size, set upper = normalized deviation + 2 × step size and lower = normalized deviation − 2 × step size. Initialize previous upper to negative infinity, previous lower to positive infinity, and trend to 0. Set trend to +1 if normalized deviation > previous upper, or −1 if normalized deviation < previous lower; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend > 0, set lower = max(lower, previous lower) and line = lower + step size. If trend < 0, set upper = min(upper, previous upper) and line = upper − step size. Save upper and lower for the next bar. Calculate separate fast and slow lines using a smaller step size for the fast line and the same normalized deviation for both.
Long: Fast step line > slow step line on the chart bar OR fast step line > slow step line on the last completed bar of a selected higher or lower timeframe.
Short: Fast step line < slow step line on the chart bar OR fast step line < slow step line on the last completed bar of a selected higher or lower timeframe.
