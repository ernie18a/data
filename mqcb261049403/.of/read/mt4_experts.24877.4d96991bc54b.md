Calculation: Trailing levels = At entry, set the long stop and target to entry-bar open minus the stop distance and plus the target distance; set the short stop and target to entry-bar open plus the stop distance and minus the target distance. At each subsequent chart-bar open, first move the stop to entry price plus the break-even offset for a long, or minus the offset for a short, if break-even is enabled, favorable movement from entry exceeds its activation distance, and the existing stop is below entry for a long or above entry for a short. Then, if candle trailing is enabled, raise the long stop to the lowest low over the trailing lookback including the current forming bar minus the padding, or lower the short stop to the highest high over that lookback plus the padding, only if this improves the stop; whenever it improves, increase the long target or decrease the short target by the trailing distance. Otherwise, if candle trailing is disabled and favorable movement from entry exceeds the trailing activation distance, raise the long stop to the current open minus the trailing distance or lower the short stop to the current open plus the trailing distance, only if this improves the stop.
Long: At chart-bar open, absolute difference between open and the second prior bar's close > entry distance AND shorter-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND the second prior bar's low < the prior bar's high.
Short: At chart-bar open, absolute difference between open and the prior bar's close > entry distance AND shorter-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price < longer-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND the prior bar's low < the second prior bar's high.
Exit: Long, bar low <= long stop OR bar high >= long target.
Exit: Short, bar high >= short stop OR bar low <= short target.
