Long: On a new chart bar, the last completed bar's longer-lookback stochastic signal line is below its shorter-lookback stochastic main line AND the last completed bar's close-based RSI is above its midpoint AND the forming bar's shorter-period linearly weighted moving average of typical price is above its longer-period counterpart AND the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high AND the absolute difference between the current open and the close two bars ago is above a price-distance threshold.
Exit: long, the low reaches or falls below the entry bar's open minus the stop-loss distance OR the high reaches or exceeds the entry bar's open plus the stop-loss distance multiplied by the reward-to-risk ratio.
