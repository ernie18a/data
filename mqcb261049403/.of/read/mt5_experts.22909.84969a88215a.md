Calculation: XFisher main line = Smooth the Fisher transform of a recursively smoothed normalized applied price on the configured indicator timeframe. Normalize the applied price within the rolling high-low range over a lookback; update the normalized value as (normalized price − 0.5) + 0.67 × its prior value; transform it as 0.5 × ln((1 + value) / (1 − value)) + 0.5 × its prior Fisher value, replacing a negative transform ratio with 1. Use the selected smoothing method and lookback. Use completed bars; the indicator timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: XFisher signal line = XFisher main line from the preceding bar on the configured indicator timeframe.
Long: XFisher main line on the latest completed bar > XFisher signal line on that bar AND XFisher main line on the preceding bar <= XFisher signal line on that bar
Short: XFisher main line on the latest completed bar < XFisher signal line on that bar AND XFisher main line on the preceding bar >= XFisher signal line on that bar
Exit: long, XFisher main line on the latest completed bar < XFisher signal line on that bar AND XFisher main line on the preceding bar >= XFisher signal line on that bar OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, XFisher main line on the latest completed bar > XFisher signal line on that bar AND XFisher main line on the preceding bar <= XFisher signal line on that bar OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
