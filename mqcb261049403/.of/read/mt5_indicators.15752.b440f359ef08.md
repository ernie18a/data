Calculation: RSI MACD = smooth the RSI of bar prices with a shorter lookback, then subtract a smoother with a longer lookback applied to that result
Calculation: Signal line = smooth the RSI MACD
Long: RSI MACD > 0 OR RSI MACD > the previous bar’s signal line
Short: RSI MACD < 0 OR RSI MACD < the previous bar’s signal line
