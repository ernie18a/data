Calculation: Adaptive line = For a selected price series, calculate its moving average M and the root mean squared deviation S of the last moving-average-lookback prices from the current M. Let P be the previous adaptive line, initialized to M through the warm-up period. Set D = (P - M)^2; set the adaptive line to P if D = 0 or D < S^2, otherwise set it to P + (1 - S^2 / D) * (M - P).
Calculation: Line direction = Up when the adaptive line rises, down when it falls, and unchanged from the previous direction when it is flat.
Calculation: Filtered change = Round the current adaptive-line change and each of two separate multipliers of the population standard deviation of the most recent line changes to the chosen price precision. The multipliers are the lower and higher thresholds.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe values = Calculate the adaptive line, line direction, and filtered change on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar. Sample them on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and hold the sampled values until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe line direction = up OR chart-timeframe filtered change > lower threshold AND chart-timeframe filtered change <= higher threshold OR chart-timeframe filtered change > higher threshold OR higher-timeframe line direction = up OR higher-timeframe filtered change > lower threshold AND higher-timeframe filtered change <= higher threshold OR higher-timeframe filtered change > higher threshold
