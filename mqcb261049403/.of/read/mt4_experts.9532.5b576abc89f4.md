Long: MACD main line (fast EMA period shorter than slow EMA period, close price) of the last completed bar > MACD main line of the bar before it AND Williams %R of the last completed bar < upper threshold (upper threshold is higher than the Short lower threshold) AND no open position or pending order; entry triggers when price trades above (high of the last completed bar + small offset), placed at least a minimum distance above current price; the trigger level is moved down to the new last-completed-bar high + offset whenever that high < the high of the bar before it; cancel the untriggered entry if MACD main line of the last completed bar < MACD main line of the bar before it
Short: MACD main line (same definition as Long) of the last completed bar < MACD main line of the bar before it AND Williams %R of the last completed bar > lower threshold AND no open position or pending order; entry triggers when price trades below (low of the last completed bar - small offset), placed at least a minimum distance below current price; the trigger level is moved up to the new last-completed-bar low - offset whenever that low > the low of the bar before it; cancel the untriggered entry if MACD main line of the last completed bar > MACD main line of the bar before it
Exit: long, price <= (low of the signal bar - small offset, where the signal bar is the last completed bar when the entry level was last set) OR price <= trailing stop (once price - entry price > trail distance, trailing stop = price - trail distance, only ever raised) OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, price >= (high of the signal bar + small offset, where the signal bar is the last completed bar when the entry level was last set) OR price >= trailing stop (once entry price - price > trail distance, trailing stop = price + trail distance, only ever lowered) OR price <= entry price - take-profit distance
