Calculation: Bullish crossover = shorter-period moving average of close was strictly below the longer-period moving average on the previous bar and is strictly above it on the current bar, AND RSI of close is above 50 if RSI is enabled.
Calculation: Bearish crossover = shorter-period moving average of close was strictly above the longer-period moving average on the previous bar and is strictly below it on the current bar, AND RSI of close is below 50 if RSI is enabled.
Calculation: Pending confirmation = initially none. When the confirmation distance is nonzero, a bullish crossover stores the current low as a pending bullish level and clears any pending bearish level; a bearish crossover stores the current high as a pending bearish level and clears any pending bullish level. After the crossover updates on each bar, clear a pending bullish level whenever high minus that level is at least the confirmation distance and close is at most high; clear a pending bearish level whenever that level minus low is at least the confirmation distance and open is at least close. These checks also apply on the crossover bar and clear the level even if the flat-distance filter blocks the signal.
Calculation: Previous signal prices = initialize the previous long-signal low and previous short-signal high separately to zero; update each only when its corresponding signal passes the flat-distance filter.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe trend = for each configured chart or higher timeframe, sample its signal on the first chart bar of each corresponding timeframe bar, using that timeframe's bar still forming. Set the trend to long if its sampled long signal is present, otherwise to short if its sampled short signal is present; otherwise retain the previous trend. Retain that trend on subsequent chart bars until the next sample.
Long: (Bullish crossover AND confirmation distance equals zero OR pending bullish level exists AND high minus pending bullish level is at least the confirmation distance AND close is at most high) AND (low is at least previous long-signal low plus flat distance OR low is at most previous long-signal low minus flat distance).
Short: (Bearish crossover AND confirmation distance equals zero OR pending bearish level exists AND pending bearish level minus low is at least the confirmation distance AND open is at least close) AND (high is at least previous short-signal high plus flat distance OR high is at most previous short-signal high minus flat distance).
Long: Multi-timeframe trend equals long.
Short: Multi-timeframe trend equals short.
