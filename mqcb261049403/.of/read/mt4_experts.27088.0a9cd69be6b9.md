Calculation: Directional vote counts = count the following true conditions separately for long and short, using chart bars at the first tick of a new bar: long: last completed-bar OsMA of open > 0; MACD main of close > signal AND main != 0; forming-bar CCI of typical price > its previous value AND < a positive threshold; short-period linear-weighted MA of typical price > longer-period linear-weighted MA, both on the last completed bar; last completed-bar ATR > forming-bar ATR; forming-bar stochastic main >= an upper threshold. Short: last completed-bar OsMA of open < 0; MACD main of close < signal AND main != 0; forming-bar CCI of typical price < its previous value AND >= the negative of the CCI threshold; short-period linear-weighted MA of typical price < longer-period linear-weighted MA, both on the last completed bar; last completed-bar ATR < forming-bar ATR; forming-bar stochastic main <= a lower threshold. Both MACD lines use the last completed bar.
Long: at the first tick of a new chart bar, long vote count > short vote count AND last completed-bar short-period EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND last completed-bar RSI of close > its midpoint AND forming-bar short-period linear-weighted MA of typical price > longer-period linear-weighted MA of typical price AND low two bars ago < last completed-bar high AND absolute difference between current open and close two bars ago > a price-distance threshold
Exit: long, all six short-group forming-bar EMAs of typical price with increasing periods are equal OR all six longer-group forming-bar EMAs of typical price with increasing periods are equal OR last completed-bar close <= last completed-bar lower Bollinger Band
Exit: short, all six short-group forming-bar EMAs of typical price with increasing periods are equal OR all six longer-group forming-bar EMAs of typical price with increasing periods are equal OR last completed-bar close <= last completed-bar lower Bollinger Band
