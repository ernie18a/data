Calculation: Upper breakout = highest high of the current and preceding lookback one-minute bars, plus an offset
Calculation: Lower breakout = lowest low of the current and preceding lookback one-minute bars, minus an offset
Long: price >= Upper breakout
Short: price <= Lower breakout
Exit: long, price <= stop-loss price OR price >= take-profit price
Exit: short, price >= stop-loss price OR price <= take-profit price
