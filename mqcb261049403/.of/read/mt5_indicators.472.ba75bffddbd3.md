Calculation: Pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Upper exit levels = Pivot + (high - low) and 2 × Pivot + high - 2 × low, using the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Lower exit levels = Pivot - (high - low) and 2 × Pivot - 2 × high + low, using the high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Open of the forming higher-timeframe bar > Pivot.
Short: Open of the forming higher-timeframe bar < Pivot.
Exit: long, high >= either upper exit level.
Exit: short, low <= either lower exit level.
