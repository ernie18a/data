Calculation: Average direction = the sign of the latest nonzero change in the rounded double-smoothed average; retain the preceding direction while the average is unchanged.
Long: Momentum filter > its previous-bar value.
Short: Momentum filter < its previous-bar value.
Long: Average direction > 0.
Short: Average direction < 0.
Long: At the close of a fast-timeframe bar, its momentum filter > its previous value AND its previous momentum-filter change <= 0 AND the average direction on the last completed slow-timeframe bar > 0.
Short: At the close of a fast-timeframe bar, its momentum filter < its previous value AND its previous momentum-filter change >= 0 AND the average direction on the last completed slow-timeframe bar < 0.
Calculation: Fast and slow timeframes = independently configurable timeframes, with the slow timeframe higher than the fast timeframe; each can be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
