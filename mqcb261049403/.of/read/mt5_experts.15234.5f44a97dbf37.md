Calculation: Signal bars = bars on a configurable timeframe that may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower; evaluate either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar according to the selected signal-bar setting. All indicator inputs below use that timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish setup = RSI is disabled OR (shorter-period shifted moving average of close was strictly below the longer-period shifted moving average on the previous signal bar AND is strictly above it on the current signal bar AND RSI of close is above its midpoint).
Calculation: Bearish setup = RSI is disabled OR (shorter-period shifted moving average of close was strictly above the longer-period shifted moving average on the previous signal bar AND is strictly below it on the current signal bar AND RSI of close is below its midpoint). Both moving averages use the same configurable method and shift.
Calculation: Pending confirmation = initially neither direction; with a nonzero confirmation distance, a bullish setup stores the current low as the pending bullish reference and clears the bearish reference, then a bearish setup stores the current high as the pending bearish reference and clears the bullish reference. On each signal bar, a bullish confirmation occurs when a bullish reference exists AND high minus that reference is at least the confirmation distance AND close is at most high; a bearish confirmation occurs when a bearish reference exists AND that reference minus low is at least the confirmation distance AND open is at least close. Clear the relevant reference after its confirmation, even if the flat-distance filter rejects the signal. A setup may confirm on the same bar.
Calculation: Bullish signal = ((confirmation distance is zero AND bullish setup) OR (confirmation distance is nonzero AND bullish confirmation)) AND absolute difference between the signal bar low and the low of the previous accepted bullish signal is at least the flat distance. Initialize the previous accepted bullish low to zero; update it only when a bullish signal is accepted.
Calculation: Bearish signal = ((confirmation distance is zero AND bearish setup) OR (confirmation distance is nonzero AND bearish confirmation)) AND absolute difference between the signal bar high and the high of the previous accepted bearish signal is at least the flat distance. Initialize the previous accepted bearish high to zero; update it only when a bearish signal is accepted.
Long: Bullish signal = true.
Short: Bearish signal = true.
Exit: long, bearish signal = true OR (no current signal AND the most recent earlier accepted signal is bearish) OR low <= entry price minus enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, bullish signal = true OR (no current signal AND the most recent earlier accepted signal is bullish) OR high >= entry price plus enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus enabled take-profit distance.
