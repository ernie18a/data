Calculation: Fast moving average = moving average of close on the indicator timeframe, using the configured method and shorter lookback; evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: Slow moving average = moving average of close on the indicator timeframe, using the configured method and longer lookback; evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close on the indicator timeframe; evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: Bullish marker = when the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average and RSI is above 50 (if the RSI filter is enabled), save that bar's low as the pending bullish level. Emit a bullish marker immediately if the confirmation threshold is zero; otherwise emit it on the first bar where high is at least the pending level plus the confirmation threshold and close is at or below high. Emit only if the marker bar's low differs from the previous bullish marker low by at least the configured flat threshold.
Calculation: Bearish marker = when the fast moving average crosses below the slow moving average and RSI is below 50 (if the RSI filter is enabled), save that bar's high as the pending bearish level. Emit a bearish marker immediately if the confirmation threshold is zero; otherwise emit it on the first bar where low is at least the confirmation threshold below the pending level and open is at or above close. Emit only if the marker bar's high differs from the previous bearish marker high by at least the configured flat threshold.
Long: Bullish marker appears on the lower indicator timeframe's last completed bar.
Short: Bearish marker appears on the lower indicator timeframe's last completed bar.
Exit: long, bearish marker appears on the lower indicator timeframe's last completed bar OR low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance.
Exit: short, bullish marker appears on the lower indicator timeframe's last completed bar OR high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance.
