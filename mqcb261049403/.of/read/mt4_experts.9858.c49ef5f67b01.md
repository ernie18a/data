Calculation: Slope Direction Line = weighted moving average of [2 × weighted moving average of price over a shorter lookback − weighted moving average of price over a longer lookback], using a smoothing lookback shorter than the longer lookback
Long: Slope Direction Line on the current forming chart bar > its value on the previous completed chart bar AND its value on the previous completed chart bar < its value two bars back
Short: Slope Direction Line on the current forming chart bar < its value on the previous completed chart bar AND its value on the previous completed chart bar > its value two bars back
Long: Slope Direction Line on the last completed chart bar > its value on the preceding completed chart bar AND Slope Direction Line on the last completed bar of each higher timeframe > its value on that timeframe’s preceding completed bar AND RSI on the current chart bar and current forming bars of each higher timeframe is > 50 AND < 90
Short: Slope Direction Line on the last completed chart bar < its value on the preceding completed chart bar AND Slope Direction Line on the last completed bar of each higher timeframe < its value on that timeframe’s preceding completed bar AND RSI on the current chart bar and current forming bars of each higher timeframe is < 50 AND > 10
Exit: long, low <= entry price − ATR from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe OR high >= entry price + ATR from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe OR low <= entry price − ATR from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
