Calculation: Asymmetric stochastic = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming for the higher timeframe, sum the closes over the slowing lookback. For each of those bars, also sum the highest high over the upper lookback and the lowest low over the lower lookback; alternatively, use the highest and lowest closes. Let range be the difference between those sums of highs and lows. If range is less than the sensitivity threshold in price units, set the range to that threshold and reduce the sum of lows by half the difference. The oscillator is 100 times (sum of closes minus adjusted sum of lows) divided by the adjusted range; if the range is zero, use the invalid oscillator value. Initialize both lookbacks to the shorter lookback. After calculating each bar, if the previous bar's smoothed oscillator is above the upper threshold, set the upper lookback to the shorter and the lower to the longer; if it is below the lower threshold, set the upper lookback to the longer and the lower to the shorter; otherwise retain them.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = moving average of the asymmetric stochastic, using the same timeframe and bar as the oscillator.
Long: Asymmetric stochastic > smoothed oscillator OR (asymmetric stochastic > oscillator midpoint AND asymmetric stochastic != previous oscillator value)
Short: Asymmetric stochastic < smoothed oscillator OR (asymmetric stochastic < oscillator midpoint AND asymmetric stochastic != previous oscillator value)
