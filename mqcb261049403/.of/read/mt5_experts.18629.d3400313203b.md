Calculation: Common pivots = At the start of each chart bar, scan completed chart bars backward. A common high or low is a nonzero ZigZag pivot at the same bar, price, and high/low type in both a smaller ZigZag and a larger ZigZag whose depth, deviation, and backstep are each proportionally larger. Index common pivots from 0 in scan order; store each common high in H[index] and each common low in L[index], leaving all other array elements zero. Stop after the third common pivot or at the end of the scan window.
Calculation: Pivot counts = During the same backward scan, set the current type to high or low whenever a common pivot is found. After processing each bar, except the bar containing the third common pivot, count each nonzero smaller ZigZag high or low pivot in U if the current type is high, or in D if it is low. Pivots before the first common pivot are not counted.
Long: At least two common pivots exist AND U > D × count ratio AND H[0] > L[1] AND H[2] > L[1] AND L[1] > 0 AND (H[2] − L[1]) × price ratio < H[0] − L[1] AND position side is flat or short.
Short: At least two common pivots exist AND U × count ratio < D AND L[0] < H[1] AND L[2] < H[1] AND H[1] > 0 AND (H[1] − L[2]) × price ratio < H[1] − L[0] AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: short, at least two common pivots exist AND U > D × count ratio AND H[0] > L[1] AND H[2] > L[1] AND L[1] > 0 AND (H[2] − L[1]) × price ratio < H[0] − L[1].
Exit: long, at least two common pivots exist AND U × count ratio < D AND L[0] < H[1] AND L[2] < H[1] AND H[1] > 0 AND (H[1] − L[2]) × price ratio < H[1] − L[0].
