Calculation: Selected price = configurable open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, trend-biased extreme price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price. Trend-biased price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open, otherwise (low + close) / 2; trend-biased extreme price is high when close > open, low when close < open, otherwise close. Apply the same formulas to Heikin-Ashi OHLC when selected.
Calculation: PDF moving average = weighted sum of selected prices divided by sum of weights, using up to the configured lookback including the current bar. For lag k, let coefficient c[k] = exp(-((k * pi / (lookback - 1) - mean * pi)^2) / (2 * variance^2)) / sqrt(2 * pi * variance^2); weight[0] = c[0], and weight[k] = c[k] * current selected price for k > 0. Clamp variance to at least 0.01 and mean to [-1, 1]. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Levels = either floating or quantile levels of the PDF moving average over a configured lookback. Floating upper and lower levels are the rolling minimum plus their respective configured fractions of the rolling maximum-minus-minimum range; the middle is their average. Quantile levels are the configured upper and lower percentiles and the percentile halfway between them, using linear interpolation between sorted values and padding unavailable early values with zeros.
Calculation: Directional state = in outer-level mode, 1 if the PDF moving average > upper level, otherwise 2 if it < lower level, otherwise 0 if it is strictly between the levels, otherwise the previous state; in middle-level mode, 1 if it > middle level, 2 if it < middle level, otherwise 0; in slope mode, 1 if it > its previous value, 2 if it < its previous value, otherwise the previous state. Initialize the state to 0. Use slope mode when levels are disabled or their lookback is at most 1.
Long: Directional state = 1
Short: Directional state = 2
