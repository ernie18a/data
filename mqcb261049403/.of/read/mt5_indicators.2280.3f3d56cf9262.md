Calculation: Stop distance = either a fixed price distance or a multiplier times the exponential moving average of ATR; the higher-timeframe candle calculation uses the ATR-based distance.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initialize to the close preceding the first calculated bar. For each bar, let previous stop be the preceding bar's trailing stop. If close equals previous stop, trailing stop equals previous stop. Otherwise, if both previous close and close are below previous stop, trailing stop is the minimum of previous stop and close plus stop distance. Otherwise, if both previous close and close are above previous stop, trailing stop is the maximum of previous stop and close minus stop distance. Otherwise, if close is above previous stop, trailing stop is close minus stop distance; otherwise it is close plus stop distance. Calculate on the chart timeframe or, for the higher-timeframe candle, on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Close > trailing stop.
Short: Close < trailing stop.
