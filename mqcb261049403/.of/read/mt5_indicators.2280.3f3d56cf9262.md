Calculation: ATR trailing stop = On the selected timeframe (same as or higher than the chart timeframe), multiply the EMA of ATR by a volatility factor. If the current close equals the previous stop, keep the previous stop. If the previous and current closes are both below the previous stop, use the lower of the previous stop and current close plus the ATR amount. If both closes are above the previous stop, use the higher of the previous stop and current close minus the ATR amount. Otherwise, use current close minus the ATR amount when current close is above the previous stop, and current close plus the ATR amount when below. The current timeframe bar may still be forming; initialize the stop to the close before the first calculated bar.
Long: Current timeframe close > current ATR trailing stop AND previous timeframe close <= previous ATR trailing stop
Short: Current timeframe close < current ATR trailing stop AND previous timeframe close >= previous ATR trailing stop
