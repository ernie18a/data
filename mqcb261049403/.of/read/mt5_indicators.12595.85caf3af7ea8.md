Calculation: Trailing level = For a selected OHLC-derived price and percentage offset k, initialize the trend as up if the second bar closes above its open, otherwise down; initialize the level from the first bar's price multiplied by (1 - k) for an uptrend or (1 + k) for a downtrend. On each subsequent bar, if the previous trend is up and price is below the previous level, switch to down and set the level to price × (1 + k); otherwise, in an uptrend set it to the greater of the previous level and price × (1 - k). If the previous trend is down and price is above the previous level, switch to up and set the level to price × (1 - k); otherwise, in a downtrend set it to the lesser of the previous level and price × (1 + k). Round each newly calculated price-based level to the selected price precision.
Calculation: Oscillator = 100 × (selected moving average of (100 × absolute value of (price - trailing level) / price / percentage offset expressed in percentage points)) raised to a selected exponent. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for a higher timeframe use the still-forming bar and the preceding completed bar. Evaluate only on the first chart bar of each oscillator-timeframe bar.
