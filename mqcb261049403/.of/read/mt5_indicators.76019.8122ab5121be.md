Calculation: Selected gaps = At the start of each chart bar, scan each selected higher, lower, or chart timeframe using its bar still forming and its preceding bars, with a requested history length equal to the scan lookback plus five bars; require at least five available bars. For each consecutive three-bar sequence, from newest to oldest, a bullish gap has bottom = high of the oldest bar and top = low of the newest bar; a bearish gap has bottom = high of the newest bar and top = low of the oldest bar. Retain gaps with top > bottom and width at least the minimum gap size. For each bullish gap, filled width is the maximum of zero and top minus each subsequent bar's low, capped at gap width; for each bearish gap, it is the maximum of zero and each subsequent bar's high minus bottom, capped at gap width. Include the forming bar among subsequent bars. Retain gaps whose filled width divided by gap width is below the fill threshold. Select the most recent retained gaps up to the per-timeframe limit, counting bullish and bearish gaps together; keep this selection until the next chart bar starts.
Long: (Chart-bar low > high two chart bars earlier AND chart-bar low minus that earlier high >= minimum gap size) OR selected bullish gap count > zero
Short: (Chart-bar high < low two chart bars earlier AND that earlier low minus chart-bar high >= minimum gap size) OR selected bearish gap count > zero
