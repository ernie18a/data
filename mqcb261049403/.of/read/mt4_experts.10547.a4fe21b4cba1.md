Long: No position is open AND the last completed chart bar's close > the preceding chart bar's close; alternatively, in tick mode, the forming chart bar's close > the preceding chart bar's close, with at most one entry attempt per bar.
Short: No position is open AND the last completed chart bar's close < the preceding chart bar's close; alternatively, in tick mode, the forming chart bar's close < the preceding chart bar's close, with at most one entry attempt per bar.
Exit: Long, price <= entry price minus stop-loss distance if stop loss is enabled OR price >= entry price plus take-profit distance if take profit is enabled OR price <= highest observed price since entry minus trailing-stop distance if trailing stop is enabled AND that highest price exceeds entry price plus trailing-stop distance.
Exit: Short, price >= entry price plus stop-loss distance if stop loss is enabled OR price <= entry price minus take-profit distance if take profit is enabled OR price >= lowest observed price since entry plus trailing-stop distance if trailing stop is enabled AND that lowest price is below entry price minus trailing-stop distance.
