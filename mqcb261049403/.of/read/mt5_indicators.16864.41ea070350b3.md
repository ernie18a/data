Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or trend-biased extreme price, calculated from ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC bars. Trend-biased price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open, otherwise (low + close) / 2. Trend-biased extreme price is high when close > open, low when close < open, otherwise close.
Calculation: Moving averages = the same configurable type for both averages: simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear weighted; applied to the selected price with shorter and longer lookbacks. Use chart bars or the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and preceding higher-timeframe bars. For the smoothed average, initialize each of the first two bars to its selected price, then update as previous average + (selected price - previous average) / lookback.
Long: Shorter-lookback moving average > longer-lookback moving average
Short: Shorter-lookback moving average < longer-lookback moving average
