Calculation: Supertrend trend = per-bar state of +1 or -1. mid=(high+low)/2; ATR=simple average of true range over a lookback; lowerBand=mid-mult*ATR; upperBand=mid+mult*ATR. Ratchet: if previous close > previous lowerBand then lowerBand=max(lowerBand, previous lowerBand); if previous close < previous upperBand then upperBand=min(upperBand, previous upperBand). First bar: trend=+1 if close>lowerBand else -1. Later bars: if previous trend=-1 and close > previous upperBand then +1; if previous trend=+1 and close < previous lowerBand then -1; otherwise unchanged. The HTF Supertrend uses the same ATR lookback and mult on bars aggregated to a higher timeframe than the chart.
Long: on each new chart bar, chart-timeframe Supertrend trend of the last completed bar = +1 AND (no open long position OR higher-timeframe Supertrend trend of the last completed higher-timeframe bar = +1). Repeats every new bar while true and adds another long position; shorts are not closed.
Short: on each new chart bar, chart-timeframe Supertrend trend of the last completed bar = -1 AND (no open short position OR higher-timeframe Supertrend trend of the last completed higher-timeframe bar = -1). Repeats every new bar while true and adds another short position; longs are not closed.
Exit: long, sum of unrealized profit of all open long positions (from each entry price and the current price) >= profit target; close all long positions.
Exit: short, sum of unrealized profit of all open short positions (from each entry price and the current price) >= profit target; close all short positions.
Exit: long and short, sum of unrealized profit of all open long and short positions combined <= -max loss (max loss is much larger than the profit target); close all positions.
