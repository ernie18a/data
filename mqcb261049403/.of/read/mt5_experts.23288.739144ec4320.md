Calculation: Order levels = when flat with no active pending orders, at the start of a new work-timeframe bar, take the highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback of work-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar; add an indent to the highest high for the buy-stop level and subtract an indent from the lowest low for the sell-stop level. The work timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the same. If time control is enabled, create levels only when the bar's time equals the configured start hour and minute. Keep both levels fixed until the end of the day; cancel the other level when either triggers.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the stop-loss distance when enabled; at each new work-timeframe bar, if its opening price minus entry price is greater than the trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is below the opening price minus the trailing distance and trailing step, raise the stop to the opening price minus the trailing distance. If no initial stop is enabled, treat the existing stop as zero. Trailing applies only when enabled.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the stop-loss distance when enabled; at each new work-timeframe bar, if entry price minus its opening price is greater than the trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is above the opening price plus the trailing distance and trailing step OR entry price minus its opening price is greater than the trailing distance plus trailing step AND no stop exists, lower the stop to the opening price plus the trailing distance. Trailing applies only when enabled.
Long: High >= active buy-stop level
Short: Low <= active sell-stop level
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
