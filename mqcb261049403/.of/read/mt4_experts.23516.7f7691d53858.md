Calculation: Fractal scan result = scan higher-timeframe Bill Williams fractals from the oldest bar in the lookback to the current forming bar; at each bar, test the upper fractal first, then the lower fractal. Return long if an upper fractal exists AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's open is below both that fractal's high and the higher-timeframe typical-price LWMA on the forming bar. Otherwise return short if a lower fractal exists AND that open is above both the same bar's upper-fractal value, or zero if absent, and the forming higher-timeframe LWMA. Otherwise stop with no result if both fractals exist; continue scanning if neither test returns a result.
Calculation: Long stop and target = initially entry price minus the stop distance and entry price plus the target distance. At each new chart bar, if enabled and open minus entry price exceeds the break-even trigger AND the stop is below entry price, move the stop to entry price plus the break-even offset. Then, if candle trailing is enabled AND the lowest low over its chart-bar lookback including the forming bar, minus the padding, is above the stop, move the stop to that level and increase the target by the trailing amount. If candle trailing is disabled AND open minus entry price exceeds the trailing trigger AND open minus the trailing amount is above the stop, move the stop to open minus the trailing amount.
Calculation: Short stop and target = initially entry price plus the stop distance and entry price minus the target distance. At each new chart bar, if enabled and entry price minus open exceeds the break-even trigger AND the stop is above entry price, move the stop to entry price minus the break-even offset. Then, if candle trailing is enabled AND the highest high over its chart-bar lookback including the forming bar, plus the padding, is below the stop, move the stop to that level and decrease the target by the trailing amount. If candle trailing is disabled AND entry price minus open exceeds the trailing trigger AND open plus the trailing amount is below the stop, move the stop to open plus the trailing amount.
Long: Fractal scan result = long AND shorter-lookback typical-price LWMA on the forming chart bar > longer-lookback typical-price LWMA on the forming chart bar AND low two chart bars ago < high of the last completed chart bar AND absolute difference between current chart-bar open and close two chart bars ago > entry distance.
Short: Fractal scan result = short AND shorter-lookback typical-price LWMA on the forming chart bar < longer-lookback typical-price LWMA on the forming chart bar AND low of the last completed chart bar < high two chart bars ago AND absolute difference between current chart-bar open and close of the last completed chart bar > entry distance.
Exit: long, chart-bar low <= long stop OR chart-bar high >= long target.
Exit: short, chart-bar high >= short stop OR chart-bar low <= short target.
