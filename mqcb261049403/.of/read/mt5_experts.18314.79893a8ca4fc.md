Calculation: Bull and bear concentration = On the chart timeframe for the standalone indicator, or on a configurable higher or lower timeframe using the last completed bar for the expert, calculate a close EMA. Over a configurable lookback, count bars whose high exceeds their EMA and bars whose low is below their EMA. For each count, concentration = max(0, min(100, 2 * (floor(100 * count / lookback) - 50))). Both counts use the same EMA period and lookback. The expert can instead use the still-forming indicator bar when its signal-bar setting selects it.
Calculation: Trading interval = With current hour h and minute m, start hour sh and minute sm, and end hour eh and minute em: if sh < eh, (h = sh AND m >= sm) OR (h > sh AND h < eh) OR (h = eh AND m < em); if sh = eh, h = sh AND m >= sm AND m < em; if sh > eh, (h >= sh AND m >= sm) OR h < eh OR (h = eh AND m < em).
Long: Bull concentration > bear concentration.
Short: Bull concentration < bear concentration.
Long: Bull concentration > bear concentration AND previous indicator bar's bull concentration <= previous indicator bar's bear concentration AND (time restriction disabled OR current time inside trading interval).
Short: Bull concentration < bear concentration AND previous indicator bar's bull concentration >= previous indicator bar's bear concentration AND (time restriction disabled OR current time inside trading interval).
Exit: long, bull concentration < bear concentration OR (time restriction enabled AND current time outside trading interval) OR (stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price - stop distance) OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + profit distance).
Exit: short, bull concentration > bear concentration OR (time restriction enabled AND current time outside trading interval) OR (stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price + stop distance) OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price - profit distance).
