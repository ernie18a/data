Calculation: Price = a selected open, high, low, close, median (high + low)/2, typical (high + low + close)/3, weighted (high + low + 2 × close)/4, OHLC average, body midpoint (open + close)/2, trend-biased price (high + close)/2 if close > open, otherwise (low + close)/2, or trend-biased extreme high if close > open, low if close < open, otherwise close; these choices may instead use Heikin-Ashi OHLC.
Calculation: Velocity = the weighted mean of price with weights (lookback − offset) squared minus the weighted mean of price with weights (lookback − offset), for offsets from the current bar backward over the velocity lookback; use available bars during initialization.
Calculation: Raw upper and lower bands = a selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average of velocity plus and minus a deviation multiplier times the population or sample standard deviation of velocity over its deviation lookback; use available bars during initialization.
Calculation: Trend and trailing bands = initialize trend to neutral. On each subsequent bar, set trend to up if velocity > the previous trailing upper band; otherwise set it to down if velocity < the previous trailing lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend. In a down trend, the trailing upper band is the minimum of the current raw upper band and previous trailing upper band; otherwise it is the current raw upper band. In an up trend, the trailing lower band is the maximum of the current raw lower band and previous trailing lower band; otherwise it is the current raw lower band. Initialize trailing bands to their raw values. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
Long: Trend changes to up.
Short: Trend changes to down.
