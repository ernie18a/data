Calculation: Index = RSI of the selected applied price or MFI; use the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe. Use completed index bars by their own bar offsets, and round the two index values used for live entry to two decimal places.
Calculation: Stop distance and target distance = fixed price distances or respective multiples of ATR at the last completed index-timeframe bar; calculate both at entry and keep them fixed for that position.
Calculation: Optimized long and short scores and levels = at each new chart bar, test every integer level in the configured index range separately for each side over the optimization lookback. Pair each completed index bar with the chart-timeframe close at the same bar offset. For a long test, enter at that close when the index strictly crosses above the level; for a short test, enter when it strictly crosses below. Starting with the following chart bar, close the simulated trade at the first close strictly beyond the current stop or target distance from its entry; add its signed close-to-entry price change to that level's score. Allow only one simulated trade per level, skip the exit bar for new entries, and exclude trades still open at the end of the lookback. Round scores to two decimals after applying a common positive monetary scaling factor. Select the highest strictly positive score for each side; use zero as the score and level if none is positive. Resolve equal positive scores by choosing the highest level for long and the lowest level for short.
Long: New chart bar AND (multiple positions allowed OR no open position) AND ((normal mode AND optimized long score > optimized short score) OR (reverse mode AND optimized short score > optimized long score)) AND (aggressive mode OR (index at the preceding completed index bar < optimized long level AND index at the last completed index bar > optimized long level)).
Short: New chart bar AND (multiple positions allowed OR no open position) AND ((normal mode AND optimized short score > optimized long score) OR (reverse mode AND optimized long score > optimized short score)) AND (aggressive mode OR (index at the preceding completed index bar > optimized short level AND index at the last completed index bar < optimized short level)).
Exit: long, price reaches entry price minus stop distance OR price reaches entry price plus target distance OR (break-even enabled AND price previously exceeded entry price plus trigger distance AND price reaches entry price plus padding distance).
Exit: short, price reaches entry price plus stop distance OR price reaches entry price minus target distance OR (break-even enabled AND price previously fell below entry price minus trigger distance AND price reaches entry price minus padding distance).
