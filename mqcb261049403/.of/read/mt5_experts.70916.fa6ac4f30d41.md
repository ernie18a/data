Calculation: Filter MA = moving average of the higher-timeframe closes from the second-most-recent completed bar through the bar at the filter lookback offset, preceded by a zero as the oldest observation; use the selected MA method, with EMA initialized at zero. Evaluate at the start of each new chart bar.
Long: On the last completed chart bar, close > shorter-lookback close MA AND shorter-lookback close MA > longer-lookback close MA AND on the preceding chart bar, shorter-lookback close MA <= longer-lookback close MA AND (filter disabled OR last completed chart-bar close > filter MA).
Short: On the last completed chart bar, close < shorter-lookback close MA AND shorter-lookback close MA < longer-lookback close MA AND on the preceding chart bar, shorter-lookback close MA >= longer-lookback close MA AND (filter disabled OR last completed chart-bar close < filter MA).
Exit: Long, Short condition OR (fast-MA exit enabled AND last completed chart-bar close <= shorter-lookback close MA) OR (stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price - effective stop-loss distance) OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + effective take-profit distance).
Exit: Short, Long condition OR (fast-MA exit enabled AND last completed chart-bar close >= shorter-lookback close MA) OR (stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price + effective stop-loss distance) OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price - effective take-profit distance).
