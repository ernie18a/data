Calculation: Source = a selectable chart-timeframe series: Accelerator Oscillator, Accumulation/Distribution, ADX, ATR, Awesome Oscillator, Bears Power, Bulls Power, CCI, DeMarker, Force Index, Momentum, MFI, MACD, OsMA, OBV, RVI, standard deviation, stochastic, volume, open, high, low, close, median price, typical price, weighted close, OHLC average, open-close average, RSI, or Williams %R. Use the selected indicator's configurable price, period, averaging method and line where applicable; use volume from the input data. Add 1 to Accelerator Oscillator, Awesome Oscillator, Bears Power, Bulls Power, MACD, OsMA and RVI; add 1000 to CCI and 100 to Force Index; divide OBV by 10000000 and add 100.
Calculation: Smoothed source = six successive exponential smoothings of Source, each initialized to zero with alpha = 2 / (1 + 1 + 0.5 × (smoothing period − 1)); combine the third through sixth smoothings as (1 + 3b + 3b² + b³) × EMA3 − 3 × (2b² + b + b³) × EMA4 + 3 × (b² + b³) × EMA5 − b³ × EMA6, with configurable b. Set the initial smoothing-period bars to zero.
Calculation: Pivot high or low = a nonzero Smoothed source value respectively above or below its configurable number of preceding bars and following bars; equality is allowed independently on either side when that side's strictness is disabled. If the preceding count is positive, compare only the smaller of the preceding and following counts on the following side; otherwise compare the full following count. Consider only pivots older than the configured following count plus the configured bar offset, within the configured history limit. On each new chart bar, select each configured pivot rank and additional backward offset as the newest pivot P1; P2, P3, and so on are successive older pivots of the same type. All comparisons below use the unsmoothed Source and the price high or low at those pivot bars.
Long: For low pivots, Source(P2) < Source(P1) AND low(P2) > low(P1).
Long: Hidden divergence enabled AND for low pivots, Source(P2) > Source(P1) AND low(P2) < low(P1).
Long: Extended bullish divergence enabled AND for low pivots there is an older pivot Pk from the third through ninth pivot such that every intermediate pivot Pi has Source(Pi) > Source(P1) AND low(Pi) > low(P1), AND Source(Pk) < Source(P1) AND low(P1) < low(Pk) AND low(Pk) < low(Pi) for every intermediate pivot.
Long: Extended bullish divergence and hidden divergence enabled AND for low pivots there is an older pivot Pk from the third through eighth pivot such that every intermediate pivot Pi has Source(Pi) > Source(P1) AND low(Pi) > low(P1), AND Source(Pk) > Source(P1) AND low(Pk) < low(P1) AND low(Pk) < low(Pi) AND Source(Pk) < Source(Pi) for every intermediate pivot.
