Calculation: Normalized close = (close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low), using a configurable chart-bar lookback including the current bar; undefined when the range is zero.
Calculation: Deviation recency = population standard deviation of normalized close over a separate configurable chart-bar lookback; find the most recent maximum and minimum deviation over the current bar and a configurable number of preceding bars, resolving ties in favor of the most recent bar.
Calculation: Live channels = start a compression when the minimum deviation becomes more recent than the maximum after previously being no more recent. End it when the maximum becomes more recent than the minimum after previously being no more recent. If the elapsed bars since the compression start exceed a configurable minimum and ATR is available, create a channel whose upper and lower edges are the highest high and lowest low from the bar after the compression start through the ending bar. If overlapping channels are disabled, create it only when its range does not strictly overlap any live channel. On each bar, process live channels newest first, including any just created; remove a channel when the selected breakout price is strictly outside either edge, and skip the next live channel after each removal.
Calculation: Breakout price = either the current chart bar's (open + close) / 2 or its close, selected by configuration.
Long: Breakout price > upper edge of a live channel being processed.
Short: Breakout price < lower edge of a live channel being processed.
