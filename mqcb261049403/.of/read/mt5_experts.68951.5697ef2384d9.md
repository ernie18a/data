Calculation: Active swing highs and lows = Initially empty ordered lists. At the start of each higher-timeframe bar, after checking the completed chart bar for sweeps, examine the higher-timeframe bar a lookback number of bars before the forming bar. Add its high if it is at least as high as every high in an equal lookback on both sides; add its low if it is at least as low as every low in those bars. The newer side includes the higher-timeframe bar still forming. On each completed chart bar, scan each list in order. Remove a high when the chart bar closes above it or sweeps it; remove a low when the chart bar closes below it or sweeps it. After a removal, advance to the next list index, so the level shifted into the removed slot is skipped, except that after a sweep it is also removed if the close is beyond it in the invalidating direction.
Long: The last completed chart bar's low < an active swing low AND its close > that swing low.
Short: The last completed chart bar's high > an active swing high AND its close < that swing high.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio.
