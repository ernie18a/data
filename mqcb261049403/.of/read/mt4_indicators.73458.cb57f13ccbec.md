Long: At the start of a new chart bar, its open > the high two bars earlier.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, its open < the low two bars earlier.
Long: Price breaks above the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high.
Short: Price breaks below the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's low.
Long: After price breaks below the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's low, price returns above that low.
Short: After price breaks above the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high, price returns below that high.
