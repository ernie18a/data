Calculation: Oscillator = if the period parameter is negative, EMA of close with the absolute period minus EMA of close with the period rounded from the absolute value of the period multiplied by a scaling parameter; if the period parameter is positive, CCI of that EMA difference using the scaled period multiplied by ADX using the unscaled period and divided by the product of an ADX normalization parameter and the scaling parameter.
Long: Oscillator on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe > upper threshold. For a higher timeframe, sample its still-forming bar at the start of that timeframe's bar and retain the result until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Oscillator on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe < lower threshold. For a higher timeframe, sample its still-forming bar at the start of that timeframe's bar and retain the result until the next higher-timeframe bar.
