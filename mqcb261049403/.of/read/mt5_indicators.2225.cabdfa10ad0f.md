Calculation: Fisher CG oscillator = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming, let n be the lookback and p[i] the midpoint of the high and low i bars ago. Calculate CG = (n+1)/2 - sum((i+1)*p[i], i=0..n-1)/sum(p[i], i=0..n-1), or 0 if the denominator is 0. Normalize CG as (CG - lowest CG)/(highest CG - lowest CG) over the current and previous n-1 bars, or 0 if the range is 0. Let s be the weighted average of the current and previous three normalized values with weights 4, 3, 2, 1 divided by 10. The oscillator is 0.5*ln((1+1.98*(s-0.5))/(1-1.98*(s-0.5))). Initialize prior CG and normalized values to 0. Sample the oscillator and its previous-bar value at the first chart bar of each oscillator-timeframe bar and hold those samples for the rest of that bar.
Long: Sampled Fisher CG oscillator > its sampled previous-bar value OR sampled Fisher CG oscillator = its sampled previous-bar value AND the most recent unequal comparison was greater than.
Short: Sampled Fisher CG oscillator < its sampled previous-bar value OR sampled Fisher CG oscillator = its sampled previous-bar value AND the most recent unequal comparison was less than.
