Calculation: Regressed moving average = At each evaluation, use close prices on the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe or equal to it. Recalculate from the currently forming indicator bar; select the last completed bar or an optionally specified older bar as the signal bar, or select the forming bar. Let T be the odd tap count, P the filter-period parameter, n=(T-1)/2, and price[j] the close j bars before the forming bar. For k=0,...,T-1, let w[k]=window[k]*sin(pi*(k-T/2)/P)/(pi*(k-T/2)), with the multiplier replaced by 1 when k=T/2. The selectable window is 1; 0.5-0.5*cos(2*pi*k/T); 0.54-0.46*cos(2*pi*k/T); 0.42-0.5*cos(2*pi*k/T)+0.08*cos(4*pi*k/T); or 0.35875-0.48829*cos(2*pi*k/T)+0.14128*cos(4*pi*k/T)-0.01168*cos(6*pi*k/T). Define F[j]=sum(k=0,...,T-1, price[j+k]*w[k])/sum(k=0,...,T-1, w[k]). Set a0=F[0], a1=F[0]-F[1], and choose a2 and a3 by least squares to fit a0+a1*i+a2*i^2+a3*i^3 to price[n-i] for i=0,...,n. At offset j from the forming bar, the regressed moving average is a0+a1*(n-j)+a2*(n-j)^2+a3*(n-j)^3 for 0<=j<=n, and is unavailable outside that range. Recalculate all three compared values together at each evaluation, since past values repaint.
Long: Regressed moving average at the signal bar > its value one indicator bar earlier AND its value two indicator bars earlier > its value one indicator bar earlier.
Short: Regressed moving average at the signal bar < its value one indicator bar earlier AND its value two indicator bars earlier < its value one indicator bar earlier.
Exit: long, regressed moving average at the signal bar < its value one indicator bar earlier OR low <= entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, regressed moving average at the signal bar > its value one indicator bar earlier OR high >= entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
