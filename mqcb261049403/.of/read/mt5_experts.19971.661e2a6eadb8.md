Calculation: Envelope bands = upper and lower percentage offsets from the long-term EMA of close; use narrow, intermediate, and wide bands.
Long: With no open position and, if enabled, within the configured trading hours, short-term EMA(close) on the current forming bar > wide lower envelope and on the previous completed bar < that envelope OR current > intermediate lower envelope and previous < that envelope OR current < narrow lower envelope and previous < that envelope OR current > long-term EMA(close) and previous < that EMA OR current > narrow upper envelope and previous < that envelope OR current > intermediate upper envelope and previous < that envelope
Short: With no open position and, if enabled, within the configured trading hours, short-term EMA(open) on the current forming bar < wide upper envelope and on the previous completed bar > that envelope OR current < intermediate upper envelope and previous > that envelope OR current < narrow upper envelope and previous > that envelope OR current < long-term EMA(close) and previous > that EMA OR current < narrow lower envelope and previous > that envelope OR current < intermediate lower envelope and previous > that envelope
Exit: long, low <= active stop-loss price OR high >= configured take-profit price
Exit: short, high >= active stop-loss price OR low <= configured take-profit price
