Calculation: Channel levels = long-period EMA of close multiplied by 1 plus or minus each of three increasing percentage offsets; all EMAs and channel levels use the chart timeframe, with current values taken from the forming bar and previous values from the last completed bar.
Calculation: Trailing stop = when enabled, update the long stop to current price minus the trailing distance if current price minus entry price is greater than trailing distance plus step and the existing stop is below current price minus trailing distance minus step; update the short stop to current price plus the trailing distance if entry price minus current price is greater than trailing distance plus step and the existing stop is absent or above current price plus trailing distance plus step. Updates occur only during enabled trading hours and after the minimum chart-bar history is available.
Long: Position is flat AND at least one trailing stop is enabled AND chart-bar count is at least the minimum AND (trading-hours filter is disabled OR current hour is between the configured start and end hours inclusive) AND ((current short-period close EMA > current widest lower channel AND previous short-period close EMA < current widest lower channel) OR (current short-period close EMA > current middle lower channel AND previous short-period close EMA < current middle lower channel) OR (current short-period close EMA < current narrowest lower channel AND previous short-period close EMA < current narrowest lower channel) OR (current short-period close EMA > current long-period close EMA AND previous short-period close EMA < current long-period close EMA) OR (current short-period close EMA > current narrowest upper channel AND previous short-period close EMA < current narrowest upper channel) OR (current short-period close EMA > current middle upper channel AND previous short-period close EMA < current middle upper channel)).
Short: Position is flat AND at least one trailing stop is enabled AND chart-bar count is at least the minimum AND (trading-hours filter is disabled OR current hour is between the configured start and end hours inclusive) AND the long condition is false AND ((current short-period open EMA < current widest upper channel AND previous short-period open EMA > current widest upper channel) OR (current short-period open EMA < current middle upper channel AND previous short-period open EMA > current middle upper channel) OR (current short-period open EMA < current narrowest upper channel AND previous short-period open EMA > current narrowest upper channel) OR (current short-period open EMA < current long-period close EMA AND previous short-period open EMA > current long-period close EMA) OR (current short-period open EMA < current narrowest lower channel AND previous short-period open EMA > current narrowest lower channel) OR (current short-period open EMA < current middle lower channel AND previous short-period open EMA > current middle lower channel)).
Exit: long, (stop loss is enabled AND price <= entry price minus long stop-loss distance) OR (take profit is enabled AND price >= entry price plus long take-profit distance) OR (trailing stop is active AND price <= trailing stop).
Exit: short, (stop loss is enabled AND price >= entry price plus short stop-loss distance) OR (take profit is enabled AND price <= entry price minus short take-profit distance) OR (trailing stop is active AND price >= trailing stop).
