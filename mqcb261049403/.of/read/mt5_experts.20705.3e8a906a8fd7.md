Calculation: RSI set = close-based RSI on the chart timeframe, an intermediate higher timeframe, and a still higher timeframe, using the same RSI lookback within each trigger; use the last completed bar on the chart and intermediate timeframes and the forming bar on the highest timeframe.
Calculation: RSI change = chart-timeframe RSI on the last completed bar minus its value on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Asymmetric RSI bands = for each timeframe, calculate the mean of a lookback of RSI values ending at the RSI set's specified bar; calculate upper and lower deviations as the square roots of the mean squared deviations of values respectively at or above and below that mean, falling back to the full-set population standard deviation when a side is empty. For a lookback below three bars, use the newest RSI as the mean and a configurable fraction of it for both deviations. Form inner and outer lower bands by subtracting their respective deviation multipliers times the lower deviation from the mean, and inner and outer upper bands by adding their respective multipliers times the upper deviation. Clamp lower bands to a configurable floor and upper bands to a configurable ceiling; outer multipliers are larger than inner multipliers.
Calculation: Volatility filter = last completed highest-timeframe close-based Bollinger Band width > its minimum AND last completed highest-timeframe ATR < its maximum AND last completed chart-timeframe close-based Bollinger Band width < its maximum; both Bollinger Bands use the same lookback and deviation multiplier.
Calculation: Trading window = current chart-bar opening hour lies in the inclusive, potentially midnight-wrapping interval from the configured start hour through the configured end hour AND (day of week is not Friday OR hour < Friday cutoff).
Long: Fixed-threshold trigger selected AND volatility filter holds AND trading window holds AND each RSI in the RSI set is strictly between its respective lower limit and oversold threshold AND RSI change > its lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main line < its oversold threshold.
Short: Fixed-threshold trigger selected AND volatility filter holds AND trading window holds AND each RSI in the RSI set is strictly between its respective overbought threshold and upper limit AND RSI change < the negation of its lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main line > its overbought threshold.
Long: Asymmetric-band trigger selected AND volatility filter holds AND trading window holds AND each RSI in the RSI set is > its outer lower band AND < its inner lower band AND RSI change > its lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main line < its oversold threshold.
Short: Asymmetric-band trigger selected AND volatility filter holds AND trading window holds AND each RSI in the RSI set is < its outer upper band AND > its inner upper band AND RSI change < the negation of its lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main line > its overbought threshold.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop distance OR high >= entry price + long profit distance; distances depend on the entry trigger.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short stop distance OR low <= entry price - short profit distance; distances depend on the entry trigger.
