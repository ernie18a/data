Calculation: WPR Histogram Vol = (Williams %R + 50) × volume of the same chart-timeframe bar (current bar, which may still be forming)
Long: WPR Histogram Vol < lower oversold level × volume OR WPR Histogram Vol < upper oversold level × volume
Short: WPR Histogram Vol > upper overbought level × volume OR WPR Histogram Vol > lower overbought level × volume
