Calculation: Pivot point = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Resistance 2 = Pivot point + (high − low) of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Resistance 3 = 2 × Pivot point + (high − 2 × low) of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Support 2 = Pivot point − (high − low) of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Support 3 = 2 × Pivot point − (2 × high − low) of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Long: current open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > Pivot point
Short: current open of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < Pivot point
Exit: long, bar high >= Resistance 2 OR bar high >= Resistance 3
Exit: short, bar low <= Support 2 OR bar low <= Support 3
