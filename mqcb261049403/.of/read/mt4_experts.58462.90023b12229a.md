Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of price, using a configurable lookback
Calculation: Stochastic = stochastic oscillator main and signal lines, using configurable lookbacks
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = upper and lower bands around a moving average, using configurable lookback and deviation
Calculation: Moving average = configurable moving average; compare a shorter-lookback average with a longer-lookback average whose lookback is five times longer
Calculation: Signal timeframe(s) may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming
Long: Trend mode and RSI > upper threshold, or both Stochastic lines > upper threshold, or price > upper Bollinger Band; CounterTrend mode and RSI < lower threshold, or both Stochastic lines < lower threshold, or price < lower Bollinger Band; when multiple indicators are enabled, require all enabled indicators to meet the corresponding condition; when multiple timeframes are combined, require the condition on every selected timeframe; when checking one selected timeframe at a time, require it on that timeframe; in the plus version, Moving Average must also agree (Trend: price > both averages; CounterTrend: price < both averages); reverse the resulting side when inverse mode is enabled
Short: Trend mode and RSI < lower threshold, or both Stochastic lines < lower threshold, or price < lower Bollinger Band; CounterTrend mode and RSI > upper threshold, or both Stochastic lines > upper threshold, or price > upper Bollinger Band; when multiple indicators are enabled, require all enabled indicators to meet the corresponding condition; when multiple timeframes are combined, require the condition on every selected timeframe; when checking one selected timeframe at a time, require it on that timeframe; in the plus version, Moving Average must also agree (Trend: price < both averages; CounterTrend: price > both averages); reverse the resulting side when inverse mode is enabled
Exit: Long, price <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, price >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price - take-profit distance
