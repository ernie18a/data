Calculation: Pivots = On each completed chart bar, scan a bounded recent history, starting at the later of the pivot-strength offset from the history start and the end minus the search window; stop pivot-strength bars before the end. A high pivot has a high strictly greater than every high within pivot strength bars on both sides; otherwise a low pivot has a low strictly less than every low within pivot strength bars on both sides. For consecutive pivots of the same type retain only the more extreme one, retaining the earlier pivot on a tie. Use the latest five cleaned pivots, numbered 1 through 5 chronologically.
Calculation: Pattern geometry = Width is the bar-index difference between pivots 5 and 1; height is the maximum minus minimum of the five pivot prices. Lines 1-3, 2-4 and 1-4 are linear in bar index through their named pivots. The intersection index is where lines 1-3 and 2-4 intersect. Convergence is the absolute difference of their slopes multiplied by width and divided by height. Point-5 distance is the absolute difference between pivot 5 and line 1-3 at pivot 5, divided by height.
Long: Five pivots exist AND their types are low, high, low, high, low AND price 3 < price 1 AND price 4 < price 2 AND width is between the minimum and maximum pattern widths inclusive AND the absolute slope difference of lines 1-3 and 2-4 is at least the numerical tolerance AND their intersection index > pivot 5 index AND height > minimum price increment AND convergence >= minimum convergence AND point-5 distance <= line tolerance AND (hide-after-target is disabled OR every completed bar from pivot 5 through the current bar has high < line 1-4 at that bar).
