Calculation: Fisher candle = For each of open and close, independently normalize the price as (price − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low), using a rolling lookback including the current bar; if the range is zero, use zero. Starting with previous value and previous Fisher value at zero, calculate value = normalized price − 0.5 + 0.67 × previous value, then Fisher = 0.5 × ln((1 + value) / (1 − value)) + 0.5 × previous Fisher; replace the logarithm's ratio with 1 when it is below 0.0000001. Smooth each Fisher series independently using the same selectable moving-average method and parameters. The candle close is the smoothed close series; the candle open is the smoothed open series, replaced by the previous candle close when the absolute difference between the price bar's open and close is at most a gap threshold. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Fisher candle close > Fisher candle open
Short: Fisher candle close < Fisher candle open
