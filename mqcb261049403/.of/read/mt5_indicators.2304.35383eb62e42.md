Calculation: StepMA stochastic histogram = (short-step line − midpoint-step line) / (maximum-step line − short-step line), with the code’s zero-denominator fallback; each line is a recursively ratcheting step line. For each bar, calculate weighted mean(high−low) over a lookback, weighting the newest range 2 and the oldest 1 + 1/lookback. Track the running minimum and maximum of this mean; derive short, maximum, and midpoint step sizes from the minimum, maximum, and their average, respectively. For each track, initialize upper/lower bands at the selected price basis ± 2 step sizes and trend neutral; set trend up when close exceeds the previous upper band and down when close falls below the previous lower band. In an uptrend, ratchet the lower band upward; in a downtrend, ratchet the upper band downward. The step line is the lower band + one step when up, or upper band − one step when down. Normalize the short and midpoint lines against the bounds formed by the maximum-step line − maximum step and short-step line + maximum step, respectively; the histogram is the difference of those normalized values. Use the indicator’s configured price basis and rounding.
