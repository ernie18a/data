Calculation: Step oscillator = For each bar, compute the weighted range as the sum over a lookback of (high - low) × (1 + (lookback - age) / lookback), divided by the lookback, where age zero is the current bar. Track the running minimum and maximum of this value, initialized to its first value. Create three step sizes by multiplying the running minimum, running maximum, and their mean by a factor and rounding each to the nearest price increment. For each step size, maintain upper and lower bands and a trend, initially zero. Initialize both bands at the first bar's close, or at its low and high respectively when the high/low option is selected. On each bar, set the provisional upper band to close + twice the step, or low + twice the step with the high/low option; set the provisional lower band to close - twice the step, or high - twice the step with that option. Set trend to positive if close exceeds the previous upper band, and to negative if close is below the previous lower band, with the negative test taking precedence; otherwise retain the previous trend. In a positive trend, raise the lower band to at least its previous value; in a negative trend, lower the upper band to at most its previous value. The step line is lower band + step in a positive trend and upper band - step in a negative trend; retain its previous value while trend is zero, starting from zero. Let A, B, and C be the lines for the minimum, maximum, and mean step sizes, and let D be the maximum step size. Set lower = B - D, upper = A + D, and denominator = upper - lower, replacing a zero denominator with a small positive constant. The oscillator is round((A - lower) / denominator, six decimal places) - round((C - lower) / denominator, six decimal places).

Calculation: Higher-timeframe sampled step oscillator = The step oscillator calculated on the higher timeframe's bar still forming, sampled on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and held until the next higher-timeframe bar.
