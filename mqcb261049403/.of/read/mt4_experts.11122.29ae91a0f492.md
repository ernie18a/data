Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = short-period simple moving average minus long-period simple moving average of the accumulation/distribution line; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = any of Bullish Engulfing, Three Outside Up, Bullish Harami, Three Inside Up, Piercing Line, Three White Soldiers, or Morning Doji Star, evaluated on the last three completed chart bars.
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = any of Bearish Engulfing, Three Outside Down, Dark Cloud Cover, Evening Doji Star, Bearish Harami, Three Inside Down, Three Black Crows, or Evening Star, evaluated on the last three completed chart bars.
Calculation: Buy votes = number of these conditions that hold: CCI is rising and below its upper threshold; RSI is at or above its midpoint; stochastic %K is at or above its upper threshold, or is above its lower range and Chaikin Oscillator is positive above its threshold; bullish candle pattern; +DI exceeds −DI, ADX is rising, and its previous value is between its lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Sell votes = number of these conditions that hold: CCI is falling and at or above its lower threshold; RSI is below its midpoint; stochastic %K is at or below its lower threshold, or is above its upper range and below its upper threshold with Chaikin Oscillator negative below its threshold; bearish candle pattern; −DI exceeds +DI, ADX is rising, and its previous value is between its lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement levels = the 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, and 78.6% levels of the high-low range over the 180 completed chart bars preceding the current bar.
Long: Buy votes > Sell votes AND (+DI > −DI AND ADX > its previous value AND its previous value is between its lower and upper bounds) AND fast smoothed moving average of close >= its previous value AND fast smoothed moving average of close >= slow smoothed moving average of close AND abs(fast smoothed moving average of close − slow smoothed moving average of close / point size) >= its minimum threshold AND (stochastic %K >= its upper threshold OR (CCI >= its previous value AND RSI >= its previous value AND RSI <= its upper bound)) AND Chaikin Oscillator >= its previous value AND momentum >= its previous value AND (Ask <= lowest low or Ask >= highest high of the preceding 180 completed chart bars OR Ask is outside the tolerance band around every Fibonacci retracement level).
Short: Sell votes > Buy votes AND (+DI < −DI AND ADX > its previous value AND its previous value is between its lower and upper bounds) AND fast smoothed moving average of open <= its previous value AND fast smoothed moving average of open <= slow smoothed moving average of open AND abs(fast smoothed moving average of open − slow smoothed moving average of open / point size) >= its minimum threshold AND (stochastic %K <= its lower threshold OR (CCI <= its previous value AND RSI <= its previous value AND RSI >= its lower bound)) AND Chaikin Oscillator <= its previous value AND momentum <= its previous value AND (Bid <= lowest low or Bid >= highest high of the preceding 180 completed chart bars OR Bid is outside the tolerance band around every Fibonacci retracement level).
Exit: Long, (Chaikin Oscillator on the chart timeframe < its previous chart-bar value OR RSI on the chart timeframe < its previous chart-bar value) AND entry price < Bid AND NOT (stochastic %K >= its upper threshold AND RSI >= its midpoint)
Exit: Short, (Chaikin Oscillator on the chart timeframe > its previous chart-bar value OR RSI on the chart timeframe > its previous chart-bar value) AND entry price > Ask AND NOT (stochastic %K <= its lower threshold AND RSI < its midpoint)
