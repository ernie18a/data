Calculation: Daily open, high, and low = open, high, and low of the still-forming higher-timeframe daily bar

Short: Current price < daily open AND (current price − daily low > configured risk distance OR (daily high − daily open >= configured risk distance AND daily open − current price <= configured proximity distance) OR (daily open − current price <= configured proximity distance AND current chart-bar close < open)) AND daily high − daily open < daily open − daily low AND CCI on the chart timeframe is nonincreasing across the current still-forming bar and the two preceding completed bars AND no short position is open AND datetime is within the configured weekday entry window
Exit: Long, current price < daily open AND (current price − daily low > configured risk distance OR (daily high − daily open >= configured risk distance AND daily open − current price <= configured proximity distance)) AND daily high − daily open < daily open − daily low AND CCI on the chart timeframe is nonincreasing across the current still-forming bar and the two preceding completed bars
Exit: Short, current price > daily open AND (daily high − current price > configured risk distance OR (daily open − daily low >= configured risk distance AND current price − daily open <= configured proximity distance)) AND daily high − daily open > daily open − daily low AND CCI on the chart timeframe is nondecreasing across the current still-forming bar and the two preceding completed bars
Exit: Long or short, datetime >= configured daily closing time
Exit: Long, configured holding duration has elapsed AND current price > entry price
Exit: Short, configured holding duration has elapsed AND current price < entry price
Exit: Long, current price >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR current price <= entry price − configured stop-loss distance
Exit: Short, current price <= entry price − configured take-profit distance OR current price >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance
