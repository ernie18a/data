Calculation: Trend state = For each chart or higher timeframe, calculate ATR and its rolling maximum and minimum over a separate lookback. Set step to the sensitivity parameter times their average, truncated down to a whole price increment. Set the candidate upper bound to low + 2 × step and the candidate lower bound to high − 2 × step; alternatively use close for both high and low. Initialize both prior bounds to close and trend to neutral. On each bar, retain the prior trend, set it bullish if close > prior upper bound, then set it bearish if close < prior lower bound. If bullish, set the lower bound to the maximum of its candidate and prior lower bound and leave the upper bound at its candidate; otherwise, set the upper bound to the minimum of its candidate and prior upper bound and leave the lower bound at its candidate. Carry both bounds and trend forward. For each higher timeframe, sample the state of its still-forming bar at the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe bar and hold it until the next higher-timeframe bar starts.
Long: Trend state is bullish.
Short: Trend state is bearish or neutral.
