Calculation: Skyscraper trend = bullish when close exceeds the prior upper trailing threshold, bearish when close falls below the prior lower trailing threshold, and otherwise retains its prior trend. For each bar, calculate ATR; over the lookback, take its highest and lowest values. The step is the instrument price increment times the integer part of 0.5 × sensitivity × (highest ATR + lowest ATR) / price increment. In high/low mode, the candidate upper and lower thresholds are low + 2 × step and high − 2 × step; in close mode they are close + 2 × step and close − 2 × step. In bullish trend, the lower threshold is the greater of its candidate and the prior lower threshold, and the upper trailing threshold is reset; in bearish trend, the upper threshold is the lesser of its candidate and the prior upper threshold, and the lower trailing threshold is reset.
Long: previous bar's trend is bearish AND current bar's trend is bullish
Short: previous bar's trend is bullish AND current bar's trend is bearish
