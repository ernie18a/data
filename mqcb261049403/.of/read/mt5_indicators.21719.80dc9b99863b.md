Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of close, open, high, low, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, trend-following price, half trend-following price, or DeMark price. Trend-following price is high if close > open, low if close < open, otherwise close. Half trend-following price is the average of close and trend-following price. DeMark price is X - (high + low)/2, where X is (high + low + close + low)/2 if close < open, (high + low + close + high)/2 if close > open, otherwise (high + low + 2 × close)/2.
Calculation: Rounded smoothed power-weighted average = round to a configurable price increment the sum of a configurable price offset and a configurable moving average of the power-weighted average of selected price. For lookback N and exponent p, the power-weighted average is sum for k = 0 to N-1 of selected price k bars ago × (N-k)^p, divided by sum for k = 0 to N-1 of (N-k)^p. Calculate on the chart timeframe or, alternatively, on a higher timeframe using its bar still forming.
Calculation: Direction = sign of the most recent nonzero change in the rounded smoothed power-weighted average; retain the previous direction when the average is unchanged.
Long: Direction > 0
Short: Direction < 0
