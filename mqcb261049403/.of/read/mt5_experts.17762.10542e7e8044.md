Calculation: Ichimoku values = Kijun-sen and the Senkou Span A, Senkou Span B, and Chikou Span indicator-buffer values at shift zero, calculated on the still-forming bar of a separately selected timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe. The Tenkan lookback is shorter than the Kijun lookback, which is shorter than the Senkou Span B lookback.
Long: No open position AND Kijun-sen > Senkou Span A AND Senkou Span A > Senkou Span B AND Senkou Span B > Chikou Span AND the still-forming Ichimoku-timeframe close > Senkou Span A AND positive directional indicator on the last completed bar of a separately selected timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe < lower threshold AND positive directional indicator on its still-forming bar > higher threshold AND absolute difference between Kijun-sen and Senkou Span A > distance threshold.
Short: No open position AND Kijun-sen < Senkou Span A AND Senkou Span A < Senkou Span B AND Senkou Span B < Chikou Span AND the still-forming Ichimoku-timeframe close < Senkou Span A AND positive directional indicator on the last completed bar of a separately selected timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe < lower threshold AND positive directional indicator on its still-forming bar > higher threshold AND absolute difference between Kijun-sen and Senkou Span A > distance threshold.
Exit: long, stop-loss distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR take-profit distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, stop-loss distance > 0 AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR take-profit distance > 0 AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
