Calculation: Higher-timeframe MA = moving average of a configurable applied price and smoothing method, with a configurable horizontal shift; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and its two preceding completed bars.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry price minus the stop-loss distance for a long, or plus it for a short, if stop loss is enabled; otherwise unset. When trailing is enabled, for a long, if price > entry price + trailing distance + trailing step AND the stop is unset OR price > stop + trailing distance + trailing step, set the stop to price minus trailing distance. For a short, if price < entry price - trailing distance - trailing step AND the stop is unset OR price < stop - trailing distance - trailing step, set the stop to price plus trailing distance.
Long: At the opening of the configurable decision-timeframe bar, whose timeframe may be the chart timeframe or higher or lower, AND close-hour filtering is disabled OR the bar's opening hour < the close hour, AND either reverse is disabled AND the still-forming higher-timeframe MA < the preceding MA AND > the still-forming higher-timeframe open AND the preceding MA < the MA before it AND > the preceding higher-timeframe open, OR reverse is enabled AND the still-forming higher-timeframe MA > the preceding MA AND < the still-forming higher-timeframe open AND the preceding MA > the MA before it AND < the preceding higher-timeframe open.
Short: At the opening of the configurable decision-timeframe bar, whose timeframe may be the chart timeframe or higher or lower, AND close-hour filtering is disabled OR the bar's opening hour < the close hour, AND either reverse is disabled AND the still-forming higher-timeframe MA > the preceding MA AND < the still-forming higher-timeframe open AND the preceding MA > the MA before it AND < the preceding higher-timeframe open, OR reverse is enabled AND the still-forming higher-timeframe MA < the preceding MA AND > the still-forming higher-timeframe open AND the preceding MA < the MA before it AND > the preceding higher-timeframe open.
Exit: long, close-hour filtering is enabled AND a decision-timeframe bar opens at an hour >= the close hour, OR the stop is set AND price <= trailing stop, OR take profit is enabled AND price >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, close-hour filtering is enabled AND a decision-timeframe bar opens at an hour >= the close hour, OR the stop is set AND price >= trailing stop, OR take profit is enabled AND price <= entry price - take-profit distance.
