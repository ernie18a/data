Calculation: Confluence score = Sum the signed weights of nine comparisons across the chart timeframe and two successively higher timeframes, using each higher timeframe's bar still forming. For each timeframe, add the stochastic weight if stochastic main > signal, otherwise subtract it; add the RSI weight if close-based RSI > its midpoint, otherwise subtract it; add the MACD weight if close-based MACD main > signal, otherwise subtract it. Use the same indicator parameters across timeframes, stochastic calculated from highs and lows with simple moving average smoothing, and MACD signal calculated as a simple moving average. The two higher timeframes have equal weights for each indicator; their stochastic weights are greater than their RSI weights, which are greater than their MACD weights. On the chart timeframe, the stochastic weight equals the higher-timeframe RSI weight, and the RSI weight is greater than the MACD weight. All weights are positive.
