Calculation: Indicator timeframes = six ordered timeframes, each potentially lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe; use each timeframe's bar still forming at the start of the chart bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initial stop at entry price minus the stop-loss distance; at each new chart bar, replace it with chart open minus the trailing distance if trailing is enabled AND chart open minus entry price exceeds the trailing distance plus the trailing step AND the existing stop is below chart open minus the trailing distance minus the trailing step.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initial stop at entry price plus the stop-loss distance; at each new chart bar, replace it with chart open plus the trailing distance if trailing is enabled AND entry price minus chart open exceeds the trailing distance plus the trailing step AND the existing stop is above chart open plus the trailing distance plus the trailing step.
Long: No open position AND reverse disabled AND, on every indicator timeframe, shorter-period moving average of close > longer-period moving average of close AND RSI of close > midpoint AND CCI of typical price > zero AND stochastic main line using high and low > lower threshold AND MACD main line of close > MACD signal line.
Long: No open position AND reverse enabled AND, on every indicator timeframe, shorter-period moving average of close < longer-period moving average of close AND RSI of close < midpoint AND CCI of typical price < zero AND stochastic main line using high and low < upper threshold AND MACD main line of close < MACD signal line.
Short: No open position AND reverse disabled AND, on every indicator timeframe, shorter-period moving average of close < longer-period moving average of close AND RSI of close < midpoint AND CCI of typical price < zero AND stochastic main line using high and low < upper threshold AND MACD main line of close < MACD signal line.
Short: No open position AND reverse enabled AND, on every indicator timeframe, shorter-period moving average of close > longer-period moving average of close AND RSI of close > midpoint AND CCI of typical price > zero AND stochastic main line using high and low > lower threshold AND MACD main line of close > MACD signal line.
Exit: long, low <= long trailing stop OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= short trailing stop OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
