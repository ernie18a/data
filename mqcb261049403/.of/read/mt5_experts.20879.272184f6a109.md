Long: chart bar high >= high of the last completed bar on the selected breakdown timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe) + breakout offset AND (fast moving average > slow moving average on the forming chart-timeframe bar, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback, if both filters are enabled) AND open long positions < configured maximum AND no long was opened during the current breakdown-timeframe bar
Short: chart bar low <= low of the last completed bar on the selected breakdown timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe) - breakout offset AND (fast moving average < slow moving average on the forming chart-timeframe bar, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback, if both filters are enabled) AND open short positions < configured maximum AND no short was opened during the current breakdown-timeframe bar
Exit: long, chart bar low <= active stop-loss price OR chart bar high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, chart bar high >= active stop-loss price OR chart bar low <= entry price - take-profit distance
