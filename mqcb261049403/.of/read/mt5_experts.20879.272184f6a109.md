Calculation: Long trailing stop = initial stop at entry price minus stop-loss distance; when trailing is enabled and current price minus entry price exceeds trailing distance plus trailing step and the existing stop is below current price minus trailing distance minus trailing step, update the stop to current price minus trailing distance.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initial stop at entry price plus stop-loss distance; when trailing is enabled and entry price minus current price exceeds trailing distance plus trailing step and the existing stop is above current price plus trailing distance plus trailing step or is absent, update the stop to current price plus trailing distance.
Long: Current price >= last completed breakout-timeframe bar high plus indent AND (moving-average filter disabled OR current chart-timeframe shorter-period moving average > current chart-timeframe longer-period moving average) AND open long position count < maximum AND long entries in the current breakout-timeframe bar = 0.
Short: Current price <= last completed breakout-timeframe bar low minus indent AND (moving-average filter disabled OR current chart-timeframe shorter-period moving average < current chart-timeframe longer-period moving average) AND open short position count < maximum AND short entries in the current breakout-timeframe bar = 0.
Exit: long, current price <= long trailing stop OR current price >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, current price >= short trailing stop OR current price <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
