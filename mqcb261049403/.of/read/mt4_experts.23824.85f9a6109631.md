Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Long: At the chart bar open, the longer-period EMA of typical price on the forming chart bar > the shorter-period linear weighted MA of typical price on the forming chart bar AND the low of the second-last completed chart bar < the high of the last completed chart bar AND the second-last completed higher-timeframe bar closes below its open AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar closes above its open AND the absolute body of the last completed higher-timeframe bar < the absolute body of the second-last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the absolute difference between the chart bar open and the close of the second-last completed chart bar > the entry-distance threshold.
Short: At the chart bar open, the longer-period EMA of typical price on the forming chart bar < the shorter-period linear weighted MA of typical price on the forming chart bar AND the low of the last completed chart bar < the high of the second-last completed chart bar AND the second-last completed higher-timeframe bar closes above its open AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar closes below its open AND the absolute body of the last completed higher-timeframe bar < the absolute body of the second-last completed higher-timeframe bar AND the absolute difference between the chart bar open and the close of the last completed chart bar > the entry-distance threshold.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
