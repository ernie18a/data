Calculation: Long-term EMA = EMA of typical price ((high + low + close) / 3) over the longer lookback on the chart timeframe, using the current bar.
Calculation: Short-term LWMA = linearly weighted moving average of typical price over the shorter lookback on the chart timeframe, using the current bar.
Long: Long-term EMA > short-term LWMA AND low two chart bars ago < high one chart bar ago AND open > close on the second-most-recent completed bar of a higher timeframe AND open < close on the most-recent completed chart bar AND the latter candle body is smaller than the higher-timeframe candle body AND absolute current price minus close two chart bars ago > the configured point-distance threshold.
Short: Long-term EMA < short-term LWMA AND low one chart bar ago < high two chart bars ago AND open < close on the second-most-recent completed bar of a higher timeframe AND open > close on the most-recent completed chart bar AND the latter candle body is smaller than the higher-timeframe candle body AND absolute current price minus close one chart bar ago > the configured point-distance threshold.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR (high > entry price + break-even activation distance AND low <= entry price + configured locked-profit distance).
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance OR (low < entry price - break-even activation distance AND high >= entry price - configured locked-profit distance).
