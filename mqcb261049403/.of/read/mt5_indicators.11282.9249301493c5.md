Calculation: Range level = highest high over a lookback ending at the current bar minus a parameterized fraction of the difference between that highest high and the lowest low over the same lookback.
Long: Range level > range level a parameterized number of chart bars earlier.
Short: Range level < range level a parameterized number of chart bars earlier.
Long: Range level on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > range level a parameterized number of higher-timeframe bars earlier.
Short: Range level on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < range level a parameterized number of higher-timeframe bars earlier.
