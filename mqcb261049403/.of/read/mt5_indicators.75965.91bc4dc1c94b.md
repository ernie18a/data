Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is strictly greater/less than the highs/lows of a configurable number of bars on each side; include the still-forming bar only when searching for the most recent stop swing.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = separately for highs and lows, take the last completed bar's high/low from the highest higher timeframe and the most recent confirmed swing highs/lows within configurable lookbacks on the chart timeframe and each of several lower higher timeframes. For each level in source order, replace its price with the average of itself and all other same-side levels within a configurable matching distance, using already replaced prices for earlier levels; its strength is the number of averaged levels. Rank by strength, preserving source order for ties, and retain a configurable maximum of levels per side, skipping levels within a configurable fraction of the matching distance of a previously retained level. The matching distance is either a fixed price distance or a configurable multiple of chart-timeframe ATR from the last completed bar, subject to a configurable minimum.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = the last completed chart bar closes above/below its open AND, if displacement is required, its body is at least a configurable multiple of its ATR AND its close is above/below the preceding bar's high/low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the most recent completed chart bar within a configurable lookback, excluding the last completed bar, that closes below/above its open and whose high or low is within a configurable ATR multiple of a retained high-side/low-side liquidity level; if displacement is required, the following bar closes above/below that candidate's high/low and its body is at least a configurable multiple of the candidate's body. ATR uses the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Long/short stop swing = the most recent confirmed swing low/high on a lower timeframe, searched within a configurable lookback starting at the second most recent bar; swing confirmation can use the still-forming lower-timeframe bar.
Long: A bullish order block exists at a retained high-side liquidity level.
Short: A bearish order block exists at a retained low-side liquidity level.
Long: At a retained low-side liquidity level, (sweep is not required OR the last completed chart bar's low < level AND close > level) AND (close confirmation is not required OR close > level) AND (displacement is not required OR bullish displacement) AND long stop swing < close AND close minus long stop swing > a configurable minimum price distance AND no higher-priority short reversal is selected.
Short: At a retained high-side liquidity level, (sweep is not required OR the last completed chart bar's high > level AND close < level) AND (close confirmation is not required OR close < level) AND (displacement is not required OR bearish displacement) AND short stop swing > close AND short stop swing minus close > a configurable minimum price distance.
Long: Breakouts are enabled AND no reversal is selected AND at a retained high-side liquidity level the last completed chart bar's close > level AND the preceding chart bar's close <= level AND bullish displacement AND long stop swing < close AND close minus long stop swing > a configurable minimum price distance.
Short: Breakouts are enabled AND no reversal or long breakout is selected AND at a retained low-side liquidity level the last completed chart bar's close < level AND the preceding chart bar's close >= level AND bearish displacement AND short stop swing > close AND short stop swing minus close > a configurable minimum price distance.
