Calculation: Oscillator = fast adaptive trend line (FATL) of close minus slow adaptive trend line (SATL) of close. Calculate on a configurable higher, lower, or chart timeframe; use the last completed bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar offset, and compare it with preceding bars of that timeframe.
Long: Oscillator on the selected bar > oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator on the bar before that > oscillator on the preceding bar
Short: Oscillator on the selected bar < oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator on the bar before that < oscillator on the preceding bar
Exit: short, oscillator on the selected bar > oscillator on the preceding bar
Exit: long, oscillator on the selected bar < oscillator on the preceding bar
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
