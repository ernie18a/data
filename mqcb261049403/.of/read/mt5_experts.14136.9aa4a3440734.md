Calculation: Applied price = select by input index: 1 open; 2 high; 3 low; 4 (high+low)/2; 5 (high+low+close)/3; 6 (high+low+2*close)/4; 7 close; 8 (open+close)/2; 9 (open+high+low+close)/4; 10 high if close>open, low if close<open, otherwise close; 11 (high+close)/2 if close>open, (low+close)/2 if close<open, otherwise close; 12 (2*close+low-high)/4 if close<open, (2*close+high-low)/4 if close>open, otherwise close/2; other indices close.
Calculation: Filter = weighted sum of applied prices on the selected indicator timeframe, with weights from the evaluated bar backward: [0.24470985659780, 0.23139774006970, 0.20613796947320, 0.17166230340640, 0.13146907903600, 0.08950387549560, 0.04960091651250, 0.01502270569607, -0.01188033734430, -0.02989873856137, -0.03898967104900, -0.04014113626390, -0.03511968085800, -0.02611613850342, -0.01539056955666, -0.00495353651394, 0.00368588764825, 0.00963614049782, 0.01265138888314, 0.01307496106868, 0.01169702291063, 0.00974841844086, 0.00898900012545, -0.00649745721156]. The indicator timeframe is configurable as higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; evaluate either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar according to the configurable signal-bar offset, and compare with its immediately preceding bar.
Calculation: Reference close = indicator-timeframe close at the evaluated bar index minus (round(seconds elapsed since midnight at the evaluated bar's opening time / indicator-timeframe duration in seconds)+1).
Calculation: Trigger = reference close plus a configurable positive price offset if filter >= reference close, otherwise reference close minus that offset.
Long: Filter > trigger AND previous filter < previous trigger.
Short: Filter < trigger AND previous filter > previous trigger.
Exit: long, filter < trigger OR low <= entry price minus configured stop distance OR high >= entry price plus configured profit distance.
Exit: short, filter > trigger OR high >= entry price plus configured stop distance OR low <= entry price minus configured profit distance.
