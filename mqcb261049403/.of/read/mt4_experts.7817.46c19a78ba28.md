Calculation: Daily pivot levels = using H, L, C of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than chart): P=(H+L+C)/3; R1=2P-L; S1=2P-H; R2=P+(H-L); S2=P-(H-L); R3=2P+H-2L; S3=2P-2H+L; M5=(R2+R3)/2; M4=(R1+R2)/2; M3=(P+R1)/2; M2=(P+S1)/2; M1=(S1+S2)/2; M0=(S2+S3)/2
Calculation: Ladder = the levels sorted ascending: S3, M0, S2, M1, S1, M2, P, M3, R1, M4, R2, M5, R3
Calculation: Resistance target / Support target = the ladder level immediately above / immediately below current price (current price = close of the still-forming bar); exception: if price is between M5 and R3, Support target = S3 and Resistance target = M0
Calculation: Indicators use chart-timeframe bars. ADX, +DI, -DI (Wilder). OsMA (MACD histogram). EMA_fast = EMA of close with a shorter period; EMA_slow = EMA of open with a longer period. "Current" means the still-forming bar, "previous" means the last completed bar. EMAs use the last completed bar only.
Long: no open position AND (Resistance target - price) > min distance AND ADX(current) > ADX threshold AND +DI(current) > +DI(previous) AND +DI(current) > -DI(current) AND (EMA_fast - EMA_slow) >= small min gap AND OsMA(current) > OsMA(previous)
Short: no open position AND (price - Support target) > min distance AND ADX(current) > ADX threshold AND -DI(current) > -DI(previous) AND +DI(current) < -DI(current) AND (EMA_slow - EMA_fast) >= small min gap AND OsMA(current) < OsMA(previous)
Exit: long, price <= entry price - fixed stop distance OR price >= Resistance target fixed at entry OR price <= trailing stop, where the trailing stop is active only after price has exceeded entry price + trail distance and equals the highest price since entry - trail distance (it only moves up)
Exit: short, price >= entry price + fixed stop distance OR price <= Support target fixed at entry OR price >= trailing stop, where the trailing stop is active only after price has fallen below entry price - trail distance and equals the lowest price since entry + trail distance (it only moves down)
