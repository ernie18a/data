Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pullback direction = scan shorter-period linear weighted moving averages of typical price in ascending period order, each shorter than the long-period average; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming for both averages and the last completed higher-timeframe bar for its open, low, and high. Return long for the first period where the shorter average > the longer average AND the completed bar low <= the shorter average AND its open > the shorter average; return short for the first period where the shorter average < the longer average AND the completed bar high >= the shorter average AND its open < the shorter average.
Long: At the chart bar open, higher-timeframe pullback direction = long AND shorter-period chart-timeframe linear weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period chart-timeframe linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous chart bar low < chart bar high two bars ago AND absolute difference between current chart bar open and chart bar close two bars ago > a price-distance threshold.
Short: At the chart bar open, higher-timeframe pullback direction = short AND shorter-period chart-timeframe linear weighted moving average of typical price < longer-period chart-timeframe linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous chart bar low < chart bar high two bars ago AND absolute difference between current chart bar open and previous chart bar close > a price-distance threshold.
Exit: long, price <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, price >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, stop adjustment enabled = true AND a chart bar open since entry < entry price minus activation distance AND that open < entry price minus stop-adjustment distance AND that open > entry price minus take-profit distance AND price >= entry price minus stop-adjustment distance.
