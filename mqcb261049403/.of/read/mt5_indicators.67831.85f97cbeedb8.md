Calculation: Bands = Bollinger Bands of close; middle is the simple moving average, and upper and lower are the middle plus or minus a standard-deviation multiple.
Calculation: High and low averages = shorter- and longer-lookback linear weighted moving averages of high and low, respectively; corresponding high and low averages use the same lookbacks.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close, with a longer lookback than the bands and weighted averages.
Calculation: Up fractal = a bar whose high is at least every high in a configurable number of preceding bars and strictly greater than every high in a configurable number of following bars; a down fractal has a low at most every preceding low and strictly below every following low. A fractal is available only after its following bars have formed.
Calculation: Extreme state = independently track long and short extreme signals. An extreme signal on the previous bar sets its side to waiting for a target and resets the opposite side. The first subsequent target signal sets that side to target reached. A qualifying fractal signal sets it to fractal reached, which also satisfies target reached. When the long side has reached its target and the previous high exceeds the previous upper band, reset the long side after evaluating the fractal signal. Process the extreme update, target signal, fractal signal, and long reset in that order on each bar.
Long: EMA <= middle band AND both high averages >= middle band AND both low averages >= middle band.
Short: EMA >= middle band AND both high averages <= middle band AND both low averages <= middle band.
Long: Both high averages < middle band AND open < close AND close > both high averages AND close < middle band.
Short: Both low averages > middle band AND open > close AND close < both low averages AND close > middle band.
Long: (Previous close < previous middle band AND close > middle band AND close > EMA) OR (previous close < previous EMA AND close > EMA AND close > middle band).
Short: (Previous close > previous middle band AND close < middle band AND close < EMA) OR (previous close > previous EMA AND close < EMA AND close < middle band).
Long: Close > both high averages AND ((open < middle band AND close > middle band) OR (previous open < previous middle band AND previous close < previous middle band AND open > middle band AND close > middle band)).
Short: Close < both low averages AND ((open > middle band AND close < middle band) OR (previous open > previous middle band AND previous close > previous middle band AND open < middle band AND close < middle band)).
Long: Open > middle band AND close > middle band AND close > both high averages AND close < upper band AND the preceding long condition is false.
Short: Open < middle band AND close < middle band AND close < both low averages AND close > lower band AND the preceding short condition is false.
Long: The shorter low average <= lower band on at least one of the current and previous two bars AND at least one of the current and previous bars has close > open AND ((low <= lower band AND close > lower band) OR (previous low <= previous lower band AND close > lower band) OR (open >= lower band AND previous close <= previous lower band)) AND no extreme long signal occurred on either of the previous two bars.
Short: The shorter high average >= upper band on at least one of the current and previous two bars AND at least one of the current and previous bars has close < open AND ((high >= upper band AND close < upper band) OR (previous high >= current upper band AND close < upper band) OR (open <= upper band AND previous close >= previous upper band)) AND no extreme short signal occurred on either of the previous two bars.
Long: Long extreme state is waiting for a target AND high >= either high average; this is a long target signal.
Short: Short extreme state is waiting for a target AND low <= either low average; this is a short target signal.
Long: Long extreme state has reached its target AND the previous bar is an available down fractal AND neither the current nor previous bar has a long target signal AND the previous bar has no momentum short signal AND previous low < previous middle band; evaluate on the current bar, although the marking is placed on the previous bar.
Short: Short extreme state has reached its target AND the previous bar is an available up fractal AND neither the current nor previous bar has a short target signal AND the previous bar has no momentum long signal AND previous high > previous middle band; evaluate on the current bar, although the marking is placed on the previous bar.
Long: Close >= upper band AND no extreme short signal occurs on the current bar; this is a momentum long signal.
Short: Close <= lower band AND no extreme long signal occurs on the current bar; this is a momentum short signal.
Long: Low <= either low average AND close >= either low average AND close >= middle band.
Short: High >= either high average AND close <= either high average AND close <= middle band.
Long: Low < EMA AND close > EMA AND, within a shorter lookback excluding the current bar, two consecutive lows were each > their respective EMAs AND, within a longer lookback excluding the current bar, absolute close minus open was > a configurable multiple of ATR.
Short: High > EMA AND close < EMA AND, within a shorter lookback excluding the current bar, two consecutive highs were each < their respective EMAs AND, within a longer lookback excluding the current bar, absolute close minus open was > a configurable multiple of ATR.

Short: EMA > upper band on at least one of the current and previous three bars AND close < open AND close < upper band AND high >= its corresponding upper band on at least one of the current and previous two bars.
Long: Low < lower band AND close > lower band AND open > lower band.
Short: High > upper band AND close < upper band AND open < upper band.
