Calculation: Signal bar = a selectable bar on the chart timeframe or on a higher or lower timeframe; select either the still-forming bar or the last completed bar. Previous signal bar = the immediately preceding bar on that timeframe.
Calculation: MACD = shorter-period EMA minus longer-period EMA of the selected price; its signal line is an SMA of MACD.
Calculation: Active stop = the initial stop, if enabled, updated after entry using the selected trailing mode. For a long position, update only when a candidate stop is above the existing stop; for a short position, update only when it is below the existing stop. An absent stop has value zero. Mode 1: the candidate is current price minus the stop-loss distance for a long, or current price plus that distance for a short. Mode 2: after profit exceeds the trailing distance, use the same candidate with the trailing distance in place of the stop-loss distance. Mode 3: when profit exceeds the first or second trigger, the respective candidate is entry price plus the trigger distance minus its offset for a long, or entry price minus the trigger distance plus its offset for a short; when profit exceeds the third trigger, the candidate is current price minus the third offset for a long, or current price plus the third offset for a short. Evaluate the three candidates in trigger order against the stop existing before the updates; the last qualifying candidate replaces it.
Long: Position is flat AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD on signal bar < its signal line) AND (stochastic level filter disabled OR stochastic main line on signal bar < lower threshold) AND (stochastic cross filter disabled OR stochastic main line on signal bar < lower threshold AND stochastic main line on signal bar > its signal line AND stochastic main line on previous signal bar < its signal line) AND (parabolic SAR filter disabled OR SAR on signal bar <= current price AND SAR on previous signal bar > SAR on signal bar) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum of open on signal bar < lower threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR shorter-period moving average on signal bar > longer-period moving average on signal bar).
Short: Position is flat AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD on signal bar > its signal line) AND (stochastic level filter disabled OR stochastic main line on signal bar > upper threshold) AND (stochastic cross filter disabled OR stochastic main line on signal bar > upper threshold AND stochastic main line on signal bar < its signal line AND stochastic main line on previous signal bar > its signal line) AND (parabolic SAR filter disabled OR SAR on signal bar >= current price AND SAR on previous signal bar < SAR on signal bar) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum of open on signal bar > upper threshold) AND (moving-average filter disabled OR shorter-period moving average on signal bar < longer-period moving average on signal bar).
Exit: long, (take profit enabled AND price >= entry price + take-profit distance) OR (active stop exists AND price <= active stop).
Exit: short, (take profit enabled AND price <= entry price - take-profit distance) OR (active stop exists AND price >= active stop).
