Calculation: Previous day high/low = highest high and lowest low of the previous calendar day
Calculation: Current day open = open of the first bar of the current calendar day
Calculation: Long entry level = previous day high − 0.382 × (previous day high − previous day low)
Calculation: Long stop level = previous day high − 0.618 × (previous day high − previous day low)
Calculation: Long targets = long entry level + 0.618, 1, and 1.618 times the previous day’s high-low range
Calculation: Short entry level = previous day low + 0.382 × (previous day high − previous day low)
Calculation: Short stop level = previous day low + 0.618 × (previous day high − previous day low)
Calculation: Short targets = short entry level − 0.618, 1, and 1.618 times the previous day’s high-low range
Calculation: Risk/reward ratio for each target = target distance from entry divided by stop distance from entry
Long: close > current day open AND risk/reward ratio for each long target > 2
Short: close < current day open AND risk/reward ratio for each short target > 2
