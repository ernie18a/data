Calculation: Regression slope = 100 times the least-squares linear regression slope of an N-bar simple moving average of close over the same N higher-timeframe bars, ordered oldest to newest; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming or a selected completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trend = 1 when regression slope is greater than its value a selected number of higher-timeframe bars earlier, -1 when it is less, and otherwise the previous higher-timeframe trend; initialize to 0.
Long: Trend = 1
Short: Trend = -1
