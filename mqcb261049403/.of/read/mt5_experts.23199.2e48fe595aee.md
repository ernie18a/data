Calculation: Long-term smoothed RSI = 6-bar simple moving average of Wilder RSI calculated from closes on its configured timeframe, using the last completed bars; its timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Short-term smoothed RSI = 6-bar simple moving average of Wilder RSI calculated from closes on its configured timeframe, using the last completed bars; its timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. The long-term RSI uses the long-term period and timeframe; the short-term RSI uses the short-term period and timeframe.
Calculation: Regression slope magnitude = absolute difference between the newest and oldest fitted values of a linear regression of closes across the lookback of higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Long: position side = flat AND regression slope magnitude > its threshold AND integer(long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) > its lower threshold AND integer(short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) < its lower threshold AND long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar > its value at the longer lookback AND short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar > its value on the prior completed bar
Short: position side = flat AND regression slope magnitude > its threshold AND integer(long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) < its upper threshold AND integer(short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) > its upper threshold AND long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar < its value at the longer lookback AND short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar < its value on the prior completed bar
Exit: long, integer(long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) < its upper threshold AND integer(short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) > its upper threshold AND long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar < its value at the longer lookback AND short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar < its value on the prior completed bar
Exit: short, integer(long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) > its lower threshold AND integer(short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar) < its lower threshold AND long-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar > its value at the longer lookback AND short-term smoothed RSI on its last completed bar > its value on the prior completed bar
