Calculation: Smoothed RSI = simple moving average of Wilder RSI of close, with the RSI period and smoothing period separately parameterized.
Calculation: Regression displacement = absolute difference between the fitted values at the newest and oldest bars of a linear regression of close, including the forming bar of a separately configured timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long-timeframe RSI = smoothed RSI on the last completed bar of a configurable timeframe; short-timeframe RSI = smoothed RSI on the last completed bar of a second, shorter timeframe. Either timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. The long-timeframe RSI uses a longer RSI period; both use the same smoothing period.
Calculation: Long signal = long-timeframe RSI truncated toward zero > long-RSI buy threshold AND short-timeframe RSI truncated toward zero < short-RSI buy threshold AND long-timeframe RSI > its value a configurable number of its own bars earlier AND short-timeframe RSI > its value on its preceding bar.
Calculation: Short signal = long-timeframe RSI truncated toward zero < long-RSI sell threshold AND short-timeframe RSI truncated toward zero > short-RSI sell threshold AND long-timeframe RSI < its value a configurable number of its own bars earlier AND short-timeframe RSI < its value on its preceding bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = at entry, either entry price minus a long stop distance or plus a short stop distance, or the lowest low for a long or highest high for a short over a configured lookback including the forming long-timeframe bar, or the low for a long or high for a short of a configured earlier long-timeframe bar. The initial stop is optional and remains fixed until replaced by a trailing stop.
Calculation: Trailing stop = optional price-distance stop updated at each chart-bar open: for a long, if open - entry price > trailing distance + trailing step AND the existing stop is absent or < open - trailing distance - trailing step, set the stop to open - trailing distance; for a short, if entry price - open > trailing distance + trailing step AND the existing stop is absent or > open + trailing distance + trailing step, set the stop to open + trailing distance.
Long: At chart-bar open, position is flat AND trend trading is enabled AND regression displacement > displacement threshold AND long signal is true.
Short: At chart-bar open, position is flat AND trend trading is enabled AND regression displacement > displacement threshold AND short signal is true.
Exit: long, at chart-bar open, trend trading is enabled AND short signal is true.
Exit: short, at chart-bar open, trend trading is enabled AND long signal is true.
Exit: long, an active initial or trailing stop exists AND chart-bar low <= the active stop.
Exit: short, an active initial or trailing stop exists AND chart-bar high >= the active stop.
