Calculation: fast = short-lookback EMA of close; slow = SMA of open with a longer lookback than fast, displaced so the value at bar t is the SMA computed a fixed number of bars earlier
Calculation: cross level = mean of fast and slow on the two completed bars that bracket the cross (fast[a], fast[a+1], slow[a], slow[a+1], where bar a is the first bar after the cross)
Calculation: envelope(tf) = displaced SMA of close on timeframe tf, upper = SMA*(1+pct), lower = SMA*(1-pct), using the forming bar of tf. Two envelopes are used: one on a daily timeframe (higher than the chart) with the wider pct and longer displacement, and one on an intermediate timeframe (higher than the chart, lower than the daily one) with the narrower pct
Calculation: RSI = RSI of typical price ((high+low+close)/3), short lookback, on the chart timeframe, forming bar
Calculation: channel = highest high / lowest low of the last N completed chart bars (N is a fixed time span divided by the chart bar length)
Long: no open position AND fast crossed above slow (fast>slow and previous bar fast<slow) on the last completed bar or the one before it AND current price >= cross level + trigger distance AND RSI > RSI upper threshold AND price > channel highest high AND price > upper daily envelope AND price > upper intermediate envelope AND the blackout duration has elapsed since the previous entry of either side
Short: no open position AND fast crossed below slow (fast<slow and previous bar fast>slow) on the last completed bar or the one before it AND current price <= cross level - trigger distance AND RSI < RSI lower threshold (lower < upper threshold) AND price < channel lowest low AND price < lower daily envelope AND price < lower intermediate envelope AND the blackout duration has elapsed since the previous entry of either side
Exit: long, price <= max(initial stop, highest price since entry - trail distance), where initial stop = low of the bar 4 bars before the entry bar, fixed at entry; OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance (position is split in two equal parts, one with a nearer and one with a farther take-profit distance, each part exits at its own target)
Exit: short, price >= min(initial stop, lowest price since entry + trail distance), where initial stop = high of the bar 4 bars before the entry bar, fixed at entry; OR price <= entry price - take-profit distance (position is split in two equal parts, one with a nearer and one with a farther take-profit distance, each part exits at its own target)
