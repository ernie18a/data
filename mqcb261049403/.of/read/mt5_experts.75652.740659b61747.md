Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the short-lookback EMA of close with the long-lookback EMA of close on the last completed bar of each of two higher timeframes; each comparison is bullish if the short EMA is greater, bearish if it is less, and neutral if equal. The combined bias is neutral if the two non-neutral biases disagree, otherwise it is the non-neutral bias, or neutral if both are neutral.
Calculation: Range position = (last completed close - lowest low) / (highest high - lowest low), using a lookback of completed chart bars that includes the last completed bar.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR on the last completed chart bar divided by the average of ATR over a lookback of completed chart bars including that bar.
Long: UTC time is within an enabled trading session AND UTC time is before the Friday cutoff AND minute of hour is at or after the configured exclusion period AND (overlap is not required OR time is within the overlap OR time is within the enabled opening window of the earlier session AND higher-timeframe bias is bullish) AND (short EMA on the forming chart bar <= long EMA on the forming chart bar AND short EMA on the last completed chart bar > long EMA on the last completed chart bar OR short EMA on the last completed chart bar > long EMA on the last completed chart bar AND last completed close > close at the momentum lookback AND last completed high >= highest high over the preceding momentum lookback ending one bar before it AND last completed close > preceding close).
Short: UTC time is within an enabled trading session AND UTC time is before the Friday cutoff AND minute of hour is at or after the configured exclusion period AND (overlap is not required OR time is within the overlap OR time is within the enabled opening window of the earlier session AND higher-timeframe bias is bearish) AND (short EMA on the forming chart bar >= long EMA on the forming chart bar AND short EMA on the last completed chart bar < long EMA on the last completed chart bar OR short EMA on the last completed chart bar < long EMA on the last completed chart bar AND last completed close < close at the momentum lookback AND last completed low <= lowest low over the preceding momentum lookback ending one bar before it AND last completed close < preceding close).
Exit: long, runners disabled AND elapsed time since entry >= configured maximum holding time.
Exit: short, runners disabled AND elapsed time since entry >= configured maximum holding time.
