Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: RSI spread = RSI of typical price minus its simple moving average, calculated on the same timeframe.
Long: On any of the indicator's ordered timeframes, the forming bar's close > the last completed bar's close. Each timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
Short: On any of the indicator's ordered timeframes, the forming bar's close < the last completed bar's close. Each timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
Long: On any of the indicator's ordered timeframes, the forming bar's close > its open. Each timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
Short: On any of the indicator's ordered timeframes, the forming bar's close < its open. Each timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
Long: Chart close > the forming bar's open on a longer reference timeframe AND the forming bar's typical price != the last completed bar's typical price on a shorter momentum timeframe. Each referenced timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
Short: Chart close < the forming bar's open on the longer reference timeframe AND the forming bar's typical price != the last completed bar's typical price on the shorter momentum timeframe.
Long: Chart close != the forming bar's open on the longer reference timeframe AND the forming bar's typical price > the last completed bar's typical price on the shorter momentum timeframe.
Short: Chart close != the forming bar's open on the longer reference timeframe AND the forming bar's typical price < the last completed bar's typical price on the shorter momentum timeframe.
Long: Chart close != the forming bar's open on the longer reference timeframe AND the forming bar's typical price > the last completed bar's typical price on the shorter momentum timeframe AND, for each of four ordered confirmation timeframes shorter than the reference timeframe, the forming reference bar's close > the confirmation timeframe's last completed close AND the confirmation timeframe's forming close > its forming open AND the forming MACD main line > its last completed value AND the forming MACD main line minus its signal line > its last completed value AND the forming RSI spread > its last completed value. The momentum timeframe is the second-shortest confirmation timeframe; MACD uses the longest confirmation timeframe, and RSI spread uses the momentum timeframe. Each confirmation timeframe may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe.
