Calculation: Step line = Calculate Williams %R plus 100. For each step size, initialize the upper band to positive infinity, the lower band to negative infinity, the trend to 0, and the line to 0. On each bar, set the candidate upper band to Williams %R plus 100 plus twice the step size, and the candidate lower band to Williams %R plus 100 minus twice the step size. Set trend to +1 if Williams %R plus 100 is greater than the previous upper band, to -1 if it is less than the previous lower band, and otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is +1, set the lower band to the greater of its candidate and its previous value, and set the line to the lower band plus the step size. If trend is -1, set the upper band to the lesser of its candidate and its previous value, and set the line to the upper band minus the step size. Otherwise retain the line. Bands not adjusted by the trend use their candidate values. Calculate a fast line with a smaller step size and a slow line with a larger step size.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = On a higher timeframe, calculate the step lines using either the still-forming bar or a selected completed bar. Set direction to +1 when the fast line crosses above the slow line and to -1 when it crosses below; otherwise retain the previous direction, initialized to 0.
Long: Chart-timeframe fast step line crosses above chart-timeframe slow step line OR higher-timeframe direction = +1
Short: Chart-timeframe fast step line crosses below chart-timeframe slow step line OR higher-timeframe direction = -1
