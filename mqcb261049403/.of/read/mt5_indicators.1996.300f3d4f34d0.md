Calculation: Smoothed line = smooth the selected price at a long lookback and at half that lookback, subtract the long-lookback result from twice the half-lookback result, then smooth that difference at a shorter, square-root-related lookback; apply the same calculation to higher-timeframe bars for the higher-timeframe line.
Long: On the last completed chart bar, the smoothed line is higher than on the prior bar AND the prior bar’s line was not higher than the bar before it; OR the higher-timeframe line is higher than on its prior bar AND its prior bar’s line was not higher than the bar before it (using the higher-timeframe bar still forming).
Short: On the last completed chart bar, the smoothed line is lower than on the prior bar AND the prior bar’s line was not lower than the bar before it; OR the higher-timeframe line is lower than on its prior bar AND its prior bar’s line was not lower than the bar before it (using the higher-timeframe bar still forming).
