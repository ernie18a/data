Calculation: ATR-based NRTR stop lines = trailing levels offset from prior bars’ lows (bullish) or highs (bearish) by ATR × a multiplier; the active trend switches when the prior bar’s low or high crosses its stop.
Calculation: Bullish reversal marker = the NRTR trend switches to bullish; bearish reversal marker = it switches to bearish.
Long: bullish reversal marker is present on the last completed bar of the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe, as configured) AND current time is within the configured trading interval
Short: bearish reversal marker is present on the last completed bar of the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe, as configured) AND current time is within the configured trading interval
Exit: long, bearish NRTR stop line is present OR current time is outside the configured trading interval
Exit: short, bullish NRTR stop line is present OR current time is outside the configured trading interval
