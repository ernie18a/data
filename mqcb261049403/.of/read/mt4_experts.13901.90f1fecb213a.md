Long: On the last completed chart-timeframe bar, ATR(1) > Volatility multiplier × ATR(longer lookback), close > open, high = highest high of the 24 most recent completed chart-timeframe bars, and 0.3 × (high − low) ≥ high − close; AND, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, Alligator Lips > Teeth > Jaw and open and close > Teeth. Use shorter lookback for ATR(1) than for the average-volatility ATR.
Short: On the last completed chart-timeframe bar, ATR(1) > Volatility multiplier × ATR(longer lookback), close < open, low = lowest low of the 20 most recent completed chart-timeframe bars, and 0.3 × (high − low) > close − low; AND, on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, Alligator Lips < Teeth < Jaw and open and close < Teeth. Use shorter lookback for ATR(1) than for the average-volatility ATR.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss factor × ATR(1) OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit factor × ATR(1) (ATR on the chart timeframe, using the last completed bar when stop and target are set)
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss factor × ATR(1) OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit factor × ATR(1) (ATR on the chart timeframe, using the last completed bar when stop and target are set)
