Calculation: All calculations use completed chart bars. States initially have no direction and retain their previous direction when neither directional condition is met; equal values retain the previous slope direction. EMA uses alpha = 2/(length+1) and starts at the first input. Volume-based indicators use volume.
Calculation: ATR trailing state = initialize Wilder ATR with the mean of true range over its lookback, trailing level = close - factor × ATR, state = long. On each subsequent bar update ATR using Wilder smoothing. If current and previous close are both above the old trailing level, set state long and set the level to max(old level, close - factor × ATR) if already long, otherwise max(close - factor × ATR, previous close - factor × previous ATR). Then, if current and previous close are both below the resulting level, set state short and set the level to min(old level, close + factor × ATR) if already short, otherwise min(close + factor × ATR, previous close + factor × previous ATR).
Calculation: Smoothed range transform = let midpoint = (high + low)/2, highest high and lowest low cover the current bar and a lookback, and x = clamp(a × ((midpoint - lowest low)/max(highest high - lowest low, small positive floor) - 0.5) + b × previous x, -limit, limit); transform = log((1+x)/(1-x))/2 + previous transform/2. Initialize x and transform to zero.
Calculation: Adjusted MACD change = current minus previous value of MACD main + (MACD main - MACD signal), with a shorter fast EMA and longer slow EMA and an SMA signal, as in MT4. Institutional threshold = ATR × factor, doubled when ADX is below its threshold. Ultimate threshold = population standard deviation of MACD main - signal over its lookback × factor.
Calculation: Component series = ALMA, DEMA, EMA, HMA, KAMA, least-squares moving average, TEMA, WMA and SMA of close; awesome oscillator; SMA of CCI; Coppock curve; Fisher transform of midpoint; MACD main and histogram; price momentum oscillator; PPO main and histogram; rank correlation index of close at three lookbacks, with one short lookback duplicated and one longer; SMA of RSI; relative vigor index; relative volatility index; two true-strength-index variants with the first using shorter smoothing lengths than the second; stochastic momentum index; Pearson correlation of close with chronological bar index; bull-bear power; Chande momentum oscillator; normalized EMA angle; and technical composite. MACD and PPO use shorter fast and longer slow EMAs. Fisher transform uses x = a × ((midpoint - rolling minimum midpoint)/max(rolling maximum midpoint - rolling minimum midpoint, positive floor) - 0.5) + b × previous x, replaces x beyond its positive or negative cutoff with the corresponding signed cap, and outputs log((1+x)/max(1-x, positive floor))/2 + previous output/2. Price momentum oscillator is a shorter second smoothing of a longer first smoothing of one-bar percentage close change, with each smoothing using alpha = 2/length and a scale factor between stages. Relative volatility index is the ratio of EMA-smoothed rolling close standard deviation on rising-close bars to the sum of that EMA and the EMA of standard deviation on all other bars. Normalized EMA angle uses y = (previous EMA - current EMA)/OHLC-average × scale × rolling lowest low/(rolling highest high - rolling lowest low), and returns the rounded angle in degrees acos(1/sqrt(1+y²)), negative when y is positive. Use zero for undefined ratios except relative volatility index, which uses its midpoint.
Calculation: Technical composite = the average of a moving-average score and an oscillator score. The moving-average score is the mean of sign(close - average) for SMA and EMA at each of several progressively longer shared lookbacks. The oscillator score is the mean of eight votes: RSI below its lower threshold and rising = +1, above its upper threshold and falling = -1; smoothed stochastic below its lower threshold and above its signal = +1, above its upper threshold and below its signal = -1; CCI below its negative threshold and rising = +1, above its positive threshold and falling = -1; awesome oscillator positive and rising = +1, negative and falling = -1; close momentum rising = +1, otherwise -1; MACD main above signal = +1, below = -1; Williams %R below its lower threshold and rising = +1, above its upper threshold and falling = -1; bull-bear power positive and rising = +1, negative and falling = -1. Other votes are zero.
Calculation: Component vote = sign of bull-bear power, Chande momentum or technical composite; normalized EMA angle votes +1 at or above its positive threshold and -1 at or below its negative threshold, otherwise zero; every other component votes the direction of its latest nonzero bar-to-bar change. Bull-bear power = high + low - 2 × EMA(close).
Calculation: Confirmed component state = after a warmup, maintain separate pending long and short levels and a direction for each component. A negative vote cancels the long level and a positive vote cancels the short level. A positive vote with close above an existing positive long level confirms long and clears that level; a negative vote with close below an existing positive short level confirms short and clears that level. Then, if a positive vote has no long level and state is not long, store current high as the long level; if a negative vote has no short level and state is not short, store current low as the short level. Neutral votes leave levels and direction unchanged. Count confirmed long and short states separately.
Calculation: Unanimous state = apply the same pending-level procedure to the counts of confirmed component states, using unanimous long as the positive vote and unanimous short as the negative vote; cancel the opposite pending level only on opposite unanimity, and confirm only while unanimity remains. The component list includes both copies of the short rank-correlation series.
Calculation: Threshold state = long when the long count reaches its threshold and the short count is below its threshold; short when the short count reaches its threshold and the long count is below its threshold. If both thresholds are met, switch only to the side whose threshold was newly reached on this bar while the other was already met; otherwise retain the previous state. The short-count threshold is higher than the long-count threshold.
Calculation: Directional states = ATR trailing state; the latest direction in which stochastic main, CCI, DeMarker, money flow index, MACD histogram, accelerator oscillator, awesome oscillator and accumulation/distribution all changed strictly together; sign of the smoothed range transform; the latest direction satisfying the relaxed Heikin-Ashi conditions; the latest direction satisfying the strict Heikin-Ashi conditions; the latest direction indicated by adjusted MACD change against the institutional threshold; the latest direction indicated by adjusted MACD change against the ultimate threshold; unanimous state; threshold state; and the latest direction in which relative vigor index, awesome oscillator, accelerator oscillator, MACD main and MACD histogram all changed strictly together. Heikin-Ashi close is OHLC average and its open is the average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, initially (open + close)/2. Relaxed long requires RSI, stochastic, CCI and Williams %R each above its respective lower threshold and Heikin-Ashi close above open; relaxed short requires each below its respective upper threshold and Heikin-Ashi close below open. Strict long requires RSI, stochastic, CCI and Williams %R above their respective central thresholds, Heikin-Ashi close above open, parabolic SAR below close and bulls power positive; strict short requires the opposite comparisons and bears power negative. The relaxed lower thresholds are below their corresponding strict thresholds and the relaxed upper thresholds are above them. Institutional long requires adjusted MACD change > institutional threshold; institutional short requires change <= negative institutional threshold. Ultimate long requires change > ultimate threshold; ultimate short requires change < negative ultimate threshold.
Calculation: Convergence state = count positive and negative directional states separately. Set long when the positive count reaches the convergence threshold and the negative count does not; set short when the negative count reaches the threshold and the positive count does not; otherwise retain the previous state. Initially it has no direction.
Long: Any directional state = long.
Short: Any directional state = short.
Long: Convergence state = long.
Short: Convergence state = short.
Long: Count of long directional states > half the total number of directional states.
Short: Count of long directional states < half the total number of directional states.
