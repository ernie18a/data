Long: No open position AND, at the first tick of the chart bar, the longer-lookback stochastic main line is above its signal line AND below a threshold AND the absolute difference between the shorter-lookback stochastic main and signal lines is above a threshold.
Short: No open position AND, at the first tick of the chart bar, the longer-lookback stochastic main line is below its signal line AND above a threshold higher than the long-entry threshold AND the absolute difference between the shorter-lookback stochastic main and signal lines is above the same threshold used for long entry.
Exit: Long, at the first tick of the chart bar, RSI of median price is above a threshold AND the longer-lookback stochastic signal line is below its previous completed-bar value AND above an upper threshold.
Exit: Short, at the first tick of the chart bar, RSI of median price is below a threshold lower than the long-exit RSI threshold AND the longer-lookback stochastic signal line is above its previous completed-bar value AND below a lower threshold.
