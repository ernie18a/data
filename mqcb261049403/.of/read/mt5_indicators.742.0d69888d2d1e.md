Calculation: Fisher = On higher-timeframe bars, let midpoint be (high + low) / 2, and let highest and lowest be the maximum and minimum midpoint over the lookback including the current bar. Initialize the smoothed value and Fisher to zero. If highest equals lowest, set the smoothed value to zero; otherwise set it to (midpoint - lowest) / (highest - lowest) - 0.5 + 0.5 * previous smoothed value. Clamp the smoothed value to [-0.9999, 0.9999]. Fisher = 0.25 * ln((1 + smoothed value) / (1 - smoothed value)) + 0.5 * previous Fisher. Evaluate on a selectable higher-timeframe bar: the still-forming bar or a completed bar.
Long: The most recent unequal comparison of Fisher with its preceding higher-timeframe bar's Fisher, through the selected bar, is Fisher > preceding Fisher.
