Calculation: Applied price = configured open, high, low, close, median (high + low)/2, typical (high + low + close)/3, weighted (high + low + 2×close)/4, or OHLC average (open + high + low + close)/4
Calculation: Hodrick–Prescott filter = HP-smoothed applied price over a lookback, using the configured filter timeframe; evaluate its current bar, which may still be forming, including when that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe
Long: Each of the configured consecutive HP-filter values is greater than or equal to the next older value AND (the previous bar had an uptrend state OR (current HP filter is greater than or equal to previous HP filter AND current applied price is greater than or equal to current HP filter))
Short: Each of the configured consecutive HP-filter values is less than or equal to the next older value AND (the previous bar had a downtrend state OR (current HP filter is less than or equal to previous HP filter AND current applied price is less than or equal to current HP filter))
