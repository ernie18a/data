Calculation: Price = a selected OHLC price, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price; trend-biased price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open and (low + close) / 2 otherwise; extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-period moving average of price minus longer-period moving average of price, using the same selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear weighted method. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Upper level = an exponential moving average of the oscillator with period equal to the longer moving-average period plus half the shorter period rounded up; update it when the oscillator > 0 and otherwise retain its previous value. Initialize it to the oscillator on the first bar if positive, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Lower level = an exponential moving average of the oscillator with the same period as the upper level; update it when the oscillator < 0 and otherwise retain its previous value. Initialize it to the oscillator on the first bar if negative, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Middle level = (upper level + lower level) / 2.
Long: Oscillator > upper level OR oscillator > middle level OR oscillator > previous oscillator value, according to the selected outer-level, middle-level, or slope mode, respectively.
Short: Oscillator < lower level OR oscillator < middle level OR oscillator < previous oscillator value, according to the selected outer-level, middle-level, or slope mode, respectively.
