Calculation: Adaptive average = For a selected price series, initialize the average to the preceding price after enough history. At each bar, efficiency = abs(price − price N bars ago) / (epsilon + sum of absolute consecutive price changes over N bars); smoothing = (efficiency × (fast coefficient − slow coefficient) + slow coefficient) raised to a configurable power; average = previous average + smoothing × (price − previous average). Each coefficient is 2 / (its moving-average period + 1); the fast period is shorter than the slow period.
Calculation: Filtered average change = Round the current adaptive-average change to the configured price precision plus two decimal places; the threshold is the similarly rounded product of a configurable multiplier and the population standard deviation of the most recent N adaptive-average changes, including the current change. N is the adaptive average's efficiency lookback.
Long: Filtered average change > threshold.
Short: Filtered average change < −threshold.
Long: At the first chart bar of any of seven independently selected higher or chart timeframes, that timeframe's still-forming bar has filtered average change > threshold; retain that directional state through the remaining chart bars of that timeframe bar.
Short: At the first chart bar of any of seven independently selected higher or chart timeframes, that timeframe's still-forming bar has filtered average change < −threshold; retain that directional state through the remaining chart bars of that timeframe bar.
