Calculation: HMA change standard deviation = population standard deviation of the current and previous consecutive one-bar changes in the Hull Moving Average, over a lookback period
Calculation: Lower threshold = smaller positive multiplier × HMA change standard deviation
Calculation: Upper threshold = larger positive multiplier × HMA change standard deviation
Long: current HMA change > lower threshold AND current HMA change ≤ upper threshold
Long: current HMA change > upper threshold
Short: current HMA change < −lower threshold AND current HMA change ≥ −upper threshold
