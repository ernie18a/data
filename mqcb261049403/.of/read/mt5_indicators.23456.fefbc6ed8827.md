Calculation: Rounded moving average = round the following value to a configurable price increment: 8×EMA1 − 28×EMA2 + 56×EMA3 − 70×EMA4 + 56×EMA5 − 28×EMA6 + 8×EMA7 − EMA8 + a configurable price offset. EMA1 is an EMA of a selected OHLC-derived price; each subsequent EMA is an EMA of the preceding EMA, all with the same length. Seed each EMA with its first available input, starting successive stages on successive bars.
Long: The most recent nonzero change in the rounded moving average is greater than zero, evaluated independently on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Short: The most recent nonzero change in the rounded moving average is less than zero, evaluated independently on the chart timeframe or on any selected higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
