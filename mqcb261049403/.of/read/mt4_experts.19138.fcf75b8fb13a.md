Long: No open position AND the close of the forming reference-timeframe bar > the open of the last completed reference-timeframe bar
Short: No open position AND the close of the forming reference-timeframe bar < the open of the last completed reference-timeframe bar
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance
Calculation: Reference-timeframe bars = bars aggregated at a timeframe that may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe
