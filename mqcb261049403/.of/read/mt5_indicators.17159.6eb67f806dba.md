Calculation: Selected price = Choose open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (open+close)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, trend-biased price, or extreme trend-biased price; the same choices may use Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high+close)/2 when close>open and (low+close)/2 otherwise. Extreme trend-biased price is high when close>open, low when close<open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Adaptive Laguerre filter = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its bar still forming, let p be selected price, N the period, and M=max(integer part of N times the smoothing parameter, 2). Let d=abs(p-previous filter), initialized to zero. Find the minimum and maximum d over the current and preceding bars with offsets less than N. If they are equal, alpha=0; otherwise alpha is the median of the latest M values of (d-minimum)/(maximum-minimum), using the current minimum and maximum for all M values and padding unavailable values with zero. Let gamma=1-alpha clamped between the smallest positive floating-point value and one minus that value. Initialize L0, L1, L2, L3 and the filter to p. On subsequent bars when N>1, set L0=(1-gamma)*p+gamma*previous L0; L1=-gamma*L0+previous L0+gamma*previous L1; L2=-gamma*L1+previous L1+gamma*previous L2; L3=-gamma*L2+previous L2+gamma*previous L3; filter=(L0+2*L1+2*L2+L3)/6. When N<=1, reset all four levels and the filter to p.
Long: Adaptive Laguerre filter > previous filter OR adaptive Laguerre filter = previous filter AND the most recent unequal filter comparison was greater.
Short: Adaptive Laguerre filter < previous filter OR adaptive Laguerre filter = previous filter AND the most recent unequal filter comparison was less.
