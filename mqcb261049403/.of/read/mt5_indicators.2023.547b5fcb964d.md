Calculation: Trend state and bands = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, calculate upper = linear-weighted moving average of high × (1 + percentage shift) + ATR × sensitivity and lower = linear-weighted moving average of low × (1 − percentage shift) − ATR × sensitivity. The two moving averages use the same lookback. Initialize the prior upper and lower bands to zero and the trend to neutral. For each bar, set the trend to up if close > prior upper band, then set it to down if close < prior lower band; otherwise retain the trend. If the trend is up, replace lower with max(lower, prior lower band). If the trend is down, replace upper with min(upper, prior upper band). Carry both bands forward to the next bar. For the higher-timeframe variant, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and evaluate signals only on the first chart bar of that higher-timeframe bar.

Short: Trend changes from up to down.
