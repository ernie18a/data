Calculation: DeMarker momentum oscillator = 100 × twice-smoothed DeMarker momentum / twice-smoothed absolute DeMarker momentum, or 0 when the denominator is 0; momentum is DeMarker minus its value a configurable number of bars earlier. Apply the same configurable smoothing method first with a shorter period and then with a longer period to both momentum series; replace a zero oscillator value with a small positive constant. Calculate on the higher timeframe using the last completed bar, or the still-forming bar when configured.
Calculation: Oscillator signal line = the same smoothing method applied to the oscillator with a longer period than either momentum smoothing period; replace a zero signal-line value with a small positive constant.
Long: Oscillator on the selected higher-timeframe bar > signal line AND oscillator on the preceding higher-timeframe bar <= signal line
Short: Oscillator on the selected higher-timeframe bar < signal line AND oscillator on the preceding higher-timeframe bar >= signal line
Exit: long, oscillator on the selected higher-timeframe bar < signal line OR low <= entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the take-profit distance
Exit: short, oscillator on the selected higher-timeframe bar > signal line OR high >= entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the take-profit distance
