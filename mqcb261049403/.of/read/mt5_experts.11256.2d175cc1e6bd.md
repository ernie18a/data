Calculation: DeMarker TSI = 100 × [selected moving-average smoothing (second lookback) of selected moving-average smoothing (first lookback) of DeMarker momentum] ÷ [selected moving-average smoothing (second lookback) of selected moving-average smoothing (first lookback) of the absolute DeMarker momentum]; DeMarker momentum = DeMarker minus its value at the momentum lookback; DeMarker = average of upward high changes over the DeMarker lookback ÷ (average upward high changes + average downward low changes) over that lookback. Use the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; evaluate its last completed bar and the preceding completed bar.
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving-average smoothing of DeMarker TSI using its signal lookback, on the selected indicator timeframe.
Long: DeMarker TSI on the last completed bar > Signal line on the last completed bar AND DeMarker TSI on the preceding completed bar ≤ Signal line on the preceding completed bar
Short: DeMarker TSI on the last completed bar < Signal line on the last completed bar AND DeMarker TSI on the preceding completed bar ≥ Signal line on the preceding completed bar
Exit: long, DeMarker TSI on the evaluated completed indicator-timeframe bar < Signal line on that bar
Exit: short, DeMarker TSI on the evaluated completed indicator-timeframe bar > Signal line on that bar
