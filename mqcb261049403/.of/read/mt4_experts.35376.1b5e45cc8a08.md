Calculation: MACD line = EMA of close with a shorter period minus EMA of close with a longer period; use independently configurable periods for long and short decisions.
Calculation: Divergence candidates = Evaluate once at the opening of each chart bar. Index chart bars backward from the newly opened bar as 0. For bullish candidates use MACD line M and low P; for bearish candidates use -M and -high P. Let T be a positive MACD threshold. Require M[1], M[2], M[3], M[4] < -T AND M[2] < M[1], M[3], M[4]. Set recent price R to P[j] at the smallest j from 0 through a price-search limit for which P[j] is less than each of P[j+1] through P[j+4]; use 0 if none exists. Scan i backward from 11 through available history. A candidate requires M[i], M[i-1], M[i-2], M[i-3], M[i-4] < -T AND M[i-2] < M[i], M[i-1], M[i-3], M[i-4]. Accept the first candidate; accept each subsequent candidate only if its M[i-2] is strictly less than that of the preceding accepted candidate, otherwise stop scanning. For each accepted candidate, set prior price Q to P[j] at the smallest j from i-4 through i plus the price-search limit satisfying the same four-older-bar price comparison; if none exists, retain the preceding Q, initially 0. A divergence exists if an accepted candidate within a maximum candidate count has (M[i-2] > M[2] AND Q < R) OR (M[i-2] < M[2] AND Q > R).
Long: Position side = flat AND RSI of close on the last completed chart bar < lower RSI threshold AND bullish divergence exists.
Short: Position side = flat AND RSI of close on the last completed chart bar > upper RSI threshold AND bearish divergence exists.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - long stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + long take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + short stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - short take-profit distance.
