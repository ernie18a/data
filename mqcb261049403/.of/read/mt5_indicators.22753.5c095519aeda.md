Calculation: A/D peak trend lines = At decision bar t, examine A/D extrema at offsets from t of unchecked-bars + 1 through unchecked-bars + search-bars - 1, inclusive. A peak is strictly higher than both adjacent A/D values; a trough is strictly lower. For each kind, choose the most recent extremum as the right endpoint and the highest peak or lowest trough as the initial left endpoint, breaking ties in favor of the most recent. A line exists only if the left endpoint is older than the right endpoint and is strictly higher for peaks or strictly lower for troughs. Visit extrema from the initial left endpoint toward, but excluding, the right endpoint. Whenever a peak is above, or a trough is below, the line through the current left and fixed right endpoints, replace the left endpoint with that extremum. Extend the final line using bar indices.
Long: Peak trend line exists AND A/D at t-1 > peak trend line at t-1 AND (A/D at t-2 <= peak trend line at t-2 OR A/D at t-3 <= peak trend line at t-3) AND (CCI filter disabled OR (CCI at t-1 <= lower threshold AND CCI at t-2 < lower threshold)).
Short: Trough trend line exists AND A/D at t-1 < trough trend line at t-1 AND (A/D at t-2 >= trough trend line at t-2 OR A/D at t-3 >= trough trend line at t-3) AND (CCI filter disabled OR (CCI at t-1 >= upper threshold AND CCI at t-2 > upper threshold)).
