Calculation: Weekly pivot levels = From the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, let H, L, and C be its high, low, and close. P=(H+L+C)/3; R1=2P−L; S1=2P−H; R0.5=(P+R1)/2; S0.5=(P+S1)/2; R2=P+(H−L); S2=P−(H−L); R1.5=(R1+R2)/2; S1.5=(S1+S2)/2; R3=2P+(H−2L); S3=2P−(2H−L); R2.5=(R2+R3)/2; S2.5=(S2+S3)/2.
Long: Current minute is one of the configured quarter-hour entry minutes AND ((last completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart is bullish AND the preceding completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart has open ≤ a weekly pivot level AND close ≥ that level, for any of P, R0.5, R1, R1.5, R2, R2.5, S1, S0.5) OR (last completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart is bullish AND each of the two preceding completed bars of a timeframe lower than the chart has low ≤ the same weekly pivot level AND close ≥ that level, for any of P, R0.5, R1, R1.5, R2, R2.5, S1, S0.5)).
Short: Current minute is one of the configured quarter-hour entry minutes AND ((last completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart is bearish AND the preceding completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart has open ≥ a weekly pivot level AND close ≤ that level, for any of P, S0.5, S1, S1.5, S2, S2.5, R1, R0.5) OR (last completed bar of a timeframe lower than the chart is bearish AND each of the two preceding completed bars of a timeframe lower than the chart has high ≥ the same weekly pivot level AND close ≤ that level, for any of P, S0.5, S1, S1.5, S2, S2.5, R1, R0.5)).
Exit: Long, low ≤ attached stop-loss OR high ≥ attached take-profit OR (day of week is Friday AND hour equals the configured close hour AND minute is within the configured close-minute interval)
Exit: Short, high ≥ attached stop-loss OR low ≤ attached take-profit OR (day of week is Friday AND hour equals the configured close hour AND minute is within the configured close-minute interval)
