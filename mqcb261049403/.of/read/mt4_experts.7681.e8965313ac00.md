Calculation: Pivot levels = From the last completed bar of a higher timeframe, let P = (high + low + close) / 3, R1 = 2P − low, S1 = 2P − high, R2 = P + high − low, S2 = P − high + low, R3 = 2P + high − 2low, and S3 = 2P − 2high + low. R0.5 and S0.5 are the midpoints of P and R1 or S1; R1.5 and S1.5 are the midpoints of R1 and R2 or S1 and S2; R2.5 and S2.5 are the midpoints of R2 and R3 or S2 and S3. Round each level to the instrument's price precision when calculated.
Long: Minute of the bar datetime equals a configured entry minute AND the last completed bar of a lower timeframe has close >= open AND the preceding bar of that timeframe has open <= a level AND close >= the same level, where the level is any of P, R0.5, R1, R1.5, R2, R2.5, S1, or S0.5.
Long: Minute of the bar datetime equals a configured entry minute AND the last completed bar of a shorter lower timeframe has close >= open AND each of the two preceding bars of that timeframe has low <= a level AND close >= the same level, where the level is any of P, R0.5, R1, R1.5, R2, R2.5, S1, or S0.5.
Short: Minute of the bar datetime equals a configured entry minute AND the last completed bar of the longer lower timeframe has close <= open AND the preceding bar of that timeframe has open >= a level AND close <= the same level, where the level is any of P, S0.5, S1, S1.5, S2, S2.5, R1, or R0.5.
Short: Minute of the bar datetime equals a configured entry minute AND the last completed bar of the shorter lower timeframe has close <= open AND each of the two preceding bars of that timeframe has high >= a level AND close <= the same level, where the level is any of P, S0.5, S1, S1.5, S2, S2.5, R1, or R0.5.
Exit: long OR short, day of week is Friday AND hour of the bar datetime equals the configured closing hour AND minute of the bar datetime is within the configured closing-minute interval.
