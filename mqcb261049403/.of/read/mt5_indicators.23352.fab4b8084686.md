Calculation: Trend state = Initialize trend, pending upward level, pending downward level, last accepted low, and last accepted high to zero. Process chart bars chronologically after the moving averages, optional RSI, and ATR are available. An upward crossover occurs when the shorter-period moving average of close was strictly below the longer-period moving average on the previous bar and is strictly above it on the current bar, with RSI disabled or RSI of close above its midpoint. A downward crossover uses the opposite strict comparisons and RSI below its midpoint. On an upward crossover, if the confirmation distance is zero, attempt an upward update; otherwise set the pending upward level to the current low and clear the pending downward level. On a downward crossover, if the confirmation distance is zero, attempt a downward update; otherwise set the pending downward level to the current high and clear the pending upward level. Then, if the pending upward level is nonzero, current high minus that level is at least the confirmation distance, and close is at most high, attempt an upward update and clear the pending upward level. If the pending downward level is nonzero, that level minus current low is at least the confirmation distance, and open is at least close, attempt a downward update and clear the pending downward level. An upward update succeeds when the absolute difference between current low and last accepted low is at least the flat distance; on success, set last accepted low to current low and trend to upward. A downward update succeeds when the absolute difference between current high and last accepted high is at least the flat distance; on success, set last accepted high to current high and trend to downward. Confirmation and flat distances are price-distance parameters.
Long: Trend state is upward.
Short: Trend state is downward.
Long: Trend state calculated independently on a higher or lower timeframe is upward on the selected bar, which may be the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
Short: Trend state calculated independently on a higher or lower timeframe is downward on the selected bar, which may be the last completed bar or the bar still forming.
