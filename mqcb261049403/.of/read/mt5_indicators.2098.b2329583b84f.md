Calculation: StepMA direction = On each calculation timeframe bar, compute the mean of high−low over that bar and the preceding Length−1 bars. Use either the fixed step (integer-truncated Kv×StepSize) or the adaptive step (integer-truncated 0.5×Kv×(maximum + minimum of these mean ranges since initialization)/point size). Form bounds around close at twice the step distance (or, in HighLow mode, lower=high−distance and upper=low+distance). Set direction up if close exceeds the previous upper bound, down if it falls below the previous lower bound, otherwise retain direction. In an up direction, raise the lower bound to at least its previous value; in a down direction, lower the upper bound to at most its previous value. Calculations use chart bars or, in the higher-timeframe version, the selected higher timeframe’s still-forming bar; signals are sampled on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe interval.
Long: StepMA direction changes from down to up.
Short: StepMA direction changes from up to down.
