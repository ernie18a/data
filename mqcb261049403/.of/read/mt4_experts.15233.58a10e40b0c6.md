Calculation: Slope Direction Line = apply a LWMA of length √n to (2 × LWMA of median price over n/2 bars − LWMA of median price over n bars); rising means its current value is above its previous value, falling means it is below. Uses the indicator timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Long: Slope Direction Line is falling AND the 400-period linear weighted moving average is above the highs of the last three completed chart bars AND the 14-period RSI is above 50 AND the 5-period RSI is below the 14-period RSI; no existing long position for this symbol and strategy.
Short: Slope Direction Line is rising AND the 400-period linear weighted moving average is below the lows of the last three completed chart bars AND the 14-period RSI is below 50 AND the 5-period RSI is above the 14-period RSI; no existing short position for this symbol and strategy.
Exit: long, Slope Direction Line is rising AND (the 400-period linear weighted moving average is below the lows of the last three completed chart bars OR the 14-period RSI is below 50 AND the 5-period RSI is above the 14-period RSI OR the Parabolic SAR is above the current bar’s high); OR price reaches the long stop loss or take profit.
Exit: short, Slope Direction Line is falling AND (the 400-period linear weighted moving average is above the highs of the last three completed chart bars OR the 14-period RSI is above 50 AND the 5-period RSI is below the 14-period RSI OR the Parabolic SAR is below the current bar’s low); OR price reaches the short stop loss or take profit.
