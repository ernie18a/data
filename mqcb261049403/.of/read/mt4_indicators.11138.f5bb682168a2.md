Calculation: Bullish CCI condition = CCI > 0 OR CCI > its previous value
Calculation: Bearish CCI condition = CCI < 0 OR CCI < its previous value
Calculation: EMA direction = EMA on a lower timeframe is rising or falling versus its previous bar
Long: Bullish CCI condition OR (Bearish CCI condition AND EMA direction is rising)
Short: Bearish CCI condition OR (Bullish CCI condition AND EMA direction is falling)
