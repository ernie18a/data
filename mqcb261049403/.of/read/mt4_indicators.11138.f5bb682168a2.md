Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed pivot-timeframe bar, let H, L, O and C be its high, low, open and close; P = (H + L + C) / 3; S1 = 2P - H; R1 = 2P - L; S2 = P - H + L; R2 = P + H - L; for each further level n through the outermost level, Sn = (n - 1)P - (n - 1)H + L and Rn = (n - 1)P + H - (n - 1)L. Strong support = S1 - a(S1 - S2). Strong resistance = R1 + b(R2 - R1) when H - O < O - L, otherwise R1 + a(R2 - R1), where b < a. Insert four intermediate levels S1 + f(R1 - S1) using increasing fractions f between zero and one. Order the levels from outermost support through strong support, S1, the intermediate levels, R1, strong resistance and the outermost resistance. The pivot timeframe is higher than or equal to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Prime buy threshold = the greatest pivot level at or below the chart close; below the outermost support, use the low of the still-forming reference-timeframe bar. At exactly the outermost resistance, retain the previously selected threshold. The reference timeframe may be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Prime sell threshold = the least pivot level at or above the chart close; above the outermost resistance, use the high of the still-forming reference-timeframe bar.
Long: (CCI of the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar > 0 OR CCI of that bar > CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar OR ((CCI of the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar < 0 OR CCI of that bar < CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar) AND EMA of close on the still-forming medium-timeframe bar > EMA of close on the preceding completed medium-timeframe bar)) AND chart close > prime buy threshold
Short: (CCI of the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar < 0 OR CCI of that bar < CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar OR ((CCI of the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar > 0 OR CCI of that bar > CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar) AND EMA of close on the still-forming medium-timeframe bar < EMA of close on the preceding completed medium-timeframe bar)) AND CCI of the still-forming pivot-timeframe bar <= 0 AND CCI of that bar <= CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar AND (CCI of that bar >= 0 AND CCI of that bar >= CCI of the preceding completed pivot-timeframe bar OR EMA of close on the still-forming medium-timeframe bar <= EMA of close on the preceding completed medium-timeframe bar) AND chart close < prime sell threshold
Long: (CCI of the still-forming trend-timeframe bar > 0 OR CCI of that bar < 0 OR CCI of that bar > CCI of the preceding completed trend-timeframe bar OR CCI of that bar < CCI of the preceding completed trend-timeframe bar) AND EMA of close on the still-forming fast-timeframe bar > EMA of close on the preceding completed fast-timeframe bar AND chart close > low of the still-forming reference-timeframe bar
Short: (CCI of the still-forming trend-timeframe bar > 0 OR CCI of that bar < 0 OR CCI of that bar > CCI of the preceding completed trend-timeframe bar OR CCI of that bar < CCI of the preceding completed trend-timeframe bar) AND EMA of close on the still-forming fast-timeframe bar < EMA of close on the preceding completed fast-timeframe bar AND chart close < high of the still-forming reference-timeframe bar
Calculation: Timeframe relationships = the trend, medium and fast timeframes may each be lower than, equal to, or higher than the chart timeframe; the fast timeframe is shorter than the medium timeframe, and the trend timeframe is longer than the medium timeframe. Both CCI calculations use the same lookback and typical price; both EMA calculations use the same lookback.
