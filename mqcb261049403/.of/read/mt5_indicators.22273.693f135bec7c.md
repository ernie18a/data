Calculation: Skyscraper state = On each calculation-timeframe bar, calculate the mean of high minus low over a lookback including the current bar. Maintain its running maximum and minimum from the first calculated bar. Set step to the sensitivity factor times their sum divided by two, truncated down to a whole number of price increments. Initialize the previous lower and upper levels to the close of the first calculated bar and the previous trend to zero. Set the candidate upper and lower levels to low + 2 × step and high − 2 × step, respectively, or to close + 2 × step and close − 2 × step when using close-based levels. Start with the previous trend; set it to positive if close > previous upper level, then set it to negative if close < previous lower level. If trend is positive, set lower level to max(candidate lower, previous lower) and upper level to candidate upper; otherwise set upper level to min(candidate upper, previous upper) and lower level to candidate lower. The active level is the lower level when trend is positive and the upper level otherwise. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe. For the higher-timeframe version, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar, sampled on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and held until the next higher-timeframe bar.
