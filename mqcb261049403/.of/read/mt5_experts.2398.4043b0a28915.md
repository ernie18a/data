Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period simple moving average of DeMarker on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Signal line = longer-period simple moving average of the fast line on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Fast line > signal line AND fast line on the preceding higher-timeframe bar <= signal line on that bar.
Short: Fast line < signal line AND fast line on the preceding higher-timeframe bar >= signal line on that bar.
Exit: long, fast line < signal line OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, fast line > signal line OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
