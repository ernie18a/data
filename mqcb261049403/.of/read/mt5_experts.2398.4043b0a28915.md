Calculation: Fast line = DeMarker smoothed with a selected averaging method and shorter lookback, on the configured indicator timeframe.
Calculation: Slow line = Fast line smoothed with the same averaging method and a longer lookback, on the configured indicator timeframe.
Long: Fast line crosses above slow line between successive completed indicator-timeframe bars.
Short: Fast line crosses below slow line between successive completed indicator-timeframe bars.
Exit: Short, fast line > slow line on a completed indicator-timeframe bar.
Exit: Long, fast line < slow line on a completed indicator-timeframe bar.
