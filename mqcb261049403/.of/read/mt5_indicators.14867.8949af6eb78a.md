Calculation: Weighted price = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Calculation: Scaled OsMA = (MACD of weighted price using a shorter and a longer EMA − EMA of that MACD using a signal period) × integer quotient of the longer EMA period divided by the shorter EMA period.
Long: Scaled OsMA on the chart timeframe >= its previous bar's value.
Short: Scaled OsMA on the chart timeframe <= its previous bar's value.
Long: Scaled OsMA on a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming, >= its previous higher-timeframe bar's value.
Short: Scaled OsMA on a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming, <= its previous higher-timeframe bar's value.
