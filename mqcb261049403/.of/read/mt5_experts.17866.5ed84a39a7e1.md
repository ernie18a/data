Calculation: Daily high = high of the still-forming bar on a higher timeframe than the chart
Calculation: Daily low = low of the still-forming bar on a higher timeframe than the chart
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume of the still-forming bar on a higher timeframe than the chart divided by volume of its last completed bar
Long: current price > daily high AND volume ratio > threshold AND daily high − daily low > minimum channel width
Short: current price < daily low AND volume ratio > threshold AND daily high − daily low > minimum channel width
Exit: long, Parabolic SAR on the chart timeframe's still-forming bar > close of the last completed chart bar OR price <= long stop-loss OR price >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, Parabolic SAR on the chart timeframe's still-forming bar < close of the last completed chart bar OR price >= short stop-loss OR price <= entry price − take-profit distance
