Calculation: Laguerre trend color = On the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe, calculate Laguerre RSI from close: L0=(1−gamma)×close+gamma×previous L0; L1=−gamma×L0+previous L0+gamma×previous L1; likewise L2 and L3 using the preceding stage and its prior values. Let CU be the sum of positive differences between adjacent L0,L1,L2,L3 values, and CD the sum of their negative differences in absolute value; RSI=100×CU/(CU+CD). Color is green when RSI>high level and red when RSI<low level. For RSI between middle and high levels (middle<RSI≤high), color is red if previous RSI>high, green if previous RSI≤middle, otherwise retain previous color. For RSI between low and middle levels (low≤RSI≤middle), color is red if previous RSI>middle, green if previous RSI<low, otherwise retain previous color.
Long: Laguerre trend color = green
Short: Laguerre trend color = red
