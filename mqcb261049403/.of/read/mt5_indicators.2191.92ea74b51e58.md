Calculation: Laguerre RSI = For each bar, set L0=(1−gamma)×close+gamma×previous L0, L1=−gamma×L0+previous L0+gamma×previous L1, L2=−gamma×L1+previous L1+gamma×previous L2, and L3=−gamma×L2+previous L2+gamma×previous L3. Initialize all four previous values to the first processed close. CU is the sum of positive differences L0−L1, L1−L2, and L2−L3; CD is the sum of their absolute negative differences. If CU+CD is nonzero, the value is 100×CU/(CU+CD); otherwise it is 100×the previous value, initially zero. Classify each value into ordered zones: below the low level, from the low level through the middle level, above the middle level through the high level, and above the high level. Begin directional classification after the indicator warmup; initialize its prior direction as neutral. Apply the calculation both to chart bars and to the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Laguerre RSI is above the high level OR its zone is above its previous zone and it is above the low level through the high level OR its zone equals its previous zone, is from the low level through the high level, and its previous direction is long
Short: Laguerre RSI is below the low level OR its zone is below its previous zone and it is from the low level through the high level OR its zone equals its previous zone, is from the low level through the high level, and its previous direction is short
