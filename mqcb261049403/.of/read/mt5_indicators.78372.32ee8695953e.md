Calculation: Box size = either a fixed positive price increment or ATR multiplied by a factor, rounded to the nearest logarithmic distance among the permitted point-and-figure box sizes. For ATR, use the last completed bar of the selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; alternatively use the last completed chart bar. Retain the previous ATR-based box size while the unrounded candidate differs from it by less than a configured fraction. Rebuild the point-and-figure history with the current box size.
Calculation: Point-and-figure columns = convert each price to a box level by rounding it to the nearest price increment and flooring its quotient by the box size. Start from the close level at the beginning of the configured history window, anchored to the first day boundary within the window when available. Use either close for both high and low, or the bar's high and low. The first move above the base creates a rising column and the first move below it creates a falling column, giving the upward move priority if both occur on the same bar. Extend a rising column if the high level exceeds its top; otherwise reverse to a falling column when the low level is at least the configured number of boxes below its top, starting the new column one box below that top. Extend a falling column if the low level is below its bottom; otherwise reverse to a rising column when the high level is at least the configured number of boxes above its bottom, starting the new column one box above that bottom. Use completed chart bars or the forming chart bar according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Qualified breakout direction = direction of the most recent earlier column whose first breakout passed all enabled structure, EMA, ADX, and session filters, including a breakout suppressed because its direction repeated the preceding qualified breakout.
Long: Current rising column first exceeds the top of the preceding rising column AND ((the preceding rising column's top equals the top of the rising column before it) OR (the preceding rising column was a triple-top breakout AND the intervening falling column's bottom is at or above the triple-top level) OR ((the higher-low filter is disabled OR the intervening falling column's bottom is above the preceding falling column's bottom) AND (the EMA filter is disabled OR breakout-bar close > EMA) AND (the ADX filter is disabled OR breakout-bar ADX >= threshold) AND (the session filter is disabled or inapplicable to the chart timeframe OR breakout-bar opening hour is in the configured session) AND (the direction-change filter is disabled OR qualified breakout direction is not long))).
Short: Current falling column first falls below the bottom of the preceding falling column AND ((the preceding falling column's bottom equals the bottom of the falling column before it) OR (the preceding falling column was a triple-bottom breakdown AND the intervening rising column's top is at or below the triple-bottom level) OR ((the lower-high filter is disabled OR the intervening rising column's top is below the preceding rising column's top) AND (the EMA filter is disabled OR breakout-bar close < EMA) AND (the ADX filter is disabled OR breakout-bar ADX >= threshold) AND (the session filter is disabled or inapplicable to the chart timeframe OR breakout-bar opening hour is in the configured session) AND (the direction-change filter is disabled OR qualified breakout direction is not short))).
