Calculation: Box size = fixed points, or ATR × multiplier from the last completed bar on the configured ATR timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), rounded to the nearest value on the 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 × powers-of-ten pip ladder.
Calculation: Point-and-figure columns = map prices to box levels; extend an X column to each higher level or an O column to each lower level, and reverse direction after price moves the configured reversal number of boxes. Use close, or high for X and low for O, according to the configured source. Build from chart-timeframe bars and evaluate signals on the last completed chart bar.
Long: Current X column top > top of the preceding X column (two columns back) AND preceding O column bottom > bottom of the prior O column (three columns back) AND (higher-low filter disabled OR preceding O bottom > prior O bottom) AND (EMA filter disabled OR breakout-bar close > chart-timeframe EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR breakout bar's server-time hour is within the configured session) AND (direction-change-only disabled OR the last accepted signal was not long).
Short: Current O column bottom < bottom of the preceding O column (two columns back) AND preceding X column top < top of the prior X column (three columns back) AND (lower-high filter disabled OR preceding X top < prior X top) AND (EMA filter disabled OR breakout-bar close < chart-timeframe EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR breakout bar's server-time hour is within the configured session) AND (direction-change-only disabled OR the last accepted signal was not short).
