Calculation: Pivot levels = classic pivot point and three support and resistance levels calculated from the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Level configuration = choose either the first support and resistance as entry levels, the second support and resistance as stop levels, and the pivot as the intermediate target; or the second support and resistance as entry levels, the third support and resistance as stop levels, and the first support and resistance as intermediate targets. Choose the corresponding first, second, or third opposing resistance and support as profit targets, subject to the first entry-level configuration allowing only the first or second profit target.
Long: No position is open AND the current time is outside the optional closing hour AND the open two chart bars ago > the current support entry level AND (the low two chart bars ago < the support entry level OR the close two chart bars ago <= the support entry level) AND the last completed chart bar close >= the support entry level AND NOT (the open two chart bars ago < the resistance entry level AND (the high two chart bars ago > the resistance entry level OR the close two chart bars ago >= the resistance entry level) AND the last completed chart bar close <= the resistance entry level).
Short: No position is open AND the current time is outside the optional closing hour AND the open two chart bars ago < the current resistance entry level AND (the high two chart bars ago > the resistance entry level OR the close two chart bars ago >= the resistance entry level) AND the last completed chart bar close <= the resistance entry level AND NOT (the open two chart bars ago > the support entry level AND (the low two chart bars ago < the support entry level OR the close two chart bars ago <= the support entry level) AND the last completed chart bar close >= the support entry level).
Exit: Long, low <= the support stop level fixed at entry OR high >= the resistance profit target fixed at entry OR the optional closing hour is enabled AND the current time is within that hour.
Exit: Short, high >= the resistance stop level fixed at entry OR low <= the support profit target fixed at entry OR the optional closing hour is enabled AND the current time is within that hour.
