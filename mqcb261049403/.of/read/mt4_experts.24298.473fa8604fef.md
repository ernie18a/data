Calculation: Outside-bar count = the number of bars in a lookback ending at the current bar for which low > EMA of median price or high < EMA of median price; median price = (high + low) / 2. At the current bar's open, use its open as its high and low.
Long: At the current bar's open, outside-bar count = lookback length AND the oldest bar in that lookback has low > its EMA of median price AND (low of the last completed bar < short-term EMA of close on the last completed bar OR low of the second-last completed bar < that EMA OR low of the third-last completed bar < that EMA) AND short-term EMA of close > medium-term EMA of close > long-term EMA of close on the last completed bar AND low of the second-last completed bar < high of the last completed bar AND absolute difference between current open and last completed close > price-distance threshold.
Short: At the current bar's open, outside-bar count = lookback length AND the oldest bar in that lookback has high < its EMA of median price AND (low of the last completed bar > short-term EMA of close on the last completed bar OR low of the second-last completed bar > that EMA OR low of the third-last completed bar > that EMA) AND short-term EMA of close < medium-term EMA of close < long-term EMA of close on the last completed bar AND low of the last completed bar < high of the second-last completed bar AND absolute difference between current open and last completed close > price-distance threshold.
