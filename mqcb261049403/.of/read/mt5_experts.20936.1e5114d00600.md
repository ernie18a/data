Calculation: Up fan = on the chart timeframe, the five EMAs with successively longer lookbacks are strictly ordered from highest to lowest; down fan is the reverse. Use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fan = the same five-EMA ordering on each higher timeframe used; use each timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Fan width = highest minus lowest of the five EMAs, measured in points.
Calculation: Stochastic cross = on the chart timeframe, the main line crosses above the signal line after being below the lower threshold for a long cross, or crosses below after being above the upper threshold for a short cross; use the two last completed bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe stochastic cross = on a higher timeframe, the main line crosses the signal line, the completed signal bar closes in the crossing direction, and either the crossing bar or preceding bar is beyond the corresponding extreme threshold; use completed bars.
Calculation: MA breakout = a completed chart-timeframe bar opens below all five EMAs and closes above all five for an upward breakout, or opens above all five and closes below all five for a downward breakout.
Calculation: Fast-MA breakout = a completed higher-timeframe bar opens below the three shortest-lookback EMAs and closes above all three, or opens above all three and closes below all three.
Calculation: Narrow-range breakout = scan backward from the bar preceding the breakout bar; find the first consecutive range whose high-low span is less than its bar count times a configurable points-per-bar coefficient, after the minimum bar count, and whose span does not exceed the configured maximum. The breakout bar opens inside and closes outside the range; also allow a two-bar breakout when both bars close in the breakout direction and the first opens inside the range.
Calculation: Bullish stochastic divergence = a stochastic trough is lower than an earlier trough, while the corresponding price low is higher; bearish stochastic divergence is a stochastic peak higher than an earlier peak while the corresponding price high is lower. The pivots are separated by at least the configured minimum bars.
Calculation: Hammer = a completed chart-timeframe candle whose range exceeds the configured minimum and whose lower shadow exceeds the configured percentage of its range while its upper shadow is below the configured maximum percentage (bullish), or vice versa (bearish).
Calculation: Narrow-source range breakout = the completed chart-timeframe bar opens inside and closes above the highest high, or below the lowest low, of the configured preceding-bar range.
Long: (chart-timeframe fan width < configured maximum AND up fan on chart timeframe AND up fan on each higher timeframe AND chart-timeframe stochastic cross is long AND completed chart-timeframe close - open > configured minimum body) OR (chart-timeframe MA breakout is upward AND up fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe AND completed chart-timeframe open = low) OR (narrow-range breakout is upward AND up fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (higher-timeframe fast-MA breakout is upward AND chart-timeframe MA breakout is upward AND up fan on each higher timeframe AND higher-timeframe fan width < configured maximum) OR (bullish stochastic divergence AND up fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (the nearest recent hammer is bullish, is the lowest low over the configured hammer lookback, the latest completed chart-timeframe candle is bullish, higher-timeframe fan width < configured maximum, and up fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (higher-timeframe stochastic cross is long AND up fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (narrow-source range breakout is upward AND bullish fan convergence occurred within the configured bar count AND up fan on each higher timeframe).
Short: (chart-timeframe fan width < configured maximum AND down fan on chart timeframe AND down fan on each higher timeframe AND chart-timeframe stochastic cross is short AND completed chart-timeframe open - close > configured minimum body) OR (chart-timeframe MA breakout is downward AND down fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe AND completed chart-timeframe open = high) OR (narrow-range breakout is downward AND down fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (higher-timeframe fast-MA breakout is downward AND chart-timeframe MA breakout is downward AND down fan on each higher timeframe AND higher-timeframe fan width < configured maximum) OR (bearish stochastic divergence AND down fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (the nearest recent hammer is bearish, is the highest high over the configured hammer lookback, the latest completed chart-timeframe candle is bearish, higher-timeframe fan width < configured maximum, and down fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (higher-timeframe stochastic cross is short AND down fan on chart timeframe and each higher timeframe) OR (narrow-source range breakout is downward AND bearish fan convergence occurred within the configured bar count AND down fan on each higher timeframe).
Exit: long, any short entry condition is true OR bar low <= entry price - configured initial stop distance OR bar high >= entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: short, any long entry condition is true OR bar high >= entry price + configured initial stop distance OR bar low <= entry price - configured take-profit distance
