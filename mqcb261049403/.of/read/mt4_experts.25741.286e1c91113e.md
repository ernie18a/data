Calculation: Moving-average envelope = SMA of close multiplied by one minus or plus a deviation parameter, for the lower and upper bands respectively.
Long: Absolute difference between current bar open and previous bar close exceeds a distance threshold AND previous bar low < current short-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar close > that moving average AND current short-period linear weighted moving average of typical price > current long-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar low < previous bar lower moving-average envelope.
Short: Absolute difference between current bar open and previous bar close exceeds a distance threshold AND previous bar high > current short-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar close < that moving average AND current short-period linear weighted moving average of typical price < current long-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND previous bar high > previous bar upper moving-average envelope.
Exit: Long, price <= entry price minus a stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price plus a take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, price >= entry price plus a stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price minus a take-profit distance.
