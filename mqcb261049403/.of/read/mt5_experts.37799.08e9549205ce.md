Calculation: Signal bars = chart-timeframe bars or bars aggregated to a configurable higher or lower timeframe; use either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar and its immediately preceding bar.
Calculation: Fast and slow CCI = commodity channel indices of the same moving average of a selected price, using respectively shorter and longer CCI lookbacks.
Calculation: Trading time = time control is disabled OR the current time of day is in the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval, including across midnight.
Long: Trading time AND long trading is allowed AND reverse is disabled AND ((previous Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0) OR (current Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND previous fast CCI < 0 AND previous fast CCI < previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI > current slow CCI)).
Long: Trading time AND long trading is allowed AND reverse is enabled AND ((previous Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0) OR (current Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND previous fast CCI > 0 AND previous fast CCI > previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI < current slow CCI)).
Short: Trading time AND short trading is allowed AND reverse is disabled AND ((previous Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0) OR (current Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND previous fast CCI > 0 AND previous fast CCI > previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI < current slow CCI)).
Short: Trading time AND short trading is allowed AND reverse is enabled AND ((previous Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0) OR (current Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND previous fast CCI < 0 AND previous fast CCI < previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI > current slow CCI)).
Exit: long, Trading time AND ((reverse is disabled AND previous Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0) OR (reverse is enabled AND previous Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0)).
Exit: short, Trading time AND ((reverse is disabled AND previous Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0) OR (reverse is enabled AND previous Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0)).
Exit: long, Trading time AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND previous fast CCI > 0 AND previous fast CCI > previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI < current slow CCI AND reverse is disabled AND (short trading is disallowed OR close opposite positions is enabled).
Exit: short, Trading time AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND previous fast CCI < 0 AND previous fast CCI < previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI > current slow CCI AND reverse is disabled AND (long trading is disallowed OR close opposite positions is enabled).
Exit: long, Trading time AND current Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND previous fast CCI < 0 AND previous fast CCI < previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI > current slow CCI AND reverse is enabled AND (short trading is disallowed OR close opposite positions is enabled).
Exit: short, Trading time AND current Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND previous fast CCI > 0 AND previous fast CCI > previous slow CCI AND current fast CCI < current slow CCI AND reverse is enabled AND (long trading is disallowed OR close opposite positions is enabled).
