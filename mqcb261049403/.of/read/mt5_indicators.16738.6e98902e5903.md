Calculation: Zero-lag TEMA = 2 × TEMA(price, period) − TEMA(TEMA(price, period), period). TEMA(x, period) = 3 × EMA(x, period) − 3 × EMA(EMA(x, period), period) + EMA(EMA(EMA(x, period), period), period). Seed each EMA with its first input.
Calculation: The two zero-lag TEMAs use independently selected periods and price inputs; neither period is required to be longer. Each price input may be open, high, low, close, median (high + low)/2, typical (high + low + close)/3, weighted (high + low + 2 × close)/4, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price, or Heikin-Ashi average (HA open + HA high + HA low + HA close)/4. HA close = (open + high + low + close)/4; HA open = (previous HA open + previous HA close)/2, initialized to first open + first close; HA high = max(high, HA open, HA close); HA low = min(low, HA open, HA close). Calculate both TEMAs on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Both periods > 1 AND (first zero-lag TEMA < second zero-lag TEMA OR first zero-lag TEMA = second zero-lag TEMA AND previous trend is long).
Short: Both periods > 1 AND (first zero-lag TEMA > second zero-lag TEMA OR first zero-lag TEMA = second zero-lag TEMA AND previous trend is short).
