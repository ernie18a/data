Calculation: Laguerre RSI = recursively filter close with four Laguerre stages using one smoothing parameter; sum the positive differences between adjacent stages as CU and the absolute negative differences as CD, then 100 × CU/(CU+CD), retaining the prior RSI when CU+CD=0.
Calculation: Laguerre color = bullish when RSI is above the upper level, or when it is between the middle and upper levels and the prior class was lower-middle/low; bearish when RSI is below the lower level, or when it is between the lower and middle levels and the prior class was high/upper-middle. In either middle band, retain the prior color when the prior class was in the same band. The upper-middle band is above middle through upper; the lower-middle band is lower through middle. At the upper level use upper-middle; at the lower level use lower-middle.
Long: Laguerre color on the chart timeframe is bullish OR Laguerre color on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar is bullish
Short: Laguerre color on the chart timeframe is bearish OR Laguerre color on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar is bearish
