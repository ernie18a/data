Calculation: Laguerre oscillator = Initialize four stages to the selected OHLC-derived price on the first calculated bar. On each bar, using the previous stages p0 through p3 and smoothing parameter g, calculate s0=(1-g)*price+g*p0, s1=-g*s0+p0+g*p1, s2=-g*s1+p1+g*p2, and s3=-g*s2+p2+g*p3. Let gains be the sum of max(s0-s1,0), max(s1-s2,0), and max(s2-s3,0); let losses be the sum of max(s1-s0,0), max(s2-s1,0), and max(s3-s2,0). If gains+losses is nonzero, set the oscillator to 100*gains/(gains+losses); otherwise set it to 100 times its previous value, initially zero.
Calculation: Laguerre direction = Starting after the indicator's warm-up, classify the oscillator as above the upper threshold, between the upper and middle thresholds, between the middle and lower thresholds, or below the lower threshold; equality with the upper threshold belongs to the upper middle band, and equality with the middle or lower threshold belongs to the lower middle band. Set direction to long above the upper threshold, short below the lower threshold, long when entering either middle band from a lower band, and short when entering either middle band from a higher band. Within the same middle band, retain the previous direction; direction is initially unset. Calculate this direction on the chart timeframe and separately on a higher timeframe, using either the still-forming higher-timeframe bar or the last completed higher-timeframe bar according to the selected signal bar.
Long: Laguerre direction on the chart timeframe is long OR Laguerre direction on the selected higher-timeframe bar is long.
Short: Laguerre direction on the chart timeframe is short OR Laguerre direction on the selected higher-timeframe bar is short.
