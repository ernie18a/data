Calculation: CCI = Commodity Channel Index calculated from median price on the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish tick volume = sum of tick volume for bullish lower-timeframe bars whose close exceeds their open by more than the volatility threshold.
Calculation: Bearish tick volume = sum of tick volume for bearish lower-timeframe bars whose open exceeds their close by more than the volatility threshold.
Long: CCI > 0 AND CCI < its limit AND current forming bar low is above the last completed bar low on each of three higher timeframes AND current price > previous completed chart bar high AND bullish tick volume in the current forming lower-timeframe bar plus the preceding shorter-lookback bars > bullish tick volume over the preceding longer-lookback bars AND that longer-lookback bullish tick volume > 0 AND chart bar high from three bars ago > chart bar high from two bars ago > previous completed chart bar high
Short: NOT (CCI > 0 AND CCI < its limit) AND current forming bar high is below the last completed bar high on each of three higher timeframes AND current price < previous completed chart bar low AND bearish tick volume in the current forming lower-timeframe bar plus the preceding shorter-lookback bars > bearish tick volume over the preceding longer-lookback bars AND that longer-lookback bearish tick volume > 0 AND chart bar low from three bars ago < chart bar low from two bars ago < previous completed chart bar low AND CCI < 0 AND CCI > -its limit
Exit: Long, current price > take-profit price reduced by one pip for each completed reduction interval OR holding time exceeds its limit OR it is after the configured Friday close time
Exit: Short, current price < take-profit price increased by one pip for each completed reduction interval OR holding time exceeds its limit OR it is after the configured Friday close time
