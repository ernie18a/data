Calculation: Stochastic oscillator = 200 × (three successive moving averages of selected price minus the lowest low over a lookback) / (the same three successive moving averages of highest high minus lowest low over that lookback) − 100; use zero when the smoothed range is zero. The moving averages use the same selected method and independently selected periods in the same order for both series.
Calculation: Signal line = moving average of the stochastic oscillator using the same method and period as the third smoothing stage.
Long: Chart-timeframe stochastic oscillator > its previous bar AND stochastic oscillator != 0
Short: Chart-timeframe stochastic oscillator < its previous bar AND stochastic oscillator != 0
Long: Chart-timeframe stochastic oscillator > signal line
Short: Chart-timeframe stochastic oscillator < signal line
Long: Stochastic oscillator of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar >= stochastic oscillator of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar
Short: Stochastic oscillator of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < stochastic oscillator of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar
Long: Stochastic oscillator of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar >= its signal line
Short: Stochastic oscillator of the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < its signal line
