Calculation: Rising sequence = every bar in a configurable consecutive sequence ending at the evaluated bar has a high and a low strictly greater than the preceding bar's high and low, respectively.
Calculation: Falling sequence = every bar in a configurable consecutive sequence ending at the evaluated bar has a high and a low strictly less than the preceding bar's high and low, respectively; use the same sequence length as the rising sequence.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, evaluate its still-forming bar for a rising or falling sequence; retain the direction of the latest such sequence until an opposite sequence occurs. Before the first sequence, the trend is undefined.
Long: The chart bar has a rising sequence.
Short: The chart bar has a falling sequence.
Long: A higher-timeframe trend is rising.
Short: A higher-timeframe trend is falling.
