Calculation: Close-based stochastic main line = 100 times the sum, over the smoothing period, of close minus the lowest close in the stochastic lookback, divided by the sum, over the same smoothing period, of the highest close minus the lowest close in that lookback; its signal line is a simple moving average of the main line.
Calculation: MACD main line = shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close; its signal line is a simple moving average of the main line with a period shorter than the shorter EMA period.
Long: Shorter-period linear weighted moving average of close > longer-period simple moving average of close AND RSI of close >= upper threshold AND close-based stochastic main line > its signal line AND MACD main line > its signal line.
Short: Shorter-period linear weighted moving average of close < longer-period simple moving average of close AND RSI of close <= lower threshold AND close-based stochastic main line < its signal line AND MACD main line < its signal line.
Calculation: Each decision is evaluated either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe. For the higher-timeframe version, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and propagate its current decision to every chart bar in that higher-timeframe interval; historical chart bars are overwritten with the final decision of their containing higher-timeframe bar.
