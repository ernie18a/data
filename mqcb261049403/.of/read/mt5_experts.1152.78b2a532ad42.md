Calculation: Bollinger Bands
Long: applied price on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), latest completed bar > upper Bollinger Band AND applied price on the same timeframe, preceding completed bar <= upper Bollinger Band
Short: applied price on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), latest completed bar < lower Bollinger Band AND applied price on the same timeframe, preceding completed bar >= lower Bollinger Band
Exit: long, applied price on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), latest completed bar <= upper Bollinger Band
Exit: short, applied price on the configured indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), latest completed bar >= lower Bollinger Band
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
