Calculation: Weighted oscillator = smooth (RSI weight × RSI + MFI weight × MFI + Williams %R weight × (Williams %R + 100) + DeMarker weight × 100 × DeMarker) divided by the sum of the weights. Calculate on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe. Use the selected signal bar, either the last completed bar or the bar still forming, and its two predecessors. The selected smoothing method includes JJMA, whose implementation is not provided in READ.
Long: direct mode AND oscillator on the signal bar > oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator two bars before the signal bar > oscillator on the preceding bar OR reverse mode AND oscillator on the signal bar < oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator two bars before the signal bar < oscillator on the preceding bar
Short: direct mode AND oscillator on the signal bar < oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator two bars before the signal bar < oscillator on the preceding bar OR reverse mode AND oscillator on the signal bar > oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator two bars before the signal bar > oscillator on the preceding bar
Exit: long, short-entry condition OR low <= entry price minus enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus enabled take-profit distance
Exit: short, long-entry condition OR high >= entry price plus enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus enabled take-profit distance
