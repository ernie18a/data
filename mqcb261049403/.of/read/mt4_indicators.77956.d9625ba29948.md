Calculation: Session range = for each enabled session, the maximum high and minimum low of chart bars whose opening times fall within that session's configured GMT start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval; convert bar times to GMT using the configured offset and associate overnight bars with the session's start date.
Calculation: Active session level = the high or low of the most recently completed instance of an enabled session, provided its start is not earlier than the earliest chart bar in the configured rolling history window and the next instance of that session has not started. Track the high and low independently. If first-sweep-only is enabled, deactivate a level after its first sweep. If breakout cancellation is enabled, deactivate the high after a bar has high > level AND close >= level, and deactivate the low after a bar has low < level AND close <= level.
Long: The closed bar is outside an enabled session AND its low < that session's active low AND its close > that session's active low.
Short: The closed bar is outside an enabled session AND its high > that session's active high AND its close < that session's active high.
