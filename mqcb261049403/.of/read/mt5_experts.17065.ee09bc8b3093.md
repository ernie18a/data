Calculation: SilverTrend lower and upper thresholds = over the SSP-bar lookback, let the range floor be the minimum of the current close and lows, and the range ceiling be the maximum of the highs; lower threshold = floor + (ceiling − floor) × (33 − risk parameter)/100; upper threshold = ceiling − (ceiling − floor) × (33 − risk parameter)/100. The trend state switches to bearish when close < lower threshold, switches to bullish when close > upper threshold, and otherwise retains its prior state.
Long: SilverTrend state changes to bullish on the configured signal bar of the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar for a nonzero signal-bar offset and the still-forming bar for a zero offset))
Short: SilverTrend state changes to bearish on the configured signal bar of the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar for a nonzero signal-bar offset and the still-forming bar for a zero offset))
Exit: Long, SilverTrend state changes to bearish OR price ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR price ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: Short, SilverTrend state changes to bullish OR price ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR price ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
