Calculation: MACD signal = simple moving average of the MACD main line.
Long: At the chart bar open, the shorter-period higher-timeframe linear weighted moving average of typical price on the forming bar > the longer-period one AND the low two chart bars ago < the high of the last completed chart bar AND four chart-timeframe exponential moving averages of typical price on the last completed bar are ordered from highest to lowest by increasing period AND absolute difference between the current open and the close two chart bars ago > a price-distance threshold AND ((higher-timeframe MACD main on the last completed bar > 0 AND > its signal) OR (higher-timeframe MACD main on the last completed bar < 0 AND > its signal) OR higher-timeframe RSI on the forming bar > RSI on the last completed bar).
