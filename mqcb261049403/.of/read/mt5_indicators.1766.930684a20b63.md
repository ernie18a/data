Calculation: ATR stop state = On the chart timeframe, initialize the trend to neutral, the lower stop to -100000, and the upper stop to 100000. Starting after the ATR-period-plus-stop-lookback warm-up, calculate the candidate lower stop as the maximum of high minus ATR multiplied by an ATR multiplier over the stop lookback, and the candidate upper stop as the minimum of low plus ATR multiplied by the same multiplier over that lookback. Set the trend to bullish if close is greater than the previous upper stop; then set it to bearish if close is less than the previous lower stop; otherwise retain the previous trend. In a bullish trend, set the lower stop to the greater of its candidate and its previous value; otherwise use its candidate. In a bearish trend, set the upper stop to the lesser of its candidate and its previous value; otherwise use its candidate.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ATR stop state = Apply the same ATR stop state calculation to a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming. Sample its state at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carry that state forward until the next higher-timeframe bar. Historical samples use the final values of their higher-timeframe bars.
Long: ATR stop state is bullish OR higher-timeframe ATR stop state is bullish.
Short: ATR stop state is bearish OR higher-timeframe ATR stop state is bearish.
