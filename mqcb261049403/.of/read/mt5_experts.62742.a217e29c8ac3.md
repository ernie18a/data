Calculation: Evaluation = at the first tick of each chart bar; index 0 is the newly forming bar and index 1 is the last completed bar.
Calculation: Price peaks and troughs = scan a fixed lookback from the newest eligible bar to the oldest, excluding a fixed number of bars at each end. A peak has a high strictly greater than the highs of that many bars on each side and greater than a configurable multiple of the mean high over twice that many bars on each side, excluding the candidate and clipping to the lookback. A trough has a low strictly less than the lows of that many bars on each side and less than a configurable multiple of the corresponding mean low. Use the two most recent peaks or troughs and the oscillator values at those price bars.
Calculation: Divergence strength = min(1, abs(recent price - older price) / max(recent price, older price) + abs(recent oscillator - older oscillator) / max(abs(recent oscillator), abs(older oscillator))).
Calculation: Bullish divergence count = number of RSI, MACD main line, and stochastic main line for which the two most recent price troughs have a lower recent low, a higher recent oscillator value, and divergence strength at least the threshold.
Calculation: Bearish divergence count = number of RSI, MACD main line, and stochastic main line for which bullish divergence is absent and the two most recent price peaks have a higher recent high, a lower recent oscillator value, and divergence strength at least the threshold.
Long: Bullish divergence count >= confirmation threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed close > last completed close EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR last completed volume > a configurable multiple of mean volume over a lookback ending at the last completed bar).
Short: Bullish divergence count < confirmation threshold AND bearish divergence count >= confirmation threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR last completed close < last completed close EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR last completed volume > a configurable multiple of mean volume over a lookback ending at the last completed bar).
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
