Calculation: Signal bars = bars of a configurable timeframe, which may be lower than, higher than, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the last completed signal bar and an earlier completed signal bar at a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus stop-loss distance if the stop loss is enabled; otherwise unset. At each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled, chart open minus entry price is greater than trailing distance plus trailing step, and the stop is unset or less than chart open minus trailing distance minus trailing step, set the stop to chart open minus trailing distance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus stop-loss distance if the stop loss is enabled; otherwise unset. At each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled, entry price minus chart open is greater than trailing distance plus trailing step, and the stop is unset or greater than chart open plus trailing distance plus trailing step, set the stop to chart open plus trailing distance.
Long: (Reverse is disabled AND last completed signal bar open > close AND earlier signal bar high minus last completed signal bar low > step distance) OR (reverse is enabled AND last completed signal bar close > open AND last completed signal bar high minus earlier signal bar low > step distance).
Short: (Reverse is disabled AND last completed signal bar close > open AND last completed signal bar high minus earlier signal bar low > step distance) OR (reverse is enabled AND last completed signal bar open > close AND earlier signal bar high minus last completed signal bar low > step distance).
Exit: long, chart low <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND chart high >= entry price plus take-profit distance OR close-opposite is enabled AND short condition is true.
Exit: short, chart high >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND chart low <= entry price minus take-profit distance OR close-opposite is enabled AND long condition is true.
