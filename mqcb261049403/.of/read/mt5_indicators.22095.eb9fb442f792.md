Calculation: Smoothed volume = selected bar volume smoothed with the same smoothing method and parameters as the CCI candle values.
Calculation: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI open/close = smooth(CCI of the corresponding bar open/close price × selected bar volume); replace the smoothed open with the prior bar’s smoothed close when the bar’s absolute open-close price difference is at most the gap threshold.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = high and low CCI levels × smoothed volume, respectively.
Long: Smoothed volume-weighted CCI close > upper band AND smoothed volume-weighted CCI close >= smoothed volume-weighted CCI open OR smoothed volume-weighted CCI close <= upper band AND smoothed volume-weighted CCI close >= lower band AND smoothed volume-weighted CCI close >= smoothed volume-weighted CCI open OR smoothed volume-weighted CCI close < lower band AND smoothed volume-weighted CCI close > smoothed volume-weighted CCI open
