Calculation: Moving average = selected moving average type and price over its configured lookback; fast lookback < mid lookback < slow lookback. Use the current forming bar and preceding bar on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: MACD = selected classic or zero-lag MACD main and signal lines, using configured fast and slow lookbacks and signal lookback. Use the current forming bar and preceding bar on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its current forming bar and preceding bar unless a condition specifies a lookback scan.
Calculation: Stochastic = main and signal lines on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current forming bar.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR = SAR on the configured indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current forming bar.
Calculation: ADX = ADX main, +DI, and -DI on the configured trend timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current forming bar.
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = upper and lower bands at the configured deviation levels on the configured band timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar.
Calculation: Damiani noise = Damiani volatility meter buffer 0 > buffer 2 on the configured noise timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current forming bar.
Long: Fast moving average crosses above mid moving average OR mid crosses above slow OR fast crosses above slow; OR MACD main and signal both rise and main > signal OR main crosses above signal while both current and preceding main values are below zero OR signal line crosses above zero; OR RSI < oversold threshold OR RSI rises while chart open rises OR RSI rises within configured buy-zone bounds; OR stochastic main > signal (and, when enabled, main > upper threshold); OR Parabolic SAR < preceding chart close; OR preceding chart low <= third lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < 30 within the configured number of preceding RSI-timeframe bars AND preceding chart close > second lower Bollinger Band.
Short: Fast moving average crosses below mid moving average OR mid crosses below slow OR fast crosses below slow; OR MACD main and signal both fall and main < signal OR main crosses below signal while both current and preceding main values are above zero OR signal line crosses below zero; OR RSI > overbought threshold OR RSI falls while chart open falls OR RSI falls within configured sell-zone bounds; OR stochastic main < signal (and, when enabled, main < lower threshold); OR Parabolic SAR >= preceding chart close; OR preceding chart high >= third upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > 70 within the configured number of preceding chart-timeframe bars AND preceding chart close < second upper Bollinger Band.
Long: The selected entry indicators (RSI, stochastic, SAR, moving average, MACD) all signal long AND the configured entry combination permits it: trend up with long signal; range-up with long signal; range-down with short signal only when the combination uses that range direction; Bollinger long signal when the combination permits bands; or long signal alone when trend and bands are disabled. AND (noise filtering is disabled OR Damiani buffer 0 <= buffer 2).
Short: The selected entry indicators (RSI, stochastic, SAR, moving average, MACD) all signal short AND the configured entry combination permits it: trend down with short signal; range-down with short signal; range-up with long signal only when the combination uses that range direction; Bollinger short signal when the combination permits bands; or short signal alone when trend and bands are disabled. AND (noise filtering is disabled OR Damiani buffer 0 <= buffer 2).
Long: ADX main < +DI AND ADX main < -DI (range-down); OR ADX main < configured main level (range-up); OR ADX main >= configured main level AND +DI > -DI (uptrend).
Short: ADX main >= configured main level AND -DI > +DI (downtrend).
Exit: Long, all selected closing indicators (RSI, stochastic, SAR, moving average, MACD) unanimously signal short.
Exit: Short, all selected closing indicators (RSI, stochastic, SAR, moving average, MACD) unanimously signal long.
Exit: Long, chart price <= entry price minus configured static stop distance OR chart price >= entry price plus configured static take-profit distance OR price has advanced beyond the configured trailing distance from entry and then retraced to the trailing stop.
Exit: Short, chart price >= entry price plus configured static stop distance OR chart price <= entry price minus configured static take-profit distance OR price has declined beyond the configured trailing distance from entry and then retraced to the trailing stop.
