Calculation: Support = lowest low over the lookback bars on the configured extrema timeframe, starting with its last completed bar; round to configured price precision. The configured timeframe is lower than or equal to the chart timeframe by default.
Calculation: Resistance = highest high over the same lookback bars on the configured extrema timeframe, starting with its last completed bar; round to configured price precision. The configured timeframe is lower than or equal to the chart timeframe by default.
Long: close > Resistance
Short: close < Support
