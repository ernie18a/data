Calculation: Resistance = highest high over a configurable lookback starting at a configurable bar offset, rounded to a configurable price increment. The calculation timeframe is configurable as the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe, or a lower timeframe; an offset of zero uses the forming bar, and a positive offset uses a completed bar.
Calculation: Support = lowest low over the same timeframe, lookback, and bar offset as Resistance, rounded to the same price increment.
Long: High > Resistance
Short: Low < Support
