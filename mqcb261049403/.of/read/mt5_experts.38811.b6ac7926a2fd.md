Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close prices on the configured working timeframe.
Calculation: Moving average of RSI = configured moving average of RSI values; its lookback and smoothing method are configurable.
Long: On the selected signal bar (forming bar or last completed bar), RSI crosses above its moving average from below AND both RSI and its moving average are below 50.
Short: On the selected signal bar (forming bar or last completed bar), RSI crosses below its moving average from above AND both RSI and its moving average are above 50.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below the active stop-loss price OR high reaches or exceeds the take-profit price OR a short signal occurs while closing opposite positions is enabled.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds the active stop-loss price OR low reaches or falls below the take-profit price OR a long signal occurs while closing opposite positions is enabled.
