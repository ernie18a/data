Calculation: Signal RSI and its moving average = RSI of the selected price and a moving average of that RSI, calculated on the configured working timeframe; when it differs from the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower and uses either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal mode.
Long: ((Reverse mode is off AND previous signal RSI < previous RSI moving average AND signal RSI > RSI moving average AND signal RSI < RSI midpoint AND RSI moving average < RSI midpoint) OR (Reverse mode is on AND previous signal RSI > previous RSI moving average AND signal RSI < RSI moving average AND signal RSI > RSI midpoint AND RSI moving average > RSI midpoint)) AND (time control is off OR current time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive trading interval) AND (no existing position OR the distance from current price to every existing entry price >= position step)
Short: ((Reverse mode is off AND previous signal RSI > previous RSI moving average AND signal RSI < RSI moving average AND signal RSI > RSI midpoint AND RSI moving average > RSI midpoint) OR (Reverse mode is on AND previous signal RSI < previous RSI moving average AND signal RSI > RSI moving average AND signal RSI < RSI midpoint AND RSI moving average < RSI midpoint)) AND (time control is off OR current time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive trading interval) AND (no existing position OR the distance from current price to every existing entry price >= position step)
Exit: long, stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance
Exit: long, opposite-position closing is enabled AND the short signal conditions are met before the position-step check
Exit: short, opposite-position closing is enabled AND the long signal conditions are met before the position-step check
