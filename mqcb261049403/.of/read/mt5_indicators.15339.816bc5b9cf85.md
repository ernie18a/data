Calculation: Smoothed detrended price = a selectable moving average of the difference between a selectable applied OHLC price and an independently configured moving average of that price; the two averages may use different methods and lengths.
Long: Smoothed detrended price on the previous bar < its value two bars ago AND its value on the current bar > its value on the previous bar.
Short: Smoothed detrended price on the previous bar > its value two bars ago AND its value on the current bar < its value on the previous bar.
Long: The most recent smoothed detrended price turning-point decision is long.
Short: The most recent smoothed detrended price turning-point decision is short.
Calculation: The turning-point decisions can be evaluated on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; alternatively, use a selected completed higher-timeframe bar.
