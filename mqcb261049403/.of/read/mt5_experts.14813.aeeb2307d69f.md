Calculation: Bounce strength index = On the chart timeframe for the indicator markings, or on a selectable higher or lower timeframe for the trading system, calculate each bar j as follows. Let H and L be the highest high and lowest low over a range lookback ending at j, R=max(H−L, minimum price increment), and S=high[j]−low[j]. Set weight W to 1 without volume weighting, or volume[j] divided by the highest volume over the same range lookback, using 1 if that maximum is zero. Set P=(close[j]−low[j])×(H−low[j])/R×W if close[j]>=close[j−1]−0.2×S, otherwise P=0; use a ratio of 1 when low[j]=L. Set N=−(high[j]−close[j])×(high[j]−L)/R×W if close[j]<=close[j−1]+0.2×S, otherwise N=0; use a ratio of 1 when high[j]=H. For each bar, calculate 100×sum(P+N)/sum(R) over a slowing lookback ending at that bar, then take its simple moving average over an averaging lookback. For the trading system use the last completed signal-timeframe bar, or the still-forming bar when configured.
Long: Bounce strength index > previous bounce strength index
Short: Bounce strength index < previous bounce strength index
Exit: long, bounce strength index < previous bounce strength index OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, bounce strength index > previous bounce strength index OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
