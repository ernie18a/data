Calculation: Bounce Strength Index (BSI) = the simple average over AvgPeriod bars of each bar’s combined positive and negative bounce strength. For each of the Slowing most recent bars, find its highest high and lowest low over RangePeriod bars; let R be their difference, floored at the minimum price increment, and let S be the sum of R over the Slowing bars. For each bar, use weight 1, volume divided by maximum volume over its RangePeriod, or tick volume divided by maximum tick volume over its RangePeriod, according to the selected weighting mode. Add (close − low) × U × weight to positive strength if close ≥ previous chronological close − 0.2 × (high − low), where U is 1 if low equals the RangePeriod low, otherwise (RangePeriod high − low) / R. Add −(high − close) × D × weight to negative strength if close ≤ previous chronological close + 0.2 × (high − low), where D is 1 if high equals the RangePeriod high, otherwise (high − RangePeriod low) / R. The bar’s combined strength is 100 × (positive strength + negative strength) / S.
Long: BSI on the selected indicator timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed signal bar, is greater than BSI on the preceding completed bar AND BSI on that preceding bar is less than or equal to BSI on the bar before it.
Short: BSI on the selected indicator timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed signal bar, is less than BSI on the preceding completed bar AND BSI on that preceding bar is greater than or equal to BSI on the bar before it.
Exit: Long, BSI on the selected indicator timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed signal bar, is less than BSI on the preceding completed bar AND BSI on that preceding bar is greater than or equal to BSI on the bar before it
Exit: Short, BSI on the selected indicator timeframe (same as, higher than, or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed signal bar, is greater than BSI on the preceding completed bar AND BSI on that preceding bar is less than or equal to BSI on the bar before it
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − configured stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit distance
