Calculation: Step oscillator = Calculate ATR on a selectable higher or lower timeframe, using its last completed bar, or its still-forming bar when configured. Maintain the running minimum and maximum ATR since initialization. For three tracks, use step sizes equal to a common multiplier times, respectively, the running minimum ATR, the running maximum ATR, and the average of those extrema times a second multiplier. Initialize each track's upper and lower bands to the first calculated close; initialize the minimum track's trend down and the other tracks' trends up. For each bar and each track, set provisional upper and lower bands to close plus and minus twice its step size. Set trend up if close is above the previous upper band, down if close is below the previous lower band, otherwise retain the previous trend. In an uptrend, replace the lower band with the maximum of its provisional and previous values; in a downtrend, replace the upper band with the minimum of its provisional and previous values. The track line is the lower band plus the step size in an uptrend, or the upper band minus the step size in a downtrend. Let the baseline be the maximum-ATR track line minus its step size, and the range be twice that step size. Fast oscillator = 100 × (minimum-ATR track line − baseline) / range; slow oscillator = 100 × (middle track line − baseline) / range.
Long: Slow oscillator > 50 AND fast oscillator > slow oscillator AND previous fast oscillator <= previous slow oscillator.
Short: Slow oscillator < 50 AND fast oscillator < slow oscillator AND previous fast oscillator >= previous slow oscillator.
Exit: long, slow oscillator < 50 OR low <= entry price − enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, slow oscillator > 50 OR high >= entry price + enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − enabled take-profit distance.
