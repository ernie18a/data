Calculation: Regression channel = Fit a least-squares polynomial of configurable degree to chart-bar closes from the current bar through a configurable number of preceding bars, using bar offset from the current bar as the independent variable. The middle is the fitted value at the current bar. The upper and lower bands are the middle plus and minus a configurable multiple of the root mean squared residual over the fitted bars.
Long: Current bar low <= lower band AND current time is within the configured trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high minus low <= a range threshold AND position side is not long.
Short: Current bar high >= upper band AND current bar low > lower band AND current time is within the configured trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high minus low <= a range threshold AND position side is not short.
Exit: long, current close >= middle OR current time is within the configured trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high minus low > a range threshold OR an enabled stop loss is reached OR an enabled take profit is reached.
Exit: short, current close <= middle OR current time is within the configured trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high minus low > a range threshold OR an enabled stop loss is reached OR an enabled take profit is reached.
