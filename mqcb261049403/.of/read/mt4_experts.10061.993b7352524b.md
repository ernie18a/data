Calculation: EA SAR set = Parabolic SAR on the still-forming bars of one to four successively higher timeframes; the longer timeframes are included only in order. Each timeframe has its own acceleration step and shares a maximum acceleration.
Calculation: EA SAR spread = for two or three timeframes, truncate (maximum current SAR − minimum current SAR) / price point toward zero; for four timeframes, first find the maximum and minimum of the three shorter-timeframe SARs, then, if the longest-timeframe SAR is greater than the maximum, replace the maximum with it, and if it is greater than or equal to the minimum, replace the maximum with it; otherwise leave the minimum unchanged. Truncate (resulting maximum − resulting minimum) / price point toward zero.
Calculation: Indicator SAR set = Parabolic SAR on the chart timeframe and optionally one to three successively higher timeframes, included only in order, with the same acceleration parameters. Higher-timeframe SARs use the still-forming bar at each chart bar; the previous value is the value associated with the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially entry price minus the stop distance for a long position or entry price plus the stop distance for a short position. After each bar, for a long position replace it with the exit SAR if that SAR is at least the entry price and above the existing stop; for a short position replace it with the exit SAR if that SAR is at most the entry price and below the existing stop. Exit SAR uses a configurable chart or higher timeframe, with its current still-forming bar and its last completed bar.
Long: No open position AND every current EA SAR is below the low of its own still-forming timeframe bar AND at least one corresponding last completed SAR is above its own timeframe bar's high AND (only one timeframe is selected OR EA SAR spread is at most the spread threshold).
Short: No open position AND at least two EA SAR timeframes are selected AND every current EA SAR is above the high of its own still-forming timeframe bar AND at least one corresponding last completed SAR is below its own timeframe bar's low AND EA SAR spread is at most the spread threshold.
Short: No open position AND only one EA SAR timeframe is selected AND zero is greater than the still-forming bar high of the next higher timeframe AND the last completed EA SAR is below its own timeframe bar's low AND the current EA SAR is above its own still-forming timeframe bar's high.
Long: Every current indicator SAR is below the current chart bar low AND at least one previous indicator SAR is above the previous chart bar high.
Short: Every current indicator SAR is above the current chart bar high AND at least one previous indicator SAR is below the previous chart bar low.
Exit: long, last completed exit SAR is below the previous chart bar low AND current exit SAR is above the current chart bar high.
Exit: short, last completed exit SAR is above the previous chart bar high AND current exit SAR is below the current chart bar low.
Exit: long, current low is at or below the trailing stop OR current high is at or above entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: short, current high is at or above the trailing stop OR current low is at or below entry price minus the take-profit distance.
