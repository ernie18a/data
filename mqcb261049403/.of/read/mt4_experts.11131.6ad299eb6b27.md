Calculation: Weighted price = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Calculation: Selected entry condition = the condition belonging to the last enabled entry method in this sequence: (1) previous bar's close − open > minimum body size; (2) previous bar's high, low and close are each greater than the corresponding value two bars ago; (3) high, low and close two bars ago are each greater than the corresponding value three bars ago; (4) previous bar's upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band > minimum separation, with independently parameterized bands; (5) previous bar's shorter-lookback RSI > longer-lookback RSI; (6) previous bar's volume > volume two bars ago AND previous bar's volume > minimum volume; (7) previous bar's MACD main line > its signal line AND previous bar's MACD main line > its value two bars ago; (8) previous bar's stochastic %K − its value two bars ago > minimum change; (9) previous bar's shorter-lookback linear weighted moving average > longer-lookback linear weighted moving average; (10) previous bar's stochastic %K > %D AND %K < oversold threshold. MACD, RSI, Bollinger bands and moving averages use weighted price. Stochastic uses close for its range and linear weighted smoothing.
