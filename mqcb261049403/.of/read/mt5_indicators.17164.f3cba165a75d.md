Calculation: Input series = a selected OHLC-derived price, optionally filtered with a simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average; apply the same filter independently to high and low when range smoothing is enabled. Sort the resulting selected price, high, and low on each bar; call the middle value P, the maximum H, and the minimum L. The selected price may use standard OHLC, median, typical, weighted, average, body midpoint, or Heikin-Ashi equivalents; trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and its extreme variant is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Step stochastic = compute on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Starting at the bar whose zero-based index equals the range window, calculate R as the sum over the range period of max(H[current], L[previous]) - min(L[current], H[previous]), divided by the full range period. Earlier R values are zero. Find the minimum and maximum R over the range window including the current bar. Define three step sizes: fast multiplier times minimum R, fast multiplier times slow multiplier times their average, and fast multiplier times maximum R. For each step size S, set upper = P + 2S and lower = P - 2S; carry forward the previous trend and line, initially zero. Set trend to +1 if P > previous upper and to -1 if P < previous lower; the latter takes precedence if both comparisons hold. If trend is positive, replace lower with max(lower, previous lower) and set line = lower + S; if negative, replace upper with min(upper, previous upper) and set line = upper - S. Before the range window, minimum-step upper and lower are initialized to an empty-value sentinel, while the other bounds, trends, lines, and R are zero. Let B = maximum-step line - maximum step and D = 2 times maximum step; the main and signal values are respectively 100 times (minimum-step line - B)/D and 100 times (middle-step line - B)/D, or both zero when D = 0.
Calculation: Direction state = +1 when the main value > signal value, -1 when the main value < signal value, and otherwise the previous direction state, initially zero. For a higher timeframe, use the state of its still-forming bar without interpolation.
Long: direction state = +1
