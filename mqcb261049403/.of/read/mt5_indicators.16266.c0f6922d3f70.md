Calculation: Directional balance = 100 times the sum, over a lookback of consecutive bars, of +1 when a bar's close is greater than the previous close and -1 otherwise, divided by the lookback length.
Long: Chart-timeframe directional balance > 0.
Short: Chart-timeframe directional balance <= 0.
Long: Directional balance using the higher-timeframe bar still forming > 0.
Short: Directional balance using the higher-timeframe bar still forming <= 0.
