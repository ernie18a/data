Calculation: MACD = EMA of close over the shorter lookback minus EMA of close over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = choose ATR, standard deviation of close around its SMA, or EMA of volume as the source. Smooth the source with an EMA using the shorter of the front and back smoothing periods. Smooth that result again using the longer period: if the front period is shorter than the back period, use the EMA result when the first smoothed value is below the previous second smoothed value and otherwise use the first smoothed value; otherwise use the EMA result when the first smoothed value is above the previous second smoothed value and otherwise use the first smoothed value. Set the threshold to max(sensitivity, volatility multiplier × max(second smoothed value, sensitivity)). Sensitivity is a volume-unit parameter for the volume source and a price-unit parameter otherwise.
Long: Chart-timeframe MACD > chart-timeframe adaptive threshold.
Short: Chart-timeframe MACD < negative chart-timeframe adaptive threshold.
Long: On the configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, MACD on the selected signal bar > its adaptive threshold AND MACD on the preceding indicator bar <= its adaptive threshold. The selected signal bar may be the last completed bar or the still-forming bar.
Short: On the configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, MACD on the selected signal bar < negative its adaptive threshold AND MACD on the preceding indicator bar >= negative its adaptive threshold. The selected signal bar may be the last completed bar or the still-forming bar.
Exit: long, MACD on the selected indicator-timeframe signal bar <= its adaptive threshold OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, MACD on the selected indicator-timeframe signal bar >= negative its adaptive threshold OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
