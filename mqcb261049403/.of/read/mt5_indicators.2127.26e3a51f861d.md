Calculation: Stochastic = stochastic main line using a lookback and an equal-length slowing period; use either high/low or close/close for its range.
Calculation: Price movement = absolute difference between the current close and the close a lookback number of bars earlier.
Long: Stochastic > upper threshold AND Price movement > ATR / divisor.
Short: Stochastic < lower threshold AND Price movement > ATR / divisor.
Calculation: For the higher-timeframe version, calculate Stochastic, Price movement, and ATR on the higher timeframe using the bar still forming.
