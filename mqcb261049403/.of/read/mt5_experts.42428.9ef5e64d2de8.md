Long: No open position AND (new-bar filter disabled OR a new bar has started on the configured timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe) AND the close of the still-forming signal-timeframe bar is below the open of the last completed signal-timeframe bar AND the close of the last completed signal-timeframe bar is above its open; the signal timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Short: No open position AND (new-bar filter disabled OR a new bar has started on the configured timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe) AND the close of the still-forming signal-timeframe bar is above the open of the last completed signal-timeframe bar AND the close of the last completed signal-timeframe bar is below its open; the signal timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
