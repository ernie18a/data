Calculation: Directional score = 100 times the sum, over a configurable lookback of consecutive bars, of (1 if the bar's high is above the preceding bar's high, otherwise 0) minus (1 if the bar's low is below the preceding bar's low, otherwise 0), divided by the lookback. Calculate on a configurable timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe, using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar as the signal bar, with the immediately preceding bar for comparison.
Long: position is flat AND ((trend-following mode AND signal-bar directional score > 0 AND preceding score <= 0) OR (countertrend mode AND signal-bar directional score <= 0 AND preceding score > 0))
Short: position is flat AND ((trend-following mode AND signal-bar directional score <= 0 AND preceding score > 0) OR (countertrend mode AND signal-bar directional score > 0 AND preceding score <= 0))
Exit: long, (trend-following mode AND signal-bar directional score <= 0) OR (countertrend mode AND signal-bar directional score > 0) OR low <= entry price minus enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus enabled take-profit distance
Exit: short, (trend-following mode AND signal-bar directional score > 0) OR (countertrend mode AND signal-bar directional score <= 0) OR high >= entry price plus enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus enabled take-profit distance
