Calculation: Schaff JCCX trend cycle = apply a stochastic calculation over the difference between fast and slow JCCX values, then a second stochastic calculation over the first result. Each JCCX is the JurX-smoothed difference between the selected price and its JJMA, divided by the JurX-smoothed absolute difference, clipped to [−1, 1]. The fast JCCX uses a shorter JJMA lookback than the slow JCCX; both use the same JurX lookback. Both stochastic stages use the same cycle lookback; scale the first to 0–100 and the second to −100–100, smoothing each recursively with equal weight on its current raw value and previous smoothed value. Calculate on the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using its last completed bar and preceding completed bar.
Long: Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > upper level AND Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar ≤ upper level
Short: Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar < lower level AND Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar ≥ lower level
Exit: long, Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar < lower level AND Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar ≥ lower level
Exit: short, Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > upper level AND Schaff JCCX trend cycle on the preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar ≤ upper level
