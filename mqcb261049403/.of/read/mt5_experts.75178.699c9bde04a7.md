Calculation: Support/resistance zone = chart-timeframe confirmed swing low/high, respectively, with symmetric lookback bars; retain only zones whose weighted volume, volatility-normalized departure, and geometric symmetry score meets the minimum. Zone bounds extend from the pivot by a volatility-based half-width.
Calculation: Pending zone = an unbroken zone touched by a completed chart-timeframe bar whose range overlaps the zone expanded by an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Broken zone = a pending support/resistance zone with the required consecutive completed chart-timeframe closes below support/above resistance by at least the greater of a zone-height fraction or ATR; a broken zone becomes active again when a completed close returns inside its bounds.
Calculation: Approach = compare bullish and bearish pressure counts over recent completed chart-timeframe bars (each close is compared with its open and the preceding close); classify the majority direction as aggressive when the absolute displacement from the oldest bar's open to the latest close, divided by ATR, exceeds a threshold, otherwise conservative; equal counts are neutral.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = the last completed chart-timeframe bar closes at or above the zone bottom and is either a bullish pin bar (lower tail at least twice body, upper tail at most 15% of range, body at most 30% of range) or a bullish engulfing pattern (previous bearish bar; current bullish bar; current body engulfs previous body, is at least 1.2 times its size, and occupies at least 55% of current range; previous body occupies at least 30% of previous range).
Calculation: Bearish rejection = the last completed chart-timeframe bar closes at or below the zone top and is either a bearish pin bar (upper tail at least twice body, lower tail at most 15% of range, body at most 30% of range) or a bearish engulfing pattern (previous bullish bar; current bearish bar; current body engulfs previous body, is at least 1.2 times its size, and occupies at least 55% of current range; previous body occupies at least 30% of previous range).
Calculation: HTF bullish bias = still-forming higher-timeframe close > its EMA and, when enabled, higher-timeframe RSI > its oversold threshold.
Calculation: HTF bearish bias = still-forming higher-timeframe close < its EMA and, when enabled, higher-timeframe RSI < its overbought threshold.
Long: pending zone score >= minimum AND still-forming chart-timeframe close is within the zone expanded by half an ATR AND ((support zone AND not broken AND not already buy-triggered AND approach is neutral or bearish conservative AND bullish rejection AND HTF bullish bias) OR (resistance zone AND broken AND not already buy-triggered AND approach is bullish aggressive AND bullish rejection AND HTF bullish bias)) AND no same-symbol position is open AND spread <= maximum.
Short: pending zone score >= minimum AND still-forming chart-timeframe close is within the zone expanded by half an ATR AND ((resistance zone AND not broken AND not already sell-triggered AND approach is neutral or bullish conservative AND bearish rejection AND HTF bearish bias) OR (support zone AND broken AND not already sell-triggered AND approach is bearish aggressive AND bearish rejection AND HTF bearish bias)) AND no same-symbol position is open AND spread <= maximum.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price - (zone height + ATR × volatility buffer); initial long target = entry price + (entry-to-stop distance × risk/reward ratio). Initial short stop = entry price + (zone height + ATR × volatility buffer); initial short target = entry price - (entry-to-stop distance × risk/reward ratio). After favorable movement exceeds the trailing-start distance, trail the stop at the configured trailing-step distance from current price, updating only in the favorable direction.
Exit: long, low <= current stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= current stop OR low <= target.
