Calculation: AC and AO = standard Accelerator Oscillator and Awesome Oscillator, calculated on each configured signal timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use the still-forming bar for current values and the last completed bar for previous values.
Calculation: Stochastic main and signal = standard stochastic oscillator lines, calculated on the configured signal timeframe; use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of close, calculated on the configured signal timeframe; use the still-forming bar.
Long: (AC on Original/AES timeframe > 0 AND previous AC < 0 AND AC > previous AC AND stochastic main > 50 AND (Original SMA filter disabled OR close > SMA)) OR (AC on Original/AES timeframe > previous AC AND stochastic main > stochastic signal AND AO on Original/AES timeframe > previous AO AND (AES SMA filter disabled OR close > SMA)) OR (AC on Squeeze-More timeframe > previous AC AND AO on Squeeze-More timeframe > previous AO AND stochastic main > stochastic signal AND stochastic main > 20 AND stochastic main < 80)
Short: (AC on Original/AES timeframe < 0 AND previous AC > 0 AND AC < previous AC AND stochastic main < 50 AND (Original SMA filter disabled OR close < SMA)) OR (AC on Original/AES timeframe < previous AC AND stochastic main < stochastic signal AND AO on Original/AES timeframe < previous AO AND (AES SMA filter disabled OR close < SMA)) OR (AC on Squeeze-More timeframe < previous AC AND AO on Squeeze-More timeframe < previous AO AND stochastic main < stochastic signal AND stochastic main < 80 AND stochastic main > 20)
