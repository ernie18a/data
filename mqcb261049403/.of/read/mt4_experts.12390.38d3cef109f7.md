Long: Current hour is within the configured signal window (start inclusive and end exclusive if the window does not wrap midnight; both endpoints inclusive if it wraps midnight) AND the forming calendar-day bar's close > its open AND each of the preceding lookback chart bars has close > open AND close - open < a multiplier of the chart-timeframe ATR calculated on the forming bar
Short: Current hour is within the configured signal window (start inclusive and end exclusive if the window does not wrap midnight; both endpoints inclusive if it wraps midnight) AND the forming calendar-day bar's close < its open AND each of the preceding lookback chart bars has close < open AND open - close < a multiplier of the chart-timeframe ATR calculated on the forming bar
Calculation: The calendar-day bar is higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe when the chart timeframe is respectively shorter than, equal to, or longer than a calendar day.
