Calculation: Oscillator = change from the previous bar in the difference between a shorter-period moving average and a longer-period moving average of the same selected price series; each average has an independently selected smoothing method.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe oscillator series = oscillator calculated on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar, sampled on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carried forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe oscillator > 0
Short: Chart-timeframe oscillator < 0
Long: Higher-timeframe oscillator series > 0
Short: Higher-timeframe oscillator series < 0
Long: Higher-timeframe oscillator series on the preceding chart bar > 0 AND higher-timeframe oscillator series on the selected signal chart bar <= 0
Short: Higher-timeframe oscillator series on the preceding chart bar < 0 AND higher-timeframe oscillator series on the selected signal chart bar >= 0
