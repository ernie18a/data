Calculation: Price Zone Oscillator = 100 × EMA(signed close) / EMA(close), where signed close is close if close > previous close, −close if close < previous close, and 0 if equal
Calculation: Floating upper level = rolling minimum of the Price Zone Oscillator + upper percentage of its rolling range
Calculation: Floating lower level = rolling minimum of the Price Zone Oscillator + lower percentage of its rolling range
Long: Price Zone Oscillator > Floating upper level
Short: Price Zone Oscillator < Floating lower level
