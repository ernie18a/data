Calculation: Oscillator and signal = On a configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe, use the last completed indicator bar by default; optionally use the forming bar or an older completed bar. For each indicator bar, find the highest high and lowest low over a lookback equal to the sum of two independently configurable lookback lengths minus one. Compute 100 × (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low), rounded to the nearest integer; use 50 if the range is zero. Apply an EMA to this series, then apply a separately parameterized EMA to the unrounded first EMA. Round each EMA to the nearest integer for comparisons; these are the oscillator and signal respectively.
Long: Oscillator > signal AND oscillator on the preceding indicator bar <= signal on the preceding indicator bar.
Short: Oscillator < signal AND oscillator on the preceding indicator bar >= signal on the preceding indicator bar.
Exit: long, oscillator < signal OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, oscillator > signal OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
