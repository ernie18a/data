Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-period moving average of the accumulation/distribution line minus its longer-period moving average, using the same averaging method for both
Calculation: Signal = moving average of the oscillator
Long: Chart-timeframe oscillator > signal AND upper Bollinger Band minus middle Bollinger Band >= flatness threshold
Short: Chart-timeframe oscillator < signal AND upper Bollinger Band minus middle Bollinger Band >= flatness threshold
Long: Oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > its signal AND oscillator on the preceding higher-timeframe bar < its signal
Short: Oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < its signal AND oscillator on the preceding higher-timeframe bar > its signal
Exit: short, oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > its signal
Exit: long, oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < its signal
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
