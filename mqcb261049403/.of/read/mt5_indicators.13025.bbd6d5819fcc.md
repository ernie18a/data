Calculation: Bulls-bears oscillator = Let X = high + low - 2 × EMA(close). Initialize L0, L1, L2 and L3 to zero; on each bar update them in order using their previous-bar values: L0 = (1 - smoothing coefficient) × X + smoothing coefficient × previous L0; L1 = -smoothing coefficient × L0 + previous L0 + smoothing coefficient × previous L1; L2 = -smoothing coefficient × L1 + previous L1 + smoothing coefficient × previous L2; L3 = -smoothing coefficient × L2 + previous L2 + smoothing coefficient × previous L3. Let U be the sum of max(L0 - L1, 0), max(L1 - L2, 0) and max(L2 - L3, 0); let D be the sum of max(L1 - L0, 0), max(L2 - L1, 0) and max(L3 - L2, 0). The oscillator is U / (U + D) when U + D != 0, otherwise zero.
Long: The oscillator on the last completed bar of any selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than or equal to the chart timeframe, > upper threshold.
Short: The oscillator on the last completed bar of any selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than or equal to the chart timeframe, < lower threshold.
