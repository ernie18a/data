Calculation: Bulls/Bears Eyes oscillator = On each configured timeframe, calculate Bulls Power as high minus the EMA of close and Bears Power as low minus the EMA of close, using the same lookback. Let P be their sum. Recursively calculate L0=(1−gamma)P+gamma·L0prev; L1=−gamma·L0+L0prev+gamma·L1prev; L2=−gamma·L1+L1prev+gamma·L2prev; L3=−gamma·L2+L2prev+gamma·L3prev, initializing all prior values to zero at the start of the available history. Let CU be the sum of max(L0−L1,0), max(L1−L2,0), and max(L2−L3,0); let CD be the sum of max(L1−L0,0), max(L2−L1,0), and max(L3−L2,0). The oscillator is CU/(CU+CD), or zero when the denominator is zero. Use the last completed bar on each configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
