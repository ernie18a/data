Calculation: Pivot levels = Select one pivot family, calculated from the last completed bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Let H, L, C be that bar's high, low, close, D = H - L, and O be the open of the still-forming bar of that timeframe. Standard: P = (H + L + C)/3, R1 = 2P - L, S1 = 2P - H, R2 = P + D, S2 = P - D, R3 = R1 + D, S3 = L - 2(H - P), R4 = R3 + R2 - R1, S4 = S3 - S1 + S2. Woodie: P = (H + L + 2O)/4, R1 = 2P - L, S1 = 2P - H, R2 = P + D, S2 = P - D. Fibonacci: P = (H + L + C)/3, R = P + kD, S = P - kD, for the standard Fibonacci pivot multipliers. Camarilla: P = C, R = C + kD, S = C - kD, for k = 1.1/12, 1.1/6, 1.1/4, 1.1/2; R5 = HC/L and S5 = 2C - R5.
Calculation: Bounce tracking = Process each last completed bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe. A pivot is crossed when its level is between the bar's low and high inclusive and differs from its open. If several pivots are crossed, select the highest crossed level for a bullish bar and the last crossed level in the family order for a bearish or doji bar. The family orders are Standard: P, S1, S2, S3, R1, R2, R3, R4, S4; Woodie: P, S1, S2, R1, R2; Fibonacci: P, ascending R levels, ascending S levels; Camarilla: P, S1 through S4, R1 through R5, S5. Maintain a state initially zero and a remembered pivot. When a pivot is crossed while state is nonzero, reset state to zero if the selected pivot's zero-based index differs from the state. If state is zero and the selected resistance closes above its level, set state to 1 and remember that pivot; if the selected support closes below its level, set state to 2 and remember that pivot. Reset state at each new pivot-timeframe bar and after a delayed bounce condition is met. For each pivot, require the configured number of bounce-timeframe bars to have elapsed since its previous bounce alert; reset this wait at each new pivot-timeframe bar.
Long: A selected support or central pivot is crossed AND the completed bounce bar closes above it AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close > open AND that pivot's bounce wait has elapsed AND, if the Bollinger filter is enabled, the current price is above the upper Bollinger band or below the lower Bollinger band of the still-forming bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: State is nonzero AND the completed bounce bar closes above the remembered pivot AND the remembered pivot is a support or the current selected pivot is a support or central pivot AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close > open AND the remembered pivot's bounce wait has elapsed AND, if the Bollinger filter is enabled, the current price is above the upper Bollinger band or below the lower Bollinger band of the still-forming bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: A selected resistance or central pivot is crossed AND the completed bounce bar closes below it AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close < open AND that pivot's bounce wait has elapsed AND, if the Bollinger filter is enabled, the current price is above the upper Bollinger band or below the lower Bollinger band of the still-forming bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: State is nonzero AND the completed bounce bar closes below the remembered pivot AND the remembered pivot is a resistance or the current selected pivot is a resistance or central pivot AND, if the candle-direction filter is enabled, close < open AND the remembered pivot's bounce wait has elapsed AND, if the Bollinger filter is enabled, the current price is above the upper Bollinger band or below the lower Bollinger band of the still-forming bar of a selectable timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
