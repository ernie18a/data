Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages on the chart (signal) timeframe, using completed bars; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback. The filter EMA uses a longer lookback than both.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Long: Fast EMA > slow EMA AND completed chart-timeframe close > filter EMA AND fast EMA > fast EMA on the previous completed chart-timeframe bar AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA AND last completed higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe fast EMA AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND +DI > -DI
Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA AND completed chart-timeframe close < filter EMA AND fast EMA < fast EMA on the previous completed chart-timeframe bar AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA AND last completed higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe fast EMA AND ADX >= minimum ADX AND -DI > +DI
