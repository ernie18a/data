Calculation: Averaging loss threshold = the chart-timeframe price range (highest high minus lowest low) over a longer lookback of completed bars, divided by a factor; clamp it between a lower and upper bound derived from a default range, then convert points to price units.
Long: No open positions AND RSI on the last completed chart bar > the lower RSI threshold AND its close > the preceding bar’s close; OR the latest open position is long AND its loss from its entry price is at least the averaging loss threshold AND the open-position count is below the maximum.
Short: No open positions AND RSI on the last completed chart bar < the upper RSI threshold AND its close ≤ the preceding bar’s close; OR the latest open position is short AND its loss from its entry price is at least the averaging loss threshold AND the open-position count is below the maximum.
Exit: Long, close ≥ the volume-weighted average entry price + take-profit distance OR (stop loss enabled AND close ≤ the volume-weighted average entry price − stop-loss distance) OR (CCI stop enabled AND chart-timeframe CCI on the forming bar < the negative CCI threshold)
Exit: Short, close ≤ the volume-weighted average entry price − take-profit distance OR (stop loss enabled AND close ≥ the volume-weighted average entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (CCI stop enabled AND chart-timeframe CCI on the forming bar > the CCI threshold)
