Calculation: Averaging distance = the rounded difference between the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending at the last completed chart bar, divided by a range divisor and a price point; clamp the result between the rounded default distance divided by the divisor and the rounded default distance multiplied by the divisor.
Calculation: Weighted entry price = the volume-weighted mean entry price of the open positions; the first position has the initial volume, and each additional position has the initial volume multiplied by a lot exponent raised to the number of positions already open plus one, rounded to the configured volume precision.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after rounded profit in points from the weighted entry price reaches the activation threshold, set the stop to current price minus the trailing distance for a long or current price plus the trailing distance for a short; subsequently replace it only when the new stop is higher than the existing stop. Reset it when an additional position changes the common take-profit level.
Long: At a new chart bar, no position is open AND RSI of the last completed chart bar is above its lower threshold AND the last completed close is above the preceding close.
Short: At a new chart bar, no position is open AND RSI of the last completed chart bar is below its upper threshold AND the last completed close is less than or equal to the preceding close.
Long: At a new chart bar, the position side is long AND the number of open positions is below the maximum AND the rounded loss in points on the most recently opened position is positive and at least the averaging distance.
Short: At a new chart bar, the position side is short AND the number of open positions is below the maximum AND the rounded loss in points on the most recently opened position is positive and at least the averaging distance.
Exit: long, current price is at or above weighted entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: short, current price is at or below weighted entry price minus the take-profit distance.
Exit: long, classic stop enabled AND trailing stop disabled AND current price is at or below weighted entry price minus the stop-loss distance.
Exit: short, classic stop enabled AND trailing stop disabled AND current price is at or above weighted entry price plus the stop-loss distance.
Exit: long, trailing stop enabled AND classic stop disabled AND a trailing stop exists AND current price is at or below the trailing stop.
Exit: short, trailing stop enabled AND classic stop disabled AND a trailing stop exists AND current price is at or above the trailing stop.
Exit: long, CCI stop enabled AND close-based CCI of the forming chart bar is below the negative CCI threshold.
Exit: short, CCI stop enabled AND close-based CCI of the forming chart bar is above the positive CCI threshold.
Exit: long, time stop enabled AND at a new chart bar the number of occupied fixed-duration time intervals from the first entry through the current time, including the interval still forming, is at least the time limit.
Exit: short, time stop enabled AND at a new chart bar the number of occupied fixed-duration time intervals from the first entry through the current time, including the interval still forming, is at least the time limit.
