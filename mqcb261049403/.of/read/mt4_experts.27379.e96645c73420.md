Calculation: Average absolute bar-body change = mean of |close − open| over the configured number of previous completed chart bars
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper band − lower band, using the configured lookback and deviation
Calculation: Fast and slow SMAs = simple moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Average volume = mean volume of the three previous completed chart bars, normalized by chart-bar duration
Calculation: Up excursion = high − open of the previous completed chart bar; down excursion = open − low of that bar
Calculation: Trend EMA = exponential moving average of close on the chart timeframe
Long: no open position or virtual pending order AND higher-timeframe still-forming bar is within the configured entry-hour window and not on a configured no-trade weekday or past the configured Friday cutoff AND average volume > configured minimum AND average absolute bar-body change > configured minimum AND Bollinger Band width > configured lower bound AND Bollinger Band width < configured upper bound AND (fast SMA − slow SMA > configured trend threshold OR fast SMA − slow SMA < −configured trend threshold) AND up excursion < configured limit AND Stochastic main line on the chart timeframe crossed upward through the configured lower setup threshold between the two previous completed bars AND a buy-stop is pending AND current Stochastic main line > configured lower execution threshold AND current price > the price at buy-stop setup + configured offset
Short: no open position or virtual pending order AND higher-timeframe still-forming bar is within the configured entry-hour window and not on a configured no-trade weekday or past the configured Friday cutoff AND average volume > configured minimum AND average absolute bar-body change > configured minimum AND Bollinger Band width > configured lower bound AND Bollinger Band width < configured upper bound AND (fast SMA − slow SMA > configured trend threshold OR fast SMA − slow SMA < −configured trend threshold) AND down excursion < configured limit AND Stochastic main line on the chart timeframe crossed downward through the corresponding upper setup threshold between the two previous completed bars AND a sell-stop is pending AND current Stochastic main line < the corresponding upper execution threshold AND current price < the price at sell-stop setup − configured offset
Exit: long, low <= entry price − configured stop distance OR high >= entry price + configured target distance OR elapsed time since entry >= configured time limit (when configured profit mode is all trades or the position is profitable) OR current price > entry price + configured trailing distance AND the trailing stop is raised to current price − configured trailing distance OR previous completed chart-bar low < trend EMA and the position is losing
Exit: short, high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR low <= entry price − configured target distance OR elapsed time since entry >= configured time limit (when configured profit mode is all trades or the position is profitable) OR entry price − current price > configured trailing distance AND the trailing stop is lowered to current price + configured trailing distance OR previous completed chart-bar high > trend EMA and the position is losing
