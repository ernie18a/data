Calculation: Bullish order block = the most recent higher-timeframe pattern in completed bars where a bearish candle is followed by a bullish candle whose close is above the bearish candle’s high, and the next newer completed candle’s low is above that high; the zone is the bearish candle’s low-to-high range.
Calculation: Bearish order block = the most recent higher-timeframe pattern in completed bars where a bullish candle is followed by a bearish candle whose close is below the bullish candle’s low, and the next newer completed candle’s high is below that low; the zone is the bullish candle’s low-to-high range.
Calculation: Recent swing high = the high of the most recent strict local high among completed lower-timeframe bars 2–19, where a bar’s high exceeds both adjacent bars’ highs; if none exists, use the highest high among bars 2–11.
Calculation: Recent swing low = the low of the most recent strict local low among completed lower-timeframe bars 2–19, where a bar’s low is below both adjacent bars’ lows; if none exists, use the lowest low among bars 2–11.
Calculation: Volume threshold = mean tick volume of the 49 most recent completed lower-timeframe bars plus the volume multiplier times their population standard deviation.
Long: A bullish order block exists AND current price is within its zone AND the previous completed daily candle closes above its open OR is not bearish AND the current time is within either configured session window, inclusive AND the previous completed lower-timeframe close > Recent swing high AND its tick volume > Volume threshold
Short: A bearish order block exists AND current price is within its zone AND the previous completed daily candle closes below its open OR is not bullish AND the current time is within either configured session window, inclusive AND the previous completed lower-timeframe close < Recent swing low AND its tick volume > Volume threshold
