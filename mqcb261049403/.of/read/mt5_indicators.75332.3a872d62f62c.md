Calculation: Higher-timeframe zone = At the start of each higher-timeframe bar, search completed higher-timeframe bars within a lookback, starting with the most recent eligible middle bar, for the first qualifying three-bar pattern. A bullish pattern has an older bearish candle, a middle bullish candle closing above the older candle's high, and a newer candle with low above the older candle's high. A bearish pattern has an older bullish candle, a middle bearish candle closing below the older candle's low, and a newer candle with high below the older candle's low. The zone spans the older candle's low through high and retains the pattern's direction until the next higher-timeframe bar; if no pattern qualifies, there is no zone.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe swing high = The high of the most recent completed lower-timeframe bar, starting with the bar before the last completed bar, whose high is strictly greater than both adjacent bars' highs, within a lookback. If none exists, use the highest high in a shorter lookback starting at the same bar.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe swing low = The low of the most recent completed lower-timeframe bar, starting with the bar before the last completed bar, whose low is strictly less than both adjacent bars' lows, within the same lookback as the swing high. If none exists, use the lowest low in the same shorter lookback as the swing high.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume threshold = Mean volume plus a multiplier times the population standard deviation of volume over a lookback of completed lower-timeframe bars ending with the last completed bar.
Long: Higher-timeframe zone direction = bullish.
Short: Higher-timeframe zone direction = bearish.
Long: Higher-timeframe zone direction = bullish AND chart close >= zone low AND chart close <= zone high AND (bias filter disabled OR last completed bias-timeframe close >= its open) AND (session filter disabled OR chart datetime hour is within either configured session's inclusive start and end hours) AND last completed lower-timeframe close > lower-timeframe swing high AND last completed lower-timeframe volume > lower-timeframe volume threshold.
Short: Higher-timeframe zone direction = bearish AND chart close >= zone low AND chart close <= zone high AND (bias filter disabled OR last completed bias-timeframe close <= its open) AND (session filter disabled OR chart datetime hour is within either configured session's inclusive start and end hours) AND last completed lower-timeframe close < lower-timeframe swing low AND last completed lower-timeframe volume > lower-timeframe volume threshold.
Calculation: Bias timeframe = A higher timeframe than the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar.
