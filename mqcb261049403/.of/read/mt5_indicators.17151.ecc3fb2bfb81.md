Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (open+close)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, trend-biased price, or extreme trend-biased price; the same choices may use Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high+close)/2 when close>open, otherwise (low+close)/2. Extreme trend-biased price is high when close>open, low when close<open, otherwise close. Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4; Heikin-Ashi open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; Heikin-Ashi high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); Heikin-Ashi low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Adaptive Laguerre filter = for selected price p, period P and smoothing parameter S, initialize stages L0, L1, L2, L3 and filter F to p on the first bar. On each later bar, d=abs(p-previous F); find the minimum and maximum d over the current bar and preceding bars whose offsets are less than P. If the maximum equals the minimum, alpha=0; otherwise alpha is the median of (d-minimum)/(maximum-minimum) for the current and preceding M-1 bars, using the current bar's minimum and maximum for all M values and padding unavailable earlier values with zero, where M=floor(max(P*S,2)). Gamma is 1-alpha clamped to [0.00000000001,0.99999999999]. When P>1, L0=(1-gamma)*p+gamma*previous L0; L1=-gamma*L0+previous L0+gamma*previous L1; L2=-gamma*L1+previous L1+gamma*previous L2; L3=-gamma*L2+previous L2+gamma*previous L3; F=(L0+2*L1+2*L2+L3)/6. When P<=1, F=p. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a configurable higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Filter direction = 1 when the adaptive Laguerre filter is greater than its preceding value, -1 when it is less, and otherwise the previous direction; initialize to 0. For a higher timeframe, calculate direction on that timeframe and use the still-forming bar's direction.
Long: Filter direction = 1
Short: Filter direction = -1
