Calculation: WATR bands = Use ATR and an offset of K × instrument point size + M × ATR. The trend is up when the previous close is above the previous upper band and that band exists; otherwise it is down. If the candle opposes the trend (down close in an uptrend, up close in a downtrend), carry both bands forward. Otherwise, in an uptrend set the upper band to the greater of the previous upper band and close minus the offset, and clear the lower band; in a downtrend set the lower band to the lesser of the previous lower band and close plus the offset, and clear the upper band. If the close crosses the active band, switch to the other band using close plus or minus the offset and clear the former band.
Long: On the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, the upper WATR band exists and did not exist on the previous bar.
Short: On the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, the lower WATR band exists and did not exist on the previous bar.
