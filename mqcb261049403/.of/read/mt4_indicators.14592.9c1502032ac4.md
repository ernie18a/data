Calculation: Selected swing extremes = Starting with a configurable lookback, find the highest high and lowest low from the current chart bar backward. Accept them if the highest high is at least the highest high of a separate scan window immediately before its bar AND the lowest low is at most the lowest low of a separate scan window immediately before its bar. Otherwise, repeat with lookbacks equal to the initial lookback plus a configurable increment multiplied by the zero-based attempt index, subject to configurable attempt and lookback limits; if none is accepted, use the extremes from the last attempt. For equal highs or lows, select the most recent occurrence.
Long: The selected highest-high bar is more recent than the selected lowest-low bar.
Short: The selected lowest-low bar is more recent than the selected highest-high bar.
