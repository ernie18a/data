Calculation: Oscillator = any enabled MACD line, MACD histogram, RSI, smoothed stochastic %K, close-based CCI, close momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin money flow, or close-based money flow index. MACD and volume-weighted MACD use shorter and longer averages; the histogram uses a signal EMA of the MACD line. Smoothed stochastic %K is an SMA of raw %K. Close-based CCI uses close in place of typical price. Close momentum is close minus its earlier close. Volume-weighted MACD is the shorter close VWMA minus the longer close VWMA. Close-based money flow index uses close times volume, classified by the sign of the close change. Use volume from the backtest data. A zero denominator makes the corresponding value unavailable, except RSI and money flow index equal their upper bound when losses or negative flow are zero and gains or positive flow are positive.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low of the selected price source that is strictly more extreme than every value over a span of preceding bars and at least as extreme as every value over the same span of following bars. Use close for both sides, or high for tops and low for bottoms. A pivot becomes available when its following span has elapsed.
Calculation: Eligible divergence = for each enabled oscillator and enabled divergence kind, compare an endpoint with any of the most recent permitted confirmed pivots on the corresponding side, within the permitted maximum distance from the evaluation bar and beyond the minimum distance. With confirmation enabled, the endpoint is the preceding bar and either the oscillator or close on the evaluation bar must move in the divergence direction from the endpoint; otherwise the endpoint is the evaluation bar. A regular bullish divergence has oscillator at the endpoint above its value at a bottom pivot AND endpoint price below pivot price; hidden bullish has oscillator below AND price above. A regular bearish divergence has oscillator at the endpoint below its value at a top pivot AND endpoint price above pivot price; hidden bearish has oscillator above AND price below. Price means the selected close, low, or high source for that pivot side. For bullish divergence, every intermediate oscillator value and close must be at or above the respective straight line between its pivot and endpoint values; for bearish divergence both must be at or below their respective lines. All required oscillator values must be available.
Calculation: Divergence counts = count each enabled oscillator and enabled divergence kind with an eligible divergence once, across both directions. Separately count oscillators having at least one eligible bullish divergence and oscillators having at least one eligible bearish divergence. Evaluate on the chart timeframe and on each selected higher or lower timeframe, including its last completed bar and its still-forming bar; the latter is a provisional evaluation using data available so far.
Long: Total divergence count is at least the required minimum AND bullish oscillator count is greater than zero.
Short: Total divergence count is at least the required minimum AND bearish oscillator count is greater than zero AND bullish oscillator count is less than the panel's oscillator capacity.
