Calculation: Selected price = a chosen bar price: open, high, low, close, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, or (open + high + low + close)/4; alternatively, use high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise; or use (high + close)/2 when close > open, (low + close)/2 when close < open, and close otherwise; or use the DeMark price.
Calculation: Stochastic momentum = 100 × the three-stage smoothed selected-price displacement from the midpoint of the lookback high and low divided by the three-stage smoothed half-range of those highs and lows; return zero if the smoothed half-range is zero. Smooth both series with the same chosen moving-average method and the same sequence of three independently parameterized periods.
Calculation: Signal = the chosen moving-average method applied to stochastic momentum using the same period as the third smoothing stage.
Long: Chart-timeframe stochastic momentum > 0 AND stochastic momentum > previous chart-bar stochastic momentum OR chart-timeframe stochastic momentum < 0 AND stochastic momentum > previous chart-bar stochastic momentum OR chart-timeframe stochastic momentum > chart-timeframe signal.
Short: Chart-timeframe stochastic momentum > 0 AND stochastic momentum < previous chart-bar stochastic momentum OR chart-timeframe stochastic momentum < 0 AND stochastic momentum < previous chart-bar stochastic momentum OR chart-timeframe stochastic momentum < chart-timeframe signal.
Long: Stochastic momentum on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar >= stochastic momentum on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar OR stochastic momentum on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar >= its signal.
Short: Stochastic momentum on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < stochastic momentum on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar OR stochastic momentum on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < its signal.
