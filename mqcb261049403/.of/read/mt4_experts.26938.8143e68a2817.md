Calculation: projected rate = For each of three lookback scales (short, medium, long), take the LWMA of typical price at bar i for that scale’s period and subtract the same LWMA at bar i plus that scale’s lookback offset; multiply the difference by the gain and add the LWMA of typical price at bar i for the corresponding supporting period. Average the three results. All bars are on the chart timeframe; the medium and long scales use longer periods and offsets than the short scale. For i from 1 through the averaging lookback minus 1, compare the projected rate at the last i with the mean of projected rates across those i values; these are completed bars.
Long: projected rate at the last i < mean projected rate AND absolute(Bid - close two chart bars ago) > candle-distance threshold AND fast LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe, current forming bar > slow LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe, current forming bar AND low two chart bars ago < high one chart bar ago AND monthly RSI(14, close), current forming bar > monthly RSI(14, close), previous completed bar
Short: projected rate at the last i > mean projected rate AND absolute(Ask - close one chart bar ago) > candle-distance threshold AND fast LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe, current forming bar < slow LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe, current forming bar AND low one chart bar ago < high two chart bars ago AND monthly RSI(14, close), current forming bar < monthly RSI(14, close), previous completed bar
Exit: long, all six chart-timeframe EMAs of typical price with periods 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, and 15 are equal OR all six chart-timeframe EMAs of typical price with periods 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, and 60 are equal OR previous completed chart-bar close <= previous completed chart-bar lower Bollinger Band (20-period, 2 deviations)
Exit: short, all six chart-timeframe EMAs of typical price with periods 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, and 15 are equal OR all six chart-timeframe EMAs of typical price with periods 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, and 60 are equal OR previous completed chart-bar close <= previous completed chart-bar lower Bollinger Band (20-period, 2 deviations)
