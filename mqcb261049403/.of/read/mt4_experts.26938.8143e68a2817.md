Calculation: Rate-line average at chart-bar offset i = the mean of three lines, each equal to an LWMA of typical price plus a gain multiplied by the change in another LWMA of typical price over a lookback. For the second and third lines, multiply both LWMA periods and the lookback by progressively larger timeframe-scaling factors; calculate all three lines on chart bars.
Calculation: Smoothed rate line = the mean of the rate-line averages at all completed chart-bar offsets from 1 through the smoothing lookback; the rate line compared with it is the rate-line average at the oldest of those offsets.
Long: Rate line < smoothed rate line AND absolute difference between current chart close and close two chart bars ago > distance threshold AND shorter-period LWMA of current typical price > longer-period LWMA of current typical price AND low two chart bars ago < previous chart-bar high AND RSI on the forming higher-timeframe bar > RSI on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, exit strategy enabled AND (all six shorter-period EMAs of current typical price are equal OR all six longer-period EMAs of current typical price are equal OR previous chart-bar close <= previous chart-bar lower Bollinger Band).
Exit: short, exit strategy enabled AND (all six shorter-period EMAs of current typical price are equal OR all six longer-period EMAs of current typical price are equal OR previous chart-bar close <= previous chart-bar lower Bollinger Band).
