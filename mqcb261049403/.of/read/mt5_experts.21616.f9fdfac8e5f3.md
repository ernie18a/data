Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry stop; when enabled, update it for a long position to current price minus the trailing distance if current price minus entry price is greater than trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is below current price minus trailing distance minus trailing step; update it for a short position to current price plus the trailing distance if entry price minus current price is greater than trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is above current price plus trailing distance plus trailing step OR no stop exists.
Long: The forming chart-bar close RSI for the shorter-lookback parameter set is below its lower threshold AND its preceding completed chart-bar RSI is greater than its forming-bar RSI AND its preceding completed chart-bar moving average is greater than its forming-bar moving average AND the forming higher-timeframe close RSI is below that set's upper threshold.
Long: The forming chart-bar close RSI for the longer-lookback parameter set is below its lower threshold AND its preceding completed chart-bar RSI is greater than its forming-bar RSI AND its preceding completed chart-bar moving average is greater than its forming-bar moving average AND the forming higher-timeframe close RSI is below that set's upper threshold AND (the trend filter is disabled OR the preceding completed higher-timeframe filter moving average is greater than the forming higher-timeframe filter moving average).
Short: The forming chart-bar close RSI for the shorter-lookback parameter set is above its upper threshold AND its preceding completed chart-bar RSI is less than its forming-bar RSI AND its preceding completed chart-bar moving average is less than its forming-bar moving average AND the forming higher-timeframe close RSI is above that set's lower threshold.
Short: The forming chart-bar close RSI for the longer-lookback parameter set is above its upper threshold AND its preceding completed chart-bar RSI is less than its forming-bar RSI AND its preceding completed chart-bar moving average is less than its forming-bar moving average AND the forming higher-timeframe close RSI is above that set's lower threshold AND (the trend filter is disabled OR the preceding completed higher-timeframe filter moving average is less than the forming higher-timeframe filter moving average).
Exit: long, price is at or below the trailing stop OR price is at or above entry price plus the take-profit factor times chart-timeframe ATR at entry.
Exit: short, price is at or above the trailing stop OR price is at or below entry price minus the stop-loss factor times chart-timeframe ATR at entry.
Calculation: Entry stop = entry price minus the stop-loss factor times chart-timeframe ATR at entry for a long position, or entry price plus the stop-loss factor times chart-timeframe ATR at entry for a short position.
