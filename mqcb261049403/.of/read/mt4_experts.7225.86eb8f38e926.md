Calculation: WPR swing trend = +1 when the latest peak is at least kATR × ATR above the preceding peak and the latest trough is above the preceding trough; −1 when the preceding trough is at least kATR × ATR below the latest trough and the preceding peak is above the latest peak; otherwise 0. Peaks are highest highs between successive WPR readings ≥ −CriteriaWPR; troughs are lowest lows between successive readings ≤ CriteriaWPR − 100. Use WPR and pivots on the chart timeframe; use ATR on that timeframe at the latest pivot.
Calculation: Composite trend = (WPR swing trend + NRTR-Gator trend from buffer 7 on the current chart-timeframe bar + NRTR-Gator trend from buffer 6 on the last completed chart-timeframe bar) / 3.
Calculation: Bullish target level = higher of the latest two peak highs; bullish support level = higher of the latest two trough lows. Bearish target level = lower of the latest two trough lows; bearish support level = lower of the latest two peak highs.
Calculation: Reward/risk ratio = (target distance − spread) / stop distance, where bullish target and stop distances are target level − support level and current bid − support level; bearish distances are support level − target level and support level − current bid. If stop distance ≤ 0 and target distance exceeds the minimum target-in-spread threshold, set the ratio to 100.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = greater of the applicable stop distance above and 2 × ATR(1) on the chart timeframe; initial target distance = greater of target level − support level and 2 × ATR(1) for bullish setups, or support level − target level and 2 × ATR(1) for bearish setups.
Calculation: Trailing stop = while the stop is on the loss side of entry, long: entry price − initial stop distance × (1 − bars-since-entry² / (2 × ZeroBar²)); short: entry price + initial stop distance × (1 − bars-since-entry² / (2 × ZeroBar²)). After the stop reaches breakeven, when bars-since-entry > ZeroBar, long: current bid − 2 × ATR(1); short: current ask + 2 × ATR(1). Count bars and calculate ATR on the entry timeframe; ATR uses its last completed bar. Higher-timeframe confirmation uses the chart timeframe plus either the timeframe two or three steps higher, using their current bars.
Long: WPR swing trend = +1 AND composite trend > 0 AND (composite trend × composite trend two timeframes higher = 1 OR composite trend × composite trend three timeframes higher = 1) AND reward/risk ratio ≥ TP/SL criterion.
Short: WPR swing trend = −1 AND composite trend < 0 AND (composite trend × composite trend two timeframes higher = 1 OR composite trend × composite trend three timeframes higher = 1) AND reward/risk ratio ≥ TP/SL criterion.
Exit: long, low ≤ initial stop price or low ≤ trailing stop or high ≥ entry price + initial target distance
Exit: short, high ≥ initial stop price or high ≥ trailing stop or low ≤ entry price − initial target distance
