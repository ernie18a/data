Calculation: VHF = absolute difference between the highest high and lowest low over its lookback, divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback; use the latest completed chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior candle closes below its open, latest completed candle closes above its open, and prior candle body is smaller than latest candle body
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = prior candle closes above its open, latest completed candle closes below its open, and prior candle body is smaller than latest candle body
Calculation: Morning star = the candle three bars back is bearish; its body is larger than the body two bars back; the latest completed candle is bullish, its body is larger than the middle candle's body, and its close is above the midpoint of the candle three bars back's body
Calculation: Evening star = the candle three bars back is bullish; its body is larger than the body two bars back; the latest completed candle is bearish, its body is larger than the middle candle's body, and its close is below the midpoint of the candle three bars back's body
Calculation: Doji = latest completed candle's body is less than its high-low range divided by the doji threshold
Long: latest completed candle closes above its open AND NOT evening star AND NOT doji AND NOT bearish engulfing AND standard deviation of close exceeds its threshold AND ((VHF meets or exceeds its threshold AND MACD histogram is positive AND greater than on the prior completed chart-timeframe bar) OR (VHF is below its threshold AND stochastic main line is above its signal line))
Short: latest completed candle closes below its open AND NOT morning star AND NOT doji AND NOT bullish engulfing AND standard deviation of close exceeds its threshold AND ((VHF meets or exceeds its threshold AND MACD histogram is negative AND less than on the prior completed chart-timeframe bar) OR (VHF is below its threshold AND stochastic main line is below its signal line))
Exit: short, bullish engulfing OR morning star OR (VHF meets or exceeds its threshold AND MACD histogram is positive) OR (VHF is below its threshold AND stochastic main line is greater than or equal to its signal line) OR price reaches the short stop-loss, take-profit, or trailing-stop level
Exit: long, bearish engulfing OR evening star OR (VHF meets or exceeds its threshold AND MACD histogram is negative) OR (VHF is below its threshold AND stochastic main line is less than or equal to its signal line) OR price reaches the long stop-loss, take-profit, or trailing-stop level
