Calculation: Fast and slow stochastic step lines = Apply the following independently to the stochastic main line multiplied by 100, using a smaller fast step and a larger slow step. Initialize the upper bound to a large negative value, the lower bound to a large positive value, the trend to 0, and the line to 0. On each bar, set candidate upper and lower bounds to the scaled stochastic plus and minus twice the step. Set the trend to +1 if the scaled stochastic is greater than the previous upper bound, or to -1 if it is less than the previous lower bound; otherwise retain the trend. If the trend is +1, set the lower bound to the greater of its candidate and previous values, and set the line to the lower bound plus the step. If the trend is -1, set the upper bound to the lesser of its candidate and previous values, and set the line to the upper bound minus the step. Otherwise retain the line. Use the candidate value for any bound not adjusted. Calculate on the chart timeframe, or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar or a selected completed bar.
Long: Previous fast step line <= previous slow step line AND fast step line > slow step line.
Short: Previous fast step line >= previous slow step line AND fast step line < slow step line.
