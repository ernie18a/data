Calculation: Upper/Lower band = Bollinger Bands of close (middle = SMA of close, upper/lower = middle +/- multiple of standard deviation), long lookback, on the chart timeframe, using the current bar
Long: no open or pending long AND bar hour > session-open hour AND bar hour < session-close hour AND lower band < close < upper band -> arm a buy stop at the upper band; the pending level is re-set to the current upper band whenever the upper band falls below it; enter long when high >= pending level; initial stop = lower band at arming time, take-profit = pending level + fixed distance
Short: no open or pending short AND bar hour > session-open hour AND bar hour < session-close hour AND lower band < close < upper band -> arm a sell stop at the lower band; the pending level is re-set to the current lower band whenever the lower band rises above it; enter short when low <= pending level; initial stop = upper band at arming time, take-profit = pending level - fixed distance
Exit: long, low <= stop level OR high >= entry price + fixed take-profit distance; stop level starts at the lower band and is ratcheted up to the current lower band whenever close > entry price AND close > lower band AND lower band > stop level
Exit: short, high >= stop level OR low <= entry price - fixed take-profit distance; stop level starts at the upper band and is ratcheted down to the current upper band whenever close < entry price AND close < upper band AND upper band < stop level
