Calculation: Bollinger Bands = bands of the close on the chart timeframe: middle = simple moving average of close over the band lookback, upper/lower = middle +/- a standard-deviation multiplier times the standard deviation of close over the same lookback; values are taken at the last completed chart bar
Long: no open position AND DEMA of close on a higher timeframe than the chart is rising, meaning its value on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar > its value on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > its value on the higher-timeframe bar before that AND last completed chart bar open < lower Bollinger Band AND that bar close > lower Bollinger Band
Short: no open position AND DEMA of close on a higher timeframe than the chart is falling, meaning its value on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar < its value on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < its value on the higher-timeframe bar before that AND last completed chart bar open > upper Bollinger Band AND that bar close < upper Bollinger Band
Exit: long, last completed chart bar open > upper Bollinger Band AND that bar close < upper Bollinger Band
Exit: short, last completed chart bar open < lower Bollinger Band AND that bar close > lower Bollinger Band
