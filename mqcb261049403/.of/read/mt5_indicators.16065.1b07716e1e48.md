Calculation: PPO = (short-lookback moving average of close − long-lookback moving average of close) / long-lookback moving average of close, calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe’s still-forming bar; the short lookback is shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: PPO signal = moving average of PPO, using the same timeframe and bar state
Long: PPO > PPO signal
Short: PPO < PPO signal
