Calculation: Smoothed volume = smooth volume using the same moving-average method and length as the volume-weighted oscillator.
Long: Volume-weighted oscillator > upper threshold × smoothed volume.
Short: Volume-weighted oscillator < lower threshold × smoothed volume.
