Calculation: Shifted smoothed moving average = smoothed moving average of median price (high + low) / 2, shifted forward by a configurable number of bars.
Calculation: Long stop = the greater of entry price minus the stop-loss distance and each chart-bar open since entry minus the trailing distance for which that open exceeds entry price plus the trailing distance; omit the trailing component when trailing is disabled.
Calculation: Short stop = the lesser of entry price plus the stop-loss distance and each chart-bar open since entry plus the trailing distance for which that open is below entry price minus the trailing distance; omit the trailing component when trailing is disabled.
Long: Last completed chart-bar close < last completed chart-bar shifted smoothed moving average AND last completed higher-timeframe close < last completed higher-timeframe shifted smoothed moving average with a longer period AND chart-bar moving average minus chart-bar close <= maximum distance AND last completed chart-bar stochastic signal < lower threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main < lower threshold AND preceding chart-bar stochastic main < last completed chart-bar stochastic signal AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main > last completed chart-bar stochastic signal.
Short: Last completed chart-bar close > last completed chart-bar shifted smoothed moving average AND last completed higher-timeframe close > last completed higher-timeframe shifted smoothed moving average with a longer period AND chart-bar close minus chart-bar moving average <= maximum distance AND last completed chart-bar stochastic signal > upper threshold AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main > upper threshold AND preceding chart-bar stochastic main > last completed chart-bar stochastic signal AND last completed chart-bar stochastic main < last completed chart-bar stochastic signal.
Exit: Long, bar low <= long stop OR bar high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, bar high >= short stop OR bar low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
