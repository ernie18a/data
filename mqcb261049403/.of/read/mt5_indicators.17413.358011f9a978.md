Calculation: Selected prices = Three independently selected OHLC-derived prices, each chosen from open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (high+low+open+close)/4, (open+close)/2, trend-biased price, or trend-biased extreme; the same choices can use Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high+close)/2 when close>open and (low+close)/2 otherwise; trend-biased extreme is high when close>open, low when close<open, and close otherwise. Sort the three selected prices on each bar into low, middle, and high.
Calculation: Slowed stochastic = 100 times the sum over the slowing lookback of (middle selected price minus the lowest selected low over the stochastic lookback), divided by the sum over the slowing lookback of (highest selected high minus lowest selected low over the stochastic lookback); use zero when the denominator is zero. Use available bars for incomplete initial lookbacks.
Calculation: Trailing bands and trend = Calculate a selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average of the slowed stochastic and its standard deviation over a separately specified lookback, with optional sample correction. The raw upper and lower bands equal the moving average plus and minus a deviation multiplier times the standard deviation. Initialize trend to neutral and bands to their raw values. On each subsequent bar, set trend to up if stochastic is above the previous trailing upper band; otherwise set it to down if stochastic is below the previous trailing lower band; otherwise retain the previous trend. If trend is down, the trailing upper band is the lesser of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw upper band. If trend is up, the trailing lower band is the greater of its raw value and its previous value; otherwise it is the raw lower band. Use available bars for initial moving-average and deviation lookbacks; initialize exponential and smoothed averages with the first stochastic value, with the smoothed average on the second bar equal to that bar's stochastic value. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use the bar still forming.
Long: Trend is up AND previous trend is not up.
Short: Trend is down AND previous trend is not down.
