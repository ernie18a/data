Long: Short-period EMA crosses above long-period EMA on the last completed chart-timeframe bar, with the preceding completed bar at or below, AND current hour is within the configured WIB session
Short: Short-period EMA crosses below long-period EMA on the last completed chart-timeframe bar, with the preceding completed bar at or below, AND current hour is within the configured WIB session
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
