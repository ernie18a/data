Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = short-lookback smoothing of the accumulation/distribution line minus its long-lookback smoothing; calculate on the chart timeframe or on the selected timeframe, which is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Signal line = moving average of the Chaikin Oscillator, calculated on the same timeframe and bar as the oscillator.
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper band minus middle band, expressed in points and calculated on the same timeframe and bar as the oscillator.
Long: Chaikin Oscillator > signal line AND Bollinger Band width >= flat threshold
Short: Chaikin Oscillator < signal line AND Bollinger Band width >= flat threshold
