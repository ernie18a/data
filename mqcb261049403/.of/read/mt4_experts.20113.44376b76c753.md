Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = moving averages using the same method and applied price, with independently configurable periods, horizontal shifts, and timeframes; the slow period equals the fast period plus a configurable offset. Each timeframe may be the chart timeframe, a higher timeframe, or a lower timeframe; use its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Trading schedule = the current bar's weekday is between the configured start and end weekdays inclusive AND its time of day is between the configured start and end times inclusive.
Long: Trading schedule is active AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND (no long position exists OR averaging up is enabled AND open > highest long entry price × (1 + averaging step fraction) OR averaging down is enabled AND open < lowest long entry price × (1 − averaging step fraction × averaging-down multiplier)) AND (hedging is enabled OR no short position exists) AND long trading is enabled.
Short: Trading schedule is active AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND (no short position exists OR averaging up is enabled AND open < lowest short entry price × (1 − averaging step fraction) OR averaging down is enabled AND open > highest short entry price × (1 + averaging step fraction × averaging-down multiplier)) AND (hedging is enabled OR no long position exists) AND short trading is enabled.
Exit: long, trading schedule is active AND close on moving-average bias change is enabled AND fast moving average < slow moving average.
Exit: short, trading schedule is active AND close on moving-average bias change is enabled AND fast moving average > slow moving average.
