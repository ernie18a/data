Calculation: NRatio = For each higher-timeframe bar, let P be the configured applied price from OHLC. Initialize the trailing stop from P and the initial trend (up if the next bar closes above its open, otherwise down). On each subsequent bar, if trend is up and P falls below the prior stop, flip down and set stop to P × (1 + Kf/100); otherwise keep the higher of the prior stop and P × (1 − Kf/100). If trend is down and P rises above the prior stop, flip up and set stop to P × (1 − Kf/100); otherwise keep the lower of the prior stop and P × (1 + Kf/100). Calculate oscillator = 100 × |P − stop| / (P × Kf), smooth it with the configured XMA, then calculate 100 × smoothed oscillator^Sharp. Evaluate on the selected higher-timeframe bar: index 0 is still forming; index 1 or greater is completed.
Long: Mode equals entry-zone mode AND NRatio > upper level AND prior NRatio ≤ upper level OR Mode equals exit-zone mode AND NRatio > lower level AND prior NRatio ≤ lower level
Short: Mode equals entry-zone mode AND NRatio < lower level AND prior NRatio ≥ lower level OR Mode equals exit-zone mode AND NRatio < upper level AND prior NRatio ≥ upper level
