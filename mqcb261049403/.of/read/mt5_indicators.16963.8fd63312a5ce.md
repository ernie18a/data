Calculation: Input price = a selected OHLC price, standard OHLC price average, or equivalent Heikin-Ashi price; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, while extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Apply the same rules to Heikin-Ashi OHLC when selected.
Calculation: Ehlers-smoothed RSI = 100 times the sum of positive changes in smoothed price divided by the sum of absolute changes over the RSI lookback; smoothed price is (current price + 2 × previous price + price two bars ago)/4.
Calculation: Inverse Fisher RSI = tanh(moving average of 0.1 × (selected RSI − 50)); select regular, Cutler's, Harris', rapid, slow, RSX, or Ehlers-smoothed RSI and a simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Levels = for a floating lookback greater than one bar, the upper and lower levels are the lookback minimum inverse Fisher RSI plus their respective configurable fractions of its lookback range, and the middle level is the midpoint of that range; otherwise, each fixed outer level is 2 × its configurable fraction − 1 and the middle level is the average of the outer levels. Include the current bar in the floating lookback.
Long: Inverse Fisher RSI > upper level in outer-level mode OR inverse Fisher RSI > middle level in middle-level mode OR inverse Fisher RSI > its previous value in slope mode.
Short: Inverse Fisher RSI < lower level in outer-level mode OR inverse Fisher RSI < middle level in middle-level mode OR inverse Fisher RSI < its previous value in slope mode.
