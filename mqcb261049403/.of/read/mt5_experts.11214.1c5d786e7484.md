Calculation: Oscillator and signal = On the chosen indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, calculate a fast moving average minus a slow moving average of the selected bar price. Calculate its change over a momentum lookback. Smooth that change and its absolute value separately with the same first smoothing length, then smooth each result again with the same second smoothing length. The oscillator is 100 times the twice-smoothed change divided by the twice-smoothed absolute change; use zero when the denominator is zero, then replace a zero oscillator with 0.000000001. The signal is a moving average of the oscillator with a separate smoothing length; replace a zero signal with 0.000000001. Use the same selected smoothing method throughout. Evaluate the last completed indicator bar and its predecessor; alternatively, if configured for a forming bar, evaluate the still-forming indicator bar and its predecessor.
Long: Oscillator > signal AND previous oscillator <= previous signal
Short: Oscillator < signal AND previous oscillator >= previous signal
Exit: long, oscillator < signal OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance
Exit: short, oscillator > signal OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance
