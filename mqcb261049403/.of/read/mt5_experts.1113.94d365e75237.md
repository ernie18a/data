Long: MACD main line on the selected chart bar > MACD main line on the preceding bar AND reverse signals is disabled OR MACD main line on the selected chart bar < MACD main line on the preceding bar AND reverse signals is enabled
Short: MACD main line on the selected chart bar < MACD main line on the preceding bar AND reverse signals is disabled OR MACD main line on the selected chart bar > MACD main line on the preceding bar AND reverse signals is enabled
Exit: long, initial take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + initial take-profit distance
Exit: short, initial take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - initial take-profit distance
Exit: long, initial stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance
Exit: short, initial stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance
