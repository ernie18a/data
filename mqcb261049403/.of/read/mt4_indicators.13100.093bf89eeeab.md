Calculation: Trend oscillator = short-period EMA of chart-timeframe close minus long-period EMA of chart-timeframe close; its signal is a simple moving average of the oscillator.
Calculation: Direction vote for each paired timeframe = long when (the forming-bar EMA of close is above its preceding completed-bar value AND the forming-bar CCI of close is above zero AND above its preceding completed-bar value) OR (the forming-bar EMA is below its preceding completed-bar value AND the forming-bar CCI is below zero AND greater than or equal to its preceding completed-bar value); short when (the forming-bar EMA is below its preceding completed-bar value AND the forming-bar CCI is below zero AND below its preceding completed-bar value) OR (the forming-bar EMA is above its preceding completed-bar value AND the forming-bar CCI is above zero AND less than or equal to its preceding completed-bar value); otherwise neutral. Each CCI timeframe is equal to or higher than its paired EMA timeframe. The paired timeframes may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: Trend oscillator > its signal AND trend oscillator > its preceding chart-bar value OR preceding trend oscillator <= its preceding signal AND trend oscillator >= its signal AND trend oscillator > its preceding chart-bar value.
Short: Trend oscillator < its signal AND trend oscillator < its preceding chart-bar value OR preceding trend oscillator >= its preceding signal AND trend oscillator <= its signal AND trend oscillator < its preceding chart-bar value.
Long: Direction vote = long.
Short: Direction vote = short.
Long: Number of long direction votes > number of short direction votes AND number of neutral direction votes < a threshold.
Short: Number of short direction votes > number of long direction votes AND number of neutral direction votes < a threshold.
Long: Forming-bar close > forming-bar open on a timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Short: Forming-bar close < forming-bar open on a timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: Forming-bar close > preceding completed-bar close on a timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe AND the number of timeframes with forming-bar stochastic main > signal exceeds the number with main < signal AND the number of timeframes with forming-bar OsMA > preceding completed-bar OsMA exceeds the number with forming-bar OsMA < preceding completed-bar OsMA. The stochastic and OsMA use corresponding timeframes, each of which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Short: Forming-bar close < preceding completed-bar close on a timeframe that may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe AND the number of timeframes with forming-bar stochastic main < signal exceeds the number with main > signal AND the number of timeframes with forming-bar OsMA < preceding completed-bar OsMA exceeds the number with forming-bar OsMA > preceding completed-bar OsMA. The stochastic and OsMA use corresponding timeframes, each of which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
