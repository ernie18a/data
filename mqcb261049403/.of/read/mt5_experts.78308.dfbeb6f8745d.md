Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the most recent bar within the search lookback whose high/low is strictly higher/lower than the corresponding highs/lows of the specified numbers of older and newer bars. Use a fixed structure timeframe independent of the chart timeframe; it may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe. The newest confirmation bar must be completed.
Calculation: Pending entry = at each scan interval while no position is open, retain existing pending entries and add a buy stop at the confirmed swing high plus its configured percentage offset if price is strictly below the high by more than the clearance percentage; add a sell stop at the confirmed swing low minus its configured percentage offset if price is strictly above the low by more than the clearance percentage. A buy stop must be above current price and a sell stop below it. Do not add an entry if a same-side pending entry is within the configured duplicate distance or the same-side pending limit is reached. Cancel remaining pending entries after a position opens; a new swing does not cancel them.
Calculation: Long stop = initially entry price minus the stop-loss percentage of entry price. Once favorable movement reaches the break-even trigger, raise it to at least entry price plus the profit-lock percentage of entry price. While favorable movement is between the trailing trigger and maximum trailing threshold, inclusive, raise it to at least current price minus the trailing-distance percentage of current price. Never lower the stop.
Calculation: Short stop = initially entry price plus the stop-loss percentage of entry price. Once favorable movement reaches the break-even trigger, lower it to at most entry price minus the profit-lock percentage of entry price. While favorable movement is between the trailing trigger and maximum trailing threshold, inclusive, lower it to at most current price plus the trailing-distance percentage of current price. Never raise the stop.
Calculation: Long target = initially entry price plus the take-profit percentage of entry price. Once adverse movement reaches the salvage trigger, replace it with entry price minus the salvage-exit percentage of entry price and retain that target.
Calculation: Short target = initially entry price minus the take-profit percentage of entry price. Once adverse movement reaches the salvage trigger, replace it with entry price plus the salvage-exit percentage of entry price and retain that target.
Calculation: Favorable movement = (current price minus entry price) divided by entry price for a long position, and (entry price minus current price) divided by entry price for a short position; adverse movement is its negative.
Long: No position is open AND price reaches or exceeds a pending buy stop.
Short: No position is open AND price reaches or falls below a pending sell stop.
Exit: Long, price reaches or falls below the long stop OR reaches or exceeds the long target.
Exit: Short, price reaches or exceeds the short stop OR reaches or falls below the short target.
