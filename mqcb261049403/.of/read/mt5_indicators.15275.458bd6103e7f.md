Calculation: Oscillator = shorter-lookback moving average of a selected price series minus longer-lookback moving average of a selected price series; the averages may use different methods and price series. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar and preceding completed bars.
Calculation: Downward event = oscillator two bars ago > oscillator one bar ago AND oscillator one bar ago < oscillator now, OR oscillator one bar ago < 0 AND oscillator now > 0.
Calculation: Upward event = oscillator two bars ago < oscillator one bar ago AND oscillator one bar ago > oscillator now, OR oscillator one bar ago > 0 AND oscillator now < 0.
Long: chart-timeframe downward event.
Short: chart-timeframe upward event.
Long: most recent higher-timeframe upward event is more recent than the most recent higher-timeframe downward event, OR a higher-timeframe upward event has occurred AND no higher-timeframe downward event has occurred.
Short: most recent higher-timeframe downward event is more recent than the most recent higher-timeframe upward event, OR a higher-timeframe downward event has occurred AND no higher-timeframe upward event has occurred.
