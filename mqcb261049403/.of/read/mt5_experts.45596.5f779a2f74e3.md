Calculation: OsMA = MACD histogram (MACD line minus its signal line) computed from typical price (high+low+close)/3; MACD fast EMA period is shorter than slow EMA period; chart timeframe
Calculation: OsMA band upper/lower = SMA of OsMA over N bars plus/minus K times the standard deviation of OsMA over the same N bars (Bollinger Bands applied to the OsMA series, not to price); chart timeframe
Calculation: OsMA MA = simple moving average of the OsMA series; chart timeframe
Calculation: bar1 = last completed bar, bar2 = the completed bar before it; every OsMA, band and MA value is read at bar1 or bar2 and evaluated once per new bar (the bar still forming is not used)
Long: (OsMA[bar2] <= lower band[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] > lower band[bar1]) OR (Long was true on the previous bar AND NOT (OsMA[bar2] >= OsMA MA[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] < OsMA MA[bar1]) AND NOT Short cross condition (OsMA[bar2] >= upper band[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] < upper band[bar1])); open a long only when flat
Short: (OsMA[bar2] >= upper band[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] < upper band[bar1]) AND NOT the long cross condition, OR (Short was true on the previous bar AND NOT (OsMA[bar2] <= OsMA MA[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] > OsMA MA[bar1]) AND NOT the long cross condition (OsMA[bar2] <= lower band[bar2] AND OsMA[bar1] > lower band[bar1])); open a short only when flat
Exit: long, Long is false on the current bar (OsMA crossed below OsMA MA with no new lower-band cross-up, or the Short condition became true; if Short is true, reverse into short on the same bar) OR price <= stop level, where stop level = highest price since entry minus a fixed distance, and the initial stop is entry price minus that same distance (the stop only ratchets up)
Exit: short, Short is false on the current bar (OsMA crossed above OsMA MA with no new upper-band cross-down, or the Long condition became true; if Long is true, reverse into long on the same bar) OR price >= stop level, where stop level = lowest price since entry plus a fixed distance, and the initial stop is entry price plus that same distance (the stop only ratchets down)
