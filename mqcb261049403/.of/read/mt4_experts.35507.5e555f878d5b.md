Calculation: Quiet-range maximum = maximum of high minus low across the configured number of completed chart bars immediately preceding the breakout bar
Calculation: Upward impulse = breakout bar close minus breakout bar open
Calculation: Downward impulse = breakout bar open minus breakout bar close
Long: no open long position AND EMA of close with shorter lookback > EMA of close with longer lookback AND EMA of close with shorter lookback > EMA of close with longer lookback AND breakout bar open > longer-lookback EMA of close AND quiet-range maximum > minimum quiet-bar range AND upward impulse >= quiet-range maximum × impulse-strength parameter AND breakout bar is bullish AND breakout bar range is within configured minimum and maximum sizes AND breakout bar close >= breakout bar high − breakout bar range × configured upper-wick limit percentage AND breakout bar open >= breakout bar low + breakout bar range × configured lower-wick minimum percentage AND breakout bar low <= shorter-lookback EMA of close
Short: no open short position AND EMA of close with shorter lookback < EMA of close with longer lookback AND EMA of close with shorter lookback < EMA of close with longer lookback AND breakout bar open < longer-lookback EMA of close AND quiet-range maximum > minimum quiet-bar range AND downward impulse >= quiet-range maximum × impulse-strength parameter AND breakout bar is bearish AND breakout bar range is within configured minimum and maximum sizes AND breakout bar open <= breakout bar high − breakout bar range × configured upper-wick minimum percentage AND breakout bar close <= breakout bar low + breakout bar range × configured lower-wick limit percentage AND breakout bar high >= shorter-lookback EMA of close
Exit: long, price <= entry price − configured stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: short, price >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price − configured take-profit distance
