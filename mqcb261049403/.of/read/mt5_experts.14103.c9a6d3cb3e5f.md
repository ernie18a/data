Calculation: Smoothed open and smoothed close = fractal adaptive moving averages of open and close, respectively, using the same period.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed close > smoothed open.
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed close < smoothed open.
Long: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, smoothed close > smoothed open AND on the preceding higher-timeframe bar, smoothed close <= smoothed open.
Short: On the last completed higher-timeframe bar, smoothed close < smoothed open AND on the preceding higher-timeframe bar, smoothed close >= smoothed open.
Exit: long, last completed higher-timeframe bar's smoothed close < smoothed open OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, last completed higher-timeframe bar's smoothed close > smoothed open OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
