Calculation: Timing oscillator = Over the latest 120 minute bars, process typical price (high+low+close)/3 from oldest to newest. Let d be the change in typical price from the preceding bar. Apply three successive smoothing stages to d: at each stage, a=2/3·a_prev+1/3·input; b=1/3·a+2/3·b_prev; output=1.5·a−0.5·b. Apply the same three stages to |d|. Set oscillator=clamp(50·(final smoothed d/final smoothed |d|+1), 0, 100); use 50 when the denominator is too small or the initial six-bar warm-up is incomplete. For decisions, use its values on the forming minute bar and the two preceding completed minute bars.
Calculation: Volatility measure = (|previous high−current low|+|current high−previous low|+|previous close−current close|) / point size × current minute tick volume / elapsed seconds in the current minute; use the forming minute bar and preceding completed minute bar.
Long: Timing oscillator on the preceding completed bar ≤ its value two completed bars ago AND timing oscillator on the forming bar > its value on the preceding completed bar AND volatility measure > its threshold AND 10-period SMA standard deviation of minute-bar closes, including the forming bar, ≤ its limit AND datetime is outside Friday at hour 23 and the year-end dates 358, 359, 365, and 366 at hour 16 or later
Short: Timing oscillator on the preceding completed bar ≥ its value two completed bars ago AND timing oscillator on the forming bar < its value on the preceding completed bar AND volatility measure > its threshold AND 10-period SMA standard deviation of minute-bar closes, including the forming bar, ≤ its limit AND datetime is outside Friday at hour 23 and the year-end dates 358, 359, 365, and 366 at hour 16 or later
Exit: Long, price ≤ active stop-loss
Exit: Short, price ≥ active stop-loss
