Calculation: Timing = T3-style oscillator on typical price. P=(high+low+close)/3, d=P-P[prev bar], computed over a window of recent chart-timeframe bars (bar 0 = current bar). Smooth(x) = apply 3 cascaded stages, each stage: e1=2/3*e1+1/3*x; e2=1/3*e1+2/3*e2; out=1.5*e1-0.5*e2. dmov=Smooth(d); amov=Smooth(|d|). Timing=clip(50*(dmov/amov+1),0,100), or 50 if amov is about 0. T0, T1, T2 = Timing at the current bar, previous bar, two bars back.
Calculation: Volatility = amplitude*mass. amplitude=|high[1]-low[0]|+|high[0]-low[1]|+|close[1]-close[0]| (bar 0 = current bar, bar 1 = previous bar). mass=volume of current bar / seconds elapsed in current bar.
Calculation: TrailStop = for each open order, initial stop = entry -/+ 2*D (long: below entry, short: above entry). Once the order's profit (long: price-entry; short: entry-price) >= S (S > D), stop = price -/+ D (long: price-D; short: price+D). The stop only ratchets in the favorable direction.
Long: T1<=T2 AND T0>T1 AND Volatility>threshold AND StdDev(close, short lookback)<=threshold AND (no position is open OR (long is open AND profit of the open long >= S AND profit of the open long >= profit at the previous add + step, where step is a fixed distance))
Short: T1>=T2 AND T0<T1 AND Volatility>threshold AND StdDev(close, short lookback)<=threshold AND (no position is open OR (short is open AND profit of the open short >= S AND profit of the open short >= profit at the previous add + step, where step is a fixed distance))
Exit: long, price<=TrailStop of that order
Exit: short, price>=TrailStop of that order
