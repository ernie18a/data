Calculation: Signal bar = the bar still forming on the configured timeframe, which may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; evaluate its open, high, and low at the current chart-bar close.
Calculation: Eligible signal = the first qualifying entry condition observed in the current signal bar; subsequent qualifying conditions in that signal bar are ignored.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initially entry price minus stop-loss distance if enabled, otherwise unset; when trailing is enabled, close minus entry price > trailing distance plus trailing step, and the stop is unset or below close minus trailing distance minus trailing step, set the stop to close minus trailing distance.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initially entry price plus stop-loss distance if enabled, otherwise unset; when trailing is enabled, entry price minus close > trailing distance plus trailing step, and the stop is unset or above close plus trailing distance plus trailing step, set the stop to close plus trailing distance.
Long: Eligible signal AND ((reverse is disabled AND close < signal-bar open AND signal-bar high minus close > rollback distance) OR (reverse is enabled AND close > signal-bar open AND close minus signal-bar low > rollback distance)).
Short: Eligible signal AND ((reverse is disabled AND close > signal-bar open AND close minus signal-bar low > rollback distance) OR (reverse is enabled AND close < signal-bar open AND signal-bar high minus close > rollback distance)).
Exit: long, (long trailing stop is set AND close <= long trailing stop) OR (take profit is enabled AND close >= entry price plus take-profit distance).
Exit: short, (short trailing stop is set AND close >= short trailing stop) OR (take profit is enabled AND close <= entry price minus take-profit distance).
Calculation: The original decisions use intrabar bid and ask ticks, which cannot be recovered from the supplied bar fields; close is a bar-based approximation, so exact reproduction is not possible from those fields alone.
