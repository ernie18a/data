Calculation: Envelope = a configurable moving average of close multiplied by (1 + deviation) for the upper boundary and (1 - deviation) for the lower boundary. Use the last completed signal-timeframe bar; the signal timeframe may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe.
Calculation: Long excursion = lower envelope boundary - low of the completed signal bar.
Calculation: Short excursion = high of the completed signal bar - upper envelope boundary.
Long: No open long position AND completed signal-bar low <= lower envelope boundary AND (completed signal-bar high < upper envelope boundary OR (absolute difference between long and short excursions < tie tolerance AND completed signal-bar close >= open) OR (absolute difference between long and short excursions >= tie tolerance AND long excursion > short excursion)).
Short: No open short position AND completed signal-bar high >= upper envelope boundary AND (completed signal-bar low > lower envelope boundary OR (absolute difference between long and short excursions < tie tolerance AND completed signal-bar close < open) OR (absolute difference between long and short excursions >= tie tolerance AND short excursion > long excursion)).
