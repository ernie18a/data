Calculation: Confirmed swing high or low = a closed bar whose high is at least as high as, or whose low is at least as low as, every bar in an equal lookback on each side. Track the most recently confirmed swing of each kind as unswept; replace it whenever a newer swing of that kind is confirmed.
Calculation: Pending sweep = while flat and with no pending sweep, a closed bar breaches the most recent unswept swing by more than a multiple of that bar's ATR and closes back across the swing. Mark the swing as swept. Check the swing high first; if it is swept, do not check the swing low on that bar, even if the trend filter rejects the sweep. Create a pending bearish or bullish sweep only if the optional session filter allows the current time and the optional trend filter allows that direction. The trend filter compares the close with the EMA of the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Retain the sweep bar's high, low, open and close. A pending sweep expires after the permitted number of subsequent closed bars; the first close through its body ends the pending sweep whether or not the entry filters pass. Update swings only while flat.
Calculation: Stop distance = absolute difference between entry price and the sweep extreme adjusted beyond the extreme by a multiple of the confirmation bar's ATR. Fix this distance at entry.
Long: Pending bullish sweep within the confirmation window AND confirmation close > maximum of sweep open and close AND confirmation high - low >= a multiple of confirmation ATR AND stop distance >= a multiple of confirmation ATR.
Short: Pending bearish sweep within the confirmation window AND confirmation close < minimum of sweep open and close AND confirmation high - low >= a multiple of confirmation ATR AND stop distance >= a multiple of confirmation ATR.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + a multiple of stop distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - a multiple of stop distance.
