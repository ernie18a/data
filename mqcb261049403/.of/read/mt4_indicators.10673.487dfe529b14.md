Calculation: Multitimeframe power = On each of an ordered set of lower, chart, and higher timeframes, evaluate the bar containing the chart bar's timestamp; higher-timeframe bars are still forming, and lower-timeframe bars are the bars containing that timestamp. Count bullish and bearish votes from the slopes of short-, medium-, and long-lookback close EMAs; close-based CCI versus zero; MACD main versus signal; +DI versus -DI; Bulls Power and Bears Power versus zero; stochastic main versus signal using close/close prices and SMA smoothing; close RSI versus its midpoint; SMA-smoothed close Force Index versus zero; close Momentum versus its neutral level; and DeMarker versus its previous value. Rising EMA, positive CCI, MACD main above signal, +DI above -DI, positive Bulls or Bears Power, stochastic main at or above signal, RSI at or above its midpoint, Force Index at or above zero, Momentum at or above its neutral level, and nondecreasing DeMarker each contribute one bullish vote; the opposite comparisons contribute one bearish vote. Equality contributes no vote for EMA, CCI, MACD, DI, Bulls Power, or Bears Power. Bull power is 100 times bullish votes divided by total votes; bear power is 100 minus bull power rounded to a whole percent.
Calculation: Oscillator analysis = Count bullish and bearish votes from chart-timeframe close RSI and high/low stochastic: RSI direction versus a fixed earlier bar, short-lookback RSI versus longer-lookback RSI now and at that earlier bar, RSI level, stochastic direction versus that earlier bar, stochastic main versus signal now and on the previous bar, and stochastic level. Rising or falling values vote bullish or bearish respectively; a short RSI crossing above or below the longer RSI between the earlier and current bars votes bullish or bearish respectively. RSI or stochastic above the midpoint through the upper threshold votes bullish; at or below the midpoint down to the lower threshold votes bearish; outside those bounds contributes no level vote. Either strict stochastic main/signal cross contributes a bullish vote. Equal directional values or absent crosses contribute no vote. Up analysis is 100 times bullish votes divided by all votes; down analysis is 100 minus up analysis. For displayed analysis comparisons, round each to a whole percent.
Calculation: Prediction = In order, select bullish trend when unrounded up analysis is at least the analysis threshold and bull power exceeds the trend-power threshold; otherwise bearish trend when unrounded down analysis is at least that threshold and bear power exceeds the trend-power threshold; otherwise bullish range when unrounded up analysis is at least that threshold and bull power is below the range-power threshold; otherwise bearish range when unrounded down analysis is at least that threshold and bear power is below the range-power threshold. The range-power threshold is below the trend-power threshold.
Calculation: Session zones = At each configured session-day boundary, treating weekend days as the preceding trading weekday, take the latest pivot calculated at a boundary of a higher master timeframe: pivot is (highest high + lowest low + twice the last close) / 4 over the preceding master period. Sample ATRs from a shorter and a longer aggregation timeframe at the start of that preceding master period, using the bar containing that timestamp, and hold the resulting levels until the next session boundary. The upper and lower entry zones are pivot plus and minus the shorter-timeframe ATR; the upper and lower first targets are pivot plus and minus the sum of both ATRs. Each ATR aggregation timeframe may be lower or higher than the chart timeframe; the sampled bar is the bar still forming at its timestamp.
