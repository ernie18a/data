Calculation: Bounce strength index = Simple moving average of (upward bounce strength + downward bounce strength). For each bar in the slowing lookback, find the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback ending at that bar; range is their difference, floored at a minimum price increment. Weight is 1 without volume weighting; otherwise it is the bar's volume divided by the maximum volume over that range lookback, or 1 if that maximum is zero. Include (close − low) × upward ratio × weight in the upward sum when close ≥ previous close − a fraction of (high − low); upward ratio is 1 when low equals the range low, otherwise (range high − low) / range. Include −(high − close) × downward ratio × weight in the downward sum when close ≤ previous close + that fraction of (high − low); downward ratio is 1 when high equals the range high, otherwise (high − range low) / range. Each bounce strength is its respective sum divided by the sum of the ranges over the slowing lookback.
Long: Bounce strength index > previous bar's bounce strength index
Short: Bounce strength index < previous bar's bounce strength index
