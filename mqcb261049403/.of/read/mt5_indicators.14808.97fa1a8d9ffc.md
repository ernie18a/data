Calculation: Bounce Strength Index = the average over a short averaging lookback of buying strength plus selling strength. For each bar in a longer range lookback, range = highest high minus lowest low; use 1 as the range-position ratio when the relevant low or high equals the range low or high. Over a shorter smoothing lookback, buying strength is 100 times the sum of (close − low) × (range high − low) / range when close ≥ prior close − 0.2 × (high − low), divided by the sum of ranges. Selling strength is 100 times the negative sum of (high − close) × (high − range low) / range when close ≤ prior close + 0.2 × (high − low), divided by the same sum of ranges. Multiply each contribution by 1, volume / its maximum over the range lookback, or tick volume / its maximum over the range lookback, according to the volume-weighting setting.
Long: Bounce Strength Index > its value on the previous chart bar
Short: Bounce Strength Index < its value on the previous chart bar
