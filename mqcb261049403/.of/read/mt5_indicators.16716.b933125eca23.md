Calculation: Selected price = a configurable OHLC price, median price, typical price, weighted close, OHLC average, body midpoint, or corresponding Heikin Ashi price; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Apply these formulas to Heikin Ashi OHLC when selected.
Calculation: Smoothed relative volatility index = smooth 100 × U/(U + D), or smooth 50 when U + D = 0. U and D are moving averages of upward and downward volatility over the same lookback used for standard deviation. Upward volatility is the selected price's rolling standard deviation when price > previous price and 0 otherwise; downward volatility is that deviation when price < previous price and 0 otherwise. Use configurable sample or population standard deviation and configurable moving-average methods and smoothing lookback. Calculate on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Upper and lower levels = configurable fixed levels when the level lookback is at most one bar; otherwise, rolling minimum of the index plus a configurable upper or lower percentage of its rolling range, including the current bar. The middle level is the midpoint of the fixed levels or, for floating levels, the midpoint of the rolling minimum and maximum.
Long: slope mode AND index > previous index OR middle mode AND index > middle level OR outer-level mode AND index > upper level
Short: slope mode AND index < previous index OR middle mode AND index < middle level OR outer-level mode AND index < lower level
