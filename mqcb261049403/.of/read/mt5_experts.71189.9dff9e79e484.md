Calculation: Breakout high = highest high over the lookback ending immediately before the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Breakout low = lowest low over the lookback ending immediately before the last completed chart bar.
Long: On a new chart bar, last completed chart bar's short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND their separation >= ATR × trend-strength threshold AND close > both EMAs AND close > breakout high − ATR × breakout buffer AND preceding close <= breakout high AND RSI >= long lower bound AND RSI <= long upper bound AND close > preceding close AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe bar's short-period EMA > long-period EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current datetime is a weekday within the session AND (Friday filter disabled OR current datetime is before the Friday cutoff)) AND (news filter disabled OR current datetime is outside every configured news exclusion interval).
Short: On a new chart bar, last completed chart bar's short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND their separation >= ATR × trend-strength threshold AND close < both EMAs AND close < breakout low + ATR × breakout buffer AND preceding close >= breakout low AND RSI >= short lower bound AND RSI <= short upper bound AND close < preceding close AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe bar's short-period EMA < long-period EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current datetime is a weekday within the session AND (Friday filter disabled OR current datetime is before the Friday cutoff)) AND (news filter disabled OR current datetime is outside every configured news exclusion interval).
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
Exit: long, partial close enabled AND high >= entry price + partial-profit distance.
Exit: short, partial close enabled AND low <= entry price − partial-profit distance.
