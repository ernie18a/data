Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the stop-loss distance when the stop loss is enabled; otherwise no stop. If trailing is enabled, at each working-bar open, update the stop to open minus the trailing distance when open minus entry price is at least trailing distance plus trailing step AND open minus trailing distance is at least entry price plus trailing activation distance AND the existing stop is absent or less than open minus trailing distance minus trailing step.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the stop-loss distance when the stop loss is enabled; otherwise no stop. If trailing is enabled, at each working-bar open, update the stop to open plus the trailing distance when entry price minus open is at least trailing distance plus trailing step AND open plus trailing distance is at most entry price minus trailing activation distance AND the existing stop is absent or greater than open plus trailing distance plus trailing step.
Long: At the start of a working bar, the last completed working-bar DeMarker is below the lower threshold AND reverse mode is off AND time control is off or the current time is in the configured interval AND no short position exists or the aggregate net profit of short positions is positive AND the one-position restriction is off or no position exists.
Long: At the start of a working bar, the last completed working-bar DeMarker is above the upper threshold AND reverse mode is on AND time control is off or the current time is in the configured interval AND no short position exists or the aggregate net profit of short positions is positive AND the one-position restriction is off or no position exists.
Short: At the start of a working bar, the last completed working-bar DeMarker is above the upper threshold AND reverse mode is off AND time control is off or the current time is in the configured interval AND no long position exists or the aggregate net profit of long positions is positive AND the one-position restriction is off or no position exists.
Short: At the start of a working bar, the last completed working-bar DeMarker is below the lower threshold AND reverse mode is on AND time control is off or the current time is in the configured interval AND no long position exists or the aggregate net profit of long positions is positive AND the one-position restriction is off or no position exists.
Exit: long, low is at or below the long stop when a stop exists OR high is at or above entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high is at or above the short stop when a stop exists OR low is at or below entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
