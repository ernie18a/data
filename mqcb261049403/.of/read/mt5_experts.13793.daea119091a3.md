Calculation: Hull trend oscillator = smooth D − moving average of D with a separate smoothing method and lookback, where D = 2 × moving average of the selected OHLC-derived price over half the main lookback, rounded down − moving average of that price over the main lookback; the moving average of D uses the same method as the price averages and a lookback equal to the square root of the main lookback, rounded down. Calculate on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar and preceding completed bars.
Long: oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator on the bar before that >= oscillator on the preceding bar
Short: oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < oscillator on the preceding bar AND oscillator on the bar before that <= oscillator on the preceding bar
Exit: long, oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < oscillator on the preceding bar OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, oscillator on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > oscillator on the preceding bar OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance
