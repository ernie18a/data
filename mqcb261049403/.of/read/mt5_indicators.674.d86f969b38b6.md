Calculation: Normalized deviation = 100 × smooth(price − price moving average) / smooth(abs(price − price moving average)); use the same deviation smoothing method and parameters for numerator and denominator, and return zero when the smoothed numerator is zero. Price and both smoothing methods are selectable.
Calculation: Step line = for each step size, maintain upper and lower bands and a trend state. Initialize the previous upper band to a very large negative value, the previous lower band to a very large positive value, and the trend to zero. On each bar, set the trend to up if normalized deviation > previous upper band, then to down if normalized deviation < previous lower band; otherwise retain it. Set the new upper band to normalized deviation + 2 × step size and the new lower band to normalized deviation − 2 × step size. In an uptrend, replace the new lower band with the maximum of it and the previous lower band; in a downtrend, replace the new upper band with the minimum of it and the previous upper band. The step line is the new lower band + step size in an uptrend, or the new upper band − step size in a downtrend. Calculate a fast line with a smaller step size and a slow line with a larger step size.
Long: Fast step line >= slow step line, calculated on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Short: Fast step line < slow step line, calculated on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
