Long: At the opening of a new configured-timeframe bar, the forming bar's Parabolic SAR < its low; place a buy limit at that SAR.
Short: At the opening of a new configured-timeframe bar, the forming bar's Parabolic SAR > its high; place a sell limit at that SAR.
