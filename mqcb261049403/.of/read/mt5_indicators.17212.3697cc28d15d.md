Calculation: Selected price = a selectable OHLC price, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price. Trend-biased price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open and (low + close) / 2 otherwise; extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Sentiment oscillator = a selectable moving average of the sign of the change in a separately smoothed selected price; the sign is +1, -1, or 0. Each smoothing independently selects simple, exponential, smoothed, linear weighted, or triple exponential moving average and its own lookback. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Oscillator levels = either floating levels or quantile levels over the oscillator lookback, including the current bar. Floating levels equal the rolling minimum plus a selectable fraction of the rolling range; their middle level is the average of the upper and lower levels. Quantile levels are linearly interpolated between sorted observations at index (lookback - 1) × quantile; their middle level uses the average of the upper and lower quantiles. Pad unavailable initial observations with zeros for quantiles. The upper fraction or quantile is greater than the lower one.
Long: Selected level mode AND sentiment oscillator > upper level OR selected middle mode AND sentiment oscillator > middle level OR selected slope mode AND sentiment oscillator > previous oscillator.
Short: Selected level mode AND sentiment oscillator < lower level OR selected middle mode AND sentiment oscillator < middle level OR selected slope mode AND sentiment oscillator < previous oscillator.
