Calculation: Signal bars = bars of the configured signal timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe or equal to it; evaluate at the first update of each new signal bar, using its still-forming close and the last completed signal bar where specified.
Calculation: Reference range = high and low of the most recent signal bar whose opening hour equals the configured level-setting hour, found by scanning backward from the still-forming signal bar for at most the number of chart bars in a day; retain the previous range if no such bar is found.
Calculation: Scan bars = the still-forming signal bar through the reference-range bar inclusive, subject to the same scan limit; mean close includes the still-forming close. The recheck close is the close of the first bar found in this scan whose opening hour equals the configured recheck hour.
Long: Signal-bar opening hour = configured entry hour AND ((contrarian mode AND last completed signal-bar close < reference low − minimum distance AND last completed signal-bar close > reference low − maximum distance AND (all-bars check disabled OR every scan-bar close >= reference low) AND (recheck disabled OR no recheck bar found OR mean close <= recheck close OR recheck close >= last completed signal-bar close)) OR (trend-following mode AND last completed signal-bar close > reference high + minimum distance AND last completed signal-bar close < reference high + maximum distance AND (all-bars check disabled OR every scan-bar close <= reference high) AND (recheck disabled OR no recheck bar found OR mean close >= recheck close OR recheck close <= last completed signal-bar close))).
Short: Signal-bar opening hour = configured entry hour AND ((contrarian mode AND last completed signal-bar close > reference high + minimum distance AND last completed signal-bar close < reference high + maximum distance AND (all-bars check disabled OR every scan-bar close <= reference high) AND (recheck disabled OR no recheck bar found OR mean close >= recheck close OR recheck close <= last completed signal-bar close)) OR (trend-following mode AND last completed signal-bar close < reference low − minimum distance AND last completed signal-bar close > reference low − maximum distance AND (all-bars check disabled OR every scan-bar close >= reference low) AND (recheck disabled OR no recheck bar found OR mean close <= recheck close OR recheck close >= last completed signal-bar close))).
Exit: long, signal exit enabled AND ((contrarian mode AND last completed signal-bar close > reference low + minimum distance) OR (trend-following mode AND last completed signal-bar close < reference high − minimum distance)).
Exit: short, signal exit enabled AND ((contrarian mode AND last completed signal-bar close < reference high − minimum distance) OR (trend-following mode AND last completed signal-bar close > reference low + minimum distance)).
Exit: long, timed exit enabled AND current hour >= configured exit hour.
Exit: short, timed exit enabled AND current hour >= configured exit hour.
Exit: long, take profit enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, take profit enabled AND low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
