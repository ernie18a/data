Calculation: Volume-weighted smoothed histogram = On indicator-timeframe bars, calculate Bulls Power (high minus EMA of close) plus Bears Power (low minus EMA of close). Recursively smooth this sum into L0, L1, L2, and L3: L0 = (1 − gamma) × sum + gamma × previous L0; each next Li = −gamma × previous Li−1 + previous Li−1 + gamma × previous Li. Let CU be the sum of max(Li − Li+1, 0) for i = 0..2, and CD the sum of max(Li+1 − Li, 0) for i = 0..2. Transform to (CU / (CU + CD) × 100 − 50), using 0 when CU + CD = 0; multiply by the configured volume field, then apply the configured moving average. The indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use completed bars only.
Long: The histogram direction changes from falling to rising between the last two completed indicator-timeframe bars.
Short: The histogram direction changes from rising to falling between the last two completed indicator-timeframe bars.
Exit: Long, the histogram direction changes from rising to falling between the last two completed indicator-timeframe bars.
Exit: Short, the histogram direction changes from falling to rising between the last two completed indicator-timeframe bars.
