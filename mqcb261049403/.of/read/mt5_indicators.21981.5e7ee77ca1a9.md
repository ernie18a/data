Calculation: MA trend = Initially neutral. On each bar, change to positive if the shorter-period moving average is above the longer-period moving average, its previous-bar value was strictly below the longer-period moving average, and the prior trend is neutral or negative. Change to negative if the shorter-period moving average is below the longer-period moving average, its previous-bar value was strictly above the longer-period moving average, and the prior trend is neutral or positive. Otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe displayed trend = Calculate MA trend on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar; carry that value forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe MA trend > neutral
Short: Chart-timeframe MA trend < neutral
Long: Higher-timeframe displayed trend > neutral
Short: Higher-timeframe displayed trend < neutral
Long: Chart-timeframe MA trend changes from neutral or negative to positive
Short: Chart-timeframe MA trend changes from neutral or positive to negative
Long: Chart bar is the first bar of a higher-timeframe bar AND higher-timeframe MA trend changes from neutral or negative to positive using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar
Short: Chart bar is the first bar of a higher-timeframe bar AND higher-timeframe MA trend changes from neutral or positive to negative using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar
