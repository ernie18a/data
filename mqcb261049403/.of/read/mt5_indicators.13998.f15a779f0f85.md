Calculation: Laguerre directional oscillator = For each of the positive and negative directional indicators from ADX, independently initialize four stages to zero and update them each bar as L0 = (1 − gamma) × DI + gamma × previous L0; L1 = −gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1; L2 = −gamma × L1 + previous L1 + gamma × previous L2; L3 = −gamma × L2 + previous L2 + gamma × previous L3. Sum the positive differences L0−L1, L1−L2, and L2−L3 as CU and the absolute values of their negative differences as CD. The directional oscillator is CU / (CU + CD) when the denominator is nonzero; otherwise retain its preceding computed value, initialized to zero. The histogram is the positive directional oscillator minus the negative directional oscillator. Calculate it on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; compare with the preceding bar of that same timeframe.
Long: histogram > 0 AND histogram != previous histogram
Short: histogram < 0 AND histogram != previous histogram
