Calculation: Coppock oscillator = smooth the sum of the applied-price rate of change over a longer lookback and its rate of change over a shorter lookback, using the selected smoothing method and lookback
Long: The oscillator on the previous completed bar is lower than on the latest completed bar AND the oscillator two bars earlier is higher than on the previous completed bar
Short: The oscillator on the previous completed bar is higher than on the latest completed bar AND the oscillator two bars earlier is lower than on the previous completed bar
Exit: Long, the oscillator on the previous completed bar is higher than on the latest completed bar
Exit: Short, the oscillator on the previous completed bar is lower than on the latest completed bar
