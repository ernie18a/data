Calculation: PA oscillator = the change from the previous bar in the spread between a shorter-lookback and a longer-lookback smoothed applied-price series, calculated on the indicator timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe) using completed bars.
Long: PA oscillator on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > 0 AND PA oscillator on the prior completed bar <= 0
Short: PA oscillator on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar < 0 AND PA oscillator on the prior completed bar >= 0
Exit: long, PA oscillator on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar < 0
Exit: short, PA oscillator on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar > 0
