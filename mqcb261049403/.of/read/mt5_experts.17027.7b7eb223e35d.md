Calculation: Fractal directional lines = On the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar as the selected signal bar, calculate each bar as follows. For a configurable applied OHLC price and lookback n, let maximum = max(0, highest price in the last n bars), minimum = min(9999999999, lowest price in the last n bars), and normalized price x = (price - minimum)/(maximum - minimum). If maximum > minimum, let length be the sum over adjacent pairs in those n bars of sqrt((x[i] - x[i-1])^2 + 1/n^2); otherwise length = 0. Let dimension = 1 + ln(2*length)/ln(2*(n-1)) when length > 0, otherwise 0. Let period p = round(normal speed/(2*(2-dimension))). For each bar, positive movement is max(high - previous high, 0), negative movement is max(previous low - low, 0); retain only the larger movement and set both to zero when equal. Divide each retained movement by true range and multiply by 100, using zero when true range is zero. For each directional line at the selected bar, seed an EMA with that directional value p bars earlier, then update it over the following p values through the selected bar with smoothing factor 2/(p+1). Use the period calculated separately for each bar when calculating that bar's lines.
Long: Positive directional line on the selected signal bar > negative directional line on the selected signal bar AND positive directional line on the preceding bar <= negative directional line on the preceding bar
Short: Positive directional line on the selected signal bar < negative directional line on the selected signal bar AND positive directional line on the preceding bar >= negative directional line on the preceding bar
Long: Positive directional line on the selected signal bar > negative directional line on the selected signal bar
Short: Positive directional line on the selected signal bar < negative directional line on the selected signal bar
Exit: long, positive directional line on the selected signal bar < negative directional line on the selected signal bar
Exit: short, positive directional line on the selected signal bar > negative directional line on the selected signal bar
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
