Calculation: MACD main = shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close; MACD signal = SMA of the MACD main.
Long: On the last completed chart bar, shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND on the chart bar offset from the second completed bar by the moving-average plot shift, shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period linear weighted moving average of typical price AND low of the second completed chart bar < high of the last completed chart bar AND absolute difference between the current chart-bar open and close of the second completed chart bar > a price-distance threshold AND chart-timeframe MACD main on the last completed bar != 0 AND chart-timeframe MACD main on the last completed bar > its signal AND MACD main on the last completed bar of an independently selected timeframe, which can be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe, != 0 AND that MACD main > its signal.
