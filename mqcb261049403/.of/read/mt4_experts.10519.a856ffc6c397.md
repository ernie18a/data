Calculation: Mean candle body = average of absolute close−open over completed bars of the decision timeframe from the start of the previous daily bar through the last completed decision-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Mean candle range = average of high−low over the same bars.
Long: No open position for this strategy AND no entry already made on this decision-timeframe bar AND within the configured entry window AND current decision-timeframe high − open ≤ configured opposite-move allowance AND decision-timeframe open − long entry price is between the configured minimum distance and twice that distance.
Short: No open position for this strategy AND no entry already made on this decision-timeframe bar AND within the configured entry window AND decision-timeframe open − current decision-timeframe low ≤ configured opposite-move allowance AND short entry price − decision-timeframe open is between the configured minimum distance and twice that distance.
Exit: long, current exit price ≥ the open of the decision-timeframe bar in which the position was opened OR the configured position lifetime has elapsed OR (the current price has crossed the trailing stop AND current profit exceeds the configured minimum profit AND trailing is enabled) OR (entry price − current exit price > 2 × max(mean candle range, configured minimum distance) AND the position is still in its entry bar) OR the configured stop-loss or take-profit price has been reached.
Exit: short, current exit price ≤ the open of the decision-timeframe bar in which the position was opened OR the configured position lifetime has elapsed OR (the current price has crossed the trailing stop AND current profit exceeds the configured minimum profit AND trailing is enabled) OR (current exit price − entry price > 2 × max(mean candle range, configured minimum distance) AND the position is still in its entry bar) OR the configured stop-loss or take-profit price has been reached.
