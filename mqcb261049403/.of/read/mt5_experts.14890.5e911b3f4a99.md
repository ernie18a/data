Calculation: Bull signal = on the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the last completed bar’s high is greater than the high N bars earlier
Calculation: Bear signal = on the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), the last completed bar’s low is lower than the low N bars earlier
Long: Bull signal AND NOT Bear signal on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar AND Bear signal on the immediately preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar
Short: Bear signal AND NOT Bull signal on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar AND Bull signal on the immediately preceding completed indicator-timeframe bar
Exit: Long, Bear signal AND NOT Bull signal on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar
Exit: Short, Bull signal AND NOT Bear signal on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar
