Calculation: JBrainTrend1Stop transition = on the configured indicator timeframe (above, below, or equal to the chart timeframe), using completed bars, compare the absolute difference between rounded JMA(close) on the latest bar and two bars earlier with the short-period ATR divided by a factor; if it exceeds that level, signal bearish when stochastic is below its lower threshold and the prior trend is not bearish, or bullish when stochastic is above its upper threshold and the prior trend is not bullish. Use a longer-period ATR than the short-period ATR for the stop lines: bearish starts at JMA(high) plus a fraction of the longer ATR and trails downward; bullish starts at JMA(low) minus a fraction of the longer ATR and trails upward.
Long: (JBrainTrend1Stop bullish transition AND a bearish stop line was present on the preceding bar AND no position is open) OR (position side is long AND add-on count is below its limit AND close minus the last entry price exceeds the add-on distance)
Short: (JBrainTrend1Stop bearish transition AND a bullish stop line was present on the preceding bar AND no position is open) OR (position side is short AND add-on count is below its limit AND the last entry price minus close exceeds the add-on distance)
Exit: long, JBrainTrend1Stop bearish transition
Exit: short, JBrainTrend1Stop bullish transition
