Calculation: Input price = a selected OHLC price, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, body midpoint, trend-biased price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price; trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise; extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Accumulated RSI = sum of the current and preceding RSI values over an accumulation lookback. Apply the selected moving average to input price before calculating the selected RSI variant. Rapid RSI is 100 × sum of gains / (sum of gains + sum of losses), or 50 if the denominator is zero. Slow RSI is Wilder smoothing of rapid RSI, initialized at zero until its lookback is available. Harris RSI is 100 − 100 / (1 + average gain / average loss), where gains and losses are averaged separately over their counts, including zero changes in the loss count; use a gain/loss ratio of 1 if average loss is zero. Cutler RSI is 100 − 100 / (1 + sum of gains / sum of losses), or 50 if sum of losses is zero. Regular RSI and RSX are also selectable.
Calculation: Floating levels = over a level lookback including the current accumulated RSI, let minimum and maximum be the lowest and highest accumulated RSI; upper = minimum + (maximum − minimum) × upper fraction; lower = minimum + (upper − minimum) × lower fraction; middle = (upper + lower)/2. Alternatively, quantile levels are linearly interpolated quantiles of accumulated RSI over the same lookback, at the upper fraction, lower fraction, and their average for the upper, lower, and middle levels respectively. If the level lookback is nonpositive, all levels equal the current accumulated RSI.
Calculation: When a higher timeframe is selected, calculate accumulated RSI and levels using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar and its preceding higher-timeframe bars; otherwise use chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Accumulated RSI > upper level when outer-level mode is selected OR accumulated RSI > middle level when middle-level mode is selected OR accumulated RSI > previous accumulated RSI when slope mode is selected OR accumulated RSI = previous accumulated RSI AND the preceding slope-mode state is long when slope mode is selected.
Short: Accumulated RSI < lower level when outer-level mode is selected OR accumulated RSI < middle level when middle-level mode is selected OR accumulated RSI < previous accumulated RSI when slope mode is selected OR accumulated RSI = previous accumulated RSI AND the preceding slope-mode state is short when slope mode is selected.
