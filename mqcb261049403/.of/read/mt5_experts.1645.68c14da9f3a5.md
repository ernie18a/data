Calculation: Smoothed RSI = a selected moving average of close-based RSI.
Calculation: RSI trailing line = let distance be a selected multiplier times a moving average of a moving average of the absolute one-bar change in smoothed RSI; both distance averages use the same method as smoothed RSI and the same lookback, equal to twice the RSI lookback minus one. Starting from zero, update the trailing line each bar: if smoothed RSI is below the previous trailing line, use smoothed RSI plus distance, capped at the previous trailing line when the previous smoothed RSI was also below it; if smoothed RSI is above the previous trailing line, use smoothed RSI minus distance, floored at the previous trailing line when the previous smoothed RSI was also above it; otherwise retain the previous trailing line.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed RSI > chart-timeframe RSI trailing line.
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed RSI < chart-timeframe RSI trailing line.
Long: Chart-timeframe smoothed RSI crosses above the RSI midpoint after crossing above the RSI trailing line.
Short: Chart-timeframe smoothed RSI crosses below the RSI midpoint after crossing below the RSI trailing line.
Long: Time of day = opening time AND smoothed RSI > RSI trailing line on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting.
Short: Time of day = opening time AND smoothed RSI < RSI trailing line on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting.
Exit: long, smoothed RSI < RSI trailing line on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting OR time of day >= closing time OR hour of day < opening hour OR low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, smoothed RSI > RSI trailing line on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar or the still-forming bar according to the signal-bar setting OR time of day >= closing time OR hour of day < opening hour OR high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
