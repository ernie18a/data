Calculation: Weekly pivot levels = From the previous completed weekly bar’s high H, low L, and close C: P=(H+L+C)/3; R1=2P−L; S1=2P−H; R0.5=(P+R1)/2; S0.5=(P+S1)/2; R2=P+(H−L); S2=P−(H−L); R1.5=(R1+R2)/2; S1.5=(S1+S2)/2; R3=2P+(H−2L); S3=2P−(2H−L); R2.5=(R2+R3)/2; S2.5=(S2+S3)/2.
Calculation: Signal-bar references = Use completed bars from the higher timeframe bars used for TimeFrame 1 and TimeFrame 3. In TimeFrame 1, bar 1 is the latest completed bar and bar 2 the prior completed bar. In TimeFrame 3, bars 1, 2, and 3 are completed bars, with 1 latest.
Calculation: Long initial stop-loss and take-profit = For a signal at P: S1−indent and R1+indent; at R0.5: S0.5−indent and R1.5+indent; at R1: P−indent and R2+indent; at R1.5: R0.5−indent and R2.5+indent; at R2: R1−indent and R3+indent; at R2.5: R1.5−indent and R3+indent; at S1: S2−indent and P+indent; at S0.5: S1.5−indent and R0.5+indent.
Calculation: Short initial stop-loss and take-profit = For a signal at P: R1+indent and S1−indent; at S0.5: R0.5+indent and S1.5−indent; at S1: P+indent and S2−indent; at S1.5: S0.5+indent and S2.5−indent; at S2: S1+indent and S3−indent; at S2.5: S1.5+indent and S3−indent; at R1: R2+indent and P−indent; at R0.5: R1.5+indent and S0.5−indent.
Long: Fewer than three positions are open AND ((TimeFrame 1 bar 1 open <= close AND (TimeFrame 1 bar 2 open <= P AND close >= P OR open <= R0.5 AND close >= R0.5 OR open <= R1 AND close >= R1 OR open <= R1.5 AND close >= R1.5 OR open <= R2 AND close >= R2 OR open <= R2.5 AND close >= R2.5 OR open <= S1 AND close >= S1 OR open <= S0.5 AND close >= S0.5)) OR (TimeFrame 3 bar 1 open <= close AND ((bar 3 low <= P AND close >= P AND bar 2 low <= P AND close >= P) OR (bar 3 low <= R0.5 AND close >= R0.5 AND bar 2 low <= R0.5 AND close >= R0.5) OR (bar 3 low <= R1 AND close >= R1 AND bar 2 low <= R1 AND close >= R1) OR (bar 3 low <= R1.5 AND close >= R1.5 AND bar 2 low <= R1.5 AND close >= R1.5) OR (bar 3 low <= R2 AND close >= R2 AND bar 2 low <= R2 AND close >= R2) OR (bar 3 low <= R2.5 AND close >= R2.5 AND bar 2 low <= R2.5 AND close >= R2.5) OR (bar 3 low <= S1 AND close >= S1 AND bar 2 low <= S1 AND close >= S1) OR (bar 3 low <= S0.5 AND close >= S0.5 AND bar 2 low <= S0.5 AND close >= S0.5))))
Short: Fewer than three positions are open AND ((TimeFrame 1 bar 1 open >= close AND (TimeFrame 1 bar 2 open >= P AND close <= P OR open >= S0.5 AND close <= S0.5 OR open >= S1 AND close <= S1 OR open >= S1.5 AND close <= S1.5 OR open >= S2 AND close <= S2 OR open >= S2.5 AND close <= S2.5 OR open >= R1 AND close <= R1 OR open >= R0.5 AND close <= R0.5)) OR (TimeFrame 3 bar 1 open >= close AND ((bar 3 high >= P AND close <= P AND bar 2 high >= P AND close <= P) OR (bar 3 high >= S0.5 AND close <= S0.5 AND bar 2 high >= S0.5 AND close <= S0.5) OR (bar 3 high >= S1 AND close <= S1 AND bar 2 high >= S1 AND close <= S1) OR (bar 3 high >= S1.5 AND close <= S1.5 AND bar 2 high >= S1.5 AND close <= S1.5) OR (bar 3 high >= S2 AND close <= S2 AND bar 2 high >= S2 AND close <= S2) OR (bar 3 high >= S2.5 AND close <= S2.5 AND bar 2 high >= S2.5 AND close <= S2.5) OR (bar 3 high >= R1 AND close <= R1 AND bar 2 high >= R1 AND close <= R1) OR (bar 3 high >= R0.5 AND close <= R0.5 AND bar 2 high >= R0.5 AND close <= R0.5))))
Exit: Long, price <= assigned stop-loss OR price >= assigned take-profit
Exit: Short, price >= assigned stop-loss OR price <= assigned take-profit
