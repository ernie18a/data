Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, P = (high + low + close) / 3; R1 = 2 × P − low; S1 = 2 × P − high; R2 = P + high − low; S2 = P − high + low; R3 = 2 × P + high − 2 × low; S3 = 2 × P − 2 × high + low; each half level is the midpoint of its adjacent whole levels, with P as level zero.
Calculation: Entry level and initial exits = select the first matching level in the applicable ordered list. For long entries, the ordered (entry, stop, target) levels are (P, S1, R1), (R0.5, S0.5, R1.5), (R1, P, R2), (R1.5, R0.5, R2.5), (R2, R1, R3), (R2.5, R1.5, R3), (S1, S2, P), (S0.5, S1.5, R0.5). For short entries, they are (P, R1, S1), (S0.5, R0.5, S1.5), (S1, P, S2), (S1.5, S0.5, S2.5), (S2, S1, S3), (S2.5, S1.5, S3), (R1, R2, P), (R0.5, R1.5, S0.5). Fix these levels at entry; subtract an offset from long stops and short targets, and add it to short stops and long targets. Evaluate breakout long, breakout short, rebound long, then rebound short, using the first matching decision.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry's stop. At each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled and fewer than the maximum allowed positions are open, update a long stop to the chart bar's open minus the trailing distance when open − entry price > trailing distance + trailing step AND the existing stop < open − trailing distance − trailing step; update a short stop to open plus the trailing distance when entry price − open > trailing distance + trailing step AND the existing stop > open + trailing distance + trailing step.
Long: At a new chart bar AND fewer than the maximum allowed positions are open AND the last completed bar of the longer higher timeframe has open <= close AND its preceding bar has open <= an entry level AND close >= that same entry level.
Short: At a new chart bar AND fewer than the maximum allowed positions are open AND the last completed bar of the longer higher timeframe has open >= close AND its preceding bar has open >= an entry level AND close <= that same entry level.
Long: At a new chart bar AND fewer than the maximum allowed positions are open AND the last completed bar of the shorter higher timeframe has open <= close AND each of its two preceding bars has low <= the same entry level AND close >= that level.
Short: At a new chart bar AND fewer than the maximum allowed positions are open AND the last completed bar of the shorter higher timeframe has open >= close AND each of its two preceding bars has high >= the same entry level AND close <= that level.
Exit: long, low <= trailing stop OR high >= entry target.
Exit: short, high >= trailing stop OR low <= entry target.
