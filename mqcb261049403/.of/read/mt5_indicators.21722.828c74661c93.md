Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, directional extreme, directional midpoint, or DeMark price. Directional extreme is high if close>open, low if close<open, otherwise close; directional midpoint is (high+close)/2 if close>open, (low+close)/2 if close<open, otherwise close. DeMark price is (r-low+r-high)/2, where r is (high+low+close+low)/2 if close<open, (high+low+close+high)/2 if close>open, otherwise (high+low+2*close)/2.
Calculation: Trend intensity = clamp(100*positive/(positive+negative), 0, 100), or 100 if positive+negative=0. Positive and negative are separately smoothed max(selected price - moving average of selected price, 0) and max(moving average of selected price - selected price, 0). The price moving average and deviation smoothing have independently configurable methods, lengths, and method parameters; the deviation smoothing uses the same settings for both components.
Long: Chart-timeframe trend intensity > upper threshold.
Short: Chart-timeframe trend intensity < lower threshold.
Long: Higher-timeframe trend intensity on the selected chart bar > upper threshold AND higher-timeframe trend intensity on the preceding chart bar <= upper threshold. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; the selected chart bar is configurable as the current or previous chart bar.
Short: Higher-timeframe trend intensity on the selected chart bar < lower threshold AND higher-timeframe trend intensity on the preceding chart bar >= lower threshold. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; the selected chart bar is configurable as the current or previous chart bar.
