Calculation: Operating bars = chart bars, or bars aggregated to a higher timeframe using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar. For the higher-timeframe version, sample the resulting decision on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carry it forward across the remaining chart bars in that higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Upward crossover = previous shorter-period close moving average < previous longer-period close moving average AND current shorter-period close moving average > current longer-period close moving average. Both moving averages use the same configurable method and shift.
Calculation: Downward crossover = previous shorter-period close moving average > previous longer-period close moving average AND current shorter-period close moving average < current longer-period close moving average. Both moving averages use the same configurable method and shift.
Calculation: Pending confirmation = initially none. When the confirmation distance is nonzero, an upward crossover with the optional RSI filter satisfied stores that bar's low as a pending long and clears any pending short; a downward crossover with the optional RSI filter satisfied stores that bar's high as a pending short and clears any pending long. Check confirmation on the crossover bar and every subsequent bar. Clear the pending direction when its confirmation condition is met, even if the flat-distance filter prevents a decision.
Calculation: Last accepted long low and last accepted short high = initially zero; update each independently to the current low or high whenever its corresponding decision passes the flat-distance filter. The flat-distance and confirmation distances are configurable price distances.
Long: ((confirmation distance = zero AND upward crossover AND (RSI filter disabled OR close RSI > 50)) OR (pending long exists AND high - pending long low >= confirmation distance AND close <= high)) AND (low >= last accepted long low + flat distance OR low <= last accepted long low - flat distance)
Short: ((confirmation distance = zero AND downward crossover AND (RSI filter disabled OR close RSI < 50)) OR (pending short exists AND pending short high - low >= confirmation distance AND open >= close)) AND (high >= last accepted short high + flat distance OR high <= last accepted short high - flat distance)
