Calculation: Input price = selectable open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (open+close)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+high+low+close)/4, (high+close)/2 if close>open else (low+close)/2, or high if close>open, low if close<open, else close. These choices can also use Heikin-Ashi OHLC, where Heikin-Ashi close=(open+high+low+close)/4, open=(previous Heikin-Ashi open+previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2, high=max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close), and low=min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: T3 = six successive exponential smoothings of the input price, initialized to the first input price, combined as -a^3*E6+(3*a^2+3*a^3)*E5-(6*a^2+3*a+3*a^3)*E4+(1+3*a+3*a^2+a^3)*E3, where a is the T3 factor. Each smoothing uses alpha=2/(1+period) for the original method or alpha=2/(2+(period-1)/2) for the modified method. For period<=1, retain the initial smoothing values. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Floating levels = the minimum T3 over the lookback including the current bar plus the range from that minimum to the corresponding maximum multiplied by a configurable fraction; the upper fraction is greater than the lower fraction. The middle level is the average of the upper and lower levels.
Long: T3 > upper floating level, OR T3 > middle floating level, OR T3 > previous T3, according to the selected comparison mode.
Short: T3 < lower floating level, OR T3 < middle floating level, OR T3 < previous T3, according to the selected comparison mode.
