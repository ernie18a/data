Long: RSI(14) on the chart timeframe crosses above 30 between the two most recently completed bars AND there is no open long position; OR a long position exists, the number of open long positions is below the configured maximum, and current bid is above the latest long entry price by the configured add-on step; OR at a configured box-entry hour with no outstanding stop orders, price reaches the previous completed higher-timeframe high plus an offset; OR on Monday during the configured entry window, with no open positions or outstanding stop orders, price reaches the highest high of the configured lookback of lower-timeframe bars plus an offset; OR at the configured daily opening hour with no open positions, the still-forming daily bar's open is below the previous completed daily bar's low.
Short: RSI(14) on the chart timeframe crosses below 70 between the two most recently completed bars AND there is no open short position; OR a short position exists, the number of open short positions is below the configured maximum, and current ask is below the latest short entry price by the configured add-on step; OR at a configured box-entry hour with no outstanding stop orders, price reaches the previous completed higher-timeframe low minus an offset; OR on Monday during the configured entry window, with no open positions or outstanding stop orders, price reaches the lowest low of the configured lookback of lower-timeframe bars minus an offset; OR at the configured daily opening hour with no open positions, the still-forming daily bar's open is above the previous completed daily bar's high.
Exit: Long, price reaches or falls below the active stop loss OR it is Friday after the configured closing hour.
Exit: Short, price reaches or rises above the active stop loss OR it is Friday after the configured closing hour.
