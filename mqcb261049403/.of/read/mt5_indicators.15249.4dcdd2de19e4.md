Calculation: NRTR trend = Initialize trend to neutral and price and threshold to the close; on each bar, if trend is nonnegative, raise price to the greater of its previous value and close, and raise threshold to the greater of its previous value and price × (1 − dK); if close falls below threshold, set price to close, threshold to price × (1 + dK), and trend to down. If trend is nonpositive, lower price to the lesser of its previous value and close, and lower threshold to the lesser of its previous value and price × (1 + dK); if close rises above threshold, set price to close, threshold to price × (1 − dK), and trend to up. dK is the mean absolute high-low range over a lookback, scaled by a configurable factor.
Long: NRTR trend changes from down to up
Short: NRTR trend changes from up to down
