Calculation: One-sided Gaussian smooth of series x at bar t = sum over i = 0 to N-1 of x[t-i] * exp(trunc(-9*i*i/(N+1)^2)), divided by the sum of those weights; trunc rounds toward zero.
Calculation: Range oscillator = 200 * (Gaussian-smoothed weighted close minus the lowest Gaussian-smoothed low over the lookback) / (highest Gaussian-smoothed high over the same lookback minus that lowest low) - 100; use 0 when the denominator is 0. Weighted close = (high + low + 2*close)/4. Use the same Gaussian length for all three series. Calculate on a configurable timeframe that may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar as configured; previous means the preceding bar of that timeframe.
Long: Range oscillator > 0 AND range oscillator > upper threshold AND (previous range oscillator <= 0 OR previous range oscillator <= upper threshold)
Short: Range oscillator < 0 AND range oscillator < lower threshold AND (previous range oscillator >= 0 OR previous range oscillator >= lower threshold)
Exit: long, (range oscillator < 0 AND range oscillator < lower threshold) OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, (range oscillator > 0 AND range oscillator > upper threshold) OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
