Calculation: Signal bars = chart-timeframe bars or bars aggregated to a configurable higher or lower timeframe; use the bar still forming and its preceding bars for all calculations.
Calculation: Fast average = short-period simple moving average of typical price; slow average = longer-period smoothed moving average of median price.
Calculation: Step direction = find the offsets of the maximum and minimum fast averages over a lookback including the forming bar, initializing both extrema and offsets from the last completed bar and scanning from oldest to newest with strict comparisons. Let divergence be fast average minus slow average. If the maximum offset is greater than the minimum offset, direction is down when forming-bar divergence is less than last-completed-bar divergence, otherwise up. If the maximum offset is less than the minimum offset, direction is up when forming-bar divergence is greater than last-completed-bar divergence, otherwise down. If the offsets are equal, direction is neutral.
Calculation: ATR stop state = for each signal bar from oldest to newest, calculate lower candidate as the maximum of high minus an ATR multiplier times ATR over a lookback, and upper candidate as the minimum of low plus the same multiplier times ATR over that lookback. Carry forward the previous state; set it positive if close exceeds the previous upper stop, then set it negative if close is below the previous lower stop. In a positive state, lower stop is the greater of its candidate and the previous lower stop; otherwise it is its candidate. In a negative state, upper stop is the lesser of its candidate and the previous upper stop; otherwise it is its candidate. Initialize state to neutral and calculate over a finite history window. A positive or negative stop side is active only when its corresponding stop is greater than zero.
Long: Last completed ATR stop side is negative AND forming ATR stop side is positive AND step direction is up.
Short: Last completed ATR stop side is positive AND forming ATR stop side is negative AND step direction is down.
Exit: long, opposite-signal closing is enabled AND last completed ATR stop side is positive AND forming ATR stop side is negative AND step direction is down.
Exit: short, opposite-signal closing is enabled AND last completed ATR stop side is negative AND forming ATR stop side is positive AND step direction is up.
