Calculation: Noise-reduced CCI = (price - twice-smoothed price) / (0.015 / lookback × max(sum of absolute differences between each price in the lookback and the current twice-smoothed price, sensitivity floor)); use zero if the denominator is zero. Price is a selected OHLC-derived price; smooth it with a first moving average, then smooth that result with a second moving average using independently selected methods and lengths. Calculate on the higher timeframe using the last completed bar and preceding completed bars.
Long: Noise-reduced CCI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > upper threshold AND noise-reduced CCI on the preceding higher-timeframe bar <= upper threshold
Short: Noise-reduced CCI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < lower threshold AND noise-reduced CCI on the preceding higher-timeframe bar >= lower threshold
Exit: long, noise-reduced CCI on the bar preceding the last completed higher-timeframe bar < middle threshold OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, noise-reduced CCI on the bar preceding the last completed higher-timeframe bar > middle threshold OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
