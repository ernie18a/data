Calculation: BrainTrend1 long signal = on a given timeframe, the stochastic oscillator is above 53 and the absolute difference between the current close and the close two bars earlier exceeds ATR divided by 2.3.
Calculation: BrainTrend1 short signal = on a given timeframe, the stochastic oscillator is below 47 and the absolute difference between the current close and the close two bars earlier exceeds ATR divided by 2.3.
Calculation: BrainTrend2 trend state = compute true range as the greatest of high−low, |high−previous close|, and |low−previous close|. Compute weighted ATR from the current and preceding bars, assigning weights from the ATR lookback down to 1, then multiply the weighted sum by 2/(7×8). Set the threshold width to 0.7×weighted ATR. Maintain a persistent state and extreme: initialize the extreme to the close at the start of available history and the state to up if the preceding close is greater than the close before it, otherwise down. In the up state, change to down when low is below extreme−width and set extreme to high; otherwise raise extreme to low when low exceeds it. In the down state, change to up when high exceeds extreme+width and set extreme to low; otherwise lower extreme to high when high is below it.
Long: BrainTrend1 long signal is true on the chart timeframe OR BrainTrend2 trend state is up on the chart timeframe OR BrainTrend1 long signal is true on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar OR BrainTrend2 trend state is up on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Short: BrainTrend1 short signal is true on the chart timeframe OR BrainTrend2 trend state is down on the chart timeframe OR BrainTrend1 short signal is true on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar OR BrainTrend2 trend state is down on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
