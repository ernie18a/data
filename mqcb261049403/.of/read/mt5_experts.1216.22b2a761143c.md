Calculation: Signal bars = bars on a configurable timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe or a higher or lower timeframe; use a configurable bar offset from the bar still forming, including the last completed bar or the forming bar, and its immediately preceding bar.
Calculation: Rounded moving average = let M be a configurable moving average of a configurable OHLC-derived price and d a configurable price distance. On each signal-timeframe bar, set R to M if M differs from the preceding M by more than d, or differs from the preceding R by more than d, or M is above preceding R and the last nonzero change of R was upward, or M is below preceding R and the last nonzero change of R was downward; otherwise retain preceding R. The last nonzero direction persists while R is unchanged.
Calculation: Channel = when R equals preceding R, set the width to ATR multiplied by a configurable factor on the first unchanged bar after a change in R, and otherwise retain the preceding width; upper and lower boundaries are R plus and minus that width. When R changes, both boundaries are zero, or retain their preceding values if channel continuity is enabled.
Long: Both boundaries on the signal bar are nonzero AND signal-bar close > signal-bar upper boundary AND (preceding upper boundary = 0 OR preceding close <= preceding upper boundary).
Short: Both boundaries on the signal bar are nonzero AND signal-bar close < signal-bar lower boundary AND (preceding lower boundary = 0 OR preceding close >= preceding lower boundary).
Exit: long, both signal-bar boundaries are nonzero AND signal-bar close < signal-bar lower boundary OR at least one signal-bar boundary = 0 AND a preceding nonzero upper boundary exists AND signal-bar close < the lower boundary on the most recent preceding bar with a nonzero upper boundary.
Exit: short, both signal-bar boundaries are nonzero AND signal-bar close > signal-bar upper boundary OR at least one signal-bar boundary = 0 AND a preceding nonzero upper boundary exists AND signal-bar close > the most recent preceding nonzero upper boundary.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus configurable stop distance OR high >= entry price plus configurable profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus configurable stop distance OR low <= entry price minus configurable profit distance.
