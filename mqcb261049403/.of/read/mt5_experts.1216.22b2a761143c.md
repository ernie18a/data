Calculation: Rounding MA = smooth the selected bar price; update its value to the smoothed value when that value differs from either the previous smoothed value or previous rounding MA by more than the rounding threshold, or when it continues moving in the previous rounding MA’s direction; otherwise retain the previous rounding MA.
Calculation: Channel boundaries = while the rounding MA is unchanged, upper/lower boundaries are the rounding MA plus/minus ATR-based width; refresh the width when the flat run begins. If continuity is enabled, carry the previous boundaries while the rounding MA changes; otherwise boundaries are absent. Use bars from the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: On the last completed indicator-timeframe bar, both channel boundaries exist AND close > upper boundary AND (the immediately preceding completed bar has no upper boundary OR its close <= its upper boundary)
Short: On the last completed indicator-timeframe bar, both channel boundaries exist AND close < lower boundary AND (the immediately preceding completed bar has no lower boundary OR its close >= its lower boundary)
Exit: short, on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar both channel boundaries exist AND close > upper boundary
Exit: long, on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar both channel boundaries exist AND close < lower boundary
Exit: short, the last completed bar has no channel boundary AND a prior bar with an upper boundary exists AND the last completed bar’s close > the upper boundary on the most recent prior bar with an upper boundary
Exit: long, the last completed bar has no channel boundary AND a prior bar with an upper boundary exists AND the last completed bar’s close < the lower boundary on that same prior bar
