Calculation: Bar indexing = index 0 is the forming bar at the opening of the current chart bar; index 1 is the last completed bar. Higher-timeframe bars are aligned to the current chart bar.
Calculation: Recent fractal exists = at least one upper or lower standard fractal has a positive price within a specified lookback of higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Active long stop = entry price minus the stop distance; after a chart-bar opening price exceeds entry price by the break-even activation distance, the stop may move to entry price plus the break-even offset.
Calculation: Active short stop = entry price plus the stop distance; after a chart-bar opening price falls below entry price by the break-even activation distance, the stop may move to entry price minus the break-even offset.
Long: Recent fractal exists AND the shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the forming chart bar is greater than the longer-period one AND chart low[2] < chart high[1] AND abs(chart open[0] - chart close[2]) > entry-distance threshold AND ((higher-timeframe open[1] > close[1] AND (high[1] - open[1]) / (high[1] - low[1]) >= pinbar threshold) OR (higher-timeframe open[1] < close[1] AND (high[1] - close[1]) / (high[1] - low[1]) >= pinbar threshold)).
Short: Recent fractal exists AND the shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the forming chart bar is less than the longer-period one AND chart low[1] < chart high[2] AND abs(chart open[0] - chart close[2]) > entry-distance threshold AND ((higher-timeframe open[1] > close[1] AND (close[1] - low[1]) / (high[1] - low[1]) >= pinbar threshold) OR (higher-timeframe open[1] < close[1] AND (open[1] - low[1]) / (high[1] - low[1]) >= pinbar threshold)).
Exit: long, chart low <= active long stop OR chart high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: short, chart high >= active short stop OR chart low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
