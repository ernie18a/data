Short: ((higher-timeframe last completed bar is bullish AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.50) OR (higher-timeframe last completed bar is bearish AND (open − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.50)) AND an up or down fractal is found in the scan of up to 200 higher-timeframe bars AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND chart-timeframe low[1] < high[2] AND |current price − chart-timeframe close[2]| > the candle-trigger distance
Exit: long, price ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR price ≥ entry price + take-profit distance OR (after price exceeds entry price by the break-even activation distance) price ≤ entry price + break-even offset
Exit: short, price ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR price ≤ entry price − take-profit distance OR (after price falls below entry price by the break-even activation distance) price ≥ entry price − break-even offset
