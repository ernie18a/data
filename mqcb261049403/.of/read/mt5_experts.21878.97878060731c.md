Calculation: Trailing stop = Initially the fixed stop-loss price, or absent if disabled. At each available price observation, if trailing is enabled and a long position's price exceeds entry price plus trailing distance plus trailing step, and its existing stop is below price minus trailing distance minus trailing step, set the stop to price minus trailing distance. If trailing is enabled and a short position's price is below entry price minus trailing distance minus trailing step, and its existing stop is absent or above price plus trailing distance plus trailing step, set the stop to price plus trailing distance.
Long: At the start of a new chart bar, MACD main line on the forming higher-timeframe bar > MACD main line on the preceding completed bar AND MACD main line on the preceding completed bar < 0 AND stochastic main line on its forming higher-timeframe bar < buy threshold AND stochastic main line on its forming bar > stochastic main line on its preceding completed bar.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, MACD main line on the forming higher-timeframe bar < MACD main line on the preceding completed bar AND MACD main line on the preceding completed bar > 0 AND stochastic main line on its forming higher-timeframe bar > sell threshold AND stochastic main line on its forming bar < stochastic main line on its preceding completed bar.
Exit: Long, price <= trailing stop OR enabled take-profit price >= entry price AND price >= entry price plus long take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, price >= trailing stop OR enabled take-profit price <= entry price AND price <= entry price minus short take-profit distance.
