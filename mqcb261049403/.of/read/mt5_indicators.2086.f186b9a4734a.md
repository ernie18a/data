Calculation: Previous-day pivot = (open + high + low of the most recent completed daily bar before today, skipping Sunday + close of the current still-forming daily bar) / 4; daily bars are higher than or equal to the chart timeframe
Calculation: Floating pivot = (highest high and lowest low since the start of today, including the current still-forming higher-timeframe bar + current higher-timeframe bar close) / 3
Long: Floating pivot > Previous-day pivot
Short: Floating pivot < Previous-day pivot
