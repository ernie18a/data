Calculation: Previous-day range = high minus low of the last completed calendar-day bar.
Calculation: Long entry, stop, and targets = previous-day high minus 38.2%, 61.8%, 38.2% − 61.8%, 100%, and 161.8% of the previous-day range, respectively.
Calculation: Short entry, stop, and targets = previous-day low plus 38.2%, 61.8%, and 38.2% of the previous-day range; short targets are the short entry minus 61.8%, 100%, and 161.8% of the previous-day range.
Calculation: Risk/reward ratio = absolute distance from entry to target divided by absolute distance from entry to stop.
Long: current price > current calendar-day open AND each of the three long-target risk/reward ratios > 2.
Short: current price < current calendar-day open AND each of the three short-target risk/reward ratios > 2.
