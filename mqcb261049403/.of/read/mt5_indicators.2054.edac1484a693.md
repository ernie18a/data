Calculation: Ergodic oscillator = 100 times the ratio of triple-smoothed momentum to triple-smoothed absolute momentum, or zero when the denominator is zero. Momentum is the selected bar price minus that price a configurable number of bars ago. Apply the same smoothing method and corresponding periods to both series, in the same order.
Calculation: Signal line = the ergodic oscillator smoothed with the same method and a separately configurable period.
Calculation: Oscillator and signal line are calculated either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; compare the higher-timeframe oscillator with its preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Ergodic oscillator > signal line
Short: Ergodic oscillator < signal line
Long: Ergodic oscillator > 0 AND ergodic oscillator != previous bar's ergodic oscillator
Short: Ergodic oscillator < 0 AND ergodic oscillator != previous bar's ergodic oscillator
