Calculation: Regression polynomial line = least-squares polynomial fit of selected bar price over a lookback window, evaluated at the current bar; when sampled from another timeframe, that timeframe is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe and uses its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Signal line = the selected smoothing of the regression polynomial line, using a shorter lookback than the regression fit.
Long: Regression polynomial line > signal line
Short: Regression polynomial line < signal line
