Calculation: Selected price = a selected open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Apply the same formulas to Heikin-Ashi OHLC when selected.
Calculation: Original average = the selected simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average of the selected price.
Calculation: Corrected average = initialize to the original average; thereafter set it to the previous corrected average + c × (original average − previous corrected average), where variance is the population variance of the selected price over the correction lookback, d is (previous corrected average − original average)², and c is 0 if d < variance or d = 0, otherwise 1 − variance/d. A negative correction setting makes variance 0; a zero correction setting uses the average lookback.
Calculation: Floating levels = the minimum corrected average over the floating-level lookback + a selected fraction of its range over that lookback; the upper fraction exceeds the lower fraction, and the middle level is the mean of the upper and lower levels.
Calculation: All averages and levels use the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of a selected higher timeframe. For a higher timeframe, use its directional state on each constituent chart bar.
Calculation: Corrected-average directional state = for the selected mode, use the corresponding long or short comparison below; on equality retain the previous state when the corrected average is unchanged, and otherwise use a neutral state. In slope mode, equality retains the previous state. Original-average directional state retains its previous state on equality.
Long: Original average > corrected average OR original-average directional state was long AND original average = corrected average.
Short: Original average < corrected average OR original-average directional state was short AND original average = corrected average.
Long: Selected corrected-average mode is slope AND corrected average > previous corrected average OR selected mode is outer levels AND corrected average > upper floating level OR selected mode is middle level AND corrected average > middle floating level OR selected mode is original average AND corrected average < original average OR corrected-average directional state retains a previous long state.
Short: Selected corrected-average mode is slope AND corrected average < previous corrected average OR selected mode is outer levels AND corrected average < lower floating level OR selected mode is middle level AND corrected average < middle floating level OR selected mode is original average AND corrected average > original average OR corrected-average directional state retains a previous short state.
