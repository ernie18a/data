Calculation: Median price = (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Fast average = EMA of median price; intermediate average = linear-weighted moving average of the fast average; slow average = linear-weighted moving average of the intermediate average; longest average = linear-weighted moving average of the slow average. Their lookbacks increase in that order.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = bands of median price; the middle band is its SMA.
Calculation: Momentum = shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close on a timeframe that may be higher or lower than, or equal to, the chart timeframe; use its still-forming bar. Momentum signal = SMA of momentum; momentum histogram = momentum minus momentum signal.
Calculation: Bullish trend = the first matching condition in this ordered sequence is bullish: bullish when fast average >= its previous value AND intermediate average > its previous value AND intermediate minus slow average >= its previous value AND open <= fast average; bearish when fast average <= its previous value AND intermediate average < its previous value AND intermediate minus slow average <= its previous value AND open >= fast average; bullish when fast average >= its previous value AND intermediate average > slow average AND slow average > middle band AND middle band > its previous value AND open <= fast average; bearish when fast average <= its previous value AND intermediate average < slow average AND slow average < middle band AND middle band < its previous value AND open >= fast average; bullish when fast average > its previous value AND fast average > upper band AND fast minus intermediate average > its previous value AND fast average minus upper band > its previous value AND open > fast average; bearish when fast average < its previous value AND fast average < lower band AND fast minus intermediate average < its previous value AND fast average minus lower band < its previous value AND open < fast average; bullish when longest average > its previous value AND middle band > its previous value AND middle band > longest average AND fast average > middle band AND fast average > its previous value; bearish when longest average < its previous value AND middle band < its previous value AND middle band < longest average AND fast average < middle band AND fast average < its previous value; bullish when fast average >= its previous value AND middle band minus longest average > its previous value AND middle band < longest average AND fast average > longest average; bearish when fast average <= its previous value AND middle band minus longest average < its previous value AND middle band > longest average AND fast average < longest average.
Calculation: Bearish trend = the first matching condition in the ordered trend sequence is bearish.
Calculation: Weighted close = (high + low + 2 * close) / 4.
Calculation: Long signal = momentum histogram > its previous value AND momentum > its previous value AND fast average > intermediate average AND bullish trend AND weighted close > previous weighted close AND close > fast average AND close > open AND high <= fast average + a fraction of the take-profit distance.
Calculation: Short signal = momentum histogram < its previous value AND momentum < its previous value AND fast average < intermediate average AND bearish trend AND weighted close < previous weighted close AND close < fast average AND close < open AND low >= fast average - a fraction of the take-profit distance.
Long: long signal = true AND day is a permitted trading day AND hour is outside the excluded hour AND (position is flat OR position is short AND reversal is enabled).
Short: short signal = true AND day is a permitted trading day AND hour is outside the excluded hour AND (position is flat OR position is long AND reversal is enabled).
Exit: long, short signal = true AND day is a permitted trading day AND hour is outside the excluded hour AND reversal is enabled.
Exit: short, long signal = true AND day is a permitted trading day AND hour is outside the excluded hour AND reversal is enabled.
