Calculation: Supertrend state = On the selected timeframe (same as or higher than the chart timeframe), calculate ATR bands from midpoint (high + low) / 2 ± multiplier × ATR. Final upper band is the basic upper band if it is below the prior final upper band or the prior close is above that band; otherwise it is the prior final upper band. Final lower band is the basic lower band if it is above the prior final lower band or the prior close is below that band; otherwise it is the prior final lower band. Initialize state as bullish when close ≥ midpoint, otherwise bearish. Thereafter, switch bearish to bullish when close > final upper band, switch bullish to bearish when close < final lower band, and otherwise retain the prior state.
Long: Supertrend state is bullish on the selected-timeframe bar mapped to the chart bar (the bar may still be forming), OR the state changes from bearish to bullish on the last chart bar of a completed selected-timeframe bar.
Short: Supertrend state is bearish on the selected-timeframe bar mapped to the chart bar (the bar may still be forming), OR the state changes from bullish to bearish on the last chart bar of a completed selected-timeframe bar.
