Calculation: ColorSTD = smooth the weighted balance of positive and negative candle bodies over a lookback, calculated as 100 × weighted positive-body sum ÷ (weighted positive-body sum + weighted negative-body sum) − 50; weight more recent bars more heavily. Use the current chart-timeframe bar; in the higher-timeframe wrapper, use its still-forming bar.
Long: (level mode AND ColorSTD > middle level) OR (direction mode AND ColorSTD > middle level AND ColorSTD > previous bar’s ColorSTD) OR (direction mode AND ColorSTD < middle level AND ColorSTD > previous bar’s ColorSTD)
Short: (level mode AND ColorSTD < middle level) OR (direction mode AND ColorSTD > middle level AND ColorSTD < previous bar’s ColorSTD) OR (direction mode AND ColorSTD < middle level AND ColorSTD < previous bar’s ColorSTD)
