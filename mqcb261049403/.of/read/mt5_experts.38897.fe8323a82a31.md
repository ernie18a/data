Calculation: Fast and slow lines = moving averages with the same smoothing method and period of RSI calculated on the selected price; the fast line uses a shorter RSI lookback than the slow line. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, using either its forming bar or its last completed bar and the preceding bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initial stop at entry price minus stop distance; when trailing is enabled and the observed price exceeds entry price by more than trailing distance plus trailing step, raise the stop to observed price minus trailing distance only if this exceeds the existing stop by more than the trailing step.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initial stop at entry price plus stop distance; when trailing is enabled and the observed price is below entry price by more than trailing distance plus trailing step, lower the stop to observed price plus trailing distance only if this is below the existing stop by more than the trailing step.

Short: Time control is disabled OR current time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive daily interval, including intervals crossing midnight; AND the fast line was strictly above the slow line on the preceding bar AND is strictly below it on the selected bar AND both lines are above the middle RSI level; reverse mode is disabled.

Short: Time control is disabled OR current time is within the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive daily interval, including intervals crossing midnight; AND the fast line was strictly below the slow line on the preceding bar AND is strictly above it on the selected bar AND both lines are below the middle RSI level; reverse mode is enabled.
Exit: long, stop loss is enabled AND price reaches the long trailing stop OR take profit is enabled AND price reaches entry price plus take-profit distance OR an enabled opposite-position close occurs on a short entry signal OR trading is restricted to long positions AND a short signal occurs.
Exit: short, stop loss is enabled AND price reaches the short trailing stop OR take profit is enabled AND price reaches entry price minus take-profit distance OR an enabled opposite-position close occurs on a long entry signal OR trading is restricted to short positions AND a long signal occurs.
