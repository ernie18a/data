Calculation: Normalized deviation = 100 × smoothed deviation / smoothed absolute deviation, or 0 when smoothed deviation is 0; deviation is selected bar price minus its moving average. Smooth deviation and absolute deviation separately with the same smoothing method and parameters; price smoothing has independent parameters.
Calculation: Step line = for each step size, initialize previous upper and lower bands to negative and positive infinity, respectively, and trend to 0. On each bar, set upper = normalized deviation + 2 × step size and lower = normalized deviation - 2 × step size. Set trend to +1 if normalized deviation > previous upper, or -1 if normalized deviation < previous lower; otherwise retain trend. If trend > 0, set lower = max(lower, previous lower) and line = lower + step size. If trend < 0, set upper = min(upper, previous upper) and line = upper - step size. Save both bands for the next bar. Calculate independent fast and slow lines, with the fast step size smaller than the slow step size.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe step lines = step lines calculated from the higher timeframe's still-forming bar at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, held constant until the next higher-timeframe bar begins.
Long: fast chart-timeframe step line > slow chart-timeframe step line OR fast higher-timeframe step line > slow higher-timeframe step line
Short: fast chart-timeframe step line < slow chart-timeframe step line OR fast higher-timeframe step line < slow higher-timeframe step line
