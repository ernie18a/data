Calculation: First fractal = the oldest upper or lower standard fractal in a fixed lookback on the higher timeframe, scanning through the bar still forming; an upper fractal takes priority if both occur on the same bar.
Long: First fractal is lower AND shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe's forming bar > longer-period linear weighted moving average of typical price on the chart timeframe's forming bar AND low of the second-last completed chart bar < high of the last completed chart bar AND absolute difference between the current chart bar's open and the second-last completed chart bar's close > a price-distance threshold.
