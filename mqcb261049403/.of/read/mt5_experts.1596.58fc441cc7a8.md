Calculation: Bullish and bearish momentum lines = On the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), take each completed bar’s applied-price change. For each rolling lookback, accumulate negative changes separately from positive changes; each accumulator carries forward its prior value only on bars with a change of that sign. Sum the positive-change series and subtract the sum of the carried negative-change series for the bullish line; sum the negative-change series and subtract the sum of the carried positive-change series for the bearish line. Apply the same selected smoother and smoothing depth to both lines.
Long: Previous completed bar bullish line < bearish line AND current completed bar bullish line >= bearish line
Short: Previous completed bar bullish line > bearish line AND current completed bar bullish line <= bearish line
Exit: long, previous completed bar bullish line > bearish line AND current completed bar bullish line <= bearish line OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, previous completed bar bullish line < bearish line AND current completed bar bullish line >= bearish line OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance
