Calculation: MA spread = (shorter-lookback SMA of bar opens - longer-lookback SMA of bar opens) / longer-lookback SMA of bar opens.
Calculation: Signal = MA spread on the shorter signal timeframe's forming bar > 0 AND ((MA spread on the longer signal timeframe's forming bar > its last completed value AND its last completed value < its preceding value AND its preceding value < the value before that) OR (MA spread on the longer signal timeframe's forming bar < its last completed value AND its last completed value > its preceding value AND its preceding value > the value before that)). Both signal timeframes can be higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus stop-loss distance initially; at each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled AND current open - entry price > trailing distance + trailing step AND current stop < current open - trailing distance - trailing step, update the stop to current open - trailing distance.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus stop-loss distance initially; at each new chart bar, if trailing is enabled AND entry price - current open > trailing distance + trailing step AND current stop > current open + trailing distance + trailing step, update the stop to current open + trailing distance.
Long: Signal AND reverse trading is disabled.
Short: Signal AND reverse trading is enabled.
Exit: long, stop loss is enabled AND low <= long stop OR take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR close opposite is enabled AND Signal AND reverse trading is enabled.
Exit: short, stop loss is enabled AND high >= short stop OR take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR close opposite is enabled AND Signal AND reverse trading is disabled.
