Calculation: Adaptive moving average = starting with the close immediately before the first calculated bar, recursively set AMA to previous AMA + smoothing factor × (close − previous AMA), where efficiency ratio is absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback plus a small positive constant, and smoothing factor is (efficiency ratio × (fast smoothing constant − slow smoothing constant) + slow smoothing constant) raised to a configurable exponent; each smoothing constant is 2 / (its period + 1), with the fast period shorter than the slow period.
Calculation: Confirmed upward LWMA cross = shorter-period close LWMA > longer-period close LWMA on a bar and the following bar, AND shorter-period close LWMA < longer-period close LWMA on the preceding bar. Confirmed downward LWMA cross reverses all three inequalities. The cross is identified on the middle bar and is confirmed on the following bar.
Calculation: MACD histogram = short-period close EMA − long-period close EMA − EMA of that difference, with the short period shorter than the long period.
Long: Adaptive moving average − previous adaptive moving average > configurable positive price-distance threshold.
Short: Previous adaptive moving average − adaptive moving average > configurable positive price-distance threshold.
Long: Confirmed upward LWMA cross.
Short: Confirmed downward LWMA cross.
Long: No open position AND current weekday and time are within the configured trading session AND no entry has occurred on the current chart bar AND ((reverse mode is off AND MACD histogram > 0 AND adaptive moving average − previous adaptive moving average > threshold AND a confirmed upward LWMA cross occurred on the indicator bar or an allowed preceding bar) OR (reverse mode is on AND MACD histogram < 0 AND previous adaptive moving average − adaptive moving average > threshold AND a confirmed downward LWMA cross occurred on the indicator bar or an allowed preceding bar)). For intrabar mode the indicator bar is the forming chart bar and the allowed preceding bars are the two previous bars; otherwise the indicator bar is the last completed chart bar and the allowed preceding bar is its previous bar.
Short: No open position AND current weekday and time are within the configured trading session AND no entry has occurred on the current chart bar AND ((reverse mode is off AND MACD histogram < 0 AND previous adaptive moving average − adaptive moving average > threshold AND a confirmed downward LWMA cross occurred on the indicator bar or an allowed preceding bar) OR (reverse mode is on AND MACD histogram > 0 AND adaptive moving average − previous adaptive moving average > threshold AND a confirmed upward LWMA cross occurred on the indicator bar or an allowed preceding bar)). For intrabar mode the indicator bar is the forming chart bar and the allowed preceding bars are the two previous bars; otherwise the indicator bar is the last completed chart bar and the allowed preceding bar is its previous bar.
Exit: long, current chart bar is later than the entry bar AND ((reverse mode is off AND MACD histogram <= 0) OR (reverse mode is on AND MACD histogram >= 0)); use the forming chart bar in intrabar mode and the last completed chart bar otherwise.
Exit: short, current chart bar is later than the entry bar AND ((reverse mode is off AND MACD histogram >= 0) OR (reverse mode is on AND MACD histogram <= 0)); use the forming chart bar in intrabar mode and the last completed chart bar otherwise.
Exit: long, current weekday is Monday through Friday AND current time is outside the configured trading session.
Exit: short, current weekday is Monday through Friday AND current time is outside the configured trading session.
Exit: long, price <= entry price − enabled stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + enabled take-profit distance OR price <= the enabled stepped trailing stop, which moves to entry price after favorable movement reaches the trailing-start distance and thereafter increases by one trailing increment for each additional full increment of favorable movement beyond the first.
Exit: short, price >= entry price + enabled stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price − enabled take-profit distance OR price >= the enabled stepped trailing stop, which moves to entry price after favorable movement reaches the trailing-start distance and thereafter decreases by one trailing increment for each additional full increment of favorable movement beyond the first.
