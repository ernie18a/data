Calculation: Bull strength = EMA of the sum of current high minus each of the highest highs over three prior-bar lookbacks: short, medium, and long. Exclude the current bar from each lookback. Seed the EMA with the first available sum.
Calculation: Bear strength = EMA, using the same smoothing period, of the sum of each of the lowest lows over the same three prior-bar lookbacks minus current low. Exclude the current bar from each lookback. Seed the EMA with the first available sum.
Calculation: Strengths are calculated on the chart timeframe or on the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Long: Bull strength > bear strength
Short: Bull strength < bear strength
