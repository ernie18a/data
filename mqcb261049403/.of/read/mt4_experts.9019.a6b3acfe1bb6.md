Calculation: Fast LWMA = linearly weighted moving average of close over a shorter lookback, on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Slow SMA = simple moving average of close over a longer lookback, on the chart timeframe.
Long: Fast LWMA on the bar before the last completed bar < Slow SMA on that bar AND Fast LWMA on the last completed bar > Slow SMA on that bar
Short: Fast LWMA on the bar before the last completed bar > Slow SMA on that bar AND Fast LWMA on the last completed bar < Slow SMA on that bar
Exit: long, Fast LWMA on the bar before the last completed bar > Slow SMA on that bar AND Fast LWMA on the last completed bar < Slow SMA on that bar
Exit: short, Fast LWMA on the bar before the last completed bar < Slow SMA on that bar AND Fast LWMA on the last completed bar > Slow SMA on that bar
