Calculation: Hammer = lower wick >= 2 × body and >= 0.5 × bar range, with upper wick <= 0.25 × range
Calculation: Shooting star = upper wick >= 2 × body and >= 0.5 × bar range, with lower wick <= 0.25 × range
Calculation: EMA trend score = number of adjacent EMA pairs aligned in the direction: among five chart-timeframe EMAs with strictly increasing periods, count pairs where the shorter-period EMA is above the longer-period EMA for uptrend, or below for downtrend
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = round-number levels at equal price intervals; confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs/lows within the lookback; the three longest chart-timeframe EMAs; lower/upper Bollinger Bands; EMAs and recent confirmed swing highs/lows from each configured higher timeframe; Fibonacci retracements at 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, and 0.618 of the high-low range over the lookback on higher timeframe B, only when that range is at least the specified ATR multiple (place retracements from the high if its bar is later than the low, otherwise from the low); and the high/low of the last completed bar on higher timeframe D. Use only completed higher-timeframe bars as of the chart bar close. A swing is a high/low at least as extreme as the preceding strength bars and strictly more extreme than the following strength bars; chart-timeframe swings are usable only when the following strength bars and one additional bar have closed, while higher-timeframe swings are usable once the following strength bars have closed.
Calculation: Confluence = number of distinct level kinds within tolerance of the touch price, excluding resistance-only levels for long setups and support-only levels for short setups; higher-timeframe kinds are higher-timeframe EMAs and swings
Calculation: Touch price = lower of current and previous lows for long setups; higher of current and previous highs for short setups
Calculation: Stop distance = max(minimum ATR-based stop distance, absolute difference between entry price and touch price + 0.1 × ATR) + spread; require the distance before adding spread to be no greater than the maximum stop ATR multiple
Calculation: Target price = nearest level in the trade direction, farther from entry than tolerance and within the maximum target ATR distance, excluding support-only levels for long targets and resistance-only levels for short targets, with at least the required number of distinct target kinds within tolerance; move the target toward entry by the specified ATR buffer
Calculation: Reward/risk = (directional distance from entry to target price − spread) / stop distance
Long: bullish pattern AND (chart-timeframe EMA trend filter disabled OR EMA trend score uptrend >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR bar hour is within the configured session) AND current bar range >= minimum ATR multiple AND no signal occurred in the preceding cooldown bars AND each active higher timeframe B and C has no downtrend when mode is no-counter, or has an uptrend when mode is aligned (missing data blocks only if configured) AND confluence at touch price >= minimum kinds AND higher-timeframe confluence kinds >= minimum AND stop distance before spread <= maximum stop ATR multiple AND (spread filter disabled OR spread <= maximum spread fraction × stop distance) AND a valid target exists AND reward > 0 AND reward/risk >= minimum
Short: bearish pattern AND (chart-timeframe EMA trend filter disabled OR EMA trend score downtrend >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR bar hour is within the configured session) AND current bar range >= minimum ATR multiple AND no signal occurred in the preceding cooldown bars AND each active higher timeframe B and C has no uptrend when mode is no-counter, or has a downtrend when mode is aligned (missing data blocks only if configured) AND confluence at touch price >= minimum kinds AND higher-timeframe confluence kinds >= minimum AND stop distance before spread <= maximum stop ATR multiple AND (spread filter disabled OR spread <= maximum spread fraction × stop distance) AND a valid target exists AND reward > 0 AND reward/risk >= minimum
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop distance OR high >= target price
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= target price
