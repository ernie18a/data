Calculation: upper channel boundary = highest high over the lookback bars, adjusted upward by the channel percentage of the high-low range, on the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using completed bars
Calculation: lower channel boundary = lowest low over the lookback bars, adjusted upward by the channel percentage of the high-low range, on the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using completed bars
Calculation: upper stop boundary = highest high over the lookback bars, adjusted downward by the combined channel and stop percentages of the high-low range, on the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using completed bars
Calculation: lower stop boundary = lowest low over the lookback bars, adjusted upward by the combined channel and stop percentages of the high-low range, on the indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using completed bars
Long: close crosses above the upper channel boundary
Short: close crosses below the lower channel boundary
Exit: long, close < upper stop boundary OR close < lower stop boundary OR close crosses below the lower channel boundary
Exit: short, close > upper stop boundary OR close > lower stop boundary OR close crosses above the upper channel boundary
