Calculation: Pair relative score = assign the first currency a score from 0 to 9 according to where the bar close falls within its high-low range: thresholds at 0%, 3%, 10%, 25%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 75%, 90%, and 97%; assign the second currency 9 minus the first currency's score.
Calculation: RSI = RSI on a lower timeframe, comparing its last completed bar with a completed bar three bars earlier.
