Calculation: Trailing level = On the signal timeframe, let P be the selected OHLC-derived price and k the percentage offset. Initialize the trend as up if the first processed bar closes above its open, otherwise down; initialize the level from the preceding bar's P multiplied by (1 − k/100) for an uptrend or (1 + k/100) for a downtrend. On each bar, if the previous trend is up and P < the previous level, set the trend down and the level to P × (1 + k/100); otherwise retain the uptrend and set the level to max(previous level, P × (1 − k/100)). If the previous trend is down and P > the previous level, set the trend up and the level to P × (1 − k/100); otherwise retain the downtrend and set the level to min(previous level, P × (1 + k/100)). Round each newly calculated price level to the chosen price precision.
Calculation: Oscillator = 100 × (moving average of (100 × abs(P − trailing level) / P / k)) raised to a chosen exponent. Calculate it on the chart timeframe or a chosen higher or lower timeframe; use the last completed signal-timeframe bar or the still-forming bar according to the selected signal-bar setting, and compare it with the preceding signal-timeframe bar.
Long: Entry-zone mode AND oscillator > upper threshold AND previous oscillator <= upper threshold OR exit-zone mode AND oscillator > lower threshold AND previous oscillator <= lower threshold.
Short: Entry-zone mode AND oscillator < lower threshold AND previous oscillator >= lower threshold OR exit-zone mode AND oscillator < upper threshold AND previous oscillator >= upper threshold.
Exit: long, the most recent applicable oscillator crossing is a short condition OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the most recent applicable oscillator crossing is a long condition OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
