Calculation: Currency strength (v1) = For each of the eight currencies, sum the percentage change from open to close of the last completed chart-timeframe bar across its seven pairs, adding the change when it is the base currency and subtracting it when it is the quote currency.
Calculation: Currency strength (v2) = For each currency, sum the percentage change in pair close from the older completed chart-timeframe bar to the last completed chart-timeframe bar across its seven pairs, adding for base currency and subtracting for quote currency; use the same lookback for all pairs.
Calculation: Currency strength (v3) = For each currency, average its seven pair RSI values on the last completed chart-timeframe bar, using RSI for base currency and 100 minus RSI for quote currency; use the same RSI lookback for all pairs.
Calculation: Currency strength (v4) = For each currency, combine seven ratios of pair LWMA(close) on the last completed chart-timeframe bar to that LWMA on an older completed chart-timeframe bar; use reciprocal ratios for quote currency and direct ratios for base currency, then subtract 7. The LWMA period is the same for all pairs and the older bar uses a longer lookback.
Calculation: Strongest/weakest currency = A currency is strongest only when its score is strictly greater than all seven other scores, and weakest only when strictly less than all seven.
Long: Absolute difference between current price and the indicated prior bar close exceeds the retracement threshold AND fast LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe (still-forming bar) > slow LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe (still-forming bar) AND low[2] < high[1] AND long currency-strength signal is true. The fast LWMA has a shorter period than the slow LWMA. For v4 only, also require higher-timeframe momentum (still-forming bar) < 100 AND at least one of the absolute differences from 100 for momentum on the last three completed higher-timeframe bars exceeds the momentum threshold. The long currency-strength signal is true when a supported chart pair's base currency is strictly strongest and its quote currency strictly weakest; additionally, on GBPUSD it is true when AUD is strongest and USD weakest.
Short: Absolute difference between current price and the indicated prior bar close exceeds the retracement threshold AND fast LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe (still-forming bar) < slow LWMA of typical price on the chart timeframe (still-forming bar) AND low[1] < high[2] AND short currency-strength signal is true. The fast LWMA has a shorter period than the slow LWMA. The short currency-strength signal is true when a supported chart pair's base currency is strictly weakest and its quote currency strictly strongest; additionally, on AUDJPY it is true when AUD is weakest and USD strongest. Supported pairs are EURJPY, EURGBP, EURAUD, EURNZD, EURUSD, EURCAD, EURCHF, GBPAUD, GBPNZD, GBPUSD, GBPCAD, GBPCHF, GBPJPY, AUDNZD, AUDCAD, AUDCHF, AUDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD, and USDJPY.
Exit: long, current price <= entry price minus the initial stop distance OR current price >= entry price plus the initial take-profit distance OR chart-timeframe MACD main (still-forming bar) > 0 AND MACD main < MACD signal (still-forming bar) AND prior-bar MACD main > prior-bar MACD signal AND MACD main > the close threshold.
Exit: short, current price >= entry price plus the initial stop distance OR current price <= entry price minus the initial take-profit distance OR chart-timeframe MACD main (still-forming bar) < 0 AND MACD main > MACD signal (still-forming bar) AND prior-bar MACD main < prior-bar MACD signal AND absolute MACD main > the close threshold.
