Calculation: Osc = Commodity Channel Index of close (alternatively RSI of close, or the percent range of CCI or RSI: 100*(x - lowest x)/(highest x - lowest x) over a lookback, 50 if range is 0), computed on the chart timeframe
Long: Osc on the current bar >= Osc on the previous bar
Short: Osc on the current bar < Osc on the previous bar
