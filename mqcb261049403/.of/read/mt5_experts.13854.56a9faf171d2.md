Calculation: Ultra-linear filter = for input x and length n, set k=3/(n+2); initialize six states to the first x; on each bar update sequentially a1=(1-k)*a1+k*x, a2=k*a1+(1-k)*a2, b1=(1-k)*b1+k*(1.5*a1-0.5*a2), b2=k*b1+(1-k)*b2, c1=(1-k)*c1+k*(1.5*b1-0.5*b2), c2=k*c1+(1-k)*c2; output 1.5*c1-0.5*c2.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = Jurik moving average of the ratio of the ultra-linear filter of the selected price change to the ultra-linear filter of its absolute value, using the same filter length; clamp the ratio to [-1,1] and treat a zero denominator as unavailable.
Calculation: Composite oscillator = weighted sum of five smoothed oscillators with successively longer filter lengths and common Jurik moving average parameters; the second and third oscillators use the same weight, while the other weights are independent.
Calculation: Signal line = composite oscillator divided by a smoothing length plus the preceding signal line multiplied by (smoothing length minus one) divided by smoothing length; initialize it to the first composite oscillator divided by the smoothing length.
Long: Composite oscillator > signal line.
Short: Composite oscillator < signal line.
Long: On the selected indicator timeframe, the composite oscillator on the selected signal bar > its signal line AND the composite oscillator on the preceding indicator bar < its signal line. The indicator timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar by default, or the forming bar when configured.
Short: On the selected indicator timeframe, the composite oscillator on the selected signal bar < its signal line AND the composite oscillator on the preceding indicator bar > its signal line. The indicator timeframe may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe; use the last completed indicator bar by default, or the forming bar when configured.
Exit: long, composite oscillator on the selected indicator timeframe and signal bar < its signal line OR low <= entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, composite oscillator on the selected indicator timeframe and signal bar > its signal line OR high >= entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
