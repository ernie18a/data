Calculation: OMA = Apply a six-stage smoothed average to the selected price. At each bar, use alpha=(2+speed)/(1+speed+period). If adaptive, set period from the ratio of absolute price change over the lookback to the sum of absolute price differences from the current price over that lookback, scaling the ratio linearly between half the base period and 2.5 times the base period; otherwise use the base period. Recursively compute e1=prev_e1+alpha*(price-prev_e1), e2=prev_e2+alpha*(e1-prev_e2), v1=1.5*e1-0.5*e2; then e3/e4 from v1/e3 and v2=1.5*e3-0.5*e4; then e5/e6 from v2/e5, returning 1.5*e5-0.5*e6. When calculated on a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar and interpolate the average onto chart bars.
Long: OMA > OMA on the prior chart bar
Short: OMA < OMA on the prior chart bar
