Calculation: Power = high + low - 2 × EMA(close), with the EMA using the indicator lookback.
Calculation: Laguerre oscillator = 100 × U / (U + D) - 50, or -50 when U + D = 0; initialize L0, L1, L2 and L3 to zero, then on each bar set L0 = (1 - smoothing factor) × Power + smoothing factor × previous L0, L1 = -smoothing factor × L0 + previous L0 + smoothing factor × previous L1, L2 = -smoothing factor × L1 + previous L1 + smoothing factor × previous L2, and L3 = -smoothing factor × L2 + previous L2 + smoothing factor × previous L3; U is the sum of positive differences L0 - L1, L1 - L2 and L2 - L3, and D is the sum of the absolute values of their negative differences.
Calculation: Smoothed oscillator = selected moving average of Laguerre oscillator × volume; smoothed volume = the same moving average with the same parameters applied to volume.
Long: Smoothed oscillator > upper overbought level × smoothed volume OR smoothed oscillator > lower overbought level × smoothed volume.
Short: Smoothed oscillator <= upper overbought level × smoothed volume AND smoothed oscillator <= lower overbought level × smoothed volume AND (smoothed oscillator < lower oversold level × smoothed volume OR smoothed oscillator < upper oversold level × smoothed volume).
