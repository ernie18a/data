Calculation: Trailing stop = when enabled, update the long stop to current price minus trailing distance if current price minus entry price > trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop < current price minus trailing distance minus trailing step; update the short stop to current price plus trailing distance if entry price minus current price > trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop > current price plus trailing distance plus trailing step OR no stop exists. Updates are evaluated on every tick, whose order and prices cannot be reconstructed from OHLC bars.
Long: At the opening of a chart bar, the higher-timeframe MACD main line on the forming bar and each of the two preceding completed bars < 0 AND the forming value > the last completed value AND the last completed value < the preceding value AND the chart-timeframe stochastic main line on the forming bar < the lower threshold.
Short: At the opening of a chart bar, the higher-timeframe MACD main line on the forming bar and each of the two preceding completed bars > 0 AND the forming value < the last completed value AND the last completed value > the preceding value AND the chart-timeframe stochastic main line on the forming bar > the upper threshold.
Exit: long, price <= the active stop loss OR price >= entry price plus the enabled long take-profit distance.
Exit: short, price >= the active stop loss OR price <= entry price minus the enabled short take-profit distance.
Calculation: Active stop loss = the enabled initial stop at entry price minus the long stop-loss distance for a long position or entry price plus the short stop-loss distance for a short position, subsequently modified by the trailing stop when enabled.
