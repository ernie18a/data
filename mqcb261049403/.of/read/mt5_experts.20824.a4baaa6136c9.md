Long: No open position AND ( (last completed chart bar is bullish, its high-to-close wick is >2 times its body, its open-to-low wick is <0.15 times its body, and the preceding chart bar is bearish) OR (last completed chart bar is bullish, preceding chart bar is bearish, the bullish body covers the preceding body, and its body is >1.5 times the preceding body; when the higher-timeframe trend filter is enabled, its last completed bar must be bullish) OR (last completed chart bar is bullish, preceding chart bar is bearish with body >0.6 times its range, the current open is below the preceding low, and the current close is above the midpoint of the preceding body; when the higher-timeframe trend filter is enabled, its last completed bar must be bullish) OR (the chart bar two bars ago is bearish with body >0.8 times its range; the next bar is bullish, closes below the first bar's close, and has body <0.3 times its range; the last completed bar is bullish with body >0.8 times its range, opens above the middle bar's close, and closes within 0.1 times the first bar's body of its open) OR (the chart bar two bars ago is bearish, the next bar is a doji closing at or below the first bar's close, the last completed bar is bullish and opens at or above the doji's close, and the last bar's open and close are within 0.1 times the first bar's body of the first bar's open and close, respectively))
Short: No open position AND ( (last completed chart bar is bearish, its high-to-open wick is <0.15 times its body, its close-to-low wick is >2 times its body, and the preceding chart bar is bullish; the higher-timeframe trend filter must be enabled and its last completed bar must be bearish or doji) OR (last completed chart bar is bearish, the preceding chart bar is bullish, the current body covers the preceding body, and its body is >1.5 times the preceding body; the higher-timeframe trend filter must be enabled and its last completed bar must be bearish or doji) OR (last completed chart bar is bearish, preceding chart bar is bullish with body >0.6 times its range, the current open is above the preceding high, and the current close is below the midpoint of the preceding body; the higher-timeframe trend filter must be enabled and its last completed bar must be bearish or doji) OR (the chart bar two bars ago is bullish with body >0.8 times its range; the next bar is bearish, closes above the first bar's close, and has body <0.3 times its range; the last completed bar is bearish with body >0.8 times its range, opens below the middle bar's close, and closes within 0.1 times the first bar's body of its open) OR (the chart bar two bars ago is bullish, the next bar is a doji closing at or above the first bar's close, the last completed bar is bearish and opens at or below the doji's close, and the last bar's open and close are within 0.1 times the first bar's body of the first bar's open and close, respectively))
Exit: Long, low <= active stop-loss OR high >= take-profit; the initial stop-loss and take-profit are respectively below and above entry by their configured buy distances, and the stop-loss trails at the configured distance after price has risen beyond the trailing distance plus step and the existing stop is below price minus that distance and step
Exit: Short, high >= active stop-loss OR low <= take-profit; the initial stop-loss and take-profit are respectively above and below entry by their configured sell distances, and the stop-loss trails at the configured distance after price has fallen beyond the trailing distance plus step and the existing stop is above price plus that distance and step (or no stop-loss is set)
Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close minus open; candle range = high minus low; upper wick = high minus the greater of open and close; lower wick = the lesser of open and close minus low; midpoint of body = (open + close) / 2. Chart pattern conditions use completed chart bars only; trend filter, when enabled, uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
