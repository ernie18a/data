Calculation: Bar i = the chart bar i bars before the forming bar; bar 1 is the last completed chart bar. Oi, Hi, Li and Ci are its open, high, low and close.
Calculation: Materially different(x, y) = rounding x − y to a configurable number of decimal places does not equal zero.
Calculation: Star alignment = (abs(O3 − C1) + abs(O1 − C3)) / (O3 − C3).
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the initial long stop, updated to current price minus the long trailing distance whenever current price − entry price > long trailing distance + trailing step AND the existing stop < current price − long trailing distance − trailing step.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the initial short stop, updated to current price plus the short trailing distance whenever entry price − current price > short trailing distance + trailing step AND (the existing stop > current price + short trailing distance + trailing step OR no stop exists).
Long: No position exists AND (the main-timeframe filter is disabled OR the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open < close) AND materially different(C1, O1) AND C1 > O1 AND (H1 − C1) / (C1 − O1) > upper-wick threshold AND (O1 − L1) / (C1 − O1) < lower-wick threshold AND O2 > C2.
Long: No position exists AND (the main-timeframe filter is disabled OR the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open < close) AND materially different(O2, C2) AND C1 > O1 AND O2 > C2 AND C1 >= O2 AND O1 <= C2 AND (C1 − O1) / (O2 − C2) > engulfing-body threshold.
Long: No position exists AND (the main-timeframe filter is disabled OR the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open < close) AND materially different(H2, L2) AND C1 > O1 AND O2 > C2 AND (O2 − C2) / (H2 − L2) > large-body threshold AND O1 < L2 AND C1 > (C2 + O2) / 2.
Long: No position exists AND materially different(O3, C3) AND materially different(H3, L3) AND materially different(H2, L2) AND materially different(H1, L1) AND O3 > C3 AND C2 > O2 AND C1 > O1 AND C2 < C3 AND O1 > C2 AND star alignment < alignment threshold AND (O3 − C3) / (H3 − L3) > star large-body threshold AND (C2 − O2) / (H2 − L2) < star small-body threshold AND (C1 − O1) / (H1 − L1) > star large-body threshold.
Long: No position exists AND materially different(O3, C3) AND O3 > C3 AND C2 = O2 AND C1 > O1 AND C2 <= C3 AND O1 >= C2 AND star alignment < alignment threshold.
Short: No position exists AND the main-timeframe filter is enabled AND the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open >= close AND materially different(O1, C1) AND O1 > C1 AND (H1 − O1) / (O1 − C1) < lower-wick threshold AND (C1 − L1) / (O1 − C1) > upper-wick threshold AND C2 > O2.
Short: No position exists AND the main-timeframe filter is enabled AND the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open >= close AND materially different(C2, O2) AND O1 > C1 AND O1 >= C2 AND C1 <= O2 AND C2 > O2 AND (O1 − C1) / (C2 − O2) > engulfing-body threshold.
Short: No position exists AND the main-timeframe filter is enabled AND the last completed bar of the configurable main timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, has open >= close AND materially different(H2, L2) AND O1 > C1 AND C2 > O2 AND (C2 − O2) / (H2 − L2) > large-body threshold AND O1 > H2 AND C1 < (O2 + C2) / 2.
Short: No position exists AND materially different(C3, O3) AND materially different(H3, L3) AND materially different(H1, L1) AND C3 > O3 AND O2 > C2 AND O1 > C1 AND C2 > C3 AND O1 < C2 AND −star alignment < alignment threshold AND (C3 − O3) / (H3 − L3) > star large-body threshold AND (O2 − C2) / (H2 − L2) < star small-body threshold AND (O1 − C1) / (H1 − L1) > star large-body threshold.
Short: No position exists AND materially different(O3, C3) AND C3 > O3 AND C2 = O2 AND O1 > C1 AND C2 >= C3 AND O1 <= C2 AND star alignment < alignment threshold.
Exit: Long, enabled stop loss AND price <= long trailing stop.
Exit: Long, enabled take profit AND price >= entry price + long take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, enabled stop loss AND price >= short trailing stop.
Exit: Short, enabled take profit AND price <= entry price − short take-profit distance.
