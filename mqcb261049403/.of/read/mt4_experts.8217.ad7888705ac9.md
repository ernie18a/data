Calculation: Total signal = At each new chart bar, set the total to zero. For each enabled indicator and nonzero-weight timeframe, process MACD, Awesome Oscillator, moving average of oscillator, Williams %R, and stochastic in that order, and within each indicator process timeframes from higher to lower. Use the last completed bar of each selected timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and its preceding bar where needed. For each comparison, assign +1 if greater, -1 if less, and 0 if equal. MACD contributes the average of its main line versus zero and its main line versus its signal line. Awesome Oscillator contributes the average of its value versus zero and its value versus its preceding value. Moving average of oscillator contributes its value versus zero. Williams %R contributes +1 if its latest value is above its oversold threshold and its preceding value is at or below that threshold, -1 if its latest value is below its overbought threshold and its preceding value is at or above that threshold, and 0 otherwise. Stochastic contributes the average of the corresponding threshold-crossing score for %K and the score for %K versus %D. Multiply each contribution by its timeframe weight, add it to the running total, and truncate the running total toward zero after each addition. MACD and moving average of oscillator use a shorter and a longer moving-average period.
Calculation: Stop and target levels = At entry, set the long stop to entry price minus the stop distance and the long target to entry price plus the target distance; set the short stop to entry price plus the stop distance and the short target to entry price minus the target distance. A zero distance disables that level. In dynamic mode, multiply both distances at entry by the total signal times a scaling parameter for a long, or by the negative total signal times that parameter for a short. On each subsequent new chart bar with a nonzero total signal, recalculate the levels from the entry price using the current signed multiplier; retain the last levels when the total signal is zero.
Long: Total signal > 0 AND no long position is open.
Short: Total signal < 0 AND no short position is open.
Exit: long, an enabled stop level is positive AND price <= stop level OR an enabled target level is positive AND price >= target level.
Exit: short, an enabled stop level is positive AND price >= stop level OR an enabled target level is positive AND price <= target level.
