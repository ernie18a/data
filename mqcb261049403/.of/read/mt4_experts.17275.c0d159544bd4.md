Long: (major direction = left AND (higher-timeframe last-completed major-pair close > its prior rolling highest high AND USDJPY close > its prior rolling highest high OR major-pair close > its close from the configured lookback)) OR (major direction = right AND (higher-timeframe last-completed major-pair close < its prior rolling lowest low AND USDJPY close > its prior rolling highest high OR major-pair close < its close from the configured lookback)); AND the corresponding USDJPY price condition for the selected price mode; AND (entry mode = both OR entry mode = averaging AND previous chart-timeframe close < open OR entry mode = pyramiding AND previous chart-timeframe close > open); AND, when enabled, RSI, CCI, RVI, and moving-average filters all pass: on the major pair, RSI is above 50, CCI above zero, RVI main above signal, and close above moving average for left direction (reverse each comparison for right direction); on USDJPY, RSI is above 50, CCI above zero, RVI main above signal, and close above moving average. Indicator and price comparisons use the last completed bar of the higher signal timeframe. The close-lookback mode compares the last completed close with the configured earlier close; the high/low mode compares it with highs/lows in the configured earlier-bar lookback, excluding the last completed bar.
Short: (major direction = left AND (higher-timeframe last-completed major-pair close < its prior rolling lowest low AND USDJPY close < its prior rolling lowest low OR major-pair close < its close from the configured lookback)) OR (major direction = right AND (higher-timeframe last-completed major-pair close > its prior rolling highest high AND USDJPY close < its prior rolling lowest low OR major-pair close > its close from the configured lookback)); AND the corresponding USDJPY price condition for the selected price mode; AND (entry mode = both OR entry mode = averaging AND previous chart-timeframe close > open OR entry mode = pyramiding AND previous chart-timeframe close < open); AND, when enabled, RSI, CCI, RVI, and moving-average filters all pass: on the major pair, RSI is below 50, CCI below zero, RVI main below signal, and close below moving average for left direction (reverse each comparison for right direction); on USDJPY, RSI is below 50, CCI below zero, RVI main below signal, and close below moving average. Indicator and price comparisons use the last completed bar of the higher signal timeframe. The close-lookback mode compares the last completed close with the configured earlier close; the high/low mode compares it with highs/lows in the configured earlier-bar lookback, excluding the last completed bar.
Exit: long, current price <= active stop-loss or current price >= take-profit, or an opposite short signal when close-on-opposite is enabled. Initial stop-loss and take-profit are configured distances from entry, measured in pips or as multiples of ATR; break-even, profit-lock, and trailing-stop rules can raise the long stop after price advances beyond their configured distances.
Exit: short, current price >= active stop-loss or current price <= take-profit, or an opposite long signal when close-on-opposite is enabled. Initial stop-loss and take-profit are configured distances from entry, measured in pips or as multiples of ATR; break-even, profit-lock, and trailing-stop rules can lower the short stop after price declines beyond their configured distances. ATR uses the last completed bar of its configured timeframe, which may be higher, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe.
