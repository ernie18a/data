Calculation: MACD = EMA of the selected applied price using a shorter lookback minus EMA using a longer lookback; calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using the higher timeframe’s still-forming bar
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of MACD; calculated on the same timeframe and bar as MACD
Calculation: MACD midpoint = (highest MACD + lowest MACD) / 2 over a rolling lookback ending at the current bar; calculated on the same timeframe and bar as MACD
Long: MACD > MACD signal line AND MACD > MACD midpoint
Short: MACD < MACD signal line AND MACD < MACD midpoint
