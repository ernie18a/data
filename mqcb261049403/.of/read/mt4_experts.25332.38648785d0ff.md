Calculation: Evaluation price = chart bar open; evaluate entries and discretionary exits once at the start of each chart bar. Model ask as the evaluation price plus a spread parameter and bid as the evaluation price.
Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Long stop and target = initially bid at entry minus the stop distance and bid at entry plus the target distance. Short stop and target = initially ask at entry plus the stop distance and ask at entry minus the target distance.
Calculation: Candle trailing levels = at each new chart bar, use the lowest low or highest high over a lookback including the forming bar. When candle trailing is enabled, raise a long stop to the lowest low minus a padding distance, or lower a short stop to the highest high plus a padding distance, only if that improves the stop; each such update also moves the target farther from price by a trailing distance.
Calculation: Price trailing levels = when candle trailing is disabled and trailing is enabled, after bid exceeds a long entry by an activation distance, raise the long stop to bid minus a trailing distance if higher; after ask falls below a short entry by an activation distance, lower the short stop to ask plus a trailing distance if lower.
Calculation: Break-even levels = when enabled, after bid exceeds a long entry by an activation distance and the stop is below entry, raise the stop to entry plus an offset; after ask falls below a short entry by an activation distance and the stop is above entry, lower the stop to entry minus an offset.
Long: Absolute difference between bid and the close two chart bars ago > a price-distance threshold AND shorter-period linear-weighted moving average of typical price > longer-period linear-weighted moving average of typical price on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND low two chart bars ago < high of the last completed chart bar AND RSI of the forming higher-timeframe bar > RSI of the last completed bar of that same timeframe.
Short: Absolute difference between ask and the close two chart bars ago > a price-distance threshold AND shorter-period linear-weighted moving average of typical price < longer-period linear-weighted moving average of typical price on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND low of the last completed chart bar < high two chart bars ago AND RSI of the forming higher-timeframe bar < RSI of the last completed bar of that same timeframe.
Exit: long, bid <= long stop OR bid >= long target.
Exit: short, ask >= short stop OR ask <= short target.
Exit: long OR short, close of the last completed chart bar <= lower Bollinger Band of that bar OR all six short-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal OR all six longer-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal.
