Calculation: Normalized Bollinger Band width = (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band, using identical settings; compare the last completed chart-timeframe bar with the bar CandlesToRetrace bars earlier.
Long: MACD line is above or below zero AND above the MACD signal line AND Normalized Bollinger Band width on the last completed chart-timeframe bar / width CandlesToRetrace bars earlier > squeeze threshold AND short-lookback LWMA of typical price on the forming chart-timeframe bar > long-lookback LWMA of typical price on the forming chart-timeframe bar AND low two chart bars ago < high one chart bar ago AND absolute difference between current chart bar open and close two chart bars ago > candle trigger distance
Short: MACD line is above or below zero AND below the MACD signal line AND Normalized Bollinger Band width on the last completed chart-timeframe bar / width CandlesToRetrace bars earlier > squeeze threshold AND short-lookback LWMA of typical price on the forming chart-timeframe bar < long-lookback LWMA of typical price on the forming chart-timeframe bar AND low one chart bar ago < high two chart bars ago AND absolute difference between current chart bar open and close one chart bar ago > candle trigger distance
