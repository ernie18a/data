Long: Shorter-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price > longer-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND low of the second-last completed chart bar < high of the last completed chart bar AND absolute difference between the still-forming chart bar's close and the second-last completed chart bar's close > a price-distance threshold
Short: Shorter-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price < longer-lookback linear weighted moving average of typical price on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar AND low of the last completed chart bar < high of the second-last completed chart bar
Exit: long OR short, all six shorter-lookback exponential moving averages of typical price on the still-forming chart bar are equal OR all six longer-lookback exponential moving averages of typical price on the still-forming chart bar are equal OR the last completed chart bar's close <= its lower Bollinger Band
