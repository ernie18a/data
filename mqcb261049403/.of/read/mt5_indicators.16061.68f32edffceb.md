Calculation: Adaptive channel = For each bar, calculate a configurable moving average M of a configurable OHLC-derived price over a lookback N. Calculate V as the mean of (price − current M)² over the same N bars. Initialize the adaptive center L to M through the moving-average warm-up plus the configured bar shift; thereafter let D = (previous L − M)² and L = previous L + k × (M − previous L), where k = 0 if D = 0 or D < V, otherwise k = 1 − V/D. Add and subtract a configurable price offset from L; round the upper result up and the lower result down to a configurable price grid. Use the resulting boundaries from the configured number of bars earlier.

Short: Chart-timeframe close < chart-timeframe adaptive channel lower boundary OR forming higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe adaptive channel lower boundary
