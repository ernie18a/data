Calculation: Momentum trend cycle = On the chart timeframe, subtract longer-lookback price momentum from shorter-lookback price momentum. Normalize the difference to its rolling cycle-lookback minimum and maximum on a 0–100 scale, carry the previous value if the range is zero, and recursively smooth it toward the previous smoothed value. Normalize that smoothed series to its rolling cycle-lookback minimum and maximum on a -100–100 scale, carry the previous value if the range is zero, and recursively smooth it toward the previous output. Price momentum is 100 times the selected OHLC-derived price divided by its price at the lookback. Initialize the rolling buffers and previous output to zero; use the same smoothing factor for both stages.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe momentum trend cycle = Momentum trend cycle calculated from the higher timeframe's bar still forming; its displayed history can be revised as that bar changes.
Long: Momentum trend cycle >= 0 OR higher-timeframe momentum trend cycle >= 0
Short: Momentum trend cycle < 0 OR higher-timeframe momentum trend cycle < 0
