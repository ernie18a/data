Calculation: JFATL candle direction = on each indicator-timeframe bar, bullish when the JMA-smoothed 39-bar FATL of close is greater than that of open, bearish when it is less, and neutral when equal; use the last completed bar (indicator timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, or the same)
Long: JFATL candle direction on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar is bullish AND its preceding completed bar is not bullish
Short: JFATL candle direction on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar is bearish AND its preceding completed bar is not bearish
Exit: Long, JFATL candle direction on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar is bearish
Exit: Short, JFATL candle direction on the last completed indicator-timeframe bar is bullish
