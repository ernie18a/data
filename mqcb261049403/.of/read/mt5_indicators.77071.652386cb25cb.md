Calculation: Session range = high and low of completed Asian session, used during the London sweep window; or high and low of completed London session, used during the New York sweep window, with session times interpreted in UTC
Calculation: ATR = Wilder average of true range
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = bar high/low strictly above/below the corresponding highs/lows of the configured number of bars on each side; use the latest qualifying pivot before the sweep within the configured lookback
Calculation: Directional displacement = absolute difference between a bar's close and open
Calculation: Directional FVG = bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier; bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier; require gap size to meet the configured ATR threshold
Calculation: FVG midpoint = average of the FVG boundaries
Calculation: Setup score = session context points + sweep-depth points + confirmed-MSS points + displacement points + FVG-quality points + retest points + optional tick-volume bonus; award sweep-depth points by penetration relative to ATR, displacement points by directional body size relative to ATR, FVG-quality points by gap size relative to ATR, and the volume bonus when MSS-bar tick-volume z-score versus prior bars meets its threshold
Calculation: Stop = beyond the frozen sweep extreme by the configured ATR buffer, widened as needed to meet minimum ATR risk; reject if risk exceeds maximum ATR risk
Calculation: TP1 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the configured risk multiple times risk
Calculation: TP2 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the smaller of the configured risk cap times risk and the distance to opposing session liquidity
Long: On a closed analysis-timeframe bar, prior close is at or above the reference range low AND current low penetrates that low by at least the configured ATR threshold AND current high does not penetrate the range high farther; within the configured wait, a later bar closes above the latest confirmed swing high and above its open with directional body at least the configured ATR threshold; within the configured wait, a bullish directional FVG forms; a still later bar overlaps that FVG within configured ATR tolerance and closes above its midpoint; no post-MSS bar breaches the frozen sweep low; setup score meets the configured viable threshold; projected risk is within configured bounds AND opposing-liquidity distance is positive AND TP2 distance divided by risk meets the configured minimum R:R AND TP1 distance is less than TP2 distance
Short: On a closed analysis-timeframe bar, prior close is at or below the reference range high AND current high penetrates that high by at least the configured ATR threshold AND current low does not penetrate the range low farther; within the configured wait, a later bar closes below the latest confirmed swing low and below its open with directional body at least the configured ATR threshold; within the configured wait, a bearish directional FVG forms; a still later bar overlaps that FVG within configured ATR tolerance and closes below its midpoint; no post-MSS bar breaches the frozen sweep high; setup score meets the configured viable threshold; projected risk is within configured bounds AND opposing-liquidity distance is positive AND TP2 distance divided by risk meets the configured minimum R:R AND TP1 distance is less than TP2 distance
Exit: Long, low <= Stop OR high >= TP1 OR high >= TP2
Exit: Short, high >= Stop OR low <= TP1 OR low <= TP2
