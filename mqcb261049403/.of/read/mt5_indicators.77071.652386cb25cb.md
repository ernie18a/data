Calculation: Analysis bars = closed bars on a configurable timeframe that may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower; use the last completed analysis bar and process every completed analysis bar in chronological order.
Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low of completed analysis bars whose opening times fall within the configured UTC session window on the same UTC date. During the first scan window use the preceding Asian range; during the second scan window use the preceding London range.
Calculation: Sweep = a bar in a scan window whose previous close is at or above the session low and whose low penetrates that low by at least a configured multiple of its current ATR, or whose previous close is at or below the session high and whose high penetrates that high by at least that multiple. If both occur, select the greater penetration, choosing the high on a tie. Freeze the session range and sweep-bar ATR. Allow only one active candidate at a time; do not start another candidate on a bar that processed an active candidate, or after a successful projection for the same date, session and swept side.
Calculation: Structure level = the most recent pivot within a configured lookback before the sweep: a pivot high is strictly higher than the configured number of bars on each side, and a pivot low is strictly lower. Its confirming bars must precede the sweep. For a low sweep select a pivot high above the sweep close; for a high sweep select a pivot low below the sweep close. If none exists, the candidate ends at the sweep.
Calculation: Confirmed sequence = after a sweep with a structure level, track the lowest low for a low sweep or highest high for a high sweep through the structure-shift bar. Within the configured maximum wait, a later bar must close beyond the structure level in the direction opposite the sweep, have a body in that direction, and have an absolute body at least a configured multiple of its ATR. Freeze the tracked extreme at that bar. On that bar or within the configured subsequent wait, the first directional three-bar gap must have size at least a configured multiple of its own bar's ATR: for a low sweep the gap runs from the high two bars earlier to the current low; for a high sweep it runs from the current high to the low two bars earlier. A later bar within the configured retest wait must overlap the gap expanded on both sides by a configured multiple of its ATR and close beyond the gap midpoint in the direction opposite the sweep. After the structure-shift bar through the retest, every low must be at or above the frozen extreme for a low sweep, or every high must be at or below it for a high sweep. Check for the required event before expiring a wait on its final bar; check invalidation before a subsequent gap or retest. End the candidate at its first retest, invalidation, or expiry.
Calculation: Quality score = configured fixed session, structure-shift and retest points plus sweep-depth points selected by frozen penetration divided by sweep-bar ATR, displacement points selected by structure-shift body divided by its ATR, gap-quality points selected by gap size divided by sweep-bar ATR, and optional volume points selected by the structure-shift bar's volume z-score against the preceding volume lookback. Use the higher point award when each configured threshold is met; otherwise use its lower award. The volume z-score uses population standard deviation and is zero if fewer than the configured minimum observations exist or the deviation is zero. Clamp the sum to its configured score bounds.
Calculation: Projection = entry is the retest close; initial stop is the frozen extreme minus an ATR buffer for a low sweep or plus it for a high sweep, using sweep-bar ATR. If absolute entry-to-stop distance is below the configured minimum ATR risk, move the stop to that minimum distance on the protective side of entry. Risk is the resulting absolute distance. The opposing-liquidity distance is session high minus entry for a low sweep or entry minus session low for a high sweep. Target distance is the smaller of opposing-liquidity distance and a configured maximum risk multiple. The first target is entry plus or minus a smaller configured risk multiple; the second target is entry plus or minus target distance, with the sign positive for a low sweep and negative for a high sweep.
Long: A selected sweep is below the session low AND no candidate was active at the start of the bar AND no successful projection exists for that date, session and swept side.
Short: A selected sweep is above the session high AND no candidate was active at the start of the bar AND no successful projection exists for that date, session and swept side.
Long: A low-sweep confirmed sequence completes AND its quality score is at least the configured viability threshold AND projection risk exceeds the configured minimum price increment and is at most the configured maximum multiple of sweep-bar ATR AND opposing-liquidity distance is positive AND target distance divided by risk is at least the configured minimum ratio AND the first-target distance is less than target distance.
Short: A high-sweep confirmed sequence completes AND its quality score is at least the configured viability threshold AND projection risk exceeds the configured minimum price increment and is at most the configured maximum multiple of sweep-bar ATR AND opposing-liquidity distance is positive AND target distance divided by risk is at least the configured minimum ratio AND the first-target distance is less than target distance.
Exit: long, a subsequent analysis bar's low is at or below the projection stop OR its high is at or above the first or second projection target.
Exit: short, a subsequent analysis bar's high is at or above the projection stop OR its low is at or below the first or second projection target.
