Calculation: Higher-timeframe short-term EMA = Exponential moving average of close on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; current value uses the bar still forming, prior value uses the last completed bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe long-term EMA = Exponential moving average of close on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe; current value uses the bar still forming, prior value uses the last completed bar
Long: prior short-term EMA is below the longer-lookback long-term EMA AND current short-term EMA is above that EMA
Short: prior short-term EMA is above the shorter-lookback long-term EMA AND current short-term EMA is below that EMA
Exit: long, low is at or below the active stop price OR high is at or above entry price plus the take-profit distance
Exit: short, high is at or above the active stop price OR low is at or below entry price minus the take-profit distance
