Calculation: Rolling low and rolling high = the lowest low and highest high over the range lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Tango center = track the most recent bottom and top reversal bars, initially the first bar of the first complete range window. A bottom reversal at the previous bar occurs when the rolling low two bars ago minus a price-distance noise threshold is greater than the previous rolling low, and the previous rolling low equals the current rolling low. A top reversal occurs when the rolling high two bars ago plus that threshold is less than the previous rolling high, and the previous rolling high equals the current rolling high. From the later of the most recent reversal bar and the start of the current range window through the current bar, average the highest high and lowest low. Take the simple moving average of this midpoint over a lookback equal to the range lookback divided by two and rounded to the nearest integer.
Calculation: Bounce signal = over the slowing lookback including the current bar, sum (close − low) × (2 − low / current Tango center) for bars whose close is at least their previous close minus 0.2 × their high-low range; separately sum (high − close) × (2 − high / current Tango center) for bars whose close is at most their previous close plus 0.2 × their high-low range. Divide each sum by the slowing lookback to obtain positive and negative strength. If their sum is nonzero, the signal is (positive strength − negative strength) × abs((positive strength − negative strength) / (positive strength + negative strength)); otherwise it is zero.
Calculation: Trend strength = sum the bounce signal over the most recent bars up to both the range lookback and the average lookback, then divide by the average lookback.
Long: Trend strength > 0
Short: Trend strength < 0
