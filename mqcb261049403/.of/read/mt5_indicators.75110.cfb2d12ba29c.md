Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is greater than every high in the preceding lookback bars and greater than or equal to every high in the following lookahead bars; confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is less than every low in the preceding lookback bars and less than or equal to every low in the following lookahead bars. Both lookback and lookahead use the same number of chart-timeframe bars; a pivot is used only once its following bars are complete.
Calculation: Weighted pivot center = latest confirmed pivot high, if present, otherwise latest confirmed pivot low; when a new pivot is confirmed, set the center to that pivot if no prior center exists, otherwise set it to (2 × prior center + pivot) / 3; otherwise retain the prior center.
Calculation: Raw upper trail = weighted pivot center − ATR × multiplier; raw lower trail = weighted pivot center + ATR × multiplier. Upper trail = max(raw upper trail, prior upper trail) if prior close > prior upper trail, otherwise raw upper trail. Lower trail = min(raw lower trail, prior lower trail) if prior close < prior lower trail, otherwise raw lower trail.
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish if current close > prior lower trail; otherwise bearish if current close < prior upper trail; otherwise retain prior direction. Initial direction is bullish.
Long: trend direction changes from bearish to bullish
Short: trend direction changes from bullish to bearish
