Calculation: Short-term Ichimoku XMA = smooth the midpoint of the High at the highest selected OHLC value and the Low at the lowest selected OHLC value over their respective lookbacks; use shorter lookbacks and smoothing than for the long-term line.
Calculation: Long-term Ichimoku XMA = the same calculation with longer lookbacks and smoothing.
Calculation: Oscillator = short-term Ichimoku XMA minus long-term Ichimoku XMA, evaluated on the configured indicator timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use only completed bars.
Long: Oscillator > its value one bar earlier AND its value one bar earlier < its value two bars earlier.
Short: Oscillator < its value one bar earlier AND its value one bar earlier > its value two bars earlier.
Exit: Long, Oscillator < its value one bar earlier.
Exit: Short, Oscillator > its value one bar earlier.
