Calculation: WATR bands = On the chart timeframe, let the offset be a fixed price offset plus a multiplier times ATR. Initialize the previous upper band to the previous close minus the fixed price offset. For each subsequent bar, define the prior trend as up if the previous upper band exists and the previous close is above it; otherwise define it as down. If the prior trend is up and close < open, or the prior trend is down and close > open, copy both previous bands. Otherwise, if the prior trend is up, set the upper band to max(close - offset, previous upper band) and remove the lower band; if the prior trend is down, set the lower band to min(close + offset, previous lower band) and remove the upper band, treating a missing previous lower band as positive infinity. Then, if the prior trend is up and close < upper band, set the lower band to close + offset and remove the upper band; if the prior trend is down and close > lower band, set the upper band to close - offset and remove the lower band. A removed band does not exist.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe WATR bands = Apply the same WATR band calculation to the bar still forming on a higher timeframe, using parameters independent of the chart-timeframe calculation.
Long: Previous upper WATR band does not exist AND current upper WATR band exists OR current chart bar is the first bar of a higher-timeframe bar AND previous higher-timeframe upper WATR band does not exist AND current higher-timeframe upper WATR band exists
Short: Previous lower WATR band does not exist AND current lower WATR band exists OR current chart bar is the first bar of a higher-timeframe bar AND previous higher-timeframe lower WATR band does not exist AND current higher-timeframe lower WATR band exists
