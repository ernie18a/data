Calculation: WATR upper and lower levels = ATR-offset trailing levels, where offset is ATR multiplied by a multiplier plus a fixed price-point offset; on an uptrend bar, set the upper level to the greater of its prior level and close minus the offset and clear the lower level; on a downtrend bar, set the lower level to the lesser of its prior level and close plus the offset and clear the upper level. An opposing-color candle carries both prior levels forward. If close crosses the active level, clear it and set the opposite level to close plus or minus the offset. Trend is up when the previous close is above the previous upper level; otherwise it is down. ATR and WATR are calculated on the chart timeframe, or on the selected timeframe (same as or higher than the chart), using its current bar, which may still be forming.
Long: WATR upper level is active on this bar AND was inactive on the previous bar
Short: WATR lower level is active on this bar AND was inactive on the previous bar
