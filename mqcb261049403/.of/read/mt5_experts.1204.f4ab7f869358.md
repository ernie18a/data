Calculation: Double-smoothed price = apply a configurable moving average to a selected OHLC-derived price, then apply a separately configured moving average to that result; the two smoothing periods and methods are independent.
Calculation: Smoothed parabolic = on the double-smoothed price M, set synthetic low L = M minus a configurable price increment and synthetic high H = M plus that increment. Initialize direction down, acceleration A = 0, extreme E = close immediately before the first calculation bar, last high = 0, last low = a large positive value, and previous parabolic = 0. On each bar calculate candidate P = previous parabolic + A × (E − previous parabolic). In an up direction, first increase A by the low-side step if E < H and the increase does not exceed the low-side maximum; if P >= L, switch down, set A to the low-side step, E and last low to L, and P to max(H, last high). Otherwise, increase A again by the low-side step if E < L and the increase does not exceed the low-side maximum; if E < H, set E and last high to H. In a down direction, first increase A by the high-side step if E > L and the increase does not exceed the high-side maximum; if P <= H, switch up, set A to the high-side step, E and last high to H, and P to min(L, last low). Otherwise, increase A again by the high-side step if E > H and the increase does not exceed the high-side maximum; if E > L, set E and last low to L. Store P for the next bar. The high-side and low-side steps and maxima are independent parameters.
Calculation: Signal bar = the chart bar for indicator markings; for trading, use a configurable indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, and a configurable bar offset: the last completed bar by default, or the still-forming bar or an older completed bar if selected.
Long: double-smoothed price on the signal bar > double-smoothed price on the preceding signal-timeframe bar.
Short: double-smoothed price on the signal bar < double-smoothed price on the preceding signal-timeframe bar.
Long: double-smoothed price on the preceding signal-timeframe bar < its smoothed parabolic AND double-smoothed price on the signal bar >= its smoothed parabolic AND both corresponding parabolic values > 0.
Short: double-smoothed price on the preceding signal-timeframe bar >= its smoothed parabolic AND double-smoothed price on the signal bar < its smoothed parabolic AND both corresponding parabolic values > 0.
Exit: long, the most recent smoothed-parabolic crossing signal through the selected signal bar is short OR low <= entry price minus the enabled stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price plus the enabled take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the most recent smoothed-parabolic crossing signal through the selected signal bar is long OR high >= entry price plus the enabled stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price minus the enabled take-profit distance.
