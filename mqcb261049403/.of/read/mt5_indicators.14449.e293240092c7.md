Calculation: Step RSI = For each step size, calculate RSI on the selected price. Initialize the previous lower bound to +999999, the previous upper bound to -999999, and trend to 0. On each bar, set lower bound to RSI minus twice the step size and upper bound to RSI plus twice the step size. If RSI is greater than the previous upper bound, set trend to +1; if RSI is less than the previous lower bound, set trend to -1. When trend is +1, raise the lower bound to the previous lower bound if it is lower; when trend is -1, lower the upper bound to the previous upper bound if it is higher. The step RSI is the lower bound plus the step size when trend is +1, or the upper bound minus the step size when trend is -1. Carry the bounds and trend forward; the step RSI is undefined while trend is 0.
