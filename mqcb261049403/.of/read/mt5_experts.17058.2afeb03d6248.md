Calculation: Breakout levels = at the configured order-placement time, fix the buy level at the higher-timeframe day’s high plus a price offset and the sell level at its low minus the same offset; use the last completed day if the placement time is the start of the day, otherwise use the day still forming. Keep the levels until the end of that day.
Calculation: Stop level = initially entry price minus the stop-loss distance for a long position, or entry price plus the stop-loss distance for a short position. If trailing is enabled, update it to price minus the trailing distance for a long, or price plus the trailing distance for a short, only when the new level is more protective and beyond entry price. If the one-time no-loss adjustment is enabled and the stop has not yet reached entry price, apply the same update using the no-loss distance; do not apply it again once the stop reaches or passes entry price.
Long: Price >= buy breakout level AND the order-placement time has occurred AND the current day has not ended AND the day's sell order has not triggered AND no buy order has previously been placed that day.
Short: Price <= sell breakout level AND the order-placement time has occurred AND the current day has not ended AND the day's buy order has not triggered AND no sell order has previously been placed that day.
Exit: long, price <= stop level OR price >= entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: short, price >= stop level OR price <= entry price minus the take-profit distance.
