Calculation: Source = configurable open, high, low, close, (high + low) / 2, or (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback; use zero if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Effective ATR = ATR multiplied by (0.5 + 0.5 × efficiency ratio) when efficiency weighting is enabled; otherwise ATR.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (current volume − mean volume) / population standard deviation of volume over its lookback; use zero if the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Trend quality = the weighted mean of efficiency ratio; a volatility component; a structure component; and a momentum component, clamped to [0, 1]. The volatility component is volume z-score linearly mapped and clamped from configurable lower and upper bounds to [0, 1] when current volume is positive; otherwise use the ratio of ATR to the moving average of a longer-period ATR, mapped and clamped from separate bounds to [0, 1]. The structure component is twice the absolute distance of the source's position within the lookback's highest-high to lowest-low range from 0.5, clamped to [0, 1]; use position 0.5 for a zero range. The momentum component is the fraction of consecutive source changes in its lookback whose sign matches the total source change over that lookback. If trend quality is disabled, use 0.5.
Calculation: Adaptive bands = on each bar, multiply the base band multiplier by 1 + adaptation strength × (0.5 − efficiency ratio) if adaptation is enabled, otherwise by 1; then multiply by 1 − quality influence + quality influence × (0.6 + 0.8 × quality deviation), where quality deviation is (1 − trend quality) raised to the quality-curve power when quality is enabled and 0.5 otherwise. If asymmetric bands and quality are enabled, multiply this result by 1 − asymmetry strength × trend quality × 0.3 for the active multiplier and by 1 + asymmetry strength × trend quality × 0.4 for the passive multiplier; otherwise both equal the result. Optionally exponentially smooth each multiplier, initializing it to its first raw value. In the previous uptrend use the active multiplier for the lower band and passive for the upper; in the previous downtrend reverse them. Raw lower and upper bands are source minus and plus their respective multiplier × effective ATR. Initialize each band to its raw value. Thereafter, if previous close is above the previous lower band, lower band is the maximum of raw and previous lower bands, otherwise raw; if previous close is below the previous upper band, upper band is the minimum of raw and previous upper bands, otherwise raw.
Calculation: Trend state = initialize up. On each bar, change from down to up if close is above the current upper adaptive band, or if character-flip detection and trend quality are enabled, previous trend quality is above its high threshold, current trend quality is below its low threshold, trend age is at least its minimum, and close is above source. Change from up to down if close is below the current lower adaptive band, or under the same character-flip conditions if close is below source. Otherwise retain the previous state. Trend age is bars since the last state change.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high strictly greater than, or a low strictly less than, all highs or lows respectively in the same configurable number of bars on both sides; recognize it only after the right-side bars have completed. At entry retain the most recently confirmed pivot low for a long or pivot high for a short; if none exists use the entry bar's low or high respectively.
Calculation: Initial risk levels = at the entry bar close, set the long stop to min(retained pivot low, entry price) − stop multiplier × entry-bar effective ATR, or the short stop to max(retained pivot high, entry price) + stop multiplier × entry-bar effective ATR. Initial risk is the absolute entry-to-stop distance. Set three targets at entry price plus initial risk × each target multiplier for a long, or minus it for a short; order the target multipliers from smallest to largest. Each target closes one third of the initial position.
Calculation: Quality influence = initialize to its configured value. If auto-calibration is enabled, after a tracked trade closes and at least the calibration lookback of completed trade results and the cooldown number of completed trades are available, compare their average realized R with the bad and good thresholds. Below the bad threshold, subtract one calibration step if quality influence is above its initial value, otherwise add one step; clamp it to its configured bounds and reset the cooldown. Above the good threshold, reset the cooldown without changing quality influence. Calculate realized R as one third of each reached target's R multiple, plus −1 R for each remaining third if stopped; on timeout, remaining thirds contribute zero. A new signal replaces the tracked trade before hit detection on that bar.
Long: The closed chart bar's trend state changes from down to up AND the warmup period has completed.
Short: The closed chart bar's trend state changes from up to down AND the warmup period has completed.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= initial stop OR high >= an unreached target OR trade age >= timeout.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= initial stop OR low <= an unreached target OR trade age >= timeout.
