Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend bands = ATR bands around the selected price source, with width adjusted by the Kaufman Efficiency Ratio and a Trend Quality Index (weighted composite of efficiency, volatility regime, market structure, and momentum persistence), then ratcheted: raise the lower band when the prior close is above it; lower the upper band when the prior close is below it.
Calculation: Initial risk = for a long, entry close minus the lower of (latest confirmed pivot low minus ATR-based stop distance) and (entry close minus ATR-based stop distance); for a short, the higher of (latest confirmed pivot high plus ATR-based stop distance) and (entry close plus ATR-based stop distance), minus entry close. If no pivot exists, use the signal bar low for a long or high for a short.
Long: On a warmed-up, completed chart bar, prior trend is short AND (close > upper band OR (previous TQI > its high threshold AND current TQI < its low threshold AND trend age meets its minimum AND close > selected price source)).
Short: On a warmed-up, completed chart bar, prior trend is long AND (close < lower band OR (previous TQI > its high threshold AND current TQI < its low threshold AND trend age meets its minimum AND close < selected price source)).
Exit: long, completed bar high >= entry price + initial risk × third take-profit risk multiple OR completed bar low <= stop price OR bars since entry >= trade timeout.
Exit: short, completed bar low <= entry price − initial risk × third take-profit risk multiple OR completed bar high >= stop price OR bars since entry >= trade timeout.
