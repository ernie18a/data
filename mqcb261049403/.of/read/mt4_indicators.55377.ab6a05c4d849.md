Calculation: Donchian upper/lower bands = over the configured lookback on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, use the bar containing each chart bar (which may still be forming); select respectively the highest high and lowest low, the averages of highest high with highest open and lowest low with lowest open, the averages of highest high with highest close and lowest low with lowest close, the highest open and lowest open, or the highest close and lowest close.
Calculation: Resistance/support inner boundaries = over the same lookback and timeframe, use respectively the low at the highest low and the high at the lowest high; for the two averaged price modes, average those values respectively with the open or close at the highest open or close and lowest open or close; for the highest-open/lowest-open and highest-close/lowest-close modes, use the low at the highest low and the high at the lowest high.
Calculation: Donchian midline = average of the upper and lower bands.
Long: candle open < Donchian midline AND candle close > Donchian midline OR candle open < resistance inner boundary AND candle close > resistance inner boundary AND candle close < Donchian upper band
Short: candle open > Donchian midline AND candle close < Donchian midline OR candle open > support inner boundary AND candle close < support inner boundary AND candle close > Donchian lower band
