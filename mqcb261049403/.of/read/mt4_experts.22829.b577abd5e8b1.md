Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = linear weighted moving average of (high + low + close) / 3 on the chart timeframe's bar still forming; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Retrace threshold = configured candle count × price point size.
Long: Stochastic main line on the chart timeframe's last completed bar >= 20 AND Stochastic main line on the preceding completed bar < 20 AND Parabolic SAR on the chart timeframe's bar still forming < the previous bar's open AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND low two bars ago < high on the previous bar AND |current bar open - close two bars ago| > retrace threshold
Short: Stochastic main line on the chart timeframe's last completed bar <= 80 AND Stochastic main line on the preceding completed bar > 80 AND Parabolic SAR on the chart timeframe's bar still forming > the previous bar's open AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND low on the previous bar < high two bars ago AND |current bar open - close on the previous bar| > retrace threshold
Exit: Long, price <= entry price - configured stop-loss distance OR price >= entry price + configured take-profit distance OR (price > entry price + configured break-even activation distance AND price <= entry price + configured break-even offset)
Exit: Short, price >= entry price + configured stop-loss distance OR price <= entry price - configured take-profit distance OR (price < entry price - configured break-even activation distance AND price >= entry price - configured break-even offset)
