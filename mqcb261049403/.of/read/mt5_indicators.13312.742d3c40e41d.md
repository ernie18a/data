Calculation: Applied price = a chosen price series: open, high, low, close, (high + low) / 2, (high + low + close) / 3, (high + low + 2 × close) / 4, (open + close) / 2, (open + high + low + close) / 4, trend-following price, or DeMark price. Trend-following price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise; an alternative trend-following price is (high + close) / 2 when close > open, (low + close) / 2 when close < open, and close otherwise. DeMark price is (X − high − low) / 2, where X is (high + low + close + low) / 2 when close < open, (high + low + close + high) / 2 when close > open, and (high + low + 2 × close) / 2 otherwise.
Calculation: Filter difference = a weighted sum of applied prices from the current bar backward, minus a longer weighted sum of applied prices from the current bar backward. The longer sum uses independent weights for its most recent bars, followed by a scaled copy of all the shorter sum's weights. The weights and scale are fixed calculation parameters. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the higher timeframe's bar still forming; in the latter case compare it with the previous completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Filter difference > previous filter difference
Short: Filter difference < previous filter difference
