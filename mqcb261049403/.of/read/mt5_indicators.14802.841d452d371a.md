Calculation: Noise-reduced stochastic = For each bar in a slowing window, find the highest high and lowest low over a lookback ending at that bar, or the highest and lowest close over that lookback if close-only prices are selected. Sum the highs, lows, and closes separately across the slowing window. If the difference between the summed highs and lows is less than a sensitivity threshold in price units, add half the threshold to the summed highs and subtract half from the summed lows. Return 100 times (summed closes minus adjusted summed lows) divided by (adjusted summed highs minus adjusted summed lows); return 100 if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Stochastic signal = Either an EMA of the noise-reduced stochastic, initialized at 50, or a rolling SMA computed recursively as previous signal plus (current stochastic minus stochastic one signal-period ago) divided by the signal period, initialized with signal and preceding stochastic values at 50. Calculate the stochastic and signal on either the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; for the higher timeframe use the bar still forming, with prior values referring to higher-timeframe bars.
Long: Noise-reduced stochastic is not 50 AND noise-reduced stochastic > previous noise-reduced stochastic
Long: Noise-reduced stochastic > stochastic signal
Short: Noise-reduced stochastic is not 50 AND noise-reduced stochastic < previous noise-reduced stochastic
Short: Noise-reduced stochastic < stochastic signal
