Calculation: Short-lookback moving average = moving average on the chart timeframe’s still-forming bar; its lookback is shorter than the long-lookback moving average.
Calculation: Long-lookback moving average = moving average on the chart timeframe’s still-forming bar; its lookback is longer than the short-lookback moving average.
Calculation: Standard deviation = standard deviation of price over a lookback, evaluated on the chart timeframe’s still-forming bar and last completed bar.
Long: short-lookback moving average > long-lookback moving average AND last completed bar close > open AND last completed bar close > long-lookback moving average AND current-bar standard deviation > last-completed-bar standard deviation
Short: short-lookback moving average < long-lookback moving average AND last completed bar close < open AND last completed bar close < long-lookback moving average AND current-bar standard deviation > last-completed-bar standard deviation
Exit: long, short condition
Exit: short, long condition
