Long: No open position AND permitted weekday AND outside the initial daily no-entry interval AND elapsed time within the current entry cycle exceeds the entry delay AND OsMA is rising on at least the required number of timeframes AND the simple moving average of highs exceeds the same-period simple moving average of lows on at least the required number of timeframes AND transformed Williams %R is below its upper threshold and rising AND RSI is below its upper threshold and rising.
Short: No open position AND permitted weekday AND outside the initial daily no-entry interval AND elapsed time within the current entry cycle exceeds the entry delay AND OsMA is falling on at least the required number of timeframes AND the simple moving average of highs is below the same-period simple moving average of lows on at least the required number of timeframes AND transformed Williams %R is above its lower threshold and falling AND RSI is above its lower threshold and falling.
Calculation: Timeframes for the OsMA and moving-average comparisons = the chart timeframe, multiple lower timeframes, and a higher timeframe; use each timeframe's still-forming bar, comparing OsMA with its last completed bar.
Calculation: Transformed Williams %R = Williams %R plus its absolute lower bound; use the chart timeframe's still-forming bar and last completed bar for both Williams %R and RSI.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance.
Exit: long, manual take-profit mode AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: long, automatic take-profit mode with a minimum profit distance AND close > entry price plus minimum profit distance AND high minus close > pullback distance.
