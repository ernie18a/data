Calculation: Volume oscillator = Let up volume = (volume + (close - open) / price point size) / 2 and down volume = volume - up volume. Smooth each separately with the same moving-average method and first-stage length, then smooth each result with the same method and a second-stage length. Let the volume index = 100 × (smoothed up volume - smoothed down volume) / (smoothed up volume + smoothed down volume), or 0 if the denominator is 0. Smooth the volume index once, then smooth that result twice in succession to obtain the ergodic line. Smooth the ergodic line to obtain its signal line. Smooth the difference between the ergodic line and signal line to obtain the volume oscillator. Use the same moving-average method throughout, with independently adjustable lengths for the seven smoothing stages. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on the higher timeframe using its still-forming bar and the preceding higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Volume oscillator > 0 AND volume oscillator != its value on the preceding oscillator bar
Short: Volume oscillator < 0 AND volume oscillator != its value on the preceding oscillator bar
