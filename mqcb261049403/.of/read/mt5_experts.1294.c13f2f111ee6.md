Calculation: Smoothed positive and negative directional indicators = For each standard ADX positive/negative directional indicator, first calculate an intermediate value as 2 × current value + (alpha1 − 2) × previous value + (1 − alpha1) × previous intermediate value; then calculate the smoothed value as alpha2 × current intermediate value + (1 − alpha2) × previous smoothed value.
Long: Smoothed positive directional indicator > smoothed negative directional indicator AND the preceding bar's smoothed positive directional indicator <= its smoothed negative directional indicator (configured indicator timeframe, higher or lower than the chart timeframe; last completed bars).
Short: Smoothed positive directional indicator < smoothed negative directional indicator AND the preceding bar's smoothed positive directional indicator >= its smoothed negative directional indicator (configured indicator timeframe, higher or lower than the chart timeframe; last completed bars).
Exit: short, smoothed positive directional indicator > smoothed negative directional indicator (configured indicator timeframe, higher or lower than the chart timeframe; last completed bar).
Exit: long, smoothed positive directional indicator < smoothed negative directional indicator (configured indicator timeframe, higher or lower than the chart timeframe; last completed bar).
