Calculation: Selected price = a chosen open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint price, using either ordinary or Heikin-Ashi OHLC; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Power-weighted moving average = weighted mean of the selected price over a lookback, including the current bar, with weight (lookback − bars ago) raised to a configurable exponent; use available bars during initialization.
Calculation: Corrected average = initialize to the power-weighted moving average; subsequently set it to its previous value + c × (current power-weighted moving average − its previous value), where d is the population standard deviation of selected prices over a correction lookback, v is the squared difference between the current power-weighted moving average and the previous corrected average, and c = 0 if v < d² or v = 0, otherwise c = 1 − d²/v. Use available bars during initialization; for no correction, use d = 0.
Calculation: Floating upper and lower levels = the minimum corrected average over a separate lookback plus, respectively, a higher and a lower configurable fraction of its range over that lookback; the middle level is their average. Include the current bar and use available bars during initialization.
Calculation: Previous direction = the preceding calculation bar's long or short result, or neutral when neither line applied; initialize neutral. All calculations use the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: (slope mode OR floating-level lookback is at most one bar) AND (corrected average > previous corrected average OR corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = long) OR outer-level mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average > upper level OR corrected average >= lower level AND corrected average <= upper level AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = long) OR middle-level mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average > middle level OR corrected average = middle level AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = long) OR original-average mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average < power-weighted moving average OR corrected average = power-weighted moving average AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = long)
Short: (slope mode OR floating-level lookback is at most one bar) AND (corrected average < previous corrected average OR corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = short) OR outer-level mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average < lower level OR corrected average >= lower level AND corrected average <= upper level AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = short) OR middle-level mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average < middle level OR corrected average = middle level AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = short) OR original-average mode AND floating-level lookback > one bar AND (corrected average > power-weighted moving average OR corrected average = power-weighted moving average AND corrected average = previous corrected average AND previous direction = short)
