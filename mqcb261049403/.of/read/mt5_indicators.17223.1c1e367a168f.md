Calculation: Original QWMA = weighted average of the selected price over a lookback, with the current price weighted by lookback^speed and each older price weighted by (lookback − age)^speed.
Calculation: Corrected QWMA = previous corrected QWMA plus c × (current Original QWMA − previous corrected QWMA), where c = 0 if the squared difference between the previous corrected QWMA and current Original QWMA is less than the squared standard deviation of the selected price, or that difference is zero; otherwise c = 1 − (squared standard deviation / squared difference). Use the same lookback and speed as Original QWMA. On the first bar, it equals Original QWMA.
Calculation: Floating levels = rolling minimum and maximum of Corrected QWMA over a lookback; each level is the minimum plus its configured percentage of the range from minimum to maximum. The middle level is the average of the upper and lower levels.
Long: Corrected QWMA > upper floating level OR Corrected QWMA > middle level OR Corrected QWMA > its previous bar value OR Corrected QWMA < Original QWMA
Short: Corrected QWMA < lower floating level OR Corrected QWMA < middle level OR Corrected QWMA < its previous bar value OR Corrected QWMA > Original QWMA
