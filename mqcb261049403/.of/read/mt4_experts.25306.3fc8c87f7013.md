Long: At the opening of a chart bar, the absolute difference between its open and the close two bars ago exceeds a price-distance threshold AND the shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price is above the longer-period one on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the low two bars ago is below the previous bar's high.
Short: At the opening of a chart bar, the absolute difference between its open and the previous bar's close exceeds the same price-distance threshold AND the shorter-period linear weighted moving average of typical price is below the longer-period one on the forming higher-timeframe bar AND the previous bar's low is below the high two bars ago.
Exit: long, the chart bar's low is at or below the entry bar's open minus a stop-loss distance OR its high is at or above the entry bar's open plus a take-profit distance.
Exit: long, the previous bar's close is at or below its lower Bollinger Band OR all six shorter-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal OR all six longer-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal.
Exit: short, the previous bar's close is at or below its lower Bollinger Band OR all six shorter-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal OR all six longer-period exponential moving averages of typical price on the forming chart bar are equal.
