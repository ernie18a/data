Calculation: RSI-volume candle open and close = (RSI of the bar’s price open and close over a lookback period − 50) × the bar’s selected volume; if the absolute price open–close difference is within a gap threshold, replace the candle open with the previous bar’s RSI-volume close.
Long: RSI-volume candle close >= RSI-volume candle open
Short: RSI-volume candle close < RSI-volume candle open
