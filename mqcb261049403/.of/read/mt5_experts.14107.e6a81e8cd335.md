Calculation: TriX candle = candle whose open and close are the TRIX values calculated separately from the source bar’s open and close on the configured indicator timeframe; use completed bars.
Long: Current TriX candle close > open AND preceding TriX candle close <= open
Short: Current TriX candle close < open AND preceding TriX candle close >= open
Exit: long, current TriX candle close < open
Exit: short, current TriX candle close > open
