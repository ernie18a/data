Calculation: Channel = For a chosen applied OHLC price and half-length H, calculate each center as the weighted mean of that bar and up to H available bars on each side, with weight H+1 at the center and decreasing by 1 per bar of distance; normalize by the weights actually available. Recalculate using data available at the decision bar. Let d be applied price minus center and N=2H+1. Starting at the oldest bar with H older bars available, seed the positive and negative variances with d² on the corresponding side and 0 on the other side, and set both bands to the center. For each newer bar, update each variance as ((N-1) times its previous value plus d² if d is on that side, otherwise plus 0)/N. Upper band = center + deviation multiplier × square root of positive variance; lower band = center - deviation multiplier × square root of negative variance. Calculate on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar as available. For a higher timeframe, map each channel value to its constituent chart bars; optionally linearly interpolate between the values at the last chart bars of consecutive higher-timeframe bars when both endpoints are available.
Long: Previous chart bar close < open AND previous low < previous lower band AND current close > open AND previous lower band is nonzero and between -1000000 and 1000000 AND (volatility filter disabled OR previous upper and lower bands and center are nonzero and between -1000000 and 1000000 AND 100 × (previous upper band - previous lower band) / absolute previous center >= minimum width) AND (volume filter disabled OR mean volume of available bars in the lookback preceding the previous bar > 0 AND previous volume >= that mean × volume multiplier) AND (cooldown disabled OR number of long and short signals in the preceding cooldown bars = 0).
Short: Previous chart bar close > open AND previous high > previous upper band AND current close < open AND previous upper band is nonzero and between -1000000 and 1000000 AND (volatility filter disabled OR previous upper and lower bands and center are nonzero and between -1000000 and 1000000 AND 100 × (previous upper band - previous lower band) / absolute previous center >= minimum width) AND (volume filter disabled OR mean volume of available bars in the lookback preceding the previous bar > 0 AND previous volume >= that mean × volume multiplier) AND (cooldown disabled OR number of long and short signals in the preceding cooldown bars = 0).
