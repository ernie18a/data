Calculation: Applied price = a selectable price derived from the bar: open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, high if close>open and low if close<open and close otherwise, (high+close)/2 if close>open and (low+close)/2 if close<open and close otherwise, or (high+low+close+x)/2-(high+low)/2 where x is low if close<open, high if close>open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Upper envelope = (1+deviation percentage/100) multiplied by the rounded value of 8*EMA1-28*EMA2+56*EMA3-70*EMA4+56*EMA5-28*EMA6+8*EMA7-EMA8, plus a price offset. EMA1 is the EMA of applied price; each subsequent EMA is the EMA of the preceding EMA, all with the same length. Seed each EMA with its first available input, starting successive stages on successive bars. Round the weighted result to a configurable price increment. Calculate either on chart bars or on higher-timeframe bars using the bar still forming.
Long: Upper envelope > its previous value OR upper envelope = its previous value AND the previous direction is long.
Short: Upper envelope < its previous value OR upper envelope = its previous value AND the previous direction is short.
