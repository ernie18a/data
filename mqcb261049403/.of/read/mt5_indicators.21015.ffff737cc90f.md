Calculation: WAMI = three successive moving averages of the bar-to-bar change in applied price; the first uses a shorter lookback than the second and third, which use equal lookbacks.
Calculation: Signal = moving average of WAMI using a shorter lookback than the second and third WAMI averages.
Long: WAMI > Signal on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Short: WAMI < Signal on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
