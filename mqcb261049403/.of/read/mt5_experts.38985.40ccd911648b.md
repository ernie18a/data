Long: ((Chaikin Oscillator on the working timeframe was strictly below its moving average on the preceding bar AND is strictly above it on the signal bar AND (zero-level filter is disabled OR both values on the signal bar are below zero) AND reversal is disabled) OR (Chaikin Oscillator was strictly above its moving average on the preceding bar AND is strictly below it on the signal bar AND (zero-level filter is disabled OR both values on the signal bar are above zero) AND reversal is enabled)) AND long trading is allowed AND (time control is disabled OR current time is in the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval)
Short: ((Chaikin Oscillator on the working timeframe was strictly above its moving average on the preceding bar AND is strictly below it on the signal bar AND (zero-level filter is disabled OR both values on the signal bar are above zero) AND reversal is disabled) OR (Chaikin Oscillator was strictly below its moving average on the preceding bar AND is strictly above it on the signal bar AND (zero-level filter is disabled OR both values on the signal bar are below zero) AND reversal is enabled)) AND short trading is allowed AND (time control is disabled OR current time is in the configured start-inclusive, end-exclusive interval)
Calculation: Signal bar = the last completed working-timeframe bar when signals are checked on a new bar, or the still-forming working-timeframe bar when signals are checked within a bar; the working timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. The Chaikin Oscillator uses a shorter and a longer moving-average period.
Exit: long, stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance OR take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance OR close-opposite is enabled AND the short-entry condition is met
Exit: short, stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance OR take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price minus take-profit distance OR close-opposite is enabled AND the long-entry condition is met
