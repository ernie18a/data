Calculation: Candle range = high - low
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close - open) / candle range
Calculation: Upper-wick ratio = (high - max(open, close)) / candle range
Calculation: Lower-wick ratio = (min(open, close) - low) / candle range
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / candle range
Long: price sweeps below the lower VWAP deviation band, then rises from the lowest price reached during that sweep by at least the snapback distance plus any required extra move AND all enabled higher-timeframe trends are bullish (last completed bar close > its moving average; use long-, medium-, and short-horizon averages on the respective higher timeframes) AND, when required, the last completed higher-timeframe setup bar is bullish (close > open) AND, when enabled, the last completed chart-timeframe candle has lower-wick ratio >= its minimum, body ratio >= its minimum if that filter is enabled, close > open if candle bias is required, and close location >= its minimum if close-location filtering is required AND, when enabled, chart-timeframe ATR is within its permitted range
Short: price sweeps above the upper VWAP deviation band, then falls from the highest price reached during that sweep by at least the snapback distance plus any required extra move AND all enabled higher-timeframe trends are bearish (last completed bar close < its moving average; use long-, medium-, and short-horizon averages on the respective higher timeframes) AND, when required, the last completed higher-timeframe setup bar is bearish (close < open) AND, when enabled, the last completed chart-timeframe candle has upper-wick ratio >= its minimum, body ratio >= its minimum if that filter is enabled, close < open if candle bias is required, and close location <= 1 minus its minimum if close-location filtering is required AND, when enabled, chart-timeframe ATR is within its permitted range
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price
