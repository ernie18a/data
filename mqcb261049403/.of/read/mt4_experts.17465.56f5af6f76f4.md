Calculation: Reference range = ATR on the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, using a longer lookback.
Calculation: Volatility variation = ((shorter-lookback ATR / longer-lookback ATR on the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar - 1) + (shorter-lookback ATR / longer-lookback ATR on the last completed chart bar - 1)) / 2.
Calculation: Asian high and low = highest high and lowest low of a lookback of completed bars immediately preceding the London open, using a higher timeframe than the chart or the chart timeframe when they are equal. Asian range = Asian high - Asian low.
Calculation: PSAR swing high = PSAR at the most recent completed chart bar within the search lookback where PSAR > close and the preceding bar's PSAR < close. PSAR swing low = PSAR at the most recent completed chart bar within the search lookback where PSAR < close and the preceding bar's PSAR > close.
Calculation: Long trigger = PSAR swing high + a positive fraction of the longer-lookback chart ATR. Short trigger = PSAR swing low - a positive fraction of the longer-lookback chart ATR. Calculate both at the signal bar and retain the applicable trigger until it is reached or the New York session close is reached.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = absolute difference between the trigger and the opposite PSAR swing, extended away from the trigger by a positive fraction of the reference range, then clamped between lower and upper positive fractions of the reference range. Calculate it at the signal bar and retain it for the position.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the maximum of the initial stop and each subsequent eligible chart-bar-open trailing candidate. A candidate is eligible when the bar open >= entry price + a positive fraction of the initial stop distance; its value is the bar open minus a smaller positive fraction of the initial stop distance if that value >= entry price, otherwise the bar open minus the initial stop distance.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = the minimum of the initial stop and each subsequent eligible chart-bar-open trailing candidate. A candidate is eligible when the bar open <= entry price - a positive fraction of the initial stop distance; its value is the bar open plus a smaller positive fraction of the initial stop distance if that value <= entry price, otherwise the bar open plus the initial stop distance.
Long: At a chart-bar open within the London session, the last completed chart bar's high - low > longer-lookback chart ATR AND absolute volatility variation <= threshold AND Asian range >= lower fraction of reference range AND Asian range <= upper fraction of reference range AND both PSAR swings exist AND the last completed bar's PSAR < the preceding bar's close AND the preceding bar's PSAR > the last completed bar's close AND the last completed bar's MACD main > MACD signal AND stochastic main <= upper threshold AND long trigger > Asian high AND PSAR swing low - a positive fraction of reference range < long trigger AND a subsequent bar's high >= the retained long trigger before cancellation.
Short: At a chart-bar open within the London session, the last completed chart bar's high - low > longer-lookback chart ATR AND absolute volatility variation <= threshold AND Asian range >= lower fraction of reference range AND Asian range <= upper fraction of reference range AND both PSAR swings exist AND the last completed bar's PSAR > the preceding bar's close AND the preceding bar's PSAR < the last completed bar's close AND the last completed bar's MACD main < MACD signal AND stochastic main >= lower threshold AND short trigger < Asian low AND PSAR swing high + a positive fraction of reference range > short trigger AND a subsequent bar's low <= the retained short trigger before cancellation.
Exit: long, the preceding completed chart bar's MACD main > MACD signal AND the last completed chart bar's MACD main < MACD signal.
Exit: short, the preceding completed chart bar's MACD main < MACD signal AND the last completed chart bar's MACD main > MACD signal.
Exit: long, low <= long trailing stop OR high >= entry price + a positive multiple of initial stop distance OR chart-bar open time >= New York session close.
Exit: short, high >= short trailing stop OR low <= entry price - a positive multiple of initial stop distance OR chart-bar open time >= New York session close.
