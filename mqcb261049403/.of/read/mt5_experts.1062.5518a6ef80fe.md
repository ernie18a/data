Calculation: X2MA = apply a configurable moving-average smoother to the selected price, then apply a second configurable moving-average smoother to that result; calculate on the configured indicator timeframe, using completed bars (if different from the chart timeframe, it may be higher or lower).
Long: The middle of three consecutive completed X2MA values is lower than both the oldest and newest values.
Short: The middle of three consecutive completed X2MA values is higher than both the oldest and newest values.
Exit: Long, the middle of the three completed X2MA values is greater than the newest value.
Exit: Short, the middle of the three completed X2MA values is less than the newest value.
