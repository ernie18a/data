Calculation: Swing pivot = a bar whose low is the lowest low (swing low) or whose high is the highest high (swing high) within a symmetric window of the same number of bars on each side of it. Only pivots with that many completed bars after them count.
Calculation: Channel = scan back from the most recent bars that have enough bars after them to confirm a pivot, within a limited lookback. The first pivot found sets the type (swing high or swing low). The second pivot is the next older pivot of the same type. Pivots of the other type are ignored. Pivot line = straight line through these two pivots (price vs. bar index), extended to the current bar. Slope = (older pivot price - newer pivot price) / (older bar index - newer bar index), per bar going back. Opposite line = line parallel to the pivot line with the same slope. If the pivots are swing highs, it passes through the lowest value of (low - slope*bars_back) over all bars from the third most recent bar back to the older pivot bar. If the pivots are swing lows, it passes through the highest value of (high - slope*bars_back) over the same bars. Upper line = the line through swing highs, lower line = the line through swing lows. Channel is recomputed on every bar, and each line is evaluated at the bar being tested.
Long: high of the current or previous bar > Upper line value at that bar
Short: low of the current or previous bar < Lower line value at that bar
