Long: Close of the still-forming signal-timeframe bar > TEMA of close on that bar; the signal timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Short: Close of the still-forming signal-timeframe bar < TEMA of close on that bar; the signal timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Exit: Long, stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price minus stop-loss distance.
Exit: Long, take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price plus take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price plus stop-loss distance.
Exit: Short, take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price minus take-profit distance.
