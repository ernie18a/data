Calculation: XCCX = 100 × smoothed deviation of typical price from its longer-lookback smoother / smoothed absolute deviation; use a shorter lookback for smoothing both deviations, and set XCCX to 0 when the smoothed signed deviation is 0.
Calculation: Fast and slow step lines = trailing step lines of XCCX. For each line with step size s, set candidate lower and upper bounds to XCCX − 2s and XCCX + 2s. Set trend up when XCCX exceeds the prior upper bound, or down when it falls below the prior lower bound; otherwise retain the prior trend. In an uptrend, raise the candidate lower bound to at least the prior lower bound and set the line to that bound + s. In a downtrend, lower the candidate upper bound to at most the prior upper bound and set the line to that bound − s. The fast line uses a smaller step size than the slow line. Initialize the prior upper bound below and prior lower bound above all valid XCCX values so the initial trend becomes up.
Calculation: Both lines and their comparisons use bars on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the signal bar after it closes and the preceding completed bar.
Long: Fast step line on the preceding bar ≤ slow step line on the preceding bar AND fast step line on the signal bar > slow step line on the signal bar
Short: Fast step line on the preceding bar ≥ slow step line on the preceding bar AND fast step line on the signal bar < slow step line on the signal bar
Exit: short, fast step line on the signal bar > slow step line on the signal bar
Exit: long, fast step line on the signal bar < slow step line on the signal bar
