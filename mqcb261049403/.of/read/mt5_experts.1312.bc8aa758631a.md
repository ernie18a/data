Calculation: Normalized deviation = let price be the selected applied OHLC price and deviation = price − Jurik moving average of price; smooth deviation and its absolute value independently with the same T3 parameters, then return 100 × smoothed deviation / smoothed absolute deviation when smoothed deviation is nonzero, otherwise return zero.
Calculation: Step line = calculate independently for a smaller fast step and a larger slow step. Initialize the prior lower bound to positive infinity, prior upper bound to negative infinity, and trend to zero. For each bar, set lower = normalized deviation − 2 × step and upper = normalized deviation + 2 × step. Set trend to positive if normalized deviation > prior upper, or negative if normalized deviation < prior lower; otherwise retain the prior trend. If trend is positive, set lower = max(lower, prior lower) and line = lower + step. If trend is negative, set upper = min(upper, prior upper) and line = upper − step. Store both bounds for the next bar. Calculate on the selected indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use its last completed bar and the preceding bar, or its still-forming bar and the preceding bar when the forming-bar option is selected.
Long: Fast step line > slow step line AND previous fast step line <= previous slow step line.
Short: Fast step line < slow step line AND previous fast step line >= previous slow step line.
Exit: short, fast step line > slow step line OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
Exit: long, fast step line < slow step line OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
