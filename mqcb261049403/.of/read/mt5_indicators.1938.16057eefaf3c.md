Calculation: Bullish event = shorter-period moving average on its selected applied price is greater than longer-period moving average on its selected applied price AND the previous bar's shorter-period moving average is less than its longer-period moving average AND close > open. The moving averages can use independently selected averaging methods and applied prices.
Calculation: Bearish event = shorter-period moving average on its selected applied price is less than longer-period moving average on its selected applied price AND the previous bar's shorter-period moving average is greater than its longer-period moving average AND close < open. The moving averages can use independently selected averaging methods and applied prices.
Calculation: Event age = number of bars from the selected bar back to the most recent bar with the specified event; infinity if no such event exists. For higher-timeframe calculations, the selected bar may be the still-forming bar or a selected completed bar.
Long: Bullish event on the chart timeframe.
Short: Bearish event on the chart timeframe.
Long: Higher-timeframe bullish event age < higher-timeframe bearish event age.
Short: Higher-timeframe bearish event age < higher-timeframe bullish event age.
