Calculation: Fractal candidates = Process chart bars chronologically. On each bar, examine the standard upper and lower fractals centered three bars earlier. Keep the highest upper fractal and lowest lower fractal seen since their respective resets, initially using 0 for the upper candidate and an initial upper bound for the lower candidate. Process the lower fractal first; if it produces a long decision, reset the upper candidate to 0 and skip the upper fractal on that bar. Otherwise process the upper fractal; if it produces a short decision, reset the lower candidate to the initial upper bound. Update each candidate before evaluating its decision.
Long: A lower fractal is present AND its value is below the previous lower candidate AND the upper candidate > 0 AND upper candidate - current open > current open - lower candidate
Short: An upper fractal is present AND its value is above the previous upper candidate AND the lower candidate < its initial upper bound AND upper candidate - current open < current open - lower candidate
