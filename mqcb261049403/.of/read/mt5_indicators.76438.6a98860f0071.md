Long: On an enabled timeframe, the shorter-period moving average of the selected price is greater than the longer-period moving average of the same price, using the same moving-average method and the bar still forming; the timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Short: On an enabled timeframe, the shorter-period moving average of the selected price is less than the longer-period moving average of the same price, using the same moving-average method and the bar still forming; the timeframe may be higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe.
