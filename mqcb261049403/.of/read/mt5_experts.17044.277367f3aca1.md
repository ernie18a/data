Calculation: Fast MA = short-period SMA of typical price on the chart timeframe, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Slow MA = longer-period SMMA of median price on the chart timeframe, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Price-position level = the low of the most recent chart bar whose open is at or below, and close is above, the average of a longer-period SMMA and a shorter-period SMA of median price; or the high of the most recent chart bar whose open is at or above, and close is below, that average. Include the bar still forming and search within a fixed lookback.
Calculation: Step direction = initialize the high and low values to the fast MA on the last completed bar and their bar offsets to zero. Scan fast MA offsets from a fixed lookback down to zero, replacing the high or low and its offset only on a strict new extreme. Let the spread change be the current fast-minus-slow MA spread minus its value on the last completed bar. Initialize direction to zero, then scan fast MA offsets from a longer lookback down to zero on the first calculation after a new chart bar, and from a shorter lookback down to zero otherwise. At each offset, in order: if the high offset exceeds the low offset and fast MA exceeds the low, set direction to 1; if the high offset exceeds the low offset and spread change is negative, set direction to -1; if the high offset is less than the low offset and fast MA is below the high, set direction to -1; if the high offset is less than the low offset and spread change is positive, set direction to 1. The shorter scan excludes the oldest offset of the extrema lookback.
Calculation: Price strength = percentage difference between chart-timeframe close and the last completed close of a separate benchmark timeframe, rounded to the configured precision. The benchmark timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Calculate current and previous strength using the forming and last completed chart bars respectively, both against the same last completed benchmark close.
Long: Forming chart-bar close > price-position level AND step direction = 1 AND forming chart-bar close > open AND forming chart-bar low < current fast MA AND current fast MA > last completed fast MA AND current price strength > previous price strength.
Short: Forming chart-bar close < price-position level AND step direction = -1 AND forming chart-bar close < open AND forming chart-bar high > current fast MA AND current fast MA < last completed fast MA AND current price strength < previous price strength.
Exit: long, opposite-signal closing enabled = true AND forming chart-bar close < price-position level AND step direction = -1 AND forming chart-bar close < open AND forming chart-bar high > current fast MA AND current fast MA < last completed fast MA AND current price strength < previous price strength.
Exit: short, opposite-signal closing enabled = true AND forming chart-bar close > price-position level AND step direction = 1 AND forming chart-bar close > open AND forming chart-bar low < current fast MA AND current fast MA > last completed fast MA AND current price strength > previous price strength.
