Calculation: Price = chart-bar close, used in place of bid and ask.
Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed higher-timeframe period, take high H and low L; take C as the close of the last completed bar of a shorter timeframe before the most recent configured time-of-day boundary. P = (H + L + C) / 3; R1 = 2P − L; S1 = 2P − H; R2 = P + H − L; S2 = P − H + L; R3 = 2P + H − 2L; S3 = 2P − 2H + L. Insert the midpoint between each adjacent pair in the ordered sequence S3, S2, S1, P, R1, R2, R3.
Calculation: Entry targets = if price is strictly between adjacent pivot levels, the lower level is the short target and the upper level is the long target; if price is strictly between the highest midpoint and R3, use S3 as the short target and the lowest midpoint as the long target. Otherwise both targets are zero. Fix the applicable target at entry.
Calculation: Indicator values = ADX and its directional indicators, a shorter-period EMA of close, a longer-period EMA of open, and OsMA of close, all on the same higher timeframe. Current values use its still-forming bar; previous values use its last completed bar.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initially the entry price minus the optional stop distance for a long, or plus it for a short. If trailing is enabled, update a long stop to price minus the trailing distance whenever price exceeds entry by more than trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is below price minus trailing distance minus trailing step. Update a short stop to price plus the trailing distance whenever price is below entry by more than trailing distance plus trailing step AND the existing stop is above price plus trailing distance plus trailing step or no stop exists.
Long: Position side is flat AND long target minus price > minimum target distance AND ADX > threshold AND current positive directional indicator > previous positive directional indicator AND current positive directional indicator > current negative directional indicator AND current close EMA minus current open EMA >= minimum EMA separation AND previous close EMA > previous open EMA AND current OsMA > previous OsMA.
Short: Position side is flat AND price minus short target > minimum target distance AND ADX > threshold AND current negative directional indicator > previous negative directional indicator AND current positive directional indicator < current negative directional indicator AND current open EMA minus current close EMA >= minimum EMA separation AND previous open EMA > previous close EMA AND current OsMA < previous OsMA.
Exit: long, price >= fixed long target OR an active stop exists AND price <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, price <= fixed short target OR an active stop exists AND price >= trailing stop.
