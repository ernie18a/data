Calculation: Daily pivot levels = using the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar's high and low and the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close at the day boundary: P=(high+low+close)/3; R1=2P-low; S1=2P-high; R2=P+(high-low); S2=P-(high-low); R3=2P+high-2low; S3=2P-2high+low. Midpoints M5=(R2+R3)/2, M4=(R1+R2)/2, M3=(P+R1)/2, M2=(P+S1)/2, M1=(S1+S2)/2, M0=(S2+S3)/2.
Calculation: Pivot zone boundaries = the adjacent levels among S3, M0, S2, M1, S1, M2, P, M3, R1, M4, R2, M5, R3 that bracket the current bid; use the lower boundary as support and upper boundary as resistance. In the highest zone (M5 to R3), use S3 as support and M0 as resistance.
Long: No open position AND resistance - ask > minimum distance threshold AND current forming higher-timeframe ADX > its threshold AND current forming higher-timeframe +DI > its last completed value AND current forming higher-timeframe +DI > current forming higher-timeframe -DI AND current forming higher-timeframe short-lookback close-price EMA - current forming higher-timeframe longer-lookback open-price EMA >= minimum distance threshold AND last completed higher-timeframe close-price EMA > last completed higher-timeframe open-price EMA AND current forming higher-timeframe OsMA > its last completed value.
Short: No open position AND bid - support > minimum distance threshold AND current forming higher-timeframe ADX > its threshold AND current forming higher-timeframe -DI > its last completed value AND current forming higher-timeframe +DI < current forming higher-timeframe -DI AND current forming higher-timeframe open-price EMA - current forming higher-timeframe close-price EMA >= minimum distance threshold AND last completed higher-timeframe open-price EMA > last completed higher-timeframe close-price EMA AND current forming higher-timeframe OsMA < its last completed value.
Exit: Long, current price <= initial stop-loss price OR current price >= resistance OR current price <= active trailing stop. Raise the trailing stop to current price minus the configured trailing distance when current price - entry price > trailing distance + step and the existing stop is below current price - trailing distance - step.
Exit: Short, current price >= initial stop-loss price OR current price <= support OR current price >= active trailing stop. Lower the trailing stop to current price plus the configured trailing distance when entry price - current price > trailing distance + step and the existing stop is above current price + trailing distance + step or unset.
