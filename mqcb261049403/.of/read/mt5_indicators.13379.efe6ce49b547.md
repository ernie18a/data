Calculation: Selected price = a selectable bar price: open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4; alternatively, select high when close>open, low when close<open, otherwise close; or select (high+close)/2 when close>open, (low+close)/2 when close<open, otherwise close; or select (X-high-low)/2, where X=(high+low+close+low)/2 when close<open, (high+low+close+high)/2 when close>open, otherwise (high+low+2*close)/2.
Calculation: Smoothed momentum = Kaufman adaptive moving average of selected price minus selected price at the momentum lookback. For its efficiency ratio, divide the absolute change in momentum over the adaptive lookback by the sum of absolute consecutive momentum changes over that lookback plus 0.000000001. Raise the interpolated smoothing constant to a selectable power; its endpoints are 2/(shorter fast period+1) and 2/(longer slow period+1). Initialize the average with the preceding momentum at the first calculation, after the momentum lookback plus the adaptive lookback plus three bars from the start of the series.
Long: Smoothed momentum on the chart timeframe > 0 OR smoothed momentum on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar > 0.
Short: Smoothed momentum on the chart timeframe < 0 OR smoothed momentum on a higher timeframe's still-forming bar < 0.
