Calculation: Smoothed Laguerre oscillator = Apply a Jurik moving average with configurable length and phase to the Laguerre RSI of a configurable OHLC-derived price, then multiply by 100. For each bar, initialize L0 through L3 to the first price; update L0 = (1 − gamma) × price + gamma × previous L0, L1 = −gamma × L0 + previous L0 + gamma × previous L1, L2 = −gamma × L1 + previous L1 + gamma × previous L2, and L3 = −gamma × L2 + previous L2 + gamma × previous L3. Sum the positive differences L0−L1, L1−L2, L2−L3 as U and the absolute negative differences as D; Laguerre RSI = U / (U + D), retaining its previous value when U + D = 0 and starting at zero. Calculate on a configurable timeframe that may be the chart timeframe, higher, or lower; use the last completed bar or the forming bar according to the configurable signal-bar selection.
Calculation: Direction state = For the smoothed oscillator, use configurable upper, middle, and lower levels in descending order. Set state to bullish when the oscillator is above the upper level, or when it is above the middle and at or below the upper level after being at or below the middle, or when it is above the lower and at or below the middle after being below the lower. Set state to bearish when the oscillator is below the lower level, or when it is above the middle and at or below the upper level after being above the upper, or when it is above the lower and at or below the middle after being above the middle. Otherwise retain the previous state; initialize it as neutral.
Long: Direction state changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Direction state changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, direction state is bearish OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, direction state is bullish OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
