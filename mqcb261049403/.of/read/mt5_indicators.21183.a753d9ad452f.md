Calculation: NRTR trend = Let K be the average of |high − low| over the configured lookback, multiplied by the configured decimal scale. Initialize trend as neutral, and seed price and line from the first close. For each bar in chronological order: while trend is bullish or neutral, set price to the greater of prior price and close, and line to the greater of prior line and price × (1 − K); if close < line, set trend bearish, price to close, and line to price × (1 + K). While trend is bearish or neutral, set price to the lesser of prior price and close, and line to the lesser of prior line and price × (1 + K); if close > line, set trend bullish, price to close, and line to price × (1 − K). Evaluate on the configured indicator timeframe, higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured, using the selected signal bar: a past bar is completed; the current bar is still forming.
Long: NRTR trend changes from bearish to bullish
Short: NRTR trend changes from bullish to bearish
Exit: long, NRTR trend changes from bullish to bearish OR low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: short, NRTR trend changes from bearish to bullish OR high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit distance
