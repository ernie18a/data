Calculation: Average volume = mean volume of prior completed chart-timeframe bars over a configurable lookback
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback, using the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: Long-term ATR = average true range over a longer lookback than ATR, using the last completed chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: Impulse body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low) of the last completed bar
Calculation: Impulse close position = (close − low) / (high − low) for an up bar; (high − close) / (high − low) for a down bar
Calculation: Impulse range ratio = range of the last completed bar / range of the preceding completed bar
Calculation: Adaptive volume threshold = base volume multiplier adjusted lower in London/New York sessions and higher in Asian/Pacific sessions
Calculation: Adaptive body-ratio threshold = base minimum body ratio adjusted lower when ATR / current bid exceeds a high-volatility threshold and higher when it is below a low-volatility threshold
Calculation: Impulse state = direction, detection-bar open and close, detection price, highest high and lowest low since detection, volume ratio at detection, and elapsed bars; initialize from an impulse bar and update the extremes and elapsed bars on each new chart bar
Calculation: Pullback ratio = (highest high since impulse − current bid) / (highest high since impulse − lowest low since impulse)
Calculation: Retracement ratio = (current ask − lowest low since impulse) / (highest high since impulse − lowest low since impulse)
Calculation: Stop loss = entry price minus an ATR multiple for long positions, or plus an ATR multiple for short positions
Calculation: Take profit = entry price plus an ATR multiple for long positions, or minus an ATR multiple for short positions; increase the distance when trailing is enabled
Long: No active impulse AND last completed bar closes above its open AND volume >= adaptive volume threshold × average volume AND impulse body ratio >= adaptive body-ratio threshold AND impulse close position >= required close-position threshold (reduced when body ratio exceeds its strong-body threshold) AND impulse range ratio >= required expansion threshold AND current bid >= current chart-timeframe moving average AND current moving average > its prior value AND ATR is within its permitted range AND ATR >= required fraction of long-term ATR (use a higher fraction in Asian/Pacific sessions) AND hour(datetime) != rollover hour AND last completed bar volume >= required fraction of average volume; OR active bullish impulse within its entry window AND (current bid >= highest high since impulse OR (pullback ratio is within its configured zone AND (forming bar close > open OR current bid > prior completed bar high)) OR (forming bar close > prior completed bar high AND forming bar volume > required fraction of average volume))
Short: No active impulse AND last completed bar closes below its open AND volume >= adaptive volume threshold × average volume AND impulse body ratio >= adaptive body-ratio threshold AND impulse close position >= required close-position threshold (reduced when body ratio exceeds its strong-body threshold) AND impulse range ratio >= required expansion threshold AND current bid <= current chart-timeframe moving average AND current moving average < its prior value AND ATR is within its permitted range AND ATR >= required fraction of long-term ATR (use a higher fraction in Asian/Pacific sessions) AND hour(datetime) != rollover hour AND last completed bar volume >= required fraction of average volume; OR active bearish impulse within its entry window AND (current ask <= lowest low since impulse OR (retracement ratio is within its configured zone AND (forming bar close < open OR current ask < prior completed bar low)) OR (forming bar close < prior completed bar low AND forming bar volume > required fraction of average volume))
Exit: Long, current price <= stop loss OR current price >= take profit
Exit: Short, current price >= stop loss OR current price <= take profit
