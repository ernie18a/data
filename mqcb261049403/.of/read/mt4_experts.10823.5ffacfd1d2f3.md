Calculation: Weighted price = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4.
Long: At a trend-check interval, RSI of weighted price on each of three higher timeframes, using each forming bar, > upper RSI threshold AND RSI on each of those timeframes at the same earlier bar offset < lower RSI threshold AND longer-period EMA of weighted price > shorter-period EMA of weighted price on the forming bar of the middle timeframe AND datetime is within the configured trading hours AND datetime is before the configured Friday closing time.
Short: At a trend-check interval, RSI of weighted price on each of three higher timeframes, using each forming bar, < upper RSI threshold AND RSI on each of those timeframes at the same earlier bar offset > lower RSI threshold AND longer-period EMA of weighted price < shorter-period EMA of weighted price on the forming bar of the middle timeframe AND datetime is within the configured trading hours AND datetime is before the configured Friday closing time.
Exit: long, configured Friday closing time is enabled AND datetime is on Friday at or after that time.
Exit: short, configured Friday closing time is enabled AND datetime is on Friday at or after that time.
