Calculation: Previous profile period's POC, value-area bounds, and profile extremes = Use the last completed bar of a higher profile timeframe. Divide its high-low range into parameterized price bins. For each bin, sum volume from lower-timeframe M1 bars whose price range includes the bin price. POC is the bin price with greatest volume. Starting at POC, expand the value area upward first and then downward, accumulating bin volume until reaching the parameterized fraction of total bin volume. Profile extremes are the completed period's high and low.
Long: current chart bar open > value-area high AND current chart bar open < profile high AND abs(current chart bar close - POC) < half the instrument's minimum price increment
Short: current chart bar open < value-area low AND current chart bar open > profile low AND abs(current chart bar close - POC) < half the instrument's minimum price increment
