Calculation: Reference high and low = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe reference bar.
Calculation: Zone edges = reference high ± half a configurable percentage of reference high, and reference low ± the same half-width. Each strategy has its own zone percentage.
Calculation: Range filter = range of the last completed higher-timeframe reference bar divided by the average high-minus-low range over a configurable lookback of completed reference bars, including the last one. The filter passes when this ratio is within its configurable inclusive bounds, or when the average is nonpositive or insufficient history is available.
Calculation: Signal close, high and low = values of completed bars on a higher signal timeframe shorter than the reference timeframe; each strategy has its own signal timeframe.
Calculation: Breakout-retest long = the last completed signal close is within the high zone AND, scanning earlier completed signal bars backward over a configurable lookback, the first close outside the high zone is above its upper edge AND at least one high encountered through that bar is at or above the upper edge multiplied by one plus a configurable breakout percentage.
Calculation: Breakout-retest short = the last completed signal close is within the low zone AND, scanning earlier completed signal bars backward over the same lookback, the first close outside the low zone is below its lower edge AND at least one low encountered through that bar is at or below the lower edge multiplied by one minus the breakout percentage.
Calculation: Breakout sequence long = after a signal close at or below the low zone's upper edge, a signal close reaches or exceeds the high zone's upper edge multiplied by one plus a configurable breakout percentage.
Calculation: Breakout sequence short = after a signal close at or above the high zone's lower edge, a signal close reaches or falls below the low zone's lower edge multiplied by one minus the breakout percentage.
Calculation: Pullback sequence long = after a signal close at or below the low zone's upper edge, a signal close reaches or exceeds that edge multiplied by one plus a configurable pullback percentage, then a signal close is within the low zone.
Calculation: Pullback sequence short = after a signal close at or above the high zone's lower edge, a signal close reaches or falls below that edge multiplied by one minus the pullback percentage, then a signal close is within the high zone.
Calculation: Sequence state = update each strategy's long and short sequences independently when the range filter passes; reset both at the start of each new reference bar and reset a completed direction after its signal is evaluated. Consecutive sequence stages may complete on the same evaluation.
Calculation: Series average entry = arithmetic mean of entry prices of the equal-sized open positions for the same strategy and side.
Long: Range filter passes AND breakout-retest long is true AND no long position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Short: Range filter passes AND breakout-retest short is true AND no short position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Long: Range filter passes AND breakout sequence long is complete AND no long position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Short: Range filter passes AND breakout sequence short is complete AND no short position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Long: Range filter passes AND pullback sequence long is complete AND no long position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Short: Range filter passes AND pullback sequence short is complete AND no short position exists for that strategy AND no position for that strategy closed on the current chart bar.
Long: A long series exists AND current price is at or below its most recent entry price multiplied by one minus that strategy's configurable grid percentage AND no grid entry has been made for that strategy on the current chart bar.
Short: A short series exists AND current price is at or above its most recent entry price multiplied by one plus that strategy's configurable grid percentage AND no grid entry has been made for that strategy on the current chart bar.
Exit: long, current price is at or below series average entry multiplied by one minus that strategy's configurable stop-loss percentage OR current price is at or above series average entry multiplied by one plus that strategy's configurable take-profit percentage.
Exit: short, current price is at or above series average entry multiplied by one plus that strategy's configurable stop-loss percentage OR current price is at or below series average entry multiplied by one minus that strategy's configurable take-profit percentage.
