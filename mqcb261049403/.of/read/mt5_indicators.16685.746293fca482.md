Calculation: Price input = a selected open, high, low, close, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint price, or the corresponding Heikin-Ashi price; alternatively, trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise, and extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. Heikin-Ashi open is the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close midpoint, initialized to the first bar's open and close midpoint; Heikin-Ashi close is the OHLC average, high is the maximum of high, Heikin-Ashi open and close, and low is the corresponding minimum.
Calculation: Composite RSI = smooth the price input with a selectable simple, exponential, smoothed, or linear-weighted moving average. Apply a cascade of exponential smoothers to the result, including an initial stage and a configurable number of subsequent stages. Initialize every stage to the first smoothed price; thereafter update each stage as its previous value plus alpha times the difference between its current input and previous value. Alpha is 2/(1 + period), or 2/(2 + (period - 1)/2) in fast mode. Sum positive differences between each stage and its next stage as U, and the absolute negative differences as D. Composite RSI is U/(U + D), or zero when U + D is zero. Calculate on the chart timeframe or the still-forming bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Floating levels = the minimum composite RSI over the lookback including the current bar plus a configurable fraction of the difference between the maximum and minimum over that lookback. The upper level uses a higher fraction than the lower level.
Calculation: Line direction = up when composite RSI is above the upper floating level or below the lower floating level and is greater than its previous value; down when it is outside those levels and is less than its previous value; unchanged from the previous bar when it is outside those levels and equals its previous value; neutral when it is within or equal to either level. Initialize to neutral.
Long: Composite RSI > upper floating level
Short: Composite RSI < lower floating level
Long: Line direction = up
Short: Line direction = down
