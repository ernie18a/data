Calculation: NVI = Start at an arbitrary base value; when volume is lower than the previous bar, multiply the previous NVI by close / previous close; otherwise carry the previous NVI forward. If calculated on a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar.
Calculation: NVI moving average = Moving average of NVI over a lookback period.
Long: NVI crosses above its moving average
Short: NVI crosses below its moving average OR price makes a higher high while NVI makes a lower low
