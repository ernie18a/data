Calculation: Momentum = 100 × selected price / selected price a momentum lookback ago.
Long: On the working timeframe, momentum on the previous signal bar < its moving average AND momentum on the signal bar > its moving average AND momentum on the previous signal bar < 100 AND reverse mode is disabled; OR momentum on the previous signal bar > its moving average AND momentum on the signal bar < its moving average AND momentum on the previous signal bar > 100 AND reverse mode is enabled. The working timeframe may equal, be higher than, or be lower than the chart timeframe; the signal bar is either the last completed working-timeframe bar or the bar still forming, as selected.
Short: On the working timeframe, momentum on the previous signal bar > its moving average AND momentum on the signal bar < its moving average AND momentum on the previous signal bar > 100 AND reverse mode is disabled; OR momentum on the previous signal bar < its moving average AND momentum on the signal bar > its moving average AND momentum on the previous signal bar < 100 AND reverse mode is enabled. The working timeframe may equal, be higher than, or be lower than the chart timeframe; the signal bar is either the last completed working-timeframe bar or the bar still forming, as selected.
Exit: long, take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance; OR close-opposite mode is enabled AND the short-entry condition is met.
Exit: short, take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance; OR close-opposite mode is enabled AND the long-entry condition is met.
