Calculation: Dynamic level and direction = On the bar one lookback before the first calculation, initialize the level to close and direction to neutral. For each subsequent bar, if high is below the previous high, the high one lookback ago, and the previous level, set the level to high plus a price offset and set direction to neutral if the previous direction was long, otherwise short. Otherwise, if low is above the previous low, the low one lookback ago, and the previous level, set the level to low plus the price offset and set direction to neutral if the previous direction was short, otherwise long. Otherwise, retain the previous level and direction; if the retained direction is neutral, change it to long when the direction two bars ago was short, or to short when it was long.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = Calculate the dynamic level and direction on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar. Sample its direction on the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and hold that sampled direction until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe direction equals long OR higher-timeframe direction equals long
Short: Chart-timeframe direction equals short OR higher-timeframe direction equals short
