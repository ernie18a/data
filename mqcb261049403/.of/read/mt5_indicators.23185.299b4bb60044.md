Calculation: Price = selectable close, open, high, low, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (high+low+2*close)/4, (open+close)/2, (open+high+low+close)/4, bullish-bar high or bearish-bar low or otherwise close, bullish-bar (high+close)/2 or bearish-bar (low+close)/2 or otherwise close, or DeMark price: let X=(high+low+close+low)/2 if close<open, (high+low+close+high)/2 if close>open, otherwise (high+low+2*close)/2; price=X-(high+low)/2.
Calculation: Volume-weighted Fisher = for each bar, let highest high and lowest low cover a lookback including that bar; v=(price-lowest low)/(highest high-lowest low)-0.5+0.67*previous v, initialized to zero; r=(1+v)/(1-v), replaced by 1 if r<0.0000001; f=0.5*ln(r)+0.5*previous f, initialized to zero; output 100 times a selected moving average of f*volume. The moving average uses a selectable smoothing method, length, and where applicable phase. Calculate on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Long: Volume-weighted Fisher > its previous bar's value on the calculation timeframe.
Short: Volume-weighted Fisher < its previous bar's value on the calculation timeframe.
