Calculation: Pivots = on each enabled chart, higher, or lower timeframe, evaluate at the start of a new bar using the last completed bar and the still-forming bar: a pivot high has high greater than or equal to all highs in its configured newer and older neighboring bars; a pivot low has low less than or equal to all corresponding lows. If both occur on one bar, retain only the high. X, A, B, C, D are the five most recent pivots in chronological order.
Calculation: Pattern quality = clamp to [0, 1] the value 1 minus a price-deviation weight times (abs(B − (A − s × B-ratio × d)) + abs(C − (B + C-ratio × (A − B))) + abs(D − (C − D-ratio × (C − B)))) / d, minus a time-deviation weight times (abs(1 − (B.time − A.time) / (D.time − C.time)) + abs(1 − (A.time − X.time) / (C.time − B.time))); s is +1 for bullish and −1 for bearish, and d = s × (A − X).
Long: X, B and D are pivot lows AND A and C are pivot highs AND d > 0 AND abs(B − (A − B-ratio × d)) <= tolerance × d AND lower BC-ratio × d <= C − B <= upper BC-ratio × d AND lower CD-ratio × d <= C − D <= upper CD-ratio × d AND D < X AND pattern quality >= minimum quality AND current hour is within the configured session if the session filter is enabled.
Short: X, B and D are pivot highs AND A and C are pivot lows AND d > 0 AND abs(B − (A + B-ratio × d)) <= tolerance × d AND lower BC-ratio × d <= B − C <= upper BC-ratio × d AND lower CD-ratio × d <= D − C <= upper CD-ratio × d AND D > X AND pattern quality >= minimum quality AND current hour is within the configured session if the session filter is enabled.
Calculation: Trade levels = retain C from the confirmed entry pattern; the three profit targets are entry price plus a first, second, or third increasing fraction of (C − entry price). The initial stop is entry price minus a stop multiplier times (second target − entry price), adjusted away from entry if its absolute distance is below the minimum stop distance.
Exit: long, price >= the applicable profit target OR price <= the initial stop.
Exit: short, price <= the applicable profit target OR price >= the initial stop.
