Calculation: Signal bar = the bar at a configurable index on the work timeframe, evaluated when a new work-timeframe bar begins; the work timeframe may equal, be higher than, or be lower than the chart timeframe. Index zero selects the still-forming bar; a positive index selects a completed bar.
Long: position is flat AND a new work-timeframe bar begins AND ((reverse mode is disabled AND signal bar close > signal bar open) OR (reverse mode is enabled AND signal bar close < signal bar open))
Short: position is flat AND a new work-timeframe bar begins AND ((reverse mode is disabled AND signal bar close < signal bar open) OR (reverse mode is enabled AND signal bar close > signal bar open))
Exit: long, (a new work-timeframe bar begins AND ((reverse mode is disabled AND signal bar close < signal bar open) OR (reverse mode is enabled AND signal bar close > signal bar open))) OR (stop loss is enabled AND low <= entry price - stop-loss distance) OR (take profit is enabled AND high >= entry price + take-profit distance)
Exit: short, (a new work-timeframe bar begins AND ((reverse mode is disabled AND signal bar close > signal bar open) OR (reverse mode is enabled AND signal bar close < signal bar open))) OR (stop loss is enabled AND high >= entry price + stop-loss distance) OR (take profit is enabled AND low <= entry price - take-profit distance)
