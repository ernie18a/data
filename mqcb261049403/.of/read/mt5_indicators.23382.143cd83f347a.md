Calculation: Upward crossover = shorter-period moving average of close on the prior bar < longer-period moving average of close on the prior bar AND shorter-period moving average of close on the current bar > longer-period moving average of close on the current bar; both averages use the same method and shift.
Calculation: Downward crossover = shorter-period moving average of close on the prior bar > longer-period moving average of close on the prior bar AND shorter-period moving average of close on the current bar < longer-period moving average of close on the current bar; both averages use the same method and shift.
Calculation: Confirmed upward event = an upward crossover AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI of close > its midpoint; if the confirmation distance is zero, the event occurs on that bar. Otherwise, store that bar's low as the pending upward level and clear any pending downward level; the event occurs on the first bar, including the crossover bar, whose high minus the pending upward level >= the confirmation distance AND whose close <= its high. Clear the pending upward level after that test succeeds, even if the flat filter rejects the event. A later qualifying upward crossover replaces the pending upward level.
Calculation: Confirmed downward event = a downward crossover AND, if the RSI filter is enabled, RSI of close < its midpoint; if the confirmation distance is zero, the event occurs on that bar. Otherwise, store that bar's high as the pending downward level and clear any pending upward level; the event occurs on the first bar, including the crossover bar, whose pending downward level minus low >= the confirmation distance AND whose open >= close. Clear the pending downward level after that test succeeds, even if the flat filter rejects the event. A later qualifying downward crossover replaces the pending downward level.
Calculation: Accepted upward event = confirmed upward event AND the absolute difference between its bar's low and the low of the previous accepted upward event >= the flat distance; initialize the previous accepted upward low to zero.
Calculation: Accepted downward event = confirmed downward event AND the absolute difference between its bar's high and the high of the previous accepted downward event >= the flat distance; initialize the previous accepted downward high to zero.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = direction of the most recent accepted event on the higher timeframe through the selected higher-timeframe bar; use either the bar still forming or a selected completed bar. If both events occur on the same bar, the downward event takes precedence.
Long: Accepted upward event on the chart timeframe OR higher-timeframe trend = upward.
Short: Accepted downward event on the chart timeframe OR higher-timeframe trend = downward.
