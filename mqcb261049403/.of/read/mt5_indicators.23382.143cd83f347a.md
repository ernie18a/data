Long: On the chart timeframe, the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average (fast was below slow on the prior bar and is above it on the current bar) AND, when the RSI filter is enabled, RSI is above 50 AND (confirmation is disabled OR a prior upward cross is pending and the current high is at least the confirmation threshold above that cross bar’s low) AND the current low is at least the flat threshold above or below the low of the previous long signal (initially 0).
Short: On the chart timeframe, the fast moving average crosses below the slow moving average (fast was above slow on the prior bar and is below it on the current bar) AND, when the RSI filter is enabled, RSI is below 50 AND (confirmation is disabled OR a prior downward cross is pending, the cross bar’s high is at least the confirmation threshold above the current low, and current open is at least current close) AND the current high is at least the flat threshold above or below the high of the previous short signal (initially 0).
