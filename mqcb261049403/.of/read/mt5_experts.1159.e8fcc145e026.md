Calculation: Short/long channel median = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the shorter/longer lookback on the configured indicator timeframe; use completed bars, which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: (Previous completed short channel median <= previous completed long channel median AND latest completed short channel median > latest completed long channel median) OR (Previous completed close <= the greater of the previous completed short and long channel medians AND latest completed close > the greater of the latest completed short and long channel medians)
Short: (Previous completed short channel median >= previous completed long channel median AND latest completed short channel median < latest completed long channel median) OR (Previous completed close >= the lesser of the previous completed short and long channel medians AND latest completed close < the lesser of the latest completed short and long channel medians)
Exit: long, (Previous completed short channel median >= previous completed long channel median AND latest completed short channel median < latest completed long channel median) OR (Previous completed close >= the lesser of the previous completed short and long channel medians AND latest completed close < the lesser of the latest completed short and long channel medians)
Exit: short, (Previous completed short channel median <= previous completed long channel median AND latest completed short channel median > latest completed long channel median) OR (Previous completed close <= the greater of the previous completed short and long channel medians AND latest completed close > the greater of the latest completed short and long channel medians)
