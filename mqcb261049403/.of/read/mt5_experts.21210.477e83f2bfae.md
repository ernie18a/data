Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of open, high, low, close, median price, typical price, weighted close, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, trend-following price, or DeMark price. Trend-following price is either high when close > open and low when close < open, or (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 when close < open; both variants equal close when close = open. DeMark price = (x - low + x - high)/2, where x = (high + low + close + low)/2 when close < open, (high + low + close + high)/2 when close > open, and (high + low + close + close)/2 when close = open.
Calculation: Double weighted average = a linear weighted moving average of a linear weighted moving average of selected price, using the same lookback for both averages and the greatest weight on the newest bar. Calculate it on the chart timeframe for indicator markings and independently on a configurable higher or lower timeframe for the trading system; the latter uses either the last completed bar or the still-forming bar, according to the signal-bar setting.
Long: Chart-timeframe double weighted average > its previous-bar value.
Short: Chart-timeframe double weighted average < its previous-bar value.
Long: Other-timeframe double weighted average > its previous-bar value AND its previous-bar value <= its value two bars earlier.
Short: Other-timeframe double weighted average < its previous-bar value AND its previous-bar value >= its value two bars earlier.
Exit: long, other-timeframe double weighted average < its previous-bar value.
Exit: short, other-timeframe double weighted average > its previous-bar value.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
