Calculation: Source price = a selected OHLC price, median, typical, weighted, OHLC average, or body midpoint; alternatively apply the same selections to Heikin-Ashi OHLC. Trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise; extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise. For Heikin-Ashi, close = (open + high + low + close)/4, open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close)/2, initialized to (open + close)/2, high = max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close), and low = min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close).
Calculation: Adaptive CCI = CCI of the selected price after optional SMA, EMA, smoothed MA, or linear weighted MA smoothing. Optionally set its lookback each bar to ceil(base CCI lookback × -ln(VHF)); use the base lookback when VHF is zero. VHF = (highest source price - lowest source price) / sum of absolute consecutive source-price changes over the base CCI lookback, or zero when the sum is zero. The CCI average and mean absolute deviation use the current adaptive lookback; CCI is zero when the deviation is zero. In higher-timeframe mode, use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: CCI levels = either floating levels or quantile levels over the level lookback, including the current CCI. Floating upper and lower levels are the lookback minimum plus their respective selected fractions of the range; the middle is the range midpoint. Quantile upper and lower levels are the selected quantiles, and the middle is the quantile at the mean of their probabilities; calculate quantiles by linear interpolation between adjacent sorted values. The upper fraction or probability is greater than the lower one. If the level lookback is disabled or equals one, use slope mode.
Calculation: Direction state = in outer-level mode, bullish when CCI > upper level, bearish when CCI < lower level, neutral when lower level < CCI < upper level, and otherwise the previous state; in middle-level mode, bullish when CCI > middle level, bearish when CCI < middle level, and otherwise the previous state; in slope mode, bullish when CCI > previous CCI, bearish when CCI < previous CCI, and otherwise the previous state. Initialize the state as neutral.
Long: Direction state = bullish
Short: Direction state = bearish
