Calculation: Period candle = smooth each OHLC series with the same XMA method and length; open is the smoothed open from the oldest bar in the lookback, high is the maximum smoothed high in the lookback (at least open and close), low is the minimum smoothed low in the lookback (at most open and close), and close is the current bar’s smoothed close.
Calculation: Candle midpoint = (period-candle high + period-candle low) / 2.
Long: On the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (same as the chart timeframe in the documented setup), period-candle open > candle midpoint AND period-candle close > candle midpoint.
Short: On the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe (same as the chart timeframe in the documented setup), period-candle open < candle midpoint AND period-candle close < candle midpoint.
Exit: Long, on the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe, period-candle open < period-candle close AND (period-candle open < candle midpoint OR period-candle close < candle midpoint) OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, on the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe, period-candle open >= period-candle close AND (period-candle open > candle midpoint OR period-candle close > candle midpoint) OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price − take-profit distance.
