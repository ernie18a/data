Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of close, open, high, low, (high + low)/2, (high + low + close)/3, (high + low + 2 × close)/4, (open + close)/2, (open + high + low + close)/4, trend-follow price, smoothed trend-follow price, or DeMark price. Trend-follow price is high if close > open, low if close < open, otherwise close. Smoothed trend-follow price is (high + close)/2 if close > open, (low + close)/2 if close < open, otherwise close. DeMark price is (x - low - high)/2, where x = (high + low + close + low)/2 if close < open, (high + low + close + high)/2 if close > open, otherwise (high + low + 2 × close)/2.
Calculation: Rounded double LWMA = selected price smoothed twice with linear weighted moving averages of the same lookback, with the newest observation weighted most, plus a configurable price offset, rounded to a configurable price increment.
Calculation: Trend state = positive when rounded double LWMA > its previous-bar value, negative when it < its previous-bar value, otherwise the previous trend state.
Long: Chart-timeframe trend state > 0 OR chart-timeframe selected price > rounded double LWMA.
Short: Chart-timeframe trend state < 0 OR chart-timeframe selected price < rounded double LWMA.
Long: In any of three independent systems, trend state > 0 AND previous-bar trend state < 1; evaluate on that system's last completed indicator bar, or its still-forming bar if configured. Each system uses its own chart, higher, or lower timeframe and independent parameters; the three indicator timeframes are ordered longest to shortest.
Short: In any of three independent systems, trend state < 0 AND previous-bar trend state > -1; evaluate on that system's last completed indicator bar, or its still-forming bar if configured.
Exit: long, the corresponding system's trend state < 0 on its last completed indicator bar, or its still-forming bar if configured, OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR high >= entry price + take-profit distance.
Exit: short, the corresponding system's trend state > 0 on its last completed indicator bar, or its still-forming bar if configured, OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR low <= entry price - take-profit distance.
