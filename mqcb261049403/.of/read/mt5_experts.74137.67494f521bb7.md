Long: On a new bar, if the last completed higher-timeframe bar is the selected weekday and (the ATR filter is disabled or its high − low ≥ minimum-range multiple × ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar), go long when direction mode is continuation and that weekday bar's close > open, or when direction mode is reversal and its close ≤ open
Short: On a new bar, if the last completed higher-timeframe bar is the selected weekday and (the ATR filter is disabled or its high − low ≥ minimum-range multiple × ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar), go short when direction mode is continuation and that weekday bar's close ≤ open, or when direction mode is reversal and its close > open
Exit: long, price ≤ entry price − stop-distance OR price ≥ entry price + stop-distance × reward/risk ratio OR hour(datetime) ≥ close hour; stop-distance is a multiple of ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, and stop and target are disabled when the stop-distance multiple is zero
Exit: short, price ≥ entry price + stop-distance OR price ≤ entry price − stop-distance × reward/risk ratio OR hour(datetime) ≥ close hour; stop-distance is a multiple of ATR of the last completed higher-timeframe bar, and stop and target are disabled when the stop-distance multiple is zero
