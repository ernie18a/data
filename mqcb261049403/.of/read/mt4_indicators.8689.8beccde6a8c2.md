Calculation: Stochastic-cross levels = Calculate the SMA stochastic main and signal lines. After an upward cross, and while the most recent stochastic cross is upward, set the upward level to the lower low of the two bars at the most recent earlier upward cross found within a lookback, excluding the current and immediately preceding bars. After a downward cross, and while the most recent stochastic cross is downward, set the downward level to the higher high of the two bars at the most recent earlier downward cross found within the same lookback and exclusions. Carry each level forward when it is not updated.
Calculation: Stochastic stop lines = Carry the long and short stop lines forward. An upward or downward level cross means the previous close was respectively at or below, or at or above, the previous level and the current close is respectively above or below the current level. On an upward cross of the upward level, mark a long signal and set the long stop to max(previous low, current upward level). On an upward cross of the downward level, set the long stop to max(previous low, current downward level) and mark a long signal if the previous long stop was absent. On a downward cross of the downward level, mark a short signal and set the short stop to min(previous high, current downward level). On a downward cross of the upward level, set the short stop to min(previous high, current upward level) and mark a short signal if the previous short stop was absent. Apply these updates in the stated order. If the previous bar had a long signal and the current bar has no short signal, clear the short stop; if the previous bar had a short signal and the current bar has no long signal, clear the long stop. Finally, raise an existing long stop to at least the lower low of the previous two bars, and lower an existing short stop to at most the higher high of the previous two bars.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi open = The average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and previous Heikin-Ashi close; initialize it with the preceding bar's OHLC average. Heikin-Ashi close is the current bar's OHLC average.
Long: On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its still-forming bar, the MACD main line using a shorter and a longer EMA is above its previous value AND the long stochastic stop line exists AND Heikin-Ashi open is above that stop AND Heikin-Ashi close is above that stop.
Short: On the chart timeframe or a selected higher or lower timeframe, using its still-forming bar, the MACD main line using a shorter and a longer EMA is below its previous value AND the short stochastic stop line exists AND Heikin-Ashi open is below that stop AND Heikin-Ashi close is below that stop.
