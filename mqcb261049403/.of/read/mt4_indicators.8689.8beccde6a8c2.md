Calculation: MACD change = current MACD main minus previous bar's MACD main, using a shorter fast lookback and a longer slow lookback.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi open = average of the previous bar's Heikin-Ashi open and close; Heikin-Ashi close = average of the current bar's open, high, low, and close. Use the current bar, which may still be forming, on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Buy-side stochastic stop = the indicator's buy-side stop level on the chart timeframe, calculated from stochastic main/signal crossovers and closes crossing the most recent buy-side level. At each up-cross, the level is the lower of the low on the crossover bar and the preceding bar; search back up to 50 bars for that crossover. Carry the level forward, update it on close crossovers, clamp it to the lower of the preceding two lows, and clear it when the opposite-side signal replaces it. Use the current, possibly forming bar.
Calculation: Sell-side stochastic stop = the indicator's sell-side stop level on the chart timeframe, calculated from stochastic main/signal crossovers and closes crossing the most recent sell-side level. At each down-cross, the level is the higher of the high on the crossover bar and the preceding bar; search back up to 50 bars for that crossover. Carry the level forward, update it on close crossovers, clamp it to the higher of the preceding two highs, and clear it when the opposite-side signal replaces it. Use the current, possibly forming bar.
Long: MACD change > 0 AND buy-side stochastic stop exists AND Heikin-Ashi open > buy-side stochastic stop AND Heikin-Ashi close > buy-side stochastic stop
Short: MACD change < 0 AND sell-side stochastic stop exists AND Heikin-Ashi open < sell-side stochastic stop AND Heikin-Ashi close < sell-side stochastic stop
