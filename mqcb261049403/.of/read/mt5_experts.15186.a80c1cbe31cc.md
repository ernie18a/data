Calculation: Adaptive Williams %R = Williams %R using the shortest lookback after a gap event, the intermediate lookback after a close-displacement event (which takes precedence if both events occur), and otherwise the longest lookback. A gap event occurs when, within the current and preceding eight bars, |open − prior close| is at least twice the mean of |high − low| over the current and preceding nine bars. A close-displacement event occurs when, within the current and preceding five bars, |close three bars later − close| is at least 4.6 times that mean. The three lookbacks are short, intermediate, and long, respectively.
Calculation: ASCtrend marking = Let q = 100 − |Adaptive Williams %R|. Define lower and upper thresholds symmetrically around 50. A buy marking occurs when q is above the upper threshold and, scanning backward through bars where q is between the thresholds, the first value outside that range is below the lower threshold. A sell marking occurs when q is below the lower threshold and the first value outside that range while scanning backward through the middle range is above the upper threshold. Evaluate the marking on the last completed bar of the indicator timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: ASCtrend buy marking
Short: ASCtrend sell marking
Exit: long, ASCtrend sell marking
Exit: short, ASCtrend buy marking
