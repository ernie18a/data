Calculation: Smoothed price series = the same configurable smoothing method and parameters applied separately to open, high, low, and selected price, where selected price is close or (high + low) / 2. Smoothing methods are SMA, EMA, SMMA, LWMA, Jurik moving average, parabolic moving average, T3, VIDYA, adaptive moving average, or ultralinear moving average. Parabolic moving average is the fitted value at the newest bar of a quadratic least-squares regression over the smoothing lookback. For the ultralinear moving average, set a = 3 / (smoothing length + 2); initialize all six stages to the first input and update sequentially: s1 = (1-a)s1 + a × input; s2 = a × s1 + (1-a)s2; s3 = (1-a)s3 + a × (1.5s1 - 0.5s2); s4 = a × s3 + (1-a)s4; s5 = (1-a)s5 + a × (1.5s3 - 0.5s4); s6 = a × s5 + (1-a)s6; output 1.5s5 - 0.5s6.
Calculation: Quality increment = let H, L, O, and C be the smoothed high, low, open, and selected price; let P be C a configurable number of bars earlier; let R = max(H - L, H - P, P - L) and D = H - L. If R and D are nonzero, the increment is abs(((C - P) / R + (C - O) / D) / 2) × ((C - P + C - O) / 2); otherwise it is the current selected price. Replace the increment with zero when its absolute value is less than the configurable price-unit filter.
Calculation: Quality direction = the sign of the latest nonzero quality increment, carrying the preceding direction forward when the increment is zero. Calculate either on the chart timeframe or on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar; for the higher-timeframe version, sample the direction at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar and carry it forward until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Quality direction > 0.
Short: Quality direction < 0.
Long: Quality direction on the configurable signal bar > 0 AND quality direction on the preceding chart bar < 0.
Short: Quality direction on the configurable signal bar < 0 AND quality direction on the preceding chart bar > 0.
