Calculation: Volatility Quality line = cumulative sum of volatility-quality increments, starting from the applied price. Smooth the applied price, open, high, and low with the same configured moving-average method and lookback; let current and previous smoothed applied prices be C and C1, and current smoothed open, high, and low be O, H, and L. Let R1 = max(H − L, H − C1, C1 − L) and R2 = H − L. If R1 and R2 are nonzero, the increment is abs(((C − C1)/R1 + (C − O)/R2) × 0.5) × ((C − C1 + C − O) × 0.5); otherwise it is the applied price. Add the increment to the prior line value, except retain the prior value when the absolute increment is below the configured price filter. For decisions, use the line on the higher timeframe, aligned to each chart bar; its bar may still be forming.
