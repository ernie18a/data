Calculation: Oscillator = Awesome Oscillator: shorter-period SMA of median price minus longer-period SMA of median price.
Calculation: Fast line = shorter-period moving average of the oscillator.
Calculation: Slow line = longer-period moving average of the fast line, using the same averaging method.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe lines = the fast and slow lines computed on the higher timeframe's still-forming bar at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe bar, then held until the next higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Fast line > slow line OR fast line = slow line AND the most recent unequal comparison had fast line > slow line.
