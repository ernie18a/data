Calculation: Anchored momentum = 100 × (EMA of the selected OHLC-derived price / SMA of the same price − 1), with equal EMA and SMA periods; undefined when the SMA is zero. Calculate separately on chart bars and on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Chart-timeframe anchored momentum > upper threshold
Short: Chart-timeframe anchored momentum <= upper threshold AND anchored momentum < lower threshold
Long: Higher-timeframe anchored momentum > upper threshold
Short: Higher-timeframe anchored momentum <= upper threshold AND anchored momentum < lower threshold
