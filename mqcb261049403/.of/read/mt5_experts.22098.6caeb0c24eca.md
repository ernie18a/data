Calculation: Channel high and channel low = highest high and lowest low of chart bars whose opening times fall within the configured channel start and end times on the current day, inclusive; use the still-forming chart bar if it falls in that interval.
Calculation: Pending entry = at each new bar of an execution timeframe lower than or equal to the chart timeframe, if no pending entry exists and the current time is within the configured placement window, inclusive, use the last completed chart bar to check the entry conditions. A qualifying long creates a buy stop at channel high plus the order-price offset; otherwise, a qualifying short creates a sell stop at channel low minus the order-price offset. Keep the pending entry at its original price until triggered or until the current time is later than the placement window end, when it is canceled.
Long: Last completed chart bar low < channel low minus the breakthrough distance AND high reaches the pending buy-stop price before cancellation.
Short: Last completed chart bar low >= channel low minus the breakthrough distance AND last completed chart bar high > channel high plus the breakthrough distance AND low reaches the pending sell-stop price before cancellation.
Exit: Long, stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price minus the stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND high >= entry price plus the take-profit distance.
Exit: Short, stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price plus the stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND low <= entry price minus the take-profit distance.
