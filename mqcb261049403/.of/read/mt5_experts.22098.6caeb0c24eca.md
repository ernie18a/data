Calculation: Channel high = highest high from the channel start time through the channel end time on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Channel low = lowest low from the channel start time through the channel end time on the chart timeframe
Long: Current time is within the order placement window AND the previous completed chart bar low < Channel low − channel breakthrough
Short: Current time is within the order placement window AND the previous completed chart bar high > Channel high + channel breakthrough AND the previous completed chart bar low >= Channel low − channel breakthrough
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − stop loss OR high >= entry price + take profit
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + stop loss OR low <= entry price − take profit
