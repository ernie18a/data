Calculation: First smoothed RSI = moving average of RSI of a selected bar price.
Calculation: Second smoothed RSI = moving average of the first smoothed RSI, with independently selected smoothing method and parameters.
Long: First smoothed RSI > second smoothed RSI.
Short: First smoothed RSI < second smoothed RSI.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe first and second smoothed RSI = the same calculations on higher-timeframe bars, sampled from the still-forming higher-timeframe bar at the first chart bar of each higher-timeframe period and held until the next period.
Long: Higher-timeframe first smoothed RSI > higher-timeframe second smoothed RSI.
Short: Higher-timeframe first smoothed RSI < higher-timeframe second smoothed RSI.
