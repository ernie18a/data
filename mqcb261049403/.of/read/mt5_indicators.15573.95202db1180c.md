Calculation: First smoothed RSI = RSI of the selected applied price, smoothed with a configurable moving-average method; for the higher-timeframe variant, use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Second smoothed RSI = first smoothed RSI smoothed again with a configurable moving-average method and a shorter lookback than the first smoothing.
Long: First smoothed RSI > second smoothed RSI
Short: First smoothed RSI < second smoothed RSI
