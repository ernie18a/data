Long: At the start of a new chart bar, the slow EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is higher on the last completed bar than on the preceding bar AND the fast EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is higher on the forming bar than on the last completed bar AND the CCI of typical price on the forming CCI-timeframe bar, truncated toward zero to an integer, is below the lower threshold.
Short: At the start of a new chart bar, the slow EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is lower on the last completed bar than on the preceding bar AND the fast EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is lower on the forming bar than on the last completed bar AND the CCI of typical price on the forming CCI-timeframe bar, truncated toward zero to an integer, is above the upper threshold.
Exit: long, at the start of a new chart bar, the slow EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is less than or equal to its value on the preceding MA-timeframe bar, using the last completed MA-timeframe bar.
Exit: short, at the start of a new chart bar, the slow EMA of median price on the MA timeframe is greater than or equal to its value on the preceding MA-timeframe bar, using the last completed MA-timeframe bar.
Calculation: The MA timeframe and CCI timeframe are independently configurable as higher than, lower than, or equal to the chart timeframe; the fast EMA has a shorter period than the slow EMA.
