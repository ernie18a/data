Clamp phase change to [0.1, 1.1], and take the median of the five buffer values. Dominant cycle = 15 if the median is zero, otherwise 6.28318 / median + 0.5. Instantaneous period = 0.67 × previous instantaneous period + 0.33 × dominant cycle; cycle period = 0.85 × previous cycle period + 0.15 × instantaneous period. Initialize both periods to 1 and historical series and buffer values to zero.
