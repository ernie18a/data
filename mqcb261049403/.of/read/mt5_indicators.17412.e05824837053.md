Calculation: Selected price = a configurable choice of open, high, low, close, median (high + low)/2, body midpoint (open + close)/2, typical (high + low + close)/3, weighted (high + low + 2 × close)/4, average (open + high + low + close)/4, trend-biased price, or extreme trend-biased price; each choice may instead use the corresponding Heikin-Ashi OHLC values. Trend-biased price is (high + close)/2 when close > open and (low + close)/2 otherwise. Extreme trend-biased price is high when close > open, low when close < open, and close otherwise.
Calculation: Bands and direction = on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its bar still forming, calculate a configurable moving average and standard deviation of selected price using independently configurable lookbacks and optional sample correction. Raw upper band = moving average + deviation multiplier × standard deviation; raw lower band = moving average - deviation multiplier × standard deviation. Initialize direction to 0. On each subsequent bar, set direction to 1 if selected price > previous upper band, otherwise to -1 if selected price < previous lower band, otherwise retain previous direction. Set upper band to the minimum of raw upper band and previous upper band when direction = -1, otherwise to raw upper band. Set lower band to the maximum of raw lower band and previous lower band when direction = 1, otherwise to raw lower band. Initialize both bands to their raw values.
Long: Direction = 1 AND previous chart bar direction != 1
Short: Direction = -1 AND previous chart bar direction != -1
