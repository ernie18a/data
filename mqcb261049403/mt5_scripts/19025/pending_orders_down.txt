The "Pending orders DOWN" script sets a grid of orders below the current price.
Input Parameters
Gap for pending orders DOWN from the current price (in pips)
- the distance from the current price to place the first pending order.
Step between orders DOWN (in pips)
- distance between pending orders.
Type of pending orders DOWN
- the type of the pending order (can be either Buy Limit or Sell Stop).
DOWN quantity
- the number of orders to be placed.
Lots
- the volume of each order.
Stop Loss (in pips)
- Stop Loss value.
Take Profit (in pips)
- Take Profit value.

asynchronous mode of trading operations
:
m_trade.SetAsyncMode(
true
);
