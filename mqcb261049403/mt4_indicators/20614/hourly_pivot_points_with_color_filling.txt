This indicator uses two ways to calculate R1/R2/R3/S1/S2/S3: normal formula and Fibonacci, and users can choose one from them.
Main parameters
PivotMode
:
Normal
: use normal formula to calculate S and R.
Fibonacci
: use Fibonacci to calculate S and R.
.
CurrentHourOnly
: only calculate lastet hourly PP if true.
