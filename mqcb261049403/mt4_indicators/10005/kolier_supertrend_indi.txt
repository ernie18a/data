What's Kolier_SuperTrend_Indi
Same logic, improvement implementation in code, can draw full chart and less resource consume.
Extended. TrendMode=0 means original logic for trend line. TrendMode=1 means new logic for trend line.
TrendMode=0
TrendMode=1
Parameters
BarsToCount
How many bars to draw trend line.
TrendMode
TrendMode=0 means original logic for trend line. TrendMode=1 means new logic for trend line.
ATR_Period
ATR Period settings.
ATR_Multiplier
In order to calculate upper and lower bands with ATR Range = Multiplier * ATR.
