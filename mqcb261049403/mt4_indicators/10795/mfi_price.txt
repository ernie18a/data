Indicator Money Flow Index, MFI uses typical price for its creation. I used
standard code
of the indicator and just added possibility to select all types of
applied prices
.
Input parameters:
Applied_price:
0 – close price,
1 – open price,
2 - high price,
3 - low price,
4 –median price, (high+low)/2,
5 - typical price,, (high+low+close)/3,
6 - weighted close price, (high+low+close+close)/4.
Indicator MFI_price
