AROON Indicator is great for reading trends, watch as the "Up Line" (Default color:Yellow) reaches 100 means a strong up trend, and watch for the "Down Line" (Default color:Dodger Blue) reaches 100 means a strong down trend. AROON Indicator watchs also as they crosses each other. It's great for signaling.
Recomendation:
interval: more than 30 minutes;
period : 20.
