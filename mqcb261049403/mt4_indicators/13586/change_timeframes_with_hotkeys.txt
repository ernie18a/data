This simple indicator will change the timeframes of the current chart with keypress.
I have added yet another functionality to it to fix/unfix chart scale as well as show/hide price scale.
Hotkeys:
1 = 1 minute
2 = 5 minutes
3 = 15 minutes
4 = 30 minutes
5 = 60 minutes (1 hour)
6 = 240 minutes (4 hour)
7 = 1440 minutes (daily)
8 = 10080 minutes (weekly)
9 = 43200 minutes (monthly)
0 = Chart Scale Fix/Unfix
p = Show/Hide Price Scale on the Right
