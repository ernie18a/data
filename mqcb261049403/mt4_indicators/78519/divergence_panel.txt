Divergence Panel: Spot Market Reversals Fast
A small panel on your chart that tells you when the price and the indicators are “disagreeing.”
Traders call this a divergence, and it can be an early hint that the market may turn around.
🟩 Green square = possible move up
🟥 Red square = possible move down
⬛ Dark square = nothing yet
Each square is a different indicator (RSI, MACD, and 8 more).
More squares of the same color = more indicators agree...
All timeframes at once, from M1 to monthly
6 columns: five bars ago and the bar happening right now
It’s a helper tool, not a crystal ball... Protect first & then profit!
