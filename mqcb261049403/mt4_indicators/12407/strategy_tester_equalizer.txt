The indicator allows to control the Strategy Tester speed.
You can slow down or speed up the speed of Strategy Tester when set to 32.
Speed
: decrease speed (less or equal to zero)
Skip Tick
: increase speed (greater or equal to zero)
Step
: interval (greater or equal to one)
Video:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uh-y9dcopmI
.
Requirements:
Checked '
Allow DLL imports
' before running the indicator.
