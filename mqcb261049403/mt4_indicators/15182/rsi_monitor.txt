Major Upgrades & New Features
Zero-Lag RSI Engine:
An optional delagged calculation mode that reacts significantly faster to price changes than standard RSI, giving you an edge in fast-moving markets.
Professional Divergence Detection:
Automated identification of Bullish and Bearish price-RSI divergences, qualified by Overbought/Oversold zone validation to filter out noise.

An intelligent aggregator that scans all timeframes (M1 to MN1) to calculate a total bias score, helping you identify high-probability "Strong Bias" zones.

New trend detection logic based on percentage-based volatility, making it highly sensitive to real momentum shifts.
