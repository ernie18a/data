What the panel shows:
Technical sentiment (proxy)
– bullish/bearish score calculated from EMA + MACD + Stochastic
Trend Riding
– direction (UPTREND / DOWNTREND / SIDEWAYS), stage (nascent / established / aging / reversal risk), momentum
Volatility & Breakout
– ATR, Bollinger squeeze, Donchian channel, breakout state (confirmed / false / testing)
Carry Trade
– long/short swap (who pays or receives overnight interest)
News Straddling
– the next important economic event for the pair’s currencies
What is drawn on the chart:
EMA Fast / Mid / Slow, Bollinger Bands, Donchian channel.
How it helps in real live trading
Saves time
– you no longer jump between 5–6 indicator windows; you see trend, volatility and news in one place.
Context filter
– before entering, you check: clear trend? squeeze? real or false breakout? news in 30 minutes?
Reduces impulsive entries
– signals such as “FALSE BREAK – FADE”, “REVERSAL RISK”, “SIDEWAYS” keep you away from ranges and false breakouts.
News timing
– countdown to high-impact events (NFP, rates, CPI, etc.) so you can avoid or straddle the volatility.
Carry
– on H4/D1 you can prefer the direction that earns positive swap.
Important limitations:
Sentiment is
technical (proxy)
, not COT data or real fundamentals.
It does not give automatic entry/exit; the decision remains yours.
The economic calendar depends on the broker’s server.
This is not financial advice – it is an educational context tool.

Practical live trading methods with this indicator
1. Trend Riding on H1 / H4 (main strategy)
Wait for
UPTREND
or
DOWNTREND
+ stage
2-ESTABLISHED
+ momentum
CONFIRMING
.
Sentiment ≥ BULLISH (or ≤ BEARISH on short).
Price pulls back to EMA Fast or Mid; enter on the retracement, stop beyond EMA Mid/Slow or the opposite Donchian edge.
Avoid entry if stage is
4-REVERSAL RISK
or momentum is
DIVERGING
.
Stay out 30–60 minutes before a high-impact news event (News section).
2. Breakout from squeeze (S5 → S4)
Look for the
[SQUEEZE]
label on Bollinger width percentile.
Wait for
CONFIRMED UP BREAKOUT
or
CONFIRMED DOWN BREAKOUT
(not just “TESTING”).
Enter in the breakout direction; stop inside the channel or under/mid the Bollinger band.
If
FALSE … BREAK – FADE
appears, do not chase the breakout – it is a fade setup (see method 3).
3. Fade on false breakout (S3)
The panel shows
FALSE UP BREAK – FADE
or
FALSE DOWN BREAK – FADE
.
Price left the Donchian channel and came back inside.
Enter against the failed breakout (short after false up), stop slightly beyond the breakout extremes.
Target: middle of the range or the opposite Bollinger band. Works better in
SIDEWAYS
, with no immediate news.
4. Straddle / avoid on news (S7)
When News shows an event
in ≤ 30 minutes
(warning color):
either stay flat,
or (if experienced) straddle: buy stop + sell stop above/below the pre-news range, cancel the opposite order after the break.
Do not mix straddle with Trend Riding on the same setup – they are different styles.
5. Carry bias on D1 (S6)
On pairs with clearly positive swap on one side (e.g. LONG carry +).
Prefer only trades in the positive-carry direction when the trend (S2) is aligned.
Hold overnight only if swap helps you and the stage is not
REVERSAL RISK
.
6. Simple multi-timeframe filter
On H4:
ESTABLISHED
trend + clear sentiment.
On M15/H1: wait for a pullback to EMA Fast/Mid or a confirmed breakout from a micro-range.
Do not enter on H1 if H4 is
SIDEWAYS
or
REVERSAL RISK
.
