The essence and logic behind the script:
Scanning:
Retrieves all financial instruments open in the ‘Market Watch’ window
.
R² trend smoothness filter:
Calculates a linear regression over a specified number of bars (50 by default)
. If the price action is too choppy and the
R²
coefficient
is less than 0.70
, the pair is rejected
.
Trend Strength Filter (ADX):
Retrieves the ADX indicator values
. If the value is below 25.0, the pair is excluded
.
Data output:
For pairs that pass the filter, the trend direction (UP/DOWN), the precision of the movement and the ADX value are displayed in the ‘Experts’ tab
.
How to use:
Simply drag and drop the script onto any hourly (H1) chart. It will immediately scan the chart and display a list of the identified trending pairs in the ‘Experts’ tab at the bottom of the terminal.
