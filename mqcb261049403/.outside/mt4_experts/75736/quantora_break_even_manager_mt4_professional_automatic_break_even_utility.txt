Quantora Break Even Manager MT4 is a utility that automatically moves stop loss to the break even level once a defined profit is reached. It does not open trades or generate signals, and focuses only on protecting existing positions.
The tool monitors open trades and applies break even based on your settings. You can define the trigger level and use an optional offset to secure a small profit. It supports managing manual trades, all positions, or trades filtered by magic number, with separate control for buy and sell orders.
It also includes symbol filtering and a simple dashboard for monitoring account and trade data. A one-click option allows you to apply break even instantly to eligible trades.
Suitable for traders who want a simple and consistent way to protect profits automatically.
