A multi-currency Expert Advisor that opens a market position (either Buy or Sell) at the user defined time. You can also set to close the position at a particular time, select currencies, as well as select the percentage of profit or loss to close all positions.
You can specify up to ten symbols in settings (symbols can be repeated). Attention: if you specify a non-existent symbol, the Expert Advisor will be unloaded with an error.
