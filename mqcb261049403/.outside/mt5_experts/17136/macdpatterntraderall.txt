Six (iMACD, MACD, Moving Average Convergence/Divergence) indicators and four (iMA, MA, Moving Average) indicators.
Opens trades when the indicator's MACD crosses its signal line:
Sell when the MACD falls below its signal line, and buy when the it rises above its signal line. It is also popular to buy/sell when the MACD goes above/below zero.
