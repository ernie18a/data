Long: the previous completed bar from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe closed above its open AND the current hour is before the entry-hour threshold AND there are no open orders.
Short: the previous completed bar from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe closed below its open AND the current hour is before the entry-hour threshold AND there are no open orders.
Exit: long, the current hour exceeds the configured market-closing hour by the exit offset.
Exit: short, the current hour exceeds the configured market-closing hour by the exit offset.
