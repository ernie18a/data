Calculation: OsMA(price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, timeframe bar at shift) = EMA(price, fast lookback) − EMA(price, slow lookback) − EMA(EMA(price, fast lookback) − EMA(price, slow lookback), signal lookback)
Long: no open position AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at fourth shift) > OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at third shift) AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at third shift) < OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at second shift) AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at second shift) < OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at first shift)
Short: no open position AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at fourth shift) < OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at third shift) AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at third shift) > OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at second shift) AND OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at second shift) > OsMA(applied price, fast lookback, slow lookback, signal lookback, T1 bar at first shift)
Exit: long, stop loss enabled AND low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance
Exit: short, stop loss enabled AND high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR take profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance
