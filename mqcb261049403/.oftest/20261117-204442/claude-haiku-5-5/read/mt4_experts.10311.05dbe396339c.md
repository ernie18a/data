Calculation: ShortMACDMain = exponential moving average of close over short MACD fast period minus exponential moving average of close over short MACD slow period, on input bars (short MACD fast period < short MACD slow period)
Calculation: ShortMACDSignal = exponential moving average of ShortMACDMain over short MACD signal period, on input bars
Calculation: ShortFastEMA = exponential moving average of median price ((High + Low) / 2) over short fast EMA period, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: ShortSlowEMA = exponential moving average of median price ((High + Low) / 2) over short slow EMA period (short fast EMA period < short slow EMA period), on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: ShortSAR = parabolic SAR with step short SAR step and maximum short SAR maximum, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: ShortBearsPower[n] = Low - exponential moving average of close over short bears power period, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, where [0] is the bar still forming and [1] is the last completed bar
Calculation: LongMACDMain = exponential moving average of close over long MACD fast period minus exponential moving average of close over long MACD slow period, on input bars (long MACD fast period < long MACD slow period)
Calculation: LongMACDSignal = exponential moving average of LongMACDMain over long MACD signal period, on input bars
Calculation: LongFastEMA = exponential moving average of median price ((High + Low) / 2) over long fast EMA period, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: LongSlowEMA = exponential moving average of median price ((High + Low) / 2) over long slow EMA period (long fast EMA period < long slow EMA period), on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: LongSAR = parabolic SAR with step long SAR step and maximum long SAR maximum, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: LongBullsPower[n] = High - exponential moving average of close over long bulls power period, on secondary-timeframe bars aggregated from input bars, where [0] is the bar still forming and [1] is the last completed bar
Calculation: LongUpperBand[n] = simple moving average of Low over band period + band deviation * standard deviation of Low over band period, on input bars, where [n] is n bars back
