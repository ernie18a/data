Calculation: Weighted = (high + low + 2 × close) / 4
Calculation: CCI = commodity channel index of Weighted over period bars
Calculation: Force = linearly weighted moving average over period bars of ((Weighted − Weighted one bar earlier) × volume)
Long: CCI > 0 AND Force > 0 AND CCI × Force >= level
Short: CCI < 0 AND Force < 0 AND CCI × Force >= level
