Long: when flat AND the current hour is strictly between the start-hour and end-hour parameters AND the MACD line is below the MACD signal line on the current bar AND the highs of the two latest completed bars are each below the Bollinger upper band calculated from low prices on their respective bars AND the short-period EMA of median price is above the long-period EMA of median price on the still-forming secondary-timeframe bar AND the parabolic SAR is below the current low on that bar AND Bulls Power is above zero and below its value on the previous completed secondary-timeframe bar
Short: when flat AND the current hour is strictly between the start-hour and end-hour parameters AND the MACD line is above the MACD signal line on the current bar AND the short-period EMA of median price is below the long-period EMA of median price on the still-forming secondary-timeframe bar AND the parabolic SAR is above the current high on that bar AND Bears Power is below zero and above its value on the previous completed secondary-timeframe bar
Exit: long, price reaches entry price minus the long stop-loss distance OR entry price plus the long take-profit distance
Exit: short, price reaches entry price plus the short stop-loss distance OR entry price minus the short take-profit distance
