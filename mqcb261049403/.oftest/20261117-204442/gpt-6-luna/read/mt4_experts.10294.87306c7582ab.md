Calculation: short EMA = EMA of median price over a shorter lookback
Calculation: long EMA = EMA of median price over a longer lookback
Calculation: Bears Power = low minus EMA of close over a separate lookback
Calculation: Bulls Power = high minus EMA of close over a separate lookback
Short: short EMA < long EMA AND Parabolic SAR on the current, still-forming bar > current bar high AND Bears Power on the current, still-forming bar < 0 AND Bears Power on the current, still-forming bar > Bears Power on the previous bar
Long: short EMA > long EMA AND Parabolic SAR on the current, still-forming bar < current bar low AND Bulls Power on the current, still-forming bar > 0 AND Bulls Power on the current, still-forming bar < Bulls Power on the previous bar
