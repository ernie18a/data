Calculation: Trend = sensitivity × (MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, corresponding bar) − MACD(close, shorter lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback, T1, preceding bar)); on chart bars, take values from the corresponding T1 bar, which may still be forming
Long: Trend > 0 AND Trend > Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) AND Trend > dead-zone threshold AND Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) > dead-zone threshold AND 100 × (Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar − Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar) / Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar ≥ explosion-power threshold AND 100 × (Trend − Trend[2 T1 bars ago]) / Trend ≥ trend-power threshold AND Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar > Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar AND Trend > Trend[2 T1 bars ago]
Short: Trend < 0 AND abs(Trend) > Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) AND abs(Trend) > dead-zone threshold AND Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) > dead-zone threshold AND 100 × (Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar − Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar) / Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar ≥ explosion-power threshold AND 100 × (abs(Trend) − abs(Trend[2 T1 bars ago])) / abs(Trend) ≥ trend-power threshold AND Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar > Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar AND abs(Trend) > abs(Trend[2 T1 bars ago])
Exit: long, Trend > 0 AND Trend < Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) AND Trend < Trend[2 T1 bars ago] AND Trend[2 T1 bars ago] > Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar AND Trend > dead-zone threshold AND Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar > dead-zone threshold
Exit: short, Trend < 0 AND abs(Trend) < Bollinger Band width(close, lookback, deviation, T1, corresponding bar) AND abs(Trend) < abs(Trend[2 T1 bars ago]) AND abs(Trend[2 T1 bars ago]) > Bollinger Band width at preceding T1 bar AND Trend > dead-zone threshold AND Bollinger Band width at corresponding T1 bar > dead-zone threshold
