Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum since session start of bar volume × (2 × close − high − low) ÷ (high − low); for zero-range bars, use +volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, and 0 otherwise.
Long: Cumulative volume delta >= 0
Short: Cumulative volume delta < 0
Short: Price forms a higher high while cumulative volume delta forms a lower high
