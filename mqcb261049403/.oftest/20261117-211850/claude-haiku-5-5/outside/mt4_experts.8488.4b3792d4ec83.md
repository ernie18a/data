Go long on the base instrument, together with a long position on the hedge instrument, when all of the following hold: the base instrument's fast EMA (fast EMA period, closing prices, fast EMA bar shift) is above its slow EMA (slow EMA period, closing prices, slow EMA bar shift); the base instrument's RSI (RSI period, closing prices, RSI bar shift) is at or below the RSI oversold level; and the correlation between the base and hedge instruments is negative, where the correlation is the sum, over the current bar and the previous (correlation window length minus one) bars of the correlation timeframe, of the product of the base instrument's deviation of close from its simple moving average (correlation SMA period) and the hedge instrument's deviation of close from its simple moving average (correlation SMA period) at the same bar.
Go short on the base instrument, together with a short position on the hedge instrument, when all of the following hold: the base instrument's fast EMA (fast EMA period, closing prices, fast EMA bar shift) is below its slow EMA (slow EMA period, closing prices, slow EMA bar shift); the base instrument's RSI (RSI period, closing prices, RSI bar shift) is at or above the RSI overbought level; and the correlation between the base and hedge instruments is negative, where the correlation is the sum, over the current bar and the previous (correlation window length minus one) bars of the correlation timeframe, of the product of the base instrument's deviation of close from its simple moving average (correlation SMA period) and the hedge instrument's deviation of close from its simple moving average (correlation SMA period) at the same bar.
