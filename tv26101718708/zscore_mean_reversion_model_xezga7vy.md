## Description

**Z-Score Mean Reversion Model**

The Z-Score Mean Reversion Model is an original quantitative indicator built on one of the most widely used statistical concepts in professional trading. It measures how far price has deviated from its rolling average in terms of standard deviations, giving you an objective, math-based answer to the question every trader asks — is price stretched too far right now?

This indicator runs as two components working together. The oscillator pane shows the Z-Score value bouncing around zero with color-coded zones, a signal line, and a histogram. The info table shows live readings for every metric including the actual dollar levels of each band so you always know exactly where the key levels are without having to guess.

The core formula is simple. Z equals price minus the rolling mean divided by the standard deviation. A reading of zero means price is exactly at its average. A reading of plus or minus two means price is two standard deviations away from its mean — a level that statistically only occurs around five percent of the time. A reading of plus or minus three is extreme territory occurring less than one percent of the time historically. These are the levels where mean reversion trades have the highest probability of working.

This is the same mathematical foundation used by quantitative hedge funds including Renaissance Technologies and Two Sigma, applied in a clean and accessible format for any asset on any timeframe.

---

**How to Use This Indicator**

**Step 1 — Understand the zones**

The oscillator pane is divided into color-coded zones based on how extreme the current Z-Score reading is.

Above plus 3 is the deep red extreme overbought zone. Price is three standard deviations above its mean. This is statistically rare and historically precedes sharp reversals or at minimum a pause in the move. This is where you start watching for red dot sell signals.

Between plus 2 and plus 3 is the red overbought zone. Price is extended above average. Momentum may still be strong but the risk of a reversion is elevated. Avoid chasing new longs here.

Between plus 1 and plus 2 is the orange mild overbought zone. Price is above average but not yet at extremes. Neutral to cautious.

Between minus 1 and plus 1 is the gray neutral zone. Price is near its rolling mean. No statistical edge in either direction from Z-Score alone. Wait for price to move to an extreme before acting.

Between minus 1 and minus 2 is the green mild oversold zone. Price is pulling back below its average. Interesting area to start watching for setups but not yet at extremes.

Between minus 2 and minus 3 is the teal oversold zone. Price is significantly below its mean. This is where you start watching seriously for green dot buy signals.

Below minus 3 is the deep teal extreme oversold zone. Price is three standard deviations below its mean. Historically one of the highest probability mean reversion zones. This is where the best buying opportunities tend to appear.

---

**Step 2 — Read the signal line and histogram**

The yellow line in the oscillator pane is the signal line. It is an EMA smoothing of the Z-Score itself. Think of it the same way you think of the signal line on a MACD.

When the Z-Score line is above the signal line the histogram bars are cyan — momentum is bullish relative to recent average.

When the Z-Score line is below the signal line the histogram bars are red — momentum is bearish relative to recent average.

The crossover between the Z-Score and signal line is what triggers the dot signals. Watch the histogram bars for early warning — when they start shrinking it means momentum is slowing and a crossover may be coming.

---

**Step 3 — Use the green and red dots**

Green dots print when two conditions are met at the same time. First, the Z-Score must have been below the dot threshold level (default minus 2) meaning price was in oversold territory. Second, the Z-Score must then cross back above the signal line meaning momentum is turning back up. Both conditions must be true together. A crossover in the neutral zone does not print a dot — the oversold requirement filters out low quality signals.

Red dots print using the exact same logic in reverse. Z-Score must have been above plus 2 and then cross back down through the signal line.

These dots are your highest quality reversion signals. They are not generated by every crossover — only by crossovers that happen after a statistically significant deviation from the mean.

---

**Step 4 — Use the info table**

The table in the bottom right corner shows everything you need at a glance.

Z-Score is the raw current reading. The color of this cell tells you the zone instantly.

Zone tells you in plain words exactly where you are — Neutral, Mild OB, Overbought, Extreme OB, and the same on the oversold side.

Signal Line is the current value of the yellow EMA line. When Z-Score is below Signal Line momentum is bearish. When above it is bullish.

Histogram tells you if momentum is Expanding Up or Expanding Down — watch this for early warning of direction changes.

Mean Price is the most important number in the table. This is where price gravitates back toward. In a mean reversion trade this is your target. If you enter a long when Z-Score is at minus 2.5, the mean price is where you aim to exit.

Upper Band Z2 and Lower Band Z2 are the actual dollar levels corresponding to the plus 2 and minus 2 Z-Score lines on the current bar. These update every bar as the rolling mean and standard deviation shift. Use these as your key levels for entries and targets.

Signal tells you if a green or red dot is printing right now or if there is no signal on this bar.

---

**Step 5 — Combine with key levels for best results**

The Z-Score tells you when price is statistically stretched. Price structure tells you where the meaningful levels are. Using both together gives you the highest quality setups.

The ideal green dot setup looks like this. Price falls into a known support zone, demand block, or previous swing low. At the same time the Z-Score drops below minus 2 or minus 3. Then the Z-Score crosses back above the signal line and a green dot prints. That combination of statistical oversold plus structural support is where the highest probability mean reversion trades occur.

The ideal red dot setup is the mirror image. Price rallies into a known resistance zone or previous swing high while Z-Score is above plus 2. When the Z-Score crosses back down and a red dot prints at that level it is a high probability short or exit signal.

Never trade a dot signal in empty space with no nearby price structure. The Z-Score measures how stretched price is. Price structure tells you where it is stretched to. You need both.

---

**Step 6 — Tune the settings for your timeframe**

The default lookback period is 50 bars. On a 30 minute chart this covers roughly 25 hours of price action. On a daily chart it covers 50 trading days.

For shorter timeframes like 5 or 15 minutes consider lowering the lookback to 20 or 30 for a faster response.

For higher timeframes like 4 hour or daily consider raising the lookback to 100 or 200 for a smoother and more reliable mean.

The dot signal threshold defaults to 2.0. Raising it to 2.5 or 3.0 produces fewer dots but only at the most extreme levels. This is recommended on noisy lower timeframes. Lowering it produces more frequent signals but with lower individual reliability.

---

**Using it alongside Moon Flow and BTC Power Law**

This indicator is designed to complement the Moon Flow oscillator and the BTC Power Law Probability Channel.

The Power Law tells you the macro picture — is BTC cheap or expensive on a multi-year scale.

Moon Flow tells you the medium term momentum picture — is money flowing in or out and what is the wave momentum doing.

The Z-Score Mean Reversion Model tells you the short term statistical picture — is price stretched too far from its mean right now.

When all three agree it is your highest conviction signal. A green dot on Z-Score plus a green dot on Moon Flow plus the Power Law channel showing below 20th percentile is the kind of multi-timeframe confluence that produces the best risk-reward setups.

## Source Code

````pine
//@version=6
indicator(
     title            = "Z-Score Mean Reversion Model",
     shorttitle       = "ZScore MR",
     overlay          = false,
     max_labels_count = 500
     )

// =======================================================
// Z-SCORE MEAN REVERSION MODEL
// Original implementation - Pine Script v6
// Components:
//   1. Z-Score oscillator with signal line and histogram
//   2. Overlay bands on price chart (via offchart plots)
//   3. Extreme reversion dot signals (green/red)
//   4. Color-coded zone fills
//   5. Live info table
// =======================================================

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 1 - INPUTS
// -------------------------------------------------------

var string G_CORE  = "Core Settings"
var string G_BAND  = "Band Settings"
var string G_SIG   = "Signal Settings"
var string G_DISP  = "Display"
var string G_ALERT = "Alerts"

// Core
i_src        = input.source(close,  "Price Source",        group = G_CORE)
i_len        = input.int(50,        "Lookback Period",
     tooltip  = "Number of bars used to calculate mean and standard deviation. Higher = slower, smoother. Lower = faster, noisier.",
     group    = G_CORE, minval = 2, maxval = 500)
i_sigLen     = input.int(9,         "Signal Line Length",
     tooltip  = "EMA of the Z-Score line. Crossovers with this line generate signals.",
     group    = G_CORE, minval = 1)

// Bands
i_z1         = input.float(1.0,  "Band 1 - Z Level",   group = G_BAND, step = 0.1, minval = 0.1)
i_z2         = input.float(2.0,  "Band 2 - Z Level",   group = G_BAND, step = 0.1, minval = 0.1)
i_z3         = input.float(3.0,  "Band 3 - Z Level",   group = G_BAND, step = 0.1, minval = 0.1)

// Signal settings
i_dotThresh  = input.float(2.0,  "Dot Signal Threshold",
     tooltip  = "Z-Score must exceed this level (positive or negative) before a reversal dot can print.",
     group    = G_SIG, step = 0.1, minval = 0.5)
i_showDots   = input.bool(true,  "Show Reversal Dots",  group = G_SIG)
i_showSig    = input.bool(true,  "Show Signal Line",    group = G_SIG)
i_showHist   = input.bool(true,  "Show Histogram",      group = G_SIG)

// Display
i_showFill   = input.bool(true,  "Show Zone Fills",     group = G_DISP)
i_showTable  = input.bool(true,  "Show Info Table",     group = G_DISP)
i_colorBars  = input.bool(true,  "Color Bars by Zone",  group = G_DISP)
i_tablePos   = input.string("Bottom Right", "Table Position",
     options  = ["Top Right", "Top Left", "Bottom Right", "Bottom Left"],
     group    = G_DISP)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 2 - COLORS
// -------------------------------------------------------

// Z-Score line colors by zone
color COL_EXTREME_UP  = color.new(#b71c1c, 0)   // deep red   - extremely overbought
color COL_HIGH_UP     = color.new(#ef5350, 0)   // red        - overbought
color COL_MID_UP      = color.new(#ff8a65, 0)   // orange     - mildly overbought
color COL_NEUTRAL     = color.new(#90a4ae, 0)   // gray       - neutral zone
color COL_MID_DN      = color.new(#66bb6a, 0)   // light green - mildly oversold
color COL_HIGH_DN     = color.new(#26a69a, 0)   // teal       - oversold
color COL_EXTREME_DN  = color.new(#004d40, 30)  // deep teal  - extremely oversold

// Signal and histogram
color COL_SIG_LINE    = color.new(#ffd600, 0)   // yellow signal line
color COL_HIST_UP     = color.new(#26c6da, 60)  // cyan histogram bull
color COL_HIST_DN     = color.new(#ef5350, 60)  // red histogram bear

// Fills
color COL_FILL_OB3    = color.new(#b71c1c, 88)
color COL_FILL_OB2    = color.new(#ef5350, 88)
color COL_FILL_OS2    = color.new(#26a69a, 88)
color COL_FILL_OS3    = color.new(#004d40, 88)

// Dots
color COL_DOT_BUY     = color.new(#00e676, 0)
color COL_DOT_SELL    = color.new(#ff1744, 0)

// Zero line
color COL_ZERO        = color.new(#ffffff, 70)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 3 - CORE Z-SCORE CALCULATION
// Z = (price - mean) / standard deviation
// Measures how many standard deviations price is
// above or below its rolling mean
// -------------------------------------------------------

float pMean  = ta.sma(i_src, i_len)
float pStdev = ta.stdev(i_src, i_len)

// Guard against zero stdev (flat price periods)
float zScore = pStdev > 1.0e-10 ? (i_src - pMean) / pStdev : 0.0

// Signal line - EMA of Z-Score
float zSig   = ta.ema(zScore, i_sigLen)

// Histogram - difference between Z-Score and signal
float zHist  = zScore - zSig

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 4 - OVERLAY BAND VALUES
// These are the actual price levels corresponding to
// each Z-Score band - used for the overlay chart
// -------------------------------------------------------

float overlayUpper3 = pMean + pStdev * i_z3
float overlayUpper2 = pMean + pStdev * i_z2
float overlayUpper1 = pMean + pStdev * i_z1
float overlayMean   = pMean
float overlayLower1 = pMean - pStdev * i_z1
float overlayLower2 = pMean - pStdev * i_z2
float overlayLower3 = pMean - pStdev * i_z3

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 5 - ZONE CLASSIFICATION
// Classify current Z-Score into a readable zone
// -------------------------------------------------------

f_zone(float z) =>
    string s = "Neutral"
    if z >= i_z3
        s := "Extreme OB"
    else if z >= i_z2
        s := "Overbought"
    else if z >= i_z1
        s := "Mild OB"
    else if z <= -i_z3
        s := "Extreme OS"
    else if z <= -i_z2
        s := "Oversold"
    else if z <= -i_z1
        s := "Mild OS"
    s

f_zoneColor(float z) =>
    color c = color.new(color.gray, 30)
    if z >= i_z3
        c := color.new(color.red, 10)
    else if z >= i_z2
        c := color.new(#ef5350, 20)
    else if z >= i_z1
        c := color.new(color.orange, 30)
    else if z <= -i_z3
        c := color.new(#004d40, 20)
    else if z <= -i_z2
        c := color.new(#26a69a, 20)
    else if z <= -i_z1
        c := color.new(color.green, 30)
    c

f_tablePos(string s) =>
    string r = position.bottom_right
    if s == "Top Right"
        r := position.top_right
    else if s == "Top Left"
        r := position.top_left
    else if s == "Bottom Left"
        r := position.bottom_left
    r

f_fmt(float v) =>
    string s = ""
    if v >= 1.0e9
        s := str.tostring(math.round(v / 1.0e9, 2)) + "B"
    else if v >= 1.0e6
        s := str.tostring(math.round(v / 1.0e6, 2)) + "M"
    else if v >= 1.0e3
        s := str.tostring(math.round(v / 1.0e3, 2)) + "K"
    else
        s := str.tostring(math.round(v, 2))
    s

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 6 - SIGNAL DETECTION
// Reversal signals when Z-Score crosses back from extreme
// Green dot: Z was below -threshold, now crossing up
// Red dot:   Z was above +threshold, now crossing down
// -------------------------------------------------------

// Pre-compute crossovers globally (avoids CW10002)
bool zCrossUp   = ta.crossover( zScore, zSig)
bool zCrossDown = ta.crossunder(zScore, zSig)

// Dot conditions
bool greenDot = i_showDots and zCrossUp   and zScore[1] <= -i_dotThresh
bool redDot   = i_showDots and zCrossDown and zScore[1] >= i_dotThresh

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 7 - BAR COLOR (applied to oscillator pane bars)
// -------------------------------------------------------

color barCol = zScore >= i_z3  ? COL_EXTREME_UP :
               zScore >= i_z2  ? COL_HIGH_UP    :
               zScore >= i_z1  ? COL_MID_UP     :
               zScore <= -i_z3 ? COL_EXTREME_DN :
               zScore <= -i_z2 ? COL_HIGH_DN    :
               zScore <= -i_z1 ? COL_MID_DN     :
                                 COL_NEUTRAL

barcolor(i_colorBars ? barCol : na)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 8 - HLINES
// -------------------------------------------------------

hline( i_z3,  "OB 3", color = color.new(#b71c1c, 40), linestyle = hline.style_dotted, linewidth = 1)
hline( i_z2,  "OB 2", color = color.new(#ef5350, 40), linestyle = hline.style_dashed, linewidth = 1)
hline( i_z1,  "OB 1", color = color.new(#ff8a65, 50), linestyle = hline.style_dashed, linewidth = 1)
hline(0.0,    "Zero", color = COL_ZERO,               linestyle = hline.style_solid,  linewidth = 1)
hline(-i_z1,  "OS 1", color = color.new(#66bb6a, 50), linestyle = hline.style_dashed, linewidth = 1)
hline(-i_z2,  "OS 2", color = color.new(#26a69a, 40), linestyle = hline.style_dashed, linewidth = 1)
hline(-i_z3,  "OS 3", color = color.new(#004d40, 40), linestyle = hline.style_dotted, linewidth = 1)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 9 - OSCILLATOR PLOTS
// -------------------------------------------------------

// Histogram
plot(i_showHist ? zHist : na, "Z Histogram",
     color     = zHist >= 0 ? COL_HIST_UP : COL_HIST_DN,
     style     = plot.style_histogram,
     linewidth = 2)

// Signal line
plot(i_showSig ? zSig : na, "Z Signal",
     color     = COL_SIG_LINE,
     linewidth = 1)

// Main Z-Score line
p_z = plot(zScore, "Z-Score",
     color     = barCol,
     linewidth = 2)

// Zero reference for fills
p_ob3 = plot( i_z3,  "Fill OB3 Ref", color = color(na), display = display.none)
p_ob2 = plot( i_z2,  "Fill OB2 Ref", color = color(na), display = display.none)
p_os2 = plot(-i_z2,  "Fill OS2 Ref", color = color(na), display = display.none)
p_os3 = plot(-i_z3,  "Fill OS3 Ref", color = color(na), display = display.none)

// Zone fills
fill(p_z, p_ob3,
     color = i_showFill and zScore >= i_z3 ? COL_FILL_OB3 : color(na),
     title = "Extreme OB Fill")
fill(p_z, p_ob2,
     color = i_showFill and zScore >= i_z2 and zScore < i_z3 ? COL_FILL_OB2 : color(na),
     title = "OB Fill")
fill(p_z, p_os2,
     color = i_showFill and zScore <= -i_z2 and zScore > -i_z3 ? COL_FILL_OS2 : color(na),
     title = "OS Fill")
fill(p_z, p_os3,
     color = i_showFill and zScore <= -i_z3 ? COL_FILL_OS3 : color(na),
     title = "Extreme OS Fill")

// Green dot - buy reversal signal
plotshape(
     greenDot ? zScore - 0.15 : na,
     title    = "Green Dot Buy",
     style    = shape.circle,
     location = location.absolute,
     color    = COL_DOT_BUY,
     size     = size.small)

// Red dot - sell reversal signal
plotshape(
     redDot ? zScore + 0.15 : na,
     title    = "Red Dot Sell",
     style    = shape.circle,
     location = location.absolute,
     color    = COL_DOT_SELL,
     size     = size.small)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 10 - INFO TABLE
// -------------------------------------------------------

var table tbl = na

if barstate.islast and i_showTable
    tbl := table.new(
         f_tablePos(i_tablePos),
         columns      = 2,
         rows         = 9,
         bgcolor      = color.new(color.black, 15),
         border_color = color.new(color.gray,  50),
         border_width = 1,
         frame_color  = color.new(color.white, 60),
         frame_width  = 1)

    color TXT    = color.white
    color BG_HDR = color.new(#1a237e, 20)
    color BG_CEL = color.new(color.black, 40)

    // Row 0 - header
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 0, "Z-Score MR",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = color.new(#311b92, 20), text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 0, "Value",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = color.new(#311b92, 20), text_size = size.small)

    // Row 1 - Z-Score
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 1, "Z-Score",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 1, str.tostring(math.round(zScore, 3)),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = f_zoneColor(zScore), text_size = size.small)

    // Row 2 - Zone
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 2, "Zone",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 2, f_zone(zScore),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = f_zoneColor(zScore), text_size = size.small)

    // Row 3 - Signal line
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 3, "Signal Line",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 3, str.tostring(math.round(zSig, 3)),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_CEL, text_size = size.small)

    // Row 4 - Histogram
    string histDir = zHist >= 0 ? "Expanding Up" : "Expanding Dn"
    color  histCol = zHist >= 0 ? color.new(color.teal, 20) : color.new(color.red, 20)
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 4, "Histogram",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 4, histDir,
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = histCol, text_size = size.small)

    // Row 5 - Rolling mean (price)
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 5, "Mean Price",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 5, "$" + f_fmt(pMean),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_CEL, text_size = size.small)

    // Row 6 - Upper band 2
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 6, "Upper Band Z" + str.tostring(i_z2),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 6, "$" + f_fmt(overlayUpper2),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = color.new(color.red, 50), text_size = size.small)

    // Row 7 - Lower band 2
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 7, "Lower Band Z" + str.tostring(i_z2),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 7, "$" + f_fmt(overlayLower2),
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = color.new(color.green, 50), text_size = size.small)

    // Row 8 - Signal
    string sigText = greenDot ? "BUY Reversal" : redDot ? "SELL Reversal" : "No Signal"
    color  sigCol  = color.new(color.gray, 30)
    if greenDot
        sigCol := color.new(color.green, 10)
    else if redDot
        sigCol := color.new(color.red, 10)
    table.cell(tbl, 0, 8, "Signal",
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = BG_HDR, text_size = size.small)
    table.cell(tbl, 1, 8, sigText,
         text_color = TXT, bgcolor = sigCol, text_size = size.small)

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 11 - OVERLAY PLOTS
// These plot the actual price-level bands on the chart
// Add this indicator a second time and set overlay = true
// OR use the companion overlay script below
// -------------------------------------------------------

// NOTE: Because this indicator uses overlay = false,
// the band price levels are shown in the table.
// To also see them ON the price chart, paste the
// companion overlay script below as a second indicator.

// -------------------------------------------------------
// SECTION 12 - ALERTS
// -------------------------------------------------------

alertcondition(greenDot,
     title   = "Z-Score Buy Reversal",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Green Dot - price returning from extreme oversold Z-Score level")

alertcondition(redDot,
     title   = "Z-Score Sell Reversal",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Red Dot - price returning from extreme overbought Z-Score level")

alertcondition(ta.crossover(zScore, i_z2),
     title   = "Z-Score Entered Overbought",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score crossed above overbought level - statistically extended")

alertcondition(ta.crossunder(zScore, -i_z2),
     title   = "Z-Score Entered Oversold",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score crossed below oversold level - statistically extended")

alertcondition(ta.crossover(zScore, 0.0),
     title   = "Z-Score Crossed Zero Up",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score crossed above zero - mean recovery bullish")

alertcondition(ta.crossunder(zScore, 0.0),
     title   = "Z-Score Crossed Zero Down",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score crossed below zero - mean recovery bearish")

alertcondition(ta.crossover(zScore, i_z3),
     title   = "Z-Score Extreme Overbought",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score entered EXTREME overbought zone - 3 standard deviations above mean")

alertcondition(ta.crossunder(zScore, -i_z3),
     title   = "Z-Score Extreme Oversold",
     message = "Z-Score MR: Z-Score entered EXTREME oversold zone - 3 standard deviations below mean")
````
