## Description

[b]Volatility Flow & Cycle Divergences
General Description[/b]
Volatility Flow & Cycle Divergences is an intermarket quantitative analysis tool designed to map liquidity flows, risk regimes, and structural anomalies between the US stock market (S&P 500 / ES) and the options market, represented by the CBOE Volatility Index (VIX).

The indicator is built on the premise that the physiological relationship between the S&P 500 and the VIX is historically inverse (moving in opposite directions roughly 80% of the time). This script does not merely calculate the raw ratio between the two assets; it mathematically isolates the rare moments of monetary asynchrony where this correlation breaks down. These anomalies are frequently the precursors to lasting cyclical reversals or the exhaustion of the primary trend.

[b]Core Features and Filtering Logic[/b]
To overcome the issue of false signals typical of traditional oscillators on intermediate timeframes, the script integrates three concurrent proprietary filters:

Intermarket Relative Strength Ratio (VIX/SPX): It calculates the relative speed between the demand for protective insurance (Put options) and the spot price of the equity basket.

[b]Asymmetric Cyclical Pivot Filter:[/b] The algorithm for detecting relative highs and lows uses an asymmetric lookback/lookahead range (calibrated by default to 8 periods on the left and 5 on the right). This isolates true macro turning points and eliminates microscopic, temporary intraday spikes.

[b]Momentum Validation [/b](Anti-Inertia Filter): Divergences are not generated purely on a mathematical basis. A divergence on market highs is validated only if the S&P 500 is in a true zone of strength/overbought territory (RSI > 55), while a divergence on market lows requires actual panic selling pressure (RSI < 45). If the market is in a neutral congestion phase, the signal is discarded at the source.

[b]Algorithmic Transparency[/b] (Under the Hood)
The indicator executes its analysis by processing three integrated algorithmic engines within the code:

[b]Geometric Pivot Algorithm [/b](ta.pivothigh/low): Constantly scans the historical series of the VIX/SPX Ratio to isolate cyclical peaks and troughs, validating them only if they are surrounded by a precise asymmetry of bar-index (8 to the left, 5 to the right).

[b]Momentum Algorithm [/b](Wilder's RSI): Monitors the first derivative of the S&P 500 price. It does not calculate the classic relative strength on the Ratio itself, but uses the RSI algorithm on the underlying equity index to act as a logical switch (Gatekeeper) for the signals.

[b]Pattern Matching Algorithm [/b](Conditional Logic): A conditional control structure that crosses the spatial-temporal coordinates of price pivots with those of volatility, filtering out false signals based on overbought/oversold regimes.

H[b]ow to Read the Signals on the Chart[/b]
The script provides a clean graphical visualization using compact "badges" on the lower panel so it does not obscure the price action:

[b]DD[/b] (Distribution Divergence - Red): The S&P 500 prints a new relative high, but the VIX/SPX Ratio shows a higher low or an anomalous support. This means that despite the price rally, institutional investors are accumulating protective hedges due to an ongoing distribution. Potential pullback ahead.

[b]DA[/b] (Accumulation Divergence - Green): The S&P 500 updates its period lows amid panic, but the Ratio registers a lower peak compared to the previous one. Implied volatility pressure is exhausting: "smart money" is stopping the hedging process and beginning to accumulate spot positions. Potential cyclical low/bounce in formation.

[b]User Interface (UI) Elements[/b]
Integrated Information Dashboard: Positioned at the bottom left with a transparent background to preserve chart real estate. It shows the real-time macro state of the market (RISK-ON or RISK-OFF) based on the position of the Ratio relative to its 20-period moving average, serving as an operational reminder for the divergence rules.

View Toggle: Through the indicator settings panel, you can hide or show the dashboard with a single click.

[b]Operational Advice and Timeframes[/b]
This indicator expresses its maximum performance when integrated into a cyclical and multi-day flow analysis logic:

Best Timeframe: 2 Hours (2H) to capture operational swings and cycles lasting 2-3 days.

Macro / Swing Analysis: Daily (1D) to map structural medium-to-long term risk regimes.

[b]Technical Note:[/b] We advise against using this on fast timeframes (below the 2-hour chart) due to the algorithmic noise generated by short-term option expirations (0DTE) and the lack of reliable spot VIX calculation during overnight sessions (Globex).

I[b]mportant Disclaimer & Operational Risk Warning[/b]

Critical Note: Never use a single indicator to make standalone trading decisions. Always contextualize what the indicator suggests within a broader market framework and combine it with other validation techniques (e.g., Price Action, Market Structure, Volume Profile, or Order Flow). No indicator is infallible, and intermarket divergences represent shifts in probability, not guaranteed certainties.

[b]Descrizione Generale[/b]
Volatility Flow & Cycle Divergences è uno strumento di analisi intermarket quantitativa progettato per mappare i flussi di liquidità, i regimi di rischio e le anomalie strutturali tra il mercato azionario statunitense (S&P 500 / ES) e il mercato delle opzioni, rappresentato dal CBOE Volatility Index (VIX).

L'indicatore si basa sul presupposto che la relazione fisiologica tra l'S&P 500 e il VIX sia storicamente inversa (circa l'80% delle volte si muovono in direzioni opposte). Questo script non si limita a calcolare il rapporto puro tra i due asset, ma isola matematicamente i rari momenti di asincronia monetaria, in cui questa correlazione si spezza. Tali anomalie sono spesso il precursore di inversioni cicliche durature o di esaurimenti del trend principale.

[b]Caratteristiche Principali e Logica di Filtraggio[/b]
Per superare il problema dei falsi segnali tipici degli oscillatori tradizionali sui timeframe intermedi, lo script integra tre filtri proprietari concorrenti:
[i]Rapporto di Forza Relativa Intermarket[/i] ($VIX/SPX$): 
Calcola la velocità relativa tra la domanda di coperture assicurative (opzioni Put) e il prezzo spot del paniere azionario.
[i]Filtro Ciclico Asimmetrico sui Pivot: [/i]
L'algoritmo di rilevamento dei massimi e minimi relativi utilizza un raggio d'azione asimmetrico (calibrato di default a 8 periodi a sinistra e 5 a destra). Questo isola i reali punti di svolta macro ed elimina i microscopici spike estemporanei intra-day.
[i]Validazione del Momentum (Filtro Anti-Inerzia):[/i] 
Le divergenze non vengono generate su base puramente matematica. Una divergenza sui massimi viene convalidata solo se l'S&P 500 si trova in una reale zona di ipercomprato/forza relativa (RSI > 55), mentre una sui minimi esige una reale pressione di panico (RSI < 45). Se il mercato si trova in una fase di congestione neutra, il segnale viene scartato all'origine.

[b]Come Leggere i Segnali sul Grafico[/b]
Lo script restituisce una visualizzazione grafica pulita mediante "badge" compatti sul pannello inferiore per non occultare l'azione del prezzo:

DD (Divergenza di Distribuzione - Rossa): L'S&P 500 stampa un nuovo massimo relativo, ma il Ratio VIX/SPX mostra un minimo crescente o un supporto anomalo. Significa che, nonostante il rialzo del prezzo, gli investitori istituzionali stanno accumulando coperture protettive a causa di una distribuzione in corso. Potenziale storno in arrivo.

DA (Divergenza di Accumulazione - Verde): L'S&P 500 aggiorna i minimi di periodo nel panico, ma il Ratio fa registrare un picco inferiore rispetto al precedente. La pressione della volatilità implicita si sta esaurendo: le "mani forti" smettono di proteggersi e iniziano ad accumulare posizioni spot. Potenziale minimo ciclico/rimbalzo in formazione.

## Source Code

````pine
//@version=5
indicator("Volatility Flow & Cycle Divergences", overlay=false)

// --- INPUTS ---
vix_ticker   = input.symbol("TVC:VIX", title="Volatility Symbol")
spx_ticker   = input.symbol("SPX", title="Equity Symbol")
show_dash    = input.bool(true, title="Show Info Dashboard")

// Optimized parameters for 2H TF (2-3 day cycles) and 1D TF
lb_left      = input.int(8, title="Pivot Lookleft (Cyclic Filter)")
lb_right     = input.int(5, title="Pivot Lookright (Confirmation)")
rsi_len      = input.int(14, title="SPX RSI Filter Length")

// --- INDICATOR CALCULATIONS ---
vix_close = request.security(vix_ticker, timeframe.period, close)
spx_close = request.security(spx_ticker, timeframe.period, close)

ratio = vix_close / spx_close
ma_ratio = ta.sma(ratio, 20)

// SPX RSI to filter false signals during strong trends
spx_rsi = ta.rsi(spx_close, rsi_len)

// Plot the oscillator
plot(ratio, color=color.blue, linewidth=2, title="VIX/SPX Ratio")
plot(ma_ratio, color=color.orange, linewidth=1, title="20-period MA")

// --- DIVERGENCE DETECTION LOGIC ---
ratio_hp = ta.pivothigh(ratio, lb_left, lb_right)
ratio_lp = ta.pivotlow(ratio, lb_left, lb_right)

var float top_ratio = na
var float top_spx   = na
var int   top_index = na

var float bot_ratio = na
var float bot_spx   = na
var int   bot_index = na

// 1. DISTRIBUTION DIVERGENCE (DD - Red)
if not na(ratio_hp)
    // FILTER: SPX price must be in a strength zone (RSI > 55) to validate distribution
    if not na(top_ratio) and ratio_hp < top_ratio and spx_close[lb_right] > top_spx and spx_rsi[lb_right] > 55
        line.new(x1=top_index, y1=top_ratio, x2=bar_index - lb_right, y2=ratio_hp, color=color.red, width=2)
        label.new(x=bar_index - lb_right, y=ratio_hp, text="DD", style=label.style_label_down, color=color.new(color.red, 10), textcolor=color.white, size=size.normal)
    
    top_ratio := ratio_hp
    top_spx   := spx_close[lb_right]
    top_index := bar_index - lb_right

// 2. ACCUMULATION DIVERGENCE (DA - Green)
if not na(ratio_lp)
    // FILTER: SPX price must be in a weakness/panic zone (RSI < 45) to validate accumulation
    if not na(bot_ratio) and ratio_lp > bot_ratio and spx_close[lb_right] < bot_spx and spx_rsi[lb_right] < 45
        line.new(x1=bot_index, y1=bot_ratio, x2=bar_index - lb_right, y2=ratio_lp, color=color.green, width=2)
        label.new(x=bar_index - lb_right, y=ratio_lp, text="DA", style=label.style_label_up, color=color.new(color.green, 10), textcolor=color.white, size=size.normal)
    
    bot_ratio := ratio_lp
    bot_spx   := spx_close[lb_right]
    bot_index := bar_index - lb_right


// --- DASHBOARD LOGIC (TABLE) ---
is_risk_off = ratio > ma_ratio
status_text = is_risk_off ? "RISK-OFF (Volatility Expanding)" : "RISK-ON (Market Stable/Rally)"
status_color = is_risk_off ? color.new(color.red, 20) : color.new(color.green, 20)

var table dash = table.new(position = position.bottom_left, columns = 1, rows = 5, bgcolor = color.new(color.white, 1), border_width = 0)

if show_dash and barstate.islast
    table.cell(dash, 0, 0, "STATUS: " + status_text, bgcolor=status_color, text_color=color.black, text_size=size.normal, text_halign=text.align_left)
    table.cell(dash, 0, 1, "• OPERATIONAL FILTER: Optimized for TF >= 2H (Multi-day cycles)", text_color=color.black, text_size=size.normal, text_halign=text.align_left)
    table.cell(dash, 0, 2, "• CROSS: Blue/Orange crossover indicates volatility regime shift", text_color=color.black, text_size=size.normal, text_halign=text.align_left)
    table.cell(dash, 0, 3, "• DD (Red): Distribution Divergence. Potential market pullback.", text_color=color.black, text_size=size.normal, text_halign=text.align_left)
    table.cell(dash, 0, 4, "• DA (Green): Accumulation Divergence. Potential market bottom/bounce.", text_color=color.black, text_size=size.normal, text_halign=text.align_left)
````
