## Description

OptionSense Predict Map visualizes key options-driven price zones for SPY directly on the chart.

It highlights the main 0DTE resistance/support walls, weekly and monthly pressure zones, high-probability scenario ranges, no-chase areas, and important market structure levels in a clean overlay.

What it shows:
• 0DTE R / 0DTE S: primary intraday options walls
• Weekly / Monthly walls: broader pressure and magnet zones
• Gravity: monthly center of attraction
• PIN / FADE / BREAKOUT: scenario zones with probability labels
• NO CALL / NO PUT: areas where chasing is unfavorable
• Key structure levels: support, resistance, MA, and liquidity vacuum zones

This indicator is designed as a visual decision map, not an automated buy/sell system. It helps traders see where price may pin, reject, break out, or lose structure, while keeping the chart readable and uncluttered.

Best used with SPY intraday charts and your own risk management.
