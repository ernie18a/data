01 — INTRODUCTION
What this indicator actually does

Imagine you walk into a car auction. There are hundreds of cars, and everybody has a price tag. But here's the thing — you can't tell just from the price tag which cars are actually worth buying. Some are overpriced. Some are bargains. The price alone doesn't tell you the story.

Now imagine someone hands you a report that shows: which cars got the most bids, who the serious buyers were, where the big money changed hands, and which cars nobody wanted. Suddenly you're not guessing anymore. You're reading the room.

That's what VP + Order Flow Edge does for financial markets. It takes all the hidden information buried inside price movement and volume — the stuff big professional traders use — and puts it right on your chart in plain sight.

THE CORE IDEA
Price tells you what happened. Volume tells you how much people cared. Together, they show you where the real money is sitting — and where it's going next.
This indicator works on 1-minute, 3-minute, and 5-minute charts and automatically adjusts its sensitivity for each timeframe. You don't need to change any settings when you switch charts — it figures it out by itself.

02 — THE FOUNDATION
Why price alone lies — the volume truth

Here's something most beginners don't know: big hedge funds and institutions can push price wherever they want, at least temporarily. They have enough money to shove it up or down to create a false impression — and they do it regularly.

But volume? Volume is much harder to fake. Volume is simply the number of transactions that happened. You can't manufacture real buyers and sellers out of thin air.

The three volume scenarios — the same price move means very different things depending on the volume behind it
The Three Scenarios You'll See Every Day

Scenario 1 — Healthy Move: Big candle, high volume. The move is real. Buyers or sellers participated in large numbers. The price is likely to keep going in that direction.

Scenario 2 — Absorption: Tiny candle, massive volume. This is the sneaky one. Buyers pushed hard (you can see it in the volume spike), but price barely moved. Why? Because there was an invisible wall — a large seller quietly soaking up every single buy order. The moment buyers run out of energy, price drops sharply. This is how smart money hides.

Scenario 3 — No Supply: Price drifts up on almost no volume. Not because buyers are strong — but because there are no sellers in the way. Price floats upward with zero resistance. This can reverse suddenly the moment a real seller shows up.

IMPORTANT — GREEN ≠ BUYING, RED ≠ SELLING
Every single trade needs a buyer AND a seller. The green or red color of a volume bar just matches the candle above it. What actually matters is the size of the bar — not its color.
03 — THE VOLUME PROFILE
The Volume Profile — seeing where money lives

The standard volume bars at the bottom of most charts answer the question: "When did a lot of trading happen?"

The Volume Profile answers a much more useful question: "At which exact price level did the most trading happen?"

Think of it like this. If you ran a restaurant and wanted to know where most of your customers sat, you wouldn't just count how many people came in each hour. You'd look at which tables got the most use. The Volume Profile shows you the "tables" where the market spent the most time doing business.

The Volume Profile — wider bars = more trading activity at that price. POC is where the market agreed most. LVNs are price levels almost nobody traded at.
The Three Things That Matter

POC — Point of Control
The yellow line. The single price where the most trading happened — the market's center of gravity. Price keeps coming back to this level because so many people have trades anchored here. Think of it as the market's "home base."
Value Area (VAH to VAL)
The teal shaded zone. Contains 70% of all trading activity — where buyers and sellers agreed that price was fair. When price is inside this zone, the market is calm and balanced. The edges (VAH and VAL) are where things get interesting.
LVN — Low Volume Nodes
The thin gray bars. Price levels where almost nobody traded. These are "fast lanes" — when price enters an LVN, it rips through quickly because there's no one there to slow it down. Great places to expect acceleration.
The Golden Rule: High volume = sticky prices (price lingers). Low volume = price rips. The market consolidates where people are. It flies where nobody is.
Profile Shapes — The Market's Story in One Shape

The overall shape of the profile tells you what kind of market you're in before you look at a single candle.

🅓
D-Shape — Balanced
Fat in the middle, thin at edges. Buyers and sellers are happy. Nobody has strong opinions. Play the edges — short from top, long from bottom, target the POC.
🅟
P-Shape — Bullish
Heavy volume at top. Buyers stepped in with conviction. Look for pullbacks to POC or the thin lower tail to go long. Day must close above 50% of range.
🅑
B-Shape — Bearish
Heavy volume at bottom. Sellers in control. Look for bounces into POC or thin upper tail to short. Day must close below 50% of range to be genuine.
⚡
Thin — Trending
Volume spread thin over wide range. Explosive trending move — nobody had time to build positions. Look for small volume clusters inside — those are your entry points on pullbacks.
04 — MARKET GENERATED LEVELS
Market Generated Levels — the fences nobody ignores

Beyond the volume profile, markets respect certain price levels that were created by previous trading activity. Think of them as fences that price has to deal with when it gets there. Big players have positions anchored to these levels, so when price returns to them, reactions are almost guaranteed.

LEVEL	COLOR	WHAT IT IS	WHY IT MATTERS
PDH / PDL	Orange	Previous Day High & Low	Every professional trader watches yesterday's extremes. Breakouts and rejections here cause big moves.
ONH / ONL	Purple	Overnight High & Low	The range set while US markets were closed (9pm–9:29am ET). Breaking this range triggers stops and creates momentum.
ORB-H / ORB-L	Teal	Opening Range High & Low (first 30 min)	A concept from the old trading pit days. Price tends to respect these all session long. Breaks of ORB often signal the day's direction.
HOW TO USE THESE LEVELS
These levels don't mean anything on their own — they become powerful when you see order flow reactions happening at them. A level + an absorption signal + a trapped buyer pattern = an A+ setup. That's exactly what the indicator looks for.
Historical Reaction Stats — Does This Level Actually Work?

Every level label includes a statistics badge showing how price has historically reacted at that exact level over the last 10 sessions. You can see at a glance whether a level is worth trading or not:

WKH [73% · 28pts · n=41]   ← Weekly High: bounced 73% of 41 touches, avg 28pt move
MDAY-H [100% · 84pts · n=6]   ← Monday High: bounced every single time
ONH [n<5]   ← not enough data yet — treat as unproven
A level showing 70%+ bounce rate with a large average move is a high-conviction level worth prioritizing. A level below 50% is barely better than a coin flip — be selective.

Extended Key Levels

Beyond the standard daily levels, the indicator automatically calculates and draws a full set of weekly, monthly, Monday, 4H, VWAP, and premarket levels. Most are off by default to keep the chart clean — turn them on as you need them.

LEVEL	DEFAULT	WHY IT MATTERS FOR NQ
Weekly High / Low / Open	ON	Major institutional reference levels. NQ often rejects hard at weekly extremes, especially on Monday and Friday.
Monday High / Low / Mid	ON	The Monday range tends to define the week's directional bias. A break of Monday High mid-week is a bullish signal.

05 — ORDER FLOW SIGNALS
Order Flow Signals — reading the big players

Order flow is the raw heartbeat of the market — the actual buy and sell orders hitting the market in real time. Most retail traders never see it. The VP + Order Flow Edge indicator watches for specific patterns that reveal when large players are active and where they're positioned.

▼
ABS↓ — Absorption (Bearish)
Green candle + massive volume + tiny body. Buyers pushed hard but went nowhere. A passive seller is absorbing every buy order. When buyers exhaust themselves, price drops sharply.
▲
ABS↑ — Absorption (Bullish)
Red candle + massive volume + tiny body. Sellers pushed hard but price barely moved. A passive buyer is absorbing all selling. When sellers give up, price launches upward.
✕
TRAP↓ — Trapped Buyers
Big green candle (lots of buyers got in), then the very next candle reverses hard below where they entered. Those buyers are now trapped — losing money and about to be forced out, pushing price even lower.
✕
TRAP↑ — Trapped Sellers
Big red candle (lots of sellers got in short), then the very next candle reverses above where they entered. Those sellers are trapped and getting squeezed higher, adding fuel to the move up.
▼
LAF↓ — Look Above & Fail
Price wicks above a resistance level (sucking in breakout buyers) then closes right back below it. Those breakout buyers are now trapped. A short setup — must occur near a key level.
▲
LBF↑ — Look Below & Fail
Price wicks below a support level (triggering stop-loss sells) then closes right back above it. The sellers who got triggered are now trapped. A long setup — must occur near a key level.
⚠
Bear Div — Bearish Divergence
Price makes a new high, but volume on that new high is significantly smaller than the previous high. The rocket is running out of fuel. Reversal warning.
✦
Bull Div — Bullish Divergence
Price makes a new low, but volume on that new low is smaller than the previous low. Sellers are exhausted — they're pushing but fewer and fewer are joining in. Bottom forming.
The Confluence Score — A+ Setups

Each signal on its own is useful. But when multiple signals align at the same level simultaneously, the probability of a successful trade increases dramatically. The indicator automatically scores each bar across four criteria:

#	CRITERION	WHAT IT MEANS
1	Near a Market Generated Level	Price is at PDH/PDL, ONH/ONL, or ORB-H/L
2	Near a Volume Profile Level	Price is at VAH, VAL, or POC
3	Absorption Signal Present	ABS↑ or ABS↓ firing right now
4	Trap Signal Present	TRAP↑ or TRAP↓ firing right now
When all four align simultaneously, a ★ A+ label appears. These are the highest-probability setups the indicator can identify. Treat them as priority trades.

VA Re-entry Rule (The Bonus Signal)

This one is special. When price has been outside the Value Area for at least 8 bars, then closes back inside it — the market is overwhelmingly likely to travel all the way across to the opposite edge.

A green circle (VA↑) means price came back in from below — target is VAH. A red circle (VA↓) means price came back in from above — target is VAL. It's a simple, objective, repeatable rule every session.

Momentum Reversal Detection — The Early Warning System

Beyond individual signals, the indicator runs a continuous exhaustion scoring system near key levels. When price is within 2 ATR of VAH, VAL, PDH, PDL, ONH, or ONL it monitors 7 conditions simultaneously. When 3 or more align, it fires a warning before the reversal fully develops.

The 7 conditions are: body shrinking, volume declining, first reversal candle, wick rejection, absorption signal, LAF/LBF signal, and trap signal. You'll see two outputs when exhaustion is confirmed:

✖
White X — Exhaustion Confirmed
Appears above or below the bar when 3 or more exhaustion conditions align at once near a key level. The move is running out of steam.
⚠
⚠ REV WARN — Reversal Warning
Orange label showing the score out of 7. A score of 5/7 means five separate exhaustion conditions fired at once — significantly more conviction than a 3/7. The higher the number, the stronger the warning.
THE EXTRA CONFIRMATION BAR
When a REV WARN fires near a pending entry, the indicator automatically requires one additional confirming bar before the entry fires. This prevents entering during the final spike that typically stops out early entries. The spike becomes your signal — not your stop.
06 — THE TWO TRADE MODELS
The Two Trade Models

Markets have two basic states: ranging (going sideways, stuck in a box) and trending (moving strongly in one direction). You need a different approach for each. Trying to trade a ranging market like a trending one, or vice versa, is how most people lose money.

The indicator automatically identifies which state you're in and tells you in the bottom-right table. Here's how to trade each one.

MODEL 1
Range Model — Fade the Extremes
When the profile is a D-shape and value is stable, the market is in a box. Price bounces back and forth between VAH and VAL like a ping-pong ball.
Wait for price to reach VAH or VAL — the edges of the value area
Look for an order flow signal confirming rejection (ABS, TRAP, or LAF/LBF)
Short from VAH, target the POC (midpoint), then VAL. Long from VAL, target POC, then VAH.
Move stop to break-even after first target hits. Never let a winning trade go red.
Avoid trading the middle — that's where you get chopped up
MODEL 2
Trend Model — Ride the Low Volume Nodes
When the profile is thin (P, B, or Thin shape) and value is migrating in one direction, the market is in a trend. You're not fading the move — you're joining it on pullbacks.
Identify the direction — is value migrating higher or lower? (See the Value Migration table top-right)
Wait for price to pull back into a Low Volume Node — the fast lanes in the profile
Watch for buyers (in an uptrend) or sellers (in a downtrend) getting aggressive at that LVN
Enter in the direction of the trend. Target the prior high/low, then trim there.
If a lower high forms in an uptrend with big trades present — exit immediately. The trend is likely ending.
Key insight: In a trending day, sellers who keep trying to short against the trend keep getting squeezed. Their forced buy-backs add more fuel to the upward move. That's why trends go further than most people expect — it's not just new buyers, it's trapped shorts getting liquidated.
07 — THE ENTRY ENGINE
Entry Engine — how trades are triggered

The indicator doesn't just show you where things might happen — it tells you exactly when to enter, where to put your stop, and what your target is, with the calculated reward-to-risk ratio right on the chart.

There are two types of entries, matching the two personalities described in the transcripts:

AGGRESSIVE ENTRY — SMALLER SIZE, FIRST SIGNAL
When you see the opportunity forming in real time and want to get in immediately. Higher risk of being wrong, but better price if you're right.

Waiting for the market to prove itself before committing. Lower risk, slightly worse price. This is the higher-probability entry.

Price touches a key level and wicks through it slightly (the "look below/above")
But closes back on the right side of the level (the "fail")
The next bar also closes in the right direction with 1.2× average volume or an order flow signal
The EMA trend filter agrees

Squeeze Entry — When the Market Reverses Hard

Sometimes a trade fires in one direction, and then price violently breaks through the level the other way — trapping everyone who entered. This is called a squeeze, and it creates one of the most powerful momentum setups in NQ.

When the indicator detects that a recent entry is being squeezed out, it fires a ▲ SQUEEZE LONG ★ or ▼ SQUEEZE SHORT ★ automatically. These are always marked with ★ because they represent self-reinforcing momentum — the trapped side's pain fuels your direction.

// TIMEFRAME-ADAPTIVE SCALING
// Detects 1m / 3m / 5m and scales all bar-count parameters proportionally.
// All durations are expressed in MINUTES then converted to bars.
// 1m → ×1, 3m → ×3, 5m → ×5, anything else → nearest supported TF.
// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

_tf_mins = timeframe.in_seconds() / 60
// Clamp to supported TFs: 1, 3, 5. Anything else snaps to nearest.
_tf_mult = _tf_mins <= 1.5 ? 1 : _tf_mins <= 4.0 ? 3 : 5

// Convert a "minute duration" to bars for the current timeframe
f_mins_to_bars(mins) => math.max(1, math.round(mins / _tf_mult))

// ── Adaptive parameters (all defined once, used everywhere) ──────────────────
// Profile lookback: covers ~50 min on 1m, ~50 min on 3m, ~50 min on 5m

f_calc_profile() =>
    _ph       = ta.highest(high, lookback)
    _pl       = ta.lowest(low,   lookback)
    _bin_size = (_ph - _pl) / rows
    _vol_arr  = array.new_float(rows, 0.0)
    _price_arr= array.new_float(rows, 0.0)
    for i = 0 to rows - 1
        array.set(_price_arr, i, _pl + i * _bin_size + _bin_size / 2)
    for b = 0 to lookback - 1
        _h  = high[b]
        _l  = low[b]
        _v  = volume[b]
        _cr = _h - _l
        if _cr > 0
            for i = 0 to rows - 1
                _bin_low  = _pl + i * _bin_size
                _bin_high = _bin_low + _bin_size
                _overlap  = math.min(_h, _bin_high) - math.max(_l, _bin_low)
                if _overlap > 0
                    array.set(_vol_arr, i, array.get(_vol_arr, i) + (_overlap / _cr) * _v)
    [_vol_arr, _price_arr, _ph, _pl, _bin_size]

f_find_poc_va(vol_arr, price_arr, bin_size) =>
    _total   = array.sum(vol_arr)
    _target  = _total * va_pct
    _poc_idx = array.indexof(vol_arr, array.max(vol_arr))
    _va_vol  = array.get(vol_arr, _poc_idx)
    _lo_i    = _poc_idx
    _hi_i    = _poc_idx
    while _va_vol < _target
        _exp_lo = _lo_i > 0        ? array.get(vol_arr, _lo_i - 1) : 0.0
        _exp_hi = _hi_i < rows - 1 ? array.get(vol_arr, _hi_i + 1) : 0.0
        if _exp_lo == 0.0 and _exp_hi == 0.0
            break
        if _exp_hi >= _exp_lo
            _hi_i  := _hi_i + 1
            _va_vol += array.get(vol_arr, _hi_i)
        else
            _lo_i  := _lo_i - 1
            _va_vol += array.get(vol_arr, _lo_i)
    _poc_p = array.get(price_arr, _poc_idx)
    _vah   = array.get(price_arr, _hi_i) + bin_size / 2
    _val   = array.get(price_arr, _lo_i) - bin_size / 2
    [_poc_p, _vah, _val, _poc_idx, _lo_i, _hi_i]

f_profile_shape(vol_arr, rows_count) =>
    _total   = array.sum(vol_arr)
    _third   = rows_count / 3
    _bot     = 0.0
    _mid     = 0.0
    _top     = 0.0
    for i = 0 to rows_count - 1
        _v = array.get(vol_arr, i)
        if i < _third
            _bot += _v
        else if i > rows_count - 1 - _third
            _top += _v
        else
            _mid += _v
    _mp = _mid / _total
    _tp = _top / _total
    _bp = _bot / _total
    _mp > 0.45 ? "D (Balanced — fade extremes)" : _tp > 0.45 ? "P (Bullish — buy VA pullbacks)" : _bp > 0.45 ? "B (Bearish — sell VA bounces)" : "Thin (Trend — trade LVN clusters)"

// HISTORICAL REACTION ANALYSIS ENGINE
// For each level, looks back over last N daily sessions and calculates:
//   bounce_pct  — % of touches where price reversed by 1+ ATR within 10 bars
//   avg_move    — average point move after touching the level
// Displayed on the level label so you can assess level quality at a glance
// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

f_reaction_stats(level_price, lookback_bars) =>
    _atr_r      = ta.atr(14)
    _touch_zone = _atr_r * 0.5
    _bounces    = 0
    _total      = 0
    _total_move = 0.0
    _last_touch = -20
    _max_bars   = math.min(lookback_bars, 5000)
    if barstate.islast
        for i = 1 to _max_bars
            _lvl     = level_price
            _touched = math.abs(low[i] - _lvl) <= _touch_zone or math.abs(high[i] - _lvl) <= _touch_zone
            if _touched and (i - _last_touch) > 10
                _last_touch := i
                _total      := _total + 1
                _max_up   = 0.0
                _max_dn   = 0.0
                for j = 1 to math.min(10, i - 1)
                    _max_up := math.max(_max_up, high[i - j] - _lvl)
                    _max_dn := math.max(_max_dn, _lvl - low[i - j])
                _bounce_move = math.max(_max_up, _max_dn)
                _total_move  := _total_move + _bounce_move
                if _bounce_move >= _atr_r
                    _bounces := _bounces + 1
    _bounce_pct  = _total > 0 ? math.round(_bounces / _total * 100) : 0
    _avg_move    = _total > 0 ? math.round(_total_move / _total)     : 0
    [_bounce_pct, _avg_move, _total]

    max_above_snap  := na

        bars_above_snap := 0

    else if close > snap_vah

        bars_below_snap := 0
        max_above_snap  := na(max_above_snap) ? close : math.max(max_above_snap, close)
    else
        bars_below_snap := 0
        bars_above_snap := 0

_deep_enough_short = not na(max_above_snap) and (max_above_snap - snap_vah) / snap_vah >= va_min_excursion_pct

_va_short_raw = barstate.isconfirmed and show_vareen and not na(snap_vah) and bars_above_snap[1] >= va_min_bars_outside and _deep_enough_short and close <= snap_vah and close >= snap_val and close < open and (bar_index - last_va_short_bar) > va_cooldown

// MOMENTUM REVERSAL DETECTION
// Tracks momentum strength near key levels and fires warning signals when
// exhaustion conditions align. Only activates within 2 ATR of a key level.
// Visual: colored dots showing momentum state → strong → weakening → exhausted
// Signal: ⚠ REV WARN fires when 3+ exhaustion conditions align simultaneously
// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

// ── Proximity gate — only active near key levels ──────────────────────────────

_near_any_level = 
     (not na(g_vah)   and math.abs(close - g_vah)   < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (not na(g_val)   and math.abs(close - g_val)   < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (not na(pd_high) and math.abs(close - pd_high) < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (not na(pd_low)  and math.abs(close - pd_low)  < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (not na(on_high) and math.abs(close - on_high) < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (not na(on_low)  and math.abs(close - on_low)  < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (orb_done and not na(orb_high) and math.abs(close - orb_high) < _mom_atr * 2) or
     (orb_done and not na(orb_low)  and math.abs(close - orb_low)  < _mom_atr * 2)

// ── Momentum metrics ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
// 1. Body shrinking — each candle smaller than the last (momentum decaying)
_body_shrinking  = candle_body < candle_body[1] and candle_body[1] < candle_body[2]

// 2. Volume declining — 3-bar volume trend falling
_vol_declining   = volume < volume[1] and volume[1] < volume[2]

// 3. Momentum direction — is the recent move bullish or bearish?
_mom_up   = close > close[3]  // net up over last 3 bars
_mom_down = close < close[3]  // net down over last 3 bars

// 4. First reversal candle — closes opposite to recent trend
_rev_candle_bear = _mom_up   and close < open  // red candle in uptrend
_rev_candle_bull = _mom_down and close > open  // green candle in downtrend

// 5. Wick rejection — upper wick > 2× body (bearish) or lower wick > 2× body (bullish)
_upper_wick      = high - math.max(open, close)
_lower_wick      = math.min(open, close) - low
_wick_reject_bear = _upper_wick > candle_body * 2.0 and _upper_wick > _mom_atr * 0.3
_wick_reject_bull = _lower_wick > candle_body * 2.0 and _lower_wick > _mom_atr * 0.3

// ── Score exhaustion conditions ───────────────────────────────────────────────
// Bearish exhaustion (uptrend losing steam near resistance)
_bear_exhaust_score = 
     (_body_shrinking  ? 1 : 0) +
     (_vol_declining   ? 1 : 0) +
     (_rev_candle_bear ? 1 : 0) +
     (_wick_reject_bear ? 1 : 0) +
     (abs_bear          ? 1 : 0) +
     (look_above_fail   ? 1 : 0) +
     (trap_buyers       ? 1 : 0)

// Bullish exhaustion (downtrend losing steam near support)

     (_body_shrinking  ? 1 : 0) +
     (_vol_declining   ? 1 : 0) +
     (_rev_candle_bull ? 1 : 0) +
     (_wick_reject_bull ? 1 : 0) +
     (abs_bull          ? 1 : 0) +
     (look_below_fail   ? 1 : 0) +
     (trap_sellers      ? 1 : 0)

// ── Momentum state classification ─────────────────────────────────────────────
// Only active near key levels

_bear_weakening = _near_any_level and _mom_up   and _bear_exhaust_score >= 1 and _bear_exhaust_score <= 2
_bear_exhausted = _near_any_level and _mom_up   and _bear_exhaust_score >= 3

_bull_strong    = _near_any_level and _mom_down and _bull_exhaust_score == 0
_bull_weakening = _near_any_level and _mom_down and _bull_exhaust_score >= 1 and _bull_exhaust_score <= 2

// ── Target calculation ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
// Uses percentage-based distance cap (0.5% max = ~145pts on NQ at 29k) so it
// works regardless of ATR magnitude. Hard R:R ceiling of 8R on display.
// If no valid level found within range, uses 2× risk as a default target.
f_target_long(entry_price, stop_price) =>
    _risk     = math.max(entry_price - stop_price, 0.0001)
    // Distance bounds based on actual risk — not arbitrary percentage
    _min_dist = _risk * 0.8   // target must be at least 0.8× risk away
    _max_dist = _risk * 3.0   // cap at 5× risk to avoid unrealistic targets
    // All resistance levels above entry that could be targets
    _vah_ok  = not na(g_vah)   and (g_vah   - entry_price) >= _min_dist and (g_vah   - entry_price) <= _max_dist
    _poc_ok  = not na(g_poc)   and (g_poc   - entry_price) >= _min_dist and (g_poc   - entry_price) <= _max_dist and g_poc > entry_price
    _pdh_ok  = not na(pd_high) and (pd_high - entry_price) >= _min_dist and (pd_high - entry_price) <= _max_dist
    _onh_ok  = not na(on_high) and (on_high - entry_price) >= _min_dist and (on_high - entry_price) <= _max_dist
    _orbh_ok = orb_done and not na(orb_high) and (orb_high - entry_price) >= _min_dist and (orb_high - entry_price) <= _max_dist
    // Pick nearest valid level above entry
    _best = float(na)
    if _vah_ok  and (na(_best) or g_vah   < _best)
        _best := g_vah
    if _poc_ok  and (na(_best) or g_poc   < _best)
        _best := g_poc
    if _pdh_ok  and (na(_best) or pd_high < _best)
        _best := pd_high
    if _onh_ok  and (na(_best) or on_high < _best)
        _best := on_high
    if _orbh_ok and (na(_best) or orb_high < _best)
        _best := orb_high
    _tp     = na(_best) ? entry_price + _risk * 2.0 : _best
    _rr_raw = (_tp - entry_price) / _risk
    _rr     = math.round(math.min(_rr_raw, 8.0) * 10) / 10
    [_tp, _rr]

f_target_short(entry_price, stop_price) =>
    _risk     = math.max(stop_price - entry_price, 0.0001)
    _min_dist = _risk * 0.8
    _max_dist = _risk * 3.0
    _val_ok  = not na(g_val)  and (entry_price - g_val)  >= _min_dist and (entry_price - g_val)  <= _max_dist
    _poc_ok  = not na(g_poc)  and (entry_price - g_poc)  >= _min_dist and (entry_price - g_poc)  <= _max_dist and g_poc < entry_price
    _pdl_ok  = not na(pd_low) and (entry_price - pd_low) >= _min_dist and (entry_price - pd_low) <= _max_dist
    _onl_ok  = not na(on_low) and (entry_price - on_low) >= _min_dist and (entry_price - on_low) <= _max_dist
    _orbl_ok = orb_done and not na(orb_low) and (entry_price - orb_low) >= _min_dist and (entry_price - orb_low) <= _max_dist
    // Pick nearest valid level below entry
    _best = float(na)
    if _val_ok  and (na(_best) or g_val  > _best)
        _best := g_val
    if _poc_ok  and (na(_best) or g_poc  > _best)
        _best := g_poc
    if _pdl_ok  and (na(_best) or pd_low > _best)
        _best := pd_low
    if _onl_ok  and (na(_best) or on_low > _best)
        _best := on_low
    if _orbl_ok and (na(_best) or orb_low > _best)
        _best := orb_low
    _tp     = na(_best) ? entry_price - _risk * 2.0 : _best
    _rr_raw = (entry_price - _tp) / _risk
    _rr     = math.round(math.min(_rr_raw, 8.0) * 10) / 10
    [_tp, _rr]

f_draw_entry(is_long, lbl_text, entry_p, level_p, sl_p, tp_p, is_conf) =>
    _box_top = level_p + touch
    _box_bot = level_p - touch
    _bcol    = is_long ? col_long_entry : col_short_entry
    _alpha   = is_conf ? 78 : 85
    // Box and label always drawn together — caller already checked _rr >= min_rr
    box.new(bar_index - 1, _box_top, bar_index + 1, _box_bot, border_color=color.new(_bcol, 50), bgcolor=color.new(_bcol, _alpha))
    // Two-step TP system from transcript:
    // TP1 = 1.5× risk (sell half, move stop to breakeven)
    // TP2 = next structural level (let remainder run)
    _risk     = math.abs(entry_p - sl_p)
    _tp1      = is_long ? entry_p + _risk * 1.5 : entry_p - _risk * 1.5
    _ep_str   = str.tostring(math.round(entry_p))
    _sl_str   = str.tostring(math.round(sl_p))
    _tp1_str  = str.tostring(math.round(_tp1))
    _tp2_str  = str.tostring(math.round(tp_p))
    _rr1      = math.round(1.5 * 10) / 10
    _rr2      = math.round(math.abs(tp_p - entry_p) / math.max(_risk, 0.0001) * 10) / 10
    _full_lbl = lbl_text + "\nE " + _ep_str + " | SL " + _sl_str + "\nTP1 " + _tp1_str + " (1.5R) → TP2 " + _tp2_str + " (" + str.tostring(_rr2) + "R)"
    _lbl_y    = is_long ? low - atr14 * 0.5 : high + atr14 * 0.5
    _lbl_style = is_long ? label.style_label_up : label.style_label_down
    _lbl_size  = size.small
    label.new(bar_index, _lbl_y, _full_lbl, color=color.new(_bcol, is_conf ? 0 : 20), textcolor=is_long ? color.black : color.white, style=_lbl_style, size=_lbl_size)
    _lw = is_conf ? 2 : 1
    if show_sl
        line.new(bar_index, sl_p,  bar_index + tf_line_ext, sl_p,  color=col_sl_line,                    width=_lw, style=line.style_dashed)
    if show_tp
        // TP1 line — brighter, solid-ish (first target, take half off)
        line.new(bar_index, _tp1,  bar_index + tf_line_ext, _tp1,  color=color.new(col_tp_line, 20),     width=_lw, style=line.style_dashed)
        // TP2 line — slightly faded (let remainder run to structural level)
        line.new(bar_index, tp_p,  bar_index + tf_line_ext, tp_p,  color=color.new(col_tp_line, 55),     width=1,   style=line.style_dotted)

// POST-BREAKOUT RETEST ENTRY
// When price breaks cleanly through a level (> 1 ATR beyond it), that level
// flips role. We then watch for price to pull back and retest it from the new

// Retest window in bars — ~25 real minutes across all TFs
tf_retest_window = _tf_mult == 1 ? 25 : _tf_mult == 3 ? 8 : 5

// Track breakouts: when price closes > break_dist beyond a level, record it
// We track the 6 MGL levels + VAH/VAL for retest opportunities
var float  bo_level      = na
var int    bo_dir        = 0
var int    bo_bar        = -999
var string bo_label      = ""
var string bo_last_label = ""   // track last fired retest label for per-level cooldown
var int    bo_last_bar   = -999 // bar of last fired retest — per-level gate

// Detect clean breakouts through any key level
// Breakout detection — ONLY during active session bars, never on session open bar
// A gap open is not a breakout — price must have traded through the level
// is_new_day and is_new_ovn are the gap bars — exclude them
_valid_bo_bar = barstate.isconfirmed and not is_new_day and not is_new_ovn

// Upward breakout: close moved more than break_dist ABOVE the level
_bo_up_pdl  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(pd_low)  and close > pd_low  + break_dist and close[1] <= pd_low  + break_dist
_bo_up_onl  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(on_low)  and close > on_low  + break_dist and close[1] <= on_low  + break_dist
_bo_up_orbl = _valid_bo_bar and orb_done and not na(orb_low)  and close > orb_low  + break_dist and close[1] <= orb_low  + break_dist
_bo_up_val  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(g_val)   and close > g_val   + break_dist and close[1] <= g_val   + break_dist
_bo_up_pdh  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(pd_high) and close > pd_high + break_dist and close[1] <= pd_high + break_dist
_bo_up_onh  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(on_high) and close > on_high + break_dist and close[1] <= on_high + break_dist
_bo_up_orbh = _valid_bo_bar and orb_done and not na(orb_high) and close > orb_high + break_dist and close[1] <= orb_high + break_dist
_bo_up_vah  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(g_vah)   and close > g_vah   + break_dist and close[1] <= g_vah   + break_dist

// Downward breakout
_bo_dn_pdh  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(pd_high) and close < pd_high - break_dist and close[1] >= pd_high - break_dist
_bo_dn_onh  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(on_high) and close < on_high - break_dist and close[1] >= on_high - break_dist
_bo_dn_orbh = _valid_bo_bar and orb_done and not na(orb_high) and close < orb_high - break_dist and close[1] >= orb_high - break_dist
_bo_dn_vah  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(g_vah)   and close < g_vah   - break_dist and close[1] >= g_vah   - break_dist
_bo_dn_pdl  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(pd_low)  and close < pd_low  - break_dist and close[1] >= pd_low  - break_dist
_bo_dn_onl  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(on_low)  and close < on_low  - break_dist and close[1] >= on_low  - break_dist
_bo_dn_orbl = _valid_bo_bar and orb_done and not na(orb_low)  and close < orb_low  - break_dist and close[1] >= orb_low  - break_dist
_bo_dn_val  = _valid_bo_bar and not na(g_val)   and close < g_val   - break_dist and close[1] >= g_val   - break_dist

if barstate.isconfirmed
    // Register the most recently broken level (priority: ORB > ONH/ONL > PDH/PDL > VA)
    if _bo_up_orbl
        bo_level := orb_low
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ORB-L"
    else if _bo_up_onl
        bo_level := on_low
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ONL"
    else if _bo_up_pdl
        bo_level := pd_low
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "PDL"
    else if _bo_up_val
        bo_level := g_val
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "VAL"
    else if _bo_up_orbh
        bo_level := orb_high
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ORB-H↑"
    else if _bo_up_onh
        bo_level := on_high
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ONH↑"
    else if _bo_up_pdh
        bo_level := pd_high
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "PDH↑"
    else if _bo_up_vah
        bo_level := g_vah
        bo_dir   := 1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "VAH↑"
    else if _bo_dn_orbh
        bo_level := orb_high
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ORB-H"
    else if _bo_dn_onh
        bo_level := on_high
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ONH"
    else if _bo_dn_pdh
        bo_level := pd_high
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "PDH"
    else if _bo_dn_vah
        bo_level := g_vah
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "VAH"
    else if _bo_dn_pdl
        bo_level := pd_low
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "PDL↓"
    else if _bo_dn_onl
        bo_level := on_low
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ONL↓"
    else if _bo_dn_orbl
        bo_level := orb_low
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "ORB-L↓"
    else if _bo_dn_val
        bo_level := g_val
        bo_dir   := -1
        bo_bar   := bar_index
        bo_label := "VAL↓"

// Retest detection — within window, price returns to the broken level
// Minimum consolidation: price must hold on the breakout side for 3+ bars
// before the retest is valid. A spike-and-reverse (0-2 bars) = LAF, not retest.
var int bo_consol_bars = 0  // count of bars price has held on breakout side

if barstate.isconfirmed
    if not na(bo_level) and bo_dir == 1
        // Upward breakout — count bars where close stays above the level
        if close > bo_level
            bo_consol_bars := bo_consol_bars + 1
        else
            bo_consol_bars := 0  // reset if price falls back through
    else if not na(bo_level) and bo_dir == -1
        // Downward breakout — count bars where close stays below the level
        if close < bo_level
            bo_consol_bars := bo_consol_bars + 1
        else
            bo_consol_bars := 0

// Reset consolidation count when a new breakout registers
// Done by checking if bo_bar changed — new breakout resets counter
var int prev_bo_bar = -999
if bo_bar != prev_bo_bar
    bo_consol_bars := 0
    prev_bo_bar    := bo_bar

_bo_confirmed     = bo_consol_bars >= 3  // must hold 3+ bars before retest is valid
_in_retest_window = not na(bo_level) and _bo_confirmed and (bar_index - bo_bar) <= tf_retest_window and (bar_index - bo_bar) > 0
_retest_long_zone = _in_retest_window and bo_dir == 1  and low  <= bo_level + atr14 * 0.8 and low  >= bo_level - atr14 * 0.5
_retest_short_zone= _in_retest_window and bo_dir == -1 and high >= bo_level - atr14 * 0.8 and high <= bo_level + atr14 * 0.5

// ── FAKEOUT FILTERS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

// 1. MINIMUM CONSOLIDATION — price must spend at least 2 bars near the level
//    before the entry fires. Instant touch-and-fire is usually a fakeout.
//    We check that the prior bar was also in the retest zone.
_consol_long  = _retest_long_zone  and (low[1]  <= bo_level + atr14 * 1.2 or low[2]  <= bo_level + atr14 * 1.2)
_consol_short = _retest_short_zone and (high[1] >= bo_level - atr14 * 1.2 or high[2] >= bo_level - atr14 * 1.2)

// 2. DECLINING VOLUME ON RETEST APPROACH — the pullback to the level should be
//    on lower volume than the breakout candle. High volume on the retest = the
//    move against you has conviction = likely fakeout or reversal.
//    Volume at the retest bar must be below the volume at the breakout bar.
_bo_vol         = volume[bar_index - bo_bar]  // volume on the original breakout bar
_retest_vol_ok  = volume < _bo_vol * 1.2      // retest volume must not exceed breakout vol

// 3. MOMENTUM CHECK — the retest candle body must be smaller than the breakout
//    candle body. If the bounce back to the level is as big as the breakout,
//    it means the opposing side has equal conviction — not a safe retest.
_bo_body        = math.abs(close[bar_index - bo_bar] - open[bar_index - bo_bar])
_retest_body_ok = candle_body < _bo_body * 1.5  // retest candle not bigger than breakout

// 4. DEEP RETRACEMENT INVALIDATION — if price has come back more than 50% of
//    the distance from the level to the post-breakout high/low, the breakout
//    is likely failing. Based on NQ data: deep retracement = 75% failure rate.
//    We approximate: if close is already more than 1.5 ATR back through the level, skip.
_not_too_deep_long  = not na(bo_level) and close > bo_level - atr14 * 1.5
_not_too_deep_short = not na(bo_level) and close < bo_level + atr14 * 1.5

_vol_conf   = volume > avg_vol * 1.2
_retest_long_conf  = _retest_long_zone  and _consol_long  and _retest_vol_ok and _retest_body_ok and _not_too_deep_long  and close > bo_level and close > open and (_vol_conf or _of_long)
_retest_short_conf = _retest_short_zone and _consol_short and _retest_vol_ok and _retest_body_ok and _not_too_deep_short and close < bo_level and close < open and (_vol_conf or _of_short)

// Per-level cooldown: if the same level label fired recently, block re-entry
// 60 bars on 1m = 60 min, 20 bars on 3m, 12 bars on 5m
_retest_level_cooldown = _tf_mult == 1 ? 90 : _tf_mult == 3 ? 30 : 18
_same_level_blocked    = bo_label == bo_last_label and (bar_index - bo_last_bar) < _retest_level_cooldown

retest_long  = show_entry and barstate.isconfirmed and _retest_long_conf  and allow_long  and (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > _adaptive_cooldown and not _same_level_blocked
retest_short = show_entry and barstate.isconfirmed and _retest_short_conf and allow_short and (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > _adaptive_cooldown and not _same_level_blocked

if retest_long
    _sl = math.min(low, bo_level) - atr14 * 1.0
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_long(close, _sl)
    _saved_bo_level = bo_level  // save before reset
    last_entry_bar := bar_index
    last_dir       := 1
    bo_last_label  := bo_label
    bo_last_bar    := bar_index
    bo_level       := na
    pend_short     := false
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(true, "▲ RETEST LONG\n" + bo_label + f_priority_star(bo_label) + "  " + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, _saved_bo_level, _sl, _tp, true)

if retest_short
    _sl = math.max(high, math.max(high[1], high[2]), bo_level) + atr14 * 1.0
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_short(close, _sl)
    _saved_bo_level = bo_level  // save before reset
    last_entry_bar := bar_index
    last_dir       := -1
    bo_last_label  := bo_label
    bo_last_bar    := bar_index
    bo_level       := na
    pend_long      := false
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(false, "▼ RETEST SHORT\n" + bo_label + f_priority_star(bo_label) + "  " + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, _saved_bo_level, _sl, _tp, true)

alertcondition(retest_long,  "▲ Retest Long",  "Post-breakout retest long — level flipped to support")
alertcondition(retest_short, "▼ Retest Short", "Post-breakout retest short — level flipped to resistance")
// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

// Confirmation window — much wider to allow for bounces before entry
// 60 min real time: catches the typical 45-min bounce before the real move
conf_max_bars = _tf_mult == 1 ? 60 : _tf_mult == 3 ? 20 : 12

// Track bounce extremes during pending window
// For shorts: track highest point reached during bounce (lower high = confirmation)
// For longs: track lowest point reached during dip (higher low = confirmation)
var float pend_bounce_high = na  // highest high seen while pend_short active
var float pend_bounce_low  = na  // lowest low seen while pend_long active

if barstate.isconfirmed
    // Update bounce tracking while pending is active
    // Cap at 30 bars to prevent the bounce high/low drifting too far from entry
    _pend_short_age = bar_index - pend_bar_s
    _pend_long_age  = bar_index - pend_bar_l

    if pend_short and _pend_short_age <= 30
        if na(pend_bounce_high)
            pend_bounce_high := high
        else if high > pend_bounce_high
            pend_bounce_high := high

    if pend_long and _pend_long_age <= 30
        if na(pend_bounce_low)
            pend_bounce_low := low
        else if low < pend_bounce_low
            pend_bounce_low := low

    _near_long_level = _level_long or (not na(g_val) and close > g_val - atr14 * 3 and close < g_val + atr14 * 3)
    _lbf_near_val    = look_below_fail and not na(g_val) and low < g_val + atr14 * 2
    _abs_near_val    = abs_bull and not na(g_val) and low < g_val + atr14 * 2
    _trap_near_val   = trap_sellers and not na(g_val) and low < g_val + atr14 * 2
    _of_setup_long   = _lbf_near_val or _abs_near_val or _trap_near_val

    _near_short_level = _level_short or (not na(g_vah) and close < g_vah + atr14 * 3 and close > g_vah - atr14 * 3)
    _laf_near_vah    = look_above_fail and not na(g_vah) and high > g_vah - atr14 * 2
    _abs_near_vah    = abs_bear and not na(g_vah) and high > g_vah - atr14 * 2
    _trap_near_vah   = trap_buyers and not na(g_vah) and high > g_vah - atr14 * 2
    _of_setup_short  = _laf_near_vah or _abs_near_vah or _trap_near_vah

    if _pend_ready and _level_long and low < _lp_long and close > _lp_long and close > open and allow_long
        pend_long        := true
        pend_lp_l        := _lp_long
        pend_ll_l        := _ll_long
        pend_bar_l       := bar_index
        pend_wick_low    := low
        pend_bounce_low  := low

    if _pend_ready and _of_setup_long and allow_long and not pend_long
        pend_long        := true
        pend_lp_l        := g_val
        pend_ll_l        := "VAL"
        pend_bar_l       := bar_index
        pend_wick_low    := low
        pend_bounce_low  := low

    if _pend_ready and _level_short and high > _lp_short and close < _lp_short and close < open and allow_short
        pend_short       := true
        pend_lp_s        := _lp_short
        pend_ll_s        := _ll_short
        pend_bar_s       := bar_index
        pend_wick_high   := high
        pend_bounce_high := high

    if _pend_ready and _of_setup_short and allow_short and not pend_short
        pend_short       := true
        pend_lp_s        := g_vah
        pend_ll_s        := "VAH"
        pend_bar_s       := bar_index
        pend_wick_high   := high
        pend_bounce_high := high

    // Expire: window elapsed OR price moved decisively AGAINST the setup
    // Long: expires if price drops more than 2 ATR below the level (level failed as support)
    // Short: expires if price rallies more than 2 ATR above the level (level failed as resistance)
    if pend_long  and (bar_index - pend_bar_l > conf_max_bars or low < pend_lp_l - atr14 * 2)
        pend_long        := false
        pend_bounce_low  := na
    if pend_short and (bar_index - pend_bar_s > conf_max_bars or high > pend_lp_s + atr14 * 2)
        pend_short       := false
        pend_bounce_high := na

// ── Confirmation triggers ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
// For shorts: require close below the bounce low — confirms lower high formed
// This catches the entry AFTER the 45-min bounce, not during it
// For longs: require close above the bounce high — confirms higher low formed
// Bounce confirmation — price must have pulled back from the bounce extreme
// For shorts: close must be BELOW the bounce high by at least 0.5 ATR (bounce failed)
// For longs: close must be ABOVE the bounce low by at least 0.5 ATR (dip failed)
_bounce_high_conf = na(pend_bounce_high) or close < pend_bounce_high - atr14 * 0.5
_bounce_low_conf  = na(pend_bounce_low)  or close > pend_bounce_low  + atr14 * 0.5

// _extra_confirm_long/short defined earlier before agg entries — used here too
conf_long  = show_entry and show_conf and barstate.isconfirmed and pend_long  and not na(pend_lp_l) and close > pend_lp_l and close > open  and _bounce_low_conf  and _extra_confirm_long  and (volume > avg_vol * 1.5 or _of_long)  and _long_allowed  and (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > _adaptive_cooldown and bar_index > pend_bar_l
conf_short = show_entry and show_conf and barstate.isconfirmed and pend_short and not na(pend_lp_s) and close < pend_lp_s and close < open  and _bounce_high_conf and _extra_confirm_short and (volume > avg_vol * 1.5 or _of_short) and _short_allowed and (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > _adaptive_cooldown and bar_index > pend_bar_s

if conf_long
    _wick_sl = (not na(pend_wick_low) ? pend_wick_low : pend_lp_l) - atr14 * 0.75
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_long(close, _wick_sl)
    last_entry_bar := bar_index
    last_dir       := 1
    pend_long        := false
    pend_short       := false
    pend_bounce_low  := na
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(true, "▲ CONF LONG\n" + pend_ll_l + f_priority_star(pend_ll_l) + "  " + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, pend_lp_l, _wick_sl, _tp, true)

if conf_short
    // Stop above the highest point reached during the bounce — the true invalidation level
    _bounce_ref = not na(pend_bounce_high) ? pend_bounce_high : (not na(pend_wick_high) ? pend_wick_high : pend_lp_s)
    _wick_sl = math.max(_bounce_ref, high[1]) + atr14 * 0.75
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_short(close, _wick_sl)
    last_entry_bar := bar_index
    last_dir       := -1
    pend_short      := false
    pend_long       := false
    pend_bounce_high := na
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(false, "▼ CONF SHORT\n" + pend_ll_s + f_priority_star(pend_ll_s) + "  " + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, pend_lp_s, _wick_sl, _tp, true)

// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
// TRAPPED POSITION SQUEEZE DETECTION
// When a short entry fires at a level but price then breaks ABOVE that level
// instead of dropping — shorts are trapped and being squeezed. The squeeze
// itself fuels the move. Fire a long entry on the breakout confirmation.
// Same logic inverted for trapped longs below a level.
// ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

var float last_short_level = na
var float last_long_level  = na
var int   last_short_entry_bar = -999
var int   last_long_entry_bar  = -999

// Track levels when entries fire
if conf_short or agg_short or retest_short
    last_short_level     := conf_short ? pend_lp_s : (retest_short ? bo_level[1] : _lp_short)
    last_short_entry_bar := bar_index

if conf_long or agg_long or retest_long
    last_long_level     := conf_long ? pend_lp_l : (retest_long ? bo_level[1] : _lp_long)
    last_long_entry_bar := bar_index

// Squeeze window: within 60 bars of the original entry
_squeeze_window = 60

// Trapped short squeeze: price breaks above the short level with momentum
// Requirements: within window, close > last short level + 1 ATR (genuine breakout)
// AND close > open (bullish bar) AND volume above average (momentum)
// AND cooldown has partially elapsed (at least 10 bars since short fired)
_short_squeeze_active = not na(last_short_level) and
     (bar_index - last_short_entry_bar) <= _squeeze_window and
     (bar_index - last_short_entry_bar) >= 20 and
     (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > (_adaptive_cooldown / 2)

_trapped_short_squeeze = show_entry and barstate.isconfirmed and _short_squeeze_active and
     close > last_short_level + atr14 * 1.5 and
     close > open and
     volume > avg_vol * 1.5 and
     last_dir == -1

_long_squeeze_active = not na(last_long_level) and
     (bar_index - last_long_entry_bar) <= _squeeze_window and
     (bar_index - last_long_entry_bar) >= 20 and
     (bar_index - last_entry_bar) > (_adaptive_cooldown / 2)

_trapped_long_squeeze = show_entry and barstate.isconfirmed and _long_squeeze_active and
     close < last_long_level - atr14 * 1.5 and
     close < open and
     volume > avg_vol * 1.5 and
     last_dir == 1

if _trapped_short_squeeze
    _sl = math.min(low, last_short_level) - atr14 * 0.75
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_long(close, _sl)
    last_entry_bar      := bar_index
    last_dir            := 1
    last_short_level    := na
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(true, "▲ SQUEEZE LONG ★\n" + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, close - atr14 * 0.5, _sl, _tp, true)

if _trapped_long_squeeze
    _sl = math.max(high, last_long_level) + atr14 * 0.75
    [_tp, _rr] = f_target_short(close, _sl)
    last_entry_bar     := bar_index
    last_dir           := -1
    last_long_level    := na
    if _rr >= min_rr
        f_draw_entry(false, "▼ SQUEEZE SHORT ★\n" + str.tostring(_rr) + "R", close, close + atr14 * 0.5, _sl, _tp, true)

// ── Entry alerts ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
alertcondition(agg_long,              "▲ Aggressive Long",    "Aggressive long — level + OF confirmation")
alertcondition(agg_short,             "▼ Aggressive Short",   "Aggressive short — level + OF confirmation")
alertcondition(conf_long,             "▲ Confirmation Long",  "Confirmation long — retest of level with volume")
alertcondition(conf_short,            "▼ Confirmation Short", "Confirmation short — retest of level with volume")
alertcondition(_trapped_short_squeeze,"▲ Squeeze Long",       "Trapped shorts being squeezed — breakout long")
alertcondition(_trapped_long_squeeze, "▼ Squeeze Short",      "Trapped longs being squeezed — breakdown short")
