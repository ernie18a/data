WHAT IS VOLUME PROFILE VIP?

Volume Profile VIP is a professional institutional-grade indicator that shows you where the most trading activity happened at every price level. Unlike regular volume bars at the bottom of your chart that only show HOW MUCH volume occurred per candle, Volume Profile VIP shows you WHERE that volume happened in terms of price.

This is the same tool used by banks, hedge funds, and professional traders to identify the most important price levels in the market. When you know where volume is concentrated, you know where the big players are positioned — and that tells you where price is likely to react.


WHO IS THIS FOR?


New traders who want to understand why price moves the way it does
Day traders who need to know the most important intraday levels
Swing traders who want to identify institutional price zones
Anyone who wants to trade with the smart money instead of against it



WHAT DOES IT DO?

Volume Profile VIP has 4 core engines working together:


1. 📊 VOLUME PROFILE — "Where did all the trading happen?"

The Volume Profile is drawn as horizontal bars on your chart. Each bar represents a price level, and the width of the bar shows how much volume traded at that price.

How to read it:


Wide bar = Lots of trading happened here — institutions were active at this price
Narrow bar = Very little trading happened here — price moved through quickly
🟢 Green portion of each bar = Bullish (buying) volume
🔴 Red portion of each bar = Bearish (selling) volume


The three most important levels:

◆ POC — Point of Control

The single most important level on the entire chart. It is the price where the MOST volume traded during the lookback period. Think of it as the center of gravity for price — the market always wants to return to this level.


Drawn as a solid gold line stretching across the chart
Labeled with the exact price and % distance from current price
If price is above POC → POC acts as support
If price is below POC → POC acts as resistance
When price returns to POC, expect a strong reaction


▲ VAH — Value Area High

The upper boundary of the Value Area — the price range where 70% of all volume traded. This is where the market considers "fair value" for this asset.


Drawn as a dashed cyan line
When price is ABOVE VAH → price is in premium territory, watch for rejection
When price BREAKS ABOVE VAH with volume → strong bullish signal
VAH often acts as resistance when approached from below


▼ VAL — Value Area Low

The lower boundary of the Value Area.


Drawn as a dashed red line
When price is BELOW VAL → price is in discount territory, watch for a bounce
When price BREAKS BELOW VAL with volume → strong bearish signal
VAL often acts as support when approached from above


The Value Area Rule (used by professional traders):


If price opens OUTSIDE the value area and returns back INTO it, there is an 80% chance it will travel all the way to the other side of the value area.
Example: Price opens below VAL, then pushes back above VAL → high probability it reaches VAH.




2. 🐂🐻 BULL / BEAR VOLUME SPLIT — "Who is in control at each level?"

Each profile bar is split into two colors:


🟢 Green (left side) = Buying volume — bulls were in control here
🔴 Red (right side) = Selling volume — bears were in control here


How to use this:


A level with mostly green = Bullish liquidity zone — buyers defended this price
A level with mostly red = Bearish liquidity zone — sellers dominated this price
POC row with mostly green = Institutional buying at the most important level = very bullish
POC row with mostly red = Institutional selling at the most important level = very bearish



3. ★ INSTITUTIONAL FIBONACCI — "Where do the big players enter?"

The Fibonacci retracement levels are automatically calculated from the highest high to the lowest low within your lookback window. These levels mark where professional and institutional traders are most likely to place orders.

All 8 levels explained:

The Golden Pocket (0.618 — 0.650):
This zone between 0.618 and 0.650 is highlighted with a filled gold box. It is the single most watched level by institutional traders worldwide. When price retraces into this zone in an uptrend and shows bullish signals, it is often the best buying opportunity. In a downtrend, it is often the best selling opportunity.

The Equilibrium (0.500):
Highlighted with a light blue zone. This is the exact midpoint between the swing high and swing low.


Price ABOVE Equilibrium = Premium zone (price is expensive relative to the swing)
Price BELOW Equilibrium = Discount zone (price is cheap relative to the swing)
Smart money buys in discount, sells in premium


Every label shows:


Full name and description of the level
Exact price value
Percentage distance from current price (+ = above you, - = below you)

HOW TO USE VOLUME PROFILE VIP — STEP BY STEP

Step 1 — Find the POC

The POC is the gold line. This is your anchor for everything.


Is price above POC? The market is bullish overall
Is price below POC? The market is bearish overall
Is price at POC? Expect a strong move — this is a high probability reaction zone


Step 2 — Identify Where You Are in the Value Area


Above VAH = Price is expensive, potential short or take profit zone
Between VAL and VAH = Price is at fair value, use other signals to determine direction
Below VAL = Price is cheap, potential long or watch for bounce
Breaking VAH upward = Bullish momentum, price may continue higher
Breaking VAL downward = Bearish momentum, price may continue lower


Step 3 — Combine with Fibonacci

Look at where the current price is relative to the Fibonacci levels:


Price pulling back to 0.618 Golden Pocket in an uptrend + POC nearby = Extremely strong buy zone
Price at VAH AND 0.786 Fibonacci resistance = Extremely strong sell zone
When Fibonacci levels and Volume Profile levels overlap = highest probability trade


Step 4 — Use the Bull/Bear Split to Confirm

Look at the color split on the profile bar at your key level:


Green dominant at POC + price above POC = Institutional buyers defending this level
Red dominant at POC + price below POC = Institutional sellers pushing price down


Step 5 — Wait for Price Action

Never enter just because price is at a level. Wait for:


A rejection candle (long wick, strong close away from the level)
A breakout candle (strong close through the level with volume)
A signal from Prophecy Signals indicator confirming the move

````pine
//@version=6
indicator('⚡ Volume Profile VIP', overlay = true, max_lines_count = 500, max_labels_count = 500, max_boxes_count = 500)

// ============================================================
//  INPUTS — VOLUME PROFILE
// ============================================================
grp_vp = 'Volume Profile'
vpRows = input.int(48, 'Profile Rows', group = grp_vp, minval = 10, maxval = 100)
vpLookback = input.int(200, 'Lookback (bars)', group = grp_vp, minval = 20, maxval = 500)
vpMaxBarW = input.int(35, 'Max Bar Width (bars)', group = grp_vp, minval = 5, maxval = 120)
showPOC = input.bool(true, 'Show POC', group = grp_vp)
showVAH = input.bool(true, 'Show VAH', group = grp_vp)
showVAL = input.bool(true, 'Show VAL', group = grp_vp)
showVPLabels = input.bool(true, 'Show VP Labels', group = grp_vp)
valueAreaPct = input.float(70, 'Value Area %', group = grp_vp, minval = 50, maxval = 95, step = 5)
showBullBear = input.bool(true, 'Bull / Bear Split', group = grp_vp)
pocColor = input.color(color.new(#FFD700, 0), 'POC Color', group = grp_vp)
vpLblGap = input.int(30, 'VP Label Gap (bars)', group = grp_vp, minval = 5, maxval = 150)
vpChartGap = input.int(15, 'Profile Chart Gap (bars)', group = grp_vp, minval = 0, maxval = 100)

// ============================================================
//  INPUTS — FIBONACCI
// ============================================================
grp_fib = 'Institutional Fibonacci'
showFib = input.bool(true, 'Show Fibonacci', group = grp_fib)
fibLookback = input.int(100, 'Swing Lookback (bars)', group = grp_fib, minval = 20, maxval = 500)
fibExtend = input.int(60, 'Extend Lines (bars right)', group = grp_fib, minval = 10, maxval = 300)

// ============================================================
//  INPUTS — EMA
// ============================================================
grp_ema = 'EMA Overlays'
showEMA = input.bool(true, 'Show EMAs', group = grp_ema)
emaFast = input.int(21, 'Fast EMA', group = grp_ema, minval = 1)
emaMid = input.int(50, 'Mid EMA', group = grp_ema, minval = 1)
emaSlow = input.int(200, 'Slow EMA', group = grp_ema, minval = 1)
emaFastClr = input.color(color.new(#00BCD4, 0), 'Fast EMA Color', group = grp_ema)
emaMidClr = input.color(color.new(#FF9800, 0), 'Mid EMA Color', group = grp_ema)
emaSlowClr = input.color(color.new(#E040FB, 0), 'Slow EMA Color', group = grp_ema)


// ============================================================
//  FIBONACCI HELPERS
// ============================================================
fibHigh = ta.highest(high, fibLookback)
fibLow = ta.lowest(low, fibLookback)
fibRng = fibHigh - fibLow
fibUptrend = close > (fibHigh + fibLow) / 2

// ============================================================
//  EMA
// ============================================================
ema1 = ta.ema(close, emaFast)
ema2 = ta.ema(close, emaMid)
ema3 = ta.ema(close, emaSlow)

bullTrend = ema1 > ema2 and ema2 > ema3
bearTrend = ema1 < ema2 and ema2 < ema3

// EMA plots — cast input.color to series color via ternary
p1 = plot(showEMA ? ema1 : na, 'Fast EMA', color = showEMA ? emaFastClr : na, linewidth = 1)
p2 = plot(showEMA ? ema2 : na, 'Mid EMA', color = showEMA ? emaMidClr : na, linewidth = 1)
plot(showEMA ? ema3 : na, 'Slow EMA', color = showEMA ? emaSlowClr : na, linewidth = 2)
fill(p1, p2, color = bullTrend ? color.new(#00BCD4, 88) : bearTrend ? color.new(#FF5252, 88) : color.new(#888888, 92))


fibPrice(float ratio) =>
    fibUptrend ? fibHigh - fibRng * ratio : fibLow + fibRng * ratio

fibRatioStr(float ratio) =>
    string s = '1.000  — Swing High/Low (100%)'
    if ratio == 0.0
        s := '0.000  — Swing High/Low (0%)'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.236
        s := '0.236  — Shallow Retracement'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.382
        s := '0.382  — Key Support / Resistance'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.5
        s := '⚖ EQUILIBRIUM  0.500  — Fair Value Midpoint'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.618
        s := '★ 0.618  — Golden Ratio  (Institutional Entry)'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.65
        s := '★ 0.650  — Golden Pocket Zone'
        s
    else if ratio == 0.786
        s := '0.786  — Deep Retracement Zone'
        s
    s

fibLineColor(float ratio) =>
    color c = color.new(#FFD700, 55)
    if ratio == 0.0 or ratio == 1.0
        c := color.new(#FFFFFF, 10)
        c
    else if ratio == 0.618 or ratio == 0.65
        c := color.new(#FFD700, 0)
        c
    else if ratio == 0.5
        c := color.new(#E0F7FA, 0)
        c
    else if ratio == 0.382 or ratio == 0.786
        c := color.new(#FFA726, 10)
        c
    else if ratio == 0.236
        c := color.new(#FFE082, 0)
        c
    c

fibLabelSize(float ratio) =>
    ratio == 0.618 or ratio == 0.65 ? size.normal : ratio == 0.5 ? size.normal : size.normal

array<float> fibRatios = array.from(0.0, 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.65, 0.786, 1.0)

var array<line> fibLines = array.new_line(0)
var array<label> fibLbls = array.new_label(0)
var box fibPocket = na

// ============================================================
//  VP STATE
// ============================================================
var float vpPOC = na
var float vpVAH = na
var float vpVAL = na
var float vpTotalVol = na

// ============================================================
//  REBUILD ON LAST BAR
// ============================================================
if barstate.islast

    // ── FIBONACCI ────────────────────────────────────────────
    if showFib
        if array.size(fibLines) > 0
            for i = 0 to array.size(fibLines) - 1 by 1
                line.delete(array.get(fibLines, i))
        array.clear(fibLines)
        if array.size(fibLbls) > 0
            for i = 0 to array.size(fibLbls) - 1 by 1
                label.delete(array.get(fibLbls, i))
        array.clear(fibLbls)
        if not na(fibPocket)
            box.delete(fibPocket)

        int fibStartX = bar_index - fibLookback
        int fibEndX = bar_index + fibExtend

        // Golden Pocket zone
        float pTop = math.max(fibPrice(0.618), fibPrice(0.65))
        float pBot = math.min(fibPrice(0.618), fibPrice(0.65))
        fibPocket := box.new(fibStartX, pTop, fibEndX, pBot, bgcolor = color.new(#FFD700, 80), border_color = color.new(#FFD700, 15), border_width = 1)

        // Equilibrium zone — subtle band around 0.5
        float eqLvl = fibPrice(0.5)
        float eqHalf = fibRng * 0.01
        box.new(fibStartX, eqLvl + eqHalf, fibEndX, eqLvl - eqHalf, bgcolor = color.new(#E0F7FA, 82), border_color = color.new(#E0F7FA, 40), border_width = 1)

        if array.size(fibRatios) > 0
            for i = 0 to array.size(fibRatios) - 1 by 1
                float ratio = array.get(fibRatios, i)
                float lvl = fibPrice(ratio)
                color clr = fibLineColor(ratio)
                bool isGold = ratio == 0.618 or ratio == 0.65 or ratio == 0.5

                line.new(fibStartX, lvl, bar_index + fibExtend * 2 / 3, lvl, color = clr, width = isGold ? 3 : 2, style = line.style_solid)

                float pctRaw = (lvl - close) / close * 100
                string pctSign = pctRaw >= 0 ? '+' : ''
                string pctStr = pctSign + str.tostring(math.round(pctRaw, 2)) + '%'
                string lblTxt = fibRatioStr(ratio) + '   ' + str.tostring(math.round(lvl, 2)) + '  (' + pctStr + ')'

                array.push(fibLbls, label.new(bar_index + fibExtend * 2 / 3, lvl, lblTxt, color = color.new(#050505, 0), textcolor = clr, style = label.style_label_left, size = fibLabelSize(ratio)))

    // ── VOLUME PROFILE DATA ──────────────────────────────────
    float rangeHigh = high
    float rangeLow = low
    for i = 0 to vpLookback - 1 by 1
        if high[i] > rangeHigh
            rangeHigh := high[i]
            rangeHigh
        if low[i] < rangeLow
            rangeLow := low[i]
            rangeLow

    float rowSize = (rangeHigh - rangeLow) / vpRows

    array<float> rowVol = array.new_float(vpRows, 0.0)
    array<float> rowBullVol = array.new_float(vpRows, 0.0)

    for i = 0 to vpLookback - 1 by 1
        float bH = high[i]
        float bL = low[i]
        float bV = volume[i]
        bool bull = close[i] >= open[i]
        float bR = math.max(bH - bL, rowSize)
        for r = 0 to vpRows - 1 by 1
            float rBot = rangeLow + r * rowSize
            float rTop = rBot + rowSize
            float ovlp = math.min(bH, rTop) - math.max(bL, rBot)
            if ovlp > 0
                float part = ovlp / bR * bV
                array.set(rowVol, r, array.get(rowVol, r) + part)
                if bull
                    array.set(rowBullVol, r, array.get(rowBullVol, r) + part)

    // POC
    float maxVol = 0.0
    int pocRow = 0
    float totalVol = 0.0
    for r = 0 to vpRows - 1 by 1
        float v = array.get(rowVol, r)
        totalVol := totalVol + v
        if v > maxVol
            maxVol := v
            pocRow := r
            pocRow

    vpTotalVol := totalVol
    vpPOC := rangeLow + (pocRow + 0.5) * rowSize

    // Value Area
    float vaVol = array.get(rowVol, pocRow)
    float vaTarget = totalVol * (valueAreaPct / 100)
    int vahRow = pocRow
    int valRow = pocRow
    while vaVol < vaTarget
        float upV = vahRow + 1 <= vpRows - 1 ? array.get(rowVol, vahRow + 1) : 0.0
        float downV = valRow - 1 >= 0 ? array.get(rowVol, valRow - 1) : 0.0
        if upV >= downV and vahRow < vpRows - 1
            vahRow := vahRow + 1
            vaVol := vaVol + upV
            vaVol
        else if downV > 0 and valRow > 0
            valRow := valRow - 1
            vaVol := vaVol + downV
            vaVol
        else if vahRow < vpRows - 1
            vahRow := vahRow + 1
            vaVol := vaVol + upV
            vaVol
        else
            break

    vpVAH := rangeLow + (vahRow + 1) * rowSize
    vpVAL := rangeLow + valRow * rowSize

    // ── PROFILE DRAWING ──────────────────────────────────────
    // Profile sits RIGHT of current bar — bars grow from left wall (bar_index) rightward
    int profLeft = bar_index - vpMaxBarW * 3 / 2 - vpChartGap
    int profRight = bar_index - vpMaxBarW - vpChartGap

    // VP labels go AFTER fib labels: bar_index + fibExtend + vpLblGap
    int vpLblX = bar_index + fibExtend + vpLblGap

    for r = 0 to vpRows - 1 by 1
        float rBot = rangeLow + r * rowSize
        float rTop = rBot + rowSize
        float rMid = (rBot + rTop) / 2
        float v = array.get(rowVol, r)
        float bVol = array.get(rowBullVol, r)
        float pct = maxVol > 0 ? v / maxVol : 0.0
        int barW = math.round(pct * vpMaxBarW)
        bool isPOC = r == pocRow
        bool isVA = r >= valRow and r <= vahRow

        // Pixel-thin gap between rows (3% inset each side)
        float gap = rowSize * 0.06
        float bxTop = rTop - gap
        float bxBot = rBot + gap

        if barW > 0
            if showBullBear
                // Bear layer (full width, muted)
                color bearClr = isPOC ? color.new(pocColor, 35) : isVA ? color.new(#EF5350, 58) : color.new(#B71C1C, 72)
                box.new(profLeft, bxTop, profLeft + barW, bxBot, bgcolor = bearClr, border_color = color.new(#000000, 100), border_width = 0)

                // Bull layer (right portion, vibrant)
                float bullPct = v > 0 ? bVol / v : 0.0
                int bullW = math.round(bullPct * barW)
                if bullW > 0
                    color bullClr = isPOC ? color.new(pocColor, 10) : isVA ? color.new(#00E676, 48) : color.new(#1B5E20, 62)
                    box.new(profLeft + barW - bullW, bxTop, profLeft + barW, bxBot, bgcolor = bullClr, border_color = color.new(#000000, 100), border_width = 0)
            else
                color fillClr = isPOC ? color.new(pocColor, 20) : isVA ? color.new(#26C6DA, 52) : color.new(#37474F, 65)
                box.new(profLeft, bxTop, profLeft + barW, bxBot, bgcolor = fillClr, border_color = color.new(#000000, 100), border_width = 0)

            // Right-edge tick line — the clean "shelf" look
            color tickClr = isPOC ? pocColor : isVA ? color.new(#26C6DA, 10) : color.new(#607D8B, 30)
            line.new(profLeft + barW, bxTop, profLeft + barW, bxBot, color = tickClr, width = isPOC ? 2 : 1)

    // ── POC line + VP label ───────────────────────────────────
    int lineEndX = vpLblX - 2

    if showPOC and not na(vpPOC)
        line.new(bar_index - vpLookback, vpPOC, lineEndX, vpPOC, color = pocColor, width = 2, style = line.style_solid)
        if showVPLabels
            string pocPct = (vpPOC >= close ? '+' : '') + str.tostring(math.round((vpPOC - close) / close * 100, 2)) + '%'
            label.new(vpLblX, vpPOC, '◆ POC   ' + str.tostring(math.round(vpPOC, 2)) + '  (' + pocPct + ')', color = color.new(#1A1400, 0), textcolor = pocColor, style = label.style_label_left, size = size.normal)

    // ── VAH line + label ─────────────────────────────────────
    if showVAH and not na(vpVAH)
        line.new(bar_index - vpLookback, vpVAH, lineEndX, vpVAH, color = color.new(#00E5FF, 0), width = 1, style = line.style_dashed)
````
