Calculation: Prior 10-bar low = lowest low of the 10 completed bars before the current bar on the signal-analysis timeframe
Calculation: Prior 10-bar high = highest high of the 10 completed bars before the current bar on the signal-analysis timeframe
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, the current signal-analysis-timeframe bar has low < Prior 10-bar low AND close > Prior 10-bar low AND (trend filter disabled OR chart-timeframe close > chart-timeframe EMA(200)) AND (session filter disabled OR the chart bar is in an enabled trading session)
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, the current signal-analysis-timeframe bar has high > Prior 10-bar high AND close < Prior 10-bar high AND (trend filter disabled OR chart-timeframe close < chart-timeframe EMA(200)) AND (session filter disabled OR the chart bar is in an enabled trading session)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR (trailing enabled AND high has reached entry price × (1 + activation percentage) AND low <= highest price since activation × (1 − trailing offset percentage))
