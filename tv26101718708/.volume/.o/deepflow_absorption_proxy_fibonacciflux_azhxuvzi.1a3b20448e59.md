Calculation: Volume z-score = (current bar volume − its moving average over a lookback) ÷ population standard deviation of volume over the same lookback; all bars are from the chart timeframe, including the current bar.
Long: Volume z-score ≥ threshold AND abs(close − open) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price tick) ≤ configured maximum body-to-range ratio capped at 1 − minimum wick-to-range ratio AND (min(open, close) − low) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price tick) ≥ minimum wick-to-range ratio AND close > low + 0.25 × max(high − low, minimum price tick) AND (same-price reaction requirement disabled OR current low is within configured ATR distance of the lowest low over the configured lookback of prior completed chart-timeframe bars)
Short: Volume z-score ≥ threshold AND abs(close − open) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price tick) ≤ configured maximum body-to-range ratio capped at 1 − minimum wick-to-range ratio AND (high − max(open, close)) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price tick) ≥ minimum wick-to-range ratio AND close < high − 0.25 × max(high − low, minimum price tick) AND (same-price reaction requirement disabled OR current high is within configured ATR distance of the highest high over the configured lookback of prior completed chart-timeframe bars)
