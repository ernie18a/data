Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the configured daily opening window; use after that window closes
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA above/below slow EMA and current close above/below fast EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar for both EMAs
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low; pivots use equal left and right lookbacks
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity sweep = low below / high above the highest high / lowest low of the preceding lookback bars, then close back above / below that level
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = last bearish / bullish candle within the preceding search window before a bullish / bearish structure break; remains active until close crosses its mitigation level. Price is in the zone when low <= zone high and close >= zone low / high >= zone low and close <= zone high
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier / current high < low two bars earlier. Use the latest gap within its age limit; price is in it when close is between its boundaries
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume above its simple moving average, with a bullish / bearish candle; if enabled, cumulative signed-volume change over a lookback must also be positive / negative
Calculation: Smart trail = ATR-based trailing level; raise it to at least close minus ATR distance when close is above it, lower it to at most close plus ATR distance when close is below it; direction is the latest such upward / downward move
Calculation: Momentum confirmation = RSI above its threshold for long / below its threshold for short, excluding respectively bearish / bullish divergence when divergence filtering is enabled; divergence is price making a higher high / lower low while RSI makes a lower high / higher low at confirmed pivots
Calculation: Confluence score = enabled-condition points: trend 1, structure break 2 (configurable weight), and 1 each for sweep, order block, fair value gap, volume, trail, momentum, and session/day eligibility
Calculation: Long/short initial stop distance = configured choice of ATR distance, distance from close to opening-range midpoint / opposite opening-range boundary, or fixed points; floor at minimum tick. Target 1/2 = entry price plus/minus stop distance times its configured R multiple
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop before target 1; after target 1, entry price adjusted by configured tick offset, optionally tightened by the smart trail
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish liquidity sweep OR (opening range is set AND close > opening-range high [or, in retest mode, a prior breakout is ready, low <= opening-range high plus configured tolerance, and close > opening-range high] AND before cutoff AND score >= configured minimum AND allowed direction includes long AND weekday and session are eligible AND enabled higher-timeframe trend filter passes AND no risk lockout AND flat)
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish liquidity sweep OR (opening range is set AND close < opening-range low [or, in retest mode, a prior breakout is ready, high >= opening-range low minus configured tolerance, and close < opening-range low] AND before cutoff AND score >= configured minimum AND allowed direction includes short AND weekday and session are eligible AND enabled higher-timeframe trend filter passes AND no risk lockout AND flat)
Exit: Long, high >= target 1 OR high >= target 2 OR low <= active stop OR past configured cutoff
Exit: Short, low <= target 1 OR low <= target 2 OR high >= active stop OR past configured cutoff
