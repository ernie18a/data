Calculation: Normalized MACD = (fast moving average − slow moving average) / highest absolute MACD over its lookback × 100; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback
Calculation: Normalized MACD oscillator = MACD histogram / highest absolute MACD histogram over its lookback × 100
Long: Normalized MACD crosses above its signal line AND (normalized MACD is at or below the bullish threshold when the normalized MACD filter is enabled) OR normalized MACD oscillator crosses above zero OR close > simple moving average OR (normalized MACD > its signal line AND normalized MACD > its previous value) OR normalized MACD >= its signal line OR normalized MACD oscillator > 0 OR, on a selected other timeframe's current/forming bar, MACD > its signal line OR MACD histogram > 0 OR close > simple moving average OR a bullish divergence occurred within the recent-bar lookback OR, at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price makes a lower low than at the previous pivot low AND the oscillator makes a higher low AND (the current or previous pivot oscillator is at or below the oversold threshold, when the divergence zone filter is enabled)
Short: Normalized MACD crosses below its signal line AND (normalized MACD is at or above the bearish threshold when the normalized MACD filter is enabled) OR normalized MACD oscillator crosses below zero OR close < simple moving average OR (normalized MACD < its signal line AND normalized MACD < its previous value) OR normalized MACD < its signal line OR normalized MACD oscillator < 0 OR, on a selected other timeframe's current/forming bar, MACD < its signal line OR MACD histogram < 0 OR close < simple moving average OR a bearish divergence occurred within the recent-bar lookback OR, at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price makes a higher high than at the previous pivot high AND the oscillator makes a lower high AND (the current or previous pivot oscillator is at or above the overbought threshold, when the divergence zone filter is enabled)
