Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Order block zone = the last opposite-colored candle at or before the confirmed pivot, searched from the pivot bar through the five preceding bars; use its body or full high-low range as zone boundaries.
Calculation: Order block eligibility = source candle range is at least the configured ATR multiple; if volume filtering is enabled, source volume is at least the configured multiple of its rolling volume average.
Calculation: Retest penetration = for a bearish order block, (highest high since retest began − zone bottom) / zone height; for a bullish order block, (zone top − lowest low since retest began) / zone height.
Calculation: Breaker quality score = rounded weighted average of six 0–100 scores: structure-break displacement in ATR units capped at 2 ATR; source-candle range in ATR units capped at 3 ATR; retest penetration scored linearly from 100 at the configured ideal ratio to 0 at the farther zone-height extreme; confirming-candle rejection-wick fraction of its range; confirmation speed scored as 100 × (1 − bars from retest start / retest window), floored at 0; and distance from source zone to structure-break level in ATR units capped at 5 ATR. Normalize by the sum of the configured weights, or use the unweighted mean if all weights are zero.
Long: A qualifying bearish order block forms at a confirmed swing high, then a close breaks above the last unbroken confirmed swing high (by the configured ATR displacement if enabled), then price retests the zone within the retest window (low ≤ zone top), then before the retest window expires the close is above zone top + confirmation ATR displacement and retest penetration ≥ configured minimum, and quality score ≥ configured minimum if quality filtering is enabled.
Short: A qualifying bullish order block forms at a confirmed swing low, then a close breaks below the last unbroken confirmed swing low (by the configured ATR displacement if enabled), then price retests the zone within the retest window (high ≥ zone bottom), then before the retest window expires the close is below zone bottom − confirmation ATR displacement and retest penetration ≥ configured minimum, and quality score ≥ configured minimum if quality filtering is enabled.
Exit: long, an active bullish breaker exists and close < its zone bottom − invalidation ATR displacement.
Exit: short, an active bearish breaker exists and close > its zone top + invalidation ATR displacement.
