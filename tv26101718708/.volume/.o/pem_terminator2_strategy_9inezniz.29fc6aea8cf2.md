Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish fire = after a bar whose direction is bullish or bearish and whose range exceeds ATR × impulse threshold, wait for a bar whose range is below ATR × compression threshold; then count such small-range bars and track their highest high and lowest low. Once the count reaches the minimum stall count, fire bullish if close exceeds the tracked high, or bearish if close falls below the tracked low, provided body fraction meets the minimum. Reset after a fire or when the small-range bar count exceeds 15. A bullish or bearish fire is confirmed only on the immediately following bar if that bar closes up or down, respectively.
Long: bullish fire confirmed AND close > long-period EMA AND close > session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3 AND position is flat
Short: bearish fire confirmed AND close < long-period EMA AND close < session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3 AND position is flat
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + first target (2 contracts) OR high ≥ entry price + second target (2 contracts) OR low ≤ runner trailing stop (2 contracts; activates after price rises by the trailing distance, then trails the highest high since activation by that distance) OR (stop loss enabled AND low ≤ entry price − stop distance)
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − first target (2 contracts) OR low ≤ entry price − second target (2 contracts) OR high ≥ runner trailing stop (2 contracts; activates after price falls by the trailing distance, then trails the lowest low since activation by that distance) OR (stop loss enabled AND high ≥ entry price + stop distance)
