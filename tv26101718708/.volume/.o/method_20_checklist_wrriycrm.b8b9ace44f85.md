Calculation: Hammer = lower wick ≥ 2 × candle body AND upper wick ≤ 0.5 × candle body
Calculation: Marubozu = candle body ≥ 0.85 × (high − low)
Long: close > open OR Hammer OR (Marubozu AND close > open) OR CCI(20) ≤ −100 OR low > previous bar high OR (close > open AND volume > previous bar volume)
Short: close < open OR (Marubozu AND close < open) OR CCI(20) ≥ 100 OR high < previous bar low OR (close < open AND volume > previous bar volume)
