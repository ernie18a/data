Calculation: Moving average = configured EMA, SMA, or HMA of chart-timeframe close
Calculation: 15-minute EMA = EMA of 15-minute close; use the latest available 15-minute bar, including its still-forming value in realtime
Calculation: 15-minute EMA slope angle = arctangent of ((current 15-minute EMA − 15-minute EMA from N bars earlier) / earlier EMA × 100 / N), converted to degrees; N is the configured lookback in 15-minute bars
Calculation: Relative volume = chart-timeframe volume / its longer-lookback simple moving average
Calculation: ATR percentage = chart-timeframe ATR / close × 100
Calculation: Filters active = forced on, or (not forced off and adaptive filters enabled and ATR percentage below the configured volatility cutoff), or (not forced off and adaptive filters disabled)
Calculation: Setup = begins when close crosses either above or below the moving average; remains active until an entry signal. While active and before retrace, record the highest high and lowest low since setup began as swing high and swing low, and track the highest high and lowest low over that same impulse period.
Calculation: Retrace = setup is active, at least five adverse candles have closed (close below open while close is above the moving average, or close above open while close is below it), and after that count is reached, a bar has both an absolute close-to-moving-average distance of at least 30% of the impulse range and, when filters are active, a distance from the moving average below the configured threshold. Once reached, retrace remains true until entry.
Calculation: Long stop = swing low; short stop = swing high
Calculation: Target = entry price plus (entry price − long stop) times the configured reward-to-risk multiple for a long, or entry price minus (short stop − entry price) times that multiple for a short
Long: setup is active AND retrace is true AND close > swing high AND (filters are inactive OR relative volume ≥ configured threshold) AND (15-minute trend filter is disabled OR close > 15-minute EMA) AND (15-minute EMA distance filter is disabled OR absolute distance from close to 15-minute EMA / 15-minute EMA < configured threshold) AND (slope filter is disabled OR 15-minute EMA slope angle ≥ configured minimum)
Short: setup is active AND retrace is true AND close < swing low AND (filters are inactive OR relative volume ≥ configured threshold) AND (15-minute trend filter is disabled OR close < 15-minute EMA) AND (15-minute EMA distance filter is disabled OR absolute distance from close to 15-minute EMA / 15-minute EMA < configured threshold) AND (slope filter is disabled OR 15-minute EMA slope angle ≤ negative configured minimum)
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ target
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ target
