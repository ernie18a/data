Calculation: Crossframe score = clamp(40 × (primary-timeframe fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart-timeframe ATR + 45 × (secondary-timeframe fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart-timeframe ATR + 12 × (chart-timeframe fast EMA − slow EMA) / chart-timeframe ATR, −100, 100); both higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed bar; fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback
Calculation: Auction value area = on the current chart-timeframe bar and preceding lookback bars, allocate each bar’s volume across price bins in proportion to its high-low overlap; POC is the bin with greatest allocated volume; expand from the POC toward the higher-volume adjacent bin until the configured share of total volume is covered
Calculation: Auction score = clamp(55 × (short-lookback SMA(volume) / long-lookback SMA(volume) − 1) + 30 × (EMA(close, 34) − EMA(close, 89)) / chart-timeframe ATR + 22 × (close − auction POC) / chart-timeframe ATR, −100, 100); short volume lookback is shorter than long volume lookback
Calculation: Constructive auction location = close above value-area high OR (close within value area AND close above POC); defensive auction location = close below value-area low OR (close within value area AND close below POC)
Calculation: Stepped midpoint = initialize to SMA(close, basis lookback); on each bar, reset to that SMA if close is above the prior midpoint plus ATR × corridor multiplier or below the prior midpoint minus ATR × corridor multiplier, otherwise retain the prior midpoint; slope is current midpoint minus prior midpoint
Calculation: Structure long condition = close above stepped midpoint AND midpoint slope is nonnegative AND (no confirmed pivot high exists OR close above the latest confirmed pivot high); structure short condition = close below stepped midpoint AND midpoint slope is nonpositive AND (no confirmed pivot low exists OR close below the latest confirmed pivot low); pivot highs and lows use configured left and right bar counts
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(1.2 × (wave − wave signal) + RSI − smoothed RSI + (RSI − 50) / 10 + 8 × (EMA(close, 34) − EMA(close, 89)) / chart-timeframe ATR, −100, 100); wave = EMA of the channel index, where channel index = (hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel lookback)), channel lookback)); wave signal is an SMA of wave; momentum signal is an EMA of momentum score
Calculation: Active absorption range = on volume above its baseline SMA times a trigger multiplier, start or restart a range if none exists, it has expired, or close is outside its bounds; otherwise extend bounds to include the bar and update range equilibrium as the equal-weight average of prior equilibrium and current hlc3, and bullish balance as the equal-weight average of prior balance and 0.65 for a bullish bar or 0.35 otherwise; expire after the configured lifetime
Calculation: Absorption long condition = no active range OR (close at or above range equilibrium AND bullish balance at or above 0.50); absorption short condition = no active range OR (close at or below range equilibrium AND bullish balance at or below 0.50)
Calculation: ATR percentile = percentage of values in the configured current-and-prior-bar ATR lookback that are less than or equal to current ATR; directional imbalance = (EMA of bar range on bullish bars, zero otherwise − EMA of bar range on bearish bars, zero otherwise) / ATR; volatility score = clamp(ATR percentile − 50 + 20 × directional imbalance, −100, 100)
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the greater of ATR × stop multiplier or, when structural anchoring is enabled and an absorption range is active, entry price minus range bottom; short stop = entry price plus the greater of ATR × stop multiplier or, when structural anchoring is enabled and an absorption range is active, range top minus entry price; enforce minimum risk of five ticks. Targets are entry price plus/minus risk times the configured first and second target multiples
Long: confirmed bar AND crossframe score > threshold AND auction score > 10 AND constructive auction location AND structure long condition AND absorption long condition AND ATR percentile within configured minimum and maximum AND volatility score > −15 AND momentum score crosses above momentum signal AND momentum score > threshold AND position side is flat or short
Short: confirmed bar AND crossframe score < −threshold AND auction score < −10 AND defensive auction location AND structure short condition AND absorption short condition AND ATR percentile within configured minimum and maximum AND volatility score < 15 AND momentum score crosses below momentum signal AND momentum score < −threshold AND position side is flat or long
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= first target OR high >= second target OR crossframe score < 0 OR momentum score < 0 OR auction score < 0 OR close < stepped midpoint
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= first target OR low <= second target OR crossframe score > 0 OR momentum score > 0 OR auction score > 0 OR close > stepped midpoint
