Calculation: Bars since prior signal = current bar index − bar index of the most recent long or short signal
Long: Shorter-lookback EMA crosses above longer-lookback EMA AND volume exceeds a threshold multiple of its lookback-period average AND RSI is above its lower bound and below its upper bound AND bars since prior signal exceeds the cooldown interval OR the last completed lower-timeframe close is above its EMA OR the current chart-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA OR the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its longer-lookback EMA
Short: Shorter-lookback EMA crosses below longer-lookback EMA AND volume exceeds a threshold multiple of its lookback-period average AND RSI is below its upper bound and above its lower bound AND bars since prior signal exceeds the cooldown interval OR the last completed lower-timeframe close is at or below its EMA OR the current chart-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA is at or below its longer-lookback EMA OR the last completed higher-timeframe close is at or below its longer-lookback EMA
