Calculation: Previous-bar highest high = highest high over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Previous-bar lowest low = lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Trend Surfers trailing stop line = while its internal state is long, the greater of the prior long stop and current close minus current ATR times its multiplier; while short, the lesser of the prior short stop and current close plus current ATR times its multiplier. Its state starts long when close crosses above the previous-bar highest high, starts short when close crosses below the previous-bar lowest low, and resets when an opposite breakout occurs or price reaches the corresponding fixed or trailing stop. The fixed stop is the state-entry close minus (long) or plus (short) ATR times its multiplier.
Calculation: Smoothed +DI and −DI = apply this smoothing to each directional indicator: for input x and smoothing length n, let e1=EMA(x,n), e2=EMA(e1,n), v1=1.5×e1−0.5×e2; e3=EMA(v1,n), e4=EMA(e3,n), v2=1.5×e3−0.5×e4; e5=EMA(v2,n), e6=EMA(e5,n), output=1.5×e5−0.5×e6. Before smoothing, +DI is Wilder-RMA of positive upward directional movement divided by Wilder-RMA true range; −DI is Wilder-RMA of positive downward directional movement divided by Wilder-RMA true range.
Long: Trend Surfers trailing stop line < previous-bar lowest low AND close > prior close AND smoothed +DI > smoothed −DI AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > configured threshold) AND not already in a long position
Short: Trend Surfers trailing stop line > previous-bar highest high AND close < prior close AND smoothed +DI < smoothed −DI AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > configured threshold) AND not already in a short position
Exit: long, close < entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + any configured take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, close > entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − any configured take-profit percentage)
