Calculation: HLC3 = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: EMA = longer-period exponential moving average of HLC3
Calculation: SMA = shorter-period simple moving average of HLC3
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of close since the current session began
Calculation: Candle body-to-range ratio = abs(close - open) / (high - low)
Calculation: Candle range percentage = (high - low) / low × 100
Long: At least the minimum delay has elapsed since the most recent bar where candle body-to-range ratio meets its minimum and candle range percentage meets its minimum, and either the bar's high-low range intersects both EMA and session VWAP or EMA crosses above SMA
