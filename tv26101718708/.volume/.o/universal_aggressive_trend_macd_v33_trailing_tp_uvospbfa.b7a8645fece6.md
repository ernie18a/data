Long: Flat AND at least the cooldown lookback bars since the previous entry (or no previous entry) AND ADX > threshold AND volume > its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier AND close > the long-term EMA AND that EMA > its value the slope lookback bars ago AND ((a bullish MACD crossover occurred within the validity lookback and MACD line > signal line) OR (MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND close > the highest high of the preceding continuation lookback bars))
Short: Flat AND at least the cooldown lookback bars since the previous entry (or no previous entry) AND ADX > threshold AND volume > its longer-lookback SMA × volume multiplier AND close < the long-term EMA AND that EMA < its value the slope lookback bars ago AND ((a bearish MACD crossunder occurred within the validity lookback and MACD line < signal line) OR (MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND close < the lowest low of the preceding continuation lookback bars))
Calculation: Peak profit = highest close-based open profit percentage since entry; for long, (close − entry price) / entry price × 100; for short, (entry price − close) / entry price × 100
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR (peak profit >= minimum profit AND current close-based profit percentage <= the trailing level) OR a bearish MACD crossunder
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR (peak profit >= minimum profit AND current close-based profit percentage <= the trailing level) OR a bullish MACD crossover
Calculation: Trailing level = peak profit × (1 − giveback percentage / 100) in relative mode, or peak profit − giveback percentage points in points mode
