Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = any enabled pattern: (lower wick ≥ body × wick/body ratio AND upper wick ≤ body × opposite-wick/body limit AND close location ≥ 0.55); (bullish candle AND previous candle bearish AND open ≤ previous close AND close ≥ previous open); (lower wick ≥ range × 0.55 AND close location ≥ 0.60 AND body/range ≤ 0.35); (high > previous high AND low < previous low AND bullish candle AND close > range midpoint); or (two bars earlier bearish AND previous body/range ≤ 0.35 AND current candle bullish AND close ≥ midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body). A pattern is enabled only when its corresponding pattern option is on.
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = any enabled pattern: (upper wick ≥ body × wick/body ratio AND lower wick ≤ body × opposite-wick/body limit AND close location ≤ 0.45); (bearish candle AND previous candle bullish AND open ≥ previous close AND close ≤ previous open); (upper wick ≥ range × 0.55 AND close location ≤ 0.40 AND body/range ≤ 0.35); (high > previous high AND low < previous low AND bearish candle AND close < range midpoint); or (two bars earlier bullish AND previous body/range ≤ 0.35 AND current candle bearish AND close ≤ midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body). A pattern is enabled only when its corresponding pattern option is on.
Calculation: Candle range = high − low (floored at one minimum price tick); body = |close − open|; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; close location = (close − low)/range; body/range = body/range.
Calculation: Pattern quality = clamp(45 × dominant wick/range + 35 × directional close quality + 20 × (1 − body/range), 0, 100). For bullish patterns, dominant wick is lower wick and directional close quality is close location; for bearish patterns, dominant wick is upper wick and directional close quality is 1 − close location.
Calculation: Key-level distance = minimum absolute distance from the candidate low (bullish) or high (bearish) to enabled same-side references: previous-day low/high, current-session low/high, most recent confirmed swing low/high, session VWAP, nearest round number, and enabled manual levels. Previous day uses the last completed daily bar; current-session extrema use the developing session; confirmed swings use pivots only after their right-side bars confirm. Key-level proximity means this distance ≤ ATR × key-level proximity parameter.
Calculation: Adjusted setup quality = clamp(pattern quality × (1 − away-from-key-level penalty/100, if no enabled key level is near; otherwise pattern quality) + max(number of enabled levels within key-level proximity − 1, 0) × confluence bonus, 0, 100).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA; otherwise neutral. Use the latest requested-timeframe values with lookahead disabled; fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback.
Long: bullish reversal pattern AND adjusted setup quality ≥ minimum pattern quality AND no active candidate (or the prior candidate expired or bullish price closed above its high) AND (confirmed bar if confirmed-bars-only is enabled) AND (high-risk-only is disabled OR trap risk ≥ probable-trap threshold).
Short: bearish reversal pattern AND adjusted setup quality ≥ minimum pattern quality AND no active candidate (or the prior candidate expired or bearish price closed below its low) AND (confirmed bar if confirmed-bars-only is enabled) AND (high-risk-only is disabled OR trap risk ≥ probable-trap threshold).
