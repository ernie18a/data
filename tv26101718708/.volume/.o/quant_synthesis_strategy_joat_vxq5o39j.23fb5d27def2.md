Calculation: Trend regime = ADX is at least its threshold AND absolute fast-EMA minus slow-EMA spread, divided by ATR, is at least its threshold
Calculation: Balance regime = ADX is below its threshold OR ATR divided by close is below its moving average by the configured factor
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > its value on the prior higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish bias = higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < its value on the prior higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Bullish structure = the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows are available, and each latest pivot is higher than its predecessor
Calculation: Bearish structure = the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows are available, and each latest pivot is lower than its predecessor
Calculation: Trend momentum = clamp(16 × normalized EMA spread + 8 × normalized 20-bar linear-regression slope + 5 × 5-bar EMA of normalized EMA spread plus normalized slope + normalized EMA spread signal + 6 × divergence bias, −25, 25); normalized values are divided by ATR; the signal is the EMA of fast EMA minus slow EMA; divergence bias is +1 when a confirmed pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low while its oscillator value is higher, −1 for the inverse at confirmed pivot highs, and 0 otherwise. Use fast and slow EMAs with the configured shorter and longer lengths, respectively.
Calculation: Volume surprise = current volume's z-score against its moving average and standard deviation exceeds its configured threshold
Calculation: Directional volume = volume × (close − open), divided by the moving average of volume × absolute close − open
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(8 × volume z-score + 10 × directional volume, −15, 15)
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the configured first bars of the current session; use the range through the current bar once those bars are complete
Calculation: Session long context = opening range is ready AND close > opening-range high AND close > opening-range midpoint
Calculation: Session short context = opening range is ready AND close < opening-range low AND close < opening-range midpoint
Calculation: Long confluence score = regime points (trend regime with positive normalized EMA spread, or expansion regime with positive normalized EMA spread) + bias points (bullish higher-timeframe bias when enabled, otherwise default points; plus bullish structure points) + positive trend momentum capped at its configured maximum + positive volume score capped at its configured maximum when volume surprise and close > open + session points when close > session open and opening-range midpoint, or when session long context and close breaks above the prior configured lookback highs
Calculation: Short confluence score = regime points (trend regime with negative normalized EMA spread, or expansion regime with negative normalized EMA spread) + bias points (bearish higher-timeframe bias when enabled, otherwise default points; plus bearish structure points) + absolute negative trend momentum capped at its configured maximum + absolute volume score capped at its configured maximum when volume surprise and close < open + session points when close < session open and opening-range midpoint, or when session short context and close breaks below the prior configured lookback lows
Long: bar is confirmed AND long confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND long confluence score > short confluence score AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND cooldown has elapsed AND session entry limit has not been reached AND position is flat or short AND (session filter disabled OR session long context)
Short: bar is confirmed AND short confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND short confluence score > long confluence score AND trend regime AND NOT balance regime AND cooldown has elapsed AND session entry limit has not been reached AND position is flat or long AND (session filter disabled OR session short context)
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum OR (bar is confirmed AND (bearish structure OR bearish higher-timeframe bias OR balance regime)) OR session ends
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum OR (bar is confirmed AND (bullish structure OR bullish higher-timeframe bias OR balance regime)) OR session ends
