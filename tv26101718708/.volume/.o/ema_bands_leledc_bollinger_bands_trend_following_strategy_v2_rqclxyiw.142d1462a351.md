Calculation: Middle bands = EMA(high, base length) and EMA(low, base length), each offset from EMA(close, same base length) by its configured multiplier.
Calculation: Inner/outer zones = 8-bar WMA of the configured zone levels: either EMA(close) offset by the corresponding middle-band EMA offset times the configured inner/outer factor, or EMA(close) ± the configured factor times RMA(high − low, base length) when Keltner zones are selected.
Calculation: Leledc events = bearish when close < open, close > close N bars ago on more than the configured qualification count of accumulated bars, and high is the highest over the configured lookback; bullish uses close > open, close < close N bars ago, and low is the lowest. Reset the corresponding count on its event; suppress events unless volume exceeds its 100-bar SMA times the configured volume factor when the volume filter is enabled.
Calculation: BB exhaustion events = bearish when prior source price > prior upper band, current source price < current upper band, and RSI > configured overbought threshold; bullish when prior source price < prior lower band, current source price > current lower band, and RSI < configured oversold threshold. Bands are SMA(source, configured length) ± configured standard deviations × standard deviation(source, length); RSI uses its configured length.
Calculation: Band cross = close finishes beyond a band and either open is on the other side or prior close was on the other side of the prior band.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger bands (SMA and standard deviation) lie inside the Keltner channel (EMA of close ± configured multiple × EMA of high − low); use configured lookbacks and current-timeframe bars or the configured other timeframe's last completed bars.
Long: close > top middle band AND (open < top middle band OR (open > top middle band AND prior close ≤ prior top middle band)) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close > slow EMA); OR flat AND open > top middle band AND close > top middle band AND low < top middle band AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close > slow EMA); OR flat AND (bullish Leledc event AND low < selected inner/outer buy zone OR bullish BB exhaustion event) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing); OR short position AND (upward cross of top middle band OR configured short exit condition) AND (for a Leledc-triggered counter-trend entry, bullish Leledc event and low < selected buy zone; for a BB-triggered entry, bullish BB exhaustion event and open < selected buy zone).
Short: close < bottom middle band AND (open > bottom middle band OR (open < bottom middle band AND prior close ≥ prior bottom middle band)) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close < slow EMA); OR flat AND open < bottom middle band AND close < bottom middle band AND high > bottom middle band AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing) AND (slow EMA filter disabled OR close < slow EMA); OR flat AND (bearish Leledc event AND high > selected inner/outer sell zone OR bearish BB exhaustion event) AND (squeeze filter disabled OR not squeezing); OR long position AND (downward cross of bottom middle band OR configured long exit condition) AND (for a Leledc-triggered counter-trend entry, bearish Leledc event and high > selected sell zone; for a BB-triggered entry, bearish BB exhaustion event and open > selected sell zone).
Exit: long, downward cross of top middle band in nearest-band mode; downward cross of bottom middle band in opposite-band or delayed-inside mode; or, in delayed-inside mode, after the configured number of bars with no middle-band breakout, the current open and close are inside the middle bands and the bar that many bars ago opened above and closed below the top band; OR bearish event number selected since entry (Leledc or BB exhaustion, when enabled); OR downward cross back through the selected inner/outer buy-zone level; OR counter-trend long and selected close condition (upward band cross, bearish Leledc, bearish BB exhaustion, or price reaching selected target) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage).
Exit: short, upward cross of bottom middle band in nearest-band mode; upward cross of top middle band in opposite-band or delayed-inside mode; or, in delayed-inside mode, after the configured number of bars with no middle-band breakout, the current open and close are inside the middle bands and the bar that many bars ago opened below and closed above the bottom band; OR bullish event number selected since entry (Leledc or BB exhaustion, when enabled); OR upward cross back through the selected inner/outer sell-zone level; OR counter-trend short and selected close condition (downward band cross, bullish Leledc, bullish BB exhaustion, or price reaching selected target) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss percentage).
