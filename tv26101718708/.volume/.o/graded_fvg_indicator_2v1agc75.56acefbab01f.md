Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high[2]; zone = [high[2], current low]. Bearish FVG = current high < low[2]; zone = [current high, low[2]]. Evaluate on confirmed bars.
Calculation: FVG grade = floor(score) / 10, where score is the weighted 0–100 sum of displacement (30%), structure (25%), quality-scaled relative volume (15%), gap size (15%), and context (15%). Measure sizes with ATR from bar[2]. Relative volume = volume[1] / SMA(volume, 20)[2]; when volume is unavailable or ignored, score = 100 × weighted average of the other four factors. Structure rewards a break of the latest intact swing or the 20-bar range ending at bar[2], with a tight-range bonus; absent a break, it rewards alignment with the last swing-break direction. Context rewards low overlap with the preceding 50 bars and a gap midpoint near or beyond that range's edge. Displacement, size, and context factor formulas use the stated linear ramp and gap-size scoring rules.
Calculation: Eligible grade = displayed tier after gates: HIGH requires grade at least the HIGH threshold, displacement sub-grade at least its gate, and gap size at least its gate; failing one gate caps the tier at MEDIUM, failing both caps it at LOW. Displayed tier is HIGH by default; display mode may include MEDIUM or LOW.
Long: Bullish FVG is created and its eligible grade meets the displayed tier; OR a bearish FVG closes through and flips to a bullish IFVG that meets the displayed tier.
Short: Bearish FVG is created and its eligible grade meets the displayed tier; OR a bullish FVG closes through and flips to a bearish IFVG that meets the displayed tier.
Calculation: IFVG grade = original FVG score × (0.70 + 0.30 × ramp(breaking candle body in break direction / ATR, 0.3, 1.5)). A close-through qualifies to flip when the breaking candle forms an opposite-direction FVG, has body at least the configured ATR multiple, or any close-through is allowed, according to the selected break requirement.
