Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over the swing lookback, confirmed after that many bars on the right; retain the latest confirmed value. For structure breaks, compare close with that retained value from the swing-lookback bars earlier.
Calculation: RSI = standard RSI of close over its lookback.
Calculation: Average body = simple moving average of |close − open| over 14 bars.
Calculation: Projected high-pivot line = latest confirmed pivot high + slope × (current bar index − pivot bar index), where slope = (latest pivot high − its value on the prior bar) / max(current bar index − pivot bar index, 1). Projected low-pivot line is calculated the same way from pivot lows.
Long: count of these true conditions ≥ minimum required signals: close crosses above the retained swing low value from the swing-lookback bars earlier; low ≤ lowest low over the divergence lookback AND RSI > prior bar's lowest RSI over that lookback; volume > its simple moving average × volume multiplier; (lower wick ≥ 2 × body AND upper wick ≤ 0.5 × body AND close > open) OR (prior close < prior open AND close > open AND close ≥ prior open AND open ≤ prior close) OR (close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND prior body < prior average body × 0.3 AND close > open AND close > midpoint of the candle two bars ago); close crosses above the projected high-pivot line.
Short: count of these true conditions ≥ minimum required signals: close crosses below the retained swing high value from the swing-lookback bars earlier; high ≥ highest high over the divergence lookback AND RSI < prior bar's highest RSI over that lookback; volume > its simple moving average × volume multiplier; (upper wick ≥ 2 × body AND lower wick ≤ 0.5 × body AND close < open) OR (prior close > prior open AND close < open AND close ≤ prior open AND open ≥ prior close) OR (close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND prior body < prior average body × 0.3 AND close < open AND close < midpoint of the candle two bars ago); close crosses below the projected low-pivot line.
