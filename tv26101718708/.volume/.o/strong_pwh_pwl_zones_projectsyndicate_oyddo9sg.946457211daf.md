Calculation: Prior-period high and low = the maximum high and minimum low of the last completed anchor period
Calculation: Sweep trap lines = prior-period high plus AWR-based offset; prior-period low minus the same AWR-based offset
Calculation: Acceptance lines = prior-period high plus a larger AWR-based offset; prior-period low minus the same larger AWR-based offset
Long: low ≤ lower sweep trap line AND close > prior-period low
Long: close < lower acceptance line AND position is not already short
Long: close > prior-period low AND the lower level was previously accepted below and is now a failed break
Long: close > upper acceptance line AND volume is expanding AND the upper level is weak or moderate, or prior-period volume is low
Short: high ≥ upper sweep trap line AND close < prior-period high
Short: close > upper acceptance line AND position is not already long
Short: close < prior-period high AND the upper level was previously accepted above and is now a failed break
Short: close < lower acceptance line AND volume is expanding AND the lower level is weak or moderate, or prior-period volume is low
Exit: long, close < lower Golden Pocket boundary OR close < lower acceptance line
Exit: short, close > upper Golden Pocket boundary OR close > upper acceptance line
