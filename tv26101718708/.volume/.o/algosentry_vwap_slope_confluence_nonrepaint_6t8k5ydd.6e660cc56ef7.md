Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 since the selected session, week, or month anchor, using backtest bars.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus its volume-weighted standard deviation times a multiplier.
Calculation: Directional slope confirmation for a series on a timeframe = sign of the ATR-normalized change in its linear-regression value over a slope lookback, provided enough votes pass: absolute normalized-slope z-score exceeds its rolling gate; Kaufman efficiency ratio is at least its rolling average times an ease factor when enabled; and rolling R-squared is at least its rolling average times the ease factor when enabled. The z-score uses the rolling mean and standard deviation of absolute normalized slope; its gate is (0.25 × rolling mean of z-score + 0.10 × rolling standard deviation of z-score) × ease factor. Compute with that timeframe's bars. For lower-timeframe confirmation, count qualifying intrabars within the chart bar; require at least the configured minimum count in the same direction. Use distinct parameters for chart and lower-timeframe calculations.
Long: fast EMA crosses above anchored VWAP OR the most recent fast-EMA/VWAP cross was upward AND the bar opens above VWAP AND its low touches VWAP or the upper deviation band AND it closes above VWAP AND volume exceeds its rolling average times a multiplier AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous retest OR after an upward retest, both VWAP and EMA(45) first have upward chart-timeframe slope confirmation and matching upward lower-timeframe confirmation.
Short: fast EMA crosses below anchored VWAP OR the most recent fast-EMA/VWAP cross was downward AND the bar opens below VWAP AND its high touches VWAP or the lower deviation band AND it closes below VWAP AND volume exceeds its rolling average times a multiplier AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the previous retest OR after a downward retest, both VWAP and EMA(45) first have downward chart-timeframe slope confirmation and matching downward lower-timeframe confirmation.
Exit: long, fast EMA crosses below anchored VWAP after an upward locked signal.
Exit: short, fast EMA crosses above anchored VWAP after a downward locked signal.
