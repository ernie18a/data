Calculation: Latest swing high = most recent high from a bar that equals the highest high in a window extending an equal lookback before and after that bar
Calculation: Latest swing low = most recent low from a bar that equals the lowest low in a window extending an equal lookback before and after that bar
Long: close crosses above latest swing high AND close > EMA(close) AND RSI(close) > 50 AND OBV > EMA(OBV)
Short: close crosses below latest swing low AND close < EMA(close) AND RSI(close) < 50 AND OBV < EMA(OBV)
