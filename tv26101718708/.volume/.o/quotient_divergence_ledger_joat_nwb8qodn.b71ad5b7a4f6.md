Calculation: Signed participation wave = EMA of [65 × (EMA(buy volume, long lookback) − EMA(sell volume, long lookback)) / EMA(max(volume, 1), long lookback) + 22 × EMA(volume × close position, long lookback) / EMA(max(volume, 1), long lookback) + 13 × EMA(volume × body pressure, long lookback) / EMA(max(volume, 1), long lookback)], using the shorter smoothing lookback; close position = 2 × ((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) − 0.5), body pressure = (close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), buy volume = volume when close > open, otherwise 0.35 × volume, and sell volume = volume when close < open, otherwise 0.35 × volume.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot high/low = local maximum/minimum of the signed participation wave, confirmed after the same configurable lookback on each side; compare each confirmed pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, using the price high/low at each pivot.
Long: At a confirmed oscillator pivot low, (price low < preceding pivot low AND oscillator pivot > preceding oscillator pivot) OR (price low > preceding pivot low AND oscillator pivot < preceding oscillator pivot), AND oscillator pivot displacement ≥ minimum oscillator move AND absolute price displacement / ATR ≥ minimum price move AND pivot gap ≥ minimum gap.
Short: At a confirmed oscillator pivot high, (price high > preceding pivot high AND oscillator pivot < preceding oscillator pivot) OR (price high < preceding pivot high AND oscillator pivot > preceding oscillator pivot), AND oscillator pivot displacement ≥ minimum oscillator move AND absolute price displacement / ATR ≥ minimum price move AND pivot gap ≥ minimum gap.
