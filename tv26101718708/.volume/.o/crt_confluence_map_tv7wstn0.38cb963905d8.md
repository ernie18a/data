Calculation: 8 AM NY range = high and low of bars in the 8 AM NY range-setting session
Calculation: Completed 4H range = high and low of the last completed 4-hour bar
Calculation: Previous trading-day range = high and low of the last completed trading day
Calculation: Opening range = high and low of bars in the selected opening interval
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars before and after it
Calculation: Estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume if high = low; estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume; pressure delta = estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume
