Calculation: Directional efficiency = the net close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback, signed by direction, smoothed with an EMA, then scaled by directional persistence.
Calculation: Adaptive momentum = RSI centered at zero and volatility-scaled; use a shorter RSI period at higher volatility and a longer period at lower volatility.
Calculation: Volatility regime = a weighted, normalized composite of ATR-to-close percentile, short-term ATR relative to longer-term ATR, and Bollinger Band width percentile.
Calculation: Flow proxy = a smoothed weighted composite of close position within the bar range, price change weighted by relative volume, and a contra-directional adjustment for high-volume small-body bars.
Calculation: Flux oscillator = the weighted sum of directional efficiency, adaptive momentum, volatility regime, and flow proxy, with weights adapting to trend, coil, or range conditions; blend with a simplified higher-timeframe oscillator when enabled. The higher-timeframe oscillator uses directional efficiency, fixed-period RSI momentum, short-versus-long ATR volatility momentum, and close position within range. Use the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming and the last completed higher-timeframe bar otherwise.
Calculation: Adjusted flux = flux oscillator multiplied by a volume factor based on current volume relative to its moving average, then divided by the flux scale.
Calculation: Flux moving average = EMA of adjusted flux.
Calculation: Flux cloud = three EMAs of adjusted flux with fast period shorter than medium, and medium shorter than slow.
Calculation: Panel higher-timeframe flux = an equal-weight composite of directional efficiency, fixed-period RSI momentum, short-versus-long ATR volatility momentum, and smoothed close position within range, calculated on each selected higher timeframe. Use the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming and the last completed higher-timeframe bar otherwise.
Long: adjusted flux > flux moving average OR flux oscillator > 0 OR fast flux EMA > medium flux EMA AND medium flux EMA > slow flux EMA OR not coil AND directional efficiency > its positive trend threshold AND adaptive momentum > 0 AND (volatility regime is expanding OR above its lower trend bound) OR bullish regular divergence OR bullish hidden divergence OR panel higher-timeframe flux > its bullish threshold
Short: adjusted flux < flux moving average OR flux oscillator < 0 OR fast flux EMA < medium flux EMA AND medium flux EMA < slow flux EMA OR not coil AND directional efficiency < its negative trend threshold AND adaptive momentum < 0 AND (volatility regime is expanding OR above its lower trend bound) OR bearish regular divergence OR bearish hidden divergence OR panel higher-timeframe flux < its bearish threshold
