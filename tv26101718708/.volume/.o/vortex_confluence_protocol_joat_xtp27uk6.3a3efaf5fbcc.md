Calculation: Structure direction = bullish after a close above the latest confirmed swing high while direction is non-bullish, or above the previous swing high while direction is bearish; bearish after a close below the latest confirmed swing low while direction is non-bearish, or below the previous swing low while direction is bullish; otherwise retain the prior direction. Swing highs and lows are confirmed with equal left and right pivot-strength lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest swing high while direction is non-bullish, or above the previous swing high while bearish / close below the latest swing low while direction is non-bearish, or below the previous swing low while bullish, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low above the high two bars earlier / current high below the low two bars earlier, respectively, with the middle bar bullish / bearish and gap size at least 0.3 ATR. Price is in a gap when the current bar's range overlaps an active gap of that direction.
Calculation: Smoothed RSI momentum = 3-period EMA of RSI; bullish when above 50, rising over three bars, and below the overbought level; bearish when below 50, falling over three bars, and above the oversold level.
Calculation: Volume delta = estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume; for a bullish candle buy volume is volume and for other candles it is volume times (close−low)/(high−low+0.0001); for a bearish candle sell volume is volume and for other candles it is volume times (high−close)/(high−low+0.0001). Cumulative delta is the sum over 10 bars. Volume direction is bullish/bearish when volume is at least its moving average times a threshold and both delta and cumulative delta are positive/negative, respectively; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = bullish when higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and RSI is above 50; bearish when close is below its EMA and RSI is below 50; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the EMA and RSI use their respective chart-timeframe lengths.
Calculation: Liquidity level = high when absolute volume delta / volume exceeds 0.7, medium when above 0.5, low when above 0.3, otherwise none. Liquidity sweep = absolute volume delta / volume moving average above 2.
Calculation: Smart-money score = 10 times the signed absolute institutional delta / volume moving average, where institutional delta is volume delta times relative volume; the sign is positive for accumulation (close change over five bars and volume change over five bars positive, and institutional delta positive), negative for distribution (both changes negative, and institutional delta negative), and zero otherwise. Multiply by 1.5 when absolute volume Z-score exceeds 2; clamp to [−100, 100].
Calculation: Quantum superposition = (coherence + entanglement)/2; coherence is the absolute value of (structure direction + momentum direction + volume direction + higher-timeframe direction)/4; entanglement is +0.5 when higher-timeframe direction equals structure direction, otherwise −0.5, plus +0.5 when higher-timeframe direction equals momentum direction, otherwise −0.5.
Calculation: Confluence score = structure points (1 for a structure break when required, otherwise 1 for non-neutral structure; add 1 for price in a direction-aligned gap when required) + momentum point (1 for non-neutral momentum when alignment is required, otherwise 1) + volume points (1 for non-neutral volume direction when filtered, plus 1 when absolute volume Z-score exceeds 2; otherwise 1) + higher-timeframe point (1 for non-neutral direction when filtered, otherwise 1) + session point (1 in active session when filtered, otherwise 1) + liquidity points (1 for medium-or-higher level and 1 for a sweep when filtered, otherwise 1) + quantum points (1 when superposition exceeds its collapse threshold and 1 when coherence exceeds 0.8, when filtered; otherwise 1) + smart-money points (1 when score exceeds 50 and 1 for accumulation or distribution activity when filtered, otherwise 1) + harmonic point (1 when the periodic phase sine multiplied by structure direction exceeds 0.5, when enabled; otherwise 1).
Calculation: Long initial stop = the lower of close minus ATR times the stop multiplier and the lowest low over twice the pivot-strength lookback minus 0.2 ATR. Short initial stop = the higher of close plus ATR times the stop multiplier and the highest high over twice the pivot-strength lookback plus 0.2 ATR. Target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the absolute entry-to-initial-stop distance times the risk-reward parameter.
