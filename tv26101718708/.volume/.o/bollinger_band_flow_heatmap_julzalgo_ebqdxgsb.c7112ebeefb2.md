Calculation: Pressure imbalance = sum of absolute accumulated SmartFlow in Bollinger channel layers whose midpoint is below the current close minus the sum for layers at or above the close. For each scanned chart-timeframe bar, compute SmartFlow as follows: let range=max(true range, minimum tick), body=close−open, upper wick=high−max(open,close), lower wick=min(open,close)−low, and candle total=body size+upper wick+lower wick (use minimum tick if zero). Buy strength=(close−low)/range×0.6 + lower wick/candle total×0.25 + (body/range if positive, else 0)×0.35. Sell strength=(1−(close−low)/range)×0.6 + upper wick/candle total×0.25 + (−body/range if negative, else 0)×0.35. Allocate raw volume between buy and sell in proportion to those strengths; delta is buy volume minus sell volume. Z-score raw volume using its configured mean and standard-deviation lookbacks, clamp to [−4,4], and set weighted delta=delta×(1+0.6×Z-score). Flow acceleration is weighted delta minus its prior-bar value; flow norm is the 10-bar mean absolute weighted delta plus a small positive constant; flow boost=min(abs(weighted delta/flow norm),5)^1.4. Multiply weighted delta×(1+flow boost) by 1.2 when its sign matches delta, otherwise 0.7; add 0.6×delta and 0.4×flow acceleration multiplied by 1 when acceleration's sign matches weighted delta, otherwise 0.5. For each scanned bar, assign SmartFlow to the layer containing that bar's HLC3 in its own Bollinger channel (basis is the configured-lookback SMA of the configured source; bands are basis ± configured deviation × source standard deviation). Add 18% of assigned flow to each adjacent layer when present. Long: Pressure imbalance > 0. Short: Pressure imbalance < 0.
