Calculation: Moving average = selected EMA, SMA, RMA, or WMA of its selected source over its selected period
Long: Within the configured backtest date range AND the active conditions assigned to long evaluate true, combined using the configured AND or OR connector; each condition compares a selectable ATR, moving average, RSI, CCI, Bollinger Band Width, ADX, MFI, or momentum value with another selectable value or a configured number using >, <, >=, <=, =, !=, crosses above, or crosses below
Short: Within the configured backtest date range AND the active conditions assigned to short evaluate true, combined using the configured AND or OR connector; each condition uses the same selectable comparisons as long; in bidirectional mode this enters short, while in spot mode it closes a long
Exit: long, enabled take-profit level is reached (entry price plus configured amount or percentage) OR enabled stop level is reached (entry price minus configured amount or percentage, unless trailing stop is enabled) OR enabled trailing stop is reached (the greater of the prior bar's trailing level and the current bar's low minus configured amount or percentage of low)
Exit: short, enabled take-profit level is reached (entry price minus configured amount or percentage) OR enabled stop level is reached (entry price plus configured amount or percentage, unless trailing stop is enabled) OR enabled trailing stop is reached (the lesser of the prior bar's trailing level and the current bar's high plus configured amount or percentage of high)
