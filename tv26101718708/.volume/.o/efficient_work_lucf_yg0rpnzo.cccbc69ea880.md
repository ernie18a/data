Calculation: Efficient Work = (close − previous close) / total work, or 0 when total work is 0; total work = high − min(open, previous close) + max(open, previous close) − low
Calculation: Signal = the selected moving average of Efficient Work, using the same averaging method and lookback on chart, medium, and highest timeframes; chart uses the current bar, and medium/highest use their last completed bars
Calculation: Main line = selected chart, medium, or highest Signal, or their weighted average
Calculation: High volatility = short-lookback ATR > long-lookback ATR, with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback
Calculation: Hi/Lo channel = highest and lowest Main line over the lookback; compare against the channel from the previous bar
Calculation: Divergence level = Main line at the most recent bar where bar direction differs from whether Main line rose; bar direction is up when close > open, or when close = open and close > previous close
Long: At least one coloring condition is enabled, every enabled condition is bullish (Main line > 0; Main line > prior Hi/Lo channel high; Main line > divergence level; Main line > medium Signal; Main line > highest Signal; chart Signal > medium Signal; chart Signal > highest Signal; medium Signal > 0; highest Signal > 0), the volatility filter passes (all volatility states, high volatility only, or low volatility only), and the previous bar was not bullish
Short: At least one coloring condition is enabled, every enabled condition is bearish (Main line < 0; Main line < prior Hi/Lo channel low; Main line < divergence level; Main line < medium Signal; Main line < highest Signal; chart Signal < medium Signal; chart Signal < highest Signal; medium Signal < 0; highest Signal < 0), the volatility filter passes (all volatility states, high volatility only, or low volatility only), and the previous bar was not bearish
