Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across the swing lookback bars on each side, known after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Uptrend = the last two confirmed swing highs and the last two confirmed swing lows are each strictly rising; it remains active until a CHoCH or distribution reset.
Calculation: Volume ratio = bar volume divided by its moving average; elevated volume is at least the elevated threshold, climactic volume is at least the higher climactic threshold, and weak volume is at most the weak-volume threshold.
Calculation: BC = highest confirmed swing high tracked during the active uptrend; its climactic flag records whether volume at that swing was at least the elevated threshold.
Calculation: PSY candidate = latest elevated-volume confirmed swing high before the current BC; a replaced BC becomes the PSY candidate if its climactic flag is set.
Calculation: AR = lowest low after the BC swing bar through the current bar.
Short: Active uptrend AND BC exists AND current close < last confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that swing low AND current bar is after the BC swing bar
Short: After CHoCH, confirmed swing high is after the CHoCH bar AND swing-bar high > BC × 1.001 AND swing-bar close < BC AND swing-bar volume is elevated
Short: After UT, confirmed swing bar is after the UT bar AND swing-bar close < AR AND swing-bar volume is elevated AND swing-bar close < swing-bar open
Short: After SOW, confirmed swing high is after the SOW bar AND swing high < BC AND swing-bar volume is weak
