Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high from two bars earlier AND the intervening bar’s high > that earlier high; zone = [earlier high, current low]. Keep the latest qualifying bullish zone while its age is at most the configured maximum and current low stays above its lower boundary; clear it when filled, expired, or used for an entry.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low from two bars earlier AND the intervening bar’s low < that earlier low; zone = [current high, earlier low]. Keep the latest qualifying bearish zone while its age is at most the configured maximum and current high stays below its upper boundary; clear it when filled, expired, or used for an entry.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback on the chart bars.
Calculation: Trend average = exponential moving average of close over the configured lookback on the chart bars.
Long: Position is flat AND active bullish FVG exists AND current low is within its zone AND close > open AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > configured multiplier × volume average) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > trend average) AND (Tokyo session is enabled AND UTC hour is 0–8 OR London/NY overlap is enabled AND UTC hour is 13–15)
Short: Position is flat AND active bearish FVG exists AND current high is within its zone AND close < open AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > configured multiplier × volume average) AND (trend filter is disabled OR close < trend average) AND (Tokyo session is enabled AND UTC hour is 0–8 OR London/NY overlap is enabled AND UTC hour is 13–15)
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − configured stop percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured stop percentage × configured risk/reward ratio)
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + configured stop percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured stop percentage × configured risk/reward ratio)
