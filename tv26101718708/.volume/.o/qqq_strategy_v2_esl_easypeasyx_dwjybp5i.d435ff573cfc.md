Calculation: Fast, slow, and filter Ultimate RSI = let H = highest(close,n), L = lowest(close,n), R = H−L, and D = close−previous close; set X to R if H > previous H, to −R if L < previous L, otherwise D; apply the selected RSI smoothing average of length n to X and to |X|, then compute 50 × average(X)/average(|X|) + 50. Fast uses the shortest length, slow a longer length, and filter the longest length.
Calculation: Ultimate RSI signal = selected signal-smoothing average of the corresponding Ultimate RSI; the same smoothing length and method are used for fast, slow, and filter.
Calculation: Fast signed standard deviation = sign(change(SMA(close,n))) × standard deviation(close,n); its signal is an EMA of it. Slow signed standard deviation uses longer lookback and signal lengths than fast.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator = EMA((high+low)/2, shorter length) − EMA((high+low)/2, longer length).
Calculation: Selected stop level = selected stop method: trailing percentage stop (long: percentage below close, short: percentage above close, ratcheted upward for long and downward for short, with reset when the prior close breaches the prior stop); ATR stop (close minus/plus ATR × multiplier, with the same ratchet/reset); high/low stop (long: lowest low over lookback, short: highest high, with the same ratchet/reset); MA stop (long: highest EMA(close) over lookback, short: lowest [2×close−EMA(close)] over lookback, with the same ratchet/reset); or rolling percentage stop (long: highest close adjusted down by percentage over lookback, short: lowest close adjusted up, then ratcheted upward/downward respectively).
Long: (close > main moving average AND its 3-bar change > 0) OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal AND fast RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR slow Ultimate RSI crosses above its signal AND slow RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND slow RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR filter RSI crosses above its signal AND filter RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses above slow Ultimate RSI AND fast RSI is above its lower limit AND slow RSI is above its lower limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit AND below its upper limit OR Awesome Oscillator crosses above zero OR fast signed standard deviation crosses above its signal OR slow signed standard deviation crosses above its signal; AND the selected long trend filter passes (none, close above long trend MA, fast signed standard deviation above its signal, filter RSI crosses above its signal, or fast RSI crosses above slow RSI).
Short: (close < main moving average AND its 3-bar change < 0) OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal AND fast RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR slow Ultimate RSI crosses below its signal AND slow RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit AND filter RSI is above its lower limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND slow RSI is below its lower limit AND above its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit OR filter RSI crosses below its signal AND filter RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit OR fast Ultimate RSI crosses below slow Ultimate RSI AND fast RSI is below its upper limit AND slow RSI is below its upper limit AND filter RSI is below its upper limit AND above its lower limit OR Awesome Oscillator crosses below zero OR fast signed standard deviation crosses below its signal OR slow signed standard deviation crosses below its signal OR close crosses below EMA(close,50) OR close crosses below EMA(close,200); AND the selected short trend filter passes (none, close below short trend MA, fast signed standard deviation below its signal, filter RSI crosses below its signal, or fast RSI crosses below slow RSI).
Exit: long, low < selected stop level OR take profit is enabled AND the selected short trend filter's take-profit condition is true (close > short trend MA, fast signed standard deviation > its signal, filter RSI crosses above its signal, fast RSI crosses above slow RSI, or no condition when the selected filter is none).
Exit: short, high > selected stop level OR take profit is enabled AND the selected short trend filter's take-profit condition is true (close > short trend MA, fast signed standard deviation > its signal, filter RSI crosses above its signal, fast RSI crosses above slow RSI, or no condition when the selected filter is none).
