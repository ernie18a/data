Calculation: Rising over a lookback = current value is greater than each value in the preceding lookback bars; falling over a lookback = current value is less than each.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than all highs/lows across a longer lookback before it and a shorter lookback after it; available after the shorter lookback completes.
Calculation: ALMA = Arnaud Legoux moving average of close, using a longer lookback than the pivot’s left lookback.
Calculation: Long bounce = When a confirmed pivot high occurs while ALMA is rising over a lookback equal to the pivot’s shorter, right-side lookback, reset the long level to that pivot high and clear its fired state. While the level is unchanged, signal once when close exceeds the lowest of the reset level and subsequent highs; update the level to the minimum of its prior value and the current high each bar. After signaling, clear the level; do not signal again until the next reset.
Calculation: Short bounce = When a confirmed pivot low occurs while ALMA is falling over a lookback equal to the pivot’s shorter, right-side lookback, reset the short level to that pivot low and clear its fired state. While the level is unchanged, signal once when close falls below the highest of the reset level and subsequent lows; update the level to the maximum of its prior value and the current low each bar. After signaling, clear the level; do not signal again until the next reset.
Long: close is rising over a 2-bar lookback AND volume is rising over a 2-bar lookback OR Long bounce
Short: close is falling over a 2-bar lookback AND volume is rising over a 2-bar lookback OR Short bounce
