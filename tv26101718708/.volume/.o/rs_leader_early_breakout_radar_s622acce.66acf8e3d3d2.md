Calculation: Relative-strength ratio = current bar close / benchmark close from the same timeframe and completed bar.
Calculation: Relative-strength high = highest relative-strength ratio over its lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Prior price high = highest high over the lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Distance below prior price high = (prior price high − current close) / prior price high × 100.
Calculation: Consolidation width = (highest high − lowest low over the range lookback, including the current bar) / current close × 100.
Calculation: Moving averages = simple moving averages of close, with fast, medium, and slow lengths in ascending order.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its simple moving average over the volume lookback.
Calculation: RS Leader score = rounded, capped sum of component scores for relative strength (increases linearly toward its lookback high), price proximity (increases linearly toward the prior price high), consolidation width (increases as width contracts), moving-average structure (points for close > fast > medium > slow and rising medium average), and relative volume (tiered by relative volume). Component weights and scoring thresholds are parameters.
Long: (RS ratio is within its tolerance of the relative-strength high AND close < prior price high AND distance below prior price high is within its maximum AND consolidation width is within its maximum AND (trend filter is disabled OR close > fast moving average AND fast moving average > medium moving average AND medium moving average > slow moving average AND medium moving average > its value several bars earlier) AND (volume dry-up filter is disabled OR relative volume is unavailable OR relative volume is within its maximum AND RS Leader score meets its minimum AND current bar is confirmed AND setup was false on the previous bar)) OR (current close > prior price high AND previous close <= previous bar's prior price high AND previous bar had an active setup AND current bar is confirmed).
