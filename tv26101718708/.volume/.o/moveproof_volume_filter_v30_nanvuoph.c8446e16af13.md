Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the selected volume average; for the adaptive average, cap each volume input at the previous bar’s raw volume EMA multiplied by a cap multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish close strength = (close − low) / (high − low); bearish close strength = (high − close) / (high − low); use 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish MA bias = close above the long-term EMA, short-term EMA above medium-term EMA above long-term EMA, and medium-term EMA rising.
Calculation: Bearish MA bias = close below the long-term EMA, short-term EMA below medium-term EMA below long-term EMA, and medium-term EMA falling.
Long: close above the highest high of the prior completed lookback bars AND close > open AND bullish close strength meets its threshold AND relative volume meets its strong threshold OR bullish MA bias AND low <= short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > medium-term EMA AND close > open AND bullish close strength meets its threshold AND relative volume is not weak.
Short: close below the lowest low of the prior completed lookback bars AND close < open AND bearish close strength meets its threshold AND relative volume meets its strong threshold OR bearish MA bias AND high >= short-term EMA AND close < short-term EMA AND close < medium-term EMA AND close < open AND bearish close strength meets its threshold AND relative volume is not weak.
