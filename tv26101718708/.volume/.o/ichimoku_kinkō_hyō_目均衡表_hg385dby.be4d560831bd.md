Calculation: Tenkan and Kijun = average of the high-low midpoint over each line’s lookback and the midpoint over a shorter lookback (lookback divided by the reactivity divider); Tenkan uses the shorter lookback.
Calculation: Senkou A = average of Tenkan and Kijun; Senkou B = high-low midpoint over a longer lookback; for current-price comparisons, use both values from the chart bar one span-offset before the current bar.
Calculation: Chikou trend = selected price source is above the highest high, or below the lowest low, over the Chikou lookback ending one Chikou offset before the current bar.
Calculation: Chikou momentum = selected price source minus the selected price source one bar before the Chikou offset.
Calculation: Trend strength = EMA-smoothed directional movement and true range converted to directional indices, then EMA-smoothed DX.
Long: Tenkan > Kijun AND Chikou momentum > 0 AND selected price source > price source at the Chikou offset AND selected price source > top of the current cloud AND selected price source > Tenkan AND Chikou trend is above its lookback highs AND selected volume oscillator > its breakout threshold AND fast ATR > slow ATR AND NOT (trend strength is below its threshold AND cloud thickness as a percentage of price is below its threshold), when this condition changes from false to true and the prior regime is not long
Short: Tenkan < Kijun AND Chikou momentum < 0 AND selected price source < price source at the Chikou offset AND selected price source < top of the current cloud AND selected price source < Tenkan AND Chikou trend is below its lookback lows AND selected volume oscillator < its breakout threshold AND fast ATR > slow ATR AND NOT (trend strength is below its threshold AND cloud thickness as a percentage of price is below its threshold), when this condition changes from false to true and the prior regime is not short
Exit: long, the short condition changes from false to true
