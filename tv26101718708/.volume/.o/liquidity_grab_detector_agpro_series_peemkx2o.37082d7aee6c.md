Calculation: Active liquidity levels = confirmed swing highs/lows (using bars on both sides of each pivot), completed session highs/lows, and the last completed daily and weekly highs/lows; deduplicate same-side levels within an ATR-based tolerance, retain untouched levels within a lookback and active-level limit, and remove swept levels after confirmation.
Calculation: Swept high levels = active high levels where current high > level and previous bar high <= level; swept low levels = active low levels where current low < level and previous bar low >= level.
Long: At least the minimum number of distinct swept low levels, with the highest minus lowest swept level <= ATR × cluster threshold, current volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier, and lower wick >= ATR × wick threshold; within the reversal window, close > the highest swept low level; and the cooldown since the last confirmed grab has elapsed. If a bearish grab also qualifies on the same bar, keep the long only when more low levels were swept than high levels.
Short: At least the minimum number of distinct swept high levels, with the highest minus lowest swept level <= ATR × cluster threshold, current volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier, and upper wick >= ATR × wick threshold; within the reversal window, close < the lowest swept high level; and the cooldown since the last confirmed grab has elapsed. If a bullish grab also qualifies on the same bar, keep the short when at least as many high levels were swept as low levels.
