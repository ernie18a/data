Calculation: Composite pressure score = 60% of the fast/slow momentum score plus 40% of the normalized momentum score. Fast and slow momentum are close changes over shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively. Fast/slow score = ((fast momentum − slow momentum) / (fast momentum + slow momentum) × 100 + 100) / 2; use 0 for the ratio when slow momentum equals negative fast momentum. Momentum score = 100 × (current momentum − lowest momentum over the last 100 bars) / (highest momentum over the last 100 bars − lowest momentum over the last 100 bars); momentum is the close change over its lookback, and the score is 50 if the highest and lowest are equal.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = high exceeds the highest high of the prior liquidity lookback bars AND close is below that prior highest high.
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = low falls below the lowest low of the prior liquidity lookback bars AND close is above that prior lowest low.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = bullish engulfing (close > open, close > prior open, open < prior close) OR hammer (lower wick > 2 × body, upper wick < 0.3 × body, high > low) OR morning star (bar two bars ago bearish, prior body ≤ 0.1 × prior range, current bullish, current close > midpoint of bar two bars ago) OR bullish large body (body > 0.8 × ATR(14), close > open).
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = bearish engulfing (close < open, close < prior open, open > prior close) OR shooting star (upper wick > 2 × body, lower wick < 0.3 × body, high > low) OR evening star (bar two bars ago bullish, prior body ≤ 0.1 × prior range, current bearish, current close < midpoint of bar two bars ago) OR bearish large body (body > 0.8 × ATR(14), close < open).
Long: composite pressure score ≥ 55 OR composite pressure score > its prior-bar value OR bearish liquidity sweep OR bullish candle pattern
Short: composite pressure score < 45 OR composite pressure score < its prior-bar value OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bearish candle pattern
