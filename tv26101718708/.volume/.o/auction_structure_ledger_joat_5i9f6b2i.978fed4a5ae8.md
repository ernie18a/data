Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the maximum/minimum over a symmetric pivot lookback, confirmed only after the right-side lookback bars close.
Calculation: Pivot cluster count = number of confirmed pivots of the same type in the cluster lookback whose prices are within an ATR-based tolerance of the current confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Auction shelves = create a resistance shelf for a confirmed pivot high or a support shelf for a confirmed pivot low when its cluster count is at least 2 and no active shelf of that type has a midpoint within the ATR-based overlap tolerance. Shelf width is ATR-based. Remove the oldest shelf when the active total exceeds its limit; remove a failed shelf after it has existed for more than a short lifecycle buffer. A resistance shelf fails when close is above its top; a support shelf fails when close is below its bottom.
Calculation: Active support/resistance counts = counts of active support/resistance shelves.
Calculation: Price-volume confluence = volume in the current close's bin divided by the largest bin volume, times 100. Build bins from closes and their volumes over a rolling lookback, spanning that lookback's highest high to lowest low.
Long: active support count > active resistance count OR active support count > active resistance count AND price-volume confluence >= its threshold OR a confirmed pivot low has a pivot cluster count of at least 2
Short: active resistance count > active support count OR active resistance count > active support count AND price-volume confluence < its threshold OR a confirmed pivot high has a pivot cluster count of at least 2
