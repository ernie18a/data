Calculation: Candle range = high − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Qualified bearish wick candle = range filter passes AND upper wick share ≥ bearish minimum AND lower wick share ≤ bearish maximum. Qualified bullish wick candle = range filter passes AND lower wick share ≥ bullish minimum AND upper wick share ≤ bullish maximum. The optional range filter requires range ≥ current ATR × its multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish window pattern = at least the required number of matching wick candles in the recent window. A pattern is valid only when the opposite pattern is absent; a formation starts when a valid pattern appears after not being valid on the prior bar.
Calculation: Formation box = highest high and lowest low across the full pattern window. Formation volume starts with intersecting window candles; for each, buy volume adds volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick), and sell volume adds volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick). While the box is active, add the same proportions for each later intersecting candle that does not break out. The box remains active until a breakout or wait-limit cancellation.
Calculation: Breakout boundary test = use close against the box boundary when close-based breakout is selected; otherwise use high for an upward breakout and low for a downward breakout.
Calculation: Breakout size filter, when enabled, requires current range ≥ prior-bar SMA of candle range × its multiplier.
Long: An active box formed by a new bullish window pattern AND upward breakout boundary test passes AND accumulated buy volume ≥ accumulated sell volume AND (breakout size filter is disabled OR it passes) AND bullish signals are enabled.
Short: An active box formed by a new bearish window pattern AND downward breakout boundary test passes AND accumulated sell volume > accumulated buy volume AND (breakout size filter is disabled OR it passes) AND bearish signals are enabled.
Calculation: Risk distance = current ATR × its multiplier when ATR-based stop is selected; otherwise entry price × stop-loss percentage. Long stop = entry price − risk distance; short stop = entry price + risk distance. Take-profit levels are entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk distance × each target risk/reward parameter.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ the farthest enabled take-profit level.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ the farthest enabled take-profit level.
