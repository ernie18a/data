Calculation: Session high/low = highest high and lowest low from the current day’s 09:15 bar through the current bar, including the current bar
Long: current bar high = session high AND RSI > 50 AND time is between 10:15 and 15:10 AND close > open
Short: current bar low = session low AND RSI < 50 AND time is between 10:15 and 15:10 AND close < open
Exit: long, close < VWMA OR time is between 15:11 and 15:30
Exit: short, close > VWMA OR time is between 15:11 and 15:30
