Calculation: Confirmed swing liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows, clustered with nearby levels within an ATR-based distance; merge their weights, increasing weight for repeated reactions and, when enabled, relative volume. Use the nearest level above or below the current close and its weight.
Calculation: Relative volume = current analysis-timeframe volume / its moving average over a lookback.
Calculation: Upper/lower liquidity zone = nearest upper/lower level ± an ATR-based height.
Calculation: Sweep rejection strength = (sweep extreme − close) / (sweep extreme − opposite edge of the zone) for an upper sweep; (close − sweep extreme) / (opposite edge of the zone − sweep extreme) for a lower sweep.
Long: Lower-zone sweep (low below the zone and close back above its level; in wick-reclaim mode, close above the lower zone edge) AND lower-level weight ≥ minimum sweep weight AND lower wick ≥ ATR-based minimum AND rejection strength ≥ minimum AND volume/session filters pass when enabled AND, in strict trend-filter mode, close ≥ trend EMA AND no lower-sweep cooldown is active; in follow-through mode, within the configured window after this setup, low stays at or above the setup low, close stays above the setup level, and close exceeds the setup close.
Short: Upper-zone sweep (high above the zone and close back below its level; in wick-reclaim mode, close below the upper zone edge) AND upper-level weight ≥ minimum sweep weight AND upper wick ≥ ATR-based minimum AND rejection strength ≥ minimum AND volume/session filters pass when enabled AND, in strict trend-filter mode, close ≤ trend EMA AND no upper-sweep cooldown is active; in follow-through mode, within the configured window after this setup, high stays at or below the setup high, close stays below the setup level, and close is below the setup close.
Exit: Long, confirmed upper-zone sweep AND upper-level weight ≥ structural-exit minimum AND close < open AND close < upper level − ATR-based offset.
Exit: Short, confirmed lower-zone sweep AND lower-level weight ≥ structural-exit minimum AND close > open AND close > lower level + ATR-based offset.
