Calculation: Un-swept liquidity levels = On chart-timeframe bars, sum volume over a shorter rolling window and normalize it by its maximum over a longer rolling lookback; compute ATR from true range. Add an upper level at a rolling local high plus ATR times normalized volume, and a lower level at a rolling local low minus ATR times normalized volume. Also add symmetric volume-qualified swing highs and lows, using a shorter pivot window and an ATR-based width. Keep levels until price sweeps them (high above an upper level or low below a lower level).
Calculation: Eligible liquidity-zone midpoint = Divide the shared price range of the longer lookback, expanded by ATR times normalized volume and by qualifying swing widths, into bins. Aggregate un-swept levels and swing-level weights into bins, snap swing levels to the shared grid, and boost bins near qualifying unswept swing extremes. A midpoint is eligible when its bin has nonzero weight and the required source confluence: at least two available external sources, or one chart source when fewer than two external sources are available. Use the current chart close when in chart-source fallback; otherwise use the mean close of available external sources.
Long: Eligible liquidity-zone midpoint < current price
Short: Eligible liquidity-zone midpoint > current price
