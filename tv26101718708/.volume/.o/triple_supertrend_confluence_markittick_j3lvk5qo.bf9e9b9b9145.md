Calculation: Supertrend direction = direction from bands around the smoothed HL2 source, with band width equal to ATR times a factor; ratchet the lower band upward and upper band downward unless the prior close crosses the prior band, and flip direction when close crosses the active opposite band. The core factor may be fixed or mapped linearly from the core ATR’s percentile rank over a longer lookback to a minimum–maximum range. The fast tracker uses a shorter ATR lookback and smaller factor; the slow tracker uses a longer ATR lookback and larger factor than the core.
Calculation: Cooldown = after a trend segment lasting no more than the cooldown limit, suppress signals in that segment’s direction for the cooldown limit of bars.
Long: On a confirmed bar, core Supertrend direction changes from down to up AND (if enabled) at least two of the three Supertrend directions agree AND (if enabled) ADX is at or above its threshold AND (if enabled) the prior completed higher-timeframe Supertrend direction is up AND (if enabled and volume is available and positive) volume is at least its moving average times the volume factor AND (if enabled) no long cooldown is active.
Short: On a confirmed bar, core Supertrend direction changes from up to down AND (if enabled) at least two of the three Supertrend directions agree AND (if enabled) ADX is at or above its threshold AND (if enabled) the prior completed higher-timeframe Supertrend direction is down AND (if enabled and volume is available and positive) volume is at least its moving average times the volume factor AND (if enabled) no short cooldown is active.
