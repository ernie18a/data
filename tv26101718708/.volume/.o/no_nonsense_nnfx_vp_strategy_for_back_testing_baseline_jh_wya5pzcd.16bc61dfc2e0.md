Calculation: Baseline = selected moving average of close
Long: close crosses above Baseline AND absolute difference between close and Baseline is no greater than current-bar ATR AND year > 2009
Short: close crosses below Baseline AND absolute difference between close and Baseline is no greater than current-bar ATR AND year > 2009
Exit: Long position, close crosses below Baseline OR low reaches the entry-price stop set at an ATR multiple below entry OR high reaches the entry-price target set at an ATR multiple above entry
Exit: Short position, close crosses above Baseline OR high reaches the entry-price stop set at an ATR multiple above entry OR low reaches the entry-price target set at an ATR multiple below entry
