Calculation: ADR = average high−low over the 10 most recently completed daily bars, aggregated from the input bars; if unavailable or nonpositive, use 0.5% of close.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot highs/lows = chart-timeframe highs/lows that are the greatest/least across equal parameterized lookbacks on both sides, recognized only after the right-side bars close; use the latest three of each.
Calculation: Equality tolerance = ADR × equality-tolerance parameter; slope threshold = ADR × slope-threshold parameter. Use these separately for level equality and swing direction.
Calculation: Pattern span = the distance in bars from the earliest first pivot to the latest third pivot; candidate patterns require the span within the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Candidate patterns use the latest three pivot highs H1,H2,H3 and lows L1,L2,L3. Head-and-shoulders: H2 exceeds H1 and H3 by more than equality tolerance, |H1−H3| ≤ 1.6× tolerance; trigger=(L2+L3)/2, invalidation=H3, height=H2−trigger. Inverse head-and-shoulders: L2 is below L1 and L3 by more than tolerance, |L1−L3| ≤ 1.6× tolerance; trigger=(H2+H3)/2, invalidation=L3, height=trigger−L2. Double top: |H2−H3| ≤ tolerance, H2>L3+tolerance, height=(H2+H3)/2−L3 ≥ tolerance×depth parameter; trigger=L3, invalidation=max(H2,H3). Double bottom: |L2−L3| ≤ tolerance, H3>L2+tolerance, height=H3−(L2+L3)/2 ≥ tolerance×depth parameter; trigger=H3, invalidation=min(L2,L3). For remaining patterns, high direction is up/down/flat according as H3−H1 is greater than the slope threshold, less than its negative, or within it; low direction is defined identically from L3−L1. Convergence means H1−L1>0 and H3−L3 ≤ (H1−L1)×convergence parameter. Let resistance=max(H1,H2,H3), support=min(L1,L2,L3), height=resistance−support. Ascending triangle: flat highs and rising lows; bullish, trigger=resistance, invalidation=L3. Descending triangle: flat lows and falling highs; bearish, trigger=support, invalidation=H3. Symmetrical triangle: falling highs, rising lows, and convergence; direction is bullish if H1+L1 < H3+L3, otherwise bearish; trigger=resistance when bullish and support when bearish; invalidation is support when bullish and resistance when bearish. Rising wedge: rising highs and lows with convergence; bearish, trigger=L3, invalidation=H3. Falling wedge: falling highs and lows with convergence; bullish, trigger=H3, invalidation=L3.
Calculation: When multiple candidates qualify, select the one with the highest precedence: head-and-shoulders or inverse head-and-shoulders, double top or bottom, ascending or descending triangle, symmetrical triangle, rising or falling wedge; within the same precedence, select the highest score. Symmetry score = rounded to 0.1 of 10×(0.65×max(0,1−level-difference/(tolerance+epsilon)) + 0.35×min(1,height/(0.6×ADR))). Triangle/wedge score = rounded to 0.1 of 10×(0.55×convergence-score + 0.45×min(1,height/(0.7×ADR))), where convergence-score is 1 for converging candidates and 0.4 otherwise.
Calculation: Pending setup expires after its configured bar limit. At entry, target height = candidate height bounded below by ADR×minimum-height parameter and above by ADR×maximum-height parameter. For a long, stop= invalidation−ADR×stop-buffer parameter (if stop≥entry, use entry−2×ADR×stop-buffer parameter); targets are entry plus target height times the respective TP1 and TP2 parameters. For a short, stop= invalidation+ADR×stop-buffer parameter (if stop≤entry, use entry+2×ADR×stop-buffer parameter); targets are entry minus target height times the respective TP1 and TP2 parameters.
Long: No active position or pending setup, bar spacing from the prior signal meets its configured minimum, a new confirmed pivot produces a qualifying bullish candidate, and its pending setup fires with close > trigger before invalidation (low < invalidation) or expiry; enter at close.
Short: No active position or pending setup, bar spacing from the prior signal meets its configured minimum, a new confirmed pivot produces a qualifying bearish candidate, and its pending setup fires with close < trigger before invalidation (high > invalidation) or expiry; enter at close.
Exit: long, high ≥ TP2 OR high ≥ TP1 OR low ≤ stop; on a bar touching multiple levels, prioritize TP2, then TP1, then stop.
Exit: short, low ≤ TP2 OR low ≤ TP1 OR high ≥ stop; on a bar touching multiple levels, prioritize TP2, then TP1, then stop.
