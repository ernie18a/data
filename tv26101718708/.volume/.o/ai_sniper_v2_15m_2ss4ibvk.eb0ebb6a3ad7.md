Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of chart-timeframe close, with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of chart-timeframe close, with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: Bias EMA = exponential moving average of higher-timeframe close, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = 3-bar simple moving average of the stochastic of chart-timeframe RSI
Calculation: Stochastic RSI D = 3-bar simple moving average of Stochastic RSI K
Calculation: ADX = average directional index on the chart timeframe
Long: chart close > Bias EMA AND lowest chart low over the pullback lookback <= max(Fast EMA, Slow EMA) AND a Stochastic RSI K cross above D occurred within the current or previous 2 bars, with K below the oversold threshold on the bar before that cross AND chart close > Fast EMA AND chart close > chart open AND ADX >= threshold when the ADX filter is enabled AND chart volume < its 10-bar simple moving average when the declining-volume filter is enabled AND abs(chart close - chart open) / chart open * 100 < maximum candle-run percentage AND current bar index - last signal bar index >= cooldown bars
Short: chart close < Bias EMA AND highest chart high over the pullback lookback >= min(Fast EMA, Slow EMA) AND a Stochastic RSI K cross below D occurred within the current or previous 2 bars, with K above the overbought threshold on the bar before that cross AND chart close < Fast EMA AND chart close < chart open AND ADX >= threshold when the ADX filter is enabled AND chart volume < its 10-bar simple moving average when the declining-volume filter is enabled AND abs(chart close - chart open) / chart open * 100 < maximum candle-run percentage AND current bar index - last signal bar index >= cooldown bars
