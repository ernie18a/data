Calculation: Momentum threshold = base percentage × (1 + 2 × 14-bar ATR / close)
Calculation: Higher- and lower-timeframe trend = bullish when chart close is above both the 20-period EMA and VWAP; bearish when below both; otherwise neutral. Use the selected timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Calculation: Recent pivot high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low using the selected pivot lookback on chart bars.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume above its long-period average AND the short-period volume average is rising.
Long: (price change from previous close > momentum threshold, when momentum filter is enabled) AND (selected higher-timeframe trend is bullish, when that filter is enabled) AND (selected lower-timeframe trend is bullish or neutral, when that filter is enabled) AND (volume confirmation, when volume filter is enabled) AND (close above the prior bar's recent-period highest high, when breakout filter is enabled) AND minimum signal spacing is met AND repeated-signal restriction permits; OR high crosses above the prior bar's recent pivot low AND close > open; OR high crosses above the preceding bar's recent pivot high AND high > that level AND close > open; OR Get Ready condition: when momentum filter is enabled, price change > pre-momentum threshold AND < momentum threshold; otherwise, minimum signal spacing is met AND the enabled trend, volume, and breakout filters pass.
Short: (price change from previous close < -momentum threshold, when momentum filter is enabled) AND (selected higher-timeframe trend is bearish, when that filter is enabled) AND (selected lower-timeframe trend is bearish or neutral, when that filter is enabled) AND (volume confirmation, when volume filter is enabled) AND (close below the prior bar's recent-period lowest low, when breakout filter is enabled) AND minimum signal spacing is met AND repeated-signal restriction permits; OR low crosses below the recent pivot high AND close < open; OR low crosses below the preceding bar's recent pivot low AND low < that level AND close < open; OR Get Ready condition: when momentum filter is enabled, price change < -pre-momentum threshold AND > -momentum threshold; otherwise, minimum signal spacing is met AND the enabled trend, volume, and breakout filters pass.
Exit: Long, price reaches the take-profit limit above or stop-loss level below the signal-bar close.
Exit: Short, price reaches the take-profit limit below or stop-loss level above the signal-bar close.
