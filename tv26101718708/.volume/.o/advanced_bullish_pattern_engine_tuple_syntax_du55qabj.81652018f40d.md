Calculation: Strong trend = close > short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA.
Calculation: Healthy trend = close > medium EMA > long EMA.
Calculation: Momentum passes = RSI is at least its configured minimum and rate of change over its configured lookback is positive.
Calculation: Volume passes = current volume > average volume over its configured lookback × configured volume multiplier.
Calculation: Candle passes = bullish engulfing OR hammer-like bullish bar OR strong bullish bar. Engulfing: current close > open, previous close < open, current open ≤ previous close, and current close ≥ previous open. Hammer-like: close > open, lower wick > body × configured ratio, upper wick ≤ body, and close is above the configured fraction of the bar range. Strong bullish bar: close > open, close is above the configured fraction of the bar range, and body/range exceeds its configured threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot highs/lows = highs/lows that are respectively greater/less than the configured numbers of bars on both sides; use only pivots confirmed by the current bar.
Calculation: Pattern candidates and stops = (1) breakout: close > highest high of the preceding configured lookback × configured breakout buffer, close > short EMA, and volume passes; stop = short EMA. (2) Pullback: strong trend, absolute distance from close to short EMA ≤ ATR, close > short EMA, and strong bullish bar; stop = medium EMA. (3) Compression expansion: previous bar had healthy trend, was an inside bar, had a range below the lowest range of the preceding configured lookback, and volume below average volume; current close > previous high and volume passes; stop = previous low. (4) Inverse head and shoulders: the latest three confirmed pivot lows have the middle low below both outer lows, outer lows within configured percentage tolerance, and the two intervening time spans within configured bar tolerance; use the highest confirmed pivot high between each adjacent pair as neckline endpoints, linearly project the neckline to the current bar, and require close above it, above short EMA, and volume passes; stop = latest pivot low. (5) Cup and handle: the latest two confirmed pivot highs are within configured percentage tolerance; the lowest confirmed pivot low between them is strictly between them and gives a depth within configured minimum and maximum; from the right pivot high through the current bar, the lowest low retraces no more than the configured fraction of the cup depth; require close above both pivot highs, above short EMA, and volume passes; stop = short EMA. (6) Double bottom: the latest two confirmed pivot lows are within configured percentage tolerance; the highest confirmed pivot high between them exists; require close above that high, above short EMA, and volume passes; stop = latest pivot low. (7) Bull flag: healthy trend and close > medium EMA; (highest high − close)/(highest high − lowest low) over the configured lookback is above the configured minimum and at most the configured maximum; close > previous high and volume passes; stop = medium EMA. (8) Ascending triangle: the latest two confirmed pivot highs are within configured percentage tolerance, the latest pivot low is above the preceding pivot low, and close exceeds the higher of the two pivot highs, is above short EMA, and volume passes; stop = latest pivot low.
Calculation: Selected pattern = valid candidate with highest configured pattern priority; its associated score and stop are used.
Calculation: Pattern score = configured base score for the selected pattern plus configured points for its applicable trend, momentum, volume, and candle conditions.
Calculation: Risk passes = 0 < (close − selected pattern stop)/close × 100 ≤ configured maximum risk percentage.
Calculation: Rounded score = nearest integer to selected pattern score + configured risk-pass points when risk passes + configured candle-pass points when candle passes.
Long: A pattern candidate exists AND rounded score ≥ configured candidate threshold.
