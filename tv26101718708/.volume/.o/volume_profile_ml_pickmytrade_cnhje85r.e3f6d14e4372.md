Calculation: Rolling volume profile = distribute each bar’s volume across price rows spanning its high–low range, weighted triangularly around (high+low+close)/3; calculate row-volume percentiles, the maximum-volume row (POC), and the value area expanded from the POC until it contains the configured share of profile volume. Rebuild on the configured cadence from closed chart bars, including the current confirmed bar.
Calculation: Node-arrival vote = at each newly entered HVN or LVN row, compare its nine-feature fingerprint (row volume share, row-volume rank, five-row local volume, three-bar approach displacement, signed distance from POC, profile position, volume versus its 20-bar average, POC concentration, and position relative to the value area) with resolved prior arrivals using summed log(1+absolute feature difference); average the K nearest distances and weight each prior HOLD/BREAK outcome by 1/(1+distance), then shrink the weighted HOLD share toward the library HOLD rate by the configured shrinkage weight.
Calculation: Arrival outcome = for an arrival approached from below, BREAK if a later close is at least the configured ATR multiple above its row price, otherwise HOLD if a later low is at least the configured ATR multiple below it; reverse the tests for an arrival approached from above. Use ATR(14) frozen at arrival, test BREAK first, and discard unresolved arrivals at timeout.
Long: Confirmed close enters a qualifying HVN or LVN row different from the prior bar’s row, the row is re-armed, the resolved-arrival library meets its minimum size, mean K-neighbour distance is within its ceiling, and ((close three bars earlier is above the row price AND HOLD vote >= 0.5) OR (close three bars earlier is below the row price AND HOLD vote < 0.5)).
