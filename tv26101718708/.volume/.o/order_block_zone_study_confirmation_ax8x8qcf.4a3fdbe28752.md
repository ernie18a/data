Calculation: Support OB zone = at a confirmed swing low, the most recent bearish candle within the source lookback, if the rise from its low to the pivot-confirmation bar’s high is at least the source candle’s ATR times the displacement threshold; center a zone of configurable ATR-based height on that candle’s midpoint.
Calculation: Resistance OB zone = at a confirmed swing high, the most recent bullish candle within the source lookback, if the fall from its high to the pivot-confirmation bar’s low is at least the source candle’s ATR times the displacement threshold; center a zone of configurable ATR-based height on that candle’s midpoint.
Calculation: OB score = configurable weighted average of origin, location, and reaction scores, scaled to 30; origin scores displacement strength, close breaking the source candle, body expansion, and relative volume; location scores premium/discount, nearby confirmed pivot support/resistance, close relative to EMA, and market bias; reaction scores invalidation, resolved target/failure, touch state, and maximum zone coverage.
Calculation: Market bias = bullish after close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high, bearish after close falls below the latest confirmed swing low, otherwise retain the prior bias.
Calculation: Support confirmation hits = count of: close ≥ fast EMA; fast EMA ≥ slow EMA or rising; RSI > 42 and non-falling; MACD ≥ signal or rising; close ≥ open or prior close; relative volume ≥ 0.90. Use a shorter fast EMA than slow EMA.
Calculation: Resistance confirmation hits = count of: close ≤ fast EMA; fast EMA ≤ slow EMA or falling; RSI < 58 and non-rising; MACD ≤ signal or falling; close ≤ open or prior close; relative volume ≥ 0.90. Use a shorter fast EMA than slow EMA.
Long: a support OB is touched (low < zone top) OR its confirmation conditions hold: zone is unrevolved, market bias is bullish, zone midpoint is below the recent-range midpoint, zone midpoint is within an ATR-based distance of the latest confirmed swing low, first touch was within the confirmation window, close > zone bottom and close ≥ open, and OB score ≥ minimum; OR confirmation add-on conditions hold: support zone was touched within its confirmation window or price is near it, price is in or near the zone, OB score ≥ minimum minus tolerance, and support confirmation hits ≥ minimum hits.
Short: a resistance OB is touched (high > zone bottom) OR its confirmation conditions hold: zone is unrevolved, market bias is bearish, zone midpoint is above the recent-range midpoint, zone midpoint is within an ATR-based distance of the latest confirmed swing high, first touch was within the confirmation window, close < zone top and close ≤ open, and OB score ≥ minimum; OR confirmation add-on conditions hold: resistance zone was touched within its confirmation window or price is near it, price is in or near the zone, OB score ≥ minimum minus tolerance, and resistance confirmation hits ≥ minimum hits.
