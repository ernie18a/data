Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI(price, length); price is the selected source after the selected optional smoothing.
Calculation: QQE trailing level = ratcheting trail around smoothed RSI: double EMA of the absolute one-bar change in smoothed RSI, with EMA length 2 × RSI length − 1, multiplied by a factor; when smoothed RSI is below the prior trail, set trail to RSI plus this distance, but do not raise it if the prior RSI was also below the prior trail; when RSI is above the prior trail, set trail to RSI minus the distance, but do not lower it if the prior RSI was also above the prior trail; otherwise retain the prior trail.
Long: On the last completed chart bar, the primary smoothed RSI changes from below the primary trail to above it, AND (if secondary QQE confirmation is enabled) the secondary smoothed RSI is above its trail, AND (if ADX filter is enabled) ADX on the last completed chart bar is at least its threshold, AND (if higher-timeframe filter is enabled) the secondary? higher-timeframe primary smoothed RSI is above its trail on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: On the last completed chart bar, the primary smoothed RSI changes from above the primary trail to below it, AND (if secondary QQE confirmation is enabled) the secondary smoothed RSI is below its trail, AND (if ADX filter is enabled) ADX on the last completed chart bar is at least its threshold, AND (if higher-timeframe filter is enabled) the higher-timeframe primary smoothed RSI is below its trail on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Exit: long, short-entry condition OR low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ third take-profit price.
Exit: short, long-entry condition OR high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ third take-profit price.
