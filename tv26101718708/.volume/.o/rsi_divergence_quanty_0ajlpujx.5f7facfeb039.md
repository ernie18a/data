Calculation: RSI pivot low = RSI value lower than the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookahead bars; confirmed after the right-lookahead bars close.
Calculation: RSI pivot high = RSI value higher than the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookahead bars; confirmed after the right-lookahead bars close.
Calculation: Pivot price low/high = lowest low/highest high in the window centered on the RSI pivot, extending the right-lookahead length on each side.
Long: On the bar a new RSI pivot low is confirmed, its gap from the prior RSI pivot low with the lowest RSI value among prior lows within the configured minimum and maximum gap is within that range AND its pivot price low < that prior pivot's price low AND its RSI value >= that prior RSI value + minimum RSI difference AND (oversold filter disabled OR (prior RSI value <= oversold level AND current RSI value < 50)).
Short: On the bar a new RSI pivot high is confirmed, its gap from the prior RSI pivot high with the highest RSI value among prior highs within the configured minimum and maximum gap is within that range AND its pivot price high > that prior pivot's price high AND its RSI value <= that prior RSI value - minimum RSI difference AND (overbought filter disabled OR (prior RSI value >= overbought level AND current RSI value > 50)).
