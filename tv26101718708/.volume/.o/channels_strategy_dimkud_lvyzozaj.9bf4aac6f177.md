Calculation: Bollinger Bands = simple moving average of close ± selected standard-deviation multiple of close’s standard deviation over the selected lookback, on chart bars
Calculation: Keltner Channel = exponential moving average of close ± selected multiple of exponential moving average of true range over the selected lookback, on chart bars
Calculation: ATR = selected moving average of true range over its lookback; the ATR exit and volatility filter may use separate lookbacks
Long: selected entry rule is low < lower channel; OR prior low < current lower channel AND close > current lower channel; OR close < lower channel; OR prior close < current lower channel AND close > current lower channel; AND ATR > 0; AND every enabled long-side RSI, MFI, and CCI range filter passes; AND, if either side’s filter is enabled for an indicator, that indicator’s long-side filter is enabled and passes; AND, if enabled, the fast ATR is less than or greater than the slow ATR as selected
Short: selected entry rule is high > upper channel; OR prior high > current upper channel AND close < current upper channel; OR close > upper channel; OR prior close > current upper channel AND close < current upper channel; AND ATR > 0; AND every enabled short-side RSI, MFI, and CCI range filter passes; AND, if either side’s filter is enabled for an indicator, that indicator’s short-side filter is enabled and passes; AND, if enabled, the fast ATR is less than or greater than the slow ATR as selected
Exit: long, low <= selected stop price OR high >= selected take-profit price
Exit: short, high >= selected stop price OR low <= selected take-profit price
