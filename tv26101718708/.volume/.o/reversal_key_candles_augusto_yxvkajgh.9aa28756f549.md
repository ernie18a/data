Calculation: Bullish/Bearish divergence for an indicator = at a confirmed pivot low/high, respectively, its value is higher/lower than at the preceding same-kind pivot while price low/high is lower/higher; the number of bars between pivots is within the configured minimum and maximum. Confirm pivots using the configured left and right bars; compare values at the pivot bars. Apply the same pivot and range parameters to RSI, OBV, MACD histogram (12, 26, 9), and 10-bar close momentum. Count enabled indicator divergences separately for each direction.
Calculation: Bullish/Bearish candlestick pattern = any enabled matching pattern in the configured search window, evaluated on the pivot bar and preceding bars: bullish hammer (lower wick/range ≥ 0.40, lower wick ≥ body × wick ratio, upper wick ≤ body × 0.5), bullish inverted hammer (bullish candle, upper wick ≥ body × wick ratio, lower wick ≤ body × 0.5, upper wick/range ≥ 0.35), bullish engulfing (bullish candle after bearish, close > prior open, open < prior close, body > prior body × 0.8), bullish pin bar (lower wick/range ≥ 0.55, upper wick/range ≤ 0.25), dragonfly (lower wick/range ≥ 0.60, body/range ≤ 0.15), doji (body/range ≤ configured doji limit), morning star (two bars earlier bearish with body/range ≥ 0.45, prior body/range ≤ 0.35, current bullish close ≥ midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body), or bullish key reversal (low < prior low, close > prior close, lower wick/range ≥ 0.30, close is in upper half of range). Bearish patterns are the corresponding bearish rules: shooting star (upper wick/range ≥ 0.40, upper wick ≥ body × wick ratio, lower wick ≤ body × 0.5), bearish engulfing (bearish after bullish, close < prior open, open > prior close, body > prior body × 0.8), bearish pin bar (upper wick/range ≥ 0.55, lower wick/range ≤ 0.25), gravestone (upper wick/range ≥ 0.60, body/range ≤ 0.15), doji, evening star (two bars earlier bullish with body/range ≥ 0.45, prior body/range ≤ 0.35, current bearish close ≤ midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body), or bearish key reversal (high > prior high, close < prior close, upper wick/range ≥ 0.30, close is in lower half of range). Use max(range, 0.0001) and max(body, 0.0001) as the range and body denominators where needed.
Calculation: Volume-above-average = any volume in the pivot-to-current window through two bars beyond the right-pivot confirmation bars exceeds its selected volume moving average. Relative-volume-high = the maximum volume in that window divided by the selected moving average at the pivot bar meets its threshold.
Calculation: Fibonacci-zone proximity = the pivot bar's low (bullish) or high (bearish) is within the configured percentage tolerance of any of the latest confirmed swing high, swing low, or 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, or 78.6% retracement levels between them. Find swing highs and lows using equal left/right lookbacks of half the configured Fibonacci pivot lookback (minimum 5). Tolerance is the pivot-bar close times the configured percentage.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confluence = the enabled divergence count meets the configured minimum divergence score, and the sum of these five true/false values meets the configured minimum signal score: directional confluence divergence; directional candlestick pattern (or candle filter disabled); volume above average (or volume filter disabled); directional Fibonacci proximity (or Fibonacci filter disabled, or relative-volume-high); relative-volume-high. Divergence confluence is enabled only when divergence calculation is enabled. RSI divergence labels require only enabled RSI divergence and divergence calculation enabled.
Calculation: Standalone bullish/bearish key reversal = current low < prior low / current high > prior high, current close > prior close / current close < prior close, lower / upper wick divided by max(current range, 0.0001) ≥ 0.30, close in upper / lower half of current range, and current volume divided by its selected moving average meets the configured standalone relative-volume minimum. Require the standalone key-reversal signal and key-reversal pattern to be enabled.
Long: enabled RSI bullish divergence OR bullish confluence OR standalone bullish key reversal.
Short: enabled RSI bearish divergence OR bearish confluence OR standalone bearish key reversal.
