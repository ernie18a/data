Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source since the current daily session began, since the selected anchor time, or over the selected rolling lookback; include the current bar.
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of the selected price source over the same VWAP window, including the current bar.
Long: (selected entry model is Aggressive AND low < VWAP − 2 × VWAP standard deviation AND close > VWAP − 2 × VWAP standard deviation) OR (selected entry model is Balanced AND the previous bar swept below the lower two-standard-deviation band and closed above it AND close > previous bar high) OR (selected entry model is Conservative AND the bar two bars ago swept below the lower two-standard-deviation band and closed above it AND close > the highest high of the current and previous bars AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI is above its oversold threshold)); AND more than the cooldown period has elapsed since the last signal.
Short: (selected entry model is Aggressive AND high > VWAP + 2 × VWAP standard deviation AND close < VWAP + 2 × VWAP standard deviation) OR (selected entry model is Balanced AND the previous bar swept above the upper two-standard-deviation band and closed below it AND close < previous bar low) OR (selected entry model is Conservative AND the bar two bars ago swept above the upper two-standard-deviation band and closed below it AND close < the lowest low of the current and previous bars AND (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI is below its overbought threshold)); AND more than the cooldown period has elapsed since the last signal.
