Calculation: Initial Balance high and low = highest high and lowest low during the configured Initial Balance session; lock them when that session ends
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured ATR lookback; use the current bar's value, with true range as fallback if ATR is unavailable, and at least one tick
Calculation: IB width score = peak score of (Initial Balance high − Initial Balance low) / ATR, with the configured ideal and upper width thresholds; peak score rises linearly from 0 to 100 between the lower and ideal thresholds, then falls linearly to 0 at the upper threshold, clamped to 0–100
Calculation: Relative volume score = clamp((current volume / simple moving average of volume over the configured volume lookback − 0.70) / (1.35 − 0.70) × 100, 0, 100); use 1.0 as the volume ratio if unavailable
Calculation: Volatility fit = peak score of ATR / simple moving average of ATR, using a lookback equal to the greater of three times the ATR lookback and the acceptance lookback; peak score is 0 at or below 0.55, 100 at 1.05, and 0 at or above 2.10, with linear interpolation and clamping
Calculation: Break close quality = 0.54 × normalized break distance score + 0.46 × close-location score. For an upside break, distance is close − Initial Balance high and close location is (close − low) / max(high − low, one tick); for a downside break, distance is Initial Balance low − close and close location is 1 − (close − low) / max(high − low, one tick). Normalize distance linearly from the configured break buffer to 0.72 × ATR as 0–100, clamp to 0–100
Calculation: Retest score = 100 after a retest hold; otherwise 18 if close has crossed the broken edge by more than the failure buffer toward the Initial Balance, 72 if close remains beyond the broken edge, or 46 otherwise
Calculation: Acceptance score = clamp(0.18 × IB width score + 0.30 × break close quality + 0.20 × retest score + 0.17 × relative volume score + 0.15 × volatility fit, 0, 100)
Calculation: Failure risk = clamp(34 if close is beyond the broken edge by more than the failure buffer toward the Initial Balance, plus 26 if close is beyond the Initial Balance midpoint toward the opposite side, plus 12 if bars since break exceed the acceptance lookback, plus 0.22 × (100 − current close-side score) + 0.18 × (100 − relative volume score) + 0.12 × (100 − IB width score), 0, 100); close-side score is close location × 100 for an upside break and (1 − close location) × 100 for a downside break
Long: Initial Balance is locked, tracking session is active, no break has occurred this session, and close > Initial Balance high + configured break buffer in ATR units; evaluate on confirmed bars unless live updating is configured
Long: An upside break has occurred, tracking session is active, bars since break > 0, low ≤ broken edge + configured retest buffer and close ≥ broken edge − configured failure buffer, the retest has not already held, and close has not crossed the broken edge beyond the failure buffer toward the Initial Balance; evaluate on confirmed bars unless live updating is configured
Long: An upside break has occurred, close > broken edge, acceptance score ≥ configured minimum score, failure risk ≤ 52, bars since break ≥ configured confirmation bars, no failure or acceptance was already recorded, and bar is confirmed
Short: Initial Balance is locked, tracking session is active, no break has occurred this session, and close < Initial Balance low − configured break buffer in ATR units; evaluate on confirmed bars unless live updating is configured
Short: A downside break has occurred, tracking session is active, bars since break > 0, high ≥ broken edge − configured retest buffer and close ≤ broken edge + configured failure buffer, the retest has not already held, and close has not crossed the broken edge beyond the failure buffer toward the Initial Balance; evaluate on confirmed bars unless live updating is configured
Short: A downside break has occurred, close < broken edge, acceptance score ≥ configured minimum score, failure risk ≤ 52, bars since break ≥ configured confirmation bars, no failure or acceptance was already recorded, and bar is confirmed
Exit: Long, an upside break has occurred and either close < Initial Balance midpoint or (close < broken edge − configured failure buffer and failure risk ≥ 58); no failure was already recorded and bar is confirmed
Exit: Short, a downside break has occurred and either close > Initial Balance midpoint or (close > broken edge + configured failure buffer and failure risk ≥ 58); no failure was already recorded and bar is confirmed
