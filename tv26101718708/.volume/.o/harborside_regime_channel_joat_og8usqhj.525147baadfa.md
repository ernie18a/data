Calculation: Structural center = EMA of the midpoint between the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low; use current bar data and confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Upper/lower band = EMA of structural center ± ATR × band-width multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish HTF bias = on the selected higher timeframe, fast EMA > slow EMA and the last completed HTF close > fast EMA.
Calculation: Bearish HTF bias = on the selected higher timeframe, fast EMA < slow EMA and the last completed HTF close < fast EMA.
Calculation: Bull regime = set on a confirmed close crossover above the upper band when the HTF filter is disabled or bullish HTF bias holds; set to bear on the corresponding lower-band cross when disabled or bearish HTF bias holds; initialize on the first confirmed bar as bull if close ≥ structural center, otherwise bear; persist until a qualifying opposing cross.
Long: confirmed close crosses above the upper band AND (HTF filter disabled OR bullish HTF bias) OR confirmed close crosses above the structural center OR bullish HTF bias OR (bull regime AND volume / EMA(volume, volume lookback) ≥ volume threshold) OR (bull regime AND bars in bull regime ≥ confirmation bars) OR compression AND confirmed close crosses above the upper band.
Short: confirmed close crosses below the lower band AND (HTF filter disabled OR bearish HTF bias) OR confirmed close crosses below the structural center OR bearish HTF bias OR (bear regime AND volume / EMA(volume, volume lookback) ≥ volume threshold) OR (bear regime AND bars in bear regime ≥ confirmation bars) OR compression AND confirmed close crosses below the lower band.
