Calculation: Bull score = sum of 14 votes: fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA; RSI > 50 and below its overbought level; MACD line crosses above signal line or is above both signal line and zero; Stochastic RSI %K crosses above %D while %K is below its overbought level; ADX >= its threshold and +DI > -DI; CCI crosses above its oversold level or is above zero and below its overbought level; close > Bollinger middle band and close <= upper band; Williams %R crosses above its oversold level; volume > its moving average; Supertrend is bullish; close > rolling VWAP; squeeze momentum > 0 and rising or a squeeze has just released; close > Parabolic SAR; MFI is below its oversold level or crosses above it. Each true condition contributes 1.
Calculation: Bear score = sum of 14 votes: fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA; RSI < 50 and above its oversold level; MACD line crosses below signal line or is below both signal line and zero; Stochastic RSI %K crosses below %D while %K is above its oversold level; ADX >= its threshold and -DI > +DI; CCI crosses below its overbought level or is below zero and above its oversold level; close < Bollinger middle band and close >= lower band; Williams %R crosses below its overbought level; volume > its moving average; Supertrend is bearish; close < rolling VWAP; squeeze momentum < 0 and falling or a squeeze has just released; close < Parabolic SAR; MFI is above its overbought level or crosses below it. Each true condition contributes 1.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the same lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 50 when the high-low range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish momentum trigger = MACD line crosses above signal line OR RSI crosses above its oversold level OR Stochastic RSI %K crosses above %D while below its overbought level OR Williams %R crosses above its oversold level OR Supertrend turns bullish OR close turns above Parabolic SAR.
Calculation: Bearish momentum trigger = MACD line crosses below signal line OR RSI crosses below its overbought level OR Stochastic RSI %K crosses below %D while above its oversold level OR Williams %R crosses below its overbought level OR Supertrend turns bearish OR close turns below Parabolic SAR.
Calculation: Long regular exit count = count of true conditions: RSI crosses below its overbought level; MACD line crosses below signal line; Stochastic RSI %K is above its overbought level and crosses below %D; close >= upper Bollinger band and bear score >= 6; CCI crosses below its overbought level; Williams %R crosses below its overbought level; MFI crosses below its overbought level; close crosses below rolling VWAP.
Calculation: Short regular exit count = count of true conditions: RSI crosses above its oversold level; MACD line crosses above signal line; Stochastic RSI %K is below its oversold level and crosses above %D; close <= lower Bollinger band and bull score >= 6; CCI crosses above its oversold level; Williams %R crosses above its oversold level; MFI crosses above its oversold level; close crosses above rolling VWAP.
Long: Choppiness Index <= its threshold AND bull score >= its minimum AND bullish momentum trigger AND bear score <= bull score AND last signal was not long.
Short: Choppiness Index <= its threshold AND bear score >= its minimum AND bearish momentum trigger AND bull score <= bear score AND last signal was not short.
Exit: long, bars since entry >= 3 AND (Supertrend turns bearish OR close turns below Parabolic SAR OR long regular exit count >= 2).
Exit: short, bars since entry >= 3 AND (Supertrend turns bullish OR close turns above Parabolic SAR OR short regular exit count >= 2).
