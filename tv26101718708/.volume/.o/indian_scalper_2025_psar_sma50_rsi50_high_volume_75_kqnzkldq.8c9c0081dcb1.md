Long: close > simple moving average of close AND parabolic SAR crosses above close AND relative strength index is at or below its midpoint AND high volume
Short: close < simple moving average of close AND parabolic SAR crosses below close AND relative strength index is at or above its midpoint AND high volume
Exit: long, close reaches the profit target above entry price OR falls to the stop below entry price
Exit: short, close reaches the profit target below entry price OR rises to the stop above entry price
