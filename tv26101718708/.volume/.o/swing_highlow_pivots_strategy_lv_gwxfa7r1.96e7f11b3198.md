Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs in equal-length windows before and after it, known after the following window completes
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows in equal-length windows before and after it, known after the following window completes
Calculation: Long entry limit = the lower of the open and close of the swing-low bar
Calculation: Short entry limit = the higher of the open and close of the swing-high bar
Long: confirmed swing low AND close > moving average of close AND position is flat AND high >= long entry limit
Long: confirmed swing low AND lower than previous confirmed swing low
Long: confirmed swing low AND higher than previous confirmed swing low
Short: confirmed swing high AND close < moving average of close AND position is flat AND low <= short entry limit
Short: confirmed swing high AND higher than previous confirmed swing high
Short: confirmed swing high AND lower than previous confirmed swing high
Exit: long, high >= take-profit OR low <= stop-loss
Exit: short, low <= take-profit OR high >= stop-loss
