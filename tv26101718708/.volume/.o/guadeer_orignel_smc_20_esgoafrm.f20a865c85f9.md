Calculation: Internal pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in the same configurable shorter number of bars on each side; use only after the right-side bars complete. Swing pivot high/low = the same calculation with a configurable longer lookback.
Calculation: Bullish gap = on a completed bar of the selected gap timeframe, the current low is above the high two bars earlier. Bearish gap = the current high is below the low two bars earlier. Use the selected gap type (fair value gap, volume imbalance, or opening gap) and its corresponding gap comparison.
Calculation: Accumulation pattern = the latest four alternating internal pivots are low, high, low, high, with the latest low higher than the prior low and the latest high lower than the prior high. Distribution pattern = the latest four alternating internal pivots are high, low, high, low, with the latest high lower than the prior high and the latest low higher than the prior low. The slower pattern uses six alternating pivots and requires the same successive higher lows/lower highs for accumulation, or lower highs/higher lows for distribution.
Long: Close crosses above the latest valid internal or swing pivot high, with at least two stored opposite-side pivots; this marks bullish BOS, CHoCH, or CHoCH+ according to the prior structure direction and pivot progression; OR a bullish gap forms; OR an accumulation pattern forms; OR price enters an active bullish order block.
Short: Close crosses below the latest valid internal or swing pivot low, with at least two stored opposite-side pivots; this marks bearish BOS, CHoCH, or CHoCH+ according to the prior structure direction and pivot progression; OR a bearish gap forms; OR a distribution pattern forms; OR price enters an active bearish order block.
