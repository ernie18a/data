Calculation: Relative volume = current selected volume divided by the average selected volume of the preceding completed chart bars.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute close-open divided by high-low (1 if range is zero).
Calculation: Lower wick fraction = (min(open, close)-low) divided by high-low (0 if range is zero).
Calculation: Upper wick fraction = (high-max(open, close)) divided by high-low (0 if range is zero).
Calculation: Estimated up/down volume = volume from lower-timeframe candles in the chart bar with close above/below open; if unavailable, use chart-bar volume according to chart-bar close versus open. Split doji volume equally between up and down.
