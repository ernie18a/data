Calculation: Swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed by equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Fair value gap = a three-bar gap: bullish when current low > high two bars ago; bearish when current high < low two bars ago.
Calculation: IFVG = a fair value gap whose price has crossed beyond its far boundary: bullish when close > the bearish gap’s top; bearish when close < the bullish gap’s bottom.
Long: A swing low is swept (low < level AND close > level), OR a previously broken swing high is retested (prior close > level AND current low < level AND close > level), OR a bearish fair value gap completes its inversion (close > its top), OR price first touches the top of an active bullish sweep zone (high >= zone top), OR price first touches an active bullish order block, OR price rejects an active support zone (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top), OR after a bullish sweep, within the validity window, close exceeds the sweep bar’s high, OR bullish bias score >= 2.
Short: A swing high is swept (high > level AND close < level), OR a previously broken swing low is retested (prior close < level AND high > level AND close < level), OR a bullish fair value gap completes its inversion (close < its bottom), OR price first touches the top of an active bearish sweep zone (high >= zone top), OR price first touches an active bearish order block, OR price rejects an active resistance zone (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top AND close < zone bottom), OR after a bearish sweep, within the validity window, close falls below the sweep bar’s low, OR bearish bias score <= -2.
