Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA of close − longer-lookback EMA of close
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) / longer-lookback EMA of volume
Long: MACD > 0 AND previous MACD ≤ 0 AND volume oscillator > 0 AND volume > previous bar volume / 2
Short: MACD < 0 AND previous MACD ≥ 0 AND volume oscillator > 0 AND volume < previous bar volume / 2
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance
Calculation: Take-profit distance = 1.1 × stop distance
