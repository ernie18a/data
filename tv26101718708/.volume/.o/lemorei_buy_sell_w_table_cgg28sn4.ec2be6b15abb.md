Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the initial RTH opening range; compare only after that range is complete
Calculation: Stop loss = the higher of the existing stop and fast EMA minus an ATR offset; initialize at entry as entry-bar low minus an ATR offset
Long: At least the cooldown interval has elapsed since the last long entry AND ((MACD line crosses above signal line OR (MACD line > signal line AND close crosses above fast EMA AND close was below fast EMA within a shorter lookback)) AND RSI is rising AND RSI > its moving average AND (outside the opening window, the RSI moving average is rising) AND (trend regime: ADX >= trend threshold OR ignition regime: ADX is below threshold and rising for consecutive bars, with close >= VWAP when required; or opening window with regime gate bypassed) AND close >= VWAP AND (outside the opening window, close > open in trend mode) OR (opening window AND close >= VWAP AND close > fast EMA AND (fast EMA > slow EMA when required) AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram is rising when required AND relative volume >= opening minimum AND opening gap <= maximum when enabled AND opening-range breakout when enabled AND RSI filter) OR (relative volume >= burst threshold AND high - low >= ATR times a range multiplier AND close > highest high over a longer lookback when breakout is required AND close is in the upper portion of the bar when required AND fast EMA > slow EMA when required AND RSI is rising AND RSI > its moving average AND RSI is below its cap when enabled AND MACD line is rising AND MACD histogram is rising AND close >= VWAP) AND (in trend mode, close > open unless opening-window candle confirmation is bypassed) AND (in ignition mode, close > open only when ignition candle confirmation is enabled)
Long: In a long position, enable buy-more reminders AND bars since last long entry >= minimum AND bars since last reminder >= cooldown AND no long entry signal AND regime and VWAP entry filter pass AND close > fast EMA AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI filter passes AND (MACD histogram >= 0 and rising when required) AND (ADX rising when required)
Exit: Long, low <= stop loss when wick-triggered or close <= stop loss when close-triggered
Exit: Long, (close - average entry price) times position size <= maximum loss
