Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × displacement threshold AND absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ minimum body fraction
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × displacement threshold AND absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ minimum body fraction
Calculation: Strong positive delta = estimated delta > 0 AND absolute(estimated delta) ≥ simple moving average of absolute estimated delta × delta-strength multiplier
Calculation: Strong negative delta = estimated delta < 0 AND absolute(estimated delta) ≥ simple moving average of absolute estimated delta × delta-strength multiplier
Calculation: Primary bullish FVG = among qualifying bullish gaps formed from the confirmation bar through the end of the FVG scan window, the gap with the lowest midpoint; a bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier and size ≥ ATR × minimum FVG size
Calculation: Primary bearish FVG = among qualifying bearish gaps formed from the confirmation bar through the end of the FVG scan window, the gap with the highest midpoint; a bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier and size ≥ ATR × minimum FVG size
Long: On 5-minute bars, after the configured opening range is complete, high > opening-range high AND close < opening-range high has occurred before low < opening-range low AND close > opening-range low; within the confirmation window after the second sweep, bullish displacement AND close > the highest high of the preceding structure lookback bars AND strong positive delta
Short: On 5-minute bars, after the configured opening range is complete, low < opening-range low AND close > opening-range low has occurred before high > opening-range high AND close < opening-range high; within the confirmation window after the second sweep, bearish displacement AND close < the lowest low of the preceding structure lookback bars AND strong negative delta
Long: On 5-minute bars, bullish setup confirmed AND primary bullish FVG is ready AND bar overlaps the FVG AND close > FVG midpoint AND close > open AND strong positive delta; only the first such signal per setup
Short: On 5-minute bars, bearish setup confirmed AND primary bearish FVG is ready AND bar overlaps the FVG AND close < FVG midpoint AND close < open AND strong negative delta; only the first such signal per setup

Short: On 1-minute bars, bearish setup confirmed on 5-minute bars AND primary bearish FVG is ready AND 1-minute bar overlaps the FVG AND close < FVG midpoint AND close < open AND strong negative delta; only the first such signal per setup
