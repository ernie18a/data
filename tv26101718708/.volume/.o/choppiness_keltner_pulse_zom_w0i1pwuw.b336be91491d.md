Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of 1-bar ATR over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback)
Calculation: Keltner Channel = EMA of close as center, plus or minus ATR over the same lookback multiplied by the channel multiplier
Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of sign of close change × volume
Calculation: Bullish score = count of: Choppiness Index below its threshold and either the prior value was at or above the threshold or the current value is below the prior value; close crosses above the Keltner center or close is above the upper channel; OBV is above its moving average and above its prior value; close is above open and the absolute open-close difference exceeds ATR multiplied by a thrust multiplier
Calculation: Bearish score = count of: Choppiness Index below its threshold and either the prior value was at or above the threshold or the current value is below the prior value; close crosses below the Keltner center or close is below the lower channel; OBV is below its moving average and below its prior value; close is below open and the absolute open-close difference exceeds ATR multiplied by a thrust multiplier
Long: (bullish score ≥ signal threshold − 1 AND bullish score < signal threshold AND (close crosses above Keltner center OR close > upper Keltner channel)) OR (bullish score ≥ signal threshold AND (close crosses above Keltner center OR close > upper Keltner channel) AND current bar index − prior bullish signal bar index > cooldown length)
Short: (bearish score ≥ signal threshold − 1 AND bearish score < signal threshold AND (close crosses below Keltner center OR close < lower Keltner channel)) OR (bearish score ≥ signal threshold AND (close crosses below Keltner center OR close < lower Keltner channel) AND current bar index − prior bearish signal bar index > cooldown length)
