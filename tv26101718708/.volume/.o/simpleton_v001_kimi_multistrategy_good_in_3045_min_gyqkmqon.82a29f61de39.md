Long: (RSI is below oversold and prior-bar RSI is at or below oversold and close is above the 200-period EMA) OR (Supertrend direction is below zero and prior-bar direction is above zero) OR (MACD line crosses above signal line, with fast lookback shorter than slow lookback) OR (fast EMA is above medium EMA and medium EMA is above slow EMA, with fast lookback shorter than medium and medium shorter than slow, and prior-bar or two-bars-prior fast EMA was at or below its corresponding medium EMA) OR (close is below the lower Bollinger Band and prior-bar close is at or below its prior lower band)
Short: (RSI is above overbought and prior-bar RSI is at or above overbought and close is below the 200-period EMA) OR (Supertrend direction is above zero and prior-bar direction is below zero) OR (MACD line crosses below signal line, with fast lookback shorter than slow lookback) OR (fast EMA is below medium EMA and medium EMA is below slow EMA, with fast lookback shorter than medium and medium shorter than slow, and prior-bar or two-bars-prior fast EMA was at or above its corresponding medium EMA) OR (close is above the upper Bollinger Band and prior-bar close is at or above its prior upper band)
Exit: long, high reaches close × (1 + take-profit %) OR low reaches close × (1 − stop-loss %)
Exit: short, low reaches close × (1 − take-profit %) OR high reaches close × (1 + stop-loss %)
