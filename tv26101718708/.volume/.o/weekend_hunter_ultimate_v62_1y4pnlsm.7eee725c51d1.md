Calculation: Trend alignment = the average of three direction scores: +1 when the last completed 4-hour close is above its 4-hour EMA, otherwise −1; +1 when the last completed 1-hour MACD line is above its signal line, otherwise −1; and +1 when the current close is above its current-timeframe EMA, otherwise −1. Use a longer EMA lookback for the 4-hour trend than for the current-timeframe trend.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bars closing above their open minus volume on bars closing below their open; bars closing at their open contribute zero.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = close below its earlier value while cumulative volume delta is above its earlier value. Bearish divergence = close above its earlier value while cumulative volume delta is below its earlier value. Use the same lookback for both comparisons.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume above its longer-lookback average times a surge factor, volume divided by its shorter-lookback average above a threshold, and volume above a minimum fraction of its longer-lookback average.
Calculation: Signal strength = a weighted score combining trend alignment, bullish momentum when RSI is oversold and rising or bearish momentum when RSI is overbought and falling, volume confirmation, bullish or bearish divergence, and weekend status, with additional boosts during the configured prime weekend window and on weekends.
Long: signal strength above the positive minimum threshold AND trend alignment above zero AND weekend trading is active AND no loss limit is active AND a position slot and sufficient capital are available AND flat AND the absolute close change over the flash-crash lookback is below its threshold AND the account emergency stop is inactive
Short: signal strength below the negative minimum threshold AND trend alignment below zero AND weekend trading is active AND no loss limit is active AND a position slot and sufficient capital are available AND flat AND the absolute close change over the flash-crash lookback is below its threshold AND the account emergency stop is inactive
Exit: Long, low <= the ATR-based stop OR high >= the ATR-based take-profit OR the trailing stop is hit OR absolute open-position profit percentage exceeds the leverage-based liquidation-distance threshold OR flash crash is detected OR account emergency stop is active OR datetime is Monday at 00:00
Exit: Short, high >= the ATR-based stop OR low <= the ATR-based take-profit OR the trailing stop is hit OR absolute open-position profit percentage exceeds the leverage-based liquidation-distance threshold OR flash crash is detected OR account emergency stop is active OR datetime is Monday at 00:00
