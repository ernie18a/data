Calculation: Volume profile POC, VAH, VAL = using the current-timeframe rolling lookback including the current bar, divide its lowest-to-highest price range into bins; distribute each bar’s volume across its body and wicks in proportion to their lengths, weighting each wick twice the body; POC is the bin with greatest volume, and the value area expands from POC toward the adjacent bin with greater volume until it contains the configured fraction of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower boundaries
Calculation: RSI = standard RSI of close
Calculation: Bar delta = volume if close > open, −volume if close < open, otherwise 0
Long: (close > VAH AND previous close ≤ VAH OR close > POC AND previous close ≤ POC AND RSI > 50) AND (RSI > bullish threshold if RSI confirmation is required) AND (bar delta > 0 if delta confirmation is required) AND the previous long signal is at least the configured signal gap bars ago
Short: (close < VAL AND previous close ≥ VAL OR close < POC AND previous close ≥ POC AND RSI < 50) AND (RSI < bearish threshold if RSI confirmation is required) AND (bar delta < 0 if delta confirmation is required) AND the previous short signal is at least the configured signal gap bars ago
