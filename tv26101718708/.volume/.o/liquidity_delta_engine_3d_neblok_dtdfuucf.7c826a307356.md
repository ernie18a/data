Calculation: Bar delta = aggressive buy volume − aggressive sell volume; from lower-timeframe bars, classify volume as buy when sub-bar close > open, sell when close < open, and split equally when equal; if using bar approximation, buy volume = volume × (close − low)/(high − low), sell volume = volume − buy volume (use a 0.5 split when high = low)
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of bar delta
Calculation: Average absolute delta = simple average of absolute bar delta over a lookback
Calculation: Average volume = simple average of volume over a lookback
Calculation: Local high/low = highest high / lowest low over a lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Pivot high/low = confirmed high/low with a lookback number of bars on each side; evaluate only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Active demand zone = non-overlapping zone from a qualifying confirmed pivot low, spanning from the pivot low to a height above it based on the pivot candle body and ATR, clamped between minimum and maximum ATR multiples; qualify when pivot-bar volume ≥ average volume at that pivot × a threshold; deactivate when price breaks below the zone's bottom, using close or low as configured
Calculation: Active supply zone = non-overlapping zone from a qualifying confirmed pivot high, spanning from a height below it based on the pivot candle body to the pivot high, clamped between minimum and maximum ATR multiples; qualify when pivot-bar volume ≥ average volume at that pivot × a threshold; deactivate when price breaks above the zone's top, using close or high as configured
Long: (bar closes confirmed AND delta < 0 AND −delta ≥ average absolute delta × spike threshold AND volume ≥ average volume × volume threshold AND close ≥ (high + low)/2 AND low ≤ local low AND this bullish absorption signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (bar closes confirmed AND low ≤ local low AND |delta| ≤ average absolute delta × weakness threshold AND close ≥ (high + low)/2 AND sum of delta over the preceding five bars < 0 AND this bullish exhaustion signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (a confirmed pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low AND cumulative delta at that pivot is higher than at the prior pivot low AND pivot spacing ≤ maximum gap AND bar closes confirmed AND this bullish divergence signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (bar closes confirmed AND an active demand zone is touched by the low, low ≥ zone bottom, close > zone top, delta > 0 AND this bullish rejection signal has not fired within its cooldown)
Short: (bar closes confirmed AND delta > 0 AND delta ≥ average absolute delta × spike threshold AND volume ≥ average volume × volume threshold AND close ≤ (high + low)/2 AND high ≥ local high AND this bearish absorption signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (bar closes confirmed AND high ≥ local high AND |delta| ≤ average absolute delta × weakness threshold AND close ≤ (high + low)/2 AND sum of delta over the preceding five bars > 0 AND this bearish exhaustion signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (a confirmed pivot high is higher than the prior confirmed pivot high AND cumulative delta at that pivot is lower than at the prior pivot high AND pivot spacing ≤ maximum gap AND bar closes confirmed AND this bearish divergence signal has not fired within its cooldown) OR (bar closes confirmed AND an active supply zone is touched by the high, high ≤ zone top, close < zone bottom, delta < 0 AND this bearish rejection signal has not fired within its cooldown)
