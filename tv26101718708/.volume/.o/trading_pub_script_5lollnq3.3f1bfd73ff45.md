Calculation: Highest-high offset = number of bars back to the highest high over the lookback.
Calculation: Lowest-low offset = number of bars back to the lowest low over the lookback.
Long: highest-high offset < lowest-low offset for both the shorter and longer lookbacks AND 10-bar EMA of close > VWAP AND current one-minute bar is within the configured trading session
Short: highest-high offset > lowest-low offset for both the shorter and longer lookbacks AND 10-bar EMA of close < VWAP AND current one-minute bar is within the configured trading session
