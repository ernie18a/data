Calculation: Signed volume = sum of lower-timeframe sub-bar volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1) over confirmed sub-bars, using 0.5 for zero-range sub-bars; if unavailable or disabled, chart-bar volume × (2 × Φ((close − previous close)/stdev(close − previous close, fallback lookback)) − 1), where Φ is the standard normal CDF.
Calculation: Flow oscillator = selected engine, optionally smoothed: (1) cumulative signed volume minus its EMA over the detrend lookback, divided by its residual standard deviation or by 1.4826 × median absolute deviation of residuals over the normalization lookback; (2) Wilder RSI of that residual over the RSI lookback; or (3) MFI over the MFI lookback, using typical price × volume as positive/negative money flow. Smoothing, when enabled, uses the selected EMA or Ehlers Ultimate Smoother.
Calculation: Divergence flow value at a price pivot = highest flow oscillator in a window centered on a pivot high, or lowest in a window centered on a pivot low; if oscillator-pivot selection is disabled, use the oscillator at the price pivot. The centered window uses the same pivot length on each side.
Calculation: Divergence minimum gap = magnitude threshold × standard deviation of the flow oscillator over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Volume percentile rank = percentile rank of current volume (or selected time-of-day volume ratio) over the VSA history lookback. Range percentile rank = percentile rank of current high − low over the same lookback.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous price pivot low, its divergence flow value is higher than the previous pivot's, and the flow-value gap meets the divergence minimum gap; AND, when the regular-divergence extreme filter is enabled, the current divergence flow value is at or below the oversold level. OR a confirmed price pivot low is higher than the previous price pivot low and its divergence flow value is lower than the previous pivot's by at least the divergence minimum gap (hidden bullish divergence). OR a confirmed flow-oscillator pivot low is at or below the lower extreme band. OR volume is positive, the low is at or below the highest low over the preceding VSA lookback, the close is in the upper 35% of the bar range, and volume percentile rank is at least 60. OR volume is positive, close is below the previous close, range percentile rank is at least 60, volume percentile rank is at least 80, and the close is at least 45% up from the bar low.
Short: A confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous price pivot high, its divergence flow value is lower than the previous pivot's, and the flow-value gap meets the divergence minimum gap; AND, when the regular-divergence extreme filter is enabled, the current divergence flow value is at or above the overbought level. OR a confirmed price pivot high is lower than the previous price pivot high and its divergence flow value is higher than the previous pivot's by at least the divergence minimum gap (hidden bearish divergence). OR a confirmed flow-oscillator pivot high is at or above the upper extreme band. OR volume is positive, the high is at or above the highest high over the preceding VSA lookback, the close is in the lower 35% of the bar range, and volume percentile rank is at least 60.
