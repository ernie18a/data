Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close > open, minus volume when close < open, and unchanged when close = open
Long: A confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at the pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot low OR a confirmed pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at the pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot low OR close > the active bearish-divergence pivot high while its trap window has not expired
