Calculation: Fair price and half-band = over the last N completed chart bars, weight each bar’s typical price (high + low + 2×close)/4 by 1 − abs(close−open)/(high−low); fair price is the weighted mean and half-band is the weighted mean absolute deviation from it. The outer fair band is fair price ± half-band.
Calculation: Pressure centre = weighted mean over a shorter rolling window of (close−low)/(high−low), using the same candle weights as fair price; pressure migration = current pressure centre minus the pressure centre from an earlier offset. Use the last completed chart bar for all signal calculations.
Calculation: Pressure state = upward pressure when migration is positive and pressure is building, moving up, or reversing; downward pressure when migration is negative and moving down or reversing. Building means pressure centre is at or near its upper threshold and migration is positive. Moving up/down means migration exceeds the respective positive/negative stall threshold. Reversing means the current and prior migrations exceed opposite stall thresholds, unless pressure is exhausted; exhaustion means pressure centre is above its upper extreme or below its lower extreme.
Calculation: Candle efficiency = abs(close−open)/(high−low); upper wick = high−max(open,close); lower wick = min(open,close)−low. Wick ratios are wick lengths divided by bar range.
Calculation: Directional efficiency = mean candle efficiency among bullish bars (long) or bearish bars (short) in the recent short window; directional dominance = the fraction of bars in that window that are bullish (long) or bearish (short).
Calculation: Range speed = current bar range divided by the mean range of the preceding short window. Volume ratio = current volume divided by the mean volume of the preceding longer window; if volume is unreliable (coefficient of variation over that longer window is at most a small threshold), use a neutral ratio.
Calculation: Displacement = (close−fair price)/half-band. Depth below/above fair price = greatest penetration of the lows/highs in the recent short window beyond fair price, divided by the current half-band.
Calculation: Trend efficiency = abs(close−close from the longer trend lookback) divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback; trend direction is the sign of that net change.
Calculation: Expansion = current range is at least the maximum range of the preceding short window and exceeds an ATR-based floor. ATR uses a separate volatility lookback.
Calculation: Normal bar = current bar is not both an oversized multiple of the preceding short-window average range and below a low efficiency threshold; also false if the preceding bar qualifies as such a spike and the current close lies within the preceding bar’s range.
Long: confirmed bar; no active position and rearmed; (long reaction: close is sufficiently below fair price; recent bullish-bar efficiency and bullish-bar fraction meet minimums; lower wick exceeds upper wick in both length and range ratio; range speed, volume ratio, and depth below fair price meet minimums; normal bar; upward pressure) OR (long continuation: close above the outer fair band; expansion; upper-wick ratio below its maximum; candle efficiency above one half; range speed and volume ratio meet minimums; trend is upward and trend efficiency meets its minimum; normal bar; positive migration with building or moving-up pressure). Apply adaptive failure tightening and any active same-direction, same-signal-family failure-memory block; suppress a repeated signal of the same direction and family until its market-behaviour reset condition occurs.
Short: confirmed bar; no active position and rearmed; (short reaction: close is sufficiently above fair price; recent bearish-bar efficiency and bearish-bar fraction meet minimums; upper wick exceeds lower wick in both length and range ratio; range speed, volume ratio, and depth above fair price meet minimums; normal bar; downward pressure) OR (short continuation: close below the outer fair band; expansion; lower-wick ratio below its maximum; candle efficiency above one half; range speed and volume ratio meet minimums; trend is downward and trend efficiency meets its minimum; normal bar; negative migration with moving-down pressure). Apply adaptive failure tightening and any active same-direction, same-signal-family failure-memory block; suppress a repeated signal of the same direction and family until its market-behaviour reset condition occurs.
Exit: long, low touches or falls below invalidation OR high touches or exceeds Path Level 2.
Exit: short, high touches or exceeds invalidation OR low touches or falls below Path Level 2.
