Calculation: Entry unit = ATR, standard deviation of close, or session-reset volume-weighted standard deviation, according to the selected band calculation
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset at each new daily session
Calculation: Lower and upper thresholds = selected baseline minus and plus (entry unit × initial gap), respectively
Calculation: HA bullish flip = Heikin Ashi close is above Heikin Ashi open after being at or below it on the prior bar; HA bearish flip = Heikin Ashi close is below Heikin Ashi open after being at or above it on the prior bar
Long: (Reversal mode AND close <= lower threshold OR Trending mode AND close >= upper threshold) on the required number of consecutive bars AND flat AND long trading enabled AND within the allowed session if session restriction is enabled AND confirmed bar AND not blocked by the post-exit wait condition; OR long position AND close <= last entry price − (ATR × scale-in step) AND (HA scale confirmation disabled OR HA bullish flip) AND confirmed bar AND within the allowed session if session restriction is enabled
Short: (Reversal mode AND close >= upper threshold OR Trending mode AND close <= lower threshold) on the required number of consecutive bars AND flat AND short trading enabled AND within the allowed session if session restriction is enabled AND confirmed bar AND not blocked by the post-exit wait condition; OR short position AND close >= last entry price + (ATR × scale-in step) AND (HA scale confirmation disabled OR HA bearish flip) AND confirmed bar AND within the allowed session if session restriction is enabled
Exit: Long, session restriction enabled AND outside allowed session OR unrealized open loss reaches the maximum dollar loss OR (trailing mode disabled AND (high >= average entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier OR low <= initial entry price − ATR × stop-loss multiplier)) OR (trailing mode enabled AND low <= active stop; activate trailing after high reaches average entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier, then stop = max(previous trailing stop, high − ATR × take-profit multiplier), initially max(average entry price, high − ATR × take-profit multiplier); before activation use initial entry price − ATR × stop-loss multiplier)
Exit: Short, session restriction enabled AND outside allowed session OR unrealized open loss reaches the maximum dollar loss OR (trailing mode disabled AND (low <= average entry price − ATR × take-profit multiplier OR high >= initial entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiplier)) OR (trailing mode enabled AND high >= active stop; activate trailing after low reaches average entry price − ATR × take-profit multiplier, then stop = min(previous trailing stop, low + ATR × take-profit multiplier), initially min(average entry price, low + ATR × take-profit multiplier); before activation use initial entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiplier)
