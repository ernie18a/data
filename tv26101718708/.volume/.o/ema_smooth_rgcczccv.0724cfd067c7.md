Calculation: Smoothed EMA = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of the EMA
Long: After the warm-up bar count is reached, EMA crosses above Smoothed EMA while flat, or a pending long reversal has waited at least the configured number of lower-timeframe bars since the short exit
Short: After the warm-up bar count is reached, EMA crosses below Smoothed EMA while flat, or a pending short reversal has waited at least the configured number of lower-timeframe bars since the long exit
Exit: short, after the warm-up bar count is reached and EMA crosses above Smoothed EMA
Exit: long, after the warm-up bar count is reached and EMA crosses below Smoothed EMA
