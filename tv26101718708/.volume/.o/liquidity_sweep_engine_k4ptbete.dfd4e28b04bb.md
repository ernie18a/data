Calculation: Eligible liquidity levels = confirmed chart-timeframe swing highs/lows; previous completed day, week, and month highs/lows; completed session highs/lows; confirmed higher-timeframe swings from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; completed-session value-area highs/lows and naked POCs; instrument-scaled round numbers; and unfilled fair-value-gap edges.
Calculation: Sweep = price pierces an eligible level, reaches the minimum penetration but not the maximum penetration, then closes back across the level within the reclaim window; a shallow pierce is a touch, and a deep pierce or a failure to reclaim retires the level.
Calculation: Level quality = weighted score of level origin timeframe, repeat touches, age, distance from recent price reach, and proximity to a round number.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = EMA(20) compared with EMA(50) on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Confluence score = proportion of applicable checks that pass: relative volume on the sweep, lower-timeframe delta agreement, directional displacement, structure shift, directional fair-value gap, agreeing higher-timeframe bias, sweep level in discount for a long or premium for a short, volatility rank above its floor, sweep wick on the correct side of anchored VWAP, active trading window, and SMT divergence. Active-window and SMT checks are omitted when inapplicable.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the configured ATR multiple; short stop = entry price plus the configured ATR multiple. The sweep wick plus an ATR buffer must fit within the stop distance.
Calculation: Target = a whole-number multiple of stop risk, at least 1R, in front of the selected opposing liquidity pool; select the nearest pool at standard confluence, the nearest major pool at high confluence, and the next major pool at very high confluence when available.
Long: On a confirmed bar, a sweep of a low-side eligible liquidity level has level quality above its minimum; within the confirmation window and before price invalidates the sweep extreme, directional displacement OR a bullish structure shift occurs; the confluence score meets its threshold; the bar is outside blocked news minutes; the sweep wick fits within the stop distance; no sweep trade is open; and the cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a confirmed bar, a sweep of a high-side eligible liquidity level has level quality above its minimum; within the confirmation window and before price invalidates the sweep extreme, directional displacement OR a bearish structure shift occurs; the confluence score meets its threshold; the bar is outside blocked news minutes; the sweep wick fits within the stop distance; no sweep trade is open; and the cooldown has elapsed.
Long: When the optional front-of-market entry is enabled and no entry is open, higher-timeframe bias is bullish OR (higher-timeframe bias is unavailable AND close is at or above anchored VWAP).
Short: When the optional front-of-market entry is enabled and no entry is open, higher-timeframe bias is bearish OR (higher-timeframe bias is unavailable AND close is below anchored VWAP).
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price OR elapsed bars since entry >= the configured holding limit multiplied by the lesser of target R and the holding cap.
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price OR elapsed bars since entry >= the configured holding limit multiplied by the lesser of target R and the holding cap.
