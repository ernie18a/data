Calculation: Current-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: 15-minute EMA = exponential moving average of close on the latest available 15-minute bar, using lookahead off (last completed bar in historical data).
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume; compare prior-bar volume with prior-bar average volume.
Long: Flat position AND within the New York 02:00–05:00 or 09:30–11:00 session AND prior-bar volume >= prior-bar average volume × volume multiplier AND (close > current-timeframe EMA when the EMA filter is enabled) AND (9:30-bar close > its open, or the morning-bias filter is disabled) AND close > 15-minute EMA AND at least one eligible level has low within the level ± sweep zone AND close > open AND close > prior close. Eligible levels are the 07:30, 08:00, 09:30, 10:00, or 18:00 New York session bar opens (each usable once per day), previous completed daily high or low, or—on Mondays only—previous completed weekly high or low.
Short: Flat position AND within the New York 02:00–05:00 or 09:30–11:00 session AND prior-bar volume >= prior-bar average volume × volume multiplier AND (close < current-timeframe EMA when the EMA filter is enabled) AND (9:30-bar close < its open, or the morning-bias filter is disabled) AND close < 15-minute EMA AND no long condition AND at least one eligible level has high within the level ± sweep zone AND close < open AND close < prior close. Eligible levels are the 07:30, 08:00, 09:30, 10:00, or 18:00 New York session bar opens (each usable once per day), previous completed daily high or low, or—on Mondays only—previous completed weekly high or low.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= long target 1 OR high >= long target 2 OR bars in trade >= time-exit threshold OR forced end-of-day close.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= short target 1 OR low <= short target 2 OR bars in trade >= time-exit threshold OR forced end-of-day close.
