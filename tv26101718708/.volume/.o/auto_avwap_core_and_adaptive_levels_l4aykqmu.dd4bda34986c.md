Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = stochastic of RSI; smooth it into K, then take a configurable moving average of K for D.
Calculation: Active resistance/support AVWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of High/Low from its confirmed high/low anchor, or HLC3 for both if selected; update each bar while active. Resistance anchors arise from an overbought episode, a candidate high, bearish K/D cross, downward ATR reaction, and the selected reaction/pivot/structure confirmation; support anchors use the inverse oversold, low, bullish cross, upward reaction, and confirmation. Adaptive anchors additionally require sufficient distance and elapsed bars from the newest active level, sufficient anchor spacing (and progressively lower highs/higher lows if enabled), and an available slot. Stop a level after the configured number of consecutive violations: resistance source above it, support source below it; source is configurable as Close, Open, or High for resistance/Low for support. A non-violating bar resets the count.
Long: At least one active support AVWAP is touched within the ATR tolerance and Close >= that AVWAP + the ATR rejection distance, OR its violation count was at least the configured partial minimum and below the full invalidation threshold and the current bar's violation source is at or above it.
Short: At least one active resistance AVWAP is touched within the ATR tolerance and Close <= that AVWAP - the ATR rejection distance, OR its violation count was at least the configured partial minimum and below the full invalidation threshold and the current bar's violation source is at or below it.
Exit: Long, an active support AVWAP reaches its configured consecutive-violation threshold with the violation source below it.
Exit: Short, an active resistance AVWAP reaches its configured consecutive-violation threshold with the violation source above it.
