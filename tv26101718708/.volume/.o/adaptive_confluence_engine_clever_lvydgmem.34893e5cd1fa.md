Calculation: Bullish score = sum 2 if close > long-term EMA; 1 if short-term EMA > medium-term EMA; 2 if Supertrend is bullish; 2 if MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0; 2 if RSI is between 45 and 70; 1 if smoothed stochastic %K > %D and %K < 80; 1 if ADX > 20 and +DI > −DI; 1 if volume > its moving average; 2 if the higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA or the higher-timeframe filter is disabled; 1 if bullish engulfing (close > prior open, open < prior close, and prior close < prior open); and 1 if ATR > 0.8 × its historical average.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum 2 if close < long-term EMA; 1 if short-term EMA < medium-term EMA; 2 if Supertrend is bearish; 2 if MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; 2 if RSI is between 30 and 55; 1 if smoothed stochastic %K < %D and %K > 20; 1 if ADX > 20 and −DI > +DI; 1 if volume > its moving average; 2 if the higher-timeframe close < its long-term EMA or the higher-timeframe filter is disabled; 1 if bearish engulfing (close < prior open, open > prior close, and prior close > prior open); and 1 if ATR > 0.8 × its historical average. Use the latest available higher-timeframe bar; use shorter-term EMA for the crossover and longer-term EMA for trend alignment.
Long: short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA AND bullish score ≥ selected signal threshold AND position is not already long
Short: short-term EMA crosses below medium-term EMA AND bearish score ≥ selected signal threshold AND position is not already short
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × third-target multiplier OR (low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × stop-loss multiplier AND no bar since entry has reached entry price + ATR at entry × first-target multiplier)
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × third-target multiplier OR (high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × stop-loss multiplier AND no bar since entry has reached entry price − ATR at entry × first-target multiplier)
