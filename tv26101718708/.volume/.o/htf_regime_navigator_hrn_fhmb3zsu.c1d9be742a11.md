Calculation: Signal EMA status = ABOVE if close exceeds the EMA by the configured tolerance without the bar spanning it; BELOW if close is below it by the configured below tolerance; otherwise TESTING. Apply the same tolerance parameters to every included signal EMA.
Calculation: Long structure = at least one signal EMA is included and none is BELOW.
Calculation: Short structure = at least one signal EMA is included and every signal EMA is BELOW, except EMA 1 may be TESTING; in symmetric mode, none may be ABOVE.
Calculation: EMA slope = (current EMA − its value the configured number of bars earlier) / absolute value of that earlier EMA. Use the configured lookback on each EMA’s own timeframe; require the configured minimum slope magnitude. EMA timeframes and lengths may differ.
Calculation: Fresh long/short license = a recent structure reclaim (no signal EMA BELOW, with at least one BELOW on the prior bar), a newly formed qualified EMA stack, or a recent qualified spring/upthrust, respectively. A stack uses signal EMAs ranked by effective length, shortest first, with duplicate effective lengths folded; require strict descending order for longs and ascending order for shorts, and the configured stack qualification and freshness. Reclaim and Wyckoff freshness windows and first-K reclaim test episodes use their respective configured parameters.
Calculation: Fib signal zone = the interval between the configured shallow and deep retracements of the selected high-low anchors, oriented by regime (or the configured display-fib direction). Anchors use the selected range, swing, or lookback source; range and swing pivots are confirmed after their right-side bars, while lookback extremes include the current bar.
Calculation: EMA-Fib confluence = selected EMA(s) lie within or near the 38.2–61.8% retracement zone by the configured fraction of anchor range, satisfying the configured Any/All EMA rule, optional price-in-zone rule, and optional slope-direction rule. EMA slope compares current EMA with its value the configured chart-bar lookback earlier.
Calculation: Qualified spring/upthrust = price pierces the range low/high by no more than the configured fraction of range height, then closes back above/below that boundary within the reclaim window; the range is not already broken in that direction, undercut/overshoot volume passes the configured qualification, and the cooldown has elapsed. Use the range-timeframe fractal boundaries, or its configured lookback fallback; range-timeframe close and fallback extremes use the current forming bar.
Calculation: Regular bullish/bearish RSI divergence = on the divergence timeframe, a confirmed pivot low/high makes a lower/higher price extreme while RSI makes a higher/lower extreme than at the preceding same-kind pivot. RSI and pivots use the current forming divergence-timeframe bar; pivots become signals only after their right-side bars confirm.
Long: Qualified spring.
Short: Qualified upthrust.

Long: Long structure AND configured long slope alignment/freshness and stack-order gates pass AND price satisfies the configured fib-zone checks and required EMA-Fib confluence/price-in-zone gates AND no higher-timeframe downtrend veto or regime-agreement veto applies AND optional volume/score gates pass AND (a fresh reclaim bar triggers, or a qualifying EMA 1 test episode occurs under a fresh license, according to the configured trigger mode) AND long structure holds for the configured confirmation closes. A fresh license bypasses slope and zone gates only when strict mode is off.
Short: Short structure AND configured short slope alignment/freshness and stack-order gates pass AND price satisfies the configured fib-zone checks and required EMA-Fib confluence/price-in-zone gates AND no higher-timeframe uptrend veto or regime-agreement veto applies AND optional volume/score gates pass AND (a fresh reclaim bar triggers, or a qualifying EMA 1 test episode occurs under a fresh license, according to the configured trigger mode) AND short structure holds for the configured confirmation closes. A fresh license bypasses slope and zone gates only when strict mode is off.
Exit: Long, close falls below the configured invalidation reference (with buffer for EMA references) OR long structure is lost.
Exit: Short, close rises above the configured invalidation reference (with buffer for EMA references) OR short structure is lost.
