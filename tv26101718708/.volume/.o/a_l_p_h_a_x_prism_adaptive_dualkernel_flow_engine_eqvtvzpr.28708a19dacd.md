Calculation: Slow and fast kernel estimates = kernel-weighted averages of close over the same lookback, using the selected kernel weights; the fast estimate uses a shorter bandwidth and shorter EMA smoothing than the slow estimate. Scale bandwidth by the ATR percentile rank when adaptive bandwidth is enabled.
Calculation: Residual = close − slow kernel; residual deviation = EMA of the standard deviation of residual over its lookback; upper/lower bands = slow kernel ± configured multiple of residual deviation; Z-score = residual ÷ residual deviation.
Calculation: Kernel spread = fast kernel − slow kernel. Bull spread = spread > 0 and greater than its prior-bar value; bear spread = spread < 0 and less than its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback ÷ sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback. Choppiness index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over its lookback ÷ high-low range over that lookback) ÷ log10(lookback).
Calculation: Regime = Chop if choppiness index is at or above its threshold; otherwise Trend if efficiency ratio is at or above its threshold and absolute kernel spread exceeds 0.15 × residual deviation (Bull if spread > 0, Bear otherwise); otherwise Balance.
Calculation: Bull rejection = close > open and (close − low) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment) > 0.52. Bear rejection = close < open and (high − close) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment) > 0.52.
Calculation: Bull divergence = current low below the latest confirmed pivot low, slow kernel above its value at that pivot, and close ≤ lower band + residual deviation. Bear divergence = current high above the latest confirmed pivot high, slow kernel below its value at that pivot, and close ≥ upper band − residual deviation. Confirm pivots using the configured number of bars on each side; compare the kernel at the pivot bar. Apply the configured per-direction divergence cooldown.
Calculation: Bull score = 1 each for (Trend Bull or bull spread), (Z-score > 0 or kernel spread > 0), aligned higher-timeframe bias, volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier, bull rejection, not Chop, and at least one qualifying long setup. Bear score uses the corresponding bearish conditions. Higher-timeframe bias uses the close and fast/slow EMAs of the current higher-timeframe bar (still forming until it closes): Bull when fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; Bear when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA.
Long: (Trend Bull AND close crosses above upper band AND bull spread AND bull rejection) OR (Trend Bull AND low ≤ slow kernel + 0.25 × ATR AND close > slow kernel AND bull spread AND bull rejection) OR (Balance AND Z-score ≤ −configured fade threshold AND bull rejection) OR (bull divergence AND bull rejection AND (Balance OR Trend Bear)); AND bull score ≥ configured minimum; AND not Chop; AND aligned higher-timeframe bias and volume filter if enabled; AND configured session active if enabled; AND signal cooldown elapsed.
Short: (Trend Bear AND close crosses below lower band AND bear spread AND bear rejection) OR (Trend Bear AND high ≥ slow kernel − 0.25 × ATR AND close < slow kernel AND bear spread AND bear rejection) OR (Balance AND Z-score ≥ configured fade threshold AND bear rejection) OR (bear divergence AND bear rejection AND (Balance OR Trend Bull)); AND bear score ≥ configured minimum; AND not Chop; AND aligned higher-timeframe bias and volume filter if enabled; AND configured session active if enabled; AND signal cooldown elapsed.
Long: Bull divergence.
Short: Bear divergence.
Exit: long or short, choppiness index ≥ its Chop threshold.
