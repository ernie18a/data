Calculation: Normalized volume = current volume as a percentage of the maximum volume over its lookback.
Calculation: Support level = the latest prior bar low that was the lowest low over its lookback, confirmed when the following bar's low is higher.
Calculation: Resistance level = the latest prior bar high that was the highest high over its lookback, confirmed when the following bar's high is lower.
Long: Normalized volume > support high-volume threshold AND a support level has been established.
Short: Normalized volume > resistance high-volume threshold AND a resistance level has been established.
