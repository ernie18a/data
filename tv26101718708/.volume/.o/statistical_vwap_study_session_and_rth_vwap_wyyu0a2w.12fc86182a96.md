Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of the selected price source from the latest session start or bar making a new session high through the current bar; it reanchors when a new session high occurs.
Calculation: RTH VWAP = volume-weighted average of its selected price source from the RTH opening bar through the current bar.
Long: At the RTH close, RTH VWAP > Session VWAP AND closing price > RTH opening price
Short: At the RTH close, RTH VWAP ≤ Session VWAP AND closing price ≤ RTH opening price
