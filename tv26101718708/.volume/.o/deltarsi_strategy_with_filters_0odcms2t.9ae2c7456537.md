Calculation: D-RSI = derivative at the newest point of a least-squares polynomial fitted to the RSI(close, configurable length) values over a configurable lookback window
Calculation: D-RSI signal line = EMA of D-RSI over a configurable smoothing length
Long: Long entries enabled AND (entry mode = zero-crossing AND D-RSI crosses above 0 OR entry mode = signal-line crossing AND D-RSI crosses above its signal line OR entry mode = direction change AND D-RSI > previous D-RSI AND previous D-RSI < prior D-RSI AND previous D-RSI < 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > multiplier × its longer-lookback average) AND (volatility filter disabled OR shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR) AND (RSI filter disabled OR lower bound < RSI(14) < upper bound)
Short: Short entries enabled AND (entry mode = zero-crossing AND D-RSI crosses below 0 OR entry mode = signal-line crossing AND D-RSI crosses below its signal line OR entry mode = direction change AND D-RSI < previous D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > prior D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > multiplier × its longer-lookback average) AND (volatility filter disabled OR shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR) AND (RSI filter disabled OR lower bound < RSI(14) < upper bound)
Exit: long, configured exit mode's bearish trigger (D-RSI crosses below 0 OR crosses below its signal line OR D-RSI < previous D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > prior D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > 0) AND (exit filters disabled OR volume > multiplier × its longer-lookback average AND shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR AND lower bound < RSI(14) < upper bound)
Exit: short, configured exit mode's bearish trigger (D-RSI crosses below 0 OR crosses below its signal line OR D-RSI < previous D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > prior D-RSI AND previous D-RSI > 0) AND (exit filters disabled OR volume > multiplier × its longer-lookback average AND shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR AND lower bound < RSI(14) < upper bound)
Exit: long, low <= fixed or trailing stop price OR high >= take-profit price OR datetime outside the configured backtest period
Exit: short, high >= fixed or trailing stop price OR low <= take-profit price OR datetime outside the configured backtest period
