Long: chart-timeframe fast EMA (shorter lookback) crosses above chart-timeframe slow EMA (longer lookback) AND the most recent crossover of those EMAs on higher-timeframe bars is upward AND the close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is above that bar’s VWAP AND the datetime is within a configured trading session AND the daily drawdown limit has not been reached
Short: chart-timeframe fast EMA (shorter lookback) crosses below chart-timeframe slow EMA (longer lookback) AND the most recent crossover of those EMAs on higher-timeframe bars is downward AND the close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below that bar’s VWAP AND the datetime is within a configured trading session AND the daily drawdown limit has not been reached
Exit: long, low reaches the stop level OR high reaches the take-profit level; levels are based on the entry-bar close and the last completed higher-timeframe ATR
Exit: short, high reaches the stop level OR low reaches the take-profit level; levels are based on the entry-bar close and the last completed higher-timeframe ATR
