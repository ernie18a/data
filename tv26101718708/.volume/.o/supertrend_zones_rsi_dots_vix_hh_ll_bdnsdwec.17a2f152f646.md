Calculation: confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than the highs of the configured bars to its left and right; confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: confirmed swing low = a bar low lower than the lows of the configured bars to its left and right; confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: RSI swing high/low = the same confirmed-pivot calculation applied to RSI
Long: Supertrend is in an uptrend
Long: Supertrend is in an uptrend AND the selected signal (raw RSI or its configured moving average) crosses above 50
Long: confirmed swing low is the first swing low or is at least as high as the previous confirmed swing low
Long: confirmed swing high is higher than the previous confirmed swing high
Long: RSI forms a higher confirmed swing low than its previous confirmed swing low AND price forms a lower low at the corresponding bars AND the pivots are within the configured separation range
Short: Supertrend is in a downtrend
Short: Supertrend is in a downtrend AND the selected signal (raw RSI or its configured moving average) crosses below 50
Short: confirmed swing low is lower than the previous confirmed swing low
Short: confirmed swing high is the first swing high or is no higher than the previous confirmed swing high
Short: RSI forms a lower confirmed swing high than its previous confirmed swing high AND price forms a higher high at the corresponding bars AND the pivots are within the configured separation range
