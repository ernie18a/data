Calculation: Previous-day high/low = the high/low of the last completed daily bar; previous-day volume = the sum of volume in that bar.
Calculation: Daily range average = the average high-minus-low of the last completed daily bars over a lookback.
Calculation: PDH/PDL strength score = weighted average, scaled to 0–10, of capped prior-day volume / average-volume ratio, prior-day range / daily-range-average ratio, rejection fraction, distance-from-round-number score, and isolation score. For PDH, rejection fraction = (previous-day high − close) / range; for PDL = (close − previous-day low) / range. Isolation score decreases as the level lies within 10% of daily range average of any of the five most recent earlier daily highs (PDH) or lows (PDL). Round-number distance score increases with distance from the nearest round number.
Calculation: Sweep trap levels = PDH plus / PDL minus an offset based on daily range average; breakout levels = PDH plus / PDL minus a larger offset based on daily range average.
Long: PDH strength score ≥ 7 AND a prior bar reached or exceeded the PDH sweep trap level AND current close > PDH AND current close is below the PDH breakout level
Long: (PDH strength score ≥ 3 AND < 6.5 OR previous-day volume is below its average) AND current close > PDH breakout level AND current volume > previous bar volume
Long: After a prior close above the PDH breakout level, current low ≤ PDH AND current close > PDH
Short: PDL strength score ≥ 7 AND a prior bar reached or fell below the PDL sweep trap level AND current close < PDL AND current close is above the PDL breakout level
Short: (PDL strength score ≥ 3 AND < 6.5 OR previous-day volume is below its average) AND current close < PDL breakout level AND current volume > previous bar volume
Short: After a prior close below the PDL breakout level, current high ≥ PDL AND current close < PDL
