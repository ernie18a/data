Calculation: Upper-zone volumes = sum buy, sell, and total volume across the highest configured number of footprint price rows; upper delta = upper-zone buy volume − upper-zone sell volume; upper sell imbalance = any inspected row has a sell imbalance
Calculation: Lower-zone volumes = sum buy, sell, and total volume across the lowest configured number of footprint price rows; lower delta = lower-zone buy volume − lower-zone sell volume; lower buy imbalance = any inspected row has a buy imbalance
Calculation: Average footprint-row volume = bar total volume / number of footprint rows
Calculation: Short score = sum of: reclaim distance ≥ configured ATR fraction; upper rejection wick / bar range ≥ configured fraction; upper sell imbalance; upper delta ≤ 0 or buyer exhaustion or buyer absorption; bar volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average; high within configured tolerance of the previous completed day’s high
Calculation: Long score = sum of: reclaim distance ≥ configured ATR fraction; lower rejection wick / bar range ≥ configured fraction; lower buy imbalance; lower delta ≥ 0 or seller exhaustion or seller absorption; bar volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average; low within configured tolerance of the previous completed day’s low
Calculation: Buyer exhaustion = upper-zone buy volume ≤ configured fraction of point-of-control-row buy volume; buyer absorption = upper-zone total volume ≥ configured multiple of average footprint-row volume AND upper delta > 0
Calculation: Seller exhaustion = lower-zone sell volume ≤ configured fraction of point-of-control-row sell volume; seller absorption = lower-zone total volume ≥ configured multiple of average footprint-row volume AND lower delta < 0
Calculation: Short reference = highest enabled swept level among the most recent confirmed swing high, the session high from bars before the current bar, and the previous completed day’s high
Calculation: Long reference = lowest enabled swept level among the most recent confirmed swing low, the session low from bars before the current bar, and the previous completed day’s low
Long: footprint data available AND low < long reference − configured sweep depth AND close > long reference AND (session level swept OR (swing or prior-day level swept AND fresh local low required or low < the prior configured lookback’s lowest low)) AND (orderflow rejection not required OR lower buy imbalance OR lower delta ≥ 0 OR seller exhaustion OR seller absorption) AND long score ≥ configured minimum AND bar confirmed AND (one signal per level disabled OR this reference differs from the last fired long reference)
