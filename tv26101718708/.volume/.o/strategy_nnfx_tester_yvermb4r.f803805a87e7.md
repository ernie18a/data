Calculation: Baseline = selected moving-average type applied to close; use the same type and lookback for its high and low bands
Calculation: Continuation SSL state = +1 when close is above the selected moving average of highs, −1 when close is below the selected moving average of lows, otherwise retain the prior state; use a shorter lookback than the baseline
Calculation: Exit SSL state = +1 when close is above the selected exit moving average of highs, −1 when close is below the selected exit moving average of lows, otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: ATR = selected moving average of true range
Calculation: C1 state = selected C1 condition: Accelerator Oscillator rising/falling; Vortex positive greater than negative and above its minimum threshold / negative at least positive and above its minimum threshold; QQE smoothed RSI minus 50 above its upper Bollinger band / below its lower Bollinger band; Aroon upper greater than lower / upper less than lower; SSL high/low moving-average state bullish/bearish; DeMarker crosses above/below zero; Squeeze Momentum crosses above/below zero; Price Momentum Oscillator crosses above/below its signal; Anchored Momentum crosses above/below its signal; WaveTrend crosses above/below zero; or close crosses above/below its ATR-based trailing stop
Calculation: C2 state = selected C2 condition: smoothed bullish strength falling / smoothed bearish strength falling; three moving averages strictly ordered short-to-long upward / downward; prior Supertrend state bullish / bearish; smoothed RSI minus 50 above its positive threshold / below its negative threshold; KDJ J above the mean of K and D / not above it; SSL high/low moving-average state bullish/bearish; Kuskus Starlight above/below zero; Ergodic CCI crosses above/below its signal; Coppock curve above/below zero; close above/below Trend Magic; or Williams %R below/above −50
Calculation: Damiani volume block = Damiani Volameter below zero on the selected timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Waddah Attar volume block = Bollinger-band width at or below the dead-zone threshold, or the MACD change is zero
Calculation: Entry confirmation = selected C1 state and selected C2 state; either confirmation passes automatically when its use is disabled
Long: (baseline use disabled OR close > Baseline) AND (baseline use disabled OR Baseline > close − ATR × baseline-distance limit) AND entry confirmation AND NOT volume block AND (movement filter disabled OR close > open) AND (one-candle rule disabled OR the second consecutive bar with close > Baseline) AND (bridge rule disabled OR confirmation has held for no more than seven consecutive bars) AND (volatility filter disabled OR prior completed daily EVZ close is at least its threshold); OR continuation: baseline and continuation SSL enabled AND close > Baseline AND close > bullish continuation SSL line AND entry confirmation AND (continuation movement filter disabled OR close > open) AND bullish-baseline duration exceeds long-exit duration AND volatility filter passes
Short: (baseline use disabled OR close < Baseline) AND (baseline use disabled OR Baseline < close + ATR × baseline-distance limit) AND entry confirmation AND NOT volume block AND (movement filter disabled OR close < open) AND (one-candle rule disabled OR the second consecutive bar with close < Baseline) AND (bridge rule disabled OR confirmation has held for no more than seven consecutive bars) AND (volatility filter disabled OR prior completed daily EVZ close is at least its threshold); OR continuation: baseline and continuation SSL enabled AND close < Baseline AND close < bearish continuation SSL line AND entry confirmation AND (continuation movement filter disabled OR close < open) AND bearish-baseline duration exceeds short-exit duration AND volatility filter passes
Exit: long, exit use enabled AND (selected exit condition: close crosses below exit SSL line with close above Baseline when baseline is enabled; or selected C1 is not bullish; or selected C2 is not bullish; OR baseline is enabled and close crosses below Baseline)
Exit: short, exit use enabled AND (selected exit condition: close crosses above exit SSL line with close below Baseline when baseline is enabled; or selected C1 is not bearish; or selected C2 is not bearish; OR baseline is enabled and close crosses above Baseline)
Exit: long, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × target multiple
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry × target multiple
