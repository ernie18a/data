Calculation: Daily VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset at each day and using current-day bars through the current bar.
Calculation: AI bull/bear confidence = for each bar, form features by clamping (fast MA − slow MA)/(2 × ATR), (close − daily VWAP)/(3 × ATR), (HMA − HMA two bars ago)/(1.5 × ATR) to [−1, 1], and min(1, relative volume/3), where relative volume is volume divided by its 20-bar SMA and ATR uses a 14-bar lookback. Compare this vector by Euclidean distance with vectors from prior bars in the lookback window; take the k nearest. Each neighbor votes bull if its close four bars after that historical bar exceeds its close eight bars after it, bear if lower, otherwise neutral. Bull/bear confidence is the respective vote count divided by k, as a percentage.
Long: AI enabled AND current bull confidence ≥ threshold AND close > daily VWAP AND ADX > threshold AND the same conditions were not all true on the prior bar AND current position state is not long; OR AI disabled AND fast MA > slow MA AND close > daily VWAP AND fast MA > daily VWAP AND HMA > HMA two bars ago AND close > HMA AND Supertrend is bullish AND ADX > threshold AND relative volume > 1.2 AND the same conditions were not all true on the prior bar AND current position state is not long; OR fast MA crosses above slow MA; OR fast MA crosses above daily VWAP.
Short: AI enabled AND current bear confidence ≥ threshold AND close < daily VWAP AND ADX > threshold AND the same conditions were not all true on the prior bar AND current position state is not short; OR AI disabled AND fast MA < slow MA AND close < daily VWAP AND fast MA < daily VWAP AND HMA < HMA two bars ago AND close < HMA AND Supertrend is bearish AND ADX > threshold AND relative volume > 1.2 AND the same conditions were not all true on the prior bar AND current position state is not short; OR fast MA crosses below slow MA; OR fast MA crosses below daily VWAP.
