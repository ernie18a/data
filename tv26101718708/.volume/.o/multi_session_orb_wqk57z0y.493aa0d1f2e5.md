Calculation: Session opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range window for each Asia, London, and NY session; use only after the window is complete.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the start of each NY trading day.
Long: In an active session after its opening range is complete (and, for NY, before its signal cutoff), either close crosses from below to at least the opening-range high plus the configured breakout distance, or low touches/breaks the opening-range low and close is at least the configured rejection buffer above the low and at or below the high; the rejection bar must not also meet either breakout condition. If enabled, require close above VWAP and the configured rejection-wick size. If enabled, allow only the first breakout and first rejection long signal per session.
Short: In an active session after its opening range is complete (and, for NY, before its signal cutoff), either close crosses from above to at most the opening-range low minus the configured breakout distance, or high touches/breaks the opening-range high and close is at least the configured rejection buffer below the high and at or above the low; the rejection bar must not also meet either breakout condition. If enabled, require close below VWAP and the configured rejection-wick size. If enabled, allow only the first breakout and first rejection short signal per session.
