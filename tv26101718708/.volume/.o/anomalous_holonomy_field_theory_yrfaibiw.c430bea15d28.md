Calculation: Signal strength = Score amplifier / 10 × (trend score + capped VWAP distance + conditional RSI adjustment + conditional MACD adjustment). Trend score: if close is above both the higher-timeframe EMA and short-term EMA, add 1, or 1.5 if also above the longer-term EMA; add positive close-to-close rate of change divided by 10 when positive, and add 0.5 when volume exceeds 1.2 times its moving average. If close is below both higher-timeframe EMA and short-term EMA, subtract 1, or 1.5 if also below the longer-term EMA; add close-to-close rate of change divided by 10 when negative, and subtract 0.5 when volume exceeds 1.2 times its moving average. Add close's percentage distance from VWAP, capped between -2 and 2. When the score is positive, add 1.5 if RSI is below 30, otherwise add 0.5 if below 40; when negative, subtract 1.5 if RSI is above 70, otherwise subtract 0.5 if above 60. Add 0.5 if MACD is above its signal line and score is positive; subtract 0.5 if MACD is below its signal line and score is negative. Use current-chart bars for indicators; use the higher-timeframe EMA from the higher-timeframe series with lookahead off (last completed higher-timeframe bar historically, potentially forming bar in real time).
Calculation: Signal threshold = sensitivity × execution-mode multiplier × volatility multiplier. Volatility is ATR divided by close as a percentage; compare it with its rolling 30th and 70th percentiles over a longer lookback. Use 0.7 for volatility below the 30th percentile, 1.3 above the 70th percentile, and 1 otherwise. The execution-mode multiplier is 1.3 in conservative mode, 0.7 in aggressive mode, and 1 otherwise.
Calculation: Long stop = greater of close minus 1.5 × ATR × mode stop multiplier, and the lesser of the recent short-lookback low and prior-bar typical price minus 0.382 × recent high-low range. Short stop = lesser of close plus 2 × ATR × mode stop multiplier, and the greater of the recent short-lookback high and prior-bar typical price plus 0.382 × recent high-low range. The typical price is (high + low + close) / 3; the range uses a longer lookback than the stop's high/low lookback.
Calculation: Take-profit distance = distance from signal-bar close to the corresponding stop multiplied by a mode-specific target multiplier.
Long: Signal strength ≥ 1.2 × signal threshold AND flat position AND entry timing allowed AND daily trade limit not reached AND bar confirmed AND within allowed session and weekday rules; in conservative mode when signal confirmation is enabled, also require close > short-term EMA AND RSI between 30 and 70.
Short: Signal strength ≤ -1.2 × signal threshold AND flat position AND entry timing allowed AND daily trade limit not reached AND bar confirmed AND within allowed session and weekday rules; in conservative mode when signal confirmation is enabled, also require close < short-term EMA AND RSI between 30 and 70.
Long: Signal strength ≥ signal threshold AND entry timing allowed AND daily trade limit not reached AND bar confirmed AND within allowed session and weekday rules.
Short: Signal strength ≤ -signal threshold AND entry timing allowed AND daily trade limit not reached AND bar confirmed AND within allowed session and weekday rules.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long take-profit OR trailing stop is active and low ≤ its trailing level OR bars held exceed the mode-specific maximum.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short take-profit OR trailing stop is active and high ≥ its trailing level OR bars held exceed the mode-specific maximum.
