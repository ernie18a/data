Calculation: Daily close = close of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Daily upper/lower band = daily VWMA(close, shared lookback) plus/minus a multiplier times daily standard deviation(close, same lookback), using the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Abnormal volume threshold = max(minimum threshold, (base factor + log factor × ln(EMA(daily volume, lookback))) × EMA(daily volume, same lookback) / (minutes per day / chart-bar minutes)); use the last completed daily bars
Calculation: Delayed red signal = on a chart bar N bars ago, volume > abnormal volume threshold and close < open
Calculation: Delayed green signal = on a chart bar N bars ago, volume > abnormal volume threshold and close > open
Calculation: Red/green reversal count = count of the corresponding delayed signals over a short chart-bar lookback
Long: flat position AND ((reversal enabled AND green reversal count ≥ 2 AND daily close > daily upper band) OR (delayed red signal AND daily close > daily upper band AND NOT (reversal enabled AND green reversal count ≥ 2)))
Short: flat position AND ((reversal enabled AND red reversal count ≥ 2 AND daily close < daily lower band) OR (delayed green signal AND daily close < daily lower band AND NOT (reversal enabled AND red reversal count ≥ 2)))
Exit: long, price ≤ stop price OR price ≥ target price; for normal entries, stop price = low of the delayed signal bar reduced by a buffer and target price = high of that bar increased by a buffer; for reversal entries, stop and target are below and above entry price by respective buffers
Exit: short, price ≥ stop price OR price ≤ target price; for normal entries, stop price = high of the delayed signal bar increased by a buffer and target price = low of that bar reduced by a buffer; for reversal entries, stop and target are above and below entry price by respective buffers
