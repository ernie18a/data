Calculation: Prior structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over the prior structure lookback, excluding the current bar; structure height = high − low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Break quality = clamp(0.20 × distance score + 0.18 × body score + 0.18 × directional close-location score + 0.12 × relative-volume score + 0.18 × directional trend score + 0.14 × structure score, 0, 100). Distance score normalizes directional close distance in ATR units from the break buffer to buffer + 0.90; body score normalizes |close − open| / (high − low) from 0.28 to 0.82; close-location score normalizes the directional close location from 0.55 to 0.95; volume score normalizes relative volume from 0.75 to the full-support ratio. For each normalization, clamp (value − lower bound) / (upper bound − lower bound) to [0,1] and multiply by 100. Structure score uses that normalization from the minimum structure height to minimum + 1.80 ATR when height is at or below the midpoint of the minimum and maximum structure heights; above the midpoint, use 100 minus the normalization from maximum − 2.25 ATR to maximum. Directional trend score is the sum of 36 points when close is on the directionally correct side of the slow EMA, 34 points when the fast EMA is directionally stacked against the slow EMA, and up to 30 points for the correctly signed slow-EMA change over the slope lookback, normalized by ATR from 0.01 to 0.28; clamp the sum to [0,100].
Calculation: Follow-through score = clamp(0.24 × breakout quality + 0.26 × progress score + 0.16 × follow-close score + 0.18 × retest score + 0.08 × room score + 0.08 × current volume score, 0, 100). Progress score normalizes best travel in ATR from 0 to 1.35 × required travel; follow-close score normalizes the count of closes beyond the breakout close from 0 to the required count; retest score is 100 minus the normalization of deepest retest depth in ATR from 0 to maximum clean retest depth; room score is 100 at target, otherwise normalizes remaining target distance in ATR from 0 to 2 × required travel; current volume score normalizes relative volume from 0.75 to the full-support ratio.
Long: close > prior structure high + ATR × break buffer AND structure height / ATR is between the minimum and maximum structure heights AND break quality ≥ minimum break quality AND no qualified breakout occurred within the breakout cooldown; OR an active bullish breakout plan has no failure or target hit, deepest retest < maximum clean retest depth, best travel ≥ required travel, follow-through closes ≥ required count, and follow-through score ≥ minimum follow-through score.
Short: close < prior structure low − ATR × break buffer AND structure height / ATR is between the minimum and maximum structure heights AND break quality ≥ minimum break quality AND no qualified breakout occurred within the breakout cooldown; OR an active bearish breakout plan has no failure or target hit, deepest retest < maximum clean retest depth, best travel ≥ required travel, follow-through closes ≥ required count, and follow-through score ≥ minimum follow-through score.
Exit: long, close < breakout level − breakout ATR × failure-rail distance.
Exit: short, close > breakout level + breakout ATR × failure-rail distance.
