Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of bar typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the start of each selected daily or weekly period.
Calculation: VWAP bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus a selected multiple of the volume-weighted standard deviation of bar typical prices from anchored VWAP.
Calculation: VWAP slope = change in anchored VWAP over a lookback, expressed as a percentage of the current upper-to-lower band width.
Calculation: Confluence tolerance = a selected fraction of the current anchor period's high-low range.
Long: close is within the selected tolerance of the lower VWAP band AND the lower VWAP band is within the confluence tolerance of the POC or value area low OR VWAP slope exceeds its rising threshold AND close is within a selected fraction of the band width from VWAP AND close and closes at both a shorter and a longer lookback are above their corresponding VWAPs OR VWAP slope exceeds its rising threshold AND close is above VWAP AND close is not near the upper VWAP band.
Short: close is within the selected tolerance of the upper VWAP band AND the upper VWAP band is within the confluence tolerance of the POC or value area high OR VWAP slope is below its falling threshold AND close is within a selected fraction of the band width from VWAP AND close and closes at both a shorter and a longer lookback are below their corresponding VWAPs OR VWAP slope is below its falling threshold AND close is below VWAP AND close is not near the lower VWAP band.
