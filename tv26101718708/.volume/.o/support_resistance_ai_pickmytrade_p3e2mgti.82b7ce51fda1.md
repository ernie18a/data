Calculation: Support/resistance zones = confirmed pivot highs/lows using left/right lookbacks; initialize each zone around its pivot price with half-width ATR × width parameter / 2. Merge a confirmed pivot into the nearest same-role live zone whose center is within ATR × merge-distance parameter; recenter by pivot-count-weighted average and reset bounds to the current width. Pivots become available only after the right-lookback bars close.
Calculation: Test arrival = on a confirmed bar with ATR > 0, a live zone is touched when low ≤ zone top and high ≥ zone bottom. Open a test only when armed, not already testing, and touched; a resistance must be approached from below and a support from above. Increment touches before recording arrival features.
Calculation: Arrival features = (1) direction-adjusted velocity: resistance (close − close[approach lookback]) / (ATR × √lookback), support its negation, clipped to [−4,4]; (2) relative volume: volume / SMA(volume, baseline lookback), capped at 4, or 1 if volume/SMA is unavailable or SMA ≤ 0; (3) prior resolved-test count for this zone, capped at 6; (4) log(1 + bars since zone birth), capped at 5; (5) direction-adjusted EMA stretch: resistance (close − EMA(close, trend lookback)) / ATR, support its negation, clipped to [−5,5]; (6) zone width / ATR, capped at 4; (7) merged pivot count, capped at 6; (8) cumulative touched-bar count including the arrival bar, capped at 8.
Calculation: Hold probability = online Gaussian Naive Bayes probability of HOLD from all previously resolved zone tests, using the eight arrival features and class counts as priors. For each class and feature use its observed mean and sample variance floored at 1e−6 (variance 1 when fewer than two observations); sum log prior and Gaussian log likelihoods, then normalize the two class scores. Before the warmup sample count, use overall historical hold fraction (0.5 before any resolution), but emit no directional marking.
Calculation: Test resolution and model update = while a test is open, resolve on decisive break (resistance close > top + ATR × break-buffer, support close < bottom − ATR × break-buffer), rejection/hold (resistance close < bottom − ATR × rejection-distance, support close > top + ATR × rejection-distance), or timeout. Label decisive breaks BREAK, rejection HOLD, and timeout BREAK when resistance close > top or support close < bottom, otherwise HOLD. Train the model with the frozen arrival features and label. A decisive break is eligible only if no role flip has occurred or bars since flip ≥ cooldown; after the first eligible decisive break flip the zone role and reset its birth bar and test count. No exit marking is produced.
Long: A new test opens, the warmup sample count is met, and ((support zone AND hold probability ≥ conviction threshold) OR (resistance zone AND hold probability ≤ 1 − conviction threshold)).
Short: A new test opens, the warmup sample count is met, and ((resistance zone AND hold probability ≥ conviction threshold) OR (support zone AND hold probability ≤ 1 − conviction threshold)).
