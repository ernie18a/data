Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Body = absolute value of close minus open
Calculation: Range = high minus low
Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) minus low
Calculation: Upper wick = high minus max(open, close)

Calculation: Accumulation range percentage = (highest high over the accumulation lookback − lowest low over the same lookback) / close × 100
Calculation: EMA spread percentage = absolute difference between fast and slow EMA / close × 100
