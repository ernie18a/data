Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low − low from 2 bars ago, with current low above the high from 2 bars ago; bearish FVG = low from 2 bars ago − current high, with current high below the low from 2 bars ago. Each gap must be at least the configured ATR multiple and, when displacement is required, the middle bar must move in the gap direction, have body size at least the configured ATR multiple, and body/range at least the configured ratio. Do not mark a gap overlapping an existing FVG.
Calculation: Displacement = candle body size ≥ configured ATR multiple AND body/range ≥ configured ratio, with candle direction matching the requested side.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity sweep = price extends beyond the latest confirmed swing low/high by at least the configured ATR multiple, and, when reclaim is required, closes back above/below that swing level.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish OB retest = within the configured bars before a bullish/bearish displacement, find the nearest opposite-color candle and store its high/low as the OB zone; a retest occurs within 100 bars of that candle if a bullish zone's low is inside the zone and close is above its low, or a bearish zone's high is inside the zone and close is below its high.
Calculation: CE tap = price reaches the midpoint of an unmitigated FVG (low ≤ midpoint for bullish FVG; high ≥ midpoint for bearish FVG); retain the FVG score and mark it tapped.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish BOS = latest confirmed pivot high/low is higher/lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high/low; pivots use the same configured left and right lookback.
Calculation: Discount/premium = close below/above the midpoint between the latest confirmed swing high and low.
Calculation: FVG score = logistic score from gap-size/ATR, fast-versus-slow EMA trend, close versus the EMA of the longest lookback plus/minus configured ATR buffer, prior-bar volume versus its moving average, FVG midpoint versus swing-range midpoint, directional BOS, killzone status, and current ATR versus longer-lookback ATR; clamp to 0.04–configured maximum. For trend signals, a CE trigger must meet the configured minimum score.
Long: Bullish FVG OR (a long trigger on the previous bar AND current bullish displacement AND relative volume ≥ configured minimum AND in a killzone AND outside configured time blackouts AND ATR is not compressed versus the longer-lookback ATR AND cooldown elapsed AND ((fast EMA > slow EMA AND bullish BOS AND close > longest-lookback EMA + configured ATR buffer AND (discount required is off OR close is in discount) AND (a bullish CE tap, bullish OB retest, or bullish sweep was enabled and occurred on the previous bar)) OR (bearish EMA trend AND a bullish sweep occurred on the previous bar))).
Short: Bearish FVG OR (a short trigger on the previous bar AND current bearish displacement AND relative volume ≥ configured minimum AND in a killzone AND outside configured time blackouts AND ATR is not compressed versus the longer-lookback ATR AND cooldown elapsed AND ((fast EMA < slow EMA AND bearish BOS AND close < longest-lookback EMA − configured ATR buffer AND (premium required is off OR close is in premium) AND (a bearish CE tap, bearish OB retest, or bearish sweep was enabled and occurred on the previous bar)) OR (bullish EMA trend AND a bearish sweep occurred on the previous bar))).
