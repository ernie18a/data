Calculation: Breakout buffer = ATR × buffer multiplier
Calculation: Recent high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high / lowest low so far in the current day’s premarket session
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low so far in the current day’s opening-range session
Calculation: Bull score = count of true conditions: close > VWAP; fast EMA > mid EMA AND close > fast EMA; RSI > bullish RSI threshold; MACD line > MACD signal line AND MACD histogram > its prior-bar value; volume >= its moving average × volume multiplier; close > slow EMA; close crosses above any of recent high, premarket high, previous day high, or opening-range high, each plus the breakout buffer
Calculation: Bear score = count of true conditions: close < VWAP; fast EMA < mid EMA AND close < fast EMA; RSI < bearish RSI threshold; MACD line < MACD signal line AND MACD histogram < its prior-bar value; volume >= its moving average × volume multiplier; close < slow EMA; close crosses below any of recent low, premarket low, previous day low, or opening-range low, each minus the breakout buffer
Long: During the regular session AND bull score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe VWAP AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe mid EMA AND higher-timeframe RSI > bullish RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe MACD line > higher-timeframe MACD signal line) AND the prior bar did not meet this same long condition
Short: During the regular session AND bear score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe VWAP AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe mid EMA AND higher-timeframe RSI < bearish RSI threshold AND higher-timeframe MACD line < higher-timeframe MACD signal line) AND the prior bar did not meet this same short condition
