Calculation: Estimated buy volume = For each lower-timeframe bar, volume × (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 of volume when high = low. If no lower-timeframe bars are available, apply the same split to the chart bar. Estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume.
Calculation: Bar delta = estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 over the current Session, Week, or Month anchor period, including the current bar.
Calculation: Upper and lower deviation bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus the buy-volume-weighted or sell-volume-weighted standard deviation of HLC3 from anchored VWAP, respectively, accumulated over the current anchor period; inner and outer bands use shorter and longer deviation multipliers, respectively.
Long: On a closed bar, after the anchor warm-up, low ≤ lower outer band AND close > lower outer band AND bar delta > 0 AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long rejection; OR close crosses above the upper outer band; OR close crosses above anchored VWAP. Rejection and crossover conditions do not occur on an anchor-reset bar.
Short: On a closed bar, after the anchor warm-up, high ≥ upper outer band AND close < upper outer band AND bar delta < 0 AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior short rejection; OR close crosses below the lower outer band; OR close crosses below anchored VWAP. Rejection and crossover conditions do not occur on an anchor-reset bar.
