Calculation: Expansion Readiness Score = weighted aggregate of body-efficiency score (absolute body ÷ candle range), wick-control score (penalizes total wick and the wick against the planned direction), relative-volume score (volume ÷ its moving average; neutral if volume is unavailable), ATR-expansion score (candle range ÷ ATR), directional-close-location score (close’s position within the candle range, measured toward the planned direction), and range-context score (candle range ÷ its moving average). Each component is normalized using configurable thresholds; ATR and moving averages use current-chart bars.
Calculation: Risk edge = expansion candle’s opposite extreme, offset outward by an ATR buffer.
Long: planned direction is bullish and readiness score meets the READY threshold, with directional close-location and body-efficiency scores meeting their quality thresholds and any selected volume or prior-bar edge confirmation gate passing; OR planned direction is bullish and the same quality checks pass, readiness score meets the WATCH threshold, and the READY condition fails. Bullish direction is close > open, or on a doji close >= prior close.
Short: planned direction is bearish and readiness score meets the READY threshold, with directional close-location and body-efficiency scores meeting their quality thresholds and any selected volume or prior-bar edge confirmation gate passing; OR planned direction is bearish and the same quality checks pass, readiness score meets the WATCH threshold, and the READY condition fails. Bearish direction is close < open, or on a doji close < prior close.
Exit: Long, close < the active bullish plan’s risk edge.
Exit: Short, close > the active bearish plan’s risk edge.
