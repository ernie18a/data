Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar’s high is a pivot high against the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmed swing low = a bar’s low is a pivot low against the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Point 3 retracement = (Point 2 − Point 3) / (Point 2 − Point 1) for bullish structures; (Point 3 − Point 2) / (Point 1 − Point 2) for bearish structures.
Long: a confirmed swing low, then swing high, then swing low form Points 1–3; Point 2 − Point 1 ≥ ATR × minimum leg threshold; Point 3 − Point 1 ≥ ATR × minimum hold threshold; Point 3 retracement is between its configured minimum and maximum; and the structure becomes armed OR, before it expires or is invalidated, close > Point 2 + ATR × neckline buffer OR, after that break and within the retest window, low ≤ Point 2 + ATR × retest-pocket width AND close ≥ Point 2 − ATR × retest-pocket width AND close > Point 2.
Short: a confirmed swing high, then swing low, then swing high form Points 1–3; Point 1 − Point 2 ≥ ATR × minimum leg threshold; Point 1 − Point 3 ≥ ATR × minimum hold threshold; Point 3 retracement is between its configured minimum and maximum; and the structure becomes armed OR, before it expires or is invalidated, close < Point 2 − ATR × neckline buffer OR, after that break and within the retest window, high ≥ Point 2 − ATR × retest-pocket width AND close ≤ Point 2 + ATR × retest-pocket width AND close < Point 2.
Exit: long, after a neckline break, close < Point 3 − ATR × invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, after a neckline break, close > Point 3 + ATR × invalidation buffer.
