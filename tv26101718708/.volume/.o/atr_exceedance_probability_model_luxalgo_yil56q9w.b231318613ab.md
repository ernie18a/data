Calculation: ATR exceedance = current bar high > previous completed bar high + its ATR × multiplier OR current bar low < previous completed bar low − its ATR × multiplier
Calculation: Short-term exceedance frequency = 100 × the average of the current and preceding exceedance indicators over the shorter lookback
Calculation: Frequency z-score = (short-term exceedance frequency − its mean over the longer baseline lookback) / its standard deviation over that baseline lookback
Long: Frequency z-score > sensitivity threshold
Short: Frequency z-score < −sensitivity threshold
