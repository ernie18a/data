Calculation: High-participation bar = volume > simple moving average of volume × participation threshold
Calculation: Range zones = on each high-participation bar, create a zone from its low to high, or merge it into the first active zone whose range overlaps after expanding its top and bottom by current ATR × merge tolerance; expand merged bounds to include the bar. Add volume to bull ownership by volume × bull share and to bear ownership by the remainder, where bull share is (max(close, open) − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), floored at 0.55 when close ≥ open and capped at 0.45 otherwise. Zone equilibrium is the volume-weighted average of each contributing bar's (high + low + close) / 3. Deactivate a zone after its age exceeds its lifetime, when close is above its top and bull ownership balance ≥ 0, or when close is below its bottom and balance ≤ 0.
Calculation: Dominant active range = active zone with greatest absolute ownership balance; if tied, use the most recently created zone. Ownership balance = (bull ownership volume − bear ownership volume) / total ownership volume.
Long: Dominant active range exists AND bar is confirmed AND ((ownership balance > 0.08 AND close > equilibrium AND close ≤ range top) OR close > range top OR close crosses above equilibrium)
Short: Dominant active range exists AND bar is confirmed AND ((ownership balance < −0.08 AND close < equilibrium AND close ≥ range bottom) OR close < range bottom OR close crosses below equilibrium)
