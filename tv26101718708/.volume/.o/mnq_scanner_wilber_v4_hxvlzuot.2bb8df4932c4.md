Long: Close > the long-term EMA calculated from 1-hour bars (last completed bar) AND within a configured London, New York, or post-New York time window (ET) AND at least 3 of: close > daily VWAP; previous 15-minute bar low <= its EMA and its close > its EMA, with the current 15-minute close > its EMA and > the previous 15-minute close; previous chart bar is bullish, its body is at least the configured minimum size and fraction of its range, its close is in the top quarter of its range, and its close > the short-term EMA and daily VWAP. The EMA used for the 15-minute pullback is calculated on 15-minute bars; the previous 15-minute bar is completed, and the current value is the latest available 15-minute bar value. The short-term EMA, momentum bar, and VWAP comparison use chart bars; momentum uses the previous completed chart bar and VWAP is the current daily VWAP.
