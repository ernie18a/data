Calculation: Opening drive = aggregate open, high, low, close, and volume over the configured opening-drive window; use the completed window after it locks.
Calculation: Opening-drive direction = bullish if drive close > drive open, or if equal and drive close ≥ (drive high + drive low) / 2; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Opening-drive quality score = clamp(round(weighted score), 0, 100), where body efficiency = |drive close − drive open| / drive range, close location = (drive close − drive low) / drive range for bullish drives or (drive high − drive close) / drive range for bearish drives, and range expansion = drive range / current-timeframe ATR. Clamp body efficiency × 125, close location × 100, and range expansion / 1.20 × 100 individually to 0–100; weight them 0.40, 0.32, 0.28. When relative volume is enabled and available, also include clamp(relative volume / 1.35 × 100, 0, 100) at weight 0.15 and use weights 0.32, 0.28, 0.25 for the other components. Relative volume = average volume of the drive window / current-timeframe SMA(volume, volume lookback).
Calculation: Follow-through confirmed = while tracking is active after the drive locks, maximum high since lock − drive high for bullish drives, or drive low − minimum low since lock for bearish drives, is at least the configured ATR multiple of current-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Pullback accepted = while tracking is active, current bar overlaps the pullback zone and closes at or beyond its drive-side boundary (close ≥ zone bottom for bullish drives; close ≤ zone top for bearish drives), and the risk line is not broken.
Long: Opening-drive direction is bullish AND opening-drive quality score ≥ minimum label quality OR opening-drive direction is bullish AND follow-through is confirmed AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold OR opening-drive direction is bullish AND follow-through is confirmed AND pullback is accepted AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold OR opening-drive direction is bullish AND target guide is reached AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold; evaluate post-lock events only during the tracking session and within the configured drive-memory bars.
Short: Opening-drive direction is bearish AND opening-drive quality score ≥ minimum label quality OR opening-drive direction is bearish AND follow-through is confirmed AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold OR opening-drive direction is bearish AND follow-through is confirmed AND pullback is accepted AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold OR opening-drive direction is bearish AND target guide is reached AND readiness score ≥ minimum quality threshold; evaluate post-lock events only during the tracking session and within the configured drive-memory bars.
Exit: Long, close < bullish drive risk line.
Exit: Short, close > bearish drive risk line.
