Calculation: Previous-session VAH and VAL = VA boundaries from the profile at the end of the immediately preceding completed session. Build the profile from the configured number of trailing sessions: split the combined high-low range into bins; distribute each in-session bar’s max(volume, 1) across bins in proportion to overlap with its high-low range, assigning zero-range bars wholly to their price bin; choose the highest-volume bin as POC, then expand the value area outward from POC by adding the adjacent bin with greater volume (ties upward) until it contains the configured percentage of total volume. VAH is the top edge and VAL the bottom edge of the included bins.
Long: In session, close[1] <= previous-session VAH AND close > previous-session VAH (first such crossing per session)
Short: In session, close[1] >= previous-session VAL AND close < previous-session VAL (first such crossing per session)
