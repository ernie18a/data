Calculation: Volume-weighted SMMA(x, n) = initialize to the first x; thereafter update as (previous value × (n−1) + x × volumeNorm) / (n−1 + volumeNorm), where volumeNorm = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), defaulting to 1 when unavailable.
Calculation: Spread = volume-weighted SMMA((fast − anchor) / ATR, spread smoothing lookback), where fast and anchor are volume-weighted SMMAs of hl2 with fast and slower lookbacks, respectively.
Calculation: Learned lower/upper penetration band = previous completed bars’ Donchian low/high (excluding the current bar) minus/plus ATR × penetration multiplier. For each side, the multiplier is the default until enough positive wick penetrations are collected; then it is the sensitivity-scaled mean of the latest bounded number of that side’s penetrations, clamped to its minimum and maximum. A penetration is the current bar’s wick distance beyond the prior Donchian low/high divided by ATR.
Calculation: Price-move Z = z-score of (close − close from the longer price lookback bars ago) / ATR, using its longer rolling mean and standard deviation; use zero if the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish RSI divergence = at a confirmed low/high pivot, respectively, price makes a lower low/higher high while RSI makes a higher low/lower high than the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type, with pivot separation no greater than the maximum pivot lookback. RSI uses close.
Long: Confirmed bar AND fast < anchor AND required consecutive bearish bars reached AND (low ≤ learned lower penetration band AND (buy spread gate disabled OR Spread ≤ buy spread threshold) OR Price-move Z ≤ buy Z threshold) AND buy throttle allows entry AND (buy count below cycle maximum OR a prior partial exit occurred in this cycle); OR bullish RSI divergence.
Short: Confirmed bar AND fast > anchor AND (high ≥ learned upper penetration band AND (sell spread gate disabled OR Spread ≥ sell spread threshold) OR Price-move Z ≥ sell Z threshold) AND sell throttle allows exit AND position is long; OR bearish RSI divergence.
Exit: long, same confirmed-bar sell condition in Short above, excluding bearish RSI divergence.
