Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band
Calculation: Long-term moving-average distance = previous close divided by the previous bar’s long-term simple moving average
Long: current close > current upper Bollinger Band AND previous completed bar’s Bollinger Band width < its ATR-scaled squeeze threshold AND (for each of the recent consecutive completed bars, upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel OR, consistently across those bars, lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel) AND (shortest-to-longest EMAs are strictly descending OR EMA trend filter is disabled) AND (current volume > its moving average OR volume filter is disabled) AND (previous close > its long-term simple moving average AND long-term moving-average distance ≥ its lower-bound threshold OR 200-MA filter is disabled)
Short: current close < current lower Bollinger Band AND previous completed bar’s Bollinger Band width < its ATR-scaled squeeze threshold AND (for each of the recent consecutive completed bars, upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel OR, consistently across those bars, lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel) AND (shortest-to-longest EMAs are strictly ascending OR EMA trend filter is disabled) AND (current volume > its moving average OR volume filter is disabled) AND (previous close < its long-term simple moving average AND previous close divided by that average ≥ its lower-bound threshold OR 200-MA filter is disabled)
Exit: long, current close < the Bollinger Band basis recorded on the entry signal bar OR current close crosses below its short-term EMA
Exit: short, current close > the Bollinger Band basis recorded on the entry signal bar OR current close crosses above its short-term EMA
Exit: long or short, intraday bar close time ≥ configured intraday exit time
