Calculation: Smoothed Z-score = EMA of (close − EMA(close, mean lookback)) / max(EWMA standard deviation of close’s deviations from its EWMA mean, 1e-8); the EWMA mean and variance use the same half-life.
Calculation: Long stretch MA = selected moving-average type of close over its lookback.
Calculation: Short stretch MA = selected moving-average type of close over its lookback.
Long: Short EMA >= long EMA OR short EMA crossed above long EMA OR previous smoothed Z-score <= −long weak threshold AND smoothed Z-score has a pivot low at the previous bar OR long stretch MA > 0 AND 100 × (long stretch MA − selected long stretch price) / long stretch MA >= long stretch threshold OR an armed long run has not yet triggered its stretch signal AND long stretch MA > 0 AND 100 × (long stretch MA − selected long stretch price) / long stretch MA >= long stretch threshold OR an armed long run AND short EMA crossed above long EMA
Short: Short EMA < long EMA OR short EMA crossed below long EMA OR previous smoothed Z-score >= short weak threshold AND smoothed Z-score has a pivot high at the previous bar OR short stretch MA > 0 AND 100 × (selected short stretch price − short stretch MA) / short stretch MA >= short stretch threshold OR an armed short run has not yet triggered its stretch signal AND short stretch MA > 0 AND 100 × (selected short stretch price − short stretch MA) / short stretch MA >= short stretch threshold OR an armed short run AND short EMA crossed below long EMA
