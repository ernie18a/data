Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − rolling volume mean) / rolling volume standard deviation
Calculation: Composite = 100 × clamp(HMA of [0.50 × (close − rolling volume-weighted median close) / EMA(high − low) + 0.35 × (Connors RSI − 50) / 50 + 0.15 × clamp(Volume Z-score / 3, −1, 1)], −1, 1); Connors RSI is the average of RSI(close), RSI of the consecutive-direction streak, and the percentile rank of one-bar close ROC, using progressively shorter lookbacks
Long: Composite > 0 OR Composite crosses above 0 OR Composite crosses upward through the oversold level OR Composite change > acceleration threshold OR at a confirmed composite pivot low, composite is higher and price low is lower than at the preceding pivot low within the divergence lookback
Short: Composite < 0 OR Composite crosses below 0 OR Composite crosses downward through the overbought level OR Composite change < −acceleration threshold OR at a confirmed composite pivot high, composite is lower and price high is higher than at the preceding pivot high within the divergence lookback
