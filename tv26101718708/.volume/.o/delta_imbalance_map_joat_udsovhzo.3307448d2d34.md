Calculation: Directional delta tilt = on the last completed chart bar, buyer volume share minus seller volume share for a bullish gap, or seller volume share minus buyer volume share for a bearish gap; on lower-timeframe bars, assign volume to buyers when close > open, sellers when close < open, and split it equally when close = open.
Calculation: Imbalance score = min(100, gap size / minimum gap size × 30 + max(directional delta tilt, 0) × 0.7 + abs(previous chart bar close − open) / ATR × 12 + volume percentile rank × 0.12).
Long: current chart bar is confirmed AND current low > high two chart bars earlier AND current low − high two bars earlier ≥ ATR × minimum gap ATR AND abs(directional delta tilt) ≥ minimum delta tilt AND current volume percentile rank ≥ minimum volume rank AND (overlap guard disabled OR every overlapping tracked zone is filled or this zone’s imbalance score exceeds its score).
Short: current chart bar is confirmed AND current high < low two chart bars earlier AND low two chart bars earlier − current high ≥ ATR × minimum gap ATR AND abs(directional delta tilt) ≥ minimum delta tilt AND current volume percentile rank ≥ minimum volume rank AND (overlap guard disabled OR every overlapping tracked zone is filled or this zone’s imbalance score exceeds its score).
