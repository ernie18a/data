Calculation: Heikin-Ashi candles = HA close is the mean of open, high, low, and close; HA open is the mean of the prior HA open and close (initially the mean of open and close). HA high/low are the maximum/minimum of high, HA open, and HA close.
Calculation: Flux Wave = blend of normalized price-vs-EMA-channel, price-vs-volume-weighted-mean, and volume-weighted candle-pressure momentum; smooth the blend and add an acceleration component. Its signal line is a short moving average of the Flux Wave. Use the same lookback for the three components; the signal smoothing is shorter. Use HA-derived price when HA is enabled.
Calculation: Money flow = moving average of (close − open) / (high − low) × volume, divided by average volume and scaled; zero-range bars contribute zero.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while its Flux Wave signal pivot low is higher; regular bearish divergence mirrors this at pivot highs. Compare only pivots within a bounded lookback; qualify pivots by the oversold/overbought threshold. Divergence remains active for a bounded number of bars after confirmation. Hidden divergence is the converse price/oscillator pivot relationship.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative price × volume divided by cumulative volume from the anchor through the current bar. Use current-session, current-week, previous-session-start, previous-week-start, and five-session-old session-start anchors; use the nearest enabled anchored VWAP to current close. The current session/week and the anchored values accumulating through the current bar are still forming.
Calculation: VWAP test up = low reaches the nearest anchored VWAP within a percentage tolerance and close finishes above it; test down mirrors this.
Calculation: Trend = HA close above/below its EMA. Structure break up/down = close above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low. Liquidity sweep up/down = low below the latest confirmed pivot low and close back above it / high above the latest confirmed pivot high and close back below it.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish HA gate = respectively, bullish/bearish HA candle, a consecutive run of that direction, minimum HA body-to-range ratio, and opposite wick no greater than a maximum fraction of HA range.
Calculation: Directional pillars = oscillator, VWAP, HA, and structure scores. Oscillator score rewards Flux Wave direction, agreement of its three momentum components, crosses/divergence, and rising/falling momentum; VWAP score rewards close on the directional side of the nearest VWAP, VWAP test, and close on the directional side of current-session and previous-session anchored VWAPs; HA score rewards the corresponding HA gate attributes; structure score rewards trend, structure break, liquidity sweep, and money-flow direction. Add fib-rejection and reversal bonuses described below. Directional Oracle score is the sum of that side's four pillars and bonuses.
Calculation: Fib levels = retracements 0.5, 0.618, 0.65, and 0.786 of the latest confirmed rising swing leg (lowest low then highest high) for support, and falling leg (highest high then lowest low) for resistance. Find each leg within a bounded lookback ending at the confirmed pivot. If unavailable, derive the corresponding leg from the latest confirmed structure pivot and the preceding extreme. A fib rejection is a touch within a percentage tolerance followed by a close back above a support level or below a resistance level. Also calculate 0.65 and 0.786 support levels from the falling leg.
Calculation: Wave rollover bullish/bearish = Flux Wave is respectively above/below its signal and rising/falling, at least two of its three momentum components point that way, and it has crossed its signal, gained component agreement, or moved beyond its value two bars earlier in that direction.
Calculation: Money-flow shift bullish/bearish = money flow changes to nonnegative/negative after being negative/nonnegative within the prior two bars. Strong shift additionally requires money flow and its Flux Wave component to move in the new direction, with that component previously on the opposite side of zero.
Calculation: Fib-wave-money reversal = major fib rejection (bullish: falling-leg 0.786/0.65 or rising-leg 0.786/0.618 support; bearish: falling-leg 0.618/0.786/0.65 resistance), matching wave rollover, matching money-flow shift, and matching divergence or oscillator extreme.
Calculation: Swing reversal arm = at a major-lookback price extreme, matching confirmed/recent divergence or oscillator extreme accompanied by fib touch, liquidity sweep, or VWAP test. Keep the arm for a short confirmation window; cancel it if the extreme is exceeded beyond an ATR buffer. Swing buy trigger = armed low, bullish Flux Wave with at least two bullish momentum components and cross or rising momentum, money flow nonnegative or improving, and close above the prior short-lookback highest close (or highest high). Swing sell mirrors at an armed high and requires close below the prior short-lookback lowest close (or lowest low); block it when close is above a rising trend EMA, ADX is strong, and positive DI exceeds negative DI.
Calculation: Strong entry gate = all four directional pillars meet their minimum, the oscillator and HA pillars meet the minimum, the required number of pillars meet the minimum, at least two momentum components agree (or all three outside reversal), money flow agrees, Flux Wave zone is not overbought for buys / not oversold for sells, trend/VWAP gates pass, chop filter passes, and directional Oracle score meets its strong threshold. Standard strong entries also require oscillator trigger, HA gate, structure/trend alignment, and VWAP confluence. Reversal strong entries use the fib-wave-money reversal and require matching divergence/exhaustion and Flux synchronization. Apply the configured signal cooldown and opposite-signal guard; after a strong signal, block the opposite direction for a bounded number of bars unless close crosses the signal bar's invalidation extreme.
Calculation: Normal entry gate = HA gate, momentum trigger, Flux Wave direction, VWAP and trend alignment, agreeing money flow, nonblocked ADX regime, at least three pillars including HA meeting the lower minimum, directional Oracle score at/above normal and below strong threshold, and permitted Flux Wave zone; exclude bars with a strong signal. Apply the signal cooldown.
Calculation: Flux synchronization = matching oscillator context (extreme/recent extreme, wave turn/cross, strong money-flow shift, and at least two agreeing momentum components) plus matching chart context (structure, fib, liquidity sweep, or VWAP with HA); the fib-wave-money reversal also qualifies directly.
Calculation: Active stop = initial entry price minus/plus entry ATR × stop-risk multiplier for long/short; move to breakeven at 1R and lock 0.5R at 2R. After the favorable move reaches the trail activation R, ratchet the stop using the maximum/minimum of prior stop, high/low minus/plus ATR × trail multiplier, and rolling swing low/high minus/plus ATR buffer. ATR uses a shorter lookback than the swing trail lookback.
Long: strong standard-entry gate AND bullish HA gate AND buy momentum trigger AND bullish VWAP/trend gates AND bullish strong-entry gate AND regime permits standard strong entries AND cooldown/guard permit; OR fib-wave-money reversal strong-entry gates pass; OR swing buy trigger with a strong signal permitted; OR normal entry gate and normal signals are enabled.
Short: strong standard-entry gate AND bearish HA gate AND sell momentum trigger AND bearish VWAP/trend gates AND bearish strong-entry gate AND regime permits standard strong entries AND cooldown/guard permit; OR fib-wave-money reversal strong-entry gates pass; OR swing sell trigger with a strong signal permitted; OR normal entry gate and normal signals are enabled.
Exit: long, low <= active stop and prior low was above prior active stop.
Exit: short, high >= active stop and prior high was below prior active stop.
Exit: long, minimum holding bars elapsed AND (opposite strong sell fired after the longer opposite-signal holding delay AND bearish HA reversal; OR HA exits enabled AND bearish HA reversal AND (trail active OR unrealized profit >= minimum R)).
Exit: short, minimum holding bars elapsed AND (opposite strong buy fired after the longer opposite-signal holding delay AND bullish HA reversal; OR HA exits enabled AND bullish HA reversal AND (trail active OR unrealized profit >= minimum R)).
