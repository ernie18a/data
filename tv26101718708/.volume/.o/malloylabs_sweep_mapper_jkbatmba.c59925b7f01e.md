Calculation: Active pivot zones = confirmed swing highs and lows using equal left/right lookbacks; process highs then lows each bar, merge each new pivot into the first existing zone whose level is within the merge threshold of the new pivot by averaging levels and incrementing touches, otherwise create a zone with that pivot’s direction; evict oldest zones beyond the active-zone limit. A zone retains its original direction and is removed from consideration after a qualifying sweep.
Calculation: Zone half-width = zone level × zone width percentage ÷ 2
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume > simple moving average of volume over the volume lookback × volume multiplier
Long: For any unswept support zone, low < zone level − zone half-width − minimum wick penetration AND close > zone level AND close > open AND volume spike; OR, for any EMA, low < EMA − minimum wick penetration AND close > EMA AND close > open AND volume spike
