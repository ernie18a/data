Calculation: Confirmed swing low/high = a bar whose low/high is the lowest/highest across the same lookback on both sides; use the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing as the liquidity pool.
Calculation: Fair Value Gap = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier, or bearish when current high < low two bars earlier, with gap size meeting a minimum ATR fraction. Inversion FVG = an opposing gap whose boundary is crossed by a closing price, still fresh within a lookback, and not subsequently invalidated by a close back through the far boundary.
Calculation: V-Score = clamp to 0–10 the weighted mean of normalized sweep depth, displacement, sharpness, rejection wick fraction, volume divided by its moving-average baseline, and available target distance in R; when bias mode is score-only, add a positive adjustment for alignment with the higher-timeframe bias and a negative adjustment otherwise. Normalize depth, displacement, sharpness, volume surge, and target room against configurable reference values; normalize wick fraction to 0–1.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close versus EMA on the last completed bar of the selected bias timeframe.
Calculation: DOL = nearest unswept qualifying swing high/low on the selected DOL timeframe, plus enabled previous-day and previous-week high/low levels; use last completed higher-timeframe bars and previous completed day/week bars. Ignore levels already crossed by price and levels outside the configured R-distance window.
Calculation: Entry risk = distance from entry to the V-extreme stop plus ATR buffer, clamped between configurable ATR floor and ceiling when risk normalization is enabled. TP1 and TP2 = entry price plus/minus configured R multiples of risk. TP3 = qualifying DOL, or entry price plus/minus a configured R multiple of risk if no DOL qualifies.
Long: Latest unraided confirmed swing low is swept (low < pool), then a close reclaims it within the reclaim window; from the lowest low since the sweep, depth and displacement meet their minimum ATR thresholds, displacement divided by bars from extreme to reclaim meets the sharpness threshold, and that width does not exceed its maximum; V-Score meets its minimum and any hard higher-timeframe bias filter is bullish; risk is positive and, if risk normalization is off, within its ATR cap; and either a qualifying bullish IFVG (preferred when allowed) or fresh bullish FVG is present, or zone requirement is disabled. Enter at reclaim close in immediate mode or when price retests the selected zone in retest mode; cancel an armed setup if close falls below its V extreme or its arm window expires.
Short: Latest unraided confirmed swing high is swept (high > pool), then a close reclaims below it within the reclaim window; from the highest high since the sweep, depth and displacement meet their minimum ATR thresholds, displacement divided by bars from extreme to reclaim meets the sharpness threshold, and that width does not exceed its maximum; V-Score meets its minimum and any hard higher-timeframe bias filter is bearish; risk is positive and, if risk normalization is off, within its ATR cap; and either a qualifying bearish IFVG (preferred when allowed) or fresh bearish FVG is present, or zone requirement is disabled. Enter at reclaim close in immediate mode or when price retests the selected zone in retest mode; cancel an armed setup if close rises above its V extreme or its arm window expires.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= TP3; if stop and any target are reached on the same bar, exit at stop, otherwise exit at TP3, or at stop after recording the highest previously reached TP1/TP2.
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= TP3; if stop and any target are reached on the same bar, exit at stop, otherwise exit at TP3, or at stop after recording the highest previously reached TP1/TP2.
