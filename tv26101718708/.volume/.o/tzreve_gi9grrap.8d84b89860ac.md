Calculation: TrendZones upper curve = 30-period SMA of the 15-bar highest value of the 5-period median of high
Calculation: TrendZones lower curve = 30-period SMA of the 15-bar lowest value of the 5-period median of low
Calculation: Center of Gravity = average of the TrendZones upper and lower curves
Calculation: True Range = maximum of high − low, absolute value of high − previous close, and absolute value of low − previous close
Long: low > Center of Gravity AND low < TrendZones upper curve
Long: low > TrendZones upper curve
Long: close >= highest of the two previous closes
Long: close >= highest of the two previous closes AND current volume > 120% of the median of the previous 50 bars' volume
