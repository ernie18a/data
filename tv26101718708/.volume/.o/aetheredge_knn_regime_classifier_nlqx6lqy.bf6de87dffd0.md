Calculation: Predicted regime = On each confirmed bar after the memory reaches its minimum sample count, find the k nearest stored feature vectors to the current vector (ADX strength, DMI differential, Bollinger Band Width ratio, RSI, volume/volume average, linear-regression slope, close position in the rolling high-low range, and 10-bar ROC). Classify by majority or distance-weighted vote. Store each completed bar’s vector with its regime label, adding it to memory after the forward window and discarding oldest samples beyond the memory limit. Assign labels in priority order: breakout if BBW ratio > its threshold AND volume ratio > its threshold AND absolute ROC > its threshold; reversal if RSI is extreme AND ROC sign differs from five bars earlier; trending up if ADX > its trend threshold AND DI+ > DI− AND slope > 0; trending down if ADX > its trend threshold AND DI− > DI+ AND slope < 0; range if ADX < its range threshold AND BBW ratio < its threshold; otherwise range. All inputs use chart-timeframe bars.
