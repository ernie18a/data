Calculation: Candle range = high − low; candle body efficiency = |close − open| / candle range; close location = (close − low) / candle range.
Calculation: Impulse size = candle range / ATR; ATR uses a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Participation ratio = volume / its configurable-lookback simple moving average. Score it with a peak-shaped normalization: zero at 0.65, 100 at 1.35, and zero again at the configurable climax ratio.
Calculation: Trend score for either direction = 42 if close is on the favorable side of the slow EMA, otherwise 16; add 34 if the fast EMA is on the favorable side of the slow EMA; add up to 24 for the slow EMA slope over a configurable lookback when its sign agrees with the direction. Normalize absolute slope divided by ATR from 0.01 to 0.30 for that last component.
Calculation: Directional impulse quality score = clamp to 0–100 the weighted sum: 30% impulse-size score, 24% body-efficiency score, 22% directional edge-close score, 10% participation score, 14% trend score. Normalize size with a peak at minimum impulse size + 0.75 ATR, starting at 70% of minimum size and falling to zero at the configurable oversized size. Normalize body efficiency upward from 85% of its minimum to 0.92; normalize directional edge-close upward from 85% of its minimum to 0.95.
