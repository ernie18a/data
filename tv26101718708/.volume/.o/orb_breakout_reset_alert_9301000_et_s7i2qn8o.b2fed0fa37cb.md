Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of the current day’s bars from 9:30 to before 10:00 ET
Calculation: Opening-range average volume = mean volume of the current day’s bars from 9:30 to before 10:00 ET
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, resetting each day
Long: current close > current opening-range high AND previous close <= previous opening-range high AND current volume >= opening-range average volume × configured volume multiplier AND current close > current VWAP AND current bar time is from 9:30 to before 11:30 ET
