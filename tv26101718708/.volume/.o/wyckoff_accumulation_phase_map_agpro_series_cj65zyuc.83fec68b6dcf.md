Calculation: ATR = average true range over a shorter lookback than the prior-downtrend lookback
Calculation: Volume baseline = moving average of volume; elevated, climactic, and weak volume are respectively above a higher, intermediate, and lower multiple of this baseline
Calculation: Swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal left and right swing lookbacks
Calculation: Qualified downtrend = latest swing high and swing low are each below their respective preceding swings, the current low is more than a minimum ATR multiple below the highest high in the prior-downtrend lookback, and the current low is within a threshold fraction of the lowest-to-highest range in that lookback
Calculation: Accumulation candidates = during a qualified downtrend, track the lowest swing low with elevated or climactic volume as the selling-climax level; when replaced by a lower such low, retain the prior level as preliminary support. Track the highest swing high after the current selling-climax low, provided it is below a maximum proportional distance above that low, as the automatic-rally level. Discard candidates when they exceed their maximum age or the close rises more than a maximum proportion above the selling-climax level
Calculation: Locked accumulation = an accumulation candidate set confirmed by a close crossing above the automatic-rally level from at or below it, with that close below a maximum proportion above the level; retain the selling-climax and automatic-rally levels and the range height between them until the accumulation expires
Long: First close above the automatic-rally level after a prior close at or below it AND close below the maximum proportional extension above that level AND valid accumulation candidates exist; OR, while an accumulation is locked and within its post-confirmation time limit, low below the selling-climax level AND close above it AND bar occurs after a minimum delay; OR, while locked and within that time limit, first swing low after the spring above the secondary-test low (or selling-climax level if no secondary test exists); OR, while locked and within that time limit, no prior sign-of-strength event AND close above the automatic-rally level AND climactic volume
Exit: Long, no exit condition
Exit: Short, no exit condition
