Calculation: MACD = faster moving average of close minus slower moving average of close; its signal line is a moving average of MACD
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed MACD pivot low, price makes a lower low and MACD makes a higher low than at the previous confirmed MACD pivot low
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed MACD pivot high, price makes a higher high and MACD makes a lower high than at the previous confirmed MACD pivot high
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = at a confirmed MACD pivot low, price makes a higher low and MACD makes a lower low than at the previous confirmed MACD pivot low
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = at a confirmed MACD pivot high, price makes a lower high and MACD makes a higher high than at the previous confirmed MACD pivot high
Long: MACD crosses above its signal line OR regular bullish divergence is confirmed OR hidden bullish divergence is confirmed
Short: MACD crosses below its signal line OR regular bearish divergence is confirmed OR hidden bearish divergence is confirmed
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR histogram momentum has weakened for the required consecutive bars OR a filtered short signal occurs
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR low <= entry price - ATR × target multiple OR histogram momentum has weakened for the required consecutive bars OR a filtered long signal occurs
