Calculation: Mean = selected EMA or SMA of close; standard deviation = rolling standard deviation of close over the mean lookback
Calculation: Stretch = (close − mean) / standard deviation
Calculation: Fresh touch = |stretch| crosses the touch threshold from below
Calculation: Trend regime = tercile of the trailing percentile rank of |fast EMA(close) − slow EMA(close)| / ATR; fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback
Calculation: Reversion probability = sigmoid(intercept + weighted sum of seven fade-oriented features: clamped |stretch|; clamped trend strength; clamped side × (RSI − 50) / 50; clamped standard-deviation / its longer-term average − 1; clamped volume / its average − 1; clamped side × (mean − mean three bars earlier) / ATR; clamped consecutive bars with |stretch| ≥ 1 divided by 10). Use side +1 for an upper-band fade and −1 for a lower-band fade. Initialize weights to zero and update online by logistic-gradient descent when each touch setup resolves. A setup resolves as success if stretch changes sign or |stretch| reaches the revert level; otherwise it resolves as failure if |stretch| reaches its initial |stretch| plus the extend distance; otherwise at the horizon label it success if |stretch| is at most half its initial value, failure otherwise. On each confirmed bar, increment setup age and apply these tests in this order.
Calculation: Adaptive probability threshold = for the current trend regime, choose the candidate threshold with the highest UCB score (mean reward + exploration factor × sqrt(log(regime setup count + 1) / candidate count)); initially untried candidates have priority. After each resolved setup, update every candidate for that setup's regime with reward equal to its outcome when the updated reversion probability meets that candidate threshold, otherwise the overall resolved success rate; use the greater of the selected threshold and the minimum probability floor.
Long: confirmed bar AND bar index exceeds warmup AND Fresh touch AND Stretch < 0 AND Reversion probability ≥ Adaptive probability threshold
Short: confirmed bar AND bar index exceeds warmup AND Fresh touch AND Stretch > 0 AND Reversion probability ≥ Adaptive probability threshold
