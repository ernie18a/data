Calculation: Up gap zone = on the current bar, first qualifying condition in the selected detection mode: open − previous close ≥ minimum gap size; or (wick separation enabled) low − previous high ≥ minimum gap size; or (body separation enabled) current body low − previous body high ≥ minimum gap size; or (displacement fallback enabled) close > previous high and close − previous close ≥ max(4 × minimum gap size, 0.55 × ATR). Zone bounds are respectively [min(open, previous close), max(open, previous close)], [previous high, low], [previous body high, current body low], or [previous close, previous close + max(minimum gap size, min(0.35 × |close − previous close|, 0.65 × ATR))].
Calculation: Down gap zone = symmetric conditions: previous close − open ≥ minimum gap size; or (wick separation enabled) previous low − high ≥ minimum gap size; or (body separation enabled) previous body low − current body high ≥ minimum gap size; or (displacement fallback enabled) close < previous low and previous close − close ≥ max(4 × minimum gap size, 0.55 × ATR). Bounds are respectively [min(open, previous close), max(open, previous close)], [high, previous low], [current body high, previous body low], or [previous close − max(minimum gap size, min(0.35 × |close − previous close|, 0.65 × ATR)), previous close]. For body bounds, body high/low are max/min(open, close). Use only the selected mode's enabled methods, in the stated precedence; require the ATR-normalized minimum gap size. Create zones only on confirmed bars when close confirmation is enabled, and enforce the configured minimum bar spacing between zones.
Calculation: Fill percentage = clamp((zone top − current low) / zone height, 0, 1) × 100 for an up gap; clamp((current high − zone bottom) / zone height, 0, 1) × 100 for a down gap. Track the maximum fill percentage during the zone's projection window.
Calculation: Zone state = accepted if an up gap closes at/below its bottom or a down gap closes at/above its top; otherwise rejected if maximum fill reaches the rejection-fill threshold, remains below the full-fill threshold, and close is beyond the far edge away from the gap (above top for up, below bottom for down); otherwise full if maximum fill reaches the full-fill threshold; otherwise partial if it reaches the rejection-fill threshold; otherwise fresh. A rejected state persists if the next state would be partial; accepted persists.
Calculation: Readiness score = rounded clamp to 0–100 of size score + fill score + reaction score + participation score + recency score. Size score = clamp((zone height / ATR) / 0.85, 0, 1) × 20. Fill score = clamp(20 − |maximum fill − 58| × 0.25, 0, 20) when fill reaches the rejection-fill threshold, otherwise 5. Reaction score = 30 for rejected, 18 if the bar overlaps the zone, 14 for partial, otherwise 6. Participation score = clamp(clamp((bar high − bar low) / ATR / 1.35, 0, 1) × 0.60 + clamp(volume / volume moving average, 0, 2) / 2 × 0.40, 0, 1) × 15. Recency score = clamp(1 − zone age / projection length, 0, 1) × 15.
Long: an up gap zone is created OR, on a confirmed bar, a tracked up gap changes into rejected state and its readiness score ≥ readiness threshold.
Short: a down gap zone is created OR, on a confirmed bar, a tracked down gap changes into rejected state and its readiness score ≥ readiness threshold.
