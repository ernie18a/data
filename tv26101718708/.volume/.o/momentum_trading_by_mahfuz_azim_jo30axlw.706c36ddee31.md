Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Fast QQE line = recursively trailed bands around Smoothed RSI; band distance = EMA(EMA(abs(change in Smoothed RSI), 2 × RSI lookback − 1), 2 × RSI lookback − 1) × QQE factor. The lower band trails upward while Smoothed RSI stays above it; the upper band trails downward while Smoothed RSI stays below it. Use the lower band in an uptrend and upper band in a downtrend; switch up when Smoothed RSI crosses above the prior upper band, and down when it crosses below the prior lower band.
Long: flat AND selected entry signal is (XC: Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 + threshold OR XQ: Smoothed RSI crosses above Fast QQE line OR XZ: Smoothed RSI crosses above 50) on the previous completed bar AND (XQ selected: threshold filter disabled OR Smoothed RSI > 50 + threshold OR Smoothed RSI < 50 − threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR (anchor multiplier = 1 AND close > medium MA AND medium MA > slow MA AND fast MA > medium MA) OR (anchor multiplier > 1 AND medium MA > longer-lookback medium MA AND close > fast MA AND fast MA > medium MA)) AND (direction filter disabled OR (anchor multiplier = 1 AND medium MA rising over 3 bars) OR (anchor multiplier > 1 AND medium MA rising over 3 bars AND longer-lookback medium MA rising over 3 bars AND close > medium MA))
Short: flat AND selected entry signal is (XC: Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 − threshold OR XQ: Smoothed RSI crosses below Fast QQE line OR XZ: Smoothed RSI crosses below 50) on the previous completed bar AND (XQ selected: threshold filter disabled OR Smoothed RSI > 50 + threshold OR Smoothed RSI < 50 − threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR (anchor multiplier = 1 AND close < medium MA AND medium MA < slow MA AND fast MA < medium MA) OR (anchor multiplier > 1 AND medium MA < longer-lookback medium MA AND close < fast MA AND fast MA < medium MA)) AND (direction filter disabled OR (anchor multiplier = 1 AND medium MA falling over 3 bars) OR (anchor multiplier > 1 AND medium MA falling over 3 bars AND longer-lookback medium MA falling over 3 bars AND close < medium MA))
Exit: long, selected close signal is (XC: Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 − threshold OR XQ: opposite QQE cross; if XQ is also the selected entry signal, only that cross applies; otherwise opposite QQE cross OR Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses below 50 − threshold OR XZ: Smoothed RSI crosses below 50)
Exit: short, selected close signal is (XC: Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 + threshold OR XQ: opposite QQE cross; if XQ is also the selected entry signal, only that cross applies; otherwise opposite QQE cross OR Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses above 50 + threshold OR XZ: Smoothed RSI crosses above 50)
