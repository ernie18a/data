Calculation: Candle body-to-range ratio = absolute difference between open and close divided by high minus low, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over its configured lookback, using the last completed bar.
Long: Fast EMA (shorter lookback) > slow EMA (longer lookback) AND last completed close > fast EMA AND last completed low > high three bars ago AND (last completed low − high three bars ago) / minimum price tick ≥ configured minimum gap AND last completed close > open AND candle body-to-range ratio > configured threshold AND last completed volume > average volume AND last completed high > the high two bars ago AND position is flat AND UTC−6 time is within the configured trading window.
Short: Fast EMA (shorter lookback) < slow EMA (longer lookback) AND last completed close < fast EMA AND last completed high < low three bars ago AND (low three bars ago − last completed high) / minimum price tick ≥ configured minimum gap AND last completed close < open AND candle body-to-range ratio > configured threshold AND last completed volume > average volume AND last completed low < the low two bars ago AND position is flat AND UTC−6 time is within the configured trading window.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below the protective stop set from the signal-bar close, or the trailing stop is reached after price advances by its configured activation distance from entry.
Exit: short, high reaches or rises above the protective stop set from the signal-bar close, or the trailing stop is reached after price declines by its configured activation distance from entry.
