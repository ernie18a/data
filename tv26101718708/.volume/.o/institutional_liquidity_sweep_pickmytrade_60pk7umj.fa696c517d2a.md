Calculation: Prior swing low/high = lowest low/highest high over the configurable lookback, excluding the current bar; a bullish sweep is current low below the prior swing low and close above it, and a bearish sweep is current high above the prior swing high and close below it.
Calculation: Composite score = min(100, 100 × (0.28 × S1 + 0.20 × S2 + 0.25 × S3 + 0.17 × S4 + 0.10 × S5) + OB bonus). For S1–S4, transform each raw value to a rolling z-score using the same configurable normalization window, then map it to 0–1 as clamp((z + 3) / 6, 0, 1). S1 raw is bullish (close−low)/(swing low−low+tick) or bearish (high−close)/(high−swing high+tick), capped at 1; on other bars use (close−low)/(high−low), or 0.5 if range is zero. S2 raw is volume. S3 raw is the z-score of single-bar CVD change, where CVD change = volume × ((close−low)−(high−close))/(high−low+tick); use this z-score on bullish sweeps, its negation on bearish sweeps, and its absolute value otherwise. S4 raw is (close−swing low)/(swing low−low+tick) on bullish sweeps, (swing high−close)/(high−swing high+tick) on bearish sweeps, and 0 otherwise; cap sweep values at 2. S5 is 1 during the configured London window, 0.85 during the configured New York window, and 0.45 otherwise, using the current bar timestamp in the configured timezone. Add the configurable OB bonus when the swept swing level lies within an active same-direction order-block zone; cap the final score at 100. An active bullish/bearish order block is the high–low zone of the last bearish/bullish candle preceding the configurable number of consecutive bullish/bearish closed bars; retain the latest of each direction until a close below/above its zone bottom/top mitigates it.
Long: bullish sweep AND composite score ≥ configurable threshold AND close > EMA(close, configurable period) AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these long conditions.
Short: bearish sweep AND composite score ≥ configurable threshold AND close < EMA(close, configurable period) AND the preceding bar did not satisfy these short conditions.
Exit: long, a short-entry condition is true.
Exit: short, a long-entry condition is true.
