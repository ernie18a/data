Calculation: Heiken-Ashi candles = HA close is (open+high+low+close)/4; HA open is (prior HA open+prior HA close)/2, initialized to (open+close)/2; HA high/low are the maximum/minimum of real high/low and HA open/close.
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted average of five scores, normalized by total weight: body expansion (min(current HA body / average prior HA body × 50, 100)); wick quality (max(100 − opposing wick / HA body × 100, 0), using lower wick for bullish HA and upper wick for bearish HA); trend slope (min(abs(EMA slope over a lookback / ATR × 100 × sensitivity), 100)); volume (min(current volume / average volume × 50, 100)); and distance from mean (a score based on abs((HA close − HA close EMA) / HA close standard deviation): rises linearly from 0 to 60 over 0–0.5 SD, to 100 over 0.5–2 SD, falls to 60 at 3 SD, then declines linearly to 0). Each factor uses its own configurable weight and lookback.
Calculation: Session VWAP standard deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of HLC3 from session VWAP, accumulated from the current session's first bar through the current bar.
Calculation: Delta = sum of volume for lower-timeframe bars closing above their open minus sum for those closing below their open within the current chart bar; if lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use +1 when chart close > open, −1 when close < open, otherwise 0.
Long: On a confirmed bar, bullish HA and HA close > HA close EMA and close > session VWAP (when VWAP filter is enabled) and momentum score ≥ bias threshold; OR momentum score > strong threshold and bullish HA and lower HA wick ≤ body × wick tolerance and body > average prior HA body and HA close > HA close EMA and close > session VWAP (when enabled) and delta > 0 (when delta filter is enabled), with prior state not strong buy and buy entry armed. Buy entry starts armed and rearms when real low ≤ HA close EMA; a fresh strong buy locks it.
Long: On a confirmed bar, session bar count ≥ minimum and session VWAP SD / close × 100 ≥ minimum and momentum score < maximum and (fading score not required or current momentum score < prior score) and (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP SD ≤ −SD threshold and current real high ≥ prior HA body high and current real low ≤ prior HA body low and current HA body < average prior HA body and prior HA candle bearish.
Short: On a confirmed bar, bearish HA and HA close < HA close EMA and close < session VWAP (when VWAP filter is enabled) and momentum score ≥ bias threshold; OR momentum score > strong threshold and bearish HA and upper HA wick ≤ body × wick tolerance and body > average prior HA body and HA close < HA close EMA and close < session VWAP (when enabled) and delta < 0 (when delta filter is enabled), with prior state not strong sell and sell entry armed. Sell entry starts armed and rearms when real high ≥ HA close EMA; a fresh strong sell locks it.
Short: On a confirmed bar, session bar count ≥ minimum and session VWAP SD / close × 100 ≥ minimum and momentum score < maximum and (fading score not required or current momentum score < prior score) and (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP SD ≥ SD threshold and current real high ≥ prior HA body high and current real low ≤ prior HA body low and current HA body < average prior HA body and prior HA candle bullish.
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar when prior state was strong buy and current state is neutral or sell, OR close < active long stop. Active long stop is next bar open after a fresh strong buy minus stop multiplier × signal-bar HA range; clear it when hit.
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar when prior state was strong sell and current state is neutral or buy, OR close > active short stop. Active short stop is next bar open after a fresh strong sell plus stop multiplier × signal-bar HA range; clear it when hit.
