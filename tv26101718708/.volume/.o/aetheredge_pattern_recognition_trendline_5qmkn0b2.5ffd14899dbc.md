Calculation: Confirmed pivot sequence = High pivots are highs greater than the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars; low pivots are lows lower than those bars. A pivot becomes available only after its following bars complete. Append confirmed pivots in confirmation order, retaining the latest 60; use their pivot-bar prices and indices.
Calculation: ATR = average true range. EMA bias = the shorter-period EMA is above, below, or equal to the longer-period EMA.
Calculation: Pattern score = weighted average of shape-fit, symmetry, volume-profile, time-profile, and trend-context scores, normalized by the sum of their weights. Volume profile = clamp(1.2 − 0.6 × late-half average volume / early-half average volume, 0, 1), or 0.5 for patterns shorter than 5 bars. Time profile is 0.3 below 15 bars; rises linearly from 0.7 at 15 to 1 at 30; falls linearly to 0.8 at 80; thereafter is max(0.3, 0.8 − (length − 80)/70). Trend-context score for bullish patterns is 1 when EMA bias is up, 0.6 when neutral, and 0.3 when down; for bearish patterns reverse up and down; for neutral patterns it is 0.9 with a nonneutral bias and 0.5 otherwise.
Calculation: Eligible pattern event = bar is confirmed, a pivot was confirmed, more than 3 bars have elapsed since the last detected pattern, and the pattern score is at least the minimum score. Evaluate detectors in this order: Head & Shoulders, inverse Head & Shoulders, Double Top, Double Bottom, triangle/wedge/rectangle, flag; accept only the first eligible detection on that bar.
Calculation: Head & Shoulders = latest five pivot types are high-low-high-low-high; middle high exceeds each outer high by more than 0.5 × ATR × tolerance; outer-high difference / ATR and inner-low difference / ATR are each less than 2 × tolerance. Shape score = max(0, 1 − (outer-high difference / ATR)/(2 × tolerance)); symmetry score uses the same formula with inner-low difference. Neckline is the mean of the inner lows. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Calculation: Inverse Head & Shoulders = latest five pivot types are low-high-low-high-low; middle low is below each outer low by more than 0.5 × ATR × tolerance; outer-low difference / ATR and inner-high difference / ATR are each less than 2 × tolerance. Shape and symmetry scores use the Head & Shoulders formulas. Neckline is the mean of the inner highs. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Calculation: Double Top = latest three pivot types are high-low-high; absolute difference between the highs / ATR is less than 1.5 × tolerance and their separation is at least 15 bars. Shape score = max(0, 1 − (high difference / ATR)/(1.5 × tolerance)); symmetry score = 0.7. Neckline is the middle low. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Calculation: Double Bottom = latest three pivot types are low-high-low; absolute difference between the lows / ATR is less than 1.5 × tolerance and their separation is at least 15 bars. Shape score = max(0, 1 − (low difference / ATR)/(1.5 × tolerance)); symmetry score = 0.7. Neckline is the middle high. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Calculation: Triangle/wedge/rectangle = latest four pivots alternate high-low-high-low or low-high-low-high. Compute each boundary slope from its two pivots, divided by ATR. A symmetrical triangle has upper slope < −0.05 and lower slope > 0.05; an ascending triangle has |upper slope| < 0.05 and lower slope > 0.05; a descending triangle has upper slope < −0.05 and |lower slope| < 0.05. A rising wedge has both slopes > 0.05 and lower slope > 1.2 × upper slope; a falling wedge has both slopes < −0.05 and upper slope < 1.2 × lower slope. A rectangle has both absolute slopes < 0.05. At the current bar, extend both boundaries by their slopes. Bullish direction is EMA bias nonnegative for symmetrical triangles and rectangles, always bullish for ascending triangles and falling wedges; bearish direction is always bearish for descending triangles and rising wedges. Shape score is 0.7 for rectangles; otherwise clamp(1 − current range / initial pivot range + 0.3, 0.3, 1). Symmetry score is clamp(1 − ||upper slope| − |lower slope||, 0, 1), or 0.7 if either slope is zero. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Calculation: Flag = latest four pivots span no more than 30 bars; pole starts 20 bars before the first pivot (or bar 0), and its end price is the mean of the first two pivot prices. Absolute pole-price change / ATR exceeds 5; pivot price range / ATR is less than 0.4 × pole size. Bullish direction is pole end price above pole start close; otherwise bearish. Shape score = clamp(1 − consolidation range / (0.4 × pole size), 0.4, 1); symmetry score = 0.75. Pattern is eligible only if its score meets the minimum.
Long: Eligible inverse Head & Shoulders or Double Bottom pattern detected, or eligible bullish triangle/wedge/rectangle or flag detected.
Long: Eligible Head & Shoulders or Double Top pattern detected, or eligible bearish triangle/wedge/rectangle or flag detected.
Long: An active bullish pattern has close > neckline + ATR × breakout-confirmation amount.
Short: An active bearish pattern has close < neckline − ATR × breakout-confirmation amount.
Exit: long, an active bullish pattern has close < its stop price.
Exit: short, an active bearish pattern has close > its stop price.
