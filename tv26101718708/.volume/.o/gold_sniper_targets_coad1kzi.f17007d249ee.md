Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND at least the configured minimum number of these bullish conditions hold: close > session VWAP; RSI > 50; MACD line > signal line; fast EMA > slow EMA; ADX > its threshold AND close > fast EMA; volume > its moving average AND close > open; confirmation-timeframe RSI > 50; Dollar Index close < its EMA while the rolling correlation between Gold close and Dollar Index close is at or below its configured negative threshold. Require the configured session and spacing filters to pass and today’s range to remain below the configured fraction of average daily range. Evaluate the crossover on the completed bar when close confirmation is enabled. The Dollar Index factor is omitted when disabled, and the minimum score is rescaled to the remaining factors.
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND at least the configured minimum number of these bearish conditions hold: close < session VWAP; RSI < 50; MACD line < signal line; fast EMA < slow EMA; ADX > its threshold AND close < fast EMA; volume > its moving average AND close < open; confirmation-timeframe RSI < 50; Dollar Index close > its EMA while the rolling correlation between Gold close and Dollar Index close is at or below its configured negative threshold. Require the configured session and spacing filters to pass and today’s range to remain below the configured fraction of average daily range. Evaluate the crossover on the completed bar when close confirmation is enabled. The Dollar Index factor is omitted when disabled, and the minimum score is rescaled to the remaining factors.
Calculation: Average daily range = average high minus low over the configured number of last completed daily bars.
Calculation: Today’s range = high minus low of the current, still-forming daily bar.
Calculation: Confirmation-timeframe RSI = RSI on the configured confirmation timeframe, using the latest available bar; the current bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Rolling correlation = correlation of Gold close and Dollar Index close over the configured lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR × configured stop multiplier.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − risk distance OR high >= entry price + 3 × risk distance; after the first target is reached with breakeven enabled, use entry price as the stop instead.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + risk distance OR low <= entry price − 3 × risk distance; after the first target is reached with breakeven enabled, use entry price as the stop instead.
