Calculation: Completed-bar volume average = simple average of volume over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Candle body = max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high / low that is greater / less than the highs / lows of the selected number of bars on both sides; usable only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: RSI recovery = RSI crosses back below its overbought threshold / above its oversold threshold
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low − high from two bars earlier; bearish FVG = low from two bars earlier − current high
Long: close > open AND volume ≥ completed-bar volume average × extreme-volume multiplier AND price is not extended above the EMA by the configured standard-deviation multiple AND body > average body × body multiplier AND close < EMA − standard deviation × configured multiple [bearish exhaustion condition]
Long: close > open AND (volume ≥ completed-bar volume average × strong-volume multiplier OR volume × (high − low) ≥ the highest prior volume-spread value over the lookback) AND candle does not meet the bullish extreme-volume condition
Long: close > open AND volume ≥ completed-bar volume average × moderate-volume multiplier AND candle meets neither the bullish strong-volume nor extreme-volume condition
Long: RSI crosses above its oversold threshold
Long: low < sweep reference AND close > sweep reference AND lower wick / candle body ≥ minimum ratio AND volume > completed-bar volume average × volume multiplier AND (range ≥ ATR OR ATR filter disabled) AND (stored major or internal structure direction is bullish OR structure filter disabled); sweep reference is either the prior rolling low or the latest confirmed major pivot low, according to the selected mode
Long: close < open AND volume > selected prior-volume reference × multiplier AND lower wick / candle body ≥ minimum ratio AND candle body < ATR × maximum-body multiplier AND low < prior-bar low AND selected location filter passes; prior-volume reference is either the completed-bar volume average or the highest volume in the prior lookback, and location is selected from prior range low, below VWAP, a bullish sweep within the preceding five bars, lower quartile of the recent range, or unrestricted
Long: volume ≥ completed-bar volume average × multiplier AND close ≥ EMA × (1 + minimum distance percentage) AND (low ≥ prior rolling high OR recent-high filter disabled)
Long: candle body > prior average body × multiplier AND volume > completed-bar volume average × multiplier AND low < prior rolling low AND close > prior rolling low AND close > open
Long: lower wick ≥ minimum wick/body ratio × candle body AND upper wick ≤ 0.2 × candle body AND volume ≥ completed-bar volume average × multiplier AND absolute distance from close to EMA ≥ configured percentage of EMA
Long: current low > high from two bars earlier AND gap size ≥ configured minimum
Long: current high > latest confirmed major pivot high and the current bar is after that pivot; use close or high for the break according to the selected break rule
Long: current high > latest confirmed internal pivot high and the current bar is after that pivot, unless a major structure break occurs on the same bar; use close or high according to the selected break rule
