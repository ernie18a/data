Calculation: Smoothed %K = stochastic %K, smoothed by two successive EMAs; use chart bars, or selected higher-timeframe bars in MTF Strict (current forming bar). In MTF Bias, use chart bars for %K.
Calculation: %D = simple moving average of smoothed %K.
Calculation: Net pressure = EMA of ((close − low) / candle range × volume / rolling average volume) minus EMA of ((high − close) / candle range × volume / rolling average volume); use the same bars as %K. In MTF Bias, the bias filter uses selected higher-timeframe bars (current forming bar).
Long: %K crosses above %D AND (explosion required is off OR an extreme oversold reading occurred within the arming window) AND 1 + count(armed bullish OR %K ≤ oversold, %K > previous %K > %K two bars ago, net pressure > 0) ≥ minimum strength AND (mid-range filter is off OR %K ≤ 40 OR %K ≥ 60) AND (MTF bias filter is off OR active bullish bias) AND (MTF slope filter is off OR active bullish slope) OR active bullish bias OR armed bullish AND %K < oversold AND no bullish signal AND net pressure > 5 OR %K > 50
Short: %K crosses below %D AND (explosion required is off OR an extreme overbought reading occurred within the arming window) AND 1 + count(armed bearish OR %K ≥ overbought, %K < previous %K < %K two bars ago, net pressure < 0) ≥ minimum strength AND (mid-range filter is off OR %K ≤ 40 OR %K ≥ 60) AND (MTF bias filter is off OR active bearish bias) AND (MTF slope filter is off OR active bearish slope) OR active bearish bias OR armed bearish AND %K > overbought AND no bearish signal AND net pressure < −5 OR %K < 50
