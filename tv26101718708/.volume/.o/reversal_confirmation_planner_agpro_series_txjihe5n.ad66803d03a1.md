Calculation: Bullish reaction score = clamp(0,100, 0.68 × max(bullish sweep score if bullish sweep else 0, bullish rejection score if bullish rejection else 0) + 0.20 × down-extension score + 0.12 × volume-support score)
Calculation: Bearish reaction score = clamp(0,100, 0.68 × max(bearish sweep score if bearish sweep else 0, bearish rejection score if bearish rejection else 0) + 0.20 × up-extension score + 0.12 × volume-support score)
Calculation: Bullish sweep = a bar whose low is below the last confirmed swing low by more than minimum sweep depth × ATR, whose close is above that swing low, and whose close location in the bar range meets the sensitivity threshold. Bearish sweep uses the last confirmed swing high, a high above it by the same ATR-scaled depth, a close below it, and close location at or below 1 minus that threshold.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = rejection-only reactions enabled, prior down context, lower wick / bar range at least the sensitivity-adjusted wick threshold, close location at least the sensitivity threshold, and close > open. Bearish rejection = rejection-only reactions enabled, prior up context, upper wick / bar range at least the sensitivity-adjusted wick threshold, close location at or below 1 minus the sensitivity threshold, and close < open. Prior down context means close below its momentum-lookback close OR below the slower EMA; prior up context means close above its momentum-lookback close OR above the slower EMA.
Calculation: Bullish sweep score = clamp(0,100, normalized sweep depth from minimum sweep depth to the sensitivity-specific ideal depth × 0.55 + close location × 45). Bearish sweep score uses bearish sweep depth and (1 − close location). Bullish rejection score = clamp(0,100, lower wick / bar range × 95 + close location × 30 + (close > open ? body / bar range × 18 : 0)); bearish rejection score uses upper wick and (1 − close location), with the body term when close < open.
Calculation: Down-extension score = clamp(0,100, (slower EMA − low) / ATR / 1.75 × 100). Up-extension score = clamp(0,100, (high − slower EMA) / ATR / 1.75 × 100).
Calculation: Volume-support score = clamp(0,100, (volume / its longer-lookback SMA − 0.65) / 0.85 × 75 + 15 + change in that volume ratio over 3 bars × 35); use ratio 1 when the baseline is zero and volume 0 when unavailable.
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp(0,100, 50 + directional momentum turn × 80 + (close above fast EMA ? 12 : −8)); directional momentum turn is (fast EMA − its momentum-lookback value) / ATR for long and its negative for short. Use EMA lengths 13 (fast) and 34 (slow).
Calculation: Confirmation score = clamp(0,100, reaction score × 0.30 + confirmation progress × 0.25 + momentum score × 0.20 + volume-support score × 0.12 + room score × 0.13). Confirmation progress is the clamped 0–100 percent of the distance from reaction reference toward confirmation line traversed by close, in the setup direction. Room score is clamp(0,100, (room-to-risk − 0.25) / max(preferred room-to-risk − 0.25, 0.10) × 100). Room-to-risk is the smaller of distance from close to the target and distance to the next opposing confirmed swing, when that swing is ahead; otherwise use distance to target, divided by confirmation-line-to-invalidation distance.
Long: a bullish reaction (bullish sweep OR bullish rejection) has the higher reaction score (ties favor bullish), its score ≥ minimum reaction score, and a setup may start (none active, failed, or expired; OR opposite side with score ≥ active reaction score + 6; OR not yet confirmed, score ≥ active reaction score + 10, and setup age > 1 bar); OR an active bullish setup closes above its confirmation line and its confirmation score ≥ confirmed threshold before prior confirmation, failure, or expiry.
Short: a bearish reaction (bearish sweep OR bearish rejection) has the higher reaction score, its score ≥ minimum reaction score, and a setup may start under the same replacement rules; OR an active bearish setup closes below its confirmation line and its confirmation score ≥ confirmed threshold before prior confirmation, failure, or expiry.
Exit: long, close < invalidation edge OR high ≥ target OR setup is unconfirmed and its age exceeds the confirmation window.
Exit: short, close > invalidation edge OR low ≤ target OR setup is unconfirmed and its age exceeds the confirmation window.
