Calculation: Event window = the event bar and the next configurable number of bars; events are New Moon, Full Moon, first-quarter and last-quarter timestamps (each halfway between adjacent New/Full Moon timestamps), and first bars of lunation days 9, 18, and 27. Permit at most one signal per event window.
Calculation: Prior-cycle GPS band = [H − 0.618×(H−L), H − 0.65×(H−L)], where H and L are the highest high and lowest low from the completed interval between consecutive events of the same type. Use New Moon, Full Moon, total lunar eclipse, total solar eclipse, partial lunar eclipse, and partial solar eclipse intervals. Events use UTC timestamps; use bars whose timestamps are at or after each event.
Calculation: Price root = repeatedly sum the digits of the absolute close, first multiplying by 10 until it reaches 1000 (at most six multiplications), then rounding to an integer; stop reduction at 1–9 or 11, 22, or 33. Repeating price = the resulting integer has at least three identical trailing digits, other than zero.
Calculation: Date root = reduce the digits of the current bar's UTC year, month, and day to one digit, preserving 11, 22, or 33. Lunation root = reduce the integer floor((bar timestamp − 2000-01-21 04:40 UTC) / 29.5305882 days) + 1, preserving 11, 22, or 33. Reduced root = map 11→2, 22→4, 33→6; otherwise unchanged.
Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range on the chart bars.
Calculation: New Moon AVWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high+low+close)/3 since the latest UTC New Moon event.
Long: Bar is in an event window with its signal slot unused AND close is within any prior-cycle GPS band AND (price root equals date root OR price root equals lunation root OR price root is 11, 22, or 33 OR price is repeating) AND NOT (reduced price root + reduced date root = 10, unless price root is 11, 22, or 33 or price is repeating) AND abs(close−open) ≥ 0.25×ATR AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (New Moon AVWAP is unavailable OR close > New Moon AVWAP).
Short: Bar is in an event window with its signal slot unused AND close is within any prior-cycle GPS band AND (price root equals date root OR price root equals lunation root OR price root is 11, 22, or 33 OR price is repeating) AND NOT (reduced price root + reduced date root = 10, unless price root is 11, 22, or 33 or price is repeating) AND abs(close−open) ≥ 0.25×ATR AND close < open AND close < previous close AND (New Moon AVWAP is unavailable OR close < New Moon AVWAP) AND long condition is false.
