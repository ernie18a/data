Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 multiplied by volume, divided by cumulative volume, reset at each session
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close and EMA = close and EMA from the current higher-timeframe bar, using lookahead off
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close - open) / open × 100
Long: Current close crosses above session VWAP OR (retest entry mode is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND the lowest low over the retest lookback is <= session VWAP) OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND (open <= session VWAP OR low <= session VWAP) AND candle body percentage >= its threshold AND volume >= volume SMA × momentum volume multiplier); AND entry-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA; AND higher-timeframe close > its EMA; AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier; AND the bar is confirmed unless intrabar entries are enabled; AND the strategy is not already long; AND the per-side daily trade limit and minimum bars between same-side trades permit entry; AND the session-opening cooldown has elapsed
Short: Current close crosses below session VWAP OR (retest entry mode is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND the highest high over the retest lookback is >= session VWAP) OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND (open >= session VWAP OR high >= session VWAP) AND candle body percentage >= its threshold AND volume >= volume SMA × momentum volume multiplier); AND entry-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA; AND higher-timeframe close < its EMA; AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier; AND the bar is confirmed unless intrabar entries are enabled; AND the strategy is not already short; AND the per-side daily trade limit and minimum bars between same-side trades permit entry; AND the session-opening cooldown has elapsed
Exit: Long, close < session VWAP AND entry-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA
Exit: Short, close > session VWAP AND entry-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA
Exit: Long, low <= current close - ATR × stop multiplier, when the ATR stop is enabled
Exit: Short, high >= current close + ATR × stop multiplier, when the ATR stop is enabled
