Calculation: A/D line = cumulative sum of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), using 0 when high = low
Long: A/D line > its signal EMA OR A/D line crosses above its signal EMA (current value > signal EMA and previous value ≤ previous signal EMA) OR a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low while A/D at the pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot low, with the pivot confirmations no more than the maximum pivot-separation lookback apart
Short: A/D line < its signal EMA OR A/D line crosses below its signal EMA (current value < signal EMA and previous value ≥ previous signal EMA) OR a confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high while A/D at the pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot high, with the pivot confirmations no more than the maximum pivot-separation lookback apart
