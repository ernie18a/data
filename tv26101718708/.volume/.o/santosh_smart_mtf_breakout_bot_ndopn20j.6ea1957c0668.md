Calculation: Trend on each of the 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute timeframes = bullish when the shorter-lookback EMA of close is above the longer-lookback EMA of close and RSI is above its upper threshold; bearish when the shorter-lookback EMA is below the longer-lookback EMA and RSI is below its lower threshold; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bar on each timeframe.
Long: 4-hour trend is bullish AND 1-hour trend is bullish AND 15-minute trend is bullish AND close is above the high of the last completed 15-minute bar AND volume is above its moving average AND ATR is at least its moving average.
Short: 4-hour trend is bearish AND 1-hour trend is bearish AND 15-minute trend is bearish AND close is below the low of the last completed 15-minute bar AND volume is above its moving average AND ATR is at least its moving average.
