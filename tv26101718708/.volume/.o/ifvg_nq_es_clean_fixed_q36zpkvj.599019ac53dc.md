Calculation: Prior HTF swing low/high = lowest low/highest high across the configured lookback of completed HTF bars before the current HTF bar.
Calculation: Bullish HTF sweep = current, possibly still-forming, HTF low < prior HTF swing low AND current HTF close > prior HTF swing low; bearish HTF sweep = current HTF high > prior HTF swing high AND current HTF close < prior HTF swing high.
Calculation: Bullish bias = set true on a bullish HTF sweep, set false on a bearish HTF sweep, otherwise retain its previous state; bearish bias = the inverse.
Calculation: Bullish gap size in ticks = (current bar low − high two chart bars ago) / tick size; bearish gap size in ticks = (low two chart bars ago − current bar high) / tick size.
Long: bullish bias AND (current bar low > high two chart bars ago AND bullish gap size in ticks ≥ configured minimum gap OR close > open AND current bar low ≤ high two chart bars ago) AND (volume > its configured lookback SMA if volume confirmation is enabled) AND (current bar is in the configured session if session filter is enabled)
Short: bearish bias AND (current bar high < low two chart bars ago AND bearish gap size in ticks ≥ configured minimum gap OR close < open AND current bar high ≥ low two chart bars ago) AND (volume > its configured lookback SMA if volume confirmation is enabled) AND (current bar is in the configured session if session filter is enabled)
