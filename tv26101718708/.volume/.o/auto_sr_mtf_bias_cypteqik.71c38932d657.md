Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively higher/lower than the configured number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after the bars on the right are available.
Calculation: Double bottom = the last three confirmed pivots are low-high-low; the two lows differ by no more than the pattern tolerance relative to the first low, and the middle high is above both lows.
Calculation: Inverse head and shoulders = the last five confirmed pivots are low-high-low-high-low; the middle low is below both outer lows, the outer lows differ by no more than the pattern tolerance relative to the first low, and the two highs differ by no more than 1.5 times that tolerance relative to the first high.
Calculation: Ascending triangle = among the last four alternating confirmed pivots, the two highs differ by less than the flat tolerance relative to the first high, and the second low is higher than the first by more than the flat tolerance relative to the first low.
Calculation: Falling wedge = among the last four alternating confirmed pivots, both the second high and second low are lower than their respective first values by more than the flat tolerance, and the highs' relative decline is greater than the lows' relative decline.
Calculation: Double top = the last three confirmed pivots are high-low-high; the two highs differ by no more than the pattern tolerance relative to the first high, and the middle low is below both highs.
Calculation: Head and shoulders = the last five confirmed pivots are high-low-high-low-high; the middle high is above both outer highs, the outer highs differ by no more than the pattern tolerance relative to the first high, and the two lows differ by no more than 1.5 times that tolerance relative to the first low.
Calculation: Descending triangle = among the last four alternating confirmed pivots, the two lows differ by less than the flat tolerance relative to the first low, and the second high is lower than the first by more than the flat tolerance relative to the first high.
Calculation: Rising wedge = among the last four alternating confirmed pivots, both the second high and second low are higher than their respective first values by more than the flat tolerance, and the lows' relative rise is greater than the highs' relative rise.
Long: the shorter-lookback simple moving average crosses above the longer-lookback simple moving average OR Double bottom OR Inverse head and shoulders OR Ascending triangle OR Falling wedge
Short: the shorter-lookback simple moving average crosses below the longer-lookback simple moving average OR Double top OR Head and shoulders OR Descending triangle OR Rising wedge
