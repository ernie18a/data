Calculation: Pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the preceding and following N bars; confirmed after the following N bars. Use N=pivot length for structure breaks and N=reversal lookback for reversal swings.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = the latest two confirmed pivot lows/highs make a lower low/higher high while RSI at those pivot bars makes a higher low/lower high.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = low below the latest reversal pivot low / high above the latest reversal pivot high, with close back above / below that pivot. Valid sweep additionally requires close below / above the trend EMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish ATR reclaim = low below / high above baseline EMA by the ATR extension multiple, and close above the lower / below the upper 1-ATR band.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish wick rejection = lower/upper wick exceeds body times the wick ratio and exceeds 0.25 ATR; use one minimum tick as body when body is zero.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish failed break = low below prior low / high above prior high, then close above prior low and open / below prior high and open.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish micro-confirmation = close above prior high / below prior low, or bullish/bearish failed break.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish Williams %R hook = prior %R at or below -80 / at or above -20 and current %R rises / falls.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish momentum fade = RSI below 45 / above 55 and rising / falling.
Calculation: Up/down volume ratio = sum of volume on bars closing above/below the prior close over the same lookback; ratio is up-volume sum divided by down-volume sum, or 1 if down-volume sum is zero.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish reversal score = sum: 25 for valid sweep, else 8 for non-valid sweep; 15 for divergence; 15 for wick rejection; 12 for volume at least the climax multiple of average volume; 15 for ATR reclaim; 10 for micro-confirmation; 10 for Williams %R hook; 8 if enough of the hourly, 4-hour, and daily SMC trends are non-bearish / non-bullish; 5 if price is within 2 ATR of the latest reversal swing low/high; 5 for momentum fade; 5 if up/down volume ratio is below 0.7 / above 1.5; 8 if volume is an all-time/highest-in-year high-volume bar and price is below/above the trend EMA. Cap at 100.
Calculation: SMC trend per hourly, 4-hour, and daily bars = bullish after close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, bearish after close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low, otherwise retain the prior trend (initially neutral). Use completed bars.
Long: close crosses above the latest structure pivot high AND prior structure trend is not bullish OR bullish reversal conditions OR high volume AND close >= open OR RSI > 50.
Short: close crosses below the latest structure pivot low AND prior structure trend is not bearish OR bearish reversal conditions OR high volume AND close < open OR RSI <= 50.
Calculation: Bullish reversal conditions = bullish sweep OR bullish ATR reclaim OR bullish failed break; bullish reversal score >= minimum; not locked since the latest confirmed reversal pivot low; bars since prior bullish reversal > cooldown; cooldown after last bearish structure break elapsed or bullish valid sweep; extension requirement disabled or close below trend EMA or bullish ATR reclaim; and not a strong bearish trend unless bullish valid sweep or bullish ATR reclaim. Strong bearish trend means close < 20-EMA < 50-EMA < 200-EMA and ADX > 30.
Calculation: Bearish reversal conditions = bearish sweep OR bearish ATR reclaim OR bearish failed break; bearish reversal score >= minimum; not locked since the latest confirmed reversal pivot high; bars since prior bearish reversal > cooldown; cooldown after last bullish structure break elapsed or bearish valid sweep; extension requirement disabled or close above trend EMA or bearish ATR reclaim; and not a strong bullish trend unless bearish valid sweep or bearish ATR reclaim. Strong bullish trend means close > 20-EMA > 50-EMA > 200-EMA and ADX > 30.
Calculation: High volume = volume > volume moving average times the volume multiplier.
