Calculation: VWAP-RSI = RSI of VWAP calculated from close
Long: VWAP-RSI crosses above its midpoint AND close > EMA of close
Short: VWAP-RSI crosses below its midpoint AND close < EMA of close
Exit: long, close is between the upper inner and outer Bollinger Bands
Exit: short, close is between the lower outer and inner Bollinger Bands
