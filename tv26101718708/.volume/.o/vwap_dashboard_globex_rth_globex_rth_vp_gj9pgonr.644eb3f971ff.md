Calculation: Globex VWAP = volume-weighted average of each bar’s OHLC4, accumulated from the Globex day start through the current bar; reset at the next Globex day start.
Calculation: RTH VWAP = volume-weighted average of each bar’s OHLC4, accumulated from the RTH session open through the current bar; available only during the RTH session.
Calculation: Globex and RTH value-area boundaries = build a live volume profile for the current session by assigning each bar’s volume to price buckets in proportion to each bucket’s overlap with the bar’s high-low range; start at the bucket with the greatest volume, then expand to adjacent buckets in descending volume order until the configured value-area fraction is reached. The boundaries are the outer edges of the selected buckets. Reset at the respective session start; RTH boundaries are available only during the RTH session.
Long: close >= Globex VWAP OR (during the RTH session AND close >= RTH VWAP) OR close > Globex value-area high OR (during the RTH session AND close > RTH value-area high)
Short: close < Globex VWAP OR (during the RTH session AND close < RTH VWAP) OR close < Globex value-area low OR (during the RTH session AND close < RTH value-area low)
