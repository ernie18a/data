Calculation: Intrabar buy/sell volume = aggregate the current chart bar’s lower-timeframe bars, using the selected granularity or its fallback; split each intrabar’s volume by the configured buy-share method (bid/ask when available, otherwise close location, body direction, or their configured blend). Use the completed chart bar’s intrabars.
Calculation: Bar delta = intrabar buy volume − intrabar sell volume; CVD = cumulative bar delta.
Calculation: Footprint profile = divide the current chart bar’s high–low range into configured equal rows; allocate lower-timeframe volume and buy/sell volume to each row in proportion to price-range overlap. POC is the row with greatest volume; POC location is its midpoint’s position in the bar range. Auction shape is B if at least two rows between the first and last rows with volume ≥70% of the maximum have volume <35% of maximum and those rows are at least three indices apart; otherwise P if POC location ≥0.62, b if ≤0.38, else D. Upper/lower excess = clamp(1 − mean volume of the two extreme rows / mean row volume, 0, 1). Stacked imbalance is the longest consecutive run of rows where buy volume is greater than the configured ratio times sell volume one row below, or sell volume is greater than the ratio times buy volume one row above; retain its direction and ending row.
Calculation: Contrarian score = clamp to 0–10 the weighted sum of: wick rejection (faded-side wick / bar range, scaled to 0–1); effort (relative volume above its 20-chart-bar average × body fraction of range, each scaled to 0–1); trapped delta (delta opposite the close’s position relative to bar midpoint, scaled by absolute delta / total volume); excess at the faded extreme; P/b shape alignment (POC position beyond midpoint); CVD divergence (new chart-bar high with CVD below the prior lookback maximum, or new low with CVD above the prior lookback minimum); and an oppositely directed stacked imbalance near the faded extreme. Multiply by 0.55 for B shape before clamping.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with a valid footprint, low < the lowest low of the preceding configured lookback of chart bars, low ≤ EMA(close, configured length) − configured ATR extension × ATR(14) when mean extension is required, lower wick ≥ upper wick, contrarian score ≥ configured minimum, auction shape ≠ B, and cooldown elapsed; the bar must not also qualify as a short candidate.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with a valid footprint, high > the highest high of the preceding configured lookback of chart bars, high ≥ EMA(close, configured length) + configured ATR extension × ATR(14) when mean extension is required, upper wick ≥ lower wick, contrarian score ≥ configured minimum, auction shape ≠ B, and cooldown elapsed.
