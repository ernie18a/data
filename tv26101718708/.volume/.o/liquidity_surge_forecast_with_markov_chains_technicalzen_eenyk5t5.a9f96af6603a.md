Calculation: Centered momentum = clamp((HMA of MFI or RSI − 50) × 2 × scale, −100, 100); use the middle HMA from a short-to-long set.
Calculation: Hull-VWMA oscillator = clamp((current HMA of VWMA(close) − its value 2 bars earlier) / its earlier value × 100 × scale, −100, 100); use the middle HMA from a short-to-long set.
Calculation: Projected value = clamp(current value + (current value − value lookback bars earlier) / lookback × forecast horizon × decay^forecast horizon, −100, 100).
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Price angle = atan(mean per-bar change over the lookback / standard deviation of one-bar changes over the lookback), in degrees; if the standard deviation is zero, use 0 for zero mean change, otherwise the mean change's signed 90° limit.
Calculation: Directional consensus = classify close, high, and low angles using short and longer lookbacks: up/down when both angles exceed the trend threshold in that direction, sideways when both absolute angles are below the sideways threshold, otherwise neutral; consensus is the state with strictly more votes than each other state. If neither up nor down has a strict vote majority, state is sideways.
Calculation: Markov next-state probabilities = exponentially decayed transition counts for the pair of preceding states to the next state, using separate counts according to whether the current state has lasted less than or at least the duration split; add equal Laplace pseudocounts to each next state before normalizing. Confident means the active context's decayed count meets the minimum sample threshold.
Long: First bar where projected centered momentum and projected selected oscillator are both above 0, and neither the Choppiness Index nor directional consensus indicates chop/sideways; OR projected centered momentum and projected selected oscillator are both above 0, the Markov prediction is not confidently Bear, the Choppiness Index does not indicate chop, and directional consensus is up or a confident Markov prediction from sideways assigns Bull at least the hatchling threshold; OR directional consensus is sideways and a confident Markov prediction assigns Bull at least the hatchling threshold.
Short: First bar where projected centered momentum and projected selected oscillator are both below 0, and neither the Choppiness Index nor directional consensus indicates chop/sideways; OR projected centered momentum and projected selected oscillator are both below 0, the Markov prediction is not confidently Bull, the Choppiness Index does not indicate chop, and directional consensus is down or a confident Markov prediction from sideways assigns Bear at least the hatchling threshold; OR directional consensus is sideways and a confident Markov prediction assigns Bear at least the hatchling threshold.
