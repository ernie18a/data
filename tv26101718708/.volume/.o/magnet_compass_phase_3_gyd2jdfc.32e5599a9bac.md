Calculation: Close location = (close of the last completed reference bar − its low) / (its high − its low)
Calculation: Prior bias = signed historical first-hit edge for the reference bar’s close-time bucket; positive favors its high, negative its low
Calculation: Live score = close-location score (+ above the upper threshold, − below the lower threshold, otherwise 0) + VWAP-side score (+ if reference close is above the previous chart-bar VWAP, otherwise −) + trend score (+ if the shorter-period EMA exceeds the longer-period EMA, − if below, otherwise 0) + prior-bias sign
Calculation: Magnet midpoint = (high + low of the last completed reference bar) / 2
Calculation: Stop level = selected stop rule: opposite magnet extreme, midpoint, fixed distance from entry, a multiple of ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe bar, or nearest unswept swing beyond entry (fall back to opposite magnet extreme); include the configured buffer
Calculation: Target level = bias-side magnet extreme or magnet midpoint
Long: At each reference-bar close, prior bias is positive, live score is positive and meets the minimum magnitude, close location is within the configured mid-band, and (swing mode: the first subsequent chart bar has not tagged the magnet high; scalp mode: magnet range meets its minimum and price reaches the resting limit below the magnet low)
Short: At each reference-bar close, prior bias is negative, live score is negative and meets the minimum magnitude, close location is within the configured mid-band, and (swing mode: the first subsequent chart bar has not tagged the magnet low; scalp mode: magnet range meets its minimum and price reaches the resting limit above the magnet high)
Exit: long, low ≤ stop level OR high ≥ target level OR at the next reference-bar close, the last chart-bar close is ≤ entry price
Exit: short, high ≥ stop level OR low ≤ target level OR at the next reference-bar close, the last chart-bar close is ≥ entry price
