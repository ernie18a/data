Calculation: NY-session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price since the 09:30 America/New_York session open, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3; reset at each session open.
Calculation: VWAP bands = NY-session VWAP ± band multiplier × weighted standard deviation, where weighted standard deviation = sqrt(cumulative volume-weighted squared difference between each bar’s typical price and that bar’s VWAP / cumulative volume).
Calculation: Close position = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low); when high = low, use 50%.
Long: During the NY trading window (delay after 09:30 through 16:00 America/New_York) AND (abs(low − VWAP) ≤ proximity threshold OR abs(high − VWAP) ≤ proximity threshold OR low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP) AND close > upper VWAP band AND close > open AND close position ≥ close-percentage threshold AND low > lower VWAP band.
Short: During the NY trading window (delay after 09:30 through 16:00 America/New_York) AND (abs(low − VWAP) ≤ proximity threshold OR abs(high − VWAP) ≤ proximity threshold OR low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP) AND close < lower VWAP band AND close < open AND close position ≤ 100% − close-percentage threshold AND high < upper VWAP band.
