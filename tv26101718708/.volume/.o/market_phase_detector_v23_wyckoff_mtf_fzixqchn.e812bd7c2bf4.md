Calculation: Trading range high/low = highest high and lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Trading range = absolute close change from the range-lookback bar divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback is below the sideways threshold.
Calculation: Trend score = clamp to [-100, 100] the sum of six scaled, individually clamped ATR-normalized components: close minus fast EMA; fast minus slow EMA; fast EMA slope; slow EMA slope; close momentum; and the average linear-regression slopes of highs and lows. Fast EMA uses a shorter length than slow EMA; slopes use a fixed prior-bar offset, and momentum and structure use configurable lookbacks.
Calculation: Accumulation pressure = capped sum of configurable points for trading-range status, CMF (more points above a positive threshold than merely above zero), rising OBV slope, rising A/D slope, up-volume dominance, recent downside sweep, confirmed Spring, and bullish structural recovery.
Calculation: Distribution pressure = capped sum of configurable points for trading-range status, CMF (more points below a negative threshold than merely below zero), falling OBV slope, falling A/D slope, down-volume dominance, recent upside sweep, confirmed UTAD, and bearish structural damage.
Calculation: Downside sweep = low below the prior trading-range low and close above it; upside sweep = high above the prior trading-range high and close below it. A sweep is recent if it occurred within the configurable sweep-confirmation window.
Calculation: Bullish structural recovery = close above the range midpoint and fast EMA, and the high linear-regression slope is positive. Bearish structural damage = close below the range midpoint and fast EMA, and the high linear-regression slope is negative.
Calculation: Confirmed Spring = recent downside sweep AND trading range AND bullish structural recovery AND CMF > 0 AND OBV slope > 0. Confirmed UTAD = recent upside sweep AND trading range AND bearish structural damage AND CMF < 0 AND OBV slope < 0.
Calculation: SOS = close above prior trading-range high AND relative volume (volume divided by its moving average) at or above the configurable breakout threshold AND CMF > 0. SOW = close below prior trading-range low AND relative volume at or above that threshold AND CMF < 0.
Calculation: Raw phase = when trading range: Accumulation if accumulation pressure meets its threshold and exceeds distribution pressure by the required margin, unless trend context selects Re-Accumulation (trend score above its minimum) or Re-Distribution (trend score below its maximum and no bullish recovery); Distribution if distribution pressure meets its threshold and exceeds accumulation pressure by the required margin, unless strong bullish context without structural damage or SOW selects Re-Accumulation, or bearish trend context selects Re-Distribution; otherwise Re-Accumulation for strong bullish trend, Re-Distribution for strong bearish trend, else Neutral. Outside a trading range: Markup if trend score meets its positive threshold, Markdown if it meets its negative threshold, else Neutral.
Calculation: Stable phase = raw phase only when it equals the raw phase on each of the required consecutive bars; otherwise Neutral.
Calculation: Event mark = event is true now and was false on the prior bar, and no same-type event mark occurred within the configurable cooldown.
Long: Stable phase changes to Accumulation, Markup, or Re-Accumulation OR confirmed Spring event mark OR SOS event mark OR downside sweep event mark.
Short: Stable phase changes to Distribution, Markdown, or Re-Distribution OR confirmed UTAD event mark OR SOW event mark OR upside sweep event mark.
