Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest in a symmetric window with an equal configurable number of bars on each side; confirmed after the right-side bars close.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order-block zone = the full high-low range of the last bearish/bullish candle among the configured number of bars ending at the earliest lowest low/high, respectively, from the swing bar through the breakout bar.
Long: On a closed bar, price makes its first touch of an active, untouched support zone (low <= zone top) and closes at or above its bottom; the zone was created on the first close above the last confirmed, unused swing high within its allowed age, the breakout candle has a body at least as large as ATR OR its low is above the high two bars earlier, and the zone did not overlap another live support zone when created.
Short: On a closed bar, price makes its first touch of an active, untouched resistance zone (high >= zone bottom) and closes at or below its top; the zone was created on the first close below the last confirmed, unused swing low within its allowed age, the breakout candle has a body at least as large as ATR OR its high is below the low two bars earlier, and the zone did not overlap another live resistance zone when created.
