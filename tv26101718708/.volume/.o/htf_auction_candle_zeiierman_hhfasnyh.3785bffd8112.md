Calculation: Estimated buy share per lower-timeframe candle = 0.5 if high = low; otherwise clamp(0.5 + 0.43 × [0.48 × (2 × clamp((close − low)/(high − low), 0, 1) − 1) + 0.32 × clamp((close − open)/(high − low), −1, 1) + 0.20 × ((min(open, close) − low − (high − max(open, close)))/(high − low))], 0.025, 0.975). Use lower-timeframe candles within the current forming higher-timeframe candle, including a forming lower-timeframe candle when available.
Calculation: Estimated buy/sell volume per price cell = lower-timeframe volume × estimated buy share / remaining volume, distributed across cells touched by that candle’s range. For nonzero range, distribute each side in proportion to touch × [1 + 2.6 × (1 − min(abs(cell midpoint − side anchor)/(high − low), 1))^concentration], normalized across touched cells separately for each side; buy anchor = clamp(0.50 × close + 0.32 × high + 0.18 × open, low, high), sell anchor = clamp(0.50 × close + 0.32 × low + 0.18 × open, low, high). For zero range, assign both sides to the cell containing close. Cells partition the current forming higher-timeframe candle’s high-low range.
Calculation: Battle score per each of 20 equal segments of the current forming higher-timeframe range = 0.70 × (segment buy volume − segment sell volume)/segment total volume + 0.30 × (buy win evidence − sell win evidence)/total win evidence; a zero denominator contributes 0. Win evidence is the absolute value of (2 × estimated buy share − 1), allocated by the fraction of the lower-timeframe candle range overlapping the segment, to the buy or sell side according to its sign. Segment buy/sell volume is the estimated cell volume apportioned by overlap with that segment.
Long: Current forming higher-timeframe close >= its open
Short: Current forming higher-timeframe close < its open
Long: Estimated buy volume in a price cell > estimated sell volume in that cell
Short: Estimated sell volume in a price cell > estimated buy volume in that cell
Long: Battle score > 0 AND segment total volume > 0 AND abs(battle score) × sqrt(segment total volume / maximum segment total volume) > 0.0001
