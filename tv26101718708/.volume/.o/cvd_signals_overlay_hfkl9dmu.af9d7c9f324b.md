Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of per-bar delta, resetting at the selected session, day, week, or month boundary, or never. Per-bar delta is volume signed positive when close ≥ open and negative otherwise; alternatively, it is volume scaled by the close’s position relative to the bar’s high-low midpoint, or volume signed by whether close rose or fell from the prior close, according to the selected estimation method.
Long: Current cumulative volume delta > its selected moving average AND prior cumulative volume delta ≤ its prior moving average AND (no prior raw downward cross exists OR bars since the most recent raw downward cross ≥ the minimum-bars-between-signals parameter).
Short: Current cumulative volume delta < its selected moving average AND prior cumulative volume delta ≥ its prior moving average AND (no prior raw upward cross exists OR bars since the most recent raw upward cross ≥ the minimum-bars-between-signals parameter).
