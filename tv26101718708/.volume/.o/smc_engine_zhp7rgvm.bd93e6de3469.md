Calculation: Open-based session range high/low = highest/lowest open among bars in the selected session; use the developing range during the session and the most recently completed session range otherwise.
Calculation: Spread anomaly = current bar high − low > multiplier × moving average of high − low over the baseline lookback.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume pressure = sum of volume for lower-timeframe bars closing above open minus volume for lower-timeframe bars closing below open within the current bar.
Long: (Spread anomaly AND low < open-based session range low AND close >= open-based session range low AND lower-timeframe volume pressure > 0) OR (Spread anomaly AND lower-timeframe volume pressure > 0 AND close > open AND NOT (low < open-based session range low AND close >= open-based session range low)) OR (NOT Spread anomaly AND close > open-based session range high AND lower-timeframe volume pressure > 0)
Short: (Spread anomaly AND high > open-based session range high AND close <= open-based session range high AND lower-timeframe volume pressure < 0) OR (Spread anomaly AND lower-timeframe volume pressure < 0 AND close < open AND NOT (high > open-based session range high AND close <= open-based session range high)) OR (NOT Spread anomaly AND close < open-based session range low AND lower-timeframe volume pressure < 0)
