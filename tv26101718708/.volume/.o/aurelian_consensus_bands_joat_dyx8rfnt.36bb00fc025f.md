Calculation: Consensus profile levels = On current-timeframe bars, distribute each bar’s volume evenly across logarithmic price rows spanning its low to high, weighted by bar recency. Use a rolling price canvas bounded by the greater of the rolling high and its EMA and the lesser of the rolling low and its EMA. The highest-volume row gives VPOC; the volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of row indices give consensus area bounds at mean ±1 standard deviation and boundary bounds at mean ±2 standard deviations. Smooth each extracted level with an EMA.
Calculation: Active anchor = selected smoothed VPOC, geometric mean of the smoothed consensus area bounds, or geometric mean of smoothed VPOC and consensus area midpoint.
Calculation: VPOC and consensus midpoint positions = each anchor’s close displacement divided by the distance from that anchor to the corresponding boundary-band edge; clamp each to −1.5 through 1.5.
Calculation: Direction = mean of the VPOC and consensus midpoint positions. Coherence = twice their product divided by the sum of their squares, or zero when that sum is zero. Consensus score = absolute direction × absolute coherence, clamped to 0 through 1.5.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the active anchor AND absolute coherence is at least the coherence threshold AND consensus score is at least the signal threshold AND close is above the lower consensus-area bound.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the active anchor AND absolute coherence is at least the coherence threshold AND consensus score is at least the signal threshold AND close is below the upper consensus-area bound.
