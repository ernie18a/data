Calculation: qualification score = count of these conditions that are true: close above the long-term EMA; shorter-term EMA above the long-term EMA; long-term EMA above its value at an earlier lookback; weekly close above weekly long-term EMA; normalized relative strength above 1; RSI within its healthy band; current shorter-term average volume above that average at an earlier lookback
Calculation: normalized relative strength = (instrument close / instrument long-term EMA) / (benchmark daily close / benchmark daily long-term EMA); use daily benchmark bars as returned without lookahead
Long: close retests the long-term EMA from above within a configurable tolerance AND close is above the long-term EMA AND no such retest occurred during the preceding deduplication lookback AND qualification score >= configurable threshold AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > its configurable average × configurable multiplier)
Exit: long, qualification score < configurable threshold AND previous bar's qualification score >= configurable threshold
