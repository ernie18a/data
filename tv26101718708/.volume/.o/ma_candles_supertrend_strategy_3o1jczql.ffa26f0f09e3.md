Calculation: MA candle open/high/low/close = apply the same selected moving average and lookback separately to each chart-bar OHLC series.
Calculation: Supertrend direction and stops = on MA candle OHLC, true range is max(MA high, prior MA close) − min(MA low, prior MA close); smooth true range with a separately selected moving average and lookback, then multiply by the ATR multiplier. Long stop starts at MA close − ATR and ratchets up to the prior long stop when prior MA low is above it; short stop starts at MA close + ATR and ratchets down to the prior short stop when prior MA high is below it. Direction starts at +1; it switches to +1 when prior direction is −1 and current MA high exceeds the prior short stop, and switches to −1 when prior direction is +1 and current MA low is below the prior long stop; otherwise it persists.
Long: supertrend direction = +1 AND (prior direction = +1 AND close > prior chart-bar close OR close > short stop) AND (close > last completed 12-month high × (1 − d-threshold) OR close > last completed 12-month low × (1 + r-threshold)) AND datetime >= configured start time AND datetime <= configured end time
Short: supertrend direction = −1 AND (prior direction = −1 AND close < prior chart-bar close OR close < long stop) AND (close < last completed 12-month low × (1 + d-threshold) OR close < last completed 12-month high × (1 − r-threshold)) AND datetime >= configured start time AND datetime <= configured end time
Exit: long, supertrend direction = −1 AND (prior direction = −1 AND close < prior chart-bar close OR close < long stop)
Exit: short, supertrend direction = +1 AND (prior direction = +1 AND close > prior chart-bar close OR close > short stop)
