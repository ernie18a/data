Calculation: Momentum slope = change over a lookback in the HMA of the selected momentum source (MFI or RSI), using a middle-length HMA.
Calculation: Price-current slope = change over the same lookback in the middle layer of the selected price-current series: either the percentage slope over two bars of a middle-length HMA of VWMA(close, lookback), or a middle-length HMA of ADX signed positive when +DI ≥ −DI and negative otherwise.
Long: Momentum slope > 0 AND Price-current slope > 0
Short: Momentum slope < 0 AND Price-current slope < 0
