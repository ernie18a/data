Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low pivot using equal left- and right-side lookbacks on the current timeframe; calculate the same pivots on the correlated symbol.
Long: On an intraday bar, low < the last completed higher-timeframe bar's low AND close > that low OR low > high two bars ago AND the gap meets the configured minimum size OR a confirmed main-symbol pivot low is lower than its preceding pivot low AND the latest two correlated-symbol pivot lows are available AND the latest correlated pivot is within the configured maximum bar age AND the latest correlated pivot low is higher than its preceding pivot low OR close > the top of an active bearish FVG zone before it has inverted.
Short: On an intraday bar, high > the last completed higher-timeframe bar's high AND close < that high OR high < low two bars ago AND the gap meets the configured minimum size OR a confirmed main-symbol pivot high is higher than its preceding pivot high AND the latest two correlated-symbol pivot highs are available AND the latest correlated pivot is within the configured maximum bar age AND the latest correlated pivot high is lower than its preceding pivot high OR close < the bottom of an active bullish FVG zone before it has inverted.
