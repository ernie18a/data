Calculation: Upper Donchian level = highest close or high, according to the level setting, over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: Lower Donchian level = lowest close or low, according to the level setting, over the lookback bars before the current bar
Calculation: ATR buffer = ATR × buffer multiplier
Calculation: Cloud top = maximum of the current Ichimoku Span A and Span B; Span A is the midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun, and Span B is the midpoint of the lookback high and low
Calculation: Cloud bottom = minimum of the current Ichimoku Span A and Span B
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its moving average
Long: (no prior signal OR bars since the prior signal > cooldown) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > minimum) AND (EMA filters disabled OR close > each enabled EMA, with the configured EMAs ordered from shorter to longer lookback) AND (long-term EMA filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR close > cloud top) AND ((close confirmation enabled AND close > upper Donchian level + ATR buffer) OR (close confirmation disabled AND high > upper Donchian level + ATR buffer)) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR close > Supertrend) AND (ATR market filter disabled OR ATR ratio > threshold)
Short: (no prior signal OR bars since the prior signal > cooldown) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > minimum) AND (EMA filters disabled OR close < each enabled EMA, with the configured EMAs ordered from shorter to longer lookback) AND (long-term EMA filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR close < cloud bottom) AND ((close confirmation enabled AND close < lower Donchian level − ATR buffer) OR (close confirmation disabled AND low < lower Donchian level − ATR buffer)) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR close < Supertrend) AND (ATR market filter disabled OR ATR ratio > threshold)
