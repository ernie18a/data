Calculation: AlphaTrend = using the prior completed chart bar’s low and high, ATR (the average true range over a lookback, shifted one bar) and MFI (computed from typical price and volume, shifted one bar): if MFI >= 50, take the greater of the prior AlphaTrend and prior low minus ATR times a coefficient; otherwise take the lesser of the prior AlphaTrend and prior high plus ATR times a coefficient.
Calculation: Breakout override = absolute change between the prior two closes > the prior ATR times a sensitivity factor.
Calculation: Signal age = number of bars since the prior raw signal of that direction; each age resets to zero when that direction’s raw signal occurred on the preceding bar, otherwise it increases by one.
Long: ((AlphaTrend[1] > AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] <= AlphaTrend[4]) OR (Breakout override AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] <= AlphaTrend[2])) AND age since raw long signal > age since raw short signal; OR a valid long signal on the last completed higher-timeframe bar at a higher-timeframe bar boundary.
Short: ((AlphaTrend[1] < AlphaTrend[3] AND AlphaTrend[2] >= AlphaTrend[4]) OR (Breakout override AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1] AND close[2] >= AlphaTrend[2])) AND age since raw short signal > age since raw long signal; OR a valid short signal on the last completed higher-timeframe bar at a higher-timeframe bar boundary.
Exit: long, position is open AND age since raw long signal < age since raw short signal AND close[1] < AlphaTrend[1]
Exit: short, position is open AND age since raw short signal < age since raw long signal AND close[1] > AlphaTrend[1]
