Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot with the configured swing lookback bars on both sides; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each kind
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA trend = fast, middle, and slow EMAs calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, with fast > middle > slow for bullish and fast < middle < slow for bearish
Long: close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high
Short: close crosses below the most recently confirmed swing low
Long: the most recent MSB break is bullish AND (fast EMA crosses above middle EMA OR middle EMA crosses above LWMA) AND higher-timeframe EMA trend is bullish
Short: the most recent MSB break is bearish AND (fast EMA crosses below middle EMA OR middle EMA crosses below LWMA) AND higher-timeframe EMA trend is bearish
