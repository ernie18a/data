Calculation: Open series and close series = the same selected moving-average type and lookback applied to chart-bar open and close, each shifted by one chart bar; use the current chart timeframe or the alternate timeframe formed by multiplying it by a parameter, using the latest completed alternate-timeframe bar
Calculation: Chop angle = the angle in degrees of a unit horizontal step and a vertical step equal to (previous-bar EMA( close, 34 ) − current EMA( close, 34 )) / current HLC3 × 25 × lowest low of the last 30 chart bars / (highest high − lowest low over the last 30 chart bars); classify the angle into zones: turquoise ≥ 5, dark green [3.57, 5), pale green [2.14, 3.57), lime [0.71, 2.14), yellow (−0.71, 0.71), light orange (−2.14, −0.71], orange (−3.57, −2.14], red (−5, −3.57], dark red ≤ −5
Long: close series crosses above open series AND the current chop-angle zone is not enabled as choppy
Short: close series crosses below open series AND the current chop-angle zone is enabled as choppy
Exit: long, close series crosses below open series when long-only mode is selected OR low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance (if enabled) OR high ≥ entry price + configured target distance (if enabled)
Exit: short, close series crosses above open series when short-only mode is selected OR high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance (if enabled) OR low ≤ entry price − configured target distance (if enabled)
