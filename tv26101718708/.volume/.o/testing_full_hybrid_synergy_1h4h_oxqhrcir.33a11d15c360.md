Calculation: Reverse MACD equilibrium = price at which the fast and slow EMAs become equal, solved from their previous-bar values; set to zero if nonpositive.
Calculation: MACD signal-cross price = price at which the MACD line equals its signal EMA, solved from previous-bar EMA values; set to zero if nonpositive.
Calculation: Squeeze off = Bollinger lower band < Keltner lower band AND Bollinger upper band > Keltner upper band.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression, at the current bar, of close minus the average of (highest high and lowest low)/2 and the close SMA, over the Keltner lookback.
Long: flat AND (4-hour current-bar close > 4-hour 200 EMA AND 4-hour 50 EMA > 4-hour 200 EMA) AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > prior chart-timeframe bar histogram AND RSI > lower threshold AND RSI < upper threshold AND close > chart-timeframe 50 EMA AND close > Reverse MACD equilibrium AND close > MACD signal-cross price AND Squeeze off AND Squeeze momentum > 0 AND Squeeze momentum > prior chart-timeframe bar value AND accumulation/distribution > prior chart-timeframe bar value
Exit: long, chart-timeframe 50 EMA < chart-timeframe 200 EMA OR RSI > upper exit threshold OR (MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < prior chart-timeframe bar histogram) OR close < MACD signal-cross price OR (Squeeze momentum < 0 AND Squeeze momentum < prior chart-timeframe bar value) OR accumulation/distribution < prior chart-timeframe bar value OR high reaches the configured profit limit above entry price OR low reaches the configured ATR trailing stop below the current close
