Calculation: Signal score = +1 for a listed long condition, −1 for a listed short condition, and 0 otherwise; for momentum and MACD, a value of zero scores −1.
Calculation: Moving-average rating = mean of 15 scores: six short-to-long lookback simple moving averages compared with chart close, the same six lookbacks again (the EMA slots also use simple moving averages), plus simple moving averages with the 9 and 20 lookbacks (used in the Hull and VWMA slots); the Ichimoku slot scores 0. All moving averages use the selected other timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = mean of the 11 oscillator scores below, calculated on the selected other timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Rating = mean of the moving-average rating and oscillator rating.
Long: rating > 0 OR any of the 14 higher-timeframe simple moving averages < chart close OR RSI < 30 and rising OR stochastic K < 20 and K > D OR CCI < −100 and falling OR Awesome Oscillator > 0 and rising OR momentum > 0 OR MACD > 0 OR Stoch RSI K < 20 and K > D OR Williams %R < −80 and rising OR Ultimate Oscillator ≥ 70 OR ADX ≥ 20 and +DI > −DI OR bear power < 0 and rising
Short: rating < 0 OR any of the 14 higher-timeframe simple moving averages > chart close OR RSI > 70 and falling OR stochastic K > 80 and K < D OR CCI > 100 and rising OR Awesome Oscillator < 0 and falling OR momentum ≤ 0 OR MACD ≤ 0 OR Stoch RSI K > 80 and K < D OR Williams %R > −20 and falling OR Ultimate Oscillator ≤ 30 OR ADX ≥ 20 and +DI < −DI OR bull power > 0 and falling
