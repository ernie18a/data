Calculation: Smoothed Stochastic %K = the moving average of 100 × (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over the %K lookback; %D = the moving average of %K over the %D smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = the latest confirmed close-price pivot low is below the previous one, while the RSI value at the latest confirmed RSI pivot low is above the previous one; bearish RSI divergence = the latest confirmed close-price pivot high is above the previous one, while the RSI value at the latest confirmed RSI pivot high is below the previous one.
Long: On any configured scan timeframe’s still-forming bar, CMF is at or below its buy threshold OR RSI is above 50 OR bullish RSI divergence is present OR smoothed Stochastic %K crosses above %D OR (%K is below 20 and the last %K/%D crossover state is neutral).
Short: On any configured scan timeframe’s still-forming bar, CMF is at or above its sell threshold OR RSI is at or below 50 OR bearish RSI divergence is present OR smoothed Stochastic %K crosses below %D OR (%K is above 80 and the last %K/%D crossover state is neutral).
