Calculation: Trend factor = clamp(0.65 × (fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR + 0.35 × (fast EMA − fast EMA from the slope lookback bars ago) / (ATR × √slope lookback), −1, 1); slow EMA length exceeds fast EMA length.
Calculation: Momentum factor = clamp((RSI − 50) / 25, −1, 1).
Calculation: Volume factor = EMA of close location × capped relative volume / 2.5, where close location = clamp((2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), −1, 1), relative volume = clamp(volume / its baseline, 0, 2.5), and volume is available only when at least 80% of baseline-lookback bars have positive volume; omit its contribution otherwise.
Calculation: Trend strength = absolute EMA spread / ATR.
Calculation: Path efficiency = clamp(abs(close − close from the regime lookback bars ago) / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback, 0, 1).
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR / its regime-lookback simple moving average.
Calculation: Regime = VOLATILE when volatility ratio reaches its entry threshold, and remain VOLATILE while it exceeds its lower hold threshold; otherwise TREND when trend strength and path efficiency both reach their entry thresholds, or both reach their lower hold thresholds if the prior regime was TREND; otherwise RANGE when trend strength or path efficiency is at or below its maximum threshold; otherwise TRANSITION.
Calculation: Regime-gated score = 100 × (weighted trend factor + weighted momentum factor + weighted volume factor) × (0.55 + 0.45 × agreement) × volatility gate. Use trend/momentum/volume weights of 55/30/15 in TREND, 15/60/25 in RANGE, 40/35/25 in VOLATILE, and 35/40/25 in TRANSITION; omit the volume weight and renormalize the remaining weights when volume is unavailable. In RANGE, negate momentum factor. Agreement = clamp(abs(sum of weighted factors) / sum of their absolute values, 0, 1). Volatility gate = clamp(volatility entry threshold / volatility ratio, 0.45, 1) in VOLATILE and 1 otherwise. Score is unavailable until its required calculations are valid and the slow EMA length exceeds the fast EMA length.
Long: Current score > directional threshold AND previous score ≤ directional threshold AND current bar is confirmed.
Short: Current score < −directional threshold AND previous score ≥ −directional threshold AND current bar is confirmed.
