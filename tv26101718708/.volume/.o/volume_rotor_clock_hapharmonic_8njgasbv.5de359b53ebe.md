Calculation: Buy volume per bar = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) when high > low; otherwise volume / 2
Calculation: Sell volume per bar = volume × (high − close) / (high − low) when high > low; otherwise volume / 2
Long: Sum of buy volume over the configured lookback, including the current bar, ≥ sum of sell volume over the same bars
Short: Sum of sell volume over the configured lookback, including the current bar, > sum of buy volume over the same bars
