Use Wilder ATR of true range, scale its distance by the path-volatility factor from fast/slow EWMA squared returns and signed-return drift, then ratchet the stop behind price and flip direction when close crosses it. Higher-timeframe calculations use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Bias score = sum of the four stop directions, positive when bullish and negative when bearish, with successively greater weights for higher timeframes.
Calculation: Trade allowed = absolute bias score exceeds the choppy-market floor plus the volatility-cluster lift; the lift increases with decaying shock intensity relative to its baseline and is capped at the major-signal threshold.
Calculation: Pair band = the interval between the two corresponding volatility stops.
