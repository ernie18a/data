Calculation: Smoothed ZLSMA = linreg(hlc3, lookback) + [linreg(hlc3, lookback) − linreg(linreg(hlc3, lookback), lookback)]
Calculation: Normalized ZLSMA = average of (Smoothed ZLSMA’s angle, normalized to [−1, 1] using its rolling high and low) and (Smoothed ZLSMA, normalized to [−1, 1] using its rolling high and low); angle is the lookback-length average of arctan(change in Smoothed ZLSMA / prior Smoothed ZLSMA), in degrees, multiplied by 10
Calculation: Box upper/lower = linear regression of the respective box boundary. The upper boundary is the most recent high that exceeded the prior box-lookback highest high, captured when bars since that breakout equals box-length minus 2 and the highest high over box-length minus 2 bars is below that over box-length minus 1 bars. The lower boundary is the lowest low over the box lookback, captured at the same time. Use the same box-length parameter for these calculations.
Calculation: Normalized box = average of the upper and lower box boundaries after each is normalized to [−1, 1] using the rolling highest upper boundary and lowest lower boundary over the same lookback.
Calculation: Normalized trend strength = EMA of the directional index difference divided by the sum of the positive and negative directional indices, using the same ADX length for Wilder-smoothed true range and positive/negative directional movement and for the EMA. Positive/negative directional movement is the greater positive upward-high/downward-low change, respectively, or zero.
Long: Normalized ZLSMA crosses above Normalized box AND (Normalized trend strength ≥ 0 OR its value two bars ago < 0 and the last three values are strictly rising)
Short: Normalized ZLSMA crosses below Normalized box AND (Normalized trend strength ≤ 0 OR its value two bars ago > 0 and the last three values are strictly falling)
