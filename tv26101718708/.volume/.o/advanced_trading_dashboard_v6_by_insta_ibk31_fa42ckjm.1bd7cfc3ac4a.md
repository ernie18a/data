Calculation: Bull score = count of these six conditions that are true: RSI is within the bullish band; shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close); MACD histogram > 0 and > its prior-bar value; stochastic %K > %D and below its upper threshold; +DI > -DI and ADX > its trend threshold; close > open and volume > its moving average.
Calculation: Bear score = count of these six conditions that are true: RSI is within the bearish band; shorter-lookback EMA(close) < longer-lookback EMA(close); MACD histogram < 0 and < its prior-bar value; stochastic %K < %D and above its lower threshold; -DI > +DI and ADX > its trend threshold; close < open and volume > its moving average.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior bar closes below its open, current bar closes above its open, current close > prior open, and current open < prior close.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = prior bar closes above its open, current bar closes below its open, current close < prior open, and current open > prior close.
Calculation: Hammer = range > 3 × absolute body, (close − low)/(range + 0.001) > 0.6, and (high − close)/(range + 0.001) < 0.1.
Calculation: Shooting star = range > 3 × absolute body, (high − close)/(range + 0.001) > 0.6, and (close − low)/(range + 0.001) < 0.1.
Calculation: Morning star = bar two bars ago closes below its open, prior bar's absolute body ≤ 10% of its range, current bar closes above its open, and current close > the midpoint of the open and close from two bars ago.
Calculation: Evening star = bar two bars ago closes above its open, prior bar's absolute body ≤ 10% of its range, current bar closes below its open, and current close < the midpoint of the open and close from two bars ago.
Calculation: Three white soldiers = current and prior two bars close above their opens, with closes increasing across the three bars.
Calculation: Three black crows = current and prior two bars close below their opens, with closes decreasing across the three bars.
Long: Bull score ≥ 3 OR bullish engulfing OR hammer OR morning star OR three white soldiers.
Short: Bear score ≥ 3 OR bearish engulfing OR shooting star OR evening star OR three black crows.
