Calculation: Directional pressure score = optionally EMA-smoothed signed percentile rank of the absolute raw pressure over a longer lookback; raw pressure = gamma proxy × one-bar close change × clamped current-volume/shorter-lookback average volume × clamped realized-volatility/shorter-lookback average realized volatility. Realized volatility is the standard deviation of one-bar log returns over a lookback; gamma proxy is the standard normal density of d1 divided by close × annualized realized volatility × square root of time to expiry, where d1 = [ln(close/synthetic strike) + (risk-free rate + 0.5 × annualized realized volatility²) × time to expiry] / [annualized realized volatility × square root of time to expiry]. Synthetic strike is close, a fixed input, or an EMA of close; time to expiry is horizon bars divided by bars per year. Volume and volatility factors may each be disabled, in which case that factor is 1. Pressure percentile counts prior lookback values of absolute raw pressure strictly below the current value, divided by the count of available prior values, times 100. The signed percentile is positive when raw pressure is positive, negative when raw pressure is negative, and zero when it is zero.
Long: Pressure score > 0 AND (Pressure score crosses above 0 from a prior value ≤ 0 OR (absolute Pressure score is in the high-pressure band and the prior bar was not in that band) OR (absolute Pressure score is in the extreme-pressure band and the prior bar was not in that band))
Short: Pressure score < 0 AND (Pressure score crosses below 0 from a prior value ≥ 0 OR (absolute Pressure score is in the high-pressure band and the prior bar was not in that band) OR (absolute Pressure score is in the extreme-pressure band and the prior bar was not in that band))
