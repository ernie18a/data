Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot, available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Acceptance score = a 0–100 weighted composite of reclaim-close distance (farther beyond the level scores higher), retest-depth quality (best at moderate depth and lower for overly deep retests), volume relative to its lookback average, directional trend alignment (fast and slow EMAs), and rejection-wick quality (rejection wick, close location, and body share).
Long: A confirmed bar closes above the latest confirmed pivot high plus the configured ATR break distance, having not closed above that threshold on the previous bar; within the configured retest window, price reaches below the pivot by the configured ATR level-loss distance and then a confirmed bar closes above the pivot by the configured ATR reclaim distance, before the setup expires or closes below the pivot by the configured ATR pocket distance; acceptance score is at least the configured watch threshold OR below it.
Short: A confirmed bar closes below the latest confirmed pivot low minus the configured ATR break distance, having not closed below that threshold on the previous bar; within the configured retest window, price reaches above the pivot by the configured ATR level-loss distance and then a confirmed bar closes below the pivot by the configured ATR reclaim distance, before the setup expires or closes above the pivot by the configured ATR pocket distance; acceptance score is at least the configured watch threshold OR below it.
