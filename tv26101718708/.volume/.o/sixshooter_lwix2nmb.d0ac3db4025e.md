Calculation: Current-day VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high+low+close)/3, reset at each day boundary; use the forming day.
Calculation: Prior-day VWAP = the current-day VWAP on the last bar of the previous completed day.
Calculation: Weekly/monthly POC = midpoint of the price bin with the greatest accumulated volume; spread each bar’s volume evenly across bins intersecting its low-to-high range. Use the forming week/month. Prior-week/month POC = the POC from the last bar of the previous completed period.
Calculation: POC compression = 100 minus the percentile rank of (highest minus lowest of the six POCs) / ATR; use a longer ranking lookback than the recent price-range lookback.
Calculation: POC direction score = sum across the six POCs of +1 if higher, −1 if lower, or 0 if unchanged versus the same shorter lookback ago.
Calculation: Price tightness = 100 minus the percentile rank of (recent highest high minus recent lowest low) / ATR.
Calculation: Early compression = POC compression at or above its lower, pre-ready threshold AND current POC range / ATR is below its value at the shorter lookback ago.
Calculation: Strong bull regime = close above the long-term moving average AND close within a parameterized fraction of the long-term high AND POC direction score strongly positive. Strong bear regime = the symmetric conditions below the moving average and near the long-term low with strongly negative POC direction score.
Calculation: Rollover down = armed by a new rolling swing high; at least a minimum number of bars later, close is a parameterized ATR distance below the rolling high, close is below its shorter-lookback close, and both high and low are below the prior bar. Disarm after a short fires with this condition; re-arm at a new rolling swing high.
Calculation: Canonical support = all six POCs are at or below close; monthly POC < weekly POC ≤ daily VWAP; prior-month POC < monthly POC; close is within a small ATR distance above daily VWAP and at least a minimum ATR distance above prior-month POC.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe regime = PSAR direction from the next higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Chart-timeframe PSAR proximity passes when absolute close-to-PSAR distance is at least a parameterized ATR distance.
Long: (Early compression AND POC direction score ≥ the positive alignment threshold AND price tightness ≥ its threshold AND higher-timeframe regime is not down AND (not chop OR high exceeds the prior recent rolling high)) OR (POC compression ≥ its ready threshold AND the same alignment, tightness, regime and chop conditions) OR (ATR(short) ≥ parameter × ATR(long) AND close is in the upper portion of its bar range AND close − close[short] ≥ parameter × ATR(short) AND POC direction score ≥ 1) OR (canonical support now AND not canonical support on the prior bar AND close > prior bar high); the selected condition must newly turn on this bar, the same-direction cooldown and opposite-direction lockout must have elapsed, chart-timeframe PSAR proximity must pass, and strong bear regime must be false.
Short: ((Early compression OR POC compression ≥ its ready threshold) AND POC direction score ≤ the negative alignment threshold AND price tightness ≥ its threshold AND higher-timeframe regime is not up) OR Rollover down, AND (not chop OR low is below the prior recent rolling low); OR (ATR(short) ≥ parameter × ATR(long) AND close is in the lower portion of its bar range AND close − close[short] ≤ −parameter × ATR(short) AND POC direction score ≤ −1); the selected condition must newly turn on this bar, the same-direction cooldown and opposite-direction lockout must have elapsed, chart-timeframe PSAR proximity must pass, and strong bull regime must be false.
