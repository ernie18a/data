Calculation: Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Overnight high/low = highest high/lowest low from the overnight session start through the morning session end; freeze the range after that session ends. Do not detect sweeps during the overnight session.
Calculation: Buy-side sweep = a tracked prior-day or overnight high was exceeded within the configured breach window and the current close is below it; sell-side sweep = a tracked prior-day or overnight low was undercut within that window and the current close is above it. Trigger each tracked level at most once per session/day. The latest sweep sets the recent sweep direction for the configured lookback.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier. Gap size is the distance between those boundaries; CE is their midpoint.
Calculation: Displacement = the middle candle of the FVG is directional and its body is at least the configured multiple of ATR(20); when volume filtering is enabled, its volume must also meet the configured multiple of the 20-bar volume average.
Calculation: Bias gate = use confirmed pivot structure on the high timeframe and EMA momentum on the middle and low timeframes (each EMA pair uses the same fast/slow relationship). Bull structure requires the latest two pivot highs and lows both to rise; bear structure requires both to fall. Bull momentum requires fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; bear momentum requires fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA. High-timeframe structure and middle/low-timeframe momentum use the current forming bar in real time and the last completed bar historically. Permit a direction when at least one timeframe confirms it, none oppose it, and the opening-range-gap state does not oppose it; the gap state is neutral if filled during the first RTH hour, otherwise its opening gap direction, locked after that hour. Previous-day levels use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Bullish OTE zone = 62%–79% retracement of the leg from the latest confirmed pivot low to a newly confirmed pivot high; bearish OTE zone = 62%–79% retracement of the leg from the latest confirmed pivot high to a newly confirmed pivot low. Use equal left/right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish order block = body of the down candle two bars before a bullish displacement FVG; bearish order block = body of the up candle two bars before a bearish displacement FVG. The entry level is the body midpoint.
Calculation: Setup stop = beyond the relevant displacement candle, pivot-leg extreme, order-block extreme, or sweep-bar extreme by the configured buffer, according to setup; reject a setup if its stop distance exceeds that setup's configured maximum. Setup target = its entry level plus the configured target distance for longs or minus it for shorts.
Long: sell-side sweep now AND close minus the sweep-bar low minus the configured stop buffer is positive and within the Sweep Reversal maximum stop AND the bias gate permits longs AND (killzone filter is disabled OR current bar is in a configured killzone).
Long: bullish Silver Bullet setup armed within its configured validity window AND low <= bullish FVG CE.
Long: bullish OTE setup armed within its configured validity window AND low <= OTE zone's 62% boundary AND close >= its 79% boundary AND close > open.
Long: bullish order-block/FVG setup armed within its configured validity window AND low <= order-block midpoint.
Long: bullish Silver Bullet setup arms when a bullish FVG meets the configured minimum size, its middle candle is bullish displacement, a sell-side sweep is recent, the bar is in a configured killzone, the bias gate permits longs, and its stop is valid. Bullish OTE setup arms when a new pivot high is confirmed after a pivot low, a sell-side sweep is recent, the bias gate permits longs, and its stop is valid. Bullish order-block/FVG setup arms when a valid bullish order block and displacement FVG form, the bias gate permits longs, and its stop is valid. Each armed setup expires after its configured bar limit and is canceled if price reaches its stop before entry.
Short: buy-side sweep now AND the sweep-bar high plus the configured stop buffer minus close is positive and within the Sweep Reversal maximum stop AND the bias gate permits shorts AND (killzone filter is disabled OR current bar is in a configured killzone).
Short: bearish Silver Bullet setup armed within its configured validity window AND high >= bearish FVG CE.
Short: bearish OTE setup armed within its configured validity window AND high >= OTE zone's 62% boundary AND close <= its 79% boundary AND close < open.
Short: bearish order-block/FVG setup armed within its configured validity window AND high >= order-block midpoint.
Short: bearish Silver Bullet setup arms when a bearish FVG meets the configured minimum size, its middle candle is bearish displacement, a buy-side sweep is recent, the bar is in a configured killzone, the bias gate permits shorts, and its stop is valid. Bearish OTE setup arms when a new pivot low is confirmed after a pivot high, a buy-side sweep is recent, the bias gate permits shorts, and its stop is valid. Bearish order-block/FVG setup arms when a valid bearish order block and displacement FVG form, the bias gate permits shorts, and its stop is valid. Each armed setup expires after its configured bar limit and is canceled if price reaches its stop before entry.
Exit: long, high >= setup target OR low <= setup stop OR bars since entry >= configured time-stop limit.
Exit: short, low <= setup target OR high >= setup stop OR bars since entry >= configured time-stop limit.
