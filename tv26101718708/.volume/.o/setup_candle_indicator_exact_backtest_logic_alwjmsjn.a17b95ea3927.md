Calculation: Candle body = |close − open|; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; candle range = high − low.
Calculation: Body, upper-wick, and lower-wick ratios = their respective sizes divided by candle range (0 if range = 0).
Calculation: Short-term SMA slope = current short-term SMA − its value two bars earlier; long-term SMA slope = current long-term SMA − its value one bar earlier; the long-term SMA uses a longer lookback than the short-term SMA.
Calculation: Long-term EMA slope = current long-term EMA − its value at a shorter prior lookback.
Long: body ratio is at or below its maximum AND lower-wick ratio is at or above its minimum AND upper-wick ratio is at or below its maximum AND lower wick > body AND lower wick > upper wick AND body bottom > short-term SMA AND low − short-term SMA is at or below a maximum multiple of ATR AND short-term SMA slope > 0 AND long-term SMA slope > 0 AND body bottom > long-term SMA AND body bottom ≥ long-term EMA AND long-term EMA slope > 0 AND volume is between its minimum and maximum multiples of its short-term SMA.
Short: body ratio is at or below its maximum AND upper-wick ratio is at or above its minimum AND lower-wick ratio is at or below its maximum AND upper wick > body AND upper wick > lower wick AND body top < short-term SMA AND short-term SMA − high is at or below a maximum multiple of ATR AND short-term SMA slope < 0 AND long-term SMA slope < 0 AND body top < long-term SMA AND body top ≤ long-term EMA AND long-term EMA slope < 0 AND volume is between its minimum and maximum multiples of its short-term SMA.
