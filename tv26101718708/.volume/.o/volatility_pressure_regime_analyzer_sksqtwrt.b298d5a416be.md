Calculation: ATR percentile = the current ATR’s percentile rank over a longer lookback; compression = 100 − ATR percentile.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute price change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price changes over the same lookback.
Calculation: Pressure = (compression percentile + 1.5 × percentile rank of absolute change in log return + 100 × (1 − efficiency ratio)) / 3.5.
Calculation: Pressure percentile = pressure’s percentile rank over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Release size = clamp(((high − low) / ATR − 0.8) / 1.2, 0, 1).
Calculation: Direction score: add 2 for a close above the prior lookback’s highest high (up) or below its lowest low (down); add 1 to up if close is at least as near the bar high as the low, otherwise add 1 to down; add 1 to up if price is no lower than its value at the direction lookback, otherwise add 1 to down.
Long: Pressure percentile reached its high threshold within the recent loading lookback AND release size meets its threshold for the confirmation count of consecutive bars AND the minimum gap since the prior signal has elapsed AND (up direction score ≥ down direction score) AND (if regime filtering is enabled, the variance-ratio regime is not pinning) AND (if trend filtering is enabled, enough current- and higher-timeframe Ichimoku cloud biases agree with the up direction).
Short: Pressure percentile reached its high threshold within the recent loading lookback AND release size meets its threshold for the confirmation count of consecutive bars AND the minimum gap since the prior signal has elapsed AND (down direction score > up direction score) AND (if regime filtering is enabled, the variance-ratio regime is not pinning) AND (if trend filtering is enabled, enough current- and higher-timeframe Ichimoku cloud biases agree with the down direction).
Long: On any of the five successive higher timeframes above the chart timeframe, pressure percentile reached its high threshold within the loading lookback AND release size meets its threshold AND the confirmation count and minimum signal gap are satisfied AND up direction score ≥ down direction score; evaluate on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
