Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low that is strictly above/below the highs/lows of the selected number of bars on both sides; the level becomes active when confirmed.
Calculation: SMA slope = current SMA minus its value one bar earlier; use three SMAs with short-, medium-, and long-term lookbacks.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted OHLC4 since the configured daily anchor; calculate each enabled anchor independently using the current chart bars.
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish candle = candle closes above/below its open and absolute body size is at least the total wick size multiplied by the configured body-to-wick factor.
Calculation: Swing-based stop = entry price adjusted toward the prior opposite pivot by the configured fraction of the entry-to-pivot distance; alternatively use the opposite pivot directly, the signal bar’s low/high, or entry price adjusted by a configured percentage. Apply configured point and percentage buffers outward.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus/minus stop risk multiplied by the configured risk-reward factor.
Long: Enter when an active confirmed swing high is reached by the bar’s high in intrabar mode OR the confirmed bar closes above it in close-confirmation mode; classify the break as BOS unless the prior break direction was bearish, otherwise CHoCH, and allow the configured BOS, CHoCH, or either type; require long direction enabled, flat or short position, valid swing high above swing low, eligible trend filter (selected rising-SMA count/mode, optional bullish SMA stack, and optional short-to-long SMA slope ordering), above each enabled anchored VWAP, eligible trading window and daily trade limit, and no prior long entry at this pivot during the current day; in close-confirmation mode also require the optional strong bullish candle filter and stop below entry.
Short: Enter when an active confirmed swing low is reached by the bar’s low in intrabar mode OR the confirmed bar closes below it in close-confirmation mode; classify the break as BOS unless the prior break direction was bullish, otherwise CHoCH, and allow the configured BOS, CHoCH, or either type; require short direction enabled, flat or long position, valid swing low below swing high, eligible trend filter (selected falling-SMA count/mode, optional bearish SMA stack, and optional short-to-long SMA slope ordering), below each enabled anchored VWAP, eligible trading window and daily trade limit, and no prior short entry at this pivot during the current day; in close-confirmation mode also require the optional strong bearish candle filter and stop above entry.
Exit: Long, bar reaches the stop or target; optionally close when a new day starts.
Exit: Short, bar reaches the stop or target; optionally close when a new day starts.
