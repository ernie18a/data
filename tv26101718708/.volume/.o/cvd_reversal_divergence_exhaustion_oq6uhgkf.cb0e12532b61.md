Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume for bars where close > open, minus volume for bars where close < open; bars where close = open contribute zero.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest among itself and the specified number of bars on each side; evaluate it when the bars on both sides are complete.
Short: (current high = highest high over the lookback window including the current bar AND close < open AND (no prior confirmed pivot high CVD exists OR current cumulative volume delta < CVD at the prior confirmed pivot high)) OR (a confirmed pivot high exists and a prior confirmed pivot high exists AND ((current pivot high > prior pivot high AND current pivot CVD < prior pivot CVD) OR (current pivot high < prior pivot high AND current pivot CVD > prior pivot CVD)))
Long: (current low = lowest low over the lookback window including the current bar AND close > open AND (no prior confirmed pivot low CVD exists OR current cumulative volume delta > CVD at the prior confirmed pivot low)) OR (a confirmed pivot low exists and a prior confirmed pivot low exists AND ((current pivot low < prior pivot low AND current pivot CVD > prior pivot CVD) OR (current pivot low > prior pivot low AND current pivot CVD < prior pivot CVD)))
