Calculation: Inside-bar break = A completed 5-minute or 15-minute bar is inside when its high is at or below the previous bar’s high and its low is at or above the previous bar’s low. An upward break occurs when a subsequent completed chart bar closes above that inside bar’s high; a downward break occurs when it closes below its low. Use the last completed inside bar; the 15-minute setup can trigger during its following 15-minute bar.
Calculation: Timeframe continuity = For each configured timeframe at least as long as the chart timeframe, compare the current close with that timeframe’s current-period open. Net continuity is the number of bullish timeframes minus the number of bearish timeframes; green means net > 0, middle means net = 0, and red means net < 0. Use periods still forming.
Calculation: VIX 15-minute regime = The 15-minute VIX twin-range filter state, using the 15-minute bar still forming; rising means bullish filter state and falling means its opposite. The filter averages two smoothed ranges, with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback, and applies the resulting range to a trailing price filter.
Calculation: Recent key-level cross = The close, high, or low crosses any of the previous day’s high/low, completed Asia or London session high/low, or current session’s 6 PM open within the last five completed chart bars. Use the last completed day/session levels.
Long: Completed chart bar closes between 9:45 AM and 12:00 PM ET AND an upward inside-bar break occurs AND ((close < session VWAP AND VIX 15-minute regime is falling AND timeframe continuity is green or middle) OR (close > session VWAP AND timeframe continuity is green))
Short: Completed chart bar closes between 9:45 AM and 12:00 PM ET AND a downward inside-bar break occurs AND ((close < session VWAP AND VIX 15-minute regime is rising) OR (close > session VWAP AND recent key-level cross AND timeframe continuity is middle or red))
