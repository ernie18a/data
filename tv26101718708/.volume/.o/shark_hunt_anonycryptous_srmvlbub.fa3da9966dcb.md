Calculation: Bull liquidity zone = a confirmed swing-low price through that price plus ATR-scaled zone width; bear liquidity zone = a confirmed swing-high price minus ATR-scaled zone width through that price. Use the zone-detection pivot lookback, scaled shorter for scalp depth and longer for major depth; confirm each pivot only after bars on both sides have completed.
Calculation: Pivot impulse score = 40 × body/range + 35 × min(volume/average volume, 4)/4 + 25 × min(range/ATR, 3)/3.
Calculation: Bull price failure = low breaks below the latest confirmed swing low and closes above it, with the prior bar's low at or above it; bear price failure = high breaks above the latest confirmed swing high and closes below it, with the prior bar's high at or below it. The price-failure pattern uses its own shorter pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bull absorption = bullish current bar, bearish prior bar, current close above prior open, and current open below prior close; bear absorption = bearish current bar, bullish prior bar, current close below prior open, and current open above prior close.
Calculation: Bull wick trap = lower wick > 2 × body and > 0.5 × ATR; bear wick trap = upper wick > 2 × body and > 0.5 × ATR.
Calculation: Bull imbalance = current low > high two bars earlier and the intervening bar is bullish; bear imbalance = current high < low two bars earlier and the intervening bar is bearish.
Calculation: Bull retest = after a close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high, a later bar does not make another such break, has low at or below that broken level, and closes at or above it; bear retest = after a close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low, a later bar does not make another such break, has high at or above that broken level, and closes at or below it. Clear the pending level after its retest or a close back through it.
Calculation: Wick rejection = the relevant lower or upper wick divided by high minus low, expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Wick penetration = for a demand zone, (zone bottom − low)/zone bottom as a percentage; for a supply zone, (high − zone top)/zone top as a percentage.
Long: Bull price failure OR bull absorption OR bull wick trap OR bull imbalance OR bull retest OR a confirmed-bar sweep below a fresh demand zone that closes above its bottom, has sufficient wick penetration, passes the enabled volume and wick-rejection filters, passes every enabled bullish pattern gate (bull price failure, bull wick trap, bullish absorption), satisfies the per-direction cooldown, and the zone has no prior tests or its rounded hold rate meets the configured minimum. The sweep is disabled in failure-entry mode.
Short: Bear price failure OR bear absorption OR bear wick trap OR bear imbalance OR bear retest OR a confirmed-bar sweep above a fresh supply zone that closes below its top, has sufficient wick penetration, passes the enabled volume and wick-rejection filters, passes every enabled bearish pattern gate (bear price failure, bear wick trap, bearish absorption), satisfies the per-direction cooldown, and the zone has no prior tests or its rounded hold rate meets the configured minimum. The sweep is disabled in failure-entry mode.
Long: In failure-entry mode, low breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low and closes above it, the prior bar's low is at or above it, the lower wick is at least the configured ATR multiple and rejection percentage, optional volume confirmation passes, and the bullish failure cooldown passes. Use the failure engine's independent pivot lookback.
Short: In failure-entry mode, high breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high and closes below it, the prior bar's high is at or below it, the upper wick is at least the configured ATR multiple and rejection percentage, optional volume confirmation passes, and the bearish failure cooldown passes. Use the failure engine's independent pivot lookback.
Exit: Long, high reaches the take-profit price OR low reaches the stop price; if both occur on one bar, take profit is recorded first. The stop is below entry by ATR × (zone width + buffer) in zone-sweep backtesting, or below the sweep low / failure low or pivot low plus the configured ATR buffer in the corresponding wick / pivot mode. Take profit = entry + stop distance × configured reward-to-risk ratio.
Exit: Short, low reaches the take-profit price OR high reaches the stop price; if both occur on one bar, take profit is recorded first. The stop is above entry by ATR × (zone width + buffer) in zone-sweep backtesting, or above the sweep high / failure high or pivot high plus the configured ATR buffer in the corresponding wick / pivot mode. Take profit = entry − stop distance × configured reward-to-risk ratio.
