Calculation: Propulsion charge = 100 × weighted mean of six scores, each clamped to [0,1]: grab depth / configured depth reference in ATR; 1 − bars to reclaim / reclaim window; rejection-wick fraction of bar range; (volume / rolling volume average − 1) / configured surge reference; (configured coil looseness reference − prior coil-window range / ATR) / its configured normalization range; reclaim displacement in ATR / configured displacement reference.
Long: A confirmed pivot low is pierced (low < level), then within the reclaim window price closes above it; grab depth meets its minimum and propulsion charge meets the arm threshold (and, if strong-only filtering is enabled, the strong threshold); before invalidation or ignition-window expiry, price closes above the high of the reclaim bar.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is pierced (high > level), then within the reclaim window price closes below it; grab depth meets its minimum and propulsion charge meets the arm threshold (and, if strong-only filtering is enabled, the strong threshold); before invalidation or ignition-window expiry, price closes below the low of the reclaim bar.
Exit: Long, low <= entry − signal-bar ATR × configured risk distance, or high >= entry + signal-bar ATR × configured risk distance × configured third-target R multiple; if the stop and a target are hit on the same bar, resolve at the stop. Track the highest target touched; if the stop is later hit after target one or two, exit at the highest target touched.
Exit: Short, high >= entry + signal-bar ATR × configured risk distance, or low <= entry − signal-bar ATR × configured risk distance × configured third-target R multiple; if the stop and a target are hit on the same bar, resolve at the stop. Track the highest target touched; if the stop is later hit after target one or two, exit at the highest target touched.
