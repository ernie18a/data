Calculation: Camarilla central pivot = close of the last completed selected regular-hours or extended-hours session
Calculation: Camarilla range = high − low of that same session
Calculation: R3 = central pivot + range × 1.1 / 4; R4 = central pivot + range × 1.1 / 2; S3 = central pivot − range × 1.1 / 4; S4 = central pivot − range × 1.1 / 2
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / rolling simple average of volume
Long: current low crosses below S3 and current close > S3
Long: current close crosses above R4 AND (high-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ configured threshold) AND current close > current open
Short: current high crosses above R3 and current close < R3
Short: current close crosses below S4 AND (high-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ configured threshold) AND current close < current open
