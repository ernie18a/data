Calculation: Per-asset flow = ((2 × close − high − low) / (high − low)) × min(volume / average volume, cap); average volume is a session-gated moving average of that asset’s volume. Invert the inverse-hedge asset’s flow.
Calculation: Composite flow = weighted average of valid per-asset flows, divided by the total weight of valid assets.
Calculation: Normalized flow = z-score of the exponentially smoothed composite flow over a rolling lookback.
Calculation: Directional breadth = the active weight of assets whose flow has the stated direction, divided by total active weight.
Calculation: Flow strength = absolute normalized flow divided by its moving average of absolute normalized flow.
Calculation: Bullish filter = RSI is below its configured lower band, or RSI is rising after being below that band, or RSI is above 50 and below its configured upper band. Bearish filter = RSI is above its configured upper band, or RSI is falling after being above that band, or RSI is below 50 and above its configured lower band.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivots = swing highs or lows confirmed after the configured number of bars to the right; compare each pivot with the previous confirmed pivot of the same type, using normalized flow and RSI from the pivot bar.
Long: In the regular session, normalized flow is positive, rising, bullish filter passes (when filter agreement is enabled), directional breadth meets its configured minimum, active asset count meets its configured minimum, and normalized flow is available; the condition has just become true and its same-direction cooldown has elapsed OR normalized flow crosses above zero, bullish filter passes (when enabled), active asset count meets its configured minimum, and normalized flow is available OR in the regular session normalized flow is positive and flow strength exceeds its configured threshold, and this condition has just become true OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the previous confirmed pivot low, normalized flow at the pivot exceeds normalized flow at the previous pivot low by at least its configured minimum, active asset count meets its configured minimum, the pivot is in the regular session, and (RSI at the pivot is at or below 40 when divergence confirmation is enabled).
Short: In the regular session, normalized flow is negative, falling, bearish filter passes (when filter agreement is enabled), directional breadth meets its configured minimum, active asset count meets its configured minimum, and normalized flow is available; the condition has just become true and its same-direction cooldown has elapsed OR normalized flow crosses below zero, bearish filter passes (when enabled), active asset count meets its configured minimum, and normalized flow is available OR in the regular session normalized flow is negative and flow strength exceeds its configured threshold, and this condition has just become true OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the previous confirmed pivot high, normalized flow at the pivot is below normalized flow at the previous pivot high by at least its configured minimum, active asset count meets its configured minimum, the pivot is in the regular session, and (RSI at the pivot is at or above 60 when divergence confirmation is enabled).
