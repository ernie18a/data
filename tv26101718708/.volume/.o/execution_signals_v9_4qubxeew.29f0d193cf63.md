Calculation: Signal-timeframe failure-high = on a completed signal-timeframe bar, high > prior completed bar high AND close <= prior completed bar high AND upper wick > 0; compare only consecutive bars whose timestamp gap is at most 1.5 signal periods
Calculation: Signal-timeframe failure-low = on a completed signal-timeframe bar, low < prior completed bar low AND close >= prior completed bar low AND lower wick > 0; compare only consecutive bars whose timestamp gap is at most 1.5 signal periods
Calculation: Signal-timeframe inside bar = on a completed signal-timeframe bar, high < prior completed bar high AND low > prior completed bar low; compare only consecutive bars whose timestamp gap is at most 1.5 signal periods
Calculation: Chart-timeframe failure-high = high > prior chart bar high AND close <= prior chart bar high AND upper wick > 0
Calculation: Chart-timeframe failure-low = low < prior chart bar low AND close >= prior chart bar low AND lower wick > 0
Calculation: Chart-timeframe inside bar = high < prior chart bar high AND low > prior chart bar low
Calculation: Stop price = for a long, the selected raw stop (prior chart bar low, lowest low of current and prior chart bars, signal-bar low, or nested setup-bar low) capped at entry price minus the minimum stop distance; for a short, the selected raw stop (prior chart bar high, highest high of current and prior chart bars, signal-bar high, or nested setup-bar high) floored at entry price plus the minimum stop distance
Long: Signal-timeframe failure-low OR (Break of Signal Bar mode AND a qualifying signal bar is armed AND close > its high AND stop distance <= maximum risk if enabled AND prior-session range bias allows longs AND time is 18:00–15:59 ET) OR (LTF Signal Bar mode AND upward break of an armed signal bar sets long bias AND, within bias expiry, a qualifying chart-timeframe failure-low or inside bar is armed AND close > its high AND stop distance <= maximum risk if enabled AND prior-session range bias allows longs AND time is 18:00–15:59 ET)
Short: Signal-timeframe failure-high OR (Break of Signal Bar mode AND a qualifying signal bar is armed AND close < its low AND stop distance <= maximum risk if enabled AND prior-session range bias allows shorts AND time is 18:00–15:59 ET) OR (LTF Signal Bar mode AND downward break of an armed signal bar sets short bias AND, within bias expiry, a qualifying chart-timeframe failure-high or inside bar is armed AND close < its low AND stop distance <= maximum risk if enabled AND prior-session range bias allows shorts AND time is 18:00–15:59 ET)
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR high >= entry price + stop distance × target R multiple OR time reaches 15:59 ET
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR low <= entry price - stop distance × target R multiple OR time reaches 15:59 ET
