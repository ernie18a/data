Calculation: Symmetrical triangle = Using completed bars, take the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows, plus the latest confirmed pivot high and low detected with a shorter right-side confirmation delay. A pivot high/low is a bar whose high/low is the extreme over the configured bars to its left and right. Require the three highs to descend, the three lows to ascend, and each sequence to be chronological. Require the lines from the first to third high and first to third low to converge (upper-line slope < lower-line slope). The triangle’s upper trigger is its third high; its lower trigger is its third low. Create a new triangle only when both third pivots are later than those used for the previously created triangle.
Long: On a monitoring triangle, close > upper trigger AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > simple moving average of volume × volume multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > RSI threshold); OR on a bear-trap triangle, close > lower trigger before its timeout AND (trap EMA confirmation disabled OR fast EMA crosses above slow EMA before timeout).
Short: On a monitoring triangle, close < lower trigger AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > simple moving average of volume × volume multiplier) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < RSI threshold); OR on a bull-trap triangle, close < upper trigger before its timeout AND (trap EMA confirmation disabled OR fast EMA crosses below slow EMA before timeout).
