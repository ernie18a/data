Calculation: Swing pivots = confirmed pivot highs and lows using major and minor lookbacks, with the major lookback longer than the minor; join consecutive pivots of the same type and depth to form projected trendlines.
Calculation: Horizontal levels = recent major swing pivots, merging levels within an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Confluence zones = group projected trendlines and horizontal levels whose prices are within an ATR-based zone-width tolerance; retain groups with at least the configured minimum sources and one trendline. Include only trendlines meeting the configured touch minimum. Score each group from its sources’ touch strength, age and slope quality, plus rolling-volume strength and a bonus when a horizontal level is nearby; select the highest-scoring group meeting the configured minimum zone score. Its midpoint is the average source price. Bias is resistance if descending sources are the majority, support if ascending sources are the majority; on a tie, bias is resistance when close is below the midpoint, otherwise support.
Long: On a confirmed bar, a new qualifying confluence zone has support bias and is not within the configured deduplication distance of an active support zone.
Short: On a confirmed bar, a new qualifying confluence zone has resistance bias and is not within the configured deduplication distance of an active resistance zone.
