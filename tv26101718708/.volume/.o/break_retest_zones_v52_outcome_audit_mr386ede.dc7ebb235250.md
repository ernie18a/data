Calculation: Support/resistance zones = bands formed from confirmed swing lows/highs; cap band height using ATR, reject overlapping zones, and track each zone as live, broken upward, broken downward, failed, or expired. A zone starts with one touch; count another touch when a bar enters it and closes outside, at least the configured spacing after the prior touch.
Calculation: Breakout volume ratio = breakout-bar volume / the short-term moving average of volume.
Calculation: Breakout displacement = breakout-bar body size / ATR.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confluence = a zone band overlaps, within an ATR-based tolerance, a previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high or low, or the previous completed daily bar's high or low.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = within the configured recent-bar window, price wicks beyond the prior lookback high or low and closes back inside it.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when current low exceeds the high two bars earlier; bearish when current high is below the low two bars earlier. Track whether a direction-matching gap occurs on the breakout bar or during its pullback.
Calculation: Setup grade = 2 points for higher-timeframe confluence, 1 each for breakout volume meeting its configured threshold, average pullback volume below breakout volume, a qualifying liquidity sweep, breakout body meeting its ATR threshold, and a direction-matching fair value gap. Apply the configured minimum grade.
Calculation: Valid session = bar time is within any enabled trading session, using New York time; if the session filter is disabled, valid at all times.
Calculation: Long stop = below the broken zone's bottom by a configured buffer; short stop = above the broken zone's top by a configured buffer. Target 1 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the entry-to-stop risk times the configured reward multiple.
Long: A live zone's close crosses above its top (also arm a newly formed zone if close is already above its top) OR an upward-broken zone has a retest bar after the configured minimum gap and within the configured retest window, low <= zone top, close > zone top, touches >= configured minimum, valid session, configured minimum grade met, and (if enabled) sufficient room to the previous completed higher-timeframe high. Cancel the armed setup if price closes below the zone bottom, the window expires, or (if enabled) close exceeds the zone top by the configured runaway distance in ATR.
Short: A live zone's close crosses below its bottom (also arm a newly formed zone if close is already below its bottom) OR a downward-broken zone has a retest bar after the configured minimum gap and within the configured retest window, high >= zone bottom, close < zone bottom, touches >= configured minimum, valid session, configured minimum grade met, and (if enabled) sufficient room to the previous completed higher-timeframe low. Cancel the armed setup if price closes above the zone top, the window expires, or (if enabled) close falls below the zone bottom by the configured runaway distance in ATR.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target 1; if both occur on one bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target 1; if both occur on one bar, exit at the stop.
