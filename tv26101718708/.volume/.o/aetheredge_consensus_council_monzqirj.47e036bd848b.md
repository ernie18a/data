Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs = EMAs with successively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low requiring five bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Distance above support = (close − latest confirmed swing low) / ATR; distance below resistance = (latest confirmed swing high − close) / ATR
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume − 20-bar mean volume) / 20-bar standard deviation of volume
Calculation: OBV direction = OBV now compared with OBV 10 bars earlier; OBV adds volume on up closes and subtracts volume on down closes
Calculation: Bollinger-band width = (upper band − lower band) / basis × 100, using a 20-bar simple moving average basis and bands two standard deviations away; expansion means width > 1.2 × its 50-bar average
Calculation: Trend vote = +1 when short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA; −1 when short-term EMA < medium-term EMA < long-term EMA; otherwise 0
Calculation: Support/resistance vote = +1 when distance above support is ≥ 0 and < 0.8 and distance below resistance is not in that range; −1 when distance below resistance is ≥ 0 and < 0.8 and distance above support is not in that range; otherwise 0
Calculation: Volume vote = +1 when volume z-score ≥ 1, OBV is higher than 10 bars earlier, and close > open; −1 when volume z-score ≥ 1, OBV is lower than 10 bars earlier, and close < open; otherwise 0
Calculation: Momentum vote = +1 when 14-bar RSI > 50, MACD histogram (12, 26, 9) > 0, and 3-bar average of 14-bar stochastic %K > 50; −1 when those values are respectively < 50, < 0, and < 50; otherwise 0
Calculation: Volatility vote = +1 during expansion when close > open; −1 during expansion when close < open; otherwise 0
Calculation: Consensus score = trend vote + support/resistance vote + volume vote + momentum vote + volatility vote
Calculation: Stop distance = clamp(max(1.5 × ATR × volatility multiplier, min(structural distance, 1.5 × ATR × 2.5)), 0.6 × ATR, 4 × ATR); volatility multiplier is 1.3 during expansion, 0.8 when Bollinger-band width < 0.6 × its 50-bar average, otherwise 1. Structural distance is max(close − latest confirmed swing low + 0.2 × ATR, 0.6 × ATR) for longs when that swing low is below close; max(latest confirmed swing high − close + 0.2 × ATR, 0.6 × ATR) for shorts when that swing high is above close; otherwise 1.5 × ATR
Calculation: Target distances = let base target 2 = stop distance × reward/risk ratio; momentum multiplier = 1.15 if momentum vote agrees with direction, otherwise 1. For longs, target 1 structure distance is max(latest confirmed swing high − close, 0.5 × ATR) if that swing high is above close, otherwise 0.55 × base target 2. For shorts, use max(close − latest confirmed swing low, 0.5 × ATR) if that swing low is below close, otherwise 0.55 × base target 2. Target 1 is the lesser of that structure distance and 0.7 × base target 2; target 2 is base target 2 × momentum multiplier; target 3 is 1.6 × base target 2 × momentum multiplier
Long: short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA OR distance above support ≥ 0 and < 0.8 and distance below resistance is not ≥ 0 and < 0.8 OR volume z-score ≥ 1 AND OBV is higher than 10 bars earlier AND close > open OR 14-bar RSI > 50 AND MACD histogram (12, 26, 9) > 0 AND 3-bar average of 14-bar stochastic %K > 50 OR Bollinger-band width > 1.2 × its 50-bar average AND close > open OR consensus score ≥ threshold AND trend vote = +1 AND support/resistance vote ≥ 0
Short: short-term EMA < medium-term EMA < long-term EMA OR distance below resistance ≥ 0 and < 0.8 and distance above support is not ≥ 0 and < 0.8 OR volume z-score ≥ 1 AND OBV is lower than 10 bars earlier AND close < open OR 14-bar RSI < 50 AND MACD histogram (12, 26, 9) < 0 AND 3-bar average of 14-bar stochastic %K < 50 OR Bollinger-band width > 1.2 × its 50-bar average AND close < open OR consensus score ≤ −threshold AND trend vote = −1 AND support/resistance vote ≤ 0
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR (maximum target level ≥ 3 AND high ≥ target 3 price)
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR (maximum target level ≥ 3 AND low ≤ target 3 price)
