Calculation: Long cRVOL = current session cumulative volume divided by the average cumulative volume through the same time slot in the longer lookback of completed sessions; on daily or higher bars, current volume divided by the SMA of the previous bars in the longer lookback
Calculation: Short cRVOL = current session cumulative volume divided by the average cumulative volume through the same time slot in the shorter lookback of completed sessions; on daily or higher bars, current volume divided by the SMA of the previous bars in the shorter lookback
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low from the current session open through the configured opening-range duration; use only after that duration completes
Long: close > a previous-day high, opening-range high, or configured manual level AND prior close <= that level AND that level has not triggered upward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND long cRVOL >= the in-play threshold AND short cRVOL >= the short-lookback confirmation threshold
Long: close > a previous-day high, opening-range high, or configured manual level AND prior close <= that level AND that level has not triggered upward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND long cRVOL < 1.0 AND short cRVOL < 1.0
Long: close > a previous-day high, opening-range high, or configured manual level AND prior close <= that level AND that level has not triggered upward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND (long cRVOL < the in-play threshold OR short cRVOL < 1.0) AND (long cRVOL >= 1.0 OR short cRVOL >= 1.0)
Short: close < a previous-day low, opening-range low, or configured manual level AND prior close >= that level AND that level has not triggered downward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND long cRVOL >= the in-play threshold AND short cRVOL >= the short-lookback confirmation threshold
Short: close < a previous-day low, opening-range low, or configured manual level AND prior close >= that level AND that level has not triggered downward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND long cRVOL < 1.0 AND short cRVOL < 1.0
Short: close < a previous-day low, opening-range low, or configured manual level AND prior close >= that level AND that level has not triggered downward earlier that session AND current time is before the configured cutoff AND (long cRVOL < the in-play threshold OR short cRVOL < 1.0) AND (long cRVOL >= 1.0 OR short cRVOL >= 1.0)
Exit: long or short, after a confirmed upward or downward level break, long cRVOL crosses below the in-play threshold
