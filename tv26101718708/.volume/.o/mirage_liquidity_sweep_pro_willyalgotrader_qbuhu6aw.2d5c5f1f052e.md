Calculation: Major swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks; retain each unswept level only within its maximum age.
Calculation: Minor pivot high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks shorter than the major swing lookback; use the most recent confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Sweep quality score = 100 × (min(max(rejection wick, 0) / ATR, 1) × 0.30 + min(max(reclaim beyond swept level, 0) / ATR, 1) × 0.25 + rejection-direction close position × 0.20 + clamped volume component × 0.15 + higher-timeframe bias component × 0.10). Bullish rejection wick = min(open, close) − low; bearish rejection wick = high − max(open, close). Bullish reclaim = close − swept level; bearish reclaim = swept level − close. Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), inverted for bearish sweeps. Volume component = clamp((volume / volume moving average − 1) / (volume spike threshold − 1), 0, 1); use 0.5 when volume is unavailable or the volume filter is off. Higher-timeframe bias component = 1 when the sweep direction agrees with higher-timeframe close versus its EMA, otherwise 0; use 0.5 when that filter is off. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for its close and EMA.
Calculation: Stop distance = max(abs(entry price − (long sweep low − ATR × stop buffer)) for long, abs((short sweep high + ATR × stop buffer) − entry price) for short, ATR × minimum stop distance).
Long: bullish sweep (an unswept recent major swing low is pierced, low < level, and reclaimed, close > level) AND sweep quality score ≥ minimum score AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND no bearish qualifying sweep on the same bar AND flat AND (confirmation disabled OR, within the confirmation window after the sweep, close > most recent minor pivot high).
Short: bearish sweep (an unswept recent major swing high is pierced, high > level, and reclaimed, close < level) AND sweep quality score ≥ minimum score AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND no bullish qualifying sweep on the same bar AND flat AND (confirmation disabled OR, within the confirmation window after the sweep, close < most recent minor pivot low).
Exit: long, on a later confirmed bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance × third target risk multiple.
Exit: short, on a later confirmed bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance × third target risk multiple.
