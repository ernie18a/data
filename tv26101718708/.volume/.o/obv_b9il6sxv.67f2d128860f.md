Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added when close rises, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged when close is unchanged.
Calculation: OBV deviation percentile = percentile rank of the absolute difference between OBV and its configured moving average, over its lookback.
Calculation: OBV direction state = switch to bullish when the deviation percentile meets its minimum and OBV is above its moving average; switch to bearish when it meets its minimum and OBV is below its moving average; otherwise retain the prior state. Allow a switch only after the configured minimum spacing since the last switch.
Calculation: Entry window = armed by an OBV direction state switch and remains open through the configured confirmation period; expires after that period.
Calculation: Regression t statistic = correlation of close with bar index over the configured regression lookback, multiplied by sqrt((lookback − 2) / (1 − correlation²)).
Calculation: Chandelier trail = for a long, highest high since entry minus the configured ATR multiple; for a short, lowest low since entry plus the configured ATR multiple. Initialize on the entry bar beyond its low for a long or high for a short, and ratchet only in the direction that tightens the stop. Use ATR calculated from the chart bars.
Long: flat AND bullish OBV direction state switch is within its entry window AND regression t statistic ≥ its positive minimum absolute threshold.
Short: flat AND bearish OBV direction state switch is within its entry window AND regression t statistic ≤ the negative of its minimum absolute threshold.
Exit: long, low ≤ prior bar's chandelier trail OR configured flip exit is enabled AND OBV direction state switches bearish.
Exit: short, high ≥ prior bar's chandelier trail OR configured flip exit is enabled AND OBV direction state switches bullish.
