Calculation: Bullish PSP = on the last completed bar of a configured timeframe, exactly one of the three correlated instruments closed above its open and the other two closed below their opens
Calculation: Bearish PSP = on the last completed bar of a configured timeframe, exactly one of the three correlated instruments closed below its open and the other two closed above their opens
Calculation: Accumulation zone = on the chart instrument and the same timeframe, at least the configured minimum number of the trailing configured lookback bars qualify; a bar qualifies when its volume exceeds its volume SMA times a configured multiplier AND its high-low range is less than its ATR times a configured multiplier. The volume SMA and ATR use their respective configured lookbacks.
Long: Bullish PSP AND configured timeframe >= chart timeframe AND configured timeframe bar has just closed AND chart instrument is one of the three correlated instruments AND (accumulation filter disabled OR accumulation zone is false on the last completed bar)
Short: Bearish PSP AND configured timeframe >= chart timeframe AND configured timeframe bar has just closed AND chart instrument is one of the three correlated instruments AND (accumulation filter disabled OR accumulation zone is false on the last completed bar)
