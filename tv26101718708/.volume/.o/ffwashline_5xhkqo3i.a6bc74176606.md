Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low exceeding the highs/lows of the same L bars on each side; use only after the L right-side bars have closed. L may adapt to chart timeframe from a reference timeframe.
Calculation: Upper/lower tail ratio = (high − max(open, close))/(high − low) / (min(open, close) − low)/(high − low), respectively; use 0 when range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish wash line = the lowest/highest tracked confirmed swing low/high pierced by a closed bar, with low/high extending more than minimum ATR-scaled depth, lower/upper tail ratio meeting its minimum, and close not beyond the level. Remove tracked levels that are too old or closed through; consume pierced levels. ATR and all bars are from the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Wash-line state = created on its poke bar with level, spike extreme, and ATR at poke. Thereafter, retire it if its configured stop is hit (wick or close), its count of closes beyond the level reaches its limit, or its age exceeds its lifetime. Count departure as the greatest excursion from the level reaching the minimum multiple of ATR at poke. A retest touch requires prior departure, current high/low reaching within the current ATR-scaled tolerance of the level, and close not beyond the level.
Calculation: Wash-line score = weighted average × 100 of: tail quality clamp((poke tail ratio − minimum tail ratio)/(full-score tail ratio − minimum tail ratio), 0, 1); pivot obviousness 0.40 × clamp(pivot touches/3, 0, 1) + 0.35 × (1 − clamp(pivot age/max pivot age, 0, 1)) + 0.25 if newest pivot (otherwise 0); cluster score 1 for at least 3 simultaneous pokes, 0.7 for 2, else 0.4; departure clamp(max excursion/(full-score departure multiple × ATR at poke), 0, 1); retest score 1 for at least 2 touches, 0.7 for 1, else 0.25; and room score clamp(farthest favorable distance to an opposing tracked swing / risk / minimum target multiple, 0, 1). Multiply by freshness 1 − (1 − end-of-life floor) × clamp(line age/lifetime, 0, 1), and by breach penalty raised to the number of closes beyond the level. Risk is the absolute distance from level to spike extreme buffered by ATR at poke, with at least one minimum tick. Use the same weights and target multiples for all wash lines.
Calculation: Inside candle = current high ≤ previous high AND current low ≥ previous low. Outside candle = current high > previous high AND current low < previous low.
Long: bullish wash-line poke OR an active bullish wash line has a retest touch and (inside candle OR lower-tail ratio ≥ rejection-tail minimum) OR its retest touch was on the previous bar and the current candle is outside with close > open; the line has at least the minimum retest touches, score ≥ minimum trigger score, and its per-line cooldown has elapsed.
Short: bearish wash-line poke OR an active bearish wash line has a retest touch and (inside candle OR upper-tail ratio ≥ rejection-tail minimum) OR its retest touch was on the previous bar and the current candle is outside with close < open; the line has at least the minimum retest touches, score ≥ minimum trigger score, and its per-line cooldown has elapsed.
