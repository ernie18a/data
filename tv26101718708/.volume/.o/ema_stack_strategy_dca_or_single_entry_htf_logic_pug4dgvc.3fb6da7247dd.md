Calculation: MA1–MA4 = configurable moving averages of configured price sources on the chart timeframe or selected higher timeframe; their lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest. Use confirmed logic-timeframe bars; update decisions when that bar closes.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on the logic timeframe.
Calculation: DCA distance = configured distance in units, percent of anchor price, or ATR multiple; successive grid intervals scale geometrically by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Swing low/high = lowest low/highest high over a configurable lookback of chart bars.
Long: flat AND MA1 > MA2 AND (MA3 filter disabled OR MA2 > MA3) AND (MA4 filter disabled OR MA3 > MA4) for the configured number of consecutive confirmed logic-timeframe closes AND base entries are not time-blocked AND base order size > 0.
Short: flat AND MA1 < MA2 AND (MA3 filter disabled OR MA2 < MA3) AND (MA4 filter disabled OR MA3 < MA4) for the configured number of consecutive confirmed logic-timeframe closes AND base entries are not time-blocked AND base order size > 0.
Long: long position AND DCA enabled AND entry count below maximum AND configured DCA-add time filter passes AND low <= anchor price − cumulative scaled DCA distance AND remaining capital cap > 0.
Short: short position AND DCA enabled AND entry count below maximum AND configured DCA-add time filter passes AND high >= anchor price + cumulative scaled DCA distance AND remaining capital cap > 0.
Exit: long, close <= MA1 AND MA1 < MA2 (DCA mode with MA kill enabled) OR low <= average entry + configured take-profit distance (DCA average-entry target) OR entries reached maximum AND high >= last DCA price + configured last-entry offset (when selected) OR active trailing stop and low <= highest high since position opened − configured trail distance.
Exit: short, close >= MA1 AND MA1 > MA2 (DCA mode with MA kill enabled) OR high >= average entry − configured take-profit distance (DCA average-entry target) OR entries reached maximum AND low <= last DCA price − configured last-entry offset (when selected) OR active trailing stop and high >= lowest low since position opened + configured trail distance.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND (MA1 crosses below MA2 when MA-cross exit selected OR bars in trade reaches configured time limit when time-stop selected) OR low <= stop price OR high >= target price (when a bracket exit is selected) OR active trailing stop and low <= highest high since position opened − configured trail distance.
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND (MA1 crosses above MA2 when MA-cross exit selected OR bars in trade reaches configured time limit when time-stop selected) OR high >= stop price OR low <= target price (when a bracket exit is selected) OR active trailing stop and high >= lowest low since position opened + configured trail distance.
Calculation: Bracket stop/target = selected ATR, risk-reward, or percentage bracket from average entry; risk-reward stop is either an ATR offset or beyond the swing low/high with configured buffer, and target is the configured reward multiple of stop risk. When break-even is enabled and price reaches its configured favorable trigger, move the bracket stop to average entry plus/minus configured offset, whichever is more protective.
