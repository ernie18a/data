Calculation: RSI support/resistance levels = lows/highs of the price bars corresponding to confirmed RSI pivot lows/highs, respectively; retain the most recent configured number of each. Support is the nearest retained support at or below close; resistance is the nearest retained resistance at or above close. If none qualifies, keep the last value.
Calculation: Prior swing low/high = lowest low/highest high over the configured lookback ending on the previous bar.
Long: low < prior swing low AND (close > prior swing low if close-back is required) AND support exists AND (tolerance ≤ 0 OR abs(low − support) ≤ ATR × tolerance) AND (volume multiplier ≤ 0 OR (volume SMA > 0 AND volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier))
Short: high > prior swing high AND (close < prior swing high if close-back is required) AND resistance exists AND (tolerance ≤ 0 OR abs(high − resistance) ≤ ATR × tolerance) AND (volume multiplier ≤ 0 OR (volume SMA > 0 AND volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier))
