Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, close > EMA and session VWAP, with both higher than on the prior higher-timeframe bar; bearish bias = close < both, with both lower than on the prior higher-timeframe bar. The bullish and bearish bias EMAs use the same lookback.
Calculation: Chart-bar EMA = EMA using the same lookback as the higher-timeframe EMA.
Calculation: Prior breakout high/low = highest high/lowest low over the breakout lookback of preceding chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: VWAP extension = abs(close − session VWAP) / ATR.
Long: bullish higher-timeframe bias AND the prior chart bar's sampled bias was not bullish OR bullish bias AND close > prior breakout high AND close > chart-bar EMA AND close > session VWAP AND close > open AND (ATR = 0 OR VWAP extension <= maximum) AND no breakout has already occurred in this bullish bias cycle OR bullish bias AND a prior close below chart-bar EMA armed a pullback during this bias cycle AND close > chart-bar EMA AND prior close <= prior chart-bar EMA AND close > session VWAP AND close > open AND (ATR = 0 OR VWAP extension <= maximum) AND no breakout occurs on this bar.
Short: bearish higher-timeframe bias AND the prior chart bar's sampled bias was not bearish OR bearish bias AND close < prior breakout low AND close < chart-bar EMA AND close < session VWAP AND close < open AND (ATR = 0 OR VWAP extension <= maximum) AND no breakout has already occurred in this bearish bias cycle OR bearish bias AND a prior close above chart-bar EMA armed a pullback during this bias cycle AND close < chart-bar EMA AND prior close >= prior chart-bar EMA AND close < session VWAP AND close < open AND (ATR = 0 OR VWAP extension <= maximum) AND no breakout occurs on this bar.
