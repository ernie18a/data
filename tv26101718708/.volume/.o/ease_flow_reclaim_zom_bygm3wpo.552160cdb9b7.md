Calculation: Ease pressure = EMA of ((current midpoint − prior midpoint) / max(volume > 0 ? (volume / 100000) / max(high − low, minimum tick) : 1 / max(high − low, minimum tick), 0.000001)); ease impulse = ease pressure − its value at the configured impulse lookback.
Calculation: Downside stress = square root of the configured lookback SMA of squared percentage distance from the rolling highest close; upside stress = square root of the configured lookback SMA of squared percentage distance from the rolling lowest close.
Calculation: Bull score = count of: ease pressure > 0 and ease impulse > 0; downside stress < its prior value and prior downside stress > the configured stress floor; fast VWMA > slow VWMA and HMA > its prior value; close > HMA and prior close ≤ prior HMA. Fast VWMA uses a shorter lookback than slow VWMA.
Calculation: Bear score = count of: ease pressure < 0 and ease impulse < 0; upside stress < its prior value and prior upside stress > the configured stress floor; fast VWMA < slow VWMA and HMA < its prior value; close < HMA and prior close ≥ prior HMA. Fast VWMA uses a shorter lookback than slow VWMA.
Long: Bull score ≥ configured required score and more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior confirmed long or short signal, OR bull score = configured required score − 1
Short: Bear score ≥ configured required score and more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior confirmed long or short signal, OR bear score = configured required score − 1
