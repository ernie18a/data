Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; recognized after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Bullish trend = short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA; bearish trend = short-term EMA < medium-term EMA < long-term EMA.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the current confirmed 5-bar RSI pivot low is higher than its value on the prior bar, while the current confirmed 5-bar price pivot low is lower than its value on the prior bar.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = the current confirmed 5-bar RSI pivot high is lower than its value on the prior bar, while the current confirmed 5-bar price pivot high is higher than its value on the prior bar.
Long: short-term EMA crosses above medium-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND RSI < its upper threshold OR close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, with the prior close at or below that pivot high OR bearish trend on the prior bar AND bullish trend on the current bar OR volume > its moving average × a multiplier AND close >= open OR MACD line crosses above its signal line OR bullish divergence condition OR a confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed pivot low occurs.
Short: short-term EMA crosses below medium-term EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND RSI > its lower threshold OR close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low, with the prior close at or above that pivot low OR bullish trend on the prior bar AND bearish trend on the current bar OR volume > its moving average × a multiplier AND close < open OR MACD line crosses below its signal line OR bearish divergence condition OR a confirmed pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed pivot high occurs.
