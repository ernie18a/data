Calculation: Opening box high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening session, updated while that session is forming; use the completed range after the session ends.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe; use the current bar's ATR.
Calculation: Opening box height = opening box high − opening box low.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume ÷ simple moving average of volume over a longer lookback including the current bar.
Calculation: 30-minute EMA regime = EMA of 30-minute closes on the last completed 30-minute bar; slope = that EMA minus the EMA on the prior completed 30-minute bar.
Long: First close crosses above opening box high + breakout buffer (previous close ≤ that threshold), and (regime filter disabled OR close > 30-minute EMA regime AND regime slope > 0), and (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum); OR, in Retest Confirmation mode, a long breakout has occurred, its first retest occurs within the maximum retest window, and low ≤ opening box high + retest tolerance AND (confirmation-close requirement enabled AND close > opening box high OR confirmation-close requirement disabled AND close > open AND close ≥ opening box high − retest tolerance), with no trade already used for this box. A retest requires low ≤ opening box high + retest tolerance AND high ≥ opening box high − retest tolerance. In either entry mode, require flat position, positive opening box height, and valid long stop below close.
Short: First close crosses below opening box low − breakout buffer (previous close ≥ that threshold), and (regime filter disabled OR close < 30-minute EMA regime AND regime slope < 0), and (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum); OR, in Retest Confirmation mode, a short breakout has occurred, its first retest occurs within the maximum retest window, and high ≥ opening box low − retest tolerance AND (confirmation-close requirement enabled AND close < opening box low OR confirmation-close requirement disabled AND close < open AND close ≤ opening box low + retest tolerance), with no trade already used for this box. A retest requires high ≥ opening box low − retest tolerance AND low ≤ opening box low + retest tolerance. In either entry mode, require flat position, positive opening box height, and valid short stop above close.
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop OR (exit mode is measured box or fixed R AND high ≥ target) OR (exit mode is next opening box AND a new opening box begins).
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop OR (exit mode is measured box or fixed R AND low ≤ target) OR (exit mode is next opening box AND a new opening box begins).
