Calculation: MACD = 10,000 × (fast moving average − slow moving average) / slow moving average; fast and slow use EMAs, or volume-weighted EMAs for VMACD, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: MACD signal = EMA of MACD
Calculation: RSI, MFI, CCI, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI = the standard indicator of that name; smooth RSI, MFI, and CCI with an EMA; smooth Stochastic %K with an SMA and use an SMA of %K as its basis; Stochastic RSI applies Stochastic to RSI
Calculation: DRM = 50 + 50 × smoothed range force / smoothed absolute range force; range force is the range of the rolling close high and low when either extends beyond its prior value, otherwise the close change; smooth both terms with an RMA
Calculation: Oscillator basis = EMA of the smoothed RSI, MFI, CCI, or DRM; for Stochastic and Stochastic RSI, use the %D SMA
Calculation: Overbought and oversold levels = equal distances above and below the oscillator midpoint; extreme levels extend those distances
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low below the prior confirmed price pivot low while the corresponding oscillator pivot low is above its prior pivot low; bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high above the prior confirmed price pivot high while the corresponding oscillator pivot high is below its prior pivot high
Long: MACD or VMACD selected AND HLC3 > trend line AND MACD crosses above MACD signal AND (MACD < 0 OR prior MACD < 0) OR selected oscillator is RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stochastic RSI AND trend signals enabled AND (trend mode is Range OR (HL2 > trend line AND close > trend line)) AND an eligible buy trigger occurs: oscillator crosses above basis while below oversold, or crosses above extreme oversold, or crosses above oversold; fast triggers require the shorter cooldown and slow triggers the longer cooldown, with slow suppressed when fast triggers; OR bullish divergence is enabled and occurs
Short: MACD or VMACD selected AND HLC3 < trend line AND MACD crosses below MACD signal AND (MACD > 0 OR prior MACD > 0) OR selected oscillator is RSI, MFI, CCI, DRM, Stochastic, or Stochastic RSI AND trend signals enabled AND (trend mode is Range OR (HL2 < trend line AND close < trend line)) AND an eligible sell trigger occurs: oscillator crosses below basis while above overbought, or crosses below extreme overbought, or crosses below overbought; fast triggers require the shorter cooldown and slow triggers the longer cooldown, with slow suppressed when fast triggers; OR bearish divergence is enabled and occurs
