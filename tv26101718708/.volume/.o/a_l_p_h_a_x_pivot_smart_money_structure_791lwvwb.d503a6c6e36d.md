Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is strictly above/below the corresponding values on the configured number of bars before and after it; use it only after the bars after it have completed.
Calculation: Structure state = neutral initially; a break above the latest swing high sets bullish state, and a break below the latest swing low sets bearish state. A bullish break from bearish/neutral state is bullish CHoCH; from bullish state it is bullish BOS. A bearish break from bullish/neutral state is bearish CHoCH; from bearish state it is bearish BOS. Break by close, or by high/low if wick mode is selected.
Calculation: Active leg = on a bullish break, start at the preceding swing low and end at the break price; on a bearish break, start at the preceding swing high and end at the break price. Extend its high/low with subsequent bar highs/lows until the next structure break.
Calculation: OTE band = for a bullish leg, [leg high − range × upper retracement, leg high − range × lower retracement]; for a bearish leg, [leg low + range × lower retracement, leg low + range × upper retracement], where upper retracement is greater than lower retracement. Bull OTE touch: current low is within the band; bear OTE touch: current high is within the band.
Calculation: Rejection candle = bullish: close > open and (close − low)/(high − low) > threshold, or close > open, prior close < prior open, and close ≥ prior open. Bearish: close < open and (high − close)/(high − low) > threshold, or close < open, prior close > prior open, and close ≤ prior open.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = bullish: current low is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars by at least the configured ATR multiple, and close > that preceding low and close > open. Bearish: current high is above the highest high of the preceding lookback bars by at least the configured ATR multiple, and close < that preceding high and close < open. Recent sweep means one occurred on this bar or either of the prior two bars.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of seven binary conditions: structure agrees with direction, OTE touched, premium/discount filter passes, HTF EMA bias agrees (fast EMA > slow EMA and HTF close > fast EMA for bullish; inverse for bearish), recent sweep in direction, rejection candle in direction, and both ADX and volume filters pass. HTF values use the selected higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off (last completed historical bar; potentially forming in real time).
Calculation: Long stop = min(latest swing low, active leg low) − configured ATR buffer; long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × configured reward multiple. Short stop = max(latest swing high, active leg high) + configured ATR buffer; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × configured reward multiple.
Long: bullish CHoCH or bullish BOS, OR bullish liquidity sweep, OR (bullish state AND bullish active leg AND bullish OTE touch AND bullish rejection candle AND discount (close < leg midpoint) if required AND (bullish state OR bullish CHoCH within its configured window AND recent bullish sweep) AND confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND configured session is active if required AND cooldown elapsed AND ADX filter passes if enabled AND volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier if enabled).
Short: bearish CHoCH or bearish BOS, OR bearish liquidity sweep, OR (bearish state AND bearish active leg AND bearish OTE touch AND bearish rejection candle AND premium (close > leg midpoint) if required AND (bearish state OR bearish CHoCH within its configured window AND recent bearish sweep) AND confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND configured session is active if required AND cooldown elapsed AND ADX filter passes if enabled AND volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier if enabled).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR bearish CHoCH.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR bullish CHoCH.
