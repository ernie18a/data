Calculation: Raw momentum = selected lookback change in close: percentage change, absolute difference, or log return
Calculation: Current drawdown = (running peak of close − close) / running peak of close; maximum drawdown = greatest current drawdown over the risk lookback
Calculation: Smoothed current and maximum drawdown = recursively smoothed drawdowns using an alpha derived from the volatility percentile, or a smoothing-period alpha when adaptive smoothing is disabled
Calculation: Risk-adjusted momentum = raw momentum × max(1 − smoothed current drawdown / smoothed maximum drawdown × risk scaling, 0.05)
Calculation: Momentum acceleration = EMA(risk-adjusted momentum − its value one acceleration lookback earlier, over the acceleration lookback)
Calculation: Predicted momentum = linear-regression value of risk-adjusted momentum over the regression lookback + regression slope × forward offset
Calculation: Leading momentum = 0.6 × risk-adjusted momentum + 0.3 × predicted momentum + 0.1 × momentum acceleration when leading mode is enabled; otherwise it equals risk-adjusted momentum
Calculation: Final momentum = leading momentum × 1.2 when risk-adjusted momentum is below the negative exhaustion threshold and volume is below its 20-bar SMA; × 0.8 when momentum is above the threshold and volume is below its 20-bar SMA; otherwise unchanged
Calculation: Normalized momentum = z-score of final momentum over the normalization lookback, clamped to [−3.5, 3.5]
Calculation: Signal line = recursively smoothed normalized momentum using an alpha derived from volatility percentile, or a signal-period alpha when adaptive smoothing is disabled
Calculation: Thresholds = either normalization-lookback SMA of normalized momentum ± threshold multiplier × its standard deviation, or configured static upper and lower thresholds
Calculation: Low-or-medium-risk condition = smoothed current drawdown < 0.7 × smoothed maximum drawdown
Long: (normalized momentum crosses above signal line AND normalized momentum > lower threshold OR leading mode is enabled AND momentum acceleration > 0 AND normalized momentum < signal line AND normalized momentum > its prior value AND normalized momentum > lower threshold) AND low-or-medium-risk condition OR normalized momentum < lower threshold AND normalized momentum < its prior value OR momentum acceleration crosses above zero OR (close < prior close AND momentum-regression slope > 0) OR (low < prior low AND low one bar earlier < low two bars earlier AND normalized momentum > its prior value AND normalized momentum one bar earlier > normalized momentum two bars earlier) OR bullish trend (normalized momentum > signal line AND normalized momentum > lower threshold)
Short: (normalized momentum crosses below signal line AND normalized momentum < upper threshold OR leading mode is enabled AND momentum acceleration < 0 AND normalized momentum > signal line AND normalized momentum < its prior value AND normalized momentum < upper threshold) OR momentum acceleration crosses below zero OR (close > prior close AND momentum-regression slope < 0) OR (high > prior high AND high one bar earlier > high two bars earlier AND normalized momentum < its prior value AND normalized momentum one bar earlier < normalized momentum two bars earlier) OR risk-adjusted momentum > exhaustion threshold AND volume < its 20-bar SMA OR normalized momentum > upper threshold AND normalized momentum > its prior value OR bearish trend (normalized momentum < signal line AND normalized momentum < upper threshold)
Exit: Long, normalized momentum crosses below signal line OR normalized momentum > upper threshold
Exit: Short, normalized momentum crosses above signal line OR normalized momentum < lower threshold
