Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(0.17 + close location within the bar range × 0.43 + absolute body/range × 0.20 + 0.20 if close ≥ open, otherwise 0.05–0.95)
Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1); CVD resets at each active-session start to current estimated delta, then cumulatively adds estimated delta
Calculation: CVD impulse = (fast EMA of CVD − slow EMA of CVD) / max(abs(slow EMA of CVD), volume); the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Session VWAP and deviation bands = during the active session, cumulative(hlc3 × volume) / cumulative(volume), with volume-weighted standard deviation bands around VWAP
Calculation: Developing profile = divide the range of the most recent active-session bars, up to the configured bar limit, into equal-price rows; assign each bar's volume and estimated buy/sell volume to the row containing hlc3. POC is the highest-volume row's midpoint. Value area expands from POC toward the higher-volume adjacent row until the configured share of total volume is covered; VAL and VAH are its lower and upper boundaries
Calculation: Bid/ask shelf power = for each profile row, signed buy imbalance (buy share − 0.5) × 2 × row volume / maximum row volume; bid and ask shelf power are the greatest positive and negative magnitudes, respectively
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CVD divergence = at confirmed pivots using the same left/right lookbacks for price and CVD, price makes a lower low / higher high while CVD makes a higher low / lower high than at the prior corresponding pivot
Calculation: Bid/ask quality = capped auction score combining location relative to session VWAP and POC, corresponding shelf power, directional CVD impulse above its threshold, corresponding divergence, value-area width relative to ATR, and directional profile skew
Long: bar closes confirmed during the active session AND close > session VWAP AND close ≥ VAL AND close ≤ upper outer session VWAP deviation band AND fast CVD EMA > slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse > its threshold AND bid quality ≥ quality gate AND bid quality > ask quality AND (close crosses above POC from at/below it OR low reaches within the configured ATR-based tolerance of VAL and close > VAL and open OR bullish CVD divergence OR bid shelf power > shelf threshold) AND no long or short signal occurred within the configured cooldown bars
Short: bar closes confirmed during the active session AND close < session VWAP AND close ≤ VAH AND close ≥ lower outer session VWAP deviation band AND fast CVD EMA < slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse < negative threshold AND ask quality ≥ quality gate AND ask quality > bid quality AND (close crosses below POC from at/above it OR high reaches within the configured ATR-based tolerance of VAH and close < VAH and open OR bearish CVD divergence OR ask shelf power > shelf threshold) AND no long or short signal occurred within the configured cooldown bars
Exit: long, bar closes confirmed AND (bearish CVD divergence OR close < session VWAP OR close < POC)
Exit: short, bar closes confirmed AND (bullish CVD divergence OR close > session VWAP OR close > POC)
