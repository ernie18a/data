Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a selected indicator makes a higher low while price makes a lower low; regular bearish divergence = indicator lower high while price makes a higher high. Hidden bullish divergence = indicator lower low while price makes a higher low; hidden bearish divergence = indicator higher high while price makes a lower high. Compare the current bar by default (or the prior bar when confirmation is enabled) with a confirmed pivot low/high among the configured number of recent pivots within the configured bar limit, requiring a distance greater than five bars. Reject a divergence if the straight segments between the compared indicator values or between their closes are crossed by an intervening indicator value or close. Price pivots use close or low/high according to the configured pivot source. Scan the configured indicators: MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, VW-MACD, CMF, MFI, and optionally an external series.
Calculation: Signal stop = the lesser of entry price and the divergence/current low, minus ATR times its configured multiplier, for bullish divergence; or the greater of entry price and divergence/current high, plus ATR times its multiplier, for bearish divergence. Cap stop distance at the configured maximum, expressed as that value times minimum tick times 10. Risk = absolute distance from entry to stop. First and second targets are entry plus risk and twice risk for bullish divergence, or entry minus risk and twice risk for bearish divergence.
Long: At least the configured minimum number of divergences is detected, the applicable configured filters pass (including any enabled regular-plus-hidden confluence or distinct-line-count filters), and a bullish regular or hidden divergence is detected.
Short: At least the configured minimum number of divergences is detected, the applicable configured filters pass (including any enabled regular-plus-hidden confluence or distinct-line-count filters), and a bearish regular or hidden divergence is detected.
Exit: Long, price <= signal stop OR price >= first target OR price >= second target.
Exit: Short, price >= signal stop OR price <= first target OR price <= second target.
