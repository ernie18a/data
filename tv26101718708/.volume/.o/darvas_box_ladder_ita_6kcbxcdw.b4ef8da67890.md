Calculation: Active Darvas box = after a bar whose high is at least the highest high over the lookback including that bar, set the ceiling to its high; each subsequent high above the ceiling resets the ceiling and starts confirmation again. The ceiling confirms after the confirmation-bar count of consecutive bars without a higher high. Set the floor to the lowest low from the ceiling’s starting bar through its confirmation; a lower low resets floor confirmation, and a higher high restarts ceiling confirmation. The floor confirms after the confirmation-bar count of consecutive bars without a lower low. The box is active only if (ceiling − floor) / floor × 100 is between the minimum and maximum box-height parameters, inclusive.
Long: Active Darvas box AND close > ceiling × (1 + breakout-buffer parameter / 100)
Exit: long, active Darvas box AND close < floor
