Calculation: Pivot low/high = low/high extreme confirmed by bars on both sides of the pivot.
Calculation: Volume strength = 100 minus clamp(96 − 12 × pivot-bar volume / pivot-bar volume SMA, 40, 92); use ratio 1 when the SMA is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish order block = confirmed pivot low whose volume strength meets the minimum; zone is pivot-bar low to pivot-bar high. Its trigger is pivot-bar low + current confirmation-bar ATR × displacement. Activate it when a bar’s high reaches the trigger during the pivot confirmation window, provided no low from the first trigger bar through confirmation is below the zone low; otherwise activate it on a confirmed close crossover above the trigger.
Calculation: Bearish order block = confirmed pivot high whose volume strength meets the minimum; zone is pivot-bar low to pivot-bar high. Its trigger is pivot-bar high − current confirmation-bar ATR × displacement. Activate it when a bar’s low reaches the trigger during the pivot confirmation window, provided no high from the first trigger bar through confirmation is above the zone high; otherwise activate it on a confirmed close crossunder below the trigger.
Long: A bullish order block is active AND ((previous low ≤ zone high AND current low > zone high) OR (previous close ≤ zone high AND current close > zone high)) AND bar is confirmed AND bars since the previous bullish retest exceed the configured cooldown.
Short: A bearish order block is active AND ((previous high ≥ zone low AND current high < zone low) OR (previous close ≥ zone low AND current close < zone low)) AND bar is confirmed AND bars since the previous bearish retest exceed the configured cooldown.
