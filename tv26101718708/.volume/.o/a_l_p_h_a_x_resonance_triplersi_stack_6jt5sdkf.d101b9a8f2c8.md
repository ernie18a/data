Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow RSI = RSI of close with progressively longer lookbacks; slope for each = current RSI minus its value three bars earlier.
Calculation: Dynamic oversold/overbought = the lower/higher configured percentile of medium RSI over a lookback.
Calculation: RSI bandwidth = highest minus lowest medium RSI over the coil lookback; coil = bandwidth < its same-length simple moving average × compression multiplier.
Calculation: Coil release = previous bar was in a coil AND current bar is not AND absolute one-bar change in fast RSI ≥ minimum move.
Calculation: Bull stack = fast RSI > midline, medium RSI > midline − 2, and slow RSI > midline − 4; bear stack = fast RSI < midline, medium RSI < midline + 2, and slow RSI < midline + 4.
Calculation: Bull slope = fast and medium RSI slopes > 0 and slow RSI slope ≥ 0; bear slope = fast and medium RSI slopes < 0 and slow RSI slope ≤ 0.
Calculation: Dynamic zones, RSI values, slopes, and bandwidth use current-timeframe bars. Confirmed price/RSI pivots use the configured bars on each side and become known only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe fast EMA >/< slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close >/< fast EMA. Use the currently available higher-timeframe bar (forming live; last completed in historical data).
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 50 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Resonance score for each side = stack points (2 for full side stack, else 1 if fast and medium RSI are on that side of midline, else 0) + slope points (2 for full side slope, else 1 if fast and medium slopes are on that side, else 0) + 1 if fast RSI is above/below medium RSI and medium RSI is ≥ slow RSI − 3/≤ slow RSI + 3 + higher-timeframe points (2 if enabled and aligned, 0 if enabled and not aligned, 1 if disabled) + 1 if volume ≥ its moving average × threshold or volume filter is disabled + 1 if Choppiness Index < threshold or chop filter is disabled + 1 if close is above/below the configured EMA or EMA filter is disabled; cap at 10.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot low/high is lower/higher than the previous confirmed price pivot while medium RSI at the pivot is higher/lower than at the previous RSI pivot, and pivot RSI ≤ dynamic oversold + 8 / ≥ dynamic overbought − 8; remains valid for up to twice the pivot lookback bars after the pivot.
Long: At least one enabled setup: (fast RSI crosses above dynamic oversold AND medium RSI rises AND slow RSI > midline − 8 AND close > open AND close > prior close) OR (fast RSI crosses above midline AND bull stack AND bull slope) OR (bullish divergence is live AND close > open AND close > prior close AND fast RSI rises AND close > the low at the pivot) OR (coil release AND fast RSI > dynamic oversold AND bull slope AND close > open); AND bullish resonance score ≥ minimum AND Choppiness Index passes its gate AND higher-timeframe bias aligns if hard alignment is enabled AND close passes the optional EMA filter; signal bar is confirmed and at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior signal.
Short: At least one enabled setup: (fast RSI crosses below dynamic overbought AND medium RSI falls AND slow RSI < midline + 8 AND close < open AND close < prior close) OR (fast RSI crosses below midline AND bear stack AND bear slope) OR (bearish divergence is live AND close < open AND close < prior close AND fast RSI falls AND close < the high at the pivot) OR (coil release AND fast RSI < dynamic overbought AND bear slope AND close < open); AND bearish resonance score ≥ minimum AND Choppiness Index passes its gate AND higher-timeframe bias aligns if hard alignment is enabled AND close passes the optional EMA filter; signal bar is confirmed and at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior signal.
