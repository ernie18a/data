Calculation: Momentum score = sum of configured points for: price above all of the simple moving average, long-term exponential moving average, and session VWAP / below all three / otherwise; RSI overbought / oversold / above midpoint / otherwise; VWMA-based momentum (percentage change in volume-weighted moving average over a lookback) strongly positive / negative / otherwise positive / otherwise; ADX high / medium / low; stochastic %K overbought / oversold / above midpoint / otherwise; CCI overbought / oversold / positive / otherwise; MFI overbought / oversold / above midpoint / otherwise; ATR high / low / otherwise relative to its moving average; and cumulative signed volume rising / falling / unchanged. Use configured lookbacks and thresholds.
Long: daily entry count below its configured cap AND inside the configured trading session (when enabled) AND momentum score below its configured low threshold AND CCI crosses above its configured oversold threshold (current CCI above threshold and previous CCI at or below it)
Short: daily entry count below its configured cap AND inside the configured trading session (when enabled) AND momentum score above its configured high threshold and below its configured upper bound AND CCI crosses below its configured overbought threshold (current CCI below threshold and previous CCI at or above it)
Exit: long, low <= the active stop OR high >= the profit target OR the configured session has just ended with force-flat enabled
Exit: short, high >= the active stop OR low <= the profit target OR the configured session has just ended with force-flat enabled
