Calculation: Profile rows = estimated volume distributed uniformly across each candle’s low-to-high range, aggregated over either a fixed recent chart-bar window or the window from the latest confirmed chart-bar pivot to the current confirmed bar, capped by the lookback limit; lower-timeframe candles may be used when available.
Calculation: Profile key levels = the point-of-control row, value-area boundaries expanded outward from the point of control toward the higher-volume adjacent row until the target share of profile volume is reached, local volume peaks and troughs, and ranked highest- or lowest-volume rows; levels may be single rows or zones.
Calculation: Level side = +1 when close is above the level or zone, -1 when close is below it, and 0 when close is within it.
Calculation: Level touch = high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top.
Calculation: Relative volume = current chart-bar volume divided by the average volume of preceding chart bars.
Long: On a confirmed bar, for an active eligible level created on an earlier bar, relative volume meets the configured minimum and the level’s signal cooldown has elapsed, AND (the stored level side changes from -1 to +1 OR a new touch episode closes on its stored +1 side OR a pending retest closes on its breakout +1 side within the retest window while touching the level).
Short: On a confirmed bar, for an active eligible level created on an earlier bar, relative volume meets the configured minimum and the level’s signal cooldown has elapsed, AND (the stored level side changes from +1 to -1 OR a new touch episode closes on its stored -1 side OR a pending retest closes on its breakout -1 side within the retest window while touching the level).
