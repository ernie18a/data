Calculation: Structural anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the current bar first made a higher high or lower low than the previous lookback bars; reset on each such break.
Calculation: Inner and outer bands = anchored VWAP ± inner or outer multiples of the long-lookback SMA of chart-timeframe ATR; the outer multiple is larger. If the ATR average is unavailable, use current ATR. Outer-band touch tolerance is an ATR multiple.
Calculation: ATR z-score = (current chart-timeframe ATR − its longer-lookback mean) / its longer-lookback standard deviation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = long-lookback EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming.
Long: No active position AND a lower outer-band touch has armed a long since the last VWAP touch or structural anchor reset, with no upper outer-band touch since that arming AND close > lower inner band AND cooldown elapsed since the last entry signal AND (ATR regime filter disabled OR ATR z-score ≥ the low-volatility threshold) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: No active position AND an upper outer-band touch has armed a short since the last VWAP touch or structural anchor reset, with no lower outer-band touch since that arming AND close < upper inner band AND cooldown elapsed since the last entry signal AND (ATR regime filter disabled OR ATR z-score ≥ the low-volatility threshold) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA).
Exit: Long, (low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP, or—when body exit is enabled—absolute close-to-VWAP distance ≤ configured ATR tolerance) OR low ≤ lower outer band + touch tolerance OR a structural anchor reset.
Exit: Short, (low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP, or—when body exit is enabled—absolute close-to-VWAP distance ≤ configured ATR tolerance) OR high ≥ upper outer band − touch tolerance OR a structural anchor reset.
