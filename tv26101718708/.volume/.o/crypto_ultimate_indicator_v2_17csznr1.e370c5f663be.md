Calculation: Body-weighted volume delta = volume × abs(close − open) / (high − low) × sign(close − open); zero when high = low
Calculation: EMA ribbon score = sum of +1 when each of EMA(8)>EMA(13), EMA(13)>EMA(21), EMA(21)>EMA(34), EMA(34)>EMA(55), and close>EMA(8), otherwise −1 for each comparison
Calculation: HTF confluence = 1×daily score + 1.5×weekly score + 0.75×custom-HTF score; use the last completed bar of each timeframe. Daily and weekly scores each equal the sign of the sum of: +1 if HMA is rising, otherwise −1; +1 if Supertrend is bullish, otherwise −1; +1 if RSI>55, −1 if RSI<45, otherwise 0. Custom-HTF score uses the same HMA and RSI terms, without Supertrend.
Calculation: Market regime score = 0.4×min(ADX/50×100,100) + 0.4×max(0,100−Choppiness Index) + 0.2×100×(current Bollinger Band width−lowest width over the recent window)/(highest width over that window−lowest width over that window); protect zero denominators with the smallest price increment
Calculation: Trending regime = market regime score>60; ranging regime = market regime score<40
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while the RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding RSI pivot low; bearish RSI divergence is the inverse at pivot highs. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while the CVD pivot low is higher than the preceding CVD pivot low; bearish CVD divergence is the inverse at pivot highs. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of body-weighted volume delta
Calculation: Confidence score = HTF points + regime points + ribbon points + RSI points + volume points + squeeze-release points + divergence points + sweep points. HTF points scale absolute HTF confluence to a maximum of 25; regime points are 20 when trending, 10 when regime score>40, otherwise 0; ribbon points are 15 when absolute ribbon score≥5, 8 when ≥3, otherwise 0; RSI points are 15 when abs(RSI−50)≥15, 8 when ≥10, otherwise 0; volume points are 15 when absolute volume delta divided by its recent average exceeds 1.5, otherwise 8; squeeze-release points are 10 on the first bar after a squeeze ends, otherwise 0; divergence points are 8 for divergence in the trade direction, otherwise 0; sweep points are 7 for a liquidity sweep in the trade direction when sweep confluence is enabled, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low>high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high<low two bars earlier. Retain active gaps until fully mitigated; a bullish gap is mitigated when low≤its lower boundary, and a bearish gap when high≥its upper boundary.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low breaks below the highest low-lookback-period low and close finishes above that level; bearish liquidity sweep = high breaks above the highest high-lookback-period high and close finishes below that level.
