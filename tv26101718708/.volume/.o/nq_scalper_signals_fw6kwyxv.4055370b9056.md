Calculation: EMA trend = close above short EMA above long EMA for uptrend; close below short EMA below long EMA for downtrend.
Calculation: Trend slope = long EMA minus its value at a longer lookback; strong up/down trend when slope exceeds the corresponding positive/negative price-scaled threshold.
Calculation: Session extremes = highest high and lowest low of the current regular session; premarket high/low = extremes of the current premarket session, locked at regular-session open.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = local high/low confirmed after bars on both sides; retain the latest two highs and latest two lows.
Calculation: Key-level proximity = absolute close-to-level distance divided by level is below a price-scaled threshold; levels are swing pivots, premarket high/low, and current-session high/low.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracements = the quarter, half, 0.618, and three-quarter retracements of current-session high-low range, measured from the more recently set extreme; proximity is an absolute price-scaled distance. A fib is confluent when a nearby fib level is within that distance of a premarket/session extreme or swing pivot.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = the sum of points from the matching signals below, capped at three. A doji-and-engulf signal contributes two points if the doji bar was at a meaningful extreme or the signal is at support/resistance, otherwise one; every other listed component contributes one point. Bearish score also includes a point for a bearish reversal candle at premarket/session resistance.
Calculation: Bullish components = centered small-body doji followed by a larger bullish candle closing above the doji high; larger bullish body engulfing the previous body; hammer at a meaningful low or support; declining-volume exhaustion at a meaningful low; that exhaustion on the doji or engulf bar; volume spike at a meaningful low with bullish close or large lower wick; bullish change of character (close above latest swing high in downtrend); bullish rejection at latest swing low; break above a descending line through the latest two swing highs; bullish EMA pullback; opening-range high breakout; bull-flag breakout; confirmed higher swing high in uptrend; oversold RSI; bullish RSI divergence; centered small-body doji at a meaningful new low; a bullish reversal candle or doji near a fib; or a bullish candle near a confluent fib.
Calculation: Bearish components = centered small-body doji followed by a larger bearish candle closing below the doji low; larger bearish body engulfing the previous body; shooting star at a meaningful high or resistance; declining-volume exhaustion at a meaningful high; that exhaustion on the doji or engulf bar; volume spike at a meaningful high with bearish close or large upper wick; bearish change of character (close below latest swing low in uptrend); bearish rejection at latest swing high; break below an ascending line through the latest two swing lows; bearish EMA pullback; opening-range low breakout; bear-flag breakout; confirmed lower swing low in downtrend; overbought RSI; bearish RSI divergence; centered small-body doji at a meaningful new high; a bearish reversal candle or doji near a fib; or a bearish candle near a confluent fib. Also count a reversal candle or small-body doji near premarket/session resistance.
Calculation: Meaningful high/low = current high/low exceeds the prior rolling extreme by an ATR-scaled margin, or price is near resistance/support; strict meaningful high/low uses only the rolling-extreme test. Reversal candle definitions: hammer has a lower wick above the minimum range fraction and body in the upper range; shooting star has an upper wick above that fraction and body in the lower range. Engulfing requires the current body to exceed the previous body; strong engulfing also requires the previous body to be smaller than the current body by a configured ratio and no prior doji. Doji requires a small body-to-range ratio and a centered body.
Calculation: Reversal volume exhaustion = consecutive declining volume bars, optionally below average volume, at a meaningful extreme, with a small body and no strong-trend exception unless near the corresponding swing pivot. Volume spike = volume at least a configured multiple of its moving average.
Calculation: RSI divergence = a new rolling price high with a lower RSI high and RSI above its midpoint, or a new rolling price low with a higher RSI low and RSI below its midpoint.
Calculation: Bullish EMA pullback = uptrend, low touches the short EMA zone, bullish close decisively above it, and prior consecutive closes above that EMA meet the configured minimum. Bearish EMA pullback is the mirrored downtrend condition.
Calculation: Opening range = high/low of the initial regular-session window, used after that window completes; breakout requires close crossing its boundary from the prior bar and, when enabled, volume above its moving average.
Calculation: Flag breakout = prior directional price move exceeds a configured percentage, recent range is less than a configured fraction of that move, and close breaks the prior recent high/low; when enabled, volume must exceed its moving average.
Calculation: Reversal engulf score = one point for the corresponding engulf pattern, plus one when its prior bar was at a meaningful extreme or the signal is at support/resistance.
Long: bullish score >= configured minimum (raised to three in a strong downtrend) AND bullish score > 0 AND cooldown elapsed AND (regular session if restricted) AND (bullish divergence if required) AND (volume exceeds prior-bar volume by the configured percentage if required) AND (close above short EMA OR engulfing reversal at support/meaningful low OR doji at strict meaningful low with oversold RSI) AND at least one bullish component.
Short: bearish score >= configured minimum (raised to three in a strong uptrend) AND bearish score > 0 AND cooldown elapsed AND (regular session if restricted) AND (bearish divergence if required) AND (volume exceeds prior-bar volume by the configured percentage if required) AND (close below short EMA OR engulfing reversal at resistance/meaningful high OR doji at strict meaningful high with overbought RSI) AND at least one bearish component.
