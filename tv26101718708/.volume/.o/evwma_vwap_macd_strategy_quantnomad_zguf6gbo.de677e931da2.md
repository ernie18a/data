Calculation: EVWMA = ((rolling sum of volume over a lookback − current volume) × prior EVWMA + current volume × close) ÷ rolling sum of volume over that lookback; initialize prior EVWMA to current close
Long: shorter-period EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA) crosses above longer-period EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA)
Short: shorter-period EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA) crosses below longer-period EMA of (VWAP − EVWMA)
