Calculation: Efficiency Ratio = absolute change in close over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (HLC3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume since the enabled session opened, including the current bar
Calculation: Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin Ashi open = prior Heikin Ashi open and close averaged, initialized as (open + close) / 2
Long: Position is flat AND current bar is within an enabled session whose trade limit is not reached AND cooldown has elapsed AND enabled filters pass (volume > its moving average × multiplier; ADX >= minimum; close > EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; if VWAP bias is enabled and VWAP Deviation mode is not selected, close > session VWAP; if ROC filter is enabled, ROC > 0) AND one selected mode condition holds: (EMA Cross mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= trending threshold AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA, with fast EMA length shorter than slow EMA length) OR (VWAP Deviation mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= ranging threshold AND close < session VWAP × (1 − deviation threshold)) OR (Heikin Ashi + RSI mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= trending threshold AND current and prior Heikin Ashi candles are bullish AND RSI > its long threshold) OR (Price Structure mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= ranging threshold AND, over the configured 2- or 3-bar sequence, each successive bar has a higher high and higher low)
Short: Position is flat AND current bar is within an enabled session whose trade limit is not reached AND cooldown has elapsed AND enabled filters pass (volume > its moving average × multiplier; ADX >= minimum; close < EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; if VWAP bias is enabled and VWAP Deviation mode is not selected, close < session VWAP; if ROC filter is enabled, ROC < 0) AND one selected mode condition holds: (EMA Cross mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= trending threshold AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA, with fast EMA length shorter than slow EMA length) OR (VWAP Deviation mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= ranging threshold AND close > session VWAP × (1 + deviation threshold)) OR (Heikin Ashi + RSI mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= trending threshold AND current and prior Heikin Ashi candles are bearish AND RSI < its short threshold) OR (Price Structure mode AND Efficiency Ratio >= ranging threshold AND, over the configured 2- or 3-bar sequence, each successive bar has a lower high and lower low)
Exit: Long, low <= long stop set at entry signal close minus ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= long target set at entry signal close plus ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: Short, high >= short stop set at entry signal close plus ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= short target set at entry signal close minus ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio
