Calculation: Bar delta = volume × abs(close − open) / (high − low), positive when close ≥ open and negative when close < open; use a body fraction of 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: Session CVD = sum of bar delta since the start of the current calendar day in the configured timezone; reset at each new day
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3 since the current day began, plus or minus one volume-weighted standard deviation; reset at each new day
Calculation: Auction tolerance = a configurable number of minimum price ticks
