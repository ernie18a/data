Calculation: For each evaluated timeframe (15-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour, and daily), use its still-forming bar when available; otherwise use its latest completed bar. Highest high and lowest low use a lookback on that timeframe's bars.
Calculation: 0.618 retracement level = lowest low + (highest high − lowest low) × 0.618
Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) ÷ 2
Calculation: Volume measure = volume × close; compare it with its simple moving average over a lookback on the same timeframe.
