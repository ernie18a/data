Calculation: Rolling volume profile = over a configurable lookback, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into configurable equal price rows; distribute each bar’s volume equally across every row its high–low range touches. POC is the center of the fullest row. Starting there, expand the value area toward the adjacent row with more volume (ties upward) until it contains the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges.
Calculation: Smoothed POC, VAH, VAL = EMA of each rolling-profile level, using the same configurable smoothing length.
Calculation: Lower and upper bands = smoothed VAL minus ATR times a configurable buffer, and smoothed VAH plus ATR times that buffer. Trail the lower band to the greater of its raw value and prior lower band when prior close is above the prior lower band; otherwise use its raw value. Trail the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and prior upper band when prior close is below the prior upper band; otherwise use its raw value.
Calculation: Trend = initialize bullish; turn bullish from bearish when the configured flip-test price (high in wick mode, otherwise close) is above the prior upper band; turn bearish from bullish when the flip-test price (low in wick mode, otherwise close) is below the prior lower band; otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: Trend strength = min(100, distance score + POC slope score + value-area position score). Distance score = min(abs(close − smoothed POC) / safe ATR, 3) / 3 × 40. POC slope score = min(abs(smoothed POC − its value one smoothing length earlier) / safe ATR, 2) / 2 × 30. Safe ATR is ATR, or the 14-bar SMA of abs(close − prior close) when ATR is zero. Position score = 30 when close is above smoothed VAH or below smoothed VAL; 10 when close is strictly between them; otherwise 20.
Long: prior trend bearish AND current trend bullish AND trend strength >= configured minimum.
Short: prior trend bullish AND current trend bearish AND trend strength >= configured minimum.
