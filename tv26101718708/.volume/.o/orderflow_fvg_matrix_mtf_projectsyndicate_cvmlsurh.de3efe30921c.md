Calculation: Range-based buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume when the range is zero; formation delta = 2 × summed buy volume − summed volume; formation absorption = sum over the three formation bars of volume × [(high − max(open, close)) + (min(open, close) − low)] / range × 0.5, or zero for a zero range.
Calculation: FVG strength = clamp(10 × weighted mean of six scores, 0, 10): gap displacement = clamp(gap size / (1.5 × ATR), 0, 1); directional delta alignment = clamp(direction × formation delta / formation volume, 0, 1), with direction +1 for bullish and −1 for bearish; relative volume = clamp(formation volume / (6 × average volume), 0, 1); flow dominance = clamp(abs(formation delta) / max(formation volume, 1), 0, 1); middle-bar body = clamp(abs(close − open) / (high − low), 0, 1), or zero for a zero range; absorption = clamp(2 × formation absorption / max(formation volume, 1), 0, 1). The six scores use separate configurable weights, normalized by their weight sum.
Long: On a confirmed bar of any enabled higher timeframe, the last three completed bars form a bullish gap (low of the most recent > high of the third-most-recent), the gap meets the configured minimum size (ATR-based, or price-percent-based when selected), and bullish FVG strength ≥ configured minimum.
Short: On a confirmed bar of any enabled higher timeframe, the last three completed bars form a bearish gap (high of the most recent < low of the third-most-recent), the gap meets the configured minimum size (ATR-based, or price-percent-based when selected), and bearish FVG strength ≥ configured minimum.
