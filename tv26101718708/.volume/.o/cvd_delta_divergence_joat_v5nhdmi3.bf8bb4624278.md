Calculation: CVD = running sum of per-bar delta, reset at the configured session, day, or week boundary, or never; per-bar delta is the sum over lower-timeframe bars of their volume (or 1 if unweighted) when each close rises from the preceding lower-timeframe close, minus that weight when it falls; ties contribute zero. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use chart-bar volume when close rises from the prior chart-bar close, negative volume when it falls, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Divergence strength = 50 × [(1 − exp(−|price-pivot change| / ATR)) + (1 − exp(−|CVD-pivot change| / standard deviation of per-bar delta))]; require strength at or above the configured minimum.
Long: On confirmation of a price pivot low, compare it with the preceding price pivot low; pair each with the latest distinct CVD pivot low within the pivot-matching tolerance; require the price pivots to be within the configured maximum gap and the class cooldown to have elapsed, and either the newer price low is lower than the prior low while its paired CVD low is higher (regular bullish divergence), OR the newer price low is higher while its paired CVD low is lower (hidden bullish divergence); require divergence strength at or above its configured minimum.
Short: On confirmation of a price pivot high, compare it with the preceding price pivot high; pair each with the latest distinct CVD pivot high within the pivot-matching tolerance; require the price pivots to be within the configured maximum gap and the class cooldown to have elapsed, and either the newer price high is higher than the prior high while its paired CVD high is lower (regular bearish divergence), OR the newer price high is lower while its paired CVD high is higher (hidden bearish divergence); require divergence strength at or above its configured minimum.
