Calculation: approximate CVD = cumulative sum of volume on bullish bars, minus volume on bearish bars, with zero delta on unchanged bars; compare it with its moving average.
Calculation: volume point of control = price bin with the greatest summed volume, assigning each bar’s volume to a bin by its close within the rolling high–low range; use its price for proximity and compare it with its value five bars earlier.
Calculation: smart buying = volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND close > open AND lower wick ≥ real body × 0.5; smart selling = volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND close < open AND close < low + (high − low) × 0.55.
Long: close is within a threshold percentage of the volume point of control AND (the point of control is not below its value five bars earlier OR close > the point of control) AND close > short-term EMA AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND (close > previous bar’s high OR close crosses above the short-term EMA) AND approximate CVD > its moving average AND (RSI < its upper threshold AND the rolling RSI low is below its lower threshold AND RSI > previous bar’s RSI AND RSI > its moving average OR smart buying OR price makes a lower low than the previous ten bars while approximate CVD is above its previous-ten-bar low and RSI < 50) AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous long signal.
Short: smart selling OR (RSI > 65 AND approximate CVD < its moving average) OR (close < short-term EMA AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND approximate CVD < its moving average), AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous short signal.
