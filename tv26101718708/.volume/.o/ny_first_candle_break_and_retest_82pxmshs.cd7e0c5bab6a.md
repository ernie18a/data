Calculation: First-candle range = high and low of the first bar in each NY session
Calculation: Break/retest window = after price exceeds the first-candle high/low by an ATR-scaled minimum, allow retests only between a minimum and maximum number of bars later; the retest bar’s low (high) must be within an ATR-scaled band around the broken high (low)
Long: During the NY session, after a high break, within the break/retest window, low is within the retest band around the first-candle high, close > first-candle high, and (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its 10-bar SMA × threshold)
Short: During the NY session, after a low break, within the break/retest window, high is within the retest band around the first-candle low, close < first-candle low, and (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its 10-bar SMA × threshold)
Calculation: Long stop = current bar low − ATR × stop multiplier; long targets = current close + (current close − long stop) × reward/risk ratio × target fractions
Calculation: Short stop = current bar high + ATR × stop multiplier; short targets = current close − (short stop − current close) × reward/risk ratio × target fractions
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after close − entry price reaches the configured fraction of the entry-to-third-target distance, raise the stop to the greater of its prior value and close − ATR × trailing amount, only if above the long stop
Calculation: Short trailing stop = after entry price − close reaches the configured fraction of the entry-to-third-target distance, lower the stop to the lesser of its prior value and close + ATR × trailing amount, only if below the short stop
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= any take-profit target OR low <= active long trailing stop OR NY session ends
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= any take-profit target OR high >= active short trailing stop OR NY session ends
