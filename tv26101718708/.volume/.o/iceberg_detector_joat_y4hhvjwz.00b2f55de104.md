Calculation: Lower-wick test = lower wick > max(candle body, minimum price increment) × wick/body threshold, low is in the lower 25% of the lookback high-low range, and volume ≥ volume moving average × volume multiplier.
Long: A support iceberg is newly confirmed by at least the minimum number of lower-wick tests within the detection window, with each test level within ATR × cluster tolerance of the current test level and at least the required percentage of tests closing at or above their open; OR an active resistance iceberg is broken by a confirmed close at least ATR × break distance above its level.
