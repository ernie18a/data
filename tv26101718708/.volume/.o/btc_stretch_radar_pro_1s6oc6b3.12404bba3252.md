Calculation: EMA stretch = (close − EMA(close) / EMA(close)) × 100
Calculation: Upper stretch extreme = average EMA stretch + standard deviation of EMA stretch × multiplier, using the same lookback for both statistics
Calculation: Lower stretch extreme = average EMA stretch − standard deviation of EMA stretch × multiplier, using the same lookback for both statistics
Calculation: Awake = ATR > simple moving average of ATR, using a longer lookback for the baseline than for ATR
Long: EMA stretch < lower stretch extreme AND Awake
Short: EMA stretch > upper stretch extreme AND Awake
