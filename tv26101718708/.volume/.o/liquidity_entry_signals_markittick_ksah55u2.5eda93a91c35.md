Calculation: Long stop = signal bar low − ATR at signal × stop multiplier
Calculation: Short stop = signal bar high + ATR at signal × stop multiplier
Calculation: Long take-profit levels = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × each configured reward multiple
Calculation: Short take-profit levels = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × each configured reward multiple
Long: low < lowest low of the previous sweep-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open AND (chart trend filter disabled OR prior bar close > prior bar SMA) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its SMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR / close × 100 ≥ minimum volatility) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume SMA × minimum volume multiple) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown length)
Short: high > highest high of the previous sweep-lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND close < open AND (chart trend filter disabled OR prior bar close < prior bar SMA) AND (higher-timeframe trend filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its SMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR / close × 100 ≥ minimum volatility) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume SMA × minimum volume multiple) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown length)
Exit: long, high ≥ any long take-profit level OR (high > highest high of the previous sweep-lookback bars AND close < that highest high AND close < open AND entry filters for short are satisfied)
Exit: short, low ≤ any short take-profit level OR (low < lowest low of the previous sweep-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low AND close > open AND entry filters for long are satisfied)
