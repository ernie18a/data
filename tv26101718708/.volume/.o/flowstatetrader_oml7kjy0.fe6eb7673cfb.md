Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Support = lowest low over a configurable lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Resistance = highest high over a configurable lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over the ATR lookback
Calculation: Trend moving average = configurable EMA or SMA of close over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of the prior long trailing stop and current close minus ATR times the configured trailing distance; initialize it to current close minus ATR times that distance when trailing activates
Calculation: Short trailing stop = minimum of the prior short trailing stop and current close plus ATR times the configured trailing distance; initialize it to current close plus ATR times that distance when trailing activates
Long: Flat AND close > support minus 0.5 × ATR AND low <= support AND volume > average volume AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average) AND (time filter disabled OR current bar passes configured trading hours, day-of-week, and lunch-hour filters)
Short: Flat AND close < resistance plus 0.5 × ATR AND high >= resistance AND volume > average volume AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average) AND (time filter disabled OR current bar passes configured trading hours, day-of-week, and lunch-hour filters)
Exit: Long, low <= support minus ATR times the configured stop-loss multiple when trailing is inactive OR low <= long trailing stop when trailing is active OR (partial close enabled AND TP1 has not been hit AND high >= support plus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP1 multiple) OR (partial close enabled AND TP1 has been hit AND high >= support plus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP2 multiple) OR (partial close disabled AND high >= support plus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP1 multiple); trailing activates when close minus entry price >= ATR times the configured activation multiple
Exit: Short, high >= resistance plus ATR times the configured stop-loss multiple when trailing is inactive OR high >= short trailing stop when trailing is active OR (partial close enabled AND TP1 has not been hit AND low <= resistance minus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP1 multiple) OR (partial close enabled AND TP1 has been hit AND low <= resistance minus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP2 multiple) OR (partial close disabled AND low <= resistance minus ATR times the configured reward-to-risk ratio times the TP1 multiple); trailing activates when entry price minus close >= ATR times the configured activation multiple
