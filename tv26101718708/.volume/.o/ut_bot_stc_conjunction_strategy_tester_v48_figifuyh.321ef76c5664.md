Calculation: UT Bot trailing stop = recursively update from close and key × ATR: initialize close − key × ATR; when current and previous closes are above the prior stop, use the greater of prior stop and close − key × ATR; when both are below, use the lesser of prior stop and close + key × ATR; otherwise use close − key × ATR if close is above prior stop, else close + key × ATR.
Calculation: Schaff Trend Cycle = smooth a stochastic normalization of the MACD line (fast EMA of close − slow EMA of close), then smooth a second stochastic normalization of that result; each stochastic uses the same lookback.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = fast-period ATR / slow-period ATR, using a shorter fast lookback than slow lookback.
Calculation: Candle range = high − low; ATR-normalized range = candle range / ATR.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (fast EMA of volume − slow EMA of volume) / slow EMA of volume, using a shorter fast lookback than slow lookback.
Calculation: Long counter-wick percentage = 100 × (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low); short counter-wick percentage = 100 × (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Trend velocity = current EMA of close − its value several bars earlier.
Calculation: Structural stop = lowest low over the active swing lookback for a long, or highest high over that lookback for a short; use a shorter lookback in high volatility than in normal or low volatility.
Long: flat position AND close crosses above the UT Bot trailing stop AND Schaff Trend Cycle ≤ its lower threshold AND, within the confirmation window after these conditions first hold, Schaff Trend Cycle is rising AND datetime is within the allowed session AND candle range is within its ATR-based floor and ceiling AND fast volume EMA > slow volume EMA AND long counter-wick percentage ≤ its limit AND trend velocity is not below its negative limit AND calculated entry price (close + volatility-regime entry offset) − structural stop ≥ minimum stop distance.
Short: flat position AND close crosses below the UT Bot trailing stop AND Schaff Trend Cycle ≥ its upper threshold AND, within the confirmation window after these conditions first hold, Schaff Trend Cycle is falling AND datetime is within the allowed session AND candle range is within its ATR-based floor and ceiling AND fast volume EMA > slow volume EMA AND short counter-wick percentage ≤ its limit AND trend velocity is not above its positive limit AND structural stop − calculated entry price (close − volatility-regime entry offset) ≥ minimum stop distance.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop level OR high ≥ take-profit level; initialize stop at structural stop and take-profit at calculated entry price + initial risk × volatility-regime reward multiple; once the highest high since entry − recorded entry close ≥ initial risk × breakeven multiple, set stop to recorded entry close. If partial exits are enabled, exit half when high reaches calculated entry price + initial risk × partial reward multiple.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop level OR low ≤ take-profit level; initialize stop at structural stop and take-profit at calculated entry price − initial risk × volatility-regime reward multiple; once recorded entry close − lowest low since entry ≥ initial risk × breakeven multiple, set stop to recorded entry close. If partial exits are enabled, exit half when low reaches calculated entry price − initial risk × partial reward multiple.
