Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low, strictly greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured left and right bars; use only after the right bars have completed.
Calculation: Bullish structure = confirmed low A, then confirmed high B, then confirmed low C with C > A; bearish structure = confirmed high A, then confirmed low B, then confirmed high C with C < A.
Calculation: Pullback depth = 100 × (B − C) / (B − A) for bullish structure; 100 × (C − B) / (A − B) for bearish structure.
Calculation: Projected leg = A–B move, measured in price points or as a percentage of A, rescaled at C; optionally multiply by the ratio of a selected moving average of recent A–B leg magnitudes to the current raw leg magnitude. Use the same selected smoothing method and lookback for both.
Calculation: Entry level = C + 0.25 × projected leg for long; C − 0.25 × projected leg for short. Targets = C ± 0.50, 0.75, and 1.00 × projected leg, in the setup direction. Stop = C adjusted beyond C by the configured tick or ATR buffer.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with the preceding completed higher-timeframe close.
Long: A–B–C bullish structure qualifies, pullback depth is within its configured minimum and maximum, B exceeds the preceding confirmed swing high when the trend filter is enabled, higher-timeframe bias is up when enabled, ADX meets its threshold when enabled, projected leg is positive, signal is unlocked, no setup is already armed or active, and the prior completed bar close is at or above the entry level while its low is not below C and the setup has not expired.
Short: A–B–C bearish structure qualifies, pullback depth is within its configured minimum and maximum, B is below the preceding confirmed swing low when the trend filter is enabled, higher-timeframe bias is down when enabled, ADX meets its threshold when enabled, projected leg is positive, signal is unlocked, no setup is already armed or active, and the prior completed bar close is at or below the entry level while its high is not above C and the setup has not expired.
Exit: Long, current bar low is at or below the managed stop OR current bar high is at or above any of the three targets.
Exit: Short, current bar high is at or above the managed stop OR current bar low is at or below any of the three targets.
