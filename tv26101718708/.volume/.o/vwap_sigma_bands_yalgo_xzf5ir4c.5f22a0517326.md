Calculation: Rolling VWAP and σ = On the selected chart or higher timeframe, use the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar: volume-weighted mean of hlc3 and the square root of volume-weighted hlc3 variance. Use the forming higher-timeframe bar when repainting is enabled; otherwise use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Optional smoothing applies three successive EMAs to both series.
Calculation: Deviation bands = For each k from 1 through 6, upper and lower bands are either VWAP ± k·σ or VWAP·exp(±k·σ/VWAP).
Calculation: RSI divergence = At confirmed RSI pivot lows, RSI makes a higher low while price makes a lower low; at confirmed pivot highs, RSI makes a lower high while price makes a higher high. Compare each pivot with the prior pivot within the configured pivot range.
Long: Close crosses above VWAP OR (RSI reversal mode: a bullish RSI event occurred within the momentum window AND (a lower wick tests within the σ-scaled tolerance of any band or VWAP, or pierces below one and closes above it, OR close is below the lower 6σ band when the extreme-outside bypass is enabled)) OR (RSI reversal mode off: close crosses above any lower 2σ–6σ band).
Long: In RSI reversal mode, a bullish RSI event is RSI at or below oversold, RSI crossing back above oversold, or bullish RSI divergence; signal only when the full bullish reversal condition is true now and was false on the prior bar.
Short: Close crosses below VWAP OR (RSI reversal mode: a bearish RSI event occurred within the momentum window AND (an upper wick tests within the σ-scaled tolerance of any band or VWAP, or pierces above one and closes below it, OR close is above the upper 6σ band when the extreme-outside bypass is enabled)) OR (RSI reversal mode off: close crosses below any upper 2σ–6σ band).
Short: In RSI reversal mode, a bearish RSI event is RSI at or above overbought, RSI crossing back below overbought, or bearish RSI divergence; signal only when the full bearish reversal condition is true now and was false on the prior bar.
