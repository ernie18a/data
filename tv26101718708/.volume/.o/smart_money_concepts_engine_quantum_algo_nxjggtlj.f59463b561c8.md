Calculation: Swing pivots = confirmed highs/lows that are the highest/lowest over symmetric lookbacks; internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots
Calculation: Equal highs/lows = consecutive confirmed swing highs/lows within a tolerance measured in ATR
Calculation: Demand/supply zone = full high-low range of the last bearish/bullish candle before a swing break; a zone is fresh until a bar closes through its far boundary
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when current low exceeds high two bars earlier, bearish when current high is below low two bars earlier, with gap size meeting an ATR-based minimum
Long: Confirmed close above an unbroken latest swing high OR internal pivot high
Long: Confirmed bar wicks below an unbroken latest swing low OR active equal-low level and closes back above it; for a swing-low sweep, the level has not been swept and at least five bars have passed since the prior single-swing sweep label
Long: Confirmed bar touches a fresh demand zone (low <= zone top and close >= zone bottom) AND swing and internal bias are bullish AND close is below the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing range
Long: Confirmed close above the top of an active bearish fair value gap, flipping it bullish
Short: Confirmed close below an unbroken latest swing low OR internal pivot low
Short: Confirmed bar wicks above an unbroken latest swing high OR active equal-high level and closes back below it; for a swing-high sweep, the level has not been swept and at least five bars have passed since the prior single-swing sweep label
Short: Confirmed bar touches a fresh supply zone (high >= zone bottom and close <= zone top) AND swing and internal bias are bearish AND close is above the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing range
Short: Confirmed close below the bottom of an active bullish fair value gap, flipping it bearish
