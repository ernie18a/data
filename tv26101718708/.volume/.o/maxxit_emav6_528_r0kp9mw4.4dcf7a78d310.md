Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with the shorter lookback; trigger EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback; trend EMA = exponential moving average of close with a long-term lookback
Calculation: ATR-normalized EMA gap = absolute difference between fast EMA and trend EMA divided by ATR
Calculation: EMA rising over lookback = current EMA is greater than each of its preceding lookback values; EMA falling over lookback = current EMA is less than each of its preceding lookback values
Calculation: Active long stop = initial stop below entry price, raised to the greatest trailing-stop level reached after price advances by the configured trail distance
Calculation: Active short stop = initial stop above entry price, lowered to the least trailing-stop level reached after price declines by the configured trail distance
Long: On a completed bar, trend EMA is rising over the configured lookback AND fast EMA crossed above trigger EMA (previous fast EMA < previous trigger EMA AND current fast EMA > current trigger EMA) AND (setback mode is off OR previous low < previous fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > trend EMA)
Long: On a completed bar, re-entry mode and stop or target management are enabled AND position is flat AND trend EMA is rising over its configured lookback AND fast EMA and high are each rising over the preceding 3 bars AND ATR-normalized EMA gap is below the overextension threshold AND fast EMA > trend EMA AND fast EMA from 3 bars ago > trend EMA AND previous low < previous fast EMA AND close > fast EMA
Short: On a completed bar, trend EMA is falling over the configured lookback AND fast EMA crossed below trigger EMA (previous fast EMA > previous trigger EMA AND current fast EMA < current trigger EMA) AND (setback mode is off OR previous high > previous fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < trend EMA)
Short: On a completed bar, re-entry mode and stop or target management are enabled AND position is flat AND trend EMA is falling over its configured lookback AND fast EMA and low are each falling over the preceding 3 bars AND ATR-normalized EMA gap is below the overextension threshold AND fast EMA < trend EMA AND fast EMA from 3 bars ago < trend EMA AND previous high > previous fast EMA AND close < fast EMA
Exit: long, close >= target price OR close <= active long stop (when stop management is enabled)
Exit: short, close <= target price OR close >= active short stop (when stop management is enabled)
