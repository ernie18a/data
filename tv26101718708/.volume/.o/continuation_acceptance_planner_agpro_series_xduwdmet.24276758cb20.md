Calculation: Fast, base, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with fast shorter than base and base shorter than slow
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Active trend context = bullish when fast EMA > base EMA > slow EMA and the base EMA's ATR-normalized change over a lookback is at least the minimum slope; bearish when fast EMA < base EMA < slow EMA and that change is at most the negative minimum slope. If alignment fails, retain the prior direction for a bounded number of bars while close remains within an ATR-based limit of the slow EMA; otherwise no active context.
Calculation: Acceptance band = base EMA ± ATR × band width
Calculation: Recent band interaction = a bar's high-low range overlaps the acceptance band within the acceptance lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Pullback depth = bullish: max(fast EMA − low, 0) / ATR; bearish: max(high − fast EMA, 0) / ATR
Calculation: Directional target obstruction = highest high, bullish, or lowest low, bearish, over the target lookback of completed bars before the current bar
Calculation: Target room score = 100 if close has cleared the obstruction in the trend direction; otherwise normalize and clamp the nonnegative distance from close to obstruction, in ATR units, from weak-room to clean-room levels
Calculation: Continuation score = round and clamp to 0–100 the sum of: trend score (20% weight; 70% of its component is 100 for a currently aligned stack or 72 for retained context, otherwise 0; 30% is normalized base-EMA slope strength), pullback-depth score (20%; peak-shaped score favoring the ideal depth and weakening toward zero depth or the maximum-risk depth), close score (25 if an accepted close and current aligned stack; otherwise 12 for recent band interaction without invalidation, 8 for retained context, or 4), volume score (15%; clamp volume divided by its moving-average baseline and the required volume ratio, scaled to 100), and target-room score (20%)
Long: bullish active trend context AND recent band interaction AND close > base EMA AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= required close-location fraction AND close >= open AND close-confirmation requirement is met AND volume requirement is met if selected AND strict-mode slope-score and target-room-score requirements are met if selected AND continuation score >= acceptance threshold AND close >= bullish invalidation rail, where bullish invalidation rail is the lower of (base EMA − ATR × band width − ATR × risk buffer) and (slow EMA − ATR × risk buffer × 0.5)
Short: bearish active trend context AND recent band interaction AND close < base EMA AND 1 − (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) >= required close-location fraction AND close <= open AND close-confirmation requirement is met AND volume requirement is met if selected AND strict-mode slope-score and target-room-score requirements are met if selected AND continuation score >= acceptance threshold AND close <= bearish invalidation rail, where bearish invalidation rail is the higher of (base EMA + ATR × band width + ATR × risk buffer) and (slow EMA + ATR × risk buffer × 0.5)
Exit: long, close < bullish invalidation rail
Exit: short, close > bearish invalidation rail
