Calculation: Volume profile resistance = highest high over the lookback minus (highest high over the lookback minus lowest low over the lookback) divided by the number of bins, using current chart bars.
Calculation: Volume profile support = lowest low over the lookback plus (highest high over the lookback minus lowest low over the lookback) divided by the number of bins, using current chart bars.
Long: Close crosses above volume profile resistance.
Short: Close crosses below volume profile support.
