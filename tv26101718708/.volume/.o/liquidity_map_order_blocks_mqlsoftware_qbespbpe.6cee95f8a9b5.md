Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the configured right-bar delay; evaluate the pivot on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Protected-swing trend = maintain prior swing highs/lows and protected high/low. Start neutral. On a confirmed swing high, while bullish, set the protected low to the prior swing low and mark an upside break if the new high exceeds the prior high; otherwise, when neutral or bearish, mark an upside break if the new high exceeds the prior high (neutral) or protected high (bearish), set trend bullish, and set protected low to the prior swing low. Update the prior swing high. On a confirmed swing low, while bearish, set the protected high to the prior swing high and mark a downside break if the new low is below the prior low; otherwise, when neutral or bullish, mark a downside break if the new low is below the prior low (neutral) or protected low (bullish), set trend bearish, and set protected high to the prior swing high. Update the prior swing low.
Long: confirmed swing high AND ((trend is bullish AND (no prior swing high OR swing high > prior swing high)) OR (trend is neutral AND swing high > prior swing high) OR (trend is bearish AND swing high > protected high)).
Short: confirmed swing low AND ((trend is bearish AND (no prior swing low OR swing low < prior swing low)) OR (trend is neutral AND swing low < prior swing low) OR (trend is bullish AND swing low < protected low)).
