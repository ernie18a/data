Calculation: Fast and slow efficiency ratios = absolute net close movement over each lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close movements over that lookback; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Current leg = close minus the latest confirmed opposite-direction pivot, signed positive in the leg direction; leg direction is up after a confirmed low and down after a confirmed high. Confirm pivots using bars on both sides.
Calculation: Leg displacement percentile = fraction of recent completed pivot-to-pivot leg distances that are less than or equal to the absolute travel of the current leg; require at least five completed legs.
Calculation: Divergence quality = 0.5 × min(1, positive efficiency-ratio drop between successive same-side pivots / scale) + 0.3 × min(1, bars between those pivots / leg-memory lookback) + 0.2 × 1 if volume at the newer pivot is lower than at the previous pivot, otherwise 0. At a high, require the newer high to be higher; at a low, require the newer low to be lower. Use fast efficiency and volume sampled at each confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster intensity = prior intensity × exp(−decay) + jump when true range exceeds its moving average times a shock factor, otherwise prior intensity × exp(−decay). Chaos = intensity percentile rank over its lookback at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Trend score = 0.5 × min(1, slow efficiency ratio / scale) + 0.5 × min(1, ADX / scale). Regime is strong trend when score meets the higher threshold, trend when it meets the lower threshold, chaos when volatility-cluster intensity meets its threshold, and otherwise not trend.
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of multi-bar close movement divided by the aggregation period times the variance of one-bar close movement, over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Reversion half-life = −ln(2) / ln(1 + beta), where beta is the correlation of close change with prior close times the standard deviation of close change divided by the standard deviation of prior close; defined only when beta is negative and 1 + beta is positive.
