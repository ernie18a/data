Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot confirmed by equal lookback bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot until another confirms.
Calculation: Structural break direction = bullish when price breaks the latest confirmed swing high and bearish when it breaks the latest confirmed swing low; update on every break, including breaks that fail filters. Each pivot level can break only once.
Calculation: Active zone range = high − low of the latest accepted CHOCH bar; upper boundary = that bar’s high + 4 × range; lower boundary = that bar’s low − 4 × range.
Long: A break above the latest confirmed swing high (close > level in close-break mode, otherwise high > level) occurs after the latest structural break was bearish, and all enabled filters pass (close above session VWAP and EMA; volume ≥ average volume × minimum multiple; signal-bar range between the configured ATR multiples; bar confirmed if required; minimum-bars cooldown satisfied) OR, since the latest accepted zone, a high exceeded its upper boundary and the close is now between the boundaries.
Short: A break below the latest confirmed swing low (close < level in close-break mode, otherwise low < level) occurs after the latest structural break was bullish, and all enabled filters pass (close below session VWAP and EMA; volume ≥ average volume × minimum multiple; signal-bar range between the configured ATR multiples; bar confirmed if required; minimum-bars cooldown satisfied) OR, since the latest accepted zone, a low fell below its lower boundary and the close is now between the boundaries.
