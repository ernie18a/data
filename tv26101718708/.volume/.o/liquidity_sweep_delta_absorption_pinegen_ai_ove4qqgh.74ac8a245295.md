Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = most recent pivot high/low, usable only after the required bars to its right have completed
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: Lower-timeframe net volume delta = sum of volume from completed lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, counting bars with close >= open as positive and bars with close < open as negative
Long: (low < most recent confirmed swing low AND close > most recent confirmed swing low AND close position >= absorption threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier) AND (delta filter disabled OR lower-timeframe net volume delta >= minimum delta)) OR (strategy entry: current setup with the same conditions and enabled trend/session filters, or prior-bar setup with those filters and current close > open when confirmation is enabled; position is flat; risk distance from signal close to sweep low minus ATR buffer is positive)
Short: (high > most recent confirmed swing high AND close < most recent confirmed swing high AND close position <= 1 - absorption threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier) AND (delta filter disabled OR lower-timeframe net volume delta <= -minimum delta)) OR (strategy entry: current setup with the same conditions and enabled trend/session filters, or prior-bar setup with those filters and current close < open when confirmation is enabled; position is flat; risk distance from sweep high plus ATR buffer to signal close is positive)
Exit: long, low <= sweep-bar low - ATR × stop buffer OR high >= signal-bar close + (signal-bar close - stop price) × reward:risk ratio
Exit: short, high >= sweep-bar high + ATR × stop buffer OR low <= signal-bar close - (stop price - signal-bar close) × reward:risk ratio
