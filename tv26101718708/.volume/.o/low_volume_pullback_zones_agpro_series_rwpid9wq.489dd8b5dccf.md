Calculation: EMA fast, mid, slow = closing-price EMAs with successively longer lookbacks
Calculation: ATR = average true range; +DI, -DI, ADX = directional movement indicators and ADX
Calculation: RVOL = volume / rolling average volume
Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA AND close > mid EMA AND (mid EMA - its value one slope lookback ago) / ATR >= minimum slope AND ADX >= minimum strength AND +DI >= -DI
Calculation: Bear trend = fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA AND close < mid EMA AND (mid EMA - its value one slope lookback ago) / ATR <= negative minimum slope AND ADX >= minimum strength AND -DI >= +DI
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish if Bull trend; bearish if Bear trend; otherwise bullish if fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND normalized mid-EMA slope > -minimum slope AND +DI >= 0.85 * -DI; bearish if fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND normalized mid-EMA slope < minimum slope AND -DI >= 0.85 * +DI; otherwise neutral
Calculation: Pullback pocket = start when no pocket is active, trend direction is bullish or bearish, price touches within an ATR tolerance of the fast or mid EMA, close remains on the trend side of the slow EMA, RVOL is below its start limit, and the close is no higher than max(open, prior close) for bullish or no lower than min(open, prior close) for bearish. Anchor range to the greater of current high and prior-lookback high, and the lesser of current low and prior-lookback low. While active, accumulate bars, highest high, lowest low, and RVOL. Reset on trigger or when depth exceeds its hard limit, close crosses the ATR-buffered invalidation boundary beyond the pocket extreme, close crosses the slow EMA against the pocket direction, or age exceeds its maximum.
Calculation: Pocket depth = for bullish, (anchor high - pocket low) / anchor range * 100; for bearish, (pocket high - anchor low) / anchor range * 100
Calculation: Dry-up score = 100 when average pocket RVOL <= preferred dry-up level; 0 when >= hard RVOL level; otherwise linear interpolation between them
Calculation: Depth score = 100 when pocket depth <= preferred depth; 0 when >= hard depth; otherwise linear interpolation between them
Calculation: Duration score = 55 * pocket bars / minimum bars when bars < minimum; 100 when bars <= maximum; otherwise 0
Calculation: Quality score = 0.20 * trend score + 0.28 * dry-up score + 0.24 * depth score + 0.12 * duration score + 0.16 * trigger-pressure score; trend score combines normalized slope score (0.42), ADX score (0.33), and 25 for a confirmed trend or 12 otherwise; trigger-pressure score = clamp(100 - 45 * ATR-normalized distance to trigger, 0, 100)
Calculation: Trigger level = bullish pocket high + ATR buffer, or bearish pocket low - ATR buffer
Calculation: Invalidation boundary = bullish pocket low - ATR buffer, or bearish pocket high + ATR buffer
Calculation: Watch = active pocket AND minimum pocket bars reached AND dry-up score >= minimum AND depth <= hard depth AND quality >= minimum watch quality AND no trigger or invalidation on this bar AND no prior watch for this pocket; apply the same-side watch cooldown
Calculation: Trigger = active pocket AND minimum pocket bars reached AND dry-up score >= minimum AND depth <= trigger depth AND quality >= minimum trigger quality AND current RVOL >= average pocket RVOL * recovery ratio AND close > trigger level AND close > open for bullish, or close < trigger level AND close < open for bearish; require bar close if close confirmation is enabled
Long: bullish Watch OR bullish Trigger
Short: bearish Watch OR bearish Trigger
