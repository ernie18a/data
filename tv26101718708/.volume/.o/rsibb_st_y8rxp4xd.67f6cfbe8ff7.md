Calculation: RSI Bollinger center = 0.6 × EMA(RSI × volume, lookback) / EMA(volume, lookback) + 0.4 × 50
Calculation: RSI upper band = RSI Bollinger center + standard deviation of RSI over the same lookback × band multiplier
Calculation: RSI lower band = RSI Bollinger center − standard deviation of RSI over the same lookback × band multiplier
Long: RSI crosses above the RSI lower band
Short: RSI crosses below the RSI upper band OR (position side is long AND close < entry price × stop-loss rate)
