Calculation: HTF ATR regime = percentile rank of HTF ATR among a longer lookback of HTF bars; classify Dead/Extreme with hysteresis, retaining the prior state until the corresponding exit threshold is crossed. Use the last completed HTF bar when confirmed HTF data is enabled; otherwise use the HTF bar still forming.
Calculation: HTF slope state = difference between current and prior points of the HTF close linear regression, divided by HTF close; compare its absolute value with a threshold scaled by HTF ATR divided by HTF close. Bullish is above the positive threshold; bearish is below the negative threshold; otherwise flat. Use the same HTF bar selection as the HTF ATR regime.
Calculation: Kaufman efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback. Blend chart-timeframe and HTF ratios, weighting the HTF ratio more heavily; valid means the blend meets its minimum threshold. The HTF ratio uses the last completed HTF bar when confirmed HTF data is enabled; otherwise use the HTF bar still forming.
Calculation: Expanding volume = volume percentile rank over a lookback shorter than the ATR-regime lookback, above its upper threshold with hysteresis; retain the prior expanding state until its exit threshold is crossed.
Long: Non-intraday horizon AND bullish HTF slope AND (valid blended efficiency ratio OR HTF ATR regime is not Extreme)
Long: Intraday horizon AND Extreme HTF ATR regime AND bullish HTF slope AND valid blended efficiency ratio AND expanding volume
Short: Non-intraday horizon AND bearish HTF slope AND (valid blended efficiency ratio OR HTF ATR regime is not Extreme)
Short: Intraday horizon AND Extreme HTF ATR regime AND bearish HTF slope AND valid blended efficiency ratio AND expanding volume
