Calculation: Session profile = build from the selected session’s bars through the current bar, including its forming bar; divide the session high–low range into equal price bins, assign each bar’s volume to the bin containing its close, take the highest-volume bin as POC, and expand the value area from POC by adding the larger adjacent-bin volume until the configured share of total volume is reached. Session high and low are the maximum high and minimum low in those bars.
Calculation: Daily profile = apply the same volume-bin and value-area method to daily bars through the current, possibly forming daily bar.
Calculation: Edge empty-bin count = count consecutive zero-volume bins inward from the session profile’s true high or low, stopping at the first non-empty bin and only scanning the configured boundary window.
Long: Low touches or passes the current session low (within the configured tick tolerance) and closes above it OR the short-term moving average crosses above the long-term moving average OR close crosses above the value-area high of the mapped higher-timeframe profile (built through its possibly forming bar) OR close is above the current session value-area high or above the session POC plus the configured fraction of its value-area width, the daily profile does not indicate short, and the volatility gate is open OR, after a low-edge rejection with enough low-side empty bins whose signal close is below the previous completed selected-session close, a bar closes at or above that close plus the configured ATR entry buffer within the watch expiry.
Short: High touches or passes the current session high (within the configured tick tolerance) and closes below it OR the short-term moving average crosses below the long-term moving average OR close crosses below the value-area low of the mapped higher-timeframe profile (built through its possibly forming bar) OR close is below the current session value-area low or below the session POC minus the configured fraction of its value-area width, the daily profile does not indicate long, and the volatility gate is open OR, after a high-edge rejection with enough high-side empty bins whose signal close is above the previous completed selected-session close, a bar closes at or below that close minus the configured ATR entry buffer within the watch expiry.
