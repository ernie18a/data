Long: At least one long condition is enabled and every enabled condition is true: its selected series is greater than, less than, crosses above, or crosses below its selected comparison series or numeric level, as configured; datetime is within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled; position size <= 0
Short: At least one short condition is enabled and every enabled condition is true: its selected series is greater than, less than, crosses above, or crosses below its selected comparison series or numeric level, as configured; datetime is within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled; position size >= 0
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + target distance OR, when trailing is enabled, the activation distance from entry has been reached and low <= highest high since activation - trailing offset
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - target distance OR, when trailing is enabled, the activation distance from entry has been reached and high >= lowest low since activation + trailing offset
