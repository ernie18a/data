Calculation: Efficiency Ratio = absolute change in close over a shorter lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Efficiency baseline = EMA of Efficiency Ratio over a longer lookback
Calculation: Ulcer Index = square root of the moving average of squared percentage drawdowns of close from its highest close over the Ulcer lookback
Calculation: Ulcer baseline = EMA of Ulcer Index over a longer lookback
Calculation: Efficiency band width = ATR multiplied by a configurable band multiplier and an efficiency factor that decreases linearly as Efficiency Ratio rises
Long: More than the configured cooldown bars since either signal AND ((close crosses above VWAP AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ the selected-mode threshold AND Ulcer Index ≤ Ulcer baseline × the selected-mode factor) OR (low ≤ VWAP − Efficiency band width AND close > VWAP − Efficiency band width AND close > open AND Efficiency Ratio > Efficiency baseline AND Efficiency Ratio > prior bar's Efficiency Ratio AND Ulcer Index < Ulcer baseline AND Ulcer Index < prior bar's Ulcer Index))
Short: More than the configured cooldown bars since either signal AND ((close crosses below VWAP AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ the selected-mode threshold AND Ulcer Index > Ulcer baseline AND Ulcer Index > prior bar's Ulcer Index) OR (high ≥ VWAP + Efficiency band width AND close < VWAP + Efficiency band width AND close < open AND Efficiency Ratio > Efficiency baseline AND Efficiency Ratio > prior bar's Efficiency Ratio AND Ulcer Index > Ulcer baseline AND Ulcer Index > prior bar's Ulcer Index))
