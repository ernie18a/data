Calculation: Long stop = lowest low from the red candle through the final green signal candle; on a signal while already long, add a stop-adjustment fraction × (signal close − this stop)
Calculation: Long take-profit = signal close + reward-risk multiple × (signal close − this stop); on a signal while already long, subtract a target-adjustment fraction × (signal close − this stop)
Calculation: Short stop = highest high from the green candle through the final red signal candle; on a signal while already short, subtract a stop-adjustment fraction × (this stop − signal close)
Calculation: Short take-profit = signal close − reward-risk multiple × (this stop − signal close); on a signal while already short, add a target-adjustment fraction × (this stop − signal close)
Long: previous candle closes below its open AND current candle closes above its open AND current close > previous high AND current volume > previous volume OR candle two bars ago closes below its open AND prior candle closes above its open AND current candle closes above its open AND prior close < high two bars ago AND current close > high two bars ago AND current volume > volume two bars ago OR candle three bars ago closes below its open AND both following prior candles close above their opens AND current candle closes above its open AND both prior green closes < high three bars ago AND current close > high three bars ago AND current volume > volume three bars ago
Short: previous candle closes above its open AND current candle closes below its open AND current close < previous low AND current volume > previous volume OR candle two bars ago closes above its open AND prior candle closes below its open AND current candle closes below its open AND prior close > low two bars ago AND current close > low two bars ago AND current volume > volume two bars ago OR candle three bars ago closes above its open AND both following prior candles close below their opens AND current candle closes below its open AND both prior red closes > low three bars ago AND current close < low three bars ago AND current volume > volume three bars ago
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long take-profit
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short take-profit
