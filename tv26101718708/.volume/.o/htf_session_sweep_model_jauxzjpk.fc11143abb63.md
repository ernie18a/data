Calculation: Reference HTF high and low = high and low of the last completed selected higher-timeframe bar, fixed at the start of each enabled session
Calculation: Prior lookback high and low = highest high and lowest low of the selected number of preceding chart bars, excluding the current bar
Long: During an active enabled session, after a low sweep (low < reference HTF low, and, in close-back-inside mode, close > reference HTF low), close > prior lookback high; OR close > reference HTF high for the selected number of consecutive bars, with the displacement and optional volume filters passing; OR a close previously exceeded reference HTF low and the current close > reference HTF low (bear trap). Apply the configured one-entry-per-side-per-session limit to sweep and breakout entries; each trap fires at most once per session.
Short: During an active enabled session, after a high sweep (high > reference HTF high, and, in close-back-inside mode, close < reference HTF high), close < prior lookback low; OR close < reference HTF low for the selected number of consecutive bars, with the displacement and optional volume filters passing; OR a close previously exceeded reference HTF high and the current close < reference HTF high (bull trap). Apply the configured one-entry-per-side-per-session limit to sweep and breakout entries; each trap fires at most once per session.
