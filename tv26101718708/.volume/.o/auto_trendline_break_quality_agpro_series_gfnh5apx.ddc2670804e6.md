Calculation: Swing pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across N bars on each side; confirmed after the N following bars complete.
Calculation: Trendline = straight-line projection through the two most recent confirmed same-side pivots; use rising pivot lows for an upward line and falling pivot highs for a downward line. Major lines use a longer pivot lookback than Minor lines. A line is valid when absolute slope / ATR ≥ the minimum slope threshold.
Long: close > a valid downward line AND previous close ≤ that line's previous-bar value AND (close-only confirmation OR volume-confirmation with volume ≥ its configured fraction of the 20-bar average volume OR impulse-confirmation with distance between close and line ≥ its configured fraction of ATR).
Short: close < a valid upward line AND previous close ≥ that line's previous-bar value AND (close-only confirmation OR volume-confirmation with volume ≥ its configured fraction of the 20-bar average volume OR impulse-confirmation with distance between close and line ≥ its configured fraction of ATR).
