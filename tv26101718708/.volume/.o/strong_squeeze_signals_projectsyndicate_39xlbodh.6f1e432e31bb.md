Calculation: Momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the short-lookback close SMA and the midpoint between the short-lookback highest high and lowest low.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands are fully inside Keltner Channels; release = prior bar squeezed and current bar not squeezed; strict expansion, if enabled, requires current Bollinger Bands fully outside the Keltner Channels.
Calculation: Coil = consecutive squeezed-bar count, plus highest high, lowest low, and minimum ratio of Bollinger half-width to Keltner half-width during the coil.
Calculation: Setup strength = score based on coil duration and tightness, normalized absolute momentum, direction-adjusted momentum acceleration, volume relative to its 20-bar average, candle body/range, candle range/ATR, Bollinger width relative to its 20-bar average, higher-timeframe alignment, and RSI agreement.
Calculation: Risk = absolute distance from entry to the coil extreme buffered by ATR, bounded by an ATR-based minimum and maximum; stop is entry minus risk for long or plus risk for short; targets are entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk times configured R multiples.
Long: release after at least the configured minimum coil duration AND Momentum > 0 (or Momentum = 0 and prior Momentum >= 0) AND Setup strength >= configured minimum (and configured strong threshold when strong-only is enabled) AND higher-timeframe close > EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar when alignment filter is enabled AND cooldown satisfied AND no open position AND ATR available.
Short: release after at least the configured minimum coil duration AND Momentum < 0 (or Momentum = 0 and prior Momentum < 0) AND Setup strength >= configured minimum (and configured strong threshold when strong-only is enabled) AND higher-timeframe close < EMA of the last completed higher-timeframe bar when alignment filter is enabled AND cooldown satisfied AND no open position AND ATR available.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= third target
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= third target
