Calculation: Bullish structure bias = latest confirmed swing high > preceding confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing low > preceding confirmed swing low; bearish structure bias = both comparisons are <. Swing pivots use the configured swing length and are confirmed after the required right-side bars.
Calculation: Demand zone = a zone extending 0.1% above and below a confirmed swing low, merged with an existing zone when the pivot falls inside it; valid after at least two pivots are counted in the zone. Supply zone is defined the same way around confirmed swing highs.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = close crosses above the second-latest confirmed swing high; bearish break of structure = close crosses below the second-latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Support retest = after a bearish break of structure, high reaches within 0.1% below the broken swing low and close is below it; resistance retest = after a bullish break of structure, low reaches within 0.1% above the broken swing high and close is above it. Each retest condition clears after it occurs.
Calculation: Long confluence score = count of bullish structure bias, close in demand zone, bullish break of structure, support retest, close above daily VWAP, shorter-period EMA above longer-period EMA, and RSI above 50.
Calculation: Short confluence score = count of bearish structure bias, close in supply zone, bearish break of structure, resistance retest, close below daily VWAP, shorter-period EMA below longer-period EMA, and RSI below 50.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price minus ATR times the stop multiplier for a long, or plus ATR times the stop multiplier for a short. Full target = entry price plus three times the entry-to-stop distance for a long, or minus three times that distance for a short.
Long: Long confluence score >= minimum score AND no active trade or trade lock AND long signal state is inactive AND bars since prior long signal > cooldown; cooldown scales with swing length and is longer on short chart timeframes than on higher ones.
Short: Short confluence score >= minimum score AND no active trade or trade lock AND short signal state is inactive AND bars since prior short signal > cooldown; cooldown scales with swing length and is longer on short chart timeframes than on higher ones.
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop OR high >= full target
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop OR low <= full target
