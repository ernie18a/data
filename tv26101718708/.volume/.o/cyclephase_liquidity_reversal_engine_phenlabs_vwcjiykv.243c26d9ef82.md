Calculation: Confirmed swing low/high = a bar’s low/high is the lowest/highest across the pivot lookback bars on each side; use the latest such pivot only after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Cycle displacement = close minus EMA(close, cycle lookback); cycle z-score = (cycle displacement minus its SMA over the cycle lookback) divided by its standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback); body-to-range ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Long: On a confirmed bar, low < latest confirmed swing low and close > that swing low; the swing low has not already qualified a setup; and either (cycle z-score ≤ −stretch threshold AND cycle displacement > its prior-bar value) OR relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND body-to-range ratio ≤ maximum body-to-range ratio in Balanced mode, or both conditions in Strict mode; reject if the corresponding short sweep also qualifies or a long setup is already armed.
Long: A long setup is armed by a qualifying long sweep; before or on its confirmation-window expiry, on a later confirmed bar, close > the sweep bar’s high, ATR is available, and (EMA context filter is off OR close ≥ context EMA).
Short: On a confirmed bar, high > latest confirmed swing high and close < that swing high; the swing high has not already qualified a setup; and either (cycle z-score ≥ stretch threshold AND cycle displacement < its prior-bar value) OR relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND body-to-range ratio ≤ maximum body-to-range ratio in Balanced mode, or both conditions in Strict mode; reject if the corresponding long sweep also qualifies or a short setup is already armed.
Short: A short setup is armed by a qualifying short sweep; before or on its confirmation-window expiry, on a later confirmed bar, close < the sweep bar’s low, ATR is available, and (EMA context filter is off OR close ≤ context EMA).
