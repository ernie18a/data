Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close minus open; candle range = maximum of high minus low and one minimum price tick.
Calculation: Engulfing score = round(100 × weighted average of body expansion, close location, rejection wick, relative volume, volatility context, and regime alignment). Body expansion = clamp(current body / maximum(previous body, one tick) − 1, 0, 1). For bullish candidates, close location = clamp((close − low) / candle range, 0, 1) and rejection wick = clamp(2 × lower wick / candle range, 0, 1); for bearish candidates use (high − close) / candle range and 2 × upper wick / candle range. Relative volume contribution = clamp((volume / its longer-lookback SMA − 0.75) / 1.25, 0, 1), or 0.5 when volume or its positive SMA is unavailable. Volatility context uses the shorter-lookback ATR divided by its longer-lookback SMA: clamp((ratio − 0.60) / 0.90, 0, 1) when ratio ≤ 1.50; otherwise clamp(1 − (ratio − 1.50) / 1.50, 0, 1). If the ATR baseline is unavailable or zero, use ratio 1. Regime alignment is 1 when the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA for a bullish candidate, or below it for a bearish candidate; otherwise 0. Divide the sum of each component times its configurable weight by the sum of weights, then multiply by 100 before rounding.
Long: previous candle closes below its open AND current candle closes above its open AND previous body ≥ previous ATR × minimum-body parameter AND current body ≥ previous body × minimum-body-ratio parameter AND current open ≤ previous close AND current close ≥ previous open AND current close > previous high AND bullish engulfing score ≥ minimum-score parameter AND no qualifying signal occurred within the shared cooldown interval; evaluate at chart-bar close. For regime alignment, use the last completed bar of a context timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe.
Short: previous candle closes above its open AND current candle closes below its open AND previous body ≥ previous ATR × minimum-body parameter AND current body ≥ previous body × minimum-body-ratio parameter AND current open ≥ previous close AND current close ≤ previous open AND current close < previous low AND bearish engulfing score ≥ minimum-score parameter AND no qualifying signal occurred within the shared cooldown interval; evaluate at chart-bar close. For regime alignment, use the last completed bar of a context timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe.
