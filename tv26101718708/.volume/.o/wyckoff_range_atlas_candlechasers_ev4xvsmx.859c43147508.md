Calculation: Active range = manually supplied support/resistance activated when prior-bar ATR is available, or an automatically detected range using the preceding lookback bars: high/low extremes, width within configured prior-ATR bounds, directional efficiency within its maximum, and minimum distinct visits to each boundary. Adjacent boundary candles count as one visit; a new visit requires the configured number of bars outside that boundary zone. Freeze support, resistance, and prior-bar ATR at activation; track range age and expire after its configured maximum.
Calculation: Directional efficiency = absolute net close change across the preceding lookback bars divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes across those bars.
Calculation: Closing location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over the preceding baseline window, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Boundary buffer = frozen ATR × configured buffer fraction; maximum sweep depth = frozen ATR × configured sweep-depth fraction.
Long: Active range and (spring: after cooldown, low is below support minus buffer but no lower than support minus maximum sweep depth, high is no higher than resistance plus buffer, close is above support and below resistance, and closing location ≥ 0.6; OR spring test: within the configured window after a spring, low is above the sweep low and no higher than support plus half frozen ATR, close is above support and below resistance, closing location ≥ 0.55, spread ≤ frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≤ test maximum and current volume below sweep-bar volume); OR SOS: close > resistance + buffer, close > open, closing location ≥ 0.7, spread ≥ 0.8 frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≥ breakout minimum); OR LPS: after an upward qualified breakout and within its configured retest window, no prior retest, low is within half frozen ATR of resistance, close > resistance + buffer, closing location ≥ 0.55, spread ≤ frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≤ test maximum)). Volume-qualified events require positive current volume and a positive available baseline.
Short: Active range and (upthrust: after cooldown, high is above resistance plus buffer but no higher than resistance plus maximum sweep depth, low is no lower than support minus buffer, close is below resistance and above support, and closing location ≤ 0.4; OR upthrust test: within the configured window after an upthrust, high is below the sweep high and no lower than resistance minus half frozen ATR, close is below resistance and above support, closing location ≤ 0.45, spread ≤ frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≤ test maximum and current volume below sweep-bar volume); OR SOW: close < support − buffer, close < open, closing location ≤ 0.3, spread ≥ 0.8 frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≥ breakout minimum); OR LPSY: after a downward qualified breakout and within its configured retest window, no prior retest, high is within half frozen ATR of support, close < support − buffer, closing location ≤ 0.45, spread ≤ frozen ATR, and (price-only volume mode OR relative volume ≤ test maximum)). Volume-qualified events require positive current volume and a positive available baseline.
Exit: Long, after an upward qualified breakout, close < resistance − half frozen ATR.
Exit: Short, after a downward qualified breakout, close > support + half frozen ATR.
