Calculation: EVWMA = recursively calculate ((sum of volume over its lookback − current volume) × previous EVWMA + current volume × close) / sum of volume over its lookback; use close when no previous EVWMA exists (chart bars)
Calculation: Smoothed VWAP = EMA of session VWAP (chart bars)
Long: Smoothed VWAP crosses above EVWMA
Short: Smoothed VWAP crosses below EVWMA
