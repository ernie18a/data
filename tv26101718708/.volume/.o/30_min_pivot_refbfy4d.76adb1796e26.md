Calculation: Daily EMA(8), Daily EMA(21) = exponential averages of completed daily closes; use the shorter and longer lookbacks respectively.
Calculation: Daily ATR(14) = average true range of daily bars, including the current forming daily bar; ATR buffer = 0.75 × Daily ATR(14).
Calculation: Daily support test = the current bar's low is within the ATR buffer around either completed-daily EMA(8) or EMA(21), or its close is within the ATR buffer of the band between those EMAs; also true if price undercut the previous completed daily low and the current close reclaims it within 0.75 × ATR buffer.
Calculation: Relative strength ratio = current close divided by the benchmark's close on the same timeframe; benchmark is a configurable index.
Calculation: RS new high = relative strength ratio exceeds its prior lookback-window high.
Calculation: Price new high = current high exceeds its prior lookback-window high; use the same lookback as for RS new high.
Calculation: Daily swing high = highest high over the daily lookback, including the current forming daily bar.
Calculation: 52-week high = highest daily high over the daily lookback, including the current forming daily bar.
Long: RS new high AND (NOT Price new high OR Price new high)
Long: On a 30-minute bar during the trading session other than the closing bar: daily EMA(8) >= 0.99 × daily EMA(21) AND Daily support test AND close > open AND (previous bar close < open OR low <= chart EMA(8) AND close >= chart EMA(8) OR low <= chart EMA(21) AND close >= chart EMA(21) OR not the first session bar) AND (volume dry-up filter disabled OR current daily volume <= 1.05 × 20-day average daily volume when the current daily bar is red) AND (minimum reward/risk filter disabled OR nearest enabled overhead resistance is at least 2 × trigger risk above the trigger entry) AND close is not below any enabled resistance moving average AND setup limit not reached AND no active trade or pending order; place a buy stop at the trigger bar's high and initial stop at its low.
Long: While that order is pending, enter when a later bar's high >= entry price, unless the order has timed out, expired, opened at or below the stop, breached the stop before reaching entry, or opened above entry by more than the allowed gap-risk multiple of initial risk.
Exit: Long, high >= target 2, where target 2 is the greater of 2.5 × initial risk above entry and the nearest applicable higher level among daily swing high, 52-week high, or entry + 3.5 × initial risk; use swing high if above entry, otherwise 52-week high if above entry, otherwise the fallback.
Exit: Long, before target 1 is reached, low <= initial stop OR open <= initial stop OR close <= initial stop.
Exit: Long, after target 1 is reached, close < current stop OR open <= current stop; at target 1 (high >= entry + 2 × initial risk), take partial profit, move stop to entry, then on each later bar raise the stop to the selected chart or completed-daily EMA(8) when that EMA is above the current stop and entry; exit the runner on the stop condition.
Exit: Long, end of trading day if end-of-day expiration is enabled.
