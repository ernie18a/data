Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = on a close crossing above the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high with positive momentum z-score above its threshold, or crossing below the latest confirmed pivot low with negative momentum z-score below its negative threshold, respectively; take the first opposite-color candle in the prior-bar search and use its high and low as the block bounds. Pivot highs/lows require the same pivot lookback on both sides; momentum z-score = (close change − average close change) / standard deviation of close change.
Calculation: Monthly, weekly, daily EMA alignment = close above the EMA for bullish alignment and below it for bearish alignment, using the corresponding higher-timeframe bar (the current bar while it is forming); EMA lookbacks increase from monthly to weekly to daily.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after price reaches the break-even trigger above entry, initialize to entry and thereafter raise to the maximum of its prior value and close minus an ATR multiple; short trailing stop = after price reaches the break-even trigger below entry, initialize to entry and thereafter lower to the minimum of its prior value and close plus an ATR multiple. ATR is from chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Flat AND (an active, unmitigated, unused bullish order block has low <= its top and close > its bottom AND all enabled macro gates are bullish OR a stagnation-exited long remains eligible for re-entry and close > its order block top).
Short: Flat AND (an active, unmitigated, unused bearish order block has high >= its bottom and close < its top AND all enabled macro gates are bearish OR a stagnation-exited short remains eligible for re-entry and close < its order block bottom).
Exit: Long, (stagnation exit enabled AND elapsed bars since entry >= its threshold AND close is between the entry block bounds, inclusive) OR low <= max(entry price − hard-stop distance, long trailing stop if initialized) OR high >= entry price + target distance.
Exit: Short, (stagnation exit enabled AND elapsed bars since entry >= its threshold AND close is between the entry block bounds, inclusive) OR high >= min(entry price + hard-stop distance, short trailing stop if initialized) OR low <= entry price − target distance.
