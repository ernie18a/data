Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a lookback period
Calculation: Climax volume = current volume > average volume × (volume-spike multiplier + 0.5)
Calculation: Average price range = simple moving average of high − low over a shorter, fixed lookback
Calculation: Midpoint of range = (highest high − lowest low over the volume lookback period) / 2 + lowest low over that period
Calculation: Most recent SC low = low of the most recent bar meeting the Selling Climax condition
Calculation: Most recent SC or ST low = low of the most recent bar meeting either the Selling Climax or Secondary Test condition; Secondary Test requires low within 0.5% of the most recent SC low, volume below average volume, and close > open
Calculation: Selling Climax condition = climax volume AND close < open AND low ≤ lowest low over a shorter lookback AND close < previous close
Calculation: Spring condition = low < lowest low over a shorter lookback excluding the current bar AND close > open AND volume > average volume AND close > low × 1.01
Calculation: Sign of Strength condition = close > previous close AND volume > average volume × 1.3 AND close − open > average price range AND close > midpoint of range
Calculation: Last Point of Support condition = low > most recent SC or ST low AND close > open AND volume < average volume AND close > midpoint of range
Long: Selling Climax condition OR (close > open AND volume > average volume AND previous low = lowest low over the prior 30 bars AND close > previous close × 1.01) OR Spring condition OR Sign of Strength condition OR Last Point of Support condition
Short: (climax volume AND close > open AND high ≥ highest high over a shorter lookback) OR (high > highest high over a shorter lookback excluding the current bar AND close < open AND volume > average volume AND close < high × 0.99) OR (high < most recent UTAD or Secondary Test high AND close < open AND volume < average volume AND close < midpoint of range)
