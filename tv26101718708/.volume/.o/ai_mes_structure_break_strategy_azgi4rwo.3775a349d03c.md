Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot high = the most recent bar high greater than the highs of the specified number of bars on both sides; use it only after the bars on both sides are complete.
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot low = the most recent bar low less than the lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use it only after the bars on both sides are complete.
Calculation: Long stop = latest confirmed pivot low minus a buffer; short stop = latest confirmed pivot high plus a buffer.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus the distance from entry price to long stop multiplied by a reward-to-risk factor; short target = entry price minus the distance from short stop to entry price multiplied by that factor.
Long: Current close > latest confirmed pivot high AND previous close <= that pivot high AND current close > the EMA of higher-timeframe closes (use the latest completed higher-timeframe bar) AND RSI > its midpoint AND current volume > its longer-lookback average volume multiplied by a threshold AND time is within an enabled session window AND that window's trade limit has not been reached AND position is flat.
Short: Current close < latest confirmed pivot low AND previous close >= that pivot low AND current close < the EMA of higher-timeframe closes (use the latest completed higher-timeframe bar) AND RSI < its midpoint AND current volume > its longer-lookback average volume multiplied by a threshold AND time is within an enabled session window AND that window's trade limit has not been reached AND position is flat.
Exit: long, bar low <= long stop OR bar high >= long target OR current time is outside all enabled session windows and the previous bar was inside one.
Exit: short, bar high >= short stop OR bar low <= short target OR current time is outside all enabled session windows and the previous bar was inside one.
