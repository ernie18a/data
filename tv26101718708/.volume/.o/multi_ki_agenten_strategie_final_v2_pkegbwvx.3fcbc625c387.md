Calculation: Long-agent count = number of true conditions: short-term EMA(close) > medium-term EMA(close) > long-term EMA(close); RSI > upper threshold; close > previous bar close AND volume > volume SMA × spike factor; close > highest high over the preceding lookback, using completed bars; close <= lowest low over the current lookback, including the current bar, + ATR × zone buffer. EMA lookbacks are ordered short < medium < long; the structure lookback is longer than the zone lookback.
Calculation: Short-agent count = number of true conditions: short-term EMA(close) < medium-term EMA(close) < long-term EMA(close); RSI < lower threshold; close < previous bar close AND volume > volume SMA × spike factor; close < lowest low over the preceding lookback, using completed bars; close >= highest high over the current lookback, including the current bar, − ATR × zone buffer. EMA lookbacks are ordered short < medium < long; the structure lookback is longer than the zone lookback.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR / SMA(ATR); the SMA lookback is longer than the ATR lookback.
Long: short-term EMA(close) > medium-term EMA(close) AND medium-term EMA(close) > long-term EMA(close) AND volatility ratio < risk threshold AND long-agent count >= minimum agent count AND short-agent count = 0
Short: short-term EMA(close) < medium-term EMA(close) AND medium-term EMA(close) < long-term EMA(close) AND volatility ratio < risk threshold AND short-agent count >= minimum agent count AND long-agent count = 0
Exit: Long, close <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple OR close >= entry price + ATR at entry × target multiple
Exit: Short, close >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple OR close <= entry price − ATR at entry × target multiple
