Calculation: Volume delta = volume if close − low > high − close; −volume if close − low < high − close; otherwise volume if close > open, −volume if close < open, and 0 if close = open
Calculation: Smoothed volume delta = EMA of volume delta
Calculation: Signal line = SMA of smoothed volume delta
Long: Smoothed volume delta crosses above signal line AND smoothed volume delta < 0
Short: Smoothed volume delta crosses below signal line AND smoothed volume delta > 0
