Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over the core lookback; use 1 if the average is missing or nonpositive
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × tanh(1.6 × pressure blend), where pressure blend = 0.28 × tanh(impulse × sensitivity) + 0.24 × tanh(slope × sensitivity) + 0.18 × tanh(momentum × sensitivity) + 0.18 × tanh(range position × sensitivity) + 0.12 × tanh(candle body pressure × sensitivity) + 0.10 × (clamp((relative volume − 0.70) / 2, 0, 1) − 0.50); impulse = (close − close core-lookback bars ago) / (ATR fast-lookback × sqrt(core lookback)); slope = (fast EMA of close − slow EMA of close) / ATR fast-lookback; momentum = rate of change of close over fast lookback / standard deviation of that rate of change over core lookback; range position = clamp((close − midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over core lookback) / half that range, −1, 1); candle body pressure = (close − open) / (high − low); each division denominator is at least the instrument's minimum price increment
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of pressure score over a shorter smoothing lookback
Calculation: Consensus = clamp(1 − (|impulse component − slope component| + |slope component − momentum component| + |momentum component − range-position component| + |range-position component − candle-body component|) / 8, 0, 1), where each component is its corresponding normalized tanh value
