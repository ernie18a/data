Calculation: Custom range-normalized RSI = Let range be the highest source value minus the lowest source value over the RSI lookback, and change be the current source minus the prior source. Momentum is range if the highest value increased, otherwise negative range if the lowest value decreased, otherwise change. Smooth momentum multiplied by volume (or 1 if volume weighting is off) with Wilder’s moving average; divide by the Wilder’s moving average of the absolute weighted momentum, then scale by 50 and add 50. The micro signal uses this RSI on the chart timeframe or the selected higher timeframe; higher-timeframe RSI uses the last completed bar.
Calculation: Macro moving averages = Four independently configured moving averages of close on the macro timeframe, ordered from shorter to longer lookback. Macro values use the still-forming macro bar, or the last completed macro bar when wait-for-close is enabled.
Long: The shorter-to-longer macro moving averages are strictly descending and macro close is above the shortest moving average, AND (the macro moving averages have just become strictly descending OR the micro RSI has previously been at or below the oversold threshold and crosses above the overbought threshold OR it crossed below the overbought threshold while above the midline and subsequently crosses above the overbought threshold OR, after previously being at or above the overbought threshold, it crossed below the midline and subsequently crosses above the midline OR a regular bullish divergence occurs (price pivot low lower than the prior qualifying pivot low and RSI pivot low higher than the prior qualifying pivot low) OR a hidden bullish divergence occurs (price pivot low higher than the prior qualifying pivot low and RSI pivot low lower than the prior qualifying pivot low)).
Short: The shorter-to-longer macro moving averages are strictly ascending and macro close is below the shortest moving average, AND (the macro moving averages have just become strictly ascending OR the micro RSI has previously been at or above the overbought threshold and crosses below the oversold threshold OR it crossed above the oversold threshold while below the midline and subsequently crosses below the oversold threshold OR, after previously being at or below the oversold threshold, it crossed above the midline and subsequently crosses below the midline OR a regular bearish divergence occurs (price pivot high higher than the prior qualifying pivot high and RSI pivot high lower than the prior qualifying pivot high) OR a hidden bearish divergence occurs (price pivot high lower than the prior qualifying pivot high and RSI pivot high higher than the prior qualifying pivot high)).
