Calculation: Observed volume score = 0.09 × percentile rank of volume − 4.5; when the chart timeframe is below the analysis timeframe, rank the rolling sum of volume over approximately one analysis-timeframe interval using a lookback scaled by the bar ratio; otherwise rank current chart-timeframe volume over the lookback. Use the current, possibly forming, bar/window.
Calculation: Observed delta score = 0.045 × percentile rank of the absolute delta total × its sign; each bar’s delta is (close − open) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume. When the chart timeframe is below the analysis timeframe, sum delta over the rolling bar-ratio window and rank its absolute total over the lookback; otherwise sum delta from the lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, then rank the absolute total over the lookback. Use the current, possibly forming, bar/window.
Long: Observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND (observed delta score > dominance threshold OR (observed delta score > 0 AND (when chart timeframe is below analysis timeframe, open of the bar one bar-ratio ago ≥ current close; otherwise, current open ≥ current close)))
Short: Observed volume score > high-volume threshold AND (observed delta score < −dominance threshold OR (observed delta score < 0 AND (when chart timeframe is below analysis timeframe, open of the bar one bar-ratio ago < current close; otherwise, current open < current close)))
