Calculation: Overnight settlement = close of the last RTH bar; overnight bars = bars in the overnight session before the next RTH open.
Calculation: Initial Balance = high and low of the first RTH hour; while it is forming, use the range accumulated so far.
Calculation: Day range = highest high minus lowest low since RTH open.
Calculation: Day VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the trading day opened.
Calculation: One-timeframing up/down = consecutive bars with higher lows / lower highs; compare the current bar's low / high with the prior bar's.
Long: Every overnight bar's low is at or above the prior RTH settlement OR (during RTH, day range extends above the Initial Balance, close is above 75% of the day range, Day VWAP is above the Initial Balance midpoint, and (one-timeframing up persists for at least five bars or price breaks the prior RTH high or breaks the overnight high while closing above the prior RTH value-area high), with day range greater than 1.5 times the Initial Balance range) OR (during RTH, price breaks both Initial Balance extremes and closes in the top 35% of the day range) OR (during RTH, price breaks only the Initial Balance high and breaks the prior RTH high or breaks the overnight high while closing above the prior RTH value-area high or day range exceeds 1.2 times the Initial Balance range or close is above the prior RTH value-area high) OR (the close of the late RTH spike window is above the pre-window RTH high).
Short: Every overnight bar's high is at or below the prior RTH settlement OR (during RTH, day range extends below the Initial Balance, close is below 25% of the day range, Day VWAP is below the Initial Balance midpoint, and (one-timeframing down persists for at least five bars or price breaks the prior RTH low or breaks the overnight low while closing below the prior RTH value-area low), with day range greater than 1.5 times the Initial Balance range) OR (during RTH, price breaks both Initial Balance extremes and closes in the bottom 35% of the day range) OR (during RTH, price breaks only the Initial Balance low and breaks the prior RTH low or breaks the overnight low while closing below the prior RTH value-area low or day range exceeds 1.2 times the Initial Balance range or close is below the prior RTH value-area low) OR (the close of the late RTH spike window is below the pre-window RTH low).
