Calculation: Footprint row buy/sell volume = Divide the current chart bar’s low-to-high range into equal price rows. Assign each lower-timeframe intrabar’s volume to the row containing its high-low midpoint. Classify as buy when its close is above the preceding intrabar close, sell when below, and split evenly when equal; for the first intrabar, compare its close with its open and split evenly when equal. If there are no intrabars or no classified volume, estimate buy volume as chart-bar volume × (close − low) / (high − low), using 0.5 when the range is zero; sell volume is the remainder.
Calculation: Bar delta = Total buy volume − total sell volume for the current chart bar.
Calculation: Diagonal buy/sell imbalance rows = A row qualifies for the optional minimum row-volume filter when its buy plus sell volume meets that minimum. A buy row qualifies when its buy volume is at least the configurable ratio times the sell volume in the row below; if that sell volume is zero, it qualifies when its buy volume is positive and its own sell volume is zero. A sell row qualifies when its sell volume is at least the ratio times the buy volume in the row above; if that buy volume is zero, it qualifies when its sell volume is positive and its own buy volume is zero. If both qualify, count only the buy row when its buy volume is at least its sell volume; otherwise count only the sell row. Rows at the profile boundary use zero for the missing adjacent row.
Calculation: POC = Center price of the current chart bar’s price row with the greatest total buy-plus-sell volume.
Long: Bar delta > 0 OR number of buy imbalance rows in the current chart bar ≥ configurable burst threshold OR at least the configurable minimum number of consecutive buy imbalance rows in the current chart bar OR bar delta changes from negative to positive after at least the configurable minimum run length of consecutive negative-delta chart bars OR current POC − previous chart bar’s POC ≥ configurable shift threshold in ticks.
Short: Bar delta < 0 OR number of sell imbalance rows in the current chart bar ≥ configurable burst threshold OR at least the configurable minimum number of consecutive sell imbalance rows in the current chart bar OR bar delta changes from positive to negative after at least the configurable minimum run length of consecutive positive-delta chart bars OR previous chart bar’s POC − current POC ≥ configurable shift threshold in ticks.
