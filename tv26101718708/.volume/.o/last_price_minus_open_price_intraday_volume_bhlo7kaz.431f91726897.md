Calculation: Price-change volume = (current close minus previous completed daily bar’s close, rounded to the minimum tick) × current bar volume
Calculation: Intraday average price-change volume = simple moving average of price-change volume over the number of bars since the previous completed daily bar’s close changed
Long: Price-change volume crosses above intraday average price-change volume AND volume crosses above its simple moving average
Short: Price-change volume crosses below intraday average price-change volume AND volume crosses below its simple moving average
