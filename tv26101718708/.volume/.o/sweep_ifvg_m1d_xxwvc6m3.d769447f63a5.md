Calculation: Liquidity swing = a confirmed pivot high/low whose price is also the highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback ending at the pivot; confirm only after the configured bars to its right complete.
Calculation: Candidate zone = a same-direction three-bar wick gap meeting the configured minimum size, with either edge extended to an adjoining body gap when absorption is enabled; or a three-bar suspension block of same-direction candles, each opening beyond the prior close, with both joins contiguous in time and the suspended span meeting the minimum size. Use the block span when both qualify.
Calculation: Break-even level = the lowest low for a bullish candidate or highest high for a bearish candidate from its qualifying sweep through the candidate bar.
Calculation: Stop level = configured choice of the far edge of the inverted zone, the body edge of the candle with the most extreme wick, or that wick extreme, measured from the candidate bar through the inversion bar.
Calculation: Target level = nearest untaken swing pool beyond entry from aggregated higher-timeframe bars, using confirmed three-bar pivots; if configured to use an R target when no pool exists, use the furthest configured risk multiple from entry to stop. A pool is untaken until price trades through it.
Long: confirmed bar wicks below the latest confirmed liquidity swing low and closes above it OR an active sellside sweep is followed within its configured validity window by a qualifying bullish candidate zone OR a bearish candidate closes above its zone top within the inversion grace window and, when required, its close is below the break-even level.
Short: confirmed bar wicks above the latest confirmed liquidity swing high and closes below it OR an active buyside sweep is followed within its configured validity window by a qualifying bearish candidate zone OR a bullish candidate closes below its zone bottom within the inversion grace window and, when required, its close is above the break-even level.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, a confirmed bar's low is at or below the stop level OR its high reaches the target level (trades through a pool; touches an R target).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, a confirmed bar's high is at or above the stop level OR its low reaches the target level (trades through a pool; touches an R target). If stop and target are both reached on the same bar, exit at the stop.
