Calculation: Wave direction = Start undetermined; track the highest selected up price and lowest selected down price. Set direction up when the up price reaches the lowest tracked price × (1 + reversal percentage); set direction down when the down price reaches the highest tracked price × (1 − reversal percentage), or ÷ (1 + reversal percentage) in symmetric mode. In an up wave, update the swing high and switch down when the down price reaches the swing high × (1 − reversal percentage), or ÷ (1 + reversal percentage) in symmetric mode. In a down wave, update the swing low and switch up when the up price reaches the swing low × (1 + reversal percentage). Selected up/down prices are high/low when enabled, otherwise close. Use the current bar’s prices; the reversal percentage and price-selection and symmetry settings are inputs.
Long: Wave direction = up
Short: Wave direction = down
