Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Explosive bar = (high − low) > ATR × move multiplier AND |close − open| > (high − low) × minimum body fraction [AND volume > moving average of volume × volume multiplier when volume confirmation is enabled]
Calculation: Demand zone = for a bullish explosive bar, zone bottom is the lowest low of the preceding base candles and zone top is the greater of their highest high and the explosive bar’s open; base candles have |close − open| < ATR × 0.5 and are not explosive
Calculation: Supply zone = for a bearish explosive bar, zone top is the highest high of the preceding base candles and zone bottom is the lesser of their lowest low and the explosive bar’s open; base candles have |close − open| < ATR × 0.5 and are not explosive
Calculation: Zone grade = score from move strength (min((explosive-bar range / its ATR) × 18, 30)), volume strength (min((explosive-bar volume / its volume average) × 13, 20), or 10 when volume confirmation is disabled), +25 when session VWAP is within the zone expanded by ATR × tolerance, +20 when the zone overlaps an active higher-timeframe zone, and +5; grades are A+ at 80+, A at 65+, B at 50+, otherwise C
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR on the signal bar
Long: A confirmed bar has an active demand zone with price tapping it (low ≤ zone top), close > zone top, close > open, lower wick ≥ candle range × minimum wick fraction, and zone grade ≥ minimum grade; the zone is unused when one-signal-per-zone is enabled, fresh when fresh-only is enabled, cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is in the configured session when session filtering is enabled; OR the preceding bar’s low < zone bottom AND its close > zone bottom AND (zone bottom − preceding low) / preceding close ≥ minimum sweep depth AND current close > current open, subject to enabled signal filters
Short: A confirmed bar has an active supply zone with price tapping it (high ≥ zone bottom), close < zone bottom, close < open, upper wick ≥ candle range × minimum wick fraction, and zone grade ≥ minimum grade; the zone is unused when one-signal-per-zone is enabled, fresh when fresh-only is enabled, cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is in the configured session when session filtering is enabled; OR the preceding bar’s high > zone top AND its close < zone top AND (preceding high − zone top) / preceding close ≥ minimum sweep depth AND current close < current open, subject to enabled signal filters
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × target multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × target multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × stop multiplier
