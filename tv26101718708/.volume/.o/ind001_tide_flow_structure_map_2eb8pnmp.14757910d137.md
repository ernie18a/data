Calculation: Main trend EMA = EMA of chart-timeframe close using a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: Upper structure = EMA of the chart-timeframe rolling highest high, using a structure lookback and a smoothing lookback
Calculation: Lower structure = EMA of the chart-timeframe rolling lowest low, using a structure lookback and a smoothing lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close compared with its longer-lookback EMA, using the latest available higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: ATR percent = chart-timeframe ATR divided by close, multiplied by 100
Long: (close > Main trend EMA AND fast EMA > Main trend EMA AND Main trend EMA > its prior value AND Higher-timeframe trend is bullish AND ADX >= its threshold AND ATR percent >= its threshold AND RSI <= its maximum AND fast EMA one-bar percentage slope >= negative deadzone AND (close > prior Upper structure OR (low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA) OR close crosses above fast EMA) AND bar is confirmed AND no long signal occurred within the cooldown) OR (close > Main trend EMA AND fast EMA > Main trend EMA AND Main trend EMA > its prior value AND Higher-timeframe trend is bullish AND ADX >= its threshold AND ATR percent >= its threshold AND close > fast EMA AND close > Main trend EMA) OR (close crosses above Upper structure AND all of the preceding trend, higher-timeframe, ADX, and ATR conditions hold) OR (at least the configured minimum number of closes in the configured recent chart-bar lookback were below Main trend EMA AND close > Main trend EMA) OR (at least the configured minimum number of closes in the configured recent chart-bar lookback were below Main trend EMA AND close > fast EMA) OR (at least the configured minimum number of closes in the configured recent chart-bar lookback were below Main trend EMA AND close > Upper structure)
Exit: long, on two consecutive confirmed chart bars close is below the greater of the ratcheting ATR stop (highest since entry of close minus the configured ATR multiple) and Lower structure
