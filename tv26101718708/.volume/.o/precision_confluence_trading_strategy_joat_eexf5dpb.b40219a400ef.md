Calculation: Daily and weekly pivots = (high + low + close) / 3, each from the last completed daily or weekly bar; daily CPR lower/upper bands = (high + low) / 2 and 2 × pivot − lower band, from the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Narrow daily CPR = (daily upper band − daily lower band) / daily pivot is below its threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CPR scores = 10 if close is above/below both daily and weekly pivots, plus 3 if close is above the daily upper band/below the daily lower band, plus 2 if daily CPR is narrow.
Calculation: HMA ribbon = Hull moving averages of close at five successively longer lookbacks; bullish/bearish full alignment means each is above/below the next. Partial bullish/bearish alignment means close is above/below the middle HMA and it is rising/falling. EMA cloud is bullish/bearish when the shorter EMA is above/below the longer EMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish HMA scores = 5 for partial alignment, plus 7 for full alignment, plus 3 for bullish/bearish EMA cloud.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the normalized difference between hlc3 and its EMA, with normalization by 0.015 times the EMA of absolute deviation; its signal is the short SMA of WaveTrend. A cross is bullish/bearish when WaveTrend crosses above/below its signal; oversold/overbought means WaveTrend is below/above its respective threshold. Bullish/bearish momentum means the WaveTrend-signal difference is positive/negative and increasing in magnitude.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish WaveTrend score = 8 for a cross above/below the signal while oversold/overbought, otherwise 5 for that cross, plus 5 for bullish/bearish WaveTrend divergence, plus 2 for bullish/bearish momentum.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = price makes a lower low/higher high at a confirmed pivot while RSI and/or WaveTrend makes a higher low/lower high, respectively; a pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks, and is compared with the preceding pivot of the same type. Strong divergence means both RSI and WaveTrend diverge in that direction.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence score = 5 for bullish/bearish RSI divergence plus 5 if both RSI and WaveTrend have divergence in that direction.
Calculation: ADX scores = 10 when ADX exceeds its strength threshold and +DI/−DI indicates the direction; otherwise 5 when +DI/−DI indicates that direction, else 0.
Calculation: High volume = volume exceeds its moving average by a multiplier; climax volume = volume exceeds that average by a larger multiplier. Bullish/bearish volume requires an up/down candle and high volume. Volume delta = cumulative up-candle volume minus cumulative down-candle volume; rising/falling delta means it is above/below its moving average.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish volume scores = 7 for bullish/bearish volume with rising/falling delta, otherwise 5 for bullish/bearish volume, plus 3 for climax volume on an up/down candle.
Calculation: Significant bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier, the intervening close confirms the gap, and gap size exceeds its threshold. Bullish/bearish order block = two preceding down/up candles followed by an up/down candle with high volume. Bullish/bearish breaker = close crosses above the last bearish order block's low / below the last bullish order block's high. Volume-confirmed low/high sweep = price pierces the lowest/highest level of the preceding lookback bars, closes back above/below it, and has high volume. Bullish/bearish displacement = candle body exceeds a multiple of ATR and has climax volume, with close above/below open.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish SMC score = 2 for significant bullish/bearish gap, plus 2 for bullish/bearish order block, plus 1 for bullish/bearish breaker, plus 2 for volume-confirmed low/high sweep, plus 3 for bullish/bearish displacement.
Calculation: Each higher-timeframe direction is bullish when its close is above its HMA and bearish otherwise; strength means its ADX exceeds its threshold. Use 15-minute, 1-hour, and 4-hour bars as returned by aggregation, including a bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish MTF score = 15 when all three higher-timeframe directions agree and all are strong, else 10 when all directions agree, else 5 when the 1-hour direction agrees, else 0.
Calculation: Key-MA bullish/bearish score = 3 when close is above/below the 50-lookback SMA, plus 4 when above/below the 200-lookback SMA, plus 3 when the shorter SMA is above/below the longer SMA.
Calculation: Bull confluence score = enabled component scores for CPR, HMA, WaveTrend, divergence, volume, and MTF, plus ADX and key-MA scores. Bear confluence score is the corresponding bearish sum.
Calculation: Session filter = true when disabled; when enabled, the bar is within either configured London or New York session.
Calculation: Dynamic long stop = the lower of close minus ATR times a signal-dependent multiplier, or the lowest low over the recent swing lookback minus 0.2 ATR. Dynamic short stop = the higher of close plus ATR times that multiplier, or the highest high over the recent swing lookback plus 0.2 ATR. The multiplier is lower for ultra signals, baseline for strong signals, and higher otherwise.
Calculation: Initial long target = close + (close − dynamic long stop) × reward-to-risk multiplier. Initial short target = close − (dynamic short stop − close) × reward-to-risk multiplier.
Long: (bull confluence score ≥ 70 AND bear confluence score < 40 OR bull confluence score ≥ 55 AND bull confluence score > bear confluence score + 15) AND bull confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND session filter.
Short: (bear confluence score ≥ 70 AND bull confluence score < 40 OR bear confluence score ≥ 55 AND bear confluence score > bull confluence score + 15) AND bear confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND session filter.
Long: bull confluence score ≥ 85 AND bear confluence score < 25 AND (WaveTrend crosses above its signal OR bullish RSI divergence OR bullish displacement).
Short: bear confluence score ≥ 85 AND bull confluence score < 25 AND (WaveTrend crosses below its signal OR bearish RSI divergence OR bearish displacement).
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ active target. Initially use the dynamic long stop and initial long target; once close ≥ entry price + (entry price − dynamic long stop) × breakeven trigger, use entry price + 0.1 × (entry price − dynamic long stop) as stop and entry price + (entry price − dynamic long stop) × reward-to-risk multiplier as target.
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ active target. Initially use the dynamic short stop and initial short target; once close ≤ entry price − (dynamic short stop − entry price) × breakeven trigger, use entry price − 0.1 × (dynamic short stop − entry price) as stop and entry price − (dynamic short stop − entry price) × reward-to-risk multiplier as target.
