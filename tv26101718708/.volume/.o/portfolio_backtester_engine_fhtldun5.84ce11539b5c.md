Long: crossover(RSI(close, lookback), oversold level) AND (when enabled: MACD line > MACD signal AND close > open AND volume > previous volume AND SMA(close, lookback) is rising for its lookback AND close - long entry stop < close × maximum stop risk percent AND close < previous close × (1 + maximum close-change percent) AND close > previous close × (1 + minimum close-change percent) AND RSI(close, lookback) <= overbought level AND close > selected moving average)
Short: crossunder(RSI(close, lookback), overbought level) AND (when enabled: MACD line < MACD signal AND close < open AND volume > previous volume AND SMA(close, lookback) is falling for its lookback AND short entry stop - close < close × maximum stop risk percent AND close > previous close × (1 - maximum close-change percent) AND close < previous close × (1 - minimum close-change percent) AND RSI(close, lookback) >= oversold level AND close < selected moving average)
Exit: long, low <= applicable stop OR an opposite short signal OR (when enabled) smoothed rate of change was rising for its lookback on the previous bar and is not rising for its lookback now OR RSI(close, lookback) crosses below the long exit level OR close > entry price + entry stop gap × target multiple OR close > entry price × (1 + target percent)
Exit: short, high >= applicable stop OR an opposite long signal OR (when enabled) smoothed rate of change was falling for its lookback on the previous bar and is not falling for its lookback now OR RSI(close, lookback) crosses above the short exit level OR close < entry price - entry stop gap × target multiple OR close < entry price × (1 - target percent)
Calculation: MACD line = EMA(close, shorter lookback) - EMA(close, longer lookback)
Calculation: MACD signal = EMA(MACD line, signal lookback)
Calculation: Smoothed rate of change = EMA(100 × ln(selected source / selected source from ROC lookback bars ago), smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Long entry stop = lower of current open and close minus (ATR(current chart bars, lookback) × ATR multiple), or lower of current open and close × (1 - stop percent), according to selected entry-stop method
Calculation: Short entry stop = higher of current open and close plus (ATR(current chart bars, lookback) × ATR multiple), or higher of current open and close × (1 + stop percent), according to selected entry-stop method
Calculation: Applicable stop = entry stop until the selected in-trade stop is enabled and its kick-in condition is met; thereafter the tighter of the previous applicable stop and the selected stop. Selected in-trade stop is one of: favorable extreme since entry minus/plus entry stop gap × multiple; favorable extreme since entry × (1 minus/plus stop percent); favorable extreme since entry minus/plus fixed price amount; entry price; mean of the prior two bars' lower/higher open-close values minus/plus current ATR × multiple; or prior bar's low/high. A long stop may only rise and a short stop may only fall. The kick-in condition is selected from: candidate stop beyond entry stop; candidate stop beyond entry price plus/minus entry stop gap × multiple; candidate stop beyond entry price × (1 plus/minus percent); candidate stop beyond entry price plus/minus fixed amount; or favorable extreme beyond entry price × (1 plus/minus percent).
