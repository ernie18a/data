Calculation: Momentum = linear regression of ((close − average((highest high + lowest low)/2, SMA((high + low)/2))) / channel true range × 100), using the same lookback for the high/low range and regression; channel true range is the maximum of channel range and its gaps from the previous close.
Calculation: Signal = simple moving average of Momentum over a shorter lookback than Momentum’s.
Calculation: Flux = RMA(((RMA(max(change(high), 0)) − RMA(max(−change(low), 0))) / (RMA(max(change(high), 0)) + RMA(max(−change(low), 0)))), using the same lookback for the inner RMAs, then RMA the ratio over half that lookback and multiply by 100; optionally use Heiken Ashi high/low instead of raw high/low.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume.
Long: Momentum crosses above Signal OR Momentum crosses above zero OR Flux crosses above zero OR Momentum > 0 AND Flux > 0 OR Momentum > 0 AND Flux ≤ 0 OR (Momentum crosses above Signal AND Momentum < −threshold AND Flux < 0 AND (volume confirmation is disabled, volume data is unavailable, or volume > Volume average)) OR regular bullish divergence (at a confirmed Momentum cross above Signal with Momentum below the negative sensitivity: current low < previous regular bullish anchor low AND current Signal > anchor Signal) OR hidden bullish divergence (at a confirmed Momentum cross above Signal: current low > previous hidden bullish anchor low AND current Signal < anchor Signal) OR squeeze release with Momentum change over the predictor lookback > slope threshold OR all four configured, valid higher timeframes have Momentum > 0.
Short: Momentum crosses below Signal OR Momentum crosses below zero OR Flux crosses below zero OR Momentum < 0 AND Flux < 0 OR Momentum < 0 AND Flux ≥ 0 OR (Momentum crosses below Signal AND Momentum > threshold AND Flux > 0 AND (volume confirmation is disabled, volume data is unavailable, or volume > Volume average)) OR regular bearish divergence (at a confirmed Momentum cross below Signal with Momentum above the positive sensitivity: current high > previous regular bearish anchor high AND current Signal < anchor Signal) OR hidden bearish divergence (at a confirmed Momentum cross below Signal: current high < previous hidden bearish anchor high AND current Signal > anchor Signal) OR squeeze release with Momentum change over the predictor lookback < negative slope threshold OR all four configured, valid higher timeframes have Momentum < 0.
Exit: Long, Momentum crosses below Signal.
Exit: Short, Momentum crosses above Signal.
