Calculation: Stochastic PC = SMA of the stochastic of the SMA of percentage change in close from open, using that SMA as the stochastic source, high, and low
Calculation: Stochastic V = SMA of the stochastic of the SMA of signed volume (positive when close ≥ open, negative otherwise), using that SMA as the stochastic source, high, and low
Calculation: Weighted K = SMA of the weighted average of enabled Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, Stochastic PC, Stochastic V, RSI, smoothed RSI, MFI, and smoothed MFI values; use each configured weight, and smooth each stochastic K and each smoothed RSI/MFI as configured
Long: Weighted K < very oversold level
Short: Weighted K > very overbought level
