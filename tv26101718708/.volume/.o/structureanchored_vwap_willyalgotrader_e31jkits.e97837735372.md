Calculation: Current leg = selected anchor mode: each confirmed swing low starts a bullish leg and each confirmed swing high starts a bearish leg; alternatively, a close beyond the prior swing starts a leg anchored at the intervening opposite extreme; Fast mode alternates new lookback highs/lows, using candle direction if both occur on one bar; Manual mode starts a bullish leg at the selected time. Confirmed swings use equal left/right lookbacks and an ATR-based minimum swing filter.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = weighted average of the selected price source from the current leg anchor through the current bar; weights are volume (or 1 when unavailable), optionally capped by a multiple of the 50-bar median, with optional exponential half-life decay.
Calculation: Leg sigma = square root of the nonnegative weighted mean of squared price minus squared anchored VWAP, over the same leg and weights.
Calculation: Retest tolerance = leg sigma × selected tolerance; when sigma is zero, use 0.1 × ATR.
Calculation: Away count = consecutive completed bars entirely beyond the retest tolerance on the current leg's side of VWAP; reset on a touch or any other bar.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop at entry ± ATR-based risk distance; after TP1 is touched without the stop being hit on that bar, set the stop to entry for subsequent bars. Targets are fixed at entry using configurable risk multiples; TP3 is the final target.
Long: Current leg is bullish AND the bar is confirmed and warmed up (bar index ≥ max(left pivot lookback + right pivot lookback, 50)) AND position is flat AND ATR-based risk distance > 0 AND the bar range intersects anchored VWAP ± retest tolerance AND the preceding away count meets the configured threshold.
Short: Current leg is bearish AND the bar is confirmed and warmed up (bar index ≥ max(left pivot lookback + right pivot lookback, 50)) AND position is flat AND ATR-based risk distance > 0 AND the bar range intersects anchored VWAP ± retest tolerance AND the preceding away count meets the configured threshold.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ TP3.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ TP3.
