Calculation: ATR = average true range on chart bars.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Model features = clipped rolling z-scores of one-bar close change, longer-lookback close change, and volume; efficiency ratio scaled to [-1,1]; RSI scaled to [-0.5,0.5]; and (close − EMA) / ATR.
Calculation: Triple-barrier training labels = open one sample per confirmed chart bar at close, storing its ATR and features; resolve when its upper or lower barrier (entry ± barrier-width × stored ATR) is first touched by high/low, label upper=1 and lower=0; if both touch on one bar, label 1 when close ≥ entry, else 0; ignore samples that time out. Train online logistic regression by SGD with L2 regularization on resolved samples, weighted by inverse one plus the number of samples already open when the sample was created. Retain recent resolved samples for k-nearest neighbors using distance equal to the sum across features of log(1 + absolute feature difference); neighbor probability is their mean label. When at least k neighbors exist, combine logistic and neighbor probabilities as sigmoid(Kish factor × [logit(logistic probability) + logit(neighbor probability)]); otherwise use logistic probability.
Calculation: Regime = trending when ADX and efficiency ratio exceed their thresholds; classify as uptrend if close > EMA, otherwise downtrend. If not trending, classify as volatile when the z-score of the decaying sum of absolute log returns exceeds its threshold; otherwise range. Shrink model probability toward 0.5 by min(1, resolved samples in current regime / warm-up sample threshold).
Calculation: Probability band = shrunk probability ± the (1 − effective alpha) quantile of recent absolute errors |label − probability recorded at sample opening|, clipped to [0,1]; unavailable until at least 10 errors. Effective alpha adapts toward target alpha using the conformal miss-rate updates. Calibrated probability = monotone isotonic mapping of shrunk probability to observed label frequencies in probability bins, once at least two bins meet their minimum sample count.
Calculation: Conviction = round(30 × model confidence + 20 × regime fit + 20 × MTF agreement + 20 × calibration quality + 10 × participation), where confidence=min(1, |shrunk probability−0.5|/0.5); regime fit is 1 for matching directional trend, 0.5 for range, otherwise 0.2; MTF agreement is matching-bias count / 3; calibration quality=clamp((0.25−Brier score)/0.15, 0, 1), or 0 if unavailable; participation is 1 when CVD direction matches, 0.6 when zero, otherwise 0.4. Score direction is the candidate direction when one exists, otherwise the direction of the shrunk probability relative to 0.5. CVD direction is the sign of cumulative chart-bar volume delta change over its lookback, with each bar's delta equal to volume times +1 when close ≥ open and −1 otherwise.
Calculation: MTF agreement counts, among three higher timeframes, bars whose close is above (bullish) or below (bearish) their EMA; use the last completed bar on each timeframe.
Long: warm-up resolved-sample threshold reached AND lower probability band > 0.5 + signal margin AND calibrated probability ≥ 0.5 AND conviction ≥ its floor AND regime is not volatile or downtrend AND bullish MTF count ≥ minimum agreement AND CVD direction is not bearish AND minimum bars since the previous signal reached.
Short: warm-up resolved-sample threshold reached AND upper probability band < 0.5 − signal margin AND calibrated probability ≤ 0.5 AND conviction ≥ its floor AND regime is not volatile or uptrend AND bearish MTF count ≥ minimum agreement AND CVD direction is not bullish AND minimum bars since the previous signal reached.
