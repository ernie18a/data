Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest high/low pivot, with the selected lookback bars on both sides; use only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Demand/supply zone = high-to-low range of the last opposite-color candle within the selected lookback before a bullish/bearish structure break; the zone is active until first price intersection.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep confirmation = the preceding bar trades above/below the latest confirmed pivot high/low, and the current completed bar closes back below/above that level, respectively; require a bearish/bullish current candle, close below/above the preceding close, and sweep extreme-to-close distance greater than the selected ATR multiple. If volume filtering is enabled, also require volume above its 20-bar average. ATR and volume average use chart-timeframe bars; apply the same test to each configured higher timeframe using its last completed bar.
Long: (current close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high) OR (a confirmed bar intersects an active demand zone AND trend state is bullish AND close > open AND demand zone has not been consumed AND latest completed higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND absolute latest swing high-low range > ATR times the selected chop threshold) OR (bullish sweep confirmation on chart timeframe OR bullish sweep confirmation on either configured higher timeframe, using its last completed bar)
Short: (current close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low) OR (a confirmed bar intersects an active supply zone AND trend state is bearish AND close < open AND supply zone has not been consumed AND latest completed higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA AND absolute latest swing high-low range > ATR times the selected chop threshold) OR (bearish sweep confirmation on chart timeframe OR bearish sweep confirmation on either configured higher timeframe, using its last completed bar)
