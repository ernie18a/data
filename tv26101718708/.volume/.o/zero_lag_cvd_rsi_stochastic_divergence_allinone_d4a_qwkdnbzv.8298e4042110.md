Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = EMA(buying volume, lookback) − EMA(selling volume, same lookback); for each bar, split volume between buying and selling by assigning the candle body to its direction and half of each wick to each side.
Calculation: Bullish divergence for an oscillator over lookback L = current close > open AND previous close < previous open AND previous low is no higher than any low from 2 through L bars ago AND the oscillator’s lowest value from 2 through L bars ago is below its previous-bar value.
Calculation: Bearish divergence for an oscillator over lookback L = current close < open AND previous close > previous open AND previous high is no lower than any high from 2 through L bars ago AND the oscillator’s highest value from 2 through L bars ago is above its previous-bar value.
Long: Bullish divergence for the selected oscillator on the shorter or longer lookback; for RSI divergence, current RSI <= its oversold threshold when the RSI extreme filter is enabled; for Stochastic divergence, current Stochastic <= its oversold threshold when the Stochastic extreme filter is enabled; in Agreement mode, at least the configured minimum of CVD, RSI, and Stochastic have bullish divergence on the same lookback, with RSI and Stochastic subject to their enabled extreme filters.
Short: Bearish divergence for the selected oscillator on the shorter or longer lookback; for RSI divergence, current RSI >= its overbought threshold when the RSI extreme filter is enabled; for Stochastic divergence, current Stochastic >= its overbought threshold when the Stochastic extreme filter is enabled; in Agreement mode, at least the configured minimum of CVD, RSI, and Stochastic have bearish divergence on the same lookback, with RSI and Stochastic subject to their enabled extreme filters.
