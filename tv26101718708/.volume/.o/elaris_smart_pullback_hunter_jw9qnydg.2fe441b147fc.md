Calculation: Fast EMA slope % = (fast EMA − fast EMA N bars ago) / fast EMA N bars ago × 100, using chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Higher low = current low > the lowest low of the preceding pullback-lookback bars; lower high = current high < the highest high of the preceding pullback-lookback bars
Calculation: Long pullback = bullish trend and (enabled fast-EMA pullback: low ≤ fast EMA and close > fast EMA; or enabled mid-EMA pullback: low ≤ mid EMA and close > mid EMA; or deep pullback: low < mid EMA, close > mid EMA, and distance from low to mid EMA ≤ the configured percentage). Short pullback uses the mirrored conditions.
Calculation: Pullback active = a corresponding long or short pullback occurred on the current bar or within the configured maximum pullback age, inclusive
Calculation: Body % = |close − open| / (high − low) × 100; lower-wick % = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) × 100; upper-wick % = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) × 100
Calculation: Long score = min(100, 20 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA, plus 15 if fast EMA slope % ≥ configured minimum, plus 10 if structure filter is off or higher low is true, plus 20 if mid-EMA pullback else 14 if fast-EMA pullback else 12 if deep pullback else 0, plus 15 if volume > its configured moving average × configured multiplier or volume filter is off, plus 20 if close > open and body % ≥ configured minimum, plus 10 if higher-timeframe filter is off or higher-timeframe fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA). Short score mirrors these conditions.
Long: on a confirmed chart bar, bullish trend (fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA, fast EMA slope % ≥ configured minimum, and structure filter off or higher low true) AND long pullback active AND close > open AND (close > fast EMA or close-back filter off) AND (volume > configured volume moving average × configured multiplier or volume filter off) AND (lower-wick % ≥ configured minimum or wick filter off) AND body % ≥ configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter off or higher-timeframe fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA) AND (score ≥ configured minimum or, when A+ only is enabled, score ≥ 90)
Long: bullish trend is true now and was false on the prior chart bar
Short: on a confirmed chart bar, bearish trend (fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA, fast EMA slope % ≤ negative configured minimum, and structure filter off or lower high true) AND short pullback active AND close < open AND (close < fast EMA or close-back filter off) AND (volume > configured volume moving average × configured multiplier or volume filter off) AND (upper-wick % ≥ configured minimum or wick filter off) AND body % ≥ configured minimum AND (higher-timeframe filter off or higher-timeframe fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA) AND (score ≥ configured minimum or, when A+ only is enabled, score ≥ 90)
