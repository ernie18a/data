Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Strong bull bar = close > open, body / (high − low) ≥ strong-body threshold, and upper tail / (high − low) ≤ maximum entry-tail threshold
Calculation: Strong bear bar = close < open, body / (high − low) ≥ strong-body threshold, and lower tail / (high − low) ≤ maximum entry-tail threshold
Calculation: Bull reversal bar = close > open, close ≥ low + 60% of bar range, and (strong bull bar OR lower tail ≥ 30% of bar range OR close > prior high)
Calculation: Bear reversal bar = close < open, close ≤ high − 60% of bar range, and (strong bear bar OR upper tail ≥ 30% of bar range OR close < prior low)
Calculation: Range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Range context = range-high-to-range-low width ≤ ATR × range-width threshold, overlap fraction over the range lookback ≥ overlap threshold, and directional efficiency over the range lookback ≤ 0.45; suppress for the configured bars after a strong breakout bar at least 1.30 ATR in range closes beyond the prior range extreme
Calculation: Trend up = EMA change over trend lookback > 0, close change over that lookback ≥ minimum trend move in ATR, fraction of closes above EMA ≥ minimum trend-side fraction, and directional efficiency over that lookback ≥ minimum efficiency
Calculation: Trend down = EMA change over trend lookback < 0, close change over that lookback ≤ negative minimum trend move in ATR, fraction of closes below EMA ≥ minimum trend-side fraction, and directional efficiency over that lookback ≥ minimum efficiency
Calculation: Wide channel up/down = trend up/down, range width ≥ ATR × wide-channel-width threshold, and overlap fraction over the range lookback ≥ wide-channel-overlap threshold
Calculation: Always-in long/short = trend up/down, close above/below EMA, not range context, and (absolute trend move in ATR ≥ minimum trend move OR fraction of strong trend-direction bars over trend lookback ≥ 0.20)
Calculation: Near key = current bar range touches within ATR × key-buffer threshold of the previous completed session high, low, or close; current session open; range high or low; EMA; or any enabled manual level
Calculation: Two-leg down/up = current low < low two bars ago and prior high > high two bars ago / current high > high two bars ago and prior low < low two bars ago
Calculation: Double bottom/top = newly confirmed pivot low/high within 0.35 ATR of the preceding pivot low/high
Calculation: Down/up wedge = three successively lower pivot lows / three successively higher pivot highs
Calculation: Bottom/top head-and-shoulders = three pivot lows/highs with the middle pivot lower/higher than both outer pivots and outer pivots within 0.50 ATR
Calculation: Confirmed reversal structure = relevant double bottom/top, wedge, or head-and-shoulders pivot confirmed within the structure-confirmation window
Calculation: Bull/bear OB/IB signal = prior bar is outside (higher high and lower low) or inside (lower high and higher low) the bar before it
Calculation: Three bear/bull bars = current and preceding two bars close below/above their opens
Calculation: First breakout = close beyond prior range high/low, open at or inside that level, body fraction ≥ strong-body threshold, and prior range width ≥ 0.75 ATR
Calculation: Break-fail-break = first breakout; within the configured maximum bars price closes back across its breakout level, then closes beyond that same level with a strong bar in the breakout direction
Calculation: Impulse midpoint = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the impulse lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Weak long/short pullback = no more than three consecutive bear/bull bars and fewer than three consecutive strong bear/bull bars
Calculation: Key touch = bar range touches the specified price within ATR × key-buffer threshold
Long: current bar is active AND (trend up on prior bar AND not wide channel up AND two-leg down AND close > open AND no strong bear bar in the last four bars) OR break-fail-break long OR near key AND ((bull reversal structure confirmed within the confirmation window AND (strong bull bar OR bull reversal bar) AND close > prior high) OR prior bar is an OB/IB signal AND bull reversal bar AND close > prior high OR trend-pullback long condition) OR range context AND low ≤ range low + key buffer AND (bull reversal bar OR close > range low) OR wide channel down at the pivot-strength lookback offset AND down wedge confirmed AND near key OR trend up AND not range context AND impulse midpoint touched within key buffer AND (two-leg down OR weak long pullback) AND fewer than three consecutive strong bear bars OR valid strong bullish breakout followed by strong bullish follow-through on the next bar, then within the trap window a later non-doji bar touches the breakout level and closes above it OR always-in long AND, after a strong bullish follow-through bar, low ≤ that bar's low AND close > that low AND not three bear bars OR always-in long on prior bar AND current and prior bars are bear AND bar two bars ago was not bear OR first EMA touch in the current always-in-long regime, with low ≤ EMA + EMA buffer AND close ≥ EMA − EMA buffer AND current bar not strong bear OR always-in long AND close > open AND close ≥ low + 50% of bar range AND body fraction ≥ strong-body threshold
Short: current bar is active AND (trend down on prior bar AND not wide channel down AND two-leg up AND close < open AND no strong bull bar in the last four bars) OR break-fail-break short OR near key AND ((bear reversal structure confirmed within the confirmation window AND (strong bear bar OR bear reversal bar) AND close < prior low) OR prior bar is an OB/IB signal AND bear reversal bar AND close < prior low OR trend-pullback short condition) OR range context AND high ≥ range high − key buffer AND (bear reversal bar OR close < range high) OR wide channel up at the pivot-strength lookback offset AND up wedge confirmed AND near key OR trend down AND not range context AND impulse midpoint touched within key buffer AND (two-leg up OR weak short pullback) AND fewer than three consecutive strong bull bars OR valid strong bearish breakout followed by strong bearish follow-through on the next bar, then within the trap window a later non-doji bar touches the breakout level and closes below it OR always-in short AND, after a strong bearish follow-through bar, high ≥ that bar's high AND close < that high AND not three bull bars OR always-in short on prior bar AND current and prior bars are bull AND bar two bars ago was not bull OR first EMA touch in the current always-in-short regime, with high ≥ EMA − EMA buffer AND close ≤ EMA + EMA buffer AND current bar not strong bull OR always-in short AND close < open AND close ≤ high − 50% of bar range AND body fraction ≥ strong-body threshold
Exit: long, trend up on prior bar AND strong bear bar AND close < impulse midpoint OR valid bullish trapped-breakout state AND close < breakout level AND strong bear bar OR prior bar strong bear and closed below EMA AND current bar bear, closes below EMA, and closes below prior close OR (always-in long OR trend up) AND three bear bars
Exit: short, trend down on prior bar AND strong bull bar AND close > impulse midpoint OR valid bearish trapped-breakout state AND close > breakout level AND strong bull bar OR prior bar strong bull and closed above EMA AND current bar bull, closes above EMA, and closes above prior close OR (always-in short OR trend down) AND three bull bars
