Calculation: Smoothed %K = 100 × (current source − lowest low over the stochastic lookback) ÷ (highest high − lowest low); when using StochRSI, apply the same formula to RSI instead. Apply the selected smoother to %K, then smooth %K to obtain %D. Use the configured source, lookbacks, and smoothing; use current-bar values.
Calculation: Calibrated bands = rolling configured percentiles of smoothed %K over the calibration lookback; use current-bar values.
Calculation: Embedded state = %K has remained at or above the overbought band, or at or below the oversold band, for the configured consecutive-bar threshold; use current-bar values.
Calculation: Trend regime = ADX exceeds its configured threshold and efficiency ratio (absolute source change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback) exceeds its configured threshold. Bull trend requires close above its configured EMA; bear trend requires close at or below it. Otherwise, the regime is range unless volatility-cluster z-score exceeds its configured threshold, in which case it is volatile.
Calculation: Adaptive trend trail = CMO-weighted adaptive moving average of %K, with a trailing band whose width is the configured percentage of that average, optionally scaled by 50-bar %K volatility; trail direction changes when the average crosses the prior trailing stop. Compare the average with the trail delayed by two bars.
Calculation: Cycle phase = apply the homodyne dominant-cycle calculation to bar midpoint; a long phase requires its detrended component at or below zero and rising, and a short phase requires it at or above zero and not rising.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed %K pivot low is higher than the previous %K pivot low while its price low is lower. Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed %K pivot high is lower while its price high is higher. Hidden bullish divergence = a confirmed %K pivot low is lower while its price low is higher. Hidden bearish divergence = a confirmed %K pivot high is higher while its price high is lower. Confirm pivots using configured left and right bars.
Long: (range regime AND not embedded down AND prior %K at or below prior oversold band AND %K crosses above %D AND long cycle phase AND configured signal-to-noise gate passes) OR (trend regime AND close above regime EMA AND (adaptive average crosses above trail OR adaptive average is above trail AND %K crosses above %D AND %K below 50) AND configured signal-to-noise gate passes AND higher-timeframe stochastic bias is nonnegative when that gate is enabled) OR regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence.
Short: (range regime AND not embedded up AND prior %K at or above prior overbought band AND %K crosses below %D AND short cycle phase AND configured signal-to-noise gate passes) OR (trend regime AND close at or below regime EMA AND (adaptive average crosses below trail OR adaptive average is below trail AND %K crosses below %D AND %K above 50) AND configured signal-to-noise gate passes AND higher-timeframe stochastic bias is nonpositive when that gate is enabled) OR regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence.
Exit: none
