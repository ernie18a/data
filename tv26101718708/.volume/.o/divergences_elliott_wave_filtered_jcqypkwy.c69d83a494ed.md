Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high/low that remains the highest high/lowest low across its configurable left and right bar windows; usable after the right window completes.
Calculation: RSI = standard close-based RSI; Stochastic RSI = SMA of the stochastic value of RSI over its lookback; MFI = standard money flow index of (high+low+close)/3; ROC oscillator = 50 + 25 × close ROC / its 50-bar standard deviation.
Calculation: Divergence pair = the newest earlier confirmed pivot of the same type whose distance to the current confirmed pivot meets the configured minimum and optional maximum bar gaps.
Calculation: Oscillator concordance = count of enabled oscillators matching the divergence: bullish regular, current oscillator > prior (RSI/Stochastic RSI/MFI also require prior below its oversold threshold); bullish hidden, current < prior; bearish regular, current < prior (RSI/Stochastic RSI/MFI also require prior above its overbought threshold); bearish hidden, current > prior. Divide count by enabled oscillator count for concordance fraction.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = clamp(log(R/S)/log(window), 0, 1), where R is the range of cumulative deviations of log close returns from their mean and S is the standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Normalized return entropy = Shannon entropy of log close returns binned across their observed range, divided by log(number of bins); use 0 if the range is zero.
Calculation: Wave context = classify confirmed alternating price swings independently at Small, Medium, and Large degrees (each degree has longer pivot lookbacks and a larger minimum swing than the preceding degree); recognize impulse/W5, diagonal, W3, W2, W4, corrective C, triangle E, or neutral using the swing geometry. Use a degree's classification only when its latest swing is within its configured proximity of the divergence. For Regular, use position scores W5 .92, diagonal .88, corrective C .65, triangle E .60, W4 .42, W2 .38, neutral .50, W3 .12; for Hidden use W2 .90, W4 .88, corrective C .70, neutral .50, W5/diagonal .15, W3 .35. Scale each score's distance from .50 by max(wave quality,.30); combine Small/Medium/Large as .45/.30/.25, add .20 for three high-score degrees or .15 for two, and apply the specified conflict penalty when Small is high and Medium/Large are low; clamp to [.05,.95]. A degree is high above .60 and low below .30. Wave quality is the impulse/diagonal quality from momentum, channel alignment, and alternation; other classified positions use their defined retracement/geometry quality.
Calculation: Trend agreement probability = .50 if no Medium or Large trend is available; otherwise compare divergence direction with the Medium trend, or Large if Medium is unavailable: Regular gets .75 when opposing and .30 otherwise; Hidden gets .80 when aligned and .25 otherwise. If Medium and Large trends agree, adjust this probability by +.08 when aligned/opposing as appropriate, otherwise -.08; clamp to [.05,.95].
Calculation: Divergence posterior = logistic(prior log-odds + weighted log-odds of concordance fraction, RSI divergence magnitude divided by its 50-bar standard deviation and scaled by the configured z threshold, Hurst regime probability, normalized return entropy, wave context, overextension probability, and trend agreement probability). Hurst regime probability favors Regular below .50 and Hidden above .50, reversing around .50; overextension probability is based on the current-vs-prior analogous swing length ratio. Weights, prior, and minimum posterior are configurable.
Long: confirmed pivot low P2 is below P1 with bullish regular oscillator concordance, OR P2 is above P1 with bullish hidden oscillator concordance; the corresponding divergence type is enabled; at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators agree; pivot gap and same-direction cooldown pass; optional slope non-intersection passes; the configured wave-position/degree filter passes when enabled; and divergence posterior >= configured minimum.
Short: confirmed pivot high P2 is above P1 with bearish regular oscillator concordance, OR P2 is below P1 with bearish hidden oscillator concordance; the corresponding divergence type is enabled; at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators agree; pivot gap and same-direction cooldown pass; optional slope non-intersection passes; the configured wave-position/degree filter passes when enabled; and divergence posterior >= configured minimum.
