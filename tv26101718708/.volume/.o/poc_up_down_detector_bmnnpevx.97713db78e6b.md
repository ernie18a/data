Calculation: Daily POC = price-bin midpoint with the greatest allocated volume, from intraday bars aggregated into each calendar-day profile; allocate volume by the configured profile method (close, HLC3, range overlap, or weighted OHLC). Use completed profiles for daily POCs; the current profile is still forming.
Calculation: Market state = update once per completed daily POC, comparing it with the preceding completed daily POC. Rising/falling POCs set bullish/bearish state only after two consecutive moves in the same direction. In bullish state, another rise keeps bullish; any other move sets neutral and saves that POC as the neutral reference and bullish as the prior trend. In bearish state, another fall keeps bearish; any other move sets neutral and saves that POC as the neutral reference and bearish as the prior trend. In neutral without a reference, a rise after a rise sets bullish and a fall after a fall sets bearish. In neutral with a reference, a rise above it sets bullish; a fall below it sets bearish if the saved prior trend was bearish, otherwise enters possible-ranging state. Possible-ranging state becomes bullish or bearish after two consecutive POC moves in that direction. Other moves leave/update state as in this sequence rule.
Long: Market state is bullish AND current close > previous completed daily POC AND at least one current-calendar-day bar has low < that POC AND current volume > current-calendar-day mean chart-bar volume × threshold AND at least 3 chart bars have occurred that calendar day AND no long signal has fired that calendar day; OR a long signal has already fired that calendar day and current open < current close AND current close > previous completed daily POC (strong-buy confirmation, once per signal).
Short: Market state is bearish AND current close < previous completed daily POC AND at least one current-calendar-day bar has high > that POC AND current volume > current-calendar-day mean chart-bar volume × threshold AND at least 3 chart bars have occurred that calendar day AND no short signal has fired that calendar day; OR a short signal has already fired that calendar day and current open > current close AND current close < previous completed daily POC (strong-sell confirmation, once per signal).
