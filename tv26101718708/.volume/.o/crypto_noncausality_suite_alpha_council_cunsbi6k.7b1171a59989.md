Calculation: Lower-timeframe cumulative volume delta = cumulative volume added on bullish lower-timeframe bars and subtracted on bearish lower-timeframe bars; unchanged on doji bars.
Calculation: Premium z-score = (premium index − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation.
Calculation: Absorption candle = volume percentile is high and either the candle body is small relative to its range, or the rejection wick is large relative to its range; bullish uses the lower wick and bearish uses the upper wick.
Calculation: Whittaker boundaries = asymmetric Whittaker-smoothed highs (upper) and lows (lower), fitted over the full topological window, including bars later than the evaluated bar.
Calculation: Polynomial regression channel = rolling polynomial regression of close, with upper and lower bounds offset by a rolling high-low range measure.
Calculation: Boundary armed = the relevant high/low touched either Whittaker or polynomial-regression boundary within the recent touch window, or a liquidation-wick anomaly or liquidity sweep occurred. A liquidation-wick anomaly requires range expansion versus ATR, a large rejection wick, climax volume, and close displacement from its EMA; a liquidity sweep requires price to pierce the prior local pivot and close back across it with directional candle confirmation and above-average volume.
Calculation: Absorption base cleared = a directionally armed absorption candle closes on the relevant side of the midpoint between the Whittaker boundaries; this remains cleared until the causal anomaly z-score crosses the midpoint in the opposing direction.
Long: Absorption base cleared long AND (lower-timeframe cumulative volume delta >= its previous value OR open interest < its previous value OR premium z-score crosses upward through its lower threshold OR USDT dominance <= its previous value OR previous-bar volume percentile is high and current-bar volume percentile is low)
Short: Absorption base cleared short AND (lower-timeframe cumulative volume delta <= its previous value OR open interest < its previous value OR premium z-score crosses downward through its upper threshold OR USDT dominance >= its previous value OR previous-bar volume percentile is high and current-bar volume percentile is low)
Exit: long, high >= the nearest active unmitigated price level from a prior bearish absorption star
Exit: short, low <= the nearest active unmitigated price level from a prior bullish absorption star
