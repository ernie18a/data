Calculation: Opening auction high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening-auction window, finalized when that window ends.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick rejection percentage = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) × 100 / (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) × 100.
Long: Supported intraday timeframe AND active session AND auction completed AND within trap evaluation window AND ((low < opening auction low AND close > opening auction low AND lower wick rejection percentage ≥ wick-quality threshold) OR (consecutive closes above opening auction high ≥ acceptance close count AND close > opening auction high)).
Short: Supported intraday timeframe AND active session AND auction completed AND within trap evaluation window AND ((high > opening auction high AND close < opening auction high AND upper wick rejection percentage ≥ wick-quality threshold) OR (consecutive closes below opening auction low ≥ acceptance close count AND close < opening auction low)).
