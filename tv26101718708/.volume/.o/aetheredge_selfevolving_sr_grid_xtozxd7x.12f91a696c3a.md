Calculation: ATR = average true range of the current chart bars.
Calculation: Candidate S/R levels = confirmed swing highs and lows, 5-bar Williams fractal highs and lows, ATR-spaced levels above and below close, and nearby round-number levels; classify each as resistance or support as in the source.
Calculation: Line score = updated each completed bar by Q ← clamp(Q + learning_rate × (reward + discount_factor × highest_active_line_score − Q), 0, 1); rewards are volume-weighted rejection, break, or confirmed S/R flip rewards, with decay for idle lines and per-bar score decay.
Calculation: Strong S/R line = active line whose score is at least the strong-line threshold.
Long: A strong support line is touched (high ≥ line price − ATR × touch tolerance AND low ≤ line price + ATR × touch tolerance) AND low ≤ line price + ATR × touch tolerance AND close > line price + ATR × touch tolerance × 0.5 AND NOT (close < line price − ATR × break confirmation AND open > line price) OR a strong resistance line is broken upward (close > line price + ATR × break confirmation) OR, 3–30 bars after a strong resistance line breaks upward, low ≤ line price + ATR × touch tolerance AND close > line price.
Short: A strong resistance line is touched (high ≥ line price − ATR × touch tolerance AND low ≤ line price + ATR × touch tolerance) AND high ≥ line price − ATR × touch tolerance AND close < line price − ATR × touch tolerance × 0.5 AND NOT (close > line price + ATR × break confirmation AND open < line price) OR a strong support line is broken downward (close < line price − ATR × break confirmation) OR, 3–30 bars after a strong support line breaks downward, high ≥ line price − ATR × touch tolerance AND close < line price.
