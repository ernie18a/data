Calculation: Weekly Alt/BTC base risk = 0.05 × MACD(EMA fast − EMA slow) normalized to 0–100 using its rolling 500-bar minimum and maximum + 0.60 × RSI + 0.35 × ((weekly close − long-term SMA) / long-term SMA + 1) × 100; use last completed weekly bars.
Calculation: Weekly risk metric = EMA of weekly base risk plus enabled configured adjustments for Fear & Greed, PMI, and social risk; use last completed weekly bars for weekly inputs and last completed daily bars for daily PMI, and calculate social risk on the chart timeframe. Social risk is EMA-smoothed RSI centered at 50, adjusted by the ATR(14)/close versus its 50-bar SMA volatility dampener and RSI extreme boosts, with values within ±5 set to zero.
Long: Weekly risk metric ≤ long-entry threshold
Exit: long, weekly risk metric ≥ first exit threshold AND first exit stage not completed; OR weekly risk metric ≥ second exit threshold AND first exit stage completed AND second exit stage not completed; OR weekly risk metric ≥ third exit threshold AND second exit stage completed AND third exit stage not completed. A long entry resets all exit stages.
