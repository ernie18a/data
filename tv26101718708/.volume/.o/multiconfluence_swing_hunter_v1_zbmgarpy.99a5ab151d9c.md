Calculation: Entry score = 2×(RSI below oversold level) + 2×(RSI below extreme-oversold level) + (RSI rising) + (MACD line below zero) + 2×(MACD line rising) + 2×(MACD histogram rising) + 2×(lower wick exceeds its configured share of bar range) + (body is below 30% of bar range) + (close > open) + 2×(quick recovery) + 4×(bull reversal signal); bullish and MACD/RSI divergence components are always false because the compared current low is below a low that includes the current bar.
Calculation: Exit score = 2×(RSI above overbought level) + 2×(RSI above extreme-overbought level) + (RSI falling) + (MACD line above zero) + 2×(MACD line falling) + 2×(MACD histogram falling) + 2×(upper wick exceeds the configured share of bar range) + (close < open) + (bear reversal signal); bearish divergence components are always false because the compared current high is above a high that includes the current bar.
Calculation: Quick recovery = any of the previous configured number of bars has a high greater than the current close multiplied by one plus the configured recovery percentage.
Calculation: Bull reversal signal = a bull reversal candidate was set when the current close was below the lows of all but one of the configured recent bars; within the configured confirmation window, before a close below the candidate low and before confirmation, the close exceeds the candidate high (and, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume exceeds its 20-bar SMA).
Calculation: Bear reversal signal = a bear reversal candidate was set when the current close was above the highs of all but one of the configured recent bars; within the configured confirmation window, before a close above the candidate high and before confirmation, the close falls below the candidate low (and, if volume confirmation is enabled, volume exceeds its 20-bar SMA).
Calculation: Stop loss = the lowest low over the configured lookback at position entry, reduced by the configured buffer percentage.
Calculation: Take profit = entry price + (entry price − stop loss) × configured risk/reward ratio.
Long: Bull reversal signal OR (entry score ≥ configured minimum AND within configured date range AND bar is confirmed AND position is flat)
Short: Bear reversal signal
Exit: Long, (risk/reward exits enabled AND (low ≤ stop loss OR high ≥ take profit)) OR (risk/reward exits disabled AND exit score ≥ configured minimum AND bar is confirmed)
