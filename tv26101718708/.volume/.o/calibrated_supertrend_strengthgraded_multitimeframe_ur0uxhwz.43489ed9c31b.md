Calculation: Primary Supertrend direction = direction from recursively trailed lower and upper bands around the selected price source, using ATR distance with adaptive ATR length and multiplier; direction changes bullish when close crosses the prior upper band and bearish when it crosses the prior lower band.
Calculation: Confirmed flip = a primary direction change whose close breaches the prior band by at least the adaptive breach threshold times ATR, after the minimum bars since the previous raw flip, evaluated on a completed bar.
Calculation: Secondary Supertrend direction = direction from recursively trailed lower and upper bands around the selected price source, using its own ATR length and multiplier; its parameters are slower than the primary Supertrend.
Long: Primary Supertrend direction changes from bearish to bullish OR Confirmed flip is bullish and conviction is at least its threshold and flip strength is at least its threshold and no volatility-spike, all-higher-timeframes-opposed, range-or-volatile-regime, or opposing-cross-asset veto applies OR Secondary Supertrend direction changes from bearish to bullish
Short: Primary Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish OR Confirmed flip is bearish and conviction is at least its threshold and flip strength is at least its threshold and no volatility-spike, all-higher-timeframes-opposed, range-or-volatile-regime, or opposing-cross-asset veto applies OR Secondary Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish
Exit: Long, close < the prior trailing lower Supertrend band
Exit: Short, close > the prior trailing upper Supertrend band
