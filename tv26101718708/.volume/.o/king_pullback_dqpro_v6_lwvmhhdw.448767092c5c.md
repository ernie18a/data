Calculation: Bullish trend = the indicator’s persistent trend state is UP; bearish trend = it is DOWN. The state changes on confirmed breaks of the latest frozen thick trend cloud, or on its swing-reversal process; a touch of that cloud sets SIDEWAY. Otherwise it follows the indicator’s trend-strength, directional-movement, RSI, MACD, moving-average, volume, and range tests.
Calculation: Pullback cloud = the indicator’s trend-direction light cloud; use its last completed-bar boundaries when averaged from prior bars, otherwise its current boundaries. Thick cloud = the trend-direction dark cloud, frozen from averages of a longer or shorter lookback selected by relative volatility; use its last completed-bar zone for touch and break tests.
Calculation: Candle strength = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 for a zero range. Strong bullish candle = close > open AND candle strength ≥ the confirmation threshold AND close ≥ low + 60% of the range. Strong bearish candle = close < open AND candle strength ≥ the confirmation threshold AND close ≤ high − 60% of the range.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close. RSI range = highest RSI − lowest RSI over its lookback. Buy RSI filters = RSI above the buy trend threshold AND below the buy extreme threshold AND highest RSI over its lookback below the historical buy block threshold AND RSI range within its maximum. Sell RSI filters = RSI below the sell trend threshold AND above the sell extreme threshold AND lowest RSI over its lookback above the historical sell block threshold AND RSI range within its maximum.
Calculation: Bullish trend strength = positive directional index > negative directional index AND ADX ≥ the trend-strength threshold. Bearish trend strength = negative directional index > positive directional index AND ADX ≥ the trend-strength threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = EMA(close, short lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND close > EMA(close, pullback lookback) AND RSI > 50 for bullish; reverse the comparisons and RSI < 50 for bearish. Use two higher timeframes and one lower timeframe; evaluate each timeframe’s current bar.
Calculation: Touch entry = current candle body or range overlaps the light cloud and closes beyond the far edge of the thick cloud, OR crosses the light-cloud edge in the trend direction and closes within/beyond the cloud. A fresh touch is true when the prior bar was not a touch; a consecutive touch is true when the prior bar was also a touch.
Calculation: Follow-through entry = within three bars after a light-cloud touch, current bar is no longer touching, is a strong candle in the trend direction, and closes beyond the basis moving average.
Calculation: Opposing pressure = volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the volume-strength factor AND candle is opposite the trend AND candle strength ≥ the opposing-pressure threshold. Trend volume confirmation = candle is in trend direction, candle strength ≥ its threshold, close is in the stronger portion of the candle range, and, when volume is strong, close advances from the prior close or volume is at least prior-bar volume.
Long: bullish trend AND buy RSI filters AND bullish trend strength AND RSI above the buy trend threshold and below the buy extreme threshold AND (normal pullback: weak candles allowed or candle strength ≥ minimum body ratio; low ≤ basis OR (mid pullback enabled AND low ≤ medium-term moving average) OR low ≤ upper edge of light cloud; close ≥ lower edge of light cloud adjusted by close tolerance; strong bullish candle; close > basis; distance above basis to the nearer applicable stop moving average ≤ ATR × location limit; and no consecutive touch OR fresh light-cloud touch OR consecutive touch with bullish rebound; OR immediate touch: fresh touch OR consecutive touch with bullish rebound; OR follow-through entry) AND (H1 bullish OR H4 bullish OR (current trend bullish AND M15 not bearish)) AND NOT opposing pressure AND session filter passes AND confirmed bar AND signal enabled AND trend order limit not reached AND (immediate touch/follow-through OR normal-entry cooldown has elapsed).
Short: bearish trend AND sell RSI filters AND bearish trend strength AND RSI below the sell trend threshold and above the sell extreme threshold AND (normal pullback: weak candles allowed or candle strength ≥ minimum body ratio; high ≥ basis OR (mid pullback enabled AND high ≥ medium-term moving average) OR high ≥ lower edge of light cloud; close ≤ upper edge of light cloud adjusted by close tolerance; strong bearish candle; close < basis; distance below basis to the nearer applicable stop moving average ≤ ATR × location limit; and no consecutive touch OR fresh light-cloud touch OR consecutive touch with bearish rebound; OR immediate touch: fresh touch OR consecutive touch with bearish rebound; OR follow-through entry) AND (H1 bearish OR H4 bearish OR (current trend bearish AND M15 not bullish)) AND NOT opposing pressure AND session filter passes AND confirmed bar AND signal enabled AND trend order limit not reached AND (immediate touch/follow-through OR normal-entry cooldown has elapsed).
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ first profit target.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ first profit target.
