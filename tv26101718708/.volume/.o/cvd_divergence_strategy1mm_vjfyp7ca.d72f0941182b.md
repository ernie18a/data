Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume × (bull pressure − bear pressure) / (bull pressure + bear pressure), splitting each bar’s volume between bull and bear pressure in proportion to the Bull & Bear Balance Indicator pressures.
Long: A confirmed CVD pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed CVD pivot low AND the bar’s low at the current pivot is lower than at the previous pivot low AND the previous pivot is within the minimum-to-maximum pivot lookback range, OR a confirmed CVD pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed CVD pivot low AND the bar’s low at the current pivot is higher than at the previous pivot low AND the previous pivot is within that range.
Short: A confirmed CVD pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed CVD pivot high AND the bar’s high at the current pivot is higher than at the previous pivot high AND the previous pivot is within the minimum-to-maximum pivot lookback range, OR a confirmed CVD pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed CVD pivot high AND the bar’s high at the current pivot is lower than at the previous pivot high AND the previous pivot is within that range.
Exit: long, short condition OR low ≤ average entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high ≥ average entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction)
Exit: short, long condition OR high ≥ average entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low ≤ average entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction)
