Calculation: Curve state = position of close between the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback of higher-timeframe bars; state is high above ⅔, low below ⅓, otherwise middle. Use the higher-timeframe value available on the current bar (historical bars use the last completed bar; realtime may use the forming bar).
Calculation: Trend state = bullish when the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA and rising; bearish when below and falling; otherwise neutral. Calculate on intermediate-timeframe bars, using the current available bar (historical bars use the last completed bar; realtime may use the forming bar).
Calculation: Demand and supply zones = active, unbroken zones detected on chart bars from a compact base followed by a qualifying directional departure. Demand zones come from bullish departures and supply zones from bearish departures; retain zone bounds, freshness/test count, strength, and time-at-zone scores. Select the highest-ranked visible zone on each side, prioritizing untested zones, then rank by zone truth, proximity, width, reversal status, and recency. A zone is near when close is within the zone or within an ATR-based distance of its bounds.
Calculation: Auction trigger = bullish for a failed sweep below demand that closes above its top with a strong close and participation, a failed move below the lower VWAP value band that closes back above it with a strong close and participation, or an accepted close above the completed opening range with participation. Bearish conditions are the mirror image; range acceptance requires consecutive closes outside the range.
Calculation: Long and short scores = sum of zone strength (capped with reaction points), time at zone, zone freshness, trend alignment, curve location, reward-to-risk, and zone rejection points, capped at 10. Long freshness scores are 2 for zero tests, 1 for one test, else 0; short uses the supply zone equivalently. Trend scores are 2 aligned, 1 neutral, else 0. Curve scores are 1 at the favorable extreme, 0.5 in the middle, else 0. Reward-to-risk scores are 2 at least 5R, 1 at least the preferred minimum R, else 0. Rejection points are the zone rejection score above 0.55, scaled by 0.75 and capped at 0.35.
Calculation: Long and short reward-to-risk = distance from close to the farther valid target divided by risk to the stop. Stops are beyond the matching zone by an ATR buffer, or beyond the current bar by ATR plus buffer if no fresh zone; targets use the favorable VWAP or minimum-R target and the opposing zone or higher-timeframe range extreme, choosing the farther favorable target.
Long: long score meets the minimum score AND curve state is not high AND (near demand OR failed lower-value auction OR accepted above opening range) AND bullish auction trigger is valid AND trend is not bearish when trend alignment is required AND close > previous bar high when structure break is required AND a matching demand zone is near or a demand sweep failed when a matching zone is required AND not middle chop inside the value bands away from zones when enabled AND reward-to-risk meets the minimum AND current bar range does not exceed the chase limit AND no fresh opposing supply zone is within the blocking distance when enabled AND candle is confirmed when closed-candle signals are required.
Short: short score meets the minimum score AND curve state is not low AND (near supply OR failed upper-value auction OR accepted below opening range) AND bearish auction trigger is valid AND trend is not bullish when trend alignment is required AND close < previous bar low when structure break is required AND a matching supply zone is near or a supply sweep failed when a matching zone is required AND not middle chop inside the value bands away from zones when enabled AND reward-to-risk meets the minimum AND current bar range does not exceed the chase limit AND no fresh opposing demand zone is within the blocking distance when enabled AND candle is confirmed when closed-candle signals are required.
