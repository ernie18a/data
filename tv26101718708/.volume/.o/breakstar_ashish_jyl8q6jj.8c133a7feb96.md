Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a lookback; volume surge = current volume ≥ a multiplier of average volume.
Calculation: Average candle body = simple moving average of |close − open| over a lookback.
Calculation: Breakout upper/lower levels = highest/lowest close over a lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Morning Star = the candle two bars ago is bearish and its body is at least a multiplier of average candle body; the intervening candle’s body is no more than a fraction of the first candle’s body; the current candle is bullish and closes at least halfway into the first candle’s body; and close two bars ago is below the close a longer lookback before that candle.
Calculation: Evening Star = the candle two bars ago is bullish and its body is at least a multiplier of average candle body; the intervening candle’s body is no more than a fraction of the first candle’s body; the current candle is bearish and closes at least halfway into the first candle’s body; and close two bars ago is above the close a longer lookback before that candle.
Long: Position is not long AND bars since last entry or flip meet the minimum signal spacing AND (Morning Star OR (close > breakout upper level AND volume surge)).
Short: Position is not short AND bars since last entry or flip meet the minimum signal spacing AND (Evening Star OR (close < breakout lower level AND volume surge)).
Calculation: Long invalidation level = initially the signal candle’s low (for a Morning Star, the lowest low of its three candles) minus an ATR buffer; thereafter, while trailing is enabled, the greater of the prior invalidation level and the highest high since entry minus a multiple of ATR, with the candidate raised to at least entry price after the breakeven profit threshold and to at least the prior close after the tighter-trail profit threshold.
Calculation: Short invalidation level = initially the signal candle’s high (for an Evening Star, the highest high of its three candles) plus an ATR buffer; thereafter, while trailing is enabled, the lesser of the prior invalidation level and the lowest low since entry plus a multiple of ATR, with the candidate lowered to at most entry price after the breakeven profit threshold and to at most the prior close after the tighter-trail profit threshold.
Calculation: Reversal-pattern bands = Hull moving average of close ± a multiplier of the standard deviation of close; average range = simple moving average of high − low over the dispersion lookback.
Calculation: Bearish reversal = Evening Star OR (after a prior rise, a non-inside-bar candle at a fresh lookback high touching the upper band, with upper wick ≥ a multiple of body, lower wick ≤ body, and positive body) OR (a bearish candle whose prior candle is bullish, whose close is below the prior open, whose open is above the prior close, which touches the upper band, and whose range is at least a multiple of average range).
Calculation: Bullish reversal = Morning Star OR (after a prior decline, a non-inside-bar candle at a fresh lookback low touching the lower band, with lower wick ≥ a multiple of body, upper wick ≤ body, and positive body) OR (a bullish candle whose prior candle is bearish, whose close is above the prior open, whose open is below the prior close, which touches the lower band, and whose range is at least a multiple of average range).
Exit: Long, close < long invalidation level OR (profit in ATR units ≥ the minimum pattern-exit threshold AND bearish reversal).
Exit: Short, close > short invalidation level OR (profit in ATR units ≥ the minimum pattern-exit threshold AND bullish reversal).
