Calculation: Fusion average = mean of EMA, SMA, WMA, ALMA, and VWAP of the close, each using the same long lookback; fusion fast and fusion slow = EMA of this average using shorter and longer smoothing lengths, respectively.
Calculation: Williams %R fast, medium, and long = Williams %R using progressively longer lookbacks; fast crosses up through oversold / down through overbought when it crosses the configured threshold in that direction.
Calculation: Pro buy/sell confidence = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: long-Williams %R points (buy: 25/20/12/5/0 above −35/−45/−55/−65/otherwise; sell: 25/20/12/5/0 below −65/−55/−45/−35/otherwise); fast-Williams %R points (buy: 20/16/12/6/0 below −94/−90/−85/−80/otherwise; sell: 20/16/12/6/0 above −6/−10/−15/−20/otherwise); signal points (20 for triple, else 18 continuation, else 17 divergence, else 14 extreme snap, else 11 exhaustion, else 8 pullback, else 0); velocity points (15 ideal, 10 strong, 3 extreme, otherwise 6); session quality divided by 10 (overlap 100, London 85, New York afternoon 60, Asia 30, otherwise 40); trend points (5 when price is above/below EMA200 and EMA50 is above/below EMA200 in the corresponding direction, 2 when only price is above/below EMA200, otherwise 0); stacking bonus (15 for at least 3 same-side signals, 8 for 2, otherwise 0); minus 10 for extreme velocity or 5 for strong velocity; minus 8/3 for RSI above 72/65 on buys or below 28/35 on sells; minus 10 in Asia or 5 at New York close; minus 5 when volume is below 0.7 times its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Pro buy/sell signal groups = OR of: divergence (the latest confirmed 5-bar pivot low is lower in price and higher in fast Williams %R than the prior pivot low, or latest pivot high is higher in price and lower in fast Williams %R than prior pivot high; pivot spacing 5–50 bars, current pivot no more than 3 bars old; fast-Williams %R velocity exceeds the session-scaled weak threshold in the matching direction); extreme snap (prior fast Williams %R below −88 / above −12, matching velocity exceeds weak threshold, medium or long Williams %R above −65 / below −35, and fast Williams %R crosses up through oversold / down through overbought); exhaustion (prior fast Williams %R below −78 / above −22, medium or long Williams %R above −75 / below −25 or −70 / −30 respectively, matching velocity exceeds weak threshold, and fast Williams %R crosses the corresponding threshold); continuation (fast crosses corresponding threshold, long and medium Williams %R meet strong/very strong trend levels, and either medium or long Williams %R slope over the trend lookback is above 0.3/0.15 for buys or below −0.3/−0.15 for sells); pullback (fast crosses corresponding threshold, medium Williams %R meets moderate trend level or is above −60 / below −40, and matching velocity exceeds weak threshold); triple (fast, medium, and long Williams %R are all oversold/overbought, matching velocity exceeds weak threshold, and fast crosses corresponding threshold). Velocity thresholds are session-scaled: weak 3, ideal 5–30, strong above 30–35, extreme above 35; session base is 1 overlap, 0.9 London, 0.8 New York afternoon, 0.5 Asia, otherwise 0.6. Strong/very strong trend levels: buy long/medium above −30/−40 or −40/−50; sell below −70/−60 or −60/−50. Moderate levels: buy below −55/−60 or medium above −60; sell above −45/−40 or medium below −40.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursively trail the selected close (regular or Heikin-Ashi) by configured ATR multiple; move upward while current and prior selected closes are above the prior stop, downward while both are below, otherwise set to selected close minus/plus ATR loss according to its side.
Calculation: Zero-lag basis = EMA(close + close − close lagged by half the lookback, using that lookback); band width = highest ATR over three times that lookback, multiplied by a configured factor. Trend state becomes bullish when close crosses above basis plus band width and bearish when close crosses below basis minus band width; otherwise it retains its prior state.
Long: close is above fusion slow and fusion slow is rising, and the prior bar was not bullish, and the prior trend-label direction was bearish or cooldown has elapsed.
Short: close is below fusion slow and fusion slow is falling, and the prior bar was not bearish, and the prior trend-label direction was bullish or cooldown has elapsed.
Long: current trend state is bullish, pro buy signal group is true, buy confidence meets its configured minimum, the trend gate passes (when enabled: unless strong downtrend, or buy recovery is detected; recovery requires the fast Williams %R lowest over recovery lookback plus one bars to be below −80 and current fast Williams %R to exceed that low by the configured minimum move), and the date filter is disabled or the bar is in its configured date range.
Short: current trend state is bearish, pro sell signal group is true, sell confidence meets its configured minimum, the trend gate passes (when enabled: unless strong uptrend, or sell recovery is detected; recovery requires the fast Williams %R highest over recovery lookback plus one bars to be above −20 and current fast Williams %R to be below that high by the configured minimum move), and the date filter is disabled or the bar is in its configured date range.
Long: selected close is above the ATR trailing stop and crosses above it.
Short: selected close is below the ATR trailing stop and crosses below it.
Long: close crosses above the zero-lag basis and zero-lag trend state is bullish on this and the prior bar.
Short: close crosses below the zero-lag basis and zero-lag trend state is bearish on this and the prior bar.
Long: zero-lag trend state changes to bullish.
Short: zero-lag trend state changes to bearish.
Long: each configured higher-timeframe zero-lag trend state is bullish; use the bar still forming on that timeframe.
Short: each configured higher-timeframe zero-lag trend state is bearish; use the bar still forming on that timeframe.
Exit: Long, current trend state is bullish, a pro sell signal passes its confidence and trend gates, a long entry signal has previously occurred in this trend, and the date filter is disabled or the bar is in its configured date range.
Exit: Short, current trend state is bearish, a pro buy signal passes its confidence and trend gates, a short entry signal has previously occurred in this trend, and the date filter is disabled or the bar is in its configured date range.
