Calculation: Confirmed upper/lower reference = most recent confirmed pivot high/low; merge with the existing same-side reference when within the ATR-based clustering tolerance, updating it by strength-weighted average and incrementing strength, otherwise replace it; reference is usable until its age exceeds the maximum.
Calculation: Directional evidence = 0.45 × clipped participation bias + 0.30 × clipped normalized slope of the long-term volume-weighted price center + 0.25 × clipped normalized close displacement from that center; participation bias is the clipped EMA of volume-weighted close location, normalized by EMA volume.
Calculation: Dynamic value upper/lower = long-term volume-weighted price center ± max(long-term price standard deviation × value-width factor, ATR × value-width floor).
Calculation: Session control and bounds = dynamic control and its bounds captured on the first completed bar of each day and held through that day; dynamic control is the long-term volume-weighted price center adjusted by participation bias, with width based on long-term price standard deviation and ATR.
Calculation: Bull/bear channel = directional evidence is respectively above/below its threshold, short-term EMA slope normalized by ATR is not materially opposed, and close is respectively above/below the long-term volume-weighted price center.
Calculation: Bull/bear channel start = completed bar is in the respective channel and the previous bar was not.
Calculation: Bull/bear micro-break = bullish/bearish candle closes above the highest high/below the lowest low of the preceding short lookback.
Calculation: Bull/bear acceptance = completed bullish/bearish candle closes beyond the respective dynamic value and session-control bounds; directional evidence, body-to-range ratio, and close location meet their respective thresholds; relative volume meets its participation threshold (or usable volume is absent).
Calculation: Bull/bear reclaim = completed bullish/bearish candle reaches beyond the respective session-control bound and closes beyond the opposite bound; directional evidence and body-to-range ratio meet their thresholds, and the rejection-side wick exceeds the other wick.
Calculation: Bull/bear liquidity turn = completed bar sweeps the preceding longer-lookback low/high and closes back above/below it, also makes the respective micro-break, has sufficiently positive/negative directional evidence, closes on the corresponding side of session control, and meets the relative-volume threshold (or usable volume is absent).
Calculation: Intraday pressure balance = (short-term average estimated buy volume − short-term average estimated sale volume) / their sum; estimated buy/sale volume is volume weighted by bullish/bearish close location. Pressure shift = change in pressure balance.
Calculation: Bull/bear intraday support score = sum of the respective points for: pressure balance beyond its directional threshold; pressure shift beyond its directional threshold; sweep of the preceding pressure-lookback extreme with directional close location; rejection wick at least the body size times its threshold with directional close location; directional candle with body at least ATR times its threshold, directional close location, and relative-volume expansion; adaptive behaviour line direction; medium-term EMA trend slope direction; micro-break; and directional channel.
Calculation: Buy/sale support = intraday bar and respective support score meets its minimum, exceeds the opposite score by its margin, and has a pressure sweep or initiative candle, or a rejection wick with pressure shifting in the same direction.
Calculation: Bull/bear votes = 2 each for respective acceptance, reclaim, and liquidity turn; 1 each for respective intraday support, channel, and directional evidence beyond its vote threshold.
Calculation: Strong bull/bear acceptance = respective acceptance plus more extreme directional evidence and body-to-range ratio thresholds.
Calculation: Bull/bear core event = respective acceptance OR reclaim OR liquidity turn.
Calculation: Buy/sale candidate = completed directional candle; not opposed by the opposite channel; not rejected at the respective confirmed reference or adaptive session envelope; sufficient room to the opposing confirmed reference; and strong acceptance OR (at least the required votes and a core event) OR (the lower vote threshold, a core event, and same-side support).
Calculation: Resolve simultaneous buy and sale candidates by keeping only the buy candidate if directional evidence is positive, only the sale candidate if negative, and neither if zero.
Long: completed bull channel start and last signal direction is short; OR, if no bull channel start, last signal direction is short and buy candidate; OR, if no prior signal, buy candidate and not sale candidate.
Short: completed bear channel start and last signal direction is long; OR, if no bear channel start, last signal direction is long and sale candidate; OR, if no prior signal, sale candidate and not buy candidate.
