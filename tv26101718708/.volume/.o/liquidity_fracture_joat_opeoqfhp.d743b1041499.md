Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is higher/lower than the bars on both sides, using equal left and right lookbacks of half the swing lookback; available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; body size = abs(close − open); candle range = high − low.
Calculation: Average volume = 20-bar simple moving average of volume, including the current bar.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND ((current low < most recent confirmed pivot low that has not already been swept AND current close > that pivot low AND current volume >= average volume) OR (candle range > an ATR-based minimum AND candle range > 0 AND lower wick / candle range > the wick-ratio threshold AND close > open AND body size < the configured fraction of candle range AND volume > average volume) OR (volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier AND previous close < previous open AND close > open AND close > previous close AND close − low > the configured multiple of high − close))
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND ((current high > most recent confirmed pivot high that has not already been swept AND current close < that pivot high AND current volume >= average volume) OR (candle range > an ATR-based minimum AND candle range > 0 AND upper wick / candle range > the wick-ratio threshold AND close < open AND body size < the configured fraction of candle range AND volume > average volume) OR (volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier AND previous close > previous open AND close < open AND close < previous close AND high − close > the configured multiple of close − low))
