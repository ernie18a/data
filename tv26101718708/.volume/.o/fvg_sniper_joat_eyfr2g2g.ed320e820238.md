Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low above the high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high below the low two bars earlier. Gap boundaries are those two prices; the middle bar is the displacement bar.
Calculation: Gap grade = 10 × min(1, 0.40 × min(gap height / ATR, 1) + 0.35 × min(middle-bar range / (2 × ATR), 1) + 0.25 × volume score), where volume score = min(middle-bar volume / current 20-bar average volume, 2) / 2 when volume and average are positive, otherwise 0.5. ATR and volume average use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: HTF bias = higher-timeframe close above its EMA means bullish; otherwise bearish. Use the HTF bar returned with lookahead off, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Risk distance = absolute distance from entry to the selected stop, clamped between configurable ATR-based floor and cap. Stop is either beyond the triggering gap boundary by an ATR buffer, or an ATR distance from entry. TP3 is entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk distance times the configured TP3 R multiple.
Long: Confirmed bar AND no open position AND enabled bullish source AND (bullish rejection OR bullish IFVG flip); bullish rejection = an eligible bullish FVG is unmitigated, uninverted, unreacted, at least one bar old and within its lifetime, current low is between its boundaries, current close is above its bottom, grade meets minimum, optional bullish candle close requires close > open, and optional HTF filter permits bullish bias; bullish IFVG flip = close above the top of an eligible bearish FVG, its grade meets minimum, and optional HTF filter permits bullish bias. A new bullish FVG must meet the minimum gap-height/ATR and middle-bar body/range thresholds. Entry is the signal bar close.
Short: Confirmed bar AND no open position AND enabled bearish source AND (bearish rejection OR bearish IFVG flip); bearish rejection = an eligible bearish FVG is unmitigated, uninverted, unreacted, at least one bar old and within its lifetime, current high is between its boundaries, current close is below its top, grade meets minimum, optional bearish candle close requires close < open, and optional HTF filter permits bearish bias; bearish IFVG flip = close below the bottom of an eligible bullish FVG, its grade meets minimum, and optional HTF filter permits bearish bias. A new bearish FVG must meet the minimum gap-height/ATR and middle-bar body/range thresholds. Entry is the signal bar close.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= TP3 OR elapsed bars >= maximum trade duration; if stop and TP3 are both touched on one bar, stop takes priority.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= TP3 OR elapsed bars >= maximum trade duration; if stop and TP3 are both touched on one bar, stop takes priority.
