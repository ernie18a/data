Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low of the first N bars of each entry session; use after those bars are complete
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the lookback ending on the current bar
Calculation: VWAP = session VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3, including the current bar
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close - open); close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: Structural long/short stop = breakout level -/+ ATR × stop buffer; stop distance = close - long stop / short stop - close
Calculation: Final target distance = min(stop distance × aggressive target multiple, per-trade profit cap / (quantity × point value)); target is valid when this distance ≥ stop distance × minimum target multiple
Long: Flat, within the London 07:00–10:00 or New York 13:30–16:00 entry session (Europe/London time), trading allowed, opening range complete, and armed; close > opening range high AND high > opening range high AND close > open AND close location ≥ breakout-strength threshold AND candle body ≥ ATR × minimum-body multiple AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP). Enter exactly the configured delay bars after this signal if still in the entry session and trading allowed, flat, close > breakout level AND close > structural stop, minimum stop distance ≤ stop distance ≤ maximum stop distance, quantity ≥ 1 with actual risk ≤ hard risk cap and actual risk < remaining drawdown - safety buffer, close ≤ signal close + (signal close - structural stop) × maximum chase multiple, and final target valid. Quantity = floor(minimum of risk budget and hard risk cap / (stop distance × point value)). Re-arm after a close inside the completed opening range while flat; disarm on signal; reset at each session.
Short: Flat, within the London 07:00–10:00 or New York 13:30–16:00 entry session (Europe/London time), trading allowed, opening range complete, and armed; close < opening range low AND low < opening range low AND close < open AND close location ≤ 1 - breakout-strength threshold AND candle body ≥ ATR × minimum-body multiple AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP). Enter exactly the configured delay bars after this signal if still in the entry session and trading allowed, flat, close < breakout level AND close < structural stop, minimum stop distance ≤ stop distance ≤ maximum stop distance, quantity ≥ 1 with actual risk ≤ hard risk cap and actual risk < remaining drawdown - safety buffer, close ≥ signal close - (structural stop - signal close) × maximum chase multiple, and final target valid. Quantity = floor(minimum of risk budget and hard risk cap / (stop distance × point value)). Re-arm after a close inside the completed opening range while flat; disarm on signal; reset at each session.
Exit: Long, low ≤ live stop OR high ≥ target OR evaluation profit target reached OR drawdown from peak ≥ trailing draw limit - safety buffer OR outside the London 07:00–10:00 and New York 13:30–16:00 management sessions (Europe/London time)
Exit: Short, high ≥ live stop OR low ≤ target OR evaluation profit target reached OR drawdown from peak ≥ trailing draw limit - safety buffer OR outside the London 07:00–10:00 and New York 13:30–16:00 management sessions (Europe/London time)
Calculation: Long live stop = maximum of prior live stop and entry price + breakeven offset after favorable high reaches 1R, entry price + 1R after 2R, entry price + 2R after 3R, and each enabled dollar-lock price after its corresponding open-profit trigger; short live stop uses the minimum and subtracts those offsets from entry. R = entry-to-original-stop distance; dollar-lock price offset = lock amount / (point value × position quantity).
