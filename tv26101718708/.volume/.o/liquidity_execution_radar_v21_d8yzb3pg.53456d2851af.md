Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND high − low > ATR × displacement multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body threshold AND (close − low) / (high − low) > close-strength threshold AND volume / 20-bar average volume ≥ dead-volume threshold
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND high − low > ATR × displacement multiplier AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > body threshold AND (high − close) / (high − low) > close-strength threshold AND volume / 20-bar average volume ≥ dead-volume threshold
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low breaks below the previous completed day low, completed Asia-session low, or qualifying low-liquidity cluster by an ATR-scaled buffer, then close reclaims above that level
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high breaks above the previous completed day high, completed Asia-session high, or qualifying high-liquidity cluster by an ATR-scaled buffer, then close falls below that level
Calculation: Low-liquidity cluster = at least two lows among prior bars within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the lowest low in the cluster lookback; high-liquidity cluster is the corresponding calculation using highs and the highest high
Calculation: Bullish market-structure shift = close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high by an ATR-scaled buffer AND bullish displacement; bearish market-structure shift uses the latest confirmed swing low and bearish displacement
Calculation: HTF bullish bias = still-forming higher-timeframe close is above both its high-low midpoint and its 20-period EMA; HTF bearish bias = below both
Calculation: Bullish final score = max(bullish reversal score, bullish continuation score) + reward-risk score + regime contribution, capped at the weak-regime cap; bearish final score is the corresponding calculation. Reversal and continuation scores sum weighted points for their stated sweep/reclaim/displacement/structure/exhaustion/value-zone/regime/liquidity or HTF-bias/midpoint/volume/trend-day/ADR/compression conditions
Calculation: Reward-risk ratio = greatest of the three target reward-to-risk ratios, using the sweep extreme as invalidation; targets are previous-day midpoint, completed Asia extreme or previous-day extreme, and the nearer of the liquidity target and ADR boundary

Long: after a bullish sweep, within the confirmation-bar limit close > previous completed day low AND close > open; within that limit, bullish displacement AND (bullish market-structure shift OR close > previous completed day midpoint) AND volume is not dead; bullish final score ≥ entry threshold, with A/A+ grade if the only-A option is enabled
Short: bearish sweep
Short: bearish market-structure shift
Short: after a bearish sweep, within the confirmation-bar limit close < previous completed day high AND close < open; within that limit, bearish displacement AND (bearish market-structure shift OR close < previous completed day midpoint) AND volume is not dead; bearish final score ≥ entry threshold, with A/A+ grade if the only-A option is enabled
