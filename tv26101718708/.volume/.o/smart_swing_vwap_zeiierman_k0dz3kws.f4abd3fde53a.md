Calculation: Rolling swing direction = for each enabled lookback, +1 when the rolling highest-high bar is later than the rolling lowest-low bar, otherwise −1; a swing event occurs when this direction changes.
Calculation: Candidate anchor = the rolling swing low and its bar when direction is +1, or the rolling swing high and its bar when direction is −1.
Calculation: Candidate move strength = geometric mean of clipped directional progress per average true range, swing-range expansion per ATR, and volume participation, multiplied by 0.5 plus 0.5 times the smallest of those three clipped components.
Calculation: Candidate score = 100 × (0.25 × agreement + 0.20 × clipped swing range per ATR + 0.55 × move strength); agreement is the mean across valid candidates of same-direction agreement, where each matching candidate contributes the greater of its exponentially decaying pivot-price proximity (scale 0.5 ATR) and pivot-bar proximity (scale 3 bars).
Calculation: Active VWAP = recursively volume-weighted hlc3 from the selected anchor, with exponentially decaying weights; tracking speed is optionally adjusted by current ATR divided by its average ATR.
Long: On a confirmed bar, accept the highest-scoring valid bullish candidate if its score and move strength meet their minimums, the minimum bars since the last anchor have elapsed, it differs from the active anchor, and either there is no active anchor, its direction differs from the active anchor, the active score is unavailable, or its score exceeds the active score by the required margin; OR close crosses above the active VWAP, provided there is an active anchor and the bar is not also a new-anchor or polarity-flip bar; OR a bullish retest is armed, active polarity is bullish, the bar touches the VWAP zone, closes above VWAP, and closes at or above open.
Short: On a confirmed bar, accept the highest-scoring valid bearish candidate if its score and move strength meet their minimums, the minimum bars since the last anchor have elapsed, it differs from the active anchor, and either there is no active anchor, its direction differs from the active anchor, the active score is unavailable, or its score exceeds the active score by the required margin; OR close crosses below the active VWAP, provided there is an active anchor and the bar is not also a new-anchor or polarity-flip bar; OR a bearish retest is armed, active polarity is bearish, the bar touches the VWAP zone, closes below VWAP, and closes at or below open.
