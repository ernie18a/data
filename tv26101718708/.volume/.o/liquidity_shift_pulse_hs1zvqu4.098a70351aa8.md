Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after configurable left and right bars; use only the latest confirmed pivot available before the current bar.
Calculation: Lower/upper wick ratio = lower/upper wick length divided by candle range; body ratio = absolute body divided by candle range; close strength = (close − low)/(high − low) for bullish candles and (high − close)/(high − low) for bearish candles.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the moving average of volume over a configurable lookback, excluding the current bar; accept when volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log(sum of true range over a configurable lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) ÷ log(lookback).
Calculation: Internal structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over a configurable lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Protected structure = initial setup low or prior-range low minus an ATR buffer; thereafter, raise it to confirmed post-entry pivot lows minus their ATR buffers, never lowering it.
Long: confirmed bar AND (low < latest confirmed swing low − ATR × sweep penetration AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND lower-wick ratio ≥ minimum sweep ratio OR active bullish sweep setup within its age limit AND close > stored internal structure high + ATR × structure buffer AND close > open AND candle range / ATR ≥ minimum displacement AND body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND bullish close strength ≥ minimum close strength OR close > highest high of the prior expansion lookback + ATR × expansion buffer AND close > open AND candle range / ATR ≥ minimum expansion range AND body ratio ≥ minimum expansion body ratio AND bullish close strength ≥ minimum expansion close strength) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable OR relative volume ≥ threshold) AND (regime filter disabled OR Choppiness Index ≤ directional threshold OR (Choppiness Index ≤ transition threshold AND below its prior value)); expansion entry also requires expansion enabled and, when regime filtering is enabled, Choppiness Index below its prior value.
Short: high > latest confirmed swing high + ATR × sweep penetration AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND upper-wick ratio ≥ minimum sweep ratio.
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND (close < protected structure OR active bearish sweep setup within its age limit AND close < stored internal structure low − ATR × structure buffer AND close < open AND candle range / ATR ≥ minimum displacement AND body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND bearish close strength ≥ minimum close strength OR large-trend protection enabled AND maximum high since entry − entry price ≥ activation ATR × ATR at entry AND giveback from that maximum to current close ≥ configured percentage AND close < open AND close < prior close).
