Calculation: Demand block = create a zone from either (1) a confirmed swing low whose lower-wick share is at least the configured minimum, or (2) a failed sweep of the latest confirmed swing low where low < swing low, close > swing low, and lower-wick share is at least the configured minimum. For a zone from a candle, bottom = its low and top = its low + clamp(abs(low − min(open, close)), ATR × configured minimum height, ATR × configured maximum height).
Calculation: Supply block = create a zone from either (1) a confirmed swing high whose upper-wick share is at least the configured minimum, or (2) a failed sweep of the latest confirmed swing high where high > swing high, close < swing high, and upper-wick share is at least the configured minimum. For a zone from a candle, top = its high and bottom = its high − clamp(abs(high − max(open, close)), ATR × configured minimum height, ATR × configured maximum height).
Long: a retained, unexpired demand block exists AND current bar is after its creation bar AND high >= block bottom AND low <= block top AND close > block top AND close > open AND close >= block bottom
Short: a retained, unexpired supply block exists AND current bar is after its creation bar AND high >= block bottom AND low <= block top AND close < block bottom AND close < open AND close <= block top
