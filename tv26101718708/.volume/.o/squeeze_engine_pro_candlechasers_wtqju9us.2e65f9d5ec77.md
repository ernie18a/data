Calculation: PRO/Early momentum = linear regression of (close − ((highest high + lowest low over the Keltner lookback)/2 + simple moving average of close over that lookback)/2) over the Keltner lookback; Classic momentum = close − close 12 bars earlier
Calculation: Rising momentum = momentum > prior bar’s momentum; falling momentum = momentum < prior bar’s momentum. Bullish acceleration = rising momentum and its change from the prior bar is greater than the prior bar’s change; bearish acceleration = falling momentum and its change is less than the prior bar’s change
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands are wholly inside Keltner Channels; PRO/Early use loose, medium, or deep channel widths, while Classic uses the medium width. Release = squeeze ends on the current bar. Fresh release = current bar is outside a squeeze, no more than the release-window lookback bars after release, and the prior squeeze lasted at least the minimum duration
Calculation: Trend = close above/below the local EMA, and, when enabled for PRO/Early, the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar’s close above/below its EMA of the same length
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / 20-bar average volume. When volume is unavailable, its filter passes and it is excluded from the score
Calculation: Directional score = round(100 × direction’s points / maximum active points). Points: squeeze 25 if its duration was at least twice the minimum, otherwise 18 for a fresh release; while squeezed, 25 deep, 18 medium, or 10 loose; trend 25; momentum 20 for acceleration in that direction, otherwise 10 if momentum is moving that way; volume 15 if relative volume meets its threshold, otherwise 7 if it is at least 80% of the threshold; ADX/DI 15 if ADX meets its threshold and DI favors that direction. Maximum points = 45 + 25 when squeeze scoring is active + 15 when volume scoring is active + 15 when ADX filtering is enabled. Squeeze scoring is inactive for permitted entries outside a squeeze and release window
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is lower than the prior pivot low, while momentum at the pivot is higher; bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high is higher than the prior pivot high, while momentum at the pivot is lower. Compare only with a prior same-side pivot within the maximum pivot distance; confirm after the configured right-side pivot bars
Long: bullish divergence
Long: confirmed bar and either Classic: release and (momentum > 0, or, when slope mode is enabled, rising momentum); or PRO/Early: eligible setup (fresh release, or Early while squeezed for at least the minimum duration, or permitted no-squeeze entry), momentum meets the zero-side requirement if enabled, bullish trend, passing enabled ADX/DI and relative-volume filters, directional impulse (Early: rising; standard PRO: bullish acceleration; stronger-confirmation PRO: bullish acceleration and momentum rose on each of the prior two bars), and long score meets its threshold. Require flat position, no exit or cooldown, positive ATR, and either an untraded squeeze episode or, for permitted no-squeeze entries, a new readiness transition
Long: confirmed bar in a squeeze lasting at least the minimum duration, rising momentum, momentum above its value two bars earlier, and bullish trend
Short: bearish divergence
Short: confirmed bar and either Classic: release and (momentum < 0, or, when slope mode is enabled, falling momentum); or PRO/Early: eligible setup (fresh release, or Early while squeezed for at least the minimum duration, or permitted no-squeeze entry), momentum meets the zero-side requirement if enabled, bearish trend, passing enabled ADX/DI and relative-volume filters, directional impulse (Early: falling; standard PRO: bearish acceleration; stronger-confirmation PRO: bearish acceleration and momentum fell on each of the prior two bars), and short score meets its threshold. Require flat position, no exit or cooldown, positive ATR, and either an untraded squeeze episode or, for permitted no-squeeze entries, a new readiness transition; suppress short entry when long entry occurs on the same bar
Short: confirmed bar in a squeeze lasting at least the minimum duration, falling momentum, momentum below its value two bars earlier, and bearish trend
Calculation: Initial risk distance = greater of ATR × configured stop multiple and minimum tick; Classic uses 2 × ATR. Long stop = entry price − risk distance; short stop = entry price + risk distance. PRO/Early target = entry price ± risk distance × reward multiple (add for long, subtract for short); Classic has no target. For PRO/Early trailing stops, update after the current bar’s stop check: long stop = max(previous stop, highest favorable high since entry − ATR × trail multiple); short stop = min(previous stop, lowest favorable low since entry + ATR × trail multiple). Updated stop applies next bar
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR (PRO/Early and high ≥ target) OR (Classic or fade exits enabled and falling momentum on this bar and the prior bar)
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR (PRO/Early and low ≤ target) OR (Classic or fade exits enabled and rising momentum on this bar and the prior bar)
