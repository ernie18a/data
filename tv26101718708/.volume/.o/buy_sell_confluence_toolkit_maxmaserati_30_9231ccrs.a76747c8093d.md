Calculation: Bullish and bearish scores = sum of the configured weights of indicators currently in their bullish or bearish state, respectively; neutral states contribute zero. Divide each score by the sum of all enabled indicator weights and multiply by 100.
Calculation: MACD state = bullish when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0; bearish when both are below zero/the signal line, respectively; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: KDJ state = compute raw K as 100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / (highest high − lowest low) over the same lookback; smooth K and then D with simple moving averages; J = 3K − 2D. State is bullish when K > D and J > K, bearish when K < D and J < K, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: RSI state = neutral at or beyond configured overbought/oversold levels; otherwise bullish above 50, bearish below 50, neutral at 50.
Calculation: CCI state = neutral at or beyond configured overbought/oversold levels; otherwise bullish above zero, bearish below zero, neutral at zero.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of source × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each selected anchor period; use the current, forming anchor period. VWAP state is bullish when close > VWAP, bearish when close < VWAP, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Moving-average state = bullish when close > the selected moving average and its current value is greater than its value two bars earlier; bearish when close < it and its current value is less than or equal to its value two bars earlier; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Bollinger %B = 100 × (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band); state is bullish above the upper configured %B boundary, bearish below the lower configured boundary, otherwise neutral. If Bollinger scoring is disabled, its state is neutral and its weight is excluded.
Calculation: Volume state = bullish when close > open and volume is at least the configured mid-tier multiple of its volume moving average; bearish when close < open and meets that volume condition; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: ATR trail = on a bullish signal initialize at low − ATR × multiplier, then trail at the greater of its prior value and low − ATR × multiplier; on a bearish signal initialize at high + ATR × multiplier, then trail at the lesser of its prior value and high + ATR × multiplier. ATR is calculated on the current chart bars.
Long: bullish score percentage >= configured buy threshold AND its prior-bar value < that threshold AND (bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled) AND (MA trend filter disabled OR close > moving average) AND (price gate disabled OR gate reference is unavailable OR close > selected gate reference) AND previous signal is not long. Gate reference is selected from anchored VWAP, ATR trail, or Bollinger basis.
Short: bearish score percentage >= configured sell threshold AND its prior-bar value < that threshold AND (bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled) AND (MA trend filter disabled OR close < moving average) AND (price gate disabled OR gate reference is unavailable OR close < selected gate reference) AND previous signal is not short. Gate reference is selected from anchored VWAP, ATR trail, or Bollinger basis.
Long: close > prior bar high AND close > open OR low < prior bar low AND close >= open AND candle is not a bullish or bearish expansion candle OR candle is not a bullish/bearish expansion or sweep candle AND close >= open.
Short: close < prior bar low AND close < open OR high > prior bar high AND close <= open AND candle is not a bullish or bearish expansion candle OR candle is not a bullish/bearish expansion or sweep candle AND close < open.
