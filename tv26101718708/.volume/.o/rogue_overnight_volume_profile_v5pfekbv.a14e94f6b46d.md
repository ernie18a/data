Calculation: Overnight profile levels = For each completed overnight session, distribute each bar’s volume across price rows in proportion to the overlap of its high–low range with each row (assign a zero-range bar’s volume to its price row). The POC is the center of the highest-volume row. Expand the value area from the POC toward the adjacent row with greater volume until it contains the configured share of total profile volume; OVAH and OVAL are the upper and lower value-area boundaries. Use the last completed overnight profile.
Long: During the New York session, after an overnight profile is complete, the first bar for which high >= OVAL − touch tolerance AND low <= OVAL + touch tolerance
Long: During the New York session, after an overnight profile is complete, the first bar for which high >= OPOC − touch tolerance AND low <= OPOC + touch tolerance AND close >= OPOC
Long: During the New York session, after a close crosses above OVAH, OPOC, or OVAL, the first later bar that touches that level within touch tolerance AND closes at or above it
Short: During the New York session, after an overnight profile is complete, the first bar for which high >= OVAH − touch tolerance AND low <= OVAH + touch tolerance
Short: During the New York session, after an overnight profile is complete, the first bar for which high >= OPOC − touch tolerance AND low <= OPOC + touch tolerance AND close < OPOC
Short: During the New York session, after a close crosses below OVAH, OPOC, or OVAL, the first later bar that touches that level within touch tolerance AND closes at or below it
