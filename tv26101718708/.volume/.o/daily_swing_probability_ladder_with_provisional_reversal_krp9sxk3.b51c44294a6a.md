Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars before and after it; known after the bars following it complete.
Calculation: Volume score = count of: volume / its moving average ≥ threshold; volume ≥ moving average × threshold; OBV above/below its value at the configured lookback (up/down); close >/< open. OBV adds volume when close rises, subtracts it when close falls, and otherwise is unchanged.
Calculation: Reversal magnitude = distance from the current swing extreme to close, measured both as a percentage of the extreme and as a multiple of ATR; apply the configured OR/AND rule to the two threshold comparisons.
Long: A confirmed swing low becomes the latest structural pivot (initial pivot, follows a swing high, or is lower than the prior swing low) OR structural direction is down, no provisional direction is active, the configured consecutive bars close above open, close exceeds the high of the configured lookback of preceding completed bars (if breakout filter enabled), close is above the fast EMA (if EMA filter enabled), upward reversal magnitude meets its configured threshold rule, and (volume confirmation is disabled or volume score meets its minimum) OR a provisional short is active, volume requirement is met if enabled, and at least the configured recovery score threshold is true among: upward recovery magnitude meets its threshold rule; close is above fast EMA; at least lookback−1 of the configured prior close comparisons are higher; at least lookback−1 prior bars have both a higher high and a low no lower than the preceding bar; close or high exceeds the preceding configured recovery-lookback high; volume score meets its minimum OR a provisional long is active and low falls below its anchor.
Short: A confirmed swing high becomes the latest structural pivot (initial pivot, follows a swing low, or is higher than the prior swing high) OR structural direction is up, no provisional direction is active, the configured consecutive bars close below open, close is below the low of the configured lookback of preceding completed bars (if breakout filter enabled), close is below the fast EMA (if EMA filter enabled), downward reversal magnitude meets its configured threshold rule, and (volume confirmation is disabled or volume score meets its minimum) OR a provisional long is active, volume requirement is met if enabled, and at least the configured recovery score threshold is true among: downward recovery magnitude meets its threshold rule; close is below fast EMA; at least lookback−1 of the configured prior close comparisons are lower; at least lookback−1 prior bars have both a low lower than and a high no higher than the preceding bar; close or low falls below the preceding configured recovery-lookback low; volume score meets its minimum OR a provisional short is active and high rises above its anchor.
