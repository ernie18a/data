Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions: shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA; close > open and close exceeds the lowest low of the lookback before the current bar while closing above the candle midpoint; bullish candle body > ATR × parameter; on 60-minute bars, three consecutive bullish candles with the current low > the high two bars earlier; on 60-minute bars, prior bearish candle followed by close > its high; OBV rises; ATR > its longer-period SMA; close is within a parameter distance of the 61.8% retracement from the recent swing high toward the recent swing low; low ≤ shorter-period EMA and close > shorter-period EMA; two consecutive higher highs and close > longer-period EMA; current low is within a parameter distance of prior low and close > current low; close is below the midpoint of the recent high-low range; bullish candle body > ATR × parameter.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of true conditions: shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA; close < open and close falls below the highest high of the lookback before the current bar while closing below the candle midpoint; bearish candle body > ATR × parameter; on 60-minute bars, three consecutive bearish candles with the current high < the low two bars earlier; on 60-minute bars, prior bullish candle followed by close < its low; OBV falls; ATR > its longer-period SMA; close is within a parameter distance of the 61.8% retracement from the recent swing low toward the recent swing high; high ≥ shorter-period EMA and close < shorter-period EMA; two consecutive lower lows and close < longer-period EMA; current high is within a parameter distance of prior high and close < current high; close is above the midpoint of the recent high-low range; bearish candle body > ATR × parameter.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added when close rises from the prior bar, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged when equal.
Long: bullish score ≥ parameter AND 15-minute shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA, using the current 15-minute bar, AND current time is outside 00:00–05:59 Europe/Budapest AND the prior bar's raw long condition was false; raw long condition = bullish score ≥ parameter AND the other stated conditions.
Short: bearish score ≥ parameter AND 15-minute shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA, using the current 15-minute bar, AND current time is outside 00:00–05:59 Europe/Budapest AND the prior bar's raw short condition was false; raw short condition = bearish score ≥ parameter AND the other stated conditions.
