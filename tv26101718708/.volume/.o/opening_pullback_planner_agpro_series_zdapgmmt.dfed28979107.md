Calculation: Opening drive = aggregate the configured opening-session window or first configured chart bars of the day; on non-intraday charts, use the first configured bars of the selected week/month/year period. Lock from completed bars when the build ends.
Calculation: Drive side = bullish when locked drive close >= (drive high + drive low)/2; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Drive quality = clamp(0.44 × clamp((drive range / ATR − 0.45)/1.25 × 100, 0, 100) + 0.36 × clamp(directional close location × 100, 0, 100) + 0.20 × clamp((average opening-drive volume / longer-lookback chart-volume SMA − 0.70)/0.80 × 100, 0, 100), 0, 100). Directional close location is (close − low)/range for bullish drive and (high − close)/range for bearish drive. ATR is measured at drive lock.
Calculation: Pullback zone = for bullish drive, [drive high − drive range × deep retracement, drive high − drive range × shallow retracement]; for bearish drive, [drive low + drive range × shallow retracement, drive low + drive range × deep retracement]. The ideal retracement lies between the shallow and deep boundaries.
Calculation: Continuation trigger = drive extreme plus (bullish) or minus (bearish) an ATR buffer. Failure line = beyond the deep zone boundary by an ATR buffer, below for bullish drive and above for bearish drive. Target guide = beyond the drive extreme by drive range × target multiple, in the drive direction.
Calculation: Pullback score = clamp(0.18 × drive quality + 0.30 × depth score + 0.27 × recovery score + 0.10 × volume support score + 0.05 × volatility score + 0.10 × room score, 0, 100). Depth score peaks at ideal depth, declining linearly to 0 at shallow depth and at deep depth + 0.18; recovery score is the close's position within the zone, scaled 0–100 in the drive direction; volume support score normalizes current volume / longer-lookback chart-volume SMA from 0.65–1.35 to 0–100; volatility score peaks when ATR / its longer-lookback SMA is 1.00 and declines linearly to 0 at 0.60 and 1.85; room score normalizes target-distance / failure-line-risk from 0.75–2.00 to 0–100.
Calculation: Pullback touched = a bar's high >= zone bottom and low <= zone top. Track the greatest drive-direction retracement from the locked drive extreme since the first touch.
Long: drive quality >= minimum drive threshold AND drive side is bullish AND (drive quality >= drive-retain label threshold OR (pullback touched AND close >= zone midpoint AND (confirmation mode is not Close + Volume or Strict OR current volume / longer-lookback chart-volume SMA >= its confirmation threshold) AND (confirmation mode is not Strict OR target-distance / failure-line-risk >= strict room threshold) AND pullback score >= minimum pullback threshold OR close > continuation trigger AND pullback touched AND pullback score >= alert score threshold).
Short: drive quality >= minimum drive threshold AND drive side is bearish AND (drive quality >= drive-retain label threshold OR (pullback touched AND close <= zone midpoint AND (confirmation mode is not Close + Volume or Strict OR current volume / longer-lookback chart-volume SMA >= its confirmation threshold) AND (confirmation mode is not Strict OR target-distance / failure-line-risk >= strict room threshold) AND pullback score >= minimum pullback threshold OR close < continuation trigger AND pullback touched AND pullback score >= alert score threshold).
Exit: long, active plan and (low < failure line in Realtime Allowed mode, otherwise close < failure line).
Exit: short, active plan and (high > failure line in Realtime Allowed mode, otherwise close > failure line).
