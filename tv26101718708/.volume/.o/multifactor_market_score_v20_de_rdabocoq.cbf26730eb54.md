Calculation: Relative-strength score = weighted average of the available 252-bar percentile ranks of: 12-month return excluding the most recent month relative to the benchmark (longest lookback); 6-month and 3-month returns relative to the benchmark (successively shorter lookbacks); close divided by the highest close over the past year; and the relative-strength line’s change over 50 daily bars. Weights, longest to shortest components: 0.40, 0.20, 0.15, 0.15, 0.10; normalize by the weights of available components and clamp to [0,1]. Relative-strength line = close divided by benchmark close. Use daily bars, including the current bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Earnings-quality score = weighted average of available scores: clamp((year-over-year EPS growth + 50)/125, 0, 1) at weight 0.50; clamp((year-over-year revenue growth + 30)/80, 0, 1) at weight 0.30; and clamp(ROE capped to [-50,50]/30, 0, 1) at weight 0.20. Normalize by weights of available components. Revenue growth uses quarterly data when available, otherwise trailing-twelve-month data; ROE uses trailing-twelve-month data, otherwise fiscal-year data, otherwise net income divided by positive equity.
Calculation: Trend-quality score = normalized weighted average of available scores: clamp((close’s percentage distance above the 200-day SMA + 10)/20, 0, 1) at weight 0.40; clamp((50-day SMA’s percentage distance above the 200-day SMA + 5)/10, 0, 1) at weight 0.30; and clamp((60-day drawdown minus the allowed drawdown threshold)/absolute allowed drawdown threshold, 0, 1) at weight 0.30. Allowed drawdown threshold is the more restrictive of the configured maximum drawdown and -2.5 times 14-day ATR as a percentage of close. Use daily bars, including the current bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Total score = weighted average of available relative-strength, earnings-quality, and trend-quality scores, using their configured weights and normalizing by the weights of available scores; clamp to [0,1]. For index instruments, smooth the total score with a 3-bar EMA. For other instruments, use the unsmoothed score.
Calculation: Coverage = weighted fraction of available relative-strength, earnings-quality, and trend-quality inputs: for equities use weights 0.45, 0.35, 0.20; otherwise use relative-strength and trend weights 0.65, 0.35. Confidence = average of coverage and history score; history score is 1 for full required history, 0.6 for minimum history, otherwise 0.2. Full history requires the configured minimum history (up to a year); minimum history requires at least half a year when a year is required, at least three months when half a year is required, and otherwise passes.
Calculation: Buy filter = close above the 200-day SMA AND 60-day drawdown at least the allowed drawdown threshold AND, for equities, 20-day average of daily close × volume at least the configured minimum turnover; for ETFs, require that turnover only when volume data exists. Use daily bars, including the current bar while it is forming. Bonds, indices, and other instruments require only the first two conditions.
Calculation: Good-buy gate = full required history AND coverage and confidence each at least their configured buy minimum AND (benchmark exists when required and strict benchmark checking is enabled) AND (filter disabled OR buy filter passes).
Calculation: Strong-buy gate = good-buy gate AND relative-strength score at least its configured strong minimum AND trend-quality score at least its configured strong minimum AND coverage and confidence each at least the greater of the configured buy minimum and strong minimum AND 60-day drawdown at least the greater of the allowed drawdown threshold and the configured strong drawdown minimum AND (not an equity OR an available earnings-quality score at least its configured minimum).
Calculation: Good confirmation = good-buy gate AND total score at or above the configured buy threshold on this and the previous bar.
Calculation: Strong confirmation = strong-buy gate AND total score at or above the configured strong-buy threshold on this and the previous bar AND total score rising over three bars AND 3-bar SMA of (total score minus its value 20 bars ago) above zero AND close at or above the previous 20-bar highest close. Use daily bars, including the current bar while it is forming.
Long: Good confirmation AND NOT good confirmation on the previous bar AND NOT strong confirmation.
Long: Strong confirmation AND NOT strong confirmation on the previous bar.
Exit: long, total score crosses below the configured buy threshold AND minimum history is met AND coverage is at least its configured watch minimum AND confidence is at least its configured watch minimum AND benchmark exists when required and strict benchmark checking is enabled.
Exit: long, total score crosses below the configured fail threshold.
