Calculation: Demand zone = latest confirmed pivot-low zone; its lower edge is the pivot low minus ATR × zone-width multiple, and its upper edge is the pivot low plus ATR × zone-width multiple. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks; set zone edges when the pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Supply zone = latest confirmed pivot-high zone; its lower edge is the pivot high minus ATR × zone-width multiple, and its upper edge is the pivot high plus ATR × zone-width multiple. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks; set zone edges when the pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: CPR pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed CPR-timeframe bar.
Long: low <= demand-zone upper edge AND low >= demand-zone lower edge minus ATR × bounce-distance multiple AND close > open AND close > previous bar's low AND EMA fast crossed above EMA slow on the current bar or one of the previous 3 bars AND RSI > 35 AND RSI < 65 AND volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiple AND close > CPR pivot
Short: high >= supply-zone lower edge AND high <= supply-zone upper edge plus ATR × bounce-distance multiple AND close < open AND close < previous bar's high AND EMA fast crossed below EMA slow on the current bar or one of the previous 3 bars AND RSI < 65 AND RSI > 35 AND volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiple AND close < CPR pivot
Exit: long, high >= entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple OR low <= entry price - ATR × stop-loss multiple
Exit: short, low <= entry price - ATR × take-profit multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiple
