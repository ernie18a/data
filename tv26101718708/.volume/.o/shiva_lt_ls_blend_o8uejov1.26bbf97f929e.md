Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme across configurable bars before and after it; available only after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Session VWAP and standard-deviation bands = volume-weighted typical price and its standard deviation accumulated from the start of the day; bands are VWAP ± 1 standard deviation
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range interval; use only after that interval completes
Calculation: Confluence count = number of these levels within a configurable percentage of price: VWAP, VWAP ± 1 standard deviation, locked opening-range high/low, previous completed day's high/low, session open, previous-day pivot point and first support/resistance, session Fibonacci 38.2%/50%/61.8%, current-timeframe EMA and SMA. Pivot point = (previous day's high + low + close) / 3; first resistance/support = 2 × pivot point − previous day's low/high. Session Fibonacci levels = running session low + (running session high − running session low) × 0.382/0.5/0.618.
Long: During the market session, current low < latest confirmed swing low AND current close > that swing low AND current close − low ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND that swing low has not already been swept AND the configurable sweep cooldown has elapsed
Long: On the configurable number of bars after a long sweep, close > the sweep bar's low AND close > open
Long: Low ≤ session VWAP AND close > session VWAP
Long: Opening range is locked AND (close > opening-range high AND previous close ≤ opening-range high OR low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high)
Long: During the market session, short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND close > session VWAP AND (absolute distance from close to the long-period EMA < configurable ATR multiple OR absolute distance from close to session VWAP < configurable ATR multiple OR opening range is locked AND (absolute distance from close to opening-range high < configurable ATR multiple OR absolute distance from close to opening-range low < configurable ATR multiple)) AND close > open
Short: During the market session, current high > latest confirmed swing high AND current close < that swing high AND high − current close ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND that swing high has not already been swept AND the configurable sweep cooldown has elapsed
Short: On the configurable number of bars after a short sweep, close < the sweep bar's high AND close < open
Short: High ≥ session VWAP AND close < session VWAP
Short: Opening range is locked AND (close < opening-range low AND previous close ≥ opening-range low OR high ≥ opening-range low AND close < opening-range low)
Short: During the market session, short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND close < session VWAP AND (absolute distance from close to the long-period EMA < configurable ATR multiple OR absolute distance from close to session VWAP < configurable ATR multiple OR opening range is locked AND (absolute distance from close to opening-range high < configurable ATR multiple OR absolute distance from close to opening-range low < configurable ATR multiple)) AND close < open
