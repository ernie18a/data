Calculation: Short-term SMA = simple moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the long-term SMA
Calculation: Long-term SMA = simple moving average of close with a longer lookback than the short-term SMA
Calculation: Bollinger lower band = simple moving average of close minus a multiplier times the standard deviation of close, using the same lookback for both
Long: Short-term SMA > long-term SMA AND close crosses above the Bollinger lower band
Short: Short-term SMA < long-term SMA AND close crosses below the Bollinger lower band
Exit: Long, short-term SMA crosses above long-term SMA to place a trailing stop; after price rises the activation distance above entry, exit if price falls the trailing offset from the highest price since activation
Exit: Short, short-term SMA crosses below long-term SMA to place a trailing stop; after price falls the activation distance below entry, exit if price rises the trailing offset from the lowest price since activation
