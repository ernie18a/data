Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursively update from the previous bar: if previous close > previous stop, max(previous stop, previous close − sensitivity × ATR); otherwise if previous close < previous stop, min(previous stop, previous close + sensitivity × ATR); otherwise previous close + sensitivity × ATR. Use the chart timeframe or, when enabled, the selected higher timeframe; higher-timeframe values may come from its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Trend cloud state = bullish when close > 20-bar SMA of highs, bearish when close < 20-bar SMA of lows, otherwise retain the previous state. Use chart-timeframe or selected higher-timeframe close, highs, and lows.
Calculation: Pulse oscillator = linear regression, over its lookback, of close minus its lookback SMA.
Calculation: Daily range position = (close − current daily low) / (current daily high − current daily low); use the daily bar still forming.
Long: (close crosses above ATR trailing stop AND ADX is above its threshold when enabled AND volume is above its 20-bar SMA when enabled AND RSI is > 50 when enabled AND close > 200-period EMA AND close > fast EMA when enabled AND the prior bar’s signal conditions are true when close confirmation is enabled AND bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown) OR trend cloud state is bullish OR previous close > ATR trailing stop OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low while the pulse oscillator at that pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot and the pivots are within the maximum bar distance OR (daily range position + RSI) / 2, clipped to 0–100, is >= 50.
Short: (close crosses below ATR trailing stop AND ADX is above its threshold when enabled AND volume is above its 20-bar SMA when enabled AND RSI is < 50 when enabled AND close < 200-period EMA AND close < fast EMA when enabled AND the prior bar’s signal conditions are true when close confirmation is enabled AND bars since the most recent long or short signal > cooldown) OR trend cloud state is bearish OR previous close < ATR trailing stop OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high while the pulse oscillator at that pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot and the pivots are within the maximum bar distance OR (daily range position + RSI) / 2, clipped to 0–100, is < 50.
