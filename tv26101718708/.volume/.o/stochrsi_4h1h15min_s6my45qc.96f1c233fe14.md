Calculation: Stochastic %D = SMA of SMA of the bar’s raw stochastic; use chart-timeframe %D and selected longer-timeframe %D. Compare the longer-timeframe value at the shorter historical offset with its value at the longer historical offset.
Calculation: RSI gate state = initialize downward; switch upward when the RSI-timeframe RSI SMA crosses above its lower threshold, and switch downward when it crosses below its upper threshold; otherwise retain the state. Use completed RSI-timeframe bars.
Calculation: RVSI histogram = EMA(RSI(EMA(cumulative sum of change(close) × volume)), short length) − EMA(the same RSI, longer length), calculated on the selected confluence resolution.
Calculation: MACD histogram = (short-length EMA(close) − longer-length EMA(close)) − EMA of that MACD line, calculated on the selected confluence resolution.
Long: flat AND chart-timeframe %D < long-entry threshold AND chart-timeframe %D > its prior value AND longer-timeframe %D at the shorter historical offset >= its value at the longer historical offset AND (RSI gate disabled OR RSI gate state is upward) AND (MACD/RVSI filter disabled OR RVSI histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > 0)
Short: flat AND chart-timeframe %D > short-entry threshold AND chart-timeframe %D < its prior value AND longer-timeframe %D at the shorter historical offset <= its value at the longer historical offset AND (RSI gate disabled OR RSI gate state is downward) AND (MACD/RVSI filter disabled OR RVSI histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < 0)
Exit: long, chart-timeframe %D crosses below long-exit threshold OR close > entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR close < ramp stop OR low <= trailing stop
Exit: short, chart-timeframe %D crosses above short-exit threshold OR close < entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR close > ramp stop OR high >= trailing stop
