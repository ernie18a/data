Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 from the current daily session’s first bar through the current bar; uses the current, still-forming bar.
Calculation: VWAP bands = session VWAP plus or minus a parameter multiple of the session’s volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3; uses the current, still-forming bar.
Calculation: Previous-session high-anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 from the bar of the previous daily session’s high through the current bar; previous-session high is from the last completed session, and the VWAP includes the current, still-forming bar. Previous-session low-anchored VWAP is calculated the same way from the bar of that session’s low.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: For A, B, and D, risk = the greater of the entry-to-signal-candle-stop distance and a minimum ATR-based distance; stop is entry minus risk for a long or entry plus risk for a short, and target is entry plus or minus a parameter multiple of risk by side. The signal-candle stop is beyond its low for a long or high for a short by an ATR-based buffer.
Calculation: For C, the stop is beyond the farthest low of a lower-band excursion for a long or farthest high of an upper-band excursion for a short, with an ATR-based buffer and minimum ATR-based risk; target is the session VWAP at entry.
Long: A: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session after at least K consecutive prior closes above session VWAP, the first touch since that uninterrupted run began (low ≤ session VWAP) closes above session VWAP; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Long: B: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session, after at least K consecutive prior closes below session VWAP, close crosses above session VWAP to arm a break; within W subsequent bars, before any close back below session VWAP, a bar touches session VWAP (low ≤ session VWAP) and closes above it; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Long: C: on a confirmed bar after at least K bars have elapsed in the current daily session, a close below the lower VWAP band is followed by the first close back at or above that band while still below session VWAP; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Long: D: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session after at least K consecutive prior closes above the previous-session low-anchored VWAP, the first touch since that uninterrupted run began (low ≤ that VWAP) closes above it; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Short: A: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session after at least K consecutive prior closes below session VWAP, the first touch since that uninterrupted run began (high ≥ session VWAP) closes below session VWAP; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Short: B: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session, after at least K consecutive prior closes above session VWAP, close crosses below session VWAP to arm a break; within W subsequent bars, before any close back above session VWAP, a bar touches session VWAP (high ≥ session VWAP) and closes below it; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Short: C: on a confirmed bar after at least K bars have elapsed in the current daily session, a close above the upper VWAP band is followed by the first close back at or below that band while still above session VWAP; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Short: D: on a confirmed bar in the current daily session after at least K consecutive prior closes below the previous-session high-anchored VWAP, the first touch since that uninterrupted run began (high ≥ that VWAP) closes below it; the setup has no open position, volume > 0, and ATR is available.
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, open ≤ stop OR low ≤ stop OR (high ≥ target AND low > stop), or at the next daily session’s first open.
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, open ≥ stop OR high ≥ stop OR (low ≤ target AND high < stop), or at the next daily session’s first open.
