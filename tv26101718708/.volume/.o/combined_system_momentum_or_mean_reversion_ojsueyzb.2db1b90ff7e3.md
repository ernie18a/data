Calculation: Twin Range Filter = average of two range widths, each multiplier × EMA(EMA(abs(close−previous close), period), 2×period−1); the two periods are long and short. Trail the filter by the averaged width: when close rises, max(previous filter, close−width), otherwise min(previous filter, close+width). Trail upper and lower bands at filter ± width; the line switches between them when close crosses a band.
Calculation: Bullish TRF state = close > TRF line; bearish TRF state = close ≤ TRF line. For 15-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour, and daily market bars, use the last completed bar and its TRF line. For 15-minute volatility-index bars, use the bar still forming and its TRF line.
Calculation: Regime score = weighted percentage of bullish TRF states across 15-minute, 1-hour, 4-hour, and daily bars; use separate scores for the market and volatility index.
Calculation: Overextended up = close ≥ session VWAP + 2 standard deviations OR (close − overnight open) / previous completed day's ATR ≥ range threshold. Overextended down = close ≤ session VWAP − 2 standard deviations OR (close − overnight open) / previous completed day's ATR ≤ −range threshold.
Calculation: Strat candle type = outside if high > previous high and low < previous low; inside if high ≤ previous high and low ≥ previous low; otherwise 2-up if high > previous high, else 2-down. Bullish 3-2-2 = outside, 2-down, 2-up; bullish 3-1-2 = outside, inside, 2-up; bullish 2-2 = 2-down, 2-up. Bearish patterns reverse the 2-up and 2-down directions.
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = session-anchored VWAP of typical price (high+low+close)/3 ± one or two standard deviations.
Long: NY time is 9:45 through 12:00 AND (confirmed 1-minute close crosses above its TRF line AND last completed 15-minute market bar is bullish AND (last completed 1-hour or 4-hour market bar is bullish) AND forming 15-minute volatility-index bar is bearish AND NOT overextended up OR (bullish 3-2-2 or 3-1-2 AND any open or close of its three pattern bars ≤ that bar's session VWAP − 1 standard deviation) OR (bullish 2-2 AND any open or close of its two pattern bars < that bar's session VWAP − 2 standard deviations AND overextended down)) AND forming 15-minute volatility-index bar is bearish; OR market regime score > high threshold AND (volatility-index regime score > high threshold OR < low threshold).
Short: NY time is 9:45 through 12:00 AND (confirmed 1-minute close crosses below its TRF line AND last completed 15-minute market bar is bearish AND forming 15-minute volatility-index bar is bullish OR (bearish 3-2-2 or 3-1-2 AND any open or close of its three pattern bars ≥ that bar's session VWAP + 1 standard deviation AND (forming 15-minute volatility-index bar is bullish OR overextended up)) OR (bearish 2-2 AND any open or close of its two pattern bars > that bar's session VWAP + 2 standard deviations AND overextended up AND (forming 15-minute volatility-index bar is bullish OR overextended up))) AND NY time is 9:45 through 12:00; OR market regime score < low threshold AND (volatility-index regime score > high threshold OR < low threshold).
Exit: long, low ≤ signal bar low − 2.2 × 1-minute ATR(14) at signal.
Exit: short, high ≥ signal bar high + 2.2 × 1-minute ATR(14) at signal.
