Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars ago; zone = [high two bars ago, current low]
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars ago; zone = [current high, low two bars ago]
Calculation: Bullish order block = on a bullish FVG bar, the nearest prior bearish candle within a configurable backward scan; zone = that candle’s low-to-high range
Calculation: Bearish order block = on a bearish FVG bar, the nearest prior bullish candle within a configurable backward scan; zone = that candle’s low-to-high range
Calculation: Zone quality score = sum of ten 0–10 scores: displacement/CISD (creating candle body-to-range ratio and range-to-ATR, plus whether its close crosses the prior candle’s open in the zone direction); liquidity sweep (whether, during the 15 bars starting at the FVG’s third bar or the order block candle, price swept the latest confirmed swing low/high or previous completed day’s low/high, or came within 0.3 ATR of that swing, plus a sweep of the 20-bar low/high); premium/discount (zone midpoint’s position in the rolling 50-bar high-low range, favoring discount for bullish zones and premium for bearish zones); market structure alignment (trend from confirmed swing pivots, favoring higher highs/higher lows for bullish zones and lower highs/lower lows for bearish zones); higher-timeframe bias (direction of the last completed configurable higher-timeframe candle); OTE confluence (zone midpoint’s proximity to the 62–79% retracement band, favoring the 70.5% level, bullish measured down from the rolling range high and bearish up from its low); overlap (overlap of more than 0%, 20%, or 50% between the zone and an active same-direction zone of the other type); volume (creating candle’s volume relative to its 20-bar average); session (creation time in London, NY AM, NY PM/Silver Bullet, Asian, or other session); freshness (zone age in bars)
Long: Bullish FVG OR bullish order block AND zone quality score >= configurable minimum
Short: Bearish FVG OR bearish order block AND zone quality score >= configurable minimum
