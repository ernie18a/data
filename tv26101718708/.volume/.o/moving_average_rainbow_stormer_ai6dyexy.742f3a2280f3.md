Calculation: Mean = arithmetic mean of 12 moving averages of close, all using the same selected moving-average type and lookbacks ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Uptrend touch = on a bar where close > Mean and MA5 > MA6 > MA7 > MA8, the first MA(i+1) for i=1…11 whose value is ≤ low ≤ MAi; otherwise undefined. Downtrend touch = on a bar where close < Mean and MA5 < MA6 < MA7 < MA8, the first MA(i+1) for i=1…11 whose value is ≤ high ≤ MAi; otherwise undefined. Use the prior bar’s touch for entry eligibility and the initial stop.
Calculation: Long stop = prior uptrend touch; if entry-bar low < prior-bar low, use the current uptrend touch if defined, otherwise the average of the prior uptrend touch and entry-bar low.
Calculation: Short stop = prior downtrend touch; if entry-bar high > prior-bar high, use the current downtrend touch if defined, otherwise the average of the prior downtrend touch and entry-bar high.
Calculation: Initial long target = entry price + |entry price − long stop| × target factor. Initial short target = entry price − |entry price − short stop| × target factor.
Calculation: Turnover long target = highest high since long entry. Turnover short target = lowest low since short entry.
Long: (flat OR short position) AND close > Mean AND MA5 > MA6 > MA7 > MA8 AND high > prior-bar high AND prior-bar uptrend touch is defined AND long stop > 0 AND initial long target > 0; for a short position, also require close < entry price if the profitable-exit option is enabled.
Short: (flat OR long position) AND close < Mean AND MA5 < MA6 < MA7 < MA8 AND low < prior-bar low AND prior-bar downtrend touch is defined AND short stop > 0 AND initial short target > 0; for a long position, also require close > entry price if the profitable-exit option is enabled.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ current long target; replace the initial target with the turnover long target when MA1 > MA2 > MA3 > MA4 AND close < Mean AND turnover long target > entry price.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ current short target; replace the initial target with the turnover short target when MA1 < MA2 < MA3 < MA4 AND close > Mean AND turnover short target < entry price.
