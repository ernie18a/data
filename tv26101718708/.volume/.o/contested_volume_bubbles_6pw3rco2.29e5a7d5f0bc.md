Calculation: Estimated buy volume = sum of intrabar volume × (close − low) / (high − low); estimated sell volume = sum of intrabar volume × (high − close) / (high − low). Use lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar when available and enabled; otherwise use the chart bar. Ignore zero-range bars when configured.
Calculation: Contested volume = the smaller of estimated buy volume and estimated sell volume.
Calculation: Net delta = estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume.
Calculation: Time-of-day trigger value = percentile-ranked residual of log(contested volume + 1) against a baseline from the last completed bars for the same and adjacent clock slots across prior sessions, adjusted by the current session's completed-bar residual average. Use the standard trigger instead when time-of-day normalization is unavailable or disabled: percentile-rank smoothed contested volume against prior chart bars.
Long: trigger value's percentile rank over its lookback ≥ the trigger threshold AND contested volume > 0 AND the activity floor is met AND (for time-of-day mode) the slot has enough prior sessions and the trigger window has valid history AND required intrabar data is available AND net delta ≥ 0
Short: trigger value's percentile rank over its lookback ≥ the trigger threshold AND contested volume > 0 AND the activity floor is met AND (for time-of-day mode) the slot has enough prior sessions and the trigger window has valid history AND required intrabar data is available AND net delta < 0
