Calculation: Macro liquidity high/low = selected mode’s latest confirmed pivot high/low (equal-length bars on each side), previous completed higher-timeframe bar high/low, or last completed session high/low.
Calculation: Grab extreme = running highest high after a high grab or running lowest low after a low grab; update on equal or more-extreme highs/lows.
Calculation: Wick percentage = upper or lower wick length divided by candle range; use 1 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Target/stop range = distance from the grab extreme to the opposite macro liquidity level, or 5 × ATR(14) if that level is unavailable.
Long: (close < macro liquidity low AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick percentage >= minimum)) OR (after a low grab, confirmed bar low > prior running lowest low AND (choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index <= threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR close > MA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR NOT (ADX > threshold AND +DI > -DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(14) >= oversold threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum > 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close > open))
Short: (close > macro liquidity high AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick percentage >= minimum)) OR (after a high grab, confirmed bar high < prior running highest high AND (choppiness filter disabled OR Choppiness Index <= threshold) AND (MA filter disabled OR close < MA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR NOT (ADX > threshold AND +DI < -DI)) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI(14) <= overbought threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum < 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA) AND (price-action filter disabled OR close < open))
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + target range × target fraction OR low <= entry price - target range × stop multiplier OR a valid short setup forms.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - target range × target fraction OR high >= entry price + target range × stop multiplier OR a valid long setup forms.
