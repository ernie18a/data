Calculation: Buy/sell volume = classify each lower-timeframe bar’s volume as buy or sell by close location, candle body direction, or their configured weighted blend; sum across the current bar’s intrabars. Use confirmed intrabars within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Bar delta = buy volume − sell volume; CVD = cumulative bar delta.
Calculation: Current-bar volume-profile shape = divide the current bar’s price range into 14 equal rows; distribute each intrabar’s volume equally among rows overlapped by its low–high range. Let POC be the highest-volume row and its location be its midpoint’s fraction of the current bar’s range. Mark rows at least 70% of maximum volume; shape is B if at least two rows below 35% of maximum lie between the first and last marked rows, otherwise P if POC location ≥ 0.62, b if ≤ 0.38, else D.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar’s buy-plus-sell volume / 20-bar simple average of chart-bar volume.
Calculation: Effort = clamp((relative volume − 1) / 1.5, 0, 1) × clamp(1 − absolute candle body / bar range, 0, 1).
Calculation: Short wick score = clamp((upper wick / bar range) / 0.50, 0, 1); long wick score = clamp((lower wick / bar range) / 0.50, 0, 1).
Calculation: Delta dominance = absolute bar delta / bar volume. Short trap score = clamp(delta dominance / 0.50, 0, 1) if bar delta > 0 and close is below the bar midpoint, else 0; long trap score = clamp(delta dominance / 0.50, 0, 1) if bar delta < 0 and close is above the midpoint, else 0.
Calculation: Short shape score = clamp((POC location − 0.50) / 0.35, 0, 1) for P shape, else 0; long shape score = clamp((0.50 − POC location) / 0.35, 0, 1) for b shape, else 0.
Calculation: Short divergence score = 1 if current CVD is below its prior-lookback highest value, else 0; long divergence score = 1 if current CVD is above its prior-lookback lowest value, else 0.
Calculation: Fade score = clamp((1.8 × directional wick score + 2.0 × effort + 2.0 × directional trap score + 1.6 × directional shape score + 1.6 × directional divergence score) × (0.55 if profile shape is B, otherwise 1), 0, 10).
Calculation: Fade walls = linear regression of the configured source over one shared channel lookback, plus/minus its standard deviation over that same lookback multiplied by the configured upper/lower wall factors.
Long: current bar is confirmed AND it contains lower-timeframe bars AND high > low AND bar volume > 0 AND low is below the lowest low of the preceding local-extreme lookback AND low ≤ lower fade wall AND lower wick ≥ upper wick AND profile shape ≠ B AND fade score ≥ configured minimum conviction AND cooldown since the preceding fade has elapsed.
Short: current bar is confirmed AND it contains lower-timeframe bars AND high > low AND bar volume > 0 AND high is above the highest high of the preceding local-extreme lookback AND high ≥ upper fade wall AND upper wick ≥ lower wick AND profile shape ≠ B AND fade score ≥ configured minimum conviction AND cooldown since the preceding fade has elapsed.
