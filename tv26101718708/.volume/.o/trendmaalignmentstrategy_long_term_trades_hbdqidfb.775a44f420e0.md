Calculation: Alignment = Assign +1 when close > shortest MA > next-shortest MA > … > longest MA; assign −1 when all inequalities are reversed. Otherwise count the satisfied upward comparisons among close > shortest MA and each successive MA pair: assign +0.5 if count > 5, −0.5 if count < 2, +0.25 if count > 3, else −0.25. The ordered MA sequence runs from shorter to longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Alignment high/low = highest/lowest Alignment over the same lookback.
Calculation: New high/low = highest high / lowest low over the shorter lookback equals highest high / lowest low over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Yearly high filter = True if the prior completed 12-month bar’s high is higher than both its values 365 and 730 chart bars earlier, OR close exceeds max(prior completed 12-month high, min(those two earlier values)); if either earlier value is unavailable, require only close above the available prior annual high values; if the prior annual high is unavailable, True.
Calculation: Yearly low filter = True if the prior completed 12-month bar’s low is lower than both its values 365 and 730 chart bars earlier, OR close is below min(prior completed 12-month low, max(those two earlier values)); if either earlier value is unavailable, require only close below the available prior annual low values; if the prior annual low is unavailable, True.
Calculation: ATR-based stop = For a long signal or long position, long stop is close minus stop distance × ATR and short stop is close plus compound distance × ATR; keep the prior long stop if the new long stop is lower or close is below the prior short stop, and keep the prior short stop if it is lower than the new short stop or close is below it. For a short signal or short position, long stop is close minus compound distance × ATR and short stop is close plus stop distance × ATR; keep the prior short stop if the new short stop is higher or close is above the prior long stop, and keep the prior long stop if it is higher than the new long stop or close is above it. Otherwise use the new stop values. Use current-bar ATR.
Long: (Alignment low > 0 OR Alignment high = +1) AND New high AND close − reentry distance × ATR > ATR-based long stop AND Yearly high filter
Short: (Alignment high < 0 OR Alignment low = −1) AND New low AND close + reentry distance × ATR < ATR-based short stop AND Yearly low filter
Exit: long, low <= ATR-based long stop OR (opposite short signal AND exit-on-signal enabled)
Exit: short, high >= ATR-based short stop OR (opposite long signal AND exit-on-signal enabled)
