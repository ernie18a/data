Calculation: Defended resistance/support = group confirmed pivot highs/lows, respectively, when each new pivot is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the current level; set a new level otherwise, and update a grouped level to the touch-count-weighted mean. A pivot is confirmed after the specified number of bars on its right. A level is live when its touch count meets the minimum and its latest pivot is no older than the maximum age.
Calculation: Breakout quality score = 30×clamp((relative volume−1)/(full-score relative volume−1),0,1) + 20×clamp(body efficiency/minimum body efficiency,0,1) + 15×clamp((directional close location−0.50)/(minimum close location−0.50),0,1) + 20×clamp(break distance in ATR/full break distance in ATR,0,1) + 10×clamp(candle range in ATR/full expansion in ATR,0,1) + 5×range quality. Range quality is clamp((preferred maximum range width in ATR−live resistance-to-support width in ATR)/preferred maximum range width in ATR,0,1) when both levels are live and resistance exceeds support; otherwise it is 0.5. Relative volume = volume/current-bar-volume SMA. Body efficiency = absolute(close−open)/candle range. Close location = (close−low)/candle range. Directional close location is close location for an upward break and 1−close location for a downward break. Break distance is the close’s distance beyond the broken level divided by ATR. Candle range in ATR = candle range/ATR. Use the same ATR for these normalizations.
