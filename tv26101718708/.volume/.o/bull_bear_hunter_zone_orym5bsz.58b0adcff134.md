Calculation: Main structure pivots = a pivot low is confirmed when the leg changes from bearish to bullish, using the low from the configured lookback bars ago; a pivot high is confirmed when the leg changes from bullish to bearish, using the high from that bar. A bearish leg begins when that past high exceeds the highest of the intervening lookback bars; a bullish leg begins when that past low is below their lowest.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low that is greater/less than the configured number of bars to its left and one completed bar to its right.
Calculation: Expanded body candle = absolute close-open exceeds the recent average absolute close-open, and both wicks are less than 36% of the body.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low and previous completed close are above the high two bars earlier, and the previous completed candle's percentage change exceeds twice the cumulative average absolute percentage change of new timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high and previous completed close are below the low two bars earlier, and the negative of the previous completed candle's percentage change exceeds twice the cumulative average absolute percentage change of new timeframe bars.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken main structure pivot high AND (the pivot is an internal pivot distinct from the swing pivot OR it is a swing pivot) OR close crosses above a confirmed pivot high while structure direction is bearish (bullish MSS) OR structure direction is bullish AND close is above a confirmed pivot high not already marked as bullish BOS or MSS OR bullish fair value gap forms OR the previous bar is an expanded body bullish candle AND current low > high two bars earlier OR open > previous close AND previous high > current low AND close > previous close AND open > previous open AND previous high < current candle body low OR close crosses above a confirmed swing high and creates a bullish order block OR a bearish order block breaker confirmation occurs OR close > 20-period EMA OR close > 34-period EMA.
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken main structure pivot low AND (the pivot is an internal pivot distinct from the swing pivot OR it is a swing pivot) OR close crosses below a confirmed pivot low while structure direction is bullish (bearish MSS) OR structure direction is bearish AND close is below a confirmed pivot low not already marked as bearish BOS or MSS OR bearish fair value gap forms OR the previous bar is an expanded body bearish candle AND current high < low two bars earlier OR open < previous close AND previous low < current high AND close < previous close AND open < previous open AND previous low > current candle body high OR close crosses below a confirmed swing low and creates a bearish order block OR a bullish order block breaker confirmation occurs OR close < 20-period EMA OR close < 34-period EMA.
