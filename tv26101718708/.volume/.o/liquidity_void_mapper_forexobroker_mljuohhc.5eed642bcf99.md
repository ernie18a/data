Calculation: Liquidity void zones = On each confirmed bar at the configured refresh interval, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high over the configured lookback into equal price levels. For each bar in that lookback, distribute its volume equally across the levels spanning its low to high. A level is a void if its volume divided by the average level volume is below the configured threshold; use the first qualifying levels up to the configured maximum. Keep these zones until the next refresh.
Long: Close is strictly inside a void zone AND previous close is at or below its lower boundary AND close is above the EMA AND volume is above its simple moving average AND the bar is confirmed AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal (initialized at bar index 0)
Short: Close is strictly inside a void zone AND previous close is at or above its upper boundary AND close is below the EMA AND volume is above its simple moving average AND the bar is confirmed AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal (initialized at bar index 0)
