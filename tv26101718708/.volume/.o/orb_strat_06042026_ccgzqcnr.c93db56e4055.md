Calculation: ORB high = high of the completed 15-minute bar from 09:30 to 09:45 Eastern; available after that bar completes
Calculation: ORB low = low of the completed 15-minute bar from 09:30 to 09:45 Eastern; available after that bar completes
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close − open) / (high − low) × 100
Calculation: Upper/lower wick percentage = (upper/lower wick length) / (high − low) × 100
Long: In the 09:45–15:30 Eastern entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, and the daily trade limit permits entry; high ≥ ORB high + alert buffer; VWAP filter passes if enabled (close > VWAP); higher-timeframe trend filter passes if enabled (last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA, and, if required, that EMA > its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar)
Long: On a confirmed bar in the entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, the backtest start-date filter passes, and the daily trade limit permits entry; breakout mode is active; close crosses above ORB high + breakout buffer (close > level and prior close ≤ level); close > open; candle body percentage ≥ minimum; VWAP and completed higher-timeframe trend filters pass if enabled; if a second close is required, the next bar must also close above the level and pass those filters; if retest is required, after the qualifying breakout, within the allowed bars and before a close below ORB high, a later bar must have low ≤ breakout level, close > breakout level, close > open, and pass VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters; otherwise, the stochastic filter must pass if enabled
Long: On a confirmed bar in the entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, the backtest start-date filter passes, and the daily trade limit permits entry; liquidity-sweep mode is active; low < ORB low; close > ORB low; lower wick percentage ≥ minimum; VWAP and completed higher-timeframe trend filters pass if enabled; stochastic filter passes if enabled
Short: In the 09:45–15:30 Eastern entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, and the daily trade limit permits entry; low ≤ ORB low − alert buffer; VWAP filter passes if enabled (close < VWAP); higher-timeframe trend filter passes if enabled (last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA, and, if required, that EMA < its value on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar)
Short: On a confirmed bar in the entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, the backtest start-date filter passes, and the daily trade limit permits entry; breakout mode is active; close crosses below ORB low − breakout buffer (close < level and prior close ≥ level); close < open; candle body percentage ≥ minimum; VWAP and completed higher-timeframe trend filters pass if enabled; if a second close is required, the next bar must also close below the level and pass those filters; if retest is required, after the qualifying breakout, within the allowed bars and before a close above ORB low, a later bar must have high ≥ breakout level, close < breakout level, close < open, and pass VWAP, trend, and stochastic filters; otherwise, the stochastic filter must pass if enabled
Short: On a confirmed bar in the entry session, ORB is ready, no position is open, the backtest start-date filter passes, and the daily trade limit permits entry; liquidity-sweep mode is active; high > ORB high; close < ORB high; upper wick percentage ≥ minimum; VWAP and completed higher-timeframe trend filters pass if enabled; stochastic filter passes if enabled
Exit: Long position, low ≤ stop level OR high ≥ target level OR the 09:45–16:00 Eastern management session ends; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, exit at the stop
Exit: Short position, high ≥ stop level OR low ≤ target level OR the 09:45–16:00 Eastern management session ends; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, exit at the stop
