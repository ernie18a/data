Calculation: Long score = count of satisfied conditions: bullish EMA stack (close above both EMAs, shorter EMA above longer EMA), close above VWAP, bullish market structure, volume spike, ATR within its volatility window, ADX above its threshold with no EMA compression, RSI above its long threshold, at least one bullish trigger (liquidity sweep, confirmed breakout, or break of structure), close above prior bar high, bullish higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and bullish FVG or order block.
Calculation: Short score = count of satisfied conditions: bearish EMA stack (close below both EMAs, shorter EMA below longer EMA), close below VWAP, bearish market structure, volume spike, ATR within its volatility window, ADX above its threshold with no EMA compression, RSI below its short threshold, at least one bearish trigger (liquidity sweep, confirmed breakout, or break of structure), close below prior bar low, bearish higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and bearish FVG or order block.
Calculation: EMA compression = absolute difference between the shorter and longer EMA is less than 0.3 × ATR.
Calculation: ATR volatility window = ATR is greater than its moving average times a minimum multiplier and less than its moving average times a maximum multiplier; the moving average uses the ATR lookback.
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume is greater than its moving average times a multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish market structure = close above the latest confirmed higher low, with a latest confirmed higher high; bearish market structure = close below the latest confirmed lower high, with a latest confirmed lower low. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks and only confirmed pivots count.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = current low below the highest/lowest-lookback prior-bar low, close back above that low, and close above open; bearish liquidity sweep = current high above the corresponding prior-bar high, close back below that high, and close below open.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish breakout = close above the prior-bar 20-bar high, volume spike, and candle body larger than its 20-bar average; confirmed bearish breakout = close below the prior-bar 20-bar low with the same volume and body conditions. A breakout is confirmed only on its breakout bar; exclude it if the following bar closes back inside the breakout range.
Calculation: Break of structure = close above the swing high confirmed one pivot lookback ago (bullish) or below the swing low confirmed one pivot lookback ago (bearish).
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low above the high two bars ago and the intervening close above that high; bearish FVG = current high below the low two bars ago and the intervening close below that low. FVG confirmation for the score uses the prior bar's FVG condition.
Calculation: Bullish order block = prior bar bearish, current bar bullish, and current body larger than 1.5 × its 20-bar average; bearish order block is the reverse.
Long: final long signal (in an enabled London or New York session, cooldown satisfied, and long score meeting an enabled signal-grade threshold, with no simultaneous short signal) OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish break of structure OR bullish FVG OR bullish order block OR bullish EMA stack.
Short: final short signal (in an enabled London or New York session, cooldown satisfied, and short score meeting an enabled signal-grade threshold, with no simultaneous long signal) OR bearish liquidity sweep OR bearish break of structure OR bearish FVG OR bearish order block OR bearish EMA stack.
