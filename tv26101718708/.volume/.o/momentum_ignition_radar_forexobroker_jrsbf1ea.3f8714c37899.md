Calculation: Ignition score = round(volume score + range score + close-extreme score + compression score), each component clipped to [0, 25]; volume score = min((volume / SMA(volume, lookback) / volume-spike multiplier) × 25, 25), using the current bar; range score = min(((high − low) / current-bar ATR / range-expansion multiplier) × 25, 25); close-extreme score = for close > open, max(min((((close − low) / (high − low)) − (1 − close-extreme threshold)) / close-extreme threshold × 25, 25), 0), otherwise max(min((close-extreme threshold − (close − low) / (high − low)) / close-extreme threshold × 25, 25), 0); compression score = max(min((compression ratio − prior average range / current-bar ATR) / compression ratio × 25, 25), 0), where prior average range is the average of high − low over the lookback bars preceding the current bar. For zero denominators, volume and range ratios are 0, close position is 0.5 when high = low, and compression ratio is 1 when ATR is zero or prior average range is unavailable.
Long: Ignition score ≥ score threshold AND close > open AND current bar is confirmed AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the last long or short signal (initial last-signal bar index = 0).
Short: Ignition score ≥ score threshold AND close ≤ open AND current bar is confirmed AND at least cooldown bars have elapsed since the last long or short signal (initial last-signal bar index = 0).
