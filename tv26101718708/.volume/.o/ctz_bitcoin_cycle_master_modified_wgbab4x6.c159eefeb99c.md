Calculation: Bullish StochRSI cross = StochRSI %K crosses above %D and %K was below its oversold threshold within the recent lookback
Calculation: Bearish StochRSI cross = StochRSI %K crosses below %D and %K was above its overbought threshold within the recent lookback
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while RSI makes a lower pivot high
Calculation: Year low = lowest low so far in the current calendar year
Calculation: 4-year target = anchor or last confirmed 4-year low plus the current target cycle length; after each confirmed 4-year low, update cycle length to the average of confirmed intervals
Long: A chart-bar pivot low is accepted when it is the first candidate, is at least the minimum spacing after the prior candidate, or is lower than the prior candidate; after acceptance, both a close above the EMA and a bullish StochRSI cross have occurred. Apply this with a shorter pivot lookback and shorter minimum spacing for the daily-cycle low, and a longer pivot lookback and longer minimum spacing for the weekly-cycle low.
Long: The bar's low is the Year low, unless it is within the minimum spacing of the last finalized yearly low and that prior low is lower; if within spacing and this low is lower, replace the prior yearly mark.
Long: A confirmed weekly-cycle pivot low is nearest the anchor date, or nearest the 4-year target after the target plus the settle period; in either case, if snapping is enabled, use the lowest confirmed weekly-cycle pivot low within the snap window. During the open snap window, replace the marked 4-year low when a lower confirmed weekly-cycle pivot low occurs.
Short: A chart-bar pivot high is accepted when it is the first candidate, is at least the minimum spacing after the prior candidate, or is higher than the prior candidate; after acceptance, both a close below the EMA and a bearish StochRSI cross have occurred. Apply this with a shorter pivot lookback and shorter minimum spacing for the daily-cycle high, and a longer pivot lookback and longer minimum spacing for the weekly-cycle high.
