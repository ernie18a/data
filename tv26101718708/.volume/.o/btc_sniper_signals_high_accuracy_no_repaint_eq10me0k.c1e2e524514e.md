Calculation: Trend structure = bullish when close is above each enabled EMA (shorter EMA above longer EMA where both are enabled) and enabled SMA; bearish is the inverse. EMA periods are ordered short to long.
Calculation: VWAP bias = according to selected mode: price above/below VWAP and rising/falling; strict mode additionally requires a VWAP retest within an ATR-based tolerance that closes back on the bias side; bias-only mode requires only the price side. VWAP slope uses a lookback.
Calculation: RSI confirmation = RSI above/below its midpoint, changing in that direction by at least a minimum delta, and not overbought/oversold. RSI chop = RSI within a configurable middle band.
Calculation: MFI confirmation = MFI above/below its midpoint and rising/falling; optionally require Chaikin Money Flow to have the same sign.
Calculation: MACD confirmation = selected combination of a fresh MACD/signal crossover or alignment and a positive/negative, rising/falling histogram; also true when the histogram has moved in the direction for two consecutive bars. Freshness is measured in chart bars.
Calculation: ATR and volume filters = ATR as a percentage of close meets its minimum; optionally volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier. Breakout volume requires volume above that average times a separate multiplier.
Calculation: Candle confirmation = body as a percentage of high-low range meets its minimum and close is in the configured upper/lower portion of the range.
Calculation: Support/resistance contexts = use recent confirmed pivot highs/lows, limited by count and age, and the nearest level on the relevant side. Bull context is a support bounce, reclaimed resistance, or confirmed resistance breakout; bear context is a resistance rejection, lost support, or confirmed support breakdown. A low/high liquidity sweep crosses the nearest support/resistance intrabar and closes back across it, with breakout volume and candle confirmation.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close above/below its EMA and VWAP, when each is enabled; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming. Higher-timeframe momentum = MACD histogram rising/falling and RSI above/below its midpoint on that timeframe.
Calculation: Chop = price is near both EMA and VWAP while MACD histogram is flat, or EMA compression and a flat histogram, or the ATR filter fails. Flat means absolute histogram is below a fraction of its moving average absolute value.
Calculation: Bull/bear scores = sum configured weights for, respectively, trend structure, VWAP bias, MFI confirmation, RSI confirmation, MACD confirmation, support/resistance context, higher-timeframe bias, and optional higher-timeframe momentum; add a fixed sweep bonus and an ADX-threshold bonus, then subtract a point when RSI is in chop. Score gap is the absolute difference between scores.
Calculation: Buy gate = support bounce OR reclaimed resistance OR bullish VWAP retest OR bullish EMA crossover with bullish structure OR bullish breakout OR low sweep OR close near/above EMA with bullish VWAP bias. Sell gate is the inverse using resistance rejection, lost support, bearish VWAP retest, bearish EMA crossover/structure, bearish breakdown, high sweep, or close near/below EMA with bearish VWAP bias.
Calculation: Regular readiness = selected mode's gate and confirmations: conservative requires matching trend structure, VWAP, MACD, and RSI; aggressive requires the gate and MACD, plus matching structure or an allowed breakout. Also require no chop, ATR and optional volume filters, no excessive extension from EMA/VWAP, and a confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Early readiness = EMA short/medium crossover, price and VWAP slope aligned, RSI beyond its directional early threshold, no chop, and optionally a fresh matching MACD cross; also require matching structure or breakout mode, ATR/volume filters, no excessive extension, non-flat MACD, and a confirmed chart bar.
Calculation: Bull/bear signal gate = regular or early readiness, cooldown elapsed since the last signal of either direction, score wins by at least the configured minimum gap, optional rising score, and that direction is enabled. With trend reset enabled, a direction is re-enabled after close crosses back beyond EMA short; otherwise it is always enabled.
Long: bull signal gate AND bull score exceeds bear score by at least the minimum gap AND (regular readiness AND bull score meets the buy threshold OR early readiness AND bull score meets one point below the buy threshold)
Short: bear signal gate AND bear score exceeds bull score by at least the minimum gap AND (regular readiness AND bear score meets the sell threshold OR early readiness AND bear score meets one point below the sell threshold)
