Calculation: Estimated delta = sum lower-timeframe volume signed positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and on doji signed by close versus the previous lower-timeframe close; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume signed by close versus open, with doji delta 0.
Calculation: Participation = the higher of the percentile rank of EMA(volume) and the percentile rank of current-bar volume over the same lookback.
Calculation: Truth Line = previous line + (close − previous line) × follow speed + fuel × lag compensation × (close − previous close), clipped so it does not overshoot close; follow speed increases with fuel. Fuel is the participation bell around its peak, floored at a minimum; on estimated-delta bars, delta agreement does not modify fuel. A bar is paid when fuel ≥ paid threshold.
Calculation: Delta-timeframe candle = sum of estimated chart-bar deltas in that candle; its price change is close − open. Use completed candles for history and the forming candle for live calculations.
Calculation: Block delta and block price change = sum of delta-timeframe candle deltas and their price changes over N consecutive candles. The live block includes the forming candle and preceding N−1 completed candles; earlier blocks contain N completed candles each. Average block |delta| = mean absolute delta of recent completed N-candle blocks; use the same delta source and a longer history than the three blocks being classified.
Calculation: Verdict = ABSORPTION UP if current block delta ≤ −absorption multiple × average block |delta| and its price change ≥ −flat tolerance × √N; ABSORPTION DOWN if current block delta ≥ absorption multiple × average block |delta| and its price change ≤ flat tolerance × √N. Otherwise BUYERS if all three blocks' deltas exceed the positive meaningful-delta threshold and their combined price change exceeds flat tolerance × √N; SELLERS if all three are below the negative threshold and combined price change is below its negative; CONSOLIDATE if all three absolute deltas are at or below the small-delta threshold; otherwise MIXED. For the working regime, use either the live verdict after it persists for the configured consecutive chart bars or the verdict from the last completed delta-timeframe candle, as configured.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = sum(hlc3 × volume) / sum(volume) over the chart bars spanning N delta-timeframe candles.
Long: price is below the Truth Line, the bar is unpaid, participation is thin, and |close − Truth Line| / ATR ≥ fade threshold; re-arm only after the gap falls below half that threshold.
Short: price is above the Truth Line, the bar is unpaid, participation is thin, and |close − Truth Line| / ATR ≥ fade threshold; re-arm only after the gap falls below half that threshold.
Long: close crosses above the Truth Line by the configured ATR hysteresis from at or below the line, the bar is paid, and estimated delta > 0; confirm immediately if delta magnitude meets the configured conviction multiple of its longer-lookback average and participation is mid-band, otherwise confirm after the configured number of chart bars holding above the line or at the next delta-timeframe close, according to the selected confirmation mode.
Short: close crosses below the Truth Line by the configured ATR hysteresis from at or above the line, the bar is paid, and estimated delta < 0; confirm immediately if delta magnitude meets the configured conviction multiple of its longer-lookback average and participation is mid-band, otherwise confirm after the configured number of chart bars holding below the line or at the next delta-timeframe close, according to the selected confirmation mode.
Long: confirmed upward line flip, working regime is BUYERS or ABSORPTION UP, participation at the flip is mid-band, the three-block memory has a consistent delta source, and close > rolling VWAP when the VWAP filter is enabled.
Short: confirmed downward line flip, working regime is SELLERS or ABSORPTION DOWN, participation at the flip is mid-band, the three-block memory has a consistent delta source, and close < rolling VWAP when the VWAP filter is enabled.
