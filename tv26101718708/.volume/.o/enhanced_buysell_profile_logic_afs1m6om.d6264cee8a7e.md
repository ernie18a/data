Calculation: Estimated buy share = candle-volume share estimated from close position, candle body, and whether the close is above or below the candle midpoint; split doji bars equally. Estimated buy/sell volume = volume × buy share / volume × (1 − buy share).
Calculation: Historical net pressure (%) = 100 × (sum of estimated buy volume − sum of estimated sell volume) / their sum over the selected rolling lookback or current session, including the bar forming.
Calculation: Profile = divide the high-to-low range of the selected rolling lookback or current session, including the bar forming, into price rows; allocate each bar's estimated buy and sell volume across its touched rows in proportion to body/wick overlap, with greater weight near the close. Row total is allocated buy plus sell volume. POC is the row with greatest total volume; VWMP is the row-midpoint volume-weighted mean. A buy (sell) imbalance row has buy (sell) volume greater than the other and absolute difference divided by row total at least the selected threshold; rows below the selected fraction of POC-row volume are excluded. A buy (sell) cluster is a run of at least the selected minimum adjacent eligible rows with buy (sell) imbalance at least the selected cluster threshold. Cluster bounds are its outer row edges; cluster score is the sum of row imbalance percentages. Use the highest-scoring cluster of each side.
Calculation: Cluster buffer = selected number of profile-row heights. Near a level means the current bar's high-low range overlaps the level ± buffer. Bear structure failure = close above POC + buffer while near POC, or above VWMP + buffer while near VWMP, or above the top of a sell cluster + buffer. Bull structure failure = close below POC − buffer while near POC, or below VWMP − buffer while near VWMP, or below the bottom of a buy cluster − buffer.
Calculation: Live structure score = +1 for close above POC and +1 for close above VWMP; −1 for close below each. Live flow score = +1 if live profile net-pressure percentage exceeds its threshold, −1 if below its negative; add +1 (or +0.5) for the higher-scoring buy cluster, and −1 (or −0.5) for the higher-scoring sell cluster, using the selected cluster-score cutoff to choose 1 versus 0.5. Bias score = structure score + flow score.
Long: A buy-imbalance profile row; or a buy cluster; or live profile net-pressure percentage ≥ its bullish threshold; or live structure score ≥ 2; or live flow score ≥ 0.5; or bias score ≥ 1; or historical net pressure changes from negative to positive; or historical net pressure is at least the strong-bull threshold; or absolute live profile net-pressure percentage is at most the selected low-net threshold and close ≥ open; or bullish absorption: a sell cluster exists, price previously armed absorption by touching its upper boundary + buffer, current low ≤ that boundary + buffer, close is above that boundary + buffer, close > open and previous close, and (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier); or bull reclaim: a sell cluster exists, price was armed by a prior close below its upper boundary, current close > that boundary + buffer, and (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier) and (candle-flip requirement disabled or close > open); or bear-pressure failure: strong historical bear pressure, bear structure failure, (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier), (previous-close confirmation disabled or close > previous close), and (structure confirmation disabled or price is near POC/VWMP or a sell cluster exists).
Short: A sell-imbalance profile row; or a sell cluster; or live profile net-pressure percentage ≤ its negative bearish threshold; or live structure score ≤ −2; or live flow score ≤ −0.5; or bias score ≤ −1; or historical net pressure changes from positive to negative; or historical net pressure is at most the negative strong-bear threshold; or absolute live profile net-pressure percentage is at most the selected low-net threshold and close < open; or bearish absorption: a buy cluster exists, price previously armed absorption by touching its lower boundary − buffer, current high ≥ that boundary − buffer, close is below that boundary − buffer, close < open and previous close, and (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier); or bear reject: a buy cluster exists, price was armed by a prior close above its lower boundary, current close < that boundary − buffer, and (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier) and (candle-flip requirement disabled or close < open); or bull-pressure failure: strong historical bull pressure, bull structure failure, (volume confirmation disabled or volume ≥ 20-bar average volume × selected multiplier), (previous-close confirmation disabled or close < previous close), and (structure confirmation disabled or price is near POC/VWMP or a buy cluster exists).
