Calculation: Bollinger bands = simple moving average of close ± a standard-deviation multiple of close; use the same lookback for all bands, with narrow, intermediate, and wide bands
Calculation: Prior-bar open/close position ratio = (prior open − prior close) ÷ (prior high − prior low)
Long: Supertrend direction flips from bearish to bullish OR buying volume > selling volume AND buying volume > current-chart-bar simple moving average of volume AND open > narrow upper Bollinger band AND open > intermediate upper Bollinger band AND open > wide upper Bollinger band AND Supertrend is bullish AND low + (1 − prior-bar open/close position ratio) < open
Short: Supertrend direction flips from bullish to bearish OR low < 20-bar simple moving average of close AND low < narrow lower Bollinger band AND close < intermediate lower Bollinger band AND 60-bar simple moving average of close crosses below 120-bar simple moving average of close AND selling volume > buying volume AND selling volume > current-chart-bar simple moving average of volume OR 3-bar simple moving average of close < 9-bar simple moving average of close AND 9-bar simple moving average of close < 20-bar simple moving average of close AND 20-bar simple moving average of close < 60-bar simple moving average of close AND selling volume > buying volume AND selling volume > current-chart-bar simple moving average of volume AND Supertrend is bearish AND high < 20-bar simple moving average of close AND close < narrow lower Bollinger band AND high < intermediate lower Bollinger band AND RSI is above its lower threshold
Exit: Long, unrealized profit > profit threshold OR RSI is above its upper threshold AND close > entry price × profit factor OR close < entry price × loss factor OR open > wide upper Bollinger band AND close > entry price × profit factor OR Supertrend is bearish
