Calculation: Filter EMA = EMA of close; short-channel upper/lower = EMAs of close with shorter/longer short-channel lookbacks; Vegas upper/lower = EMAs of close with shorter/longer Vegas lookbacks. If Vegas MTF is enabled, use the selected higher timeframe's current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Mid-channel upper/lower = EMAs of close with shorter/longer mid-channel lookbacks; long-channel upper/lower = EMAs of close with shorter/longer long-channel lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence for RSI, MACD histogram, and Stochastic = on a bar with both a confirmed price pivot low/high and oscillator pivot low/high (using the same left/right pivot lookbacks), compare the oscillator value at the most recent price pivot with its value at the most recent oscillator pivot: bullish if higher (or lower when hidden divergence is enabled); bearish if lower (or higher when hidden divergence is enabled). Apply the configured confirmation-bar condition.
Calculation: Delta = volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, otherwise zero. Delta bullish/bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low/high and delta pivot low/high, with delta at the most recent price pivot respectively greater/less than delta at the most recent delta pivot.
Calculation: Strong volume = volume > 1.5 × 20-bar average volume AND volume > prior-bar volume.
Calculation: Bollinger breakout = close crosses above the upper or below the lower Bollinger Band, bandwidth exceeds its expansion threshold, and prior-bar bandwidth was not below its squeeze threshold.
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish structure = respectively close > Vegas lower AND Vegas upper > Vegas lower AND close > Filter EMA; or close < Vegas upper AND Vegas upper < Vegas lower.
Calculation: Confirmed low/high pivot = low/high pivot using the configured left/right pivot lookbacks; BOS and breaker use three times those lookbacks, and MSS uses four times those lookbacks.
Calculation: Confluence score = rounded and clamped to 0–100 sum of: Vegas component (1.2 × Vegas weight × regime boost when either strong structure holds, otherwise half Vegas weight); bullish RSI, MACD-histogram, and Stochastic divergence count × divergence weight × 1.1; strong-volume component (1.3 × volume weight, otherwise half volume weight); MTF component (1.2 × MTF weight when MTF-divergence confirmation is enabled and the matching MTF bias and strong structure hold, otherwise half MTF weight); and Delta-divergence component (1.3 × Delta weight × regime boost when either Delta divergence holds, otherwise half Delta weight). Regime boost is 1.25 for either strong-structure regime, otherwise 1.0; the Delta boost is 1.3 in either strong-structure regime, otherwise 1.0.
Long: close > Filter EMA OR close > short-channel upper OR close > Vegas upper OR close > mid-channel upper OR close > long-channel upper OR strong bullish structure OR bullish Bollinger breakout OR bullish RSI divergence OR bullish MACD-histogram divergence OR bullish Stochastic divergence OR (confirmed low pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close > Vegas upper AND volume > 1.3 × 20-bar average volume) OR (confirmed low pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close < Vegas lower) OR (confirmed low pivot using four-times lookbacks AND close > Vegas upper) OR (confirmed low pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close > Vegas upper AND prior close < prior Vegas upper) OR (close > 50-bar SMA AND 50-bar SMA > 100-bar SMA AND 100-bar SMA > 200-bar SMA) OR (confluence score ≥ extreme threshold AND strong bullish structure AND bullish Delta divergence AND strong volume AND close > 240-minute Vegas lower AND 240-minute Vegas upper > 240-minute Vegas lower).
Short: close ≤ Filter EMA OR close ≤ short-channel upper OR close ≤ Vegas upper OR close ≤ mid-channel upper OR close ≤ long-channel upper OR strong bearish structure OR bearish Bollinger breakout OR bearish RSI divergence OR bearish MACD-histogram divergence OR bearish Stochastic divergence OR (confirmed high pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close < Vegas lower AND volume > 1.3 × 20-bar average volume) OR (confirmed high pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close > Vegas upper) OR (confirmed high pivot using four-times lookbacks AND close < Vegas lower) OR (confirmed high pivot using three-times lookbacks AND close < Vegas lower AND prior close > prior Vegas lower) OR (close < 50-bar SMA AND 50-bar SMA < 100-bar SMA AND 100-bar SMA < 200-bar SMA) OR (confluence score ≥ extreme threshold AND strong bearish structure AND bearish Delta divergence AND strong volume AND close < 240-minute Vegas upper AND 240-minute Vegas upper < 240-minute Vegas lower).
