Calculation: Bull/bear confluence score = short-term EMA-smoothed weighted mean of eight directional factor scores: WaveTrend (score bullish when oscillator > signal: oversold below zero, above zero, or otherwise; bearish symmetrically when oscillator < signal), EMA alignment (fast > slow > baseline, or fast > slow; bearish symmetrically), VWAP position (price beyond VWAP by more than ATR or within ATR; bearish symmetrically), RSI regime (bullish above 50, with stronger score above 60; bearish below 50, stronger below 40), MACD histogram (bullish above zero, stronger when rising; bearish below zero, stronger when falling), ADX (stronger above 40, moderate above 25, otherwise a nonzero base score), volume (stronger above its longer-term average with a directional candle, moderate above 80% of average), and squeeze (stronger on release with directional momentum, moderate while compressed). Use shorter fast EMA, longer slow EMA, and still longer baseline EMA; the other factor lookbacks are parameters. Smooth each directional score with a short EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs = EMAs of close on aggregated higher-timeframe bars, using lookahead-off values (last completed bar historically; current forming bar in real time); fast lookback is shorter than slow.
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR × stop multiplier; initial stop = entry price − risk for long or + risk for short; targets = entry price ± risk × each target multiple, in the position direction. After target 1, ratcheting stop is at least breakeven; after target 2, at least target 1; after target 3, at least target 2. After target 1, also trail at 0.5 ATR from the bar low for long or bar high for short; retain the tighter stop.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bull confluence score ≥ score threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA).
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bear confluence score ≥ score threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA).
Exit: long, low ≤ ratcheting stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ ratcheting stop.
