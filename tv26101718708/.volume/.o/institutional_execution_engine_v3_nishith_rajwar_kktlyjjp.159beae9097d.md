Calculation: VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3, reset at the configured session or daily boundary
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: POI score = points for relative volume, liquidity sweep, distance from VWAP in ATRs, efficiency ratio, and POI age; apply configured time decay when enabled
Calculation: Demand POI = confirmed pivot low with a subsequent rally greater than ATR and initial POI score at least the minimum; zone is from the pivot low to pivot low plus an ATR fraction
Calculation: Supply POI = confirmed pivot high with a subsequent drop greater than ATR and initial POI score at least the minimum; zone is from pivot high minus an ATR fraction to the pivot high
Calculation: Active POI = POI not invalidated by a close beyond its far edge, below the minimum active score, or older than the maximum age
Calculation: Touched POI = active demand POI with low below its upper edge and close above its lower edge, or active supply POI with high above its lower edge and close below its upper edge; select the touched POI with the highest score
Long: Demand POI is confirmed OR a touched POI is demand AND its current score is at least the minimum AND ATR regime is not compression AND session permits entry AND session PnL is above the loss limit AND no position is open AND bar is confirmed AND close > open AND (demand-zone upper edge - low) / zone depth < 0.5 AND close > demand-zone lower edge - ATR buffer
Short: Supply POI is confirmed OR a touched POI is supply AND its current score is at least the minimum AND ATR regime is not compression AND session permits entry AND session PnL is above the loss limit AND no position is open AND bar is confirmed AND close < open AND (high - supply-zone lower edge) / zone depth < 0.5 AND close < supply-zone upper edge + ATR buffer
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target; after high > entry price + ATR, stop is entry price. Initial stop is below demand-zone lower edge by an ATR buffer; target is entry price plus stop risk times the configured reward multiple.
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target; after low < entry price - ATR, stop is entry price. Initial stop is above supply-zone upper edge by an ATR buffer; target is entry price minus stop risk times the configured reward multiple.
