Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = previous cumulative value plus the current bar’s delta; reset it at the configured session, daily, or weekly boundary, or never if resets are disabled. Current-bar delta is the sum of lower-timeframe sub-bar volumes signed + for close > open and − for close < open; when unavailable or zero, use chart-bar volume signed by chart-bar direction, with doji volume signed + when lower wick exceeds upper wick, − when upper wick exceeds lower wick, and 0 when equal.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low that is the greatest/least within the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete. Pair each swing with CVD at the swing bar, and compare the latest two confirmed swings of the same type.
Calculation: One-bar CVD change = current CVD − previous CVD.
Calculation: CVD-change standard deviation = rolling standard deviation of one-bar CVD changes over the configured lookback.
Long: On a confirmed bar, cooldown has elapsed since the previous signal, and either (latest swing low < prior swing low AND CVD at latest swing low > CVD at prior swing low) OR (latest swing low > prior swing low AND CVD at latest swing low < CVD at prior swing low) OR (one-bar CVD change < −configured threshold × CVD-change standard deviation AND close > open AND CVD-change standard deviation > 0).
Short: On a confirmed bar, cooldown has elapsed since the previous signal, and either (latest swing high > prior swing high AND CVD at latest swing high < CVD at prior swing high) OR (latest swing high < prior swing high AND CVD at latest swing high > CVD at prior swing high) OR (one-bar CVD change > configured threshold × CVD-change standard deviation AND close < open AND CVD-change standard deviation > 0).
