Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side
Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Upward structure break = bar closes above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high plus a configurable ATR buffer; it is a CHoCH if the prevailing trend was down, otherwise a BOS
Calculation: Downward structure break = bar closes below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low minus a configurable ATR buffer; it is a CHoCH if the prevailing trend was up, otherwise a BOS
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = bar low falls below the latest unswept confirmed swing low and bar closes back above it
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = bar high rises above the latest unswept confirmed swing high and bar closes back below it
Long: An upward structure break or bullish liquidity sweep is selected by the configured anchor event, evaluated on bar close
Short: A downward structure break or bearish liquidity sweep is selected by the configured anchor event, evaluated on bar close
