Calculation: Support/resistance levels = up to 15 active levels created at the close from ln bars ago when that close is the highest or lowest close in the 2×ln+1-bar window; reject pivots identified as a swing failure or frontrun/overshoot of an active level. Count bars that open and close across a level and remove it when its break count reaches the maximum or its distance from close reaches 30×(ATR(20)/close×100/2) percent.
Calculation: Level test = for any active level, a bar opening below it, reaching above the level minus 10×sensitivity×minimum tick, and closing below it; or opening above it, reaching below the level plus that tolerance, and closing above it.
Long: Level test at support (open > active level, low < active level + tolerance, close > active level) AND no open position AND within the configured time window
Short: Level test at resistance (open < active level, high > active level − tolerance, close < active level) OR open below an active level and close above it
Exit: Long, level test at resistance OR (stop loss enabled and greater than maximum safety-order deviation and low < stop level) OR (trailing take profit disabled and high >= take-profit level) OR (trailing take profit enabled and trailing stop level reached after high >= take-profit level)
Exit: Short, (stop loss enabled and greater than maximum safety-order deviation and high > stop level) OR (trailing take profit disabled and low <= take-profit level) OR (trailing take profit enabled and trailing stop level reached after low <= take-profit level)
