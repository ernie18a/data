Calculation: Highest volume = maximum volume over the current bar and preceding lookback bars
Calculation: Candle body direction = current chart-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close and open when Heikin-Ashi mode is selected; otherwise regular close and open
Long: Highest volume > Highest volume on the previous bar AND candle close > candle open
Short: Highest volume > Highest volume on the previous bar AND candle close < candle open
