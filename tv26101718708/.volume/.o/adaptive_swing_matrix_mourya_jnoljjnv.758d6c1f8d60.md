Calculation: Per-timeframe trend state = on each of the 11 configured timeframes’ current bars, +2 when the fast EMA crosses above the medium or slow EMA and the slow EMA is above the baseline EMA; −2 for the inverse crosses with the slow EMA below the baseline; +1 when the fast EMA is above both medium and slow EMAs and the slow EMA is above the baseline; −1 for the inverse; otherwise 0. For each nonzero state, also require the matching RSI and MFI threshold, ADX above its minimum, MACD line above (bullish) or below (bearish) its signal line, and close above (bullish) or below (bearish) VWAP.
Calculation: Core swing score = sum of the signs of the trend states on the seven configured core timeframes, where each sign is +1, 0, or −1. Use each timeframe’s current bar, including the still-forming bar.
Long: On any of the 11 configured timeframes, trend state > 0 OR RSI ≥ its bullish threshold OR MFI ≥ its bullish threshold OR (MACD < 0 AND MACD > signal) OR (MACD ≥ 0 AND MACD ≥ signal) OR close > VWAP; OR core swing score ≥ 4.
Short: On any of the 11 configured timeframes, trend state < 0 OR RSI ≤ its bearish threshold OR MFI ≤ its bearish threshold OR (MACD < 0 AND MACD ≤ signal) OR (MACD ≥ 0 AND MACD < signal) OR close ≤ VWAP; OR core swing score ≤ −4.
