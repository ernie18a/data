Calculation: Upper drift = average of the most recent N confirmed pivot highs; a pivot high is a high greater than the highs of the specified number of bars before and after it.
Calculation: Lower drift = average of the most recent N confirmed pivot lows; a pivot low is a low lower than the lows of the specified number of bars before and after it.
Long: close crosses above upper drift AND prior trend is bearish
Short: close crosses below lower drift AND prior trend is bullish
