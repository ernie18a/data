Calculation: Fast, middle, and slow EMA = EMAs of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Long score = 2 × (higher-timeframe fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA and higher-timeframe close > middle EMA) + 2 × (current fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA) + 2 × (current close > current VWAP) + 2 × (long trigger) + 1 × (long structure break) + 1 × (lower-timeframe fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA and lower-timeframe close > lower-timeframe VWAP and current fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA and current close > current VWAP).
Calculation: Short score = 2 × (higher-timeframe fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA and higher-timeframe close < middle EMA) + 2 × (current fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA) + 2 × (current close < current VWAP) + 2 × (short trigger) + 1 × (short structure break) + 1 × (lower-timeframe fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA and lower-timeframe close < lower-timeframe VWAP and current fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA and current close < current VWAP).
Calculation: Long trigger = (fast EMA crosses above VWAP OR current fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA and close > VWAP and low <= fast EMA and close > open) AND (fast EMA slope is up when the slope filter is enabled).
Calculation: Short trigger = (fast EMA crosses below VWAP OR current fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA and close < VWAP and high >= fast EMA and close < open) AND (fast EMA slope is down when the slope filter is enabled).
Calculation: Long structure break = high exceeds the highest high over the preceding structure lookback OR close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high OR low falls below the lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback and close > open.
Calculation: Short structure break = low falls below the lowest low over the preceding structure lookback OR close falls below the latest confirmed pivot low OR high exceeds the highest high over the preceding sweep lookback and close < open.
Calculation: Chase block = current high-low range exceeds the average high-low range over the candle lookback multiplied by the configured expansion factor.
Calculation: Long stop distance = close minus the lowest low over the stop lookback.
Calculation: Short stop distance = the highest high over the stop lookback minus close.
Long: within the configured active session AND (long score >= configured minimum score OR C-grade trades are enabled) AND ATR >= configured minimum AND NOT chase block AND long stop distance > 0 AND long stop distance <= configured maximum AND long trigger AND no long signal within the configured cooldown bars.
Short: within the configured active session AND (short score >= configured minimum score OR C-grade trades are enabled) AND ATR >= configured minimum AND NOT chase block AND short stop distance > 0 AND short stop distance <= configured maximum AND short trigger AND no short signal within the configured cooldown bars.
