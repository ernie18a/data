Calculation: HMA = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half length) − WMA(close, length), rounded square root of length)
Calculation: Bullish confluence = count of bullish trend, bullish momentum, volume confirmation, and volatility confirmation
Calculation: Bearish confluence = count of bearish trend, bearish momentum, volume confirmation, and volatility confirmation
Long: HMA > previous HMA AND close > WMA(close, longer length) AND RSI > bullish threshold AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > previous histogram OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold AND shorter-period volume SMA > longer-period volume SMA OR ATR percentile rank is between its low and high thresholds, inclusive; bullish confluence >= minimum AND previous bar's bullish confluence < minimum AND last signal was not long AND bars since last signal >= cooldown
Short: HMA < previous HMA AND close < WMA(close, longer length) AND RSI < bearish threshold AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < previous histogram OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold AND shorter-period volume SMA > longer-period volume SMA OR ATR percentile rank is between its low and high thresholds, inclusive; bearish confluence >= minimum AND previous bar's bearish confluence < minimum AND last signal was not short AND bars since last signal >= cooldown
