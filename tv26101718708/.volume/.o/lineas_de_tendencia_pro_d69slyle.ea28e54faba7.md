Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it; use only after the bars after it have completed.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars before and after it; use only after the bars after it have completed.
Calculation: Rising support line = the straight line through the two most recent confirmed pivot lows, projected to the current bar; the later pivot low is higher than the earlier one.
Calculation: Falling resistance line = the straight line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs, projected to the current bar; the later pivot high is lower than the earlier one.
Calculation: Strong volume = current volume > volume simple moving average × a multiplier.
Long: close is within a configured percentage tolerance of the rising support line AND Strong volume AND close > open
Short: close is within a configured percentage tolerance of the falling resistance line AND Strong volume AND close < open
