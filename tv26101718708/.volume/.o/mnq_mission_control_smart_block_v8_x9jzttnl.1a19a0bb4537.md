Calculation: Fast, slow, and trend EMA = exponential moving averages of 5-minute closes with progressively longer lookbacks.
Long: A bullish reversal forms when at least the configured minimum of these four crosses occurred within the configured lookback: close crosses above fast EMA, fast EMA crosses above slow EMA, fast EMA crosses above trend EMA, or close crosses above trend EMA; the newest bullish cross is at least as recent as the newest bearish cross; and the bullish reversal state was false on the prior bar. OR, while that bullish reversal state holds, the configured number of consecutive 5-minute closes are above slow EMA, or within the configured post-signal window a bar’s low reaches slow EMA plus the configured ATR buffer and closes above slow EMA, causing bullish reversal confirmation.
Short: A bearish reversal forms when at least the configured minimum of these four crosses occurred within the configured lookback: close crosses below fast EMA, fast EMA crosses below slow EMA, fast EMA crosses below trend EMA, or close crosses below trend EMA; the newest bearish cross is more recent than the newest bullish cross; and the bearish reversal state was false on the prior bar. OR, while that bearish reversal state holds, the configured number of consecutive 5-minute closes are below slow EMA, or within the configured post-signal window a bar’s high reaches slow EMA minus the configured ATR buffer and closes below slow EMA, causing bearish reversal confirmation.
