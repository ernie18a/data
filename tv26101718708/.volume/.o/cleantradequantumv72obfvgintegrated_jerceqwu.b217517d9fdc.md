Calculation: Weighted bullish/bearish probability = weighted counts of positive/negative close changes across the prior lookback bars, with larger weights for more recent changes; confidence = the larger probability; momentum = their absolute difference; direction = the larger probability's side.
Calculation: Directional memory = prior memory × decay + signed (0.18 + 0.30 × momentum), clipped to [−1,1]; memory bias is bullish/bearish when it exceeds the corresponding positive/negative threshold. Preferred direction is probability direction when nonzero, otherwise memory bias.
Calculation: Bull/bear sniper percentage = percentage of seven conditions true: close above/below VWAP; RSI(14) above/below 50; MACD line above/below signal; EMA(9) above/below EMA(21); ADX(14) above 25 with close above/below EMA(9); volume above its 20-bar SMA with a bullish/bearish candle; and RSI(14) above/below 50 on the 5-minute bar (bar still forming).
Calculation: Bull/bear cluster votes and weights: probability direction (1 vote, weight 12), memory bias (1, 8), EMA trend (1, 8), higher-timeframe EMA trend (1, 10), close above/below VWAP (1, 8), MACD line above/below signal (1, 8), ADX above 20 with EMA(9) above/below EMA(21) (1, 6), bullish/bearish candle with body at least the configured fraction of its range (1, 6), volume above its configured SMA threshold with bullish/bearish candle (1, 6), sniper bias (1, 10), sniper cross trigger (1, 10), valid touched order block (1, configured weight), and fair-value-gap condition (1, configured weight). Cluster direction requires one side's share of all votes to meet its threshold; strength is the larger side's total weight divided by 108; conflict is the smaller vote count divided by all votes.
Calculation: Long/short score = 2 points for matching preferred direction; 1 each for matching probability direction, memory bias, enabled EMA and higher-timeframe EMA filters, enabled volume filter, ATR filter, enabled candle filter, strong matching sniper bias, close on matching side of VWAP, matching MACD, ADX above 25, matching 5-minute RSI, plus 2 for the matching sniper-percentage lead and configured bonuses for matching TMA pattern and cluster direction. Short score is calculated symmetrically.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = recursive ATR-based stop using the configured key value; UT long/short trigger occurs when the source crosses above/below it. KAMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of the configured source and lengths.
Calculation: Long/short stop = configured ATR stop or valid structure stop; in combined mode use the closer valid stop. ATR stop is entry signal close minus/plus configured ATR multiple; structure stop is the latest confirmed swing low/high minus/plus configured ATR buffer; apply configured tick slippage outward. Risk = absolute distance from signal close to stop. TP1 and TP5 = signal close plus/minus risk times their configured multiples. After TP1, trail from the highest high/lowest low since activation by the configured ATR-based distance.
Long: flat AND error guard permits long AND ( (session open AND chop filter permits entry AND cooldown/rearm permit entry AND long score meets its minimum AND long score lead meets its minimum AND long entry gates pass) OR (session open AND chop filter permits entry AND bullish sniper trigger AND long entry gates pass) OR (optional HFT layer enabled AND its long score and lead meet their minimums AND its enabled KAMA, CISD, TMA, cooldown, session, chop, and rearm gates pass) ); if both directions qualify, long is suppressed when short score is greater or tied.
Short: flat AND error guard permits short AND ( (session open AND chop filter permits entry AND cooldown/rearm permit entry AND short score meets its minimum AND short score lead meets its minimum AND short entry gates pass) OR (session open AND chop filter permits entry AND bearish sniper trigger AND short entry gates pass) OR (optional HFT layer enabled AND its short score and lead meet their minimums AND its enabled KAMA, CISD, TMA, cooldown, session, chop, and rearm gates pass) ); if both directions qualify, short is suppressed when long score is greater or tied.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= TP1 OR high >= TP5 OR opposite short signal.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= TP1 OR low <= TP5 OR opposite long signal.
