Calculation: Session balance high/low = maximum high and minimum low of the first configured number of bars in each configured intraday session.
Long: In the configured session, after the balance window is complete and before an expansion side is set, the selected confirmation condition is true above the session balance high: close exceeds the high by the configured break buffer; or, in wick mode, high exceeds the buffered high and close is within 10% of the balance range below the high; or, in strict mode, close exceeds the buffered high and the previous close exceeds the high. If both long and short confirmation conditions are true, close is at or above the balance midpoint.
Short: In the configured session, after the balance window is complete and before an expansion side is set, the selected confirmation condition is true below the session balance low: close is below the low by the configured break buffer; or, in wick mode, low is below the buffered low and close is within 10% of the balance range above the low; or, in strict mode, close is below the buffered low and the previous close is below the low. If both long and short confirmation conditions are true, close is below the balance midpoint.
