Calculation: VWAP zone = session VWAP ± clamp(ATR × zone multiplier, minimum zone width, maximum zone width)
Calculation: Chop risk = enabled and active-session VWAP cross count over the lookback ≥ cross threshold
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume over its lookback
Long: In the active session AND (close crosses from at or below session VWAP to above it OR a bullish reclaim is active, the lowest close over the hold lookback is above VWAP, and low ≤ upper VWAP zone and high ≥ lower VWAP zone and close > VWAP and close > open OR the prior bar confirmed that bullish retest and high > its high and chop risk is false and the elevated-volume requirement is disabled or relative volume ≥ its threshold); a reclaim is active only until it fails on a close below VWAP or times out without a retest
Short: In the active session AND (close crosses from at or above session VWAP to below it OR a bearish rejection is active, the highest close over the hold lookback is below VWAP, and high ≥ lower VWAP zone and low ≤ upper VWAP zone and close < VWAP and close < open OR the prior bar confirmed that bearish retest and low < its low and chop risk is false and the elevated-volume requirement is disabled or relative volume ≥ its threshold); a rejection is active only until it fails on a close above VWAP or times out without a retest
