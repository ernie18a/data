Calculation: Buy volume = volume × (absolute(close − open) + min(open, close) − low) ÷ (high − low), using 1 as the divisor when high = low
Calculation: Sell volume = volume × (absolute(close − open) + high − max(open, close)) ÷ (high − low), using 1 as the divisor when high = low
Calculation: Buy-to-sell ratio = buy volume when sell volume is 0; otherwise buy volume ÷ sell volume
Long: buy-to-sell ratio >= 1
Short: buy-to-sell ratio < 1
