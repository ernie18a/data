Calculation: Weighted price = high + low + 2 × close
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume ÷ moving average of volume
Calculation: Volatility normalization = configured rolling high − low range or Wilder ATR
Calculation: Buying pressure = moving average of volume ratio adjusted exponentially downward when weighted price falls from its prior-bar value; use the same lookback and moving-average type as the volume average
Calculation: Selling pressure = moving average of volume ratio adjusted exponentially downward when weighted price rises from its prior-bar value; use the same lookback and moving-average type as the volume average
Long: Buying pressure >= selling pressure
Short: Selling pressure > buying pressure
