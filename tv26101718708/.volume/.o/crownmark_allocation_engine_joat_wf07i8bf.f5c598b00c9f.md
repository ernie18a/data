Calculation: Regime score = 35 × signed range position of (fast EMA − slow EMA) over the slow EMA lookback + 10 × clamp(z-score of the fast-minus-slow EMA spread’s one-bar linear-regression slope over the volatility lookback, −2, 2) + 25 × clamp((ADX − 20) / 25, −1, 1) × sign(fast EMA − slow EMA) + 20 × clamp(2 × efficiency ratio − 1, −1, 1) × sign(fast EMA − slow EMA) + 5 × clamp(z-score of (close standard deviation / close SMA) over the volatility lookback, −2, 2) × sign(fast EMA − slow EMA). Efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over its lookback / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive baseline = KAMA of close: efficiency ratio is absolute net change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes; smoothing = (ER × (2/(fast parameter + 1) − 2/(slow parameter + 1)) + 2/(slow parameter + 1))²; each value = prior KAMA + smoothing × (close − prior KAMA), initialized to close.
Calculation: Corridor width = (0.65 × ATR + 0.35 × close standard deviation) over the width lookback, multiplied by a width factor. Upper/lower corridor = baseline ± width; inner upper/lower corridor = baseline ± half width.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = sum(typical price × volume) / sum(volume) over the auction lookback; if summed volume is zero, use the typical-price SMA over that lookback. Auction bounds = rolling VWAP ± (typical-price standard deviation × value-area-width factor) over that lookback.
Calculation: Pressure composite = EMA of pressure raw over the smoothing lookback. Pressure raw = 100 × relative volume × (0.40 × clamp((close − open) / true range, −1, 1) + 0.35 × tanh(4 × (close − open) / ATR) + 0.25 × clamp(((close − low) − (high − close)) / true range, −1, 1)); relative volume = volume / volume SMA over its lookback. Pressure signal = EMA of pressure composite over 1.5 times the smoothing lookback (integer, minimum 2). Volatility expansion measure = z-score of true range over the regime volatility lookback.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = EMA over 3 bars of (1.2 × (14-bar RSI − 50) + 8-bar ROC). A confirmed bearish divergence occurs when a price pivot high and oscillator pivot high on the same pivot bar are both confirmed, the price pivot high exceeds the prior such price pivot high, and its oscillator pivot is below the prior such oscillator pivot. A confirmed bullish divergence is the inverse using pivot lows. Recent bearish/bullish divergence = corresponding event within the suppression window, including the event bar.
Long: regime score > bullish regime threshold AND close > adaptive baseline AND adaptive baseline > its prior-bar value AND low <= inner upper corridor AND close > adaptive baseline − ATR × pullback tolerance AND close <= upper corridor AND (auction acceptance disabled OR auction low <= close <= auction high) AND (VWAP alignment disabled OR close >= rolling VWAP) AND pressure composite > pressure threshold AND pressure composite > pressure signal AND (volatility expansion requirement disabled OR volatility expansion measure > −0.10) AND (divergence guard disabled OR no recent bearish divergence), on a confirmed bar.
Short: regime score < bearish regime threshold AND close < adaptive baseline AND adaptive baseline < its prior-bar value AND high >= inner lower corridor AND close < adaptive baseline + ATR × pullback tolerance AND close >= lower corridor AND (auction acceptance disabled OR auction low <= close <= auction high) AND (VWAP alignment disabled OR close <= rolling VWAP) AND pressure composite < −pressure threshold AND pressure composite < pressure signal AND (volatility expansion requirement disabled OR volatility expansion measure > −0.10) AND (divergence guard disabled OR no recent bullish divergence), on a confirmed bar.
Exit: Long, low <= max(entry price − ATR × stop multiple, lower corridor − ATR × 0.25, adaptive baseline − ATR × trail multiple) OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR regime score < 0 OR pressure composite < 0 OR close < adaptive baseline OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade OR recent bearish divergence OR (opposite-signal flattening enabled AND short condition).
Exit: Short, high >= min(entry price + ATR × stop multiple, upper corridor + ATR × 0.25, adaptive baseline + ATR × trail multiple) OR low <= entry price − ATR × target multiple OR regime score > 0 OR pressure composite > 0 OR close > adaptive baseline OR bars since entry >= maximum bars in trade OR recent bullish divergence OR (opposite-signal flattening enabled AND long condition).
