Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = latest pivot high/low formed with the configured number of bars on each side; each remains unbroken until a bar closes above/below it, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = high and low of the nearest preceding down-close/up-close candle, respectively, within the configured search depth when a qualifying break occurs; retain it until mitigated or signaled.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by its moving-average baseline; skip the volume filter when the baseline is zero.
Calculation: Order-block grade = sum of: displacement in ATR points (≥2.0: 4.0; ≥1.4: 3.2; ≥1.0: 2.5; ≥0.6: 1.7; otherwise 1.0); volume ratio (≥2.2: 3.5; ≥1.6: 2.7; ≥1.2: 1.8; ≥0.9: 1.0; otherwise 0.4); and breaking-candle body/range ratio (≥0.8: 2.5; ≥0.6: 1.8; ≥0.4: 1.0; otherwise 0.4), capped at 10.
Calculation: Long stop = bullish order-block low − ATR × stop-buffer; short stop = bearish order-block high + ATR × stop-buffer. Target 2 = entry price ± absolute(entry price − stop) × target-2 risk multiple, upward for long and downward for short.
Long: bullish detection enabled AND confirmed swing high exists and is unbroken AND ATR > 0 AND close > open AND close − swing high > ATR × displacement multiple AND (volume filter disabled OR volume baseline ≤ 0 OR volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier) AND a preceding down-close candle exists within search depth; then, on a later bar while that order block is unmitigated, low ≤ order-block high AND close > order-block low AND close > open AND order-block grade ≥ minimum grade.
Short: bearish detection enabled AND confirmed swing low exists and is unbroken AND ATR > 0 AND close < open AND swing low − close > ATR × displacement multiple AND (volume filter disabled OR volume baseline ≤ 0 OR volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier) AND a preceding up-close candle exists within search depth; then, on a later bar while that order block is unmitigated, high ≥ order-block low AND close < order-block high AND close < open AND order-block grade ≥ minimum grade AND no long signal occurs on the same bar.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target 2.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target 2.
