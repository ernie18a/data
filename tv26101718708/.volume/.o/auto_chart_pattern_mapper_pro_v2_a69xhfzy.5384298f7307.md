Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side; retain alternating pivots, replacing the latest same-type pivot only with a more extreme one.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = the latest confirmed pivots meet any of these rules, checked in order: cup and handle (five high-low-high-low-high pivots; first and third highs near; middle low at least the configured minimum pattern height below both; fourth pivot recovers at least 35% of the decline but stays below the rim; fifth low is within 0.75 ATR below the third pivot); inverse cup and handle (the price-reversed equivalent); inverse head and shoulders (five low-high-low-high-low pivots; middle low at least 0.50 ATR below both outer lows; outer lows near and intervening highs near); triple bottom (five low-high-low-high-low pivots with all three lows near); double bottom (latest low-high-low pivots with lows near and middle high at least the minimum pattern height above both lows); bullish pennant (up impulse of at least 4 ATR followed by converging falling highs and rising lows); bullish flag (up impulse of at least 4 ATR followed by falling highs and lows whose slopes differ by no more than 0.03 ATR per bar); falling wedge (falling highs and lows, with upper slope less than lower slope); ascending triangle (near-equal highs and rising lows). Near means price difference no greater than the larger absolute price times the configured matching tolerance, multiplied by 1.5 for cup rims and 2 for head-and-shoulders shoulders/neckline. For triangle, wedge, flag, and pennant rules, each slope must exceed 0.002 ATR per bar in its stated direction; triangle and wedge patterns also require the latest three-pivot height to be at least the configured minimum pattern height in ATR.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = the latest confirmed pivots meet any of these rules, checked in order: inverted cup and handle (the price-reversed cup and handle rule); head and shoulders (five high-low-high-low-high pivots; middle high at least 0.50 ATR above both outer highs; outer highs near and intervening lows near); triple top (five high-low-high-low-high pivots with all three highs near); double top (latest high-low-high pivots with highs near and middle low at least the minimum pattern height below both highs); bearish pennant (down impulse of at least 4 ATR followed by converging falling highs and rising lows); bearish flag (down impulse of at least 4 ATR followed by rising highs and lows whose slopes differ by no more than 0.03 ATR per bar); rising wedge (rising highs and lows, with lower slope greater than upper slope); descending triangle (falling highs and near-equal lows). Apply the same near, slope, and height definitions as for bullish patterns.
Calculation: Symmetrical triangle = converging falling highs and rising lows, with the same slope and height requirements above.
Calculation: Rectangle = latest alternating four pivots have near-equal highs and near-equal lows, with the same height requirement above.
Long: latest classified pattern is bullish (use the bullish pattern priority above; after ascending triangle, a symmetrical triangle or rectangle is bullish when the current close is at least the midpoint of the latest high and low pivots).
Short: latest classified pattern is bearish (use the bearish pattern priority above; after descending triangle, a symmetrical triangle or rectangle is bearish when the current close is below the midpoint of the latest high and low pivots).
