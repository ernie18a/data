Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its simple moving average over the same ATR lookback
Calculation: Bull regime = the most recent fast/slow EMA crossover was upward; bear regime = it was downward
Calculation: Long-active = two consecutive bars with close > open; bull regime; absolute fast/slow EMA difference > minimum gap; close > fast EMA > slow EMA; close > VWAP; and either US Eastern 09:30–11:00, or 08:30–09:30 or 14:00–16:00 with ATR ratio > threshold
Calculation: Short-active = two consecutive bars with close < open; bear regime; absolute fast/slow EMA difference > minimum gap; close < fast EMA < slow EMA; close < VWAP; and either US Eastern 09:30–11:00, or 08:30–09:30 or 14:00–16:00 with ATR ratio > threshold
Long: Long-active AND NOT Long-active on the previous bar
Short: Short-active AND NOT Short-active on the previous bar
