Calculation: Pivot-based ATR trend direction = use confirmed pivot highs/lows; update the center to the first pivot point, then for each later pivot point set center = (2 × previous center + pivot point) / 3. Set lower band = center − ATR × factor and upper band = center + ATR × factor. Carry the lower band forward as the maximum of itself and its prior value while the prior close is above the prior lower band; otherwise reset it to the lower band. Carry the upper band forward as the minimum of itself and its prior value while the prior close is below the prior upper band; otherwise reset it to the upper band. Trend turns positive when close exceeds the prior upper band, negative when close falls below the prior lower band, and otherwise retains its prior direction (initially positive).
Calculation: Triangle setup = set true when the current bar crosses the SMA from above to below (high > SMA and low < SMA), each of the preceding short fixed run of bars has both high and low above the current SMA, and a scan back through a longer lookback finds a prior bar with low below its own SMA and close above its own SMA before encountering a bar with open below the prior bar's SMA and high below its own SMA. The found prior bar must be more than one bar ago. Keep the setup true until a long entry; clear it while a long position is open.
Long: Triangle setup is true AND pivot-based ATR trend direction is positive AND close crosses above session VWAP
Exit: Long, close is above entry price AND session VWAP crosses below SMA OR close crosses below the stop price (entry price reduced by the configured stop-loss percentage)
