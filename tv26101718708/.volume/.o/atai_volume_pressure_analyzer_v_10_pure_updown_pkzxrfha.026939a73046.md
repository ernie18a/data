Calculation: Buy/sell volume = in geometry mode, volume × (close − low)/(high − low) and volume × (high − close)/(high − low), split equally when high = low; in intrabar mode, absolute up/down volume summed from lower-timeframe bars within the current host bar
Calculation: Buy/sell volume spike = volume ≥ its own longer-lookback SMA × ratio threshold OR (volume − its own SMA)/its own longer-lookback standard deviation ≥ z-score threshold
Calculation: Overbought/oversold votes = count enabled conditions: RSI ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; smoothed stochastic K and D both ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; Williams %R ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; CCI ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; MFI ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; DeMarker ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold; stochastic RSI ≥ overbought threshold / ≤ oversold threshold
Calculation: Windowed vote count = maximum of the current vote count and counts from up to the configured number of immediately preceding bars
Long: buy volume spike AND windowed oversold votes ≥ minimum votes AND (NOT (sell volume spike AND windowed overbought votes ≥ minimum votes) OR windowed oversold votes > windowed overbought votes)
Short: sell volume spike AND windowed overbought votes ≥ minimum votes AND (NOT (buy volume spike AND windowed oversold votes ≥ minimum votes) OR windowed overbought votes ≥ windowed oversold votes)
