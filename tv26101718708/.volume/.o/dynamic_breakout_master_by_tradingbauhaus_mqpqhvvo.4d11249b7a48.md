Calculation: Support/resistance zones = cluster confirmed pivot highs and lows from highs/lows or candle-body highs/lows; group pivots within a maximum width based on the last 300 bars’ high-low range, keep clusters meeting minimum pivot strength, rank by pivot strength plus historical touches within the lookback, and use each cluster’s top and bottom as zone bounds
Long: (previous close <= a zone top AND close > that zone top AND close is outside every zone OR close crosses below the last breakout level from at or above it) AND shorter moving average > longer moving average AND RSI > oversold threshold AND RSI < overbought threshold AND ATR > ATR × volatility multiplier AND hour(datetime) is within the configured trading hours AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier
Short: (previous close >= a zone bottom AND close < that zone bottom AND close is outside every zone OR close crosses above the last breakout level from at or below it) AND shorter moving average < longer moving average AND RSI < overbought threshold AND RSI > oversold threshold AND ATR > ATR × volatility multiplier AND hour(datetime) is within the configured trading hours AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier
Exit: long, RSI > 80 OR the longer moving average crosses above the shorter moving average OR price reaches the stop or take-profit order levels calculated from the entry signal bar’s close and ATR
Exit: short, RSI < 20 OR the shorter moving average crosses above the longer moving average OR price reaches the stop or take-profit order levels calculated from the entry signal bar’s close and ATR
