Calculation: First moving average = selected moving-average type of selected price source over its selected lookback, calculated on its selected timeframe using that timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Calculation: Second moving average = independently selected moving-average type of selected price source over its selected lookback, calculated on its selected timeframe using that timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Calculation: Third moving average = independently selected moving-average type of selected price source over its selected lookback, calculated on its selected timeframe using that timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Long: First moving average crosses above second moving average OR first moving average crosses above third moving average OR second moving average crosses above third moving average; for the crossed pair, both averages are enabled and that pair is enabled for trading
Short: First moving average crosses below second moving average OR first moving average crosses below third moving average OR second moving average crosses below third moving average; for the crossed pair, both averages are enabled and that pair is enabled for trading
Exit: long, first moving average crosses below second moving average OR first moving average crosses below third moving average OR second moving average crosses below third moving average OR bar high >= entry price + (bar close × take-profit percentage) OR bar low <= entry price − (bar close × stop-loss percentage); for a crossover exit, both averages are enabled and that pair is enabled for trading
