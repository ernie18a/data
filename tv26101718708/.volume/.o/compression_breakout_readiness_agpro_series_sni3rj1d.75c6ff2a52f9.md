Calculation: Compression range = highest high and lowest low over the compression lookback; range width = their difference.
Calculation: Range contraction ratio = current compression range width divided by its longer-term average width.
Calculation: ATR compression ratio = current ATR divided by its longer-term average ATR.
Calculation: Pivot tightening ratio = recent half-lookback high-low range divided by the preceding half-lookback high-low range.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by its average over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Directional bias = clamp(0.38 × range-position pressure + 0.27 × EMA trend bias + 0.20 × candle pressure + 0.15 × recent-versus-prior range-midpoint shift normalized by ATR, −1, 1). EMA trend bias is +1 when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA and the longer EMA is rising, −1 when below and falling, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Readiness score = 0.23 × range-contraction score + 0.18 × ATR-compression score + 0.17 × pivot-tightening score + 0.15 × volume-dry-up score + 0.17 × side-bias score + 0.10 × compression-age score. Each score is normalized to 0–100: lower is better for the first four ratios, higher is better for absolute directional bias and age; missing volume gives a neutral volume score. Compression age counts consecutive bars where range contraction and ATR compression are both within their compression thresholds.
Calculation: Watch side = bullish if explicitly set bullish, bearish if explicitly set bearish, or in automatic mode bullish when directional bias meets the positive bias threshold and bearish when it meets the negative threshold; otherwise no side.
Calculation: Readiness state = INVALID EDGE if close crosses the opposite range edge buffered by ATR; otherwise HOT RISK if ATR compression ratio exceeds its hot threshold; otherwise EDGE REVIEW if close crosses the watch-side range edge by the ATR buffer and score meets the watch threshold; otherwise PREMATURE if compression age is below its minimum; otherwise NO BIAS if there is no watch side; otherwise READY WATCH if score meets its higher threshold and risk-edge distance is within its minimum and maximum ATR bounds; otherwise WATCH if score meets the watch threshold; otherwise BUILDING.
Long: bullish watch side AND readiness state is READY WATCH or WATCH.
Short: bearish watch side AND readiness state is READY WATCH or WATCH.
