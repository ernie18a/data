Calculation: Daily/weekly/monthly volume-profile levels = for each period, distribute each bar’s volume equally across price bins spanning its high–low; POC is the highest-volume bin; VAH/VAL bound the contiguous bins accumulated from POC until the value-area fraction of total volume is reached, expanding toward the higher-volume adjacent bin. Current-period profiles use the bar still forming; prior-period profiles use the last completed period.
Calculation: Daily VWAP and standard deviation = volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of (high + low + close) / 3 from the current day’s bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a lookback.
Calculation: CVD direction = sign of the sum over a lookback of volume × (close + close − high − low) / (high − low), with zero contribution when high = low; reset the cumulative sum at each new day.
Calculation: Level touch = bar high ≥ level − tolerance AND bar low ≤ level + tolerance; levels are current and prior daily, weekly, and monthly profile POC/VAH/VAL, daily/weekly/session VWAP, and completed initial-balance high/low.
Calculation: Bullish commit = close > open AND (high > low) AND abs(close − open)/(high − low) ≥ body threshold AND close > prior bar high.
Calculation: Bearish commit = close < open AND (high > low) AND abs(close − open)/(high − low) ≥ body threshold AND close < prior bar low.
Calculation: Daily value-area position = close above prior daily VAH: +2; else above prior daily POC: +1; else below prior daily VAL: −2; else below prior daily POC: −1; otherwise 0. Use the last completed daily profile.
