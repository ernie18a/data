Calculation: ATR trailing stop = on the first bar, close − multiplier × ATR; thereafter, bullish when close > prior stop and bearish when close < prior stop (otherwise retain prior direction). In a bullish state, stop = close − multiplier × ATR if the prior state was bearish, otherwise the greater of prior stop and close − multiplier × ATR. In a bearish state, stop = close + multiplier × ATR if the prior state was bullish, otherwise the lesser of prior stop and close + multiplier × ATR.
Calculation: Normalized stop oscillator = (close − ATR trailing stop) / ATR.
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving average of the normalized stop oscillator.
Long: close > ATR trailing stop OR current stop-oscillator > current signal line AND prior stop-oscillator ≤ prior signal line OR prior trend bearish AND close > prior ATR trailing stop; evaluate alert conditions on the completed bar when close confirmation is enabled.
Short: close ≤ ATR trailing stop OR current stop-oscillator < current signal line AND prior stop-oscillator ≥ prior signal line OR prior trend bullish AND close < prior ATR trailing stop; evaluate alert conditions on the completed bar when close confirmation is enabled.
