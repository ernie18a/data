Calculation: Composite asset score = clamp(weighted mean of fast-, medium-, and slow-lookback percentage-return rank scores, acceleration rank score, RSI quality, trend quality, and volume quality, minus exhaustion penalty, to 0–100; each rank score is 100 if strictly above both peer assets, 0 if strictly below both, otherwise 50; acceleration = fast percentage return minus that return from the shorter acceleration lookback ago; RSI quality = 15 at RSI ≤30, 15+1.75×(RSI−30) at RSI <50, 50+2.5×(RSI−50) at RSI ≤68, 95−2×(RSI−68) at RSI ≤ overbought, otherwise clamp(81−4×(RSI−overbought), 0, 81); trend quality = 100 if price > fast EMA > slow EMA, 65 if price > slow EMA, 45 if fast EMA > slow EMA, otherwise 10; volume quality = clamp((volume / its moving average − 0.70) / 0.90 × 100, 0, 100) for nonnegative fast return, otherwise 100 minus that value; exhaustion penalty = maximum penalty × max(clamp((RSI−overbought)/(100−overbought), 0, 1) when RSI > overbought, clamp((percentage extension above slow EMA−maximum extension)/(maximum extension), 0, 1) when extension exceeds its limit, and 0), or 0 if disabled. Fast, medium, and slow returns use progressively longer lookbacks; the fast and medium EMAs use shorter lengths than the slow EMA. Scores use the same respective parameters and weights for all assets.
Long: A tracked asset has the highest composite score (ties follow the indicator’s BTC, then ETH, then SOL priority), differs from the current holding, its score meets the minimum leader score, its score advantage over the current holding meets the minimum score edge, the signal cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal, and candle-close confirmation is satisfied if enabled.
Exit: Long, a tracked asset is the leader, its score is below the cash threshold, all tracked asset prices are below their respective slow EMAs, cash rotation is enabled, current holding differs from cash, the signal cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal, and candle-close confirmation is satisfied if enabled.
