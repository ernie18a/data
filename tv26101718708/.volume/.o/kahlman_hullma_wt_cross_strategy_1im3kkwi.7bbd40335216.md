Calculation: Long WT = EMA((source − EMA(source, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel length)), channel length)), average length); source is a selectable average of the current bar’s OHLC, and signal is its 4-bar SMA
Calculation: Short WT = same calculation as Long WT, using its own independently parameterized source and lengths; signal is its 4-bar SMA
Calculation: First Kahlman Hull line = Kahlman-filtered HMA of a selectable current-bar OHLC source; HMA = WMA(2 × WMA(source, lookback/2) − WMA(source, lookback), round(sqrt(lookback)))
Calculation: Second Kahlman Hull line = Kahlman-filtered WMA(3 × WMA(close, lookback/6) − WMA(close, lookback/4) − WMA(close, lookback/2), lookback/2)
Long: position is flat or long AND second Kahlman Hull line crosses above first AND (Long WT crosses above its signal OR previous-bar Long WT crosses above its signal OR Long WT from two bars ago crosses above its current signal) AND Long Stochastic RSI K > D AND volume > configured multiplier × mean of volume SMA, VWMA, and WMA AND configured ADX filter passes AND within configured date, session, and weekday range
Short: position is flat or short AND first Kahlman Hull line crosses above second AND Short WT crosses below its signal AND Short Stochastic RSI K < D AND volume > configured multiplier × mean of volume SMA, VWMA, and WMA AND configured ADX filter passes AND within configured date, session, and weekday range
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − configured long stop percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured long take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured second long take-profit percentage) OR (high <= entry price OR high >= long position-check level) AND (high >= long position-check level AND (Long WT crosses below its signal OR Short WT crosses below its signal OR (first Kahlman Hull line > second AND (Long WT < its signal OR Short WT < its signal) AND volume filter fails) OR first Kahlman Hull line crosses above second) AND selected long check source < long position-check level) OR (high >= entry price × (1 + configured long take-profit percentage) AND selected long TP1 source <= that TP level) OR (high >= entry price × (1 + configured second long take-profit percentage) AND (selected long TP2 source <= that TP level OR first Kahlman Hull line > second))
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + configured short stop percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured short take-profit percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured second short take-profit percentage) OR (low >= entry price OR low <= short position-check level) AND (low <= short position-check level AND (Short WT crosses above its signal OR Long WT crosses above its signal OR (first Kahlman Hull line < second AND (Short WT > its signal OR Long WT > its signal) AND volume filter fails) OR second Kahlman Hull line crosses above first) AND selected short check source > short position-check level) OR (low <= entry price × (1 − configured short take-profit percentage) AND selected short TP1 source >= that TP level) OR (low <= entry price × (1 − configured second short take-profit percentage) AND (selected short TP2 source >= that TP level OR first Kahlman Hull line < second)
