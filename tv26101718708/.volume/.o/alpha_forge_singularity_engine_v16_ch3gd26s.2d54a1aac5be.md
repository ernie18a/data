Calculation: Raw energy = clamp(volume / SMA(volume, volume lookback), 0.10, 5) × (0.60 × abs(close − open) / close × 100 + 0.40 × (high − low) / close × 100) × clamp(range% / ATR(14)% , 0.25, 4) × (1 + 0.35 × 2 × abs((close − low) / (high − low) − 0.5)); use 0.5 for close location when range is zero
Calculation: Critical energy threshold = SMA(raw energy, energy lookback) + sensitivity × standard deviation(raw energy, energy lookback)
Calculation: Energy score = 100 × (raw energy − lowest(raw energy, normalization lookback)) / (highest(raw energy, normalization lookback) − lowest(raw energy, normalization lookback)); use 0 if the denominator is zero
Calculation: Directional force score = 100 × EMA(force base, force smoothing) / highest(abs(EMA(force base, force smoothing)), normalization lookback), clamped to −100..100; force base = (((close − open) / close × 100 × 0.65) + (signed range% × 0.20) + (((close − low) / (high − low) − 0.5) × 2 × ATR(14)% × 0.15)) × volume mass, where signed range% is positive when close ≥ open and negative otherwise; use 0.5 for close location when range is zero
Calculation: Singularity = raw energy > critical energy threshold AND energy score ≥ minimum singularity score
Calculation: Bull memory = a bull singularity occurred within the memory lookback and its most recent occurrence is more recent than any bear singularity; bear memory is the converse. A tie gives neither memory.
Calculation: Range compression = ATR(14)% < SMA(ATR(14)%, compression lookback) × compression factor
Calculation: Volume creep = volume mass > 0.95 OR volume mass is rising over 3 bars
Calculation: Force building = abs(directional force score) > directional confirmation threshold OR abs(directional force score) is rising over 3 bars
Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over the trap lookback, excluding the current bar
Long: Singularity AND directional force score ≥ directional confirmation threshold OR directional force score ≥ force burst level AND energy score < minimum singularity score OR NOT singularity AND range compression AND volume creep AND force building AND energy score between charging minimum and maximum AND directional force score > 0 OR bear memory AND directional force score ≥ force burst level OR low < prior low AND close > prior low AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ rejection wick ratio OR, in confirmed trap mode, the preceding bar met that low sweep and rejection condition AND close > preceding bar high
Short: Singularity AND directional force score ≤ −directional confirmation threshold OR directional force score ≤ −force burst level AND energy score < minimum singularity score OR NOT singularity AND range compression AND volume creep AND force building AND energy score between charging minimum and maximum AND directional force score < 0 OR bull memory AND directional force score ≤ −force burst level OR high > prior high AND close < prior high AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ rejection wick ratio OR, in confirmed trap mode, the preceding bar met that high sweep and rejection condition AND close < preceding bar low
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry, high ≥ take-profit price OR low ≤ stop-loss price
Exit: Short, on a bar after entry, low ≤ take-profit price OR high ≥ stop-loss price
Calculation: Take-profit and stop-loss prices = entry price adjusted by configured percentage or by configured multiple of ATR(14), in the favorable and adverse directions respectively; if both are touched on one bar, stop loss takes precedence
