Calculation: Double-top/bottom setup = On each newly confirmed pivot high/low, scan up to the preceding five same-side pivots, newest first. Use the first candidate whose pivot spacing is within its minimum and maximum, whose prices differ by no more than a volatility-average-based tolerance, whose intervening opposite-side extreme defines the neckline, and whose intervening same-side prices do not exceed the more extreme peak/trough. Require pattern height (outer peak/trough to neckline) to meet its volatility-average-based minimum. Measure the prior trend from the extreme in the lookback before the first pivot to that pivot. Use the volatility average at confirmation; pivots use left/right confirmation bars.
Calculation: Pattern quality score = round(25 × max(0, 1 − peak/trough difference ÷ tolerance) + 20 for RSI divergence (second peak lower RSI for tops; second trough higher RSI for bottoms, otherwise 0) + volume-divergence points (15 if the second pivot’s 3-bar volume sum is lower; 0 if not, or 7.5 if either sum is unavailable) + 15 × min(1, pattern height ÷ (3 × volatility average)) + 10 × shorter leg length ÷ longer leg length + 15 × min(1, prior-trend move ÷ pattern height)).
Long: On a valid double-bottom setup, close > neckline AND quality score ≥ minimum AND long signals enabled AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (volume-surge filter disabled OR 20-bar average volume ≤ 0 OR volume ≥ its 20-bar average × configured multiplier).
Short: On a valid double-top setup, close < neckline AND quality score ≥ minimum AND short signals enabled AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (volume-surge filter disabled OR 20-bar average volume ≤ 0 OR volume ≥ its 20-bar average × configured multiplier).
Exit: Long, low ≤ (entry price if breakeven tracking is enabled and TP1 was hit, otherwise lower trough − volatility average × stop buffer) OR high ≥ neckline + pattern height × TP2 multiplier OR bars since entry > 3 × neckline-break window.
Exit: Short, high ≥ (entry price if breakeven tracking is enabled and TP1 was hit, otherwise higher peak + volatility average × stop buffer) OR low ≤ neckline − pattern height × TP2 multiplier OR bars since entry > 3 × neckline-break window.
