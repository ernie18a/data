Calculation: cRSI = cyclically smoothed RSI: Wilder-smoothed gains and losses produce RSI; adjust RSI using its lag by half the vibration period, then smooth recursively with torque 2/(vibration + 1).
Calculation: cRSI upper/lower bands = adaptive bands over cRSI values in cyclic memory: scan the observed range in 1% increments and select the upper/lower threshold where the corresponding share of values reaches the leveling percentage.
Calculation: cRSI WMA = weighted moving average of cRSI.
Calculation: cRSI top = cRSI rises for the configured detector lookback, then falls; bottom = cRSI falls for that lookback, then rises.
Calculation: Strong impulse = cRSI top and the prior bar's cRSI is at or above the prior upper band.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a cRSI low (wave A or possible wave C), cRSI is higher than at the most recent prior cRSI low within the lookback, while close is lower; hidden bullish divergence = cRSI is lower and close is higher.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = at a cRSI high (strong or weak impulse), cRSI is lower than at the most recent prior cRSI high within the lookback, while close is higher; hidden bearish divergence = cRSI is higher and close is lower.
Calculation: Wave A = cRSI bottom with prior cRSI below the lower band, excluding wave C and E. Wave B = a cRSI top below the WMA or 50, following A during the same corrective regime. Wave C = a cRSI bottom following AB, or a bottom below the WMA. Wave D = a B-type top following C; wave E = a C-type bottom following D; wave F = a B-type top following E; wave G = a C-type bottom following F. Search backward for the preceding wave within the lookback; a motive regime invalidates the search.
Long: cRSI crosses above its WMA OR (wave A OR wave C) AND regular bullish divergence OR wave E with the longer top/bottom detector setting OR wave G with the shorter setting OR regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence
Short: (strong impulse OR cRSI is above its upper band now or within the prior two bars, and cRSI is falling) AND upper band is above its high level OR regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence
