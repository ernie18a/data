Calculation: Stage = On each chart bar, score Stage 1: +30 if the simple moving average is flat (its percent change over a lookback is within ± a threshold), +20 if neither higher-high/higher-low nor lower-high/lower-low pivot structure exists, +15 if close is within ±10% of the average, +15 if average up-close volume exceeds average down-close volume by a factor, +10 if ATR is not expanding by a factor, +10 if the flat average's slope is curling up; Stage 2: +35 if average is rising, +25 if close is above it, +20 for higher-high/higher-low pivots, +10 if relative strength is positive and enabled, +10 if volume is not distributing; Stage 3: +25 if average is flat, +25 if volume is distributing, +20 for a lower pivot high, +15 if ATR is expanding, +10 if churning, +5 if the flat average's slope is curling down; Stage 4: +35 if average is falling, +25 if close is below it, +20 for lower-high/lower-low pivots, +10 if relative strength is negative and enabled, +10 if volume is distributing. Flat/rising/falling uses the average's percent change over a lookback against a threshold. Pivot structure compares the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows, each using the same left/right lookback. Volume distribution/accumulation compares average volume on down-close/up-close bars over one lookback; expanding ATR compares ATR(14) with its average over another lookback; churning means volume exceeds its average and percent body size is less than 0.3 times average percent range. Relative strength is close divided by benchmark close, compared with its value one lookback ago; benchmark bars use the chart timeframe and current bar. Raw stage is the highest score, ties favor stages 1, then 2, then 3, then 4. Change the persistent stage to the raw stage only when the raw stage equals its current value on every bar in a confirmation lookback; otherwise retain the prior stage. A transition is the current stage differing from the prior bar's stage.
Calculation: Profit exhaustion score = Add points for: close at least an overextension threshold above the simple moving average (+1), at least 2.5 times that threshold (+1), at least 5 times (+2); gain over a shorter lookback at least 50% (+2), gain over a longer lookback at least 100% (+2); volume greater than 2.5 times its average (+2); upper wick at least a configured fraction of range and close below the range midpoint (+1); bearish RSI divergence at a confirmed pivot high (+1); RSI above 80 (+1); prior bar made a 20-bar high and current close is below prior low and open (+1); close crosses below a 10-bar simple moving average (+1); or close crosses below the highest high since Stage 2 entry minus ATR(22) times a multiplier (+2). Use two lookbacks for gains, with the longer exceeding the shorter. Score window is the maximum score over current and prior two bars. Profit gate requires Stage 2 and close gain from the Stage 1-to-2 transition close at least a minimum gain threshold.
Calculation: Market breadth score = For the market index on the chart timeframe and current bar, add 2 if its stage is 4 or its stage is 3 with RSI(14) falling over two comparisons; add 2 if its close is at least a threshold above its 30-bar simple moving average, RSI(14) is at least a warning level, and RSI is falling over two comparisons; add 1 if RSI is at least that warning level, or ATR expands by a factor against its 30-bar average, or its 30-bar average is flat; add 1 if that average is falling. Classify index stage as 2 when close is above its 30-bar average and the average rises, 4 when close is below it and the average falls, 3 when the average is flat and close is above it, otherwise 1. Flat/rising/falling use the same slope threshold and lookback as the stock average. Index ATR is the 14-bar simple average of true range; compare it with its 30-bar simple average.
Long: Stage transitions from 1 to 2.
Short: Stage transitions from 3 to 4.
Exit: Long, Stage transitions from 2 to 3 OR Stage 2 profit gate and (profit score window >= strong threshold OR close is at least 5 times the overextension threshold above the moving average OR (short-lookback gain >= 50% OR long-lookback gain >= 100%) and volume > 2.5 times its average OR chandelier stop is crossed downward OR market breadth is enabled, breadth danger/climax is true, and profit score window >= 1); strong threshold is 3 if breadth is enabled and breadth score >= 2, otherwise 4.
Exit: Long, Stage 2 profit gate, not strong, profit score window >= caution threshold, and no strong/caution signal fired in the prior two bars; caution threshold is 1 if breadth is enabled and breadth score >= 2, otherwise 2.
Exit: Short, Stage transitions from 4 to 1
