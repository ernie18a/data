Calculation: Fast MA = selected moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow MA
Calculation: Slow MA = selected moving average of close with a longer lookback than the fast MA
Calculation: 15-minute fast SMA = SMA of close over the fast MA lookback on the last completed 15-minute bar
Calculation: 15-minute slow SMA = SMA of close over the slow MA lookback on the last completed 15-minute bar
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume > its moving average over a configurable lookback multiplied by a configurable factor
Calculation: Volatility = ATR / close
Long: position is flat AND close > fast MA AND fast MA > slow MA AND close > slow MA + ATR × configurable entry-filter multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR 15-minute fast SMA >= 15-minute slow SMA) AND volume >= configurable minimum AND Volume spike AND hour(datetime) is within the inclusive configurable trading window AND Volatility <= configurable threshold
Short: position is flat AND close < fast MA AND fast MA < slow MA AND close < slow MA - ATR × configurable entry-filter multiplier AND (trend filter disabled OR 15-minute fast SMA <= 15-minute slow SMA) AND volume >= configurable minimum AND Volume spike AND hour(datetime) is within the inclusive configurable trading window AND Volatility <= configurable threshold
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR × configurable stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR × configurable target multiplier OR (high has reached entry price + ATR × configurable trailing distance and low <= the highest high since activation - ATR × configurable trailing distance)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × configurable stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR × configurable target multiplier OR (low has reached entry price - ATR × configurable trailing distance and high >= the lowest low since activation + ATR × configurable trailing distance)
