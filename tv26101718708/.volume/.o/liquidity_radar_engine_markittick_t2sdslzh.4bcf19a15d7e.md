Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than or equal to every high in an equally sized window before and after it; available after the following window completes. Confirmed swing low = a bar low less than or equal to every low in the same window.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Estimated candle volume delta = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Displacement = current candle body size > its recent average body size × a multiplier.
Long: A sell-side sweep (low < previous completed daily low AND close > previous completed daily low) occurred within the CISD window AND close > the latest confirmed swing high AND prior close ≤ that swing high AND (displacement is not required OR displacement OR bullish fair value gap) AND (if enabled, RSI > its threshold AND RSI > prior RSI) AND (if enabled, bullish divergence: sweep low < latest confirmed swing low AND current RSI > RSI at that swing low, and/or current MACD line > MACD line at that swing low, according to the selected source) AND (if enabled, sweep-bar volume / its volume moving average ≥ the RVOL threshold) AND (if enabled, close > long-term EMA) AND (if enabled, estimated candle volume delta > 0) AND (if enabled, ADX > threshold AND +DI > −DI) AND (if enabled, current ATR / its moving average ≥ threshold) AND (if enabled, reference-symbol trend agrees, using its last completed bar and EMA, with inverse direction when configured) AND (if enabled, bar is in the selected session) AND bar is outside any enabled news blackout AND (if FVG entry is enabled, bullish fair value gap exists on this bar) AND more than five bars have elapsed since the previous long signal.
Short: A buy-side sweep (high > previous completed daily high AND close < previous completed daily high) occurred within the CISD window AND close < the latest confirmed swing low AND prior close ≥ that swing low AND (displacement is not required OR displacement OR bearish fair value gap) AND (if enabled, RSI < its threshold AND RSI < prior RSI) AND (if enabled, bearish divergence: sweep high > latest confirmed swing high AND current RSI < RSI at that swing high, and/or current MACD line < MACD line at that swing high, according to the selected source) AND (if enabled, sweep-bar volume / its volume moving average ≥ the RVOL threshold) AND (if enabled, close < long-term EMA) AND (if enabled, estimated candle volume delta < 0) AND (if enabled, ADX > threshold AND −DI > +DI) AND (if enabled, current ATR / its moving average ≥ threshold) AND (if enabled, reference-symbol trend agrees, using its last completed bar and EMA, with inverse direction when configured) AND (if enabled, bar is in the selected session) AND bar is outside any enabled news blackout AND (if FVG entry is enabled, bearish fair value gap exists on this bar) AND more than five bars have elapsed since the previous short signal.
Exit: Long, an opposite short signal occurs OR close < current stop OR low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ third profit target.
Exit: Short, an opposite long signal occurs OR close > current stop OR high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ third profit target.
