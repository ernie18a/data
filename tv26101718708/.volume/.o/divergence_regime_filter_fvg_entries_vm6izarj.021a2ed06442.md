Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = on the selected oscillator (RSI, MACD histogram, CCI, Stoch RSI, or MFI), a confirmed oscillator pivot low is lower than the prior oscillator pivot low while the corresponding price low is lower by at least a parameterized multiple of ATR at the pivot; the oscillator lows are higher by at least a parameterized multiple of its 100-bar standard deviation, and the pivots are separated by a parameterized minimum and maximum number of bars. Confirm after the right-side pivot bars print.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = on the selected oscillator, a confirmed oscillator pivot high is higher than the prior oscillator pivot high while the corresponding price high is higher by at least a parameterized multiple of ATR at the pivot; the oscillator highs are lower by at least a parameterized multiple of its 100-bar standard deviation, and the pivots are separated by a parameterized minimum and maximum number of bars. Confirm after the right-side pivot bars print.
Calculation: Bullish trend = close above the trend EMA, and the EMA change over a parameterized lookback is positive with absolute ATR-normalized change at least a parameterized multiple of its 100-bar average absolute value.
Calculation: Bearish trend = close below the trend EMA, and the EMA change over a parameterized lookback is negative with absolute ATR-normalized change at least a parameterized multiple of its 100-bar average absolute value.
Calculation: Bullish regime = bullish trend AND bearish-divergence confirmations in the parameterized recent-bar window are at least the minimum count and outnumber bullish-divergence confirmations in that window.
Calculation: Bearish regime = bearish trend AND bullish-divergence confirmations in the parameterized recent-bar window are at least the minimum count and outnumber bearish-divergence confirmations in that window.
Calculation: Bullish displacement FVG = current low above the high two bars earlier, the intervening bar closes above its open, the gap is at least a parameterized multiple of current ATR, and the intervening candle body is at least a parameterized multiple of current ATR.
Calculation: Bearish displacement FVG = current high below the low two bars earlier, the intervening bar closes below its open, the gap is at least a parameterized multiple of current ATR, and the intervening candle body is at least a parameterized multiple of current ATR.
Long: regular bullish divergence AND bearish regime OR a pending bullish setup from such a divergence with age at least one bar and no greater than the parameterized FVG window AND bullish displacement FVG AND (pivot-break cancellation disabled OR close at or above the divergence pivot low)
Short: regular bearish divergence AND bullish regime OR a pending bearish setup from such a divergence with age at least one bar and no greater than the parameterized FVG window AND bearish displacement FVG AND (pivot-break cancellation disabled OR close at or below the divergence pivot high)
