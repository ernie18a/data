Calculation: Moving average #1 = selected moving average of its selected price source using a shorter lookback than moving average #2
Calculation: Moving average #2 = selected moving average of its selected price source using a longer lookback than moving average #1
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar high greater than the highs of the same number of bars before and after it; available after those later bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar low lower than the lows of the same number of bars before and after it; available after those later bars complete
Calculation: Temporary pivot high = a bar high greater than the highs of the 3 bars before and after it; available after those later bars complete
Calculation: Temporary pivot low = a bar low lower than the lows of the 3 bars before and after it; available after those later bars complete
Long: Confirmed pivot low OR temporary pivot low lower than the previous temporary pivot low OR close > latest confirmed pivot high OR moving average #1 crosses above moving average #2 OR close crosses above moving average #2
Short: Confirmed pivot high OR temporary pivot high higher than the previous temporary pivot high OR close < latest confirmed pivot low OR moving average #1 crosses below moving average #2 OR close crosses below moving average #2
