Calculation: Bar delta = estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume; estimate each bar from signed lower-timeframe intrabars (up bars buy, down bars sell, dojis split equally), or split chart-bar volume by blending close position in the range with candle-body direction.
Calculation: Pressure = either the z-score of EMA-smoothed bar delta over a rolling lookback, or bar delta as a percentage of bar volume.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed price pivot low, price makes a lower low while cumulative bar delta at that pivot makes a higher low than at the prior pivot low; active for a recent-bar window.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a confirmed price pivot high, price makes a higher high while cumulative bar delta at that pivot makes a lower high than at the prior pivot high; active for a recent-bar window.
Long: bullish divergence at a confirmed pivot OR pressure crosses above zero AND absolute bar delta is at least a multiple of its recent average absolute value AND (price confirmation disabled OR close > prior close) AND (divergence requirement disabled OR bullish divergence is active) AND volume is available AND the bar is confirmed AND no long signal occurred within the cooldown.
Short: bearish divergence at a confirmed pivot OR pressure crosses below zero AND absolute bar delta is at least a multiple of its recent average absolute value AND (price confirmation disabled OR close < prior close) AND (divergence requirement disabled OR bearish divergence is active) AND volume is available AND the bar is confirmed AND no short signal occurred within the cooldown.
