Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of the selected source, reset each selected session, day, or week; use the current bar as it forms
Calculation: Upper band = VWAP + standard deviation of the selected source over a lookback of chart bars × multiplier; lower band = VWAP − that same amount
Calculation: Fixed-tick long target/stop = entry price +/− selected tick counts × minimum tick; short target/stop = entry price −/+ those amounts
Calculation: ATR target/stop = entry price +/− ATR × multiplier for long; entry price −/+ ATR × multiplier for short
Calculation: Capped ATR target/stop = ATR target/stop constrained to be no farther from entry than the selected percentage cap
Calculation: Trailing stop = after favorable movement reaches the selected trigger in ticks, stop trails the best favorable price by the selected tick distance
Long: selected entry mode is close crossing above VWAP OR close crossing above the upper band OR close crossing below the upper band; AND entry session, allowed weekday, daily trade limit not reached, daily limit not reached, trade direction allows long, position is flat or long below the pyramiding limit, and enabled confirmations pass (all enabled RSI < its long threshold, close > EMA, MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0 in AND mode; at least one in OR mode)
Short: selected entry mode is close crossing below VWAP OR close crossing below the lower band OR close crossing above the lower band; AND entry session, allowed weekday, daily trade limit not reached, daily limit not reached, trade direction allows short, position is flat or short below the pyramiding limit, and enabled confirmations pass (all enabled RSI > its short threshold, close < EMA, MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0 in AND mode; at least one in OR mode)
Exit: long, selected fixed-tick or ATR-based exit and high reaches target OR low reaches stop OR (for fixed ticks plus trailing stop) price reaches trailing stop; OR selected VWAP-cross exit and close crosses below VWAP; OR selected upper-band exit and close crosses below upper band; OR selected lower-band exit and close crosses above lower band; OR daily PnL reaches take-profit or loss limit; OR enabled scheduled close time
Exit: short, selected fixed-tick or ATR-based exit and low reaches target OR high reaches stop OR (for fixed ticks plus trailing stop) price reaches trailing stop; OR selected VWAP-cross exit and close crosses above VWAP; OR selected upper-band exit and close crosses below upper band; OR selected lower-band exit and close crosses above lower band; OR daily PnL reaches take-profit or loss limit; OR enabled scheduled close time
