Calculation: Short CCI = (hl2 − SMA(hl2, short lookback)) / (0.015 × mean absolute deviation of hl2 from that SMA over the short lookback)
Calculation: Long CCI = same CCI formula using a longer lookback than the Short CCI
Calculation: Smoothed CCI = EMA of its corresponding CCI, using a separate smoothing lookback
Long: Current close > close one displacement back AND latest higher-timeframe close > that timeframe’s close one displacement back (higher-timeframe bar may still be forming) AND (Short CCI crosses above −100 OR Short CCI crosses above its smoothed CCI when that trigger is enabled OR Long CCI crosses above −100 OR Long CCI crosses above its smoothed CCI when that trigger is enabled) AND, when the EMA filter is enabled, (fast EMA > slow EMA OR the corresponding smoothed CCI is at or below its buy-override threshold) AND, when the volume filter is enabled, volume > its moving average AND, when the ATR filter is enabled, ATR > its moving average AND, when the lower-timeframe Chikou filter is enabled, latest lower-timeframe close > that timeframe’s close one displacement back AND, when the depth filter is enabled, the corresponding smoothed CCI is at or below its buy-depth threshold
Short: Current close < close one displacement back AND latest higher-timeframe close < that timeframe’s close one displacement back (higher-timeframe bar may still be forming) AND (Short CCI crosses below 100 OR Short CCI crosses below its smoothed CCI when that trigger is enabled OR Long CCI crosses below 100 OR Long CCI crosses below its smoothed CCI when that trigger is enabled) AND, when the EMA filter is enabled, (fast EMA < slow EMA OR the corresponding smoothed CCI is at or above its sell-override threshold) AND, when the volume filter is enabled, volume > its moving average AND, when the ATR filter is enabled, ATR > its moving average AND, when the lower-timeframe Chikou filter is enabled, latest lower-timeframe close < that timeframe’s close one displacement back AND, when the depth filter is enabled, the corresponding smoothed CCI is at or above its sell-depth threshold
Exit: Long, low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier, on a later bar while the stop line is active
Exit: Short, high ≥ signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier, on a later bar while the stop line is active
