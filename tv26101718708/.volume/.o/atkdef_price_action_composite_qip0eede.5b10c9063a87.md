Calculation: Body strength = (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Long: close > shorter-period SMA(close) AND shorter-period SMA(close) > longer-period SMA(close) OR body-to-range ratio ≥ 0.15 AND body strength > 0 OR Chaikin Money Flow > 0.05
Short: close < shorter-period SMA(close) AND shorter-period SMA(close) < longer-period SMA(close) OR body-to-range ratio ≥ 0.15 AND body strength ≤ 0 OR Chaikin Money Flow < −0.05
