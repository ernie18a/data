Calculation: Swing AVWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source from the accepted swing pivot through the current bar, including pivot-to-confirmation bars; use nonnegative volume as weight and ignore zero-volume samples.
Calculation: Accepted swing anchor = a confirmed pivot high or low, using high/low and left/right pivot windows; accept it if there is no prior opposing confirmed pivot or its distance from the latest opposing pivot divided by ATR at the candidate pivot is at least the prominence threshold. If both types qualify on the same bar, accept the one with the greater distance-to-ATR ratio; ties choose the high. Reset the AVWAP and deviation at each accepted anchor.
Calculation: Outer deviation bands = Swing AVWAP plus or minus the volume-weighted standard deviation of the selected price source since the anchor, multiplied by the outer-band multiplier.
Long: Current close crosses above Swing AVWAP AND the bar is confirmed AND volume-weighted data is available AND no new anchor is accepted on this bar.
Short: Current close crosses below Swing AVWAP AND the bar is confirmed AND volume-weighted data is available AND no new anchor is accepted on this bar.
