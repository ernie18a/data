Long: bullishCount ≥ minimum aligned indicators AND (divergence requirement disabled OR bullish divergence active) AND (volume requirement disabled OR volume > moving average of volume × volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle requirement disabled OR bullish reversal candle) AND within NY session; swap with the short condition when signal flipping is enabled
Short: bearishCount ≥ minimum aligned indicators AND (divergence requirement disabled OR bearish divergence active) AND (volume requirement disabled OR volume > moving average of volume × volume multiplier) AND (reversal-candle requirement disabled OR bearish reversal candle) AND within NY session; swap with the long condition when signal flipping is enabled
Calculation: bullishCount = number of these conditions that are true: RSI below oversold threshold and rising; MACD histogram positive OR MACD line crosses above signal line; Williams %R below oversold threshold and rising; close below lower Bollinger Band OR previous close below previous lower Bollinger Band
Calculation: bearishCount = number of these conditions that are true: RSI above overbought threshold and falling; MACD histogram negative OR MACD line crosses below signal line; Williams %R above overbought threshold and falling; close above upper Bollinger Band OR previous close above previous upper Bollinger Band
Calculation: bullish reversal candle = close > open AND close − open > 2 × (high − close) AND open − low > 0.6 × (high − low)
Calculation: bearish reversal candle = close < open AND open − close > 2 × (close − low) AND high − open > 0.6 × (high − low)
Exit: Long, close ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR short signal
Exit: Short, close ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) OR long signal
