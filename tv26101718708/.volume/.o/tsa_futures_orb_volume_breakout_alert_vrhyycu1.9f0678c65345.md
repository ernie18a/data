Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the selected session's opening-range window, using completed bars; use these levels only after that window ends.
Calculation: Prior-bar average volume = simple moving average of volume over the selected lookback, excluding the current bar.
Long: (during selected session after opening range ends AND current bar is confirmed AND close > opening-range high AND current volume >= prior-bar average volume × selected multiplier AND long signal has not already occurred since price last closed within the opening range) OR (current bar is confirmed AND low > high two bars earlier)
Short: (during selected session after opening range ends AND current bar is confirmed AND close < opening-range low AND current volume >= prior-bar average volume × selected multiplier AND short signal has not already occurred since price last closed within the opening range) OR (current bar is confirmed AND high < low two bars earlier)
