Calculation: session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of bar closes for the current session, including the current bar
Long: close > session VWAP AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND previous bar's short-term EMA <= previous bar's long-term EMA
Short: close < session VWAP AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND previous bar's short-term EMA >= previous bar's long-term EMA
