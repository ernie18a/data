Calculation: VWAP-deviation oscillator = robust z-score of close minus the current session’s cumulative hlc3-by-volume VWAP, using rolling median and 1.4826 × median absolute deviation.
Calculation: Oscillator value at a price pivot = the highest (price pivot high) or lowest (price pivot low) oscillator value in the window centered on that pivot, spanning the pivot length on each side.
Long: A newly confirmed price pivot low is within the maximum pivot gap of the previous confirmed price pivot low AND ((the new price pivot low is lower than the previous one AND the new oscillator value is higher than the previous one by at least the minimum oscillator gap AND the new oscillator value is at or below the oversold threshold) OR (the new price pivot low is higher than the previous one AND the new oscillator value is lower than the previous one by at least the minimum oscillator gap)) OR a newly confirmed oscillator pivot low is at or below the lower extreme threshold.
Short: A newly confirmed price pivot high is within the maximum pivot gap of the previous confirmed price pivot high AND ((the new price pivot high is higher than the previous one AND the new oscillator value is lower than the previous one by at least the minimum oscillator gap AND the new oscillator value is at or above the overbought threshold) OR (the new price pivot high is lower than the previous one AND the new oscillator value is higher than the previous one by at least the minimum oscillator gap)) OR a newly confirmed oscillator pivot high is at or above the upper extreme threshold.
