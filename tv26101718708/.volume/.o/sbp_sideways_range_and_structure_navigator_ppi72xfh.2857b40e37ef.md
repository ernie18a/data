Calculation: ATR = Average True Range
Calculation: strong bullish candle = close > open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= configured body-ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= configured body-size threshold
Calculation: strong bearish candle = close < open AND abs(close - open) / (high - low) >= configured body-ratio threshold AND abs(close - open) / ATR >= configured body-size threshold
Calculation: daily volume profile = distribute each bar's volume across price rows in proportion to its range overlap; control = midpoint of the highest-volume row; core and outer boundaries = expand from that row through adjacent rows by descending volume until their respective configured fractions of total volume are included. Use bars from the current day through the last completed bar.
Calculation: premium and discount = center and upper/lower channel levels derived from the current-day core boundaries; use the prior completed bar's levels.
Calculation: directional evidence = weighted sum of normalized directional-movement balance, fast-versus-slow EMA alignment, ATR-normalized EMA of close changes, and ATR-normalized close distance from its EMA center.
Calculation: acceptance = EMA of EMA center adjusted by directional evidence and ATR.
