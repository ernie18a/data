Calculation: Long score = 2 if fast EMA > slow EMA > 55-period EMA > long-term EMA; +1 if close > fast and slow EMAs; +2 if fast EMA crosses above slow EMA; +1 if RSI > 50, below its overbought threshold, and rising for three consecutive bars; +2 if the 14-bar lowest close is below the lowest close of the preceding 14 bars while the corresponding 14-bar RSI low is higher; +1 if MACD line > signal line, MACD histogram > 0, and histogram rises for three consecutive bars; +2 if MACD line crosses above signal line; +1 if Bollinger Band width < its 50-bar average; +1 if close <= lower Bollinger Band * 1.02; +2 if close crosses above upper Bollinger Band; +1 if ADX > its threshold and +DI > -DI; +2 if ADX > 1.5 times its threshold and +DI > -DI; +1 if ADX > its value 3 bars earlier and +DI > -DI; +1 if volume > 2 times its moving average; +1 if volume > its moving average; +1 if stochastic RSI %K < 20. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA; the long-term EMA uses a longer lookback. RSI overbought threshold is above 50; MACD fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback. Bollinger width uses the same lookback as its bands; band width average and volume average use their own lookbacks.
Calculation: Short score = +1 if close < fast and slow EMAs; +2 if fast EMA crosses below slow EMA; +1 if RSI < 50, above its oversold threshold, and falling for three consecutive bars; +2 if the 14-bar highest close is above the highest close of the preceding 14 bars while the corresponding 14-bar RSI high is lower; +1 if MACD line < signal line, MACD histogram < 0, and histogram falls for three consecutive bars; +2 if MACD line crosses below signal line; +1 if Bollinger Band width < its 50-bar average; +1 if close >= upper Bollinger Band * 0.98; +2 if close crosses below lower Bollinger Band; +1 if ADX > its threshold and -DI > +DI; +2 if ADX > 1.5 times its threshold and -DI > +DI; +1 if ADX > its value 3 bars earlier and -DI > +DI; +1 if volume > 2 times its moving average; +1 if volume > its moving average; +1 if stochastic RSI %K > 80. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA. RSI oversold threshold is below 50; MACD fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback. Bollinger width uses the same lookback as its bands; band width average and volume average use their own lookbacks.
Long: flat position AND long score >= minimum confirmations AND (trend filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR > its 50-bar average * volatility multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average)
Short: flat position AND short score >= minimum confirmations AND (trend filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR > its 50-bar average * volatility multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average)
Exit: long, low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction) OR (high has reached entry price * (1 + trailing fraction) and low <= highest high since activation - entry price * trailing fraction)
Exit: short, high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction) OR (low has reached entry price * (1 - trailing fraction) and high >= lowest low since activation + entry price * trailing fraction)
