Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows of the selected number of bars to its left and right; usable after the right-side bars close
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high higher than the highs of the selected number of bars to its left and right; usable after the right-side bars close
Long: On a confirmed bar, regular divergence enabled AND current confirmed pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND at least the minimum confluence of enabled oscillators have a higher value at the current pivot than at the previous pivot; OR hidden divergence enabled AND current confirmed pivot low > previous confirmed pivot low AND at least the minimum confluence of enabled oscillators have a lower value at the current pivot than at the previous pivot
Short: On a confirmed bar, regular divergence enabled AND current confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND at least the minimum confluence of enabled oscillators have a lower value at the current pivot than at the previous pivot; OR hidden divergence enabled AND current confirmed pivot high < previous confirmed pivot high AND at least the minimum confluence of enabled oscillators have a higher value at the current pivot than at the previous pivot
