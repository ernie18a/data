Calculation: latest confirmed fractal swing points = on the selected structure timeframe, the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each requiring configurable bars on both sides; use completed structure bars. A bullish leg has the latest low before the latest high and the low price below the high price; a bearish leg has the latest high before the latest low and the high price above the low price.
Calculation: retracement zone edges = for a bullish leg, swing high minus the deeper or shallower ratio times the swing range; for a bearish leg, swing low plus the deeper or shallower ratio times the swing range.
Calculation: extension targets = for a bullish leg, swing high plus each configurable extension ratio times the swing range; for a bearish leg, swing low minus each such extension ratio times the swing range.
Long: bullish leg is valid (chart close >= swing low minus configurable tick buffer) AND chart close crosses above the shallow zone edge AND the lowest chart low over a configurable lookback is <= the deep zone edge AND the chart bar is confirmed AND at least the configurable cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior long or short signal
Short: bearish leg is valid (chart close <= swing high plus configurable tick buffer) AND chart close crosses below the shallow zone edge AND the highest chart high over a configurable lookback is >= the deep zone edge AND the chart bar is confirmed AND at least the configurable cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior long or short signal
Exit: long, the first bar whose range touches any extension target
Exit: short, the first bar whose range touches any extension target
