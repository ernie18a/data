Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the current day’s configured opening-range window beginning at 9:30 a.m. ET; levels remain fixed after the window ends.
Calculation: Buy level = opening-range high + configured buffer; sell level = opening-range low − configured buffer.
Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR calculated from daily bars, using the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after price reaches entry price + configured trail distance, the highest high since activation − trail distance; the hard stop remains entry price − configured stop distance.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = after price reaches entry price − configured trail distance, the lowest low since activation + trail distance; the hard stop remains entry price + configured stop distance.
Long: Current bar high > buy level AND this is the first break of either opening-range level today AND no entry signal has fired today AND the opening-range range is within the configured maximum multiple of daily ATR (when the ATR filter is enabled) AND current close > session VWAP (when the VWAP filter is enabled) AND the current time is after the opening-range window and within the configured entry window AND long entries are allowed by the directional bias.
Short: Current bar low < sell level AND this is the first break of either opening-range level today AND no entry signal has fired today AND the opening-range range is within the configured maximum multiple of daily ATR (when the ATR filter is enabled) AND current close < session VWAP (when the VWAP filter is enabled) AND the current time is after the opening-range window and within the configured entry window AND short entries are allowed by the directional bias.
Exit: long, previous close >= previous session VWAP AND current close < current session VWAP (when VWAP-cross exit is enabled) OR current high >= entry price + configured profit distance OR current low <= entry price − configured stop distance OR (trailing-stop mode is enabled AND current low <= long trailing stop).
Exit: short, previous close <= previous session VWAP AND current close > current session VWAP (when VWAP-cross exit is enabled) OR current low <= entry price − configured profit distance OR current high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR (trailing-stop mode is enabled AND current high >= short trailing stop).
