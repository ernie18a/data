Calculation: Pressure signal = EMA of the equal-weight average of four close percentage changes, using progressively longer lookbacks from fast to macro; pressure volatility = standard deviation of the pressure signal over a longer lookback; upper/lower pressure thresholds = ± pressure volatility × a multiplier.
Calculation: Regime = classify each bar as accumulation when ATR divided by its longer-term ATR average is below a threshold and the highest high minus lowest low over a balance window, divided by ATR times that window, is below a threshold; expansion when the ATR ratio is above a threshold and absolute pressure exceeds a fraction of the pressure threshold; exhaustion otherwise. Change the regime only after the candidate persists for the required number of confirmed bars.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish after a confirmed close above the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot high; bearish after a confirmed close below the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot low; retain the latest bias otherwise. Pivots are confirmed after the same number of bars to their right as their lookback.
Calculation: Liquidity anchors = within a rolling window, the highest high among bars whose candle body reaches the window’s highest body high, and the lowest low among bars whose body reaches the window’s lowest body low. Touch counts = bars in that window reaching or exceeding the upper anchor, or reaching or falling below the lower anchor. A lower sweep failure is a confirmed low below the lower anchor by more than an ATR fraction followed by a close above it; an upper sweep failure is the reverse. Relative volume = volume divided by its shorter-term average.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity = lower touch count plus one is at least the upper touch count, and a lower sweep failure or pressure above one-quarter of the lower pressure threshold or close above the fast EMA, and relative volume above its floor. Bearish liquidity = upper touch count plus one is at least the lower touch count, and an upper sweep failure or pressure below one-quarter of the upper pressure threshold or close below the fast EMA, and relative volume above its floor.
Calculation: Initial long stop = the lower of the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot low minus an ATR fraction and entry price minus an ATR multiple; if no such pivot exists, use the ATR-based stop. Initial short stop = the higher of the latest confirmed short-lookback pivot high plus an ATR fraction and entry price plus an ATR multiple; if no such pivot exists, use the ATR-based stop. Target = entry price plus/minus initial risk multiplied by a risk multiple. Trailing stop = entry price minus/plus an ATR multiple initially, then update each bar to the more protective of its prior value and close minus/plus the ATR multiple.
Long: confirmed bar AND regime is not exhaustion AND structure bias is bullish AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe candle close >= its open) AND pressure signal > upper pressure threshold × a fraction AND bullish liquidity AND flat position AND daily drawdown below its limit AND cooldown complete.
Short: confirmed bar AND regime is not exhaustion AND structure bias is bearish AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe candle close <= its open) AND pressure signal < lower pressure threshold × a fraction AND bearish liquidity AND flat position AND daily drawdown below its limit AND cooldown complete.
Exit: long, low <= initial stop OR high >= target OR close < trailing stop OR regime is exhaustion OR a confirmed close below the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot low.
Exit: short, high >= initial stop OR low <= target OR close > trailing stop OR regime is exhaustion OR a confirmed close above the latest confirmed medium-lookback pivot high.
