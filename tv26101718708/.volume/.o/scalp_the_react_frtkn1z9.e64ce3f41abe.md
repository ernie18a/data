Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = the latest local high/low that is higher/lower than the same-length bar lookback on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Kinetic MA = the selected moving average of close, trailing by taking the maximum of its current value and prior trail in bullish Supertrend direction, or the minimum in bearish direction; bullish bias is close above the trail, bearish bias is otherwise.
Calculation: Active Fibonacci setup = in Scalp mode, anchor the latest opposite pivot and broken pivot when the close breaks beyond the previous setup range; in Intraday mode, do so only on a change of character. In HTF mode, use the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high/low and direction (close >= open is bullish). In Session mode, use each configured session's completed high/low and current Kinetic MA bias; in First 4H mode, use the configured first-session range when it completes and current bias. Project levels from the directional start (low for bullish, high for bearish) by the absolute anchor range; liquidity zone is the levels at -0.21 and -0.29.
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when a bar's low is above the high two bars earlier, or bearish when its high is below the low two bars earlier; retain the gap until mitigated.
Long: Current close > latest unbroken confirmed pivot high, or current high > it when wick breaks are selected, OR (bullish Fibonacci setup AND current low < the upper liquidity-zone boundary AND current close >= that boundary) OR (current low < the top of an unmitigated bullish fair value gap AND current close > that top).
Short: Current close < latest unbroken confirmed pivot low, or current low < it when wick breaks are selected, OR (bearish Fibonacci setup AND current high > the lower liquidity-zone boundary AND current close <= that boundary) OR (current high > the bottom of an unmitigated bearish fair value gap AND current close < that bottom).
