Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low of bars in the configured New York opening-range session, aggregated at the configured ORB calculation timeframe; use the completed range after that session ends
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier
Calculation: Internal bullish FVG = a bullish FVG whose high from two bars earlier is inside the opening range; internal bearish FVG = a bearish FVG whose low from two bars earlier is inside the opening range
Calculation: External bullish FVG = a bullish FVG with current low above the opening range high; external bearish FVG = a bearish FVG with current high below the opening range low
Calculation: Bullish retracement reached at a selected ratio = after a close above the opening range high, the low reaches or falls below the breakout peak minus the distance from the opening range high to that peak multiplied by the ratio; bearish retracement reached at a selected ratio = after a close below the opening range low, the high reaches or rises above the breakout trough plus the distance from the opening range low to that trough multiplied by the ratio
Long: within the configured date range AND outside the opening-range session after the opening range is established AND (flat for the day OR currently short) AND close > opening range high AND (volume > its 20-bar average × configured multiplier OR volume > previous bar volume, if volume filter enabled) AND RSI(14) > configured threshold, if RSI filter enabled AND bullish FVG, if FVG filter enabled AND MACD histogram > 0, if MACD filter enabled AND close > EMA of the previous completed 4-hour bar, if 4-hour EMA filter enabled AND fast EMA > slow EMA, if EMA cross filter enabled AND an internal bullish FVG has occurred today, if internal FVG filter enabled AND an external bullish FVG has occurred today, if external FVG filter enabled AND every selected retracement ratio has been reached today, if any retracement filter is enabled
Short: within the configured date range AND outside the opening-range session after the opening range is established AND (flat for the day OR currently long) AND close < opening range low AND (volume > its 20-bar average × configured multiplier OR volume > previous bar volume, if volume filter enabled) AND RSI(14) < 100 minus configured threshold, if RSI filter enabled AND bearish FVG, if FVG filter enabled AND MACD histogram < 0, if MACD filter enabled AND close < EMA of the previous completed 4-hour bar, if 4-hour EMA filter enabled AND fast EMA < slow EMA, if EMA cross filter enabled AND an internal bearish FVG has occurred today, if internal FVG filter enabled AND an external bearish FVG has occurred today, if external FVG filter enabled AND every selected retracement ratio has been reached today, if any retracement filter is enabled
Exit: long, after the entry bar, fast EMA crosses below slow EMA
Exit: short, after the entry bar, fast EMA crosses above slow EMA
