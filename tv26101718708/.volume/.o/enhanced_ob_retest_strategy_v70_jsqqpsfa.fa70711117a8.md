Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish/bearish BOS = current forming higher-timeframe close is above/below the highest/lowest high/low of the preceding swing-lookback completed higher-timeframe bars by at least the configured breakout percentage.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order-block zone = on the respective BOS, take the most recent opposing candle among the preceding OB-search bars on the chart timeframe, and use the corresponding higher-timeframe candle’s open and close as zone boundaries; require body size at least the configured ATR multiple. Merge overlapping same-side zones; retain zones until flipped or replaced. BOS uses the current forming higher-timeframe bar and prior completed higher-timeframe bars; the selected candle is a prior completed chart bar.
Calculation: High/low wick zone = when the current chart bar makes the highest high/lowest low of the wick lookback, its zone spans high to max(open, close)/min(open, close) to low, respectively; invalidate the high/low zone when close rises above its top/falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Newly overlapping wick/OB zones = high-wick zone overlaps bearish OB, or low-wick zone overlaps bullish OB, and that overlap was absent on the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Long/short trailing stop = initialize at entry-signal close minus/plus configured ATR multiple times chart ATR; each bar ratchet it to the greater/lesser of the prior stop and close minus/plus that ATR distance. If breakeven is enabled and close reaches entry price plus/minus the initial stop risk, ratchet the stop to at least/at most entry price.
Long: Long entries enabled AND current datetime >= configured start time AND no open position AND minimum bars since last entry elapsed AND bullish and bearish OB zones do not overlap AND close is strictly inside the bullish OB zone AND (bullish OB retrace depth from its top is at least the configured minimum and at most the configured maximum OR newly overlapping wick/OB zones OR low is below the low-wick zone top and close crosses above that top).
Short: Short entries enabled AND current datetime >= configured start time AND no open position AND minimum bars since last entry elapsed AND bullish and bearish OB zones do not overlap AND close is strictly inside the bearish OB zone AND (bearish OB retrace depth from its bottom is at least the configured minimum and at most the configured maximum OR newly overlapping wick/OB zones OR high is above the high-wick zone bottom and close crosses below that bottom).
Exit: Long, low <= long trailing stop
Exit: Short, high >= short trailing stop
