Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close)/3, accumulated from the selected timeframe reset or date anchor through the current bar; include the forming bar.
Calculation: Anchored population standard deviation = volume-weighted population standard deviation of typical price accumulated over the same anchor period through the current bar.
Calculation: Upper/lower 23.6 core = Anchored VWAP ± (configured deviation multiplier × Anchored population standard deviation × 0.236).
Calculation: Reaction-zone boundaries = each 23.6 core ± configured half-width; half-width is either ATR × configured multiplier or configured tick distance.
Calculation: Configured Fibonacci bands = Anchored VWAP ± (configured deviation multiplier × Anchored population standard deviation × configured ratio).
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = configured moving-average type and lookback applied to the configured source; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback. Use the preceding completed bar for a higher timeframe, the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe, and the chart bar for the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Gann HiLo = direction turns bullish when close exceeds the prior bar's simple moving average of highs and bearish when close falls below the prior bar's simple moving average of lows; otherwise retain direction. Level is the simple moving average of lows in bullish direction and of highs in bearish direction. Use the preceding completed bar for a higher timeframe and the last available intrabar for a lower timeframe.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of the configured source accumulated during the selected session; use the current session's accumulated value.
Calculation: Trailing stop = entry price − position side × configured distance, rounded to minimum tick; distance is either standard-price ATR × configured multiplier or configured tick distance. On each later confirmed bar, ratchet the long stop upward to the greater of its prior value and standard close − distance, or the short stop downward to the lesser of its prior value and standard close + distance.
Long: Confirmed bar close > an enabled VWAP or upper Fibonacci level AND prior confirmed close ≤ that level's prior-bar value AND (MA cloud filter disabled OR fast MA > slow MA AND close > both MAs) AND (Gann filter disabled OR Gann direction is bullish AND close > Gann level) AND (session VWAP filter disabled OR no sessions selected OR selected-session rule passes with close > the selected session VWAPs) AND no active trade lock or stop-exit on this bar.
Long: Reaction signal allowed AND reaction-zone touch (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom) AND ((Reject: prior close > prior zone top AND close > core AND close > open) OR (Hold: approach condition and closing boundary pass for configured sensitivity) OR (BK+RT: a prior close below the zone bottom armed a long reclaim within the configured bar window AND current close > core AND prior close ≤ prior core) OR (Accept: fresh close below the zone bottom and configured consecutive closes below it)); bar confirmed when required, cooldown passed, and no active trade lock or stop-exit on this bar.
Short: Confirmed bar close < an enabled VWAP or lower Fibonacci level AND prior confirmed close ≥ that level's prior-bar value AND (MA cloud filter disabled OR fast MA < slow MA AND close < both MAs) AND (Gann filter disabled OR Gann direction is bearish AND close < Gann level) AND (session VWAP filter disabled OR no sessions selected OR selected-session rule passes with close < the selected session VWAPs) AND no active trade lock or stop-exit on this bar.
Short: Reaction signal allowed AND reaction-zone touch (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom) AND ((Reject: prior close < prior zone bottom AND close < core AND close < open) OR (Hold: approach condition and closing boundary pass for configured sensitivity) OR (BK+RT: a prior close above the zone top armed a short reclaim within the configured bar window AND current close < core AND prior close ≥ prior core) OR (Accept: fresh close above the zone top and configured consecutive closes above it)); bar confirmed when required, cooldown passed, and no active trade lock or stop-exit on this bar.
Exit: Long, confirmed standard close < prior trailing stop.
Exit: Short, confirmed standard close > prior trailing stop.
