Calculation: Volume force = volume when close > open, otherwise 0, divided by volume (0.5 when volume is zero)
Calculation: Bull confluence score = count of: close > EMA(close); MACD line > signal line; volume force > bull threshold; close inside either current demand zone; close within 0.5 ATR of any cluster POC
Calculation: Bear confluence score = count of: close < EMA(close); MACD line < signal line; volume force < bear threshold; close inside either current supply zone; close within 0.5 ATR of any cluster POC
Calculation: Demand zone = latest confirmed pivot low zone, from pivot low to pivot low + ATR × zone-depth multiplier; supply zone = latest confirmed pivot high zone, from pivot high − ATR × zone-depth multiplier to pivot high
Calculation: Cluster POC = for each price cluster from volume-weighted K-means clustering of recent bars’ hl2, the midpoint of the price bin with the greatest volume allocated proportionally to each bar’s overlap with the bin
