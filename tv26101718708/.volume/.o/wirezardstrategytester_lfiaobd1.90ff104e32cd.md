Calculation: RSI score = positive when RSI is above 50 or at/below its oversold threshold; negative when below 50 or at/above its overbought threshold. Scale by direction, distance state, and whether RSI is rising or falling; boost for matching price/RSI divergence, capped at its directional weight.
Calculation: MACD score = positive when the fast-minus-slow EMA is rising and its histogram is rising; negative when both are falling. Otherwise score partial positive for positive histogram with rising Elder EMA, partial negative for negative histogram with falling Elder EMA, or a smaller directional score when a negative histogram is rising or a positive histogram is falling and has moved further in that direction over the longer comparison. Use the configured MACD lengths and a separate Elder EMA length.
Calculation: VIDYA score = positive when VIDYA is rising, negative when falling; scale by whether close is on the same side of VIDYA as its slope and whether its slope is accelerating. Give a smaller score when price and slope disagree.
Calculation: Volume score = zero when volume is below the low-volume threshold; otherwise score positive when volume exceeds its moving average and OBV is above its moving average and rising, or negative when volume exceeds its moving average and OBV is below its moving average and falling. Scale by whether close is in the corresponding half of the bar and whether volume exceeds the spike threshold.
Calculation: Depth score = positive for a bullish candle body larger than half the bar range, negative for a bearish candle body of that size; scale by whether the high-low range is below the ATR-based tight-spread threshold. Increase the magnitude for a matching rejection wick, capped at its directional weight. For other candles, assign a smaller positive score for a lower rejection wick alone or a smaller negative score for an upper rejection wick alone.
Calculation: Sector score = compare the sector ETF's percentage change with SPY's over the same lookback, smooth the difference with a short EMA, then scale and cap it at the sector weight; use a positive full weight for strong outperformance, a negative full weight for strong underperformance, and a proportionate score between those thresholds. Use the current chart-timeframe series with lookahead off.
Calculation: Composite score = sum of RSI, MACD, VIDYA, volume, depth, and sector scores, divided by the sum of their weights and multiplied by 100, capped to the range -100 to +100.
Long: Composite score >= configured buy threshold AND volume > its moving average AND volume is not below the low-volume threshold AND RSI, MACD, and VIDYA scores are all positive AND bar is confirmed.
Short: Composite score <= configured sell threshold AND volume > its moving average AND volume is not below the low-volume threshold AND RSI, MACD, and VIDYA scores are all negative AND bar is confirmed.
Exit: long, sell condition OR composite score < configured negative exit threshold, evaluated on a confirmed bar.
Exit: short, buy condition OR composite score > configured positive exit threshold, evaluated on a confirmed bar.
