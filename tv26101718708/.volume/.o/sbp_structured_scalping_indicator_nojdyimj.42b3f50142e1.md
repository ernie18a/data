Calculation: Session value distribution = From the current daily session’s bars through the current bar, allocate each bar’s volume across price bins in proportion to range overlap; take the highest-volume bin as control, then expand its contiguous range toward the larger adjacent-volume bin until the configured share of session volume is covered. The resulting range edges are core lower and core upper.
Calculation: ATR = Average true range on chart bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current volume / 20-bar EMA of volume; use 1 when current volume is zero.
Calculation: Pressure balance = EMA of signed volume / 20-bar EMA volume, where signed volume is positive on an up-close bar, negative on a down-close bar, and zero on an unchanged bar. Pressure change = current pressure balance − prior-bar pressure balance.
Calculation: Normalized momentum = EMA of bar-to-bar close change / ATR.
Calculation: Adaptive center = Adaptive average of HLC3 using the 10-bar close-path efficiency ratio (absolute 10-bar close change / sum of absolute close changes); interpolate between fast and slow smoothing constants and square the result. Center slope = current adaptive center − its value two chart bars earlier.
Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA of close > longer-period EMA of close AND close ≥ adaptive center AND center slope ≥ 0. Bear trend = fast EMA of close < longer-period EMA of close AND close ≤ adaptive center AND center slope ≤ 0.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low), using a minimum range of one tick. Body = absolute close − open; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low of the configured recent chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bull core rejection = low is within the configured tolerance above core lower or below it, close > core lower, lower wick ≥ configured fraction of max(body, one tick), and close location ≥ configured threshold. Bear core rejection = high is within the configured tolerance below core upper or above it, close < core upper, upper wick ≥ configured fraction of max(body, one tick), and close location ≤ configured threshold. Tolerance uses the larger of an ATR-based and a core-width-based amount.
Calculation: Bull control reclaim = low ≤ control AND close > control AND close > open AND pressure change > 0. Bear control reclaim = high ≥ control AND close < control AND close < open AND pressure change < 0.
Calculation: Bull liquidity sweep = low < prior low AND close > prior low AND close location ≥ configured threshold. Bear liquidity sweep = high > prior high AND close < prior high AND close location ≤ configured threshold.
Calculation: Bull value acceptance = close > current core upper AND prior close ≤ prior bar’s core upper AND close location ≥ configured threshold. Bear value acceptance = close < current core lower AND prior close ≥ prior bar’s core lower AND close location ≤ configured threshold.
Calculation: Bull initiative = relative volume ≥ configured threshold AND close > open AND body ≥ configured ATR multiple AND close location ≥ configured threshold. Bear initiative = relative volume ≥ configured threshold AND close < open AND body ≥ configured ATR multiple AND close location ≤ configured threshold.
Calculation: Bull location = close ≥ midpoint of core range OR bull core rejection OR bull control reclaim OR bull liquidity sweep. Bear location = close ≤ midpoint of core range OR bear core rejection OR bear control reclaim OR bear liquidity sweep.
Calculation: Bull trigger = bull core rejection OR bull control reclaim OR bull liquidity sweep OR bull value acceptance OR bull initiative. Bear trigger = bear core rejection OR bear control reclaim OR bear liquidity sweep OR bear value acceptance OR bear initiative.
Calculation: Bull evidence count = count of: bull trend, pressure balance > 0, pressure change > 0, normalized momentum > 0, bull location, close location ≥ configured threshold. Bear evidence count = count of: bear trend, pressure balance < 0, pressure change < 0, normalized momentum < 0, bear location, close location ≤ configured threshold.
Calculation: Bull score = weighted sum of: bull trend, pressure balance > 0, pressure change > 0, normalized momentum > 0, bull core rejection, bull control reclaim, bull liquidity sweep, bull value acceptance, bull initiative. Bear score uses the corresponding bear conditions and weights.
Long: bar is confirmed AND bull trigger AND bull evidence count ≥ configured minimum AND bull score ≥ configured minimum AND bull score ≥ bear score + configured advantage AND NOT (bear trend AND pressure balance < configured negative threshold AND normalized momentum < 0) AND last emitted signal is neutral or short.
Short: bar is confirmed AND bear trigger AND bear evidence count ≥ configured minimum AND bear score ≥ configured minimum AND bear score ≥ bull score + configured advantage AND NOT (bull trend AND pressure balance > configured positive threshold AND normalized momentum > 0) AND last emitted signal is neutral or long.
