Calculation: Permutation entropy = normalized Shannon entropy of ordinal patterns of three price samples at three scales (base delay, twice, and four times the base delay); use the lowest entropy, and define entropy predictability as one minus that value.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = slope of log mean absolute price changes over a common lookback at lags 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16; clamp to [0,1]. Structure = 2 × |Hurst − 0.5|. Trendiness = clamp(2 × (Hurst − 0.5), 0, 1); revertiness = clamp(2 × (0.5 − Hurst), 0, 1).
Calculation: Composite predictability = weighted blend of entropy predictability and structure. Composite rank = the same weighted blend of each component’s percentile rank over a common lookback. Gate = entropy history is warmed up AND composite rank is at least its threshold AND composite predictability is at least its floor.
Calculation: Price trend vote = sign of the shorter-lookback EMA minus the longer-lookback EMA. VWAP position vote = sign of price minus session VWAP calculated from typical price and volume. Order-flow vote = sign of the EMA of signed volume; sign each lower-timeframe bar’s volume by its close change, sum within the current chart bar, and use the finest available lower-timeframe bars, falling back to chart-bar volume weighted by close location within its high-low range.
Calculation: RSI fade vote = +1 below its lower threshold, −1 above its upper threshold, otherwise 0. Band fade vote = +1 when price is more than one standard deviation below its moving average, −1 when more than one standard deviation above, otherwise 0. Range fade vote = +1 when price is in the bottom fifth of the rolling high-low range, −1 when in the top fifth, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Routed committee vote = sum of each active expert’s vote × its weight × its regime affinity, divided by sum of weight × affinity. The three trend experts are price trend, VWAP position, and order flow; the three reversion experts are RSI fade, band fade, and range fade. Their affinities are trendiness and revertiness, respectively. Initialize active weights equally. On each completed bar, update weights using the prior bar’s votes scored against the current price change: loss is 0 for a matching nonzero direction, 1 for a wrong direction, and 0.5 for a zero vote; multiply each weight by exp(−learning-rate × loss), normalize, then shrink toward equal weights. Freeze learning during a distribution shift, defined when (absolute difference between short- and long-lookback mean returns + absolute difference between their standard deviations) / long-lookback standard deviation exceeds its threshold. During a shift, multiply the routed vote by 0.4. Set committee vote to zero when the gate is closed.
Long: Flat AND gate is open AND committee vote ≥ entry threshold.
Short: Flat AND gate is open AND committee vote ≤ −entry threshold.
Exit: Long, committee vote ≤ 0 OR gate is closed OR committee vote ≤ −entry threshold.
Exit: Short, committee vote ≥ 0 OR gate is closed OR committee vote ≥ entry threshold.
