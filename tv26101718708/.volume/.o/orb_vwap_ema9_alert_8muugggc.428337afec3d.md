Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the opening-range session; use the completed range.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Volume expansion = current volume > average of the prior five bars' volume × an expansion factor.
Calculation: EMA slope up/down = current EMA >/< its value at the shorter slope lookback plus/minus ATR × a minimum-slope factor; VWAP slope up/down is defined the same way.
Calculation: Pullback hold (long) = low ≤ reference level + ATR × buffer factor AND close > reference level; pullback hold (short) = high ≥ reference level − ATR × buffer factor AND close < reference level. Reference level is the opening-range boundary, VWAP, or EMA.
Long: During the trading session, after a fresh close breakout above the completed opening-range high within the maximum breakout age, and before a close below EMA invalidates that breakout, close > opening-range high AND close > VWAP AND EMA > VWAP AND EMA slope up AND VWAP slope up AND relative volume ≥ minimum AND volume expansion AND close > open AND close > prior bar high AND at least one pullback hold (opening-range high, VWAP, or EMA) has not yet produced an entry since the breakout AND close is above VWAP for the instrument and applicable market proxies; require both proxies for tech-classified instruments, otherwise require one proxy. Allow at most one long entry per day.
Short: During the trading session, after a fresh close breakout below the completed opening-range low within the maximum breakout age, and before a close above EMA invalidates that breakout, close < opening-range low AND close < VWAP AND EMA < VWAP AND EMA slope down AND VWAP slope down AND relative volume ≥ minimum AND volume expansion AND close < open AND close < prior bar low AND at least one pullback hold (opening-range low, VWAP, or EMA) has not yet produced an entry since the breakout AND close is below VWAP for the instrument and applicable market proxies; require both proxies for tech-classified instruments, otherwise require one proxy. Allow at most one short entry per day.
