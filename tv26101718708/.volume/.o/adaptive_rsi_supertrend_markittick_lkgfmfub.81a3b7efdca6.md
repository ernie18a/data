Calculation: Adaptive RSI = Let U and L be the highest and lowest closes over a lookback; let R=U−L and d=close−previous close. Set x=R if U exceeds its previous value, x=−R if L is below its previous value, otherwise x=d. Adaptive RSI = 50 + 50 × RMA(x) / RMA(|x|), using the same lookback for both RMAs.
Calculation: Oscillator Supertrend = Apply trailing ATR bands to Adaptive RSI: ATR is RMA of the absolute bar-to-bar change in Adaptive RSI; bands are Adaptive RSI ± multiplier × ATR. Start bullish with the upper band; thereafter turn bullish when Adaptive RSI exceeds the prior Supertrend, bearish when it falls below, otherwise retain direction. In bullish state, Supertrend is the greater of the lower band and prior Supertrend; in bearish state, it is the lesser of the upper band and prior Supertrend.
Calculation: Merged line = Average of the filtered Adaptive RSI and Oscillator Supertrend; optionally smooth this average.
Calculation: RSI angle = arctangent of (Adaptive RSI minus its value one lookback ago) divided by the lookback, in degrees. Angle signal = SMA of RSI angle over the same lookback.
Long: Adaptive RSI crosses above the Merged line between the bar two bars ago and the previous bar, and (volume is at least its moving average times the multiplier if the volume filter is enabled), and (the Adaptive RSI calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is at least 50 if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled); OR RSI angle > Angle signal AND Adaptive RSI ≥ Merged line; OR Oscillator Supertrend changes to bullish.
Short: Adaptive RSI crosses below the Merged line between the bar two bars ago and the previous bar, and (volume is at least its moving average times the multiplier if the volume filter is enabled), and (the Adaptive RSI calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is below 50 if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled); OR RSI angle < Angle signal AND Adaptive RSI ≤ Merged line; OR Oscillator Supertrend changes to bearish.
