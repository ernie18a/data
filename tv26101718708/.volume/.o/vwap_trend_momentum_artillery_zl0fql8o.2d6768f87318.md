Calculation: Session VWAP = running volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset each day
Calculation: EMA slope = current trend EMA minus its value at the slope lookback
Long: flat position AND within session window AND daily trade count below limit AND ATR > 0 AND close > Session VWAP AND EMA slope > 0 AND absolute EMA slope > an ATR-scaled threshold AND (EMA50 filter disabled OR close > EMA50) AND (close is within the VWAP-distance threshold now or on any of the prior three bars) AND close > prior bar high AND (rejection filter disabled OR lower wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range AND real body is at least its configured fraction of range AND close > open) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX is at least its configured floor)
Short: flat position AND within session window AND daily trade count below limit AND ATR > 0 AND short-only restriction disabled AND close < Session VWAP AND EMA slope < 0 AND absolute EMA slope > an ATR-scaled threshold AND (EMA50 filter disabled OR close < EMA50) AND (close is within the VWAP-distance threshold now or on any of the prior three bars) AND close < prior bar low AND (rejection filter disabled OR upper wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range AND real body is at least its configured fraction of range AND close < open) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX is at least its configured floor)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR × breakeven trigger multiple (then stop is entry price) OR session-end condition
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR low <= entry price - ATR × target multiple OR low <= entry price - ATR × breakeven trigger multiple (then stop is entry price) OR session-end condition
