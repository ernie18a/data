Calculation: Money Flow Index = RSI of the lookback sum of volume × source on bars where source rises and the lookback sum of volume × source on bars where source falls; bars with the opposite movement contribute zero
Long: RSI > overbought level AND stochastic RSI > overbought level AND Money Flow Index > overbought level AND Williams %R > overbought level
Short: RSI < oversold level AND stochastic RSI < oversold level AND Money Flow Index < oversold level AND Williams %R < oversold level
Exit: long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance in ticks OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance in ticks
Exit: short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance in ticks OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance in ticks
