Calculation: Bullish order block = On a confirmed chart bar, the oldest candle in the lookback closes below its open and every newer candle closes at or above its open; the block spans the oldest candle’s low to high.
Calculation: Bearish order block = On a confirmed chart bar, the oldest candle in the lookback closes above its open and every newer candle closes at or below its open; the block spans the oldest candle’s low to high.
Calculation: Maximum-volume zone = Divide an order block’s price range into configured equal bins. For each bin, sum lower-timeframe volume from the completed lower-timeframe bars inside the block’s oldest candle, allocating each bar’s volume to a bin in proportion to the overlap of their price ranges. Use the highest-volume bin; if tied, use the lowest-price tied bin. Discard the block if total allocated volume is zero.
Calculation: Active order block = A created block remains active until a confirmed chart bar crosses its configured mitigation threshold: for a bullish block, close or low falls below its top minus the configured fraction of its height; for a bearish block, close or high rises above its bottom plus that fraction. The configured source is close for close-based mitigation and low/high for wick-based mitigation.
Long: A new bullish order block forms OR an active bullish block’s maximum-volume zone contains the current close OR the current bar’s low is at or below that zone’s bottom and its close is at or above that bottom.
Short: A new bearish order block forms OR an active bearish block’s maximum-volume zone contains the current close OR the current bar’s high is at or above that zone’s top and its close is at or below that top.
