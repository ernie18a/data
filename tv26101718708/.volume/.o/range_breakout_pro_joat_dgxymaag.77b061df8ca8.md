Calculation: Breakout range = high and low of the selected basis: the last completed daily or weekly bar, or the high and low accumulated during the selected session window once it closes; use the locked range until it refreshes.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the same ATR for breakout buffers, retest tolerance, stop distances, and risk clamps.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of closes on the selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Average daily range = average high minus low over the selected number of daily bars, using completed daily bars only.
Calculation: Breakout strength = score candidate breaks using candle body/range, candle range/ATR, volume/average volume, 3-bar and 5-bar close momentum in the trade direction, penetration beyond the range edge/ATR, RSI posture, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, first-signal status for the period, and range size/average daily range; component thresholds and weights are parameters.
Calculation: Trade levels = entry at the confirming close; choose stop from the opposite range edge, range midpoint, or an ATR distance, with an ATR buffer and minimum/maximum ATR risk clamps; targets are entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk times each selected target multiple.
Long: range is ready AND (direct mode: close > range high AND prior close <= range high AND close - range high >= break-buffer × ATR; retest mode: close first crosses above range high, price later reaches range high + retest-tolerance × ATR or lower, then closes above the armed level, without first closing below it - retest-tolerance × ATR) AND close > higher-timeframe EMA when that filter is enabled and EMA is available AND range size / average daily range is within the selected minimum and maximum when that filter is enabled AND strength >= selected minimum and, when strong-only is enabled, strength >= selected strong threshold AND signal-count/direction limits for the period allow it AND no trade is active AND ATR is available.
Short: range is ready AND (direct mode: close < range low AND prior close >= range low AND range low - close >= break-buffer × ATR; retest mode: close first crosses below range low, price later reaches range low - retest-tolerance × ATR or higher, then closes below the armed level, without first closing above it + retest-tolerance × ATR) AND close < higher-timeframe EMA when that filter is enabled and EMA is available AND range size / average daily range is within the selected minimum and maximum when that filter is enabled AND strength >= selected minimum and, when strong-only is enabled, strength >= selected strong threshold AND signal-count/direction limits for the period allow it AND no long signal is also active AND no trade is active AND ATR is available.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high reaches target 3 OR stop is hit after target 1 or 2 has been reached OR the period boundary occurs.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low reaches target 3 OR stop is hit after target 1 or 2 has been reached OR the period boundary occurs.
