Calculation: Adaptive forecast mean = recursively updated linear forecast of the selected price source using forgetting-factor least squares; reset its intercept to the source and slope to zero at each rebase interval. Its error variance is an exponentially smoothed squared forecast error.
Calculation: Forecast bands = adaptive forecast mean ± max(minimum price increment, band multiplier × square root of forecast error variance + ATR × ATR blend).
Calculation: Extreme channel = track the highest high and lowest low as upper and lower levels; when no new extreme occurs, increment that level’s age and, at each reset interval, move it toward the current high or low by the reset alpha. Channel midpoint = mean of the levels; range = upper minus lower.
Calculation: Channel bias = 1 after a new upper extreme, −1 after a new lower extreme; otherwise tanh((close − channel midpoint) / channel range). On a confirmed bar, set it to 1 if there is a new lower extreme, close > channel midpoint, and close > open; set it to −1 if there is a new upper extreme, close < channel midpoint, and close < open.
Calculation: Pressure bias = tanh((tanh(ROC over pressure lookback / standard deviation of that ROC over the same lookback) + 2 × (close − lowest low over pressure lookback) / (highest high − lowest low over pressure lookback) − 1) / 2).
Calculation: Structure bias = 1 when the shorter-lookback EMA of close is ≥ the longer-lookback EMA; otherwise −1.
Calculation: Forecast bias = tanh((close − adaptive forecast mean) / forecast band spread).
Calculation: Regime score = 0.40 × forecast bias + 0.30 × channel bias + 0.20 × pressure bias + 0.10 × structure bias.
Calculation: Bull votes = count of forecast bias > 0, channel bias > 0, and pressure bias > pressure threshold. Bear votes = count of forecast bias < 0, channel bias < 0, and pressure bias < −pressure threshold.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / average volume over its lookback.
Long: confirmed bar AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bull votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score > 0.12 AND (close crosses above adaptive forecast mean AND close > channel midpoint AND pressure bias > pressure threshold OR close crosses above lower forecast band AND channel bias ≥ 0 AND pressure bias > 0).
Short: confirmed bar AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND bear votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score < −0.12 AND (close crosses below adaptive forecast mean AND close < channel midpoint AND pressure bias < −pressure threshold OR close crosses below upper forecast band AND channel bias ≤ 0 AND pressure bias < 0).
Exit: long, low ≤ max(min(entry price − ATR × stop multiplier, lower channel level − minimum price increment), entry price − minimum price increment) OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × target multiplier OR (confirmed bar AND bear votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score < −0.10).
Exit: short, high ≥ max(min(entry price + ATR × stop multiplier, upper channel level + minimum price increment), entry price + minimum price increment) OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × target multiplier OR (confirmed bar AND bull votes ≥ vote threshold AND regime score > 0.10).
