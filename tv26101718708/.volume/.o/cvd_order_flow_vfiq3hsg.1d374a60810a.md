Calculation: Bar delta = sum of completed lower-timeframe intrabars within the chart bar: +volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, and 0 when equal.
Calculation: CVD = accumulated bar delta, reset at each session start or carried continuously; use completed chart bars. For divergence, compare EMA-smoothed CVD values at confirmed price pivots.
Calculation: Price pivot = confirmed high or low pivot using either chart-bar highs/lows or closes, with left and right bars; compare consecutive pivots of the same type.
Calculation: Average absolute delta = chart-bar average of |bar delta| over the absorption period.
Calculation: Pivot volume average = 20-chart-bar average volume, evaluated at the pivot bar.
Long: Confirmed bullish divergence: (price pivot low < previous price pivot low AND smoothed CVD at pivot > its previous pivot value) OR (price pivot low > previous price pivot low AND smoothed CVD at pivot < its previous pivot value); AND pivot separation is within the minimum and maximum bar bounds; AND the price swing from the previous opposite pivot meets the ATR-based minimum; AND the absolute CVD pivot move meets the average-absolute-delta-based minimum; AND second pivot volume meets the 20-bar-average-volume threshold.
Long: Previous completed chart bar has negative bar delta with |bar delta| meeting the absorption-period average-absolute-delta multiple, and its high−low is at most the ATR-based range threshold; current completed chart bar close > open.
Short: Confirmed bearish divergence: (price pivot high > previous price pivot high AND smoothed CVD at pivot < its previous pivot value) OR (price pivot high < previous price pivot high AND smoothed CVD at pivot > its previous pivot value); AND pivot separation is within the minimum and maximum bar bounds; AND the price swing from the previous opposite pivot meets the ATR-based minimum; AND the absolute CVD pivot move meets the average-absolute-delta-based minimum; AND second pivot volume meets the 20-bar-average-volume threshold.
