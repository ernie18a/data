Calculation: Long breakout level = selected reference + distance multiple × ATR; reference is the current session’s first-bar open, prior completed session close, or prior completed session high.
Calculation: Short breakout level = selected reference − distance multiple × ATR; reference is the current session’s first-bar open, prior completed session close, or prior completed session low.
Calculation: ATR = ATR on chart bars or ATR from the last completed daily bar, according to the selected source.
Long: Current high crosses above the long breakout level AND, when enabled, the bar is inside the configured session AND ATR is at or below its percentile threshold over a lookback AND volume is above its average AND fast ATR is below or above slow ATR according to the selected mode AND ADX is rising, above a threshold, or within a band according to the selected mode AND the bar is a narrow-range or inside bar according to the selected mode AND relative volume is at or above its threshold AND close is above the current session’s first-bar open AND the prior bar closed above its open; at most one long signal per session.
Short: Current low crosses below the short breakout level AND, when enabled, the bar is inside the configured session AND ATR is at or below its percentile threshold over a lookback AND volume is above its average AND fast ATR is below or above slow ATR according to the selected mode AND ADX is rising, above a threshold, or within a band according to the selected mode AND the bar is a narrow-range or inside bar according to the selected mode AND relative volume is at or above its threshold AND close is below the current session’s first-bar open AND the prior bar closed below its open; at most one short signal per session.
