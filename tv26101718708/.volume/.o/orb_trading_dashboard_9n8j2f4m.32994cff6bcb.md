Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during each opening window, aggregated from completed 1-minute bars.
Long: After the opening window, close > its opening-range high.
Long: A completed 1-minute bar closes above the opening-range high; a later bar touches or crosses that high; a still later bar closes above it again.
Short: After the opening window, close < its opening-range low.
Short: A completed 1-minute bar closes below the opening-range low; a later bar touches or crosses that low; a still later bar closes below it again.
