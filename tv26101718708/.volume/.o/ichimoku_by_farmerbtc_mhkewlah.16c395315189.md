Calculation: Leading Span A = average of the midpoints of the shorter and longer Ichimoku high-low lookbacks
Calculation: Leading Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback longer than either Leading Span A lookback
Long: close > max(Leading Span A, Leading Span B) AND Leading Span A > Leading Span B AND close > the simple moving average of closes over a higher timeframe’s last completed bar AND volume > its simple moving average × a multiplier
Exit: long, close < min(Leading Span A, Leading Span B)
