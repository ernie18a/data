Calculation: Moving average = simple moving average of close; use the same lookback for all macro inputs. Use the real-yield timeframe for real yield and the macro timeframe for other macro inputs; use the bar still forming in real time and the last completed bar historically.
Calculation: Macro score = sum of signed configured weights: +weight when real yield, dollar index, US 10-year yield, US 2-year yield, or USD/JPY is below its moving average, or when TLT, silver, gold miners, VIX, or high-yield credit is above its moving average; −weight for the reverse; 0 when equal.
Calculation: Combined technical rating = TradingView combined technical rating on its timeframe, using the bar still forming in real time and the last completed bar historically.
Calculation: Market regime scores = safe-haven (VIX above average, high-yield credit below average, gold miners above average, dollar index above average, TLT above average); yield-pressure (real yield, US 10-year yield, US 2-year yield, USD/JPY, or dollar index above average, or TLT below average); dollar-pressure (dollar index, USD/JPY, or real yield above average); inflation-hedge (silver or gold miners above average, or dollar index or real yield below average); yield-relief (real yield, US 10-year yield, US 2-year yield, or USD/JPY below average, or TLT or dollar index above average). Each satisfied comparison adds 1. Regime is the highest score if at least 2, otherwise mixed; ties are resolved in the listed order. Safe-haven, inflation-hedge, and yield-relief are supportive; yield-pressure and dollar-pressure are opposing; mixed is neutral.
Calculation: Final bias score = 2 × sign(macro score) + technical direction + regime direction, where technical direction is +1 at rating ≥ 0.1, −1 at rating ≤ −0.1, otherwise 0; regime direction is +1 supportive, −1 opposing, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Trend-strength score = round(clamp((macro score / sum of configured macro weights) × 6 + 2 × combined technical rating + regime component, −10, 10)), where regime component is +2 supportive, −2 opposing, otherwise 0.
