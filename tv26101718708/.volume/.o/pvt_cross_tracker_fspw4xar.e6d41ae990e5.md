Calculation: 3 AM AVWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset on the first bar of each 3 AM hour
Calculation: Cross AVWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset on each cross of the long-term SMA and 3 AM AVWAP
Long: After a cross arms or re-arms the setup, a completed 1-minute bar closes above both the long-term SMA and Cross AVWAP while flat; enter at the next bar's open. After a stop-out, allow only one opposite-direction re-entry for that cross.
Short: After a cross arms or re-arms the setup, a completed 1-minute bar closes below both the long-term SMA and Cross AVWAP while flat; enter at the next bar's open. After a stop-out, allow only one opposite-direction re-entry for that cross.
Long: Short-term EMA crosses above the long-term SMA on a completed 1-minute, 5-minute, or 15-minute bar
Short: Short-term EMA crosses below the long-term SMA on a completed 1-minute, 5-minute, or 15-minute bar
Long: Short-term EMA crosses above 3 AM AVWAP on a completed 1-minute, 5-minute, or 15-minute bar
Short: Short-term EMA crosses below 3 AM AVWAP on a completed 1-minute, 5-minute, or 15-minute bar
Long: Low < the prior N-bar lowest low AND close > that lowest low
Short: High > the prior N-bar highest high AND close < that highest high
Exit: Long, low <= the cross candle's low OR (high >= entry price + target distance AND low > the cross candle's low)
Exit: Short, high >= the cross candle's high OR (low <= entry price - target distance AND high < the cross candle's high)
