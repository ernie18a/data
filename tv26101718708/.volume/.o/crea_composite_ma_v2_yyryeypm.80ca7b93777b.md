Calculation: Composite score = EMA(3) of the weighted average of: EMA bias, MFI, normalized DI+ minus DI−, inverse normalized ATR as a percentage of close averaged with EMA bias, normalized close minus open, directional volume score, and normalized rate of change. EMA bias = EMA(3) of 50 for each of (close above both EMAs; shorter-period EMA above longer-period EMA), summed. Directional volume score = 50 + (clamped 0–100 volume z-score − 50) × (1 if close ≥ open, otherwise −1); volume z-score uses the volume moving average and standard deviation over the same lookback. Normalize a value as (value − lowest(value, lookback)) / (highest(value, lookback) − lowest(value, lookback)) × 100; use 50 if the denominator is zero. The weighted average uses the component weights divided by their sum.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe composite score = the same composite calculation applied to higher-timeframe OHLCV, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Composite score > bull threshold AND previous bar's composite score ≤ bull threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe composite score ≥ higher-timeframe bull minimum)
Short: Composite score < bear threshold AND previous bar's composite score ≥ bear threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe composite score ≤ higher-timeframe bear maximum)
