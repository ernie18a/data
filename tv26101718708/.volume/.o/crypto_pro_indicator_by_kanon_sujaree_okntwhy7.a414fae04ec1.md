Calculation: VWAP = session VWAP of typical price (high + low + close)/3; upper/lower bands = VWAP ± band multiplier × standard deviation of typical-price-minus-VWAP over a lookback
Calculation: RSI scores = 20 for current RSI oversold with higher-timeframe RSI > 50, or overbought with higher-timeframe RSI < 50; 10 for current RSI between 50 and overbought, or between oversold and 50, respectively, with the same higher-timeframe direction; otherwise 0. Higher-timeframe RSI uses the bar still forming, without lookahead.
Calculation: MACD scores = 20 on a MACD/signal-line crossover in the matching direction; otherwise 10 when the MACD line is on that side of its signal line and the histogram is positive and rising for long, or negative and falling for short; otherwise 0. MACD uses a shorter and a longer moving-average lookback and a signal-line lookback.
Calculation: ADX score = 30, 20, 10, or 0 as ADX meets successively lower trend-strength thresholds; add the full score to the direction whose directional indicator is greater, otherwise add half the score, rounded.
Calculation: Structure scores = 10 on a close crossing above the latest confirmed swing high for long, or below the latest confirmed swing low for short; otherwise 5 for the direction of the stored structure bias, or 0. Swing points require the configured number of bars on both sides for confirmation; structure bias starts bullish and changes on these crossings.
Calculation: EMA scores = 10 when close is above the EMA and the EMA is higher than its value a short lookback ago, or below it and lower than its value then; otherwise 5 when close is on the matching side, or 0.
Calculation: Volume scores = 10 when volume exceeds a multiple of its moving average and the candle closes in the matching direction; 5 when volume is between lower and upper multiples of that average and the candle closes in that direction; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Divergence scores = 10 for long when low is below the prior rolling low, RSI is above its prior rolling low, and RSI is below a threshold; 10 for short when high is above the prior rolling high, RSI is below its prior rolling high, and RSI is above a threshold; otherwise 0. Rolling comparisons use the prior bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: VWAP scores = 20 when price is above VWAP and the low reaches the lower band for long, or price is below VWAP and the high reaches the upper band for short; otherwise 10 when price is on the matching side of VWAP; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Long confidence = sum of long VWAP, RSI, MACD, ADX, structure, EMA, volume, and divergence scores. Short confidence = sum of their short scores.
Long: Long confidence ≥ confidence threshold AND previous bar's long confidence < confidence threshold AND last signal is not long
Short: Short confidence ≥ confidence threshold AND previous bar's short confidence < confidence threshold AND last signal is long
