Calculation: Price Volume Trend (PVT) = cumulative volume × close percentage change from the prior bar, accumulated over bars; calculate on active-chart bars and on daily bars, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Linear regression of close = current regression value compared with its value on the prior active-chart bar
Long: Linear regression of close is rising AND active-chart PVT is above its EMA AND daily PVT is above its EMA
Long: Linear regression of close is falling AND daily PVT is above its EMA AND active-chart PVT is above its EMA
Long: Linear regression of close is falling AND daily PVT is above its EMA AND active-chart PVT is at or below its EMA
Short: Linear regression of close is falling AND daily PVT is at or below its EMA AND active-chart PVT is at or below its EMA
Short: Linear regression of close is rising AND daily PVT is at or below its EMA AND active-chart PVT is at or below its EMA
Short: Linear regression of close is rising AND daily PVT is at or below its EMA AND active-chart PVT is above its EMA
