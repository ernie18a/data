Calculation: VWAP and SD bands = within each anchor period, calculate the volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of HLC3; bands are the mean plus or minus SD multiplied by ascending SD1, SD2, and SD3 multipliers.
Calculation: Volume delta = sum, over the lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, of volume × sign(close − open).
Long: close > open AND (low ≤ lower SD1 OR low ≤ lower SD2 OR low ≤ lower SD3 OR (low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP, if VWAP rejection signals are enabled)) AND volume delta > delta threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR × range multiplier) AND (clean-signal filter disabled OR last signal direction is not long OR price has reached the neutral zone since the last signal OR current rejection level is deeper than the last long signal’s level) AND current bar index − last signal bar index ≥ cooldown bars
Short: close < open AND (high ≥ upper SD1 OR high ≥ upper SD2 OR high ≥ upper SD3 OR (low ≤ VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP, if VWAP rejection signals are enabled)) AND volume delta < −delta threshold AND (volatility filter disabled OR high − low ≥ ATR × range multiplier) AND (clean-signal filter disabled OR last signal direction is not short OR price has reached the neutral zone since the last signal OR current rejection level is deeper than the last short signal’s level) AND current bar index − last signal bar index ≥ cooldown bars
