Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Moving-average alignment score = rolling sum over a configurable lookback of seven comparisons: close > MA(5), MA(5) > MA(10), MA(10) > MA(20), MA(20) > MA(30), MA(30) > MA(50), MA(50) > MA(100), and MA(100) > MA(200); use the same MA type throughout
Calculation: New-high filter = highest high over the shorter lookback equals highest high over the longer lookback; new-low filter = lowest low over the shorter lookback equals lowest low over the longer lookback
Calculation: Breakout bar state = +1 when close > prior close and either an upward price move exceeds ATR times the trend threshold with volume above its longer-lookback volume average plus markup, or the high-volume or large-breakout condition holds; = -1 for the corresponding downward move; otherwise 0. The price-move threshold is shorter than the large-breakout threshold. Reverse-volume comparison uses its own volume average and markup.
Calculation: Up target = high + directional markup × true range; down target = low − directional markup × true range. Up stop target = low − ATR × stop factor; down stop target = high + ATR × stop factor. Each target/stop line carries forward its last nonmissing value.
Calculation: Direction = Supertrend direction. Moving-average Supertrend direction = Supertrend direction calculated on a moving average of OHLC4.
Calculation: Yearly high/low filter = compare the latest completed annual high or low with the preceding two completed annual highs or lows, allowing for unavailable prior annual bars; also allow long when close is above the latest annual high or the specified nested maximum of available prior annual highs, and allow short when close is below the latest annual low or the corresponding nested minimum of available prior annual lows.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = target − stop multiplier × ATR, adjusted to never decrease while long; short trailing stop = target + stop multiplier × ATR, adjusted to never increase while short. While long, the stop multiplier applies to the long stop and the compound multiplier to the opposite stop; while short, these multipliers apply in reverse. Target is the entry target for breakout/pullback entries, close for reverse entries, and the current low/high while holding long/short.
Long: Within the configured date range AND yearly high filter AND new-high filter AND (moving-average alignment score > positive threshold OR alignment filter disabled) AND ((moving-average Supertrend direction is up OR that filter disabled) AND (source percent-rank over its lookback > 50 OR net profit >= 0) AND (weekly Supertrend direction is down OR weekly filter disabled) AND (monthly Supertrend direction is down OR monthly filter disabled)) AND either ((direction is down AND breakout bar state is nonzero) OR ((direction is not down) AND ((low crosses above prior up target AND close > prior down target) OR low crosses above prior down stop target))) AND (trade type permits the applicable breakout, pullback, or reverse category) AND (close crosses above the lower 100-bar Bollinger Band when range exit mode is Bollinger Bands, OR the entry qualifies as breakout/pullback/reverse and close satisfies the compound-entry condition); breakout category: direction down and breakout bar state +1; pullback category: direction down and breakout bar state −1; reverse category: buy signal or sell signal.
Short: Within the configured date range AND yearly low filter AND new-low filter AND (moving-average alignment score < negative threshold OR alignment filter disabled) AND ((moving-average Supertrend direction is down OR that filter disabled) AND (source percent-rank over its lookback < 50 OR net profit >= 0) AND (weekly Supertrend direction is up OR weekly filter disabled) AND (monthly Supertrend direction is up OR monthly filter disabled)) AND either ((direction is up AND breakout bar state is nonzero) OR ((direction is not up) AND ((high crosses below prior down target AND close < prior up target) OR high crosses below prior up stop target))) AND (trade type permits the applicable breakout, pullback, or reverse category) AND (entry qualifies as breakout/pullback/reverse and close satisfies the compound-entry condition); breakout category: direction up and breakout bar state −1; pullback category: direction up and breakout bar state +1; reverse category: buy signal or sell signal.
Exit: long, low crosses below the long trailing stop OR (exit-on-signal enabled, or range exit mode is exit-on-signal while ranging) AND within the configured date range AND breakout bar state is not +1 AND ((direction is down and close crosses below prior down target or prior up stop target) OR (direction is not down and low crosses below prior down target or prior up stop target)) OR close crosses below the upper 100-bar Bollinger Band while ranging in Bollinger Bands mode
Exit: short, high crosses above the short trailing stop OR (exit-on-signal enabled, or range exit mode is exit-on-signal while ranging) AND within the configured date range AND breakout bar state is not −1 AND ((direction is up and close crosses above prior up target or prior down stop target) OR (direction is not up and high crosses above prior up target or prior down stop target))
