Calculation: Pullback limit price = pullback EMA minus entry depth × ATR for long setups; plus entry depth × ATR for short setups
Calculation: Risk = absolute distance from filled entry price to the pullback swing extreme beyond which the stop is buffered by ATR, clamped between minimum and maximum ATR risk; stop = entry price − risk for long positions and entry price + risk for short positions
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus risk × each configured R multiple for long positions, and minus risk × each configured R multiple for short positions
Calculation: Setup score = configurable weighted average scaled to 0–10 of higher-timeframe alignment, session, fast/slow EMA separation normalized by ATR, entry depth, directional RSI momentum, and volume relative to its 20-bar average
Long: A long setup is armed when fast EMA > slow EMA, close > slow EMA, fast EMA > its value at the longer slope lookback, close > the highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars, close > open, and candle body ≥ ATR × minimum breakout-body ratio; after the minimum wait, while the setup has not expired or flipped out of the bullish trend and cooldown permits, low ≤ pullback limit price, close > slow EMA, and (higher-timeframe filter disabled or close > EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar or that EMA unavailable) AND (session filter disabled or current exchange hour is not configured as weak) AND (RSI filter disabled or RSI ≥ configured long minimum) AND (close confirmation disabled or close > open) AND (strong-only filter disabled or setup score ≥ configured threshold)
Short: A short setup is armed when fast EMA < slow EMA, close < slow EMA, fast EMA < its value at the longer slope lookback, close < the lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars, close < open, and candle body ≥ ATR × minimum breakout-body ratio; after the minimum wait, while the setup has not expired or flipped out of the bearish trend and cooldown permits, high ≥ pullback limit price, close < slow EMA, and (higher-timeframe filter disabled or close < EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar or that EMA unavailable) AND (session filter disabled or current exchange hour is not configured as weak) AND (RSI filter disabled or RSI ≤ configured short maximum) AND (close confirmation disabled or close < open) AND (strong-only filter disabled or setup score ≥ configured threshold)
Exit: long, low ≤ stop or high ≥ third profit target
Exit: short, high ≥ stop or low ≤ third profit target
