Calculation: Oscillator = EMA of the current close’s position as a percentage of the rolling range, where the rolling range is highest high to lowest low over a lookback; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Scaled POC = the midpoint of the highest-volume equal-width price bin in the rolling range, expressed as a percentage of that range and clamped to 0–100. Allocate each bar’s volume to the bin containing its high-low midpoint.
Calculation: Scaled value-area boundaries = expand from the POC bin, adding the adjacent bin with greater volume until the selected percentage of rolling volume is included; express the resulting upper and lower price boundaries as percentages of the rolling range, clamped to 0–100.
Long: Current oscillator > current scaled POC + POC break filter AND previous oscillator <= previous scaled POC + POC break filter OR current oscillator > current scaled value-area high + value-area break filter AND previous oscillator <= previous scaled value-area high + value-area break filter OR oscillator has been strictly above scaled POC for the required consecutive bars and was not above it for that many bars on the previous bar.
Short: Current oscillator < current scaled POC - POC break filter AND previous oscillator >= previous scaled POC - POC break filter OR current oscillator < current scaled value-area low - value-area break filter AND previous oscillator >= previous scaled value-area low - value-area break filter OR oscillator has been strictly below scaled POC for the required consecutive bars and was not below it for that many bars on the previous bar.
