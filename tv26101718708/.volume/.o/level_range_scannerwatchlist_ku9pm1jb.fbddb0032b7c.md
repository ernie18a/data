Calculation: Previous-day high = highest high across all extended-hours bars in the last completed day
Calculation: Previous-day low = lowest low across all extended-hours bars in the last completed day
Calculation: Premarket high = highest high across the latest completed premarket session
Calculation: Premarket low = lowest low across the latest completed premarket session
Long: close >= previous-day high OR close >= premarket high
Short: close <= previous-day low OR close <= premarket low
