Calculation: Lower VWAP band = session VWAP − rolling standard deviation of close × band multiplier
Calculation: Upper VWAP band = session VWAP + rolling standard deviation of close × band multiplier
Calculation: Synthetic velocity = (close − previous bar close) × (volume ÷ rolling simple moving average of volume)
Long: low ≤ lower VWAP band AND synthetic velocity < −2 × rolling standard deviation of synthetic velocity
Short: high ≥ upper VWAP band AND synthetic velocity > 2 × rolling standard deviation of synthetic velocity
