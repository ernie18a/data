Calculation: Above key averages = close > short-term EMA AND close > medium-term SMA
Calculation: Entry extension acceptable = close is at or above the short-term EMA AND no more than 10% above it
Calculation: Higher low = the lowest low in the recent half of a lookback is greater than the lowest low in the preceding half of that lookback
Calculation: Relative strength versus a benchmark = close divided by benchmark close
