Calculation: Price-change Z-score = (close − previous close − rolling mean of close changes) / rolling standard deviation of close changes
Calculation: Bullish zone = the bounds of the most recent bearish candle within the configured origin scan before a qualifying bullish impulse; bearish zone = the bounds of the most recent bullish candle before a qualifying bearish impulse. Use candle bodies or full high–low ranges according to the zone setting.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × configured stop multiple; target distance = stop distance × configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: Price-change Z-score > configured threshold OR a confirmed bullish candle touches an active, unmitigated bullish zone, closes above its open with close − low greater than the configured fraction of zone height, and lower wick / max(abs(close − open), (high − low) × 0.001) ≥ configured minimum wick-to-body ratio; for zone creation, require the Z-score to exceed the threshold, close > open, close > the highest close over the configured impulse lookback excluding the current bar, and enabled bullish EMA, VWAP, ATR-band, and session filters to pass
Short: Price-change Z-score < −configured threshold OR a confirmed bearish candle touches an active, unmitigated bearish zone, closes below its open with high − close greater than the configured fraction of zone height, and upper wick / max(abs(close − open), (high − low) × 0.001) ≥ configured minimum wick-to-body ratio; for zone creation, require the Z-score to be below −threshold, close < open, close < the lowest close over the configured impulse lookback excluding the current bar, and enabled bearish EMA, VWAP, ATR-band, and session filters to pass
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + target distance
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − target distance
