Calculation: Active range = Start with the first bar after no range is active; add each following bar while its combined high-to-low width is no greater than a configurable multiple of ATR. The range bounds are the highest high and lowest low of included bars. Confirm after a configurable minimum number of bars. If a bar neither produces a breakout nor fits within the width limit, reset; begin a new range on the next bar.
Calculation: Average volume = Simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback.
Long: A confirmed active range exists AND close > its high before the current bar AND close − that high ≥ a configurable multiple of ATR AND volume > a configurable multiple of average volume AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA)
Short: A confirmed active range exists AND close < its low before the current bar AND that low − close ≥ a configurable multiple of ATR AND volume > a configurable multiple of average volume AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA)
