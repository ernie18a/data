Calculation: Bull score = count of: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > slow EMA; MACD line > signal line; RSI > 50 and < 75; ADX > minimum and +DI > -DI; volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier; current higher-timeframe close > its EMA
Calculation: Bear score = count of: fast EMA < slow EMA and close < slow EMA; MACD line < signal line; RSI < 50 and > 25; ADX > minimum and -DI > +DI; volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier; current higher-timeframe close < its EMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull score = count of: current higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA; current higher-timeframe close > its EMA; current higher-timeframe RSI > 50
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bear score = count of: current higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA; current higher-timeframe close < its EMA; current higher-timeframe RSI < 50
Calculation: Bullish structure = the two latest confirmed pivot highs rise AND the two latest confirmed pivot lows rise
Calculation: Bearish structure = the two latest confirmed pivot highs fall AND the two latest confirmed pivot lows fall
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest close since entry minus ATR × trailing-stop multiplier
Calculation: Short trailing stop = lowest close since entry plus ATR × trailing-stop multiplier
Long: bull score >= minimum entry score AND bull score > bear score AND bull score − bear score >= neutral-gap threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (no prior exit OR bars since prior exit > cooldown length) AND (chop filter disabled OR absolute difference between fast and slow EMA > ATR × minimum EMA-gap ratio) AND (higher-timeframe score filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bull score >= 2) AND (structure filter disabled OR bullish structure) AND (liquidity-sweep filter disabled OR low < lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback bars and close > that low OR close > highest high of the preceding sweep lookback bars) AND (candle-confirmation filter disabled OR close > open AND body is at least the configured percentage of candle range AND close is in the top 30% of the range) AND (close-confirmation enabled only if the bar is closed) AND current position is not long
Short: bear score >= minimum entry score AND bear score > bull score AND bear score − bull score >= neutral-gap threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (no prior exit OR bars since prior exit > cooldown length) AND (chop filter disabled OR absolute difference between fast and slow EMA > ATR × minimum EMA-gap ratio) AND (higher-timeframe score filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bear score >= 2) AND (structure filter disabled OR bearish structure) AND (liquidity-sweep filter disabled OR high > highest high of the preceding sweep lookback bars and close < that high OR close < lowest low of the preceding sweep lookback bars) AND (candle-confirmation filter disabled OR close < open AND body is at least the configured percentage of candle range AND close is in the bottom 30% of the range) AND (close-confirmation enabled only if the bar is closed) AND current position is not short
Exit: long, (close-confirmation enabled only if the bar is closed) AND (bear entry condition OR bull score <= exit-score threshold OR close < VWAP OR close < long trailing stop)
Exit: short, (close-confirmation enabled only if the bar is closed) AND (bull entry condition OR bear score <= exit-score threshold OR close > VWAP OR close > short trailing stop)
