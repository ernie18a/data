Calculation: Chart range flip = initialize the chart-timeframe range from a bar’s high and low; replace its bounds with a bar’s high and low when its close breaks above the range high or below the range low. An up flip is an upside break when the prior range direction was not up; a down flip is a downside break when it was not down.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range = apply the same close-break rule to each newly completed higher-timeframe bar, replacing the bounds with that bar’s high and low; midline = (range high + range low) / 2. Use only completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure bias = confirm swing highs and lows using the configured pivot length on each side. BOS bias turns bullish when a completed higher-timeframe close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high, bearish when it falls below the latest confirmed swing low, and otherwise retains its prior direction. Sequence bias is bullish when the latest two confirmed swing highs and lows are both higher, bearish when both are lower, and neutral otherwise. Use the configured BOS, sequence, or agreement rule.
Calculation: Daily or weekly regime = calculate structure bias by the same method on the configured daily or weekly bars, using their last completed close and confirmed pivots; apply the configured BOS, sequence, or agreement rule.
Calculation: Long stop = select the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low, the current flip candle low, or the prior chart range low, as configured. If the selected value is below the signal close, use the lower of it and the current chart range low; otherwise use the current chart range low. Subtract the configured tick buffer.
Calculation: Short stop = select the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high, the current flip candle high, or the prior chart range high, as configured. If the selected value is above the signal close, use the higher of it and the current chart range high; otherwise use the current chart range high. Add the configured tick buffer.
Calculation: Range depth = for longs, (close − higher-timeframe range low) / range width; for shorts, (higher-timeframe range high − close) / range width.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP distance = absolute close-to-anchored-VWAP distance divided by chart-timeframe ATR; anchor VWAP to the configured session or week.
Calculation: Volatility measure = either the percentile rank of ATR on the configured timeframe against its longer lookback, or ATR divided by its longer-lookback average, using the last completed bar of that timeframe.
Long: up chart range flip AND flat position AND long direction enabled AND close below the higher-timeframe range midline in pullback mode (above it in breakout mode; no midline restriction when location mode is off) AND (structure filter disabled OR higher-timeframe structure bias bullish) AND (regime disabled OR daily/weekly regime neutral or bullish) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility measure meets its configured minimum) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR configured long-side VWAP rule passes, including its maximum ATR distance when applicable) AND (depth gate does not apply OR long range depth is below its configured cutoff) AND long stop distance is positive and within any configured minimum and maximum widths AND within the configured general entry window and date range, if enabled.
Short: down chart range flip AND flat position AND short direction enabled AND close above the higher-timeframe range midline in pullback mode (below it in breakout mode; no midline restriction when location mode is off) AND (structure filter disabled OR higher-timeframe structure bias bearish) AND (regime disabled OR daily/weekly regime neutral or bearish) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility measure meets its configured minimum) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR configured short-side VWAP rule passes, including its maximum ATR distance when applicable) AND (depth gate does not apply OR short range depth is below its configured cutoff) AND short stop distance is positive and within any configured minimum and maximum widths AND within both the configured general and short entry windows and date range, if enabled.
Exit: long, low ≤ frozen stop (or the configured breakeven stop after price reaches the configured favorable R threshold) OR high ≥ entry price + initial stop risk × configured long reward multiple.
Exit: short, high ≥ frozen stop (or the configured breakeven stop after price reaches the configured favorable R threshold) OR low ≤ entry price − initial stop risk × configured short reward multiple.
