Calculation: Opening range high/low = maximum high and minimum low of bars opening during the first configured N minutes of each configured session; available from the first bar opening after that window.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / 20-bar SMA of volume.
Long: After the opening range completes, the first confirmed close above its high + configured ATR(14) buffer, with relative volume at or above its configured minimum when that filter is enabled; OR, in stop-order mode, high reaches the range high + buffer; OR, after an up-breakout, price retests the range high within the configured retest window; OR, after a down-breakout, a close above the range low within the configured fake-out window.
Short: After the opening range completes, the first confirmed close below its low − configured ATR(14) buffer, with relative volume at or above its configured minimum when that filter is enabled; OR, in stop-order mode, low reaches the range low − buffer; OR, after a down-breakout, price retests the range low within the configured retest window; OR, after an up-breakout, a close below the range high within the configured fake-out window.
Exit: Long, low reaches the active stop OR high reaches target 1 or target 2 OR a breakout position closes after a confirmed fake-out when that exit is enabled OR the configured maximum holding time is reached OR the session ends. The stop may be the opposite range edge, range midpoint, an ATR(14) or range multiple from entry, or beyond the breakout bar; for fade positions it may be beyond the breakout extreme by an ATR(14) amount or an ATR(14) distance from entry. Targets may be an R multiple, ATR(14) multiple, range extension, points, percentage, opposite range edge, range midpoint, or session end; target 1 may close a configured fraction and target 2 the remainder. Move the stop to break-even after target 1 or at the configured R threshold, and update a configured ATR(14)- or range-based trailing stop when enabled.
Exit: Short, high reaches the active stop OR low reaches target 1 or target 2 OR a breakout position closes after a confirmed fake-out when that exit is enabled OR the configured maximum holding time is reached OR the session ends. The stop may be the opposite range edge, range midpoint, an ATR(14) or range multiple from entry, or beyond the breakout bar; for fade positions it may be beyond the breakout extreme by an ATR(14) amount or an ATR(14) distance from entry. Targets may be an R multiple, ATR(14) multiple, range extension, points, percentage, opposite range edge, range midpoint, or session end; target 1 may close a configured fraction and target 2 the remainder. Move the stop to break-even after target 1 or at the configured R threshold, and update a configured ATR(14)- or range-based trailing stop when enabled.
