Calculation: Regime = ATR divided by its simple moving average over a separate lookback; volatile if this ratio > 1.4, ranging if ADX < its threshold and the ratio < 0.9, otherwise trending.
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend band and direction = use hl2 ± ATR × multiplier; multiplier is the base multiplier in trending conditions, base × [1 + (ATR ratio − 1) × 0.4] in volatile conditions, and base × 0.85 in ranging conditions, clamped to 0.5–2 times the base. While direction is up, band = max(hl2 − multiplier × ATR, prior band), flipping down when close < band and resetting band to hl2 + multiplier × ATR. While direction is down, band = min(hl2 + multiplier × ATR, prior band), flipping up when close > band and resetting band to hl2 − multiplier × ATR.
Calculation: Directional signal score = sum these points, capped at 100: volume/volume SMA (separate lookback) ≥ 2.5: 20; ≥ 1.5: 14; ≥ 1: 8; otherwise 3. Directional displacement beyond the current SuperTrend band, divided by ATR: ≥ 1.5: 25; ≥ 0.8: 18; ≥ 0.3: 12; > 0: 5; otherwise 0. Direction agrees with close versus trend EMA (separate lookback) and EMA distance/ATR > 0.5: 20; agrees: 14; otherwise EMA distance/ATR < 0.3: 8; otherwise 2. Regime trending: 15; volatile: 8; ranging: 3. Absolute prior-bar distance between close and SuperTrend band, divided by current ATR: ≥ 2: 20; ≥ 1: 14; ≥ 0.5: 8; otherwise 3.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × stop multiplier, or close × stop percentage, or absolute difference between close and SuperTrend band, according to selected stop mode. Target distance = stop distance × reward-to-risk multiple, or close × target percentage, or none, according to selected target mode.
Long: Current confirmed bar; within backtest date window; bars since last entry > cooldown; SuperTrend direction flips up; directional signal score ≥ minimum; close > trend EMA; regime is not ranging; volume > its SMA; longs enabled.
Short: Current confirmed bar; within backtest date window; bars since last entry > cooldown; SuperTrend direction flips down; directional signal score ≥ minimum; close < trend EMA; regime is not ranging; volume > its SMA; shorts enabled.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR (target enabled AND high ≥ target price) OR (SuperTrend stop mode AND direction flips down) OR short-entry condition.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR (target enabled AND low ≤ target price) OR (SuperTrend stop mode AND direction flips up) OR long-entry condition.
