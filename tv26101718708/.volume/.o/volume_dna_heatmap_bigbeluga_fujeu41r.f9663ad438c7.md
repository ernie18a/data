Calculation: Volume Z-score = (current bar volume − rolling mean of volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume
Calculation: Spread Z-score = (current bar high − current bar low − rolling mean of high−low spread) / rolling standard deviation of high−low spread
Long: Volume Z-score ≥ volume threshold AND Spread Z-score ≥ half the spread threshold AND close ≥ open
Short: Volume Z-score ≥ volume threshold AND Spread Z-score ≥ half the spread threshold AND close < open
