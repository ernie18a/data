Calculation: smoothed Rate of Flow = WMA of Rate of Flow over a smoothing period. Rate of Flow = 2 × (100 − 100 ÷ (1 + average(positive flow) ÷ max(average(negative flow), ε))) − 100, where flow = mean of (1) bar close location within its range, (2) close−open as a percentage of its range, (3) normalized absolute close−open signed by its direction, (4) close location within the two-bar high-low range, (5) close change as a percentage of the two-bar high-low range, and (6) normalized absolute close change signed by its direction, multiplied by normalized volume ÷ 100. Normalize a value by dividing its absolute magnitude by its moving average over a common lookback, then assign 100 if the ratio > 1.50, 90 if > 1.20, 80 if > 1.00, 70 if > 0.80, 60 if > 0.60, 50 if > 0.40, 25 if > 0.20, otherwise 10; restore the original sign for signed price components. Normalize volume the same way without restoring sign. Positive flow is max(flow, 0); negative flow is −min(flow, 0). Average positive and negative flow over a common period using the selected Rate of Flow average type. Use the prior close for close change and the current and prior bars for two-bar ranges. If volume is unavailable, use normalized volume of 100.
Long: smoothed Rate of Flow ≥ 0
Short: smoothed Rate of Flow < 0
