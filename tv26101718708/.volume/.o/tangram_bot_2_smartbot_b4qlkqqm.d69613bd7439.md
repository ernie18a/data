Calculation: HiLo state = bullish when close > the lookback SMA of high; bearish when close < the lookback SMA of low; otherwise 0
Calculation: HiLo stop = lowest low over the lookback when HiLo state is bullish, highest high when bearish, otherwise the previous stop
Calculation: ATR stop = ATR-multiple below close if close and prior close are above the prior stop; ATR-multiple above close if both are below it; otherwise ATR-multiple below close when close is above the prior stop, or above close when it is not
Long: Entry is enabled, at least one entry indicator is enabled, and every enabled entry indicator is bullish: short moving average crosses above or is above long moving average; OR HiLo state changes from bearish to bullish or HiLo stop is below low; OR MACD line crosses above or is above its signal line and, if filtered, MACD line is at least the filter threshold; OR +DI crosses above or is above -DI, ADX is within its minimum and maximum, and any selected ADX trend filter is satisfied; OR stochastic %K crosses above or is above %D and %K is not above its maximum threshold; OR close crosses above or is above VWAP, or crosses below or is below VWAP when the inverse VWAP mode is selected; OR RSI crosses above its oversold threshold or is below it; OR source crosses above the lower Bollinger Band or is below it; OR close crosses above the ATR stop or is above it; OR close crosses above Parabolic SAR or is above it; all enabled entry indicators must satisfy their selected bullish condition; entry mode permits long; bar is confirmed and within the entry date and session; daily stop is not reached; and, if repeat prevention is enabled, the prior bar did not have all enabled entry indicators bullish
Short: Entry is enabled, at least one entry indicator is enabled, and every enabled entry indicator is bearish: short moving average crosses below or is below long moving average; OR HiLo state changes from bullish to bearish or HiLo stop is not below low; OR MACD line crosses below or is below its signal line and, if filtered, MACD line is at most the filter threshold; OR -DI crosses above or is above +DI, ADX is within its minimum and maximum, and any selected ADX trend filter is satisfied; OR stochastic %K crosses below or is below %D and %K is not below its minimum threshold; OR close crosses below or is below VWAP, or crosses above or is above VWAP when the inverse VWAP mode is selected; OR RSI crosses below its overbought threshold or is above it; OR source crosses below the upper Bollinger Band or is above it; OR close crosses below the ATR stop or is below it; OR close crosses below Parabolic SAR or is below it; all enabled entry indicators must satisfy their selected bearish condition; entry mode permits short; bar is confirmed and within the entry date and session; daily stop is not reached; and, if repeat prevention is enabled, the prior bar did not have all enabled entry indicators bearish
Exit: Long, at least one enabled exit indicator is bearish (using its selected entry signal condition) or all enabled exit indicators are bearish when exit-all mode is selected, while within the work session; OR close reaches the fixed stop below entry, fixed target above entry, or activated trailing stop
Exit: Short, at least one enabled exit indicator is bullish (using its selected entry signal condition) or all enabled exit indicators are bullish when exit-all mode is selected, while within the work session; OR close reaches the fixed stop above entry, fixed target below entry, or activated trailing stop
Exit: Long, outside the work session or daily profit/loss stop is reached
Exit: Short, outside the work session or daily profit/loss stop is reached
