Calculation: Swing low = a bar’s low that is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it; use the most recently confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Average body size = simple moving average of the absolute difference between close and open.
Long: On the still-forming order-block-timeframe bar, close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high AND volume > average volume × configured volume multiplier AND absolute(close − open) > average body size × configured body-size multiplier AND the nearest earlier bearish candle within the configured search window has at least one gap where low[j] > high[j+2] for j from 0 through 3.
