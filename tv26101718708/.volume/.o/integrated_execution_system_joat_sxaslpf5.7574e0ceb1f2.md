Calculation: Bull qualification score = 3 if bullish bias, else 0; + bullish momentum score + bullish structure score + 2 if trending regime + 1 each for buying pressure, low sweep, squeeze release, and higher-timeframe bullish MACD alignment.
Calculation: Bear qualification score = 3 if bearish bias, else 0; + bearish momentum score + bearish structure score + 2 if trending regime + 1 each for selling pressure, high sweep, squeeze release, and higher-timeframe bearish MACD alignment.
Calculation: Bullish bias = 30 if fast EMA > slow EMA > trend EMA, +20 if close > fast and slow EMA, +20 if close > trend EMA, +30 if +DI > -DI; bearish bias is the inverse. Bias qualifies at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Bullish momentum score = count of RSI above its bullish level, RSI above its value three bars ago, and MACD histogram above zero and rising; bearish momentum score counts the inverse conditions.
Calculation: Bullish structure score = count of close above the latest confirmed pivot low (or current low if none) and close above fast EMA; bearish structure score = count of close below the latest confirmed pivot high (or current high if none) and close below fast EMA.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR / its longer-period SMA. Trending regime = ADX at or above its threshold; volatile regime = volatility ratio at or above its expansion threshold while ADX is below its threshold; ranging regime = volatility ratio at or below its contraction threshold while ADX is below its threshold.
Calculation: Buying pressure = smoothed EMA of each bar's (close − low) / (high − low), or its increase over the pressure lookback, exceeds the respective threshold; selling pressure uses the inverse bar location or decrease. For zero-range bars, bar location is 0.5.
Calculation: Squeeze release = Bollinger Bands were inside Keltner Channels on the previous bar and are not inside them on the current bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe MACD alignment = the configured majority of the 1-hour, 4-hour, and daily MACD histograms have the same sign, using the latest completed bar for each timeframe.
Calculation: Low sweep = current low < latest confirmed pivot low and current close > that pivot low; high sweep = current high > latest confirmed pivot high and current close < that pivot high.
Calculation: Long risk = close − min(latest pivot low, close − ATR × adaptive stop distance); short risk = max(latest pivot high, close + ATR × adaptive stop distance) − close. Use close minus/plus ATR × adaptive stop distance if the respective pivot is unavailable. Adaptive stop distance is the base ATR multiple, adjusted by regime when adaptive stops are enabled: volatile × its multiplier, ranging × 0.85, trending × 1.1.
Calculation: Long trailing stop starts at the long stop; when close − entry price ≥ current long risk × trail-start multiple, raise it to the greater of its prior value and close − current long risk × trail-offset multiple. Short trailing stop starts at the short stop; when entry price − close ≥ current short risk × trail-start multiple, lower it to the lesser of its prior value and close + current short risk × trail-offset multiple.
Calculation: Profit targets = current close ± current risk × target multiple × volatility adjustment; adjustment is 1.3 when volatility ratio is at/above expansion threshold, 0.85 when at/below contraction threshold, otherwise 1. Long uses plus and short uses minus.
Long: Bull qualification score ≥ minimum score AND (close crosses above fast EMA OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR RSI > its value three bars ago AND RSI > 50 OR squeeze release AND buying pressure OR low sweep) AND (volatile regime is allowed) AND (structure filter disabled OR bullish structure score ≥ 1) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiplier) AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade limit not reached AND (MTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish alignment) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND (flat OR flipping from short is enabled).
Short: Bear qualification score ≥ minimum score AND (close crosses below fast EMA OR MACD histogram crosses below zero OR RSI < its value three bars ago AND RSI < 50 OR squeeze release AND selling pressure OR high sweep) AND (volatile regime is allowed) AND (structure filter disabled OR bearish structure score ≥ 1) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiplier) AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade limit not reached AND (MTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish alignment) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR bar is confirmed) AND (flat OR flipping from long is enabled).
Exit: Long, low ≤ trailing stop OR high ≥ second long profit target; also exit the remaining position when high ≥ first long profit target if scale-out is enabled.
Exit: Short, high ≥ trailing stop OR low ≤ second short profit target; also exit the remaining position when low ≤ first short profit target if scale-out is enabled.
