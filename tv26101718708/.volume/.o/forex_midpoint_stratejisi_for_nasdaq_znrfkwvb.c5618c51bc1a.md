Calculation: Smoothed moving average = selected moving average (SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA) of the SMA of close
Calculation: Adjusted midpoint = ((highest high over lookback + lowest low over the same lookback) / 2) × (1 + percentage), using its value from the specified number of bars earlier
Long: Smoothed moving average > adjusted midpoint AND previous bar’s smoothed moving average ≤ previous bar’s adjusted midpoint
Short: Smoothed moving average < adjusted midpoint AND previous bar’s smoothed moving average ≥ previous bar’s adjusted midpoint
