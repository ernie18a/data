Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the configured session opening window; freeze when that window ends
Calculation: Average Daily Range = average high minus low over the configured lookback of last completed daily bars
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of closes on the configured higher timeframe, using its last completed bar
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Breakout strength score = configurable weighted score of breakout candle body/range, candle range/ATR, volume/longer-lookback average volume, close momentum over short and longer lookbacks, penetration beyond the range edge/ATR, RSI direction, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, close versus the opening-window first bar's open, whether this is the day's first breakout, and opening-range width/average daily range; each factor is scored by configurable thresholds
Calculation: Stop = configured range-opposite-edge, range-midpoint, or ATR-based stop with ATR buffer, then set to the entry price plus or minus risk clamped between configured minimum and maximum ATR distances
Calculation: Targets = entry price plus or minus stop risk multiplied by each configured target R multiple
Long: opening range is formed AND current bar is in the configured trade window AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND (break buffer disabled OR close - opening-range high >= configured ATR fraction) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / average daily range is within the configured minimum and maximum) AND (no direction recorded today OR daily breakout limit > 1) AND strength score >= configured minimum AND (strong-only filter disabled OR strength score >= configured strong threshold) AND today's breakout count < configured daily limit AND no trade is active AND no exit occurred on this bar AND ATR is available
Short: opening range is formed AND current bar is in the configured trade window AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND (break buffer disabled OR opening-range low - close >= configured ATR fraction) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / average daily range is within the configured minimum and maximum) AND (no direction recorded today OR daily breakout limit > 1) AND strength score >= configured minimum AND (strong-only filter disabled OR strength score >= configured strong threshold) AND today's breakout count < configured daily limit AND no trade is active AND no exit occurred on this bar AND ATR is available AND no long signal occurs on this bar
Exit: long, bar low <= stop OR (bar high reaches a target and bar low <= stop on the same bar, unless a higher target stage was previously reached) OR stop is hit after a prior target stage (exit at that prior target) OR bar high >= highest target OR trade window ends
Exit: short, bar high >= stop OR (bar low reaches a target and bar high >= stop on the same bar, unless a higher target stage was previously reached) OR stop is hit after a prior target stage (exit at that prior target) OR bar low <= highest target OR trade window ends
