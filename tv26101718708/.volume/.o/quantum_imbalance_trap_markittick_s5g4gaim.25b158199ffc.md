Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume over the signal lookback.
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = close > open AND body-to-range ratio ≥ threshold AND volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiplier AND short-lookback close SMA > longer-lookback close SMA.
Calculation: Bearish imbalance = close < open AND body-to-range ratio ≥ threshold AND volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiplier AND short-lookback close SMA < longer-lookback close SMA.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the signal candle's low is below the low from the signal lookback earlier AND its RSI is above the RSI from that earlier bar; bearish divergence reverses both comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = the lowest low of the three bars before the signal candle is below the lowest low over the signal lookback, shifted four bars earlier, AND the signal candle closes above that reference; bearish liquidity sweep uses the highest highs and a close below the reference.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar compared with its 20-period close SMA from that same completed bar.
Long: The previous bar is a bullish imbalance AND the bar before it was not a bullish imbalance AND (liquidity-sweep filter disabled OR bullish liquidity sweep) AND (RSI-divergence filter disabled OR bullish divergence) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close > its SMA) AND (session filter disabled OR the previous bar is within an enabled London or New York session).
Short: The previous bar is a bearish imbalance AND the bar before it was not a bearish imbalance AND (liquidity-sweep filter disabled OR bearish liquidity sweep) AND (RSI-divergence filter disabled OR bearish divergence) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its SMA) AND (session filter disabled OR the previous bar is within an enabled London or New York session).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stored stop price (entry price − stop-distance multiplier × ATR on the signal bar); Short, high ≥ stored stop price (entry price + stop-distance multiplier × ATR on the signal bar).
