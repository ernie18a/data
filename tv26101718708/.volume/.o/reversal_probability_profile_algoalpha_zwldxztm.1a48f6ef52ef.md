Calculation: Pivot profile bins = divide the high-low range of the current and preceding calculation-lookback bars into equal price bins; count confirmed pivot highs and lows from within the pivot memory and lookback, adding each pivot to its bin and neighboring bins within the smoothing radius. A pivot high is a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the configured bars on both sides; a pivot low is a bar whose low is below the lows of the configured bars on both sides.
Calculation: Relative bin density = a bin’s smoothed pivot count divided by the highest smoothed pivot count across all bins.
Long: close is within a profile bin AND its relative bin density is at least the configured high-probability threshold AND its smoothed pivot-low count exceeds its smoothed pivot-high count.
Short: close is within a profile bin AND its relative bin density is at least the configured high-probability threshold AND its smoothed pivot-high count exceeds its smoothed pivot-low count.
