Calculation: Adaptive moving-average length = At each recalibration, test up to 25 ascending candidate lengths across the recent evaluation window. For each, score every close crossing its simple moving average by the signed return from the crossing close to the close the evaluation-forward-bars later (positive for an upward crossing, negated for a downward crossing); use the mean score if the candidate has at least the minimum event count. Adopt the candidate with the highest score; ties go to the shorter length. Keep the prior length if no candidate qualifies. Recalibrate only when the history buffer is full, then at each recalibration interval; use the adopted length between recalibrations.
Calculation: Adaptive RSI = Wilder RSI of close using the adaptive RSI length: seed average gains and losses from the first length close changes, then update each as (prior average × (length − 1) + current gain or loss) / length. For recalibration, calculate each candidate RSI the same way over the recent evaluation window, ignoring its first length + 30 bars. Score each oversold or overbought event by its forward return, positive for oversold and negated for overbought; average the scores if the candidate has at least the minimum event count. Adopt the candidate with the highest score; ties go to the shorter length. Keep the prior length if no candidate qualifies. Recalibrate only when the history buffer is full, then at each recalibration interval; use the adopted length between recalibrations.
Long: (moving-average mode AND close crosses above the adaptive moving average OR RSI mode AND adaptive RSI crosses above the oversold level) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ its simple moving average over the volume-confirmation lookback × the volume-confirmation multiplier)
Short: (moving-average mode AND close crosses below the adaptive moving average OR RSI mode AND adaptive RSI crosses below the overbought level) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ its simple moving average over the volume-confirmation lookback × the volume-confirmation multiplier)
