Calculation: Overnight VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 since 18:00 ET; update with the current bar.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 since 09:30 ET; update with the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish twin range filter = close above a recursively updated range-filter line; the line uses the average of fast- and slow-lookback exponentially smoothed absolute close changes, each scaled by its multiplier. The fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Long swing high = maximum high from the current green candle run through the following red candle run, confirmed at the next red-to-green flip. Long swing low = minimum low of that red run, confirmed at the same flip. A green candle has close >= open; a red candle has close < open, with a doji retaining the preceding candle's color.
Calculation: Short swing low = minimum low from the current red candle run through the following green candle run, confirmed at the next green-to-red flip. Short swing high = maximum high of that green run, confirmed at the same flip.
Long: During 09:45–12:00 ET, a pending structure has a latest confirmed swing high above the preceding confirmed swing high and a latest confirmed swing low above the preceding confirmed swing low; high >= the maximum high of the red pullback run; close > overnight VWAP AND close > session VWAP (when available) AND the latest completed 15-minute bar is bullish by the twin range filter; a confirmed touch of either VWAP during the regular session has armed entries since the prior long entry; price has not fallen below the higher swing low since the structure formed
Short: During 09:45–12:00 ET, a pending structure has a latest confirmed swing low below the preceding confirmed swing low and a latest confirmed swing high below the preceding confirmed swing high; low <= the minimum low of the green bounce run; close < overnight VWAP AND the latest completed 15-minute bar of the volatility index is bullish by the twin range filter; price has not risen above the lower swing high since the structure formed
