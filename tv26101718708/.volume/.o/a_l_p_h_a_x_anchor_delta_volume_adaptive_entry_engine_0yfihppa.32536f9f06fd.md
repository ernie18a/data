Calculation: Band 4 = outer one-way trailing band: in an uptrend, ratchet the greater of the previous Band 4 and close − ATR × (base band multiplier + 3) × adaptive scale; in a downtrend, ratchet the lesser of the previous Band 4 and close + ATR × (base band multiplier + 3) × adaptive scale. Flip the trend when close crosses the previous Band 4, then reset Band 4 to the opposite side of close at the same ATR offset. Adaptive scale is the minimum scale when Bollinger Bands are inside Keltner Channels, the maximum scale when ATR percentile rank over 100 bars exceeds 75, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Trend equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low since the latest trend flip, including the current bar.
Calculation: Volume delta = EMA of estimated bull volume minus EMA of estimated bear volume; estimated bull volume is volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half of volume when high = low; estimated bear volume is volume minus estimated bull volume. Use the shorter lookback parameter for volume smoothing.
Calculation: HVNs = local peak price-bin midpoints in the volume profile built from bars since the latest trend flip through the current bar. Divide the segment high-low range into equal bins; assign each bar's HLC3 to a bin and weight its volume by estimated bull volume in an uptrend or estimated bear volume in a downtrend. An HVN bin's volume exceeds both adjacent bins. HVN confluence is true when close or Band 4 is within 0.35 × ATR of any HVN.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of six votes: trend agrees with direction; higher-timeframe fast EMA is above (long) or below (short) slow EMA and higher-timeframe close is above (long) or below (short) fast EMA; ADX meets its minimum; volume delta is positive (long) or negative (short); close is below (long) or above (short) trend equilibrium; HVN confluence. For disabled HTF, ADX, volume-delta, or equilibrium filters, that layer votes true. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for its close and EMAs.
Long: Bullish trend AND low ≤ Band 4 + 0.35 × ATR AND close > Band 4 AND long confluence score ≥ minimum AND enabled session filter passes AND signal cooldown elapsed AND entry signals enabled.
Short: Bearish trend AND high ≥ Band 4 − 0.35 × ATR AND close < Band 4 AND short confluence score ≥ minimum AND enabled session filter passes AND signal cooldown elapsed AND entry signals enabled.
Long: Close crosses above the previous Band 4 and trend flips bullish.
Short: Close crosses below the previous Band 4 and trend flips bearish.
Exit: long, close < previous Band 4.
Exit: short, close > previous Band 4.
