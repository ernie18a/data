Calculation: PMARP = the percentile rank, over a lookback of daily bars, of source price divided by its moving average, using daily bars; the current daily bar may still be forming
Calculation: PMARP slope = current daily PMARP minus daily PMARP from a shorter lookback ago; the current daily bar may still be forming, and the earlier daily bar is completed
Long: PMARP slope > 0
Short: PMARP slope <= 0
