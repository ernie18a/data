Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of close
Long: Fast EMA > Slow EMA AND close > VWAP AND RSI > 55 AND close > open AND current bar is closed
Short: Fast EMA < Slow EMA AND close < VWAP AND RSI < 45 AND close < open AND current bar is closed
