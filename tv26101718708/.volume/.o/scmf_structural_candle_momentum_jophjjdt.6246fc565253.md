Calculation: Close Position Index = 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1; on a doji, use sign(open − previous close) × 0.30.
Calculation: Wick Absorption = clamp(((lower wick − upper wick) / (high − low)) × (1 + abs(close − open) / (high − low)) / 2, −1, 1); on a doji, use clamp((lower wick − upper wick) / (lower wick + upper wick) × 0.30, −1, 1), or 0 if both wicks are zero.
Calculation: Net Volume Delta = volume-weighted mean across lower-timeframe bars of the average of (close − low) / (high − low) and the tick direction (1 if close exceeds the previous intraday close, 0 if below, otherwise the former fraction), with each bar weighted by volume divided by max(1, range / mean intraday range); normalize to [−1, 1]. Multiply by 0.70 when its sign disagrees with the daily close’s position relative to volume-weighted average typical price.
Calculation: Temporal Volume Distribution = clamp(weighted volume across lower-timeframe bars × direction of the final conviction-window close versus its reference close, −1, 1); use normal phase weights on non-event bars and event-adjusted phase weights on event bars. If those closes are equal, use the sign of Close Position Index.
Calculation: Price Path Topology = clamp(final-direction × fraction of lower-timeframe close-to-close steps in the final direction + 0.30 × clamp(late normalized close change − early normalized close change, −1, 1), −1, 1); normalize changes by daily range, or ATR if range is zero. Split the lower-timeframe closes at the midpoint.
Calculation: Adjusted Composite Momentum = weighted mean of Close Position Index, Wick Absorption, Net Volume Delta, Temporal Volume Distribution, and Price Path Topology, renormalizing weights over available dimensions; suppress when the quality coefficient is below 0.25, the liquidity abort fires, or data validity fails. Regime-adjust weights: ranging emphasizes Close Position and Wick Absorption; trending emphasizes Net Volume Delta and Temporal Volume Distribution; otherwise use default weights.
Long: Adjusted Composite Momentum > 0.10 OR (Close Position Index < −0.35 AND Net Volume Delta > 0.25 AND Wick Absorption > 0.15 AND quality coefficient ≥ 0.25 AND liquidity abort is false AND data validity passes)
Short: Adjusted Composite Momentum < −0.10 OR (Close Position Index > 0.35 AND Net Volume Delta < −0.25 AND Temporal Volume Distribution < −0.15 AND quality coefficient ≥ 0.25 AND liquidity abort is false AND data validity passes)
