Calculation: Signal price = close, or Heikin Ashi close if selected
Calculation: UT trailing stop = recursively update from prior stop: if current and prior signal prices are above prior stop, take max(prior stop, current signal price − ATR × sensitivity); if both are below, take min(prior stop, current signal price + ATR × sensitivity); otherwise use current signal price − ATR × sensitivity when above prior stop, or + ATR × sensitivity when at or below it (initial prior stop = 0)
Calculation: Pressure oscillator = EMA(((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) × volume, smoothing length) / EMA(volume, same smoothing length); use 0 for the bar pressure when high = low, and 0 for the oscillator when the denominator is not positive
Long: Signal price > UT trailing stop AND prior signal price ≤ prior UT trailing stop AND close > EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND pressure oscillator > 0 AND low ≤ Fib 0.618 + close × zone tolerance AND high ≥ Fib 0.786 − close × zone tolerance AND bars since the prior long or short signal ≥ cooldown length
Short: Signal price < UT trailing stop AND prior signal price ≥ prior UT trailing stop AND close < EMA(close, trend lookback) AND volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND pressure oscillator < 0 AND high ≥ Fib 0.382 − close × zone tolerance AND low ≤ Fib 0.236 + close × zone tolerance AND bars since the prior long or short signal ≥ cooldown length
