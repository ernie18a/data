Calculation: SuperTrend = ATR-based trailing line and direction state; a flip occurs when the state changes from bearish to bullish or bullish to bearish.
Calculation: Flip probability = sigmoid of a direction-specific logistic regression score over the enabled default features: flip-candle body, upper wick, range, and volume relative to their recent averages; signed consecutive-candle momentum; 1-minute delta volume (up-close volume minus down-close volume, divided by total volume); average signed body relative to average range; ATR change from the lookback bars ago; SuperTrend distance divided by ATR; previous trend duration; RSI centered on 50; and close position relative to Bollinger basis and deviation. Normalize features to [-1, 1] as applicable. Train separate bull and bear models on resolved same-direction flips, labeled win when the close at the next opposite flip is respectively above or below the signal close, using exponentially age-weighted gradient descent with L2 regularization; predict only after the minimum resolved-sample gate is met.
Long: bullish SuperTrend flip AND resolved bull-signal count meets the minimum sample gate AND bull flip probability >= minimum probability.
Short: bearish SuperTrend flip AND resolved bear-signal count meets the minimum sample gate AND bear flip probability >= minimum probability.
Exit: long, bearish SuperTrend flip.
Exit: short, bullish SuperTrend flip.
