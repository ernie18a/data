Calculation: HalfTrend baseline = initialize trend=up, nextTrend=up, maxLow=current low, minHigh=current high, upLine=downLine=0; over the configurable amplitude lookback, find the low at the highest high (highPivot), the high at the lowest low (lowPivot), and the averages of highs and lows. If nextTrend is down, maxLow=max(lowPivot,maxLow); when averageHigh<maxLow and close<previous low, set trend=down, nextTrend=up, minHigh=highPivot. Otherwise, minHigh=min(highPivot,minHigh); when averageLow>minHigh and close>previous high, set trend=up, nextTrend=down, maxLow=lowPivot. When trend is up, on a down-to-up flip set upLine to the previous downLine; otherwise upLine=max(maxLow,previous upLine). When trend is down, on an up-to-down flip set downLine to the previous upLine; otherwise downLine=min(minHigh,previous downLine). Use the current bar's values; prior references are previous bars.
Calculation: HalfTrend bands = baseline ± (channel deviation × ATR(100)/2).
Calculation: Uptrend regime = turn on when close crosses above the upper band; turn off when close crosses below the lower band; otherwise retain its prior state.
Calculation: Pending top fractal = most recently confirmed five-bar Williams top fractal (its center high is greater than the other four highs), formed during the uptrend regime; clear it on a regime flip or after its breakout. The center is two bars before confirmation.
Calculation: Pending bottom fractal = most recently confirmed five-bar Williams bottom fractal (its center low is less than the other four lows), formed during the downtrend regime; clear it on a regime flip or after its breakout. The center is two bars before confirmation.
Long: uptrend regime AND a pending top fractal exists AND confirmed bar close > its level.
Short: downtrend regime AND a pending bottom fractal exists AND confirmed bar close < its level.
