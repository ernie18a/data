Calculation: Positive divergence = for an enabled indicator, compare its value at the current endpoint with its value at a prior confirmed pivot low, searching the configured number of recent pivots within the configured bar limit; require endpoint distance greater than the configured minimum. Regular: indicator higher and price lower; hidden: indicator lower and price higher. Price uses close for pivot source Close, otherwise low. For confirmed mode, endpoint is the prior bar; for unconfirmed mode, it is the current bar. Require either the indicator or close to rise from the preceding bar in confirmed mode. Between endpoints, neither indicator nor close may fall below its straight line between endpoint values.
Calculation: Negative divergence = for an enabled indicator, compare its value at the current endpoint with its value at a prior confirmed pivot high, searching the configured number of recent pivots within the configured bar limit; require endpoint distance greater than the configured minimum. Regular: indicator lower and price higher; hidden: indicator higher and price lower. Price uses close for pivot source Close, otherwise high. For confirmed mode, endpoint is the prior bar; for unconfirmed mode, it is the current bar. Require either the indicator or close to fall from the preceding bar in confirmed mode. Between endpoints, neither indicator nor close may rise above its straight line between endpoint values.
Long: At least the configured minimum number of enabled indicator divergences qualify as positive regular or hidden divergences, subject to the selected divergence types.
Short: At least the configured minimum number of enabled indicator divergences qualify as negative regular or hidden divergences, subject to the selected divergence types.
