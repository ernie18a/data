Calculation: FVG strength percentage = round(earned points / possible points × 100). Award one point each when the gap is at least the smaller ATR multiple, the gap is at least the larger ATR multiple, and the middle candle’s high-low range is at least the displacement ATR multiple. For each enabled optional score, add one possible point and award it when its condition holds: volume confirmation (previous bar volume > previous bar volume moving average × multiplier); trend confirmation (bullish: close > shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA; bearish: close < shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA); structure confirmation (bullish: close > highest high over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar; bearish: close < lowest low over that lookback); sweep confirmation (bullish: previous bar low < lowest low over the sweep lookback before that bar and previous bar closes above its open; bearish: previous bar high > highest high over that lookback and previous bar closes below its open). Use chart-timeframe bars; ATR, EMAs, and volume moving average include the current bar unless specified otherwise.
Long: low > high two bars earlier AND bullish FVG strength percentage >= minimum strength threshold
Short: high < low two bars earlier AND bearish FVG strength percentage >= minimum strength threshold
