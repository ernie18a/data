Calculation: Fair Value Gap = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier, spanning [high two bars earlier, current low]; bearish when current high < low two bars earlier, spanning [current high, low two bars earlier]. Track unfilled gaps, delaying mitigation by three bars; remove a gap when filled. A crossfire zone forms from the overlap of an unfilled gap and a new opposite-direction gap. An opposite gap overlapping a live zone flips it into the overlap and increments its star count. Retain live zones until mitigated.
Calculation: Zone retest = a bar overlaps a live zone by its high-low range after the preceding bar did not overlap it; the zone must have been created before the current bar.
Calculation: Break of Structure = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high plus a buffer for bullish direction, or below the latest confirmed pivot low minus a buffer for bearish direction. Track pivot highs and lows independently of zones.
Calculation: Displacement = trigger-bar range is at least a configurable multiple of ATR, and absolute body divided by range is at least a configurable threshold; optionally require a bullish close (close > open) for longs or bearish close (close < open) for shorts.
Calculation: Active VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close)/3, anchored at the day-open session start; use the session VWAP during its session after a configurable elapsed-time grace period, and otherwise use the day-open VWAP.
Calculation: Stop and target = entry is signal-bar close. Stop is beyond either the lowest low or highest high over a configurable lookback of preceding bars, or the zone's far edge, with a configurable tick buffer; target is entry plus (long) or minus (short) the stop distance times a configurable reward-to-risk multiple. If the calculated stop is on the wrong side of entry, place it one tick beyond entry.
Long: bullish zone retest OR ((BOS-required mode AND a qualifying bullish zone retest occurred within the configured bar window AND bullish BOS) OR (BOS-disabled mode AND bullish zone retest)) AND zone star count >= minimum AND (displacement disabled OR displacement passes for long) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > active VWAP) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured trading window) AND no prior long signal or configured cooldown bars elapsed AND bar is confirmed
Short: bearish zone retest OR ((BOS-required mode AND a qualifying bearish zone retest occurred within the configured bar window AND bearish BOS) OR (BOS-disabled mode AND bearish zone retest)) AND zone star count >= minimum AND (displacement disabled OR displacement passes for short) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < active VWAP) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in either configured trading window) AND no prior short signal or configured cooldown bars elapsed AND bar is confirmed
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= target
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= target
