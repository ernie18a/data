Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high and lowest low of the current day's premarket bars, fixed when that session ends
Calculation: Previous daily high/low = high and low of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Retest buffer = close × configured percentage buffer
Long: The first post-premarket close outside the premarket range was above the premarket high AND a later bar has low within the retest buffer of the premarket high and close >= premarket high OR low within the retest buffer of the previous daily high and close >= previous daily high AND close > chart-timeframe EMA AND close > session VWAP AND volume > chart-timeframe volume SMA AND datetime is within the configured entry window
Short: The first post-premarket close outside the premarket range was below the premarket low AND a later bar has high within the retest buffer of the premarket low and close <= premarket low OR high within the retest buffer of the previous daily low and close <= previous daily low OR high within the retest buffer of session VWAP and close <= session VWAP AND close < chart-timeframe EMA AND close < session VWAP AND volume > chart-timeframe volume SMA AND datetime is within the configured entry window
