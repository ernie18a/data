Calculation: Fast and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: ATR = average true range; ATR average = moving average of ATR
Calculation: Bullish EMA state = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA
Calculation: Bearish EMA state = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA
Calculation: Volume strength = volume compared with its moving average; strong volume exceeds the average times a configurable multiplier, and acceptable volume exceeds a lower configurable threshold
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint of the recent high-low range and the moving average of close; bullish when above its signal EMA and rising, bearish when below its signal EMA and falling
Calculation: Recent squeeze = a squeeze occurred within a configurable lookback; squeeze release = a squeeze was active on the previous bar and is no longer active
Calculation: Directional flux = EMA of close change divided by ATR; bullish when above its signal EMA and above zero, bearish when below its signal EMA and below zero
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low using equal configurable left and right lookbacks; retain the latest confirmed high and low
Calculation: Liquidity sweep down = price breaks below the latest confirmed swing low by an ATR-based margin and closes and opens above it, OR price breaks below the prior range low and closes above it with acceptable volume; liquidity sweep up is the inverse at highs
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low, once per swing; classify as change of character when the prior structure bias opposed the break, otherwise as break of structure; retain the resulting structure bias
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block retest = after a bullish/bearish structure break, price revisits the range of the latest bearish/bullish candle and closes above its low / below its high; deactivate the zone after a close beyond its invalidation boundary
Calculation: Fib impulse = from the latest opposing confirmed swing to the structure-break bar extreme, extending the extreme if price makes a new one; fib retracement zone is the configurable interval within that range; in-zone when the bar's high-low overlaps the zone
Calculation: Fib long confluence = bullish Fib impulse and in the retracement zone and (liquidity sweep down OR bullish order block retest OR momentum rising OR close above the midpoint); Fib short confluence is the inverse
Calculation: Range breakout = close crosses above the prior range high / below the prior range low with acceptable volume and either a recent squeeze or prior close on the other side; retain the level and allow a retest within a configurable bar window, requiring the bar to touch the level and close back above / below it
Calculation: Bullish candle = lower wick exceeds body by a configurable multiple and close > open, OR bullish engulfing candle; bearish candle is the inverse
Calculation: Bullish/bearish RSI divergence = confirmed price pivot makes a lower low / higher high while RSI at that pivot makes a higher low / lower high, compared with the preceding corresponding pivot
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA, bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA, otherwise neutral; use the last completed bar on each configured higher timeframe when closed-bar mode is enabled, otherwise the forming bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull/bear counts = number of configured higher-timeframe states that are bullish/bearish; long/short MTF confirmation requires the corresponding count to meet its configurable minimum; MTF not against a side means the opposing count is at most its configured limit
Calculation: Adaptive trail = close adjusted by ATR times a factor that increases as trend quality falls; update as a ratcheting trailing level according to consecutive closes above or below the prior trail
Calculation: Trend quality = clipped average of directional efficiency, normalized ADX, persistence of rising/falling squeeze momentum, and ATR stability; directional efficiency is absolute close change over its lookback divided by summed absolute close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Trend up = bullish EMA state AND close > adaptive trail AND ADX meets its trend threshold AND bullish higher-timeframe count meets its trend minimum; trend down is the inverse
Calculation: Late long = trend up AND close exceeds adaptive trail by an ATR-based distance AND RSI is high AND trend quality is below its strong threshold; late short is the inverse
Calculation: News block = enabled manual news time is within the configured pre-news interval; news-up/down = within the configured post-news interval AND candle direction and close-to-prior-close agree AND volume exceeds its average
Calculation: Long score = configurable weighted sum of: bullish EMA state, close above adaptive trail, ADX threshold met, strong or acceptable volume, bullish higher-timeframe count, bullish squeeze momentum, bullish flux, sweep down, bullish divergence, bullish candle, breakout up, retest up, bullish structure event/bias, bullish Fib confluence or bullish discount, bullish order-block retest/valid zone, strong/adequate trend quality, and news-up; subtract for pre-news block and for bullish Fib premium when trend quality is not strong; clamp to the score range
Calculation: Short score = inverse-side counterpart of long score: bearish EMA state, close below adaptive trail, ADX threshold met, strong or acceptable volume, bearish higher-timeframe count, bearish squeeze momentum, bearish flux, sweep up, bearish divergence, bearish candle, breakout down, retest down, bearish structure event/bias, bearish Fib confluence or bearish premium, bearish order-block retest/valid zone, trend quality, and news-down; subtract for pre-news block and bearish Fib discount when trend quality is not strong; clamp to the score range
Calculation: Long-ready = long score meets its configurable entry threshold AND exceeds short score by the configurable lead AND long MTF confirmation; short-ready is the inverse
Calculation: Early long setup = enabled AND (bullish squeeze with bullish flux OR sweep down with rising momentum or bullish candle OR bullish divergence with rising momentum OR upward momentum-signal cross with RSI low) AND not bearish context; early short setup is the inverse. Bearish context is trend down or a strong bearish higher-timeframe count; bullish context is trend up or a strong bullish higher-timeframe count
Calculation: Early long = early long setup AND bullish squeeze momentum AND bullish flux AND acceptable volume AND MTF not against long; early short is the inverse
Calculation: Pullback long = enabled AND trend up AND re-entry interval elapsed AND low reaches the fast EMA plus an ATR-based allowance AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND momentum rising AND long score meets the early threshold AND MTF not against long AND not late long; pullback short is the inverse
Calculation: Continuation long = trend up AND re-entry interval elapsed AND close exceeds the prior short-lookback high AND acceptable volume AND long score meets entry threshold AND long MTF confirmation AND not late long AND trend quality meets its weak threshold; continuation short is the inverse
Calculation: Safe long setup = retest up OR bullish order-block retest OR bullish Fib confluence OR pullback long OR (sweep down AND bullish candle AND MTF not against long); safe short is the inverse
Calculation: Aggressive long setup = breakout up OR bullish structure break OR early long OR continuation long OR news-up; aggressive short is the inverse
Calculation: Valid setup = safe setup in safe mode, aggressive setup in aggressive mode, or either setup in hybrid mode
Long: Confirmed bar AND position is not long AND not blocked by news AND not late long AND ((long-ready AND valid long setup) OR (early long AND long score meets its configurable early threshold AND long score exceeds short score by its configurable early lead AND MTF not against long) OR pullback long OR continuation long)
Short: Confirmed bar AND position is not short AND not blocked by news AND not late short AND ((short-ready AND valid short setup) OR (early short AND short score meets its configurable early threshold AND short score exceeds long score by its configurable early lead AND MTF not against short) OR pullback short OR continuation short)
Calculation: Break-even stop = entry price after the position's first target has been touched; otherwise the active stop
Calculation: Long exhaustion = trend up AND (RSI is high OR trend quality is falling below its weak threshold while momentum falls) AND close is far above fast EMA by an ATR-based distance AND volume is not strong; short exhaustion is the inverse
Exit: long, Confirmed bar AND no long signal AND (close < adaptive trail OR close < slow EMA OR close <= break-even stop OR short score exceeds long score by a configurable margin OR long exhaustion)
Exit: short, Confirmed bar AND no short signal AND (close > adaptive trail OR close > slow EMA OR close >= break-even stop OR long score exceeds short score by a configurable margin OR short exhaustion)
