Calculation: Bullish reversal score = 0.5 for a bullish engulfing pattern, 0.4 for a hammer in a ranging regime or 0.2 otherwise, 0.2 for a morning star, 0.2 for a piercing pattern, and 0.3 for a double bottom in a volatile regime or 0.15 otherwise; add the applicable values. Each pattern also requires price near the 20-bar low and volume greater than 1.5 times its 20-bar average. Bullish engulfing: previous close < previous open, current close > current open, current close > previous open, and current open < previous close. Hammer: range > 3 × absolute(open − close) and (close − low)/(0.001 + range) > 0.6. Morning star: two-bars-ago close < open, previous candle body < 0.2 × its range, current close > open, and current close > the midpoint of the two-bars-ago open and close. Piercing: previous close < open, current close > open, current close > the midpoint of the previous open and close, and current open < previous close. Double bottom: current low < previous low, previous low > low two bars ago, low two bars ago < low three bars ago, and current close > open. Near the 20-bar low means current low ≤ 1.01 × the lowest low of the last 20 bars.
Calculation: Bearish reversal score = 0.5 for a bearish engulfing pattern, 0.4 for a shooting star in a ranging regime or 0.2 otherwise, 0.2 for an evening star, 0.2 for a dark cloud, and 0.3 for a double top in a volatile regime or 0.15 otherwise; add the applicable values. Each pattern also requires price near the 20-bar high and volume greater than 1.5 times its 20-bar average. Bearish engulfing: previous close > previous open, current close < current open, current close < previous open, and current open > previous close. Shooting star: range > 3 × absolute(open − close) and (high − close)/(0.001 + range) > 0.6. Evening star: two-bars-ago close > open, previous candle body < 0.2 × its range, current close < open, and current close < the midpoint of the two-bars-ago open and close. Dark cloud: previous close > open, current close < open, current close < the midpoint of the previous open and close, and current open > previous close. Double top: current high > previous high, previous high < high two bars ago, high two bars ago > high three bars ago, and current close < open. Near the 20-bar high means current high ≥ 0.99 × the highest high of the last 20 bars.
Calculation: Market regime = trending if ADX(14) > 25 and the absolute difference between the 9-period EMA and 34-period EMA > 0.5 × ATR(12); otherwise ranging if ADX(14) < 20 and (highest high − lowest low over 20 bars)/close < 0.02; otherwise volatile if 20-period Bollinger Band width > 1.75 × its 50-bar average and ATR(12) > 1.5 × its 50-bar average; otherwise quiet if band width < 0.6 × its 50-bar average and ATR(12) < 0.7 × its 50-bar average; otherwise other. Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band)/20-period SMA(close), with bands at ±2 standard deviations.
Calculation: Bull score = bullish reversal score + 0.2 if 9-period EMA > 34-period EMA + 0.15 if RSI(14) < 30 + 0.15 if MACD(12,26,9) line > signal + 0.1 if ATR(12) > 1.2 × its 50-bar average.
Calculation: Bear score = bearish reversal score + 0.2 if 9-period EMA < 34-period EMA + 0.15 if RSI(14) > 70 + 0.15 if MACD(12,26,9) line < signal + 0.1 if ATR(12) > 1.2 × its 50-bar average.
Long: Bull score ≥ 1.0
Short: Bear score ≥ 1.0
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target
