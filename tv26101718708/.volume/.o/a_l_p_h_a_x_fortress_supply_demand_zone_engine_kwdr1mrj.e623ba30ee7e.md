Calculation: Demand zone = the range from the lowest low of the immediately preceding low-momentum base through the greater of the base high and bullish departure open; the departure bar must be bullish, explosive relative to ATR, and meet the body-quality filter and optional volume filter.
Calculation: Supply zone = the range from the lesser of the base low and bearish departure open through the highest high of the immediately preceding low-momentum base; the departure bar must be bearish, explosive relative to ATR, and meet the body-quality filter and optional volume filter.
Calculation: Zone grade = A+, A, B, or C from a score based on departure range/ATR, volume ratio (or a fixed score when volume confirmation is off), session VWAP proximity, overlap with an active same-side higher-timeframe zone, premium/discount placement at formation, and a base bonus.
Calculation: Equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the configured dealing-range lookback.
Calculation: Estimated volume delta = EMA of volume weighted by the bar's close location in its range minus EMA of the remaining volume; use the same smoothing lookback for both.
Long: At least one active demand zone has grade at or above the configured minimum, is unused when one-signal-per-zone is enabled, and is untested when fresh-zone-only is enabled; signal cooldown and configured session filter pass; close is above the trend EMA when enabled; ADX meets its minimum when enabled; close is below equilibrium when premium/discount filtering is enabled; estimated volume delta is positive when enabled; and either (low is within the zone from its bottom minus ATR tolerance through its top, close > open, close > zone bottom, and lower wick/bar range meets the configured minimum) OR (sweep signals are enabled, low < zone bottom minus minimum ATR sweep depth, close > zone bottom, body-close mitigation is satisfied when selected, and the zone has not already produced a sweep).
Short: At least one active supply zone has grade at or above the configured minimum, is unused when one-signal-per-zone is enabled, and is untested when fresh-zone-only is enabled; signal cooldown and configured session filter pass; close is below the trend EMA when enabled; ADX meets its minimum when enabled; close is above equilibrium when premium/discount filtering is enabled; estimated volume delta is negative when enabled; and either (high is within the zone from its bottom through its top plus ATR tolerance, close < open, close < zone top, and upper wick/bar range meets the configured minimum) OR (sweep signals are enabled, high > zone top plus minimum ATR sweep depth, close < zone top, body-close mitigation is satisfied when selected, and the zone has not already produced a sweep).
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × configured target distance OR low <= entry price - ATR at entry × configured stop distance OR close < the demand-zone bottom.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - ATR at entry × configured target distance OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × configured stop distance OR close > the supply-zone top.
