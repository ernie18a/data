Calculation: RSI score = 5 if RSI < 20, 4 if RSI < 30, 1 if RSI > 80, 2 if RSI > 70, otherwise 3.
Calculation: EMA score = 1 for a strict descending stack across six EMAs ordered shortest to longest; 2 if the shortest > second and second < third; 3 if the shortest > second > third and third < fourth; 4 if the first four are strictly ascending and the full six are not; 5 for a strict ascending stack across all six; otherwise 3.
Calculation: MACD score = 1 if MACD < 0, histogram < 0, and histogram has fallen for three bars; 2 if MACD < 0 and (histogram ≥ 0 or histogram has risen for three bars); 3 if MACD crosses above its signal line at or below zero, or crosses below it at or above zero; 5 if MACD > 0, histogram > 0, MACD has risen for five bars, and histogram has risen for three bars; 4 if MACD > 0, histogram > 0, and (MACD > signal line or histogram has risen for three bars); otherwise 3. Use a shorter fast moving average and a longer slow moving average for MACD; the signal line is a moving average of MACD.
Calculation: ADX score = 1 if ADX < 20 and DI− > DI+; 2 if 20 ≤ ADX < 25 and DI− > DI+; 3 if 20 ≤ ADX < 25 and |DI+ − DI−| is within the configured equality tolerance; 4 if 25 ≤ ADX ≤ 40 and DI+ > DI−; 5 if ADX > 40 and DI+ > DI−; otherwise 3.
Calculation: Ichimoku score = 1 if Tenkan < Kijun, close is below the cloud, and close is below its value from one cloud displacement ago; 2 if Tenkan < Kijun and (close is inside the cloud or is not below its value from one displacement ago); 3 if Tenkan crosses Kijun while close is inside the cloud; 5 if Tenkan > Kijun, close is above the cloud, close is above its value from one displacement ago, and the current cloud is bullish and thickening; 4 if Tenkan > Kijun, close is above the cloud, and close is above its value from one displacement ago; otherwise 3. Compare close with the cloud formed by spans from one displacement ago; use current spans to assess future-cloud direction and thickening.
Calculation: Bollinger score = 1 if close < lower band, or close ≤ lower band while the lower band or basis has fallen for three bars; 5 if close > upper band, or close ≥ upper band while the upper band or basis has risen for three bars; 2 if lower band ≤ close < basis; 4 if basis < close ≤ upper band; otherwise 3.
Calculation: OBV score = 1 if OBV is below its moving average and has fallen over the short lookback; 2 if OBV is below its moving average and has risen over the short lookback, or is at least the moving average times (1 − near-MA tolerance); 3 if OBV is within the near-MA tolerance of its moving average; 4 if OBV is above its moving average and has risen over the short lookback; 5 if OBV is above its moving average, has risen over the longer lookback, and its one-bar increase exceeds the spike multiplier times the average absolute OBV change; otherwise 3. The longer rising lookback exceeds the shorter one. OBV is cumulative volume signed by whether close rose or fell from the previous bar.
Long: RSI score ≥ 4 OR EMA score ≥ 4 OR MACD score ≥ 4 OR ADX score ≥ 4 OR Ichimoku score ≥ 4 OR Bollinger score ≥ 4 OR OBV score ≥ 4
Short: RSI score ≤ 2 OR EMA score ≤ 2 OR MACD score ≤ 2 OR ADX score ≤ 2 OR Ichimoku score ≤ 2 OR Bollinger score ≤ 2 OR OBV score ≤ 2
