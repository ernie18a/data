Calculation: EMA-MACD score = +2 when fast EMA > next EMA and close > medium EMA, else −2 when fast EMA < next EMA and close < medium EMA; +1 when close > long EMA, else −1 when close < long EMA; +2 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, else −2 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; +1 on MACD bullish crossover, else −1 on bearish crossover; +1 when RSI > bullish level, else −1 when RSI < bearish level; +1 when ADX ≥ trend minimum and +DI > −DI, else −1 when ADX ≥ trend minimum and −DI > +DI; +1 when volume > its moving average and close > open, else −1 when volume > its moving average and close < open; and, when enabled, +1 when higher-timeframe close > its EMA, else −1 when higher-timeframe close < its EMA. Use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: BuySell score = +2 when RSI ≥ bullish level, else −2 when RSI ≤ bearish level; +1 when RSI crosses up through its oversold level, else −1 when it crosses down through its overbought level; +2 when smoothed Stochastic %K > %D and %K > 50, else −2 when %K < %D and %K < 50; add +1 on a %K/%D bullish crossover below the overbought level, else −1 on a bearish crossover above the oversold level; add +1 when %K crosses up through the oversold level, else −1 when it crosses down through the overbought level; +2 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, else −2 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; +1 on MACD bullish crossover, else −1 on bearish crossover; when enabled, +1 for fast EMA > slow EMA and close > trend EMA, else −1 for fast EMA < slow EMA and close < trend EMA; when enabled, +1 when volume > its moving average and close > open, else −1 when volume > its moving average and close < open. Use shorter and longer lookbacks for fast and slow averages, and smooth %K and MACD signal with their configured lookbacks.
Calculation: Mean = EMA of close; stretch = ATR; use the same ATR for entry stretch and stop distance.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price makes a lower low and the oscillator a higher low than at the prior pivot low; regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, price makes a higher high and the oscillator a lower high than at the prior pivot high. Hidden bullish divergence = pivot-low price higher low and oscillator lower low; hidden bearish divergence = pivot-high price lower high and oscillator higher high. Require pivots to be within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation; confirm each pivot after the configured right-side bars.
Long: In mean-reversion mode, close < mean − stretch × ATR AND short-period RSI < oversold level AND long trend gate passes; OR in momentum mode, selected signal fires AND long trend gate passes AND ADX ≥ trend minimum AND close − fast EMA ≤ maximum extension × ATR AND RSI < maximum-long level. EMA-MACD signal fires when its score first reaches the positive entry threshold; BuySell signal fires when its score first reaches its positive entry threshold. Both-agree mode requires both signals; either-signal mode requires either; EMA-MACD-only and BuySell-only modes require their respective signal. The long trend gate is close > long EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled, higher-timeframe EMA unavailable, or higher-timeframe close > its EMA), unless trend alignment is disabled. In automatic mode, use mean reversion on shorter intraday charts and EMA-MACD momentum on longer charts. Also mark long when EMA-MACD score crosses into its positive entry threshold; or when EMA-MACD score enters its positive threshold-minus-one band from below; or when EMA-MACD score ≥ 2 OR BuySell score ≥ 3; or on a regular or enabled hidden bullish divergence.
Short: In mean-reversion mode, close > mean + stretch × ATR AND short-period RSI > overbought level AND short trend gate passes; OR in momentum mode, shorts are enabled AND selected signal fires AND short trend gate passes AND ADX ≥ trend minimum AND fast EMA − close ≤ maximum extension × ATR AND RSI > minimum-short level. EMA-MACD signal fires when its score first reaches the negative entry threshold; BuySell signal fires when its score first reaches its negative entry threshold. Both-agree mode requires both signals; either-signal mode requires either; EMA-MACD-only and BuySell-only modes require their respective signal. The short trend gate is close < long EMA AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled, higher-timeframe EMA unavailable, or higher-timeframe close < its EMA), unless trend alignment is disabled. In automatic mode, use mean reversion on shorter intraday charts and EMA-MACD momentum on longer charts. Also mark short when EMA-MACD score crosses into its negative entry threshold; or when EMA-MACD score enters its negative threshold-plus-one band from above; or when EMA-MACD score ≤ −2 OR BuySell score ≤ −3; or on a regular or enabled hidden bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × applicable stop multiplier OR high ≥ target (mean for mean reversion; otherwise entry price + entry ATR × stop multiplier × reward/risk) OR held bars ≥ maximum hold OR, outside mean-reversion mode, an opposite short signal when flip exit is enabled OR both scores = 0 when neutral exit is enabled.
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × applicable stop multiplier OR low ≤ target (mean for mean reversion; otherwise entry price − entry ATR × stop multiplier × reward/risk) OR held bars ≥ maximum hold OR, outside mean-reversion mode, an opposite long signal when flip exit is enabled OR both scores = 0 when neutral exit is enabled.
