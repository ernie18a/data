Calculation: Upper-wick trapped-buyer volume = sum over lower-timeframe bars of their volume-per-price-range, adjusted by the ratio of that value to its 20-bar SMA (clamped to 0.5–2.5), times the bar’s clamped close-position fraction, allocated in proportion to overlap with the parent bar’s upper wick. Count only when the upper wick is at least the configured minimum share of the parent bar’s range; if deep-wick mode is enabled, count only overlap midpoints in the outer half of the wick.
Calculation: Lower-wick trapped-seller volume = same allocation using the parent bar’s lower wick and the lower-timeframe bar’s clamped sell-strength (1 minus close-position fraction); apply the corresponding lower-wick and optional deep-wick conditions.
Calculation: ML-adjusted trap volume = trap volume multiplied by twice the success fraction among the configured number of nearest prior trap bars, when enough labeled samples exist; otherwise use raw trap volume. Similarity uses normalized body/ATR, upper- and lower-wick proportions, close position, range/ATR, and both trap volumes. A prior upper-wick trap succeeds if the next close is below its close; a prior lower-wick trap succeeds if the next close is above its close.
Calculation: Visible-volume cutoff = configured lower percentile of trap volumes in the retained label cache.
Long: ML-adjusted lower-wick trapped-seller volume >= minimum trap volume AND ML-adjusted lower-wick trapped-seller volume >= visible-volume cutoff
Short: ML-adjusted upper-wick trapped-buyer volume >= minimum trap volume AND ML-adjusted upper-wick trapped-buyer volume >= visible-volume cutoff
