Calculation: ATR = average true range of the current chart bars
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low with the configured number of bars on each side; use the last confirmed pivot
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = closing price above the latest confirmed swing pivot high / below the latest confirmed swing pivot low; swing pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots
Calculation: Structure displacement = the greater of the absolute close-to-close move from the broken pivot bar and the absolute distance from the close to the broken pivot level, divided by ATR
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the selected recent bearish/bullish candle zone associated with a bullish/bearish swing structure break whose displacement meets the minimum; require a three-candle gap between the break bar and that candle
Long: bullish structure break AND structure displacement >= minimum AND (the break is a change of character OR no same-direction break was marked within the minimum spacing) OR bullish swing structure break AND structure displacement >= minimum AND a qualifying bullish order block is formed OR low > high two bars earlier AND low - high two bars earlier >= minimum gap size in ATR multiples OR price sweeps below a clustered equal-low liquidity level and closes at or above it OR close is below the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high-low range
Short: bearish structure break AND structure displacement >= minimum AND (the break is a change of character OR no same-direction break was marked within the minimum spacing) OR bearish swing structure break AND structure displacement >= minimum AND a qualifying bearish order block is formed OR high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier - high >= minimum gap size in ATR multiples OR price sweeps above a clustered equal-high liquidity level and closes at or below it OR close is above the midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high-low range
