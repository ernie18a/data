Calculation: Nearest magnet = strike-step multiple nearest to current close.
Calculation: Pin range = current-bar ATR × configured width.
Calculation: Pin pressure score = clamp(58 × clamp(1 − distance from close to nearest magnet / pin range, 0, 1) + 26 × clamp((1.20 − current ATR / EMA of ATR) / 0.55, 0, 1) + 16 × clamp(1.25 − relative volume, 0, 1), 0, 100); relative volume = current volume / SMA of volume.
Calculation: Delta pressure = EMA of candle close location × current volume / max(SMA of volume, 1) × 100; candle close location = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Delta pressure slope = current delta pressure − previous bar’s delta pressure.
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Body share = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Prior pin pressure = highest pin pressure score over the configured release lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Release energy = clamp((prior pin pressure − 45) × 0.72 + (current ATR / EMA of ATR − 1) × 38 + (relative volume − 1) × 16 + abs(delta pressure) × 0.20 + clamp(abs(close − previous bar’s nearest magnet) / pin range, 0, 2.2) × 16, 0, 100).
Long: signal cooldown has elapsed AND ((prior pin pressure ≥ fade threshold AND close > previous bar’s nearest magnet + pin range AND previous close ≤ previous bar’s nearest magnet + pin range AND current ATR / EMA of ATR > 1.04 AND relative volume > 1.05 AND body share > 0.45 AND release energy ≥ release threshold AND delta pressure > 0) OR (low ≤ nearest magnet − 0.72 × pin range AND close > nearest magnet − 0.38 × pin range AND lower wick > 1.25 × upper wick AND delta pressure slope > 0 AND highest pin pressure score over the current and previous 3 bars ≥ fade threshold)).
Short: signal cooldown has elapsed AND ((prior pin pressure ≥ fade threshold AND close < previous bar’s nearest magnet − pin range AND previous close ≥ previous bar’s nearest magnet − pin range AND current ATR / EMA of ATR > 1.04 AND relative volume > 1.05 AND body share > 0.45 AND release energy ≥ release threshold AND delta pressure < 0) OR (high ≥ nearest magnet + 0.72 × pin range AND close < nearest magnet + 0.38 × pin range AND upper wick > 1.25 × lower wick AND delta pressure slope < 0 AND highest pin pressure score over the current and previous 3 bars ≥ fade threshold)).
