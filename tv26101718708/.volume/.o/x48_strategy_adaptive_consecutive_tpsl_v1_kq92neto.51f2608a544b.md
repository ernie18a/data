Calculation: Fast, slow, mid-term, and long-term moving averages = configurable moving-average types of close on the current bar; their lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Fast and slow ATR trails = recursively updated close-based trails using ATR times a multiplier; the slow trail uses a longer ATR lookback and larger multiplier. Initialize the prior trail to 0. If close and prior close are both above the prior trail, trail = max(prior trail, close − ATR × multiplier); if both are below, trail = min(prior trail, close + ATR × multiplier); otherwise trail = close − ATR × multiplier when close is above the prior trail, and close + ATR × multiplier otherwise.
Long: Close has risen for the configured number of consecutive bars AND close > long-term moving average AND fast moving average > long-term moving average.
Short: Close has fallen for the configured number of consecutive bars AND close < long-term moving average AND fast moving average < long-term moving average.
Exit: Long, close has fallen for the configured stop count AND close > long-term moving average AND close two bars ago, one bar ago, and now are each below the mid-term moving average OR fast moving average crossed below mid-term moving average OR close < highest high over the configured lookback × (1 − callback percentage) OR fast ATR trail crossed below slow ATR trail OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage).
Exit: Short, close has risen for the configured entry count AND close < long-term moving average AND close two bars ago, one bar ago, and now are each above the mid-term moving average OR fast moving average crossed above mid-term moving average OR close > lowest low over the configured lookback × (1 + callback percentage) OR fast ATR trail crossed above slow ATR trail OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
