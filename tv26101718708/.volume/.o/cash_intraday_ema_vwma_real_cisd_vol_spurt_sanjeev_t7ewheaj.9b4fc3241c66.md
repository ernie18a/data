Calculation: CISD = Start with the first non-doji candle’s direction as the state and its open-based level: for an up state, the lowest open in the contiguous up-candle run ending at that candle; for a down state, the highest open in the contiguous down-candle run. Skip doji candles when finding runs. While in an up state, each new high updates the level from the latest up-candle run; while in a down state, each new low updates it from the latest down-candle run. A close above the level in a down state is bullish CISD; a close below the level in an up state is bearish CISD. On a CISD event, switch state and reset the level from the current candle’s same-direction run.
Calculation: Volume spurt = volume exceeds its moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Long: bullish CISD OR close > open AND volume spurt OR short-term EMA crosses above short-term VWMA AND medium-term EMA > longer-term VWMA
Short: bearish CISD OR close < open AND volume spurt OR short-term EMA crosses below short-term VWMA AND medium-term EMA < longer-term VWMA
