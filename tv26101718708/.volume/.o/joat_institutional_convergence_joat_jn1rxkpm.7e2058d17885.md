Calculation: Fast, middle, and slow Hann filters = weighted averages of close over progressively longer lookbacks, using weight 1 − cos(2πi/(length+1)) for lag i=0…length−1; the middle lookback is longer than the fast, and the slow is longer than the middle.
Calculation: Structure trend = start neutral; detect a swing high when the previous bar’s high was the lookback high and the current high is lower than the current lookback high; detect a swing low analogously using lows. On each detected swing high, set bullish if its price exceeds the previous swing high; on each detected swing low, set bearish if its price is below the previous swing low. A confirmed close above the latest swing high sets bullish structure and a close below the latest swing low sets bearish structure. Otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish break of structure = confirmed close above the latest swing high, or below the latest swing low, respectively, if that swing level has not already triggered a break.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish fair value gap = most recent three-bar gap where the middle bar closes above its open and the first bar’s high is below the current bar’s low, or where the middle bar closes below its open and the first bar’s low is above the current bar’s high, respectively.
Long: (Fast Hann filter crosses above middle Hann filter OR bullish break of structure AND fast Hann filter > slow Hann filter) AND structure trend ≥ 0 AND ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds inclusive AND (session filter disabled OR current UTC weekday hour is in an enabled Asia (01:00–09:00), London (07:00–16:00), or New York (13:00–22:00) session) AND (FVG filter disabled OR close is within the configured ATR distance of the latest bullish fair value gap) AND flat position
Short: (Fast Hann filter crosses below middle Hann filter OR bearish break of structure AND fast Hann filter < slow Hann filter) AND structure trend ≤ 0 AND ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds inclusive AND (session filter disabled OR current UTC weekday hour is in an enabled Asia (01:00–09:00), London (07:00–16:00), or New York (13:00–22:00) session) AND (FVG filter disabled OR close is within the configured ATR distance of the latest bearish fair value gap) AND flat position
Exit: long, close < entry price − max(entry price − (lowest low over 20 bars − ATR buffer), 0.5 ATR) OR close ≥ entry price + max(entry price − (lowest low over 20 bars − ATR buffer), 0.5 ATR) × reward-to-risk ratio OR price reaches the trailing stop OR bearish break of structure
Exit: short, close > entry price + max((highest high over 20 bars + ATR buffer) − entry price, 0.5 ATR) OR close ≤ entry price − max((highest high over 20 bars + ATR buffer) − entry price, 0.5 ATR) × reward-to-risk ratio OR price reaches the trailing stop OR bullish break of structure
