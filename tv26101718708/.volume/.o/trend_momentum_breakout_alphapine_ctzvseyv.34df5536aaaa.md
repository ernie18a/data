Calculation: Active trendlines = On each confirmed bar, form resistance lines between descending swing highs and support lines between ascending swing lows. Use 5/5 pivots for smaller structure and 25/25 pivots for larger structure, after the configured maturity delay. Feed matured pivots into separate ATR zigzags: same-direction pivots replace the prior pivot only when more extreme; an opposite pivot joins only after a move greater than the prior pivot's ATR times the structure multiplier, with the larger-structure multiplier greater than the smaller one. Pair each new zigzag point with earlier points within its configured scan depth (larger depth longer than smaller); require the configured minimum touches, counting the two anchors and same-side 3/3 pivots for smaller lines or 5/5 pivots for larger lines within the configured ATR tolerance. Reject lines whose intervening candle bodies crossed the line, whose slope violates configured limits, whose first anchor is outside the lookback, or whose projected extension has expired. Retire overlapping or nested lines according to structure scale and touch count, and cap active lines per side and scale by retiring the oldest. Extend each admitted line from its anchors; its maximum extension is its anchor distance times a factor increasing with touch count.
Long: close > active resistance trendline for the configured number of consecutive confirmed closes AND (distance filter disabled OR close − trendline ≥ configured ATR multiple × ATR(14)) AND (volume filter disabled OR at least one of those closes has volume > configured multiple × SMA(volume, 20)) AND (MA filter off OR close > selected moving average)
Short: close < active support trendline for the configured number of consecutive confirmed closes AND (distance filter disabled OR trendline − close ≥ configured ATR multiple × ATR(14)) AND (volume filter disabled OR at least one of those closes has volume > configured multiple × SMA(volume, 20)) AND (MA filter off OR close < selected moving average)
Exit: long, close < the broken resistance trendline within the configured negation window after the long decision
Exit: short, close > the broken support trendline within the configured negation window after the short decision
