Calculation: timeframe score = 0.35 × trend direction + 0.25 × momentum direction + 0.20 × candle direction + 0.20 × trend direction × volume strength, where trend direction is 1 if close is above the medium-term EMA, −1 if below, otherwise 0; momentum direction is 1 if close is above its longer-lookback close, −1 if below, otherwise 0; candle direction is 1 if (close − open)/(high − low) > 0.25, −1 if below −0.25, otherwise 0 (0 when high = low); volume strength is 1 if volume / its longer-lookback average is at least 1.5, 0.5 if at least 1, otherwise 0. Calculate on the last completed bar of each configured timeframe.
Calculation: final score = 0.10 × score on the shortest configured timeframe + 0.15 × score on the next-shortest + 0.25 × score on the middle timeframe + 0.30 × score on the next-longest + 0.20 × score on the longest timeframe.
Long: final score ≥ 0.70 AND close > medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA OR final score > 0 OR any configured timeframe score > 0
Short: final score ≤ −0.70 AND close < medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA OR final score < 0 OR any configured timeframe score < 0
