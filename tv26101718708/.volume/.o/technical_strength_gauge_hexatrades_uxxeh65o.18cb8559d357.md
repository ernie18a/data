Calculation: Indicator vote = +1 for a bullish condition, −1 for a bearish condition, and 0 otherwise; average votes within each group using their configured weights, excluding disabled votes and unavailable volume votes.
Calculation: Leading rating = weighted average of RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Williams %R, MFI, ROC, Momentum, Ultimate Oscillator, Awesome Oscillator, Bollinger midpoint, Donchian midpoint, Aroon, Stochastic RSI, TSI, Fisher Transform, and CMO votes.
Calculation: Lagging rating = weighted average of MACD histogram, ten moving-average families (EMA, SMA, VWMA, WMA, HMA, RMA, ALMA, DEMA, TEMA, KAMA) at five lengths ordered shortest to longest, Supertrend, ADX, Ichimoku, Parabolic SAR, linear-regression slope, Vortex, and DMI votes. Each moving-average vote is bullish when close is above the average and bearish when below.
Calculation: Summary rating = (Leading rating + Lagging rating) / 2. Calculate ratings on the selected analysis timeframe, using its last completed bar when close confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: RSI, CCI, Williams %R, MFI, Ultimate Oscillator, Stochastic, Stochastic RSI, and CMO votes = bullish when oversold and turning up; bearish when overbought and turning down. For Stochastic, both %K and %D must be beyond the same threshold and %K must cross %D in the reversal direction.
Calculation: ROC vote = bullish above zero, bearish below zero. Momentum vote = bullish when rising versus its prior value, bearish when falling. MACD histogram vote = bullish when positive and rising, bearish when negative and falling.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator vote = bullish on crossing above zero or forming a rising local trough; bearish on crossing below zero or forming a falling local peak. TSI vote = bullish above its signal line and rising, bearish below its signal line and falling. Fisher Transform vote = bullish above zero and rising, bearish below zero and falling.
Calculation: Supertrend vote = its bullish or bearish direction. ADX vote = bullish when ADX exceeds its threshold and +DI > −DI; bearish when ADX exceeds its threshold and +DI < −DI. DMI vote = bullish when +DI > −DI, bearish when +DI < −DI.
Calculation: Ichimoku vote = bullish when leading span 1 > leading span 2 and close is above leading span 1, base line, and conversion line; bearish when leading span 1 < leading span 2 and close is below leading span 2, base line, and conversion line.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR vote = bullish when close is above SAR, bearish when below. Bollinger and Donchian votes = bullish when close is above their midpoint, bearish when below. Aroon vote = bullish when Aroon Up > Aroon Down, bearish when Aroon Up < Aroon Down. Linear-regression vote = bullish when its value rises, bearish when it falls. Vortex vote = bullish when positive movement exceeds negative movement, bearish when negative movement exceeds positive movement.

Short: Summary rating crosses below the negative normal-buy or strong-buy trigger.
