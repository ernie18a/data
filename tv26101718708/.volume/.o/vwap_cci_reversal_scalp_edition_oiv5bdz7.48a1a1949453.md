Calculation: Daily VWAP and standard deviation bands = from the start of the current day through the current bar, volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of (high + low + close) / 3; form bands at configurable standard-deviation multiples.
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = from the start of the current week through the current bar, volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Smoothed CCI = EMA-smoothed Commodity Channel Index of (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Chikou touch = within a configurable recent-bar lookback, the current close (or the segment from the prior close to the current close) overlaps the high-low range from a configurable number of bars earlier, allowing a configurable tick tolerance; use 1-minute bars when enabled on a chart above 1 minute, otherwise chart bars.
Long: Smoothed CCI crosses above -100 AND its lowest value over a configurable recent lookback is below -120 AND low is within the buffered zone from the lower 2-standard-deviation band to the lower 3-standard-deviation band (or, in wide-zone mode, close is below the lower 1-standard-deviation band) AND (weekly VWAP bias filter is off OR close > Weekly VWAP) AND (volume confirmation is off OR volume > its configurable-length SMA × multiplier) AND (reversal-candle confirmation is off OR close > open) AND (CCI extreme-visit filter is off OR the recent extreme condition is met) AND (Chikou filter is off OR Chikou touch).
Short: Smoothed CCI crosses below +100 AND its highest value over a configurable recent lookback is above +120 AND high is within the buffered zone from the upper 2-standard-deviation band to the upper 3-standard-deviation band (or, in wide-zone mode, close is above the upper 1-standard-deviation band) AND (weekly VWAP bias filter is off OR close < Weekly VWAP) AND (volume confirmation is off OR volume > its configurable-length SMA × multiplier) AND (reversal-candle confirmation is off OR close < open) AND (CCI extreme-visit filter is off OR the recent extreme condition is met) AND (Chikou filter is off OR Chikou touch).
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × either take-profit multiplier.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry × either take-profit multiplier.
