Calculation: Active BSL/SSL levels = confirmed swing-high/swing-low pivot prices using equal left and right lookbacks; create each level only after the right-side bars complete. Merge a new pivot within an ATR-based tolerance of an active same-side level, keeping the higher BSL or lower SSL price; a swept or broken level becomes inactive.
Calculation: Bullish EMA stack = shorter-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA; bearish EMA stack = shorter-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its longer-lookback simple moving average.
Long: An active SSL is swept when low < SSL − minimum ATR-based pierce and close > SSL; within the confirmation window, a later close > the sweep bar's high and close >= its low, with the bullish confluence score >= the minimum score. The score counts enabled bullish EMA-stack and relative-volume-above-threshold conditions.
Short: An active BSL is swept when high > BSL + minimum ATR-based pierce and close < BSL; within the confirmation window, a later close < the sweep bar's low and close <= its high, with the bearish confluence score >= the minimum score. The score counts enabled bearish EMA-stack and relative-volume-above-threshold conditions.
