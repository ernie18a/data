Calculation: Swing score = weighted sum of bullish criteria: close > short-term EMA; short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA; relative strength > its threshold; highest high minus lowest low over a lookback < close × its threshold; current low > an earlier low; volume > average volume; close / highest high over a longer lookback > its threshold; and SPY close > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA. Relative strength and SPY trend use SPY daily bars; use the latest available daily bar, including the still-forming bar when applicable.
Calculation: Relative strength = (close / close at the start of the lookback) / (SPY daily close / SPY daily close at the start of the same lookback).
Long: Swing score >= setup threshold OR (swing score >= elite threshold AND close > short-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA AND relative strength > its threshold AND SPY daily close > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA)
