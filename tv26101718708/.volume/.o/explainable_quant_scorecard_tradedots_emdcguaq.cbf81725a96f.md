Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when the shorter-period EMA of close is above the longer-period EMA; bearish when below; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend score = clamp to 0–100 the min-max normalization, over a trend normalization lookback, of the shorter-period EMA’s change over a short lookback.
Calculation: Momentum score = clamp to 0–100 the min-max normalization, over a generic normalization lookback, of close’s rate of change over a momentum lookback.
Calculation: Volatility regime score = 30 for a low Bollinger Bandwidth percentile, 70 for a high percentile, and 50 otherwise; calculate the percentile over the generic normalization lookback.
Calculation: Participation score = volume percentile over the generic normalization lookback, multiplied by a close-location alignment factor: close location within the bar’s high-low range for bullish trend direction, its inverse for bearish direction, and neutral alignment otherwise; blend this factor with 0.5 alignment, then clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Relative strength score = clamp to 0–100 the percentile rank, over a longer lookback, of (asset return minus benchmark return over the same return horizon) divided by the standard deviation of the asset’s one-bar rate of change over a volatility lookback. Use the last completed benchmark bar.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = clamp to 0–100 the value 100 minus 25 times the absolute distance between close and the shorter-period EMA divided by ATR.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe alignment score = average across three higher timeframes of 100 when that timeframe’s fast/slow EMA direction matches chart-timeframe trend direction, 50 when its direction is neutral, and 0 otherwise. Use each timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Composite score = weighted average of the seven scores. When ADX is at or above the trend threshold, multiply trend and momentum weights by 1.5 and exhaustion weight by 0.5; when ADX is at or below the range threshold, multiply trend and momentum weights by 0.5 and exhaustion weight by 1.5; otherwise use base weights. Leave other weights unchanged.
Long: Composite score is at or above the high-score threshold AND trend direction is bullish.
Short: Composite score is at or above the high-score threshold AND trend direction is bearish.
