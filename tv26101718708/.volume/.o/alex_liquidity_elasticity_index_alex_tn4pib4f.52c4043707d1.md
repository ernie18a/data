Calculation: ALEX score = median-filter close and volume with the same median-smoothing length, then Gaussian-filter each with the same short filter length. Let illiquidity = (filtered close − prior filtered close) / (filtered close × filtered volume); z = (illiquidity − its SMA over the calculation window) / its standard deviation over that window, using 0.0001 if the standard deviation is zero. Let price momentum = ROC of filtered close over the calculation window; volume momentum = ROC of the SMA of filtered volume over that same window, measured over that same window. Let raw = z × (price momentum − volume momentum), base = raw / standard deviation of raw over twice the calculation window (use 1 if zero), signal = Gaussian-filter base with the signal-smoothing length, and macro = median of signal over the short median window. Normalize macro over the scaling lookback using its rolling high and low: scaled = 200 × (macro − low) / (high − low) − 100 (use 0 if high equals low). Score = 100 × (exp(2 × scaled / 100) − 1) / (exp(2 × scaled / 100) + 1).
Calculation: Upper extreme = min(90, 50 + score standard deviation over the scaling lookback × volatility sensitivity × 0.5); lower extreme = max(−90, −50 − score standard deviation over the scaling lookback × volatility sensitivity × 0.5).
