Calculation: Long/short range pulse = 100 × ((close − lowest low over the longer lookback) ÷ (highest high over the longer lookback − lowest low over the longer lookback)) × ((close − lowest low over the shorter lookback) ÷ (highest high over the shorter lookback − lowest low over the shorter lookback)); use selected-resolution bars, with the shorter lookback less than the longer lookback. The selected-resolution bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Fear EKG = 100 × (1 − long-range close position) × (1 − short-range close position), where each close position is (close − lowest low) ÷ (highest high − lowest low) over the corresponding lookback; use selected-resolution bars, with the shorter lookback less than the longer lookback. The selected-resolution bar may still be forming.

Long: Fear EKG one chart bar ago < extreme threshold AND Fear EKG two chart bars ago ≥ extreme threshold
Exit: Long, long/short range pulse one chart bar ago < profit-taking threshold AND long/short range pulse two chart bars ago ≥ profit-taking threshold
