Calculation: Order-flow index = T3-smoothed ratio over a lookback of summed volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low + epsilon) − 1) to summed volume; use current-chart bars.
Calculation: Momentum shift = the current index lies between its two immediately preceding values, with the preceding value being a local peak or trough.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = higher-timeframe close compared with its 50-period SMA; use higher-timeframe bars and the value supplied by lookahead_off (the current higher-timeframe bar’s value, repeated across chart bars until updated).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe momentum = (higher-timeframe 14-period RSI − 50)/50; use higher-timeframe bars and the same lookahead_off value.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe volatility filter = higher-timeframe 14-period ATR exceeds 0.8 times its 20-period SMA; use higher-timeframe bars and the same lookahead_off value.
Calculation: Local trend = close above or below its 50-period SMA, or neutral when equal.
Calculation: Signal volatility filter = local 14-period ATR exceeds 0.8 times its 50-period SMA, AND higher-timeframe volatility filter is true.
Calculation: Signal filter = signal volatility filter AND (local trend is non-neutral OR higher-timeframe trend is non-neutral).
Calculation: Local momentum signal = (14-period RSI − 50)/50; confidence = its absolute value.
Calculation: Mean-reversion signal = −(close − 20-period SMA)/(2 × 20-period standard deviation); confidence = the absolute value of (close − 20-period SMA)/(2 × 20-period standard deviation).
Calculation: Order-flow strategy signal = 1.5 × order-flow index when momentum shifts, otherwise the index; confidence = the index’s absolute value, multiplied by 1.3 and capped at 1 when momentum shifts.
Calculation: Trend strategy signal = (14-period ADX − 25)/25; confidence = its absolute value.
Calculation: Strategy agreement = bullish if at least the required number of selected strategy signals exceed 0.2; bearish if at least that number are below −0.2; otherwise neutral. Select the first configured number of strategies in this order: local momentum, mean reversion, order flow, trend, higher-timeframe momentum.
Calculation: Composite signal = confidence-weighted mean of selected strategy signals, multiplied by (1 + 0.382 × local trend direction) and (1 + 0.382 × higher-timeframe trend direction), then clamped to [−1, 1]. Trend direction is +1 above its SMA, −1 below, and 0 when equal.
Calculation: L-function = tanh of the weighted mean of the latest close-to-prior-close returns, where each return is (close[n−1] − close[n])/close[n], weighted by Möbius μ(n)/n^s; use the configured lookback and omit unavailable history.
Calculation: Structure rank = percentile rank of 14-period ATR over the Frobenius lookback, divided by 100; price-volume correlation = correlation of close and volume over that lookback.
Calculation: Correspondence = 1 − absolute difference between the L-function and price-volume correlation.
Calculation: Raw score = L-function × its weight + structure score × its weight + composite signal × its weight + (correspondence × sign of composite signal) × its weight. Structure score is negative structure rank when 14-period ATR exceeds 1.2 times its 20-period SMA, otherwise positive structure rank; force it negative when higher-timeframe trend is bearish. Multiply a negative raw score by 1.5; multiply a positive raw score by 1.5. For positive scores, additionally multiply by 1.2 when not in market stress, by 1.3 when order-flow index > 0.1, and by 1.15 when higher-timeframe trend is bullish. For negative scores, additionally multiply by 1.2 in market stress, by 1.3 when order-flow index < −0.1, and by 1.15 when higher-timeframe trend is bearish. Clamp to [−1, 1] and multiply by 10.
Calculation: Flow accelerating = absolute order-flow index exceeds its immediately preceding value.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle closes above its open, prior candle closes below its open, current open is below prior close, and current close is above prior open. Morning star = two-bars-prior candle is bearish, prior candle body is less than 30% of its range, and current candle is bullish with close above the two-bars-prior close. Three white soldiers = three consecutive bullish candles with successively higher closes. Bullish pattern is any of these.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle closes below its open, prior candle closes above its open, current open is above prior close, and current close is below prior open. Evening star = two-bars-prior candle is bullish, prior candle body is less than 30% of its range, and current candle is bearish with close below the two-bars-prior close. Three black crows = three consecutive bearish candles with successively lower closes. Bearish pattern is any of these.
Long: flat position AND more than 10 chart bars since the last entry AND ((signal filter AND score > 0.6 × score threshold AND (strategy agreement bullish OR neutral)) OR (order-flow index > 0.15 AND composite signal > 0.2) OR (bullish pattern AND score > 0.3 × score threshold AND order-flow index > 0) OR (higher-timeframe trend bullish AND momentum shift AND order-flow index > 0) OR (order-flow index > 0.3 AND score > 0)).
Short: flat position AND more than 10 chart bars since the last entry AND ((signal filter AND score < −0.8 × score threshold AND (strategy agreement bearish OR neutral)) OR (order-flow index < −0.15 AND composite signal < −0.2) OR (bearish pattern AND score < −0.4 × score threshold AND order-flow index < 0) OR (higher-timeframe trend bearish AND momentum shift AND order-flow index < 0) OR (order-flow index < −0.3 AND score < 0)).
Exit: long, score < 1 OR signal filter is false OR (order-flow index < −0.15 AND flow accelerating).
Exit: short, score > −1 OR signal filter is false OR (order-flow index > 0.15 AND flow accelerating).
