Calculation: Active support and resistance zones = ATR-width zones seeded at confirmed pivot lows and highs using ATR from the pivot bar; merge same-side candidates within the ATR merge distance only if the merged height stays within the ATR height limit; invalidate support when close is below its lower boundary minus the ATR buffer and resistance when close is above its upper boundary plus the ATR buffer.
Long: A confirmed bar completes the configured evaluation window for an active support zone touch, where the touch began on the first overlap after a non-overlapping bar, cooldown since the prior touch is greater than the evaluation window plus two bars, and the zone is not invalidated.
Short: A confirmed bar completes the configured evaluation window for an active resistance zone touch, where the touch began on the first overlap after a non-overlapping bar, cooldown since the prior touch is greater than the evaluation window plus two bars, and the zone is not invalidated.
