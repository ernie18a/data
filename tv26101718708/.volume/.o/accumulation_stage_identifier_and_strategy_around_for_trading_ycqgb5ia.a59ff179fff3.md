Calculation: PVT = cumulative sum of volume × (close − previous close) ÷ previous close
Calculation: MACD of PVT = short-lookback EMA of PVT − longer-lookback EMA of PVT; signal line = shorter-lookback EMA of MACD
Long: close > 200-period SMA AND 50-period SMA > 90-period SMA AND high > previous bar’s high AND 200-period SMA > its previous-bar value AND MACD of PVT > its signal line
Short: MACD of PVT < its signal line AND low < previous bar’s low AND close < previous bar’s close AND close < open
