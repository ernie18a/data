Calculation: Active zones = bullish/bearish order blocks created by a close beyond the latest unbroken confirmed swing pivot with a sufficiently large candle body, using the most recent prior opposite-color candle as the zone; and three-bar fair value gaps meeting minimum gap-size and optional middle-candle-body filters. A bullish gap is current low above the high two bars earlier; bearish is current high below the low two bars earlier. Use body or full range for order-block bounds as configured. Remove zones on close through their far boundary, expiry, or side-cap trimming; a zone can trigger only once.
Calculation: Zone mass = ATR-normalized displacement body for order blocks or ATR-normalized gap height for gaps, multiplied by a volume-percentile factor. Effective mass decays exponentially with zone age using a half-life.
Calculation: Gravity bias = 100 × (bullish field − bearish field) / (bullish field + bearish field), where each field sums active zones’ effective mass divided by (squared ATR-normalized distance from close to zone midpoint + softening epsilon squared).
Calculation: Structure regime = bullish after a close above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high; bearish after a close below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low; otherwise retain the prior regime.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted composite of structure regime, signed Kaufman efficiency ratio, and clamped ATR-normalized Hull moving-average slope. Trend state switches bullish or bearish when the score crosses the corresponding entry threshold and returns neutral when its absolute value falls below the lower exit threshold; otherwise retain its prior state.
Calculation: Zone rejection = bullish when a later bar’s low reaches or passes an active bullish zone’s top and its close is at or above that top; bearish when its high reaches or passes an active bearish zone’s bottom and its close is at or below that bottom. If multiple unused zones qualify on a bar, use the one with greatest effective mass.
Calculation: Signal quality = weighted composite of trend alignment, gravity alignment, triggering-zone effective-mass rank among active same-side zones, and rejection strength (bullish: close’s position from low to high; bearish: high’s position from low to high, measured inversely).
Long: Bullish zone rejection AND trend state bullish AND (gravity bias is positive if gravity alignment is required) AND signal quality meets the primary threshold AND position is flat or short AND cooldown has elapsed.
Long: Bullish zone rejection in an existing long position AND zone was born after the previous entry bar AND trend state is not bearish AND (gravity bias is positive if gravity alignment is required) AND signal quality meets the re-add threshold AND add count is below its cap AND cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Bearish zone rejection AND trend state bearish AND (gravity bias is negative if gravity alignment is required) AND signal quality meets the primary threshold AND position is flat or long AND cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Bearish zone rejection in an existing short position AND zone was born after the previous entry bar AND trend state is not bullish AND (gravity bias is negative if gravity alignment is required) AND signal quality meets the re-add threshold AND add count is below its cap AND cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, trend state becomes bearish OR a bearish structure break occurs.
Exit: short, trend state becomes bullish OR a bullish structure break occurs.
