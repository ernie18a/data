Calculation: Directional midpoint = average of the fast EMA and fast HMA of close; structural midpoint = average of the slower EMA and slower HMA of close.
Calculation: Regime strength = clamp(0.60 × abs(directional midpoint − structural midpoint) / ATR × 100 + 0.80 × abs(heat − 50), 0, 100), where heat = 100 × (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over a rolling heat window.
Calculation: Regime persistence = consecutive completed bars for which directional midpoint > structural midpoint has remained unchanged.
Calculation: Liquidity bias = (buy volume − sell volume) / max(buy volume + sell volume, 1). Over a rolling liquidity lookback, assign each bar’s volume to a price bin by its HLC3 within the current lookback’s highest-high to lowest-low range; count it as buy volume when close ≥ open, otherwise sell volume. Sum volume across bins.
Calculation: Bullish structure reference = most recent confirmed pivot low, or the rolling swing low if none exists; bearish structure reference = most recent confirmed pivot high, or the rolling swing high if none exists. Pivot highs/lows use equal left and right pivot lengths.
Calculation: Bull gap = current low > high two bars ago AND intervening high > high two bars ago AND (current low − high two bars ago) / max(200-bar standard deviation of (low − high two bars ago), minimum tick) > gap threshold.
Calculation: Bear gap = current high < low two bars ago AND intervening low < low two bars ago AND (low two bars ago − current high) / max(200-bar standard deviation of (low two bars ago − high), minimum tick) > gap threshold.
Calculation: Pressure RSI = RSI of close momentum, using shorter momentum and RSI lookbacks.
Calculation: Displacement = clamp(40 × abs(fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR + abs(pressure RSI − 50), 0, 100).
Calculation: Long stop at entry = min(entry-bar close − ATR × stop multiple, min(bullish structure reference, entry-bar low) − ATR × 0.15); long risk = max(entry-bar close − long stop, 10 × minimum tick). Long targets = entry-bar close + long risk × first or second target multiple.
Calculation: Short stop at entry = max(entry-bar close + ATR × stop multiple, max(bearish structure reference, entry-bar high) + ATR × 0.15); short risk = max(short stop − entry-bar close, 10 × minimum tick). Short targets = entry-bar close − short risk × first or second target multiple.
Calculation: Active long stop = entry stop, ratcheted upward to the maximum of itself and the current 5-bar lowest low − ATR × 0.20 when trailing is enabled and regime strength ≥ 60. Active short stop = entry stop, ratcheted downward to the minimum of itself and the current 5-bar highest high + ATR × 0.20 under the same condition.
Long: confirmed bar AND directional midpoint > structural midpoint AND regime strength ≥ regime threshold AND regime persistence ≥ persistence threshold AND liquidity bias > liquidity threshold AND close ≥ EMA(close, 55) AND close > bullish structure reference AND close > slow EMA AND (bull gap OR (confirmed bar AND displacement ≥ displacement threshold AND pressure RSI > 54 AND close > fast EMA AND directional midpoint > structural midpoint) OR (confirmed bar AND continuation enabled AND pressure RSI ≥ continuation threshold AND close > directional midpoint AND low ≤ fast EMA)) AND position size ≤ 0.
Short: confirmed bar AND directional midpoint ≤ structural midpoint AND regime strength ≥ regime threshold AND regime persistence ≥ persistence threshold AND liquidity bias < −liquidity threshold AND close ≤ EMA(close, 55) AND close < bearish structure reference AND close < slow EMA AND (bear gap OR (confirmed bar AND displacement ≥ displacement threshold AND pressure RSI < 46 AND close < fast EMA AND directional midpoint < structural midpoint) OR (confirmed bar AND continuation enabled AND pressure RSI ≤ 100 − continuation threshold AND close < directional midpoint AND high ≥ fast EMA)) AND position size ≥ 0.
Exit: Long, low ≤ active long stop OR high ≥ first long target OR high ≥ second long target.
Exit: Short, high ≥ active short stop OR low ≤ first short target OR low ≤ second short target.
