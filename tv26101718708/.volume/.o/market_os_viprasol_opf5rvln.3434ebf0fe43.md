Calculation: Adaptive baseline = Kaufman Adaptive Moving Average of close; its efficiency-ratio lookback and fast/slow smoothing parameters are configurable.
Calculation: Structure reversal up = after the required count of bearish continuation breaks, close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; structure reversal down = after the required count of bullish continuation breaks, close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. Confirm pivots using bars on both sides; maintain structure direction and continuation-break counts, resetting counts after a reversal.
Calculation: Market score = weighted composite of direction, quality, participation, and cost. Direction score is highest when structure direction agrees with close above/below the adaptive baseline, intermediate when structure is set but disagrees, and lowest when unset. Quality score scales smoothed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio by its trending threshold, capped at its maximum. Participation score is highest for high relative volume and high close-to-close movement relative to ATR; lowest for high relative volume and low movement; intermediate for low volume with high movement; otherwise neutral. Cost score is neutral when unavailable; otherwise it decreases as the rolling EDGE OHLC effective-spread estimate ranks higher in its historical percentile. Use configurable component weights.
Calculation: Signal grade = highest grade at or above the upper score threshold, middle grade at or above the minimum signal threshold, otherwise lowest grade.
Long: Upward structure reversal AND close > adaptive baseline AND minimum bars since the previous signal have elapsed AND (market score >= minimum signal threshold OR lowest-grade signals are enabled) AND (signal grade is not the lowest OR lowest-grade signals are enabled).
Short: Downward structure reversal AND close < adaptive baseline AND minimum bars since the previous signal have elapsed AND (market score >= minimum signal threshold OR lowest-grade signals are enabled) AND (signal grade is not the lowest OR lowest-grade signals are enabled).
