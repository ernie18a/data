Calculation: Timeframe state = Bull when close > fast EMA > slow EMA, the trend Supertrend is bullish, RSI is above its midline, MACD histogram is rising, and ADX meets that timeframe’s minimum; Bear when the inverse conditions hold; otherwise Flat. Use the current chart bar for the chart timeframe and each enabled higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Bull/bear counts = number of enabled timeframes, including the chart timeframe, with that state.
Calculation: Active timeframe count = number of enabled timeframes whose state is not Flat.
Calculation: Required alignment = with adaptive denominator, max(active timeframe count − allowed disagreements, min(total enabled timeframes, 2)); otherwise max(total enabled timeframes − allowed disagreements, 1).
Calculation: Weighted bull/bear percentage = sum of weights for enabled timeframes in that state divided by the effective total weight, times 100. The effective total is the sum of active timeframe weights when adaptive denominator is enabled and at least two timeframes are active; otherwise it is the sum of all enabled timeframe weights.
Calculation: Average ADX = arithmetic mean of ADX across the chart timeframe and all enabled higher timeframes; use each higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = current volume compared with its moving average over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Trailing exit Supertrend = a separate chart-timeframe Supertrend used for exits. If progressive ladder tightening is enabled, reduce its factor per reached profit tier, with a floor of one quarter of the configured factor.
Long: long confluence qualifies (in equal-weight mode, bull count ≥ required alignment and > 0; in weighted mode, weighted bull percentage ≥ minimum required percentage) AND no enabled higher-timeframe opposition veto applies AND (average ADX gate disabled OR average ADX ≥ its minimum) AND (ADX-rising gate disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX > its value at the configured lookback) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR current volume ≥ its moving average × confirmation multiplier) AND the long confluence gate has remained open for the configured consecutive bars and has just become confirmed AND current position is not long AND (cooldown disabled OR no recent long trailing-stop exit within the cooldown bars).
Short: short confluence qualifies (in equal-weight mode, bear count ≥ required alignment and > 0; in weighted mode, weighted bear percentage ≥ minimum required percentage) AND no enabled higher-timeframe opposition veto applies AND (average ADX gate disabled OR average ADX ≥ its minimum) AND (ADX-rising gate disabled OR chart-timeframe ADX > its value at the configured lookback) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR current volume ≥ its moving average × confirmation multiplier) AND the short confluence gate has remained open for the configured consecutive bars and has just become confirmed AND current position is not short AND (cooldown disabled OR no recent short trailing-stop exit within the cooldown bars).
Exit: long, trailing exit Supertrend direction flips from bullish to bearish.
Exit: short, trailing exit Supertrend direction flips from bearish to bullish.
