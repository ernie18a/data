Calculation: MA alignment score = +1 when close > MA1 > MA2 > MA3 > MA4 > MA5 > MA6 > MA7; −1 when all inequalities are reversed; otherwise, if partial alignment is enabled, count the seven upward comparisons (close > MA1 through MA6 > MA7): score +0.5 for count >5, −0.5 for count <2, +0.25 for count >3, otherwise −0.25; if partial alignment is disabled, score 0. MA1–MA7 are the configured type of moving average of close, with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: MA alignment direction = direction of the rolling sum of MA alignment scores: compare it with its value t bars ago when the sum is positive, otherwise with its value r bars ago; return +1 if greater, −1 if less, otherwise retain the last nonzero direction.
Calculation: High/low value = +1 when the current high equals the highest high over the configured lookback; otherwise −1 when the current low equals the lowest low over that lookback; otherwise 0.
Calculation: High/low direction = direction of the rolling sum of high/low values, using the same lookback and t-bar/r-bar comparison and last-nonzero rule as MA alignment direction.
Calculation: Yearly high/low filter = use the high and low of the last completed 12-month bar, plus their values 365 and 730 chart bars earlier. The high condition is true if the filter is disabled, or the current yearly high exceeds both available earlier yearly highs, or close exceeds the current yearly high when no earlier high is available, the greater of the current and one earlier high when only one earlier high is available, or the greater of the current high and the lesser of the two earlier highs when both are available. The low condition is the inverse: current yearly low below both available earlier yearly lows, or close below the current low, the lesser of the current and one earlier low, or the lesser of the current low and the greater of the two earlier lows, according to availability; it is also true when the filter is disabled.
Calculation: Risk-free = for a long, Supertrend > entry price + ATR × Supertrend multiplier × risk/reward; for a short, Supertrend < entry price − ATR × Supertrend multiplier × risk/reward; false when flat.
Calculation: Trailing stop = Supertrend when not risk-free; when risk-free, use Supertrend + 2 ATR if Supertrend direction is −1, otherwise Supertrend − 2 ATR. For a long, do not lower the stop below its prior value; for a short, do not raise it above its prior value.
Long: MA alignment direction = +1 AND high/low direction = +1 AND yearly high condition AND configured moving average of close > its value at the shorter lag AND Supertrend direction ≠ +1 AND low > trailing stop AND (flat OR risk-free).
Short: MA alignment direction = −1 AND high/low direction = −1 AND yearly low condition AND configured moving average of close < its value at the shorter lag AND Supertrend direction ≠ −1 AND low < trailing stop AND (flat OR risk-free).
Exit: long, Supertrend direction = +1 OR low ≤ trailing stop.
Exit: short, Supertrend direction = −1
