Calculation: Williams VIX Fix = (highest close over its lookback − current low) / highest close over its lookback × 100
Calculation: Volatility spike = Williams VIX Fix ≥ its upper Bollinger Band OR Williams VIX Fix ≥ the prior lookback’s highest Williams VIX Fix × percentile threshold
Calculation: Long take-profit levels = entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier × each target R multiple; short take-profit levels = entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier × each target R multiple
Long: (current or previous bar is a volatility spike) AND close > open AND close > previous close AND (spike-strength filter disabled OR Williams VIX Fix z-score ≥ Bollinger multiplier + configured strength threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR ≥ its same-length moving average × configured minimum multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × configured minimum multiplier AND volume > previous volume) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close > its 21-bar SMA > that SMA on the prior completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend SMA > its value five bars earlier) AND (cooldown disabled OR bars since previous long signal ≥ configured cooldown)
Short: Williams VIX Fix crosses below its Bollinger basis AND a volatility spike occurred within the recent-spike window AND close < open AND close < previous close AND (volatility filter disabled OR ATR ≥ its same-length moving average × configured minimum multiplier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × configured minimum multiplier AND volume > previous volume) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR previous completed higher-timeframe close < its 21-bar SMA < that SMA on the prior completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend SMA < its value five bars earlier) AND (cooldown disabled OR bars since previous short signal ≥ configured cooldown)
Exit: Long, short-entry condition OR high ≥ any long take-profit level
Exit: Short, long-entry condition OR low ≤ any short take-profit level
