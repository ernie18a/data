Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close
Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of the stated field
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: Weekly 10 EMA = 10-period EMA of close on the currently forming weekly bar
Calculation: Previous daily high, low, close = values from the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Prior 10-bar low = lowest low of the 10 bars before the current bar
Calculation: Volume average = 20-bar SMA of volume
Calculation: Base breakout = close crosses above the high of a tracked base lasting at least 10 bars; track a confirmed pivot high (5 bars left and 3 right, or 3 left on weekly/monthly charts), requiring at least 10% prior rise unless no base high exists; track the lowest low since the pivot, reset the high to a new high if reached before 10 bars, and discard if depth exceeds 65% or duration exceeds 250 bars. Classify the breakout as high-tight flag if duration ≤25 and depth ≤12%; cup-with-handle if the recent quarter's low is in the upper half of the base and depth ≥15%; double bottom if the mid-peak between the two troughs reaches the lower 75% of the base and depth ≥15%; flat base if duration ≤30 and depth ≤15%; otherwise base breakout.
Long: (close > EMA(10) AND close > EMA(20) AND (previous close ≤ previous EMA(10) OR previous close ≤ previous EMA(20)) AND close > open AND at least 2 of the previous 4 closes < their corresponding EMA(10)) OR (low < previous high AND low > previous low) OR (previous candle upper wick > 40% of its range AND open > max(previous open, previous close) AND open < previous high AND close > open) OR (low < previous low AND close > previous high AND close > open) OR (low < prior 10-bar low AND close > prior 10-bar low AND close > open) OR (abs(close − open) ≥ ATR(10) × 1.5 AND close position ≥ 0.75 AND volume > volume average × 1.2) OR (volume ≥ volume average × 2 AND close position ≥ 0.80) OR (low < prior 10-bar low AND close > prior 10-bar low AND close > open) OR Base breakout
