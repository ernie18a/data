Calculation: State baseline = Gaussian-weighted average of the current and previous 23 closes, with weights decreasing by lag.
Calculation: Trend direction = sign of 0.65 × recursive Kalman velocity + 0.35 × change in state baseline, each divided by ATR; positive above a small positive threshold, negative below its negative, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend quality = clamp(0.60 × 20-bar linear-regression fit quality + 0.40 × absolute 20-bar directional efficiency ratio, 0, 1); fit quality is 1 minus the standard deviation of regression residuals divided by the standard deviation of closes.
Calculation: Market state = trend if trend quality meets the profile-adjusted continuation threshold and price is within the profile-adjusted ATR stretch limit of the state baseline; chop if trend quality is below its fixed threshold or price exceeds that stretch limit; otherwise transition. Adopt a state only after its raw classification persists for at least two bars.
Calculation: Gate = normalized trend strength exceeds the profile-adjusted gate threshold, absolute 10-bar directional efficiency ratio meets its profile-adjusted minimum, and absolute close-to-baseline distance in ATRs is within its profile-adjusted maximum. Trend strength combines absolute Kalman velocity/ATR, absolute baseline slope/ATR, and trend quality with weights 0.45, 0.20, and 0.35.
Calculation: Gate-ready = current bar is confirmed, gate is open, trend direction is non-neutral, ATR divided by its 50-bar average is between profile-adjusted volatility bounds, and trend direction has persisted for the profile-adjusted minimum bars.
Calculation: Reclaim long = price crosses above the state baseline after having crossed below it within the previous four bars, or crosses above the prior four-bar lowest low after having crossed below that level within the previous four bars; the close is in the upper 40% of the bar.
Calculation: Reclaim short = price crosses below the state baseline after having crossed above it within the previous four bars, or crosses below the prior four-bar highest high after having crossed above that level within the previous four bars; the close is in the lower 40% of the bar.
Calculation: Long setup = an enabled continuation long, reversal long, or reclaim long valid for the current market state. Continuation long requires positive trend direction on this and the previous bar, close above the prior four-bar high and state baseline. Reversal long requires positive trend direction, non-positive direction on the previous bar, and close above the state baseline and 9-bar ALMA. Reclaim long is valid only with positive trend direction. Under strict regime filtering, continuation requires trend state and reversal or reclaim requires chop state.
Calculation: Short setup = an enabled continuation short, reversal short, or reclaim short valid for the current market state. Continuation short requires negative trend direction on this and the previous bar, close below the prior four-bar low and state baseline. Reversal short requires negative trend direction, non-negative direction on the previous bar, and close below the state baseline and 9-bar ALMA. Reclaim short is valid only with negative trend direction. Under strict regime filtering, continuation requires trend state and reversal or reclaim requires chop state.
Calculation: Setup confidence = profile-adjusted blend of live confidence and, when eligible, memory confidence. Live confidence combines normalized trend strength, inverse normalized ATR stretch, normalized absolute 10-bar efficiency ratio, trend quality, and absolute 10-bar efficiency ratio. Memory confidence uses up to K nearest completed setups of the current trend direction, weighted by recency and inverse standardized distance across five features: normalized Kalman velocity, normalized ATR expansion, adjusted trend quality, normalized distance from state baseline, and normalized distance from 9-bar ALMA. Memory use requires the configured minimum stored samples and at least four matching neighbors; adaptive blending additionally requires memory confidence at least 0.50. Reject memory when volatility is suppressed, dispersion of neighbor outcomes relative to their mean exceeds its profile-adjusted limit, or neighbor directional agreement is below its profile-adjusted minimum. Setup confidence blends live and memory confidence according to memory fill until memory is ready, then equally; subtract the configured US stock market-open penalty when applicable.
Long: long setup AND (strict regime filtering is off OR market state is non-transition) AND gate-ready AND (no prior accepted setup or bars since it meet the profile-adjusted cooldown) AND absolute Kalman velocity exceeds 0.08 ATR AND absolute one-bar price change/ATR meets the profile-adjusted thrust minimum AND current bar range/10-bar average range meets the profile-adjusted minimum AND (volume is absent or current volume/20-bar average volume meets the profile-adjusted minimum) AND memory dispersion and agreement filters pass AND setup confidence meets its profile-adjusted minimum (lowered in relaxed hybrid mode) AND active pending long setups are below the configured per-direction limit AND all pending setups are below the configured total limit.
Short: short setup AND (strict regime filtering is off OR market state is non-transition) AND gate-ready AND (no prior accepted setup or bars since it meet the profile-adjusted cooldown) AND absolute Kalman velocity exceeds 0.08 ATR AND absolute one-bar price change/ATR meets the profile-adjusted thrust minimum AND current bar range/10-bar average range meets the profile-adjusted minimum AND (volume is absent or current volume/20-bar average volume meets the profile-adjusted minimum) AND memory dispersion and agreement filters pass AND setup confidence meets its profile-adjusted minimum (lowered in relaxed hybrid mode) AND active pending short setups are below the configured per-direction limit AND all pending setups are below the configured total limit.
