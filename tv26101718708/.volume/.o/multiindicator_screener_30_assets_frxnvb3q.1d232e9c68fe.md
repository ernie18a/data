Calculation: EMA slope angle = atan((current EMA − EMA from the slope lookback bars ago) / ((highest high − lowest low over the latest 200 bars) / 200)) × 180/π; the EMA and slope lookback are parameterized.
Calculation: Normalized bar delta = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low; smooth it with an EMA.
Long: Active mode is RSI and (RSI > its SMA OR RSI > 50); OR active mode is MACD and histogram ≥ 0; OR active mode is EMA slope and slope angle > 0; OR active mode is OBV Trend and the shorter-lookback OBV moving average > the longer-lookback OBV moving average; OR active mode is Supertrend and close > Supertrend line; OR active mode is VWAP and close > session VWAP; OR active mode is ADX/DMI and +DI > −DI; OR active mode is BB %B and %B ≥ 0.5; OR active mode is Stochastic RSI and (K > D OR K > 50); OR active mode is Vol Delta and smoothed normalized bar delta > 0.
Short: Active mode is RSI and RSI ≤ its SMA AND RSI ≤ 50 AND (RSI < its SMA OR RSI < 50); OR active mode is MACD and histogram < 0; OR active mode is EMA slope and slope angle < 0; OR active mode is OBV Trend and the shorter-lookback OBV moving average < the longer-lookback OBV moving average; OR active mode is Supertrend and close ≤ Supertrend line; OR active mode is VWAP and close < session VWAP; OR active mode is ADX/DMI and +DI ≤ −DI; OR active mode is BB %B and %B < 0.5; OR active mode is Stochastic RSI and K ≤ D AND K ≤ 50 AND (K < D OR K < 50); OR active mode is Vol Delta and smoothed normalized bar delta < 0.
