Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band) / Bollinger basis × 100, with bands defined as basis ± multiplier × standard deviation
Calculation: Distance from long-term SMA = absolute value of (close − long-term SMA) / long-term SMA × 100
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / average volume
Calculation: Tight range = highest high over a shorter lookback − lowest low over that lookback
Long: close > long-term SMA AND short-term SMA > long-term SMA AND distance from long-term SMA ≤ threshold AND Bollinger width < lowest Bollinger width over a shorter lookback AND relative volume > 1 AND RSI > threshold AND current RSI > each of its preceding values over a shorter lookback AND tight range < ATR × threshold
