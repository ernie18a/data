Calculation: Swing highs and lows = On each bar, an up-breakout occurs when high is at least the highest high over the zigzag lookback; a down-breakout occurs when low is at most the lowest low over that lookback. Zigzag trend starts upward and flips down on a down-breakout or up on an up-breakout. On an upward flip, record the lowest low since the preceding up-breakout as a swing low; on a downward flip, record the highest high since the preceding down-breakout as a swing high. Keep the latest and preceding recorded highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish market-structure break = While market state is bearish, latest swing high exceeds the preceding swing high and exceeds it by more than the absolute distance from the preceding swing high to latest swing low times the breakout-confirmation fraction, and neither latest swing level equals its value at the previous market-state change; then market state becomes bullish.
Calculation: Bearish market-structure break = While market state is bullish, latest swing low is below the preceding swing low and below it by more than the absolute distance from the latest swing high to preceding swing low times the breakout-confirmation fraction, and neither latest swing level equals its value at the previous market-state change; then market state becomes bearish.
Calculation: Current-day high and low = Highest high and lowest low from the start of the current day through the current bar.
Calculation: Upper and lower liquidity levels = The greater of latest swing high and current-day high; the lesser of latest swing low and current-day low.
Calculation: Unreset bullish sweep = A bar's low is below the lower liquidity level and its close is above that level; remains true until a bearish or bullish market-structure break, or a long signal.
Calculation: Unreset bearish sweep = A bar's high is above the upper liquidity level and its close is below that level; remains true until a bearish or bullish market-structure break, or a short signal.
Long: Bearish market state AND latest swing high > preceding swing high AND latest swing high > preceding swing high + abs(preceding swing high - latest swing low) * breakout-confirmation fraction AND latest swing high differs from its value at the previous market-state change AND latest swing low differs from its value at the previous market-state change OR unreset bullish sweep AND close > prior bar's open AND close > prior bar's high AND open < prior bar's close AND low <= lower liquidity level * 1.005
Short: Bullish market state AND latest swing low < preceding swing low AND latest swing low < preceding swing low - abs(latest swing high - preceding swing low) * breakout-confirmation fraction AND latest swing high differs from its value at the previous market-state change AND latest swing low differs from its value at the previous market-state change OR unreset bearish sweep AND close < prior bar's open AND close < prior bar's low AND open > prior bar's close AND high >= upper liquidity level * 0.995
