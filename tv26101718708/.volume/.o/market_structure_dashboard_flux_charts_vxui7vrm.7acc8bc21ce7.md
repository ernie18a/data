Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed levels and the prior level of the same type. Across configured timeframes, evaluate the timeframe bar available to the indicator, including a still-forming bar; also evaluate the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = on a close crossing above/below the latest confirmed swing high/low, use the lowest-low/highest-high candle, respectively, from after that pivot through the crossing bar (search at most 50 bars); its low-to-high range is the zone. Remove bullish zones when close is below their low and bearish zones when close is above their high. The nearest zone is the direction whose nearest boundary (bullish zone high or bearish zone low) has the smallest absolute distance from close; ties select bearish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = respectively, low/high of the bar two bars ago is above/below high/low three bars ago, the intervening bar is bullish/bearish (close > open or close > prior close; bearish is close < open or close < prior close), and the additional gap checks are low[1] < high[2] and low[2] < high[3] / high[1] > low[2] and high[2] > low[3]. The zone spans high[3] to low[1] for bullish gaps and high[1] to low[3] for bearish gaps. Remove bullish gaps when low falls below the zone low and bearish gaps when high rises above the zone high. The nearest gap direction uses the nearest boundary and smallest absolute distance as for order blocks; ties select bearish.
Calculation: Market structure = classify each confirmed swing high as higher high or lower high, and each confirmed swing low as higher low or lower low, relative to the previous swing of the same type. A close above the previous swing high overrides the high classification to higher high; a close below the previous swing low overrides the low classification to lower low. Structure is bullish when both latest classifications are higher, bearish when both are lower, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Swing position = (close − latest swing low) / (latest swing high − latest swing low). For weighted trend bias, assign bullish when below 0, or below 30%; bearish when above 100%, or above 70%; otherwise neutral. For the swing display, assign bullish above the swing high, bearish below the swing low, bullish in the lower half, and bearish in the upper half; a low sweep and reclaim is bullish and a high sweep and reclaim is bearish, taking precedence over the in-range half classification. A low sweep and reclaim means low < latest swing low and close > that low; a high sweep and reclaim means high > latest swing high and close < that high.
Calculation: Trend bias = sum, across enabled configured timeframes, of each enabled factor's direction score multiplied by that timeframe's weight, divided by the sum of enabled timeframe weights multiplied by the number of enabled factors. Factors are structure, nearest order block direction, nearest fair value gap direction, close above/below the timeframe EMA, and swing position; bullish = +1, bearish = −1, neutral = 0. Use the timeframe bar available to the indicator, including a still-forming bar. EMA uses the same configurable length on each timeframe.
Calculation: Previous-period range = high and low of the last completed day, week, or month, aggregated from the input bars. Its position is (close − period low) / (period high − period low). A low sweep and reclaim means low < period low and close > period low; a high sweep and reclaim means high > period high and close < period high.
Long: close > EMA on a configured timeframe OR close above its latest swing high OR its latest swing low was swept and reclaimed OR swing position is in the lower half and its latest swing high was not swept and reclaimed OR market structure is bullish OR nearest order block is bullish OR nearest fair value gap is bullish OR weighted trend bias is above 20% OR close is above a previous-period high OR a previous-period low was swept and reclaimed OR previous-period position is below 50% and its high was not swept and reclaimed.
Short: close <= EMA on a configured timeframe OR close below its latest swing low OR its latest swing high was swept and reclaimed OR swing position is in the upper half OR market structure is bearish OR nearest order block is bearish OR nearest fair value gap is bearish OR weighted trend bias is below −20% OR close is below a previous-period low OR a previous-period high was swept and reclaimed OR previous-period position is at least 50%.
