Calculation: Confirmed swing highs/lows = high/low pivots confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides, using the last two confirmed pivots of each type.
Calculation: Upper/lower boundaries = straight lines through the two most recent confirmed swing highs/lows, extrapolated to the current and previous bars.
Calculation: Upper/lower slopes = price change between the two corresponding pivots divided by the number of bars between them.
Calculation: Initial width = upper minus lower boundary at the earlier of the older high and low pivot bars; current width = upper minus lower boundary at the current bar; wedge height = initial width; contraction = (initial width − current width) / initial width; span = current bar minus that earlier pivot bar; apex = the bar where the boundaries intersect.
