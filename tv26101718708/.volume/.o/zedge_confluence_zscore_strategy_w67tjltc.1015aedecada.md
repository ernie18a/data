Calculation: Momentum Z-score = Z-score of source rate of change over the momentum lookback, standardized using its mean and standard deviation over the Z-score lookback
Calculation: RSI Z-score = Z-score of source RSI, standardized using its mean and standard deviation over the Z-score lookback
Calculation: Relative-volume Z-score = Z-score of volume divided by its moving average, standardized using its mean and standard deviation over the Z-score lookback
Calculation: Composite Z-score = weighted average of the momentum, RSI, and relative-volume Z-scores, with weights normalized by their sum (use 1 if their sum is 0)
Calculation: Smoothed composite = composite smoothed by a recursive EMA; when adaptive smoothing is enabled, its length decreases linearly from the maximum to minimum smoothing length as the ATR percentile rank over its lookback rises from 0 to 100; otherwise use the base smoothing length
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price's latest pivot low is below its previous pivot low and the smoothed composite's latest pivot low is above its previous pivot low
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price's latest pivot high is above its previous pivot high and the smoothed composite's latest pivot high is below its previous pivot high
Long: (entry mode = zero cross AND smoothed composite crosses above 0) OR (entry mode = threshold reversion AND smoothed composite crosses above the long entry threshold) OR bullish divergence
Short: (entry mode = zero cross AND smoothed composite crosses below 0) OR (entry mode = threshold reversion AND smoothed composite crosses below the short entry threshold) OR bearish divergence
Exit: long, smoothed composite crosses below the long exit level OR low is at or below the fixed stop set at the entry signal bar's close minus that bar's ATR times the stop multiplier
Exit: short, smoothed composite crosses above the short exit level OR high is at or above the fixed stop set at the entry signal bar's close plus that bar's ATR times the stop multiplier
