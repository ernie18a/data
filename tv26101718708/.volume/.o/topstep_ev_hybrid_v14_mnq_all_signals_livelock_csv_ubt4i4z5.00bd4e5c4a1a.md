Calculation: Body ratio = absolute(close - open) / (high - low)
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a parameterized lookback
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close over a parameterized lookback
Calculation: Bullish/bearish flow = candle closes above/below its open, relative volume exceeds its threshold, and close is above/below the chart-timeframe EMA; relative volume = volume / its moving average
Calculation: Bullish/bearish HTF score = weighted sum of bullish/bearish HTF trend, EMA slope, displacement, volume flow, fair value gap, and close-through-prior-bar evidence, plus entry-window evidence, minus midday, overextension, and bad-market-condition penalties; compare this score with the opposing score and parameterized score thresholds. HTF close uses the last completed HTF bar; HTF open, EMA, ATR, slope, volume flow, fair value gap, and close-through-prior-bar evidence use the HTF bar forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish micro score = count of bullish/bearish lower-timeframe fair value gap, close-through-prior-bar, and order-block conditions; use lower-timeframe bars aggregated from the input bars, including the bar forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = price moves beyond the latest confirmed pivot low/high and closes back above/below it; pivots use equal parameterized left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier / high < low two bars earlier
Calculation: Bullish/bearish close-through-prior-bar = close > prior high / close < prior low
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = prior candle is bearish/bullish and current close exceeds prior high / falls below prior low
Calculation: Displacement = candle direction agrees and absolute open-close distance exceeds ATR times a parameterized threshold
Calculation: Hard bad conditions = EMA slope is flat, or ATR is below its moving-average threshold, or the recent range is compressed below its ATR threshold, or body ratio is weak, or either wick exceeds its threshold on this or the prior bar
Calculation: HTF overextended = absolute distance between last completed HTF close and HTF EMA exceeds HTF ATR times a parameterized threshold
Calculation: Bullish/bearish HTF setup = matching HTF trend, score meets its required threshold, score is in the tradable golden range or qualifies as a clean high score, and—when counter-trend blocking is enabled—matching score dominates the opposing score by its threshold
Calculation: Bullish/bearish LTF trigger = matching EMA trend and slope with matching flow or strong pressure, OR matching sweep/fair value gap/close-through-prior-bar/order block with matching flow, OR matching micro confirmation (at least one micro condition in the golden score range, otherwise at least two)
Calculation: Entry quality = body ratio meets its threshold, volatility or recent range expansion meets its threshold, hard bad conditions are false, and HTF overextension is false
Calculation: Entry window = configured entry hours; opening window = configured first entry interval; primary and secondary windows = configured subdivisions of the entry window
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low minus ATR times stop multiplier; long target = signal close plus long risk times target multiple. Short default stop = signal-bar high plus ATR times stop multiplier; short default target = signal close minus short risk times target multiple.
Calculation: Short structure objective = nearest untapped downside level below signal close among the prior four lows, latest confirmed swing low, and prior completed daily low, subject to their enable filters. Prior-day liquidity uses the last completed daily bar. Short structure stop = nearest eligible meaningful high above close plus ATR times a buffer; short objective risk/reward = (close - objective) / (structure stop - close).
Calculation: Breakout exception = confirmed close in entry window beyond the prior parameterized lookback high/low, matching EMA side and slope, adequate body and range, matching HTF score, and at least one matching micro condition or displacement or strong pressure; HTF extension, low volatility, and compression filters must pass.
Calculation: Reversal short exception = confirmed entry-window bearish sweep, bearish displacement and close-through-prior-bar, adequate body and bear HTF score, at least one bearish micro condition, acceptable HTF extension, and no low volatility or compression
Calculation: Iceberg short = confirmed entry-window bearish early breakdown and rejection context, bearish confluence and impulse, adequate body, relative volume, and close location, an untapped open objective, acceptable EMA extension and risk, objective risk/reward meeting its threshold, and no low volatility or compression
Long: confirmed bar AND entry window AND ((bullish HTF setup AND bullish LTF trigger AND entry quality AND opening-window filter) OR bullish breakout exception) AND long weekday filters pass AND body ratio meets the long minimum AND no trade is active
Short: confirmed bar AND entry window AND ((bearish HTF setup AND bearish LTF trigger AND entry quality AND opening-window filter AND five-minute-chart short filter) OR bearish breakout exception OR reversal short exception OR iceberg short) AND short weekday filters pass AND body ratio is outside the blocked short-body interval AND no trade is active
Exit: Long, high >= target OR (low <= stop AND breakeven is not active) OR (low <= entry price AND breakeven is active) OR noon exit time OR backup trade-duration limit
Exit: Short, low <= target OR (high >= stop AND breakeven is not active) OR (high >= entry price AND breakeven is active) OR noon exit time OR backup trade-duration limit
Calculation: Breakeven becomes active when enabled and price reaches the entry-price level offset by initial risk times the breakeven trigger multiple; long triggers on high >= level and short triggers on low <= level
