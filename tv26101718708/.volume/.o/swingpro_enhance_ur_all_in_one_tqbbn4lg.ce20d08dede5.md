Calculation: Relative volume = current daily volume divided by the average volume of prior completed daily bars; if projected mode is used during the session, project end-of-day volume from elapsed and remaining session time before dividing
Calculation: Bullish candle patterns = bullish engulfing (current bullish body covers prior bearish body, is at least 10% larger, prior body is at least 35% of its range, and current body is at least 45% of its range); dragonfly doji (body at most 5% of range, lower wick at least 65%, upper wick at most 5%); morning star (two bars earlier bearish with body at least 35% of range, intervening body at most 25% of that body, current bullish body at least 45% of range and close at or above 55% of the earlier bearish body); piercing line (prior bearish body at least 35% of range, current opens below prior low, is bullish with body at least 45% of range, and closes above the midpoint but below prior open); hammer (current body at most 28% of range, lower wick at least twice its body, upper wick at most 8% of range, body in upper 45% of range, prior candle bearish); bullish harami (prior bearish body at least 35% of range, current bullish body inside prior body and at most 60% of its size); three white soldiers (three bullish candles, each body at least 55% of range and upper wick at most 20%, each later close higher, each later open inside the prior body)
