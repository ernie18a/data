Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2; Heikin-Ashi high/low = maximum/minimum of the bar high/low and Heikin-Ashi open/close
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi ATR = Wilder average of true range computed from Heikin-Ashi high, low, and previous Heikin-Ashi close
Calculation: Fast and slow SuperTrend directions = apply the script’s trailing-band SuperTrend to Heikin-Ashi bars using Heikin-Ashi ATR; the fast multiplier is smaller than the slow multiplier
Calculation: Strong Heikin-Ashi candle = absolute Heikin-Ashi close − open is at least a configurable fraction of Heikin-Ashi ATR and of its range; against-move wick is at most a configurable fraction of its range
Long: In either non-filter mode, fast or slow SuperTrend direction changes from short to long; OR in filter mode, SuperTrend direction is long AND high exceeds the previous configured lookback’s highest high AND the last filter signal was not long; OR SuperTrend direction is long AND Heikin-Ashi candle is bullish AND its same-colour run is within the configured early-run limit AND it is a strong candle with a short lower wick AND enough bars have passed since the previous confirmation AND there is no main signal on this or the previous bar
Short: In either non-filter mode, fast or slow SuperTrend direction changes from long to short; OR in filter mode, SuperTrend direction is short AND low falls below the previous configured lookback’s lowest low AND the last filter signal was not short; OR SuperTrend direction is short AND Heikin-Ashi candle is bearish AND its same-colour run is within the configured early-run limit AND it is a strong candle with a short upper wick AND enough bars have passed since the previous confirmation AND there is no main signal on this or the previous bar
Exit: long, high reaches any configured profit target above the entry price, each target offset by its configured multiple of Heikin-Ashi ATR at entry
Exit: short, low reaches any configured profit target below the entry price, each target offset by its configured multiple of Heikin-Ashi ATR at entry
