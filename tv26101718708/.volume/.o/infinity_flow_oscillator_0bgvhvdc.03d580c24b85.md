Calculation: Flow = EMA of the weighted average of normalized RMI, MFI, WaveTrend, and ATR expansion multiplied by directional bias; clamp the average to [-100, 100] before smoothing. RMI is based on HLC3 momentum over a lookback, with smoothed gains and losses mapped to 0–100 then centered and scaled to [-100, 100]. MFI is based on HLC3 and volume, centered and scaled to [-100, 100]. WaveTrend is the EMA of (HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, fast lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, fast lookback)), fast lookback)), using a slower EMA lookback, then divided by 1.5 and clamped to [-100, 100]. ATR expansion is (ATR over a shorter lookback / SMA of ATR over a longer lookback − 1) × 100, clamped to [-100, 100]. Directional bias is +1 when close is at or above its EMA, otherwise −1. Normalize the weighted average by the sum of its component weights; smooth the result with an EMA using the flow-smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Signal = EMA(Flow, signal-smoothing lookback).
Long: Flow crosses above Signal AND Flow > −strong-pressure threshold.
Short: Flow crosses below Signal AND Flow < strong-pressure threshold.
