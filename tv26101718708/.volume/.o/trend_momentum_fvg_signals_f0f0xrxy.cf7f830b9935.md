Calculation: Fast, slow, and macro EMA = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks; fast is shorter than slow, and macro is longer than slow.
Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > macro EMA
Calculation: Bear trend = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < macro EMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bull/bear = fast/slow EMA comparison on the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Bullish/bearish RSI divergence = the latest two confirmed pivot lows/highs show a lower price low/high respectively and a higher/lower RSI at those pivots, using the same left and right pivot lookbacks
Calculation: Extension z-score = (close − close SMA) / close standard deviation, using the same lookback for both; use 0 when standard deviation is 0
Calculation: Continuation score = 20 if ADX > 25, else 10 if ADX > 20, else 0; plus 15 if (bull trend AND close > fast EMA) OR (bear trend AND close < fast EMA); plus 15 if (bull trend AND MACD histogram > 0 AND greater than its prior-bar value) OR (bear trend AND MACD histogram < 0 AND less than its prior-bar value); plus 10 if (bull trend AND RSI > 50 AND < 70) OR (bear trend AND RSI < 50 AND > 30); plus 10 if volume / volume SMA > 1.2; plus 10 if (bull trend AND higher-timeframe bull) OR (bear trend AND higher-timeframe bear)
Calculation: Reversal score = 15 if ADX < 20; plus 10 if ADX > 25 AND below its value three bars ago; plus 10 if (bull trend AND close < fast EMA) OR (bear trend AND close > fast EMA); plus 10 if (bull trend AND MACD histogram is below each of its prior two values) OR (bear trend AND MACD histogram is above each of its prior two values); plus 15 if (bull trend AND RSI > 70) OR (bear trend AND RSI < 30); plus 10 if volume / volume SMA > 1.5 AND (bull trend AND close < open OR bear trend AND close > open); plus 15 if absolute extension z-score > 2, else 8 if > 1.5; plus 10 if (bull trend AND higher-timeframe bear) OR (bear trend AND higher-timeframe bull); plus 15 if (bull trend AND bearish RSI divergence) OR (bear trend AND bullish RSI divergence)
Calculation: Continuation percentage = 50 if continuation score + reversal score = 0, else continuation score / (continuation score + reversal score) × 100; reversal percentage = 100 − continuation percentage
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close > macro EMA AND continuation percentage ≥ confidence threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bull)
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < macro EMA AND continuation percentage ≥ confidence threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bear)
Exit: Long, bull trend AND reversal percentage ≥ confidence threshold AND (bearish RSI divergence OR RSI > 75 OR absolute extension z-score > 2)
Exit: Short, bear trend AND reversal percentage ≥ confidence threshold AND (bullish RSI divergence OR RSI < 25 OR absolute extension z-score > 2)
