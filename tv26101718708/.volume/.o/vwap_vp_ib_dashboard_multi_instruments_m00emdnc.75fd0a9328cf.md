Calculation: VWAP bands = volume-weighted mean and population standard deviation of each bar’s (open + high + low + close) / 4, accumulated from the Globex day or active Tokyo, London, or RTH session open; bands are the mean plus or minus the standard deviation
Calculation: Value area high/low = divide a fixed price range into rows, allocate each bar’s volume across rows in proportion to high-low overlap, start at the highest-volume row, then expand to the adjacent row with greater volume (ties upward) until the selected share of total volume is reached; Globex range resets at the Globex day boundary, session range resets at each Tokyo, London, or RTH open
Long: close >= Globex VWAP OR close > Globex upper band OR close > Globex value area high OR (active Tokyo, London, or RTH session AND close >= that session’s VWAP) OR (active Tokyo, London, or RTH session AND close > that session’s value area high) OR (initial balance complete AND close > initial balance high)
Short: close < Globex VWAP OR close < Globex lower band OR close < Globex value area low OR (active Tokyo, London, or RTH session AND close < that session’s VWAP) OR (active Tokyo, London, or RTH session AND close < that session’s value area low) OR (initial balance complete AND close < initial balance low)
