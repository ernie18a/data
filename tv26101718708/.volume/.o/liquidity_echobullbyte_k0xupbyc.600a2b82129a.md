Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: HTF compression zone = range [low, high] of a higher-timeframe bar whose range is below a threshold times the average range of that bar and the two preceding HTF bars; use the current HTF bar, which may still be forming. Register zones on confirmed chart bars, retain them for a configurable lifetime, and select the nearest eligible zone low below close for bullish sweeps or zone high above close for bearish sweeps. If HTF zones are disabled, use the latest confirmed pivot low/high, with seven bars left and two bars right.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / rolling average volume; choose the averaging lookback based on volume coefficient of variation, using a longer lookback when it is high and a shorter one otherwise.
Calculation: Pressure gradient = [current close location within its high-low range − close location within the high-low range two bars earlier] / 2, where close location = (2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick.
Long: Bullish sweep: eligible zone low exists AND volume has remained below its calibrated threshold for the required consecutive bars AND (low < zone low AND close >= zone low − ATR × maximum body distance AND lower wick >= ATR × calibrated minimum wick AND pressure gradient > calibrated threshold OR a low within the configured lookback was below zone low − 0.1 ATR AND close > zone low AND close < zone low + ATR × maximum grind distance AND close > open AND pressure gradient > calibrated threshold)
Short: Bearish sweep: eligible zone high exists AND volume has remained below its calibrated threshold for the required consecutive bars AND (high > zone high AND close <= zone high + ATR × maximum body distance AND upper wick >= ATR × calibrated minimum wick AND pressure gradient < −calibrated threshold OR a high within the configured lookback was above zone high + 0.1 ATR AND close < zone high AND close > zone high − ATR × maximum grind distance AND close < open AND pressure gradient < −calibrated threshold)
Long: Confirmed bullish sweep is still within its configurable confirmation window AND no position is active AND minimum signal spacing has elapsed AND current bar is bullish AND its body is at least the configured fraction of ATR AND volume ratio is at least the configured minimum AND close is above the sweep zone AND close is above EMA AND (two-bar confirmation is disabled OR previous bar is bullish and closed above the sweep zone)
Short: Confirmed bearish sweep is still within its configurable confirmation window AND no position is active AND minimum signal spacing has elapsed AND current bar is bearish AND its body is at least the configured fraction of ATR AND volume ratio is at least the configured minimum AND close is below the sweep zone AND close is below EMA AND (two-bar confirmation is disabled OR previous bar is bearish and closed below the sweep zone)
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR high >= entry price + ATR × second-target multiple OR first target has not been reached and elapsed bars since entry exceed the timeout
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR low <= entry price − ATR × second-target multiple OR first target has not been reached and elapsed bars since entry exceed the timeout
