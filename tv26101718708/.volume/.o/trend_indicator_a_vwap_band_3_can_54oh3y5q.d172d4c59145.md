Calculation: Trend oscillator = 100 × (MA of Heikin-Ashi close − MA of Heikin-Ashi open) / max(0.000001, MA of Heikin-Ashi high − MA of Heikin-Ashi low), using the same configured MA type and lookback for each; calculated on current-timeframe bars.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = cumulative volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of the configured price source since the configured reset (New York calendar day or Asia, London, or New York session start), using current bars including the bar still forming; upper/lower third bands are the mean ± the configured multiple of the standard deviation. Treat missing or zero volume as 1.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot low has lower price and higher RSI than the preceding confirmed pivot low; when configured, at least one pivot RSI must be oversold.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot high has higher price and lower RSI than the preceding confirmed pivot high; when configured, at least one pivot RSI must be overbought.
Calculation: Three-minute trend oscillator = Trend oscillator calculated from three-minute Heikin-Ashi bars, using the current three-minute bar, which may still be forming.
Long: Trend oscillator > 0 AND previous bar's Trend oscillator ≤ 0 AND a lower third VWAP band event occurred within the configured lookback, capped by bars since reset, after the configured minimum bars since reset AND (configured band mode is wick touch and low ≤ lower band OR close beyond and close ≤ lower band OR wick through and close back inside and low ≤ lower band and close > lower band) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI was at or below the configured oversold threshold within its configured lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bullish RSI divergence occurred within its configured lookback) AND (three-minute filter disabled OR three-minute trend oscillator > 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 14-bar ATR ≥ configured minimum) AND (closed-bar confirmation disabled OR current bar is confirmed).
Short: Trend oscillator < 0 AND previous bar's Trend oscillator ≥ 0 AND an upper third VWAP band event occurred within the configured lookback, capped by bars since reset, after the configured minimum bars since reset AND (configured band mode is wick touch and high ≥ upper band OR close beyond and close ≥ upper band OR wick through and close back inside and high ≥ upper band and close < upper band) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI was at or above the configured overbought threshold within its configured lookback) AND (divergence filter disabled OR bearish RSI divergence occurred within its configured lookback) AND (three-minute filter disabled OR three-minute trend oscillator < 0) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 14-bar ATR ≥ configured minimum) AND (closed-bar confirmation disabled OR current bar is confirmed).
