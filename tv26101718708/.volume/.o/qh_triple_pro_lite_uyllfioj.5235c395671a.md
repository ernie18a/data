Calculation: Chandelier direction = bullish when close exceeds the prior trailing short stop, bearish when close falls below the prior trailing long stop, otherwise retain the prior direction; trailing long stop is the greater of the current highest close over the lookback minus an ATR multiple and the prior long stop when prior close exceeded that stop, otherwise the current value; trailing short stop is the lesser of the current lowest close over the lookback plus an ATR multiple and the prior short stop when prior close was below that stop, otherwise the current value
Calculation: Long score = 25 if fast EMA > slow EMA, plus 25 if Chandelier direction is bullish, plus 20 if smoothed RSI > 50 and rising, plus 20 if higher-timeframe bias passes, plus volume points
Calculation: Short score = 25 if fast EMA < slow EMA, plus 25 if Chandelier direction is bearish, plus 20 if smoothed RSI < 50 and falling, plus 20 if higher-timeframe bias passes, plus volume points
Calculation: Volume points = 10 if volume / its moving average >= 1.0, else 7 if >= 0.75, else 4 if >= 0.50, else 0
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias passes long when the faster EMA exceeds the slower EMA on both daily and weekly bars; passes short when the faster EMA is below the slower EMA on both daily and weekly bars. Use the still-forming daily and weekly bars.
Calculation: Confidence = the score for the direction with the greater score, or 0 if scores tie
Long: Long score > short score AND confidence >= 90 OR long score > short score AND confidence >= 80 AND higher-timeframe bias passes long AND (low <= fast EMA OR close > prior bar high) AND cooldown has elapsed AND (one-signal-per-direction is disabled OR the prior signal was not long)
Short: Short score > long score AND confidence >= 90 OR short score > long score AND confidence >= 80 AND higher-timeframe bias passes short AND (high >= fast EMA OR close < prior bar low) AND cooldown has elapsed AND (one-signal-per-direction is disabled OR the prior signal was not short)
