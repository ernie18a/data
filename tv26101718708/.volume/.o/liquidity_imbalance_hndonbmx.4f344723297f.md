Calculation: Upper value boundary = session VWAP + standard deviation of close over a lookback
Long: (Low < lowest low of the prior lookback bars AND close > that lowest low) OR (close > open AND high - low > average high-low range over a lookback AND volume > average volume over a lookback)
Short: (High > highest high of the prior lookback bars AND close < that highest high) OR (close < open AND high - low > average high-low range over a lookback AND volume > average volume over a lookback) OR (during regular trading hours AND latest available higher-timeframe bar high > previous higher-timeframe bar high AND previous higher-timeframe bar high > the high two higher-timeframe bars ago AND latest available higher-timeframe bar close < previous higher-timeframe bar high AND previous bar high > previous bar's upper value boundary AND close < upper value boundary AND high < upper value boundary AND volume < average volume over a lookback). The latest available higher-timeframe bar may still be forming.
