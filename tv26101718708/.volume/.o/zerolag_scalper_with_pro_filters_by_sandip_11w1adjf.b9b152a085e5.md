Calculation: Zero-Lag EMA = EMA of close + (close − close lagged by floor((length − 1) / 2))
Long: close > Zero-Lag EMA AND previous close ≤ previous Zero-Lag EMA AND ADX > minimum ADX AND close > VWAP
Short: close < Zero-Lag EMA AND previous close ≥ previous Zero-Lag EMA AND ADX > minimum ADX AND close < VWAP
