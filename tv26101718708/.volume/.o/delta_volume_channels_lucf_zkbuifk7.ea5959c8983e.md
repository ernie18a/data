Calculation: Intrabar delta volume = sum intrabar volume when close > open or, on a doji, close > previous intrabar close; subtract volume when close < open or, on a doji, close < previous intrabar close; if unchanged, use the last known polarity.
Calculation: Delta-volume percentage = intrabar delta volume / total intrabar volume.
Calculation: Relative-volume weight = percentile rank of total intrabar volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Reference average = moving average of the selected price source; the same average type and lookback are used for both lines.
Calculation: Delta-weighted average = moving average of the selected source adjusted by the sign of delta-volume percentage and its absolute fraction multiplied by relative-volume weight; cap the adjustment at a configurable number of standard deviations of the source over the reference lookback.
Calculation: Bull delta-volume state = delta-weighted average > reference average; strong bull = bull state AND close > reference average AND both averages rising. Bear state = delta-weighted average <= reference average; strong bear = bear state AND close < reference average AND both averages falling.
Calculation: Divergence = delta volume != 0 AND the reference average and delta-weighted average have different slope signs.
Calculation: Divergence channel = retain the selected price levels (high/low or open/close) of divergence bars; expand levels when later divergences exceed them; after price breaches a boundary, start a new channel at the next divergence. Breach uses the selected price rule: low above top and high below bottom; high above top and low below bottom; or a selected price (close, open, hl2, hlc3, hlcc4, or ohlc4) above top or below bottom. Bull/bear state follows an upper/lower breach; strong bull/bear also requires the matching strong delta-volume state.
Long: Bull or strong bull delta-volume state OR bull or strong bull divergence-channel state OR both channels are strong bull.
Short: Bear or strong bear delta-volume state OR bear or strong bear divergence-channel state OR both channels are strong bear.
Long: On confirmed bar close, transition into a configured bull/strong-bull state of either channel, AND any enabled filters: up bar; close > previous close; CCI(close, reference lookback) > 0; volume > previous volume; divergence occurred within the configured recent-bar lookback; Efficient Work is bullish.
Short: On confirmed bar close, transition into a configured bear/strong-bear state of either channel, AND any enabled filters: down bar; close < previous close; CCI(close, reference lookback) < 0; volume > previous volume; divergence occurred within the configured recent-bar lookback; Efficient Work is bearish.
