Calculation: Range z-score = (current high-low range − rolling mean of high-low range) / rolling standard deviation of high-low range over the same lookback; use 0 when standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Range CV change = current rolling standard deviation of high-low range / rolling mean of high-low range − that ratio from one rhythm-window ago; use 0 for the ratio when its mean is 0.
Calculation: Rhythm score = round(min(100, min(max(range z-score − 1, 0) × 30, 40) + min(max(range CV change, 0) × 50, 25) + min(max((volume / rolling average volume − 1) × 40, 0), 20) + min(abs(EMA change over 3 bars) / (ATR + 0.00001) × 20, 15))).
Long: On a confirmed bar, range z-score ≥ rhythm-break threshold AND close > open AND close > previous close AND volume ≥ rolling average volume × volume multiplier AND close > bias EMA AND bias EMA change over 3 bars > 0 AND rhythm score ≥ score threshold AND at least cooldown bars since the previous signal
Short: On a confirmed bar, range z-score ≥ rhythm-break threshold AND close < open AND close < previous close AND volume ≥ rolling average volume × volume multiplier AND close < bias EMA AND bias EMA change over 3 bars < 0 AND rhythm score ≥ score threshold AND at least cooldown bars since the previous signal
