Calculation: Bollinger %B for each OHLC value = (value − lower Bollinger Band) / (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) × 100; bands use a simple moving average and standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Transformed OHLC = the selected moving average of each corresponding Bollinger %B OHLC series over one common lookback, or the unsmoothed %B values when smoothing is disabled.
Calculation: ATR = the selected moving average of true range over a lookback, multiplied by a factor; true range = max(transformed high, prior transformed close) − min(transformed low, prior transformed close).
Calculation: Direction = starts at +1 and retains its prior value unless prior direction is −1 and current transformed close (or transformed high when wick mode is enabled) > prior short stop, in which case it becomes +1; or prior direction is +1 and current transformed close (or transformed low when wick mode is enabled) < prior long stop, in which case it becomes −1. Long stop = transformed close − ATR, carried forward as the greater of its current value and prior long stop when prior transformed close (or prior transformed low in wick mode) > prior long stop. Short stop = transformed close + ATR, carried forward as the lesser of its current value and prior short stop when prior transformed close (or prior transformed high in wick mode) < prior short stop.
Calculation: New-high filter = rolling highest high over a shorter lookback equals rolling highest high over a longer lookback, or the filter is disabled.
Calculation: New-low filter = rolling lowest low over a shorter lookback equals rolling lowest low over a longer lookback, or the filter is disabled.
Calculation: Yearly-high filter = disabled, or the prior completed 12-month high is higher than the highs from 365 and 730 chart bars earlier, or current close exceeds the applicable yearly-high threshold: prior completed 12-month high if the 365-bar-ago value is unavailable; otherwise the greater of the prior completed 12-month high and the lesser of the 365- and 730-bar-ago values if the 730-bar-ago value is available; otherwise the greater of the prior completed 12-month high and the 365-bar-ago value. Missing yearly values satisfy the corresponding comparisons.
Calculation: Yearly-low filter = disabled, or the prior completed 12-month low is lower than the lows from 365 and 730 chart bars earlier, or current close is below the applicable yearly-low threshold: prior completed 12-month low if the 365-bar-ago value is unavailable; otherwise the lesser of the prior completed 12-month low and the greater of the 365- and 730-bar-ago values if the 730-bar-ago value is available; otherwise the lesser of the prior completed 12-month low and the 365-bar-ago value. Missing yearly values satisfy the corresponding comparisons.
Long: direction has been +1 for the entire signal-delay lookback AND yearly-high filter AND new-high filter.
Short: direction has been −1 for the entire signal-delay lookback AND yearly-low filter AND new-low filter.
Exit: long, direction has been −1 for the entire signal-delay lookback.
Exit: short, direction has been +1 for the entire signal-delay lookback.
