Calculation: Pivot supply zone = on confirmation of a pivot high (a high exceeding the configured left and right neighboring bars), zone top is the pivot high and bottom is pivot high minus ATR times zone depth; use ATR from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Pivot demand zone = on confirmation of a pivot low (a low below the configured left and right neighboring bars), zone bottom is the pivot low and top is pivot low plus ATR times zone depth; use ATR from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Active flip zone = the latest unexpired source zone after a close breaks its outer edge by the ATR-based break buffer; a supply break above creates a bullish flip, and a demand break below creates a bearish flip. Retain the source zone edges and break bar.
Calculation: Flip acceptance score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: break strength score (clamp directional close distance beyond flip edge divided by ATR and 0.70, then ×21); retest depth score (clamp directional penetration into zone divided by zone width and 0.75, then ×20); rejection wick score (directional wick divided by bar range and 0.55, then ×17); body score (absolute body divided by bar range and 0.62, then ×12); source-zone score (source zone width divided by ATR and 0.65, then clamp to 0–1 and ×13); freshness score (clamp 1 minus flip age divided by retest window to 0–1, then ×7); and relative-volume score (clamp current volume divided by its moving average and 1.6 to 0–1, then ×10; if disabled, use 7). Use current bar OHLCV and the active flip state.
Long: A new pivot demand zone is confirmed OR close > active source supply top + ATR-based break buffer OR bullish flip is active and the bar intersects its zone and a bar within the last 5 bars closed above its top + ATR-based acceptance buffer and the acceptance score meets the configured minimum (adjusted by confirmation mode).
Short: A new pivot supply zone is confirmed OR close < active source demand bottom − ATR-based break buffer OR bearish flip is active and the bar intersects its zone and a bar within the last 5 bars closed below its bottom − ATR-based acceptance buffer and the acceptance score meets the configured minimum (adjusted by confirmation mode).
Exit: Long, close < bullish flip zone bottom − ATR-based invalidation buffer.
Exit: Short, close > bearish flip zone top + ATR-based invalidation buffer.
