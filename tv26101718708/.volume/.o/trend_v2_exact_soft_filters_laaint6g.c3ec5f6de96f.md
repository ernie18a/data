Calculation: Purple = 100 × EMA(rolling Pearson correlation of close and bar index, lookback length; smoothing length)
Calculation: Yellow = EMA(Purple, signal length)
Calculation: True range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close))
Long: Set buy-ready when Yellow ≤ buy strength threshold AND Purple ≤ buy touch threshold AND last strong signal is not buy; keep it set until a strong signal. While buy-ready AND last strong signal is not buy, mark Weak Buy on the first bar buy-ready becomes true OR signal Strong Buy when Purple crosses above Yellow AND (turn filter disabled OR Purple > Purple[turn lookback]) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume[volume lookback]) AND (liquidity filter disabled OR selected liquidity proxy > its value[liquidity lookback]), where the proxy is volume × True range or volume × close. After Strong Buy, set last strong signal to buy and clear both ready states. Initial last strong signal is none.
Short: Set sell-ready when Yellow ≥ sell strength threshold AND Purple ≥ sell touch threshold AND last strong signal is not sell; keep it set until a strong signal. While sell-ready AND last strong signal is not sell, mark Weak Sell on the first bar sell-ready becomes true OR signal Strong Sell when Purple crosses below Yellow AND (turn filter disabled OR Purple < Purple[turn lookback]) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume < volume[volume lookback]) AND (liquidity filter disabled OR selected liquidity proxy < its value[liquidity lookback]), where the proxy is volume × True range or volume × close. After Strong Sell, set last strong signal to sell and clear both ready states. Initial last strong signal is none.
