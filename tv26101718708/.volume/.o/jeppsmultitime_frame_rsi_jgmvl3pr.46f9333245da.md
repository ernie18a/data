Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot low (5 bars left and right) is higher than the previous RSI pivot low, while the corresponding price low is lower than the previous price low; the pivot detections are 5–60 bars apart.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot high (5 bars left and right) is lower than the previous RSI pivot high, while the corresponding price high is higher than the previous price high; the pivot detections are 5–60 bars apart.
Long: On any configured timeframe, RSI crosses below its oversold threshold (at most once per timeframe bar) OR bullish RSI divergence occurs OR the number of configured timeframes with RSI at or below their oversold thresholds enters 2 or 3 from a count below that number. Use each timeframe’s current bar; historical higher-timeframe values update at that bar’s close.
Short: On any configured timeframe, RSI crosses above its overbought threshold (at most once per timeframe bar) OR bearish RSI divergence occurs OR the number of configured timeframes with RSI at or above their overbought thresholds enters 2 or 3 from a count below that number. Use each timeframe’s current bar; historical higher-timeframe values update at that bar’s close.
