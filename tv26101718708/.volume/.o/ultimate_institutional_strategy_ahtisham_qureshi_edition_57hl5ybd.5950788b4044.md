Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same lookback number of bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Break level = EMA value on the latest close crossover above EMA for bullish breaks, or close crossunder below EMA for bearish breaks.
Calculation: Price zone score = mean of the percentage differences between close and TEMA, LSMA, and EMA, each divided by that average.
Calculation: Bullish consensus = at least 3 of: RSI > 50; stochastic %K > 50 or %K > %D; stochastic RSI > 50; MACD line > signal line; Williams %R > -50; CCI > 0. Bearish consensus = at least 3 of the corresponding opposite comparisons.
Calculation: Trend exhausted = trend has lasted longer than twice the minimum trend duration, and at least one of: absolute mean slope of TEMA over 5 bars, LSMA over 10 bars, and EMA over 20 bars, divided by ATR, is below its threshold; trend alignment has failed; or RSI diverges from close over 10 bars. Trend duration counts consecutive bars with all three averages rising or all three falling; divergence means close makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower value in a rising trend, or close makes a lower value while RSI makes a higher value in a falling trend.
Long: Position is flat AND ((IDEAL conditions are enabled AND no IDEAL sell condition applies AND ((low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > prior bar high AND prior bar high − low > ATR times minimum sweep size AND close > EMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) OR (close retests the latest bullish break level within its tolerance AND close > open AND TEMA > LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier) OR (price zone is oversold AND close > EMA AND TEMA > LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier))) OR (no IDEAL action applies AND reversals are enabled AND close > KAMA AND KAMA is rising AND (ADX > its strong-trend threshold OR +DI > −DI) AND bullish consensus AND volume > volume SMA times reversal volume multiplier) OR (neither prior entry path applies AND ((trend alignment is required AND TEMA, LSMA, and EMA are all rising OR trend alignment is not required AND TEMA > LSMA AND close > EMA) AND TEMA crosses above LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier OR trend-exhaustion filtering is enabled AND trend is exhausted AND current trend is not bullish AND TEMA crosses above LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier)).
Short: (high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < prior bar low AND high − prior bar low > ATR times minimum sweep size) OR (Position is flat AND ((IDEAL conditions are enabled AND a sell condition applies: close retests the latest bearish break level within its tolerance AND close < open AND TEMA < LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier; OR high < latest confirmed pivot high AND high > prior bar high AND price zone is overbought AND TEMA < LSMA; OR EMA test count is at least its maximum AND close < EMA AND TEMA < LSMA AND close crosses below EMA) OR (no IDEAL action applies AND reversals are enabled AND close < KAMA AND KAMA is falling AND (ADX > its strong-trend threshold OR −DI > +DI) AND bearish consensus AND volume > volume SMA times reversal volume multiplier) OR (neither prior entry path applies AND ((trend alignment is required AND TEMA, LSMA, and EMA are all falling OR trend alignment is not required AND TEMA < LSMA AND close < EMA) AND TEMA crosses below LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier OR trend-exhaustion filtering is enabled AND trend is exhausted AND current trend is bullish AND TEMA crosses below LSMA AND volume > volume SMA times volume multiplier))).
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR times stop-loss multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR times take-profit multiplier OR the ATR-based trailing stop is hit OR bars held >= maximum bars in trade.
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR times stop-loss multiplier OR low <= entry price − ATR times take-profit multiplier OR the ATR-based trailing stop is hit OR bars held >= maximum bars in trade.
