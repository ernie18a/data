Calculation: Volatility regime = classify current ATR against its rolling lower and upper ATR percentiles, using hysteresis: enter Low below the lower threshold minus its buffer and leave Low above the lower threshold plus its buffer; enter High above the upper threshold plus its buffer and leave High below the upper threshold minus its buffer; otherwise retain the prior regime. Initialize as Normal; when ATR or percentile thresholds are unavailable, use Normal.
Calculation: SuperTrend line = ATR-based trailing band using the current timeframe bars: source is the average of high and low; band width is ATR multiplied by the base multiplier, scaled down in Low regime and up in High regime. Upper band = min(current source + width, prior upper band), except reset to current source + width when it is below the prior upper band or prior close is above the prior upper band. Lower band = max(current source - width, prior lower band), except reset to current source - width when it is above the prior lower band or prior close is below the prior lower band. Initialize direction bullish; after ATR is available, switch bearish when prior direction is bullish and close < prior lower band; switch bullish when prior direction is bearish and close > prior upper band. SuperTrend line is the lower band in bullish direction and upper band in bearish direction.
Long: prior direction bearish AND close > prior upper band
Short: prior direction bullish AND close < prior lower band
Exit: long, close < SuperTrend line
Exit: short, close > SuperTrend line
