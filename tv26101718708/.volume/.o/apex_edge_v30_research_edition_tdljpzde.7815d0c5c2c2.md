Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening-range session; reset each day
Calculation: Trend-up regime = ADX > trend threshold AND +DI > −DI AND higher-timeframe 20-period EMA > 50-period EMA
Calculation: Trend-down regime = ADX > trend threshold AND −DI > +DI AND higher-timeframe 20-period EMA < 50-period EMA
Calculation: Range regime = ADX < range threshold
Calculation: Near 20-period EMA long = (low <= EMA(20) OR abs(close − EMA(20)) < 0.5 × ATR) AND close > EMA(20)
Calculation: Near 20-period EMA short = (high >= EMA(20) OR abs(close − EMA(20)) < 0.5 × ATR) AND close < EMA(20)
Calculation: Strong body = abs(close − open) > 0.3 × ATR
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = (low wick > 1.5 × body AND upper wick < 0.5 × body AND close > (high + low)/2) OR (bullish engulfing candle with body larger than prior bar)
Calculation: Bearish reversal candle = (upper wick > 1.5 × body AND low wick < 0.5 × body AND close < (high + low)/2) OR (bearish engulfing candle with body larger than prior bar)
Calculation: In-play volume = current bar volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume >= relative-volume threshold
Calculation: Volume filter = current bar volume > 20-bar simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = 20-period EMA compared with 50-period EMA on the configured higher-timeframe bar, using its value as available on the current bar (the bar may still be forming)
Calculation: Opening-range breakout window = opening range is complete, current bar is after the range and within the configured bar limit, and no opening-range breakout signal has fired today
Calculation: Liquidity sweep high/low = current high > / low < the latest confirmed pivot high / low, respectively, and current close < / > that level; pivot confirmation uses the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier; initial short stop = entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier
Calculation: Long targets = entry price + initial risk × target reward/risk multiplier; short targets = entry price − initial risk × target reward/risk multiplier
Long: position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed since the last signal AND ((trend-up regime AND near 20-period EMA long AND strong body AND close > open AND relative volume >= threshold AND RSI(14) < 70 AND close < EMA(50) + 4 × ATR) OR (opening-range breakout window AND close > opening-range high AND close > open AND relative volume >= threshold AND higher-timeframe 20-period EMA > 50-period EMA) OR (close > SMA(200) AND RSI(2) < 5 AND close < EMA(20) AND bullish reversal candle AND volume > 20-bar average) OR (trend-up regime AND prior close <= prior VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND volume > 20-bar average AND RSI(14) < 70) OR (range regime AND close < VWAP − 1.5 × ATR AND bullish reversal candle AND volume > 20-bar average) OR (prior bar swept below latest confirmed pivot low AND current close > open AND close > that pivot low AND volume > 20-bar average); secondary branches apply only when no primary long signal fires on the bar
Short: position is flat AND cooldown has elapsed since the last signal AND ((trend-down regime AND near 20-period EMA short AND strong body AND close < open AND relative volume >= threshold AND RSI(14) > 30 AND close > EMA(50) − 4 × ATR) OR (opening-range breakout window AND close < opening-range low AND close < open AND relative volume >= threshold AND higher-timeframe 20-period EMA < 50-period EMA) OR (close < SMA(200) AND RSI(2) > 95 AND close > EMA(20) AND bearish reversal candle AND volume > 20-bar average) OR (trend-down regime AND prior close >= prior VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND volume > 20-bar average AND RSI(14) > 30) OR (range regime AND close > VWAP + 1.5 × ATR AND bearish reversal candle AND volume > 20-bar average) OR (prior bar swept above latest confirmed pivot high AND current close < open AND close < that pivot high AND volume > 20-bar average); secondary branches apply only when no primary short signal fires on the bar
Exit: long, high >= long target 2 OR low <= current stop OR bars held >= time-stop limit; after high >= long target 1, current stop becomes entry price
Exit: short, low <= short target 2 OR high >= current stop OR bars held >= time-stop limit; after low <= short target 1, current stop becomes entry price
