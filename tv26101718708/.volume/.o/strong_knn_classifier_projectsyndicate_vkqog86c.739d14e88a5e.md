Calculation: Order-flow feature vector = z-scores over a rolling window of (1) bar delta divided by volume + 1, (2) buy/sell imbalance, (3) high-volume absorption adjusted for candle-body progress, (4) cumulative-delta slope, and (5) close’s distance from VWAP in ATR units. Reconstruct delta and buy/sell volume from intrabar closes and volumes: rising intrabar close adds volume to buy and delta, falling close adds volume to sell and subtracts from delta, unchanged close splits volume evenly; if intrabar data is unavailable, use chart-bar close versus prior close. Use the current chart bar’s data and session VWAP.
Calculation: Training label = continuation when the price change over the outcome horizon has the same sign as the price change over the trend-reference lookback preceding that horizon; otherwise reversal. Admit a label only when the absolute outcome move exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold. Train only on resolved, confirmed chart bars; retain a rolling, capped pool.
Calculation: Reversal probability = logistic(sharpness × shrunken weighted-neighbour margin). Find the k nearest resolved feature vectors by summed Lorentzian distance (sum of log(1 + absolute feature differences)); weight each label by exp(−distance / temperature). The margin is (reversal weight − continuation weight) / total weight; shrink it toward zero by total weight / (total weight + prior strength). Confidence = 2 × absolute(reversal probability − 0.5). Classify as reversal if probability > 0.5, otherwise continuation; require confidence at least the configured threshold and a sufficiently large training pool.
Calculation: Structural zone = close within an ATR-scaled width of VWAP or the latest confirmed swing high or low. Use the current chart bar’s VWAP and ATR, and the last confirmed pivots.
Long: confirmed eligible signal inside the required structural zone and outside the signal cooldown, with (continuation class and close > close at the trend-reference lookback) OR (reversal class and close < close at the trend-reference lookback).
Short: confirmed eligible signal inside the required structural zone and outside the signal cooldown, with (continuation class and close < close at the trend-reference lookback) OR (reversal class and close > close at the trend-reference lookback).
