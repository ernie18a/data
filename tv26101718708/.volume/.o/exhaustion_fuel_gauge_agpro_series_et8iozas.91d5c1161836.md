Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback
Calculation: Safe ATR = greater of ATR and the minimum price increment
Calculation: Bar range = greater of high − low and the minimum price increment
Calculation: Direction = 1 if close − close from the fuel lookback bars ago > 0.22 × Safe ATR; −1 if it is < −0.22 × Safe ATR; otherwise 1 if the shorter-term EMA of close is above the longer-term EMA, −1 if below, or 0 if equal. The longer-term EMA uses twice the shorter-term EMA lookback.
Calculation: Fuel score = clamp(0–100, 0.32 × persistence + 0.24 × participation + 0.18 × range expansion + 0.26 × efficiency), where persistence is the percentage of the fuel-lookback bars whose close rose from the prior close when Direction > 0 or fell when Direction < 0; participation = clamp(0–100, current volume ÷ fuel-lookback SMA of volume × 50); range expansion = clamp(0–100, current bar range ÷ fuel-lookback SMA of bar range × 50); efficiency = clamp(0–100, absolute close change over the fuel lookback ÷ sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback × 100). Use the current chart bar and prior chart bars.
Calculation: Exhaustion risk = clamp(0–100, 0.35 × extension + 0.25 × fuel fade + 0.24 × wick pressure + 0.16 × participation fade), where extension = clamp(0–100, absolute distance from close to the longer-term EMA ÷ Safe ATR × 18); fuel fade = 100 − Fuel score; wick pressure = clamp(0–100, upper wick ÷ bar range × 100) when Direction > 0, lower wick ÷ bar range × 100 when Direction < 0, or 45 when Direction = 0; participation fade = clamp(0–100, prior participation − current participation + 22) if current participation is below prior participation, otherwise 0. The longer-term EMA uses twice the shorter-term EMA lookback. Use the current chart bar and prior chart bars.
Long: Direction > 0 AND Fuel score ≥ fuel-fade threshold AND Exhaustion risk < exhaustion threshold
Short: Direction < 0 AND Fuel score ≥ fuel-fade threshold AND Exhaustion risk < exhaustion threshold
