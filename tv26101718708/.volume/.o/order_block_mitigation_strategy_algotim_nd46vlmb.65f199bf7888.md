Calculation: ATR = Wilder-smoothed average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over equal configurable numbers of bars before and after the pivot; usable only after the bars on both sides have completed
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = on a confirmed close above the latest unbroken swing high by more than a configurable ATR multiple, find the nearest bearish candle between that pivot and a configurable bounded lookback toward the break; if its high-low range is at least a configurable ATR multiple measured at that candle, its low and high define the zone [bottom, top]
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = on a confirmed close below the latest unbroken swing low by more than a configurable ATR multiple, find the nearest bullish candle between that pivot and a configurable bounded lookback toward the break; if its high-low range is at least a configurable ATR multiple measured at that candle, its low and high define the zone [bottom, top]
Calculation: Zone mitigation percentage = for a bullish zone, max(0, (top − lowest low since zone creation) / (top − bottom) × 100); for a bearish zone, max(0, (highest high since zone creation − bottom) / (top − bottom) × 100); the extreme is ratcheted and never retreats
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Long rejection = selected pattern: (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.5; OR close > open AND close > prior close AND open ≤ prior close; OR (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.75; OR both the first and third conditions
Calculation: Short rejection = selected pattern: (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≥ 0.5; OR close < open AND close < prior close AND open ≥ prior close; OR (high − close) / (high − low) ≥ 0.75; OR both the first and third conditions
Long: On a confirmed bar, low is within a live bullish zone [bottom, top] AND its mitigation percentage is at most the configurable entry cap AND the zone has not previously signaled AND Long rejection AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close ≥ (top + bottom) / 2) AND (ATR filter disabled OR high − low ≥ configurable ATR multiple × ATR) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ configurable multiple × Volume average); signal only for the newest qualifying bullish zone on the bar
Short: On a confirmed bar, high is within a live bearish zone [bottom, top] AND its mitigation percentage is at most the configurable entry cap AND the zone has not previously signaled AND Short rejection AND (midpoint filter disabled OR close ≤ (top + bottom) / 2) AND (ATR filter disabled OR high − low ≥ configurable ATR multiple × ATR) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ configurable multiple × Volume average); signal only for the newest qualifying bearish zone on the bar
