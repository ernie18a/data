Calculation: Main regression channel = least-squares linear regression of close over the main lookback, with upper and lower boundaries at the regression endpoint plus or minus the residual standard deviation times a multiplier.
Calculation: Short regression channel = the same calculation over a shorter lookback than the main channel.
Calculation: Range boundaries = weighted close average over a lookback, plus or minus ATR times a base multiplier adjusted by recent-to-average volatility; recent volatility is the standard deviation of close changes, averaged over a longer lookback. The weighted average weights each close by its absolute change from the next older close divided by that older close.
Calculation: Stored support and resistance levels = confirmed pivot lows and highs, range boundaries, and main and short regression channel boundaries. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of right-side bars. Pivot strength is the count of closes within 0.5% of its price over a lookback, with a minimum strength of 1; range boundaries have strength 3, main channel boundaries strength 2, and short channel boundaries strength 1. Levels within 0.2% are merged, retaining the greater strength.
Long: A long signal is newly triggered when the close crosses above the main regression channel upper boundary, or crosses above the range top while a range is active, or crosses above any stored resistance level of strength at least 2; the shared signal cooldown has elapsed; and volume confirmation is disabled or volume is at least its moving average times the volume multiplier. Also mark a long pullback when an active long signal first has close above the main channel lower boundary and at or below its midpoint.
Short: A short signal is newly triggered when the close crosses below the main regression channel lower boundary, or crosses below the range bottom while a range is active, or crosses below any stored support level of strength at least 2; the shared signal cooldown has elapsed; and volume confirmation is disabled or volume is at least its moving average times the volume multiplier. Also mark a short pullback when an active short signal first has close below the main channel upper boundary and at or above its midpoint.
