Calculation: Normalized pressure = (selected price field − configurable moving average) ÷ highest absolute moving-average deviation over a lookback × 100
Calculation: Pressure signal = EMA of normalized pressure
Calculation: Active pressure state = classify each bar, in order: overheated (+3/−3) when pressure signal ≥ upper extreme/≤ lower extreme; expansion (+2/−2) when signal slope is positive/negative and signal is above upper warning/below lower warning; compression (0) when absolute signal slope < 2 and absolute signal < 0.65 × warning; otherwise +1 if signal > 0, −1 if signal < 0, or 0. Change active state to the candidate state on a confirmed bar after it has remained pending for the state-hold window; initialize a zero active state to the candidate on a confirmed bar.
Long: pressure signal crosses above 0 OR prior normalized pressure ≤ −extreme and current normalized pressure > −extreme OR pressure signal > warning and normalized pressure > pressure signal OR active pressure state increases from the previous bar OR active pressure state > 0 and pressure signal ≥ its SMA over a longer lookback OR active pressure state = −3 and pressure signal > −warning
Short: pressure signal crosses below 0 OR prior normalized pressure ≥ extreme and current normalized pressure < extreme OR pressure signal < −warning and normalized pressure < pressure signal OR active pressure state decreases from the previous bar OR active pressure state < 0 and pressure signal ≤ its SMA over a longer lookback OR active pressure state = 3 and pressure signal < warning
