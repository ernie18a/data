Calculation: Fourier-adjusted fast, mid, and slow averages = the selected moving average of close plus, respectively, the third, second, and first sinusoidal components from the Fourier transform of the last N closes; the fast average uses a shorter moving-average lookback than the mid, which is shorter than the slow. If enabled, apply the same linear-regression calculation to each average.
Long: Fourier-adjusted fast average > Fourier-adjusted mid average AND close > Fourier-adjusted slow average
Short: Fourier-adjusted fast average < Fourier-adjusted mid average AND close < Fourier-adjusted slow average
Exit: long, take-profit distance is enabled AND close >= entry price + take-profit distance OR stop-loss distance is enabled AND close <= entry price - stop-loss distance OR trailing stop is enabled AND price has reached entry price + activation distance AND low <= highest high since activation - trailing offset
Exit: short, take-profit distance is enabled AND close <= entry price - take-profit distance OR stop-loss distance is enabled AND close >= entry price + stop-loss distance OR trailing stop is enabled AND price has reached entry price - activation distance AND high >= lowest low since activation + trailing offset
