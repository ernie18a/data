Calculation: fast, medium, and slow EMA = EMAs of close with successively longer lookbacks
Calculation: range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, including the current bar; updated on confirmed bars
Calculation: range midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2
Calculation: near low/high = low <= range low + 2% of range width / high >= range high - 2% of range width
Calculation: current average volume = average volume of accumulated prior bars inside the range before a breakout; if none are accumulated, SMA(volume, range lookback)
Calculation: locked average volume = current average volume captured on the first raw breakout; retained until the range high or low changes
Calculation: pending long/short reversal = set when price is respectively in the discount/premium zone, near the corresponding range edge, closes respectively up/down, and volume is respectively >= or < current average volume; cleared after either confirmation
