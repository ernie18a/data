Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = the highest high/lowest low (or highest/lowest candle-body price, per setting) across N chart bars before and after; available after N right-side bars. Fast, Medium, and Slow tiers use separate, progressively longer N values.
Calculation: Active tier trendline = stateful line built from confirmed pivots; initialize from the prior opposite pivot on a higher-high/lower-low structure change, update its slope at later pivots using intervening closes, and retain it until broken.
Long: An enabled tier emits an upward trendline-break event (close > active line, or a confirmed pivot-bar close >= the line at that pivot) AND, if the HTF anchor gate is enabled, its last two pivot highs and last two pivot lows are both rising AND, if the LTF pivot-break gate is enabled, close > its latest pivot high AND, if the volume gate is enabled, volume >= its lookback percentile threshold AND, if rising volume is required, volume > prior-bar volume.
Short: An enabled tier emits a downward trendline-break event (close < active line, or a confirmed pivot-bar close <= the line at that pivot) AND, if the HTF anchor gate is enabled, its last two pivot highs and last two pivot lows are both falling AND, if the LTF pivot-break gate is enabled, close < its latest pivot low AND, if the volume gate is enabled, volume >= its lookback percentile threshold AND, if rising volume is required, volume > prior-bar volume.
