Calculation: Price and oscillator pivot ratios = each latest zigzag pivot value divided by the preceding pivot value of the same direction.
Calculation: Trend = sign of the external trend value at the latest pivot, or sign of the moving average change from the preceding comparison pivot to the latest pivot, or sign of latest pivot direction × (price ratio at the preceding pivot − 1), according to the selected trend method.
Long: At a latest low pivot, price and oscillator pivot directions differ AND ((trend equals pivot direction AND oscillator pivot ratio < price pivot ratio) OR (trend differs from pivot direction AND oscillator pivot ratio > price pivot ratio)) AND every intermediate price and oscillator value lies on or above its straight line between the compared pivots.
Short: At a latest high pivot, price and oscillator pivot directions differ AND ((trend equals pivot direction AND oscillator pivot ratio < price pivot ratio) OR (trend differs from pivot direction AND oscillator pivot ratio > price pivot ratio)) AND every intermediate price and oscillator value lies on or below its straight line between the compared pivots.
