Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the mean volume from the same relative position in prior units of the selected timeframe; current volume is either volume accumulated from the start of the current, possibly forming unit, or the current bar's volume, and prior units use completed bars. The timeframe unit is selected by auto-steps, a fixed higher timeframe, or a time-of-day anchor (auto-steps on non-intraday charts).
Calculation: Relative volume average = Hull moving average of relative volume.
Calculation: Directional relative volume average = Hull moving average of relative volume signed positive on up bars and negative on down bars; an up bar has rounded close > rounded open, or equal rounded close > prior rounded close, and a down bar has rounded close < rounded open, or equal rounded close < prior rounded close.
Calculation: Threshold high/low = selected fixed threshold; or relative-volume simple moving average plus/minus standard deviation times a factor (low is floored at zero); or highest/lowest relative volume since the current timeframe unit began, since the beginning of the relative-volume lookback units, or over a fixed lookback. Use the prior chart bar's threshold values in coloring comparisons.
Calculation: High volatility = short-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR.
Long: Up marker enabled AND at least one coloring condition enabled AND every enabled condition is bullish AND volatility filter passes AND previous bar was not in the bullish state; bullish conditions are: up bar; relative volume > 1; relative volume > relative volume average; relative volume > threshold high; directional relative volume average > 0; directional relative volume average > threshold high; relative volume average > 1; relative volume average > threshold high. Volatility filter passes when set to all, high volatility, or low volatility and high volatility is false, respectively.
Short: Down marker enabled AND at least one coloring condition enabled AND every enabled condition is bearish AND volatility filter passes AND previous bar was not in the bearish state; bearish conditions are: down bar; relative volume < 1; relative volume < relative volume average; relative volume < threshold low; directional relative volume average < 0; directional relative volume average < -threshold high; relative volume average < 1; relative volume average < threshold low. Volatility filter passes when set to all, high volatility, or low volatility and high volatility is false, respectively.
