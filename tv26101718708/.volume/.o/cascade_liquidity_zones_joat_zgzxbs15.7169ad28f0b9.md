Calculation: Demand zone = [confirmed pivot low, confirmed pivot low + ATR × zone width]; supply zone = [confirmed pivot high − ATR × zone width, confirmed pivot high]; use ATR on the pivot-confirmation bar
Calculation: Impulse score = 40 × (absolute candle body / candle range) + 35 × (min(volume / average volume, 4) / 4) + 25 × (min(candle range / ATR, 3) / 3), measured on the pivot bar
Calculation: Active zones = confirmed pivot zones separated by at least the minimum ATR distance; when zones cluster, keep the higher-score zone; retain no more than the configured maximum per side
Calculation: Wick rejection percentage = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100 for long signals; (high − close) / (high − low) × 100 for short signals
Calculation: Failure pivots = most recently confirmed pivot low and pivot high, using the failure-entry pivot lookback
Calculation: Long stop = zone bottom − ATR × stop buffer for zone sweeps, or failure pivot low − ATR × stop buffer for failure entries; short stop = zone top + ATR × stop buffer for zone sweeps, or failure pivot high + ATR × stop buffer for failure entries
Calculation: Long target = entry price + absolute(entry price − long stop) × reward/risk ratio; short target = entry price − absolute(short stop − entry price) × reward/risk ratio
Long: An active fresh demand zone exists AND low < zone bottom AND (zone bottom − low) / zone bottom × 100 ≥ minimum wick penetration AND close > zone bottom AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × minimum volume multiple) AND (wick rejection filter is disabled OR long wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum) AND current bar index ≥ last long signal bar index + cooldown bars AND bar is confirmed OR low < most recent confirmed failure pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND previous bar low ≥ that pivot low AND pivot low − low ≥ ATR × minimum failure wick size AND long wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum AND (failure volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × minimum volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ last long signal bar index + failure cooldown bars AND bar is confirmed
Short: An active fresh supply zone exists AND high > zone top AND (high − zone top) / zone top × 100 ≥ minimum wick penetration AND close < zone top AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × minimum volume multiple) AND (wick rejection filter is disabled OR short wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum) AND current bar index ≥ last short signal bar index + cooldown bars AND bar is confirmed OR high > most recent confirmed failure pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND previous bar high ≤ that pivot high AND high − pivot high ≥ ATR × minimum failure wick size AND short wick rejection percentage ≥ minimum AND (failure volume confirmation is disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × minimum volume multiple) AND current bar index ≥ last short signal bar index + failure cooldown bars AND bar is confirmed
Exit: Long, high ≥ long target OR low ≤ long stop
Exit: Short, low ≤ short target OR high ≥ short stop
