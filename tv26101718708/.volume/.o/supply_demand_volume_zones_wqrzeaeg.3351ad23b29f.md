Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configurable displacement lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = current close > open, current high − low ≥ ATR × the sensitivity threshold, and |close − open| ≥ ATR × its sensitivity threshold. Bearish displacement uses close < open.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume ≥ volume moving average × configurable multiplier, or volume confirmation is disabled.
Calculation: Zone quality score = min(100, min(35, break strength × 28) + min(25, body size / ATR × 24) + min(20, candle range / ATR × 12) + volume component + 5 if close breaks the prior range); volume component is min(15, volume / volume moving average × 9) when volume confirmation is enabled, otherwise 12. Break strength is the close's distance beyond the corresponding prior range boundary divided by ATR, floored at zero.
Calculation: Displacement demand zone = the most recent bearish candle within the configurable search lookback; bounds use the configured full-candle, body-only, or hybrid source and are expanded symmetrically to at least the configured minimum ATR height.
Calculation: Displacement supply zone = the most recent bullish candle within the configurable search lookback; bounds use the configured full-candle, body-only, or hybrid source and are expanded symmetrically to at least the configured minimum ATR height.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = pivot high or low with configurable left and right bars; it becomes available after the right bars complete.
Calculation: Pivot demand zone = range of the configurable base bars ending at the confirmed pivot low, using the configured wick, body, or hybrid bounds, adjusted to the configured minimum and maximum ATR heights.
Calculation: Pivot supply zone = range of the configurable base bars ending at the confirmed pivot high, using the configured wick, body, or hybrid bounds, adjusted to the configured minimum and maximum ATR heights.
Calculation: Pivot zone score = (base-bar average volume / volume moving average × volume weight + min(reaction move / ATR, 3) / 2 × reaction weight) / (volume weight + reaction weight), floored at 0.01. Reaction move is from the pivot low to the highest high through confirmation for demand, or from the pivot high to the lowest low through confirmation for supply.
Calculation: Demand zone qualifies when created by bullish displacement with volume confirmation (if enabled), close beyond prior range if required, quality score ≥ weak-zone threshold, and a prior bearish candle exists; or by a confirmed pivot low with pivot volume filter (if enabled) passed. Supply zone qualifies symmetrically using bearish displacement and a prior bullish candle, or a confirmed pivot high.
Calculation: Zone state = active until configured mitigation occurs; mitigated until price closes beyond the far zone edge by the ATR breaker tolerance; breaker until it closes beyond that edge by the breaker-confirmation ATR distance. A mitigated demand zone becomes held if, within the reaction window, high ≥ zone top + ATR × reaction threshold and close > zone top; a mitigated supply zone becomes rejected if low ≤ zone bottom − ATR × reaction threshold and close < zone bottom. Mitigation is the configured wick-touch, close-inside, or penetration-depth condition, after the configured delay.
Long: A qualifying demand zone is created OR close is within an active or mitigated demand zone OR a demand zone becomes held.
Short: A qualifying supply zone is created OR close is within an active or mitigated supply zone OR a supply zone becomes rejected.
