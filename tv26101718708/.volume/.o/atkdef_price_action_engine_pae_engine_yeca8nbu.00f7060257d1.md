Calculation: Candle body strength = (close - open) / (high - low)
Calculation: Upper/lower wick ratio = upper/lower wick length divided by candle range
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average
Calculation: CMF = volume-weighted sum of the close's position within each candle range, divided by summed volume over its lookback
Calculation: Turn strength = signed score from price-direction and CMF-direction reversals, gated by relative-volume expansion and minimum absolute candle body strength; use a higher score when both directions reverse than when only one reverses
Calculation: Net momentum = moving average of positive candle body strength weighted by clamped relative volume minus the moving average of negative candle body strength weighted by clamped relative volume
Calculation: Trend direction = sign of the shorter-term EMA minus the longer-term EMA
Calculation: Short price momentum = percentage change from the close a short lookback ago
Calculation: Medium price momentum = percentage change from the close a longer lookback ago
Calculation: Price acceleration = short price momentum minus the prior short-lookback price momentum
Calculation: Volume momentum = percentage change in the volume moving average over a lookback
Calculation: Behavior index = weighted sum of short and medium price momentum, price acceleration, volume momentum, and CMF
Calculation: Bullish consistency = short and medium price momentum, price acceleration, and CMF are all positive
Calculation: Bearish consistency = short and medium price momentum, price acceleration, and CMF are all negative
Long: turn strength > its positive signal threshold OR net momentum > its positive signal threshold OR trend direction > 0 OR CMF > its positive flow threshold OR (bullish consistency AND behavior index > its mild bullish threshold) OR (bullish consistency AND behavior index > its strong bullish threshold) OR (behavior index > its bullish divergence threshold AND NOT bearish consistency) OR (candle body strength > its minimum positive strength AND relative volume > its expansion threshold AND upper wick ratio < its maximum threshold AND (trend direction >= 0 OR close exceeds the prior lookback highest high)) OR (candle body strength > its minimum positive strength AND relative volume > its expansion threshold AND upper wick ratio > its minimum rejection threshold AND (trend direction <= 0 OR close does not exceed the prior lookback highest high)) OR (real bullish liquidity push AND CMF > its positive flow threshold) OR (fake bullish liquidity push) OR (CMF > 0 AND candle body strength > 0)
Short: turn strength < its negative signal threshold OR net momentum < its negative signal threshold OR trend direction < 0 OR CMF < its negative flow threshold OR (bearish consistency AND behavior index < its mild bearish threshold) OR (bearish consistency AND behavior index < its strong bearish threshold) OR (behavior index < its bearish divergence threshold AND NOT bullish consistency) OR (candle body strength < its minimum negative strength AND relative volume > its expansion threshold AND lower wick ratio < its maximum threshold AND (trend direction <= 0 OR close falls below the prior lookback lowest low)) OR (candle body strength < its minimum negative strength AND relative volume > its expansion threshold AND lower wick ratio > its minimum rejection threshold AND (trend direction >= 0 OR close does not fall below the prior lookback lowest low)) OR (real bearish liquidity push AND CMF < its negative flow threshold) OR (fake bearish liquidity push) OR (CMF < 0 AND candle body strength < 0)
