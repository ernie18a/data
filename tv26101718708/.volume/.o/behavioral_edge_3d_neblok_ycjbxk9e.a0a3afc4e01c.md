Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume over the relative-volume lookback; use 1 when volume is unavailable or the average is zero.
Calculation: Fair value = volume-weighted moving average of close over the anchor lookback when volume is available; otherwise exponential moving average of close over that same lookback.
Calculation: Extension = (close − fair value) / ATR.
Calculation: Herding score = EMA of bar direction × min(relative volume, 3), over the herding lookback; bar direction is 1 when close > open, −1 when close < open, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Range position = (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over the range lookback; use 0.5 if highest high equals lowest low.
Calculation: Crowd Emotion Index (CEI) = EMA of 50 × (1 + weighted mean of: clamp(Extension / extension cap, −1, 1), clamp((RSI − 50) / 25, −1, 1), clamp(Herding score, −1, 1), clamp(EMA of (close − previous close) / ATR over 3 bars, divided by 1.5, −1, 1), and 2 × Range position − 1); smooth over the CEI smoothing lookback. Use the respective component weights in the weighted mean.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close is in the top 40% of the bar range and (lower wick is at least 35% of range or close > open); bearish rejection = close is in the bottom 40% and (upper wick is at least 35% of range or close < open). Wicks are measured from the body to the bar low/high.
Calculation: Volume climax = relative volume ≥ climax multiplier; recent climax = climax on this bar or either of the prior 2 bars.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the lowest low of the last 3 bars is at or below the lowest low over the divergence lookback, and the lowest CEI of the last 3 bars is more than 3 points above the lowest CEI over that lookback. Bearish divergence = the highest high of the last 3 bars is at or above the highest high over the divergence lookback, and the highest CEI of the last 3 bars is more than 3 points below the highest CEI over that lookback.
Calculation: Swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over the swing lookback, excluding the current bar. Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed day, aggregated from intraday bars.
Calculation: Sellers trapped = current low is below swing low or (on intraday bars) prior-day low, and current close is back above that level, with lower wick ≥ ATR × trap-wick threshold. Buyers trapped = current high is above swing high or (on intraday bars) prior-day high, and current close is back below that level, with upper wick ≥ ATR × trap-wick threshold.
Calculation: Uptrend = fast EMA of close > slow EMA of close and close > fair value. Downtrend = fast EMA of close < slow EMA of close and close < fair value; the fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Reversal long quality = count of: CEI reached panic threshold within the emotion-gate lookback; recent volume climax; bullish rejection; sellers trapped on this or the prior bar; bullish divergence. Reversal short quality = count of: CEI reached euphoria threshold within the emotion-gate lookback; recent volume climax; bearish rejection; buyers trapped on this or the prior bar; bearish divergence.
Calculation: Trap long quality = count of: sweep below prior-day low; sweep below swing low; bullish rejection; current volume climax; CEI ≤ fear threshold. Trap short quality = count of: sweep above prior-day high; sweep above swing high; bearish rejection; current volume climax; CEI ≥ greed threshold.
Calculation: Herd long quality = count of: uptrend; relative volume ≥ 1; herding score ≥ 0.3; Extension < 2; Range position > 0.5. Herd short quality = count of: downtrend; relative volume ≥ 1; herding score ≤ −0.3; Extension > −2; Range position < 0.5.
Long: (CEI reached fear threshold within the emotion-gate lookback AND CEI > previous CEI AND reversal long quality ≥ minimum reversal quality) OR (sellers trapped AND bullish rejection AND trap long quality ≥ minimum trap quality) OR (uptrend AND CEI crosses above 50 AND herd long quality ≥ minimum herd quality).
Short: (CEI reached greed threshold within the emotion-gate lookback AND CEI < previous CEI AND reversal short quality ≥ minimum reversal quality) OR (buyers trapped AND bearish rejection AND trap short quality ≥ minimum trap quality) OR (downtrend AND CEI crosses below 50 AND herd short quality ≥ minimum herd quality).
