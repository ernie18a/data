Calculation: Zigzag swing highs and lows = track rolling highs and lows over the zigzag lookback; when the trend flips down, record the swing high, and when it flips up, record the swing low. Use the latest and previous recorded highs (H0, H1) and lows (L0, L1).
Calculation: Market structure = on a confirmed bar, switch from bearish to bullish when H0 > H1, H0 > H1 + abs(H1 − L0) × threshold, and neither latest pivot equals the corresponding pivot recorded at the previous market switch; switch from bullish to bearish when L0 < L1, L0 < L1 − abs(H0 − L1) × threshold, and neither latest pivot equals the corresponding pivot recorded at the previous market switch.
Calculation: Order-block zones = on a bullish structure switch, use the high-to-low range of the last bearish candle from the H1-to-L0 swing; on a bearish switch, use the range of the last bullish candle from the L1-to-H0 swing. A bullish zone is removed when close falls below its low; a bearish zone is removed when close rises above its high.
Calculation: Breaker-block zones = on a bullish structure switch, use the range of the last bullish candle from L1 minus the zigzag lookback through H1; on a bearish switch, use the range of the last bearish candle from H1 minus the zigzag lookback through L1. A bullish zone is removed when close rises above its high; a bearish zone is removed when close falls below its low.
Long: Market structure switches from bearish to bullish
Long: A bearish structure switch is followed within the failure lookback by a close above its broken swing-low level by at least the minimum reversal fraction of the prior swing range
Long: Close enters an active bullish order-block or breaker-block zone from outside, and that zone has never previously been entered by a closing price
Short: Market structure switches from bullish to bearish
Short: A bullish structure switch is followed within the failure lookback by a close below its broken swing-high level by at least the minimum reversal fraction of the prior swing range
Short: Close enters an active bearish order-block or breaker-block zone from outside, and that zone has never previously been entered by a closing price
