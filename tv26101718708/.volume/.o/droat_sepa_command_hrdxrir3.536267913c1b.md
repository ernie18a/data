Calculation: Stage 2 trend = close above the intermediate- and long-lookback SMAs; intermediate SMA above long SMA; long SMA above its value a configured lookback earlier; short SMA above intermediate and long SMAs; close above a configured multiple of the 52-week low and at least a configured fraction of the 52-week high; close above short SMA.
Calculation: VCP score = configured weighted score for base depth within configured bounds, short-range contraction versus medium range, medium-range contraction versus long range, short ATR below medium ATR by a configured factor, medium ATR below long ATR, volume dry-up, and right-side tightness (short range below medium range by a configured factor). Ranges are highest high minus lowest low over short, medium, and long lookbacks, divided by close; ATR uses corresponding short, medium, and long lookbacks. Volume dry-up means current volume / medium-lookback average volume is below a configured threshold AND short-lookback average volume / medium-lookback average volume is below a configured threshold. Base depth is (highest high − lowest low) / highest high over a configured base lookback.
Calculation: Pivot = highest high over a configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / medium-lookback average volume.
Calculation: Breakout setup = Stage 2 trend AND VCP score at or above its configured minimum AND close above Pivot by no more than a configured maximum percentage AND Relative volume at or above its configured minimum.
