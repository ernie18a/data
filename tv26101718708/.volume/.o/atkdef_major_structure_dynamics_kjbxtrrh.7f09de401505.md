Calculation: Pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmed after the bars to its right have closed
Calculation: Pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmed after the bars to its right have closed
Calculation: Duplicate-zone threshold = the configured ATR-, percentage-, or points-based threshold
Long: confirmed pivot low AND no prior accepted pivot low exists OR absolute difference from the prior accepted pivot low is at least the duplicate-zone threshold
Short: confirmed pivot high AND no prior accepted pivot high exists OR absolute difference from the prior accepted pivot high is at least the duplicate-zone threshold
