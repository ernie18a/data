Calculation: Execution bar = last completed bar of the execution timeframe
Calculation: MTF direction = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA, close > slow EMA, and slow EMA > its prior value; bearish when all inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral. Use last completed bars on each MTF.
Calculation: MTF bullish/bearish count = number of bullish/bearish directions among the four core MTFs; the separate scout direction uses the last completed bar of the scout timeframe.
Calculation: Bull/bear confidence = count of satisfied directional conditions divided by 36, as a percentage; rank = rounded count divided by 3.6. Bull conditions: MTF bullish agreement; bullish count > bearish count; bullish direction on each of the two shortest core MTFs; bullish direction on either of the other two core MTFs; close > fast EMA; fast EMA > slow EMA; close > slow EMA; slow EMA rising; middle EMA > long EMA; close > middle EMA; Hull MA rising; close > Hull MA; RSI above 50; RSI rising; MACD line > signal line; MACD histogram > 0; CCI > 0; stochastic %K > %D and %K > 50; MFI > 50; Williams %R > -50; ADX > its threshold and +DI > -DI; SAR < close; OBV rising; VWMA rising and close > VWMA; close > Bollinger basis and midpoint between basis and upper band; close > midpoint of the recent high-low channel; close > prior structure high; bullish candle body meets its ATR threshold; lower wick > upper wick; bullish fractal ribbon; bullish structure clearance. Bear conditions reverse these comparisons, using close < midpoint between basis and lower band, close < prior structure low, upper wick > lower wick, bearish ribbon, and bearish structure clearance.
Calculation: Fractal ribbon = EMA of execution-timeframe RSI with shorter lookback > longer lookback and > 50 for bullish, or shorter < longer and < 50 for bearish; also require close beyond the latest confirmed fractal high/low or beyond the slow EMA in the same direction. Use completed execution bars.
Calculation: Support/resistance zones = lowest low / highest high over a lookback on the zone timeframe, with zone thickness based on that timeframe's ATR; use its last completed bar. Support zone spans the low to low plus ATR thickness; resistance zone spans the high minus ATR thickness to the high.
Calculation: Zone confirmation = bullish close above support-zone top after a recent zone touch, or bullish post-breakout retest closing above that top; bearish is the inverse at resistance. Post-breakout retest must occur within its lookback and within an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Structure clearance = close above prior structure high, or prior high minus close at least an ATR-based clearance; bearish is the inverse using prior structure low.
Calculation: Depth = recent high-low range >= ATR-based minimum depth.
Calculation: Confirming candle = bullish close > open with body >= ATR-based minimum; bearish is the inverse.
Calculation: Pattern direction/score = assign direction and score for structure break; doji, hammer, or double bottom at recently touched support; doji, shooting star, or double top at recently touched resistance; bullish/bearish flag; engulfing or marubozu; trap reversal; or confirmed zone post-retest. Use the pattern's candle, zone-touch, breakout, and lookback comparisons; pattern score is higher for support/resistance context, retest, or aligned MTF/ribbon as defined by the pattern conditions.
Calculation: Pattern compatibility = no opposing pattern with score >= the compatibility threshold.
Calculation: Base signal = MTF agreement, confidence and rank above their minimums, sufficient depth, directional structure clearance, confirming candle, zone confirmation, and pattern compatibility; require the matching fractal ribbon when that gate is enabled.
Calculation: Early signal = base signal is false; at least two MTFs agree, the two shortest core MTFs agree in direction, opposing MTF count <= 1, confidence >= early threshold, rank meets its minimum, matching fractal ribbon, confirming candle, zone confirmation, and pattern compatibility.
Long: bullish base signal or bullish early signal, with bullish scout direction when scout confirmation is enabled; emit only on its false-to-true transition and subject to signal cooldown and one-signal-per-trend restrictions.
Short: bearish base signal or bearish early signal, with bearish scout direction when scout confirmation is enabled; emit only on its false-to-true transition and subject to signal cooldown and one-signal-per-trend restrictions.
Long: bullish base signal, confidence and rank above strong thresholds, bullish MTF count meeting the agreement minimum, bullish fractal ribbon, and bullish post-retest when required; emit only on its false-to-true transition and subject to signal repeat restrictions.
Short: bearish base signal, confidence and rank above strong thresholds, bearish MTF count meeting the agreement minimum, bearish fractal ribbon, and bearish post-retest when required; emit only on its false-to-true transition and subject to signal repeat restrictions.
Calculation: Strong-move candidate = directional confidence and rank above their thresholds, confidence greater than opposing confidence, candle body >= ATR-based threshold, candle closes in the directional portion of its range, a matching major pattern/structure break/zone event, and matching ribbon or MTF agreement.
Calculation: Projected bias = compare bullish and bearish projection scores. Scores add points for aligned sentiment, support bounce, zone events, patterns, MTF agreement, confidence lead, ribbon, and base/early/strong signals; support bounce also reduces bearish score. Classify by configurable positive/negative score-gap thresholds.
Long: new bullish strong-move candidate, bullish projected bias, bullish confidence > bearish confidence, and bullish scout direction when enabled; exclude repeated active candidates and apply dot cooldown.
Short: new bearish strong-move candidate, bearish projected bias, bearish confidence > bullish confidence, and bearish scout direction when enabled; exclude repeated active candidates and apply dot cooldown.
Long: bullish pattern label when active pattern direction is bullish and pattern score >= label threshold.
Short: bearish pattern label when active pattern direction is bearish and pattern score >= label threshold.
Calculation: Active sentiment score = add points for directional zone event, breakout/retest, qualifying pattern and reversal/continuation, confidence lead, MTF-count lead, ribbon, fractal ribbon, and base/early/strong signal. Qualify when matching scout gate passes, score reaches minimum, and exceeds opposing score by the required margin.
