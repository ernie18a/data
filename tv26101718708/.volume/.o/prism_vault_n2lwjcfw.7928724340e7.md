Calculation: Bullish/bearish vote count = sum of ten 0/1 votes: (1) momentum index > 0 and rising / < 0 and falling; (2) close inside an active demand / supply zone or above the latest active bullish / below the latest active bearish fair value gap; (3) wave momentum above all four bases or above their average plus two standard deviations / below all four bases or below their average minus two standard deviations; (4) wick sweeps below a prior swing low and closes back above it / above a prior swing high and closes back below it; (5) bullish trend or bullish countertrend setup / bearish trend or bearish countertrend setup; (6) regular bullish divergence within its active window / regular bearish divergence within its active window; (7) breakout above / below the Keltner basis on the bar after a squeeze; (8) positive / negative flow phase or matching volume-confirmed buying / selling pressure; (9) close in the lower 40% of the rolling high-low range and trend is non-bearish / upper 40% and trend is non-bullish; (10) volume surge on an up candle / down candle. Each vote contributes at most one.
Calculation: Momentum index = EMA of [(Laguerre RSI − 0.5)×60 + Fisher Transform×15 + TSI×0.6 + (MFI−50)×0.3 + (when volume exists) VFI×10 + normalized 3-bar OBV change×5 + normalized 3-bar A/D change×5 + CMF×30].
Calculation: Trend state = trend score using Hull MA direction over two bars, SuperTrend direction, alignment of fast/middle/slow EMAs, and close relative to the two longer SMAs; classify score ≥2 with ADX above its strength threshold as strong bullish, score ≥1 as bullish, score ≤−2 with ADX above threshold as strong bearish, score ≤−1 as bearish, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Countertrend long = bearish trend, momentum index rising for two consecutive bars, and close crosses above Hull MA. Countertrend short = bullish trend, momentum index falling for two consecutive bars, and close crosses below Hull MA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = at a confirmed price pivot low/high, price makes a lower low/higher high while at least two of RSI, MACD line, TSI, and Stochastic make higher lows/lower highs; compare with the prior same-side pivot within the configured minimum and maximum pivot spacing. Hidden bullish/bearish divergence uses higher low/lower high in price and lower high/higher low in at least two oscillators.
Calculation: Flow phase = smoothed flow index, where flow index combines CMF, MFI relative to 50, and whether OBV and A/D are above their 10-bar EMAs; phase is positive above 8 and negative below −8. A phase flip against a position is a change from nonnegative to negative or nonpositive to positive.
Long: Bullish vote count ≥ minimum confluence AND bullish vote count − bearish vote count ≥ minimum edge AND close > Hull MA AND (the raw condition was false on the prior bar) AND bars since the last long or short signal > minimum signal gap.
Long: Countertrend long.
Long: Regular or hidden bullish divergence.
Short: Bearish vote count ≥ minimum confluence AND bearish vote count − bullish vote count ≥ minimum edge AND close < Hull MA AND (the raw condition was false on the prior bar) AND bars since the last long or short signal > minimum signal gap.
Short: Countertrend short.
Short: Regular or hidden bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, a short signal occurs OR flow phase flips from nonnegative to negative.
Exit: Short, a long signal occurs OR flow phase flips from nonpositive to positive.
