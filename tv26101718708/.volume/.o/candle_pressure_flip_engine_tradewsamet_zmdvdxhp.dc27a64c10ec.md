Calculation: Move in ATR = absolute difference between current close and close N bars ago divided by current ATR; N is the exhaustion lookback.
Calculation: Directional bar counts = number of close-to-close rises and falls among the N bars ending at the current bar.
Calculation: Candle body %, lower-wick %, upper-wick %, and close position = respectively, absolute close-open, lower wick, upper wick, and close-low, each divided by high-low and expressed as a percentage; a zero-range candle has body and wick percentages of 0 and close position of 50%.
Calculation: Long score = clamp(move in ATR / required move in ATR × 25, 0, 25) + clamp(down-bar count / N × 10, 0, 10) + clamp(lower-wick % / required wick % × 20, 0, 20) + clamp(close position / close-pressure threshold × 15, 0, 15) + clamp(body % / minimum body % × 10, 0, 10) + 10 if volume passes its optional filter + 10 if RSI passes its optional filter.
Calculation: Short score = clamp(move in ATR / required move in ATR × 25, 0, 25) + clamp(up-bar count / N × 10, 0, 10) + clamp(upper-wick % / required wick % × 20, 0, 20) + clamp((100 − close position) / close-pressure threshold × 15, 0, 15) + clamp(body % / minimum body % × 10, 0, 10) + 10 if volume passes its optional filter + 10 if RSI passes its optional filter.
Calculation: Risk distance = selected risk mode: signal-wick distance from entry to signal-bar low for a long or high for a short, plus a tick buffer; ATR × multiplier; or the larger of those distances for hybrid mode.
Long: current bar is confirmed AND close < close N bars ago AND move in ATR ≥ required move AND down-bar count ≥ minimum AND lower-wick % ≥ required wick % AND close position ≥ close-pressure threshold AND close > open AND body % ≥ minimum body % AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ required multiple of its moving average) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > RSI from its turn lookback bars ago AND RSI ≤ its bullish cap) AND long score ≥ required score AND long score ≥ short score AND cooldown elapsed AND (one-active-trade restriction disabled OR no trade is active).
Short: current bar is confirmed AND close > close N bars ago AND move in ATR ≥ required move AND up-bar count ≥ minimum AND upper-wick % ≥ required wick % AND close position ≤ 100% − close-pressure threshold AND close < open AND body % ≥ minimum body % AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ required multiple of its moving average) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < RSI from its turn lookback bars ago AND RSI ≥ its bearish floor) AND short score ≥ required score AND short score > long score AND cooldown elapsed AND (one-active-trade restriction disabled OR no trade is active).
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry + risk distance × target risk/reward OR low ≤ entry − risk distance.
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry − risk distance × target risk/reward OR high ≥ entry + risk distance.
