Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; signed efficiency score = 50 + 50 × efficiency ratio × sign of net close change.
Calculation: Adaptive average = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close, using a shorter fast smoothing length and a longer slow smoothing length, adapted by the efficiency ratio.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = current ATR divided by its longer-lookback average; volatility score = a higher reading for expansion with price above a rising adaptive average, a lower reading for expansion with price below a falling adaptive average, an intermediate reading for expansion without that alignment, and separate readings for normal and contracted volatility.
Calculation: Composite regime score = weighted average of signed efficiency score, adaptive-average one-bar slope normalized by ATR, close’s distance from the adaptive average normalized by ATR, volatility score, current volume relative to its moving average, and higher-timeframe alignment score. The higher-timeframe score uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar: above its adaptive average with that average rising indicates upward alignment; below with it falling indicates downward alignment; otherwise neutral. The other components use the chart-timeframe bar, including the bar still forming.
Long: warmup complete AND ((prior state is Uptrend AND (held bars < minimum hold OR composite regime score >= Uptrend exit threshold)) OR (prior state is not Uptrend AND held bars >= minimum hold AND composite regime score >= Uptrend entry threshold))
Short: warmup complete AND ((prior state is Downtrend AND (held bars < minimum hold OR composite regime score <= Downtrend exit threshold)) OR (prior state is not Downtrend AND held bars >= minimum hold AND composite regime score <= Downtrend entry threshold))
