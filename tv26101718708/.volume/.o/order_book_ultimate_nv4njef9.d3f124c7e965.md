Calculation: ATR = Average true range of current-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Volume baselines = shorter- and longer-lookback simple averages of volume; use the shorter baseline for pressure and volume filters, and the longer baseline for synthetic depth and trend context.
Calculation: Synthetic bid/ask depths = For each ATR-spaced level below/above the current close, combine a Gaussian distance-decayed volume baseline with round-price proximity, directional pressure, OBV direction, volatility/liquidity regime, and volume-weighted rejection memory from recent bars touching that level. Sum levels by side; each side’s wall is its deepest level, and its wall ratio is that depth divided by average depth on that side. A wall is confirmed when its ratio exceeds the wall threshold and is at least 85% of the opposing wall ratio; it is stable after the same wall zone remains confirmed for the stability lookback.
Calculation: Pressure = Smoothed normalized sum of candle delta over a shorter lookback, optionally blended with normalized intrabar delta from lower-timeframe bars inside the current chart bar. Intrabar flow uses available bars, including the current forming bar. Buy/sell pressure estimates use each bar’s close position within its range.
Calculation: Historical bid/ask zones = Stable confirmed walls retained as price zones, merged by proximity and decayed over time; select the strongest bid zone at or below close and strongest ask zone at or above close.
Calculation: Bid absorption = Current low is within an ATR-scaled zone of the strongest bid wall or selected historical bid zone AND volume exceeds a multiple of the shorter-lookback volume average AND lower wick exceeds body length AND close is above the lower 45% of the bar range. Ask absorption is symmetric at the high/ask zone, with upper wick and close below the upper 45%.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = Current low moves at least an ATR-scaled distance below the strongest bid wall or selected historical bid zone AND close finishes above that level. Bearish sweep is symmetric above an ask wall/zone, with close below it.
Calculation: Pro score = A weighted composite of normalized pressure, bid/ask depth imbalance, bid/ask wall-ratio imbalance, recent-bar bullish-versus-bearish candle proportion, OBV direction, RSI, absorption, sweep, and directional trend regime; map the result to a 0–100 scale.
Calculation: Confidence = Agreement score across the directions of pressure, depth imbalance, wall imbalance, candle trend, OBV, intrabar flow quality, absorption, sweep, and market-regime quality.
Long: Bid absorption OR bullish sweep OR (bar is confirmed when confirmed-bar signals are enabled AND pro score >= long-bias threshold AND confidence >= minimum AND (noise filter disabled OR (volume >= minimum multiple of shorter-lookback volume average AND pressure > negative pressure limit AND (stable bid wall OR bid absorption OR bullish sweep) AND regime is not low liquidity))).
Short: Ask absorption OR bearish sweep OR (bar is confirmed when confirmed-bar signals are enabled AND pro score <= short-bias threshold AND confidence >= minimum AND (noise filter disabled OR (volume >= minimum multiple of shorter-lookback volume average AND pressure < positive pressure limit AND (stable ask wall OR ask absorption OR bearish sweep) AND regime is not low liquidity))).
Exit: Long, close < strongest bid wall price minus an ATR-scaled invalidation distance AND bid wall is confirmed OR high >= entry price + ATR-at-entry-scaled target distance OR low <= entry price - ATR-at-entry-scaled stop distance OR elapsed bars since entry >= maximum holding bars.
Exit: Short, close > strongest ask wall price plus an ATR-scaled invalidation distance AND ask wall is confirmed OR low <= entry price - ATR-at-entry-scaled target distance OR high >= entry price + ATR-at-entry-scaled stop distance OR elapsed bars since entry >= maximum holding bars.
