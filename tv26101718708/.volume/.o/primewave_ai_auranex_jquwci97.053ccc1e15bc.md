Calculation: Adaptive Donchian midpoint = average of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback adapted from its base period using ATR volatility: normalize current ATR within its rolling minimum–maximum range, then scale the base period inversely with that ratio and clamp it to the base period scaled by 1 ± adaptation strength. Conversion, base, and Span B use progressively longer base periods.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Tenkan = adaptive Donchian midpoint using the shortest base period; Kijun = adaptive Donchian midpoint using the intermediate base period; Span B = adaptive Donchian midpoint using the longest base period; Span A = average of Tenkan and Kijun.
Calculation: Current cloud = Span A and Span B from the displacement minus one bars ago; cloud top and bottom are their maximum and minimum.
Calculation: Chikou confirmation = current close compared with close from the displacement bars ago.
Calculation: Confluence score for each side (0–100) = 20 points for close beyond the cloud on that side, or 8 if close is inside; 15 for Tenkan aligned with Kijun on that side; 15 for Chikou confirmation on that side; 10 if the three-bar change in Span A exceeds the three-bar change in Span B for bullish, or does not for bearish; 8 if current cloud thickness is at least 30% of its 50-bar average, otherwise 2; 10 if volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the volume confirmation multiplier (or if volume is unavailable); 12 if smoothed momentum is beyond the side's stronger threshold, or 6 if it is merely positive for bullish or negative for bearish; and 10 if momentum is rising while positive for bullish, or falling while negative for bearish. Cap at 100. Smoothed momentum is a 5-bar EMA of the value clamped to −100…100 from 40 times Tenkan–Kijun spread divided by ATR, plus 100 times the change in that normalized spread over three bars, plus 30 times the difference between the five-bar changes in Span A and Span B divided by ATR.
Calculation: Stop and target levels = on entry, choose the stop from the selected mode: long cloud-bottom minus ATR buffer, Kijun minus ATR buffer, or entry price minus twice ATR; for short use cloud-top plus ATR buffer, Kijun plus ATR buffer, or entry price plus twice ATR. Risk distance is the greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and half ATR. Long targets are entry plus risk distance times each target risk multiple; short targets are entry minus those amounts. The final target is TP3.
Long: On a completed bar after warmup, Tenkan crosses above Kijun AND the cloud is not thin when the thickness filter is enabled AND volume confirmation passes when its filter is enabled AND bullish Chikou confirmation passes when its filter is enabled AND bullish confluence score is at least the minimum score.
Long: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses from at or below the previous bar's cloud top to above the current cloud top AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the breakout volume multiplier (or volume is unavailable).
Long: On a completed bar after warmup, 2–10 bars after the most recent upward cloud breakout, low is at or below the current cloud top AND close is above the current cloud top.
Short: On a completed bar after warmup, Tenkan crosses below Kijun AND the cloud is not thin when the thickness filter is enabled AND volume confirmation passes when its filter is enabled AND bearish Chikou confirmation passes when its filter is enabled AND bearish confluence score is at least the minimum score.
Short: On a completed bar after warmup, close crosses from at or above the previous bar's cloud bottom to below the current cloud bottom AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the breakout volume multiplier (or volume is unavailable).
Short: On a completed bar after warmup, 2–10 bars after the most recent downward cloud breakout, high is at or above the current cloud bottom AND close is below the current cloud bottom.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the stop level OR high is at or above TP3.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the stop level OR low is at or below TP3.
