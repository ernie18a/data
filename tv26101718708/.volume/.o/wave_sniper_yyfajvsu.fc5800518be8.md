Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = chart-timeframe highs/lows that are the highest/lowest over equal configurable left and right lookbacks; a pivot is available after the right lookback completes. Keep alternating pivot directions, replacing a same-direction pivot only with a more extreme one.
Calculation: A/B and C/A ratios = 100 × |B−A| / |A−origin| and 100 × |C−B| / |A−origin|, respectively.
Long: A confirmed four-pivot flat (origin, A, B, C) has alternating pivot directions, A and C in the same direction, and either regular ratios (B/A ≥ configured minimum minus tolerance and ≤ 100 + tolerance; C/A ≥ 100 − tolerance and ≤ 105 + tolerance), expanded ratios (B/A ≥ 100 − tolerance and ≤ configured maximum plus tolerance; C/A ≥ configured minimum minus tolerance and ≤ configured maximum plus tolerance), or running ratios (B/A > 100 and C/A < 100 − tolerance); and (the flat is running and A is a pivot high, or the flat is regular/expanded and A is a pivot low).
Short: A confirmed four-pivot flat (origin, A, B, C) has alternating pivot directions, A and C in the same direction, and either regular ratios (B/A ≥ configured minimum minus tolerance and ≤ 100 + tolerance; C/A ≥ 100 − tolerance and ≤ 105 + tolerance), expanded ratios (B/A ≥ 100 − tolerance and ≤ configured maximum plus tolerance; C/A ≥ configured minimum minus tolerance and ≤ configured maximum plus tolerance), or running ratios (B/A > 100 and C/A < 100 − tolerance); and (the flat is running and A is a pivot low, or the flat is regular/expanded and A is a pivot high).
Long: A confirmed three-wave pullback has four alternating pivots, A and C in the same direction, |C/A − 100| ≤ configured pullback tolerance, and either (origin is a pivot low and C > origin × (1 − tolerance/100)) or (origin is a pivot high and C > origin × (1 + tolerance/100)); C is a pivot low.
Short: A confirmed three-wave pullback has four alternating pivots, A and C in the same direction, |C/A − 100| ≤ configured pullback tolerance, and either (origin is a pivot high and C < origin × (1 + tolerance/100)) or (origin is a pivot low and C < origin × (1 − tolerance/100)); C is a pivot high.
