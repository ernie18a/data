Calculation: Consolidation zone = high and low of the first bar in a run of at least the configured minimum number of consecutive bars whose open and close remain within that first bar’s high-low range, allowing an ATR(14) × 0.01 margin; when the oversized-candle filter is enabled, reject the run if any bar’s high-low range exceeds the configured ATR multiple of ATR calculated before that bar.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of configured weights for: breakout-bar body > average absolute body × multiplier; ADX > threshold; volume > average volume × multiplier; zone width / ATR(14) < threshold; consolidation duration ≥ minimum duration + premium; and the directional conditions below. Use the same configured average-body, ADX, volume, and ATR calculations for both directions.
Calculation: Trend direction uses the close relative to an EMA; slope uses a separate EMA with a shorter lookback than the trend EMA.
Long: close > consolidation-zone high AND long confluence score ≥ minimum score; score includes close > trend EMA, slope EMA > its prior value, and RSI > 50.
Short: close < consolidation-zone low AND short confluence score ≥ minimum score; score includes close < trend EMA, slope EMA < its prior value, and RSI < 50.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × configured stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × configured target distance OR holding duration exceeds its limit.
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × configured stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × configured target distance OR holding duration exceeds its limit.
