Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the initial opening interval of each day, using chart bars; use after the interval is complete.
Long: After the opening range is set, close crosses above the opening range high OR (close crosses above the signal-timeframe VWAP and signal-timeframe RSI(14) > 50 OR RSI(14) crosses above 50 while close is above the signal-timeframe VWAP), AND current higher-timeframe RSI(14) > 50. Use the current, still-forming signal-timeframe and higher-timeframe bars.
Short: After the opening range is set, close crosses below the opening range low OR (close crosses below the signal-timeframe VWAP and signal-timeframe RSI(14) < 50), AND current higher-timeframe RSI(14) < 50. Use the current, still-forming signal-timeframe and higher-timeframe bars.
