Calculation: Volatility-adjusted EMA = recursively smoothed close with its effective length divided by the current ATR-to-50-bar-average-ATR ratio, clipped to a bounded range; calculate fast, mid, and slow lengths in that order.
Calculation: Trend = bullish when fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA and fast EMA’s ATR-normalized slope over a short lookback exceeds a positive threshold; bearish when the inverse ordering holds and the slope is below the corresponding negative threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Trend-adaptive RSI signals = RSI above a bullish threshold and rising in a bullish trend, or below oversold and rising otherwise; bearish is RSI below a bearish threshold and falling in a bearish trend, or above overbought and falling otherwise. Extreme long = RSI below oversold (use a lower threshold in a bearish trend); extreme short = RSI above overbought (use a higher threshold in a bullish trend).
Calculation: OBV flow = cumulative volume signed by close-to-close direction, smoothed with a shorter EMA and compared with a longer EMA; bullish/bearish flow requires the smoothed OBV rising/falling over a short lookback and above/below its longer EMA.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price (high + low + close)/3, reset at each day boundary and updated through the current bar. Reclaim up/down = close crosses above/below VWAP from at or below/above it.
Calculation: Pivot break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low; pivots use equal left and right lookbacks. Bullish/bearish liquidity grab = low breaks below the latest pivot low / high breaks above the latest pivot high by at least an ATR-scaled depth, then close returns above/below that pivot.
Calculation: Orderblock interaction = current bar range overlaps an active bullish/bearish zone created on a bullish/bearish pivot break from the nearest qualifying opposite-color candle in a bounded prior-bar search; retain only a bounded number of zones and expire them after a bounded number of bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = on each of up to four selected timeframes, score fast EMA versus mid EMA, close versus slow EMA, and RSI versus bullish/bearish thresholds; classify bullish/bearish when at least two components agree. Use lookahead-off values (the current higher-timeframe bar while forming in realtime; the latest completed bar historically).
Calculation: Long and short scores = weighted sums of trend (full weight for aligned trend, partial for fast/mid EMA alignment), RSI (weight extreme condition else continuation signal), volume/VWAP (weight aligned OBV flow with price above/below VWAP or reclaim, else partial weight for price above/below VWAP), structure (weights for zone interaction, pivot break, and liquidity grab), and higher-timeframe bias (weight per aligned timeframe). When volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier, add a bonus to the higher score, or split it equally on a tie. Apply the configured score threshold, minimum score gap, and same-side cooldown.
Long: (long score >= configured threshold AND long score > short score AND long score - short score >= configured minimum gap AND long-side cooldown elapsed AND confirmed-bar requirement satisfied when enabled AND active position is not long) OR bullish liquidity grab OR bullish pivot break with a qualifying opposite-color candle found in the bounded search.
Short: (short score >= configured threshold AND short score > long score AND short score - long score >= configured minimum gap AND short-side cooldown elapsed AND confirmed-bar requirement satisfied when enabled AND active position is not short) OR bearish liquidity grab OR bearish pivot break with a qualifying opposite-color candle found in the bounded search.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR-scaled stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance times first-target risk multiple OR bars since entry exceeds maximum holding period.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR-scaled stop distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance times first-target risk multiple OR bars since entry exceeds maximum holding period.
