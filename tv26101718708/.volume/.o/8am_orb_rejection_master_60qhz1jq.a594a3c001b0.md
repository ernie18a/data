Calculation: Prior-period levels = high and low of the last completed daily, weekly, and monthly bars
Calculation: Prior-session levels = high and low of the last completed Asia (8:00 PM–midnight), London (midnight–7:00 AM), and New York (9:30 AM–4:00 PM) sessions, New York time
Calculation: Key-level proximity = ATR-scaled distance; resistance levels are prior-period/session highs and support levels are their lows
Calculation: Bearish rejection score = weighted sum of: near resistance, upper wick larger than body and lower wick with bearish close, close below EMA, volume above its recent-average threshold, RSI above its bearish threshold, body at least its minimum range fraction, and proximity to prior-week or prior-month high
Calculation: Bullish rejection score = weighted sum of: near support, lower wick larger than body and upper wick with bullish close, close above EMA, volume above its recent-average threshold, RSI below its bullish threshold, body at least its minimum range fraction, and proximity to prior-week or prior-month low
