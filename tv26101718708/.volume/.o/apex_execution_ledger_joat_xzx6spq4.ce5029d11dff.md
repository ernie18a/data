Calculation: Long score = clamp to 0–100 of (20 if fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA, else 0) + (10 if close ≥ VWAP, else 0) + (8 if ATR / close × 100 ≥ configured minimum, else 0) + (8 if ADX > 14 and +DI > −DI, else 0) + (12 if RSI > 50 and RSI ≥ prior RSI, else 0) + (12 if volume expansion, else 0) + (8 if pressure delta > 10; else 4 if pressure delta > 0; else 0) + (7 if CVD > its EMA signal and that signal ≥ its prior value, else 0) + (5 if VWAP distance in ATRs is below the negative VWAP band and close > open, else 0) + (10 if lower wick > 1.4 × max(body, minimum tick), close > open, and close > low + 0.65 × bar range, else 0) + (12 if low < the lowest low of the preceding structure lookback and close > that low and open, else 0) + (8 if prior bar was compressed, close > the highest high of the preceding 8 bars, and volume expansion, else 0) + (8 if bullish regime continuation probability > 58%; else 5 if flat-regime breakout probability > 60% and current regime is bullish; else 0). Use the preset-adjusted fast/slow EMA and ATR lengths; fast EMA length is shorter than slow EMA length.
Calculation: Short score = clamp to 0–100 of (20 if fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA, else 0) + (10 if close ≤ VWAP, else 0) + (8 if ATR / close × 100 ≥ configured minimum, else 0) + (8 if ADX > 14 and −DI > +DI, else 0) + (12 if RSI < 50 and RSI ≤ prior RSI, else 0) + (12 if volume expansion, else 0) + (8 if pressure delta < −10; else 4 if pressure delta < 0; else 0) + (7 if CVD < its EMA signal and that signal ≤ its prior value, else 0) + (5 if VWAP distance in ATRs is above the VWAP band and close < open, else 0) + (10 if upper wick > 1.4 × max(body, minimum tick), close < open, and close < low + 0.35 × bar range, else 0) + (12 if high > the highest high of the preceding structure lookback and close is below that high and open, else 0) + (8 if prior bar was compressed, close < the lowest low of the preceding 8 bars, and volume expansion, else 0) + (8 if bearish regime continuation probability > 58%; else 5 if flat-regime breakout probability > 60% and current regime is bearish; else 0).
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > 1.25 × its moving average OR its volume Z-score > 1.
Calculation: Pressure delta = 100 × sum(buy volume − sell volume, pressure lookback) / sum(volume, pressure lookback); buy volume = volume × buy share, sell volume = volume × (1 − buy share). Buy share = clamp to [0,1] of (0.54 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) + 0.30 × body direction + 0.32 × body power × (body direction − 0.5) + 0.14), where body direction is 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, otherwise 0.5, and body power = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of buy volume − sell volume; CVD EMA signal = its EMA.
Calculation: VWAP distance in ATRs = (close − VWAP) / ATR.
Calculation: Compression = 24-bar high-low range / close × 100 < 8 × ATR / close × 100 AND abs(fast EMA − slow EMA) < 1.7 × ATR.
Calculation: Bullish regime = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close ≥ VWAP; bearish regime = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close ≤ VWAP; otherwise flat. Initialize each regime transition counter at 1 and update it on confirmed bars: for the previous regime, increment its hold counter when the current regime matches it, and its turn counter when the current regime is the opposite directional regime; for a prior flat regime, increment the corresponding bullish or bearish counter when that regime occurs. Bullish/bearish continuation probability = 100 × hold counter / (hold counter + turn counter). Flat-regime breakout probability = 100 × max(flat-to-bullish counter, flat-to-bearish counter) / their sum.
Long: (bar is confirmed when confirmed-bar signaling is enabled) AND more than the configured cooldown interval bars since the last long or short signal AND ATR / close × 100 ≥ configured minimum AND (trend agreement is disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA) AND (volume expansion is not required OR volume expansion) AND long score ≥ configured score threshold AND long score > short score + 5 OR long score > short score + 5 OR (bar is confirmed and (downside sweep OR bullish expansion break OR discount reclaim)) OR (bar is confirmed and discount reclaim) OR (bar is confirmed and pressure delta > 18).
Short: (bar is confirmed when confirmed-bar signaling is enabled) AND more than the configured cooldown interval bars since the last long or short signal AND ATR / close × 100 ≥ configured minimum AND (trend agreement is disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA) AND (volume expansion is not required OR volume expansion) AND short score ≥ configured score threshold AND short score > long score + 5 OR short score > long score + 5 OR (bar is confirmed and (upside sweep OR bearish expansion break OR premium rejection)) OR (bar is confirmed and premium rejection) OR (bar is confirmed and pressure delta < −18).
Calculation: Downside sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding structure lookback AND close > that low AND close > open; upside sweep = high > highest high of the preceding structure lookback AND close < that high AND close < open. Discount reclaim = VWAP distance in ATRs < −VWAP band AND close > open; premium rejection = VWAP distance in ATRs > VWAP band AND close < open. Bullish expansion break = prior bar was compressed AND close > highest high of the preceding 8 bars AND volume expansion; bearish expansion break = prior bar was compressed AND close < lowest low of the preceding 8 bars AND volume expansion.
