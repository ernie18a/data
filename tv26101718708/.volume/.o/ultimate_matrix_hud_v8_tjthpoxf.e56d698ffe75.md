Calculation: Bullish score = 40 if the higher-timeframe fast EMA is above the slow EMA and close is above the fast EMA, plus 30 if chart-timeframe RSI is above 50 and below 70, plus 30 if volume exceeds its chart-timeframe moving average times a multiplier and close is above open; otherwise each component contributes 0. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Bearish score = 40 if the higher-timeframe fast EMA is below the slow EMA and close is below the fast EMA, plus 30 if chart-timeframe RSI is below 50 and above 30, plus 30 if volume exceeds its chart-timeframe moving average times a multiplier and close is below open; otherwise each component contributes 0. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Relative strength = (sector close / benchmark close now ÷ sector close / benchmark close at the longer lookback − 1) × 100. Use rotation-timeframe closes sampled from completed bars; the strength lookback is longer than the momentum lookback.
Calculation: Relative momentum = (sector close / benchmark close now ÷ sector close / benchmark close at the shorter lookback − 1) × 100. Use rotation-timeframe closes sampled from completed bars.
Long: Bullish score ≥ 70 OR (bullish score < 70 AND bearish score < 70 AND bullish score ≥ 40 AND bullish score > bearish score) OR (relative strength ≥ 0 AND relative momentum ≥ 0)
Short: Bearish score ≥ 70 OR (bullish score < 70 AND bearish score < 70 AND bearish score ≥ 40 AND bearish score ≥ bullish score) OR (relative strength < 0 AND relative momentum < 0)
