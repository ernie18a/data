Calculation: Price average = linear regression of close over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Composite = sum of the first-degree contributions of Parabolic SAR, Supertrend, RSI, MFI, Balance of Power, Chande Momentum Oscillator, Center of Gravity, ADX, Stochastic, symmetrically weighted moving average, Williams %R, TSI, Intraday Intensity, Negative Volume Index, Positive Volume Index, On-Balance Volume, Accumulation/Distribution, Price-Volume Trend, true range, VWAP, Williams Accumulation/Distribution, SMA, EMA, CCI, Chop Zone, Ease of Movement, DPO, Advance/Decline Line, Bull-Bear Power, and smoothed high, low, open, and close. For each indicator except Parabolic SAR and Supertrend, its contribution is its lookback standard deviation × its correlation with the price average × the lookback standard deviation of (indicator − its 5-bar SMA). For Parabolic SAR and Supertrend, omit the final standard-deviation factor. Use the source and smoothing configured for each indicator; the indicator lookbacks share the same general lookback.
Calculation: Correlation composite = Composite × correlation(Composite, price average) over the general lookback.
Calculation: Volatility composite = correlation(Composite, price average) × standard deviation(Composite) over the general lookback.
Calculation: Correlated correlation = Correlation composite × correlation(Correlation composite, price average) over the general lookback.
Calculation: Correlated volatility = correlation(Correlation composite, price average) × standard deviation(Correlation composite) over the general lookback.
Calculation: Correlated volatility of volatility = correlation(Volatility composite, price average) × standard deviation(Volatility composite) over the general lookback.
Calculation: Correlated volatility change = correlated volatility of volatility − its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Price average is falling = current price average is lower than at least one of its values in the preceding configured falling lookback.
Calculation: Average price = EMA of hl2 over the general lookback.
Calculation: Longest-to-shortest entry-price range = lowest low over a longer lookback than the general indicator lookback.
Long: Price average is falling AND Correlated volatility < 0.
Long: Price average is falling AND Correlated volatility of volatility crosses 0 in either direction.
Long: Price average is falling AND the rate of change of the Composite's 2-bar change crosses 0 AND the rate of change of the Correlation composite's 2-bar change crosses 0 AND the rate of change of the Volatility composite's 2-bar change crosses 0.
Long: Price average is falling AND the Composite's 2-bar change crosses 0 AND the Correlation composite's 2-bar change crosses 0 AND the Volatility composite's 2-bar change crosses 0.
Long: Price average is falling AND Correlation composite crosses 0 AND Volatility composite crosses 0 AND the Composite's 2-bar change crosses 0.
Long: Price average is falling AND the Composite's 2-bar change crosses the Correlation composite's 2-bar change AND the Composite's 2-bar change crosses the Volatility composite's 2-bar change AND the Volatility composite's 2-bar change crosses the Correlation composite's 2-bar change.
Long: Flat position AND one of the first three long conditions AND current open < the tracked low point.
Long: Long position AND one of the first four long conditions AND Average price < average entry price × (1 − configured add-entry discount) AND open trade count ≤ 75% of maximum trade count.
Long: Long position AND the sixth long condition AND Average price < average entry price AND open trade count > 75% of maximum trade count AND open trade count ≤ maximum trade count.
Exit: Long, sell Supertrend direction changes from up to down OR Supertrend direction is down AND any of the six long conditions, AND accumulated profit target < Average price AND a prior tracked high point and tracked low point exist.
Exit: Long, intermediate selling is enabled AND open trade count is unchanged from the prior bar AND 9-bar SMA(close) > minimum tracked entry price × (1 + configured intermediate profit) AND average entry price > Average price AND a prior tracked high point and tracked low point exist.
