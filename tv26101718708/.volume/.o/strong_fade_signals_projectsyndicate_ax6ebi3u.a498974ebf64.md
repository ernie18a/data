Calculation: ATR band = selected moving average of close ± band-width × ATR
Calculation: Up-burst magnitude = max(0, (high − upper ATR band) / ATR); down-burst magnitude = max(0, (lower ATR band − low) / ATR)
Calculation: Stack height = min(maximum stack, 1 + integer part of burst magnitude / ATR per stack step)
Calculation: Peak-bar rejection = upper wick / candle range for an up burst; lower wick / candle range for a down burst
Calculation: Setup score = weighted mean, scaled to 0–10, of normalized burst magnitude, stack height, directional RSI exhaustion, peak-bar rejection, basis-slope fit, inverse 100-bar ATR percentile, and distance from entry to basis; clamp each component and the final score to [0,1] and [0,10], respectively
Long: A down-burst peak occurred on the previous bar (previous down-burst magnitude > 0, at least as large as the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than current down-burst magnitude) AND previous peak stack height ≥ minimum stack AND previous down-burst magnitude ≥ minimum magnitude AND (rejection filter disabled OR previous lower-wick share ≥ minimum rejection) AND (RSI filter disabled OR previous RSI ≤ oversold threshold) AND long side allowed AND setup-score tier ≥ minimum tier AND no position active AND signal spacing elapsed; OR a down-burst peak has maximum stack height
Short: An up-burst peak occurred on the previous bar (previous up-burst magnitude > 0, at least as large as the magnitude two bars ago, and greater than current up-burst magnitude) AND previous peak stack height ≥ minimum stack AND previous up-burst magnitude ≥ minimum magnitude AND (rejection filter disabled OR previous upper-wick share ≥ minimum rejection) AND (RSI filter disabled OR previous RSI ≥ overbought threshold) AND short side allowed AND setup-score tier ≥ minimum tier AND no position active AND signal spacing elapsed; OR an up-burst peak has maximum stack height
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ final target OR stop is hit after an earlier target was reached OR bars since entry reach maximum holding period
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ final target OR stop is hit after an earlier target was reached OR bars since entry reach maximum holding period
