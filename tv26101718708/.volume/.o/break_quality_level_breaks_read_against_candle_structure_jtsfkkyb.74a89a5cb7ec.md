Calculation: Watched high/low = highest high/lowest low over the same configured lookback of prior completed chart bars, excluding the current bar; or the most recently confirmed pivot high/low, activated after the configured right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Break tolerance = configured tick amount or configured multiple of current-bar ATR.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the watched high plus break tolerance (current close above it and prior close at or below it), the level is valid, ATR is available, the configured session restriction is satisfied, the upward-break cooldown has elapsed, no upward break is unresolved, and, in pivot mode, the pivot is unconsumed.
Long: When sweep marking is enabled, on a confirmed bar with a valid watched low and available ATR, within the configured session if restricted: low is below watched low minus break tolerance AND close is at or above watched low AND prior low is at or above watched low minus break tolerance.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the watched low minus break tolerance (current close below it and prior close at or above it), the level is valid, ATR is available, the configured session restriction is satisfied, the downward-break cooldown has elapsed, no downward break is unresolved, and, in pivot mode, the pivot is unconsumed.
Short: When sweep marking is enabled, on a confirmed bar with a valid watched high and available ATR, within the configured session if restricted: high is above watched high plus break tolerance AND close is at or below watched high AND prior high is at or below watched high plus break tolerance.
