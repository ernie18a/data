Calculation: Four moving averages = the selected moving-average type applied to the selected source, with successively longer lookbacks; evaluate on the chart timeframe and on each enabled dashboard timeframe, using each timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Joint slope = the weaker signed slope of the two fastest moving averages, each measured over the same lookback and divided by that timeframe’s ATR; evaluate on each enabled dashboard timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = candle quote turnover divided by average candle quote turnover over the preceding 24 hours, minus 1; evaluate on the momentum timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Stack Score = weighted average across enabled dashboard timeframes of each timeframe’s mean of the three adjacent moving-average comparisons (+1 for above, −1 for below, 0 for equal), scaled by the separation of the fastest and slowest averages in ATR units; longer timeframes have greater weight when weighting is enabled. Use last completed bars.
Long: The four moving averages are strictly ordered fastest to slowest on the chart timeframe OR on any enabled dashboard timeframe OR all enabled dashboard timeframes are bullish; OR any adjacent faster moving average is above the next slower one on a dashboard timeframe; OR the two fastest averages both slope upward on a dashboard timeframe; OR the majority of enabled adjacent timeframe pairs have a given moving average higher on the shorter timeframe than on the next longer timeframe; OR Stack Score > 0; OR a momentum-timeframe candle within the lookback has a percentage gain at least the configured threshold and passes the candle-turnover and relative-volume minimums; OR live Bitcoin change over the configured interval is at least the positive consolidation threshold.
Short: The four moving averages are strictly ordered fastest to slowest in reverse on the chart timeframe OR on any enabled dashboard timeframe OR all enabled dashboard timeframes are bearish; OR any adjacent faster moving average is below the next slower one on a dashboard timeframe; OR the two fastest averages both slope downward on a dashboard timeframe; OR the majority of enabled adjacent timeframe pairs have a given moving average lower on the shorter timeframe than on the next longer timeframe; OR Stack Score < 0; OR a momentum-timeframe candle within the lookback has a percentage loss at least the configured threshold and passes the candle-turnover and relative-volume minimums; OR live Bitcoin change over the configured interval is at most the negative consolidation threshold.
