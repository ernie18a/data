Calculation: RSI points = 3 if RSI ≤ oversold threshold minus deep-zone width; else 2 if ≤ oversold threshold; else 1 if ≤ oversold threshold plus buffer; else 0. Peak points mirror this using the overbought threshold and upper buffer.
Calculation: Stochastic points = 3/2/1 on an upward %K/%D crossover when %K is respectively in the deep/ordinary/near oversold zone; absent that crossover, score 1 when %K is at or below oversold, otherwise 0. Peak points mirror this for a downward crossover and overbought zones.
Calculation: Bollinger points = 3/2/1 when close is respectively beyond the lower band by a deep-band offset / at or below the lower band / at or below the lower band plus 30% of the distance to the middle band; otherwise 0. Peak points mirror this at the upper band.
Calculation: CCI points = 3/2/1 when CCI is at or below the negative deep/medium/light thresholds; otherwise 0. Peak points use the corresponding positive thresholds.
Calculation: Williams %R points = 3/2/1 when Williams %R is at or below the negative deep/medium/light oversold thresholds; otherwise 0. Peak points use thresholds at −(100 minus the deep/medium/light overbought levels).
Calculation: MFI points = 3/2/1 when MFI is at or below the deep/medium/light oversold thresholds; otherwise 0. Peak points use thresholds at 100 minus the corresponding overbought levels.
Calculation: ADX/DI points = 3 when the directional indices cross in the signal direction and ADX is at least the strong-trend threshold; else 2 when the signal-direction index is greater and ADX exceeds its value from a configured number of bars earlier; else 1 when the signal-direction index is greater; otherwise 0.
Calculation: RSI-price divergence points = 3 for a fresh divergence, 2 for a divergence within its active expiry, 1 for one within five bars after expiry, otherwise 0. Bullish divergence: a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low while RSI at the pivot is higher and below 50. Bearish divergence mirrors this at pivot highs, with RSI above 50. Pivot confirmation uses the configured lookback bars on both sides.
Calculation: Raw score = sum of the eight component point scores; active voters = number of components scoring at least 1. Effective score = raw score multiplied by a volume multiplier: baseline multiplier, increased at the configured volume-to-average thresholds; volume average is a simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Bollinger bands use a moving average and standard-deviation width; Williams %R = −100 × (highest high − close) / (highest high − lowest low) over its lookback; MFI uses typical price and volume. Each indicator uses its configured lookback.
Calculation: Trend EMA = exponential moving average of close; fast and slow EMAs use shorter and longer lookbacks respectively. ATR factor = 1 + (clamp(current ATR / 50-bar ATR average, 0.5, 2) − 1) × ATR weight; ATR uses its configured lookback. Effective trail activation and distance equal their base percentages times this factor. Trail score factor = clamp(1 − opposing raw score / 24 × tighten rate + own-side raw score / 24 × loosen rate, 0.15, 1.5); effective trail distance = ATR-adjusted base distance × trail score factor.
Long: (long score voters ≥ minimum voters AND long effective score ≥ minimum total score AND bar confirmed, with the optional trend filter inactive or close ≥ trend EMA) AND position is not long; OR after a long exit, cooldown elapsed, re-entry count below maximum, bars since exit within timeout, pullback from the highest high since exit ≥ configured percentage, close above both fast and slow EMAs, long voters ≥ re-entry minimum, and bar confirmed; OR pullback entry enabled, close ≥ slow EMA, close is within configured distance of slow EMA, a low or high in the recent touch window is within 1.5 times that distance of slow EMA, all bars in the bounce window close above open, close > trend EMA, +DI > −DI, ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold, long voters ≥ pullback minimum, and bar confirmed. Direction setting must allow longs.
Short: (short score voters ≥ minimum voters AND short effective score ≥ minimum total score AND bar confirmed, with the optional trend filter inactive or close ≤ trend EMA) AND position is not short; OR after a short exit, cooldown elapsed, re-entry count below maximum, bars since exit within timeout, rise from the lowest low since exit ≥ configured percentage, close below both fast and slow EMAs, short voters ≥ re-entry minimum, and bar confirmed; OR pullback entry enabled, close ≤ slow EMA, close is within configured distance of slow EMA, a low or high in the recent touch window is within 1.5 times that distance of slow EMA, all bars in the bounce window close below open, close < trend EMA, −DI > +DI, ADX ≥ strong-trend threshold, short voters ≥ pullback minimum, and bar confirmed. Direction setting must allow shorts.
Long: long voters ≥ minimum voters − 1 AND long effective score ≥ minimum total score − 3 AND bar confirmed AND the regular long signal is false (near-long warning marking).
Short: short voters ≥ minimum voters − 1 AND short effective score ≥ minimum total score − 3 AND bar confirmed AND the regular short signal is false (near-short warning marking).
Long: active bullish RSI-price divergence AND no long entry signal.
Short: active bearish RSI-price divergence AND no short entry signal.
Exit: long, opposite-side short entry signal OR low ≤ stop price OR (trailing enabled AND trail active AND low ≤ trail stop price). Stop price starts at entry price reduced by the configured stop percentage (or the tighter pullback-entry percentage); after close profit reaches breakeven phase 2 it is raised to entry price, and after phase 3 to entry price plus the configured locked-profit percentage. Trail activates when the highest high since entry reaches the ATR-adjusted activation profit percentage; trail stop is that high reduced by effective trail distance.
Exit: short, opposite-side long entry signal OR high ≥ stop price OR (trailing enabled AND trail active AND high ≥ trail stop price). Stop price starts at entry price increased by the configured stop percentage (or the tighter pullback-entry percentage); after close profit reaches breakeven phase 2 it is lowered to entry price, and after phase 3 to entry price minus the configured locked-profit percentage. Trail activates when the lowest low since entry reaches the ATR-adjusted activation profit percentage; trail stop is that low increased by effective trail distance.
