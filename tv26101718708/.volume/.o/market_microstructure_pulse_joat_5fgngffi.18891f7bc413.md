Calculation: LTF tick direction = compare each lower-timeframe close within the current chart bar with the preceding LTF close; the first close is seeded against itself. An uptick is buy-classified, a downtick sell-classified, and a flat tick is ignored. Use LTF bars within the current chart bar, including any still forming.
Calculation: Tick imbalance = (buy-classified ticks − sell-classified ticks) / (buy-classified ticks + sell-classified ticks); volume imbalance = (buy-classified volume − sell-classified volume) / (buy-classified volume + sell-classified volume).
Calculation: Pulse = EMA of the bar's weighted tick imbalance (0.55 × tick imbalance + 0.45 × volume imbalance), clamped to [−1, 1].
Calculation: Maximum up/down streak = the longest consecutive run of buy-classified/sell-classified LTF ticks within the current chart bar; flat ticks neither extend nor end a run. Ending dominance is the side with the longer ending streak, or neutral if tied.
Long: Pulse > 0 OR Pulse crosses above 0 OR Pulse crosses above the extreme threshold OR Pulse crosses above the extreme threshold AND ending dominance is up AND maximum up streak ≥ the streak minimum OR ending dominance changes from down to up AND maximum up streak ≥ the streak minimum AND at least 3 chart bars have elapsed since the prior marked flip OR buy-classified ticks / total directional ticks ≥ the imbalance threshold AND total directional ticks ≥ 10
Short: Pulse < 0 OR Pulse crosses below 0 OR Pulse crosses below the negative extreme threshold OR Pulse crosses below the negative extreme threshold AND ending dominance is down AND maximum down streak ≥ the streak minimum OR ending dominance changes from up to down AND maximum down streak ≥ the streak minimum AND at least 3 chart bars have elapsed since the prior marked flip OR sell-classified ticks / total directional ticks ≥ the imbalance threshold AND total directional ticks ≥ 10
