Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range period after the regular-hours open; use only after that period is complete
Calculation: Initial balance high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured initial-balance period after the regular-hours open; use only after that period is complete
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative typical price (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by volume since the configured anchor; use weight 1 when volume is unavailable
Calculation: Anchored VWAP standard deviation = square root of the nonnegative difference between the volume-weighted mean squared typical price and the squared anchored VWAP, since the configured anchor
Long: during regular hours AND (opening range is complete AND opening-range upside break has not occurred this session AND close > opening range high OR initial balance is complete AND initial-balance upside break has not occurred this session AND close > initial balance high OR low <= anchored VWAP - 2 × anchored VWAP standard deviation AND anchored VWAP standard deviation > 0)
Short: during regular hours AND (opening range is complete AND opening-range downside break has not occurred this session AND close < opening range low OR initial balance is complete AND initial-balance downside break has not occurred this session AND close < initial balance low OR high >= anchored VWAP + 2 × anchored VWAP standard deviation AND anchored VWAP standard deviation > 0)
