Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands (SMA basis ± multiplier × standard deviation) are inside Keltner Channels (EMA basis ± multiplier × ATR); release = squeeze was active on the previous bar and is inactive now
Calculation: Compression score = 30 if squeeze active, else 15 if it released on the previous bar, else 0; plus 20 if current high−low is the lowest in its lookback; plus 15 if narrow-range bars in the coil-duration window ≥ coil duration−1; plus 15 if volume < its moving average × dry-volume fraction; plus 10 if dry-volume bars in that window ≥ coil duration−1; plus 10 if ATR percentile rank over its lookback <25, else 5 if <40, else 0; capped at 100
Calculation: Fully coiled = compression score ≥75 and squeeze active
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish candle = close >/< open and absolute(close−open) ≥ body threshold × ATR
Calculation: ATR expanding = ATR > previous-bar ATR and ATR > its short-term SMA
Calculation: Bias vector = volume-delta contribution (+25 if positive and rising, −25 if negative and falling, else +10 if positive, −10 if negative, else 0) + close-position contribution (+20 if its EMA >0.55, −20 if <0.45, else 0) + structure contribution (+20 if a higher high or higher low, −20 if a lower low or lower high, else 0) + HTF contribution (+20 if HTF close > HTF EMA, −20 if below, 0 if HTF alignment is disabled) + price-vs-Bollinger-basis contribution (+15 above, −15 below, else 0), clipped to [−100,100]. Volume delta = EMA of estimated bull volume minus EMA of estimated bear volume; estimated bull volume = volume × (close−low)/max(high−low, minimum tick), and bear volume = volume−estimated bull volume. Close position = EMA of (close−low)/max(high−low, minimum tick). Structure compares current high/low with the preceding structure-lookback bars. HTF close and EMA use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long ignition score = 20 if fully coiled or compression score ≥55, else 10 if compression score ≥40, else 0; +20 if squeeze released, else +10 if squeeze was active on the previous bar, else 0; +25 if bias vector ≥50, else +15 if ≥35, else 0; +15 if HTF alignment is disabled or HTF close > HTF EMA; +15 for a strong bullish candle, else +8 if close > open, else 0; +5 if ATR expanding; +10 if volume delta is positive and rising, else +5 if positive, else 0; −15 if bias vector <0; clipped to [0,100]
Calculation: Short ignition score = long ignition score with bullish and bearish conditions inverted
Calculation: Regime is COILED after BUILDING when fully coiled; it remains COILED until ignition or compression score <30 while no longer coiling. IGNITION transitions to TRENDING on the next bar. TRENDING transitions to EXHAUSTION on an exhaustion condition; EXHAUSTION resets to BUILDING if compression score ≥35, otherwise QUIET. QUIET transitions to BUILDING at compression score ≥35; BUILDING returns to QUIET below 20.
Long: Previous regime is COILED AND ((strong bullish candle OR squeeze released and close > open) AND bias vector ≥35 AND (squeeze release required is disabled OR squeeze released now OR squeeze was active on the previous bar)) AND ATR expanding AND long ignition score ≥ threshold AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour is in an enabled session) AND (no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown)
Short: Previous regime is COILED AND ((strong bearish candle OR squeeze released and close < open) AND bias vector ≤−35 AND (squeeze release required is disabled OR squeeze released now OR squeeze was active on the previous bar)) AND ATR expanding AND short ignition score ≥ threshold AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour is in an enabled session) AND (no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown)
Exit: Long, TRENDING transitions to EXHAUSTION with trend direction bullish AND ((RSI ≥ overbought threshold AND volume > volume moving average × climax-volume multiple AND upper wick/bar range > rejection-wick fraction) OR (squeeze active AND volume is dry) OR (strong bearish candle AND close < EMA(close,21)))
Exit: Short, TRENDING transitions to EXHAUSTION with trend direction bearish AND ((RSI ≤ oversold threshold AND volume > volume moving average × climax-volume multiple AND lower wick/bar range > rejection-wick fraction) OR (squeeze active AND volume is dry) OR (strong bullish candle AND close > EMA(close,21)))
