Calculation: Sweep count = number of bars among the current and prior lookback−1 chart bars whose low is above the current close (bullish) or whose high is below the current close (bearish).
Long: On the chart bar immediately after a confirming bar, the latest bullish sweep candidate is active and unfired, its sweep count was at least floor(lookback × threshold), its confirming bar closed above the sweep bar’s high within the confirmation window, and the candidate was not canceled by a close below its sweep low or by a close above the latest bearish sweep bar’s high; the confirming bar’s volume is greater than its volume SMA × multiplier, it is within the configured UTC session, and the current higher-timeframe close is above its EMA. Use the previous completed chart bar for the confirmation, volume, and session checks; use the higher-timeframe bar containing the signal evaluation (which may still be forming).
Short: On the chart bar immediately after a confirming bar, the latest bearish sweep candidate is active and unfired, its sweep count was at least floor(lookback × threshold), its confirming bar closed below the sweep bar’s low within the confirmation window, the confirming bar’s volume is greater than its volume SMA × multiplier, it is within the configured UTC session, and the current higher-timeframe close is below its EMA. Use the previous completed chart bar for the confirmation, volume, and session checks; use the higher-timeframe bar containing the signal evaluation (which may still be forming).
