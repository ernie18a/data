Calculation: Squeeze active = Bollinger upper (SMA(close, BB length) + BB multiplier × standard deviation(close, BB length)) < Keltner upper (SMA(close, KC length) + KC multiplier × ATR(KC length)) AND Bollinger lower > Keltner lower; evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: Qualifying release = Squeeze active on the previous completed bar AND not active on the current completed bar AND the squeeze duration meets the minimum-duration parameter AND no earlier squeeze outcome is still pending.
Calculation: KNN upward vote percentage = among prior qualifying squeezes whose outcome window has completed, take up to K samples with smallest Euclidean distance across these five features: duration/40 capped at 1; maximum compression depth capped to [0,1], where compression depth = 1 − Bollinger width/Keltner width; average volume/(SMA(volume, 20)) during the squeeze divided by 3 and capped at 1; release momentum/ATR(14) clamped to [−1,1], where momentum = linreg(close, 20, 0) − linreg(close, 20, 1); and ATR(14)/SMA(ATR(14), baseline length) divided by 3 and capped at 1. A sample votes upward if its close at the end of the outcome window is above its close on the release bar; otherwise it votes downward. Percentage = upward votes / selected samples × 100. If fewer than the minimum training samples exist, use 50%.
Long: Qualifying release AND KNN upward vote percentage >= 50%
