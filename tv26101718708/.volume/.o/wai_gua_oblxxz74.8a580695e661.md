Calculation: Slow average = mean of two same-type moving averages of close with longer lookbacks than the fast pair.
Long: Position is flat AND this is the first bar where, on both this bar and the preceding bar, (fast average − slow average) > 0 and this spread is greater than its value on the prior bar AND the fast pair's shorter-lookback MA − longer-lookback MA > 0 and this spread is greater than its value on the prior bar AND the slow pair's shorter-lookback MA − longer-lookback MA > 0 and this spread is greater than its value on the prior bar.
