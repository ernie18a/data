Calculation: CVD = cumulative 1-minute volume signed positive when the 1-minute close is above its open, negative when below, and zero when equal; resets at the configured daily or weekly session boundary. Uses the latest available 1-minute bar, which may still be forming.
Long: On confirmation of a new CVD pivot low (local minimum using the same configurable lookback on both sides), the latest CVD pivot low is higher than the preceding CVD pivot low AND the close at the latest pivot bar is lower than the close at the preceding pivot bar.
Short: On confirmation of a new CVD pivot high (local maximum using the same configurable lookback on both sides), the latest CVD pivot high is lower than the preceding CVD pivot high AND the close at the latest pivot bar is higher than the close at the preceding pivot bar.
