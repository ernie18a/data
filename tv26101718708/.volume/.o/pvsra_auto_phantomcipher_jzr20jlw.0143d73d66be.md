Calculation: Average volume = average volume of the last 10 completed bars on the current timeframe
Calculation: Highest prior volume-weighted range = highest volume × (high − low) among the last 10 completed bars on the current timeframe
Long: Close > open AND (volume ≥ 1.5 × average volume OR volume × (high − low) ≥ highest prior volume-weighted range)
Short: Close ≤ open AND (volume ≥ 1.5 × average volume OR volume × (high − low) ≥ highest prior volume-weighted range)
