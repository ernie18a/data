Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume for the current London or NY session, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Rolling flow base = when the highest high minus lowest low over the lookback is less than the sum of lookback-period average bar ranges times the compression factor, and the sum of lookback-period average volume is greater than the lookback-period average volume times the lookback times the volume factor; retain the highest high and lowest low from the latest qualifying base.
Calculation: Rolling flow bull breakout = high and close exceed the retained base high, and current bar range exceeds average bar range over the lookback times the breakout factor.
Calculation: Rolling flow bear breakout = low and close are below the retained base low, and current bar range exceeds average bar range over the lookback times the breakout factor.
Calculation: Previous NY session = the last completed 09:30–16:00 America/New_York session; its range is high minus low, its body is the absolute difference between close and open, and its direction is up when close > open or down when close < open.
Calculation: Previous NY session base = previous session body / range < compression factor AND previous session volume > the 20-day average daily volume from the last completed daily bar × volume factor.
Calculation: Previous NY session bull flow = previous session direction is up AND previous session body / range > breakout factor / 2 AND previous NY session base is false.
Calculation: Previous NY session bear flow = previous session direction is down AND previous session body / range > breakout factor / 2 AND previous NY session base is false.
Calculation: Previous NY close position = (previous completed NY session close − low) / (high − low).
