Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Accumulation setup = on a confirmed bar, enter the climax scan when its low is at or below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars, its volume is at least the volume average times a threshold, and the close min(current bar index, 30) bars ago is at least its low times (1 + a minimum downtrend percentage); require at least 5 prior bars. In the scan, replace the climax low if a later low is below 99.5% of it and volume is at least 75% of the volume average times the threshold. After at least 2 bars from the latest climax low, track the highest high; establish the trading range when at least 5 bars have elapsed, that high is at least the climax low times (1 + a minimum rally percentage), and the current close is at least 1.5% below that high. Abandon the scan if more than 60 bars elapse without establishing the range. In the range, abandon if more than the lookback bars elapse, or the close falls below 94% of the climax low. A spring advances the setup to spring-confirmation; a strength breakout advances it to post-breakout. Abandon a spring-confirmation setup after more than the lookback bars from the climax. After a strength breakout, a qualifying last support advances to post-breakout support; a close above 112% of range resistance advances either post-breakout state to the active-uptrend state. In the active-uptrend state, track the highest high and abandon the setup when the close falls below that peak by the configured reset drawdown percentage. Abandonment clears all setup levels and returns to the climax scan.
Long: On a confirmed bar, in the trading-range state, low < climax low AND close >= 99.7% of climax low AND close <= 101% of range resistance AND (close - low) / max(high - low, 0.0001) > 0.45 (spring); OR in the trading-range or spring-confirmation state, close > range resistance AND volume > volume average times the volume-spike threshold times 0.85 AND close > open (strength breakout); OR in the post-breakout state, at least 2 bars have elapsed since the breakout AND low is between 95% and 102% of range resistance, inclusive AND volume < volume average AND low > climax low AND close >= open (last support). For the last-support marking, require at least 5 bars since the previous last-support marking, if any.
