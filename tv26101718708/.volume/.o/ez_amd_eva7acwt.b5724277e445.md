Calculation: ATR = average true range using Wilder smoothing
Calculation: Support zone = for each confirmed pivot low, bottom is the pivot bar low and top is the greater of the pivot bar’s lower open/close and bottom plus ATR-scaled padding; replace it on a new confirmed pivot low and invalidate it when close < bottom
Calculation: Resistance zone = for each confirmed pivot high, top is the pivot bar high and bottom is the lesser of the pivot bar’s higher open/close and top minus ATR-scaled padding; replace it on a new confirmed pivot high and invalidate it when close > top
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick ≥ body × rejection ratio AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ strong-close threshold
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND upper wick ≥ body × rejection ratio AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≤ 1 − strong-close threshold
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND bar range ≥ ATR × displacement threshold AND body / bar range ≥ displacement-body threshold
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND bar range ≥ ATR × displacement threshold AND body / bar range ≥ displacement-body threshold
Calculation: Bullish microstructure break = close > the highest high of the preceding microstructure lookback
Calculation: Bearish microstructure break = close < the lowest low of the preceding microstructure lookback
Calculation: Bull reaction = bullish rejection OR bullish displacement OR bullish microstructure break OR (volume ≥ volume moving average × volume threshold AND close > open AND close-location ≥ strong-close threshold, when relative-volume reactions are enabled)
Calculation: Bear reaction = bearish rejection OR bearish displacement OR bearish microstructure break OR (volume ≥ volume moving average × volume threshold AND close < open AND close-location ≤ 1 − strong-close threshold, when relative-volume reactions are enabled)
Long: On the execution chart, the current support zone is valid AND this is its first confirmed defense since creation or invalidation AND no resistance-zone defense occurs on this bar AND ((high ≥ support bottom AND low ≤ support top AND close > support top AND close[1] > support top AND close > open) OR (close > support top AND close[1] ≤ support top AND bull reaction) OR (high ≥ support bottom AND low ≤ support top AND close > support top AND bullish rejection) OR (high ≥ support bottom AND low ≤ support top AND close > support top AND (bullish displacement OR bullish microstructure break OR enabled bullish relative-volume reaction)) OR (low < support bottom AND close > support top AND bull reaction))
Short: On the execution chart, the current resistance zone is valid AND this is its first confirmed defense since creation or invalidation AND no support-zone defense occurs on this bar AND ((high ≥ resistance bottom AND low ≤ resistance top AND close < resistance bottom AND close[1] < resistance bottom AND close < open) OR (close < resistance bottom AND close[1] ≥ resistance bottom AND bear reaction) OR (high ≥ resistance bottom AND low ≤ resistance top AND close < resistance bottom AND bearish rejection) OR (high ≥ resistance bottom AND low ≤ resistance top AND close < resistance bottom AND (bearish displacement OR bearish microstructure break OR enabled bearish relative-volume reaction)) OR (high > resistance top AND close < resistance bottom AND bear reaction))
Exit: long, close < the support-zone bottom associated with the entry OR that support zone is replaced
Exit: short, close > the resistance-zone top associated with the entry OR that resistance zone is replaced
