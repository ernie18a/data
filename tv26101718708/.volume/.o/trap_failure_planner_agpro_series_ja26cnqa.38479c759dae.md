Calculation: Upper/lower rail = highest high/lowest low over the lookback bars before the current bar; midpoint = average of the rails.
Calculation: Failure score = weighted sum, clamped to 0–100: 18% probe quality + 22% close recovery + 18% follow-through + 14% relative-volume score + 18% acceptance + 10% persistence. Probe quality peaks at an ideal probe depth and declines for probes that are too shallow or too deep; close recovery measures close distance beyond the trap edge relative to zone height; follow-through measures that distance relative to ATR; relative volume compares current volume with its moving-average baseline; acceptance combines close location (45%), persistence (35%), and follow-through (20%); persistence measures consecutive qualifying closes against the required close count. Each component is normalized to 0–100 using its configured thresholds.
Long: On a confirmed bar with no active thesis, low < lower rail − minimum probe depth in ATR, close > lower rail, and close < midpoint; OR an active lower-trap thesis is within its failure window, close < its fixed edge − return buffer in ATR for the required consecutive closes, and failure score ≥ minimum score.
Short: On a confirmed bar with no active thesis, high > upper rail + minimum probe depth in ATR, close < upper rail, and close > midpoint; OR an active upper-trap thesis is within its failure window, close > its fixed edge + return buffer in ATR for the required consecutive closes, and failure score ≥ minimum score.
