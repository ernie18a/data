Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is a pivot using left and right lookbacks; use the latest confirmed pivot of each kind. Classify each new high as HH if higher than the preceding confirmed high, otherwise LH; classify each new low as HL if higher than the preceding confirmed low, otherwise LL.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average of volume. Volume expansion = relative volume >= its threshold; quiet volume = relative volume <= its threshold.
Calculation: Breakout score (long) = 25 + 24 if close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high from at/below it on the preceding bar + 18 if a retest holds above that high + 18 if volume expands + 13 if close > open, close is in the top 28% of the bar's range, and body/range >= 0.48 + 10 if the latest confirmed low is HL + 5 if the latest swing's accumulated volume exceeds the preceding recorded swing volume. Short score uses the mirrored conditions: cross below/retest below the latest confirmed swing low, close < open in the bottom 28% with body/range >= 0.48, latest confirmed high is LH; volume and swing-volume terms are the same.
Calculation: Sweep score (long) = 38 + 25 if low pierces the latest confirmed swing low and close reclaims above it with lower wick > 1.2 × body + 12 if volume expands + 10 if close is above 62% of the bar's range. Short sweep score mirrors this at the latest confirmed swing high: high pierces it, close rejects below it, upper wick > 1.2 × body, and close is below 38% of the range; add the same volume points.
Long: On a confirmed bar, after more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous accepted state (or if none), (price crosses above the latest confirmed swing high, or retests and holds above it) AND long breakout score >= minimum score, OR bullish sweep condition AND long sweep score >= minimum score.
Short: On a confirmed bar, after more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous accepted state (or if none), (price crosses below the latest confirmed swing low, or retests and holds below it) AND short breakout score >= minimum score, OR bearish sweep condition AND short sweep score >= minimum score.
