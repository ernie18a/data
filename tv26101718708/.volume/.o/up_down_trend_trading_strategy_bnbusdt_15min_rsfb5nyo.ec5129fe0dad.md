Calculation: Up/down trend line = Supertrend value smoothed with an SMA, separately for up-direction and down-direction bars; use the current bar.
Calculation: Long trend flip = the trend changes from down to up when close > the prior down line, or from up to down when close < the prior up line; otherwise retain the prior trend.
Calculation: Fast/medium/slow RSI = RSI of close/HLCC4/HLCC4 with progressively longer lookbacks; bucket each down to the greatest multiple of 10 below it.
Calculation: Fast/medium/slow RVI = 100 × EMA(positive close-to-close changes weighted by standard deviation) / the sum of that EMA and the corresponding EMA for negative changes; use HLC3/HLCC4/HLCC4 and progressively longer lookbacks, with the standard-deviation and EMA lookbacks matched at each length.
Calculation: Fast/medium/slow MFI = Money Flow Index of open/high/low with progressively longer lookbacks; bucket each down to the greatest multiple of 10 below it.
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power = SMA of 50 × (close − open) / (high − low) + 50.
Calculation: Market-bottom compound = slow RSI at or below its lower threshold AND medium RSI < slow RSI AND fast RSI ≤ medium RSI AND slow RVI at or below its lower threshold AND medium RVI ≤ slow RVI AND fast RVI ≤ medium RVI AND slow MFI at or below its lower threshold AND medium MFI ≤ slow MFI AND fast MFI ≤ medium MFI AND smoothed balance of power at or below its lower threshold.
Calculation: Critical-bottom compound = slow RSI ≤ 30 AND medium RSI < slow RSI AND fast RSI ≤ medium RSI AND slow RVI ≤ 30 AND medium RVI ≤ slow RVI AND fast RVI ≤ medium RVI AND slow MFI ≤ 30 AND medium MFI ≤ slow MFI AND fast MFI ≤ medium MFI AND smoothed balance of power ≤ 50.
Calculation: Market-up compound = slow RSI at or above its upper threshold AND medium RSI > slow RSI AND fast RSI ≥ medium RSI AND slow RVI at or above its upper threshold AND medium RVI ≥ slow RVI AND fast RVI ≥ medium RVI AND slow MFI at or above its upper threshold AND medium MFI ≥ slow MFI AND fast MFI ≥ medium MFI AND smoothed balance of power at or above its upper threshold.
Calculation: Critical-up compound = slow RSI ≥ 60 AND medium RSI > slow RSI AND fast RSI ≥ medium RSI AND slow RVI ≥ 60 AND medium RVI ≥ slow RVI AND fast RVI ≥ medium RVI AND slow MFI ≥ 60 AND medium MFI ≥ slow MFI AND fast MFI ≥ medium MFI AND smoothed balance of power ≥ 60.
Calculation: Bottom signal = market-bottom compound was true on the prior bar and is false now.
Calculation: Composite trend-entry conditions = each of: (open or high ≥ down trend line) AND (open or high ≥ up trend line) AND (open or high ≥ SMA of HLCC4) AND (open or high ≥ parabolic SAR) AND ALMA of open is rising; (close or low < SMA of HLCC4) AND (open or high ≥ down trend line) AND (open or high ≥ up trend line) AND (open or high ≥ parabolic SAR); or (close or low < down trend line) AND (close or low < up trend line) AND (open or high ≥ SMA of HLCC4) AND (open or high ≥ parabolic SAR) AND ALMA of open is rising. A rising/falling ALMA means its value rises/falls from the prior bar.
Calculation: Composite exit conditions = each of: all of close or low < down trend line, close or low < up trend line, close or low < SMA of HLCC4, close or low < parabolic SAR, and ALMA of open falling; (close or low < SMA of HLCC4) AND (close or low < parabolic SAR) AND (open or high ≥ down trend line) AND (open or high ≥ up trend line); (close or low < parabolic SAR) AND (open or high ≥ down trend line) AND (open or high ≥ up trend line) AND (open or high ≥ SMA of HLCC4); (open or high ≥ up trend line) AND (close or low < down trend line) AND (close or low < parabolic SAR) AND (close or low < SMA of HLCC4); or (open or high ≥ up trend line) AND (close or low < down trend line) AND (close or low < parabolic SAR).
Calculation: Bottom sequence = a buy event occurs when the rolling lowest low is crossed by low and that rolling low is lower than its prior value; then the next bar sets the sequence to 2 if open > close or the open from two bars ago > current close; from sequence 2, the next bar sets it to 3 if open < close; otherwise reset to 0.
Calculation: Expected profit level = position average entry price × (1 + profit percentage).
Calculation: Grid levels = entry price × (1 − grid spacing percentage × level number).
Long: Trend changes from down to up AND flat position; OR Bottom sequence = 2 AND flat position; OR Bottom signal AND flat position; OR, while long, Bottom sequence = 2 AND prior high < entry price; OR, while long, Bottom signal OR a composite trend-entry condition OR low crosses under an entry-price discount level AND SMA(close, 9) is falling over its specified lookback AND SMA(low, 1) < 0.99 × entry price; OR, while long, Bottom sequence = 2 AND position size ≥ 1 AND prior high < entry price; OR, while long in grid mode, prior close < any active grid level AND Bottom sequence = 2.
Short: 
Exit: Long, (trend changes from up to down OR any composite exit condition OR low crosses under expected profit level) AND expected profit level ≤ SMA(low, 1); OR market-up compound AND expected profit level < SMA(low, 1); OR low crosses under the stop-loss level AND open trades ≥ 90% of the configured pyramid limit AND stop loss is enabled.
Exit: Long, (close > entry price OR market-up compound) AND cooldown is false AND intermediate profit taking is enabled.
