Calculation: Squeeze release = Bollinger Bands are fully inside Keltner Channels for at least a configurable number of consecutive completed bars, followed by a completed bar where they are no longer fully inside.
Calculation: Bullish RSI hook = RSI crosses above the bullish threshold, or is above it and rises on each of the last two bar-to-bar changes.
Calculation: Bearish RSI hook = RSI crosses below the bearish threshold, or is below it and falls on each of the last two bar-to-bar changes.
Long: Squeeze release AND close ≥ Keltner basis AND (HTF filter disabled OR last completed HTF close > its EMA AND that EMA > its value at a longer lookback) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ its threshold) AND (DI filter disabled OR +DI > −DI) AND (session filter disabled OR UTC time is within a selected session) AND (RSI > bullish threshold AND (RSI hook disabled OR bullish RSI hook) + (MACD confirmation disabled OR MACD histogram > 0 OR rising) + (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × multiplier) ≥ configurable minimum score) AND cooldown elapsed AND short conditions are false.
Short: Squeeze release AND close ≤ Keltner basis AND (HTF filter disabled OR last completed HTF close < its EMA AND that EMA < its value at a longer lookback) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ its threshold) AND (DI filter disabled OR −DI > +DI) AND (session filter disabled OR UTC time is within a selected session) AND (RSI < bearish threshold AND (RSI hook disabled OR bearish RSI hook) + (MACD confirmation disabled OR MACD histogram < 0 OR falling) + (volume confirmation disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × multiplier) ≥ configurable minimum score) AND cooldown elapsed AND long conditions are false.
