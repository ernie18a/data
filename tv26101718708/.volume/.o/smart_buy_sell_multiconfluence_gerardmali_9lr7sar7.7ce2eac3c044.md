Calculation: Fast and medium EMA slopes = each EMA is rising (falling) when its current value is greater (less) than its value at the slope lookback bars earlier; fast lookback is shorter than medium lookback.
Calculation: Bullish (bearish) MACD histogram momentum = current histogram is greater (less) than its values on each of the two preceding bars.
Calculation: Bullish (bearish) RSI divergence = close is higher (lower) than its value at the divergence lookback bars earlier, while RSI is lower (higher) than its earlier value and above (below) its bearish (bullish) RSI threshold.
Calculation: Upper (lower) range close = close is in the upper (lower) portion of the current bar's high-low range.
Calculation: Overextension = absolute distance between close and the longest EMA, divided by that EMA; veto a long (short) when close is above (below) that EMA and overextension exceeds its threshold.
Calculation: Bull bonus = 2 if fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, plus 1 for each of: close > longest EMA; RSI > its midpoint and rising versus its lookback; stochastic K > D and K at its earlier lookback is below its low threshold; +DI > -DI.
Calculation: Bear bonus = 2 if fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA, plus 1 for each of: close < longest EMA; RSI < its midpoint and falling versus its lookback; stochastic K < D and K at its earlier lookback is above its high threshold; -DI > +DI.
Long: ADX > trend threshold AND fast and medium EMA slopes rising AND RSI within its long-entry range AND bullish MACD histogram momentum AND upper range close AND NOT overextended above the longest EMA AND NOT bearish RSI divergence AND bull bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses above signal line with prior histogram below zero OR stochastic K crosses above D with prior K below its low trigger threshold OR RSI crosses above its midpoint with RSI two bars earlier below its low trigger threshold OR fast EMA crosses above medium EMA while medium EMA is rising and ADX > its crossover threshold) AND no long signal within the cooldown lookback bars.
Short: ADX > trend threshold AND fast and medium EMA slopes falling AND RSI within its short-entry range AND bearish MACD histogram momentum AND lower range close AND NOT overextended below the longest EMA AND NOT bullish RSI divergence AND bear bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses below signal line with prior histogram above zero OR stochastic K crosses below D with prior K above its high trigger threshold OR RSI crosses below its midpoint with RSI two bars earlier above its high trigger threshold OR fast EMA crosses below medium EMA while medium EMA is falling and ADX > its crossover threshold) AND no short signal within the cooldown lookback bars.
