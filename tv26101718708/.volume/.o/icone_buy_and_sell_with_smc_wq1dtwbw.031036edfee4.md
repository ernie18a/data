Calculation: EMA20, EMA50, EMA200, ATR, RSI, relative volume = volume / 20-bar average volume, candle body ratio = abs(close-open) / max(high-low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Long trend filter = close > EMA200 AND EMA20 > EMA20 three bars earlier; short trend filter = close < EMA200 AND EMA20 < EMA20 three bars earlier. When enabled, require the matching filter.
Calculation: HTF trend = last completed configured higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA; long requires close > EMA and short requires close < EMA. When enabled, require the matching trend.
Calculation: MTF bullish count = number of the five configured timeframe last completed closes above their EMA50; used only by high-quality trend entries.
Calculation: Bull impulse = close - lowest low over the longer impulse lookback >= ATR × impulse threshold AND close > EMA20; bear impulse = highest high over that lookback - close >= ATR × impulse threshold AND close < EMA20.
Calculation: Structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over the shorter structure lookback, excluding the current bar. Previous range = high-low range over the immediately preceding structure-length window. Compression = current structure range < previous range × compression factor AND ATR < its structure-length average AND structure range / ATR within the configured bounds.
Calculation: Valid structure = compression AND (both high regression slopes down and low regression slopes up, OR either slope is in that direction). Equilibrium = midpoint of structure high and low.
Calculation: Raw breakout = valid structure on the prior bar AND current close crosses above structure high + ATR buffer (long), or below structure low - ATR buffer (short).
Calculation: IC score = sum of the applicable points: trend (8 for price on matching side of EMA200, 7 for EMA20 slope in matching direction, 5 for EMA200 slope over 10 bars); impulse (10 for matching impulse, plus 5 if efficiency >= 0.25 or 3 if >= 0.15); compression (6 contracting range, 5 contracting ATR, 4 valid width); structure (10 triangle shape, 5 valid structure); breakout (8 raw breakout, 4 strong matching candle, 3 close beyond structure by 0.25 ATR); momentum (5 matching RSI filter, 5 volume filter); liquidity/context (3 matching HTF trend, 4 sweep of matching structure boundary, 3 matching discount/premium, 4 within 0.5 ATR of equilibrium). Efficiency = abs(close - close at the longer impulse lookback) / sum of absolute close changes over that lookback. Cap score at 100.
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish candle = matching candle direction, body ratio >= configured minimum, and close in the top/bottom quarter of its range. Volume filter passes when disabled or relative volume >= configured minimum. RSI filter passes when disabled or RSI is 50–78 long / 22–50 short.
Calculation: Retest = within the configured bars after a raw breakout, price touches the breakout level within configured ATR tolerance but not more than 0.75 ATR beyond it, then closes back beyond the level with a matching candle. Cancel the pending retest if price closes 0.5 ATR beyond the level in the wrong direction or the time limit expires.
Calculation: Fakeout = valid structure on prior bar, price pierces structure high/low by 0.15 ATR, then closes back inside with the opposite candle; apply the configured fakeout cooldown. Low/high sweep = low below structure low / high above structure high, with close back above / below that boundary.
Calculation: Entry risk = distance from entry close to the selected stop: ATR stop at 1.7 ATR by default, structural stop at the more distant of the recent 10-bar extreme and breakout level offset by 0.25 ATR; configured stop method selects ATR, structure, or the more distant stop. Targets are entry plus/minus risk multiplied by configured target multiples; current stop starts at initial stop, moves to entry plus/minus configured ATR buffer after target 1, and trails at configured ATR distance from close after target 2.
Long: confirmed bar AND allowed direction AND IC score >= minimum score AND (raw breakout OR approved retest OR trend continuation OR sweep long) AND timing filters pass AND macro filter passes AND daily trend filter passes AND flat (or opposite position when flipping enabled). Approved breakout additionally requires strict filters when strict mode is selected. Retest-prioritized mode takes approved retests, or approved breakouts with body ratio >= 0.70 and relative volume >= 1.30; direct-breakout mode takes approved breakouts; retest-only mode takes approved retests. Trend continuation requires enabled trend mode, long trend and HTF filters, a low touching EMA20 within the configured pullback lookback, close > EMA20 and prior high, bullish candle, and (in high-quality mode) RSI >= 50, body ratio >= 0.35, relative volume >= configured minimum, and MTF bullish count >= 3. Sweep long requires enabled sweeps, a confirmed bear fakeout, and (when trend alignment is enabled) regime direction >= 0. Apply enabled headroom filter: close > structure high OR structure high - close >= ATR. Strict mode additionally requires long trend, impulse, volume, RSI, candle, and HTF filters for breakouts; retests require long trend and HTF filters.
Short: confirmed bar AND allowed direction AND IC score >= minimum score AND (raw breakout OR approved retest OR trend continuation OR sweep short) AND timing filters pass AND macro filter passes AND daily trend filter passes AND flat (or opposite position when flipping enabled). Approved breakout additionally requires strict filters when strict mode is selected. Retest-prioritized mode takes approved retests, or approved breakouts with body ratio >= 0.70 and relative volume >= 1.30; direct-breakout mode takes approved breakouts; retest-only mode takes approved retests. Trend continuation requires enabled trend mode, short trend and HTF filters, a high touching EMA20 within the configured pullback lookback, close < EMA20 and prior low, bearish candle, and (in high-quality mode) RSI <= 50, body ratio >= 0.35, relative volume >= configured minimum, and MTF bullish count <= 2. Sweep short requires enabled sweeps, a confirmed bull fakeout, and (when trend alignment is enabled) regime direction <= 0. Apply enabled headroom filter: close < structure low OR close - structure low >= ATR. Strict mode additionally requires short trend, impulse, volume, RSI, candle, and HTF filters for breakouts; retests require short trend and HTF filters.
Exit: long, low <= current stop OR high >= target 1, target 2, target 3, or target 4; reduce configured portions at targets 1–3 and close the remainder at target 4. Short, high >= current stop OR low <= target 1, target 2, target 3, or target 4; reduce configured portions at targets 1–3 and close the remainder at target 4.
Exit: long, close < structure low (close all); short, close > structure high (close all). For guide warnings, after target 1 in automatic mode, each new opposing fakeout, close crossing EMA20 against the position, two low-volume bars with a counter-direction candle while profitable, opposing five-bar candle volume exceeding matching volume by the configured ratio, or nearby structure/previous-day-or-week extreme/liquidity level while profitable advances the guide one level: reduce 25%, then reduce 75%, then close all. The guide advances immediately to close-all on the structure-break condition.
