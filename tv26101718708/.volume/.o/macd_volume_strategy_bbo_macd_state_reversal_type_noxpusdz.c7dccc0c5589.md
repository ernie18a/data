Calculation: MACD = moving average of close over the shorter lookback minus moving average of close over the longer lookback; each average is SMA or EMA.
Calculation: MACD signal line = SMA or EMA of MACD over its lookback.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ longer-lookback EMA of volume.
Calculation: Recent swing low = lowest low over a lookback; recent swing high = highest high over a lookback.
Long: MACD crosses above zero AND volume oscillator > 0 AND MACD > MACD signal line
Short: MACD crosses below zero AND volume oscillator > 0 AND MACD < MACD signal line
Exit: Long, short condition OR low ≤ recent swing low OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − recent swing low) × reward multiple
Exit: Short, long condition OR high ≥ recent swing high OR low ≤ entry price − (recent swing high − entry price) × reward multiple
