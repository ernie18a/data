Calculation: relative effort = current volume divided by its 50-bar average; if volume is missing or zero, use current high−low spread divided by the 50-bar average of that spread
Calculation: prior trend score = 100 × (close − close 30 bars earlier) ÷ (ATR(14) × 30), clipped to [−100, 100]; uptrend if score ≥ threshold, downtrend if score ≤ negative threshold
Calculation: range = boundaries set from the secondary test and automatic rally; during Phase B, expand each boundary to include new highs/lows, capped at a fraction of the original range height
Calculation: maturity score = weighted sum of whether prior trend, climax, automatic rally, secondary test, Phase C, strength move, last point of support/supply, and Phase E acceptance have occurred
Calculation: validation score = weighted sum of whether prior trend, absorption, valid range height, sufficient same-edge tests, opposite-edge tests, no failed tests, sufficient range traversals, Phase C, strength move, and last point of support/supply have occurred
Long: while no structure is active, downtrend and relative effort is elevated and low is near the 30-bar lowest low, with a qualifying selling climax seeded within the allowed bars (preliminary support); OR an uptrend, new 30-bar high, elevated relative effort, and expanded spread seed a buying climax, with its close in the lower part of its range or effort at a 30-bar high (buying climax); OR in an active accumulation or re-accumulation, price probes below range support by a bounded ATR distance, closes back inside, and the probe score passes; OR in Phase C after the required wait and score gates, a bullish dominance bar has sufficient spread or 5-bar price advance and elevated relative effort (sign of strength); OR in Phase D, a bar near the broken range high has low relative effort, narrow spread, holds above the broken edge, and occurs at least two bars after strength (last point of support); OR the active structure has bullish outcome set, maturity above the selected strictness threshold, readiness score at least 5, and the bar is the spring or last point of support (standard or conservative entry), or the strength bar with no spring/UTAD (aggressive entry)
Short: while no structure is active, uptrend and relative effort is elevated and high is near the 30-bar highest high, with a qualifying buying climax seeded within the allowed bars (preliminary supply); OR a downtrend, new 30-bar low, elevated relative effort, and expanded spread seed a selling climax, with its close in the upper part of its range or effort at a 30-bar high (selling climax); OR in an active distribution or re-distribution, price probes above range resistance by a bounded ATR distance, closes back inside, and the probe score passes; OR in Phase C after the required wait and score gates, a bearish dominance bar has sufficient spread or 5-bar price decline and elevated relative effort (sign of weakness); OR in Phase D, a bar near the broken range low has low relative effort, narrow spread, holds below the broken edge, and occurs at least two bars after weakness (last point of supply); OR the active structure has bearish outcome set, maturity above the selected strictness threshold, readiness score at least 5, and the bar is the UTAD or last point of supply (standard or conservative entry), or the weakness bar with no spring/UTAD (aggressive entry)
