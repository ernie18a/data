Calculation: Trend = (14-period RSI of OHLC4 + RSI of 14-bar sums of volume×HLC3 on rising HLC3 bars and volume×HLC3 on falling HLC3 bars + Bollinger oscillator of OHLC4 using a 25-bar SMA and 2 standard deviations + one third of a 21-bar stochastic of OHLC4 smoothed by 3 bars) / 2
Calculation: Regression trend = the current value of the linear regression of Trend on bar index over 8 bars, calculated as bar_index × slope + intercept, where slope = correlation(bar_index, Trend, 8) × stdev(Trend, 8) / stdev(bar_index, 8), and intercept = EMA(Trend, 8) − slope × EMA(bar_index, 8)
Calculation: Major reversal event = +1 when the consecutive count of closes below their close 4 bars earlier exceeds the longer count threshold, the current bar closes above its open, and its low is at or below the lowest low over the longer lookback; −1 when the consecutive count of closes above their close 4 bars earlier exceeds the longer count threshold, the current bar closes below its open, and its high is at or above the highest high over the longer lookback; otherwise 0. Reset the relevant count to zero when its event occurs.
Calculation: Minor reversal event = same as the major reversal event, using a shorter count threshold and shorter lookback.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursively trail close by one 10-bar ATR: when current and previous closes are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and current close minus ATR; when both are below it, use the lesser of the prior stop and current close plus ATR; otherwise set it to current close minus ATR if current close is above the prior stop, or current close plus ATR otherwise. Initialize prior stop to zero.
Long: (Minor reversal event = +1 OR Major reversal event = +1) AND Regression trend <= minimum level OR Regression trend is rising on each of the last two bar-to-bar comparisons AND current Regression trend <= minimum level OR Trend crosses above minimum level OR Regression trend crosses below Trend AND Regression trend <= minimum level AND Trend <= minimum level OR Regression trend is below minimum level on the current and prior two bars AND current Regression trend is rising AND current Trend <= minimum level OR (Regression trend or Trend <= minimum level on any of the current or prior two bars) AND close > ATR trailing stop AND close crosses above ATR trailing stop
Exit: Long, (low <= entry price after a partial take-profit exit) OR high >= entry price + daily ATR × first take-profit percentage OR high >= entry price + daily ATR × second take-profit percentage
