Calculation: Daily session VWAP and SD bands = volume-weighted mean and standard-deviation bands of HLC3, reset each day; lower band is VWAP minus the configured standard-deviation multiple. Use current bar data.
Calculation: Pip size = configured pip size, or 10 times the instrument's minimum tick when automatic sizing is used.
Calculation: StochRSI %K = smoothed stochastic of RSI; %D = smoothed %K.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ADX = DMI ADX from the last completed bar of the configured higher timeframe.
Long: Flat AND the lower-band-to-VWAP distance meets the configured minimum AND (ADX filter disabled OR higher-timeframe ADX is above or below its threshold as configured) AND close is below the lower band to start the setup; while the setup is active, retain the first dip bar as anchor, refresh the setup timeout on each further close below the band before oscillator confirmation, and discard it if the next required confirmation is more than the configured window after the latest timeout reset; oscillator confirmation is either RSI above its level with an up-cross within the window at or after the dip, StochRSI %K above its threshold with an up-cross within the window at or after the dip, or (in K/D mode) %K above %D with an up-cross within the window at or after the dip; in strict mode require the selected oscillator's fresh up-cross after the dip instead; then MACD confirmation is either MACD line above signal line with an up-cross within the window at or after the dip, or MACD line above zero with an up-cross within the window at or after the dip, according to mode, with this confirmation arriving within the configured window after oscillator confirmation; in strict mode require the corresponding fresh MACD up-cross; enter when MACD confirmation completes.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target; stop is entry price minus the configured fixed-pip distance or the ATR multiple frozen at entry, raised to at least entry plus the configured lock after high reaches entry plus the breakeven trigger; target is either the live daily VWAP minus its buffer, floored at entry plus the minimum-profit distance, the configured fixed-pip target, or the greater of buffered VWAP, entry plus the ATR target frozen at entry, and the minimum-profit floor; subtract configured spread from both exit order levels.
