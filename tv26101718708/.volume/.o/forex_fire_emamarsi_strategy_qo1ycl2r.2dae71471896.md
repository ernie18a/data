Long: On the current entry bar, short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND close > entry-bar moving average AND fast RSI > slow RSI AND fast RSI > 50 AND volume > its longer-lookback average AND higher-timeframe short-period EMA > long-period EMA AND current close > higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe slow RSI > 40 AND higher-timeframe fast RSI > slow RSI AND (entry-bar short-period EMA crosses above long-period EMA OR entry-bar close crosses above its moving average); higher-timeframe indicators use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Short: On the current entry bar, short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND close < entry-bar moving average AND fast RSI < slow RSI AND fast RSI < 50 AND volume > its longer-lookback average AND higher-timeframe short-period EMA < long-period EMA AND current close < higher-timeframe moving average AND higher-timeframe slow RSI < 60 AND higher-timeframe fast RSI < slow RSI AND (entry-bar short-period EMA crosses below long-period EMA OR entry-bar close crosses below its moving average); higher-timeframe indicators use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long stop-loss level = entry price reduced by the configured percentage; short stop-loss level = entry price increased by the configured percentage.
Calculation: Long take-profit level = entry price increased by the configured percentage; short take-profit level = entry price reduced by the configured percentage.
Calculation: Long trailing-stop level = initially entry price reduced by the configured percentage; when close exceeds entry price, update to the greater of its prior level and close reduced by that percentage.
Calculation: Short trailing-stop level = initially entry price increased by the configured percentage; when close is below entry price, update to the lesser of its prior level and close increased by that percentage.
Calculation: Long ATR stop level = current close minus configured ATR multiple times current-bar ATR; short ATR stop level = current close plus configured ATR multiple times current-bar ATR.
Exit: Long, (enabled stop loss AND low < long stop-loss level) OR (enabled take profit AND high > long take-profit level) OR (enabled trailing stop AND low < long trailing-stop level) OR short-period EMA < long-period EMA OR fast RSI > configured overbought level OR (enabled ATR exit AND low <= long ATR stop level).
Exit: Short, (enabled stop loss AND high > short stop-loss level) OR (enabled take profit AND low < short take-profit level) OR (enabled trailing stop AND high > short trailing-stop level) OR short-period EMA > long-period EMA OR fast RSI < configured oversold level OR (enabled ATR exit AND high >= short ATR stop level).
