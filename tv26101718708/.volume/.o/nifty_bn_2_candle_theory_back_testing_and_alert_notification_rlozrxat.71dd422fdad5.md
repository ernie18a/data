Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each daily session and including the current forming session
Calculation: Volume-weighted moving average = volume-weighted average of close over its lookback
Calculation: Supertrend long/short stops = ATR-based trailing stops
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Long: (RSI > 50 AND RSI <= 80 AND close > session VWAP AND close > Supertrend long stop AND close > volume-weighted moving average AND close > Parabolic SAR AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND volume > cutoff AND previous bar volume > cutoff AND close > open AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND within date range AND (session filter disabled OR outside excluded session) AND flat) OR (RSI > 50 AND RSI <= 80 AND volume > cutoff AND previous bar volume > cutoff AND close > open AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND Supertrend long stop crosses above session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses above session VWAP)
Short: (RSI < 40 AND RSI >= 20 AND close < session VWAP AND close < Supertrend short stop AND close < volume-weighted moving average AND close < Parabolic SAR AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND volume > cutoff AND previous bar volume > cutoff AND close < open AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND within date range AND (session filter disabled OR outside excluded session) AND flat) OR (RSI < 40 AND RSI >= 20 AND volume > cutoff AND previous bar volume > cutoff AND close < open AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND Supertrend short stop crosses below session VWAP AND volume-weighted moving average crosses below session VWAP)
Exit: long, low <= stop (previous bar low at entry) OR high >= entry price + (entry price - stop) × risk-to-reward multiplier
Exit: short, high >= stop (previous bar high at entry) OR low <= entry price - (stop - entry price) × risk-to-reward multiplier
