Calculation: Opening-range high/low = maximum high/minimum low of bars in the configured opening-range session; use only after that session completes.
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening-range high − opening-range low, measured in ticks or as a percentage of the range midpoint.
Calculation: Stop level = selected stop method: opposite opening-range boundary, opening-range midpoint, entry price offset by a multiple of current-bar ATR, fixed ticks, or a percentage of entry price; offset against the position.
Calculation: Final target = selected target method at the configured final target: entry risk distance multiple, opening-range size multiple, current-bar ATR multiple, fixed ticks, or percentage of entry price; offset with the position.
Long: opening range locked AND active weekday AND opening-range size within enabled limits AND confirmed bar outside the opening-range session AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no trade taken today) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND ((retest disabled AND (close > opening-range high if close confirmation, otherwise high > opening-range high) AND (all breakouts enabled OR no prior long signal today)) OR (retest enabled AND prior breakout above opening-range high AND low <= opening-range high AND (close > opening-range high if close confirmation, otherwise high > opening-range high) AND (all breakouts enabled OR no prior long signal today)))
Short: opening range locked AND active weekday AND opening-range size within enabled limits AND confirmed bar outside the opening-range session AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no trade taken today) AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > its moving average) AND ((retest disabled AND (close < opening-range low if close confirmation, otherwise low < opening-range low) AND (all breakouts enabled OR no prior short signal today)) OR (retest enabled AND prior breakout below opening-range low AND high >= opening-range low AND (close < opening-range low if close confirmation, otherwise low < opening-range low) AND (all breakouts enabled OR no prior short signal today)))
Exit: long, confirmed bar low <= stop level OR high >= final target
Exit: short, confirmed bar high >= stop level OR low <= final target
