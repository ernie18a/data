Calculation: Fast and slow Kalman estimates = recursive scalar Kalman filters of ohlc4, initialized to the first source value with error 1; use shorter and longer lookbacks respectively, with process noise inversely proportional to lookback and equal measurement noise.
Calculation: Structure bias and activity = smooth the impulse from close crossing above the latest confirmed pivot high (+1) or below the latest confirmed pivot low (−1); activity smooths the absolute impulse. Use the same pivot lookback for highs and lows.
Calculation: Kalman Flow basis = recursively smooth the fast Kalman estimate with a smoothing factor interpolated from slower to faster as structure activity rises; add an ATR-scaled adjustment from structure bias and pull toward the midpoint of the latest confirmed pivot high and low, weighted by structure activity.
Calculation: Flow boundaries = Kalman Flow basis plus or minus ATR multiplied by a boundary factor.
Calculation: Kalman Flow direction = initialize bullish when close is at or above the basis, otherwise bearish; switch bullish when close is above the upper boundary and, when slope confirmation is enabled, the basis is rising; switch bearish when close is below the lower boundary and, when enabled, the basis is falling; otherwise retain the prior direction.
Long: Kalman Flow direction is bullish OR (Kalman Flow direction is bearish AND close is above the upper flow boundary OR close is at least the upper flow boundary minus an ATR-scaled warning distance)
Short: Kalman Flow direction is bearish OR (Kalman Flow direction is bullish AND close is below the lower flow boundary OR close is at most the lower flow boundary plus an ATR-scaled warning distance)
