Calculation: Estimated slot ask/bid volume = For each chart bar, divide its price range into equal slots. For each lower-timeframe bar within it, distribute its volume evenly across the slots touched by its low-to-high range; classify that volume as ask when lower-timeframe close ≥ open, otherwise bid. If the chart bar has no lower-timeframe bars, divide chart-bar volume equally across all slots and classify it as ask when chart close ≥ open, otherwise bid. Use forming lower-timeframe bars for the live chart bar and completed lower-timeframe bars for completed chart bars.
Calculation: Estimated candle delta = sum of estimated slot ask volume minus sum of estimated slot bid volume.
Long: Estimated candle delta ≥ 0 OR any slot’s estimated ask volume > its estimated bid volume OR any slot above the lowest slot has estimated ask volume ≥ the preceding slot’s estimated bid volume × the imbalance ratio, where preceding-slot bid volume > 0.
Short: Estimated candle delta < 0 OR any slot’s estimated bid volume > its estimated ask volume OR any slot below the highest slot has estimated bid volume ≥ the following slot’s estimated ask volume × the imbalance ratio, where following-slot ask volume > 0.
