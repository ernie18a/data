Calculation: Volume moving average = SMA or WMA of volume over a configurable lookback; coarser and finer configured timeframes use separate lookbacks.
Long: For any configured timeframe, using its latest available bar (which may still be forming), close > open AND abs(close − open) ≥ body-size multiplier × ATR AND close > the highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND volume ≥ volume multiplier × volume moving average, and this condition was false on the previous chart bar.
Short: For any configured timeframe, using its latest available bar (which may still be forming), close < open AND abs(close − open) ≥ body-size multiplier × ATR AND close < the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND volume ≥ volume multiplier × volume moving average, and this condition was false on the previous chart bar.
