Calculation: Score = clamp to [-100, 100] the sum of enabled modules: +trend weight when fast EMA > slow EMA and fast EMA is rising over 3 bars, −trend weight when fast EMA < slow EMA and falling over 3 bars; RSI weight × min(1, |RSI − midpoint| / 15), rounded, positive above midpoint and negative below; +DMI/ADX weight when +DI > −DI and ADX ≥ minimum, −weight when −DI > +DI and ADX ≥ minimum; +TSI weight when TSI > its signal EMA, −weight when below; volume weight when volume > volume SMA × multiplier, positive if close ≥ open and negative otherwise; squeeze weight when Bollinger Bands are not inside Keltner Channels and momentum is positive and rising over 2 bars, negative when momentum is negative and falling over 2 bars; SMC weight × 0.6, rounded, for a close above the last swing high, and its negative for a close below the last swing low; SMC weight negative for a high sweep and positive for a low sweep. RSI bias is scaled by its distance from the midpoint; fast EMA uses a shorter length than slow EMA. Bollinger and Keltner lookbacks are independently parameterized; momentum is the linear regression of close minus its SMA over the Keltner lookback, evaluated at the current bar. A high sweep occurs when high exceeds the latest equal-high level by more than one quarter of the ATR-based tolerance and close is below that level; a low sweep is the inverse at the latest equal-low level. Equal-high/low levels are averages of consecutive confirmed pivot highs/lows within the ATR-based tolerance and SMC lookback; the latest swing levels are the latest confirmed pivot high/low. Apply the higher-timeframe EMA penalty after summing: multiply a negative score by 0.6 when higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA, or a positive score by 0.6 when higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA, rounding the result. Use the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming and the last completed bar otherwise. All lookbacks, weights, thresholds, multipliers, and module enablement are parameters.
Long: Score ≥ entry threshold AND (ADX/DMI filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) OR low sweep
Short: Score ≤ −entry threshold AND (ADX/DMI filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) OR high sweep
Exit: Long, Score ≤ exit threshold OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × target multiplier OR enabled trailing stop is reached
Exit: Short, Score ≥ −exit threshold OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × target multiplier OR enabled trailing stop is reached
