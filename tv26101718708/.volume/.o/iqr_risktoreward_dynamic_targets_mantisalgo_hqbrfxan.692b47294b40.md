Calculation: Two-pole SuperSmoother(x, L) = a = exp(-1.414π/L), b = 2a cos(1.414π/L), c2 = b, c3 = -a², c1 = 1 - c2 - c3; output = x on initialization, otherwise c1(x + previous x)/2 + c2(previous output) + c3(output two bars ago)
Calculation: Median Wave = Two-pole SuperSmoother(typical price, longer smoothing length), where typical price = (high + low + close)/3
Calculation: Rolling IQR = 75th percentile minus 25th percentile of close over a rolling sample; use linear interpolation between sorted observations
Calculation: Smoothed IQR = Two-pole SuperSmoother(Rolling IQR, shorter smoothing length than the Median Wave length)
Calculation: Lower/Upper Band = Median Wave minus/plus the band-width multiplier times Smoothed IQR
Calculation: Lower/Upper Fence = Median Wave minus/plus the outlier multiplier times Smoothed IQR
Long: (low <= its Lower Band on any of the previous three bars OR current low <= current Lower Fence) AND close > Median Wave AND previous close <= previous Median Wave AND volume >= short-term average volume × activity threshold AND bars since last accepted signal >= cooldown interval AND close - recent short-lookback lowest low > 0 AND max(current Upper Fence, prior-bar highest high over the liquidity lookback) - close > 0 AND [max(current Upper Fence, prior-bar highest high over the liquidity lookback) - close] / [close - recent short-lookback lowest low] >= minimum risk-to-reward threshold
Short: (high >= its Upper Band on any of the previous three bars OR current high >= current Upper Fence) AND close < Median Wave AND previous close >= previous Median Wave AND volume >= short-term average volume × activity threshold AND bars since last accepted signal >= cooldown interval AND recent short-lookback highest high - close > 0 AND close - min(current Lower Fence, prior-bar lowest low over the liquidity lookback) > 0 AND [close - min(current Lower Fence, prior-bar lowest low over the liquidity lookback)] / [recent short-lookback highest high - close] >= minimum risk-to-reward threshold
