Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than every other high in the 2 × swing-length bars around it; confirmed swing low = a low lower than every other low in those bars. Use the latest confirmed swing of each kind available before the current bar.
Calculation: Structure-break cooldown = eligible only when at least swing-length bars have elapsed since the previous structure break.
Long: Previous bar close > latest active confirmed swing high AND structure-break cooldown is clear
Short: Previous bar close < latest active confirmed swing low AND structure-break cooldown is clear
