Calculation: Heiken Ashi candle = close is the mean of raw open, high, low, and close; open is the mean of the prior Heiken Ashi open and close (initially the mean of raw open and close); high is the maximum of raw high, Heiken Ashi open, and Heiken Ashi close; low is their minimum
Calculation: Heiken Ashi net volume = raw volume × (up fraction − down fraction) × mean of Heiken Ashi open, high, low, and close; for a bullish Heiken Ashi candle, up fraction is (top wick + bottom wick + 2 × body) / [2 × (top wick + bottom wick + body)] and down fraction is (top wick + bottom wick) / [2 × (top wick + bottom wick + body)]; for a bearish candle, reverse these fractions; top wick, bottom wick, and body are measured from the Heiken Ashi candle
Calculation: Net volume regression = linear regression value of Heiken Ashi net volume over a lookback, evaluated at an offset equal to the negative of that lookback
Long: shorter-lookback SMA(close) > shorter-lookback EMA(close) AND the EMA with a slightly longer lookback than that EMA < that EMA AND the three progressively longer-lookback SMA(close) values < that EMA AND the longest-lookback EMA(close) < that EMA AND the intermediate-lookback EMA(close) < that EMA AND that intermediate EMA plus half the same-lookback RMA(true range) < that EMA AND that intermediate EMA minus half the same-lookback RMA(true range) < that EMA AND Net volume regression > 0
