Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of the first bar of each trading day
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of the first bar of each trading day
Long: Close crosses above opening-range high OR (close crosses above same-timeframe VWAP AND same-timeframe RSI > 50) OR (same-timeframe RSI crosses above 50 AND close > same-timeframe VWAP)
Short: Close crosses below opening-range low OR (close crosses below same-timeframe VWAP AND same-timeframe RSI < 50)
