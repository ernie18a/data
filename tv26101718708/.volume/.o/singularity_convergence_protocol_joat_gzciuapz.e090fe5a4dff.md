Calculation: Momentum score = +1 when the weighted composite of volume-flow indicator, Laguerre RSI, smoothed Fisher transform, true strength index, money flow index, normalized on-balance volume, normalized accumulation/distribution, and Chaikin money flow is above zero and rising; −1 when below zero and falling; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Structure score = +1 when an active bullish order block or a bullish fair-value gap coincides with bullish market structure and close below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback; −1 when an active bearish order block or bearish fair-value gap coincides with bearish market structure and close above that midpoint; otherwise 0. A bullish fair-value gap is low above the high two bars earlier; a bearish fair-value gap is high below the low two bars earlier. Market structure turns bullish when a confirmed swing high exceeds the prior recorded swing high or a confirmed swing low is not below the prior recorded swing low; it turns bearish under the opposite comparisons. Swing points use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Velocity score = +1 when the shortest basis exceeds the next-shortest basis, the shortest basis has risen over the last two bars, and the spread between the shortest and longest bases is above a threshold; −1 when the shortest basis is below the next-shortest basis, has fallen over the last two bars, and that spread is below the negative threshold; otherwise +1 if momentum exceeds all five bases, −1 if momentum is at or below all five bases and the ALMA-adjusted momentum, otherwise 0. The five bases are pairwise averages of momentum EMAs with progressively longer lookbacks, cycling the longest EMA back to the shortest. Momentum is the close change over a lookback; ALMA-adjusted momentum is twice the ALMA of close minus momentum.
Calculation: Liquidity score = +1 when price breaks below the latest confirmed swing low and volume exceeds its longer-lookback average times a threshold; −1 when price breaks above the latest confirmed swing high with the same volume condition; otherwise 0. Swing points use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Trend score = +1 when the Hull moving average is rising, SuperTrend is bullish, ADX exceeds a threshold, and the short, medium, and long EMAs are ordered descending; −1 when the Hull moving average is falling, SuperTrend is bearish, ADX exceeds the threshold, and those EMAs are ordered ascending; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Divergence score = +1 on regular bullish divergence between price swing lows and RSI swing lows, with at least two of RSI, MACD, true strength index, and smoothed stochastic rising versus five bars earlier, and their composite oscillator below a negative threshold; −1 on regular bearish divergence between price swing highs and RSI swing highs, with at least two of those measures falling versus five bars earlier, and the composite oscillator above a positive threshold; otherwise 0. Divergence compares the current confirmed swing with the preceding confirmed swing; price and RSI swings use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Volatility score = +1 when a Bollinger-band squeeze ends, close exceeds the prior close, and the mean of the current ATR, Bollinger-width, and historical-volatility percentiles within their respective lookbacks is below a threshold; −1 under the same conditions when close is below the prior close; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Flow score = +1 when the institutional flow index is above a threshold, Chaikin money flow is positive, and money flow index is above its midpoint; −1 when the flow index is below the negative threshold, Chaikin money flow is negative, and money flow index is below its midpoint; otherwise 0. The flow index is the average of scaled Chaikin money flow, money flow index minus its midpoint, normalized on-balance volume minus its midpoint, and normalized accumulation/distribution minus its midpoint.
Calculation: Bullish signal count = number of bullish conditions among momentum (composite above zero and rising), structure, velocity, liquidity, trend, divergence, volatility, and flow. Bearish signal count = number of their bearish counterparts.
Calculation: Confluence total = sum of the eight system scores. Bullish bonus count = number of true conditions among extreme bullish velocity (shortest basis and ALMA-adjusted momentum above the basis-average mean plus a multiple of its standard deviation, with ALMA-adjusted momentum rising versus three bars earlier), all five velocity layers bullish, divergence confluence equal to four, and volume surge with flow index above a threshold. Bearish bonus count uses the corresponding bearish conditions; divergence confluence equal to four adds a bonus to both sides.
Calculation: Bullish total score = bullish signal count + bullish bonus count + max(confluence total, 0). Bearish total score = bearish signal count + bearish bonus count + max(−confluence total, 0).
Long: bullish total score ≥ minimum signal threshold AND bullish total score > bearish total score.
Short: bearish total score ≥ minimum signal threshold AND bearish total score > bullish total score.
Exit: Long, low ≤ current close − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ current close + ATR × stop multiplier × reward-risk ratio OR low ≤ current close − ATR × trailing multiplier.
Exit: Short, high ≥ current close + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ current close − ATR × stop multiplier × reward-risk ratio OR high ≥ current close + ATR × trailing multiplier.
