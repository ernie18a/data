Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of the configured price source.
Calculation: RSI pivot high/low = a local high/low in RSI confirmed after the configured number of bars to its right; pair it with the price high/low on that pivot bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA; positive when close is above the EMA, negative otherwise. Use the configured higher timeframe and no-lookahead data (last completed bar historically; current forming bar in real time).
Calculation: Ranging = ADX is below its configured threshold.
Calculation: Side confluence score = count of these five conditions: higher-timeframe bias agrees with the side; ranging; a same-side divergence occurred within the configured recent-divergence window; usable volume is above its 20-bar average; RSI rose on this bar for long or fell for short.
Calculation: Volatility-cluster ratio = decaying shock intensity divided by the 100-bar average shock rate. A shock occurs when the absolute log close return exceeds a configurable multiple of its 20-bar standard deviation. On each confirmed bar, intensity becomes prior intensity × exp(−decay) plus excitation if there is a shock.
Calculation: Effective minimum confluence = configured minimum plus the rounded volatility lift when that filter is enabled; the lift is zero unless the ratio exceeds its warning threshold, then scales proportionally with the excess and is capped at its configured maximum.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = count of the three configured higher-timeframe closes above their respective EMAs for long, or below for short. Use no-lookahead data (last completed bars historically; current forming bars in real time).
Long: (three consecutive RSI declines, with the RSI three bars ago below the oversold threshold and four bars ago above it; and, if enabled, higher-timeframe bias is positive; and, if enabled, ADX is below its threshold; and, if enabled, the session warmup has elapsed; and long confluence score ≥ effective minimum; and, if enabled, higher-timeframe agreement meets its minimum) OR (a confirmed RSI pivot low whose paired price low is below the prior paired pivot low and whose RSI is at least the configured minimum gap above the prior pivot RSI, or whose paired price low is above the prior paired pivot low and whose RSI is at least that gap below the prior pivot RSI; same-side pivot gap is within configured minimum and maximum bars; and the configured same-side divergence cooldown has elapsed).
Short: (three consecutive RSI rises, with the RSI three bars ago above the overbought threshold and four bars ago below it; and, if enabled, higher-timeframe bias is negative; and, if enabled, ADX is below its threshold; and, if enabled, the session warmup has elapsed; and short confluence score ≥ effective minimum; and, if enabled, higher-timeframe agreement meets its minimum) OR (a confirmed RSI pivot high whose paired price high is above the prior paired pivot high and whose RSI is at least the configured minimum gap below the prior pivot RSI, or whose paired price high is below the prior paired pivot high and whose RSI is at least that gap above the prior pivot RSI; same-side pivot gap is within configured minimum and maximum bars; and the configured same-side divergence cooldown has elapsed).
