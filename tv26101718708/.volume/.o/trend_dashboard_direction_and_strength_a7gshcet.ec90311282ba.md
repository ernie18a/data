Calculation: Market structure = Confirm swing highs and lows using equal left/right pivot lookbacks; register an upward break when close or high (per break-confirmation setting) exceeds the active swing high, and a downward break when close or low falls below the active swing low. Direction is the latest break. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud = Cloud top/bottom are the maximum/minimum of Span A and Span B; Span A is the mean of the midpoints of the 9-bar and 26-bar high/low ranges, and Span B is the midpoint of the 52-bar high/low range. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = Cumulative sum of volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), using zero when high equals low; compare current value and close with their values the lookback bars ago. Omit this vote when volume is unavailable. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
Calculation: OBV divergence = Bearish when close equals the highest close in its lookback window and OBV is below its window high; bullish when close equals the lowest close and OBV is above its window low. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
Calculation: RSI divergence = Bearish when close equals the highest close in its lookback window and RSI is below its window high; bullish when close equals the lowest close and RSI is above its window low. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Directional score = Sum of the eight votes: fast/mid EMA relation, mid/slow EMA relation, close/fast EMA relation, MACD, RSI, SuperTrend, price/cloud relation, and CVD; each vote is +1, −1, or 0. Omit CVD when volume is unavailable. EMA lengths are ordered fast < mid < slow. Use each assigned timeframe’s current bar; lookahead is off, so a requested bar may still be forming.
