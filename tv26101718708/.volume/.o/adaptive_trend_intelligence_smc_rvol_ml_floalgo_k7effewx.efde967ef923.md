Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend direction = calculate ATR on the selected lookback and on a shorter lookback; scale the base ATR multiplier by the shorter/longer ATR ratio, clamped to half through 1.5 times the base multiplier. Form upper/lower bands around (high+low)/2; carry the upper band forward as the lower of its current value and prior band unless prior close exceeded prior upper band, and carry the lower band as the higher of its current value and prior band unless prior close fell below prior lower band. Direction flips bearish-to-bullish when close exceeds the carried upper band, and bullish-to-bearish when close falls below the carried lower band. Confirm a flip after price remains in the new direction for the selected number of bars; zero bars confirms immediately.
Calculation: Directional volume percentile = split volume into buy volume = volume×(close−low)/(high−low) and sell volume = volume×(high−close)/(high−low); for each, divide current directional volume by its rolling median and multiply by 50, capped at 100. Use the percentile for the confirmed SuperTrend direction; require volume and a completed lookback window when filtering is enabled.
Calculation: Online logistic-regression confidence = logistic probability from normalized candle body, upper/lower wick, range, volume, momentum, directional volume delta, signed-body trend, ATR slope, volume trend, SuperTrend distance, trend duration, RSI, Bollinger position, directional volume percentile, and market-structure bias. Train separate bullish- and bearish-flip models on prior same-direction flip outcomes, resolved at the next confirmed flip by whether close is above (bullish) or below (bearish) the prior flip close; use the resulting probability as the signal confidence.
Calculation: Market-structure swings = smooth stochastic %K with a 3-bar average. Record the highest high while %K is at/above overbought and confirm it as a pivot when %K falls below overbought; record the lowest low while %K is at/below oversold and confirm it when %K rises above oversold. Keep alternating high and low pivots, updating a same-side pivot only when more extreme. Label each high HH or LH against the prior high, and each low HL or LL against the prior low. Structure bias becomes bullish when the latest high is HH and latest low HL, bearish when latest high is LH and latest low LL, and otherwise retains its prior bias. After a pivot, set a continuation level to the preceding same-side swing in the bias direction, or a reversal level to the preceding same-side swing against the bias; on a confirmed close beyond that level, mark BOS in the bias direction or CHoCH in the opposite direction, with CHoCH changing the bias.
Long: Confirmed bullish SuperTrend flip AND (volume filter disabled OR directional volume percentile ≥ threshold) AND (confidence unavailable OR confidence ≥ minimum) AND (structure filter disabled OR market-structure bias ≥ neutral) OR bullish BOS OR bullish CHoCH OR an accepted HH or HL pivot label.
Short: Confirmed bearish SuperTrend flip AND (volume filter disabled OR directional volume percentile ≥ threshold) AND (confidence unavailable OR confidence ≥ minimum) AND (structure filter disabled OR market-structure bias ≤ neutral) OR bearish BOS OR bearish CHoCH OR an accepted LH or LL pivot label.
