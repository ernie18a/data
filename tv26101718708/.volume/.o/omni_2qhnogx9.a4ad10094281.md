Calculation: Confirmed swing bottom = a pivot low, confirmed after the right-side bars, where the pivot is at least the minimum swing size (a parameter times chart-timeframe ATR(14)) below the last top, or no last top exists; it qualifies only if the previous confirmed swing was not a bottom. Confirmed swing top is the converse, using the last bottom and requiring the previous confirmed swing not to be a top. A qualifying pivot is evaluated on its confirmation bar.
Calculation: Swing structure = higher highs and higher lows when both the latest and preceding confirmed tops and bottoms exist and each latest point is above its preceding point; lower highs and lower lows are the converse.
Calculation: Bias score = number of bullish votes minus number of bearish votes from EMA trend, higher-timeframe trend, VWAP position, RSI momentum, swing structure, and previous-day position.
Long: Confirmed swing bottom OR short-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA OR close > 50-period EMA of the selected higher timeframe (bar still forming) OR close > session VWAP OR RSI(14) > 50 OR swing structure is higher highs and higher lows OR close > previous-day close (last completed daily bar) OR bias score > 0
Short: Confirmed swing top OR short-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA OR close < 50-period EMA of the selected higher timeframe (bar still forming) OR close < session VWAP OR RSI(14) < 50 OR swing structure is lower highs and lower lows OR close < previous-day close (last completed daily bar) OR bias score < 0
