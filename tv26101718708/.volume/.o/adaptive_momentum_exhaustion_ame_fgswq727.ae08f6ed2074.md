Calculation: Normalized momentum = (close − close N bars ago) / ATR
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume
Long: Normalized momentum > 1.5 AND shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND relative volume > 1.2 AND (RSI <= 70 OR close <= upper Bollinger Band)
Short: Normalized momentum < -1.5 AND shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND relative volume > 1.2 AND (RSI >= 30 OR close >= lower Bollinger Band)
