Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across equal lookbacks before and after it; available after the following lookback bars. Use the latest confirmed swing high as the upper liquidity pool and the latest confirmed swing low as the lower liquidity pool.
Calculation: Lower/upper wick = distance from the low/high to the nearer of open and close; body = absolute difference between close and open. Wick-to-body ratio = wick divided by body, or 5 when body is zero and wick is positive, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Candle delta proxy = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when range is zero. Delta ratio = candle delta proxy / absolute value of its moving average over the same lookback as the volume moving average, or 0 when that average is zero.
Calculation: Absorption score = weighted average of three components: volume ratio, wick-to-body ratio, and directional delta ratio, each scaled to 0–100 as 100 × ratio / its soft cap and clamped to 0–100. The soft caps are 1.8 × the minimum volume ratio, 2 × the minimum wick-to-body ratio, and 2 for directional delta ratio. For long, use the lower-wick ratio and max(delta ratio, 0); for short, use the upper-wick ratio and max(−delta ratio, 0).
Long: Low < latest confirmed swing low − ATR × sweep buffer AND lower wick ≥ ATR × minimum sweep wick AND (close > latest confirmed swing low OR close-back-inside requirement disabled) AND low < previous bar low AND volume / its moving average ≥ minimum volume ratio AND lower-wick-to-body ratio ≥ minimum wick-to-body ratio AND bullish absorption score ≥ minimum score AND (ATR floor filter disabled OR ATR ≥ its longer-lookback moving average × floor multiplier OR that moving average is unavailable or zero) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close > EMA).
Short: High > latest confirmed swing high + ATR × sweep buffer AND upper wick ≥ ATR × minimum sweep wick AND (close < latest confirmed swing high OR close-back-inside requirement disabled) AND high > previous bar high AND volume / its moving average ≥ minimum volume ratio AND upper-wick-to-body ratio ≥ minimum wick-to-body ratio AND bearish absorption score ≥ minimum score AND (ATR floor filter disabled OR ATR ≥ its longer-lookback moving average × floor multiplier OR that moving average is unavailable or zero) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close < EMA).
