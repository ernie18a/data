Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume > its longer-lookback average volume × volume multiplier AND lower-timeframe delta / volume ≤ −delta threshold AND close ≥ (high + low) / 2; lower-timeframe delta is the sum of up-candle volume minus down-candle volume
Calculation: Bearish absorption = volume > its longer-lookback average volume × volume multiplier AND lower-timeframe delta / volume ≥ delta threshold AND close ≤ (high + low) / 2; lower-timeframe delta is the sum of up-candle volume minus down-candle volume
Calculation: Bullish FVG retest = price touches an active bullish gap formed when the current low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; count only its first retest when first-touch mode is enabled, and remove the gap after a close below its lower boundary
Calculation: Bearish FVG retest = price touches an active bearish gap formed when the current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; count only its first retest when first-touch mode is enabled, and remove the gap after a close above its upper boundary
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish or bearish FVG retest = price touches an active gap formed from completed higher-timeframe bars, using the configured gap-size minimum; count only its first retest and remove the gap after a chart-bar close through its far boundary
Calculation: Bullish and bearish bias = close above and EMA rising, or close below and EMA falling, respectively, on each of the two bias timeframes; use completed bars in confirmed mode and forming bars otherwise
Long: bullish absorption OR (signal moving average is touched by the low and close > it, and its slope over the configured lookback is upward when slope filtering is enabled, OR bullish chart-FVG retest, OR bullish higher-timeframe FVG retest, OR bullish absorption; at least the configured minimum number of enabled triggers agree; bullish trigger count ≥ configured minimum and > bearish trigger count; rank of score ≥ configured minimum grade, where score = bullish trigger count + 1 if close > VWAP + 1 if both timeframe biases are bullish; satisfy any enabled VWAP, bias-alignment, and signal-window filters; confirmed bar; ATR available; and bars since prior signal > cooldown)
Short: bearish absorption OR (signal moving average is touched by the high and close < it, and its slope over the configured lookback is downward when slope filtering is enabled, OR bearish chart-FVG retest, OR bearish higher-timeframe FVG retest, OR bearish absorption; at least the configured minimum number of enabled triggers agree; bearish trigger count ≥ configured minimum and > bullish trigger count; rank of score ≥ configured minimum grade, where score = bearish trigger count + 1 if close < VWAP + 1 if both timeframe biases are bearish; satisfy any enabled VWAP, bias-alignment, and signal-window filters; confirmed bar; ATR available; and bars since prior signal > cooldown)
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier × target-R multiple
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier × target-R multiple
