Calculation: EMA ribbon = EMAs of close with progressively longer lookbacks; the pullback uses the selected shallow, middle, or deep ribbon EMA
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Bias EMA slope = current bias EMA minus its value at the longer slope lookback; rising means slope exceeds a positive price-scaled flat threshold, falling means it is below the negative threshold
Calculation: Ribbon spread = absolute difference between the shortest and longest ribbon EMAs
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = bullish when its fast EMA exceeds its slow EMA, the fast EMA exceeds its value at the slope lookback, and their spread is at least the higher-timeframe ATR times a minimum; bearish when all inequalities reverse; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bar on each of the two configured higher timeframes.
Calculation: Recent bullish pullback = within the pullback lookback, a bar had open above the shortest ribbon EMA and low at or below the selected pullback EMA, with partial bullish ribbon alignment and close at or above the fifth ribbon EMA maintained through the current bar
Calculation: Recent bearish pullback = within the pullback lookback, a bar had open below the shortest ribbon EMA and high at or above the selected pullback EMA, with partial bearish ribbon alignment and close at or below the fifth ribbon EMA maintained through the current bar
Long: Recent bullish pullback AND close > shortest ribbon EMA AND close > open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR × minimum body threshold AND (bias filter disabled OR close > bias EMA) AND (bias slope filter disabled OR bias EMA slope is not falling) AND (ribbon alignment filter disabled OR first five ribbon EMAs are strictly descending in length order) AND (ranging filter disabled OR ribbon spread >= ATR × minimum spread) AND (MTF filter disabled OR MTF disabled OR both higher-timeframe states are not bearish in no-opposition mode OR both are bullish in strict-alignment mode)
Short: Recent bearish pullback AND close < shortest ribbon EMA AND close < open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR × minimum body threshold AND (bias filter disabled OR close < bias EMA) AND (bias slope filter disabled OR bias EMA slope is not rising) AND (ribbon alignment filter disabled OR first five ribbon EMAs are strictly ascending in length order) AND (ranging filter disabled OR ribbon spread >= ATR × minimum spread) AND (MTF filter disabled OR MTF disabled OR both higher-timeframe states are not bullish in no-opposition mode OR both are bearish in strict-alignment mode)
