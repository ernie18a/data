Calculation: ATR = average true range; compare the shorter-lookback ATR with the longer-lookback ATR for compression.
Calculation: Structure trend = bullish when a bar closes above the latest confirmed pivot high after the prior close was at or below it; bearish when it closes below the latest confirmed pivot low after the prior close was at or above it; otherwise retain the prior trend. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: HTF bias = for each of three successively higher timeframes, compare the shorter-period EMA with the longer-period EMA on that timeframe’s bar still forming; sum the three directions. On daily-or-higher charts, use weekly, monthly, and quarterly bars instead. Bullish bias is a sum ≥ 2; bearish bias is ≤ −2; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Range position = (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) over a lookback; discount is < 0.382, premium is > 0.618, and golden zone is 0.618–0.786 inclusive.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high-low zone of the candle before an opposite-colored candle at the configured impulse offset, when the current candle’s body exceeds ATR times the displacement threshold and closes up. Bearish order block is the converse. Cap zone height at the configured ATR multiple. An active block is invalidated by a close beyond its far edge; within the reclaim window, a close beyond its near edge reactivates it once and adds the configured score boost.
Calculation: Order-block score = min(10, 2 + 2 if the opposite-side liquidity sweep is valid + 1 each for matching fair-value gap, matching structure trend, aligned HTF bias when enabled, volume spike, matching discount/premium zone, strong displacement, matching HTF candle color when enabled, matching active HTF order-block zone, and matching volume delta). A bullish sweep is a low below the latest pivot low with close back above it; bearish sweep is a high above the latest pivot high with close back below it; each requires displacement. A fair-value gap is a three-bar gap in the matching direction meeting the configured minimum size, and in strict mode also requires displacement. Volume spike is volume above its moving average times a threshold. Volume delta is the moving average of normalized close location within the bar range; bullish and bearish thresholds have equal magnitude and opposite signs. HTF candle color uses the first higher-timeframe candle still forming. HTF order-block zones use the selected higher timeframe’s displacement candle and preceding opposite candle; use daily bars when chart timeframe is at least that timeframe, or weekly bars on daily-or-higher charts.

Short: Valid bearish sweep.

Short: Premium range position AND bearish structure trend AND range high > range low.
Long: Golden-zone range position AND bullish structure trend AND close < prior close AND range high > range low.
Short: Golden-zone range position AND bearish structure trend AND close > prior close AND range high > range low.
Long: Active bullish order block with score ≥ minimum AND session allowed (or chart is daily-or-higher, or session filter disabled) AND HTF bias aligned (or neutral, or filter disabled) AND not compression when compression blocking is enabled AND low ≤ block midpoint AND close > block bottom AND bullish structure trend.
Short: Active bearish order block with score ≥ minimum AND session allowed (or chart is daily-or-higher, or session filter disabled) AND HTF bias aligned (or neutral, or filter disabled) AND not compression when compression blocking is enabled AND high ≥ block midpoint AND close < block top AND bearish structure trend.
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + initial risk × first reward multiple OR low ≤ initial stop.
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − initial risk × first reward multiple OR high ≥ initial stop.
