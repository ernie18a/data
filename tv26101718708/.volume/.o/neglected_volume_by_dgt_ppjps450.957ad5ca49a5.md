Calculation: OBV momentum oscillator = least-squares linear regression of ((OBV minus OBV from the analysis lookback ago) / analysis lookback / simple moving average of volume over the analysis lookback × golden ratio) over a smoothing lookback; OBV cumulatively adds volume when close rises, subtracts volume when close falls, and is unchanged when close is unchanged
Long: a confirmed oscillator pivot low has a higher value than the preceding oscillator pivot low AND the corresponding price low is lower than the preceding price low OR a confirmed oscillator pivot low has a lower value than the preceding oscillator pivot low AND the corresponding price low is higher than the preceding price low; in either case, the preceding pivot is within the configured minimum and maximum pivot age
Short: a confirmed oscillator pivot high has a lower value than the preceding oscillator pivot high AND the corresponding price high is higher than the preceding price high OR a confirmed oscillator pivot high has a higher value than the preceding oscillator pivot high AND the corresponding price high is lower than the preceding price high; in either case, the preceding pivot is within the configured minimum and maximum pivot age
