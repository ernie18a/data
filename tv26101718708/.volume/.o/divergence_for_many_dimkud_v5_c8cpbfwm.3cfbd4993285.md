Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = For any enabled built-in indicator, compare its current value (or previous-bar value when confirmation is required) with its value at one of the most recent confirmed price pivot lows: the indicator must be higher and price must be lower. A pivot uses close or low, according to the selected pivot source, and requires the configured number of bars on both sides. Check pivots newest first, up to the configured pivot-count and bar-distance limits, requiring a distance greater than five bars; every intervening indicator and close must be at or above the straight line between endpoints. With confirmation enabled, require the current indicator or close to be higher than its previous value.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = For any enabled built-in indicator, compare its current value (or previous-bar value when confirmation is required) with its value at one of the most recent confirmed price pivot highs: the indicator must be lower and price must be higher. A pivot uses close or high, according to the selected pivot source, and requires the configured number of bars on both sides. Check pivots newest first, up to the configured pivot-count and bar-distance limits, requiring a distance greater than five bars; every intervening indicator and close must be at or below the straight line between endpoints. With confirmation enabled, require the current indicator or close to be lower than its previous value.
Long: A regular bullish divergence exists AND, when enabled, the selected channel condition is met AND fast ATR is less than or greater than slow ATR as selected AND fast smoothed volume is less than or greater than slow smoothed volume as selected AND Supertrend direction matches the selected normal or opposite direction AND each enabled RSI, CCI, and momentum filter is within its configured long range on its specified timeframe using the last completed timeframe bar AND, when enabled, MFI is within its configured long range on its specified timeframe using the bar still forming AND, when enabled, MFI is rising or falling over its configured lookback on that timeframe using the bar still forming.
Short: A regular bearish divergence exists AND, when enabled, the selected channel condition is met AND fast ATR is less than or greater than slow ATR as selected AND fast smoothed volume is less than or greater than slow smoothed volume as selected AND Supertrend direction matches the selected normal or opposite direction AND each enabled RSI, CCI, and momentum filter is within its configured short range on its specified timeframe using the last completed timeframe bar AND, when enabled, MFI is within its configured short range on its specified timeframe using the bar still forming AND, when enabled, MFI is rising or falling over its configured lookback on that timeframe using the bar still forming.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price times (1 + take-profit fraction) OR low <= entry price times (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR, when opposite-signal exits are enabled, a regular bearish divergence exists and all enabled short-entry filters pass.
Exit: Short, low <= entry price times (1 - take-profit fraction) OR high >= entry price times (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR, when opposite-signal exits are enabled, a regular bullish divergence exists and all enabled long-entry filters pass.
