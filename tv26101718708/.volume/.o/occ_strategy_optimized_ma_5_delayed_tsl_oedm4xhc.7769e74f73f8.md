Calculation: Open and close moving averages = the selected moving-average type and lookback applied to open and close; when alternate resolution is enabled, calculate on the higher-timeframe bar still forming, otherwise on the current bar. Apply the configured delay to the source bars first.
Calculation: ADX = smooth the ratio of the absolute difference between smoothed positive and negative directional indicators to their sum, then multiply by 100; directional indicators use smoothed directional movement divided by smoothed true range.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price × (1 − fixed stop fraction) until the high exceeds entry price × (1 + activation fraction); afterward use the greater of high × (1 − trail fraction) and the prior stop.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price × (1 + fixed stop fraction) until the low falls below entry price × (1 − activation fraction); afterward use the lesser of low × (1 + trail fraction) and the prior stop.
Long: Close moving average crosses above open moving average AND long trades are enabled AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX is above the configured threshold; when adaptive threshold is enabled, use the median ADX over the configured lookback).
Short: Close moving average crosses below open moving average AND short trades are enabled AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX is above the configured threshold; when adaptive threshold is enabled, use the median ADX over the configured lookback).
Exit: long, close moving average crosses below open moving average when long-only mode is selected OR low ≤ long stop when trailing stop is enabled OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) when take profit is enabled.
Exit: short, close moving average crosses above open moving average when short-only mode is selected OR high ≥ short stop when trailing stop is enabled OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit fraction) when take profit is enabled.
