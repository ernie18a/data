Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute price change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar price changes over that lookback; the slow lookback is longer than the fast lookback.
Calculation: Trend regime = slow efficiency ratio clears its trend floor and ADX clears its trend floor; strong trend additionally requires the higher efficiency-ratio floor, the higher ADX floor, and rising ADX. Chaos = volatility-cluster intensity is above its high-percentile threshold and a recent true-range shock occurred. Reversion = variance ratio is below 1 (or unavailable), ADX is below its trend floor, and slow efficiency ratio is below its trend floor.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = +1 when the latest available bar on that timeframe closes above its EMA and the EMA is non-falling; −1 when it closes below its EMA and the EMA is non-rising; otherwise 0. Use two higher timeframes, both longer than the chart timeframe, with lookahead off.
Calculation: Major-level proximity = close is within a volatility-scaled tolerance of any session, weekly, or monthly VWAP; volume-profile POC, VAH, or VAL; swing-anchored VWAP using the primary, longer, or longest swing lookback; or prior-day or prior-week high or low. Use the current session/week/month VWAP and the last completed day/week bars for prior-period levels.
Calculation: Swing flip = the swing trend changes direction when price makes a new high or low over the swing lookback; bullish means the trend changes to up, bearish means it changes to down. Grade each flip with the composite of level proximity grade, move from the pivot relative to ATR, volume relative to its moving average, recent lower-timeframe flip agreement, and pivot-to-pivot movement per bar relative to ATR. A flip qualifies when it is at a graded level or has a top-tier grade, its pivot move clears a minimum ATR multiple, and its grade clears the configured minimum tier. Lower-timeframe flips use two timeframes below the chart and count if they occurred within the recency window.
Calculation: Signed-volume flow direction = +1 when cumulative signed volume is at or above its EMA, otherwise −1. Estimate signed volume from lower-timeframe candle closes within their ranges; use the bulk-volume-classification estimate when lower-timeframe data is unavailable. Toxicity = the moving average of absolute buy/sell volume imbalance divided by average total volume; toxic means it clears its threshold.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe flow alignment = sum of the signs of robust z-scores of candle-geometry net buying pressure on the chart timeframe and two higher timeframes (3× and 5× chart duration); a rung is positive or negative only outside its neutral band. Use lookahead off for higher-timeframe values.
Calculation: Confirmed channel break = a confirmed close beyond the latest valid convex-hull edge of recent confirmed swing highs or lows, beyond an ATR-scaled buffer, with sufficient bar range, candle body, close location, edge slope, and edge touches. In fade mode, a resistance break that closes back below its line within the failure window is a short event; a support break that closes back above its line within the window is a long event. In chase mode, the original resistance/support break is the long/short event.
Long: confirmed bullish swing flip qualifies and is not killed by session, volatility, ADX acceleration, or chaos; regime is not strong trend; flip mode is not muted by the optional rolling performance gate; flow is not both toxic and opposing; multi-timeframe flow does not strongly oppose; and no directional signal occurred within the global cooldown.
Short: confirmed bearish swing flip qualifies and is not killed by session, volatility, ADX acceleration, or chaos; regime is not strong trend; flip mode is not muted by the optional rolling performance gate; flow is not both toxic and opposing; multi-timeframe flow does not strongly oppose; and no directional signal occurred within the global cooldown.
Long: a qualifying long channel-break event occurs; continuation mode is not muted by the optional rolling performance gate; regime is non-strong-trend in fade mode or trending in chase mode; in chase mode, higher-timeframe bias sum is positive when HTF agreement is required; tape is not toxic; price is not leading flow in chase mode; multi-timeframe flow does not strongly oppose; no opposing bearish swing flip occurred within the global cooldown; and no directional signal occurred within the global cooldown.
Short: a qualifying short channel-break event occurs; continuation mode is not muted by the optional rolling performance gate; regime is non-strong-trend in fade mode or trending in chase mode; in chase mode, higher-timeframe bias sum is negative when HTF agreement is required; tape is not toxic; price is not leading flow in chase mode; multi-timeframe flow does not strongly oppose; no opposing bullish swing flip occurred within the global cooldown; and no directional signal occurred within the global cooldown.
Exit: long, low <= active stop level OR high >= active second-target level
Exit: short, high >= active stop level OR low <= active second-target level
