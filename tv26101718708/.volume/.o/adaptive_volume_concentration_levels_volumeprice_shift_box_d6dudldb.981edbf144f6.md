Calculation: Shift score = sensitivity × (bullish score − bearish score), where the bullish score adds the component weight for each true condition: current session VWAP > its prior-bar value; OBV > its moving average; A/D line > its moving average; volume > its moving average AND close > open; close > its value lookback bars ago AND close > current session VWAP. The bearish score adds the corresponding component weight when VWAP is not rising, OBV is not above its moving average, A/D line is not above its moving average, volume > its moving average AND close < open, or close is not above its lookback-bars-ago value AND close < current session VWAP. Use the same lookback for the OBV, A/D, and volume moving averages and for price momentum; moving averages use the selected EMA or SMA method. A/D line = cumulative (((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) × volume), with a zero increment when high = low. Use current chart-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
