Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price ((high + low + close) / 3) over the rolling lookback or from the start of the selected anchor period through the current bar; use a time-weighted average if volume is unavailable
Calculation: VWAP deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of typical price over the same rolling lookback or anchor period, floored at a fraction of ATR; smooth it in rolling mode
Calculation: Normalized distance = (close − VWAP) / VWAP deviation
Calculation: VWAP slope = (current VWAP − VWAP from an earlier bar) / current VWAP deviation; the comparison lookback is longer than the band-signal lookback
Long: On a confirmed bar, the close reaches the lower band’s inner edge and normalized distance reaches the stretch threshold for the first time in that visit; OR the close returns above the lower band’s inner edge on an up candle within the bounce window after the last close inside that band, the visit’s deepest normalized distance below VWAP reaches the stretch threshold and is at least the required increment deeper than the prior long signal in that visit, and the minimum gap since the prior long signal is met; if the trend filter is enabled, VWAP slope is nonnegative. A visit ends when normalized distance returns within the visit-end threshold of VWAP; allow only one stretch tag per visit and further long signals only after the required additional depth.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the close reaches the upper band’s inner edge and normalized distance reaches the stretch threshold for the first time in that visit; OR the close returns below the upper band’s inner edge on a down candle within the bounce window after the last close inside that band, the visit’s deepest normalized distance above VWAP reaches the stretch threshold and is at least the required increment deeper than the prior short signal in that visit, and the minimum gap since the prior short signal is met; if the trend filter is enabled, VWAP slope is nonpositive. A visit ends when normalized distance returns within the visit-end threshold of VWAP; allow only one stretch tag per visit and further short signals only after the required additional depth.
