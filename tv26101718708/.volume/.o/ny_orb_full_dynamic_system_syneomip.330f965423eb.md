Calculation: ORB High = highest high during the configured opening-range window of the current trading day
Calculation: ORB Low = lowest low during the configured opening-range window of the current trading day
Calculation: ORB Range = ORB High − ORB Low
Calculation: Stop distance = fixed tick distance, ATR × stop multiplier, the smaller of ATR × stop multiplier and the configured maximum tick distance, or ORB Range × stop multiplier, according to the selected stop method
Calculation: Profit target distance = (ATR or ORB Range, according to the selected target type) × target multiplier
Long: Close > ORB High AND (confirmation disabled OR close at the configured earlier-bar offset ≤ ORB High) AND (second-break filter disabled OR price previously closed above ORB High, then returned to or below ORB High, and now closes above ORB High) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < overbought threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line > signal line) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > simple moving average) AND long trading is allowed AND current time is in the entry window AND current day is enabled AND daily entry lockout is inactive
Short: Close < ORB Low AND (confirmation disabled OR close at the configured earlier-bar offset ≥ ORB Low) AND (second-break filter disabled OR price previously closed below ORB Low, then returned to or above ORB Low, and now closes below ORB Low) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > oversold threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line < signal line) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < simple moving average) AND short trading is allowed AND current time is in the entry window AND current day is enabled AND daily entry lockout is inactive
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop (entry price − stop distance, or entry price after the breakeven trigger; include the configured trailing stop) OR high ≥ profit target (entry price + profit target distance, or entry price after the breakeven trigger) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND selected average is crossed downward AND (no target is set OR close < target)) OR daily total PnL ≥ daily profit limit OR daily total PnL ≤ daily loss limit OR current time ≥ configured hard exit time
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop (entry price + stop distance, or entry price after the breakeven trigger; include the configured trailing stop) OR low ≤ profit target (entry price − profit target distance, or entry price after the breakeven trigger) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND selected average is crossed upward AND (no target is set OR close > target)) OR daily total PnL ≥ daily profit limit OR daily total PnL ≤ daily loss limit OR current time ≥ configured hard exit time
