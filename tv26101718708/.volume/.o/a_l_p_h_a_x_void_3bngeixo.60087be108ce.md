Calculation: ATR = average true range on the current timeframe
Calculation: Bull FVG = a gap with bottom = high two bars ago and top = current low, where current low > high two bars ago, the prior bar's absolute body is at least the 14-bar average absolute body times a displacement threshold, and gap width is at least ATR times a minimum-gap threshold
Calculation: Bear FVG = a gap with bottom = current high and top = low two bars ago, where current high < low two bars ago, the prior bar's absolute body is at least the 14-bar average absolute body times a displacement threshold, and gap width is at least ATR times a minimum-gap threshold
Calculation: Active FVG = a qualifying gap not yet filled; a bull gap fills when a later low <= its bottom, and a bear gap fills when a later high >= its top; exclude gaps older than a maximum age
Calculation: Selected retest = the most recently formed active FVG of the relevant direction whose zone overlaps the current bar (low <= top AND high >= bottom), whose bar closes in the gap's direction (bull: close > open; bear: close < open), and whose close is at or beyond the gap midpoint in the rejection direction when midpoint rejection is enabled
Calculation: Bull EMA vote = fast EMA > slow EMA AND slow EMA > signal EMA AND close > fast EMA; bear EMA vote = fast EMA < slow EMA AND slow EMA < signal EMA AND close < fast EMA
Calculation: Bull momentum vote = linear regression of close minus the average of the recent high-low midpoint and recent SMA(close) is > 0 AND greater than its prior-bar value; bear momentum vote = the same value is < 0 AND less than its prior-bar value
Calculation: Bull volume vote = EMA of estimated bullish candle volume minus estimated bearish candle volume > 0 AND its 5-bar EMA > 0 AND OBV minus OBV from the configured lookback bars ago > 0; estimate bullish volume as volume * (close - low)/(high - low), or volume/2 when high = low, and bearish volume as volume minus bullish volume; bear vote requires all three inequalities reversed
Calculation: Bull VWAP vote = close > session-anchored VWAP; bear VWAP vote = close < session-anchored VWAP
Calculation: Bull ADX vote = ADX >= minimum AND +DI > -DI; bear ADX vote = ADX >= minimum AND -DI > +DI. Calculate +DI/-DI from smoothed directional movement divided by smoothed true range, then ADX as the smoothed DX; use the same lookback for directional movement and ADX smoothing
Calculation: Directional score = count of matching EMA, momentum, volume, VWAP, and ADX votes; each contributes one vote
Calculation: Bull stop = selected FVG bottom minus ATR times stop buffer (or signal-bar low minus buffer when FVG-edge stops are disabled); bear stop = selected FVG top plus ATR times stop buffer (or signal-bar high plus buffer when disabled)
Calculation: Bull targets = entry price + ATR times first-target and full-target multiples; bear targets = entry price - ATR times those multiples
Calculation: Bull trailing stop = initialize at bull stop base minus ATR times trail multiple, then each bar after entry update to the greater of its prior value and current low minus ATR times trail multiple; bear trailing stop = initialize at bear stop base plus ATR times trail multiple, then update to the lesser of its prior value and current high plus ATR times trail multiple
Long: qualifying bull FVG forms OR selected bull retest exists AND directional score >= minimum required votes AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session) AND bars since the last bull signal >= cooldown AND (EMA alignment disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA) AND these setup conditions were false on the prior bar
Short: qualifying bear FVG forms OR selected bear retest exists AND directional score >= minimum required votes AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session) AND bars since the last bear signal >= cooldown AND (EMA alignment disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA) AND these setup conditions were false on the prior bar
Exit: long, after at least one bar in the position, current low <= bull stop OR current high >= full target; OR after more than one bar in the position, current low <= bull trailing stop
Exit: long, current high >= first target (partial exit)
Exit: short, after at least one bar in the position, current high >= bear stop OR current low <= full target; OR after more than one bar in the position, current high >= bear trailing stop
Exit: short, current low <= first target (partial exit)
