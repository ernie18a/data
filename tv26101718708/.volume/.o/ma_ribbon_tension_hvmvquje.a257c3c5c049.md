Calculation: Six moving averages of the same price source and type, with strictly increasing lookbacks from fastest to slowest.
Calculation: Ribbon tension = the sum of the five adjacent MA gaps after multiplying each gap by the ribbon direction and flooring at zero, then dividing by the selected normalizer (ATR, price/100, or 1); ribbon direction is bullish when the fastest MA is at or above the slowest MA, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: A tension peak is a local maximum over equal left and right pivot lookbacks, retained only when its value is at least a configurable multiple of the 100-bar average tension at the peak.
Calculation: At each tension peak, record the highest high and lowest low over a window centered on that peak, with width equal to twice the pivot lookback plus one.
Calculation: Compare each qualifying peak with the previous qualifying peak of the same ribbon direction, only if both are in the same direction leg and their peak bars are within a configurable maximum distance. Confirm the current peak after its right-side pivot lookback.
Long: At a bearish-direction peak, current recorded lowest low < previous same-leg bearish peak's lowest low AND current tension peak < previous bearish tension peak OR at a bullish-direction peak, current recorded highest high < previous same-leg bullish peak's highest high AND current tension peak > previous bullish tension peak.
Short: At a bullish-direction peak, current recorded highest high > previous same-leg bullish peak's highest high AND current tension peak < previous bullish tension peak OR at a bearish-direction peak, current recorded lowest low > previous same-leg bearish peak's lowest low AND current tension peak > previous bearish tension peak.
