Calculation: Volume average = Simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Volume concentration = (volume / volume average) / max(abs(close − open) / simple moving average of abs(close − open) over the same lookback, 0.25).
Long: Confirmed-direction mode; an untriggered spike burst is active; current bar is later than its selected trigger candle and within the confirmation window measured from the latest qualifying spike; and, in Wick mode, high > trigger-candle high, in Body mode, max(open, close) > trigger-candle high, or in Close mode, close > trigger-candle high. A qualifying spike is a completed bar where volume >= volume average × the weak threshold; in graded mode the medium and strong thresholds also qualify. The first qualifying spike selects the trigger candle; later qualifying spikes extend the window and replace the trigger only if their volume concentration is greater. Do not reuse a bar that confirms a burst as the first spike of a new burst.
