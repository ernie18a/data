Calculation: RSI moving average = selected moving average of RSI
Calculation: Oversold setup = activates when RSI is at or below the lower threshold while inactive; stays active until RSI crosses above its moving average or reaches the upper threshold
Calculation: Overbought setup = activates when RSI is at or above the upper threshold while inactive; stays active until RSI crosses below its moving average or reaches the lower threshold
Long: RSI is at or below the lower threshold while the oversold setup is inactive OR the oversold setup is active and RSI crosses above its moving average
Short: RSI is at or above the upper threshold while the overbought setup is inactive OR the overbought setup is active and RSI crosses below its moving average
