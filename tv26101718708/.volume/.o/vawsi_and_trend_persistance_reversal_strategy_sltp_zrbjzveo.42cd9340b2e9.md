Calculation: Dynamic lookback = rounded-up dominant cycle length derived from current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close using a Hilbert-transform cycle estimator; use this same lookback for VAWSI, trend persistence, and normalized range change.
Calculation: VAWSI = RSI-style strength of Heikin-Ashi close changes, replacing each smoothed gain/loss average with a moving average weighted by volume and one-bar ATR relative to their lookback averages; normalize as 1 / max(2 × abs(VAWSI − 50), 20).
Calculation: Trend persistence = RSI-style strength of the change in the smoothed maximum of the cumulative changes in distance from the lookback high and low of Heikin-Ashi close; use the dynamic lookback and a separate smoothing length. Normalize as its reciprocal.
Calculation: Normalized range change = 100 × abs(change of the lookback RMA of max(regular high − regular low, abs(change of Heikin-Ashi close))) / Heikin-Ashi close.
Calculation: Adjustment = (weighted normalized VAWSI + weighted reciprocal trend persistence − weighted normalized range change) × combination multiplier; each weight is an independently applied multiplier.
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = in long regime, the rolling highest of min(max(rolling highest regular close × (1 − adjustment), minimum-stop close), regular open); in short regime, the rolling lowest of max(min(rolling lowest regular close × (1 + adjustment), minimum-target close), regular open). Rolling extrema span bars since the previous threshold cross, including the current bar; minimum-stop close and minimum-target close are regular close reduced and increased, respectively, by the same minimum percentage. Start in long regime; switch to short after a downward cross and to long after an upward cross.
Long: Current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close crosses above the adaptive threshold on a confirmed bar.
Short: Current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close crosses below the adaptive threshold on a confirmed bar.
