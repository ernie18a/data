Calculation: Planning side = forced long or short; otherwise long when fast EMA >= slow EMA and close >= slow EMA, short when fast EMA <= slow EMA and close <= slow EMA, else retain the prior side (initially compare close with the range midpoint).
Calculation: Range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the decision lookback; range span = max(range high - range low, ATR); range midpoint = range low + half the span.
Calculation: Latest confirmed pivot high/low = most recent pivot high/low using the configured pivot length on both sides; if unavailable, use range high/low.
Calculation: Decision level = closest of the side's structure shelf (long: pivot low; short: pivot high), balance edge (long: range low + configured fraction of span; short: range high - that fraction), and slow EMA, measured by absolute distance from close divided by ATR; ties favor structure shelf, then balance edge.
Calculation: Decision zone = decision level plus/minus configured ATR half-width; touched = current bar's high-low range overlaps the zone; near = touched or normalized distance to decision level <= configured reach.
Calculation: Reaction = touched, close on the planned side of decision level, candle direction agrees with side, body/range >= configured minimum, and side-facing wick/ATR >= configured minimum. Soft reaction = touched and close on the planned side. Volume supported = volume / its configured-length moving average >= configured minimum. Accepted reaction = reaction and not invalidated.
Calculation: Invalidation level = long: zone bottom minus configured ATR buffer; short: zone top plus that buffer. Invalidated = long close below level or short close above level. Risk distance = nonnegative side-adjusted distance from close to invalidation level divided by ATR.
Calculation: Target room = nonnegative side-adjusted distance from close to the nearer-side obstacle (long: max(range high, pivot high); short: min(range low, pivot low)), divided by ATR. Room open = target room >= configured minimum.
Calculation: Decision score = round(clamp(0.25*location + 0.23*reaction + 0.13*volume + 0.14*trend + 0.14*room + 0.07*risk + 0.04*volatility, 0, 100)), capped at 55 when not near, 70 when room is not open, 64 when risk distance is below its minimum, 62 when above its maximum, and 28 when invalidated. Location is 100 when touched, else a 0–100 decreasing linear score from zero at distance 0 to zero at 1.6 times reach. Reaction is 100 for accepted and volume-supported, 84 for accepted without volume support, 68 for soft reaction, 48 when near, otherwise 18. Volume is a 0–100 increasing linear score from ratio 0.60 to max(1.45 times its configured minimum, 1.10). Trend is 100 when fast/slow EMA and close agree with side, 70 when close is on the side of slow EMA, otherwise 38. Room is a 0–100 increasing linear score from 0.35 times minimum room to configured strong-room distance. Risk is a 0–100 peak score, rising linearly from zero at minimum risk distance to 100 at ideal distance, then falling linearly to zero at maximum distance. Volatility score is 72 when ATR / its longer-period ATR average <= 0.70, 100 through 1.35, 62 through 1.85, otherwise 34.
Calculation: Action ready = decision score >= configured action threshold and accepted reaction and room open and not invalidated.
Long: planning side is long AND action ready AND previous bar was not action ready.
Short: planning side is short AND action ready AND previous bar was not action ready.
