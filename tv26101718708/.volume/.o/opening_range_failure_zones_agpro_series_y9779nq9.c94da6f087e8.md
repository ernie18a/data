Calculation: Opening Range high/low = highest high / lowest low of the configured opening bars or session window; use only after the range is locked.
Calculation: Failure quality score = weighted score of probe depth relative to ATR, reclaim-bar wick proportion, close distance back inside the range relative to range span, bars to reclaim, number of closes outside the range, and optionally relative volume (current volume / its recent average).
Long: A downside probe extends below Opening Range low by more than the configured ATR buffer, then within the reclaim window a confirmed close is above Opening Range low plus the reclaim buffer, and failure quality score meets its minimum; only while the locked range is being tracked, within its tracking session and watch period, with no probe already active and no downside failure already printed for the range.
Short: An upside probe extends above Opening Range high by more than the configured ATR buffer, then within the reclaim window a confirmed close is below Opening Range high minus the reclaim buffer, and failure quality score meets its minimum; only while the locked range is being tracked, within its tracking session and watch period, with no probe already active and no upside failure already printed for the range.
