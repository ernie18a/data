Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured session-opening window; use only after the window completes. Opening-range midpoint = average of its high and low; width = high minus low.
Calculation: Long/short confidence = weighted score of trend, momentum, volume, volatility, structure, breakout quality, and liquidity; short trend and momentum scores are the inverses of the long scores. Trend combines fast/mid/slow EMA alignment, ADX direction, higher-timeframe close versus its EMA, and fast-EMA slope. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Momentum combines RSI level and change, MACD histogram level and expansion, close change, and candle body. Volume combines volume relative to its moving average, volume percentile, volume spike, and OBV versus its EMA. Volatility combines ATR versus its moving average, Bollinger Band width versus its moving average, and bar range versus its moving average. Structure combines session VWAP alignment, ATR-scaled room to the nearest resistance/support, close versus the latest confirmed swing high/low, and session-open gap context versus the prior session close and previous-day high/low. Breakout quality combines ATR-scaled distance beyond the range, candle body-to-range ratio, consecutive closes beyond the range, elapsed time since range lock, and retest status. Liquidity combines range sweeps and equal-high/equal-low context. Regime adjusts score weights and the minimum confidence threshold; breakout-day status further adjusts the threshold.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average. ATR percent = ATR / close. A retest is a return to within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the broken range edge that closes beyond it, counted once per return episode. A sweep is a wick beyond a range edge that closes back inside.
Calculation: Resistance/support = nearest level beyond price among previous-day high/low, latest confirmed swing high/low, and current-session premarket high/low. Previous-day levels use the last completed day; swing levels are used only after confirmation; premarket extrema use bars so far in the current day.
Calculation: Long/short KNN vote = winning fraction among the K nearest resolved past qualified breakouts by Lorentzian distance across direction-adjusted trend, momentum, volume, volatility, structure, and breakout-quality scores. Resolve outcomes stop-first; discard records touching neither the 1R target nor stop within the outcome window.
Long: Opening range locked AND (close > opening-range high OR (intrabar-touch mode AND high > opening-range high) OR (sweep-reversal enabled AND a prior low sweep occurred within the configured window AND close > opening-range midpoint)) AND long confidence >= effective threshold AND (non-conservative mode OR at least two consecutive closes above range high OR a retest) AND required retests reached AND long veto count <= risk-mode limit AND ATR percent >= minimum when ATR filter enabled AND relative volume >= minimum when volume filter enabled AND long trend score >= minimum when trend filter enabled AND close >= session VWAP when VWAP filter enabled AND NOT wide-range opening-range filter AND before entry cutoff AND outside blackout AND news switch off AND signal count below session maximum AND long not already used AND first-break direction permits long AND (KNN gate inactive OR vote >= minimum).
Short: Opening range locked AND (close < opening-range low OR (intrabar-touch mode AND low < opening-range low) OR (sweep-reversal enabled AND a prior high sweep occurred within the configured window AND close < opening-range midpoint)) AND short confidence >= effective threshold AND (non-conservative mode OR at least two consecutive closes below range low OR a retest) AND required retests reached AND short veto count <= risk-mode limit AND ATR percent >= minimum when ATR filter enabled AND relative volume >= minimum when volume filter enabled AND short trend score <= maximum when trend filter enabled AND close <= session VWAP when VWAP filter enabled AND NOT wide-range opening-range filter AND before entry cutoff AND outside blackout AND news switch off AND signal count below session maximum AND short not already used AND first-break direction permits short AND (KNN gate inactive OR vote >= minimum).
Long: close > opening-range high OR (close > opening-range high AND low <= opening-range high + ATR-scaled tolerance AND a prior close broke above the range).
Short: close < opening-range low OR (close < opening-range low AND high >= opening-range low - ATR-scaled tolerance AND a prior close broke below the range).
Long: high > opening-range high AND close <= opening-range high (sweep mark).
Short: low < opening-range low AND close >= opening-range low (sweep mark).
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= active target OR (dynamic EMA target mode AND fast EMA > entry price + ATR-scaled buffer AND close < fast EMA) OR (after TP1, dynamic EMA target mode AND fast EMA > prior target + ATR-scaled buffer AND close < fast EMA, repeated through final target) OR (within false-breakout window after entry and before TP1, close < opening-range high after prior close >= opening-range high or entry-bar touch closed inside) OR opposite short signal OR session rollover.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= active target OR (dynamic EMA target mode AND fast EMA < entry price - ATR-scaled buffer AND close > fast EMA) OR (after TP1, dynamic EMA target mode AND fast EMA < prior target - ATR-scaled buffer AND close > fast EMA, repeated through final target) OR (within false-breakout window after entry and before TP1, close > opening-range low after prior close <= opening-range low or entry-bar touch closed inside) OR opposite long signal OR session rollover.
