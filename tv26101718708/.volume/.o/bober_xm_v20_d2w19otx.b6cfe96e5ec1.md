Calculation: Keltner Channel = EMA(source, basis lookback) ± EMA(true range, ATR lookback) × multiplier; its middle line is the EMA.
Calculation: MLMA = moving-average-centered, recency-weighted RBF-kernel forecast of the source over a lookback window and forecast horizon; its bands are the forecast ± a moving average of absolute forecast error × band multiplier × ATR(14)/moving-average(ATR(14), 50), with that ratio clipped to [0.5, 1.5]. Correct the forecast by 0.2 × the prior source minus prior forecast. Bull trend state is set when trend source > upper band and forecast rises; bear state is set when trend source < lower band and forecast falls; otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Pivot SuperTrend = pivot center updated to (2 × prior center + latest confirmed pivot high or low)/3; bands are center ± ATR × factor, with SuperTrend direction switching bullish when close exceeds the prior lower trend band and bearish when close falls below the prior upper trend band. A tracked direction retains the latest nonzero direction.
Calculation: KEMAD bands = EMA of an order-stage Kalman-filtered price ± separate upper/lower multipliers × ATR; each Kalman stage updates its estimate from the price using gain = predicted covariance/(predicted covariance + measurement noise), with predicted covariance = prior covariance + process noise.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative sign(change(source)) × volume.
Calculation: ADX and +DI/−DI = Wilder-smoothed directional movement and true range; ADX is Wilder-smoothed absolute DI difference divided by DI sum.
Long: Selected channel and entry mode condition: Breakout = entry source crosses above upper band; Pullback = entry source crosses above middle line; Mean Reversion = entry source crosses above lower band. For MLMA, also require bullish MLMA trend; for either channel require tracked Pivot SuperTrend direction bullish. AND (entry source > WMA OR WMA confirmation disabled) AND (ADX momentum filter disabled OR (ADX > threshold AND (+DI > −DI OR DI check disabled))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < overbought level) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility / its moving average < threshold) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close < upper band) AND (volume filter disabled OR smoothed volume / volume moving average > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter disabled OR price source > upper KEMAD band) AND long entries enabled AND position is flat or short.
Short: Selected channel and entry mode condition: Breakout = entry source crosses below lower band; Pullback = entry source crosses below middle line; Mean Reversion = entry source crosses below upper band. For MLMA, also require bearish MLMA trend; for either channel require tracked Pivot SuperTrend direction bearish. AND (entry source < WMA OR WMA confirmation disabled) AND (ADX momentum filter disabled OR (ADX > threshold AND (−DI > +DI OR DI check disabled))) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > oversold level) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility / its moving average < threshold) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR close > lower band) AND (volume filter disabled OR smoothed volume / volume moving average > threshold) AND (KEMAD filter disabled OR price source < lower KEMAD band) AND short entries enabled AND position is flat or long.
Long: MLMA trend state changes to bullish OR KEMAD price source > upper band.
Short: MLMA trend state changes to bearish OR KEMAD price source < lower band.
Exit: Long, OBV crosses below its moving average AND (exit ADX confirmation disabled OR +DI < −DI), OR Pivot SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish, OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage), OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − stop price) × risk/reward ratio, OR an activated trailing stop is hit (after favorable movement reaches the activation distance, stop trails the highest price by the current channel-lower-band distance).
Exit: Short, OBV crosses above its moving average AND (exit ADX confirmation disabled OR +DI > −DI), OR Pivot SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish, OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage), OR low ≤ entry price − (stop price − entry price) × risk/reward ratio, OR an activated trailing stop is hit (after favorable movement reaches the activation distance, stop trails the lowest price by the current channel-upper-band distance).
Calculation: Use the chart bar for current-timeframe sources. Configured higher-timeframe sources use aggregated MTF1 or MTF2 bars, including the bar still forming. Within each calculation, use the configured source and lookbacks; when paired settings are distinct, long and short channels use their respective settings.
