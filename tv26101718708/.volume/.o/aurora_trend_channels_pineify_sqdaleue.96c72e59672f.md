Calculation: Basis = EMA of the volume-weighted moving average of close
Calculation: Upper band = EMA of the volume-weighted moving average of close plus ATR multiplied by a band multiplier
Calculation: Lower band = EMA of the volume-weighted moving average of close minus ATR multiplied by a band multiplier
Long: close > Basis OR close crosses above Upper band
Short: close <= Basis OR close crosses below Lower band
