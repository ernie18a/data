Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening window, finalized when that window ends; its midpoint and width are derived from those edges.
Calculation: Strength score = a 0–10 score from breakout candle body/range, range relative to ATR, volume relative to its 20-bar average, 3- and 5-bar close momentum, breakout penetration relative to ATR, RSI, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and close relative to session open; round to one decimal and clamp to 0–10.
Calculation: ADR = average daily high-low range over the configured lookback, using last completed daily bars.
Calculation: Risk = absolute distance from entry to the selected stop basis (ATR distance, opposite opening-range edge, or opening-range midpoint), floored and capped by configured ATR multiples; stop is entry minus risk for longs and plus risk for shorts.
Calculation: Targets = entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk multiplied by each configured target R multiple.
Long: opening range finalized AND within trade window AND flat AND daily trade cap not reached AND (close > opening-range high OR (wick trigger selected AND high > opening-range high)) AND NOT (close < opening-range low OR (wick trigger selected AND low < opening-range low)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / ADR is within configured bounds) AND strength score >= configured minimum AND ATR available.
Short: opening range finalized AND within trade window AND flat AND daily trade cap not reached AND (close < opening-range low OR (wick trigger selected AND low < opening-range low)) AND NOT (close > opening-range high OR (wick trigger selected AND high > opening-range high)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADR filter disabled OR opening-range width / ADR is within configured bounds) AND strength score >= configured minimum AND ATR available.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= third target OR trade window ends; after high >= first target without the stop being hit on that bar, active stop moves to entry.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= third target OR trade window ends; after low <= first target without the stop being hit on that bar, active stop moves to entry.
