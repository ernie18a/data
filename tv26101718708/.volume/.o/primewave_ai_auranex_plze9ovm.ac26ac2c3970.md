Calculation: Tenkan and Kijun = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over short and medium lookbacks, respectively; use either fixed lookbacks or ATR-adaptive lookbacks. Adaptive lookbacks scale their respective base lookbacks by the same volatility factor, are clamped by the same adaptation strength, and remain short < medium < long (the long lookback is used for Span B).
Calculation: Cloud top and bottom = higher and lower of Span A and Span B, each calculated from the current Tenkan/Kijun or long-lookback midpoint values and shifted back by the cloud displacement to align with the current bar.
Calculation: Thin cloud = current cloud thickness < 0.3 × its 50-bar simple moving average.
Calculation: Chikou confirmation = current close > close from the displacement lookback for bullish confirmation, or < it for bearish confirmation.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume > 1.2 × 20-bar simple moving average of volume; if volume is absent or zero, confirmation passes. Strong volume = volume > breakout-volume multiplier × the same average; if volume is absent or zero, it passes.
Calculation: Momentum = clamp to −100…100 the sum of 40 × Tenkan–Kijun spread / ATR(14), 100 × the change in that normalized spread over 3 bars, and 30 × the 5-bar change in (Span A slope − Span B slope) / ATR(14). Smooth with a 5-bar exponential moving average. Bullish acceleration means smoothed momentum > 0 and rising; bearish acceleration means it is < 0 and falling.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confluence score = sum of: 20 points if close is above cloud top / below cloud bottom, 8 if inside cloud, otherwise 0; 15 if Tenkan is above / below Kijun; 15 if bullish / bearish Chikou confirmation; 10 if the 3-bar Span A change exceeds / does not exceed the 3-bar Span B change; 8 if cloud is not thin, otherwise 2; 10 if volume confirmation passes; for bullish / bearish momentum, 12 if smoothed momentum is > 20 / < −20, 6 if > 0 / < 0, otherwise 0; and 10 if momentum is bullish / bearish accelerating. Cap at 100.
Calculation: Stop loss = selected method: for long, cloud bottom or Kijun minus an ATR buffer, or entry price minus 2 × ATR(14); for short, cloud top or Kijun plus an ATR buffer, or entry price plus 2 × ATR(14). Risk distance = greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and 0.5 × ATR(14). Take-profit 3 = entry price plus / minus risk distance × the selected third risk multiple.
Long: Tenkan crosses above Kijun AND cloud is not thin AND volume confirmation passes AND bullish Chikou confirmation holds AND bullish confluence score meets the selected minimum; OR close crosses above cloud top from at or below the prior bar's cloud top AND strong volume passes; OR, 2–10 bars after an upward cloud breakout, low touches or falls below cloud top AND close finishes above cloud top.
Short: Tenkan crosses below Kijun AND cloud is not thin AND volume confirmation passes AND bearish Chikou confirmation holds AND bearish confluence score meets the selected minimum; OR close crosses below cloud bottom from at or above the prior bar's cloud bottom AND strong volume passes; OR, 2–10 bars after a downward cloud breakout, high touches or rises above cloud bottom AND close finishes below cloud bottom.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop loss OR high ≥ take-profit 3.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop loss OR low ≤ take-profit 3.
