Calculation: EMA bias = Long when the bar close is above both the fast and slow EMAs plus the configured ATR buffer; Short when below both EMAs minus the buffer; otherwise Neutral. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback. Calculate separately on daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, and configured mini-timeframe bars, using the last completed bar of each timeframe.
Calculation: Previous-day midpoint = (previous completed daily bar high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low during the configured cash opening interval) / 2; use only after that interval is complete, and reset each cash day.
Long: Daily EMA bias is Long AND 4-hour EMA bias is Long AND current close is above the selected previous-day level (previous-day midpoint, previous-day close, or both) AND 1-hour EMA bias is Long AND current close is above session VWAP AND current close is above the completed opening-range midpoint; OR daily EMA bias is Long AND 4-hour EMA bias is Long AND current close is above the selected previous-day level; OR 1-hour EMA bias is Long AND current close is above session VWAP AND current close is above the completed opening-range midpoint; OR mini-timeframe EMA bias is Long AND (mini-timeframe close is above session VWAP, if that filter is active) AND the resulting mini bias has just changed to Long from a non-Long state.
Short: Daily EMA bias is Short AND 4-hour EMA bias is Short AND current close is below the selected previous-day level (previous-day midpoint, previous-day close, or both) AND 1-hour EMA bias is Short AND current close is below session VWAP AND current close is below the completed opening-range midpoint; OR daily EMA bias is Short AND 4-hour EMA bias is Short AND current close is below the selected previous-day level; OR 1-hour EMA bias is Short AND current close is below session VWAP AND current close is below the completed opening-range midpoint; OR mini-timeframe EMA bias is Short AND (mini-timeframe close is below session VWAP, if that filter is active) AND the resulting mini bias has just changed to Short from a non-Short state.
