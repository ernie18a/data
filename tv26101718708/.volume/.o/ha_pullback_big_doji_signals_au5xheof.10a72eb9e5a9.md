Calculation: Heikin-Ashi candles = standard Heikin-Ashi OHLC calculated on the backtest timeframe
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of Heikin-Ashi close
Calculation: Candle range = Heikin-Ashi high − Heikin-Ashi low
Calculation: Body = absolute value of Heikin-Ashi close − Heikin-Ashi open
Calculation: Upper wick = Heikin-Ashi high − max(Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close)
Calculation: Lower wick = min(Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close) − Heikin-Ashi low
Calculation: Consecutive long pullback count = number of immediately preceding bars where Heikin-Ashi close > EMA, Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open, and upper wick ≤ candle range × flat-wick tolerance
Calculation: Consecutive short pullback count = number of immediately preceding bars where Heikin-Ashi close < EMA, Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open, and lower wick ≤ candle range × flat-wick tolerance
Calculation: Doji = body ≤ candle range × maximum-body percentage AND upper wick ≥ candle range × minimum-wick percentage AND lower wick ≥ candle range × minimum-wick percentage
Calculation: Strong volume = current volume ≥ the maximum volume over the configured number of immediately preceding bars; this filter is optional
Long: Heikin-Ashi close > EMA AND consecutive long pullback count ≥ configured minimum AND Doji AND ((consecutive long pullback count = 2 AND current candle range > each of the previous 2 candle ranges) OR (consecutive long pullback count ≥ 3 AND current candle range > each of the previous 3 candle ranges)) AND (volume filter disabled OR Strong volume) AND (doji-color preference disabled OR Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open)
Short: Heikin-Ashi close < EMA AND consecutive short pullback count ≥ configured minimum AND Doji AND ((consecutive short pullback count = 2 AND current candle range > each of the previous 2 candle ranges) OR (consecutive short pullback count ≥ 3 AND current candle range > each of the previous 3 candle ranges)) AND (volume filter disabled OR Strong volume) AND (doji-color preference disabled OR Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open)
