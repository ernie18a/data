Calculation: Fast Tilson T3 = apply three times GD(x,n,a), where GD(x,n,a) = EMA(x,n) × (1+a) − EMA(EMA(x,n),n) × a; use a shorter lookback than the slow Tilson T3.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2; first value = (open + close) / 2. Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4.
Calculation: Configured moving average = the selected average of the configured source and lookback; HEMA is EMA of Heikin-Ashi open. For the two averages used together, use the shorter and longer configured lookbacks.
Calculation: TSI = 100 × EMA(EMA(close − previous close, short lookback), long lookback) / EMA(EMA(abs(close − previous close), short lookback), long lookback); signal = EMA(TSI, signal lookback), with the long lookback longer than the short lookback.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Parabolic SAR = standard parabolic stop-and-reverse value using configured start, increment, and maximum.
Long: Fast Tilson T3 ≥ slow Tilson T3 AND close > slow Tilson T3 OR fast Tilson T3 ≤ slow Tilson T3 AND close > slow Tilson T3 OR close crosses above slow Tilson T3 OR (TSI crosses above its signal AND RSI > 50) OR (RSI crosses above 50 AND TSI > its signal) OR (TSI crosses above its signal AND RSI crosses above 50) OR (TSI < its signal AND TSI > its previous value) OR close > both configured moving averages OR close crosses above either configured moving average while above the other OR close crosses above both configured moving averages OR RSI crosses above 50 OR RSI crosses above 70 OR RSI crosses above 30 OR close crosses above Parabolic SAR
Short: Fast Tilson T3 ≤ slow Tilson T3 AND close < slow Tilson T3 OR fast Tilson T3 ≥ slow Tilson T3 AND close < slow Tilson T3 OR close crosses below slow Tilson T3 OR (TSI crosses below its signal AND RSI < 50) OR (RSI crosses below 50 AND TSI < its signal) OR (TSI crosses below its signal AND RSI crosses below 50) OR (TSI > its signal AND TSI < its previous value) OR close < both configured moving averages OR close crosses below either configured moving average while below the other OR close crosses below both configured moving averages OR RSI crosses below 50 OR RSI crosses below 70 OR RSI crosses below 30 OR close crosses below Parabolic SAR
