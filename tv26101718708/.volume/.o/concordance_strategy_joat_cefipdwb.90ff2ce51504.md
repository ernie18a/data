Calculation: Local baseline state = fast EMA vs. medium EMA vs. slow EMA, with fast, medium, and slow lookbacks in increasing order.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe baseline state = the same three EMAs on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Directional trend score = weighted directional contributions from local and higher-timeframe baseline ordering, fast and medium EMA slopes, directional-index ordering when ADX exceeds its gate, and volatility expansion direction relative to the slow EMA when bands are not squeezed.
Calculation: Bull regime = directional trend score exceeds its positive gate and ADX exceeds its regime gate; bear regime = score is below its negative gate and ADX exceeds its regime gate.
Calculation: Soft bull state = positive trend score, bullish local baseline ordering, and higher-timeframe fast EMA at least the higher-timeframe medium EMA; soft bear state = negative score, bearish local ordering, and higher-timeframe fast EMA at most the higher-timeframe medium EMA.
Calculation: Daily and weekly liquidity levels = highs and lows of the last completed daily and weekly bars.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = current low pierces below a prior daily or enabled weekly low by an ATR buffer and current close reclaims that level; bearish sweep = current high pierces above a prior daily or enabled weekly high by the buffer and close falls back below it.
Calculation: Structure levels = most recently confirmed local pivot high and pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity stack = a bullish sweep occurred within the sweep-confirmation window, close crosses above the latest structure high, and (displacement is not required or bullish candle body exceeds its longer-lookback average by a multiplier); bearish liquidity stack = corresponding bearish sweep, cross below structure low, and optional bearish displacement.
Calculation: Local and higher-timeframe retracement levels = Fibonacci levels from swing extremes; higher-timeframe extremes use the last completed higher-timeframe bars. Local swing direction is determined by whether the latest pivot low is later than the latest pivot high; higher-timeframe direction uses the corresponding extreme ordering.
Calculation: OTE interaction = close lies between the local 61.8% and 78.6% retracement levels, with an upswing for long and downswing for short.
Calculation: HTF agreement = local and higher-timeframe swing directions match, unless HTF agreement is disabled.
Calculation: Retracement confluence count = number of matching local and higher-timeframe Fibonacci levels within a percentage tolerance of close.
Calculation: Pressure composite = normalized cumulative money-flow volume, RSI momentum bias, and normalized candle delta combined with fixed weights; bullish pressure is positive and rising, bearish pressure is negative and falling.
Calculation: Sigma channel re-entry = close crosses above the lower regression channel with positive regression slope for long, or crosses below the upper channel with negative slope for short; select the highest-R² qualifying regression among the candidate lookbacks, requiring minimum fit quality.
Calculation: Participation = relative volume versus its volume average; bullish or bearish ledger confirmation additionally requires volume intensity versus its longer lookback above a gate, and positive or negative normalized candle delta respectively.
Calculation: Long and short scores = weighted sums of the respective regime and liquidity stack, enabled OTE requirement, HTF agreement, retracement confluence, directional pressure, directional sigma re-entry, directional ledger confirmation, and relative-volume gate.
Calculation: Soft-entry score gate = the entry score gate reduced by a parameter, subject to a minimum gate.
Calculation: Initial long risk = close minus a stop below the lowest of current low, prior daily low, prior weekly low, and latest structure low, with ATR structure buffer and ATR stop distance; initial short risk is the mirrored distance above the highest corresponding highs.
Calculation: Live long stop = maximum of entry price minus initial risk, entry price when break-even is triggered, and close minus ATR trail distance; live short stop is the mirrored minimum using close plus ATR trail distance.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus or minus initial risk multiplied by the first and second reward-to-risk parameters.
Long: confirmed bar AND session filter passes AND long score meets entry gate AND bull regime AND HTF agreement OR confirmed bar AND session filter passes AND long score meets soft-entry gate AND soft bull state AND bullish pressure AND bullish sigma re-entry AND (retracement confluence count >= 1 OR bullish ledger confirmation); additionally, position is not already long and initial long risk exceeds minimum tick size.
Short: confirmed bar AND session filter passes AND short score meets entry gate AND bear regime AND HTF agreement OR confirmed bar AND session filter passes AND short score meets soft-entry gate AND soft bear state AND bearish pressure AND bearish sigma re-entry AND (retracement confluence count >= 1 OR bearish ledger confirmation); additionally, position is not already short and initial short risk exceeds minimum tick size.
Exit: Long, low <= live long stop OR high >= first profit target OR high >= second profit target OR trend score <= 0 AND pressure composite < 0 OR enabled time exit and bars since entry >= time limit.
Exit: Short, high >= live short stop OR low <= first profit target OR low <= second profit target OR trend score >= 0 AND pressure composite > 0 OR enabled time exit and bars since entry >= time limit.
