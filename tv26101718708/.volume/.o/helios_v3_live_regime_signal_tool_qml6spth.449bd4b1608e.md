Calculation: Extended score = sum of six votes: MACD posture (+1 when MACD line and signal line are both above zero, −1 when both are below zero, otherwise 0); close-average versus open-average (each over the same lookback; +1 if close-average is higher, −1 if lower, otherwise 0); close versus its longer-lookback prior close (+1 if higher, −1 if lower, otherwise 0); close versus a longer-term close SMA (+1 if higher, otherwise −1); OBV versus its SMA (+1 if higher, −1 if lower, otherwise 0); and relative-strength ratio versus its SMA (+1 if higher, otherwise −1; omit this vote when relative strength is disabled). All bars and indicators use the chart timeframe; the benchmark close uses the same timeframe.
Calculation: Realized volatility = standard deviation of log close-to-close returns over a longer lookback, annualized by the square root of the number of trading days per year and expressed as a percentage; optionally EMA-smoothed.
Calculation: Extension z-score = (close − long-term close SMA) / standard deviation of close over that SMA's lookback.
Long: Flat AND extended score ≥ 4 on at least the required consecutive bars AND realized volatility ≤ volatility cap AND extension z-score ≤ buy extension limit.
Exit: Long, extended score ≤ −4 on at least the required consecutive bars AND realized volatility ≤ volatility cap AND extension z-score ≥ sell extension limit.
