Calculation: Opening Range High = highest high of 5-minute bars from 9:30 a.m. New York time until the configured opening-range end; use after that period ends
Calculation: Opening Range Low = lowest low of the same 5-minute bars; use after that period ends
Calculation: Session VWAP = session cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, using current chart bars
Long: flat AND no entry has been taken that day AND before the force-flat time AND month and weekday pass their respective enabled filters AND (opening-range size <= configured maximum when its filter is enabled) AND (close > session VWAP when its filter is enabled) AND ((enabled 5-minute breakout: current 5-minute close > Opening Range High, after the range ends, and no earlier 5-minute breakout that day) OR (enabled 5-minute retest: a 5-minute close has exceeded Opening Range High after the range ended, and current 5-minute low <= Opening Range High + configured retest buffer AND current 5-minute close >= Opening Range High, with no earlier retest that day) OR (enabled 15-minute breakout: current 15-minute close > Opening Range High, after the range ends))
Short: flat AND no entry has been taken that day AND before the force-flat time AND month and weekday pass their respective enabled filters AND (opening-range size <= configured maximum when its filter is enabled) AND (close < session VWAP when its filter is enabled) AND ((enabled 5-minute breakout: current 5-minute close < Opening Range Low, after the range ends, and no earlier 5-minute breakout that day) OR (enabled 5-minute retest: a 5-minute close has fallen below Opening Range Low after the range ended, and current 5-minute high >= Opening Range Low - configured retest buffer AND current 5-minute close <= Opening Range Low, with no earlier retest that day) OR (enabled 15-minute breakout: current 15-minute close < Opening Range Low, after the range ends))
Exit: long, low <= Opening Range Low - configured stop buffer OR high >= entry price + (entry price - (Opening Range Low - configured stop buffer)) OR New York time >= configured force-flat time
Exit: short, high >= Opening Range High + configured stop buffer OR low <= entry price - ((Opening Range High + configured stop buffer) - entry price) OR New York time >= configured force-flat time
