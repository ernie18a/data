Calculation: Candle body midpoint = (open + close) / 2
Calculation: Upper ATR bands = VWMA + ATR multiplied by each of three increasingly large multipliers
Calculation: Lower ATR bands = VWMA - ATR multiplied by each of three increasingly large multipliers
Long: Supertrend is in an uptrend AND (Supertrend < VWMA OR candle body midpoint > VWMA OR candle body midpoint > any upper ATR band)
Short: Supertrend is in a downtrend AND (Supertrend > VWMA OR candle body midpoint < VWMA OR candle body midpoint < any lower ATR band)
