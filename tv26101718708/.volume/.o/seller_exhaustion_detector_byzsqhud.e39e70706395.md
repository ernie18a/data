Calculation: Session cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on up or unchanged bars and negative volume on down bars, reset at each session start.
Calculation: Fast volume delta = 5-bar SMA of signed volume, where signed volume is positive on up or unchanged bars and negative on down bars.
Calculation: Down volume = volume on down bars and zero otherwise; compare its 5-bar SMA with its longer-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Near swing low = current low is no more than a parameterized ATR multiple above the rolling lowest low over the swing-low lookback.
Calculation: Selling climax = down bar with volume greater than a parameterized multiple of its volume SMA and low at or below the rolling lowest low over the swing-low lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed lower-wick rejection = the prior bar was near a swing low, had a lower wick greater than a parameterized multiple of its body (using minimum tick as the minimum body), and closed in the upper half of its range; current close is above prior high.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low while RSI at the respective pivot bars is higher; use the same pivot left/right lookback for price and RSI.
Calculation: Bullish cumulative-volume-delta divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low while session cumulative volume delta at the respective pivot bars is higher; use the same pivot left/right lookback as for price and RSI.
Calculation: Higher pivot low = a newly confirmed pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Sell-volume dry-up = near swing low, down-volume fast SMA is below a parameterized fraction of its longer-lookback SMA, and the longer-lookback SMA is positive.
Calculation: Absorption = near swing low, volume exceeds a parameterized multiple of its volume SMA, bar range is below a parameterized ATR multiple, and close is in the upper half of the range.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep and reclaim = current low pierces an active, unexpired confirmed pivot-low pool and closes above its level, or within the reclaim window after a recorded pierce, close crosses above that level from a prior close at or below it. Track at most a parameterized number of newest pools; expire pools by age and remove them after a close below or a sweep.
Calculation: Exhaustion score = 3 if a liquidity sweep and reclaim occurred within the watch window, plus 3 if bullish cumulative-volume-delta divergence occurred within the watch window, plus 2 if bullish RSI divergence occurred within the watch window, plus 2 if sell-volume dry-up is true, plus 2 if absorption occurred within the watch window, plus 2 if a higher pivot low occurred within the watch window, plus 1 if a selling climax occurred within the watch window, plus 1 if confirmed lower-wick rejection occurred within the watch window; otherwise each contribution is zero.
Long: Selling climax.
Long: Liquidity sweep and reclaim.
Long: Exhaustion score is at least its threshold AND near swing low AND (the rolling swing low has held for the required number of bars OR a sweep and reclaim occurred within the reclaim window) AND close is above the 9-period EMA AND the 9-period EMA is not falling by more than a parameterized ATR amount over 5 bars AND fast volume delta is positive AND no exhaustion signal occurred within the prior watch window.
Long: After an exhaustion signal, within twice the watch window, close is above the highest high of the prior 5 bars AND close is above the 9-period EMA AND fast volume delta is positive; signal only when this condition becomes true after being false.
Exit: Long, current low is below the swing low stored at the most recent exhaustion signal.
