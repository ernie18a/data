Calculation: Fast EMA and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback; trend EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than both.
Calculation: MACD line and signal line = MACD of close and its signal line.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Bollinger bands = bands around a moving average of close.
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus the stop-loss percentage; after close reaches the trailing activation percentage above entry, raise it to the greater of its prior value and close minus the trailing percentage of close.
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus the stop-loss percentage; after close reaches the trailing activation percentage below entry, lower it to the lesser of its prior value and close plus the trailing percentage of close.
Calculation: Long target = entry price plus the take-profit percentage; short target = entry price minus the take-profit percentage.
Long: (close > trend EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) AND volume > volume average AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND RSI < upper boundary AND (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR MACD line crosses above signal line OR (prior RSI < oversold boundary AND RSI > oversold boundary AND RSI > prior RSI) OR (close > highest high of the prior 20 bars AND volume > volume average) OR (close > fast EMA AND low <= fast EMA × 1.01 AND bullish candle body > 60% of high-low range) OR (prior close <= current lower Bollinger band AND close > current lower Bollinger band AND bullish candle body > 60% of high-low range)).
Short: (close < trend EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA) AND volume > volume average AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND RSI > lower boundary AND (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR MACD line crosses below signal line OR (prior RSI > overbought boundary AND RSI < overbought boundary AND RSI < prior RSI) OR (close < lowest low of the prior 20 bars AND volume > volume average) OR (close < fast EMA AND high >= fast EMA × 0.99 AND bearish candle body > 60% of high-low range) OR (prior close >= current upper Bollinger band AND close < current upper Bollinger band AND bearish candle body > 60% of high-low range)).
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR bars since position opened >= maximum hold bars.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR bars since position opened >= maximum hold bars.
