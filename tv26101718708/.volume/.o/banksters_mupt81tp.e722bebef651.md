Calculation: MFI = 100 − 100 / (1 + positive money-flow sum / negative money-flow sum), where money flow is volume × typical price ((high + low + close) / 3), assigned positive or negative by the typical-price change; sums use a configurable lookback selected for the chart timeframe.
Calculation: MFI peak trackers = peak price and peak MFI; reset each to the current close or MFI when current MFI is the highest in its lookback, otherwise carry forward its prior value, then keep the greater of that value and the current close or MFI.
Calculation: MFI trough trackers = trough price and trough MFI; reset each to the current close or MFI when current MFI is the lowest in its lookback, otherwise carry forward its prior value, then keep the lesser of that value and the current close or MFI.
Long: close crosses above a confirmed swing high (the high exceeds highs over the configured left and right lookbacks) AND close > open AND (high − low) > 0 AND (close − open) / (high − low) × 100 ≥ the configured minimum body percentage OR prior-bar trough-price tracker < its value two bars earlier AND prior-bar trough-MFI tracker < prior-bar MFI AND current MFI ≥ prior-bar MFI AND (current or prior smoothed stochastic is below its oversold threshold) OR trough-MFI tracker = its value two bars earlier AND its value two bars earlier ≠ its value three bars earlier AND (current or prior smoothed stochastic is below its oversold threshold) OR an active bullish breakout level has survived its false-break check, the configured retest delay has elapsed, and low ≤ level + its configured percentage buffer.
Short: close crosses below a confirmed swing low (the low is below lows over the configured left and right lookbacks) AND close < open AND (high − low) > 0 AND (open − close) / (high − low) × 100 ≥ the configured minimum body percentage OR prior-bar peak-price tracker > its value two bars earlier AND prior-bar peak-MFI tracker > prior-bar MFI AND current MFI ≤ prior-bar MFI AND (current or prior smoothed stochastic is above its overbought threshold) OR peak-MFI tracker = its value two bars earlier AND its value two bars earlier ≠ its value three bars earlier AND (current or prior smoothed stochastic is above its overbought threshold) OR an active bearish breakout level has survived its false-break check, the configured retest delay has elapsed, and high ≥ level − its configured percentage buffer.
