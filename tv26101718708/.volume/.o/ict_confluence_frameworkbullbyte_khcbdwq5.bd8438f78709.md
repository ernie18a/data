Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the three bars on each side; use only after all three right-side bars have closed. Track the latest confirmed high and low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep-reclaim = after price first trades below/above the latest confirmed pivot low/high, a later bar within the configurable reclaim window closes above/below that pivot and its low/high still touches or exceeds it. Each pivot can be swept once until a new pivot forms.
Calculation: Stopping-volume bar = volume at or above its rolling percentile threshold, at a local extreme versus the previous half-swing-lookback bars, with the close in the configured rejection portion of the bar range. Bullish rejection closes near the high at a low; bearish rejection closes near the low at a high.
Calculation: Compression-expansion = the previous bar has the smallest range in the preceding configurable compression window; the current bar's range is at least the configured multiple of current ATR. Direction is current close versus open.
Calculation: Order-block impulse = current candle body is at least the configured multiple of current ATR. A bullish impulse after a bearish candle stores that previous candle's high and low as the bullish zone; a bearish impulse after a bullish candle stores it as the bearish zone. Keep the most recently stored zone of each side until mitigated.
Calculation: Order-block mitigation = bullish: current low is within the stored bullish zone and close is above its top; bearish: current high is within the stored bearish zone and close is below its bottom. Clear the zone after mitigation.
Calculation: Break of structure = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high from at or below it, or crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low from at or above it.
Calculation: Pattern count = number of distinct directional pattern types (sweep-reclaim, stopping volume, compression-expansion, break of structure, order-block mitigation) that fired on the current or recent bars within a short timeframe-scaled confluence window.
Calculation: Opposing residual score = current opposing pattern score when positive; otherwise the prior residual score decreases by one per bar, floored at zero. Reset both sides' residual scores on trade closure. Pattern scores weight each active pattern by its rolling follow-through reliability (or equally when adaptive weighting is off), reduce break-of-structure weight in a range and expansion weight in a volatile regime, and reduce the whole counter-trend side's score in a strong trend.
Calculation: Required pattern count = configured minimum, increased by one in the active volatility phase and capped at five; active phase is current ATR percentile rank in its rolling distribution at or above the active threshold. Quiet and normal phases keep the configured minimum.
Calculation: Entry levels = entry price is signal-bar close. Long invalidation is entry minus a configurable ATR multiple; short invalidation is entry plus that distance. Reference level 2 is two-sided from entry by the configured risk-reward multiple of that distance.
Long: A confirmed pivot low is first swept when low < pivot low.
Long: Bullish sweep-reclaim OR bullish stopping-volume bar OR bullish compression-expansion OR bullish break of structure OR bullish order-block mitigation.
Long: At least the required number of distinct bullish patterns are active in the confluence window AND bullish pattern count exceeds bearish pattern count AND bearish opposing residual score is below the required count AND no position is active AND cooldown has ended AND the bar is warmed up; if quiet mode is enabled, at least three bullish patterns must be active.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is first swept when high > pivot high.
Short: Bearish sweep-reclaim OR bearish stopping-volume bar OR bearish compression-expansion OR bearish break of structure OR bearish order-block mitigation.
Short: At least the required number of distinct bearish patterns are active in the confluence window AND bearish pattern count exceeds bullish pattern count AND bullish opposing residual score is below the required count AND no position is active AND cooldown has ended AND the bar is warmed up; if quiet mode is enabled, at least three bearish patterns must be active.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= invalidation level OR high >= reference level 2.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= invalidation level OR low <= reference level 2. If both levels are touched on one bar, resolve as invalidation.
