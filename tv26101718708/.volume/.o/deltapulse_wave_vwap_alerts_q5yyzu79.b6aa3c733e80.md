Calculation: Delta wave = EMA of 100 × (EMA(volume when close > open, long lookback) − EMA(volume when close < open, long lookback)) / EMA(volume when volume > 0, otherwise 1, same long lookback), smoothed by a shorter-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = At a bar where the delta wave is below the lower threshold scaled by 0.2 and forms a local trough (the prior bar's wave is lower than the waves one bar before and after it), the prior bar's low is below the last stored trough low, its wave is above the last stored trough wave, and the prior trough is at least the minimum gap bars after the previously stored trough. Update the stored trough low, wave, and index at every such local trough. Initialize stored values and index to zero.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = At a bar where the delta wave is above the upper threshold scaled by 0.2 and forms a local peak (the prior bar's wave is higher than the waves one bar before and after it), the prior bar's high is above the last stored peak high, its wave is below the last stored peak wave, and the prior peak is at least the minimum gap bars after the previously stored peak. Update the stored peak high, wave, and index at every such local peak. Initialize stored values and index to zero.
Calculation: Session VWAP = Cumulative volume-weighted average of hlc3 (the average of high, low, and close) from the start of the current day through the current bar; reset at each new day.
Calculation: Session VWAP 1 SD bands = Session VWAP plus or minus the square root of the nonnegative difference between the current day's cumulative volume-weighted mean of hlc3 squared and Session VWAP squared.
Long: Bullish divergence OR (Bullish divergence AND close > Session VWAP AND close < upper Session VWAP 1 SD band)
Short: Bearish divergence OR (Bearish divergence AND close < Session VWAP AND close > lower Session VWAP 1 SD band)
