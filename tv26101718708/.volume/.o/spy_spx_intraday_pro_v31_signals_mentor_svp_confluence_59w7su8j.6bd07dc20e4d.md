Calculation: 15-minute bias = close above or below VWAP calculated from the current forming 15-minute bar
Calculation: 5-minute structure = close above VWAP and fast EMA above slow EMA, or close below VWAP and fast EMA below slow EMA, calculated from the current forming 5-minute bar
Calculation: Candle body fraction = absolute(close − open) / (high − low)
Calculation: Pullback continuation = price touched the fast EMA, slow EMA, or VWAP within a shorter lookback, then closed on the directional side of VWAP and the fast EMA, within an ATR-based extension limit, with a directional candle
Calculation: Confluence score = configurable weighted sum of aligned 15-minute bias, aligned 5-minute structure, strong volume, ADX threshold, directional RSI range, close beyond slow EMA, favorable or unfavorable session value area location, entry path quality, session timing, and directional volume
Long: (VWAP reclaim within a configurable bar window OR close crossed above the locked opening range high OR Pullback continuation OR momentum entry with close above VWAP, fast EMA above slow EMA, and close above fast EMA OR counter-VWAP bounce) AND bullish 15-minute bias (or not bearish when strict bias is disabled) AND 1-minute bullish EMA alignment (or close above VWAP when relaxed) AND close above VWAP and fast EMA (except relaxed opening range breakout) AND bullish candle closing above prior close with minimum body fraction (except bounce, which requires bullish candle and minimum body fraction) AND volume meets its configured minimum (when required) AND close is not within the configured VWAP chop distance (when enabled) AND 5-minute structure is not bearish AND ADX meets its configured entry threshold (when enabled) AND RSI is within its configured entry bound (when enabled) AND session is not avoided AND (bounce OR confluence score meets its threshold when the confluence gate is enabled)
Short: (VWAP rejection within a configurable bar window OR close crossed below the locked opening range low OR Pullback continuation OR momentum entry with close below VWAP, fast EMA below slow EMA, and close below fast EMA OR counter-VWAP rejection) AND bearish 15-minute bias (or not bullish when strict bias is disabled) AND 1-minute bearish EMA alignment (or close below VWAP when relaxed) AND close below VWAP and fast EMA (except relaxed opening range breakdown) AND bearish candle closing below prior close with minimum body fraction (except rejection, which requires bearish candle and minimum body fraction) AND volume meets its configured minimum (when required) AND close is not within the configured VWAP chop distance (when enabled) AND 5-minute structure is not bullish AND ADX meets its configured entry threshold (when enabled) AND RSI is within its configured entry bound (when enabled) AND session is not avoided AND (rejection OR confluence score meets its threshold when the confluence gate is enabled)
Long: (15-minute bullish bias AND close above VWAP AND session not avoided) OR (below VWAP, RSI below its upper bound and rising over the longer comparison interval, fast EMA rising over that interval, higher low, bullish candle with minimum body fraction, and volume meets its strong threshold)
Short: (15-minute bearish bias AND close below VWAP AND session not avoided) OR (above VWAP, RSI above its lower bound and falling over the longer comparison interval, fast EMA falling over that interval, lower high, bearish candle with minimum body fraction, and volume meets its strong threshold)
Exit: long, confirmed close at or below the greater of the trailing stop and the rolling swing low, OR after the minimum holding period when at least two are true: close below entry-bar low, close below fast EMA, close below VWAP (except bounce entries)
Exit: short, confirmed close at or above the lesser of the trailing stop and the rolling swing high, OR after the minimum holding period when at least two are true: close above entry-bar high, close above fast EMA, close above VWAP (except rejection entries)
