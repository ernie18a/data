Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the preceding and following swingLen bars; available after the following swingLen bars complete.
Calculation: Previous pivot high/low = most recently confirmed pivot high/low.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity grab = current low < previous pivot low AND close > previous pivot low; bearish liquidity grab = current high > previous pivot high AND close < previous pivot high.
Calculation: Advanced bullish grab = bullish liquidity grab AND previous pivot low − low > 1.1 × abs(open − close) AND abs(open − close) < 0.5 × (high − low) AND volume > 1.4 × 20-bar volume SMA; bearish grab mirrors this using high − previous pivot high.
Calculation: Effective bullish candle pattern = any of: (1) bullish engulfing candle (close > open, prior close < prior open, close > prior open, open < prior close) at a confirmed pivot low or when close[1] < close; (2) hammer (range > 3 × body, close and open each above 60% of range from low) when prior bar range > ATR(10); (3) morning star (bearish first candle, middle body ≤ 30% of its range, middle low ≤ first low, bullish current candle closing at least halfway through its body, current low > middle low) at a confirmed pivot low or when close[1] < close; or (4) piercing pattern (prior bullish candle, current bearish candle, current open < prior close, current close > prior open and < prior close) when close[1] < close. Effective bearish candle pattern = any of: (1) bearish engulfing candle (close < open, prior close > prior open, close < prior open, open > prior close) at a confirmed pivot high or when close[1] > close; (2) shooting star (range > 3 × body, close and open each above 60% of range from high) at a confirmed pivot high or when close[1] > close; (3) hanging man (range > 3 × body, close and open each above 60% of range from low) at a confirmed pivot high or when close[1] > close; or (4) evening star (bullish first candle, middle body ≤ 30% of its range, middle high ≥ first high, bearish current candle closing at least halfway through its body, current high < middle high) at a confirmed pivot high or when close[1] > close. A doji is abs(close − open) ≤ 10% of range; high-volume doji is a doji with volume > 1.5 × 20-bar volume SMA.
Calculation: Bullish iceberg = volume > 1.5 × volLength-bar volume SMA AND range > 0 AND abs(close − open) / range < 0.18 AND close > open; bearish iceberg is the same with close < open.
Calculation: POV volume confirmation = volume > povPct × cumulative volume for the current day.
Calculation: Session/cumulative VWAP = session VWAP when session mode is selected; otherwise cumulative(close × volume) / cumulative volume. TWAP = twapLen-bar SMA of close. Higher-timeframe VWAP = VWAP on the selected higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Bullish trend confluence = close > higher-timeframe VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > TWAP; bearish trend confluence = close < all three.
Calculation: Equal-high/low pool = midpoint of two consecutive confirmed pivot highs/lows whose price difference is at most tolerance (ATR(atrLenTol) × atrMultTol, or close × pctTol / 100) and whose pivot bars are at least minBarsGap apart. A pool is mitigated after a close above its level plus tolerance (high pool) or below its level minus tolerance (low pool). Nearest pool = unmitigated pool of that type closest to close.
Calculation: Bullish smart-money pool reversal = nearest low pool exists AND abs(close − pool level) < 0.5 × ATR(3) AND close > open AND open − low > 2 × abs(open − close) AND volume > 1.4 × 20-bar volume SMA; bearish reversal mirrors this at the nearest high pool, with close < open and high − close > 2 × body.
Calculation: Buy score = count of bullish trend confluence, advanced bullish grab, POV volume confirmation, bullish iceberg, and effective bullish engulfing. Sell score counts their bearish counterparts. Lite buy = buy score > sell score OR (scores equal AND buy score ≥ 1 AND bullish directional count > bearish directional count); lite sell mirrors this. Directional counts exclude POV volume confirmation. A lite trigger occurs when its lite condition is true and was false on the prior bar.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution line = cumulative(((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) × volume), using zero for a zero-range bar. It rises when greater than its prior value and falls when less.
Calculation: Signal bias = BUY when more buy than sell signals occur among the latest two stored signals; SELL when more sell than buy; otherwise neutral. Store +1 on a buy lite trigger with rising accumulation/distribution line, and −1 on a sell lite trigger with falling accumulation/distribution line.
Long: advanced bullish grab OR effective bullish candle pattern OR bullish smart-money pool reversal OR bullish iceberg OR (bar close confirmed AND advanced bullish grab AND effective bullish engulfing AND POV volume confirmation AND bullish iceberg AND bullish trend confluence) OR (buy score > sell score OR (buy score = sell score AND buy score ≥ 1 AND bullish directional count > bearish directional count) AND accumulation/distribution line rises AND this is a new lite-buy trigger AND signal bias changes to BUY).
Short: advanced bearish grab OR effective bearish candle pattern OR bearish smart-money pool reversal OR bearish iceberg OR (bar close confirmed AND advanced bearish grab AND effective bearish engulfing AND POV volume confirmation AND bearish iceberg AND bearish trend confluence) OR (sell score > buy score OR (sell score = buy score AND sell score ≥ 1 AND bearish directional count > bullish directional count) AND accumulation/distribution line falls AND this is a new lite-sell trigger AND signal bias changes to SELL).
Exit: long, signal bias changes to SELL or neutral.
Exit: short, signal bias changes to BUY or neutral.
