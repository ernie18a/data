Calculation: Selected moving average = user-selected ALMA, EMA, VWMA, Hull moving average, or TSI-based price series of close
Calculation: Moving-average slope = selected moving average now minus its value slope-lookback bars ago
Long: ADX is at or above its threshold and, when required, rising; cooldown has passed; and (moving-average slope crosses above zero or re-entry is enabled, cooldown has passed, slope is positive and greater than its value at the last exit, and slope has risen for the required consecutive bars) OR close crosses above the selected moving average and the same ADX, cooldown, and re-entry conditions apply
Short: ADX is at or above its threshold and, when required, rising; cooldown has passed; and (moving-average slope crosses below zero or re-entry is enabled, cooldown has passed, slope is negative and less than its value at the last exit, and slope has fallen for the required consecutive bars) OR close crosses below the selected moving average and the same ADX, cooldown, and re-entry conditions apply
Exit: long, close crosses below the selected moving average OR close crosses below VWMA OR slope is positive and has fallen for the required consecutive bars OR slope crosses below zero OR low reaches the ratcheting stop below the entry price OR high reaches the take-profit above the entry price OR, after price reaches the trailing-start level above entry, low reaches the trailing stop below the highest high since entry
Exit: short, close crosses above the selected moving average OR close crosses above VWMA OR slope is negative and has risen for the required consecutive bars OR slope crosses above zero OR high reaches the ratcheting stop above the entry price OR low reaches the take-profit below the entry price OR, after price reaches the trailing-start level below entry, high reaches the trailing stop above the lowest low since entry
