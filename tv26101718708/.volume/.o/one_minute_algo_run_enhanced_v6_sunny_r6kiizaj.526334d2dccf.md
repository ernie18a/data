Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of the opening-range window; lock from its first completed one-minute bar
Calculation: Breakout body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Opening bias = long when the shorter and longer EMAs are above their respective longer-period EMAs on the last completed bars of both the faster and slower higher timeframes, and range-lock price is at or above opening VWAP; short when both EMA comparisons are below and range-lock price is at or below opening VWAP
Calculation: Range-based target 1/2 = opening-range high + opening-range width × long target multiplier, and opening-range low − opening-range width × short target multiplier
Long: opening bias is long OR directional outlook score is at least the long threshold OR (opening range is locked AND current bar is in the trade window AND opening range width meets its minimum AND ADX meets its effective minimum AND close is outside the daily POC balance zone AND breakout body ratio meets its effective minimum AND (opening bias is not required OR opening bias is long) AND close crosses above opening-range high AND daily long-signal frequency limit permits a signal)
Short: opening bias is short OR directional outlook score is at most the short threshold OR (opening range is locked AND current bar is in the trade window AND opening range width meets its minimum AND ADX meets its effective minimum AND close is outside the daily POC balance zone AND breakout body ratio meets its effective minimum AND (opening bias is not required OR opening bias is short) AND close crosses below opening-range low AND daily short-signal frequency limit permits a signal)
Exit: long, high reaches target 2 OR (target 1 was reached and low reaches entry price) OR (target 1 was not reached and low reaches opening-range midpoint)
Exit: short, low reaches target 2 OR (target 1 was reached and high reaches entry price) OR (target 1 was not reached and high reaches opening-range midpoint)
