Calculation: Money Flow = ATR-normalized close-to-close return × relative volume (volume / its simple moving average), smoothed with an EMA, divided by 2.5× its rolling standard deviation, scaled by 50, and clipped to [-50, 50].
Calculation: Hyperwave = the same impulse normalization as Money Flow, using a shorter EMA length than Money Flow.
Calculation: Fade edge = Q(state, fade) − Q(state, wait). State is the current Money Flow regime (above the positive overflow threshold, below the negative threshold, or neither) and whether Hyperwave is rising. Initialize wait Q values to 0 and fade Q values to 0.5. On each confirmed bar, update the previous state/action Q value with Q ← Q + α × (reward + γ × max Q(current state) − Q), where the reward for a previous fade is −close-to-close return / ATR for positive overflow, +close-to-close return / ATR for negative overflow, or −flat penalty × absolute return / ATR without overflow; reward for wait is 0. Choose fade when its Q value exceeds wait, otherwise wait. Enable decisions after the warmup period.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed Money Flow pivot low is higher than the preceding Money Flow pivot low while its corresponding price low is lower than the preceding price low. Bearish divergence = a confirmed Money Flow pivot high is lower than the preceding Money Flow pivot high while its corresponding price high is higher than the preceding price high.
Calculation: Divergence reliability = the Beta posterior mean (successes + 1) / (successes + failures + 2), maintained separately for bullish/bearish divergences and whether Money Flow at the pivot was beyond the corresponding overflow threshold. After the outcome horizon from the pivot, count a bullish divergence as a success if close exceeds the pivot-bar close, and a bearish divergence as a success if close is below it.
Long: confirmed bearish-flow exhaustion (previous bar Money Flow below the negative overflow threshold and current Money Flow rising) AND fade edge > 0 AND warmup complete OR bullish divergence AND its reliability ≥ reliability threshold.
Short: confirmed bullish-flow exhaustion (previous bar Money Flow above the positive overflow threshold and current Money Flow falling) AND fade edge > 0 AND warmup complete OR bearish divergence AND its reliability ≥ reliability threshold.
