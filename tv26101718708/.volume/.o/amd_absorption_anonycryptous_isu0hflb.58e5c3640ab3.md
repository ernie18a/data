Calculation: Accumulation range = highest high and lowest low over a rolling lookback; its width as a percentage of its lowest low must be at or below a threshold.
Long: In an enabled session, after an accumulation range forms, within the following search window, a bar has low < accumulation low; then, before the window expires and absent a new accumulation, current low − high two bars ago > ATR × minimum gap threshold AND current close > accumulation low AND (absorption filter disabled OR the immediately preceding bar's volume > its rolling average volume × threshold AND its absolute close−open divided by high−low ≤ threshold).
Short: In an enabled session, after an accumulation range forms, within the following search window, a bar has high > accumulation high; then, before the window expires and absent a new accumulation, low two bars ago − current high > ATR × minimum gap threshold AND current close < accumulation high AND (absorption filter disabled OR the immediately preceding bar's volume > its rolling average volume × threshold AND its absolute close−open divided by high−low ≤ threshold).
