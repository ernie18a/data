Calculation: ORB High/Low = highest high / lowest low during each configured opening-range window, finalized at the close of its last range bar
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; each gap must meet the configured minimum ATR size and, if required, remain outside the ORB
Long: After ORB completion, high >= ORB High + long buffer (touch); OR close > ORB High + long buffer (close); OR first-break continuation is confirmed by the configured rule: touch, close outside, or retest of ORB High with close > ORB High; OR a bullish ORB retest meets the configured confirmation (boundary touch, close > ORB High, or low <= ORB High AND close > open AND close > ORB High); OR price touches the configured entry level of a valid bullish FVG after an upside breakout; OR low < ORB Low - short buffer AND close > ORB Low (low sweep), optionally requiring close > open; OR the first downside break closes > ORB Low within the failure window (failed low); OR the first upside break fails with close < ORB High, then first-break continuation/reversal rules apply. Apply enabled direction, session, time, weekday, entry-capacity, ORB-range, EMA, VWAP, previous-close, relative-volume, ADX, and maximum-candle-size filters.
Short: After ORB completion, low <= ORB Low - short buffer (touch); OR close < ORB Low - short buffer (close); OR first-break continuation is confirmed by the configured rule: touch, close outside, or retest of ORB Low with close < ORB Low; OR a bearish ORB retest meets the configured confirmation (boundary touch, close < ORB Low, or high >= ORB Low AND close < open AND close < ORB Low); OR price touches the configured entry level of a valid bearish FVG after a downside breakout; OR high > ORB High + long buffer AND close < ORB High (high sweep), optionally requiring close < open; OR the first upside break closes < ORB High within the failure window (failed high); OR the first downside break fails with close > ORB Low, then first-break continuation/reversal rules apply. Apply enabled direction, session, time, weekday, entry-capacity, ORB-range, EMA, VWAP, previous-close, relative-volume, ADX, and maximum-candle-size filters.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= active target OR configured time exit reached OR configured force-close time reached
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= active target OR configured time exit reached OR configured force-close time reached
