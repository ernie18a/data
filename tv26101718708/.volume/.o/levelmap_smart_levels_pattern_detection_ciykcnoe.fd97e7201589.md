Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle closes above its open, previous candle closes below its open, current close exceeds previous open, current open is below previous close, and current body is larger than previous body
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle closes below its open, previous candle closes above its open, current close is below previous open, current open exceeds previous close, and current body is larger than previous body
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = candle has a positive range, lower wick is at least the configured multiple of body size, upper wick is no larger than body size, and candle closes above its open
Calculation: Bearish pin bar = candle has a positive range, upper wick is at least the configured multiple of body size, lower wick is no larger than body size, and candle closes below its open
Calculation: Key-level proximity = candle low or high is within the configured percentage tolerance of the corresponding low or high from the previous completed daily bar, previous completed 4-hour bar, current forming weekly range, or current forming or most recently completed Asia or Europe session range
Calculation: Liquidity sweep low = current low is below the lowest low of the configured number of preceding bars and current close is above that low; liquidity sweep high = current high is above the highest high of the configured number of preceding bars and current close is below that high
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a local high/low confirmed after the same number of bars on each side; compare each new pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type to classify higher high, lower high, higher low, or lower low
Calculation: Bullish structure = most recent swing classification is higher high or higher low; bearish structure = most recent swing classification is lower high or lower low
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding swing low while RSI at the new pivot is higher than RSI at the preceding pivot; bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding swing high while RSI at the new pivot is lower than RSI at the preceding pivot
Calculation: Double bottom/top = a confirmed swing low/high is within the configured percentage tolerance of the preceding swing low/high
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative typical price times volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each new day; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Long: Bullish engulfing OR liquidity sweep low OR (bullish pin bar AND within key-level proximity of a low AND RSI below its configured threshold AND a confirmed swing low or a liquidity sweep low on the current or either of the prior two bars) OR double bottom OR bullish RSI divergence OR (close above the shorter EMA AND shorter EMA above the longer EMA AND close above daily VWAP AND bullish structure) OR (higher-low classification AND the preceding bullish-trend conditions)
Short: Bearish engulfing OR liquidity sweep high OR (bearish pin bar AND within key-level proximity of a high AND RSI above its configured threshold AND a confirmed swing high or a liquidity sweep high on the current or either of the prior two bars) OR double top OR bearish RSI divergence OR (close below the shorter EMA AND shorter EMA below the longer EMA AND close below daily VWAP AND bearish structure) OR (lower-high classification AND the preceding bearish-trend conditions)
