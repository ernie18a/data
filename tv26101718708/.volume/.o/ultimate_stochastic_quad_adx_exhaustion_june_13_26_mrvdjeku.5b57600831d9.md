Calculation: Active smoothed stochastic = SMA of the close’s stochastic over a lookback, then smoothed over a second lookback; the main signal uses one selected stochastic, and continuation crosses use another selected stochastic.
Calculation: Visited oversold = active smoothed stochastic reached or fell below the oversold threshold since the prior qualifying long signal or reset on reaching the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Visited overbought = active smoothed stochastic reached or rose above the overbought threshold since the prior qualifying short signal or reset on reaching the oversold threshold.
Calculation: ADX threshold reached = ADX reached or exceeded its threshold while the active smoothed stochastic was at or beyond either extreme; reset on an opposite-extreme reset or a qualifying long or short signal.
Calculation: Bull mode = set by a qualifying long signal and cleared by a qualifying short signal. Bear mode = set by a qualifying short signal and cleared by a qualifying long signal.
Long: Active smoothed stochastic crosses above 50 AND visited oversold AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX threshold reached AND current ADX < prior-bar ADX) AND (candle filter disabled OR close > open) AND (structure filter disabled OR low > the prior bar’s lowest low over the pivot lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > 0.8 × its 50-bar SMA) OR bull mode AND continuation smoothed stochastic crosses above 50.
Short: Active smoothed stochastic crosses below 50 AND visited overbought AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX threshold reached AND current ADX < prior-bar ADX) AND (candle filter disabled OR close < open) AND (structure filter disabled OR high < the prior bar’s highest high over the pivot lookback) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its SMA) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR > 0.8 × its 50-bar SMA) OR bear mode AND continuation smoothed stochastic crosses below 50.
