Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band
Calculation: Long profit level = selected percentage target from average entry price, Donchian upper channel, or the greater of those levels
Calculation: Short profit level = selected percentage target from average entry price, Donchian lower channel, or the lesser of those levels
Calculation: Long stop level = selected percentage stop from average entry price, ATR trailing stop, or the greater of those levels
Calculation: Short stop level = selected percentage stop from average entry price, ATR trailing stop, or the lesser of those levels
Long: fast EMA > slow EMA AND slow EMA > prior slow EMA AND +DI > −DI AND ADX > threshold AND Parabolic SAR < close AND MACD histogram > 0 AND volume > volume SMA × volume factor
Long: (position is flat or short AND (long conditions above OR (ADX is not bearish AND prior low ≥ prior lower Bollinger Band AND low < lower Bollinger Band AND long EMA conditions above AND Bollinger Band width > new-position minimum))) OR (position is long AND close < previous entry price × (1 − better-price threshold) AND (long conditions above OR (ADX is not bearish AND prior low ≥ prior lower Bollinger Band AND low < lower Bollinger Band AND long EMA conditions above AND Bollinger Band width > pyramiding minimum)))
Short: fast EMA < slow EMA AND slow EMA < prior slow EMA AND +DI < −DI AND ADX > threshold AND Parabolic SAR > close AND MACD histogram < 0 AND volume > volume SMA × volume factor
Short: (position is flat or long AND (short conditions above OR (ADX is not bullish AND prior high ≤ prior upper Bollinger Band AND high > upper Bollinger Band AND short EMA conditions above AND Bollinger Band width > new-position minimum))) OR (position is short AND close > previous entry price × (1 + better-price threshold) AND (short conditions above OR (ADX is not bullish AND prior high ≤ prior upper Bollinger Band AND high > upper Bollinger Band AND short EMA conditions above AND Bollinger Band width > pyramiding minimum)))
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop level OR (high ≥ long profit level, if trailing is disabled) OR (profit level reached and price falls by the trailing offset from the post-activation high)
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop level OR (low ≤ short profit level, if trailing is disabled) OR (profit level reached and price rises by the trailing offset from the post-activation low)
