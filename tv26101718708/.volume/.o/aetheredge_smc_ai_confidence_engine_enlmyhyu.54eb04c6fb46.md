Calculation: Swing high/low = a high/low exceeding the highs/lows of the preceding and following pivot-length bars; use the latest confirmed swing.
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − 20-bar SMA(volume)) / 20-bar standard deviation(volume).
Long: (close > open AND abs(close − open) / ATR > 1.5 AND Volume Z-score > 0.5 AND close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high AND a prior down candle exists within the order-block lookback) OR (low > high two bars ago AND low − high two bars ago > FVG minimum size × ATR) OR (low < latest confirmed swing low − sweep tolerance × ATR AND close > latest confirmed swing low)
Short: (close < open AND abs(close − open) / ATR > 1.5 AND Volume Z-score > 0.5 AND close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low AND a prior up candle exists within the order-block lookback) OR (high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago − high > FVG minimum size × ATR) OR (high > latest confirmed swing high + sweep tolerance × ATR AND close < latest confirmed swing high)
