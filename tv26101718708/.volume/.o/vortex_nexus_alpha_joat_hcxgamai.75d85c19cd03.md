Calculation: Efficiency = abs(close − close[n]) / sum(abs(close − prior close), n), or 0 if the denominator is 0
Calculation: Fractal efficiency = clamp((log(rangeSum / totalRange) / log(span) + 1) / 2, 0, 1), where rangeSum sums true ranges over span bars and totalRange is highest high minus lowest low over span; use 0 if totalRange is 0
Calculation: Adaptive Laguerre momentum = clamp(EMA5((Laguerre RSI − 50) × (1 + fractal efficiency)), −100, 100). Laguerre RSI is 100 × upSum / (upSum + downSum), or 50 if both sums are 0. Compute four cascading Laguerre levels from a fourth-order Gaussian-smoothed close, with gamma = 0.7 × (1 − fractal efficiency) + 0.1 × fractal efficiency; upSum sums positive adjacent level differences and downSum sums their absolute negative differences
Calculation: Volume pressure index = SMA14(buy pressure − sell pressure) / SMA14(volume) × 100. For an up candle, buy pressure = volume × (body + 0.5 × upper wick) / bar range; for a down candle, buy pressure = volume × (upper wick + 0.3 × body) / bar range; for a doji, buy pressure = volume / 2. Sell pressure = volume − buy pressure
Calculation: Directional regime = directional score > 60, where directional score = directional power / (directional power + equilibrium power + chaos power) × 100; directional power = (100 × efficiency + 100 − chaos level + (ADX > 25 ? 20 : 0)) / 2.2; equilibrium power = (100 − volatility percentile rank + 100 × (1 − efficiency) + (ADX < 25 ? 20 : 0)) / 2.2; chaos power = (chaos level + 100 × (1 − efficiency)) / 2; chaos level = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over volatility period / (highest high − lowest low over volatility period)) / log10(volatility period). Bullish directional regime additionally requires +DI > −DI; bearish requires −DI > +DI
Calculation: Bull strength = sum of six binary scores: one if any bullish scalp condition is true; one if any bullish swing condition is true; one if any bullish momentum-continuation condition is true; one if any bullish structure-confirmation condition is true; one if the bullish ribbon-compression condition is true; and one if the bullish net-flow condition is true
Calculation: Bullish scalp conditions = (Laguerre RSI < 30 AND flow > 0 AND close > fast EMA) OR (volume pressure index > 30 AND flow direction reversed this bar AND flow > 0) OR (adaptive Laguerre momentum > 20 AND volume > 1.5 × SMA20(volume) AND close > middle EMA) OR (close > open AND close − open > ATR × 0.3 AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume pressure index > 20) OR (adaptive Laguerre momentum > 0 AND flow change over 3 bars > 0 AND ribbon aligned)
Calculation: Bullish swing conditions = (fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND bullish directional regime) OR (bullish structure break AND bullish structure direction AND adaptive Laguerre momentum > 20) OR (prior candle down, current candle up, volume > 1.2 × SMA20(volume), flow > 0, and ADX > 20) OR (low > high two bars ago AND gap > ATR × 0.3 AND volume pressure index > 40 AND ribbon aligned) OR (close breaks above the prior bar's 20-bar high AND volume surge AND cumulative volume pressure > 0 AND abs(flow) / ATR > 0.5)
Calculation: Bullish momentum-continuation conditions = (adaptive Laguerre momentum > 40 AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND ADX > 25) OR (Laguerre RSI < 30 AND flow > 0 AND volume surge) OR (abs(adaptive Laguerre momentum) > 60 AND adaptive Laguerre momentum > 0 AND EMA ribbon spread is increasing) OR (volume pressure index > 70 AND flow change over 3 bars > 0 AND bullish directional regime) OR (+DI > −DI AND +DI > 25 AND ribbon aligned)
Calculation: Bullish structure-confirmation conditions = (bullish structure shift AND volume surge) OR (bullish reversal candle and volume confirmation as defined above AND close > last pivot low AND adaptive Laguerre momentum > 20) OR (gap up as defined above AND flow > 0 AND fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (bullish structure break AND volume pressure index > 50) OR (volume > 1.5 × SMA20(volume) AND bar range < 0.5 × SMA20(bar range) AND buy pressure > sell pressure AND close > middle EMA)
Calculation: Bullish ribbon-compression condition = ribbon spread / close × 100 < 0.7 × its 20-bar SMA AND ribbon spread is increasing AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume surge
Calculation: Bullish net-flow condition = EMA10(up-candle volume minus down-candle volume) > 0 AND temporal force > 0.5 AND bullish directional regime; temporal force = (EMA(close, flow period) − EMA(close, 2 × flow period)) / ATR × (volume / EMA10(volume) − 1) × abs(flow) / ATR
Calculation: Bear strength = sum of six binary scores using the bearish counterparts of the scalp, swing, momentum-continuation, and structure-confirmation conditions below, plus the bearish ribbon-compression condition and bearish net-flow condition
Calculation: Bearish scalp conditions = (Laguerre RSI > 70 AND flow < 0 AND close < fast EMA) OR (volume pressure index < −30 AND flow direction reversed this bar AND flow < 0) OR (adaptive Laguerre momentum < −20 AND volume surge AND close < middle EMA) OR (close < open AND open − close > ATR × 0.3 AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume pressure index < −20) OR (adaptive Laguerre momentum < 0 AND flow change over 3 bars < 0 AND ribbon aligned)
Calculation: Bearish swing conditions = (fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND bearish directional regime) OR (bearish structure break AND bearish structure direction AND adaptive Laguerre momentum < −20) OR (prior candle up, current candle down, volume > 1.2 × SMA20(volume), flow < 0, and ADX > 20) OR (high < low two bars ago AND gap > ATR × 0.3 AND volume pressure index < −40 AND ribbon aligned) OR (close breaks below the prior bar's 20-bar low AND volume surge AND cumulative volume pressure < 0 AND abs(flow) / ATR > 0.5)
Calculation: Bearish momentum-continuation conditions = (adaptive Laguerre momentum < −40 AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND ADX > 25) OR (Laguerre RSI > 70 AND flow < 0 AND volume surge) OR (abs(adaptive Laguerre momentum) > 60 AND adaptive Laguerre momentum < 0 AND EMA ribbon spread is increasing) OR (volume pressure index < −70 AND flow change over 3 bars < 0 AND bearish directional regime) OR (−DI > +DI AND −DI > 25 AND ribbon aligned)
Calculation: Bearish structure-confirmation conditions = (bearish structure shift AND volume surge) OR (bearish reversal candle and volume confirmation as defined above AND close < last pivot high AND adaptive Laguerre momentum < −20) OR (gap down as defined above AND flow < 0 AND fast EMA < slow EMA) OR (bearish structure break AND volume pressure index < −50) OR (volume > 1.5 × SMA20(volume) AND bar range < 0.5 × SMA20(bar range) AND buy pressure < sell pressure AND close < middle EMA)
Calculation: Bearish ribbon-compression condition = ribbon spread / close × 100 < 0.7 × its 20-bar SMA AND ribbon spread is increasing AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume surge
Calculation: Bearish net-flow condition = EMA10(up-candle volume minus down-candle volume) < 0 AND temporal force < −0.5 AND bearish directional regime
Calculation: Flow = EMA(close, flow period) − EMA(close, 2 × flow period); flow direction is positive when flow > 0 and negative otherwise. Ribbon aligned means (close > middle EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (close < middle EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA). Volume surge means volume > 1.5 × SMA20(volume). Cumulative volume pressure is the running sum of buy pressure minus sell pressure
Calculation: Structure direction and breaks/shifts = track confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left/right spans; on a higher pivot high, mark a break if direction is bullish or a shift and set direction bullish if direction was bearish; on a lower pivot low, mark a break if direction is bearish or a shift and set direction bearish if direction was bullish. Track the latest pivot high and low
Long: bull strength ≥ minimum signal strength AND position is flat
Short: bear strength ≥ minimum signal strength AND position is flat
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier × target multiplier OR (trailing stop enabled AND low ≤ close − ATR × trail offset)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier × target multiplier OR (trailing stop enabled AND high ≥ close + ATR × trail offset)
