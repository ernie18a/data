Calculation: Buy pressure = moving average of positive candle body × volume on up bars, otherwise lower wick × volume × configured wick weight
Calculation: Sell pressure = moving average of positive candle body × volume on down bars, otherwise upper wick × volume × configured wick weight
Calculation: Bull evidence = weighted sum of bullish points for close above each EMA, both EMA slopes positive, close above session VWAP (or VWAP unavailable), buy pressure dominant, buy pressure rising, positive range oscillator, strong positive range oscillator, +DI above −DI, and RSI above its bullish threshold
Calculation: Bear evidence = weighted sum of bearish points for close below each EMA, both EMA slopes negative, close below session VWAP (or VWAP unavailable), sell pressure dominant, sell pressure rising, negative range oscillator, strong negative range oscillator, −DI above +DI, and RSI below its bearish threshold
Calculation: Range oscillator = (close − recent change-weighted average close) / ATR; each close's weight is its absolute change from the preceding close divided by that preceding close; strong positive/negative means close is more than one ATR above/below that average
Calculation: Short EMA slope = short EMA minus its value at the configured slope lookback; long EMA slope = long EMA minus its value at the same lookback; short EMA lookback is shorter than long EMA lookback
Long: bullish EMA alignment (close above both short and long EMAs, short EMA above long EMA, and both EMA slopes positive) AND close above session VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND (buy pressure > sell pressure OR range oscillator positive) OR NOT bullish EMA alignment AND NOT bearish EMA alignment AND close above short EMA AND short EMA slope positive AND close above session VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND buy pressure > sell pressure AND range oscillator nonnegative AND bull evidence >= bear evidence + configured margin OR NOT bullish EMA alignment AND NOT bearish EMA alignment AND NOT (bullish turn condition) AND NOT (bearish turn condition with bear evidence >= bull evidence + configured margin) AND bull evidence >= bear evidence + configured margin
Short: NOT bullish EMA alignment AND bearish EMA alignment (close below both short and long EMAs, short EMA below long EMA, and both EMA slopes negative) AND close below session VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND (sell pressure > buy pressure OR range oscillator negative) OR NOT bullish EMA alignment AND NOT bearish EMA alignment AND NOT (bullish turn condition with bull evidence >= bear evidence + configured margin) AND close below short EMA AND short EMA slope negative AND close below session VWAP or VWAP unavailable AND sell pressure > buy pressure AND range oscillator nonpositive OR NOT bullish EMA alignment AND NOT bearish EMA alignment AND NOT (bullish turn condition with bull evidence >= bear evidence + configured margin) AND NOT (bearish turn condition) AND bear evidence >= bull evidence + configured margin
