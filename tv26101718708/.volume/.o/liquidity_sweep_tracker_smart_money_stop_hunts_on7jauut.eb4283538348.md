Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low over the swing lookback, confirmed only after the same number of later bars has completed.
Calculation: ATR regime ratio = current-bar ATR / long-lookback average of ATR.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low; structure bias changes to that direction only when it differs from the prior bias.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair-value gap = current low above the high two bars earlier / current high below the low two bars earlier; gap midpoint = average of its bounds. A gap is filled once price reaches its near edge.
Calculation: Confidence = weighted mean of seven 0–100 scores: sweep depth (triangular score of sweep distance divided by ATR, ideal between a minimum and maximum); touch count (touches divided by a cap, capped at 100); structure alignment (100 for matching bias, 55 neutral, 25 opposing); ATR regime (linear score from a low to high ratio, clamped to 0–100); displacement (rejection distance from sweep extreme divided by ATR and an ideal displacement, capped at 100); volume (50 when volume filter is off, otherwise volume ratio score, clamped to 0–100); HTF proximity (50 when HTF filter is off or no HTF swing exists, otherwise 100 minus normalized distance, floored at 0). Use the configured weights.
Calculation: HTF swing high/low = confirmed pivot on the configured higher timeframe, using only completed higher-timeframe bars; HTF proximity = absolute distance from the swept level to the matching latest HTF swing divided by chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Sweep stop = sweep extreme plus/minus ATR times stop buffer times ATR regime ratio clamped to its configured bounds; cap stop distance at the configured maximum ATR distance. Structural trail = latest confirmed post-entry swing low minus / swing high plus ATR times trail buffer. Stop used = long: maximum of sweep stop and structural trail; short: minimum; if enabled, also apply the ATR trail backstop using current close minus/plus ATR times its multiplier.
Calculation: Reward:risk = long: (target minus entry close) / (entry close minus stop); short: (entry close minus target) / (stop minus entry close). Target is the nearest unswept opposing swing beyond the close, or the configured ATR fallback if allowed. Interim target is the nearest unfilled gap midpoint between close and that target; if none, use the opposing-swing target.
Long: An unswept confirmed swing low is swept when low < level − ATR × sweep buffer; within the confirmation window, close > that level; AND confidence meets its minimum; AND each enabled filter passes (volume at sweep bar > its moving average × multiplier; HTF distance ≤ proximity limit; at least one enabled confirmation trigger passes: bullish engulfing, bullish displacement, or close above the sweep candle high; reward:risk meets its minimum); AND an opposing target exists.
Short: An unswept confirmed swing high is swept when high > level + ATR × sweep buffer; within the confirmation window, close < that level; AND confidence meets its minimum; AND each enabled filter passes (volume at sweep bar > its moving average × multiplier; HTF distance ≤ proximity limit; at least one enabled confirmation trigger passes: bearish engulfing, bearish displacement, or close below the sweep candle low; reward:risk meets its minimum); AND an opposing target exists.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop used OR high ≥ interim target OR (structure invalidation enabled AND bearish structure break) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry ≥ limit AND close ≤ entry price).
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop used OR low ≤ interim target OR (structure invalidation enabled AND bullish structure break) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry ≥ limit AND close ≥ entry price).
