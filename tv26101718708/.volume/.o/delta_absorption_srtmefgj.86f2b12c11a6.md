Calculation: Aggression ratio = absolute current Estimated Delta / average absolute Estimated Delta over the preceding delta lookback bars
Calculation: Delta shock = current Estimated Delta - previous bar's Estimated Delta
Calculation: Shock ratio = absolute current Delta shock / average absolute Delta shock over the preceding shock lookback bars
Calculation: Volume average = average volume over the preceding volume-average lookback bars
