Calculation: ATR trailing-stop direction = bullish when previous close < previous stop and current close > previous stop; bearish when previous close > previous stop and current close < previous stop; otherwise retain prior direction. Update stop to max(previous stop, close − ATR × multiplier) when current and previous closes are above previous stop; min(previous stop, close + ATR × multiplier) when both are below; otherwise close − ATR × multiplier if close > previous stop, else close + ATR × multiplier.
Calculation: Displacement = abs(close − open) + abs(open − previous close)
Long: ATR trailing-stop direction is bullish AND close > open AND displacement > its SMA × an expansion factor AND (volume is missing or volume ≤ 0 OR volume > its SMA × an expansion factor) AND no active long-signal state
Short: ATR trailing-stop direction is bearish AND close < open AND displacement > its SMA × an expansion factor AND (volume is missing or volume ≤ 0 OR volume > its SMA × an expansion factor) AND no active short-signal state
