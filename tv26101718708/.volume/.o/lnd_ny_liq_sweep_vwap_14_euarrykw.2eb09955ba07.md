Calculation: London/NY range high and low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, during that session’s range window.
Calculation: 20/80 level = nearest price-step multiple offset by the configured 20/80 level offset.
Calculation: Consecutive one-minute declines = number of consecutive one-minute closes lower than the preceding one-minute close.
Long: (London or NY trade window AND its range low has been swept during that trade window with consecutive one-minute declines ≥ the configured minimum AND low ≤ the 20 level AND close > the 20 level AND (displacement filter disabled OR current high−low > the maximum high−low of the prior five bars) AND (FVG filter disabled OR low > high two bars ago AND close > high two bars ago)) OR (London or NY trade window AND low < its range low AND consecutive one-minute declines ≥ the configured minimum AND close < open)
Short: (London or NY trade window AND its range high has been swept during that trade window with consecutive one-minute advances ≥ the configured minimum AND high ≥ the 80 level AND close < the 80 level AND (displacement filter disabled OR current high−low > the maximum high−low of the prior five bars) AND (FVG filter disabled OR high < low two bars ago AND close < low two bars ago)) OR (London or NY trade window AND high > its range high AND consecutive one-minute advances ≥ the configured minimum AND close > open)
