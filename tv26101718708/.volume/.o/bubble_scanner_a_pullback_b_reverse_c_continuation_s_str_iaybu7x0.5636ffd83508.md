Calculation: EMA cloud = the faster EMA compared with the slower EMA; bullish when faster is above slower and bearish when below; a cloud flip is a cross.
Calculation: Long-term EMA = EMA with a longer lookback than the cloud EMAs.
Calculation: Hull average = weighted moving average of twice the half-lookback weighted moving average of close minus the full-lookback weighted moving average of close, smoothed by a weighted moving average with lookback equal to the rounded square root of the full lookback; rising/falling compares its current value with the previous bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = sum of six configured timeframe trends, each +1 when the faster cloud EMA is at or above the slower cloud EMA and −1 otherwise; use the bars still forming.
Calculation: Swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides. A bullish/bearish structure break occurs when price exceeds the latest unused swing high/low, using close or wick as configured; classify it as CHoCH if the prior structure trend was opposite, otherwise BOS. A higher low/lower high compares consecutive confirmed swing lows/highs.
Calculation: Equal-high/equal-low zone = two or more confirmed swing highs/lows within a configured ATR-based tolerance. An equal-high zone is swept when high exceeds its upper bound; an equal-low zone is swept when low falls below its lower bound.
Calculation: Volume delta = buy volume minus sell volume from lower-timeframe intrabars; count intrabar volume as buy when its close exceeds its open, sell when its close is below its open, and split it equally when equal. Relative volume = current intrabar volume divided by its configured rolling median or average. A buy/sell bubble requires relative volume above its threshold and that side's share above its dominance threshold; within the configured window, keep only the strongest bubble per side (or overall if configured).
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative volume delta reset at the configured day, week, or never boundary. Bullish divergence occurs when a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous pivot low while cumulative delta at those pivots is higher; bearish divergence is the reverse at pivot highs. Compare pivots within the same reset period.
Calculation: Three-bar delta = sum of volume delta over the latest three bars. Recent condition = the event occurred within the configured lookback, inclusive.
Calculation: Zone touch = the bar's high-low range intersects an active equal-low zone (long) or equal-high zone (short), the latest upward/downward broken structure level, or the long-term EMA, allowing configured ATR tolerance.
Calculation: Sideways range = the prior configured lookback's high-low range is no wider than configured ATR multiple, or cloud flips in the lookback meet the configured count. A range breakout occurs when the prior bar was sideways and the current close exceeds the prior range high/low. Range gate allows signals when range filtering is off, the market is not sideways, or a same-direction breakout occurred within the configured post-breakout window.
