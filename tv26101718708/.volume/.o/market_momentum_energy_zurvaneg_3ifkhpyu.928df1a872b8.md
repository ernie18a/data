Calculation: Directional energy = smoothed signed displacement or linear-regression slope, normalized by recent volatility; optionally multiply by normalized volume or true-range effort, normalize by recent absolute energy, and scale by a quality factor.
Calculation: Quality score = a blend of squared correlation to time and efficiency ratio; optionally normalize against its recent average.
Long: Directional energy crosses above its positive ignition threshold AND quality score meets its gate AND energy is below positive saturation AND short-term HMA slope is positive AND the long signal is armed AND alternation permits long; OR, within the retry window after a qualifying upward cross failed short-term direction confirmation, energy remains above the threshold AND quality meets its gate AND energy is below saturation AND short-term HMA slope is positive AND long remains armed AND alternation permits long; OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the previous qualifying pivot low while energy at the new pivot is higher than at the previous pivot, both pivot energies are negative, pivot quality meets its gate, and the pivots are within the maximum gap.
Short: Directional energy crosses below its negative ignition threshold AND quality score meets its gate AND energy is above negative saturation AND short-term HMA slope is negative AND the short signal is armed AND alternation permits short; OR, within the retry window after a qualifying downward cross failed short-term direction confirmation, energy remains below the negative threshold AND quality meets its gate AND energy is above negative saturation AND short-term HMA slope is negative AND short remains armed AND alternation permits short; OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the previous qualifying pivot high while energy at the new pivot is lower than at the previous pivot, both pivot energies are positive, pivot quality meets its gate, and the pivots are within the maximum gap.
