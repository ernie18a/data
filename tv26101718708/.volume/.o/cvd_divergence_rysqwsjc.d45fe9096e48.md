Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) = running sum, without session resets, of lower-timeframe candle volume signed positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and zero when close = open; use lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar.
Long: At a confirmed CVD pivot low, the price low is below its value at the previous CVD pivot low AND the CVD value is above its value at the previous CVD pivot low AND the previous pivot low is between the minimum and maximum allowed bar distances away
Long: At a confirmed CVD pivot low, the price low is above its value at the previous CVD pivot low AND the CVD value is below its value at the previous CVD pivot low AND the previous pivot low is between the minimum and maximum allowed bar distances away
Short: At a confirmed CVD pivot high, the price high is above its value at the previous CVD pivot high AND the CVD value is below its value at the previous CVD pivot high AND the previous pivot high is between the minimum and maximum allowed bar distances away
Short: At a confirmed CVD pivot high, the price high is below its value at the previous CVD pivot high AND the CVD value is above its value at the previous CVD pivot high AND the previous pivot high is between the minimum and maximum allowed bar distances away
