Calculation: Repeated-pivot levels = create an upper level from two confirmed pivot highs, or a lower level from two confirmed pivot lows, when their price distance is within the matching tolerance and their confirmation bars are separated by at least the minimum gap; use the higher pivot price for an upper level and the lower pivot price for a lower level. A pivot is confirmed after the configured right-side bars complete. Keep each level active until it breaks or reaches its maximum lifetime.
Calculation: Break threshold = active level plus (upper) or minus (lower) the configured ATR-based buffer, using ATR from the current completed chart-timeframe bar; test either the current bar's high/low (wick mode) or close (close mode), as configured.
Long: An active upper level breaks upward OR the first bar after an upper-level break has low <= level AND close > level.
Short: An active lower level breaks downward OR the first bar after a lower-level break has high >= level AND close < level.
