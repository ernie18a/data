Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close
Calculation: RSI slope = current RSI − previous RSI
Calculation: RSI acceleration = current RSI slope − previous RSI slope
Calculation: Recent RSI low/high = lowest/highest RSI over the structure lookback
Calculation: Recent price low/high = lowest low/highest high over the structure lookback
Calculation: Price range = high − low
Calculation: Average range = simple moving average of price range
Calculation: RSI pivot low/high = an RSI pivot confirmed after the configured right-side lookback, using the configured left-side lookback
Long: (RSI < 100 AND RSI > 100) OR (RSI > previous recent RSI high AND (price-delay filter disabled OR low >= previous recent price low) AND (slope filter disabled OR RSI slope > slope threshold AND RSI acceleration >= 0) AND (compression filter disabled OR price range < average range × compression threshold) AND (price-confirmation mode disabled OR close > previous high)) OR (previous confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the prior pivot low AND corresponding price low is lower than the prior pivot low AND the prior pivot is within the configured pivot range) OR (previous bar was compressed AND RSI slope > 1.5 × slope threshold)
Short: (RSI < previous recent RSI low AND (price-delay filter disabled OR high <= previous recent price high) AND (slope filter disabled OR RSI slope < −slope threshold AND RSI acceleration <= 0) AND (compression filter disabled OR price range < average range × compression threshold) AND (price-confirmation mode disabled OR close < previous low)) OR (previous confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the prior pivot high AND corresponding price high is higher than the prior pivot high AND the prior pivot is within the configured pivot range) OR (previous bar was compressed AND RSI slope < −1.5 × slope threshold)
