Calculation: Filtered capitulation = WVF = 100 × (rolling highest close − low) / rolling highest close; it fires when the prior WVF was at or above its upper volatility band or its scaled rolling-range high, current WVF is below both, low > prior low, close > prior high, close > close at either of two shorter lookbacks, and close > close at a separate short lookback. Use the current and prior bars of each evaluation-timeframe series.
Calculation: Bullish EMA alignment = shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close).
Calculation: VIDYA = adaptive moving average with alpha = 2/(length+1) × absolute Chande momentum oscillator / 100; initialize from close and update recursively. Bullish when close > VIDYA.
Calculation: OBV slope is positive when cumulative signed volume (volume if close ≥ open, otherwise −volume) is greater than its value at a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Signed-volume EMA is positive when the EMA of signed volume (volume if close ≥ open, otherwise −volume) is above zero.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = current low − high two bars earlier > 0 and its percentage of current close meets the gap-size threshold.
Calculation: Trend Quality Index = mean of (efficiency ratio; clipped ATR-to-longer-term-average-ATR quality; clipped close distance above a longer-lookback SMA scaled by ATR; clipped percentage momentum quality). Use shorter lookbacks for the fast ATR and momentum components than for the ATR average and structure SMA.
Calculation: Volatility rank = percentile rank of ATR within its rolling lookback; high band is at or above the upper cut, low band is below the lower cut.
Calculation: Entry score = weighted sum of bullish EMA alignment and VIDYA alignment armed within the core window after a recent filtered-capitulation event, positive OBV slope, positive signed-volume EMA armed within the delta window after that event, a bullish fair-value gap within the recent-event window, TQI at or above its threshold, and high volatility rank; subtract the weighted low-volatility term. FVG and TQI score terms are included only when enabled.
Calculation: Bollinger lower band = SMA(close, lookback) − multiplier × standard deviation(close, lookback).
Calculation: Williams VIX Fix top reversal = prior top-WVF was at or below its lower volatility band or scaled rolling-range low, and current top-WVF is above both; top-WVF = 100 × (rolling lowest close − high) / rolling lowest close.
Calculation: CVI = (close − rolling lowest low) / (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), or 0.5 if the range is zero.
Long: On the primary entry timeframe, filtered capitulation occurred within its arm window AND entry score meets the BASE floor AND the optional TQI dead-zone exclusion is not active AND the tier is not suppressed; tier is FULL when score meets the higher tier floor and ADX is below its threshold, SCALE when score meets that floor otherwise, and BASE otherwise. A recent Bollinger lower-band close-break within the anti-signal window after capitulation demotes tiers above BASE by one. Optional BTC-bull requirement demotes FULL when BTC close is not above its EMA. Require a fresh tier transition from zero to positive; apply the configured entry delay.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above the Bollinger basis, close > Bollinger basis, and the shorter-minus-longer SMA of hl2 is nonnegative and rising.
Long: The downside momentum bias crosses below its prior value while above its rolling bound and greater than the upside momentum bias; or the upside momentum bias exceeds the downside momentum bias.
Long: Trend-panel score > 0.5, where trend state is +1 if the current close is above both the EMA and VWAP of that same timeframe, −1 if below both, otherwise 0; add +0.5/−0.5/0 for the sign of close minus its value three bars earlier, and add +0.5 when ATR exceeds its own longer-lookback SMA (otherwise 0 by default). These inputs use the last completed bars of each requested timeframe.

Short: The upside momentum bias crosses below its prior value while above its rolling bound and greater than the downside momentum bias.
Short: Trend-panel score < −0.5, using the same-timeframe close, EMA, VWAP, three-bar momentum, and ATR comparison defined above.
