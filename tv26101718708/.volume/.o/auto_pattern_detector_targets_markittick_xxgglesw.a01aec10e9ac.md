Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is strictly greater/less than every high/low in the surrounding pivot-lookback window; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Breakout threshold = boundary +/− (ATR × volatility multiplier, or boundary × percentage threshold).
Calculation: Projected boundary = straight-line extension through the two specified swing points.
Calculation: Pattern size is valid when its height exceeds both the price-percentage minimum and the ATR-multiple minimum.
Calculation: Adjusted risk/reward = (absolute target-to-entry distance minus slippage and round-trip commission, floored at zero) / absolute entry-to-stop distance.
Long: After the global history and pivot-count gates, a valid bullish pattern breaks above its boundary by the breakout threshold: double bottom (two near-equal swing lows around an intervening high); triple bottom (three near-equal swing lows with intervening highs); inverted head and shoulders (three swing lows, middle lowest, outer lows near-equal, neckline break); cup and handle (near-equal swing-high rims, intervening low, handle low above the cup low and below the rim, rim break); bullish flag or pennant (both upper and lower boundaries slope down, pole move exceeds 3 ATR, upper-boundary break); falling wedge (both boundaries slope down, lower slope is steeper, upper-boundary break); triangle (boundaries converge, with descending or flat upper boundary and rising lower boundary, upper-boundary break); or rectangle (near-flat boundaries, upper-boundary break). Require valid pattern size, breakout confirmation, and adjusted risk/reward at least the minimum; if volume confirmation is enabled, breakout-bar volume must exceed its moving average times the volume multiplier. If enabled, require breakout close beyond threshold, full candle body beyond threshold, and/or consecutive confirming closes as configured. Only emit a new marking when the pattern start is farther than the cooldown from the last marked start.
Short: After the global history and pivot-count gates, a valid bearish pattern breaks below its boundary by the breakout threshold: double top (two near-equal swing highs around an intervening low); triple top (three near-equal swing highs with intervening lows); head and shoulders (three swing highs, middle highest, outer highs near-equal, neckline break); inverted cup and handle (near-equal swing-low rims, intervening high, handle high below the cup high and above the rim, rim break); bearish flag or pennant (both upper and lower boundaries slope up, pole move exceeds 3 ATR, lower-boundary break); rising wedge (both boundaries slope up, lower slope is steeper, lower-boundary break); triangle (boundaries converge, with descending upper boundary and rising or flat lower boundary, lower-boundary break); or rectangle (near-flat boundaries, lower-boundary break). Require valid pattern size, breakout confirmation, and adjusted risk/reward at least the minimum; if volume confirmation is enabled, breakout-bar volume must exceed its moving average times the volume multiplier. If enabled, require breakout close beyond threshold, full candle body beyond threshold, and/or consecutive confirming closes as configured. Only emit a new marking when the pattern start is farther than the cooldown from the last marked start.
