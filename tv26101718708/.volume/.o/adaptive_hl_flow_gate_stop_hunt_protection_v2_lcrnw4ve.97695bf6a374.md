Calculation: Hybrid volatility = blend of ATR and Parkinson volatility, each using the same lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive trail = recursive volatility trail using hybrid volatility and an adaptive multiplier; bullish state flips bearish when close falls below the prior upper trail, and bearish state flips bullish when close rises above the prior lower trail.
Calculation: Flow gates = smooth the rolling highest high and lowest low using the same structure lookback and smoothing; center their midpoint and set gate width to the larger of their range or twice hybrid volatility, multiplied by a width parameter. Upper and lower gates are center plus and minus that width.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that same lookback.
Calculation: Momentum direction = MFI direction when volume is available, otherwise RSI direction; bullish above 50 plus a deadband, bearish below 50 minus the deadband, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Candle pressure direction = EMA of normalized close position within the candle range, weighted by relative volume; bullish above a positive threshold, bearish below its negative, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Higher- and middle-timeframe directions = direction of the EMA slope on each respective timeframe, using the same EMA length; use the most recently available bar without lookahead.
Calculation: Flow score = weighted sum of adaptive-trail direction, flow-gate direction, momentum direction, ADX-qualified DMI direction, candle-pressure direction, volume-pulse direction, sweep direction, decaying sweep-risk direction, compression-expansion direction, higher- and middle-timeframe directions, and intraday close-versus-session-VWAP direction.
Calculation: Chop = efficiency ratio below its minimum OR ADX below its minimum.
Calculation: Entry firewall = chop OR weak-body candle (body/range below minimum with either wick/range above minimum) OR excessive close-to-trail distance in hybrid-volatility units OR opening gap from prior close above a hybrid-volatility multiple OR opposing higher- or middle-timeframe direction OR active same-side sweep risk above its threshold before reclaim.
Calculation: Flow state = bullish when score reaches the positive threshold and the long firewall is clear; bearish when score reaches the negative threshold and the short firewall is clear; neutral when enabled and absolute score is below its reset threshold. State changes require the minimum bars between non-neutral flips.
Long: Flow state changes to bullish.
Short: Flow state changes to bearish.
Long: Flow state is bullish AND bars since its change are at least the retest delay AND low <= upper flow gate AND close > upper flow gate AND close > adaptive trail AND momentum direction is neutral or bullish AND no active blocking buy-side sweep risk.
Short: Flow state is bearish AND bars since its change are at least the retest delay AND high >= lower flow gate AND close < lower flow gate AND close < adaptive trail AND momentum direction is neutral or bearish AND no active blocking sell-side sweep risk.
Long: Flow state is bullish AND close > adaptive trail AND close > upper flow gate AND no active blocking buy-side sweep risk.
Short: Flow state is bearish AND close < adaptive trail AND close < lower flow gate AND no active blocking sell-side sweep risk.
