Calculation: Candle body = absolute(close − open); candle range = high − low, or one tick if zero; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; close position = (close − low) / adjusted candle range
Calculation: Bullish rejection/doji = (enabled bullish rejection: lower wick ≥ body × wick/body threshold, upper wick ≤ body × 0.75, and close position ≥ 0.45) OR (enabled doji: body / adjusted candle range ≤ doji body threshold)
Calculation: Bearish rejection/doji = (enabled bearish rejection: upper wick ≥ body × wick/body threshold, lower wick ≤ body × 0.75, and close position ≤ 0.55) OR (enabled doji: body / adjusted candle range ≤ doji body threshold)
Calculation: Bullish five-bar pivot = the low two bars ago is lower than the lows four, three, one, and zero bars ago; bearish five-bar pivot = the high two bars ago is higher than the highs four, three, one, and zero bars ago
Calculation: Bullish two-bar reversal = the previous bar’s lower wick ≥ its body × wick/body threshold, current close > current open, and current lower wick ≤ current body × 0.5; bearish two-bar reversal = the previous bar’s upper wick ≥ its body × wick/body threshold, current close < current open, and current upper wick ≤ current body × 0.5
Calculation: Bullish/bearish swing start = the selected five-bar pivot with bullish/bearish rejection or doji on its center bar, or the selected two-bar reversal; when prior-pivot sweep is required, bullish five-bar pivots must be below the preceding bullish pivot low, bearish five-bar pivots above the preceding bearish pivot high, bullish two-bar reversals below the low two bars ago, and bearish two-bar reversals above the high two bars ago
Calculation: Bullish/bearish EMA trend = fast EMA > / < slow EMA and close > / < fast EMA; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish/bearish two-point structure = the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows are both rising / falling; three-point structure = the latest three confirmed pivot highs and lows are all rising / falling; use the selected strength, or require both strengths when both are selected
Calculation: Directional trend filter = true when disabled or off; otherwise require the selected EMA trend, structure trend, or both
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = current candle is bullish/bearish, its body ≥ ATR × displacement threshold, and close > / < fast EMA
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low − high two bars ago ≥ minimum FVG size in ticks; bearish FVG = low two bars ago − current high ≥ minimum FVG size in ticks
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = a still-valid bearish FVG whose top is below current close and whose size meets the minimum IFVG size; bearish IFVG = a still-valid bullish FVG whose bottom is above current close and whose size meets the minimum IFVG size. Mark the gap invalid after it flips.
Calculation: Qualifying bullish/bearish zone = after a bullish/bearish swing start, on a later bar with matching displacement, a matching FVG or newly flipped IFVG forms; if volume confirmation is enabled, current volume must exceed its moving average × volume threshold
Calculation: Bullish/bearish retest confirmation = price has touched the whole matching zone (or its half extending to the zone midpoint, when refined-half retest is required), and a bullish/bearish candle closes above the zone top / below the zone bottom when close-out is required; if volume confirmation is enabled, current volume must exceed its moving average × volume threshold
Long: bullish swing start AND directional trend filter AND (qualifying bullish zone in Gap Close mode OR qualifying bullish zone followed by bullish retest confirmation in Retest mode) OR bullish swing start AND directional trend filter
Short: bearish swing start AND directional trend filter AND (qualifying bearish zone in Gap Close mode OR qualifying bearish zone followed by bearish retest confirmation in Retest mode) OR bearish swing start AND directional trend filter
