Calculation: Price-position score = 100 × the fraction of enabled VWAP endpoints below the reference price; use the close of the window-end bar as reference price.
Calculation: Stack score = 50 × (signed adjacent-pair total + pair count) / (2 × pair count), where enabled VWAPs are ordered by increasing lookback; a pair scores +1 when the shorter-lookback VWAP exceeds the longer-lookback VWAP by more than the tolerance, −1 when it is lower by more than the tolerance, and 0 otherwise. The tolerance is the anchor-timeframe ATR multiplied by an adjustable factor.
Calculation: Structure score = half the Price-position score plus half the Stack score.
Long: Structure score >= 55 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique; OR Price-position score >= 55 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique; OR Stack score >= 55 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique
Short: Structure score < 45 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique; OR Price-position score < 45 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique; OR Stack score < 45 AND every enabled VWAP is available AND enabled lookbacks are unique
