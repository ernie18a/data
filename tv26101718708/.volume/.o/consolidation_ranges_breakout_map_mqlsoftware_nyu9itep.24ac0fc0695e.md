Calculation: Candidate range = highest high and lowest low over the same lookback; width = highest high − lowest low
Calculation: Range Compression Index = 100 × (0.40 × inefficiency + 0.35 × volatility compression + 0.25 × close containment), where inefficiency = 1 − min(|close − close[lookback]| / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback, 1); volatility compression = clamp((1.15 − ATR(short lookback) / ATR(long lookback)) / 0.75, 0, 1), with the long ATR lookback four times the short; close containment = min(1, share of closes within the central 90% of the candidate range / containment threshold)
Long: On a confirmed bar, a valid live range exists after the compression threshold and width limit held for the required consecutive confirmed bars, and close > live range high + ATR buffer, and (volume gate disabled or volume data unavailable or volume ≥ configured multiple of its rolling median)
Short: On a confirmed bar, a valid live range exists after the compression threshold and width limit held for the required consecutive confirmed bars, and close < live range low − ATR buffer, and (volume gate disabled or volume data unavailable or volume ≥ configured multiple of its rolling median)
