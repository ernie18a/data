Calculation: Basis = selected moving average of close: SMA, EMA, HMA, KAMA, or volume-weighted MA.
Calculation: Upper/lower 2σ bands = basis ± 2 × deviation; deviation is standard deviation of close over the basis lookback, or, in asymmetric mode, the corresponding upside/downside semi-deviation × √2, floored at half the standard deviation.
Calculation: %B = (close − lower 2σ band) / (upper 2σ band − lower 2σ band); use 0.5 if the band range is zero.
Calculation: Squeeze = selected test: 2σ bands nested inside Keltner bands; bandwidth percentile below its threshold; or both. Keltner bands use the basis ± a multiplier times the average true range (or high−low range) over its lookback. Bandwidth is band range / basis × 100, ranked against its historical lookback.
Long: active WALK_DOWN (close below the lower 2σ band for the configured consecutive-bar count, and Squeeze is false) AND a new lowest close during the walk has %B greater than at the previous extreme by more than the configured minimum decay AND consecutive decay count reaches its configured threshold.
Short: active WALK_UP (close above the upper 2σ band for the configured consecutive-bar count, and Squeeze is false) AND a new highest close during the walk has %B lower than at the previous extreme by more than the configured minimum decay AND consecutive decay count reaches its configured threshold.
