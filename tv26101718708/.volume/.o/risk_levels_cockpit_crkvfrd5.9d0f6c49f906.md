Calculation: Theil–Sen channel = On each completed chart bar, fit the median of all pairwise slopes between closes in the lookback; center the line at the current bar, and calculate the median absolute residual (MAD) from that line. Channel rails are center ± rail-width × MAD; break levels are center ± break-width × MAD.
Calculation: Model stop = On each completed chart bar, use the larger of (a) the POT-GPD return-tail quantile, scaled by close and square root of the holding horizon (fall back to ATR × its multiplier if the fit is unavailable), and (b) the distance from close to the adverse channel rail. If enabled, also use the distance to VAL for a long or VAH for a short when that level is adverse and within the configured cap; use the largest applicable distance. Stop price is close minus that distance for a long, or plus it for a short.
Calculation: Target levels = By default, close ± each configured R multiple × the corresponding side's stop distance. In VP-level mode, when the opposite value-area edge is beyond close, use POC (if beyond close) or that edge for target 1, and the edge for target 2; otherwise use the R-multiple targets. Theil–Sen, EVT, ATR, and volume-profile calculations use current chart bars, including the current bar at its close.
Long: Theil–Sen slope > 0
Short: Theil–Sen slope <= 0
Exit: Long, (low <= long model stop AND previous low > previous long model stop) OR (high >= long target 1 AND previous high < previous long target 1) OR (high >= long target 2 AND previous high < previous long target 2) OR (low <= lower break level AND previous low > previous lower break level)
Exit: Short, (high >= short model stop AND previous high < previous short model stop) OR (low <= short target 1 AND previous low > previous short target 1) OR (low <= short target 2 AND previous low > previous short target 2) OR (high >= upper break level AND previous high < previous upper break level)
