Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / moving average of volume, using the same lookback as the volume confirmation checks.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions among bullish EMA structure, bullish RSI, bullish MACD, bullish ADX/DMI, relative volume confirmation, bullish higher-timeframe confirmation, and bullish candle strength.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of true conditions among bearish EMA structure, bearish RSI, bearish MACD, bearish ADX/DMI, relative volume confirmation, bearish higher-timeframe confirmation, and bearish candle strength.
Calculation: Breakout score = count of true conditions among the direction’s EMA structure, momentum RSI, MACD, ADX/DMI, higher-timeframe confirmation, and candle strength.
Calculation: Flash momentum score = count of true conditions among the direction’s breakout, momentum RSI, MACD, DMI direction, and candle strength.
Long: On a completed bar, a bullish Supertrend direction with a bullish Supertrend flip within the confirmation lookback AND bullish score at or above the minimum score; OR close above the highest high of the prior breakout lookback bars AND relative volume at or above the breakout minimum AND bullish breakout score at or above its minimum. Emit only if the previous confirmed signal direction is not long. OR, while the current bar is forming, close > open AND abs(close − open) / ATR at or above the flash move minimum AND (high − low) / ATR at or above the flash range minimum AND relative volume at or above the flash volume minimum AND bullish flash momentum score at or above its minimum; emit at most once per bar.
Short: On a completed bar, a bearish Supertrend direction with a bearish Supertrend flip within the confirmation lookback AND bearish score at or above the minimum score; OR close below the lowest low of the prior breakout lookback bars AND relative volume at or above the breakout minimum AND bearish breakout score at or above its minimum. Emit only if the previous confirmed signal direction is not short. OR, while the current bar is forming, close < open AND abs(close − open) / ATR at or above the flash move minimum AND (high − low) / ATR at or above the flash range minimum AND relative volume at or above the flash volume minimum AND bearish flash momentum score at or above its minimum; emit at most once per bar.
