Calculation: PVT = previous PVT + ((close − previous close) / previous close) × volume
Long: position is flat or short AND shorter-lookback CCI > 0 AND longer-lookback CCI > 0 AND PVT > its simple moving average
Short: position is flat or long AND shorter-lookback CCI < 0 AND longer-lookback CCI < 0 AND PVT < its simple moving average
