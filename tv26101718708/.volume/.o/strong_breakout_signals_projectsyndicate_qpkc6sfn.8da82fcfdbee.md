Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the selected lookback bars on each side; available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Tested-zone width = ATR × zone-width parameter. Clustered resistance/support = at least the required number of stored swing highs/lows within that width; retain pivots within the zone-history limit. A resistance break requires a bullish bar closing above the previous lookback high and the clustered resistance, with its open at or below that resistance; a support break is the bearish mirror, closing below the previous lookback low and clustered support, with its open at or above support.
Calculation: Structure break = price exceeds the latest confirmed swing high/low (close or wick, as selected) on a bullish/bearish bar, respectively.
Calculation: Breakout strength = score from pivot-test count, zone age, candle-body/range ratio, candle-range/ATR, volume/20-bar average volume, directional close changes over 3 and 5 bars, clean-break distance, RSI, agreement of both breakout engines, and higher-timeframe alignment; cap at 0–10 and round to one decimal. Directional momentum and RSI add points when aligned and momentum can subtract points when neither lookback aligns.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of the previous completed bar on the selected higher timeframe.
Calculation: Trade levels = enter at signal-bar close; place stop beyond the broken zone by an ATR buffer, with stop distance floored and capped by ATR risk parameters; targets are entry ± stop distance × each configured R multiple, in the position direction.
Long: (clustered resistance break OR structure break above swing high) AND strength meets minimum and optional strong-score gate AND close above higher-timeframe EMA (when enabled) AND cooldown elapsed (when enabled) AND no active trade AND (no short signal or long strength >= short strength).
Short: (clustered support break OR structure break below swing low) AND strength meets minimum and optional strong-score gate AND close below higher-timeframe EMA (when enabled) AND cooldown elapsed (when enabled) AND no active trade AND (no long signal or short strength > long strength).
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= third target
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= third target
