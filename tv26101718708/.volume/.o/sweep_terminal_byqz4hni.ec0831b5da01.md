Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across equal configurable lookback windows before and after it; use the latest confirmed pivot of each type until replaced or broken
Long: close >= the open of the still-forming daily bar OR close >= the open of the still-forming weekly bar OR close >= current session VWAP OR RSI >= 50 OR DI+ > DI- OR, after price has traded above the high of the last completed bar on any enabled higher timeframe, a bar closes back at or below that high and the excursion high exceeded it OR price breaks above the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high (close if body confirmation is configured, otherwise high) OR a bullish FVG forms where current low > high two bars earlier and the gap meets the configured ATR threshold
Short: close < the open of the still-forming daily bar OR close < the open of the still-forming weekly bar OR close < current session VWAP OR RSI < 50 OR DI- > DI+ OR, after price has traded below the low of the last completed bar on any enabled higher timeframe, a bar closes back at or above that low and the excursion low was below it OR price breaks below the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low (close if body confirmation is configured, otherwise low) OR a bearish FVG forms where current high < low two bars earlier and the gap meets the configured ATR threshold
