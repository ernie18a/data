Calculation: Bullish divergence = the latest confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low, RSI at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot, and the latest pivot is within the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = the latest confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high, RSI at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot, and the latest pivot is within the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = a bullish candle whose lower wick is at least the configured fraction of its range, OR a bullish candle whose open is below the previous low and close is above the previous high.
Calculation: Bearish reversal candle = a bearish candle whose upper wick is at least the configured fraction of its range, OR a bearish candle whose open is above the previous high and close is below the previous low.
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = the number of these conditions that hold: bullish divergence; current volume is at least its moving average times the configured climax multiplier; bullish reversal candle; current low is within the configured ATR tolerance of the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bearish confluence count = the number of these conditions that hold: bearish divergence; current volume is at least its moving average times the configured climax multiplier; bearish reversal candle; current high is within the configured ATR tolerance of the latest confirmed swing high.
Long: bullish divergence OR (bullish confluence count >= minimum confluences AND bullish confluence count > bearish confluence count AND cooldown elapsed since the previous signal AND current bar is complete)
Short: bearish divergence OR (bearish confluence count >= minimum confluences AND bearish confluence count > bullish confluence count AND cooldown elapsed since the previous signal AND current bar is complete)
