Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hl2 from the start of the selected current session, week, or month, using chart bars
Calculation: Pressure = EMA(EMA((close−open)/max(high−low, minimum price increment) × (0.5 + abs(close−open)/max(high−low, minimum price increment)) × (0.5 + volume/short-term average volume) × 100, pressure length) + EMA((close−anchored VWAP)/ATR, half pressure length) × 18, normalization length z-score), smoothing length)
Calculation: Crowding = EMA(z-score(EMA(2×(RSI(hlc3, crowding length)−50) + 10×(close−anchored VWAP)/ATR + 24×(lower wick−upper wick)/max(high−low, minimum price increment) + 8×(close position in range−0.5), crowding length), normalization length), smoothing length)
Calculation: Volatility = EMA(z-score((ATR / SMA(ATR, volatility length)−1)×100 + (ATR percentile rank over volatility length−50)×0.35, normalization length), smoothing length)
Calculation: Skew = EMA(z-score(EMA(((close−low)−(high−close))/max(high−low, minimum price increment)×100 + 12×(close position in range−0.5) + 7×(close−anchored VWAP)/ATR, skew length), normalization length), smoothing length)
Calculation: Composite = EMA(pressure×pressure weight + crowding×crowding weight + volatility×volatility weight + skew×skew weight, smoothing length)
Calculation: Risk = clamp(32×clamp(abs(crowding)/crowding extreme, 0, 1) + 22×clamp(volatility/volatility expansion, 0, 1) + 18×clamp(abs(close−anchored VWAP)/ATR/stretch cap, 0, 1) + 12×clamp(abs(skew)/1.25, 0, 1) + 16×pressure conflict, 0, 100); pressure conflict = 1 when (Composite > 0 and pressure < −0.10) or (Composite < 0 and pressure > 0.10), otherwise 0
Long: on a confirmed bar, (Risk ≥ critical risk and Composite > 0) OR (Composite ≥ bull threshold and pressure > 0 and crowding > −0.20 and volatility < volatility expansion OR abs(Composite) ≥ 0.35)
Short: on a confirmed bar, (Risk ≥ critical risk and Composite < 0) OR (Composite ≤ −bear threshold and pressure < 0 and crowding < 0.20 and volatility < volatility expansion OR abs(Composite) ≥ 0.35)
