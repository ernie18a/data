Calculation: Active shelf = a candidate band formed by merging adjacent price-grid levels whose rolling occupation time is at or above the chosen percentile of all occupied levels; each confirmed chart bar adds equal total time across the levels covered by its range, and contributions older than the dwell lookback are removed. Promote the band when Coil >= its threshold and the fraction of closes in the containment lookback within the band plus/minus the buffer times ATR14 >= its threshold.
Calculation: Coil = 1 - ATR(fast lookback) / ATR(slow lookback), using confirmed chart bars; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Bar close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: VSA spread rank = percentile rank of high - low over the VSA history lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: VSA volume rank = percentile rank over the VSA history lookback of volume, or, when time-of-day normalization is enabled, volume divided by its current-inclusive EMA for the same time-of-day slot.
Calculation: Prior high/low = highest high / lowest low over the new-extreme lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: VSA peak volume = highest VSA volume over the VSA history lookback, including the current bar.
Long: an active shelf has been touched, its first approach was from above (the previous close was at or above its dwell-weighted center), and close > shelf high + flip distance × ATR14 AND the previous bar's low <= shelf high + flip distance × ATR14; OR volume > 0 AND low <= prior low AND bar close position >= 0.65 AND VSA volume rank >= 60; OR volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND VSA spread rank >= 60 AND VSA volume rank >= 80 AND bar close position >= 0.45; OR volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND VSA volume >= VSA peak volume AND VSA spread rank >= 70; OR volume > 0 AND close < previous close AND VSA spread rank <= 35 AND VSA volume rank <= 35.
Short: an active shelf has been touched, its first approach was from below (the previous close was below its dwell-weighted center), and close < shelf low - flip distance × ATR14 AND the previous bar's high >= shelf low - flip distance × ATR14; OR volume > 0 AND high >= prior high AND bar close position <= 0.35 AND VSA volume rank >= 60; OR volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND VSA volume rank >= 60; OR volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND VSA volume >= VSA peak volume AND VSA spread rank >= 70; OR volume > 0 AND close > previous close AND VSA spread rank <= 35 AND VSA volume rank <= 35.
