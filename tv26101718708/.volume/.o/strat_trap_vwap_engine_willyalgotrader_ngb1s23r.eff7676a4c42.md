Calculation: STRAT bar type = inside if high <= prior high and low >= prior low; outside if high > prior high and low < prior low; 2U if high > prior high and low >= prior low; otherwise 2D if low < prior low and high <= prior high.
Calculation: PVTE regime = selected moving average of close with ATR bands; enter bullish on close crossing above the upper outer band and bearish on close crossing below the lower outer band; bullish becomes neutral on close crossing below the lower inner band, bearish becomes neutral on close crossing above the upper inner band; outer bands use a longer ATR lookback than the basis lookback.
Calculation: Trade risk = distance from entry price to initial stop; targets are entry price plus or minus trade risk times their respective configurable R multiples, according to position side.
Long: confirmed pattern sequence is 3-2U, 2D-2U, 2U-2U, 2D-1-2U, 2U-1-2U, 2D-1-3, 1-2D-2U, 3-2D-2U, or 2U-2U-2D-2U; OR an outside bar closes above the prior high (extended pattern); OR STRAT trigger is pending bullish and high exceeds the setup bar high before close falls below its low and before expiry; OR STRAT type is 2D and close > open. Entry signals require flat position and, when enabled, bullish PVTE regime (or neutral if neutral entries are allowed), entry volume > its longer-lookback volume average times a configurable factor (unless volume is unavailable), the configured minimum count of visible higher-timeframe forming bars with close >= open, and risk within the configured ATR cap.
Short: confirmed pattern sequence is 3-2D, 2U-2D, 2D-2D, 2U-1-2D, 2D-1-2D, 2U-1-3, 1-2U-2D, 3-2U-2D, or 2D-2D-2U-2D; OR an outside bar closes below the prior low (extended pattern); OR STRAT trigger is pending bearish and low falls below the setup bar low before close rises above its high and before expiry; OR STRAT type is 2U and close < open. Entry signals require flat position and, when enabled, bearish PVTE regime (or neutral if neutral entries are allowed), entry volume > its longer-lookback volume average times a configurable factor (unless volume is unavailable), the configured minimum count of visible higher-timeframe forming bars with close <= open, and risk within the configured ATR cap.
Exit: long, high >= third target OR low <= current stop, except a same-bar touch of the first or third target takes priority over the stop; after the first target is touched, current stop is entry price.
Exit: short, low <= third target OR high >= current stop, except a same-bar touch of the first or third target takes priority over the stop; after the first target is touched, current stop is entry price.
