Calculation: Doji high/low = high and low of the most recent bar whose absolute body divided by its range is at most the doji body fraction; range must be positive
Calculation: EMA stack = four EMAs of close with strictly increasing lookbacks, from fastest to slowest; fast EMAs are the two shorter-lookback averages and slow EMAs the two longer-lookback averages
Calculation: ATR-normalized EMA30 distance = (close − slowest EMA) / ATR; ATR is the average true range over a shorter lookback than the baseline ATR used below
Calculation: Reversal score = round(min(100, EMA direction score + ATR stretch score + momentum fade score + EMA-distance-limit proximity score)); EMA direction score is 25 for a fully ascending or descending EMA stack, 15 if (fast EMA 1 > slow EMA 1 and fast EMA 2 > slow EMA 2) or both inequalities are reversed, otherwise 5; ATR stretch score is 25 × min(|ATR-normalized EMA30 distance| / 3, 1); momentum fade score is 25 if the absolute gap between the two fastest EMAs is smaller than on the previous bar, otherwise 0; EMA-distance-limit proximity score is 25 × min(max(|distance| / |long distance limit|, |distance| / |short distance limit|), 1)
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative sum of (typical price × volume) / cumulative volume, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3; VWAP standard deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative volume-weighted typical-price squared / cumulative volume − VWAP²)
