Calculation: Global VWAP and bands = within each configured reset period, volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of typical price (high+low+close)/3; bands are VWAP ± standard deviation × configured multipliers.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current-timeframe low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current-timeframe high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Buy pressure and sell pressure = volume-weighted buy/sell shares formed by averaging close-in-range shares and candle-body shares in hybrid mode, or using either share method in its selected mode; optionally blend 78% of each share with 22% of a flow share derived from the normalized change in close since the prior bar (65%) and lower-minus-upper wick (35%).
Calculation: Big-trade threshold = configured fixed threshold, or the greater of 1 and the shorter-lookback average volume times a multiplier.
Calculation: Swing liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows, each requiring equal left and right lookback; use the latest unswept level on the relevant side.
Calculation: Trend bar = confirmed bar whose body is at least 1.10 times its 20-bar average, volume is at least its 20-bar average, largest wick is no more than the configured fraction of the body, and range touches Global VWAP or an enabled band within the configured ATR-based tolerance; consecutive trend bars must satisfy the configured minimum spacing.
Calculation: Doji bar = confirmed bar whose body/range is at most the configured maximum and volume is at least the configured fraction of its 20-bar average.
Long: A bullish macro setup has formed during a New York macro window from a current-timeframe bullish FVG whose middle candle touches Global VWAP or an enabled band and has an earlier opposite candle in the bounded lookback, plus a bullish or bearish FVG on the configured secondary timeframe formed wholly within that window; the current bar's range touches either FVG zone before that setup has completed OR a confirmed bar sweeps an unswept swing low and, within the allowed sequence period, a bullish candle closes above the first open of the consecutive bullish sequence OR a confirmed trend bar or doji bar closes at or above its open OR a confirmed bar qualifies for a buy print: buy pressure ≥ big-trade threshold, pressure dominance ≥ configured minimum, body percentage ≥ configured minimum, and candle bias permits the print (bullish/doji or non-strict bias, or bearish counter-print enabled with buy share ≥ configured counter-dominance) OR close ≥ open.
Short: A bearish macro setup has formed during a New York macro window from a current-timeframe bearish FVG whose middle candle touches Global VWAP or an enabled band and has an earlier opposite candle in the bounded lookback, plus a bullish or bearish FVG on the configured secondary timeframe formed wholly within that window; the current bar's range touches either FVG zone before that setup has completed OR a confirmed bar sweeps an unswept swing high and, within the allowed sequence period, a bearish candle closes below the first open of the consecutive bearish sequence OR a confirmed trend bar or doji bar closes below its open OR a confirmed bar qualifies for a sell print: sell pressure ≥ big-trade threshold, pressure dominance ≥ configured minimum, body percentage ≥ configured minimum, and candle bias permits the print (bearish/doji or non-strict bias, or bullish counter-print enabled with sell share ≥ configured counter-dominance) OR close < open.
