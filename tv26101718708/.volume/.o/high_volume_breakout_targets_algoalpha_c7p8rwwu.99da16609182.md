Calculation: Bearish zone = for two consecutive confirmed pivot highs no more than the maximum zone age apart, the later pivot high wick lies within the earlier pivot candle body; zone top is the higher wick high and zone bottom is the lower body top.
Calculation: Bullish zone = for two consecutive confirmed pivot lows no more than the maximum zone age apart, the later pivot low wick lies within the earlier pivot candle body; zone top is the higher body bottom and zone bottom is the lower wick low.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is a turning point with the selected number of bars on both sides; use completed bars.
Calculation: Zone height = zone top − zone bottom.
Calculation: TP3 = breakout close + position direction × zone height, where long direction is +1 and short direction is −1.
Long: On a completed bar, close > active bearish zone top AND [high − max(low, zone top)] / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ minimum breakout range percentage.
Short: On a completed bar, close < active bullish zone bottom AND [min(high, zone bottom) − low] / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ minimum breakout range percentage.
Exit: Long, high ≥ TP3 for that long breakout.
Exit: Short, low ≤ TP3 for that short breakout.
