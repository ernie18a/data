Calculation: Trend regime = Using the selected trend filter: EMA filter is up when close > EMA and EMA > its value at the shorter slope lookback ago, down when close < EMA and EMA < its value then; ADX/DMI filter is up when ADX > threshold and +DI > -DI, down when ADX > threshold and -DI > +DI; combined filter requires both filters to agree; otherwise no trend. EMA length is longer than the slope lookback; ADX and DI use the same length.
Calculation: Selected oscillator = One selected from RSI, stochastic, stochastic RSI (stochastic of RSI), Williams %R, CCI, MFI, WaveTrend, or Fisher Transform, using its selected lookback; stochastic, stochastic RSI, and WaveTrend use a selected smoothing length. WaveTrend = EMA of (hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel length)) / (0.015 × max(EMA(abs(hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, channel length)), channel length), minimum tick)), with average length twice the channel length, then smoothed. Fisher Transform = recursively smooth the normalized hl2 position in its lookback high-low range, clamp it to [-0.999, 0.999], then recursively smooth 0.5 × ln((1 + value) / (1 − value)).
Long: Trend regime is up AND selected oscillator crosses above the uptrend pullback level OR trend regime is no trend AND selected oscillator crosses above the oversold level
Short: Trend regime is down AND selected oscillator crosses below the downtrend pullback level OR trend regime is no trend AND selected oscillator crosses below the overbought level
