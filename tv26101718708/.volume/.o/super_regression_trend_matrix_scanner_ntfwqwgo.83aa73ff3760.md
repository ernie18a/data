Calculation: Regression trend bands = Fit a least-squares linear regression of close over a lookback; calculate RMSE as the square root of the sum of squared differences between each close and its corresponding regression value over the lookback, divided by lookback minus one. Raw bands are the current regression value plus or minus RMSE times a multiplier. Initialize trend bullish and bands at their raw values. While bullish, set the lower band to the greater of its raw value and prior lower band, and the upper band to its raw value; while bearish, set the upper band to the lesser of its raw value and prior upper band, and the lower band to its raw value. Change bullish trend to bearish when close falls below the lower band; change bearish trend to bullish when close rises above the upper band; otherwise retain the trend.
Long: Prior trend is bearish AND close > upper band
Short: Prior trend is bullish AND close < lower band
