Calculation: Mean = selected moving average of the configured source over the mean lookback
Calculation: Extreme bands = mean ± selected deviation: standard deviation of the source over the deviation lookback × multiplier; ATR over the ATR lookback × multiplier; or the average of those two deviations
Calculation: Bullish candle rejection = lower wick / max(body, minimum price increment) ≥ wick-to-body threshold AND upper wick / bar range × 100 ≤ maximum opposite-wick percentage
Calculation: Bearish candle rejection = upper wick / max(body, minimum price increment) ≥ wick-to-body threshold AND lower wick / bar range × 100 ≤ maximum opposite-wick percentage
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close ≥ EMA of higher-timeframe close for bullish bias, or ≤ it for bearish bias; uses the higher-timeframe value available with lookahead disabled (last completed bar historically, forming bar in real time)
Long: selected signal style is Touch Extreme and low ≤ lower band OR selected signal style is Reclaim Mean Direction and the lowest low over the extreme lookback ≤ lower band AND close > lower band AND close > open OR selected signal style is Candle Rejection and the lowest low over the extreme lookback ≤ lower band AND bullish candle rejection AND close > open; AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled; AND wick rejection is satisfied if required for Candle Rejection; AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous long signal; AND ADX ≤ maximum if the strong-trend filter is enabled; AND close ≥ regime EMA if the EMA regime filter is enabled and countertrend signals are disallowed; AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier if volume confirmation is enabled; AND ATR / its moving average ≤ maximum if the volatility expansion block is enabled; AND RSI ≤ oversold threshold if the RSI filter is enabled; AND MFI ≤ oversold threshold if the MFI filter is enabled; AND higher-timeframe close ≥ higher-timeframe EMA if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled in Same Side Only mode
Short: selected signal style is Touch Extreme and high ≥ upper band OR selected signal style is Reclaim Mean Direction and the highest high over the extreme lookback ≥ upper band AND close < upper band AND close < open OR selected signal style is Candle Rejection and the highest high over the extreme lookback ≥ upper band AND bearish candle rejection AND close < open; AND bar is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled; AND wick rejection is satisfied if required for Candle Rejection; AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous short signal; AND ADX ≤ maximum if the strong-trend filter is enabled; AND close ≤ regime EMA if the EMA regime filter is enabled and countertrend signals are disallowed; AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier if volume confirmation is enabled; AND ATR / its moving average ≤ maximum if the volatility expansion block is enabled; AND RSI ≥ overbought threshold if the RSI filter is enabled; AND MFI ≥ overbought threshold if the MFI filter is enabled; AND higher-timeframe close ≤ higher-timeframe EMA if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled in Same Side Only mode
