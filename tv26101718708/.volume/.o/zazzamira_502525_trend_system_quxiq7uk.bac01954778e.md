Calculation: Opening-range height = opening-range high − opening-range low
Calculation: Long Fibonacci extension levels = opening-range high + configured extension ratios × opening-range height
Calculation: Short Fibonacci extension levels = opening-range low − configured extension ratios × opening-range height
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest high since entry − configured offset; the offset changes from an ATR-based offset to range-based offsets as price reaches the first and second long Fibonacci extension levels
Calculation: Short trailing stop = lowest low since entry + configured offset; the offset changes from an ATR-based offset to range-based offsets as price reaches the first and second short Fibonacci extension levels
Long: close crosses above the opening-range high AND outside the opening-range session AND during the entry session
Long: close crosses above the opening-range high AND the short-to-long ordered moving averages are strictly ascending AND current high is below the first long Fibonacci extension level AND during the entry session
Long: current low is below the opening-range high AND the prior bar had an active long breakout setup; a setup becomes active on the preceding long breakout condition and resets if high exceeds the first long Fibonacci extension level
Short: close crosses below the opening-range low AND outside the opening-range session AND during the entry session
Short: close crosses below the opening-range low AND the short-to-long ordered moving averages are strictly descending AND current low is above the first short Fibonacci extension level AND during the entry session
Short: current high is above the opening-range low AND the prior bar had an active short breakout setup; a setup becomes active on the preceding short breakout condition and resets if low falls below the first short Fibonacci extension level
Exit: Long, current low is below the long trailing stop OR current high reaches or exceeds a long Fibonacci target OR the forced-close session begins
Exit: Short, current high is above the short trailing stop OR current low reaches or falls below a short Fibonacci target OR the forced-close session begins
