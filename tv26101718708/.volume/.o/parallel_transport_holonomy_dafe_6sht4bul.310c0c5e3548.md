Calculation: Long/short signal score = directional structural base score plus directional confirmation score, reduced by liquidity, price-impact, danger, and structural-confidence adjustments; long is positive and short is negative.
Calculation: Trend direction = EMA(9) above/below EMA(50); trend strength = |EMA(9) − EMA(50)| / ATR(14).
Calculation: Entry stop and targets = entry price ± ATR(14) times the side- and setup-adjusted stop/target distances; first target is halfway to the final target. After first target, move stop to entry.
Long: Confirmed bar after warmup AND no active position AND long signal score > 0 AND (adjusted score reaches tier 1 or tier 2 threshold, cooldown has elapsed, counter-trend qualification holds, and (tier 2 OR relative volume ≥ its minimum)) AND RCM allows long AND RCM gate passes; OR continuation enabled AND no regular signal tier qualifies AND extended cooldown elapsed AND established uptrend AND relative volume ≥ its minimum AND trend strength > its minimum AND close > EMA(21) AND |close − EMA(21)| / ATR(14) < its maximum AND RSI(14) is within its continuation range AND pressure score is positive above its minimum AND long signal score > 0 AND positive transport persistence confirms the trend AND RCM allows long AND RCM gate passes.
Short: Confirmed bar after warmup AND no active position AND short signal score < 0 AND (adjusted score reaches tier 1 or tier 2 threshold, cooldown has elapsed, counter-trend qualification holds, and (tier 2 OR relative volume ≥ its minimum)) AND RCM allows short AND RCM gate passes; OR continuation enabled AND no regular signal tier qualifies AND extended cooldown elapsed AND established downtrend AND relative volume ≥ its minimum AND trend strength > its minimum AND close < EMA(21) AND |close − EMA(21)| / ATR(14) < its maximum AND RSI(14) is within its continuation range AND pressure score is negative beyond its minimum AND short signal score < 0 AND negative transport persistence confirms the trend AND RCM allows short AND RCM gate passes.
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ final target OR elapsed bars ≥ time limit OR a short signal or short continuation qualifies.
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ final target OR elapsed bars ≥ time limit OR a long signal or long continuation qualifies.
