Calculation: Ergotic MACD = EMA(close, longer lookback) − EMA(close, shorter lookback); signal line = EMA(Ergotic MACD, signal lookback)
Calculation: Custom MFI = RSI of the sum of volume × close over the lookback when close rises, and the sum of volume × close when close falls
Calculation: Price trend = close or its EMA, according to the configured source; “rising”/“falling” means higher/lower than the value the configured lookback bars ago
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest configured-timeframe HLC3 since it first reached entry price × (1 + trigger percentage), multiplied by (1 − stop percentage); use the latest available bar from that timeframe with lookahead off
Long: Within the configured date window AND Ergotic MACD < its signal line (retain the prior direction if equal) AND (long RSI trend filter disabled OR RSI rising over its configured lookback) AND (long MFI trend filter disabled OR custom MFI rising over its configured lookback) AND RSI <= its buy cutoff AND custom MFI <= its upper cutoff AND (long price trend filter disabled OR price trend rising over its configured lookback) AND long trailing stop not triggered
Short: Within the configured date window AND ((Ergotic MACD > its signal line (retain the prior direction if equal) AND RSI >= its sell cutoff AND custom MFI >= its lower cutoff AND (short price trend filter disabled OR price trend falling over its configured lookback)) OR long position open AND configured-timeframe HLC3 <= long trailing stop)
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)
