Calculation: Yang–Zhang volatility σ = square root of the nonnegative sum of the rolling variance of log(open / prior close), k times the rolling variance of log(close / open), and (1−k) times the rolling mean of the Rogers–Satchell term; k = 0.34 / (1.34 + (length+1)/(length−1)). Use the calculation bar selected for each chart bar: the forming bar by default, or the preceding completed bar when configured.
Calculation: Block geometric mean = sqrt(highest high × lowest low) over a block; block periods are consecutive, non-overlapping blocks ending at the calculation bar. For each block sequence, find the longest run of consecutive block means moving in the same direction from the newest block backward; compute the ICS angle as atan of the slope between the oldest and newest means in that run after transforming each mean to log(price)/σ. Trend direction is up or down by block-mean order, except it is flat when the absolute angle is within the configured range threshold.
Calculation: Structural channel rails = for the longest monotonic block run, find the block high maximum and minimum and block low maximum and minimum, and project two lines through the corresponding extreme points in ICS space to the calculation bar. In an uptrend, upper rail joins the minimum and maximum block highs; lower rail joins the minimum and maximum block lows. In a downtrend, upper rail joins the maximum and minimum block highs; lower rail joins the maximum and minimum block lows. In a flat trend, rails are the maximum block high and minimum block low. Use the primary block period for the active channel and the same group count, range threshold, and σ for each scale.
Calculation: Six-scale consensus = count of up, down, or flat trends from block periods 3, 7, 13, 19, 29, and 47, using the same group count, range threshold, and σ.
Calculation: Channel position = (log(close)−log(lower rail))/(log(upper rail)−log(lower rail)); one σ outside a rail means the absolute log-price distance from that rail divided by σ is at least 1.
Calculation: Fast and slow scale net directions = sign of the sum of trend directions for periods 3, 7, 13 and 19, 29, 47, respectively. Channel-width ratio = current (log(upper rail)−log(lower rail))/σ divided by its moving average over a longer lookback.
Long: Active channel trend changes to up from a non-up trend OR six-scale up consensus becomes 5 or 6 OR channel state enters upward breakout, upward retest, or confirmed upward breakout OR fast scale net direction is up while slow scale net direction is down OR channel-width ratio was compressed on the prior bar and is expanded now while active channel trend and fast scale net direction are up.
Short: Active channel trend changes to down from a non-down trend OR six-scale down consensus becomes 5 or 6 OR channel state enters downward breakout, downward retest, or confirmed downward breakout OR fast scale net direction is down while slow scale net direction is up OR channel-width ratio was compressed on the prior bar and is expanded now while active channel trend and fast scale net direction are down.
Exit: Long, no exit decision is defined.
Exit: Short, no exit decision is defined.
