Calculation: MTF bullish count = number of configured intraday and daily bars where the short-period EMA is above the long-period EMA; use their current bar values, including a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when higher-timeframe close > short-period EMA > long-period EMA; bearish when close < short-period EMA < long-period EMA. Use the dynamically selected higher timeframe’s current bar, including a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks; a pivot becomes available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure change = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high while prior structure is bearish / close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low while prior structure is bullish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a bullish/bearish three-bar fair-value gap with current volume above its moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order-block zone = the high-to-low range of the candle two bars before a bullish/bearish displacement, when that candle is bearish/bullish and its volume exceeds the current volume moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Inverted fair-value-gap zone = a prior bullish gap inverted when close falls below its lower boundary, or a prior bearish gap inverted when close rises above its upper boundary; retain only gaps meeting a configurable ATR size threshold.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high and low; if either is unavailable, use the current bar’s typical price.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish momentum triggers = any of stochastic K crossing above/below stochastic D, RSI crossing above/below its configurable threshold, MACD histogram crossing above/below zero, or close crossing above/below the fast EMA.
Long: On a confirmed bar, momentum signals enabled, sufficient history, cooldown elapsed, and session allowed if the session filter is enabled; bullish momentum trigger; and selected trend filter passes (close above EMA50, Supertrend bullish, at least three of five MTF bars bullish, or no trend requirement).
Short: On a confirmed bar, momentum signals enabled, sufficient history, cooldown elapsed, and session allowed if the session filter is enabled; bearish momentum trigger; and selected trend filter passes (close below EMA50, Supertrend bearish, at most two of five MTF bars bullish, or no trend requirement); and no long signal on that bar.
Long: While flat, after a bullish order-block or inverted-gap zone is tapped (low <= zone top), a confirmed bullish candle closes at or above the zone midpoint within the confirmation window; enabled setup class agrees with higher-timeframe trend classification; and enabled structure, discount-location, and entry-volume filters pass.
Short: While flat, after a bearish order-block or inverted-gap zone is tapped (high >= zone bottom), a confirmed bearish candle closes at or below the zone midpoint within the confirmation window; enabled setup class agrees with higher-timeframe trend classification; and enabled structure, premium-location, and entry-volume filters pass.
Long: While flat, an active equal-low liquidity pool is swept (low <= pool level), then within the confirmation window a bullish structure change occurs on a bullish candle; enabled setup class agrees with higher-timeframe trend classification and the enabled entry-volume filter passes.
Short: While flat, an active equal-high liquidity pool is swept (high >= pool level), then within the confirmation window a bearish structure change occurs on a bearish candle; enabled setup class agrees with higher-timeframe trend classification and the enabled entry-volume filter passes.
Exit: Long, momentum-signal state is long and MACD histogram crosses below zero, or close crosses below the fast EMA, or the selected trend filter flips bearish.
Exit: Short, momentum-signal state is short and MACD histogram crosses above zero, or close crosses above the fast EMA, or the selected trend filter flips bullish.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop (initially zone bottom minus ATR buffer; moved to entry after target 1), or high >= target 3, or trade duration exceeds its class limit, or bearish structure change, or enabled smart exit occurs, or opposite momentum signal occurs when flip is enabled.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop (initially zone top plus ATR buffer; moved to entry after target 1), or low <= target 3, or trade duration exceeds its class limit, or bullish structure change, or enabled smart exit occurs, or opposite momentum signal occurs when flip is enabled.
Calculation: Smart exit = MACD histogram crosses against the position, or opposite displacement occurs, or (position is profitable, current and prior volume are below their moving averages, and the current candle closes against the position).
Calculation: Target 1/2/3 = the first/second/third distinct price levels beyond entry plus an ATR buffer in trade direction, selected from confirmed and prior swing highs/lows, 1-hour and 4-hour equilibrium, point of control, prior day/week/month highs/lows, and active liquidity pools; if unavailable, use successive equal risk-distance targets, where risk is the greater of entry-to-stop distance and 0.2 ATR.
