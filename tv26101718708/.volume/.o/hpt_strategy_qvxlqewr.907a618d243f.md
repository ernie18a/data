Calculation: Smoothed stochastic %K = the stochastic oscillator smoothed over a lookback; smoothed %D = smoothed %K smoothed again, using the same smoothing lookback. Overbought and oversold thresholds are parameters.
Calculation: Trend direction = sum, over a lookback range of earlier bars, +1 when current close ≥ that bar’s close and −1 otherwise; direction is +1 when the sum exceeds the range length, −1 when it is below the negative range length, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: ADX = Wilder-smoothed directional movement strength: calculate upward movement from changes in highs and downward movement from changes in lows; retain only the positive dominant movement, smooth each over the same lookback, divide each by smoothed true range, then calculate the Wilder-smoothed absolute difference of the two percentages divided by their sum (use 1 if the sum is 0), multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Trend regime = trend direction when current and prior ADX are at least the ADX threshold and current ADX exceeds the highest ADX of the prior three bars; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = simple moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Prior-bar midpoint = (prior high + prior low) / 2.
Calculation: Breakout offset threshold = highest-high lookback offset from the prior bar plus half the prior bar’s close standard deviation over the breakout lookback; downside threshold uses the lowest-low offset instead.
Long: (Trend regime = 0 AND prior close > prior-bar midpoint AND smoothed %D crosses above the oversold threshold AND smoothed %K > smoothed %D) OR (Trend regime ≠ 0 AND prior close > prior-bar midpoint AND prior open < prior-bar midpoint AND fast moving average > slow moving average AND close > fast moving average) OR (close > breakout offset threshold AND volume > its simple moving average over the breakout lookback times a volume threshold)
Short: (Trend regime = 0 AND prior close > prior-bar midpoint AND smoothed %D crosses below the overbought threshold AND smoothed %K < smoothed %D) OR (Trend regime ≠ 0 AND prior close < prior-bar midpoint AND prior open > prior-bar midpoint AND fast moving average < slow moving average AND close < fast moving average) OR (close < downside breakout offset threshold AND volume > its simple moving average over the breakout lookback times a volume threshold)
Exit: long, smoothed %D crosses below the overbought threshold AND smoothed %K < smoothed %D OR fast moving average < slow moving average AND close < fast moving average AND prior close < prior-bar midpoint AND prior open > prior-bar midpoint OR five bars have elapsed since a long breakout condition OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction)
Exit: short, smoothed %D crosses above the oversold threshold AND smoothed %K > smoothed %D OR fast moving average > slow moving average AND close > fast moving average AND prior close > prior-bar midpoint AND prior open < prior-bar midpoint OR five bars have elapsed since a short breakout condition OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction)
