Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the session’s configured opening-range build period; use the completed range during that session’s trade period.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish fair-value gap = current high < low two bars earlier; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. Use the latest gap of each direction formed since the session began.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Long: Valid configured session and chart interval AND opening range available and within its configured maximum size AND session trade limit not reached AND a bullish candle’s body high > opening-range high + configured breakout buffer AND, on a later bar, price touches the opening-range high or overlaps the latest bullish fair-value gap
Long: Valid configured session and chart interval AND opening range available and within its configured maximum size AND session trade limit not reached AND a bullish breakout as defined above AND a subsequent retest has been found AND a later confirmed bullish candle closes above the breakout candle’s body high AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average)
Short: Valid configured session and chart interval AND opening range available and within its configured maximum size AND session trade limit not reached AND a bearish candle’s body low < opening-range low − configured breakout buffer AND, on a later bar, price touches the opening-range low or overlaps the latest bearish fair-value gap
Short: Valid configured session and chart interval AND opening range available and within its configured maximum size AND session trade limit not reached AND a bearish breakout as defined above AND a subsequent retest has been found AND a later confirmed bearish candle closes below the breakout candle’s body low AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average)
