Calculation: Support and resistance = most recently confirmed pivot low and high; pivot uses left and right lookbacks, and becomes available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Support/resistance zone = pivot price ± its percentage tolerance.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish pin bar = respectively, lower/upper wick is at least the wick-ratio multiple of the body and at least twice the opposite wick; body and candle range are positive.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish engulfing = current candle is respectively bullish/bearish, prior candle is respectively bearish/bullish, current body spans the prior open and close, and current body is at least the configured percentage of prior body.
Calculation: Volume delta = sum over the volume lookback of volume on candles with close ≥ open, minus volume on candles with close < open.
Calculation: Stop and targets at entry = for long, stop is the latest pivot low below entry with a percentage buffer, otherwise entry minus ATR multiple; TP1 is the latest pivot high above entry, otherwise entry plus ATR multiple; TP2 is the second latest pivot high above TP1, otherwise entry plus a larger ATR multiple, and must be above TP1. For short, reverse the pivot and price comparisons and use the corresponding ATR offsets; TP2 must be below TP1.
Long: Flat position AND ((bullish pin bar AND high is within the support zone) OR (bullish engulfing AND high is within the support zone) OR (close crosses above the resistance zone upper boundary)) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND fast/slow EMA distance ≥ minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < overbought threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × threshold AND volume delta > 0) AND (strength filter disabled OR candle body ≥ ATR × minimum AND body/range percentage ≥ minimum AND volume > volume moving average × spike multiple AND (confirmation candle disabled OR prior close > prior open)).
Short: Flat position AND ((bearish pin bar AND low is within the resistance zone) OR (bearish engulfing AND low is within the resistance zone) OR (close crosses below the support zone lower boundary)) AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND fast/slow EMA distance ≥ minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > oversold threshold) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close ≤ VWAP) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × threshold AND volume delta < 0) AND (strength filter disabled OR candle body ≥ ATR × minimum AND body/range percentage ≥ minimum AND volume > volume moving average × spike multiple AND (confirmation candle disabled OR prior close < prior open)).
Exit: long, low ≤ stop or high ≥ TP1 or high ≥ TP2.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop or low ≤ TP1 or low ≤ TP2.
