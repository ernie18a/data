Calculation: Confirmed structure swing high/low = a high/low fractal using equal numbers of bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot of each side as its unbroken candidate, replacing it when a newer same-side pivot confirms.
Calculation: Structure break = the candidate high/low is exceeded by the close or by the bar’s high/low, according to the selected break method; if both directions break on one bar, the candle direction selects the break.
Calculation: Bullish/Bearish Shift = an upward/downward structure break when the preceding structure break was downward/upward, respectively. A first break or a break in the same direction is a continuation.
Calculation: Bullish/Bearish Pivot MA = the selected moving average of lows/highs, activated by a confirmed low/high pivot that meets the selected pivot qualification. It is active when unbroken, close is at/above (bullish) or at/below (bearish) the MA, and the MA is rising/falling; only one side can be active, with a downward bar or new bearish pivot giving the bearish side priority if both qualify.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of the selected price source, resetting at the selected session, week, or month boundary; use the current bar’s value.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close; use the current bar’s value.
Long: Bullish Shift OR bullish continuation OR first bullish MA retest after a Bullish Shift OR latest Shift is Bullish AND bullish Pivot MA is active
Short: Bearish Shift OR bearish continuation OR first bearish MA retest after a Bearish Shift OR latest Shift is Bearish AND bearish Pivot MA is active
Exit: Long, bearish Shift OR bullish Pivot MA break (price crosses below it using the selected close or wick method)
Exit: Short, bullish Shift OR bearish Pivot MA break (price crosses above it using the selected close or wick method)
