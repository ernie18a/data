Calculation: Bollinger Bands = use hlc3 as the source; upper/lower bands are its rolling SMA plus/minus a parameterized multiplier times its rolling standard deviation.
Calculation: Momentum = close minus close from a parameterized number of bars earlier; momentum change = current momentum minus prior-bar momentum.
Calculation: Aroon up/down = 100 × (lookback length + highestbars(high)/lowestbars(low) offset, respectively) ÷ lookback length, using a window of lookback length + 1 bars.
Calculation: Schaff Trend Cycle = normalize the fast EMA minus slow EMA to 0–100 over a rolling lookback; smooth recursively with factor 1.5; normalize that smoothed series to 0–100 over the same lookback; smooth recursively again with factor 1.5. When a normalization range is zero, carry forward its prior value.
