Calculation: Lunar momentum = sin(2π × lunar age / synodic month), where lunar age is elapsed time since a known new moon modulo the synodic month.
Calculation: Bull momentum score = weighted sum of: 24 if close > trend EMA, fast EMA > slow EMA, and fast EMA rising; 8 if close > slow EMA; 16 if RSI ≥ bull RSI threshold; 5 if RSI > bull RSI threshold + 4; 16 if MACD histogram > 0 and nondecreasing; 10 if close > session VWAP; 11 if relative volume ≥ its threshold, close > open, close is in the upper 40% of the bar, and body size ≥ 0.22 ATR; 10 if the higher-timeframe filter is off or bullish. Clamp to 0–100. EMA lookbacks are ordered fast < slow < trend.
Calculation: Bear momentum score = weighted sum of the mirrored bearish criteria: 24 if close < trend EMA, fast EMA < slow EMA, and fast EMA falling; 8 if close < slow EMA; 16 if RSI ≤ bear RSI threshold; 5 if RSI < bear RSI threshold − 4; 16 if MACD histogram < 0 and nonincreasing; 10 if close < session VWAP; 11 if relative volume ≥ its threshold, close < open, close is in the upper 40% from the bar low, and body size ≥ 0.22 ATR; 10 if the higher-timeframe filter is off or bearish. Clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Lunar long score = max(lunar momentum, 0) × 100; lunar short score = max(−lunar momentum, 0) × 100.
Calculation: Long confluence = clamp(0.74 × bull momentum score + 0.26 × lunar long score + 5 when a new moon or first-quarter phase begins with positive lunar momentum, to 0–100).
Calculation: Short confluence = clamp(0.74 × bear momentum score + 0.26 × lunar short score + 5 when a full moon or last-quarter phase begins with negative lunar momentum, to 0–100).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend EMA and higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA; bearish = higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe trend EMA and higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Bar is confirmed AND direction permits long AND cooldown since the prior long signal has elapsed AND lunar momentum > its positive threshold AND bull momentum score ≥ its minimum AND long confluence ≥ its minimum AND (close > highest high over the breakout lookback excluding the current bar AND close > open OR lowest low over the pullback lookback ≤ fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND close > prior close) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe bullish).
Short: Bar is confirmed AND direction permits short AND cooldown since the prior short signal has elapsed AND lunar momentum < negative threshold AND bear momentum score ≥ its minimum AND short confluence ≥ its minimum AND (close < lowest low over the breakout lookback excluding the current bar AND close < open OR highest high over the pullback lookback ≥ fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND close < prior close) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe bearish).
Exit: long, low ≤ stop set at entry to lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR buffer OR high ≥ second target set at entry to entry price plus the second risk multiple times the greater of entry price minus stop and 10 minimum ticks OR short decision.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop set at entry to highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR buffer OR low ≤ second target set at entry to entry price minus the second risk multiple times the greater of stop minus entry price and 10 minimum ticks OR long decision.
