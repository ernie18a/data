Calculation: Adaptive regression model = for each candidate lookback, fit an ordinary least-squares line to close prices from oldest to newest (x = 0 to lookback−1); calculate R² and residual sigma = sqrt(sum of squared residuals / max(lookback−2, 1)); keep candidates with R² at or above the minimum, then select the one maximizing R² × ln(lookback) + 0.35 × abs(slope) / ATR − 0.10 × residual sigma / ATR
Calculation: Centerline = selected regression line’s value at the newest bar
Calculation: Core and outer bands = centerline ± selected residual sigma × the respective core or outer multiplier
Calculation: Residual Z = (close − centerline) / selected residual sigma
Long: selected regression slope > 0 AND ((close > lower core band AND previous close ≤ previous lower core band AND (EMA bias gate disabled OR close ≥ EMA) AND (RSI gate disabled OR RSI ≥ 50) AND (volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold)) OR (close crosses above centerline AND Residual Z ≤ 0.75 AND (EMA bias gate disabled OR close ≥ EMA) AND (volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold)) OR (close crosses below upper outer band AND previous close > previous upper outer band))
