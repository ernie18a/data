Calculation: Direction score = weighted combination of centered RSI, signed ADX (positive when +DI ≥ −DI), close relative to a short moving average normalized by ATR, short-minus-long moving-average distance normalized by ATR, ROC normalized by its rolling standard deviation, and the difference between rising-close and falling-close counts over the lookback; long moving average uses a longer lookback than the short one.
Calculation: Confidence score = bounded smoothed combination of normalized direction score, quality score, and Bollinger Band extremity; quality score combines the ratio of longer-lookback ATR to shorter-lookback ATR, volume relative to its moving average, Bollinger Band width relative to ATR, and absolute signed ADX. Use the current chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Adaptive trend state = ratcheting Supertrend state using current chart-timeframe ATR and median price (high + low)/2, with band width scaled inversely by confidence. While bullish, ratchet the lower band upward; while bearish, ratchet the upper band downward. Flip bearish when close falls below the ratcheted lower band by a confidence-adjusted ATR buffer, or when ATR exceeds its longer-lookback baseline by a volatility-spike threshold and centered RSI changes sign to negative with sufficient magnitude and direction score is negative. Flip bullish by the corresponding upper-band cross, or on that regime shift with positive RSI sign and nonnegative direction score.
Long: adaptive trend state was bullish on the previous completed bar and bearish two bars ago.
Short: adaptive trend state was bearish on the previous completed bar and bullish two bars ago.
Exit: long, adaptive trend state was bullish on the previous completed bar and bearish two bars ago OR adaptive trend state was bearish on the previous completed bar and bullish two bars ago.
