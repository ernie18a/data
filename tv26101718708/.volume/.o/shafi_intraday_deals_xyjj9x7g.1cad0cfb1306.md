Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of close from the start of the current session through the current bar
Long: shorter-lookback EMA of close crosses above longer-lookback EMA of close AND close > Session VWAP
Short: shorter-lookback EMA of close crosses below longer-lookback EMA of close AND close < Session VWAP
Exit: long, shorter-lookback EMA of close crosses below longer-lookback EMA of close AND close < Session VWAP
Exit: short, shorter-lookback EMA of close crosses above longer-lookback EMA of close AND close > Session VWAP
