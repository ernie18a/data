Calculation: Key levels = enabled daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour 50- and 200-bar simple moving averages of close, using the last completed bar of each timeframe; previous calendar-day high and low; previous completed configured session high and low; 1-hour Fibonacci levels at 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, and 0.786 between the latest confirmed 1-hour pivot low and pivot high; and active 1-hour pivot highs and lows that formed when 1-hour volume exceeded both its moving average and the preceding 1-hour volume. Remove a pivot high after a bar closes above it, a pivot low after a bar closes below it, or either after its configured maximum age.
Calculation: VWAP slope = session VWAP minus session VWAP at the configured shorter lookback.
Calculation: Strong candle = absolute(close − open) / (high − low) meets the configured minimum; when enabled, volume must also exceed its configured moving average.
Long: For at least one enabled key level L: ((low ≤ L + configured touch buffer AND close > L) OR (previous close < L AND close > L)) AND close > open AND enabled candle-strength and volume filters pass; AND the configured VWAP bias is off, or close > session VWAP for price mode, or VWAP slope > 0 for slope mode, or both for combined mode; AND the corresponding short condition is false.
Short: For at least one enabled key level L: ((high ≥ L − configured touch buffer AND close < L) OR (previous close > L AND close < L)) AND close < open AND enabled candle-strength and volume filters pass; AND the configured VWAP bias is off, or close < session VWAP for price mode, or VWAP slope < 0 for slope mode, or both for combined mode; AND the corresponding long condition is false.
