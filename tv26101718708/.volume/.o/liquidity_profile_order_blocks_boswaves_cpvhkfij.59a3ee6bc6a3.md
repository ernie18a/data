Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish order block = after a confirmed swing low, current close minus the pivot bar's low is at least the ATR at that pivot times the impulse threshold; its zone is built from the configured formation bars ending at the pivot, with top equal to the highest open or close and bottom equal to the lowest low, then its height is clamped around its midpoint to the configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples at the pivot.
Calculation: Bearish order block = after a confirmed swing high, the pivot bar's high minus current close is at least the ATR at that pivot times the impulse threshold; its zone is built from the configured formation bars ending at the pivot, with top equal to the highest high and bottom equal to the lowest open or close, then its height is clamped around its midpoint to the configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples at the pivot.
Calculation: Armed order block = a confirmed bar has overlapped the block since it was created or since its previous confirmation; overlap means high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top.
Long: an armed bullish order block overlaps the current bar AND close > zone midpoint AND (previous close <= zone midpoint OR low <= zone midpoint).
Short: an armed bearish order block overlaps the current bar AND close < zone midpoint AND (previous close >= zone midpoint OR high >= zone midpoint).
