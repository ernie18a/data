Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows of the chosen number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = volume-weighted average price, reset at the start of each day.
Calculation: Structure stop = the lower of entry price minus one minimum tick and the greater of (support recorded for the trade minus an ATR buffer) and (entry price minus a maximum ATR distance); use entry price minus the maximum ATR distance as the raw stop if structure stops are disabled.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus a configurable ATR multiple of ATR captured at entry.
Long: While waiting for support, a confirmed pivot low sets support; then low <= support × (1 + tolerance) AND close > open AND close > previous bar high; then, after that reversal, a confirmed pivot low > support; then close > the reversal bar high; then volume >= its moving average over a longer lookback × a configurable multiplier AND ((close > EMA20 AND EMA9 > EMA20 AND EMA20 > EMA20 five bars earlier) OR (close > EMA200 AND EMA9 > EMA20 AND EMA20 > EMA50 AND EMA200 > EMA200 five bars earlier)) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > daily VWAP).
Exit: Long, low <= structure stop OR (take profit enabled AND high >= profit target) OR (EMA20 exit enabled AND close < EMA20) OR (support-break exit enabled AND close < support recorded for the trade) OR (maximum holding period enabled AND bars since entry >= its limit).
