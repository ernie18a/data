Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of the first regular-session bar(s) beginning at 09:30 ET; use completed bars
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of the first regular-session bar(s) beginning at 09:30 ET; use completed bars
Calculation: Opening-range average volume = mean volume of those opening-range bars; use completed bars
Long: close > opening-range high AND volume ≥ volume multiplier × opening-range average volume AND close > session VWAP AND bar time is after the opening range and before 11:30 ET AND no long signal has fired that session
Short: close < opening-range low AND volume ≥ volume multiplier × opening-range average volume AND close < session VWAP AND bar time is after the opening range and before 11:30 ET AND no short signal has fired that session
