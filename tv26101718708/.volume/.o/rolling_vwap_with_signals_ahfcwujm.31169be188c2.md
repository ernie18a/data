Calculation: Rolling VWAP = over the trailing time window, sum of (hlc3 × volume) divided by sum of volume on the selected RVWAP timeframe, including its still-forming bar; require at least 10 bars
Calculation: Rolling standard deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3 around the rolling VWAP over the same window and timeframe, including the still-forming bar; require at least 10 bars
Calculation: Upper band = rolling VWAP + standard-deviation multiplier × rolling standard deviation
Calculation: Lower band = rolling VWAP − standard-deviation multiplier × rolling standard deviation
Calculation: Band width = (upper band − lower band) / rolling VWAP
Calculation: Average band width = mean band width over a trailing 24-hour window on chart-timeframe bars; require at least 10 bars
Calculation: Smoothed ATR = 4-sample symmetric weighted moving average of chart-timeframe ATR
Calculation: Average volume = mean volume over a trailing 24-hour window on chart-timeframe bars; require at least 10 bars
Calculation: Up-bar volume mean = mean volume over the longer lookback of chart-timeframe bars whose close is above open; require at least 3 qualifying bars
Calculation: Down-bar volume mean = mean volume over the same lookback of chart-timeframe bars whose close is below open; require at least 3 qualifying bars
Long: close crosses above upper band AND smoothed ATR > its value on the prior chart-timeframe bar AND close > upper band AND rolling VWAP > its value one RVWAP-timeframe interval earlier (prior completed interval) AND volume > average volume × relative-volume threshold AND up-bar volume mean on the prior chart-timeframe bar > down-bar volume mean on the prior chart-timeframe bar AND band width on the prior chart-timeframe bar ≤ average band width on that bar × narrow-band threshold
Short: close crosses below lower band AND smoothed ATR > its value on the prior chart-timeframe bar AND close < lower band AND rolling VWAP < its value one RVWAP-timeframe interval earlier (prior completed interval) AND volume > average volume × relative-volume threshold AND down-bar volume mean on the prior chart-timeframe bar > up-bar volume mean on the prior chart-timeframe bar AND band width on the prior chart-timeframe bar ≤ average band width on that bar × narrow-band threshold
