Calculation: Up/down trend = close is above/below its value at the efficiency-ratio lookback, and absolute displacement divided by summed absolute close changes exceeds an ATR-adjusted threshold.
Calculation: Overextension score = weighted sum of normalized distance beyond EMA21 and VWAP, structure exhaustion, money-flow and breadth extremes, opposing order flow, low relative volume, rejection wick, distance from latest high-volume zone, Bollinger free bar, RSI extreme, VWAP band breach, and opposing SMT divergence; long and short use mirrored downside/upside components.
Calculation: Pivot structure = latest confirmed pivot high/low compared with the preceding confirmed pivot high/low; bullish structure requires higher highs and higher lows, bearish structure lower lows and lower highs.
Calculation: RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while RSI makes a lower pivot high, or price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low; hidden divergence is the reverse pivot relationship.
Calculation: Delta divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while cumulative order-flow delta falls over its comparison window, or price makes a lower pivot low while cumulative delta rises; strong divergence also requires matching RSI divergence.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price exceeds the prior lookback high/low and closes back below/above it, with qualifying wick, relative volume, range expansion, and wick significance.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the midpoint of the highest high, lowest low, and average close over the same lookback; squeeze fires when Bollinger Bands move out of Keltner Channels after being inside.
Calculation: HTF vote = weighted directional votes from fast versus slow EMA, close versus the midpoint of the recent high-low range, and close versus fast EMA on 5-, 15-, and 60-minute bars; use the bar still forming. Use 4-hour fast/slow EMA on the bar still forming for the 4-hour trend filter. Daily pivots use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Long/short confidence = count of the respective overextension, mean-reversion regime, divergence, volume climax, rejection, band extreme, momentum reversal, breadth, structure break, SMT divergence, and reversal-candle conditions, with context-dependent weights.
Calculation: Long stop = recent swing low, optionally extended to a nearby completed RTH low, less an ATR-scaled buffer, constrained to a bounded ATR distance; short stop mirrors this above the recent swing high/RTH high. Sweep entries extend the stop beyond the sweep extreme.
Calculation: Long target = confidence-selected nearer, midpoint, or farther valid target above entry among EMA21, VWAP, pivot, session midpoints, footprint value levels, and EMA100, scaled by context; short target mirrors this below entry.
Long: (uptrend score condition for displayed bullish signals: bullish delta divergence OR bullish RSI divergence OR bullish absorption at long overextension OR qualifying volume-zone support retest OR qualifying low sweep OR bullish CHoCH OR bullish SMT OR bullish squeeze release OR bullish momentum flip) OR (longs enabled AND liquidity adequate AND downtrend AND long overextension AND long confidence at least one AND no blocking hidden bearish divergence AND HTF vote above its long veto AND ADX above minimum AND (fade confirmation count reaches its context-adjusted minimum OR delta-divergence confirmation count reaches its context-adjusted minimum OR low-sweep plus order-flow confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum OR aggressive-buy flow plus order-flow confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum OR volume-zone support retest plus confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum) AND target distance meets minimum profit and risk/reward floor).
Short: (uptrend score condition for displayed bearish signals: bearish delta divergence OR bearish RSI divergence OR bearish absorption at short overextension OR qualifying volume-zone resistance retest OR qualifying high sweep OR bearish CHoCH OR bearish SMT OR bearish squeeze release OR bearish momentum flip) OR (shorts enabled AND liquidity adequate AND uptrend AND short overextension AND short confidence reaches its context-adjusted minimum AND no blocking hidden bullish divergence AND HTF vote below its short veto AND ADX above minimum AND (fade confirmation count reaches its context-adjusted minimum OR delta-divergence confirmation count reaches its context-adjusted minimum OR high-sweep plus order-flow confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum OR aggressive-sell flow plus order-flow confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum OR volume-zone resistance retest plus confirmations reach their context-adjusted minimum) AND target distance meets minimum profit and risk/reward floor).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
