Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume on bars closing at or above their open minus volume on bars closing below their open; if no intrabars are available, use the chart bar's volume with the same direction rule
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of intrabar delta, reset at each configured session anchor
Calculation: Volume z-score = (current volume − rolling mean volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume
Calculation: Buy imbalance = buy volume / chart-bar volume; sell imbalance = sell volume / chart-bar volume
Calculation: Bullish absorption = volume ≥ its shorter-lookback average × a threshold AND high − low ≤ ATR × a threshold AND intrabar delta < 0 AND close ≥ (high + low) / 2
Calculation: Bearish absorption = volume ≥ its shorter-lookback average × a threshold AND high − low ≤ ATR × a threshold AND intrabar delta > 0 AND close ≤ (high + low) / 2
Calculation: Session VWAP = running volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at each configured session anchor
Calculation: Rolling value mean = rolling volume-weighted mean of (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Bullish OTE zone = the retracement interval between configurable fractions of the latest confirmed swing high-to-low leg, when swing bias is bullish; swing bias is bullish when the latest swing high and low are both higher than their prior counterparts, or when only the latest swing low is higher
Calculation: Bearish OTE zone = the retracement interval between configurable fractions of the latest confirmed swing low-to-high leg, when swing bias is bearish; swing bias is bearish when the latest swing high and low are both lower than their prior counterparts, or when only the latest swing high is lower
Calculation: Higher-timeframe OTE zones = bullish or bearish OTE zones calculated separately on two higher-timeframe series, using their latest confirmed swing pivots and the same retracement fractions; use the higher-timeframe bars as they develop
Calculation: Bullish IFVG retest = a qualifying bearish gap has flipped bullish, remains valid, and low ≤ its upper boundary while close > its upper boundary
Calculation: Bearish IFVG retest = a qualifying bullish gap has flipped bearish, remains valid, and high ≥ its lower boundary while close < its lower boundary
Calculation: Bull score = count of: intrabar delta > 0; cumulative delta > its value several bars earlier; close > session VWAP; close > rolling value mean; volume z-score > a threshold; buy imbalance or bullish absorption
Calculation: Bear score = count of: intrabar delta < 0; cumulative delta < its value several bars earlier; close < session VWAP; close ≤ rolling value mean; volume z-score > a threshold; sell imbalance or bearish absorption
Long: buy imbalance ≥ its threshold on this and the prior bar OR bullish absorption OR close enters the bullish OTE zone OR bullish IFVG retest OR (confirmed bar AND bull score ≥ the configured minimum AND bull score > bear score AND (location gate disabled OR close is in bullish OTE zone OR bullish IFVG retest OR close is in either bullish higher-timeframe OTE zone) AND bars since the previous signal > cooldown)
Short: sell imbalance ≥ its threshold on this and the prior bar OR bearish absorption OR close enters the bearish OTE zone OR bearish IFVG retest OR (confirmed bar AND bear score ≥ the configured minimum AND bear score > bull score AND (location gate disabled OR close is in bearish OTE zone OR bearish IFVG retest OR close is in either bearish higher-timeframe OTE zone) AND bars since the previous signal > cooldown)
