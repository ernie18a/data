Calculation: Frozen ATR = ATR on the bar immediately before the impulse candle
Calculation: Acceptance boundary = impulse low + hold fraction × (impulse high − impulse low) for a bullish impulse; impulse high − hold fraction × (impulse high − impulse low) for a bearish impulse
Calculation: Required extension = impulse high + extension multiple × frozen ATR for a bullish impulse; impulse low − extension multiple × frozen ATR for a bearish impulse
Calculation: Follow-through efficiency = max(0, directional progress from impulse close to current close) ÷ sum of absolute close-to-close changes since the impulse × 100
Long: confirmed bullish candle with high − low ≥ minimum range multiple × frozen ATR, |close − open| ÷ (high − low) ≥ minimum body fraction, close > open, and (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≥ 1 − extreme fraction; if the volume filter is enabled, volume ≥ minimum relative volume × its moving average; OR an active bullish impulse has lasted at least the required number of bars, the required number of consecutive closes are above its acceptance boundary, high reaches its required extension, and follow-through efficiency ≥ minimum efficiency
Short: confirmed bearish candle with high − low ≥ minimum range multiple × frozen ATR, |close − open| ÷ (high − low) ≥ minimum body fraction, close < open, and (close − low) ÷ (high − low) ≤ extreme fraction; if the volume filter is enabled, volume ≥ minimum relative volume × its moving average; OR an active bearish impulse has lasted at least the required number of bars, the required number of consecutive closes are below its acceptance boundary, low reaches its required extension, and follow-through efficiency ≥ minimum efficiency
Exit: long, active bullish impulse and close < its impulse low or acceptance boundary
Exit: short, active bearish impulse and close > its impulse high or acceptance boundary
