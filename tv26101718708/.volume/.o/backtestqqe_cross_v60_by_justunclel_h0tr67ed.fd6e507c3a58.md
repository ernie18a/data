Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Fast QQE line = a trailing RSI band built around Smoothed RSI; band distance is EMA(EMA(abs(change in Smoothed RSI), 2 × RSI lookback − 1), 2 × RSI lookback − 1) × QQE factor. The lower band trails upward by taking the greater of its previous value and the current lower band while both current and prior Smoothed RSI are above the previous lower band; otherwise it resets to the current lower band. The upper band trails downward by taking the lesser of its previous value and the current upper band while both current and prior Smoothed RSI are below the previous upper band; otherwise it resets to the current upper band. The Fast QQE line is the lower band in the upward trend and the upper band in the downward trend; trend switches upward when Smoothed RSI crosses the previous upper band and downward when the previous lower band crosses Smoothed RSI.
Long: Smoothed RSI crosses above the Fast QQE line OR Smoothed RSI crosses up through 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses up through 50 + threshold OR the selected entry signal occurs on the previous completed bar AND its enabled entry filters pass (with the optional first-signal-in-trend restriction)
Short: Smoothed RSI crosses below the Fast QQE line OR Smoothed RSI crosses down through 50 OR Smoothed RSI crosses down through 50 − threshold OR the selected entry signal occurs on the previous completed bar AND its enabled entry filters pass (with the optional first-signal-in-trend restriction)
Exit: long, selected bearish QQE exit signal occurs
Exit: short, selected bullish QQE exit signal occurs
