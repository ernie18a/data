Calculation: Source = close or same-timeframe Heikin Ashi close, as configured
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursively set from the prior stop (or current source if uninitialized): if current and prior source are above prior stop, max(prior stop, current source − key value × ATR); if both are below, min(prior stop, current source + key value × ATR); otherwise current source − key value × ATR when current source is above prior stop, else current source + key value × ATR
Calculation: Candle dominance = (close − low) / (high − low) for buying, or (high − close) / (high − low) for selling; when high equals low, both are 0.5
Calculation: Volume fading = volume is lower than each of the preceding configured number of bars
Long: long signals are permitted AND position is not long AND source > ATR trailing stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close > slow EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) AND (candle dominance filter disabled OR buying candle dominance ≥ configured threshold) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier) AND (breakout filter is Off OR (Close Break Only and close > prior high and close > open) OR (Strict and low > prior open and close > prior high and close > open))
Short: short signals are permitted AND position is not short AND source < ATR trailing stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close < slow EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA) AND (candle dominance filter disabled OR selling candle dominance ≥ configured threshold) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier) AND (breakout filter is Off OR (Close Break Only and close < prior low and close < open) OR (Strict and high < prior open and close < prior low and close < open))
Exit: long, source < ATR trailing stop OR (volume fade exit enabled AND volume fading AND close < open)
Exit: short, source > ATR trailing stop OR (volume fade exit enabled AND volume fading AND close > open)
