Calculation: Swing and internal pivot highs/lows = alternating structure-leg extremes confirmed using the longer and shorter lookbacks, respectively; a new bearish leg confirms a pivot high at the lookback bars ago when that bar’s high exceeds the highest high of the intervening bars, and a new bullish leg confirms a pivot low when that bar’s low is below the lowest low of the intervening bars.
Calculation: H1 EMAs = exponential moving averages of close using longer and shorter lookbacks, on the current forming H1 bar in real time and the last completed H1 bar historically.
Calculation: M5 EMA = exponential moving average of close, on the current forming M5 bar in real time and the last completed M5 bar historically.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken swing pivot high OR close crosses above the latest unbroken internal pivot high OR the shorter-lookback H1 EMA is above the longer-lookback H1 EMA AND M5 close is above the M5 EMA AND close crosses above the latest unbroken internal pivot high when internal structure bias was bearish AND volume is above its shorter-lookback simple moving average.
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken swing pivot low OR close crosses below the latest unbroken internal pivot low OR the shorter-lookback H1 EMA is below the longer-lookback H1 EMA AND M5 close is below the M5 EMA AND close crosses below the latest unbroken internal pivot low when internal structure bias was bullish AND volume is above its shorter-lookback simple moving average.
