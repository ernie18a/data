Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the 30 minutes after 18:00 ET, locked for the following session
Calculation: Opening range midpoint = (opening range high + opening range low) / 2
Calculation: Long stop = min(opening range low − stop buffer, entry price − ATR × stop multiplier)
Calculation: Short stop = max(opening range high + stop buffer, entry price + ATR × stop multiplier)
Calculation: Breakout long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio
Calculation: Breakout short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward-to-risk ratio
Long: During the session after the opening range is locked, opening range size is within its minimum and maximum, trade limit is not reached, and position is flat, AND ((close > opening range high + breakout buffer AND previous close <= opening range high + breakout buffer AND RSI > breakout-long threshold AND volume > its moving average × breakout-volume multiplier AND close > EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) OR (high >= opening range high − reversion buffer AND close < opening range high AND previous close >= opening range high AND RSI > overbought threshold AND volume > its moving average × fade-volume multiplier AND close < EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar))
Long: During the session after the opening range is locked, opening range size is within its minimum and maximum, AND low <= opening range low + reversion buffer AND close > opening range low AND previous close <= opening range low AND RSI < oversold threshold AND volume > its moving average × fade-volume multiplier AND close > EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Short: During the session after the opening range is locked, opening range size is within its minimum and maximum, trade limit is not reached, and position is flat, AND ((close < opening range low − breakout buffer AND previous close >= opening range low − breakout buffer AND RSI < breakout-short threshold AND volume > its moving average × breakout-volume multiplier AND close < EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) OR (low <= opening range low + reversion buffer AND close > opening range low AND previous close <= opening range low AND RSI < oversold threshold AND volume > its moving average × fade-volume multiplier AND close > EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar))
Short: During the session after the opening range is locked, opening range size is within its minimum and maximum, AND high >= opening range high − reversion buffer AND close < opening range high AND previous close >= opening range high AND RSI > overbought threshold AND volume > its moving average × fade-volume multiplier AND close < EMA of higher-timeframe close from the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= (fade entry ? opening range midpoint : breakout long target) OR session ends
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= (fade entry ? opening range midpoint : breakout short target) OR session ends
