Calculation: Smoothed stochastic RSI = the smoothed stochastic of RSI(close), using the selected timeframe’s latest completed close.
Calculation: Stochastic SuperTrend direction = a recursive direction state based on the smoothed stochastic RSI: form upper and lower bands at the stochastic RSI plus or minus a multiplier, trail each band using the prior band and prior stochastic RSI, then set direction to bullish when the prior state was upper-band-based and the stochastic RSI exceeds the upper band, or when it was lower-band-based and the stochastic RSI falls below the lower band; otherwise retain the corresponding bearish or bullish state.
Calculation: EVEREX oscillator = normalize volume, absolute candle body, and absolute close change by their respective simple moving averages over a common lookback. Normalization assigns 100 times 1.00, 0.90, 0.80, 0.70, 0.60, 0.50, 0.25, or 0.10 when the input/average ratio is respectively >1.50, >1.20, >1.00, >0.80, >0.60, >0.40, >0.20, or otherwise. Multiply normalized volume by the mean of six signed measures: close location within the candle range; body divided by candle range; normalized absolute body with the sign of the body; close location within the two-bar high-low range; close change divided by that range; and normalized absolute close change with the sign of the change. Split this flow into positive and negative parts, take a weighted moving average of each, transform their ratio with 2×(100−100/(1+positive/negative))−100, then smooth it with a weighted moving average. Use current chart bars.
Long: EVEREX oscillator > its prior value AND its prior value ≤ the value before that AND (Stochastic SuperTrend direction changes to bullish with smoothed stochastic RSI < 50 on this bar OR it changed to bullish 1–2 bars ago AND smoothed stochastic RSI > its prior value).
Short: EVEREX oscillator ≤ its prior value AND its prior value > the value before that AND (Stochastic SuperTrend direction changes to bearish with smoothed stochastic RSI > 50 on this bar OR it changed to bearish 1–2 bars ago AND smoothed stochastic RSI < its prior value).
