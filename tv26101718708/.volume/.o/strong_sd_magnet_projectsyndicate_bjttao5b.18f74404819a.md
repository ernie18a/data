Calculation: Supply and demand zones = confirmed swing highs and lows using equal left/right lookbacks; center an ATR-sized band on the pivot price. A zone is tested when a bar’s high-low range overlaps its band; it is invalid when a close (or, under the selected invalidation rule, a wick) passes beyond the band.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted mean of capped departure impulse, formation volume relative to its volume moving average, rejection-wick fraction, and freshness (decreasing with retests), multiplied by an idle-time decay factor and scaled to 0–10.
Calculation: Eligible source and target = unbroken zones among the top-scoring displayed zones of the same session; source has at least the required retests and meets the source score threshold; target meets the target score threshold and is the nearest qualifying opposite zone within the maximum ATR distance. Retain only the highest-scoring source-target pulls up to the per-session limit.
Long: Demand source zone is eligible AND supply target zone is eligible AND supply target midpoint > demand source midpoint
Short: Supply source zone is eligible AND demand target zone is eligible AND demand target midpoint < supply source midpoint
