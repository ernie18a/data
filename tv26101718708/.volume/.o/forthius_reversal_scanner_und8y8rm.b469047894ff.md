Calculation: Bullish pattern = any of: bullish engulfing (previous candle bearish; current bullish; current close ≥ previous open; current open ≤ previous close; current body > previous body × engulfing factor; both bodies > minimum body); morning star (candle two bars ago bearish with body > 2 × minimum body; previous body/range < doji limit; current bullish with body > 2 × minimum body and close above the midpoint of the candle two bars ago; bearish gap condition); three advancing bullish candles (all bullish, successively higher closes, each later open within the prior body, each body > minimum body); hammer (body/range ≤ body fraction limit, lower wick ≥ ATR × wick factor, upper wick ≤ body × wick fraction limit, range ≥ ATR × range factor); piercing pattern (previous bearish candle with body > minimum body; current bullish with body > minimum body, open below previous close or previous low when true-gap mode is enabled, close above previous body midpoint but below previous open); bullish tweezer (previous bearish, current bullish, lows within ATR × tweezer tolerance, current lower wick ≥ body).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = any of: bearish engulfing (previous bullish; current bearish; current close ≤ previous open; current open ≥ previous close; current body > previous body × engulfing factor; both bodies > minimum body); evening star (candle two bars ago bullish with body > 2 × minimum body; previous body/range < doji limit; current bearish with body > 2 × minimum body and close below the midpoint of the candle two bars ago; bullish gap condition); three declining bearish candles (all bearish, successively lower closes, each later open within the prior body, each body > minimum body); shooting star (body/range ≤ body fraction limit, upper wick ≥ ATR × wick factor, lower wick ≤ body × wick fraction limit, range ≥ ATR × range factor); dark-cloud pattern (previous bullish candle with body > minimum body; current bearish with body > minimum body, open above previous close or previous high when true-gap mode is enabled, close below previous body midpoint but above previous open); bearish tweezer (previous bullish, current bearish, highs within ATR × tweezer tolerance, current upper wick ≥ body).
Calculation: Reversal context = for bullish engulfing, piercing, or bearish-to-bullish tweezer, the prior bar is in a downtrend; for morning star, the bar two ago is in a downtrend; for three advancing candles, the bar three ago is in a downtrend; for hammer, the current bar is in a downtrend. Reverse these directions and offsets for bearish patterns. Downtrend = close below the shorter-period EMA, shorter-period EMA below longer-period EMA, and the shorter-period EMA change over three bars divided by (ATR × 3) below the negative slope threshold. Uptrend uses the reverse comparisons and positive slope threshold.
Calculation: Pattern quality = for engulfing, add 2 if current body / previous body ≥ the higher body-ratio threshold, else 1 if ≥ the lower threshold; add 2 if close is in the outermost close-to-extreme fraction of the candle in the signal direction, else 1 if it is in the next outer fraction; cap at 3. For hammer/shooting star, add 2 or 1 according to whether the relevant wick / ATR meets the higher or lower wick-quality threshold; add 1 if the body is in the signal-side portion of the range and 1 if the opposite wick ≤ body × its quality fraction; cap at 3. For morning/evening star, add 2 or 1 according to whether the previous body/range meets the tighter or looser doji-quality threshold; add 2 or 1 according to whether penetration of the first candle meets the higher or lower penetration threshold; cap at 3. For piercing/dark-cloud, assign 3, 2, 1, or 0 by descending penetration thresholds. For three-candle patterns, add 2 or 1 according to whether the mean body/range meets the higher or lower body-quality threshold, and add 1 if mean close location meets the close-quality threshold; cap at 3. For tweezers, add 2 or 1 according to whether the low/high distance divided by ATR meets the tighter or looser tolerance; add 1 if the relevant wick/body ratio meets its quality threshold; cap at 3.
Calculation: Confluence = sum of five Boolean checks: volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the volume multiplier; price is within ATR × proximity tolerance of the latest confirmed pivot low (bullish) or pivot high (bearish), using the configured pivot lookback on both sides; RSI is below the bullish RSI threshold or above the bearish RSI threshold; ADX exceeds the minimum; and directional divergence holds (bullish: low below the prior lookback lowest low and RSI above the prior lookback lowest RSI; bearish: high above the prior lookback highest high and RSI below the prior lookback highest RSI).
Calculation: Pattern volume score = 2 if current volume / 20-bar average is at least the higher volume-ratio threshold, else 1 if at least the lower threshold, else 0 for hammer/shooting star; use current volume / previous volume for engulfing, piercing, dark-cloud, or tweezer; use current volume / mean of the two previous volumes for star or three-candle patterns.
Calculation: Signal strength = selected pattern quality + confluence + pattern volume score. If multiple patterns match in one direction, select the first in this order: engulfing, star, three-candle, hammer/shooting star, piercing/dark-cloud, tweezer.
Long: bullish pattern AND bullish reversal context AND signal strength ≥ configured minimum strength for selected grade AND confirmed bar when confirmation is enabled AND more bars have elapsed since the previous bullish signal than the configured same-direction cooldown.
Short: bearish pattern AND bearish reversal context AND signal strength ≥ configured minimum strength for selected grade AND confirmed bar when confirmation is enabled AND more bars have elapsed since the previous bearish signal than the configured same-direction cooldown.
