Calculation: Adaptive baseline = blend EMA(close, long lookback) with KAMA(close, shorter lookback), weighting toward KAMA by the efficiency ratio; smooth the blend with an EMA. Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[lookback]) / sum(abs(close − prior close), lookback). KAMA smoothing constant = (ER × (2/(fast period+1) − 2/(slow period+1)) + 2/(slow period+1))².
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(50 + 50 × tanh(((ATR − SMA(ATR, lookback)) / stdev(ATR, lookback)) / 2), 0, 100).
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions: bullish regime; close above session VWAP; close below rolling-range midpoint or lower quartile; RSI above its midpoint threshold, fast EMA above slow EMA, and smoothed close-open volume pressure above its lower threshold; volatility score within bounds and ATR as a percent of close below its bound; bullish sweep reclaim or previous-day-low reclaim; VWAP bounce or channel reclaim or squeeze release; bullish bias from the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Short score = count of true conditions: bearish regime; close below session VWAP; close above rolling-range midpoint or upper quartile; RSI below its midpoint threshold, fast EMA below slow EMA, and smoothed close-open volume pressure below its upper threshold; volatility score within bounds and ATR as a percent of close below its bound; bearish sweep reclaim or previous-day-high rejection; VWAP rejection or channel rejection or squeeze release; bearish bias from the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish regime = close above rising adaptive baseline and fast EMA above slow EMA, excluding range regime. Bearish regime = close below falling adaptive baseline and fast EMA below slow EMA, excluding range regime. Range regime = ADX below its threshold and volatility score below its bound.
Calculation: Bullish sweep reclaim = low below the highest low of the prior shorter lookback, close back above that low and above open, and volume above its moving average times a threshold. Bearish sweep reclaim = high above the highest high of the prior shorter lookback, close back below that high and below open, and volume above its moving average times a threshold. Daily-level reclaims/rejections compare low/close with the previous completed daily low, or high/close with the previous completed daily high.
Calculation: VWAP bounce = low at/below session VWAP minus one rolling standard deviation of typical-price deviation, close back above that level and above open. VWAP rejection is the inverse at the upper band. Channel reclaim/rejection = low below the lower channel and close back above it and above open, or high above the upper channel and close back below it and below open. Squeeze release = current bar is outside the squeeze after the prior bar was inside.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = last completed confirmation-timeframe close > its 21-period EMA > its 55-period EMA; bearish bias = close < 21-period EMA < 55-period EMA.
Long: Confirmed bar AND (session restriction disabled OR inside configured session) AND cooldown elapsed AND volatility gate AND (compression avoidance disabled OR no active squeeze) AND daily equity drawdown below its limit (or guard disabled) AND planned reward/risk at least its minimum AND close above initial stop AND long score at least its minimum AND (close crosses above adaptive baseline OR close crosses above session VWAP OR bullish sweep reclaim OR previous-day-low reclaim OR VWAP bounce OR channel reclaim) AND position is flat or short.
Short: Confirmed bar AND (session restriction disabled OR inside configured session) AND cooldown elapsed AND volatility gate AND (compression avoidance disabled OR no active squeeze) AND daily equity drawdown below its limit (or guard disabled) AND planned reward/risk at least its minimum AND close below initial stop AND short score at least its minimum AND (close crosses below adaptive baseline OR close crosses below session VWAP OR bearish sweep reclaim OR previous-day-high rejection OR VWAP rejection OR channel rejection) AND position is flat or long.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= active target OR (regime-flip exit enabled AND confirmed bearish regime AND close < adaptive baseline) OR confirmed bars held >= maximum OR daily equity drawdown reaches its limit.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= active target OR (regime-flip exit enabled AND confirmed bullish regime AND close > adaptive baseline) OR confirmed bars held >= maximum OR daily equity drawdown reaches its limit.
