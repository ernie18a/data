Calculation: Hull average = selected HMA, EHMA, or THMA of close; HMA/EHMA use length L, THMA uses L/2
Calculation: QQE blue state = smoothed RSI minus 50 is above the upper Bollinger band of the QQE trailing line minus 50; the trailing line uses smoothed RSI and a smoothed absolute change scaled by a factor
Calculation: QQE red state = smoothed RSI minus 50 is below the lower Bollinger band of the QQE trailing line minus 50; the trailing line uses smoothed RSI and a smoothed absolute change scaled by a factor
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) / longer-lookback EMA of volume
Calculation: Swing low = lowest low over the configured lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Swing high = highest high over the configured lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Long risk fraction = (entry signal close − swing low) / entry signal close
Calculation: Short risk fraction = (swing high − entry signal close) / entry signal close
Long: flat position AND inside configured date range AND close > Hull average AND Hull average > its value 2 bars ago AND volume oscillator > 0 AND QQE blue state AND NOT QQE blue state on prior bar AND smoothed RSI2 − 50 > configured positive threshold AND NOT (smoothed RSI2 − 50 > configured positive threshold on prior bar) AND (minimum stop distance filter disabled OR entry signal close − swing low > configured minimum distance)
Short: flat position AND inside configured date range AND close < Hull average value 2 bars ago AND Hull average < its value 2 bars ago AND volume oscillator > 0 AND QQE red state AND NOT QQE red state on prior bar AND smoothed RSI2 − 50 < negative configured threshold AND NOT (smoothed RSI2 − 50 < negative configured threshold on prior bar) AND (minimum stop distance filter disabled OR swing high − entry signal close > configured minimum distance)
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − long risk fraction) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + configured target-stop ratio × long risk fraction)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + short risk fraction) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − configured target-stop ratio × short risk fraction)
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + first target multiple × long risk fraction); after this target is reached, exit remaining position if low ≤ entry price OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + second target multiple × long risk fraction)
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − first target multiple × short risk fraction); after this target is reached, exit remaining position if high ≥ entry price OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − second target multiple × short risk fraction)
