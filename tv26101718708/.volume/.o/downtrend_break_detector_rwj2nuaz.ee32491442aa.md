Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high greater than the highs in the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars; it becomes available after the following bars complete.
Calculation: Descending resistance line = among valid candidates, choose the greatest-span line through two confirmed pivot highs or through an older confirmed pivot high and the current soft anchor (the highest high among the current and preceding pivot-lookback bars). The older anchor must exceed the newer anchor; span and percentage drop must meet minimums. Count highs between anchors exceeding the line by more than pierce tolerance; count intermediate confirmed pivot highs within touch tolerance below or pierce tolerance above the line. Require violations at or below the maximum, and touches between the minimum and maximum. The line value on each bar is its left-anchor value plus slope times bars since that anchor.
Calculation: Signal quality score = touch points (2: 8, 3: 18, 4: 28, 5 or more: 35); plus spacing points (standard deviation of gaps between touch bars divided by anchor span: <=0.05: 25, <=0.10: 20, <=0.18: 14, <=0.28: 8, otherwise 3); plus recency points (age of newest touch: <=5 bars: 25, <=15: 20, <=30: 14, <=60: 8, otherwise 3); plus higher-low points (latest confirmed pivot low > preceding pivot low by more than 2%: 15, otherwise greater: 8, otherwise 0; 0 if either is unavailable).
Long: A valid selected line has not fired, its right anchor is no older than the maximum age, close > latest confirmed pivot low * 0.95, signal quality score >= minimum, volume / its simple moving average >= surge threshold, and close > line value * (1 + break buffer) and close > open for each of the required consecutive bars (1 if score >= 65 and automatic confirmation is enabled, otherwise 2 if enabled, otherwise the configured confirmation count); this condition is true now and was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price minus configured risk, where risk is either a multiple of ATR or a percentage of entry price. Targets 1, 2, and 3 = entry price plus their configured ATR multiples or percentages.
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry, high >= target 3 OR low <= trailing stop; trailing stop starts at initial stop, moves to entry price after target 1 is reached, and moves to target 1 after target 2 is reached. If both exit conditions occur on the same bar, target 3 takes precedence.
