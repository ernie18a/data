Calculation: Absorption score = (current volume / SMA of volume over the volume/range averaging lookback) / max((high − low) / SMA of (high − low) over that same lookback, 0.05)
Calculation: Score percentile = percentile rank of the current absorption score over a ranking window including the current bar
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: PVSRA climax = current volume ≥ SMA of volume over the preceding completed PVSRA lookback bars × climax multiplier OR current volume × (high − low) ≥ the highest volume × (high − low) over those preceding completed bars
Calculation: Net intrabar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume where close > open, minus sum where close < open; split volume equally between both sums where close = open. Use lower-timeframe bars within the current bar.
Long: volume ≥ SMA of volume over the volume/range averaging lookback × minimum volume ratio AND absorption score ≥ minimum score floor AND score percentile ≥ minimum percentile AND (climax requirement disabled OR PVSRA climax) AND close position ≥ close threshold AND (sweep requirement disabled OR low < lowest low of the preceding completed sweep lookback bars) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < SMA of close over the trend lookback) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR net intrabar delta < 0) AND (no prior signal OR current bar index − last signal bar ≥ minimum bars between signals)
Short: volume ≥ SMA of volume over the volume/range averaging lookback × minimum volume ratio AND absorption score ≥ minimum score floor AND score percentile ≥ minimum percentile AND (climax requirement disabled OR PVSRA climax) AND close position ≤ 1 − close threshold AND (sweep requirement disabled OR high > highest high of the preceding completed sweep lookback bars) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > SMA of close over the trend lookback) AND (delta confirmation disabled OR net intrabar delta > 0) AND (no prior signal OR current bar index − last signal bar ≥ minimum bars between signals)
