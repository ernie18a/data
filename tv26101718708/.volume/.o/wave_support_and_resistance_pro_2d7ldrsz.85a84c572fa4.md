Calculation: Previous day high = high of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Previous day low = low of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Previous week high = high of the last completed weekly bar
Calculation: Previous week low = low of the last completed weekly bar
Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the same number of bars immediately before and after it; use the latest confirmed swing high
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low lower than the lows of the same number of bars immediately before and after it; use the latest confirmed swing low
Long: close > a resistance level AND previous close <= that level OR close > previous day high AND previous close <= previous day high OR close > previous week high AND previous close <= previous week high OR close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= latest confirmed swing high
Short: close < a support level AND previous close >= that level OR close < previous day low AND previous close >= previous day low OR close < previous week low AND previous close >= previous week low OR close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= latest confirmed swing low
