Calculation: Feature vector = configured subset of normalized RSI, WaveTrend, CCI, ADX, MFI and Fisher Transform features; compare current vector with stored historical vectors using the configured weighted distance metric (Lorentzian, Manhattan, Euclidean or their 50/50 hybrid).
Calculation: Prediction and confidence = distance-weighted vote and percent agreement of the nearest historical feature vectors. Each sample pairs features from a bar with the source-price move over the following horizon; label that move long or short only when it exceeds the neutral-zone threshold, otherwise neutral. Use the recent training window and configured chronological spacing; adapt neighbor count to recent ATR rank when enabled.
Calculation: Signal = long when prediction > 0 and confidence >= minimum confidence and enabled ATR-volatility, EMA-separation/ATR regime, and ADX filters pass; short when prediction < 0 and the same gates pass; otherwise retain the previous signal.
Calculation: Kernel estimates = rational-quadratic regression of source price and Gaussian regression of source price, using the configured lookback, relative weighting, regression level and lag. Bullish kernel = rational-quadratic estimate rising versus its prior bar, or Gaussian estimate >= rational-quadratic estimate in smoothing mode; bearish kernel = rational-quadratic estimate falling, or Gaussian estimate <= rational-quadratic estimate in smoothing mode. Bullish/bearish kernel changes are the respective slope reversals, or Gaussian/rational-quadratic crossovers in smoothing mode.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on long entry, close minus ATR times the configured multiplier, then the greater of the prior stop and current close minus ATR times the multiplier; on short entry, close plus ATR times the multiplier, then the lesser of the prior stop and current close plus ATR times the multiplier.
Long: Signal = long now and signal changed from the prior bar AND (if enabled, close > EMA AND close > SMA AND close > EMA of the current higher-timeframe bar) AND (if enabled, bullish kernel).
Short: Signal = short now and signal changed from the prior bar AND (if enabled, close < EMA AND close < SMA AND close < EMA of the current higher-timeframe bar) AND (if enabled, bearish kernel).
Exit: long, (dynamic exits enabled and valid: bearish kernel change and the most recent bearish kernel change is after the long entry) OR (otherwise: bars held = horizon and the signal at entry was long and its EMA/SMA trend filters passed at entry, OR bars held < horizon and a new short signal occurs and the signal at entry was long and its EMA/SMA trend filters passed at entry) OR (ATR trailing stop enabled and close < trailing stop)
Exit: short, (dynamic exits enabled and valid: bullish kernel change and the most recent bullish kernel change is after the short entry) OR (otherwise: bars held = horizon and the signal at entry was short and its EMA/SMA trend filters passed at entry, OR bars held < horizon and a new long signal occurs and the signal at entry was short and its EMA/SMA trend filters passed at entry) OR (ATR trailing stop enabled and close > trailing stop)
