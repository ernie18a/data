Calculation: Estimated buying volume = sum lower-timeframe volume where close > open, or a doji close is above its previous close; unresolved dojis inherit the last resolved direction, initially neutral.
Calculation: Estimated selling volume = sum lower-timeframe volume where close < open, or a doji close is below its previous close; unresolved dojis inherit the last resolved direction, initially neutral.
Calculation: Net volume share = (estimated buying volume − estimated selling volume) / total classified volume, including neutral volume.
Calculation: Relative volume = current total classified volume / average total classified volume on the immediately preceding 30 chart bars; all 30 bars must have valid source data.
Calculation: Prior ATR = ATR(14) on the last completed chart bar.
Calculation: Aligned body response = sign(net volume share) × (close − open) / prior ATR.
Calculation: Rolling flow mean = volume-weighted mean of lower-timeframe HLC3 over the selected chart-bar window, using completed chart bars; requires valid data on at least 80% of the window.
Calculation: Rolling pressure = (buying volume − selling volume) / total classified volume over the same rolling window as the flow mean.
Long: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its pressure threshold AND |net volume share| ≥ its pressure threshold AND prior ATR > 0 AND aligned body response ≥ its drive threshold AND net volume share > 0, subject to shared minimum spacing between raw events.
Short: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its pressure threshold AND |net volume share| ≥ its pressure threshold AND prior ATR > 0 AND aligned body response ≥ its drive threshold AND net volume share < 0, subject to shared minimum spacing between raw events.
Long: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its entry threshold AND net volume share ≥ its entry threshold AND aligned body response ≥ its drive threshold AND net volume share > 0 AND rolling pressure ≥ its entry threshold AND close > rolling flow mean AND rolling flow mean > its value at the selected earlier chart bar AND |close − rolling flow mean| / prior ATR ≤ its maximum; only while flat, after cooldown, with long side armed, and within the plan start time.
Short: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its entry threshold AND net volume share ≤ − its entry threshold AND aligned body response ≥ its drive threshold AND net volume share < 0 AND rolling pressure ≤ − its entry threshold AND close < rolling flow mean AND rolling flow mean < its value at the selected earlier chart bar AND |close − rolling flow mean| / prior ATR ≤ its maximum; only while flat, after cooldown, with short side armed, and within the plan start time.
Long: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its pressure threshold AND |net volume share| ≥ its pressure threshold AND prior ATR > 0 AND aligned body response ≤ its resisted-response ceiling AND net volume share > 0, subject to shared minimum spacing between raw events.
Short: valid source data AND relative volume ≥ its pressure threshold AND |net volume share| ≥ its pressure threshold AND prior ATR > 0 AND aligned body response ≤ its resisted-response ceiling AND net volume share < 0, subject to shared minimum spacing between raw events.
Exit: long position, open ≤ active stop OR low ≤ active stop OR open ≥ target OR high ≥ target OR a qualified short entry condition OR adverse-flow condition holds for the required consecutive closes OR maximum holding bars reached.
Exit: short position, open ≥ active stop OR high ≥ active stop OR open ≤ target OR low ≤ target OR a qualified long entry condition OR adverse-flow condition holds for the required consecutive closes OR maximum holding bars reached.
Calculation: Initial risk = prior ATR on the entry signal bar × selected risk multiple, rounded up to a whole tick with a two-tick minimum; initial stop is entry price minus (long) or plus (short) this risk, and target is entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk × target multiple.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, tightened only: after favorable excursion reaches its selected break-even threshold, move to entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk × buffer; after excursion reaches its selected trailing threshold, move to favorable extreme minus (long) or plus (short) risk × trailing multiple. Updated levels apply from the next bar.
Calculation: Adverse-flow condition = valid source data AND rolling flow mean is ready AND position-side × (close − rolling flow mean) < 0 AND position-side × rolling pressure ≤ − entry pressure threshold.
Exit: pending entry, the next bar does not provide valid OHLC prices, or is not the immediately following bar.
