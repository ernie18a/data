Calculation: Average daily body range = average of absolute close-minus-open over a lookback of completed daily bars
Calculation: Range distance = average daily body range × configured buffer multiplier, optionally divided by 2
Calculation: Daily anchored VWAP = cumulative (HLC3 × volume) / cumulative volume since the start of the day
Calculation: VWAP neutral buffer = confirmation-timeframe close × configured neutral-buffer percentage
Long: Average daily body range > 0 AND range distance ≥ configured minimum AND (VIX ≤ configured maximum AND VIX ≤ moving average of VIX closes over a lookback of completed daily bars, when applicable and enabled) AND (prior completed daily ATR / close × 100 ≤ configured maximum, when enabled) AND (prior completed daily ATR / average daily body range ≤ configured maximum, when enabled) AND (absolute difference between fast and slow close EMAs ≤ prior completed daily ATR × configured limit, when enabled) AND (external market fast close EMA ≥ external market slow close EMA, when enabled) AND (time is within the configured entry window, when enabled) AND (VWAP trade type is disabled OR confirmation-timeframe close > daily anchored VWAP + VWAP neutral buffer)
