Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentile rank of the rolling sum of true range over the accumulation lookback, ranked over the longer percentile lookback.
Calculation: Volume percentile = percentile rank of the rolling sum of volume over the same accumulation lookback, ranked over the same longer percentile lookback.
Long: Volatility percentile > its threshold AND Volume percentile > its threshold AND close > open
Short: Volatility percentile > its threshold AND Volume percentile > its threshold AND close <= open
