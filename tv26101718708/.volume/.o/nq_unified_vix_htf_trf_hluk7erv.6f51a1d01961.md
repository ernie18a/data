Calculation: Twin Range Filter (TRF) = average two smoothed ranges around a recursive range filter of close. Each smoothed range is EMA(EMA(abs(close − prior close), lookback), 2 × lookback − 1) × its multiplier; use separate shorter and longer lookbacks. The range filter holds its prior value unless close moves beyond it by the averaged range, then moves to close minus the range when rising or close plus the range when falling. Its recursively bounded upper band is filter + range, updated when that value falls below its prior value or prior close is above its prior value; its lower band is filter − range, updated when that value rises above its prior value or prior close is below its prior value. TRF switches between those bands according to whether close crosses the band it followed on the prior bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price minus (ATR × stop multiplier) for long positions, or entry price plus (ATR × stop multiplier) for short positions; ATR uses chart bars.
Calculation: Target = entry price plus (initial stop distance × reward/risk multiple) for long positions, or entry price minus that distance for short positions.
Calculation: Upper extension = maximum of the previous completed daily bar's low + its range × extension ratio and, once complete, the current day's 9:30–9:45 New York opening-range low + its range × extension ratio. Before the opening range is complete, use only the previous-day extension.
Calculation: Lower extension = minimum of the previous completed daily bar's low + its range × (1 − extension ratio) and, once complete, the current day's 9:30–9:45 New York opening-range low + its range × (1 − extension ratio). Before the opening range is complete, use only the previous-day extension.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until a long close is at or above the upper extension or a short close is at or below the lower extension; thereafter it is the entry price.
Long: Chart close crosses above chart-bar TRF AND the bar is confirmed AND the current forming 15-minute VIX close is below its TRF AND the prior completed 15-minute instrument close is above its TRF AND time is 9:30 a.m. through 11:59 a.m. New York time.
Short: Chart close crosses below chart-bar TRF AND the bar is confirmed AND the current forming 15-minute VIX close is above its TRF AND time is 9:30 a.m. through 11:59 a.m. New York time.
Exit: long, bar low <= active stop OR bar high >= target OR New York time is 12:00 p.m. or later OR a new day begins.
Exit: short, bar high >= active stop OR bar low <= target OR current forming 15-minute VIX close is below its TRF OR New York time is 12:00 p.m. or later OR a new day begins.
