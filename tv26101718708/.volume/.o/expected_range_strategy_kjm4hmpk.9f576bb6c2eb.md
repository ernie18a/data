Calculation: For each of ten progressively longer lookbacks Lᵢ, Rᵢ = highest(high, Lᵢ) − lowest(low, Lᵢ); Mᵢ = SMA((open+high+low+close)/4, Lᵢ). Use the last completed bar’s Rᵢ and Mᵢ to form Uᵢ = Mᵢ[1]+Rᵢ[1]/2 and Dᵢ = Mᵢ[1]−Rᵢ[1]/2; placeᵢ = −100×((close−Dᵢ)/(Uᵢ−Dᵢ)−0.5). Sentiment = Σ(weightᵢ×Rᵢ×placeᵢ/L₁₁₋ᵢ) / Σ(weightᵢ), pairing each lookback with its reverse-order lookback.
Calculation: Positive accumulation = if sentiment > 0, prior positive accumulation + sentiment − prior sentiment; otherwise 0. Negative accumulation = if sentiment < 0, prior negative accumulation − (sentiment − prior sentiment); otherwise 0.
Long: Negative accumulation > highest(positive accumulation, longest lookback) × long threshold AND sentiment has crossed zero at least once after more bars than the longest lookback AND within the configured date window AND no take-profit lockout (cleared by a raw short signal).
Short: Positive accumulation > highest(negative accumulation, longest lookback) × short threshold AND sentiment has crossed zero at least once after more bars than the longest lookback AND within the configured date window AND no take-profit lockout (cleared by a raw long signal).
Exit: Long, highest high since entry > entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction).
Exit: Short, lowest low since entry < entry price / (1 + take-profit fraction) OR high >= entry price / (1 − stop-loss fraction).
