Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low, confirmed after the same number of bars on each side; major and internal pivots use different lengths, with the internal length shorter.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: High volume = volume exceeds its moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Bull reversal score = 25 if price sweeps below the latest confirmed reversal swing low and closes back above it while below the trend EMA, otherwise 8 for that sweep alone; add 15 for bullish RSI divergence, 15 for bullish wick rejection, 12 for climax volume, 15 for a lower ATR-band excursion followed by a close back above the inner lower band, 10 for bullish micro-confirmation, 10 for a bullish Williams %R hook, 8 when enough of the three higher-timeframe structure trends are non-bearish, 5 near the latest reversal swing low, 5 for rising RSI below 45, 5 when the 50-bar up/down volume ratio is below 0.7, and 8 for a highest-volume or highest-in-year bar while below the trend EMA; cap at 100.
Calculation: Bear reversal score = 25 if price sweeps above the latest confirmed reversal swing high and closes back below it while above the trend EMA, otherwise 8 for that sweep alone; add 15 for bearish RSI divergence, 15 for bearish wick rejection, 12 for climax volume, 15 for an upper ATR-band excursion followed by a close back below the inner upper band, 10 for bearish micro-confirmation, 10 for a bearish Williams %R hook, 8 when enough of the three higher-timeframe structure trends are non-bullish, 5 near the latest reversal swing high, 5 for falling RSI above 55, 5 when the 50-bar up/down volume ratio exceeds 1.5, and 8 for a highest-volume or highest-in-year bar while above the trend EMA; cap at 100.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed major swing high while the current major structure trend is not bullish OR close crosses above the latest confirmed internal swing high OR high volume AND close >= open OR RSI > 50 OR bullish reversal score reaches its minimum threshold AND (a bullish sweep OR a close back inside the lower ATR band after extending below it OR a bullish failed breakdown) AND (bullish reversal allowed by the ADX filter) AND (no bullish reversal lock since the latest confirmed reversal swing low) AND (bull reversal cooldown elapsed) AND (bullish reversal CHoCH cooldown elapsed or a valid bullish sweep) AND (extension requirement satisfied) AND (bullish reversal score reaches its minimum threshold).
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed major swing low while the current major structure trend is not bearish OR close crosses below the latest confirmed internal swing low OR high volume AND close < open OR RSI <= 50 OR bearish reversal score reaches its minimum threshold AND (a bearish sweep OR a close back inside the upper ATR band after extending above it OR a bearish failed breakout) AND (bearish reversal allowed by the ADX filter) AND (no bearish reversal lock since the latest confirmed reversal swing high) AND (bear reversal cooldown elapsed) AND (bearish reversal CHoCH cooldown elapsed or a valid bearish sweep) AND (extension requirement satisfied) AND (bearish reversal score reaches its minimum threshold).
