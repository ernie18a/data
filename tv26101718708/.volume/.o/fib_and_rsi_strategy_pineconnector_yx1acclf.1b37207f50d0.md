Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Fibonacci center = volume-weighted moving average of typical price over a lookback
Calculation: Fibonacci deviation = multiplier × standard deviation of typical price over the same lookback
Calculation: Outer Fibonacci bands = Fibonacci center ± Fibonacci deviation
Calculation: Fibonacci target bands = Fibonacci center ± 0.764 × Fibonacci deviation
Long: low < lower outer Fibonacci band AND previous bar's RSI crosses above oversold AND high < lower Fibonacci target band AND year ≥ 2000
Short: high > upper outer Fibonacci band AND previous bar's RSI crosses below overbought AND low > upper Fibonacci target band AND year ≥ 2000
Exit: long, high > lower Fibonacci target band captured when the position opened OR low < close × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: short, low < upper Fibonacci target band captured when the position opened OR high > close × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
