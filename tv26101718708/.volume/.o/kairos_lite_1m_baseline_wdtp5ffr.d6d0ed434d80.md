Calculation: VFI = Sum over the lookback of signed, capped volume divided by the prior-bar volume average; sign each bar’s volume positive/negative/zero when the change in hlc3 exceeds the positive/negative cutoff (cutoff = coefficient × stdev of log(hlc3) changes × close); otherwise zero. VFI signal = EMA(VFI, shorter lookback).
Calculation: VSA initiative up/down = volume / its lookback average ≥ 1, body size / prior ATR ≥ threshold, candle direction up/down, and close in the corresponding outer portion of its range. VSA absorption up/down = relative volume ≥ threshold, body size / prior ATR ≤ threshold, corresponding rejection wick / range ≥ threshold, and close in the corresponding outer portion of its range.
Calculation: Signed candle-volume turn up/down = current candle volume fraction (up candle +1, down candle −1, doji 0) is positive/negative, or its change from the prior bar meets the configured turn threshold while the fraction is above −0.35 / below 0.35, respectively.
Calculation: Down/up sweep = price penetrates by at least the configured tick amount below/above any of the previous completed daily low/high, current session low/high, completed opening-range low/high, or latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low/high, then closes back above/below that level. Use prior completed daily bars; session and opening-range levels exclude the current bar when evaluating it.
Calculation: Confirmed 15-minute direction = direction from the latest completed 15-minute bar: +1 when its close breaks above the latest confirmed 15-minute pivot high, −1 when it breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low, otherwise retain its prior direction; direction is neutral if either pivot is missing or older than the configured maximum age. Pivots use equal left/right lookbacks.
Calculation: Long/short setup seed = qualifying continuation or reversal seed; retain its bar, extreme, trigger, sweep and excursion state. It expires after the configured maximum age or if price breaches its stored extreme by more than one tick. For continuation, use the first seed only; trigger is the greater of seed-bar high and prior structure high for longs, or the lesser of seed-bar low and prior structure low for shorts. The later first close through that trigger consumes the seed even if another gate blocks entry. For reversal, trigger is the prior structure high/low. Prior structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured short lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Prior ATR = ATR from the preceding chart-timeframe bar. VWAP deviation = (close − anchored VWAP) / max(VWAP standard deviation, one tick); anchored VWAP and deviation use the selected anchor and current chart-timeframe data.
Long: confirmed close and (continuation OR reversal) AND ready data, allowed session/context, no enabled news blackout, and all enabled common filters pass. Continuation = enabled and (15-minute direction is up) and a valid long continuation seed and a later close above its frozen trigger and close > open and body ≥ configured fraction of prior ATR and enough consecutive closes above VWAP from the seed and close above VWAP and VWAP deviation ≤ continuation limit and (sweep required is off OR seed was swept) and (flow filter is off OR VFI > 0 and VFI ≥ its signal and VFI slope ≥ minimum and VSA initiative up and no opposing VFI/VSA initiative) and (trend filter is off OR Supertrend is up and close > VWAP and deviation ≤ continuation limit) and (repeat filter is off OR a fresh qualifying pullback pivot occurred after the prior long signal). Reversal = enabled and (sweep requirement is off OR valid long seed was swept) and (flow filter is off OR no opposing VFI/VSA initiative and no strong opposing VSA initiative and a current or remembered VFI turn up, with the configured signed candle-volume turn condition) and (close reclaims VWAP, session midpoint, lower one-sigma VWAP band, or long structure trigger) and the seed had the configured downside VWAP excursion and current deviation is between −2 and 0.75 and (trend filter is off OR Supertrend is up) and close > open and body ≥ configured fraction of prior ATR and (repeat filter is off OR a fresh qualifying pullback pivot occurred after the prior long signal). Common optional volatility filter: prior ATR ≥ configured minimum, same-time relative volume ≥ configured minimum when available, and bar range ≤ configured multiple of prior ATR. If both directions qualify, emit neither.
Short: confirmed close and (continuation OR reversal) AND ready data, allowed session/context, no enabled news blackout, and all enabled common filters pass. Continuation = enabled and (15-minute direction is down) and a valid short continuation seed and a later close below its frozen trigger and close < open and body ≥ configured fraction of prior ATR and enough consecutive closes below VWAP from the seed and close below VWAP and VWAP deviation ≥ negative continuation limit and (sweep required is off OR seed was swept) and (flow filter is off OR VFI < 0 and VFI ≤ its signal and VFI slope ≤ negative minimum and VSA initiative down and no opposing VFI/VSA initiative) and (trend filter is off OR Supertrend is down and close < VWAP and deviation ≥ negative continuation limit) and (repeat filter is off OR a fresh qualifying pullback pivot occurred after the prior short signal). Reversal = enabled and (sweep requirement is off OR valid short seed was swept) and (flow filter is off OR no opposing VFI/VSA initiative and no strong opposing VSA initiative and a current or remembered VFI turn down, with the configured signed candle-volume turn condition) and (close reclaims below VWAP, session midpoint, upper one-sigma VWAP band, or short structure trigger) and the seed had the configured upside VWAP excursion and current deviation is between −0.75 and 2 and (trend filter is off OR Supertrend is down) and close < open and body ≥ configured fraction of prior ATR and (repeat filter is off OR a fresh qualifying pullback pivot occurred after the prior short signal). Common optional volatility filter: prior ATR ≥ configured minimum, same-time relative volume ≥ configured minimum when available, and bar range ≤ configured multiple of prior ATR. If both directions qualify, emit neither.
