Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset at each trading day
Calculation: VWAP slope = current session VWAP − previous bar’s session VWAP
Calculation: Neutral zone = a fraction of the distance between the current session VWAP and its upper standard-deviation band
Long: current bar is closed AND close > session VWAP + neutral zone AND VWAP slope > 0
Short: current bar is closed AND close < session VWAP − neutral zone AND VWAP slope < 0
