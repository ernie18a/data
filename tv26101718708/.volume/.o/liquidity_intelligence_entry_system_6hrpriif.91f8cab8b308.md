Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the same number of bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each type
Calculation: Upper/lower rejection ratio = upper/lower wick length divided by candle range, where candle range is max(high − low, minimum tick)
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = close >/< open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × minimum displacement-body fraction AND absolute(close − open) / candle range ≥ minimum body-to-range fraction
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high two bars earlier AND low − high two bars earlier ≥ ATR × minimum FVG-size fraction; bearish FVG = high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier − high ≥ ATR × minimum FVG-size fraction
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish FVG = a bullish/bearish FVG occurred within the recent-bar lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Trade risk distance = max(ATR × stop-risk multiplier, minimum tick)
Calculation: Stop = entry price − trade risk distance for long positions; entry price + trade risk distance for short positions
Calculation: Third target = entry price + trade risk distance × third-target R multiple for long positions; entry price − trade risk distance × third-target R multiple for short positions
