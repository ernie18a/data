Calculation: Major swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low, where a pivot is the highest high/lowest low across equal lookbacks before and after it; use a longer lookback than for internal pivots.
Calculation: Internal swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low, where a pivot is the highest high/lowest low across equal lookbacks before and after it; use a shorter lookback than for major pivots.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = a three-bar gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier / the current high is below the low two bars earlier.
Long: (low < latest confirmed major swing low AND close > that swing low AND that swing low − low ≥ ATR × minimum sweep threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average)) OR (low > high two bars earlier AND low − high two bars earlier ≥ ATR × minimum FVG threshold) OR (a bullish sweep has occurred since the most recent bearish sweep and has not yet produced a bullish CSD AND close > latest confirmed internal swing high)
Short: (high > latest confirmed major swing high AND close < that swing high AND high − that swing high ≥ ATR × minimum sweep threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average)) OR (high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier − high ≥ ATR × minimum FVG threshold) OR (a bearish sweep has occurred since the most recent bullish sweep and has not yet produced a bearish CSD AND close < latest confirmed internal swing low)
