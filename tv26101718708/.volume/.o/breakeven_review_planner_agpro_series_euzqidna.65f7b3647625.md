Calculation: Prior structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over the longer structure lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Bullish/bearish close location = (close − low)/(high − low) / (high − close)/(high − low), respectively
Calculation: Bullish/bearish invalidation candidate = latest confirmed swing low/high minus/plus ATR buffer; if unavailable, lowest low/highest high over the structure lookback minus/plus ATR buffer
Long: no active context or the active context is invalid, stale, currently invalid, currently stale, or has an opposite-side raw activation AND close > prior structure high + ATR × minimum impulse threshold AND close > open AND candle body ratio ≥ minimum body-ratio threshold AND bullish close location ≥ minimum close-location threshold AND trend support (early: close > slow EMA; normal: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; strict: fast EMA > slow EMA, fast EMA > its value three bars earlier, and close > fast EMA) AND close − bullish invalidation candidate > max(ATR × minimum risk threshold, 10 × minimum price increment)
