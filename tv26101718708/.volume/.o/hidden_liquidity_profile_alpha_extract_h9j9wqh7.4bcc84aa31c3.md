Calculation: Supply and demand liquidity by price bin = Over the rolling lookback of current-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming, set the price range from the highest high to lowest low and divide it into equal bins. For each bar, compute energy = volume × (high − low) × (age/lookback)^decayPower, where age is 1 for the newest bar and increases for older bars. Add energy × (upperWick × wickWeight + body × bodyWeight × sellBias) to the supply profile at high − 0.45 × upperWick; add energy × (lowerWick × wickWeight + body × bodyWeight × buyBias) to the demand profile at low + 0.45 × lowerWick. If the respective wick is zero, use high − 0.2 × range or low + 0.2 × range. Body is |close − open|; sellBias is 1 for a down or flat bar, otherwise 0.55; buyBias is 1 for an up or flat bar, otherwise 0.55. Clip each profile price to the range and distribute its strength to the containing bin and neighboring bins within the distribution radius, weighted by exp(−(distance/radius)^2); radius zero adds all strength to the containing bin. Normalize each profile by its own maximum. For each bin, dominantNorm is the greater of normalized supply and demand; combinedNorm is the sum of unnormalized supply and demand divided by the maximum such sum across bins.
Calculation: Selected key liquidity bins = Repeatedly select up to the configured key-level count: among bins whose dominantNorm meets the key-level threshold, choose the highest candidate score (0.7 × dominantNorm + 0.3 × combinedNorm), requiring it to be at least as strong as both adjacent bins and not within the configured minimum spacing of an already selected bin. Minimum spacing is max(1, rounded(distribution radius + bin count/45)).
Long: A selected key liquidity bin has normalized demand > normalized supply.
Short: A selected key liquidity bin has normalized supply ≥ normalized demand.
