Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset each session; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: MACD = short-period EMA of selected price minus longer-period EMA of selected price; signal = simple moving average of MACD
Calculation: STC = EMA-smoothed stochastic of MACD, then stochastic of that smoothed value, then EMA-smoothed and clamped to 0–100; MACD fast period is shorter than its slow period
Calculation: Trailing long stop = running maximum, while long, of close × (1 − trailing-stop percentage); trailing short stop = running minimum, while short, of close × (1 + trailing-stop percentage)
Long: STC crosses above its lower threshold AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset > selected-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset > session VWAP) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD crosses above its signal AND MACD < 0) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset crosses below the lower band) AND datetime is within the configured backtest window
Short: STC crosses below its upper threshold AND (EMA filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset < selected-period EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset < session VWAP) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD crosses below its signal AND signal > 0) AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR selected price at configured lookback offset crosses above the upper band) AND datetime is within the configured backtest window
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR trailing stop enabled AND low <= trailing long stop
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR trailing stop enabled AND high >= trailing short stop
