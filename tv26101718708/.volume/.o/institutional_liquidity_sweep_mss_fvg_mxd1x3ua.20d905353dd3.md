Calculation: Asia, London, and New York session highs/lows = highest high and lowest low of the current session, retained until that session starts again.
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: 4-hour high/low = high/low of the last completed 4-hour bar.
Long: (low < Asia session low AND close > Asia session low OR low < London session low AND close > London session low OR low < New York session low AND close > New York session low OR low < previous-day low AND close > previous-day low OR low < 4-hour low AND close > 4-hour low) AND the same long sweep condition was true on the previous bar AND close > previous bar high AND low > high two bars ago AND close > open AND close - open > half an ATR AND close > an EMA of close AND volume > its simple moving average
Short: (high > Asia session high AND close < Asia session high OR high > London session high AND close < London session high OR high > New York session high AND close < New York session high OR high > previous-day high AND close < previous-day high OR high > 4-hour high AND close < 4-hour high) AND the same short sweep condition was true on the previous bar AND close < previous bar low AND high < low two bars ago AND close < open AND open - close > half an ATR AND close < an EMA of close AND volume > its simple moving average
