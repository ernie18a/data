Calculation: Bullish confluence score = weighted sum of bullish candle quality, RSI level and acceleration, volume spike and expansion, OBV slope or OBV above its average, MACD acceleration or bullish MACD, Bollinger bandwidth expansion, ATR expansion, close above the Bollinger middle band on the current and previous bars, recent squeeze release, and prior squeeze duration; subtract penalties for prior upper-wick rejection and a bullish head fake. Bearish score uses the corresponding bearish conditions and prior lower-wick rejection.
Calculation: Candle quality = candle body / (high − low) meets a minimum ratio, close is within a required fraction of the high for bullish or low for bearish, and candle direction agrees; when confirmation bars ≥ 2, the previous candle must also agree in direction.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger upper band below Keltner upper band AND Bollinger lower band above Keltner lower band; release = prior bar was in a squeeze and current bar is not. Squeeze duration is the number of consecutive preceding bars in a squeeze; recent release remains active for a configured window.
Long: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak above the Bollinger upper band equals the configured confirmation count.
Long: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak above the Bollinger upper band equals the configured confirmation count AND bullish confluence score meets its minimum AND all enabled bullish filters pass.
Long: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak above the Bollinger upper band equals the configured confirmation count AND (bullish confluence score is below its minimum OR an enabled bullish filter fails).
Long: The current bar is confirmed AND close is above the Bollinger upper band AND the consecutive-close streak above it meets the configured band-walk count.
Short: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak below the Bollinger lower band equals the configured confirmation count.
Short: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak below the Bollinger lower band equals the configured confirmation count AND bearish confluence score meets its minimum AND all enabled bearish filters pass.
Short: The current bar is confirmed AND the consecutive-close streak below the Bollinger lower band equals the configured confirmation count AND (bearish confluence score is below its minimum OR an enabled bearish filter fails).
Short: The current bar is confirmed AND close is below the Bollinger lower band AND the consecutive-close streak below it meets the configured band-walk count.
