Calculation: Liquidity Pivot = From the selected lookback of chart bars, bin each bar’s hlc3-weighted volume by price, weighting newer bars more; smooth activity across nearby bins, take the center price of the strongest bin, smooth that price with an EMA, then clamp it to the value area expanded from that bin until it contains the selected share of smoothed activity. Uses the current chart bar, including while it is forming.
Calculation: Structural liquidity qualification = Average daily close × volume and the 10th percentile of daily close × volume over the short daily lookback meet their minimums, and the share of zero-volume bars over the continuity-timeframe lookback meets its maximum. Uses daily bars and continuity-timeframe bars, including each bar while it is forming; thresholds depend on the selected liquidity-quality mode.
Long: Close > Liquidity Pivot
Long: Close crosses above Liquidity Pivot OR (structural liquidity qualification AND close > Liquidity Pivot + one tick AND high > Liquidity Pivot AND distance from close to Liquidity Pivot as a percent of pivot is within the maximum AND distance from close to fast EMA as a percent of fast EMA is within the mode limit AND (crossed above Liquidity Pivot within the mode’s reclaim window OR a recent three-bar retest touched within the specified band around the pivot and current close is above it OR crossed above Liquidity Pivot now) AND (trend filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA is not falling) AND relative volume is at least its minimum AND volume >= its moving average AND close > open AND close location value >= bullish threshold AND (smoothed signed volume delta > its baseline OR five-bar average close location value >= buyer threshold OR close > prior high) AND (mode is Early OR Liquidity Pivot slope meets the mode condition) AND cooldown has elapsed)
Short: Close < Liquidity Pivot
Short: Close crosses below Liquidity Pivot
