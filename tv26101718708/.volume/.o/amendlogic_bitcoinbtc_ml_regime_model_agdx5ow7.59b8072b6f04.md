Calculation: Final upward probability = walk-forward logistic-regression probability using normalized features: 1-, 6-, and 21-bar log returns; 14-bar RSI; close minus 50-period EMA divided by 14-bar ATR; volume; signed volume (1-bar return feature × volume feature / 3); ATR/close volatility; 6-bar return feature × volatility feature / 3; DXY and TIP close-to-50-period-EMA ratios; close-to-200-period-EMA ratio; and DXY feature × 6-bar return feature / 3. Normalize each raw feature by its rolling mean and standard deviation over the same lookback, then clip features to [−3, 3]. Train online logistic regression on the features from H bars ago and the label whether current close exceeds that bar’s close, replaying K successive historical samples per confirmed bar. Use the model probability only after the minimum training count is reached and the rolling hit rate over resolved H-bar predictions meets its minimum; otherwise use 0.5 + 0.2 × (2 × trend direction + DXY direction + TIP direction) / 4, where each direction is +1 when its respective ratio is positive (DXY is negative when its ratio is below zero), and −1 otherwise. If demand sensing is enabled, blend this base probability with sigmoid(0.8 × (1-bar return feature + signed volume feature) / 2) using the sensing weight; otherwise use the base probability.
Calculation: Anomaly = absolute normalized 1-bar log return, normalized volume, or normalized ATR/close is at least the anomaly threshold.
Long: final upward probability ≥ long-entry threshold AND NOT anomaly AND (daily-trend filter disabled OR close-to-200-period-EMA ratio > 0) AND position is flat or (position is short AND final upward probability > short-exit threshold) AND minimum holding period has elapsed.
Short: final upward probability ≤ short-entry threshold AND NOT anomaly AND (daily-trend filter disabled OR close-to-200-period-EMA ratio ≤ 0) AND position is flat or (position is long AND final upward probability < long-exit threshold) AND minimum holding period has elapsed.
Exit: long, final upward probability < long-exit threshold AND minimum holding period has elapsed
Exit: short, final upward probability > short-exit threshold AND minimum holding period has elapsed
