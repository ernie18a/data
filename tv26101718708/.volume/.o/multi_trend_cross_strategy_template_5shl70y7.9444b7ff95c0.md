Calculation: Fast trend = independently selected trend calculation applied to the selected price source, using a shorter lookback; choices are ALMA, DEMA, DSMA, EMA, HMA, JMA, KAMA, LSMA, RMA, SMA, SMMA, price source, TEMA, TMA, VAMA, VIDYA, VMA, VWMA, WMA, WWMA, or ZLEMA
Calculation: Slow trend = independently selected trend calculation applied to the same price source, using a longer lookback; choices are the same as for the fast trend
Long: Fast trend > slow trend
Short: Fast trend < slow trend
Exit: Long, fast trend < slow trend
Exit: Short, fast trend > slow trend
