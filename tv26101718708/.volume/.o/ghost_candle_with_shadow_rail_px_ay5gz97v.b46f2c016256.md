Calculation: Smoothed candle = Heikin-Ashi-style OHLC, with close=(open+high+low+close)/4, open initialized to (open+close)/2 then recursively averaged with the prior smoothed close, and high/low equal to the maximum/minimum of high, smoothed open, and smoothed close.
Calculation: Trend quality = absolute change in smoothed close over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that same lookback.
Calculation: Shadow Rail trend = initialize up when rail references first exist; in an uptrend, update the lower rail using the running maximum of lower reference and damped changes in that reference, then switch down when smoothed close falls below the lower rail; in a downtrend, update the upper rail using the running minimum of upper reference and damped changes in that reference, then switch up when smoothed close rises above the upper rail. Lower/upper references are smoothed close minus/plus mean smoothed-candle true range over the swing lookback, multiplied by rail spread adjusted for one-bar smoothed-close impulse and smoothed volume flow. Damping is adjusted by trend quality; rising reference changes use faster damping than falling changes. Volume flow is the EMA of candle-body position within its range times volume, divided by the EMA of volume and clamped to [-1,1].
Long: Shadow Rail trend changes from down to up AND trend quality is at least the minimum quality threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA).
Short: Shadow Rail trend changes from up to down AND trend quality is at least the minimum quality threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close is at or below its EMA).
