Calculation: Uptrend = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA; Downtrend = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA
Calculation: ATR percentage = ATR / close × 100
Calculation: Enough range = ATR percentage ≥ minimum ATR percentage
Calculation: Directional tape = ADX(14,14) > 14
Calculation: Momentum up = RSI > 50 AND RSI ≥ prior-bar RSI; Momentum down = RSI < 50 AND RSI ≤ prior-bar RSI
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > volume moving average × 1.25 OR volume z-score > 1
Calculation: Buy share = clamp(0.54 × close position in bar range + 0.30 × body direction + 0.32 × body power × (body direction − 0.5) + 0.14, 0, 1); close position = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); body direction = 1 if close > open, 0 if close < open, otherwise 0.5; body power = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Pressure delta = 100 × sum over pressure window of (volume × (2 × buy share − 1)) / sum over pressure window of volume
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume × (2 × buy share − 1); CVD signal = EMA of CVD
Calculation: CVD bullish = CVD > CVD signal AND CVD signal ≥ prior-bar CVD signal; CVD bearish = CVD < CVD signal AND CVD signal ≤ prior-bar CVD signal
Calculation: Auction discount = (close − VWAP) / ATR < −VWAP band ATR multiple AND close > open; auction premium = (close − VWAP) / ATR > VWAP band ATR multiple AND close < open
Calculation: Bullish rejection = lower wick > 1.4 × max(body, minimum price increment) AND close > open AND close > low + 0.65 × bar range; bearish rejection = upper wick > 1.4 × max(body, minimum price increment) AND close < open AND close < low + 0.35 × bar range
Calculation: Downside sweep = low < lowest low of the preceding structure window AND close > that lowest low AND close > open; upside sweep = high > highest high of the preceding structure window AND close < that highest high AND close < open
Calculation: Compression = (highest high − lowest low over 24 bars) / close × 100 < 8 × ATR percentage AND abs(fast EMA − slow EMA) < 1.7 × ATR
Calculation: Long expansion break = prior-bar compression AND close > highest high of the preceding 8 bars AND volume expansion; short expansion break = prior-bar compression AND close < lowest low of the preceding 8 bars AND volume expansion
Calculation: Regime = 1 when uptrend AND close ≥ VWAP, −1 when downtrend AND close ≤ VWAP, otherwise 0. On each confirmed bar, increment the persistent bullish-hold count when the prior regime was 1 and current regime is 1, and bullish-turn count when prior regime was 1 and current regime is −1; likewise increment bearish-hold/bearish-turn counts for prior regime −1 and current regime −1/1, and flat-to-bull/flat-to-bear counts for prior regime 0 and current regime 1/−1. All counts start at 1 and never reset. Bull continuation = 100 × bullish-hold / (bullish-hold + bullish-turn); bear continuation = 100 × bearish-hold / (bearish-hold + bearish-turn); flat break = 100 × max(flat-to-bull, flat-to-bear) / (flat-to-bull + flat-to-bear)
Calculation: Long score = clamp(20 × uptrend + 10 × (close ≥ VWAP) + 8 × enough range + 8 × (directional tape AND +DI > −DI) + 12 × momentum up + 12 × volume expansion + pressure points + 7 × CVD bullish + 5 × auction discount + 10 × bullish rejection + 12 × downside sweep + 8 × long expansion break + transition points, 0, 100); pressure points = 8 if pressure delta > 10, else 4 if > 0, else 0; transition points = 8 if bull continuation > 58, else 5 if flat break > 60 AND regime = 1, else 0
Calculation: Short score = clamp(20 × downtrend + 10 × (close ≤ VWAP) + 8 × enough range + 8 × (directional tape AND −DI > +DI) + 12 × momentum down + 12 × volume expansion + pressure points + 7 × CVD bearish + 5 × auction premium + 10 × bearish rejection + 12 × upside sweep + 8 × short expansion break + transition points, 0, 100); pressure points = 8 if pressure delta < −10, else 4 if < 0, else 0; transition points = 8 if bear continuation > 58, else 5 if flat break > 60 AND regime = −1, else 0
Long: long score > short score + 5 OR (confirmed-bar condition when enabled AND cooldown ready AND enough range AND (trend requirement disabled OR uptrend) AND (volume requirement disabled OR volume expansion) AND long score ≥ score threshold AND long score > short score + 5) OR (downside sweep OR long expansion break OR auction discount) on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled
Short: short score > long score + 5 OR (confirmed-bar condition when enabled AND cooldown ready AND enough range AND (trend requirement disabled OR downtrend) AND (volume requirement disabled OR volume expansion) AND short score ≥ score threshold AND short score > long score + 5) OR (upside sweep OR short expansion break OR auction premium) on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled
Long: auction discount on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled OR pressure delta > 18 on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled
Short: auction premium on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled OR pressure delta < −18 on a confirmed bar when confirmed-bar signals are enabled
