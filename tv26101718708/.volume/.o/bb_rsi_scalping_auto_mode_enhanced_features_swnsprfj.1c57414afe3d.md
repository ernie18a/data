Calculation: Dynamic oversold RSI threshold = lowest RSI over a lookback + buffer
Calculation: Dynamic overbought RSI threshold = highest RSI over a lookback - buffer
Calculation: Recent bullish MACD inflection = MACD histogram rises after falling, within a recent-bar window
Calculation: Recent bearish MACD inflection = MACD histogram falls after rising, within a recent-bar window
Calculation: Recent volume confirmation = within a recent-bar window, volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier; optionally also require volume to exceed the prior bar
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot low has lower price and higher RSI than the preceding pivot low, within a maximum bar gap
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot high has higher price and lower RSI than the preceding pivot high, within a maximum bar gap
Calculation: BB width = upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band
Calculation: Dynamic long exit = highest value of the prior dynamic-exit-lookback rolling lows minus a tick buffer
Calculation: Dynamic short exit = lowest value of the prior dynamic-exit-lookback rolling highs plus a tick buffer
