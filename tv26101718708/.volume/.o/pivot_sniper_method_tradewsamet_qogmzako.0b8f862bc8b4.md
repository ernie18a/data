Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a candle low below the lows of the configured number of candles on each side; confirmed on the current bar only after all configured right-side candles complete. Confirmed pivot high = a candle high above the highs of the configured number of candles on each side; confirmed on the current bar only after all configured right-side candles complete.
Calculation: Long strength = round(min(100, long pivot-distance score + long pivot-wick score + long confirmation-candle score + volume score + long trend score)); short strength uses the corresponding short-direction scores. Long pivot-distance score = min(max((close − confirmed pivot low) / ATR, 0), 2) × 10; short pivot-distance score = min(max((confirmed pivot high − close) / ATR, 0), 2) × 10. Long pivot-wick score = min(max(lower wick / pivot-candle range, 0), 1) × 20; short pivot-wick score = min(max(upper wick / pivot-candle range, 0), 1) × 20. The pivot candle is the confirmed pivot’s candle, offset by the configured right-side length. Each confirmation-candle score = min(15 × abs(close − open) / (high − low) + 5 when the close is directional (long: close > open; short: close < open), 20). Volume score = min(max(volume / volume SMA, 0) / 1.5, 1) × 20; use 10 when volume or its average is unavailable. Each trend score adds 10 when price is on the directional side of the EMA and 10 when the EMA is moving in that direction (long: close > EMA and EMA > its prior-bar value; short: close < EMA and EMA < its prior-bar value). ATR, EMA, and volume SMA use their respective configured lookbacks on chart bars.
Calculation: Entry = close of the accepted signal-confirmation bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = selected mode: ATR distance from Entry; confirmed pivot price; pivot price buffered outward by ATR; or signal-bar low (long) / high (short). If the selected stop is not below Entry by at least one minimum price increment (long) or above Entry by at least one minimum price increment (short), use the ATR stop instead. ATR stop distance = max(ATR × configured multiplier, one minimum price increment). Risk distance = max(abs(Entry − initial stop), one minimum price increment). Each target = Entry + risk distance × its configured R multiple (long), or Entry − risk distance × its configured R multiple (short).
Long: current bar is confirmed AND confirmed pivot low exists AND confirmed pivot high does not exist AND configured bias allows long AND configured session is active AND (strength filter is disabled OR long strength ≥ configured minimum) AND current bar index − last accepted signal bar > configured cooldown bars.
Short: current bar is confirmed AND confirmed pivot high exists AND confirmed pivot low does not exist AND configured bias allows short AND configured session is active AND (strength filter is disabled OR short strength ≥ configured minimum) AND current bar index − last accepted signal bar > configured cooldown bars.
Calculation: Configured session is active = all times, the selected London session (08:00–17:00 Europe/London), the selected New York session (09:30–16:00 America/New_York), either of those sessions, or the configured custom session in its selected time zone.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop until break-even activates; thereafter Entry plus the configured favorable tick offset for long, or Entry minus that offset for short. Break-even activates after the configured TP1 or TP2 target is touched; the updated stop applies starting on the next bar. No activation when break-even is off.
Exit: long, on a bar after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ TP3; if both stop and any target are touched on the same bar, exit at the active stop. Otherwise, on an accepted new long or short signal with ATR available, exit at that signal bar’s close unless the trade already exited by stop or TP3 on that bar.
Exit: short, on a bar after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ TP3; if both stop and any target are touched on the same bar, exit at the active stop. Otherwise, on an accepted new long or short signal with ATR available, exit at that signal bar’s close unless the trade already exited by stop or TP3 on that bar.
