Calculation: Daily, 4-hour, and 1-hour supply/demand zones = confirmed 3-bar swing highs/lows; for supply, extend the latest qualifying bullish candle’s low to the higher of its high and the swing high; for demand, extend the latest qualifying bearish candle’s high to the lower of its low and the swing low; if no opposing candle qualifies, use the swing price and an ATR-based zone width. Use the last confirmed pivot and its preceding bars; zone proximity and status use the current chart bar.
Calculation: HTF bullish bias count = number of bullish conditions across daily close > daily 21-period EMA > daily 50-period EMA, and 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute close > their 21-period EMA; bearish bias count is the symmetric set. Use the HTF bars available on the current bar, including a bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confluence = count of satisfied pillars. Bullish pillars: bullish engulfing, bullish pin bar, bullish doji, or bullish demand-zone reversal; RSI/stochastic bullish turn or oversold condition; volume > its 20-bar average × threshold; fast EMA ≥ shorter-middle EMA, fast EMA crossover above shorter-middle EMA, or close > middle EMA; or at least one bullish HTF bias, bullish SuperTrend, bullish EMA crossover, or positive MACD histogram. Bearish pillars are the symmetric candle, oscillator, EMA, HTF/SuperTrend, crossover, and MACD conditions. EMAs and indicators use chart bars; EMA lookbacks are ordered fast to slow.
Calculation: Demand-zone reversal = price is near an active daily, 4-hour, or 1-hour demand zone, low ≤ zone top, close > zone bottom and open, and lower wick > half the candle body; supply-zone reversal is the symmetric condition.
Calculation: Oscillator extreme = smoothed RSI < oversold threshold OR stochastic %K < oversold threshold OR RSI < oversold threshold; bearish extreme uses the corresponding overbought thresholds.
Calculation: Rejection trigger = any of: at a demand (supply) zone, lower (upper) wick > candle body × threshold and > ATR × threshold; bullish (bearish) engulfing at the zone; after the prior bar intersects the zone, current close crosses beyond the zone’s far edge in the reversal direction with matching candle direction; or a small-body close inside the zone that moves in the reversal direction versus the prior close.
Calculation: Take-profit levels = three configured targets from entry price, ordered TP1 nearer than TP2 nearer than TP3; targets may be fixed-percentage, ATR-multiple, or opposing-zone based. Initial stop = beyond the demand bottom (long) or supply top (short) by an ATR buffer, or an ATR-multiple from entry. When trailing is enabled and TP1 is not the full exit, after TP1 move stop at least to entry; after TP2 trail at 1.5 ATR from close.
Long: Enter when armed at an active daily, 4-hour, or 1-hour demand-zone touch/proximity and, before the arm window expires, a bullish reversal OR bullish confluence ≥ required arm threshold with any required oscillator extreme OR a bullish rejection trigger occurs; bullish HTF bias count ≥ required threshold; no simultaneous supply-zone touch; zone is fresh if required (a fresh return tap may qualify); cooldowns have elapsed; ADX ≥ minimum if enabled; SuperTrend is bullish if required; long is allowed by regime or oscillator extreme; zone-distance ratio favors demand (or only demand is in proximity); normalized active-range position ≤ long limit; and no entry-blocking veto applies.
Short: Enter when armed at an active daily, 4-hour, or 1-hour supply-zone touch/proximity and, before the arm window expires, a bearish reversal OR bearish confluence ≥ required arm threshold with any required oscillator extreme OR a bearish rejection trigger occurs; bearish HTF bias count ≥ required threshold; no simultaneous demand-zone touch; zone is fresh if required (a fresh return tap may qualify); cooldowns have elapsed; ADX ≥ minimum if enabled; SuperTrend is bearish if required; short is allowed by regime or oscillator extreme; zone-distance ratio favors supply (or only supply is in proximity); normalized active-range position ≥ short limit; and no entry-blocking veto applies.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ TP3 OR full-exit-at-TP1 is enabled and high ≥ TP1 OR full-exit-at-TP1 is disabled, TP1 has been hit, and the fast EMA crosses below the shorter-middle EMA.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ TP3 OR full-exit-at-TP1 is enabled and low ≤ TP1 OR full-exit-at-TP1 is disabled, TP1 has been hit, and the fast EMA crosses above the shorter-middle EMA.
