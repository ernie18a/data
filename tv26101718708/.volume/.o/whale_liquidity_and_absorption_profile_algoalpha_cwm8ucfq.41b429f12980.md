Calculation: Strength threshold = the value at the configured percentile in the ascending list of lower-timeframe volumes from the rolling chart-bar lookback, including the last completed bar.
Calculation: Price bins = divide the range of lower-timeframe closing prices in the rolling chart-bar lookback into equal-width bins; assign each intrabar to the bin containing its close.
Calculation: Bin delta = sum of volume from lower-timeframe bars with close > open minus sum of volume from bars with close ≤ open in that bin.
Calculation: Absorption per bin = sum lower-timeframe volume where a bar with close > open closes within its chart bar’s upper wick, or a bar with close ≤ open closes within its chart bar’s lower wick. An absorption peak is a bin whose absorption exceeds both adjacent bins.
Long: A lower-timeframe bar in the current chart bar has close > open and volume ≥ strength threshold OR the bin containing the current close has positive delta whose absolute value is at least the greatest absolute delta across all bins OR an absorption-peak bin has current close above its midpoint.
Short: A lower-timeframe bar in the current chart bar has close ≤ open and volume ≥ strength threshold OR the bin containing the current close has negative delta whose absolute value is at least the greatest absolute delta across all bins OR an absorption-peak bin has current close at or below its midpoint.
