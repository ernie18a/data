Calculation: Range basis = midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low.
Calculation: KAMA ATR = ATR smoothed by Kaufman’s adaptive moving average using close-price efficiency ratio; initialize with ATR.
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − rolling mean volume) / rolling standard deviation of volume.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = clamp to [0.3, 0.7] of log(R/S long window ÷ R/S short window) / log(long window ÷ short window); each R/S is the range of cumulative deviations of log returns from their window mean, divided by the standard deviation of returns. Use 0.5 when unavailable. The long window is longer than the short window.
Calculation: Stage 1, 2, and 3 are sequential Supertrend directions using KAMA ATR: Stage 1 uses the range basis; Stage 2 uses Stage 1’s output and its factor scaled by clamp(1 + 0.4 × Volume Z-score, 0.5, 2.5); Stage 3 uses Stage 2’s output and its factor scaled by clamp(1 + Hurst estimate − 0.5, 0.5, 1.8). Each stage has its own factor.
Long: Confirmed bar AND (Stage 3 changes from bearish to bullish OR Stage 3 is bullish AND open < Stage 2 AND close > Stage 2 AND this bounce condition was false on the previous bar OR all three stages become bullish together OR Stage 3 changes to bullish and Stage 2 is bullish now or on either of the prior two bars).
Short: Confirmed bar AND (Stage 3 changes from bullish to bearish OR Stage 3 is bearish AND open > Stage 2 AND close < Stage 2 AND this bounce condition was false on the previous bar OR all three stages become bearish together OR Stage 3 changes to bearish and Stage 2 is bearish now or on either of the prior two bars).
