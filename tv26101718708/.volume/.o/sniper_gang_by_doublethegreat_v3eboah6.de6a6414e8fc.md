Calculation: Bull score = count of: close above chart VWAP; 14-period RSI above 50; MACD line above signal; shorter-period EMA above longer-period EMA; 14-period ADX above 25 while close is above the shorter EMA; volume above its 20-bar average while close is above open; RSI above 50 on the fixed lower timeframe.
Calculation: Bear score = count of the corresponding bearish conditions: close below chart VWAP; 14-period RSI below 50; MACD line below signal; shorter-period EMA below longer-period EMA; 14-period ADX above 25 while close is below the shorter EMA; volume above its 20-bar average while close is below open; lower-timeframe RSI below 50.
Calculation: Active score = bull score for a long position, bear score for a short position, otherwise the greater of the two.
Calculation: Weighted market-structure oscillator = weighted average of three normalized close-position series for short-, intermediate-, and long-term structure breaks, using configurable weights and smoothing each series with the same configurable SMA. A bullish break occurs once when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; a bearish break occurs once when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. Intermediate pivots are formed from short-term pivots; long-term pivots are formed from intermediate pivots. Each series normalizes close between its running minimum and maximum since the latest change in break direction, then smooths and scales to 0–100.
Calculation: Cycle oscillator = weighted market-structure oscillator minus its EMA, plus 50.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = close-based ATR trailing stop: use sensitivity times ATR as the offset; while consecutive closes remain above (below) the prior stop, trail upward with the maximum (downward with the minimum) of the prior stop and close minus (plus) the offset; otherwise reset to close minus (plus) the offset according to which side close is on.
Calculation: Quad bullish divergence = all four stochastic lines first enter oversold together; store that bar's low and fast stochastic value; afterward, while fast stochastic is at most 10 points above oversold, set divergence when low revisits or undercuts the stored low, fast stochastic is above its stored value, and fast stochastic is rising. Quad bearish divergence is the mirrored overbought condition: all four lines first enter overbought together; store that bar's high and fast stochastic value; afterward, while fast stochastic is at least 10 points below overbought, set divergence when high revisits or exceeds the stored high, fast stochastic is below its stored value, and fast stochastic is falling. The fast, medium, slow and trend stochastic lookbacks are progressively longer; the trend stochastic uses its configured other timeframe's bar still forming.
Long: shorter-period EMA crosses above longer-period EMA AND no prior long signal is active AND (HTF filter disabled OR each enabled configured HTF has its shorter-period EMA above its longer-period EMA and chart close above that HTF's VWAP; use the HTF bar still forming) OR close crosses above the UT trailing stop OR weighted market-structure oscillator crosses above 50 OR cycle oscillator crosses above 50 OR a long A+ setup occurs (active score >= 5 AND [quad bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence OR trend stochastic >= 60 AND fast stochastic <= oversold level AND fast stochastic rising] AND weighted market-structure oscillator >= 50 AND close above chart VWAP AND HTF filter disabled or aligned for long) OR quad bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence (trend stochastic >= strong-bull level AND medium and slow stochastic >= 50 AND fast stochastic <= oversold level AND fast stochastic rising) OR trend stochastic >= 60 AND fast stochastic <= oversold level AND fast stochastic rising OR active score >= 4 AND weighted market-structure oscillator >= 55 AND close above chart VWAP OR weighted market-structure oscillator >= 60 OR trend stochastic >= 60 OR a bullish short-term or intermediate-term structure break OR bull score exceeds bear score by at least 40 percentage points OR bull score exceeds bear score.
Short: shorter-period EMA crosses below longer-period EMA AND no prior short signal is active AND (HTF filter disabled OR each enabled configured HTF has its shorter-period EMA below its longer-period EMA and chart close below that HTF's VWAP; use the HTF bar still forming) OR close crosses below the UT trailing stop OR weighted market-structure oscillator crosses below 50 OR cycle oscillator crosses below 50 OR a short A+ setup occurs (active score >= 5 AND [quad bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence OR trend stochastic <= 40 AND fast stochastic >= overbought level AND fast stochastic falling] AND weighted market-structure oscillator <= 50 AND close below chart VWAP AND HTF filter disabled or aligned for short) OR quad bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence (trend stochastic <= 100 minus strong-bull level AND medium and slow stochastic <= 50 AND fast stochastic >= overbought level AND fast stochastic falling) OR trend stochastic <= 40 AND fast stochastic >= overbought level AND fast stochastic falling OR active score >= 4 AND weighted market-structure oscillator <= 45 AND close below chart VWAP OR weighted market-structure oscillator <= 40 OR trend stochastic <= 40 OR a bearish short-term or intermediate-term structure break OR bear score exceeds bull score by at least 40 percentage points OR bear score >= bull score.
Exit: Long, fast stochastic >= overbought level AND trend stochastic is falling AND close below the longer-period EMA OR active score <= 1.
Exit: Short, fast stochastic <= oversold level AND trend stochastic is rising AND close above the longer-period EMA OR active score <= 1.
