Calculation: RSMK = 100 × EMA of ln(instrument close / benchmark close) minus its value from the longer relative-strength lookback ago, using a shorter smoothing period; use benchmark closes from the same timeframe.
Long: position is flat AND 5-bar SMA(volume) > minimum volume AND RSMK > its longer-lookback SMA AND RSMK > entry-strength threshold AND RSMK = its rolling highest value over a short lookback AND RSMK < overbought threshold AND (benchmark rate of change over a short lookback > negative market-risk threshold AND instrument/benchmark correlation over a longer lookback > minimum correlation OR benchmark rate of change over a short lookback < negative market-risk threshold AND instrument/benchmark correlation over a longer lookback < negative minimum correlation) AND lowest RSI over a longer lookback < oversold threshold AND current RSI > RSI from a shorter lookback ago AND shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback SMA(close) from a prior bar.
Exit: Long, (RSMK < negative entry-strength threshold AND instrument/benchmark correlation > strong-correlation threshold) OR close >= entry price × (1 + profit target) OR bars since entry >= rebalance period
