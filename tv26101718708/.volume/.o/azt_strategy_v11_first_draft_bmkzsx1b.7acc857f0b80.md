Calculation: Structure bias = Track the latest confirmed swing high and low; set bullish when close breaks above the latest swing high, bearish when close breaks below the latest swing low, and otherwise retain the prior bias. Compute separately on the chart, intermediate, and higher timeframes. Higher-timeframe values use the latest available aggregated bar (last completed historically; forming in real time).
Calculation: Bullish candle follow-through = The prior bar is either a bullish engulfing bar, a small-body bar with a long lower wick and upper wick no larger than its body, or a doji with a long lower wick; current close is above prior high. Bearish candle follow-through is the mirrored pattern with current close below prior low.
Calculation: EMA–VWAP breakout = EMA and VWAP are within the configured tolerance, and close is above both for bullish or below both for bearish. On the chart timeframe use its configured tolerance mode; on the intermediate timeframe use an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Non-chop regime = A structural break occurred within the configured recency window and the distance between the latest swing high and low is not below the configured ATR threshold.
Long: On the chart timeframe, the higher-timeframe structure is bullish on at least two of the three higher timeframes, intermediate-timeframe structure is bullish, chart/intermediate/next-higher structure biases agree bullish, the intermediate-timeframe fast EMA is above its slow EMA and its bullish cross is within the configured recent-bar window, EMA–VWAP breakout is bullish, bullish candle follow-through is present, and regime is non-chop; signal tier meets the selected minimum. OR, on the intermediate timeframe, the same higher-timeframe majority and bullish intermediate/next-higher structure alignment hold, the intermediate fast EMA is above its slow EMA with a recent bullish cross, EMA–VWAP breakout and bullish candle follow-through are present, regime is non-chop, and signal tier meets the selected minimum.
Short: On the chart timeframe, the higher-timeframe structure is bearish on at least two of the three higher timeframes, intermediate-timeframe structure is bearish, chart/intermediate/next-higher structure biases agree bearish, the intermediate-timeframe fast EMA is below its slow EMA and its bearish cross is within the configured recent-bar window, EMA–VWAP breakout is bearish, bearish candle follow-through is present, and regime is non-chop; signal tier meets the selected minimum. OR, on the intermediate timeframe, the same higher-timeframe majority and bearish intermediate/next-higher structure alignment hold, the intermediate fast EMA is below its slow EMA with a recent bearish cross, EMA–VWAP breakout and bearish candle follow-through are present, regime is non-chop, and signal tier meets the selected minimum.
