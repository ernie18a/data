Calculation: Smoothed momentum = EMA of the close rate of change; optionally multiply by the lesser of 2 and volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Confirmed positive momentum peak = a local maximum of smoothed momentum confirmed after the configured right-side lookback, above the configured minimum strength, and sufficiently separated from the prior valid peak; pair it with the high of that peak bar. Confirmed negative momentum trough = the corresponding local minimum below the negative minimum strength, paired with that bar’s low.
Calculation: Momentum efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Breakout displacement = close’s distance beyond the paired prior peak high or trough low, divided by ATR.
Long: A new momentum sweep occurs when smoothed momentum exceeds the latest confirmed positive peak by the configured sweep threshold; OR smoothed momentum is positive and crosses above the prior bar’s available confirmed positive peak while close exceeds its paired high, EMA 1 exceeds EMA 2, and any enabled momentum-expansion, breakout-displacement, and efficiency filters pass; OR a confirmed negative momentum trough has a lower paired price low and higher momentum value than the preceding confirmed negative trough. For primary BUY signals, require the signal cooldown and same-direction spacing checks to pass, and no live trade.
Short: A new momentum sweep occurs when smoothed momentum falls below the latest confirmed negative trough by the configured sweep threshold; OR smoothed momentum is negative and crosses below the prior bar’s available confirmed negative trough while close is below its paired low, EMA 1 is below EMA 2, and any enabled momentum-expansion, breakout-displacement, and efficiency filters pass; OR a confirmed positive momentum peak has a higher paired price high and lower momentum value than the preceding confirmed positive peak. For primary SELL signals, require the signal cooldown to pass, and no live trade.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the EMA 2 value saved at entry, or high reaches entry plus five times the entry-to-stop distance.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the EMA 2 value saved at entry, or low reaches entry minus five times the stop-to-entry distance.
