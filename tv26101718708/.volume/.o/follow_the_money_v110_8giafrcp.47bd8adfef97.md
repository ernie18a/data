Calculation: High volume = current volume ≥ its moving average × (1 + configured expansion threshold).
Calculation: Sustained above/below 150-day average = close above/below the average for the configured number of consecutive bars.
Calculation: Breakout confirmation = breakout enabled is false, or close exceeds the highest prior close over the configured breakout lookback, or close exceeds the configured fraction of the highest prior 20 closes.
Calculation: Relative strength = asset return minus configured benchmark return over the configured lookback; strong when > 0, very strong when > the configured strong threshold.
Calculation: Close quality = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added on an up-close, subtracted on a down-close, unchanged on an equal close. A/D = cumulative volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), using zero when high = low.
Calculation: Institutional score = 10 if 150-day average > 200-day average, plus 10 if the 200-day average is rising over its configured lookback, plus 5 if close > 150-day average; plus 10 if high volume and 10 if close quality meets its threshold; plus 10 if ATR > its 20-bar average, else 5 if ATR < its 20-bar average; plus 10 if OBV > its 20-bar average and 10 if A/D > its 20-bar average; plus 20 if relative strength exceeds the configured strong threshold, else 10 if relative strength > 0; plus 15 if benchmark close > its shorter moving average and that average > its longer moving average; plus 5 if close quality meets its threshold.
Long: On a confirmed bar within the configured date range, no excluded-symbol filter, cooldown elapsed, 150-day average > 200-day average, 200-day average rising over its configured lookback, sustained above 150-day average, breakout confirmation, high volume, strong relative strength, bull market, and institutional score ≥ configured minimum.
Long: On a confirmed bar within the configured date range, position at stage 1, profit from entry price ≥ configured confirmation-add threshold, close > 150-day average, and strong relative strength.
Long: On a confirmed bar within the configured date range, position at stage 2, profit from entry price ≥ configured expansion-add threshold, and close > 150-day average.
Exit: long, sustained below 150-day average and high volume occurred during the current below-average streak.
Exit: long, close < 150-day average × (1 − configured backstop percentage).
Exit: long, 150-day average absolute percentage slope over its configured lookback ≤ flattening threshold, close > 150-day average, and not already trimmed during this trade.
