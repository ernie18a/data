Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = swing highs/lows whose high/low is the extreme among an equal configured number of bars before and after; a level becomes available after those later bars complete. Retain the newest configured number per side and remove a level after price sweeps it, whether or not the sweep qualifies.
Long: For any tracked swing low, low < level AND (close > level if close-back confirmation is enabled) AND (close - low) / (high - low) ≥ minimum rejection fraction AND level - low ≥ minimum sweep depth × ATR(14) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close) on the configured trend timeframe, using the value returned with lookahead off) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average × configured multiple) AND bars since the previous signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (no-repeat disabled OR previous signal was not long) AND no short signal qualifies on this bar; use the formula only when high > low.
Short: For any tracked swing high, high > level AND (close < level if close-back confirmation is enabled) AND (high - close) / (high - low) ≥ minimum rejection fraction AND high - level ≥ minimum sweep depth × ATR(14) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close) on the configured trend timeframe, using the value returned with lookahead off) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume average × configured multiple) AND bars since the previous signal ≥ configured cooldown AND (no-repeat disabled OR previous signal was not short) AND no long signal qualifies on this bar; use the formula only when high > low.
