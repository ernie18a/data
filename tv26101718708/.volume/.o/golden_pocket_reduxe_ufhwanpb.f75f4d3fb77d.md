Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirmation waits for the bars on the right to complete. Use chart-timeframe bars or the selected overlay-timeframe bars; overlay values may use the currently forming bar.
Calculation: Swing range = latest pivot high minus latest pivot low; a new pivot high updates the bullish zone, and a new pivot low updates the bearish zone.
Calculation: Bullish Golden Pocket = [pivot high − swing range × deeper retracement level, pivot high − swing range × shallower retracement level]; bearish Golden Pocket = [pivot low + swing range × shallower retracement level, pivot low + swing range × deeper retracement level]. The deeper level is greater than the shallower level. Create each zone only when its configured MA filter passes.
Calculation: Quick range = highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback on chart-timeframe or selected overlay-timeframe bars; use confirmed bars. Its bullish and bearish Golden Pockets use the same formulas as above.
Calculation: Persistent Day/Week/Month range = high and low of the last completed day, week, or month, respectively; use the prior completed period, not the forming period. Its Golden Pocket uses the bullish formula above.
Calculation: ATR = 14-period Wilder average of true range, where true range is the greatest of high−low, |high−prior close|, and |low−prior close|.
Calculation: Demand zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle within the configured lookback before an up impulse; supply zone = range of the most recent bullish candle before a down impulse. An impulse has body size greater than ATR × the configured multiplier and closes in its direction; when the structure filter is enabled, it must also close above the prior lookback high for demand or below the prior lookback low for supply. Clamp zone height to the configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = zone [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; bearish FVG = zone [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. Create only on a confirmed bar and when the gap is at least the configured ATR multiple.
Long: price first enters an active bullish Golden Pocket, bullish Quick Golden Pocket, prior-period Golden Pocket, demand zone, bullish FVG, or bullish Golden Pocket extension: current low <= zone top AND prior low > zone top OR current high >= zone bottom AND prior high < zone bottom.
Short: price first enters an active bearish Golden Pocket, bearish Quick Golden Pocket, supply zone, bearish FVG, or bearish Golden Pocket extension: current low <= zone top AND prior low > zone top OR current high >= zone bottom AND prior high < zone bottom.
