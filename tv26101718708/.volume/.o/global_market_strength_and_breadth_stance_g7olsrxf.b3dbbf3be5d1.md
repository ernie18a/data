Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow SMA = arithmetic mean of close over progressively longer lookbacks on the selected analysis timeframe, using its current bar (which may still be forming).
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[10]) / sum(abs(close − close[1]), 10) on the selected analysis timeframe.
Calculation: Hybrid trend = bullish when close is above the 20-bar Donchian midpoint and farther than 15% of the Donchian range from it, and the SMA ribbon is not bearish (close < fast SMA < medium SMA < slow SMA); bearish when close is below the midpoint and farther than 15% of the range from it, and the ribbon is not bullish (close > fast SMA > medium SMA > slow SMA); otherwise sideways. Donchian range uses the highest high and lowest low over 20 bars on the selected analysis timeframe, including its current bar.
Long: Efficiency ratio ≥ 0.20 AND ((medium SMA > slow SMA AND fast SMA > medium SMA AND close > fast SMA) OR (medium SMA > slow SMA AND fast SMA > medium SMA AND close ≤ fast SMA AND close > medium SMA) OR (Hybrid trend is bullish AND Efficiency ratio > 0.40))
Short: Efficiency ratio ≥ 0.20 AND ((medium SMA ≤ slow SMA AND close < fast SMA) OR (Hybrid trend is bearish AND Efficiency ratio > 0.40))
