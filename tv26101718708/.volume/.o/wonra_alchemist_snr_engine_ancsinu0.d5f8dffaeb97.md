Calculation: Confirmed swings = ATR-threshold zigzag turns, confirmed after a configurable number of bars; same-direction turns retain only the more extreme point.
Calculation: Body S/R level = the close of a bullish candle followed by a bearish candle (resistance), or a bearish candle followed by a bullish candle (support); its band spans that close and the next open. If no such body level is found near a swing, use the swing extreme.
Calculation: Close-to-open gap level = midpoint of the previous close and current open when their separation meets a minimum ATR-scaled size; an upward gap is support and a downward gap is resistance.
Calculation: Level proof = confirmed touches plus near-miss swing turns; count a new touch only after price has moved away from the level and returned. A level broken by a close beyond the far edge of its band flips its support/resistance role once.
Calculation: QML pattern score = a 0–100 weighted composite of liquidity sweep, structure-break quality, unmitigated same-direction fair-value gap near the shoulder, displacement, volume/body ratio, RSI divergence, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and volatility regime.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA alignment = 50-period EMA above 200-period EMA for bullish alignment, below for bearish; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: The latest five confirmed swings form low–high–low–higher high–lower low; the head is below the prior low, the high before the shoulder is above the neck, structure height meets its ATR-scaled minimum, no intervening swing contradicts the shoulder or neck, and a close after the head breaks above the neck by the required ATR margin within the timeout; current close is above the shoulder, its level is fresh, and pattern score meets the minimum.
Short: The latest five confirmed swings form high–low–high–lower low–higher high; the head is above the prior high, the low before the shoulder is below the neck, structure height meets its ATR-scaled minimum, no intervening swing contradicts the shoulder or neck, and a close after the head breaks below the neck by the required ATR margin within the timeout; current close is below the shoulder, its level is fresh, and pattern score meets the minimum.
Long: A fresh bullish QML is retested after its structure break, price intersects the shoulder level within the ATR tolerance, and the bar confirms a close reclaim: open and close above the level, close above it by the minimum ATR distance, and either close is at least open or the lower wick exceeds 0.8 times the upper wick.
Short: A fresh bearish QML is retested after its structure break, price intersects the shoulder level within the ATR tolerance, and the bar confirms a close reclaim: open and close below the level, close below it by the minimum ATR distance, and either close is at most open or the upper wick exceeds 0.8 times the lower wick.
Long: A confirmed swing low forms after a fresh bullish QML's break, lies between its shoulder and the configured fraction of the shoulder-to-break distance (with level tolerance), and coincides with a support level whose proof meets the minimum.
Short: A confirmed swing high forms after a fresh bearish QML's break, lies between its shoulder and the configured fraction of the shoulder-to-break distance (with level tolerance), and coincides with a resistance level whose proof meets the minimum.
Long: A support level with proof at or above the minimum is touched after price has moved away; within the confirmation window, the bar meets the bullish close-reclaim condition above, and the bullish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: A resistance level with proof at or above the minimum is touched after price has moved away; within the confirmation window, the bar meets the bearish close-reclaim condition above, and the bearish signal cooldown has elapsed.
Long: A rising trendline through two successively higher confirmed body-low pivots intersects, within ATR tolerance, either a fresh bullish QML shoulder or a fresh support level meeting the minimum proof; the current bar intersects that same price area.
Short: A falling trendline through two successively lower confirmed body-high pivots intersects, within ATR tolerance, either a fresh bearish QML shoulder or a fresh resistance level meeting the minimum proof; the current bar intersects that same price area.
Long: A bullish engulfing candle closes above its open, engulfs the previous bearish candle's body (or full range when wick engulfing is required), has a larger body than the previous candle, and its body meets the ATR-scaled minimum; or a bearish engulfing zone has flipped bullish after a close above its top.
Short: A bearish engulfing candle closes below its open, engulfs the previous bullish candle's body (or full range when wick engulfing is required), has a larger body than the previous candle, and its body meets the ATR-scaled minimum; or a bullish engulfing zone has flipped bearish after a close below its bottom.
