Calculation: Lunar bias = negative cosine of the current bar’s timestamp’s position in the lunar cycle, with new moon at cycle position 0 and full moon at 0.5; lunar long score = positive part of lunar bias, scaled to 0–100; lunar short score = negative part of lunar bias, scaled to 0–100
Calculation: Bull momentum score = weighted sum of these bullish conditions: close above trend EMA, fast EMA above slow EMA and rising, close above slow EMA, RSI above its bullish threshold (with an additional component above that threshold plus a margin), MACD histogram positive and nondecreasing, close above session VWAP, bullish relative-volume participation, and higher-timeframe bullish alignment; bear momentum score uses the corresponding bearish conditions, with fast EMA falling, MACD histogram negative and nonincreasing, close below session VWAP, and bearish participation. Relative volume = volume divided by the longer-lookback average volume. Participation requires relative volume above its threshold, candle direction aligned with the side, close in the top (bullish) or bottom (bearish) portion of the range, and body size above an ATR multiple. Higher-timeframe alignment uses the higher-timeframe close and fast, slow, and trend EMAs from the last completed higher-timeframe bar: bullish when close is above trend EMA and fast EMA above slow EMA; bearish when close is below trend EMA and fast EMA below slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish breakout = close above the highest high of prior bars over the breakout lookback and close above open; bearish breakout = close below the lowest low of prior bars over the breakout lookback and close below open. Bullish pullback resumption = lowest low over the shorter pullback lookback at or below fast EMA, with close above fast EMA, above open, and above prior close; bearish pullback resumption is the inverse
Calculation: Long confluence = weighted blend of bull momentum score and lunar long score, plus a bonus on a newly entered full-moon phase; short confluence = weighted blend of bear momentum score and lunar short score, plus a bonus on a newly entered new-moon phase
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the swing lookback minus ATR times a buffer; short stop = highest high over the swing lookback plus ATR times a buffer. Risk distance = greater of entry-to-stop distance and a minimum tick-size floor. Long target 2 = entry price plus risk distance times the farther target risk multiple; short target 2 = entry price minus risk distance times the farther target risk multiple
Long: lunar bias above its positive threshold AND bull momentum score at least its minimum AND long confluence at least its minimum AND (bullish breakout OR bullish pullback resumption) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe alignment bullish) AND long direction enabled AND no long signal within the cooldown lookback
Short: lunar bias below the negative threshold AND bear momentum score at least its minimum AND short confluence at least its minimum AND (bearish breakout OR bearish pullback resumption) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe alignment bearish) AND short direction enabled AND no short signal within the cooldown lookback
Exit: long, low at or below long stop OR high at or above long target 2 OR short-entry condition
Exit: short, high at or above short stop OR low at or below short target 2 OR long-entry condition
