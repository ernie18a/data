Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of HLC3, reset at the start of the regular trading session and accumulated through the current bar.
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured premarket session, accumulated through the current premarket bar and fixed after it ends.
Calculation: VWAP slope = current session VWAP minus its value at the shorter configured lookback.
Calculation: Recent range = highest high minus lowest low over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Body percentage above/below a level = the percentage of the candle body above/below that level; zero unless the close is above/below it.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the configured stop lookback minus the configured buffer; short stop = highest high over that lookback plus the buffer.
Long: within the regular trading session AND flat AND cooldown inactive AND (regime filter disabled OR (absolute VWAP slope >= configured minimum AND recent range >= configured minimum)) AND ( (VWAP retest enabled AND low <= session VWAP + configured buffer AND close > session VWAP AND close > higher-timeframe EMA) OR (premarket breakout enabled AND premarket high available AND ((close mode AND body percentage above premarket high >= configured minimum AND close > higher-timeframe EMA) OR (retest mode AND low <= premarket high + configured buffer AND close > premarket high AND close > higher-timeframe EMA))) OR (EMA retest enabled AND low <= entry EMA + configured buffer AND close > entry EMA AND close > higher-timeframe EMA)) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > entry EMA)
Short: within the regular trading session AND flat AND cooldown inactive AND (regime filter disabled OR (absolute VWAP slope >= configured minimum AND recent range >= configured minimum)) AND ( (VWAP retest enabled AND high >= session VWAP - configured buffer AND close < session VWAP AND close < higher-timeframe EMA) OR (premarket breakout enabled AND premarket low available AND ((close mode AND body percentage below premarket low >= configured minimum AND close < higher-timeframe EMA) OR (retest mode AND high >= premarket low - configured buffer AND close < premarket low AND close < higher-timeframe EMA))) OR (EMA retest enabled AND high >= entry EMA - configured buffer AND close < entry EMA AND close < higher-timeframe EMA)) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < entry EMA)
Exit: long, close body percentage below exit EMA >= configured minimum OR (VWAP exit override active AND close body percentage below session VWAP >= configured minimum) OR (low <= long stop) OR (premarket partial enabled AND premarket high available AND entry price < premarket high AND high >= premarket high) OR (day-level partial enabled AND high >= current session high) OR (not within regular trading session)
Exit: short, close body percentage above exit EMA >= configured minimum OR (VWAP exit override active AND close body percentage above session VWAP >= configured minimum) OR (high >= short stop) OR (premarket partial enabled AND premarket low available AND entry price > premarket low AND low <= premarket low) OR (day-level partial enabled AND low <= current session low) OR (not within regular trading session)
Calculation: Entry EMA = EMA of close over the configured entry lookback; exit EMA = EMA of close over the configured exit lookback; higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close over the configured trend lookback on the configured higher timeframe, using its developing bar in realtime and the latest available value historically.
