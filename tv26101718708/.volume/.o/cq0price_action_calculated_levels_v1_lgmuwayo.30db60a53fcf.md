Calculation: Confirmed zigzag leg = chart-timeframe pivots from a rolling lookback: a bar is a swing high when its high is the window maximum, or a swing low when its low is the window minimum; alternate pivot direction on a new opposite pivot, updating the latest pivot to a more extreme price in the same direction. Use the leg before the still-forming leg: its start and end are the third-newest and second-newest pivots, respectively.
Calculation: Leg level(r) = round(P0 + r × (P100 − P0)), where P0 is the confirmed leg end and P100 is its start; use the opposite anchors if the level orientation is inverted.
Long: After the confirmed leg end, on a completed bar, high ≥ an as-yet-untouched-since-leg-end level(0.40, 0.50, 0.60, or 1.00) when P100 > P0, OR low ≤ one of those levels when P100 < P0; trigger each level only on its first touch per leg.
Exit: long, after the confirmed leg end, on a completed bar, high ≥ an as-yet-untouched-since-leg-end level(1.25, 1.60, or 2.00) when P100 > P0, OR low ≤ one of those levels when P100 < P0; trigger each level only on its first touch per leg.
