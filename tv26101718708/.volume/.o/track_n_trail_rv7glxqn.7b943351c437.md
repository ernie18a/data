Calculation: Fast and slow Supertrend trends = use the same ATR length; calculate final bands from hl2 ± factor × ATR, carrying the prior final upper band forward as max(current upper band, prior upper band) when prior close > prior final upper band, otherwise using current upper band; carry the prior final lower band forward as min(current lower band, prior lower band) when prior close < prior final lower band, otherwise using current lower band. Trend is bullish when close > prior final upper band, bearish when close < prior final lower band, otherwise it retains the prior trend (initially bullish). Fast factor < slow factor.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative(close × volume) / cumulative(volume) within the session.
Long: Fast Supertrend trend = bullish AND slow Supertrend trend = bullish AND close > SMA(high) AND close > session VWAP AND close > EMA(close)
Short: Fast Supertrend trend = bearish AND slow Supertrend trend = bearish AND close < SMA(low) AND close < session VWAP AND close < EMA(close)
