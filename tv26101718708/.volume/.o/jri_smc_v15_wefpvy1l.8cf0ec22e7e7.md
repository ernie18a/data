Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest confirmed swing high and swing low are both higher than their respective prior confirmed swing high and swing low; bearish structure = both are lower. A swing high/low is a high/low with the configured number of lower highs/higher lows on each side.
Calculation: Upward break = close crosses above the second-most-recent confirmed swing high; downward break = close crosses below the second-most-recent confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Retest after an upward break = low reaches within the configured percentage buffer above the broken swing high and close finishes above it; retest after a downward break = high reaches within the buffer below the broken swing low and close finishes below it. Each break can trigger only one retest.
Calculation: Demand/supply zone = the price range within the configured percentage buffer of a confirmed swing low/high, respectively; merge a new pivot into an existing zone it overlaps. A zone qualifies after at least two pivots; price is in a zone when close is within any qualifying zone.
Calculation: Long score = count of true conditions: bullish structure; close in demand zone; upward break; upward-break retest; close above current-day VWAP; shorter-period EMA above longer-period EMA; RSI above 50. Short score = count of true conditions: bearish structure; close in supply zone; downward break; downward-break retest; close below current-day VWAP; shorter-period EMA below longer-period EMA; RSI below 50.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR multiplied by the configured stop multiplier, using a smaller multiplier on scalp charts than other charts. Long stop = entry price − initial stop distance; short stop = entry price + initial stop distance. First, second, and third targets are 1, 2, and 3 times the initial stop distance from entry in the trade direction.
Long: Long score ≥ minimum score AND no long or short signal is active AND trading is not locked AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the last long signal. A long signal remains active until long score < minimum score − 1. Enter at the next bar's open if no trade is active.
Short: Short score ≥ minimum score AND no long or short signal is active AND trading is not locked AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the last short signal. A short signal remains active until short score < minimum score − 1. Enter at the next bar's open if no trade is active.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price
Exit: long, high ≥ third target
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price
Exit: short, low ≤ third target
