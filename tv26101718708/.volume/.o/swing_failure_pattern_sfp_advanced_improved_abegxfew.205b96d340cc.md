Calculation: Swing high = a chart-bar high greater than the highs of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; becomes available after those later bars close. Track the most recent configured number of confirmed swing highs, expiring levels older than the configured age; remove swept levels if configured.
Calculation: Swing low = a chart-bar low less than the lows of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; becomes available after those later bars close. Track the most recent configured number of confirmed swing lows, expiring levels older than the configured age; remove swept levels if configured.
Calculation: Bar close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, low < a tracked swing low AND swing low − low ≥ configured minimum sweep × ATR(14) AND close > swing low AND bar close position ≥ configured minimum close position; if volume confirmation is enabled, volume ≥ its configured moving average; if the higher-timeframe trend filter is enabled, the latest available close on its configured higher timeframe > its EMA; if the session filter is enabled, the bar time is within either configured UTC session window.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, high > a tracked swing high AND high − swing high ≥ configured minimum sweep × ATR(14) AND close < swing high AND bar close position ≤ 1 − configured minimum close position; if volume confirmation is enabled, volume ≥ its configured moving average; if the higher-timeframe trend filter is enabled, the latest available close on its configured higher timeframe < its EMA; if the session filter is enabled, the bar time is within either configured UTC session window.
Long: After a bullish sweep, on a later confirmed chart bar, low is within configured ATR(14)-multiple tolerance of the swept swing low AND close > that level.
Short: After a bearish sweep, on a later confirmed chart bar, high is within configured ATR(14)-multiple tolerance of the swept swing high AND close < that level.
