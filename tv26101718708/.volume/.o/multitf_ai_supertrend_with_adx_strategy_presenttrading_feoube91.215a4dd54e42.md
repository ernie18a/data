Calculation: SuperTrend 1 = On the first higher-timeframe series, calculate the selected moving average of (higher-timeframe close × chart-bar volume) divided by the selected moving average of chart-bar volume; add and subtract the ATR multiplied by its factor to form upper and lower bands. Carry the lower band forward unless it rises or the prior chart close is below it; carry the upper band forward unless it falls or the prior chart close is above it. Set direction bullish when chart close crosses above the carried upper band, or remains above the lower band; otherwise set it bearish. Use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: SuperTrend 2 = Calculate as SuperTrend 1 using the second, different higher timeframe and its separately configured length and factor. Use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: AI trend classification for each SuperTrend = For each of the n most recent SuperTrend values, label the observation 1 when the weighted moving average of chart close is greater than the weighted moving average of that SuperTrend; otherwise label it 0. Compare the current SuperTrend value to those n values using absolute distance, select the k nearest, and compute the inverse-distance-weighted average of their labels (weight = 1 / (distance + 0.000001)). The classification is bullish only when this average equals 1, and bearish only when it equals 0. Calculate separately for each SuperTrend, with independently configured lookbacks, n, and k.
Calculation: ADX and directional indicators = On the current ADX-timeframe bars, calculate upward movement as the increase in high when it exceeds the downward movement and is positive; calculate downward movement as the decrease in low when it exceeds the upward movement and is positive; otherwise each movement is zero. Smooth each movement with a weighted moving average and divide by the weighted moving average of true range, then multiply by 100 to obtain +DI and -DI. Calculate DX as 100 × abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI), and ADX as the weighted moving average of DX. Use the current ADX-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish flip signal = SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND its AI trend classification is bullish.
Calculation: Bearish flip signal = SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND its AI trend classification is bearish.
Long: SuperTrend 1 direction is bullish AND SuperTrend 1 AI trend classification is bullish AND SuperTrend 2 direction is bullish AND SuperTrend 2 AI trend classification is bullish AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX > 20 AND +DI > -DI))
Short: SuperTrend 1 direction is bearish AND SuperTrend 1 AI trend classification is bearish AND SuperTrend 2 direction is bearish AND SuperTrend 2 AI trend classification is bearish AND (ADX filter disabled OR (ADX > 20 AND -DI > +DI))
Exit: long, low <= SuperTrend 1 - (ATR 1 × SuperTrend 1 factor) AND neither SuperTrend has a bullish flip signal
Exit: short, high >= SuperTrend 1 + (ATR 1 × SuperTrend 1 factor) AND neither SuperTrend has a bearish flip signal
