Calculation: Daily VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 since the current day began, including the forming day bar
Calculation: Hourly VWAP = daily VWAP calculated from hourly bars, using the forming hourly bar where available and otherwise the latest completed hourly bar
Long: close crossed above Daily VWAP within the lookback AND the lowest low since that crossover <= current Daily VWAP AND close > Daily VWAP AND close > open AND (rejection filter disabled OR lower wick > abs(close - open) * wick ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= average volume * volume factor) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - Daily VWAP) > minimum distance * ATR) AND (hourly bias filter disabled OR close > Hourly VWAP) AND session filter disabled OR current bar is in the selected session AND trades today < daily trade limit AND position is flat
Short: short setups enabled AND close crossed below Daily VWAP within the lookback AND the highest high since that crossover >= current Daily VWAP AND close < Daily VWAP AND close < open AND (rejection filter disabled OR upper wick > abs(close - open) * wick ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= average volume * volume factor) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - Daily VWAP) > minimum distance * ATR) AND (hourly bias filter disabled OR close < Hourly VWAP) AND session filter disabled OR current bar is in the selected session AND trades today < daily trade limit AND position is flat
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop-loss ATR multiple * ATR OR high >= entry price + take-profit ATR multiple * ATR
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop-loss ATR multiple * ATR OR low <= entry price - take-profit ATR multiple * ATR
