Calculation: Selected moving averages = one selected MA type applied to close at six progressively longer lookbacks; each slope compares its current value with the prior chart bar
Long: all six moving averages are rising OR the shortest one is rising and the other five are falling OR the two shortest are rising and the other four are falling OR the three shortest are rising and the other three are falling OR the four shortest are rising and the two longest are falling OR the five shortest are rising and the longest is falling
Short: all six moving averages are falling OR the shortest one is falling and the other five are rising OR the two shortest are falling and the other four are rising OR the three shortest are falling and the other three are rising OR the four shortest are falling and the two longest are rising OR the five shortest are falling and the longest is rising
