Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart bars; evaluate signals on closed bars
Calculation: Fresh low/high = pattern's low/high extreme is below/above the corresponding extreme of the immediately preceding pivot-lookback bars
Calculation: Bullish/bearish engulf = previous candle is red/green and current candle is green/red, with current real body covering the previous real body
Calculation: Compressed base = high-to-low range of the base-lookback bars immediately before the signal bar <= ATR × maximum base-range multiple
Calculation: Pole = largest real body among the pole-search-window bars immediately before the flag bars; pole is large when its body >= ATR × minimum pole-body multiple
Calculation: Tight flag = high-to-low range of the flag-consolidation bars immediately before the signal bar <= ATR × maximum flag-range multiple
Calculation: Strong close up/down = current close is within the required fraction of its high/low range; apply to the candles specified by the pattern
Calculation: Long stop = for small candle or engulf, lower low of the two pattern candles; for compression breakout, base low; for flag, flag low; for soldiers, lowest low of the three candles
Calculation: Short stop = for small candle or engulf, higher high of the two pattern candles; for compression breakout, base high; for flag, flag high; for crows, highest high of the three candles
Calculation: Take profit = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk distance × reward-risk multiple, where risk distance is the absolute difference between entry price and stop
Long: (two consecutive green candles, both bodies <= ATR × small-body maximum, first candle's lower wick > 0 and >= its body × minimum wick/body ratio, and first candle makes a fresh low) OR (bullish engulf and engulf candles make a fresh low, with any enabled size, volume, and strong-close requirements met) OR (compressed base and green signal candle body >= ATR × minimum breakout-body multiple and close > base high + ATR × breakout buffer, with any enabled volume, strong-close, and fresh-range requirements met) OR (large pole and tight flag and green signal candle body >= ATR × minimum flag-breakout-body multiple and close > flag high, and, when reversal-only is enabled, pole is red) OR (three consecutive green candles each body >= ATR × minimum three-candle body, each close higher than the previous close, each opens within the prior candle's real body when required, each has a strong close when required, each close advances by the required ATR amount when required, and the three-candle span makes a fresh low); AND ADX >= its threshold or enabled ADX-rising override (ADX > its value at the longer lookback) or enabled volatility-breakout override (signal-bar range >= ATR × its range multiple), when ADX filter is required; AND ATR >= its own moving average when range filter is required; AND no accepted long or short signal occurred within the cooldown lookback when cooldown is required; AND long stop < entry price (entry price is signal-bar close)
Short: (two consecutive red candles, both bodies <= ATR × small-body maximum, first candle's upper wick > 0 and >= its body × minimum wick/body ratio, and first candle makes a fresh high) OR (bearish engulf and engulf candles make a fresh high, with any enabled size, volume, and strong-close requirements met) OR (compressed base and red signal candle body >= ATR × minimum breakout-body multiple and close < base low - ATR × breakout buffer, with any enabled volume, strong-close, and fresh-range requirements met) OR (large pole and tight flag and red signal candle body >= ATR × minimum flag-breakout-body multiple and close < flag low, and, when reversal-only is enabled, pole is green) OR (three consecutive red candles each body >= ATR × minimum three-candle body, each close lower than the previous close, each opens within the prior candle's real body when required, each has a strong close when required, each close advances by the required ATR amount when required, and the three-candle span makes a fresh high); AND ADX >= its threshold or enabled ADX-rising override (ADX > its value at the longer lookback) or enabled volatility-breakout override (signal-bar range >= ATR × its range multiple), when ADX filter is required; AND ATR >= its own moving average when range filter is required; AND no accepted long or short signal occurred within the cooldown lookback when cooldown is required; AND short stop > entry price (entry price is signal-bar close)
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long take profit (check stop first if both are touched on the same bar)
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short take profit (check stop first if both are touched on the same bar)
