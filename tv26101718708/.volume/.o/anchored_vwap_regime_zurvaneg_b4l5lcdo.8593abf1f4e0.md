Calculation: Anchored VWAP = weighted mean of the selected price source since the current anchor, using volume weights or equal time weights when configured or volume is unavailable; include the current, still-forming bar.
Calculation: Effective deviation = maximum of the anchored weighted standard deviation and ATR multiplied by a floor factor.
Calculation: Acceptance gate = bullish after the required consecutive closes above Anchored VWAP plus a dead-zone multiple of Effective deviation; bearish after the required consecutive closes below Anchored VWAP minus that amount; otherwise retain the last accepted side. Reset at each anchor.
Calculation: Trend spread = (fast exponential volume- or time-weighted average of the price source minus the corresponding slow average) / ATR; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: Rotation count = number of changes between bullish and bearish closes outside the dead zone in the recent lookback, clipped to the current anchor.
Calculation: Trend-up candidate = mature anchor and either (prior regime is Trend Up, Acceptance gate is not bearish, and Trend spread is above the negative exit threshold) or (Acceptance gate is bullish, Trend spread is at least the entry threshold, and Rotation count is at most its maximum). After a previously confirmed Trend Up, use the lower resumption threshold. Balance takes precedence when Rotation count reaches its balance threshold.
Calculation: Trend-down candidate = mature anchor and either (prior regime is Trend Down, Acceptance gate is not bullish, and Trend spread is below the positive exit threshold) or (Acceptance gate is bearish, Trend spread is at most the negative entry threshold, and Rotation count is at most its maximum). After a previously confirmed Trend Down, use the lower resumption threshold. Balance takes precedence when Rotation count reaches its balance threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed Trend Up/Down = the corresponding candidate persists for the required consecutive closed bars.
Long: Confirmed Trend Up OR close returns inside the lower saturation boundary after the previous bar closed at or below that boundary, unless the optional trend filter is enabled and the confirmed regime is Trend Down.
Short: Confirmed Trend Down OR close returns inside the upper saturation boundary after the previous bar closed at or above that boundary, unless the optional trend filter is enabled and the confirmed regime is Trend Up.
