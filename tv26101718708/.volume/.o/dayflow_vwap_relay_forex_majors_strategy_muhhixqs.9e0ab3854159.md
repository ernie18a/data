Calculation: Daily anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 since the start of the current daily session, including the current completed chart bar; its upper and lower bands are the VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of the session’s volume-weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: VWAP residual = close − daily anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Expansion ratio = standard deviation of VWAP residual over a lookback ÷ standard deviation of close over the same lookback.
Calculation: Residual upper/lower thresholds = 75th/25th linear-interpolated percentiles of VWAP residual over the same lookback.
Calculation: VWAP slope = current linear-regression estimate of daily anchored VWAP over the lookback − its estimate one bar earlier.
Calculation: Micro flow = average of the signs (+1, 0, −1) of consecutive close changes across up to the configured number of latest lower-timeframe transitions within the current chart bar; use completed lower-timeframe bars and return 0 if fewer than two are available.
Calculation: ATR = Wilder-smoothed average true range over its configured lookback.
Long: (expansion ratio < configured expansion threshold AND VWAP residual ≤ residual lower threshold OR expansion ratio ≥ configured expansion threshold AND close > upper VWAP band AND VWAP slope > 0) AND micro flow > configured positive threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within configured session) AND (no position has closed yet OR bars since last closed position ≥ configured cooldown) AND long side enabled.
Short: (expansion ratio < configured expansion threshold AND VWAP residual ≥ residual upper threshold OR expansion ratio ≥ configured expansion threshold AND close < lower VWAP band AND VWAP slope < 0) AND micro flow < negative configured threshold AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within configured session) AND (no position has closed yet OR bars since last closed position ≥ configured cooldown) AND short side enabled.
Exit: long, short decision is true OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured target multiple.
Exit: short, long decision is true OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured target multiple.
