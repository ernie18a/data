Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low from the first 15 minutes of the regular session, fixed when that interval ends
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the most recent confirmed swing
Calculation: Timeframe sweep bias = scan prior bars, newest first, for the most recent bullish sweep (low below the prior bar's low, high above that low, and close above it) or bearish sweep (high above the prior bar's high and close below it); compare the current timeframe bar's close with the sweep bar's close: bullish sweep with close at/above it is bullish, below it is broken; bearish sweep with close at/below it is bearish, above it is broken; no sweep is neutral. Calculate separately on 4-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly bars; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish timeframe count = number of 4-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly biases that are bullish or have a bearish sweep broken
Calculation: Bearish timeframe count = number of 4-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly biases that are bearish or have a bullish sweep broken
Calculation: Confirmed daily-range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured daily lookback, using completed daily bars
Long: Close above the most recent confirmed swing high OR low below the most recent confirmed swing low AND close above it (bullish liquidity sweep) OR bullish opening-range breakout was previously confirmed AND time is 9:30–10:30 ET AND low reaches the opening-range high within a tick-based tolerance AND close above the opening-range high AND low below the previous bar's low AND high above the previous bar's low AND close above the previous bar's low AND bullish timeframe count >= 3 AND daily S5FD close <= 85 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its longer-lookback average)
Short: Close below the most recent confirmed swing low OR high above the most recent confirmed swing high AND close below it (bearish liquidity sweep) OR bearish opening-range breakout was previously confirmed AND time is 9:30–10:30 ET AND high reaches the opening-range low within a tick-based tolerance AND close below the opening-range low AND high above the previous bar's high AND close below the previous bar's high AND bearish timeframe count >= 3 AND daily S5FD close >= 15 AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume > its longer-lookback average)
