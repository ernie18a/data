Calculation: Session VWAP = current-session cumulative (H+L+C)/3 weighted by volume, including the current bar; weighted standard deviation uses the same bars and weights.
Calculation: Session profile = use the latest up to N bars of the current session, including the current bar; divide their combined high-low range into R equal rows, assign each bar's volume to the row containing its (H+L+C)/3, and split volume into buy/sell using buy share = clamp(0.17 + 0.43×(C−L)/max(H−L,tick) + 0.20×|C−O|/max(H−L,tick) + 0.20×(C≥O), 0.05, 0.95). POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume row; VAH/VAL are the upper/lower edges of the contiguous rows expanded outward from POC, each step adding the larger adjacent row volume until the included volume reaches the value-area fraction of total volume.
Calculation: Bid/ask shelf power = maximum, over profile rows, of the positive bid/ask signed shelf; signed shelf = (2×buy fraction−1)×(row volume / maximum row volume). Bid uses signed shelf; ask uses its negation.
Calculation: Profile skew = (total buy volume−total sell volume)/total profile volume.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative volume×(2×buy share−1), reset at each session; fast and slow CVD are EMAs with shorter and longer lookbacks. CVD impulse = (fast EMA−slow EMA)/max(|slow EMA|, current volume).
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = on a confirmed bar, a newly confirmed price low pivot is lower than the prior paired price low pivot while its CVD low pivot is higher than the prior paired CVD low pivot. Bearish divergence = a newly confirmed price high pivot is higher than the prior paired price high pivot while its CVD high pivot is lower than the prior paired CVD high pivot. Pivots use the configured left/right bars and are evaluated when confirmed.
Calculation: Bid/ask quality = clamp(32 + location score + clamp(shelf power×34,0,18) + clamp((directional CVD impulse−impulse threshold)×85,0,20) + 14 if matching divergence + compression score + 18×max(directionally signed profile skew,0), 0,100). Bid location score is 18 if close is above session VWAP, else 10 if above POC, else 0; ask mirrors below. Compression score is 8 if value-area width/ATR≤2.6, 4 if ≤4, else 0. Directional CVD impulse is CVD impulse for bid and its negation for ask.
Calculation: POC reclaim = confirmed close crosses above POC from prior close at or below it; POC rejection = confirmed close crosses below POC from prior close at or above it. Value reclaim = low≤VAL+max(0.20×ATR,5 ticks), close>VAL, and close>open. Value rejection = high≥VAH−max(0.20×ATR,5 ticks), close<VAH, and close<open.
Long: bullish CVD divergence OR (confirmed bar AND active session AND close>session VWAP AND close≥VAL AND close≤session VWAP+2×weighted standard deviation AND fast CVD EMA>slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse>impulse threshold AND bid quality≥quality gate AND bid quality>ask quality AND (POC reclaim OR value reclaim OR bullish CVD divergence OR bid shelf power>shelf gate) AND no auction signal occurred within the cooldown interval).
Short: bearish CVD divergence OR (confirmed bar AND active session AND close<session VWAP AND close≤VAH AND close≥session VWAP−2×weighted standard deviation AND fast CVD EMA<slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse<−impulse threshold AND ask quality≥quality gate AND ask quality>bid quality AND (POC rejection OR value rejection OR bearish CVD divergence OR ask shelf power>shelf gate) AND no auction signal occurred within the cooldown interval).
Exit: long, confirmed close<session VWAP OR close<POC OR bearish CVD divergence
Exit: short, confirmed close>session VWAP OR close>POC OR bullish CVD divergence
