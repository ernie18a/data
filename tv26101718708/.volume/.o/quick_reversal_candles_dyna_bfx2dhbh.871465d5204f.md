Calculation: Bullish pattern = bullish pin bar OR bullish engulfing bar OR bullish inside-bar breakout. A bullish pin bar has a lower wick at least the configured wick ratio times the greater of body size and minimum tick, longer than the upper wick, and at least half the candle range (or minimum tick). A bullish engulfing bar closes above its open, the prior bar closes below its open, and its body contains the prior body. A bullish inside-bar breakout closes above the high of a bar whose range is inside the preceding bar's range.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = bearish pin bar OR bearish engulfing bar OR bearish inside-bar breakout. A bearish pin bar has an upper wick at least the configured wick ratio times the greater of body size and minimum tick, longer than the lower wick, and at least half the candle range (or minimum tick). A bearish engulfing bar closes below its open, the prior bar closes above its open, and its body contains the prior body. A bearish inside-bar breakout closes below the low of a bar whose range is inside the preceding bar's range.
Calculation: Activity points = 20 × clamp((signal-bar activity / signal-bar activity moving average − 1) / (activity multiplier − 1), 0, 1). Activity is volume if cumulative volume is positive, otherwise candle range; the moving average uses the same activity series.
Calculation: Bull location points = 20 × clamp(1 − (signal-bar low − lowest low over the preceding swing lookback bars) / (ATR × maximum swing distance in ATRs), 0, 1). Bear location points = 20 × clamp(1 − (highest high over the preceding swing lookback bars − signal-bar high) / (ATR × maximum swing distance in ATRs), 0, 1). Use ATR on the signal bar; use the greater of ATR and minimum tick.
Calculation: Bull prior-move points = 20 × clamp((close N bars before the signal bar − signal-bar close) / (ATR × minimum prior move in ATRs), 0, 1). Bear prior-move points = 20 × clamp((signal-bar close − close N bars before the signal bar) / (ATR × minimum prior move in ATRs), 0, 1). N is the prior-move lookback; use ATR on the signal bar, floored at minimum tick.
Calculation: Bull conviction = 40 + activity points + bull location points + bull prior-move points when a bullish pattern exists. Bear conviction = 40 + activity points + bear location points + bear prior-move points when a bearish pattern exists.
Long: bullish pattern AND bull conviction >= minimum conviction score
Short: bearish pattern AND bear conviction >= minimum conviction score
