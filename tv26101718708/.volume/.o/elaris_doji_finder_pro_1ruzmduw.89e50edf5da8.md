Calculation: Body fraction = absolute(close - open) / (high - low); use 100 when high = low.
Calculation: Upper wick fraction = (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low); lower wick fraction = (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low); use 0 for both when high = low.
Calculation: Dragonfly Doji = body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND lower wick fraction >= long-wick threshold AND upper wick fraction <= small-wick threshold.
Calculation: Gravestone Doji = body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND upper wick fraction >= long-wick threshold AND lower wick fraction <= small-wick threshold.
Calculation: Long-legged Doji = body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND upper wick fraction >= long-wick threshold AND lower wick fraction >= long-wick threshold.
Calculation: Neutral Doji = body fraction <= maximum body fraction AND not Dragonfly, Gravestone, or Long-legged Doji.
Calculation: Last support and resistance = most recent confirmed pivot low and pivot high, respectively; a pivot is confirmed after the configured number of bars to its right. Use the latest confirmed levels available on the current bar.
Calculation: Near support/resistance = absolute(current low - last support) / absolute(current high - last resistance) <= ATR * proximity threshold, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish context = Dragonfly Doji OR near support OR close > open; bearish context = Gravestone Doji OR near resistance OR close < open.
Long: at least one enabled Doji type is present AND high - low >= current-bar ATR * minimum-range threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume >= current-bar SMA of volume * volume multiplier) AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR near support OR near resistance) AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish context AND close < current-bar EMA OR bearish context AND close > current-bar EMA OR Long-legged Doji) AND bullish context AND NOT bearish context; evaluate on bar close when close confirmation is enabled.
Short: at least one enabled Doji type is present AND high - low >= current-bar ATR * minimum-range threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume >= current-bar SMA of volume * volume multiplier) AND (support/resistance filter disabled OR near support OR near resistance) AND (trend filter disabled OR bullish context AND close < current-bar EMA OR bearish context AND close > current-bar EMA OR Long-legged Doji) AND bearish context AND NOT bullish context; evaluate on bar close when close confirmation is enabled.
