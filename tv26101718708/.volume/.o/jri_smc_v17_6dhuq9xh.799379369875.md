Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when each of the latest three confirmed swing highs and latest three confirmed swing lows is strictly higher than its predecessor; bearish when both sequences are strictly lower. Confirm a swing using the swing-length number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Structure break = bullish when close crosses above the second-latest confirmed swing high; bearish when close crosses below the second-latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Retest = after a bearish structure break, high reaches within 0.1% below its broken swing low and close remains below it; after a bullish structure break, low reaches within 0.1% above its broken swing high and close remains above it. Clear the corresponding retest state when it occurs; set it on the corresponding structure break.
Calculation: Confirmed supply or demand zone = a swing high or low price ±0.1%, respectively, with at least two confirmed swing highs or lows in that zone. Price is in the zone when close is within its bounds.
Calculation: Confirmation score = count of these nine true conditions: either structure bias exists; either the current high exceeds the previous 10-bar high while close is below that high or the current low falls below the previous 10-bar low while close is above that low; absolute candle body exceeds twice its 20-bar average; either a bullish gap (low above the high two bars ago and prior close above that high) or bearish gap (high below the low two bars ago and prior close below that low); either structure break; either retest; RSI is above 60 with bullish structure bias or below 40 with bearish structure bias; close is in a confirmed supply or demand zone; ATR times the stop multiplier is positive.
Long: bullish structure bias AND bullish structure break AND confirmation score >= minimum confirmations AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the London or New York session on weekdays) AND no active signal lock AND no active opposite-side signal AND same-side signal is absent or more than its cooldown bars ago. Clear an active signal when confirmation score falls below minimum confirmations minus two; set the lock after the configured consecutive-loss limit and clear it on a new day or after a target win.
Short: bearish structure bias AND bearish structure break AND confirmation score >= minimum confirmations AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the London or New York session on weekdays) AND no active signal lock AND no active opposite-side signal AND same-side signal is absent or more than its cooldown bars ago. Clear an active signal when confirmation score falls below minimum confirmations minus two; set the lock after the configured consecutive-loss limit and clear it on a new day or after a target win.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR high >= entry price plus three times that stop distance.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus entry-bar ATR times stop multiplier OR low <= entry price minus three times that stop distance.
