Calculation: Fast, middle, slow, and trend EMAs = exponential moving averages of close with successively longer lookbacks; slope is rising/falling when the EMA is above/below its value two bars earlier.
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line, using a shorter fast lookback than slow lookback; acceleration up/down means the current histogram is greater/less than each of its two prior values.
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic K = the stochastic of close, high, and low smoothed by a moving average; D = a moving average of K. K crossing above/below D means it was at or below/above D on the prior bar and is now above/below it.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band. Consolidating = current width is below its moving average over a longer lookback multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: Latest resistance/support = the latest confirmed pivot high/low, each requiring the configured number of bars on both sides; use only pivots confirmed by the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout = latest resistance exists, current close is above it and prior close is at or below it, volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier, MACD histogram is positive, and not consolidating. Breakdown = latest support exists, current close is below it and prior close is at or above it, the same volume condition holds, MACD histogram is negative, and not consolidating.
Calculation: False breakout = latest resistance exists and a close within the configured prior-bar lookback was above it while current close is below it. False breakdown = latest support exists and a close within that lookback was below it while current close is above it.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = close is above its value 14 bars ago, RSI is below its value 14 bars ago, and RSI is above 55. Bullish RSI divergence = close is below its value 14 bars ago, RSI is above its value 14 bars ago, and RSI is below 45.
Calculation: Consolidation filter = current Bollinger width is below its longer-lookback average width times the squeeze threshold.
Calculation: Long bonus = count of these true conditions: fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA; close > trend EMA; RSI > its value 3 bars ago; K crosses above D with prior K < 30; +DI > −DI; RSI crosses above 50; latest support exists and distance from close to support < ATR times a proximity multiplier; breakout.
Calculation: Short bonus = count of these true conditions: fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA; close < trend EMA; RSI < its value 3 bars ago; K crosses below D with prior K > 70; −DI > +DI; RSI crosses below 50; latest resistance exists and distance from close to resistance < ATR times a proximity multiplier; breakdown.
Long: (ADX > its threshold AND fast and middle EMAs rising AND RSI is between 38 and 72 inclusive AND MACD histogram accelerating up AND close >= low + 0.45 × (high − low) AND close <= trend EMA × (1 + overextension percentage) AND NOT bearish RSI divergence AND NOT false breakout AND NOT consolidating AND long bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses above signal line with prior histogram < 0 OR K crosses above D with prior K < 35 OR RSI crosses above 50 with RSI two bars ago < 44 OR fast EMA crosses above middle EMA with middle EMA rising and ADX > its trigger threshold OR breakout) AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long signal) OR breakout OR false breakdown.
Short: (ADX > its threshold AND fast and middle EMAs falling AND RSI is between 28 and 62 inclusive AND MACD histogram accelerating down AND close <= high − 0.45 × (high − low) AND close >= trend EMA × (1 − overextension percentage) AND NOT bullish RSI divergence AND NOT false breakdown AND NOT consolidating AND short bonus >= minimum bonus AND (MACD line crosses below signal line with prior histogram > 0 OR K crosses below D with prior K > 65 OR RSI crosses below 50 with RSI two bars ago > 56 OR fast EMA crosses below middle EMA with middle EMA falling and ADX > its trigger threshold OR breakdown) AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior short signal) OR breakdown OR false breakout
