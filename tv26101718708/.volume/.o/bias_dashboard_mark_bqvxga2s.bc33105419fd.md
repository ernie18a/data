Calculation: Trend direction and strength = Up when the latest confirmed pivot high and low are both higher than their respective previous pivots; Down when both are lower; otherwise neither. Trend is Strong or Moderate when that direction exists and the absolute linear-regression slope over the longer trend lookback, divided by ATR, meets the upper or lower threshold respectively; otherwise Weak.
Calculation: Momentum/volume tier = Count rising and falling candles in the shorter recent momentum window; the dominant direction qualifies when its count meets the required share of the window. Strong requires the average candle body-to-range ratio and current volume relative to its moving average to meet their higher thresholds; Moderate requires their lower thresholds; otherwise Weak. No qualifying direction has tier Weak.
Calculation: Adjusted directional conviction scores = For each side, base score is zero unless trend direction matches that side. Otherwise use this matrix by trend tier (Strong, Moderate, Weak) and matching-side momentum tier (Strong, Moderate, Weak): (highest, next, middle); (next, middle, lower); (middle, lower, zero). Add one rank if both VWAP and EMA votes agree, subtract one rank if both fight, otherwise add zero; clamp to the available conviction ranks. VWAP agrees long when close is above current session VWAP and VWAP is rising, and agrees short when close is below it and falling; the opposite position or slope fights. Determine slope from current VWAP versus its older lookback, falling back to a shorter lookback when unavailable. EMA agrees long when short-period > medium-period > long-period EMA, and agrees short in reverse; the reverse stack fights. Mixed EMA alignment is neutral.
Long: adjusted long conviction score > adjusted short conviction score AND adjusted long conviction score > zero
Short: adjusted short conviction score > adjusted long conviction score AND adjusted short conviction score > zero
