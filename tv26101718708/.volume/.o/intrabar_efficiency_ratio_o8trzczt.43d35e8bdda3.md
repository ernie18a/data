Calculation: Intrabar Efficiency Ratio = (current chart-bar close minus previous chart-bar close) divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes across the current chart bar’s lower-timeframe intrabars; optionally multiply by the percentile rank of the absolute chart-bar close change over a configurable lookback, divided by 100.
Calculation: Short and long moving averages = the same selected moving-average type applied to the Intrabar Efficiency Ratio, with the short lookback no longer than the long lookback.
Long: Short moving average > long moving average
Short: Short moving average ≤ long moving average
Long: The 100-bar percentile rank of any of the short, medium, or long moving averages is >50 and ≤90
Short: The 100-bar percentile rank of any of the short, medium, or long moving averages is ≥10 and ≤50
