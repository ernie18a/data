Calculation: Fast/slow EMA separation = (shorter-lookback EMA of lower-timeframe closes − longer-lookback EMA of lower-timeframe closes) / longer-lookback EMA × 100
Calculation: Window move = percentage change from the close at the start of the rolling horizon to the latest lower-timeframe close
Calculation: RSI vote = +1 above its bullish threshold, −1 below its bearish threshold, otherwise 0; compute RSI on resampled lower-timeframe closes
Calculation: Volume vote = sign of the window move when current-window volume / mean volume of preceding equal-length blocks meets its surge threshold; otherwise 0
Calculation: EMA vote = +1 when fast/slow EMA separation exceeds its positive deadband, −1 when below its negative deadband, otherwise 0
Calculation: Window vote = +1 when window move exceeds its positive deadband, −1 when below its negative deadband, otherwise 0
Calculation: Momentum vote = sign of the EMA-smoothed lower-timeframe close returns when it exceeds its positive deadband or is below its negative deadband; otherwise 0
Calculation: Acceleration vote = sign of current smoothed momentum minus its value at a shorter lag when that difference exceeds its positive deadband or is below its negative deadband; otherwise 0
Calculation: Tick vote = +1 when net up-minus-down close changes over the short lookback reaches its positive threshold, −1 when it reaches its negative threshold, otherwise 0
Long: Buffer ready AND absolute window move meets the strong-trend threshold AND fast/slow EMA separation × window move > 0 AND at least one of RSI, momentum, acceleration, and tick votes is positive and none is negative AND the number of positive votes among EMA, RSI, volume, window, momentum, acceleration, and tick exceeds the number of negative votes AND previous bar was ready and did not classify as UPTREND
Short: Buffer ready AND absolute window move meets the strong-trend threshold AND fast/slow EMA separation × window move < 0 AND at least one of RSI, momentum, acceleration, and tick votes is negative and none is positive AND the number of negative votes among EMA, RSI, volume, window, momentum, acceleration, and tick exceeds the number of positive votes AND previous bar was ready and did not classify as DOWNTREND
