Calculation: Support/resistance zones = zones from swing highs/lows on the chart, Daily, and Weekly bars; order blocks (the last opposite-color candle before a volume-confirmed impulsive move that closes beyond the highs/lows of bars 2–5); highest-volume price bins over a lookback; three-bar fair-value gaps with an impulsive middle candle; equal-high/low clusters; anchored VWAP zones; and prior-day/week and premarket highs/lows and unfilled opening gaps. Retain zones until invalidated; after two closes through a zone, reverse its support/resistance side; remove it after three violations.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted 0–100 combination of touch volume, confluence, timeframe origin, touch count, creation volume, regime fit, freshness, and (when enabled) structure fit, less a violation penalty; add historical bounce/break and flip bonuses. Grade A is score ≥80, B ≥65, C ≥50, D <50.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart bars.
Calculation: Market regime = SQUEEZE when Bollinger bandwidth ranks below its lookback bottom 10% and ATR-percent rank is below 15%; otherwise STRONG_BULL when ADX >25 and EMA slope >0.5%, WEAK_BULL when ADX >18 and slope >0.1%, STRONG_BEAR when ADX >25 and slope <−0.5%, WEAK_BEAR when ADX >18 and slope <−0.1%, otherwise RANGE. EMA slope compares the EMA with its value five bars earlier.
Calculation: Market structure = BULL after a confirmed close above the latest unbroken swing high; BEAR after a confirmed close below the latest unbroken swing low; otherwise retain the prior state (initially RANGE). Swings require the configured left and right bars.
Calculation: Luxy Energy Index = 0–100 exhaustion score based on extension from VWAP, five-bar velocity, and volume exhaustion.
Calculation: Approach speed = absolute change in close over two bars divided by ATR; volume ratio = volume divided by the 20-bar average volume.
Calculation: Bullish reversal trigger = bullish engulfing body, bullish pin bar, or inside-bar break above the prior bar's high. Bearish reversal trigger = bearish engulfing body, bearish pin bar, or inside-bar break below the prior bar's low. A pin has a rejection wick at least the configured fraction of candle range, body at most 35% of range, and close in the rejection direction's half. A trigger remains valid for the configured number of bars.
Calculation: Long plan = selected highest-score qualifying support zone's top + 0.1 ATR entry; bottom − 0.5 ATR stop; nearest qualifying resistance midpoint above entry target. Short plan = selected highest-score qualifying resistance zone's bottom − 0.1 ATR entry; top + 0.5 ATR stop; nearest qualifying support midpoint below entry target. Risk/reward = target distance divided by entry-to-stop distance.
Long: A support zone has score >60, close is above its top and its midpoint is <1.5 ATR away, regime is STRONG_BULL, WEAK_BULL, RANGE, or SQUEEZE, structure is not BEAR if structure gating is enabled, a qualifying resistance target exists, risk/reward meets the configured minimum, AND either candle-trigger gating is disabled or a bullish reversal trigger is still valid and the close is within the configured ATR distance of the zone top.
Short: A resistance zone has score >60, close is below its bottom and its midpoint is <1.5 ATR away, regime is STRONG_BEAR, WEAK_BEAR, RANGE, or SQUEEZE, structure is not BULL if structure gating is enabled, a qualifying support target exists, risk/reward meets the configured minimum, AND either candle-trigger gating is disabled or a bearish reversal trigger is still valid and the close is within the configured ATR distance of the zone bottom. Use a short plan only when no qualifying long plan is selected.
Long: Within 1.2 ATR of a touched zone, Luxy Energy Index ≥70; OR the index is ≤30 at a resistance while close > close two bars ago; OR the index is neutral/unavailable and speed <0.20 ATR with volume ratio <0.80; OR the index is ≤30, or neutral/unavailable with speed >0.40 ATR and volume ratio >1.50, at a resistance while close is not > close two bars ago. Apply the two-bar approach comparison only when the index is ≤30 or the speed/volume condition indicates fast movement.
Short: Within 1.2 ATR of a touched zone, Luxy Energy Index ≥70 at a resistance; OR the index is ≤30 at a support while close < close two bars ago; OR the index is neutral/unavailable and speed <0.20 ATR with volume ratio <0.80 at a resistance; OR the index is ≤30, or neutral/unavailable with speed >0.40 ATR and volume ratio >1.50, at a support while close is not < close two bars ago. Apply the two-bar approach comparison only when the index is ≤30 or the speed/volume condition indicates fast movement.
Exit: long, low ≤ plan stop OR high ≥ plan target
Exit: short, high ≥ plan stop OR low ≤ plan target
