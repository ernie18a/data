Calculation: Bar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume signed positive when the lower-timeframe close is above its open and negative when below; use backtest bars to aggregate the lower timeframe. Bar delta direction is positive or negative.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = volume-weighted average of the configured price source over the configured rolling or anchored window of chart bars, including the current forming bar; use only bars admitted by the configured session window.
Calculation: VWAP deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of the same source over the same window; its upper and lower value edges are VWAP plus and minus the configured multiple of this deviation.
Calculation: VWAP slope direction = current VWAP minus VWAP at the configured longer lookback; rising or falling only when the change exceeds the configured flat threshold.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the prior same-session-time-slot volume baseline when sufficiently warmed up, otherwise divided by the rolling volume baseline.
Calculation: Aggression print = absolute delta's modified z-score against the rolling median and median absolute deviation exceeds its configured threshold, relative volume exceeds its configured threshold, and absolute delta exceeds its configured floor; emit every qualifying bar or only a new session record for its print type, as configured.
Calculation: Absorption print = relative volume exceeds its configured threshold and either (as selected by the configured range/body mode) volume per range tick or volume per body tick divided by its own rolling median exceeds its configured threshold while the corresponding range or body divided by its rolling median is below its configured threshold; apply the same configured every-bar or session-record rule as for aggression.
Calculation: Print type = reversal when a qualifying print reaches exactly one VWAP value edge and flow fails there; flow fails when absorption delta points into that edge or aggression delta points back toward VWAP. Otherwise it is continuation when it is an aggression print whose close is on the delta side of VWAP; otherwise it is raw.
Calculation: Continuation score = count of these true conditions: close is on the delta side of VWAP; VWAP slope agrees with delta; close is in the configured strong-close portion of the bar in the delta direction.
Calculation: Reversal score = 1 plus one if flow fails at the reached value edge plus one if the close is in the configured strong-close portion of the bar toward VWAP; it is zero when the bar does not reach exactly one value edge.
Calculation: EMA bias = direction with a strict majority, or unanimity when configured, of enabled chart, daily, and weekly moving-average votes; each vote is up when close is above its average and down when below, and may additionally require the average's slope to agree. Daily and weekly averages and slopes use their configured live forming or last completed day/week values; bias votes use completed day/week values when configured.
Long: confirmed aggression print with positive delta OR confirmed absorption print with negative delta OR continuation print with positive delta and score at least the configured minimum OR reversal print at the lower value edge with score at least the configured minimum OR reversal setup triggers OR trend setup triggers; apply the configured setup bias filter where enabled.
Short: confirmed aggression print with negative delta OR confirmed absorption print with positive delta OR continuation print with negative delta and score at least the configured minimum OR reversal print at the upper value edge with score at least the configured minimum OR reversal setup triggers OR trend setup triggers; apply the configured setup bias filter where enabled.
Calculation: Reversal setup arm = qualifying reversal print with score at least the configured arm minimum, and absorption-only unless any reversal print is allowed; save direction and the print's opposite extreme as invalidation price. It expires after the configured number of bars, is canceled by a close beyond invalidation, and requires a close back inside the corresponding VWAP value edge plus the configured slope rule (off, aligned, or already aligned/improving).
Calculation: Trend setup arm = continuation print with score at least the configured arm minimum, VWAP slope agreeing with its direction, and configured bias filter passing; after arming, require the configured number of consecutive closes on that side of VWAP without VWAP slope turning against it, canceling on a failed hold. A zero hold count triggers on the arm bar.
