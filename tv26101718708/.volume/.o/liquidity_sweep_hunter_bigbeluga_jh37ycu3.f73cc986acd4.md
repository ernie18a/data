Calculation: Volume change (%) = (volume − previous bar volume) / volume × 100
Calculation: Active support/resistance levels = confirmed pivot lows/highs with volume change at the pivot bar above the threshold; each pivot uses equal lookbacks before and after the pivot and becomes available after the later lookback bars complete. Remove a level after a confirmed bar has high above it and low below it.
Long: Confirmed bar AND low below an active support level AND close above that level
Short: Confirmed bar AND high above an active resistance level AND close below that level
