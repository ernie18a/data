Calculation: Efficiency ratio = (current close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over the same N bars; direction gate = max(configured floor, rolling mean + 0.5 × standard deviation of absolute efficiency ratio over the configured adaptive lookback)
Calculation: Confirmed trend = efficiency ratio > direction gate or < −direction gate, with absolute close displacement over N bars ≥ configured minimum × ATR; ATR uses the configured lookback
Calculation: Commitment = signed body strength: body direction × |close − open| / max(high − low, minimum tick) × min(volume / 20-bar average volume, 3) / 3 when volume is used; otherwise body direction × 2 × |close − (high + low)/2| / max(high − low, minimum tick). Use volume in forced volume mode when available, or in auto mode for non-index instruments with positive volume; otherwise use the wick formula
Calculation: Commitment anomaly = (mean − current value) / standard deviation, where mean and standard deviation use prior bars in the configured trend-statistics lookback whose commitment, signed by the confirmed trend direction, is positive; current value is commitment signed by that direction
Calculation: Failure score = velocity strength × (1 + wick strength) × (1 + anomaly strength) × (1 + range strength); velocity strength = |close − prior close| / ATR; wick strength = min(trend-side wick / max(body size, minimum tick), 5) / 5; anomaly strength = min(max(commitment anomaly, 0), 3) / 3; range strength = min((high − low) / ATR, 3) / 3
Calculation: Target multiplier = 1 + min(failure score / max(pressure baseline, 0.0001), 1.5) × configured score nudge; pressure baseline = rolling mean + configured multiplier × standard deviation of the pressure meter over the configured baseline lookback. Pressure meter uses velocity strength when velocity reversal is present, otherwise half velocity strength, multiplied by (1 + wick strength) × (1 + anomaly strength) × (1 + range strength)
Long: confirmed bear trend AND at least the configured signal-mode count of these four conditions: close > prior close; lower wick / max(body size, minimum tick) ≥ configured wick ratio; commitment anomaly ≥ configured sigma; high − low ≥ configured range multiple × ATR. Signal mode requires either all four, at least three, or velocity reversal plus at least one other condition. Require sufficient history for the configured direction, trend-statistics, and adaptive lookbacks; bar is closed; no active trade; configured post-closure cooldown has elapsed
Short: confirmed bull trend AND at least the configured signal-mode count of these four conditions: close < prior close; upper wick / max(body size, minimum tick) ≥ configured wick ratio; commitment anomaly ≥ configured sigma; high − low ≥ configured range multiple × ATR. Signal mode requires either all four, at least three, or velocity reversal plus at least one other condition. Require sufficient history for the configured direction, trend-statistics, and adaptive lookbacks; bar is closed; no active trade; configured post-closure cooldown has elapsed
Exit: long, after the entry bar has closed, a later closed bar has high ≥ entry + configured target-2 ATR distance × target multiplier OR low ≤ entry − configured stop ATR distance
Exit: short, after the entry bar has closed, a later closed bar has low ≤ entry − configured target-2 ATR distance × target multiplier OR high ≥ entry + configured stop ATR distance
