Calculation: ATR = Average True Range of chart bars.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of typical price within the session.
Calculation: Volume EMA = exponential moving average of chart-bar volume.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars ago AND middle-bar range >= configured ATR multiple of current ATR AND gap size >= configured ATR multiple of current ATR AND previous bar’s Session VWAP > high two bars ago AND previous-bar volume > configured multiple of current Volume EMA AND middle-bar body / range > configured proportion.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars ago AND middle-bar range >= configured ATR multiple of current ATR AND gap size >= configured ATR multiple of current ATR AND previous bar’s Session VWAP < low two bars ago AND previous-bar volume > configured multiple of current Volume EMA AND middle-bar body / range > configured proportion.
Calculation: Bullish POI = within the configured impulse lookback, a close-to-close rise exceeds the configured ATR multiple of ATR at the impulse start and at least the configured rounded proportion of close-to-close steps are rising; before the impulse start, the first candle within the configured base lookback whose range is below the configured ATR multiple of its ATR supplies the POI zone.
Calculation: Bearish POI = within the configured impulse lookback, a close-to-close fall exceeds the configured ATR multiple of ATR at the impulse start and at least the configured rounded proportion of close-to-close steps are non-rising; before the impulse start, the first candle within the configured base lookback whose range is below the configured ATR multiple of its ATR supplies the POI zone.
Long: Bullish FVG OR (Bullish POI AND a qualifying tight-range base candle exists AND the current bar is confirmed AND the configured POI cooldown has elapsed).
Short: Bearish FVG OR (Bearish POI AND a qualifying tight-range base candle exists AND the current bar is confirmed AND the configured POI cooldown has elapsed).
