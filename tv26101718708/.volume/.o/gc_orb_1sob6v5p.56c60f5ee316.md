Calculation: Price partition = floor(close / partition size) × partition size to (floor(close / partition size) + 1) × partition size; partition size is instrument-dependent
Calculation: Near a gold level = close is within configured tolerance × price partition range of any partition-range level at 0%, 3%, 11%, 17%, 29%, 41%, 47%, 50%, 53%, 59%, 71%, 83%, 89%, 97%, or 100%
Calculation: INDICATOR gold candle = current New York hour + minute is within 1 of 3, 11, 17, 29, 41, 47, 53, 59, 71, 83, 89, or 97; near a gold level using the INDICATOR tolerance; time is 09:30–14:59 New York; and stage is ready. Stage is ready if a clue was hit during the current partition day, or a clue exists and the GBT date has passed, or the current bar is 11 or 17 bars after partition-day open. A clue is a gap or wick in the configured lookback whose size is within the configured tolerance of the current month’s partition number; a clue is hit when its midpoint is within 0.5% of close during an active GBT date or on bar 11 or 17 after partition-day open. Canonical GBT dates use the month’s configured GBT day, adjusted to the prior Friday or Monday when it falls on a weekend.
Calculation: DR bullish gold candle = current New York hour + minute equals a multiple of 3 from 3 through 99; near a gold level using the DR tolerance; time is 03:00–14:59 New York; Supertrend has held bullish direction for at least the configured minimum bars; and close is below 41% of the price partition range.
Calculation: DR bearish gold candle = current New York hour + minute equals a multiple of 3 from 3 through 99; near a gold level using the DR tolerance; time is 03:00–14:59 New York; Supertrend has held bearish direction for at least the configured minimum bars; and close is above 59% of the price partition range.
Calculation: Stop distance = configured fixed points, configured percentage of price partition range, or configured multiple of current-timeframe ATR, according to the selected stop method
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − stop distance; long target = entry price + stop distance × configured reward-to-risk ratio
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + stop distance; short target = entry price − stop distance × configured reward-to-risk ratio
Long: no open position AND ((INDICATOR mode AND INDICATOR gold candle AND close > open) OR (DR mode AND DR bullish gold candle))
Short: no open position AND ((INDICATOR mode AND INDICATOR gold candle AND close <= open) OR (DR mode AND DR bearish gold candle))
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
