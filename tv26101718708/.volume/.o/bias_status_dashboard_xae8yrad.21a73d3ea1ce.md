Calculation: Structure direction = 1 after close breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high, −1 after close breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Session sweep direction = 1 after price sweeps below the selected session’s running low, −1 after price sweeps above its running high; otherwise retain the previous direction. The selected session is Asia or the active session, as configured.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = bullish when at least two of (fast moving average > medium > slow, RSI ≥ bullish threshold, Stochastic RSI K > D and K > 50) are true and more are true than bearish counterparts; bearish by the inverse rule; otherwise neutral. Evaluate each configured higher timeframe using its latest completed bar.
Calculation: Overall score = sum of enabled component directions (trend, VWAP, momentum, structure, timeframe, session), with neutral = 0; enabled count = number of enabled components.
Long: fast moving average > medium moving average > slow moving average OR close > VWAP + ATR × VWAP neutral band OR bullish momentum score > bearish momentum score OR structure direction = 1 OR session sweep direction = 1 OR timeframe bias is bullish on at least the configured minimum number of timeframes and exceeds bearish count OR selected bias direction = 1 and absolute overall score / enabled count × 100 ≥ neutral-strength threshold
Short: fast moving average < medium moving average < slow moving average OR close < VWAP − ATR × VWAP neutral band OR bearish momentum score > bullish momentum score OR structure direction = −1 OR session sweep direction = −1 OR timeframe bias is bearish on at least the configured minimum number of timeframes and exceeds bullish count OR selected bias direction = −1 and absolute overall score / enabled count × 100 ≥ neutral-strength threshold
