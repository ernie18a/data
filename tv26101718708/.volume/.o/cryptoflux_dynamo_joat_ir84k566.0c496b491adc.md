Calculation: Volatility regime = ATR smoothed with a longer EMA, divided by close and expressed as a percentage; classify below the lower threshold as compression, between thresholds as expansion, and at or above the upper threshold as velocity.
Calculation: Bullish EMA ribbon = fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA; bearish EMA ribbon = fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA.
Calculation: Middle EMA slope = (middle EMA − its value at a shorter lookback ago) / that lookback; convert to degrees with arctan.
Calculation: Smoothed MACD histogram = EMA of the MACD histogram; adaptive baseline = EMA of the absolute raw MACD histogram over twice the smoothing lookback, multiplied by a sensitivity factor.
Calculation: Volume impulse = current volume > volume SMA × multiplier OR (current volume − volume SMA) / volume standard deviation > threshold.
Calculation: Cycle deviation = (close − cycle EMA) / cycle EMA × 100; bullish when above a positive threshold, bearish when below its negative.
Calculation: Squeeze release long = squeeze was on on the previous bar and is off now, close > previous bar's upper Bollinger Band, smoothed MACD histogram > 0, and its absolute value > adaptive baseline × release gate. Squeeze release short uses close < previous bar's lower Bollinger Band and smoothed MACD histogram < 0, with the same magnitude condition. Bollinger Bands use a shorter lookback and narrower multiplier than the Keltner Channel's range multiplier.
Calculation: Micro pullback long = bullish EMA ribbon, close ≤ fast EMA and > middle EMA, and the previous bar's low is the lowest low over the pullback lookback ending on that bar. Short = bearish EMA ribbon, close ≥ fast EMA and < middle EMA, and the previous bar's high is the highest high over that lookback.
Calculation: Structure break long = bullish EMA ribbon and close > the highest high over the structure lookback ending on the previous bar. Short = bearish EMA ribbon and close < the lowest low over that lookback ending on the previous bar.
Calculation: Long composite strength = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 30 for bullish ribbon plus positive middle EMA slope plus structure break; 30 for smoothed histogram > adaptive baseline, MACD line > signal, RSI > trigger, MFI > trigger, and volume impulse; 25 for squeeze release long; 15 for micro pullback long plus smoothed histogram > 0 and volume impulse; +5 if volume impulse else −5; +5 in velocity regime, −2 in compression, else 0; +5 if cycle bullish else −5. Short strength is the corresponding bearish sum: 30 for bearish ribbon plus negative middle EMA slope plus structure break; 30 for smoothed histogram < −adaptive baseline, MACD line < signal, RSI < 100 − trigger, MFI < 100 − trigger, and volume impulse; 25 for squeeze release short; 15 for micro pullback short plus smoothed histogram < 0 and volume impulse; the same volume and regime adjustments; +5 if cycle bearish else −5.
Long: session filter passes if enabled AND kill switch inactive AND cycle deviation > positive threshold AND volume impulse AND long composite strength ≥ minimum AND (dominance filter disabled, or BTC dominance's 5-bar SMA of changes < −threshold or its absolute value < threshold) AND long quantity > 0 AND (same-bar re-entry enabled or position is flat/short); if both long and short qualify, retain long only when smoothed MACD histogram > 0.
Short: session filter passes if enabled AND kill switch inactive AND cycle deviation < negative threshold AND volume impulse AND short composite strength ≥ minimum AND (dominance filter disabled, or BTC dominance's 5-bar SMA of changes > threshold or its absolute value < threshold) AND short entries enabled AND short quantity > 0 AND (same-bar re-entry enabled or position is flat/long); if both long and short qualify, retain short only when smoothed MACD histogram ≤ 0.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − regime-adjusted ATR stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + regime-adjusted ATR target distance OR (trailing enabled AND close − regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset > initial stop AND low ≤ close − trailing offset) OR raw MACD histogram < 0 OR EMA ribbon is not bullish OR a short entry qualifies.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + regime-adjusted ATR stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − regime-adjusted ATR target distance OR (trailing enabled AND close + regime-adjusted ATR trailing offset < initial stop AND high ≥ close + trailing offset) OR raw MACD histogram > 0 OR EMA ribbon is not bearish OR a long entry qualifies.
