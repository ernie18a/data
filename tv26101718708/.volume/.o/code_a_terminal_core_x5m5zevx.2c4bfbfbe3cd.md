Calculation: Trend-up state = HMA is above its projection from the previous HMA, projected using the change between the previous HMA and the HMA one lookback before it, and the current HMA is above the previous HMA; exclude the state when the HMA is below its projection.
Calculation: Wave = EMA of (hlc3 − EMA(hlc3)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(hlc3 − EMA(hlc3)))), using the same lookback for both EMAs and a shorter smoothing EMA.
Calculation: Wave signal = 3-bar SMA of Wave.
Calculation: Wave difference = Wave minus Wave signal, rounded to one decimal place.
Calculation: Money flow = SMA of ((close − open) / (high − low)) × scale, minus offset.
Calculation: Short-lookback stochastic = 2-bar SMA of Stochastic, using a shorter lookback than the long-lookback stochastic.
Calculation: Long-lookback stochastic = 2-bar SMA of Stochastic.
Calculation: Bias score = WMA of a score that adds 1 for each close above its close 11 through 20 bars earlier and subtracts 1 otherwise, scaled by 100/12.
Long: high > previous high AND (EMA(11) crosses above EMA(33) OR Wave crosses above Wave signal now or two bars ago) AND consecutive bars with close > close four bars earlier <= maximum series count AND close > Parabolic SAR AND open > previous open AND close > previous close AND min(open, close) = low AND Wave difference >= difference threshold AND Trend-up state AND ((Wave signal > 0 AND Money flow > 0 AND Wave signal < strategy #2 band) OR (Wave signal < 0 AND short-lookback stochastic > long-lookback stochastic AND Wave signal < negative strategy #1 band)) AND (Bias score > previous Bias score OR Bias score >= 83 OR Bias score <= -83) AND volume >= minimum volume AND no prior long signal since Trend-up state last became false.
Short: low < previous low AND (EMA(5) crosses below EMA(11) OR Wave crosses below Wave signal now or two bars ago) AND consecutive bars with close < close four bars earlier <= maximum series count AND close < Parabolic SAR AND open < previous open AND close < previous close AND max(open, close) = high AND Wave difference <= negative difference threshold AND Trend-up state is false with HMA below its projection and falling AND ((Wave signal < 0 AND Money flow <= 0 AND Wave signal > negative strategy #2 band) OR (Wave signal > 0 AND short-lookback stochastic <= long-lookback stochastic AND Wave signal > strategy #1 band)) AND (Bias score < previous Bias score OR Bias score <= -83 OR Bias score >= 83) AND volume >= minimum volume AND no prior short signal since the short trend state last became false.
Exit: Long, close < open AND Wave difference <= 0.2.
Exit: Short, close > open AND Wave difference >= 0.2.
