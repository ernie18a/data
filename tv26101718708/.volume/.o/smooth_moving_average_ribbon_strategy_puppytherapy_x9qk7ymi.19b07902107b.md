Calculation: Ribbon open and close = optionally transform each bar’s OHLC to Heikin-Ashi OHLC, apply the same selected moving-average variant and lookback repeatedly to each OHLC field, then optionally transform the result to Heikin-Ashi OHLC; use the resulting open and close fields
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (prior Heikin-Ashi open + prior Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized from the current open and close; high = max(high, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close); low = min(low, Heikin-Ashi open, Heikin-Ashi close)
Calculation: Trend filter moving average = selected moving-average variant and lookback applied to the configured source, which may be close or Heikin-Ashi close
Long: Ribbon close crosses above ribbon open AND (trend filter disabled OR (rising/falling mode enabled AND trend filter moving average is higher than its prior value) OR (rising/falling mode disabled AND configured source is Heikin-Ashi close or close is above the trend filter moving average)) AND datetime is within the configured backtest period
Short: Ribbon close crosses below ribbon open AND (trend filter disabled OR (rising/falling mode enabled AND trend filter moving average is lower than its prior value) OR (rising/falling mode disabled AND configured source is Heikin-Ashi close or close is below the trend filter moving average)) AND datetime is within the configured backtest period
Exit: Long or short, ribbon close crosses ribbon open in either direction
