Calculation: confirmed pivot high/low = a local high/low confirmed after the configured number of higher-timeframe bars on each side; use its price at the pivot bar
Calculation: climax volume = pivot-bar volume > its moving average volume × the volume multiplier
Calculation: wide-range bar = pivot-bar high − low > its ATR × the wide-range multiplier
Calculation: downtrend = shorter-lookback EMA of close < longer-lookback EMA of close; uptrend = shorter-lookback EMA of close > longer-lookback EMA of close
Long: for each enabled higher timeframe, evaluate on each current higher-timeframe bar (which may still be forming); from no active structure, downtrend AND confirmed pivot low AND (climax volume OR wide-range bar); OR in accumulation phase B, confirmed pivot low < climax low (Spring); OR in accumulation phase B or C, confirmed pivot high > automatic-reaction high (SOS); OR in accumulation phase D, confirmed pivot low > climax low (LPS). Climax volume and wide-range bar are evaluated at the pivot bar; trend and state are evaluated on the current bar. Pivots become available only after the configured right-side bars confirm them.
Short: for each enabled higher timeframe, evaluate on each current higher-timeframe bar (which may still be forming); from no active structure, uptrend AND confirmed pivot high AND (climax volume OR wide-range bar); OR in distribution phase B, confirmed pivot high > buying-climax high (UTAD); OR in distribution phase B or C, confirmed pivot low < automatic-reaction low (SOW); OR in distribution phase D, confirmed pivot high < buying-climax high (LPSY). Climax volume and wide-range bar are evaluated at the pivot bar; trend and state are evaluated on the current bar. Pivots become available only after the configured right-side bars confirm them.
