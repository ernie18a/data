Calculation: Session high/low = highest high and lowest low among bars in the current Europe/Berlin 00:00–07:00 session; use only after that session is complete.
Calculation: Support zone = session low ± current chart-timeframe ATR × zone-width parameter; resistance zone = session high ± current chart-timeframe ATR × zone-width parameter.
Calculation: Recent signed volume delta = sum over the shorter chart-bar lookback of each bar’s lower-timeframe intrabar delta. For each intrabar, delta is +volume if close > open, −volume if close < open; when equal, use +volume if close > the preceding intrabar close, −volume if lower, otherwise carry forward the preceding nonzero direction (zero if none).
Long: Position is flat AND either (a) pending support reclaim: a bar intersected the support zone after its preceding close was above the zone, the pending state has not timed out or been cancelled by a close below the zone, current close > support-zone top, recent signed volume delta ≥ positive threshold, and the same-side cooldown has elapsed; OR (b) pending support bounce: the same support-zone touch from above is pending and valid, current close crosses above support-zone top, and the corresponding cooldown has elapsed; OR (c) pending resistance bounce: a bar intersected the resistance zone after its preceding close was above the zone, the pending state has not timed out or been cancelled by a close below the zone, current close crosses above resistance-zone top, and the corresponding cooldown has elapsed; OR (d) a new bar closes above resistance-zone top by at least the breakout percentage threshold; OR (e) after such an upward breakout, a later bar intersects resistance-zone top and closes above it while that breakout remains active.
Short: Position is flat AND either (a) pending resistance rejection: a bar intersected the resistance zone after its preceding close was below the zone, the pending state has not timed out or been cancelled by a close above the zone, current close < resistance-zone bottom, recent signed volume delta ≤ negative threshold, and the same-side cooldown has elapsed; OR (b) pending support rejection: a bar intersected the support zone after its preceding close was below the zone, the pending state has not timed out or been cancelled by a close above the zone, current close crosses below support-zone bottom, and the corresponding cooldown has elapsed; OR (c) pending resistance rejection marker: the same resistance-zone touch from below is pending and valid, current close crosses below resistance-zone bottom, and the corresponding cooldown has elapsed; OR (d) a new bar closes below support-zone bottom by at least the breakout percentage threshold; OR (e) after such a downward breakout, a later bar intersects support-zone bottom and closes below it while that breakout remains active.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage)
