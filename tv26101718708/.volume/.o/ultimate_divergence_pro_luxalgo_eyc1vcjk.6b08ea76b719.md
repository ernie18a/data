Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: RSI pivot low pair = each confirmed local RSI low, paired with the price low on its bar; retain the preceding pair of the same type
Calculation: RSI pivot high pair = each confirmed local RSI high, paired with the price high on its bar; retain the preceding pair of the same type
Long: At a confirmed RSI pivot low, its bar is within the configured minimum and maximum separation from the preceding RSI pivot low, close > EMA(close) on the pivot bar, and volume > 20-period SMA(volume) on the pivot bar, AND ((price low < preceding pivot low AND RSI > preceding pivot RSI AND (RSI <= oversold threshold OR preceding pivot RSI <= oversold threshold)) OR (price low > preceding pivot low AND RSI < preceding pivot RSI) OR (absolute difference between current and preceding pivot lows <= 0.15 × ATR on the pivot bar AND RSI > preceding pivot RSI))
Short: At a confirmed RSI pivot high, its bar is within the configured minimum and maximum separation from the preceding RSI pivot high, close < EMA(close) on the pivot bar, and volume > 20-period SMA(volume) on the pivot bar, AND ((price high > preceding pivot high AND RSI < preceding pivot RSI AND (RSI >= overbought threshold OR preceding pivot RSI >= overbought threshold)) OR (price high < preceding pivot high AND RSI > preceding pivot RSI) OR (absolute difference between current and preceding pivot highs <= 0.15 × ATR on the pivot bar AND RSI < preceding pivot RSI))
