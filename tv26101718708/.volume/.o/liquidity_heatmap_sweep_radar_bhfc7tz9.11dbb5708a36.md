Calculation: Active liquidity pools = confirmed swing highs (buy-side) and swing lows (sell-side), using equal left/right swing lookbacks; merge a new pivot into the nearest same-side pool within an ATR-scaled tolerance by averaging their levels, otherwise create a pool. Keep at most the configured maximum per side, removing the oldest excess pool. Evaluate sweeps and remove swept pools before adding that bar’s newly confirmed pivots. A buy-side pool is swept when high > pool level AND close < pool level; a sell-side pool is swept when low < pool level AND close > pool level. Sweeps are confirmed at bar close. When close-through removal is enabled, remove a buy-side pool when close > its level and a sell-side pool when close < its level.
Long: An active sell-side pool is swept (low < pool level AND close > pool level at bar close)
Short: An active buy-side pool is swept (high > pool level AND close < pool level at bar close)
