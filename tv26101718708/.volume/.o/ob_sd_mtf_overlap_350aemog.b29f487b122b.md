Calculation: Bullish order block = For each enabled timeframe at or above the chart timeframe, when a bar closes above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high, define a zone from the candle with the lowest low between that swing and the break bar; its bounds are that candle’s low and high. Keep it if its height is no greater than a configurable multiple of that timeframe’s ATR. A zone remains live until price breaks below its bottom, using the configured wick-or-close invalidation rule.
Calculation: Bearish order block = For each enabled timeframe at or above the chart timeframe, when a bar closes below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low, define a zone from the candle with the highest high between that swing and the break bar; its bounds are that candle’s low and high. Keep it if its height is no greater than a configurable multiple of that timeframe’s ATR. A zone remains live until price breaks above its top, using the configured wick-or-close invalidation rule.
Calculation: Demand zone = For each enabled timeframe at or above the chart timeframe, when all candles in a configurable consecutive-bar window are bullish and each body is at least a configurable fraction of the longer-lookback average body size, define a zone from the candle immediately before that window. Clamp the zone height to a configurable multiple of that timeframe’s ATR. A zone remains live until price breaks below its bottom, using the configured wick-or-close invalidation rule; require configurable minimum spacing between zones.
Calculation: Supply zone = For each enabled timeframe at or above the chart timeframe, when all candles in a configurable consecutive-bar window are bearish and each body is at least a configurable fraction of the longer-lookback average body size, define a zone from the candle immediately before that window. Clamp the zone height to a configurable multiple of that timeframe’s ATR. A zone remains live until price breaks above its top, using the configured wick-or-close invalidation rule; require configurable minimum spacing between zones.
Calculation: Combined zone = Iteratively merge zones only when they have the same type and direction and overlap in both time and price; merged bounds are the union of their price ranges. Keep zones from enabled timeframes at or above the chart timeframe. Use each timeframe’s developing bar for detection and invalidation, and the chart bar for retest and break checks.
Long: A live demand zone or bullish order block has existed for at least the configured minimum age AND low < zone top AND more than the configured cooldown bars have passed since the previous retest of that zone type.
Long: A demand zone or bullish order block is invalidated on this bar AND its duration exceeds the configured minimum age.
Short: A live supply zone or bearish order block has existed for at least the configured minimum age AND high > zone bottom AND more than the configured cooldown bars have passed since the previous retest of that zone type.
Short: A supply zone or bearish order block is invalidated on this bar AND its duration exceeds the configured minimum age.
