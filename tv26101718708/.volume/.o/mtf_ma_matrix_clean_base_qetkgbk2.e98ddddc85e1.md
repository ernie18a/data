Calculation: Normalized long-MA slope = (long-term MA − its value one lookback ago) / ATR; use the current chart timeframe for the main signal and each monitored higher timeframe’s latest available bar for alignment.
Calculation: Relative MA change = (long-term MA − its value one lookback ago) / its value one lookback ago.
Calculation: MA separation score = weighted sum of the ATR-normalized absolute separations between each pair of adjacent short-, medium-, and long-term moving averages, using progressively longer lookbacks.
Long: On the current chart timeframe, the medium-term MA > the long-term MA > the longest-term MA AND close > the short-term MA AND normalized long-MA slope > its threshold AND relative MA change > its threshold AND MA separation score > its threshold AND the count of monitored higher timeframes whose normalized long-MA slope exceeds the long threshold meets the minimum alignment count; trigger only if this condition was false on the previous bar OR the same local long condition holds on any monitored higher timeframe.
Short: On the current chart timeframe, the medium-term MA < the long-term MA < the longest-term MA AND close < the short-term MA AND normalized long-MA slope < the negative slope threshold AND relative MA change < the negative change threshold AND MA separation score > its threshold AND the count of monitored higher timeframes whose normalized long-MA slope is below the negative slope threshold meets the minimum alignment count; trigger only if this condition was false on the previous bar OR the same local short condition holds on any monitored higher timeframe.
Exit: long, a short entry triggers OR price reaches the trailing stop, which trails the highest price since activation by an ATR-based distance after price has moved that distance above entry.
Exit: short, a long entry triggers OR price reaches the trailing stop, which trails the lowest price since activation by an ATR-based distance after price has moved that distance below entry.
