Calculation: Golden buy/sell score = round(100 × directional points / maximum points). Add 10 points for bullish/bearish momentum; 20 for bullish/bearish EMA alignment; 20 for close above/below VWAP; 15 for RSI at/above the buy threshold or at/below the sell threshold; 15 for ADX at/above its strength threshold with +DI above −DI or −DI above +DI; 10 for valid relative volume; and 10 for valid relative ATR. Volume and ATR points apply to both scores. Include only enabled EMA, VWAP, RSI, ADX, volume, and ATR components in points and maximum points; momentum is always included. Maximum points are the sum of included component weights.
Long: bullish momentum AND golden buy score >= minimum score AND (EMA filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (sideways filter disabled OR (absolute fast/slow EMA spread / ATR >= minimum spread ratio AND ADX >= minimum sideways strength)) AND (trend-entry filter disabled OR lowest low over the entry lookback <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA) AND last confirmed signal != long AND bar is confirmed
Short: bearish momentum AND golden sell score >= minimum score AND (EMA filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (sideways filter disabled OR (absolute fast/slow EMA spread / ATR >= minimum spread ratio AND ADX >= minimum sideways strength)) AND (trend-entry filter disabled OR highest high over the entry lookback >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA) AND last confirmed signal != short AND bar is confirmed
