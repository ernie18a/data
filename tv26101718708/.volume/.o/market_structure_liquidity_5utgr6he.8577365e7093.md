Calculation: Swing pivots = confirmed highs and lows using equal left/right lookbacks; the swing lookback is longer than the internal-structure lookback
Calculation: Validated structure break = close crosses the latest confirmed swing pivot, with displacement, close-position, pivot-quality, and protected-swing gates satisfied when enabled; displacement is a range expansion with a substantial candle body or a compression release
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price wicks beyond an equal-high/equal-low pool or latest swing pivot, then the source close returns across that level; a swing-pivot sweep also requires a candle body in the reversal direction
Calculation: Fair value gap = current low exceeds the high two bars earlier, or current high is below the low two bars earlier, by at least the minimum ATR-relative gap
Long: Validated structure break above the latest confirmed swing high OR internal structure break above the latest confirmed internal swing high that changes internal bias to bullish OR liquidity sweep below an equal-low pool or latest swing low with source close back above it OR bullish fair value gap
Short: Validated structure break below the latest confirmed swing low OR internal structure break below the latest confirmed internal swing low that changes internal bias to bearish OR liquidity sweep above an equal-high pool or latest swing high with source close back below it OR bearish fair value gap
