Calculation: Opening range = the 9:30 session bar’s body high and low, or its full high and low, according to the configured range method.
Calculation: Close position (%) = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low); use 50 when high = low.
Calculation: Strong bullish bias = close above session VWAP and the configured bias EMA, with both above their values the configured slope lookback bars earlier. Strong bearish bias = close below both, with both below their values that many bars earlier.
Calculation: Bullish acceptance count = consecutive confirmed bars in the trade window closing above the opening-range high; reset to zero otherwise. Bearish acceptance count = consecutive confirmed bars in the trade window closing below the opening-range low; reset to zero otherwise.
Calculation: Recent swing high/low = the latest confirmed pivot high/low, using the configured pivot strength on both sides.
Long: Confirmed bar AND opening range set AND session time from 9:45 to before 11:00 AND close above opening-range high by at least the configured minimum break distance (multiplied by the configured Thursday factor in strict mode) AND bullish acceptance count meets the configured minimum (unless acceptance is disabled) AND no signal has already occurred that day AND Thursday mode does not disable trading that day AND close position meets the configured close-strength threshold (plus the configured Thursday addition in strict mode) AND (bias filter disabled OR countertrend blocking disabled OR strong bearish bias is false) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close above session VWAP) AND (REV suppression disabled OR no bullish REV warning).
Short: Confirmed bar AND opening range set AND session time from 9:45 to before 11:00 AND close below opening-range low by at least the configured minimum break distance (multiplied by the configured Thursday factor in strict mode) AND bearish acceptance count meets the configured minimum (unless acceptance is disabled) AND no signal has already occurred that day AND Thursday mode does not disable trading that day AND close position is at or below 100 minus the configured close-strength threshold (including the configured Thursday addition in strict mode) AND (bias filter disabled OR countertrend blocking disabled OR strong bullish bias is false) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close below session VWAP) AND (REV suppression disabled OR no bearish REV warning).
