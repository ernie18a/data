Calculation: Bullish score = sum the configured importance weights of these active conditions: close above the long-term EMA; shorter EMA rising while longer EMA falls; Supertrend bullish or reversing bullish; price above PSAR or reversing above it; close above HMA or HMA pivoting up; bullish divergence; RSI oversold or crossing above 50; MACD histogram pivoting up below zero or MACD crossing above its signal below zero; WaveTrend crossing up below its lower threshold; stochastic crossing up or bullish divergence; close below the lower Bollinger Band; ATR stop direction reversing up; RVI rising or bullish divergence; HMA crossing above a support/resistance level with close above its close two bars ago; CMF crossing above or above zero; VWAP/HMA bullish cross; EMA above VWAP; bullish engulfing. Divergence means a confirmed oscillator pivot forms a higher low while price forms a lower low, with the prior matching pivot within the configured bar range; apply the oscillator-specific constraints: RSI, WaveTrend, stochastic, RVI, and OBV use this definition; MACD additionally requires the oscillator pivots to remain below zero. Divergence conditions remain active on the signal bar and the next two bars.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum the configured importance weights of these active conditions: close below the long-term EMA; shorter EMA falling while longer EMA rises; Supertrend bearish or reversing bearish; price above PSAR or reversing below it; close below HMA or HMA pivoting down; bearish divergence; RSI overbought or crossing below 50; MACD histogram pivoting down above zero or MACD crossing below its signal above zero; WaveTrend crossing down above its upper threshold; stochastic crossing down or bearish divergence; open above the upper Bollinger Band; ATR stop direction reversing down; RVI falling or bearish divergence; HMA crossing below a support/resistance level with close below its close two bars ago; CMF crossing below or below zero; VWAP/HMA bearish cross; EMA below VWAP; bearish engulfing. Bearish divergence means a confirmed oscillator pivot forms a lower high while price forms a higher high, with the prior matching pivot within the configured bar range; apply the oscillator-specific constraints: RSI, WaveTrend, stochastic, RVI, and OBV use this definition; MACD additionally requires the oscillator pivots to remain above zero. Divergence conditions remain active on the signal bar and the next two bars.
Long: Bullish score >= strong-entry threshold and (alternate signals are disabled OR bullish score was below the strong-entry threshold on the prior bar and bearish score was below its strong-entry threshold on the prior bar); if the trend filter is enabled, the long-term EMA is rising. OR bullish score >= weak-entry threshold.
Short: Bearish score >= strong-entry threshold and (alternate signals are disabled OR bearish score was below the strong-entry threshold on the prior bar and bullish score was below its strong-entry threshold on the prior bar); if the trend filter is enabled, the long-term EMA is falling. OR bearish score >= weak-entry threshold.
Exit: long, a short-entry transition occurs OR close crosses above entry price times (1 minus the configured stop-loss fraction).
Exit: short, a long-entry transition occurs OR close crosses below entry price times (1 plus the configured stop-loss fraction).
