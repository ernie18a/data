Calculation: Confirmed pivot = ZigZag swing high/low from chart-bar highs/lows, confirmed on a bar close after price reverses by a configurable deviation; the extreme-making bar cannot also confirm the pivot.
Calculation: External impulse = group confirmed pivots in one direction, extending its extreme when a later same-direction pivot exceeds it; end and start the opposite impulse when a counter-move retraces at least the external threshold. Track its origin, extreme, direction, and greatest pullback since the extreme.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = a confirmed pivot level is pierced by a later bar's wick and that bar closes back across the level.
Calculation: Retracement = distance from the external impulse extreme to the current counter-extreme divided by the absolute distance from its origin to its extreme.
Calculation: MACD line = fast-period EMA of close minus slow-period EMA of close; MACD signal = EMA of the MACD line.
Calculation: Slow MACD = MACD line and signal calculated from higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Trend pullback zone = external-impulse retracement from the internal impulse threshold through, but excluding, the external impulse threshold.
Calculation: Stop and target = trend stop is beyond the pullback extreme by an ATR buffer; counter-trend stop is beyond the sweep wick by an ATR buffer. ATR uses chart bars. Risk is the distance from entry close to stop; target is entry close plus or minus a configurable risk multiple in the trade direction.
Long: On a confirmed bar, external impulse is bullish AND slow MACD meets the selected bullish rule (line above zero, line above signal, or both) AND external retracement is in the trend pullback zone AND price traded into that zone within the trigger window AND fast MACD line > fast MACD signal AND no trend signal has fired for this external impulse extreme; OR a confirmed pivot low was swept within the trigger window (and fast MACD divergence from its value at the pivot is present if required) AND price has not broken the sweep wick AND fast MACD line > fast MACD signal AND close > swept level.
Short: On a confirmed bar, external impulse is bearish AND slow MACD meets the selected bearish rule (line below zero, line below signal, or both) AND external retracement is in the trend pullback zone AND price traded into that zone within the trigger window AND fast MACD line < fast MACD signal AND no trend signal has fired for this external impulse extreme; OR a confirmed pivot high was swept within the trigger window (and fast MACD divergence from its value at the pivot is present if required) AND price has not broken the sweep wick AND fast MACD line < fast MACD signal AND close < swept level.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target
