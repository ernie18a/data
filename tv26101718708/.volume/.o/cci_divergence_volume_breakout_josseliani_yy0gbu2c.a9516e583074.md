Calculation: CCI divergence = On a confirmed bar, for each pivot strength in {3,5,7,9}, pair successive confirmed CCI lows (bullish) or highs (bearish) no more than the maximum pivot separation apart. The first CCI pivot must be at or beyond the selected negative (bullish) or positive (bearish) extreme; the second must be higher (bullish) or lower (bearish). Match each CCI pivot to a price pivot of the same kind within the selected radius on either side, using the valid pair with the lowest score (sum of absolute offsets plus 0.1 × percentage span difference). Require the price pivots to be later/earlier in order, make a lower low (bullish) or higher high (bearish), have span difference within the selected tolerance, and have no intervening same-kind price pivot more extreme than the second. Confirm pivots using bars to their right. Reset a pending bullish (bearish) first pivot when CCI reaches the positive (negative) extreme; replace it with a newer pivot at that extreme if it is at least as extreme as the stored pivot. Ignore duplicate divergences with the same direction and both matched price-pivot bars within 5 bars of a previously signaled pair.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / simple moving average of volume over the selected lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Volume level = For a confirmed divergence, select the qualifying candle with greatest volume from the CCI-pivot span, where relative volume meets the selected minimum. If none qualifies, search from 9 bars before through 9 bars after the second CCI pivot and select the greatest-volume qualifying candle. Also allow qualifying candles after divergence confirmation through the selected candidate window, up to the selected candidate limit. For bullish divergence the level is that candle's high; for bearish divergence it is its low. Each level is valid only on confirmed bars after its source candle through its signal window.
Long: Confirmed bullish CCI divergence OR a confirmed close above an active bullish volume level.
Short: Confirmed bearish CCI divergence OR a confirmed close below an active bearish volume level.
