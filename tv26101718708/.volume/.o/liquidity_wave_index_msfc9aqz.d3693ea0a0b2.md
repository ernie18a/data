Calculation: Liquidity Pressure = EMA(((close − open) / (high − low)) × volume) / EMA(volume), with zero when high = low; both EMAs use the same smoothing length.
Calculation: Adaptive EMA(source, length, lag) = recursively smooth source with alpha = [2 / (length + 1)] × min(1, abs(source − source[lag]) / highest(abs(source − source[lag]), length)); initialize with source.
Calculation: Cycle Line = EMA(Adaptive EMA(z, longer smoothing length, fast lag), ribbon smoothing length), where z = (source − baseline) / (0.015 × deviation), baseline = Adaptive EMA(source, base length, adaptation lookback), deviation = Adaptive EMA(abs(source − baseline), base length, adaptation lookback).
Calculation: Signal Line = EMA(Adaptive EMA(unsmoothed Cycle Line, shorter smoothing length derived from the Cycle Line smoothing length, slow lag), ribbon smoothing length).
Calculation: Confirmed price/oscillator pivot = a high or low greater/less than the bars on both sides for the configured pivot length; available only after the right-side bars complete. Match each confirmed price pivot to the nearest of the three most recent confirmed Cycle Line pivots of the same type within the configured maximum gap. Compare with the newest eligible of the three prior matched pairs, subject to the configured minimum and maximum distance between price pivots.
Long: On a confirmed bar, Cycle Line crosses above Signal Line AND (threshold filter is off OR Cycle Line is below the negative threshold) AND (pressure confirmation is off OR same-direction mode is on and Liquidity Pressure > 0 OR zero-cross mode is on and Liquidity Pressure crosses above 0).
Short: On a confirmed bar, Cycle Line crosses below Signal Line AND (threshold filter is off OR Cycle Line is above the positive threshold) AND (pressure confirmation is off OR same-direction mode is on and Liquidity Pressure < 0 OR zero-cross mode is on and Liquidity Pressure crosses below 0).
Long: On a confirmed bar, a matched price swing low is lower than an eligible prior matched price swing low AND its matched Cycle Line swing low is higher; if zero-line context is enabled, both Cycle Line lows are at or below zero.
Long: When hidden divergences are enabled, on a confirmed bar, a matched price swing low is higher than an eligible prior matched price swing low AND its matched Cycle Line swing low is lower; if zero-line context is enabled, both Cycle Line lows are at or below zero.
Short: On a confirmed bar, a matched price swing high is higher than an eligible prior matched price swing high AND its matched Cycle Line swing high is lower; if zero-line context is enabled, both Cycle Line highs are at or above zero.
Short: When hidden divergences are enabled, on a confirmed bar, a matched price swing high is lower than an eligible prior matched price swing high AND its matched Cycle Line swing high is higher; if zero-line context is enabled, both Cycle Line highs are at or above zero.
