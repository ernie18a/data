Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the session’s opening range; use the completed range.
Calculation: Session AVWAP = cumulative (high + low + close)/3 × volume divided by cumulative volume from session open through the AVWAP cutoff; use current-session bars, then hold the value fixed after the cutoff.
Long: Opening range is complete AND current close > current opening-range high AND previous close ≤ previous opening-range high AND current close > session AVWAP AND current close > its moving average AND moving average > its previous value AND current volume > its moving average × a configured multiplier AND NOT (current high > opening-range high AND current close < opening-range high)
Short: Opening range is complete AND current close < current opening-range low AND previous close ≥ previous opening-range low AND current close < session AVWAP AND current close < its moving average AND moving average < its previous value AND current volume > its moving average × a configured multiplier AND NOT (current low < opening-range low AND current close > opening-range low)
