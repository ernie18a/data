Calculation: Selected VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the start of the selected daily, weekly, monthly, or custom session period, using only bars in that period; use the current, still-forming period value.
Calculation: Candle body ratio = absolute(close − open) / (high − low); use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Reaction distance = absolute(close − selected VWAP) / ATR; use 0 when ATR is unavailable or zero. ATR uses a shorter lookback than the volume average.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a longer lookback than the ATR lookback.
Long: Selected VWAP is available AND low ≤ selected VWAP AND close > selected VWAP AND close > open AND close location ≥ its minimum threshold AND candle body ratio ≥ its minimum threshold AND reaction distance ≥ its minimum threshold AND (volume or its average is unavailable OR volume ≥ average volume) AND selected VWAP is rising versus the previous bar without a selected-period reset AND the current bar is closed AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the last reaction marker.
Short: Selected VWAP is available AND high ≥ selected VWAP AND close < selected VWAP AND close < open AND close location ≤ its maximum threshold AND candle body ratio ≥ its minimum threshold AND reaction distance ≥ its minimum threshold AND (volume or its average is unavailable OR volume ≥ average volume) AND selected VWAP is falling versus the previous bar without a selected-period reset AND the current bar is closed AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the last reaction marker.
