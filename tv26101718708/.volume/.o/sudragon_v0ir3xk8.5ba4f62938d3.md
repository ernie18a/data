Calculation: Dragon trend = Up when close > the previous bar's final upper band, down when close < the previous bar's final lower band, otherwise retain the prior trend; final upper band is basic upper band (HL2 + ATR × multiplier) if it is below the prior final upper band or prior close is above that band, otherwise retain the prior final upper band; final lower band is basic lower band (HL2 − ATR × multiplier) if it is above the prior final lower band or prior close is below that band, otherwise retain the prior final lower band. Use the last completed bar's values for prior-bar references.
Calculation: Bias = 20 × (Dragon trend up ? 1 : −1) + 20 × (longer-lookback EMA ≥ longer-lookback EMA ? 1 : −1) + 10 × (shorter-lookback EMA ≥ longer-lookback EMA ? 1 : −1) + 15 × (linear-regression value over the learning lookback ≥ its prior-bar value ? 1 : −1) + 10 × (+DI ≥ −DI ? 1 : −1) + RSI vote (15 if RSI ≥ upper RSI threshold, −15 if RSI ≤ lower RSI threshold, 8 if RSI ≥ middle RSI threshold, otherwise −8) + volume vote (5 if volume > its longer-lookback average and close ≥ open, −5 if volume > its average and close < open, otherwise 0); clamp to [−100, 100] and round to the nearest integer. The two EMAs in the first comparison use the main and slow lookbacks; the fast and mid EMAs use shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively.
Calculation: Score = round(clamp(50 + abs(Bias) / 2, 0, 100)); forecast direction is up when Bias ≥ 0, otherwise down.
Calculation: Choppy = ADX < its threshold OR (highest high − lowest low over the chop lookback) < ATR × its threshold OR abs(main-lookback EMA − slow-lookback EMA) < ATR × its threshold.
Long: (forecast direction is up AND Dragon trend is up AND fast-lookback EMA > mid-lookback EMA AND close > main-lookback EMA AND Score ≥ entry-score threshold AND NOT Choppy AND (entry mode is every signal OR prior bar did not meet this same setup)) OR fast-lookback EMA crosses above main-lookback EMA.
Short: (forecast direction is down AND Dragon trend is down AND fast-lookback EMA < mid-lookback EMA AND close < main-lookback EMA AND Score ≥ entry-score threshold AND NOT Choppy AND (entry mode is every signal OR prior bar did not meet this same setup)) OR fast-lookback EMA crosses below main-lookback EMA.
