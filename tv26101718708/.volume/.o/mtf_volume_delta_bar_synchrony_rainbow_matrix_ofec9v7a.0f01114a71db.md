Calculation: Volume percentile rank = the percentile rank of volume over its lookback on each of five configured timeframes, ordered from fastest (TF1) to slowest (TF5), using each timeframe’s latest available bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Master volume percentile = weighted average of TF1–TF5 volume percentile ranks, with weights 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.25, 0.15, clamped to [0, 100].
Long: close > open OR TF1 volume percentile ≤ 30 AND TF5 volume percentile ≥ 70 OR TF1 volume percentile ≤ 35 AND TF5 volume percentile ≥ 60 AND NOT (TF1 volume percentile ≥ 70 AND TF5 volume percentile ≤ 30) OR, at a confirmed pivot low of any configured timeframe’s volume percentile or the Master volume percentile, price low is lower than at the preceding confirmed volume-percentile pivot low AND volume percentile is higher than at that preceding pivot low.
Short: close < open OR TF1 volume percentile ≥ 70 AND TF5 volume percentile ≤ 30 OR TF1 volume percentile ≥ 65 AND TF5 volume percentile ≤ 40 AND NOT (TF1 volume percentile ≥ 70 AND TF5 volume percentile ≤ 30) OR, at a confirmed pivot high of any configured timeframe’s volume percentile or the Master volume percentile, price high is higher than at the preceding confirmed volume-percentile pivot high AND volume percentile is lower than at that preceding pivot high.
