Calculation: High EMA = exponential moving average of high on the configured signal timeframe, including the current forming bar
Calculation: Prior-period highest high/lowest low = highest high/lowest low among the preceding price-lookback completed signal-timeframe bars
Calculation: Prior-period highest volume = highest volume among the preceding volume-lookback completed signal-timeframe bars
Calculation: Trend average = simple moving average of close on the configured signal timeframe, including the current forming bar
Long: On the configured signal timeframe, close > current high EMA AND each of the prior three closes < its corresponding prior high EMA OR close > prior-period highest high AND volume > prior-period highest volume AND close > trend average; OR on the daily timeframe, previous close < previous open AND current open > previous open OR current open = current low OR current open < previous low AND current close > previous low OR current high <= previous high AND current low >= previous low OR previous high < high three daily bars ago AND current close > each of the previous three daily highs
Short: On the configured signal timeframe, close < prior-period lowest low AND volume > prior-period highest volume AND close < trend average; OR on the daily timeframe, current open = current high OR current open > previous high AND current close <= previous high OR current high > previous high AND current low < previous low OR previous low > low three daily bars ago AND current close < each of the previous three daily lows
