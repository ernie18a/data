Calculation: CVD bar delta = sum of signed volume across the lower-timeframe bars within the current bar; sign each lower-timeframe volume positive when its close is above its open, negative when below, and otherwise positive when its close is at least the preceding lower-timeframe close, negative otherwise. CVD candles accumulate these deltas from the selected anchor reset.
Calculation: Average absolute delta = moving average of the absolute CVD bar delta; absorption and turnaround use the same lookback.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the backtest bars.
Calculation: Near a lookback high = the current or preceding bar’s high equals the highest high in the lookback; near a lookback low = the current or preceding bar’s low equals the lowest low in the lookback.
Calculation: Absolute-delta percentile = percentile rank of absolute CVD bar delta over a lookback.
Calculation: Average CVD range = moving average of CVD candle high minus low.
Long: On bar close, either (CVD bar delta is at most negative configured multiple of average absolute delta AND -(close - open) / ATR is at most the configured progress limit AND price is near a lookback low), or (price closes above its open AND displayed CVD candle closes below its open), or (CVD bar delta is positive after at least the configured number of consecutive negative-delta bars AND delta is at least the configured multiple of average absolute delta AND delta is at least the configured fraction of the absolute net delta of that prior run AND close - open is at least the configured ATR multiple AND, when enabled, close exceeds the preceding bar’s high), or (absolute-delta percentile meets the configured threshold AND CVD bar delta is negative AND, when enabled, price is near a lookback low AND, when enabled, close is sufficiently far above the bar low as a fraction of its range), or (CVD open - CVD low is at least the configured multiple of average CVD range AND exceeds CVD high - CVD open AND (CVD close - CVD low) / (CVD open - CVD low) meets the configured give-back fraction AND, when enabled, price is near a lookback low).
Short: On bar close, either (CVD bar delta is at least the configured multiple of average absolute delta AND (close - open) / ATR is at most the configured progress limit AND price is near a lookback high), or (price closes below its open AND displayed CVD candle closes above its open), or (CVD bar delta is negative after at least the configured number of consecutive positive-delta bars AND absolute delta is at least the configured multiple of average absolute delta AND absolute delta is at least the configured fraction of the absolute net delta of that prior run AND open - close is at least the configured ATR multiple AND, when enabled, close is below the preceding bar’s low), or (absolute-delta percentile meets the configured threshold AND CVD bar delta is positive AND, when enabled, price is near a lookback high AND, when enabled, close is sufficiently far below the bar high as a fraction of its range), or (CVD high - CVD open is at least the configured multiple of average CVD range AND exceeds CVD open - CVD low AND (CVD high - CVD close) / (CVD high - CVD open) meets the configured give-back fraction AND, when enabled, price is near a lookback high).
Exit: Long, no exit decision.
Exit: Short, no exit decision.
