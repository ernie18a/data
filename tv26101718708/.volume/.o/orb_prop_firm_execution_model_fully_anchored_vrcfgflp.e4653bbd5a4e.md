Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the session’s opening range; available after the range completes.
Calculation: Session-anchored VWAP = cumulative ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume from session open through the current bar.
Calculation: Close simple moving average = simple moving average of close; uses the same lookback as the volume simple moving average.
Calculation: Volume simple moving average = simple moving average of volume; uses the same lookback as the close simple moving average.
Long: Opening range is complete AND current close > opening-range high AND previous close ≤ previous opening-range high AND current close > session-anchored VWAP AND current close > close simple moving average AND current close simple moving average > previous close simple moving average AND current volume > a multiple of volume simple moving average AND NOT (current high > opening-range high AND current close < opening-range high)
Short: Opening range is complete AND current close < opening-range low AND previous close ≥ previous opening-range low AND current close < session-anchored VWAP AND current close < close simple moving average AND current close simple moving average < previous close simple moving average AND current volume > a multiple of volume simple moving average AND NOT (current low < opening-range low AND current close > opening-range low)
