Long: buy-signal series crosses above 0 OR (long line-cross enabled AND (line-cross direction is below-to-above AND close crosses above the selected line source OR line-cross direction is above-to-below AND close crosses below the selected line source))
Short: sell-signal series crosses above 0 OR (short line-cross enabled AND (line-cross direction is below-to-above AND close crosses above the selected line source OR line-cross direction is above-to-below AND close crosses below the selected line source))
Calculation: Fibonacci Bollinger basis = selected moving average of HLC3 over a lookback, optionally smoothed by a selected moving average over a separate lookback
Calculation: Fibonacci Bollinger bands = basis ± level × (multiplier × ATR over the basis lookback) × max(current ATR deviation / ATR deviation from one basis lookback ago, 0.5); levels are 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.764, and 1.0
Exit: Long, close return from entry price ≥ take-profit percentage OR close return from entry price ≤ negative stop-loss percentage OR (close return from entry price ≥ break-even activation percentage AND low ≤ entry price) OR (close-on-opposite enabled AND short condition) OR (Fibonacci Bollinger long stop-loss enabled AND low ≤ selected stop line) OR (Fibonacci Bollinger long take-profit enabled AND high ≥ selected target line)
Exit: Short, close return from entry price ≥ take-profit percentage OR close return from entry price ≤ negative stop-loss percentage OR (close return from entry price ≥ break-even activation percentage AND high ≥ entry price) OR (close-on-opposite enabled AND long condition) OR (Fibonacci Bollinger short stop-loss enabled AND high ≥ selected stop line) OR (Fibonacci Bollinger short take-profit enabled AND low ≤ selected target line)
