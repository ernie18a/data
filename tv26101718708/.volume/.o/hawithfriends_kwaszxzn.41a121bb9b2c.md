Calculation: Heiken Ashi values = HA close is the average of open, high, low, and close; first HA open is the average of open and close, then each HA open is the average of the prior HA open and close; HA high/low are the maximum/minimum of high, HA open, and HA close.
Calculation: HA momentum candle = HA body is at least the minimum body size, its body-to-range percentage is at least the minimum body ratio, and its direction-side wick-to-range percentage is at most the maximum wick ratio.
Calculation: ATR wave direction = direction of the current ATR-sized wave; a new wave close is set to the selected current-bar price extreme when it exceeds the prior wave close by more than the selected ATR multiple, otherwise the prior wave close is retained. Use the selected price source and true-range setting.
Calculation: LazyBear wave direction = update movement direction when the close's change sign differs from the prior bar's change sign; when close is rising or falling over the trend-detection lookback, update wave direction to movement direction; otherwise retain prior wave direction.
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line, using the shorter fast and longer slow close lookbacks.
Long: First bullish HA momentum candle after HA changes from bearish to bullish, before it changes back to bearish; OR HA is bullish AND ATR wave direction is up AND LazyBear wave direction is up AND MACD histogram is at least zero, and these four bullish states were not all true on the prior bar.
Short: First bearish HA momentum candle after HA changes from bullish to bearish, before it changes back to bullish; OR HA is bearish AND ATR wave direction is down AND LazyBear wave direction is down AND MACD histogram is below zero, and these four bearish states were not all true on the prior bar.
