Calculation: Bar delta = sum of volume for lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar that close above their open, minus volume for those that close below their open; if no lower-timeframe bars are available, use chart-bar volume with the same direction rule (zero if close equals open)
Calculation: CVD = running sum of bar delta
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 if high = low
Calculation: ATR percentile = percentile rank of ATR(14) over the current and preceding bars in its lookback
Long: position is flat AND low <= lowest low over the lookback including the current bar AND CVD > highest CVD over the preceding lookback bars excluding the current bar AND bar delta < 0 AND close position > 1 − effort threshold AND ATR percentile >= minimum AND, within the confirmation window after that setup bar, a later close is above its midpoint before price makes a lower low than its low
Short: position is flat AND high >= highest high over the lookback including the current bar AND CVD < highest CVD over the preceding lookback bars excluding the current bar AND bar delta > 0 AND close position < effort threshold AND ATR percentile >= minimum AND, within the confirmation window after that setup bar, a later close is below its midpoint before price makes a higher high than its high
Exit: long, low <= setup low − ATR buffer OR high >= confirmation close + target multiple × (confirmation close − stop price) OR close < position entry price AND bar delta < 0 for the required consecutive bars OR elapsed bars since entry signal >= time limit
Exit: short, high >= setup high + ATR buffer OR low <= confirmation close − target multiple × (stop price − confirmation close) OR close > position entry price AND bar delta > 0 for the required consecutive bars OR elapsed bars since entry signal >= time limit
