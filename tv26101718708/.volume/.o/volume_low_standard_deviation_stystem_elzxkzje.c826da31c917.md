Calculation: Adjusted volume = current volume if nonzero, otherwise the previous nonzero volume; if this value is greater than 2 × factor × the previous bar’s long-lookback standard deviation of adjusted volume, use this value; otherwise use the previous bar’s adjusted volume.
Long: (reversal-only entry mode AND short-term SMA(close) > its previous value AND its previous value ≤ its value two bars ago) OR (other entry mode AND short-term SMA(close) > its previous value AND short-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) > long-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) − factor × long-lookback standard deviation of adjusted volume)
Short: (reversal-only entry mode AND short-term SMA(close) < its previous value AND its previous value ≥ its value two bars ago) OR (other entry mode AND short-term SMA(close) < its previous value AND short-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) > long-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) − factor × long-lookback standard deviation of adjusted volume)
Exit: Long, (trend-only exit mode AND short-term SMA(close) < its previous value) OR (low-volume exit mode AND (short-term SMA(close) < its previous value OR short-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) ≤ long-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) − factor × long-lookback standard deviation of adjusted volume))
Exit: Short, (trend-only exit mode AND short-term SMA(close) > its previous value) OR (low-volume exit mode AND (short-term SMA(close) > its previous value OR a long position is open AND short-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) ≤ long-lookback SMA(adjusted volume) − factor × long-lookback standard deviation of adjusted volume))
