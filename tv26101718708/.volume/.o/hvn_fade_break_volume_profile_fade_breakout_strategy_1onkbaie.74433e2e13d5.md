Calculation: Rolling volume profile = At each profile-period boundary, aggregate the last N completed profile periods from chart bars. Divide the combined low-to-high range into equal rows; allocate each bar’s volume equally across the rows touched by its high-low range. POC is the highest-volume row midpoint. Expand the value area from the POC row, each time adding the higher-volume adjacent row (ties go to the upper row), until reaching the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower boundaries. Freeze POC, VAH, and VAL for the following profile period.
Calculation: HVN zones = In the rolling profile, select local-maximum rows at or above the configured fraction of the highest row, comparing within the configured row window (ties go to the lower row). For each peak, find the lowest row volume on each side before a higher-volume row; prominence is peak volume minus the larger of those two minima. Keep peaks meeting the configured prominence threshold, extend each zone from its peak across adjacent rows whose volume is at least peak volume minus prominence times the configured zone-width fraction, then retain the configured number of highest-volume zones. A zone price is its midpoint.
Calculation: Naked POCs = The POC of each just-completed profile period, kept until a later chart bar’s high-low range includes that price.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = A swing high/low is a bar high/low greater than/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Long: Profile exists AND ((previous bar low < VAL AND close > VAL AND close > previous close AND RSI(close) <= oversold threshold) OR (previous close <= VAH AND close > VAH AND volume > SMA(volume, configured lookback) × configured volume multiplier)) AND (single-position restriction disabled OR position is flat) AND (close − long stop > 0), where long stop is the lower of (VAH − configured ATR multiple × ATR) and the last confirmed swing low for a breakout, or the lower of (current low − 0.5 × configured ATR multiple × ATR) and the last confirmed swing low for a fade; if no swing low exists, use the other candidate.
Short: Profile exists AND ((previous bar high > VAH AND close < VAH AND close < previous close AND RSI(close) >= overbought threshold) OR (previous close >= VAL AND close < VAL AND volume > SMA(volume, configured lookback) × configured volume multiplier)) AND (single-position restriction disabled OR position is flat) AND (short stop − close > 0), where short stop is the higher of (VAL + configured ATR multiple × ATR) and the last confirmed swing high for a breakout, or the higher of (current high + 0.5 × configured ATR multiple × ATR) and the last confirmed swing high for a fade; if no swing high exists, use the other candidate.
Exit: Long, low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target; fade target is profile POC, breakout target is the nearest HVN midpoint or enabled naked POC above entry (choose the nearer; ties choose the naked POC), or, if neither exists, entry price + (entry price − stop) × configured R multiple.
Exit: Short, high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target; fade target is profile POC, breakout target is the nearest HVN midpoint or enabled naked POC below entry (choose the nearer; ties choose the naked POC), or, if neither exists, entry price − (stop − entry price) × configured R multiple.
