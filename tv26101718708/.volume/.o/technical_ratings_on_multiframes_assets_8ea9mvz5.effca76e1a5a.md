Calculation: Moving-average rating = average of votes from multiple simple and exponential moving averages using progressively longer lookbacks, plus a Hull moving average, volume-weighted moving average, and Ichimoku vote. Each moving-average vote is +1 when close is above the average, -1 when below, and 0 when equal. Ichimoku vote is +1 when the leading span 1 is above span 2, close is above span 1 but below the base line, and close crossed above the conversion line; -1 for the inverse conditions; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = average of available votes from RSI, stochastic, CCI, directional movement/ADX, Awesome Oscillator, momentum, MACD, stochastic RSI, Williams %R, bull/bear power, and Ultimate Oscillator. Votes are +1 for: RSI below its oversold level and rising; stochastic K and D below their oversold level with K crossing above D; CCI below its negative threshold and rising; ADX above its threshold with positive DI crossing above negative DI; Awesome Oscillator crossing above zero or rising after two positive bars; momentum rising; MACD above its signal line; stochastic RSI K and D below their oversold level with K crossing above D while close is below its medium-term EMA; Williams %R below its oversold level and rising; bear power below zero and rising while close is above its medium-term EMA; Ultimate Oscillator above its upper threshold. Votes are -1 for the corresponding opposite conditions: RSI overbought and rising; stochastic K and D overbought with K crossing below D; CCI above its positive threshold and falling; ADX above its threshold with positive DI crossing below negative DI; Awesome Oscillator crossing below zero or falling after two negative bars; momentum falling; MACD below its signal line; stochastic RSI K and D overbought with K crossing below D while close is above its medium-term EMA; Williams %R overbought and falling; bull power above zero and falling while close is below its medium-term EMA; Ultimate Oscillator below its lower threshold. Each other available vote is 0. Use the same lookback for each indicator's current and prior values; use shorter, middle, and longer lookbacks where an indicator requires them.
Calculation: Overall rating = average of the available moving-average rating and oscillator rating.
Long: On any configured timeframe, moving-average rating, oscillator rating, or overall rating is above its positive decision threshold.
Short: On any configured timeframe, moving-average rating, oscillator rating, or overall rating is below its negative decision threshold.
