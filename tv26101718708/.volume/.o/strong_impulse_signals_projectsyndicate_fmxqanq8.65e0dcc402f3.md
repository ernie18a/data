Calculation: True range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close)); ATR = Wilder average of true range
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot
Calculation: Coil range = highest high and lowest low over the coil lookback, excluding the current bar; coil is tight when its range / ATR ≤ the coil threshold
Calculation: Impulse strength = round to 0.1 and clamp to 0–10 the sum of: range/ATR points (≥2.5: 2.5; ≥1.8: 1.9; ≥1.3: 1.3; ≥1.0: 0.8; otherwise 0.3); body/range points (≥0.85: 1.5; ≥0.70: 1.1; ≥0.55: 0.7; otherwise 0.3); direction-adjusted close-in-range points (≥0.85: 1.0; ≥0.70: 0.7; ≥0.60: 0.4; otherwise 0); absolute net close change over the momentum run window / ATR points (≥3: 1.0; ≥2: 0.7; ≥1.2: 0.4; otherwise 0); consecutive rising (long) or falling (short) close points (≥4: 0.5; ≥3: 0.3; otherwise 0); volume/current rolling mean points (≥2.5: 1.5; ≥2: 1.1; ≥1.5: 0.7; ≥1.3: 0.4; otherwise 0); acceleration points (0.5 if the 3-bar close change is directionally positive/negative and exceeds the prior 3-bar change in that direction); coil points (0.8 if tight); structure-break points (0.7 if close breaks the latest confirmed swing); trend points (0.5 if both chart EMA and higher-timeframe EMA align, 0.25 if either aligns, otherwise 0). The chart EMA is aligned when its current value is rising/falling versus two bars earlier; the higher-timeframe EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Stop distance = clamp the distance from entry close to the selected stop anchor, including an ATR buffer, between an ATR-based floor and ceiling; anchor is the lowest low (long) or highest high (short) over the momentum run window, or the ignition bar low/high
Calculation: Targets = entry price ± stop distance × each configured R multiple, adding for long and subtracting for short
Long: bullish ignition candle AND range/ATR ≥ displacement threshold AND body/range ≥ minimum body dominance AND (close − low)/range ≥ close-in-range threshold AND (structure break required is off OR close > latest confirmed swing high) AND (coil release required is off OR coil is tight) AND (volume thrust required is off OR volume / rolling volume mean ≥ volume multiplier) AND impulse strength ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only filter is off OR impulse strength ≥ strong threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR current close > EMA of last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND cooldown satisfied AND no active trade AND long strength ≥ short strength
Short: bearish ignition candle AND range/ATR ≥ displacement threshold AND body/range ≥ minimum body dominance AND (1 − (close − low)/range) ≥ close-in-range threshold AND (structure break required is off OR close < latest confirmed swing low) AND (coil release required is off OR coil is tight) AND (volume thrust required is off OR volume / rolling volume mean ≥ volume multiplier) AND impulse strength ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only filter is off OR impulse strength ≥ strong threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR current close < EMA of last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND cooldown satisfied AND no active trade AND short strength > long strength
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR (high ≥ third target AND low > stop price)
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR (low ≤ third target AND high < stop price)
