Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on the current chart bars.
Calculation: Structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over a configurable lookback of completed bars before the current bar; the prior-bar levels use the same lookback ending one bar earlier.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = a bullish candle whose body is at least a configurable ATR multiple, body/range meets a configurable minimum, and close is in the top 20% of its range; OR at least two of the last three candles are bullish, current close exceeds the close two bars ago, and current close minus the three-bar low is at least a configurable ATR multiple. Bearish displacement is the inverse, using bearish candles, the bottom 20%, and the three-bar high minus current close.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish displacement = the corresponding displacement occurred within a configurable number of bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish BOS = on a confirmed bar, current close crosses above/below the prior-bar structure high/low plus/minus an ATR buffer, with the previous close at or below/above the corresponding prior threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish Order Block zone = the first bearish/bullish candle found by searching backward within a configurable lookback after the corresponding setup; its zone uses the configured source-candle range mode. Zone size in ATR = zone height divided by ATR at creation.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish Order Block retest = after price closes beyond the zone in its expected direction by a configurable ATR departure distance, a confirmed bar touches the zone after the minimum age and before any earlier qualifying retest. A touch occurs when low is at or below zone top and high is at or above zone bottom.
Long: confirmed bullish BOS AND recent bullish displacement AND bullish quality meets its configurable minimum AND all enabled bullish confirmation filters pass AND a qualifying bearish source candle exists with a unique origin AND any enabled zone-size filter passes; OR a bullish Order Block retest.
Short: confirmed bearish BOS AND recent bearish displacement AND bearish quality meets its configurable minimum AND all enabled bearish confirmation filters pass AND a qualifying bullish source candle exists with a unique origin AND any enabled zone-size filter passes; OR a bearish Order Block retest.
