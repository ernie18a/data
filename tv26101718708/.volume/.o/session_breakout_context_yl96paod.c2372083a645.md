Calculation: 1-minute pivot bias = +2 when the current 1-minute close is above its latest confirmed pivot high (3 bars on each side); otherwise −2 when below its latest confirmed pivot low (3 bars on each side); otherwise another value.
Calculation: CVD pressure = +1 when the 20-bar average of volume × (2×close−high−low)/max(high−low, 0.001) is positive and above its 5-bar average; −1 when negative and below its 5-bar average; otherwise 0. If volume is unavailable, use close−open instead of the volume-weighted expression.
Calculation: VSA code = +1 for high-volume, narrow-body bars whose lower wick is over 1.5 times the upper wick and larger than the body; −1 for the corresponding upper-wick condition. +2 is a high-volume wide-body bar closing at or above open; −2 is a high-volume wide-body bar closing below open. High volume means volume / 20-bar average volume > 1.8; low volume means < 0.6; narrow body means body < 25% of range; wide body means body > 75% of range.
Calculation: Exhaustion upper/lower = 20-bar EMA of close ± 3 × 14-bar ATR.
Calculation: Session reference range = high and low of the completed preceding session: Asia (18:00–02:00 Chicago) for London (02:00–07:00); London for New York (07:00–14:00); New York for Asia (18:00–02:00).
Calculation: Stop level = for a breakout, the selected session midpoint or opposite range extreme; for a fade short, upper boundary + (upper boundary − midpoint); for a fade long, lower boundary − (midpoint − lower boundary). Use the relevant reference session range and its midpoint.
Long: During London, New York, or Asia session, with no prior signal in that session, current high > reference range high AND 1-minute pivot bias = +2 AND CVD pressure = +1 AND VSA code ≠ +2.
Long: During London, New York, or Asia session, with no prior signal in that session, current high ≤ reference range high AND current low < reference range low AND (1-minute pivot bias = +2 OR VSA code = +1 OR current low < exhaustion lower).
Short: During London, New York, or Asia session, with no prior signal in that session, current high ≤ reference range high AND current low < reference range low AND 1-minute pivot bias = −2 AND CVD pressure = −1 AND VSA code ≠ −2.
Short: During London, New York, or Asia session, with no prior signal in that session, current high > reference range high AND (1-minute pivot bias = −2 OR VSA code = −1 OR current high > exhaustion upper).
Exit: Long, low < stop level OR Chicago time reaches 07:00 for a London signal, 14:00 for a New York signal, or 02:00 for an Asia signal.
Exit: Short, high > stop level OR Chicago time reaches 07:00 for a London signal, 14:00 for a New York signal, or 02:00 for an Asia signal.
