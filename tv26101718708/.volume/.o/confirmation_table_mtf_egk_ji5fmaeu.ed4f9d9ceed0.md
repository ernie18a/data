Calculation: Trend-timeframe indicators use the current trend-timeframe bar, which may still be forming; entry-timeframe indicators use the current entry-timeframe bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: MACD = fast EMA minus slow EMA; signal line = EMA of MACD.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added when close rises, subtracted when close falls, unchanged when close is equal.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = shorter-lookback volume SMA minus longer-lookback volume SMA; the shorter lookback is 5 and the longer is 20 in the source.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume divided by the 20-period volume SMA.
Calculation: Order-block bias = persistent state set to long when close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high (3 bars left and right), or short when close falls below the latest confirmed pivot low (3 bars left and right); otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Trend long score = sum of enabled bullish EMA, MACD, ADX, and Supertrend conditions; trend short score = sum of enabled bearish conditions.
Calculation: Entry long score = sum of enabled bullish RSI, OBV, volume, order-block, and ATR conditions; entry short score = sum of enabled bearish conditions.
Long: On the trend-timeframe bar, fast EMA > slow EMA OR (MACD > signal line AND MACD > 0) OR (ADX > 20 AND +DI > -DI) OR Supertrend direction is bullish OR trend long score > trend short score; OR, on the entry-timeframe bar, RSI > 50 OR (OBV > its 20-period SMA AND OBV > prior OBV) OR (volume oscillator > 0 AND volume ratio > 1) OR ATR(14) > its 50-period SMA OR order-block bias is long OR entry long score > entry short score.
Short: On the trend-timeframe bar, fast EMA < slow EMA OR (MACD < signal line AND MACD < 0) OR (ADX > 20 AND -DI > +DI) OR Supertrend direction is bearish OR trend short score > trend long score; OR, on the entry-timeframe bar, RSI < 50 OR (OBV < its 20-period SMA AND OBV < prior OBV) OR (volume oscillator < 0 AND volume ratio < 1) OR ATR(14) <= its 50-period SMA OR order-block bias is short OR entry short score > entry long score.
