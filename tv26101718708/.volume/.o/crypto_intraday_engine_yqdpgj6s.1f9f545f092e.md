Calculation: UTC daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 since 00:00 UTC, reset each UTC day
Calculation: VWAP neutral band = ATR × configurable distance
Calculation: Support zone = confirmed pivot low through pivot low + ATR at the pivot × configurable zone depth; resistance zone = confirmed pivot high − ATR at the pivot × configurable zone depth. Confirm pivots after the configured right-side bars; remove a support when close is below its bottom minus the ATR invalidation buffer, or a resistance when close is above its top plus the buffer. Use the nearest valid support at or below price and nearest valid resistance at or above price.
Calculation: Compressed area = nearest resistance bottom − nearest support top is less than or equal to ATR × configurable minimum gap, or the zones overlap
Long: close > UTC daily VWAP + VWAP neutral band OR a support reaction is detected: after a closed bar touches the nearest support zone while close > UTC daily VWAP + VWAP neutral band and the zones are not compressed, within the configured number of subsequent closed bars close is above both that zone's top and UTC daily VWAP; evaluate each zone once
Short: close < UTC daily VWAP − VWAP neutral band OR a resistance reaction is detected: after a closed bar touches the nearest resistance zone while close < UTC daily VWAP − VWAP neutral band and the zones are not compressed, within the configured number of subsequent closed bars close is below both that zone's bottom and UTC daily VWAP; evaluate each zone once
