Calculation: Zigzag leg direction = use the selected mini-chart timeframe’s bars; an exclusive highest high over the Zigzag lookback sets an upward leg, and an exclusive lowest low sets a downward leg. The current leg may use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the Trend Pivot lookback on both sides; it is available only after that many later bars complete on the selected mini-chart timeframe.
Calculation: HH / LH = confirmed swing high > / ≤ the preceding confirmed swing high; HL / LL = confirmed swing low > / ≤ the preceding confirmed swing low.
Long: latest zigzag leg is upward OR a confirmed HH OR a confirmed HL
Short: latest zigzag leg is downward OR a confirmed LH OR a confirmed LL
