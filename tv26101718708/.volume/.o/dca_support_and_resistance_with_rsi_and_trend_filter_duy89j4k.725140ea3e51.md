Calculation: Support = low at the most recent bar whose low equals the lowest low over the support lookback
Calculation: Resistance = high at the most recent bar whose high equals the highest high over the resistance lookback
Calculation: WaveTrend bull = WaveTrend crosses above its signal while below its oversold level; WaveTrend bear = WaveTrend crosses below its signal while above its overbought level
Calculation: Fast and slow oscillator and trend values = calculate on the respective faster and slower timeframe, using the last completed bar
Calculation: Trend direction = the configured combination of correlation, Schaff Trend Cycle, MACD histogram, directional indicators, and Gaussian-band trend; positive is up, negative is down
Calculation: Average entry price = accumulated entry cost divided by absolute position size
Long: (chart oscillator crosses above its oversold threshold when WaveTrend and divergence signals are disabled OR chart oscillator is below its oversold threshold and enabled WaveTrend bull or bullish/hidden bullish RSI divergence occurs OR fast oscillator is below its oversold threshold and enabled fast WaveTrend bull occurs on a new fast-timeframe bar) AND (close is at or above support when support/resistance filter is enabled) AND (close is below the lower Bollinger Band when Bollinger filter is enabled) AND (fast trend is up or fast filter is disabled) AND (slow trend is up or slow filter is disabled or open trades are below its entry threshold or the fast entry signal occurs) AND fast and slow oscillators are below their overbought thresholds when their trend filters are enabled AND no enabled slow bearish WaveTrend full-sell signal AND entry cooldown elapsed AND no exit occurred this bar AND position is non-short AND trade type permits long (for BOTH, close is at or above the slower-timeframe EMA unless already long)
