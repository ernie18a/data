Calculation: Range filter = Let B be the EMA of |close − previous close|; let R be the EMA of B with a longer lookback, multiplied by sensitivity. Starting with the first close, update the filter each bar: if close > prior filter, use the greater of the prior filter and close − R; otherwise use the lesser of the prior filter and close + R.
Calculation: Wave oscillator = EMA((hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, fast lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(|hlc3 − EMA(hlc3, fast lookback)|, fast lookback)), slow lookback); its signal is the 3-bar SMA of the oscillator. The slow lookback is distinct from the fast lookback.
Calculation: Volume node = hlc3 of the most recent bar whose volume equals the highest volume over the lookback.
Long: Flat position AND (within the configured session if the session filter is enabled) AND close > EMA(close, long lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, long lookback) AND close > Range filter AND Wave oscillator crosses above its signal AND RSI(close, 14) > 50 AND volume > SMA(volume, 20) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP(hlc3)) AND (momentum filter disabled OR (RSI(close, 14) > 50 AND MACD > MACD signal AND close > EMA(close, shortest lookback))) AND (volume-zone filter disabled OR volume node unavailable OR close > volume node OR |close − volume node| / close < 0.003) AND close > previous bar's high; OR long position AND the required number of prior adds (0, 1, 2, or 3) AND close ≤ first entry price × (1 − add-step percentage × (prior adds + 1) / 100).
Short: Flat position AND (within the configured session if the session filter is enabled) AND close < EMA(close, long lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, long lookback) AND close < Range filter AND Wave oscillator crosses below its signal AND RSI(close, 14) < 50 AND volume > SMA(volume, 20) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP(hlc3)) AND (momentum filter disabled OR (RSI(close, 14) < 50 AND MACD < MACD signal AND close < EMA(close, shortest lookback))) AND (volume-zone filter disabled OR volume node unavailable OR close < volume node OR |close − volume node| / close < 0.003) AND close < previous bar's low; OR short position AND the required number of prior adds (0, 1, 2, or 3) AND close ≥ first entry price × (1 + add-step percentage × (prior adds + 1) / 100).
Exit: long, close ≥ average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100) OR close ≤ average entry price × (1 − drawdown-stop percentage / 100)
Exit: short, close ≤ average entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage / 100) OR close ≥ average entry price × (1 + drawdown-stop percentage / 100)
