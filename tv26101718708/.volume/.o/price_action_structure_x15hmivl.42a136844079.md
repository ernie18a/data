Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low exceeding the highs/lows of the selected number of bars on both sides; internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots. A pivot becomes available only after its right-side bars complete.
Long: Close > the latest unbroken confirmed internal or swing pivot high
Short: Close < the latest unbroken confirmed internal or swing pivot low
Calculation: Swing-point labels = compare each newly confirmed swing-point pivot with the previous pivot of the same type; a first or equal/higher high is HH, a lower high is LH, a first or equal/lower low is LL, and a higher low is HL.
Long: A confirmed swing pivot is HH or HL
Short: A confirmed swing pivot is LH or LL
Long: Current selected-timeframe low > high two selected-timeframe bars earlier
Short: Current selected-timeframe high < low two selected-timeframe bars earlier
Calculation: Active swing range = latest confirmed swing pivot high and low; midpoint = their average. Swing bias = direction of the latest swing-structure break, initially neutral.
Calculation: Bullish order block = zone from the lowest-low candle between the latest confirmed internal pivot low and an internal bullish break, bounded by that candle's low and the higher of its open and close. Bearish order block = zone from the highest-high candle between the latest confirmed internal pivot high and an internal bearish break, bounded by the lower of its open and close and its high. Keep active zones until price closes beyond the far edge, or a wick passes it if wick mitigation is selected.
Long: An active bullish order block is touched (current low <= its top and previous low > its top), and an internal bullish BOS occurs within the selected setup window after the touch, and the selected quality filter passes: strict requires close <= active swing-range midpoint AND non-bearish swing bias; balanced requires either condition; aggressive has no additional filter
Short: An active bearish order block is touched (current high >= its bottom and previous high < its bottom), and an internal bearish BOS occurs within the selected setup window after the touch, and the selected quality filter passes: strict requires close >= active swing-range midpoint AND non-bullish swing bias; balanced requires either condition; aggressive has no additional filter
