Calculation: Prior-range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = configured fraction of the previous bar’s ATR.
Calculation: Major resistance/support = nearest aged level above/below the previous close, formed by clustering the configured number of swing highs/lows from the configured lookback within the configured ATR tolerance; a swing high/low has the configured number of lower highs/higher lows on each side.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the median volume baseline lagged one bar; on intraday bars, use the median of prior sessions’ volumes at the same time slot when enough observations exist, otherwise use the prior-bar-lagged median of recent bars.
Calculation: HawkEye green/red = classify each bar using its range and volume versus their configured moving averages (falling back to the shorter baseline length until available), its close versus the previous bar’s midpoint and bands at that midpoint plus/minus the previous bar’s range divided by the configured divisor, and the listed conditions: green if close > previous midpoint, or (range > range average AND close > upper band AND volume > volume average), or (high > previous high AND range < range average / configured divisor AND volume < volume average), or (low < previous low AND range < range average / configured divisor AND volume > volume average); red if not gray, not green, and (range > range average AND close < lower band AND volume > volume average, or close < previous midpoint). Gray takes precedence when (range > range average AND lower band < close < upper band AND volume is between volume average and its configured multiple AND volume > previous volume), or (range < range average / configured divisor AND volume < volume average / configured divisor), or (lower band < close < upper band).
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Tight-base filter = prior-range high minus prior-range low, divided by the previous bar’s ATR; pass when at or below the configured maximum, or when this filter is disabled or unavailable.
Long: (first close above prior-range high + breakout buffer, with the previous close at or below the previous bar’s prior-range high + previous breakout buffer, OR close above the previous bar’s major resistance + breakout buffer) AND HawkEye green AND close location at or above the configured threshold AND relative volume at or above its configured threshold AND tight-base filter passes AND (weekly close > weekly EMA, both from the last completed week, or weekly trend filter is inactive/unavailable).
Long: A qualifying breakout that failed only the current-bar relative-volume threshold, then held its broken level (no close back below it) through the configured following-bar window, and whose average relative volume over the breakout bar and that window is at or above its configured threshold; the other Long filters must have passed on the breakout bar.
Short: (first close below prior-range low - breakout buffer, with the previous close at or above the previous bar’s prior-range low - previous breakout buffer, OR close below the previous bar’s major support - breakout buffer) AND HawkEye red AND close location at or below one minus the configured threshold AND relative volume at or above its configured threshold AND tight-base filter passes AND (weekly close < weekly EMA, both from the last completed week, or weekly trend filter is inactive/unavailable).
Short: A qualifying breakdown that failed only the current-bar relative-volume threshold, then held its broken level (no close back above it) through the configured following-bar window, and whose average relative volume over the breakdown bar and that window is at or above its configured threshold; the other Short filters must have passed on the breakdown bar.
