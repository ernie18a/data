Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is above/below the highs/lows of the pivot lookback bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Long: Close > latest confirmed pivot high AND Close > main EMA AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND Volume > its SMA
Short: Close < latest confirmed pivot low AND Close < main EMA AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND Volume > its SMA
Exit: Long, Low <= entry price − stop multiple × ATR OR Low <= highest high since entry − trailing multiple × ATR
Exit: Short, High >= entry price + stop multiple × ATR OR High >= lowest low since entry + trailing multiple × ATR
