Calculation: Flow Vector = sum of directional scores: +22 for EMA(8)>EMA(21)>EMA(50), otherwise −22 for the inverse stack; if neither stack holds, +8 when close>EMA(21), −8 when close<EMA(21); +18/−18 for a rising/falling KAMA; +15/−15 for rising/falling OBV over its lookback; +12/−12 for positive/negative volume delta (EMA of bull volume minus EMA of bear volume); +18/−18 for bullish/bearish higher-timeframe EMA bias; +15/−15 for close above/below KAMA. Clamp to [−100,+100]. Higher-timeframe bias uses fast and slow EMAs and close on the higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) ÷ log10(lookback).
Calculation: Balance bands = lookback SMA of close ± a configurable multiple of the lookback standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Bull pin bar = close>open, lower wick≥42% of bar range, upper wick≤32%, body≥0.10×ATR, and close in the upper 28% of range; bear pin bar is the mirror condition.
Calculation: Regime = Chop if Choppiness Index≥configured threshold or (Efficiency Ratio≤configured chop threshold and ADX<configured trend threshold); otherwise Trend Bull if ADX≥trend threshold, Efficiency Ratio≥trend threshold, Flow Vector>20, and close>KAMA; otherwise Trend Bear if the corresponding directional conditions hold with Flow Vector<−20 and close<KAMA; otherwise Balance.
Calculation: Confluence score = 2 points for a trend pullback or impulse setup, 1 for a band fade or flow zero-cross setup, plus 1 each for flow accelerating in the signal direction, higher-timeframe bias aligned (or filter disabled), volume meeting its configured average threshold, regime not Chop, and RSI(7) in the direction’s allowed range.
Long: (Trend Bull and Flow Vector≥configured strong threshold and Flow Vector>its prior value≥its value two bars ago and (low≤EMA(8)+ATR tolerance and close>EMA(8) or low≤KAMA+ATR tolerance and close>KAMA) and bull pin bar and lowest low over the latest 5 bars≤min(EMA(8),KAMA)+0.18×ATR and close−KAMA≤configured maximum extension×ATR and 40<RSI(7)<70) OR ((Trend Bull or Balance) and close>highest high of the preceding configured micro-swing lookback and prior close≤that high and body≥0.85×configured impulse minimum×ATR and Flow Vector>0.65×configured strong threshold and volume delta>0 and bull pin bar and higher-timeframe filter passes and close−KAMA≤configured maximum extension×ATR) OR (Balance and high≥upper balance band−0.08×ATR and (Flow Vector≤−configured extreme threshold or (Flow Vector>its prior value and prior Flow Vector<−configured extreme threshold+12)) and bull pin bar and RSI(7)<42) OR (not Chop and Flow Vector crosses above zero and bull pin bar with body≥configured impulse minimum×ATR and volume delta>0 and higher-timeframe filter passes); require confluence score≥configured minimum, volume filter passes, configured session filter passes, and signal cooldown elapsed.
