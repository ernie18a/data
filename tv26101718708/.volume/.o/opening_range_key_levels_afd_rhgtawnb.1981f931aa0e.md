Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low from the exchange-session open through the configured opening window; freeze on the first confirmed bar at or after the window ends.
Calculation: Retest tolerance = configured multiple of either the 14-session daily ATR (RMA of true range, using completed sessions) or chart-bar ATR, or a configured number of minimum ticks.
Long: On a confirmed in-session bar, close > a locked, width-valid opening-range high; OR a later bar after an upward break touches within tolerance of that high and closes above it; OR a confirmed close crosses above an enabled static key level (prior-day/week/month, overnight, pre-market, or current-session open); OR a later bar after an upward key-level break touches within tolerance of the level and closes above it. Apply the configured killzone and, when enabled, OR-width filters to OR events.
Long: After a downward opening-range or key-level break, a later retest touches within tolerance of the broken low/level and closes above it.
Short: On a confirmed in-session bar, close < a locked, width-valid opening-range low; OR a later bar after a downward break touches within tolerance of that low and closes below it; OR a confirmed close crosses below an enabled static key level (prior-day/week/month, overnight, pre-market, or current-session open); OR a later bar after a downward key-level break touches within tolerance of the level and closes below it. Apply the configured killzone and, when enabled, OR-width filters to OR events.
Short: After an upward opening-range or key-level break, a later retest touches within tolerance of the broken high/level and closes below it.
