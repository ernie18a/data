Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of: chart-timeframe EMA(fast) > EMA(medium) > EMA(slow) > EMA(long-term); last completed H1 close > H1 EMA(50) > H1 EMA(200); ADX >= minimum and +DI > -DI; RSI within the configured long range and rising; MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0 and rising; bullish candle pattern; Bollinger Bands outside Keltner Channels and volume > 0.9 × its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = count of: chart-timeframe EMA(fast) < EMA(medium) < EMA(slow) < EMA(long-term); last completed H1 close < H1 EMA(50) < H1 EMA(200); ADX >= minimum and -DI > +DI; RSI within the configured short range and falling; MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0 and falling; bearish candle pattern; Bollinger Bands outside Keltner Channels and volume > 0.9 × its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = (close > open and body > 50% of range and close in the upper 40% of range) OR (lower wick >= 2 × body and upper wick <= 0.5 × body and range > 0.4 × ATR) OR (current bullish body engulfs the previous completed bar's body).
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = (close < open and body > 50% of range and close in the lower 40% of range) OR (upper wick >= 2 × body and lower wick <= 0.5 × body and range > 0.4 × ATR) OR (current bearish body engulfs the previous completed bar's body).
Calculation: ADX, +DI, and -DI = calculate directional movement from completed chart-timeframe bars; smooth directional movement and true range with the same lookback, derive +DI and -DI, then smooth DX with that lookback to obtain ADX.
Calculation: Initial long stop = clamp(entry price - ATR × stop-distance bounds, lowest low over the configured lookback before the entry bar - ATR × stop padding); initial short stop = clamp(entry price + ATR × stop-distance bounds, highest high over the configured lookback before the entry bar + ATR × stop padding). Use ATR and prior-bar indicators from the last completed chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) initial stop distance multiplied by each configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: bullish confluence score >= configured minimum AND close > chart-timeframe EMA(long-term) AND (low <= EMA(medium) × configured touch tolerance AND close > EMA(medium) OR abs(close - EMA(medium)) < 1.2 × ATR) AND close > EMA(medium) AND ATR > 0.5 × its 50-bar average AND ATR < 3.5 × its 50-bar average AND (previous bar's UTC hour is in the configured London or New York session, if session filtering is enabled) AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade count below its limit AND drawdown below its configured limit AND flat.
Short: bearish confluence score >= configured minimum AND close < chart-timeframe EMA(long-term) AND (high >= EMA(medium) × configured touch tolerance AND close < EMA(medium) OR abs(close - EMA(medium)) < 1.2 × ATR) AND close < EMA(medium) AND ATR > 0.5 × its 50-bar average AND ATR < 3.5 × its 50-bar average AND (previous bar's UTC hour is in the configured London or New York session, if session filtering is enabled) AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade count below its limit AND drawdown below its configured limit AND flat.
Exit: long, price reaches any profit target or the current stop; after the first target is reached, raise the stop to close - ATR × configured trailing multiple when that is above the existing stop.
Exit: short, price reaches any profit target or the current stop; after the first target is reached, lower the stop to close + ATR × configured trailing multiple when that is below the existing stop.
