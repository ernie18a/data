Calculation: ATR = average true range; use 14 bars for pool widths, penetration, and sweep score, and 100 bars for AVWAP stretch.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = active resistance pools from confirmed swing highs, higher-timeframe swing highs, previous completed day/week highs, and equal highs; support pools use corresponding lows. Merge nearby same-side levels, set zone boundaries around the merged midpoint using ATR, and track hits, health, age, source weight, state, and pending confirmation. Use only unconsumed pools within maximum age; pool zones are midpoint ± half-width.
Calculation: Sweep quality score = weighted mean of normalized penetration, reclaim, wick ratio, candle direction, pool hits/health/source weight, absolute impulse-AVWAP stretch, higher-timeframe trend agreement, volume ratio, micro trend reversal, break-of-structure distance, and position within the nearest support-to-resistance channel. Adjust component weights by trend/range/coil regime; multiply by session weight in multiplier mode, coil bonus, pool-age penalty, and volume-vacuum bonus. Use the same score calculation for long and short sweeps, with direction-appropriate wick, candle, trend, and structure inputs.
Calculation: Impulse AVWAP = volume-weighted HLC3 since the most recent bar that exceeds the prior anchor-lookback high or falls below the prior anchor-lookback low; reset on either event. Daily AVWAP = volume-weighted HLC3 since the current day began. For SFP calculations, use the current forming day/bar values.
Calculation: SFP percentile rank = percentile rank over the longer lookback of 100 × tanh(1.1 × EMA5(excess) + 0.9 × curvature), where excess = (close − impulse AVWAP)/ATR100 + (daily AVWAP enabled ? 0.8 × (close − daily AVWAP)/ATR100 : 0) + 0.7 × (upper wick − lower wick)/true range + 0.35 × max(volume z-score, 0) × sign((close − impulse AVWAP)/ATR100); volume z-score uses the shorter 50-bar mean and standard deviation. Curvature = −(9-bar rate of change of EMA34 close − its 3-bar EMA at the prior bar).
Calculation: Pending SFP arm = on a confirmed bar, arm short for the configured TTL when SFP percentile rank is at/above its upper threshold and either impulse-AVWAP stretch is above its threshold with volume z-score above its threshold, or a short sweep occurs; arm long symmetrically at/below the lower percentile threshold with negative stretch and volume condition, or a long sweep. A new arm requires its side's cooldown to be zero and that side not already armed; decrement arm TTL and cooldown bar by bar.
Long: Confirmed bar AND session gate passes AND an unconsumed support pool has a spike sweep (low below pool bottom, close above midpoint, penetration/ATR above minimum, and close above low) OR a grind sweep (a close within the prior grind lookback was below pool bottom by the configured ATR margin, current close is above bottom and below top plus configured margin, and close > open); pool state is unused; sweep score passes the configured score filter if enabled and the reversal-type minimum score (grind uses its own minimum; spike type is ABS when volume z-score and lower-wick ratio and reclaim exceed their thresholds, REJ when lower-wick ratio and reclaim exceed their thresholds and close > open, DIV when volume z-score and reclaim exceed their thresholds and lower-wick ratio is below its threshold); cooldown since last sweep passes; and, if confirmation window is enabled, within that window after the raw sweep close > pool top, close is not below the prior low, and the stored sweep score passes its applicable filters. OR confirmed bar AND session gate passes AND an SFP long arm is active AND close > the prior-bar highest high over the shorter BOS lookback AND SFP trigger cooldown passes.
Short: Confirmed bar AND session gate passes AND an unconsumed resistance pool has a spike sweep (high above pool top, close below midpoint, penetration/ATR above minimum, and close below high) OR a grind sweep (a close within the prior grind lookback was above pool top by the configured ATR margin, current close is below top and above bottom minus configured margin, and close < open); pool state is unused; sweep score passes the configured score filter if enabled and the reversal-type minimum score (grind uses its own minimum; spike type is ABS when volume z-score and upper-wick ratio and reclaim exceed their thresholds, REJ when upper-wick ratio and reclaim exceed their thresholds and close < open, DIV when volume z-score and reclaim exceed their thresholds and upper-wick ratio is below its threshold); cooldown since last sweep passes; and, if confirmation window is enabled, within that window after the raw sweep close < pool bottom, close is not above the prior high, and the stored sweep score passes its applicable filters. OR confirmed bar AND session gate passes AND an SFP short arm is active AND close < the prior-bar lowest low over the shorter BOS lookback AND SFP trigger cooldown passes.
