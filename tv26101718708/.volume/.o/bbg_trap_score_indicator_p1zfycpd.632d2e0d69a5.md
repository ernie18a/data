Calculation: 14-bar ATR; 20-bar SMA of volume; reference high/low = highest high / lowest low of the prior N completed bars
Calculation: Bar delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), or 0 if high = low; average absolute delta = 20-bar SMA of absolute bar delta
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trap score = sum of weighted conditions: 15 for a qualifying sweep (bear: high > reference high, close < reference high, sweep distance ≤ 0.5 ATR, upper wick/range ≥ 0.35; bull: low < reference low, close > reference low, sweep distance ≤ 0.5 ATR, lower wick/range ≥ 0.35); 15 for a reclaim (bear: a high broke reference high within the last 3 bars and close < reference high; bull: a low broke reference low within the last 3 bars and close > reference low); 10 for extreme delta (bear: positive delta ≥ 2 × average absolute delta; bull: absolute negative delta ≥ 2 × average absolute delta); 10 for volume ≥ 1.5 × volume SMA; 15 for absorption (corresponding extreme delta and volume condition, and directional price progress ≤ 0.15 ATR); 10 for delta-price divergence (bear: delta ≥ prior 5-bar highest delta and close < prior 5-bar highest close; bull: delta ≤ prior 5-bar lowest delta and close > prior 5-bar lowest close); 10 for large-trade absorption (volume ≥ 2.5 × volume SMA, directional price progress ≤ 0.15 ATR, and corresponding wick/range ≥ 0.40); 10 if the minimum distance from current high/low to any reference high/low, previous-day high/low, previous-week high/low, or higher-timeframe high/low is ≤ 0.15 × higher-timeframe ATR; 5 for rejection of session VWAP or its ±1 standard-deviation band (20-bar close standard deviation); 5 for confirmation (bear: close < prior low or [close < reference high and delta ≤ 0]; bull: close > prior high or [close > reference low and delta ≥ 0]); 5 when volume has declined for 3 consecutive bars and range < 0.50 ATR. Subtract 5 if sweep and reclaim both score and neither absorption nor divergence scores. Clamp score to 0–100. Higher-timeframe high/low and ATR use the current forming bar in realtime and the last completed bar historically; previous-day and previous-week levels use the last completed bar.
Long: bullish trap score ≥ minimum entry score
Short: bearish trap score ≥ minimum entry score
Exit: Long, low ≤ sweep low − 0.10 × ATR OR high ≥ entry price + risk OR high ≥ session VWAP OR high ≥ range midpoint OR high ≥ opposing value area OR high ≥ previous-week high OR high ≥ entry price + 3 × risk
Exit: Short, high ≥ sweep high + 0.10 × ATR OR low ≤ entry price − risk OR low ≤ session VWAP OR low ≤ range midpoint OR low ≤ opposing value area OR low ≤ previous-week low OR low ≤ entry price − 3 × risk
