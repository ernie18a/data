Calculation: Pair flow score = 100 × EMA of the weighted mean of five components: (1) ATR-normalized momentum, clamp((close − close[momentum lookback]) / ATR / 4, −1, 1); (2) signed relative volume, clamp((clamp(volume / SMA(volume, relative-volume lookback), 0.25, 4) − 1) × sign(momentum) / 2, −1, 1); (3) trend, clamp((EMA(close, fast trend length) − EMA(close, slow trend length)) / ATR / 3, −1, 1), with fast length shorter than slow; (4) volatility expansion, clamp((ATR / close / SMA(ATR / close, relative-volume lookback) − 1), −1, 1) × sign(momentum); and (5) breadth, +1 when close is above the fast EMA, otherwise −1. The weighted mean divides the sum of each component times its weight by the sum of the five weights. Use calculation-timeframe bars; by default use the last completed bar, or the bar still forming in live mode.
Calculation: Currency flow score = average of the scores for its seven connected pairs, adding each pair score when the currency is its base and subtracting it when it is its quote. Use the same calculation-timeframe bar selection as the pair flow score.
Long: pair flow score ≥ 10 OR (base currency has the highest currency flow score AND quote currency has the lowest currency flow score)
Short: pair flow score ≤ −10 OR (base currency has the lowest currency flow score AND quote currency has the highest currency flow score)
