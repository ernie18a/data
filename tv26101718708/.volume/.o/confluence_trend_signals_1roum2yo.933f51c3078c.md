Calculation: Fast Coral = six cascaded exponential smoothings of close, combined with the Coral coefficients; direction is bullish when its value rises, bearish when it falls. Slow Coral uses the same smoothing length and a larger smoothing factor than Fast Coral.
Calculation: Pivot percentages = EMA of (close / most recent confirmed pivot high × 100) and EMA of (most recent confirmed pivot low / close × 100); pivot high and low use the same left, right, and smoothing lengths. Bullish when high percentage > low percentage; bearish when low percentage > high percentage. Strong bullish/bearish when the respective percentage exceeds its threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot percentages use the same pivot calculation on higher-timeframe bars, with lookahead off (last completed bar historically; the forming bar in realtime).
Calculation: MACD = fast EMA of close − slow EMA of close; signal = SMA of MACD; histogram = MACD − signal. Bullish when MACD > signal and histogram > 0; bearish when MACD < signal and histogram < 0. The fast EMA length is shorter than the slow EMA length.
Calculation: Order blocks = on a close crossing above a confirmed pivot high, create a bullish block from the candle with the lowest parsed low since that pivot; on a close crossing below a confirmed pivot low, create a bearish block from the candle with the highest parsed high since that pivot. Parsed high/low swap to low/high respectively when candle range is at least twice the volatility ATR. Track swing and internal pivots, with the swing lookback longer than the internal lookback; remove a bullish block when close falls below its low and a bearish block when close rises above its high. Near bullish block means close is from its low through its high plus proximity distance; near bearish block means close is from its low minus proximity distance through its high. Proximity distance = ATR × proximity factor.
Calculation: Entry score = 1×matching fast Coral direction + 0.5×matching fast Coral flip + 0.5×matching slow Coral direction + 1×matching pivot direction + 1×matching strong pivot + 0.75×matching higher-timeframe pivot direction + 1×matching MACD direction + 0.5×matching MACD crossover + 1.5×near matching order block + 1×matching VWAP side + 0.5×matching VWAP momentum. VWAP momentum requires VWAP rising for long or falling for short over its lookback, as well as price on the matching side. VWAP is session VWAP. Score must meet the minimum-score threshold.
Calculation: Maintenance score = entry score excluding fast Coral flip and MACD crossover bonuses.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price ± ATR × stop multiplier; if structure stops are enabled, use the tighter of that stop and the nearest matching swing extreme or matching order-block edge, each offset by 0.2 ATR, but keep at least 0.5 ATR distance. Initial risk = absolute distance from entry to stop. Targets = entry price ± initial risk × each target multiple. After target 1, trailing stop moves to entry; after target 2, to target 1; after target 3, to target 2.
Long: Fast Coral is bullish (or flips bullish on this bar when flip-only is enabled) AND pivot is bullish AND MACD is bullish AND entry score meets its threshold AND, when enabled, slow Coral is bullish AND higher-timeframe pivot is bullish AND price is above VWAP AND price is near a bullish order block; bar is confirmed and the prior signal direction is not long.
Short: Fast Coral is bearish (or flips bearish on this bar when flip-only is enabled) AND pivot is bearish AND MACD is bearish AND entry score meets its threshold AND, when enabled, slow Coral is bearish AND higher-timeframe pivot is bearish AND price is below VWAP AND price is near a bearish order block; bar is confirmed and the prior signal direction is not short.
Exit: long, after entry bar, low <= current stop (initial stop or trailing stop) OR high >= a configured take-profit target that closes some or all of the position OR the bearish entry signal is confirmed OR the long maintenance score is below its configured decay threshold for the configured consecutive bars, subject to any configured minimum target-hit gate.
Exit: short, after entry bar, high >= current stop (initial stop or trailing stop) OR low <= a configured take-profit target that closes some or all of the position OR the bullish entry signal is confirmed OR the short maintenance score is below its configured decay threshold for the configured consecutive bars, subject to any configured minimum target-hit gate.
