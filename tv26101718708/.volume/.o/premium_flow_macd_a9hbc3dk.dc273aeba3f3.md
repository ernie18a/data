Calculation: MACD histogram = EMA(close, fast length) − EMA(close, slow length) − EMA of that MACD line over the signal length
Calculation: Projected histogram = MACD histogram + projection bars × (3-bar linear regression of the histogram at offset 0 − the same regression at offset 1)
Calculation: Low sweep is active = current low is below the lowest low among the preceding sweep-lookback bars; keep it active through the maximum bars-after-sweep limit, counting the sweep bar as bar 0, then clear it. Reset it after a long signal.
Calculation: High sweep is active = current high is above the highest high among the preceding sweep-lookback bars; keep it active through the maximum bars-after-sweep limit, counting the sweep bar as bar 0, then clear it. Reset it after a short signal.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND abs(close − open) / (high − low + 1e-10) > displacement body-ratio threshold
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND abs(close − open) / (high − low + 1e-10) > displacement body-ratio threshold
Long: low sweep is active AND (bullish displacement OR (volume > 20-bar simple moving average of volume × volume-spike multiplier AND close > open)) AND ((MACD histogram < zero tolerance AND projected histogram > 0) OR (MACD histogram > 0 AND previous-bar MACD histogram <= 0)) AND MACD histogram < maximum histogram distance for entry AND MACD histogram > previous-bar MACD histogram AND the same full condition was false on the previous bar AND bar is confirmed
Short: high sweep is active AND (bearish displacement OR (volume > 20-bar simple moving average of volume × volume-spike multiplier AND close < open)) AND ((MACD histogram > −zero tolerance AND projected histogram < 0) OR (MACD histogram < 0 AND previous-bar MACD histogram >= 0)) AND MACD histogram > −maximum histogram distance for entry AND MACD histogram < previous-bar MACD histogram AND the same full condition was false on the previous bar AND bar is confirmed
