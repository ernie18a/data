Calculation: Stochastic RSI = smoothed stochastic of RSI, using the configured lookback and smoothing lengths; close-based RSI and resulting close stochastic RSI use the configured analysis timeframe, while high- and low-based RSI and stochastic RSI use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI bar direction = green when close stochastic RSI is at least its prior value, otherwise red; signal direction is 100 for green, 0 for red, and 50 when neither direction is assigned by the bar-color rules.
Calculation: Plotted stochastic low = the lower of high- and low-based stochastic RSI, capped at the close stochastic RSI or the bar's open stochastic RSI as in the plotted-bar calculation; plotted stochastic high = the higher of high- and low-based stochastic RSI, capped at the bar's open stochastic RSI as in that calculation.
Calculation: RSI divergence = bullish when the lowest close over 3 bars is below its lowest over 5 bars and current analysis-timeframe RSI is above its 5-bar low; bearish when the highest close over 3 bars is above its highest over 5 bars and current analysis-timeframe RSI is below its 5-bar high.
Calculation: Uptrend = the 50-bar simple moving average of close has risen for 5 bars; downtrend = it has fallen for 5 bars.
Long: During the configured trade window and (volume bias is disabled or volume exceeds its configured moving average and close stochastic RSI exceeds its 13-bar exponential moving average), any selected entry rule is true: (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is below 90 and below its value 1 bar ago, which is below its current value, and signal direction is 100 now and on each of the prior 2 bars); OR (close stochastic RSI crosses above 90 and its 8-bar exponential moving average is below 85); OR (3-bar exponential moving average of signal direction is below 50 and current signal direction is 100); OR (4-bar exponential moving average of close stochastic RSI and each of its prior 2 values are below 19, prior signal direction is 0, and current signal direction is 100); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is below 19, prior signal direction is 0, and current signal direction is 100); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is below 81, signal direction 2 bars ago is 0, and current and prior signal direction are 100); OR (prior signal direction is 0 and current signal direction is 100); OR (signal direction 2 bars ago is 0 and current and prior signal direction are 100); OR (any of close stochastic RSI values 3, 2, or 1 bars ago is at least the configured long pivot, prior close stochastic RSI is below current, and any of values 2, 1, or current is at least the configured long-entry threshold); OR (close stochastic RSI 3 bars ago is at least the configured long pivot, prior close stochastic RSI is below current, and any of values 2, 1, or current is at least the configured long-entry threshold); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago crosses above its 2-bars-ago trail EMA, current close stochastic RSI is at least the configured long-entry threshold and above its value 2 bars ago); OR (bullish RSI divergence); OR (analysis-timeframe RSI is below 30); OR (uptrend and analysis-timeframe RSI is below 50); OR (uptrend, analysis-timeframe RSI is below 50, and RSI crosses above 50); OR (analysis-timeframe RSI crosses above its 10-bar simple moving average); OR (daily RSI using the configured RSI length is above 50 and analysis-timeframe RSI crosses above 50).
Short: During the configured trade window and (volume bias is disabled or volume exceeds its configured moving average and close stochastic RSI is below its 13-bar exponential moving average), any selected entry rule is true: (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is above 10 and above its value 1 bar ago, which is above its current value, and signal direction is 0 now and on each of the prior 2 bars); OR (close stochastic RSI crosses below 10 and its 8-bar exponential moving average is above 15); OR (3-bar exponential moving average of signal direction is above 50 and current signal direction is 0); OR (4-bar exponential moving average of close stochastic RSI and each of its prior 2 values are above 81, prior signal direction is 100, and current signal direction is 0); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is above 81, prior signal direction is 100, and current signal direction is 0); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago is above 19, signal direction 2 bars ago is 100, and current and prior signal direction are 0); OR (prior signal direction is 100 and current signal direction is 0); OR (signal direction 2 bars ago is 100 and current and prior signal direction are 0); OR (any of close stochastic RSI values 3, 2, or 1 bars ago is at most the configured short pivot, prior close stochastic RSI is above current, and any of values 2, 1, or current is at most the configured short-entry threshold); OR (close stochastic RSI 3 bars ago is at most the configured short pivot, prior close stochastic RSI is above current, and any of values 2, 1, or current is at most the configured short-entry threshold); OR (close stochastic RSI 2 bars ago crosses below its 2-bars-ago trail EMA, current close stochastic RSI is at most the configured short-entry threshold and below its value 2 bars ago); OR (bearish RSI divergence); OR (analysis-timeframe RSI is above 70); OR (downtrend and analysis-timeframe RSI is above 50); OR (downtrend, analysis-timeframe RSI is above 50, and RSI crosses below 50); OR (analysis-timeframe RSI crosses below its 10-bar simple moving average); OR (daily RSI using the configured RSI length is below 50 and analysis-timeframe RSI crosses below 50).
Exit: Long, current position is long and two consecutive signal directions are 0; OR current position is long and the low reaches or falls below the trailing stop, which is the greatest of the prior trailing stop and close multiplied by one minus the configured trail percentage; OR current position is long and close is at or below the entry-price-based stop multiplied by one plus the configured proximity threshold, or close is at or above the entry-price-based target multiplied by one minus that threshold; OR current position is long and its size was zero the configured number of bars ago; OR end-of-day closing is enabled and current time is from the configured close time through the configured reopen time.
Exit: Short, current position is short and two consecutive signal directions are 100; OR current position is short and the high reaches or exceeds the trailing stop, which is the least of the prior trailing stop and close multiplied by one plus the configured trail percentage; OR current position is short and close is at or above the entry-price-based stop multiplied by one minus the configured proximity threshold, or close is at or below the entry-price-based target multiplied by one plus that threshold; OR current position is short and its size was zero the configured number of bars ago; OR end-of-day closing is enabled and current time is from the configured close time through the configured reopen time.
