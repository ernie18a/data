Calculation: MACD signal line = simple moving average of the MACD line; the MACD line is the short-lookback EMA of close minus the longer-lookback EMA of close
Calculation: VPT-derived smoothed price line = simple moving average of out, where spread volume = (close − open) × volume / (high − low), cumulative VPT = cumulative sum of spread volume, smoothed VPT = simple moving average of cumulative VPT, shadow = (cumulative VPT − smoothed VPT) / standard deviation(cumulative VPT − smoothed VPT) × standard deviation(high − low), and out = high + shadow when shadow > 0, otherwise low + shadow
Calculation: Second upper envelope = simple moving average of close + ATR × second Fibonacci ratio; second lower envelope = simple moving average of close − ATR × second Fibonacci ratio. The moving average and ATR use the same lookback.
Long: RSI > overbought threshold AND MACD line > MACD signal line AND prior qualifying signal was short OR VPT-derived smoothed price line crosses above the second lower envelope OR previous-bar range > ATR × reversal multiplier AND previous-bar body / range is between the minimum and maximum body percentages AND previous bar closed below its open AND current bar closes above its open OR previous-bar range > ATR × outside-reversal multiplier AND current low < previous low AND current close > previous high
Short: RSI < oversold threshold AND MACD line < MACD signal line AND prior qualifying signal was long OR VPT-derived smoothed price line crosses below the second upper envelope OR previous-bar range > ATR × reversal multiplier AND previous-bar body / range is between the minimum and maximum body percentages AND previous bar closed above its open AND current bar closes below its open OR previous-bar range > ATR × outside-reversal multiplier AND current high > previous high AND current close < previous low
