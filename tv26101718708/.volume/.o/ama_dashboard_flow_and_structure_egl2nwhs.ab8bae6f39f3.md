Calculation: AMA(price, length) = Kaufman adaptive moving average of HLC3: efficiency ratio = |price − price[length]| / sum(length bars of |price − prior price|); smoothing constant = (ER × (2/(fast end+1) − 2/(slow end+1)) + 2/(slow end+1))²; recursively update prior AMA by smoothing constant × (price − prior AMA). The two AMAs use the same fast/slow parameters and different lengths; one is shorter and one longer.
Calculation: Trend direction = bullish when close is above both AMAs, bearish when below both, otherwise neutral; usable only after both AMA windows fill.
Calculation: Trend angle = arctan(((HLC3 − HLC3[lookback]) / lookback) / ATR) in degrees, using ATR(14). Strength grade is based on angle versus the trend direction and prior absolute angle: Counter if direction × angle ≤ 0; otherwise Weak, Improving Weak, Moderate, Improving Moderate, Strong, or Very Strong according to configured angle thresholds and whether absolute angle is rising. Map these grades to 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3.5 respectively; Counter maps to 0.
Calculation: Momentum grade = compare the trend-direction-adjusted angle change over the momentum period with its prior-bar value and with the fraction of bars whose one-bar angle change agrees with the trend. Classify as Strongly Strengthening, Strengthening, Weakly Strengthening, Stable, Weakly Weakening, Weakening, or Strongly Weakening using configured consistency threshold and change magnitudes; map these grades to 3.5, 2.5, 2, 1.5, 1, 0.5, 0 respectively.
Calculation: Flow grade = net volume share from the configured lookback, where each bar's volume is weighted by 2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1 (use 0.5 when high = low); classify buying/selling using the sign of net share and whether close is above/below VWAP(HLC3), with strong buying/selling when the close location is above 0.8/below 0.2. Score flow as configured: aligned flow gets 2 or 3 points, opposing flow 0.5, neutral 1; add 0.5 or 1 for aligned flow according to whether absolute net share × capped relative volume exceeds its configured threshold. Omit flow score and flow weight if no positive volume has occurred.
Calculation: Grade index = first matching tier 0–4 for normalized score ≥ configured thresholds, where score is weighted strength grade + weighted momentum grade + configured ROC bonus when ROC sign agrees with trend + flow score; normalize by the maximum possible score using the same weights. Index tiers in descending grade order A++, A+, A, B+, B. Ready only when both AMAs are warm, direction is non-neutral, and angle, momentum lookback, ATR, and volatility ratio are available. Volatility ratio = ATR(configured length) / SMA of that ATR over the same length.
Calculation: Distance = |close − shorter AMA| / ATR(14). Setup location is AT THE AMA when distance ≤ configured near threshold × volatility ratio, STRETCHED when above that threshold and ≤ configured far threshold × volatility ratio.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low from the swing-length bars ago only when it is strictly greater/less than every other high/low in the surrounding 2 × swing-length bars. Track the most recent confirmed high and low, whether each has been broken by a close beyond it, structure state (initially neutral), and protected high/low. In neutral state, a close above an unbroken last swing high with a last swing low switches state bullish and protects that low; a close below an unbroken last swing low with a last swing high switches state bearish and protects that high. In bullish state, a close below the protected low with a last swing high switches state bearish and protects that high; otherwise a close above an unbroken last swing high marks it broken and updates the protected low to the last swing low. In bearish state, a close above the protected high with a last swing low switches state bullish and protects that low; otherwise a close below an unbroken last swing low marks it broken and updates the protected high to the last swing high.
Long: Ready AND trend direction is bullish AND ((distance ≤ configured near threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured 1-ATR limit AND grade index ≤ 2) OR (distance > configured near threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured far threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured 1.5-ATR limit AND grade index ≤ 3)) OR structure state switches to bullish.
Short: Ready AND trend direction is bearish AND ((distance ≤ configured near threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured 1-ATR limit AND grade index ≤ 2) OR (distance > configured near threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured far threshold × volatility ratio AND distance ≤ configured 1.5-ATR limit AND grade index ≤ 3)) OR structure state switches to bearish.
