Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = bar high (swing high) or low (swing low) that is the extreme over the pivot lookback on both sides; recognize it only after the bars on its right have completed. Keep alternating pivots, replacing the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type; use the latest three as A, B, C.
Calculation: BC/AB retracement ratio = absolute(C − B) / absolute(B − A); it matches when within the configured tolerance of any enabled 0.382, 0.500, 0.618, or 0.786 level.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Long: A is a swing low, B a swing high, and C a swing low; a new confirmed pivot completes C; BC/AB matches an enabled Fibonacci level; volume ≥ volume average × configured multiplier; signal bar closes above its open and above the midpoint of its high and low; enough configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior signal; bar is closed. A pending setup from this pattern may instead signal within the next 5 bars when volume, bullish candle, cooldown, and bar-closed conditions hold.
Short: A is a swing high, B a swing low, and C a swing high; a new confirmed pivot completes C; BC/AB matches an enabled Fibonacci level; volume ≥ volume average × configured multiplier; signal bar closes below its open and below the midpoint of its high and low; enough configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior signal; bar is closed. A pending setup from this pattern may instead signal within the next 5 bars when volume, bearish candle, cooldown, and bar-closed conditions hold.
