Calculation: VWAP slope = percentage change in session VWAP over a lookback; rising means above a positive threshold, falling means below the corresponding negative threshold, and flat means between those thresholds
Long: close crosses above the previous completed day's high (previous close <= that high) OR close crosses above the previous completed day's low (previous close <= that low) OR close crosses above the previous completed day's low within the sweep window after closing below it OR the prior N bars each closed below session VWAP and current close > session VWAP AND close > open AND close < previous completed day's high OR close > previous completed day's high AND session VWAP slope is rising AND close > session VWAP
Short: high >= previous completed day's high AND close < that high AND previous bar's high < that high OR close crosses below the previous completed day's low (previous close >= that low) OR close crosses below the previous completed day's high within the sweep window after closing above it OR the prior N bars each closed above session VWAP and current close < session VWAP AND close < open AND close > previous completed day's low OR close < previous completed day's low AND session VWAP slope is falling AND close < session VWAP
Exit: long, close > previous completed day's high AND session VWAP slope is flat or falling
