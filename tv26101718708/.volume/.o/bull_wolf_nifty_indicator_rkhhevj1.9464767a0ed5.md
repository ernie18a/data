Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted mean of hlc3 over bars in the current session, including the current bar
Calculation: Session volume-weighted standard deviation = square root of the cumulative volume-weighted mean of hlc3 squared minus Session VWAP squared, over the same bars
Long: within the trading window AND not in post-stop cooldown AND Session volume-weighted standard deviation > 0 AND stop level (Session VWAP − stop-distance multiplier × Session volume-weighted standard deviation) < entry limit (Session VWAP − 3 × Session volume-weighted standard deviation + entry tolerance) < target (Session VWAP − entry tolerance) AND risk-sized quantity rounded down to the quantity increment > 0 AND low ≤ entry limit
Short: within the trading window AND not in post-stop cooldown AND Session volume-weighted standard deviation > 0 AND target (Session VWAP + entry tolerance) < entry limit (Session VWAP + 3 × Session volume-weighted standard deviation − entry tolerance) < stop level (Session VWAP + stop-distance multiplier × Session volume-weighted standard deviation) AND risk-sized quantity rounded down to the quantity increment > 0 AND high ≥ entry limit
Exit: long, high ≥ current target (Session VWAP − entry tolerance) OR low ≤ stop level fixed when the entry order was submitted OR force-exit cutoff reached OR position carried over from a prior session
Exit: short, low ≤ current target (Session VWAP + entry tolerance) OR high ≥ stop level fixed when the entry order was submitted OR force-exit cutoff reached OR position carried over from a prior session
