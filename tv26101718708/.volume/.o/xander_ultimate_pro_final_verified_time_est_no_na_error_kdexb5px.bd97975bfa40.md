Calculation: Fast, slow, and major EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback, and the major lookback longer than both.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K and D = normalize RSI within its own lookback range to 0–100; K is the short-lookback simple moving average of that value, and D is the simple moving average of K. RSI and its normalization use the same lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = latest pivot high/low on current-timeframe bars, using equal left and right lookbacks; use a longer lookback on daily-and-higher charts than on lower-timeframe charts.
Calculation: Adaptive tolerance = current close multiplied by a smaller percentage on lower-timeframe charts and a larger percentage on daily-and-higher charts.
Long: Fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > major EMA AND (bar range intersects the range between fast and slow EMAs OR low is within adaptive tolerance of the latest confirmed swing low OR close <= latest confirmed swing low + 2 × adaptive tolerance) AND Stochastic RSI K crosses above D while K is below the oversold threshold AND volume > its simple moving average.
Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < major EMA AND (bar range intersects the range between fast and slow EMAs OR high is within adaptive tolerance of the latest confirmed swing high OR close >= latest confirmed swing high − 2 × adaptive tolerance) AND Stochastic RSI K crosses below D while K is above the overbought threshold AND volume > its simple moving average.
