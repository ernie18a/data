Calculation: Trend score = +1 for fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, +1 when fast EMA rises for two consecutive bars, +1 when medium EMA rises, and +1 when close is above anchor EMA; use -1 for each corresponding bearish condition and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Bull/bear confluence score = weighted sum of trend strength, RSI pullback or recovery, stochastic crossover or exit from an extreme zone, MACD alignment, volume quality, directional candle and body strength, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, and session quality, minus opposing RSI, low-volatility, Asia-session, EMA-extension, and aged-trend penalties; clamp to 0–100. Higher-timeframe alignment uses fast and slow EMAs with lookbacks five times their respective trend-engine lookbacks, calculated on the chart bars.
Calculation: ATR-based stop, targets, and trailing stop = from entry price, place the stop one ATR multiple against the position and targets at configured ATR multiples in its favor; trail at close minus an ATR multiple for long positions or close plus an ATR multiple for short positions, ratcheting only in the favorable direction.
Long: (RSI crosses above its bull pullback threshold OR stochastic %K crosses above %D below its overbought threshold OR %K rises from below to above its oversold threshold) AND close > open AND trend score is positive AND bull confluence score meets the minimum signal tier AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous long signal AND (volume filter disabled OR volume / 20-bar volume SMA meets its minimum) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR as a percentage of close meets its minimum) AND (candle-strength filter disabled OR absolute close-open / (high-low) meets its minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR current UTC hour is in the London or New York window) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line > signal line).
Short: (RSI crosses below its bear pullback threshold OR stochastic %K crosses below %D above its oversold threshold OR %K falls from above to below its overbought threshold) AND close < open AND trend score is negative AND bear confluence score meets the minimum signal tier AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous short signal AND (volume filter disabled OR volume / 20-bar volume SMA meets its minimum) AND (ATR filter disabled OR ATR as a percentage of close meets its minimum) AND (candle-strength filter disabled OR absolute close-open / (high-low) meets its minimum) AND (session filter disabled OR current UTC hour is in the London or New York window) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line < signal line).
Exit: long, high reaches or exceeds any profit target OR low reaches or falls below the stop loss OR low falls below the ratcheting trailing stop.
Exit: short, low reaches or falls below any profit target OR high reaches or exceeds the stop loss OR high rises above the ratcheting trailing stop.
