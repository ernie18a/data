Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed pivot low, the current low is lower than the previous pivot low and the oscillator is higher than at that previous pivot low; bearish divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, the current high is higher and the oscillator is lower. Evaluate RSI, MACD histogram, CCI, MFI, and OBV at the pivot bars.
Calculation: Divergence stack = count of these five oscillators whose latest same-direction divergence occurred at the current pivot or within the stack window; use the exact-pivot count if it reaches the minimum stack, otherwise use the window count if enabled and it reaches the minimum.
Calculation: Readiness score = clamp(round(0.34 × 20 × stack + 0.20 × normalized swing size in ATR + 0.15 × trend score + 0.20 × confirmation score + 0.11 × (100 − normalized distance from pivot in ATR) + sensitivity adjustment), 0, 100). Normalize swing size between its lower and upper thresholds and distance between its lower and upper thresholds, clamping each normalized value to 0–1. Trend score depends on the relative position of the fast and slow EMAs and close; confirmation score depends on confirmation mode.
Calculation: Long invalidation = current pivot low − 0.18 × ATR at the pivot; short invalidation = current pivot high + 0.18 × ATR at the pivot. Long target = the latest pivot high if it is above close, otherwise close + max(1.35, 1.10 × swing size in ATR) × ATR at the pivot. Short target = the latest pivot low if it is below close, otherwise close − max(1.35, 1.10 × swing size in ATR) × ATR at the pivot.
Long: Confirmed pivot low AND bullish divergence stack >= minimum stack AND readiness score >= watch threshold.
Short: Confirmed pivot high AND bearish divergence stack >= minimum stack AND readiness score >= watch threshold.
Exit: Long, low <= active long invalidation OR high >= active long target.
Exit: Short, high >= active short invalidation OR low <= active short target.
