Calculation: Volume divergence up = close > close 3 bars ago AND mean volume over the current and prior 2 bars < 0.8 × volume moving average; volume divergence down = close < close 3 bars ago AND the same volume condition
Calculation: Volume trend up = volume is greater than each of the preceding configured number of bars minus one; volume trend down = volume is less than each of them
Calculation: Strong volume = volume > volume moving average × (normal volume-spike multiplier × 1.3); extreme volume = volume > volume moving average × (normal volume-spike multiplier × 2); volume spike = volume > volume moving average × normal volume-spike multiplier
Calculation: Uptrend = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend-filter EMA AND SuperTrend direction is bullish; downtrend = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend-filter EMA AND SuperTrend direction is bearish
Calculation: Sideways = Bollinger Band width as a percentage of its basis < 3% OR (sideways filter enabled AND ADX <= its threshold)
Calculation: Supply zones = confirmed pivot highs; demand zones = confirmed pivot lows. Keep zones within the configured persistence period; use the nearest valid zone and its test count. At-zone means close is within the configured tolerance percentage of that zone and its test count meets the configured strength. Near-zone means close is within 1.5 times that tolerance below to 0.5 times that tolerance above the zone.
Calculation: Institutional buy = bullish candle body > ATR × big-candle multiplier AND strong volume AND (at or near a demand zone); institutional sell = bearish candle body > ATR × big-candle multiplier AND strong volume AND (at or near a supply zone)
Calculation: V-shape recovery = close is down more than the configured recovery percentage from the highest high over the configured lookback AND up more than 0.6 times that percentage from the lowest low over that lookback AND RSI is between extreme oversold and 40 AND strong volume AND volume trend up AND close > prior close AND no upward volume divergence
Calculation: Inverse V-shape decline = close is up more than the configured recovery percentage from the lowest low over the configured lookback AND down more than 0.6 times that percentage from the highest high over that lookback AND RSI is between 60 and extreme overbought AND strong volume AND volume trend up AND close < prior close AND no downward volume divergence
Calculation: RSI recovery = RSI was below extreme oversold in any of the prior 3 bars AND RSI > prior RSI > RSI 2 bars ago AND strong volume AND close > prior close AND no upward volume divergence; RSI decline = RSI was above extreme overbought in any of the prior 3 bars AND RSI < prior RSI < RSI 2 bars ago AND strong volume AND close < prior close AND no downward volume divergence
Calculation: Bull score = 1 each for (uptrend), (close > fast EMA AND close > VWAP), (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA), (RSI > 50 AND RSI < overbought), (volume spike AND no upward divergence), (low > SuperTrend); plus 2 each for (institutional buy AND not sideways), ((V-shape recovery OR RSI recovery) AND not sideways)
Calculation: Bear score = 1 each for (downtrend), (close < fast EMA AND close < VWAP), (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA), (RSI < 50 AND RSI > oversold), (volume spike AND no downward divergence), (high < SuperTrend); plus 2 each for (institutional sell AND not sideways), ((inverse V-shape decline OR RSI decline) AND not sideways)
Calculation: Regular long condition = bull score >= minimum score AND bear score < 3 AND minimum bars since last signal have passed AND allowed trading time AND fast EMA rising AND close > prior close AND not sideways AND no upward volume divergence AND (not counter-trend OR volume > volume moving average × counter-trend multiplier AND volume trend up), where counter-trend means downtrend
Calculation: Regular short condition = bear score >= minimum score AND bull score < 3 AND minimum bars since last signal have passed AND allowed trading time AND fast EMA falling AND close < prior close AND not sideways AND no downward volume divergence AND (not counter-trend OR volume > volume moving average × counter-trend multiplier AND volume trend up), where counter-trend means uptrend
Calculation: Early long condition = early entry enabled AND (institutional buy OR ((V-shape recovery OR RSI recovery) AND not sideways)) AND fast EMA rising AND close > prior close AND close > open AND strong volume AND no upward volume divergence AND not sideways AND allowed trading time AND last direction is not long AND at least 2 bars since last signal AND (not counter-trend OR extreme volume), where counter-trend means downtrend
Calculation: Early short condition = early entry enabled AND (institutional sell OR ((inverse V-shape decline OR RSI decline) AND not sideways)) AND fast EMA falling AND close < prior close AND close < open AND strong volume AND no downward volume divergence AND not sideways AND allowed trading time AND last direction is not short AND at least 2 bars since last signal AND (not counter-trend OR extreme volume), where counter-trend means uptrend
