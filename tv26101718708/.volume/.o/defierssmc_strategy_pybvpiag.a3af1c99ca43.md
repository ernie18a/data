Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Local trend = bullish after a bullish structure break and bearish after a bearish structure break; otherwise retain the prior state. A bullish structure break is a close above the latest swing high when local trend is neutral or bearish; bearish is a close below the latest swing low when trend is neutral or bullish. A bullish change of character is a close above the latest lower high when trend is bearish; bearish is a close below the latest higher low when trend is bullish. A market structure shift is a change of character whose candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = high above an unswept clustered swing-high level and close below it (buy-side sweep), or low below an unswept clustered swing-low level and close above it (sell-side sweep). Cluster same-kind swing levels within the configured tolerance.
Calculation: Active bullish/bearish order block = the most recent qualifying opposite-color candle found within the configured lookback after a same-direction structure break; its volume divided by the 20-bar average volume meets the configured minimum. It is invalid after its maximum age or once a close enters its price range.
Calculation: Active fair value gap = bullish when current low is above the high two bars earlier, or bearish when current high is below the low two bars earlier; the gap size meets the configured ATR multiple. It remains active until fully filled or past its maximum age.
Calculation: Active point of interest = price range intersects an active same-direction order block, an unfilled same-direction fair value gap, or the OTE zone between the 62% and 79% retracement levels of the range between the latest swing high and low. The retracement orientation follows local trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = close above/below the 50-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; neutral when equal or unavailable.
Calculation: Long/short score = 100 × (0.25 × higher-timeframe alignment × session weight + 0.20 × recent aligned local structure + 0.20 × recent aligned liquidity sweep + 0.20 × active aligned point of interest + 0.15 × correct premium/discount location). Session weight is 1 in configured active sessions; outside them it is 1, 0.7, or blocks entry according to the selected session mode. Recent structure and sweep use their respective configured recency windows. Long location is close at or below the midpoint of the latest swing range; short location is close at or above it. Count an active point of interest when its score is at least 0.5; count each other active factor when its score is 1.
Calculation: Effective entry threshold = configured threshold adjusted by volatility regime: add the configured adjustment when ATR divided by its 100-bar average exceeds the high-regime limit; subtract it when below the low-regime limit; otherwise leave unchanged.
Calculation: Long stop = latest swing low minus the configured ATR buffer, or close minus 2 ATR if unavailable. Short stop = latest swing high plus the buffer, or close plus 2 ATR if unavailable. Risk distance is the larger of distance from close to stop and 0.1 ATR. TP1 is close plus (long) or minus (short) configured TP1 risk multiple times risk distance. TP2 is the nearest unswept opposite-side liquidity level beyond TP1 when enabled and available; otherwise it is close plus (long) or minus (short) configured TP2 risk multiple times risk distance.
Calculation: Long/short potential reward-to-risk = distance from close to TP2 divided by risk distance.
Long: confirmed bar AND flat position AND long trades allowed AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish when that filter is enabled AND session is not blocked AND long score ≥ effective entry threshold AND at least the configured number of long factors are active AND all enabled mandatory long factors are active AND long potential reward-to-risk ≥ configured TP1 risk multiple.
Short: confirmed bar AND flat position AND short trades allowed AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish when that filter is enabled AND session is not blocked AND short score ≥ effective entry threshold AND at least the configured number of short factors are active AND all enabled mandatory short factors are active AND short potential reward-to-risk ≥ configured TP1 risk multiple.
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish change of character (including market structure shift) OR sell-side liquidity sweep.
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish change of character (including market structure shift) OR buy-side liquidity sweep.
Exit: long, low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ TP1 OR high ≥ TP2.
Exit: short, high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ TP1 OR low ≤ TP2.
