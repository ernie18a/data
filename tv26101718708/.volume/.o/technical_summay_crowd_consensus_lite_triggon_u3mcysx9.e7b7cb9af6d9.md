Calculation: Oscillator votes = one vote per enabled indicator: +1 bullish, −1 bearish, 0 neutral. RSI: bullish below its midpoint unless overbought; bearish above its midpoint unless oversold; neutral at midpoint. Stochastic and Stochastic RSI: bullish when %K is above %D unless %K is overbought; bearish when %K is below %D unless %K is oversold; neutral when equal. CCI: bullish above zero, bearish below zero (extreme positive/negative readings retain that direction). MACD: bullish when MACD line is above signal line; bearish when below; neutral when equal. Awesome Oscillator: bullish above zero; bearish below. Momentum: bullish when current close exceeds its earlier close; bearish when below. Williams %R: bullish above its midpoint unless overbought; bearish below its midpoint unless oversold. Ultimate Oscillator: bullish above its midpoint unless overbought; bearish below its midpoint unless oversold. Bull/Bear Power: bullish when close exceeds its EMA; bearish when below.
Calculation: Moving-average votes = one vote per enabled average: +1 when close is above it, −1 when below, 0 when equal. Includes EMA and SMA at five successively longer lookbacks, VWMA, and HMA.
Calculation: Total buy votes = bullish oscillator votes plus bullish moving-average votes; total sell votes = bearish oscillator votes plus bearish moving-average votes; total votes = all enabled votes, including neutral votes.
Calculation: Consensus = greater of total buy votes and total sell votes divided by total votes; dominant side is bullish when buy votes are greater than or equal to sell votes, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Raw bias = neutral when consensus is below the neutral threshold; otherwise the dominant side. The daily phase resets to undecided at each new day on intraday bars; on each confirmed bar, it adopts the raw bias if non-neutral and changes only when that bias opposes the existing phase.
Calculation: EMA alignment = the entire bar is above the EMA when low is above it; the entire bar is below when high is below it. EMA source and lookback are configurable.
Long: On a confirmed intraday bar, total buy votes are greater than or equal to total sell votes, consensus is at or above the neutral threshold, and the daily phase changes from bearish to bullish; mark the bullish phase flip regardless of trade filters. Enter long only if consensus meets the configured at-least or at-most threshold and, when the EMA filter is enabled, low is above the EMA.
Short: On a confirmed intraday bar, total sell votes exceed total buy votes, consensus is at or above the neutral threshold, and the daily phase changes from bullish to bearish; mark the bearish phase flip regardless of trade filters. Enter short only if consensus meets the configured at-least or at-most threshold and, when the EMA filter is enabled, high is below the EMA.
Exit: Long, a short entry signal. Exit: Short, a long entry signal.
