Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close; use the same lookback for all RSI calculations.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = RSI is below its oversold threshold, low is below the low stored at the previous oversold RSI maximum, and RSI is above the RSI stored there; update the stored low and RSI when an oversold RSI is lower, and expire the record after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = RSI is above its overbought threshold, high is above the high stored at the previous overbought RSI maximum, and RSI is below the RSI stored there; update the stored high and RSI when an overbought RSI is higher, and expire the record after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish RSI divergence = bullish RSI divergence and its event count over the divergence lookback is at least the required event count.
Calculation: Confirmed bearish RSI divergence = bearish RSI divergence and its event count over the divergence lookback is at least the required event count.
Calculation: MFI = calculate typical price as (high + low + close) / 3 and raw money flow as typical price × volume; over the MFI lookback, sum raw money flow when typical price rises and when it falls, then compute 100 − 100 / (1 + positive sum / negative sum), or 100 when the negative sum is zero.
Calculation: Bullish MFI divergence = MFI is below its oversold threshold, low is below the low stored at the previous oversold MFI maximum, and MFI is above the MFI stored there; update the stored low and MFI when an oversold MFI is lower, and expire the record after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bearish MFI divergence = MFI is above its overbought threshold, high is above the high stored at the previous overbought MFI maximum, and MFI is below the MFI stored there; update the stored high and MFI when an overbought MFI is higher, and expire the record after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = any of: bullish engulfing (bullish bar, previous bar bearish, current close above previous open, current open below previous close, current body larger than previous body); bullish pin bar (lower wick ≥ 2 bodies, upper wick ≤ 1.2 bodies, close above low + 60% of bar range); hammer (previous close < close five bars earlier, lower wick ≥ 2 bodies, upper wick ≤ 0.8 bodies, close above low + 60% of bar range); morning star (two bars earlier bearish body > 0.8 × its 20-bar average body, prior body < 0.55 × that body, current bar bullish and closes above the midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body); inside-bar upside break (previous bar lies inside the bar before it and current close > previous high); bullish outside bar (current high and low exceed previous range and current body > previous body); or bullish marubozu (body ≥ 75% of range, each wick ≤ 12% of range, bullish bar).
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = any of: bearish engulfing (bearish bar, previous bar bullish, current close below previous open, current open above previous close, current body larger than previous body); bearish pin bar (upper wick ≥ 2 bodies, lower wick ≤ 1.2 bodies, close below high − 60% of bar range); shooting star (previous close > close five bars earlier, upper wick ≥ 2 bodies, lower wick ≤ 0.8 bodies, close below high − 60% of bar range); evening star (two bars earlier bullish body > 0.8 × its 20-bar average body, prior body < 0.55 × that body, current bar bearish and closes below the midpoint of the two-bars-earlier body); inside-bar downside break (previous bar lies inside the bar before it and current close < previous low); bearish outside bar (current high and low exceed previous range and current body > previous body); or bearish marubozu (body ≥ 75% of range, each wick ≤ 12% of range, bearish bar).
Calculation: Bullish GEM = within the confirmation window, RSI was oversold, bullish RSI divergence occurred, and a bullish candle pattern occurred; signal only when this combined condition becomes true.
Calculation: Bearish GEM = within the confirmation window, RSI was overbought, bearish RSI divergence occurred, and a bearish candle pattern occurred; signal only when this combined condition becomes true.
Calculation: Long entry trigger = after a confirmed bullish RSI divergence setup, within its signal lifetime, current close > setup-bar high; cancel the setup if a subsequent bar low < setup-bar low.
Calculation: Short entry trigger = after a confirmed bearish RSI divergence setup, within its signal lifetime, current close < setup-bar low; cancel the setup if a subsequent bar high > setup-bar high.
Long: Confirmed bullish RSI divergence OR long entry trigger OR bullish MFI divergence OR bullish candle pattern OR bullish GEM
Short: Confirmed bearish RSI divergence OR short entry trigger OR bearish MFI divergence OR bearish candle pattern OR bearish GEM
