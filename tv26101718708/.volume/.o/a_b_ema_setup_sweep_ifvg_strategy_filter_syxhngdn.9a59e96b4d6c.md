Calculation: Local bullish trend = close > fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA; local bearish trend = close < fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish/bearish trend = last completed higher-timeframe close and fast, medium, and slow EMAs strictly ordered bullish/bearish.
Calculation: Intermediate-timeframe bullish/bearish trend = last completed intermediate-timeframe close and fast, medium, and slow EMAs strictly ordered bullish/bearish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = price breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low/high and closes back above/below it; recent means within the sweep-valid lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest confirmed pivot high/below the latest confirmed pivot low; recent means within the structure-valid lookback. A change-of-character is such a break against the prior break direction. Internal shift uses a shorter pivot lookback than the structure break.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = a bullish/bearish candle body larger than an ATR-scaled threshold; recent means within the momentum-valid lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confirmed momentum = recent bullish/bearish displacement, close above/below the fast EMA, and the fast EMA higher/lower than its value several bars earlier.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish EMA expansion = bullish/bearish local trend and the fast-to-slow EMA spread greater than it was several bars earlier.
Calculation: Accepted above/below fast EMA = three consecutive closes above/below it.
Calculation: Rejection at lower/higher fast EMA = low below it and close above it, with lower wick greater than an ATR-scaled threshold / high above it and close below it, with upper wick greater than an ATR-scaled threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish IFVG = a close crosses above the top of the latest bearish fair-value gap / below the bottom of the latest bullish fair-value gap; the gap is formed by a three-bar price gap exceeding an ATR-scaled threshold. Recent means within the IFVG-valid lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CISD = after a bullish/bearish sweep, a later close within the CISD-valid lookback exceeds the sweep bar high/falls below its low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence warning = within the divergence-valid lookback, confirmed price pivot highs make a higher high while RSI pivot highs make a lower high / price pivot lows make a lower low while RSI pivot lows make a higher low.
Calculation: Distance allowed = close is within an ATR-scaled distance of the fast EMA, or the distance filter is disabled.
Calculation: Matrix bullish/bearish support = on the matrix confirmation timeframe, the bullish/bearish share of directional votes meets the support threshold. Votes compare EMA pairs, RSI, MACD and signal, MACD histogram, directional indicators, MFI, OBV change, and Supertrend direction. Matrix strong/weak trend = ADX is above/below its respective threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish probability = the normalized share of bullish/bearish points. Points are awarded or deducted for the corresponding local, higher-timeframe, and intermediate-timeframe trend; recent sweep, structure break, change-of-character, internal shift, and displacement; confirmed or weak momentum; opposing divergence; recent IFVG and fib-zone touch; acceptance or rejection; EMA expansion or compression; proximity to the opposing liquidity extreme; session; and aligned/opposing matrix support and trend strength.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish A+ score = capped 0–100 score from intermediate-timeframe trend, recent same-direction sweep, recent structure break/change-of-character/internal shift, EMA expansion or confirmed momentum, acceptance or rejection, recent displacement or confirmed momentum, EMA retest held, and aligned/opposing matrix support and trend strength. Cap at 70 if a required same-direction sweep is absent; cap at 85 for opposing divergence or excessive EMA distance.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish EMA retest held = within the retest-valid lookback after a close crossing above/below the fast EMA, low touches/breaches the EMA and the bar closes above it with close >= open / high touches/breaches the EMA and the bar closes below it with close <= open.
Long: (bullish probability >= scalp threshold AND < forming threshold AND NOT bearish EMA expansion AND NOT accepted below fast EMA) OR (bullish probability >= forming threshold AND < ready threshold AND NOT bearish EMA expansion) OR (bullish probability >= ready threshold AND < now threshold AND a recent bullish structure break, change-of-character, or internal shift AND NOT accepted below fast EMA) OR (bullish probability >= now threshold AND distance allowed AND accepted above fast EMA AND recent bullish structure break AND (confirmed bullish momentum OR recent bullish displacement with bullish matrix support) AND (IFVG/CISD filter disabled OR recent bullish IFVG/CISD) AND no bearish divergence warning AND bullish probability exceeds bearish probability by the final edge) OR (bullish A+ score >= forming threshold AND < ready threshold AND exceeds bearish A+ score AND bullish EMA retest not held) OR (bullish A+ score >= ready threshold AND exceeds bearish A+ score AND bullish EMA retest held AND matrix requirement disabled OR bullish matrix support) OR (not bullish A+ ready/forming AND bullish probability >= 70 AND exceeds bearish probability by 10 AND recent bullish structure break/change-of-character/internal shift AND accepted above fast EMA AND (confirmed bullish momentum OR bullish EMA expansion OR bullish matrix support) AND no bearish divergence warning) OR (not bullish B+ nor bullish A+ ready/forming AND bullish probability >= 60 AND exceeds bearish probability AND NOT accepted below fast EMA AND no bearish divergence warning).
Short: (bearish probability >= scalp threshold AND < forming threshold AND NOT bullish EMA expansion AND NOT accepted above fast EMA) OR (bearish probability >= forming threshold AND < ready threshold AND NOT bullish EMA expansion) OR (bearish probability >= ready threshold AND < now threshold AND a recent bearish structure break, change-of-character, or internal shift AND NOT accepted above fast EMA) OR (bearish probability >= now threshold AND distance allowed AND accepted below fast EMA AND recent bearish structure break AND (confirmed bearish momentum OR recent bearish displacement with bearish matrix support) AND (IFVG/CISD filter disabled OR recent bearish IFVG/CISD) AND no bullish divergence warning AND bearish probability exceeds bullish probability by the final edge) OR (bearish A+ score >= forming threshold AND < ready threshold AND exceeds bullish A+ score AND bearish EMA retest not held) OR (bearish A+ score >= ready threshold AND exceeds bullish A+ score AND bearish EMA retest held AND matrix requirement disabled OR bearish matrix support) OR (not bearish A+ ready/forming AND bearish probability >= 70 AND exceeds bullish probability by 10 AND recent bearish structure break/change-of-character/internal shift AND accepted below fast EMA AND (confirmed bearish momentum OR bearish EMA expansion OR bearish matrix support) AND no bullish divergence warning) OR (not bearish B+ nor bearish A+ ready/forming AND bearish probability >= 60 AND exceeds bullish probability AND NOT accepted above fast EMA AND no bullish divergence warning).
