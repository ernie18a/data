Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides; use it only after the bars to its right have completed.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low. A bullish/bearish change of character is such a break when the prior structure bias is neutral or bearish / neutral or bullish.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish break = a bullish/bearish structure break occurred within the configured recent-bar window.
Calculation: Anchored profile POC, VAH, VAL = levels from the selected anchor window, calculated from its bars including the current bar; distribute each bar's volume uniformly across its high-low range, then take the highest-volume price bin as POC and expand value area from POC toward the adjacent higher-volume bin until the configured volume percentage is included. VA height = VAH − VAL.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND (if liquidity sweep is required: low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND close > open; otherwise: close > latest confirmed swing low AND close > open AND close > previous close) AND (if structure confirmation is required: a recent bullish break OR a bullish change of character) AND (if VA edge/POC testing is required: low is within the configured fraction of VA height of VAL or POC) AND (if EMA trend filtering is enabled: close > EMA).
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND no long signal fires AND (if liquidity sweep is required: high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND close < open; otherwise: close < latest confirmed swing high AND close < open AND close < previous close) AND (if structure confirmation is required: a recent bearish break OR a bearish change of character) AND (if VA edge/POC testing is required: high is within the configured fraction of VA height of VAH or POC) AND (if EMA trend filtering is enabled: close < EMA).
