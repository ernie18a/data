Calculation: Weighted bullish fraction = sum of weights for positive close changes over the lookback / sum of weights for all nonzero close changes; weight each change by 1 + (lookback − bars ago) / lookback, using prior completed chart bars
Calculation: Weighted bearish fraction = sum of weights for negative close changes over the lookback / sum of weights for all nonzero close changes; use the same weights and bars as the weighted bullish fraction
Calculation: Trend direction = 1 when weighted bullish fraction > weighted bearish fraction, −1 when weighted bearish fraction > weighted bullish fraction, otherwise 0
Calculation: Momentum = absolute difference between weighted bullish and bearish fractions
Calculation: Confidence = the greater of weighted bullish and bearish fractions
Calculation: Memory bias = 1 when the recursive decayed memory exceeds its positive threshold, −1 when it is below its negative threshold, otherwise 0; update memory each bar as prior memory × decay plus (0.18 + 0.30 × momentum) for upward trend, minus that amount for downward trend, or zero for neutral trend; clamp memory to [−1, 1]
Calculation: Preferred direction = trend direction when nonzero, otherwise memory bias
Calculation: Bullish sniper percentage = 100/7 × count of: close > VWAP; RSI > 50; MACD line > signal line; shorter EMA > longer EMA; ADX > threshold and close > shorter EMA; volume > volume SMA and close > open; higher-timeframe RSI > 50. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for its RSI.
Calculation: Bearish sniper percentage = 100/7 × count of the corresponding bearish conditions: close < VWAP; RSI < 50; MACD line < signal line; shorter EMA < longer EMA; ADX > threshold and close < shorter EMA; volume > volume SMA and close < open; higher-timeframe RSI < 50. Use the same higher-timeframe bar as for the bullish sniper percentage.
Calculation: Long score = 2 if preferred direction is 1, plus 1 if trend direction is 1, plus 1 if memory bias is 1, plus 1 if confidence meets its threshold, plus 1 if momentum meets its threshold, plus 1 if the enabled EMA filter passes (close above trend EMA and EMA rising), plus 1 if the enabled higher-timeframe filter passes (last completed higher-timeframe close above its trend EMA and that EMA rising), plus 1 if the enabled volume filter passes (volume above volume SMA), plus 1 if enabled ATR filter passes (ATR at least its SMA times a threshold), plus 1 if the enabled candle filter passes (bullish candle body/range meets a threshold), plus 2 if bullish sniper percentage > bearish sniper percentage, plus 1 if their difference meets the strong-bias threshold, plus 1 if close > VWAP, plus 1 if MACD line > signal line, plus 1 if ADX > threshold, plus 1 if last completed higher-timeframe RSI > 50
Calculation: Short score = 2 if preferred direction is −1, plus 1 if trend direction is −1, plus 1 if memory bias is −1, plus 1 if confidence meets its threshold, plus 1 if momentum meets its threshold, plus 1 if the enabled EMA filter passes (close below trend EMA and EMA falling), plus 1 if the enabled higher-timeframe filter passes (last completed higher-timeframe close below its trend EMA and that EMA falling), plus 1 if the enabled volume filter passes (volume above volume SMA), plus 1 if enabled ATR filter passes (ATR at least its SMA times a threshold), plus 1 if the enabled candle filter passes (bearish candle body/range meets a threshold), plus 2 if bearish sniper percentage > bullish sniper percentage, plus 1 if their difference meets the strong-bias threshold, plus 1 if close < VWAP, plus 1 if MACD line < signal line, plus 1 if ADX > threshold, plus 1 if last completed higher-timeframe RSI < 50
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR × stop multiplier, measured at entry; entry price is the position average fill
Calculation: Effective long stop = greater of entry price − initial risk and active trailing stop; trailing stop activates after the highest high since entry rises by the activation ATR distance from entry, then ratchets upward to the greater of its prior value and highest high since entry − ATR × trailing multiplier
Calculation: Effective short stop = lesser of entry price + initial risk and active trailing stop; trailing stop activates after the lowest low since entry falls by the activation ATR distance from entry, then ratchets downward to the lesser of its prior value and lowest low since entry + ATR × trailing multiplier
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price ± initial risk × target step × target number, adding for long and subtracting for short; target numbers are consecutive integers starting at 1
Long: confirmed bar AND flat position AND long entries allowed AND (session filter passes AND signal interval elapsed AND (last signal was not long OR close ≥ last long signal price + ATR × minimum move) AND (sniper bias filter disabled OR bullish sniper percentage ≥ minimum and > bearish percentage OR bullish-minus-bearish percentage difference ≥ strong-bias threshold) AND long score ≥ minimum score AND long score − short score ≥ minimum edge OR EMA short-term crosses above EMA long-term AND bullish sniper percentage ≥ minimum and > bearish percentage AND close > VWAP AND MACD line > signal line AND last completed higher-timeframe RSI > 50); if long and short signals both qualify, retain long when bullish sniper percentage ≥ bearish percentage
Short: confirmed bar AND flat position AND short entries allowed AND (session filter passes AND signal interval elapsed AND (last signal was not short OR close ≤ last short signal price − ATR × minimum move) AND (sniper bias filter disabled OR bearish sniper percentage ≥ minimum and > bullish percentage OR bearish-minus-bullish percentage difference ≥ strong-bias threshold) AND short score ≥ minimum score AND short score − long score ≥ minimum edge OR EMA short-term crosses below EMA long-term AND bearish sniper percentage ≥ minimum and > bullish percentage AND close < VWAP AND MACD line < signal line AND last completed higher-timeframe RSI < 50); if long and short signals both qualify, retain short when bearish sniper percentage > bullish sniper percentage
Exit: long, low ≤ effective long stop OR (take-profits enabled AND high ≥ any profit target) OR (confirmed bar AND minimum hold elapsed AND (early exit enabled AND (low-to-high? close crosses below exit EMA OR RSI crosses down through its overbought threshold OR bearish engulfing candle) AND (TP1 gate disabled OR TP1 reached) OR close-based opposite-signal exits enabled AND raw short signal)
Exit: short, high ≥ effective short stop OR (take-profits enabled AND low ≤ any profit target) OR (confirmed bar AND minimum hold elapsed AND (early exit enabled AND (close crosses above exit EMA OR RSI crosses up through its oversold threshold OR bullish engulfing candle) AND (TP1 gate disabled OR TP1 reached) OR close-based opposite-signal exits enabled AND raw long signal)
