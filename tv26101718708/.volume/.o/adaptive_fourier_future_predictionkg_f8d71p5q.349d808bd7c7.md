Calculation: Adaptive Fourier model = For each enabled lookback window, linearly detrend log close, compute its DFT, rank frequency bins by power × frequency^red-noise-compensation, and select the window with the highest score from normalized spectral energy, amplitude, stability, phase consistency, and directional agreement. Reconstruct log-price cycles from the selected window’s strongest harmonics and add its damped linear trend.
Calculation: Forecast move = reconstructed cycle plus damped trend change over the rounded dominant-cycle length divided by six; forecast direction is up/down when this move divided by ATR/close × square root of that horizon exceeds the positive/negative dead-zone, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Cycle phase = bullish near a trough (cosine < −0.8) or while rising away from one (−sine > 0.1); bearish near a peak (cosine > 0.8) or while falling away from one (−sine < −0.1).
Calculation: Cycle stability = weighted score of frequency, phase, amplitude, and energy consistency with the previous confirmed model, plus agreement among enabled windows whose dominant periods are within 30% of the selected period.
Calculation: Confidence = direction-specific weighted score of cycle stability, forecast direction, cycle amplitude relative to ATR, cycle phase, forecast quality, ZLEMA confirmation, volume confirmation, and room to nearest confirmed pivot support/resistance; apply the stability multiplier and optional higher-timeframe multiplier. Forecast quality combines forecast directional accuracy and normalized forecast error over a lookback, with reconstruction fit. Higher-timeframe direction uses the same six-bin Fourier projection on log close minus its 32-bar EMA; use only the last completed bar of each selected higher timeframe.
Calculation: Support/resistance = nearest confirmed pivot high above close and pivot low below close, using equal pivot lookbacks on both sides; retain the most recent 14 confirmed pivots. If none exists, use the script’s default room value (4 ATR upward, 2 ATR downward).
Calculation: ZLEMA = EMA(close + (close − close[lag]), length), where lag is floor((length − 1)/2). Volume class = bullish/bearish candle direction graded by current volume divided by its SMA; neutral when volume is unavailable or below the weak-volume threshold.
Calculation: Projected BUY level = projected dominant-cycle trough price when its trough is 1 to the adaptive forecast horizon bars ahead, adjusted to nearby support within 0.75 ATR; retain only if below close. Projected SELL level = corresponding projected peak price, adjusted to nearby resistance within 0.75 ATR; retain only if above close.
Calculation: Trade stop and targets = At entry close, set stop initially 1.5 ATR against the position, adjusted toward the nearest support (long) or resistance (short) when within the stated conditions and capped at 1.6 times the initial stop distance. Targets combine ATR multiples 1.5, 2.5, and 4 with the first projected favorable cycle extreme when it is more than 0.6 ATR away; adjust for nearby resistance (long) or support (short), enforce at least 0.25 ATR between targets, and cap the third target at twice its ATR multiple.
Long: Confirmed-bar BUY event: the adaptive Fourier model exists AND forecast direction is up AND forecast move > 0 AND cycle phase is bullish AND (weak cycles allowed OR stability ≥ minimum) AND (ZLEMA filter disabled OR close > ZLEMA) AND (volume unavailable OR volume class ≥ 1 when strict volume is enabled, otherwise ≥ 0) AND close − ZLEMA ≤ extension limit × ATR AND cycle value ≤ 0.85 × total harmonic amplitude AND optional higher-timeframe confirmation passes AND long confidence ≥ BUY threshold AND previous raw signal was not long AND bars since previous signal ≥ cooldown AND current position is not long. OR: projected BUY level exists (projected trough is within the adaptive forecast horizon and adjusted level < close).
Short: Confirmed-bar SELL event: the adaptive Fourier model exists AND forecast direction is down AND forecast move < 0 AND cycle phase is bearish AND (weak cycles allowed OR stability ≥ minimum) AND (ZLEMA filter disabled OR close < ZLEMA) AND (volume unavailable OR volume class ≤ −1 when strict volume is enabled, otherwise ≤ 0) AND ZLEMA − close ≤ extension limit × ATR AND cycle value ≥ −0.85 × total harmonic amplitude AND optional higher-timeframe confirmation passes AND short confidence ≥ BUY threshold AND previous raw signal was not short AND bars since previous signal ≥ cooldown AND current position is not short. OR: projected SELL level exists (projected peak is within the adaptive forecast horizon and adjusted level > close).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ third target OR bars since entry > maximum trade duration.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ third target OR bars since entry > maximum trade duration.
