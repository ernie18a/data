Calculation: Synthetic Renko engine = Use either the micro or macro engine, each with its own box size, on the chart bars. Initialize bottom = floor(close / box size) × box size, top = bottom + box size, and direction = bullish if close ≥ open, otherwise bearish. While bullish, a low ≤ bottom − box size flips direction bearish and moves top to the old bottom and bottom down one box; while bearish, a high ≥ top + box size flips direction bullish and moves bottom to the old top and top up one box. Continue moving one box at a time in the direction of each qualifying high or low, updating the bounds. An upward or downward brick trigger is true if at least one brick in that direction prints on the bar; a direction flip is true when the engine changes direction on that bar.
Long: Selected engine prints at least one upward brick AND (flip-only filter is off OR prior direction is bearish AND current direction is bullish).
Short: Selected engine prints at least one downward brick AND (flip-only filter is off OR prior direction is bullish AND current direction is bearish).
