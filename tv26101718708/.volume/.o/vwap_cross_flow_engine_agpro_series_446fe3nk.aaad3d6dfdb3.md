Calculation: Anchored VWAP = since the current session, week, or month anchor began, cumulative price-source × volume divided by cumulative volume; use the current forming anchor (price source configurable, default HLC3)
Calculation: Cross quality score = 100 × (directional RSI momentum score + volume score + optional sigma bonus) / maximum possible score; momentum score = min(max(direction-adjusted RSI distance from 50, 0) / 25 × 40, 40), volume score = min(volume / its longer-lookback SMA / 3 × 30, 30), and sigma bonus = 5 when enabled and the prior bar touched the opposing 2σ band, otherwise 0; maximum is 75 with the bonus enabled and bands shown, otherwise 70
Long: close crosses below anchored VWAP AND cross quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND bar gap since last plotted cross ≥ minimum bar gap AND (no prior plotted cross OR absolute distance from its close ≥ current ATR × minimum ATR distance)
Short: close crosses above anchored VWAP AND cross quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND bar gap since last plotted cross ≥ minimum bar gap AND (no prior plotted cross OR absolute distance from its close ≥ current ATR × minimum ATR distance)
