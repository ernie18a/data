Calculation: EMA slope = (current EMA − previous-bar EMA) / previous-bar EMA × 100
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume
Long: position side = flat AND close > EMA AND RSI > 55 AND Relative volume ≥ 1 AND EMA slope > 0
Short: position side = flat AND close < EMA AND RSI < 45 AND Relative volume ≥ 1 AND EMA slope < 0
Exit: long, close < EMA OR (RSI < 50 AND previous-bar RSI ≥ 50)
Exit: short, close > EMA OR (RSI > 50 AND previous-bar RSI ≤ 50)
