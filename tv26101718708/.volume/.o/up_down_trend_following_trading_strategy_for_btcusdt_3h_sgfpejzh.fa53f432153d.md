Calculation: Binned RSI values = floor(RSI / 10) × 10, using HLC3 for the shortest lookback and OHLC4 for the two longer lookbacks
Calculation: Binned RVI values = floor(RVI / 10) × 10; for each lookback, RVI = EMA(standard deviation of source on rising source changes, EMA length) / [EMA(standard deviation on rising changes, EMA length) + EMA(standard deviation on non-rising changes, EMA length)] × 100; sources are OHLC4, HLC3, and HL2 from shortest to longest lookback
Calculation: Binned MFI values = floor(MFI / 10) × 10, using HLCC4 for the shortest lookback and HLC3 for the two longer lookbacks
Calculation: Smoothed BOP = SMA(50, ((close − open) / (high − low)) × 100 + 50)
Calculation: General bottom = long-lookback binned RSI ≤ lower RSI threshold AND middle-lookback binned RSI < long-lookback binned RSI AND short-lookback binned RSI ≤ middle-lookback binned RSI AND long-lookback binned RVI ≤ lower RVI threshold AND middle-lookback binned RVI ≤ long-lookback binned RVI AND short-lookback binned RVI ≤ middle-lookback binned RVI AND long-lookback binned MFI ≤ lower MFI threshold AND middle-lookback binned MFI ≤ long-lookback binned MFI AND short-lookback binned MFI ≤ middle-lookback binned MFI AND smoothed BOP ≤ lower BOP threshold
Calculation: General rise = long-lookback binned RSI ≥ higher RSI threshold AND middle-lookback binned RSI > long-lookback binned RSI AND short-lookback binned RSI ≥ middle-lookback binned RSI AND long-lookback binned RVI ≥ higher RVI threshold AND middle-lookback binned RVI ≥ long-lookback binned RVI AND short-lookback binned RVI ≥ middle-lookback binned RVI AND long-lookback binned MFI ≥ higher MFI threshold AND middle-lookback binned MFI ≥ long-lookback binned MFI AND short-lookback binned MFI ≥ middle-lookback binned MFI AND smoothed BOP ≥ higher BOP threshold
Calculation: Critical bottom = same ordering conditions as General bottom, with long-lookback binned RSI, RVI, and MFI ≤ 30 and smoothed BOP ≤ 50
Calculation: Critical rise = same ordering conditions as General rise, with long-lookback binned RSI, RVI, and MFI ≥ 60 and smoothed BOP ≥ 60
Calculation: ALMA rising/falling = 10-period ALMA of open, with offset 0.99 and sigma 1, rises/falls versus the preceding bar
Calculation: Uptrend long conditions = open or high ≥ the corresponding smoothed Supertrend line, SMA of high, or SAR; or open or high crosses above that line. The Supertrend direction lines are separately SMA-smoothed using the same length.
Calculation: Downtrend short conditions = close or low < the corresponding smoothed Supertrend line, SMA of high, or SAR; or close or low crosses below that line. The Supertrend direction lines are separately SMA-smoothed using the same length.
Calculation: Breakout long signal = the conjunction of all four Uptrend long conditions and ALMA rising, OR the third Downtrend short condition with the first, second, and fourth Uptrend long conditions, OR the first and second Downtrend short conditions with the third and fourth Uptrend long conditions and ALMA rising; signal occurs only on the first bar the conjunction becomes true
Calculation: Bottom-bounce state = buy when the rolling-low series crosses the current low and its previous value is greater than its current value; then set state to 1. From state 1, set state to 2 when open > close or open two bars ago > close. From state 2, set state to 3 when open < close. From state 3, reset to 0 when open < close or open > close. Otherwise reset to 0.
Calculation: Lower-price crossing = low crosses below any of 95%, 90%, 85%, or 80% of entry price
Calculation: Sell-side signal = all four Downtrend short conditions and ALMA falling, OR the third Downtrend short condition with the fourth Downtrend short condition and first and second Uptrend long conditions, OR the fourth Downtrend short condition with first, second, and third Uptrend long conditions, OR the second Uptrend long condition with first, third, and fourth Downtrend short conditions, OR the second Uptrend long condition with first and fourth Downtrend short conditions
Calculation: Profit-level crossing = low crosses below entry price × (1 + configured profit percentage)
Calculation: Stop-level crossing = low crosses below entry price × (1 − configured stop percentage)
Long: General bottom was true on the previous bar and is false now OR Bottom-bounce state = 2, while flat, inside the configured date window, and outside the 10-bar post-position cooldown
Long: (General bottom was true on the previous bar and is false now OR Lower-price crossing OR Breakout long signal) AND 1-period SMA of low < 0.99 × entry price AND 9-period SMA of close is falling over 4 bars, while already long and inside the configured date window
Long: Bottom-bounce state = 2 AND prior bar high < entry price, while already long
Long: Critical bottom is true AND 1-period SMA of low < entry price (critical-bottom marking)
Long: Critical rise is true (critical-rise marking)
Short: General rise is true (general-rise marking)
Exit: Long, Sell-side signal OR Profit-level crossing, inside the configured date window, and expected profit price < 1-period SMA of low, with at least 8 open trades
Exit: Long, General rise is true, inside the configured date window, and expected profit price < 1-period SMA of low, with fewer than 8 open trades
Exit: Long, Stop-level crossing with at least 90% of the configured maximum open trades, when stop loss is enabled
Exit: Long, close > entry price OR General rise is true, outside the 10-bar post-position cooldown, when partial profit taking is enabled
