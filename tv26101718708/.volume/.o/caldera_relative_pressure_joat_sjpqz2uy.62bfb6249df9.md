Calculation: Pressure composite = EMA of 100 × (volume / its longer-lookback SMA) × the weighted sum of: effort (0.55 × body / range + 0.45 × abs(body) / range × sign(body)); result (tanh of 4 × body / ATR, clamped to [−2.5, 2.5]); and location (close location + 0.5 × lower-wick-minus-upper-wick ratios, clamped to [−1, 1]). Use effort, result, and location weights respectively.
Calculation: Trend context = close above a rising EMA for bullish context, or close below a falling EMA for bearish context.
Long: Pressure composite > drive threshold AND pressure composite > its signal EMA AND close location > 0 AND (trend context is bullish or neutral)
