Calculation: Bar delta = buy-attributed volume minus sell-attributed volume from the selected OHLCV proxy model, optionally multiplied by the square root of relative volume (current volume divided by the prior bar’s volume average)
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum of bar delta, reset according to the selected reset mode
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving average of cumulative volume delta
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior price pivot low, while cumulative volume delta at the pivot is higher than at the prior pivot; require both pivots in the same reset period, within the selected maximum gap, and CVD separation at least the selected minimum strength times the EMA of absolute bar delta at the current pivot
Calculation: Bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior price pivot high, while cumulative volume delta at the pivot is lower than at the prior pivot; require both pivots in the same reset period, within the selected maximum gap, and CVD separation at least the selected minimum strength times the EMA of absolute bar delta at the current pivot
Long: Bar delta >= 0 OR cumulative volume delta >= 0 OR cumulative volume delta >= signal line OR current relative volume >= selected spike threshold AND bar delta > 0 OR bullish divergence OR cumulative volume delta crosses above 0 OR cumulative volume delta crosses above signal line
Short: Bar delta < 0 OR cumulative volume delta < 0 OR cumulative volume delta < signal line OR current relative volume >= selected spike threshold AND bar delta < 0 OR bearish divergence OR cumulative volume delta crosses below 0 OR cumulative volume delta crosses below signal line
