Calculation: Hull moving average = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half length) − WMA(close, full length), square-root-of-length); round the derived lengths.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a local low/high with the configured number of bars on each side; detect it on the confirmation bar and use RSI/CMO from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = current confirmed pivot low < preceding pivot low AND oscillator at current pivot > oscillator at preceding pivot. Regular bearish divergence = current confirmed pivot high > preceding pivot high AND oscillator at current pivot < oscillator at preceding pivot.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = current confirmed pivot low > preceding pivot low AND RSI at current pivot < RSI at preceding pivot. Hidden bearish divergence = current confirmed pivot high < preceding pivot high AND RSI at current pivot > RSI at preceding pivot.
Calculation: CMO = 100 × (sum of positive close changes − sum of negative close changes) / (sum of positive close changes + sum of negative close changes), over its configured lookback.
Calculation: VWAP bands = daily-reset VWAP of hlc3 ± configured band width × ATR.
Long: close > Hull moving average OR Hull moving average > its value 2 bars ago OR regular bullish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) OR regular bullish RSI divergence (9-period RSI, same pivot lookback) OR regular bullish CMO divergence (14-period CMO, 5-bar pivot lookback, against any of the previous 3 pivots) OR hidden bullish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) OR volume ≥ configured climax multiple × 20-bar volume SMA AND low ≤ lowest low over configured swing lookback AND close > (high + low) / 2 OR low ≤ lower VWAP band + configured ATR buffer AND close > lower VWAP band OR Hull moving average minus its value 3 bars ago > configured ATR slope threshold AND the prior bar's slope ≤ its prior ATR slope threshold OR Hull moving average rising AND close > daily-reset VWAP AND regular bullish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) AND 14-period RSI < 75 AND volume > 0.8 × 20-bar volume SMA.
Short: close ≤ Hull moving average OR Hull moving average ≤ its value 2 bars ago OR regular bearish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) OR regular bearish RSI divergence (9-period RSI, same pivot lookback) OR regular bearish CMO divergence (14-period CMO, 5-bar pivot lookback, against any of the previous 3 pivots) OR hidden bearish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) OR volume ≥ configured climax multiple × 20-bar volume SMA AND high ≥ highest high over configured swing lookback AND close < (high + low) / 2 OR high ≥ upper VWAP band − configured ATR buffer AND close < upper VWAP band OR Hull moving average minus its value 3 bars ago < negative configured ATR slope threshold AND the prior bar's slope ≥ its prior negative ATR slope threshold OR Hull moving average not rising AND close < daily-reset VWAP AND regular bearish RSI divergence (14-period RSI, configured pivot lookback) AND 14-period RSI > 25 AND volume > 0.8 × 20-bar volume SMA.
