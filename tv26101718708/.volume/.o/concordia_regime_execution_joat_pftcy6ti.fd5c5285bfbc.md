Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume since the current day began
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume since the current week began
Calculation: Expansion regime = ADX above its floor AND choppiness below its ceiling AND (lookback high − lookback low) / (ATR × lookback length) at or above its compression threshold
Calculation: Trend bias = bullish when at least 3 of these votes are positive, bearish when at least 3 are negative, otherwise neutral: fast EMA above mid EMA; mid EMA above slow EMA; fast EMA rising over its slope lookback; mid EMA rising over that same lookback; close above session VWAP; and, when enabled, close above weekly VWAP. A vote is negative when its comparison is false.
Calculation: Structural bias = direction of the latest confirmed close break above a prior confirmed pivot high or below a prior confirmed pivot low; retain the last break direction until another break
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Sell-side/buy-side sweep = confirmed low falls below the latest confirmed pivot low and closes back above it / confirmed high rises above the latest confirmed pivot high and closes back below it
Calculation: Momentum spread = average of the first three lag-reduced, smoothed RSI values minus 50, minus the average of the next three such values minus 50; RSI lookbacks increase by a fixed step from shortest to longest
Calculation: Momentum bias = bullish when momentum spread exceeds a threshold, bearish when it is below the negative threshold, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Pressure composite = 0.35 × z-score of ((close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) × volume) + 0.25 × z-score of (close − session VWAP) + 0.20 × z-score of (close − average of the three EMAs) − 0.20 × z-score of ATR; each z-score uses the same lookback
Calculation: Pressure bias = bullish when pressure composite exceeds its threshold, bearish when below its negative, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Crowding composite = average of the z-score of (RSI(14) − 50) and the z-score of (close − weekly VWAP), using the same normalization lookback
Calculation: Liquidation risk = clamp(50 + 14 × abs(crowding composite) + 10 × max(z-score of ATR, 0) + 8 when either sweep occurs, to 0–100); risk is allowed when this is below its ceiling
Calculation: Long score = weighted sum: expansion regime, bullish trend bias, bullish structural bias, bullish structure break, sell-side sweep, bullish momentum bias, bullish pressure bias, close above session VWAP, and (when weekly VWAP alignment is enabled) close above weekly VWAP; subtract a penalty when risk is not allowed
Calculation: Short score = weighted sum: expansion regime, bearish trend bias, bearish structural bias, bearish structure break, buy-side sweep, bearish momentum bias, bearish pressure bias, close below session VWAP, and (when weekly VWAP alignment is enabled) close below weekly VWAP; subtract a penalty when risk is not allowed
Calculation: Long trigger = bullish structure break OR sell-side sweep OR fast ribbon crosses above slow ribbon OR close crosses above fast EMA
Calculation: Short trigger = bearish structure break OR buy-side sweep OR fast ribbon crosses below slow ribbon OR close crosses below fast EMA
Calculation: Effective long stop = greater of entry price − ATR × stop multiple and (when trailing is active) highest high over the trailing lookback − ATR × trail multiple; trailing activates when close reaches entry price + ATR × stop multiple × trail trigger multiple
Calculation: Effective short stop = lesser of entry price + ATR × stop multiple and (when trailing is active) lowest low over the trailing lookback + ATR × trail multiple; trailing activates when close reaches entry price − ATR × stop multiple × trail trigger multiple
Long: confirmed bar AND cooldown complete AND long score meets entry threshold AND long score is at least short score minus a tolerance AND (expansion regime OR ADX is at least a fraction of its floor) AND trend bias is neutral or bullish AND momentum bias is neutral or bullish AND pressure composite is above its negative soft threshold AND long trigger AND liquidation risk is below its soft ceiling AND position is flat or short
Short: confirmed bar AND cooldown complete AND short score meets entry threshold AND short score is at least long score minus a tolerance AND (expansion regime OR ADX is at least a fraction of its floor) AND trend bias is neutral or bearish AND momentum bias is neutral or bearish AND pressure composite is below its positive soft threshold AND short trigger AND liquidation risk is below its soft ceiling AND position is flat or long
Exit: long, low <= effective long stop OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR confirmed bar AND trend bias is not bullish OR momentum bias is bearish OR pressure bias is bearish OR risk is not allowed
Exit: short, high >= effective short stop OR low <= entry price − ATR × target multiple OR confirmed bar AND trend bias is not bearish OR momentum bias is bullish OR pressure bias is bullish OR risk is not allowed
