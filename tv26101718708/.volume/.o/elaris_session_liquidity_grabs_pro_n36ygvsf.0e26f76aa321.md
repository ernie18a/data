Calculation: Session high/low = highest high and lowest low during each enabled London, New York, or Asia session; use the last completed session’s levels.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.

Long: For any enabled session, outside that session, low < its last completed low AND abs(low − its last completed low) ≥ a configurable ATR fraction AND ((wick mode: low < level AND close > level) OR (close-break mode: close < level)) AND (close > level if close-back-inside is required) AND (volume > its configurable multiple of the volume SMA if the volume filter is enabled) AND (close > EMA if the trend filter is enabled) AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous long signal to satisfy the cooldown AND (outside all sessions if that filter is enabled) AND (if strong reversal close is required: close > open AND close − low ≥ a configurable ATR fraction AND candle body percentage ≥ its configurable minimum).
Short: For any enabled session, outside that session, high > its last completed high AND abs(high − its last completed high) ≥ a configurable ATR fraction AND ((wick mode: high > level AND close < level) OR (close-break mode: close > level)) AND (close < level if close-back-inside is required) AND (volume > its configurable multiple of the volume SMA if the volume filter is enabled) AND (close < EMA if the trend filter is enabled) AND enough bars have elapsed since the previous short signal to satisfy the cooldown AND (outside all sessions if that filter is enabled) AND (if strong reversal close is required: close < open AND high − close ≥ a configurable ATR fraction AND candle body percentage ≥ its configurable minimum).
