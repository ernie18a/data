Calculation: bar delta = if high > low and close > (high + low) / 2, volume × abs(close − open) / (high − low); otherwise, −volume × abs(close − open) / (high − low) (0 when high = low)
Calculation: delta average = simple moving average of bar delta over a lookback
Calculation: consecutive negative bar-delta count = number of consecutive bars ending on the current bar with bar delta < 0
Long: (highest(high, base lookback) − lowest(low, base lookback)) / lowest(low, base lookback) ≤ tight-base threshold AND volume ≥ volume-spike multiplier × SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND close > SMA(close, short lookback) AND delta average > 0 AND low > low[5] AND low[5] > low[10] AND (close − SMA(close, short lookback)) / SMA(close, short lookback) × 100 ≤ maximum extension AND close > highest(high, base lookback) over prior bars
Long: close > 0.95 × SMA(close, short lookback) AND close < 0.95 × highest(close, 10 bars) AND volume < SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND delta average > 0 AND SMA(close, short lookback) > its value 3 bars ago AND low > low[5] AND low[5] > low[10] AND consecutive negative bar-delta count < weak-delta threshold
Long: volume ≥ ignition multiplier × SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND close > open AND close > highest(high, base lookback) over prior bars AND SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) < quiet-volume threshold
Long: SMA(close, short lookback) > its value 3 bars ago AND close > SMA(close, short lookback) AND close > SMA(close, short lookback) on the prior bar AND close[1] ≤ SMA(close, short lookback) on the prior bar AND volume ≥ volume-spike multiplier × SMA(volume, volume-average lookback) AND delta average > 0 AND (close − SMA(close, short lookback)) / SMA(close, short lookback) × 100 ≤ maximum extension
