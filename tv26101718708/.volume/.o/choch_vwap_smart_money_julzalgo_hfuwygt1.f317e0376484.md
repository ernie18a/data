Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars before and after it; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each type.
Calculation: Internal trend = direction of the most recent break of an unbroken confirmed pivot high or low; starts neutral. Mark that pivot broken after its first break, and reset its broken status when a newer pivot of that type is confirmed.
Long: Internal CHoCH is enabled AND internal trend before this bar is bearish AND the most recently confirmed pivot high has not been broken AND (close crosses above that pivot high when confirmation mode is Close OR high > that pivot high AND previous high <= that pivot high when confirmation mode is Wick)
Short: Internal CHoCH is enabled AND internal trend before this bar is bullish AND the most recently confirmed pivot low has not been broken AND (close crosses below that pivot low when confirmation mode is Close OR low < that pivot low AND previous low >= that pivot low when confirmation mode is Wick)
