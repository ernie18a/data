Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; use the shorter and longer configured lengths for fast and mid EMAs, and the longer-term EMA for the trend EMA
Calculation: SMA = simple moving average of volume over its configured lookback; the signal also uses a shorter-period SMA of close
Calculation: Qualified demand zone = zone around a confirmed pivot low whose pivot-bar volume exceeds its volume-average threshold; qualified supply zone = corresponding zone around a confirmed pivot high; zone widths and proximity use ATR multiples; discard a demand zone when close falls below its lower edge by an ATR buffer, and a supply zone when close rises above its upper edge by an ATR buffer
Calculation: Near demand / near supply = close lies within the configured ATR distance of any active qualified zone
Calculation: Hurst estimate = 1 − [100 × log10(sum of true range over the configured lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / (100 × log10(lookback))]
Calculation: POC = midpoint of the highest-volume bucket after dividing the configured lookback's high-low range into equal buckets and allocating each bar's volume to buckets in proportion to its range overlap; VAH and VAL expand from that bucket until the configured share of total bucket volume is included
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish = fast EMA > mid EMA and RSI > its bullish threshold on the higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe bearish = fast EMA < mid EMA and RSI < its bearish threshold on the higher-timeframe bar; use the higher-timeframe value with lookahead off
Long: not (Hurst < chop threshold AND ADX < trend-strength threshold) AND ((near demand AND close > open AND fast EMA rising AND RSI rising AND RSI within bounce bounds AND volume > volume SMA × bounce multiplier AND (higher-timeframe bullish OR (ADX > fallback threshold AND close > close from two bars earlier)) AND (POC filter disabled OR close > POC OR distance to POC > ATR × distance threshold) AND (volume/risk filters not applied by this setup)) OR (fast EMA crosses above mid EMA AND close > trend EMA AND RSI within cross bounds AND close SMA rising AND volume > volume SMA × cross multiplier AND higher-timeframe bullish AND ADX > trend-strength threshold AND (POC filter disabled OR NOT (close < POC AND near POC)) AND (POC filter disabled OR NOT (close below VAH and within its ATR distance)) AND (volume/risk filters not applied by this setup)) OR (trend EMA rising over its longer lookback AND low within ATR distance of trend EMA AND close > trend EMA AND (bullish lower wick OR close > open) AND RSI > retest threshold AND higher-timeframe bullish AND volume > volume SMA AND (POC filter disabled OR close > POC OR distance to POC > ATR × distance threshold) AND (volume/risk filters not applied by this setup)) OR (near demand AND (close > open OR lower wick > candle body × wick multiplier) AND RSI > RSI from two bars earlier AND RSI within support bounds AND volume > volume SMA AND NOT near supply AND (close > previous close OR previous low < low from two bars earlier) AND ADX < support ceiling) OR (fast EMA's rise over its lookback is positive and accelerating AND close > fast EMA AND close > close from two bars earlier AND close > open AND RSI > bullish threshold AND RSI > RSI from two bars earlier AND volume > volume SMA × momentum multiplier AND NOT near supply AND ADX > trend-strength threshold AND (near demand OR close > trend EMA) AND (POC filter disabled OR close > POC OR distance to POC > ATR × distance threshold)))
