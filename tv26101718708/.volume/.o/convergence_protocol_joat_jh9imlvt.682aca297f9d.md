Calculation: Baseline = double-smoothed moving average of close, using a shorter lookback than Step.
Calculation: Step = double-smoothed moving average of high minus low, using a longer lookback than Baseline.
Calculation: Swing highs/lows = confirmed local high/low pivots using equal left and right lookbacks; track the latest and preceding pivot of each type.
Calculation: Bullish structure = latest pivot high > preceding pivot high AND latest pivot low > preceding pivot low; bearish structure = latest pivot high < preceding pivot high AND latest pivot low < preceding pivot low.
Calculation: Bull score = count of: close above Baseline + Step; close above Baseline + 2 × Step; latest pivot high > preceding pivot high; latest pivot low > preceding pivot low.
Calculation: Bear score = count of: close below Baseline − Step; close below Baseline − 2 × Step; latest pivot low < preceding pivot low; latest pivot high < preceding pivot high.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when Baseline is rising and Bull score meets its threshold; bearish when Baseline is falling and Bear score meets its threshold; otherwise neutral. Update on completed bars.
Calculation: Volatility expansion = short-period ATR / long-period ATR exceeds a threshold AND the smoothed ATR ratio exceeds its signal average. Squeeze = short-period ATR is below both a fraction of long-period ATR and its own moving average. Use a longer period for the long-period ATR.
Calculation: Delta = volume on up bars, negative volume on down bars, and zero on unchanged bars; calculate fast and slow EMAs, with the fast EMA using the shorter period. Bullish delta = normalized fast-minus-slow EMA exceeds a threshold and its signal EMA; bearish delta = normalized fast-minus-slow EMA is below the negative threshold and its signal EMA.
Calculation: Bullish delta cross = fast delta EMA crosses above slow delta EMA; bearish delta cross = fast delta EMA crosses below slow delta EMA.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = latest confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish BOS = latest confirmed close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. Keep the latest BOS direction until an opposite BOS.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low extends beyond the latest pivot low by an ATR-scaled buffer AND close finishes above that pivot low; bearish sweep = high extends beyond the latest pivot high by an ATR-scaled buffer AND close finishes below that pivot high.
Calculation: Long confluence score = count of: bullish regime; bullish structure; volatility expansion; no squeeze; bullish delta; latest BOS bullish; bullish sweep. Short confluence score uses the corresponding bearish conditions, with volatility expansion and no squeeze shared.
Long: On a completed bar with no open position, bullish regime AND (long confluence score meets its threshold OR (price is within a fraction of Step from Baseline AND low reaches the Baseline zone AND close > Baseline AND bullish delta) OR (squeeze ended AND bullish delta) OR (bullish delta cross AND no squeeze) OR (bullish sweep AND bullish delta)); apply the optional volatility-expansion filter to the confluence, pullback, and delta-cross pathways when enabled.
Long: Bullish regime AND bullish delta cross.
Short: On a completed bar with no open position, bearish regime AND (short confluence score meets its threshold OR (price is within a fraction of Step from Baseline AND high reaches the Baseline zone AND close < Baseline AND bearish delta) OR (squeeze ended AND bearish delta) OR (bearish delta cross AND no squeeze) OR (bearish sweep AND bearish delta)); apply the optional volatility-expansion filter to the confluence, pullback, and delta-cross pathways when enabled.
Short: Bearish regime AND bearish delta cross.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − initial ATR-based risk distance OR high >= entry price + first risk-multiple target OR high >= entry price + second risk-multiple target OR, after the first target is reached, low <= highest high since activation − ATR-based trailing offset OR regime is bearish.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + initial ATR-based risk distance OR low <= entry price − first risk-multiple target OR low <= entry price − second risk-multiple target OR, after the first target is reached, high >= lowest low since activation + ATR-based trailing offset OR regime is bullish.
