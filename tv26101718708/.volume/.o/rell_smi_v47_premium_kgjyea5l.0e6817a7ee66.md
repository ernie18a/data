Calculation: Bullish structure break = price (close or high, according to the configured break method) crosses above a live confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = price (close or low) crosses below a live confirmed pivot low. Internal and swing pivots use different lengths, with swing longer than internal; pivots are confirmed using bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = price breaks below a sell-side liquidity zone and closes back above its boundary; bearish liquidity sweep = price breaks above a buy-side liquidity zone and closes back below its boundary. For crypto, also count a low below the prior bar's low followed by a close above it as bullish, and a high above the prior bar's high followed by a close below it as bearish.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier, the intervening bar closes above that earlier high, and configured gap-size, ATR, and displacement filters pass. Bearish fair value gap is the inverse. A bullish or bearish inversion gap occurs when an active gap is fully filled and price closes through it, with configured displacement and structure confirmation.
Calculation: Bullish trendline break = close breaks above the descending line projected from the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish trendline break = close breaks below the ascending line projected from the latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Bull and bear confluence scores = sums of their respective points for active same-side fair value gaps and their quality, structure breaks, liquidity sweeps, trendline breaks, trend alignment, higher-timeframe structure, premium/discount location, order-block retests, inversion gaps, session, daily levels, relative volume, volatility, and higher-timeframe context. The automatic minimum score starts from a market-profile threshold and adjusts for volatility compression, weak relative volume, momentum expansion, strong trend, and choppy trend.
Calculation: Bullish higher-timeframe context = bullish structure direction, bullish structure break, or bullish change of character on the configured context timeframe; bearish context is the inverse. Macro structure direction uses the configured macro timeframe. Liquidity, context, and macro calculations use the configured higher timeframes' bars as they update; the previous-day high/low use the last completed daily bar, and daily open uses the current daily bar.
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish trendline break OR bullish fair value gap OR bullish inversion gap OR bullish confluence score meets the automatic minimum and exceeds the bear score OR approved long IT3 score meets its threshold OR bullish breaker retest OR volume-confirmed resistance break OR (demand reaction AND (bullish structure break OR bullish trend alignment)) OR (BTC cycle discount AND bullish liquidity sweep AND bullish change of character) OR (higher-timeframe sell-side sweep AND (bullish structure break OR bullish change of character) AND context or macro structure is non-bearish) OR (bullish higher-timeframe fair value gap is touched AND bullish local gap retest) OR (sell-side sweep setup AND bullish change of character) OR (bullish gap or inversion gap is active and its midpoint is tapped with qualifying gap quality) OR (bullish breakout AND higher-timeframe structure is non-bearish or trend alignment is bullish) OR (bullish trend alignment AND bullish higher-timeframe structure AND discount location) OR (support is broken with sufficient relative volume and volume expansion or volume above its average)
