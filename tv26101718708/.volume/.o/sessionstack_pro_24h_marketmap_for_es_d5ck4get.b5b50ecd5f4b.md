Calculation: Globex VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 from the 18:00 ET Globex session start through the current bar, including the current bar; reset at each 18:00 ET start
Long: Globex VWAP is available AND close >= Globex VWAP
Short: Globex VWAP is available AND close < Globex VWAP
