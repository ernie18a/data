Calculation: Closed structure resistance/support = high/low of the last completed structure-timeframe bar
Calculation: Closed bias close and slow EMA = close and slow EMA of the last completed bias-timeframe bar; prior bias EMA values are from the two preceding completed bias-timeframe bars
Calculation: Bullish/bearish bias = bias close above/below slow EMA, with the slow EMA rising/falling versus its prior value
Calculation: Bull/bear momentum score = 25 points for RSI at least 55 / at most 45, else 18 for at least 50 / at most 50, else 10 for at least 45 / at most 55, else 0; plus 20 if RSI rises/falls, 20 if 3-bar EMA-smoothed close change is positive/negative, 20 if fast EMA is above/below slow EMA, and 15 if 3-bar ROC is positive/negative
Calculation: Bull/bear expansion = fast EMA above/below slow EMA and slow EMA nondecreasing/nonincreasing over 3 bars, with regime strength at least the expansion threshold; regime strength is 100 × (0.35 × clamp(ADX/40, 0, 1) + 0.30 × efficiency ratio + 0.20 × clamp(absolute fast/slow EMA separation divided by ATR and 1.25, 0, 1) + 0.15 × clamp(absolute 3-bar slow-EMA change divided by ATR and 0.75, 0, 1)); efficiency ratio is absolute close change over the regime lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Support/resistance touch = current bar range intersects the corresponding closed structure level ± ATR zone
Calculation: Buy/sell reaction = support/resistance touch, close above/below the structure level, lower/upper wick divided by candle range at least the rejection-wick threshold, and close above/below open
Calculation: Recent buy/sell reaction or pullback = corresponding reaction or pullback occurred within the reaction-memory lookback, inclusive; pullback is a support/resistance or equilibrium touch with bullish/bearish candle and close at/above/below equilibrium; equilibrium is VWAP in scalping mode and fast EMA in swing mode
Calculation: Microstructure break = close above/below the highest/lowest high/low of the preceding microstructure lookback bars
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-bar average volume when available; volume gate passes when volume is unavailable, volume confirmation is disabled, or relative volume is at least its minimum; strong volume passes when volume is unavailable or relative volume is at least its strong-volume threshold
Calculation: Long/short risk-to-resistance/support = distance from close to the closed structure resistance/support when that level is above/below close, divided by the distance from close to the recent lookback low/high padded by ATR; the ratio passes when it is at least the minimum risk/reward threshold, or when resistance/support is not above/below close
Calculation: Long/short execution score = maximum of the eligible continuation and reversal scores, each capped at 100. Continuation score = 25 for matching expansion (otherwise 12 for matching transition, else 0) + 15 for matching bias + 15 for recent matching reaction (otherwise 12 for recent matching pullback, else 0) + 20% of matching momentum score + 10 for matching microstructure break + 5 for strong volume + 10 for passing risk/reward + intrabar directional score add. Reversal score = 20 for opposite expansion within reversal-memory lookback and no longer expanding + 20 for recent matching reaction + 20% of matching momentum score + 15 for matching microstructure break + 10 for close beyond fast EMA + 5 for strong volume + 10 for passing risk/reward + intrabar directional score add
Calculation: Intrabar directional evidence (scalping when enabled) = at least the configured number of matching one-minute pulses within the chart bar and either the latest pulse matches that direction or its best pulse score exceeds the opposite best score; without active intrabar inspection, evidence passes. A pulse requires its score to reach the pulse threshold and at least one RSI turn from an extreme, RSI momentum from near an extreme, or close break of the prior intrabar high/low. Pulse score adds points for that RSI condition, candle direction, prior-bar break, fast/slow EMA alignment, relative volume at least 1, and RSI rising/falling for two consecutive changes; HQ pulse also reaches the higher HQ score threshold
Calculation: Continuation long/short candidate = matching expansion and bias, recent matching reaction or pullback, matching microstructure break, momentum score at least the continuation minimum, volume gate, risk/reward pass, and intrabar evidence gate. Reversal long/short candidate = recent opposite expansion that is no longer active, recent matching reaction, close above/below fast EMA, matching microstructure break, momentum score at least the reversal minimum, volume gate, risk/reward pass, and intrabar evidence gate
Calculation: Momentum exit weakness count = number of true conditions among close below/above fast EMA, RSI below/above 50, and smoothed close change below/above zero
Calculation: Long/short stop = lowest low/highest high over the stop lookback minus/plus ATR padding; target 3 = entry price plus/minus initial risk times the third target multiple
Long: Buy reaction OR eligible continuation/reversal long candidate with execution score at least the entry minimum, on the first bar it becomes ready, while flat and past the entry cooldown; if both directions become ready together, long wins a score tie
Short: Sell reaction OR eligible continuation/reversal short candidate with execution score at least the entry minimum, on the first bar it becomes ready, while flat and past the entry cooldown; if both directions become ready together, short requires a strictly higher score
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target 3 OR (momentum exit weakness count ≥ 2 AND bull momentum score < 50)
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target 3 OR (momentum exit weakness count ≥ 2 AND bear momentum score < 50)
