Calculation: Accumulation high/low = highest high and lowest low of the current accumulation session so far.
Calculation: Lower/upper extreme = a low at or below any enabled lower boundary, or a high at or above any enabled upper boundary. Boundaries are the asymmetric least-squares outer envelope, the Nadaraya–Watson attractor plus/minus its smoothed absolute-error envelope, and the polynomial-regression estimate plus/minus its residual standard deviation times its width multiplier.
Calculation: WESO = normalized close position between the asymmetric least-squares outer bounds, multiplied by 0.3 + 0.7 times the inner-to-outer envelope width ratio.
Calculation: Hilbert phase sine/lead = sine of the phase angle and sine of that angle plus one eighth turn; phase angle is atan2(8 × the two-bar change in the Nadaraya–Watson attractor, attractor minus the midpoint of the four asymmetric least-squares envelopes).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish armed state = price has touched the corresponding upper/lower extreme, with no opposing cascade, kernel-slope/acceleration lockdown, pulse lockdown, or unabsorbed positive ATR acceleration; pulse lockdown uses accumulating directional candle-close-location pulses with decay and lock/release thresholds.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish cleared state = an armed bar passes the corresponding prior-bar open/strict wick-break test, true-range deceleration test, and time-in-extreme test; a failed time test can pass only with the corresponding break. The cleared state persists until close crosses the 24-bar close mean in the adverse direction.
Calculation: Execution trigger = on TF1 (configured base timeframe, using its bar still forming), any enabled trigger: CVD or OBV changes against its 10-bar mean (retail), or at least 75% of valid anomaly-anchor votes agree (institutional); RSI crosses back below/above its overbought/oversold threshold (retail), or the institutional RSI-anchor vote threshold is met; Stoch RSI crosses its signal in the overbought/oversold region; MACD crosses its signal (retail), or its institutional vote threshold is met; Aroon up/down cross (retail), or its institutional vote threshold is met; or price crosses the armed-period volume-weighted average price in the corresponding direction.
Long: Low-session sweep (low below accumulation low, close above it, during manipulation session) AND lower extreme; OR WESO reached the oversold region and the outer lower envelope or WESO reversed upward; OR Hilbert phase sine crosses above lead sine; OR bullish armed state AND bullish cleared state AND close > open AND bullish execution trigger.
Short: High-session sweep (high above accumulation high, close below it, during manipulation session) AND upper extreme; OR WESO reached the overbought region and the outer upper envelope or WESO reversed downward; OR Hilbert phase sine crosses below lead sine; OR bearish armed state AND bearish cleared state AND close < open AND bearish execution trigger.
