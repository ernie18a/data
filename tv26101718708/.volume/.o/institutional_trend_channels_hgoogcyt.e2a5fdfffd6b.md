Calculation: Regression midline = linear regression of close over a lookback, evaluated at the current bar
Calculation: Upper channel boundary = regression midline + standard deviation of close over the same lookback × channel-width parameter
Calculation: Lower channel boundary = regression midline − standard deviation of close over the same lookback × channel-width parameter
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a lookback
Long: Close crosses above the upper channel boundary AND volume ≥ average volume × confirmation multiplier
Short: Close crosses below the lower channel boundary AND volume ≥ average volume × confirmation multiplier
