Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: HA close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; HA open = (previous HA open + previous HA close) / 2, initialized as (open + close) / 2; HA high = maximum of high, HA open, and HA close; HA low = minimum of low, HA open, and HA close
Calculation: Strong bullish HA candle = HA close > HA open and HA open − HA low ≤ minimum tick; strong bearish HA candle = HA close < HA open and HA high − HA open ≤ minimum tick
Calculation: Fast and slow EMA slopes = each EMA minus its value at the same prior-bar lookback
Long: (strong bullish HA candle AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (fast EMA > slow EMA AND both EMA slopes > 0 AND absolute EMA separation ≥ configured minimum AND previous HA candle is bearish AND strong bullish HA candle AND close > fast EMA) OR (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND strong bullish HA candle); AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > VWAP AND cooldown elapsed since either-direction signal AND outside the weekday late-session kill zone in New York time AND (time filter disabled OR current bar is within an enabled session in New York time)
Short: (strong bearish HA candle AND close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA) OR (fast EMA < slow EMA AND both EMA slopes < 0 AND absolute EMA separation ≥ configured minimum AND previous HA candle is bullish AND strong bearish HA candle AND close < fast EMA) OR (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND strong bearish HA candle); AND close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < VWAP AND cooldown elapsed since either-direction signal AND outside the weekday late-session kill zone in New York time AND (time filter disabled OR current bar is within an enabled session in New York time)
