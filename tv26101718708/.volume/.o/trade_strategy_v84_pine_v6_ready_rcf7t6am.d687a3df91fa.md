Calculation: Latest confirmed swing low = the low of the most recent pivot low, lower than the configured number of bars on each side; update only when the pivot is confirmed after its right-side bars. Latest confirmed swing high = the high of the most recent pivot high, higher than the configured number of bars on each side; update only when confirmed.
Calculation: Buy pressure = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low. Sell pressure = (high − close) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry-signal bar low − minimum tick; initial short stop = entry-signal bar high + minimum tick. Entry risk = distance from average entry price to initial stop.
Calculation: While long, activate trailing when close ≥ average entry price + entry risk × configured R threshold; then stop = max(previous stop, average entry price, close − ATR × configured ATR multiplier). While short, activate trailing when close ≤ average entry price − entry risk × configured R threshold; then stop = min(previous stop, average entry price, close + ATR × configured ATR multiplier).
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; body = abs(close − open).
Long: latest confirmed swing low exists AND low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND close > open AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured Asia/Taipei entry session) AND volume > configured multiplier × its configured lookback simple moving average AND buy pressure ≥ configured threshold AND position is flat.
Short: latest confirmed swing high exists AND high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND close < open AND (session filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured Asia/Taipei entry session) AND volume > configured multiplier × its configured lookback simple moving average AND sell pressure ≥ configured threshold AND position is flat.
Exit: long, low ≤ current stop OR configured cap-exit mode is direct close AND volume > configured cap-volume multiplier × the same volume average AND upper wick ≥ 1.5 × body AND (close − average entry price) / entry risk ≥ configured minimum R profit OR time stop is enabled AND bars since entry ≥ configured bar count AND close < average entry price. If cap-exit mode tightens the stop, set stop = max(current stop, close − ATR); exit when low ≤ resulting stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ current stop OR configured cap-exit mode is direct close AND volume > configured cap-volume multiplier × the same volume average AND lower wick ≥ 1.5 × body AND (average entry price − close) / entry risk ≥ configured minimum R profit OR time stop is enabled AND bars since entry ≥ configured bar count AND close > average entry price. If cap-exit mode tightens the stop, set stop = min(current stop, close + ATR); exit when high ≥ resulting stop.
