Calculation: MACD line = shorter-period EMA(close) minus longer-period EMA(close); signal line = EMA(MACD line)
Long: MACD line > signal line AND RSI > 50 AND close > EMA(close) AND volume > SMA(volume)
Short: MACD line < signal line AND RSI < 50 AND close < EMA(close) AND volume > SMA(volume)
Exit: long, RSI crosses below 50
Exit: short, RSI crosses above 50
