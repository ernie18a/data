Calculation: Spread rank = percentile rank of the current bar’s high-low range divided by the immediately preceding bar’s ATR, compared with the same measure on prior bars.
Calculation: Relative-volume rank = percentile rank of current volume relative to its baseline: volume divided by the median for the same time of day over recent sessions, or, until enough same-time observations exist, divided by the mean volume of prior bars. Compare with prior bars’ relative-volume values.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low); for a zero-range bar, use 0.5.
Calculation: Leg = (close of the immediately preceding bar − close K bars before it) / ATR of the immediately preceding bar.
Calculation: Prior 10-bar low/high = minimum low / maximum high of the previous 10 completed bars.
Long: close location is in the upper 30% of the bar AND relative-volume rank is above its high-volume threshold AND current low is below the prior 10-bar low OR relative-volume rank is at or above its extreme-volume threshold AND spread rank is at or above its wide-spread threshold AND close is below the prior close AND close location is in the lower half of the bar AND leg is at or below its downward-move threshold OR relative-volume rank is at or above its high-volume threshold AND spread rank is at or below its narrow-spread threshold AND close location is in the upper half of the bar AND leg is at or below its downward-move threshold OR close is below the prior close AND spread rank and relative-volume rank are at or below their low thresholds AND current volume is below each of the two preceding bars’ volumes.
Short: close location is in the lower 30% of the bar AND relative-volume rank is above its high-volume threshold AND current high is above the prior 10-bar high OR relative-volume rank is at or above its extreme-volume threshold AND spread rank is at or above its wide-spread threshold AND close is above the prior close AND close location is in the lower half of the bar AND leg is at or above its upward-move threshold OR relative-volume rank is at or above its high-volume threshold AND spread rank is at or below its narrow-spread threshold AND close location is in the lower half of the bar AND leg is at or above its upward-move threshold OR close is above the prior close AND spread rank and relative-volume rank are at or below their low thresholds AND current volume is below each of the two preceding bars’ volumes.
