Calculation: Basis = selected moving average of close (EMA, SMA, or VWMA)
Calculation: Raw upper/lower bands = basis ± ATR × band multiplier
Calculation: Final upper band = current raw upper band if it is below the previous final upper band or the previous close is above the previous final upper band; otherwise the previous final upper band. Final lower band = current raw lower band if it is above the previous final lower band or the previous close is below the previous final lower band; otherwise the previous final lower band.
Calculation: Trend state = initialize to bullish when close is at or above the basis, otherwise bearish; change from bearish to bullish on a qualifying close above the previous final upper band, and from bullish to bearish on a qualifying close below the previous final lower band; otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Adaptive slope threshold = clamp the smoothed average of absolute basis change divided by ATR, multiplied by a scaling factor, between lower and upper bounds. Use a basis-change lookback and smoothing lookback selected by chart timeframe, with longer lookbacks on faster chart timeframes.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR expansion threshold = clamp the smoothed average of current ATR divided by its smoothed average, multiplied by a scaling factor, between lower and upper bounds. Use smoothing lookbacks selected by chart timeframe, with longer lookbacks on faster chart timeframes.
Long: prior trend state is bearish AND close > previous final upper band AND basis change over the selected lookback > ATR × adaptive slope threshold AND current ATR / its smoothed average >= adaptive ATR expansion threshold AND (if the higher-timeframe RSI filter is enabled, RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is in the lower zone) AND (if the higher-timeframe EMA filter is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND (bar-close confirmation is satisfied if enabled) AND prior signal direction is not long AND bars since the prior signal >= minimum spacing
Short: prior trend state is bullish AND close < previous final lower band AND basis change over the selected lookback < −ATR × adaptive slope threshold AND current ATR / its smoothed average >= adaptive ATR expansion threshold AND (if the higher-timeframe RSI filter is enabled, RSI on the last completed higher-timeframe bar is in the upper zone) AND (if the higher-timeframe EMA filter is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND (bar-close confirmation is satisfied if enabled) AND prior signal direction is not short AND bars since the prior signal >= minimum spacing
