Calculation: cumulative force = cumulative sum of volume × (close − previous bar close)
Calculation: short-term EMA of cumulative force = EMA of cumulative force with a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA
Calculation: long-term EMA of cumulative force = EMA of cumulative force with a longer lookback than the short-term EMA
Calculation: short-lookback ATR = ATR with a shorter lookback than the long-lookback ATR
Calculation: long-lookback ATR = ATR with a longer lookback than the short-lookback ATR
Long: short-term EMA of cumulative force > long-term EMA of cumulative force AND short-lookback ATR > long-lookback ATR AND (previous bar short-term EMA of cumulative force < previous bar long-term EMA of cumulative force OR previous bar short-lookback ATR < previous bar long-lookback ATR) AND close > open
Short: short-term EMA of cumulative force < long-term EMA of cumulative force AND short-lookback ATR > long-lookback ATR AND (previous bar short-term EMA of cumulative force > previous bar long-term EMA of cumulative force OR previous bar short-lookback ATR < previous bar long-lookback ATR) AND close < open
