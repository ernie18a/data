Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high and lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the current bar's value.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its moving-average baseline; smoothed relative volume = its 3-bar simple moving average.
Calculation: Fast and slow trend = EMAs of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Breakout quality score = rounded 0–100 sum of: volume score (relative volume mapped from 0.85 to the full-volume ratio onto 0–25); candle body efficiency (absolute close-open divided by high-low, times 18); directional close location (for up, (close-low)/(high-low); for down, (high-close)/(high-low), times 17); breakout distance in ATR mapped from the buffer to four times the buffer onto 0–15; trend score (15 if fast EMA is on the directional side of slow EMA and close is beyond fast EMA, 8 if either is true, otherwise 2); and range-fit score (10 within the minimum and maximum range-height-to-ATR bounds, scaled down proportionally outside them).
Long: Close crosses above the prior range high plus an ATR-scaled buffer AND breakout quality score is at least the weak-path floor AND a new path is allowed; OR the same breakout condition has score at least the active quality threshold; OR price is below but within 0.65 ATR of prior range high AND relative volume is at least the sustained-volume threshold AND close > open AND close > fast EMA; OR an active upward path has an expansion pulse; OR an active upward path becomes accepted.
Short: Close crosses below the prior range low minus an ATR-scaled buffer AND breakout quality score is at least the weak-path floor AND a new path is allowed; OR the same breakout condition has score at least the active quality threshold; OR price is above but within 0.65 ATR of prior range low AND relative volume is at least the sustained-volume threshold AND close < open AND close < fast EMA; OR an active downward path has an expansion pulse; OR an active downward path becomes accepted.
Exit: Long, an active upward path is invalidated OR close returns below its broken level with smoothed relative volume at or below the weak-volume threshold OR, after the minimum acceptance age, close is not above the acceptance band and smoothed and current relative volume are below their thresholds.
Exit: Short, an active downward path is invalidated OR close returns above its broken level with smoothed relative volume at or below the weak-volume threshold OR, after the minimum acceptance age, close is not below the acceptance band and smoothed and current relative volume are below their thresholds.
