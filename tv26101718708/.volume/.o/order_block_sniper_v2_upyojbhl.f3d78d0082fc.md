Calculation: Displacement thresholds = ATR-scaled minimum body size and minimum body-to-range percentage; their values are auto-calibrated from the 100-bar average wick/body ratio and current ATR position within its 100-bar range.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = bullish displacement bar closing above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish BOS = bearish displacement bar closing below the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Order block = high-to-low range of the nearest opposite-color candle found within a backward lookback from the BOS bar.
Calculation: Sweep = bullish when the low pierces the prior 20-bar low and the close recovers above it; bearish when the high pierces the prior 20-bar high and the close falls below it. Classify by wick depth in ATR units, or as grind when the prior two bars also pierce the level and the current close recovers; require the configured minimum class.
Calculation: Volume vacuum = recent volume average is below a fraction of the longer volume average.
Calculation: Compression = highest high minus lowest low over the compression lookback is below a maximum multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Directional confluence = normalized weighted score of directional efficiency, ATR-scaled momentum, ATR percentile, normalized smoothed candle-direction volume flow, and volume-weighted momentum; add points when momentum and flow agree with the direction and when an active divergence agrees with it, capped at 100.
Calculation: Directional divergence = confirmed lower price pivot with higher oscillator readings for bullish divergence, or confirmed higher price pivot with lower readings for bearish divergence, requiring agreement from the configured minimum number of KP RSI, CCI, and stochastic oscillators; remains active for up to twice the pivot confirmation length.
Calculation: Cross-timeframe gate = count of bullish (close above HMA) or bearish (close below HMA) results on two timeframes, plus matching signs of five-bar close change on three timeframes; use the same HMA length on both timeframes and the same five-bar lookback on all three. Use the latest available timeframe bars, including a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Entry level = bullish zone top minus zone height times the entry fraction; bearish zone bottom plus zone height times the entry fraction.
Calculation: Stop = bullish zone bottom minus ATR times the effective stop multiplier; bearish zone top plus ATR times the effective stop multiplier. The effective multiplier is the base multiplier scaled by the ATR percentile when volatility scaling is enabled.
Calculation: Target 3 = entry price plus three times the distance from entry to stop for a long, or entry price minus three times that distance for a short.
Long: Bullish BOS AND close above EMA(200) AND bullish cross-timeframe gate score meets its minimum AND bullish sweep meets its minimum class AND volume vacuum AND compression AND bullish directional confluence meets its minimum AND order-block grade meets its minimum AND an active bullish order block exists AND low <= its entry level AND close > its bottom AND no trade is open.
Short: Bearish BOS AND close below EMA(200) AND bearish cross-timeframe gate score meets its minimum AND bearish sweep meets its minimum class AND volume vacuum AND compression AND bearish directional confluence meets its minimum AND order-block grade meets its minimum AND an active bearish order block exists AND high >= its entry level AND close < its top AND no trade is open.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= Target 3
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= Target 3
