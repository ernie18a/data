Calculation: Fast moving average = configured moving-average type of close over the shorter lookback, calculated on the current chart timeframe or its configured timeframe; configured-timeframe values use lookahead off.
Calculation: Slow moving average = configured moving-average type of close over the longer lookback, calculated on the current chart timeframe or its configured timeframe; configured-timeframe values use lookahead off.
Long: Fast moving average crosses above slow moving average
Short: Fast moving average crosses below slow moving average
