Calculation: Profile row volume = sum each bar’s volume apportioned to each touched price row by overlap with the bar’s high-low range; estimated buy volume is row volume × (close − low) / (high − low), and estimated sell volume is row volume minus estimated buy volume. For a candidate pivot, use the rolling lookback ending at the pivot bar, with only completed bars.
Calculation: Profile evidence = the highest score among profile rows within the ATR-based search distance of the pivot; each row score is 50% row volume relative to the profile POC + 20% local peak quality versus adjacent rows + 20% directional volume share (buy share for a low pivot, sell share for a high pivot) + 10% proximity to the pivot.
Calculation: Final level = raw pivot + (selected profile row price − raw pivot) × profile pull scaled by profile evidence. Final level score = 50% profile evidence + 20% absorption strength + 15% pivot volume strength + 15% pivot wick rejection. Absorption strength is the maximum around the pivot of the square root of small-body strength × elevated-volume strength; pivot volume strength compares pivot volume with its recent average; wick rejection compares the pivot-side wick with the pivot bar’s range.
Long: A confirmed swing low has profile row strength relative to the POC ≥ minimum node threshold AND profile evidence ≥ minimum evidence threshold AND final level score ≥ minimum score threshold.
Short: A confirmed swing high has profile row strength relative to the POC ≥ minimum node threshold AND profile evidence ≥ minimum evidence threshold AND final level score ≥ minimum score threshold.
Exit: Long, close < active support level − ATR-based break buffer.
Exit: Short, close > active resistance level + ATR-based break buffer.
