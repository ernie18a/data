Calculation: Bullish regular divergence = for an enabled indicator, its value at the signal endpoint is higher than at a prior pivot low while the selected price source is lower than at that pivot; from pivot to endpoint, neither the indicator nor close falls below the straight line between its endpoint values.
Calculation: Bearish regular divergence = for an enabled indicator, its value at the signal endpoint is lower than at a prior pivot high while the selected price source is higher than at that pivot; from pivot to endpoint, neither the indicator nor close rises above the straight line between its endpoint values.
Calculation: Bullish hidden divergence = for an enabled indicator, its value at the signal endpoint is lower than at a prior pivot low while the selected price source is higher than at that pivot; from pivot to endpoint, neither the indicator nor close falls below the straight line between its endpoint values.
Calculation: Bearish hidden divergence = for an enabled indicator, its value at the signal endpoint is higher than at a prior pivot high while the selected price source is lower than at that pivot; from pivot to endpoint, neither the indicator nor close rises above the straight line between its endpoint values.
Long: the configured minimum number of divergences is met AND at least one enabled indicator has bullish regular or bullish hidden divergence
Short: the configured minimum number of divergences is met AND at least one enabled indicator has bearish regular or bearish hidden divergence
Exit: long, low <= entry price × (1 − configured stop percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, high >= entry price × (1 + configured stop percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured take-profit percentage)
