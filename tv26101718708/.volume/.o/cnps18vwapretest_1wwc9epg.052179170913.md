Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 on current-chart bars
Calculation: Higher-timeframe VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming
Long: Close crosses from at or below VWAP to above VWAP within the configured maximum lookback bars AND at least one low from the breakout bar through the current bar is at or below the current VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > configured ATR multiple × ATR) AND (rejection filter disabled OR min(open, close) - low > abs(close - open) × configured wick/body ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configured multiplier × volume moving average)
Short: Close crosses from at or above VWAP to below VWAP within the configured maximum lookback bars AND at least one high from the breakout bar through the current bar is at or above the current VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP) AND (distance filter disabled OR abs(close - VWAP) > configured ATR multiple × ATR) AND (rejection filter disabled OR high - max(open, close) > abs(close - open) × configured wick/body ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= configured multiplier × volume moving average)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - configured stop distance OR high >= entry price + configured target distance OR current bar crosses the configured session-end time
Exit: short, high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR low <= entry price - configured target distance OR current bar crosses the configured session-end time
