Calculation: Classified buy/sell volume = allocate each lower-timeframe bar’s volume as buy when its close exceeds the preceding lower-timeframe close, or sell when it is below; for equal closes, use close versus open, splitting volume evenly if unchanged. For the first lower-timeframe bar, compare its close with the chart bar’s open.
Calculation: Delta % = (classified buy volume − classified sell volume) / total classified volume × 100.
Calculation: Relative volume = current total classified volume / its simple moving average over a lookback.
Calculation: Session delta % = cumulative classified volume delta / cumulative classified volume × 100 for the current configured session, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Day delta % = cumulative classified volume delta / cumulative classified volume × 100 for the current calendar day, including the day still forming.
Calculation: Session CVD = cumulative classified volume delta within the current configured session.
Calculation: Footprint row imbalance = classified volume on one side of a price row divided by opposing classified volume on the adjacent price row; a stacked imbalance is a consecutive run of such rows meeting the configured threshold and row-count minimum. Assign lower-timeframe bar volume to the row nearest its (high + low + close) / 3 price.
Calculation: Session POC = the price row with the greatest total classified volume in the developing configured-session profile. POC migration occurs when it moves by at least the configured row-distance threshold from the last migration reference.
Long: Delta % ≥ extreme-delta threshold AND relative volume ≥ minimum threshold OR negative Delta % ≤ −extreme-delta threshold AND (close > open OR (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≥ absorption close-edge threshold) OR a stacked buy imbalance OR a confirmed session CVD bullish divergence (a lower price pivot low and higher session CVD pivot low than the preceding confirmed pivot low in the same session) OR session delta % ≥ extreme-delta threshold after the session maturity minimum with no missing session pressure data OR day delta % ≥ extreme-delta threshold after the day maturity minimum with no missing day pressure data OR developing session POC migrates up by the configured row-distance threshold.
Short: Delta % ≤ −extreme-delta threshold AND relative volume ≥ minimum threshold OR positive Delta % ≥ extreme-delta threshold AND (close < open OR (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≤ absorption close-edge threshold) OR a stacked sell imbalance OR a confirmed session CVD bearish divergence (a higher price pivot high and lower session CVD pivot high than the preceding confirmed pivot high in the same session) OR session delta % ≤ −extreme-delta threshold after the session maturity minimum with no missing session pressure data OR day delta % ≤ −extreme-delta threshold after the day maturity minimum with no missing day pressure data OR developing session POC migrates down by the configured row-distance threshold.
