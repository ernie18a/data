Calculation: Buyer volume, seller volume, total volume, and delta = from lower-timeframe bars at the selected resolution, or split chart-bar volume geometrically: buyer volume = volume × (close − low)/(high − low), seller volume = volume × (high − close)/(high − low), delta = buyer volume − seller volume; if high = low, split volume equally.
Calculation: CVD pressure = change in the EMA of cumulative delta divided by 14-period ATR.
Calculation: Bollinger basis = selected SMA, EMA, or VWMA of close; upper/lower band = basis ± selected multiplier × standard deviation of close. %B = (close − lower band)/(upper band − lower band).
Calculation: Normalized bandwidth = (current bandwidth − lowest bandwidth)/(highest bandwidth − lowest bandwidth) over the selected squeeze lookback, where bandwidth = (upper band − lower band)/basis; if highest equals lowest, use 0.
Calculation: Effort without result = high > low AND abs(close − open)/(high − low) < selected maximum ratio.
Calculation: Volume spike = current total volume > its SMA over the Bollinger length × selected spike multiplier. High-volume bar = current total volume > its SMA over the Bollinger length × selected volume threshold.
Long: More than max(Bollinger length, squeeze lookback) chart bars of volume data are available AND (close > prior bar's upper band AND Volume spike AND normalized bandwidth is at or below its threshold now or on any of the prior 1–3 chart bars AND delta > 0 AND CVD pressure > selected threshold) OR (%B < 0.05 AND High-volume bar AND (Effort without result OR delta > 0) AND CVD pressure > negative selected confirmation threshold).
Short: More than max(Bollinger length, squeeze lookback) chart bars of volume data are available AND (close < prior bar's lower band AND Volume spike AND normalized bandwidth is at or below its threshold now or on any of the prior 1–3 chart bars AND delta < 0 AND CVD pressure < negative selected threshold) OR (%B > 0.95 AND High-volume bar AND (Effort without result OR delta < 0) AND CVD pressure < selected confirmation threshold) AND no Long condition is true.
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop (signal-bar close − ATR × selected stop multiplier) for an absorption entry; for a breakout entry, low <= trailing stop, initialized at signal-bar close − ATR × selected stop multiplier and updated each bar to the greater of its prior value and close − ATR × selected trailing multiplier.
Exit: Long, absorption entry AND its selected target (current basis or upper band) is above entry price AND high >= target.
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop (signal-bar close + ATR × selected stop multiplier) for an absorption entry; for a breakout entry, high >= trailing stop, initialized at signal-bar close + ATR × selected stop multiplier and updated each bar to the lesser of its prior value and close + ATR × selected trailing multiplier.
Exit: Short, absorption entry AND its selected target (current basis or lower band) is below entry price AND low <= target.
