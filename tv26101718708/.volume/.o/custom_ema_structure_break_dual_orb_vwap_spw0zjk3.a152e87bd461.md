Long: Long entry is initially enabled or a bar has closed above the fast EMA since the previous long signal AND close > trend EMA AND candle body > ATR × strength threshold AND ((close > mid EMA AND prior close <= mid EMA) OR (close > trend EMA AND prior close <= trend EMA) OR (close > slow EMA AND prior close <= slow EMA)); mid, trend, and slow EMA lookbacks are progressively longer than the fast EMA. Disable after a long signal; re-enable when close > fast EMA.
Short: Short entry is initially enabled or a bar has closed below the fast EMA since the previous short signal AND close < trend EMA AND candle body > ATR × strength threshold AND ((close < mid EMA AND prior close >= mid EMA) OR (close < trend EMA AND prior close >= trend EMA) OR (close < slow EMA AND prior close >= slow EMA)); mid, trend, and slow EMA lookbacks are progressively longer than the fast EMA. Disable after a short signal; re-enable when close < fast EMA.
Exit: Long, close < fast EMA AND prior close >= fast EMA
Exit: Short, close > fast EMA AND prior close <= fast EMA
