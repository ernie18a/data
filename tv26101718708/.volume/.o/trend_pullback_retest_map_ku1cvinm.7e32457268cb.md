Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the current bar and prior bars.
Calculation: Trend EMA, fast EMA, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages, with trend and slow lengths longer than fast length.
Calculation: Trend slope = absolute difference between trend EMA and its value 5 bars earlier, divided by ATR; trend gap = absolute difference between fast and trend EMAs, divided by ATR.
Calculation: Trend progress = (current close − lowest low over the progress lookback) / ATR for uptrend; (highest high over that lookback − current close) / ATR for downtrend.
Calculation: Pullback depth = (highest high over the pullback lookback − current low) / ATR for uptrend; (current high − lowest low over that lookback) / ATR for downtrend.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / simple moving average of volume; close position = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100; bearish close position = (high − close) / (high − low) × 100.
Calculation: SET score = sum of clipped components: trend slope / (2 × minimum slope) × 18, trend gap / (2 × minimum gap) × 16, [22 − |pullback depth − midpoint of the allowed depth range| / width of that range × 22], close position / 100 × 20, volume component × 14, and setup range / (1.6 × minimum setup range) × 10. Clip each component to its stated point range (0–18, 0–16, 0–22, 0–20, 0–14, 0–10); clip total to 0–100. Volume component is (maximum pullback volume ratio − volume ratio + 0.45) / 0.85 clipped to 0–1 when the volume filter is enabled; otherwise 1. Setup range is highest high minus lowest low over the setup range lookback, divided by ATR.
Calculation: RT score = sum of clipped components: trend slope / (2 × minimum slope) × 16, trend gap / (2 × minimum gap) × 14, close position / 100 × 24, volume ratio / (1.5 × minimum retest volume ratio) × 18, and nonnegative trigger break distance / (trigger break threshold + 0.15) × 28. Clip components to 0–16, 0–14, 0–24, 0–18, 0–28 respectively and total to 0–100; use bearish close position and bearish trigger break distance for shorts. When the volume filter is disabled, the volume component is 18.
Long: Uptrend (fast EMA > trend EMA, trend EMA rising versus prior bar, trend slope and fast/trend gap meet their minimums, upward trend progress meets its minimum, and slow regime passes: trend EMA and close are no farther than the ATR buffer below slow EMA and slow EMA is rising versus 3 bars earlier) AND pullback hold (low ≥ trend EMA − hold buffer × ATR and close ≥ trend EMA) AND pullback into fast EMA area (low ≤ fast EMA + touch buffer × ATR and low ≥ trend EMA − hold buffer × ATR) AND pullback depth is within its minimum and maximum AND bullish reclaim (close > open, close > fast EMA, close ≥ prior close, close position ≥ setup threshold) AND volume ratio ≤ pullback maximum AND setup filters pass (trend age ≥ minimum; setup range ≥ minimum; current candle range / ATR ≤ maximum; fast EMA rising; chop range / ATR ≥ minimum; no hard-extension block; and structure bias: close > trend EMA + minimum close gap × ATR, recent impulse passes, and no large bearish body) AND SET score ≥ minimum AND no hard-extension context; with no active long cycle and outside SET cooldown.
Long: After a long SET, once the minimum bars have elapsed and before the cycle times out: uptrend AND retest conditions pass (either close > trigger line + trigger buffer × ATR, or momentum retest: close > prior high + prior-bar buffer × ATR, close > fast EMA and open, close position ≥ retest threshold, close > highest high of the prior RT structure lookback, and structure bias passes) AND post-extension block/reset conditions pass AND volume ratio ≥ minimum retest ratio AND RT score ≥ minimum AND no hard-extension context; alternatively, direct RT requires no active long cycle, an uptrend, a pull into the fast EMA area within the direct pullback lookback, the same momentum retest and setup filters, post-extension block/reset conditions, and direct RT cooldown elapsed.
Long: Weak-pullback marker when uptrend AND pullback depth ≥ deep-pullback threshold AND close < fast EMA; outside weak-marker cooldown.
Short: Downtrend (fast EMA < trend EMA, trend EMA falling versus prior bar, trend slope and fast/trend gap meet their minimums, downward trend progress meets its minimum, and slow regime passes: trend EMA and close are no farther than the ATR buffer above slow EMA and slow EMA is falling versus 3 bars earlier) AND pullback hold (high ≤ trend EMA + hold buffer × ATR and close ≤ trend EMA) AND pullback into fast EMA area (high ≥ fast EMA − touch buffer × ATR and high ≤ trend EMA + hold buffer × ATR) AND pullback depth is within its minimum and maximum AND bearish reclaim (close < open, close < fast EMA, close ≤ prior close, bearish close position ≥ setup threshold) AND volume ratio ≤ pullback maximum AND setup filters pass (trend age ≥ minimum; setup range ≥ minimum; current candle range / ATR ≤ maximum; fast EMA falling; chop range / ATR ≥ minimum; no hard-extension block; and structure bias: close < trend EMA − minimum close gap × ATR, recent impulse passes, and no large bullish body) AND SET score ≥ minimum AND no hard-extension context; with no active short cycle and outside SET cooldown.
Short: After a short SET, once the minimum bars have elapsed and before the cycle times out: downtrend AND retest conditions pass (either close < trigger line − trigger buffer × ATR, or momentum retest: close < prior low − prior-bar buffer × ATR, close < fast EMA and open, bearish close position ≥ retest threshold, close < lowest low of the prior RT structure lookback, and structure bias passes) AND post-extension block/reset conditions pass AND volume ratio ≥ minimum retest ratio AND RT score ≥ minimum AND no hard-extension context; alternatively, direct RT requires no active short cycle, a downtrend, a pull into the fast EMA area within the direct pullback lookback, the same momentum retest and setup filters, post-extension block/reset conditions, and direct RT cooldown elapsed.
Short: Weak-pullback marker when downtrend AND pullback depth ≥ deep-pullback threshold AND close > fast EMA; outside weak-marker cooldown.
Exit: Long, active long SET cycle AND (early failure: bearish candle, body / ATR ≥ early-fail threshold, close position ≤ failure threshold, (close < fast EMA OR close < setup low − half the invalidation buffer × ATR), and volume ratio ≥ early-fail minimum; OR hard failure: close < trend EMA − hold buffer × ATR OR close < setup low − invalidation buffer × ATR OR pullback depth ≥ deep-pullback threshold); outside FAIL cooldown.
Exit: Short, active short SET cycle AND (early failure: bullish candle, body / ATR ≥ early-fail threshold, bearish close position ≤ failure threshold, (close > fast EMA OR close > setup high + half the invalidation buffer × ATR), and volume ratio ≥ early-fail minimum; OR hard failure: close > trend EMA + hold buffer × ATR OR close > setup high + invalidation buffer × ATR OR pullback depth ≥ deep-pullback threshold); outside FAIL cooldown.
