Calculation: Estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low); if high = low, use volume when close ≥ open, otherwise 0
Calculation: Delta = 2 × estimated buy volume − volume
Calculation: Gap probability = 10 × min(100, 2 × (volume > 20-bar average volume × volume-spike multiplier) + 3 × (delta > volume × delta-strength multiplier) + 2 × (close > highest high of the previous 10 bars) + (9-period EMA > 21-period EMA) + (9-period EMA on the configured higher timeframe > 21-period EMA on that timeframe) + (14-period RSI > 55))
Calculation: Daily strength = (daily close − daily open) / daily open ≥ 2% AND (daily close − daily low) / (daily high − daily low) > 0.8
Calculation: Strong daily delta = daily delta > daily volume × 0.08, where daily delta is twice the estimated daily buy volume minus daily volume; estimate daily buy volume using the daily OHLCV fields as for estimated buy volume
Calculation: Limit pressure = (close − low) / (high − low) > 0.80 AND delta > volume × 0.12 AND volume > 20-bar average volume × 1.4 AND close > highest high of the previous 10 bars; when high = low, the first condition is close ≥ open
Calculation: Order book ratio = sum of estimated sell volume over the last 5 bars / sum of estimated buy volume over the last 5 bars; estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume; use 999 when summed buy volume is zero
Calculation: Order book score = 100 when order book ratio < 1; 100 − 20 × (ratio − 1) when ratio < 2; 80 − 20 × (ratio − 2) when ratio < 3; otherwise max(0, 60 − 10 × (ratio − 3))
Calculation: Market pressure score = 0.30 × order book score + 0.25 × min(abs(delta) / volume × 500, 100) + 0.20 × max(0, min((volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) × 50, 100)) + 0.15 × (close − low) / (high − low) × 100 + 0.10 × (50 when 9-period EMA > 21-period EMA, plus 50 when higher-timeframe 9-period EMA > 21-period EMA); use 0 for delta score when volume is zero, 1 for volume ratio when average volume is zero, and 50 for price action score when high = low
Long: no active setup AND time is within the configured end-of-day window AND gap probability ≥ configured minimum AND delta > 0 AND (daily strength AND strong daily delta OR limit pressure) AND weekday is not Thursday AND daily volume ≥ configured minimum AND (daily high − daily low) / daily open ≥ configured minimum range AND previous daily close is nonzero and (daily open − previous daily close) / previous daily close ≤ 1% AND order book ratio < configured maximum AND market pressure score ≥ configured minimum
Exit: long, time is intraday and hour = 10 and minute < 30 and the setup was active on the previous bar
