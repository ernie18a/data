Calculation: Body = absolute difference between open and close; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the rejection-block timeframe, using its last completed bar
Calculation: Rejection-block levels = wick-to-body and wick-to-ATR comparisons, using the configured minimum ratios
Calculation: NY Fib levels = pivot (session high + session low + session close) / 3, plus and minus each enabled configured Fibonacci fraction of the range (session high − session low); use the current, still-forming NY session
Calculation: 1-hour Fib levels = pivot (high + low + close) / 3, plus and minus each enabled configured Fibonacci fraction of the range (high − low); use the last completed 1-hour bar
Calculation: Session volume profile = distribute each bar’s volume equally among price bins touched by its low-to-high range; POC is the highest-volume bin’s center, and VAH/VAL are the bounds reached by expanding from POC toward the higher-volume adjacent bin until the configured value-area percentage is reached; use the latest completed profile for the configured session
Calculation: Global midpoint = middle level after sorting all included NY Fib levels, 1-hour Fib levels, and latest completed profile POC/VAH/VAL; use the upper middle level when there is an even count
Long: close > global midpoint OR the last completed higher-timeframe candle has a nonzero body, a lower wick greater than the configured wick-to-body ratio times body, and a lower wick greater than the configured minimum ATR multiple times ATR; evaluate the rejection-block condition when a higher-timeframe bar closes and the rejection-block timeframe is at least the chart timeframe
Short: close <= global midpoint OR the last completed higher-timeframe candle has a nonzero body, an upper wick greater than the configured wick-to-body ratio times body, and an upper wick greater than the configured minimum ATR multiple times ATR; evaluate the rejection-block condition when a higher-timeframe bar closes and the rejection-block timeframe is at least the chart timeframe
