Calculation: Confirmed 3-candle swing high = the middle bar’s high when it exceeds the highs of the immediately preceding and following bars; confirmed on the following bar. Swing low = the middle bar’s low when it is below both neighboring lows; confirmed on the following bar.
Calculation: Bearish QM break level = the lowest low from the swept swing high’s bar through the bar before the sweep. Bullish QM break level = the highest high from the swept swing low’s bar through the bar before the sweep.
Calculation: Active range high and low = the maximum and minimum, respectively, of the sweep and break levels of the latest two confirmed QMs of opposite directions, if they formed within the range gap.
Long: After the latest confirmed swing low is swept (low < swing low and close >= swing low), close above the bullish QM break level within the QM break lookback, with no intervening close below the swept swing low; OR close above the active range high.
Short: After the latest confirmed swing high is swept (high > swing high and close <= swing high), close below the bearish QM break level within the QM break lookback, with no intervening close above the swept swing high; OR close below the active range low.
