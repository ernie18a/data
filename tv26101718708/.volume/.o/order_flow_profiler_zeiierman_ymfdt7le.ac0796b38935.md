Calculation: Profile row buy/sell volume = For the selected lookback of chart bars, including the current forming bar, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into equal price cells. For each positive-volume bar, estimate buy share as 0.5 + 0.43 × [0.48 × (2 × (close−low)/(high−low)−1) + 0.32 × (close−open)/(high−low) + 0.20 × (lower wick−upper wick)/(high−low)], clamping close position to [0,1], body to [−1,1], and share to [0.025,0.975]; use 0.5 when the range is zero. Buy volume is volume × share; sell volume is the remainder. Distribute each side’s volume over the cells touched by the bar, proportional to touched-range fraction × [1 + 2.6 × (1−min(|cell midpoint−side center|/(high−low),1))^concentration], normalizing weights across touched cells. Buy center = clamp(0.50×close+0.32×high+0.18×open, low, high); sell center = clamp(0.50×close+0.32×low+0.18×open, low, high). For zero-range bars, assign volume to the cell containing the close.
Calculation: Control cell = cell with the greatest combined buy and sell volume.
Calculation: Buy pressure = A cell has buy pressure when its combined volume is at least the minimum activity fraction of the busiest cell, the sell volume in the cell immediately below is positive, and its buy volume exceeds that sell volume times the pressure ratio. Sell pressure is symmetric, comparing sell volume in a cell to positive buy volume immediately above. If both qualify in one cell, use the side with the larger ratio; ties resolve to buy. Only the nearest qualifying cell above and below the control cell is flagged.
Calculation: Segment buy/sell volume = Sum of profile row buy/sell volumes within each of the equal price segments spanning the profile range. A segment is BUY when buy volume exceeds sell volume and max(buy,sell)/max(min(buy,sell), segment total×0.000001) is at least the balance threshold; it is SELL under the symmetric condition.
Long: A buy pressure flag exists OR any segment is BUY.
Short: A sell pressure flag exists OR any segment is SELL.
