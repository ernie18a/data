Calculation: ATR-Renko open and close = open and close of the last completed ATR-based Renko bar at the configured Renko resolution.
Calculation: Prior swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured number of preceding chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: MTF bullish/bearish score = count of the three configured higher timeframes where current close is above/below the 50-period SMA of that timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick cluster = at least 3 consecutive bars with an upper/lower wick whose highs/lows are within 0.1 ATR of the cluster’s anchor; reset when a bar has no such wick or falls outside that distance.
Calculation: Renko volume average = moving average of volume from completed ATR-Renko bars over the configured lookback.
Long: ATR-Renko close > ATR-Renko open OR (ATR-Renko close > ATR-Renko open AND prior bar was not bullish AND (MTF filter disabled OR MTF bullish score >= 2) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within the configured UTC session)) OR low < prior swing low AND close > prior swing low AND close > open OR lower wick cluster OR (first bullish bar AND ATR-Renko volume <= Renko volume average)
Short: ATR-Renko close < ATR-Renko open OR (ATR-Renko close < ATR-Renko open AND prior bar was not bearish AND (MTF filter disabled OR MTF bearish score >= 2) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within the configured UTC session)) OR high > prior swing high AND close < prior swing high AND close < open OR upper wick cluster OR (first bearish bar AND ATR-Renko volume <= Renko volume average)
