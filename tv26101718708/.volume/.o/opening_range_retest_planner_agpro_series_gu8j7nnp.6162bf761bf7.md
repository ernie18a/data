Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of bars in the configured opening-range window; lock these values when that window ends. The range is valid when its width exceeds the minimum valid range.
Calculation: Retest score = bounded 0–100 weighted sum of pocket-touch quality, close response, time since breakout, relative volume, opening-range volatility fit, and available target room. Relative volume compares current volume with its recent average; volatility fit compares opening-range width with ATR; target room measures distance to the first projected target in ATR units.
Long: opening range is valid and locked AND tracking session is active AND no breakout has occurred AND close > opening range high + ATR-scaled breakout buffer; then, before the retest window expires and before the retest is resolved, price touches the pocket around the broken high AND close >= opening range high AND retest score >= configured minimum.
Short: opening range is valid and locked AND tracking session is active AND no breakout has occurred AND close < opening range low − ATR-scaled breakout buffer; then, before the retest window expires and before the retest is resolved, price touches the pocket around the broken low AND close <= opening range low AND retest score >= configured minimum.
