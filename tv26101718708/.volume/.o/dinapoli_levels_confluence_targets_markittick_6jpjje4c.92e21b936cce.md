Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high/low that is strictly above/below the Pivot Left preceding bars and at least as high/low as the Pivot Right following bars; it is confirmed after those following bars. Keep alternating pivots; replace the latest pivot only with a more extreme pivot on the same side.
Calculation: A, B, C = the third, second, and latest pivots; A2 = the fifth latest pivot. Bull structure = A low, B high, C low; bear structure = A high, B low, C high.
Calculation: Swing = B − A; retracement = (B − C) / (B − A).
Calculation: Primary nodes = B − 0.382 × swing and B − 0.618 × swing. Entry = the 61.8% node if retracement ≥ 0.618, otherwise the 38.2% node.
Calculation: Confluence exists when the minimum distance among all four pairings of primary and secondary 38.2%/61.8% nodes is ≤ the configured ATR tolerance; secondary nodes use swing B − A2.
Calculation: Stop = C − ATR × configured stop buffer for a long, or C + ATR × configured stop buffer for a short. XOP = C + 1.618 × swing.
Long: Bull structure AND retracement ≥ 0.382 AND retracement ≤ configured maximum AND swing ≥ ATR × configured minimum AND (DMA filter disabled OR close > the DMA-length SMA of close from the configured displacement earlier) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close > the selected moving average of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ its configured minimum) AND (HTF filter disabled OR no prior completed HTF close exists OR close > the last completed HTF close) AND (confluence requirement disabled OR confluence exists) AND signal bar is confirmed AND C has not already fired a signal.
Short: Bear structure AND retracement ≥ 0.382 AND retracement ≤ configured maximum AND |swing| ≥ ATR × configured minimum AND (DMA filter disabled OR close < the DMA-length SMA of close from the configured displacement earlier) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close < the selected moving average of close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ its configured minimum) AND (HTF filter disabled OR no prior completed HTF close exists OR close < the last completed HTF close) AND (confluence requirement disabled OR confluence exists) AND signal bar is confirmed AND C has not already fired a signal.
Exit: long, low ≤ stored stop OR high ≥ stored XOP OR an opposite short signal fires while the long trade is open.
Exit: short, high ≥ stored stop OR low ≤ stored XOP OR an opposite long signal fires while the short trade is open.
