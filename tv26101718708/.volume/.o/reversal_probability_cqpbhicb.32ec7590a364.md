Calculation: Bullish probability = 100 × the number of enabled bullish factors met divided by the number of enabled factors, rounded to the nearest integer; bullish factors are RSI below its oversold threshold, volume above 1.5 times its 20-bar average with close > open, close below the midpoint of the zone-lookback highest high and lowest low, lower wick at least the configured fraction of the bar's high-low range, and close above the EMA.
Calculation: Bearish probability = 100 × the number of enabled bearish factors met divided by the number of enabled factors, rounded to the nearest integer; bearish factors are RSI above its overbought threshold, volume above 1.5 times its 20-bar average with close < open, close above the midpoint of the zone-lookback highest high and lowest low, upper wick at least the configured fraction of the bar's high-low range, and close below the EMA.
Long: After a close below every low in the preceding lookback bars, close above that trigger bar's high within the confirmation window AND (probability filter disabled OR bullish probability >= minimum probability threshold)
Short: After a close above every high in the preceding lookback bars, close below that trigger bar's low within the confirmation window AND (probability filter disabled OR bearish probability >= minimum probability threshold)
