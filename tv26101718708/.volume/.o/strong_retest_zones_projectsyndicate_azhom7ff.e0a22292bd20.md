Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the configured number of bars to its left and no higher than the lows of the configured number of bars to its right; usable after those right-side bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is above the highs of the configured number of bars to its left and no lower than the highs of the configured number of bars to its right; usable after those right-side bars complete
Calculation: Retest-zone boundaries = pivot price ± half the configured zone height when normalized, otherwise pivot price ± the configured zone height on the inward side; zone height is configured as an ATR multiple or a percentage of price
Calculation: Zone strength = 10 × the weighted average of touch count capped at its configured full-score count, average zone volume divided by the volume moving average, and average rejection-wick depth divided by ATR; apply configured idle-age decay based on bars since the last touch
Long: For an active bearish pivot-high zone, low ≤ zone top AND close > zone top AND the marker-spacing rule allows a mark AND (strong-only filtering is off OR zone strength calculated with zero idle age ≥ the configured threshold); OR, for an active bearish zone already broken above its top, low ≤ zone top AND close > zone top AND the marker-spacing rule allows a mark AND (strong-only filtering is off OR zone strength calculated with zero idle age ≥ the configured threshold)
Short: For an active bullish pivot-low zone, low ≤ zone top AND close > zone top AND the marker-spacing rule allows a mark AND (strong-only filtering is off OR zone strength calculated with zero idle age ≥ the configured threshold); OR, for an active bearish pivot-high zone, high ≥ zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND the marker-spacing rule allows a mark AND (strong-only filtering is off OR zone strength calculated with zero idle age ≥ the configured threshold); OR, for an active bullish zone already broken below its bottom, high ≥ zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND the marker-spacing rule allows a mark AND (strong-only filtering is off OR zone strength calculated with zero idle age ≥ the configured threshold)
