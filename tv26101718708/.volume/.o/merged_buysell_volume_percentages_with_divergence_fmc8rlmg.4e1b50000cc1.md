Calculation: Session buy volume percentage = 100 × cumulative session volume × (session close − session low) ÷ (session high − session low) ÷ cumulative session volume; use the current forming regular or overnight session aggregated from input bars, and set to 0 when session high equals session low or total volume is not positive.
Calculation: Session sell volume percentage = 100 × cumulative session volume × (session high − session close) ÷ (session high − session low) ÷ cumulative session volume; use the current forming regular or overnight session aggregated from input bars, and set to 0 when session high equals session low or total volume is not positive.
Long: During the regular or overnight session, session buy volume percentage > session sell volume percentage OR (current session buy volume percentage > current session sell volume percentage AND previous session buy volume percentage ≤ previous session sell volume percentage AND both percentages > 1 AND session total volume > 0)
