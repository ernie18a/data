Calculation: EMA structure score = +2 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA and close > all three; +1 if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA or close > slow EMA; -2 if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA and close < all three; -1 if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA or close < slow EMA; otherwise 0. Fast, mid, and slow EMAs have progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume added when close rises, subtracted when close falls, and unchanged otherwise.
Calculation: OBV-flow score = +1 when OBV > its SMA and the SMA is rising; -1 when OBV <= its SMA and the SMA is falling; otherwise 0. The SMA slope compares its current value with its value at an earlier lookback.
Calculation: Supertrend score = +1 when its line is below price; otherwise -1.
Calculation: Timeframe score = EMA structure score + Supertrend score + OBV-flow score.
Long: Fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA OR close > slow EMA OR OBV > its SMA OR (OBV <= its SMA AND its SMA is rising) OR Supertrend flips bullish OR mid EMA crosses above slow EMA OR timeframe score >= 1 OR at least 3 of 4 configured timeframe scores are > 0 OR the number of positive configured timeframe scores exceeds the number of negative scores. For the configured timeframes, use the latest available bar: the bar still forming in realtime and the last completed bar on historical bars.
Short: Fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA OR close < slow EMA OR (OBV <= its SMA AND its SMA is flat or falling) OR Supertrend flips bearish OR mid EMA crosses below slow EMA OR timeframe score <= -1 OR at least 3 of 4 configured timeframe scores are < 0 OR the number of negative configured timeframe scores exceeds the number of positive scores. For the configured timeframes, use the latest available bar: the bar still forming in realtime and the last completed bar on historical bars.
