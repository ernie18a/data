Calculation: Trend tracker = WMA of true range × ATR multiplier from the current high/low; in an uptrend, ratchet the lower line upward and flip down when close falls below it, resetting the line above the current high; in a downtrend, ratchet the upper line downward and flip up when close rises above it, resetting below the current low.
Calculation: Equilibrium = Hull moving average of linear regression of close.
Calculation: Trend score = percentage of enabled checks voting bullish (+1) or bearish (−1): tracker direction, close above/below equilibrium, equilibrium rising/falling, RSI above/below 50, RSI above/below its simple moving average, and +DI above/below −DI; divide the vote sum by the number of enabled checks.
Calculation: Squeeze zone = a consecutive required count of bars where the highest high minus lowest low over a lookback is no greater than ATR times a width multiplier; zone bounds are that lookback's highest high and lowest low.
Long: (trend tracker flips up AND (trend score filter disabled OR trend score ≥ minimum) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≥ long minimum) AND (equilibrium filter disabled OR close > equilibrium) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable/zero OR volume > its simple moving average × multiplier) AND (MTF filter disabled OR number of enabled higher-timeframe trackers up ≥ minimum agreement) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown) AND (zone filter off OR zone blocking disabled and no active zone OR matching up-breakout occurred within its window) AND (date filter disabled OR datetime within date range) AND long trading enabled) OR (tracker is up AND delayed entries enabled AND no signal in this trend yet AND within delayed-entry window AND all applicable filters above pass); alternatively, RSI[1] < RSI[2] AND RSI > RSI[1] AND RSI[1] ≤ oversold level (bullish exhaustion marking), or a live squeeze zone exists AND close > its upper bound (up-breakout marking).
Short: (trend tracker flips down AND (trend score filter disabled OR trend score ≤ negative minimum) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI ≤ short maximum) AND (equilibrium filter disabled OR close < equilibrium) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable/zero OR volume > its simple moving average × multiplier) AND (MTF filter disabled OR number of enabled higher-timeframe trackers down ≥ minimum agreement) AND (cooldown disabled OR no prior signal OR bars since prior signal ≥ cooldown) AND (zone filter off OR zone blocking disabled and no active zone OR matching down-breakout occurred within its window) AND (date filter disabled OR datetime within date range) AND short trading enabled) OR (tracker is down AND delayed entries enabled AND no signal in this trend yet AND within delayed-entry window AND all applicable filters above pass); alternatively, RSI[1] > RSI[2] AND RSI < RSI[1] AND RSI[1] ≥ overbought level (bearish exhaustion marking), or a live squeeze zone exists AND close < its lower bound (down-breakout marking).
Exit: Long, close ≤ active stop OR close ≥ an enabled take-profit target OR opposite trend flip when enabled OR bars in trade ≥ maximum when enabled OR datetime > backtest end when date limit is enabled.
Exit: Short, close ≥ active stop OR close ≤ an enabled take-profit target OR opposite trend flip when enabled OR bars in trade ≥ maximum when enabled OR datetime > backtest end when date limit is enabled.
