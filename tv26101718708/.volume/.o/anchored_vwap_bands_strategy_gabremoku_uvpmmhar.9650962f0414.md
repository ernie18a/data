Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of OHLC4 from the selected anchor bar through the current bar; the anchor is selected by time or bars back.
Calculation: First upper/lower deviation bands = Anchored VWAP plus/minus the selected deviation multiplied by the first-band multiplier; deviation is either volume-weighted standard deviation of OHLC4 since the anchor or rolling standard deviation of OHLC4.
Long: (low <= Anchored VWAP + retest tolerance AND close > Anchored VWAP AND previous close <= previous Anchored VWAP) OR (low <= first lower deviation band AND close > first lower deviation band), AND (close > open if close confirmation is enabled), AND (Anchored VWAP is rising over the selected lookback if the slope filter is enabled).
Long: low <= first lower deviation band + band tolerance AND (close > open OR close >= low + 0.30 * (high - low)).
Short: (high >= Anchored VWAP - retest tolerance AND close < Anchored VWAP AND previous close >= previous Anchored VWAP) OR (high >= first upper deviation band AND close < first upper deviation band), AND (close < open if close confirmation is enabled), AND (Anchored VWAP is falling over the selected lookback if the slope filter is enabled).
