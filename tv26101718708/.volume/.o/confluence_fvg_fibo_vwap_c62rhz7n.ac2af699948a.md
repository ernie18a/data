Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, with the gap size as a percentage of that earlier high meeting a minimum threshold
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, with the gap size as a percentage of the low two bars earlier meeting a minimum threshold
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement levels = levels 0.382 and 0.618 of the range between the latest confirmed high and low pivots on the selected higher timeframe, measured from the pivot low
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative typical price (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by volume, reset at each day boundary
Long: Bullish FVG AND close between the 0.382 and 0.618 Fibonacci levels inclusive AND latest high pivot occurred after latest low pivot AND close > VWAP
Short: Bearish FVG AND close between the 0.382 and 0.618 Fibonacci levels inclusive AND latest low pivot occurred after latest high pivot AND close < VWAP
