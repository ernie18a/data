Calculation: Advance mean = Episode-anchored mean HLC3 of the seed and subsequent bars whose close-to-close change agrees with the seed direction; volume-weighted by positive volume, or equal-weighted for the whole episode when equal weighting is selected or an included bar has missing/nonpositive volume.
Calculation: Retracement mean = Episode-anchored mean HLC3 of subsequent bars whose close-to-close change opposes the seed direction, using the same weighting mode as the advance mean; unchanged closes are excluded.
Calculation: Outer mean = greater of the advance and retracement means for a long setup; lesser for a short setup.
Calculation: Fresh impulse = Current bar is confirmed and valid; close breaks the preceding rolling high/low range; directional body is at least the impulse threshold times ATR(14) from the preceding bar; close is in the outer quarter of the candle; and the preceding bar did not qualify as the same directional impulse.
Calculation: Prepared pullback = Since the latest close beyond the previous running directional extreme, adverse close-to-close bars meet the minimum count, pullback depth from its peak/trough meets the minimum depth relative to the frozen seed ATR, and close is at or before the outer mean buffered in the seed direction. Readiness remains armed until a new directional extreme close resets it.
Calculation: Recovery stop distance = Seed-direction distance from the recovery close to the signal-inclusive pullback extreme padded against the seed direction by the stop-padding fraction of frozen seed ATR.
Long: Flat and outside cooldown, with no active setup or plan; fresh long impulse seeds setup; before setup expiry and without origin invalidation, prepared pullback is armed; a later confirmed close is above the prior-bar outer mean plus the recovery buffer and previous high; chase beyond that prior outer mean is within its ATR limit; and recovery stop distance is positive and within its ATR limit.
Short: Flat and outside cooldown, with no active setup or plan; fresh short impulse seeds setup; before setup expiry and without origin invalidation, prepared pullback is armed; a later confirmed close is below the prior-bar outer mean minus the recovery buffer and previous low; chase beyond that prior outer mean is within its ATR limit; and recovery stop distance is positive and within its ATR limit.
