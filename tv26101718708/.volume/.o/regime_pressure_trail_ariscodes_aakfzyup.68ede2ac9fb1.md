Calculation: ATR regime ratio = current ATR / its longer-lookback simple moving average; classify LOW below the low threshold, HIGH above the high threshold, otherwise MEDIUM.
Calculation: Trail = ATR Chandelier level using current ATR times the regime-specific multiplier; ratchet the bullish level upward and bearish level downward, and flip bullish when close crosses above the prior bearish trail or bearish when close crosses below the prior bullish trail.
Calculation: Trend score = 100 × (0.40 × min(|14-period RSI − RSI from the longer slope lookback| / 20, 1) + 0.35 × min(max(volume / longer-lookback volume SMA − 1, 0) / 1.5, 1) + 0.25 × min(max(current ATR / shorter-lookback ATR SMA − 1, 0) / 0.5, 1)).
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of volume when close ≥ open and negative volume otherwise.
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × (0.40 × |bar delta / volume| + 0.35 × min(max(volume / pressure-volume SMA − 1, 0) / 1.5, 1) + 0.25 × min(|close − open| / (high − low), 1)); use zero for the body fraction when high = low.
Calculation: Pressure RSI = RSI of close using the pressure engine's RSI lookback.
Long: Flat, cooldown complete, confirmed bar, trail engine enabled and regime-armed, bullish trail flip, and trend score ≥ its minimum; OR flat, cooldown complete, confirmed bar, pressure engine enabled and regime-armed, cumulative delta crosses above its moving average, pressure score ≥ its minimum, pressure RSI < its overbought threshold, and close ≥ open. In confluence mode require both engine conditions; regime-arms mode arms trail in MEDIUM/HIGH and pressure in MEDIUM/LOW; either-fires mode imposes no regime gate.
Short: Flat, cooldown complete, confirmed bar, trail engine enabled and regime-armed, bearish trail flip, and trend score ≥ its minimum; OR flat, cooldown complete, confirmed bar, pressure engine enabled and regime-armed, cumulative delta crosses below its moving average, pressure score ≥ its minimum, pressure RSI > its oversold threshold, and close < open. In confluence mode require both engine conditions; regime-arms mode arms trail in MEDIUM/HIGH and pressure in MEDIUM/LOW; either-fires mode imposes no regime gate.
Exit: Long, high ≥ take-profit level OR low ≤ stop level OR bars since entry ≥ maximum hold bars OR (trail-flip exit enabled AND bearish trail flip).
Exit: Short, low ≤ take-profit level OR high ≥ stop level OR bars since entry ≥ maximum hold bars OR (trail-flip exit enabled AND bullish trail flip).
Calculation: Trail-engine entry levels = entry close ± current ATR × target ATR multiplier for take profit (add for long, subtract for short); stop is the trail level at entry. Pressure-engine entry levels = entry close adjusted by its target and stop percentages (take profit in the position direction, stop in the opposite direction). Confluence uses trail-engine levels when trend score ≥ pressure score, otherwise pressure-engine levels.
