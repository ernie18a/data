Calculation: candle fingerprint = [absolute(open−close)/range, (high−max(open,close))/range, (min(open,close)−low)/range, (min(open,close)−low)/range, min(volume/average volume, 3)/3, min(range/ATR, 2)/2, (clamp((close−EMA)/ATR, −2, 2)+2)/4, candle context], where range=high−low; average volume is a moving average of volume; candle context is 0 if the bar is inside the previous bar, 1 if it is outside, otherwise 0.5. Use the current bar and each historical candidate bar, with each candidate’s context compared to its preceding bar.
Calculation: similarity score = 100 × (1 − Euclidean distance between the current and candidate fingerprints / square root of the number of fingerprint dimensions).
Calculation: best qualifying historical match = the candidate with the highest similarity score among the lookback candidates whose absolute outcome is at least the minimum outcome move; candidate bars are at least the forward-window length plus two bars before the current bar. For each candidate, outcome compares (the high of the bar exactly the forward-window length later − candidate close)/candidate ATR with (candidate close − the low of that later bar)/candidate ATR; outcome is the larger value if the upward move is greater, otherwise the negative of the downward move.
Long: best qualifying historical match similarity score ≥ minimum score AND outcome > 0 AND bars since the last signal ≥ cooldown
Short: best qualifying historical match similarity score ≥ minimum score AND outcome ≤ 0 AND bars since the last signal ≥ cooldown
