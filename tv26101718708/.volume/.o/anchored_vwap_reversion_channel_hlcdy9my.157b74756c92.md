Calculation: Anchored regression center and residual σ = volume-weighted polynomial regression of typical price from the most recent confirmed swing high or low, whichever is more recent; use the center’s fitted residual standard deviation. If neither pivot exists, use a rolling anchor.
Calculation: Outer bands = anchored regression center ± outer-band multiplier × residual σ.
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of k-bar log returns divided by k times the variance of one-bar log returns, over the same sample window.
Calculation: Reversion correlation = rolling correlation between the prior bar’s close minus its rolling close mean and the current close-to-close return.
Calculation: Multi-factor consensus = count enabled factors whose rolling z-score reaches the shared extreme threshold: close minus session VWAP; high-low range signed by the direction of the lookback price change; price efficiency ratio; price minus reference close; signed Parabolic SAR extension divided by ATR; RSI or Stoch RSI; linear-regression deviation divided by ATR; KST; and cumulative volume delta minus its EMA. Use the same normalization lookback and extreme threshold for all factors; require a timeframe-adaptive or configured minimum count. A high-side consensus is overbought; a low-side consensus is oversold.
Long: low ≤ lower outer band AND (close > lower outer band OR a bullish divergence printed within the configured recency window OR a fresh low sweep OR an oversold multi-factor consensus) AND the configured bar cooldown has elapsed AND (variance ratio ≤ 1 minus its neutral band OR reversion correlation ≤ negative its threshold).
Long: a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low, its RSI or Stoch RSI is higher than at the preceding pivot, and the price pivot reaches the configured lower band; OR a multi-factor consensus is oversold.
Short: high ≥ upper outer band AND (close < upper outer band OR a bearish divergence printed within the configured recency window OR a fresh high sweep OR an overbought multi-factor consensus) AND the configured bar cooldown has elapsed AND (variance ratio ≤ 1 minus its neutral band OR reversion correlation ≤ negative its threshold).
Short: a confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high, its RSI or Stoch RSI is lower than at the preceding pivot, and the price pivot reaches the configured upper band; OR a multi-factor consensus is overbought.
