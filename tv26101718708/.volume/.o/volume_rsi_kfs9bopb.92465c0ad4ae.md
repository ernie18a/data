Calculation: Volume Power = Smooth the rolling RMA of the scaled bar-strength volume metric on the chart timeframe: bar strength is (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low; multiply it by a configurable blend of volume raised to a configurable power and a constant, using 0 volume when volume is not positive; divide by the RMA of its absolute value over a configurable number of days converted to chart bars, multiply by 100, clamp to [−100, 100], then apply a configurable smoother (none, SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, or WMA). The scaling RMA uses a days-based lookback; the rolling RMA and smoother use bar-based lookbacks.
Long: Volume Power ≥ long threshold OR (RSI(close) ≥ midline AND Volume Power is between the short and long thresholds)
Short: Volume Power ≤ short threshold OR (RSI(close) < midline AND Volume Power is between the short and long thresholds)
