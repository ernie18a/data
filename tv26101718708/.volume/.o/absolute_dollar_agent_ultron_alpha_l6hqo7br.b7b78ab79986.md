Calculation: Macro Hull = configured Hull variant of close using a longer lookback than Fast Hull; bullish when rising versus two bars earlier, bearish when falling
Calculation: Fast Hull = same Hull variant of close using a shorter lookback than Macro Hull; bullish when rising versus two bars earlier, bearish when falling
Calculation: Trail = trailing stop from EMA(close) offset by ATR; it flips bearish when close crosses below it with the fast EMA below the slow EMA, and flips bullish when close crosses above it with the fast EMA above the slow EMA; otherwise it trails monotonically in the current direction
Calculation: Ultron gate = direction of a double-smoothed EMA of hlc3, or price above/below EMA(close) with a 200-bar lookback when that direction test does not agree
Calculation: Fib regime = direction of the slope of a double-smoothed EMA of hlc3; higher-timeframe regime and basis use the last completed bar of the configured higher timeframe
Calculation: Bull and bear scores = counts of enabled matching nodes: Macro Hull direction, Fast Hull direction, Trail direction, active CHoCH direction, price within 1.5 active-zone ranges of either zone edge in that direction, directional momentum, Ultron gate clear, and volume at least its moving average times a threshold; volume adds one point to both scores
Calculation: Directional momentum = (close − close from a lookback bar) / ATR; bullish and bearish when above a positive threshold or below its negative
Calculation: Active zone confidence = fraction of these corroborations that hold: matching higher-timeframe CHoCH, clear Fib gate, nearby unbroken low liquidity for long or high liquidity for short, discount/premium position, confirming volume, directional momentum, NY AM or London–New York session, matching higher-timeframe Fib, and price in the matching active zone
Calculation: Bullish CHoCH = close (or high, if close confirmation is disabled) breaks the latest confirmed swing high after the previous structural break was bearish; bearish CHoCH is the mirrored swing-low break after a bullish break. A tradeable zone also passes the enabled width, higher-timeframe Fib agreement, and extension filters; its bounds are the break bar's high and low
Calculation: Fib gate = configured LTF, HTF, or both Fib regimes agree with direction, price is on the matching side of each enabled basis filter, and enabled flip cooldowns have elapsed; it also passes when the directional score is at least a threshold and Trail agrees
Long: bullish Trail flip OR bullish CHoCH with confidence above its minimum OR configured RSI smart signal OR configured zone retest; AND Trail bullish, bull score at least its minimum, bullish Ultron gate clear, and Fib gate clear; RSI smart signal requires RSI above its bullish threshold and below exhaustion, its momentum EMA rising, and all enabled Trail, Hull, and Fib gates agreeing; zone retest requires a prior bullish zone, within its retest window, low at or below zone high, close above zone low, and bullish Trail
Short: bearish Trail flip OR bearish CHoCH with confidence above its minimum OR configured RSI smart signal OR configured zone retest; AND Trail bearish, bear score at least its minimum, bearish Ultron gate clear, and Fib gate clear; RSI smart signal requires RSI below its bearish threshold and above exhaustion, its momentum EMA falling, and all enabled Trail, Hull, and Fib gates agreeing; zone retest requires a prior bearish zone, within its retest window, high at or above zone low, close below zone high, and bearish Trail
Exit: long, confirmed close at or below active stop (or low at or below it when stop-close confirmation is disabled) OR confirmed bearish Trail flip
Exit: short, confirmed close at or above active stop (or high at or above it when stop-close confirmation is disabled) OR confirmed bullish Trail flip
