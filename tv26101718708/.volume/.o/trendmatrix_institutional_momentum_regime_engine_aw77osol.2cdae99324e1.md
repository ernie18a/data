Calculation: Price source = close, or the configurable-length EMA of close
Calculation: Centerline = 10-bar EMA of the midpoint of the upper and lower bands
Calculation: Upper band = greater of (0.7 × highest price source over the shared trend lookback + 0.3 × its linear regression value at that lookback − upper width) and (0.7 × lowest price source over that lookback + 0.3 × its linear regression value + lower width)
Calculation: Lower band = lesser of those same two band candidates
Calculation: Each width = its configurable base multiplier × ATR over the shared trend lookback × (1 + 0.5 × macro volatility percentile / 100) × (1 − 0.4 × its logistic compression); upper compression = 1 / (1 + exp(−z-score)), lower compression = 1 / (1 + exp(z-score)); z-score = (price source − EMA over its configurable z-score lookback) / standard deviation over that same lookback
Calculation: Macro volatility percentile = percentile rank over 252 macro-timeframe bars of the 20-bar EMA of standard deviation of macro-timeframe close over its configurable volatility lookback; use the macro-timeframe bar available with lookahead off (the current bar when available, otherwise the latest completed bar)
Long: Prior signal regime was not bullish AND current source crosses above the centerline OR (within the selected conservative trigger, a source cross above centerline occurred within the last 2 bars AND current source is above centerline AND the linear regression value over the shared trend lookback is rising) OR current source crosses above the lower band
Short: Prior signal regime was bullish AND current source crosses below the centerline OR (within the selected conservative trigger, a source cross below centerline occurred within the last 2 bars AND current source is below centerline AND the linear regression value over the shared trend lookback is falling) OR current source crosses below the upper band
