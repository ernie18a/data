Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high / lowest low so far in the current premarket session.
Calculation: Yesterday high/low = high / low of the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish key break = a close crossed above the premarket high or yesterday high / below the premarket low or yesterday low, respectively, on the current bar or within the configured setup window.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish flag boundary = highest high / lowest low over the configured flag lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish micro-structure boundary = highest high / lowest low over the configured micro-structure lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Ignition range boundary = highest high / lowest low over the configured ignition lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish pullback touch = within the configured pullback lookback, the minimum distance from a low to the 9 EMA, 20 EMA, or VWAP is at most ATR times the configured rejection distance.
Calculation: Bearish pullback touch = within the configured pullback lookback, the minimum distance from a high to the 9 EMA, 20 EMA, or VWAP is at most ATR times the configured rejection distance.
Long: Bar time is within the configured signal window AND recent bullish key break AND (absolute distance from close to premarket high or yesterday high is at most ATR times the configured proximity multiplier OR close is above either key high OR close is above VWAP) AND close > VWAP AND 9 EMA > 20 EMA AND close > 9 EMA AND close > close from the configured impulse lookback ago plus ATR times the configured impulse multiplier AND close > open AND (MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled) AND (volume > its configured moving average times the configured breakout volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) AND lowest low over the configured flag lookback > 20 EMA AND close > VWAP AND close > bullish flag boundary OR bar time is within the configured signal window AND close > VWAP AND 9 EMA > 20 EMA AND close > 9 EMA AND (absolute distance from close to premarket high or yesterday high is at most ATR times the configured proximity multiplier OR close is above either key high OR close is above VWAP) AND bullish pullback touch AND close > open AND close > 9 EMA AND low <= 9 EMA AND close > prior-bar close AND close > bullish micro-structure boundary AND close > open AND (MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram >= its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled) AND (volume >= its configured moving average times the configured continuation volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) OR bar time is within the configured signal window AND close > VWAP AND 9 EMA > 20 EMA AND close > 9 EMA AND close > ignition range boundary AND close > open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR times the configured body multiplier AND (volume >= its configured moving average times the configured continuation volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) AND (MACD histogram > its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled); enter only if the position side is not already long.
Short: Bar time is within the configured signal window AND recent bearish key break AND (absolute distance from close to premarket low or yesterday low is at most ATR times the configured proximity multiplier OR close is below either key low OR close is below VWAP) AND close < VWAP AND 9 EMA < 20 EMA AND close < 9 EMA AND close < close from the configured impulse lookback ago minus ATR times the configured impulse multiplier AND close < open AND (MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled) AND (volume > its configured moving average times the configured breakout volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) AND highest high over the configured flag lookback < 20 EMA AND close < VWAP AND close < bearish flag boundary OR bar time is within the configured signal window AND close < VWAP AND 9 EMA < 20 EMA AND close < 9 EMA AND (absolute distance from close to premarket low or yesterday low is at most ATR times the configured proximity multiplier OR close is below either key low OR close is below VWAP) AND bearish pullback touch AND close < open AND close < 9 EMA AND high >= 9 EMA AND close < prior-bar close AND close < bearish micro-structure boundary AND close < open AND (MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram <= its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled) AND (volume >= its configured moving average times the configured continuation volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) OR bar time is within the configured signal window AND close < VWAP AND 9 EMA < 20 EMA AND close < 9 EMA AND close < ignition range boundary AND close < open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR times the configured body multiplier AND (volume >= its configured moving average times the configured continuation volume multiplier, when the volume filter is enabled) AND (MACD histogram < its prior-bar value, when the MACD filter is enabled); enter only if the position side is not already short.
Exit: long, close < 9 EMA OR close < VWAP OR (MACD histogram < its prior-bar value AND MACD histogram > 0) OR short-entry condition.
Exit: short, close > 9 EMA OR close > VWAP OR (MACD histogram > its prior-bar value AND MACD histogram < 0) OR long-entry condition.
