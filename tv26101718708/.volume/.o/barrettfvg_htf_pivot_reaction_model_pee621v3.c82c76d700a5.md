Calculation: Qualified pivot level = confirmed swing high/low from any enabled higher timeframe, merged with pivots within the merge tolerance; the higher-timeframe pivot uses completed bars. Qualify when reaction count and number of source timeframes meet their minimums. Count a reaction after price touches the level band and moves away in the expected direction by the required multiple of the primary timeframe’s ATR within the reaction window.
Calculation: Structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high plus the buffer, or below the latest confirmed swing low minus the buffer; use the same pivot length for both.
Calculation: Accumulation range = the high-to-low range since a qualified-level tap or reclaim; freeze it after the minimum formation time if its width remains within the ATR-based maximum. Discard it if it exceeds that width or the total time limit.
Calculation: Recent major level = previous completed day/week high or low, or the current trading day’s open; a bar touches it when its range intersects the touch band.
Long: A qualified support or resistance level is touched without exceeding the tap overshoot, approached from above, and a structure break upward occurs within the BOS window; OR a qualified level is broken beyond the overshoot, a confirmed swing low forms above it beyond the touch band, price retests the level within the reclaim window from above, and a structure break upward occurs within the BOS window; OR an active accumulation range closes above its high within its time limit and breakout volume meets the configured requirement. Require an allowed session, a confirmed bar, and the long signal cooldown to have elapsed.
Short: A qualified support or resistance level is touched without exceeding the tap overshoot, approached from below, and a structure break downward occurs within the BOS window; OR a qualified level is broken beyond the overshoot, a confirmed swing high forms below it beyond the touch band, price retests the level within the reclaim window from below, and a structure break downward occurs within the BOS window; OR an active accumulation range closes below its low within its time limit and breakout volume meets the configured requirement. Require an allowed session, a confirmed bar, and the short signal cooldown to have elapsed.
Long: A long entry has occurred and either a descending channel breaks upward within the confluence lookback, or price is above a recently touched major level; OR a long entry is awaiting confluence and a descending channel breaks upward within the forward wait window.
Short: A short entry has occurred and either an ascending channel breaks downward within the confluence lookback, or price is below a recently touched major level; OR a short entry is awaiting confluence and an ascending channel breaks downward within the forward wait window.
Exit: long, low <= stop or high >= target. Stop is below the lower of entry-bar low and the latest confirmed chart swing low below entry (falling back to the setup level, or accumulation low); target is entry plus configured reward-to-risk multiple of stop distance.
Exit: short, high >= stop or low <= target. Stop is above the higher of entry-bar high and the latest confirmed chart swing high above entry (falling back to the setup level, or accumulation high); target is entry minus configured reward-to-risk multiple of stop distance.
