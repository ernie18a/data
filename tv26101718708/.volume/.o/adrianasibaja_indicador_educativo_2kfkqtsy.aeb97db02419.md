Calculation: Bull trend score = count of: close > long EMA; medium EMA > long EMA; short EMA > medium EMA; +DI > -DI.
Calculation: Bear trend score = count of: close < long EMA; medium EMA < long EMA; short EMA < medium EMA; -DI > +DI.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = price pivot high/low with equal lookback and confirmation bars; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed price swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed price swing low, while RSI at those pivots is higher at the newer pivot; require both pivots to use the same lookback and confirmation bars.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed price swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed price swing high, while RSI at those pivots is lower at the newer pivot; require both pivots to use the same lookback and confirmation bars.
Long: Bull trend score >= trend threshold AND ADX > quality threshold AND 35 < RSI < 65 AND higher-timeframe current close > its medium EMA AND higher-timeframe current close > its long EMA AND more than cooldown bars since the previous quality long signal; OR bullish RSI divergence AND more than cooldown bars since the previous bullish divergence; OR close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high.
Short: Bear trend score >= trend threshold AND ADX > quality threshold AND 35 < RSI < 65 AND higher-timeframe current close < its medium EMA AND higher-timeframe current close < its long EMA AND more than cooldown bars since the previous quality short signal; OR bearish RSI divergence AND more than cooldown bars since the previous bearish divergence; OR close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low.
