Calculation: Sentiment score = Normalize the sum of eight votes on each of four progressively longer timeframes: candle direction; close in the upper/lower configured portion of the bar range; short/medium/long EMA stack; RSI above/below its configured zone; MACD histogram positive/negative; candle direction when volume exceeds its moving average by the configured factor; Stochastic above/below its configured zone; and three consecutive closes rising/falling. Each vote is +1, −1, or 0; normalize the sum from −8…+8 to 0…100. Use each timeframe’s current bar, which may still be forming.
Long: The three shortest-timeframe sentiment scores are at or above the configured bullish threshold, were not all at or above it on the prior chart bar, and the longest-timeframe score is below that threshold; OR all four scores are at or above the threshold and were not all at or above it on the prior chart bar. In either case, the configured number of chart bars has elapsed since the prior long signal.
Short: The three shortest-timeframe sentiment scores are at or below the configured bearish threshold, were not all at or below it on the prior chart bar, and the longest-timeframe score is above that threshold; OR all four scores are at or below the threshold and were not all at or below it on the prior chart bar. In either case, the configured number of chart bars has elapsed since the prior short signal.
