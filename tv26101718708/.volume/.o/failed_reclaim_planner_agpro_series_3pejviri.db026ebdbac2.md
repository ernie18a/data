Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low at a pivot with configurable left and right bars; use the most recently confirmed pivot of each type.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; shelf half-width = max(ATR × shelf-width parameter, minimum tick); break buffer = ATR × sensitivity parameter.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick); upper/lower wick share = upper/lower wick length / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Failure score = clamp(0.18 × retest-depth score + 0.28 × close-rejection score + 0.20 × wick score + 0.14 × volume score + 0.10 × room score + 0.10 × follow-through score, 0, 100). For each side, depth score is clamp(retest penetration into the shelf zone / (2 × shelf half-width) × 100, 0, 100); close-rejection score is clamp(45 + distance of close beyond shelf in the rejection direction / ATR × 82 + 12 when close location is at least 0.64 for bullish or at most 0.36 for bearish, 0, 100); wick score is clamp(rejection-side wick share × 180, 0, 100). Volume score is clamp(45 + (volume / its longer-lookback simple average − 1) × 42, 0, 100). Room score is clamp(available continuation room / fallback room distance × 100, 0, 100), where available room uses the nearest prior low below price for bearish or prior high above price for bullish within a lookback, falling back to ATR × a configurable multiple. Follow-through score is clamp(70 × (1 − close location) + 32 × body share + 12 if close < prior close for bearish; for bullish use 70 × close location + 32 × body share + 12 if close > prior close; clamp each result to 0–100).
Long: close > most recently confirmed swing high + break buffer AND close location ≥ 0.52 AND no bullish shelf is already active.
Long: a bullish shelf is active, the bar is after its break and within the configurable retest window, low ≤ shelf level + shelf half-width, close > shelf level + configurable failure buffer, close location ≥ 0.52, lower wick share ≥ configurable minimum, and failure score ≥ configurable minimum.
Short: close < most recently confirmed swing low − break buffer AND close location ≤ 0.48 AND no bearish shelf is already active.
Short: a bearish shelf is active, the bar is after its break and within the configurable retest window, high ≥ shelf level − shelf half-width, close < shelf level − configurable failure buffer, close location ≤ 0.48, upper wick share ≥ configurable minimum, and failure score ≥ configurable minimum.
