Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot, with lookback scaled up on higher chart timeframes.
Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-period SMA = simple moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Session and daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of OHLC4 since each configured anchor, using the current forming period.
Calculation: Long stop = selected prior pivot low (or configured fraction of entry-to-pivot distance), signal-bar low, or entry reduced by configured percentage, then reduced by configured buffers.
Calculation: Short stop = selected prior pivot high (or configured fraction of entry-to-pivot distance), signal-bar high, or entry increased by configured percentage, then increased by configured buffers.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × configured reward/risk ratio; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × configured reward/risk ratio.
Long: Active latest confirmed pivot high is crossed by the configured entry mode (high reaches a pending stop above the bar’s high, or a confirmed close is above the pivot) AND the pivot high is above the latest confirmed pivot low AND the bullish break qualifies under the configured BOS/CHoCH selection AND the long stop is below entry and target is above entry AND long entries are allowed AND position is flat or short AND the same pivot has not already been used for a long entry today AND the daily trade limit and configured trading window allow entry AND each enabled filter passes: configured SMA slope mode (all three rising, at least two rising, short and medium rising, short rising, or close above all three), optional SMA stacking (short > medium > long), optional slope divergence (short slope > medium slope > long slope), optional session/daily VWAP (close above each enabled VWAP), and, in close-entry mode only, optional bullish candle filter (close > open and body ≥ configured multiple of total wick).
Short: Active latest confirmed pivot low is crossed by the configured entry mode (low reaches a pending stop below the bar’s low, or a confirmed close is below the pivot) AND the pivot low is below the latest confirmed pivot high AND the bearish break qualifies under the configured BOS/CHoCH selection AND the short stop is above entry and target is below entry AND short entries are allowed AND position is flat or long AND the same pivot has not already been used for a short entry today AND the daily trade limit and configured trading window allow entry AND each enabled filter passes: configured SMA slope mode (all three falling, at least two falling, short and medium falling, short falling, or close below all three), optional SMA stacking (short < medium < long), optional slope divergence (short slope < medium slope < long slope), optional session/daily VWAP (close below each enabled VWAP), and, in close-entry mode only, optional bearish candle filter (close < open and body ≥ configured multiple of total wick).
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR a new day begins with end-of-day closing enabled.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR a new day begins with end-of-day closing enabled.
