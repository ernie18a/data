Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle closes above its open, previous candle closes below its open, current close is above previous open, and current open is below previous close
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle closes below its open, previous candle closes above its open, current close is below previous open, and current open is above previous close
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago
Calculation: Bullish order block = current close > highest high of the previous two bars and current volume > 20-bar average volume
Calculation: Bearish order block = current close < lowest low of the previous two bars and current volume > 20-bar average volume
Long: bar is confirmed AND session filter passes if enabled AND (bullish engulfing OR bullish order block OR bullish fair value gap OR (volume-supported breakout above the highest high of the lookback bars, with current volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier, if enabled)) AND (if enabled, close > Hull moving average calculated from close on the selected other-timeframe bar, using that bar's close and Hull period; the bar may still be forming) AND (if enabled, ADX > threshold AND +DI > -DI) AND (if enabled, close > EMA) AND (if enabled, RSI < 70) AND (if enabled, absolute EMA slope over the slope lookback > EMA × slope threshold) AND (if enabled, current high - current low > ATR × volatility multiplier) AND ((main entry: position state is flat AND no long or short exit is signaled) OR (repeat entry: position state is long AND minimum bars since last entry have elapsed if cooldown is enabled AND virtual treasury is empty or early threshold has been reached if treasury is enabled))
Short: bar is confirmed AND session filter passes if enabled AND (bearish engulfing OR bearish order block OR bearish fair value gap OR (volume-supported breakout below the lowest low of the lookback bars, with current volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier, if enabled)) AND (if enabled, close < Hull moving average calculated from close on the selected other-timeframe bar, using that bar's close and Hull period; the bar may still be forming) AND (if enabled, ADX > threshold AND -DI > +DI) AND (if enabled, close < EMA) AND (if enabled, RSI > 30) AND (if enabled, absolute EMA slope over the slope lookback > EMA × slope threshold) AND (if enabled, current high - current low > ATR × volatility multiplier) AND ((main entry: position state is flat AND no long or short exit is signaled) OR (repeat entry: position state is short AND minimum bars since last entry have elapsed if cooldown is enabled AND virtual treasury is empty or early threshold has been reached if treasury is enabled))
Exit: Long, bar is confirmed AND (opposite short-entry conditions are met if reverse-signal exit is enabled OR low < entry-bar low × (1 - stop buffer percentage / 100) if stop-buffer exit is enabled OR bars since entry >= maximum bars if time exit is enabled)
Exit: Short, bar is confirmed AND (opposite long-entry conditions are met if reverse-signal exit is enabled OR high > entry-bar high × (1 + stop buffer percentage / 100) if stop-buffer exit is enabled OR bars since entry >= maximum bars if time exit is enabled)
