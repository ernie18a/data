Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; 0 if the sum is 0.
Calculation: Regime = start CAUTION; from CAUTION, enter RANGE below the range threshold or TREND above the trend threshold; from RANGE, remain RANGE below the range-exit threshold, otherwise enter TREND above the trend threshold or CAUTION; from TREND, remain TREND above the trend-exit threshold, otherwise enter RANGE below the range threshold or CAUTION.
Calculation: Up confluence count = number of true conditions: efficiency ratio meets the trend threshold; shorter-lookback HMA is above longer-lookback HMA; RSI meets the bullish threshold; MACD histogram is positive and has risen for the last two bars; close is above session VWAP.
Calculation: Down confluence count = number of true conditions: efficiency ratio meets the trend threshold; shorter-lookback HMA is below longer-lookback HMA; RSI meets the bearish threshold; MACD histogram is negative and has fallen for the last two bars; close is below session VWAP.
Calculation: Flip stops = in BULL state, each candidate is close minus ATR times its multiplier and each stop ratchets upward by taking the greater of its prior value and candidate; in BEAR state, each candidate is close plus ATR times its multiplier and each stop ratchets downward by taking the lesser of its prior value and candidate. Initialize in BULL; on a flip, set the new state's stops to that bar's candidates and clear the other state's stops. The outermost stop uses a longer ATR multiple than the other stops.
Long: Regime changes to TREND AND close is above session VWAP AND up confluence meets the configured minimum tier AND same-direction lockout and cooldown have elapsed AND the configured higher-timeframe filter passes; OR flip state is BEAR AND close is above the prior bar's outermost BEAR stop.
Short: Regime changes to TREND AND close is at or below session VWAP AND down confluence meets the configured minimum tier AND same-direction lockout and cooldown have elapsed AND the configured higher-timeframe filter passes; OR flip state is BULL AND close is below the prior bar's outermost BULL stop.
