Calculation: Smoothed OBV = EMA of cumulative volume signed positive when close rises from the prior bar, negative when it falls, and zero when unchanged
Calculation: Fast and slow OBV averages = EMAs of Smoothed OBV, with the fast average using a shorter lookback than the slow average
Calculation: OBV impulse = EMA of the one-bar change in Smoothed OBV
Calculation: OBV normalization = standard deviation of Smoothed OBV over a lookback
Calculation: Fast and slow price averages = EMAs of close, with the fast average using a shorter lookback than the slow average
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a low/high no greater/less than the surrounding bars over the pivot lookback on both sides; use the pivot bar's price and indicator values once the bars on both sides are complete
Calculation: Divergence quality score = clamp to a nonnegative capped score formed from weighted, capped components for price swing divided by ATR, OBV improvement or decline divided by OBV normalization, pivot separation, OBV pressure (fast/slow OBV relationship and impulse sign), proximity to the corresponding edge of the lookback price range, and fast/slow price-average trend context
Long: At a confirmed pivot low, current pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND current pivot Smoothed OBV > previous pivot Smoothed OBV AND pivot separation meets its minimum AND price decline between pivots >= current pivot ATR × minimum swing factor AND (divergence quality score >= quality threshold OR weak events are enabled) AND (no prior long event or separation from the prior long event meets its cooldown OR divergence quality score >= premium threshold)
Long: A bullish setup is pending within its confirmation window AND (close > its saved trigger level when close-based confirmation is used, otherwise high > its saved trigger level) AND fast OBV average > slow OBV average
Short: At a confirmed pivot high, current pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND current pivot Smoothed OBV < previous pivot Smoothed OBV AND pivot separation meets its minimum AND price rise between pivots >= current pivot ATR × minimum swing factor AND (divergence quality score >= quality threshold OR weak events are enabled) AND (no prior short event or separation from the prior short event meets its cooldown OR divergence quality score >= premium threshold)
Short: A bearish setup is pending within its confirmation window AND (close < its saved trigger level when close-based confirmation is used, otherwise low < its saved trigger level) AND fast OBV average < slow OBV average
