Calculation: Estimated buy and sell volume = candle volume multiplied by each side’s strength, then normalized so the two estimates sum to candle volume; buy strength = 0.60 × close position + 0.25 × lower-wick fraction + 0.35 × positive body ratio (only when positive); sell strength = 0.60 × (1 − close position) + 0.25 × upper-wick fraction + 0.35 × absolute negative body ratio (only when negative). Close position = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); body ratio = (close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); wick fractions use each wick divided by body size plus both wick sizes. Buy and sell totals are summed over the recent lookback bars, including the current bar while it is forming. Imbalance = |buy total − sell total| / (buy total + sell total).
Long: buy total > sell total AND current imbalance > previous bar’s imbalance
Short: sell total > buy total AND current imbalance > previous bar’s imbalance
