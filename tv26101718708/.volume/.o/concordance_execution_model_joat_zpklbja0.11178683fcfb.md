Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / the moving average of volume over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Delta pressure = the moving average of (((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) − ((high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment))) × volume, divided by the moving average of volume over the same lookback
Calculation: Structure direction = bullish after a confirmed close breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high by an ATR-based buffer; bearish after a confirmed close breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low by an ATR-based buffer; otherwise retain the previous direction. Use pivots with equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: ALMA direction vote = sum of direction states from four configured higher timeframes, using each timeframe's last completed bar. On each timeframe, form ALMA-based upper and lower bands using standard deviation; ratchet the upper band down and lower band up unless the prior source close crossed the prior band. Set direction bullish when the source close exceeds the upper band, bearish when it falls below the lower band, and otherwise retain the prior direction.
Calculation: Kinetic trend = estimated velocity / ATR, where position, velocity, and acceleration are updated each bar with a constant-acceleration prediction and residual correction; increase correction gains when the absolute residual exceeds an ATR-based threshold.
Calculation: Regression confidence = clamp(1 − variance(close − linear regression of close over a lookback) / variance(close over the same lookback), 0, 1)
Calculation: Long score = weighted sum of bullish structure direction, higher-timeframe close above its EMA, bullish ALMA direction vote, positive kinetic trend with nonnegative acceleration, regression confidence above its threshold with rising regression, positive delta score, and close above the shorter EMA while the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA.
Calculation: Short score = weighted sum of bearish structure direction, higher-timeframe close below its EMA, bearish ALMA direction vote, negative kinetic trend with nonpositive acceleration, regression confidence above its threshold with falling regression, negative delta score, and close below the shorter EMA while the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA.
Calculation: Delta score = clamp(base score + directional adjustments for delta pressure, OBV versus its moving average, close versus VWAP, and relative volume versus its threshold, 0, 100)
Long: bar is confirmed AND cooldown since the last entry has elapsed AND ATR / close exceeds a minimum volatility threshold AND long score meets its threshold AND delta pressure exceeds its positive gate AND relative volume meets its threshold
Short: bar is confirmed AND cooldown since the last entry has elapsed AND ATR / close exceeds a minimum volatility threshold AND short score meets its threshold AND delta pressure is below its negative gate AND relative volume meets its threshold
Exit: long, low <= max(entry price − ATR × stop multiple, ratcheting trailing stop based on highest close since entry minus ATR × trail multiple) OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR bars since entry reaches the time limit OR structure direction is bearish OR short score exceeds long score by a threshold
Exit: short, high >= min(entry price + ATR × stop multiple, ratcheting trailing stop based on lowest close since entry plus ATR × trail multiple) OR low <= entry price − ATR × target multiple OR bars since entry reaches the time limit OR structure direction is bullish OR long score exceeds short score by a threshold
