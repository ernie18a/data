Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks; remove a low when a later low is below it and a high when a later high is above it. The front low is the highest remaining level below close; the front high is the lowest remaining level above close.
Calculation: Demand zone = on a trigger of consecutive bullish candles (run length parameter), with the preceding bar's volume above its rolling average times a threshold and the demand-zone trigger gate open, find the nearest bearish candle among the current and five preceding bars; use its high and low as the zone bounds. Skip creation if the bounds overlap an existing demand zone. Latch the zone as liquidity-qualified if a front low is above its top. Retain it only until close falls below its bottom or, after price has been above its top, a later low reaches its top. The trigger gate reopens after its bar counter reaches its reset threshold.
Calculation: Supply zone = on a trigger of consecutive bearish candles (same run-length parameter as for demand), with the preceding bar's volume above its rolling average times the same threshold and the supply-zone trigger gate open, find the nearest bullish candle among the current and five preceding bars; use its high and low as the zone bounds. Skip creation if the bounds overlap an existing supply zone. Latch the zone as liquidity-qualified if a front high is below its bottom. Retain it only until close rises above its top or, after price has been below its bottom, a later high reaches its bottom. The trigger gate uses the same reset threshold as for demand.
Long: close is above the top of a liquidity-qualified, untested demand zone, and fewer than the configured number of other liquidity-qualified demand zones are closer to close.
Short: close is below the bottom of a liquidity-qualified, untested supply zone, and fewer than the configured number of other liquidity-qualified supply zones are closer to close.
