Long: fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > highest high over the breakout lookback on the prior completed bar AND close > open AND volume > 1.5 × volume SMA
Short: fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < lowest low over the breakout lookback on the prior completed bar AND close < open AND volume > 1.5 × volume SMA
Exit: long, low <= prior completed bar close - prior completed bar ATR(14) × stop multiplier
Exit: short, high >= prior completed bar close + prior completed bar ATR(14) × stop multiplier
