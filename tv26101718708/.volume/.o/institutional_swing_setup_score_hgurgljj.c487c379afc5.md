Calculation: Trend score = 30 if close > short-term SMA > mid-term SMA > long-term SMA, plus 25 if the long-term SMA is above its value at a prior, longer lookback, plus 15 if close > long-term SMA, plus 15 if close > 1.30 × the lowest low and 15 if close > 0.75 × the highest high over the long-term range lookback; otherwise each term is 0
Calculation: Relative-strength score = 35 if close / benchmark close > its SMA, plus 35 if that ratio is at least 0.999 × its highest value over a longer lookback, plus 30 × min(10 × relative performance, 1) if relative performance is positive; otherwise each term is 0. Relative performance = (close / prior close − 1) − (benchmark close / its prior close − 1), using the same lookback
Calculation: Structure score = 0.40 × tightness score + 0.30 × compression score + 0.30 × higher-low score. Tightness score = clamp(100 − range percentage / effective reference range × 100); compression score = clamp(100 − distance-from-high percentage / (0.6 × effective reference range) × 100); effective reference range = reference range / tightness sensitivity. Range percentage = (highest high − lowest low) / highest high × 100 and distance-from-high percentage = (highest high − close) / highest high × 100, over the same base lookback. Higher-low score = min(confirmed rising pivot-low count, 3) / 3 × 100; pivot lows use equal left and right lookbacks
Calculation: Volatility score = 30 if ATR as a percentage of close is below its SMA, plus 30 if Bollinger Band width as a percentage of its basis is below its SMA, plus 0.40 × (100 − percentile rank of ATR percentage); the two SMAs use the same lookback
Calculation: Volume score = 20 for each true condition: short-term volume SMA < long-term volume SMA × dry-up factor; up-volume / down-volume ratio over a lookback > demand threshold; cumulative (close-location value × volume) > its SMA; close < prior close and volume < long-term volume SMA; an up-close bar with volume ≥ long-term volume SMA × participation multiplier occurred within the participation lookback. Close-location value = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low. Up/down volume sums include bars whose close rose/fell from the preceding close; ratio is 1 if both sums are zero and twice the threshold if only up-volume is positive
Calculation: Breakout-readiness score = clamp(0.20 × trend score + 0.20 × relative-strength score + 0.25 × volume score + 0.15 × structure score + 0.10 × volatility score + 5 if close is within the proximity threshold below the base high + 5 if the base range is tight and resistance was tested enough times + confirmation bonus if breakout is confirmed). Resistance tests count highs within the proximity threshold below the highest high over the breakout lookback
Calculation: Composite score = clamp(weighted average of trend, relative-strength, structure, volatility, volume, and breakout-readiness scores, using their respective configurable weights)
Long: Composite score crosses above the entry threshold OR a breakout is newly confirmed (high ≥ 0.999 × highest high over the base lookback AND a qualifying up-volume thrust occurred within the participation lookback AND close > short-term SMA > mid-term SMA > long-term SMA AND close / benchmark close > its SMA)
Exit: Long, composite score crosses below the exit threshold
