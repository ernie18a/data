Calculation: Long classifier score = gradient-boosting classifier probability from RSI, relative volume (volume / its moving average), close-price Z-score (close minus its moving average, divided by its standard deviation), and ADX; trained on prior closed feature/outcome pairs labeled positive when the following forward window's maximum high exceeds the entry close by at least one ATR.
Calculation: Short classifier score = same features and training method, labeled positive when the entry close exceeds the following forward window's minimum low by at least one ATR.
Calculation: Long excursion = gradient-boosting regression prediction, trained on the same features and forward window to predict the maximum favorable move in ATR units.
Calculation: Short excursion = gradient-boosting regression prediction, trained on the same features and forward window to predict the maximum favorable move in ATR units.
Long: current low < lowest low of prior-bar lookback AND current close > current low + (current high − current low) / 2 AND long classifier score ≥ score threshold AND (long classifier score − short classifier score) ≥ directional-edge threshold AND ADX is within its configured inclusive range AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND (if CCI filter enabled, recent lowest CCI ≤ negative CCI threshold) AND (if EMA filter is with-trend, close > EMA; if counter-trend, close < EMA) AND (if relative-volume filter enabled, relative volume ≥ its floor)
Short: current high > highest high of prior-bar lookback AND current close < current low + (current high − current low) / 2 AND short classifier score ≥ score threshold AND (short classifier score − long classifier score) ≥ directional-edge threshold AND ADX is within its configured inclusive range AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND (if CCI filter enabled, recent highest CCI ≥ CCI threshold) AND (if EMA filter is with-trend, close < EMA; if counter-trend, close > EMA) AND (if relative-volume filter enabled, relative volume ≥ its floor)
Exit: long, high ≥ TP3 OR low ≤ current stop; after TP1 is touched, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled. Initial stop is below the signal-bar low by an ATR buffer, with minimum distance of half an ATR; TP3 is entry plus the greater of long excursion × ATR and initial risk × TP3 floor.
Exit: short, low ≤ TP3 OR high ≥ current stop; after TP1 is touched, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled. Initial stop is above the signal-bar high by an ATR buffer, with minimum distance of half an ATR; TP3 is entry minus the greater of short excursion × ATR and initial risk × TP3 floor.
