Calculation: Adaptive pivot lookback = rounded, bounded inverse scaling of the ratio of shorter-lookback ATR to longer-lookback ATR; a confirmed pivot high/low is the corresponding local extreme over that lookback and the right confirmation lookback on the current timeframe, using the pivot bar's high/low after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = same-side confirmed pivots matched when their price distance is within ATR-based equality tolerance and their age is within the matching lookback; pool level is the touch-count-weighted average, with zone bounds half a tolerance above and below it. New touches update the level and last-touch bar.
Calculation: Pool strength = the sum of capped touch-count points, exponentially decaying recency points based on bars since the last touch and a half-life, capped volume points based on cumulative zone-touch volume relative to the median pool volume, and an optional bonus when a same-side pivot on the configured higher timeframe is within 1.5 equality tolerances; clamp the total to 0–100. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price offset against the position by ATR times the stop multiplier, or beyond the signal bar's sweep extreme by an ATR buffer when sweep-wick placement is selected and that distance is at least 0.1 ATR; otherwise use the ATR stop. Profit targets = entry price offset with the position by initial stop distance times each target multiplier. After the first target is touched without a stop touch on that bar, the active stop moves to entry.
Long: On a confirmed bar, an active low-pivot pool is eligible after the pivot confirmation cooldown, its strength is at least the signal threshold, and the bar's low is below the zone bottom AND (the close is above the pool level when body reversal is required, or body reversal is disabled).
Short: On a confirmed bar, an active high-pivot pool is eligible after the pivot confirmation cooldown, its strength is at least the signal threshold, and the bar's high is above the zone top AND (the close is below the pool level when body reversal is required, or body reversal is disabled).
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= third profit target
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= third profit target
