Calculation: Modified SuperTrend direction = use ATR bands around hl2; trail the lower band to the greater of its current value and prior lower band unless prior close is below the prior lower band, and trail the upper band to the lesser of its current value and prior upper band unless prior close is above the prior upper band. Initialize direction as +1; when prior SuperTrend equals prior upper band, set direction to -1 if close > current upper band, otherwise +1; otherwise set it to +1 if close < current lower band, otherwise -1. Direction +1 is bearish and -1 is bullish.
Calculation: Bull/bear volume ratio = sum of volume on up-close bars over the last 3 bars divided by the greater of the sum of volume on down-close bars over the last 3 bars and 0.01; bars with close = open contribute zero.
Long: Modified SuperTrend direction crosses from +1 to -1 AND date is on or after the configured start year and month AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close > configured-length SMA of close) AND (trend-strength filter disabled OR ADX > configured threshold).
Short: Modified SuperTrend direction crosses from -1 to +1 AND date is on or after the configured start year and month AND (moving-average filter disabled OR close < configured-length SMA of close) AND (trend-strength filter disabled OR ADX > configured threshold).
Exit: Long, bars since entry >= configured monitoring delay AND absolute value of (bull/bear volume ratio - 1) <= 1 - configured volume-ratio threshold.
Exit: Short, bars since entry >= configured monitoring delay AND absolute value of (bull/bear volume ratio - 1) <= 1 - configured volume-ratio threshold.
