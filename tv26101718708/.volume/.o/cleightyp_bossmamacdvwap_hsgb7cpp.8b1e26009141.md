Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides lower/higher; use the latest confirmed pivot until broken or replaced.
Calculation: SMA slope = current simple moving average of close minus its value one bar earlier; calculate short-, medium-, and long-lookback SMAs.
Calculation: NY VWAP and daily VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of OHLC4 since the first bar at or after each configured daily anchor; use the current developing anchored period.
Calculation: Strong bullish/bearish candle = close above/below open and absolute body size >= (high - low - body size) * (1 + configured percentage / 100).
Calculation: Long stop = previous swing low, entry-bar low, or entry price * (1 - configured stop percentage), according to stop mode, less configured point and percentage buffers.
Calculation: Short stop = previous swing high, entry-bar high, or entry price * (1 + configured stop percentage), according to stop mode, plus configured point and percentage buffers.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus/minus stop risk multiplied by the configured risk/reward ratio.
Long: An active confirmed swing high is reached intrabar (high >= swing high) in tick-entry mode, or exceeded at bar close (close > swing high) in confirmed-close mode; the break qualifies as BOS (previous break was not bearish) or CHoCH (previous break was bearish) as selected; long trend filter passes (selected SMA slope mode: all three slopes positive, at least two positive, short- and medium-lookback slopes positive, short-lookback slope positive, or close above all three SMAs); and, when enabled, short-to-long SMA stacking is ascending, short-to-long SMA slopes are descending in magnitude order, close is above each enabled anchored VWAP, and the confirmed-close candle is strong bullish; position is flat or short, the pivot has not already been used for a long entry that day, and the daily trade limit permits entry.
Short: An active confirmed swing low is reached intrabar (low <= swing low) in tick-entry mode, or exceeded downward at bar close (close < swing low) in confirmed-close mode; the break qualifies as BOS (previous break was not bullish) or CHoCH (previous break was bullish) as selected; short trend filter passes (selected SMA slope mode: all three slopes negative, at least two negative, short- and medium-lookback slopes negative, short-lookback slope negative, or close below all three SMAs); and, when enabled, short-to-long SMA stacking is descending, short-to-long SMA slopes are ascending in magnitude order, close is below each enabled anchored VWAP, and the confirmed-close candle is strong bearish; position is flat or long, the pivot has not already been used for a short entry that day, and the daily trade limit permits entry.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long profit target OR a new day begins when end-of-day closing is enabled.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short profit target OR a new day begins when end-of-day closing is enabled.
