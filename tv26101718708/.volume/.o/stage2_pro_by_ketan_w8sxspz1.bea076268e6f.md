Calculation: Weekly higher high = a weekly high exceeds every high in the preceding longer lookback window, and that event occurred within the shorter hold window; use weekly bars, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Weekly SMA = simple moving average of weekly closes; use weekly bars, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Monthly SMA = simple moving average of monthly closes; use monthly bars, including the bar still forming
Long: weekly close > weekly SMA AND weekly close > monthly SMA AND weekly higher high AND monthly RSI > its minimum threshold
