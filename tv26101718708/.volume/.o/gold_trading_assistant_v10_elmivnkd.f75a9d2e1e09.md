Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = bullish engulfing (current candle closes above open; previous candle closes below open; current close is at or above previous open; current open is at or below previous close; current body is at least a configurable fraction of the previous body) OR hammer (lower wick is at least a configurable multiple of body, upper wick is at most half the body, and body is positive)
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = bearish engulfing (current candle closes below open; previous candle closes above open; current close is at or below previous open; current open is at or above previous close; current body is at least a configurable fraction of the previous body) OR shooting star (upper wick is at least a configurable multiple of body, lower wick is at most half the body, and body is positive)
Calculation: Signal volatility filter = current candle body is no greater than a configurable multiple of ATR, and the high-to-low range over a configurable recent lookback is no greater than a configurable multiple of ATR
Calculation: MTF alignment = count of bullish or bearish states among: higher-timeframe close versus fast EMA versus slow EMA; current-timeframe close versus fast EMA versus slow EMA; lower-timeframe close versus fast EMA. Higher- and lower-timeframe values use the last completed bar; fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Structure levels = previous completed daily high/low, previous completed weekly high/low, the two latest confirmed swing highs/lows, and configurable round-number levels
Calculation: Long first target = nearest structure level above entry and no farther than the configurable first risk/reward target, less a configurable buffer; if none exists, use the first risk/reward target
Calculation: Short first target = nearest structure level below entry and no farther than the configurable first risk/reward target, plus a configurable buffer; if none exists, use the first risk/reward target
Calculation: Long risk = entry minus the lower of the recent swing low minus an ATR buffer and entry minus an ATR-based stop distance
Calculation: Short risk = the higher of the recent swing high plus an ATR buffer and entry plus an ATR-based stop distance, minus entry
Calculation: Confluence score = fixed pattern points + points for directional MTF alignment + directional macro score + volume points + nearby-structure points + fast-EMA-slope points + news-clear points. Macro score is derived from lookback percentage changes in DXY and silver and basis-point change in US10Y, normalized by their respective rolling means and standard deviations when adaptive scoring is enabled; use the last completed macro-timeframe bars. News-clear points apply when news filtering is enabled, no configured event is within its impact-based blackout window, and no configured event is within the configured upcoming-event window.
Long: bullish reversal pattern AND volume meets its configurable average-volume confirmation if enabled AND signal volatility filter passes AND the configurable minimum bars since the prior signal has elapsed AND bar is confirmed if strict mode is enabled AND (MTF alignment is not required OR at least two states are bullish) AND (macro opposition is not blocked OR macro score is nonnegative) AND no configured news blackout is active AND confluence score meets its configurable minimum AND (entry minus long first target) / long risk meets the configurable minimum risk/reward
Short: bearish reversal pattern AND volume meets its configurable average-volume confirmation if enabled AND signal volatility filter passes AND the configurable minimum bars since the prior signal has elapsed AND bar is confirmed if strict mode is enabled AND (MTF alignment is not required OR at least two states are bearish) AND (macro opposition is not blocked OR macro score is nonpositive) AND no configured news blackout is active AND confluence score meets its configurable minimum AND (entry minus short first target) / short risk meets the configurable minimum risk/reward
