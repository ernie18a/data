Calculation: Upper/lower liquidity levels = Rolling highest(high)/lowest(low) over fast, medium, and slow lookbacks; accept the initial extreme, then update when the extreme equals its prior-bar value and is respectively above/below its value three bars earlier. Store accepted prices, merge levels within two minimum ticks, and keep them active until swept or older than the configured maximum age.
Calculation: Visible liquidity bands = On each completed heatmap-cell interval, draw cells at each of the three nearest active levels above and below close. Cell height is the recent highest high minus lowest low divided by the configured resolution, with a minimum of one tick per division. A level is visible if its last cell was drawn within one cell-width of bars; overlapping cell price ranges form one band.
Calculation: Normalized level strength = Accumulated directional volume, capped at the configured maximum: volume divided by its 100-bar average, multiplied by +1 for close > open or -1 for close < open. Add the absolute value to the three nearest upper levels on down bars, or lower levels on up bars, weighted respectively 1, 0.25, and 0.05. Divide each active level's strength by the greatest strength among the three nearest upper and lower levels, capped to [0,1]. A band's strength is the maximum normalized strength of its member levels.
Calculation: Sweep/reclaim state = On a completed bar, count visible upper bands crossed by high and set their reclaim level to the lowest swept upper-level price; count visible lower bands crossed by low and set their reclaim level to the highest swept lower-level price. Record a sweep only when at least two distinct bands were crossed and, when the strength filter is enabled, their mean strength meets its threshold. Keep it pending for up to two bars.
Long: A pending lower-band sweep has close > its reclaim level within two bars.
Short: A pending upper-band sweep has close < its reclaim level within two bars.
Calculation: Long stop and target = At the bar after a long signal, entry price is open; stop is the lowest low of the four preceding bars; final target is entry price plus the stop distance multiplied by the configured risk/reward ratio.
Calculation: Short stop and target = At the bar after a short signal, entry price is open; stop is the highest high of the four preceding bars; final target is entry price minus the stop distance multiplied by the configured risk/reward ratio.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long final target OR bars since entry > configured signal expiry.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short final target OR bars since entry > configured signal expiry.
