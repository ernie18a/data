Calculation: Inside bar = high <= prior high AND low >= prior low
Calculation: Outside bar = high > prior high AND low < prior low
Calculation: Up bar = high > prior high AND low >= prior low
Calculation: Down bar = low < prior low AND high <= prior high
Calculation: HTF agreement count = number of matching candle directions across the scanner timeframe, 4-hour, daily, and weekly bars; each direction is up when close >= open and down otherwise. Use the bars still forming.
Long: (two bars ago down AND prior bar inside AND current bar up) OR (two bars ago outside AND prior bar inside AND current bar up) OR (prior bar down AND current bar up) OR (prior bar inside AND current bar up) OR (prior bar inside AND current bar outside AND current close > current open) OR (prior bar outside AND current bar up) OR (two bars ago down AND prior bar inside AND current bar inside) OR (two bars ago outside AND prior bar down AND current bar inside); AND HTF agreement count >= configured minimum AND current volume > its simple moving average. Evaluate patterns on scanner-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming. The volume filter can be disabled.
Short: (two bars ago up AND prior bar inside AND current bar down) OR (two bars ago outside AND prior bar inside AND current bar down) OR (prior bar up AND current bar down) OR (prior bar inside AND current bar down) OR (prior bar inside AND current bar outside AND current close < current open) OR (prior bar outside AND current bar down) OR (two bars ago up AND prior bar inside AND current bar inside) OR (two bars ago outside AND prior bar up AND current bar inside); AND HTF agreement count >= configured minimum AND current volume > its simple moving average. Evaluate patterns on scanner-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming. The volume filter can be disabled.
