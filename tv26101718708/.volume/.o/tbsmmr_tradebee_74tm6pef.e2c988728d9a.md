Calculation: Short-term EMA and long-term EMA = exponential moving averages of each asset’s close on the current analysis-timeframe bar; the short-term lookback is shorter. Use the forming bar in realtime and the latest completed bar historically.
Calculation: Relative strength = primary asset’s percentage return over the relative-strength lookback minus the benchmark’s percentage return over the same lookback, using current analysis-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Directional efficiency = absolute change in the primary asset’s close over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback, using current analysis-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current primary-asset volume divided by its average volume over the volume lookback, using current analysis-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Short-term rate of change = primary asset’s percentage price change over a shorter lookback than the relative-strength lookback, using current analysis-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Yield change = current yield minus its value at the yield lookback, converted to basis points, using current analysis-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Composite score = weighted directional contributions from primary-asset close versus short-term EMA, short-term versus long-term EMA, confirmation-asset close versus short-term EMA, relative strength above or below zero, directional efficiency above its threshold, volume ratio above its threshold with short-term rate of change above or below zero, and yield change above or below its pressure thresholds.
Long: Composite score >= bullish threshold AND directional efficiency >= minimum efficiency threshold OR primary-asset close > short-term EMA AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR confirmation-asset close > short-term EMA AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR relative strength > 0 OR directional efficiency >= minimum efficiency threshold OR volume ratio >= strong-volume threshold OR yield change <= negative yield-pressure threshold
Short: Composite score <= bearish threshold OR primary-asset close < long-term EMA AND relative strength < 0 AND directional efficiency >= minimum efficiency threshold OR primary-asset close < short-term EMA AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA OR confirmation-asset close < short-term EMA AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA OR relative strength < 0 OR yield change >= yield-pressure threshold
