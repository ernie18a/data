Calculation: HTF liquidity bounds = highest high and lowest low over a lookback of completed HTF bars; use the latest completed HTF bar. HTF prior-bar levels = high and low of the HTF bar immediately before the latest completed HTF bar.
Calculation: Narrative direction = bullish when the latest completed HTF close exceeds the prior-bar high; bearish when it is below the prior-bar low. Otherwise retain the prior direction. Initially, use bullish when the current bar close is at or above the midpoint of the HTF liquidity bounds, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Protected points = latest confirmed LTF pivot low in a bullish narrative, or latest confirmed LTF pivot high in a bearish narrative.
Calculation: Displacement = candle body size at least ATR(14) times a threshold, with close above the highest high or below the lowest low of the preceding range lookback bars; bullish requires close > open, bearish requires close < open.
Calculation: Bullish absorption confirmation = a confirmed bullish sweep-and-reclaim of the HTF low or latest confirmed LTF pivot low, with volume / SMA(volume, lookback) at least a threshold, penetration no more than a threshold times ATR(14), and lower wick / candle range at least a threshold; the swept level must be reclaimed by the close. Within the following confirmation window, a confirmed bullish candle must have body size at least a threshold times ATR(14), close above both the candidate candle high and the highest high of the preceding structure lookback bars. On the next completed bar, close must remain above the swept level and at or above the displacement candle midpoint.
Calculation: Bearish absorption confirmation = a confirmed bearish sweep-and-reclaim of the HTF high or latest confirmed LTF pivot high, with volume / SMA(volume, lookback) at least a threshold, penetration no more than a threshold times ATR(14), and upper wick / candle range at least a threshold; the swept level must be reclaimed by the close. Within the following confirmation window, a confirmed bearish candle must have body size at least a threshold times ATR(14), close below both the candidate candle low and the lowest low of the preceding structure lookback bars. On the next completed bar, close must remain below the swept level and at or below the displacement candle midpoint. If both bullish and bearish absorption candidates occur on one bar, reject both.
Long: narrative direction is bullish OR bullish absorption confirmation.
Short: narrative direction is bearish OR bearish absorption confirmation.
