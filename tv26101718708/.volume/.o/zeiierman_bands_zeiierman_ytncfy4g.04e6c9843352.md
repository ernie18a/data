Calculation: Liquidity mean = blend of the simple mean close and the acceptance-weighted mean of wick-adjusted close; acceptance weight = relative volume divided by relative true range.
Calculation: Upper/lower deviation = square root of close dispersion about the liquidity mean squared plus the corresponding wick and liquidity-stress excursion RMS terms squared.
Calculation: Standardized displacement = (close − liquidity mean) / upper deviation above the mean, or (close − liquidity mean) / lower deviation below the mean.
Calculation: Inner reclaim threshold = outer deviation threshold multiplied by the reclaim ratio.
Calculation: OU mean-reversion filter = the log(close / liquidity mean) has an AR(1) half-life within the permitted bounds and normalized liquidity-mean drift below its limit; fit the AR(1) over a longer window than the band calculation. Apply this filter only when enabled.
Calculation: Tension regime = smooth the product of close’s normalized position within the previous completed selected higher-timeframe bar’s range and path efficiency (net displacement divided by summed absolute one-bar changes); cap tension. Use separate build and release smoothing lengths. Bull regime is tension at or above its positive threshold; bear regime is at or below its negative threshold.
Long: OU filter passes (when enabled) AND standardized displacement was at or below the negative outer threshold and remains armed until displacement reaches zero, the OU filter fails, or either reclaim fires AND standardized displacement crosses above the negative inner reclaim threshold and is below zero AND (when trend alignment is enabled) tension regime is bull.
Short: OU filter passes (when enabled) AND standardized displacement was at or above the positive outer threshold and remains armed until displacement reaches zero, the OU filter fails, or either reclaim fires AND standardized displacement crosses below the positive inner reclaim threshold and is above zero AND (when trend alignment is enabled) tension regime is bear.
