Calculation: Adaptive baseline = EMA(close, long lookback), blended with an efficiency-ratio adaptive moving average using the same source and then EMA-smoothed; the adaptive average updates recursively with squared smoothing constant derived from the efficiency ratio and fast/slow periods.
Calculation: Volatility score = clamp(50 + 50 × tanh(((ATR − its longer-window SMA) / its longer-window standard deviation) / 2), 0, 100).
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 for the current session; use EMA(HLC3, 34) when VWAP is unavailable.
Calculation: Channel mean = EMA((adaptive baseline + session VWAP) / 2, max(5, channel lookback / 4)). Base channel distance = ATR × base-channel multiplier × (0.75 + clamp(volatility score / 100, 0, 1)). Outer channel distance = ATR × outer-channel multiplier × (0.85 + clamp(volatility score / 140, 0, 0.85)).
Calculation: Equilibrium = lowest low over the anchor lookback + half the difference between highest high and lowest low over that lookback. Premium level = lowest low + 75% of that difference.
Long: (close > adaptive baseline AND adaptive baseline > its prior-bar value AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (bar confirmed AND low < channel mean − outer channel distance AND close > channel mean − base channel distance) OR (bar confirmed AND close > equilibrium AND prior close <= prior-bar equilibrium AND close < premium level)
Short: (close < adaptive baseline AND adaptive baseline < its prior-bar value AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback)) OR (bar confirmed AND high > channel mean + outer channel distance AND close < channel mean + base channel distance) OR (bar confirmed AND close < equilibrium AND prior close >= prior-bar equilibrium AND close > lowest low + 25% of the anchor-range difference)
