Calculation: Primary VWAP = volume-weighted mean source price accumulated from the start of the current primary anchor period, including the current forming bar; use lower-timeframe bars where available, otherwise chart bars
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = volume-weighted mean source price accumulated from the start of the current week, including the current forming bar
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = Primary VWAP ± the selected multiple of the volume-weighted standard deviation of source price from Primary VWAP, accumulated over the current primary anchor period
Calculation: VWAP slope state = compare (current Primary VWAP − Primary VWAP from the shorter of the slope lookback and bars since the primary-anchor reset) / ATR against a flat threshold; rising above it, falling below its negative, otherwise flat
Calculation: Recent pullback zone = within the setup lookback, a bar has low ≤ Primary VWAP and low > lower band 2 (long), or high ≥ Primary VWAP and high < upper band 2 (short)
Calculation: Lower/upper rejection wick = distance from the bar's low to the lower of open and close / distance from the bar's high to the higher of open and close, respectively; each must be at least the selected fraction of the bar range
Calculation: Setup swing low/high = lowest low/highest high over the setup lookback including the current bar
Long: On a confirmed bar close with Primary VWAP available, positive deviation, after the warm-up and not on the anchor-reset bar, and (Weekly VWAP filter disabled or unavailable or close > Weekly VWAP), and the applicable setup re-armed: (rising VWAP slope state AND Recent pullback zone AND every close in the setup lookback ≥ lower band 1 AND close > Primary VWAP AND close > open AND (lower rejection wick meets threshold OR prior close ≤ prior Primary VWAP) AND (upper band 2 − close) / (close − (setup swing low − ATR buffer)) ≥ minimum reward-to-risk) OR (flat VWAP slope state AND within the setup lookback a bar's low ≤ lower band 2 AND close > lower band 2 AND close < Primary VWAP AND close > open AND (lower rejection wick meets threshold OR prior close ≤ prior lower band 2) AND (Primary VWAP − close) / (close − (setup swing low − ATR buffer)) ≥ minimum reward-to-risk)
Short: On a confirmed bar close with Primary VWAP available, positive deviation, after the warm-up and not on the anchor-reset bar, and (Weekly VWAP filter disabled or unavailable or close < Weekly VWAP), and the applicable setup re-armed: (falling VWAP slope state AND within the setup lookback a bar has high ≥ Primary VWAP and high < upper band 2 AND every close in the setup lookback ≤ upper band 1 AND close < Primary VWAP AND close < open AND (upper rejection wick meets threshold OR prior close ≥ prior Primary VWAP) AND (close − lower band 2) / ((setup swing high + ATR buffer) − close) ≥ minimum reward-to-risk) OR (flat VWAP slope state AND within the setup lookback a bar's high ≥ upper band 2 AND close < upper band 2 AND close > Primary VWAP AND close < open AND (upper rejection wick meets threshold OR prior close ≥ prior upper band 2) AND (close − Primary VWAP) / ((setup swing high + ATR buffer) − close) ≥ minimum reward-to-risk)
