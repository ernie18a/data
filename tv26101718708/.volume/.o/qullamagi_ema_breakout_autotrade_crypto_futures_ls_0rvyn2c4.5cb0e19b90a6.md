Calculation: Five moving averages = fastest EMA, second-fastest EMA, then three progressively slower SMAs.
Calculation: Box high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback of completed bars before the current bar.
Calculation: Tight box = box high − box low <= ATR × box-height multiplier.
Calculation: Pullback proximity = low (long) or high (short) is within the pullback-line distance of ATR × tolerance / close; pullback line is a selected one of the three fastest moving averages.
Calculation: Recent breakout = highest high (long) or lowest low (short) over the breakout lookback > current box high or < current box low, respectively.
Calculation: Trailing stop = selected EMA or SMA; long stop = trailing stop × (1 − buffer), short stop = trailing stop × (1 + buffer).
Calculation: Initial risk = long entry price − long stop at entry; short stop at entry − short entry price.
Calculation: Effective stop = long: max(trailing stop, breakeven stop if activated, hard stop if enabled); short: min(trailing stop, breakeven stop if activated, hard stop if enabled). Breakeven activates when high >= long entry price + initial risk × activation multiple, or low <= short entry price − initial risk × activation multiple; its stop is entry price adjusted by the breakeven offset. Hard stops are entry price adjusted by the maximum-loss percentage.
Long: Position is flat AND ((close > fastest EMA > second-fastest EMA > third MA AND third MA > fourth MA > fifth MA AND each MA > its prior-bar value AND (close crossed above prior-bars box high OR current high > box high and prior close <= box high) AND (box filter disabled OR tight box) AND volume > volume SMA × breakout multiplier) OR (fastest EMA > second-fastest EMA > third MA AND fourth MA > fifth MA AND pullback proximity AND close > pullback line AND close > open if bounce required AND recent breakout AND volume > volume SMA × pullback multiplier)) AND (higher-timeframe close > its 200-period SMA if filter enabled) AND (high − low <= ATR × maximum-candle-range multiplier if wide-candle filter enabled) AND (ADX >= long minimum AND ADX <= maximum if ADX filter enabled) AND (RSI < overbought level if RSI filter enabled) AND cooldown and date-range filters pass.
Short: Position is flat AND ((close < fastest EMA < second-fastest EMA < third MA AND third MA < fourth MA < fifth MA AND each MA < its prior-bar value AND (close crossed below prior-bars box low OR current low < box low and prior close >= box low) AND (box filter disabled OR tight box) AND volume > volume SMA × breakout multiplier) OR (fastest EMA < second-fastest EMA < third MA AND fourth MA < fifth MA AND pullback proximity AND close < pullback line AND close < open if bounce required AND recent breakout AND volume > volume SMA × pullback multiplier)) AND (higher-timeframe close < its 200-period SMA if filter enabled) AND (high − low <= ATR × maximum-candle-range multiplier if wide-candle filter enabled) AND (ADX >= short minimum AND ADX <= maximum if ADX filter enabled) AND (RSI > oversold level if RSI filter enabled) AND (each of the configured preceding bars closes below its open, if required) AND (close < fifth MA if required) AND (higher-timeframe close < its 200-period SMA if required) AND cooldown and date-range filters pass.
Exit: Long, close >= entry price + initial risk × scale-out multiple AND scale-out enabled AND not already scaled.
Exit: Short, close <= entry price − initial risk × scale-out multiple AND scale-out enabled AND not already scaled.
Exit: Long, close < effective stop OR (time exit enabled AND bars in trade >= maximum bars AND close < entry price).
Exit: Short, close > effective stop OR (time exit enabled AND bars in trade >= maximum bars AND close > entry price).
