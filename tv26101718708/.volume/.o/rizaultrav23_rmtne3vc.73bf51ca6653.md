Calculation: WaveTrend WT1 = EMA of ci on the configured WaveTrend timeframe’s still-forming bars, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3, esa = EMA(typical price, shorter lookback), deviation = EMA(abs(typical price − esa), shorter lookback), ci = (typical price − esa) / (0.015 × deviation), and ci’s EMA uses a longer lookback
Calculation: Long trailing stop = entry price × (1 − initial stop %) until a bar’s high exceeds entry price × (1 + activation %); after activation, use the greater of high × (1 − trail %) and the prior long trailing stop, or the fixed stop if no prior stop exists
Calculation: Short trailing stop = entry price × (1 + initial stop %) until a bar’s low falls below entry price × (1 − activation %); after activation, use the lesser of low × (1 + trail %) and the prior short trailing stop, or the fixed stop if no prior stop exists
Long: (MTF1 short SMA > MTF1 long SMA AND prior bar MTF1 short SMA ≤ MTF1 long SMA AND MTF2 short SMA > MTF2 long SMA) OR (MTF1 short SMA > MTF1 long SMA AND MTF2 short SMA > MTF2 long SMA AND prior bar MTF2 short SMA ≤ MTF2 long SMA), AND (WaveTrend WT1 > 0 OR WaveTrend filter disabled)
Short: (MTF1 short SMA < MTF1 long SMA AND prior bar MTF1 short SMA ≥ MTF1 long SMA AND MTF2 short SMA < MTF2 long SMA) OR (MTF1 short SMA < MTF1 long SMA AND MTF2 short SMA < MTF2 long SMA AND prior bar MTF2 short SMA ≥ MTF2 long SMA), AND (WaveTrend WT1 < 0 OR WaveTrend filter disabled)
Exit: Long, prior bar long trailing stop exists AND current low < prior bar long trailing stop
Exit: Short, prior bar short trailing stop exists AND current high > prior bar short trailing stop
