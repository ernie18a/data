Calculation: Regime = ranging when ADX is below its configured threshold; otherwise bullish when close is above the long-term SMA and bearish when below.
Calculation: Swing high/low = latest structural pivot highs/lows, using a pivot lookback that shortens as ATR rises relative to its longer-term average and lengthens as it falls; retain the latest structural levels, updating the high in an uptrend and the low in a downtrend. Use the levels from before the current bar's updates for sweep comparisons.
Calculation: Golden Pocket = the middle retracement zone between the latest swing high and low, oriented in the swing direction.
Calculation: Four-hour trap = four-hour price overlaps the Golden Pocket with volume above its longer-term average by the configured multiplier, or the four-hour bar sweeps below the prior swing low and closes back above it, or sweeps above the prior swing high and closes back below it. Use the current four-hour bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Ready conditions = not ranging; the current daily close is above its long-term SMA for an up swing or below it for a down swing; the current four-hour bar overlaps the Golden Pocket; a four-hour trap occurred within the configured recent-bar window; and reward-to-risk to the base swing target, using the deep retracement level as risk, meets the configured minimum. Use the current daily bar, which may still be forming.
Long: (bar low < prior swing low AND bar close > prior swing low AND bar is confirmed AND not ranging AND bar close > long-term SMA) OR (ready conditions become true AND swing direction is up)
Short: (bar high > prior swing high AND bar close < prior swing high AND bar is confirmed AND not ranging AND bar close < long-term SMA) OR (ready conditions become true AND swing direction is down)
