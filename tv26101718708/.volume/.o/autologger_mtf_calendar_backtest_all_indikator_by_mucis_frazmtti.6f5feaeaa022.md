Calculation: Analysis-timeframe close = close of the selected analysis-timeframe bar, including the bar still forming
Long: Within the backtest date range AND ((selected strategy is EMA Crossover AND the shorter-period EMA crosses above the longer-period EMA AND analysis-timeframe close > the longer-term regime EMA) OR (selected strategy is RSI Strategy AND RSI crosses below the oversold threshold) OR (selected strategy is MACD Strategy AND MACD line crosses above its signal line) OR (selected strategy is Bollinger Bands Breakout AND analysis-timeframe close crosses above the lower band) OR (selected strategy is SuperTrend AND its direction changes from downtrend to uptrend) OR (selected strategy is Custom AND the shorter-period SMA crosses above the longer-period SMA))
Short: Within the backtest date range AND ((selected strategy is EMA Crossover AND the shorter-period EMA crosses below the longer-period EMA AND analysis-timeframe close < the longer-term regime EMA) OR (selected strategy is RSI Strategy AND RSI crosses above the overbought threshold) OR (selected strategy is MACD Strategy AND MACD line crosses below its signal line) OR (selected strategy is Bollinger Bands Breakout AND analysis-timeframe close crosses below the upper band) OR (selected strategy is SuperTrend AND its direction changes from uptrend to downtrend) OR (selected strategy is Custom AND the shorter-period SMA crosses below the longer-period SMA))
Exit: Long, low <= entry price minus the stop-loss percentage of entry price OR high >= entry price plus the take-profit percentage of entry price OR trailing stop is hit after price rises by half the take-profit percentage of entry price, with the stop trailing the favorable high by half the stop-loss percentage of entry price
Exit: Short, high >= entry price plus the stop-loss percentage of entry price OR low <= entry price minus the take-profit percentage of entry price OR trailing stop is hit after price falls by half the take-profit percentage of entry price, with the stop trailing the favorable low by half the stop-loss percentage of entry price
