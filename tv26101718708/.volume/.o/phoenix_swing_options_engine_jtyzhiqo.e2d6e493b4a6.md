Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band; bands use a moving average and standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Volatility compression = 100 − 100 × (current Bollinger Band width − its rolling minimum) / (its rolling maximum − its rolling minimum), using a longer lookback for the width history than for the bands.
Calculation: Directional momentum score = trend score + RSI score + ROC score. Trend score is +2 when fast EMA > middle EMA > slow EMA, +1 when fast EMA > middle EMA otherwise, −2 when fast EMA < middle EMA < slow EMA, −1 when fast EMA < middle EMA otherwise, and 0 otherwise. RSI score is +1 above 55, −1 below 45, otherwise 0. ROC score is +2 when ROC > 0 and its change from 3 bars earlier > 0, +1 when ROC > 0 otherwise, −2 when ROC < 0 and its change from 3 bars earlier < 0, −1 when ROC < 0 otherwise, and 0 otherwise. The three EMA lookbacks are ordered fast, middle, slow; RSI and ROC use their own lookbacks.
Calculation: Energy buildup = the 5-bar moving average of ATR is below its value 5 bars earlier AND the volume moving average is above its value 5 bars earlier.
Calculation: Upper trigger = Donchian midpoint + ATR buffer; lower trigger = Donchian midpoint − ATR buffer. Donchian midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the same lookback.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume > volume moving average × surge multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish armed state is set when volatility compression meets its threshold, directional momentum score meets the positive threshold, and energy buildup is true; it remains set until the score turns negative or the bars since arming exceed the arm window. Bearish armed state is set when volatility compression meets its threshold, directional momentum score meets the negative threshold, and energy buildup is true; it remains set until the score turns positive or the bars since arming exceed the arm window.
Long: bullish armed state AND current close > current upper trigger AND previous close <= previous upper trigger AND volume surge
Short: bearish armed state AND current close < current lower trigger AND previous close >= previous lower trigger AND volume surge
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target multiple × ATR at entry OR low <= entry price − stop multiple × ATR at entry OR directional momentum score <= negative threshold OR bars since entry >= time stop
Exit: short, low <= entry price − target multiple × ATR at entry OR high >= entry price + stop multiple × ATR at entry OR directional momentum score >= positive threshold OR bars since entry >= time stop
