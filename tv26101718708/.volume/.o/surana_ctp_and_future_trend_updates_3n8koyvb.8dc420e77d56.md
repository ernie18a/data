Calculation: Signed volume = volume when close > open; otherwise −volume
Calculation: Forecast signed-volume mean = mean, over offsets 0 through one period, of the average signed volume at that offset and at one and two periods earlier
Long: Sum of signed volume over the most recent period > 0 OR over the immediately preceding period > 0 OR over the period two periods earlier > 0 OR forecast signed-volume mean > 0
Short: Sum of signed volume over the most recent period ≤ 0 OR over the immediately preceding period ≤ 0 OR over the period two periods earlier ≤ 0 OR forecast signed-volume mean ≤ 0
