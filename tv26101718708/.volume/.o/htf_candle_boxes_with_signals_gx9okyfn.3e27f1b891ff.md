Calculation: Opening zone = current higher-timeframe candle’s open ± a multiple of ATR from the selected ATR timeframe, captured when the higher-timeframe candle opens
Calculation: Bull setup = during the current higher-timeframe candle, price first extends below its open by at least a minimum ATR multiple, then a bar closes inside the opening zone; retain the setup until that higher-timeframe candle ends
Calculation: Bear setup = during the current higher-timeframe candle, price first extends above its open by at least a minimum ATR multiple, then a bar closes inside the opening zone; retain the setup until that higher-timeframe candle ends
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ longer-lookback EMA of volume
Long: Bull setup AND previous close is within the opening zone AND current close is above the zone’s upper bound AND the bar is confirmed AND outside the initial buffer after the current higher-timeframe candle opens AND signal cooldown has elapsed AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume oscillator ≥ threshold) AND (last-candles filter is disabled OR every configured number of bars before the zone-touch bar closed below its open)
Short: Bear setup AND previous close is within the opening zone AND current close is below the zone’s lower bound AND the bar is confirmed AND outside the initial buffer after the current higher-timeframe candle opens AND signal cooldown has elapsed AND (volume filter is disabled OR volume oscillator ≥ threshold) AND (last-candles filter is disabled OR every configured number of bars before the zone-touch bar closed above its open)
