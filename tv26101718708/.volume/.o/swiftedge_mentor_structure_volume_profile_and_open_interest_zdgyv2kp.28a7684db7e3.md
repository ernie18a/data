Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar’s high is at least as high as the highs of the configurable number of bars on each side; confirm only after those right-side bars complete. Confirmed swing low = a bar’s low is at most as low as the lows of the configurable number of bars on each side; confirm only after those right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Higher-high trend = newest confirmed swing high > preceding confirmed swing high AND newest confirmed swing low > preceding confirmed swing low. Lower-low trend = newest confirmed swing high < preceding confirmed swing high AND newest confirmed swing low < preceding confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Impulse bar = current high − current low > a configurable multiple of the median high − low over a longer lookback AND |close − open| ≥ a configurable fraction of the current bar’s range.
Long: Higher-high trend
Long: Close > latest confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing high is not higher than the preceding confirmed swing high
Long: Impulse bar AND close > open
Short: Lower-low trend
Short: Close < latest confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing low is not lower than the preceding confirmed swing low
Short: Impulse bar AND close < open
