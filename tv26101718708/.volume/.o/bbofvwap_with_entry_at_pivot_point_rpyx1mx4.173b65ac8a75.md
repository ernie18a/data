Calculation: Pivot point = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed daily or weekly bar
Calculation: Stop-loss level = current close × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Long: VWAP crosses above its Bollinger basis AND close ≥ pivot point
Exit: Long, VWAP crosses below its Bollinger lower band OR (VWAP crosses below its Bollinger upper band AND close > entry price) OR close crosses below the stop-loss level
