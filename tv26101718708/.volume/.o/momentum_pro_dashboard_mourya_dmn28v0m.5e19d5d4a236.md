Calculation: Cumulative buy-volume share = cumulative volume on bars where close > open, plus half the volume on bars where close = open, divided by cumulative volume classified the same way as buy or sell volume.
Long: Cumulative buy-volume share > 50%
Short: Cumulative buy-volume share <= 50%
Long: Current 1-second bar close > previous 1-second bar close
Short: Current 1-second bar close < previous 1-second bar close
