Calculation: SuperTrend = Choose one moving average type (SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, or VWMA) and length; compute it separately on close × volume and volume, then divide the first average by the second. Add/subtract ATR(length) × multiplier for the initial upper/lower bands. Carry the lower band forward unless the new lower band rises or the previous close is below the prior lower band; carry the upper band forward unless the new upper band falls or the previous close is above the prior upper band. Initialize direction to 1 until prior ATR exists; thereafter set direction to 1 if the prior SuperTrend equals the prior upper band and close is not above the current upper band, otherwise −1 in that case; when the prior SuperTrend does not equal the prior upper band, set direction to 1 if close is below the current lower band, otherwise −1. SuperTrend is the lower band when direction is −1, otherwise the upper band.
Calculation: KNN score = For each of the n most recent SuperTrend values, label it 1 if the weighted moving average of close over the price lookback is greater than the weighted moving average of SuperTrend over the prediction lookback, otherwise 0. Find the k values nearest to the current SuperTrend by absolute distance; weight each label by 1/(distance + ε), and divide their weighted label sum by the sum of their weights. n is at least k. The price and prediction lookbacks are independently set.
Long: KNN score = 1 AND prior KNN score ≠ 1 OR (direction = −1 AND prior direction = 1 AND KNN score = 1)
Short: KNN score = 0 AND prior KNN score ≠ 0 OR (direction = 1 AND prior direction = −1 AND KNN score = 0)
Exit: Long, neither (direction = −1 AND KNN score = 1) nor (direction = 1 AND KNN score = 0), AND low ≤ SuperTrend − ATR(length) × multiplier
Exit: Short, neither (direction = −1 AND KNN score = 1) nor (direction = 1 AND KNN score = 0), AND high ≥ SuperTrend + ATR(length) × multiplier
