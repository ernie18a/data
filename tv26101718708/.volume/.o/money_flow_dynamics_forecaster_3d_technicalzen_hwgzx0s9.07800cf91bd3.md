Calculation: Money-flow middle slope = change per forecast lookback bar in the centered, clamped HMA of the selected MFI or RSI series, using the middle momentum layer; use the same lookback for both middle-layer slopes
Calculation: Second-signal middle slope = change per forecast lookback bar in the middle oscillator layer: either the 2-bar percentage slope of an HMA of VWMA(close), or an HMA of ADX signed by the +DI/−DI direction; use the same lookback as the money-flow middle slope
Long: Money-flow middle slope > 0 AND second-signal middle slope > 0
Short: Money-flow middle slope < 0 AND second-signal middle slope < 0
Exit: long, Money-flow middle slope < 0 AND second-signal middle slope < 0
Exit: long, NOT (Money-flow middle slope > 0 AND second-signal middle slope > 0) AND NOT (Money-flow middle slope < 0 AND second-signal middle slope < 0)
Exit: short, NOT (Money-flow middle slope > 0 AND second-signal middle slope > 0) AND NOT (Money-flow middle slope < 0 AND second-signal middle slope < 0)
