Calculation: Bullish reversal context = fast EMA < base EMA < slow EMA and the base EMA’s change over five bars divided by ATR is at or below a negative trend-slope threshold, with the most recent qualifying pullback touch within the failure window; bearish reversal context is the inverse.
Calculation: Pullback touch = in a bullish trend, low ≤ fast EMA + ATR × reach and close < fast EMA + ATR × touch allowance; in a bearish trend, high ≥ fast EMA − ATR × reach and close > fast EMA − ATR × touch allowance.
Calculation: Control rail = base EMA − ATR × control buffer for a bearish reversal, or base EMA + ATR × control buffer for a bullish reversal.
Calculation: Invalidation rail = fast EMA + ATR × invalidation buffer for a bullish reversal, or fast EMA − ATR × invalidation buffer for a bearish reversal.
Calculation: Failure score = 0.23 × depth quality + 0.27 × control-loss quality + 0.20 × close quality + 0.15 × participation quality + 0.15 × follow-through quality, rounded and clamped to 0–100; depth quality is a 0–100 normalized peak score of pullback depth, highest at the preferred depth and lower for shallower or excessive depth; control-loss quality is the normalized distance beyond the control rail; close quality is the normalized close location toward the reversal end of the candle range, multiplied by 0.55 when the candle body does not point in the reversal direction; participation quality is current volume divided by its moving average, normalized by a volume threshold; follow-through quality is the normalized distance of the close beyond the prior three-bar low for a bearish reversal or high for a bullish reversal.
Long: bullish reversal context AND confirmed bar AND close ≥ invalidation rail AND ((close ≤ control rail AND failure score ≥ watch threshold) OR (close > control rail AND (close > open OR volume meets confirmation threshold) AND strict-mode requirements are met AND failure score ≥ minimum threshold))
Short: bearish reversal context AND confirmed bar AND close ≤ invalidation rail AND ((close ≥ control rail AND failure score ≥ watch threshold) OR (close < control rail AND (close < open OR volume meets confirmation threshold) AND strict-mode requirements are met AND failure score ≥ minimum threshold))
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND bullish reversal context AND close < invalidation rail
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND bearish reversal context AND close > invalidation rail
