Calculation: Short score = count of these 8 conditions that are true: fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA AND fast and medium EMAs are each below their value from a shorter lookback; close < medium EMA; RSI < 55 AND above its oversold threshold AND below its prior value; MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram <= its prior value AND MACD line crossed below signal line within the recent 3 bars; smoothed stochastic %K crossed below %D while %K > its oversold threshold OR %K < %D AND %K is between its oversold and overbought thresholds; close crossed below the Bollinger middle band OR high >= upper band AND close < upper band; ADX >= its minimum AND -DI > +DI; close < open AND open - close > 0.4 * (high - low).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend filter = fast EMA > slow EMA for long, or fast EMA < slow EMA for short, using the same fast and slow EMA lookbacks as the chart timeframe and the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Long score >= minimum confluence score AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA (when that filter is enabled) AND ATR < its 20-bar SMA * maximum ATR multiplier AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its 20-bar SMA * volume multiplier) AND bars since last long signal >= cooldown AND NOT (short conditions also qualify).
Short: Short score >= minimum confluence score AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA (when that filter is enabled) AND ATR < its 20-bar SMA * maximum ATR multiplier AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its 20-bar SMA * volume multiplier) AND bars since last short signal >= cooldown AND NOT (long conditions also qualify).
