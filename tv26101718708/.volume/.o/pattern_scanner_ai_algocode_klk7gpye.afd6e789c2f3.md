Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high greater than the highs in the configured number of bars on both sides; usable after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows in the configured number of bars on both sides; usable after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Equality tolerance = ATR × tolerance parameter in ATR mode, or close × percentage parameter in percent mode
Calculation: Near(a,b) = |a−b| ≤ Equality tolerance
