Calculation: Adaptive fast/slow exponential averages = exponential averages of close using fast and slow base lengths multiplied by 1.6 minus the efficiency ratio smoothed with a shorter EMA; the fast base length is shorter than the slow base length. Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the slow base lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Ribbon direction = fast average above slow average for up, below for down; a flip occurs when this direction changes. Trend age = bars since the latest flip.
Calculation: Chop = number of ribbon flips in the recent lookback window exceeds the allowed maximum.
Calculation: Trend strength = 3-bar EMA of a score combining average separation (absolute fast/slow difference divided by ATR, capped at 2 and halved) and smoothed efficiency; when volume is available and included, also combine directional volume support. Without volume, weights are 0.55 separation and 0.45 × 1.5 × efficiency; with volume, weights are 0.4 separation, 0.35 × 1.5 × efficiency, and 0.25 volume support. Clamp the score to 0–1. Volume support is the 20-bar sum of volume positive on up candles and negative otherwise, divided by 20-bar average volume × 20; align its sign with ribbon direction and clamp to 0–1. Steady strength is score ≥ 0.32.
Long: ribbon direction is up AND trend age ≥ confirmation period AND close > both ribbon averages AND direction differs from the previous trend-start signal AND NOT chop.
Short: ribbon direction is down AND trend age ≥ confirmation period AND close < both ribbon averages AND direction differs from the previous trend-start signal AND NOT chop.
Long: ribbon direction is up AND no flip on this bar AND trend age ≥ minimum trend age AND direction matches the previous trend-start signal AND a pullback was previously armed by low reaching at least the configured depth into the ribbon without closing back above its top AND close > both ribbon averages AND close > open AND bars since the previous pullback ≥ minimum spacing AND trend strength ≥ 0.32 AND NOT chop.
Short: ribbon direction is down AND no flip on this bar AND trend age ≥ minimum trend age AND direction matches the previous trend-start signal AND a pullback was previously armed by high reaching at least the configured depth into the ribbon without closing back below its bottom AND close < both ribbon averages AND close < open AND bars since the previous pullback ≥ minimum spacing AND trend strength ≥ 0.32 AND NOT chop.
