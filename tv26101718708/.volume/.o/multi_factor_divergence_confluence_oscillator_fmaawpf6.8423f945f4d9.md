Calculation: normalized lens = (value − rolling mean(value, normalization lookback)) / rolling standard deviation(value, normalization lookback)
Calculation: VWAP deviation lens = normalized (close − session VWAP of typical price, weighted by volume)
Calculation: directional range lens = normalized (high − low) × sign(close − close at a shorter lookback)
Calculation: efficiency lens = normalized ((close − close at a lookback) / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback)
Calculation: SAR extension lens = normalized (capped distance from the parabolic stop to the high when close is above the stop, or to the low when close is below it, divided by ATR) × direction of close versus the stop
Calculation: momentum lens = normalized RSI of close
Calculation: equilibrium lens = normalized ((close − linear regression of close over a lookback) / ATR)
Calculation: cycle lens = normalized weighted sum of smoothed close rate-of-change series, using progressively longer lookbacks and smoothing periods
Calculation: order-flow lens = normalized (cumulative signed volume − its exponential moving average); signed volume = volume × (2 × clamp((close − low) / (high − low), 0, 1) − 1), using 0.5 when high equals low
Calculation: composite = mean of the active, available lenses; exclude the basis lens because it requires an external symbol series
Calculation: composite extreme bands = ± composite rolling standard deviation × an extreme multiplier
Calculation: divergence gap threshold = a minimum-gap multiplier × rolling standard deviation of the composite
Long: at least the timeframe-adaptive minimum number of active lenses are at or below their negative extreme threshold OR at consecutive confirmed price low pivots within the maximum pivot gap, the later low is lower, the composite swing low is higher by at least the divergence gap threshold, and the later composite swing low is at or below the negative composite overbought/oversold band OR the later price low pivot is higher and the later composite swing low is lower by at least the divergence gap threshold OR a confirmed composite pivot low is at or below the negative composite extreme band
Short: at least the timeframe-adaptive minimum number of active lenses are at or above their positive extreme threshold OR at consecutive confirmed price high pivots within the maximum pivot gap, the later high is higher, the composite swing high is lower by at least the divergence gap threshold, and the later composite swing high is at or above the positive composite overbought/oversold band OR the later price high pivot is lower and the later composite swing high is higher by at least the divergence gap threshold OR a confirmed composite pivot high is at or above the positive composite extreme band
