Calculation: Volume profile levels = on each completed chart bar, distribute each of the recent lookback bars’ volume equally among the price bins spanned by its low–high range; POC is the bin with most volume, and VAH/VAL bound the contiguous bins expanded from POC toward the heavier neighboring bin until the selected fraction of total volume is enclosed.
Calculation: Acceptance probability = logistic-regression probability from seven features, oriented to the edge-break direction: volume share at the broken edge bin relative to the largest bin; tanh of the 3-bar close change divided by ATR; volume divided by its 20-bar average minus 1; close’s distance from POC divided by ATR; tanh of the short-term EMA minus the longer-term EMA divided by ATR; ATR divided by its 50-bar average minus 1; and value-area width divided by ATR. Initialize model weights at zero; update online by logistic loss with L2 regularization when a pending edge event resolves. Label acceptance when price closes beyond the edge by the acceptance ATR distance, rejection when it closes back beyond the edge by the rejection ATR distance, and discard unresolved events at the horizon.
Calculation: Acceptance threshold = lowest threshold plus the selected bandit arm times the threshold step; select the arm by upper-confidence bound separately for the low, middle, or high ATR-percentile regime. Update arm reward when its edge event resolves, rewarding a correct breakout/rejection call, penalizing an incorrect call, and giving a small reward when neither call was made.
Long: after warmup, on a completed bar, (close crosses above VAH AND acceptance probability for the upward break >= acceptance threshold) OR (close crosses below VAL AND acceptance probability for the downward break <= 1 − acceptance threshold)
Short: after warmup, on a completed bar, (close crosses below VAL AND acceptance probability for the downward break >= acceptance threshold) OR (close crosses above VAH AND acceptance probability for the upward break <= 1 − acceptance threshold)
