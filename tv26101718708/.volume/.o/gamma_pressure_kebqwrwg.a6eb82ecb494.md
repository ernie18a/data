Calculation: composite pressure = EMA-smoothed, weighted average of normalized current-timeframe signals: close change over a lookback divided by ATR; inverse change in the selected volatility index; session-reset signed volume with per-bar decay divided by average volume; mean of normalized fast- and slow-EMA slopes, where the slow EMA lookback is longer; inverse DXY change; and candle body-to-range ratio multiplied by relative volume. Clip each signal to [-1, 1] and scale the result to [-100, 100]. Use same-timeframe volatility-index and DXY closes.
Long: composite pressure crosses above zero OR crosses above its positive strong level OR exceeds its positive extreme level
Short: composite pressure crosses below zero OR crosses below its negative strong level OR falls below its negative extreme level
