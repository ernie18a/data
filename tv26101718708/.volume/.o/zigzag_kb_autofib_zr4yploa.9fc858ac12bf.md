Calculation: Recent downswing = the latest confirmed ZigZag swing from a high A to a lower low B
Calculation: Entry-zone upper boundary = B plus the configured upper Fibonacci retracement of the distance from B to A
Calculation: Take-profit lower boundary = B plus the configured lower Fibonacci retracement of the distance from B to A
Long: close > entry-zone upper boundary AND previous close <= entry-zone upper boundary AND close > both the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs AND no bar from B through the current bar has reached the take-profit lower boundary
Exit: Long, high >= take-profit lower boundary OR close < both the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs
