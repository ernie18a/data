Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low of bars in the configured opening-range session, updated as that session forms
Long: During the market session, either close crosses above opening-range high OR (two bars ago low < opening-range high AND two bars ago close > opening-range high AND previous bar low > opening-range high AND previous bar close > opening-range high AND current close > previous bar low AND current low > opening-range high); AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA)
Short: During the market session, either close crosses below opening-range low OR (two bars ago high > opening-range low AND two bars ago close < opening-range low AND previous bar high < opening-range low AND previous bar close < opening-range low AND current close < previous bar high AND current high < opening-range low); AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA)
