Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the prior lookback bars, excluding the current bar; lock both values when a test begins
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close[lookback]) / (lookback × average(abs(change in close), lookback))
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over the lookback
Long: On a confirmed bar with no active test or accepted state, close > prior range high + ATR × break distance AND NOT (close < prior range low − ATR × break distance); OR while testing above the locked range high, the accumulated count of closes above locked range high + ATR × break distance reaches the required count AND efficiency ratio ≥ minimum AND (volume check disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum) AND test duration is within its maximum; OR while accepted above the locked range high and within the discovery duration, the first bar after acceptance has low ≤ locked range high + ATR × retest tolerance AND close ≥ locked range high AND close is not below locked range high − ATR × failure distance; OR while testing below the locked range low, close < locked range low − ATR × failure distance
Short: On a confirmed bar with no active test or accepted state, close < prior range low − ATR × break distance AND NOT (close > prior range high + ATR × break distance); OR while testing below the locked range low, the accumulated count of closes below locked range low − ATR × break distance reaches the required count AND efficiency ratio ≥ minimum AND (volume check disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum) AND test duration is within its maximum; OR while accepted below the locked range low and within the discovery duration, the first bar after acceptance has high ≥ locked range low − ATR × retest tolerance AND close ≤ locked range low AND close is not above locked range low + ATR × failure distance; OR while testing above the locked range high, close < locked range high − ATR × failure distance
