Calculation: Per-timeframe sentiment = round((raw score + 8) / 16 × 100), where raw score adds: +1/−1/0 for close above/below/equal open; +1 if close is at least 65% of the bar range from its low, −1 if at most 35%, otherwise 0; +1/−1/0 for EMA(8)>EMA(21)>EMA(55) / EMA(8)<EMA(21)<EMA(55); +1/−1/0 for RSI(14)>58 / RSI(14)<42; +1/−1/0 for MACD(12,26,9) histogram >0 / <0; +1/−1/0 for an up/down bar with volume >1.5×SMA(volume,20); +1/−1/0 for Stochastic(14)>65 / <35; and +1/−1/0 when current close is above/below both the prior close and the close two bars earlier. Apply on four configured timeframes in increasing duration, using each timeframe’s current forming bar.
Calculation: Total sentiment = round(10% × shortest-timeframe sentiment + 20% × next-shortest + 30% × next-longest + 40% × longest-timeframe sentiment).
Calculation: Composite = round(40% × RSI(14) + 35% × clamp(MACD(12,26,9) histogram / close × 10000 + 50, 0, 100) + 25% × clamp(((EMA(volume,5) − EMA(volume,20)) / EMA(volume,20)) × 100 + 50, 0, 100)).
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the latest confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low and the composite at the latest pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot. Bearish divergence = the latest confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high and the composite at the latest pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot. Hidden bullish divergence = latest pivot low is higher and its composite is lower; hidden bearish divergence = latest pivot high is lower and its composite is higher. Confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks; evaluate divergence when the right-side confirmation completes.
Calculation: Bullish divergence strength = count of these conditions: RSI(14)<50 and above its value two pivot-lookbacks ago; MACD histogram<0 and above its value two pivot-lookbacks ago; volume oscillator<0 and above its value two pivot-lookbacks ago. Bearish strength counts the inverse conditions: RSI>50 and below its earlier value; MACD histogram>0 and below its earlier value; volume oscillator>0 and below its earlier value. Volume oscillator = (EMA(volume,5) − EMA(volume,20)) / EMA(volume,20) × 100.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias points = count of: weekly close>weekly open; weekly close>weekly EMA(21); daily close>daily open; daily close>daily EMA(21); daily close>daily EMA(55); daily close>daily EMA(200); daily EMA(21)>daily EMA(55); and daily recovery, where (daily close − lowest daily low over the lookback)/(highest daily high over the lookback − lowest daily low over the lookback)>0.5 and daily close>daily EMA(21). Use current forming weekly and daily bars and a lookback of 20 daily bars.
