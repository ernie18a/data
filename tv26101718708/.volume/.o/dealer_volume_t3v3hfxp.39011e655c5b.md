Calculation: Smoothed regime score = EMA of tanh(0.5 × delta + 0.3 × tanh((current close − volume-weighted bin midpoint) / ATR(14)) + 0.2 × value-area position), using current-bar data; delta = (buy volume − sell volume) / total volume, and value-area position is 1 above VAH, −1 below VAL, otherwise 0. Build bins over a rolling lookback including the current, possibly forming bar, spreading each bar’s volume uniformly across its high–low range and splitting it by candle-body direction (50/50 for a doji). The volume-weighted bin midpoint is weighted by total bin volume. Starting from the highest-volume bin, expand the value area by adding the larger adjacent-volume bin until it contains 70% of total volume.
Calculation: Regime state = bullish when the smoothed regime score exceeds the upper threshold; bearish when it falls below the lower threshold. Otherwise retain the prior bullish or bearish state; from neutral, remain neutral.
