Calculation: Rally day = number of bars since the most recent bar with the lowest low in the rally lookback, plus one (the low bar is day 1)
Calculation: Distribution day = close < previous close × (1 − minimum decline fraction) AND volume > previous volume
Calculation: Fifth distribution day low = low of the fifth most recent distribution day among the current and previous 19 bars
Long: rally day is 4–7 AND close / previous close − 1 ≥ minimum follow-through gain AND volume > previous volume when higher-volume confirmation is required
Short: Distribution day
Short: fifth distribution day low exists AND close < fifth distribution day low
