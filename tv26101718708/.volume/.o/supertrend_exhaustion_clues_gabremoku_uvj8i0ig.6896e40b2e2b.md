Calculation: Bullish clue score = count of +DI > -DI, close > session VWAP, close > previous close, and current |close − SuperTrend|/ATR > previous bar’s value; compare with configured directional clue threshold.
Calculation: Bearish clue score = count of -DI > +DI, close < session VWAP, close < previous close, and current |close − SuperTrend|/ATR > previous bar’s value; compare with configured directional clue threshold.
Calculation: Bullish quality score = count of bullish strength, bullish acceptance, distance expansion, and bullish compression release. Bullish strength is true when ADX context is disabled, or ADX meets its configured threshold, +DI > -DI, and (rising ADX is not required or ADX > previous ADX). Bullish acceptance is true when VWAP context is disabled, or close > session VWAP and close > SuperTrend. Distance expansion is true when distance expansion is disabled, or current |close − SuperTrend|/ATR > its previous-bar value. Bullish compression release is true when a squeeze ends and the SuperTrend direction is up.
Calculation: Bearish quality score = count of bearish strength, bearish acceptance, distance expansion, and bearish compression release. Bearish strength is true when ADX context is disabled, or ADX meets its configured threshold, -DI > +DI, and (rising ADX is not required or ADX > previous ADX). Bearish acceptance is true when VWAP context is disabled, or close < session VWAP and close < SuperTrend. Distance expansion is true when distance expansion is disabled, or current |close − SuperTrend|/ATR > its previous-bar value. Bearish compression release is true when a squeeze ends and the SuperTrend direction is down.
Calculation: Exhausted uptrend = SuperTrend direction is up, the direction did not flip on this bar, bullish quality score is at most the configured exhaustion maximum, and no bullish compression release qualifies as a compressed break at its configured minimum score.
Calculation: Exhausted downtrend = SuperTrend direction is down, the direction did not flip on this bar, bearish quality score is at most the configured exhaustion maximum, and no bearish compression release qualifies as a compressed break at its configured minimum score.
Long: bullish clue score meets its configured threshold AND ((countertrend exhaustion is enabled AND SuperTrend direction is down AND Exhausted downtrend) OR (continuation exhaustion is enabled AND SuperTrend direction is up AND Exhausted uptrend)) AND bullish setup was false on the previous bar AND no long sequence is active.
Short: bearish clue score meets its configured threshold AND ((countertrend exhaustion is enabled AND SuperTrend direction is up AND Exhausted uptrend) OR (continuation exhaustion is enabled AND SuperTrend direction is down AND Exhausted downtrend)) AND bearish setup was false on the previous bar AND no short sequence is active.
