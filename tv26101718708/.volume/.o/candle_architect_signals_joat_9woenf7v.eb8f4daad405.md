Calculation: Candidate score = pattern base score + trend contribution (full weight when trend matches, partial when mixed, penalty when opposite) + location bonus + structure bonus + momentum bonus or penalty + volume bonus + wick/strong-body bonus + capped absolute regression-slope-to-ATR ratio; each candidate uses its pattern-specific base score and applicable bullish or bearish context tests. Select the highest score by checking candidates in this order and replacing the winner only on a strictly higher score: bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing, hammer, inverted hammer, shooting star, hanging man, morning star, evening star, piercing, dark cloud, bullish harami, bearish harami, inside-bar upside break, inside-bar downside break, bullish marubozu, bearish marubozu, three advancing bullish candles, three declining bearish candles, tweezer low, tweezer high, bullish rail, bearish rail, bullish reclaim, bearish rejection. A bullish/bearish engulfing is a confirmed candle respectively closing above/below its open, opposite in direction to the prior candle, opening at or beyond the prior close in the engulfing direction, closing at or beyond the prior open, and with body at least a fraction of the prior body. Hammer and shooting star require a directional candle, dominant corresponding wick relative to body, opposite wick limited relative to range, and close location near the relevant range extreme. Inverted hammer requires a bullish candle, long upper wick, sufficiently high close location, and proximity to the lower regression field. Hanging man requires a bearish candle, long lower wick, proximity to the upper regression field, and a close location below its threshold. Morning/evening star requires a directional first candle, a small-bodied middle candle, and a final opposite-direction candle closing beyond the midpoint of the first candle's body. Piercing/dark cloud requires the opposite-direction prior candle, a gap beyond its close, and a current close beyond the midpoint of its body but short of its open. Bullish/bearish harami requires opposite-direction consecutive candles, current range inside the prior range, and current close beyond the prior open in the current candle's direction. Inside-bar breaks require the prior bar inside the bar before it and current close beyond the prior high/low. Marubozu requires a directional candle with large body fraction, close near the corresponding range extreme, and a small opposing wick. Three soldiers/crows require three consecutive same-direction candles with successively advancing/declining closes and a sufficiently large current body fraction. Tweezer low/high requires adjacent lows/highs within an ATR-based tolerance, opposite-direction consecutive candles, and close near the corresponding range extreme. Rail requires opposite-direction consecutive candles, sufficiently large current body fraction, similar body sizes within an ATR-based tolerance, and close beyond the prior high/low. Bullish reclaim requires a low below the latest confirmed pivot low, a close back above it, and bullish wick rejection; bearish rejection requires a high above the latest confirmed pivot high, a close back below it, and bearish wick rejection. Context uses fast and slow EMAs (fast shorter than slow), regression slope and residual standard deviation, RSI, ATR, and current volume relative to its longer-lookback average. Structure levels are the latest confirmed pivot high and low, using left- and right-side pivot bars. Candle and score thresholds, score bases and weights, pivot lookbacks, cooldown, and indicator lookbacks are parameters. All references to current candle values use the bar being evaluated; prior-candle references use completed earlier bars. Calculation: Bullish CHoCH = confirmed bar closes above the latest confirmed pivot high and the preceding close was at or below that level; bearish CHoCH = confirmed bar closes below the latest confirmed pivot low and the preceding close was at or above that level. Calculation: Cooldown ready = no prior accepted signal, or bars since the prior accepted signal exceed the cooldown parameter. Long: bullish CHoCH OR (confirmed bar AND cooldown ready AND highest candidate score meets the minimum score AND highest-scoring candidate is bullish). Short: bearish CHoCH OR (confirmed bar AND cooldown ready AND highest candidate score meets the minimum score AND highest-scoring candidate is bearish).
