Calculation: ORB High = highest high during the opening-range window
Calculation: ORB Low = lowest low during the opening-range window
Calculation: ORB Midpoint = (ORB High + ORB Low) / 2
Long: ORB formed AND within the daily entry window AND no trade has been taken that day AND session not cancelled AND (range filter disabled OR ORB High - ORB Low > ATR × minimum-range multiplier) AND a prior close > ORB High AND current bar is later than that breakout bar AND low < ORB High AND min(open, close) > ORB High AND minimum wick percentage ≤ (ORB High - low) / (ORB High - ORB Midpoint) × 100 ≤ maximum wick percentage AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (4-hour VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP of the still-forming 4-hour bar)
Short: ORB formed AND within the daily entry window AND no trade has been taken that day AND session not cancelled AND (range filter disabled OR ORB High - ORB Low > ATR × minimum-range multiplier) AND a prior close < ORB Low AND current bar is later than that breakout bar AND high > ORB Low AND max(open, close) < ORB Low AND minimum wick percentage ≤ (high - ORB Low) / (ORB Midpoint - ORB Low) × 100 ≤ maximum wick percentage AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (4-hour VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP of the still-forming 4-hour bar)
Exit: long, low ≤ ORB Midpoint OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price - ORB Midpoint) × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: short, high ≥ ORB Midpoint OR low ≤ entry price - (ORB Midpoint - entry price) × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: long, if partial take profit is enabled and close ≥ entry price + (entry price - ORB Midpoint) × partial reward-to-risk ratio, close the configured partial position; if stop trailing is enabled, thereafter exit when low ≤ ORB High or high ≥ entry price + (entry price - ORB Midpoint) × reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: short, if partial take profit is enabled and close ≤ entry price - (ORB Midpoint - entry price) × partial reward-to-risk ratio, close the configured partial position; if stop trailing is enabled, thereafter exit when high ≥ ORB Low or low ≤ entry price - (ORB Midpoint - entry price) × reward-to-risk ratio
