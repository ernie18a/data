Calculation: Tenkan = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shorter Ichimoku lookback
Calculation: Kijun = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than Tenkan
Calculation: Senkou A = average of Tenkan and Kijun
Calculation: Senkou B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback longer than Kijun
Calculation: Cloud upper/lower = maximum/minimum of Senkou A and Senkou B from cloud offset minus one bars ago
Calculation: Chikou momentum = close minus close from Chikou offset minus one bars ago
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line, using close with a shorter fast lookback than slow lookback
Long: Tenkan > Kijun AND Chikou momentum > 0 AND close > Cloud upper AND MACD histogram > 0 AND CMF > 0.1 AND TSI > 0
Short: Tenkan < Kijun AND Chikou momentum < 0 AND close < Cloud lower AND MACD histogram < 0 AND CMF < -0.1 AND TSI < 0
Exit: long, Tenkan < Kijun AND Chikou momentum < 0 AND close < Cloud lower AND MACD histogram < 0 AND CMF < -0.1 AND TSI < 0
Exit: short, Tenkan > Kijun AND Chikou momentum > 0 AND close > Cloud upper AND MACD histogram > 0 AND CMF > 0.1 AND TSI > 0
