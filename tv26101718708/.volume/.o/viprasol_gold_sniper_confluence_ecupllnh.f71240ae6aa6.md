Calculation: Bull score = count of: close > VWAP; RSI(close) > 50; MACD line > signal line; fast EMA > slow EMA; ADX > threshold and close > fast EMA; volume > SMA(volume) and close > open; confirmation-timeframe RSI(close) > 50.
Calculation: Bear score = count of the mirrored conditions: close < VWAP; RSI(close) < 50; MACD line < signal line; fast EMA < slow EMA; ADX > threshold and close < fast EMA; volume > SMA(volume) and close < open; confirmation-timeframe RSI(close) < 50.
Calculation: ADR = SMA of daily high minus low over the configured lookback, using completed daily bars only.
Calculation: Today's range = highest high minus lowest low so far in the current day, aggregated from chart bars.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA on a confirmed bar AND bull score meets the configured minimum AND (session filter is off OR chart is daily or higher OR time is in either configured trading session) AND (ADR filter is off OR chart is weekly/monthly OR today's range divided by ADR is below the configured block threshold) AND (gold-only filter is off OR chart is identified as gold) AND (no prior entry exists OR current bar is at least the configured minimum gap after the prior entry).
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA on a confirmed bar AND bear score meets the configured minimum AND (session filter is off OR chart is daily or higher OR time is in either configured trading session) AND (ADR filter is off OR chart is weekly/monthly OR today's range divided by ADR is below the configured block threshold) AND (gold-only filter is off OR chart is identified as gold) AND (no prior entry exists OR current bar is at least the configured minimum gap after the prior entry).
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low <= working stop OR high >= the configured final target; working stop is entry price minus ATR-based initial risk, moving to entry price after the first target is touched when breakeven is enabled.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high >= working stop OR low <= the configured final target; working stop is entry price plus ATR-based initial risk, moving to entry price after the first target is touched when breakeven is enabled.
