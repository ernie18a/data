Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi open and close = apply RMA to open, high, low, and close; calculate Heikin-Ashi close as their average and Heikin-Ashi open as the average of the prior Heikin-Ashi open and close (initialize with the average of smoothed open and close); apply WMA to the resulting Heikin-Ashi open and close
Calculation: Classic daily pivot levels = from the last completed daily bar, P = (high + low + close) / 3; R1 = 2P − low; R2 = P + high − low; S1 = 2P − high; S2 = P − high + low
Long: (EMA(close, short) > EMA(close, longer) AND EMA(close, longer) > SMA(close, short) AND SMA(close, short) > SMA(close, longer) AND smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND ADX is rising and above its threshold AND +DI > −DI AND a volume spike occurred on this bar or within the prior 3 bars) OR (low or close is within 0.5 ATR of S1 OR low is within 0.5 ATR of S2) AND smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND fast triple-EMA TRIX > slow triple-EMA TRIX
Short: (EMA(close, short) < EMA(close, longer) AND EMA(close, longer) < SMA(close, short) AND SMA(close, short) < SMA(close, longer) AND smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND ADX is rising and above its threshold AND −DI > +DI AND a volume spike occurred on this bar or within the prior 3 bars) OR (high or close is within 0.5 ATR of R1 OR high is within 0.5 ATR of R2) AND smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= prior smoothed Heikin-Ashi open AND fast triple-EMA TRIX <= slow triple-EMA TRIX
