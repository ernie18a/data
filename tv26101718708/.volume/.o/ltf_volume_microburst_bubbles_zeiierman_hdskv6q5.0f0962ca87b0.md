Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume ratio = lower-timeframe volume divided by its EMA volume baseline, calculated separately for each enabled session or across all bars if no session is enabled; include the current lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Directional efficiency = (lower-timeframe close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); a qualifying spike has volume ratio at least the spike threshold and absolute efficiency at least the minimum efficiency.
Calculation: Microburst score = 100 × directional energy balance × concentration weight. For each qualifying spike, energy = (0.25 + max(volume ratio − spike threshold, 0)) × absolute efficiency. Directional energy balance = (sum of positive-efficiency energy − sum of negative-efficiency energy) / total energy. Concentration is the normalized sum of squared energy shares, with 1 for a single spike and 0 for none. Participation = 1 − exp(−total energy / saturation constant); concentration weight = sqrt(participation × (0.35 + 0.65 × concentration)). Aggregate over active lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar.
Long: Valid lower timeframe AND at least one active lower-timeframe bar with a qualifying spike AND Microburst score > 0
