Calculation: ATR = average true range; normalize distances by the current ATR.
Calculation: Trend side = bullish when fast EMA > base EMA > slow EMA; bearish when fast EMA < base EMA < slow EMA; otherwise no active trend.
Calculation: Pullback depth = for bullish trend, (highest high over the pullback lookback − current low) / ATR; for bearish trend, (current high − lowest low over the pullback lookback) / ATR.
Calculation: Target room = distance in the trend direction from close to the prior directional extreme over the target lookback, using only completed bars; if that reference is missing or leaves less than the minimum usable room, use the configured ATR-based room benchmark.
Calculation: Continuation score = clamp to 0–100 the weighted sum of trend score (24%), pullback-depth score (22%), volume score (16%), defense-response score (22%), and target-room score (16%). Trend score combines EMA-stack strength (62%) and normalized base-EMA slope (38%). Depth score is highest near the configured ideal ATR depth and declines for shallower or deeper pullbacks. Volume score rewards lower current volume relative to its moving average during an active pullback or against-trend candle. Defense-response score combines close location in the bar (38%), rejection wick in the trend direction (30%), and close relative to the base EMA or pocket (32%). Target-room score increases with ATR-normalized room.
Calculation: Pullback pocket = the area spanning the base EMA and slow EMA, expanded by the configured ATR pocket width on both sides.
Calculation: Invalidation level = slow EMA minus the configured ATR buffer for bullish trend; slow EMA plus the buffer for bearish trend.
Long: bullish trend AND current bar touches the pullback pocket AND invalidation has not occurred AND continuation score ≥ effective ready score AND defense-response score ≥ configured minimum AND target room ≥ the greater of 45% of configured clean-room distance and configured follow-through distance AND the risk-watch condition is false.
Short: bearish trend AND current bar touches the pullback pocket AND invalidation has not occurred AND continuation score ≥ effective ready score AND defense-response score ≥ configured minimum AND target room ≥ the greater of 45% of configured clean-room distance and configured follow-through distance AND the risk-watch condition is false.
Long: a qualified bullish follow-through episode reaches its frozen favorable ATR target before its frozen invalidation level.
Short: a qualified bearish follow-through episode reaches its frozen favorable ATR target before its frozen invalidation level.
Exit: long, close < the bullish invalidation level.
Exit: short, close > the bearish invalidation level.
