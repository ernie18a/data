Calculation: CPR = pivot range from the previous completed selected daily or weekly bar: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3, bottom = min((high + low) / 2, 2 × pivot − (high + low) / 2), top = max((high + low) / 2, 2 × pivot − (high + low) / 2)
Calculation: Current anchor open, high, and low = the open and running high and low of the current selected daily or weekly period, aggregated from the input bars.
Calculation: ATR = average true range of input bars; use the same ATR for normalizing CPR width, target room, and invalidation distance.
Calculation: Active side = forced bullish or bearish side when configured; otherwise bullish if close > CPR top, bearish if close < CPR bottom, else bullish if current anchor open > CPR midpoint, bearish if it is below the midpoint, else bullish if close ≥ midpoint and bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Accepted closes = consecutive input-bar closes beyond the CPR edge on the active side; reset to zero when a close is not beyond that edge or the active side changes.
Calculation: Expansion score = rounded 0–100 weighted sum: 24% narrow-CPR-width score, 16% anchor-open-nearness score, 25% acceptance score, 18% range-expansion score, and 17% target-room score. Normalize each component linearly to 0–100 between its weak and strong thresholds, clamping at 0 and 100. Acceptance score is 62% accepted-closes score plus 38% distance-beyond-edge score. Range-expansion score is 72% current-anchor-range/prior-range score plus 28% current-anchor-range/recent-anchor-average-range score. The recent average uses a longer anchor-period lookback than the prior-range calculation, which uses the immediately preceding completed anchor bar.
Calculation: Target rail = active-side CPR edge projected outward by the greatest of a parameterized ATR distance, a parameterized fraction of the previous completed anchor bar's range, and five CPR widths. Target room = distance from close to target rail in the active direction, divided by ATR. Invalidation shelf = opposite CPR edge projected outward by a parameterized ATR buffer.
Calculation: Confirmed acceptance = close beyond the active-side CPR edge, accepted closes ≥ a parameterized requirement, and confirmation-mode gates pass: bar is confirmed unless realtime confirmation is allowed; relative volume meets its parameterized minimum in volume-confirmation modes; strict mode also requires minimum range expansion, target room, and width score.
Calculation: Ready = confirmed acceptance, expansion score ≥ ready threshold, close has not crossed the active-side invalidation shelf, and target room is not below its blocked threshold.
Calculation: Watch = not Ready, close has not crossed the invalidation shelf, close is beyond the CPR edge on the active side, and expansion score ≥ watch threshold.
Calculation: Room blocked = close has not crossed the invalidation shelf, close is beyond the CPR edge on the active side, target room is below its blocked threshold but not yet at or beyond the target rail, and expansion score ≥ watch threshold.
Calculation: Major follow-through = confirmed acceptance, current anchor range / previous completed anchor range ≥ follow-through threshold, and expansion score ≥ alert threshold.
Long: active side is bullish AND (Ready OR Watch OR Room blocked OR Major follow-through OR Ready with a previously recorded Ready side that was bearish)
Short: active side is bearish AND (Ready OR Watch OR Room blocked OR Major follow-through OR Ready with a previously recorded Ready side that was bullish)
Exit: long, close < bullish invalidation shelf
Exit: short, close > bearish invalidation shelf
