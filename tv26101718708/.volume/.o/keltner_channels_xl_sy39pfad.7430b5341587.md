Calculation: Basis = selected moving average (SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, VWMA, HMA, DEMA, TEMA, ALMA, or LSMA) of the selected price source; use the prior bar’s source in non-repainting mode, otherwise the current bar’s source
Calculation: Normalized basis change = (Basis − Basis from the longer lookback bars ago) / ATR
Long: Color mode is Trend Color AND Normalized basis change > neutral threshold
Short: Color mode is Trend Color AND Normalized basis change < −neutral threshold
