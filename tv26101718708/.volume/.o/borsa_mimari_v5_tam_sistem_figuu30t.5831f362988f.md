Calculation: Score = +1 each for MACD line > signal line; RSI > 50 and RSI ≤ 70; ADX > 20 and +DI > −DI; close > short-term EMA; close > long-term EMA; SuperTrend bullish; and volume > 1.1 × its moving average; −1 for each condition that is false.
Calculation: Accumulation band = from long-term EMA − ATR × (dip multiplier + 0.5) to long-term EMA − ATR × (dip multiplier − 0.5).
Calculation: Upper distribution threshold = short-term EMA + ATR × (distribution multiplier − 0.5).
Calculation: HA open = first bar’s (open + close) / 2, then each bar’s prior HA open and prior HA close averaged; HA close = (open + high + low + close) / 4.
Calculation: Descending resistance = line extended right through the two latest confirmed pivot highs, if the older pivot high is higher; pivots use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Targets = on a new dip signal, the lowest low of the last five bars plus ATR multiplied by each of three progressively larger target multipliers; on a new strong buy signal, if targets are absent or all were hit, close plus ATR multiplied by each target multiplier.
Calculation: Trailing stop = set to SuperTrend on a new buy signal or dip signal; while SuperTrend is bullish, update to the greater of the prior stop and current SuperTrend. Clear when close falls below it.
Long: SuperTrend bullish AND close < upper edge of accumulation band AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI > 50 AND RSI ≤ 70 AND Score ≥ 1 OR SuperTrend bullish AND close < upper edge of accumulation band AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI > 50 AND volume > 1.1 × its moving average AND Score ≥ 3 OR SuperTrend bullish AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI > 50 AND RSI ≤ 70 AND ADX > 20 AND +DI > −DI AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND Score ≥ 6 OR prior close < lower Bollinger band AND current close is within the current Bollinger bands OR close crosses above descending resistance OR Heikin-Ashi turns bullish OR Heikin-Ashi is bullish with its low equal to HA open OR Heikin-Ashi is bearish and HA close > (HA open + HA close) / 2 OR Score ≥ 2.
Short: SuperTrend bearish AND close > upper distribution threshold AND MACD line ≤ signal line AND RSI ≥ 30 AND Score ≤ −1 OR SuperTrend bearish AND close > upper distribution threshold AND MACD line ≤ signal line AND RSI < 50 AND volume > 1.1 × its moving average AND Score ≤ −3 OR SuperTrend bearish AND MACD line ≤ signal line AND RSI < 50 AND ADX > 20 AND +DI ≤ −DI AND close ≤ short-term EMA AND close ≤ long-term EMA AND Score ≤ −4 OR Heikin-Ashi turns bearish OR Heikin-Ashi is bearish with its high equal to HA open OR Heikin-Ashi is bullish and HA close < (HA open + HA close) / 2 OR Score < 0.
Exit: Long, close < trailing stop OR high reaches any active target.
