Calculation: Average Supertrend = mean of three Supertrend values using the same ATR lookback and progressively larger factors
Calculation: Smoothed Supertrend = EMA of Average Supertrend
Calculation: AMA Midline = midpoint of adaptive high and low bands; each band is recursively updated from its prior value toward the current high or low using the square of the lookback SMA of whether the lookback high increased or the lookback low decreased; initialize each band to its first high or low
Calculation: Long stop = entry price minus either a percentage of entry price or an ATR multiple, using ATR captured at entry; when trailing is active, update using the same formula based on the highest high since entry and never lower the stop; after take profit, if break-even is enabled, keep the stop at or above entry
Calculation: Short stop = entry price plus either a percentage of entry price or an ATR multiple, using ATR captured at entry; when trailing is active, update using the same formula based on the lowest low since entry and never raise the stop; after take profit, if break-even is enabled, keep the stop at or below entry
Calculation: Long take-profit level = entry price plus either a percentage of entry price, an ATR multiple using ATR captured at entry, or a risk/reward multiple of the entry-to-initial-stop distance
Calculation: Short take-profit level = entry price minus either a percentage of entry price, an ATR multiple using ATR captured at entry, or a risk/reward multiple of the initial-stop-to-entry distance
Calculation: Enabled MTF RSI = RSI of close on its specified lower timeframe, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Enabled MTF MACD = MACD line and signal line of close on its specified lower timeframe, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Enabled MTF volume = volume and its moving average on its specified lower timeframe, using the bar still forming
Long: position is flat AND low < Average Supertrend AND close > Smoothed Supertrend AND prior close > AMA Midline AND close > AMA Midline AND (MTF RSI filter disabled or RSI > its threshold) AND (MTF MACD filter disabled or MACD line > signal line) AND (MTF volume filter disabled or volume > its moving average times its multiplier)
Short: position is flat AND high > Average Supertrend AND close < Smoothed Supertrend AND prior close < AMA Midline AND close < AMA Midline AND (MTF RSI filter disabled or RSI < its threshold) AND (MTF MACD filter disabled or MACD line < signal line) AND (MTF volume filter disabled or volume > its moving average times its multiplier)
Exit: long, close[2] >= Smoothed Supertrend[1] AND close[1] < Smoothed Supertrend OR low <= long stop OR high >= long take-profit level OR take-profit trailing is enabled AND take-profit level was reached AND price falls by the trailing deviation from the highest high since reaching that level
Exit: short, close[2] <= Smoothed Supertrend[1] AND close[1] > Smoothed Supertrend OR high >= short stop OR low <= short take-profit level OR take-profit trailing is enabled AND take-profit level was reached AND price rises by the trailing deviation from the lowest low since reaching that level
