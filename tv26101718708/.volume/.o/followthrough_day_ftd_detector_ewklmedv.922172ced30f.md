Calculation: Prior-bar rolling low = lowest low across the configured lookback, ending on the previous bar
Calculation: Rally state = when inactive, start at day 1 if close > previous close and (low <= prior-bar rolling low or previous low <= prior-bar rolling low), recording the lower of current and previous low as the rally floor; when active, reset if low < rally floor or day count > latest rally day, then start a new day 1 on that bar if the start condition holds, otherwise remain inactive; if neither reset applies, increment the day count by one
Long: close > previous close AND (low <= prior-bar rolling low OR previous low <= prior-bar rolling low) AND rally state starts at day 1 OR rally day count is between the earliest and latest eligible days AND ((close - previous close) / previous close * 100) >= minimum gain threshold AND (higher-volume filter is disabled OR volume > previous bar volume)
