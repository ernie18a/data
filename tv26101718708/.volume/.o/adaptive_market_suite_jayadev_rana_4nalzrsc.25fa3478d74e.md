Calculation: Adaptive moving average = close recursively smoothed with a squared coefficient interpolated between faster and slower smoothing according to the efficiency ratio (absolute close change over the lookback divided by summed absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback)
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the extreme across equal lookback lengths of bars before and after it; usable after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Order-flow oscillator = EMA of ((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × 2 − 1) × (volume / SMA(volume)); use 1 instead of the volume ratio when volume or its average is zero. The EMA and volume SMA use the same lookback.
Long: Adaptive moving average > its previous value OR close crosses above the most recent confirmed swing high OR a bullish order block is created on an upward structure break (a bearish candle exists within the order-block lookback) OR low > high two bars earlier OR order-flow oscillator crosses above zero
Short: Adaptive moving average < its previous value OR close crosses below the most recent confirmed swing low OR a bearish order block is created on a downward structure break (a non-bearish candle exists within the order-block lookback) OR high < low two bars earlier OR order-flow oscillator crosses below zero
