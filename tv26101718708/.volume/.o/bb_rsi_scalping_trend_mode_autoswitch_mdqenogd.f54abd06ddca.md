Calculation: Lower/upper Bollinger Band = moving average of close ± a multiplier × close standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: Dynamic RSI oversold/overbought thresholds = lowest/highest RSI over a lookback, respectively, adjusted inward by a buffer.
Calculation: MACD histogram bullish/bearish inflection = histogram rises after falling / falls after rising; recent = an inflection occurred within a lookback.
Calculation: Recent qualifying volume = volume exceeded its moving average × a multiplier within a lookback; when volume-increasing is required, it also exceeded prior-bar volume.
Calculation: ADX and +DI/−DI = directional movement index from the same lookback; trend is active when ADX ≥ trend threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot low/high makes a lower/higher price but a higher/lower RSI than the prior same-side pivot, within a maximum pivot gap.
Calculation: Auto entry mode = aggressive when Bollinger width exceeds its average and ATR exceeds its average and volume exceeds its moving average × a high-volume multiplier; conservative when width does not exceed its average and volume does not exceed that threshold; balanced otherwise. A manually selected mode overrides auto mode.
