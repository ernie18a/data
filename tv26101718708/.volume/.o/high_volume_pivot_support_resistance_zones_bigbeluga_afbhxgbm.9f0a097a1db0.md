Calculation: High-volume pivot = a swing high or low confirmed after its pivot lookback bars, with pivot-bar volume > pivot-bar volume moving average × threshold multiplier
Calculation: Resistance zone = [pivot candle body high, pivot candle body high + ATR]; support zone = [pivot candle body low − ATR, pivot candle body low]; use ATR and volume averages from the pivot bar
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum since the pivot of volume when close ≥ open and negative volume when close < open
Long: First bar closing above the latest resistance zone top, while that zone has not already been marked broken
Long: Latest support zone is unbroken, close > its bottom, low[1] ≤ its top, and low > its top
Long: Latest resistance zone is broken, close > its top, low[1] ≤ its top, and low > its top, excluding the breakout bar and the following bar
Short: First bar closing below the latest support zone bottom, while that zone has not already been marked broken
Short: Latest resistance zone is unbroken, close < its top, high[1] ≥ its bottom, and high < its bottom
Short: Latest support zone is broken, close < its bottom, and high[1] ≥ its bottom and high < its bottom
Short: High enters the latest resistance zone and cumulative volume delta < its value on the prior bar
