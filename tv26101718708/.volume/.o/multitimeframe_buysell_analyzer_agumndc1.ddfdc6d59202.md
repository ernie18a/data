Calculation: MACD line = shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA of close; signal line = EMA of MACD line
Calculation: Bullish MTF alignment = on each of four configured timeframes, use the latest bar available without lookahead: TF2, TF3, and TF4 have close above their trend EMA and fast EMA above slow EMA; TF1 is not bearish (it does not have close below its trend EMA and fast EMA below slow EMA)
Calculation: Bearish MTF alignment = on each of four configured timeframes, use the latest bar available without lookahead: TF2, TF3, and TF4 have close below their trend EMA and fast EMA below slow EMA; TF1 is not bullish (it does not have close above its trend EMA and fast EMA above slow EMA)
Long: (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR MACD line crosses above signal line OR (close above trend EMA AND fast EMA above slow EMA AND close at or below fast EMA AND above slow EMA)) AND at least 2 of these are true: (close above trend EMA AND fast EMA above slow EMA), (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR MACD line crosses above signal line), (RSI above the buy threshold and below 70), (volume above its moving average times the volume multiplier) AND bullish MTF alignment when the MTF filter is enabled
Short: (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR MACD line crosses below signal line OR (close below trend EMA AND fast EMA below slow EMA AND close at or above fast EMA AND below slow EMA)) AND at least 2 of these are true: (close below trend EMA AND fast EMA below slow EMA), (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR MACD line crosses below signal line), (RSI below the sell threshold and above 30), (volume above its moving average times the volume multiplier) AND bearish MTF alignment when the MTF filter is enabled
