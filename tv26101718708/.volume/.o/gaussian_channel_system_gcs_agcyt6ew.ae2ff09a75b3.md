Calculation: Gaussian moving average = the Gaussian-weighted average of the source over a lookback; each bar’s weight is exp(-0.5 × ((bar offset − floor(offset parameter × (lookback − 1))) / sigma parameter)^2).
Calculation: Residual standard deviation = rolling standard deviation of (source − Gaussian moving average) over the Gaussian moving average lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower channel = Gaussian moving average ± residual standard deviation × channel-width parameter.
Calculation: Mid-upper and mid-lower channel = Gaussian moving average ± half the distance from the Gaussian moving average to the corresponding outer channel.
Calculation: Z-score = (source − rolling mean of source) / rolling standard deviation of source, using the same lookback for the mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Normalized Gaussian slope = (current Gaussian moving average − Gaussian moving average five bars earlier) / (five × ATR).
Long: (low ≤ mid-lower channel AND normalized Gaussian slope > its positive threshold AND previous-bar Z-score < its lower threshold AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous long signal) OR (close crosses above upper channel AND ADX > its trend threshold AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous long signal) OR (fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close > Gaussian moving average AND ADX > its trend threshold AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous long signal).
Short: (high ≥ mid-upper channel AND normalized Gaussian slope < its negative threshold AND previous-bar Z-score > its upper threshold AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous short signal) OR (close crosses below lower channel AND ADX > its trend threshold AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous short signal) OR (fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < Gaussian moving average AND ADX > its trend threshold AND volume > its 20-bar average × its volume factor AND more than the cooldown lookback bars since the previous short signal).
