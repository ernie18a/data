Calculation: Proportional volume delta = (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) × volume; zero when high = low
Calculation: Smoothed volume delta = EMA of proportional volume delta over a lookback
Calculation: Volume surge = current volume > recent mean volume + multiplier × recent standard deviation of volume, using the same lookback for both statistics
Long: Volume surge AND smoothed volume delta > 0
Short: Volume surge AND smoothed volume delta < 0
