Calculation: Long score = count of these conditions that are true: close > VWAP; fast EMA > slow EMA; RSI is within its configured bullish range; volume >= its moving average; close > fast SMA and fast SMA > slow SMA.
Calculation: Short score = count of these conditions that are true: close < VWAP; fast EMA < slow EMA; RSI is within its configured bearish range; volume >= its moving average; close < fast SMA and fast SMA < slow SMA.
Calculation: Bullish momentum = close > close 5 bars ago AND close > close 3 bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish momentum = close < close 5 bars ago AND close < close 3 bars ago.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND long score >= strong-signal threshold OR (long trigger AND long cooldown is ready AND NOT (long trigger on prior bar AND long cooldown was ready on prior bar)); long trigger is fast EMA crossing above slow EMA AND long score >= configured minimum when EMA-cross requirement is enabled, otherwise long score >= configured minimum AND (momentum filter disabled OR bullish momentum).
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND short score >= strong-signal threshold OR (short trigger AND short cooldown is ready AND NOT (short trigger on prior bar AND short cooldown was ready on prior bar)); short trigger is fast EMA crossing below slow EMA AND short score >= configured minimum when EMA-cross requirement is enabled, otherwise short score >= configured minimum AND (momentum filter disabled OR bearish momentum).
Exit: long, long score >= configured minimum AND (momentum filter disabled OR bullish momentum) AND (RSI is overbought OR close is at or above the upper Bollinger Band threshold) AND shared trim cooldown is ready.
Exit: short, short score >= configured minimum AND (momentum filter disabled OR bearish momentum) AND (RSI is oversold OR close is at or below the lower Bollinger Band threshold) AND shared trim cooldown is ready.
