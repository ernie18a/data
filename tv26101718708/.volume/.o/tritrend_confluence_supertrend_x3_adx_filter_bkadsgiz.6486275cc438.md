Long: ((entry mode = all agree AND close > fast SuperTrend AND close > medium SuperTrend AND close > slow SuperTrend) OR (entry mode = majority AND at least 2 of close > fast SuperTrend, close > medium SuperTrend, close > slow SuperTrend) OR (entry mode = fast trigger, slope confirm AND close > fast SuperTrend AND close > slow SuperTrend)) AND ADX > threshold AND (+DI > −DI) AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > ADX from the configured number of bars earlier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × configured multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close > HTF SuperTrend)
Short: ((entry mode = all agree AND close <= fast SuperTrend AND close <= medium SuperTrend AND close <= slow SuperTrend) OR (entry mode = majority AND the sum of +1 for each close above its fast, medium, or slow SuperTrend and −1 otherwise is >= −1) OR (entry mode = fast trigger, slope confirm AND close <= fast SuperTrend AND close <= slow SuperTrend)) AND ADX > threshold AND (−DI > +DI) AND (ADX rising filter disabled OR ADX > ADX from the configured number of bars earlier) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume SMA × configured multiplier) AND (HTF filter disabled OR close <= HTF SuperTrend)
Exit: long, close <= fast SuperTrend OR low <= slow SuperTrend
Exit: short, close > fast SuperTrend OR high >= slow SuperTrend
Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow SuperTrends use progressively longer ATR lookbacks and separately configured multipliers. The HTF SuperTrend uses HTF bars aggregated from the input bars and the last completed HTF bar.
