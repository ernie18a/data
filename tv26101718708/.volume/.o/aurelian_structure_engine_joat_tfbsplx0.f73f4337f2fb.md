Calculation: Bullish structure break = on a confirmed bar, close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high plus an ATR buffer; at least two swing highs must have been confirmed. Swing highs use the same left and right pivot length.
Calculation: Bearish structure break = on a confirmed bar, close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low minus an ATR buffer; at least two swing lows must have been confirmed. Swing lows use the same left and right pivot length.
Calculation: Year anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price (high + low + close)/3 since the current calendar year began, using chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish value stack = close > 50-period EMA and close > year anchored VWAP and 50-period EMA > year anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Bearish value stack = close < 50-period EMA and close < year anchored VWAP and 50-period EMA < year anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish = last completed higher-timeframe close > its 50-period EMA. Higher-timeframe bearish = last completed higher-timeframe close < its 50-period EMA.
Calculation: Bullish pressure = 7-period EMA of [(((close − low)/max(high − low, minimum price increment)) − ((high − close)/max(high − low, minimum price increment))) × volume] divided by max(7-period EMA of volume, 1), greater than a threshold.
Calculation: Bearish pressure = bullish pressure formula result less than the negative pressure threshold.
Calculation: Relative volume threshold = current volume / max(20-period simple moving average of volume, 1) is at least a threshold.
Calculation: Near support = any of: price touches the latest bid block; close is within 0.45 ATR of the lower inner structure band; or close is within 0.45 ATR of year anchored VWAP. Near resistance = any of: price touches the latest offer block; close is within 0.45 ATR of the upper inner structure band; or close is within 0.45 ATR of year anchored VWAP. Structure bands use a midpoint equal to the 8-period EMA of the higher of EMA and VWAP in a bullish stack, the lower of EMA and VWAP in a bearish stack, or their average otherwise. Band width is the greater of 0.90 times the 10-period EMA of ATR and 0.65 times the 10-period EMA of the absolute EMA–VWAP difference; inner bands are 0.65 widths above and below the midpoint.
Calculation: Bid block touch = current low <= latest bid block top and current close >= its bottom. Offer block touch = current high >= latest offer block bottom and current close <= its top. Bid blocks are formed at bullish structure breaks from the highest-scoring qualifying down candle among the prior lookback bars; offer blocks are formed at bearish structure breaks from the highest-scoring qualifying up candle. For a bid candidate, top=max(open,close), bottom=low; for an offer candidate, top=high, bottom=min(open,close). A candidate must be on the correct side of the break close by at least 0.08 ATR and separated from the opposing block by at least 0.05 ATR. Candidate score is distance from its top (bid) or bottom (offer) to the structure midpoint + 0.35 × candle range + bar offset × ATR × 0.015 − candle body × 0.25; choose the lowest score. Blocks are removed when invalidated by a close beyond their far edge by 0.10 ATR, or when overlapping blocks are resolved in favor of the block matching structure direction.
Calculation: Signal cooldown permits a signal when no earlier signal exists or at least the cooldown length in bars has passed since the previous long or short signal.
Long: Signal cooldown permits AND bullish structure break AND bullish value stack AND higher-timeframe bullish AND ((bullish pressure AND relative volume threshold AND near support) OR ((bullish pressure OR relative volume threshold) AND NOT (bullish pressure AND relative volume threshold AND near support))).
Short: Signal cooldown permits AND bearish structure break AND bearish value stack AND higher-timeframe bearish AND ((bearish pressure AND relative volume threshold AND near resistance) OR ((bearish pressure OR relative volume threshold) AND NOT (bearish pressure AND relative volume threshold AND near resistance))).
