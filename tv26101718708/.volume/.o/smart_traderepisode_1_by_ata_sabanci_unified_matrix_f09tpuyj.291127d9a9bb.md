Calculation: Buy and sell volume = either volume split by the close’s position within the bar’s high-low range (equal halves for a zero-range bar), or up-volume and down-volume from lower-timeframe intrabars; use the selected method on completed bars.
Calculation: Highest buyers (HB), highest sellers (HS), lowest buyers (LB), and lowest sellers (LS) = the bars with respectively the greatest buy volume, greatest sell volume, least positive buy volume, and least positive sell volume in the rolling event lookback; ties use the most recent bar. HB and LB levels are their bars’ highs; HS and LS levels are their bars’ lows.
Calculation: Buyer remaining power = HB buy volume minus sell volume accumulated after the HB event, expressed as a percentage of HB buy volume. Cumulative delta = buy volume minus sell volume accumulated after that event. Use completed bars after the event, excluding the event bar.
Calculation: Trend channel = linear regression of the segment high extremes and, separately, segment low extremes across the rolling lookback. Uptrend means both slopes are positive beyond an ATR-scaled threshold; downtrend means both are negative beyond it; otherwise ranging. Channel zone = HB price’s position between the regressed channel bounds at the HB bar: bottom, lower, middle, upper, or top.
Calculation: Nearest event = the most recent among HB, HS, LB, LS, and the buyer/seller depletion and dominance events. HB, LB, seller depletion, and seller dominance are bullish events; HS, LS, buyer depletion, and buyer dominance are bearish events. Post-event momentum = whether bullish or bearish candles outnumber the candles after that event in a bounded recent window; candle direction is close versus the prior close, using the bar midpoint when no prior close is available. Momentum is mixed on a tie or neutral if there are no directional candles.
Long: A recent bearish event with bullish post-event momentum and at least the configured confirmation count of bullish candles, OR (no recent qualifying event override AND uptrend AND cumulative delta > 0), OR (no recent qualifying event override AND downtrend AND cumulative delta > 0 AND buyer remaining power >= the configured long minimum AND bullish post-event momentum), OR (no recent qualifying event override AND ranging AND cumulative delta > 0 AND bullish post-event momentum); AND HB or HS line is absent or close is above at least one of them; AND both event lines exist; AND the long stop is below close and long target is above close.
Short: A recent bearish event with bearish post-event momentum, OR a recent bullish event with bullish post-event momentum, OR a recent bullish event with bearish post-event momentum and at least the configured confirmation count of bearish candles, OR (no recent qualifying event override AND uptrend AND buyer remaining power is below the opponent-dominance threshold AND cumulative delta < 0 AND bearish post-event momentum), OR (no recent qualifying event override AND downtrend AND cumulative delta < 0), OR (no recent qualifying event override AND ranging AND cumulative delta < 0 AND bearish post-event momentum); AND HB or HS line is absent or close is not above both; AND both event lines exist; AND the short stop is above close and short target is below close.
Exit: Long, current signal = SHORT OR bar low <= the long stop OR bar high >= the long target.
Exit: Short, current signal = LONG OR bar high >= the short stop OR bar low <= the short target.
