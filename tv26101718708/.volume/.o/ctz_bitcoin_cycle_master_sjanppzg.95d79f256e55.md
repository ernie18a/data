Calculation: Short EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the long EMA
Calculation: Stoch RSI %K/%D = RSI normalized to its rolling range, with %K and %D smoothing; bullish cross is %K crossing above %D after %K was below the oversold threshold within a recent lookback, and bearish cross is the inverse after %K was above the overbought threshold
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a bar whose low/high is the lowest/highest across a configurable lookback on both sides
Long: A confirmed pivot low at the shorter swing lookback remains the active low candidate under the minimum-spacing rule (or replaces it by making a lower low), then a later close is above the short EMA AND a bullish Stoch RSI cross occurs
Long: A confirmed pivot low at the longer swing lookback remains the active low candidate under its minimum-spacing rule (or replaces it by making a lower low), then a later close is above the short EMA AND a bullish Stoch RSI cross occurs
Long: The bar makes a new lowest low of the in-progress calendar year; retain that year's low through year-end, then finalize it at the year change unless it is within the minimum spacing of the previous yearly low, in which case retain only the lower of the two
Long: The anchor date is reached for the initial 4-year low, or a running 4-year target date is reached for a later low; select the confirmed longer-swing pivot low nearest that date and, when enabled, replace it with the lowest such pivot within the snap window
Short: A confirmed pivot high at the shorter swing lookback remains the active high candidate under the minimum-spacing rule (or replaces it by making a higher high), then a later close is below the short EMA AND a bearish Stoch RSI cross occurs
Short: A confirmed pivot high at the longer swing lookback remains the active high candidate under its minimum-spacing rule (or replaces it by making a higher high), then a later close is below the short EMA AND a bearish Stoch RSI cross occurs
