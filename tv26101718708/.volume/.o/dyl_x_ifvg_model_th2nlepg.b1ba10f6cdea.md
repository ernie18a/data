Calculation: Fair value gap = On higher-, lower-, or inverse-gap-timeframe bars, a bullish gap exists when the current bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; its zone is between those prices. A bearish gap exists when the current bar’s high is below the low two bars earlier; its zone is between those prices. The third bar may still be forming.
Long: A bullish fair value gap forms on a higher- or lower-timeframe bar OR the current bar overlaps a bullish higher- or lower-timeframe gap zone and closes above its bottom OR the current bar closes above the top of a bearish inverse-gap-timeframe gap zone.
Short: A bearish fair value gap forms on a higher- or lower-timeframe bar OR the current bar overlaps a bearish higher- or lower-timeframe gap zone and closes below its top OR the current bar closes below the bottom of a bullish inverse-gap-timeframe gap zone.
