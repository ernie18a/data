Calculation: RSI good zone = RSI of current-timeframe close is between the configured good-zone minimum and maximum, inclusive
Calculation: RSI bullish zone = RSI is above 50 and at or below the configured good-zone maximum
Calculation: RSI recovery zone = RSI is at or above the configured oversold level and below 55
Calculation: RSI rising = current RSI > previous bar RSI > RSI two bars ago
Calculation: Recent volume spike = volume exceeded its volume moving average times the configured spike multiplier on any of the current or configured number of prior bars; compare each bar with its own moving average
Calculation: Good volume = recent volume spike OR (current volume > its moving average AND the 3-bar volume SMA > the 8-bar volume SMA)
Calculation: In consolidation = the consecutive count of bars whose rolling high-to-low range over the configured lookback is at most the configured maximum range percentage has reached the configured minimum count
Calculation: Near breakout = close is above the midpoint of the rolling consolidation high and low and no farther above the high than the configured proximity threshold
Long: (RSI good zone OR RSI bullish zone) AND RSI rising AND good volume AND in consolidation AND near breakout AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND market-reference fast EMA > market-reference slow EMA AND market-reference close > market-reference long-term EMA OR (RSI good zone OR RSI recovery zone) AND good volume AND in consolidation AND (close > fast EMA AND close > long-term EMA OR close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) AND NOT (RSI good zone OR RSI bullish zone) AND RSI rising AND good volume AND in consolidation AND near breakout AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND market-reference fast EMA > market-reference slow EMA AND market-reference close > market-reference long-term EMA OR in consolidation AND (RSI good zone OR RSI bullish zone) AND RSI rising AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND NOT (RSI good zone OR RSI recovery zone) AND good volume AND in consolidation AND (close > fast EMA AND close > long-term EMA OR close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA)
