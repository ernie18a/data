Calculation: Directional strength = equal-weight mean of RSI, MFI, MACD, CCI, TSI, RVI, Stochastic %K, Williams %R, ROC, momentum, and CMO after normalization: RSI/MFI (value−50)/20; CCI and CMO /100; Stochastic %K (3-bar SMA) (value−50)/50; Williams %R (value+50)/50; MACD, TSI, ROC, and momentum x divided by the rolling highest x when x>0, otherwise by the absolute rolling lowest x; RVI = ((up/(up+down)×100)−50)/20, where up/down are EMAs of close standard deviation on positive/negative close changes, respectively. Component lookbacks are independently configurable; the overall-length multiplier applies to all except RSI and MFI.
Calculation: Environmental quality = signed normalization of the equal-weight mean of enabled ATR, ADX, linear-regression strength, and absolute-correlation components. For ATR and ADX use ((raw/EMA(raw)−1)×(1+stdev(raw)))−1 for ATR, and (raw/EMA(raw)−1)×(1+stdev(raw)) for ADX. Linear-regression strength is absolute change between current and prior one-bar-offset linear regression of close, transformed as (slope/EMA(slope)−1)×(1+stdev(slope))−1. Absolute correlation is abs(correlation(close, bar index)), transformed the same way as linear-regression strength. Signed normalization divides a positive value by its rolling highest value, or a negative value by the absolute rolling lowest value; normalize the mean using its rolling highest/lowest values.
Long: (selected directional source, either directional strength or its moving average, > buy threshold AND prior value ≤ buy threshold AND environmental quality ≥ filter) OR (confirmed pivot low with a lower price low and higher directional-strength low than the prior pivot low, pivot lows 5–60 bars apart, and at least one of the two oscillator lows outside [−1,1]).
Short: (selected directional source, either directional strength or its moving average, < sell threshold AND prior value ≥ sell threshold AND environmental quality ≥ filter) OR (confirmed pivot high with a higher price high and lower directional-strength high than the prior pivot high, pivot highs 5–60 bars apart, and at least one of the two oscillator highs outside [−1,1]).
