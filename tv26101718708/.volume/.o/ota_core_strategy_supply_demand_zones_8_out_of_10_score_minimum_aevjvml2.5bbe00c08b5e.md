Calculation: Bull leg = a consecutive run of N bars where each close > open and each candle body / close × 100 ≥ minimum leg move; bear leg = a consecutive run where each close < open and each body / close × 100 ≥ minimum leg move.
Calculation: Valid base = a consecutive run of B bars immediately before the leg where average body < average leg body / leg-to-base body ratio and every base body < 0.65 × average leg body.
Calculation: Demand zone = [lowest base low, highest base body edge]; supply zone = [lowest base body edge, highest base high]. The body edge is max(open, close) for the demand top and min(open, close) for the supply bottom.
Calculation: Zone score = tight-base points (2 if B ≤ 2; 1 if B ≤ 4; else 0) + explosive-leg points (2 if average leg body / average base body ≥ 4; 1 if ≥ 2; else 0) + freshness points (2 if no post-base/pre-leg bars intersect the zone; 1 if exactly one intersects; else 0) + 1 if the first leg candle closes in the top 30% of its range for demand or bottom 30% for supply + 1 if its volume exceeds 1.5 × average volume of the 20 bars immediately before the leg + 1 if the sum of leg candle ranges exceeds 1.5 × ATR(14) × N; cap at 10.
Calculation: Rolling demand zone = [lowest low of the last 20 bars, highest open or close of the last 20 bars]; rolling supply zone = [lowest open or close of the last 20 bars, highest high of the last 20 bars].
Long: a valid bull leg and base form a demand zone with score ≥ minimum zone score, and the zone is unmitigated or mitigated zones are shown, and fresh-only is off or no prior bar since formation intersected the zone; OR close is within the rolling demand zone and the previous bar's close was not within it.
Short: a valid bear leg and base form a supply zone with score ≥ minimum zone score, and the zone is unmitigated or mitigated zones are shown, and fresh-only is off or no prior bar since formation intersected the zone; OR close is within the rolling supply zone and the previous bar's close was not within it.
