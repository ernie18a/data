Calculation: Live net force = For each confirmed pivot high and pivot low (each pivot must be strictly higher than the highs, or strictly lower than the lows, respectively, in the same configurable number of bars on both sides), register its price with the 5-bar average volume ending on the pivot bar. Merge each pivot with the nearest stored level within the current ATR(14) times a configurable percentage; update the level price by volume-weighted average, add its volume, increment its touch count, and refresh its last-update bar. Consider levels updated within a configurable lookback and meeting a configurable minimum touch count. For each considered level, mass is the weighted average of its volume divided by the maximum volume among recently updated levels and its touch count divided by the maximum touch count among those levels. Its gravity is a configurable constant times mass divided by the square of the greater of its absolute distance from close and 1% of ATR(14). Sum gravity for levels at or below close and subtract gravity for levels above close.
