Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks; use only after the right-side bars complete. Retain the latest confirmed high and low, and retain whether each newly confirmed high is higher/lower than the preceding confirmed high and each newly confirmed low is higher/lower than the preceding confirmed low.
Calculation: Bull structure score = structure weight if the latest confirmed high is higher or the latest confirmed low is higher, and close > latest confirmed low; otherwise 0. Bear structure score = structure weight if the latest confirmed low is lower or the latest confirmed high is lower, and close < latest confirmed high; otherwise 0.
Calculation: OBV slope = slope of a linear regression of cumulative signed volume, where each bar adds volume times the sign of the close change; use the regression slope over the selected lookback. Bull volume score = volume weight if OBV slope > 0, otherwise 0. Bear volume score = volume weight if OBV slope < 0, otherwise 0.
Calculation: KAMA = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close. Bull momentum count = number of these true conditions: close > KAMA, RSI > its midpoint, Williams %R > its upper threshold. Bear momentum count = number of these true conditions: close < KAMA, RSI < its midpoint, Williams %R < its lower threshold. Bull/bear momentum score = respective count / 3 × momentum weight.
Calculation: Bull sweep score = liquidity weight if low < latest confirmed swing low and close > that swing low; otherwise 0. Bear sweep score = liquidity weight if high > latest confirmed swing high and close < that swing high; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Relative ATR = ATR / its simple moving average over a longer lookback. Volatility score = volatility weight when relative ATR is between its lower and upper bounds, inclusive of neither bound; otherwise 0. Award it to bulls when bull momentum score > bear momentum score and bull momentum count >= 2; award it to bears when bear momentum score > bull momentum score and bear momentum count >= 2.
Calculation: Developing daily range = highest high and lowest low so far in the current day, including the current bar. Range position = (close − daily range midpoint) / daily range width × 100. Bull session score = session weight capped at its maximum, proportional to the absolute range position, when session scoring is enabled and range position < 0; otherwise 0. Bear session score = same calculation when session scoring is enabled and range position > 0; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = 50-period EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Add the MTF weight to the bull score when current close > higher-timeframe trend and MTF scoring is enabled; add it to the bear score when current close < higher-timeframe trend and MTF scoring is enabled.
Calculation: Bull score = bull structure score + bull volume score + bull momentum score + bull sweep score + bull volatility score + bull session score + bull MTF weight. Bear score = corresponding bear scores and weight.
Calculation: VWMA = volume-weighted moving average of close. Supertrend direction = bullish when its direction is up and bearish when its direction is down. Ultra Ribbon direction = bullish when the shortest HMA is above the longest HMA, bearish when it is below; HMA lengths increase from shortest to longest.
Long: Bull score >= signal threshold AND bull score − bear score >= directional lead AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (VWMA filter disabled OR close > VWMA) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend is bullish) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR Ultra Ribbon is bullish) AND previous bar did not meet this long condition.
Short: Bear score >= signal threshold AND bear score − bull score >= directional lead AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in the configured session) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (VWMA filter disabled OR close < VWMA) AND (Supertrend filter disabled OR Supertrend is bearish) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR Ultra Ribbon is bearish) AND previous bar did not meet this short condition.
Calculation: Entry stop = selected stop mode: ATR stop at entry price minus/plus the regime-specific ATR multiple for long/short; latest confirmed swing low/high for pivot mode, falling back to that ATR stop if unavailable; Supertrend value at entry for Supertrend mode; or longest HMA at entry for trailing mode. Regime is scalp when ADX is below its lower boundary, intraday when ADX is at least that boundary and below its upper boundary, and swing otherwise.
Calculation: Entry target = entry price plus/minus ATR × regime-specific target multiple for long/short.
Exit: Long, high >= entry target OR low <= entry stop.
Exit: Short, low <= entry target OR high >= entry stop.
