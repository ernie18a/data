Calculation: Directional efficiency = absolute net close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Kaufman adaptive moving average (KAMA) = standard KAMA of close.
Calculation: Long command score = 42 if completed command-bar close > fast EMA > slower EMA and close > KAMA, plus 16 if fast EMA rises, 18 if efficiency meets its minimum, 12 if ATR / its longer-lookback average meets its minimum, and 12 if close > active session VWAP.
Calculation: Short command score = 42 if completed command-bar close < fast EMA < slower EMA and close < KAMA, plus 16 if fast EMA falls, 18 if efficiency meets its minimum, 12 if ATR / its longer-lookback average meets its minimum, and 12 if close < active session VWAP.
Calculation: Long structure score = 40 if completed structure-bar close > fast EMA > slower EMA and close > KAMA, plus 18 if fast EMA rises, 16 if execution close > active session VWAP, 14 if execution closes are accepted above the active session opening-range high (or 7 if close is above it without acceptance), 6 if the absolute completed-bar close change is at least 0.35 ATR, and 6 if execution EMA rises.
Calculation: Short structure score = 40 if completed structure-bar close < fast EMA < slower EMA and close < KAMA, plus 18 if fast EMA falls, 16 if execution close < active session VWAP, 14 if execution closes are accepted below the active session opening-range low (or 7 if close is below it without acceptance), 6 if the absolute completed-bar close change is at least 0.35 ATR, and 6 if execution EMA falls.
Calculation: Bull impulse = close > open, candle body / high-low range meets its minimum, and close is in the upper 28% of the bar; bear impulse is the inverse.
Calculation: Core setup score = 26 for aligned higher-timeframe command and structure, plus 20 for opening-range retest, 18 for VWAP/KAMA pullback, 18 for compression setup, 22 for failed-auction reclaim, 8 for expanding relative volume, 8 for directional impulse, and 4 for accepted opening-range break; cap at 100.
Calculation: MicroPulse context score = 28 for aligned higher-timeframe command and structure, plus 13 for matching adaptive-trail direction, 14 for matching fast/slow EMA and KAMA stack with fast EMA slope, 9 for adequate efficiency, 7 for adequate relative volume, 8 for extension within the chase limit, 11 for a matching pulse setup, 4 for accepted opening-range break, and 3 for a matching trail-direction flip; cap at 100. MicroPulse score adds 12 per triggered pattern and 6 for enough same-side impulse bars; cap at 100.
Long: Long command score meets its minimum and exceeds short command score OR long structure score meets its minimum and exceeds short structure score OR a long core setup is armed OR a long MicroPulse setup is armed OR a long core or MicroPulse trigger fires; command and structure scores must each meet their minimum and exceed the opposing score for matrix-gated setups; apply trading-session, time-window, chart/timeframe, warm-up, cooldown, session-quota, active-pulse-ride, bar-confirmation, and enabled-module gates to armed and firing signals. A long core setup requires at least one: close above VWAP with low within the ATR pullback tolerance of VWAP; close above execution KAMA with low within that tolerance of KAMA; close above opening-range high with low retesting within the ATR buffer; compressed range and close near its prior high; or low sweeps below prior completed RTH low, active-session low, or opening-range low by the ATR sweep buffer and close reclaims that level. Require core score and risk checks: stop below entry at the recent lookback low minus ATR buffer, target at the nearest eligible level above entry (or ATR fallback target), reward/risk at least the minimum, and stop width no greater than its ATR maximum. Trigger pullback/retest with bull impulse and (close > prior high or close > execution EMA); trigger compression with close > prior compression high and bull impulse; trigger failed-auction reclaim with bull impulse and close > execution EMA. A long MicroPulse setup requires matching matrix direction and adaptive trail, adequate efficiency, extension within chase limit, valid micro risk, and context score meeting arm threshold. Its patterns are: (1) close near/above the prior pulse-lookback high, then close above it with bull impulse and enough bull bars; (2) low reaches the fast EMA/KAMA zone within ATR tolerance and close reclaims both with bull impulse and close > prior high; or (3) compressed pulse-lookback range, close near its high, then close above it with bull impulse. Fire only when MicroPulse score meets its fire threshold.
Short: Short command score meets its minimum and exceeds long command score OR short structure score meets its minimum and exceeds long structure score OR a short core setup is armed OR a short MicroPulse setup is armed OR a short core or MicroPulse trigger fires; command and structure scores must each meet their minimum and exceed the opposing score for matrix-gated setups; apply trading-session, time-window, chart/timeframe, warm-up, cooldown, session-quota, active-pulse-ride, bar-confirmation, and enabled-module gates to armed and firing signals. A short core setup requires at least one: close below VWAP with high within the ATR pullback tolerance of VWAP; close below execution KAMA with high within that tolerance of KAMA; close below opening-range low with high retesting within the ATR buffer; compressed range and close near its prior low; or high sweeps above prior completed RTH high, active-session high, or opening-range high by the ATR sweep buffer and close rejects/reclaims below that level. Require core score and risk checks: stop above entry at the recent lookback high plus ATR buffer, target at the nearest eligible level below entry (or ATR fallback target), reward/risk at least the minimum, and stop width no greater than its ATR maximum. Trigger pullback/retest with bear impulse and (close < prior low or close < execution EMA); trigger compression with close < prior compression low and bear impulse; trigger failed-auction reclaim with bear impulse and close < execution EMA. A short MicroPulse setup requires matching matrix direction and adaptive trail, adequate efficiency, extension within chase limit, valid micro risk, and context score meeting arm threshold. Its patterns are: (1) close near/below the prior pulse-lookback low, then close below it with bear impulse and enough bear bars; (2) high reaches the fast EMA/KAMA zone within ATR tolerance and close rejects both with bear impulse and close < prior low; or (3) compressed pulse-lookback range, close near its low, then close below it with bear impulse. Fire only when MicroPulse score meets its fire threshold.
Exit: Long, close below adaptive pulse trail or KAMA or trail direction turns short, OR the configured maximum pulse ride bars are reached.
Exit: Short, close above adaptive pulse trail or KAMA or trail direction turns long, OR the configured maximum pulse ride bars are reached.
