Calculation: Volume confirmation metric = apply the selected smoothing method and lookback to an RMA of the scaled candle-volume metric. For each chart bar, calculate candle direction as (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 if the range is 0; multiply it by (volume^(1 − volume boost) × volume factor + 1 − volume factor), using 0 for the powered volume when volume ≤ 0. Divide by the RMA of the absolute resulting value over the scaling lookback in days (convert days to chart bars using 6.5 hours per day); multiply by 100, or use the undivided value × 100 if the RMA is 0; clamp to [−100, 100]. Apply the rolling RMA, then the selected smoothing method.
