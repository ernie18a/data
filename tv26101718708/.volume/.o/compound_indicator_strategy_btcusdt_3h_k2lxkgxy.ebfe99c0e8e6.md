Calculation: Compound score = prior bar’s score plus that bar’s signed increments: volume oscillator crossings of its configured levels (+ on upward crossings and − on downward crossings for bullish candles, reversed for bearish candles); Chande momentum oscillator crossings between each pair of four lookbacks and across configured levels; stochastic %K crossings between each pair of four lookbacks (+ up, − down), plus +1 if any of the first three %K values is at least its %D and −1 if any is below; RSI, MFI, RVI, and smoothed BOP crossings between each pair of four lookbacks and across their configured levels (+ up, − down); SMA and EMA crossings between each pair of four lookbacks (+ up, − down); +1 if Parabolic SAR is at or below close, otherwise −1; +1 for bullish Supertrend direction, otherwise −1; if close is above the Bollinger middle band, add floor(close / upper band) + 1, otherwise subtract floor(close / lower band) + 1; +1 if 10-period center of gravity is above its 3-period SMA, otherwise −1. The four lookbacks within each indicator are ordered shortest to longest. Volume oscillator is 100 × (short volume average − long volume average) / long volume average. Chande momentum oscillator is 100 × (sum of positive close changes − sum of absolute negative close changes) / their sum, over its lookback. Stochastic %K is the simple moving average of the lookback stochastic, and %D is the simple moving average of %K. Relative volatility index is 100 × smoothed standard deviation of positive close changes / (smoothed standard deviation of positive changes + smoothed standard deviation of nonpositive changes). Smoothed BOP is the selected moving average of 100 × (close − open) / (high − low) + 50. Calculation: Price rising/falling = the 9-period SMA of close is higher/lower than each of its prior five values. Calculation: Price-score covariance = covariance over the latest 11 bars of compound score and (open + close) / 2, divided by 100. Calculation: Score-high covariance = covariance over the latest 11 bars of compound score and the rolling lookback high of compound score / 100. Calculation: Score-low covariance = covariance over the latest 11 bars of compound score and the rolling lookback low of compound score / 100. Calculation: Each covariance reference = the rolling five-bar highest or lowest value of that covariance, selected by whether the corresponding unreferenced score-high or score-low covariance is positive or nonpositive. Calculation: Supertrend sell trigger = Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish, or direction is bearish while point two or point three is true. Calculation: Point one = prior price-score covariance equals its prior five-bar high AND current covariance is below that high AND at or above its positive cutoff AND price is falling. Calculation: Point two = prior score-high covariance equals its prior selected covariance reference AND current score-high covariance is below that reference AND at or above its positive cutoff AND price is rising. Calculation: Point three = same as point two, using score-high covariance and its selected reference. Calculation: Point five = prior score-low covariance equals its prior selected covariance reference AND current score-low covariance is below that reference AND at or above its positive cutoff AND price is falling. Calculation: Point six = same as point five, but current score-low covariance is above its selected reference and at or below its negative cutoff. Calculation: Point seven = same as point two, but price is falling. Long: Point one AND (flat AND NOT Supertrend sell trigger OR long position AND 9-period SMA of close < position average entry price × 0.99) AND NOT Supertrend sell trigger; OR (point five OR point six OR point seven) AND long position AND 9-period SMA of close < position average entry price × 0.99 AND NOT Supertrend sell trigger. Short: (point two OR point three) AND Supertrend sell trigger AND (9-period SMA of close > position average entry price × configured profit multiplier OR (last open trade entry price × configured intermediate profit multiplier < 9-period SMA of close AND 9-period SMA of close < position average entry price × 0.98)). Exit: Long, Supertrend sell trigger AND 9-period SMA of close > position average entry price × configured profit multiplier OR Supertrend sell trigger AND last open trade entry price × configured intermediate profit multiplier < 9-period SMA of close AND 9-period SMA of close < position average entry price × 0.98 AND intermediate selling is enabled.
