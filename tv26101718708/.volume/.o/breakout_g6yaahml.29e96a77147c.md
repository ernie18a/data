Calculation: Bullish/bearish regime zone = a candle overlaps a zone-aware demand/supply context: either a confirmed higher-timeframe swing-low/swing-high zone or a local strong-pivot/key-magnet demand/supply context; key magnets are pivot levels clustered by ATR distance, strengthened by repeated reactions, and retained until invalidated by a close through the zone. Higher-timeframe zones use the two auto-selected higher anchor timeframes and their latest confirmed swing pivots and ATR; use the latest completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low/high is lower/higher than the preceding pivot low/high while its RSI (14) is higher/lower; pivots use five bars on each side. Strong pivots use ten bars on each side.
Calculation: Abnormal volume = current volume / its lookback average meets the spike multiple OR its volume z-score meets the spike threshold. Directional volume spike additionally requires a bullish/bearish candle whose body exceeds the corresponding close-side wick.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish wick rejection = close is in the upper/lower part of the candle range (above 65% / below 40%) and the candle is bullish/bearish.
Calculation: Major bullish/bearish reversal = a new bar of a hard structural reversal at a key demand/supply magnet, with reversal score at least the minimum major-reversal score and no lower than / strictly greater than the opposing score. Hard structure is a strong-zone sweep, qualifying divergence at the zone, an ultimate reversal, the specified opposite vector climax at the key magnet, or a liquidity reclaim at the key magnet. The score adds weighted contributions for liquidity sweep, strong-zone sweep, elite divergence, stacked higher-timeframe zones, higher-timeframe zone overlap, regime flip, order-block boost, ultimate reversal, vector candle, and liquidity reclaim; bullish and bearish scores use their corresponding conditions.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish strong-zone sweep = price pierces a confirmed strong pivot low/high and closes back above/below it on a bullish/bearish candle with high relative volume.
Calculation: Ultimate bottom = bearish regime, key demand magnet touch, bullish wick rejection, sufficient volume, and RSI oversold or bullish divergence at the zone or a small candle body. Ultimate top = bullish regime, or an extended rally at supply; key supply magnet touch or higher-timeframe supply touch near a key magnet; a bearish rejection pattern; sufficient volume; and exhaustion from RSI, divergence, a small body, or a large upper wick. Ultimate signals fire only on the first qualifying bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity reclaim = a confirmed sweep-reclaim candidate whose impact meets the timeframe-adjusted threshold, or (outside the shortest timeframes) an abnormal-volume sweep of a recent low/high with bullish/bearish close confirmation; enforce the cooldown, suppress the counter-regime candidate on short timeframes, and if both candidates qualify retain the higher-impact side.
Long: major bullish reversal OR bullish regime flip at demand (bullish score meets its minimum, is at least the bearish score, and demand context holds) OR ultimate bottom OR bullish liquidity sweep (bullish wick rejection AND abnormal volume AND demand context) OR bullish liquidity reclaim OR, while flat, major bullish reversal with score at least its minimum
Short: major bearish reversal OR bearish regime flip at supply (bearish score meets its minimum, exceeds the bullish score, and supply context holds) OR ultimate top OR bearish liquidity sweep (bearish wick rejection AND abnormal volume AND supply context) OR bearish liquidity reclaim OR, while flat, major bearish reversal with score at least its minimum
Exit: long, low <= stop OR (low > stop AND high >= target) OR (a major bearish reversal occurs and its score is at least the bullish score)
Exit: short, high >= stop OR (high < stop AND low <= target) OR (a major bullish reversal occurs and its score is at least the bearish score)
