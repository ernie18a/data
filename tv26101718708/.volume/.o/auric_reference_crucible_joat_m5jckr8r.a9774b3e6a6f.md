Calculation: Daily open, weekly open, and monthly open = each period’s open from its still-forming higher-timeframe bar; previous close = close of the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: ATR = current-timeframe ATR; touch zone = ATR × touch-zone parameter.
Calculation: For each enabled anchor, track state (untouched, touched, tested, held, reclaimed, rejected), bias, and a counter. On an allowed confirmed bar, touch means the bar’s range intersects the anchor ± touch zone. First touch sets state to touched and bias by whether close is at/above the anchor; later contact sets tested. A bullish rejection is contact with close above the anchor, lower wick greater than body × rejection parameter, and close in the upper 35% of the range; bearish rejection is contact with close below the anchor, upper wick greater than body × rejection parameter, and close in the lower 35%. Rejection sets rejected state and directional bias. A cross up is close above anchor + zone after prior close at/below anchor; cross down is close below anchor − zone after prior close at/above anchor. Increment the counter while above the zone with prior bias nonpositive or below the zone with prior bias nonnegative; otherwise reset it. A cross with counter meeting the reclaim-bars parameter sets reclaimed state and bias by cross direction. Contact after reclaimed state sets held unless rejected. Reset daily-open and previous-close state/counter at each new day, weekly-open state/counter at each new week, and monthly-open state/counter at each new month.
Calculation: Anchor state weight = reclaimed 6, rejected 5, touched 4, tested 3, held 2, untouched 0.5. Anchor score = max(0, 1 − distance from close in ATRs / fade-distance parameter) × state weight for enabled anchors; best score is the maximum and control is its anchor, with ties resolved daily, weekly, monthly, then previous close. Control bias and price come from that anchor.
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × sum(buy volume − sell volume) / sum(buy volume + sell volume) over the pressure window. Per bar, buy share is clamp(0.56 × close position in range + 0.28 × body direction + 0.32 × body fraction × (body direction − 0.5) + 0.14, 0, 1); buy volume = volume × buy share and sell volume = volume × (1 − buy share). Bull pressure is score above its positive threshold; bear pressure is below its negative threshold.
Calculation: Long/short quality scores = clamp(0–100) of: 22 if best anchor score meets minimum control score, otherwise 6 × best score; plus 20 for matching trend (close above/below EMA and EMA slope over 3 bars nonnegative/nonpositive), else 10 if close is above/below EMA; plus 18 for matching pressure, else 9 if pressure score has matching sign; plus 16 for matching control bias; plus 12 if control is in range (high ≥ price − zone and low ≤ price + zone) or within 0.75 ATR; plus 8 for matching candle direction; plus 4 if any anchor reclaimed. Trend EMA and all indicators use current-timeframe bars.
Long: Confirmed bar AND any enabled anchor is reclaimed or rejected with nonnegative bias AND best anchor score ≥ minimum control score AND long quality ≥ its minimum AND long quality > short quality AND no setup occurred within the execution cooldown.
Short: Confirmed bar AND any enabled anchor is reclaimed or rejected with negative bias AND best anchor score ≥ minimum control score AND short quality ≥ its minimum AND short quality > long quality AND no setup occurred within the execution cooldown.
