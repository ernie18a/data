Calculation: Klinger oscillator = EMA of signed volume with the shorter lookback minus EMA of signed volume with the longer lookback; signed volume is volume when close >= prior close, otherwise -volume.
Calculation: Smoothed Klinger = selected moving average of the Klinger oscillator.
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving average of Smoothed Klinger.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot highs and lows = oscillator highs and lows with configurable left and right lookbacks, confirmed after the right lookback bars; compare with the preceding pivot of the same kind only when the pivot spacing is within a configurable minimum and maximum.
Long: selected Klinger series crosses above its signal series (when smoothing is enabled, Smoothed Klinger crosses above the Signal line; otherwise Klinger oscillator crosses above Smoothed Klinger) OR at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, oscillator value > preceding pivot-low oscillator value AND low < preceding pivot-low price AND pivot spacing is within range OR at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, oscillator value < preceding pivot-low oscillator value AND low > preceding pivot-low price AND pivot spacing is within range.
Short: selected Klinger series crosses below its signal series (when smoothing is enabled, Smoothed Klinger crosses below the Signal line; otherwise Klinger oscillator crosses below Smoothed Klinger) OR at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, oscillator value < preceding pivot-high oscillator value AND high > preceding pivot-high price AND pivot spacing is within range OR at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, oscillator value > preceding pivot-high oscillator value AND high < preceding pivot-high price AND pivot spacing is within range.
