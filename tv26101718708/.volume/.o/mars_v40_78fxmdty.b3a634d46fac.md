Calculation: Fast bullish trend = the two shorter moving averages are ordered upward, each is rising over its shorter slope lookback, and close is above both
Calculation: Slow bullish trend = the two longer moving averages are ordered upward, each is rising over its longer slope lookback, and close is above at least one
Calculation: Upper-wick fraction = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum range)
Calculation: Demand dominance = count of bars with close > open exceeds count of bars with close < open over a lookback
Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute change in close over a lookback / sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Fast extension in ATRs = (close − shorter fast moving average) / ATR
Calculation: Fast cloud = range between the two shorter moving averages; slow cloud = range between the two longer moving averages
Calculation: Fast cloud above slow cloud = bottom of fast cloud > top of slow cloud
Calculation: Strong alignment = Fast bullish trend AND Slow bullish trend AND close > top of fast cloud
Calculation: Wick filter passes = upper-wick fraction <= maximum allowed fraction, unless the wick filter is disabled
Calculation: Turnaround entry = a slow bearish trend occurred within a lookback AND fast cloud is not wholly above slow cloud AND both fast moving averages are rising AND top of fast cloud >= bottom of slow cloud AND close > fast-cloud top AND close is in the upper quarter of the bar AND close > prior pivot high formed during this turn phase AND Wick filter passes; allow only one until another slow bearish trend
Calculation: Momentum ignition entry = no turnaround entry has occurred since the last slow bearish trend; a slow bearish trend occurred within a lookback; high exceeds the prior-bar rolling high; close > open and close is within the configured upper portion of the bar; bar range exceeds its moving average; Wick filter passes; and configured optional conditions hold for close crossing above the shorter fast average or being above fast-cloud top, fast-average slope, and proximity to the slow cloud; enforce the signal cooldown
Calculation: Re-acceleration entry = not fast bearish AND close > fast-cloud top AND the required count of lower-or-equal-low events occurs within the pullback lookback AND high exceeds the prior-bar rolling high AND close > open AND enough bars have elapsed since the preceding such higher high AND Wick filter passes; require a down bar or touch of the shorter fast average before refiring when enabled; enforce signal cooldown, post-turn suppression, and trend-lock suppression
Calculation: Continuation entry = Fast bullish trend AND close > both longer moving averages AND a lower-low sequence arms the setup AND high (or, if configured, close) breaks the anchor high AND required up-bar, wick, optional fast-cloud-touch, and demand-dominance filters pass AND no higher low has occurred since the prior continuation; OR rotational continuation conditions hold: shorter fast average > the other shorter average, close > fast-cloud bottom and slow-cloud top, not fast bearish, a pullback touches fast-cloud top, high breaks the prior-bar rolling high, required up-bar, wick, and demand-dominance filters pass, and rotational cooldown passes
Calculation: Ideal entry = Strong alignment AND fast cloud above slow cloud AND close > fast-cloud top AND trend maturity score within its configured range AND trend efficiency >= its configured minimum AND fast extension in ATRs <= its configured maximum AND opportunity is compression setup, optimal pullback, add on pullback, or re-acceleration (or optionally core trend hold) AND risk is low (or optionally normal) AND not exhaustion risk AND not extended chase risk; optionally require a fresh re-acceleration or continuation signal; otherwise trigger only on the first bar the condition becomes true
Calculation: Portfolio build/add = ideal entry signal OR (Strong alignment AND fresh re-acceleration or continuation signal AND trend maturity score within configured bounds AND trend efficiency >= configured minimum AND fast extension in ATRs <= configured maximum AND not high risk, extended chase risk, or avoid/reduce opportunity AND opportunity is not chase risk)
Calculation: Portfolio small-parcel signal = Strong alignment AND fresh re-acceleration or continuation signal AND not portfolio build, extended, or high risk AND (elevated risk OR chase-risk opportunity OR maturity exceeds its configured level OR extension exceeds its configured level)
Calculation: Portfolio extended signal = strong alignment, structurally intact, orderly, not portfolio build or deteriorating, and (late-stage extension OR extended chase risk OR high risk OR avoid/reduce opportunity OR configured maturity and extension thresholds); also persist the state for the configured recent-bar window while alignment and maturity conditions hold
Calculation: Portfolio trim condition = (avoid/reduce opportunity AND structural deterioration) OR (late-stage extension confirmed AND structural deterioration) OR exhaustion reversal OR (high risk AND structural deterioration); require it to persist for the configured confirmation bars
Calculation: Portfolio review condition = (exhaustion-risk stage OR fast bearish with close below fast-cloud top OR close below slow-cloud top and longer fast average OR transition without strong alignment with close below fast-cloud bottom and low efficiency) AND deterioration has persisted for the configured confirmation bars; exclude trim and extended-hold states
Calculation: Portfolio marker = condition first becomes true and portfolio marker cooldown passes
Long: Momentum ignition entry OR Turnaround entry OR Re-acceleration entry OR Continuation entry OR Ideal entry OR Portfolio build/add OR Portfolio small-parcel signal
Exit: Long, Portfolio extended signal OR Portfolio trim condition OR Portfolio review condition
