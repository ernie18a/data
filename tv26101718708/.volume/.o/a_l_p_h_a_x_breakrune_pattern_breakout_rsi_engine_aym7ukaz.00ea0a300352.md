Calculation: Valid pattern = the last two confirmed pivot highs and lows form a range (both pairs within flat-level tolerance), an ascending triangle (flat highs and rising lows), a descending triangle (flat lows and falling highs), a bull channel (rising highs and lows), or a bear channel (falling highs and lows); the pattern height meets the minimum ATR multiple and the newest contributing pivot is within the freshness limit. Use the current chart bar’s ATR for these comparisons.
Calculation: Pattern resistance/support = for a range or triangle, the higher of the last two pivot highs / lower of the last two pivot lows; for a channel, the latest pivot high / latest pivot low.
Calculation: Raw long break = valid pattern with non-bearish bias, and close crosses above resistance plus the break threshold times current ATR. Raw short break = valid pattern with non-bullish bias, and close crosses below support minus the break threshold times current ATR.
Calculation: Pending true break = a raw break’s pending direction and level persist; within the decision window, the required consecutive closes all remain beyond that level, with no fakeout classified. Pending fakeout = within that window, close returns inside the broken level and the rejection wick divided by bar range meets the minimum ratio (upper wick after a long break; lower wick after a short break). Expire unresolved pending breaks after the window.
Calculation: Fast RSI = RSI with the shorter lookback; Core RSI = RSI with the longer lookback. Each RSI slope is its current value minus its value at the slope lookback.
Calculation: HTF bullish = higher-timeframe close above its fast EMA and fast EMA above slow EMA; HTF bearish = higher-timeframe close below its fast EMA and fast EMA below slow EMA. Use the last completed HTF bar historically and the still-forming HTF bar in realtime.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) ÷ log10(lookback).
Calculation: Quality score = 2 if pattern height meets its minimum, plus 2 if the corresponding true-break RSI conditions hold, plus 2 for a true break or 1 for a trap reversal, plus 1 if volume meets its average-times-multiplier threshold (or volume confirmation is disabled), plus 1 if choppiness is below its threshold (or the chop filter is disabled), plus 2 if HTF agrees when HTF scoring is enabled, 0 if it opposes, or 1 if HTF scoring is disabled.
Long: fakeout mark for a failed short break (pending short direction, close above its broken support, and lower wick/bar range meets the rejection threshold) OR ((pending true long break AND Fast RSI > long trigger AND Core RSI > 50 AND Fast RSI slope > 0 AND Core RSI slope >= 0) OR (enabled pending fakeout reversal of a short break AND Fast RSI crosses above 50 AND Fast RSI slope > 0)) AND choppiness is below its threshold when the chop filter is enabled AND HTF is bullish when hard HTF alignment is enabled AND quality score meets its minimum AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: fakeout mark for a failed long break (pending long direction, close below its broken resistance, and upper wick/bar range meets the rejection threshold) OR ((pending true short break AND Fast RSI < short trigger AND Core RSI < 50 AND Fast RSI slope < 0 AND Core RSI slope <= 0) OR (enabled pending fakeout reversal of a long break AND Fast RSI crosses below 50 AND Fast RSI slope < 0)) AND choppiness is below its threshold when the chop filter is enabled AND HTF is bearish when hard HTF alignment is enabled AND quality score meets its minimum AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
