Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close − open; candle range = high − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Average body = simple moving average of candle body over a lookback
Calculation: Trend average = simple moving average of close over a lookback; for each pattern context, compare the specified earlier close with the trend average on that same bar
Calculation: Small body = candle body ≤ average body × small-body threshold; large body = candle body ≥ average body × large-body threshold; the thresholds are separate parameters
Calculation: Down context at lag k = close[k] < trend average[k]; up context at lag k = close[k] > trend average[k]
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior candle bearish AND current candle bullish AND current open ≤ prior close AND current close ≥ prior open
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = prior candle bullish AND current candle bearish AND current open ≥ prior close AND current close ≤ prior open
Calculation: Hammer shape = small body AND lower wick ≥ candle body × long-wick ratio AND upper wick ≤ candle body × 0.5
Calculation: Inverted hammer shape = small body AND upper wick ≥ candle body × long-wick ratio AND lower wick ≤ candle body × 0.5
Calculation: Bullish hammer = hammer shape AND down context at lag 1
Calculation: Bearish hanging man = hammer shape AND up context at lag 1
Calculation: Bullish inverted hammer = inverted hammer shape AND down context at lag 1
Calculation: Bearish shooting star = inverted hammer shape AND up context at lag 1
Calculation: Harami inside = current candle body bounds are within or equal to prior candle body bounds AND current body ≤ prior body × 0.6
Calculation: Bullish harami = harami inside AND prior candle bearish AND down context at lag 2
Calculation: Bearish harami = harami inside AND prior candle bullish AND up context at lag 2
Calculation: Bullish morning star = candle two bars ago bearish AND its body is large AND prior body ≤ its body × 0.5 AND current candle bullish AND current close > midpoint of candle two bars ago AND down context at lag 3
Calculation: Bearish evening star = candle two bars ago bullish AND its body is large AND prior body ≤ its body × 0.5 AND current candle bearish AND current close < midpoint of candle two bars ago AND up context at lag 3
Calculation: Bullish piercing line = prior candle bearish AND its body is large AND current candle bullish AND current open < prior close AND current close > prior body midpoint AND current close < prior open AND down context at lag 2
Calculation: Bearish dark cloud cover = prior candle bullish AND its body is large AND current candle bearish AND current open > prior close AND current close < prior body midpoint AND current close > prior open AND up context at lag 2
Calculation: Three white soldiers = current and prior two candles bullish AND their closes rise successively AND each candle opens inside the preceding candle body AND each body ≥ half its candle range
Calculation: Three black crows = current and prior two candles bearish AND their closes fall successively AND each candle opens inside the preceding candle body AND each body ≥ half its candle range
Calculation: Pattern reliability eligible = for each pattern separately, grade each occurrence after the evaluation-forward-bars horizon by whether close then is above its occurrence close for bullish patterns or below it for bearish patterns; reliability is the fraction of successful occurrences in the rolling window of that pattern's most recent graded occurrences; eligible when its graded count is below the minimum, reliability is unavailable, or reliability ≥ the minimum reliability threshold
Calculation: Higher-timeframe directional agreement = at least one enabled higher timeframe has any bullish pattern for a bullish base pattern, or any bearish pattern for a bearish base pattern; inspect each higher timeframe's last completed bar
