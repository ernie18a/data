Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the maximum/minimum over the configured number of chart bars on each side; use only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Valid resistance/support trendline = among lines through pairs of confirmed swing highs/lows, choose the line with the most pivots within the current chart-bar ATR tolerance, requiring at least the configured minimum touches; reject slopes steeper than the configured ATR-per-bar limit, any resistance line crossed above tolerance by sampled highs or support line crossed below tolerance by sampled lows between its first and last touches, and lines whose touch span is below the configured minimum. Keep the configured number of most recent valid lines per side.
Calculation: Fast swing high/low = a confirmed swing high/low using a shorter lookback on each side than the one used for valid trendlines.
Calculation: Trend and volatility gates = if enabled, bullish decisions require chart-bar close above the chart-bar 200-period EMA and bearish decisions require close below it; either direction is suppressed when chart-bar ATR's percentile rank over the configured lookback exceeds the configured cutoff.
Long: a valid support trendline has a confirmed swing-low touch among its latest configured-lookback bars OR a confirmed fast swing low touches a valid support trendline OR the current chart-bar low is within the ATR tolerance of a valid support trendline, AND trend and volatility gates pass when enabled, AND the signal is confirmed at bar close unless repainting is enabled
Short: a valid resistance trendline has a confirmed swing-high touch among its latest configured-lookback bars OR a confirmed fast swing high touches a valid resistance trendline OR the current chart-bar high is within the ATR tolerance of a valid resistance trendline, AND trend and volatility gates pass when enabled, AND the signal is confirmed at bar close unless repainting is enabled
