Calculation: Bar range = high - low; body = abs(close - open); upper wick = high - max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) - low; body fraction = body / bar range (0 if range is zero)
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR(14) / ATR(20); volatility-adjusted ATR = ATR(14) * clamp(ATR ratio, 0.5, 2)
Calculation: Structural levels = POC, VAH, VAL from the current bar and preceding 59 bars, plus confirmed swing highs and lows using the configured pivot length; structural proximity holds within 1 ATR of POC or a swing, within 0.7 ATR of VAH or VAL, or within the MSL siege corridor. Use current-bar levels.
Calculation: Continuation score = count of these 8 true conditions: (close > SMA(50) for long / close < SMA(50) for short); (wavelet strength > 0 / < 0); volume > SMA(volume,20); enabled Lorentzian confidence > its threshold and prediction > 0 / < 0; enabled RCM allows the side; enabled MSL and structural proximity; delta ratio > 0.1 / < -0.1; MACD line > signal line / < signal line.
Calculation: Reversal score = the same 8-condition count, replacing the SMA(50) condition with RSI(14) < 40 for long / > 60 for short.
Calculation: Wavelet alignment = bullish when four smoothed wavelet approximation bands, from shortest to longest scale, strictly decrease; bearish when they strictly increase. Wavelet strength = the sum of their 1-, 2-, 4-, and 8-bar changes, respectively, divided by ATR(14) and weighted 1, 2, 4, and 8.
Calculation: Delta ratio = footprint delta / footprint volume when available; otherwise use 0.6 times the shorter fallback-timeframe estimated delta plus 0.4 times the longer fallback-timeframe estimated delta, divided by chart-bar volume. Estimated delta = volume * (2 * (close - low) / max(high - low, epsilon) - 1). Use current, possibly forming bars for other timeframes.

Candle—body fraction < 0.35, lower wick > 2*body, volume > 1.3*SMA(volume,20), close > open; or lower wick > 3*body, low <= lowest(low,20), close > bar midpoint; or lower wick > 2*body, upper wick < 0.5*body, close > open; or bullish engulfing (current bullish body engulfs prior bearish body and is larger); or body fraction > 0.85, range > 1.2*volatility-adjusted ATR, close > open; or body fraction < 0.15, structural proximity, close > close[5]. Structure—close > latest confirmed swing high, close > close[1], volume > SMA(volume,20); or close > latest swing high and latest swing low > its prior confirmed value; or low < latest swing low, close > that low, close > open; or near VAL/support siege, close > open, lower wick > body. Structure—close < latest confirmed swing low, close < close[1], volume > SMA(volume,20); or close < latest swing low and latest swing high < its prior confirmed value; or high > latest swing high, close < that high, close < open; or near VAH/resistance siege, close < open, upper wick > body. VSA—range < 0.7*volatility-adjusted ATR, volume < 0.7*SMA(volume,20), close > open, close < SMA(20); or volume > 2.5*SMA(volume,50), close < midpoint, high >= highest(high,20); or volume > 1.8*SMA(volume,20), range > 1.5*volatility-adjusted ATR, close > open, high >= highest(high,10), close < high - half the range.
