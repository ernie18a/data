Calculation: Risk-fit score = 0 if risk in ATR units exceeds the maximum; below the minimum, clamp(risk / minimum × 70, 0, 70); from minimum to ideal risk, 70 + clamp((risk − minimum) / (ideal − minimum) × 30, 0, 30); from ideal to maximum, 100 − clamp((risk − ideal) / (maximum − ideal) × 50, 0, 50), where ideal is 1.15 clamped to the minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Long activation score = 30% trend score + 28% candle score + 26% risk-fit score + 16% volume score. Trend score is 100 if fast EMA > slow EMA, slow EMA − its value 5 bars ago ≥ 0, and close ≥ fast EMA; otherwise 62 if close > slow EMA, else 25. Candle score = 45 × |close − open| / max(high − low, tick) + 55 × clamp((close − low) / max(high − low, tick), 0, 1). Risk-fit score uses the long risk in ATR units. Volume score is 55 without valid volume; otherwise clamp((volume / 20-bar average volume − 0.75) / (1.80 − 0.75) × 100, 0, 100).
Calculation: Short activation score = 30% trend score + 28% candle score + 26% risk-fit score + 16% volume score. Trend score is 100 if fast EMA < slow EMA, slow EMA − its value 5 bars ago ≤ 0, and close ≤ fast EMA; otherwise 62 if close < slow EMA, else 25. Candle score = 45 × |close − open| / max(high − low, tick) + 55 × (1 − clamp((close − low) / max(high − low, tick), 0, 1)). Risk-fit score uses the short risk in ATR units. Volume score is 55 without valid volume; otherwise clamp((volume / 20-bar average volume − 0.75) / (1.80 − 0.75) × 100, 0, 100).
Calculation: Long invalidation = lowest low of the previous stop-lookback bars − ATR × buffer; long risk = |close − long invalidation|.
Calculation: Short invalidation = highest high of the previous stop-lookback bars + ATR × buffer; short risk = |close − short invalidation|.
Long: On a confirmed bar when no cycle is active and bar index exceeds the structure lookback: long invalidation < close; long risk / ATR is within the configured minimum and maximum; long activation score ≥ minimum activation score; selected activation mode permits a range break (close > highest high of the previous structure-lookback bars AND close > open), a trend pulse (fast EMA > slow EMA AND slow EMA − its value 5 bars ago ≥ 0 AND close ≥ fast EMA AND low ≤ fast EMA AND (close − low) / max(high − low, tick) ≥ 0.58), or either; and either no valid short candidate exists or long activation score ≥ short activation score.
Short: On a confirmed bar when no cycle is active and bar index exceeds the structure lookback: short invalidation > close; short risk / ATR is within the configured minimum and maximum; short activation score ≥ minimum activation score; selected activation mode permits a range break (close < lowest low of the previous structure-lookback bars AND close < open), a trend pulse (fast EMA < slow EMA AND slow EMA − its value 5 bars ago ≤ 0 AND close ≤ fast EMA AND high ≥ fast EMA AND 1 − (close − low) / max(high − low, tick) ≥ 0.58), or either; and either no valid long candidate exists or short activation score > long activation score.
Exit: Long, low ≤ the stored long invalidation level OR active bars ≥ maximum tracking bars.
Exit: Short, high ≥ the stored short invalidation level OR active bars ≥ maximum tracking bars.
