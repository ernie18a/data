Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of the first bar in the configured opening-range session
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of the first bar in the configured opening-range session
Calculation: Opening-range average volume = total volume during the opening-range session / number of bars in that session
Long: Opening range is complete AND within the configured trading window AND no long breakout has occurred today AND (if one-breakout-per-day is enabled, no breakout has occurred today) AND (close of the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar > opening-range high when using higher-timeframe confirmation, otherwise current bar close > opening-range high) AND (if volume filter is enabled, current volume >= configured multiplier × opening-range average volume) AND (if VWAP filter is enabled, current close > session VWAP) AND (if EMA filter is enabled, current close > EMA) AND (if range-quality filter is enabled, opening-range width is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of the prior completed daily 14-period ATR)
Short: Opening range is complete AND within the configured trading window AND no short breakout has occurred today AND (if one-breakout-per-day is enabled, no breakout has occurred today) AND (close of the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar < opening-range low when using higher-timeframe confirmation, otherwise current bar close < opening-range low) AND (if volume filter is enabled, current volume >= configured multiplier × opening-range average volume) AND (if VWAP filter is enabled, current close < session VWAP) AND (if EMA filter is enabled, current close < EMA) AND (if range-quality filter is enabled, opening-range width is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of the prior completed daily 14-period ATR)
Long: Opening range is complete AND within the configured trading window AND a long breakout has occurred today AND it has not failed or already retested AND no new long breakout occurs on this bar AND current low <= opening-range high AND current close > opening-range high
Short: Opening range is complete AND within the configured trading window AND a short breakout has occurred today AND it has not failed or already retested AND no new short breakout occurs on this bar AND current high >= opening-range low AND current close < opening-range low
Long: A short breakout is active AND has not failed AND (close of the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar is strictly between opening-range low and high when using higher-timeframe confirmation, otherwise current bar close is strictly between opening-range low and high)
Short: A long breakout is active AND has not failed AND (close of the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar is strictly between opening-range low and high when using higher-timeframe confirmation, otherwise current bar close is strictly between opening-range low and high)
