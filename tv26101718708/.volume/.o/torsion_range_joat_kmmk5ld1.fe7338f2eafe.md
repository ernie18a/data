Calculation: Impulse score = sum over a directional lookback of +1 when close > prior close, -1 when close < prior close, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Pressure drift = EMA of (signal source − open) / max(high − low, minimum price tick).
Calculation: Wave direction = selected impulse direction (impulse score above/below its threshold), pressure direction (pressure drift above/below its threshold), or hybrid direction (0.55 × impulse score + 0.45 × pressure drift × 100 above/below the impulse threshold); carry the previous nonzero direction forward, initially +1 if neutral. Use the configured signal source.
Calculation: Weighted energy = EMA of ((absolute close − open × body weight + (high − low) × range weight) × optional relative volume) / ATR; relative volume is volume / its SMA, or 1 when volume weighting is disabled. Use the configured body and range weights.
Calculation: Wave accumulator = weighted energy × wave direction, accumulated each bar and reset to that value when wave direction changes.
Calculation: Torsion core = EMA of the wave accumulator divided by its rolling highest absolute value, scaled by 100 and clamped to −100…100. Its normalization lookback is longer than the smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Torsion signal = EMA of torsion core, with a longer smoothing lookback than the torsion core.
Calculation: Compression index = clamp(50 × (2 − (0.45 × fast/slow range EMA ratio + 0.35 × fast/slow body EMA ratio + 0.20 × fast/slow weighted-energy EMA ratio)), 0, 100); each fast EMA lookback is shorter than its corresponding slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Release impulse = fast weighted-energy EMA minus an EMA of that fast EMA.
Calculation: Release bias = +1 when torsion core > torsion signal, −1 when torsion core < torsion signal, otherwise wave direction.
Calculation: Adaptive regime band = configured base band + EMA of absolute torsion core × configured expansion boost; clamp to 8…95. The lower band is its negative.
Calculation: Wave stretch = absolute torsion core minus its EMA.
Long: (wave direction = +1 AND wave direction differs from the prior bar) OR (compression index crosses below its configured compression threshold after remaining at or above it for at least the configured minimum number of bars AND absolute release impulse ≥ its configured threshold AND release bias = +1); when confirmed-event mode is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
Short: (wave direction = −1 AND wave direction differs from the prior bar) OR (compression index crosses below its configured compression threshold after remaining at or above it for at least the configured minimum number of bars AND absolute release impulse ≥ its configured threshold AND release bias = −1); when confirmed-event mode is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
Exit: Long, wave direction = +1 AND wave age ≥ its configured minimum AND torsion core ≥ upper adaptive regime band + configured exhaustion offset AND torsion core is falling AND wave stretch ≥ its configured threshold; when confirmed-event mode is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
Exit: Short, wave direction = −1 AND wave age ≥ its configured minimum AND torsion core ≤ lower adaptive regime band − configured exhaustion offset AND torsion core is rising AND wave stretch ≥ its configured threshold; when confirmed-event mode is enabled, require a confirmed bar.
