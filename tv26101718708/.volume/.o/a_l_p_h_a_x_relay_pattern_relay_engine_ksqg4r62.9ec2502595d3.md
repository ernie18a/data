Calculation: Pivot high/low = a confirmed local high/low using equal configurable left and right lookbacks; use confirmed pivots, ordered newest to oldest.
Calculation: Projected trendline = straight-line projection through two pivots to the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart bars.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = fast EMA > slow EMA AND current higher-timeframe close > fast EMA; bearish bias = fast EMA < slow EMA AND current higher-timeframe close < fast EMA. Use the higher-timeframe bar value available on the current chart bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Pattern completion = pattern base completion, plus points when price is on the breakout side of its boundary and when within the configured ATR distance of that boundary; use each pattern family’s own base score and bonuses.
Calculation: Confluence score = points for completion, higher-timeframe alignment (or neutral when disabled), passing the choppiness gate, phase (FIRED > ARMED > FORMING), low volume relative to its moving average when enabled, reward-to-risk meeting its minimum, and pattern size meeting its ATR minimum.
Calculation: Candidate rank = confluence score + 0.05 × completion percentage + phase bonus (FIRED > ARMED > FORMING).
Long: Bullish candidate is the highest-ranked active pattern, completion and confluence meet their minima, the choppiness gate passes, and either (a) it is ARMED, with close within the configured ATR distance of its upper breakout boundary, or (b) it is FIRED, with close above that boundary by the configured ATR break margin, reward-to-risk meets its minimum, and (if the hard higher-timeframe filter is enabled) higher-timeframe bias is bullish; AND the bar is confirmed, the candidate event differs from the previous event, and cooldown has elapsed. Bullish candidates: Double Bottom (latest two pivot lows are approximately equal, with the intervening pivot high between them, and height above ATR); Inverted Head & Shoulders (three pivot lows with the middle lower than both outer lows, outer lows approximately equal, and two intervening pivot highs defining a projected neckline); ascending or selected symmetrical Triangle (latest two pivot highs and lows define converging projected lines with upper above lower and base above ATR; upper slope is near flat with rising lower slope, or upper slope is falling); Bull Flag (pole exceeds its ATR minimum and both projected trendlines slope down); Falling Wedge (projected upper and lower lines slope down, lower slope is more negative, and upper is above lower).
Short: Bearish candidate is the highest-ranked active pattern, completion and confluence meet their minima, the choppiness gate passes, and either (a) it is ARMED, with close within the configured ATR distance of its lower breakout boundary, or (b) it is FIRED, with close below that boundary by the configured ATR break margin, reward-to-risk meets its minimum, and (if the hard higher-timeframe filter is enabled) higher-timeframe bias is bearish; AND the bar is confirmed, the candidate event differs from the previous event, and cooldown has elapsed. Bearish candidates: Double Top (latest two pivot highs are approximately equal, with the intervening pivot low between them, and height above ATR); Head & Shoulders (three pivot highs with the middle higher than both outer highs, outer highs approximately equal, and two intervening pivot lows defining a projected neckline); descending or selected symmetrical Triangle (latest two pivot highs and lows define converging projected lines with upper above lower and base above ATR; lower slope is near flat with falling upper slope, or upper slope is falling); Bear Flag (pole exceeds its ATR minimum and both projected trendlines slope up); Rising Wedge (projected upper and lower lines slope up, lower slope is more positive, and upper is above lower).
