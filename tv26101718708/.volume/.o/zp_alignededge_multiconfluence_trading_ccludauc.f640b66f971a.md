Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar high/low that is the highest/lowest across equal lookback windows before and after it; use only after the later window completes.
Calculation: Bullish MSB state = turns on when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high and turns off when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low; bearish MSB state is the reverse.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high OR (fast EMA crosses above mid EMA AND bullish MSB state is active AND (fast, mid, and slow EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar are in descending lookback order of length, fast > mid > slow) AND current bar is complete) OR (mid EMA crosses above the linearly weighted moving average AND bullish MSB state is active AND (fast, mid, and slow EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar are in descending lookback order of length, fast > mid > slow) AND current bar is complete) OR (fast EMA > mid EMA AND mid EMA > slow EMA)
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low OR (fast EMA crosses below mid EMA AND bearish MSB state is active AND (fast, mid, and slow EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar are in ascending lookback order of length, fast < mid < slow) AND current bar is complete) OR (mid EMA crosses below the linearly weighted moving average AND bearish MSB state is active AND (fast, mid, and slow EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar are in ascending lookback order of length, fast < mid < slow) AND current bar is complete) OR (fast EMA < mid EMA AND mid EMA < slow EMA)
