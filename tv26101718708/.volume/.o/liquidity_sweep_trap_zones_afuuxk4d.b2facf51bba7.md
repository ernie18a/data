Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Sweep penetration = distance beyond the latest confirmed swing level divided by ATR
Calculation: Wick ratio = upper or lower wick length divided by high − low
Calculation: Trap score = score from reclaim, wick ratio, sweep penetration, volume relative to its moving average, ADX, DMI alignment, EMA or Ichimoku regime alignment, premium/discount location in the recent range, and candle range relative to ATR
Long: A confirmed swing low is swept by at least the minimum penetration and no more than the maximum; close reclaims above it; and, in wick-reclaim mode, the lower-wick ratio meets its minimum; low is a local extreme; activity and ADX filters pass; DMI chop, spike, post-spike, opposing-regime, hard-block, and mitigated-level filters do not block; and cooldown, same-level distance, and trap-spacing restrictions pass
Long: A bullish trap is triggered by a qualifying bullish sweep, immediately or after the configured confirmation delay when close reaches the sweep close plus the confirmation ATR move; its trap score is at least the valid threshold (strong and valid labels) or below the valid threshold (weak label)
Short: A confirmed swing high is swept by at least the minimum penetration and no more than the maximum; close reclaims below it; and, in wick-reclaim mode, the upper-wick ratio meets its minimum; high is a local extreme; activity and ADX filters pass; DMI chop, spike, post-spike, opposing-regime, hard-block, and mitigated-level filters do not block; and cooldown, same-level distance, and trap-spacing restrictions pass
Short: A bearish trap is triggered by a qualifying bearish sweep, immediately or after the configured confirmation delay when close reaches the sweep close minus the confirmation ATR move; its trap score is at least the valid threshold (strong and valid labels) or below the valid threshold (weak label)
