Calculation: Exponential moving average = standard EMA of close; use distinct configurable lengths for each average.
Calculation: Average true range = standard ATR of high, low, and close.
Calculation: Normalized value = 100 × (value − its lowest value over the lookback) / (its highest value − its lowest value); use 50 if the range is zero.
Calculation: Trend score = +10 if close > long EMA and short EMA > medium EMA > intermediate EMA; −10 for the inverse; otherwise +6 if close > intermediate EMA and short EMA > medium EMA; −6 for the inverse; otherwise +3 if short EMA > medium EMA; −3 for the inverse; otherwise 0. Convert a score to a percentage by clamping (5 × score + 50) to 0–100.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe trend state = on each of four configured higher timeframes, initialize direction from close versus 38-bar linear regression of (high + low)/2; use a trailing rail at that regression ± 2.4 ATR(14), ratcheting in the direction of travel. On a confirmed higher-timeframe bar, switch up when close crosses above the prior rail while direction is down, or switch down when close crosses below it while direction is up. Use the last completed bar on each higher timeframe. Multi-timeframe bullish percentage = 25 × number of up states.
Calculation: Price-volume-momentum percentage = 0.40 × EMA(50 × (correlation of close changes and volume changes over the lookback + 1), short smoothing) + 0.35 × EMA(normalize(ATR(14)/EMA(ATR(14), lookback) × volume/EMA(volume, lookback), long lookback), short smoothing) + 0.25 × EMA(normalize(ROC(close, lookback), long lookback), short smoothing).
Calculation: Volume-efficiency percentage = 75 if (EMA(34-period percentile rank of positive close change divided by volume, smoothing) > corresponding negative-close-change efficiency, EMA of up-candle volume is nonincreasing, and EMA of down-candle volume is nondecreasing); 25 for the inverse; otherwise clamp(50 + 0.35 × (bull efficiency − bear efficiency), 0, 100). Zero-direction candle volume is excluded.
Calculation: Ichimoku percentage = convert the sum of +3.5/−3.5/0 for close above cloud / below cloud / inside cloud, +2/−2/0 for leading span A above / below span B, +2/−2/0 for conversion line above / below base line, and +1.5/−1.5/0 for close above / below its value 26 bars earlier, to a percentage using the score conversion above. Each line is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over its respective lookback; the cloud uses leading spans A and B.
Calculation: RSI-structure percentage = convert to a percentage the clamped score (−10 to 10) that is +8 when RSI is above its latest confirmed pivot high and smoothed RSI acceleration is positive, −8 when RSI is below its latest confirmed pivot low and acceleration is negative, and otherwise (RSI − 50)/5 + 2 × smoothed RSI acceleration. RSI acceleration is the change in RSI change, EMA-smoothed; pivots use equal configurable left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: RSI-price-band percentage = 78 on a squeeze release above the upper band, 22 on a release below the lower band, otherwise 65 above the upper band, 35 below the lower band, 50 during a squeeze, and 52 otherwise. Bands are price SMA ± (RSI SMA ± RSI standard deviation − 50) × price standard deviation/50. A squeeze is RSI band width below its SMA; release is squeezed on the prior bar and not squeezed now.
Calculation: Regression-flow percentage = clamp(50 + 0.5 × flow power, 0, 100) when the smoothed regression core rises; clamp(50 − 0.5 × flow power, 0, 100) when it falls; otherwise 50. The core is an EMA of the linear regression of (high + low)/2, adjusted by its slope and curve; flow power is an EMA of 0.40 × normalized absolute slope + 0.20 × normalized absolute curve + 0.25 × clamped distance from close to core divided by max(EMA absolute deviation, ATR) × 50 + 0.15 × normalized ATR.
Calculation: Structure percentage = convert the clamped (−10 to 10) sum of +2 for latest swing high above prior swing high (or −2 if not above), +2 for latest swing low above prior swing low (or −2 if not above), +4 when close exceeds latest swing high, and −4 when close is below latest swing low. Swings are confirmed pivots with equal configurable left and right lookbacks; retain alternating pivots, replacing a same-type pivot only when more extreme.
Calculation: OTE-structure percentage = 68 when the last four alternating confirmed pivots form a bullish impulse (third pivot low exceeds first low and fourth high exceeds second high); 32 for the inverse bearish impulse; otherwise 50.
Calculation: Power-zone bullish and bearish scores = add 3 for price within the 38.2%–61.8% range of the rolling swing range and above the latest pivot low (bullish), or below the latest pivot high (bearish); add 3 for a qualifying directional pin bar, or 2.5 for a qualifying engulfing candle; add min(volume / SMA(volume), 5) when volume exceeds its configured multiple of its SMA and candle direction agrees; add 2 when the corresponding latest pivot level has at least two tests within 0.2%. A pin bar has the relevant wick > 2 × body, opposite wick < 0.7 × body, matching candle direction, and range > 0.5 ATR(14). An engulfing candle has opposite prior candle direction, matching current direction, body engulfing the prior body, and current body > 1.2 × prior body. Power-zone raw directional condition = corresponding score ≥ its configured threshold. Power-zone event = raw condition true now and false on prior bar, with elapsed bars since same-side event greater than its cooldown.
Calculation: Liquidity percentage = convert the clamped score: +7 for a low sweep (low below the prior lookback low and close back above it) with close−low > ATR(14) × sensitivity; −7 for the inverse high sweep with high−close > that amount; otherwise +4 for close above the prior lookback high, −4 for close below the prior lookback low, else 0.
Calculation: Volume-delta percentage = convert the clamped score (−10 to 10) of 20 × (mean of three normalized values − 0.5). The values are normalized over the same lookback: EMA-smoothed merged-candle signed volume, EMA of cumulative merged-candle signed volume, and EMA of cumulative merged-volume signed by change in merged close. A merged candle spans the configured number of bars, sums their volume, and signs it by merged close versus merged open.
Calculation: Harmonic percentage = start at 50; with five latest alternating pivots, use the final three leg lengths to calculate AB/XA, BC/AB, and CD/BC. Bullish ABCD (pivot types low-high-low-high) sets 64; bearish ABCD (high-low-high-low) sets 36 when CD/BC is within the configured tolerance of 0.88–1.272. Gartley-like (AB/XA 0.55–0.70 and BC/AB 0.382–0.886), Bat-like (0.382–0.55 and 0.382–0.886), or Butterfly-like (AB/XA 0.70–0.886 and CD/BC ≥ 1.272) changes a value above 50 to 66/65/67, respectively, or a value below 50 to 34/35/33.
Calculation: Volatility percentage = convert the clamped score (−10 to 10): +5 when ATR(14)/EMA(ATR(14)) > 1.10 and close > medium EMA; −5 when the ratio > 1.10 and close < medium EMA; 0 when ratio < 0.90; otherwise +2 above medium EMA, −2 below it, or 0.
Calculation: Fibonacci-pullback percentage = convert the clamped directional score (−10 to 10). From the latest confirmed impulse pivot pair, calculate retracement: (impulse high − close)/range for an up impulse, or (close − impulse low)/range for a down impulse. Within the configured healthy range, score 3/5/8/10 for retracement bands 0.382–0.500/0.500–0.618/0.618–0.786/0.786–0.886; score −3 beyond the maximum. Apply the positive score for an up-impulse reaction (close > open OR close > prior close OR smoothed RSI acceleration > 0), its negative for the inverse down-impulse reaction, and otherwise 0. When pullbacks are disabled, percentage is 50. A bullish/bearish pullback bonus requires the matching reaction and score ≥ its configured bonus threshold.
Calculation: Final percentage = weighted mean of core percentage, structure/flow percentage, power/liquidity percentage, and Fibonacci-pullback percentage (include the latter only when enabled). Core percentage is the weighted mean of trend, multi-timeframe, price-volume-momentum, volume-efficiency, and Ichimoku percentages. Structure/flow percentage is the weighted mean of RSI-structure, RSI-price-band, regression-flow, structure, and OTE-structure percentages. Power/liquidity percentage is the weighted mean of power-zone, liquidity, volume-delta, harmonic, and volatility percentages. Use independently configurable component weights. Clamp the result to 0–100.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish agreement counts = number of qualifying components: trend percentage ≥60 / ≤40; at least 3 of 4 multi-timeframe states up / down; price-volume-momentum, volume-efficiency, RSI-price-band, regression-flow, structure, OTE-structure, liquidity, volume-delta, harmonic, and volatility percentage ≥60 / ≤40; Ichimoku and RSI-structure percentage ≥60 / ≤40; plus the corresponding Fibonacci-pullback bonus. Power-zone percentage ≥60 / ≤40 also counts. Agreement difference is bullish count minus bearish count.
Calculation: Net directional force = signed square root of the sum, over trend, multi-timeframe, price-volume-momentum, volume-efficiency, Ichimoku, RSI-structure, RSI-price-band, regression-flow, structure, OTE-structure, power-zone, liquidity, volume-delta, harmonic, volatility, and Fibonacci-pullback percentages, of each percentage minus 50. Bull cloud line = EMA(close, basis lookback) − abs(net directional force) × ATR(configured length) × multiplier × cloud-width multiplier; bear cloud line uses plus instead of minus.
Calculation: Final edge = final percentage − EMA(final percentage, configured lookback).
Long: Confirmed bar AND final percentage ≥ configured bull threshold AND bullish agreement count ≥ configured minimum AND bullish count > bearish count AND (edge filter disabled OR final edge > configured minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR net directional force ≥ configured signal threshold AND close > bull cloud line) AND elapsed bars since prior long signal > configured cooldown AND position signal state is not long.
Short: Confirmed bar AND final percentage ≤ configured bear threshold AND bearish agreement count ≥ configured minimum AND bearish count > bullish count AND (edge filter disabled OR final edge < −configured minimum edge) AND (net-force filter disabled OR net directional force ≤ −configured signal threshold AND close < bear cloud line) AND elapsed bars since prior short signal > configured cooldown AND position signal state is not short.
Long: Power-zone event bullish score condition.
Short: Power-zone event bearish score condition.
Long: Net directional force ≥ configured signal threshold AND close > bull cloud line.
Short: Net directional force ≤ −configured signal threshold AND close < bear cloud line.
Exit: Long, final percentage < 50.
Exit: Short, final percentage > 50.
