Calculation: Breakout zone = saved breakout level ± current ATR × configured zone buffer.
Long: A close crosses above the previous completed daily high OR the high of the first 5 session bars OR the high of the first 15 session bars OR the highest high of the 10 previous completed daily bars; AND the bar is in session after the configured opening skip; AND today’s open is within the configured percentage gap range from the previous completed daily close; AND close > session VWAP AND session VWAP > prior bar’s VWAP; AND volume / its configured moving average >= configured breakout threshold; AND day-pace RVOL >= configured threshold when enabled; AND high − low <= current ATR × configured maximum; AND no setup is active.
Long: An active setup has not yet had a retest; the bar’s range intersects the breakout zone; close >= zone low; and close >= session VWAP.
