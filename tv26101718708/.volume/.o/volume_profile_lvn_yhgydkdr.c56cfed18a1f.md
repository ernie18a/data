Calculation: Volume profile zones = Divide the current session, or a trailing lookback of same-timeframe bars ending at the current bar, into configurable equal price bins from the lowest low to the highest high; allocate each bar’s volume to bins in proportion to price-range overlap. A qualifying low-volume node is a bin whose volume exceeds a configurable fraction of the maximum bin volume and is strictly lower than each of a configurable number of neighboring bins on both sides. Merge contiguous qualifying bins; add zones spanning the top and bottom three profile bins; retain the configurable number of zones nearest the current close. A zone is support when its midpoint is below the close and resistance when above it.
Long: (low <= support zone top AND high >= support zone bottom AND close > support zone top) OR (previous close < resistance zone bottom AND close > resistance zone top AND volume > 0)
Short: (high >= resistance zone bottom AND low <= resistance zone top AND close < resistance zone bottom) OR (previous close > support zone top AND close < support zone bottom AND volume > 0)
