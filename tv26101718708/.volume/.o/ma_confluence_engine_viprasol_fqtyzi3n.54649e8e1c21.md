Calculation: 15 moving averages = apply the same selected moving-average method to close using lookbacks that increase from one average to the next
Calculation: Consensus score = 100 × (number of the 15 averages below close + number of the 15 averages higher than their values on the previous bar) / 30
Calculation: Ribbon width = (highest of the 15 averages − lowest of the 15 averages) / ATR
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over a lookback / sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback
Long: Consensus score crosses above the bullish threshold AND ribbon width is at least its minimum threshold AND efficiency ratio is at least its minimum threshold
Short: Consensus score crosses below the bearish threshold AND ribbon width is at least its minimum threshold AND efficiency ratio is at least its minimum threshold
