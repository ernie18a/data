Calculation: Pivot = highest high over the lookback, excluding the current daily bar
Calculation: Relative volume = current daily volume / longer-lookback average daily volume
Calculation: VCP score = weighted score for base depth within bounds, short-range contraction versus longer ranges, short ATR contraction versus longer ATRs, current and average volume dry-up versus their thresholds, and right-side short-range tightness; short lookbacks are shorter than the ranges they are compared with
Long: Daily close is above the short-, intermediate-, and long-term simple moving averages AND the intermediate-term average is above the long-term average AND the long-term average is above its value at an earlier lookback AND the close is at least a threshold above the lowest low in the 52-week range AND within a threshold of the highest high in that range AND VCP score meets its minimum AND ((close is at or below Pivot and no farther below it than the READY-zone threshold) OR (close is above Pivot but no farther above it than the maximum-chase threshold AND Relative volume meets its minimum))
