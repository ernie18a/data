Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is strictly higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on each side; available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Latest unbroken pivot high/low = the most recently confirmed pivot high/low not yet crossed by a closing price; mark it broken when price crosses it.
Calculation: Zone = the high and low of the most recent opposite-color candle within the configured lookback before a structural break; bullish zones use a bearish candle, bearish zones use a bullish candle.
Calculation: Zone strength = clamp(round((mean volume of the six bars spanning two bars before through three bars after the zone’s origin / 20-bar volume SMA on the break bar) × 60), 0, 100).
Long: close > latest unbroken pivot high AND previous close <= that pivot high OR bullish zone exists with strength >= configured zone-creation minimum AND strength >= configured signal minimum AND zone is not mitigated AND current low is within the zone AND previous low was outside the zone AND zone has not been retested OR bullish zone exists with strength >= configured zone-creation minimum AND strength >= configured signal minimum AND zone is not mitigated AND zone has been retested AND close > zone bottom AND previous close <= zone bottom
Short: close < latest unbroken pivot low AND previous close >= that pivot low OR bearish zone exists with strength >= configured zone-creation minimum AND strength >= configured signal minimum AND zone is not mitigated AND current high is within the zone AND previous high was outside the zone AND zone has not been retested OR bearish zone exists with strength >= configured zone-creation minimum AND strength >= configured signal minimum AND zone is not mitigated AND zone has been retested AND close < zone top AND previous close >= zone top
