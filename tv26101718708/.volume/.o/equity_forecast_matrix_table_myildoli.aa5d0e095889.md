Calculation: Weekly regime = bullish when the current weekly close > weekly EMA(10) > weekly EMA(30); bearish when close < EMA(10) < EMA(30); otherwise neutral. Use the weekly bar still forming.
Calculation: Internal/external structure = bullish when both the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low exceed their respective previous pivots; bearish when both are lower. External pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish BOS = on a completed bar, close crosses above the latest internal/external pivot high / below the latest internal/external pivot low, respectively, with relative volume above its threshold. Relative volume = volume / longer-lookback average volume.
Calculation: kNN forecast = inverse-distance-weighted mean future log return among the nearest historical patterns. Compare patterns by weighted Euclidean distance over a lookback of ATR-normalized returns, ranges, bodies, wick imbalance, relative volume, and close-to-EMA position. Train only on patterns whose forecast horizon has completed; smooth the forecast with an EMA.
Calculation: Bull/bear kNN score = clamp(50 ± (clamp(abs(predicted log return / (ATR% × sqrt(forecast horizon))) × 22, 0, 30) + (kNN direction confidence − 50) × 0.60), 0, 100), using the positive sign for the direction of the forecast and the negative sign for the opposite direction.
Calculation: Bull/bear regression score = clamp(50 ± regression slope / residual standard deviation × 20 − extension penalty, 0, 100), using the positive sign for bullish and negative for bearish; the penalty is 8 × positive regression position for bulls and 8 × absolute negative regression position for bears. Regression uses log close, with a longer lookback for its fitted line and a shorter lookback for slope.
Calculation: Bull/bear structure score = clamp(30 + 15 if the persistent structure trend matches the side + 8 for matching internal structure + 12 for matching external structure + 10 for matching internal BOS + 16 for matching external BOS + 22 for matching CHoCH + 18 for a failed BOS in the opposite direction, 0, 100); persistent structure trend is set by the latest BOS or CHoCH and otherwise carries forward.
Calculation: Bull/bear volume score = clamp(relative-volume points (25 above the BOS threshold, else 15 at least 1, else 5) + 20 for a matching candle body + 20 for matching close change with rising volume + 25 for matching OBV slope + 10 when volume exceeds its average, 0, 100). OBV slope compares its EMA with an earlier value.
Calculation: Bull/bear score = clamp(0.40 × bull/bear kNN score + 0.20 × bull/bear regression score + 0.25 × bull/bear structure score + 0.15 × bull/bear volume score + weekly adjustment, 0, 100). Weekly adjustment is +5 when aligned, −8 when opposed, and 0 when neutral.
Long: bull score − bear score ≥ minimum directional edge.
Short: bear score − bull score ≥ minimum directional edge.
