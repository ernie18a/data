Calculation: High-price average = simple moving average of high over a longer lookback
Calculation: Smoothed open = Arnaud Legoux moving average of open; rising/falling means higher/lower than the previous bar
Calculation: Binned RSI = floor(RSI / 10) * 10, using a shorter lookback on OHLC4, a medium lookback on high, or a longer lookback on OHLC4
Calculation: Binned relative volatility = floor(relative volatility / 10) * 10, where relative volatility is 100 times the EMA of standard deviation on positive source changes divided by the sum of the EMAs of standard deviation on positive and nonpositive source changes; use high for the shorter lookback and close for the medium and longer lookbacks
Calculation: Binned money flow index = floor(money flow index / 10) * 10, using high with shorter, medium, and longer lookbacks
Calculation: Smoothed balance of power = simple moving average over a longer lookback of 100 * (close - open) / (high - low) + 50
Calculation: Buy compound = all of: longer-lookback binned RSI is at or below the lower RSI threshold and medium < longer and shorter <= medium; longer-lookback binned relative volatility is at or below its lower threshold and medium <= longer and shorter <= medium; longer-lookback binned money flow index is at or below its lower threshold and medium <= longer and shorter <= medium; smoothed balance of power is at or below its lower threshold
Calculation: Critical-bottom compound = same as buy compound, with the longer-lookback RSI, relative volatility, and money flow index thresholds set to 30, and smoothed balance of power at or below 50
Calculation: Critical-top compound = longer-lookback binned RSI is at least 60 and medium > longer and shorter >= medium; longer-lookback binned relative volatility is at least 60 and medium >= longer and shorter >= medium; longer-lookback binned money flow index is at least 60 and medium >= longer and shorter >= medium; smoothed balance of power is at least 60
Calculation: Buy-compound falling edge = buy compound was true on the previous bar and is false on the current bar
