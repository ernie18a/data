Calculation: BSL/SSL pools = confirmed swing highs/lows, respectively, merged when within an ATR-based tolerance; pool price is the touch-count-weighted average of merged pivots. Track touches, age, and whether tapped, raided, or restacked; discard pools past the age limit and retain only the configured maximum per side.
Calculation: Pool density = touches × touch weight + average relative volume × volume weight + HTF boost; relative volume = volume / volume SMA, capped at 4. HTF boost applies when a confirmed higher-timeframe pivot is within the configured tolerance of the pool. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Raid buffer = ATR × configured wick-buffer multiplier.
Long: A BSL pool is not already raided, high ≥ pool price + raid buffer, close > pool price, and pool density ≥ minimum signal density; OR, on a BSL raid, an unraided BSL pool beyond the raided price has density ≥ minimum signal density and is the densest such pool.
Short: An SSL pool is not already raided, low ≤ pool price − raid buffer, close < pool price, and pool density ≥ minimum signal density; OR, on an SSL raid, an unraided SSL pool beyond the raided price has density ≥ minimum signal density and is the densest such pool.
