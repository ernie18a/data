Calculation: Dynamic RSI oversold = lowest RSI over the dynamic RSI lookback + buffer; dynamic RSI overbought = highest RSI over that lookback − buffer.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume exceeds its volume moving average × volume multiplier; if rising volume is required, volume must also exceed the previous bar’s volume. Recent volume confirmation means this occurred within the confirmation window.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish MACD inflection = the MACD histogram formed a local trough/peak within the confirmation window.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low while its RSI is higher, with the pivots no farther apart than the maximum divergence span. Bearish divergence is the inverse using pivot highs.
Calculation: Effective mode = the selected non-auto mode; in auto mode, aggressive when Bollinger Band width and ATR exceed their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its auto-volume threshold; otherwise conservative when both band width and volume fail those expansion tests; otherwise balanced.
Calculation: Near lower/upper band = low ≤ lower band + band width × near-band percentage / 100; high ≥ upper band − band width × near-band percentage / 100, respectively.
Long: Flat position AND (configured session filter is disabled or current bar is in session) AND ((effective mode is conservative AND ((low < lower band AND RSI ≤ dynamic RSI oversold AND recent bullish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation AND close > lower band AND close > previous close) OR (bullish divergence AND pivot low < lower band at pivot AND pivot RSI ≤ dynamic RSI oversold at pivot AND recent bullish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation))) OR (effective mode is balanced AND near lower band AND RSI ≤ dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry RSI buffer AND close > open) OR (effective mode is aggressive AND near lower band AND RSI ≤ dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry RSI buffer) OR (continuation enabled AND low ≤ Bollinger basis AND close > basis AND close > open AND RSI > 50 AND MACD histogram > previous histogram AND MACD line ≥ signal line AND recent volume confirmation) OR (near-long heads-up condition: near lower band AND RSI ≤ dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry RSI buffer) OR (continuation-long warning condition: continuation enabled and at least the configured score threshold of low ≤ basis, close > basis, close > open, RSI > 50, rising MACD histogram, MACD line ≥ signal line, and recent volume confirmation).
Short: Flat position AND (configured session filter is disabled or current bar is in session) AND ((effective mode is conservative AND ((high > upper band AND RSI ≥ dynamic RSI overbought AND recent bearish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation AND close < upper band AND close < previous close) OR (bearish divergence AND pivot high > upper band at pivot AND pivot RSI ≥ dynamic RSI overbought at pivot AND recent bearish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation))) OR (effective mode is balanced AND near upper band AND RSI ≥ dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry RSI buffer AND close < open) OR (effective mode is aggressive AND near upper band AND RSI ≥ dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry RSI buffer) OR (continuation enabled AND high ≥ Bollinger basis AND close < basis AND close < open AND RSI < 50 AND MACD histogram < previous histogram AND MACD line ≤ signal line AND recent volume confirmation) OR (near-short heads-up condition: near upper band AND RSI ≥ dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry RSI buffer) OR (continuation-short warning condition: continuation enabled and at least the configured score threshold of high ≥ basis, close < basis, close < open, RSI < 50, falling MACD histogram, MACD line ≤ signal line, and recent volume confirmation).
Exit: Long, while long: selected trade-type exit condition OR low ≤ initial invalidation price OR low ≤ dynamic exit price OR (trailing stop enabled and activated and low ≤ trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled and activated and low ≤ entry price) OR configured full take-profit condition OR (maximum holding time enabled and current bar time ≥ entry time + maximum holding duration) OR (exit-soon marking enabled and histogram < previous histogram).
Exit: Short, while short: selected trade-type exit condition OR high ≥ initial invalidation price OR high ≥ dynamic exit price OR (trailing stop enabled and activated and high ≥ trailing stop) OR (break-even stop enabled and activated and high ≥ entry price) OR configured full take-profit condition OR (maximum holding time enabled and current bar time ≥ entry time + maximum holding duration) OR (exit-soon marking enabled and histogram > previous histogram).
Calculation: Reversal trade exit = selected mode: momentum reversal (enabled bearish histogram inflection, bearish MACD cross, or RSI crossing below 50 for long; enabled bullish histogram inflection, bullish MACD cross, or RSI crossing above 50 for short), first retrace (red candle for long, green candle for short), or retrace plus MACD weakness (red candle and falling histogram for long; green candle and rising histogram for short).
Calculation: Continuation trade exit = selected mode: the same momentum reversal condition; MACD cross only (bearish cross for long, bullish cross for short); or basis break plus MACD weakness (close below basis and falling histogram for long; close above basis and rising histogram for short).
Calculation: Dynamic exit price starts at the lowest low over the dynamic exit lookback minus the tick buffer for a long, or highest high plus the buffer for a short; while holding, it can only rise for a long or fall for a short.
Calculation: Trailing stop activates after price moves the configured ATR multiple from entry; it follows the highest high minus ATR multiple for a long, or lowest low plus ATR multiple for a short, and only tightens.
Calculation: Full take-profit condition = with take-profit enabled, a target is reached and either target 2 is reached after target 1, or target 3 is reached. Targets are set from entry using the configured ATR multiples in the favorable direction.
