Calculation: Adaptive prior-day S/R = high and low of the last completed daily bar; if their range as a percentage of the low is below a minimum, use the rolling high and low of the last two completed daily bars, extending to longer completed-day windows until the minimum is met or the maximum lookback is reached. Midpoint = (adaptive high + adaptive low) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low while RSI at the new pivot is higher than RSI at the previous pivot. Bearish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high while RSI at the new pivot is lower than RSI at the previous pivot. Confirm pivots using left and right lookbacks; compare RSI values from the pivot bars. A divergence remains fresh for a limited number of bars after confirmation.
Long: (bullish divergence is newly confirmed) OR (flat, no cycle already completed today, before the UTC entry cutoff, low <= adaptive prior low × (1 + touch-zone fraction), low > adaptive prior low × (1 − 1%), volume >= volume multiplier × 20-bar simple moving average of volume, and a fresh bullish RSI divergence)
Short: (bearish divergence is newly confirmed) OR (flat, no cycle already completed today, before the UTC entry cutoff, high >= adaptive prior high × (1 − touch-zone fraction), high < adaptive prior high × (1 + 1%), volume >= volume multiplier × 20-bar simple moving average of volume, and a fresh bearish RSI divergence)
Long: in a long position with an unfilled add level, low <= worst long entry × (1 − DCA spacing)
Short: in a short position with an unfilled add level, high >= worst short entry × (1 + DCA spacing)
Exit: long, high >= (before an add: adaptive midpoint × (1 − midpoint offset); after an add: first entry price × (1 + post-add target fraction)) OR low <= max(worst long entry × (1 − stop fraction), after breakeven activation: first entry price × (1 + breakeven buffer fraction)) OR (at or after the UTC close hour, unless this trade has not previously been held past that hour and close is at least the hold threshold fraction above first entry)
Exit: short, low <= (before an add: adaptive midpoint × (1 + midpoint offset); after an add: first entry price × (1 − post-add target fraction)) OR high >= min(worst short entry × (1 + stop fraction), after breakeven activation: first entry price × (1 − breakeven buffer fraction)) OR (at or after the UTC close hour, unless this trade has not previously been held past that hour and close is at least the hold threshold fraction below first entry)
Exit: long or short, at or after the UTC close hour on the day following a skipped close
