Calculation: Dealing range = the range between the most recent confirmed pivot high and most recent confirmed pivot low; each pivot uses a lookback on both sides and is available only after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Equilibrium = dealing-range midpoint.
Calculation: Buy OTE = 21.4%–38.2% of the dealing range above its low; Sell OTE = 61.8%–78.6% above its low.
Long: close < Equilibrium OR close is within Buy OTE OR low[1] > high[3] AND low[1] - high[3] >= ATR(14) × minimum FVG-size multiplier OR close > open AND abs(close - open) >= ATR(14) × displacement multiplier AND (high - close) / (high - low) <= maximum wick-to-range ratio AND volume > SMA(volume, 20) × 1.5 OR low < the lowest low of the preceding IDM-lookback bars AND close > that low AND that low - low >= ATR(14) × minimum IDM-wick multiplier
