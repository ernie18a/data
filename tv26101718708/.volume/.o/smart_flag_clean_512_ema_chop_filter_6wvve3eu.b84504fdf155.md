Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA; its slope is its one-bar percentage change.
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close; its slope is its one-bar percentage change.
Calculation: Trend SMA = simple moving average of close with a longer lookback than either EMA; its slope is its one-bar percentage change.
Calculation: ATR = average true range.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price.
Calculation: Lower room (ATR) = (close − lowest low over the swing lookback) / ATR; use 0 when ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Upper room (ATR) = (highest high over the swing lookback − close) / ATR; use 0 when ATR is not positive.
Calculation: Bear rejection = close < open, body size / (high − low) ≥ the rejection threshold, and close is in the bottom portion of the candle range; use 0 for body fraction when range is zero.
Calculation: Bull rejection = close > open, body size / (high − low) ≥ the rejection threshold, and close is in the top portion of the candle range; use 0 for body fraction when range is zero.
Calculation: Lower-high context = high < prior high AND prior high ≤ the high two bars ago.
Calculation: Higher-low context = low > prior low AND prior low ≥ the low two bars ago.
Calculation: Failed pop = high is below the highest high over the swing lookback AND close < open AND close < fast EMA.
Calculation: Lost fast EMA = close < fast EMA AND close < slow EMA.
Calculation: Chop zone = both EMA slopes are within the flat-slope threshold OR the absolute EMA spread is within 0.10 ATR.
Calculation: Mean chop = chop filter is enabled AND, for the configured consecutive-bar lookback, each bar has close within the configured ATR distance of trend SMA, trend SMA slope within the configured flatness threshold, and (if candle overlap is required) low ≤ trend SMA ≤ high.
Calculation: Long score = +1 fast EMA > slow EMA; +2 both EMA slopes exceed the positive flat-slope threshold and fast EMA > slow EMA; +1 close > trend SMA; +1 trend SMA slope ≥ 0; +1 higher-low context; +1 bull rejection; +1 volume ≥ volume average; +1 volume ≥ 1.20 × volume average; +1 upper room (ATR) ≥ minimum room; +1 upper room (ATR) ≥ strong room; +1 (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP); −2 chop zone; −2 upper room (ATR) < minimum room; −3 mean chop.
Calculation: Trend short score = +1 fast EMA < slow EMA; +2 both EMA slopes are below the negative flat-slope threshold and fast EMA < slow EMA; +1 close < trend SMA; +1 trend SMA slope ≤ 0; +1 lower-high context; +1 bear rejection; +1 volume ≥ volume average; +1 volume ≥ 1.20 × volume average; +1 lower room (ATR) ≥ minimum room; +1 lower room (ATR) ≥ strong room; +1 (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP); −2 chop zone; −2 lower room (ATR) < minimum room; −3 mean chop.
Calculation: Reversal short score = +1 fast EMA < slow EMA; +1 fast EMA slope below the negative flat-slope threshold; +1 slow EMA slope below the negative flat-slope threshold; +1 lower-high context; +1 bear rejection; +1 failed pop; +1 lost fast EMA; +1 volume ≥ volume average; +1 volume ≥ 1.20 × volume average; +1 lower room (ATR) ≥ minimum room; +1 lower room (ATR) ≥ strong room; +1 (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP); −1 chop zone; −1 close lies between 35% and 65% of the range from the swing low to swing high; −2 lower room (ATR) < minimum room; −3 mean chop.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND NOT mean chop AND long score ≥ 4.
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND NOT mean chop AND ((trend short score ≥ 6 AND both EMA slopes are below the negative flat-slope threshold AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend SMA AND lower room (ATR) ≥ strong room AND NOT chop zone AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP)) OR (reversal short score ≥ 5 AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND lower room (ATR) ≥ minimum room AND NOT both EMA slopes within the flat-slope threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP)) OR (NOT either of the preceding A short conditions AND (trend short score ≥ 4 OR reversal short score ≥ 4))).
