Calculation: Short-term volume EMA = EMA of volume with a shorter lookback than the long-term volume EMA
Calculation: Long-term volume EMA = EMA of volume with a longer lookback than the short-term volume EMA
Calculation: Percentage Volume Oscillator = (short-term volume EMA - long-term volume EMA) / long-term volume EMA × 100
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of the Percentage Volume Oscillator
Long: Signal line > Percentage Volume Oscillator
Short: Percentage Volume Oscillator > Signal line
