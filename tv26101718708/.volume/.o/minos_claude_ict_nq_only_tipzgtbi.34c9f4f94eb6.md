Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; zone spans that earlier high to current low
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier; zone spans current high to that earlier low
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low with the selected swing-length number of bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot of each type
Calculation: Bullish order block = the most recent bearish candle among the prior 15 bars when a bullish structure break occurs; zone spans that candle’s low to high. Bearish order block = the most recent bullish candle among the prior 15 bars when a bearish structure break occurs; zone spans that candle’s low to high. Retain the latest zone of each type
Calculation: Equal HTF highs/lows = adjacent historical values of the selected higher-timeframe high/low whose absolute difference divided by the first value is below the selected threshold; compare offsets 2 through the selected lookback and each next offset. Use lookahead-off HTF values; offsets are chart bars and may refer to a still-forming HTF bar in realtime
Long: current low > high two bars earlier OR current low ≤ top of any bullish fair value gap and current close ≥ its bottom OR current close > latest confirmed swing high and previous close ≤ that swing high OR current low ≤ bullish order-block high and current close ≥ its low and current close > previous close OR current low < the lower of an equal HTF-low pair and current close > that lower level
Short: current high < low two bars earlier OR current high ≥ bottom of any bearish fair value gap and current close ≤ its top OR current close < latest confirmed swing low and previous close ≥ that swing low OR current high ≥ bearish order-block low and current close ≤ its high and current close < previous close OR current high > the higher of an equal HTF-high pair and current close < that higher level
