Calculation: Four ratcheting trail bands = source ± ATR × four proportional multipliers (base, base×(1+step), base×(1+2×step), base×(1+3×step)); in an uptrend each lower trail is the maximum of its current candidate and prior value, in a downtrend each upper trail is the minimum; on a flip, reseed on the opposite side of price.
Calculation: Trend age = number of bars since the latest flip.
Calculation: Retest depth score = highest band touched against the current trend during the pending retest window: Band 2 = 25, Band 3 = 18, Band 1 = 15, Band 4 = 10.
Calculation: Retest quality score = depth score + candle score + volume score + higher-timeframe bias score + trend-age score. Candle score: for longs, close-location value (close−low)/(high−low); for shorts, its mirror (high−close)/(high−low); above the high threshold = 20, above the middle threshold = 12, otherwise 5. Volume score: compare current volume with the previous bar's short-term volume SMA; above the high relative threshold = 20, above baseline = 12, otherwise 5; if volume is unavailable, 12. Bias score: aligned = 20, unavailable = 10, opposed = 0. Trend-age score: middle age range = 15, younger = 8, older = 5.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = previous completed higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA; if no higher timeframe is configured, use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Entry stop and target levels = use the selected stop mode: ATR-distance stop from entry, or wick-anchored stop beyond the signal bar's low (long) or high (short), buffered by ATR and kept at least the minimum ATR distance away. Risk = distance from entry to stop; targets 1–3 = entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk multiplied by the selected target multiples.
Long: After warm-up, confirmed bar close is above the prior bar's selected trail band while trend is down (bullish flip); OR trend is up, a low touched a trail band within the retest window, close is above Band 1 and open, retest quality score meets its threshold, and the shared signal cooldown has elapsed (long retest).
Short: After warm-up, confirmed bar close is below the prior bar's selected trail band while trend is up (bearish flip); OR trend is down, a high touched a trail band within the retest window, close is below Band 1 and open, retest quality score meets its threshold, and the shared signal cooldown has elapsed (short retest).
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ target 3 OR a short signal occurs; if both stop and target are touched on the same bar, stop takes precedence. After target 1 is touched, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled; that new stop cannot trigger on that bar.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ target 3 OR a long signal occurs; if both stop and target are touched on the same bar, stop takes precedence. After target 1 is touched, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled; that new stop cannot trigger on that bar.
