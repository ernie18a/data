Calculation: Bull score = 2 for a close above the prior lookback bars’ highest high, plus 1 each for volume above its moving average times the configured spike multiplier on an up candle, current-timeframe RSI above its configured bull threshold, 1-hour RSI above its midpoint, a bullish engulfing candle, an up candle whose body exceeds the configured fraction of its 10-bar average body, positive DI above negative DI, ADX at or above its configured strong-trend threshold, close above session VWAP, and close within one ATR of either Camarilla L3 or L4 from the previous completed daily bar.
Calculation: Bear score = 2 for a close below the prior lookback bars’ lowest low, plus 1 each for volume above its moving average times the configured spike multiplier on a down candle, current-timeframe RSI below its configured bear threshold, 1-hour RSI below its midpoint, a bearish engulfing candle, a down candle whose body exceeds the configured fraction of its 10-bar average body, negative DI above positive DI, ADX at or above its configured strong-trend threshold, close below session VWAP, and close within one ATR of either Camarilla H3 or H4 from the previous completed daily bar.
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper band minus lower band; squeeze = width below its 50-bar average times the configured fraction.
Calculation: Initial long stop and target = entry price minus/plus the configured ATR multiples using ATR at entry; initial short stop and target = entry price plus/minus those multiples using ATR at entry. After favorable movement reaches the configured ATR activation distance, long trailing stop = the greater of the initial stop and highest high since entry minus the configured current-ATR multiple; short trailing stop = the lesser of the initial stop and lowest low since entry plus the configured current-ATR multiple.
Long: Bull score >= configured minimum AND ADX >= configured minimum AND Bollinger Band width is not in a squeeze AND close > the longer-period EMA AND flat position AND cooldown is zero.
Short: Bear score >= configured minimum AND ADX >= configured minimum AND Bollinger Band width is not in a squeeze AND close < the longer-period EMA AND flat position AND cooldown is zero.
Exit: long, close <= initial stop OR close >= initial target OR close <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, close >= initial stop OR close <= initial target OR close >= trailing stop.
