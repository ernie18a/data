Calculation: Intrabar buy volume = sum of each lower-timeframe bar’s volume weighted by (close − low) / (high − low), clipped to [0,1]; sell volume = total volume − buy volume. If high = low, assign all volume to buy when close ≥ open, otherwise to sell.
Calculation: For each enabled chart or higher timeframe, use its retained completed bars plus its currently forming bar. Delta bias = 2 × buy volume / (buy volume + sell volume) − 1; candle-direction bias = 2 × fraction of those bars with close ≥ open − 1.
Calculation: Structure state = persistent +1 after a timeframe bar closes above its latest confirmed pivot high, or −1 after it closes below its latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the prior state. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Flow toxicity = SMA(abs(buy volume − sell volume), long lookback) / SMA(buy volume + sell volume, same lookback).
Calculation: Panel probability = logistic(fusion gain × direction score × disagreement factor × toxicity factor), where direction score = (1 − structure weight) × delta bias + structure weight × structure state; disagreement factor is 1 when structure is neutral or agrees with delta, otherwise the divergence dampener; toxicity factor = 0.5 + min(toxicity, 1) when toxicity scaling is enabled, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Combined probability = logistic(correlation discount × weighted mean of active panel logits), weighting each panel by the square root of its timeframe duration; use equal weights if timeframe weighting is disabled. Include the chart-timeframe panel when enabled.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = rolling sum of chart-timeframe buy volume − sell volume over a shorter lookback. Delta z-score = (cumulative delta − its SMA over a longer lookback) / its standard deviation over that same longer lookback.
Calculation: Price range position = (close − rolling low) / (rolling high − rolling low), using the shorter lookback. If rolling high equals rolling low, use 0.5.
Long: Any active panel probability > 0.53 OR combined probability > 0.53 OR price range position ≤ 1 − extreme threshold AND delta z-score > −confirmation threshold AND (toxicity requirement is disabled OR flow toxicity > its SMA over a longer lookback × toxicity multiplier).
Short: Any active panel probability < 0.47 OR combined probability < 0.47 OR price range position ≥ extreme threshold AND delta z-score < confirmation threshold AND (toxicity requirement is disabled OR flow toxicity > its SMA over a longer lookback × toxicity multiplier).
