Calculation: Fast and slow ribbon averages = for each ribbon, the average of its shorter-lookback and longer-lookback moving averages of close; both fast lookbacks are shorter than both slow lookbacks.
Calculation: Ribbon expansion count = consecutive bars on which the specified difference is positive and greater than its value on the prior bar; otherwise 0. Use fast-average minus slow-average for the overall count, fast shorter-lookback MA minus fast longer-lookback MA for the fast count, and slow shorter-lookback MA minus slow longer-lookback MA for the slow count.
Calculation: Long ribbon score = 0 unless all three expansion counts are at least 2; when they are, use 2 if the prior bar's long ribbon score was 0, otherwise prior score + 1.
Calculation: Short ribbon score = 0 unless all three expansion counts are at least 2 with each difference negative and smaller than on the prior bar; when they are, use 2 if the prior bar's short ribbon score was 0, otherwise prior score + 1.
Calculation: Channel midpoint = midpoint of the 21-bar highest high and lowest low.
Calculation: Lower and upper channels = 50-bar SMA of close minus and plus 0.25 times the 50-bar EMA of true range, respectively.
Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close with a 5-bar lookback.
Calculation: Long RSI event = long setup is true now and was false on the prior bar; long setup means channel midpoint is above its value 5 bars ago, close is above the lower channel and below the channel midpoint, and RSI is below 35.
Calculation: Short RSI event = short setup is true now and was false on the prior bar; short setup means channel midpoint is below its value 5 bars ago, close is below the upper channel and above the channel midpoint, and RSI is above 65.
Calculation: Long score = long ribbon score + 1 if the long RSI event is true + 1 if the parabolic SAR is above the bar's high.
Calculation: Short score = short ribbon score + 1 if the short RSI event is true + 1 if the parabolic SAR is below the bar's low.
Calculation: Effective high and low = high and low, or in Renko mode the greater and lesser of open and close, respectively.
Calculation: Long extreme = greatest effective high since long entry; short extreme = least effective low since short entry.
Calculation: Trailing profit percentage = the lesser of the trailing-profit and take-profit percentages.
Long: Long score >= 3 AND trade state is flat AND bar is confirmed or historical AND long orders are enabled AND no long or short close is active; if both scores qualify while flat, long takes priority.
Short: Short score >= 3 AND trade state is flat AND bar is confirmed or historical AND short orders are enabled AND no long or short close is active; if both scores qualify while flat, long takes priority.
Exit: Long, take profit is enabled AND long extreme >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) AND effective low <= long extreme * (1 - trailing profit percentage), OR stop loss is enabled AND effective low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage), OR trailing stop is enabled AND effective low <= long extreme * (1 - trailing-stop percentage), OR (opposite-trade close is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) AND the long exit trigger changes from false to true; long exit trigger = prior-bar fast ribbon average < prior-bar slow ribbon average AND current open < prior-bar slow ribbon average; do not exit on a bar with a long entry condition.
Exit: Short, take profit is enabled AND short extreme <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) AND effective high >= short extreme * (1 + trailing profit percentage), OR stop loss is enabled AND effective high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage), OR trailing stop is enabled AND effective high >= short extreme * (1 + trailing-stop percentage), OR (opposite-trade close is enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) AND the short exit trigger changes from false to true; short exit trigger = prior-bar fast ribbon average > prior-bar slow ribbon average AND current open > prior-bar slow ribbon average; do not exit on a bar with a short entry condition.
