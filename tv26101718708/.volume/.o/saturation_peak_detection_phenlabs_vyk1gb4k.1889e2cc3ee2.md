Calculation: Momentum = selected RSI, ROC, MFI, or the average of RSI, normalized ROC, and MFI; normalized ROC = clamp(50 + 50 × ROC / (2 × 100-bar standard deviation of ROC + 0.0001), 0, 100)
Calculation: Rolling peak/trough = highest/lowest Momentum over the peak lookback, including the current bar; peak/trough price and volume = high/low and volume on the bar of that extreme
Calculation: Adaptive pullback = configured pullback percentage × clamp((ATR / close) / (50-bar SMA of ATR / close), 0.6, 1.8), or the configured percentage when adaptation is off
Calculation: Bear strength = round(clamp((peak RSI-space momentum − 50) × 0.8, 0, 40) + clamp((100 × (rolling peak − Momentum) / (rolling peak + 0.0001) − adaptive pullback) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((peak volume / (20-bar SMA of volume + 0.0001) − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + 20 if bear divergence and divergence scoring is enabled, otherwise 0)
Calculation: Bull strength = round(clamp((50 − trough RSI-space momentum) × 0.8, 0, 40) + clamp((100 × (Momentum − rolling trough) / (abs(rolling trough) + 0.0001) − adaptive pullback) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((trough volume / (20-bar SMA of volume + 0.0001) − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + 20 if bull divergence and divergence scoring is enabled, otherwise 0)
Long: current bar is confirmed AND Momentum ≥ rolling trough + abs(rolling trough) × adaptive pullback / 100 AND this trough has not triggered before AND bars since the last zone ≥ configured cooldown AND (absolute-extreme gate is off OR trough RSI-space momentum ≤ configured oversold threshold) AND (volume filter is off OR trough volume > 20-bar SMA of volume × configured volume multiplier) AND (pivot confirmation is zero OR trough is at least the configured number of bars old) AND Bull strength ≥ configured minimum AND (no previous accepted trough exists OR trough-bar low < previous accepted trough-bar low AND rolling trough > previous accepted rolling trough)
Short: current bar is confirmed AND Momentum ≤ rolling peak × (1 − adaptive pullback / 100) AND this peak has not triggered before AND bars since the last zone ≥ configured cooldown AND (absolute-extreme gate is off OR peak RSI-space momentum ≥ configured overbought threshold) AND (volume filter is off OR peak volume > 20-bar SMA of volume × configured volume multiplier) AND (pivot confirmation is zero OR peak is at least the configured number of bars old) AND Bear strength ≥ configured minimum AND (no previous accepted peak exists OR peak-bar high > previous accepted peak-bar high AND rolling peak < previous accepted rolling peak)
