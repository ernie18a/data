Calculation: H1 MA bias = Sort the configured visible moving averages by increasing lookback. If fewer than two are visible or any pair is closer than the configured fraction of ATR, bias is 0. Otherwise let F be the shortest-lookback MA, S the longest, M the second-longest, and T the third-longest (using the script’s fallback to M when unavailable). Bias is +2 if M>S, T>M, F>T, and close>F; otherwise +1 if M>S, T>M, and close>T; otherwise −2 if M<S, T<M, F<T, and close<F; otherwise −1 if M<S, T<M, and close<T; otherwise 0.
Calculation: ATR spacing = ATR multiplied by a configured fraction; ATR uses a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Ladder bias = Count the true conditions among the four support comparisons (longer-lookback low < next-shorter-lookback low) minus the count among the four resistance comparisons (longer-lookback high > next-shorter-lookback high), using lookbacks ordered longest to shortest. Map net count ≥2 to +2, 1 to +1, −1 to −1, ≤−2 to −2, and 0 to 0. Each high/low is the highest high/lowest low over its lookback, including the current chart bar.
Calculation: H1TF bias = Set to 0 if any configured visible MA pair is closer than ATR spacing; otherwise add H1 MA bias and Ladder bias. Map sum ≥3 to +2, >0 to +1, ≤−3 to −2, <0 to −1, and 0 to 0.
Calculation: HTF bias = Separately for H4 and daily bars, compare the current chart close with the EMA using the shorter lookback, and compare that EMA with the longer-lookback EMA and SMA. Each timeframe contributes +1 when close > EMA(short) > EMA(long) > SMA(longest), −1 when all inequalities are reversed, and 0 otherwise. Use the latest available H4 and daily bars; on historical chart bars use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Add both contributions and map sums >1 to +2, 1 to +1, −1 to −1, <−1 to −2, and 0 to 0.
Calculation: Engulfing bar = On the previous completed chart bar, its body strictly contains the preceding bar’s body, its direction is bullish (close > open) or bearish (close < open), its range is at least a configured fraction of the prior bar’s ATR, and its body/range is at least a configured minimum.
Calculation: Long-tail bar = On the previous completed chart bar, its range is at least a configured fraction of the prior bar’s ATR; its lower tail is at least a configured fraction of range and body is in the upper part of range for bullish, or its upper tail meets that fraction and body is in the lower part for bearish; its close is respectively above or below its open. If enabled, require body/range to meet a configured minimum.
Calculation: RSI breakout = RSI crosses from below to at-or-above its configured upper threshold, or from above to at-or-below its configured lower threshold.
Calculation: RSI retracement = RSI was below the lower threshold and reaches or exceeds it while RSI is at or above its configured lower RSI-average threshold and crosses above its RSI moving average; or RSI was above the upper threshold and reaches or falls below it while RSI is at or above its configured upper RSI-average threshold and crosses below its RSI moving average. RSI average is a simple moving average of RSI.
Calculation: VWAP = Volume-weighted average price of close for the current chart session.
Long: H1 MA bias > 0 OR Ladder bias > 0 OR H1TF bias > 0 OR HTF bias > 0 OR close > VWAP OR bullish engulfing bar OR bullish long-tail bar OR RSI breakout crosses above its upper threshold OR RSI retracement crosses above its RSI moving average OR HTF bias + H1TF bias = 4.
Short: H1 MA bias < 0 OR Ladder bias < 0 OR H1TF bias < 0 OR HTF bias < 0 OR close < VWAP OR bearish engulfing bar OR bearish long-tail bar OR RSI breakout crosses below its lower threshold OR RSI retracement crosses below its RSI moving average OR HTF bias + H1TF bias = −4.
