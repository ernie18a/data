Calculation: Weekly trend average = simple moving average of weekly closes over a configurable longer lookback, using the latest completed weekly bar; compare it with its value at a configurable earlier weekly lookback. Rising means the change is positive and its absolute percentage of the current average exceeds a configurable flatness threshold; falling means the change is negative and exceeds that threshold.
Calculation: Stage = initialize as Stage 1 unless Stage 2 conditions hold; thereafter update from the previous stage: Stage 2 if previous stage was Stage 1, Stage 3, or unset and weekly close is above a rising weekly trend average, or previous stage was Stage 2 and weekly close remains above the average; Stage 4 if previous stage was Stage 3 and weekly close is below a falling average, or previous stage was Stage 4 and close remains below the average; Stage 3 if previous stage was Stage 2 and Stage 2 conditions no longer hold, if previous stage was Stage 1 and weekly close is below a falling average, or if previous stage was Stage 3 and Stage 2 conditions do not hold; otherwise Stage 1 if previous stage was Stage 4 and Stage 4 conditions no longer hold, if previous stage was Stage 1 and Stage 2 conditions do not hold, or if stage is unset and Stage 2 conditions do not hold.
Long: Stage = Stage 2
Short: Stage = Stage 4
Exit: Long, Stage = Stage 4
