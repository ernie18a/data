Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of volume multiplied by the sign of the close change
Long: OBV crosses above the highest OBV of the preceding lookback bars
Short: OBV crosses below the lowest OBV of the preceding lookback bars
Exit: short, OBV crosses above the highest OBV of the preceding lookback bars
Exit: long, OBV crosses below the lowest OBV of the preceding lookback bars
