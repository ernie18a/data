Calculation: Moving-average rating = average of 15 votes: for each SMA and EMA with lookbacks 10, 20, 30, 50, 100, and 200, VWMA(20), and HMA(9), vote +1 when close is above the average, −1 when below, and 0 when equal or undefined; Ichimoku vote +1 when close is above both lagged cloud spans and conversion line is above base line, −1 when close is below both spans and conversion line is below base line, otherwise 0. Ichimoku conversion line = midpoint of highest high and lowest low over 9 bars; base line uses 26 bars; span A = midpoint of conversion and base lines; span B uses 52 bars; use span A and B from 25 bars earlier.
Calculation: Oscillator rating = average of 11 votes: RSI(14) votes +1 when below 30 and rising, −1 when above 70 and falling; Stochastic(14), smoothed by SMA(3) for %K and again for %D, votes +1 when both are below 20 and %K > %D, −1 when both are above 80 and %K < %D; CCI(20) votes +1 when below −100 and rising, −1 when above 100 and falling; DMI(14,14) votes +1 when ADX > 20, +DI > −DI, and ADX rising, −1 when ADX > 20, −DI > +DI, and ADX rising; Awesome Oscillator = SMA(hl2,5) − SMA(hl2,34), votes +1 when positive and rising, −1 when negative and falling; Momentum(10) votes +1 when rising, −1 when falling; MACD(12,26,9) votes +1 when MACD > signal, −1 when MACD < signal; Stochastic RSI uses RSI(14), Stochastic(14) of RSI, and SMA(3) smoothing for %K and %D, with the same thresholds and crossover rules as Stochastic; Williams %R(14) votes +1 when below −80 and rising, −1 when above −20 and falling; Bull Bear Power = (high − EMA(close,13)) + (low − EMA(close,13)), votes +1 when positive and rising, −1 when negative and falling; Ultimate Oscillator uses buying pressure and true range over 7, 14, and 28 bars, weighted 4:2:1, and votes +1 above 70, −1 below 30. All other cases vote 0.
Calculation: Technical rating = (moving-average rating + oscillator rating) / 2.
Long: technical rating ≥ buy threshold AND position side is not long
Short: technical rating ≤ −buy threshold AND position side is not short
Exit: long, technical rating ≤ −buy threshold
Exit: short, technical rating ≥ buy threshold
