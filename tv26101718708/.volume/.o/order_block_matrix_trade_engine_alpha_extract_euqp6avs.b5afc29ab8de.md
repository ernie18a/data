Calculation: Bullish order block = On a confirmed bar, either (1) the bar N−1 bars ago closed below its open, each bar from N−2 through 1 bars ago closed at or above its open, and the current bar is confirmed; or (2) the bar N bars ago had a range greater than 0.8 × its ATR, closed above its open, and its low was below the low N+1 bars ago. Its zone top is that bar’s high; its bottom is its low in Full mode or the lower of its high and its low plus current ATR in Breadth mode. Its volume is that bar’s volume.
Calculation: Bearish order block = On a confirmed bar, either (1) the bar N−1 bars ago closed above its open, each bar from N−2 through 1 bars ago closed at or below its open, and the current bar is confirmed; or (2) the bar N bars ago had a range greater than 0.8 × its ATR, closed below its open, and its high was above the high N+1 bars ago. Its zone bottom is that bar’s low; its top is its high in Full mode or the higher of its low and its high minus current ATR in Breadth mode. Its volume is that bar’s volume.
Calculation: Block strength = block volume divided by the current 20-bar simple moving average of volume, or zero when that average is not positive.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = low ≤ active bullish block bottom AND close ≥ that bottom.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = high ≥ active bearish block top AND close ≤ that top.
Long: A bullish order block forms OR close is within an active bullish block’s zone OR bullish rejection occurs OR no position is open AND a bullish block’s strength meets the minimum AND (Touch mode: close is within its zone; Rejection mode: bullish rejection occurs); on an entry bar, select the eligible bullish block with greatest strength. Remove a bullish block when min(open, close) < its bottom in Close mitigation mode or low < its bottom in Wick mitigation mode. Entries are evaluated on confirmed bars.
Short: A bearish order block forms OR close is within an active bearish block’s zone OR bearish rejection occurs OR no position is open AND no long entry occurs AND a bearish block’s strength meets the minimum AND (Touch mode: close is within its zone; Rejection mode: bearish rejection occurs); on an entry bar, select the eligible bearish block with greatest strength. Remove a bearish block when max(open, close) > its top in Close mitigation mode or high > its top in Wick mitigation mode. Entries are evaluated on confirmed bars.
Exit: long, a later bar’s high ≥ take-profit price OR low ≤ stop-loss price.
Exit: short, a later bar’s low ≤ take-profit price OR high ≥ stop-loss price.
