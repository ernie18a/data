Calculation: Session IB = highest high and lowest low in each configured Tokyo, London, and New York IB window, using the last completed window; IB range = IB high − IB low
Calculation: Long OTE zone = [IB low + a × IB range, IB low + b × IB range]; Short OTE zone = [IB low + c × IB range, IB low + d × IB range], where a and b define the lower retracement band and c and d define the upper retracement band
Long: close is within the Long OTE zone of any session's last completed IB
Short: close is within the Short OTE zone of any session's last completed IB
