Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across equal configured numbers of bars before and after it; becomes confirmed when the bars after it close.
Calculation: Close correlation = rolling correlation of chart close and reference close over the configured lookback, evaluated on the signal bar.
Long: At a newly confirmed swing low, chart swing low < previous chart swing low AND at least one enabled valid reference has a higher low at the same swing than its previous swing low AND (correlation filter is off OR that reference's close correlation >= configured minimum) AND the selected confirmation is satisfied (none, RSI rising from prior bar, close > open, or both) AND the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed AND no short signal occurs on this bar.
Short: At a newly confirmed swing high, chart swing high > previous chart swing high AND at least one enabled valid reference has a lower high at the same swing than its previous swing high AND (correlation filter is off OR that reference's close correlation >= configured minimum) AND the selected confirmation is satisfied (none, RSI falling from prior bar, close < open, or both) AND the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed AND no long signal occurs on this bar.
