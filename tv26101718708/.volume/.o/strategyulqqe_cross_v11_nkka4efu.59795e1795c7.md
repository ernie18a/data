Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow moving averages = configurable moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI(close, RSI lookback), using a smoothing lookback
Calculation: Fast QQE trailing line = RSI-based trailing band formed from the smoothed RSI and a smoothed, QQE-factor-scaled EMA of its absolute change; use the active band selected by whether the smoothed RSI crossed the prior band
Calculation: Medium-MA direction = rising if the medium moving average has risen for three bars, falling if it has fallen for three bars, otherwise unchanged
Long: First bar where smoothed RSI > 50 + threshold AND (MA filter disabled OR (close > fast MA AND fast MA > medium MA AND medium MA > slow MA)) AND (direction filter disabled OR (medium-MA direction is rising AND close > slow MA)); OR smoothed RSI crosses above 50 AND QQE zero-cross display is enabled AND buy signal is not on its first qualifying bar; OR fast QQE trailing line crosses above smoothed RSI AND QQE cross display is enabled AND buy signal is not on its first qualifying bar
Short: First bar where smoothed RSI < 50 - threshold AND (MA filter disabled OR (close < fast MA AND fast MA < medium MA AND medium MA < slow MA)) AND (direction filter disabled OR (medium-MA direction is falling AND close < slow MA)); OR smoothed RSI crosses below 50 AND QQE zero-cross display is enabled AND sell signal is not on its first qualifying bar; OR fast QQE trailing line crosses below smoothed RSI AND QQE cross display is enabled AND sell signal is not on its first qualifying bar
Exit: Long, medium-MA direction changes from its prior bar OR price reaches the configured take-profit, stop-loss, or trailing-stop threshold
Exit: Short, medium-MA direction changes from its prior bar OR price reaches the configured take-profit, stop-loss, or trailing-stop threshold
