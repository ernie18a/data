Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback, using the current bar
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot
Calculation: Retest quality score = sum of six rounded 0–100 point components: reclaim (25 × clamp(correct-side close distance ÷ (0.60 × ATR at break), 0, 1)); rejection (20 × clamp((retest-side wick ÷ bar range + 0.20 if candle closes in the break direction + 0.20 × body ÷ bar range) ÷ 1.20, 0, 1)); depth (20 × clamp(1 − overshoot beyond broken level ÷ (0.80 × ATR at break), 0, 1)); speed (15 × clamp(1 − (bars since break − 1) ÷ (maximum retest bars − 1), 0, 1)); volatility (10 × clamp(1 − max(bar range − ATR at break, 0) ÷ (2 × ATR at break), 0, 1)); volume (10 if disabled, otherwise 10 × clamp((volume ÷ 20-bar volume SMA) ÷ 1.50, 0, 1)). Use a minimum denominator of one tick for ATR and bar range; round each component to the nearest integer before summing.
