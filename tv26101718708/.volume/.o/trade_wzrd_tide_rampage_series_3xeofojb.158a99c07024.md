Calculation: Ownership map = over a rolling lookback, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into equal price rows; split each bar’s volume between buyers and sellers in proportion to its close’s position within its high-low range, then allocate each part to touched rows in proportion to overlap. A buyer shelf has buyer share at least the configured dominance threshold; a seller shelf has seller share at least that threshold. Use the highest buyer shelf wholly below close for longs and the lowest seller shelf wholly above close for shorts.
Calculation: Volume center = midpoint of the first row where cumulative total row volume reaches at least half of total volume; center position = its percentage location within the range.
Calculation: Kinetic fuel = volume × absolute change in close, normalized between its rolling 100-bar minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Absorption = current volume / 30-bar average volume ≥ 2 AND current high-low range / 30-bar average range ≤ 0.7.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = at least two confirmed pivot highs or lows within the configured ATR-based tolerance, represented by their average price; pivots use configured left and right bar counts.
Calculation: Long stop = bottom of the selected buyer shelf minus configured ATR buffer; if this is at or above entry close, use entry close minus ATR. Short stop = top of the selected seller shelf plus configured ATR buffer; if this is at or below entry close, use entry close plus ATR. Freeze stop at entry.
Calculation: Target = volume center if it is in the trade direction by more than 0.3 ATR and no farther than 3 times the entry-to-stop risk; otherwise use entry plus/minus configured risk multiple. Freeze target at entry.
Long: Buyer shelf exists AND low ≤ its top edge AND close > its top edge AND this shelf has not already fired AND (volume center position < 50% OR balance filter is disabled) AND (kinetic fuel ≥ configured minimum OR minimum is zero) AND (that long dominance tier has fewer than the configured minimum samples OR its prior long win rate ≥ configured minimum win probability) AND global cooldown has elapsed AND ATR is available AND bar is confirmed AND no short signal fires on this bar.
Long: A confirmed pivot low sweeps a resting liquidity pool when low < pool price AND close > pool price.
Long: Absorption AND close location within the bar’s range ≥ 50%.
Short: Seller shelf exists AND high ≥ its bottom edge AND close < its bottom edge AND this shelf has not already fired AND (volume center position > 50% OR balance filter is disabled) AND (kinetic fuel ≥ configured minimum OR minimum is zero) AND (that short dominance tier has fewer than the configured minimum samples OR its prior short win rate ≥ configured minimum win probability) AND global cooldown has elapsed AND ATR is available AND bar is confirmed AND no long signal fires on this bar.
Short: A confirmed pivot high sweeps a resting liquidity pool when high > pool price AND close < pool price.
Short: Absorption AND close location within the bar’s range < 50%.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ frozen stop OR high ≥ frozen target; if both occur on one bar, stop takes priority.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ frozen stop OR low ≤ frozen target; if both occur on one bar, stop takes priority.
