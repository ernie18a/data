Calculation: Simple moving averages = arithmetic means of close over configurable lookbacks; use short-, medium-, and long-lookback averages, with short < medium < long.
Calculation: Relative-strength score = 0.4 × (daily close / daily close 11 bars earlier) + 0.2 × (daily close / daily close 21 bars earlier) + 0.2 × (daily close / daily close 42 bars earlier) + 0.2 × (daily close / daily close 63 bars earlier); use the current, still-forming daily bar.
Long: In ordinary mode, RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the last 10 bars AND the short-lookback simple moving average crosses above VWAP; in flip mode, RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the last 10 bars AND the short-lookback simple moving average crosses below VWAP; AND the position is flat unless entries while positioned are enabled; AND no qualifiers are enabled or every enabled qualifier passes: close > medium- and long-lookback simple moving averages; medium-lookback simple moving average > long-lookback simple moving average; long-lookback simple moving average is rising over a configurable lookback; short-lookback simple moving average > medium- and long-lookback simple moving averages; close > short-lookback simple moving average; close is at least 25% above the lowest low over a configurable lookback; close is within 25% of the highest high over a configurable lookback; all Trend-Template conditions above plus relative-strength score ≥ its threshold; or relative-strength score ≥ its threshold.
Short: In ordinary mode, RSI was at or above its overbought threshold within the last 10 bars AND the short-lookback simple moving average crosses below VWAP; in flip mode, RSI was at or below its oversold threshold within the last 10 bars AND the short-lookback simple moving average crosses above VWAP; AND short entries are enabled.
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) when take profit is enabled OR low ≤ the trailing stop (highest high since entry × (1 − trailing percentage)) when trailing stop is enabled OR, with trailing stop disabled, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) when stop loss is enabled.
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) when take profit is enabled OR high ≥ the trailing stop (lowest low since entry × (1 + trailing percentage)) when trailing stop is enabled OR, with trailing stop disabled, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) when stop loss is enabled.
