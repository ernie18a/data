Calculation: Upper drift = average of the latest N confirmed pivot highs; a pivot high is confirmed after the required bars on both sides have lower highs.
Calculation: Lower drift = average of the latest N confirmed pivot lows; a pivot low is confirmed after the required bars on both sides have higher lows.
Long: Close crosses above upper drift AND upper and lower drift are defined
Short: Close crosses below lower drift AND upper and lower drift are defined
Exit: long, close crosses below lower drift
Exit: short, close crosses above upper drift
