Calculation: Active level = configured nonzero level nearest to the current close
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Arrival strength = round(clamp(0.40 × min(|close − close[n]| / ATR × 25, 100) + 0.30 × min(SMA(high − low, n) / ATR × 100, 100) + 0.30 × min((volume / SMA(volume, m)) × 50, 100), 1, 100))
Calculation: Level quality = weak when the number of closes within the touch zone over the level-test lookback exceeds the maximum; otherwise fresh for at most one touch and used for up to the maximum
Calculation: Order-flow delta percent = 100 × ((volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick)) − (volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick))) / volume
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line minus its signal line
Long: close < close[n] OR bullish MACD divergence (price pivot low < previous price pivot low AND MACD-histogram pivot low > previous MACD-histogram pivot low AND price is within the configured pre-alert ATR distance of the active level) OR active level exists AND close < close[n] AND low < active level AND close > active level AND order-flow delta percent < negative pressure threshold OR active level exists AND low < active level − ATR × sweep distance AND close > active level OR previous bar crossed below active level and current close > active level, with close < close[n], OR reaction is rejection AND arrival strength ≤ entry-strength maximum AND compression filter passes AND EMA-extension filter passes AND level quality is not weak AND close ≥ close[n]; entry signals also require the level to be eligible for another trade and not invalidated, and are blocked against Supertrend only when that filter is enabled
Short: close > close[n] OR bearish MACD divergence (price pivot high > previous price pivot high AND MACD-histogram pivot high < previous MACD-histogram pivot high AND price is within the configured pre-alert ATR distance of the active level) OR active level exists AND close > close[n] AND high > active level AND close < active level AND order-flow delta percent > pressure threshold OR active level exists AND high > active level + ATR × sweep distance AND close < active level OR previous bar crossed above active level and current close < active level, with close > close[n], OR reaction is rejection AND arrival strength ≤ entry-strength maximum AND compression filter passes AND EMA-extension filter passes AND level quality is not weak AND close > close[n]; entry signals also require the level to be eligible for another trade and not invalidated, and are blocked against Supertrend only when that filter is enabled
Exit: short, close ≤ EMA(close, exit lookback)
Exit: long, close ≥ EMA(close, exit lookback)
