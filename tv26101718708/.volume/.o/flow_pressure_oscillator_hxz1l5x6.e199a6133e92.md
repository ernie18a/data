Calculation: Bullish regime = quality passes its adaptive minimum, and the bullish score (quality, bullish structure, positive trend pressure, positive momentum, positive oscillator) reaches its activation threshold and exceeds the bearish score by the required margin; it remains active until price falls below the slow EMA, trend pressure becomes sufficiently negative, the bearish EMA stack forms, or its score falls below its deactivation threshold
Calculation: Bearish regime = the mirror of the bullish regime; it remains active until price rises above the slow EMA, trend pressure becomes sufficiently positive, the bullish EMA stack forms, or its score falls below its deactivation threshold
Calculation: Quality = weighted combination of normalized ADX, directional efficiency, ATR relative to its long-term EMA, and volume z-score; compare it with an adaptive minimum based on chop load and trend strength
Calculation: Bullish structure = short-, medium-, and long-term source EMAs are stacked upward and close is above the long-term close EMA; bearish structure is the reverse
Calculation: Oscillator = bounded compression of a smoothed, quality-scaled combination of ATR-normalized EMA spreads and price displacement, normalized rate-of-change, trend pressure, structure, volatility, and volume; trend pressure is the smoothed ATR-normalized distance from the long-term close EMA combined with its normalized slope
Calculation: Momentum histogram = smoothed combination of oscillator minus its signal EMA, oscillator velocity and acceleration, compressed engine pressure, and trend bias; histogram smoothing length varies with quality
Calculation: Bullish continuation = bullish regime AND quality passes its adaptive minimum AND oscillator is above zero and its signal EMA AND signal is above its lower bound AND bullish momentum condition; bullish momentum condition is a rising histogram above its positive-strength threshold OR a nonnegative histogram with nonnegative slope OR a histogram above its lower tolerance with rising slope and positive velocity
Calculation: Bearish continuation = bearish regime AND quality passes its adaptive minimum AND oscillator is below zero and its signal EMA AND signal is below its upper bound AND bearish momentum condition; bearish momentum condition is a falling histogram below its negative-strength threshold OR a nonpositive histogram with nonpositive slope OR a histogram below its upper tolerance with falling slope and negative velocity
Calculation: Bullish reload = bullish regime AND quality passes its adaptive minimum AND oscillator is below its positive reload bound and below its signal EMA AND histogram is above its lower tolerance and rising AND velocity is increasing; bearish reload is the mirror condition
Calculation: Bullish expansion = bullish continuation AND oscillator is above its inner adaptive band AND velocity is positive AND histogram is strongly bullish; bearish expansion is the mirror condition
Calculation: Extreme pressure = a bullish or bearish expansion, or exhaustion; exhaustion is a reversal condition in an active regime or neutral regime based on oscillator extremes and weakening or improving velocity and histogram
Long: bullish regime OR (quality passes its adaptive minimum AND flow trail is positive AND not in extreme pressure) OR bullish continuation becomes true after being false OR bullish reload becomes true after being false OR bullish expansion becomes true after being false
Short: bearish regime OR (quality passes its adaptive minimum AND flow trail is negative AND not in extreme pressure) OR bearish continuation becomes true after being false OR bearish reload becomes true after being false OR bearish expansion becomes true after being false
