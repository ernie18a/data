Calculation: Tenkan = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shortest Ichimoku lookback; Kijun uses a longer lookback; Span B uses the longest lookback. Span A = midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun. Current cloud top/bottom = max/min of Span A and Span B from the displacement bars earlier.
Calculation: Market structure = bullish after a close breaks the latest confirmed swing high and bearish after a close breaks the latest confirmed swing low; swing pivots require the same lookback on both sides and are confirmed after the bars to their right complete.
Long: Selected price source > current cloud top AND previous selected price source <= previous cloud top OR Span A crosses above Span B OR close > latest confirmed swing high AND market structure is bearish
Short: Selected price source < current cloud bottom AND previous selected price source >= previous cloud bottom OR Span A crosses below Span B OR close < latest confirmed swing low AND market structure is bullish
