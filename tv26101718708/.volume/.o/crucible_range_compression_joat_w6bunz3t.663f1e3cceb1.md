Calculation: Compression intensity = 0.4 × (1 − BB-width percentile / 100) + 0.4 × (1 − ATR(14) percentile / 100) + 0.2 × clamp(1 − (body Z-score + 2) / 4, 0, 1). Use the same lookback for both percentiles; BB width is (upper band − lower band) / basis, and body Z-score is (|close − open| − its 20-bar SMA) / its 20-bar standard deviation, or 0 when that deviation is 0.
Calculation: Compression chamber = at the first bar with intensity above the threshold, seed its high and low from the highest high and lowest low over the chamber initial-range lookback; on each following active bar, extend those bounds to include that bar’s high and low. Compression ends when intensity is no longer above the threshold; use the bounds and bias from the last active bar.
Calculation: Chamber CVD bias = sign of cumulative signed volume since the compression-start bar, divided by the number of active bars. Signed volume is +volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, and 0 otherwise. The start bar sets the cumulative-volume anchor and bias to zero; update bias on subsequent active bars.
Long: Previous bar was in compression (intensity > threshold), current bar ends compression (intensity <= threshold), close > chamber high, and chamber CVD bias > 0.
Short: Previous bar was in compression (intensity > threshold), current bar ends compression (intensity <= threshold), close < chamber low, and chamber CVD bias < 0.
