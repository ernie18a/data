Calculation: Volume ratio = short-lookback SMA(volume) divided by longer-lookback SMA(volume); elevated volume means the ratio is at least the normal-regime threshold.
Calculation: Projected trend line = EMA of the rolling lowest low plus smoothed RSI(close) times the rolling high-low range divided by 100; smoothed RSI is an SMA of RSI(close).
Calculation: Trend tolerance = a multiplier times the 100-bar SMA of absolute close-open body size. On each completed bar, trend state becomes bullish when close is above the projected trend line plus tolerance, bearish when close is below the projected trend line minus tolerance, and otherwise retains its prior state (initially bearish).
Calculation: Regime box = after sufficient history and cooldown, start a box when elevated volume begins; set its boundaries to that bar's high and low and expand them to include each subsequent elevated-volume bar up to the build limit. When volume is no longer elevated, freeze and arm the box. Cancel it if the build limit is reached while volume remains elevated; expire it after the armed limit. A breakout resolves the box and starts cooldown.
Long: completed bar AND sufficient history AND ((armed regime box AND close > frozen box high AND previous close <= frozen box high) OR (trend state changes from bearish to bullish)).
Short: completed bar AND sufficient history AND ((armed regime box AND close < frozen box low AND previous close >= frozen box low) OR (trend state changes from bullish to bearish)).
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR times a multiplier, or 1% of entry price times a multiplier when percentage-based risk is selected. Stop = entry price minus position direction times risk distance; third target = entry price plus position direction times risk distance times the third risk-reward parameter.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= third target OR bars since entry exceed the trade-line life limit.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= third target OR bars since entry exceed the trade-line life limit.
