Calculation: Range bounds = highest and lowest body prices (or wicks, if configured) over the range lookback; a range is eligible when its width is within the configured ATR limits. Lock eligible bounds as an active range. After a breakout, suppress signals until price closes back past the invalidation level, which re-arms the same range, or the range expires and a new eligible range is found.
Calculation: Range age = number of bars since the active range began.
Calculation: Invalidation level = range top − configured fraction × (range top − range bottom).
Long: Active range AND close above range top (or entire candle body above it, if configured) AND all enabled filters pass: range age meets minimum; absolute candle body size meets its ATR threshold; volume meets its average-volume threshold; close is above the configured EMA or Ichimoku cloud; range width meets its minimum ATR threshold; and buy confirmation is nonzero within the configured lookback.
Short: Active range AND close below range bottom (or entire candle body below it, if configured) AND all enabled filters pass: range age meets minimum; absolute candle body size meets its ATR threshold; volume meets its average-volume threshold; close is below the configured EMA or Ichimoku cloud; range width meets its minimum ATR threshold; and sell confirmation is nonzero within the configured lookback.
