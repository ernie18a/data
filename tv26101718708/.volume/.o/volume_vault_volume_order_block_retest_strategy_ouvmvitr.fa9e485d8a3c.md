Calculation: Bullish order block zone = the previous candle’s high-to-low range, or its body range if body-only zones are selected; create it on a bullish displacement candle.
Calculation: Bearish order block zone = the previous candle’s high-to-low range, or its body range if body-only zones are selected; create it on a bearish displacement candle.
Calculation: Displacement = current candle body size ≥ previous bar’s ATR × displacement multiplier AND current volume ≥ previous bar’s volume SMA × volume multiplier; optionally require the previous candle to have the opposite color and the displacement close to break the previous candle’s range.
Calculation: Active zone = a created zone that has not closed through its far edge, exceeded its maximum age, or been removed by the active-zone limit; ignore overlapping zones in the same direction.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the current bar’s ATR for stop buffers and the previous bar’s ATR for displacement.
Calculation: Volume SMA = simple moving average of volume; use the previous bar’s value for displacement.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close.
Long: bullish displacement candle AND long entries allowed OR active bullish zone touched (high ≥ zone bottom AND low ≤ zone top) AND close > zone bottom AND (if enabled, close > open) AND (if enabled, lower wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick percentage) AND (if enabled, volume ≤ zone impulse volume × retest-volume multiplier) AND (if enabled, close > EMA) AND (if first-touch is enabled, zone has not been used) AND flat.
Short: bearish displacement candle AND short entries allowed OR active bearish zone touched (high ≥ zone bottom AND low ≤ zone top) AND close < zone top AND (if enabled, close < open) AND (if enabled, upper wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick percentage) AND (if enabled, volume ≤ zone impulse volume × retest-volume multiplier) AND (if enabled, close < EMA) AND (if first-touch is enabled, zone has not been used) AND flat.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop (initially zone bottom − current ATR × buffer; move to at least entry price after high ≥ entry price + initial risk × break-even trigger) OR high ≥ target (signal close + initial risk × target multiple) OR elapsed bars since entry ≥ time-stop limit.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop (initially zone top + current ATR × buffer; move to at most entry price after low ≤ entry price − initial risk × break-even trigger) OR low ≤ target (signal close − initial risk × target multiple) OR elapsed bars since entry ≥ time-stop limit.
