Calculation: Qualifying gap = bullish: open > prior high, or low > prior high, or current candle body low > prior candle body high, or (in adaptive mode with displacement fallback enabled) close > prior high and absolute(close - open) >= ATR × displacement threshold; bearish: open < prior low, or high < prior low, or current candle body high < prior candle body low, or (in adaptive mode with displacement fallback enabled) close < prior low and absolute(close - open) >= ATR × displacement threshold. Apply the selected gap mode to determine which gap types qualify, and require gap height >= ATR × minimum gap size.
Calculation: Active gap = latest qualifying gap accepted after the gap cooldown; bullish gap top/bottom are the higher/lower candidate boundaries and its reclaim edge is the top; bearish gap top/bottom are the higher/lower candidate boundaries and its reclaim edge is the bottom. It is active until resolved, expired, or cleared for distance.
Calculation: Entered gap = high >= active gap bottom AND low <= active gap top.
Calculation: Reclaim score = clamp(time score + entry score + close score + volume score + size score + room score, 0, 100); time score = clamp(18 × (1 − bars since gap / reclaim window), 0, 18); entry score = 16 if entered gap, otherwise 0; close score = 26 if close reclaims the edge, otherwise 10 if entered gap, otherwise 0; volume score = clamp((volume / its moving average) / 1.6, 0, 1) × 14 when relative volume scoring is enabled, otherwise 10; size score = clamp(gap height / (ATR × 0.75), 0, 1) × 12; room score = clamp(room to first target in ATR units / 1.2, 0, 1) × 14, where first target is the reclaim edge plus (bullish) or minus (bearish) gap height × target multiplier.
Calculation: Fill risk = bullish: clamp((gap top − low) / gap height × 100, 0, 100); bearish: clamp((high − gap bottom) / gap height × 100, 0, 100).
Long: active gap is bullish AND not resolved or reclaimed AND entered gap AND close >= reclaim edge AND reclaim score >= required score AND fill risk < fill risk threshold.
Short: active gap is bearish AND not resolved or reclaimed AND entered gap AND close <= reclaim edge AND reclaim score >= required score AND fill risk < fill risk threshold.
