Calculation: Smoothed RSI = exponentially smoothed RSI of close
Long: Volume spike (current volume > long-term volume SMA × buy threshold AND smoothed RSI < buy level AND either previous-bar or two-bars-ago volume > current short-term volume SMA) OR divergence (smoothed RSI < buy level AND close > the lowest close over the lookback AND close < that lowest close × buy proximity multiplier AND smoothed RSI > the lowest smoothed RSI over the lookback AND smoothed RSI > previous-bar smoothed RSI); when both components are enabled, either component must occur within the confirmation window of the other; when only one is enabled, that component alone qualifies; component and overall signal-spacing requirements are met
Short: Volume spike (current volume > long-term volume SMA × sell threshold AND smoothed RSI > sell level) OR divergence (smoothed RSI > sell level AND close < the highest close over the lookback AND close > that highest close × sell proximity multiplier AND smoothed RSI < the highest smoothed RSI over the lookback); when both components are enabled, either component must occur within the confirmation window of the other; when only one is enabled, that component alone qualifies; component and overall signal-spacing requirements are met
