Calculation: Long score = 2 × (RSI ≤ oversold threshold OR RSI crosses above oversold threshold OR (RSI < 42 AND RSI > prior-bar RSI)) + 2 × (lower-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close > low + 0.50 × (high − low)) + 2 × (low < lowest low of prior swing-lookback bars AND close > that lowest low) + (low ≤ lower Bollinger Band OR close ≤ lower Bollinger Band + 0.25 × ATR) + (close > open AND prior close < prior open AND close > prior open AND open ≤ prior close) + (close > prior close) + (high − low > ATR × minimum range multiple)
Calculation: Short score = 2 × (RSI ≥ overbought threshold OR RSI crosses below overbought threshold OR (RSI > 58 AND RSI < prior-bar RSI)) + 2 × (upper-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close < high − 0.50 × (high − low)) + 2 × (high > highest high of prior swing-lookback bars AND close < that highest high) + (high ≥ upper Bollinger Band OR close ≥ upper Bollinger Band − 0.25 × ATR) + (close < open AND prior close > prior open AND close < prior open AND open ≥ prior close) + (close < prior close) + (high − low > ATR × minimum range multiple)
Calculation: Lower/upper Bollinger Bands = SMA(close, Bollinger lookback) −/+ Bollinger multiplier × standard deviation(close, Bollinger lookback)
Calculation: Lower/upper-wick share = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) and (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low), respectively; both are 0 when high = low
Calculation: Long TP3/stop = entry price +/− ATR at entry × TP3/stop ATR multiple
Calculation: Short TP3/stop = entry price −/+ ATR at entry × TP3/stop ATR multiple
Long: bar is confirmed AND long score ≥ score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 20-bar SMA(volume) × volume multiple) AND (soft EMA context enabled OR close > EMA) AND no long signal within cooldown bars AND (alternation disabled OR previous signal was not long)
Short: bar is confirmed AND short score ≥ score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 20-bar SMA(volume) × volume multiple) AND (soft EMA context enabled OR close < EMA) AND no short signal within cooldown bars AND (alternation disabled OR previous signal was not short)
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long TP3
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short TP3
