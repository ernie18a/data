Calculation: Trend-template score = sum of eight binary conditions: close above medium- and long-term moving averages; medium-term average above long-term average; long-term average rising versus an earlier value; short-term average above both; close above short-term average; price sufficiently above its rolling low; close near its rolling high; weighted price-momentum composite above its threshold. Use progressively longer momentum lookbacks, with greater weight on the shortest.
Calculation: Relative performance = (symbol return over a lookback / benchmark return over the same lookback) − 1.
Calculation: Relative-strength ratio = symbol close / benchmark close.
Calculation: Extension from short-term average = (close − short-term average) / short-term average, normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Range contraction = rolling high-low range normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Volume dry-up ratio = short-lookback average volume / longer-lookback average volume.

Long: Close > long-term SMA AND relative performance is positive over the short lookback AND is nonpositive over the intermediate lookback
