Calculation: Valid formation = From confirmed fast, base, and macro swing pivots, merge nearby same-side pivots; fit upper and lower lines to pivot pairs at least a minimum anchor gap apart. Keep converging candidates within the minimum/maximum duration and apex-progress limits, with minimum starting height, contraction, touches on both lines, containment, and structural anchoring. Require a clustered horizontal pivot zone for the flat side of an ascending or descending triangle. Require both boundaries to slope in the same direction, converge, and meet the slope-gap and slope-ratio limits for a rising or falling wedge. Reject candidates below the minimum geometry score; select the best candidate, preferring a sufficiently longer candidate within the score slack. If a valid triangle exists, use it; otherwise use a valid wedge. A candidate is invalid when its formation key changes or its structure ceases to qualify.
Calculation: Breakout levels = Current upper/lower formation boundaries plus/minus the greater of the percentage buffer, ATR buffer, and minimum tick buffer. For a symmetrical triangle, also require the close to clear the most recent internal swing high/low plus/minus that buffer. Breakouts require minimum apex progress.
Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on the chart timeframe.
Long: Valid ascending triangle and low <= lower boundary + ATR * near-boundary distance and close >= lower boundary, with a long entry at lower boundary + ATR * entry buffer and a stop at lower boundary - ATR * stop buffer whose distance is within both maximum risk limits; OR valid rising wedge or valid triangle and close > its upper breakout level (for a symmetrical triangle, also above its internal swing confirmation level) after minimum apex progress; OR a confirmed upward breakout has retested its breakout level within the retest window, has not failed, and the long entry at breakout level + ATR * entry buffer with stop at breakout level - ATR * stop buffer is within both maximum risk limits.
Short: Valid descending triangle and high >= upper boundary - ATR * near-boundary distance and close <= upper boundary, with a short entry at upper boundary - ATR * entry buffer and a stop at upper boundary + ATR * stop buffer whose distance is within both maximum risk limits; OR valid falling wedge or valid triangle and close < its lower breakout level (for a symmetrical triangle, also below its internal swing confirmation level) after minimum apex progress; OR a confirmed downward breakout has retested its breakout level within the retest window, has not failed, and the short entry at breakout level - ATR * entry buffer with stop at breakout level + ATR * stop buffer is within both maximum risk limits.
