Calculation: Ease of Movement value = If current high equals current low, 0; otherwise 1,000,000 × (change in half-range from the previous bar minus that change on the previous bar) ÷ (current volume ÷ current range), where range = high − low and half-range = range ÷ 2.
Long: (reverse is off AND (Ease of Movement value > upper threshold OR (Ease of Movement value ≥ lower threshold AND position side is long))) OR (reverse is on AND (Ease of Movement value < lower threshold OR (Ease of Movement value ≤ upper threshold AND position side is long)))
Short: (reverse is off AND (Ease of Movement value < lower threshold OR (Ease of Movement value ≤ upper threshold AND position side is short))) OR (reverse is on AND (Ease of Movement value > upper threshold OR (Ease of Movement value ≥ lower threshold AND position side is short)))
