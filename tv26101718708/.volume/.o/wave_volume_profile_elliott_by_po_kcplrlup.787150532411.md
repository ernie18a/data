Calculation: effective minimum A drop = configured minimum, or, when adaptive thresholds are enabled and at least 10 prior high-to-following-low drops below 80% exist, the median of the most recent 50 such drops multiplied by 0.75 and clamped to the configured bounds
Calculation: volume profile POC = divide the price range from the configured profile start through its end into configured equal rows; allocate each bar’s volume to rows in proportion to the overlap of the bar’s high-low range with each row (put zero-range bar volume in its price row); use the midpoint of the row with the greatest allocated volume
