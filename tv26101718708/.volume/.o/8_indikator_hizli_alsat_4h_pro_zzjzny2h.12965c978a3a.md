Calculation: Custom SuperTrend = ATR-based bands using SMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × ATR; carry the lower band forward as the maximum while prior close is above it, and the upper band forward as the minimum while prior close is below it; direction turns up when close exceeds the prior upper band and down when close falls below the prior lower band, otherwise direction carries forward.
Calculation: Fast bullish count = number of true conditions among CCI below its oversold threshold, Williams %R below its oversold threshold, and close above Custom SuperTrend with upward direction.
Calculation: Fast bearish count = number of true conditions among CCI above its overbought threshold, Williams %R above its overbought threshold, and close below Custom SuperTrend with downward direction.
Calculation: Full bullish count = number of true conditions among RSI below oversold, MACD line crossing above signal line, close below the lower Bollinger Band, Stochastic %K below oversold and crossing above %D, faster SMA crossing above slower SMA, CCI below oversold, Williams %R below oversold, and close above Custom SuperTrend with upward direction.
Calculation: Full bearish count = number of true conditions among RSI above overbought, MACD line crossing below signal line, close above the upper Bollinger Band, Stochastic %K above overbought and crossing below %D, faster SMA crossing below slower SMA, CCI above overbought, Williams %R above overbought, and close below Custom SuperTrend with downward direction.
Long: (Fast bullish count meets its minimum OR Full bullish count meets its minimum) AND volume > its 20-bar SMA AND daily SuperTrend direction is upward; daily SuperTrend uses the still-forming daily bar.
Short: (Fast bearish count meets its minimum OR Full bearish count meets its minimum) AND volume > its 20-bar SMA AND daily SuperTrend direction is downward; daily SuperTrend uses the still-forming daily bar.
