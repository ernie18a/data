Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest two confirmed swing highs and the latest two confirmed swing lows on aggregated structure-timeframe bars are each rising.
Calculation: Bearish structure = the latest two confirmed swing highs and the latest two confirmed swing lows on aggregated structure-timeframe bars are each falling.
Calculation: Golden retracement zone = the configured Fibonacci retracement band of the latest swing, measured from the latest swing high and low according to which pivot came later; in ATR mode, use a band centered at the midpoint of the configured Fibonacci levels with ATR-based width. Use confirmed pivots from aggregated structure-timeframe bars; use the current chart bar’s ATR.
Calculation: Bullish confirmation candle = a bullish engulfing candle OR a bullish pin bar whose close is in the configured upper fraction of its range. Bearish confirmation candle = a bearish engulfing candle OR a bearish pin bar whose close is in the configured lower fraction of its range.
Long: close is inside the golden retracement zone AND bullish structure AND close >= latest swing low AND (bullish confirmation candle OR confirmation-candle filter disabled) AND (fast EMA > slow EMA on the current higher-timeframe bar OR higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled) AND (positive cumulative candle-volume delta over the configured lookback AND volume exceeds its configured multiple of average volume OR volume-alignment filter disabled)
Short: close is inside the golden retracement zone AND bearish structure AND close <= latest swing high AND (bearish confirmation candle OR confirmation-candle filter disabled) AND (fast EMA < slow EMA on the current higher-timeframe bar OR higher-timeframe alignment filter disabled) AND (negative cumulative candle-volume delta over the configured lookback AND volume exceeds its configured multiple of average volume OR volume-alignment filter disabled)
