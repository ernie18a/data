Calculation: Pivot-anchored levels = For each enabled lookback, confirmed pivot highs and lows on the chart timeframe and configured higher timeframes anchor volume-weighted average prices; chart-timeframe anchors also produce volume-profile POC and 70%-volume-area edges. Higher-timeframe calculations use the latest value returned without lookahead (forming bar in real time; completed bar historically).
Calculation: Confluence zone = Sort eligible pivot-anchored levels; group consecutive levels whose gaps are within a price-scaled tolerance, retain the group with the largest contributor count when it meets the minimum, and merge it into a nearby active zone or create a new one. A zone’s center is the midpoint of its edges.
Calculation: Active-zone directional bias = Pivot-low AVWAP count minus pivot-high AVWAP count, plus 1 when the zone center is below close or minus 1 when above close, if its distance from close is at most 3 ATR; bias is positive at 2 or more and negative at -2 or less.
Long: An active zone’s directional bias is positive and its star rating meets the configured marker threshold, OR a pending zone’s close after the configured outcome wait is more than the configured percentage above its center, OR a resolved resistance zone has the configured number of consecutive closes above its upper edge by the configured percentage.
Short: An active zone’s directional bias is negative and its star rating meets the configured marker threshold, OR a pending zone’s close after the configured outcome wait is more than the configured percentage below its center, OR a resolved support zone has the configured number of consecutive closes below its lower edge by the configured percentage.
