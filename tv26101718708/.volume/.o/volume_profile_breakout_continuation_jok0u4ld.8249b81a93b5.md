Calculation: Setup volume profile = divide the detected accumulation range into rows; spread each accumulation bar’s volume evenly across the rows its high–low spans; POC is the highest-volume row; expand the value area outward from the POC until it contains the configured volume percentage; VAL and VAH are its lower and upper bounds.
Calculation: Take-profit and stop-loss distances = configured percentages of entry price or configured multiples of chart-timeframe ATR at entry, according to the selected mode.
Long: On a confirmed bar, after a detected accumulation has lasted at least the configured minimum, close > accumulation high AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR the last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND a later bar within the configured wait closes >= VAL, has low <= POC + configured tolerance (a percentage of accumulation range height), and a subsequent confirmed bar closes > POC AND close > open AND the position is flat AND entry cooldowns have elapsed AND the bar is before the configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
Short: On a confirmed bar, after a detected accumulation has lasted at least the configured minimum, close < accumulation low AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR the last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND a later bar within the configured wait closes <= VAH, has high >= POC - configured tolerance (a percentage of accumulation range height), and a subsequent confirmed bar closes < POC AND close < open AND the position is flat AND entry cooldowns have elapsed AND the bar is before the configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop-loss price OR high >= take-profit price OR confirmed bars since entry >= configured maximum OR confirmed bar time >= configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop-loss price OR low <= take-profit price OR confirmed bars since entry >= configured maximum OR confirmed bar time >= configured New York end-of-day flatten time.
