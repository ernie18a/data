Calculation: ATR regime = ATR divided by its longer-term average; normalize it over a still longer lookback.
Calculation: Fast ATR regime = ATR with a shorter length divided by its shorter-term average; normalize it over that average's lookback.
Calculation: Bollinger bandwidth = (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) / absolute Bollinger basis; normalize it over a long lookback. Fast bandwidth score uses a shorter normalization lookback.
Calculation: Historical volatility = standard deviation of log close-to-close returns; normalize it over a long lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average.
Calculation: ATR rising = ATR regime > its prior-bar value; ATR accelerating = its current increase > its prior increase; ATR turning up = current increase > 0 and prior increase ≤ 0. Bandwidth rising, accelerating, and turning up are defined the same way using Bollinger bandwidth.
Calculation: Bull trend = close > fast EMA > slow EMA; bear trend = close < fast EMA < slow EMA. The slow EMA length exceeds the fast EMA length.
Calculation: Bull direction = bull trend AND positive rate of change; bear direction = bear trend AND negative rate of change. Bull momentum acceleration = rate of change > its prior value AND its increase > its prior increase; bear momentum acceleration = rate of change < its prior value AND its decrease > its prior decrease.
Calculation: Bull impulse = close > open AND close > fast EMA AND absolute candle body / ATR exceeds an impulse threshold; bear impulse = close < open AND close < fast EMA AND the same body-size condition. Strong impulses additionally close above the prior bar's high (bull) or below its low (bear).
Calculation: Bull micro-breakout = confirmed bar close above the highest high of the preceding short lookback AND above fast EMA AND fast EMA rising. Bear micro-breakout = confirmed bar close below the lowest low of that lookback AND below fast EMA AND fast EMA falling. A breakout is recent if it occurred within a short memory window.
Calculation: Confirmed volatility score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized ATR, normalized Bollinger bandwidth, normalized historical volatility, relative-volume score, and an acceleration score. The acceleration score is highest when ATR and bandwidth are both accelerating, intermediate when both are rising, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Fast volatility score = smoothed weighted combination of fast normalized ATR, fast normalized bandwidth, impulse score, momentum-acceleration score, breakout score, and relative-volume score. ATR and bandwidth component scores are raised when they turn up or accelerate; breakout score is highest on a current micro-breakout and intermediate while a breakout remains recent.
Calculation: Bull and bear setup scores = sums of points for, respectively: close on the corresponding side of fast EMA; fast EMA on the corresponding side of slow EMA; fast EMA rising/falling; rate of change positive/negative; corresponding momentum acceleration; corresponding impulse; corresponding current/recent micro-breakout; fast score above its early threshold together with corresponding direction or momentum acceleration; relative volume above its breakout threshold together with a nonnegative/nonpositive candle; and ATR plus bandwidth rising together with corresponding momentum acceleration. A current breakout earns more points than a recent one; cap each score at 100.
Calculation: Bull dominance = bull setup score − bear setup score ≥ dominance threshold; bear dominance = that difference ≤ negative dominance threshold.
Calculation: Compression active = Bollinger bands inside Keltner bands OR normalized ATR below its compression threshold AND normalized bandwidth below its compression threshold OR confirmed score below its compression threshold. Compression exit = current confirmed bar is no longer compressed, prior bar was compressed, and the compressed run lasted at least a minimum duration.
Calculation: Bull/bear ignition points = count of these true conditions: ATR rising; bandwidth rising; corresponding impulse; corresponding momentum acceleration; corresponding breakout recent; fast score above its early threshold; close above/below fast EMA; compression exit. False-expansion risk = a risk-point count for low relative volume, ATR not rising, bandwidth not rising, small body/ATR, confirmed score below compression threshold, and no recent breakout, reduced for higher-timeframe expansion, strong dominance, aligned direction and dominance, and aligned recent breakout. Higher-timeframe regime score uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; classify it as expansion/extreme, compression, or normal using the corresponding score thresholds.
Calculation: Bull armed = confirmed bar, cycle neutral, cooldown elapsed, fast score above early threshold, bull setup score above its minimum, bull dominant, and bull momentum acceleration OR recent bull breakout OR bull direction. Bear armed is symmetric. Cycle state advances from neutral to the corresponding armed state on these conditions.
Calculation: Normal bull ignition = confirmed bar, bull armed state, cooldown elapsed, bull ignition points at least their threshold, bull dominant, false-expansion risk not very high, and recent bull breakout OR compression exit OR fast score crossing above its early threshold. Normal bear ignition is symmetric.
Calculation: Fast bull ignition = confirmed bar, bull armed state, cooldown elapsed, fast score above breakout threshold, bull setup score above its fast minimum, bull dominant, relative volume above breakout threshold, recent bull breakout, and false-expansion risk not very high. Fast bear ignition is symmetric. Either ignition advances the cycle to its directional ignition state and starts the signal cooldown.
Calculation: Bull release = confirmed bar, bull ignition state, confirmed score above expansion threshold, bull setup score ≥ bear setup score, and bull direction OR recent bull breakout. Bear release is symmetric. Release advances the cycle to its directional release state.
Calculation: Release quality = capped 0–100 score from the higher of the directionally applicable bull/bear quality scores, plus a modifier for the last completed higher-timeframe regime. Each quality score awards points for fast score above breakout threshold, confirmed score above expansion threshold, ATR accelerating, bandwidth accelerating, relative volume above breakout threshold, corresponding recent breakout, corresponding direction, and corresponding dominance; the first two conditions award more points.
Calculation: Bull continuation = confirmed bar, bull cycle direction, no prior continuation in the cycle, bull macro lock clear, enough bars since ignition, fast score and bull setup score above continuation thresholds, bull setup advantage above its threshold and bull dominant, bull direction and close > fast EMA, fast score increasing with its smoothed signal nondecreasing, bull setup score increasing, release quality above its minimum, false-expansion risk low or medium, and neither bull ignition nor bull release. Bear continuation is symmetric, using bear setup advantage, bear direction, and close < fast EMA. A continuation fires at most once per cycle and sets its directional macro lock; opposite aligned direction and dominance clears that lock.
Calculation: Opposite-cycle invalidation = confirmed bar, opposing cycle active, opposing setup score above reversal minimum, opposing dominance, opposing recent breakout, fast score above early threshold, and false-expansion risk not very high. It resets the cycle; a bullish invalidation marks the bear cycle invalidated and vice versa.
Calculation: Bull extreme event = bull cycle direction and confirmed score crosses above the extreme threshold; bear extreme event is symmetric.
Calculation: Volatility exhaustion = confirmed bar, prior confirmed score at or above extreme threshold, confirmed score lower than on each of the prior two bars, and Bollinger bandwidth lower than its prior-bar value.
Long: normal bull ignition OR fast bull ignition OR bull release OR bull continuation OR bullish opposite-cycle invalidation OR bull extreme event.
Short: normal bear ignition OR fast bear ignition OR bear release OR bear continuation OR bearish opposite-cycle invalidation OR bear extreme event.
Exit: long, volatility exhaustion.
Exit: short, volatility exhaustion.
