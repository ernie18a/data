Calculation: Liquidity support = lowest low over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Liquidity resistance = highest high over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Recent high/low = highest high/lowest low over a separate lookback
Calculation: First-bar high/low = high/low of the first bar detected each day, captured while that bar is forming and then frozen
Calculation: First-bar range = first-bar high − first-bar low
Long: (close crosses above liquidity support AND close > liquidity support + ATR × breakout threshold) OR (close > liquidity support AND close < recent high AND not already in a liquidity-based trade AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its simple moving average) AND (session filter disabled OR within configured session)) OR (first-bar high was crossed upward AND low <= first-bar high AND close > first-bar high AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND strategy position is flat AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < oversold threshold))
Short: (close crosses below liquidity resistance AND close < liquidity resistance − ATR × breakout threshold) OR (close < liquidity resistance AND close > recent low AND not already in a liquidity-based trade AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its simple moving average) AND (session filter disabled OR within configured session)) OR (first-bar low was crossed downward AND high >= first-bar low AND close < first-bar low AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND strategy position is flat AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > overbought threshold))
Exit: long, close >= entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier OR close <= liquidity support − ATR × stop-loss multiplier OR high >= first-bar high + first-bar range × risk/reward ratio OR low <= first-bar low − ATR × stop-loss buffer multiplier
Exit: short, close <= entry price − ATR × take-profit multiplier OR close >= liquidity resistance + ATR × stop-loss multiplier OR low <= first-bar low − first-bar range × risk/reward ratio OR high >= first-bar high + ATR × stop-loss buffer multiplier
