Calculation: Last swing high/low = the most recent confirmed pivot high/low, using equal left- and right-side lookbacks; update only when a pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of true conditions: close > long-lookback EMA(close); higher-timeframe close > shorter-lookback EMA(close) on the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar; low < last swing low AND close > last swing low; close > previous bar high; close > open AND close - open > ATR × displacement threshold; RSI > upper RSI threshold; volume > volume moving average × volume threshold; ATR > moving average of ATR; close < (highest high + lowest low over a lookback) / 2; low < previous bar low AND RSI > previous bar RSI; UTC hour is within either configured trading session.
Calculation: Bearish score = count of true conditions: close < long-lookback EMA(close); higher-timeframe close < shorter-lookback EMA(close) on the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar; high > last swing high AND close < last swing high; close < previous bar low; close < open AND open - close > ATR × displacement threshold; RSI < lower RSI threshold; volume > volume moving average × volume threshold; ATR > moving average of ATR; close > (highest high + lowest low over a lookback) / 2; high > previous bar high AND RSI < previous bar RSI; UTC hour is within either configured trading session.
Long: bullish score >= signal threshold
Short: bearish score >= signal threshold
