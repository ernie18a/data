Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected OHLC price source from the session open, including the current bar.
Calculation: Overnight VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected OHLC price source from the overnight anchor, including the current bar; active through the NY session end.
Calculation: Prior-anchor VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected OHLC price source from the previous NY session open through the current bar, including overnight bars; evaluated during the current NY session.
Calculation: Low-anchor VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected OHLC price source since the current NY session's most recent lowest-low bar, including the current bar.
Calculation: High-anchor VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted selected OHLC price source since the current NY session's most recent highest-high bar, including the current bar.
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low among bars from the NY session open through the selected opening-range window; use the current bar while the window is forming and fixed values after it ends. Calculate both a shorter and a longer window.
Long: Close > Session VWAP OR Close > Overnight VWAP OR Close > Prior-anchor VWAP OR Close > Low-anchor VWAP OR Close > High-anchor VWAP OR Close > the midpoint of either opening range OR (after either opening range is fixed, previous close <= its high AND current close > its high) OR (at least one VWAP is enabled AND all enabled VWAPs have Close > VWAP) OR (at least four VWAPs are enabled AND exactly one enabled VWAP has Close <= VWAP)
Short: Close <= Session VWAP OR Close <= Overnight VWAP OR Close <= Prior-anchor VWAP OR Close <= Low-anchor VWAP OR Close <= High-anchor VWAP OR Close < the low of either opening range OR (after either opening range is fixed, previous close >= its low AND current close < its low) OR (at least one VWAP is enabled AND all enabled VWAPs have Close <= VWAP) OR (at least four VWAPs are enabled AND exactly one enabled VWAP has Close > VWAP)
