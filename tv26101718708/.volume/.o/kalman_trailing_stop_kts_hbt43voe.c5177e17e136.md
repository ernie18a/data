Calculation: Kalman level and slope = level and velocity from a 2-state constant-velocity Kalman filter of close; measurement noise is rolling close variance, system noise is that variance scaled by responsiveness, and measurement noise is adjusted by relative volume when volume weighting is enabled
Calculation: Kalman trailing stop = ratcheting stop bounded by Kalman level ± trailing-distance × EMA of true range; in an uptrend it ratchets upward and in a downtrend downward, with structural swing, momentum, volatility-relaxation, and optional smoothing adjustments
Calculation: Dynamic sweep threshold = base sweep multiplier × √(clamped return kurtosis / normal-distribution kurtosis) × EMA of squared close-to-Kalman-level error
Calculation: Short-term swing high/low = middle of three successive non-inside bars when its high is at least both neighbors' highs and strictly exceeds the later neighbor's high, or its low is at most both neighbors' lows and strictly below the later neighbor's low
Calculation: Intermediate-term swing high/low = middle of three successive short-term swing highs/lows when it is at least both neighboring highs and strictly exceeds the later high, or at most both neighboring lows and strictly below the later low
Long: flat and (uptrend begins when close > Kalman level, or close > previous trailing stop − signal offset × EMA true range and close > previous close and Kalman slope > 0, or bullish plunger, or pyramid-long trigger)
Short: flat and (downtrend begins when close < Kalman level, or close < previous trailing stop + signal offset × EMA true range and close < previous close and Kalman slope < 0, or bearish plunger, or pyramid-short trigger)
Long: low is the lowest low over a short lookback and close > open and close is within the configured top fraction of the bar range and bar range ≥ half the ATR and low < Kalman level − dynamic sweep threshold
Short: high is the highest high over a short lookback and close < open and close is within the configured bottom fraction of the bar range and bar range ≥ half the ATR and high > Kalman level + dynamic sweep threshold
Long: close crosses above the latest short-term swing high and prior pullback average volume < relative-volume threshold × median volume and current volume > prior pullback average volume and long position is active and trailing stop ≥ entry price and add cooldown is zero
Short: close crosses below the latest short-term swing low and prior pullback average volume < relative-volume threshold × median volume and current volume > prior pullback average volume and short position is active and trailing stop ≤ entry price and add cooldown is zero
Exit: long, trend direction is short or enabled take-profit is reached (high ≥ entry price + target multiple × EMA true range) or enabled session filter is outside its session
Exit: short, trend direction is long or enabled take-profit is reached (low ≤ entry price − target multiple × EMA true range) or enabled session filter is outside its session
