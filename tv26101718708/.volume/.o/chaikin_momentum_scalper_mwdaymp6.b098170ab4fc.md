Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = EMA of the accumulation/distribution line with a shorter lookback minus its EMA with a longer lookback
Long: Chaikin Oscillator > 0 AND previous bar's Chaikin Oscillator <= 0 AND close > long-term SMA of close
Short: Chaikin Oscillator < 0 AND previous bar's Chaikin Oscillator >= 0 AND close < long-term SMA of close
Exit: Long, low <= entry-signal-bar close - ATR × stop multiple OR high >= entry-signal-bar close + ATR × target multiple
Exit: Short, high >= entry-signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiple OR low <= entry-signal-bar close - ATR × target multiple
