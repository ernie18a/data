Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high greater than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest pivot after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest pivot after its right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (shorter-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ longer-lookback EMA of volume.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high AND open − low ≤ close − open AND volume oscillator > threshold
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low AND open − close ≥ high − open AND volume oscillator > threshold
