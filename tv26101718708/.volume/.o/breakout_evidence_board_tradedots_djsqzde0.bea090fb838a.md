Calculation: Bollinger Bandwidth percentile = percentile rank of Bollinger Bandwidth over a lookback; Bollinger Bandwidth is the upper-to-lower Bollinger Band width divided by the basis and expressed as a percentage.
Long: Current close > the highest close of the prior Donchian lookback bars AND previous close ≤ the highest close of the Donchian lookback bars ending one bar before that AND (compression filter disabled OR prior bar’s Bollinger Bandwidth percentile is below its compression threshold AND the percentile three bars before that is below its earlier-compression threshold)
Short: Current close < the lowest close of the prior Donchian lookback bars AND previous close ≥ the lowest close of the Donchian lookback bars ending one bar before that AND (compression filter disabled OR prior bar’s Bollinger Bandwidth percentile is below its compression threshold AND the percentile three bars before that is below its earlier-compression threshold)
