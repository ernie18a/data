Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low from the session start through the configured opening-range duration; use the completed range after that duration.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by the baseline. Baseline is either the rolling average over the configured number of chart bars, or the EMA of volume for the same bar-of-session slot across prior sessions; use the rolling average until the configured minimum session history is available. The time-of-day baseline excludes the current bar; zero or unavailable baseline/volume yields unavailable relative volume.
Long: Within the session and configured signal window after the opening range completes, opening-range high > opening-range low AND close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high AND ((relative volume >= threshold AND no confirmed long has fired this session) OR (relative volume < threshold or unavailable AND no unconfirmed long has fired this session))
Short: Within the session and configured signal window after the opening range completes, opening-range high > opening-range low AND close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low AND ((relative volume >= threshold AND no confirmed short has fired this session) OR (relative volume < threshold or unavailable AND no unconfirmed short has fired this session))
