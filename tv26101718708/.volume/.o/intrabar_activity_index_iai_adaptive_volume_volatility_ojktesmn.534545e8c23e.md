Calculation: Main-timeframe volume percentile = percentile rank of volume on the current, possibly forming main-timeframe bar over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Main-timeframe volatility percentile = percentile rank of the sum of true range across lower-timeframe bars in the current, possibly forming main-timeframe bar over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Rolling volume percentile = percentile rank of the sum of volume across the current and preceding chart bars spanning one main-timeframe interval, over a lookback scaled by the number of chart bars per main-timeframe interval.
Calculation: Rolling volatility percentile = percentile rank of the sum of true range across the current and preceding chart bars spanning one main-timeframe interval, over a lookback scaled by the number of chart bars per main-timeframe interval.
Long: (chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe AND main-timeframe volume percentile > volume threshold AND main-timeframe volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND close > open) OR (chart timeframe is below the main timeframe AND rolling volume percentile > volume threshold AND rolling volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND close > open from one main-timeframe interval ago)
Short: (chart timeframe is at least the main timeframe AND main-timeframe volume percentile > volume threshold AND main-timeframe volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND close <= open) OR (chart timeframe is below the main timeframe AND rolling volume percentile > volume threshold AND rolling volatility percentile > volatility threshold AND close <= open from one main-timeframe interval ago)
