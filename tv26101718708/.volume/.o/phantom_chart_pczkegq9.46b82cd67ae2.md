Calculation: Overlay OHLC = current chart bars aggregated to the selected overlay timeframe; use the still-forming bar unless configured to use only the last completed bar. All sequential calculations and pattern lookbacks advance by overlay bar.
Calculation: Heikin Ashi = close is the mean of overlay OHLC; open is the first bar’s (open + close)/2, then each new bar’s (prior Heikin Ashi open + prior Heikin Ashi close)/2; high/low are the maximum/minimum of overlay high/low and Heikin Ashi open/close. If double-smoothed, apply the same calculation to the first Heikin Ashi OHLC.
Calculation: Baseline source = selected overlay price source (open, close, high-low midpoint, high-low-close mean, or OHLC mean), optionally smoothed by a selected moving average. Baseline reference = configured constant or first chart bar’s high-low midpoint.
Calculation: Trend EMA = exponential moving average of the selected trend source; source is chart close or overlay close, except Heikin Ashi mode uses Heikin Ashi close. Inversion reverses the above/below comparison.
Calculation: Bullish candlestick pattern = any enabled bullish pattern: hammer (lower wick ≥ 2× body, upper wick ≤ body, close below context EMA); inverted hammer (upper wick ≥ 2× body, lower wick ≤ body, close below context EMA); dragonfly doji (body ≤ configured fraction of range, lower wick ≥ 60% of range, upper wick ≤ 10%); tweezer bottom (prior down bar, current up bar, lows within max(one tick, 5% of current range)); bullish marubozu (up bar, body ≥ 95% of range); bullish engulfing (prior down/current up, current close ≥ prior open, current open ≤ prior close); bullish harami (prior down/current up, current open ≥ prior close, current close ≤ prior open, current body smaller); morning star (bar two bars back down, prior body < half the body two bars back, current up and close above midpoint of bar two bars back); three white soldiers (three up bars with successively higher closes and each later open inside the preceding body); piercing line (prior down/current up, current open below prior close, current close above prior body midpoint and below prior open). Context EMA uses a shorter lookback than any long-term trend EMA.
Calculation: Bearish candlestick pattern = any enabled bearish pattern: hanging man (lower wick ≥ 2× body, upper wick ≤ body, close above context EMA); shooting star (upper wick ≥ 2× body, lower wick ≤ body, close above context EMA); gravestone doji (body ≤ configured fraction of range, upper wick ≥ 60% of range, lower wick ≤ 10%); tweezer top (prior up bar, current down bar, highs within max(one tick, 5% of current range)); bearish marubozu (down bar, body ≥ 95% of range); bearish engulfing (prior up/current down, current open ≥ prior close, current close ≤ prior open); bearish harami (prior up/current down, current open ≤ prior close, current close ≥ prior open, current body smaller); evening star (bar two bars back up, prior body < half the body two bars back, current down and close below midpoint of bar two bars back); three black crows (three down bars with successively lower closes and each later open inside the preceding body); dark cloud cover (prior up/current down, current open above prior close, current close below prior body midpoint and above prior open).
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price makes a lower low while the oscillator makes a higher low (regular), or price makes a higher low while the oscillator makes a lower low (hidden), versus the preceding confirmed oscillator pivot low.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price makes a higher high while the oscillator makes a lower high (regular), or price makes a lower high while the oscillator makes a higher high (hidden), versus the preceding confirmed oscillator pivot high.
Long: Heikin Ashi changes from bearish to bullish; or bullish Heikin Ashi has no lower wick; or enabled bullish candlestick pattern; or enabled bullish divergence; or baseline source crosses above baseline reference; or baseline source is above baseline reference when baseline fill is enabled; or selected bar coloring is bullish: close ≥ open in Direction mode, close ≥ previous close in Momentum mode, or trend source is above Trend EMA in Trend mode.
Short: Heikin Ashi changes from bullish to bearish; or bearish Heikin Ashi has no upper wick; or enabled bearish candlestick pattern; or enabled bearish divergence; or baseline source crosses below baseline reference; or baseline source is below baseline reference when baseline fill is enabled; or selected bar coloring is bearish: close < open in Direction mode, close < previous close in Momentum mode, or trend source is below Trend EMA in Trend mode.
