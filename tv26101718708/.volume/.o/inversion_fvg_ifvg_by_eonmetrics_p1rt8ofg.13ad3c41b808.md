Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when a completed bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish when its high is below the low two bars earlier. Gap boundaries are those two prices.
Long: A bullish fair value gap forms on the current completed bar, its height is at least the configured minimum multiple of ATR measured on the preceding bar, and it overlaps an unspent bearish fair value gap remembered within the configured memory period; the overlap height is at least the same configured minimum multiple of ATR, and the bearish gap has not closed beyond its upper boundary more than the configured pairing window ago.
Short: A bearish fair value gap forms on the current completed bar, its height is at least the configured minimum multiple of ATR measured on the preceding bar, and it overlaps an unspent bullish fair value gap remembered within the configured memory period; the overlap height is at least the same configured minimum multiple of ATR, and the bullish gap has not closed beyond its lower boundary more than the configured pairing window ago.
