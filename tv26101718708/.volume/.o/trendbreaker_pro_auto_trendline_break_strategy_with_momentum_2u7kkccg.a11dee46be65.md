Calculation: Resistance line = Projected line through the two most recent consecutive confirmed pivot highs, valid only when the newer high is lower and the pivots are at least the minimum span apart; a pivot requires the pivot lookback number of bars on each side, and the line is invalid if a close pierced it while the newer pivot was being confirmed.
Calculation: Support line = Projected line through the two most recent consecutive confirmed pivot lows, valid only when the newer low is higher and the pivots are at least the minimum span apart; a pivot requires the pivot lookback number of bars on each side, and the line is invalid if a close pierced it while the newer pivot was being confirmed.
Calculation: Smoothed Awesome Oscillator = EMA of (SMA of hl2 over the shorter lookback minus EMA of hl2 over the longer lookback), using a smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = Greater of the entry signal close’s distance to the initial stop and 0.5 ATR; the initial stop is either the entry signal close minus/plus an ATR multiple for long/short, or the lowest low/highest high over a lookback minus/plus ATR padding for long/short.
Calculation: Active stop = Initial stop, moved to entry price after price reaches the break-even R trigger, then trailed by the greater of the prior stop and close minus ATR multiple for long, or the lesser of the prior stop and close plus ATR multiple for short, once price reaches the trail-start R trigger.
Calculation: Profit target = Entry price plus/minus initial risk distance times the target R multiple for long/short.
Long: Close > valid, unbroken resistance line + ATR buffer AND line age <= maximum line age AND (momentum filter disabled OR smoothed Awesome Oscillator > its prior value) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable OR volume > its 20-bar SMA times multiplier).
Short: Close < valid, unbroken support line - ATR buffer AND line age <= maximum line age AND (momentum filter disabled OR smoothed Awesome Oscillator < its prior value) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable OR volume > its 20-bar SMA times multiplier).
Exit: long, Low <= active stop OR (take profit enabled AND High >= profit target) OR (maximum bars in trade enabled AND bars in trade >= maximum).
Exit: short, High >= active stop OR (take profit enabled AND Low <= profit target) OR (maximum bars in trade enabled AND bars in trade >= maximum).
