Calculation: Bullish and bearish OB zones = On each confirmed swing high/low with lookback L on both sides, scan the preceding 1 to 2L bars from nearest to farthest. For a swing high, select the first bearish candle whose body is at least the minimum OB size in ATR multiples; its body bounds form the bullish zone. For a swing low, select the first bullish candle meeting that size; its body bounds form the bearish zone. Retain each zone until a close moves beyond its far boundary by the invalidation distance in ATR multiples, then clear it. A bar touches a zone when its high-low range intersects the zone. Permit only one signal during each uninterrupted sequence of bars touching the same-side zone; rearm after a bar no longer touches it.
Long: Bar touches a bullish OB zone AND close > open AND close > daily-reset VWAP calculated from chart bars through the current bar AND close > EMA AND ADX >= trend threshold AND timestamp is in the London or New York session AND no long signal has fired during the current uninterrupted bullish-zone touch.
Short: Bar touches a bearish OB zone AND close < open AND close < daily-reset VWAP calculated from chart bars through the current bar AND close < EMA AND ADX >= trend threshold AND timestamp is in the London or New York session AND no short signal has fired during the current uninterrupted bearish-zone touch.
