Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source from the selected anchor; for the rolling anchor, use bars in the configured rolling window
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted mean squared price minus squared anchored VWAP, over the same bars as the VWAP
Calculation: VWAP bands = anchored VWAP ± each configured standard-deviation multiple; the setup multiple is wider than the trigger multiple, and the outer stretch multiple is wider than the setup multiple
Calculation: Same-time relative volume = current bar volume divided by the average volume of recent bars at the same time of day
Calculation: Bar delta = buying volume minus selling volume, from footprint data when available, otherwise signed lower-timeframe volume; higher-timeframe delta and volume are sums within the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Gate count = number of enabled gates passed: acceptance (close at least the configured distance beyond the crossed level and in the directional outer portion of the bar), size (same-time relative volume meets its threshold), flow (bar delta points in the signal direction and its absolute share of bar volume meets its threshold), pressure (current higher-timeframe delta points in the signal direction and its share of higher-timeframe volume meets its threshold), and trend (VWAP slope over the configured lookback points in the signal direction, or price agrees with the majority of the 233-period chart EMA and the 20-week EMA; the 20-week EMA uses the current, still-forming weekly bar)
Long: confirmed bar; VWAP data valid and past the minimum anchor bars; within the configured session if enabled; long side enabled; and any enabled mean-reversion rule passes: (low touched or crossed below the setup band within the setup lifetime AND close crossed above the lower trigger band from at or below its prior-bar value AND close is below VWAP AND (delta-flow gate is disabled or bar delta passes the long flow threshold)) OR (close crossed back above the lower band 2 from at or below its prior-bar value after a close below that band 3 AND close is below VWAP) OR (prior close was at or below the prior lower trigger band AND current close is above the lower trigger band and below the upper trigger band) OR (low touched or crossed below the lower setup band within the setup lifetime AND close crossed above the lower trigger band from at or below its prior-bar value)
Short: confirmed bar; VWAP data valid and past the minimum anchor bars; within the configured session if enabled; short side enabled; and any enabled mean-reversion rule passes: (high touched or crossed above the setup band within the setup lifetime AND close crossed below the upper trigger band from at or above its prior-bar value AND close is above VWAP AND (delta-flow gate is disabled or bar delta passes the short flow threshold)) OR (close crossed back below the upper band 2 from at or above its prior-bar value after a close above that band 3 AND close is above VWAP) OR (prior close was at or above the prior upper trigger band AND current close is below the upper trigger band and above the lower trigger band) OR (high touched or crossed above the upper setup band within the setup lifetime AND close crossed below the upper trigger band from at or above its prior-bar value)
Long: confirmed bar; VWAP data valid and past the minimum anchor bars; within the configured session if enabled; long side enabled; and either a break crosses upward through an enabled VWAP or band from at or below its prior-bar value and passes the required enabled-gate count, or a reclaim crosses upward through an enabled lower band from at or below its prior-bar value while close remains below VWAP and passes the required enabled-gate count; any configured confirmation bars also close above the crossed level
Short: confirmed bar; VWAP data valid and past the minimum anchor bars; within the configured session if enabled; short side enabled; and either a break crosses downward through an enabled VWAP or band from at or above its prior-bar value and passes the required enabled-gate count, or a reclaim crosses downward through an enabled upper band from at or above its prior-bar value while close remains above VWAP and passes the required enabled-gate count; any configured confirmation bars also close below the crossed level
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price OR bars since entry >= timeout; original mean-reversion exits at stop or target with stop taking priority if both are touched, while other mean-reversion and break/reclaim trades also exit when close is below their stop band or target is touched; A setup may exit when band 1 rejects price; B setup target is capped at the opposite trigger band
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price OR bars since entry >= timeout; original mean-reversion exits at stop or target with stop taking priority if both are touched, while other mean-reversion and break/reclaim trades also exit when close is above their stop band or target is touched; A setup may exit when band 1 rejects price; B setup target is capped at the opposite trigger band
