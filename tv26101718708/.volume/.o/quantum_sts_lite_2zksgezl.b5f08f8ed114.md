Calculation: Long session level = low of the immediately preceding completed session minus its configured offset
Calculation: Short session level = high of the immediately preceding completed session plus its configured offset
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the first configured number of minutes after an enabled session opens; finalize after that period and invalidate when its owning session ends
Long: close crosses the active long session level OR (opening-range signals are enabled and allowed for this side, the range is finalized and valid, and either (retest is disabled and close > opening range high) or (retest is enabled, a prior bar closed above opening range high, and low <= opening range high and close > opening range high)); each level cross and each opening-range signal occurs at most once per level or range side
Short: close crosses the active short session level OR (opening-range signals are enabled and allowed for this side, the range is finalized and valid, and either (retest is disabled and close < opening range low) or (retest is enabled, a prior bar closed below opening range low, and high >= opening range low and close < opening range low)); each level cross and each opening-range signal occurs at most once per level or range side
