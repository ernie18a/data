Calculation: True range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close)); robust ATR = the greater of the moving average of true range capped at a multiple of its rolling median and a price-based minimum floor
Calculation: Auction pressure = buy volume − sell volume; for each bar or lower-timeframe sub-bar, buy volume = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume and sell volume = (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume; use summed lower-timeframe values when available, otherwise chart-bar values
Calculation: Normalized pressure = 100 × auction pressure / rolling maximum of absolute auction pressure; normalize the selected pressure source independently and bound the result to −100…100
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Fingerprint score = min(100, 0.4 × abs(normalized pressure) + 20 × min(1, candle body ratio / body-ratio scale) + 15 × min(1, (high − low) / (2 × robust ATR)) + volume-balance score); volume-balance score = 25 × abs(buy volume − sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume) when the denominator is positive, otherwise its neutral score; with chart-bar proxy pressure, use 50 × abs((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) − 0.5)
Calculation: Reference levels = current session high and low, session midpoint, upper session reference at 0.999 × session high, lower session reference at 1.001 × session low, nearest round-number level, prior completed day's high and low, and prior completed week's high and low
Calculation: Level hit = abs(close − reference level) ≤ close × level-tolerance fraction; ladder score = 100 × sum of each hit's weight × (1 − abs(close − reference level) / (close × level-tolerance fraction)) / sum of active reference-level weights; use higher weights for POC/VAH/VAL when available, otherwise session-proxy weights; weight prior-day and weekly levels above session and round-number levels
Calculation: Composite score = min(100, 0.60 × fingerprint score + 0.40 × ladder score)
Calculation: Initial Balance = high − low over the first configured number of bars of the current session; compare with the median Initial Balance range of prior completed sessions only; Trend Day if current range ≥ 1.25 × median, Balance Day if current range ≤ 0.75 × median, otherwise Neutral Day; effective score threshold is base threshold + 5 on Trend Day, −5 on Balance Day, unchanged on Neutral Day
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA slope = EMA on the higher timeframe's last completed bar compared with its preceding completed bar
Calculation: Sizing unit = close × volatility percentage, where volatility percentage interpolates between the long-lookback 10th and 90th percentiles of robust ATR as a percentage of price using robust ATR's rank between its shorter-lookback rolling minimum and maximum
Calculation: Extension distance multiplier = max(reaction distance multiplier + 0.5, base extension multiplier × Initial-Balance scalar × volatility scalar); Initial-Balance scalar is 1.15 for Trend Day, 0.85 for Balance Day, and 1 otherwise; volatility scalar is robust ATR / its longer-lookback average, bounded to 0.8…1.6
Long: normalized pressure ≥ minimum pressure AND close > open AND candle body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND level-hit count ≥ minimum confluence count AND composite score ≥ effective score threshold AND higher-timeframe EMA slope is rising AND current session Initial Balance is formed AND at least three prior completed session Initial Balances exist AND no active position AND cooldown has expired
Short: normalized pressure ≤ −minimum pressure AND close < open AND candle body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND level-hit count ≥ minimum confluence count AND composite score ≥ effective score threshold AND higher-timeframe EMA slope is falling AND current session Initial Balance is formed AND at least three prior completed session Initial Balances exist AND no active position AND cooldown has expired
Exit: long, low ≤ entry − invalidation distance × sizing unit OR high ≥ entry + extension distance multiplier × sizing unit; if both occur on one bar, exit at the level closer to the previous bar's close, resolving an equal-distance tie at extension
Exit: short, high ≥ entry + invalidation distance × sizing unit OR low ≤ entry − extension distance multiplier × sizing unit; if both occur on one bar, exit at the level closer to the previous bar's close, resolving an equal-distance tie at extension
