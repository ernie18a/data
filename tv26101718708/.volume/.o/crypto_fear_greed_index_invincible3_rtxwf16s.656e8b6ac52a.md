Calculation: Fear & Greed index = clamp to 0–100 the selected moving average of the weighted mean of: momentum score (50 + 15 × z-score of close ROC); volatility score (if short-term ROC < 0, 100 − ATR-percentile rank, otherwise 50 + half the ATR-percentile rank); RSI; buying volume divided by total up/down volume; close position within the lookback high-low range; breadth (share of configured crypto assets above their EMAs); inverse USDT-dominance change; mean TOTAL and TOTAL2 scores (50 + 250 × distance from their EMAs as a fraction of EMA); and BTC-dominance context (75/60 when TOTAL rises and dominance falls/rises, 25/35 when TOTAL falls and dominance rises/falls, otherwise 50). Normalize each factor to 0–100 where applicable; replace unavailable factors with 50. Use configured factor weights normalized by their sum. Use current chart-timeframe bars and configured lookbacks; the referenced market series use their current chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bull regime = close > asset EMA and market score >= 50; bear regime = close < asset EMA and market score < 50.
Calculation: Momentum fading = Fear & Greed index > 75 and shorter-lookback close ROC < longer-lookback close ROC.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than an earlier pivot low while the Fear & Greed index at the pivot is higher by the configured minimum; both index pivot values meet the configured fear-zone filter when enabled, pivots meet the configured spacing limit, and intervening price and index values stay on or above their respective straight lines between pivots. Detect on the pivot-confirmation bar.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than an earlier pivot high while the Fear & Greed index at the pivot is lower by the configured minimum; both index pivot values meet the configured greed-zone filter when enabled, pivots meet the configured spacing limit, and intervening price and index values stay on or below their respective straight lines between pivots. Detect on the pivot-confirmation bar.
Long: Fear & Greed index crosses above 25 AND bull regime AND more than the configured cooldown bars since the previous accumulation marker.
Long: Fear & Greed index crosses above 55 AND bull regime AND more than the configured cooldown bars since the previous trend marker.
Long: Regular bullish divergence.
Short: Regular bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, Fear & Greed index > 75 AND shorter-lookback close ROC < longer-lookback close ROC AND momentum fading was false on the previous bar AND more than the configured cooldown bars since the previous trim marker.
