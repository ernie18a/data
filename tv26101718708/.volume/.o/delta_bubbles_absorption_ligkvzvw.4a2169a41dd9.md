Calculation: Candle delta = sum of lower-timeframe bar volumes signed positive when that bar closes above its open, negative when below, and otherwise by its close versus the previous close; if lower-timeframe data is unavailable, estimate as volume × (2×close − high − low) / (high − low), or zero for a zero-range bar. Use the current chart bar’s data.
Calculation: Absolute delta = absolute value of candle delta.
Calculation: Delta percentile thresholds = rolling percentiles of absolute delta over the same lookback; the strong-bubble threshold is higher than the ordinary bubble threshold. Use the current bar.
Calculation: Absorption wick ratio = upper wick / candle range for positive delta, or lower wick / candle range for negative delta; zero for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Bubble trap level = bubble close minus ATR-scaled buffer for a buy bubble, or bubble close plus ATR-scaled buffer for a sell bubble; use ATR on the bubble bar.
Long: delta > 0 AND close > open AND absolute delta ≥ ordinary bubble percentile threshold AND (absolute delta ≥ strong-bubble percentile threshold AND absolute delta / volume ≥ dominance threshold OR bars since prior displayed bubble > minimum spacing) AND NOT a bullish absorption bar
Long: delta < 0 AND absolute delta ≥ absorption percentile threshold AND (absolute body size ≤ body threshold × ATR OR delta and candle direction disagree) AND low is the lowest low in the absorption lookback AND absorption wick ratio ≥ wick threshold AND bars since prior absorption > minimum spacing
Long: a prior sell bubble’s follow-up close > its trap level within the trap window
Short: delta < 0 AND close < open AND absolute delta ≥ ordinary bubble percentile threshold AND (absolute delta ≥ strong-bubble percentile threshold AND absolute delta / volume ≥ dominance threshold OR bars since prior displayed bubble > minimum spacing) AND NOT a bearish absorption bar
Short: delta > 0 AND absolute delta ≥ absorption percentile threshold AND (absolute body size ≤ body threshold × ATR OR delta and candle direction disagree) AND high is the highest high in the absorption lookback AND absorption wick ratio ≥ wick threshold AND bars since prior absorption > minimum spacing
Short: a prior buy bubble’s follow-up close < its trap level within the trap window
