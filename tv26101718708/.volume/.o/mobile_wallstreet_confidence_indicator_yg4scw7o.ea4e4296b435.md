Calculation: MTF bullish/bearish direction = on each of the four counted timeframes, fast EMA >/< slow EMA, close >/< slow EMA, and slow EMA >/< its value one bar earlier; use the last completed bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish MTF count = number of counted timeframes with bullish/bearish direction
Calculation: MTF bullish/bearish agreement = corresponding count >= required count AND greater than the opposite count
Calculation: Fractal ribbon = RSI(14) smoothed by short and long EMAs; bullish when short > long, short > 50, and close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high or the slow EMA; bearish when short < long, short < 50, and close is below the latest confirmed pivot low or the slow EMA
Calculation: Buy zone = low of the zone-timeframe lookback range through its current bar, up to that low plus zone-timeframe ATR times a thickness factor; sell zone = high of that range, down to that high minus the same ATR-scaled thickness
Calculation: Buy-zone exit = a buy-zone touch within the allowed recent bars, followed by close above the buy zone and above open; sell-zone exit = a sell-zone touch within the allowed recent bars, followed by close below the sell zone and below open
Calculation: Buy post-retest = within the allowed bars after close first crossed above the buy zone, low retests within an ATR-scaled tolerance, and close is above the zone and above open; sell post-retest = corresponding cross below the sell zone, high retest within tolerance, and close below the zone and below open
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confidence = round(100 × the count of satisfied bullish/bearish conditions divided by 36): MTF agreement; corresponding MTF count exceeds the opposite count; the two shortest counted timeframe directions; either of the two longer counted timeframe directions; close above/below fast EMA; fast EMA above/below slow EMA; close above/below slow EMA; slow EMA rising/falling; middle EMA above/below long EMA; close above/below middle EMA; Hull MA rising/falling; close above/below Hull MA; RSI(14) above/below 50 and rising/falling; MACD line above/below signal and histogram above/below zero; CCI(20) above/below zero; stochastic %K above/below %D and above/below 50; MFI(14) above/below 50; Williams %R above/below -50; ADX(14) > 15 with +DI > -DI / -DI > +DI; Parabolic SAR below/above close; OBV rising/falling; VWMA(20) rising/falling and close above/below VWMA; close above/below the 20-period Bollinger basis and above/below the midpoint between basis and upper/lower band; close above/below the midpoint of the 20-period Donchian high/low; close above prior structure high / below prior structure low; directional candle body at least 0.12 ATR; body at least 0.12 ATR; lower wick > upper wick / upper wick > lower wick; bullish/bearish fractal ribbon; bullish/bearish structure clearance
Calculation: Bullish/bearish rank = round(bullish/bearish confidence score divided by 10)
Calculation: Depth filter = current execution-timeframe 10-bar high minus low >= ATR times minimum depth factor
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure clearance = close > prior execution-timeframe lookback high OR prior high minus close >= ATR times clearance factor / close < prior lookback low OR close minus prior low >= ATR times clearance factor
Calculation: Bullish/bearish body confirmation = close >/< open and absolute close-open >= 0.12 ATR
Calculation: Buy/sell zone filter = zone-exit requirement disabled OR corresponding zone-exit OR post-retest condition
Long: bullish MTF agreement AND bullish confidence >= minimum confidence AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND depth filter AND bullish structure clearance AND (body confirmation disabled OR bullish body confirmation) AND buy-zone filter AND pattern compatibility AND (fractal-ribbon gate disabled OR bullish fractal ribbon)
Long: bullish MTF count >= 2 AND both shortest counted timeframe directions bullish AND bearish MTF count <= 1 AND bullish confidence >= early-confidence threshold AND bullish rank >= minimum rank AND bullish fractal ribbon AND bullish body confirmation when required AND buy-zone filter AND pattern compatibility
Short: bearish MTF agreement AND bearish confidence >= minimum confidence AND bearish rank >= minimum rank AND depth filter AND bearish structure clearance AND (body confirmation disabled OR bearish body confirmation) AND sell-zone filter AND pattern compatibility AND (fractal-ribbon gate disabled OR bearish fractal ribbon)
Short: bearish MTF count >= 2 AND both shortest counted timeframe directions bearish AND bullish MTF count <= 1 AND bearish confidence >= early-confidence threshold AND bearish rank >= minimum rank AND bearish fractal ribbon AND bearish body confirmation when required AND sell-zone filter AND pattern compatibility
