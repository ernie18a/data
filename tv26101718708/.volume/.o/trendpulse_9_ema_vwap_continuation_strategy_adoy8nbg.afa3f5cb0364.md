Calculation: Basis = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Upper channel = Basis + ATR × channel multiplier; lower channel = Basis − ATR × channel multiplier
Calculation: Bullish regime = Choppiness Index < its threshold AND |Basis − Basis from the slope lookback bars ago| / ATR > its threshold AND (fraction of closes above Basis over the slope lookback > its threshold OR fraction below Basis > its threshold); expanding regime = ATR / its moving average <lookback> > its threshold AND Choppiness Index < its threshold plus a buffer; quiet regime = Choppiness Index >= its threshold AND ATR / its moving average <lookback> < 1; otherwise regime is noisy
Calculation: Long score = 100 × [0.18 × clip(relative volume, 0.8, 2.2) + 0.18 × clip((close − upper channel) / ATR, −0.2, 1.5) + 0.18 × clip(5-bar rate of change, −1, 3.5) + 0.14 × clip(|close − open| / ATR, 0.1, 1.2) + 0.12 × clip((fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR, −0.1, 1.2) + 0.20 × regime weight]; clip(x,a,b) = max(0,min(1,(x−a)/(b−a))); regime weight is 1 for bullish regime, 0.75 for expanding, 0.20 for quiet, and 0.40 for noisy
Calculation: Short score = 100 × [0.18 × clip(relative volume, 0.8, 2.2) + 0.18 × clip((lower channel − close) / ATR, −0.2, 1.5) + 0.18 × clip(−5-bar rate of change, −1, 3.5) + 0.14 × clip(|close − open| / ATR, 0.1, 1.2) + 0.12 × clip((slow EMA − fast EMA) / ATR, −0.1, 1.2) + 0.20 × regime weight]; use the same regime weights as the long score
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-bar average volume
Calculation: Average dollar volume = moving average of close × volume
Calculation: Pullback seen long = lowest low over pullback lookback <= fast EMA + ATR × pullback-zone multiple OR <= Basis + ATR × pullback-zone multiple; pullback seen short = highest high over pullback lookback >= fast EMA − ATR × pullback-zone multiple OR >= Basis − ATR × pullback-zone multiple
Calculation: Long continuation trigger = bullish bias AND pullback seen long AND (retest filter disabled OR lowest low over 3 bars > Basis − ATR × retest-zone multiple) AND current high > previous high AND current close > previous high AND current close > fast EMA; short continuation trigger = bearish bias AND pullback seen short AND (retest filter disabled OR highest high over 3 bars < Basis + ATR × retest-zone multiple) AND current low < previous low AND current close < previous low AND current close < fast EMA
Calculation: Entry risk = absolute difference between signal-bar close and (for long) the lower of signal-bar low and Basis minus ATR × stop multiple, or (for short) the higher of signal-bar high and Basis plus ATR × stop multiple; retain this risk for the position
Long: On a confirmed bar within the configured date window, bars since last trade > cooldown AND long side allowed AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > Basis AND average dollar volume >= liquidity threshold (when enabled) AND last completed benchmark-timeframe close > its EMA (when enabled) AND relative volume >= threshold (when enabled) AND long score >= minimum AND (breakout mode enabled AND close > upper channel AND close > previous high OR continuation mode enabled AND long continuation trigger)
Short: On a confirmed bar within the configured date window, bars since last trade > cooldown AND short side allowed AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < Basis AND average dollar volume >= liquidity threshold (when enabled) AND last completed benchmark-timeframe close < its EMA (when enabled) AND relative volume >= threshold (when enabled) AND short score >= minimum AND (breakout mode enabled AND close < lower channel AND close < previous low OR continuation mode enabled AND short continuation trigger)
Exit: long, low <= average entry price − entry risk OR high >= average entry price + entry risk × reward-risk multiple OR bars since entry >= time-stop bars OR short entry condition
Exit: short, high >= average entry price + entry risk OR low <= average entry price − entry risk × reward-risk multiple OR bars since entry >= time-stop bars OR long entry condition
