Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range on the decision timeframe.
Calculation: Qualified cup = on each of several nested lookbacks, identify a central extreme and left/right rims; require bounded depth, central extreme position, sufficient recovery, rounded bottom, U-curve fit, rim match, rising arms, and current price on the active right arm with momentum agreement. Use longer lookbacks as larger-scale candidates.
Calculation: Qualified double pivot = the latest two confirmed pivot lows (bullish) or highs (bearish), with bounded spacing, similar prices, minimum neckline height, valid pivot width classes, fresh second pivot, intact second pivot, and a confirmed reversal leg with sufficient completion.
Calculation: Selected structure = highest-scoring qualifying candidate above the minimum score; compare bullish cup candidates across scales with a size bonus, allow bearish candidates only in net bearish context, and reject a selected bearish candidate in bullish context. Prefer qualifying larger macro candidates when their score is within the stated margin of the current candidate. A higher-timeframe bridge uses 12-hour bars on daily charts; other higher-timeframe context uses 4-hour, 6-hour, 12-hour, and daily bars returned without lookahead.
Calculation: Entry/stop pair = a directionally valid entry and stop whose distance is within both percentage-risk and ATR-risk limits.
Calculation: Active structure = a selected structure retained until its extreme is breached beyond an ATR buffer, its target is reached, or the opposite strong EMA trend crosses the neckline beyond an ATR buffer.
Long: Active bullish structure AND (early-stage stored entry/stop pair is valid, belongs to the active structure, and has not been missed OR completed-stage neckline entry/stop pair is valid AND close >= entry OR post-break retest entry/stop pair is valid, belongs to the active structure, and remains within its retest distance OR confirmed smart-handle breakout/retest entry/stop pair is valid).
Short: Active bearish structure AND (early-stage stored entry/stop pair is valid, belongs to the active structure, and has not been missed OR completed-stage neckline entry/stop pair is valid AND close <= entry OR post-break retest entry/stop pair is valid, belongs to the active structure, and remains within its retest distance OR confirmed smart-handle breakout/retest entry/stop pair is valid).
Exit: long, close < active stop OR close >= active structure target OR active structure is invalidated.
Exit: short, close > active stop OR close <= active structure target OR active structure is invalidated.
