Calculation: Daily Kijun = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a 26-bar lookback on daily bars; uses the current daily bar while forming
Calculation: Fibonacci pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar on the selected pivot timeframe
Calculation: Fibonacci range = high − low of the last completed bar on the selected pivot timeframe
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = pivot ± range × 0.382, 0.618, or 1.000
Calculation: Pinbar = (close > open AND high − close > 2 × abs(close − open)) OR (close < open AND open − low > 2 × abs(close − open))
Calculation: Near a Fibonacci level = abs(close − level) ≤ close × 0.002 for any of the pivot, resistance, or support levels
Calculation: Kijun on each of 5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour, and daily bars = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a 26-bar lookback; uses the current bar while forming
Long: close > Daily Kijun
Long: close > the Kijun on any of the 5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour, or daily bars
Long: Near a Fibonacci level AND Pinbar AND close > open
Short: close ≤ Daily Kijun
Short: close ≤ the Kijun on any of the 5-minute, 15-minute, 1-hour, or daily bars
Short: Near a Fibonacci level AND Pinbar AND close < open
