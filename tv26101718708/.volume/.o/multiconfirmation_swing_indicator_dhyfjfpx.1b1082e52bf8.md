Calculation: Fast, trend, and major EMAs = close EMAs with ordered lookbacks fast < trend < major.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: Bullish smoothed Heikin Ashi = post-smoothed Heikin Ashi close ≥ post-smoothed Heikin Ashi open; first smooth open, high, low, close with a selected moving average, form Heikin Ashi candles (initial open = average of smoothed open and close; later open = average of prior Heikin Ashi open and close), then smooth each Heikin Ashi field with a selected moving average.
Calculation: Impulse MACD = MD, where source is (high+low+close)/3, hi and lo are RMA(high) and RMA(low), mi = EMA(source) + EMA(source) − EMA(EMA(source)); MD = mi−hi if mi>hi, mi−lo if mi<lo, otherwise 0. Signal = SMA(MD); bullish when MD>Signal and MD>0; bearish when MD<Signal and MD<0.
Calculation: Trendline bullish break = bullish break state changes from false to true. Pivot highs/lows use equal left and right lookbacks. On a pivot high, reset upper line to pivot and its slope to the selected slope (ATR/lookback, standard deviation of close/lookback, or the absolute close/index linear-regression slope, each times a multiplier); otherwise decrement upper line by its saved slope. Upper break state resets on a pivot high, otherwise becomes true when close exceeds upper line minus saved slope × lookback and otherwise retains its prior state. Bearish break is the corresponding pivot-low lower line, incremented by saved slope, with state true when close is below lower line plus saved slope × lookback. A break is a state change from false to true.
Calculation: Support and resistance = latest qualifying close pivot low and high, respectively, using equal left and right lookbacks. Qualify a low when signed volume exceeds the recent highest signed volume divided by 2.5; qualify a high when signed volume is below the recent lowest signed volume divided by 2.5. Signed volume is volume for an up-close bar and negative volume for a down-close bar, retaining the prior sign on unchanged close. Support zone bottom = support minus ATR(200) × width multiplier; resistance zone top = resistance plus ATR(200) × width multiplier.
Calculation: Support hold = low crosses above support; resistance hold = high crosses below resistance. Resistance break = low crosses above resistance zone top; support break = high crosses below support.
Calculation: Zone Shift bullish state turns on when low crosses above the zone top while state is bearish; it turns off when high crosses below the zone bottom while bullish. Zone middle = average of EMA(close, zone lookback) and HMA(close, max(zone lookback−40, 1)); zone top/bottom = middle ± SMA(high−low, 200). Trend initiation level is set to low on bullish turn-on and high on bearish turn-off.
Calculation: Bullish Zone Retest = bullish state, trend initiation level exists, and either close crosses above it or low crosses above it, with more than five bars since the prior bullish or bearish zone retest. Bearish Zone Retest = bearish state, trend initiation level exists, and either close crosses below it or high crosses below it, with the same cooldown.
Long: bullish trend EMA > major EMA AND close > fast EMA > trend EMA > major EMA AND close and prior close are each above their corresponding fast EMA AND trend EMA and major EMA each rise from prior bar AND RSI > bullish threshold AND this condition was false on the prior bar; OR bullish trend EMA > major EMA AND close > fast EMA > trend EMA > major EMA AND all enabled master filters pass AND the master buy condition was false on the prior bar; OR bullish trendline break OR resistance break OR support hold OR bullish Zone Retest.
Short: trend EMA < major EMA AND close < fast EMA < trend EMA < major EMA AND close and prior close are each below their corresponding fast EMA AND trend EMA and major EMA each fall from prior bar AND RSI < bearish threshold AND this condition was false on the prior bar; OR trend EMA < major EMA AND close < fast EMA < trend EMA < major EMA AND all enabled master filters pass AND the master sell condition was false on the prior bar; OR bearish trendline break OR support break OR resistance hold OR bearish Zone Retest.
Calculation: Master bullish filters: EMA filter passes when trend EMA > major EMA and close > fast EMA > trend EMA > major EMA; Zone filter passes when Zone Shift is bullish; Heikin Ashi filter passes when bullish smoothed Heikin Ashi; Impulse MACD filter passes when bullish; trendline filter passes on bullish trendline break; support/resistance filter passes on resistance break or support hold. Master bearish filters use the respective bearish conditions, with support break or resistance hold for the support/resistance filter. Disabled filters pass automatically.
