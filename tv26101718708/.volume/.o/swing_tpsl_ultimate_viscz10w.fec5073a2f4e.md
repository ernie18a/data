Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest within the same number of bars before and after it; calculate at three local lookbacks (fast, mid, slow) and one higher-timeframe lookback, confirming after the right-side bars. Use completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Latest swing high/low = the most recent confirmed high/low pivot that is above/below the confirmation bar's close.
Calculation: Final TP/SL percentage = the configured selection of the simple mean, EMA-weighted mean, or trimmed mean of positive pivot samples, or the per-bar EMA; blended mode averages the trimmed pivot mean and per-bar EMA. High-pivot samples are (pivot high − confirmation close) / confirmation close × 100; low-pivot samples are (confirmation close − pivot low) / confirmation close × 100. Per-bar samples are (high − open) / open × 100 for TP and (open − low) / open × 100 for SL, each EMA-smoothed. Pivot lookbacks use separate fast, mid, and slow lengths; the higher-timeframe pivot uses aggregated higher-timeframe bars.
Long: a confirmed swing low occurs OR (close ≤ latest swing low × (1 + final SL percentage × 0.5) AND RSI ≤ oversold threshold AND close > open AND (volume > its 20-bar SMA × confirmation multiplier, if volume confirmation is enabled))
Short: a confirmed swing high occurs OR (close ≥ latest swing high × (1 − final TP percentage × 0.5) AND RSI ≥ overbought threshold AND close < open AND (volume > its 20-bar SMA × confirmation multiplier, if volume confirmation is enabled))
