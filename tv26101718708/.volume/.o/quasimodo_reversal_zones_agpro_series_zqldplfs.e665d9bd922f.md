Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot = a high or low that is the highest high or lowest low over the pivot-length bars on each side; use the pivot only once its right-side bars have completed. Keep alternating pivots, replacing the latest pivot of the same type only when the new high is higher or the new low is lower.
Calculation: QM pattern score = round(clamp(head score + break score + spacing score + swing-range score + time-balance score + volume score, 0, 100)), where head score = 25 × clamp(head extension / (2 × ATR × minimum head extension), 0, 1); break score = 25 × clamp(sequence break / (2 × ATR × minimum sequence break), 0, 1); spacing score = 15 × clamp(smallest pivot gap / (2 × minimum swing spacing), 0, 1); swing-range score = 15 × clamp(average absolute price change across the three pivot legs / (1.25 × ATR), 0, 1); time-balance score = 10 × (1 − clamp(abs(first pivot gap − second pivot gap) / average of the three pivot gaps, 0, 1)); volume score = 10 × clamp(current volume / 20-bar average volume / 1.35, 0, 1) when relative volume is enabled, otherwise 10. ATR uses the ATR normalization lookback.
Calculation: QM reaction zone = QM level ± max(ATR × zone-width parameter, 2 minimum price increments); QM level is the first pivot price. Invalidation level = head pivot price − ATR × invalidation-buffer parameter for bullish patterns, or + ATR × buffer for bearish patterns.
Long: The latest four alternating confirmed pivots are low, high, low, high; third pivot low < first pivot low; fourth pivot high > second pivot high; first-to-second, second-to-third, and third-to-fourth pivot gaps are each at least the minimum swing spacing; first pivot low − third pivot low ≥ ATR × minimum head extension; fourth pivot high − second pivot high ≥ ATR × minimum sequence break; QM pattern score ≥ minimum pattern score; bars since the previous accepted pattern ≥ signal cooldown; fourth pivot is not the fourth pivot of the previous detected pattern OR the current bar's high ≥ active zone bottom AND low ≤ active zone top AND close > active zone top + ATR × reaction-confirmation parameter after the zone has been touched.
Short: The latest four alternating confirmed pivots are high, low, high, low; third pivot high > first pivot high; fourth pivot low < second pivot low; first-to-second, second-to-third, and third-to-fourth pivot gaps are each at least the minimum swing spacing; third pivot high − first pivot high ≥ ATR × minimum head extension; second pivot low − fourth pivot low ≥ ATR × minimum sequence break; QM pattern score ≥ minimum pattern score; bars since the previous accepted pattern ≥ signal cooldown; fourth pivot is not the fourth pivot of the previous detected pattern OR the current bar's high ≥ active zone bottom AND low ≤ active zone top AND close < active zone bottom − ATR × reaction-confirmation parameter after the zone has been touched.
Exit: long, low ≤ active invalidation level.
Exit: short, high ≥ active invalidation level.
