Calculation: Daily supply and demand zones = At each day boundary, use the just-completed day’s bars. Divide its high–low range into equal steps. From the high downward, accumulate volume of bars whose highs fall in each step; the first level where cumulative volume exceeds the configured percentage of daily volume defines a supply zone from that level to the day’s high. From the low upward, accumulate volume of bars whose lows fall in each step; the first level exceeding that threshold defines a demand zone from the day’s low to that level. A zone remains active until a bar closes above the supply zone or below the demand zone, respectively.
Calculation: Volume delta = Lower-timeframe signed volume delta; if it is zero and fallback is enabled, use current-bar volume signed by close versus open, or, for a doji, by close versus the previous close; otherwise use zero.
Calculation: Delta dominance = 100 × (accumulated buyer pressure − accumulated seller pressure) / total accumulated pressure during the current session, or zero when total pressure is zero. Buyer pressure adds positive delta magnitude and seller pressure adds negative delta magnitude, each weighted by relative volume and candle quality when enabled. Relative volume is current volume divided by its longer-lookback average. Candle quality is (close−low)/range + body/range + 0.5×lower-wick/range for positive delta, and (high−close)/range + body/range + 0.5×upper-wick/range for negative delta; range is at least one minimum price increment. Reset accumulated pressures at the first session bar.
Calculation: Opening delta threshold = Maximum of the absolute volume delta and 1 on the first session bar; reset at the start of each day when daily reset is enabled.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish challenge = During the session, positive/negative volume delta whose absolute value is at least the opening delta threshold times the configured challenge factor.
Calculation: Superior bullish/bearish delta = During the session, but not on the first session bar, positive/negative volume delta whose absolute value is at least the opening delta threshold times the superior-delta factor, with candle quality and relative volume at least their configured minimums.
Long: On the first session bar, volume delta > 0; OR during the session, the prior bar’s delta dominance < 0 and current delta dominance ≥ the configured flip threshold; OR bullish challenge; OR superior bullish delta; OR a qualifying demand-zone rejection with buyer confirmation. A qualifying rejection touches an active demand zone created before the current bar and, if required, an untested zone; with the default close-outside requirement, close > zone top (otherwise close > open and lower wick > 0). Buyer confirmation is during-session positive volume delta OR buyer dominance above its configured threshold OR bullish dominance flip OR bullish challenge OR superior bullish delta. Block when control favors sellers if opposite-control blocking is enabled. Enforce the configured buy cooldown.
Short: On the first session bar, volume delta < 0; OR during the session, the prior bar’s delta dominance > 0 and current delta dominance ≤ the negative configured flip threshold; OR bearish challenge; OR superior bearish delta; OR a qualifying supply-zone rejection with seller confirmation. A qualifying rejection touches an active supply zone created before the current bar and, if required, an untested zone; with the default close-outside requirement, close < zone bottom (otherwise close < open and upper wick > 0). Seller confirmation is during-session negative volume delta OR seller dominance below the negative configured threshold OR bearish dominance flip OR bearish challenge OR superior bearish delta. Block when control favors buyers if opposite-control blocking is enabled. Enforce the configured sell cooldown.
