Calculation: Weighted composite score = 100 × (weighted sum of factor votes / total factor weights). Each vote is +weight for its bullish condition, −weight for its bearish condition, or 0 otherwise; the ATR regime vote is +weight when active and 0 otherwise. Bullish/bearish conditions: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA / fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA; Supertrend bullish / bearish direction; MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0 / MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; RSI > upper threshold / RSI < lower threshold; ADX > minimum and +DI > −DI / ADX > minimum and −DI > +DI; close > VWAP / close < VWAP; close > Bollinger basis and close < upper band / close < basis and close > lower band; Stoch RSI %K > %D and within its neutral bounds / %K < %D and within its neutral bounds; OBV > its EMA / OBV < its EMA.
Calculation: ATR regime active = ATR as a percentage of close > minimum ATR percentage.
Long: weighted composite score ≥ long threshold AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND Supertrend is bullish AND ADX > minimum AND ATR regime active AND position is flat or short.
Short: weighted composite score ≤ short threshold AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND Supertrend is bearish AND ADX > minimum AND ATR regime active AND position is flat or long.
Exit: long, weighted composite score < exit threshold OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple.
Exit: short, weighted composite score > −exit threshold OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × take-profit multiple.
