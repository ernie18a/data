Calculation: Moving average candles = apply the selected moving average to each bar’s open, high, low, and close, using the same lookback for all four.
Calculation: MA-candle momentum = moving average candle close minus a selected moving average of that close; its lookback is shorter than the moving average candle lookback.
Calculation: Momentum regression = linear regression of MA-candle momentum over the momentum lookback, offset 0.
Calculation: MA-candle trend direction = Supertrend direction on moving average candles, using the same moving average type and lookback as the moving average candles, ATR multiplier 1, and close-only crossings. Start direction at +1; change from -1 to +1 when candle high crosses above the prior short stop, or from +1 to -1 when candle close crosses below the prior long stop. Long stop is candle close minus the moving average of true range; short stop is candle close plus that average. Carry each stop forward as in Supertrend: while the prior close remains above the prior long stop, use the greater of the current and prior long stops; while the prior close remains below the prior short stop, use the lesser of the current and prior short stops. True range uses moving average candle high, low, and prior close.
Calculation: VIX Fix = (highest moving average candle close over the momentum lookback minus moving average candle low) / highest moving average candle close over that lookback × 100.
Calculation: VIX Fix regression = linear regression of VIX Fix over the momentum lookback, offset 0.
Calculation: VIX Fix extreme state = 1 when VIX Fix is at least either its upper Bollinger Band (SMA plus the selected multiple of standard deviation, both over the momentum lookback) or its rolling high multiplied by the selected high-percentile factor. Otherwise 0; while flat, carry forward the greater of this value and the prior state.
Long: Within the configured date range AND MA-candle trend direction is positive AND either (VIX Fix mode is selected AND VIX Fix extreme state is 1 AND VIX Fix regression is positive and no greater than its prior value) OR (VIX Fix mode is not selected AND (momentum regression is nonpositive and at least its prior value while its prior value was nonpositive and less than the value before it OR aggressive entry is enabled and momentum regression is positive and greater than its prior value while the prior regression was not positive and rising)).
Exit: Long, when the selected exit mode’s MA-candle momentum band classification matches on the current and prior bars, and the band classification two bars ago matches its preceding classification: for long-trade mode, current and prior are negative extreme and two bars ago is neutral; otherwise, current and prior are neutral and two bars ago is positive extreme. Negative extreme means momentum is at or below its lower Bollinger Band or rolling low times the selected low-percentile factor; positive extreme means at or above its upper Bollinger Band or rolling high times the selected high-percentile factor; neutral means neither extreme.
