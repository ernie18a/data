Calculation: Energy wave = EMA of (0.35 × normalized OBV pressure + 0.35 × normalized 8-bar close rate of change + 0.20 × normalized MACD histogram + 0.10 × normalized π × ATR²), minus 50. Normalize each component to 0–100 using its rolling low and high over the same lookback; use 50 when the high equals the low. OBV adds volume when close rises from the prior bar, subtracts volume when close falls, and is unchanged otherwise. OBV pressure is the 8-bar rate of change of a 13-bar EMA of OBV. MACD histogram is the difference between the MACD line and its signal EMA; the MACD line is the difference between the fast and slow close EMAs.
Calculation: Wave signal = EMA of Energy wave.
