Calculation: AMV bands = center line is a selected HMA, EMA, or SMA of close; bandwidth is the weighted sum of close standard deviation, ATR, and the absolute difference between VWMA and SMA of close using the VWMA lookback; upper band = center line + bandwidth; lower band = center line − bandwidth. Apply the same center-line calculation to higher-timeframe bars.
Long: close > upper band AND volume > its SMA × volume multiplier AND (RSI > 50 if momentum confirmation is enabled) AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe center line, using the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming
Short: close < lower band AND volume > its SMA × volume multiplier AND (RSI < 50 if momentum confirmation is enabled) AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe center line, using the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming
