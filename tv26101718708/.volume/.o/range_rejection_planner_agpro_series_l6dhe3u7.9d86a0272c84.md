Calculation: Active range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding range lookback bars, excluding the current bar; midline = their average.
Calculation: ATR = average true range. Edge-zone width = ATR × edge-zone parameter; invalidation buffer = ATR × invalidation parameter.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick share = upper/lower wick length divided by candle high minus low.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average; if unavailable, use a neutral volume score.
Calculation: Rejection score = rounded 0–100 weighted sum: 15% edge-proximity score, 24% wick-share score, 25% close-back-inside score, 14% relative-volume score, 17% midline-room score, and 5% range-quality score. Each component is linearly normalized to 0–100 and clamped; higher is better for proximity (smaller distance), wick share, close-back distance, relative volume, midline room, and range quality. Range quality peaks at the ideal ATR-normalized range width and declines linearly toward the low and high width bounds.
Long: Mature range AND lower edge is allowed AND current low is within the edge zone of the active range low AND close is above the active range low by at least the close-back parameter in ATR units AND lower wick share is at least 0.8 × the minimum wick-share parameter AND room from close to midline is at least 0.35 × the minimum midline-room parameter in ATR units AND lower rejection score is at least the edge-watch threshold AND (mode is lower-edge rejection OR mode is Auto and lower score is greater than upper score, or tied with an eligible upper candidate).
Short: Mature range AND upper edge is allowed AND current high is within the edge zone of the active range high AND close is below the active range high by at least the close-back parameter in ATR units AND upper wick share is at least 0.8 × the minimum wick-share parameter AND room from close to midline is at least 0.35 × the minimum midline-room parameter in ATR units AND upper rejection score is at least the edge-watch threshold AND (mode is upper-edge rejection OR mode is Auto and upper score is at least the lower score, or lower candidate is ineligible).
Exit: long, high >= the midline saved when the rejection plan starts OR close < the saved range low minus the ATR invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, low <= the midline saved when the rejection plan starts OR close > the saved range high plus the ATR invalidation buffer.
