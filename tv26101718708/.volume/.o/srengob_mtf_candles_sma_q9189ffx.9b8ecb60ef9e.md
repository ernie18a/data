Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low from the bar that becomes a swing after the swing lookback bars confirm it; retain each as the latest swing until replaced.
Calculation: Bullish order block = range from the selected high to selected low of the bar with the lowest selected low between a confirmed swing high and the close that first breaks above it; selected prices are candle body extremes when body mode is used, otherwise full high/low. Remove the block when the candle body low falls below its bottom. Bearish order block is the mirrored construction from a close breaking below a confirmed swing low; remove it when the candle body high rises above its top.
Calculation: Bullish structure = close above the latest confirmed swing high. Bearish structure = close below the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: ROC = percentage change in close over a lookback; fast ROC uses a shorter lookback than slow ROC.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = fast ROC above slow ROC, fast ROC rising, and close below prior close. Bearish divergence = fast ROC below slow ROC, fast ROC falling, and close above prior close.
Calculation: Bullish volume pressure = volume relative to its moving average exceeds its threshold and close is above open. Bearish volume pressure is the mirrored condition. The moving average uses a short lookback.
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = close-to-low distance exceeds the high-to-close distance by its threshold and volume exceeds its moving average. Bearish imbalance is the mirrored condition.
Calculation: Bullish acceleration = close momentum is positive and its second difference is positive. Bearish acceleration is the mirrored condition.
Calculation: Early bullish signal = bullish acceleration and (bullish divergence or bullish volume pressure or bullish imbalance). Early bearish signal is the mirrored condition.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below bullish order block bottom and close above it. Bearish sweep = high above bearish order block top and close below it.
Calculation: Quant score = sum of configurable weighted contributions for structure, price inside either order block, order-block size relative to fast ATR, rejection wick, futures-volume confirmation, sweep, trend alignment, active session, strong momentum, choppy-range penalty, quick reversal, and momentum/ATR contribution. Trend alignment compares close with its SMA; momentum/ATR contribution uses early signal, impulse, and momentum-strength conditions. Volume confirmation uses GC1! volume versus its moving average, including volume spike, above-average volume, or rising volume. Fast ATR uses a shorter lookback than slow ATR.
Long: Early bullish signal OR bullish sweep OR (quant score at least its threshold AND bullish structure AND close inside bullish order block AND close above its SMA).
Short: Early bearish signal OR bearish sweep OR (quant score at least its threshold AND bearish structure AND close inside bearish order block AND close below its SMA).
