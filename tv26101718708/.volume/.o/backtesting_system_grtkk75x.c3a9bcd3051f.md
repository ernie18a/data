Long: Short-term EMA crosses above long-term EMA
Short: Short-term EMA crosses below long-term EMA
Calculation: Stop-loss level = entry price − (long) or + (short) ATR-based stop distance
Calculation: Take-profit level = entry price + (long) or − (short) stop-loss distance × reward-to-risk multiple
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop-loss level OR high ≥ take-profit level
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop-loss level OR low ≤ take-profit level
