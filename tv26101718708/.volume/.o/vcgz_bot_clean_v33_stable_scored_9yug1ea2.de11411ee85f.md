Calculation: 15-minute bias = current 15-minute close, EMA, and RSI; EMA slope = current 15-minute EMA minus previous 15-minute EMA. Use the current 15-minute bar (which may still be forming) and previous completed 15-minute EMA.
Calculation: Long retracement zone = impulse high − impulse range × [higher retracement parameter, lower retracement parameter]; short retracement zone = impulse low + impulse range × [lower retracement parameter, higher retracement parameter]. Zone midpoint = average of its bounds.
Calculation: Long score = count of these conditions: bullish 15-minute bias; close above rising chart-timeframe EMA; locked long impulse range ≥ ATR × strong-impulse parameter; zone reaction and confirmation candle; bullish directional volume. Short score = count of bearish equivalents. Directional volume means volume > its moving average × volume multiplier and candle direction agrees with the side.
Calculation: Long stop = entry-time minimum of entry-bar low and lowest low over the previous three bars, minus entry-time ATR × stop-buffer parameter; long target = entry price + (entry price − stop) × target-R parameter. Short stop = entry-time maximum of entry-bar high and highest high over the previous three bars, plus entry-time ATR × stop-buffer parameter; short target = entry price − (stop − entry price) × target-R parameter.
Long: At bar close, flat; bullish 15-minute bias (15-minute close > EMA, EMA slope > 0, RSI ≥ bullish threshold); session filter disabled or current bar within configured session; no long impulse currently locked; over the impulse lookback, highest high − lowest low ≥ ATR × minimum-impulse parameter and the lowest low occurred earlier than the highest high; the resulting impulse extreme is within the freshness limit; price has touched the long retracement zone and this bar is within the confirmation-bar limit from first touch; this bar overlaps the zone and closes above its midpoint; close > open; body / ATR ≥ minimum-body parameter; close is in the upper configured fraction of the bar range; and either low < the lowest low of the preceding sweep-lookback bars or lower wick > body × wick parameter; close > chart-timeframe EMA and EMA > previous bar EMA; long score ≥ minimum score; and long entry price − long stop > minimum tick.
Short: At bar close, flat; bearish 15-minute bias (15-minute close < EMA, EMA slope < 0, RSI ≤ bearish threshold); session filter disabled or current bar within configured session; no short impulse currently locked; over the impulse lookback, highest high − lowest low ≥ ATR × minimum-impulse parameter and the highest high occurred earlier than the lowest low; the resulting impulse extreme is within the freshness limit; price has touched the short retracement zone and this bar is within the confirmation-bar limit from first touch; this bar overlaps the zone and closes below its midpoint; close < open; body / ATR ≥ minimum-body parameter; close is in the lower configured fraction of the bar range; and either high > the highest high of the preceding sweep-lookback bars or upper wick > body × wick parameter; close < chart-timeframe EMA and EMA < previous bar EMA; short score ≥ minimum score; and short stop − short entry price > minimum tick.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry-time long stop OR high ≥ entry-time long target.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry-time short stop OR low ≤ entry-time short target.
