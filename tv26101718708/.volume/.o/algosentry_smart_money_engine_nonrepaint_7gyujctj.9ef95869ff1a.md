Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is a pivot high over equal lookback and confirmation windows; available after the confirmation bars close
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is a pivot low over equal lookback and confirmation windows; available after the confirmation bars close
Long: A newly confirmed swing high is higher than the previous confirmed swing high
Long: A newly confirmed swing low is higher than the previous confirmed swing low
Short: A newly confirmed swing high is not higher than the previous confirmed swing high, or no previous confirmed swing high exists
Short: A newly confirmed swing low is not higher than the previous confirmed swing low, or no previous confirmed swing low exists
Long: Close > the latest confirmed swing high not yet broken
Short: Close < the latest confirmed swing low not yet broken
Long: Low < the latest confirmed swing low not yet swept AND close > that swing low
Short: High > the latest confirmed swing high not yet swept AND close < that swing high
Long: Low > high two bars earlier
Short: High < low two bars earlier
Long: Close > the latest confirmed swing high not yet broken AND at least one of the preceding lookback bars is bearish
Short: Close < the latest confirmed swing low not yet broken AND at least one of the preceding lookback bars is bullish
