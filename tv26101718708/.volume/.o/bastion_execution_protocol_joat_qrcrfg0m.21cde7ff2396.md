Calculation: Bullish/bearish regime = Bollinger-band width is in the lowest configured percentile (squeeze); otherwise bullish if VWAP slope exceeds its configured positive threshold or short-, medium-, and long-term SMAs are strictly ascending; bearish if VWAP slope is below its configured negative threshold or those SMAs are strictly descending; otherwise range. VWAP slope is the change over its configured lookback divided by ATR times the square root of that lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure = persistent state set to bullish when a confirmed close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high while state is neutral or bearish, or set to bearish when a confirmed close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low while state is neutral or bullish; otherwise retain state.
Calculation: SMI = 100 × double-smoothed EMA of (close − midpoint of the configured lookback high/low range) divided by half the double-smoothed EMA of that range; bullish when above zero, bearish when below zero.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume; estimated buy volume is full volume when close ≥ open, otherwise volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price tick); sell volume is volume minus buy volume.
Calculation: Displacement candle = body / range meets its configured minimum and body is at least its configured multiple of the 20-bar average body, with positive range; bullish/bearish displacement also requires close > open / close < open.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish engulfing = current candle is bullish/bearish, prior candle bearish/bullish, current body strictly engulfs the prior body and is larger, and current volume exceeds its configured multiple of the 20-bar average volume.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish pin bar = lower/upper wick exceeds twice the body and upper/lower wick is less than half the body, with positive range.
Long: bullish displacement OR (pattern confirmation enabled AND (bullish engulfing OR bullish pin bar)) OR (confirmed bar AND bullish regime AND bullish structure AND RSI above configured bull threshold AND SMI > 0 AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR CVD > its configured-lookback moving average) AND (daily trade count below configured limit) AND (within an enabled NY or London session AND weekday enabled, unless session filter disabled) AND (squeeze avoidance disabled OR not squeeze) AND (trend-regime requirement disabled OR regime is trending) AND close > short-term SMA AND close > VWAP)
Short: bearish displacement OR (pattern confirmation enabled AND (bearish engulfing OR bearish pin bar)) OR (confirmed bar AND bearish regime AND bearish structure AND RSI below configured bear threshold AND SMI < 0 AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR CVD < its configured-lookback moving average) AND (daily trade count below configured limit) AND (within an enabled NY or London session AND weekday enabled, unless session filter disabled) AND (squeeze avoidance disabled OR not squeeze) AND (trend-regime requirement disabled OR regime is trending) AND close < short-term SMA AND close < VWAP)
Exit: long, price reaches the active stop or fixed target; or configured end-of-day close time is reached. Initial stop = entry price − ATR-at-entry × configured stop multiplier; target = entry price + initial stop distance × configured reward-risk ratio. If trailing is enabled and (close − entry price) / current ATR-based stop distance reaches the configured activation threshold, raise the stop to the greater of its prior value and close − current ATR × configured trail multiplier.
Exit: short, price reaches the active stop or fixed target; or configured end-of-day close time is reached. Initial stop = entry price + ATR-at-entry × configured stop multiplier; target = entry price − initial stop distance × configured reward-risk ratio. If trailing is enabled and (entry price − close) / current ATR-based stop distance reaches the configured activation threshold, lower the stop to the lesser of its prior value and close + current ATR × configured trail multiplier.
