Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars ago, current high two bars ago < previous high, current low two bars ago < current low, and normalized gap size > threshold
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars ago, current low two bars ago > previous low, current high two bars ago > current high, and normalized gap size > threshold
Calculation: Normalized gap size = gap size divided by the 100th percentile nearest-rank of gap size over a longer lookback, multiplied by 100; gap size = (current low − high two bars ago) / current low × 100 when the previous bar closed above its open, otherwise (low two bars ago − current high) / current high × 100
Calculation: FVG mitigation % = for a bullish FVG, 0% at or above its top, 100% at or below its bottom, otherwise (top − source) / FVG height × 100; for a bearish FVG, 0% at or below its bottom, 100% at or above its top, otherwise (source − bottom) / FVG height × 100. Source is current close, or current low for bullish and current high for bearish, according to the selected mitigation source.
Calculation: FVG volume and shares = sum volume on lower-timeframe bars with close above their previous close for bullish volume, and below for bearish volume, aggregated over the last completed chart bar; shares are each sum as a percentage of their total. If both sums are zero, use the last completed chart bar's volume, split by the close's position in its high-low range for bullish share, with the remainder bearish.
Long: Flat position AND cooldown since last entry elapsed AND an intersecting, uncrossed bullish FVG exists whose mitigation % ≥ long threshold, age in chart bars ≥ long minimum, volume ≥ long minimum and ≤ long maximum when maximum is enabled, bullish share ≥ long minimum, bearish share ≤ long maximum, and current close > open if candle confirmation is enabled; select the qualifying FVG with the most recent creation, and do not enter again on an FVG already traded
Short: Flat position AND cooldown since last entry elapsed AND an intersecting, uncrossed bearish FVG exists whose mitigation % ≥ short threshold, age in chart bars ≥ short minimum, volume ≥ short minimum and ≤ short maximum when maximum is enabled, bearish share ≥ short minimum, bullish share ≤ short maximum, and current close < open if candle confirmation is enabled; select the qualifying FVG with the most recent creation, and do not enter again on an FVG already traded
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss %) OR, after high reaches entry price × (1 + trailing activation %), low ≤ highest high since activation − entry price × trailing distance %
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss %) OR, after low reaches entry price × (1 − trailing activation %), high ≥ lowest low since activation + entry price × trailing distance %
