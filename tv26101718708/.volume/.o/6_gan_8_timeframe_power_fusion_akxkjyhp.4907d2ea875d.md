Calculation: 8-timeframe up count = number of monitored timeframes whose sine-adjusted close is higher than its prior chart-bar value; each uses that timeframe’s current close, including its forming bar
Calculation: Sine-adjusted close = current close from one of the eight monitored timeframes + chart-timeframe ATR × an amplitude parameter × sine of chart-bar index over that timeframe’s cycle length, with a distinct phase per timeframe
Calculation: Composite slope = average of the eight sine-adjusted closes is higher than its prior chart-bar value
Calculation: GAN up count = number of eight linear regressions of chart-timeframe close, with progressively longer lookbacks, whose current value is higher than its prior chart-bar value
Calculation: Volume fusion = average of the 100-bar normalized values of the smoothed merged-bar delta, smoothed cumulative delta, and smoothed cumulative OBV; merged-bar delta is positive or negative merged volume according to merged close versus merged open, and OBV step is positive or negative merged volume according to merged close versus prior merged close
Calculation: Volume fusion resistance break = volume fusion crosses above its prior-bar rolling high; support break = volume fusion crosses below its prior-bar rolling low; volume bullish/bearish = volume fusion is above/below its EMA
Calculation: Power score = rounded weighted sum of up-count percentage, GAN-up-count percentage, close-vs-EMA trend score, volume score, RSI momentum score, and ATR score; trend score is 100 above EMA and 0 below; RSI score is 100 at/above its bullish threshold, 0 at/below its bearish threshold, otherwise 50; volume score is 100 on resistance break, 0 on support break, 75 when volume fusion is bullish, 25 when bearish, otherwise 50; ATR score is 100/0 when ATR exceeds its average and the current candle is bullish/bearish, otherwise 50
Long: power score >= buy threshold AND composite slope is up AND close > EMA AND GAN up count meets the bullish-alignment threshold AND no volume fusion support break AND previous signal is not long
Short: power score <= sell threshold AND composite slope is down AND close < EMA AND GAN down count meets the bearish-alignment threshold AND no volume fusion resistance break AND previous signal is not short
