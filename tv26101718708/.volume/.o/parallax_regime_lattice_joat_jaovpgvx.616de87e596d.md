Calculation: Fast, intermediate, and structural EMAs = EMAs of the primary source with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Trend basis = average of the fast, intermediate, and structural EMAs
Calculation: Direction state = +1 if at least two of these are true, −1 if at least two are false, otherwise 0: fast EMA > intermediate EMA; intermediate EMA > structural EMA; close > trend basis; +DI > −DI
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed using equal left and right pivot strengths
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = on a confirmed bar, low < an unswept confirmed swing low and close > that swing low
Calculation: Buy-side sweep = on a confirmed bar, high > an unswept confirmed swing high and close < that swing high
Calculation: Bullish absorption = on a confirmed bar, volume exceeds its 20-bar average by the configured multiple, body/range is below the configured maximum, close >= open, and close is in the lower portion of the bar
Calculation: Bearish absorption = on a confirmed bar, volume exceeds its 20-bar average by the configured multiple, body/range is below the configured maximum, close <= open, and close is in the upper portion of the bar
Calculation: Trend health = ADX >= its configured floor AND choppiness index < its configured ceiling
Calculation: Expansion = trend health AND compression ratio >= its configured threshold, where compression ratio = lookback high-low range / (ATR × lookback)
Calculation: Bias score = clipped to [−100, 100]: +22 for bullish direction state, −22 for bearish direction state; +18 for bullish structure state, −18 for bearish structure state; in healthy trend, +15 for bullish direction state or −15 for bearish direction state; +15 for bullish break of structure or −15 for bearish break of structure; +10 for bullish absorption or −10 for bearish absorption; +9 for the latest sweep being sell-side or −9 for it being buy-side; +8 when normalized average EMA slope > 55 or −8 when it < 45
Long: confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high OR sell-side sweep OR bullish absorption OR direction state = +1 OR expansion AND bias score > 0 OR bias score crosses above its bullish threshold
Short: confirmed close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low OR buy-side sweep OR bearish absorption OR direction state = −1 OR expansion AND bias score < 0 OR bias score crosses below its bearish threshold
