Calculation: True range = max(high − low, |high − previous close|, |low − previous close|); ATR = Wilder average of true range over the context lookback; normalized volume = volume / context-lookback SMA(volume), or 1 if volume or its average is unavailable.
Calculation: Effort = level(sqrt((true range / ATR) × normalized volume)); response = level(0.55 × |close − open| / ATR + 0.45 × |close − previous close| / ATR); level(x) = 100 × (x / reference)^curve / (1 + (x / reference)^curve), using the applicable positive reference and curve.
Calculation: Path efficiency = clamp(|close − close[context lookback]| / (context lookback × context-lookback SMA(|close − previous close|)), 0, 1), or 0 when the denominator is zero; efficiency evidence = level(path efficiency). Context lookback is shared by ATR, volume average, path average, and net displacement.
Calculation: Direction = 0 when |raw direction| is below its dead zone; otherwise raw direction, where close location = clamp(((close − low) − (high − close)) / true range, −1, 1), body direction is 1 if close > open, −1 if close < open, else 0, body fraction = |close − open| / true range, and raw direction = clamp(0.60 × close location + 0.40 × body direction × body fraction, −1, 1). Direction conviction = level(|direction|); bullish/bearish direction evidence is conviction / 100 when direction is respectively positive/negative, else 0.
Calculation: Response Field = EMA(response level × direction × (0.35 + 0.65 × path efficiency)); commit regime changes only on confirmed bars.
Calculation: Bull/Bear Drive evidence = weighted geometric mean of (effort/100, response/100, efficiency evidence/100, corresponding directional evidence), with weights (0.15, 0.35, 0.25, 0.25). For absorption, lower rejection = clamp(2 × lower wick / true range, 0, 1) × clamp((close location + 1)/2, 0, 1); upper rejection = clamp(2 × upper wick / true range, 0, 1) × clamp((1 − close location)/2, 0, 1); one-sided lower/upper rejection = that rejection × (1 − the other rejection). Bull/Bear Absorption evidence = weighted geometric mean of (effort/100, 1 − response/100, corresponding one-sided rejection, 1 − efficiency evidence/100), weights (0.30, 0.30, 0.30, 0.10). Churn evidence = weighted geometric mean of (effort/100, 1 − response/100, sqrt(lower rejection × upper rejection), 1 − direction conviction/100), weights (0.25, 0.25, 0.30, 0.20). Balance evidence = weighted geometric mean of (1 − effort/100, 1 − response/100, 1 − efficiency evidence/100), weights (0.35, 0.35, 0.30). Weighted geometric mean = 100 × product(max(component, 0.000001)^weight).
Calculation: Active candidate = highest-evidence state among Bull Drive, Bear Drive, Bull Absorption, Bear Absorption, and Churn; candidate qualifies when its evidence meets the active-entry threshold and exceeds the second-highest active evidence by the entry-separation threshold. Primary regime state machine: start in Transition; from Transition enter a qualifying active candidate, otherwise enter Balance when balance evidence meets its entry threshold and top active evidence is below the active-entry threshold. In Balance, enter a qualifying active candidate after it remains the candidate for the balance-escape confirmation count; otherwise hold while balance evidence meets its hold threshold, else return to Transition. In an active state, switch to a different active candidate only when it qualifies, exceeds the current state's evidence by the switch margin, and remains the candidate for the switch confirmation count. For Bull/Bear Drive, two consecutive confirmed bars with Response Field respectively ≤ 0 / ≥ 0 release the state to Balance if balance evidence meets the active-to-balance threshold and top active evidence is below the active-entry threshold, otherwise to Transition. Otherwise, active evidence below the hold threshold for the active-exit confirmation count exits to Balance if balance evidence meets the active-to-balance threshold and top active evidence is below the active-entry threshold for the balance confirmation count; else exits to Transition. Active candidate selection and switching take priority over these exits.
Calculation: Bull/Bear Vacuum evidence = weighted geometric mean of (leverage evidence, response/100, efficiency evidence/100, corresponding directional evidence), weights (0.25, 0.30, 0.25, 0.20); leverage evidence = level(max(ln((response level + epsilon)/(effort level + epsilon)), 0), ln(leverage ratio reference), leverage curve). A bullish/bearish Vacuum-like condition requires its evidence to meet the modifier threshold and direction to be respectively positive/negative.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the primary regime changes into Bull Drive or Bull Absorption, OR the bullish Vacuum-like condition becomes true after being false on the prior bar.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the primary regime changes into Bear Drive or Bear Absorption, OR the bearish Vacuum-like condition becomes true after being false on the prior bar.
