Calculation: Bullish displacement ratio = (origin open − lowest close from the origin through the trigger bar) / (origin open − lowest open in the contiguous bullish candle run immediately before the origin)
Calculation: Bearish displacement ratio = (highest close from the origin through the trigger bar − origin open) / (highest open in the contiguous bearish candle run immediately before the origin − origin open)
Calculation: Bullish CISD = a bearish candle following a bullish candle seeds its open as an origin; on a later bar, close > origin open and bullish displacement ratio > the displacement threshold
Calculation: Bearish CISD = a bullish candle following a bearish candle seeds its open as an origin; on a later bar, close < origin open and bearish displacement ratio > the displacement threshold
