Calculation: Confirmation score = add points for close above (long) / below (short) entry level, close above / below setup level, candle closing up / down, close position in bar range above one-third / below two-thirds, close above / below session VWAP, and close above / below prior close; use the direction-appropriate score
Calculation: Confirmation threshold = base threshold for the selected confirmation mode, increased when ATR(14) / its longer-term simple average is elevated, increased further at extreme volatility, and increased after a prior stop loss that day
Long: A valid configured A+ long setup is armed when, after the session start, close is within the configured priority range of its level, crosses above that level, or retests it after crossing; before the setup is consumed or another trade is active, a confirmed bar touches the configured entry (low <= entry) and closes no farther than half the entry-to-stop distance below it; no target was reached after arming and before the touch; then, outside the US cash-open window, the confirmation score meets the threshold on a confirmed bar within the configured maximum bars after touch
Short: A valid configured A+ short setup is armed when, after the session start, close is within the configured priority range of its level, crosses below that level, or retests it after crossing; before the setup is consumed or another trade is active, a confirmed bar touches the configured entry (high >= entry) and closes no farther than half the entry-to-stop distance above it; no target was reached after arming and before the touch; then, outside the US cash-open window, the confirmation score meets the threshold on a confirmed bar within the configured maximum bars after touch
Long: When no A+ level is in reach and no trade is active, close crosses above a valid configured B long level
Short: When no A+ level is in reach and no trade is active, close crosses below a valid configured B short level
Exit: Long, after entry bar, high >= configured TP1 (partial) OR high >= configured TP2 OR low <= active stop (configured stop, or entry after break-even is active) OR, before TP1, the trade-health hard-exit condition is met
Exit: Short, after entry bar, low <= configured TP1 (partial) OR low <= configured TP2 OR high >= active stop (configured stop, or entry after break-even is active) OR, before TP1, the trade-health hard-exit condition is met
Calculation: Trade-health progress = best favorable excursion since entry as a percentage of distance from entry to TP1; risk used = worst adverse excursion as a percentage of entry-to-stop distance; MFE/MAE = best favorable excursion divided by the greater of worst adverse excursion and minimum price increment; minutes since best MFE = elapsed time since the bar of greatest favorable excursion
Calculation: Expected TP1 progress = age-based expected progress, using separate profiles for with-day-type, balanced, against-day-type, and neutral trades; classify day type from configured text and compare trend direction with position side
Calculation: Trade-health score = progress score (progress / expected progress), efficiency score (MFE/MAE), momentum score (minutes since best MFE), age score, current price-position score, and day-type context score, summed and bounded to 0–100; use the author’s ordered score bands for healthy, OK, warning, critical, and TOT
Calculation: Trade-health hard-exit condition = before TP1, any of: age exceeds the against-day-type or other age limit while TP1 progress is below its limit and risk used exceeds its limit; age exceeds its limit while TP1 progress is below its limit and MFE <= MAE; on a balanced day, age exceeds its limit while TP1 progress is below its limit; on an against-day-type trade, age exceeds its limit while TP1 progress is below its limit; age and time since best MFE both exceed their limits while TP1 progress is below its limit; or age exceeds its limit, current PnL <= 0, TP1 progress is below its limit, and MFE <= MAE; also exit when the trade-health score is TOT
Exit: Long, before TP1, trade-health recommends break-even when any of: TP1 progress and age meet their limits, time since best MFE meets its limit, current PnL > 0, and close is within the entry-return zone; MFE reaches the risk distance, time since best MFE meets its limit, and current PnL > 0; or an against-day-type trade meets its progress, age, and stalling limits with current PnL > 0; then exit if a later bar reaches entry
Exit: Short, before TP1, trade-health recommends break-even when any of: TP1 progress and age meet their limits, time since best MFE meets its limit, current PnL > 0, and close is within the entry-return zone; MFE reaches the risk distance, time since best MFE meets its limit, and current PnL > 0; or an against-day-type trade meets its progress, age, and stalling limits with current PnL > 0; then exit if a later bar reaches entry
