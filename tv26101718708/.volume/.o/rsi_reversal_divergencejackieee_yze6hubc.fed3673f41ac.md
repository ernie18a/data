Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Long: At a newly confirmed pivot low, RSI at that pivot > RSI at the preceding confirmed pivot low AND its low < the preceding pivot low; OR the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bullish (close > open), the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar is bearish (close < open), and current close >= preceding open AND current open <= preceding close; OR the last completed higher-timeframe bar's lower wick > configured fraction of its high-low range AND its body < configured fraction of its high-low range
Short: At a newly confirmed pivot high, RSI at that pivot < RSI at the preceding confirmed pivot high AND its high > the preceding pivot high; OR the last completed higher-timeframe bar is bearish (close < open), the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar is bullish (close > open), and current close <= preceding open AND current open >= preceding close; OR the last completed higher-timeframe bar's upper wick > configured fraction of its high-low range AND its body < configured fraction of its high-low range
