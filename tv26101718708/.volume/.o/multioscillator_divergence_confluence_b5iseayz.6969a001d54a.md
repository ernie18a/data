Calculation: Oscillator set = RSI, MACD line, smoothed Stochastic, CCI, MFI, and smoothed OBV; only enabled oscillators contribute.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish lean counts = number of enabled oscillators whose momentum over a shared lookback is opposite to price momentum over that lookback; bullish requires price falling and oscillator rising, bearish requires price rising and oscillator falling.
Calculation: Pressure = EMA of 50 plus the net lean count scaled by 50/6; net lean count = bullish lean count minus bearish lean count.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot divergence counts = count of enabled oscillators showing the same divergence type at the same confirmed price pivot, compared with the preceding same-side pivot within the configured minimum and maximum pivot spacing. Regular bullish: lower price low and higher oscillator value; hidden bullish: higher price low and lower oscillator value. Regular bearish: higher price high and lower oscillator value; hidden bearish: lower price high and higher oscillator value.
Calculation: Divergence score = oscillator confluence count plus points for applicable EMA context, oscillator exhaustion, volume participation, pivot spacing quality, and regime alignment, with a penalty when fewer than four oscillators are enabled; cap the result to the score range. Regular bullish context is price below the EMA, regular bearish context is price above it; hidden bullish context is price above it, hidden bearish context is price below it. Exhaustion is any enabled RSI, Stochastic, CCI, or MFI in its respective oversold or overbought zone. Pivot spacing quality decreases with distance from the midpoint of the allowed spacing range. Regime alignment is ranging or quiet for regular divergences and trending for hidden divergences.
Calculation: Regime = trending when ADX is at or above its threshold; otherwise volatile when ATR percentile is high, quiet when it is low, and ranging otherwise.
Long: Confirmed bar AND ((pressure crosses above the setup threshold AND bullish lean count >= 2 AND low is within the configured ATR distance of the recent swing low) OR (confirmed price pivot low AND pivot spacing is in range AND ((lower low AND at least the configured minimum oscillator count show regular bullish divergence AND regular bullish score >= the density score threshold) OR (higher low AND at least the configured minimum oscillator count show hidden bullish divergence AND price at pivot > EMA AND hidden bullish score >= the density score threshold)))
Short: Confirmed bar AND ((pressure crosses below 100 minus the setup threshold AND bearish lean count >= 2 AND high is within the configured ATR distance of the recent swing high) OR (confirmed price pivot high AND pivot spacing is in range AND ((higher high AND at least the configured minimum oscillator count show regular bearish divergence AND regular bearish score >= the density score threshold) OR (lower high AND at least the configured minimum oscillator count show hidden bearish divergence AND price at pivot < EMA AND hidden bearish score >= the density score threshold)))
