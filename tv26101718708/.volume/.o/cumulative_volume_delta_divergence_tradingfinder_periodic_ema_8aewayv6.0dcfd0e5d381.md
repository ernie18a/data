Calculation: Candle volume delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) ÷ (high − low); use zero when high = low
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = either the rolling sum or EMA of candle volume delta over a configurable lookback
Long: A confirmed pivot low (n bars on each side) has close n bars earlier above the current EMA(close, 50), and its cumulative volume delta is below zero; the latest two such pivots have negative cumulative volume delta, the latest low is below the previous low, the latest cumulative volume delta is above the previous one, the price difference exceeds the minimum pivot price difference, the latest pivot is fewer than 30 bars old, and the pivots are fewer than 30 bars apart
Short: A confirmed pivot high (n bars on each side) has close n bars earlier below the current EMA(close, 50), and its cumulative volume delta is above zero; the latest two such pivots have positive cumulative volume delta, the latest high is above the previous high, the latest cumulative volume delta is below the previous one, the price difference exceeds the minimum pivot price difference, the latest pivot is fewer than 30 bars old, and the pivots are fewer than 30 bars apart
