Calculation: Bull and bear scores = sum of configurable pillar weights when each pillar is bullish or bearish: (1) confirmed pivot structure: a higher latest swing high or low, with close above the latest swing low; mirrored for lower swings and close below the latest swing high; (2) positive or negative linear-regression slope of OBV; (3) the fraction of three momentum checks that agree: close above/below KAMA, RSI above/below its midpoint, and Williams %R above/below its midpoint; (4) a sweep below the latest confirmed swing low followed by a close above it, or above the latest confirmed swing high followed by a close below it; (5) ATR relative to its longer-term average is within a configurable range and at least two momentum checks agree; (6) current close's position within the current day's developing high-low range, favoring the lower half for bulls and the upper half for bears, with score increasing by distance from midpoint and capped at the pillar weight; (7) close above/below an EMA on a selected higher timeframe, using lookahead off; (8) cumulative signed volume crossing its moving average upward/downward while a composite pressure score of signed-volume ratio, volume relative to its average, and candle body relative to range exceeds its threshold. Use confirmed pivots; compare each new pivot high/low with the preceding pivot of the same type. The OBV slope uses linear regression. The daily range uses bars aggregated from the backtest data and includes the current forming day. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the selected higher-timeframe series with lookahead off (completed bars historically; developing bar in real time). Parameters and weights are configurable.
Calculation: Stop distance = distance from signal close to Supertrend; alternatively the smaller of that distance and an ATR-based cap, a fixed percentage of close, or distance to the latest confirmed swing low for a long / swing high for a short, according to the selected stop mode.
Calculation: Take-profit levels = entry price plus/minus stop distance multiplied by configured reward-to-risk multiples; the backtest exit target is the second take-profit level.
Long: Bull score ≥ configurable threshold AND bull score minus bear score ≥ configurable minimum lead AND (VWMA gate disabled OR close > VWMA) AND (Supertrend gate disabled OR Supertrend is bullish) AND (ribbon gate disabled OR shorter-anchor HMA > longer-anchor HMA) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX > configurable minimum) AND (session gate disabled OR current bar is within the configured session) AND long condition is false on the previous bar AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND bar is confirmed.
Short: Bear score ≥ configurable threshold AND bear score minus bull score ≥ configurable minimum lead AND (VWMA gate disabled OR close < VWMA) AND (Supertrend gate disabled OR Supertrend is bearish) AND (ribbon gate disabled OR shorter-anchor HMA < longer-anchor HMA) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX > configurable minimum) AND (session gate disabled OR current bar is within the configured session) AND short condition is false on the previous bar AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND bar is confirmed.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ second take-profit price.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ second take-profit price.
