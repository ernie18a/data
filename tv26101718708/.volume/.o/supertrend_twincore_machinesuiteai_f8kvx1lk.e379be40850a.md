Calculation: Fast Supertrend = shorter-lookback Supertrend; slow Supertrend = longer-lookback Supertrend. An uptrend is price above its trailing stop; a downtrend is price below it.
Calculation: Active fast Supertrend = adaptive fast Supertrend when its measured win rate exceeds the fixed fast Supertrend’s by the configured margin and both have the configured minimum sample; otherwise use the fixed fast Supertrend.
Calculation: Fast flip count = number of active-fast trend-direction changes in the configured lookback.
Calculation: Measured ADX gate = enabled when the high-ADX candidate win rate exceeds the low-ADX candidate win rate by the configured margin and both have the configured minimum sample; candidate win means the close after the configured outcome horizon is in the candidate’s direction.
Long: Active fast Supertrend is up AND slow Supertrend is up AND they were not both up on the previous bar AND fast flip count is below its suppression threshold AND (measured ADX gate is off OR ADX is not below its threshold) AND (strict higher-timeframe gate is off OR the last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend is up)
Short: Active fast Supertrend is down AND slow Supertrend is down AND they were not both down on the previous bar AND fast flip count is below its suppression threshold AND (measured ADX gate is off OR ADX is not below its threshold) AND (strict higher-timeframe gate is off OR the last completed higher-timeframe Supertrend is down)
Exit: Long, selected stop core flips down OR (fixed-stop mode AND low <= entry price − configured ATR multiple × ATR) OR (fixed-stop mode AND slow Supertrend flips down)
Exit: Short, selected stop core flips up OR (fixed-stop mode AND high >= entry price + configured ATR multiple × ATR) OR (fixed-stop mode AND slow Supertrend flips up)
