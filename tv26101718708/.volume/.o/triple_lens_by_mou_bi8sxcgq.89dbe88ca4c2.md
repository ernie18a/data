Calculation: Net accumulation = count over the last 25 chart bars of bars whose close rose by more than the configured daily-change threshold and whose volume exceeded the 50-bar average volume, minus count of bars whose close fell by more than that threshold and whose volume exceeded that average.
Calculation: 30-week SMA slope = current 30-week simple moving average of close minus that average from the configured number of weeks earlier; use the current weekly bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Breadth recovery = the S5FI daily close is above the recovery threshold and its lowest value over the last 30 daily bars is below the low threshold; use the current daily bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Breadth bearish = the S5FI daily close is below the recovery threshold and its highest value over the last 30 daily bars is above the overheat threshold; use the current daily bar, which may still be forming.
Long: current close > current 30-week SMA AND 30-week SMA slope > 0 AND net accumulation >= accumulation threshold AND breadth recovery AND NOT (the same conditions held on the previous chart bar)
Exit: long, (current close > current 30-week SMA AND 30-week SMA slope <= 0 AND distribution-day count over the last 25 chart bars >= distribution warning threshold) OR (current close < current 30-week SMA AND previous chart bar satisfied current close > current 30-week SMA AND 30-week SMA slope > 0) OR breadth bearish; AND NOT (the same exit conditions held on the previous chart bar)
