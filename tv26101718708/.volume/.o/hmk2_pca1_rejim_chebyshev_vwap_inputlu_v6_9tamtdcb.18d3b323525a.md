Calculation: PCA-1 = mean of the z-scores of RSI(14), MFI(14), and ROC(5), each using the same lookback; each z-score is (current value − lookback mean) / lookback standard deviation, or 0 when the standard deviation is 0
Long: PCA-1 crosses above the threshold AND EMA(close, z-score lookback) > its previous value AND EMA(ADX > 20 ? 1 : 0, regime lookback) > 0.50 AND the return over its return lookback is strictly between its rolling mean ± factor × rolling standard deviation over the independently parameterized averaging lookback, with standard deviation > 0 AND close >= VWAP
Short: PCA-1 crosses below the negative threshold AND EMA(close, z-score lookback) < its previous value AND EMA(ADX > 20 ? 1 : 0, regime lookback) > 0.50 AND the return over its return lookback is strictly between its rolling mean ± factor × rolling standard deviation over the independently parameterized averaging lookback, with standard deviation > 0 AND close <= VWAP
Exit: long, close crosses below VWAP OR PCA-1 crosses below 0 OR low <= entry price − ATR × stop factor OR high >= entry price + ATR × target factor
Exit: short, close crosses above VWAP OR PCA-1 crosses above 0 OR high >= entry price + ATR × stop factor OR low <= entry price − ATR × target factor
