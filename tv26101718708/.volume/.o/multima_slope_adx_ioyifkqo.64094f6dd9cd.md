Calculation: Selected MA = the configured ALMA, EMA, VWMA, Hull MA, or price-mapped TSI; price-mapped TSI = EMA(close) × (1 + TSI(close))
Calculation: MA slope = selected MA − selected MA from the configured number of bars earlier
Calculation: Slope acceleration = MA slope has increased (long) or decreased (short) on each of the configured consecutive bars
Calculation: KSI = true range² ÷ (volume × EMA(volume)); KSI signal = EMA(KSI)
Calculation: Dynamic stop = for a long, the higher of the initial stop at entry price × (1 − ATR-based stop distance) and the highest high since entry × (1 − ATR-based stop distance); for a short, the lower of the initial stop at entry price × (1 + ATR-based stop distance) and the lowest low since entry × (1 + ATR-based stop distance)
Long: On a confirmed bar, flat or short, and cooldown since the last exit has passed, and ADX is at or above its configured threshold (and rising if required), and the configured KSI filter passes, and either MA slope crosses above zero (slope-entry mode), or close crosses above the selected MA (price-cross-entry mode), or mid-trend re-entry is enabled and MA slope is positive, above its value at the last exit, rising, and passes slope acceleration
Short: On a confirmed bar, flat or long, and cooldown since the last exit has passed, and ADX is at or above its configured threshold (and rising if required), and the configured KSI filter passes, and either MA slope crosses below zero (slope-entry mode), or close crosses below the selected MA (price-cross-entry mode), or mid-trend re-entry is enabled and MA slope is negative, below its value at the last exit, falling, and passes slope acceleration
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar if close crosses below the selected MA, or close crosses below VWMA, or MA slope crosses below zero, or MA slope is positive but has decreased for the configured consecutive bars, or a configured KSI spike occurs; or intrabar if low reaches the dynamic stop, configured take-profit price, or configured trailing stop
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar if close crosses above the selected MA, or close crosses above VWMA, or MA slope crosses above zero, or MA slope is negative but has increased for the configured consecutive bars, or a configured KSI spike occurs; or intrabar if high reaches the dynamic stop, configured take-profit price, or configured trailing stop
