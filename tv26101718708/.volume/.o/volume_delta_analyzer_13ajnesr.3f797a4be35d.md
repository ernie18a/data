Calculation: Volume delta = ((close − open) / (high − low)) × volume; use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of volume delta.
Calculation: Normalized cumulative volume delta = (cumulative volume delta − lowest cumulative volume delta over its lookback) / (highest − lowest) over the same lookback; use 0.5 when the range is 0.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a low/high that is lower/higher than the surrounding bars over the pivot confirmation lookback on each side; confirm after the bars on the right have completed.
Long: Volume delta >= 0 OR normalized cumulative volume delta >= its prior-bar value OR (volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) <= maximum body ratio AND close >= high − 0.35 × (high − low) AND volume delta < 0) OR (latest confirmed pivot low < preceding confirmed pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at latest pivot > cumulative volume delta at preceding pivot AND pivot gap is between the pivot confirmation lookback and maximum pivot distance AND absolute pivot-price difference >= ATR × minimum swing multiple AND bullish-exhaustion cooldown has elapsed) OR (latest confirmed pivot low > preceding confirmed pivot low AND cumulative volume delta at latest pivot < cumulative volume delta at preceding pivot AND pivot gap is between the pivot confirmation lookback and maximum pivot distance AND absolute pivot-price difference >= ATR × minimum swing multiple AND bullish-absorption cooldown has elapsed)
