Calculation: Candle body = absolute difference between the candle’s close and open
Calculation: ROC = percentage change in close over its lookback on the selected timeframe
Long: On any dashboard timeframe, the current forming candle or the last completed candle closes above its open
Short: On any dashboard timeframe, the current forming candle or the last completed candle closes below its open
Long: On the 1-hour, 4-hour, or daily timeframe, the last completed candle closes above its open and the candle before it closes below its open, with both candle bodies at least the timeframe’s ATR multiplied by the minimum-body multiplier
Short: On the 1-hour, 4-hour, or daily timeframe, the last completed candle closes below its open and the candle before it closes above its open, with both candle bodies at least the timeframe’s ATR multiplied by the minimum-body multiplier
Long: ROC is positive OR ROC crosses above zero from a previous value at or below zero OR ROC crosses above its configured upper threshold from a previous value at or below that threshold
Short: ROC is negative OR ROC crosses below zero from a previous value at or above zero OR ROC crosses below its configured lower threshold from a previous value at or above that threshold
