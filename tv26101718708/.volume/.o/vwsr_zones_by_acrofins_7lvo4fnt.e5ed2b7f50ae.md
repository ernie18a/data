Calculation: ATR = average true range on the signal timeframe.
Calculation: Pivot zones = confirm swing highs and lows using equal left and right lookbacks. For each pivot, score = min(100, 10 + volume score + reaction score); volume score is 20 by default, otherwise min(40, max(0, pivot-bar volume / prior-bar volume average × 20)); reaction score = min(30, max(0, greatest move away during the following reaction window / ATR at pivot × 10)). Create a zone around the pivot price with bounds ±0.15 × ATR. Merge into the closest unbroken zone of the same type when its midpoint is within merge-distance × ATR; expand bounds to include pivot price ±0.15 × ATR, increment touches, and increase score by 0.3 × new pivot score + 0.5 × min(20, √touch count × 4), capped at 100. On each confirmed bar, decay scores by age-decay-rate × 0.1; remove zones that exceed maximum age, fall below score 5, or remain broken more than twice the maximum retest window. If over the zone limit, remove lowest-scored zones.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when higher-timeframe close and fast EMA are both above the slow EMA; bearish when both are below; otherwise neutral. Use the higher-timeframe bar supplied with lookahead off (last completed on historical bars; may be forming in real time); fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Break filters = if enabled, volume ≥ prior-bar volume average × volume multiplier; if enabled, volume ≤ prior-bar volume average × volume cap; if enabled, high − low ≥ ATR × momentum multiplier. A close-confirmation distance, when enabled, is ATR × configured distance.
Calculation: Bull break = on a confirmed bar, close > an unbroken resistance-zone top + close-confirmation distance and previous close ≤ that zone top, with break filters passing. Bear break = close < an unbroken support-zone bottom − close-confirmation distance and previous close ≥ that zone bottom, with break filters passing. If multiple zones qualify in one direction, select the highest-scored; if both directions qualify, select the higher-scored direction (bull wins ties).
Calculation: Bull retest = within the configured minimum-to-maximum bars after a bullish break, low ≤ broken zone top, close > broken zone top, and close − low ≥ ATR × minimum reaction. Bear retest = within that window after a bearish break, high ≥ broken zone bottom, close < broken zone bottom, and high − close ≥ ATR × minimum reaction. If mitigation filtering is enabled, a broken zone can signal only one retest. Track up to six pending broken zones, removing the oldest when full.
Long: bullish break OR bullish retest, with break filters applied to breaks and the retest window and reaction conditions applied to retests.
Short: bearish break OR bearish retest, with break filters applied to breaks and the retest window and reaction conditions applied to retests.
Long: bullish break or retest, no position already open, risk management enabled, and entry gates pass: session and opening buffer, expiry-day cutoff, intraday entry cutoff, daily loss cap, and (if configured) no counter-trend entry. Counter-trend means bullish signal while higher-timeframe trend is bearish.
Short: bearish break or retest, no position already open, risk management enabled, and the same entry gates pass; counter-trend means bearish signal while higher-timeframe trend is bullish.
Exit: long, after at least one bar from entry, low ≤ stop-loss OR high ≥ third profit target OR intraday force-close time reached.
Exit: short, after at least one bar from entry, high ≥ stop-loss OR low ≤ third profit target OR intraday force-close time reached.
