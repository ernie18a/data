Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of: bullish fair value gap (current low > high two bars ago); bullish change of character (close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high); bullish smart bias; Supertrend uptrend; close above the 0.5 retracement between the latest confirmed pivot high and low; positive cumulative volume delta; close above the point of control.
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = count of: bearish fair value gap (current high < low two bars ago); bearish change of character (close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low); bearish smart bias; Supertrend downtrend; close below the 0.5 retracement between the latest confirmed pivot high and low; negative cumulative volume delta; close below the point of control.
Calculation: Smart bias = bullish when the higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and execution-timeframe VSA is not bearish; bearish when the higher-timeframe close is below its EMA and execution-timeframe VSA is not bullish; otherwise neutral. VSA is bullish or bearish when spread and volume exceed their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its average by the configured relative-volume threshold, with direction from close versus open. Use the higher-timeframe bar available at the current execution bar (completed historical bar; developing bar in real time).
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative volume signed positive when close > open and negative when close < open, resetting at the daily session boundary when daily reset is selected.
Calculation: Point of control = midpoint of the equal-width price bin with the most volume, aggregating typical-price volume over the configured lookback of execution-timeframe bars.
Long: Current low > high two bars ago
Short: Current high < low two bars ago
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low
Long: Bullish confluence score ≥ configured sensitivity AND volume > its moving average when the volume filter is enabled AND absolute candle body > half the ATR when the volatility filter is enabled
Short: Bearish confluence score ≥ configured sensitivity AND volume > its moving average when the volume filter is enabled AND absolute candle body > half the ATR when the volatility filter is enabled
