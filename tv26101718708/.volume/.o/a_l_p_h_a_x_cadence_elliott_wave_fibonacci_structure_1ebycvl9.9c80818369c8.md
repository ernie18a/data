Calculation: Confirmed swing sequence = A swing high/low is confirmed after the configured number of bars on each side; retain alternating pivots, replacing the latest pivot if a same-direction pivot arrives before an opposite pivot and its price change is less than the minimum leg size (ATR × configured multiplier); otherwise add it, retaining only the configured number of swings.
Calculation: Wave phase and impulse points = From the latest three alternating swings, classify W2 when they form low-high-low with the high above the first low, or high-low-high with the high below the first high. From the latest five alternating swings, classify W4 when they form low-high-low-high-low with W3 > W1 > W0, or high-low-high-low-high with W3 < W1 < W0; use W0–W4 as those five points. W4 classification takes precedence. Use confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Market structure = Bullish after price breaks above the latest confirmed swing high, bearish after it breaks below the latest confirmed swing low; use the close for breaks when close-only mode is enabled, otherwise use the high or low. A break sets that direction until an opposite break.
Calculation: Bullish golden zone = W1 − (W1 − W0) × configured retracement bounds for W2; for W4 use W3 − (W3 − W2) × the same bounds. Bearish zones mirror these calculations above W1 using W0 − W1 for W2 and W2 − W3 for W4. The zone spans the configured lower and upper retracement fractions.
Calculation: Bull trigger = A bullish candle whose close is more than the configured fraction of its range above its low, or a bullish engulfing candle (current close ≥ prior open and prior candle bearish). Bear trigger = A bearish candle whose close is more than the configured fraction of its range below its high, or a bearish engulfing candle (current close ≤ prior open and prior candle bullish).
Calculation: Bull zone rejection = Low is inside the bullish golden zone, close is at or above its bottom, and a bull trigger is present. Bear zone rejection = High is inside the bearish golden zone, close is at or below its top, and a bear trigger is present.
Calculation: HTF bias = On the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA for bullish bias; fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA for bearish bias.
Calculation: Layer score = Sum one point each for setup-specific structure alignment, setup-specific Fibonacci-zone confirmation, setup-specific trigger confirmation, HTF alignment (or one neutral point when HTF bias is disabled), volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier (or true when disabled), Choppiness Index below its configured maximum (or true when disabled), RSI above its configured long minimum/below its configured short maximum (or true when disabled), close above/below its EMA (or true when disabled), RSI rising/falling (or true when disabled), and one base point. W3 uses true for Fibonacci and trigger layers; ABC uses true for Fibonacci.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = At a confirmed price pivot high and confirmed RSI pivot high, price pivot high exceeds its preceding pivot high and RSI pivot value is below its preceding pivot value, with bearish structure. Bearish RSI divergence = At confirmed price and RSI pivot lows, price pivot low is below its preceding pivot low and RSI pivot value is above its preceding pivot value, with bullish structure. Compare pivots within the configured divergence lookback.
Long: Bar is confirmed AND signal cooldown has elapsed AND Choppiness Index is below its configured maximum (or chop filter disabled) AND HTF bias is bullish (or hard HTF alignment disabled) AND setup layer score meets its configured minimum AND any of: W2 phase AND bullish impulse AND (bullish structure OR neutral structure with bullish HTF bias) AND bullish W2 zone rejection AND W2 > W0 AND W1 − W0 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; W4 phase AND bullish impulse AND bullish structure AND bullish W4 zone rejection AND W1 − W0 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; W2 or W4 phase AND close crosses above the latest swing high from a low-high swing pair AND (bullish structure OR neutral structure with bullish HTF bias) AND W1 − W0 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; ABC setup enabled AND latest three swings form low-high-low AND bullish structure AND low reaches or passes the 61.8% retracement of the high-to-low leg AND close recovers above that level AND bull trigger.
Short: Bar is confirmed AND signal cooldown has elapsed AND Choppiness Index is below its configured maximum (or chop filter disabled) AND HTF bias is bearish (or hard HTF alignment disabled) AND setup layer score meets its configured minimum AND any of: W2 phase AND bearish impulse AND (bearish structure OR neutral structure with bearish HTF bias) AND bearish W2 zone rejection AND W2 < W0 AND W0 − W1 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; W4 phase AND bearish impulse AND bearish structure AND bearish W4 zone rejection AND W0 − W1 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; W2 or W4 phase AND close crosses below the latest swing low from a high-low swing pair AND (bearish structure OR neutral structure with bearish HTF bias) AND W0 − W1 ≥ ATR × configured minimum-leg multiplier; ABC setup enabled AND latest three swings form high-low-high AND bearish structure AND high reaches or exceeds the 61.8% retracement of the high-to-low leg AND close falls below that level AND bear trigger.
Exit: Long, bearish RSI divergence.
Exit: Short, bullish RSI divergence.
