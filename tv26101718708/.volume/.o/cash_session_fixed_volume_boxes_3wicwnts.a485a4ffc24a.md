Calculation: Current box volume = sum of volume from the first bar of the current cash-session box through the current bar; reset after reaching the configured volume threshold and at cash-session boundaries.
Calculation: Current box open = open of the first bar of the current box.
Long: Current bar is in the configured cash session AND current box volume >= configured volume threshold AND close > current box open
Short: Current bar is in the configured cash session AND current box volume >= configured volume threshold AND close < current box open
