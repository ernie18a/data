Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on each side; use the latest unbroken confirmed swing.
Calculation: Bullish order block = after price breaks above the latest unbroken swing high, find the lowest low from the prior bar back to the lesser of the swing’s age and the search limit. Use that candle as the origin, or the first bearish candle within the next three older bars if the extreme candle is bullish and the last-opposite-candle option is selected. The zone spans the origin candle’s high to the lower of its low and the move’s lowest low (or the configured body range). Qualify only if the zone remains intact, its height is within the configured ATR limit, it does not overlap an active bullish order block when overlap filtering is enabled, and enabled quality filters pass. Displacement means at least one candle from the origin to the break has an absolute open-close body at least the configured multiple of ATR. Other score components are a fair value gap in the move, the move’s extreme exceeding the prior confirmed opposite swing, and origin-candle volume at least the configured multiple of its 20-bar average; apply the configured minimum score.
Calculation: Bearish order block = after price breaks below the latest unbroken swing low, find the highest high from the prior bar back to the lesser of the swing’s age and the search limit. Use that candle as the origin, or the first bullish candle within the next three older bars if the extreme candle is bearish and the last-opposite-candle option is selected. The zone spans the higher of the origin candle’s high and the move’s highest high to the origin candle’s low (or the configured body range). Qualify only if the zone remains intact, its height is within the configured ATR limit, it does not overlap an active bearish order block when overlap filtering is enabled, and enabled quality filters pass. Displacement means at least one candle from the origin to the break has an absolute open-close body at least the configured multiple of ATR. Other score components are a fair value gap in the move, the move’s extreme exceeding the prior confirmed opposite swing, and origin-candle volume at least the configured multiple of its 20-bar average; apply the configured minimum score.
Long: a qualifying bullish order block forms OR an active bearish order block fails (price crosses above its top using the configured close-or-wick rule, and any enabled displacement condition passes) OR an active bullish order block or bullish breaker is retested (low <= zone top AND prior low > zone top).
Short: a qualifying bearish order block forms OR an active bullish order block fails (price crosses below its bottom using the configured close-or-wick rule, and any enabled displacement condition passes) OR an active bearish order block or bearish breaker is retested (high >= zone bottom AND prior high < zone bottom).
