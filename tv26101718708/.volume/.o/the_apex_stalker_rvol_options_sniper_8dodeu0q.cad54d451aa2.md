Calculation: RVOL = current scan-timeframe bar volume divided by the moving average of volume over a lookback on scan-timeframe bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Percent change = (current scan-timeframe close − previous completed scan-timeframe close) / previous completed scan-timeframe close × 100.
Calculation: ATR percentage = absolute change between the current, still-forming macro-timeframe close and the previous completed macro-timeframe close, divided by ATR on the current, still-forming macro-timeframe bar, × 100.
Long: RVOL ≥ its threshold AND Percent change > 0 AND ATR percentage < its threshold AND current, still-forming macro-timeframe close > its EMA.
Short: RVOL ≥ its threshold AND ATR percentage ≥ its threshold
