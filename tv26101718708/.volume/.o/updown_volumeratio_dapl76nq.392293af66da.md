Calculation: Log up/down volume ratio = natural log of rolling summed volume on bars whose close is above the prior close divided by rolling summed volume on bars whose close is below the prior close, using the same lookback for both sums
Calculation: Smoothed log ratio = EMA of the log up/down volume ratio
Long: Log up/down volume ratio crosses above smoothed log ratio
Short: Log up/down volume ratio crosses below smoothed log ratio
