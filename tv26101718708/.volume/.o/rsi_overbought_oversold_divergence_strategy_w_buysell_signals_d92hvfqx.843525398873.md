Calculation: Oscillator = selected RSI, OBV, RVSI, MFI, or RSI of the triple-EMA log-source rate of change; RVSI is RSI of an EMA of cumulative(change(source) × volume)
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at any configured oscillator pivot-low left lookback (only the first if that option is enabled), the confirmed pivot is a lower low in price and higher low in oscillator than the prior pivot, with the prior pivot within the configured bar range; hidden bullish divergence = higher low in price and lower low in oscillator under the same pivot and range rules. Pivot values are from the pivot bar, confirmed after the configured right lookback.
Calculation: Long filters = each enabled filter passes: close lies within at least one enabled manual price interval; close is below the enabled upper SMA and above the enabled lower SMA; close satisfies each enabled anchored VWAP or one-standard-deviation band bound; the enabled upper Fibonacci level is above the two-bar lowest low and the enabled lower Fibonacci level is below the two-bar highest low; enabled uptrend filter has a rising EMA; and, when the oscillator limit filter is enabled, its recent low is below the oversold threshold and its current value is below the maximum bullish value. Disabled filters pass.
Calculation: Delayed bullish signal = after a bullish divergence, within the next 10 bars, the enabled gates pass: oscillator exceeds its EMA by more than 1 within the configured cross lookback; close exceeds the configured EMA; and the configured high-minus-low bounce exceeds its threshold. Emit only the first qualifying signal for that divergence. If no delay option is enabled, use the divergence bar directly.
Long: (regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence) AND long filters AND (delayed bullish signal if any delay option is enabled, otherwise bullish divergence occurs on this bar)
Exit: long, high >= entry price × (1 + configured take-profit fraction) OR (stop loss enabled and close/price reaches the configured stop: entry price × (1 − configured static stop fraction), or, in low-based mode during the first two bars after entry and signal, lowest low over the configured lookback adjusted by the configured dollar or percentage offset)
