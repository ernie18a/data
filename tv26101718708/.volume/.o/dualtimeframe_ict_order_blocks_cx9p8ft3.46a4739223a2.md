Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low exceeding the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes available after the right-side bars complete, on the primary order-block timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = after a completed bar closes above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high, select the lowest-low bar since that swing that swept below the most recent earlier confirmed swing low; its high and low define the zone. Bearish order-block zone = after a completed bar closes below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low, select the highest-high bar since that swing that swept above the most recent earlier confirmed swing high; its high and low define the zone. Apply the configured optional fair-value-gap and multi-candle-zone rules, and reject zones exceeding the configured ATR-based size limit. Use primary timeframe bars (chart timeframe if no custom timeframe); use confirmed swing points and completed bars for break and zone formation.
Long: An active primary bullish order-block zone exists AND the current bar is after its creation AND its low is at or below the zone's lower boundary plus 75% of its height AND its high is at or above the zone's lower boundary plus 25% of its height.
Short: An active primary bearish order-block zone exists AND the current bar is after its creation AND its low is at or below the zone's lower boundary plus 75% of its height AND its high is at or above the zone's lower boundary plus 25% of its height.
