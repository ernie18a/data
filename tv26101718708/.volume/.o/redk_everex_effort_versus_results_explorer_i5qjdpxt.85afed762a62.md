Calculation: Normalize(x, average) = 100 if x/average > 1.50; 90 if > 1.20; 80 if > 1.00; 70 if > 0.80; 60 if > 0.60; 50 if > 0.40; 25 if > 0.20; otherwise 10
Calculation: Relative volume = 100 if volume is missing; otherwise Normalize(volume, average volume over the lookback). Use either SMA or the RROF average type, as selected.
Calculation: Relative price strength = the average of six values: (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1) × 100; (close − open)/(high − low) × 100; sign(close − open) × Normalize(abs(close − open), average absolute spread over the lookback); (2 × (close − lowest low over current and previous bar)/(highest high over current and previous bar − lowest low over those bars) − 1) × 100; (close − previous close)/(highest high over current and previous bar − lowest low over those bars) × 100; sign(close − previous close) × Normalize(abs(close − previous close), average absolute close change over the lookback). Use the same selected averaging method for the three lookback averages.
Calculation: Relative flow = relative price strength × relative volume / 100. Bull flow = max(relative flow, 0); bear flow = max(−relative flow, 0). RROF = 2 × (100 − 100/(1 + average bull flow / average bear flow)) − 100, using the same averaging type and length for both. Smoothed RROF = WMA of RROF over the smoothing length.
Calculation: Relative price = relative price strength clamped to [−100, 100]. Relative volume band = relative volume clamped to [−100, 100]. Price-to-volume ratio = 100 × abs(relative price) / relative volume band.
Calculation: Bias = 2 × (100 − 100/(1 + average bull flow / average bear flow)) − 100, using the same averaging type and a longer averaging length than RROF. Smoothed bias = WMA of bias over the smoothing length.
Long: Smoothed RROF > 0 AND previous smoothed RROF ≤ 0 OR smoothed RROF ≥ 0 OR smoothed bias ≥ 0 OR relative price > 0 AND (price-to-volume ratio ≤ 50 OR (abs(relative price) ≥ 80 AND relative volume band ≥ 80) OR (price-to-volume ratio ≥ 120 AND NOT (abs(relative price) ≥ 80 AND relative volume band ≥ 80)))
