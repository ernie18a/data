Calculation: simple moving average of RSI(close) = arithmetic mean of RSI(close) over its lookback
Long: simple moving average of RSI(close) > its value on the previous bar
Short: simple moving average of RSI(close) < its value on the previous bar
Exit: long, simple moving average of RSI(close) < its value on the previous bar
Exit: short, simple moving average of RSI(close) > its value on the previous bar
