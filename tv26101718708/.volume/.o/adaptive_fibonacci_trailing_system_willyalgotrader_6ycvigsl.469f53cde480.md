Calculation: Valid swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low with equal left/right lookback, accepted only when its score meets the configured minimum; score = distance to the latest earlier opposing valid swing (or ATR at the pivot if none exists) / ATR at the pivot × max(volume / 20-bar average volume, 0.5), using 1 when volume is unavailable. Keep the latest five valid swings.
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = latest valid high minus (latest valid high − latest valid low) × 0.382, 0.5, or 0.618; use only when both anchors exist and high > low.
Calculation: Regime = volatile when ATR(14) / its 50-bar average reaches the configured volatility threshold; remain volatile until below its hysteresis exit threshold, then classify as trending if ADX reaches its configured trend threshold, otherwise ranging. Outside volatile state, enter trending at the trend threshold and leave trending below its hysteresis exit threshold; otherwise retain the prior regime. Update on confirmed bars.
Calculation: Breakout confirmation = absolute candle body / (high − low) meets its configured minimum AND close penetrates the breakout level by more than the configured ATR(14) multiple; a zero-range candle has body ratio 0. Regime gate passes when trending, when volatile and breakout confirmation passes, or when ranging and ADX reaches its configured fallback threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive trailing stop = use the latest swing range and ATR(14)/50-bar average ATR ratio; choose retracement 0.618 in trending, 0.5 in volatile, and 0.382 in ranging (or the configured fixed retracement). Adjust retracement distance by clamp(1 / volatility ratio, 0.7, 1.5). Bull stop = swing low + range × (1 − retracement) × adjustment; bear stop = swing high − that distance. Ratchet a long stop upward only and a short stop downward only. Initialize direction long when close > Fib 0.5, otherwise short. Flip long to short after close below its stop with breakout confirmation and regime gate; flip short to long after close above its stop with those confirmations. A configured consecutive wrong-side-bar limit may force the flip.
Calculation: Confidence score = 100 × [0.20 × clamp(anchor swing score / 2, 0, 1) + 0.20 × regime-alignment score + 0.15 × clamp(ADX / 40, 0, 1) + 0.15 × volume score + 0.15 × volatility score + 0.15 × confluence score]. Use latest swing low for long and high for short. Volume score is 0.5 without reliable volume; otherwise volume/20-bar average ramps linearly to 1 at 1.5, stays 1 through 3, then declines linearly to a 0.3 floor at 5. Volatility score is a linear tent with score 0 at ratio 0.3 and 2.5 and score 1 at 1. Regime-alignment score: trending = 1 if trail direction agrees, otherwise 0.3 (or 0.7/0.6/0.5 for cross mode with strict/relaxed/none); volatile = 0.6 if close is on the direction's side of SMA(50), otherwise 0.4; ranging = in cross mode clamp(cross-mode base score − 0.1 + 0.2 if SMA(50) agrees, 0, 1), where base is 0.7/0.6/0.5 for strict/relaxed/none; otherwise 0.5 if SMA(50) agrees, else 0.3. Confluence score = clamp(1 − abs(reference level − SMA(50)) / ATR(14) / 2, 0, 1), using Fib 0.5 in cross mode or the direction's selected trailing stop in trail mode; default to 0.5 if unavailable.
Long: confirmed bar and warmed up AND no active position AND (configured mode is Trail Flip and trailing direction just changed to long OR configured mode is 0.5 Cross and previous close <= current Fib 0.5 AND close > current Fib 0.5 AND (strictness is None OR breakout confirmation at Fib 0.5 AND (strictness is Relaxed OR regime gate passes))) AND (confidence filter disabled OR long confidence score >= configured minimum).
Short: confirmed bar and warmed up AND no active position AND (configured mode is Trail Flip and trailing direction just changed to short OR configured mode is 0.5 Cross and previous close >= current Fib 0.5 AND close < current Fib 0.5 AND (strictness is None OR breakout confirmation at Fib 0.5 AND (strictness is Relaxed OR regime gate passes))) AND (confidence filter disabled OR short confidence score >= configured minimum).
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= take-profit 3; after take-profit 1 is first reached without the stop being hit on that bar, active stop becomes entry price from the next bar.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= take-profit 3; after take-profit 1 is first reached without the stop being hit on that bar, active stop becomes entry price from the next bar.
