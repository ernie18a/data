Calculation: Tenkan-Sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback than Kijun-Sen
Calculation: Kijun-Sen = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback than Tenkan-Sen
Calculation: Senkou Span A = average of Tenkan-Sen and Kijun-Sen
Calculation: Senkou Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback longer than Kijun-Sen
Calculation: Cloud high/low = maximum/minimum of Senkou Span A and Senkou Span B from the prior bar at the cloud displacement offset
Calculation: Chikou comparison = current close compared with close at the Chikou offset
Calculation: CMF = sum of ((2 × close − low − high) / (high − low)) × volume, divided by sum of volume over its lookback; use zero for the numerator when close equals both high and low or high equals low
Long: Tenkan-Sen > Kijun-Sen AND close > close at the Chikou offset AND close > cloud high AND MACD histogram > 0 AND CMF > 0.1 AND TSI > 0
Short: Tenkan-Sen < Kijun-Sen AND close < close at the Chikou offset AND close < cloud low AND MACD histogram < 0 AND CMF < −0.1 AND TSI < 0
Exit: long, Tenkan-Sen < Kijun-Sen AND close < close at the Chikou offset AND close < cloud low AND MACD histogram < 0 AND CMF < −0.1 AND TSI < 0
Exit: short, Tenkan-Sen > Kijun-Sen AND close > close at the Chikou offset AND close > cloud high AND MACD histogram > 0 AND CMF > 0.1 AND TSI > 0
