Calculation: Bullish FVG = current bar low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current bar high < low two bars earlier, on the chart timeframe and on the 1, 5, 15, 30, and 60 minute bars used below. Use each timeframe's current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish EMA bias = shorter-lookback EMA of close > longer-lookback EMA of close; bearish EMA bias = shorter-lookback EMA of close <= longer-lookback EMA of close. Use current bars on the 1, 5, 15, 30, and 60 minute timeframes; the shorter EMA lookback is less than the longer one, and the bars may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of the five timeframes with bullish EMA bias; bearish confluence score = count with bearish EMA bias.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot with equal lookback on both sides, confirmed after the bars on the right have completed. Use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = chart-timeframe close > latest confirmed, unbroken swing high; bearish BOS = chart-timeframe close < latest confirmed, unbroken swing low. A broken swing is cleared until another pivot is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish iFVG trigger = a previously formed bullish FVG remains active and chart-timeframe close < its upper boundary; bearish iFVG trigger = a previously formed bearish FVG remains active and chart-timeframe close > its lower boundary. A trigger clears that active FVG; a newly formed FVG becomes active.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = previous bar closed below its open, current bar closes above its open, current close >= previous open, and current open <= previous close. Bearish engulfing is the inverse.
Calculation: Hammer = lower wick >= 2 × body, upper wick <= 0.5 × body, and close > open. Shooting star = upper wick >= 2 × body, lower wick <= 0.5 × body, and close < open.
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = lower wick >= 0.6 × bar range and body <= 0.25 × bar range; bearish pin bar = upper wick >= 0.6 × bar range and body <= 0.25 × bar range.
Long: bullish FVG OR bullish iFVG trigger OR bullish BOS OR bullish engulfing OR hammer OR bullish pin bar OR (selected signal method is SMC confluence AND bullish confluence score >= 3 AND at least one bullish FVG on the 1, 5, or 15 minute current bars AND (bullish engulfing OR hammer OR bullish pin bar) AND a swing low is confirmed on the current chart bar) OR (selected signal method is FVG + Swing AND chart-timeframe bullish FVG AND a swing low is confirmed on the current chart bar AND close > latest confirmed swing low) OR (selected signal method is BOS + FVG AND bullish BOS AND chart-timeframe bullish FVG)
Short: bearish FVG OR bearish iFVG trigger OR bearish BOS OR bearish engulfing OR shooting star OR bearish pin bar OR (selected signal method is SMC confluence AND bearish confluence score >= 3 AND at least one bearish FVG on the 1, 5, or 15 minute current bars AND (bearish engulfing OR shooting star OR bearish pin bar) AND a swing high is confirmed on the current chart bar) OR (selected signal method is FVG + Swing AND chart-timeframe bearish FVG AND a swing high is confirmed on the current chart bar AND close < latest confirmed swing high) OR (selected signal method is BOS + FVG AND bearish BOS AND chart-timeframe bearish FVG)
