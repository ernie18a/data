Calculation: Ratio = Asset A close / Asset B close
Calculation: Confluence score = 10 × (sum of each of the nine engine scores × its asset-class weight) / sum of the nine weights; each engine score is +1, 0, or −1 as follows: relative strength is +1 when the Z-score of Asset A ROC minus Asset B ROC is >0.5 and the ratio is above its EMA, −1 when the Z-score is <−0.5 and the ratio is below its EMA; trend is +1 for a bullish EMA stack or ratio above the slow and macro EMAs, with positive 20-bar linear-regression slope and any enabled higher-timeframe confirmation, −1 for the reverse; momentum is +1 when volume-weighted RSI >52 and MACD histogram >0, −1 when RSI <48 and histogram <0; volatility is +1 when BB width exceeds its 100-bar average, ATR percentile exceeds 0.6 and ratio is above the BB basis, or on squeeze release above the Keltner basis, −1 for the reverse; statistical extremes are +1 when ratio Z-score is below −1.5 and percentile below 0.20, −1 when Z-score is above 1.5 and percentile above 0.80; macro, liquidity and correlation scores follow their configured asset-class direction rules; volume participation is +1 when OBV slope is positive, ratio is above its fast EMA and Asset A relative volume exceeds Asset B, −1 for the reverse. Use current-timeframe bars; higher-timeframe trend confirmation uses the selected asset’s close and 50-period EMA on the selected higher timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = ratio forms a lower pivot low while volume-weighted RSI forms a higher pivot low; bearish divergence = ratio forms a higher pivot high while RSI forms a lower pivot high. Compare successive confirmed pivots.
Long: Confluence score ≥ moderate-signal threshold OR (bullish divergence AND Confluence score > 0)
Short: Confluence score ≤ −moderate-signal threshold OR (bearish divergence AND Confluence score < 0)
