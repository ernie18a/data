Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price (high + low + close)/3, reset at the selected session, week, or month boundary; use current chart bars, including the developing bar.
Calculation: VWAP deviation bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus a standard deviation multiplier times the square root of the nonnegative volume-weighted variance of typical price; SD2 uses a larger multiplier than SD1.
Calculation: Zone bounds = each VWAP or deviation-band center plus or minus its configured ATR width; ATR uses current chart bars, including the developing bar.
Calculation: Zone CVD percentage = 100 × accumulated buy volume / (accumulated buy volume + accumulated sell volume) for that zone. On each chart bar whose close is within the zone and whose volume meets the minimum, add the selected CVD-timeframe bar's volume to buy volume when its delta is positive, or to sell volume when negative; delta = timeframe volume × (2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, a small positive value). Reset accumulators daily when CVD reset is enabled. The selected timeframe is requested with lookahead off: use the value available on each chart bar; its bar may still be forming in realtime, while historical values use the available completed bar.
Long: close is within the VWAP zone AND volume meets the minimum AND VWAP-zone CVD percentage ≥ the bullish threshold
Long: close is within either lower SD1 or lower SD2 zone AND volume meets the minimum AND that zone's CVD percentage ≥ the bullish threshold
Short: close is within the VWAP zone AND volume meets the minimum AND VWAP-zone CVD percentage ≤ 100 − the bullish threshold
Short: close is within either upper SD1 or upper SD2 zone AND volume meets the minimum AND that zone's CVD percentage ≤ 100 − the bullish threshold
