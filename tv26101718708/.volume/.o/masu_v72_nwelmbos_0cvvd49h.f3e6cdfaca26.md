Calculation: Long/short confluence score = sum of the corresponding weighted votes: higher-timeframe alignment, Nadaraya–Watson ribbon alignment, order-flow filter, smart-money filter, ADX above its threshold, ELM directional vote weighted by its calibrated accuracy, and Nadaraya–Watson fast-line slope direction; compare with the opposite-side score where required.
Calculation: Nadaraya–Watson ribbon = eight Gaussian-weighted regressions of close over a common lookback, with bandwidths in a 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89 ratio; bullish if all seven adjacent pairs descend, bearish if all seven ascend, or use the configured relaxed pair-count threshold or expanding-width direction.
Calculation: ELM probability = sigmoid of a weighted sum of normalized RSI, 5-bar rate of change, distance from the 200-bar EMA, ADX, Nadaraya–Watson fast-line slope, ribbon-order difference, and volume relative to its 20-bar average; update weights on confirmed bars by decayed gradient steps against whether close exceeds close from the target lookback bars ago. Calibrated vote weight is based on rolling prediction accuracy.
Calculation: Order-flow bullish = (cumulative volume delta above its moving average while close falls and delta rises, OR buy/sell volume ratio above its threshold) AND close above or crossing up through daily VWAP; bearish = (cumulative volume delta below its moving average while close rises and delta falls, OR buy/sell volume ratio below its threshold) AND close below or crossing down through daily VWAP. VWAP resets each day.
Calculation: Smart-money bullish = bullish engulfing-body pattern, bullish fair-value gap, low liquidity sweep, or bullish break of structure; bearish = bearish engulfing-body pattern, bearish fair-value gap, high liquidity sweep, or bearish break of structure. A break of structure is a close crossing above the latest confirmed pivot high or below the latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Volatility-adapted stop and target distances = ATR times regime multipliers; regime uses ATR percentile, Bollinger-width expansion/contraction, and ADX trend/range classification.
Long: flat position AND in session AND cost filter passes AND equity guard is inactive AND long confluence score meets threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA with higher-timeframe close above fast EMA AND bullish ribbon AND order-flow filter passes AND smart-money filter passes AND ELM does not block long AND risk/reward meets minimum AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close above the long-term EMA AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times multiplier) OR (close exceeds the prior-bar value of the rolling highest high AND volume spike AND close above long-term EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close below lower Bollinger Band AND RSI below oversold level AND RSI crosses above oversold level)). OR flat position AND bullish BOS AND in session AND cost filter passes AND equity guard inactive AND long confluence score meets threshold. OR bullish BOS while flat and in session with cost filter passing and equity guard inactive.
Short: flat position AND in session AND cost filter passes AND equity guard is inactive AND short confluence score meets threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA with higher-timeframe close below fast EMA AND bearish ribbon AND order-flow filter passes AND smart-money filter passes AND ELM does not block short AND risk/reward meets minimum AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close below the long-term EMA AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times multiplier) OR (close falls below the prior-bar value of the rolling lowest low AND volume spike AND close below long-term EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close above upper Bollinger Band AND RSI above overbought level AND RSI crosses below overbought level)). OR flat position AND bearish BOS AND in session AND cost filter passes AND equity guard inactive AND short confluence score meets threshold. OR bearish BOS while flat and in session with cost filter passing and equity guard inactive.
Exit: long, low <= entry price minus the entry-time stop distance OR high >= entry price plus the entry-time target distance OR price reaches the trailing activation distance and falls to the trailing stop.
Exit: short, high >= entry price plus the entry-time stop distance OR low <= entry price minus the entry-time target distance OR price reaches the trailing activation distance and rises to the trailing stop.
