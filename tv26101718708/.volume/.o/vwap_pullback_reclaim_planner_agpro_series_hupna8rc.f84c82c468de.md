Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (typical price × volume) / cumulative volume, resetting at the selected session, week, or month boundary; include bars in the current forming anchor.
Calculation: Pullback freshness = clamp(1 − bars since the direction-specific pullback / pullback lookback, 0, 1); a long pullback occurs when low ≤ VWAP − ATR × pocket depth, and a short pullback when high ≥ VWAP + ATR × pocket depth.
Calculation: Reclaim score = capped weighted sum of normalized close distance from VWAP, pullback freshness, favorable wick / bar range, absolute body / bar range, relative volume (current volume / its shorter-lookback average, when enabled), VWAP slope alignment, and absolute close distance from VWAP; normalize and weight each component, then cap the total at 0–100. VWAP slope compares current VWAP with VWAP three bars earlier.
Calculation: Pullback quality = clamp((pullback freshness score + favorable-wick score + close-distance score) / their maximum combined score × 100, 0, 100).
Long: valid VWAP AND low ≤ VWAP − ATR × pocket depth within the pullback lookback AND close > VWAP + ATR × reclaim buffer AND previous close ≤ previous VWAP AND volume ≥ minimum volume (when enabled) AND reclaim score ≥ required ready score on a confirmed bar (when close confirmation is enabled)
Long: long pullback watch condition AND pullback quality ≥ watch score AND pullback quality ≥ historical label score on a confirmed bar AND no ready reclaim
Short: valid VWAP AND high ≥ VWAP + ATR × pocket depth within the pullback lookback AND close < VWAP − ATR × reclaim buffer AND previous close ≥ previous VWAP AND volume ≥ minimum volume (when enabled) AND reclaim score ≥ required ready score on a confirmed bar (when close confirmation is enabled)
Short: short pullback watch condition AND pullback quality ≥ watch score AND pullback quality ≥ historical label score on a confirmed bar AND no ready reclaim
Exit: long, an active long reclaim is within its reclaim window, T2 has not been reached, and confirmed close < reclaim VWAP − reclaim ATR × invalidation shelf
Exit: short, an active short reclaim is within its reclaim window, T2 has not been reached, and confirmed close > reclaim VWAP + reclaim ATR × invalidation shelf
