Calculation: Oscillator = RSI or MFI of close
Calculation: Fast MA = moving average of the oscillator using a shorter lookback than the Slow MA
Calculation: Slow MA = moving average of the oscillator using a longer lookback than the Fast MA
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = moving-average basis of the oscillator with upper and lower bands offset by a standard-deviation multiple
Calculation: Bollinger Band width = upper band minus lower band
Long: Fast MA crosses above Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses above Fast MA OR Slow MA crosses above Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses back inside the lower Bollinger Band OR Bollinger Band width is falling AND Slow MA crosses above the upper Bollinger Band
Short: Fast MA crosses below Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses below Fast MA OR Slow MA crosses below Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses back inside the upper Bollinger Band OR Bollinger Band width is falling AND Slow MA crosses below the lower Bollinger Band
Exit: Long, Fast MA crosses below Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses below Fast MA OR Slow MA crosses below Bollinger basis OR Bollinger Band width is falling AND Slow MA crosses outside the Bollinger Bands
Exit: Short, Fast MA crosses above Bollinger basis OR Slow MA crosses above Fast MA OR Slow MA crosses above Bollinger basis OR Bollinger Band width is falling AND Slow MA crosses outside the Bollinger Bands
