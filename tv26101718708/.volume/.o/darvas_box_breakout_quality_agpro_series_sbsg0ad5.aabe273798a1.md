Calculation: Darvas box = start on a bar whose high exceeds the highest high over the preceding seed lookback; set top=high, bottom=low, start bar=current. While forming, a higher high resets top, bottom, and start bar; otherwise bottom=min(previous bottom, low). Activate when age is at least the minimum and (top-bottom)/ATR is within the configured minimum and maximum. Discard if age exceeds the maximum. Keep active boundaries fixed until breakout or expiry.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Compression score = clamp(0.70 × clamp(100 − ((box height/ATR − minimum box-height ATR)/(maximum box-height ATR − minimum box-height ATR) × 100), 0, 100) + 0.30 × age score, 0, 100), where age score rises linearly to 100 at max(3 × minimum age, minimum age + 1), then declines at 35 points per excess age ratio, clamped to [30, 100].
Calculation: Breakout quality score = clamp(0.30 × compression score + 0.25 × volume score + 0.20 × distance score + 0.15 × close-location score + 0.10 × body score, 0, 100). Volume score = clamp((volume / volume SMA) / volume-support threshold × 100, 0, 100); distance score = clamp((close beyond boundary / ATR) / target break distance ATR × 100, 0, 100); body score = clamp(abs(close-open)/max(high-low, minimum tick) × 100, 0, 100); close-location score = clamp((close-low)/(high-low) × 100, 0, 100) for an upward break, or ((high-close)/(high-low) × 100) for a downward break.
Long: active Darvas box AND confirmed bar AND close > box top (or high > box top when close confirmation is disabled) AND volume / volume SMA >= volume-support threshold when volume support is required AND breakout quality score >= minimum breakout score OR a prior upward box break occurred within the failure window AND close < its captured top.
Short: active Darvas box AND confirmed bar AND close < box bottom (or low < box bottom when close confirmation is disabled) AND volume / volume SMA >= volume-support threshold when volume support is required AND breakout quality score >= minimum breakout score OR a prior downward box break occurred within the failure window AND close > its captured bottom.
