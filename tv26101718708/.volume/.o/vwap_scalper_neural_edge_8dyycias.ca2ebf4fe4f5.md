Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average price reset at the start of each trading session
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Upper/lower VWAP bands = session VWAP plus/minus a configurable ATR multiple
Calculation: Normalized volume-weighted delta = smoothed candle delta multiplied by volume relative to its recent average, with a cap; candle delta is the close's position within the bar range, scaled from -1 to +1
Calculation: Normalized velocity = smoothed close-price change over a short lookback, divided by ATR
Calculation: Acceleration = normalized velocity minus its value one velocity lookback ago
Calculation: Bullish component count = number of true conditions: normalized volume-weighted delta exceeds its positive threshold; close is above session VWAP; normalized velocity exceeds its positive threshold. Bearish component count uses the corresponding negative thresholds and below-VWAP condition
Long: Bullish component count > bearish component count OR (at least the configured minimum bullish components agree AND (close is at or below the lower VWAP band plus a reversal buffer and normalized volume-weighted delta is rising and acceleration is positive OR (close crosses above session VWAP OR close is above session VWAP and normalized volume-weighted delta crosses above its configured threshold) OR close is above the lower VWAP band and below session VWAP plus a configurable ATR buffer and normalized volume-weighted delta has risen for two consecutive bars and exceeds its positive strength threshold) AND within an allowed trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close is above its VWAP AND the configured cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed)
Short: Bearish component count > bullish component count OR (at least the configured minimum bearish components agree AND (close is at or above the upper VWAP band minus a reversal buffer and normalized volume-weighted delta is falling and acceleration is negative OR (close crosses below session VWAP OR close is below session VWAP and normalized volume-weighted delta crosses below its configured threshold) OR close is below the upper VWAP band and above session VWAP minus a configurable ATR buffer and normalized volume-weighted delta has fallen for two consecutive bars and is below its negative strength threshold) AND within an allowed trading session AND the last completed higher-timeframe bar's close is below its VWAP AND the configured cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed)
Exit: Long, low <= stop level OR high >= farthest profit target
Exit: Short, high >= stop level OR low <= farthest profit target
