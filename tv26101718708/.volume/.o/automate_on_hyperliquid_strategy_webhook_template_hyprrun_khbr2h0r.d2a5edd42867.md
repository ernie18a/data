Calculation: Recent volatility break = low < open − 1.5 × 5-bar ATR, occurring within the current or previous 3 bars
Calculation: Swing high/low = most recent confirmed pivot high/low, using 5 bars left and 5 bars right
Calculation: SFP(L,R) = bullish when low breaks below the latest confirmed pivot low, open and close finish above it, and low breaks the lowest low of the prior R bars; bearish when high breaks above the latest confirmed pivot high, open and close finish below it, and high breaks the highest high of the prior R bars. A bullish/bearish setup is valid unless invalidated by a close below/above its setup low/high, or by the setup low/high being no longer below/above the prior 2-bar lowest/highest close. Confirm within 5 bars: bullish close above the setup high and at least 0.05% above the previous high; bearish close below the setup low and at least 0.05% below the previous low. Suppress a direction if the preceding bar already signaled that direction.
Calculation: Full-body candle = absolute(open − close) ≥ 0.666 × (high − low)
Calculation: Bullish confirmation candle = full-body bullish candle, or lower wick > 25% of range and upper wick < 10% of range, or lower wick > 27.5% and upper wick < 10%, or lower wick > 55%, or lower wick > 50%
Calculation: Bearish confirmation candle = full-body bearish candle, or upper wick > 25% of range and lower wick < 10% of range, or upper wick > 27.5% and lower wick < 10%, or upper wick > 55%, or upper wick > 50%
Calculation: Long/short trailing stop = selected mode: LinReg(close, lookback, offset 1) minus/plus its side’s ATR multiple; in A3.1 use plain ATR, in A4.2 use volume-weighted ATR, in A4.0 use volume-weighted ATR multiplied by (0.8 + 0.4 × efficiency ratio) and ratchet the long stop upward / short stop downward; in A4.1 use highest high minus / lowest low plus volume-weighted ATR, using the first-bar multiplier until the ratchet condition applies, then the side’s ATR multiple. Volume-weighted ATR = SMA(true range × volume/SMA(volume, same side-specific lookback), same side-specific lookback). Efficiency ratio = absolute(close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over N bars, where N is the short-side ATR lookback. Long and short ATR lookbacks and multipliers are separate.
