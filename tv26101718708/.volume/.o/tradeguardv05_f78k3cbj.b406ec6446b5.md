Calculation: EMA(n) = exponential moving average of close over n bars; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA, which uses a shorter lookback than slowest EMA.
Calculation: Choppy = absolute fast-to-slow EMA distance as a percentage of slow EMA is below a threshold, OR the 25-bar Bollinger Bands (2 standard deviations) lie inside the 25-bar EMA ± 1.5 ATR channel.
Calculation: Uptrend = not Choppy AND fast EMA > slow EMA > slowest EMA AND close > fast EMA. Downtrend = not Choppy AND fast EMA < slow EMA < slowest EMA AND close < fast EMA.
Calculation: Buyer-confirmed volume = volume > its 10-bar SMA AND close is at or above the candle's high-low midpoint. Seller-confirmed volume = volume > its 10-bar SMA AND close is below the candle's high-low midpoint.
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar. Previous-week high/low = high/low of the last completed weekly bar. Premarket high/low = current day's high/low from 04:00 to 09:30. Opening-range high/low = current day's high/low during the first configured opening-range interval, used after that interval completes.
Calculation: Regime score = count of: Uptrend or Downtrend; 5-minute EMA(13)>EMA(48) with close>EMA(200) for an uptrend, or EMA(13)<EMA(48) with close<EMA(200) for a downtrend; same alignment on 15-minute bars; close above session VWAP in an uptrend or below it in a downtrend; not Choppy squeeze; and after the first 15 minutes of the regular session through 15:30. Use current 5-minute and 15-minute bars. Regime allowed = score >= 3.
Long: Regime allowed AND not (0DTE mode AND time >= 14:00) AND ((Uptrend AND low <= fast EMA × 1.002 AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND buyer-confirmed volume AND time is 09:45–15:30) OR (Uptrend AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA AND time is 09:45–15:30 AND a close crosses above premarket high or previous-day high, followed within 10 bars by a retest with low <= level × 1.0015, close >= level, close > open, and buyer-confirmed volume) OR (Uptrend AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA AND time is 09:45–15:30 AND close crosses above completed opening-range high, followed by a retest with low <= level × 1.0015, close >= level, close > open, and buyer-confirmed volume) OR (Uptrend AND a close crosses below fast EMA, followed by a reclaim with low <= fast EMA × 1.002, close > fast EMA, close > open, buyer-confirmed volume, and time is 09:45–15:30) OR (Uptrend AND bullish liquidity sweep)).
Long: Bullish liquidity sweep = any of: low < previous-day low × (1 − buffer) AND close > previous-day low AND close > open; same condition using previous-week low; low < fast EMA × (1 − buffer) AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND (Uptrend OR fast EMA > slow EMA); or low < prior 10-bar low × (1 − buffer) AND close > prior 10-bar low AND close > open AND the prior bar's low is within the equal-low threshold of its prior 10-bar low AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA. Buffer and equal-low threshold are parameters.
Short: Regime allowed AND not (0DTE mode AND time >= 14:00) AND ((Downtrend AND high >= fast EMA × 0.998 AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND seller-confirmed volume AND time is 09:45–15:30) OR (Downtrend AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA AND time is 09:45–15:30 AND a close crosses below premarket low or previous-day low, followed within 10 bars by a retest with high >= level × 0.9985, close <= level, close < open, and seller-confirmed volume) OR (Downtrend AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA AND time is 09:45–15:30 AND close crosses below completed opening-range low, followed by a retest with high >= level × 0.9985, close <= level, close < open, and seller-confirmed volume) OR (Downtrend AND a close crosses above fast EMA, followed by a rejection with high >= fast EMA × 0.998, close < fast EMA, close < open, seller-confirmed volume, and time is 09:45–15:30) OR (Downtrend AND bearish liquidity sweep)).
Short: Bearish liquidity sweep = any of: high > previous-day high × (1 + buffer) AND close < previous-day high AND close < open; same condition using previous-week high; high > fast EMA × (1 + buffer) AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND (Downtrend OR fast EMA < slow EMA); or high > prior 10-bar high × (1 + buffer) AND close < prior 10-bar high AND close < open AND the prior bar's high is within the equal-high threshold of its prior 10-bar high AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA. Buffer and equal-high threshold are parameters.
Exit: Long, Uptrend AND close < fast EMA, OR (0DTE mode AND no fast-EMA bounce for the configured consecutive-bar limit), OR close unchanged from prior close for the configured stall-bar limit; exit state is suppressed during the first 15 minutes, after 14:00 in 0DTE mode, in Choppy/no-trend or regime score < 3, and when a long or short entry signal has priority.
Exit: Short, Downtrend AND close > fast EMA, OR (0DTE mode AND no fast-EMA bounce for the configured consecutive-bar limit), OR close unchanged from prior close for the configured stall-bar limit; exit state is suppressed during the first 15 minutes, after 14:00 in 0DTE mode, in Choppy/no-trend or regime score < 3, and when a long or short entry signal has priority.
