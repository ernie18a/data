Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (typical price × volume) divided by cumulative volume, reset at the start of the New York 09:30–16:00 session
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar changes over that lookback
Calculation: Smoothed efficiency ratio = exponential moving average of the efficiency ratio
Calculation: Adaptive KAMA = previous KAMA plus its adaptive smoothing factor times (close − previous KAMA); the factor is the square of the smoothed efficiency ratio weighted between fast and slow smoothing constants
Calculation: VWAP trend strength = (session VWAP − session VWAP from a chart-interval-dependent lookback ago) divided by 14-bar ATR
Calculation: CPI = (2 × close − high − low) divided by (high − low), or zero when range is zero or below the configured minimum range
Long: Adaptive KAMA > previous Adaptive KAMA AND its smoothed efficiency ratio > 0.30 OR within the New York session, the session VWAP is in bullish state AND close > session VWAP; bullish state means either VWAP trend strength exceeds the greater of half its 20-bar standard deviation and a positive floor, VWAP efficiency > 0.30, and session initialization is complete, or close > session VWAP, the minimum startup bars have elapsed, and CPI > 0.25
Short: Adaptive KAMA < previous Adaptive KAMA AND its smoothed efficiency ratio > 0.30 OR within the New York session, the session VWAP is in bearish state AND close < session VWAP; bearish state means either VWAP trend strength is below the negative of the greater of half its 20-bar standard deviation and a positive floor, VWAP efficiency > 0.30, and session initialization is complete, or close < session VWAP, the minimum startup bars have elapsed, and CPI < −0.25
