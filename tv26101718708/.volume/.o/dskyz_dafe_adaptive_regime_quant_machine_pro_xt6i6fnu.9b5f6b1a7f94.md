Calculation: HTF bullish trend = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, MACD line > signal line AND RSI > its midpoint; HTF bearish trend = MACD line < signal line AND RSI < its midpoint
Calculation: Regime = trending if ADX is high, fast/slow EMA spread is wide relative to ATR, and either HTF trend holds; else range if ADX and recent price range relative to close are low and neither HTF trend holds; else volatile if Bollinger Band width and ATR are high relative to their longer lookback averages and HTF RSI is overbought or oversold; else quiet if band width and ATR are low relative to their longer lookback averages; else other
Calculation: Bullish pattern score = weighted sum of bullish engulfing, hammer, morning star, piercing, and double bottom patterns; each pattern also requires low near the shorter-lookback rolling low and current volume above its longer-lookback average; hammer and double-bottom weights vary by regime
Calculation: Bearish pattern score = weighted sum of bearish engulfing, shooting star, evening star, dark cloud, and double top patterns; each pattern also requires high near the shorter-lookback rolling high and current volume above its longer-lookback average; shooting-star and double-top weights vary by regime
Calculation: Bull score = bullish pattern score plus weighted bullish factors: fast EMA > slow EMA, RSI oversold, MACD line > signal, ATR expanded relative to its longer-lookback average, and HTF bullish trend with HTF RSI below its overbought threshold
Calculation: Bear score = bearish pattern score plus weighted bearish factors: fast EMA < slow EMA, RSI overbought, MACD line < signal, ATR expanded relative to its longer-lookback average, and HTF bearish trend with HTF RSI above its oversold threshold
Calculation: Long stop = rolling swing low over a shorter lookback minus stop distance; short stop = rolling swing high over a shorter lookback plus stop distance; stop distance is either an ATR multiple or a fixed distance
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close plus twice the distance from signal-bar close to long stop; short target = signal-bar close minus twice the distance from short stop to signal-bar close
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the greater of its prior value and close minus an ATR multiple; short trailing stop = the lesser of its prior value and close plus an ATR multiple
Long: bull score meets the entry threshold AND position is flat or short
Short: bear score meets the entry threshold AND position is flat or long
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR low ≤ long trailing stop after close > half the long target
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR high ≥ short trailing stop after close < half the short target
