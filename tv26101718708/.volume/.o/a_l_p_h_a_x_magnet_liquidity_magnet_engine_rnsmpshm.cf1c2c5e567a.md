Calculation: Active buy-side pool = nearest level above close among (a) pivot-high clusters with at least the configured pivot touches and age, merging nearby pivots by touch-weighted average, and (b) the highest high in the configured lookback when enough highs are within ATR-based pool tolerance; active sell-side pool is the nearest level below close, using the corresponding pivot-low clusters and lookback low.
Calculation: Sweep state = most recent qualifying sweep level and bar; a sell-side sweep occurs when low is below the active sell-side pool by more than the ATR-based sweep depth, and a buy-side sweep when high is above the active buy-side pool by more than that depth.
Calculation: Bull trigger = close > open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ configured ATR-based minimum body AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) > 0.50; bear trigger = close < open AND the same body requirement AND (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick) > 0.50.
Calculation: Confluence score = 2 points for any primary sweep/reclaim setup, otherwise 1 for any secondary absorption/FVG setup, plus 1 each for a valid corresponding pool, aligned higher-timeframe bias, volume at or above its configured multiple of its moving average (or unconditionally if disabled), structural bias (long: close above structure EMA OR RSI > 45; short: close below structure EMA OR RSI < 55), exhaustion (long: RSI < 38 with a recent sell-side sweep; short: RSI > 62 with a recent buy-side sweep), non-chop (Choppiness Index below its configured limit, or unconditionally if disabled), and being within pool tolerance.
Long: enabled setup is any of (active sell-side pool swept this bar and close > pool and bull trigger and NOT (close < open AND body > 0.45 × ATR AND close < structure EMA AND structure EMA < 50-period EMA)); (sell-side sweep 1 through configured reclaim-window bars ago and close > swept level and low ≤ swept level + pool tolerance and bull trigger); (low ≤ active sell-side pool + pool tolerance and lower wick / range > 0.42 and body / range < 0.38 and close > open and volume ≥ configured multiple of volume moving average and close > pool); (low > high two bars ago and the gap [high two bars ago, low] overlaps the active sell-side pool within pool tolerance and bull trigger); AND bull confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND Choppiness Index below its configured limit (unless chop filter disabled) AND configured session condition (if enabled) AND configured signal cooldown elapsed.
Short: enabled setup is any of (active buy-side pool swept this bar and close < pool and bear trigger and NOT (close > open AND body > 0.45 × ATR AND close > structure EMA AND structure EMA > 50-period EMA)); (buy-side sweep 1 through configured reclaim-window bars ago and close < swept level and high ≥ swept level − pool tolerance and bear trigger); (high ≥ active buy-side pool − pool tolerance and upper wick / range > 0.42 and body / range < 0.38 and close < open and volume ≥ configured multiple of volume moving average and close < pool); (high < low two bars ago and the gap [high, low two bars ago] overlaps the active buy-side pool within pool tolerance and bear trigger); AND bear confluence score ≥ configured minimum AND Choppiness Index below its configured limit (unless chop filter disabled) AND configured session condition (if enabled) AND configured signal cooldown elapsed.
