Calculation: Squeeze fired = BB upper = SMA(close, BB length) + BB multiplier × standard deviation(close, BB length); BB lower = SMA(close, BB length) − BB multiplier × standard deviation(close, BB length); KC upper/lower = the same SMA ± KC multiplier × ATR(BB length); squeeze is on when BB upper < KC upper AND BB lower > KC lower; squeeze is off when BB upper > KC upper OR BB lower < KC lower; fired when squeeze is off AND at least one prior bar within the squeeze lookback was squeeze on
Calculation: Phoenix TCI = EMA(((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, N1)) / max(EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, N1)), N1), epsilon)) / 0.025, N2) + 50, where HLC3 = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Money flow = 100 − 100 / (1 + sum(HLC3 × volume when HLC3 > prior HLC3, N3) / max(sum(HLC3 × volume when HLC3 < prior HLC3, N3), epsilon))
Calculation: Phoenix composite = average of Phoenix TCI, Money flow, and RSI(HLC3, N3); Phoenix signal = SMA(Phoenix composite, 6); Phoenix LSMA = linear regression of Phoenix composite over its lookback
Calculation: Volatility rank = percentile rank of ATR(14) over its lookback
Calculation: EMA filter = EMA(close, 4 × EMA filter length), calculated on the entry-timeframe bars
Calculation: Entry ATR = ATR(ATR length) on the entry bar; long trailing stop = max(previous trailing stop, close − Entry ATR × stop multiple), initialized to entry-bar close − Entry ATR × stop multiple; short trailing stop = min(previous trailing stop, close + Entry ATR × stop multiple), initialized to entry-bar close + Entry ATR × stop multiple
Calculation: Long take-profit = entry-bar close + Entry ATR × take-profit multiple; short take-profit = entry-bar close − Entry ATR × take-profit multiple
Long: Squeeze fired AND close > EMA filter AND HMA > prior HMA AND Phoenix composite > Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite > prior Phoenix composite AND Phoenix LSMA > 50 AND Volatility rank ≥ minimum AND Volatility rank ≤ maximum AND ADX ≥ threshold
Short: Squeeze fired AND close < EMA filter AND HMA < prior HMA AND Phoenix composite < Phoenix signal AND Phoenix composite < prior Phoenix composite AND Phoenix LSMA < 50 AND Volatility rank ≥ minimum AND Volatility rank ≤ maximum AND ADX ≥ threshold
Exit: Long, close ≤ long trailing stop OR close ≥ long take-profit OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars OR Phoenix composite crosses below Phoenix signal OR Phoenix composite has decreased on every bar throughout the exit-slope lookback
Exit: Short, close ≥ short trailing stop OR close ≤ short take-profit OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars OR Phoenix composite crosses above Phoenix signal OR Phoenix composite has increased on every bar throughout the exit-slope lookback
