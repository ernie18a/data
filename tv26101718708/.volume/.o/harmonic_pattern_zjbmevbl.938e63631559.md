Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides; use the pivot bar’s high or low.
Calculation: Pattern score = weighted combination of ratio fit, PRZ tightness, pivot timing symmetry, and context score; accept only scores at or above the configured minimum.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = an enabled bullish harmonic or structural pattern formed by alternating confirmed pivots and passing its geometry, size, PRZ, context, and score filters. Harmonic patterns use X-A-B-C-D ratios and PRZ confluence; structural patterns include three drives, double or triple bottoms, inverse head and shoulders, triangles, and falling wedges.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = an enabled bearish harmonic or structural pattern formed by alternating confirmed pivots and passing its geometry, size, PRZ, context, and score filters. Harmonic patterns use X-A-B-C-D ratios and PRZ confluence; structural patterns include three drives, double or triple tops, head and shoulders, triangles, and rising wedges.
Long: A newly detected bullish pattern on a confirmed bar.
Short: A newly detected bearish pattern on a confirmed bar.
Exit: long, low <= stop level OR high >= any target level
Exit: short, high >= stop level OR low <= any target level
