Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low − high two bars earlier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]
Calculation: Bearish FVG = low two bars earlier − current high; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; available only after the bars on the right have completed
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback
Calculation: FVG candidate score = weighted score of proximity to the structure-break price, timing relative to the break, gap size, and age; adaptive profiles also include formation displacement, formation volume, range location, prior touches, and break strength
Calculation: Reaction strength = weighted score of retest candle close location, directional wick, body size relative to ATR, candle direction, and penetration depth into the gap
Calculation: Setup score = weighted score of candidate score, reaction strength, volume quality, directional-force quality, and higher-timeframe alignment
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe close and fast/slow EMAs = values from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA
Long: confirmed close crosses above the last unbroken confirmed swing high plus the configured ATR clearance (or, when wick breaks are selected, high crosses above it) AND the break passes any enabled body-size filter AND a bullish FVG meets the configured gap-width and any enabled displacement filters, matches the break direction, is within the configured break-distance and scan windows, and passes prior-touch and candidate-score thresholds AND it is the highest-scoring eligible candidate and becomes armed AND the retest occurs after the configured delay and before expiry, with the selected bullish retest condition satisfied (wick overlap; body overlap; close inside the gap; bullish reclaim above the gap top; directional recovery with wick overlap, close at/above the midpoint, and close above open, or body overlap with reclaim; edge reclaim; or reclaim with bullish close and lower wick at least 30% of candle range) AND any enabled reaction-strength, volume, DI/ADX, higher-timeframe, and minimum-grade filters pass AND no trade is active and cooldown is complete AND the bar closes
Short: confirmed close crosses below the last unbroken confirmed swing low minus the configured ATR clearance (or, when wick breaks are selected, low crosses below it) AND the break passes any enabled body-size filter AND a bearish FVG meets the configured gap-width and any enabled displacement filters, matches the break direction, is within the configured break-distance and scan windows, and passes prior-touch and candidate-score thresholds AND it is the highest-scoring eligible candidate and becomes armed AND the retest occurs after the configured delay and before expiry, with the selected bearish retest condition satisfied (wick overlap; body overlap; close inside the gap; bearish reclaim below the gap bottom; directional recovery with wick overlap, close at/below the midpoint, and close below open, or body overlap with reclaim; edge reclaim; or reclaim with bearish close and upper wick at least 30% of candle range) AND any enabled reaction-strength, volume, DI/ADX, higher-timeframe, and minimum-grade filters pass AND no trade is active and cooldown is complete AND the bar closes
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= final target OR all target allocations are complete
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= final target OR all target allocations are complete
Exit: long, low <= entry price after the stop has moved to breakeven following the first target
Exit: short, high >= entry price after the stop has moved to breakeven following the first target
