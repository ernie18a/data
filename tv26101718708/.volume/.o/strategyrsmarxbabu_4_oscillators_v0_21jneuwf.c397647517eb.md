Calculation: MFI = RSI applied to the lookback sum of volume × source on rising bars and the lookback sum of volume × source on falling bars; unchanged bars contribute to neither sum. Use the same lookback as the other oscillators.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = stochastic of RSI(source), with the RSI and stochastic lookbacks equal to each other and to the other oscillators; no additional smoothing.
Long: RSI < oversold level AND Stochastic RSI < oversold level AND MFI < oversold level AND Williams %R < oversold level
Short: RSI > overbought level AND Stochastic RSI > overbought level AND MFI > overbought level AND Williams %R > overbought level
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + profit distance
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − profit distance
