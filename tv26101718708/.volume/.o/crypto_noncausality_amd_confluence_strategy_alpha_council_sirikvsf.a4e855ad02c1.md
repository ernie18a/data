Calculation: Session high/low = highest high and lowest low accumulated during the configured Asian session; retain them for the subsequent configured London session.
Calculation: Laguerre RSI = CU / (CU + CD), where L0 = (1 − γ) × close + γ × previous L0; L1 = −γ × L0 + previous L0 + γ × previous L1, with L2 and L3 defined recursively the same way from the preceding level; CU sums positive differences between consecutive levels, and CD sums their positive downward differences.
Calculation: Hurst proxy = 1 − log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Valid regime = when the regime engine is enabled, its state is stationary or compressed; otherwise, the Hurst proxy is below its stationarity threshold. The enabled engine is compressed when volatility ratio is below its compression threshold and Hurst proxy below its compression limit; otherwise, when Hurst proxy exceeds its trend threshold and ADX exceeds its trend threshold, it is bullish or bearish if the corresponding emission score is greater, and that score meets its minimum. Bull emission score counts RSI above its midpoint, positive DI above negative DI with ADX above its threshold, and positive candle-signed volume; bear emission score counts the opposite conditions. All other states are stationary. Volatility ratio = current ATR divided by its longer-lookback average.
Calculation: Cross-market close change = current close minus previous close of the corresponding market series on the same timeframe.
Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close using the configured exit lookback.
Long: low < retained Asian session low AND close > retained Asian session low AND during the configured London session AND Laguerre RSI < its lower threshold AND valid regime AND cross-market close change < 0
Short: high > retained Asian session high AND close < retained Asian session high AND during the configured London session AND Laguerre RSI > its upper threshold AND valid regime AND cross-market close change > 0
Exit: Long, (close − entry price) / entry price ≥ configured profit threshold AND close < fast EMA OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − configured loss threshold) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − configured catastrophic loss threshold)
Exit: Short, (entry price − close) / entry price ≥ configured profit threshold AND close > fast EMA OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + configured loss threshold) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + configured catastrophic loss threshold)
