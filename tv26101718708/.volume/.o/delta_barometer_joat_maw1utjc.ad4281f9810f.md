Calculation: Volatility regime = current ATR / moving average of ATR; low if below the low threshold, high if above the high threshold, otherwise medium. Use the current bar.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR trail = current close minus/plus current ATR times the regime’s multiplier for the upward/downward trail; ratchet the active trail upward in an uptrend and downward in a downtrend, switching direction when close crosses the trail.
Calculation: Trend strength score = 100 × (0.40 × clamp(abs(current RSI(14) − RSI(14) from a shorter lookback ago) / 20, 0, 1) + 0.35 × clamp(max(current volume / EMA(volume, 20) − 1, 0) / 1.5, 0, 1) + 0.25 × clamp(max(current ATR / shorter-period ATR − 1, 0) / 0.5, 0, 1)).
Calculation: Cumulative signed volume = running sum of volume when close ≥ open, otherwise negative volume; its signal line is a moving average of cumulative signed volume.
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × (0.40 × abs(signed volume / volume) + 0.35 × volume-rate component + 0.25 × candle-body-to-range ratio), where volume-rate component = clamp(max(current volume / EMA(volume, 20) − 1, 0) / 1.5, 0, 1) and candle-body-to-range ratio = min(abs(close − open) / (high − low), 1), or 0 when high = low.
Long: On a completed bar, cooldown has elapsed AND ((mode is Both Engines AND bullish ATR trail flip AND bullish cumulative-volume crossover AND their respective regime arming, enable, and score gates pass) OR (mode is Priority A AND (qualified bullish ATR trail flip OR qualified bullish cumulative-volume crossover AND no bearish ATR trail flip)) OR (mode is Priority B AND (qualified bullish cumulative-volume crossover OR qualified bullish ATR trail flip AND no bearish cumulative-volume crossover)) OR (mode is Either Engine AND (qualified bullish ATR trail flip OR qualified bullish cumulative-volume crossover))). A bullish ATR trail flip is a prior downtrend with current close above the adaptive ATR trail and trend strength score at least its minimum; a bullish cumulative-volume crossover is cumulative signed volume crossing above its signal line with pressure score at least its minimum and RSI below its overbought gate. Engine qualification requires that engine to be enabled and armed for the current volatility regime.
Short: On a completed bar, cooldown has elapsed AND ((mode is Both Engines AND bearish ATR trail flip AND bearish cumulative-volume crossover AND their respective regime arming, enable, and score gates pass) OR (mode is Priority A AND (qualified bearish ATR trail flip OR qualified bearish cumulative-volume crossover AND no bullish ATR trail flip)) OR (mode is Priority B AND (qualified bearish cumulative-volume crossover OR qualified bearish ATR trail flip AND no bullish cumulative-volume crossover)) OR (mode is Either Engine AND (qualified bearish ATR trail flip OR qualified bearish cumulative-volume crossover))). A bearish ATR trail flip is a prior uptrend with current close below the adaptive ATR trail and trend strength score at least its minimum; a bearish cumulative-volume crossover is cumulative signed volume crossing below its signal line with pressure score at least its minimum and RSI above its oversold gate. Engine qualification requires that engine to be enabled and armed for the current volatility regime.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop or high ≥ take-profit; at entry, stop = min(adaptive ATR trail, entry price − half the current ATR), or entry price reduced by the configured stop percentage if the trail is unavailable; take-profit = entry price + current ATR × configured target multiple.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop or low ≤ take-profit; at entry, stop = max(adaptive ATR trail, entry price + half the current ATR), or entry price increased by the configured stop percentage if the trail is unavailable; take-profit = entry price − current ATR × configured target multiple.
