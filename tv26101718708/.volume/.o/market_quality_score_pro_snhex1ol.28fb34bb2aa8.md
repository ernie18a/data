Calculation: Relative-strength score = weighted average of the percentile ranks of benchmark-relative 12-month return excluding the most recent month, 6-month return, 3-month return, distance from the 252-day high, and 50-day change in the price-to-benchmark ratio; use longer lookbacks for longer-term returns and shorter lookbacks for shorter-term returns.
Calculation: Trend score = weighted average of normalized distance above the 200-day moving average, normalized spread of the 50-day over the 200-day moving average, and normalized drawdown from the 60-day high.
Calculation: Total score = weighted average of the available relative-strength, earnings-quality, and trend scores, renormalized by the weights of available components; the earnings-quality score is the available weighted average of normalized year-over-year EPS growth, revenue growth, and ROE.
Calculation: Coverage = weighted fraction of available relative-strength, earnings-quality (for equities), and trend inputs.
Calculation: Confidence = average of coverage and history score; history score is highest when the configured minimum history is met, intermediate when minimum history is met but full history is not, and lowest otherwise.
Long: Good-buy condition was false on the previous bar AND history meets the full-history gate AND coverage and confidence meet the buy minimum AND the required benchmark is available AND (safety filter is disabled OR price is above its 200-day moving average AND drawdown from its 60-day high is within the allowed threshold AND, for equities, average 20-day dollar turnover meets its minimum; for ETFs, either volume is unavailable or turnover meets its minimum) AND total score is at or above the buy threshold on this and the previous bar AND strong-buy condition is false
Long: Strong-buy condition was false on the previous bar AND all good-buy gates pass AND relative-strength score meets its strong threshold AND trend score meets its strong threshold AND coverage and confidence meet their strong thresholds AND drawdown from its 60-day high meets its stricter strong threshold AND (not an equity OR earnings-quality score meets its strong threshold) AND total score is at or above the strong-buy threshold on this and the previous bar AND total score is higher than three bars ago AND smoothed change in total score over 20 bars is positive AND closing price makes a 20-bar high
Exit: long, total score crosses below the buy threshold AND history meets the minimum-history gate AND coverage meets the watch minimum
