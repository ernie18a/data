Calculation: Session VWAP = sum(hl2 × volume) / sum(volume) from the start of the current session through the current bar; reset at each new day.
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = sum(hl2 × volume) / sum(volume) from the start of the current week through the current bar; reset at each new week.
Calculation: Monthly VWAP = sum(hl2 × volume) / sum(volume) from the start of the current month through the current bar; reset at each new month.
Calculation: VWAP regime gate = compare close with the selected Session, Weekly, or Monthly VWAP; Any passes if at least one selected comparison passes, and All passes only if all three pass. Bullish means close ≥ VWAP; bearish means close < VWAP.
Calculation: Ratcheting trail = ATR × trail-distance below the low for a bullish trail or above the high for a bearish trail. Ratchet the target so a bullish target cannot decrease and a bearish target cannot increase. Each bar move the prior trail toward that target by a chase fraction, capped so the bullish trail stays below the low and the bearish trail above the high. The chase fraction increases with a decaying, capped velocity accumulator and ATR-normalized distance from the selected VWAP; reset velocity to its base on a direction flip. Initialize direction by whether close is at or above the selected VWAP, and initialize the trail at the corresponding target.
Long: bar closes confirmed AND prior trend is bearish AND close > prior bar's ratcheting trail AND bullish VWAP regime gate passes AND bars since last confirmed flip ≥ minimum flip gap.
Short: bar closes confirmed AND prior trend is bullish AND close < prior bar's ratcheting trail AND bearish VWAP regime gate passes AND bars since last confirmed flip ≥ minimum flip gap.
