Calculation: Trend state = On the chart timeframe and each of four selected other timeframes, calculate a fast linear regression of hl2 and ATR-based upper/lower rails. Initialize direction bullish when close ≥ the regression, otherwise bearish; initialize the rail to the lower rail when bullish and upper rail when bearish. On a confirmed bar, flip bearish to bullish when close > the prior rail, or bullish to bearish when close < the prior rail. Otherwise, while bullish, raise the rail to the maximum of its prior value and the current lower rail; while bearish, lower it to the minimum of its prior value and the current upper rail. Use the current bar as it forms on other timeframes; with lookahead off, historical values update as those bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish timeframe votes = number of the four other-timeframe trend states that are bullish.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, chart-timeframe trend state flips from bearish to bullish AND bullish timeframe votes ≥ 3.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, chart-timeframe trend state flips from bullish to bearish AND bullish timeframe votes ≤ 1.
