Calculation: MACD = selected moving average of the selected source at the shorter length minus the same moving average at the longer length.
Calculation: Signal = selected moving average of MACD at the signal length.
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR × stop multiplier; target distances = risk distance × target multiples sorted from nearest to farthest.
Long: (MACD > Signal AND previous MACD <= previous Signal AND bar is closed AND shorter length < longer length AND risk distance > minimum tick AND entry price − risk distance > 0 AND volume is available when using a volume-based average AND chart uses standard bars AND latest bar is not locked AND no long position is open) OR (candle mode compares MACD with Signal AND MACD > Signal) OR (candle mode compares MACD with zero AND MACD > 0).
Short: (MACD < Signal AND previous MACD >= previous Signal AND bar is closed AND shorter length < longer length AND risk distance > minimum tick AND entry price + risk distance > 0 AND entry price − farthest target distance > 0 AND volume is available when using a volume-based average AND chart uses standard bars AND latest bar is not locked AND no short position is open) OR (candle mode compares MACD with Signal AND MACD < Signal) OR (candle mode compares MACD with zero AND MACD < 0).
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high >= entry price + nearest target distance OR high >= entry price + middle target distance OR high >= entry price + farthest target distance OR (bar is closed AND close < long stop) OR (bar is closed AND MACD < Signal AND previous MACD >= previous Signal).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, low <= entry price − nearest target distance OR low <= entry price − middle target distance OR low <= entry price − farthest target distance OR (bar is closed AND close > short stop) OR (bar is closed AND MACD > Signal AND previous MACD <= previous Signal).
