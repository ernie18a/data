Calculation: MACD signal line = moving average of MACD
Calculation: MACD crossover = MACD is above its signal line now and was at or below it on the prior bar
Calculation: MACD crossunder = MACD is below its signal line now and was at or above it on the prior bar
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: Double exponential moving average = 2 × EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close))
Calculation: Triple exponential moving average = 3 × (EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close))) + EMA(EMA(EMA(close)))
Calculation: Pivot low/high = a low/high confirmed using left and right lookbacks
