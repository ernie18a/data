Calculation: Primary magnet = midpoint of the equal-width price bin with the most bar high-low ranges covering it, among bins spanning the highest high to lowest low of the lookback; count bars over the lookback including the current bar. Ties use the lowest-price tied bin.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over its lookback.
Calculation: Magnet score = round(min(100, min(6 × primary-bin touch count, 40) + min(max((volume / average volume − 1) × 40, 0), 25) + 35 if an up or down break occurs, otherwise 0)).
Calculation: Bias EMA = exponential moving average of close; its slope is the current EMA minus the EMA three bars earlier.
Long: primary magnet touch count ≥ minimum AND close > primary magnet + ATR × break-margin AND previous close ≤ previous bar's primary magnet + previous bar's ATR × break-margin AND volume ≥ average volume × volume-confirm multiplier AND close > bias EMA AND bias EMA > its value three bars earlier AND magnet score ≥ score threshold AND cooldown elapsed since the previous signal
