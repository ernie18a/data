Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = on a confirmed bar, close crosses above the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high / below the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low; pivots are confirmed using left- and right-side bars.
Calculation: ATR trend = Supertrend direction, with bullish direction when its direction is bullish and fast trend EMA > slow trend EMA; bearish is the reverse.
Calculation: Momentum = (fast ribbon EMA − slow ribbon EMA) / ATR; its signal is an EMA of momentum.
Calculation: Active Order Block = after a bullish/bearish structure break or qualifying same-direction displacement, the most recent opposing candle within a lookback that passes ATR-range, body-percent, and optional volume filters, whose zone has not already been invalidated; the zone uses candle wicks or body and remains active until invalidated or expired. A touch occurs when the current bar's range overlaps an active zone created earlier.
Calculation: Dealing range = rolling highest high and lowest low; premium boundary = range high minus the configured premium fraction of range width; discount boundary = range low plus the configured discount fraction of range width.
Calculation: Confluence score = 25 for matching flow bias + 15 for matching structure state + 15 for matching momentum + 25 for touching a matching active Order Block + 20 for close in the matching location (discount for long, premium for short).
Long: confirmed bullish structure break
Short: confirmed bearish structure break
Long: confirmed bar when flow bias changes to bullish: bullish structure break with bullish ATR trend, or bullish structure state with fast trend EMA crossing above slow trend EMA and bullish ATR trend; if flow bias is unset, bullish structure state with bullish ATR trend
Short: confirmed bar when flow bias changes to bearish: bearish structure break with bearish ATR trend, or bearish structure state with fast trend EMA crossing below slow trend EMA and bearish ATR trend; if flow bias is unset, bearish structure state with bearish ATR trend
Long: confirmed bar when momentum crosses above its signal
Short: confirmed bar when momentum crosses below its signal
Long: flow bias is bullish AND structure state is bullish AND (active bullish Order Block touched, when required) AND (close is at or below the discount boundary, when required) AND (fast ribbon EMA > slow ribbon EMA AND momentum > its signal AND momentum > 0, when required) AND confluence score is at least the configured minimum AND these requirements have just changed from false to true AND the configured signal cooldown has elapsed
Short: flow bias is bearish AND structure state is bearish AND (active bearish Order Block touched, when required) AND (close is at or above the premium boundary, when required) AND (fast ribbon EMA < slow ribbon EMA AND momentum < its signal AND momentum < 0, when required) AND confluence score is at least the configured minimum AND these requirements have just changed from false to true AND the configured signal cooldown has elapsed
