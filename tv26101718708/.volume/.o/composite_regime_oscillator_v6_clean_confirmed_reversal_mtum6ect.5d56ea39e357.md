Calculation: Regime stress = smoothed weighted combination of normalized fast-to-slow ATR ratio, drawdown from the lookback highest high, and rebound from the lookback lowest low
Calculation: Stretch score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized absolute distance between close and EMA divided by ATR, and normalized absolute distance of the close's position in the lookback range from its midpoint
Calculation: Price-location score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized close position in the lookback range, normalized distance from the lookback low, and inverse normalized distance from the lookback high
Calculation: Bullish overreaction score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized RSI oversoldness and normalized negative close-to-EMA distance in ATR units
Calculation: Bearish overreaction score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized RSI overboughtness and normalized positive close-to-EMA distance in ATR units
Calculation: Pressure score = smoothed weighted combination of normalized current volume relative to its average and normalized fast-to-slow ATR ratio
Calculation: Bullish exhaustion score = EMA-smoothed weighted combination of inverse price-location score, bullish overreaction score, regime stress, and pressure score
Calculation: Bearish exhaustion score = EMA-smoothed weighted combination of price-location score, bearish overreaction score, inverse regime stress, and pressure score
Calculation: Strong uptrend = close above fast EMA AND fast EMA above slow EMA AND fast EMA above its value at the slope lookback
Calculation: Strong downtrend = close below fast EMA AND fast EMA below slow EMA AND fast EMA below its value at the slope lookback
Calculation: ADX cooling = ADX falling over its lookback
Calculation: ADX rising = ADX rising over its lookback
Long: bullish exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND (close > previous bar high OR (close > open AND previous close < previous open) OR RSI crosses above its oversold threshold OR close has risen over its momentum lookback) AND (close crosses above EMA OR close > previous bar high) AND NOT (strong downtrend AND ADX > strong-trend threshold AND NOT ADX cooling) AND bullish base condition was false on the previous bar AND no long signal occurred within the cooldown lookback OR bullish exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND NOT bullish confirmed signal AND strong downtrend AND ADX > strong-trend threshold AND NOT ADX cooling
Short: bearish exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND (close < previous bar low OR (close < open AND previous close > previous open) OR RSI crosses below its overbought threshold OR close has fallen over its momentum lookback) AND (close crosses below EMA OR close < previous bar low) AND NOT (strong uptrend AND ADX > strong-trend threshold AND NOT ADX cooling) AND bearish base condition was false on the previous bar AND no short signal occurred within the cooldown lookback OR bearish exhaustion score >= exhaustion threshold AND NOT bearish confirmed signal AND strong uptrend AND ADX > strong-trend threshold AND NOT ADX cooling
