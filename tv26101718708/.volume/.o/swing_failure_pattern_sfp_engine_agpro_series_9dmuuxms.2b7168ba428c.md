Calculation: Eligible swing low = a confirmed pivot low, using the configured number of bars on each side, within the lookback and not previously swept
Calculation: Eligible swing high = a confirmed pivot high, using the same pivot and lookback parameters as swing lows, within the lookback and not previously swept
Calculation: SFP score = round(min(100, clamp((sweep-side wick ratio − 0.30) / 0.65 × 40, 0, 40) + clamp(reclaim distance in ATR × 30, 0, 30) + (volume confirmed ? 15 : 0) + min(15, swing age in bars / 50 × 15)))
Long: current low ≤ the highest eligible swing low it reaches and current close > that swing low AND (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≥ minimum wick ratio AND (close − swing low) / safe ATR ≥ minimum reclaim distance AND (volume confirmation disabled OR volume > 20-bar SMA(volume) × volume multiplier) AND SFP score ≥ minimum score AND bar is confirmed
