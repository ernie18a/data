Calculation: Active consolidation zone = On the chart timeframe, after the cooldown, start a zone when the highest high minus lowest low over the minimum zone length of completed bars is ≤ maximum zone width × current ATR, and the absolute difference between the current close and the close minimum zone length bars ago is ≤ that range × maximum drift fraction. Set zone edges to that window’s high and low and lock the current ATR. On each subsequent completed bar, test for a break before updating the edges. If there is no break, expand the edges to include that bar’s high and low only if the resulting width is ≤ maximum zone width × locked ATR. Clear the zone after a break; wait the cooldown before starting another.
Long: Active consolidation zone AND completed bar close > zone upper edge + breakout buffer × ATR locked at zone formation
Short: Active consolidation zone AND completed bar close < zone lower edge − breakout buffer × ATR locked at zone formation
