Calculation: Rolling POC and value area = Using the current bar and preceding lookback bars, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into price bins. Apportion each bar’s volume to each bin in proportion to its high-low overlap with that bin. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Starting there, add adjacent bins until the value-area volume percentage is reached, choosing the higher-volume adjacent bin each step and the lower bin on ties; value-area boundaries are the outer edges of the included bins.
Calculation: Auction score = Clamp to [-100, 100] the sum of 55 × (short-lookback SMA(volume) / long-lookback SMA(volume) − 1), 30 × (short-lookback EMA(close) − long-lookback EMA(close)) / ATR, and 22 × (close − POC) / max(ATR × distance normalizer, minimum tick). The short lookbacks are shorter than the corresponding long lookbacks. ATR uses its own lookback.
Long: Auction score ≥ 10 OR (close > current value-area high AND previous close > previous bar’s value-area high AND NOT (previous close > previous bar’s value-area high AND the close two bars ago > that bar’s value-area high))
Short: Auction score ≤ −10 OR (close < current value-area low AND previous close < previous bar’s value-area low AND NOT (previous close < previous bar’s value-area low AND the close two bars ago < that bar’s value-area low))
