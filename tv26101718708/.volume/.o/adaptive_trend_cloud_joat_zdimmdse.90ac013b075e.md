Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend direction = Calculate ATR and rank the current ATR within its recent lookback, then scale the base ATR multiplier by that rank relative to 0.5 and its adaptive range, with a floor of 0.25. Build bands around the selected price basis (hl2, close, or ohlc4) using this multiplier times ATR; trail the upper band downward and lower band upward according to standard SuperTrend rules. Direction flips up when close crosses above the prior upper band and down when close crosses below the prior lower band.
Long: Adaptive SuperTrend direction flips up AND close > its EMA AND (momentum filter disabled OR (RSI mode selected AND RSI > its midline) OR (MACD mode selected AND MACD histogram > 0))
Short: Adaptive SuperTrend direction flips down AND close < its EMA AND (momentum filter disabled OR (RSI mode selected AND RSI < its midline) OR (MACD mode selected AND MACD histogram < 0))
