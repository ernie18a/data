Calculation: Power score = clamp(blend(Base score, completed-bar multi-timeframe score, PVM score, and completed-bar multi-timeframe PVM score using the configured MTF and PVM settings), 0, 100). Base score = 0.40 × Organ score + 0.25 × MATC position + 0.20 × EMA ribbon score + 0.15 × RSI(14). Organ score = clamp(EMA(2 × z(EMA trend change) + 1.5 × z(ROC) + z(OBV change) + 0.5 × z(ATR change × price direction), smoothing length) / 6 × 50 + 50, 0, 100); each z-score uses the same lookback, and price direction is +1 when close is above EMA(20), otherwise −1. MATC position = clamp(50 × (close − band midpoint) / band range + 50, 0, 100), where the midpoint and range are formed from smoothed mirrored upper and lower bands around hl2. EMA ribbon score = 65 when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA, 35 when below, otherwise 50. PVM score = 0.40 × EMA(normalized rolling correlation of price and volume changes) + 0.35 × EMA(normalized product of relative volume and ATR expansion) + 0.25 × EMA(normalized price ROC); each normalization uses its own longer lookback. Multi-timeframe scores use the same score calculations on each configured higher timeframe's last completed bar, weighted 0.30 for the fastest, 0.35 for the middle, and 0.35 for the slowest. The base and multi-timeframe scores are blended 0.45/0.55 when MTF is enabled; otherwise use the base score. Blend the resulting raw and PVM scores using the configured PVM weight when enabled; otherwise use the raw score.
Long: Power score ≥ configured bull threshold AND current trend direction is not long
Short: Power score ≤ configured bear threshold AND current trend direction is not short
