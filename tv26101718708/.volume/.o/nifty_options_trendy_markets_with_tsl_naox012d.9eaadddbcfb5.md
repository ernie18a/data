Calculation: Volume spike = volume > the simple moving average of volume multiplied by a multiplier
Calculation: Long trailing stop = close − ATR multiplied by a multiplier
Calculation: Short trailing stop = close + ATR multiplied by a multiplier
Long: Close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band AND ADX > entry threshold AND volume spike AND close > Supertrend
Short: Close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band AND ADX > entry threshold AND volume spike AND close < Supertrend
Exit: Long, MACD line crosses below signal line OR close < long trailing stop OR ADX < exit threshold
Exit: Short, MACD line crosses above signal line OR close > short trailing stop OR ADX < exit threshold
