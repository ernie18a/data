Calculation: Inside bar = current high is no higher than the prior bar’s high and current low is no lower than the prior bar’s low.
Calculation: Setup trigger levels = the high and low of the latest inside bar, or the first inside bar in a consecutive run if that anchoring mode is selected.
Calculation: ATR = simple average of true range on the selected timeframe, using its last completed bar; use the chart timeframe when it is higher than the selected timeframe.
Calculation: Structural stop = below the inside-bar low for a long or above its high for a short, offset by an ATR buffer; alternatively, place the stop an ATR multiple from the trigger. Freeze ATR and stop at setup formation.
Calculation: Risk in ATR = the greater of the planned long and short stop distances divided by ATR.
Long: A valid inside-bar setup exists, its risk in ATR is within the configured maximum when the risk filter is enabled, and a completed bar closes above the setup’s high trigger; if wick confirmation is selected, the bar’s high instead exceeds the trigger. If regime gating is enabled, the timeframe alignment direction is bullish; if relative-strength gating is enabled, the instrument’s return relative to the benchmark over the shorter configured lookback is positive.
Short: A valid inside-bar setup exists, its risk in ATR is within the configured maximum when the risk filter is enabled, and a completed bar closes below the setup’s low trigger; if wick confirmation is selected, the bar’s low instead falls below the trigger. If regime gating is enabled, the timeframe alignment direction is bearish; if relative-strength gating is enabled, the instrument’s return relative to the benchmark over the shorter configured lookback is negative.
Exit: Long, low is at or below the frozen long stop, or a completed bar closes below the long trigger before any target is reached, or the highest configured R-multiple target is reached, or the configured tracking period expires.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the frozen short stop, or a completed bar closes above the short trigger before any target is reached, or the highest configured R-multiple target is reached, or the configured tracking period expires.
