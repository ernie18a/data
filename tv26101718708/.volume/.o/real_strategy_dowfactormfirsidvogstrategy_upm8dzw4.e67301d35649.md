Calculation: Selected momentum = RSI of close, or RSI of the rolling sum of volume × close on up bars against the rolling sum of volume × close on down bars; both use the same lookback.
Calculation: Adjusted R² = adjusted multiple R² for close against volume and the selected, market-type-adjusted index close, using the same lookback; index values use the chart timeframe’s bar still forming.
Long: Selected momentum > 50 − 25 × adjusted R²
Short: Selected momentum < 50 − 25 × adjusted R²
