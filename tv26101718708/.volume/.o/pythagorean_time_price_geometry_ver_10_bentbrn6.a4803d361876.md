Calculation: Anchor swing = the accepted high/low pivot pair within the configured history whose bar separation is closest to the configured expected duration and whose price range, range-to-duration ratio, stability, and score pass the configured swing-selection rules; swing duration is the absolute bar distance and swing range is the absolute price difference.
Calculation: Grid cell = selected triple’s price-leg/time-leg ratio × anchor swing range × grid scale; grid levels are each cell’s bottom, top, and midpoint, repeated from the configured grid origin. A close is at a grid level when its distance from any such level is less than 15% of one cell.
Calculation: Pythagorean time node = true when the bar’s distance from the earlier anchor pivot, modulo the rounded hypotenuse period, equals the rounded half-square node, rounded half-hypotenuse node, full period, or swing duration; test both the base and doubled swing duration. For each duration, the triangle legs both equal that duration and the period is the rounded hypotenuse of those legs.
Calculation: Strong candle = absolute(close − open) ≥ 0.3 × ATR over the configured base period.
Long: (Pythagorean time node AND close is at a grid level OR configured price-only signals are enabled AND NOT Pythagorean time node AND close is within 15% of a grid cell of its bottom level) AND close > open AND Strong candle
Short: (Pythagorean time node AND close is at a grid level OR configured price-only signals are enabled AND NOT Pythagorean time node AND close is within 15% of a grid cell of its top level) AND close < open AND Strong candle
