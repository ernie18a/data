Calculation: Bar delta = volume when close > open, minus volume when close < open; when close = open, use the sign of close minus the prior close to sign volume, or zero if unchanged. For higher-timeframe bars, sum constituent bar deltas; use the still-forming bar for live higher-timeframe reads.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on each side; use the latest pivot only after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Market structure = bullish after a close above the latest confirmed pivot high, bearish after a close below the latest confirmed pivot low, and otherwise retains its previous state.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP and deviation = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close)/3 over a rolling window of completed higher-timeframe bars, and the volume-weighted standard deviation over that same window.
Calculation: Session value area = the price interval containing the configured share of session volume, expanded outward from the session point of control; distribute each bar's volume across its high-low range. Use the current session profile.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias score = weighted sum of acceptance, structure, and flow votes on the selected higher timeframe's own bars. Acceptance is the direction of the rolling-profile point of control's change versus its value the configured number of higher-timeframe bars earlier; structure is the latched pivot-break state; flow is the direction of recency-weighted signed volume, normalized by average absolute signed volume, when it exceeds the configured dead zone. Use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bias score = weighted sum of acceptance, market-structure, and flow votes. Acceptance is the direction of the rolling profile point of control's change over the lookback; flow is the direction of recency-weighted bar delta, normalized by average absolute bar delta, when it exceeds the configured dead zone.
Calculation: MDP read score = weighted sum of acceptance, structure, location, flow, and print-path votes, multiplied by a sponsorship factor. The votes are respectively the direction of rolling-profile point-of-control migration, market structure, price above/below the selected profile's value area, live higher-timeframe delta direction, and whether the newer half of recent qualifying-print midpoint prices is higher/lower than the older half by at least the configured ATR distance. Sponsorship is the average of participation rank divided by 100 and fuel, scaled between the configured damp floor and 1.
Long: Any enabled candle-paint mode reads bullish: live delta-timeframe delta > 0; or bar delta > 0 for delta-percent or fuel-by-delta; or close above the reference point of control plus its neutral band for delta-size modes; or live point-of-control migration > 0 for POC trend; or close above the latest confirmed pivot high (chart bars or delta-timeframe bars, according to mode); or market structure is bullish; or close > EMA; or close > previous completed day's high; or close > the completed Asian-session high; or close > rolling VWAP (including when stretched beyond the exhaustion threshold with bar delta > 0); or close > session value-area high; or close > open for candle conviction; or RSI > 50 (unless an active confirmed bearish pivot divergence overrides it); or a release-up condition is met; or bias score reaches its bullish threshold; or higher-timeframe bias score reaches its bullish threshold; or MDP read score reaches its bullish threshold; or the qualifying-print tally reaches its bullish threshold.
Short: Any enabled candle-paint mode reads bearish: live delta-timeframe delta < 0; or bar delta < 0 for delta-percent or fuel-by-delta; or close below the reference point of control minus its neutral band for delta-size modes; or live point-of-control migration < 0 for POC trend; or close below the latest confirmed pivot low (chart bars or delta-timeframe bars, according to mode); or market structure is bearish; or close < EMA; or close < previous completed day's low; or close < the completed Asian-session low; or close < rolling VWAP (including when stretched beyond the exhaustion threshold with bar delta < 0); or close < session value-area low; or close < open for candle conviction; or RSI < 50 (unless an active confirmed bullish pivot divergence overrides it); or a release-down condition is met; or bias score reaches its bearish threshold; or higher-timeframe bias score reaches its bearish threshold; or MDP read score reaches its bearish threshold; or the qualifying-print tally reaches its bearish threshold.
Long: A qualifying MDP print is accepted and bar delta > 0.
Short: A qualifying MDP print is accepted and bar delta < 0.
