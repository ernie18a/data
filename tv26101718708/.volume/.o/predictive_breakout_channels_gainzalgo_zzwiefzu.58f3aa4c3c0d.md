Calculation: Anchored linear regression channel = Confirm pivots using left and right lookbacks; anchor at the latest confirmed pivot high or low, with a high taking precedence if both confirm on the same bar. Regress closes from the anchor (pivot price at x=0); channel boundaries are the regression line plus or minus a standard deviation multiplier times the standard deviation of regression residuals since the anchor.
Calculation: ANOVA Z-score = Calculate a one-way F-statistic over the longer lookback for prior-bar close, low, and high as three groups: between-group mean square divided by within-group mean square. Z-score that F-statistic against its moving average and standard deviation over the same lookback.
