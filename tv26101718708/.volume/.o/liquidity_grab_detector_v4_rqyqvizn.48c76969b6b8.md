Calculation: Buy/sell liquidity pools = active levels formed from confirmed chart-timeframe swing lows/highs, prior completed daily high/low, equal swing lows/highs, and confirmed 1H/4H swing lows/highs; merge nearby levels and assign an ATR-scaled zone around each level.
Calculation: Qualifying buy-pool sweep = low < pool zone bottom, close > pool midpoint, and penetration > its minimum; alternatively, a recent close was below the zone and the current bullish candle closes back inside or slightly above it. Qualifying sell-pool sweep is the inverse.
Calculation: Sweep score = weighted score from penetration, reclaim, wick share, candle direction, pool touches/health/source, impulse-AVWAP stretch, HTF EMA alignment, relative volume, micro trend change, break distance, and channel position; apply pool-age penalty.
Calculation: Sweep reversal type = absorption when volume z-score > its threshold, wick share > its threshold, and reclaim > its threshold; otherwise rejection when wick share and reclaim exceed their thresholds and candle direction agrees; otherwise divergence when volume z-score and reclaim exceed their thresholds and wick share is below its threshold; otherwise exhaustion. Grind sweeps use the grind type.
Calculation: SFP percentile rank = percentile rank of an exhaustion score over its lookback; exhaustion score combines normalized close distance from impulse AVWAP and daily AVWAP, wick skew, positive volume z-score, and EMA-rate-of-change curvature.
Long: a qualifying buy-pool sweep with an allowed reversal type and its minimum score, subject to the sweep cooldown; OR an armed long SFP with SFP enabled, close above the highest high of the preceding BOS lookback, session gate passed, and SFP cooldown passed. Arm long SFP when SFP percentile rank is at or below its lower threshold and either impulse-AVWAP stretch is sufficiently negative with positive volume z-score, or a qualifying buy-pool sweep occurs.
Short: a qualifying sell-pool sweep with an allowed reversal type and its minimum score, subject to the sweep cooldown; OR an armed short SFP with SFP enabled, close below the lowest low of the preceding BOS lookback, session gate passed, and SFP cooldown passed. Arm short SFP when SFP percentile rank is at or above its upper threshold and either impulse-AVWAP stretch is sufficiently positive with positive volume z-score, or a qualifying sell-pool sweep occurs.
