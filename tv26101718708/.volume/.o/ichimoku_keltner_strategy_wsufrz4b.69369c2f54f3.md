Calculation: Keltner upper and lower bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± SMA(true range, same lookback) × multiplier
Calculation: Ichimoku conversion line = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback; base line = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a longer lookback
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume ÷ previous bar's volume × 100
Calculation: Average volume ratio = average of volume[i] ÷ volume[i+1] × 100 over a lookback, including the current and previous bars
Long: close > Keltner upper band AND Ichimoku conversion line > Ichimoku base line AND volume ratio > average volume ratio × multiplier
Short: close < Keltner lower band AND Ichimoku conversion line < Ichimoku base line AND volume ratio > average volume ratio × multiplier
Exit: long, close < Keltner lower band AND Ichimoku conversion line < Ichimoku base line AND volume ratio > average volume ratio × multiplier
Exit: short, close > Keltner upper band AND Ichimoku conversion line > Ichimoku base line AND volume ratio > average volume ratio × multiplier
