Calculation: Signed delta = estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume, using the selected intrabar tick, bulk-volume, or candle-geometry classifier; if unavailable, estimate buy and sell volume from the close’s position in the bar range.
Calculation: Delta z-score = current signed delta standardized against its rolling history using either median and median absolute deviation or mean and standard deviation, as selected.
Calculation: Timeframe flow z-score = candle-geometry signed volume delta, standardized against its rolling history by median and median absolute deviation. Calculate separately for each rung of the selected flow timeframe ladder, including the chart timeframe; use the latest value available with lookahead off (a forming higher-timeframe bar in live data, completed bars in historical data).
Calculation: Cumulative delta = running sum of signed delta, reset at each session when session anchoring is enabled.
Calculation: Cumulative-delta channel = rolling linear-regression value of cumulative delta, with upper and lower bands one selected multiple of residual standard deviation from the regression.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = ratcheting stop at a selected multiple of ATR from price extremes; use the selected chart or higher-timeframe ATR, with the latest value available using lookahead off.
Long: Delta z-score crosses above its positive signal threshold AND its long excursion is armed AND the selected regime filter passes AND (multi-timeframe gating is off OR timeframe-flow alignment is positive).
Short: Delta z-score crosses below its negative signal threshold AND its short excursion is armed AND the selected regime filter passes AND (multi-timeframe gating is off OR timeframe-flow alignment is negative).
Long: Timeframe-flow alignment reaches the positive alignment threshold AND the long alignment episode is armed AND cooldown has elapsed AND, if enabled, flow velocity is positive and above its threshold AND the highest two active timeframe flows are nonnegative (when that filter is enabled) AND fused conviction meets its minimum AND, within the selected confluence window, a same-direction cumulative-delta channel breakout is recent if that gate is enabled AND flow toxicity is below its suppression threshold if suppression is enabled AND the long side is not muted by the enabled edge gate.
Short: Timeframe-flow alignment reaches the negative alignment threshold AND the short alignment episode is armed AND cooldown has elapsed AND, if enabled, flow velocity is negative and below its negative threshold AND the highest two active timeframe flows are nonpositive (when that filter is enabled) AND fused conviction meets its minimum AND, within the selected confluence window, a same-direction cumulative-delta channel breakout is recent if that gate is enabled AND flow toxicity is below its suppression threshold if suppression is enabled AND the short side is not muted by the enabled edge gate.
Long: On an enabled higher-timeframe stack rung, its flow z-score changes from neutral or negative to above the positive neutral threshold.
Short: On an enabled higher-timeframe stack rung, its flow z-score changes from neutral or positive to below the negative neutral threshold.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low is below the prior confirmed pivot low AND cumulative delta at the new pivot is above cumulative delta at the prior pivot (when divergence markers are enabled).
Short: A confirmed price pivot high is above the prior confirmed pivot high AND cumulative delta at the new pivot is below cumulative delta at the prior pivot (when divergence markers are enabled).
Long: ATR trailing-stop direction changes from short to long.
Short: ATR trailing-stop direction changes from long to short.
Exit: Long, close <= ATR trailing stop OR low <= active stop-loss OR high >= active second target.
Exit: Short, close >= ATR trailing stop OR high >= active stop-loss OR low <= active second target.
