Calculation: Bull score = 1 if short EMA > medium EMA > long EMA, plus 1 if either the latest confirmed swing high or latest confirmed swing low exceeds its prior confirmed value, plus 1 if RSI > 50 and RSI < overbought, plus 1 if MACD line > signal line and MACD line > 0, plus 1 if MACD line crosses above signal line, plus 1 if smoothed stochastic K > D and K < 80, plus 3 if volume > volume moving average × high-volume multiplier and close > open, plus 1 if a bullish candle pattern occurs, plus 2 if price is above Supertrend, plus 2 if the current bar overlaps the latest bullish order-block range, plus 3 if the 14-bar low is below the 28-bar low and the 14-bar RSI low is above the 28-bar RSI low
Calculation: Bear score = 1 if short EMA < medium EMA < long EMA, plus 1 if either the latest confirmed swing high or latest confirmed swing low is below its prior confirmed value, plus 1 if RSI < 50 and RSI > oversold, plus 1 if MACD line < signal line and MACD line < 0, plus 1 if MACD line crosses below signal line, plus 1 if smoothed stochastic K < D and K > 20, plus 3 if volume > volume moving average × high-volume multiplier and close < open, plus 1 if a bearish candle pattern occurs, plus 2 if price is below Supertrend, plus 2 if the current bar overlaps the latest bearish order-block range, plus 3 if the 14-bar high is above the 28-bar high, the 14-bar RSI high is below the 28-bar RSI high, and short EMA < medium EMA
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = bullish engulfing OR bullish pin bar OR prior inside bar followed by close above the high two bars ago OR doji after a down candle; pin bars and engulfing, inside-bar, and doji patterns count only when enabled
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = bearish engulfing OR bearish pin bar OR prior inside bar followed by close below the low two bars ago OR doji after an up candle; pin bars and engulfing, inside-bar, and doji patterns count only when enabled
Calculation: Bullish order-block range = high and low of the bar before the latest bar whose close is more than 1% above the prior close; bearish order-block range = high and low of the bar before the latest bar whose close is more than 1% below the prior close
Calculation: Supertrend = standard Supertrend using the configured ATR length and factor
Calculation: Risk-reward ratio = configured take-profit ATR multiple / configured stop-loss ATR multiple
Long: Bull score >= score threshold AND within the configured session excluding configured opening and closing avoid periods AND ATR / close × 100 < configured maximum ATR percentage AND risk-reward ratio >= configured minimum AND flat
Short: Bear score >= score threshold AND within the configured session excluding configured opening and closing avoid periods AND ATR / close × 100 < configured maximum ATR percentage AND risk-reward ratio >= configured minimum AND flat
Exit: Long, bear trend OR low <= close - ATR × configured stop-loss multiple OR high >= close + ATR × configured take-profit multiple OR trailing stop is hit
Exit: Short, bull trend OR high >= close + ATR × configured stop-loss multiple OR low <= close - ATR × configured take-profit multiple OR trailing stop is hit
