Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a pivot high confirmed after the selected swing length of bars on both sides; use the latest unbroken confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a pivot low confirmed after the selected swing length of bars on both sides; use the latest unbroken confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially undefined; set bullish after a bullish break and bearish after a bearish break. A break consumes that swing, so it can signal only once.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above high two bars earlier, and the gap is at least the minimum FVG size.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high is below low two bars earlier, and the gap is at least the minimum FVG size.
Long: close > VWAP AND close > EMA(200) AND EMA(50) > EMA(200) OR close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high OR bullish FVG
Short: close < VWAP AND close < EMA(200) AND EMA(50) < EMA(200) OR close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low OR bearish FVG
