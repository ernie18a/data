Calculation: Long trend-valid = EMA(short) > EMA(medium) > EMA(long), with shorter, medium, then longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Short trend-valid = EMA(short) < EMA(medium) < EMA(long), with shorter, medium, then longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Directional trend-valid score = ADX score (3 if ADX ≥ 40, 2 if ≥ 30, 1 if ≥ its configured threshold, else 0) + 1 if the current-timeframe MACD histogram agrees with the direction + 1 if RSI > 55 for long or < 45 for short + 2 if the shorter EMA is on the directional side of the longer EMA + 1 if volume exceeds its configured SMA; valid when score ≥ 4 and the shorter EMA is on that directional side.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its longer-lookback SMA; volume spike = relative volume > its configured multiplier; volume rising = current volume > prior volume and volume spike; volume SMA rising = its configured SMA > its prior value.
Calculation: OBV confirmation = OBV is above its configured SMA, its configured-lookback slope is positive, its SMA slope is positive, and close exceeds its configured-lookback close for long; reverse all comparisons for short.
Calculation: Entry confidence = absolute current-timeframe MACD histogram / its configured SMA of absolute histogram; confident when ≥ its configured threshold.
Calculation: PAC upper/lower = highest high / lowest low over its configured lookback, respectively, plus/minus its configured multiplier times close standard deviation over that lookback.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper band − lower band) / basis, using the configured SMA and standard-deviation lookback and multiplier.
Calculation: ATR volatility valid = ATR / close < its configured limit.
Calculation: Configured fast-HTF EMA trend = its medium-lookback EMA > current-timeframe long-lookback EMA for long, or < for short; slow-HTF EMA trend uses the configured slower HTF in the same comparison. Use the still-forming HTF bars.
Calculation: 15-minute MACD direction = MACD line > signal line for long, or < for short, using the configured MACD lengths. Use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Hourly trend-valid = long or short trend-valid on the hourly bars. Use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ATR volatility mode = high if fast-HTF ATR exceeds 1.2 times its 20-bar SMA, medium if it exceeds that SMA, otherwise low. Use the still-forming fast-HTF bar.
Calculation: Confidence weight = 0.30 if either direction is trend-valid + 0.25 if volume spike + 0.20 if either direction has OBV confirmation + 0.25 if fast-HTF MACD has either direction; trade block when weight < 0.5.
Calculation: Trend score up = ADX score + 1 if fast-HTF MACD is bullish + 1 if RSI > 55 + 2 if shorter EMA > longer EMA + 1 if volume exceeds its configured SMA.
Calculation: Trend score down = ADX score + 1 if fast-HTF MACD is bearish + 1 if RSI < 45 + 2 if shorter EMA < longer EMA + 1 if volume exceeds its configured SMA.
Calculation: Reversal cooldown = active while its counter is positive; on a confirmed opposite-direction reversal, reset it to the configured cooldown, decrement it on each bar, and suppress reversal markings while active. Confirmed reversal requires RSI in the configured overbought/oversold zone, a MACD cross in the corresponding direction, and reversal strength ≥ 50; approaching reversal requires the same RSI zone with MACD already on the opposing side of its signal. Reversal strength uses the RSI-zone, MACD-cross, and normalized OBV-delta scores, plus the configured weighted HTF confirmation when enabled. Use the still-forming fast-HTF RSI and MACD bars.
Calculation: Strictness entry = for High, directional trend-valid and not ranging and matching fast-HTF MACD and close beyond prior high/low and matching candle direction and close beyond prior close ± ATR × configured multiplier; for Medium, directional trend-valid and (matching fast-HTF MACD or current MACD histogram direction) and (close beyond prior high/low or shorter EMA) and (matching candle direction or RSI on the directional side of 50) and close beyond 98% of the upper band for long or 102% of the lower band for short; for Low, directional trend-valid and (matching fast-HTF MACD or current MACD histogram direction) and (matching candle direction or RSI on the directional side of 50) and the same 98%/102% band comparison. In Low mode, also allow ranging entries when MACD histogram, RSI, volume spike, close versus shorter EMA, and close versus the ATR band all agree directionally. Auto mode selects strictness from higher-timeframe ATR volatility mode; otherwise use the configured strictness.
Calculation: Valid execution candle = for long, close > open and (close − open)/(high − low) > 0.5; for short, close < open and (open − close)/(high − low) > 0.5.
Calculation: Structure stop = for long, the higher of entry price − 0.5 × ATR and the configured-lookback lowest low; for short, the lower of entry price + 0.5 × ATR and the configured-lookback highest high.
Calculation: Structure target = entry price plus (entry price − long structure stop) × configured risk/reward for long; entry price minus (short structure stop − entry price) × configured risk/reward for short.
Long: (strictness entry is long AND confidence weight ≥ 0.5 AND hourly trend-valid is long AND close differs from the close on the prior bar where long strictness entry was true) OR (long trend-valid AND confidence weight ≥ 0.85 AND volume spike AND fast-HTF MACD bullish AND trend score up > 2 AND close exceeds the prior five-bar highest high AND close differs from the last valid buy or sell signal close) OR (oversold RSI and bullish MACD cross and not reversal cooldown) OR (oversold RSI and MACD above signal and not reversal cooldown) OR (strictness entry is long AND confidence weight ≥ 0.5 AND hourly trend-valid is long AND valid execution candle AND Bollinger width exceeds its configured threshold AND ADX exceeds its configured trend threshold AND confidence weight ≥ 0.85).
Short: (strictness entry is short AND confidence weight ≥ 0.5 AND hourly trend-valid is short AND close differs from the close on the prior bar where short strictness entry was true) OR (short trend-valid AND confidence weight ≥ 0.85 AND volume spike AND fast-HTF MACD bearish AND trend score down > 2 AND close falls below the prior five-bar lowest low AND close differs from the last valid buy or sell signal close) OR (overbought RSI and bearish MACD cross and not reversal cooldown) OR (overbought RSI and MACD below signal and not reversal cooldown) OR (strictness entry is short AND confidence weight ≥ 0.5 AND hourly trend-valid is short AND valid execution candle AND Bollinger width exceeds its configured threshold AND ADX exceeds its configured trend threshold AND confidence weight ≥ 0.85).
Exit: Long, hourly trend-valid is short OR price reaches the entry limit at signal close + ATR × (configured base multiplier + confidence weight × 0.01) OR the ATR trailing stop is hit (trail activates after the configured ATR distance and trails by its configured ATR offset) OR low ≤ long structure stop OR high ≥ long structure target.
Exit: Short, hourly trend-valid is long OR price reaches the entry limit at signal close − ATR × (configured base multiplier + confidence weight × 0.01) OR the ATR trailing stop is hit (trail activates after the configured ATR distance and trails by its configured ATR offset) OR high ≥ short structure stop OR low ≤ short structure target.
