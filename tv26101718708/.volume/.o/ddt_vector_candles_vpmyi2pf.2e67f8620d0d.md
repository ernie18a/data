Calculation: Average volume = average volume over a lookback of prior completed bars on the same timeframe; use the same lookback as for highest volume spread
Calculation: Volume spread = volume × (high − low)
Calculation: Highest volume spread = maximum volume spread over a lookback of prior completed bars on the same timeframe; use the same lookback as for average volume
Long: close > open AND (volume ≥ configured multiple × average volume OR volume spread ≥ highest volume spread)
Short: close ≤ open AND (volume ≥ configured multiple × average volume OR volume spread ≥ highest volume spread)
