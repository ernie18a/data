Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume / simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: ATR-normalized spread = (high − low) / ATR; body share = abs(close − open) / (high − low); close location = (close − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Trend alignment score = clamp to 0–100: 34 if close is on the event side of the slow EMA, otherwise 12; plus 36 if the fast EMA is on the event side of the slow EMA; plus up to 30 for the slow EMA slope on the event side, linearly normalized by abs(slope)/ATR from a small positive floor to a larger threshold. Use a shorter-lookback fast EMA and a longer-lookback slow EMA; slope compares the slow EMA with its value a configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Event quality score = clamp to 0–100 of 32% volume score + 20% spread score + 18% event-side close-location score + 14% body-share score + 16% trend alignment score. Volume score rises linearly from a lower relative-volume threshold to an ideal, then falls linearly to zero at a higher ceiling; other component scores rise linearly from their weak thresholds to their strong thresholds. Clamp component scores to 0–100. Use the bull close location for long events and its inverse for short events.
Calculation: Event reference levels = event close; event ATR; and risk rail at event low minus event ATR buffer for long events or event high plus event ATR buffer for short events.
Calculation: Follow-through score = clamp to 0–100 of 28% event quality + 28% best favorable progress score + 18% next-bar close progress score + 12% follow-through close-count score + 10% risk-rail defense score + 4% time score. Favorable progress is the best high/low excursion from event close, in event ATRs; next-bar progress is the next bar's close movement from event close, in event ATRs. The progress, close-count, defense, and time scores are linearly normalized and clamped to 0–100; defense is zero after a rail break. Count closes beyond event close on the event side during the follow-through window.
Long: close >= open AND relative volume >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum volume AND relative volume <= maximum volume ceiling AND ATR-normalized spread >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum spread AND body share >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum body share AND close location >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum close edge AND bull event quality score >= minimum event quality AND at least the configurable cooldown bars since the previous qualified event.
Short: close <= open AND relative volume >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum volume AND relative volume <= maximum volume ceiling AND ATR-normalized spread >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum spread AND body share >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum body share AND (1 − close location) >= sensitivity-adjusted minimum close edge AND bear event quality score >= minimum event quality AND at least the configurable cooldown bars since the previous qualified event.
Long: an active long event is within its tracking window AND close > event close AND risk rail is not broken AND follow-through score >= minimum planner score AND progress score >= 45 AND follow-through close count >= minimum follow-through closes.
Short: an active short event is within its tracking window AND close < event close AND risk rail is not broken AND follow-through score >= minimum planner score AND progress score >= 45 AND follow-through close count >= minimum follow-through closes.
Exit: long, close <= the active event's risk rail (event low minus event ATR buffer).
Exit: short, close >= the active event's risk rail (event high plus event ATR buffer).
