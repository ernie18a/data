Calculation: Adjusted fast moving average = fast moving average minus ATR when the fast moving average is above the slower moving average; otherwise, fast moving average plus ATR. Both moving averages use the selected signal timeframe and same type; the slower uses a longer lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed lower band = Gaussian-kernel weighted average of the lower Bollinger band over past bars. The lower Bollinger band is the moving average of typical price minus a deviation multiple of its standard deviation; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Smoothed upper band = Gaussian-kernel weighted average of the upper Bollinger band over past bars. The upper Bollinger band is the moving average of typical price plus a deviation multiple of its standard deviation.
Calculation: Dynamic cooldown = base cooldown, or base cooldown multiplied by long-lookback ATR divided by short-lookback ATR when adaptive cooldown is enabled; apply the configured minimum.
Long: selected-timeframe low crosses above adjusted fast moving average AND trend is bullish (fast moving average > slower moving average) AND trend did not change on this bar AND short-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR multiplied by the volatility threshold when the volatility filter is enabled AND current bar index minus the bar index of the last trend retest signal > dynamic cooldown AND no trend retest signal has occurred since the latest trend change when the smart filter is enabled AND close > smoothed lower band when the band filter is enabled AND bar is confirmed
Short: selected-timeframe high crosses below adjusted fast moving average AND trend is bearish (fast moving average <= slower moving average) AND trend did not change on this bar AND short-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR multiplied by the volatility threshold when the volatility filter is enabled AND current bar index minus the bar index of the last trend retest signal > dynamic cooldown AND no trend retest signal has occurred since the latest trend change when the smart filter is enabled AND close < smoothed upper band when the band filter is enabled AND bar is confirmed
Long: close crosses above smoothed lower band AND no trend retest signal has occurred since the latest trend change
Short: close crosses below smoothed upper band AND no trend retest signal has occurred since the latest trend change
