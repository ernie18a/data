Calculation: Candle conviction = clamp(0.55 × signed candle body/range + 0.30 × (2 × close − high − low)/range + 0.15 × (lower wick − upper wick)/range, −1, 1), where signed body/range is positive for an up candle, negative for a down candle, and zero for an unchanged candle.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its simple moving average.
Calculation: Price impulse = clamp((close − close N bars ago) / (ATR × √N), −C, C) / C, where N is the impulse lookback and C is the component clamp.
Calculation: Range expansion = clamp(true range / its simple moving average − 1, 0, C) / C.
Calculation: Trend alignment = clamp(0.5 × clamp((close − trend EMA) / ATR, −C, C) / C + 0.5 × clamp((trend EMA − its value N bars ago) / ATR, −C, C) / C, −1, 1), using a longer EMA length than the slope lookback.
Calculation: Momentum efficiency = 0.65 × clamp((RSI − 50) / 25, −1, 1) + 0.35 × sign(close − close N bars ago) × clamp(abs(close − close N bars ago) / (N × average absolute one-bar close change), 0, 1).
Calculation: Market energy = EMA of clamp(100 × weighted average of price impulse, candle conviction, directional volume, directional range expansion, trend alignment, and momentum efficiency + 12 × clamp(ADX / 50, 0, 1) × (positive DI − negative DI)/(positive DI + negative DI), −100, 100). Directional volume is candle conviction × (0.40 + 0.60 × clamp(relative volume − 1, 0, C)/C); directional range expansion is clamp((0.60 × candle conviction + 0.40 × price impulse) × range expansion, −1, 1). Each component's weight is configurable.
Calculation: Energy signal line = EMA of market energy with a longer smoothing length than the market-energy EMA.
Calculation: Energy acceleration = EMA of (market energy − market energy N bars ago), with a short smoothing length.
Long: Market energy crosses above the positive entry threshold AND market energy > energy signal line AND energy acceleration ≥ minimum acceleration AND trend alignment > 0 AND positive DI > negative DI AND close > open AND candle body/range ≥ minimum body ratio AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long-entry signal.
Long: A compressed energy reading occurred within the recent lookback AND market energy crosses above the active threshold AND energy acceleration > minimum acceleration AND trend alignment > 0 AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior bullish release signal.
Long: Market energy crosses above the strong-energy threshold.
Short: Market energy crosses below the negative entry threshold AND market energy < energy signal line AND energy acceleration ≤ negative minimum acceleration AND trend alignment < 0 AND negative DI > positive DI AND close < open AND candle body/range ≥ minimum body ratio AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior short-entry signal.
Short: A compressed energy reading occurred within the recent lookback AND market energy crosses below the negative active threshold AND energy acceleration < negative minimum acceleration AND trend alignment < 0 AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior bearish release signal.
Short: Market energy crosses below the negative strong-energy threshold.
Exit: long, the highest market energy in the recent exhaustion lookback is at least the extreme threshold AND exceeds current market energy by at least the exhaustion drop AND energy acceleration < 0 AND (close < open OR close < prior close) AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior bullish-exhaustion signal.
Exit: short, the lowest market energy in the recent exhaustion lookback is at most the negative extreme threshold AND current market energy exceeds it by at least the exhaustion drop AND energy acceleration > 0 AND (close > open OR close > prior close) AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior bearish-exhaustion signal.
