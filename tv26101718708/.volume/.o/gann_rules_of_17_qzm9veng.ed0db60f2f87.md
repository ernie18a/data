Calculation: FallingCloseCount(n) = count of consecutive close-to-prior-close declines across the last n bars
Calculation: BullRuleCount = count of active bullish rules: Friday bar makes the rolling 5-bar high; Tuesday bar makes the rolling 5-bar low and closes above open; rolling 20-bar high is made and exceeds prior high; close crosses above the moving average; positive candle body is at least 2 ATR; rolling 3-bar high is made, close is above the moving average, and high exceeds prior high; close exceeds close n bars ago and high exceeds high n bars ago and FallingCloseCount(3-bar lookback) is at least 2; close is within 1 ATR of the midpoint of the rolling 10-bar high-low range and close is above the moving average; low is below lows 2 and 4 bars ago and close is above open; ATR exceeds 1.5 times its longer-lookback average and candle body is at least ATR with close above the moving average; rolling 60-bar high is made and exceeds prior high
Calculation: BearRuleCount = count of active bearish rules: Friday bar makes the rolling 5-bar low; Wednesday bar makes the rolling 5-bar high and closes below open; rolling 20-bar low is made and is below prior low; the growing-correction condition is true; close exceeds close n bars ago and high exceeds high n bars ago and FallingCloseCount(5-bar lookback) is at least 3 and close is below the moving average; close is within 1 ATR of the midpoint of the rolling 10-bar high-low range and close is below the moving average; high is above highs 2 and 4 bars ago and close is below open; ATR exceeds 1.5 times its longer-lookback average and candle body is at least ATR with close below the moving average; rolling 60-bar low is made and is below prior low
Calculation: GrowingCorrection = absolute difference between the high 5 bars ago and current low exceeds both absolute difference between the high 10 bars ago and low 5 bars ago, and 1.5 ATR
Long: any bullish rule is active OR (BullRuleCount >= minimum rule count AND BullRuleCount > BearRuleCount)
Short: any bearish rule is active OR (BearRuleCount >= minimum rule count AND BearRuleCount > BullRuleCount)
