Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low + minimum price increment).
Calculation: Bull and bear posterior probabilities = Gaussian Naive Bayes posteriors for three classes (bullish when price and CVD rates of change are both positive; bearish when both are negative; otherwise divergent). Features are z-scored CVD rate of change, price rate of change minus CVD rate of change, and z-scored linear-regression slope of CVD. Estimate each class's feature means, standard deviations, and prior from previously completed bars; multiply the three Gaussian likelihoods by the class prior and normalize across classes. Use the same rate-of-change lookback for CVD and price, and the same z-score lookback for all features.
Long: Cumulative volume delta > its value on the previous bar OR trained bar count ≥ warmup threshold AND bull posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close > EMA(close).
Short: Cumulative volume delta ≤ its value on the previous bar OR trained bar count ≥ warmup threshold AND bear posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close < EMA(close).
