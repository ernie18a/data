Calculation: Impulse size = absolute difference between current close and close from the pole lookback bars earlier; impulse ATR multiple = impulse size / current ATR; impulse efficiency = impulse size / sum of true ranges over the pole lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Trend alignment = for a bullish setup, current close is above the trend EMA or the shorter EMA is above the trend EMA; for a bearish setup, current close is below the trend EMA or the shorter EMA is below the trend EMA. The shorter EMA lookback is approximately half the trend EMA lookback.
Calculation: Flag boundaries = highest high and lowest low over the flag window, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Flag depth = bullish: (pole end close − flag low) / impulse size; bearish: (flag high − pole end close) / impulse size
Calculation: Flag range = (flag high − flag low) / impulse size
Calculation: Flag compression = bullish: slope of the flag-window linear regression of close is at most 0.24 ATR; bearish: that slope is at least −0.24 ATR
Calculation: Pole candidate = a bullish candidate when the impulse is positive, exceeds the minimum ATR multiple, meets the minimum efficiency, and has bullish trend alignment; bearish is the corresponding negative impulse with bearish alignment. Lock a qualifying candidate; replace it with an opposite-side candidate, or with a same-side candidate whose ATR multiple is more than 1.14 times the locked candidate's. A candidate expires after the flag window plus the greater of 8 bars or half the flag window (rounded).
Calculation: Setup quality = round and clamp to 0–100 the sum of: clamp((candidate pole ATR multiple / minimum pole ATR multiple) × 28, 0, 30); clamp((candidate pole efficiency / minimum pole efficiency) × 15, 0, 18); clamp(((1 − flag depth) / maximum flag depth) × 18, 0, 20); clamp(((1 − flag range / maximum flag range) × 14), 0, 15); 10 if flag compression passes, otherwise 3; 10 if trend aligned, otherwise 4; 7 if volume passes, otherwise 0; and clamp(breakout distance in ATR × 8, 0, 8). Breakout distance in ATR is bullish: (close − flag high) / ATR; bearish: (flag low − close) / ATR.
