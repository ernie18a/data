Calculation: ATR = average true range; EMA = exponential moving average; ROC = percentage change in close over the lookback; smoothed momentum = EMA of ROC; momentum slope = smoothed momentum minus its value at the slope lookback; normalized base-EMA slope = (base EMA − its value at the slope lookback) / ATR
Calculation: Bull momentum context = fast EMA > base EMA > slow EMA, normalized base-EMA slope meets the configured minimum positive slope, and smoothed momentum meets the configured minimum positive momentum; bear momentum context is the inverse. Apply the configured side restriction and timeframe-profile gate.
Calculation: Reset pocket = for bull context, [base EMA − ATR × pocket width, fast EMA + ATR × 0.10]; for bear context, [fast EMA − ATR × 0.10, base EMA + ATR × pocket width]. A pocket touch occurs when the bar range intersects it; recent reset means a valid pocket touch occurred within the configured reset window, including the current bar.
Calculation: Pullback depth in ATR = for bull context, (highest high over the impulse lookback − current low) / ATR; for bear context, (current high − lowest low over that lookback) / ATR. The impulse lookback is configurable.
Calculation: Trigger = fast EMA. Invalidation rail = for bull context, the lower of the reset-pocket bottom and slow EMA minus ATR × risk buffer; for bear context, the higher of the reset-pocket top and slow EMA plus ATR × risk buffer. Target guide = for bull context, highest high over the impulse lookback plus ATR × 0.35; for bear context, lowest low over that lookback minus ATR × 0.35.
Calculation: Continuation score = clamp to 0–100 of 0.20 × trend score + 0.22 × momentum score + 0.24 × reset-quality score + 0.18 × recovery score + 0.08 × volume score + 0.08 × target-room score. Trend score weights normalized directional base-EMA slope 0.55 and normalized fast-to-slow EMA spread in ATR 0.45. Momentum score weights normalized directional smoothed momentum 0.55 and normalized directional momentum slope 0.45. Reset-quality score weights normalized pullback depth, scored highest at the configured ideal depth and declining toward configured shallow and maximum depths, 0.68, and pocket score 0.32 (100 for recent reset, otherwise 80 for a current touch, otherwise 45 for a valid pocket). Recovery score weights normalized directional close-to-trigger distance in ATR 0.58 and normalized close location within the bar 0.42. Volume score uses normalized inverse volume/current volume SMA over its lookback when not triggering (weighted 0.65 plus 35); on a qualified trigger, use normalized volume ratio. Target-room score is normalized directional distance from close to target guide in ATR. Normalization maps the configured weak/good and strong/bad bounds linearly to 0–100 and clamps to that range; peak scoring rises to the ideal depth then falls.
