Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close; bearish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot high in price is higher than the preceding price pivot high while the latest RSI pivot high is lower than the preceding RSI pivot high; bullish divergence = a newly confirmed pivot low in price is lower than the preceding price pivot low while the latest RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding RSI pivot low
Calculation: Resistance levels = the latest quick pivot high and the latest three confirmed standard pivot highs; support levels = the latest quick pivot low and the latest three confirmed standard pivot lows; each pivot is available after its right-side confirmation bars
Calculation: Major pivot high and low = the latest major swing highs and lows from the confirmed pivot structure; a pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks, and major pivots are distinguished from intervening minor pivots
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Long: (price wicks below the latest major support and closes back above it OR close is within the minimum S/R spacing percentage of any support level) AND the latest major resistance is above the latest major support AND the distance between them exceeds the minimum S/R spacing percentage of support AND (RSI is at or below oversold OR bullish divergence) AND RSI is at or below oversold AND RSI is rising AND the preceding bar did not satisfy this full condition
Long: After a sell-side liquidity sweep below the rolling higher-timeframe-range low, once the sweep bar has passed, price high exceeds the previous bar's highest high over the short market-structure lookback
Long: Close crosses above the current major pivot high and that pivot has not already been broken
Long: Close is above the current minor pivot high and that pivot has not already been broken
Long: Close is above the ATR trailing stop and crosses above it
Short: (price wicks above the latest major resistance and closes back below it OR close is within the minimum S/R spacing percentage of any resistance level) AND the latest major resistance is above the latest major support AND the distance between them exceeds the minimum S/R spacing percentage of support AND (RSI is at or above the near-overbought threshold OR bearish divergence) AND RSI is at or above the near-overbought threshold AND RSI is falling AND the preceding bar did not satisfy this full condition
Short: After a buy-side liquidity sweep above the rolling higher-timeframe-range high, once the sweep bar has passed, price low falls below the previous bar's lowest low over the short market-structure lookback
Short: Close crosses below the current major pivot low and that pivot has not already been broken
Short: Close is below the current minor pivot low and that pivot has not already been broken
Short: Close is below the ATR trailing stop and crosses below it
Exit: Long, price high is at or above the take-profit target OR price low is at or below the stop-loss target
Exit: Short, price low is at or below the take-profit target OR price high is at or above the stop-loss target
Calculation: Higher-timeframe range high/low = highest high and lowest low over the number of chart bars in one higher-timeframe interval; use the rolling range at setup creation
Calculation: Market-structure high/low = highest high and lowest low over the short market-structure lookback; compare the prior bar's values for entry confirmation
Calculation: Dynamic stop-loss = market-structure low minus ATR multiple for long, or market-structure high plus ATR multiple for short; take-profit = entry price plus/minus the risk distance multiplied by the reward/risk factor
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = a stateful stop that trails below the selected price source by sensitivity times ATR in rising conditions and above it in falling conditions; it ratchets while price and prior source remain on the same side, and switches to the opposite offset on a side change
