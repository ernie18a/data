Calculation: RSI pressure = SMA of a weighted blend of RSI, RSI range position, z-score-scaled fast-minus-slow RSI EMA spread, z-score-scaled RSI rate of change, RSI Bollinger %B, and relative-volume-weighted RSI; range position and %B are scaled to 0–100 and clipped, z-score components are mapped to 0–100 and clipped, and relative volume is volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Pressure trail = EMA(hlc3) plus or minus ATR multiplied by a base multiplier increased in proportion to the absolute distance of the smoothed RSI pressure from 50; use minus for the bullish trail and plus for the bearish trail.
Calculation: Pressure regime = bullish when smoothed RSI pressure is at or above its upper threshold, bearish when at or below its lower threshold, and otherwise retains the prior regime.
Calculation: RSI moving-average pairs = fast and slow moving averages of RSI, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback; the script has separate pairs for RSI and RSI pressure.
Long: RSI 5 or RSI 14 is above its upper neutral threshold OR fast RSI moving average is at or above slow RSI moving average OR fast RSI-pressure moving average is at or above slow RSI-pressure moving average OR RSI pressure is at or above its upper regime threshold AND close is at or above the bullish pressure trail minus its ATR-based tolerance OR RSI pressure is at or below its lower regime threshold AND close is above the bearish pressure trail plus its ATR-based tolerance OR a newly confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low while its matching price low is lower OR a newly confirmed RSI pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot low while its matching price low is higher.
Short: RSI 5 or RSI 14 is below its lower neutral threshold OR fast RSI moving average is below slow RSI moving average OR fast RSI-pressure moving average is below slow RSI-pressure moving average OR RSI pressure is at or below its lower regime threshold AND close is at or below the bearish pressure trail plus its ATR-based tolerance OR RSI pressure is at or above its upper regime threshold AND close is below the bullish pressure trail minus its ATR-based tolerance OR a newly confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high while its matching price high is higher OR a newly confirmed RSI pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed RSI pivot high while its matching price high is lower.
