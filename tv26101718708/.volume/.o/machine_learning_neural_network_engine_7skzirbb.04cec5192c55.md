Calculation: Bull probability = sigmoid(2.6 × combined score), where combined score is the clamped weighted blend of a causal online 6–5–1 tanh neural network output and a structural score. The network takes six clamped features: (EMA21−EMA55)/ATR14/3; (RSI14−50)/50; (close−20-bar linear regression)/ATR14/2; (close−close 10 bars earlier)/sum of absolute close changes over 10 bars; ATR14/SMA(ATR14, normalization lookback)−1; and ((close−open)/(high−low)) × (volume/SMA(volume, 50)) / 2 (use 1 when volume is unavailable). Train on each completed daily bar using the feature vector from the forecast horizon earlier and its now-known close change, normalized by the earlier ATR; use the network’s current weights to predict that outcome, then update weights by clipped-error RMS-scaled gradient descent with regularization. The structural score is clamp(0.32 × first feature + 0.13 × second + 0.10 × third + 0.35 × fourth − 0.10 × fifth, −1, 1). Blend network and structural scores using a neural influence that ramps up during warmup, then depends on recent matured prediction error relative to structural-score error; clamp the blend to [−1, 1].
Calculation: Crisis alarm = (close/close 10 bars earlier − 1 ≤ −max(0.06, 6 × ATR14/close) AND ATR14/SMA(ATR14, 100) ≥ 1.45) OR (close/highest close over 63 bars − 1 ≤ −0.14 AND close < EMA120 AND ATR14/SMA(ATR14, 100) ≥ 1.25).
Long: On a completed daily bar, bull probability ≥ entry threshold AND close > EMA34 AND (close > close 20 bars earlier OR close > EMA120) AND no crisis alarm for the configured number of consecutive bars.
Exit: long, crisis alarm OR (bull probability ≤ exit threshold AND close < EMA120 AND close < EMA250) for the configured number of consecutive completed daily bars; crisis alarm exits immediately.
