Calculation: Pivot = (previous completed daily high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: BC = (previous completed daily high + low) / 2
Calculation: TC = 2 × Pivot − BC
Calculation: CPR top = max(TC, BC)
Long: close > TC AND low <= TC
Long: low <= BC
Long: close > CPR top AND previous close <= previous CPR top
Short: close < BC AND high >= BC
Short: high >= TC
Exit: long, high >= TC
Exit: short, low <= BC
