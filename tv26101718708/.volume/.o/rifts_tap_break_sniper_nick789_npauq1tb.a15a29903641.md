Calculation: zone tap = bar high >= zone bottom AND bar low <= zone top; a zone remains tapped once tapped, until invalidated or used
Calculation: bullish zone reaction = tapped active bullish zone AND current close > current open AND current close > zone top AND current body percentage >= rejection body threshold AND current range >= ATR × rejection range threshold; reaction remains valid for the configured reaction window
Calculation: bearish zone reaction = tapped active bearish zone AND current close < current open AND current close < zone bottom AND current body percentage >= rejection body threshold AND current range >= ATR × rejection range threshold; reaction remains valid for the configured reaction window
Calculation: bullish structure break = selected structure engine breaks its latest unbroken swing high upward; bearish structure break = selected structure engine breaks its latest unbroken swing low downward. Classic engine uses confirmed pivot highs/lows with equal left/right lookback; responsive and major engines use their respective lookbacks to detect a new extreme and track alternating pivot legs. Break source is either close or wick, and the prior bar's source must be at or below the high / at or above the low before the break. A break against the engine's prior bias is CHoCH; otherwise it is BOS. Each swing level can break only once.
Calculation: bullish structure pass = bullish structure break AND selected break type allows its BOS/CHoCH classification AND (displacement filter disabled OR current close > current open AND body percentage >= break body threshold AND current range >= ATR × break range threshold)
Calculation: bearish structure pass = bearish structure break AND selected break type allows its BOS/CHoCH classification AND (displacement filter disabled OR current close < current open AND body percentage >= break body threshold AND current range >= ATR × break range threshold)
Calculation: volume pass = volume filter disabled OR volume >= volume moving average × volume multiplier
Calculation: bullish trend pass = trend filter disabled OR close >= EMA; bearish trend pass = trend filter disabled OR close <= EMA
