Calculation: opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low from the session open through the configured opening-range duration; lock the levels when that duration ends
Calculation: body fraction = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: nearest resistance/support = nearest level above/below close among the previous completed daily bar’s high, low, and close, and confirmed swing highs/lows from chart bars; swing highs/lows use the same configured lookback on both sides, retaining the most recent 30 of each
Long: opening range is locked AND within regular trading hours and the configured signal session AND close > opening-range high AND open > opening-range high AND body fraction ≥ configured minimum (when body filter is enabled) AND volume > its configured moving average (when volume filter is enabled) AND nearest resistance is absent or at least the configured target distance above close (when support/resistance filter is enabled) AND no long signal since the last close < opening-range high or start of day
Short: opening range is locked AND within regular trading hours and the configured signal session AND close < opening-range low AND open < opening-range low AND body fraction ≥ configured minimum (when body filter is enabled) AND volume > its configured moving average (when volume filter is enabled) AND nearest support is absent or at least the configured target distance below close (when support/resistance filter is enabled) AND no short signal since the last close > opening-range low or start of day
