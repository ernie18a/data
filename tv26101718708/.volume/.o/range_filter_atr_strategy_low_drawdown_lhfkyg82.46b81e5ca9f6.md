Calculation: Selected close/open/high/low = regular OHLC or Heikin-Ashi OHLC; Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4, open = prior Heikin-Ashi open/close average (initially (open + close) / 2), high/low = max/min of regular high/low and Heikin-Ashi open/close; optionally smooth each with a moving average.
Calculation: Range center = moving average of selected close; range deviation = configured multiplier × standard deviation of selected close over the same lookback. Apply the configured range-lock rule to these values (dynamic, lock to close, lock on trend change, lock on high volume, or fixed-period lock).
Calculation: Upper/lower range bands = range center ± range deviation.
Calculation: Upper/lower band entry levels = upper band +/− (upper band − lower band) × configured band buffer.
Calculation: Midpoint pass zone = range center ± range center × configured midpoint buffer.
Calculation: ATR = average true range of regular OHLC.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = configured rule: volume above its moving average × multiplier; or volume exceeds its prior two bars and its moving average; or volume above its moving average × surge multiplier; or volume above its moving average × multiplier with absolute close-open movement / open above a threshold. Each rule also requires volume above a minimum.
Calculation: Directional volume ratio = moving average of bullish estimated volume / (moving average of bullish estimated volume + moving average of bearish estimated volume), using configured lookback. Estimate bullish/bearish volume by configured method: tick direction (allocate by close-open movement within bar range), price action (allocate all volume to the direction of close versus prior close), or close position within bar range; split equally when direction/range is unchanged or zero.
Calculation: Momentum = selected close minus selected close from the configured lookback; momentum average = moving average of momentum over a shorter lookback; RSI = RSI of selected close.
Calculation: Swing stop for long = min(lowest selected low over twice the swing lookback minus buffer, selected close minus 0.5 ATR); swing stop for short = max(highest selected high over twice the swing lookback plus buffer, selected close plus 0.5 ATR). Risk distance = selected close minus long swing stop, or short swing stop minus selected close.
Long: Configured trade direction permits long AND configured time/session filter permits the bar AND (cross of selected close from at/below to above range center with its optional volume and delta-volume filters passing OR cross from at/below to above upper band entry level with selected close at least configured ATR distance above upper band and its optional volume and delta-volume filters passing OR selected close is within midpoint pass zone AND absolute change from prior selected close is at least configured ATR movement AND selected close > prior selected close AND selected close > selected open AND selected close > range center AND its optional volume and delta-volume filters pass) AND (if swing-based risk/reward exits are selected, risk distance > 0.5 ATR).
Short: Configured trade direction permits short AND configured time/session filter permits the bar AND (cross of selected close from at/above to below range center with its optional volume and delta-volume filters passing OR cross from at/above to below lower band entry level with selected close at least configured ATR distance below lower band and its optional volume and delta-volume filters passing OR selected close is within midpoint pass zone AND absolute change from prior selected close is at least configured ATR movement AND selected close < prior selected close AND selected close < selected open AND selected close < range center AND its optional volume and delta-volume filters pass) AND (if swing-based risk/reward exits are selected, risk distance > 0.5 ATR).
Exit: Long, selected close - selected close from momentum lookback < 0 AND absolute momentum < absolute momentum average × configured threshold, OR RSI > configured overbought level, OR enabled volume-decline rule passes, OR an opposite short entry condition occurs when opposite-signal exits are enabled, OR configured time exit is enabled AND bars since entry ≥ configured holding limit, OR low ≤ active stop, OR high ≥ active profit target.
Exit: Short, selected close - selected close from momentum lookback > 0 AND absolute momentum < absolute momentum average × configured threshold, OR RSI < configured oversold level, OR enabled volume-decline rule passes, OR an opposite long entry condition occurs when opposite-signal exits are enabled, OR configured time exit is enabled AND bars since entry ≥ configured holding limit, OR high ≥ active stop, OR low ≤ active profit target.
