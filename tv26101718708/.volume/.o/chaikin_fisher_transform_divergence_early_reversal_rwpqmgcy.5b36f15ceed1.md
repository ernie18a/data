Calculation: Chaikin oscillator = short-period EMA of the Accumulation/Distribution line minus its longer-period EMA.
Calculation: Fisher line = recursively smooth the Chaikin oscillator normalized to its rolling high-low range, clamp the normalized value to (-1, 1), then apply the Fisher logarithmic transform; Fisher trigger = Fisher line from the previous bar.
Long: Fisher line crosses above its trigger OR a confirmed Fisher pivot low has a lower corresponding price low and a higher Fisher value than the preceding pivot low within the maximum pivot separation OR a confirmed Fisher pivot low has a higher corresponding price low and a lower Fisher value than the preceding pivot low within the maximum pivot separation OR the previous Fisher value is at or below oversold and the current Fisher value is above the previous value while the previous value is at or below the value two bars ago, with at least the cooldown interval since the previous early bullish signal.
Short: Fisher line crosses below its trigger OR a confirmed Fisher pivot high has a higher corresponding price high and a lower Fisher value than the preceding pivot high within the maximum pivot separation OR a confirmed Fisher pivot high has a lower corresponding price high and a higher Fisher value than the preceding pivot high within the maximum pivot separation OR the previous Fisher value is at or above overbought and the current Fisher value is below the previous value while the previous value is at or above the value two bars ago, with at least the cooldown interval since the previous early bearish signal.
