Calculation: Bollinger Bands = simple moving average of close ± a multiple of close’s standard deviation
Calculation: Keltner Channels = the same simple moving average of close ± a multiple of the simple moving average of true range
Long: Position is flat AND EMA(shorter lookback) crossed above EMA(longer lookback) 1–3 bars ago AND volume > its simple moving average AND close > previous bar’s high
Long: Position is flat AND EMA(shorter lookback) > EMA(longer lookback) for at least 10 bars since crossing above AND low ≤ EMA(longer lookback) AND close > EMA(longer lookback)
Long: Position is flat AND volume > its simple moving average and high − low < 0.8 × previous bar’s ATR(5) for at least 3 consecutive bars AND close > the highest high of the previous 3 bars
Long: Position is flat AND Bollinger Bands were inside Keltner Channels for at least 15 consecutive completed bars through the previous bar AND they are no longer inside on the current bar AND close > current upper Bollinger Band
Short: Bearish EMA crossover AND close < previous bar’s low
Exit: Long, close < EMA(longer lookback)
Exit: Short, close > the highest high of the current and previous 4 bars
