Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is more extreme than the following structure-length bars; confirm it after those bars complete.
Calculation: Break level = the latest confirmed swing high or low; replace it when a newer swing on that side is confirmed.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = price crosses above the break high / below the break low, using close in close-break mode or high/low in wick-break mode; count each level once. If both break on one bar, assign the break to the candle direction.
Calculation: Market direction = direction of the latest structure break; initialize bullish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CHoCH = bullish/bearish structure break when the prior market direction was bearish/bullish. Bullish/bearish BOS = bullish/bearish break when the prior market direction was bullish/bearish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = a gap where current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier, and the intervening candle close exceeds its low / falls below its high by at least the ATR displacement threshold; confirm and create the gap on the following completed bar. A gap remains active until its configured mitigation condition invalidates it.
Calculation: Confirmed BOSS pivot = a confirmed local swing high or low over an odd, symmetrical pivot window. Strong bearish high = no prior high pivot or pivot high at or below the prior high pivot; weak bearish high = above it. Strong bullish low = no prior low pivot or pivot low at or above the prior low pivot; weak bullish low = below it.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish rejected block = after a bullish/bearish CHoCH, a confirmed swing low/high whose lower/upper wick meets both the minimum share of candle range and minimum ATR size; the block spans from the pivot low to the bottom of its body / from the top of its body to the pivot high. It is invalidated by a close below/above its far boundary.
Calculation: Rejected-block retest = after price first moves entirely above/below the bullish/bearish block, a later bar's range overlaps the block.
Calculation: FVG/BOS pair = a directional BOS and same-direction FVG formed within the configured maximum bar distance after the rejected-block retest.
Calculation: Final FVG retest = after the FVG/BOS pair, price touches the gap before its configured mitigation condition invalidates it.
Calculation: CHoCH retracement zone = the 0.382–0.618 retracement of the range from the opposite break level to the CHoCH bar's high / low, for bullish / bearish CHoCH.
Calculation: Liquidity trend line = line from the completed prior CHoCH regime's low/high to the first later confirmed swing low/high above/below that point, for a bullish/bearish CHoCH.
Long: bullish CHoCH OR bullish BOS OR confirmed BOSS pivot low OR (market direction is bullish AND close > open AND low < prior low AND the bar range overlaps an active bullish FVG) OR bullish rejected block forms OR bullish rejected-block retest OR bullish FVG/BOS pair OR final bullish FVG retest OR (bullish CHoCH retracement zone was touched and a later completed bar closes above its top) OR (bearish liquidity trend line is broken: close below it in close-break mode, otherwise high above it)
Short: bearish CHoCH OR bearish BOS OR confirmed BOSS pivot high OR (market direction is bearish AND close < open AND high > prior high AND the bar range overlaps an active bearish FVG) OR bearish rejected block forms OR bearish rejected-block retest OR bearish FVG/BOS pair OR final bearish FVG retest OR (bearish CHoCH retracement zone was touched and a later completed bar closes below its bottom) OR (bullish liquidity trend line is broken: close below it in close-break mode, otherwise low below it)
