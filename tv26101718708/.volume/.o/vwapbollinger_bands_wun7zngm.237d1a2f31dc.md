Calculation: Upper VWAP Bollinger Band = SMA of VWAP over its lookback + standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of VWAP over a separate lookback
Calculation: Lower VWAP Bollinger Band = SMA of VWAP over its lookback − standard-deviation multiplier × standard deviation of VWAP over a separate lookback
Long: high > Upper VWAP Bollinger Band
Exit: long, low < Lower VWAP Bollinger Band
