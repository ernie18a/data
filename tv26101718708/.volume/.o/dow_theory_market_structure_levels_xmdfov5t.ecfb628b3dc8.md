Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is the highest across the swing lookback before and after it; available after the bars following it complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is the lowest across the swing lookback before and after it; available after the bars following it complete.
Long: Confirmed swing high > previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low >= previous confirmed swing low
Short: Confirmed swing high <= previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low < previous confirmed swing low
