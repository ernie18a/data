Calculation: Price velocity = (close − previous close) / (high − low), using the instrument’s minimum tick as denominator when high equals low
Calculation: Volume-weighted velocity pulse = price velocity × (volume / 50-bar SMA of volume)
Calculation: Pulse acceleration = current pulse − pulse from the lookback bars ago
Long: Pulse acceleration > 0 AND ATR >= 50-bar SMA of ATR AND volume > 20-bar SMA of volume × volume sensitivity AND the same long conditions were false on the previous bar
Short: Pulse acceleration < 0 AND ATR >= 50-bar SMA of ATR AND volume > 20-bar SMA of volume × volume sensitivity AND the same short conditions were false on the previous bar
