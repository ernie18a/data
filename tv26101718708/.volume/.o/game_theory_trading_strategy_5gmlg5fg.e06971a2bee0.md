Calculation: Price momentum = current close minus close at a shorter lookback; compare it with its longer-lookback simple moving average.
Calculation: Accumulation/distribution line = cumulative sum of (((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low)) × volume.
Calculation: Smart money = ((close − open) / (high − low)) × volume; compare it with its simple moving average.
Calculation: Nash upper/lower bands = simple moving average of close over the Nash lookback plus/minus close's standard deviation over that same lookback times a deviation factor.
Long: Herd-selling condition (RSI below its oversold threshold AND volume above its moving average times the herd-volume multiplier AND price momentum below its moving average) AND (accumulation (accumulation/distribution line above its moving average AND volume above its moving average times the institutional-volume multiplier) OR down liquidity trap (low below the prior bar's recent-low level AND close above that level AND volume above its moving average times the herd-volume multiplier)); OR close below the lower Nash band AND smart money above its moving average AND NOT herd-buying condition; OR close below the lower Nash band AND close above the previous bar's close AND volume above its moving average; AND no existing long position.
Short: Herd-buying condition (RSI above its overbought threshold AND volume above its moving average times the herd-volume multiplier AND price momentum above its moving average) AND (distribution (accumulation/distribution line below its moving average AND volume above its moving average times the institutional-volume multiplier) OR up liquidity trap (high above the prior bar's recent-high level AND close below that level AND volume above its moving average times the herd-volume multiplier)); OR close above the upper Nash band AND smart money at or below its moving average AND NOT herd-selling condition; OR close above the upper Nash band AND close below the previous bar's close AND volume above its moving average; AND no existing short position.
Exit: Long, low at or below entry price minus the stop-loss percentage OR (take-profit enabled AND high at or above entry price plus the take-profit percentage).
Exit: Short, high at or above entry price plus the stop-loss percentage OR (take-profit enabled AND low at or below entry price minus the take-profit percentage).
