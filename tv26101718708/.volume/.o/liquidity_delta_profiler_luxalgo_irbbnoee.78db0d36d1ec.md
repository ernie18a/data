Calculation: Buy-side liquidity zone = confirmed swing high; zone top is the pivot candle’s high and bottom is the greater of its open and close, widened to at least 0.1 ATR if needed.
Calculation: Sell-side liquidity zone = confirmed swing low; zone bottom is the pivot candle’s low and top is the lesser of its open and close, widened to at least 0.1 ATR if needed.
Calculation: Zone quadrant delta = cumulative sum, for each of four equal-height zone slices, of bar volume × (close − open) ÷ (high − low) × the fraction of the bar’s range overlapping that slice; use zero bar delta when high equals low.
Calculation: Outer delta = cumulative delta in the highest slice of a buy-side zone or the lowest slice of a sell-side zone. Absolute delta total = sum of the absolute cumulative deltas across all four slices.
Calculation: Bar delta ratio = absolute bar delta ÷ volume.
Long: For an active, unsignaled sell-side zone with absolute delta total greater than zero, any of: price sweeps below the zone and outer delta is positive with outer delta ÷ (absolute delta total + 0.0001) > 0.2; price sweeps below the zone and that ratio < 0.1; the close is within the zone and outer delta is negative with that ratio > 0.6; or price sweeps below the zone, closes above its midpoint, bar delta is positive, and bar delta ratio > 0.2.
Short: For an active, unsignaled buy-side zone with absolute delta total greater than zero, any of: price sweeps above the zone and outer delta is negative with outer delta ÷ (absolute delta total + 0.0001) > 0.2; price sweeps above the zone and that ratio < 0.1; the close is within the zone and outer delta is positive with that ratio > 0.6; or price sweeps above the zone, closes below its midpoint, bar delta is negative, and bar delta ratio > 0.2.
