Calculation: Active bullish FVG references = on each confirmed chart bar, add the high from two bars earlier when current low > that high and previous close > that high; remove each reference when a confirmed close is below it; retain at most the configured maximum, removing oldest first. Active bearish FVG references = on each confirmed chart bar, add the low from two bars earlier when current high < that low and previous close < that low; remove each reference when a confirmed close is above it; retain at most the configured maximum, removing oldest first.
Calculation: Upper and lower channel boundaries = take the average of each active reference set, using the chart-close SMA as that side’s value when its set is empty; apply two consecutive SMAs with the same configurable length to each side’s values; upper boundary is the higher smoothed value and lower boundary the lower. Use chart bars and confirmed-bar FVG updates.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the lower channel boundary after at least the configured minimum number of consecutive prior closes below it, and the bullish signal cooldown has elapsed, and (volume confirmation is disabled or current volume > its lookback SMA × configured multiplier).
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the upper channel boundary after at least the configured minimum number of consecutive prior closes above it, and the bearish signal cooldown has elapsed, and (volume confirmation is disabled or current volume > its lookback SMA × configured multiplier).
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, high >= target or low <= stop; if both occur on the same bar, exit at stop. Target is either entry price × (1 + configured standard or Super target percentage) or the selected internal channel level when that level is above entry; stop is entry price × (1 − configured standard or Super stop percentage).
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, low <= target or high >= stop; if both occur on the same bar, exit at stop. Target is either entry price × (1 − configured standard or Super target percentage) or the selected internal channel level when that level is below entry; stop is entry price × (1 + configured standard or Super stop percentage).
