Calculation: Total score = adaptive oscillator direction (+3 if it rises, −3 if it falls, otherwise 0) + sequential close-count direction (+2/−2/0) + custom Supertrend direction (+1/−1) + rate-of-change direction (+1 if positive, otherwise −1) + directional-movement direction (+2 if positive or zero, otherwise −2) + CCI direction (+2 if positive, otherwise −2) + volume-wave direction (+2/−2) + close vs. 20-period SMA (+1/−1) + Awesome Oscillator direction (+1/−1/0) + 14-bar momentum direction (+1/−1/0) + MACD histogram direction (+3 if rising, otherwise −3) + bull/bear pressure direction (+1/−1) + Ultimate Oscillator direction (+1 above 70, −1 below 30, otherwise 0) + close vs. Ichimoku base line (+1/−1) + close vs. Hull moving average (+2/−2). Use shorter and longer lookbacks for the MACD fast and slow averages; the Ultimate Oscillator combines short-, medium-, and long-lookback averages. The sequential close-count direction is +2 if the current positive count exceeds its prior completed run's count, −2 if the current negative count exceeds its prior completed run's count, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Custom Supertrend direction = +1 when close is above the prior bar's upper trailing band, −1 when close is below the prior bar's lower trailing band, otherwise retain the prior direction. Bands are based on an EMA of a volume-price trend shadow, offset by an ATR multiple; trail the upper band with the maximum while the prior close is above the prior upper trend, and trail the lower band with the minimum while the prior close is below the prior lower trend. The shadow is the cumulative sum of (close−open)×volume/(high−low), less its moving average, divided by the standard deviation of that difference and multiplied by the standard deviation of high−low; add the shadow to high when positive, otherwise to low, then take its EMA.
Calculation: Bull/bear pressure direction = +1 when the moving average of bull pressure minus bear pressure is positive, otherwise −1. Bull and bear pressure are the candle-range measures computed by comparing open, close, prior close, high, and low, using the source's conditional candle formulas.
Calculation: Volume-wave direction = +2 when accumulated volume in the current rising wave exceeds volume in the other wave, otherwise −2. A wave begins when the close-to-close direction changes while close is rising or falling over its lookback; accumulate volume while the wave direction persists.
Calculation: Adaptive oscillator direction = +3 when the adaptive smoothed oscillator rises, −3 when it falls, otherwise 0. The oscillator is a relative-strength calculation from separately smoothed positive and negative changes of close relative to its short EMA; adapt its threshold using an efficiency ratio and a weighted blend of shorter- and longer-lookback standard deviations, updating the smoothed value only when price exceeds the prior value by that threshold.
Calculation: Smoothed total score = moving average of the total score.
Long: Smoothed total score crosses above zero.
Short: Smoothed total score crosses below zero.
