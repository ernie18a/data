Calculation: WaveTrend = WT1, the EMA of the channel index (source minus its EMA, divided by 0.015 times the EMA of its absolute deviation); WT2 = the moving average of WT1. Use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = smoothed stochastic of RSI; use its K line for divergences.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator bottom fractal, price makes a lower low while the oscillator makes a higher low than at the previous bottom fractal; regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator top fractal, price makes a higher high while the oscillator makes a lower high than at the previous top fractal. For WaveTrend and RSI, apply their configured divergence thresholds; Stochastic RSI divergences have no threshold.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator bottom fractal, price makes a higher low while the oscillator makes a lower low than at the previous bottom fractal; hidden bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator top fractal, price makes a lower high while the oscillator makes a higher high than at the previous top fractal. Use no thresholds when hidden-divergence limits are disabled.
Long: WT2 < WT1 OR regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR hidden bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI
Short: WT2 > WT1 OR regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR hidden bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI
Exit: long, WT2 > WT1 OR regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR hidden bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI
Exit: short, WT2 < WT1 OR regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR hidden bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI
