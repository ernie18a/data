Calculation: Aroon Up = 100 × (N − bars since highest high over the last N+1 bars) / N
Calculation: Aroon Down = 100 × (N − bars since lowest low over the last N+1 bars) / N
Calculation: Baseline = selected moving average of close
Long: Aroon Up crosses above Aroon Down AND MACD histogram > 0 AND RSI > 50 AND close > PSAR AND close ≤ Baseline + ATR
Short: Aroon Down crosses above Aroon Up AND MACD histogram < 0 AND RSI < 50 AND close < PSAR AND close ≥ Baseline − ATR
Exit: long, Aroon Down crosses above Aroon Up OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + target distance
Exit: short, Aroon Up crosses above Aroon Down OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − target distance
