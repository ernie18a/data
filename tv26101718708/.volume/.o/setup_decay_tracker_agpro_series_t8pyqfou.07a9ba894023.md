Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback; use its value on the current bar.
Calculation: Trend EMA = exponential moving average of close; Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback, about 45% of the Trend EMA lookback.
Calculation: Prior high/low = highest high/lowest low over the setup lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Close location = (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Validation quality = 42% travel score + 24% directional close score + 20% trend score + 14% volume score. Travel score scales favorable excursion in ATR units from 35% to 135% of required validation travel into 0–100. Directional close score is close location for long setups or 1 − close location for short setups when the trigger side holds, otherwise 15. Trend score is 76 when close is on the setup side of the Trend EMA, otherwise 34. Volume score scales volume/current-volume-average from 0.70 to 1.35 into 0–100; if volume or its average is unavailable, use neutral volume ratio 1.
Long: Selected side is long (forced long, or auto: Fast EMA ≥ Trend EMA and Trend EMA ≥ its value five bars earlier; or Fast EMA ≤ Trend EMA and Trend EMA ≤ its value five bars earlier and close is in the upper half of its recent range; or neither trend case and close is in the upper half of its recent range); selected trigger model fires (range acceptance: close crosses above prior high; trend reclaim: close crosses above Trend EMA and close location ≥ 0.58; impulse continuation: close > open, close > previous high, body/range normalized by ATR ≥ 0.45 adjusted by sensitivity, and close location ≥ 0.60); and a tracked setup is not yet validated or invalidated, the bar is confirmed, setup age is greater than zero and within the validation window, favorable excursion from trigger price is at least the required ATR travel, validation quality meets its minimum, and close ≥ trigger price.
Short: Selected side is short (forced short, or auto: Fast EMA ≤ Trend EMA and Trend EMA ≤ its value five bars earlier; or Fast EMA ≥ Trend EMA and Trend EMA ≥ its value five bars earlier and close is in the lower half of its recent range; or neither trend case and close is in the lower half of its recent range); selected trigger model fires (range acceptance: close crosses below prior low; trend reclaim: close crosses below Trend EMA and close location ≤ 0.42; impulse continuation: close < open, close < previous low, body/range normalized by ATR ≥ 0.45 adjusted by sensitivity, and close location ≤ 0.40); and a tracked setup is not yet validated or invalidated, the bar is confirmed, setup age is greater than zero and within the validation window, favorable excursion from trigger price is at least the required ATR travel, validation quality meets its minimum, and close ≤ trigger price.
Long: A validated long setup's decay score crosses the review threshold.
Short: A validated short setup's decay score crosses the review threshold.
