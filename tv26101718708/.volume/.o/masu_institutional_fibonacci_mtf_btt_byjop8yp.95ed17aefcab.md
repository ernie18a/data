Calculation: Volume spike = volume > its 20-bar simple moving average × volume multiplier
Calculation: Bullish ribbon = at least (alignment threshold − 1) of the 7 adjacent comparisons in an 8-moving-average ribbon are bullish; moving-average lengths increase from shortest to longest
Calculation: Bearish ribbon = at least (alignment threshold − 1) of the 7 adjacent comparisons in an 8-moving-average ribbon are bearish; moving-average lengths increase from shortest to longest
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish = fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA; use the configured higher-timeframe bar (last completed bar historically, forming bar in real time)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish = fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close < fast EMA; use the configured higher-timeframe bar (last completed bar historically, forming bar in real time)
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 since the start of the current day
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume on up-close bars minus volume on down-close bars
Calculation: Bullish order flow = ((CVD > its CVD-period simple moving average AND close < previous close AND CVD > previous CVD) OR (up-bar volume / max(down-bar volume, 1) > 1 + CVD threshold)) AND (close crossed above VWAP OR close > VWAP)
Calculation: Bearish order flow = ((CVD < its CVD-period simple moving average AND close > previous close AND CVD < previous CVD) OR (up-bar volume / max(down-bar volume, 1) < 1 − CVD threshold)) AND (close crossed below VWAP OR close < VWAP)
Calculation: Fib swing high/low = latest bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the swing lookback plus current bar, respectively, and whose distance from the opposite extreme over that window is at least the minimum-swing-ATR multiple; retain the latest qualifying high and low
Calculation: Fib upswing = retained swing-high bar is later than retained swing-low bar
Calculation: Fib 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% = retained swing high − (0.382, 0.500, 0.618 × (retained swing high − retained swing low)), respectively
Calculation: Fib bullish = enabled, both retained swing bars are at least 2 bars old, upswing, low is within fib-zone ATR multiple of an enabled retracement level, AND (lower-wick fraction ≥ minimum wick fraction OR (close > open AND close > nearest touched level)); lower-wick fraction = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low)
Calculation: Fib bearish = enabled, both retained swing bars are at least 2 bars old, not upswing, high is within fib-zone ATR multiple of an enabled retracement level, AND (upper-wick fraction ≥ minimum wick fraction OR (close < open AND close < nearest touched level)); upper-wick fraction = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low)
Calculation: ADX = directional movement index ADX using the configured period
Calculation: Long confluence score = count of: higher-timeframe bullish filter passes; bullish ribbon filter passes; order-flow filter passes; fib filter passes; ADX > threshold. A disabled filter passes; order-flow passes if disabled, bullish order flow, or close > VWAP; fib passes if disabled, fib bullish, or upswing
Calculation: Short confluence score = count of: higher-timeframe bearish filter passes; bearish ribbon filter passes; order-flow filter passes; fib filter passes; ADX > threshold. A disabled filter passes; order-flow passes if disabled, bearish order flow, or close < VWAP; fib passes if disabled, fib bearish, or not upswing
Long: (ADX > threshold AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND fast EMA crossed above slow EMA AND volume spike OR close > highest high of the prior breakout lookback bars AND volume spike AND close > long-term EMA OR ADX ≤ threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI crossed above oversold level) AND higher-timeframe bullish filter passes AND bullish ribbon filter passes AND order-flow filter passes AND fib filter passes AND within configured trading session
Short: (ADX > threshold AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND fast EMA crossed below slow EMA AND volume spike OR close < lowest low of the prior breakout lookback bars AND volume spike AND close < long-term EMA OR ADX ≤ threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI crossed below overbought level) AND higher-timeframe bearish filter passes AND bearish ribbon filter passes AND order-flow filter passes AND fib filter passes AND within configured trading session
Long: Fib bullish
Short: Fib bearish
Long: Long confluence score > short confluence score
Short: Short confluence score > long confluence score
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop-loss multiple OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop is hit) OR (trailing disabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple)
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiple OR (trailing enabled AND trailing stop is hit) OR (trailing disabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR × take-profit multiple)
