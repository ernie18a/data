Calculation: Daily pivot and support/resistance levels = use the previous completed daily bar’s high, low, and close: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; R1 = 2 × pivot − low; R2 = pivot + high − low; R3 = high + 2 × (pivot − low); S1 = 2 × pivot − high; S2 = pivot − high + low; S3 = low − 2 × (high − pivot)
Calculation: Multitimeframe bias score = on each still-forming daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute bar, add 1 for each of: close > fast EMA, close > slow EMA, fast EMA > slow EMA, RSI > 50, MACD line > 0, close > Parabolic SAR; otherwise subtract 1 for that comparison. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Long: Daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, or 15-minute bias score ≥ 2 OR close > Parabolic SAR OR RSI < 30 OR (RSI > 50 AND RSI ≤ 70) OR MACD line > MACD signal line OR (ADX > 20 AND +DI > −DI) OR close > fast EMA
Short: Daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, or 15-minute bias score ≤ −2 OR close < Parabolic SAR OR RSI > 70 OR (RSI ≥ 30 AND RSI ≤ 50) OR MACD line < MACD signal line OR (ADX > 20 AND +DI ≤ −DI) OR (close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA)
