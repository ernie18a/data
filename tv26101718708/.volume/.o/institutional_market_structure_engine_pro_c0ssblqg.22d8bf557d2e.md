Calculation: Confirmed macro swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured longer left and right lookbacks, available after the right-lookback bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed micro swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured shorter left and right lookbacks, available after the right-lookback bars complete
Calculation: Bullish/bearish swing break = current close above/below the latest unbroken swing high/low, or current high/low above/below it when wick confirmation is configured
Calculation: Bullish/bearish swing sweep = current low below the second-newest confirmed macro swing low and close above it / current high above the second-newest confirmed macro swing high and close below it, when that swing is unbroken
Calculation: High-volume bar = current volume above its configured moving average
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement FVG = current low above the high two bars earlier / current high below the low two bars earlier, with the middle bar a same-direction candle whose body exceeds half its range and whose range exceeds ATR, the gap exceeding the configured ATR fraction, and current volume above its moving average
Calculation: Bullish/bearish OB-coupled FVG = bullish/bearish displacement FVG and a preceding opposite-direction candle among the configured preceding bars; when strong OB filtering is enabled, that candle must also sweep the preceding candle's low/high
Calculation: Bullish/bearish IFVG inversion = close below the bottom of an active bullish FVG / close above the top of an active bearish FVG
Calculation: EMA trend on each configured dashboard timeframe = shorter-period EMA of close above the longer-period EMA of close, evaluated using that timeframe's current bar (which may still be forming)
Long: bullish macro swing break OR bullish micro swing break OR bullish macro swing sweep OR bullish OB-coupled FVG OR bearish IFVG inversion OR EMA trend on any configured dashboard timeframe is bullish OR the latest macro swing break is bullish
Short: bearish macro swing break OR bearish micro swing break OR bearish macro swing sweep OR bearish OB-coupled FVG OR bullish IFVG inversion OR EMA trend on any configured dashboard timeframe is bearish OR the latest macro swing break is bearish
