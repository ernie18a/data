Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: RSI moving average = simple moving average of RSI
Calculation: MFI = money flow index of typical price (high + low + close) / 3 and volume
Calculation: MACD = fast EMA of close minus slow EMA of close; signal is EMA of MACD; histogram is MACD minus signal
Calculation: Moving average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA of close
Calculation: Fast moving average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or HMA of close, with a shorter lookback than the moving average used as the slow line
Calculation: Supertrend = ATR-based trend line using a configurable ATR lookback and multiplier
Long: Long trading enabled AND not in an active post-loss cool-off period AND the enabled entry comparisons are ALL true when all mode is selected, otherwise at least one is true; comparisons are RSI below or above a threshold, RSI moving average below or above a threshold, MFI below or above a threshold, MACD crossing above its signal or histogram/MACD below a threshold, close below/above the moving average or at least a configured percentage below/above it, fast moving average crossing above/below the moving average, or Supertrend uptrend/downtrend or its corresponding trend-change signal
Short: Short trading enabled AND not in an active post-loss cool-off period AND the enabled entry comparisons are ALL true when all mode is selected, otherwise at least one is true; each enabled comparison is either the corresponding long comparison or its configured inverse (reverse inequality/crossing/trend and reflect oscillator threshold around 50 or MACD threshold around zero)
Exit: Long, enabled exit comparisons are ALL true when all mode is selected, otherwise at least one is true: RSI/RSI moving average/MFI above or below a threshold, MACD crossing below its signal or histogram/MACD above a threshold, close relative to the moving average or configured percentage distance from it, fast moving average crossing in the configured direction, or Supertrend in the configured direction or its corresponding trend-change signal; unless take profit overrides indicator exits; OR close <= entry price minus a configured dollar amount or entry price times (1 minus a configured percentage); OR close >= entry price plus a configured dollar amount or entry price times (1 plus a configured percentage)
Exit: Short, enabled exit comparisons are ALL true when all mode is selected, otherwise at least one is true: each configured long exit comparison or its configured inverse (reverse inequality/crossing/trend and reflect oscillator threshold around 50 or MACD threshold around zero); unless take profit overrides indicator exits; OR close >= entry price plus a configured dollar amount or entry price times (1 plus a configured percentage); OR close <= entry price minus a configured dollar amount or entry price times (1 minus a configured percentage)
