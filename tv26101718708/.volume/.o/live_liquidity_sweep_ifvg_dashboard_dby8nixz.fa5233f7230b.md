Calculation: Latest confirmed swing high/low = most recent pivot high/low, confirmed using equal lookbacks on both sides.
Calculation: Active bullish sweep = a bar with low below the latest confirmed swing low and close above it; it remains active through the signal window, is cleared by a bearish sweep, and is cleared after a long signal. Active bearish sweep is the reverse and is cleared by a bullish sweep or after a short signal.
Calculation: Bullish score = 35 if an active bullish sweep is within the signal window, plus 20 if current low > high two bars earlier, plus 15 if current close > open and > prior close, plus 15 if current close > EMA, plus 10 if RSI > 40 and < 72, plus 5 if current volume > its moving average. Bearish score = 35 if an active bearish sweep is within the signal window, plus 20 if current high < low two bars earlier, plus 15 if current close < open and < prior close, plus 15 if current close < EMA, plus 10 if RSI < 60 and > 25, plus 5 if current volume > its moving average.
Long: Bullish score >= minimum score AND no signal has occurred before, or current bar index minus the last signal bar index > cooldown bars.
Short: Bearish score >= minimum score AND no signal has occurred before, or current bar index minus the last signal bar index > cooldown bars.
