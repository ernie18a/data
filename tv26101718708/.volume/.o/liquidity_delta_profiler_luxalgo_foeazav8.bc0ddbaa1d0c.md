Calculation: Buy-side liquidity zone = at a confirmed pivot high (same lookback and lookforward length), from the pivot bar’s high down to the greater of its open and close; if thinner than a volatility-based minimum, extend the bottom down to that minimum. Create only if it is not overlapped by an active zone, keeping the higher zone when overlapping.
Calculation: Sell-side liquidity zone = at a confirmed pivot low (same lookback and lookforward length), from the pivot bar’s low up to the lesser of its open and close; if thinner than a volatility-based minimum, extend the top up to that minimum. Create only if it is not overlapped by an active zone, keeping the lower zone when overlapping.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume × (close − open) ÷ (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Zone quadrant delta = cumulative bar delta × the fraction of the bar’s high-low range overlapping that quadrant; divide by zero range as zero. Each zone has four equal price quadrants. The outer quadrant is the highest for a buy-side zone and the lowest for a sell-side zone.
Calculation: Outer delta ratio = absolute outer quadrant delta ÷ (sum of absolute deltas across all four quadrants + a small positive epsilon).
Short: An unsignaled active buy-side zone is hit or swept, and (high > zone top AND outer quadrant delta < 0 AND outer delta ratio > threshold OR high > zone top AND outer delta ratio < threshold OR close is within zone bounds AND outer quadrant delta > 0 AND outer delta ratio > threshold OR high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND absolute bar delta ÷ (volume + epsilon) > threshold).
Long: An unsignaled active sell-side zone is hit or swept, and (low < zone bottom AND outer quadrant delta > 0 AND outer delta ratio > threshold OR low < zone bottom AND outer delta ratio < threshold OR close is within zone bounds AND outer quadrant delta < 0 AND outer delta ratio > threshold OR low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND absolute bar delta ÷ (volume + epsilon) > threshold).
