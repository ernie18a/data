Calculation: Mapped levels = previous regular-session high/low, current premarket high/low, completed opening-range high/low, and latest confirmed swing high/low; merge levels within the configured tick distance.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe bias = last completed higher-timeframe close, EMA 9, and EMA 20; bullish when close > EMA 9 > EMA 20, bearish when close < EMA 9 < EMA 20.
Calculation: RVOL = current bar volume / average volume of the preceding 20 regular-session bars.
Calculation: Retest band = mapped level ± configured fraction of ATR frozen when the setup is armed.
Calculation: Setup stop = tracked setup extreme adjusted outward by the configured fraction of frozen ATR.
Calculation: Target = nearest mapped level or opposing supply/demand zone, with configured ATR clearance, capped by the configured maximum risk multiple.
Long: Signal window and eligibility gates pass AND bullish higher-timeframe bias AND ((price closes above a mapped level after the prior close was at or below it AND RVOL meets its threshold AND close is within the configured maximum ATR extension AND a later bar touches the retest band, closes above the level, and has volume below breakout volume when the quiet-retest filter is enabled AND a still later bar closes above the retest bar's high with a bullish body and close above VWAP and EMA 9 > EMA 20) OR (a bar's low penetrates below a mapped level by at least the configured ATR fraction AND closes above the level, above its open, and in the upper half of its range AND a later bar touches the retest band and closes above the level AND a still later bar closes above the retest bar's high with a bullish body)) AND setup has not failed, expired, or lost bullish bias AND entry extension, stop distance, modeled costs, and net reward-to-stop-risk gates pass AND level/direction is unused and plan limits pass.
Short: Signal window and eligibility gates pass AND bearish higher-timeframe bias AND ((price closes below a mapped level after the prior close was at or above it AND RVOL meets its threshold AND close is within the configured maximum ATR extension AND a later bar touches the retest band, closes below the level, and has volume below breakout volume when the quiet-retest filter is enabled AND a still later bar closes below the retest bar's low with a bearish body and close below VWAP and EMA 9 < EMA 20) OR (a bar's high penetrates above a mapped level by at least the configured ATR fraction AND closes below the level, below its open, and in the lower half of its range AND a later bar touches the retest band and closes below the level AND a still later bar closes below the retest bar's low with a bearish body)) AND setup has not failed, expired, or lost bearish bias AND entry extension, stop distance, modeled costs, and net reward-to-stop-risk gates pass AND level/direction is unused and plan limits pass.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR regular session ends OR test end is reached.
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR regular session ends OR test end is reached.
