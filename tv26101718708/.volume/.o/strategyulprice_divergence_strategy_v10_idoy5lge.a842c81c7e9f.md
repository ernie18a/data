Calculation: Selected divergence oscillator = chosen method applied to high and low: RSI(high/low), MACD histogram (SMA(source, fast) − SMA(source, slow), minus its EMA signal), stochastic(close, high, low), SMA(volume), smoothed cumulative money-flow-volume ((2×close−low−high)/(high−low)×volume; use 0 when high=low), Fisher transform, or CCI(high/low). Use the same method and parameters for both series.
Calculation: Oscillator pivot high/low = selected high/low oscillator value two bars ago when it is respectively greater/less than each of the two oscillator values on either side; confirm on the current bar.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot high has a higher price high and lower oscillator value than the preceding confirmed pivot high. Hidden bearish divergence = lower price high and higher oscillator value. Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot low has a lower price low and higher oscillator value than the preceding confirmed pivot low. Hidden bullish divergence = higher price low and lower oscillator value.
Calculation: Murrey oscillator = (close − (lowest low + 4 × 0.125 × (highest high − lowest low))) / ((highest high − lowest low) / 2), using the configured lookback on the current chart bars.
Long: regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence, AND Murrey oscillator on the previous completed bar is above the configured positive quadrant threshold (threshold selected from 0, 0.25, 0.5, or 0.75).
Short: regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence, AND Murrey oscillator on the previous completed bar is below the negative of the configured positive quadrant threshold (threshold selected from 0, 0.25, 0.5, or 0.75).
Exit: long, Murrey oscillator ≤ 0.
Exit: short, Murrey oscillator ≥ 0.
