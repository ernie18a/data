Calculation: Normalized EMA slope = (100 / π) × atan((long moving average − its value N bars earlier) / max(standard deviation of that N-bar difference over a longer lookback, 1e−10)); use the completed current bar and prior completed bars.
Calculation: Centered RSI = smoothed RSI − 50.
Calculation: Slope acceleration = min(50 × (abs(current normalized slope − prior-bar normalized slope) × 2 × N / highest value of that product over 200 bars), 50); N is the slope-difference lookback.
Calculation: Centered RSI pivot high/low and price high/low = confirmed pivots using equal left and right lookbacks, where pivot lookback = 16 − sensitivity; price pivots use high/low and RSI pivots use smoothed RSI. Compare each new confirmed pivot with up to the configured number of prior pivots.
Calculation: Bullish price-RSI divergence = a confirmed price low is below a prior price low AND its corresponding RSI pivot low is above the prior RSI pivot low. Bearish price-RSI divergence = a confirmed price high is above a prior price high AND its corresponding RSI pivot high is below the prior RSI pivot high.
Calculation: Slope-RSI gap = absolute difference between normalized EMA slope and centered RSI.
Calculation: Bullish slope-RSI exhaustion = slope acceleration meets its threshold AND normalized slope change is negative AND normalized EMA slope is below the negative NTZ threshold AND centered RSI exceeds its values at both the configured lookback and twice that lookback AND slope-RSI gap meets its threshold (or the gap threshold is disabled). Bearish slope-RSI exhaustion = slope acceleration meets its threshold AND normalized slope change is positive AND normalized EMA slope is above the NTZ threshold AND centered RSI is below its values at both lookbacks AND slope-RSI gap meets its threshold (or the gap threshold is disabled).
Calculation: NTZ threshold = configured threshold when NTZ is enabled, otherwise zero.
Calculation: MA stretch percentage = (long moving average − short moving average) / short moving average × 100; the short moving average uses a shorter lookback than the long moving average.
Calculation: Smoothed ATR = Wilder ATR over the configured period.
Long: Position is flat or short AND absolute normalized EMA slope is greater than the NTZ threshold AND current and prior bars close above their opens AND any of: normalized EMA slope crosses above the NTZ threshold OR is above it with positive slope change whose absolute value meets the minimum delta, with MA stretch percentage ≥ 0 or ≥ −maximum stretch and (trend filter disabled or short moving average > long moving average); OR slope acceleration meets its entry threshold AND absolute slope change meets minimum delta AND slope change is positive AND normalized slope is above the NTZ threshold AND the same stretch and trend filters pass; OR bullish price-RSI divergence occurs (and, if enabled, bullish slope-RSI exhaustion also occurs); OR bullish slope-RSI exhaustion occurs; OR centered RSI crosses above normalized EMA slope while absolute normalized slope is above the NTZ threshold, unless both are already above the positive NTZ threshold when that cross occurs, then within the configured timeout both centered RSI and normalized slope rise versus the prior bar. Apply the configured close-confirmation requirement where enabled.
Short: Position is flat or long AND absolute normalized EMA slope is greater than the NTZ threshold AND current and prior bars close below their opens AND any of: normalized EMA slope crosses below the negative NTZ threshold OR is below it with negative slope change whose absolute value meets the minimum delta, with MA stretch percentage ≤ 0 or ≤ maximum stretch and (trend filter disabled or short moving average < long moving average); OR slope acceleration meets its entry threshold AND absolute slope change meets minimum delta AND slope change is negative AND normalized slope is below the negative NTZ threshold AND the same stretch and trend filters pass; OR bearish price-RSI divergence occurs (and, if enabled, bearish slope-RSI exhaustion also occurs); OR bearish slope-RSI exhaustion occurs; OR centered RSI crosses below normalized EMA slope while absolute normalized slope is above the NTZ threshold, unless both are already below the negative NTZ threshold when that cross occurs, then within the configured timeout both centered RSI and normalized slope fall versus the prior bar. Apply the configured close-confirmation requirement where enabled.
Exit: long, centered RSI crosses below normalized EMA slope and both fall versus the prior bar within the configured cross-confirmation window AND the current bar closes below its open; OR, for a standard entry, normalized slope crosses from above the positive NTZ threshold to at or below it and remains at or below it for more than the configured exit-confirmation bars; OR the applicable trailing stop is breached after its grace period; OR, for an oscillator entry, the opposite oscillator short signal occurs.
Exit: short, centered RSI crosses above normalized EMA slope and both rise versus the prior bar within the configured cross-confirmation window AND the current bar closes above its open; OR, for a standard entry, normalized slope crosses from below the negative NTZ threshold to at or above it and remains at or above it for more than the configured exit-confirmation bars; OR the applicable trailing stop is breached after its grace period; OR, for an oscillator entry, the opposite oscillator long signal occurs.
Calculation: Long trailing stop initializes at entry close minus entry ATR × entry factor, then after the entry-type grace period updates to the greater of its prior value and bar high minus trailing ATR × entry-type factor. Short trailing stop initializes at entry close plus entry ATR × entry factor, then after the entry-type grace period updates to the lesser of its prior value and bar low plus trailing ATR × entry-type factor. Standard trailing stops require trailing-stop enablement; divergence and oscillator stops use their respective entry-type ATR factor and grace period.
