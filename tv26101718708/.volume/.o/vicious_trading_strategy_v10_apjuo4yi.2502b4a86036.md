Calculation: Composite trend score = 16 plus the count of bullish criteria (add 2 for a bullish Supertrend flip), minus the count of bearish criteria (subtract 2 for a bearish Supertrend flip); criteria are the candle-pattern, moving-average, band, SAR, Supertrend, Trend Trader, Ichimoku, RSI, Slingshot, Guppy, MACD, and price conditions defined in the source
Calculation: Supertrend direction = bullish when close crosses above the prior upper trailing band; bearish when close crosses below the prior lower trailing band; otherwise retain the prior direction
Calculation: Sigma trend state = bullish when price is above the basis plus the tolerance standard deviation band; bearish when below the basis minus that band; otherwise retain the prior state. The reference state uses higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Short RSI trend = 2-period RSI of close
Long: Within the configured date window AND (composite trend score crosses above 24 OR crosses above 28 OR crosses above its 15-period EMA OR Supertrend flips bearish to bullish OR sigma trend turns bullish OR lower-timeframe sigma trend turns bearish while higher-timeframe sigma trend remains bullish)
Short: Within the configured date window AND NOT (Supertrend flips bearish to bullish OR sigma trend turns bullish OR lower-timeframe sigma trend turns bearish while higher-timeframe sigma trend remains bullish OR fast EMA is at least the slow EMA OR fast EMA is above slow EMA AND close is above the 200-period SMA AND close is below the 5-period SMA AND 2-period RSI is below 10) AND ((composite trend score is below 20 AND crosses below 19) OR Supertrend flips bullish to bearish OR sigma trend turns bearish OR lower-timeframe sigma trend turns bullish while higher-timeframe sigma trend remains bearish)
Exit: Long, (within the configured date window AND composite trend score crosses below 20) OR Supertrend flips bullish to bearish OR close is below the 200-period SMA AND above the 5-period SMA AND 2-period RSI is above 90 AND fast EMA is below slow EMA OR lower-timeframe sigma trend turns bullish while higher-timeframe sigma trend remains bearish
Exit: Short, (within the configured date window AND composite trend score crosses above 19) OR Supertrend flips bearish to bullish OR sigma trend turns bullish OR lower-timeframe sigma trend turns bearish while higher-timeframe sigma trend remains bullish OR fast EMA is at least the slow EMA OR fast EMA is above slow EMA AND close is above the 200-period SMA AND close is below the 5-period SMA AND 2-period RSI is below 10 OR composite trend score crosses above its 15-period EMA
