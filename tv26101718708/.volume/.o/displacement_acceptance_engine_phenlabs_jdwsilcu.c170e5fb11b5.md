Calculation: Liquidity pools = confirmed swing highs and lows, using bars on the chart timeframe; retain the most recent pools on each side up to the pool limit.
Calculation: Acceptance score = min(10, round(10 × (min(2, sweep depth / 0.35) + min(2.5, 1.4 × displacement / minimum displacement) + min(2, 1.2 × displacement candle body / minimum body) + volume score + structure-break score + higher-timeframe score)) / 10), where sweep depth, displacement, and body are divided by current-timeframe ATR; volume score is min(2, 1 + (volume / volume SMA − volume threshold) × 0.6) when volume / volume SMA ≥ threshold, otherwise min(1, (volume / volume SMA) / volume threshold); structure-break score is 1 if the close breaks the pre-sweep 10-bar high for an up move or low for a down move, or closes beyond the swept pool by 0.25 ATR, otherwise 0; higher-timeframe score is 1 when move direction matches the current higher-timeframe close versus its previous completed bar, 0.4 when equal, and 0 when opposed (or 0.5 if higher-timeframe scoring is disabled).
Long: idle with no cooldown AND a bar pierces an active sellside pool by more than the ATR buffer, with wick at least the minimum sweep wick plus buffer, and (close above the pool if reclaim is required) AND within the displacement window after that sweep, close above the pool with an up candle, displacement from sweep low ≥ minimum displacement × ATR, and candle body ≥ minimum body × ATR OR within the acceptance window after that displacement, close ≥ the hold line and low > displacement origin, with no rejection, and acceptance score ≥ 0.7 × minimum acceptance score.
Short: idle with no cooldown AND a bar pierces an active buyside pool by more than the ATR buffer, with wick at least the minimum sweep wick plus buffer, and (close below the pool if reclaim is required) AND within the displacement window after that sweep, close below the pool with a down candle, displacement from sweep high ≥ minimum displacement × ATR, and candle body ≥ minimum body × ATR OR within the acceptance window after that displacement, close ≤ the hold line and high < displacement origin, with no rejection, and acceptance score ≥ 0.7 × minimum acceptance score.
Exit: long, after an up displacement, close below the swept pool and below the open OR acceptance window expires without acceptance.
Exit: short, after a down displacement, close above the swept pool and above the open OR acceptance window expires without acceptance.
