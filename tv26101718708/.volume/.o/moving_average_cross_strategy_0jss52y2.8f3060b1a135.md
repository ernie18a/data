Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of open with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA
Long: Williams %R crosses below the oversold threshold
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA
Short: Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + profit distance OR Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - profit distance OR Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold
