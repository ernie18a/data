Calculation: VWAP deviation = (close − VWAP) / (20-bar close standard deviation × 1.5) for FAM; use 2m, 5m, and 15m bars (latest available: completed historically, potentially forming in real time).
Calculation: Flow absorption = VWAP deviation magnitude meets its timeframe threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND volume / 20-bar average volume meets its minimum; count a timeframe bullish when deviation is negative, bearish when positive.
Calculation: Directional bias = 0.6 × (bullish-bar count − bearish-bar count) / lookback + 0.4 × mean of (close − prior close) / prior close over the lookback; bullish above a positive threshold, bearish below its negative.
Calculation: Support/resistance proximity = close is within 1% of any of the five most recent confirmed pivot lows/highs, respectively; pivots use configurable left/right bars.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = count of confirmed pivot pairs where price makes a lower low and RSI a higher low; bearish divergence = count where price makes a higher high and RSI a lower high. Reset counts every 50 bars; use the configured RSI length and equal left/right pivot lookback.
Calculation: Statistical reversion = Bollinger Bands from close (moving-average basis ± multiplier × standard deviation) and RSI; oversold means close below lower band and RSI below its lower threshold, overbought means close above upper band and RSI above its upper threshold.
Calculation: Institutional order-flow signal = volume / 20-bar average volume × (1 − candle body / candle range) exceeds its threshold, and candle direction is bullish or bearish; confidence is 40 if this condition holds, plus 30 if either directional toxicity exceeds its threshold, plus 30 if either directional toxicity exceeds 0.5. Directional toxicity is aggressive directional volume divided by total volume over its lookback; aggressive buys are bullish candles closing above 75% of range, aggressive sells bearish candles closing below 25% of range.
Calculation: Anchor deviation = VWAP deviation using the selected method: (close − VWAP) / (20-bar close standard deviation × 1.5), (close − VWAP) / 20-bar close standard deviation, or (close − VWAP) / 20-bar standard deviation of (close − VWAP). For each 2m, 5m, and 15m bar, also calculate the absolute difference between anchor deviation and the 20-bar z-score of the 14-bar stochastic value. Use latest available bars (completed historically, potentially forming in real time).
Calculation: Trend score = sum, over the configured range of prior bars, +1 when the current zero-lag EMA exceeds its value that many bars ago, otherwise −1. Zero-lag EMA = EMA(close + close − close[ floor((length − 1) / 2)]); band width = highest ATR over three times its length × multiplier.
Calculation: Mean-reversion average = selected WMA, SMA, RMA, EMA, or HMA of close; first lower/upper deviation levels = average × (1 − deviation) / (1 + deviation).
Calculation: Bull count = number of true bullish signals among: at least two timeframe flow-absorption signals; bullish directional bias; support proximity; bullish divergence count meets its minimum; statistical oversold; bullish institutional order-flow signal with confidence meeting its minimum; anchor deviation negative on at least two timeframes meeting their thresholds AND oscillator gap meeting its threshold on at least two timeframes; close below first lower deviation level; trend score above its bullish threshold AND close above zero-lag EMA plus band width.
Calculation: Bear count = number of true bearish signals among: at least two timeframe flow-absorption signals; bearish directional bias; resistance proximity; bearish divergence count meets its minimum; statistical overbought; bearish institutional order-flow signal with confidence meeting its minimum; anchor deviation positive on at least two timeframes meeting their thresholds AND oscillator gap meeting its threshold on at least two timeframes; close above first upper deviation level; trend score below its bearish threshold AND close below zero-lag EMA minus band width.
Long: position is flat AND long trades enabled AND (confluence enabled and bull count ≥ minimum OR confluence disabled and bull count > 0) AND (short condition is false OR bull count > bear count).
Short: position is flat AND short trades enabled AND (confluence enabled and bear count ≥ minimum OR confluence disabled and bear count > 0) AND (long condition is false OR bear count > bull count).
Exit: Long, short-entry condition is true OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage).
Exit: Short, long-entry condition is true OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage).
