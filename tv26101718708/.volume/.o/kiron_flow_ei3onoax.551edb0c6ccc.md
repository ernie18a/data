Calculation: Bar delta = sum lower-timeframe intrabar delta estimates; use the selected method: Close Location assigns volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or Up/Down Bar assigns positive volume when close > open and negative volume when close < open. For zero-range intrabars use the close versus prior intrabar close tick rule; if intrabars are unavailable, estimate from the chart bar by the same method.
Calculation: Session CVD = cumulative bar delta, reset at each VWAP anchor; CVD high/low are the running intrabar CVD extremes within the bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by the average volume of the same time slot over prior sessions.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP and bands = volume-weighted mean and standard-deviation bands since the selected anchor; band 1 and band 2 are the configured multiples of standard deviation above/below VWAP.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = on a confirmed bar, relative volume ≥ its threshold AND high − low ≤ its range limit × ATR(14) AND bar delta < 0 AND close ≥ low + 0.5 × (high − low) AND ((low ≤ VWAP AND close ≥ VWAP) OR low ≤ lower band 2).
Calculation: Bearish absorption = on a confirmed bar, relative volume ≥ its threshold AND high − low ≤ its range limit × ATR(14) AND bar delta > 0 AND close ≤ high − 0.5 × (high − low) AND ((high ≥ VWAP AND close ≤ VWAP) OR high ≥ upper band 2).
Calculation: Bearish CVD divergence = on a confirmed bar, high exceeds the prior N-bar highest high, is at/above the selected upper band zone, occurs at least the minimum gap after the previous such high in this anchor period, and current CVD high < CVD high at that previous price high.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = on a confirmed bar, low is below the prior N-bar lowest low, is at/below the selected lower band zone, occurs at least the minimum gap after the previous such low in this anchor period, and current CVD low > CVD low at that previous price low.
Long: Bullish absorption OR bullish CVD divergence OR (bullish absorption or bullish CVD divergence armed within the trigger window, not cancelled by a close below the event bar low or anchor reset, AND close > open AND close > prior close AND bar delta > 0 AND close > lower band 2).
Short: Bearish absorption OR bearish CVD divergence OR (bearish absorption or bearish CVD divergence armed within the trigger window, not cancelled by a close above the event bar high or anchor reset, AND close < open AND close < prior close AND bar delta < 0 AND close < upper band 2).
