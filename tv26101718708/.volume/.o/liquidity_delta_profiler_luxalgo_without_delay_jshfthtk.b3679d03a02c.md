Calculation: Bar delta = volume × (close − open) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Resistance liquidity zone = confirmed pivot-high bar’s high to max(open, close); if its height is less than the configured fraction of ATR, use that ATR-based minimum height. Support liquidity zone = confirmed pivot-low bar’s low to min(open, close); apply the same minimum-height rule. Pivots use configured left and right bar counts on the input timeframe.
Calculation: Zone deltas = accumulate each bar’s bar delta multiplied by the fraction of that bar’s range overlapping each of the zone’s four equal-height quadrants. The outer delta is the top-quadrant delta for resistance and bottom-quadrant delta for support. Outer-delta share = abs(outer delta) ÷ (sum of absolute quadrant deltas + epsilon).
Short: Active resistance zone, not previously signaled, and current bar overlaps or sweeps the zone, and (high > zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND outer-delta share > absorption threshold OR high > zone top AND outer-delta share < exhaustion threshold OR close is within zone AND outer delta > 0 AND outer-delta share > divergence threshold OR high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND abs(bar delta) ÷ (volume + epsilon) > rejection threshold), with each later alternative considered only if no earlier alternative is true.
Long: Active support zone, not previously signaled, and current bar overlaps or sweeps the zone, and (low < zone bottom AND outer delta > 0 AND outer-delta share > absorption threshold OR low < zone bottom AND outer-delta share < exhaustion threshold OR close is within zone AND outer delta < 0 AND outer-delta share > divergence threshold OR low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND abs(bar delta) ÷ (volume + epsilon) > rejection threshold), with each later alternative considered only if no earlier alternative is true.
