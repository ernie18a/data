KNN probabilities are inverse-(1 + Euclidean distance)-weighted votes among the nearest historical feature vectors; labels use the sign of the future horizon return beyond a threshold. Neural probabilities are a 3-class softmax linear classifier on the same features, updated online by cross-entropy gradient descent with weight decay.
