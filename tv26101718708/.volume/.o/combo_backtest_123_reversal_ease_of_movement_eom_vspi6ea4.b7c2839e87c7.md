Calculation: Reversal signal state = +1 when close[2] < close[1], close > close[1], fast stochastic < slow stochastic, and fast stochastic > its level; −1 when close[2] > close[1], close < close[1], fast stochastic > slow stochastic, and fast stochastic < its level; otherwise retain the previous state, initially 0.
Calculation: Ease of Movement signal state = +1 when 1,000,000 × (current half-range change − previous half-range change) ÷ (volume ÷ current range) > its upper threshold; −1 when it is < its lower threshold; otherwise retain the previous state, initially 0. Half-range is (high − low) ÷ 2; half-range change is the current half-range minus the previous half-range.
Long: reverse is off AND reversal signal state = +1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = +1, OR reverse is on AND reversal signal state = −1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = −1
Short: reverse is off AND reversal signal state = −1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = −1, OR reverse is on AND reversal signal state = +1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = +1
Exit: long, NOT (reverse is off AND reversal signal state = +1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = +1) AND NOT (reverse is on AND reversal signal state = −1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = −1)
Exit: short, NOT (reverse is off AND reversal signal state = −1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = −1) AND NOT (reverse is on AND reversal signal state = +1 AND Ease of Movement signal state = +1)
