Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the simple moving average of volume over a shorter lookback than the volume Z-score lookback
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (current volume − volume simple moving average) divided by volume standard deviation over a longer lookback
Calculation: Normalized bar range = (high − low) divided by average true range
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) divided by the bar range
Calculation: Normalized volume delta = clamp((volume × (2 × close position − 1)) divided by the volume simple moving average, to −100 through 100
Calculation: Flow momentum = normalized volume delta smoothed by two successive exponential moving averages
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of volume delta
Calculation: Smart score = clamp(0.45 × flow momentum + 0.35 × normalized volume delta + 0.20 × (close position − 0.5) × 100, to −100 through 100)
