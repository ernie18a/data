Calculation: ATR-normalized spread = (shorter-lookback SMMA(hl2) − longer-lookback SMMA(hl2)) / ATR × 100
Calculation: Spread Z-score = (ATR-normalized spread − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation
Calculation: Pressure Z-score = volume-weighted price change from a cost basis (0.4 × recent-short-lookback VWAP + 0.6 × longer-lookback VWAP), weighted by volume / 20-bar average volume, then standardized by its rolling mean and standard deviation
Calculation: Buy band = previous-bar Donchian low − ATR × learned buy penetration multiplier; sell band = previous-bar Donchian high + ATR × learned sell penetration multiplier. Each multiplier is the average of up to a longer-lookback window of positive ATR-normalized penetrations beyond the corresponding Donchian extreme, scaled by sensitivity and clamped to minimum/maximum; use the default multiplier until the minimum sample count is reached.
