Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = swing highs and lows confirmed after the same configurable number of bars on each side; merge a new swing into a waiting level within the ATR-scaled retest zone, otherwise add a level. Drop the oldest when the configured level limit is exceeded; remove an unbroken level after 500 bars.
Calculation: Break buffer = ATR-scaled distance beyond a level required for a break.
Calculation: Retest zone = ATR-scaled band around a level.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the retest bar low, prior bar low, or level, less an ATR-scaled buffer; cap it at least an ATR-scaled minimum distance below entry. Short stop = the higher of the retest bar high, prior bar high, or level, plus an ATR-scaled buffer; cap it at least an ATR-scaled minimum distance above entry.
Calculation: Risk distance = absolute difference between entry and stop.
Calculation: Long targets = entry plus risk distance times each configured target multiple; short targets = entry minus risk distance times each configured target multiple.
Long: A waiting swing level is crossed upward when close > level + break buffer AND prior close <= level; if strong-break filtering is enabled, the breakout candle body is at least one ATR OR available volume is greater than 1.5 times its recent moving average.
Short: A waiting swing level is crossed downward when close < level - break buffer AND prior close >= level; if strong-break filtering is enabled, the breakout candle body is at least one ATR OR available volume is greater than 1.5 times its recent moving average.
Long: A level has broken upward at least two bars earlier AND no more than the configured retest window ago AND low <= level + retest zone AND close > level AND close > open AND (strong-break filtering is disabled OR the break met its strength condition) AND (trend filtering is disabled OR close > the trend EMA) AND risk distance <= the configured maximum ATR-scaled stop distance AND current position is not long.
Short: A level has broken downward at least two bars earlier AND no more than the configured retest window ago AND high >= level - retest zone AND close < level AND close < open AND (strong-break filtering is disabled OR the break met its strength condition) AND (trend filtering is disabled OR close < the trend EMA) AND risk distance <= the configured maximum ATR-scaled stop distance AND current position is not short.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= third target OR a valid short entry occurs.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= third target OR a valid long entry occurs.
