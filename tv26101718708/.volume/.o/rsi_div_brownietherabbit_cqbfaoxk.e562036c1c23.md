Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close
Calculation: RSI pivot = RSI high/low confirmed after the specified left and right lookbacks; use the pivot bar's RSI and price
Calculation: Divergence ray = line through consecutive confirmed RSI pivots whose price and RSI make regular or hidden bullish divergence (low pivots) or bearish divergence (high pivots), with pivot separation within the specified range
Calculation: Ray cross up = previous RSI is below the selected ray at the previous bar and current RSI is above it at this bar; Ray cross down = previous RSI is above the ray and current RSI is below it
Calculation: RSI channel break = cross up through the upper of the two latest bearish divergence rays, or cross down through the lower of the two latest bullish divergence rays; the pair must meet the configured slope and gap limits, and RSI must have stayed within the configured buffered channel for the configured prior bars
Calculation: Strict bullish cross = cross up through the selected bearish ray after RSI was at least the configured gap below it within the configured lookback, that deepest point was at least the configured bars ago, RSI stayed beyond the configured buffer below the ray for the configured pre-break bars, and current RSI rise meets the configured minimum jump; strict bearish cross is the inverse for the selected bullish ray
Calculation: EMA trend stack = short-lookback EMA, medium-lookback EMA, and long-lookback EMA of close, ordered for bullish or bearish trend
Calculation: Volume average = SMA of volume over its configured lookback
Long: bullish ray cross OR bullish RSI channel break, using strict bullish cross instead of ray cross when strict-only mode is selected; AND enabled volume, trend, RSI-zone, higher-timeframe RSI, fan-out, key-level proximity, and bar-confirmation filters pass. In tiered trend mode, a counter-trend signal must also pass the strict bullish cross and volume filters; in hard-block mode, counter-trend signals are blocked. For a neutral RSI zone, require the configured neutral-zone allowance, strict bullish cross, and volume filter. Higher-timeframe RSI uses the configured higher timeframe's current forming bar. On 1H, 4H, weekly, or monthly bars, an enabled intrabar gate accepts a green forming candle; otherwise require a completed bar.
Short: bearish ray cross OR bearish RSI channel break, using strict bearish cross instead of ray cross when strict-only mode is selected; AND enabled volume, trend, RSI-zone, higher-timeframe RSI, fan-out, key-level proximity, and bar-confirmation filters pass. In tiered trend mode, a counter-trend signal must also pass the strict bearish cross and volume filters; in hard-block mode, counter-trend signals are blocked. For a neutral RSI zone, require the configured neutral-zone allowance, strict bearish cross, and volume filter. Higher-timeframe RSI uses the configured higher timeframe's current forming bar. On 1H, 4H, weekly, or monthly bars, an enabled intrabar gate accepts a red forming candle; otherwise require a completed bar.
Long: close > latest active swing decision level × (1 + configured buffer percentage / 100)
Short: close < latest active swing decision level × (1 − configured buffer percentage / 100)
Exit: long, close < latest active swing decision level
Exit: short, close > latest active swing decision level
