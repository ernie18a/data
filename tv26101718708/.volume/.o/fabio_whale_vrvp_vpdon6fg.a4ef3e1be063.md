Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the high-low range over that same lookback; trending means efficiency exceeds a threshold, with up/down direction set by whether current close is above/below the lookback close.
Calculation: Volume profile = over a lookback including the current bar, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into rows; allocate each bar's volume to each row in proportion to the overlap of the bar's high-low range with that row. POC is the highest-volume row's midpoint. Starting at POC, add the adjacent row with greater volume (upper row on ties) until the configured fraction of total volume is reached; the outer row midpoints are VAH and VAL.
Calculation: Near a profile level = absolute distance from close to that level divided by close is below a tolerance.
Calculation: Buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low); sell volume = volume × (high − close) / (high − low); both are zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Whale baseline = the moving average and standard deviation of buy or sell volume over a lookback, using only bars completed before the current bar. Tier thresholds increase with tier; the same baseline and sigma parameter apply, with higher tiers using larger sigma multiples.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low − ATR × stop multiplier; long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward-risk multiplier. Short stop = signal-bar high + ATR × stop multiplier; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward-risk multiplier.
Long: Buy volume exceeds its tier-1 whale threshold OR (in the configured session AND position is flat AND cooldown since the prior entry has elapsed AND buy volume meets the configured minimum tier AND ((trending up AND near POC, VAH, or VAL) OR (ranging AND near VAL or POC) OR (close below VAH-to-VAL area AND near VAL))).
Short: Sell volume exceeds its tier-1 whale threshold OR (in the configured session AND position is flat AND cooldown since the prior entry has elapsed AND sell volume meets the configured minimum tier AND ((trending down AND near POC, VAH, or VAL) OR (ranging AND near VAH or POC) OR (close above VAH-to-VAL area AND near VAH))).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR bars since entry ≥ maximum bars OR outside the configured session.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR bars since entry ≥ maximum bars OR outside the configured session.
