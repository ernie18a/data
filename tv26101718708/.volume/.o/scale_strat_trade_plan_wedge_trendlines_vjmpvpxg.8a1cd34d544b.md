Calculation: Strat up reversal = current bar is an up-directional bar (high > prior high and low >= prior low) after a down-directional bar; OR after an inside bar preceded two bars ago by a down-directional or outside bar
Calculation: Strat down reversal = current bar is a down-directional bar (low < prior low and high <= prior high) after an up-directional bar; OR after an inside bar preceded two bars ago by an up-directional or outside bar
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = pivot, S1, S2, R1, R2 calculated from the previous completed bar of the configurable anchor timeframe; previous week high/low from the last completed week; and latest confirmed swing low/high, where a swing requires the configured number of bars on both sides
Calculation: Near support/resistance = close is within the configured percentage of close of any support or resistance level
Calculation: Long setup score = sum of five true/false factors: near any support/resistance; doji or hammer or bullish engulfing candle; bullish daily/weekly/monthly candle alignment or Strat up reversal or hammer; low sweep or bullish break of structure or close inside the latest bullish fair-value gap; daily EMA confirmation
Calculation: Short setup score = sum of five true/false factors: near any support/resistance; doji or shooter or bearish engulfing candle; bearish daily/weekly/monthly candle alignment or Strat down reversal or shooter; high sweep or bearish break of structure or close inside the latest bearish fair-value gap; daily EMA confirmation
Calculation: Daily EMA confirmation = fast daily EMA crossed above (long) or below (short) slow daily EMA within the configured recent-bar window, OR close is above (long) or below (short) the slow daily EMA and within the configured proximity percentage of it; daily values use the current forming daily bar
Calculation: Low/high sweep = low below latest confirmed swing low and close above it / high above latest confirmed swing high and close below it
Calculation: Bullish/bearish break of structure = close crosses above latest confirmed swing high / below latest confirmed swing low
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair-value gap = latest three-bar gap has low above the high two bars ago / high below the low two bars ago; price is inside that gap when close lies between its boundaries
Calculation: Long entry trigger = Strat up reversal when reversal mode is enabled; otherwise long setup score crosses up to the configured minimum score
Calculation: Short entry trigger = Strat down reversal when reversal mode is enabled; otherwise short setup score crosses up to the configured minimum score
Calculation: Noise filter pass = when enabled, ATR divided by close is at most the configured maximum, volume exceeds its 20-bar average if volume filtering is enabled, and the bar is directionally clean: long closes above open with upper wick at most 40% of range and body at least 30% of range; short is the inverse
Calculation: Level gate pass = when enabled, long is near support or makes a bullish structure break while not near resistance; short is near resistance or makes a bearish structure break while not near support
Calculation: Sniper direction = 15-minute fast EMA is above slow EMA or close is above slow EMA for long; otherwise short; uses the current forming 15-minute bar
Long: Strat up reversal OR (long setup score >= configured minimum score AND long entry trigger AND noise filter passes AND level gate passes AND (sniper filter disabled OR sniper direction is long) AND (late-entry filter disabled OR New York hour < 15) AND (high - low) >= configured minimum reversal size × ATR)
Short: Strat down reversal OR (short setup score >= configured minimum score AND short entry trigger AND noise filter passes AND level gate passes AND (sniper filter disabled OR sniper direction is short) AND (late-entry filter disabled OR New York hour < 15) AND (high - low) >= configured minimum reversal size × ATR)
Exit: long, low <= long stop and prior low > long stop OR high >= first long target and prior high < first long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop and prior high < short stop OR low <= first short target and prior low > first short target
