Calculation: Momentum-adaptive ATR trailing stop = ratcheting stop whose distance is ATR times sensitivity adjusted by asset type, volatility, relative volume, and Kaufman efficiency ratio; in an uptrend it can only rise, and in a downtrend it can only fall
Calculation: Hull moving average = weighted moving average of twice the half-period weighted moving average of close minus the full-period weighted moving average of close, over the square root of the period
Calculation: RSI divergence = compare successive confirmed price pivot lows with RSI at those lows, and successive price pivot highs with RSI at those highs
Calculation: Stop loss = on entry, calculate the configured ATR-, percentage-, tick-, swing-, scaled-ATR-, smart-adaptive-, or widest-of-these-methods stop on the loss side of entry, then add the configured buffer; swing stops use recent lows for longs and highs for shorts
Calculation: Risk distance = absolute difference between entry price and the unbuffered stop loss
Calculation: Take-profit level = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the risk distance multiplied by each enabled reward multiple
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above the momentum-adaptive ATR trailing stop AND (if enabled) the signal passes the configured direction confirmation, cooldown, ADX regime, volume, high-volatility, RSI, Hull moving average, SuperTrend, higher-timeframe EMA trend, resistance-zone, full-candle, swing-structure, and percent-change filters AND the last emitted signal was not long; OR a confirmed bullish RSI divergence occurs (a lower price pivot low and higher RSI pivot low)
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below the momentum-adaptive ATR trailing stop AND (if enabled) the signal passes the configured direction confirmation, cooldown, ADX regime, volume, high-volatility, RSI, Hull moving average, SuperTrend, higher-timeframe EMA trend, support-zone, full-candle, swing-structure, and percent-change filters AND the last emitted signal was not short; OR a confirmed bearish RSI divergence occurs (a higher price pivot high and lower RSI pivot high)
Exit: long, low reaches the stop loss or high reaches the farthest enabled take-profit level, or a valid short entry signal occurs
Exit: short, high reaches the stop loss or low reaches the farthest enabled take-profit level, or a valid long entry signal occurs
