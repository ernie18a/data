Calculation: Trend = simple moving average of close over the longest lookback
Calculation: Fast = exponential moving average over the shortest lookback, using close unless dynamic sourcing is enabled, then low when close > Trend and high otherwise
Calculation: Slow = simple moving average over an intermediate lookback, using close unless dynamic sourcing is enabled, then low when close > Trend and high otherwise
Long: configured bullish crossover (Fast crosses above Slow, Fast crosses above Trend, or Slow crosses above Trend) AND (in-trend filter disabled OR Fast >= Slow AND Slow >= Trend) AND long entries enabled AND (entry volume filter disabled OR volume > configured multiplier * simple moving average of volume) AND (entry volatility filter disabled OR ATR over the shorter lookback > ATR over the longer lookback) AND (entry RSI filter disabled OR RSI > configured lower bound AND RSI < configured upper bound)
Short: configured bearish crossover (Fast crosses below Slow, Fast crosses below Trend, or Slow crosses below Trend) AND (in-trend filter disabled OR Fast <= Slow AND Slow <= Trend) AND short entries enabled AND (entry volume filter disabled OR volume > configured multiplier * simple moving average of volume) AND (entry volatility filter disabled OR ATR over the shorter lookback > ATR over the longer lookback) AND (entry RSI filter disabled OR RSI > configured lower bound AND RSI < configured upper bound)
Exit: long, configured bearish crossover OR (stop loss enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - configured stop percentage)) OR (take profit enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + configured target percentage))
Exit: short, configured bullish crossover OR (stop loss enabled AND high >= entry price * (1 + configured stop percentage)) OR (take profit enabled AND low <= entry price * (1 - configured target percentage))
