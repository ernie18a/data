Calculation: Bullish active swing order block = latest unmitigated zone made from the high and low of the bar at the swing-lookback offset when a swing low is confirmed; it is mitigated when a later close falls below its bottom
Calculation: Bearish active swing order block = latest unmitigated zone made from the high and low of the bar at the swing-lookback offset when a swing high is confirmed; it is mitigated when a later close rises above its top
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of signed volume, with positive volume when the bar closes at or above its open and negative otherwise; when intrabar delta is selected, sum signed volume from lower-timeframe bars instead
Long: low < the prior 10-bar lowest chart low AND ((low < the prior 10-bar lowest chart low AND VIX high < its prior 10-bar highest high) OR (low < the prior 10-bar lowest chart low AND PCC high < its prior 10-bar highest high) OR (low < the prior 10-bar lowest chart low AND comparison instrument low > its prior 10-bar lowest low)) AND (RSI < 30 AND low < lower Bollinger Band, if enabled) AND (current bar range overlaps the active bullish swing order block, if enabled) AND (low < prior 5-bar lowest low AND cumulative volume delta > its prior 5-bar lowest value, if enabled) AND (low < low of the last completed SFP-reference-timeframe bar AND close > that low, if enabled)
Short: high > the prior 10-bar highest chart high AND ((high > the prior 10-bar highest chart high AND VIX low > its prior 10-bar lowest low) OR (high > the prior 10-bar highest chart high AND PCC low > its prior 10-bar lowest low) OR (high > the prior 10-bar highest chart high AND comparison instrument high < its prior 10-bar highest high)) AND (RSI > 70 AND high > upper Bollinger Band, if enabled) AND (current bar range overlaps the active bearish swing order block, if enabled) AND (high > prior 5-bar highest high AND cumulative volume delta < its prior 5-bar highest value, if enabled) AND (high > high of the last completed SFP-reference-timeframe bar AND close < that high, if enabled)
Exit: long, short-entry condition OR high >= take-profit price OR low <= stop-loss price
Exit: short, long-entry condition OR low <= take-profit price OR high >= stop-loss price
