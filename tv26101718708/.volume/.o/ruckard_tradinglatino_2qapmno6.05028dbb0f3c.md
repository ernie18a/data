Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression, over the shorter lookback, of close minus the average of (highest high + lowest low)/2 and the close moving average; light green = above zero and rising, dark green = above zero and falling, light red = below zero and falling, dark red = below zero and rising.
Long: Squeeze momentum is dark red OR light green OR the fast EMA exceeds the slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars OR (that higher-timeframe EMA condition AND (squeeze momentum is dark red OR light green) AND the bar's high exceeds the highest recent volume-profile point of control below the bar's low) AND the point of control supports a valid long stop distance; suppress entry if the long exit condition is true or a new entry is too recent.
Short: Squeeze momentum is dark green OR light red OR the fast EMA is below the slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars OR (that higher-timeframe EMA condition AND (squeeze momentum is dark green OR light red) AND the bar's low is below the lowest recent volume-profile point of control above the bar's high) AND the point of control supports a valid short stop distance; suppress entry if the short exit condition is true or a new entry is too recent.
Exit: Long, squeeze momentum is dark green OR the higher-timeframe fast EMA is below the slow EMA OR the bar close is below the entry price by the stop distance OR a triggered trailing stop is hit OR the order is old and the close is below entry by the minimum loss.
Exit: Short, squeeze momentum is dark red OR the higher-timeframe fast EMA exceeds the slow EMA OR the bar close is above the entry price by the stop distance OR a triggered trailing stop is hit OR the order is old and the close is above entry by the minimum loss.
