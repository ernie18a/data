Calculation: ZLEMA = 2 × EMA(source, lookback) − EMA(EMA(source, lookback), lookback)
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed RSI pivot low while source at the corresponding price pivot is lower than at the previous price pivot; confirm after the right-side pivot lookback bars
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed RSI pivot high while source at the corresponding price pivot is higher than at the previous price pivot; confirm after the right-side pivot lookback bars
Calculation: Structural floor = lowest low over the structural lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Structural ceiling = highest high over the structural lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = while long, source minus ATR × trailing multiplier, updated to the maximum of this value and the prior stop; while short, source plus ATR × trailing multiplier, updated to the minimum of this value and the prior stop; reset when flat
Long: source crosses above ZLEMA + ATR × multiplier AND ZLEMA > prior-bar ZLEMA OR source crosses above the upper Bollinger Band OR bullish RSI divergence OR MACD line crosses above MACD signal line OR (source > macro EMA AND source crosses above ZLEMA AND RSI < overbought threshold); AND, when enabled, source > trend EMA; AND, when enabled, volume ≥ volume EMA × volume multiplier
Short: source crosses below ZLEMA − ATR × multiplier AND ZLEMA < prior-bar ZLEMA OR source crosses below the lower Bollinger Band OR bearish RSI divergence OR MACD line crosses below MACD signal line OR (source < macro EMA AND source crosses below ZLEMA AND RSI > oversold threshold); AND, when enabled, source < trend EMA; AND, when enabled, volume ≥ volume EMA × volume multiplier
Exit: Long, source crosses below ZLEMA OR (trailing stop enabled and source crosses below ATR trailing stop) OR RSI crosses above overbought level OR MACD line crosses below MACD signal line OR source crosses below structural floor OR source crosses below macro EMA OR bar low ≤ stop level OR bar high ≥ target level
Exit: Short, source crosses above ZLEMA OR (trailing stop enabled and source crosses above ATR trailing stop) OR RSI crosses below oversold level OR MACD line crosses above MACD signal line OR source crosses above structural ceiling OR source crosses above macro EMA OR bar high ≥ stop level OR bar low ≤ target level
Calculation: Stop level = on the entry signal bar, close − ATR × stop multiplier or close × (1 − stop percentage) for long; close + ATR × stop multiplier or close × (1 + stop percentage) for short
Calculation: Target level = on the entry signal bar, close + ATR × target multiplier or close × (1 + target percentage) for long; close − ATR × target multiplier or close × (1 − target percentage) for short
