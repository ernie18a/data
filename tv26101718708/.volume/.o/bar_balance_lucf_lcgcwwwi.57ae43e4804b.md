Calculation: Up, down, and neutral counts = counts of constituent lower-timeframe bars in the current chart bar with close > open, close < open, and close = open, respectively; use completed chart bars. Total count = their sum; active-bar ratio = (up count + down count) / total count.
Calculation: Neutral dampener = 1, the active-bar ratio, or an EMA of that ratio, according to the selected setting.
Calculation: Instant balance = (up count − down count) × neutral dampener.
Calculation: Average balance = (EMA of up count − EMA of down count) × neutral dampener.
Calculation: Momentum balance = RSI of [(EMA of up count − SMA of up count at twice the EMA lookback) − (EMA of down count − SMA of down count at twice the EMA lookback)], multiplied by neutral dampener. The EMA lookback is the same as for Average balance.
Calculation: Combined balance = the sum of +1 for each positive Instant, Average, or Momentum balance and −1 for each negative one, multiplied by neutral dampener. It is full bullish when it equals three times the dampener and full bearish when it equals negative three times the dampener.
Calculation: Marker bias = the lookback sum of bullish marker occurrences minus bearish marker occurrences for markers 1–4, multiplied by neutral dampener.
Calculation: Divergence up = the selected Instant, Average, Momentum, or Combined balance is positive while the chart bar closes at or below its open. Divergence down = that selected balance is negative while the chart bar closes above its open. A selected neutral-count divergence also occurs when the active-bar ratio is below 0.5.
Calculation: Divergence levels = upper and lower levels initialized from the selected balance on a divergence; extend to the highest and lowest selected-balance values across consecutive divergences. After the balance breaches either level, reset both levels to the selected balance at the next divergence.
Long: Instant, Average, and Momentum balances are all positive OR up count / total count exceeds the selected percentage and close > open OR previous bar had Divergence up and current close > open and current bar has neither Divergence up nor Divergence down OR selected Average, Momentum, or Combined balance crosses above zero OR Marker bias crossed above zero on the previous bar OR selected balance is above the previous upper divergence level and was not above it on the previous bar.
Short: Instant, Average, and Momentum balances are all negative OR down count / total count exceeds the selected percentage and close <= open OR previous bar had Divergence down and current close <= open and current bar has neither Divergence up nor Divergence down OR selected Average, Momentum, or Combined balance crosses below zero OR Marker bias crossed below zero on the previous bar OR selected balance is below the previous lower divergence level and was not below it on the previous bar.
