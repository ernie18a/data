Calculation: Momentum = the rolling z-score of price minus its linear regression over a longer lookback, low-lag filtered.
Calculation: Money flow = the low-lag-filtered, recursively smoothed z-score of the volume-weighted Hull moving-average ratio, where volume is positive when price exceeds its value two bars earlier and negative otherwise.
Calculation: Oscillator = the low-lag-filtered weighted blend of Momentum and Money flow, scaled by 50; Signal line = a low-lag filter of the Oscillator.
Calculation: Confluence score = 100 × the fraction of these seven conditions that are true: Oscillator > 0; Oscillator > Signal line; smoothed money flow > 0; shorter-period EMA fan has more shorter-above-longer than shorter-below-longer pair orderings; close > session VWAP (if unavailable, Momentum’s z-score > 0); price change over the momentum lookback divided by ATR times that lookback > 0; last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA using the momentum lookback.
Calculation: Efficiency Ratio = absolute price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar price changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Oversold level = rolling Oscillator mean minus a multiple of its rolling standard deviation; overbought level = rolling mean plus that multiple.
Calculation: Volume-flow fast wave = low-lag-filtered smoothed money flow, normalized by its rolling standard deviation and bounded with a hyperbolic tangent; its oversold and overbought boundaries are symmetric.
Calculation: Climax = volume exceeds its rolling average by a multiplier.
Long: Oscillator crosses above Signal line AND Oscillator is below the oversold level AND Confluence score meets the bullish gate AND Efficiency Ratio meets the non-choppy threshold AND volume-flow fast wave is not overbought.
Long: Climax AND Oscillator below the oversold level AND the same condition was false on the prior bar.
Long: At a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price low is below its value at the preceding confirmed pivot low, oscillator is above its value at that preceding pivot, and the pivots are within the maximum separation; OR price low is above its value at the preceding confirmed pivot low, oscillator is below its value at that preceding pivot, and the pivots are within the maximum separation.
Long: Confluence score >= 85.
Short: Oscillator crosses below Signal line AND Oscillator is above the overbought level AND Confluence score meets the bearish gate AND Efficiency Ratio meets the non-choppy threshold AND volume-flow fast wave is not oversold.
Short: Climax AND Oscillator above the overbought level AND the same condition was false on the prior bar.
Short: At a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price high is above its value at the preceding confirmed pivot high, oscillator is below its value at that preceding pivot, and the pivots are within the maximum separation; OR price high is below its value at the preceding confirmed pivot high, oscillator is above its value at that preceding pivot, and the pivots are within the maximum separation.
Short: Confluence score <= 15.
