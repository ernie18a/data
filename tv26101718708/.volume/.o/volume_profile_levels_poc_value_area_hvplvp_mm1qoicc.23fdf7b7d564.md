Calculation: Volume-profile levels = Over a rolling lookback of profile-timeframe bars, divide the high-low range into equal-price bins; distribute each bar's volume evenly across the bins its range spans. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Starting there, expand the Value Area toward the larger adjacent bin until it contains the target share of volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower boundaries. Use the current profile-timeframe bar (forming in real time; historical values become available when that bar completes).
Calculation: High- and low-volume peaks = A high-volume peak is an interior bin whose volume is greater than the preceding bin, at least the following bin, and greater than average bin volume times a threshold. A low-volume peak is an interior bin whose volume is less than the preceding bin, at most the following bin, and less than average bin volume times a threshold. Select up to six of each; rank high-volume peaks by descending volume and low-volume peaks by ascending volume.
Long: (close > POC AND previous close <= POC) OR (close > VAH AND previous close <= VAH) OR (close > VAL AND previous close <= VAL) OR (close > any high- or low-volume peak AND previous close <= that peak)
Short: (close < POC AND previous close >= POC) OR (close < VAH AND previous close >= VAH) OR (close < VAL AND previous close >= VAL) OR (close < any high- or low-volume peak AND previous close >= that peak)
