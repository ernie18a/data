Calculation: Recent lower-timeframe up volume = sum of volume across the latest configured number of positive-volume lower-timeframe bars, excluding the two newest; classify a bar as up when its close is greater than the preceding lower-timeframe close.
Calculation: Recent lower-timeframe down volume = sum of volume across the same bars not classified as up, including bars with unchanged close.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe cumulative volume delta = buy volume minus sell volume across the latest configured number of positive-volume lower-timeframe bars; classify volume by close change versus the preceding lower-timeframe close, splitting volume equally when unchanged.
Calculation: Chart cumulative volume delta = the configured SUM, EMA, or SMA over the configured lookback of candle volume delta; candle volume delta is positive for a bullish candle and negative for a bearish candle, with magnitude equal to volume times the candle body as a fraction of its high-low range (using at least one tick as the range).
Calculation: Recent average lower-timeframe volume = recent lower-timeframe up volume plus recent lower-timeframe down volume, divided by the number of included lower-timeframe bars.
