Calculation: Moving average = selected moving-average type applied to the selected source over a lookback
Long: bar time is within the configured trading window AND position is flat AND moving average crosses above session VWAP
Short: bar time is within the configured trading window AND position is flat AND moving average crosses below session VWAP
Exit: Long, low reaches or falls below the configured stop-loss level OR high reaches or exceeds the configured take-profit level OR bar time is at or after the configured close-all time
Exit: Short, high reaches or exceeds the configured stop-loss level OR low reaches or falls below the configured take-profit level OR bar time is at or after the configured close-all time
