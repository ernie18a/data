Calculation: Seed rank = For each opposing candle in the backward search window, body fraction × 0.36 + direction-specific wick fraction × 0.24 + volume percentile rank ÷ 100 × 0.22 + recency fraction × 0.18; choose the candle with the highest rank. Body fraction is absolute open-close distance ÷ high-low range. Wick fraction is lower wick ÷ range for an upward break, upper wick ÷ range for a downward break. Recency fraction decreases linearly from the most recent candle to the oldest searched candle.
Calculation: Block quality = the lesser of 100 and (seed rank × 58 + break drive × 16 + the lesser of relative volume × 10 and 18 + volume percentile rank × 0.08); break drive is the close's distance beyond the pivot divided by ATR, and relative volume is current volume divided by its moving average.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close > last confirmed pivot high AND prior close ≤ that pivot high AND upward close change ÷ ATR ≥ minimum break impulse AND a highest-ranked opposing candle exists in the backward search window AND block quality ≥ minimum score AND (overlap guard is disabled OR block quality > every overlapping active block's score)
Short: On a confirmed bar, close < last confirmed pivot low AND prior close ≥ that pivot low AND downward close change ÷ ATR ≥ minimum break impulse AND a highest-ranked opposing candle exists in the backward search window AND block quality ≥ minimum score AND (overlap guard is disabled OR block quality > every overlapping active block's score)
