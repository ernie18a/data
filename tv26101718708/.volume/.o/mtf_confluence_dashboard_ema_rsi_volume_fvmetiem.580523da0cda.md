Calculation: Per-timeframe score = +1 for four EMAs in strictly descending order of lookback length, +1 when RSI exceeds its upper threshold, and +1 when volume exceeds its longer-lookback average times a multiplier and close > open; use -1 for the inverse EMA order, RSI below its lower threshold, or a volume spike with close < open; otherwise each component is 0. Sum the three components; total confluence is the sum across four configured timeframes, from shorter to longer. Use the last completed bar of each timeframe.
Long: Any timeframe score >= 2 OR total confluence >= aligned threshold and prior total was below it OR total confluence is >= lean threshold and < aligned threshold and prior total was not in that lean range or aligned range; an opposite prior signal additionally requires a neutral-zone reset if enabled and the minimum bar gap.
Short: Any timeframe score <= -2 OR total confluence <= -aligned threshold and prior total was above it OR total confluence is <= -lean threshold and > -aligned threshold and prior total was not in that lean range or aligned range; an opposite prior signal additionally requires a neutral-zone reset if enabled and the minimum bar gap.
