Calculation: RSI(n) = Relative Strength Index of close over n bars
Calculation: Donchian midpoint(n) = (lowest low over n bars + highest high over n bars) / 2
Calculation: Chandelier long stop(n,m) = ratcheting maximum of highest high over n bars minus m × ATR(n); reset to that value when prior close is not above the prior stop
Calculation: Chandelier short stop(n,m) = ratcheting minimum of lowest low over n bars plus m × ATR(n); reset to that value when prior close is not below the prior stop
Calculation: Ichimoku filter = Tenkan midpoint(20), Kijun midpoint(60), their average, and midpoint(120); compare current spans and close with spans displaced 30 bars back as specified in the conditions below
Calculation: MA squeeze state = bullish when EMA(short) > EMA(long) and their distance is at least 0.4 × ATR(50); bearish when EMA(short) < EMA(long) and that distance is at least 0.4 × ATR(50)
Calculation: Entry stop = selected method: prior external stop value; min(open,close) minus ATR(n) × m for long / max(open,close) plus ATR(n) × m for short; min(open,close) × (1 − p) for long / max(open,close) × (1 + p) for short; or min(open,close) minus fixed distance for long / max(open,close) plus fixed distance for short
Calculation: In-trade stop = selected method: highest high since entry minus m × entry stop distance for long / lowest low since entry plus m × entry stop distance for short; highest high × (1 − p) / lowest low × (1 + p); highest high minus fixed distance / lowest low plus fixed distance; Donchian midpoint(n); mean of prior two min(open,close) minus ATR(n) × m / mean of prior two max(open,close) plus ATR(n) × m; Chandelier stop(n,m); volatility stop using ATR(n) × m and the running close extreme; or prior bar low for long / prior bar high for short. Use only if it is on the valid side of the bar and passes the selected kick-in threshold; thereafter it may move only in the trade’s favor. The entry stop applies until then.
Long: Bull filter state: every enabled filter allows long — rising random filter; external state = +1; close > open; volume > prior volume; SMA(close,n) rises for k bars; close − long entry stop < close × p; close < prior close × (1 + p); Ichimoku bullish (either spans and displaced spans are bullish with close above displaced cloud, or Tenkan > Kijun and both exceed the displaced cloud top with close above it), with Tenkan and Kijun rising; RSI(n) ≤ oversold/overbought ceiling; Chandelier filter direction bullish; or MA squeeze bullish
Long: While flat, selected entry condition occurs: random trigger; external entry signal > 0 except protocol values +1 and +3; bull filter state transitions from false to true; bull filter state is true; RSI(n) crosses above its selected level; or Donchian midpoint(short) crosses above Donchian midpoint(long)
Long: While already long, selected long entry condition occurs and pyramiding is enabled, the pyramid count is below its limit, and any required filters allow long; also satisfy the selected pyramid rule: close > last entry price + m × initial entry stop distance; close > last entry price × (1 + p); close > last entry price + fixed increment; same entry signal as prior entry; different entry signal from prior entry; or any entry opportunity
Short: Bear filter state: every enabled filter allows short — falling random filter; external state = −1; close < open; volume > prior volume; SMA(close,n) falls for k bars; short entry stop − close < close × p; close > prior close × (1 − p); Ichimoku bearish (either spans and displaced spans are bearish with close below displaced cloud, or Tenkan < Kijun and both are below the displaced cloud top with close below it), with Tenkan and Kijun falling; RSI(n) ≥ oversold floor; Chandelier filter direction bearish; or MA squeeze bearish
Short: While flat, selected entry condition occurs: random trigger; external entry signal < 0 except protocol values −1 and −3; bear filter state transitions from false to true; bear filter state is true; RSI(n) crosses below its selected level; or Donchian midpoint(short) crosses below Donchian midpoint(long)
Short: While already short, selected short entry condition occurs and pyramiding is enabled, the pyramid count is below its limit, and any required filters allow short; also satisfy the selected pyramid rule: close < last entry price − m × initial entry stop distance; close < last entry price × (1 − p); close < last entry price − fixed increment; same entry signal as prior entry; different entry signal from prior entry; or any entry opportunity
Exit: Long, close < prior applicable in-trade stop; OR selected hard exit occurs: external signal = +3; bear filter state transitions to true; RSI(n) crosses below its selected level; Donchian midpoint(short) crosses below Donchian midpoint(long); or close exceeds entry fill + m × initial entry stop distance
Exit: Short, close > prior applicable in-trade stop; OR selected hard exit occurs: external signal = −3; bull filter state transitions to true; RSI(n) crosses above its selected level; Donchian midpoint(short) crosses above Donchian midpoint(long); or close falls below entry fill − m × initial entry stop distance
