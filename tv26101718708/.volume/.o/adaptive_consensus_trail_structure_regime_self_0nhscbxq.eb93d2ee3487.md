Calculation: Adaptive consensus trail = a ratcheting stop chosen from a regime-adaptive ATR band or a structural consensus level; the band widens in high volatility and non-trending regimes, and the structural level is used only when that side’s confidence meets its threshold. Structural consensus is the reliability-weighted densest cluster of enabled support or resistance references within an ATR-scaled band: anchored VWAP band, nearest eligible session or unfilled prior-session volume POC, latest fair-value-gap midpoint, latest confirmed swing pivot, and absorption level. Reference reliability is the Wilson lower bound of its rolling respect rate, using a neutral weight until enough samples; POC respect is scored by forward rejection or break. Confidence combines cluster agreement, tightness, reliability, and regime quality, then is reduced by recent regime thrash. The trail ratchets upward while long and downward while short; flip confirmation breach increases with thrash and when not trending.
Calculation: Regime = trending when ADX and efficiency ratio exceed their thresholds and (if enabled) Hurst is not mean-reverting; otherwise it is volatile when the volatility-cluster score exceeds its threshold, or range. Trend direction is close versus a moving average.
Long: Previous direction is short AND close > trail + confirmation-breach distance AND the minimum bars between flips have elapsed.
Short: Previous direction is long AND close < trail - confirmation-breach distance AND the minimum bars between flips have elapsed.
Exit: Long, close < trail - confirmation-breach distance AND the minimum bars between flips have elapsed.
Exit: Short, close > trail + confirmation-breach distance AND the minimum bars between flips have elapsed.
