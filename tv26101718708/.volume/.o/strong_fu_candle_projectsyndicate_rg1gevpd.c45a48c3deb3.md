Calculation: Bullish FU = current bar sweeps below the previous bar’s low by at least the configured grab threshold, then either closes above the previous high and previous open by at least the engulf threshold (full FU), or closes above the previous candle body top but at or below its high by at least the engulf threshold (attempted FU; enabled by configuration)
Calculation: Bearish FU = current bar sweeps above the previous bar’s high by at least the configured grab threshold, then either closes below the previous low and previous open by at least the engulf threshold (full FU), or closes below the previous candle body bottom but at or above its low by at least the engulf threshold (attempted FU; enabled by configuration)
Calculation: Quality score = sum of body score (body/range: ≥0.70=3, ≥0.60=2.5, ≥0.50=2, ≥0.40=1, otherwise 0.5), opposite-wick score (opposite wick/body: ≤0.20=2, ≤0.50=1.5, ≤1=1, otherwise 0), grab-wick score (grab wick/body: ≤0.40=2, ≤0.60=1.5, ≤1=1, otherwise 0), close-position score (≥0.90=2, ≥0.85=1.5, ≥0.75=1, otherwise 0), and 0.5 each when bar range is 0.8–2.0 ATR(14) or volume is at least its simple moving average; cap at 10
Calculation: Tier = C below 5, B from 5, A from 6.5, S from 8 (minimum tier and minimum score are configurable)
Long: bullish FU AND configured minimum bar range/body and maximum body constraints pass AND (rejection filter disabled OR upper wick ≤ body) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (fractal filter disabled OR low < the lowest low of the preceding configured lookback bars) AND (BIW-only disabled OR previous bar’s body ≤ 0.4 of its range and both wicks are nonzero) AND quality score ≥ configured minimum AND tier ≥ configured minimum; under Fade direction logic, use bearish FU instead
Short: bearish FU AND configured minimum bar range/body and maximum body constraints pass AND (rejection filter disabled OR lower wick ≤ body) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (fractal filter disabled OR high > the highest high of the preceding configured lookback bars) AND (BIW-only disabled OR previous bar’s body ≤ 0.4 of its range and both wicks are nonzero) AND quality score ≥ configured minimum AND tier ≥ configured minimum; under Fade direction logic, use bullish FU instead
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ TP3; stop is below the FU low by the configured buffer and TP3 is entry plus three configured risk multiples, where risk is the absolute distance from entry to stop
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ TP3; stop is above the FU high by the configured buffer and TP3 is entry minus three configured risk multiples, where risk is the absolute distance from entry to stop
