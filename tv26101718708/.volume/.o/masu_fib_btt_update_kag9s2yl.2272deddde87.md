Calculation: Qualifying swing high/low = most recent bar highs/lows that are respectively the highest high/lowest low over a rolling lookback plus the current bar, and whose distance from the opposite rolling extreme is at least a minimum ATR multiple; use the latest qualifying high and low, only when both are at least two bars old. Swing direction is upswing when the qualifying high occurred after the qualifying low.
Calculation: Fibonacci retracement levels = qualifying swing high minus 38.2%, 50%, or 61.8% of the range between the qualifying swing high and low.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on up-close bars minus volume on down-close bars; flat-close bars contribute zero.
Long: Confirmed bar AND (low is within an ATR-based zone of an enabled Fibonacci retracement level in an upswing AND (lower wick is at least a configured fraction of bar range OR bullish close is above the nearest touched level) AND both qualifying swing points are at least two bars old OR (position is flat AND (ADX is above its threshold AND short-term EMA crosses above longer-term EMA AND short-term EMA is above longer-term EMA AND close is above a still-longer-term EMA AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times a multiplier OR close exceeds the prior bar's rolling highest high AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times a multiplier AND close is above the longer-term EMA OR ADX is at or below its threshold AND close is below the lower Bollinger Band AND RSI is below its oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above its oversold threshold) AND (higher-timeframe fast EMA is above its slower EMA and higher-timeframe close is above its fast EMA, or that filter is disabled) AND (at least the configured number of consecutive short-to-long ribbon averages are in descending-period bullish order, or that filter is disabled) AND (order-flow filter is disabled OR close is above session VWAP OR (cumulative volume delta is above its moving average and close is below prior close and cumulative volume delta is above prior value OR up-close volume divided by max(down-close volume, 1) exceeds 1 plus a threshold) AND (close is above session VWAP or crosses above it)) AND (Fibonacci filter is disabled OR Fibonacci bullish condition above OR qualifying swing direction is upswing) AND configured session filter passes))
Short: Confirmed bar AND (high is within an ATR-based zone of an enabled Fibonacci retracement level in a downswing AND (upper wick is at least a configured fraction of bar range OR bearish close is below the nearest touched level) AND both qualifying swing points are at least two bars old OR (position is flat AND (ADX is above its threshold AND short-term EMA crosses below longer-term EMA AND short-term EMA is below longer-term EMA AND close is below a still-longer-term EMA AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times a multiplier OR close is below the prior bar's rolling lowest low AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times a multiplier AND close is below the longer-term EMA OR ADX is at or below its threshold AND close is above the upper Bollinger Band AND RSI is above its overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below its overbought threshold) AND (higher-timeframe fast EMA is below its slower EMA and higher-timeframe close is below its fast EMA, or that filter is disabled) AND (at least the configured number of consecutive short-to-long ribbon averages are in descending-period bearish order, or that filter is disabled) AND (order-flow filter is disabled OR close is below session VWAP OR (cumulative volume delta is below its moving average and close is above prior close and cumulative volume delta is below prior value OR up-close volume divided by max(down-close volume, 1) is below 1 minus a threshold) AND (close is below session VWAP or crosses below it)) AND (Fibonacci filter is disabled OR Fibonacci bearish condition above OR qualifying swing direction is downswing) AND configured session filter passes))
Exit: Long, low reaches the signal-bar close minus an ATR-based stop distance OR (trailing stop enabled and price falls by its ATR-based trail distance from the highest price since entry) OR (trailing stop disabled and high reaches the signal-bar close plus an ATR-based profit distance)
Exit: Short, high reaches the signal-bar close plus an ATR-based stop distance OR (trailing stop enabled and price rises by its ATR-based trail distance from the lowest price since entry) OR (trailing stop disabled and low reaches the signal-bar close minus an ATR-based profit distance)
