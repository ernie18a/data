Calculation: Smoothed RSI = RSI of close, smoothed with an EMA; compare it with its prior-bar value and an EMA signal line.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Daily score = sum the configured points for: smoothed RSI in the buy zone; smoothed RSI above its signal line; smoothed RSI rising from the prior bar; close above the daily EMA when trend filtering is enabled; relative volume at or above its minimum when volume filtering is enabled.
Calculation: Weekly score = sum the configured points for: weekly RSI at or above its minimum; weekly RSI above its signal line; weekly RSI rising from the prior weekly bar; weekly close above the weekly EMA when trend filtering is enabled. Weekly values use the current weekly bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Composite score = rounded sum of daily score and weekly score multiplied by their respective weights, with weights normalized by their sum.
Calculation: Drawdown percentage = (1 − close / highest high over the configured lookback) × 100.
Long: transition into extreme opportunity (drawdown percentage at or above its threshold AND weekly RSI at or below its maximum AND daily smoothed RSI at or below its maximum AND, when required, daily smoothed RSI rising from the prior bar AND, when required, daily smoothed RSI above its signal line) OR transition into approved state (composite score at or above the approval threshold and below the high conviction threshold AND daily smoothed RSI not above its avoid level) OR transition into high conviction state (composite score at or above the high conviction threshold AND daily and weekly RSI above their respective signal lines AND relative volume at or above its high conviction threshold AND daily smoothed RSI not above its avoid level).
