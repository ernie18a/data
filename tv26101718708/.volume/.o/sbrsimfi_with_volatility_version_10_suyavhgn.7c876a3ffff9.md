Calculation: MFI = RSI of the rolling sum of volume × HLC3 on rising HLC3 bars and the rolling sum of volume × HLC3 on falling or unchanged HLC3 bars, using the same lookback
Calculation: Lower and upper Bollinger Bands = SMA of MFI over a lookback minus or plus a multiplier times its standard deviation over that same lookback
Long: MFI > lower Bollinger Band AND previous MFI ≤ previous lower Bollinger Band
Short: MFI < upper Bollinger Band AND previous MFI ≥ previous upper Bollinger Band
