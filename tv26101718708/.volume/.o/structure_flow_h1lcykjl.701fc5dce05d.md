Calculation: Yearly anchored VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by volume since the start of the current year
Calculation: ATR = average true range; used for the structure-break buffer, location tolerance, and market-activity filter
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after bars on both sides; retain the two latest of each
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close above the latest confirmed swing high plus an ATR-based buffer, with the latest swing high above the prior one; bearish structure break = close below the latest confirmed swing low minus the buffer, with the latest swing low below the prior one. Count only the first confirmed bar of each break condition and do not repeat a break for the same swing level.
Calculation: Trend stack = bullish when close and the 50-period EMA are above yearly anchored VWAP and the EMA is above VWAP; bearish when both are below VWAP and the EMA is below VWAP
Calculation: Normalized flow = EMA(estimated buying pressure minus estimated selling pressure, short lookback) / EMA(volume, same lookback), where buying pressure = (close − low) / (high − low) × volume and selling pressure = (high − close) / (high − low) × volume
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / longer-lookback SMA of volume
Calculation: Location tolerance = ATR-based distance; near a level means the bar range overlaps the level expanded by this tolerance; near a zone means the bar range overlaps the zone expanded by this tolerance
Calculation: Valid long location = near an unmitigated active bullish order-block zone OR latest confirmed swing high OR lower support band OR yearly anchored VWAP; valid short location = near an unmitigated active bearish order-block zone OR latest confirmed swing low OR upper resistance band OR yearly anchored VWAP. Order blocks are selected at a structure break from opposing candles in the preceding search window whose body is at least a configurable fraction of their range; select the candidate with the lowest score combining distance to break level, range, wick, body, and departure. Zones expire after a configurable bar count, may be hidden when too far from price, and are mitigated when price crosses their far edge by the configured wick or close rule.
Calculation: Lower support and upper resistance bands = smoothed trail bands around a trail derived from the 50-period EMA and yearly anchored VWAP, with width based on smoothed ATR and their gap; support is below the trail and resistance is above it
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = close versus 50-period EMA on the last completed bar of a configured timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Long: On a confirmed bar, bullish stack AND bullish structure break AND valid long location (unless location gating is disabled) AND ATR / close above the configured activity threshold AND (normalized flow above its positive threshold AND relative volume above its threshold AND higher-timeframe direction bullish (or filter disabled) OR B-tier enabled AND (normalized flow above 70% of its positive threshold AND relative volume above threshold OR normalized flow above its positive threshold AND relative volume below threshold) AND higher-timeframe direction bullish if required for B-tier). Require the configured cooldown since the prior long or short signal.
Short: On a confirmed bar, bearish stack AND bearish structure break AND valid short location (unless location gating is disabled) AND ATR / close above the configured activity threshold AND (normalized flow below its negative threshold AND relative volume above its threshold AND higher-timeframe direction bearish (or filter disabled) OR B-tier enabled AND (normalized flow below 70% of its negative threshold AND relative volume above threshold OR normalized flow below its negative threshold AND relative volume below threshold) AND higher-timeframe direction bearish if required for B-tier). Require the configured cooldown since the prior long or short signal.
