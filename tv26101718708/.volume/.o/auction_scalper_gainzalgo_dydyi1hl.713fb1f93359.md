Calculation: Previous-day value levels = build the completed prior day’s volume profile by dividing its high-low range into equal price bins, distributing each source bar’s volume equally across every bin touched by its low-high range; POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin, and VAH/VAL bound the contiguous bins around POC accumulated until they contain the configured fraction of total volume, adding the higher-volume adjacent bin first (ties upward).
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Buy/sell pressure ratio = [volume × (close − low)/(high − low) − volume × (high − close)/(high − low)] / volume; use zero if volume is zero.
Calculation: Volume percentile = current volume’s percentile rank among a longer lookback of volumes.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with its 20-period EMA calculated on that same completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long/short setup score = weighted sum of volume percentile, directional pressure score (clamp 50 ± pressure ratio × 250), rejection quality, value/break-level location, higher-timeframe alignment (100 aligned, 55 neutral, 15 opposed), ATR percentile score (below 25: percentile × 4; 25–90: 100; above 90: clamp 100 − (percentile − 90) × 6), first-target reward/risk score (clamp reward/risk × 40), and session-time score; respective weights are 0.15, 0.15, 0.20, 0.10, 0.10, 0.10, 0.10, 0.10. Clamp component scores to 0–100. Reversal rejection quality = clamp(0.65 × close-position score + 0.35 × clamp(20 + sweep depth in ATR × 60)); squeeze rejection quality = clamp(0.5 × close-position score + 0.5 × retest proximity score). For longs, close-position score is 100 × (close − low)/(high − low), sweep depth is (VAL − swept low)/ATR, location is clamp(100 − (close − VAL)/ATR × 35), and retest proximity is 100 × [1 − min(1, distance from low to broken level / retest-zone width)]. For shorts, close-position score is 100 × (high − close)/(high − low), sweep depth is (swept high − VAH)/ATR, location is clamp(100 − (VAH − close)/ATR × 35), and retest proximity is 100 × [1 − min(1, distance from high to broken level / retest-zone width)]. Squeeze location is clamp(100 − distance from close to broken level/ATR × 35). Session-time score is 60 if session filtering is off; otherwise 40 before session open, 100 through the first two hours, 70 through four hours, then 45.
Long: confirmed intraday bar AND inside configured session and after opening lockout and before late-entry cutoff AND no active trade AND cooldown elapsed AND no prior long setup that day (if enabled) AND close > open AND (participation gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR (volume percentile ≥ configured threshold AND pressure ratio > 0.02)) AND [((low < VAL AND close > VAL AND no preceding close below VAL) OR (close crosses up through VAL AND preceding consecutive closes below VAL are between 1 and configured maximum)) OR (accepted upward break AND retest holds)] AND (not chop OR reversal setup) AND score ≥ configured minimum + (15 if chop else 0) + configured countertrend penalty if reversal and higher-timeframe bias is bearish AND stop distance ≤ configured maximum ATR multiple. An accepted upward break is a bullish candle closing above VAH with body/range ≥ configured fraction; its retest must occur after the break, within the configured bar window, have low ≤ broken VAH + configured ATR tolerance, and close above VAH.
Short: confirmed intraday bar AND inside configured session and after opening lockout and before late-entry cutoff AND no active trade AND cooldown elapsed AND no prior short setup that day (if enabled) AND close < open AND (participation gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR (volume percentile ≥ configured threshold AND pressure ratio < −0.02)) AND [((high > VAH AND close < VAH AND no preceding close above VAH) OR (close crosses down through VAH AND preceding consecutive closes above VAH are between 1 and configured maximum)) OR (accepted downward break AND retest holds)] AND (not chop OR reversal setup) AND score ≥ configured minimum + (15 if chop else 0) + configured countertrend penalty if reversal and higher-timeframe bias is bullish AND stop distance ≤ configured maximum ATR multiple. An accepted downward break is a bearish candle closing below VAL with body/range ≥ configured fraction; its retest must occur after the break, within the configured bar window, have high ≥ broken VAL − configured ATR tolerance, and close below VAL.
Exit: long, low ≤ initial stop price OR (TP1 was reached on an earlier bar AND high ≥ TP2)
Exit: short, high ≥ initial stop price OR (TP1 was reached on an earlier bar AND low ≤ TP2)
