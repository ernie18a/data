Calculation: Bull score = sum of these six conditions: close > VWAP; fast EMA > mid EMA; bullish sweep within the recent-event lookback; bullish BOS within the recent-event lookback; volume ratio >= configured spike threshold; close > upper value-area band.
Calculation: Bear score = sum of these six conditions: close < VWAP; fast EMA < mid EMA; bearish sweep within the recent-event lookback; bearish BOS within the recent-event lookback; volume ratio >= configured spike threshold; close < lower value-area band.
Calculation: Flow meter = 100 × (bull score − bear score) / (bull score + bear score); use 0 when both scores are 0.
Calculation: Trend strength = min(ADX / 40, 1) × 40 + EMA component + VWAP component + volume component; EMA component is 30 for a full fast/mid/slow EMA stack in either direction, 15 when fast and mid EMAs differ without a full stack, otherwise 0; VWAP component is min(abs(close − VWAP) / ATR, 1) × 20; volume component is 10 when volume ratio >= configured spike threshold, 5 when volume ratio > 1, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / its simple moving average over the configured lookback; use 0 if the average is 0.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low AND volume ratio >= configured spike threshold.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high AND volume ratio >= configured spike threshold.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = close crosses above latest confirmed swing high AND latest swing high and low are each higher than their prior confirmed values.
Calculation: Bearish BOS = close crosses below latest confirmed swing low AND latest swing high and low are each lower than their prior confirmed values.
Calculation: Bullish CHoCH = close crosses above latest confirmed swing high AND latest swing high and low are each lower than their prior confirmed values.
Calculation: Bearish CHoCH = close crosses below latest confirmed swing low AND latest swing high and low are each higher than their prior confirmed values.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = latest confirmed swing low < prior confirmed swing low AND RSI at the latest swing low > RSI at the prior swing low.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = latest confirmed swing high > prior confirmed swing high AND RSI at the latest swing high < RSI at the prior swing high.
