Calculation: Confirmed liquidity swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is the highest/lowest in a centered window with the liquidity lookback bars on each side; available only after the right-side bars close. Use a longer lookback than for structure swings.
Calculation: Confirmed structure swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is the highest/lowest in a centered window with the structure lookback bars on each side; available only after the right-side bars close.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = current close − current open ≥ current ATR, OR current low > the high two bars earlier; ATR uses a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = current open − current close ≥ current ATR, OR current high < the low two bars earlier; ATR uses the same lookback as bullish displacement.
Calculation: Breaker zone = the open-to-close range of the first candle in the six-bar scan from the broken structure swing bar backward that closed in the shift direction; if none does, use the broken swing bar’s open-to-close range.
Long: A confirmed liquidity swing low is swept (low < swing low), then price closes above the latest applicable confirmed structure swing high with bullish displacement; on a later bar, an active resulting zone has not been invalidated (close ≥ zone bottom), has not previously been touched, and current low ≤ zone top.
Short: A confirmed liquidity swing high is swept (high > swing high), then price closes below the latest applicable confirmed structure swing low with bearish displacement; on a later bar, an active resulting zone has not been invalidated (close ≤ zone top), has not previously been touched, and current high ≥ zone bottom.
