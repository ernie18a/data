Calculation: Volume oscillator = either OBV, the cumulative sum of sign(close change) × volume, or volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low); use 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low extremum over configured left and right bars, available after the right bars; use the same pivot lengths for price and the volume oscillator.
Long: (two consecutive confirmed price pivot lows no more than the configured maximum lookback apart, current low < previous low, and volume oscillator at current pivot > volume oscillator at previous pivot) OR (two consecutive confirmed volume oscillator pivot lows no more than the configured maximum lookback apart, current low at oscillator pivot < previous low at oscillator pivot, and current oscillator pivot > previous oscillator pivot), AND for either method the absolute price change as a percentage of the absolute previous price is at least the configured minimum price change (or that minimum is 0), and the absolute oscillator change as a percentage of the absolute previous oscillator is at least the configured minimum oscillator change (or that minimum is 0; if previous oscillator is 0, require current oscillator != previous oscillator).
