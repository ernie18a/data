Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot with equal configurable numbers of bars on both sides; use the latest unbroken pivot of each type. A pivot becomes available only after right-side confirmation.
Calculation: Structure break = on a completed bar, price breaks the latest unbroken swing high/low, using close by default or high/low if wick breaks are selected. If both sides break, an up break requires close ≥ open and a down break requires close < open. A break opposite the prior direction is a CHoCH; a break in the established direction is a BOS.
Calculation: Rejected-block zone = after a CHoCH, a confirmed pivot low for bullish direction or pivot high for bearish direction, formed after the CHoCH, whose relevant wick is at least the configured share of the candle range and configured ATR multiple. Bullish zone spans pivot low to the lower body edge; bearish zone spans the upper body edge to pivot high. ATR uses chart bars.
Calculation: Fair-value gap = bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier and the intervening bar closes above its low plus the configured ATR(200) threshold; bearish when the low two bars earlier exceeds the current high and the intervening bar closes below its high minus that threshold. Use the resulting gap bounds. A gap is mitigated at its far edge using the selected close, wick, or midpoint rule.
Calculation: Final directional gap = the latest gap in the active CHoCH direction associated with a BOS within the configured bar window, after a rejected-block retest.
Calculation: Fibonacci entry zone = the 0.382–0.618 retracement of the distance from the pre-break opposite swing to the CHoCH candle extreme. It becomes touched when a later bar overlaps it.
Calculation: Validated liquidity trend line = line from the prior regime extreme (low for bullish CHoCH, high for bearish CHoCH) to the first later confirmed pivot in the same direction (higher low or lower high).
Long: Bullish CHoCH OR bullish BOS OR bullish rejected block forms OR price retests a valid bullish rejected block OR bullish BOS and bullish fair-value gap associate within the configured window after that retest OR price first touches the valid final bullish gap before its mitigation OR price closes above the bullish Fibonacci entry zone after touching it OR price breaks above a validated bearish liquidity trend line.
Short: Bearish CHoCH OR bearish BOS OR bearish rejected block forms OR price retests a valid bearish rejected block OR bearish BOS and bearish fair-value gap associate within the configured window after that retest OR price first touches the valid final bearish gap before its mitigation OR price closes below the bearish Fibonacci entry zone after touching it OR price breaks below a validated bullish liquidity trend line.
