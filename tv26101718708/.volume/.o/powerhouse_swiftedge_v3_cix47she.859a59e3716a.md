Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the selected structure length of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed, unbroken level.
Calculation: Momentum = (current close − close N bars ago) / ATR; require it to be at least the selected threshold in the signal direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = close versus EMA on the selected higher timeframe, using the last completed bar; bullish above EMA, bearish below EMA.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe trend = close versus EMA on the selected lower timeframe, using the bar still forming; bullish above EMA, bearish below EMA.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / 20-bar average volume; require it to be at least the selected threshold.
Calculation: Breakout = close above the highest high, or below the lowest low, of the selected lookback’s preceding bars.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative estimated buy volume minus sell volume from lower-timeframe candles (buy volume when lower-timeframe close ≥ open, otherwise sell volume); require its change over the selected lookback to point in the signal direction.
Calculation: Adaptive trend score = EMA of a score clipped to [−1, 1], formed from 0.6 × (adaptive moving average change over 5 bars / ATR) + 0.4 × ((close − adaptive moving average) / (2 × ATR)); the adaptive moving average uses Kaufman efficiency ratio with a shorter adaptive length and a longer slow length.
Calculation: Stop price = entry price minus position direction × risk; risk is either selected ATR multiple × ATR, or the distance from entry to the opposite latest swing plus 0.1 × ATR, clamped between selected minimum and maximum ATR distances.
Calculation: Target price = entry price + position direction × target distance; target distance is the selected target multiple × risk, selected target multiple × ATR, or selected points, according to target mode.
Long: (close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND break kind is allowed by signal mode AND (each enabled filter agrees: momentum is positive by at least its threshold; higher-timeframe close > EMA; lower-timeframe close > EMA; volume ratio ≥ threshold; breakout is above the prior lookback high; cumulative volume delta change > 0; adaptive trend score > −0.2) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in selected session) AND (one-signal-per-direction disabled OR last signal direction is not long)) OR (close < latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND close ≥ swing high − selected ATR distance AND the same enabled filters except momentum and breakout agree AND session condition holds AND one-signal-per-direction condition holds).
Short: (close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND break kind is allowed by signal mode AND (each enabled filter agrees: momentum is negative by at least its threshold; higher-timeframe close < EMA; lower-timeframe close < EMA; volume ratio ≥ threshold; breakout is below the prior lookback low; cumulative volume delta change < 0; adaptive trend score < 0.2) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in selected session) AND (one-signal-per-direction disabled OR last signal direction is not short)) OR (close > latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND close ≤ swing low + selected ATR distance AND the same enabled filters except momentum and breakout agree AND session condition holds AND one-signal-per-direction condition holds).
Exit: Long position, low ≤ stop price OR (high ≥ every enabled target price) OR a valid short signal occurs.
Exit: Short position, high ≥ stop price OR (low ≤ every enabled target price) OR a valid long signal occurs.
