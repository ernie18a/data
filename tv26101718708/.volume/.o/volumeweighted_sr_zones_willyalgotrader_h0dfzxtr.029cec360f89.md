Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low exceeds the highs/lows of the N bars on each side; use it after the N right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: ATR = chart-timeframe average true range over a configurable lookback, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Volume average = average volume over a configurable lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Resistance/support zone = confirmed pivot high/low ± 0.15 × ATR at confirmation; merge same-type zones when pivot prices are within the configured ATR merge distance, expanding the zone to include both. Track whether each zone is broken and the bar it broke.
Calculation: Breakout stop distance = ATR × configured stop multiplier; with zone-aware stops, long stop = max(entry price − stop distance, broken zone bottom − 0.2 × ATR), short stop = min(entry price + stop distance, broken zone top + 0.2 × ATR). Without zone-aware stops, use entry price ± stop distance.
Calculation: Take-profit 3 = entry price ± stop distance × configured third-target risk multiple, adding for long and subtracting for short.
Long: flat AND (an unbroken resistance zone has close > zone top AND previous close ≤ zone top AND enabled break-volume/momentum filters pass) OR (after a bullish break, within the configured retest bar window, low ≤ broken zone top AND close > broken zone top AND close − low ≥ ATR × configured minimum reaction AND enabled first-retest filter passes)
Short: flat AND (an unbroken support zone has close < zone bottom AND previous close ≥ zone bottom AND enabled break-volume/momentum filters pass) OR (after a bearish break, within the configured retest bar window, high ≥ broken zone bottom AND close < broken zone bottom AND high − close ≥ ATR × configured minimum reaction AND enabled first-retest filter passes)
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ take-profit 3; if both occur on one bar, exit at the stop. After take-profit 1 is touched without a stop touch on that bar, move the active stop to entry price when break-even trailing is enabled.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ take-profit 3; if both occur on one bar, exit at the stop. After take-profit 1 is touched without a stop touch on that bar, move the active stop to entry price when break-even trailing is enabled.
