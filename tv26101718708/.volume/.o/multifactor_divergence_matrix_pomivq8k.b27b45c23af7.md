Calculation: Composite = mean of the active rolling z-scores of RSI, KST, two-pole-smoothed up/down strength, fractionally differenced log price, signed efficiency ratio, signed SAR distance in ATR units, signed fast-minus-slow efficiency ratio, price-minus-session-VWAP, linear-regression deviation in ATR units, signed high-low range, normalized cumulative signed-volume residual, and MFI. Use one normalization lookback for all lenses; fast efficiency lookback is shorter than slow. Exclude lenses requiring external symbols.
Calculation: Family value = mean of its active aspects: Momentum (oscillatory RSI/KST aspect, low-lag strength, fractional difference); Trend/Efficiency (efficiency ratio, SAR distance, efficiency gap); Location/Mean (VWAP deviation, regression deviation); Volatility (directional range); Flow/Volume (cumulative signed-volume residual, MFI). A family is split when at least one aspect is at or above the positive lean threshold and at least one is at or below its negative. A family is stretched when any aspect is at or beyond the corresponding extreme threshold.
Calculation: Extreme threshold = specified multiple of the composite's rolling standard deviation; OB/OS threshold = specified multiple of that standard deviation.
Calculation: Price slope and composite slope = current minus prior-offset linear-regression values over the same lookback.
Calculation: Divergence pivot values = price high/low pivots and the corresponding composite rolling high/low over a window based on the pivot lookback; compare each with the prior same-side pivot within the maximum pivot gap.
Long: At a confirmed price low pivot, composite pivot is higher than the prior composite low pivot by at least the minimum composite gap, price makes a lower low, and composite pivot is at or below the negative OB/OS threshold; OR price low is within the equal-extreme ATR tolerance of the prior low, with the same composite and threshold conditions; OR price makes a higher low while composite pivot makes a lower low by at least the minimum composite gap; OR price slope is negative and composite slope positive; OR rolling price/composite correlation crosses below its threshold while price slope is negative; OR RSI has declined on each of the last three bars and its value three bars ago was below the oversold level while four bars ago it was above; OR at least one family is split and composite is at or below the negative OB/OS threshold; OR at least the required number of families have an aspect at or below the negative extreme threshold; OR, at a confirmed composite pivot low, composite is at or below the negative composite extreme threshold; OR at a lower or equal price low pivot, more than the required number of family values are higher than at the prior price low pivot.
Short: At a confirmed price high pivot, composite pivot is lower than the prior composite high pivot by at least the minimum composite gap, price makes a higher high, and composite pivot is at or above the positive OB/OS threshold; OR price high is within the equal-extreme ATR tolerance of the prior high, with the same composite and threshold conditions; OR price makes a lower high while composite pivot makes a higher high by at least the minimum composite gap; OR price slope is positive and composite slope negative; OR rolling price/composite correlation crosses below its threshold while price slope is positive; OR RSI has risen on each of the last three bars and its value three bars ago was above the overbought level while four bars ago it was below; OR at least one family is split and composite is at or above the positive OB/OS threshold; OR at least the required number of families have an aspect at or above the positive extreme threshold; OR, at a confirmed composite pivot high, composite is at or above the positive composite extreme threshold; OR at a higher or equal price high pivot, more than the required number of family values are lower than at the prior price high pivot.
