Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume, adding volume when close is above the previous close and subtracting it when close is below the previous close
Calculation: Upper band = OBV + multiplier × standard deviation of OBV over its lookback
Calculation: Lower band = OBV − multiplier × standard deviation of OBV over its lookback
Long: OBV > previous completed bar's upper band AND OBV > its simple moving average
Short: OBV < previous completed bar's lower band AND OBV < its simple moving average
