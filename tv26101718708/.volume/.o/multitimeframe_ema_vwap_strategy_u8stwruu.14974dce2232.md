Calculation: Short-term EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA.
Calculation: Long-term EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the short-term EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of close on higher-timeframe bars; use the last completed bar historically and the still-forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Psychological zone = close modulo the psychological-level interval is at most the zone width or at least the interval minus the zone width.
Long: Short-term EMA crosses above long-term EMA AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND close > daily VWAP AND close is outside all psychological zones.
Short: Short-term EMA crosses below long-term EMA AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND close < daily VWAP AND close is outside all psychological zones.
