Calculation: Higher-timeframe close = close of the latest completed higher-timeframe bar available at the chart-bar evaluation.
Calculation: Medium-timeframe close = close of the chart-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe close = close of the latest completed lower-timeframe bar available within the chart bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe SMA = SMA of the higher-timeframe close series, with a longer lookback than the medium- and lower-timeframe SMAs.
Calculation: Medium-timeframe SMA = SMA of the chart-timeframe close series, with a shorter lookback than the higher-timeframe SMA and a longer lookback than the lower-timeframe SMA.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe SMA = SMA of the lower-timeframe close series.
Calculation: RMI = average of (1) RSI computed from smoothed positive and negative close changes and (2) MFI computed from close and volume.
Calculation: TWAP = SMA of (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: TEMA = 3 × EMA(close) − 3 × EMA(EMA(close)) + EMA(EMA(EMA(close))).
Calculation: DEMA = 2 × EMA(close) − EMA(EMA(close)).
Long: At least one enabled London (0800–1700 GMT+1), New York (0800–1700 GMT−5), or Tokyo (0900–1800 GMT+9) session is active AND trade direction is Long or Both AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe SMA AND medium-timeframe close crosses above medium-timeframe SMA AND (ADX filtering is disabled OR ADX > its threshold) AND ((selected indicator is RMI AND RMI > its overbought level) OR (selected indicator is TWAP AND close > TWAP) OR (selected indicator is TEMA AND close > TEMA) OR (selected indicator is DEMA AND close > DEMA) OR (selected indicator is MA AND close > SMA(close)) OR (selected indicator is MFI AND MFI > its overbought level) OR (selected indicator is VWMA AND close > VWMA(close, volume)) OR (selected indicator is PSAR AND close > PSAR).
Short: At least one enabled London (0800–1700 GMT+1), New York (0800–1700 GMT−5), or Tokyo (0900–1800 GMT+9) session is active AND trade direction is Short or Both AND higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe SMA AND medium-timeframe close crosses below medium-timeframe SMA AND (ADX filtering is disabled OR ADX > its threshold) AND ((selected indicator is RMI AND RMI < its oversold level) OR (selected indicator is TWAP AND close < TWAP) OR (selected indicator is TEMA AND close < TEMA) OR (selected indicator is DEMA AND close < DEMA) OR (selected indicator is MA AND close < SMA(close)) OR (selected indicator is MFI AND MFI < its oversold level) OR (selected indicator is VWMA AND close < VWMA(close, volume)) OR (selected indicator is PSAR AND close < PSAR).
Exit: Long, at least one enabled London (0800–1700 GMT+1), New York (0800–1700 GMT−5), or Tokyo (0900–1800 GMT+9) session is active AND trade direction is Long or Both AND lower-timeframe close crosses below lower-timeframe SMA.
Exit: Short, at least one enabled London (0800–1700 GMT+1), New York (0800–1700 GMT−5), or Tokyo (0900–1800 GMT+9) session is active AND trade direction is Short or Both AND lower-timeframe close crosses above lower-timeframe SMA.
