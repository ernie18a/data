Calculation: Trend score = 0.4 × EMA alignment score + 0.3 × price-versus-EMA score + 0.3 × ADX directional score; EMA periods are fast < medium < slow. EMA alignment score is +100 when fast > medium > slow, −100 when fast < medium < slow, otherwise add +33 for each ascending adjacent pair, −33 for each descending pair, and 0 for each equal pair. Price-versus-EMA score is +100 when close is above all three EMAs, −100 when below all three, otherwise +50 when above the medium EMA, −50 when below it, and 0 when equal. ADX directional score = min(100, ADX / strong-ADX threshold × 50) × (+1 if +DI > −DI, otherwise −1).
Calculation: Momentum score = 0.35 × (2 × (RSI − 50)) + 0.4 × MACD score + 0.25 × (2 × (stochastic %K − 50)). MACD fast lookback < slow lookback. MACD score = clamp(20 × MACD histogram / close × 10000, −100, 100) × direction factor, where direction factor is +1 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, −1 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0, otherwise +0.5 when histogram > 0 or −0.5 when histogram ≤ 0. Stochastic %D is the moving average of %K.
Calculation: Volatility score = +30 when Bollinger Band width is below the 20th percentile of its lookback range; otherwise −20 when ATR percentage is above the 80th percentile of its lookback range, +10 when above the 40th percentile, or +20 otherwise. ATR percentage = ATR / close × 100; Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / basis × 100.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(0.35 × OBV score + 0.3 × (200 × CMF) + 0.35 × (2 × (MFI − 50)), −100, 100), where OBV score is +50 when OBV > its 20-period EMA and −50 otherwise.
Calculation: Weighted composite = weighted average of enabled trend, momentum, volatility, and volume scores using their respective weights; disabled modules contribute zero and are excluded from the weight total.
Calculation: Signal probability = max(bull probability, bear probability) × (0.7 × alignment / 100 + 0.3), where bull probability = clamp(50 + weighted composite / 2, 0, 100), bear probability = 100 − bull probability, and alignment = max(0, 100 − population standard deviation of enabled trend, momentum, and volume scores).
Long: weighted composite crosses above 0 AND signal probability ≥ minimum signal probability.
Short: weighted composite crosses below 0 AND signal probability ≥ minimum signal probability.
