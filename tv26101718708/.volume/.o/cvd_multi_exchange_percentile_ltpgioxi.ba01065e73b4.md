Calculation: Daily CVD = sum, across the four exchanges, of each intrabar’s estimated delta, accumulated from zero within the current UTC day; for each intrabar, delta = volume × ((close − low) / (high − low)) for an up or unchanged candle, and volume × (((close − low) / (high − low)) − 1) for a down candle (delta is zero when high = low).
Calculation: Bull-session peak percentile = percentage of stored positive peaks from completed sessions in the rolling lookback that are less than or equal to the current session’s highest Daily CVD; available only with at least five stored peaks.
Calculation: Bear-session peak percentile = percentage of stored negative peaks from completed sessions in the rolling lookback that are greater than or equal to the current session’s lowest Daily CVD; available only with at least five stored peaks.
Long: the lowest close in the current swing-length window is below the lowest close in the immediately preceding, non-overlapping swing-length window AND the lowest Daily CVD in the current window is above the lowest Daily CVD in the preceding window AND Bull-session peak percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND close > the simple moving average of close over the trend-filter lookback AND the divergence conditions were false on the previous bar OR close < previous close AND Daily CVD > previous Daily CVD AND volume > its simple moving average over the absorption-volume lookback × absorption-volume multiplier AND Bull-session peak percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND the absorption conditions were false on the previous bar.
Short: the highest close in the current swing-length window is above the highest close in the immediately preceding, non-overlapping swing-length window AND the highest Daily CVD in the current window is below the highest Daily CVD in the preceding window AND Bear-session peak percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND close < the simple moving average of close over the trend-filter lookback AND the divergence conditions were false on the previous bar OR close > previous close AND Daily CVD < previous Daily CVD AND volume > its simple moving average over the absorption-volume lookback × absorption-volume multiplier AND Bear-session peak percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND the absorption conditions were false on the previous bar.
