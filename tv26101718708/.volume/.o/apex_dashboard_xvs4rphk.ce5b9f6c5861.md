Calculation: Regression slope = linear regression of close over a lookback at the current bar minus its value one bar earlier.
Calculation: Chandelier direction = bullish when close exceeds the prior short trail, bearish when close falls below the prior long trail, otherwise retain the prior direction. Long trail uses highest high over its lookback minus its ATR multiple; short trail uses lowest low plus its ATR multiple. Each trail resets to its candidate when the prior close crosses its prior trail; otherwise the long trail is the greater of its candidate and prior trail, and the short trail is the lesser.
Calculation: Volume delta slope = the change in the rolling sum of signed volume (positive when close ≥ open, negative otherwise) over a shorter bar lag.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over a lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low below a level, close above it, close above open, and (level − low) / max(high − low, 1e-10) ≥ minimum wick ratio.
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = high above a level, close below it, close below open, and (high − level) / max(high − low, 1e-10) ≥ minimum wick ratio.
Calculation: Major pivots use a longer symmetric left/right lookback than minor pivots; use the latest confirmed pivot high or low.
Calculation: Tier-1 bullish sweep = bullish liquidity sweep of the previous completed daily low or latest confirmed major pivot low. Tier-1 bearish sweep = bearish liquidity sweep of the previous completed daily high or latest confirmed major pivot high.
Calculation: Any bullish sweep = Tier-1 bullish sweep or bullish liquidity sweep of the latest confirmed minor pivot low. Any bearish sweep = Tier-1 bearish sweep or bearish liquidity sweep of the latest confirmed minor pivot high.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = bullish when that timeframe's close > EMA(20) > EMA(50), bearish when close < EMA(20) < EMA(50), otherwise neutral. Use the currently forming bars on the daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, 15-minute, and 5-minute timeframes; bullish/ bearish scores count the bullish/bearish timeframe biases.
Calculation: Trending = Choppiness Index ≤ its threshold; choppy = Choppiness Index > its threshold.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open and close − open > ATR(14) × displacement multiplier. Bearish displacement = close < open and open − close > ATR(14) × displacement multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish macro sweep = low ≤ previous completed daily low or latest confirmed major pivot low. Bearish macro sweep = high ≥ previous completed daily high or latest confirmed major pivot high.
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish macro sweep = a bullish/bearish macro sweep occurred within the last 8 bars.
Calculation: Bullish runaway = bullish timeframe score ≥ 4, bullish Chandelier direction, trending, bullish displacement, and (prior close < prior open or low ≤ prior low). Bearish runaway = bearish timeframe score ≥ 4, bearish Chandelier direction, trending, bearish displacement, and (prior close > prior open or high ≥ prior high).
Long: Tier-1 bullish sweep AND Regression slope > 0 AND bullish Chandelier direction AND Volume delta slope > 0 AND (trending OR bullish displacement) AND London/New York session active when the session gate is enabled AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last long signal (reset by a short signal).
Long: (Any bullish sweep OR bullish runaway) AND Regression slope > 0 AND bullish Chandelier direction AND (trending OR bullish displacement) AND London/New York session active when the session gate is enabled AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last long signal (reset by a short signal) AND NOT the Tier-1 long condition.
Long: Any bullish sweep AND (Regression slope > 0 OR bullish Chandelier direction) AND trending AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last long signal (reset by a short signal) AND NOT the Tier-1 or regular long condition.
Long: Recent bullish macro sweep AND close > open AND Regression slope > 0 AND (bullish Chandelier direction OR bullish displacement) AND more than 20 bars since the prior swing long.
Short: Tier-1 bearish sweep AND Regression slope < 0 AND bearish Chandelier direction AND Volume delta slope < 0 AND (trending OR bearish displacement) AND London/New York session active when the session gate is enabled AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last short signal (reset by a long signal).
Short: (Any bearish sweep OR bearish runaway) AND Regression slope < 0 AND bearish Chandelier direction AND (trending OR bearish displacement) AND London/New York session active when the session gate is enabled AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last short signal (reset by a long signal) AND NOT the Tier-1 short condition.
Short: Any bearish sweep AND (Regression slope < 0 OR bearish Chandelier direction) AND trending AND more than the signal-gap bars since the last short signal (reset by a long signal) AND NOT the Tier-1 or regular short condition.
Short: Recent bearish macro sweep AND close < open AND Regression slope < 0 AND (bearish Chandelier direction OR bearish displacement) AND more than 20 bars since the prior swing short.
