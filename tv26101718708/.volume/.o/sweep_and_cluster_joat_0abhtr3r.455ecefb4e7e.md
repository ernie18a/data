Calculation: Eligible pivot high/low = most recent confirmed minor pivot high/low among the last pivot-scan-depth confirmed minor pivots, at least the minimum pivot age; a minor pivot uses equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; candle range = high − low.
Long: (low < eligible pivot low AND close > eligible pivot low AND lower wick ≥ ATR(14) × wick-ATR-multiplier AND lower wick / max(candle range, 0.0001) ≥ wick-range ratio AND bars since last bullish sweep > sweep cooldown AND (overlap allowed OR no unresolved bullish sweep)) OR (within the cluster window after a bullish sweep, at least the minimum number of subsequent bars close ≥ open AND sum of their bodies / sum of their ranges ≥ minimum body ratio AND cumulative volume since the sweep / (volume average captured at sweep × elapsed bars) ≥ minimum cumulative-volume multiplier)
Short: (high > eligible pivot high AND close < eligible pivot high AND upper wick ≥ ATR(14) × wick-ATR-multiplier AND upper wick / max(candle range, 0.0001) ≥ wick-range ratio AND bars since last bearish sweep > sweep cooldown AND (overlap allowed OR no unresolved bearish sweep)) OR (within the cluster window after a bearish sweep, at least the minimum number of subsequent bars close < open AND sum of their bodies / sum of their ranges ≥ minimum body ratio AND cumulative volume since the sweep / (volume average captured at sweep × elapsed bars) ≥ minimum cumulative-volume multiplier)
