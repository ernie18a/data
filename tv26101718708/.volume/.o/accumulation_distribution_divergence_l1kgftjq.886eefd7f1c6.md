Calculation: A/D oscillator = 100 × tanh(z/3), where z is the robust z-score of the A/D line minus its EMA; the A/D line is the cumulative sum of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), using 0 for the fraction when high = low. The robust z-score subtracts the rolling median and divides by 1.4826 × the rolling median absolute deviation.
Long: A confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND the oscillator at the new pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot, OR the confirmed pivot low is higher AND the oscillator at the new pivot is lower; the two pivots are no farther apart than the maximum pivot separation.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the oscillator at the new pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot, OR the confirmed pivot high is lower AND the oscillator at the new pivot is higher; the two pivots are no farther apart than the maximum pivot separation.
