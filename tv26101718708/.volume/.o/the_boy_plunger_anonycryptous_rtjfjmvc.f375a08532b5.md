Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = the selected moving average of close using a shorter and a longer lookback, respectively; the ribbon trend is bullish when fast > slow and bearish when fast < slow. For the intraday preset use HMA; for the Livermore preset use EMA.
Calculation: Nearest higher-timeframe pivot level = the price of the closest, by absolute distance from close, among the four most recent confirmed pivot highs and four most recent confirmed pivot lows on the selected higher timeframe; pivots use equal left and right lookbacks. Use higher-timeframe values without lookahead.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume > the moving average of volume over its lookback × the spike multiplier.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Active fair value gap = a bullish gap formed when low > high two bars earlier and the middle close > that earlier high, or a bearish gap formed when high < low two bars earlier and the middle close < that earlier low; gap size must exceed the configured fraction of ATR(144). A gap remains active until price fully fills it.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at confirmed price/RSI pivot lows, price makes a lower low and RSI(14) a higher low than at the prior paired pivot, within the configured maximum bar distance. Bearish divergence = at confirmed price/RSI pivot highs, price makes a higher high and RSI(14) a lower high within that distance.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe reversal = on each of three selected higher timeframes, either EMA(9) crosses EMA(21) against the current ribbon trend, or RSI(14) crosses back below 70 or above 30; use the higher-timeframe values without lookahead.
Calculation: Trade risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. Initial stop is the ATR(14)-based stop, the lowest low (long) or highest high (short) over the swing lookback, or the farther of those two stops, according to the selected stop mode. Targets are entry price plus/minus trade risk × each configured R multiple, in the position direction.
Long: A bullish candle (close >= open) closes above the nearest higher-timeframe pivot level AND the selected preset gates pass: Livermore requires level proximity, volume spike, and body ratio threshold; Boy Plunger requires bullish ribbon trend, level proximity, volume spike, and body threshold; Ribbon requires bullish ribbon trend (level proximity only if enabled); Sensitive requires level proximity and volume spike; Balanced requires bullish ribbon trend, level proximity, and volume spike; FVG Entry requires Balanced gates and close inside an active bullish fair value gap; Custom requires each enabled trend, level, volume, and body gate. Level proximity means distance to the nearest level <= ATR(14) × configured distance. Also require cooldown satisfied, close within configured ATR distance of the fast moving average if that filter is enabled, and (if pullback is enabled) an unexpired bullish breakout setup with close between slow moving average − 0.5 ATR and fast moving average + 0.5 ATR. For the pullback setup, a prior bullish candle must have closed above the nearest level while level proximity and volume spike held.
Long: Fast moving average crosses above slow moving average.
Long: At least two of the three higher timeframes have a bullish reversal.
Long: Bullish divergence.
Short: A bearish candle (close < open) closes below the nearest higher-timeframe pivot level AND the selected preset gates pass: Livermore requires level proximity, volume spike, and body ratio threshold; Boy Plunger requires bearish ribbon trend, level proximity, volume spike, and body threshold; Ribbon requires bearish ribbon trend (level proximity only if enabled); Sensitive requires level proximity and volume spike; Balanced requires bearish ribbon trend, level proximity, and volume spike; FVG Entry requires Balanced gates and close inside an active bearish fair value gap; Custom requires each enabled trend, level, volume, and body gate. Level proximity means distance to the nearest level <= ATR(14) × configured distance. Also require cooldown satisfied, close within configured ATR distance of the fast moving average if that filter is enabled, and (if pullback is enabled) an unexpired bearish breakout setup with close between fast moving average − 0.5 ATR and slow moving average + 0.5 ATR. For the pullback setup, a prior bearish candle must have closed below the nearest level while level proximity and volume spike held.
Short: Fast moving average crosses below slow moving average.
Short: At least two of the three higher timeframes have a bearish reversal.
Short: Bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, within the configured bars after a long entry, close < that entry's pivot level; OR after the entry bar, low <= active stop; OR high >= the configured third target. The active stop is initial stop until trailing applies; after target 1 it moves to breakeven, and after target 2 it trails at the fast moving average − ATR(14) × configured buffer. If target 3 and stop are both touched in one bar, target 3 takes precedence.
Exit: Short, within the configured bars after a short entry, close > that entry's pivot level; OR after the entry bar, high >= active stop; OR low <= the configured third target. The active stop is initial stop until trailing applies; after target 1 it moves to breakeven, and after target 2 it trails at the fast moving average + ATR(14) × configured buffer. If target 3 and stop are both touched in one bar, target 3 takes precedence.
