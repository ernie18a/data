Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar’s high that is the highest within the configured bars before and after it
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar’s low that is the lowest within the configured bars before and after it
Calculation: Upper trendline = the straight line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs, extended forward
Calculation: Lower trendline = the straight line through the two most recent confirmed pivot lows, extended forward
Long: Close crosses above the upper trendline AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND volume > its SMA
Short: Close crosses below the lower trendline AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND volume > its SMA
