Calculation: ATR = average true range of recent bars; short-period ATR is used to scale price distances and normalize slopes.
Calculation: HMA = Hull moving average of close; use progressively longer lookbacks for short-, medium-, and long-term HMAs.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average of volume over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Return(n) = close / close n bars earlier − 1; use short, medium, and long lookbacks.
Calculation: Range position(n) = (close − lowest low over n bars) / (highest high over n bars − lowest low over n bars); use short, medium, and long lookbacks, with extrema taken from bars before the current bar where the strategy does so.
Calculation: Candle close position = (close − low) / (high − low); body and wick proportions are measured against the current bar’s high−low range.
Calculation: Efficiency(n) = absolute close change over n bars / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over n bars; use shorter and longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Normalized HMA slope = change in an HMA over a lookback, divided by short-period ATR; use different lookbacks for each HMA.
Calculation: HMA acceleration = change in normalized HMA slope between successive lookback intervals.
Calculation: Distance from HMA = (close − HMA) / short-period ATR.
Calculation: Macro bull = close above long HMA, medium HMA above long HMA, long HMA above the longest HMA, and long-HMA slope above its negative tolerance. Macro bear is the inverse ordering with long-HMA slope below its positive tolerance.
Calculation: Uptrend = short HMAs ordered above successively longer HMAs, medium HMAs ordered upward, and medium-HMA slope above its positive threshold. Downtrend is the inverse ordering and slope condition.
Calculation: Soft uptrend = short HMA above medium HMA, medium HMA above a longer HMA, close above long HMA, and long-HMA slope above its negative tolerance.
Calculation: Not-dead-chop = short-lookback efficiency exceeds its minimum OR longer-lookback efficiency exceeds its minimum OR short-lookback range divided by ATR exceeds its wide-range threshold.
Calculation: Volatility-valid = short-period ATR / close exceeds its minimum and ATR is positive.
