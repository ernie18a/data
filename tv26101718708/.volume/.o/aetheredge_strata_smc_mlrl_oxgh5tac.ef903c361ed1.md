Calculation: Bullish structure = close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish structure = close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low. A break is BOS if it continues the prior direction, otherwise CHoCH.
Calculation: Bullish order-block pressure = +1 for each active bullish block within one ATR of close; bearish blocks count −1; sum and clamp to [−1,1]. A block is the last opposite-color candle within the origin search window at a structure break, and is active until close passes its far edge.
Calculation: Bullish FVG pressure = +1 for each unfilled bullish three-bar gap within one ATR of close; bearish gaps count −1; sum and clamp to [−1,1]. A bullish gap has current low above the high two bars earlier; a bearish gap has current high below the low two bars earlier. Remove a gap when price trades into it.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep score = +1 for a sell-side sweep, −1 for a buy-side sweep, otherwise the prior score decays by 10% per bar. Sell-side sweep: low breaks below an equal-low level by more than the sweep-depth threshold times ATR and close returns above it. Buy-side sweep: high breaks above an equal-high level by more than that amount and close returns below it. Equal levels are confirmed swing highs or lows within the tolerance threshold times ATR of the preceding swing of the same type.
Calculation: Range position = clamp((close − midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback) / half that range, −1, 1), using current and prior bars.
Calculation: Online ML probability = logistic-regression probability that close will be higher after horizon H bars. Use seven features: (EMA(close, short) − EMA(close, long))/ATR; order-block pressure; FVG pressure; liquidity sweep score; range position; (close − SMA(close, oscillator lookback))/standard deviation(close, oscillator lookback); and (close − close five bars earlier)/(ATR × √5). Standardize features with online EWMA mean and variance, clamp standardized values to [−6,6], and train by SGD with L2 regularization on the realized H-bar direction label. Update and evaluate on completed bars.
Calculation: RL action = action with the greatest Q value for the current state. State is the three-way discretization of EMA trend feature (below −0.10, between −0.10 and 0.10, above 0.10), ATR volatility z-score (below −0.50, between −0.50 and 0.50, above 0.50), and oscillator z-score (below −1, between −1 and 1, above 1). Update tabular Q values on completed bars using TD(0), reward equal to prior action's close-to-close return divided by ATR for long, its negative for short, and negative flat-penalty times absolute return for flat; bootstrap from the maximum next-state Q value.
Calculation: Smart Money Score = round(100 × clamp(0.65 × online ML probability + 0.35 × RL contribution, 0, 1)), where RL contribution is RL confidence for long, 1 minus RL confidence for short, or 0.5 for flat; RL confidence is sigmoid(4 × (maximum state Q − mean state Q)).
Long: completed bar, warmup bars exceeded, online ML probability > long threshold, RL action = long (when RL gate enabled), and (online ML probability crossed above the long threshold OR bullish structure OR liquidity sweep score > 0.8), and (when confluence is required, (trend direction is bullish OR bullish CHoCH) AND (bullish order-block pressure > 0 OR bullish FVG pressure > 0 OR liquidity sweep score > 0) AND range position < 0.5).
Short: completed bar, warmup bars exceeded, online ML probability < short threshold, RL action = short (when RL gate enabled), and (online ML probability crossed below the short threshold OR bearish structure OR liquidity sweep score < −0.8), and (when confluence is required, (trend direction is bearish OR bearish CHoCH) AND (bearish order-block pressure < 0 OR bearish FVG pressure < 0 OR liquidity sweep score < 0) AND range position > −0.5).
Long: RL action = long.
Short: RL action = short.
Long: Smart Money Score ≥ 50.
Short: Smart Money Score < 50.
