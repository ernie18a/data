Calculation: Directional Convexity Index = 100 × sqrt(DX/100 × ((1 − convexity weight) × efficiency + convexity weight × convexity score)); DX = 100 × |+DI − −DI|/(+DI + −DI), where +DI and −DI are smoothed positive high changes and negative low changes, each divided by smoothed true range; efficiency = clamp(|close − close N bars ago| / sum of absolute close changes over N bars, efficiency floor, 1); convexity score = 0.5 × (1 + (smoothed positive second close changes − smoothed negative second close changes)/(their sum)). Uses signal-timeframe bars, including the current forming bar; N is the DCX lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive volatility envelope bands = selected moving average of the source ± adaptive multiplier × blended volatility; blended volatility = weight × high true-range percentile + (1 − weight) × ATR, where weight = clamp((standard deviation of true range / EMA of true range) / reference, 0, 1), and adaptive multiplier = base multiplier × (1 + adaptation × (clamp(DCX/100, 0, 1) − 0.5) × 2). Uses signal-timeframe bars, including the current forming bar; the moving average, ATR, percentile, and volatility lookbacks use the AVE window.
Calculation: Weekly ATR = ATR of weekly bars, using the current bar with lookahead off.
Long: close > upper adaptive volatility envelope band AND Directional Convexity Index > threshold AND Directional Convexity Index is rising over its rise window AND +DI > −DI AND (DXY bias disabled OR DXY close < its EMA over the DXY trend window). Signal and envelope use signal-timeframe bars, including the current forming bar; DXY close uses the DXY timeframe, including its current forming bar.
Short: close < lower adaptive volatility envelope band AND Directional Convexity Index > threshold AND Directional Convexity Index is falling over its rise window AND −DI > +DI AND (DXY bias disabled OR DXY close > its EMA over the DXY trend window). Signal and envelope use signal-timeframe bars, including the current forming bar; DXY close uses the DXY timeframe, including its current forming bar.
Exit: long, low ≤ initial stop OR high ≥ initial target; initial stop = min(lower envelope band at entry, entry price − stop multiplier × weekly ATR at entry), and initial target = entry price + reward multiple × (entry price − initial stop). If trailing is enabled, also exit when low ≤ max(current lower envelope band, average entry price − current weekly ATR).
Exit: short, high ≥ initial stop OR low ≤ initial target; initial stop = max(upper envelope band at entry, entry price + stop multiplier × weekly ATR at entry), and initial target = entry price − reward multiple × (initial stop − entry price). If trailing is enabled, also exit when high ≥ min(current upper envelope band, average entry price + current weekly ATR).
