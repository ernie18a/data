Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low with the configured equal-length left and right bars on each side; use only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structural break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low (current close above/below and prior close at or below/at or above the level). Trend is the direction of the latest structural break and persists until an opposite break.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish inducement = in bullish/bearish trend, current low is below / current high is above the lowest low / highest high of the previous 3 bars, and current close is back above / below that level.
Calculation: Confluence score = count of 8 true checks: trend agrees with close above/below the main EMA; RSI is above 40 and below its configured upper band in bullish trend, or below 60 and above its configured lower band in bearish trend; MACD line is above/below its signal line in bullish/bearish trend; volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the configured multiplier; trend agrees with close above/below the main EMA (count this duplicate check separately); weekend filter passes; current ATR exceeds prior-bar ATR; and close is at/above or at/below the EMA calculated on the higher-timeframe bar still forming for bullish/bearish trend.
Long: position is flat AND trend is bullish AND (bullish structural break OR inducement enabled and bullish inducement) AND confluence score >= configured threshold AND close >= EMA calculated on the higher-timeframe bar still forming AND (weekend trading enabled OR datetime is Monday-Friday)
Short: position is flat AND trend is bearish AND (bearish structural break OR inducement enabled and bearish inducement) AND confluence score >= configured threshold AND close <= EMA calculated on the higher-timeframe bar still forming AND (weekend trading enabled OR datetime is Monday-Friday)
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close - ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= signal-bar close - ATR × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio
