Calculation: Candle centroid = low + (high − low) × clamp(0.5 + 0.3 × volume-flow direction + 0.15 × wick asymmetry + 0.05 × candle momentum, 0.05, 0.95); use the candle midpoint if its range is zero. Volume-flow direction is twice the close’s fractional position in the candle range minus 1; wick asymmetry is (lower wick − upper wick) / total wick; candle momentum is (close − open) / max(abs(close − open), epsilon).
Long: close > candle centroid OR smoothed buy-power percentage > 50% OR (candle range > 0 AND velocity percentile > 0.90 AND normalized imbalance > 75% AND close > open) OR (velocity percentile < 0.30 AND wick percentile > 0.85 AND lower wick > 1.5 × upper wick AND close > candle centroid AND no expansion signal) OR (velocity percentile > 0.75 AND close < open AND lower wick / candle range > 0.25 AND close > candle centroid AND no expansion or liquid-sweep signal)
Short: close <= candle centroid OR smoothed sell-power percentage > 50% OR (candle range > 0 AND velocity percentile > 0.90 AND normalized imbalance > 75% AND close <= open) OR (velocity percentile < 0.30 AND wick percentile > 0.85 AND upper wick > 1.5 × lower wick AND close < candle centroid AND no expansion signal) OR (velocity percentile > 0.75 AND close > open AND upper wick / candle range > 0.25 AND close < candle centroid AND no expansion or liquid-sweep signal)
