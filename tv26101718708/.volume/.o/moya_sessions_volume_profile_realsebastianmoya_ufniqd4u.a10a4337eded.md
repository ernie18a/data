Calculation: Balance = the last completed session’s volume profile has a centered POC and a wide value area, with a similar value area across several completed sessions
Calculation: Accepted breakout = price closes beyond the last completed session’s VAH or VAL for several consecutive bars, and the still-forming session’s POC is beyond that boundary
Long: Accepted breakout above the last completed session’s VAH AND current low <= that VAH AND current close >= that VAH
Long: A prior bar closed below the last completed session’s VAL AND current close >= that VAL AND the still-forming profile has little volume below that VAL relative to the completed profile
Short: Balance AND current high >= the last completed session’s VAH AND current close < that VAH AND volume is not increasing
Exit: Long, price reaches the selected target level OR price reaches the applicable stop level
Exit: Short, price reaches the POC of the last completed session OR price reaches the applicable stop level
