Calculation: Volume percentile rank = percentile rank of current volume among the latest 100 bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Spread percentile rank = percentile rank of current high − low among the latest 100 bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Effort = high if volume percentile rank ≥ the high-volume threshold, low if ≤ the low-volume threshold, otherwise normal; spread class = ultra-wide, wide, normal, narrow, or ultra-narrow by ordered percentile-rank thresholds.
Calculation: Close position = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), classified high, middle, or low by a threshold on its absolute value.
Calculation: Background = strong when the rolling sum of bullish effort/result scores minus bearish effort/result scores exceeds a threshold; weak when below its negative; otherwise neutral. Bullish scores per bar: 3 for high effort with strong upward result, 1 for low effort with strong upward result, 2 for normal effort with strong or moderate upward result. Bearish scores are the inverse. Add 5 to the bullish or bearish sum according to the trend classifier. Use a configurable short, medium, or long rolling lookback.
Calculation: Rejection bar = upper rejection when upper wick/range exceeds a threshold, is longer than the lower wick, and body/range is below a threshold; lower rejection is the inverse.
Calculation: Strong-move z-score = (log(close / close three bars earlier) − 20-bar SMA of that log return) / its 20-bar population standard deviation.
Long: Previous bar was a down bar with low effort or volume below each of the prior two bars, narrow or ultra-narrow spread, and middle close position; current close > previous close.
Long: Previous bar was a lower-rejection bar with middle or high close position, normal or wider spread, and strong background; current bar is an up bar and close > previous close.
Long: Current bar is a down bar with high effort, narrow or ultra-narrow or normal spread, middle close position, and strong-move z-score ≤ −2 on one of the current or prior four bars.
Short: Previous bar was an up bar with low effort or volume below each of the prior two bars, narrow or ultra-narrow spread, and middle close position; current close < previous close.
Short: Previous bar was an upper-rejection bar with middle close position, normal or wider spread, and weak background; current bar is a down bar and close < previous close.
Short: Current bar is a down bar with high effort, narrow or ultra-narrow or normal spread, middle close position, and strong-move z-score ≤ −2 on one of the current or prior four bars, and background is not strong.
