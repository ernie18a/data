Calculation: Volume-profile peaks = on each profile recalculation, distribute each of the current and preceding lookback bars’ volume uniformly across the price bins overlapped by each bar’s high-low range; retain local bin-volume maxima meeting the configured strength threshold, minimum spacing, and peak-count limit, ranked strongest first. Use the current bar, including while it is forming.
Calculation: Order-block zones = when a low or high is confirmed as a pivot using equal configured lookbacks on both sides, select and classify its source candle by the following rules (green means close > open): pivot low: red pivot followed by green → bullish zone from pivot candle; green pivot with preceding candle not green → bullish zone from preceding candle; three consecutive green candles → bearish zone from preceding candle. Pivot high: green pivot not followed by green → bearish zone from pivot candle; red pivot preceded by green → bearish zone from preceding candle; three consecutive red candles → bullish zone from preceding candle. Zone bounds are the source candle’s full high-low range or body, according to the wick setting; require zone height ≥ configured multiplier × the SMA of absolute candle bodies over the configured period, excluding the current bar. Confirmed pivots use completed bars.
Calculation: Fair-value-gap zones = bullish zone [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago; bearish zone [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago. Require gap height ≥ configured multiplier × the SMA of absolute candle bodies over the configured period, excluding the current bar. Use the current bar, including while it is forming.
Calculation: Zone confluence = at least one retained volume-profile peak lies within [zone bottom − ATR × configured margin, zone top + ATR × configured margin]. For order blocks, test the source candle’s full wick range regardless of displayed zone bounds. Reevaluate active zones using the current profile peaks and ATR.
Long: an active, confluent bearish order-block or fair-value-gap zone has current close > zone top after prior close was within the zone; OR an active, confluent bullish order-block or fair-value-gap zone has prior close within the zone and current close > zone top.
Short: an active, confluent bullish order-block or fair-value-gap zone has current close < zone bottom; OR an active, confluent bearish order-block or fair-value-gap zone has prior close within the zone and current close < zone bottom.
