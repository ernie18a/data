Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the opening-range session, accumulated through its completion and then held for the rest of the day
Long: Fast EMA (shorter lookback) crosses above slow EMA (longer lookback) OR, during regular trading hours after the opening range is complete, close > opening-range high and no earlier long breakout occurred that day OR, after a long breakout and before any long retest that day, low <= opening-range high and close > opening-range high and close > VWAP and fast EMA > slow EMA OR, during regular trading hours after a qualifying long retest and before any long confirmation that day, close > open and close > previous bar high
Short: Fast EMA (shorter lookback) crosses below slow EMA (longer lookback) OR, during regular trading hours after the opening range is complete, close < opening-range low and no earlier short breakout occurred that day OR, after a short breakout and before any short retest that day, high >= opening-range low and close < opening-range low and close < VWAP and fast EMA < slow EMA OR, during regular trading hours after a qualifying short retest and before any short confirmation that day, close < open and close < previous bar low
