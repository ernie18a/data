Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the Slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the Fast EMA
Calculation: Third EMA = exponential moving average of close
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a shorter lookback than the structure pivot length; when volume is unavailable, volume filters pass
Calculation: Valid swing high/low = confirmed pivot of high/low, with the pivot lookback bars on each side; its price-to-opposite-extreme distance exceeds ATR times a sensitivity factor, and, when the swing-size filter is enabled, its swing size meets the configured threshold based on the recent average swing size
Calculation: Structure break level = most recent valid swing high or low
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening window of an enabled Sydney, Tokyo, London, or New York session, using the configured lower timeframe or chart bars as fallback
Calculation: Session, weekly, or monthly VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of the configured price source since the selected session, week, or month began; use volume 1 when volume is unavailable
Calculation: Recent swing high/low = most recent confirmed pivot high/low within the configured swing lookback
Calculation: Rejection wick = high minus the greater of open and close for an upper wick; the lesser of open and close minus low for a lower wick
Long: Fast EMA crosses above Slow EMA OR close >= Fast EMA AND Fast EMA >= Slow EMA OR close >= Third EMA OR close >= VWAP OR close > Structure break level + ATR * configured displacement AND volume passes the optional structure volume filter AND the swing high is valid AND (structure trend is not bearish OR this is the second consecutive qualifying bullish break while trend is bearish) OR close > internal swing high + ATR * configured displacement AND volume passes the optional structure volume filter AND no main structure event occurs on this bar OR low < Recent swing low AND close > Recent swing low AND lower rejection wick > ATR * configured minimum AND lower rejection wick > (high - low) * configured wick fraction AND optional sweep-depth, volume, and trend filters pass AND, when enabled, the next bar closes above the swept swing low AND, when enabled, bullish structure event occurred within the configured confluence window OR opening range is locked and close > opening range high
Short: Fast EMA crosses below Slow EMA OR close < Fast EMA AND Fast EMA < Slow EMA OR close < Third EMA OR close < VWAP OR close < Structure break level - ATR * configured displacement AND volume passes the optional structure volume filter AND the swing low is valid AND (structure trend is not bullish OR this is the second consecutive qualifying bearish break while trend is bullish) OR close < internal swing low - ATR * configured displacement AND volume passes the optional structure volume filter AND no main structure event occurs on this bar OR high > Recent swing high AND close < Recent swing high AND upper rejection wick > ATR * configured minimum AND upper rejection wick > (high - low) * configured wick fraction AND optional sweep-depth, volume, and trend filters pass AND, when enabled, the next bar closes below the swept swing high AND, when enabled, bearish structure event occurred within the configured confluence window OR opening range is locked and close < opening range low
