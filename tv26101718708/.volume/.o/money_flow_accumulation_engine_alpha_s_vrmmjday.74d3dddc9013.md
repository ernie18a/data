Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its longer-lookback simple moving average
Calculation: Composite flow = EMA of 0.30 × centered MFI + 0.30 × CMF-style flow + 0.25 × normalized OBV deviation + 0.15 × price-volume pressure; centered MFI = (MFI − 50) × 2; CMF-style flow = clamp(100 × sum(close-location multiplier × volume) / sum(volume), −100, 100), where close-location multiplier = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), or 0 for a zero-range bar; normalized OBV deviation = clamp(28 × (OBV − OBV EMA) / OBV standard deviation, −100, 100), or 0 when standard deviation is zero, with OBV adding volume on an up close, subtracting it on a down close, and otherwise unchanged; price-volume pressure = clamp(30 × (0.65 × close location + 0.35 × candle-body direction) × min(relative volume, 3), −100, 100), where close location = 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1 (or 0 for zero range), and candle-body direction is 1, −1, or 0 for close above, below, or equal to open
Calculation: Flow signal = EMA of composite flow using a longer smoothing length than the composite flow
Calculation: Smart-money line = EMA of composite flow; smart-money slope = current smart-money line − its value one bar earlier
Calculation: Accumulation zone = accumulation candidate true for at least the selected minimum consecutive bars; accumulation candidate requires close <= close from the selected accumulation lookback ago OR that lookback's high-low range <= ATR × selected flat-price multiplier; close <= lookback low + lookback range × selected lower-range fraction; smart-money line rising; composite flow > flow signal + selected margin; smart-money line > selected negative limit; relative volume >= selected minimum
Calculation: Distribution zone = distribution candidate true for at least the selected minimum consecutive bars; distribution candidate requires close >= close from the selected distribution lookback ago OR that lookback's high-low range <= ATR × selected flat-price multiplier; close >= lookback low + lookback range × selected upper-range fraction; smart-money line falling; composite flow < flow signal − selected margin; smart-money line < selected positive limit; relative volume >= selected minimum
Calculation: Accumulation score = round(min(100, 48 + clamp((smart-money line + 28) × 0.65, 0, 30) + 12 if smart-money line rose for two consecutive bars or composite flow turned up, otherwise 0, + clamp(relative volume × 6, 0, 10)))
Calculation: Distribution score = round(min(100, 48 + clamp((28 − smart-money line) × 0.65, 0, 30) + 12 if smart-money line fell for two consecutive bars or composite flow turned down, otherwise 0, + clamp(relative volume × 6, 0, 10)))
Calculation: Confirmation score = round(min(100, abs(composite flow) × 0.8 + min(relative volume, 3) × 10 + abs(smart-money slope) × 2))
Calculation: Divergence score = round(min(100, abs(composite flow − its value at the divergence lookback ago) × 1.5 + abs(smart-money slope) × 8 + 40))
