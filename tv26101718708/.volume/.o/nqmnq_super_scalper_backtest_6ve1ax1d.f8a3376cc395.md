Calculation: Trend filter = bullish when 5m, 15m, and 60m EMA(9) > EMA(21), 4h EMA(9) is not decisively below EMA(21), chart EMA(9) > EMA(21) > EMA(100), close > session VWAP, and ADX > its minimum; bearish is the inverse. HTF values use bars available on the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its 20-bar average; ATR uses a session-selected fast, medium, or slow lookback, with the fast lookback shorter than medium and slow longer than medium.
Calculation: Long base = bullish trend filter AND bullish RSI(7) (inside dynamic session/regime bounds and rising) AND two consecutive closes above successive prior 5-bar swing highs, with current close clearing the latest swing high by an ATR-scaled margin.
Calculation: Short base = bearish trend filter AND bearish RSI(7) (inside dynamic session/regime bounds and falling) AND two consecutive closes below successive prior 5-bar swing lows.
Calculation: ORB levels = high and low of the opening-range bars, fixed after the range-building window completes.
Calculation: ORB breakout-retest = price first closes beyond the fixed ORB boundary by a percentage margin, then retests that boundary within a dynamic percentage band and closes back beyond it with either rejection wick or directional close on rising volume.
Calculation: ORB rotation = before either breakout is recorded, price reaches near an ORB boundary and closes back inside with a rejection wick; candle range and body must each exceed ATR-relative minimums.
Calculation: Swing structure = confirmed pivot highs and lows; bullish structure requires the latest two pivot highs and latest two pivot lows to be higher, bearish structure requires both pairs to be lower. Pivots are confirmed using bars to the right.
Calculation: BOS = close breaks the highest high or lowest low over the configurable lookback excluding the current bar, by an ATR-scaled margin, with relative volume above its dynamic threshold and candle direction agreeing; repeated BOS signals are separated by a cooldown. Bullish and bearish BOS confluence scores count direction-specific alignment with HTF EMA, VWAP, ADX, trend regime, session window, FVG, EMA(21), relative volume, delta slope, buy/sell volume ratio, engulfing candle, non-doji candle, and matching swing structure.
Calculation: Bullish order-block signal = a bullish displacement candle with elevated relative volume and a prior bearish candle in the lookback; count bullish HTF alignment, close below VWAP, bullish FVG, elevated relative volume, and kill-zone timing. Bearish order-block signal is the inverse, using a prior bullish candle and close above VWAP. Require the configured minimum rule count, increased in thin sessions.
Calculation: Retest signal = in an AER uptrend, low below EMA(21), close above EMA(21), and no bearish lower-low structure; bearish is the inverse in an AER downtrend, requiring high above EMA(21), close below EMA(21), and no bullish higher-low structure. Score ADX, relative volume, VWAP, HTF alignment, candle body/range, and directional delta; require the configured score, increased when broader context opposes the retest.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = current high/low exceeds the prior lookback extreme and closes back inside, with a sufficiently large wick, wick/body ratio, relative volume, bar range versus its recent average, and wick size versus its recent mean and standard deviation. Swept highs are bearish; swept lows are bullish.
Calculation: Long structural veto = prior-range upper extreme, bearish HTF confluence veto, or bearish auxiliary blocker count reaching its dynamic threshold. Short structural veto = prior-range lower extreme, bullish HTF confluence veto, or bullish auxiliary blocker count reaching its dynamic threshold. Prior range uses a session/regime-selected lookback; blocker counts combine opposing swing structure, the relevant range extreme, capitulation, and opposing wick rejection.
Calculation: Long trend alignment = close above EMA(100) plus a dynamic ATR buffer and EMA(100) rising over a dynamic lookback; short alignment is the inverse. Long/short overextension = corresponding dynamic score of distance from EMA(21) and VWAP, opposing swing exhaustion, MFI exhaustion, low relative volume, and opposing wick rejection reaches its dynamic threshold.
Calculation: Long volume/structure gate = at least one short structural gate (descending 15m and 60m EMA(21), non-rising VWAP, or close sufficiently below the recent high), with the required number of eight bearish factors: elevated relative volume, sell-volume dominance, falling cumulative delta, falling EMA(21), close sufficiently below VWAP, AER downtrend, room to nearest support, and decisive bearish candle. Short gate is the code's `shortVolumeOk` condition; it is also required for short BOS, OB, retest, and sweep entries.
Calculation: Directional vetoes = early direction score (15m/60m EMA(21) slopes, chart EMA(9)/EMA(21), VWAP position, and AER regime) blocks or tightens entries at dynamic thresholds; full direction score combines chart/HTF momentum, VWAP, breadth, volatility, delta/flow, MFI, AER, prior-day bias, Hurst, relative strength, MACD, volume zones, pivots, BOS, squeeze, retest, ORB, absorption, divergence, and swing structure. Apply the direction-specific full-score veto. Macro veto blocks a counter-structure entry unless its weighted opposing HTF, delta, direction-score, session, ADX, volume, and Hurst support reaches a dynamic threshold.
Calculation: Opening-drive signal = during an opening/data window, a volume spike and strong non-doji directional candle occur; directional confidence counts premarket midpoint, HTF EMA, previous completed day close, overnight range break, prior-volume buildup, engulfing/hammer or shooting-star pattern, SMT divergence, CHoCH, swing structure, and delta with dynamic weights. Require dynamic minimum confidence, no opposing HTF-confluence veto, and no veto from the corresponding extreme of the premarket range.
