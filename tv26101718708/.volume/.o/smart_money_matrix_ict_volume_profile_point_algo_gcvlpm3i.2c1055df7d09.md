Calculation: Active swing high = most recently confirmed pivot high, where the pivot bar’s high is the highest across the pivot bar and `length` bars on each side; clear it after a bearish sweep or bullish break.
Calculation: Active swing low = most recently confirmed pivot low, where the pivot bar’s low is the lowest across the pivot bar and `length` bars on each side; clear it after a bullish sweep or bearish break.
Calculation: Volume profile levels = over the lookback bars including the current bar, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into equal bins and assign each bar’s volume to the bin containing its high-low midpoint; POC is the center of the highest-volume bin, and VAH/VAL are the upper/lower edges of the contiguous bins expanded from POC by adding the larger-volume adjacent bin until the selected percentage of total volume is included.
Long: low < active swing low AND close > active swing low OR close > active swing high OR low > high[2] AND close[1] > high[2] OR close crosses above POC OR close crosses above VAL AND close[1] < VAL
Short: high > active swing high AND close < active swing high OR close < active swing low OR high < low[2] AND close[1] < low[2] OR close crosses below POC OR close crosses below VAH AND close[1] > VAH
