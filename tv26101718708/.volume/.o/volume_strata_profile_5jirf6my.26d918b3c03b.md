Calculation: Volume-at-price row volume = sum volume allocated to each price row in the lookback, in proportion to each bar’s high-low overlap with that row; use lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart bars. The lookback includes the current, possibly forming, chart bar and is divided chronologically into strata.
Calculation: Profile POC = center of the row with the greatest total volume; stratum POC = center of the greatest-volume row within that stratum. Ties select the lowest row.
Calculation: VAH = upper boundary of the value area grown from the profile POC until it contains the configured share of total volume, adding the higher adjacent row first when adjacent volumes tie.
Calculation: Newest-stratum half POCs = POCs of the older and newer halves of the newest stratum; split at the midpoint, with the extra bar in the newer half.
Calculation: Newest-to-prior POC change = newest-stratum POC row minus prior-stratum POC row; prior-to-earlier change is the prior-stratum POC row minus the stratum before it. An upward change exceeds the configured row tolerance; a downward change is below its negative.
Calculation: Migration quality = sign(newest-stratum POC price − oldest-stratum POC price) × migration efficiency × min(1, absolute net POC change divided by the configured migration-range fraction); migration efficiency = absolute net POC change divided by the sum of absolute consecutive stratum POC changes.
Calculation: Recent migration supports long = newest-to-prior POC change is upward, or it is upward while the prior-to-earlier change is downward, or it is upward without either persistence condition.
Calculation: Newest activity ratio = newest-stratum volume per chart bar divided by total profile volume per chart bar.
Calculation: Intrabar quality = 100 × square root of intrabar coverage fraction × density adequacy; coverage and density are measured over the newest three strata, and density adequacy is average intrabars per covered chart bar divided by the maximum observed intrabars per chart bar, capped at 1. The quality gate passes when intrabar mode is inactive or quality meets its configured minimum.
Long: (newest-to-prior POC change is upward AND newest-stratum newer-half POC row ≥ older-half POC row − configured row tolerance AND recent migration supports long AND migration quality score > 0) OR (newest-to-prior POC change is upward AND prior-to-earlier POC change is upward AND newer-half POC acceptance condition passes AND migration quality score ≥ configured minimum AND recent migration supports long AND newest activity ratio ≥ configured minimum AND close ≥ VAH) OR (intrabar quality gate passes AND at least five of these six conditions hold: newest-to-prior POC change is upward; prior-to-earlier POC change is upward; newer-half POC acceptance condition passes; migration quality score ≥ configured minimum AND recent migration supports long; close ≥ profile POC; close ≥ VAH − ATR × configured distance AND newest-to-prior POC change is upward AND close ≥ profile POC).
