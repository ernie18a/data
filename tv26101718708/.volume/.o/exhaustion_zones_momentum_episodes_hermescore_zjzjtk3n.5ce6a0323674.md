Calculation: Exhaustion high zone = from the highest high during an RSI episode at or above the configured high threshold to that bar’s body top; pad to minimum ATR thickness and reject if taller than the configured ATR cap.
Calculation: Exhaustion low zone = from the lowest low during an RSI episode at or below the configured low threshold to that bar’s body bottom; pad to minimum ATR thickness and reject if taller than the configured ATR cap.
Calculation: Zone score = points for deep RSI saturation, extreme-bar volume above its volume average, RSI returning inside within the configured fade window after its peak, divergence from the previous same-side episode (more extreme price extreme with less extreme peak RSI), and rejection wick longer than the configured fraction of the body.
Long: RSI returns above the configured low threshold after a low-side episode and a valid exhaustion low zone forms, or the close enters an active exhaustion low zone for the first time; if the strength filter is enabled, zone score meets its configured minimum.
Short: RSI returns below the configured high threshold after a high-side episode and a valid exhaustion high zone forms, or the close enters an active exhaustion high zone for the first time; if the strength filter is enabled, zone score meets its configured minimum.
