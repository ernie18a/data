Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 from the start of the selected session, week, or month, including the current forming bar; anchored standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted variance of HLC3 over the same anchor period.
Calculation: ATR zones = each center level plus and minus its ATR width; centers are anchored VWAP, anchored VWAP plus or minus a shorter standard-deviation multiple, and anchored VWAP plus or minus a longer standard-deviation multiple. Use current chart bars, including the forming bar.
Calculation: Zone CVD percentage = 100 × accumulated volume with positive delta / accumulated volume with nonzero delta, accumulated separately for each zone and reset at the configured reset boundary. Delta is volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, 0.0001) on the selected CVD timeframe; use that timeframe’s current value with lookahead off, including its forming bar when available. Count its volume when chart close is inside the zone and chart volume meets the minimum threshold.
Long: (close is within the VWAP ATR zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND VWAP-zone CVD percentage ≥ bullish threshold) OR (close is within the lower short-standard-deviation zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND lower-zone CVD percentage ≥ bullish threshold) OR (close is within the lower long-standard-deviation zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND lower-zone CVD percentage ≥ bullish threshold)
Short: (close is within the VWAP ATR zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND VWAP-zone CVD percentage ≤ 100 − bullish threshold) OR (close is within the upper short-standard-deviation zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND upper-zone CVD percentage ≤ 100 − bullish threshold) OR (close is within the upper long-standard-deviation zone AND volume ≥ minimum threshold AND upper-zone CVD percentage ≤ 100 − bullish threshold)
