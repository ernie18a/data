Calculation: Long pullback setup = a bearish candle during an uptrend arms a setup at that bar; it remains valid for the configured number of following bars, unless a close falls below that pullback candle’s low minus the configured buffer or the fast EMA is no longer above the slow EMA.
Calculation: Short pullback setup = a bullish candle during a downtrend arms a setup at that bar; it remains valid for the configured number of following bars, unless a close rises above that pullback candle’s high plus the configured buffer or the fast EMA is no longer below the slow EMA.
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × 100.
Long: flat position AND entry time is within the configured session window AND daily trade limit and cooldown permit entry AND valid long pullback setup from an earlier bar AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its value at the configured slope lookback AND close > open AND candle body percentage ≥ configured minimum AND close > previous bar high AND close > fast EMA
Short: flat position AND entry time is within the configured session window AND daily trade limit and cooldown permit entry AND valid short pullback setup from an earlier bar AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND fast EMA < its value at the configured slope lookback AND close < open AND candle body percentage ≥ configured minimum AND close < previous bar low AND close < fast EMA
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured target distance OR the bar closes at or after the configured session end
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured target distance OR the bar closes at or after the configured session end
