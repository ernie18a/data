Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: Bar change (%) = 100 × (close − open) / open, or 0 when open ≤ 0
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low that is a swing extreme versus the configured bars to its left and right; use its pivot-bar values when confirming it after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Prior trend = up when the 50-bar simple moving average of close is greater than its value 50 bars earlier; otherwise down
Long: In idle, a confirmed pivot low has pivot-bar volume > its volume average × 0.85 × climax multiplier, pivot-bar close position ≥ 0.30, and prior trend is down (preliminary support); OR in idle, it has pivot-bar volume > its volume average × climax multiplier, pivot-bar close position ≥ 0.40, and prior trend is down (selling climax)
Long: After a selling climax or buying climax, the first qualifying pivot that sets the opposite range boundary makes the range width ≥ minimum range (automatic rally/reaction); OR after a range rally, a confirmed pivot low is within the configured support test tolerance, allows only the configured spring-tolerance undershoot, and has pivot-bar volume < its volume average × test-volume maximum (secondary test); OR after a spring, a confirmed pivot low has pivot-bar volume < its volume average × test-volume maximum and is at least 98% of support (test); OR after spring/test setup, close > resistance and bar change ≥ minimum move and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (sign of strength); OR after sign of strength, a confirmed pivot low has low volume and is above support (last point of support); OR after last point of support, close > resistance (markup); OR after markup, a confirmed pivot low has low volume and is above support (last point of support); OR during markdown, a confirmed pivot high formed after markdown began is at least 3 × the configured secondary-test tolerance above the lowest close since markdown began (automatic rally)
Short: In idle, a confirmed pivot high has pivot-bar volume > its volume average × 0.85 × climax multiplier, pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.70, and prior trend is up (preliminary supply); OR in idle, it has pivot-bar volume > its volume average × climax multiplier, pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.60, and prior trend is up (buying climax); OR after a buying climax, a confirmed pivot high is between resistance × (1 − 4 × secondary-test tolerance) and resistance × (1 + spring tolerance), and has low pivot-bar volume (secondary test); OR after a buying-climax range rally, close > resistance × (1 + spring tolerance), bar change ≥ minimum move, and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (sign of strength); OR after a buying-climax range rally, close < support × (1 − 1.5 × secondary-test tolerance) (sign of weakness); OR after secondary test, high breaks resistance but is no more than the spring-tolerance amount above it, close ≤ resistance × 1.005, and close position ≤ 0.45 (upthrust after distribution); OR after secondary test, low breaks support beyond the spring-tolerance amount, bar change ≤ −minimum move, and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (sign of weakness); OR after upthrust, close < support, bar change ≤ −minimum move, and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (sign of weakness); OR after sign of weakness, a confirmed pivot high has low volume and is below resistance (last point of supply); OR after last point of supply, close < support and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (markdown); OR during markup, close is below the highest close since markup began by more than 5 × the configured secondary-test tolerance (failed-markup markdown); OR during markup, a confirmed pivot high has climax volume, pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.45, and is at least 95% of the highest close since markup began (buying-climax sub-cycle); OR after markdown, a confirmed pivot high formed after markdown began is at least 3 × the configured secondary-test tolerance above the lowest close since markdown began, has pivot-bar volume > its volume average, and pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.60 (buying climax)
Long: After a selling-climax range rally, close > resistance, bar change ≥ minimum move, and volume > volume average × strength-volume minimum (sign of strength); OR after a spring, low ≤ support and ≥ support × (1 − spring tolerance), close ≥ support × 0.995, and close position ≥ 0.55 (spring); OR after a spring, low < support × (1 − spring tolerance) and close position ≥ 0.40 (deep spring)
Short: After secondary test, high breaks resistance but is no more than the spring-tolerance amount above it, close ≤ resistance × 1.005, and close position ≤ 0.45 (upthrust after distribution)
Exit: Long, close < support × (1 − 1.5 × secondary-test tolerance) during sign-of-strength/last-point-of-support setup; OR close > lowest close since sign of weakness by more than 2 × the secondary-test tolerance during sign-of-weakness/last-point-of-supply setup
Exit: Short, close > lowest close since sign of weakness by more than 2 × the secondary-test tolerance during sign-of-weakness/last-point-of-supply setup; OR a qualifying post-markdown automatic rally
