Calculation: Lower box boundary = lowest low over the box lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Upper box boundary = highest high over the box lookback, including the current bar
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = current low is below the lowest low in the preceding left lookback and no higher than the lowest low in the right lookback including the current bar, AND current low is below the lowest low in the preceding left lookback for divergence comparison, AND current RSI is above the lowest RSI in that preceding lookback, AND current RSI is below the oversold threshold; RSI is calculated from low
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = current low is above the highest low in the preceding left lookback and no lower than the highest low in the right lookback including the current bar, AND current low is above the highest low in the preceding left lookback for divergence comparison, AND current RSI is below the highest RSI in that preceding lookback, AND current RSI is above the overbought threshold; RSI is calculated from low
Calculation: Moving averages = selected moving average type of custom-timeframe close; use the last completed custom-timeframe bar. The four lookbacks are ordered short to long, with the longest as the reference.
Calculation: Uptrend = (shorter, second-shorter, and second-longest moving averages are each above the longest moving average AND custom-timeframe close is above the longest moving average) OR (chart-bar high is above all four moving averages)
Calculation: Downtrend = (shorter, second-shorter, and second-longest moving averages are each below the longest moving average AND custom-timeframe close is below the longest moving average) OR (chart-bar low is below all four moving averages)
Long: Downtrend AND chart-bar close is at or below the lower box boundary AND bullish RSI divergence
Short: Uptrend AND chart-bar close is at or above the upper box boundary AND bearish RSI divergence
Exit: long, Uptrend AND chart-bar close is at or above the upper box boundary AND bearish RSI divergence
