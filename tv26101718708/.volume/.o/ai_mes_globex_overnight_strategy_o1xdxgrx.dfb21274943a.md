Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured ET range-building window; freeze at its end. Range midpoint = average of the frozen high and low.
Calculation: Valid range = frozen range width is within the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: HTF EMA = EMA of closes on the configured higher-timeframe bars, using the latest value available without lookahead; compare chart close with it for bullish/bearish bias.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Breakout volume = chart-bar volume exceeds its moving average times the configured breakout multiplier. Fade volume = chart-bar volume exceeds the same moving average times the configured fade multiplier.
Calculation: Long/short stop = the farther stop from the entry-bar close between the ATR-based stop and the most recent opposite pivot beyond its configured buffer; use the ATR-based stop if no such pivot exists.
Calculation: Breakout/trend target = entry-bar close plus/minus the corresponding stop risk times the configured reward-to-risk ratio. Fade target = opening range midpoint.
Long: In the configured Globex session AND outside the enabled news-blackout window AND below the configured per-session trade limit AND flat AND (valid range AND (close crosses above frozen range high plus breakout buffer AND RSI is above the breakout-long threshold AND breakout volume AND (HTF filter disabled OR close above HTF EMA) OR confirmed pivot high is crossed upward AND close above HTF EMA AND RSI is above breakout-long threshold AND breakout volume OR low tags within fade buffer of frozen range low AND close crosses back above frozen range low AND RSI is below oversold threshold AND fade volume AND (HTF filter disabled OR close above HTF EMA)))
Short: In the configured Globex session AND outside the enabled news-blackout window AND below the configured per-session trade limit AND flat AND (valid range AND (close crosses below frozen range low minus breakout buffer AND RSI is below breakout-short threshold AND breakout volume AND (HTF filter disabled OR close below HTF EMA) OR confirmed pivot low is crossed downward AND close below HTF EMA AND RSI is below breakout-short threshold AND breakout volume OR high tags within fade buffer of frozen range high AND close crosses back below frozen range high AND RSI is above overbought threshold AND fade volume AND (HTF filter disabled OR close below HTF EMA)))
Exit: Long, low is at or below the stored long stop OR high is at or above the stored long target OR the bar exits the configured session after the prior bar was in session.
Exit: Short, high is at or above the stored short stop OR low is at or below the stored short target OR the bar exits the configured session after the prior bar was in session.
