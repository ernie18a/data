Calculation: RSI features = base RSI value; 3-bar RSI change and its change in slope; absolute distance from 50; percentile rank of RSI; standard deviation of RSI over 14 bars; fast RSI (half the base length) minus slow RSI (twice the base length); and EMA( RSI, 20 ) minus 50. Scale the slope, acceleration, RSI standard deviation, fast/slow spread, and regime values to [0,1] using their rolling 100-bar minimum and maximum (equal bounds map to 0.5); divide RSI value and percentile rank by 100, and distance from 50 by 50.
Calculation: Analog score = distance-weighted average of labels from the k nearest stored feature vectors, using distance Σ(weight × log(1 + absolute feature difference)) and vote weight 1/(1 + distance). Store each confirmed bar's features with its label once the forward horizon is known: label is +3/+2/+1 for forward source-price change greater than 2/1/0 times the ATR-scaled threshold, and −3/−2/−1 for change less than −2/−1/0 times that threshold; otherwise 0. Search stored rows at every fourth row, within the memory-depth limit. Feature weights are manual or adapt from Fisher separation of positive versus negative stored labels, scaled to [0.5,10] and EMA-smoothed. Bias is bullish above 0.15, bearish below −0.15, otherwise neutral. Also calculate agreement as the distance-weight share voting for the bias, and tightness as clamp(1 − mean neighbor distance/(0.45 × sum of feature weights), 0, 1).
Calculation: Adaptive Supertrend = recursive Supertrend using ATR(10), centered on its selected price source, with multiplier = base multiplier × (1 + adaptivity × (1 − drive)); drive = clamp(0.5 × absolute EMA(analog score / 1.5, 10) + 0.3 × tightness + 0.2 × agreement, 0, 1), multiplied by 0.35 when the chop filter is enabled and active. Trend turns bullish when prior trend was bearish and close exceeds the prior bearish band; turns bearish when prior trend was bullish and close falls below the prior bullish band. Trail bands ratchet using the prior close and prior corresponding band.
Calculation: Rank = clamp(25×agreement + 15×tightness + 10×slope-fit + 5×not-stretched + 10×Supertrend-aligned + volatility points + regime points + 5×smooth-oscillator-aligned + min(5, stance age) − penalties, 0, 100). Slope-fit means RSI rises over 3 bars for bullish bias or does not rise for bearish bias; stretched means RSI >70 for bullish bias or <30 for bearish bias. Volatility points are 10 when ATR's 100-bar percentile is within the configured healthy band, 5 below its lower bound, otherwise 3. Regime points are 10 when EMA(RSI,20)>55 for bullish bias or <45 for bearish bias, 4 when between 45 and 55, otherwise 6. Smooth-oscillator alignment means EMA(RSI,5) rises for bullish bias or does not rise for bearish bias. Penalties are min(20, 8 if choppy + 6 if stretched + 6 if an early flip + 5×(k−available neighbors)/k if fewer than k neighbors).
Calculation: Confidence = clamp(40×agreement + 25×tightness + 15×min(1, stance age/5) + 10×slope-fit − 15 if early flip − 10×(k−available neighbors)/k if fewer than k neighbors, 0, 100).
Long: bullish stance changes from non-bullish to bullish AND rank meets its minimum AND confidence meets its minimum AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed; bullish stance requires bullish analog bias, bullish Supertrend if trend gate enabled, ATR percentile within its configured band if volatility filter enabled, and not choppy if chop filter enabled. OR Supertrend turns bullish.
Short: bearish stance changes from non-bearish to bearish AND rank meets its minimum AND confidence meets its minimum AND cooldown has elapsed AND bar is confirmed; bearish stance requires bearish analog bias, bearish Supertrend if trend gate enabled, ATR percentile within its configured band if volatility filter enabled, and not choppy if chop filter enabled. OR Supertrend turns bearish.
Exit: long, Supertrend turns bearish.
Exit: short, Supertrend turns bullish.
