Calculation: Smoothed range = Hull moving average of high − low.
Calculation: Adaptive factor = base factor × ATR / simple moving average of ATR, clipped to the configured minimum and maximum; use the base factor when adaptation is disabled. Upper/lower trailing bands start at (high + low + 2 × close) / 4 ± factor × smoothed range. Each band ratchets toward price: the upper band updates to its new value if that value is lower than its previous value or the previous close is above the previous upper band; otherwise it stays at its previous value. The lower band updates to its new value if that value is higher than its previous value or the previous close is below the previous lower band; otherwise it stays at its previous value. Initialize direction as down. Thereafter, if the previous trend line was the previous upper band, direction is up when close > upper band and down otherwise; if it was the previous lower band, direction is down when close < lower band and up otherwise.
Long: trend direction = up.
Short: trend direction = down.
