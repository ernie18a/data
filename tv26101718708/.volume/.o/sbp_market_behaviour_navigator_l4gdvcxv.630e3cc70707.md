Calculation: Developing session volume profile = distribute each confirmed bar’s volume evenly across 30 price bins spanning the current session’s low–high range; use the point of control and the value area containing 68% of volume. If the session reaches 300 bars, use the rolling 300-bar low–high range. Use the profile including the current confirmed bar.
Calculation: Smoothed value area = smooth the developing profile’s midpoint and half-width by 40% of their difference per confirmed bar, limiting midpoint and width changes by ATR-based bounds; minimum half-width is two minimum ticks.
Calculation: Direction score = weighted sum of normalized directional movement, fast-versus-slow EMA alignment, EMA-smoothed close momentum, close displacement from an HLC3 EMA, and close position relative to the smoothed value area. Normalize the components by ATR or value-area half-width and clamp them to their defined ranges.
Calculation: Bullish response = at least one of: bullish sweep, bullish break, bullish value-area acceptance, or bullish trendline reversal; and lower wick is at least 70% of upper wick. A bullish sweep makes a lower low than the prior six completed bars and closes above that low and above the open. A bullish break closes above the highest high of the prior two completed bars and above the open. Bullish value-area acceptance closes above the upper value boundary on two consecutive bars. Bullish trendline reversal touches within 0.12 ATR above the dynamic trendline, closes above it and the open, has a longer lower wick than upper wick, trendline slope is at least −0.015 ATR and has not fallen by more than 0.02 ATR from the prior bar, and direction score is above 0.05.
Calculation: Bearish response = at least one of: bearish sweep, bearish break, bearish value-area acceptance, or bearish trendline reversal; and upper wick is at least 70% of lower wick. A bearish sweep makes a higher high than the prior six completed bars and closes below that high and below the open. A bearish break closes below the lowest low of the prior two completed bars and below the open. Bearish value-area acceptance closes below the lower value boundary on two consecutive bars. Bearish trendline reversal touches within 0.12 ATR below the dynamic trendline, closes below it and the open, has a longer upper wick than lower wick, trendline slope is at most 0.015 ATR and has not risen by more than 0.02 ATR from the prior bar, and direction score is below −0.05.
Calculation: Compression = the highest high minus lowest low over the last eight bars is at most 1.50 ATR, and absolute net close change over those bars divided by the sum of absolute consecutive close changes is at most 0.30.
Calculation: Participation expansion = volume is unavailable or current volume divided by its 20-bar average is at least 1.02.
Calculation: Bullish vote count = number of true conditions among: close above open; close above prior close; momentum rising; fast EMA above slow EMA; +DI above −DI; direction score above 0.10; close above developing point of control; and body-to-range ratio at least 0.34 with close in the upper 42% of the candle. Bearish vote count uses the corresponding bearish comparisons and close in the lower 42%.
Calculation: Buy candidate = confirmed bar, value area ready, not compressed, participation expansion, bullish response, at least six bullish votes, direction score at least 0.12, and bullish votes exceed bearish votes.
Calculation: Sale candidate = confirmed bar, value area ready, not compressed, participation expansion, bearish response, at least six bearish votes, direction score at most −0.12, and bearish votes exceed bullish votes.
Calculation: Dynamic trendline = EMA of the WMA of twice the shorter WMA of close minus the longer WMA of close. Trendline slope = its change from the prior bar divided by ATR.
Calculation: Bull ribbon = on a confirmed bar, both the current and prior bars have cloud direction at least 0.12, close at or above cloud center, and short HLC3 EMA slope at least −0.01 ATR. Cloud direction is a weighted sum of volume-weighted candle close-location participation, normalized cloud-center slope, and close displacement from cloud center; cloud center is a long VWMA, falling back to a long HLC3 EMA. Bear ribbon uses cloud direction at most −0.12, close at or below cloud center, and short HLC3 EMA slope at most 0.01 ATR.
Long: buy candidate AND (no prior signal and no sale candidate, OR prior signal was sale); OR bull ribbon; OR dynamic trendline is rising or unchanged.
Short: sale candidate AND (no prior signal and no buy candidate, OR prior signal was buy); OR bear ribbon; OR dynamic trendline is falling.
