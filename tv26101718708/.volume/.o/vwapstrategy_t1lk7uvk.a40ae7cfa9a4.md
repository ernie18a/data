Calculation: short-term SMA = simple moving average of close
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 over the current session
Long: short-term SMA is above VWAP on the current bar AND was at or below VWAP on the previous bar
Short: VWAP is above short-term SMA on the current bar AND was at or below short-term SMA on the previous bar
Exit: long, VWAP is above short-term SMA on the current bar AND was at or below short-term SMA on the previous bar
Exit: short, short-term SMA is above VWAP on the current bar AND was at or below VWAP on the previous bar
