Calculation: Prior channel high = previous bar's maximum of the highest high over the longer lookback and the highest high over the shorter lookback; prior channel low = previous bar's minimum of the corresponding lowest lows. The longer lookback is Average # of bars; the shorter is Price Lookback Period.
Calculation: Channel midpoint = (prior channel high + prior channel low) / 2.
Calculation: Range filter = recursively filtered high source using a range equal to Price Lookback Period × EMA(EMA(abs(close − previous close), slow length), 2 × slow length − 1); when the source is above the prior filter value, the new filter is max(source − range, prior filter), otherwise it is min(source + range, prior filter). Upward count increments while the filter rises and resets when it falls; downward count increments while it falls and resets when it rises. Uptrend = high source > range filter AND upward count > 0. Downtrend = high source < range filter AND downward count > 0.
Calculation: Fast momentum line = 10 × the percentage change in the triple EMA of OHLC4, each with fast length. Slow momentum line = 10 × ALMA(2-bar SMA of that percentage change, length 3, offset 0.85, sigma 6).
Calculation: Stochastic MACD = 100 × (fast stochastic − slow stochastic), where fast stochastic = (fast-length moving average of close − highest high over fast length) / (highest high − lowest low over fast length), and slow stochastic = (slow-length moving average of close − lowest low over slow length) / (highest high − lowest low over slow length). Use the selected moving-average type for both averages. Its signal line is the selected moving average of Stochastic MACD over the longer Average # of bars lookback.
Calculation: Bullish volume condition = current up-bar volume ≥ average volume of up bars over the Price Lookback Period AND cumulative up-bar volume exceeds cumulative down-bar volume, with neither volume-trend contradiction present. Bearish volume condition = current down-bar volume ≥ average volume of down bars over that period AND cumulative down-bar volume exceeds cumulative up-bar volume, with neither contradiction present. A contradiction is positive net volume flow on a down bar while prior flow was positive, or negative net volume flow on an up bar while prior flow was negative. BullShit = either contradiction.
Calculation: Moving-average bands = channel midpoint ± the selected percentage band; for the Fibonacci trigger, bands 1 and 2 instead equal channel midpoint ± ATR over the Price Lookback Period multiplied by Fibonacci level 1 and level 2, respectively.
Long: selected trigger is Average Price Channel AND ((low < prior channel low AND Uptrend) OR (Fast momentum line > Slow momentum line AND Fast momentum line < 0 AND Uptrend))
Long: selected trigger is Moving Average AND low < selected lower moving-average band AND (Uptrend OR Stochastic MACD crosses above its signal line)
Long: selected trigger is Fibonacci AND ((low three bars ago, low two bars ago, previous low, and current low are each ≤ lower band 2) OR (low ≤ lower band 2 AND BullShit) OR (low ≤ lower band 1 AND Stochastic MACD crosses above its signal line))
Long: selected trigger is Peaks and Valleys AND low < prior channel low AND (Stochastic MACD crosses above its signal line OR BullShit)
Short: selected trigger is Average Price Channel AND ((high ≥ prior channel high AND Downtrend) OR (Fast momentum line < Slow momentum line AND Fast momentum line > 0 AND Downtrend) OR ((previous high − previous close) / previous close > knife percentage))
Short: selected trigger is Moving Average AND high > selected upper moving-average band AND (Downtrend OR Stochastic MACD crosses below its signal line)
Short: selected trigger is Fibonacci AND ((previous high ≥ upper band 1 AND current high ≥ upper band 2) OR (previous high ≥ upper band 1 AND close < upper band 2) OR (high ≥ upper band 1 AND BullShit) OR (high > upper band 2))
Short: selected trigger is Peaks and Valleys AND high ≥ prior channel high AND (Stochastic MACD crosses below its signal line OR BullShit)
Exit: Long, selected trigger's short condition OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + target-profit fraction)
Exit: Short, selected trigger's long condition OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − target-profit fraction)
