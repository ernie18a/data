Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose value is lower than the values of the N bars before and N bars after it; available after those N later bars complete. Use the same N for price lows and volume lows.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose value is higher than the values of the N bars before and N bars after it; available after those N later bars complete. Use the same N for price highs and volume highs.
Long: A confirmed price pivot low and confirmed volume pivot low occur at the same bar AND (that pivot's low is below the previous paired pivot low and its volume is above the previous paired pivot low's volume OR that pivot's low is above the previous paired pivot low and its volume is below the previous paired pivot low's volume)
Short: A confirmed price pivot high and confirmed volume pivot high occur at the same bar AND (that pivot's high is above the previous paired pivot high and its volume is below the previous paired pivot high's volume OR that pivot's high is below the previous paired pivot high and its volume is above the previous paired pivot high's volume)
