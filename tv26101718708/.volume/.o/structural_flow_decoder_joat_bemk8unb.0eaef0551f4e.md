Calculation: Main swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the same lookback bars on each side; retain the latest confirmed high and low separately. Nested swing high/low = pivot high/low calculated the same way with a shorter lookback than the main swings; retain the latest confirmed nested high and low separately.
Calculation: Structure state = initially bullish; switch to bearish when a confirmed main swing low is lower than the prior confirmed main swing low, and switch to bullish when a confirmed main swing high is higher than the prior confirmed main swing high.
Long: Confirmed main swing high > prior confirmed main swing high OR (structure state is bullish AND confirmed main swing low > prior confirmed main swing low) OR (structure state is bullish AND confirmed nested swing high > prior confirmed nested swing high) OR RSI crosses above 50 AND MACD histogram > 0
