Calculation: Range high/low = maximum/minimum of the latest five confirmed pivot highs/lows, respectively; if none exist, use the rolling lookback high/low. If range size is nonpositive, use the rolling lookback high/low. Range midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2; range size = range high − range low.
Calculation: Confirmed pivots = pivot highs/lows requiring the configured left and right bars; use only pivots confirmed by the current bar.
Calculation: HTF trend score (accumulation/distribution) = add 1 for each of: last completed higher-timeframe close below/above the fast EMA, below/above the slow EMA, and fast EMA below/above the slow EMA, respectively. The fast EMA uses a shorter period than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Structure score (accumulation/distribution) = for the two latest confirmed pivot lows, add 2 to accumulation if the latest is higher, or 1 to distribution if lower; for the two latest confirmed pivot highs, add 1 to accumulation if the latest is higher, or 2 to distribution if lower.
Calculation: Volume ratio = average volume on up bars divided by average volume on down bars over the range lookback (add a small epsilon to the denominator). Volume score: ratio above the high threshold adds 2 to accumulation; otherwise, above the lower accumulation threshold adds 1. Ratio below the low threshold adds 2 to distribution; otherwise, below the higher distribution threshold adds 1.
Calculation: Climax volume = volume greater than the volume moving average times the climax multiplier. If any of the current or prior ten bars is a climax down bar and current close is in the bottom 30% of the range, add 2 to accumulation volume score. If any is a climax up bar and current close is in the top 30%, add 2 to distribution volume score.
Calculation: VSA score (accumulation/distribution) = add 1 for each applicable condition: stopping volume (down bar, high volume, body/range > 0.5, close in upper half of bar) below range midpoint adds to accumulation; no demand (up bar, body/range < 0.4, low volume, close in top 30%) adds to distribution; no supply (down bar, body/range < 0.4, low volume, close in bottom 30%) adds to accumulation; test supply (high in top 5% of range but below range high, low volume) adds to distribution; test demand (low in bottom 5% but above range low, low volume) adds to accumulation; SOS (up bar, body/range > 0.7, high volume, close above midpoint) adds to accumulation; SOW (down bar, body/range > 0.7, high volume, close below midpoint) adds to distribution. High/low volume means volume above/below its moving average times the corresponding threshold multiplier.
Calculation: Position score = based on (close − range low) / range size: below 0.25 adds 2 to accumulation; otherwise below 0.4 adds 1. Above 0.75 adds 2 to distribution; otherwise above 0.6 adds 1.
Calculation: Accumulation total / distribution total = sum of the respective HTF trend, structure, volume, VSA, and position scores.
Long: Accumulation total ≥ trade threshold AND accumulation total > distribution total + 2
Long: Accumulation total ≥ weak threshold AND accumulation total > distribution total
Short: Distribution total ≥ trade threshold AND distribution total > accumulation total + 2
Short: Distribution total ≥ weak threshold AND distribution total > accumulation total
