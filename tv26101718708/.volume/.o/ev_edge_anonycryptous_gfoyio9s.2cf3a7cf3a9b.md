Calculation: Consolidation = rolling highest high minus rolling lowest low over a lookback is less than ATR times that lookback times a compression factor, and this compression condition has held for the required consecutive bars; evaluate the breakout against the last completed bar’s consolidation.
Calculation: Prior impulse = close at the consolidation lookback ago minus low from the consolidation lookback plus impulse lookback ago; positive is upward, negative is downward.
Calculation: VW RSI = RSI of close changes weighted by current volume divided by its moving average; MFI = Money Flow Index from typical price times volume.
Calculation: EV score = start at 60; each tracked bar, add 0.3 times the weighted blend of price delta and optional volume delta, clamped to 0–100. Price delta is favorable close drift from entry divided by ATR times 8, less 15 plus 10 times retracement depth in ATR if close retraces into/past the consolidation. Volume delta is (volume/current volume moving average − 1) times 10, less 5 when absolute drift in ATR is below 0.1 and volume ratio exceeds 1.2. Use configured price and volume weights; with volume disabled, use price delta alone.
Long: flat AND previous bar was in consolidation AND close > previous consolidation high + ATR × breakout buffer AND (impulse alignment not required OR prior impulse > 0) AND (confirmation filter disabled OR every enabled VW RSI/MFI confirmation is above its midline).
Short: flat AND previous bar was in consolidation AND close < previous consolidation low − ATR × breakout buffer AND (impulse alignment not required OR prior impulse < 0) AND (confirmation filter disabled OR every enabled VW RSI/MFI confirmation is below its midline).
Exit: long or short, (bars held >= minimum bars before early exit AND (EV score >= high exit threshold OR EV score <= low exit threshold)) OR bars held >= maximum bars to track
