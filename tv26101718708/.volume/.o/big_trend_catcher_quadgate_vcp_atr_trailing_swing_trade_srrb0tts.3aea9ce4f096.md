Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band; quiet zone = current width below its own longer-lookback SMA.
Calculation: Smoothed ROC = EMA of percentage change in close over a longer lookback; the EMA smoothing lookback is shorter.
Calculation: Long EMA slope = current long EMA minus its value several bars earlier.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = a ratcheting stop based on the rolling highest value of high minus an ATR multiple; update to that value when close is above the prior stop and prior close, otherwise retain the prior stop, resetting to the rolling value when close falls below the stop.
Long: While flat, a pending signal exists within its confirmation window; close is above both short and long EMAs; smoothed ROC is nonnegative; long EMA slope is nonnegative; and close is above the ATR trailing stop. Create the pending signal when volume exceeds its longer-lookback SMA by a threshold and either (close crosses above the short EMA or close exceeds the prior bar's high while above the short EMA) AND (quiet zone or trend death occurred within the recovery window). Cancel it when close crosses below the short EMA or the signal exceeds its confirmation window.
Long: While long, add when gain from average entry exceeds a threshold, close is above both EMAs, long EMA slope is nonnegative, and close is above the ATR trailing stop; OR when low crosses below the short EMA while close remains above both EMAs, long EMA slope is nonnegative, and close is above the ATR trailing stop.
Long: A pending signal exists and at least one entry gate is unmet: smoothed ROC is negative, long EMA slope is negative, or close is at or below the ATR trailing stop.
Exit: Long, close is below the ATR trailing stop OR close crosses below the long EMA.
