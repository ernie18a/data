Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR / SMA(ATR); adaptive stop multiplier = low-volatility multiplier when ATR ratio < its lower boundary, high-volatility multiplier when above its upper boundary, otherwise medium-volatility multiplier
Calculation: Bull score = sum of configured pillar weights earned: structure weight if a confirmed higher high or higher low exists and close > latest confirmed pivot low; OBV weight if 20-bar OBV linear-regression slope > 0; momentum weight × fraction of close > KAMA, RSI > 50, and Williams %R > −40; liquidity weight if low < latest confirmed pivot low and close > that pivot low; volatility weight if ATR / SMA(ATR) is within its configured productive range; FER weight if fractal efficiency ratio meets its minimum
Calculation: Bear score = sum of configured pillar weights earned: structure weight if a confirmed lower low or lower high exists and close < latest confirmed pivot high; OBV weight if 20-bar OBV linear-regression slope < 0; momentum weight × fraction of close < KAMA, RSI < 50, and Williams %R < −60; liquidity weight if high > latest confirmed pivot high and close < that pivot high; volatility weight if ATR / SMA(ATR) is within its configured productive range; FER weight if fractal efficiency ratio meets its minimum
Calculation: Fractal efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Trending regime = ADX > its threshold; bullish regime if trending and close > EMA(50), bearish regime if trending and close < EMA(50)
Calculation: Adaptive trailing stop = close − ATR × adaptive stop multiplier for long positions; close + ATR × adaptive stop multiplier for short positions; ratchet only in the favorable direction, and after the first target is reached keep the stop at least at entry price
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR × adaptive stop multiplier; targets = entry-signal close ± initial stop distance × configured target multiples
Long: confirmed bar AND bullish regime AND close > VWMA AND fast HMA > slow HMA AND close > EMA(50) on the configured higher timeframe using lookahead off (latest completed higher-timeframe bar) AND bull score ≥ minimum score AND bull score − bear score ≥ minimum directional lead AND (long setup was false on the prior bar OR position is flat AND bull score ≥ minimum score + configured re-entry margin)
Short: confirmed bar AND bearish regime AND close < VWMA AND fast HMA < slow HMA AND close < EMA(50) on the configured higher timeframe using lookahead off (latest completed higher-timeframe bar) AND bear score ≥ minimum score AND bear score − bull score ≥ minimum directional lead AND (short setup was false on the prior bar OR position is flat AND bear score ≥ minimum score + configured re-entry margin)
Exit: long, low ≤ ratcheted stop OR high ≥ first target (close 50%) OR high ≥ second target (close remaining position) OR bars held ≥ configured maximum
Exit: short, high ≥ ratcheted stop OR low ≤ first target (close 50%) OR low ≤ second target (close remaining position) OR bars held ≥ configured maximum
