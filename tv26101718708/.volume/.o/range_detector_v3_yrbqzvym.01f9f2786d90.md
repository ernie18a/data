Calculation: Bandwidth percentile = (current Bollinger bandwidth − lowest bandwidth over the last 100 bars) / (highest bandwidth over the last 100 bars − lowest bandwidth over the last 100 bars); use 0.5 if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Range factor score = count of these five conditions: ADX below its ceiling; Choppiness Index above its floor; Bandwidth percentile below its ceiling; Bollinger Bands inside the Keltner Channel; absolute linear regression slope divided by ATR below its ceiling.
Calculation: Active range = high and low extremes from the first bar of a candidate range through the current bar. A candidate starts when the factor score reaches the required count, continues only while it does, and becomes confirmed after the required consecutive qualifying bars and minimum range length. It is reset if qualification fails, after a breakout, or when its age exceeds the maximum range length. On each confirmed-range bar, extend the upper rail to the high if it exceeds the rail by less than 0.35 ATR, and extend the lower rail to the low if it falls below the rail by less than 0.35 ATR; then evaluate breakout conditions against the resulting rails.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / 50-bar average volume; use 1 if the average is zero.
Long: Active confirmed range AND current high > upper rail AND current close > upper rail AND absolute(close − open) ≥ displacement threshold × ATR AND relative volume ≥ participation threshold AND close > open.
Short: Active confirmed range AND current low < lower rail AND current close < lower rail AND absolute(close − open) ≥ displacement threshold × ATR AND relative volume ≥ participation threshold AND close < open.
Long: A bullish range breakout occurred 2 to the retest lookback number of bars ago AND current low ≤ its broken upper rail AND current close > that rail AND close > open.
Short: A bearish range breakout occurred 2 to the retest lookback number of bars ago AND current high ≥ its broken lower rail AND current close < that rail AND close < open.
