Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range session; freeze when that session ends.
Calculation: Entry VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 from the configured anchor, accumulated through the current bar; the anchor is either the regular-session open or overnight-session open.
Calculation: Positioning score = sum of +1 when close is above, −1 when below, and 0 when equal to or unavailable for each of the regular-session VWAP, overnight VWAP, previous-session VWAP, high-of-day VWAP, low-of-day VWAP, and opening range (above its high = +1, below its low = −1, otherwise 0).
Calculation: Correlated-ticker positioning score = the same positioning score calculated for each configured correlated ticker.
Calculation: Previous-day ATR = ATR of the configured length on completed daily bars only.
Calculation: Initial stop and target = configured stop and target levels from the entry price. Stop choices: fixed distance, multiple of previous-day ATR, entry VWAP, opposite opening-range boundary, or recent swing extreme over the configured lookback; target choices: configured multiple of initial risk, opening-range size multiple, fixed distance, or none.
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop, moved to entry price after price reaches 1 initial-risk unit in profit when breakeven is enabled; when trailing is enabled, once best price since entry reaches the configured activation multiple of initial risk, ratchet the stop to the configured distance behind that best price, only tightening it.
Long: opening range is complete AND a bar has closed above its high since completion AND current close > entry VWAP AND current low <= entry VWAP plus configured entry offset AND within configured entry time window AND weekday/month and trade-count gates pass AND (opening-range size gate is disabled OR size is within its configured point or previous-day-ATR band) AND (positioning-score gate is disabled OR positioning score >= configured threshold) AND correlated-ticker gate passes if enabled AND configured direction allows longs AND initial stop is below entry price AND stop-distance limits pass AND position size is at least one contract.
Short: opening range is complete AND a bar has closed below its low since completion AND current close < entry VWAP AND current high >= entry VWAP minus configured entry offset AND within configured entry time window AND weekday/month and trade-count gates pass AND configured short-side time, month, and weekday exclusions pass AND (opening-range size gate is disabled OR size is within its configured point or previous-day-ATR band) AND (positioning-score gate is disabled OR positioning score <= negative configured threshold) AND correlated-ticker gate passes if enabled AND configured direction allows shorts AND initial stop is above entry price AND stop-distance limits pass AND position size is at least one contract.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR (fixed target is active AND high >= target) OR configured end-of-session flat time is reached while holding a position.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR (fixed target is active AND low <= target) OR configured end-of-session flat time is reached while holding a position.
