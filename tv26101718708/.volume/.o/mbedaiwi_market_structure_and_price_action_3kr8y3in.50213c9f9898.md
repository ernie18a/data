Calculation: Internal pivots = confirmed structure pivots using a shorter lookback than swing pivots; swing pivots use a longer lookback. A pivot is confirmed only after the required bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = a confirmed close above the latest unbroken pivot high; bearish structure break = a confirmed close below the latest unbroken pivot low. A break continuing the prior structure trend is BOS; a break against it is CHoCH. CHoCH+ additionally requires the intervening confirmed higher low for a bullish change or lower high for a bearish change.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = bullish candle whose body exceeds both a multiple of average body size and a multiple of ATR, with close in the upper quarter of its range; bearish displacement is the reverse. Recent displacement means one occurred within a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity grab = price penetrates a previously confirmed pivot low and the candle body closes above it, after the prior close was at or above it; bearish grab reverses these comparisons. Apply the configured minimum ATR penetration and rejection-wick fraction. A grab level is consumed at its first penetration, even if rejection filters fail.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = last completed higher-timeframe close compared with its 50-period EMA on that timeframe.
Calculation: EMA alignment = short-period EMA > medium-period EMA and close > long-period EMA for bullish alignment; reverse all comparisons for bearish alignment.
Calculation: Volume filter = volume > its moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: RSI filter = RSI lies within the configured long range or short range, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = a qualifying upward gap between the current and two-bars-prior ranges, or the configured alternative imbalance type; bearish imbalance is the reverse. For a higher source timeframe, use completed source bars.
Calculation: Bullish order-block interaction = current bar range overlaps an active, unmitigated bullish order-block zone; bearish interaction uses an active bearish zone. FVG interaction is defined the same way for active gaps.
Calculation: Fibonacci impulse levels = retracement and extension levels calculated from the latest confirmed swing structure break and its impulse low/high; update the impulse extreme as price extends. Discount is close at or below the 0.5 retracement for a bullish impulse, and at or above it for a bearish impulse. OTE is close between the 0.618 and 0.786 retracements.
Calculation: Confluence score = configurable weighted sum of matching conditions: swing and internal trend, higher-timeframe trend, recent BOS/CHoCH, recent liquidity grab, order-block interaction, FVG interaction, OTE alignment, recent displacement, volume filter, and EMA alignment.
Calculation: Planned entry, stop, and targets = levels from the selected market, Fibonacci, or zone entry method; structure, ATR, or zone stop method; and risk-multiple, swing/liquidity/Fibonacci, or hybrid target method. Risk is the distance from entry to stop. After target 1, the stop may move to entry.
Long: confirmed bullish internal or swing BOS/CHoCH; OR confirmed bullish trendline break (close above a descending line through confirmed swing highs); OR bullish liquidity grab; OR higher-high or higher-low confirmed pivot; OR qualifying bullish imbalance formation; OR (configured Strict setup: bullish swing trend AND recent bullish structure event AND recent bullish displacement AND bullish impulse AND discount AND OTE AND bullish order-block or FVG interaction AND recent bullish liquidity grab AND (higher-timeframe trend bullish if enabled) AND bullish candle AND all enabled filters); OR (configured Score setup: bullish score >= configured minimum AND bullish impulse AND OTE AND bullish candle AND all enabled filters).
Short: confirmed bearish internal or swing BOS/CHoCH; OR confirmed bearish trendline break (close below an ascending line through confirmed swing lows); OR bearish liquidity grab; OR lower-high or lower-low confirmed pivot; OR qualifying bearish imbalance formation; OR (configured Strict setup: bearish swing trend AND recent bearish structure event AND recent bearish displacement AND bearish impulse AND premium AND OTE AND bearish order-block or FVG interaction AND recent bearish liquidity grab AND (higher-timeframe trend bearish if enabled) AND bearish candle AND all enabled filters); OR (configured Score setup: bearish score >= configured minimum AND bearish impulse AND OTE AND bearish candle AND all enabled filters).
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= target 3 after targets 1 and 2 have been reached OR bearish swing BOS/CHoCH.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= target 3 after targets 1 and 2 have been reached OR bullish swing BOS/CHoCH.
