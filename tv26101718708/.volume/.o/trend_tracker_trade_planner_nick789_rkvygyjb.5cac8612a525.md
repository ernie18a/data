Calculation: Trend direction = Use a fast EMA of OHLC4 as the basis in scalp mode; in trend mode use the average of fast and slow EMAs. Add/subtract ATR times a multiplier for upper/lower bands. Initialize bullish; thereafter turn bullish when OHLC4 exceeds the prior upper band, bearish when it falls below the prior lower band, otherwise retain prior direction. In bullish state, tracker = max(lower band, prior tracker if prior state was bullish, otherwise lower band); in bearish state, tracker = min(upper band, prior tracker if prior state was bearish, otherwise upper band).
Calculation: Trend delta = cumulative volume of up-close candles minus cumulative volume of down-close candles since the latest trend-direction change; an optional lower-timeframe source sums the same candle volumes from its intrabars.
Long: Trend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (MTF filter disabled OR selected-timeframe tracker direction is bullish); OR a previously blocked bullish flip is still active, the selected-timeframe filter changes from failing to passing, and re-entry is enabled; OR a pending bullish signal remains within its wait limit, trend direction is bullish, and trend delta is nonnegative.
Short: Trend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (MTF filter disabled OR selected-timeframe tracker direction is bearish); OR a previously blocked bearish flip is still active, the selected-timeframe filter changes from failing to passing, and re-entry is enabled; OR a pending bearish signal remains within its wait limit, trend direction is bearish, and trend delta is nonpositive.
Exit: Long, high >= any take-profit level or low <= stop level.
Exit: Short, low <= any take-profit level or high >= stop level.
