Calculation: Configured moving average = selected SMA, WMA, or VWMA of the selected price source; each fast lookback is shorter than its paired slow lookback.
Calculation: Profit target = entry-signal bar close + configurable ATR multiple × ATR; hold fixed during the position.
Calculation: Loss target = entry-signal bar close − configurable ATR multiple × ATR; hold fixed during the position.
Long: Position is flat AND every configured fast moving average is above its paired slow moving average AND profit target > 0
Exit: long, at least one configured fast moving average is below its paired slow moving average AND (high > profit target OR low < loss target)
