Calculation: VWIR Z-score = (raw VWIR − its rolling mean over the normalization lookback) / its rolling standard deviation over that lookback, using 1 if the standard deviation is 0; raw VWIR = information clarity × current volume / its rolling average over the entropy lookback × current log return; information clarity = 1 − Shannon entropy of log returns over the entropy lookback / log₂(number of entropy bins). For entropy, divide the range from the minimum to maximum log return into the specified number of equal-width bins (minimum width 0.000001), count returns in each bin including its lower edge and excluding its upper edge, divide each count by the lookback, and sum −p × log₂(p) for nonzero probabilities.
Long: current VWIR Z-score > upper threshold AND previous VWIR Z-score ≤ upper threshold
Short: current VWIR Z-score < negative upper threshold AND previous VWIR Z-score ≥ negative upper threshold
