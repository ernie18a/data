Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a bar whose high (resistance pivot) or low (support pivot) is an extreme over the configured left and right lookbacks; it becomes available after the right lookback completes.
Calculation: Pivot weight = (1 + volume relative to its moving-average baseline + rejection-wick length relative to ATR) × an age-decay factor; high-pivot wick = high − max(open, close), low-pivot wick = min(open, close) − low.
Calculation: Support/resistance zones = separately cluster stored low/high pivots in chronological order: seed each unused pivot, add later unused pivots within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the seed price only when each is at least the configured spacing from every cluster member; retain clusters meeting the minimum-touch threshold. Zone center is the pivot-weighted mean price, bounds are centered on it with ATR-scaled padding, strength is summed pivot weight, and volume is summed pivot volume. Keep the strongest configured number per side. A zone is active until a bar closes beyond its outer bound; rebuild clusters when a new pivot confirms, retaining matching active-zone state and excluding previously broken levels.
Calculation: Live broken zone = an active resistance zone closed above its top with the previous close at or below its top, or an active support zone closed below its bottom with the previous close at or above its bottom.
Calculation: Same-zone cooldown = suppress a successful-test signal when a prior such signal occurred within the configured cooldown for a zone within ATR-scaled cluster tolerance.
Long: For any active support zone, low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom AND close >= open AND same-zone cooldown has elapsed; OR for any broken resistance zone, bars since break > 0 AND bars since break <= configured retest window AND low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom AND close >= open; OR for any active support zone, low <= zone top AND close > zone bottom; OR a live resistance break.
Short: For any active resistance zone, high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND close <= open AND same-zone cooldown has elapsed; OR for any broken support zone, bars since break > 0 AND bars since break <= configured retest window AND high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND close <= open; OR for any active resistance zone, high >= zone bottom AND close < zone top; OR a live support break.
