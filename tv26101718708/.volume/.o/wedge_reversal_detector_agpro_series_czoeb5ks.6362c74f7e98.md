Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low greater/less than the bars on each side over the pivot confirmation lookback; use the pivot bar’s index and value.
Calculation: Wedge rails = lines through a pair of confirmed pivot highs and a pair of confirmed pivot lows whose pivot intervals overlap; require the latest defining pivot to be within the candidate-age limit. A rising wedge has both slopes positive and the lower slope greater than the upper slope; a falling wedge has both slopes negative and the upper slope less than the lower slope. Require positive start and end widths, start width at least the minimum ATR multiple, end/start width at most the maximum ratio, midline travel at least the minimum ATR multiple, and at least one additional stored pivot within the ATR tolerance of either rail. Require minimum span and price within the rails with the ATR tolerance. Among qualifying candidates, choose strict over non-strict, then greatest maturity, then most recent end; a strict wedge additionally meets the stricter span, start-width, end-width-ratio, travel, and extra-touch thresholds. Maturity combines capped compression and span scores.
Calculation: Break boundary = active wedge’s upper rail for a falling wedge and lower rail for a rising wedge, evaluated on the current bar.
Calculation: Break buffer = ATR times the configured break-buffer multiple.
Calculation: Invalidation level = opposite active-wedge rail at the break bar, offset outward by ATR times the configured invalidation-buffer multiple; freeze at break.
Calculation: Target zone = break close offset in the break direction by the initial wedge width times the configured target multiple, with half-width equal to ATR times the configured zone-width multiple; freeze at break.
Long: Confirmed strict active falling wedge, after its latest defining pivot, not already broken or invalidated, break cooldown elapsed, and close above upper rail plus buffer (or high above it if close confirmation is disabled).
Short: Confirmed strict active rising wedge, after its latest defining pivot, not already broken or invalidated, break cooldown elapsed, and close below lower rail minus buffer (or low below it if close confirmation is disabled).
Exit: Long, close below the frozen invalidation level OR high reaches the lower edge of the target zone OR the configured projection window expires.
Exit: Short, close above the frozen invalidation level OR low reaches the upper edge of the target zone OR the configured projection window expires.
