Calculation: Upper gate = highest high of the preceding structure lookback bars; lower gate = lowest low of those bars.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; relative volume = volume / moving average of volume; body commitment = |close − open| / max(high − low, minimum tick) × 100; close location = clamp((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) × 100, 0, 100).
Calculation: Breakout quality = clamp(0.32 × volume credit + 0.27 × body credit + 0.18 × direction-adjusted close location + 0.13 × range credit + 0.10 × trend credit, 0, 100), where volume credit = clamp(relative volume / minimum breakout relative volume × 100, 0, 150), body credit = clamp(body commitment / minimum body commitment × 100, 0, 140), range credit = clamp(max(high − low, minimum tick) / ATR × 55, 0, 120), and trend credit is 100 when close is on the breakout side of its EMA and 35 otherwise. Direction-adjusted close location is close location for an upside break and 100 − close location for a downside break.
Calculation: Active gate = the gate broken by the latest confirmed upside or downside break, with its direction, breakout quality, breakout bar, and shelf boundaries (gate ± breakout-bar ATR × retest shelf width) stored until replaced by another break.
Calculation: Retest window = bars since the active break is greater than zero and no greater than the retest evaluation window; retest touch = within that window and the current bar's range overlaps the stored shelf.
Calculation: Retest quality = clamp(0.50 × active breakout quality + 0.20 × volume credit + 0.15 × body credit + 0.15 × (100 − min(bars since break / retest evaluation window × 100, 100)), 0, 100), calculated on a retest touch.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close > upper gate + ATR × breakout close buffer OR an active upside gate has a retest hold (within the retest window, retest touch, close > active gate, close > open, retest quality ≥ minimum retest quality, and no retest hold already recorded since the latest break) OR an active upside gate expands (within the retest window, close > active gate + ATR × expansion distance, relative volume ≥ 0.85 × minimum breakout relative volume, and no expansion already recorded since the latest break).
Short: On a confirmed bar, close < lower gate − ATR × breakout close buffer OR an active downside gate has a retest hold (within the retest window, retest touch, close < active gate, close < open, retest quality ≥ minimum retest quality, and no retest hold already recorded since the latest break) OR an active downside gate expands (within the retest window, close < active gate − ATR × expansion distance, relative volume ≥ 0.85 × minimum breakout relative volume, and no expansion already recorded since the latest break).
Exit: long, an active upside gate is within the retest window and close < active gate − ATR × fakeout distance, with no fakeout already recorded since the latest break.
Exit: short, an active downside gate is within the retest window and close > active gate + ATR × fakeout distance, with no fakeout already recorded since the latest break.
