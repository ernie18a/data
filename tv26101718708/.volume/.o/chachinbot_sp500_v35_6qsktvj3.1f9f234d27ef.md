Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) / (high − low)
Long: Arm a setup when close crosses above the fast EMA, fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA of the medium EMA length, RSI > 50 and < its overbought threshold, volume > its longer-lookback volume average × its minimum multiplier, ADX ≥ its threshold when enabled, and the bar is inside the configured session and outside the configured lunch interval when those filters are enabled. Signal while that setup remains armed once low ≤ fast EMA + ATR × pullback depth has occurred and close > open, close > fast EMA, and candle body percentage ≥ its minimum; cancel after a signal, when the EMA stack breaks, when close > fast EMA + 2 × ATR before a pullback, or after the configured pullback window expires.
