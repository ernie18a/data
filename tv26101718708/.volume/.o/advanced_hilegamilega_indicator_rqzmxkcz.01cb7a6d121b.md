Calculation: Short RSI average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or VWMA of RSI over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Long RSI average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or VWMA of RSI over a longer lookback than the short RSI average
Calculation: Price average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or VWMA of close
Long: Within the configured test interval AND ((RSI crosses above the long RSI average) OR (short RSI average crosses above the long RSI average) OR (RSI and short RSI average both cross above the long RSI average on the same bar) OR (long RSI average crosses above the RSI midline) OR (RSI crosses above the RSI midline) OR (RSI is below the oversold level now or on any of the prior five bars AND RSI crosses above the long RSI average) OR (RSI crosses above the oversold level) OR (close crosses above the price average AND RSI crosses above the long RSI average))
Exit: long, (Within the configured test interval AND ((RSI crosses below the long RSI average) OR (short RSI average crosses below the long RSI average) OR (RSI and short RSI average both cross below the long RSI average on the same bar) OR (long RSI average crosses below the RSI midline) OR (RSI crosses below the RSI midline) OR (RSI is above the overbought level now or on any of the prior five bars AND short RSI average crosses below the long RSI average) OR (RSI crosses below the overbought level) OR (RSI crosses below the oversold level) OR (close crosses below the price average AND RSI crosses below the long RSI average))) OR (low reaches the stop price set at entry price times one minus the configured stop percentage) OR (high reaches the target price set at entry price times one plus the configured target percentage) OR (datetime is outside the configured test interval)
