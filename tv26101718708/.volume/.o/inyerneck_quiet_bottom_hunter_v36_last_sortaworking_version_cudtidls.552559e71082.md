Calculation: Prior-bar highest high = highest high over the selected lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Drop from prior-bar high = (close − prior-bar highest high) / prior-bar highest high
Calculation: Green/flat streak = consecutive bars ending on the current bar where close ≥ open
Long: Drop from prior-bar high ≤ selected maximum drop AND volume ≤ selected fraction of its 50-bar average AND RSI( selected period ) ≤ selected maximum AND green/flat streak ≥ selected minimum
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + selected profit-target tick distance OR low ≤ entry price − selected stop-loss tick distance
