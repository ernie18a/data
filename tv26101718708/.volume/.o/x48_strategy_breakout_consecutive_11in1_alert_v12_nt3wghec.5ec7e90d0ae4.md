Calculation: Fast and slow moving averages = independently selected moving-average types of close; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: MACD histogram = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback), minus an EMA of that difference; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: ATR trailing stops = for each stop, use close ± (ATR × multiplier); when close and prior close are above the prior stop, raise the stop to max(prior stop, close − ATR × multiplier); when both are below, lower it to min(prior stop, close + ATR × multiplier); otherwise use close minus ATR × multiplier if close is above the prior stop, or close plus ATR × multiplier if not. The fast stop uses a shorter ATR lookback and lower multiplier than the slow stop.
Calculation: Tenkan and Kijun lines = for each line, set upper = hl2 + ATR(lookback) × multiplier and lower = hl2 − ATR(lookback) × multiplier; carry upper as min(current upper, prior upper) when prior close is below prior upper, otherwise use current upper; carry lower as max(current lower, prior lower) when prior close is above prior lower, otherwise use current lower. Set state to 1 when close exceeds upper, 0 when close falls below lower, otherwise keep prior state. Set support to lower in state 1, otherwise upper; track the running maximum and minimum of close, resetting both to close when close crosses support; the line is their average. Tenkan uses a shorter lookback than Kijun.
Long: high > highest high of the preceding breakout lookback bars OR close has risen for at least the configured consecutive-bar count OR (high − low) > (range multiplier × standard deviation of high − low over the range lookback + its simple moving average over that lookback) AND close > close[direction lookback] OR fast moving average crosses above slow moving average OR fast moving average > slow moving average in repeat mode OR MACD histogram crosses above zero OR MACD histogram > 0 in repeat mode OR Tenkan crosses above Kijun OR Tenkan > Kijun in repeat mode OR Aroon-Up crosses above Aroon-Down OR Aroon-Up > Aroon-Down OR (when used as confirmation) RSI <= its overbought threshold.
Short: low < lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback bars OR close has fallen for at least the configured consecutive-bar count OR fast moving average crosses below slow moving average OR fast moving average < slow moving average in repeat mode OR MACD histogram crosses below zero OR MACD histogram < 0 in repeat mode OR Tenkan crosses below Kijun OR Tenkan < Kijun in repeat mode OR Aroon-Up crosses below Aroon-Down OR Aroon-Up < Aroon-Down OR (when used as confirmation) RSI >= its oversold threshold.
Exit: Long, close < most recent confirmed pivot low (pivot requires left- and right-side bars, then one additional bar to confirm) OR close < entry price − configured stop-loss offset OR close > entry price + configured take-profit offset OR fast ATR trailing stop crosses below slow ATR trailing stop OR (callback mode) close <= highest high of the breakout lookback bars × (1 − callback percentage) OR the still-forming 1-minute close <= entry price OR (range-break short condition) range condition is true AND close < close[direction lookback].
Exit: Short, close > most recent confirmed pivot high (pivot requires left- and right-side bars, then one additional bar to confirm) OR close > entry price + configured stop-loss offset OR close < entry price − configured take-profit offset OR fast ATR trailing stop crosses above slow ATR trailing stop OR (callback mode) close >= lowest low of the breakout lookback bars × (1 + callback percentage) OR the still-forming 1-minute close >= entry price.
