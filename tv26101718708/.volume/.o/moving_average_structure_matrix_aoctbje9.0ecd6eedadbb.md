Calculation: Selected source = one of open, high, low, close, (high+low)/2, (high+low+close)/3, (open+high+low+close)/4, or (high+low+2×close)/4.
Calculation: MA bundle = the enabled configured moving averages of the selected source; bundle high/low = their maximum/minimum; centroid = their mean.
Calculation: Normalized distance = distance divided by the configured ATR, absolute centroid, or rolling source standard deviation; for percentage mode multiply by 100.
Calculation: Pair order = weighted mean of eligible pair votes × 100, where each vote is +1 if the faster MA exceeds the slower MA by more than the configured normalized separation, −1 if it is below by more than that separation, otherwise 0; pair weights use the configured horizon-distance weighting. Skip equal-horizon pairs.
Calculation: Slope coherence = 100 × sum of enabled MAs’ normalized changes over the slope lookback divided by the sum of their absolute normalized changes.
Calculation: Cohort spread = normalized difference between the mean fast-cohort MA and mean slow-cohort MA; cohort membership follows the configured rank/length split.
Calculation: Bias = sign of 0.45 × pair order + 0.30 × slope coherence + 0.25 × clamp(100 × cohort spread / configured cohort scale, −100, 100), only when its absolute value reaches the configured bias threshold; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Directional-cycle release/expansion = a confirmed entry into the corresponding local phase, with phase age meeting the configured minimum. Release requires a ready engine, nonzero bias, compression within the configured origin lookback (not on the current bar), bias-aligned pair order above its release threshold, normalized bundle width expansion above its threshold (and geometric width expansion above that threshold when required), and bias-aligned centroid acceleration above its threshold. Expansion requires a ready engine, nonzero bias, bias-aligned pair order and slope coherence above their thresholds, close on the bias side of the centroid, and neither release nor pullback phase.
Calculation: First touch = while a directional cycle is active and untouched, current low <= previous completed chart bar’s bundle high AND current high >= its bundle low, with current bias still matching the cycle direction.
Calculation: Reclaim = after a first touch on an earlier bar and before another reclaim in that cycle, close crosses the selected boundary (default: bundle high/low frozen at touch; alternative: previous completed chart bar’s bundle high/low), with configured tick clearance; current bias matches the cycle direction and pair order × cycle direction meets the configured pullback-order floor.
Long: Directional-cycle release with positive bias OR directional-cycle expansion with positive bias OR first touch in an active positive-bias cycle OR reclaim in a positive-bias cycle.
Short: Directional-cycle release with negative bias OR directional-cycle expansion with negative bias OR first touch in an active negative-bias cycle OR reclaim in a negative-bias cycle.
