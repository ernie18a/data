Calculation: Regular bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high while RSI at the pivot is lower; regular bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low while RSI at the pivot is higher. Divergence strength = rounded sum of 50 × min(RSI difference / 20, 1) and 50 × min(relative pivot-price difference / 0.02, 1).
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed pivot high while its RSI is higher; hidden bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed pivot low while its RSI is lower.
Calculation: Reversal extension = the average of the signed distance from the EMA mean and signed distance from the session VWAP, each divided by ATR; top extension is positive above both means, bottom extension is positive below both means.
Calculation: Early-warning confidence = 100 × the sum of weights for currently true enabled top or bottom factors divided by the sum of weights for all enabled early-warning factors. Factors: RSI extreme, volume above its moving average times a multiplier, current high/low piercing the corresponding Bollinger band, extension beyond its threshold, Stoch-RSI extreme, wick rejection, higher-timeframe RSI extreme, and liquidity sweep. A top sweep takes out the previous lookback high and closes below its high; a bottom sweep takes out the previous lookback low and closes above its low. Wick rejection means the corresponding wick is at least a parameterized multiple of the body and at least as long as the opposite wick.
Calculation: Confirmed confidence = 100 × clamp((factor-weight sum + regime boost − opposing-regime penalty − hidden-divergence penalty) / (sum of enabled factor weights + regime weight), 0, 1). Factor votes use the pivot bar: RSI extreme, RSI divergence, volume climax, Bollinger pierce, extension, Stoch-RSI extreme, wick rejection, engulfing candle, higher-timeframe RSI extreme, and liquidity sweep. Top factors use overbought/high conditions and bottom factors use oversold/low conditions; engulfing is bearish for tops and bullish for bottoms. Regime boost applies when trend direction agrees with the reversal, and penalty when it opposes; magnitude is greater in a strong trend. Strong trend uses efficiency ratio at or above its threshold. Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback. Trend up/down requires fast EMA above/below slow EMA and extension EMA above/below its value at the slope lookback. Hidden divergence penalizes the corresponding reversal score. Factor and regime weights and thresholds are parameters.
Long: Early-warning bottom confidence crosses up through its threshold on the current forming bar.
Long: A pivot low confirms after the required right-side bars AND bottom confidence meets its threshold AND bottom factor votes meet their minimum AND the bars since the prior confirmed bottom signal meet the cooldown.
Short: Early-warning top confidence crosses up through its threshold on the current forming bar.
Short: A pivot high confirms after the required right-side bars AND top confidence meets its threshold AND top factor votes meet their minimum AND the bars since the prior confirmed top signal meet the cooldown.
Exit: Long, close < pivot low − hard-invalidation ATR distance OR low <= target.
Exit: Short, close > pivot high + hard-invalidation ATR distance OR high >= target.
Calculation: Exit target = the nearest eligible opposing level between signal close and the fallback target: for a long, the lowest of EMA mean, session VWAP, and last confirmed pivot high that is above signal close; for a short, the highest of EMA mean, session VWAP, and last confirmed pivot low that is below signal close. If none is eligible, use signal close plus/minus two times hard-invalidation risk. The hard-invalidation level is the confirmed pivot low/high minus/plus the hard-invalidation ATR distance. Higher-timeframe RSI and EMA direction use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Session VWAP uses the current session; other indicators use chart bars. Confirmed factor values are sampled at the pivot bar.
