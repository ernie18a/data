Calculation: Blended oscillator = weighted average of CCI (weight 1), normalized RSI, normalized Stoch RSI K, and normalized Fisher; omit disabled components. Normalize RSI as (RSI − 50) / (RSI overbought − 50) × upper level; normalize Stoch RSI K as (K − 50) / 50 × upper level; normalize Fisher as Fisher × upper level / Fisher overbought.
Calculation: Stoch RSI K = smooth the stochastic of RSI: (RSI − lowest RSI over the Stoch RSI lookback) / (highest RSI over that lookback − lowest RSI over that lookback) × 100; use 50 if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Fisher = 0.5 × ln((1 + clamped value) / (1 − clamped value)) + 0.5 × previous Fisher, where value = 2 × (close − lowest low over the Fisher lookback) / (highest high over that lookback − lowest low over that lookback) − 1, clamped to [−0.999, 0.999]; use 0 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized to (open + close) / 2.
Long: Blended oscillator ≥ its signal moving average AND (blended oscillator crosses above zero OR RSI crosses above its oversold level OR Stoch RSI K crosses above its oversold level OR Fisher crosses above its oversold level); OR blended oscillator > upper level AND blended oscillator ≥ its signal moving average; OR blended oscillator ≥ its signal moving average; OR blended oscillator > 0; OR Heikin-Ashi close ≥ Heikin-Ashi open; OR a confirmed pivot low has price lower than the previous pivot low and blended oscillator higher than the previous pivot low by more than the divergence threshold; OR a confirmed pivot low has price lower than the previous pivot low and RSI higher than the previous pivot low by more than the RSI divergence threshold.
Short: Blended oscillator < its signal moving average AND (blended oscillator crosses below zero OR RSI crosses below its overbought level OR Stoch RSI K crosses below its overbought level OR Fisher crosses below its overbought level); OR blended oscillator < lower level AND blended oscillator < its signal moving average; OR blended oscillator < its signal moving average; OR blended oscillator ≤ 0; OR Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open; OR a confirmed pivot high has price higher than the previous pivot high and blended oscillator lower than the previous pivot high by more than the divergence threshold; OR a confirmed pivot high has price higher than the previous pivot high and RSI lower than the previous pivot high by more than the RSI divergence threshold.
