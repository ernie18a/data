Calculation: Smoothed ATR = an EMA-smoothed ATR calculated from chart bars.
Calculation: Demand and supply zones = cluster confirmed pivot lows and pivot highs, respectively, within the analysis lookback when their prices are within an ATR-scaled tolerance; use the cluster’s average price and its lowest pivot low or highest pivot high as its extreme. Require at least two pivots. Use a shorter pivot lookback for trendline pivots than for zone pivots.
Calculation: Trendline confluence = count rising lines through pairs of pivot lows for demand, or falling lines through pairs of pivot highs for supply, that pass within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the zone price and receive at least the required number of touches.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted combination of logarithmically normalized cluster touches, capped trendline confluence, recency of the latest pivot, cluster age, and an optional bonus for average relative volume at the pivots.
Calculation: Qualified zone = a zone whose score meets the minimum, is among the highest-scoring zones up to the zone-count limit, and, when the proximity filter is enabled, is within the ATR-scaled proximity range of close. Its height is ATR-scaled around the cluster average. Reject demand if close is below the zone base by the ATR-scaled break buffer or below the cluster average by the ATR-scaled wrong-side buffer; reject supply if close is above the zone top by the break buffer or above the cluster average by the wrong-side buffer. Merge overlapping zones of the same side before ranking.
Calculation: Trade plan = for demand, entry is the zone top and the stop is below either the zone base or the lowest pivot extreme by an ATR-scaled buffer, according to the selected stop mode; for supply, entry is the zone base and the stop is above either the zone top or the highest pivot extreme by that buffer. TP1 and TP2 are the nearest and second-nearest opposing qualified zone edges beyond entry, if available; otherwise use successively farther ATR-scaled fallback targets. A plan is valid when TP1 reward-to-risk meets the minimum ratio.
Long: A valid trade plan exists for a qualified demand zone AND its entry price equals the zone top.
Short: A valid trade plan exists for a qualified supply zone AND its entry price equals the zone base.
Exit: long, low <= the plan stop OR high >= TP1 OR high >= TP2
Exit: short, high >= the plan stop OR low <= TP1 OR low <= TP2
