Calculation: Current-timeframe bullish FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier, with a gap at least the minimum FVG size.
Calculation: Current-timeframe bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier, with a gap at least the minimum FVG size.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish FVG = higher-timeframe current low is above its high two bars earlier; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish FVG = higher-timeframe current high is below its low two bars earlier; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low using equal lookbacks on both sides; it becomes available after the right-side bars complete. Track the latest confirmed high and low, clearing each after its break.
Long: Current-timeframe bullish FVG OR higher-timeframe bullish FVG OR close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high.
Short: Current-timeframe bearish FVG OR higher-timeframe bearish FVG OR close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low.
