Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (source × volume) / cumulative volume, reset at each configured anchor-period boundary; source is configurable.
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of cumulative (volume × source²) / cumulative volume − Anchored VWAP², using the same anchor period.
Calculation: Volatility regime = classify current ATR reading into the nearest of three ascending k-means centroids fitted to recent ATR readings: Low, Medium, High. Confidence = (1 − distance to nearest centroid / distance to second-nearest centroid) × 100; regime is trusted only when the lookback is full and confidence meets its threshold. ATR length, clustering lookback, and reclustering interval are configurable.
Calculation: Volume regime = classify current volume into the nearest of three ascending k-means centroids fitted to recent volume readings: Low, Normal, High. Use the same clustering lookback, reclustering interval, and iteration count as for volatility.
Calculation: Upper/Lower Band 2 = Anchored VWAP ± VWAP standard deviation × Band 2 multiplier × band scale; band scale is the configured scale for the trusted current volatility regime when adaptive width is enabled, otherwise 1.
Long: In Mean Reversion mode, current close crosses above Lower Band 2 + buffer; OR in Breakout mode, current close crosses above Upper Band 2 + buffer; OR in Auto mode, do the Breakout comparison when the trusted volatility regime is High, otherwise do the Mean Reversion comparison; AND the bar is confirmed; AND volume regime is Normal or High, unless volume filtering is disabled or its clustering is not ready; AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the previous signal. A cross above means current close > current threshold and previous close ≤ previous threshold.
Short: In Mean Reversion mode, current close crosses below Upper Band 2 − buffer; OR in Breakout mode, current close crosses below Lower Band 2 − buffer; OR in Auto mode, do the Breakout comparison when the trusted volatility regime is High, otherwise do the Mean Reversion comparison; AND the bar is confirmed; AND volume regime is Normal or High, unless volume filtering is disabled or its clustering is not ready; AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the previous signal. A cross below means current close < current threshold and previous close ≥ previous threshold.
Exit: Long, a Short signal occurs.
Exit: Short, a Long signal occurs.
