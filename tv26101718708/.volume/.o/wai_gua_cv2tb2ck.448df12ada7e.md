Calculation: Fast ribbon average = mean of two moving averages of close using the same MA type; one has a shorter lookback than the other.
Calculation: Slow ribbon average = mean of two moving averages of close using the same MA type; both lookbacks are longer than the fast pair.
Long: Flat and no close signal is active, and this is the first bar on which each of these conditions has held for at least two consecutive bars: fast ribbon average > slow ribbon average and their difference is increasing; fast lower-lookback MA > fast upper-lookback MA and their difference is increasing; slow lower-lookback MA > slow upper-lookback MA and their difference is increasing.
Short: Flat and no close signal is active, and this is the first bar on which each of these conditions has held for at least two consecutive bars: fast ribbon average < slow ribbon average and their difference is decreasing; fast lower-lookback MA < fast upper-lookback MA and their difference is decreasing; slow lower-lookback MA < slow upper-lookback MA and their difference is decreasing.
Exit: Long, the low reaches the highest high since entry less the trailing-stop percentage OR the high since entry reaches entry plus the take-profit percentage and the low reaches that high less the trailing-profit percentage OR the low reaches entry less the stop-loss percentage OR opposite-ribbon exit is enabled (or both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) and this is the first bar where the prior bar's fast ribbon average < slow ribbon average and current open < prior slow ribbon average.
Exit: Short, the high reaches the lowest low since entry plus the trailing-stop percentage OR the low since entry reaches entry less the take-profit percentage and the high reaches that low plus the trailing-profit percentage OR the high reaches entry plus the stop-loss percentage OR opposite-ribbon exit is enabled (or both trailing stop and stop loss are disabled) and this is the first bar where the prior bar's fast ribbon average > slow ribbon average and current open > prior slow ribbon average.
