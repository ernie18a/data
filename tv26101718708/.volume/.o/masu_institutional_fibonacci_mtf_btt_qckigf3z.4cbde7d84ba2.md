Calculation: ATR = average true range of the current timeframe.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close; trend and higher-timeframe fast lookbacks are shorter than slow lookbacks, and the trend filter lookback is longer than both.
Calculation: Ribbon alignment = at least the configured threshold minus one of seven adjacent pairs among eight moving averages, using one configured MA type and progressively longer lookbacks; bullish means each counted shorter-lookback MA is above the next, bearish means below.
Calculation: VWAP = current-day cumulative sum of volume × (high + low + close)/3 divided by current-day cumulative volume.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = cumulative candle volume delta above its moving average and rising while close falls; bearish CVD divergence = cumulative candle volume delta below its moving average and falling while close rises. Candle volume delta is volume on up-close candles minus volume on down-close candles.
Calculation: Fib swing levels = retain the latest bar that is the highest high over the swing lookback plus current bar and whose distance above the lowest low over that window is at least the minimum ATR swing; likewise retain the latest qualifying lowest-low bar whose distance below the window's highest high is at least that minimum. With both swings available, an upswing has the retained high later than the retained low; levels are retained high minus 38.2%, 50%, or 61.8% of the high-low range. The extension is not used for decisions.
Long: (ADX > threshold AND EMA fast crosses above EMA slow AND EMA fast > EMA slow AND close > EMA filter AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier OR close > prior bar's breakout-lookback highest high AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier AND close > EMA filter OR ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI crosses above oversold level) AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA AND bullish ribbon alignment AND (order flow disabled OR close > VWAP OR ((bullish CVD divergence OR up-candle volume / max(down-candle volume, 1) > 1 + CVD threshold) AND (close crosses above VWAP OR close > VWAP))) AND upswing AND within configured trading session; enter only when flat. Higher-timeframe values use the requested timeframe: its still-forming bar in realtime and last completed bar historically.
Long: Fib swing is an upswing AND the current low is within the configured ATR zone of any enabled retracement level AND (lower-wick fraction of bar range meets the configured minimum OR close > open and close > the nearest touched enabled retracement level). Use the first touched level in order 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%; require both retained swing bars to be at least two bars old.
Short: (ADX > threshold AND EMA fast crosses below EMA slow AND EMA fast < EMA slow AND close < EMA filter AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier OR close < prior bar's breakout-lookback lowest low AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier AND close < EMA filter OR ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI crosses below overbought level) AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close < fast EMA AND bearish ribbon alignment AND (order flow disabled OR close < VWAP OR ((bearish CVD divergence OR up-candle volume / max(down-candle volume, 1) < 1 - CVD threshold) AND (close crosses below VWAP OR close < VWAP))) AND not an upswing AND within configured trading session; enter only when flat. Higher-timeframe values use the requested timeframe: its still-forming bar in realtime and last completed bar historically.
Short: Fib swing is not an upswing AND the current high is within the configured ATR zone of any enabled retracement level AND (upper-wick fraction of bar range meets the configured minimum OR close < open and close < the nearest touched enabled retracement level). Use the first touched level in order 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%; require both retained swing bars to be at least two bars old.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price minus entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR (trailing enabled AND price has advanced from entry by the configured ATR trail distance and falls by that distance from the highest high since entry) OR (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price plus entry-bar ATR × take-profit multiplier).
Exit: Short, high >= entry price plus entry-bar ATR × stop multiplier OR (trailing enabled AND price has declined from entry by the configured ATR trail distance and rises by that distance from the lowest low since entry) OR (trailing disabled AND low <= entry price minus entry-bar ATR × take-profit multiplier).
