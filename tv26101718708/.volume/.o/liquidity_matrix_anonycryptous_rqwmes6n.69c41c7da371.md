Calculation: Trend bands = a basis formed by applying an EMA and then an SMA to closing price, plus and minus an EMA of absolute closing-price deviation from that basis multiplied by a width factor.
Calculation: Bullish trailing stop = the greater of its prior value and low minus ATR times a multiplier; bearish trailing stop = the lesser of its prior value and high plus ATR times a multiplier.
Long: close crosses above the upper trend band and trend direction was not bullish OR low sweeps below the lowest low of the prior twice-lookback bars and closes back above it, with penetration at least a configured ATR multiple, lower-wick rejection at least a configured fraction of candle range, optional volume and EMA filters satisfied, and same-side cooldown elapsed OR a confirmed RSI pivot low has higher RSI and lower price than the preceding RSI pivot low OR RSI is above 50 and below its configured overbought level.
Short: close crosses below the lower trend band and trend direction was not bearish OR high sweeps above the highest high of the prior twice-lookback bars and closes back below it, with penetration at least a configured ATR multiple, upper-wick rejection at least a configured fraction of candle range, optional volume and EMA filters satisfied, and same-side cooldown elapsed OR a confirmed RSI pivot high has lower RSI and higher price than the preceding RSI pivot high OR RSI is below 50 and above its configured oversold level.
Exit: long, trailing stop is active AND (close is below the trailing stop OR RSI is at or above its configured overbought level).
Exit: short, trailing stop is active AND (close is above the trailing stop OR RSI is at or below its configured oversold level).
