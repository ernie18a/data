Calculation: Prior signal-timeframe candle range = high − low of the last completed signal-timeframe candle.
Calculation: Bull OTE zone = from prior candle high − 61.8% of its range to prior candle high − 50% of its range.
Calculation: Bear OTE zone = from prior candle low + 50% of its range to prior candle low + 61.8% of its range.
Calculation: NY dealing-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the New York opening range; use the completed range.
Calculation: Signal-timeframe swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the immediately adjacent highs/lows, among the four bars 2–5 bars before the current signal-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 for the current session.
Long: On the first configured-window bar of a signal-timeframe candle, current bar high > prior completed signal-timeframe candle high AND current bar low >= its low; then, before another signal fires in that signal-timeframe candle, low <= bull OTE zone top AND close >= bull OTE zone bottom AND the preceding bar did not meet those OTE conditions AND time is within the configured session; additionally, if enabled, prior candle range <= maximum, no signal-timeframe swing high above the prior high was breached by the sweep, close > session VWAP, confirmed NY dealing-range bias is not bearish once active, and time is past the gate. OR current forming bar on a monitored timeframe breaks the last completed bar's high but not its low. OR both are broken and the current bar close >= its open. OR the required consecutive closes are above the completed NY dealing-range high during the NY extension session.
Short: On the first configured-window bar of a signal-timeframe candle, current bar low < prior completed signal-timeframe candle low AND current bar high <= its high; then, before another signal fires in that signal-timeframe candle, high >= bear OTE zone bottom AND close <= bear OTE zone top AND the preceding bar did not meet those OTE conditions AND time is within the configured session; additionally, if enabled, prior candle range <= maximum, no signal-timeframe swing low below the prior low was breached by the sweep, close < session VWAP, confirmed NY dealing-range bias is not bullish once active, and time is past the gate. OR current forming bar on a monitored timeframe breaks the last completed bar's low but not its high. OR both are broken and the current bar close < its open. OR the required consecutive closes are below the completed NY dealing-range low during the NY extension session.
Exit: Long, high >= target OR low <= stop; if both occur on one bar, target takes precedence.
Exit: Short, low <= target OR high >= stop; if both occur on one bar, target takes precedence.
