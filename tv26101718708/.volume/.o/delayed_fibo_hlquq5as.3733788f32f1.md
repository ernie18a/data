Calculation: Fibonacci bands = EMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × SMA(true range, lookback), where true range is the maximum of high−low, |high−previous close|, and |low−previous close|; use the lower band selected from multipliers 1, 1.618, 2.618, 4.236 for long entries and the upper band with multiplier 2.618 for exits
Calculation: Volume-gated price = close when the sum of volume over the shorter lookback exceeds the sum over the immediately preceding older lookback multiplied by a configured threshold; otherwise carry forward its prior value. The shorter lookback is no longer than the longer lookback.
Long: Within the configured date window AND (volume-gated price crosses below the selected lower Fibonacci band OR, when band signals are disabled, current low ≠ previous low)
Exit: Long, volume-gated price crosses either above or below the upper Fibonacci band with multiplier 2.618 OR high reaches entry price plus the configured take-profit percentage OR low reaches entry price minus the configured stop-loss percentage OR the trailing stop is activated after price rises by the configured take-profit percentage plus 0.1% and is hit
