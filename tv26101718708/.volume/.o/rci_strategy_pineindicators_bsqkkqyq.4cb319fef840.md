Calculation: RCI = Spearman rank correlation between chronological order and source-price rank over a lookback, multiplied by 100
Calculation: RCI moving average = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of RCI over a lookback
Long: trade direction allows long AND RCI > RCI moving average AND previous-bar RCI <= previous-bar RCI moving average
Short: trade direction allows short AND RCI < RCI moving average AND previous-bar RCI >= previous-bar RCI moving average
Exit: short, RCI > RCI moving average AND previous-bar RCI <= previous-bar RCI moving average
Exit: long, RCI < RCI moving average AND previous-bar RCI >= previous-bar RCI moving average
