Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias total = sum across configured timeframes of +1 when that timeframe's close is above its 200-period EMA, otherwise -1; +1 when its 20-period EMA is above its 50-period EMA, otherwise -1; and +1 when its close is above its 20-period EMA, otherwise -1. Use the last completed bar on each requested timeframe.
Calculation: Bull/bear structure break = current close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low; confirm swing pivots using the configured lookback on both sides. Classify the break as a change of character when it reverses the prior structure direction, otherwise as a break of structure.
Calculation: Bull/bear FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier. Its zone is between those two prices.
Calculation: Bull/bear IFVG flip = an unmitigated bear/bull FVG becomes bullish/bearish when the current close is above its top / below its bottom.
Calculation: Bull/bear liquidity sweep = current low is below the second-latest confirmed swing low and close is above it / current high is above the second-latest confirmed swing high and close is below it.
Calculation: Bull/bear order block = on a bull/bear break of structure, a bearish/bullish candle exists among the configured number of immediately preceding bars.
Calculation: CVD = rolling sum over the configured lookback of volume signed positive when close exceeds open, negative when close is below open, and zero when equal.
Calculation: Daily range midpoint = midpoint of the previous completed daily bar's high and low.
Calculation: Killzone = bar is within the configured London, New York AM, or New York PM session.
Calculation: NY midnight open = open of the bar beginning at midnight New York time for the current day.
Calculation: Bull confluence score = 2 if higher-timeframe bias total is at least 4, else 1 if it is at least 2; plus 1 each for a bull structure break, bull IFVG flip, bull liquidity sweep, close below daily range midpoint, rising CVD, close above NY midnight open, being in the killzone, and bullish CVD divergence (close below its lookback-ago close while CVD is above its lookback-ago value).
Calculation: Bear confluence score = 2 if higher-timeframe bias total is at most -4, else 1 if it is at most -2; plus 1 each for a bear structure break, bear IFVG flip, bear liquidity sweep, close above daily range midpoint, falling CVD, close below NY midnight open, being in the killzone, and bearish CVD divergence (close above its lookback-ago close while CVD is below its lookback-ago value).
Long: current low > high two bars earlier OR bull structure break OR bull IFVG flip OR bull liquidity sweep OR bull order block OR bull confluence score >= configured minimum score AND bull confluence score > bear confluence score
Short: current high < low two bars earlier OR bear structure break OR bear IFVG flip OR bear liquidity sweep OR bear order block OR bear confluence score >= configured minimum score AND bear confluence score > bull confluence score
