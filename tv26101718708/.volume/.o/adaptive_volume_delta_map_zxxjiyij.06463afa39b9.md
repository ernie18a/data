Calculation: Signed volume = volume when the lower-timeframe bar closes above its open, −volume when it closes below; when equal, use the direction of its close versus the previous lower-timeframe close, retaining the prior direction if also equal
Calculation: Anchored cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of signed volume on the finest available lower timeframe, reset at the start of each configured anchor period
Long: Anchored cumulative volume delta > its value at the start of the anchor period
Short: Anchored cumulative volume delta ≤ its value at the start of the anchor period
