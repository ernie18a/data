Calculation: Order block = on a confirmed bar, a bullish break is a close crossover above the prior highest high over the structure lookback; a bearish break is a close crossunder below the prior lowest low. Require absolute candle body / ATR to exceed the minimum displacement. The block is the high-low range of the nearest opposing candle among the preceding origin-lookback bars; its direction is the break direction.
Calculation: Order-block quality = online logistic-regression probability using features captured at formation: block range / ATR, absolute formation-bar body / ATR, tanh((EMA(close, 50) − EMA(close, 200)) / ATR) × direction, ((RSI(close, 14) − 50) / 50) × direction, and block-candle volume / SMA(volume, 20). Clamp these features respectively to [0,10], [0,10], [−3,3], [−1,1], and [0,5]. Initialize all model weights to zero; quality is sigmoid(intercept + sum(weight × feature)). After each block resolves, update the weights by one logistic-gradient step using its features and label (1 held, 0 broken), learning-rate and L2-regularization parameters. A bullish block resolves broken if close < its bottom; otherwise held if it was retested and close > its top; otherwise, at the resolve horizon, label it held iff retested. For bearish blocks, broken if close > its top; otherwise held if retested and close < its bottom; otherwise, at the horizon, label it held iff retested. Apply these checks in this order.
Long: On a confirmed bar after the warmup, an unresolved bullish order block has quality ≥ threshold and has not been retested before, and low ≤ its top; the block was formed by a bullish break with displacement above the minimum.
Short: On a confirmed bar after the warmup, an unresolved bearish order block has quality ≥ threshold and has not been retested before, and high ≥ its bottom; the block was formed by a bearish break with displacement above the minimum.
