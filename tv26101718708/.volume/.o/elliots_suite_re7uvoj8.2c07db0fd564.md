Calculation: Confirmed fractal high = the high of a bar higher than the highs of the preceding left lookback bars and following right lookback bars; use the latest confirmed level until price crosses above it
Calculation: Confirmed fractal low = the low of a bar lower than the lows of the preceding left lookback bars and following right lookback bars; use the latest confirmed level until price crosses below it
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed fractal high
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed fractal low
