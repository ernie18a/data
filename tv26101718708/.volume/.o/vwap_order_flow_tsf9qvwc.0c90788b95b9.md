Calculation: Anchored VWAP and standard-deviation bands = volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of each bar’s typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the selected session, week, or month boundary; use the current bar’s values.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low); when the range is zero, use positive or negative volume according to the close’s change from the prior bar, or zero if unchanged.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum of bar delta since the selected anchor boundary.
Calculation: CVD slope = current cumulative volume delta minus its value at the configured lookback; bullish acceleration means this slope exceeds its value three bars earlier, and bearish acceleration means it is lower.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute close-open difference divided by bar range. Lower- and upper-wick ratios = respective wick length divided by body size; when body size is at or below the configured doji threshold, use the indicator’s doji fallback ratio.
Calculation: Consecutive bars on the wrong side of VWAP = count of prior consecutive closes below their corresponding VWAPs for a long reclaim, or above their VWAPs for a short reclaim; reset on an anchor change or when the prior close is on the other side or equal to VWAP.
Calculation: Bullish CVD pivot divergence = a confirmed price pivot low lower than the prior price pivot low while the corresponding CVD pivot low is higher than the prior CVD pivot low. Bearish CVD pivot divergence = a confirmed price pivot high higher than the prior price pivot high while the corresponding CVD pivot high is lower than the prior CVD pivot high. Use the same configured pivot lookback and right-side confirmation lag for price and CVD.
Long: (at least the configured minimum consecutive prior bars closed below VWAP AND current open < VWAP AND current close > VWAP AND body ratio ≥ the configured minimum AND bar delta > the configured positive average-volume multiple AND CVD slope > 0 AND bullish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured reclaim average-volume multiple AND bar is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in an allowed session) AND enough bars have passed since the prior long A/B signal) OR (current low extends beyond the lower outer VWAP band by more than the configured minimum extension AND current close is above the lower inner VWAP band AND lower-wick ratio > the configured minimum AND bar delta > the configured positive average-volume multiple AND bullish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured absorption average-volume multiple AND bar is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in an allowed session) AND enough bars have passed since the prior long A/B signal) OR (bullish CVD pivot divergence AND bar is confirmed AND absolute distance from close to VWAP ≤ the configured VWAP-zone standard-deviation multiple AND bullish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured divergence average-volume multiple).
Short: (at least the configured minimum consecutive prior bars closed above VWAP AND current open > VWAP AND current close < VWAP AND body ratio ≥ the configured minimum AND bar delta < the negative configured average-volume multiple AND CVD slope ≤ 0 AND bearish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured reclaim average-volume multiple AND bar is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in an allowed session) AND enough bars have passed since the prior short A/B signal) OR (current high extends beyond the upper outer VWAP band by more than the configured minimum extension AND current close is below the upper inner VWAP band AND upper-wick ratio > the configured minimum AND bar delta < the negative configured average-volume multiple AND bearish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured absorption average-volume multiple AND bar is confirmed AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in an allowed session) AND enough bars have passed since the prior short A/B signal) OR (bearish CVD pivot divergence AND bar is confirmed AND absolute distance from close to VWAP ≤ the configured VWAP-zone standard-deviation multiple AND bearish CVD acceleration AND volume > the configured divergence average-volume multiple).
