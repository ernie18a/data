Calculation: Support and resistance zones = On each confirmed bar, detect swing lows and highs using equal configurable lookbacks on both sides; create support from lows and resistance from highs. Seed each zone around its pivot with a half-width proportional to the current median high-low range; store its width and break margin at creation. Discard a seed if a close between pivot and confirmation breached its proposed boundary plus margin. Merge a pivot into the nearest active same-role zone within a median-range-based tolerance, widening but never shrinking the zone. Evaluate existing zones before widening: close beyond a support's lower edge minus its stored margin or a resistance's upper edge plus its stored margin breaks it and flips its role. Remove active zones after configurable consecutive bars without overlap; retain broken zones for a configurable period. Track touches from the founding pivot; add one touch only when a bar's range first overlaps a known zone after a bar outside it. A break does not add a touch. Apply the configurable minimum-touch visibility threshold to zone-entry decisions. Use chart bars only; evaluate decisions on completed bars.
Long: bar range overlaps an active support zone AND the previous bar's range was outside that zone AND the zone's touch count meets the minimum-touch threshold AND the bar does not break the zone
Short: bar range overlaps an active resistance zone AND the previous bar's range was outside that zone AND the zone's touch count meets the minimum-touch threshold AND the bar does not break the zone
