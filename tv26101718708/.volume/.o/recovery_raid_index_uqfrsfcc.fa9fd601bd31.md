Calculation: Long sweep level = the eligible level below price with the highest rank among the latest confirmed swing low, the mean of the two latest confirmed swing lows when they are within an ATR-based tolerance, the last completed daily low, the last completed weekly low, and the developing UTC-day low as of the previous bar; eligibility requires level − low > sweep-buffer × ATR and level − low ≤ maximum-sweep-depth × ATR. Rank = source importance × 100 − (level − low) / ATR × 10.
Calculation: Short sweep level = the eligible level above price with the highest rank among the latest confirmed swing high, the mean of the two latest confirmed swing highs when they are within an ATR-based tolerance, the last completed daily high, the last completed weekly high, and the developing UTC-day high as of the previous bar; eligibility requires high − level > sweep-buffer × ATR and high − level ≤ maximum-sweep-depth × ATR. Rank = source importance × 100 − (high − level) / ATR × 10.
Calculation: Effective reclaim window = base reclaim bars multiplied by the session factor (UTC 08:00–11:59 London, 13:00–16:59 New York, otherwise Other), rounded to an integer and bounded below by one bar.
Calculation: Recovery score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of weighted, clamped-to-0–1 components: source importance / maximum source importance; sweep depth in ATR / depth target; reclaim speed (1 − bars since sweep / effective reclaim window); maximum rejection-wick-to-range ratio during the pending sweep / wick target; maximum relative volume during the pending sweep / RVOL target; directional flow / flow target; close-back distance in ATR / close-back target; and the FVG-confluence weight when an active same-direction FVG overlaps the swept level within an ATR-based tolerance. Directional flow = EMA of [(close − low) / range × volume − (high − close) / range × volume] divided by EMA(volume), using the same smoothing length; use its positive part for long recovery and absolute negative part for short recovery. Depth and speed weights vary: depth weight is lower in high volatility (ATR > its longer-term SMA × threshold), and speed weight is lower in low volatility (ATR < that SMA × threshold).
Long: confirmed bar AND (a pending sweep below the selected long sweep level is reclaimed with close > level + reclaim-buffer × ATR within the effective reclaim window, lower-timeframe confirmation passes if enabled, and recovery score ≥ minimum score) OR (a pending sweep above the selected short sweep level has lasted at least the acceptance wait and close > level + acceptance-buffer × ATR).
Short: confirmed bar AND (a pending sweep above the selected short sweep level is reclaimed with close < level − reclaim-buffer × ATR within the effective reclaim window, lower-timeframe confirmation passes if enabled, and recovery score ≥ minimum score) OR (a pending sweep below the selected long sweep level has lasted at least the acceptance wait and close < level − acceptance-buffer × ATR).
