Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of bars overlapping the configured intraday range window; use only after that window completes. Trading window = configured period after range completion; signals are allowed only within it.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart bars; prior daily ATR = ATR on the last completed daily bar. Opening-range width rank = percentile rank of range width as a percentage of prior daily ATR against earlier completed sessions.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish if the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and that EMA is at least its value one higher-timeframe bar earlier; bearish if below and at most its earlier value; otherwise neutral. Both EMA values use the same lookback.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted typical price from the opening-range session start through the current bar.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume-weighted sum of lower-timeframe bar directional weights when intrabars are available; otherwise chart-bar volume times its directional weight. Directional weight = mean of (2×close−high−low)/(high−low) and (close−open)/(high−low); use sign(close−previous close) if high equals low.
Calculation: Signal score = weighted mean, scaled 0–10, of: volume surge clamp((bar volume / its moving-average baseline − 0.8) / (high-volume threshold × 1.5 − 0.8)); directional close location (long: (close−low)/(high−low), short: (high−close)/(high−low)); directional close displacement from the broken edge divided by ATR and the full-score displacement threshold; range-width score (failed-break setup: width rank, otherwise 1−width rank); higher-timeframe alignment (1 aligned, 0.5 neutral, 0 opposed); directional bar delta divided by volume and 0.5, clamped 0–1; and VWAP side (1 if close is on the signal side, 0 otherwise, 0.5 if unavailable). Use configurable weights normalized by their sum; clamp component scores to 0–1.
Calculation: Extension targets = opening-range high/low extended outward by configured multiples of opening-range width. Failed-break targets = range midpoint, opposite range edge, then first extension beyond that edge.
Calculation: Initial stop = configured stop basis (opposite range edge, midpoint, ATR distance from entry, or signal-bar extreme); for failed-break setups use the failed edge adjusted by the configured ATR buffer. Clamp risk distance from entry to configured minimum and maximum ATR risk. Targets use configured extension levels unless too close to entry or the previous target, then use configured increasing risk multiples.
Calculation: Trailing stop after target 2 = for long, maximum of prior stop and close minus configured ATR multiple; for short, minimum of prior stop and close plus that multiple. Use a longer or equal ATR lookback than the signal ATR.
Long: Opening range is complete AND current bar is inside the trading window AND (an upward close-break of the range high beyond the configured ATR buffer is the first such break or within the per-side break limit OR, within the retest window after an upward break, price reaches the configured tolerance of the high and closes above it with close > open OR, within the failure window after a downward break, close is inside the range by at least the configured fraction of its width) AND bar is confirmed AND signal score ≥ configured minimum AND counter-higher-timeframe blocking is off or higher-timeframe bias is not bearish AND session signal count is below its cap.
Short: Opening range is complete AND current bar is inside the trading window AND (a downward close-break of the range low beyond the configured ATR buffer is the first such break or within the per-side break limit OR, within the retest window after a downward break, price reaches the configured tolerance of the low and closes below it with close < open OR, within the failure window after an upward break, close is inside the range by at least the configured fraction of its width) AND bar is confirmed AND signal score ≥ configured minimum AND counter-higher-timeframe blocking is off or higher-timeframe bias is not bullish AND session signal count is below its cap.
Exit: long, low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ final target OR current bar starts a new opening-range session OR current bar is outside the trading window OR configured maximum holding bars have elapsed.
Exit: short, high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ final target OR current bar starts a new opening-range session OR current bar is outside the trading window OR configured maximum holding bars have elapsed.
