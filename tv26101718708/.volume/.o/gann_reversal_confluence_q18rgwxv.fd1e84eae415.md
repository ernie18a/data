Calculation: Prior swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding N completed bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Long confluence score = count of enabled factors that pass: bar range ≥ ATR × range multiplier; volume ≥ its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier (or volume unavailable); RSI ≤ oversold threshold; one point for enabled EMA trend factor; enabled hour ruler is Venus/Jupiter; enabled weekday ruler is Venus/Jupiter
Calculation: Short confluence score = count of enabled factors that pass: bar range ≥ ATR × range multiplier; volume ≥ its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier (or volume unavailable); RSI ≥ overbought threshold; one point for enabled EMA trend factor; enabled hour ruler is Mars/Saturn; enabled weekday ruler is Mars/Saturn
Long: On bar close, selected reversal pattern is true AND long confluence score ≥ minimum score AND current direction ≤ 0 AND bars since last signal ≥ minimum gap (or no prior signal); selected pattern is one of: close (or high if wick confirmation) > prior swing high; low < previous bar low AND (close > previous close AND close > open if close confirmation, otherwise high > previous bar high); high > previous bar high AND low < previous bar low AND (close > open if close confirmation, otherwise high > low)
Short: On bar close, selected reversal pattern is true AND short confluence score ≥ minimum score AND current direction ≥ 0 AND bars since last signal ≥ minimum gap (or no prior signal); selected pattern is one of: close (or low if wick confirmation) < prior swing low; high > previous bar high AND (close < previous close AND close < open if close confirmation, otherwise low < previous bar low); high > previous bar high AND low < previous bar low AND (close < open if close confirmation, otherwise low < high)
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop set at entry (entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple) OR high ≥ target set at entry (entry price + ATR at entry × target multiple) OR an unfiltered raw short reversal occurs; check stop before target, and either before opposite reversal
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop set at entry (entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple) OR low ≤ target set at entry (entry price − ATR at entry × target multiple) OR an unfiltered raw long reversal occurs; check stop before target, and either before opposite reversal
