Calculation: Pattern skeleton = on each confirmed current-timeframe bar, select 7 close-price points across a cycle-adaptive lookback by repeatedly inserting the point with greatest vertical deviation from the segments between selected points; normalize their prices to [0,1].
Calculation: Template candidate = the normalized skeleton's highest-fitting match among double top/bottom, head-and-shoulders/inverse, and triple top/bottom templates; fit = max(0, 1 − RMSE / tolerance). Directions: top, head-and-shoulders, triple top → short; bottom, inverse, triple bottom → long.
Calculation: Geometric candidate = fit least-squares lines to the skeleton's interior local highs and lows; require at least two of each and both R² values above the minimum. Classify converging/diverging lines by their slopes and width change: flat upper with rising lower → ascending triangle/long; flat lower with falling upper → descending triangle/short; falling upper with rising lower → symmetrical triangle/neutral; both rising with narrowing width → rising wedge/short, otherwise ascending channel/long; both falling with narrowing width → falling wedge/long, otherwise descending channel/short; widening width → broadening/neutral. Geometric fit = lesser R², multiplied by 0.6 if either line has only two pivots. Use the geometric candidate only when its fit exceeds the template candidate's fit.
Calculation: Pattern direction and confidence = the selected candidate's direction and fit until the resolved-pattern memory is mature; thereafter, match the normalized skeleton by dynamic time warping to the k nearest resolved skeletons, and use the sign of their mean forward close move (measured in each pattern's entry ATR) as direction and the fraction moving at least the follow-through ATR multiple in that direction as confidence.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that same lookback.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price wicks beyond an equal swing-high/low pool or latest swing high/low and closes back inside; a low sweep is bullish and a high sweep bearish. Equal pools use the two latest confirmed pivots within an ATR-scaled tolerance. Swing pivots use the same left and right lookback; use confirmed pivots only.
Calculation: Dealing-range position = (close − latest confirmed swing low) / (latest confirmed swing high − latest confirmed swing low).
Calculation: Breakout quality = 0.34 × min(1, true range / ATR / 2) + 0.20 × min(1, absolute(close − open) / (high − low) / 0.8) + 0.20 × clamp((volume z-score over a lookback + 0.5) / 2.5, 0, 1) + 0.26 × sweep-valid (1 if swept in the pattern direction within the sweep lookback, otherwise 0).
Calculation: Setup grade = score 100 × (0.50 × confidence + 0.30 × breakout quality + 0.10 × sweep-valid + 0.10 × correct-location) / 0.90; correct-location means a long below the dealing-range midpoint or a short above it. Map scores to A+ (≥80), A (≥68), B (≥55), C (≥45), otherwise no grade.
Long: confirmed bar AND selected pattern direction > 0 AND final skeleton pivot is recent AND minimum bars since previous emission elapsed AND pattern range ≥ minimum ATR multiple AND NOT (reversal pattern in trending regime OR continuation pattern in choppy regime OR range pattern in choppy regime when range veto is enabled) AND (analog motif strength meets its minimum OR analog memory is not mature OR motif gate is off) AND NOT (long dealing-range position exceeds the premium veto threshold) AND setup grade meets the minimum grade AND setup grade is enabled.
Short: confirmed bar AND selected pattern direction < 0 AND final skeleton pivot is recent AND minimum bars since previous emission elapsed AND pattern range ≥ minimum ATR multiple AND NOT (reversal pattern in trending regime OR continuation pattern in choppy regime OR range pattern in choppy regime when range veto is enabled) AND (analog motif strength meets its minimum OR analog memory is not mature OR motif gate is off) AND NOT (short dealing-range position is below one minus the premium veto threshold) AND setup grade meets the minimum grade AND setup grade is enabled.
