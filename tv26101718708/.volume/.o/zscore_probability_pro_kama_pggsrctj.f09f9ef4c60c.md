Calculation: Baseline = selected moving average of close
Calculation: Z-score = standardize the selected close-to-baseline residual (absolute, percentage, or log deviation) using its rolling standard deviation or median absolute deviation, then apply the selected Z-score smoother
Calculation: Confirmed bullish divergence = the latest two confirmed Z-score pivot lows are no more than the configured maximum gap apart and either price makes a lower low while Z-score makes a higher low with the latest Z-score pivot below the configured oversold threshold, or price makes a higher low while Z-score makes a lower low
Calculation: Confirmed bearish divergence = the latest two confirmed Z-score pivot highs are no more than the configured maximum gap apart and either price makes a higher high while Z-score makes a lower high with the latest Z-score pivot above the configured overbought threshold, or price makes a lower high while Z-score makes a higher high
Calculation: Hurst regime = smooth the clipped log variance ratio of lagged close changes to one-bar close changes; classify as trending above the configured upper threshold, mean-reverting below the configured lower threshold, and otherwise random
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Z-score = calculate the Z-score on aggregated higher-timeframe bars and use the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = percentage of total component weights earned by the respective Z-score reversal, divergence, baseline slope, regime agreement, higher-timeframe agreement, and volume confirmation components; component weights and enabled components are configurable
Long: confirmed bullish divergence OR bullish score meets the configured weak-signal threshold, exceeds bearish score, and does not meet the configured conviction threshold OR bullish score meets the configured conviction threshold and exceeds bearish score
Short: confirmed bearish divergence OR bearish score meets the configured weak-signal threshold, exceeds bullish score, and does not meet the configured conviction threshold OR bearish score meets the configured conviction threshold and exceeds bullish score
