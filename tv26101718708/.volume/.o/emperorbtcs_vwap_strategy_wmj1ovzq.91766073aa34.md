Calculation: VWAP = within each selected timeframe period, cumulative(volume × (open + high + low + close) / 4) / cumulative(volume), using the current period bar as it forms
Calculation: Daily pivot levels = use the previous completed daily bar's high, low, and close to calculate pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; support and resistance levels use either Fibonacci offsets (0.382, 0.618, and 1 times the previous day's high-low range) or Traditional formulas
Calculation: Stochastic RSI %K/%D = %K is the smoothed stochastic of RSI; %D is the smoothed %K, using the same RSI and stochastic lookbacks and separate smoothing lookbacks
Long: (low < VWAP AND VWAP < high AND close > open) OR (%K[1] < %D[1] AND %K > %D) OR (low < any of support 1, support 2, support 3, or pivot AND close > that level) OR (a bullish Stochastic RSI cross or bullish VWAP cross occurred on either of the two preceding bars AND a bullish pivot-level cross occurred on the preceding bar AND close[1] > open[1] AND close > open AND long entries are allowed AND bar datetime is within the configured date range)
Short: (high > VWAP AND VWAP > low AND close < open) OR (%K[1] > %D[1] AND %K < %D) OR (high > any of support 1, support 2, support 3, or pivot AND close < that level) OR (a bearish Stochastic RSI cross or bearish VWAP cross occurred on either of the two preceding bars AND a bearish pivot-level cross occurred on the preceding bar AND close[1] < open[1] AND close < open AND short entries are allowed AND bar datetime is within the configured date range)
Exit: long, close > entry price × (1 + configured take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, close < entry price × (1 − configured take-profit percentage)
Exit: long or short, bar datetime < configured start datetime OR bar datetime >= configured end datetime
