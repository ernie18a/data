Calculation: Narrow spread = current bar’s high-low spread is at or below the specified low percentile of recent spreads
Calculation: Low volume = current volume is at or below the specified low percentile of recent volumes
Calculation: Demand-zone bounds = centered on the base bar’s midpoint, with height equal to the greater of its spread and a specified multiple of the median recent spread
Calculation: Supply-zone bounds = centered on the base bar’s midpoint, with height equal to the greater of its spread and a specified multiple of the median recent spread
Long: A demand base exists (narrow spread AND low volume AND close above the EMA), and the current close is above its zone top
Short: A supply base exists (narrow spread AND low volume AND close below the EMA), and the current close is below its zone bottom
