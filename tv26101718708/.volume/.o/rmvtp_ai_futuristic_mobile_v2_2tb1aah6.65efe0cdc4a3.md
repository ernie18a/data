Calculation: Per-timeframe trend score = +1 if close is above the medium-term EMA, -1 if below, otherwise 0; momentum score = +1 if close is above its value at the shorter momentum lookback, -1 if below, otherwise 0; candle score = +1 if (close − open) / (high − low) exceeds the positive pressure threshold, -1 if below the negative pressure threshold, otherwise 0 (zero if high = low); volume score = 1.0 if volume / its moving average is at least the strong-volume threshold, 0.5 if at least the normal-volume threshold, otherwise 0 (zero if the average is zero). Use the configured medium-term EMA, shorter momentum lookback, and volume-average lookback.
Calculation: Per-timeframe score = 0.35 × trend score + 0.25 × momentum score + 0.20 × candle score + 0.20 × (trend score × volume score).
Calculation: Final score = weighted sum of per-timeframe scores from five configured timeframes ordered shortest to longest; weights rank fourth > third > fifth > second > first. Use each timeframe's bar as it forms; historical values update when that timeframe's bar completes.
Long: Final score is at least the bullish-signal threshold AND close > medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA.
Short: Final score is at most the bearish-signal threshold AND close < medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA.
