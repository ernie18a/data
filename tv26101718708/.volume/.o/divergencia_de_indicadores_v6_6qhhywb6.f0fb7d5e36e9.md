Calculation: MACD = difference between the shorter-period and longer-period EMAs of close; MACD histogram = MACD minus its signal EMA
Calculation: Stochastic = 3-period SMA of the stochastic oscillator
Calculation: Volume-weighted MACD = shorter-period VWMA of close minus longer-period VWMA of close
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = SMA of ((2 × close − high − low) / (high − low)) × volume divided by SMA of volume, using the same lookback for both SMAs
Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta = cumulative lower-timeframe up-volume minus down-volume, reset at the start of each selected anchor period
Calculation: Up/Down Volume Delta = lower-timeframe up-volume minus down-volume
Calculation: Bullish divergence = for any enabled indicator among MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, MFI, cumulative volume delta, or up/down volume delta, compare the candidate point with one of the most recent configured number of confirmed price pivot lows within the configured bar limit. A pivot uses close for both highs and lows, or high for highs and low for lows. At the candidate, the indicator must be higher and the selected price (close, or low) lower for regular divergence; or the indicator lower and selected price higher for hidden divergence. The indicator and close must stay on or above their respective straight lines between the two points. The pivot must be more than five bars back. Unless unconfirmed signals are enabled, use the previous chart bar as the candidate and require the current indicator or close to rise versus the prior bar; otherwise use the current bar. Divergence types are included only when selected.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = for any enabled indicator among MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, stochastic, CCI, momentum, OBV, volume-weighted MACD, Chaikin Money Flow, MFI, cumulative volume delta, or up/down volume delta, compare the candidate point with one of the most recent configured number of confirmed price pivot highs within the configured bar limit. A pivot uses close for both highs and lows, or high for highs and low for lows. At the candidate, the indicator must be lower and the selected price (close, or high) higher for regular divergence; or the indicator higher and selected price lower for hidden divergence. The indicator and close must stay on or below their respective straight lines between the two points. The pivot must be more than five bars back. Unless unconfirmed signals are enabled, use the previous chart bar as the candidate and require the current indicator or close to fall versus the prior bar; otherwise use the current bar. Divergence types are included only when selected.
Long: Bullish divergence exists among the first 11 listed indicators AND the total number of bullish and bearish divergences across all enabled indicators, including any external indicator, is at least the configured minimum
Short: Bearish divergence exists among the first 11 listed indicators AND the total number of bullish and bearish divergences across all enabled indicators, including any external indicator, is at least the configured minimum
