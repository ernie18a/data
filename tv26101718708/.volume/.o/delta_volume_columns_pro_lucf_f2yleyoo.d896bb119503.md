Calculation: Buy and sell volume = sum volume of completed lower-timeframe bars within each chart bar, classified buy when close > open, sell when close < open, otherwise by close versus previous lower-timeframe close, otherwise by the last known polarity; a neutral bar inherits the last known polarity.
Calculation: On-bar balance = buy volume − sell volume.
Calculation: Average balance = EMA(buy volume, L) − EMA(sell volume, L).
Calculation: Momentum balance = RSI((EMA(buy volume, L) − SMA(buy volume, 2L)) − (EMA(sell volume, L) − SMA(sell volume, 2L)), M) − 50.
Calculation: Relative balance = ALMA(max(0, buy volume − EMA(buy volume, L)) − max(0, sell volume − EMA(sell volume, L)), R).
Calculation: Percent balance = ALMA(100 × on-bar balance / chart-bar volume, P).
Calculation: Combined balance = sum of the signs of the on-bar, average, momentum, relative, and percent balances; a zero balance contributes zero.
Calculation: Marker bias = rolling sum over B bars of the signed occurrences of markers 1–4; each up marker contributes +1 and each down marker −1. Marker 1 occurs when all five balances are positive (up) or all five are negative (down). Marker 2 occurs when the two preceding bars each have the same direction and on-bar balance sign, buy volume (up) or sell volume (down) above its EMA(L) and rising, and the most recent preceding bar's close is rising (up) or falling (down) versus its previous close.
Long: All five balances > 0 OR the two preceding bars each have close > previous close, on-bar balance > 0, buy volume > EMA(buy volume, L), and buy volume rising, and the most recent preceding bar has close > its previous close OR the selected divergence balance was rising on the bar two bars ago while that bar's close was below its previous close, the preceding bar's close is above its previous close, and the preceding bar has neither a rising selected divergence balance with falling close nor a falling selected divergence balance with rising close OR the selected marker balance crossed above 0 on the preceding bar OR marker bias crossed above 0 on the preceding bar.
Short: All five balances < 0 OR the two preceding bars each have close < previous close, on-bar balance < 0, sell volume > EMA(sell volume, L), and sell volume rising, and the most recent preceding bar has close < its previous close OR the selected divergence balance was falling on the bar two bars ago while that bar's close was above its previous close, the preceding bar's close is below its previous close, and the preceding bar has neither a rising selected divergence balance with falling close nor a falling selected divergence balance with rising close OR the selected marker balance crossed below 0 on the preceding bar OR marker bias crossed below 0 on the preceding bar.
