Calculation: Compression score = weighted score of the current compression window’s range relative to a longer window, close dispersion, short-average drift, range width relative to ATR, and largest true range relative to the window range; reject windows dominated by one bar.
Calculation: Accumulation quality = weighted score of compression, range width relative to ATR, defended touches on both boundaries, similarity of repeated highs and lows, and range duration.
Calculation: Liquidity pool quality = weighted score of the raided boundary’s match to completed previous-week, previous-day, or completed Asia high/low, or the last confirmed swing high/low; include the number of clustered reference levels and defended boundary touches. Swing levels become available only after confirmation.
Calculation: Manipulation quality = weighted score of sweep depth relative to locked-range ATR, inverse acceptance beyond the raided boundary, reclaim distance beyond that boundary, rejection-wick share of the reclaim bar, liquidity pool quality, and excursion duration. Acceptance uses volume when available, otherwise time beyond the boundary.
Calculation: Distribution quality = weighted score of displacement from the raided boundary, the first qualifying three-bar imbalance, close clearance beyond the opposite locked-range boundary, strongest expansion-bar body relative to its range, and time since reclaim.
Calculation: Structural score = weighted geometric mean of accumulation, manipulation, and distribution quality, adjusted for opposing raids.
Calculation: Context score = weighted score of directional alignment with the last completed higher-timeframe bar, premium/discount location within that bar’s range, session fit, volatility and structure regime fit, target room, and position within the developing range and expansion leg.
Calculation: Raw confluence score = structural score scaled by context score.
Calculation: Trade risk = absolute distance from signal close to the stop beyond the sweep extreme, buffered by locked-range ATR and widened to a minimum distance; target is the nearest opposing previous-week, previous-day, completed Asia, or confirmed swing level beyond the locked range and at least one risk unit away, or the configured risk multiple if none qualifies.
Long: A qualifying compressed range has held for the required minimum duration and passes its range-size and accumulation-quality gates; price sweeps below its locked low at least the volatility-adjusted minimum, the low-side liquidity pool qualifies and is fresh, and price reclaims above that low within the manipulation window with manipulation quality above its floor, acceptance and sweep depth within their limits; then a bullish displacement bar forms within the post-reclaim window, no sweep-extreme break or opposite raid invalidates the setup, and within the distribution window price closes above the locked range high; all phase-quality floors pass, raw confluence score meets its minimum, trade risk is within its maximum, and any enabled adaptive gate passes.
Short: A qualifying compressed range has held for the required minimum duration and passes its range-size and accumulation-quality gates; price sweeps above its locked high at least the volatility-adjusted minimum, the high-side liquidity pool qualifies and is fresh, and price reclaims below that high within the manipulation window with manipulation quality above its floor, acceptance and sweep depth within their limits; then a bearish displacement bar forms within the post-reclaim window, no sweep-extreme break or opposite raid invalidates the setup, and within the distribution window price closes below the locked range low; all phase-quality floors pass, raw confluence score meets its minimum, trade risk is within its maximum, and any enabled adaptive gate passes.
Exit: long, low <= stop or high >= target or the configured outcome horizon expires; if stop and target are both touched on the same bar, resolve at the stop.
Exit: short, high >= stop or low <= target or the configured outcome horizon expires; if stop and target are both touched on the same bar, resolve at the stop.
