Calculation: Long confluence score = add 2 for an upward opening-range breakout; 2 for confirmed bullish structure when structure confirmation is required; 2 for a bullish gap retest; 1 for an active bullish order block and 1 more if its strength is at least 4; 1 in the discount zone; 1 in the golden zone; 2 for RSI below its configured oversold level while close is above the long-term EMA; 2 for bullish divergence; 1 for liquidity below; 1 for a volume spike; 1 for bullish momentum; and 2 for a strong bullish candle pattern.
Calculation: Short confluence score = add 2 for a downward opening-range breakout; 2 for confirmed bearish structure when structure confirmation is required; 2 for a bearish gap retest; 1 for an active bearish order block and 1 more if its strength is at least 4; 1 in the premium zone; 1 in the golden zone; 2 for RSI above its configured overbought level while close is below the long-term EMA; 2 for bearish divergence; 1 for liquidity above; 1 for a volume spike; 1 for bearish momentum; and 2 for a strong bearish candle pattern.
Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening interval of the current day; use the completed range after that interval ends.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish gap = current high is below the low two bars earlier. The active gap is the latest gap meeting the configured minimum size. A bullish retest has low at or below the gap top and close at or above its bottom; a bearish retest has high at or above the gap bottom and close at or below its top.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal configured lookbacks on both sides. Bullish structure is confirmed after a swing high exceeds the prior swing high; bearish structure is confirmed after a swing low falls below the prior swing low. Structure state changes to bullish on the former and bearish on the latter.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the prior bar is bearish, the current bar is bullish, current range exceeds the configured ATR multiple, and current volume exceeds the configured average-volume multiple; bearish order block is the inverse. The active block is the latest detected block of that side. Strength = capped sum of volume strength (3 above the higher volume threshold, 2 above the lower threshold, otherwise 1) and range strength (3 above the higher ATR threshold, 2 above the lower threshold, otherwise 1).
Calculation: Premium/discount range = highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback; equilibrium is their midpoint. Premium is close above the midpoint and above the midpoint-to-high halfway level; discount is close below the midpoint and below the low-to-midpoint halfway level.
Calculation: Golden zone = close lies between the 0.618 retracement and 1.052 times that retracement, calculated from the primary configured swing range. The primary swing lookback is shorter than the secondary swing lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior price pivot low while the corresponding RSI pivot low is higher; bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior price pivot high while the corresponding RSI pivot high is lower. Price and RSI pivots use equal left/right lookbacks.
Calculation: Strong bullish patterns = bullish hammer with volume above its configured average-volume multiple, bullish engulfing with volume above the prior bar's volume multiple, or morning star. Strong bearish patterns are the corresponding shooting star, bearish engulfing, or evening star.
Calculation: Bullish momentum = RSI change over its configured lookback is positive and MACD histogram is positive; bearish momentum = both are negative. A volume spike is volume above its configured average-volume multiple.
Long: close crosses above the completed opening-range high (prior close at or below it)
Short: close crosses below the completed opening-range low (prior close at or above it)
Long: bullish gap retest
Short: bearish gap retest
Long: a confirmed bullish-structure state after a higher swing high
Short: a confirmed bearish-structure state after a lower swing low
Long: bullish divergence
Short: bearish divergence
Long: strong bullish hammer OR strong bullish engulfing OR morning star
Short: strong shooting star OR strong bearish engulfing OR evening star
Long: equal lows across three bars OR successive confirmed swing lows within the configured tolerance
Short: equal highs across three bars OR successive confirmed swing highs within the configured tolerance
Long: long confluence score >= configured minimum AND regime filter allows trading AND current time is in an allowed session (or session filter is disabled) AND daily trade count is below its configured maximum AND position is flat AND volume > configured average-volume multiple AND (partial setups are allowed OR (close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA, OR short-term EMA > medium-term EMA > long-term EMA)); EMA lookbacks are ordered short < medium < long.
Short: short confluence score >= configured minimum AND regime filter allows trading AND current time is in an allowed session (or session filter is disabled) AND daily trade count is below its configured maximum AND position is flat AND volume > configured average-volume multiple AND (partial setups are allowed OR (close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA, OR short-term EMA < medium-term EMA < long-term EMA)); EMA lookbacks are ordered short < medium < long.
Calculation: Long stop = completed opening-range low when the opening-range filter is enabled and the range is established, otherwise current low minus configured ATR multiple. Short stop = completed opening-range high under the same condition, otherwise current high plus configured ATR multiple. Risk = entry price minus long stop, or short stop minus entry price. Target = entry price plus/minus risk times the configured reward multiple, increased by its configured volatility factor when ATR exceeds its moving average times the configured threshold.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR current time is in the configured end-of-day exit interval
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR current time is in the configured end-of-day exit interval
