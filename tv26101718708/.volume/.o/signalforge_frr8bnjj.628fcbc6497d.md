Calculation: RSI score = clamp to [-1,1] of: (oversold−RSI)/oversold if RSI≤oversold; −(RSI−overbought)/(100−overbought) if RSI≥overbought; otherwise (50−RSI)/50 × mid-range multiplier; multiply by 1.4 on a cross back above oversold or below overbought, then add 0.08 in the direction of an RSI change whose absolute size exceeds 5, and clamp again
Calculation: Volume score = clamp to [-1,1] of a raw score × volume factor. Raw score is +1 when OBV is above its volume-length SMA and its 5-bar slope is positive; −1 when below and slope is negative; otherwise +0.7 for a volume spike with a rising close and volume/average-volume ratio >2.5, −0.7 for a spike with a falling close and ratio >2.5, or 0. The factor is min(volume ratio/spike multiplier, 1.5) on a spike and 0.5 otherwise
Calculation: VWAP score = clamp to [-1,1] of (VWAP−close)/ (20-bar close standard deviation × deviation multiplier); add 0.2 when close rises and previous close was below VWAP, subtract 0.2 when close falls and previous close was above VWAP. With momentum override enabled, a volume-spike cross above VWAP or strong rising price above VWAP changes the score to +min(1, |score|×0.8); a volume-spike cross below VWAP or strong falling price below VWAP changes it to −min(1, |score|×0.8). Strong means VWAP distance exceeds 0.8 deviation bands and volume ratio >1.8
Calculation: EMA score = clamp to [-1,1] of 0.3 × (fast EMA−slow EMA)/ATR(14); add 0.3 on a fast-over-slow cross, subtract 0.3 on a fast-under-slow cross, then add 0.08 when close is above both EMAs or subtract 0.08 when below both; clamp again. Fast EMA length is shorter than slow EMA length
Calculation: MACD score = clamp to [-1,1] of 2 × MACD histogram/ATR(14); add 0.35 on MACD-line cross above signal line, subtract 0.35 on cross below; add 0.15 on MACD-line cross above zero, subtract 0.15 on cross below zero; add 0.05 when positive histogram grows, subtract 0.05 when negative histogram becomes more negative; clamp again. MACD fast length is shorter than slow length
Calculation: Bollinger score = clamp to [-1,1] of (basis−close)/deviation, where basis is the close SMA and deviation is its standard deviation × band multiplier. Multiply by 1.3, clamped, when band width is below 75% of its 50-bar SMA. If close is above the upper band on two consecutive bars, set score to +min(1, |score|×0.6); if below the lower band on two consecutive bars, set it to −min(1, |score|×0.6). Add 0.25 on a lower-band touch followed by a rising close; subtract 0.25 on an upper-band touch followed by a falling close; clamp again
Calculation: Volume delta = volume × (2×(close−low)/(high−low)−1), using 0.5 as close position when high=low. Cumulative delta is the running sum of volume delta
Calculation: Volume Intelligence score = clamp to [-1,1] of 0.35 × regime + 0.25 × clamp(volume delta/(3×delta standard deviation),−1,1) + 0.20 × clamp(cumulative-delta fast-EMA 3-bar slope/(ATR(14)×volume/1000),−1,1) + 0.10 for delta z-score above its threshold or −0.10 below its negative threshold + 0.10 for a confirmed shift to buying or −0.10 for a confirmed shift to selling. Regime is +1 if at least 3 of these 5 are true: fast cumulative-delta EMA > slow cumulative-delta EMA; fast delta EMA > slow delta EMA; volume > volume SMA and close > open; at least 3 consecutive positive delta bars; OBV > its volume-length SMA. Regime is −1 if at least 3 corresponding selling conditions are true, otherwise 0. Delta z-score uses its cumulative-delta lookback mean and standard deviation. Fast/slow delta EMAs use the delta smoothing length and twice that length; fast/slow cumulative-delta EMAs use the delta smoothing length and the longer cumulative-delta lookback. A confirmed shift is a change to a nonzero regime after the prior regime persisted for at least the regime-detection length, with the new regime confirmed for 2 bars
Calculation: Strategy agreement = count of the seven scores (RSI, volume, VWAP, EMA, MACD, Bollinger, Volume Intelligence) above 0.1 for bullish agreement and below −0.1 for bearish agreement
Calculation: Composite = normalized-weighted sum of the seven strategy scores. Each weight starts at its own base weight; when adaptive learning is enabled, update each on each bar by learning rate × (rolling accuracy−0.5), clamped to [0.05,0.6], then normalize weights to sum to 1. Rolling accuracy compares each score with the close move at the outcome-check horizon over the accuracy lookback; ignore moves whose absolute percentage is below the minimum move threshold, and score a call correct when bullish agrees with an upward move or bearish with a downward move
Calculation: Adaptive threshold = base signal threshold × clamp(1.3−0.3×(ATR(14)/SMA(ATR(14),50)), 0.80, 1.10) when adaptive threshold is enabled; otherwise it equals the base threshold
Calculation: Buy/sell signal gate = composite ≥ adaptive threshold for buy or composite ≤ −adaptive threshold for sell; corresponding strategy agreement must meet the minimum; ADX must meet its minimum when the ADX filter is enabled; at least the minimum signal-gap bars must have elapsed. If the candidate reverses the last signal within twice the minimum gap, its composite magnitude must also be at least adaptive threshold × reversal multiplier
Calculation: Bullish pattern = hammer OR bullish engulfing OR morning star OR three white soldiers OR double bottom OR bull flag. Hammer: body < 35% of range, lower shadow > 2×body, upper shadow < 0.5×body, and the prior three closes descend. Bullish engulfing: prior candle bearish with body > 0.3×14-bar average body; current candle bullish with body > 1.1×prior body, wraps prior body, and close two bars ago < close three bars ago. Morning star: bearish candle two bars ago with body > average body; intervening body < 0.5×average; current bullish body > 0.7×average and close above midpoint of the candle two bars ago. Three white soldiers: three bullish candles with bodies > 0.5×average, successive opens and closes rise, and each later open is no higher than the prior close. Double bottom: current low within 0.5×ATR(14) of the lowest low in the pattern lookback, that low occurred 6 to lookback−1 bars ago, an intervening high exceeded it by 1.5×ATR(14), and current close rises and exceeds open. Bull flag: close[5]−close[10] > 2×ATR(14), the three preceding highs descend, 5-bar close standard deviation is <0.6×the standard deviation of closes[5] over 5 bars, and close > prior high
Calculation: Bearish pattern = shooting star OR bearish engulfing OR evening star OR three black crows OR double top OR bear flag. Shooting star: body < 35% of range, upper shadow > 2×body, lower shadow < 0.5×body, and the prior three closes rise. Bearish engulfing: prior candle bullish with body > 0.3×14-bar average body; current candle bearish with body > 1.1×prior body, wraps prior body, and close two bars ago > close three bars ago. Evening star: bullish candle two bars ago with body > average body; intervening body < 0.5×average; current bearish body > 0.7×average and close below midpoint of the candle two bars ago. Three black crows: three bearish candles with bodies > 0.5×average, successive opens and closes fall, and each later open is no lower than the prior close. Double top: current high within 0.5×ATR(14) of the highest high in the pattern lookback, that high occurred 6 to lookback−1 bars ago, an intervening low fell below it by 1.5×ATR(14), and current close falls and is below open. Bear flag: close[10]−close[5] > 2×ATR(14), the three preceding lows rise, 5-bar close standard deviation is <0.6×the standard deviation of closes[5] over 5 bars, and close < prior low
Long: buy signal gate OR bullish pattern OR buy-volume anomaly (volume-delta z-score > its threshold) OR confirmed regime shift from selling to buying
Short: sell signal gate OR bearish pattern OR sell-volume anomaly (volume-delta z-score < negative threshold) OR confirmed regime shift from buying to selling
