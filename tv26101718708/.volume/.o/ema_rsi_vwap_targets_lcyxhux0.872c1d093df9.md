Long: close > EMA AND close > VWAP AND RSI > RSI buy level AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar
Short: close < EMA AND close < VWAP AND RSI < RSI sell level AND the same three conditions were not all true on the previous bar
Calculation: Target levels = for a long position, entry price × (1 + each target percentage); for a short position, entry price × (1 − each target percentage)
Exit: long, high >= any target level
Exit: short, low <= any target level
