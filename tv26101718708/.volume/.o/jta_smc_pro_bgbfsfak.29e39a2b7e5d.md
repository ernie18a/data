Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across equal left and right lookbacks; available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback period.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed structure pivot high AND structure bias is neutral or bearish
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed structure pivot low AND structure bias is neutral or bullish
Long: low > high two bars earlier AND low - high two bars earlier >= configured minimum gap size times ATR
Short: high < low two bars earlier AND low two bars earlier - high >= configured minimum gap size times ATR
Long: bullish CHoCH AND a prior bearish candle exists within three structure lookbacks
Short: bearish CHoCH AND a prior bullish candle exists within three structure lookbacks
Long: the latest two confirmed pattern pivot lows are within the ATR-scaled tolerance AND the latest confirmed pattern pivot high is above both lows by more than that tolerance
Short: the latest two confirmed pattern pivot highs are within the ATR-scaled tolerance AND the latest confirmed pattern pivot low is below both highs by more than that tolerance
