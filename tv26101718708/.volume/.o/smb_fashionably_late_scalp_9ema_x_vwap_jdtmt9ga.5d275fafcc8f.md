Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (HLC3 × volume) / cumulative volume, reset at each new day; use bars through the current bar.
Calculation: Day low/high = lowest low / highest high since the current day began, including the current bar.
Calculation: EMA slope (%) = (current EMA − EMA from its lookback bars ago) / current EMA × 100; VWAP slope (%) is calculated the same way over a longer lookback than the EMA slope.
Calculation: Long stop = VWAP − stop fraction × (VWAP − day low); short stop = VWAP + stop fraction × (day high − VWAP).
Calculation: Long target = VWAP + (VWAP − day low); short target = VWAP − (day high − VWAP).
Calculation: Anticipated cross price = (session VWAP − (1 − α) × current EMA) / α, where α = 2 / (EMA length + 1); long trigger is the greater of this price and close plus one tick, short trigger the lesser of this price and close minus one tick.
Long: Entry mode is anticipate: EMA < session VWAP AND anticipated cross price > close AND day low < session VWAP AND long stop < long trigger < long target AND (minimum reward:risk is disabled OR (long target − long trigger) / (long trigger − long stop) ≥ minimum reward:risk); OR entry mode is react: EMA crosses above session VWAP AND day low < session VWAP AND long stop < close < long target AND (minimum reward:risk is disabled OR (long target − close) / (close − long stop) ≥ minimum reward:risk); AND EMA slope exceeds its positive threshold AND VWAP slope ≤ its positive flat-band threshold AND (turn requirement disabled OR bars since day low ≥ turn lookback) AND (session filter disabled OR time is 10:00–10:45 or 10:46–13:30 America/New_York) AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > its moving average) AND (flat-veto filter disabled OR longest flat EMA run since the latest day extreme is shorter than the veto duration) AND position is flat AND re-entry cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Entry mode is anticipate: EMA > session VWAP AND anticipated cross price < close AND day high > session VWAP AND short target < short trigger < short stop AND (minimum reward:risk is disabled OR (short trigger − short target) / (short stop − short trigger) ≥ minimum reward:risk); OR entry mode is react: EMA crosses below session VWAP AND day high > session VWAP AND short target < close < short stop AND (minimum reward:risk is disabled OR (close − short target) / (short stop − close) ≥ minimum reward:risk); AND EMA slope is below its negative threshold AND VWAP slope ≥ negative flat-band threshold AND (turn requirement disabled OR bars since day high ≥ turn lookback) AND (session filter disabled OR time is 10:00–10:45 or 10:46–13:30 America/New_York) AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume > its moving average) AND (flat-veto filter disabled OR longest flat EMA run since the latest day extreme is shorter than the veto duration) AND position is flat AND re-entry cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target
