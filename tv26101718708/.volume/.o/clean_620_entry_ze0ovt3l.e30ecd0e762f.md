Calculation: MACD line = shorter-lookback EMA(close) − longer-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA(MACD line)
Long: shorter-lookback EMA(close) crosses above longer-lookback EMA(close) AND MACD line > MACD signal line
Short: shorter-lookback EMA(close) crosses below longer-lookback EMA(close) AND MACD line < MACD signal line
