Calculation: Quality score = round(100 × (0.30 × min(|close − open| / ATR, 3) / 3 + 0.30 × min(volume / average volume, 3) / 3 + 0.20 × min(max(displacement distance / ATR, 0), 3) / 3 + 0.20 × trend alignment)); use the current completed bar’s ATR, average volume, and EMA, with longer lookbacks for EMA than for average volume and ATR. Trend alignment is 1 when close is above the EMA for a bullish block or below it for a bearish block, otherwise 0. Displacement distance is close minus the highest high of the prior displacement lookback for bullish blocks, or the lowest low of that lookback minus close for bearish blocks.
Long: completed bar closes above the highest high of the prior displacement lookback AND close > open AND quality score ≥ minimum score AND at least one bearish candle exists in the prior origin lookback
Short: completed bar closes below the lowest low of the prior displacement lookback AND close < open AND quality score ≥ minimum score AND at least one bullish candle exists in the prior origin lookback
