Calculation: Value area = upper and lower edges of the 70% volume area grown from the point of control within its contiguous volume cluster, using a rolling profile of chart bars weighted by age; allocate each bar’s volume by lower-timeframe closes and volume when available, otherwise across its range with the candle body weighted three times the wicks. Use the profile through the current bar.
Calculation: Confirmed value-side transition = price closes beyond an edge, or back inside an edge, for the configured number of consecutive bars, and price moves in the transition direction by more than zero and at least as far as the crossed edge moves toward price over those bars. For re-entry, compare against the edge crossed; use the upper edge for re-entry from above and the lower edge for re-entry from below. Confirm on the closing bar.
Long: Confirmed value-side transition is from inside or below value to above value OR from below value to inside value.
Short: Confirmed value-side transition is from inside or above value to below value OR from above value to inside value.
