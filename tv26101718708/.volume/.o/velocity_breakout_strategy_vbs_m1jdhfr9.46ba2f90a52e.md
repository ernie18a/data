Calculation: Recent low = lowest low over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Higher low = recent low > the running low since the latest fast-EMA/slow-EMA bearish cross; reset that running low to the current low on each bearish cross and update it to the minimum low while fast EMA ≤ slow EMA
Calculation: Run-up % = (close − recent low) / recent low × 100
Calculation: Discount threshold = rolling lowest low + 0.60 × (rolling highest high − rolling lowest low), using the same lookback for both extremes
Calculation: ATR trail = recursively update the prior trail using close, prior close, and loss; loss = ATR × the tight multiplier when ADX is below its threshold and a long position is profitable, otherwise ATR × the base multiplier. If both closes are above the prior trail, use the greater of the prior trail and close − loss; if both are below, use the lesser of the prior trail and close + loss; otherwise use close − loss when close is above the prior trail, or close + loss when below
Calculation: Trail direction = +1 when close crosses above the prior ATR trail, −1 when close crosses below it, otherwise retain the prior direction
Calculation: Master candle bounds = high and low of the current master candle; reset bounds and inside-bar count when close moves above the upper bound or below the lower bound, otherwise increment the count. Arm after the count reaches the required number of bars
Calculation: Bull armed = initially true; set true when close exceeds the prior bar's highest high over a short lookback; set false after a reload signal
Long: Flat position AND confirmed fast EMA crossing above slow EMA AND run-up % ≤ maximum AND (higher low required is false OR higher low) AND (chop filter is disabled OR ATR over a shorter lookback ≥ ATR over a longer lookback)
Long: Flat position AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND bull armed AND lowest low over a short lookback ≤ fast EMA AND (discount-zone filter is disabled OR close ≤ discount threshold) AND ((close > fast EMA AND close ≥ open) OR (lower wick > 1.5 × absolute(open − close) AND close ≥ open)) AND trail direction = +1 AND no confirmed fast EMA crossing above slow EMA on this bar AND (chop filter is disabled OR ATR over a shorter lookback ≥ ATR over a longer lookback)
Long: Flat position AND master candle breakout armed AND close > master candle upper bound AND close ≥ open AND (bull-trend breakout filter is disabled OR (fast EMA > slow EMA AND trail direction = +1)) AND (chop filter is disabled OR ATR over a shorter lookback ≥ ATR over a longer lookback)
Exit: Long, trail direction changes from +1 to −1
Exit: Long, confirmed fast EMA crossing below slow EMA
