Long: Flat position AND current bar is confirmed and within configured date/session filters AND EMA with shorter lookback > EMA with longer lookback AND a long setup was armed by a bar closing at or below the shorter-lookback EMA while the position was flat and the shorter-lookback EMA was above the longer-lookback EMA, with no intervening bar where the shorter-lookback EMA was below the longer-lookback EMA AND current close >= shorter-lookback EMA AND current Parabolic SAR < current low AND previous completed bar's Parabolic SAR > its high
Short: Flat position AND current bar is confirmed and within configured date/session filters AND EMA with shorter lookback < EMA with longer lookback AND a short setup was armed by a bar closing at or above the shorter-lookback EMA while the position was flat and the shorter-lookback EMA was below the longer-lookback EMA, with no intervening bar where the shorter-lookback EMA was above the longer-lookback EMA AND current close <= shorter-lookback EMA AND current Parabolic SAR > current high AND previous completed bar's Parabolic SAR < its low
Calculation: Stop loss = Parabolic SAR on the entry bar - (EMA of true range × ATR buffer) for long; Parabolic SAR on the entry bar + (EMA of true range × ATR buffer) for short
Calculation: Take profit = entry price + (entry price - long stop loss) × risk/reward ratio for long; entry price - (short stop loss - entry price) × risk/reward ratio for short
Exit: Long, current low <= long stop loss OR current high >= long take profit
Exit: Short, current high >= short stop loss OR current low <= short take profit
