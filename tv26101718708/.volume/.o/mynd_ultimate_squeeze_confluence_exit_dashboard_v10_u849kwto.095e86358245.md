Calculation: Squeeze state (each configured timeframe) = Bollinger Bands are the SMA of close over a shared lookback ± a standard-deviation multiplier, inside Keltner Channels centered on the same SMA with bands formed from the SMA of true range over that lookback; tier is 3, 2, 1, or 0 for containment inside the tightest, middle, loosest, or no channel. Squeeze is active inside the loosest channel; it fires when the previous bar was squeezed and the last completed bar is not.
Calculation: Squeeze direction (each configured timeframe) = sign of the linear regression, over the shared squeeze lookback, of close minus the average of the midpoint of the lookback high-low range and the lookback SMA of close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe lean = sign of the sum, across enabled timeframes longer than the timeframe being checked, of each timeframe's squeeze direction multiplied by its tier plus one; use last completed bars. A firing timeframe conflicts when its fired direction opposes this lean.
Long: Any configured timeframe's squeeze direction > 0 OR at least the configured minimum number of enabled timeframes fired bullishly and more timeframes fired bullishly than bearishly, with no firing timeframe opposing the net higher-timeframe lean when that filter is enabled; use last completed bars for the configured timeframes.
Short: Any configured timeframe's squeeze direction < 0 OR at least the configured minimum number of enabled timeframes fired bearishly and more timeframes fired bearishly than bullishly, with no firing timeframe opposing the net higher-timeframe lean when that filter is enabled; use last completed bars for the configured timeframes.
Calculation: Momentum deceleration = z-score of the smoothed second difference of EMA-smoothed RSI; long-side signal when it is at or below a negative threshold, short-side signal when it is at or above the corresponding positive threshold.
Calculation: Climax rejection = volume z-score meets its threshold AND bar range is at least a multiple of ATR AND the bar rejects in the fired direction: for a long, close position in range is at or below one minus a threshold or upper-wick/range meets that threshold; for a short, close position is at or above the threshold or lower-wick/range meets it.
Calculation: Squeeze exit score = weighted mean, scaled to 0–100, of momentum deceleration strength, climax strength, post-fire re-compression, higher-timeframe conflict, and staleness; use chart-resolution bars for these exit calculations and the last completed bars of configured higher timeframes for alignment. Staleness is bars since chart-timeframe squeeze fire exceeding the typical hold length.
Exit: Long, squeeze exit score enters CAUTION or EXIT, OR a squeeze reforms within the configured post-fire window.
Exit: Short, squeeze exit score enters CAUTION or EXIT, OR a squeeze reforms within the configured post-fire window.
