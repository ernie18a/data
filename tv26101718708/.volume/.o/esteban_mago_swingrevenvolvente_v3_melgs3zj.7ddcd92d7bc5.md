Calculation: Equal-high pivot = a high that is greater than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; equal-low pivot = a low that is lower than the lows of that many bars on both sides. An equal level exists when the current confirmed pivot is within the configured percentage tolerance of another confirmed pivot of the same kind in the configured lookback.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume for an up-close bar, negative volume for a down-close bar, and zero for an unchanged close. Average absolute bar delta = simple moving average of the absolute bar delta.
