Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low on the structure timeframe, requiring the configured left and right bars; use the latest confirmed pivots. The structure timeframe bar is the latest available bar (developing in real time).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = current chart high > latest confirmed swing high × (1 + break buffer) / current chart low < latest confirmed swing low × (1 − break buffer), respectively; an event occurs only when the corresponding break was false on the prior chart bar. Classify a bullish event as CHOCH when prior structure bias ≤ 0 and BOS when it equals 1; classify a bearish event as CHOCH when prior bias ≥ 0 and BOS when it equals −1.
Calculation: MACD = EMA(fast) − EMA(slow), its signal line = EMA(MACD, signal), and its histogram = MACD − signal. Calculate classic and fusion profiles on the configured MACD timeframe, or the chart timeframe if none is configured; use the latest available bar, including a developing bar in real time. The fusion profile uses shorter fast, slow, and signal lengths than the classic profile.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback, on the configured ATR timeframe or the chart timeframe if none is configured; use the latest available bar, including a developing bar in real time.
Calculation: Quality score = mean of structure score (bullish break event: 1, bearish break event: 0, otherwise 0.5), momentum score (bullish MACD confirmation: 1, bearish: 0, otherwise 0.5), trend score (close above execution EMA: 1, below: 0, otherwise 0.5), volatility score (ATR / 14-bar SMA of ATR, clamped to [0, 1.5] and divided by 1.5; 1 if the SMA is not positive), and volume score (volume / volume SMA divided by volume threshold, clamped to [0, 1.5] and divided by 1.5; 1 if the volume gate is disabled).
Long: bullish structure break event OR (ATR is available and meets the minimum threshold or that threshold is disabled AND bullish structure break event AND classic MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > 0 AND classic histogram > prior histogram AND [fusion disabled OR (fusion MACD line > signal line AND fusion MACD line > 0 AND fusion histogram > prior histogram)] AND close > directional EMA AND close > execution EMA AND [volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold] AND [quality gate disabled OR quality score ≥ threshold] AND [crypto mode disabled OR impulse fast EMA > impulse slow EMA] AND trade count for the day < daily limit AND cooldown bars elapsed since the previous entry AND no long-only restriction applies to shorts).
Short: bearish structure break event OR (ATR is available and meets the minimum threshold or that threshold is disabled AND bearish structure break event AND classic MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < 0 AND classic histogram < prior histogram AND [fusion disabled OR (fusion MACD line < signal line AND fusion MACD line < 0 AND fusion histogram < prior histogram)] AND close < directional EMA AND close < execution EMA AND [volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold] AND [quality gate disabled OR quality score ≥ threshold] AND [crypto mode disabled OR impulse fast EMA < impulse slow EMA OR impulse fast EMA ≥ impulse slow EMA] AND trade count for the day < daily limit AND cooldown bars elapsed since the previous entry AND short-only restriction disabled).
Exit: Long, low ≤ the greater of (close − ATR × stop multiple) and the active trailing stop, OR high ≥ close + ATR × target multiple, OR short entry condition is true. The trailing stop activates when the highest high since entry − entry price ≥ ATR × activation multiple, and is the highest high since entry − ATR × trail-distance multiple.
Exit: Short, high ≥ the lesser of (close + ATR × stop multiple) and the active trailing stop, OR low ≤ close − ATR × target multiple, OR long entry condition is true. The trailing stop activates when entry price − the lowest low since entry ≥ ATR × activation multiple, and is the lowest low since entry + ATR × trail-distance multiple.
