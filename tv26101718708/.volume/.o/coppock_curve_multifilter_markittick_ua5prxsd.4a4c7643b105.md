Calculation: Coppock Curve = weighted moving average of long-lookback rate of change plus short-lookback rate of change of the source; the long lookback is longer than the short lookback. An optional selected adaptive filter is applied to the source first.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot low in price is lower than the preceding pivot low, while Coppock at the pivots is higher; bearish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot high in price is higher than the preceding pivot high, while Coppock at the pivots is lower. Pivots are strict extrema over equal numbers of bars before and after the pivot.
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × configured stop multiple at entry; each target distance = stop distance × its configured R multiple.
Long: Coppock is at or above zero OR (Coppock crosses above its prior-bar value AND, if enabled, ADX ≥ threshold AND no bearish divergence AND current slope > prior slope AND volume > its moving average AND higher-timeframe Coppock is rising AND the prior falling streak is at least the configured persistence length) OR (Coppock crosses above zero AND, if enabled, ADX ≥ threshold AND |Coppock| > its standard deviation × configured band multiple).
Short: Coppock is below zero OR (Coppock crosses below its prior-bar value AND, if enabled, ADX ≥ threshold AND no bullish divergence AND current slope < prior slope AND volume > its moving average AND higher-timeframe Coppock is not rising AND the prior rising streak is at least the configured persistence length) OR (Coppock crosses below zero AND, if enabled, ADX ≥ threshold AND |Coppock| > its standard deviation × configured band multiple).
Exit: long, an opposing short signal OR, on a bar after entry, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ any target price; a stop hit suppresses target hits on that bar.
Exit: short, an opposing long signal OR, on a bar after entry, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ any target price; a stop hit suppresses target hits on that bar.
Calculation: Entry price = close of the signal bar; long stop price = entry price − stop distance and short stop price = entry price + stop distance. Long targets are above entry and short targets below entry by their respective target distances.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Coppock rising = Coppock on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > Coppock on the preceding completed higher-timeframe bar.
