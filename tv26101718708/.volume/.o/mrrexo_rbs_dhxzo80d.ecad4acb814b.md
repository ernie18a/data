Calculation: Fast/slow high-low bands = selected moving average of high and low over shorter/longer lookbacks; each band midpoint is the mean of its high and low lines.
Calculation: Bullish trend = fast midpoint > slow midpoint and each midpoint > its value at the trend lookback bars ago; bearish trend is the inverse.
Calculation: Bands are consolidated when they overlap or the gap between them is below the minimum band gap.
Calculation: A bullish/bearish pivot is a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same lookback number of bars on both sides; track the latest confirmed pivot of each kind until it is broken.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low/high source is above/below the corresponding high/low source two bars ago by at least the minimum gap size; source is wick or candle body, as selected.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish price gap = current open is above/below the previous bar's close by at least the minimum gap size.
Calculation: Daily, weekly, and monthly bias uses either the current timeframe close versus the previous completed bar's high/low, or fast versus slow moving averages of close on that timeframe; the current timeframe bar may still be forming.
Long: (entry signal spacing has elapsed AND bands are not consolidated AND bullish trend AND (close crosses above a selected band's upper line within the cross lookback after closing below its lower line OR, on a later bar within the return lookback after that cross, low <= the band's upper line AND close >= its lower line OR low <= the band's upper line AND close > its upper line)) OR (entry signal spacing has elapsed AND bands are not consolidated AND close[1] <= fast upper line[1] AND close > fast upper line AND close < slow lower line) OR close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high OR current low source > low source two bars ago by at least the minimum fair value gap size OR open > previous close by at least the minimum price gap size OR daily, weekly, or monthly bias is bullish.
Short: (entry signal spacing has elapsed AND bands are not consolidated AND bearish trend AND (close crosses below a selected band's lower line within the cross lookback after closing above its upper line OR, on a later bar within the return lookback after that cross, high >= the band's lower line AND close <= its upper line OR high >= the band's lower line AND close < its lower line)) OR (entry signal spacing has elapsed AND bands are not consolidated AND close[1] >= fast lower line[1] AND close < fast lower line AND close > slow upper line) OR close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low OR current high source < high source two bars ago by at least the minimum fair value gap size OR open < previous close by at least the minimum price gap size OR daily, weekly, or monthly bias is bearish.
