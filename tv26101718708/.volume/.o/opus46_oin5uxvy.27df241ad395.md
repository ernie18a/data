Calculation: Fast and slow SMMA = smoothed moving averages of HL2, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback; bearish regime means fast SMMA < slow SMMA, bullish means fast SMMA ≥ slow SMMA.
Calculation: ATR-normalized spread = (fast SMMA − slow SMMA) / ATR(14) × 100; spread Z-score = its deviation from its longer-lookback mean divided by its standard deviation.
Calculation: Learned lower/upper bands = prior-bar Donchian low/high over a lookback window, extended outward by ATR(14) times a side-specific multiplier. Each multiplier is the sensitivity factor times the mean of the most recent penetration depths (each depth is wick excess beyond the prior-bar Donchian extreme divided by ATR), clamped to a minimum and maximum; use a default multiplier until enough samples exist.
Calculation: Pressure Z-score = z-score of ((close − blended rolling VWAP) / blended rolling VWAP × 100 × volume / SMA(volume, 20)); blended rolling VWAP weights the longer-lookback VWAP 40% and the shorter-lookback VWAP 60%.
Calculation: Bullish pivot divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while either ATR-normalized spread or RSI(14) at that pivot makes a higher low than at the previous pivot low. Bearish pivot divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while either measure at that pivot makes a lower high than at the previous pivot high. Confirm pivots after the right-side pivot bars.
Long: bullish pivot divergence OR (bar is confirmed AND (low ≤ learned lower band AND bearish regime AND (bearish regime age ≥ minimum bars OR its maximum absolute ATR-normalized spread / 100 ≥ minimum depth AND pressure Z-score was ≤ −capitulation threshold within the recent pain window) OR spread Z-score ≤ buy override threshold) AND number of buys in cycle < maximum AND buy cooldown elapsed AND close is at least the minimum ATR distance from the last buy price AND tail-risk breaker is inactive)
Short: bearish pivot divergence
Exit: long, bar is confirmed AND (high ≥ learned upper band AND bullish regime AND (bullish regime age ≥ minimum bars OR its maximum absolute ATR-normalized spread / 100 ≥ minimum depth) AND high exceeds the high at the last sell (or no prior sell in cycle) AND close is at least the minimum ATR distance above the last sell price (or no prior sell in cycle) AND sell cooldown elapsed AND pressure Z-score was ≥ euphoria threshold within the recent pain window OR spread Z-score ≥ sell override threshold) AND number of sells in cycle < maximum AND position quantity > 0 AND (no prior sell in cycle OR bars since last sell ≥ adaptive sell cooldown), where adaptive sell cooldown = base cooldown × round(1 + min(maximum multiplier − 1, max(0, spread Z-score) × scale))
