Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative(volume × typical price) / cumulative volume, resetting at each selected session, week, or month boundary; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of max(0, cumulative(volume × typical price²) / cumulative volume − VWAP²); each band = VWAP ± standard deviation × its selected multiplier
Calculation: VWAP slope % = 100 × (current VWAP − VWAP from the selected lookback bars ago) / VWAP from the selected lookback bars ago
Calculation: Band width % = 100 × (upper band 1 − lower band 1) / close
Calculation: VWAP deviation in ATR = (close − VWAP) / ATR
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR × selected ATR multiple, or absolute distance from close to the selected structural band; trend mode uses band 1, mean reversion uses band 3
Long: Flat AND (trend mode AND ((VWAP-cross trigger: close crosses above VWAP) OR (band 1-break trigger: close crosses above upper band 1) OR (band 2-break trigger: close crosses above upper band 2)) AND (slope filter disabled OR VWAP slope % > minimum positive slope) AND (secondary indicator is none OR (RSI selected AND RSI > its trend-long threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close > EMA) OR (secondary indicator is none)) OR mean-reversion mode AND ((outside-band trigger: close < selected lower band) OR (band-cross trigger: close crosses below selected lower band) OR (band-reclaim trigger: low < selected lower band AND close > selected lower band)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX < maximum) AND absolute VWAP deviation in ATR ≤ maximum AND (secondary indicator is none OR (RSI selected AND RSI crosses above oversold threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close > EMA))) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × selected multiplier) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 100 × ATR / close > minimum) AND (band-width filter disabled OR band width % > minimum)
Short: Flat AND (trend mode AND ((VWAP-cross trigger: close crosses below VWAP) OR (band 1-break trigger: close crosses below lower band 1) OR (band 2-break trigger: close crosses below lower band 2)) AND (slope filter disabled OR VWAP slope % < negative minimum slope) AND (secondary indicator is none OR (RSI selected AND RSI < its trend-short threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close < EMA)) OR mean-reversion mode AND ((outside-band trigger: close > selected upper band) OR (band-cross trigger: close crosses above selected upper band) OR (band-reclaim trigger: high > selected upper band AND close < selected upper band)) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX < maximum) AND absolute VWAP deviation in ATR ≤ maximum AND (secondary indicator is none OR (RSI selected AND RSI crosses below overbought threshold) OR (EMA regime selected AND close < EMA))) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × selected multiplier) AND (ATR filter disabled OR 100 × ATR / close > minimum) AND (band-width filter disabled OR band width % > minimum)
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop price OR high ≥ current long target; stop is entry price − initial stop distance, raised to at least entry price after high reaches entry price + entry ATR × selected break-even multiple when enabled; target is VWAP, or in trend mode upper band 2 and in mean-reversion mode VWAP, optionally fixed at entry
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop price OR low ≤ current short target; stop is entry price + initial stop distance, lowered to at most entry price after low reaches entry price − entry ATR × selected break-even multiple when enabled; target is VWAP, or in trend mode lower band 2 and in mean-reversion mode VWAP, optionally fixed at entry
