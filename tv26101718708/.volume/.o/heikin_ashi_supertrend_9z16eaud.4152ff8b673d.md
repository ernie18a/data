Calculation: Heikin Ashi Supertrend = On current-timeframe Heikin Ashi bars, calculate ATR and basic bands as (high + low) / 2 ± ATR × multiplier. Carry the lower band forward as max(current lower band, prior lower band) when prior Heikin Ashi close > prior lower band; otherwise use current lower band. Carry the upper band forward as min(current upper band, prior upper band) when prior Heikin Ashi close < prior upper band; otherwise use current upper band. Direction is bullish when current Heikin Ashi close > prior upper band, bearish when it < prior lower band, otherwise retain prior direction (default bullish).
Calculation: Profit target = entry price × (1 + target percentage) for long positions; entry price × (1 − target percentage) for short positions.
Calculation: Stop trigger price = entry price × (1 − stop percentage) × (1 + stop offset) for long positions; entry price × (1 + stop percentage) × (1 − stop offset) for short positions. After the position's high (long) or low (short) crosses entry price adjusted by the move-after percentage, replace the stop price with entry price adjusted by the move-to percentage, then apply the stop offset as above.
Long: Heikin Ashi Supertrend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Heikin Ashi Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: long, close > profit target OR close < stop trigger price AND more than 2 bars have elapsed since entry OR manual close is enabled and current bar time equals the configured close time.
Exit: short, close < profit target OR close > stop trigger price AND more than 2 bars have elapsed since entry OR manual close is enabled and current bar time equals the configured close time.
