Calculation: Active pivot pairs = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs/lows using configurable left and right lookbacks; each pair has a solid level at the pivot high/low and a dashed level at the next bar’s high/low, adjusted to that bar’s body when the two levels are within a configurable proximity. Ignore duplicate same-side pivots within a configurable level tolerance; remove a pair after a close beyond its solid level. Evaluate the newest pair and the nearest remaining pair on each side by distance from close.
Calculation: Dashed rejection count = for each active pair, count consecutive-bar-gap rejections of its dashed level; reset to 1 when the gap since the prior rejection exceeds 10 bars, otherwise increment. Reset to 0 after a qualifying third-or-later rejection.
Long: For an active support pair, low < solid level AND close > dashed level AND close > open; OR low < solid level AND close > open AND close <= dashed level; OR close > solid level after a prior bar had low < solid level AND close > dashed level AND close <= open, AND close > open; OR the close crosses above an active resistance solid level; OR the dashed rejection count reaches 3 AND low < dashed level AND close >= dashed level AND close > open AND abs(close - open) >= (high - low) × configurable body ratio.
Short: For an active resistance pair, high > solid level AND close < dashed level AND close < open; OR high > solid level AND close < open AND close >= dashed level; OR close < solid level after a prior bar had high > solid level AND close < dashed level AND close >= open, AND close < open; OR the close crosses below an active support solid level; OR the dashed rejection count reaches 3 AND high > dashed level AND close <= dashed level AND close < open AND abs(close - open) >= (high - low) × configurable body ratio.
