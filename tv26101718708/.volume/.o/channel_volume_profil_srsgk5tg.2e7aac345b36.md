Calculation: Channel basis = linear regression of close over the channel regression lookback, evaluated on the current bar
Calculation: Channel upper rail = channel basis + max(high − channel basis over the channel lookback) × width multiplier
Calculation: Channel lower rail = channel basis + min(low − channel basis over the channel lookback) × width multiplier
Calculation: Channel range = channel upper rail − channel lower rail
Calculation: Bullish rail sweep = low ≤ channel lower rail + channel range × rail-touch tolerance AND close > channel lower rail
Calculation: Bearish rail sweep = high ≥ channel upper rail − channel range × rail-touch tolerance AND close < channel upper rail
Calculation: Bullish value-area sweep = low ≤ VAL AND close > VAL
Calculation: Bearish value-area sweep = high ≥ VAH AND close < VAH
Calculation: Bullish pivot sweep = low < latest confirmed liquidity low − ATR × sweep tolerance AND close > latest confirmed liquidity low
Calculation: Bearish pivot sweep = high > latest confirmed liquidity high + ATR × sweep tolerance AND close < latest confirmed liquidity high
Calculation: Bullish true sweep = (bullish pivot sweep OR bullish value-area sweep OR bullish rail sweep) AND lower wick / max(abs(close − open), minimum tick) ≥ 0.8 × wick-rejection multiplier
Calculation: Bearish true sweep = (bearish pivot sweep OR bearish value-area sweep OR bearish rail sweep) AND upper wick / max(abs(close − open), minimum tick) ≥ 0.8 × wick-rejection multiplier
Calculation: Bullish A score = 20 if bullish rail sweep + 20 if bullish value-area sweep + 20 if lower wick / body ≥ wick-rejection multiplier, else 10 if that ratio ≥ 0.7 × wick-rejection multiplier + 10 if volume > volume moving average × signal-volume multiplier + 15 if low < POC AND close > POC + 15 if a validated bullish IDM occurred within the IDM confluence window; absent terms contribute 0
