Calculation: Breakout state = +1 if close > prior close and ((Supertrend direction = -1 and price breakout and volume > 1.15 × SMA(volume) or volume is unavailable) or (direction = +1 and price breakout and volume > 1.25 × SMA(volume) or volume is unavailable) or volume > 5 × SMA(volume) or high-breakout); -1 uses the corresponding close < prior close and reversed direction/volume conditions; otherwise 0. Price breakout = |close − prior close| > ATR; high-breakout = |close − prior close| > 2 × ATR. The optional high-low range breakout tests are disabled.
Calculation: Breakout targets = for state +1, up target = high + (direction = -1 ? 15% : 30%) × true range; for state -1, down target = low − (direction = +1 ? 15% : 30%) × true range. True range = max(high − low, |high − prior close|, |low − prior close|). Up/down stop targets = low − ATR / high + ATR, respectively. Each target line carries forward its last nonmissing value.
Calculation: Yearly high/low conditions = use the high/low of the last completed annual bar and its values 365 and 730 chart bars earlier. High condition is (current annual high exceeds both earlier highs, when available) OR (close exceeds current annual high if earlier annual highs are unavailable; otherwise exceeds max(current high, min(the earlier highs))) . Low condition is the corresponding low comparison: current annual low below both earlier lows, OR close below current annual low if earlier annual lows are unavailable; otherwise below min(current low, max(the earlier lows)).
Calculation: New high/low = highest/lowest over the shorter lookback equals highest/lowest over the longer lookback.
Calculation: Moving-average alignment index = sum over the longer lookback of a per-bar score: count the seven bullish comparisons close > SMA(5), SMA(5) > SMA(10), SMA(10) > SMA(20), SMA(20) > SMA(30), SMA(30) > SMA(50), SMA(50) > SMA(100), SMA(100) > SMA(200); score is +1 for all seven, -1 for all seven bearish, otherwise +0.5 if count > 5, -0.5 if count < 2, +0.25 if count > 3, and -0.25 otherwise.
Calculation: MA Supertrend direction = Supertrend direction calculated on SMA(ohlc4, shorter lookback), using ATR; direction flips up when the SMA crosses above the prior short stop and down when it crosses below the prior long stop.
Calculation: Percent rank = percentile rank of ohlc4 over a lookback.
Calculation: Long/short stop = initialize to close − stop multiplier × ATR / close + stop multiplier × ATR. While breakout state is positive or position is long, long stop is close − stop multiplier × ATR and short stop is close + compound multiplier × ATR; carry forward the prior long stop if the new one is lower or close is below the prior short stop, and carry forward the prior short stop if it is lower than the prior short stop or close is below the prior short stop. While breakout state is negative or position is short, long stop is close − compound multiplier × ATR and short stop is close + stop multiplier × ATR; carry forward the prior short stop if the new one is higher or close is above the prior long stop, and carry forward the prior long stop if the new one is higher than the prior long stop or close is above the prior long stop.
Long: Breakout state = +1.
Short: Breakout state = -1.
Long: (crossover(high if Supertrend direction = -1, otherwise low, prior up target line) AND (last breakout state > 0 OR reverse breakouts are ignored) AND close > prior down target line OR crossover(high if direction = -1, otherwise low, prior down stop-target line) AND (last breakout state < 0 OR reverse breakouts are ignored) OR breakout state = +1 AND (high if direction = -1, otherwise low) > prior up target line) AND yearly high condition AND breakout state != -1 AND (new high AND moving-average alignment index > 0 AND MA Supertrend direction = +1 AND percent rank > 70 OR cumulative net profit >= 0) AND (flat OR close > short stop OR immediate compounding is enabled).
Short: (crossunder(low if Supertrend direction = +1, otherwise high, prior down target line) AND (last breakout state < 0 OR reverse breakouts are ignored) AND close < prior up target line OR crossunder(low if direction = +1, otherwise high, prior up stop-target line) AND (last breakout state > 0 OR reverse breakouts are ignored) OR breakout state = -1 AND (low if direction = +1, otherwise high) < prior down target line) AND yearly low condition AND breakout state != +1 AND (new low AND moving-average alignment index < 0 AND MA Supertrend direction = -1 AND percent rank < 30 OR cumulative net profit >= 0) AND (flat OR close < long stop OR immediate compounding is enabled).
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR (signal exits enabled AND (crossunder(close if Supertrend direction = -1, otherwise low, prior down target line) OR crossunder(close if direction = -1, otherwise low, prior up stop-target line))).
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR (signal exits enabled AND (crossover(close if Supertrend direction = +1, otherwise high, prior up target line) OR crossover(close if direction = +1, otherwise high, prior down stop-target line))).
