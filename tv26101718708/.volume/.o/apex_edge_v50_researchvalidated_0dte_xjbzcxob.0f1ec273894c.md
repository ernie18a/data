Calculation: RVOL = current bar volume / short-lookback average volume
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the opening-range session; opening-range direction = opening-range close compared with its open; opening-range volatility = its high minus its low
Calculation: Noise-band upper/lower = current day open ± long-lookback average true range × multiplier
Calculation: Gap return = (current day open − previous completed day's close) / previous completed day's close
Calculation: Rest-of-day return = (current close − current day open) / current day open
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = short-lookback EMA compared with long-lookback EMA, calculated using the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Signal quality score = 2 points for strong RVOL, otherwise 1 for qualifying RVOL; 2 for ADX above its trend threshold and directional DI confirmation; 2 for a primary signal, otherwise 1; 1 when (close > VWAP) equals the raw long-side condition; 1 when (higher-timeframe bullish trend equals raw long-side condition) or (higher-timeframe bearish trend equals raw short-side condition)
Calculation: Initial long stop = opening-range low for an opening-range breakout, otherwise entry price − ATR × stop multiplier; initial short stop = opening-range high for an opening-range breakout, otherwise entry price + ATR × stop multiplier; first target = entry price ± initial risk; second target = entry price ± twice initial risk
Long: Within the configured morning entry window, opening-range session is complete, breakout eligibility window remains open, no opening-range signal has fired today, close > opening-range high, close > open, RVOL meets its threshold, and (higher-timeframe bullish trend or close > VWAP); OR, within the morning entry window, close crosses above the noise-band upper line; OR, within the configured late-day entry window, opening-range direction is bullish, opening-range volatility exceeds an ATR-based threshold, close > VWAP, and volume > its short-lookback average; OR, within that late-day window, rest-of-day return exceeds its threshold, close > VWAP, and volume > its short-lookback average; AND no qualifying gap-down blocks longs during the configured post-opening-range period; AND the hard exit time has not passed, cooldown has elapsed, and signal quality score meets its minimum
Short: Within the configured morning entry window, opening-range session is complete, breakout eligibility window remains open, no opening-range signal has fired today, close < opening-range low, close < open, RVOL meets its threshold, and (higher-timeframe bearish trend or close < VWAP); OR, within the morning entry window, close crosses below the noise-band lower line; OR, within the configured late-day entry window, opening-range direction is bearish, opening-range volatility exceeds an ATR-based threshold, close < VWAP, and volume > its short-lookback average; OR, within that late-day window, rest-of-day return is below its negative threshold, close < VWAP, and volume > its short-lookback average; AND no qualifying gap-up blocks shorts during the configured post-opening-range period; AND the hard exit time has not passed, cooldown has elapsed, and signal quality score meets its minimum
Exit: Long, high ≥ second target OR low ≤ current stop OR bars held ≥ configured time-stop length OR hard exit time has passed
Exit: Short, low ≤ second target OR high ≥ current stop OR bars held ≥ configured time-stop length OR hard exit time has passed
