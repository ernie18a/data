Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the session’s opening range; use the completed range for breakout checks
Calculation: Value-area high/low = opening-range high/low adjusted inward by the same configurable fraction of the opening-range size
Long: after the active session’s opening range is complete, the configured number of consecutive closes are above the opening-range high OR value-area high
Short: after the active session’s opening range is complete, the configured number of consecutive closes are below the opening-range low OR value-area low
Exit: long, close < opening-range high after a bullish breakout
Exit: short, close > opening-range low after a bearish breakout
