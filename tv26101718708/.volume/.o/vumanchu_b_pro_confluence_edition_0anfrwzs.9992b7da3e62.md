Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of the channel index (source minus EMA(source), divided by 0.015 times EMA of its absolute deviation from that EMA); signal line = SMA of WaveTrend. Bullish/bearish cross = WaveTrend crosses above/below its signal line.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator trough, price makes a lower low and oscillator a higher low than at the previous trough; regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator peak, price makes a higher high and oscillator a lower high than at the previous peak. Hidden bullish divergence = higher price low and lower oscillator low; hidden bearish divergence = lower price high and higher oscillator high. A trough/peak is centered two bars before confirmation and is lower/higher than the two bars on either side. WT divergences require the pivot WT value to be at or below the configured bullish limit or at or above the configured bearish limit; RSI divergences use corresponding configured RSI limits.
Calculation: Buy score = configured weighted sum of (WT bullish cross while signal line is oversold, WT bullish divergence, RSI bullish divergence, positive MFI area, Stoch RSI K crossing above D while K is below its configured low threshold, and enabled bullish trend); sell score uses the corresponding bearish cross in the overbought zone, bearish divergences, negative MFI area, K crossing below D above its configured high threshold, and enabled bearish trend.
Long: (PRO mode AND buy score ≥ configured minimum AND enabled ADX, volume, and ATR filters pass AND NOT (enabled trend filter is bearish AND ADX exceeds its configured maximum)) OR (classic mode AND WT bullish cross while signal line is oversold), subject to no same-direction signal within the configured cooldown bars.
Long: WT bullish divergence.
Long: WT bullish divergence AND WT signal line two bars ago ≤ configured extreme oversold level AND current WT signal line > that level AND current WT signal line is at least 5 points above its value two bars ago AND RSI at the most recent WT trough < 30 AND enabled ADX, volume, and ATR filters pass.
Long: RSI bullish divergence.
Short: (PRO mode AND sell score ≥ configured minimum AND enabled ADX, volume, and ATR filters pass AND NOT (enabled trend filter is bullish AND ADX exceeds its configured maximum)) OR (classic mode AND WT bearish cross while signal line is overbought), subject to no same-direction signal within the configured cooldown bars.
Short: WT bearish divergence.
Short: RSI bearish divergence.
