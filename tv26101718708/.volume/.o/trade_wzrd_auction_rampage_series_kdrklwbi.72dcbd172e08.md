Calculation: Forming auction delta = 100 × (buy volume − sell volume) / total volume across chart bars since the current higher-timeframe auction began; each bar’s volume is split by its close’s location within its high-low range. Use the still-forming auction.
Calculation: Auction control price = volume-weighted mean of each auction bar’s typical price, (high + low + close) / 3. Use the still-forming auction.
Calculation: Auction POC = midpoint of the highest-volume price row in the auction profile, with each bar’s volume distributed across its high-low range. Use the last completed higher-timeframe auction for settled POC and extremes; use the still-forming auction for delta and control price.
Calculation: Kinetic fuel = normalize volume × absolute close change to the lowest and highest values over a lookback; larger lookback than the fuel threshold’s parameter. Use chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish or bearish absorption = volume ≥ 2 × 30-bar average volume AND bar range ≤ 0.7 × 30-bar average range; bullish when close location in the range ≥ 0.5, bearish when < 0.5. Use chart bars.
Long: (close crosses above the last completed auction’s POC after at least the acceptance-bar count of closes below it OR forming auction delta crosses above its positive trigger and close > forming auction control price) AND (delta > 0 when auction alignment is required) AND (kinetic fuel ≥ its minimum when enabled) AND (if the delta-strength tier has at least the minimum sample count, its historical win rate ≥ minimum win probability) AND cooldown elapsed AND ATR available AND bar confirmed; OR bullish absorption.
Short: (close crosses below the last completed auction’s POC after at least the acceptance-bar count of closes above it OR forming auction delta crosses below its negative trigger and close < forming auction control price) AND (delta < 0 when auction alignment is required) AND (kinetic fuel ≥ its minimum when enabled) AND (if the delta-strength tier has at least the minimum sample count, its historical win rate ≥ minimum win probability) AND cooldown elapsed AND ATR available AND bar confirmed AND no long signal; OR bearish absorption.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop or high ≥ target or a short signal; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, exit at the stop. For reclaim entries, stop is beyond the last completed POC by an ATR buffer; for drive entries, beyond the forming control price by the buffer. Target is the last completed auction’s far extreme when it is beyond the minimum ATR distance and within the maximum risk multiple; otherwise use the configured risk multiple.
Exit: short, high ≥ stop or low ≤ target or a long signal; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, exit at the stop. For reclaim entries, stop is beyond the last completed POC by an ATR buffer; for drive entries, beyond the forming control price by the buffer. Target is the last completed auction’s far extreme when it is beyond the minimum ATR distance and within the maximum risk multiple; otherwise use the configured risk multiple.
