Calculation: Momentum vote = sign of MACD histogram on the signal timeframe, using the last completed bar; use fast, slow, and signal lookbacks.
Calculation: Stretch vote = sign of (close − EMA(close, fair-value lookback)) on the signal timeframe, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Flow vote = sign of the z-score of cumulative signed volume minus its EMA, on the signal timeframe, using the last completed bar; sign each bar’s volume by its return normalized by the longer return-volatility lookback.
Calculation: Fear vote = sign of (EMA(implied-volatility, trend lookback) − implied-volatility) on the signal timeframe, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Cross-market vote = sign of the weighted average of correlated leader instruments’ normalized momentum, adjusted for each leader’s correlation direction, on the signal timeframe, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Bias = sign of the sum of active axis votes on the higher bias timeframe, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Axis weight = weight floor + max(0, edge lower bound); edge lower bound is the axis’s forward triple-barrier win-rate lower confidence bound minus the direction-matched baseline win rate, measured on the signal timeframe with recency decay and sample-uniqueness weighting. Use equal weights when calibration is disabled.
Calculation: Weighted score = (sum of weights for positive active votes − sum of weights for negative active votes) / sum of active-axis weights; direction is its sign, and method confluence is the count of active votes matching that direction.
Calculation: Conviction = round(100 × absolute weighted score × volatility scale × efficiency scale); volatility scale decreases with the percentile rank of return volatility, and efficiency scale increases with the ratio of net price change to summed absolute price changes over the efficiency lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at confirmed score-line pivot lows, price makes a lower low while the score line makes a higher low; bearish divergence = at confirmed score-line pivot highs, price makes a higher high while the score line makes a lower high. Compare each pivot with the preceding same-type pivot within the maximum pivot gap.
Long: weighted score > 0 AND method confluence ≥ minimum axes agreeing AND conviction ≥ minimum conviction AND (bias gate disabled OR bias = 0 OR bias > 0) AND signal is armed and cooldown has elapsed OR bullish divergence is confirmed, within pivot-gap and divergence-cooldown limits, and is not suppressed by an opposing short direction at or above strong-signal conviction.
Short: weighted score < 0 AND method confluence ≥ minimum axes agreeing AND conviction ≥ minimum conviction AND (bias gate disabled OR bias = 0 OR bias < 0) AND signal is armed and cooldown has elapsed OR bearish divergence is confirmed, within pivot-gap and divergence-cooldown limits, and is not suppressed by an opposing long direction at or above strong-signal conviction.
