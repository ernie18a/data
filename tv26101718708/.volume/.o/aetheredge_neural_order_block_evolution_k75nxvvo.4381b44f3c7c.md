Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the swing-length bars on each side; use it once the bars on both sides have completed.
Calculation: Bullish OB = the nearest bearish candle among the prior obLookback bars when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high; its zone is [min(close, low), max(open, high)] and its displacement is the highest high from the current bar through the bars before that candle minus the zone top. Bearish OB = the nearest bullish candle among the prior obLookback bars when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low; its zone is [min(open, low), max(close, high)] and its displacement is the zone bottom minus the lowest low over the same span.
Calculation: OB quality score = weighted mean of four scores, divided by the sum of their weights: volume score = sigmoid(0.7 × (OB-candle volume − its 20-bar mean) / its 20-bar standard deviation, using zero for the z-score if deviation is zero); liquidity score = sigmoid(1.5 × min(cluster count / 8, 1) + 1.2 × sweep − 1), where cluster count is the number of lows (bullish) or highs (bearish) within 0.5 ATR of the zone edge over the OB candle and following 30 bars, and sweep is 1 when that candle makes a low within 0.1 ATR of the prior 30-bar low and closes above its range midpoint (bullish), or makes a high within 0.1 ATR of the prior 30-bar high and closes below its midpoint (bearish), otherwise 0; MTF score = sigmoid(weighted matching bias sum − 1.5), where each of three configured higher-timeframe biases is +1 when its last completed bar closes above its 21-bar EMA and −1 otherwise, with weights 1, 1.2, and 1.5; displacement score = sigmoid(displacement / ATR − minimum displacement multiple). sigmoid(x) = 1 / (1 + exp(−x)).
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high AND bullish OB displacement > ATR × minimum displacement multiple AND bullish OB quality score ≥ minimum quality AND no unbroken bullish OB has zone overlap divided by candidate zone size > 0.5
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low AND bearish OB displacement > ATR × minimum displacement multiple AND bearish OB quality score ≥ minimum quality AND no unbroken bearish OB has zone overlap divided by candidate zone size > 0.5
Exit: Long, an active bullish OB is broken: close (close mitigation) or low (wick mitigation) < its zone bottom − ATR × break buffer
Exit: Short, an active bearish OB is broken: close (close mitigation) or high (wick mitigation) > its zone top + ATR × break buffer
