Calculation: Active liquidity walls = on each completed bar, decay each active wall's intensity by a factor of 0.5^(1/half-life); remove walls touched at or above their price for above-price walls or at or below their price for below-price walls, using the high/low when wick purge is enabled and the candle body otherwise; remove walls whose decayed intensity falls below 0.04 times the volume moving average. Add walls at hlc3 × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance-margin fraction) below price and hlc3 × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance-margin fraction) above price for leverage tiers 100, 50, 25, and 10; each has intensity volume × (0.35 + min(volume / volume moving average, 4) × 0.65) × its profile weight. Merge same-side walls within ATR × merge-distance by summing intensity. Add confirmed pivot highs as above-price walls and pivot lows as below-price walls, each with intensity equal to pivot-bar volume × stop-liquidity weight; merge them by the same rule. If the active-wall limit is exceeded, remove the weakest walls.
Long: an active below-price wall is within the maximum magnet distance from close AND its intensity is greater than every other active below-price wall within that distance.
Short: an active above-price wall is within the maximum magnet distance from close AND its intensity is greater than every other active above-price wall within that distance.
