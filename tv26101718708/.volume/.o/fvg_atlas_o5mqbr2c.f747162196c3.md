Calculation: Confirmed fair value gap zone = from the last three completed bars of a selected timeframe, aggregated from chart bars: bullish when bar 3 low > bar 1 high, with zone [bar 1 high, bar 3 low]; bearish when bar 3 high < bar 1 low, with zone [bar 3 high, bar 1 low]. Confirm when the next bar of that timeframe begins.
Long: A confirmed bullish fair value gap has its first wick-range overlap on a confirmed chart bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top), AND within the configured chart-bar window including the touch bar a confirmed close > zone top, before any confirmed close <= zone bottom.
Short: A confirmed bearish fair value gap has its first wick-range overlap on a confirmed chart bar (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top), AND within the configured chart-bar window including the touch bar a confirmed close < zone bottom, before any confirmed close >= zone top.
