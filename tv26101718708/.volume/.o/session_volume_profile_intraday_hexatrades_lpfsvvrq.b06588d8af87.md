Calculation: Session POC, VAH, VAL = For the current developing profile session, divide its high-low range into equal price bins; distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the bins spanned by its low-high range. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bin. Starting there, expand the value area toward the higher-volume adjacent bin until it contains the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges. Use confirmed bars, including the current bar.
Long: close > Session POC AND previous close <= previous bar’s Session POC OR close > Session VAH AND previous close <= previous bar’s Session VAH OR close > the last completed day’s high AND previous close <= that level
Short: close < Session POC AND previous close >= previous bar’s Session POC OR close < Session VAL AND previous close >= previous bar’s Session VAL OR close < the last completed day’s low AND previous close >= that level
