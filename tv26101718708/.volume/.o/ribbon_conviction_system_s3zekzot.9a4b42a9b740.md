Calculation: Ribbon = 8-period HMA, 13-period EMA, and 21-, 34-, and 55-period KAMAs; the 8-period HMA is the crossover line and the others are selectable crossover lines.
Calculation: Conviction score = the normalized weighted mean of directional lower-timeframe buy/sell flow, effort-versus-move, chart-timeframe Kaufman Efficiency Ratio, and value-location readings; reduce it with the flow-toxicity proxy and scale it by agreement from adaptive volatility-stop directions on 3×, 5×, and 15× timeframes. Lower-timeframe flow uses sub-bars within the current chart bar; the higher-timeframe stop directions use bars still forming.
Calculation: Value-location readings combine the current anchor-period VWAP slope, swing-anchored VWAP position and slope, current anchor-period value area, and prior anchor-period VWAP and POC. Use the prior completed period for prior VWAP and POC; use the current, forming period for VWAP and value area.
Long: The 8-period HMA crosses above any selected ribbon line AND conviction score ≥ configured minimum AND (clearance filter disabled OR close is above the crossed line by the configured ATR multiple) AND (session filter disabled OR the bar is after the configured opening-bar exclusion); OR the 8-period HMA crosses above the 55-period KAMA AND conviction score ≥ configured minimum AND the session filter is disabled or the bar is after the configured opening-bar exclusion.
Short: The 8-period HMA crosses below any selected ribbon line AND conviction score ≥ configured minimum AND (clearance filter disabled OR close is below the crossed line by the configured ATR multiple) AND (session filter disabled OR the bar is after the configured opening-bar exclusion); OR the 8-period HMA crosses below the 55-period KAMA AND conviction score ≥ configured minimum AND the session filter is disabled or the bar is after the configured opening-bar exclusion.
