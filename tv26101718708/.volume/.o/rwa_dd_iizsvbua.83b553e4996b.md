Calculation: RSI = standard RSI of close
Calculation: RSI EMA = EMA of RSI with a shorter lookback than RSI WMA
Calculation: RSI WMA = WMA of RSI with a longer lookback than RSI EMA
Long: RSI EMA crosses above RSI WMA OR, when a new RSI pivot low is confirmed, price makes a lower low and RSI makes a higher low than at the previous confirmed RSI pivot low, with pivot separation within the configured divergence range
Short: RSI EMA crosses below RSI WMA OR, when a new RSI pivot high is confirmed, price makes a higher high and RSI makes a lower high than at the previous confirmed RSI pivot high, with pivot separation within the configured divergence range
