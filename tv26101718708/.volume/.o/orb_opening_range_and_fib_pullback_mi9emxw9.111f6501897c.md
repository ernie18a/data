Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low from bars during the configured opening interval; opening price = first bar's open; closing price = last bar's close
Calculation: Opening range direction = bullish when its closing price > its opening price; otherwise bearish
Calculation: Fib pullback levels = 50%, 61.8%, and 70% retracements of the opening range, measured down from its high for a bullish range and up from its low for a bearish range
Calculation: Pullback zone = ATR × configured zone tolerance
Calculation: Bullish confirmation = a bullish candle with lower wick ≥ body × configured wick ratio OR a bullish engulfing candle (previous candle bearish, current candle bullish, current close ≥ previous open, and current open ≤ previous close)
Calculation: Bearish confirmation = a bearish candle with upper wick ≥ body × configured wick ratio OR a bearish engulfing candle (previous candle bullish, current candle bearish, current close ≤ previous open, and current open ≥ previous close)
Calculation: Heavy volume = current volume > average volume over the configured lookback × configured volume multiplier
Long: Opening range completed AND current time is within the configured signal window AND current bar is outside the opening interval AND opening range direction is bullish AND current low is within the pullback zone of any enabled Fib pullback level AND bullish confirmation
Long: Opening range completed AND current time is within the configured signal window AND current bar is outside the opening interval AND current close > opening range high AND heavy volume
Short: Opening range completed AND current time is within the configured signal window AND current bar is outside the opening interval AND opening range direction is bearish AND current high is within the pullback zone of any enabled Fib pullback level AND bearish confirmation
Short: Opening range completed AND current time is within the configured signal window AND current bar is outside the opening interval AND current close < opening range low AND heavy volume
