Calculation: Overall % change = 100 × (current close − open N bars ago) / open N bars ago
Calculation: Recent high % = 100 × (highest high from N bars ago through current bar − open N bars ago) / open N bars ago
Calculation: Recent low % = 100 × (lowest low from N bars ago through current bar − open N bars ago) / open N bars ago
Calculation: Previous-open-to-high % = 100 × (that recent high − previous bar open) / previous bar open
Calculation: Previous-open-to-low % = 100 × (that recent low − previous bar open) / previous bar open
Calculation: Previous-bar % change = 100 × (previous bar close − previous bar open) / previous bar open
Calculation: True dip = Overall % change < 0 AND Recent high % > 0 AND Recent low % < 0 AND Previous-open-to-high % > 0 AND Previous-open-to-low % < 0 AND Previous-bar % change < 0
Calculation: True peak = Overall % change > 0 AND Recent high % > 0 AND Recent low % > 0 AND Previous-open-to-high % > 0 AND Previous-open-to-low % < 0 AND Previous-bar % change > 0
Calculation: RSI high threshold = rounded average of RSI values above the configured minimum over the current bar and the preceding configured lookback bars
Calculation: RSI low threshold = rounded average of RSI values below the configured minimum at lookback offsets 5, 10, 15, … through the configured lookback
Calculation: RSI EMA fast = double EMA of RSI using the fast length; RSI EMA slow = double EMA of RSI using the slow length (fast length shorter than slow length)
Long: Flat position AND long trading enabled AND within configured date window AND ((stochastic strategy selected AND K crosses above D AND both K and D are rising AND K < RSI AND D < RSI) OR (RSI cross-back strategy selected AND RSI EMA > RSI AND K < D AND RSI crosses above oversold AND Overall % change > 0) OR (MACD strategy selected AND RSI EMA > RSI AND K < D AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI < RSI low threshold AND Overall % change < 0) OR (Connors RSI strategy selected AND RSI EMA > RSI AND K < D AND 20-period SMA of close < 50-period SMA of close AND 50-period SMA of close > close AND RSI <= oversold AND True dip) OR (EMA strategy selected AND RSI EMA crosses above both fast and slow RSI EMAs AND fast RSI EMA > slow RSI EMA AND EMA(RSI, RSI length) >= its previous value) OR (default strategy AND RSI < oversold AND True dip) OR (RSI < oversold AND True dip))
Short: Flat position AND short trading enabled AND within configured date window AND ((stochastic strategy selected AND K crosses below D AND both K and D are falling AND K < RSI AND D < RSI) OR (RSI cross-back strategy selected AND RSI EMA < RSI AND D < K AND RSI crosses below overbought AND Overall % change < 0) OR (MACD strategy selected AND RSI EMA < RSI AND D < K AND MACD line < signal line AND RSI > RSI high threshold AND Overall % change > 0) OR (Connors RSI strategy selected AND RSI EMA < RSI AND D < K AND 3-period SMA of close > 20-period SMA of close AND current open > 50-period SMA of close AND RSI >= overbought AND True peak) OR (EMA strategy selected AND RSI EMA crosses below both fast and slow RSI EMAs AND fast RSI EMA < slow RSI EMA AND EMA(RSI, RSI length) <= its previous value) OR (default strategy AND RSI EMA < RSI AND D < K AND RSI > overbought AND True peak) OR (RSI > overbought AND True peak))
Exit: Long, close <= entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss fraction) OR close >= entry price × (1 + configured target-profit fraction) OR (short trading enabled AND RSI EMA < RSI AND D < K AND RSI > overbought AND True peak)
Exit: Short, close >= entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss fraction) OR close <= entry price × (1 − configured target-profit fraction) OR (long trading enabled AND RSI < oversold AND True dip)
