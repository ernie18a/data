Calculation: Prior completed higher-timeframe swing high = highest high over the lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, excluding the most recent completed bar
Calculation: Prior completed higher-timeframe swing low = lowest low over the lookback of completed higher-timeframe bars, excluding the most recent completed bar
Calculation: Bullish retracement zone = from swing high − (swing range × long maximum retracement) to swing high − (swing range × long minimum retracement), where swing range = swing high − swing low
Calculation: Bearish retracement zone = from swing low + (swing range × short minimum retracement) to swing low + (swing range × short maximum retracement)
Calculation: Bullish fib touch = close is within the bullish retracement zone on any of the current or preceding allowed number of bars
Calculation: Bearish fib touch = close is within the bearish retracement zone on any of the current or preceding allowed number of bars
Long: Longs allowed AND (Supertrend bullish if its filter is enabled) AND (ADX above its threshold AND ADX rising if required, if its filter is enabled) AND (ATR above its moving average AND ATR as a percentage of close at least its minimum if its filter is enabled) AND (volume above its average times its multiplier AND close > open AND volume > prior bar volume if required, if its filter is enabled) AND (bullish fib touch if its filter is enabled) AND (close > prior bar high if required) AND (absolute close − open at least ATR times its minimum body multiplier if required) AND ((Donchian mode AND (close > prior Donchian high if close break is required, otherwise high > prior Donchian high)) OR (Pullback mode AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND lowest low over the pullback lookback <= fast EMA AND close > prior bar high) OR (Keltner mode AND close > upper Keltner band AND close > prior bar high)) AND (prior bar's raw long condition is false if one signal per side is enabled)
Short: Shorts allowed AND (Supertrend bearish if its filter is enabled) AND (ADX above its threshold AND ADX rising if required, if its filter is enabled) AND (ATR above its moving average AND ATR as a percentage of close at least its minimum if its filter is enabled) AND (volume above its average times its multiplier AND close < open AND volume > prior bar volume if required, if its filter is enabled) AND (bearish fib touch if its filter is enabled) AND (close < prior bar low if required) AND (absolute close − open at least ATR times its minimum body multiplier if required) AND ((Donchian mode AND (close < prior Donchian low if close break is required, otherwise low < prior Donchian low)) OR (Pullback mode AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND highest high over the pullback lookback >= fast EMA AND close < prior bar low) OR (Keltner mode AND close < lower Keltner band AND close < prior bar low)) AND (prior bar's raw short condition is false if one signal per side is enabled)
