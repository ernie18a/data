Calculation: Hull mid = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half Hull length) − WMA(close, full Hull length), square-root Hull length)
Calculation: Inner cloud upper/lower = Hull mid ± ATR × cloud-width multiplier
Calculation: ADX = RMA(DX, ADX length), where +DM = max(high − previous high, 0) only when high − previous high > previous low − low, otherwise 0; −DM = max(previous low − low, 0) only when previous low − low > high − previous high, otherwise 0; +DI and −DI = 100 × RMA(respective DM, ADX length) / RMA(true range, ADX length); DX = 100 × abs(+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI), or 0 when their sum is 0
Calculation: Retest score = integer part of min(100, proximity score + volume score + RSI score + ADX score). Proximity score = min(50 × penetration depth / cloud width, 50), where long penetration depth = max(inner cloud upper − low, 0), short penetration depth = max(high − inner cloud lower, 0), and cloud width = ATR × cloud-width multiplier. Volume score = min(15 × volume / max(SMA(volume, volume lookback), 1), 25). Long RSI score = min(0.5 × max(55 − RSI, 0), 15); short RSI score = min(0.5 × max(RSI − 45, 0), 15). ADX score = min(0.4 × ADX, 10).
Long: low ≤ inner cloud upper AND close > Hull mid AND close > open AND EMA(close, half Hull length) > EMA(close, full Hull length) AND long retest score ≥ minimum score AND bar is closed AND at least max(3 × Hull length, 50) bars have elapsed
Short: high ≥ inner cloud lower AND close < Hull mid AND close < open AND EMA(close, half Hull length) < EMA(close, full Hull length) AND short retest score ≥ minimum score AND bar is closed AND at least max(3 × Hull length, 50) bars have elapsed
