Calculation: Relative strength = (instrument close / instrument close 10 bars ago) / (benchmark close / benchmark close 10 bars ago), using benchmark bars on the same timeframe
Calculation: Fund-flow score = count of true conditions among: instrument close above its long-term SMA AND shorter-term SMA above long-term SMA; volume above its average; RSI above its threshold AND rising over its lookback; relative strength above its threshold; benchmark shorter-term SMA above long-term SMA
Long: Fund-flow score at or above its threshold AND (instrument close above its long-term SMA AND below that SMA times the accumulation multiplier OR instrument close above the highest high over the breakout lookback, including the current bar, AND relative volume above its breakout threshold)
