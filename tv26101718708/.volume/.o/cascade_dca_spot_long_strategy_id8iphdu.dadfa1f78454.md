Calculation: Lateral zone = Bollinger Band Width below its threshold OR Average Directional Index (ADX) below its threshold; use AND instead if the require-both setting is enabled.
Calculation: DCA drop at level n = first DCA drop + (n−1) × linear increment, or first DCA drop × geometric multiplier^(n−1), according to the selected progression.
Calculation: DCA trigger at level n = initial entry price × (1 − DCA drop at level n / 100).
Calculation: Volume-Weighted Momentum (VWM) = EMA of (ROC of close × current volume / SMA of volume), using separate lookbacks for ROC, volume SMA, and EMA smoothing.
Long: Flat, initial entry not yet made, close ≤ highest high over the swing lookback × (1 − initial drop percentage / 100), and in the lateral zone.
Long: Flat after a position has closed, trend re-entry is enabled, Supertrend is bullish, ADX ≥ trend-strength threshold, +DI > −DI, close > trend EMA, close > close from the slope lookback bars ago, and not in the lateral zone.
Long: In a position with a pending DCA level, that level is within the configured maximum; the cycle low since the last fill has touched or crossed its trigger and is at least the minimum gap below the last fill; the DCA is armed; no new cycle low has occurred for at least the confirmation-bar count; close ≥ cycle low × (1 + rebound percentage / 100); the candle is green if required; and in the lateral zone. If rebound confirmation is disabled, use close ≤ the pending DCA trigger instead of the armed, confirmation, rebound, and optional green-candle conditions. Execute at most one DCA per bar.
Exit: Long, close > average position cost × (1 + minimum profit percentage / 100) AND (VWM has declined for the required consecutive bars and VWM that many bars ago is above its threshold OR enabled Supertrend flips from bullish to bearish).
Exit: Long, rescue mode is active after the configured number of DCA fills, breakeven has been armed by close reaching average position cost × (1 + breakeven-arm percentage / 100), and open profit ≤ 0.
