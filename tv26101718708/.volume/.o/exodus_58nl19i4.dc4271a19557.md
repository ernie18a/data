Calculation: SPR = During the regular trading session, (close − previous completed session’s close) × cumulative session volume; subtract this score from the score N bars earlier, then divide by ATR. Valid only during the session when the previous session close is available.
Calculation: VWMO = SMA((close − close N bars earlier) × volume / max(SMA(volume, L), 1), S).
Calculation: VEI = SMA(ΔM − EMA(ΔI, E), 3), where efficiency = (high − low) / max(volume, 1), inertia = max(volume, 1) / max(high − low, minimum price increment × 0.1), M = EMA(efficiency, E) − M N bars earlier, and I = EMA(inertia, E) − I N bars earlier.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over L bars / (highest high − lowest low over L bars)) / log10(L).
Long: SPR > SPR threshold AND VWMO > VWMO threshold AND VEI > VEI threshold AND ADX > minimum ADX AND ATR > SMA(ATR, L) × minimum ATR multiplier AND ATR > ATR 10 bars earlier × expansion multiplier AND Choppiness Index < maximum AND NOT (|SPR| < SPR threshold / 2 AND |VWMO| < VWMO threshold / 2 AND |VEI| < VEI threshold / 2)
Short: SPR < −SPR threshold AND VWMO < −VWMO threshold AND VEI < −VEI threshold AND ADX > minimum ADX AND ATR > SMA(ATR, L) × minimum ATR multiplier AND ATR > ATR 10 bars earlier × expansion multiplier AND Choppiness Index < maximum AND NOT (|SPR| < SPR threshold / 2 AND |VWMO| < VWMO threshold / 2 AND |VEI| < VEI threshold / 2)
Exit: long, low ≤ close − ATR × stop multiplier OR high ≥ close + ATR × target multiplier OR fewer than two of SPR > SPR threshold, VWMO > VWMO threshold, and VEI > VEI threshold are true
Exit: short, high ≥ close + ATR × stop multiplier OR low ≤ close − ATR × target multiplier OR fewer than two of SPR < −SPR threshold, VWMO < −VWMO threshold, and VEI < −VEI threshold are true
