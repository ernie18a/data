Calculation: Bollinger bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × standard deviation of close over that lookback
Calculation: Keltner bands = the same SMA(close) basis ± multiplier × SMA(true range) over the band lookback
Calculation: Z-score = (close − EMA(close, lookback)) / standard deviation of close over that lookback; use 0 when standard deviation is 0
Calculation: Up/down exhaustion score = round(100 × (0.30 × min(1, consecutive up/down closes / run length) + 0.30 × min(1, max(0, ±Z-score) / stretch threshold) + 0.25 × min(1, max(0, RSI − overbought level / oversold level − RSI) / max(1, 100 − overbought level / oversold level)) + 0.15 × min(1, (high − low) / ATR / volatility-spike threshold)); use the up form for up closes, positive Z-score, and RSI above overbought; use the down form for down closes, negative Z-score, and RSI below oversold
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = number of true conditions among low ≤ lower Bollinger or Keltner band; RSI ≤ oversold; close < previous close and RSI > previous RSI and RSI < 50; volume / SMA(volume, lookback) ≥ volume threshold (use 1 if the SMA is 0); and Z-score ≤ −stretch threshold
Calculation: Bearish confluence count = number of true conditions among high ≥ upper Bollinger or Keltner band; RSI ≥ overbought; close > previous close and RSI < previous RSI and RSI > 50; volume / SMA(volume, lookback) ≥ volume threshold (use 1 if the SMA is 0); and Z-score ≥ stretch threshold
Calculation: Bullish reversal = bullish engulfing (close > open, previous close < previous open, close ≥ previous open, and open ≤ previous close) OR a bullish pin (low below either lower band, close above lower Bollinger band, lower wick / bar range ≥ wick fraction, and close > open)
Calculation: Bearish reversal = bearish engulfing (close < open, previous close > previous open, close ≤ previous open, and open ≥ previous close) OR a bearish pin (high above either upper band, close below upper Bollinger band, upper wick / bar range ≥ wick fraction, and close < open)
Long: bullish reversal AND down exhaustion score ≥ minimum exhaustion AND bullish confluence count ≥ minimum confluences AND down exhaustion score > up exhaustion score AND no signal within the cooldown interval
Short: bearish reversal AND up exhaustion score ≥ minimum exhaustion AND bearish confluence count ≥ minimum confluences AND up exhaustion score ≥ down exhaustion score AND no signal within the cooldown interval AND long condition is false
Calculation: Signal stop = for a long, min(signal-bar low, previous-bar low) − ATR × stop padding; for a short, max(signal-bar high, previous-bar high) + ATR × stop padding
Calculation: Initial risk = absolute value of entry price − signal stop; use ATR if this is 0. First target = for a long, entry price + initial risk × first-target multiple; for a short, entry price − initial risk × first-target multiple
Exit: Long, high ≥ first target OR low ≤ signal stop OR a new long or short signal occurs
Exit: Short, low ≤ first target OR high ≥ signal stop OR a new long or short signal occurs
