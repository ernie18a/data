Calculation: Signed volume imbalance = buy volume minus sell volume; classify each lower-timeframe bar as buy/sell by close versus open, splitting equal bars evenly; or split each bar's volume by the normal CDF of its return divided by the standard deviation of returns; or split volume by close position within the bar's range. If total classified volume is zero, use the range-based split. Imbalance fraction = signed volume imbalance / total classified volume.
Calculation: OFI z-score = selected robust (current signed volume imbalance minus its rolling median, divided by 1.4826 times the rolling median absolute deviation) or classic (minus rolling mean, divided by rolling standard deviation) standardization; clamp to [-4, 4].
Calculation: Buy and sell cascade intensities = prior intensity times exponential decay, plus excitation gain times positive imbalance fraction if it exceeds the event threshold (buy), or times its absolute value if it is below the negative event threshold (sell). Cascade direction = buy intensity minus sell intensity. Cascade strength = percentile rank of the sum of both intensities over its rolling window.
Calculation: Cross-asset OFI z-score = standard score of signed volume imbalance estimated from the correlated symbol's candle geometry. Cross-asset confirmation = previous completed bar's cross-asset z-score has the same sign as current OFI z-score and absolute value greater than 0.5.
Calculation: Volume percentile and range percentile = percentile ranks of current bar volume and high-low range over the rolling history; use the selected time-of-day-normalized volume instead of raw volume when enabled.
Calculation: Close position = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0.5 when range is zero.
Calculation: Prior high and prior low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Long: Buy cascade direction > 0 AND cascade strength > 60 AND OFI z-score > 0.5 AND (cross-asset confirmation disabled OR cross-asset confirmation) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar is complete) AND previous bar did not meet the buy-cascade conditions.
Short: Buy cascade direction < 0 AND cascade strength > 60 AND OFI z-score < -0.5 AND (cross-asset confirmation disabled OR cross-asset confirmation) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar is complete) AND previous bar did not meet the sell-cascade conditions.
Long: Volume is available and positive AND low <= prior low AND close position >= 0.65 AND volume percentile >= 60.
Short: Volume is available and positive AND high >= prior high AND close position <= 0.35 AND volume percentile >= 60.
Long: Volume is available and positive AND close < previous close AND range percentile >= 60 AND volume percentile >= 80 AND close position >= 0.45.
Short: Volume is available and positive AND close > previous close AND volume >= highest volume over the rolling lookback including the current bar AND range percentile >= 70.
Long: Volume is available and positive AND close < previous close AND volume >= highest volume over the rolling lookback including the current bar AND range percentile >= 70.
