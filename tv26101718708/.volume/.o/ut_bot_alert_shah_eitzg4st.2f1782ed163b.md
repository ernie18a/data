Calculation: UT trailing stop = initialize at close − ATR-based loss; thereafter, if current and previous close are above the previous stop, use the greater of the previous stop and close − loss; if both are below it, use the lesser of the previous stop and close + loss; otherwise use close − loss when close is above the previous stop, else close + loss. ATR-based loss = ATR × key multiplier.
Long: close crosses above the UT trailing stop AND shorter-period EMA(close) > longer-period EMA(close) AND close > longer-period EMA(close) AND shorter-period EMA(close) > longer-period EMA(close) on the last completed 15-minute bar AND ADX > minimum AND Supertrend is bullish AND volume > 20-bar SMA(volume) AND bar time is in the 03:00–12:00 or 08:00–17:00 session AND more than the cooldown bar count has elapsed since the last entry AND position side is flat or short.
Short: close crosses below the UT trailing stop AND shorter-period EMA(close) < longer-period EMA(close) AND close < longer-period EMA(close) AND shorter-period EMA(close) < longer-period EMA(close) on the last completed 15-minute bar AND ADX > minimum AND Supertrend is bearish AND volume > 20-bar SMA(volume) AND bar time is in the 03:00–12:00 or 08:00–17:00 session AND more than the cooldown bar count has elapsed since the last entry AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, high reaches any of the five successive profit targets above entry price OR low reaches the active stop; active stop starts below entry price, moves to entry after the first target is reached, then advances to the preceding target each time the next target is reached through the fourth target.
Exit: short, low reaches any of the five successive profit targets below entry price OR high reaches the active stop; active stop starts above entry price, moves to entry after the first target is reached, then advances to the preceding target each time the next target is reached through the fourth target.
