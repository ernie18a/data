Calculation: Range SMA = moving average of high − low over the volatility lookback
Calculation: Custom trend bands = hl2 ± multiplier × Range SMA; trail the lower band upward unless the prior close is below its prior value, and trail the upper band downward unless the prior close is above its prior value. Trend is up when close is above the active upper band (or the prior stop was the upper band); otherwise down when close is below the active lower band, and up otherwise. Initialize trend up while the prior Range SMA is unavailable.
Calculation: Bullish order block = on an uptrend bar confirming a pivot low with equal left and right pivot lookbacks, top is the lower of the pivot bar's open and close; bottom is top minus current Range SMA. Ignore it if it overlaps the active block.
Calculation: Bearish order block = on a downtrend bar confirming a pivot high with equal left and right pivot lookbacks, bottom is the higher of the pivot bar's open and close; top is bottom plus current Range SMA. Ignore it if it overlaps the active block.
Calculation: Active block buy-volume ratio = volume on bars with close ≥ open, divided by total volume, across the confirmation bar and the preceding pivot-lookback bars; use 0.5 if total volume is zero. Sell-volume ratio = 1 − buy-volume ratio.
Long: low crosses above the active bullish order block top AND active block buy-volume ratio ≥ minimum buy-volume threshold AND no pivot low is confirmed AND trend direction did not change on this bar AND bar is confirmed
Short: high crosses below the active bearish order block bottom AND active block sell-volume ratio ≥ minimum sell-volume threshold AND no pivot high is confirmed AND trend direction did not change on this bar AND bar is confirmed
