Calculation: Open crossover pair = selected moving average of current chart bar open and selected moving average of previous chart bar open, sampled on the configured multiple of the chart timeframe and held between refreshes; use the higher-timeframe value available at refresh, including a forming bar when returned
Calculation: Variant filter = selected moving average of close on its configured higher timeframe, using the value available at refresh
Calculation: RSI filter = RSI of close on its configured higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: ADX and directional indicators = DMI values on their configured higher timeframes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bars; uptrend = EMA(2) of (+DI − −DI) > 0
Long: the open crossover pair's first value crosses above its second value at the configured cross offset AND (ATR >= minimum ATR when enabled) AND (RSI is between its lower and upper thresholds when enabled) AND (close >= variant filter when enabled) AND (ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds when enabled) AND (uptrend when enabled) AND (the second ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds when enabled) AND (the second uptrend condition when enabled)
Exit: long, the selected moving average of current chart bar open < entry price × (1 − fixed-loss fraction) OR entry price <= entry price × (1 − fixed-loss fraction) OR entry price >= entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) OR close > entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction)
