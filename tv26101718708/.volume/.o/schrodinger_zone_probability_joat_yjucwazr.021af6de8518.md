Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago, the prior close > high two bars ago, and the gap ≥ ATR × minimum-gap multiple.
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago, the prior close < low two bars ago, and the gap ≥ ATR × minimum-gap multiple.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the selected qualifying bearish candle within the look-back window before an upward displacement bar; bearish order-block zone = the selected qualifying bullish candle before a downward displacement bar. Displacement requires body ≥ ATR × displacement multiple and body/range ≥ 0.55; the selected candle has range ≤ 2.5 × ATR and body/range ≥ 0.25. Strict-volume mode also requires its volume ≥ volume moving average; extreme mode selects the lowest low for bullish zones or highest high for bearish zones, otherwise select the most recent qualifying candle.
Calculation: Support probability = clamp(0.5 + 0.5 × side score, 0.01, 0.99); resistance probability = 1 − support probability. Side score = (normalized direction weight × clamped((close − zone midpoint) / (4 × zone height), −1, 1) + normalized flow weight × flow bias) × (0.4 + 0.6 × (normalized distance weight × distance pull + (1 − normalized distance weight) × 0.5)). Distance pull = exp(−ln(2) × (abs(close − zone midpoint) / max(current ATR, half the zone-formation ATR)) / distance half-life in ATR units). Flow bias = clamp(((EMA(close, flow window) − prior EMA) + ROC(close, half the flow window)) / (2 × ATR(max(14, flow window)) × 1.5), −1, 1), with unavailable terms treated as zero. Normalize the three weights by their sum.
