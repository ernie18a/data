Calculation: Selected Supertrend direction = direction of the chosen ATR-based Supertrend; choose the best-scoring length/multiplier pair, the pair with the highest average score among its 3×3 grid neighborhood, or a manual pair. Score each pair over the learning window using its selected performance metric on trades closed when its direction flips; require the minimum trade count.
Calculation: Agreement = percentage of the 100 grid Supertrend directions matching the selected direction.
Calculation: Momentum score = count of five conditions: close above (long) or below (short) the fast EMA; fast EMA above (long) or below (short) the slower EMA; RSI at or above (long) or at or below (short) its threshold; close above (long) or below (short) the previous close; volume at or above its moving average.
Long: selected Supertrend direction flips from short to long OR remains long and close crosses above the fast EMA while above the slower EMA; AND close above the trend EMA; AND long momentum score at least the minimum; AND a valid best score exists; AND agreement at least the minimum; AND ATR as a percentage of close within the minimum and maximum; AND cooldown elapsed.
Short: selected Supertrend direction flips from long to short OR remains short and close crosses below the fast EMA while below the slower EMA; AND close below the trend EMA; AND short momentum score at least the minimum; AND a valid best score exists; AND agreement at least the minimum; AND ATR as a percentage of close within the minimum and maximum; AND cooldown elapsed.
