Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = highs or lows that are the extreme over a configurable number of bars on both sides; accept a new pivot only if its distance from the previous opposite pivot meets a configurable ATR-based minimum, and extend a same-side pivot when a more extreme value occurs. A pivot becomes usable only after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Impulse count = the latest alternating pivots forming waves 1–2–3–4–5: wave 1 moves in the count direction; wave 2 retraces; wave 3 resumes and passes wave 1's end; wave 4 retraces; wave 5 resumes (and may be truncated if enabled). Wave 2 must not pass the start of wave 1; wave 4 must not enter wave 1's price territory. Wave 3 must not be the shortest of waves 1, 3, and 5 when all are present; if the strict rule is selected, it must be at least as long as waves 1 and 5. Diagonals may overlap wave 1, but wave 4 must not pass the end of wave 2. The count direction is up when wave 1 rises and down when it falls.
Calculation: Wave 2 retracement = distance from wave 1 end to wave 2 pivot divided by wave 1 length. Wave 4 retracement = distance from wave 3 end to wave 4 pivot divided by wave 3 length.
Calculation: Wave 3 target 1 = wave 2 pivot plus wave 1 length times a configurable extension ratio in the count direction. Wave 5 target 1 = wave 4 pivot plus wave 1 length in the count direction.
Calculation: Three-bar fair value gap = bullish when the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier, or bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier; gap size must meet a configurable ATR-based minimum. A qualifying gap must be unfilled, in the entry zone, in the count direction, and formed since the preceding impulse pivot.
Calculation: Minor swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using the same short, configurable left and right lookback.
Long: Count direction is up AND wave 2 is forming after a valid wave 1 and its retracement is within the configurable wave 2 acceptance band AND wave 2 has not passed the start of wave 1 AND no position is open AND the selected trigger occurs within its configurable bar limit after the wave 2 pivot AND the optional guideline-count and fair-value-gap filters pass AND reward/risk to wave 3 target 1 meets the configurable minimum. Trigger is the selected one: price intersects the wave 2 Fibonacci zone; OR a bar closes above the previous bar's high and its open; OR a bar closes above the last confirmed minor swing high after the previous close was at or below it.
Short: Count direction is down AND wave 2 is forming after a valid wave 1 and its retracement is within the configurable wave 2 acceptance band AND wave 2 has not passed the start of wave 1 AND no position is open AND the selected trigger occurs within its configurable bar limit after the wave 2 pivot AND the optional guideline-count and fair-value-gap filters pass AND reward/risk to wave 3 target 1 meets the configurable minimum. Trigger is the selected one: price intersects the wave 2 Fibonacci zone; OR a bar closes below the previous bar's low and its open; OR a bar closes below the last confirmed minor swing low after the previous close was at or above it.
Long: Count direction is up AND waves 1–4 form a valid count AND wave 4 retracement is within the configurable wave 4 acceptance band AND wave 4 has not entered wave 1's price territory (or, for a diagonal, has not passed the end of wave 2) AND no position is open AND the selected trigger occurs within its configurable bar limit after the wave 4 pivot AND the optional guideline-count and fair-value-gap filters pass AND reward/risk to wave 5 target 1 meets the configurable minimum. Trigger is the selected one: price intersects the wave 4 Fibonacci zone; OR a bar closes above the previous bar's high and its open; OR a bar closes above the last confirmed minor swing high after the previous close was at or below it.
Short: Count direction is down AND waves 1–4 form a valid count AND wave 4 retracement is within the configurable wave 4 acceptance band AND wave 4 has not entered wave 1's price territory (or, for a diagonal, has not passed the end of wave 2) AND no position is open AND the selected trigger occurs within its configurable bar limit after the wave 4 pivot AND the optional guideline-count and fair-value-gap filters pass AND reward/risk to wave 5 target 1 meets the configurable minimum. Trigger is the selected one: price intersects the wave 4 Fibonacci zone; OR a bar closes below the previous bar's low and its open; OR a bar closes below the last confirmed minor swing low after the previous close was at or above it.
Exit: long, current high >= target 1 OR current low <= active stop OR close is below the wave 1 start while wave 2 is active, or below the wave 1 end while wave 4 is active. If break-even management is enabled, move the stop to entry when high reaches entry plus the configured R multiple of initial risk.
Exit: short, current low <= target 1 OR current high >= active stop OR close is above the wave 1 start while wave 2 is active, or above the wave 1 end while wave 4 is active. If break-even management is enabled, move the stop to entry when low reaches entry minus the configured R multiple of initial risk. When target and stop are both reached in one bar, resolve the target first.
