Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range period; use only after that period is complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Resistance/support level = nearest level above/below the current close among confirmed swing highs/lows, the previous completed daily bar’s high, low, and close, and the most recently completed overnight, Asia, and London session highs/lows.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high = low.
Long: Opening range is complete AND current time is within the configured signal session AND no earlier long breakout remains active for the day (reset after a close below opening-range high) AND (break mode and high > opening-range high OR close mode and close > opening-range high and open > opening-range high) AND (break mode OR body fraction meets its minimum and volume > its moving average) AND (room filter disabled OR no resistance level exists OR nearest resistance − close ≥ target distance).
Short: Opening range is complete AND current time is within the configured signal session AND no earlier short breakout remains active for the day (reset after a close above opening-range low) AND (break mode and low < opening-range low OR close mode and close < opening-range low and open < opening-range low) AND (break mode OR body fraction meets its minimum and volume > its moving average) AND (room filter disabled OR no support level exists OR close − nearest support ≥ target distance).
