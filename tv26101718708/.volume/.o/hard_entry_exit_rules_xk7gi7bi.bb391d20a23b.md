Calculation: PDL = low of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: PMH = highest high so far in the current day's premarket session
Calculation: PML = lowest low so far in the current day's premarket session
Calculation: RTH HOD/LOD = highest high / lowest low so far in the current regular session
Long: bar is in the configured entry window AND no position is open AND daily trade count is below its configured limit AND close > VWAP AND shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA AND close > longer-period EMA AND close > shorter-period EMA AND (close > PDH OR close > PMH) AND ((low <= PDH × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close > PDH AND close > open) OR (low <= PMH × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close > PMH AND close > open) OR (high >= PDH × (1 − configured tolerance) AND low <= PDH × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close > PDH AND close > open) OR (high >= PMH × (1 − configured tolerance) AND low <= PMH × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close > PMH AND close > open) OR (low <= shorter-period EMA AND close > shorter-period EMA AND close > open))
Short: bar is in the configured entry window AND no position is open AND daily trade count is below its configured limit AND close < VWAP AND shorter-period EMA < longer-period EMA AND close < longer-period EMA AND close < shorter-period EMA AND (close < PDL OR close < PML) AND ((high >= PDL × (1 − configured tolerance) AND close < PDL AND close < open) OR (high >= PML × (1 − configured tolerance) AND close < PML AND close < open) OR (high >= PDL × (1 − configured tolerance) AND low <= PDL × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close < PDL AND close < open) OR (high >= PML × (1 − configured tolerance) AND low <= PML × (1 + configured tolerance) AND close < PML AND close < open) OR (high >= shorter-period EMA AND close < shorter-period EMA AND close < open))
Exit: Long, high >= the RTH HOD at entry + one minimum tick OR low <= longer-period EMA OR close < shorter-period EMA OR (entry was triggered by a level AND close < that level) OR bar is in the configured end-of-day flatten window
Exit: Short, low <= the RTH LOD at entry − one minimum tick OR high >= longer-period EMA OR close > shorter-period EMA OR (entry was triggered by a level AND close > that level) OR bar is in the configured end-of-day flatten window
