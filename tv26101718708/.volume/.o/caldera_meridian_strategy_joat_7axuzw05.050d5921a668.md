Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA and close > mid EMA; bear trend is the inverse. The three EMA lookbacks are ordered fast to slow.
Calculation: Bull momentum = MACD histogram > 0, RSI above its bullish threshold, and +DI > -DI; bear momentum uses the inverse comparisons.
Calculation: Bull VWAP condition = close > VWAP and VWAP rising; bear VWAP condition = close < VWAP and VWAP falling.
Calculation: Volume impulse = volume / its moving average.
Calculation: Pressure oscillator = EMA of ((close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) × volume) divided by EMA of the absolute value of that quantity, scaled to a bounded percentage.
Calculation: Bid pressure = pressure oscillator above its positive threshold and volume impulse above its threshold; ask pressure uses the corresponding negative pressure threshold and same volume condition.
Calculation: Bull absorption = low < previous low, close > open, pressure oscillator rising, and volume impulse above its threshold; bear absorption = high > previous high, close < open, pressure oscillator falling, and volume impulse above its threshold.
Calculation: Auction center = SMA(hlc3 × volume) / SMA(volume), using the same lookback for both averages. Value bands = auction center ± standard deviation of hlc3 × a deviation parameter.
Calculation: Auction discount = close below the lower value band and close above low + 45% of the bar range; auction premium = close above the upper value band and close below high − 45% of the bar range. Bull reclaim = close crosses above the lower band; bear rejection = close crosses below the upper band.
Calculation: Bull structure = bullish structure bias, or a sweep below the latest confirmed pivot low that closes back above it, or a bullish gap (low > high two bars earlier and close > open). Bear structure = bearish structure bias, or a sweep above the latest confirmed pivot high that closes back below it, or a bearish gap (high < low two bars earlier and close < open). Structure bias turns bullish when a close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high with displacement, and bearish when it crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low with displacement. Displacement = absolute candle body > ATR × a multiplier and volume impulse > 1.
Calculation: Continuation probability = rolling conditional frequency that the current regime follows the same prior regime, using the same lookback for numerator and denominator. Regime is bull, bear, or neutral as defined by the EMA trend conditions. Probability passes when continuation probability meets a threshold or ATR percentage rank over a lookback exceeds a threshold.
Calculation: Long score = weighted sum of bull trend, bull momentum, bull VWAP, bid pressure, bull absorption, bull structure, auction discount or bull reclaim, higher-timeframe filter pass, and probability pass, plus a bounded ADX contribution. Short score is the corresponding weighted sum of bear conditions and auction premium or bear rejection. The higher-timeframe filter compares close with an EMA calculated from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; it passes long above the EMA and short below it.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of entry price − ATR × stop multiplier and latest confirmed pivot low, using the ATR stop if no pivot low exists. Short stop = the higher of entry price + ATR × stop multiplier and latest confirmed pivot high, using the ATR stop if no pivot high exists. Risk = the greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and a fraction of ATR. Long targets = entry price + risk × each of three increasing reward multiples; short targets = entry price − risk × those multiples.
Long: bar confirmed AND session filter passes if enabled AND flat AND long score meets the minimum score AND long score exceeds short score by a margin AND higher-timeframe filter passes if enabled AND NOT auction premium.
Short: bar confirmed AND session filter passes if enabled AND flat AND short score meets the minimum score AND short score exceeds long score by a margin AND higher-timeframe filter passes if enabled AND NOT auction discount.
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= any long target OR (regime exit enabled AND bear regime) OR (pressure exit enabled AND pressure oscillator below its negative threshold) OR short score exceeds long score by a margin.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= any short target OR (regime exit enabled AND bull regime) OR (pressure exit enabled AND pressure oscillator above its positive threshold) OR long score exceeds short score by a margin.
