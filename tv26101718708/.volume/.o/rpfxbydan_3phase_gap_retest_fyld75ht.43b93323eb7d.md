Calculation: Volatility = ATR on the chart bars, or on 5-minute bars if that optional threshold setting is enabled; use the bar available at the decision time.
Calculation: Consolidation = rolling highest high − rolling lowest low < a multiple of Volatility; its high and low bounds expand to include each bar that stays within the sweep threshold. The bounds then freeze when a qualifying sweep occurs. Both-sided sweeps reset the setup.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < consolidation low − sweep threshold × Volatility, (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ minimum wick ratio, and volume > its moving average if the optional volume filter is enabled. Bearish sweep = high > consolidation high + sweep threshold × Volatility, (high − close) / (high − low) ≥ minimum wick ratio, and the same optional volume condition.
Calculation: Structure-break level = consolidation high (long) or low (short) in strict mode; consolidation midpoint in midpoint mode; consolidation low (long) or high (short) in basic mode.
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low − high two bars earlier; bearish gap = low two bars earlier − current high. Gap zone is (high two bars earlier, current low) for bullish gaps and (current high, low two bars earlier) for bearish gaps. Gap size ≥ minimum gap threshold × Volatility. If the optional displacement filter is enabled, current or previous bar must have a same-direction body whose body/range ratio meets its threshold.
Calculation: Active gap zone = first qualifying gap after the structure break, replaced while waiting only by a wider qualifying gap in the same direction. The gap must form at least one bar after the break. Setup state resets at each new day and on timeout.
Calculation: Trend filter, if enabled = current close of the selected higher timeframe bar > all three EMAs with longer, intermediate, and shorter lookbacks for long; < all three for short. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3. If the optional VWAP filter is enabled, long requires close > VWAP and the active bullish gap's lower boundary > VWAP; short requires close < VWAP and the active bearish gap's upper boundary < VWAP.
Long: After a bullish sweep, within the structure-break timeout a close > the bullish structure-break level; then within the gap-search timeout a qualifying bullish gap forms; then, within the retest timeout and at least one bar after the active gap forms, (previous close ≥ active gap lower boundary AND current low ≤ active gap upper boundary AND current close ≥ active gap lower boundary OR, in gap-break mode, the gap was touched from above and current close > active gap upper boundary); AND the optional trend and VWAP filters pass.
Short: After a bearish sweep, within the structure-break timeout a close < the bearish structure-break level; then within the gap-search timeout a qualifying bearish gap forms; then, within the retest timeout and at least one bar after the active gap forms, (previous close ≤ active gap upper boundary AND current high ≥ active gap lower boundary AND current close ≤ active gap upper boundary OR, in gap-break mode, the gap was touched from below and current close < active gap lower boundary); AND the optional trend and VWAP filters pass.
