Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of volume when close > prior close, minus volume when close < prior close, unchanged when equal
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot high/low = a close higher/lower than the closes of the preceding and following N bars; it is known after the N following bars complete
Long: current confirmed price pivot low < preceding confirmed price pivot low AND OBV at current pivot > OBV at preceding pivot
Short: current confirmed price pivot high > preceding confirmed price pivot high AND OBV at current pivot < OBV at preceding pivot
