Calculation: Profile = Divide each aggregated period’s high–low range into configured equal-price rows; distribute each bar’s volume equally across rows touched by its high–low. POC is the highest-volume row’s midpoint; value area expands from the POC toward the higher-volume adjacent row until it contains the configured share of volume.
Calculation: Weekly value area = Profile of the last completed week; daily value area and VPOC = Profile of the last completed day. Aggregate from execution-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Daily VPOC trend = Compare successive VPOCs from the configured number of most recent completed days; rising/falling trend requires the configured share of steps to rise/fall.
Calculation: Micro-profile shape = Profile of the current impulse-plus-balance window; POC position in the upper configured portion of its high–low range is P, and in the lower configured portion is b.
Calculation: Single prints = Price rows in the current session profile, within the impulse high–low range, touched by no more than the configured number of bars; require at least the configured minimum number of rows.
Calculation: Impulse = Highest high minus lowest low over the configured impulse lookback; upward impulse requires range at least the configured ATR multiple and close in the upper part of the range; downward impulse requires close in the lower part. ATR uses execution-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Weekly rejection = After a bar closes outside the prior completed week’s value area, a later bar tests that edge within the configured ATR tolerance and closes back outside it in the rejection direction, with a same-direction candle and range at least the configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Initial risk = Distance from entry close to the structural stop; by default, stop is beyond the micro-balance edge by the configured ATR buffer. TP1 and TP2 are configured multiples of initial risk from entry; after TP1, move the stop to entry.
Long: No open position and session signal count below its configured limit AND close above the prior completed week’s value-area high AND weekly rejection upward AND configured number of most recent completed daily VPOCs trend upward AND close above the prior completed day’s value-area high AND upward impulse crosses that reference (lookback low below it, close above it) AND single-print requirement is met AND micro-profile shape is P AND balance has reached its configured minimum duration without exceeding its configured maximum height, age, or an impulse-origin invalidation AND (close above balance high and close above open OR low reaches the lower quarter of the balance and close is above its midpoint, above open, and above the reference).
Short: No open position and session signal count below its configured limit AND close below the prior completed week’s value-area low AND weekly rejection downward AND configured number of most recent completed daily VPOCs trend downward AND close below the prior completed day’s value-area low AND downward impulse crosses that reference (lookback high above it, close below it) AND single-print requirement is met AND micro-profile shape is b AND balance has reached its configured minimum duration without exceeding its configured maximum height, age, or an impulse-origin invalidation AND (close below balance low and close below open OR high reaches the upper quarter of the balance and close is below its midpoint, below open, and below the reference).
Exit: Long, high >= TP2 OR low <= current stop OR (TP1 not reached and configured holding-bar limit reached); after TP1, current stop is entry.
Exit: Short, low <= TP2 OR high >= current stop OR (TP1 not reached and configured holding-bar limit reached); after TP1, current stop is entry.
