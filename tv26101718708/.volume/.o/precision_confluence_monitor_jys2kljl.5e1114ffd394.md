Calculation: Fast and slow SMAs = shorter- and longer-lookback simple moving averages of close, calculated separately for the chart and paired index.
Calculation: Average volume = longer-lookback simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Failed attempts per level = count of prior C1 states that failed before entry; reset daily.
Long: (no active C1 state AND a valid level is supplied AND close > level AND (no paired level is supplied OR paired-index close > paired level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its threshold × average volume) AND (SMA filter disabled OR (close > fast SMA AND fast SMA >= slow SMA AND paired-index close > paired-index fast SMA AND paired-index fast SMA >= paired-index slow SMA))) OR (prior C1-up state AND close > level AND (no paired level is supplied OR paired-index close > paired level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its threshold × average volume) AND failed attempts < its limit)
Short: (no active C1 state AND a valid level is supplied AND close < level AND (no paired level is supplied OR paired-index close < paired level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its threshold × average volume) AND (SMA filter disabled OR (close < fast SMA AND fast SMA <= slow SMA AND paired-index close < paired-index fast SMA AND paired-index fast SMA <= paired-index slow SMA))) OR (prior C1-down state AND close < level AND (no paired level is supplied OR paired-index close < paired level) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its threshold × average volume) AND failed attempts < its limit)
Exit: Long, close < level
Exit: Short, close > level
