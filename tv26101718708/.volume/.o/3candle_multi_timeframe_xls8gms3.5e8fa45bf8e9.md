Calculation: Zone half-width = configured tick size × configured tick count, or configured level price × configured percentage / 100, according to the selected zone mode
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100; use 50% when high = low
Calculation: Long close/body test = close position ≥ 75%, or close position ≥ 50% and close > open
Calculation: Short close/body test = close position ≤ 25%, or close position ≤ 50% and close < open
Calculation: Merged bar = previous entry bar and current entry bar combined: open from the previous bar, highest high, lowest low, and close from the current bar
Calculation: Developing POC = close of the entry bar with the greatest volume so far in the currently forming configured higher-timeframe bar; on a volume tie, use the latest such close
Calculation: Developing delta = sum of entry-bar volume for bullish bars minus entry-bar volume for bearish bars so far in the currently forming configured higher-timeframe bar; doji bars contribute zero
