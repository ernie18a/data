Calculation: Group high = maximum high within a consecutive block of bars; the current block ends at the current bar and may still be forming, while the immediately preceding block is complete. Use separate short-, medium-, and long-term block sizes, with short < medium < long.
Calculation: Group low = minimum low within the same consecutive block of bars as the group high.
Long: current group high > preceding group high AND current group low > preceding group low
Short: current group high < preceding group high AND current group low < preceding group low
