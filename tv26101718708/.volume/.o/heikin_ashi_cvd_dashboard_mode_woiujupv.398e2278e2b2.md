Calculation: Signed volume delta = volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, and 0 otherwise; optionally sum signed volume delta from lower-timeframe bars within the current bar. For delta high and low, use the maximum and minimum cumulative intrabar delta, or max(0, delta) and min(0, delta) when using chart bars.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative signed volume delta, reset to the current delta at each NY midnight when daily reset is enabled. CVD open = 0 on a reset bar, otherwise the prior CVD close; CVD high and low = CVD open plus delta high and low.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi CVD = standard Heikin-Ashi OHLC calculated from CVD OHLC; its open uses the prior Heikin-Ashi open and close, and its close is the mean of CVD open, high, low, and close.
Long: Heikin-Ashi CVD close crosses above 0 OR short-term EMA of Heikin-Ashi CVD close > longer-term EMA of Heikin-Ashi CVD close OR Heikin-Ashi CVD close >= Heikin-Ashi CVD open OR signed volume delta > configurable multiplier × SMA of absolute signed volume delta over a configurable lookback OR Heikin-Ashi CVD close >= Heikin-Ashi CVD open AND Heikin-Ashi CVD low = Heikin-Ashi CVD open AND close < open
Short: Heikin-Ashi CVD close crosses below 0 OR short-term EMA of Heikin-Ashi CVD close < longer-term EMA of Heikin-Ashi CVD close OR Heikin-Ashi CVD close < Heikin-Ashi CVD open OR signed volume delta < −(configurable multiplier × SMA of absolute signed volume delta over a configurable lookback) OR Heikin-Ashi CVD close < Heikin-Ashi CVD open AND Heikin-Ashi CVD high = Heikin-Ashi CVD open AND close > open
