Calculation: Price pivot high/low = local high/low over configured left and right anchor bars, confirmed after the right bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed price pivot low below the prior confirmed pivot low while RSI at the new pivot is above RSI at the prior pivot.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed price pivot high above the prior confirmed pivot high while RSI at the new pivot is below RSI at the prior pivot.
Calculation: ADX exhaustion at a pivot = ADX on the pivot bar is below ADX on the preceding anchor bar.
Calculation: Bullish OI divergence = a confirmed price pivot low below the prior confirmed pivot low while open interest close at the new pivot is above its close at the prior pivot.
Calculation: Bearish OI divergence = a confirmed price pivot high above the prior confirmed pivot high while open interest close at the new pivot is below its close at the prior pivot.
Long: Bullish RSI divergence OR (bullish price pivot below prior confirmed price pivot low AND ADX exhaustion at that pivot) OR bullish OI divergence; compare pivots no farther apart than the configured maximum lookback.
Short: Bearish RSI divergence OR (bearish price pivot above prior confirmed price pivot high AND ADX exhaustion at that pivot) OR bearish OI divergence; compare pivots no farther apart than the configured maximum lookback.
