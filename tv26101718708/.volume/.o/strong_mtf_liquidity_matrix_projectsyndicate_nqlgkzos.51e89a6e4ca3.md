Calculation: Bullish FVG = a higher-timeframe bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = its high is below the low two bars earlier. Use each panel’s latest forming bar and earlier completed bars.
Calculation: Bullish OB = a swing-low pivot (using the same number of bars on each side) with an earlier bearish candle found within the OB lookback whose low is below the pivot low by more than that candle’s range times the displacement multiple; bearish OB = the mirrored swing-high pattern with an earlier bullish candle. Exclude mitigated zones: bullish OB if a later close is below its lower boundary; bearish OB if a later close is above its upper boundary. Use each panel’s latest forming bar and earlier completed bars.
Calculation: Zone strength = OB displacement score (1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, or 3.0 at displacement/ATR thresholds 1, 1.5, 2, and 3) + 1.5 + age score (2.0 under 50 bars, 1.5 under 100, 1.0 under 200, otherwise 0.5) + zone-height score (3.0 when height/ATR is 0.5–2.0, 2.0 when 0.3–3.0, otherwise 1.0), capped at 10; FVG strength = gap/ATR score (1.5 below 0.5, 3.0 from 0.5, 4.5 from 0.75, 6.0 from 1.0, 8.0 from 1.5), capped at 10.
Long: price reaches a bullish OB or FVG on a higher-timeframe panel with strength >= 7 AND an unconsumed liquidity pool lies beyond the zone in the long direction.
Short: price reaches a bearish OB or FVG on a higher-timeframe panel with strength >= 7 AND an unconsumed liquidity pool lies beyond the zone in the short direction.
Exit: long, a bar closes through the zone and accepts beyond it.
Exit: short, a bar closes through the zone and accepts beyond it.
