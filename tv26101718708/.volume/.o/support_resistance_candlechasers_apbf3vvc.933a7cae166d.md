Calculation: Active support/resistance levels = confirmed chart-timeframe swing lows/highs, optionally combined with confirmed swings from completed higher-timeframe bars; a swing uses a role-specific left lookback and a shared right confirmation window. Keep a candidate only if the close has moved away from its pivot by at least the minimum multiple of ATR measured at the pivot, and it has not already broken. Group nearby same-role candidates within the minimum spacing and keep the first level price fixed. An active level qualifies for an alert only if it meets the minimum strength grade and evidence score; grade increases with reaction size and separated swing evidence, while score also accounts for confluence, pivot relative volume, rejections, visits, and age. Evaluate events on the completed chart bar; use the selected close-or-wick break mode and the level's fixed buffer. Use configured alert scope, volume filters, and per-level cooldown where applicable.
Long: Eligible active support has a fresh contact (bar high >= level price - contact tolerance AND bar low <= level price + contact tolerance) OR support rejection (contact AND bar low >= level price - contact tolerance AND close > level price + contact tolerance AND close > open) OR approach (close > level price AND abs(close - level price) <= approach distance AND previous bar was not near AND bar is not in contact); apply the corresponding enabled event, volume filter, visit separation, and cooldown. OR eligible active resistance is broken (selected close or high > level price + buffer) with transition volume filter. OR an eligible broken resistance is retested within the configured window (bar low within retest tolerance of level AND close > level price AND close > open), with transition volume filter.
Short: Eligible active resistance has a fresh contact (bar high >= level price - contact tolerance AND bar low <= level price + contact tolerance) OR resistance rejection (contact AND bar high <= level price + contact tolerance AND close < level price - contact tolerance AND close < open) OR approach (close < level price AND abs(close - level price) <= approach distance AND previous bar was not near AND bar is not in contact); apply the corresponding enabled event, volume filter, visit separation, and cooldown. OR eligible active support is broken (selected close or low < level price - buffer) with transition volume filter. OR an eligible broken support is retested within the configured window (bar high within retest tolerance of level AND close < level price AND close < open), with transition volume filter.
