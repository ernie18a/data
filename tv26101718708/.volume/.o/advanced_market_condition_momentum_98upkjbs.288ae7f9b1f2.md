Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the bars on both sides over equal lookbacks; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Average candle range = average of high minus low over the momentum lookback, including the current bar.
Long: low < last confirmed pivot low AND close > last confirmed pivot low AND close - low > an ATR-based threshold AND high - low > average candle range
Short: high > last confirmed pivot high AND close < last confirmed pivot high AND high - close > an ATR-based threshold AND high - low > average candle range
