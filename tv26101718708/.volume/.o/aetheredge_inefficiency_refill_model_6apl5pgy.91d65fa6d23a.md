Calculation: Log-return z-score = (ln(close / previous close) − rolling mean of log returns) / rolling standard deviation of log returns.
Calculation: Volume-adjusted displacement = log-return z-score × 1 when volume is below its rolling average; otherwise × a fade factor.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over a lookback / sum of absolute close changes over that same lookback.
Calculation: Refill probability = online logistic probability from six event-time features: absolute volume-adjusted displacement, clamped 1 − volume / rolling average volume, absolute log-return z-score, efficiency ratio, ATR / its longer-term rolling average, and clamped absolute distance from close to the close a displacement lookback ago / ATR. Standardize features using online EWMA means and variances; initialize logistic weights at zero, and update them by online logistic-gradient steps when each void resolves: label 1 if price reaches its origin, otherwise label 0 if it remains unfilled through the refill horizon. The probability is the sigmoid of the weighted standardized features.
Long: On a confirmed bar after warmup, volume-adjusted displacement crosses below the negative void threshold AND refill probability > minimum refill probability.
Short: On a confirmed bar after warmup, volume-adjusted displacement crosses above the void threshold AND refill probability > minimum refill probability.
