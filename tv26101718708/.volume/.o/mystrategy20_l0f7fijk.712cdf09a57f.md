Calculation: SuperTrend direction = ATR is Wilder-smoothed true range; source is (high + low)/2. Set upper band to source − multiplier × ATR, clamped upward to its prior value when prior close was above that prior band. Set lower band to source + multiplier × ATR, clamped downward to its prior value when prior close was below that prior band. Start direction at +1; change −1 to +1 when close exceeds the prior lower band, or +1 to −1 when close falls below the prior upper band; otherwise retain direction.
Long: SuperTrend direction changes from −1 to +1 AND MACD line crosses above its signal line AND RSI > 50 AND close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band AND volume > its moving average × multiplier AND ATR / close > threshold
Short: SuperTrend direction changes from +1 to −1 AND MACD line crosses below its signal line AND RSI < 50 AND close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band AND volume > its moving average × multiplier AND ATR / close > threshold
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop multiplier × ATR at entry OR high ≥ entry price + each of the three take-profit multiples × ATR at entry OR (after position size decreases and close ≥ entry price + first take-profit multiple × ATR at entry) low ≤ entry price OR the active trailing stop is reached
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop multiplier × ATR at entry OR low ≤ entry price − each of the three take-profit multiples × ATR at entry OR (after position size decreases and close ≤ entry price − first take-profit multiple × ATR at entry) high ≥ entry price OR the active trailing stop is reached
