Calculation: ATR percentile rank = percentile rank of ATR over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Directional movement bias = (EMA of positive high movement when it exceeds negative low movement − EMA of positive low movement when it exceeds positive high movement) / their sum; zero if the sum is zero.
Calculation: Normalized EMA spread = (shorter-lookback EMA of close − longer-lookback EMA of close) / ATR.
Calculation: Raw regime = volatile if ATR percentile rank exceeds the higher volatility threshold; otherwise bullish trending if it exceeds the trend threshold, directional movement bias is positive, and normalized EMA spread is positive; otherwise bearish trending if it exceeds the trend threshold, directional movement bias is negative, and normalized EMA spread is negative; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Confirmed regime = raw regime after the same raw regime has occurred for two consecutive bars; until then, retain the previously confirmed regime.
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum index = EMA(EMA(change in close × log(1 + (shorter-lookback SMA of volume / longer-lookback SMA of volume) × sensitivity), shorter lookback) − EMA(same volume-weighted price change, longer lookback), short smoothing lookback).
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum signal = EMA of the volume-weighted momentum index over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Statistical upper and lower bands = SMA of close over a lookback ± standard deviation of close over the same lookback × multiplier.
Calculation: Volume ratio = shorter-lookback SMA of volume / longer-lookback SMA of volume.
Calculation: Ceiling and floor rails = ceiling and floor multiples of an increment around the EMA of close; proximity tolerance = rail spacing × proximity fraction.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = the greater of the prior long trailing stop and current close − ATR × trail multiple; short trailing stop = the lesser of the prior short trailing stop and current close + ATR × trail multiple.
Long: the long conditions were false on the prior bar AND (regime gate disabled OR confirmed regime is bullish trending) AND shorter-lookback trend MA > longer-lookback trend MA AND volume-weighted momentum index > its signal AND close < statistical upper band AND (volume gate disabled OR volume ratio >= threshold).
Short: the short conditions were false on the prior bar AND (regime gate disabled OR confirmed regime is bearish trending) AND shorter-lookback trend MA < longer-lookback trend MA AND volume-weighted momentum index < its signal AND close > statistical lower band AND (volume gate disabled OR volume ratio >= threshold).
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR (trailing shield enabled AND low <= long trailing stop) OR confirmed regime is bearish trending OR (lattice extreme exit enabled AND close >= statistical upper band) OR (rail interaction exit enabled AND high is within proximity tolerance of the ceiling rail).
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR low <= entry price − ATR × target multiple OR (trailing shield enabled AND high >= short trailing stop) OR confirmed regime is bullish trending OR (lattice extreme exit enabled AND close <= statistical lower band) OR (rail interaction exit enabled AND low is within proximity tolerance of the floor rail).
