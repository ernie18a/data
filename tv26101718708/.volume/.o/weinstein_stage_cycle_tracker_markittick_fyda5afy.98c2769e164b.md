Calculation: Stage average = simple moving average of the selected source (close by default, optionally smoothed first) over the stage-average lookback.
Calculation: Slope = percentage change in the stage average from its value at the slope lookback; rising is above the positive flat-slope threshold, falling is below its negative, and otherwise it is flat.
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Stage = initialize to Stage 2 when slope is rising and source is above the stage average, or Stage 4 when slope is falling and source is at or below it. Thereafter, from Stage 1, change to Stage 4 on a downside breakdown or Stage 2 on an upside breakout; from Stage 2, change to Stage 4 on a downside breakdown, else Stage 3 when slope is no longer rising and it has risen at least once during this Stage 2, else Stage 1 when slope is no longer rising, source is at or below the average, and it has never risen during this Stage 2; otherwise remain Stage 2. From Stage 3, change to Stage 4 on a downside breakdown or Stage 2 on an upside breakout. From Stage 4, change to Stage 2 on an upside breakout, else Stage 1 when slope is no longer falling and it has fallen at least once during this Stage 4, else Stage 3 when slope is no longer falling, source is at or above the average, and it has never fallen during this Stage 4; otherwise remain Stage 4. An upside breakout means source is above the prior range high and stage average, and slope is not falling; a downside breakdown means source is below the prior range low and stage average, and slope is not rising. The rise/fall history is counted from the start of the current stage.
