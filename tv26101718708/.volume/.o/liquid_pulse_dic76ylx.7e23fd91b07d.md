Calculation: High-volume bar = volume >= volume-spike threshold × moving average of volume
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low undercuts the prior rolling swing low AND close exceeds the prior bar midpoint by the configured fraction of its range; bearish liquidity sweep = high exceeds the prior rolling swing high AND close falls below the prior bar midpoint by that fraction
Calculation: Trend direction = positive directional index > negative directional index for bullish, reversed for bearish; trend bias = ADX above trend threshold AND close above (long) or below (short) the close EMA
Calculation: Trend alignment = fast TEMA > slow SMMA
Calculation: Long trade score = trend points (strong trend with bullish direction scores more than moderate trend with bullish direction) + momentum points (MACD bullish crossover and trend alignment score more than either alone) + volatility points (strong trend scores more than moderate trend, which scores more than chop) + volume points (high-volume bar scores more than volume above its average) + pattern points (bullish sweep and bullish pin bar together score more than either alone); short score is the mirrored bearish version
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = low undercuts the prior rolling low AND close > open AND body > 2 × lower wick; bearish pin bar = high exceeds the prior rolling high AND close < open AND body > 2 × upper wick
Calculation: Long confluence count = number true among close above higher-timeframe VWAP, high-volume bar, bullish MACD crossover, bullish liquidity sweep, and close > open; short count = number true among close below higher-timeframe VWAP, high-volume bar, bearish MACD crossunder, bearish liquidity sweep, and close < open
Calculation: Volatility-scaled profit target = configured side-specific profit ticks × tick size × clamp(1 + 0.5 × (ATR / its moving average − 1), 0.5, 2); volatility-scaled hard stop distance = min(1.5 × ATR, 15 ticks) × clamp(1 + 0.5 × (ATR / its moving average − 1), 0.5, 1.5)
Long: long confluence count >= minimum confluence AND trend alignment AND ADX > chop threshold AND (ADX <= trend threshold OR (positive directional index > negative directional index AND trend bias)) AND long trade score >= long score threshold AND volume index below pause level AND daily entry count below limit
Short: short confluence count >= minimum confluence AND NOT trend alignment AND ADX > chop threshold AND (ADX <= trend threshold OR (negative directional index > positive directional index AND trend bias)) AND short trade score >= short score threshold AND NOT (ATR > 2 × its moving average) AND volume index below pause level AND daily entry count below limit
Exit: Long, close <= entry price − volatility-scaled hard stop distance OR close >= entry price + volatility-scaled profit target OR close >= entry price + maximum profit distance OR (trailing activated after minimum favorable move AND price reaches trailing stop) OR bars in position exceed time limit
Exit: Short, close >= entry price + volatility-scaled hard stop distance OR close <= entry price − volatility-scaled profit target OR close <= entry price − maximum profit distance OR (trailing activated after minimum favorable move AND price reaches trailing stop) OR bars in position exceed time limit
