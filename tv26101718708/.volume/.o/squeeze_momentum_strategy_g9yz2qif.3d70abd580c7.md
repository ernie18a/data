Calculation: Bollinger bands = SMA(close, BB lookback) ± BB multiplier × standard deviation(close, BB lookback)
Calculation: Keltner bands = SMA(close, KC lookback) ± SMA(true range or high−low, KC lookback) × KC multiplier
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression over the KC lookback of close − average((highest high + lowest low)/2, SMA(close, KC lookback)); use the current bar’s value
Calculation: Volume-adjusted value = prior close × (volume base − current volume) / volume base + current close × current volume / volume base, where volume base is either cumulative volume or the rolling sum of volume over its lookback
Long: long trading enabled AND current bar is within the configured date window AND current lower Bollinger band < lower Keltner band AND current upper Bollinger band > upper Keltner band AND previous bar’s lower Bollinger band > lower Keltner band AND previous bar’s upper Bollinger band < upper Keltner band AND squeeze momentum > 0 AND (volume filter disabled OR volume-adjusted value > 0)
Short: short trading enabled AND current bar is within the configured date window AND current lower Bollinger band < lower Keltner band AND current upper Bollinger band > upper Keltner band AND previous bar’s lower Bollinger band > lower Keltner band AND previous bar’s upper Bollinger band < upper Keltner band AND squeeze momentum < 0 AND (volume filter disabled OR volume-adjusted value < 0)
Exit: long or short, NOT ((squeeze momentum > 0 AND squeeze momentum > its previous-bar value) OR (squeeze momentum < 0 AND squeeze momentum < its previous-bar value))
