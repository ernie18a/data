Calculation: Volatility component = (volatility distance > jump threshold × sensitivity AND more than five bars since the prior jump) OR jump intensity > 0.2 × sensitivity; volatility distance is the root mean square of per-feature z-scores for the selected 2–5 features: rolling standard deviation of close returns, rolling standard deviation of volume deviation from its rolling mean divided by that mean, rolling standard deviation of (high−low)/close, rolling standard deviation of rolling close-volume correlation, and rolling standard deviation of (close−(high+low)/2)/(high−low+0.0001); each feature uses the primary lookback, and each z-score compares its current value with the mean and standard deviation of its previous primary-lookback feature values; jump intensity resets to 1 on a jump and otherwise equals its prior value × decay.
Calculation: Hurst regime component = in trending regime, close > SMA(close) AND close > prior close; in mean-reverting regime, close > upper Bollinger Band OR close < lower Bollinger Band; in random regime, Bollinger Band width / middle band < squeeze threshold AND (close > prior upper band OR close < prior lower band). The Hurst exponent is log(range of cumulative deviations of primary-lookback log returns from their mean / population standard deviation of those returns) / log(primary lookback), or 0.5 if the ratio is zero; smooth it with an EMA. Regime starts random, switches to trending above the trending threshold + hysteresis margin, switches to mean-reverting below the mean threshold − hysteresis margin, and otherwise retains its regime until more than the regime persistence period elapses, then becomes random.
Calculation: Information-flow component = volume leads OR price leads OR volume > volume SMA × (1 + sensitivity × volume-spike factor). For volume-to-price and price-to-volume flow, take log(volume+1) and close returns over the primary lookback; for each series pair, map values to 10 equal-range bins and compute binary entropy −p log(p) − (1−p) log(1−p), where p is the fraction of lagged bin pairs with equal bin indices; use zero if either range is zero. EMA-smooth both entropies, subtract price-to-volume from volume-to-price, and EMA-smooth the difference. Volume leads when the difference > entropy threshold × sensitivity; price leads when it is < −entropy threshold × sensitivity.
Calculation: Active component count = number of true components among volatility, Hurst regime, and information-flow components.
Long: position is flat AND more than five bars since the prior signal AND active component count meets the selected mode requirement (aggressive: at least one; confluence: at least two; conservative: all three) AND RSI > 50 AND close > short-period EMA.
Short: position is flat AND more than five bars since the prior signal AND active component count meets the selected mode requirement (aggressive: at least one; confluence: at least two; conservative: all three) AND RSI < 50 AND close < short-period EMA.
Exit: long, bars held > maximum holding period OR (close − entry price) / entry price > profit target OR (close − entry price) / entry price < −loss limit
Exit: short, bars held > maximum holding period OR (entry price − close) / entry price > profit target OR (entry price − close) / entry price < −loss limit
