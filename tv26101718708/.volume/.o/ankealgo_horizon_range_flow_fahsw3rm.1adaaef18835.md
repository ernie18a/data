Calculation: Previous reference-period high/low = high/low of the last completed reference period; update at the start of each new reference period.
Calculation: Previous-period average volume = average bar volume of the last completed reference period.
Calculation: Reference-period trend = bullish if the last completed period's high and low are both above those of the preceding completed period; bearish if both are below; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Recent average volume = moving average of bar volume over a configurable lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: SuperTrend = standard SuperTrend calculated on the current timeframe.
Long: low < previous reference-period low AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume >= selected volume baseline × multiplier, where the selected baseline is previous-period average volume, recent average volume, or previous bar volume) AND (trend filter disabled OR reference-period trend is bearish) AND (linkage disabled OR a prior qualifying break is armed and SuperTrend flips bullish)
Short: high > previous reference-period high AND (volume filter disabled OR current volume >= selected volume baseline × multiplier, where the selected baseline is previous-period average volume, recent average volume, or previous bar volume) AND (trend filter disabled OR reference-period trend is bullish) AND (linkage disabled OR a prior qualifying break is armed and SuperTrend flips bearish)
Exit: long, current low <= trailing stop OR (SuperTrend Exit mode AND SuperTrend flips bearish) OR (ATR Dynamic Exit mode AND current high >= entry price + entry-bar ATR × target multiple) OR (ATR Scale-Out mode AND current high >= an unfilled ATR-based target at 1R or 2R, or at 3R when the remainder exits at 3R)
Exit: short, current high >= trailing stop OR (SuperTrend Exit mode AND SuperTrend flips bullish) OR (ATR Dynamic Exit mode AND current low <= entry price - entry-bar ATR × target multiple) OR (ATR Scale-Out mode AND current low <= an unfilled ATR-based target at 1R or 2R, or at 3R when the remainder exits at 3R)
