Calculation: Short, medium, and long smoothed sources = EMA and recursively smoothed moving average of the configured source, using the shortest, intermediate, and longest lookbacks respectively; the first entry also compares the medium and long recursively smoothed averages.
Calculation: Configured moving average = selected moving-average method applied to its configured price source.
Calculation: Stored high/low points = persistent points updated from the prior rolling highest configured high and rolling lowest configured low when those rolling extrema fall/rise respectively; compare the configured lower-point source with the stored low point.
Calculation: Positive composite graph = the sum, over RSI, MFI, CMO, momentum, center of gravity, ADX, balance of power, stochastic, symmetrically weighted average, TSI, Williams %R, accumulation/distribution, intraday intensity, negative volume index, positive volume index, OBV, price-volume trend, and true range, of price-gap score × volume-gap score × their correlation. For each family, each score sums standard deviation of its indicator gap × standard deviation of the corresponding price or volume gap × their correlation, across short-to-long EMA lookbacks, with greater weights for shorter lookbacks. Each gap is the indicator or price/volume EMA minus the Bollinger basis of its longest-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Positive-event series = a running score adjusted on each bar by the first occurring crossover or crossunder among the listed indicator families’ short, medium, and long EMA pairs; shorter-vs-longer pairs receive larger absolute adjustments.
Calculation: Positive-signal graph = product of the price score, volume score, and their correlation; each score is the product of the standard deviation of changes in the positive-event series, standard deviation of the corresponding price or volume gap, and their correlation.
Calculation: Composite graph change levels = first through fifth successive differences of the positive composite graph.
Calculation: Swing signal = change in the product of the standard deviations of the positive composite graph’s and positive-signal graph’s deviations from their Bollinger bases, multiplied by the correlation of those standard deviations.
Calculation: Swing moving average = selected moving-average method of its configured price source.
Calculation: Downtrend command = Supertrend direction changes from up to down, OR Supertrend is down and swing signal crosses zero while the swing moving average has been falling.
Calculation: Profit line = adjusted running sum of the position-average-price profit target contribution on entry or position increase; when adjustment is disabled, it is the current position-average price multiplied by one plus the configured profit target.
Long: short EMA crosses above short recursively smoothed average AND long recursively smoothed average <= medium recursively smoothed average.
Long: (composite graph change level 4 crosses zero OR composite graph change level 5 crosses zero OR short EMA crosses above medium EMA) AND within the configured date window AND flat position AND configured lower-point source < stored low point.
Long: (swing signal crosses zero while swing moving average is falling OR composite graph change level 2 crosses zero OR composite graph change level 4 crosses zero OR composite graph change level 5 crosses zero) AND within the configured date window AND long position AND swing moving average < position average price × 0.99 AND open-trade count <= 75% of maximum trade count AND configured lower-point source < stored low point.
Long: swing signal crosses zero while swing moving average is falling AND swing moving average < position average price AND within the configured date window AND long position AND open-trade count > 75% of maximum trade count AND open-trade count <= maximum trade count AND configured lower-point source < stored low point.
Exit: long, configured moving average of the intermediate-sale source > minimum recorded entry price × (1 + configured swing-profit threshold) AND intermediate selling is enabled and open-trade count has not changed since the prior bar AND prior stored high point and current stored low point are nonzero AND position average price > swing moving average.
Exit: long, configured price source has risen over its configured lookback AND configured moving average of the downtrend-sale source < selected high entry price × 100 / (100 + configured downtrend threshold) AND downtrend selling is enabled AND swing moving average < position average price × 0.99.
Exit: long, (downtrend command AND profit line < swing moving average AND prior stored high point is nonzero AND (composite graph change level 1 crosses zero OR level 2 crosses zero OR level 3 crosses zero OR level 5 crosses zero) AND short EMA < medium recursively smoothed average) OR (short EMA crosses below short recursively smoothed average AND profit line < swing moving average AND short recursively smoothed average > stored high point).
