Calculation: Core moving averages = the same selected average type (EMA, WMA, SMA, HMA, or VWMA) applied to the selected price source, ordered shortest to longest lookback.
Long: Any adjacent core pair has the shorter-lookback average above the longer-lookback average OR the primary pair crosses above OR the fastest pair crosses above OR all four adjacent core pairs become bullishly aligned OR the primary pair is bullish and at least 3 of 4 adjacent core pairs are bullishly aligned
Short: Any adjacent core pair has the shorter-lookback average below the longer-lookback average OR the primary pair crosses below OR the fastest pair crosses below OR all four adjacent core pairs become bearishly aligned OR the primary pair is not bullish and at least 3 of 4 adjacent core pairs are bearishly aligned
