Calculation: Bullish SMT = current confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low, while at least one comparison instrument’s low at those pivot bars is higher at the current pivot; bearish SMT = current confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high, while at least one comparison instrument’s high at those pivot bars is lower at the current pivot. Use same-timeframe bars; pivots are confirmed after the pivot-length right-side bars.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low breaks the latest confirmed pivot low and close recovers above it, with lower wick greater than the configured fraction of the bar’s high-low range; bearish sweep = high breaks the latest confirmed pivot high and close falls below it, with upper wick greater than that fraction. Use confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Bullish iFVG = a bearish three-bar gap (current high below the high two bars earlier, with the intervening low above the current high) is inverted when close exceeds its upper boundary, remains active until low breaks its lower boundary, and price overlaps the zone; bearish iFVG is the mirrored bullish-gap inversion. Use current chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the prior bar’s body after a close above the preceding high with a bullish body larger than ATR(14), provided its body is no larger than 2× ATR(14); it remains active until close falls below the configured mitigation level. Bearish order block is mirrored. Use current chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish confirmed pattern = bullish sweep with bullish candle, displacement, and entry within the maximum ATR distance of the latest confirmed pivot low, OR bullish iFVG with bullish candle and displacement, OR price overlapping an active bullish order block with bullish candle and displacement. Bearish confirmed pattern is mirrored. Displacement means absolute candle body exceeds the configured multiple of ATR(14).
Calculation: Bullish confluence count = 1 each for bullish confirmed pattern, bullish SMT within the recent-bar window, bullish sweep within 5 bars, bullish CHoCH or current bullish trend state, overlap with a bullish FVG/iFVG/order block, bullish CISD label on this bar, discount zone, and close above today’s 9:30 New York open. Bearish count is the mirrored set, using premium zone and close below the open. A bullish (bearish) CISD label requires two or more consecutive rising (falling) closes, a different previous CISD direction, and the configured minimum bars since the prior label.
Calculation: Discount zone = close below the midpoint between the lowest low and the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback; premium zone = close above the midpoint between the highest high and that midpoint. Use current chart-timeframe bars, including the current bar.
Long: bullish SMT OR bullish CISD label OR bullish CHoCH (close above the latest confirmed pivot high while trend state is not bullish) OR bullish sweep with bullish candle OR bullish iFVG inversion OR early bullish pattern (low breaks latest confirmed pivot low OR price overlaps bullish iFVG OR bullish SMT fires) and not chop OR (bullish confirmed pattern AND bullish confluence count ≥ B minimum AND below A++ minimum AND not chop AND more than cooldown bars since last bullish signal) OR (bullish confirmed pattern AND bullish confluence count ≥ A++ minimum AND session gate passes AND not chop AND volume gate passes AND premium-block gate passes AND more than cooldown bars since last bullish signal). Chop gate: ATR(14) exceeds configured multiple of ATR(50). Volume gate: volume exceeds configured multiple of its longer-lookback simple average. Session gate: within enabled New York morning, New York afternoon, or London windows, unless strict session gate is disabled. Premium-block gate: premium zone is allowed unless warned signals are blocked. Early bullish pattern is subject to a 2-bar per-direction cooldown.
Short: bearish SMT OR bearish CISD label OR bearish CHoCH (close below the latest confirmed pivot low while trend state is not bearish) OR bearish sweep with bearish candle OR bearish iFVG inversion OR early bearish pattern (high breaks latest confirmed pivot high OR price overlaps bearish iFVG OR bearish SMT fires) and not chop OR (bearish confirmed pattern AND bearish confluence count ≥ B minimum AND below A++ minimum AND not chop AND more than cooldown bars since last bearish signal) OR (bearish confirmed pattern AND bearish confluence count ≥ A++ minimum AND session gate passes AND not chop AND volume gate passes AND discount-block gate passes AND more than cooldown bars since last bearish signal). Chop, volume, session, cooldown, and warned-signal gates are as defined for Long; discount-block gate allows discount zone unless warned signals are blocked.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry − ATR(14) × stop multiple OR high ≥ entry + ATR(14) × stop multiple × first-target multiple
Exit: short, high ≥ entry + ATR(14) × stop multiple OR low ≤ entry − ATR(14) × stop multiple × first-target multiple
