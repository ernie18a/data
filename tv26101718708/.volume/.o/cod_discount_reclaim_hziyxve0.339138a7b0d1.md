Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low exceeding the highs/lows of an equal configurable number of preceding and following bars; usable after the following bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = three consecutive same-color bars with a gap between the current bar and the bar two bars earlier at least the configurable minimum gap; the middle bar spans the gap, the current bar breaks beyond the middle bar in the gap direction, and the current candle body is not contained within the middle bar’s range. A bullish gap is mitigated when low reaches its lower edge; a bearish gap when high reaches its upper edge. Price is inside a gap when close is between its edges.
Calculation: Parent-timeframe FVG = the same bullish/bearish three-bar pattern on the selected parent timeframe, with gap size at least a configurable fraction of parent-timeframe ATR. Use the parent bar currently forming, if any.
Calculation: Bullish impulse anchors = the active higher low (HL) and subsequent higher high (HH), initialized by a bullish CHoCH or BOS; a bullish CHoCH waits for an HL then a later HH. Fib price at retracement fraction r = HH × (HL/HH)^r. Define shallow, middle, and deep retracement levels in increasing retracement order. The discount band spans the shallow through deep levels; the reclaim band spans the middle through deep levels. The invalidation floor is HL.
Calculation: Structural bias = bullish or bearish state. It initializes bullish when price breaks above a confirmed swing high, or bearish when price breaks below a confirmed swing low. In bullish state, breaking a new eligible swing low flips to bearish CHoCH; in bearish state, breaking a new eligible swing high flips to bullish CHoCH. A bullish-state break above the structural HH is bullish BOS; a bearish-state break below the structural LL is bearish BOS. Each structural level fires once. A CHoCH break also requires candle body size or range to meet a configurable fraction of current-timeframe ATR. Macro bias becomes bullish on bullish CHoCH, initializes with the first structural bias, and becomes bearish when close falls below the active HL.
Long: bullish CHoCH (high > active structural ceiling AND displacement condition) OR bullish BOS (bullish structural bias AND high > structural HH) OR (bullish structural bias OR bullish macro bias) AND close >= deep fib floor AND close <= middle fib level AND close >= HL AND (close > open AND (close > previous bar high OR close crosses above the shallow fib level)) AND (price is inside a chart-timeframe or parent-timeframe FVG OR price is inside the discount band).
Short: bearish CHoCH (low < active structural floor AND displacement condition) OR bearish BOS (bearish structural bias AND low < structural LL).
Exit: long, close < active HL
