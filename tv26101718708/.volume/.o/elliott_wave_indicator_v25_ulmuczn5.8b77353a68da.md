Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = pivot highs/lows using equal left/right lookback; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Alternating swing sequence = keep the more extreme pivot when consecutive pivots have the same type; append an opposite-type pivot only when its percentage distance from the last pivot meets a volatility-adjusted deviation threshold.
Calculation: Bull wave structure = five alternating pivots forming rising W1, W2 above W0 and below W1, W3 above W1 and at least 0.9×W1 length, W4 above W1 and below W3, and W5 above W3, with W3 at least as long as the shorter of W1 and W5. The W2-complete and W4-complete states occur when their respective pivots are confirmed; W5-complete occurs when the fifth wave passes these rules.
Calculation: Bear wave structure = five alternating pivots forming falling W1, W2 below W0 and above W1, W3 below W1 and at least 0.9×W1 length, W4 below W1 and above W3, and W5 below W3, with W3 at least as long as the shorter of W1 and W5. The W2-complete and W4-complete states occur when their respective pivots are confirmed; W5-complete occurs when the fifth wave passes these rules.
Calculation: W2 confidence = score the W2 retracement of W1: highest near 0.618, then 0.500, 0.786, 0.382; otherwise give a lower nonzero score only within 0.236–0.886, and zero outside. Nearness uses the configured Fibonacci tolerance.
Calculation: W4 confidence = 50% W4 retracement score + 30% W3-to-W1 length-ratio score + 20% W3 volume score. Retracement targets, in descending score order, are 0.382, 0.236, 0.500; a lower nonzero score applies within 0.146–0.618, otherwise zero. Length-ratio targets are 1.618 and 2.618; a lower score applies within 1–4.236, or a still lower score at 0.9 or above. W3 volume score is high if its volume passed the volume-spike filter, lower if the filter is enabled and it failed, and intermediate if volume filtering is disabled. Nearness uses the configured Fibonacci tolerance.
Calculation: W5 confidence = weighted sum of Fibonacci fit, wave-ratio fit, and time symmetry, with configurable normalized weights, then add a positive regime adjustment when the direction's regime is valid and a negative adjustment otherwise; cap the result to 0–100. Fibonacci fit scores W2 retracement, W3/W1 length, W4 retracement, and W5/W1 length against their configured Fibonacci targets and ranges. Wave-ratio fit awards points when W3 is at least the shorter of W1 and W5, W2/W1 is within 0.382–0.886, W4 is shorter than W2, and W5 is within the configured multiple of W3. Time symmetry is 100 minus 100 times the median relative deviation of W1, W3, and W5 durations from their mean, floored at zero.
Calculation: Dynamic confidence threshold = configured base threshold plus the smaller of 15 and 200 × ATR(14)/close when volatility adjustment is enabled; otherwise use the base threshold.
Calculation: Valid directional regime = current-timeframe fast EMA is above (bull) or below (bear) slow EMA, current-timeframe ADX exceeds its configured threshold, and the higher-timeframe EMA(20)/EMA(50) direction agrees with a higher-timeframe ADX above that threshold. Use the mapped higher timeframe with lookahead off; when no higher timeframe is mapped, use the current-timeframe EMA direction for confirmation.
Calculation: Liquid market = 20-bar average volume exceeds the configured minimum when liquidity filtering is enabled; otherwise true.
Calculation: W3 volume confirmation = W3 pivot-bar volume exceeds its 20-bar average volume times the configured multiplier when volume filtering is enabled; otherwise true.
Calculation: Long W5 RSI divergence = W5 pivot price is higher than W3 pivot price and W5 pivot RSI is lower than W3 pivot RSI. Short W5 RSI divergence = W5 pivot price is lower than W3 pivot price and W5 pivot RSI is higher than W3 pivot RSI. Compare RSI at the confirmed pivot bars.
Long: On a confirmed bar after warmup, bull W2-complete state and W2 confidence ≥ dynamic W2 threshold and valid bull regime and liquid market and ADX ≥ 0.8× its configured threshold and cooldown elapsed and long direction enabled and W2 signal not already fired in this wave.
Long: On a confirmed bar after warmup, bull W4-complete state and W4 confidence ≥ dynamic W4 threshold and valid bull regime and liquid market and ADX ≥ 0.8× its configured threshold and cooldown elapsed and long direction enabled and W4 entry enabled and W3 volume confirmation and W3 length ≥ 0.9×W1 length and W4 projection valid and W4 signal not already fired in this wave. W4 projection is valid when the bull wave is W4-complete, W4 confidence meets its dynamic threshold, and the bull regime is valid.
Short: On a confirmed bar after warmup, bear W2-complete state and W2 confidence ≥ dynamic W2 threshold and valid bear regime and liquid market and ADX ≥ 0.8× its configured threshold and cooldown elapsed and short direction enabled and W2 signal not already fired in this wave.
Short: On a confirmed bar after warmup, bear W4-complete state and W4 confidence ≥ dynamic W4 threshold and valid bear regime and liquid market and ADX ≥ 0.8× its configured threshold and cooldown elapsed and short direction enabled and W4 entry enabled and W3 volume confirmation and W3 length ≥ 0.9×W1 length and W4 projection valid and W4 signal not already fired in this wave. W4 projection is valid when the bear wave is W4-complete, W4 confidence meets its dynamic threshold, and the bear regime is valid.
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after warmup when bull W5-complete state and W5 confidence ≥ dynamic W5 threshold and RSI filtering is disabled or long W5 RSI divergence holds, and W5 exit not already fired in this wave.
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after warmup when bear W5-complete state and W5 confidence ≥ dynamic W5 threshold and RSI filtering is disabled or short W5 RSI divergence holds, and W5 exit not already fired in this wave.
