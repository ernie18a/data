Calculation: Macro trend = on the last completed macro-timeframe bar, bullish if its close > its 200-period EMA and that EMA > its value one macro bar earlier; bearish if close < EMA and EMA < its value one macro bar earlier
Calculation: Support = nearest level below close among the latest confirmed 5-left/5-right swing low, last completed context-timeframe low, previous completed day's low and close, current-timeframe VWAP, and current-timeframe 200-period EMA
Calculation: Resistance = nearest level above close among the latest confirmed 5-left/5-right swing high, last completed context-timeframe high, previous completed day's high and close, current-timeframe VWAP, and current-timeframe 200-period EMA
Calculation: Support/resistance zone width = ATR × zone-width parameter
Calculation: Bullish trigger = (close > prior-bar resistance and prior-bar close ≤ prior-bar resistance) OR (low ≤ support + zone width and close ≥ support − zone width and close > prior-bar high and close > open); also require absolute(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body fraction, current MACD histogram > prior-bar histogram, and +DI > −DI
Calculation: Bearish trigger = (close < prior-bar support and prior-bar close ≥ prior-bar support) OR (high ≥ resistance − zone width and close ≤ resistance + zone width and close < prior-bar low and close < open); also require absolute(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body fraction, current MACD histogram < prior-bar histogram, and −DI > +DI
Calculation: Bullish risk = close − (min(low, support) − ATR × stop-buffer parameter), using low if support is unavailable; bullish room = resistance − close, or ATR × target-multiple parameter if resistance is unavailable
Calculation: Bearish risk = (max(high, resistance) + ATR × stop-buffer parameter) − close, using high if resistance is unavailable; bearish room = close − support, or ATR × target-multiple parameter if support is unavailable
Calculation: Bullish score = 2 if macro trend is bullish, 1 if macro trend is neither bullish nor bearish, else 0; plus 2 if near support or bullish breakout, plus 1 if near VWAP or 200-period EMA, plus 1 if RSI > 50, plus 1 if MACD histogram is rising and MACD line > signal line, plus 1 if +DI > −DI and ADX ≥ minimum ADX and rising, plus 1 if bullish trigger, plus 1 if bullish risk > minimum tick and bullish room ≥ bullish risk × minimum-room parameter
Calculation: Bearish score = 2 if macro trend is bearish, 1 if macro trend is neither bearish nor bullish, else 0; plus 2 if near resistance or bearish breakout, plus 1 if near VWAP or 200-period EMA, plus 1 if RSI < 50, plus 1 if MACD histogram is falling and MACD line < signal line, plus 1 if −DI > +DI and ADX ≥ minimum ADX and rising, plus 1 if bearish trigger, plus 1 if bearish risk > minimum tick and bearish room ≥ bearish risk × minimum-room parameter
Long: completed bar, within allowed session, flat, bars since last signal > cooldown parameter, bullish trigger, bullish risk > minimum tick, bullish room ≥ bullish risk × minimum-room parameter, bullish score ≥ minimum score, and no bearish trigger
Short: completed bar, within allowed session, flat, bars since last signal > cooldown parameter, bearish trigger, bearish risk > minimum tick, bearish room ≥ bearish risk × minimum-room parameter, bearish score ≥ minimum score, and no bullish trigger
Exit: long, after entry bar, low ≤ stored stop OR high ≥ stored target OR (close < entry price and MACD histogram is falling); also exit when bars since entry ≥ maximum-bars parameter or outside allowed session
Exit: short, after entry bar, high ≥ stored stop OR low ≤ stored target OR (close > entry price and MACD histogram is rising); also exit when bars since entry ≥ maximum-bars parameter or outside allowed session
