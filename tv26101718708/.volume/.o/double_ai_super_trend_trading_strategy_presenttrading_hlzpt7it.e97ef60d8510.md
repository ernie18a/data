Calculation: Volume-weighted average = WMA(close × volume, primary SuperTrend lookback) ÷ WMA(volume, primary SuperTrend lookback); both SuperTrends use this average.
Calculation: Primary and secondary SuperTrend directions = build upper/lower bands as volume-weighted average ± their respective ATR × factor; ratchet lower bands upward and upper bands downward using the prior band and prior close. Direction is −1 when the SuperTrend is the lower band (uptrend), and +1 when it is the upper band (downtrend). The primary uses the primary ATR lookback and factor; the secondary uses its own ATR lookback and factor.
Calculation: Primary and secondary AI classifications = for each of the n=max(k, data lookback) current-and-prior SuperTrend values, label it 1 if WMA(close, its price lookback) > WMA(that SuperTrend, its prediction lookback), else 0. Find the k nearest values to the current SuperTrend by absolute distance; weight each label by 1/(distance + 0.000001). Classification is 1 if the weighted average equals 1, else 0. Both classifications use the same k and n; each uses its own SuperTrend, price lookback, and prediction lookback.
Long: primary SuperTrend direction = −1 AND secondary SuperTrend direction = −1 AND primary AI classification = 1 AND secondary AI classification = 1
Short: primary SuperTrend direction = +1 AND secondary SuperTrend direction = +1 AND primary AI classification = 0 AND secondary AI classification = 0
Exit: Long, neither SuperTrend changes from +1 to −1 with its AI classification = 1 AND low ≤ primary SuperTrend − primary ATR × primary factor
Exit: Short, neither SuperTrend changes from −1 to +1 with its AI classification = 0 AND high ≥ primary SuperTrend + primary ATR × primary factor
