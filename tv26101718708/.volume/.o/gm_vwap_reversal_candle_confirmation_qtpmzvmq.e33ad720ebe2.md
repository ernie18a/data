Calculation: Anchored VWAP deviation bands = VWAP of close or HLC3, anchored to session, week, or month, plus/minus the same anchor’s source standard deviation scaled by the outer-band and entry-zone multipliers; outer distance > entry distance. Use the confirmed current bar’s values.
Calculation: ADX = smoothed directional movement index; rising = current ADX > previous bar’s ADX.
Long: On a confirmed, data-ready bar, arm when low < lower entry band AND low < min(open, close) AND high < VWAP AND the low is within the configured tolerance of a zero-anchored round-price level; OR, in close-break mode, when close < lower outer band. Require setup eligibility: after a VWAP-touch reset, or when repeat setups are enabled and the minimum setup spacing has elapsed. After a later bullish C1, a later bullish C2 with close > C1 close, and a subsequent bar with close > C2 close, emit long only if close <= lower entry band AND NOT (ADX > threshold AND ADX rising). The first close > C2 close consumes the setup even if the entry-zone or ADX condition fails. Cancel on a VWAP touch (high >= VWAP in wick mode; close >= VWAP in close-break mode), expiry after the configured wait from C1, or, after C2, close below the lowest close from the arming bar through C2. In C1/C2 phase, a close below the running lowest close restarts the leading phase. Reset pending setups at a new VWAP anchor or when data is not ready.
Short: On a confirmed, data-ready bar, arm when high > upper entry band AND high > max(open, close) AND low > VWAP AND the high is within the configured tolerance of a zero-anchored round-price level; OR, in close-break mode, when close > upper outer band. Require setup eligibility: after a VWAP-touch reset, or when repeat setups are enabled and the minimum setup spacing has elapsed. After a later bearish C1, a later bearish C2 with close < C1 close, and a subsequent bar with close < C2 close, emit short only if close >= upper entry band AND NOT (ADX > threshold AND ADX rising). The first close < C2 close consumes the setup even if the entry-zone or ADX condition fails. Cancel on a VWAP touch (low <= VWAP in wick mode; close <= VWAP in close-break mode), expiry after the configured wait from C1, or, after C2, close above the highest close from the arming bar through C2. In C1/C2 phase, a close above the running highest close restarts the leading phase. Reset pending setups at a new VWAP anchor or when data is not ready.
