Calculation: Composite baseline = average of the midpoints of the 9-bar, 26-bar, and 52-bar high-low ranges, and the average of the first two midpoints.
Calculation: Regime boundaries = the composite baseline plus/minus the average candle body over a lookback; clamp the upper boundary to the lower of that value and the rolling average of (high+low)/2 plus an average-range offset, and clamp the lower boundary to the higher of its corresponding value and the average minus that offset.
Calculation: Regime state = +1 when a confirmed bar closes above both the upper boundary and composite baseline; −1 when it closes below both the lower boundary and baseline; otherwise retain the previous state.
Calculation: Compression = current average high-low range divided by its longer-lookback average; compressed when this ratio is below a threshold.
Calculation: Pressure composite = EMA of a weighted blend of normalized signed candle-body pressure over a rolling window and the close's normalized position within a channel centered on the average of (high+low)/2. Pressure signal = SMA of the pressure composite over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Mapped midpoint = lowest low over a lookback plus smoothed RSI multiplied by the high-low span over that lookback, divided by 100 and multiplied by a scale. Mapping slope = EMA of the midpoint's bar-to-bar change. Bullish mapping means close above the midpoint and slope nonnegative; bearish mapping means close below it and slope nonpositive.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the close and EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; bullish if close is higher, bearish if lower.
Calculation: Structure zones = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low and the latest high-volume bullish and bearish candle zones; zones and pivots remain active for a limited number of bars. Bullish structure means close is above an active pivot low or within an active bullish zone; bearish structure means close is below an active pivot high or within an active bearish zone.
Calculation: Wave direction = sign of the sum of bar-to-bar close direction over a lookback (or the current bar-to-bar direction); retain the prior nonzero direction. Wave is bullish/bearish when direction is positive/negative and the running sum of candle body size plus half the bar range (or body size alone) is at least its longer-lookback average.
Long: confirmed bar AND long entries enabled AND regime state >= 0 AND at least two of bullish pressure, bullish mapping, bullish structure, and bullish wave AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias >= 0) AND (when compression release is required, bullish release OR bullish wave; otherwise bullish wave AND not compressed) AND (when demand retest is required, close within an active bullish zone; otherwise bullish structure OR close above composite baseline) AND (pressure composite crosses above pressure signal OR close crosses above mapped midpoint OR close crosses above composite baseline).
Short: confirmed bar AND short entries enabled AND regime state <= 0 AND at least two of bearish pressure, bearish mapping, bearish structure, and bearish wave AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bias <= 0) AND (when compression release is required, bearish release OR bearish wave; otherwise bearish wave AND not compressed) AND (when supply retest is required, close within an active bearish zone; otherwise bearish structure OR close below composite baseline) AND (pressure composite crosses below pressure signal OR close crosses below mapped midpoint OR close crosses below composite baseline).
Exit: long, low <= effective stop OR high >= target OR confirmed bar AND regime-failure exit enabled AND (regime state < 0 OR pressure composite < 0 OR bearish mapping) OR bars in trade >= maximum.
Exit: short, high >= effective stop OR low <= target OR confirmed bar AND regime-failure exit enabled AND (regime state > 0 OR pressure composite > 0 OR bullish mapping) OR bars in trade >= maximum.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price minus/plus ATR times a stop multiple for long/short; target = entry price plus/minus initial risk times a reward-to-risk multiple. After price reaches the entry price plus/minus initial risk times the activation multiple, trail at close minus/plus ATR times a trail multiple for long/short, updating the long trail only upward and the short trail only downward; effective stop is the tighter of initial and trailing stops.
