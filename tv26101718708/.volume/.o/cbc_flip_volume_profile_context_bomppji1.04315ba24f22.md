Calculation: Master volume profile = Over a trailing window ending at the last completed bar, divide the window’s high-low range into equal bins; assign each bar’s volume to the bin containing its HLC3 price. The POC is the center of the fullest bin. Starting there, expand the value area by repeatedly adding the heavier adjacent bin until it contains the configured share of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges.
Calculation: CBC control = Bulls take control when close > previous bar high; bears take control when close < previous bar low; otherwise control persists. A flip occurs only when control changes sides.
Long: Confirmed bull flip AND (close > VAH OR close <= POC) AND (the same direction and profile class was not the previous displayed signal) AND (NOT (close > VAH AND close - VAH > (VAH - POC) × maximum extension fraction)) AND (if enabled, close > open) AND (if enabled, high - low >= minimum flip range in ATR multiples)
Short: Confirmed bear flip AND (close < VAL OR close >= POC) AND (the same direction and profile class was not the previous displayed signal) AND (NOT (close < VAL AND VAL - close > (POC - VAL) × maximum extension fraction)) AND (if enabled, close < open) AND (if enabled, high - low >= minimum flip range in ATR multiples)
