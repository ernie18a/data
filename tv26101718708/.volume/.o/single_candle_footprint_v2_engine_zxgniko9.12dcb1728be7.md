Calculation: Per-level ask and bid volume = Divide each chart bar’s price range into configurable rows. For each lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar, divide its volume equally among the rows its high-low range touches; assign it to ask if close > open, otherwise to bid. Use the lower-timeframe bars available for the current chart bar, including any still forming.
Calculation: Total footprint delta = Sum of per-level ask volume minus bid volume across the current chart bar’s rows.
Long: At least the configurable number of consecutive rows have ask volume > bid volume × the configurable imbalance ratio OR close < open AND total footprint delta > 0 OR the highest row has ask volume > bid volume × the configurable imbalance ratio AND highest-row bid volume is below a configurable tick-size-based threshold OR total footprint delta > 0 AND neither absorption condition is true
Short: At least the configurable number of consecutive rows have bid volume > ask volume × the configurable imbalance ratio OR close > open AND total footprint delta < 0 OR the lowest row has bid volume > ask volume × the configurable imbalance ratio AND lowest-row ask volume is below a configurable tick-size-based threshold OR total footprint delta <= 0 AND neither absorption condition is true
