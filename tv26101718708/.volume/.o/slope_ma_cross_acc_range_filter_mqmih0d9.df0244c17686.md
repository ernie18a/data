Calculation: Selected moving average = ALMA, EMA, VWMA, or Hull moving average of close
Calculation: Moving average slope = selected moving average on the current chart bar minus its value at the slope lookback
Calculation: Slope acceleration = current moving average slope minus the moving average slope on the previous chart bar
Calculation: Slope angle = degrees(arctan(100 × (current selected moving average − selected moving average at the slope lookback) ÷ selected moving average at the slope lookback × angle multiplier))
Calculation: Typical price = (high + low + close) ÷ 3
Calculation: Long stop = the higher of entry price × (1 − stop distance) and the active tightening stop; the tightening stop starts only after the bar's high reaches the profit threshold and, when enabled, the slope is positive and lower than on the previous bar. Its candidate is the higher of typical price × (1 − stop distance × tightening multiplier) and entry price × (1 − stop distance); retain the highest candidate reached while the position is open.
Calculation: Short stop = the lower of entry price × (1 + stop distance) and the active tightening stop; the tightening stop starts only after the bar's low reaches the profit threshold and, when enabled, the slope is negative and higher than on the previous bar. Its candidate is the lower of typical price × (1 + stop distance × tightening multiplier) and entry price × (1 + stop distance); retain the lowest candidate reached while the position is open.
Long: ((current moving average slope > 0 AND previous slope <= 0) OR (current close > current selected moving average AND previous close <= previous selected moving average) OR (current slope acceleration > 0 AND previous slope acceleration <= 0) OR (current close > previous chart bar high AND current moving average slope > 0 AND current close > current selected moving average)) AND current slope angle >= minimum trend angle
Short: ((current moving average slope < 0 AND previous slope >= 0) OR (current close < current selected moving average AND previous close >= previous selected moving average) OR (current slope acceleration < 0 AND previous slope acceleration >= 0) OR (current close < previous chart bar low AND current moving average slope < 0 AND current close < current selected moving average)) AND current slope angle <= −minimum trend angle
Exit: Long, (current moving average slope < 0 AND previous slope >= 0) OR (current close < current selected moving average AND previous close >= previous selected moving average) OR (current slope acceleration < 0 AND previous slope acceleration >= 0) OR current low <= long stop OR current high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit distance) OR (trailing activated and current low <= trailing stop)
Exit: Short, (current moving average slope > 0 AND previous slope <= 0) OR (current close > current selected moving average AND previous close <= previous selected moving average) OR (current slope acceleration > 0 AND previous slope acceleration <= 0) OR current high >= short stop OR current low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit distance) OR (trailing activated and current high >= trailing stop)
