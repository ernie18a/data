Calculation: Neural oscillator = weighted sum of rolling-Z-score-normalized RSI, MACD line, Stochastic, MFI, and CCI; weights are each oscillator’s positive rolling correlation with forward close return, normalized to sum to one, or equal when all correlations are nonpositive.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a local high or low with the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars to its right have completed.
Calculation: Divergence confidence = average of a capped score based on the relative price-pivot change and absolute neural-oscillator-pivot change, and the fraction of the five normalized oscillators that move in the confirming direction between the corresponding price-pivot bars.
Long: A new confirmed price low pivot exists AND the distance from the prior price low pivot is within the configured minimum and maximum AND ((current price low < prior price low AND current neural-oscillator low pivot > prior neural-oscillator low pivot) OR (current price low > prior price low AND current neural-oscillator low pivot < prior neural-oscillator low pivot)) AND divergence confidence >= minimum confidence AND (no prior long signal exists OR bars since prior long signal > cooldown).
Short: A new confirmed price high pivot exists AND the distance from the prior price high pivot is within the configured minimum and maximum AND ((current price high > prior price high AND current neural-oscillator high pivot < prior neural-oscillator high pivot) OR (current price high < prior price high AND current neural-oscillator high pivot > prior neural-oscillator high pivot)) AND divergence confidence >= minimum confidence AND (no prior short signal exists OR bars since prior short signal > cooldown).
