Calculation: Adaptive equilibrium = the current rail range’s lower rail plus its width times a bounded, centre-shifted average of the EMA and volume-weighted moving average of the normalized HLC3 position within the rails; use a weighted moving average if volume-weighted data is unavailable. The rail lookback is adaptive and the rails use current chart bars.
Long: Close crosses above the previous completed bar’s rolling highest high AND (the prior trend state was bullish OR bearish)
Short: Close crosses below the previous completed bar’s rolling lowest low AND (the prior trend state was bearish OR bullish)
Long: A later confirmed bar, before the pending setup expires, has low <= stored breakout level AND close > stored breakout level AND close > open AND close position within the bar >= 58% AND close > adaptive equilibrium AND stored breakout level > adaptive equilibrium AND equilibrium is rising AND the active rail width meets the configured minimum relative to stop distance AND any configured maximum structural-stop distance is met; the bullish trend remains active
Short: A later confirmed bar, before the pending setup expires, has high >= stored breakout level AND close < stored breakout level AND close < open AND close position within the bar <= 42% AND close < adaptive equilibrium AND stored breakout level < adaptive equilibrium AND equilibrium is falling AND the active rail width meets the configured minimum relative to stop distance AND any configured maximum structural-stop distance is met; the bearish trend remains active
Exit: Long, low <= active stop price OR high >= active target price
Exit: Short, high >= active stop price OR low <= active target price
