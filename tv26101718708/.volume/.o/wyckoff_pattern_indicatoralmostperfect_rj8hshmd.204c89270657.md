Calculation: Relative range position = (close − lowest low over the range lookback) / max(highest high over the range lookback − lowest low over the range lookback, 0.01 × ATR(14)); the range lookback is a parameter.
Calculation: Relative volatility = 20-bar standard deviation of close / 20-bar SMA of close; compare it with its SMA over the range lookback, using the same range lookback as above.
Calculation: Accumulation and distribution scores = add points for: relative range position in the lower or upper range region, respectively; long-term EMA trend down or up, respectively; relative volatility indicating a range, both; low volume during a range, both; RSI below or above its midpoint, respectively; and MACD histogram rising or falling versus three bars earlier, respectively. Require a qualifying score to exceed the opposing score; confirm either result after three consecutive qualifying bars.
Calculation: Accumulation phases = reset to 0 when accumulation is not confirmed; otherwise progress 0→1, 1→2 when relative volatility indicates a range, 2→3 on a Spring, 3→4 on a Sign of Strength, and 4→5 when the short-term EMA trend is up and relative volatility no longer indicates a range. Distribution phases reset and progress analogously: 0→1, 1→2 in a range, 2→3 on an Upthrust After Distribution, 3→4 on a Sign of Weakness, and 4→5 when the short-term EMA trend is down and relative volatility no longer indicates a range.
Calculation: Selling Climax = close < open AND volume exceeds its lookback SMA by the climax multiplier AND (high − close) / max(high − low, 0.0001) > 0.6 AND close ≤ lowest low over the range lookback × 1.002.
Calculation: Buying Climax = close > open AND volume exceeds its lookback SMA by the climax multiplier AND (close − low) / max(high − low, 0.0001) > 0.6 AND close ≥ highest high over the range lookback × 0.998.
Calculation: Spring = close > open AND low < the previous bar's lowest low over the 20-bar lookback AND close > that previous-bar low AND volume > 5-bar volume SMA.
Calculation: Upthrust After Distribution = close < open AND high > the previous bar's highest high over the 20-bar lookback AND close < that previous-bar high AND volume > 5-bar volume SMA.
Calculation: Sign of Strength = close > open AND close > the previous bar's highest close over the 10-bar lookback AND volume > 10-bar volume SMA AND MACD histogram > 0. Sign of Weakness uses the corresponding lowest close, close < open, volume > 10-bar volume SMA, and MACD histogram < 0.
Calculation: Last Point of Support = close > open AND volume does not exceed its lookback SMA by the climax multiplier AND short-term EMA trend is up AND relative range position > 0.4. Last Point of Supply uses close < open, short-term EMA trend down, and relative range position < 0.6, with the same volume condition.
Calculation: New BUY or SELL state = its raw state is true and the previous signal state was not that same state; raw BUY is true when not in distribution and (accumulation phase is 3 or 4, or Spring outside distribution, or Last Point of Support during accumulation). Raw SELL is true when not in accumulation and (distribution phase is 3 or 4, or Upthrust After Distribution outside accumulation, or Last Point of Supply during distribution). The tracked signal state is BUY, SELL, HOLD, or none, in that priority order; it resets to none when all raw states are false.
Long: Selling Climax OR Spring outside distribution OR Sign of Strength OR Last Point of Support during accumulation OR new BUY state.
Short: Buying Climax OR Sign of Weakness OR Upthrust After Distribution outside accumulation OR Last Point of Supply during distribution OR new SELL state.
