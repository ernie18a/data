Calculation: Per-scale speed = tick rate or volume rate divided by its own EMA baseline, or the square root of their product when blended; calculate separately for 1T, 10T, 100T, and 1000T. Use current chart-bar 1T trades and each tick scale’s latest available candles, including a forming candle where present.
Calculation: Per-scale direction = price direction or volume delta, according to the selected basis. For 10T, 100T, and 1000T, price direction is net open-to-open move divided by total absolute move over the configured recent candles; volume delta is up-candle volume minus down-candle volume divided by total volume over those moves. These use the latest available tick candles, including the forming candle’s open for price direction and completed candles’ volume for volume delta. For 1T, price direction is upticks minus downticks divided by their total within the current chart bar; volume delta is buyer-initiated minus seller-initiated trade volume divided by total trade volume in that bar.
Calculation: Tick Velocity = weighted average across enabled scales with data of per-scale direction × the lesser of per-scale speed and the configured speed cap, divided by the sum of their weights.
Long: Tick Velocity crosses above its configured positive threshold.
Long: At least two enabled scales with data have per-scale direction greater than 0.05 and per-scale speed above the configured surge threshold, and all enabled scales with data meet both conditions.
Short: Tick Velocity crosses below the negative of its configured threshold.
Short: At least two enabled scales with data have per-scale direction less than -0.05 and per-scale speed above the configured surge threshold, and all enabled scales with data meet both conditions.
