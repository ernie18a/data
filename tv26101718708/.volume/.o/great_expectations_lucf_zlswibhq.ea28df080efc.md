Long: SMA(close, short lookback) rises for its configured lookback OR +DI crosses above −DI while ADX > threshold; AND (close > open if the bar-direction filter is enabled); AND (volume rises from the previous bar if the rising-volume filter is enabled); AND (SMA(close, independent filter lookback) rises for its configured lookback if the MA filter is enabled); AND (long-only filter is not enabled)
Short: SMA(close, short lookback) falls for its configured lookback OR −DI crosses above +DI while ADX > threshold; AND (close < open if the bar-direction filter is enabled); AND (volume rises from the previous bar if the rising-volume filter is enabled); AND (SMA(close, independent filter lookback) falls for its configured lookback if the MA filter is enabled); AND (short-only filter is not enabled)
Calculation: +DI, −DI, and ADX = directional indicators and average directional index calculated from OHLC bars
Calculation: Active stop = initial entry stop, then the more protective of the prior stop and the selected in-trade stop; in-trade stop is either the average of the two preceding bars’ minimum (long) or maximum (short) of open and close adjusted by an ATR multiple, or a trailing stop from the highest high (long) or lowest low (short) since entry adjusted by a multiple of entry-stop distance or a fixed percentage
Exit: Long, close < active stop from the preceding bar
Exit: Short, close > active stop from the preceding bar
