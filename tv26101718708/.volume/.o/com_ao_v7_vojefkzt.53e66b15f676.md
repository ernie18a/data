Calculation: confirmed swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low, each requiring the configured number of bars on both sides for confirmation
Calculation: higher highs = the latest confirmed swing high is above the preceding confirmed swing high; higher lows = the latest confirmed swing low is above the preceding confirmed swing low; lower highs/lows are the corresponding below comparisons
Calculation: bullish structure = higher highs AND higher lows; bearish structure = lower highs AND lower lows
Calculation: bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier
Calculation: bullish FVG retest = after a bullish FVG forms, low <= its top and close > its bottom; bearish FVG retest = after a bearish FVG forms, high >= its bottom and close < its top
Calculation: bullish order block = the high and low of the latest bearish candle when close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish order block = the high and low of the latest bullish candle when close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low
Calculation: bullish order block retest = low <= bullish order block high AND close >= its low; bearish order block retest = high >= bearish order block low AND close <= its high
Calculation: Fib retracement zone = for the latest confirmed swing high and low, long levels are high minus the range times 0.5, 0.618, and 0.786; short levels are low plus the range times those ratios
Calculation: long Fib zone = low <= long 0.5 level AND close >= long 0.786 level; short Fib zone = high >= short 0.5 level AND close <= short 0.786 level
Calculation: strong rise/fall = close is above/below its value at the configured lookback by at least the configured percentage
