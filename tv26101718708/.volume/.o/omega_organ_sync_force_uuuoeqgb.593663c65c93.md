Calculation: Organ Flow = EMA of the EMA-smoothed sum of four Z-scores: the change in the trend EMA, the close ROC, the change in OBV, and the change in ATR multiplied by +1 when close is above its volume-direction EMA and −1 otherwise. Each Z-score is (value − lookback mean) / lookback standard deviation, or 0 when the standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Organ Signal = EMA of Organ Flow, using the same EMA length as Organ Flow’s final smoothing.
Calculation: Chaos = EMA of the mean of the absolute differences between the trend, momentum, and OBV Z-scores.
Calculation: Timeframe Force = EMA of the weighted sum of Organ Flow from three other timeframes, using each timeframe’s last completed bar.
Long: Organ Flow crosses above the positive sync-flip threshold AND Chaos < the low-chaos threshold AND Timeframe Force > 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values > 0 OR Organ Flow > Organ Signal AND Timeframe Force > 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values > 0 AND the absolute difference between Organ Flow and Organ Signal is greater than on the previous bar OR Chaos crosses below the collapse threshold AND the highest Chaos over its lookback > the high-chaos threshold AND Organ Flow > the positive sync-flip threshold AND Timeframe Force > 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values > 0.
Short: Organ Flow crosses below the negative sync-flip threshold AND Chaos < the low-chaos threshold AND Timeframe Force < 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values < 0 OR Organ Flow < Organ Signal AND Timeframe Force < 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values < 0 AND the absolute difference between Organ Flow and Organ Signal is greater than on the previous bar OR Chaos crosses below the collapse threshold AND the highest Chaos over its lookback > the high-chaos threshold AND Organ Flow < the negative sync-flip threshold AND Timeframe Force < 0 AND at least two of the three timeframe Organ Flow values < 0.
