Calculation: Double-top score = round to 1 decimal the sum of: price match (max(0, 1 − abs(peak1 − peak2) / max(ATR-at-peak2 × tolerance, 0.000001)) × 25); RSI divergence (if RSI2 < RSI1, min(25, 10 + (RSI1 − RSI2) × 1), else 0); volume divergence (if volume2 < volume1, min(25, 10 + min((volume1 − volume2) / max(volume1, 1), 1) × 15), else 0); and height (if height / ATR-at-peak2 ≥ minimum-height multiplier, min(25, 10 + min(max(0, (height / ATR-at-peak2 − minimum-height multiplier) / (2 × minimum-height multiplier)), 1) × 15), else 0).
Calculation: Double-bottom score = the same score, using RSI divergence only when RSI2 > RSI1; other components are unchanged.
Calculation: Valid double top = two confirmed pivot highs, with the second later than the first by between the minimum and maximum spacing; their price difference ≤ ATR at the second pivot × tolerance; the neckline is the lowest low between them, below both highs; no intervening high exceeds the higher pivot price plus half the allowed price difference; pattern height (higher pivot price − neckline) ≥ ATR at the second pivot × minimum-height multiplier; and double-top score ≥ minimum score. Use the most recent earlier pivot that passes all criteria. A pivot is confirmed using the configured left and right lookbacks; its price, RSI, volume, and ATR are taken from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Valid double bottom = the corresponding rule using confirmed pivot lows; the neckline is the highest intervening high, above both lows; no intervening low falls below the lower pivot price minus half the allowed price difference; height is neckline − lower pivot price; and double-bottom score ≥ minimum score. Use the most recent earlier pivot that passes all criteria.
Calculation: Breakout targets and stop = for a long, target 1 = neckline + pattern height, target 2 = neckline + 1.618 × pattern height, stop = lower pivot price − ATR-at-breakout × stop-buffer; for a short, target 1 = neckline − pattern height, target 2 = neckline − 1.618 × pattern height, stop = higher pivot price + ATR-at-breakout × stop-buffer.
Long: A valid, uncompleted double bottom has not been invalidated (low ≥ lower pivot price − current ATR × stop-buffer) or expired (bars since second pivot ≤ maximum breakout wait); close > neckline AND (previous close ≤ neckline OR this is the second pivot's confirmation bar); and trend filter disabled OR close > EMA(close, configured lookback).
Short: A valid, uncompleted double top has not been invalidated (high ≤ higher pivot price + current ATR × stop-buffer) or expired (bars since second pivot ≤ maximum breakout wait); close < neckline AND (previous close ≥ neckline OR this is the second pivot's confirmation bar); and trend filter disabled OR close < EMA(close, configured lookback).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR low ≥ target 2.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR high ≤ target 2.
