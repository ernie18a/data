Calculation: WaveTrend Fast and Slow = On the current timeframe, let source be HLC3, channel length C, average length A, and slow smoothing length M. ESA = EMA(source,C); deviation = EMA(abs(source−ESA),C); channel index = (source−ESA)/(0.015×deviation); Fast = EMA(channel index,A); Slow = SMA(Fast,M).
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a value higher/lower than the two bars on each side; detected two bars after the pivot.
Calculation: WaveTrend regular divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, price high exceeds the previous qualifying pivot high while WaveTrend Slow is lower; at a confirmed pivot low, price low is lower than the previous qualifying pivot low while WaveTrend Slow is higher. Qualifying bearish pivot highs meet the configured minimum WaveTrend level; qualifying bullish pivot lows meet the configured maximum WaveTrend level. The indicator uses two such threshold pairs, one stricter than the other.
Calculation: WaveTrend hidden divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, price high is lower while WaveTrend Slow is higher than at the previous pivot high; at a confirmed pivot low, price low is higher while WaveTrend Slow is lower than at the previous pivot low. These pivots have no level restriction.
Calculation: RSI = standard RSI of close. RSI regular divergence = confirmed RSI pivot high with price high higher and RSI lower than at the previous qualifying pivot high, or confirmed pivot low with price low lower and RSI higher; bearish pivots meet the configured minimum RSI level and bullish pivots meet the configured maximum RSI level. RSI hidden divergence reverses the price and RSI comparisons and has no level restriction.
Calculation: Money Flow = SMA(((close−open)/(high−low))×multiplier, lookback)−offset, calculated on the current timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Fast WT impulse = WaveTrend Fast−WaveTrend Slow, calculated on the configured higher timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi direction = Heikin-Ashi close > open for bullish, < open for bearish, using each configured higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: WaveTrend Fast crosses above WaveTrend Slow OR WaveTrend regular bullish divergence OR WaveTrend hidden bullish divergence OR RSI regular bullish divergence OR RSI hidden bullish divergence OR the realtime RSI bullish divergence scan finds a valid regular or hidden divergence between current RSI/close and a prior scanned price pivot low (within configured pivot-count and bar limits, with both RSI and close remaining above their straight lines between endpoints) OR Fast WT impulse crosses above zero with Money Flow above its configured bullish level and WaveTrend Slow below its configured bullish level OR WaveTrend Fast crosses above WaveTrend Slow with WaveTrend Slow at or below its configured bullish level and both higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles bullish OR (WaveTrend regular bullish divergence OR RSI regular bullish divergence) AND the prior WaveTrend pivot low is at or below the configured extreme level AND current WaveTrend Slow is above that level by at least the configured minimum recovery AND RSI at the prior WaveTrend pivot is below 30.
Short: WaveTrend Fast crosses below WaveTrend Slow OR WaveTrend regular bearish divergence OR WaveTrend hidden bearish divergence OR RSI regular bearish divergence OR RSI hidden bearish divergence OR the realtime RSI bearish divergence scan finds a valid regular or hidden divergence between current RSI/close and a prior scanned price pivot high (within configured pivot-count and bar limits, with both RSI and close remaining below their straight lines between endpoints) OR Fast WT impulse crosses below zero with Money Flow below its configured bearish level and WaveTrend Slow above its configured bearish level OR WaveTrend Fast crosses below WaveTrend Slow with WaveTrend Slow at or above its configured bearish level and both higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles bearish.
