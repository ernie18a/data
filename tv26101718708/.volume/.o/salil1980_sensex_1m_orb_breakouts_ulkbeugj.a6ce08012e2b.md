Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the session’s opening range, fixed when that range ends
Long: Close crosses above the opening-range high after it is fixed OR (close crosses above VWAP AND RSI > 50) OR (RSI crosses above 50 AND close > VWAP)
Short: Close crosses below the opening-range low after it is fixed OR (close crosses below VWAP AND RSI < 50)
