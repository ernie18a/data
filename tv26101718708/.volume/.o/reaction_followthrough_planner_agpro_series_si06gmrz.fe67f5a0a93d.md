Calculation: Confirmed swing low/high = the latest low/high that is the lowest/highest among itself and the configured number of bars on each side; it becomes available after the bars to its right complete.
Calculation: Reaction tolerance = ATR × configured zone-width factor; use the current completed bar’s ATR, falling back to true range if ATR is unavailable.
Calculation: Bull reaction components = count of (close > latest confirmed swing low, close ≥ open, lower wick ≥ upper wick, close position in bar range ≥ 0.55); bear reaction components = count of (close < latest confirmed swing high, close ≤ open, upper wick ≥ lower wick, close position in bar range ≤ 0.45). A reaction requires the configured minimum count for the selected response mode.
Calculation: Reaction strength = clamp(100 × (0.38 × close defense + 0.32 × directional wick / bar range + 0.15 × body / bar range + 0.15 × directional body agreement), 0, 100); for bullish reactions close defense is close position and directional body agreement is 1 when close ≥ open, otherwise 0; for bearish reactions they are 1 − close position and 1 when close ≤ open, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its configured-length simple moving average; use 1 when the average is not positive. Reaction volume score = clamp(relative volume / 1.70 × 100, 0, 100).
Calculation: Bull reaction zone = top max(anchor + tolerance, min(open, close)), bottom min(low, anchor − tolerance), where anchor is the latest confirmed swing low. Bear reaction zone = top max(high, anchor + tolerance), bottom min(anchor − tolerance, max(open, close)), where anchor is the latest confirmed swing high.
Calculation: Invalidation = bullish reaction zone bottom − ATR × configured invalidation-buffer factor; bearish reaction zone top + ATR × configured invalidation-buffer factor.
Calculation: Follow-through progress score = clamp(best favorable high/low excursion from reaction close, divided by ATR and by the configured fallback-target ATR multiple, × 100, 0, 100); track the maximum excursion since the reaction bar.
Calculation: Next-bar score = clamp(close progress in the reaction direction on the first bar after the reaction, divided by ATR × 0.55, × 100, 0, 100); calculate once.
Calculation: Defense score = 0 after invalidation; otherwise clamp(100 − worst adverse excursion from reaction close, divided by ATR and by max(invalidation-buffer factor + zone-width factor, 0.10), × 38, 0, 100); track the maximum adverse excursion since the reaction bar.
Calculation: Time score = clamp(100 − elapsed bars / configured tracking-window bars × 42, 25, 100).
Calculation: Follow-through score = clamp(0.24 × reaction strength + 0.28 × progress score + 0.18 × next-bar score + 0.12 × reaction volume score + 0.12 × defense score + 0.06 × time score, 0, 100).
Long: On a completed bar, after the configured reaction cooldown, the bar intersects the latest confirmed swing low ± reaction tolerance, bullish reaction components meet the selected response-mode minimum, and the low is at least as close to the close as the latest confirmed swing high (or no swing high is touched) OR an active bullish reaction is not invalidated, elapsed bars > 0, follow-through score ≥ configured minimum, and progress score ≥ 48.
Short: On a completed bar, after the configured reaction cooldown, the bar intersects the latest confirmed swing high ± reaction tolerance, bearish reaction components meet the selected response-mode minimum, and the high is closer to the close than the latest confirmed swing low (or no swing low is touched) OR an active bearish reaction is not invalidated, elapsed bars > 0, follow-through score ≥ configured minimum, and progress score ≥ 48.
Exit: long, close < active bullish invalidation
Exit: short, close > active bearish invalidation
