Calculation: Active upper/lower liquidity levels = stored swing highs/lows from three lookbacks ordered fast < medium < slow. For each lookback, add a level when the rolling highest high (lowest low) first becomes available, or when it equals its value on the prior bar and is strictly higher (lower) than its value three bars earlier; merge nearby duplicate levels. Keep levels active until mitigated by a wick through them or they expire; evaluate signals against levels active before the current bar’s level updates.
Long: Bar is confirmed AND (low < min(open, close) AND low <= an active lower level < min(open, close) OR close > open AND the candle body strictly crosses at least two active upper levels AND (open + close) / 2 is above the lowest crossed upper level).
Short: Bar is confirmed AND (high > max(open, close) AND max(open, close) < an active upper level <= high OR close < open AND the candle body strictly crosses at least two active lower levels AND (open + close) / 2 is below the highest crossed lower level).
