Calculation: Survival score = Sum of eight scores: (1) MACD line (shorter-period EMA of close minus longer-period EMA) versus its signal EMA: +1 when the line is positive and above signal, 0 when positive but not above signal, −1 when nonpositive and not above signal, otherwise 0; (2) MACD histogram (line minus signal): +1 when nonnegative and rising, 0 when nonnegative and not rising or negative and rising, −1 when negative and not rising; (3) smoothed RSI: +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its lower threshold, otherwise 0; (4) shorter-period EMA of close versus longer-period EMA: +1 when the shorter is above the longer, otherwise −1; (5) ADX rising flag: when ADX is above its shorter-period EMA, score +1 if close is above its shorter-period EMA, otherwise −1; otherwise 0. ADX is the smoothed ratio of the absolute difference between smoothed positive and negative directional movement to their sum, with directional movement normalized by smoothed true range; (6) smoothed Williams %R: +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its lower threshold, otherwise 0; (7) volume-weighted close-location ratio, calculated as the rolling sum of ((2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) × volume) divided by rolling volume: +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its negative upper threshold, otherwise 0; (8) ATR-based Supertrend direction, initialized positive and switched negative when close falls below its trailing lower band, or positive when close rises above its trailing upper band.
Calculation: Adjusted score = 0 when the score changes across zero or touches zero after being on the opposite side; otherwise the survival score.
Calculation: Level and level direction = on an adjusted-score transition from negative to nonnegative, set level to high plus a small percentage buffer and direction to green; on a transition from positive to nonpositive, set level to low minus a small percentage buffer and direction to red; otherwise retain the prior direction. For the level, if the prior level is nonzero, set it to zero; otherwise use the applicable transition level, or the prior level if there is no transition.
Long: high > level AND level direction is green OR prior adjusted score > 0 AND adjusted score > 0
Exit: long, low < level AND level direction is red OR prior adjusted score < 0 AND adjusted score < 0
