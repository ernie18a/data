Calculation: ORMA = apply the selected moving average to sqrt(close), then compute core = base × (base × 2/(1+length) + prior base × (1−2/(1+length))); regime MA = core × (1−ATR(full length)×factor / ATR(rounded half length)×factor) + prior core × (ATR(full length)×factor / ATR(rounded half length)×factor). Upper/lower bands = regime MA ± ATR(full length)×factor.
Calculation: Trend state = 1 when close is above the upper band, −1 when close is below the lower band, otherwise retain the previous state. Compression = Bollinger Band width is below 0.85 × its 50-bar SMA; width = 2 × Bollinger multiplier × standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Displacement = EMA of 0.7 × oscillator composite + 0.3 × volume-weighted candle body. Oscillator composite is the mean of: clamped [-1,1] (2 × Bollinger %B − 1), clamped [-1,1] CCI/200, and clamped [-1,1] ROC/(2 × its 50-bar standard deviation). Volume-weighted candle body = candle direction × abs(close−open)/(high−low) × clamped [-1,1] (volume/SMA(volume,20)−1); direction is 1 if close>open, −1 if close<open, otherwise 0. Bull displacement = displacement > 1.5 × its 100-bar standard deviation; bear displacement = displacement < −1.5 × its 100-bar standard deviation.
Calculation: Confluence score = round and clamp to [0,100] ((0.4 × current-timeframe score + 0.6 × higher-timeframe score + 1)/2 × 100). Current-timeframe score is the mean of: 1 if close>EMA(close,length), otherwise −1; oscillator composite; clamped [-1,1] 3 × (Bollinger Band width / its 50-bar SMA − 1); structure score; and candle direction × min(1, volume intensity). Structure state becomes 0 if high[length] exceeds the highest high of the last length bars, becomes 1 if low[length] is below the lowest low of the last length bars, otherwise retains its prior state; structure score is 1 for state 1, otherwise −1. Volume intensity is clamped [-1,1] (volume/SMA(volume,20)−1). Higher-timeframe score = mean of 1 if higher-timeframe close>EMA(close,length), otherwise −1, and a momentum score of 1 if higher-timeframe Bollinger %B>0.5, −1 if %B<0, otherwise 0. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = for any offset 1–10, low[offset]>high[offset+2] and close[offset+1]>high[offset+2]. Bearish FVG = for any offset 1–10, high[offset]<low[offset+2] and close[offset+1]<low[offset+2].
Calculation: Active session = UTC hour is within any configured session range. When session filtering is enabled, require an active session.
Calculation: Initial stop distance = ATR(length) × stop multiplier; target distance = initial stop distance × reward-to-risk ratio. Long trailing stop candidate = close − ATR(length) × trail multiplier; short candidate = close + ATR(length) × trail multiplier. When trailing is enabled, update the long stop to the greater of its prior value and candidate, or the short stop to the lesser of its prior value and candidate.
Long: Trend state = 1 AND NOT compression AND confluence score ≥ long threshold AND bull displacement AND (session filtering disabled OR active session) AND (FVG filtering disabled OR bullish FVG) AND current bar confirmed AND prior bar's full long setup was false AND position flat.
Short: Trend state = −1 AND NOT compression AND confluence score ≤ short threshold AND bear displacement AND (session filtering disabled OR active session) AND (FVG filtering disabled OR bearish FVG) AND current bar confirmed AND prior bar's full short setup was false AND position flat.
Exit: Long, trend state = −1 OR compression OR bear displacement OR low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ target.
Exit: Short, trend state = 1 OR compression OR bull displacement OR high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ target.
