Calculation: Relative effort = volume divided by its time-of-day baseline built from prior sessions; use true range instead when volume is unavailable, and use a rolling average fallback until the baseline has enough samples.
Calculation: Path efficiency = absolute net log-price change over the gravity lookback divided by the sum of absolute log returns over that lookback.
Calculation: Displacement = absolute net log-price change over the gravity lookback divided by the standard deviation of log returns over the volatility lookback times the square root of the gravity lookback.
Calculation: Gravity = EMA of [log(1 + average relative effort) − log(displacement) + inefficiency weight × log(1 − path efficiency)], where inefficiency is 1 − path efficiency. Directional gravity uses the same formula with positive or negative close-location-weighted effort and the corresponding upside or downside cumulative log-return path.
Calculation: Robust z-score = (value − rolling median) / (1.4826 × rolling median absolute deviation); use a longer lookback for these z-scores than for the underlying gravity measures.
Calculation: Upward effort imbalance = (positive effort − negative effort) / (positive effort + negative effort); directional gravity spread = upward gravity − downward gravity.
Calculation: Wedge edges = straight lines through the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows, projected to the current bar; wedge start is the later of the two older pivots.
Calculation: Adaptive structure = Kaufman adaptive average of close, with efficiency ratio and ATR-based bands; bullish regime requires close above the average, positive slope exceeding an ATR-scaled minimum, and efficiency above a minimum; bearish regime uses the corresponding inverse conditions. A regime persists until close crosses its opposite band for consecutive bars, then becomes the opposite regime only if its raw conditions hold, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Cup depth = highest high over the cup lookback excluding the current bar minus the lowest low over the lookback including it; handle high = highest high over the handle lookback excluding the current bar; handle depth fraction = (cup rim − lowest low over the handle lookback including current bar) / cup depth.
Calculation: Completed higher-timeframe power = close above its long-term simple moving average, close crossing above its short-term exponential moving average, and volume at least a multiple of its average; the short setup reverses the price and cross directions. On a lower chart timeframe, calculate from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; on the matching timeframe, use its confirmed bar; disable above it.
Long: A descending converging wedge qualifies when both latest pivots are lower than their predecessors, both edge slopes are negative with the lower edge steeper downward, current width is no more than a fraction of start width, age is within a maximum, and these conditions persist to maturity; OR a bullish regime shift occurs OR close exceeds the projected upper wedge edge after a matured descending wedge, with the bullish wedge maturity having occurred within the recent-entry window. The maturity event itself indicates long bias. OR a confirmed cup candidate persists to maturity and close exceeds the prior handle high, with structure neutral or bullish, cup depth at least an ATR multiple, close near the rim, handle depth within its maximum, handle high near the rim, and the pattern-break cooldown elapsed. OR completed higher-timeframe power turns bullish.
Short: An ascending converging wedge qualifies when both latest pivots are higher than their predecessors, both edge slopes are positive with the upper edge steeper upward, current width is no more than a fraction of start width, age is within a maximum, and these conditions persist to maturity; its maturity event indicates short bias. OR downside log displacement is negative, path efficiency is at least a high threshold, and at least a threshold count of these nine conditions hold: downside move; path efficiency above a pre-gate; path efficiency rising; gravity easing; displacement z-score rising; effort z-score above a low threshold; effort imbalance negative; directional gravity spread positive; and either recent high gravity/recent pressure or nonpositive gravity acceleration. Require the short condition to have just turned true, the signal cooldown to have elapsed, and no cup breakout on this bar. OR completed higher-timeframe power turns bearish.
Exit: long, low at or below the initial stop, or (after the first target has been reached, starting on the next bar) low at or below the profit-lock stop, or high at or above the third target, or holding bars reach the maximum.
Exit: short, high at or above the initial stop, or (after the first target has been reached, starting on the next bar) high at or above the profit-lock stop, or low at or below the third target, or holding bars reach the maximum.
