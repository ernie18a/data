Calculation: Signed volume delta = volume signed by price change (positive if close > prior close, negative if close < prior close, zero otherwise), summed from the finest available lower-timeframe bars; if unavailable, use volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), or zero for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Participation threshold = fixed threshold when positive; otherwise the greater of rolling standard deviation of signed volume delta × a multiplier and 1.
Calculation: Synthetic bars = accumulate chart-bar high, low, close, volume, and signed volume delta until absolute cumulative delta reaches the participation threshold; then close a synthetic bar and reset the accumulators. Resolve structure, regime, and entries only on confirmed chart bars.
Calculation: Imbalance levels = on each synthetic-bar close, compare the current and two-prior synthetic bars: create a bullish level from the two-prior high to current low when current low is above that high, or a bearish level from current high to the two-prior low when current high is below that low; require gap size ≥ minimum ATR multiple and absolute middle-bar delta ≥ impulse multiple of the participation threshold. ATR uses chart bars. On later synthetic bars, bullish levels are killed if price fully crosses below the level and close is below it with delta ≤ −surge threshold; bearish levels are killed if price fully crosses above and close is above it with delta ≥ surge threshold. A fully crossed bullish level becomes a breaker if close > its top and delta ≥ absorption threshold; a fully crossed bearish level becomes a breaker if close < its bottom and delta ≤ −absorption threshold. Otherwise fully crossed levels lose mid-touch strength; partial touches lose mid-touch strength if they reach the level midpoint, or light-touch strength otherwise. Each later synthetic bar also applies time decay; levels at zero strength die, except breakers. A breaker is not killed by decay.
Calculation: Leading bullish level = highest midpoint below close among live levels whose strength ≥ minimum strength. Leading bearish level = lowest midpoint above close among such levels. Uptrend trail = leading bullish midpoint; downtrend trail = leading bearish midpoint; otherwise no trail.
Calculation: Trend regime = on synthetic bars, efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the regime lookback / sum of absolute consecutive close changes over that lookback. Relative volatility = latest absolute synthetic-close change / average absolute consecutive synthetic-close change over the same lookback. Regime is high volatility if relative volatility exceeds its threshold; otherwise uptrend or downtrend if efficiency ratio ≥ trend cutoff, according to the sign of the net close change; range if efficiency ratio ≤ range cutoff; otherwise transition. Use completed synthetic bars.
Calculation: CVD slope = cumulative synthetic-bar delta change over its lookback. Flow toxicity = sum of absolute synthetic-bar deltas / sum of synthetic-bar volumes over its lookback.
Calculation: Confidence = logistic of the sum of: (leading-level strength − 0.5) × 3 (or −1 if no leading level); +1 in either trend, −0.5 in transition, or −1.2 otherwise; +0.9 when CVD slope agrees with trail direction, −0.9 when it disagrees, or 0 when zero/no trail; −1.1 when flow toxicity exceeds its threshold; and the optional external-bias and implied-volatility alignment terms. Implied-volatility change = its same-timeframe close minus its close the volatility lookback ago, divided by that earlier close; use the last completed bar. External bias is off by default.
Long: Uptrend regime AND confidence ≥ minimum confidence threshold.
Short: Downtrend regime AND confidence ≥ minimum confidence threshold.
Exit: long, close < stop (stop starts at entry trail and ratchets upward to the maximum of itself and each available uptrend trail) OR regime is not uptrend.
Exit: short, close > stop (stop starts at entry trail and ratchets downward to the minimum of itself and each available downtrend trail) OR regime is not downtrend.
