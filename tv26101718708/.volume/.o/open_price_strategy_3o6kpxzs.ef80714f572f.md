Calculation: Open price = selected price source on the first bar of the selected open-price time window, using the selected timeframe; hold that value until the next window
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = sum(((2 × close − low − high) / (high − low)) × volume) / sum(volume) over its lookback; use 0 for the numerator when close = high = low or high = low
Long: (datetime within backtest range AND ASAP entry window AND entry source > open price AND (ASAP EMA filter disabled OR close > ASAP EMA) AND (ASAP CMF filter disabled OR ASAP CMF > 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND ASAP entry window AND entry source < open price AND (ASAP EMA filter disabled OR close < ASAP EMA) AND (ASAP CMF filter disabled OR ASAP CMF < 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND wait-entry window AND wait EMA crosses above open price AND (wait EMA filter disabled OR close > wait EMA) AND (wait CMF filter disabled OR wait CMF > 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND wait-entry window AND wait EMA crosses below open price AND (wait EMA filter disabled OR close < wait EMA) AND (wait CMF filter disabled OR wait CMF < 0))
Short: (datetime within backtest range AND ASAP entry window AND entry source < open price AND (ASAP EMA filter disabled OR close < ASAP EMA) AND (ASAP CMF filter disabled OR ASAP CMF < 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND ASAP entry window AND entry source > open price AND (ASAP EMA filter disabled OR close > ASAP EMA) AND (ASAP CMF filter disabled OR ASAP CMF > 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND wait-entry window AND wait EMA crosses below open price AND (wait EMA filter disabled OR close < wait EMA) AND (wait CMF filter disabled OR wait CMF < 0)) OR (datetime within backtest range AND wait-entry window AND wait EMA crosses above open price AND (wait EMA filter disabled OR close > wait EMA) AND (wait CMF filter disabled OR wait CMF > 0))
Exit: long, exit window has ended OR stop EMA crosses below open price OR stop loss is reached OR take profit is reached
Exit: short, exit window has ended OR stop EMA crosses above open price OR stop loss is reached OR take profit is reached
