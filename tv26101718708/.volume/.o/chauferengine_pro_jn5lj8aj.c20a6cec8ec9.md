Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low during the initial session period; use after it is complete.
Calculation: Afternoon range = highest high and lowest low during the afternoon range window; use after it is complete.
Calculation: Fast, middle, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed by bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price for the current session.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; volume average = simple moving average of volume; volume surge = volume greater than its average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: RSI rising = current RSI greater than prior-bar RSI; bullish RSI alignment = RSI below its overbought threshold and rising; bearish RSI alignment = RSI above its oversold threshold and not rising.
Calculation: ADX = average directional index; ADX confirmation = ADX at or above its threshold.
Calculation: Previous-day high, low, and close = high, low, and close of the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high and lowest low during the premarket window.
Calculation: Weekly high/low = current still-forming weekly bar's high/low, except on Monday use the last completed weekly bar's high/low.
Calculation: Equal high/low pool = the higher/lower, respectively, of two consecutive confirmed pivot highs/lows whose difference is within a configurable tolerance based on ATR or close; retain until close crosses beyond the pool.
Long: During the active session range-breakout window, close crosses above the completed opening or afternoon range high after the prior close was at or below it, then a later green bar closes above the crossing bar's high (if required); apply configured VWAP, volume, RSI, and ADX confirmations, grade suppression, and breakout cooldown.
Short: During the active session range-breakout window, close crosses below the completed opening or afternoon range low after the prior close was at or above it, then a later red bar closes below the crossing bar's low (if required); apply configured VWAP, volume, RSI, and ADX confirmations, grade suppression, and breakout cooldown.
Long: During an active completed range, a green bar's low is within the configured band around or inside the range low, then a later green bar closes above that bar's high; allow only one such reversion until price closes below the range low, and suppress if a breakout signal fires on the confirmation bar.
Short: During an active completed range, a red bar's high is within the configured band around or inside the range high, then a later red bar closes below that bar's low; allow only one such reversion until price closes above the range high, and suppress if a breakout signal fires on the confirmation bar.
Long: Fast EMA > middle EMA and close > slow EMA, and (if required) middle EMA > slow EMA; low <= fast EMA arms a setup with the latest confirmed swing high as the break level; while the trend remains valid, close > that level and (if required) close > VWAP, and (if required) within session.
Short: Fast EMA < middle EMA and close < slow EMA, and (if required) middle EMA < slow EMA; high >= fast EMA arms a setup with the latest confirmed swing low as the break level; while the trend remains valid, close < that level and (if required) close < VWAP, and (if required) within session.
Long: A bar touches the VWAP proximity band while closing above its lower edge; a green reversal occurs within the confirmation scope, then a later green bar closes above the reversal close; apply configured volume and bullish RSI gates, session gate, and same-direction cooldown.
Short: A bar touches the VWAP proximity band while closing below its upper edge; a red reversal occurs within the confirmation scope, then a later red bar closes below the reversal close; apply configured volume and bearish RSI gates, session gate, and same-direction cooldown.
Long: Within the configured session gate, low < a selected key level (previous-day low, premarket low, completed opening/afternoon range low, weekly low, equal-low pool, or previous-day close) and min(open, close) > that level.
Short: Within the configured session gate, high > a selected key level (previous-day high, premarket high, completed opening/afternoon range high, weekly high, equal-high pool, or previous-day close) and max(open, close) < that level.
Long: On the selected trigger timeframe, RSI >= 50 plus its configured momentum band, MACD line is above its signal line and above zero, absolute MACD-line/ATR is at least the configured minimum, and the histogram expands in its positive direction; the configured number of enabled timeframes must have matching bullish RSI/MACD-signal bias, and bullish alignment must outnumber bearish alignment.
Short: On the selected trigger timeframe, RSI <= 50 minus its configured momentum band, MACD line is below its signal line and below zero, absolute MACD-line/ATR is at least the configured minimum, and the histogram expands in its negative direction; the configured number of enabled timeframes must have matching bearish RSI/MACD-signal bias, and bearish alignment must outnumber bullish alignment.
