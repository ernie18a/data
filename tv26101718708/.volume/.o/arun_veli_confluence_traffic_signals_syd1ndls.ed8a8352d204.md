Calculation: EMA ribbon = fast EMA, intermediate EMA, and slow EMA of close, with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: Gaussian midline = n-stage recursive Gaussian filter of HLC3; β = (1 − cos(4π/period)) / (1.414^(2/n) − 1), α = −β + √(β² + 2β); each stage = α × input + (1 − α) × prior stage
Calculation: Session VWAP bands = session-anchored volume-weighted average of HLC3 and its volume-weighted standard-deviation bands
Calculation: Opening range = high and low during the first configured N minutes of regular trading hours, reset each session
Long: On a confirmed bar during the configured allowed session, after the signal cooldown, at least the configured minimum of these seven conditions are true: fast EMA > intermediate EMA > slow EMA; close > session VWAP; close > Gaussian midline and Gaussian midline > its prior value; MACD histogram > 0 and > its prior value; RSI > 50 and < its overbought threshold; volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average; close crosses above fast EMA or session VWAP, or closes above opening-range high after prior close was at or below it. OR lower-band rejection: low ≤ lower 2-standard-deviation VWAP band, RSI ≤ its oversold threshold, and close > open; subject to the same session, cooldown, and confirmed-bar gates, and only if no scored long or short signal fires on that bar
Short: On a confirmed bar during the configured allowed session, after the signal cooldown, at least the configured minimum of these seven conditions are true: fast EMA < intermediate EMA < slow EMA; close < session VWAP; close < Gaussian midline and Gaussian midline ≤ its prior value; MACD histogram < 0 and < its prior value; RSI < 50 and > its oversold threshold; volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average; close crosses below fast EMA or session VWAP, or closes below opening-range low after prior close was at or above it. OR upper-band rejection: high ≥ upper 2-standard-deviation VWAP band, RSI ≥ its overbought threshold, and close < open; subject to the same session, cooldown, and confirmed-bar gates, and only if no scored long or short signal fires on that bar
