Calculation: Basis = EMA of hlc3, smoothed by a second EMA.
Calculation: Smoothed ATR = ATR, smoothed by an EMA using the same smoothing parameter as the basis.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish candidate trails = basis minus or plus smoothed ATR times trail distance. Bullish trail = max(current bullish candidate, prior bullish trail), reset to current candidate when close is below the prior bullish trail; bearish trail = min(current bearish candidate, prior bearish trail), reset to current candidate when close is above the prior bearish trail. Initialize each trail to its candidate.
Calculation: Bull score = count of true conditions: faster EMA(close) > slower EMA(close); RSI > upper neutral threshold; MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0; stochastic %K > %D and %K > lower neutral threshold; CCI(hlc3) > 0; +DI > -DI and ADX >= minimum; ROC > 0; MFI > upper neutral threshold; OBV > its EMA; close > session VWAP. Bear score = count of the mirrored conditions, using the lower neutral threshold for RSI and MFI, the upper neutral threshold for stochastic %K, and reversing each directional comparison. Use a shorter EMA for the faster EMA and a longer EMA for the slower EMA.
Calculation: Trend state starts bullish when close >= basis and bearish otherwise; it changes only on a qualifying long or short signal. Signal direction starts unset; only alternate signals are allowed.
Calculation: On each entry signal, set entry price to that bar's close and lock that bar's smoothed ATR. Long take-profit levels are entry price plus locked ATR times each of three take-profit distances; short levels are entry price minus locked ATR times those distances. Each level is checked once per trade, beginning on the bar after entry; reset the levels and hit flags on the next opposite signal.
Long: Confirmed bar; trend state is bearish; close > bearish trail and basis; bull score >= required confirmations; and the previous signal direction is not long.
Short: Confirmed bar; trend state is bullish; close < bullish trail and basis; bear score >= required confirmations; and the previous signal direction is not short.
Exit: long, a bearish signal occurs OR high >= any not-yet-hit long take-profit level.
Exit: short, a bullish signal occurs OR low <= any not-yet-hit short take-profit level.
