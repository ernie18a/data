Calculation: Composite score = +1 for each condition that is true, otherwise −1, summed across: MACD line > MACD signal line; stochastic %K > %D; momentum > 0; RSI > 50; Vortex positive > Vortex negative; +DI > −DI; MFI > 50; Fisher transform > its signal line.
Long: Composite score ≥ long-entry threshold AND RSI < long RSI limit AND position is flat or short
Short: Composite score ≤ short-entry threshold AND RSI > short RSI limit AND position is flat or long
Exit: Long, composite score ≤ long-exit threshold OR PSAR > close
Exit: Short, composite score ≥ short-exit threshold OR PSAR < close
