Calculation: Composite pressure = smooth an oscillator formed by blending normalized weighted candle pressure with smoothed volatility-channel position; candle pressure combines normalized candle body, close location within the bar, normalized range expansion, and optionally normalized volume impulse.
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of composite pressure using a longer smoothing length than the composite pressure's final smoothing length.
Calculation: Pressure regime = bullish when composite pressure exceeds the upper threshold plus a hysteresis buffer; bearish when it falls below the lower threshold minus the buffer. From bullish, switch to neutral below the upper threshold minus the buffer; from bearish, switch to neutral above the lower threshold plus the buffer; otherwise retain the prior regime.
Long: Pressure regime is bullish OR composite pressure > signal line OR composite pressure crosses above signal line OR (Pressure regime is bullish AND composite pressure > upper extreme threshold AND absolute change in composite pressure > expansion threshold) OR (at a confirmed pivot low, price makes a lower low and composite pressure a higher low than at the prior pivot low, OR price makes a higher low and composite pressure a lower low than at the prior pivot low; pivot spacing is within the configured minimum and maximum).
Short: Pressure regime is bearish OR composite pressure < signal line OR composite pressure crosses below signal line OR (Pressure regime is bearish AND composite pressure < lower extreme threshold AND absolute change in composite pressure > expansion threshold) OR (at a confirmed pivot high, price makes a higher high and composite pressure a lower high than at the prior pivot high, OR price makes a lower high and composite pressure a higher high than at the prior pivot high; pivot spacing is within the configured minimum and maximum).
