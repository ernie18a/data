Calculation: Bearish imbalance bar = close < open AND candle body ratio ≥ strong-body threshold AND volume ≥ volume moving average × impulse multiplier
Calculation: Bullish absorption bar = volume ≥ volume moving average × absorption-volume multiplier AND candle body ratio ≤ absorption-body limit AND close ≥ open
Calculation: Bearish absorption bar = volume ≥ volume moving average × absorption-volume multiplier AND candle body ratio ≤ absorption-body limit AND close ≤ open
Calculation: Bullish structure = the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows are each higher than their predecessors; a pivot uses equal left and right swing lookbacks
Calculation: Bearish structure = the last two confirmed pivot highs and last two confirmed pivot lows are each lower than their predecessors; a pivot uses equal left and right swing lookbacks
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish filter = current higher-timeframe close > its 50-period EMA; use the bar still forming
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish filter = current higher-timeframe close < its 50-period EMA; use the bar still forming
Calculation: Bullish level = the lower of the preceding sweep-lookback-bar low and the previous completed daily low
Calculation: Bearish level = the higher of the preceding sweep-lookback-bar high and the previous completed daily high
Calculation: Bullish break level = the highest high of the preceding break-range bars; bearish break level = the lowest low of those bars
