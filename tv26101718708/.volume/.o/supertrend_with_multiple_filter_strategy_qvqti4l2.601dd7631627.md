Long: within configured backtest date range AND long-only mode is off AND no long signal has occurred since the last short signal AND (EMA-cross filter is off OR short-period EMA crosses above long-period EMA) AND (TRAMA filter is off OR close > TRAMA) AND (each enabled Supertrend filter has upward direction) AND (EMA-200 filter is off OR close and prior close > EMA-200) AND (stochastic filter is off OR stochastic K crosses above oversold with prior K below oversold) AND (MACD filter is off OR MACD line > signal line AND short-period SMA crosses above long-period SMA) AND (MFI filter is off OR MFI crosses above its EMA signal) AND (CCI-channel filter is off OR close crosses above upper CCI channel) AND (VWAP filter is off OR close > anchored VWAP) AND (Chaikin money flow filter is off OR Chaikin money flow > 0) AND (Ichimoku filter is off OR close > both cloud spans) AND (candlestick-pattern filter is off OR hammer OR inverted hammer OR white marubozu pattern) AND (support/resistance filter is off OR prior and current close > latest confirmed pivot high) AND (MavilimW filter is off OR MavilimW crosses above its prior value) AND (inverse Fisher CCI filter is off OR inverse-Fisher-transformed weighted CCI > 0.5) AND (second/third Supertrend filters are off OR their respective directions are upward)
Short: within configured backtest date range AND long-only mode is off AND no short signal has occurred since the last long signal AND (EMA-cross filter is off OR short-period EMA crosses below long-period EMA) AND (TRAMA filter is off OR close < TRAMA) AND (each enabled Supertrend filter has downward direction) AND (EMA-200 filter is off OR close and prior close < EMA-200) AND (stochastic filter is off OR stochastic K crosses below overbought with prior K above overbought) AND (MACD filter is off OR MACD line > signal line AND short-period SMA crosses above long-period SMA) AND (MFI filter is off OR MFI crosses below its EMA signal) AND (CCI-channel filter is off OR close crosses below lower CCI channel) AND (VWAP filter is off OR close < anchored VWAP) AND (Chaikin money flow filter is off OR Chaikin money flow < 0) AND (Ichimoku filter is off OR close < both cloud spans) AND (candlestick-pattern filter is off OR shooting star OR hanging man OR black marubozu pattern) AND (support/resistance filter is off OR prior and current close < latest confirmed pivot low) AND (MavilimW filter is off OR MavilimW crosses below its prior value) AND (inverse Fisher CCI filter is off OR inverse-Fisher-transformed weighted CCI < -0.5) AND (second/third Supertrend filters are off OR their respective directions are downward)
Exit: long, close < entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss fraction) OR (indicator exits enabled AND long position AND all enabled ATR-band, Bollinger-band, and trailing-ATR exit conditions hold) OR (indicator exits disabled AND close > entry price × (1 + configured fixed-profit fraction) AND fixed-profit exit enabled) OR (percentage trailing-stop exit enabled AND low reaches the greatest prior/current close-based trailing stop)
Exit: short, close > entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss fraction) OR (indicator exits enabled AND short position AND all enabled ATR-band, Bollinger-band, and trailing-ATR exit conditions hold) OR close < entry price × (1 − configured fixed-profit fraction) OR (percentage trailing-stop exit enabled AND high reaches the least prior/current close-based trailing stop)
Calculation: CCI channel = Bollinger-style bands around the 20-bar SMA and standard deviation of the average of the custom CCI-derived optimal line and its EMA smoothings; upper/lower bands are basis ± one standard deviation
Calculation: TRAMA = adaptive moving average of close, with squared smoothing coefficient from the rolling average of whether the rolling highest high or lowest low changed
Calculation: Inverse-Fisher-transformed weighted CCI = (exp(2x) − 1)/(exp(2x) + 1), where x is 0.1 × CCI divided by 4, then weighted moving average smoothed
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset at the selected session or period anchor
Calculation: Chaikin money flow = rolling sum of volume-weighted close-location value divided by rolling volume sum
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud spans = midpoint of 52-bar high/low range shifted by 26 bars, and midpoint of conversion/base lines shifted by the base-line lookback
Calculation: Pivot high/low = latest confirmed pivot using the selected left/right bar lookbacks
Calculation: MavilimW = recursively weighted moving average of close, with each stage's lookback derived from the prior stage lengths
Calculation: ATR-band long/short exit levels = current open ± ATR × configured take-profit/stop-loss factors
Calculation: Bollinger exit bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± configured multiplier × standard deviation(close, lookback)
Calculation: Trailing ATR stop = highest value of high − multiplier × ATR over the configured lookbacks; initialize to close during the first 15 bars
Calculation: Percentage trailing stop = greatest close × (1 − configured fraction) since long entry, or least close × (1 + configured fraction) since short entry
