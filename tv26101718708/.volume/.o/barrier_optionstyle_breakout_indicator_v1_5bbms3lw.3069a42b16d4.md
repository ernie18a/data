Calculation: Upper/lower barrier = in range mode, highest high/lowest low over the lookback of completed bars before the current bar; in manual mode, the configured upper/lower level; in expected-move mode, anchor ± barrier multiplier × expected move, where anchor is current close, EMA, or VWAP and expected move = anchor × selected annualized volatility × √(horizon bars / bars per year). Selected annualized volatility is annualized standard deviation of log returns, annualized ATR divided by close, or 60% of the former plus 40% of the latter.
Calculation: Breakout buffer = ATR × configured multiplier, close × configured percentage, or minimum tick × configured tick count, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Retest tolerance = ATR × configured multiplier, active barrier × configured percentage, or minimum tick × configured tick count, according to the selected mode.
Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentage of selected volatility values in the preceding lookback that are below current selected volatility.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / moving average volume over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Quality filter = volatility percentile meets its configured minimum when the volatility filter is enabled, AND volume ratio meets its configured minimum when the volume filter is enabled; disabled filters pass.
Calculation: Active barrier and breakout age = on a quality-confirmed bullish breakout, store the upper barrier and breakout bar; on a quality-confirmed bearish breakout, store the lower barrier and breakout bar. Age is bars since that breakout. A new confirmed breakout in the opposite direction replaces the stored state. After both monitoring windows expire, clear the state if a retest or failure was confirmed.
Long: upper barrier touched by current high, or current close > upper barrier, or current close > upper barrier + breakout buffer, according to confirmation mode; AND quality filter passes; AND active breakout direction is not bullish.
Long: upper barrier touched by current high, or current close > upper barrier, or current close > upper barrier + breakout buffer, according to confirmation mode; AND quality filter fails.
Long: active breakout direction is bullish; age is 1 through the retest window; current low ≤ active barrier + retest tolerance; AND current close ≥ active barrier.
Short: lower barrier touched by current low, or current close < lower barrier, or current close < lower barrier − breakout buffer, according to confirmation mode; AND quality filter passes; AND active breakout direction is not bearish.
Short: lower barrier touched by current low, or current close < lower barrier, or current close < lower barrier − breakout buffer, according to confirmation mode; AND quality filter fails.
Short: active breakout direction is bearish; age is 1 through the retest window; current high ≥ active barrier − retest tolerance; AND current close ≤ active barrier.
Exit: long, active breakout direction is bullish; age is 1 through the failure window; AND current close < active barrier when failure requires a close back inside, otherwise current low < active barrier.
Exit: short, active breakout direction is bearish; age is 1 through the failure window; AND current close > active barrier when failure requires a close back inside, otherwise current high > active barrier.
