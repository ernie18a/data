Calculation: Dealing range = highest high and lowest low over the lookback bars.
Calculation: Volume equilibrium = midpoint of the 100 equal price bins spanning the dealing range where cumulative volume first reaches half of total volume; distribute each bar’s volume uniformly across the portion of its high-low range overlapping each bin. If total volume is zero, use the dealing-range midpoint.
Calculation: Volume deviation = volume-weighted standard deviation of the 100 bin midpoints using their allocated volumes; if total volume is zero, use one quarter of the dealing-range width.
Calculation: Outer walls = volume equilibrium ± (volume deviation × outer-wall multiplier).
Calculation: Extension = distance beyond the relevant outer wall divided by ATR.
Calculation: Signal volume filter = current volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiplier; pass when volume or its average is unavailable, or the multiplier is zero.
Long: (signal model allows traps AND close > lower outer wall AND ((low < lower outer wall AND prior close > prior lower outer wall AND prior consecutive lower-wall breach count ≤ trap window) OR (prior close < prior lower outer wall AND prior consecutive lower-wall breach count ≤ trap window)) AND extension ≤ maximum extension) OR (signal model allows reclaims AND close > volume equilibrium AND prior close ≤ prior volume equilibrium AND close − volume equilibrium > 0.2 × ATR); AND signal volume filter; AND, for a trap, its long-trap historical win rate is at least the minimum probability when its closed-trade sample count reaches the minimum samples; AND, for a reclaim, its long-reclaim historical win rate is at least the minimum probability when its closed-trade sample count reaches the minimum samples; AND, if balance alignment is enabled, volume equilibrium is below 55% of the dealing range; AND cooldown has elapsed; AND bar is confirmed.
Short: (signal model allows traps AND close < upper outer wall AND ((high > upper outer wall AND prior close < prior upper outer wall AND prior consecutive upper-wall breach count ≤ trap window) OR (prior close > prior upper outer wall AND prior consecutive upper-wall breach count ≤ trap window)) AND extension ≤ maximum extension) OR (signal model allows reclaims AND close < volume equilibrium AND prior close ≥ prior volume equilibrium AND volume equilibrium − close > 0.2 × ATR); AND signal volume filter; AND, for a trap, its short-trap historical win rate is at least the minimum probability when its closed-trade sample count reaches the minimum samples; AND, for a reclaim, its short-reclaim historical win rate is at least the minimum probability when its closed-trade sample count reaches the minimum samples; AND, if balance alignment is enabled, volume equilibrium is above 45% of the dealing range; AND cooldown has elapsed; AND bar is confirmed; AND no long signal on this bar. Historical trap rates are tracked separately by side and extension bucket (<0.25, 0.25–<0.5, 0.5–<1, ≥1 ATR beyond the wall); reclaim rates are tracked separately by side and whether equilibrium is on the trade’s aligned side (long below 50%, short above 50%). Pass the probability filter when the relevant sample is below minimum or no rate exists.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target; if both are touched on one bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target; if both are touched on one bar, exit at the stop.
Calculation: Long stop = one ATR buffer below the signal bar low or below the lower outer wall, according to stop model; if that is at or above signal close, use signal close − ATR.
Calculation: Short stop = one ATR buffer above the signal bar high or above the upper outer wall, according to stop model; if that is at or below signal close, use signal close + ATR.
Calculation: Long target = volume equilibrium when it is more than 0.2 ATR above signal close; otherwise, for the equilibrium target model, use signal close + (signal close − long stop) × reward:risk. For the half-sigma model, use the midpoint between the lower wall and equilibrium when more than 0.2 ATR above signal close; otherwise use the reward:risk target.
Calculation: Short target = volume equilibrium when it is more than 0.2 ATR below signal close; otherwise, for the equilibrium target model, use signal close − (short stop − signal close) × reward:risk. For the half-sigma model, use the midpoint between the upper wall and equilibrium when more than 0.2 ATR below signal close; otherwise use the reward:risk target.
