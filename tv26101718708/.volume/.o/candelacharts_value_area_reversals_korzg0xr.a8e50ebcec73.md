Calculation: Prior-session VAH and VAL = From the last completed profile-timeframe session, divide its high-low range into equal bins; allocate each bar's volume to bins in proportion to range overlap (assign zero-range bars to the bin containing the close). Starting at the highest-volume bin, expand the value area toward the adjacent bin with greater volume, including the upper bin on ties, until it contains the configured percentage of session volume; VAH and VAL are the upper and lower boundaries.
Long: low <= prior-session VAL AND close > open AND volume exceeds each of the previous N bullish bars' volumes (same-type mode) OR each of the previous N bars' volumes (all-type mode)
Short: high >= prior-session VAH AND close < open AND volume exceeds each of the previous N bearish bars' volumes (same-type mode) OR each of the previous N bars' volumes (all-type mode)
