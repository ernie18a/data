Calculation: MA vote(MA) = 1 if close > MA; -1 if close < MA; otherwise 0
Calculation: Broad direction = 1 if total of MA votes for base-length HMA, shorter-length THMA, efficiency-adaptive KAMA, base-length rolling VWAP, base-length ALMA, and Heikin-Ashi vote is at least the agreement threshold AND close > consensus hybrid; -1 if the vote total is at most the negative threshold AND close < consensus hybrid; otherwise 0. Heikin-Ashi vote is 1 if HA close > HA open AND close > HMA of HA midpoint; -1 if HA close < HA open AND close < that HMA; otherwise MA vote of that HMA. Consensus hybrid is the weighted blend of fast consensus (mean of HMA, THMA, and KAMA) and slow consensus (mean of rolling VWAP, ALMA, HMA of HA midpoint, and slow line); fast weight = clamp(0.25 + abs(vote total)/12 + abs(close - slow line)/ATR × 0.08, 0.20, 0.82). Slow line is the mean of slow-length ALMA, slow-length rolling VWAP, and slow-length KAMA. Slow length is at least the base length and approximately four times it; fast and efficiency lengths are approximately 0.60 times base length.
Calculation: Switch direction = 1 if the sum of six votes is at least the agreement threshold AND close > trend switch; -1 if the sum is at most the negative threshold AND close < trend switch; otherwise 0. Votes are: 1/-1/0 by whether base-length HMA is rising/falling/unchanged; 1/-1/0 by whether source is above/below/equal to efficiency-length KAMA; 1/-1/0 by whether HA close is above/below/equal to HA open; 1/-1/0 by whether source is above/below/equal to base-length rolling VWAP; 1/-1/0 by whether trend switch is rising/falling/unchanged; and 1/-1/0 by whether trigger EMA is above/below/equal to trend switch. Trigger EMA length is shorter than base length, approximately 0.25 times it.
Calculation: Trend switch = recursive average of raw switch, initialized to raw switch; each later bar equals prior trend switch + reactive alpha × (raw switch − prior trend switch). Raw switch = 0.28 × base-length HMA + 0.18 × 0.75-base-length EHMA + 0.24 × efficiency-length KAMA + 0.18 × base-length HMA of HA midpoint + 0.12 × base-length rolling VWAP. Reactive alpha = clamp(base alpha + abs(price pressure) × 0.12 + (trigger crosses prior trend switch ? cross-pull strength : 0), 0.03, 0.88). Base alpha = clamp(1 − trend memory + efficiency × 0.18, 0.03, 0.65); efficiency = clamp(abs(source − source[efficiency length]) / sum of absolute source changes over that length, 0, 1); price pressure = clamp((trigger EMA − prior trend switch) / ATR, −1, 1).
Calculation: Aligned direction = 1 when broad direction and switch direction are both 1; -1 when both are -1; otherwise 0.
Long: aligned direction = 1 AND previous bar's aligned direction != 1
Short: aligned direction = -1 AND previous bar's aligned direction != -1
