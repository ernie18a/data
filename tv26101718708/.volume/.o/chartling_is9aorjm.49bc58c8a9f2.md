Calculation: Flow line = selected moving average of the flow base, where each bar’s base starts at 2 × (previous bar’s open + close) − current high − low − open; if below the current low, replace it with its average with the low, then if above the current high, replace it with its average with the high.
Calculation: Wave upper and lower boundaries = apply a short-lookback selected SMA, EMA, or HMA separately to OHLC (use raw OHLC until available); calculate Heikin-Ashi close as their four-value average, open as the average of the prior Heikin-Ashi open and close (initially the smoothed open and close average), high as the maximum of smoothed high, Heikin-Ashi open, and close, and low as their minimum; apply a longer-lookback selected moving average to Heikin-Ashi high and low. Use the current chart timeframe when the configured trend timeframe is equal to or below it; otherwise use the last completed trend-timeframe bar.
Long: close >= Flow line OR low > Wave upper boundary
Short: close < Flow line OR high < Wave lower boundary
