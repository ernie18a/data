Calculation: Resistance shelf = highest high over the preceding shelf lookback, excluding the current bar; support shelf = lowest low over the same bars.
Calculation: Shelf flatness = (highest minus lowest of the preceding highs for resistance, or preceding lows for support) / resistance shelf or support shelf × 100 <= maximum shelf variation.
Calculation: Tight base = (highest high minus lowest low over the base lookback) / lowest low × 100 <= maximum base range.
Calculation: Higher lows = lowest low in the oldest third of the base < lowest low in its middle third <= lowest low in its most recent third.
Calculation: Lower highs = highest high in the oldest third of the base > highest high in its middle third >= highest high in its most recent third.
Calculation: VCP long score = count of: close > fast EMA (and, when strict trend is enabled, fast EMA > slow EMA); flat resistance shelf; tight base; higher lows; ATR / its simple average <= compression limit; Bollinger Band width / its simple average <= compression limit; volume / its simple average >= expansion threshold; RSI >= long threshold.
Calculation: VCP short score = count of: close < fast EMA (and, when strict trend is enabled, fast EMA < slow EMA); flat support shelf; tight base; lower highs; ATR / its simple average <= compression limit; Bollinger Band width / its simple average <= compression limit; volume / its simple average >= expansion threshold; RSI <= short threshold.
Calculation: Continuation long score = count of: close > fast EMA; flat continuation resistance shelf; tight continuation base; higher lows across thirds of the continuation base; volume / its simple average >= expansion threshold; RSI >= long threshold; either ATR / its simple average or Bollinger Band width / its simple average <= its compression limit.
Calculation: Continuation short score = count of: close < fast EMA; flat continuation support shelf; tight continuation base; lower highs across thirds of the continuation base; volume / its simple average >= expansion threshold; RSI <= short threshold; either ATR / its simple average or Bollinger Band width / its simple average <= its compression limit.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = initially the lower of (close minus ATR times the initial stop multiple) and current low; thereafter the higher of the prior stop and that initial-stop level. Raise it to the higher of itself and each confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Short trailing stop = initially the higher of (close plus ATR times the initial stop multiple) and current high; thereafter the lower of the prior stop and that initial-stop level. Lower it to the lower of itself and each confirmed swing high.
Long: Long trading is enabled AND (market trend filter disabled OR (close > slow trend EMA AND fast trend EMA > slow trend EMA)) AND cooldown has elapsed AND not in a short position AND (not in a long position OR continuation adds enabled) AND ((close > resistance shelf × (1 + breakout buffer / 100) AND close > open AND VCP long score >= minimum VCP score AND distance above fast EMA <= maximum extension) OR (continuation scanner enabled AND close > continuation resistance shelf × (1 + breakout buffer / 100) AND close > open AND continuation long score >= minimum continuation score AND distance above fast EMA <= maximum extension)). The market trend EMA pair is configurable as 50/200 or 20/50.
Short: Short trading is enabled AND (market trend filter disabled OR (close < slow trend EMA AND fast trend EMA < slow trend EMA)) AND cooldown has elapsed AND not in a long position AND (not in a short position OR continuation adds enabled) AND ((close < support shelf × (1 - breakout buffer / 100) AND close < open AND VCP short score >= minimum VCP score AND distance below fast EMA <= maximum extension) OR (continuation scanner enabled AND close < continuation support shelf × (1 - breakout buffer / 100) AND close < open AND continuation short score >= minimum continuation score AND distance below fast EMA <= maximum extension)). The market trend EMA pair is configurable as 50/200 or 20/50.
Exit: long, close < long trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled, otherwise low < long trailing stop.
Exit: short, close > short trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled, otherwise high > short trailing stop.
