Calculation: Sub-bar flow = for lower-timeframe bars contained in the evaluated bar, sum volume where close > open as buy volume and where close < open as sell volume; total volume includes all sub-bars. Use completed sub-bars.
Calculation: Buy-side and sell-side bar magnitudes = bar volume magnitude × (close − low)/(high − low) and × (high − close)/(high − low), respectively; when high = low, each fraction is 0.5.
Calculation: Bang and extreme thresholds = selected method applied to the preceding lookback of completed bars, excluding the evaluated bar: mean + multiplier × standard deviation, or median + multiplier × median absolute deviation × 1.4826, or the configured percentile. The extreme threshold uses a larger multiplier or percentile than the Bang threshold.
Calculation: Close position = 2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1; when high = low, 0.
Calculation: Normalized excess = amount above the active threshold divided by standard deviation for the mean/standard-deviation method, by median absolute deviation for the median method, or by the threshold for the percentile method.
