Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = EMAs of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on the selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar: bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA
Calculation: Mean = session VWAP of hlc3 on intraday bars when available; otherwise EMA of close
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR > its longer-lookback simple moving average × expansion threshold
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger bandwidth (2 × standard deviation of close / SMA of close) < its longer-lookback simple moving average × compression threshold
Calculation: Range regime = compression OR (ADX < range threshold AND absolute fast/slow EMA separation divided by ATR < minimum separation); directional regime = NOT range regime AND (ADX ≥ trend threshold OR EMA separation divided by ATR ≥ structure threshold OR ATR expansion)
Calculation: Confirmed major pivots = pivot highs and lows confirmed after the same number of bars to their right as to their left; classify each high as higher/lower than the preceding confirmed high, and each low as higher/lower than the preceding confirmed low
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure direction = the direction of the latest qualifying close break of the last confirmed major high/low; qualify a break only on a confirmed bar in a directional, non-range, non-compression regime
Calculation: Bull/bear score = sum of: trend alignment (15), higher-timeframe alignment (20), structure direction (20), momentum (0–15: 10 for RSI > 52 / < 48 and 5 for MACD histogram > / < 0), mean location (10), volume participation (0–10; when volume is unavailable or disabled, 5), and ADX/DMI alignment (10). Volume participation requires volume > average volume × threshold and close > / < prior close. ADX/DMI alignment requires ADX ≥ trend threshold and +DI > -DI / -DI > +DI
Long: confirmed bullish major structure band: a higher high with the latest low classified higher, OR a higher low with the latest high classified higher; average strength of the paired pivots ≥ minimum strength and pivot-to-pivot distance / ATR ≥ minimum leg size
Long: confirmed close > last confirmed major high + ATR × break buffer, with a directional, non-range, non-compression regime
Long: within the retest window after a bullish break, low is within the ATR tolerance and maximum penetration of the broken level, close > broken level, close > open, and regime is not range
Long: confirmed bar AND not range regime AND not compression AND directional regime AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > mean AND (higher-timeframe trend bullish when required) AND (structure direction bullish when required) AND (ADX/DMI bullish when required) AND (volume participation confirmed when required and usable) AND bull score ≥ minimum score AND bull score − bear score ≥ minimum advantage; signal only when neutral or when switching from short state
Short: confirmed bearish major structure band: a lower high with the latest low classified lower, OR a lower low with the latest high classified lower; average strength of the paired pivots ≥ minimum strength and pivot-to-pivot distance / ATR ≥ minimum leg size
Short: confirmed close < last confirmed major low − ATR × break buffer, with a directional, non-range, non-compression regime
Short: within the retest window after a bearish break, high is within the ATR tolerance and maximum penetration of the broken level, close < broken level, close < open, and regime is not range
Short: confirmed bar AND not range regime AND not compression AND directional regime AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < mean AND (higher-timeframe trend bearish when required) AND (structure direction bearish when required) AND (ADX/DMI bearish when required) AND (volume participation confirmed when required and usable) AND bear score ≥ minimum score AND bear score − bull score ≥ minimum advantage; signal only when neutral or when switching from long state
