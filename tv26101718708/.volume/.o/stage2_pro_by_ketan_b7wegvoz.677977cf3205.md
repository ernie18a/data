Calculation: Weekly high-breakout recency = number of weekly bars since the weekly high reached or exceeded the highest high of the preceding longer lookback of weekly bars; use the latest available weekly bar (last completed historically; forming in real time).
Long: latest available weekly close > its longer-lookback weekly simple moving average AND latest available weekly close > latest available monthly simple moving average AND weekly high-breakout recency <= its hold period AND latest available monthly RSI > its threshold; higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar historically and the forming bar in real time
Exit: long, latest available bar's high < latest available monthly simple moving average; use the last completed monthly bar historically and the forming bar in real time
