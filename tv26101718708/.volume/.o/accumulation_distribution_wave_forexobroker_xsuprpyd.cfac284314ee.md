Calculation: A/D line = cumulative sum of (((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low)) * volume; use 0 for the multiplier when high = low
Calculation: A/D wave = A/D line - EMA(A/D line)
Long: A/D wave > minimum wave threshold OR (close = lowest close over divergence lookback AND A/D line > lowest A/D line over divergence lookback) OR (A/D wave > minimum wave threshold AND close > reclaim EMA AND previous close <= previous reclaim EMA AND (divergence-only mode is off OR (close = lowest close over divergence lookback AND A/D line > lowest A/D line over divergence lookback)) AND (session filter is off OR bar is within configured session) AND position is not long AND bars since last signal >= configured cooldown)
Short: A/D wave < -minimum wave threshold OR (close = highest close over divergence lookback AND A/D line < highest A/D line over divergence lookback) OR (A/D wave < -minimum wave threshold AND close < reclaim EMA AND previous close >= previous reclaim EMA AND (divergence-only mode is off OR (close = highest close over divergence lookback AND A/D line < highest A/D line over divergence lookback)) AND (session filter is off OR bar is within configured session) AND position is not short AND bars since last signal >= configured cooldown)
