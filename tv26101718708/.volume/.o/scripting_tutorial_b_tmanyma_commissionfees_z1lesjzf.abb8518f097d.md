Long: (MA A > MA B AND previous MA A <= previous MA B AND both MAs visible AND this pair is enabled for trading) OR (MA A > MA C AND previous MA A <= previous MA C AND both MAs visible AND this pair is enabled for trading) OR (MA B > MA C AND previous MA B <= previous MA C AND both MAs visible AND this pair is enabled for trading)
