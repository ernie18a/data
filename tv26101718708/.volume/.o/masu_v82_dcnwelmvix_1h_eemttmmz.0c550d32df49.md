Calculation: Bollinger Bands = 20-bar simple moving average of close ± 2 standard deviations
Calculation: Donchian upper/lower = highest high / lowest low over the lookback; midpoint = their average
Calculation: NW ribbon = eight Gaussian-weighted averages of close over the lookback, with bandwidths increasing from shortest to longest; adaptive bandwidth scales by current ATR as a percentage of close relative to its 50-bar average ATR percentage
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume on up-close bars minus volume on down-close bars
Calculation: ELM probability = logistic output of an online weighted model using normalized RSI(14), 5-bar rate of change, distance from EMA(200), ADX, NW1 slope, NW ordering difference, and volume relative to its 20-bar average; update weights on confirmed bars using the prediction error from the configured lookahead target and weight decay
Calculation: VIX sentiment score = +1.0 extreme greed, +0.5 greed, 0 neutral, -0.5 fear, -1.0 extreme fear; add +0.5 when daily VIX rate of change over its lookback is below -5%, subtract 0.5 when above +5%, and subtract 0.5 when daily VIX exceeds 1.2 times its 20-day SMA
Calculation: Long/short confluence score = sum of configured weights for passing directional filters: 4-hour EMA(21)/EMA(55) and close alignment, NW ribbon ordering, order flow, smart-money filter, ADX above threshold, ELM probability vote, NW1 slope direction, Donchian midpoint side, daily SPX close above its 200-day EMA for longs (short filter always passes), and VIX directional vote; use calibrated ELM weight

Long: bullish liquidity sweep = low < prior lookback lowest low AND close > prior lookback lowest low AND close > open
Short: bearish break of structure = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low
Short: bearish liquidity sweep = high > prior lookback highest high AND close < prior lookback highest high AND close < open
