Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar’s high is the highest across equal lookbacks before and after it; confirmed after the lookback bars to its right
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar’s low is the lowest across equal lookbacks before and after it; confirmed after the lookback bars to its right
Calculation: Exponential moving average = exponential moving average of close
Long: Exponential moving average of close is higher than its value on the previous bar OR a swing low is confirmed OR close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high using the structure lookback
Short: Exponential moving average of close is lower than its value on the previous bar OR a swing high is confirmed OR close crosses below the most recently confirmed swing low using the structure lookback
