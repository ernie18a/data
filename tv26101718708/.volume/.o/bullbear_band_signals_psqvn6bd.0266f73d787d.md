Calculation: Selected source = Open, High, Low, Close, (High + Low) / 2, (High + Low + Close) / 3, or (Open + High + Low + Close) / 4, selected from the current chart-timeframe bar
Calculation: Upper band = higher of the selected-source SMA and EMA, calculated on current chart-timeframe bars; the EMA lookback is slightly longer than the SMA lookback
Calculation: Lower band = lower of the selected-source SMA and EMA, calculated on current chart-timeframe bars
Long: On a completed bar, selected source > upper band AND on the previous completed bar, selected source <= upper band
Short: On a completed bar, selected source < lower band AND on the previous completed bar, selected source >= lower band
