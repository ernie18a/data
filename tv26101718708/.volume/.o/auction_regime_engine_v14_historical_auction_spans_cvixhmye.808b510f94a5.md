Calculation: Frozen active value area = VAH, VAL, and POC of the selected volume cluster, rebuilt periodically from completed bars in the event lookback; each bar contributes volume across its footprint value area and an additional POC weight
Calculation: Relative volume = current footprint total volume / prior-bar moving average of footprint total volume; use a longer lookback above five-minute charts than on shorter charts
Calculation: Range expansion = current high-low range / ATR
Calculation: Bullish and bearish participation = weighted combination of normalized relative volume and, respectively, positive and negative footprint delta
Calculation: Acceptance score by direction = weighted combination of normalized current footprint volume outside the corresponding value boundary, whether current footprint POC is outside that boundary, and the fraction of recent closes outside it
Calculation: Directional event score = weighted combination of participation, range expansion, and acceptance score
Long: Event tape ready AND (state is balance or compression AND (upper-value rejection condition OR high > frozen VAH AND close > frozen VAH) OR state is probe down AND close >= frozen VAL OR state is accepted down AND close >= frozen VAL OR state is rotate up AND high > frozen VAH AND close > frozen VAH); upper-value rejection condition = high >= frozen VAH minus ATR tolerance AND close <= frozen VAH AND close < open AND bullish event score < acceptance threshold; accepted-down failure condition as stated is a long signal
Short: Event tape ready AND (state is balance or compression AND (lower-value rejection condition OR low < frozen VAL AND close < frozen VAL) OR state is probe up AND close <= frozen VAH OR state is accepted up AND close <= frozen VAH OR state is rotate down AND low < frozen VAL AND close < frozen VAL); lower-value rejection condition = low <= frozen VAL plus ATR tolerance AND close >= frozen VAL AND close > open AND bearish event score < acceptance threshold
