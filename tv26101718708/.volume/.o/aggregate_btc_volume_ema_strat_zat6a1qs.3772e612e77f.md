Calculation: Aggregated volume = sum of volume from the configured source markets for the current chart-timeframe bar
Long: Price crosses above the fast EMA AND price is above the slow EMA
Short: Price crosses below the fast EMA AND price is below the slow EMA
Exit: Long, aggregated volume > the selected fast- or slow-lookback volume SMA × volume multiplier
Exit: Short, aggregated volume > the selected fast- or slow-lookback volume SMA × volume multiplier
Exit: Long, price crosses below the slow EMA
Exit: Short, price crosses above the slow EMA
