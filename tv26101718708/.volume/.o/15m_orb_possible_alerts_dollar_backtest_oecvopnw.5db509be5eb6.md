Calculation: ORB high and low = high and low of the completed 15-minute bar from 9:30–9:45 a.m. Eastern
Calculation: Breakout levels = ORB high plus the selected tick buffer for long; ORB low minus the selected tick buffer for short
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × 100; use 0 when high = low
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of closes on the last completed bar of the selected higher timeframe; prior EMA = the same EMA one completed higher-timeframe bar earlier
Calculation: Stochastic K = smoothed stochastic of close, high, and low; Stochastic D = smoothed Stochastic K
Long: During the entry session after the ORB is ready, high ≥ ORB high + possible-alert buffer AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe close > completed higher-timeframe EMA AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe EMA > prior EMA)) OR close > long breakout level AND close[1] ≤ long breakout level AND close > open AND candle body percentage ≥ minimum AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe close > completed higher-timeframe EMA AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe EMA > prior EMA)) AND (two-close confirmation enabled AND the next bar closes above the long breakout level with the same VWAP and higher-timeframe filters OR retest enabled and, within the allowed bars after breakout, a later bar has low ≤ long breakout level AND close > long breakout level AND close > open AND the same filters AND (stochastic filter disabled OR Stochastic K > Stochastic D AND Stochastic K > 50) OR both confirmations disabled AND (stochastic filter disabled OR Stochastic K > Stochastic D AND Stochastic K > 50)) AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-day limit disabled OR no trade has opened today) AND entry risk from close to ORB low minus stop buffer ≥ one tick
Short: During the entry session after the ORB is ready, low ≤ ORB low − possible-alert buffer AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe close < completed higher-timeframe EMA AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe EMA < prior EMA)) OR close < short breakout level AND close[1] ≥ short breakout level AND close < open AND candle body percentage ≥ minimum AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe close < completed higher-timeframe EMA AND (EMA slope filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe EMA < prior EMA)) AND (two-close confirmation enabled AND the next bar closes below the short breakout level with the same VWAP and higher-timeframe filters OR retest enabled and, within the allowed bars after breakout, a later bar has high ≥ short breakout level AND close < short breakout level AND close < open AND the same filters AND (stochastic filter disabled OR Stochastic K < Stochastic D AND Stochastic K < 50) OR both confirmations disabled AND (stochastic filter disabled OR Stochastic K < Stochastic D AND Stochastic K < 50)) AND no active trade AND (one-trade-per-day limit disabled OR no trade has opened today) AND entry risk from close to ORB high plus stop buffer ≥ one tick
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ ORB low − stop buffer OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − (ORB low − stop buffer)) × reward-to-risk ratio OR the manage session ends
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ ORB high + stop buffer OR low ≤ entry price − ((ORB high + stop buffer) − entry price) × reward-to-risk ratio OR the manage session ends
