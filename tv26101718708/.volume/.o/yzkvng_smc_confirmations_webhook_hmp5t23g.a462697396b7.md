Calculation: Bullish confirmation count = the number of these five conditions that are true: (1) bullish candle close is required or close > open AND close > previous close; (2) volume confirmation is not required or current volume > its 20-bar average × the volume multiplier; (3) momentum confirmation is not required or RSI is at least the bullish threshold and below 70; (4) price action is not required or the lower wick is at least the wick-ratio fraction of the candle range; (5) opposing pressure is not required or none of the previous opposing-pressure lookback bars is bearish with a body larger than 0.5 × that bar’s ATR. A disabled requirement counts as true.
Calculation: Bearish confirmation count = the number of these five conditions that are true: (1) bearish candle close is required or close < open AND close < previous close; (2) volume confirmation is not required or current volume > its 20-bar average × the volume multiplier; (3) momentum confirmation is not required or RSI is at most the bearish threshold and above 30; (4) price action is not required or the upper wick is at least the wick-ratio fraction of the candle range; (5) opposing pressure is not required or none of the previous opposing-pressure lookback bars is bullish with a body larger than 0.5 × that bar’s ATR. A disabled requirement counts as true.
Calculation: Bullish trend slope = current EMA minus the EMA from the slope lookback bars earlier.
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing low/high = the most recent pivot low/high confirmed using the swing lookback bars on each side.
Calculation: Market type = trending up when ADX ≥ its threshold AND bullish trend slope > 0.5 × ATR; trending down when ADX ≥ its threshold AND bullish trend slope < −0.5 × ATR; otherwise ranging when close is within the highest high and lowest low of the 50-bar range AND that range exceeds 2 × ATR; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Bull zone touch = low ≤ latest confirmed swing low + ATR × zone buffer AND low ≥ latest confirmed swing low − 0.5 × ATR.
Calculation: Bear zone touch = high ≥ latest confirmed swing high − ATR × zone buffer AND high ≤ latest confirmed swing high + 0.5 × ATR.
Calculation: Trigger candle = when required, the selected bullish/bearish close, engulfing, or pin-bar pattern; otherwise always true. A bullish/bearish close means close >/< open AND close >/< previous close. Engulfing requires opposite-color previous candle and current body that contains the previous body. Pin bar requires the direction-opposing wick > 2 × body and the other wick < 0.5 × body.
Long: A bullish zone touch has persisted for at least the configured wait bars, the trigger candle is bullish, and bullish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations, AND (market type is trending up OR bullish trend slope > 0); OR close ≤ the 50-bar lowest low + 0.5 × ATR, bullish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations, and market type is ranging.
Short: A bearish zone touch has persisted for at least the configured wait bars, the trigger candle is bearish, and bearish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations, AND (market type is trending down OR bullish trend slope < 0); OR close ≥ the 50-bar highest high − 0.5 × ATR, bearish confirmation count ≥ minimum confirmations, and market type is ranging.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop (latest confirmed swing low − ATR × stop multiplier for trend setup; 50-bar lowest low − ATR × stop multiplier for range setup) OR high ≥ target (entry reference + risk distance × reward/risk ratio).
Exit: short, high ≥ stop (latest confirmed swing high + ATR × stop multiplier for trend setup; 50-bar highest high + ATR × stop multiplier for range setup) OR low ≤ target (entry reference − risk distance × reward/risk ratio).
