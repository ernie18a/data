Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is higher/lower than the corresponding values of N bars before and N bars after; available after the Nth following bar
Calculation: Watched pivot levels = confirmed highs/lows retained until swept or older than the configured maximum age; skip a new level if it is within the configured ATR distance of an existing level on the same side
Calculation: Candle body = abs(close − open); upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low
Calculation: Sweep midpoint = average of the sweep bar’s close and its extreme wick price (highest high for a high sweep, lowest low for a low sweep)
Calculation: Planned long stop = sweep low − ATR × configured stop multiplier; planned short stop = sweep high + ATR × configured stop multiplier; risk is the distance from signal-bar close to the planned stop; target is signal-bar close plus/minus risk × configured reward:risk ratio
Long: Flat AND a watched pivot low has low < level and close > level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its configured moving average × multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR lower wick/body ≥ configured ratio, or body = 0 and lower wick > 0) AND (confirmation disabled OR on the bar immediately after the sweep, close > sweep midpoint) AND (session filter disabled OR within configured session) AND longs enabled AND planned risk ≥ ATR × configured minimum-risk multiplier
Short: Flat AND a watched pivot high has high > level and close < level AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its configured moving average × multiplier) AND (wick filter disabled OR upper wick/body ≥ configured ratio, or body = 0 and upper wick > 0) AND (confirmation disabled OR on the bar immediately after the sweep, close < sweep midpoint) AND (session filter disabled OR within configured session) AND shorts enabled AND planned risk ≥ ATR × configured minimum-risk multiplier
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ target; if breakeven management is enabled, once high reaches the configured fraction of the entry-to-target distance, replace the stop with entry price
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ target; if breakeven management is enabled, once low reaches the configured fraction of the entry-to-target distance, replace the stop with entry price
