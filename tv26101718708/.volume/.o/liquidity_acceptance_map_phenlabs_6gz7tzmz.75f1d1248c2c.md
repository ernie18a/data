Calculation: Equal-high pool = average of the two most recent confirmed swing highs when their percentage difference is within the equal-level tolerance; detect pivots with balanced left/right windows, each half the swing lookback.
Calculation: Equal-low pool = average of the two most recent confirmed swing lows when their percentage difference is within the equal-level tolerance; detect pivots with balanced left/right windows, each half the swing lookback.
Calculation: Acceptance score = weighted sum of volume and ATR expansion scores. Volume expansion score = clamp(((volume / its lookback SMA - 1) / (volume expansion multiplier - 1)) × 100, 0, 100); ATR expansion score = clamp(((ATR / its lookback SMA - 1) / (ATR expansion multiplier - 1)) × 100, 0, 100). Use the same lookback for both averages.
Long: confirmed bar AND equal-low pool exists AND (close if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise low) <= equal-low pool × (1 − sweep margin %) AND acceptance score >= minimum score AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR HTF EMA is rising) AND (no prior bullish sweep pool exists OR current pool differs from the last bullish sweep pool by more than the equal-level tolerance).
Short: confirmed bar AND equal-high pool exists AND (close if close confirmation is enabled, otherwise high) >= equal-high pool × (1 + sweep margin %) AND acceptance score >= minimum score AND (HTF bias filter disabled OR HTF EMA is falling) AND (no prior bearish sweep pool exists OR current pool differs from the last bearish sweep pool by more than the equal-level tolerance).
