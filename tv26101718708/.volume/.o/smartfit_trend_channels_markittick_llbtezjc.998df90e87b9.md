Calculation: Filtered close = previous completed close passed through the selected adaptive filter (or used directly); regression and all signal comparisons use completed-bar data.
Calculation: Active regression channel = ordinary least-squares regression of filtered closes from the active anchor through the latest completed bar; calculate its Pearson correlation r and residual standard deviation σ. Upper/lower bands = regression endpoint ± selected deviation multiple × σ. Require the selected minimum bar count and |r| threshold for a valid breakout.
Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot = a high or low exceeding or undercutting every high or low, respectively, in a symmetric window; accept it only after bars on both sides have completed. After a breakout, anchor the next channel at the most recent qualifying opposite pivot (its regression has |r| at least the fit threshold and the pivot is after the current anchor); otherwise anchor at the breakout bar. If channel merging is enabled and the candidate channel's band gap from the truncated active channel is within the selected tolerance × current σ, retain the current anchor.
Long: On a confirmed bar, previous completed close > upper band AND active channel bar count >= minimum AND |r| >= fit threshold AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold) AND the same qualifying upper-band breakout was false at the preceding evaluation.
Short: On a confirmed bar, previous completed close < lower band AND active channel bar count >= minimum AND |r| >= fit threshold AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold) AND the same qualifying lower-band breakout was false at the preceding evaluation.
Exit: long, Short condition
Exit: short, Long condition
