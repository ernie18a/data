Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper Bollinger Band − lower Bollinger Band) / middle Bollinger Band
Long: previous bar’s Bollinger Band width < its longer-lookback average × squeeze threshold AND close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band AND volume > its shorter-lookback average AND position is flat
Short: previous bar’s Bollinger Band width < its longer-lookback average × squeeze threshold AND close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band AND volume > its shorter-lookback average AND position is flat
Exit: long, low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR at signal × stop multiplier OR high ≥ signal-bar close + ATR at signal × target multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ signal-bar close + ATR at signal × stop multiplier OR low ≤ signal-bar close − ATR at signal × target multiplier
