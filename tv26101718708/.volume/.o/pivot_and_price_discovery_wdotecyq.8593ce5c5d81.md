Calculation: Regression channel = linear regression of OHLC4 over lookback L; center is its fitted value at the current bar, width is the root mean square of the L residuals times deviation D; upper/lower bands = center ± width
Calculation: Skewness = adjusted sample third moment over L bars, using the OHLC4 volume-weighted moving average as the mean; standard deviation uses the sample second moment
Calculation: Excess kurtosis = adjusted sample fourth moment over L bars, using the OHLC4 volume-weighted moving average as the mean and sample standard deviation
Calculation: Skew threshold = 2.0 when L>300, otherwise 3.29 times skew standard error when L>50, otherwise 1.96 times skew standard error; skew standard error = sqrt(6L(L−1)/((L−2)(L+1)(L+3)))
Calculation: Kurtosis threshold = 10.0 when L>300, otherwise 3.29 times kurtosis standard error when L>50, otherwise 1.96 times kurtosis standard error; kurtosis standard error = 2 × skew standard error × sqrt((L²−1)/((L−3)(L+5)))
Calculation: Inside-range candle = previous high ≥ current high AND previous low ≤ current low
Calculation: Short-lookback rising/falling = OHLC4 rises above/falls below the preceding values over the shorter lookback L/5
Long: NOT inside-range candle AND close > open AND ((skewness > 2 × skew threshold AND excess kurtosis > kurtosis threshold) OR short-lookback rising OR (close > previous close AND OHLC4 > previous OHLC4 AND close > (previous close + previous open)/2 AND current hl2 > hl2 two bars ago))
Long: (NOT inside-range candle AND close > lower regression band AND previous hl2 < lower regression band) OR (open > previous close AND low > previous low AND previous close < close two bars ago AND NOT inside-range candle AND abs(skewness) < skew threshold AND excess kurtosis > −kurtosis threshold)
Short: NOT inside-range candle AND close < open AND ((skewness < 2 × skew threshold AND excess kurtosis > kurtosis threshold) OR short-lookback falling OR (close < previous close AND OHLC4 < previous OHLC4 AND close < (previous close + previous open)/2 AND current hl2 < hl2 two bars ago))
Short: (NOT inside-range candle AND close < upper regression band AND previous hl2 > upper regression band) OR (open < previous close AND high < previous high AND previous close > close two bars ago AND NOT inside-range candle AND abs(skewness) < skew threshold AND excess kurtosis > −kurtosis threshold)
