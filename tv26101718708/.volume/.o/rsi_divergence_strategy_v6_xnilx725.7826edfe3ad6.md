Calculation: RSI = Wilder Relative Strength Index of the selected source over its lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot low/high = an RSI low/high with the specified left and right lookbacks; its pivot price is the price low/high on that pivot bar. Compare each new confirmed pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = confirmed RSI pivot lows whose bars are within the specified minimum and maximum distance, with either a lower price low and higher RSI low, or a higher price low and lower RSI low.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = confirmed RSI pivot highs whose bars are within the specified minimum and maximum distance, with either a higher price high and lower RSI high, or a lower price high and higher RSI high.
Calculation: Divergence strength = absolute percentage change between the paired price pivots × absolute RSI change between the paired RSI pivots × (shorter-lookback volume SMA / longer-lookback volume SMA).
Long: bullish divergence strength ≥ minimum strength AND (minimum strength ≤ 0 OR divergence strength ≥ minimum strength) AND (the bullish divergence is eligible to be marked after the minimum spacing from the prior marked bullish divergence OR divergence entries are enabled AND ((entry delay is zero AND bullish divergence AND RSI ≤ long threshold) OR (entry delay is positive AND bars since bullish divergence was last true ≥ entry delay AND RSI ≤ long threshold)) OR divergence entries are disabled AND RSI ≤ long threshold); if both long and short entry conditions hold, RSI < 50
Short: bearish divergence strength ≥ minimum strength AND (minimum strength ≤ 0 OR divergence strength ≥ minimum strength) AND (the bearish divergence is eligible to be marked after the minimum spacing from the prior marked bearish divergence OR divergence entries are enabled AND ((entry delay is zero AND bearish divergence AND RSI ≥ short threshold) OR (entry delay is positive AND bars since bearish divergence was last true ≥ entry delay AND RSI ≥ short threshold)) OR divergence entries are disabled AND RSI ≥ short threshold); if both long and short entry conditions hold, RSI > 50
Exit: long, low ≤ the higher of entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) and, when trailing is active, highest high since entry × (1 − trailing percentage); trailing activates when high ≥ entry price × (1 + activation percentage)
Exit: short, high ≥ the lower of entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) and, when trailing is active, lowest low since entry × (1 + trailing percentage); trailing activates when low ≤ entry price × (1 − activation percentage)
