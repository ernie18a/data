Calculation: liquidity high/low = selected previous completed day or session high/low, or the high/low of the first N bars of the current trade session once those bars are complete
Calculation: sweep distance = max(minimum tick distance, ATR(14) × sweep multiplier)
Calculation: confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after the configured right-side bars; use the latest confirmed pivot
Calculation: bullish/bearish pattern = selected double bottom/top (two confirmed pivot lows/highs separated by at least the minimum gap and differing by no more than ATR × tolerance), tight-range breakout (preceding N-bar range width ≤ ATR × maximum width, then close above/below that range), or converging-pivot breakout (latest high lower and latest low higher than their respective predecessors, then close above latest high/below latest low)
Long: confirmed close crosses above EMA(13) AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA(13) > its prior-bar value) AND ((low breaches liquidity low by more than sweep distance and a close back above liquidity low occurs within the allowed bars, with the sweep still valid, AND bullish BOS is confirmed within the allowed bars after the EMA cross) OR (bullish BOS is confirmed within the allowed bars before the EMA cross) OR (bullish BOS and EMA cross occur on the same bar), with the configured BOS timing mode applied; AND (a valid bullish sweep OR no valid bullish sweep and a bullish pattern occurred within its validity bars); AND configured entry mode condition (breakout close, low ≤ EMA(13) and close > EMA(13), or low ≤ broken swing high and close > broken swing high); AND enabled directional EMA, stacked EMA, volume, ATR, and session filters; AND daily loss limit not reached; AND trade allowed; AND selected stop produces risk greater than one tick
Short: confirmed close crosses below EMA(13) AND (slope filter disabled OR EMA(13) < its prior-bar value) AND ((high breaches liquidity high by more than sweep distance and a close back below liquidity high occurs within the allowed bars, with the sweep still valid, AND bearish BOS is confirmed within the allowed bars after the EMA cross) OR (bearish BOS is confirmed within the allowed bars before the EMA cross) OR (bearish BOS and EMA cross occur on the same bar), with the configured BOS timing mode applied; AND (a valid bearish sweep OR no valid bearish sweep and a bearish pattern occurred within its validity bars); AND configured entry mode condition (breakout close, high ≥ EMA(13) and close < EMA(13), or high ≥ broken swing low and close < broken swing low); AND enabled directional EMA, stacked EMA, volume, ATR, and session filters; AND daily loss limit not reached; AND trade allowed; AND selected stop produces risk greater than one tick
Exit: long, close ≥ entry price + initial risk (partial TP1 at 1R); OR close ≤ effective stop (initial stop until TP1, then breakeven or configured trailing stop); OR after TP1, close ≥ configured 2R/3R target or opposite liquidity high
Exit: short, close ≤ entry price − initial risk (partial TP1 at 1R); OR close ≥ effective stop (initial stop until TP1, then breakeven or configured trailing stop); OR after TP1, close ≤ configured 2R/3R target or opposite liquidity low
