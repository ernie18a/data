Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest among itself and the pivot-length bars before and after it; use only after the bars after it have completed.
Calculation: Structure state = neutral initially; update to bullish when the long condition occurs and bearish when the short condition occurs.
Long: close > most recent confirmed swing high AND prior structure state is neutral or bearish
Short: close < most recent confirmed swing low AND prior structure state is neutral or bullish
