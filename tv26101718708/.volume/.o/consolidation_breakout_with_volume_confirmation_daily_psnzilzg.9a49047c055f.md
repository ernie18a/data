Calculation: Active consolidation zone = on each bar, select the longest lookback within the configured range whose highest high minus lowest low, divided by their midpoint, is no greater than the configured tightness threshold; zone top and bottom are that lookback’s highest high and lowest low, and zone average volume is its average volume. Establish or update a zone only when the cooldown since the last breakout has elapsed. Deactivate it after a breakout, or when no qualifying lookback has been found for more than the maximum lookback since its last update.
Long: Active consolidation zone AND close > zone top AND volume ≥ zone average volume × configured volume multiple
Short: Active consolidation zone AND close < zone bottom AND volume ≥ zone average volume × configured volume multiple
