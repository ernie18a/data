Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range.
Calculation: Eligible area = a previously registered lower value edge (VAL), upper value edge (VAH), or centre (POC/DPOC/earlier POC), with band = level ± max(0.15 × ATR, native footprint row width); use the prior completed profile/reference, never a developing level. A centre test requires the prior close outside the band; a lower-edge test requires the bar to touch the band from above, and an upper-edge test from below.
Calculation: Localized pressure = total volume > 1.8 × mean volume of the prior 20 valid bars AND band volume ≥ 0.20 × total volume AND opposing signed band delta ≥ 0.20 × band volume AND (close progress against pressure ≤ 0.35 × ATR OR the response close crosses the band by more than max(tick, 0.05 × ATR)); calculate band volume and delta by weighting footprint rows by their overlap with the band.
Calculation: Material divergence = price extends beyond the prior same-side pivot by at least max(native row width, 0.10 × ATR) while cumulative footprint delta moves in the opposite direction by at least the mean absolute delta of the prior 20 valid bars; require same uninterrupted valid-data run, pivot separation ≤ 120 bars, and prominence ≥ 0.75 × ATR. Use confirmed 8-left/8-right pivots; an unconfirmed current extreme may support only a current eligible area test.
Long: On a valid-data bar, a live eligible lower-edge or centre area test has localized pressure opposing selling OR material bullish divergence, and close > band upper + max(tick, 0.05 × ATR); the qualifying evidence may occur on this close or within the preceding three bars for the same area, provided the response deadline has not expired and no cancellation condition occurred.
Short: On a valid-data bar, a live eligible upper-edge or centre area test has localized pressure opposing buying OR material bearish divergence, and close < band lower − max(tick, 0.05 × ATR); the qualifying evidence may occur on this close or within the preceding three bars for the same area, provided the response deadline has not expired and no cancellation condition occurred.
