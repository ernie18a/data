Calculation: Selected moving average = the selected moving-average type applied to close at the specified lookback; fast, medium, and slow lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest. Scaled moving averages use the same type and lookback multiplied by a common scale greater than 1.
Calculation: Medium average direction = bullish when the medium average has risen for 3 consecutive bars; bearish when it has fallen for 3 consecutive bars; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI(close, RSI lookback), using the selected RSI smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Fast QQE trailing line = smoothed RSI trailing band. Set the band distance to the EMA of the EMA of the absolute one-bar change in smoothed RSI, each EMA using lookback 2 × RSI lookback − 1, multiplied by the QQE factor. The lower band is smoothed RSI minus this distance and the upper band is smoothed RSI plus it. Trail the lower band upward while both current and previous smoothed RSI exceed the previous lower band; otherwise reset it to the lower band. Trail the upper band downward while both current and previous smoothed RSI are below the previous upper band; otherwise reset it to the upper band. Use the lower band when the latest crossing of smoothed RSI and the prior upper band was upward; otherwise use the upper band.
Long: Scaled medium average direction changed from bearish to bullish OR selected QQE long signal occurred on the previous completed bar and its enabled filters pass. The selected QQE long signal is the first bar with smoothed RSI above 50 + threshold (XC), above the fast QQE trailing line (XQ), or at/above 50 (XZ); XQ also requires smoothed RSI outside the threshold channel when that filter is enabled. Enabled MA filters require either (scale = 1: close > medium average > slow average and fast average > medium average; scale > 1: medium average > scaled medium average, close > fast average > medium average) and/or bullish medium-average direction (when scale > 1, both medium-average directions bullish and close > medium average).
Short: Scaled medium average direction changed from bullish to bearish OR selected QQE short signal occurred on the previous completed bar and its enabled filters pass. The selected QQE short signal is the first bar with smoothed RSI below 50 − threshold (XC), below the fast QQE trailing line (XQ), or below 50 (XZ); XQ also requires smoothed RSI outside the threshold channel when that filter is enabled. Enabled MA filters require either (scale = 1: close < medium average < slow average and fast average < medium average; scale > 1: medium average < scaled medium average, close < fast average < medium average) and/or bearish medium-average direction (when scale > 1, both medium-average directions bearish and close < medium average).
Exit: Long, close crosses the stop level at entry price × (1 − stop percentage) OR the long take-profit condition occurs on either of its first two occurrences while long: slow average > scaled fast average, slow average > scaled slow average, and medium-average direction changes from bullish to bearish; OR the selected QQE short close signal occurs. The close signal is the first bar with smoothed RSI below 50 − threshold (XC), below the fast QQE trailing line (XQ), or below 50 (XZ); when close signal is XQ and entry signal is not XQ, any of these three short conditions triggers.
Exit: Short, close crosses the stop level at entry price × (1 + stop percentage) OR the short take-profit condition occurs on either of its first two occurrences while short: slow average < scaled fast average, slow average < scaled slow average, and medium-average direction changes from bearish to bullish; OR the selected QQE long close signal occurs. The close signal is the first bar with smoothed RSI above 50 + threshold (XC), above the fast QQE trailing line (XQ), or at/above 50 (XZ); when close signal is XQ and entry signal is not XQ, any of these three long conditions triggers.
