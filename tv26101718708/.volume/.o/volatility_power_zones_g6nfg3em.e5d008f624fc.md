Calculation: WVF = 100 × (highest high over lookback − close) / highest high over lookback; smooth it with an EMA using the same lookback.
Calculation: Fitted WVF line = lowest low over the WVF lookback + smoothed WVF × 1% of that lowest low.
Calculation: Upper/lower WVF zones = lowest low over the WVF lookback + (smoothed WVF × respective upper/lower multiplier) × 1% of that lowest low.
Calculation: Omega Score = round(100 × (sum of trend, RSI, WVF, and volume powers + 4) / 8). Trend power is +1 when the shorter-period EMA exceeds the longer-period EMA, otherwise −1. RSI power is +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its lower threshold, otherwise 0. WVF power is +1 when close exceeds the fitted WVF line, otherwise −1. Volume power is +1 when volume exceeds its moving average and close > open, −1 when volume exceeds its moving average and close ≤ open, otherwise 0.
Long: close > fitted WVF line OR fitted WVF line > its prior-bar value OR Omega Score ≥ bullish threshold OR a pivot low is confirmed OR Omega Score crosses from below the bullish threshold to at or above it OR close crosses above the upper WVF zone OR (Omega Score ≥ bullish threshold AND close > fitted WVF line)
Short: close ≤ fitted WVF line OR fitted WVF line ≤ its prior-bar value OR Omega Score ≤ bearish threshold OR a pivot high is confirmed OR Omega Score crosses from above the bearish threshold to at or below it OR close crosses below the lower WVF zone OR (Omega Score ≤ bearish threshold AND close < fitted WVF line)
