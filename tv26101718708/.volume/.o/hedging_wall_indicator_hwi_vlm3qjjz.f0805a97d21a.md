Calculation: Upper wall = nearest level above close among enabled positive manual options levels, volume-profile POC, selected high-volume nodes, and selected pivot clusters; profile and pivot levels use the current bar and prior lookback bars.
Calculation: Lower wall = nearest level at or below close among the same enabled levels.
Calculation: High-volume nodes = local peaks in a volume profile whose range spans the lookback high to low, with each bar’s volume (or 1 if unavailable) split evenly across touched rows; retain peaks above the average row volume times the threshold, selecting the strongest up to the configured maximum.
Calculation: Pivot clusters = means of stored confirmed pivot highs and lows grouped within a tolerance based on current ATR; retain groups meeting the minimum touch count, suppress nearby duplicate groups, and select up to the configured maximum.
Calculation: Flow = moving average of volume-weighted close-location value divided by moving average volume; close-location value is (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), or 0 for a zero-range bar. Use volume 1 when unavailable.
Calculation: Near upper/lower wall = distance from close to the nearest wall in that direction, divided by ATR; ATR is the average true range.
Long: close > previous bar's upper wall AND previous close <= previous bar's upper wall OR low <= previous bar's lower wall AND close > previous bar's lower wall OR near upper wall AND Flow > 0.15 OR NOT near upper wall AND near lower wall AND Flow >= -0.15
Short: close < previous bar's lower wall AND previous close >= previous bar's lower wall OR high >= previous bar's upper wall AND close < previous bar's upper wall OR near upper wall AND Flow <= 0.15 OR NOT near upper wall AND near lower wall AND Flow < -0.15
