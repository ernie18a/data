Calculation: Kernel = one-sided Nadaraya–Watson weighted average of close over the lookback, using the selected Gaussian, Epanechnikov, or tricube kernel; bandwidth is fixed base bandwidth or base bandwidth × (1 + ATR / close × scale factor).
Calculation: Bands = kernel ± max(EMA of rolling standard deviation of (close − kernel) × sigma multiplier, ATR × band-floor multiplier); ATR uses the configured lookback.
Calculation: State = bullish after the required consecutive closes above the upper band; bearish after the required consecutive closes below the lower band; otherwise retain the most recent state. Bullish flip = state becomes bullish; bearish flip = state becomes bearish.
Calculation: Momentum up = kernel > its previous-bar value.
Calculation: Dead zone = enabled and score ≥ threshold; score adds 1 for |kernel − kernel 10 bars ago| < ATR × slope threshold, 1 for the 20-bar highest-high minus lowest-low < ATR × range threshold, 2 for at least 3 state changes in 30 bars, and 1 for current volume < 0.8 × 20-bar average volume (only when that average is positive).
Calculation: Reversal at upper band = enabled, cooldown elapsed, high ≥ upper band, and doji (body ≤ 0.15 × range), upper pin (upper wick ≥ 2 × body and ≥ 0.55 × range), or bearish engulfing (red candle, body larger than prior body, and current open ≥ prior close and current close ≤ prior open). Lower-band reversal is the mirrored lower pin or bullish engulfing, or a doji, with low ≤ lower band.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = enabled, cooldown elapsed, close change over the slope window < −ATR × window × price threshold and kernel change over that window > ATR × window × kernel threshold. Bearish divergence uses the opposite inequalities.
Calculation: Blow-off = max(current range, prior-bar range) > ATR × blow-off multiplier. Structure low/high = lowest low / highest high over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar.
Long: bullish flip OR low-band reversal OR bullish divergence OR bullish kernel state on the 5m, 15m, 1h, or 4h screener bars (use the still-forming bar in real time) OR entry: state bullish and not already long, and an arm trigger occurred (bullish flip, dead zone cleared, or while flat, close crossed above kernel with momentum up); the arm remains valid through its wait window and is canceled when state ceases to be bullish or the window expires; session filter passes if enabled (within configured London or New York UTC session), not dead zone if enabled, not blow-off, minimum-entry spacing passes, extension (close − upper band) ≤ maximum-extension multiplier × band half-width, and, when enabled, momentum is up and close > open.
Short: bearish flip OR upper-band reversal OR bearish divergence OR bearish kernel state on the 5m, 15m, 1h, or 4h screener bars (use the still-forming bar in real time) OR entry: state bearish and not already short, and an arm trigger occurred (bearish flip, dead zone cleared, or while flat, close crossed below kernel with momentum not up); the arm remains valid through its wait window and is canceled when state ceases to be bearish or the window expires; session filter passes if enabled (within configured London or New York UTC session), not dead zone if enabled, not blow-off, minimum-entry spacing passes, extension (lower band − close) ≤ maximum-extension multiplier × band half-width, and, when enabled, momentum is not up and close < open.
Exit: long, bearish flip OR upper-band reversal while open profit > 0 OR peak profit ≥ minimum peak and current profit ≤ peak profit × (1 − giveback percentage) OR close < structure low while open profit > 0.
Exit: short, bullish flip OR lower-band reversal while open profit > 0 OR peak profit ≥ minimum peak and current profit ≤ peak profit × (1 − giveback percentage) OR close > structure high while open profit > 0.
