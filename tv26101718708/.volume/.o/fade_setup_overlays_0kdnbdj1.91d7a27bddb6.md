Calculation: Swing extreme = highest high (short) or lowest low (long) over the configured lookback; extreme age = bars since its most recent occurrence
Calculation: Regime ADX = ADX(14) on the last completed bar of the configured higher timeframe (automatically one rung above the chart timeframe)
Short: Regime ADX >= unlock threshold AND ((extreme age <= impulse threshold AND NOT (high < previous high AND close < previous low)) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND off-extreme percentage <= armed-distance threshold AND NOT (high < previous high AND close < previous low)) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND high < previous high AND close < previous low) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND high < previous high AND close < previous low AND off-extreme percentage <= window-distance threshold))
Long: Regime ADX >= unlock threshold AND ((extreme age <= impulse threshold AND NOT (low > previous low AND close > previous high)) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND off-extreme percentage <= armed-distance threshold AND NOT (low > previous low AND close > previous high)) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND low > previous low AND close > previous high) OR (extreme age <= freshness threshold AND low > previous low AND close > previous high AND off-extreme percentage <= window-distance threshold))
Exit: Long position, previous bar was FADE ARMED or FADE WINDOW AND current bar is neither
Exit: Short position, previous bar was FADE ARMED or FADE WINDOW AND current bar is neither
