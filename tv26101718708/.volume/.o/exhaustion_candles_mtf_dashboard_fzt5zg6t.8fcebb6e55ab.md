Calculation: Average range = simple moving average of high minus low over the lookback period.
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over the same lookback period.
Calculation: Body reference = absolute close minus open; when zero, use the instrument’s minimum price increment.
Short: On the chart bar, or a dashboard timeframe bar, high minus low > average range × range-spike multiplier AND upper wick > body reference × wick-to-body ratio AND upper wick > lower wick AND (volume spike is disabled OR volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier)
Long: On the chart bar, or a dashboard timeframe bar, high minus low > average range × range-spike multiplier AND lower wick > body reference × wick-to-body ratio AND lower wick > upper wick AND (volume spike is disabled OR volume > average volume × volume-spike multiplier)
Exit: Long, no exit condition
Exit: Short, no exit condition
