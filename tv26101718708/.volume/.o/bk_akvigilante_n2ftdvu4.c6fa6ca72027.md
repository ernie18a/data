Calculation: Smoothed CCI = the selected moving average of CCI (SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or volume-weighted moving average)
Calculation: CCI slope = one-bar change in CCI, optionally smoothed with an EMA
Calculation: Histogram = CCI − Smoothed CCI
