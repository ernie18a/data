Calculation: Equilibrium = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a rolling lookback of 2-hour bars.
Calculation: 2-hour trend = close above its 20-period EMA and close above open (bullish); close below its 20-period EMA and close below open (bearish).
Calculation: 4-hour and 8-hour trends = close above the 20-period EMA and above open (bullish), or close below the 20-period EMA and below open (bearish), using each timeframe’s current bar (forming live; last completed historically).
Calculation: Daily trend = daily close above its 20-period EMA (bullish) or below it (bearish), using the current daily bar (forming live; last completed historically).
Calculation: ATR = 14-period average true range on 2-hour bars.
Long: 2-hour trend is bullish AND close is below Equilibrium.
Short: 2-hour trend is bearish AND close is above Equilibrium.
Long: 4-hour trend is bullish AND 8-hour trend is bullish.
Short: 4-hour trend is bearish AND 8-hour trend is bearish.
Long: 2-hour candle body is greater than 1.5 × ATR AND close is above the prior bar’s high AND close is above open AND the prior bar closed at or below its open.
Short: 2-hour candle body is greater than 1.5 × ATR AND close is below the prior bar’s low AND close is below open AND the prior bar closed at or above its open.
