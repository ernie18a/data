Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high above the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides, or a low below the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Inverse H&S = five successive confirmed pivots low-high-low-high-low; the middle low is below both outer lows, and the outer lows are within the configured shoulder tolerance. Neckline = the higher of the two intervening highs. H&S = five successive confirmed pivots high-low-high-low-high; the middle high is above both outer highs, and the outer highs are within the configured shoulder tolerance. Neckline = the lower of the two intervening lows.
Calculation: Bullish PVM = smoothed correlation of close changes and volume changes, mapped from [-1,1] to [0,100], is above 50; smoothed normalized close rate-of-change is above 50; and smoothed normalized impulse is above 45. Bearish PVM uses the same trend and impulse conditions, with normalized close rate-of-change below 50. Impulse = normalized ATR expansion times relative volume; ATR expansion is ATR divided by its EMA, and relative volume is volume divided by its EMA. Normalize each series to [0,100] using its rolling minimum and maximum; use 50 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Cup window = configured lookback of current and prior bars. Cup depth = (highest high − lowest low) / ATR(14); cup bottom position is strictly between 25% and 75% of the window. Inverse cup uses the highest high position with the same bounds. Cup breakout level = highest high of the preceding window; inverse cup breakdown level = lowest low of the preceding window.
Long: Confirmed inverse H&S neckline breakout (current close > neckline AND previous close <= previous neckline) OR, when throwback is disabled, cup breakout (current close > cup breakout level AND previous close <= previous cup breakout level AND cup depth >= configured minimum AND cup bottom position is strictly between 25% and 75% of the window AND close > EMA(close, max(5, one quarter of cup lookback))) OR, when throwback is enabled, after an inverse H&S neckline breakout, within the configured maximum bars low <= neckline + ATR(14) × configured tolerance AND close > neckline; require Bullish PVM when PVM confirmation is enabled, and require the applicable pattern quality score to meet the configured minimum when quality filtering is enabled.
Short: Confirmed H&S neckline breakdown (current close < neckline AND previous close >= previous neckline) OR, when throwback is disabled, inverse cup breakdown (current close < inverse cup breakdown level AND previous close >= previous inverse cup breakdown level AND cup depth >= configured minimum AND cup top position is strictly between 25% and 75% of the window AND close < EMA(close, max(5, one quarter of cup lookback))) OR, when throwback is enabled, after an H&S neckline breakdown, within the configured maximum bars high >= neckline − ATR(14) × configured tolerance AND close < neckline; require Bearish PVM when PVM confirmation is enabled, and require the applicable pattern quality score to meet the configured minimum when quality filtering is enabled.
