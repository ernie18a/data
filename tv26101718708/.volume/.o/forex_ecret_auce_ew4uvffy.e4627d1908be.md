Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars before and after it; confirm only after the bars after it complete
Calculation: 8AM CRT range = highest high and lowest low from 8:00–9:00 New York time; use during the configured projection session
Calculation: 4H CRT range = high and low of the last completed 4H bar; P4H range = high and low of the completed 4H bar before it
Calculation: Daily CRT range = high and low of the last completed trading-day bar
Calculation: ORB range = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range interval
Calculation: Key level = an active Daily CRT, 8AM CRT, 4H CRT, P4H, or ORB high, low, or midpoint; near a key level means within the configured tolerance, measured either in pips or as a percentage of the previous completed daily ATR(14)
Calculation: Buying/selling pressure = volume weighted by the close's position within the bar range; buying pressure is volume × (close − low)/(high − low), selling pressure is volume − buying pressure
Calculation: Bullish pressure zone = low to the greater of open and the lower 30% of the bar range on a strong buying-pressure bar; bearish pressure zone = the lesser of open and the upper 30% of the bar range to high on a strong selling-pressure bar
Long: 8AM CRT sweep: low < 8AM CRT low AND close > 8AM CRT low during its projection session; OR last completed 4H CRT sweep: low < 4H CRT low AND close > 4H CRT low; OR Daily CRT sweep: low < previous completed trading-day low AND close > that low; OR ORB breakout: close > ORB high within the configured signal window after the opening range completes; OR bullish SFP: low < latest unswept confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low [AND the swing low is near a key level if key-level-only filtering is enabled] [AND within the configured reversal session if session-only filtering is enabled]; OR bullish MSS: after a bullish SFP, close > the confirmed swing high that preceded it within the configured bar window AND close has not closed below the SFP bar's low; OR strong buying pressure: buying pressure > average absolute bar pressure over the configured lookback × configured multiplier AND close > open AND volume > average volume over that lookback; OR price retests an active bullish pressure zone: low <= zone top AND prior bar low > zone top; OR both high and low of the last completed 4H bar exceed those of the preceding completed 4H bar; OR both high and low of the last completed trading-day bar exceed those of the preceding completed trading-day bar; OR latest confirmed swing high and swing low both exceed their respective preceding confirmed swings
Short: 8AM CRT sweep: high > 8AM CRT high AND close < 8AM CRT high during its projection session; OR last completed 4H CRT sweep: high > 4H CRT high AND close < 4H CRT high; OR Daily CRT sweep: high > previous completed trading-day high AND close < that high; OR ORB breakout: close < ORB low within the configured signal window after the opening range completes; OR bearish SFP: high > latest unswept confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high [AND the swing high is near a key level if key-level-only filtering is enabled] [AND within the configured reversal session if session-only filtering is enabled]; OR bearish MSS: after a bearish SFP, close < the confirmed swing low that preceded it within the configured bar window AND close has not closed above the SFP bar's high; OR strong selling pressure: selling pressure > average absolute bar pressure over the configured lookback × configured multiplier AND close < open AND volume > average volume over that lookback; OR price retests an active bearish pressure zone: high >= zone bottom AND prior bar high < zone bottom; OR both high and low of the last completed 4H bar are below those of the preceding completed 4H bar; OR both high and low of the last completed trading-day bar are below those of the preceding completed trading-day bar; OR latest confirmed swing high and swing low are both below their respective preceding confirmed swings
