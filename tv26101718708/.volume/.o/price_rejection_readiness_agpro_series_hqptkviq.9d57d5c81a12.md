Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; body = max(abs(close − open), minimum price tick); range = max(high − low, minimum price tick).
Calculation: Wick ratio = wick ÷ body; wick share = wick ÷ range; close location = (close − low) ÷ range.
Calculation: Reference high/low = highest high/lowest low among the prior longer lookback bars, excluding the current bar. Short reference high/low = highest high/lowest low among prior shorter lookback bars, excluding the current bar; the shorter lookback is one third of the longer lookback, with a minimum of five bars.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume ÷ the longer-lookback simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Long readiness score = rounded to nearest integer after clamping to 0–100 the sum of: lower-wick score (clamp(lower-wick ratio ÷ (minimum wick ratio × 2.20) × 30, 0, 30)); close score (clamp((close location − 0.45) ÷ 0.55 × 20, 0, 20)); reference score (18 if low is within 0.35 ATR above the longer-lookback reference low, else 10 if low is below the shorter-lookback reference low plus 0.30 ATR, else 4); volume score (clamp((volume ratio − 0.80) ÷ 1.20 × 15, 0, 15)); and follow-through score (17 if close > open, else 10 if close > prior close, else 4).
Calculation: Short readiness score = rounded to nearest integer after clamping to 0–100 the sum of: upper-wick score (clamp(upper-wick ratio ÷ (minimum wick ratio × 2.20) × 30, 0, 30)); close score (clamp((0.55 − close location) ÷ 0.55 × 20, 0, 20)); reference score (18 if high is within 0.35 ATR below the longer-lookback reference high, else 10 if high is above the shorter-lookback reference high minus 0.30 ATR, else 4); volume score (clamp((volume ratio − 0.80) ÷ 1.20 × 15, 0, 15)); and follow-through score (17 if close < open, else 10 if close < prior close, else 4). Minimum wick ratio, minimum wick share, minimum close location, minimum readiness score, and ATR/volume lookbacks are configurable; wick thresholds depend on sensitivity.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = lower-wick ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND lower-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close location ≥ minimum close location AND lower wick > upper wick. Bearish rejection = upper-wick ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND upper-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close location ≤ 1 − minimum close location AND upper wick > lower wick.
Calculation: Confirmation rail = rejection high + 0.05 ATR for bullish plans; rejection low − 0.05 ATR for bearish plans. Invalidation rail = rejection low − ATR × configured buffer for bullish plans; rejection high + ATR × configured buffer for bearish plans.
Long: Confirmed current bar AND bullish rejection AND long readiness score ≥ configured minimum readiness score AND readiness-label cooldown elapsed.
Short: Confirmed current bar AND bearish rejection AND short readiness score ≥ configured minimum readiness score AND readiness-label cooldown elapsed.
Long: Active bullish plan, later confirmed bar, not already confirmed or failed, and selected confirmation rule holds: close > confirmation rail (strict); or close > rejection body top AND close > open (balanced); or high ≥ confirmation rail AND close > rejection body top (fast).
Short: Active bearish plan, later confirmed bar, not already confirmed or failed, and selected confirmation rule holds: close < confirmation rail (strict); or close < rejection body bottom AND close < open (balanced); or low ≤ confirmation rail AND close < rejection body bottom (fast).
Exit: Long, confirmed bar after plan creation and close < bullish invalidation rail.
Exit: Short, confirmed bar after plan creation and close > bearish invalidation rail.
