Calculation: OU mean, reversion speed, half-life, and stationary standard deviation = fit a lag-1 linear regression to the selected price series over a lookback; derive the equilibrium mean, half-life, and stationary standard deviation from the regression.
Calculation: OU z-score = (current selected price − OU mean) / stationary standard deviation.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = rescaled-range estimate from log returns over the OU lookback.
Calculation: ADF statistic = t-statistic for the lagged price coefficient in a regression of price changes on lagged price over the OU lookback.
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of q-bar price changes divided by q times the variance of one-bar price changes, using q=4 and the OU lookback.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP deviation z-score = z-score of (close − VWAP of hlc3) over a lookback.
Calculation: Session-anchored VWAP deviation z-score = (close − volume-weighted hlc3 since session start) / the volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3 deviations from that VWAP since session start.
Calculation: Valid statistical setup = OU half-life is positive and below its maximum AND Hurst exponent is below its ceiling AND ADF statistic is below its cutoff AND variance ratio is below its cutoff AND the OU lookback contains no session gap.
Calculation: Statistical signal = −1 when a valid setup has OU z-score above its short-entry threshold; +1 when it is below the negative long-entry threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Microstructure signal = combine the mean-reversion directions indicated by the rolling VWAP deviation and session-anchored VWAP deviation z-scores; when both are available and disagree, use neither direction; otherwise use the available nonzero direction.
Calculation: Composite score = weighted sum of the statistical signal and microstructure signal, with each signal scaled by its absolute z-score relative to its threshold; apply the volatility-regime and other enabled multiplier adjustments, then clamp the multiplier.
Long: Valid statistical setup AND composite score is positive and crosses an enabled horizon threshold, with that horizon's variance-ratio and half-life requirements satisfied, AND expected distance from close to OU mean exceeds the horizon-adjusted transaction-cost threshold, AND outside the opening, closing, lunch, and session-warmup exclusion periods, AND not in the blocked high-trend regime.
Short: Valid statistical setup AND composite score is negative and its absolute value crosses an enabled horizon threshold, with that horizon's variance-ratio and half-life requirements satisfied, AND expected distance from close to OU mean exceeds the horizon-adjusted transaction-cost threshold, AND outside the opening, closing, lunch, and session-warmup exclusion periods, AND not in the blocked high-trend regime.
