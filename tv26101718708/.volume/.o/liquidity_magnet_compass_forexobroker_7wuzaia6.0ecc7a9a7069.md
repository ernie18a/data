Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that is higher/lower than the corresponding highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; the zone becomes available when the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Active liquidity zones = confirmed pivot highs and lows that have not been swept or expired; retain at most the configured number, removing the oldest when exceeded, and remove zones older than the configured age.
Long: An active unswept pivot-low zone is pierced (low < zone price) by at least the configured ATR fraction and, if rejection close is required, close > zone price; AND volume ≥ its configured-length SMA × configured multiplier; AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal; AND bar is confirmed; AND (no qualifying short sweep on this bar OR close < open).
Short: An active unswept pivot-high zone is pierced (high > zone price) by at least the configured ATR fraction and, if rejection close is required, close < zone price; AND volume ≥ its configured-length SMA × configured multiplier; AND the configured cooldown has elapsed since the prior signal; AND bar is confirmed; AND (no qualifying long sweep on this bar OR close ≥ open).
