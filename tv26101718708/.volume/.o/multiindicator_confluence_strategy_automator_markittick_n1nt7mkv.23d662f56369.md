Calculation: Hurst exponent = for the lookback window of completed closes, cumulatively sum each close minus the window SMA; divide the range of cumulative sums by the window standard deviation, then take the logarithm of that ratio divided by the logarithm of the window length
Calculation: Momentum inflation ratio = (absolute close change / ATR on the last completed bar) / (RMA of absolute close changes / RMA of ATR, both over a longer lookback and evaluated on the last completed bar)
Calculation: Lead-lag correlation = rolling Pearson correlation between the primary asset’s close on the last completed bar and the secondary asset’s close lagged by the configured number of bars, using the same timeframe
Calculation: Decayed directional score = count of aligned baseline, DMI, and CCI conditions multiplied by max(0, 1 − consecutive full-confluence bar count / maximum decay bars)
Long: On the last completed bar, close > HMA + 0.15 × ATR AND ADX > trend threshold AND +DI > −DI AND entry CCI > its positive threshold AND volume > volume RMA × volume multiplier AND (Hurst filter disabled OR Hurst exponent > trending threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum inflation ratio ≤ its threshold) AND (lead-lag filter disabled OR absolute lead-lag correlation < its threshold OR lead-lag correlation > 0) AND (decay filter disabled OR decayed bullish score ≥ minimum score) AND this full entry condition was false on the preceding bar AND the minimum bar gap since the prior signal has elapsed
Short: On the last completed bar, close < HMA − 0.15 × ATR AND ADX > trend threshold AND −DI > +DI AND entry CCI < − its positive threshold AND volume > volume RMA × volume multiplier AND (Hurst filter disabled OR Hurst exponent > trending threshold) AND (momentum filter disabled OR momentum inflation ratio ≤ its threshold) AND (lead-lag filter disabled OR absolute lead-lag correlation < its threshold OR lead-lag correlation < 0) AND (decay filter disabled OR decayed bearish score ≥ minimum score) AND this full entry condition was false on the preceding bar AND the minimum bar gap since the prior signal has elapsed
Exit: long, exit CCI with a shorter lookback than entry CCI crosses below zero on the last completed bar
Exit: short, exit CCI with a shorter lookback than entry CCI crosses above zero on the last completed bar
