Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple, closes above its open with close in the upper part of its range, closes above the prior open, opens below the prior close, and volume is at least the configured multiple of its moving average
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple, closes below its open with close in the lower part of its range, closes below the prior open, opens above the prior close, and volume is at least the configured multiple of its moving average
Calculation: Previous high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: High sweep = prior bar high is at or above the previous high and current close is below the previous high
Calculation: Low sweep = prior bar low is at or below the previous low and current close is above the previous low
Calculation: Long score = capped sum of: higher-timeframe bullish sweep bias, in-session, volume spike, correlated-symbol low sweep, positive relative return versus the correlated symbol over the configured lookback, valid bullish higher-timeframe fair value gap, bullish daily fair value gap, proximity to previous-day low, falling daily VIX, at least two consecutive low sweeps, proximity to long structure, twice bullish structure flip, and twice bullish fair-value-gap entry
Calculation: Short score = capped sum of: higher-timeframe bearish sweep bias, in-session, volume spike, correlated-symbol high sweep, negative relative return versus the correlated symbol over the configured lookback, valid bearish higher-timeframe fair value gap, bearish daily fair value gap, proximity to previous-day high, rising daily VIX, at least two consecutive high sweeps, proximity to short structure, twice bearish structure flip, and twice bearish fair-value-gap entry
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep bias = most recent high sweep sets bearish bias; most recent low sweep sets bullish bias; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fair value gap = bullish when the last completed bar's low exceeds the high from two completed bars earlier by at least the configured minimum gap; bearish when the last completed bar's high is below the low from two completed bars earlier by at least that gap
Calculation: Daily fair value gap = bullish when the prior completed daily bar's low exceeds the high from two completed daily bars earlier by at least the configured minimum gap; bearish when its high is below the low from two completed daily bars earlier by at least that gap
Calculation: Long structure proximity = close is within the configured ATR distance of previous-day low, current weekly open, current daily open, or the midpoint of the latest bullish higher-timeframe or daily fair value gap
Calculation: Short structure proximity = close is within the configured ATR distance of previous-day high, current weekly open, current daily open, or the midpoint of the latest bearish higher-timeframe or daily fair value gap
Calculation: Long structure flip = bullish engulfing and long structure proximity
Calculation: Short structure flip = bearish engulfing and short structure proximity
Calculation: Bullish fair-value-gap entry = a bullish current-timeframe gap exists from within the configured recent-bar window, close is inside that gap, and the current candle is bullish engulfing
Calculation: Bearish fair-value-gap entry = a bearish current-timeframe gap exists from within the configured recent-bar window, close is inside that gap, and the current candle is bearish engulfing
Calculation: Bullish box break = while in a range whose height is no greater than the configured ATR multiple, close crosses above the tracked range high and candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Bearish box break = while in a range whose height is no greater than the configured ATR multiple, close crosses below the tracked range low and candle body is at least the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Long BOS confirmation = after a low sweep, close exceeds the highest high of the configured prior-bar lookback captured at that sweep
Calculation: Short BOS confirmation = after a high sweep, close falls below the lowest low of the configured prior-bar lookback captured at that sweep
Calculation: Long stop = previous low minus configured ATR multiple, captured on a low sweep
Calculation: Short stop = previous high plus configured ATR multiple, captured on a high sweep
Long: bullish engulfing OR low sweep AND long score >= configured minimum score OR long BOS confirmation OR bullish fair-value-gap entry OR long structure flip OR bullish box break AND volume spike OR high sweep AND long score > short score AND long score >= configured contrarian threshold OR low sweep AND long score > short score AND (dominant score >= configured strong threshold OR (dominant score >= configured minimum verdict threshold AND score gap >= configured minimum gap)) AND NOT conflict OR long score >= configured maximum-alert threshold
Short: bearish engulfing OR high sweep AND short score >= configured minimum score OR short BOS confirmation OR bearish fair-value-gap entry OR short structure flip OR bearish box break AND volume spike OR low sweep AND short score > long score AND short score >= configured contrarian threshold OR high sweep AND short score > long score AND (dominant score >= configured strong threshold OR (dominant score >= configured minimum verdict threshold AND score gap >= configured minimum gap)) AND NOT conflict OR short score >= configured maximum-alert threshold
Exit: long, close < captured long stop
Exit: short, close > captured short stop
