Calculation: Adaptive flow line = Kaufman adaptive moving average of the price source: efficiency ratio is absolute source change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes; smoothing is the squared interpolation between fast and slow smoothing constants using that ratio. Optionally update only when the raw update exceeds ATR times a threshold.
Calculation: Stop line = selected stop mode based on the adaptive flow line: ratcheting ATR trailing or Supertrend bands; Chandelier rolling flow-line high/low offset by ATR; Donchian rolling flow-line high/low; or last confirmed flow-line pivot low/high offset by ATR, falling back to an ATR trailing distance until a pivot is available. Use the selected mode's current stop and trend state.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = price high/low pivot with left- and right-bar lookbacks; use the pivot only after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Structural trend and BOS counts = start with neutral trend and zero counts; a close above the latest confirmed swing high is bullish BOS when structural trend is bullish, otherwise bullish CHoCH when bearish BOS count meets the requirement, then set trend bullish and reset both counts. A close below the latest confirmed swing low is bearish BOS when structural trend is bearish, otherwise bearish CHoCH when bullish BOS count meets the requirement, then set trend bearish and reset both counts. Increment the matching BOS count on BOS.
Long: (adaptive flow trend changes from bearish to bullish AND price source > EMA 200 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in session) AND bars since prior bullish signal >= cooldown) OR (structure enabled AND close > latest confirmed swing high AND structural trend is bullish) OR (structure enabled AND close > latest confirmed swing high AND structural trend is not bullish AND bearish BOS count >= required count).
Short: (adaptive flow trend changes from bullish to bearish AND price source < EMA 200 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (session filter disabled OR bar is in session) AND bars since prior bearish signal >= cooldown) OR (structure enabled AND close < latest confirmed swing low AND structural trend is bearish) OR (structure enabled AND close < latest confirmed swing low AND structural trend is not bearish AND bullish BOS count >= required count).
