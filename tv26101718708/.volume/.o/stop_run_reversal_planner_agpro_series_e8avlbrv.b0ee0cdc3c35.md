Calculation: Range high/low = highest high and lowest low over the prior reference lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; EMA = exponential moving average; volume baseline = average volume over its lookback.
Calculation: Wick share = wick length / max(high − low, minimum tick); body share = |close − open| / max(high − low, minimum tick); close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Normalized score(x, weak, strong) = clamp(100 × (x − weak) / (strong − weak), 0, 100). Peak score(x, weak, ideal, excessive) = normalized score(x, weak, ideal) when x ≤ ideal, otherwise clamp(100 × (excessive − x) / (excessive − ideal), 0, 100).
Calculation: Bull/bear seed score = 0.56 × normalized score(respective lower/upper wick share, 0.75 × required wick share, 0.78) + 0.34 × peak score(respective sweep depth in ATR, minimum sweep depth, 0.42, 1.50) + 0.10 × normalized score(seed-bar volume / volume baseline, 0.85, 1.70).
Calculation: Reversal readiness score = 0.22 × (0.58 × seed wick score + 0.42 × seed penetration score) + 0.22 × close-back score + 0.22 × confirmation score + 0.12 × seed-bar volume score + 0.14 × target-room score + 0.08 × trend-turn score. Bull/bear close-back score is normalized score((close − swept low reference) / ATR, −0.04, 0.46) / normalized score((swept high reference − close) / ATR, −0.04, 0.46). Confirmation score = 0.38 × normalized score(body share, 0.60 × required body share, 0.72) + 0.28 × normalized score((close − prior high) / ATR for bull or (prior low − close) / ATR for bear, −0.20, 0.32) + 0.22 × directional-body score + 0.12 × normalized score(close location for bull or 1 − close location for bear, required close location − 0.12, 0.92); directional-body score is 86 when the candle body points in the reversal direction, otherwise 48. Target is the opposite range reference if it is beyond the close in the reversal direction, otherwise close ± ATR × target fallback; target-room score is normalized score(distance to target in ATR, 0.45, 2.35). Trend-turn score for bull = (48 if close > fast EMA else 28) + (26 if fast EMA rises from prior bar else 8) + (18 if fast EMA > slow EMA else 6); for bear use close < fast EMA, falling fast EMA, and fast EMA < slow EMA, respectively.
Long: On a completed bar, either a downside sweep below the prior range low meets the required ATR penetration and lower-wick share, is accepted as a pending seed, and its seed score ≥ watch threshold; OR a pending downside seed is within its confirmation window, close ≥ swept low reference + ATR × reclaim pad, close location ≥ required close location, body share ≥ 0.70 × required body share, readiness score ≥ minimum score, and the confirmed-plan cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a completed bar, either an upside sweep above the prior range high meets the required ATR penetration and upper-wick share, is accepted as a pending seed, and its seed score ≥ watch threshold; OR a pending upside seed is within its confirmation window, close ≤ swept high reference − ATR × reclaim pad, close location ≤ 1 − required close location, body share ≥ 0.70 × required body share, readiness score ≥ minimum score, and the confirmed-plan cooldown has elapsed.
