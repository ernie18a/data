Calculation: Composite = Hull-smoothed weighted sum of normalized Money Flow Index, daily-reset signed-volume cumulative delta percentile rank, and normalized z-score of On-Balance Volume rate of change; the shorter rate-of-change lookback is standardized against a longer rolling mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a bar whose composite value is the lowest/highest across the configured left and right pivot windows; use its low/high price and composite value. Compare each pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type, 5–60 bars earlier.
Calculation: Bull zone = the range from the lowest body price (min(open, close)) to the lowest low over the configured zone lookback, anchored at a confirmed bullish divergence pivot. Bear zone = the range from the highest body price (max(open, close)) to the highest high over that lookback, anchored at a confirmed bearish divergence pivot. A zone remains active until mitigated or expired.
Long: Confirmed regular bullish divergence (pivot low price lower than preceding pivot low and composite pivot value higher) OR confirmed hidden bullish divergence (pivot low price higher than preceding pivot low and composite pivot value lower) OR active bull zone overlaps the bar range (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) AND close > open OR at least 3 active bull zones AND confirmed regular bullish divergence OR composite > 0 for 5 consecutive confirmed bars. If FVG confluence is enabled, divergence-based conditions also require a bullish 3-bar gap within the configured lookback.
Short: Confirmed regular bearish divergence (pivot high price higher than preceding pivot high and composite pivot value lower) OR confirmed hidden bearish divergence (pivot high price lower than preceding pivot high and composite pivot value higher) OR active bear zone overlaps the bar range (high >= zone bottom AND low <= zone top) AND close < open OR at least 3 active bear zones AND confirmed regular bearish divergence OR composite < 0 for 5 consecutive confirmed bars. If FVG confluence is enabled, divergence-based conditions also require a bearish 3-bar gap within the configured lookback.
