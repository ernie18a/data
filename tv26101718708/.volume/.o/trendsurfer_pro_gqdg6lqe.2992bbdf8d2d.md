Calculation: Volume strength = round(5 × current volume / its moving average), bounded from 1 to 10; the moving-average lookback is shorter than the long-term price trend lookback.
Calculation: Price-change z-score = (current close change − moving average of close changes) / standard deviation of close changes.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = the high-low range of the lowest-low candle in the lookback before a bullish impulse; bearish order-block zone = the high-low range of the highest-high candle in the lookback before a bearish impulse.
Calculation: Swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is more extreme than the configured number of bars on both sides; use only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Sweep level = most recently confirmed swing high or swing low.
Calculation: Session high/low/midpoint = high, low, and their midpoint over the completed configured session.
Calculation: Bullish TP confirmation count = number of enabled bullish TP conditions met: RSI above its upper threshold, stochastic bearish cross while overbought, Supertrend bullish, CCI above its upper threshold, or close above an earlier close. Bearish count uses RSI below its lower threshold, stochastic bullish cross while oversold, Supertrend bearish, CCI below its lower threshold, or close below an earlier close.
Long: Close > EMA(750) AND close > EMA(25) AND smoothed stochastic > 50 AND the smoothed price filter turns upward AND close > open AND volume strength > 5 AND configured volume-strength filter passes; OR fast EMA crosses above the shorter fast EMA and RSI > 50 and close is above EMA(21), SMA(50), EMA(55), EMA(89), and EMA(750), and (smoothed stochastic > 52 OR close crosses above EMA(89) during an allowed time window OR bullish engulfing candle); OR bullish engulfing candle (current bullish body fully covers prior bearish body) AND close > its filter EMA; OR low < latest confirmed swing low and close > that level, or a close below that level within the configured recent-bar lookback is followed by close above it; OR bullish impulse has z-score above its threshold, close > open, and close exceeds the highest prior close in the impulse lookback; OR bullish order-block rejection: an active bullish zone is touched, close > open, close − low exceeds the configured fraction of zone height, and close is above the zone EMA; OR bullish Butterworth rejection: low touches the Butterworth line, the candle body is above it, close > open, and close is above its filter EMA; OR bullish VIDYA rejection: low touches VIDYA, the body is above it, close > open, and close is above its filter EMA; OR bullish rejection of an active Master Trend signal line: a later bar touches the line and opens and closes above it; OR first touch of an active Master Trend signal line while close > EMA(750); OR bullish session-high rejection after a close above the completed session high, with a later low touching that high and the candle body remaining above it; OR bullish session-midpoint rejection: low touches the midpoint and the body remains above it; OR bullish EMA(750) rejection: low touches EMA(750), the body remains above it, and close > open; OR Supertrend changes to bullish; OR close crosses into or out above an active bullish balanced-price-range zone; OR at least five bullish TP confirmations, more than bearish confirmations, and close meets the configured time, VWAP, and master-trend filters.
Short: Close < EMA(750) AND close < EMA(25) AND smoothed stochastic < 50 AND the smoothed price filter turns downward AND close < open AND volume strength > 5 AND configured volume-strength filter passes; OR fast EMA crosses below the shorter fast EMA and RSI < 50 and close is below EMA(21), SMA(50), EMA(55), EMA(89), and EMA(750), and (smoothed stochastic < 48 OR close crosses below EMA(89) during an allowed time window OR bearish engulfing candle); OR bearish engulfing candle (current bearish body fully covers prior bullish body) AND close < its filter EMA; OR high > latest confirmed swing high and close < that level, or a close above that level within the configured recent-bar lookback is followed by close below it; OR bearish impulse has z-score below the negative threshold, close < open, and close is below the lowest prior close in the impulse lookback; OR bearish order-block rejection: an active bearish zone is touched, close < open, high − close exceeds the configured fraction of zone height, and close is below the zone EMA; OR bearish Butterworth rejection: high touches the Butterworth line, the body is below it, close < open, and close is below its filter EMA; OR bearish VIDYA rejection: high touches VIDYA, the body is below it, close < open, and close is below its filter EMA; OR bearish rejection of an active Master Trend signal line: a later bar touches the line and opens and closes below it; OR first touch of an active Master Trend signal line while close < EMA(750); OR bearish session-low rejection after a close below the completed session low, with a later high touching that low and the candle body remaining below it; OR bearish session-midpoint rejection: high touches the midpoint and the body remains below it; OR bearish EMA(750) rejection: high touches EMA(750), the body remains below it, and close < open; OR Supertrend changes to bearish; OR close crosses into or out below an active bearish balanced-price-range zone; OR at least five bearish TP confirmations, more than bullish confirmations, and close meets the configured time, VWAP, and master-trend filters.
Exit: Long, the selected trade-management stop level is reached (low <= stop) OR its target level is reached (high >= target) OR a bearish TP signal fires.
Exit: Short, the selected trade-management stop level is reached (high >= stop) OR its target level is reached (low <= target) OR a bullish TP signal fires.
