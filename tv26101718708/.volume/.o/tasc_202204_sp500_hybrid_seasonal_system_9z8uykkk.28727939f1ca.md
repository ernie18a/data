Calculation: Implied volatility rise % = (current implied volatility index close / highest close over the lookback, excluding the current bar − 1) × 100; use the configured index timeframe and the last completed bar.
Calculation: ATR rise % = (current ATR / highest ATR over the same lookback, excluding the current bar − 1) × 100; calculate ATR from the backtest bars.
Calculation: VFI = EMA of the lookback sum of signed capped volume divided by prior-bar average volume. For each bar, compare the HLC3 change from the prior bar with a cutoff equal to close × a coefficient × the standard deviation of log HLC3 changes over a shorter lookback. Assign capped volume (the lesser of current volume and prior-bar average volume × a coefficient) when the change exceeds the cutoff, its negative when below the negative cutoff, or zero otherwise.
Calculation: VFI moving average = simple moving average of VFI, using a shorter lookback than VFI's volume-flow lookback.
Long: Month is October or later OR before the configured sell month, AND on the prior completed bar VFI is above its critical threshold AND (implied volatility rise % is below its threshold OR ATR rise % is below the ATR threshold, which is a multiple of the implied volatility threshold).
Short: Month equals the configured sell month OR on the prior completed bar implied volatility rise % is above twice its threshold OR VFI crosses its critical threshold and its moving average is lower than on the preceding bar.
Exit: Long, month equals the configured sell month OR on the prior completed bar implied volatility rise % is above twice its threshold OR VFI crosses its critical threshold and its moving average is lower than on the preceding bar.
