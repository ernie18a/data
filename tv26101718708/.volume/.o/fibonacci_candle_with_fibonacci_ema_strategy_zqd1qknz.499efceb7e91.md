Calculation: Fibonacci candle OHLC = for each field (open, high, low, close), the equal-weight average of that field on the current chart bar and bars 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, and 55 bars earlier
Calculation: Fibonacci candle EMA average = the equal-weight average of the EMAs of Fibonacci candle close, using lookbacks 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, and 55 chart bars
Calculation: Fibonacci volume average = the equal-weight average of volume on the current chart bar and bars 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, and 55 bars earlier
Calculation: Average stochastic = the mean of smoothed %K and %D, where %K is the smoothing-period simple moving average of 100 × (close − lowest low over the %K lookback) / (highest high over the %K lookback − lowest low over the %K lookback), and %D is the %D-lookback simple moving average of %K

Exit: long, Fibonacci candle open > Fibonacci candle close AND Fibonacci candle close < prior bar's Fibonacci candle close AND Fibonacci candle low < prior bar's Fibonacci candle low AND prior bar's Fibonacci candle close < Fibonacci candle close from two bars earlier AND prior bar's Fibonacci candle low < Fibonacci candle low from two bars earlier AND Fibonacci volume average crosses below its EMA AND Average stochastic < its threshold AND Fibonacci candle close < Fibonacci candle EMA average
