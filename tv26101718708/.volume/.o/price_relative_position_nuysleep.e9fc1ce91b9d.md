Calculation: Daily moving averages = SMA or EMA of daily closes, using the current daily bar while it is forming
Calculation: 52-week high = highest daily high over the 52-week lookback, including the current daily bar while it is forming
Calculation: 52-week low = lowest daily low over the 52-week lookback, including the current daily bar while it is forming
Calculation: Day high = highest high from the start of the current session through the current bar
Calculation: Day low = lowest low from the start of the current session through the current bar
Long: close >= daily moving average OR close >= 52-week high OR close >= 52-week low OR close >= day high OR close >= day low OR close >= VWAP
Short: close < daily moving average OR close < 52-week high OR close < 52-week low OR close < day high OR close < day low OR close < VWAP
