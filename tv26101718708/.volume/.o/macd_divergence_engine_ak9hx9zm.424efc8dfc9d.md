Calculation: Normalized MACD = (fast moving average of close − slower moving average of close) ÷ selected scale; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback. The scale is ATR, MACD standard deviation, close ÷ 100, or 1, according to the selected normalization.
Calculation: Signal line = selected moving average of Normalized MACD; Histogram = Normalized MACD − Signal line.
Calculation: Regular bullish candidate = confirmed pivot-low pair within the configured bar-gap range where the newer price low is lower and its Normalized MACD pivot is higher; regular bearish candidate = confirmed pivot-high pair where the newer price high is higher and its Normalized MACD pivot is lower.
Calculation: Hidden bullish candidate = confirmed pivot-low pair within the configured bar-gap range where the newer price low is higher and its Normalized MACD pivot is lower; hidden bearish candidate = confirmed pivot-high pair where the newer price high is lower and its Normalized MACD pivot is higher. Compare each confirmed pivot with up to the configured number of prior pivots; pivot confirmation uses the configured left and right bars.
Calculation: Zero-line eligibility = selected discipline: strict requires the entire Normalized MACD segment between pivots on the prescribed side of zero; soft requires both pivot values on that side; off imposes no condition. Regular bullish and hidden bearish require below zero; regular bearish and hidden bullish require above zero.
Calculation: Trend power = average of (absolute close change over the efficiency lookback ÷ sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback) and clamped ADX ÷ 45. Trend direction is up when +DI > −DI and close > structural EMA; down when +DI ≤ −DI and close ≤ structural EMA; otherwise transition. ADX and DI use the configured lengths; the structural EMA has a longer lookback than the efficiency lookback.
Calculation: Candidate score = 100 × weighted mean of ten scores: magnitude, extremity, spacing, regime fit, extension, volume, higher-timeframe bias, momentum shift, zero-line integrity, and bar structure. Each score is clamped to 0–1; weights are configurable.
Calculation: Magnitude score = square root of [clamp(abs(price-pivot change) ÷ (2 × ATR), 0, 1) × clamp(abs(MACD-pivot change) ÷ oscillator standard deviation, 0, 1)]. Use the configured normalization ATR length; oscillator standard deviation uses twice that lookback.
Calculation: Extremity score = regular: clamp(abs(newer MACD pivot) ÷ (1.5 × oscillator standard deviation), 0, 1); hidden: clamp(1 − extremity ÷ 1.2, 0, 1).
Calculation: Spacing score = clamp(1 − abs(pivot gap − midpoint of configured minimum and maximum gaps) ÷ that midpoint, 0, 1).
Calculation: Regime-fit score = hidden: with-trend clamp(1.2 × trend power, 0, 1), against-trend 0.05, transition 0.40 × trend power; regular: with-trend 0.85, against-trend 0.85 × (1 − trend power), transition 0.60. For bullish, up is with-trend; for bearish, down is with-trend.
Calculation: Extension score = let d = (close − structural EMA) ÷ ATR at the newer pivot, signed toward the trade (−d for bullish, d for bearish); regular = clamp(d ÷ 3, 0, 1), hidden = clamp((1 − d) ÷ 3, 0, 1).
Calculation: Volume score = compare 3-bar volume averages at the newer and older pivots; if either is nonpositive use 0.5, otherwise regular = clamp((1.4 − newer ÷ older) ÷ 0.8, 0, 1), hidden = clamp((newer ÷ older − 0.6) ÷ 0.8, 0, 1).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias score = use EMA MACD and its EMA signal on the selected higher timeframe; bullish bias is MACD > signal and MACD > 0, bearish bias is MACD < signal and MACD < 0, otherwise neutral. The selected higher-timeframe bar may still be forming. Score aligned = 1, neutral = 0.5, opposed = 0.1; if the filter is disabled use 0.5.
Calculation: Momentum-shift score = 0.6 if the histogram turns up for bullish or down for bearish on the confirmation bar, plus 0.4 if Normalized MACD is above its signal for bullish or below for bearish.
Calculation: Zero-line integrity score = 1 if the entire segment is on the prescribed side of zero, else 0.5 if both pivots are on that side, else 0.
Calculation: Bar-structure score = 0.6 × RSI score + 0.4 × rejection-wick score, at the newer pivot; RSI score is bullish clamp((45 − RSI) ÷ 20, 0, 1) or bearish clamp((RSI − 55) ÷ 20, 0, 1); wick score is the relevant lower or upper wick ÷ bar range, divided by 0.5 and clamped to 0–1. RSI uses a shorter lookback than the normalization lookback.
Calculation: Emitted candidate = highest-scoring eligible regular or hidden candidate for that pivot, among the scanned prior pivots; emit only when its score meets the configured minimum. Evaluate on bar close when that option is enabled.
Long: emitted regular bullish candidate OR emitted hidden bullish candidate
Short: emitted regular bearish candidate OR emitted hidden bearish candidate
