Calculation: Gap % = (current close − previous completed RTH session close) / previous completed RTH session close × 100
Calculation: ORB high = highest high during the opening range; ORB low = lowest low during the opening range. Use the completed range after the opening range ends.
Calculation: Premarket high = highest high from 04:00 to 09:30 ET in the current session.
Calculation: Stop level = ORB low − ATR × stop multiplier
Calculation: Target 1 = entry price + ATR × target 1 multiplier
Calculation: Target 2 = entry price + ATR × target 2 multiplier
Calculation: Retest touch = low ≤ ORB high × (1 + retest buffer) AND close ≥ ORB high × (1 − retest buffer)
Long: First bar after the opening range ends with close > ORB high AND previous bar close ≤ previous bar ORB high AND Gap % ≥ minimum gap AND close > previous completed RTH session close AND close > VWAP AND (premarket high unavailable OR close > premarket high) AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume ≥ volume moving average × volume surge multiplier AND within the early-session window
Long: A primary long signal has occurred AND at least two bars have elapsed since it AND a retest touch has occurred since it AND close > ORB high AND close > open AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND within the early-session window; at most one retest signal per primary signal
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop level OR high ≥ Target 1 OR high ≥ Target 2
