Calculation: Regime = assign the current feature vector to its nearest centroid in a K-means model trained on a trailing window and periodically retrained. The vector is: absolute EMA(21)−EMA(50) divided by ATR(14); standard deviation of close over 20 bars divided by close; highest high−lowest low over a feature lookback divided by ATR(14); and absolute EMA(21) change over that lookback divided by ATR(14) times the lookback. Normalize each feature to 0–1 using its trailing training-window minimum and maximum. Classify each centroid as Trend if its first plus fourth component is at least both its second component and 1−third−first; otherwise Volatile if its second component is at least 1−third−first; otherwise Range. Use the current bar’s features and the latest trained centroids.
Calculation: Channel bounds = choose by current regime: Range uses midpoint=(highest high+lowest low)/2 over a range-channel lookback and width=max((highest high−lowest low)/2×0.85, ATR(14)×range width factor); Trend uses linear-regression close over a trend-channel lookback as midpoint and width=max(RMS residual from that regression, ATR(14)×trend width factor); Volatile uses EMA(close, volatility-channel lookback) as midpoint and ATR(14)×volatility width factor as width. Upper=midpoint+width; lower=midpoint−width. Calculate from current and earlier chart bars.
Long: Close > upper channel + ATR(14) × breakout threshold for the configured number of consecutive bars.
Short: Close < lower channel − ATR(14) × breakout threshold for the configured number of consecutive bars.
