Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum over the heatmap window of volume × (2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), using the current bar even while it is forming.
Calculation: Active liquidity wall = a price row whose maximum intensity in the most recent 40% of heatmap columns exceeds the wall threshold; intensity is computed by distributing each bar’s volume across overlapping price rows, accumulating each row from oldest to newest with per-column decay, then normalizing by the 85th percentile of column maxima, applying the cutoff and gamma. Exclude rows within one row of the highest-volume row (POC); rank the remaining qualifying rows by intensity. The first ranked row is the strongest active wall.
Long: volume ≥ bubble threshold × average volume of the previous 50 bars AND close ≥ (high + low) / 2 OR Cumulative volume delta ≥ 0 AND there is no active liquidity wall within 0.4% of close.
Short: volume ≥ bubble threshold × average volume of the previous 50 bars AND close < (high + low) / 2 OR Cumulative volume delta < 0 AND there is no active liquidity wall within 0.4% of close.
