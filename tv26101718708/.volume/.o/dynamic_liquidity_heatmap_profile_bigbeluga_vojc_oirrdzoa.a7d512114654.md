Calculation: Liquidity profile bin = Divide the range from the lowest low minus its volatility offset to the highest high plus its volatility offset over a configurable lookback of current-timeframe bars into configurable bins. Volatility offset is the rolling highest, over that lookback, of (5-bar ATR / 50) × normalized volume, where normalized volume is the 10-bar volume sum divided by its highest value over the lookback. Scan the same range at 100 equal intervals. On a bar whose high is a 2-bar high, create upper liquidity levels at interval midpoints within one interval of high plus volatility offset; on a bar whose low is a 2-bar low, create lower liquidity levels at interval midpoints within one interval of low minus volatility offset, shifted down by volatility offset. Remove an upper level when a later high exceeds it; remove a lower level when a later low falls below it. For each profile bin, sum the 10-bar volume sums of active levels less than one bin width from its midpoint.
Long: close > midpoint of a nonempty liquidity profile bin AND close is outside that bin AND (proximity filter is disabled OR abs(close − midpoint) / close ≤ configured percentage
Short: close ≤ midpoint of a nonempty liquidity profile bin AND close is outside that bin AND (proximity filter is disabled OR abs(close − midpoint) / close ≤ configured percentage
