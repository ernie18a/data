Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme relative to the configured number of chart bars on both sides; usable only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Sweep depth = (high − swing high) / ATR for a high sweep; (swing low − low) / ATR for a low sweep.
Calculation: Candle close location = (high − close) / range for short setups; (close − low) / range for long setups, where range = max(high − low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Candle body strength = abs(close − open) / ATR. Directional rejection wick ratio = upper wick / range for short setups; lower wick / range for long setups.
Calculation: Setup quality score = rounded, clamped sum of sweep-depth fit, directional close location, rejection wick, level freshness, relative volume, displacement, market condition, and level cleanliness scores; each component is normalized to its configured point weight. Relative volume compares current volume with its moving average. Displacement combines body strength and close distance beyond the level, both ATR-normalized. Market condition combines candle range/ATR and current ATR relative to its moving average.
Calculation: EMA bias = chart close versus chart EMA, and/or previous completed higher-timeframe close versus EMA calculated on previous completed higher-timeframe bars; optional slope agreement compares the EMA with its preceding value.
Calculation: Market efficiency = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback. Volatility ratio = ATR divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Long stop = entry minus ATR multiple, or swept low extreme minus ATR buffer. Short stop = entry plus ATR multiple, or swept high extreme plus ATR buffer. Target distance = entry-to-stop risk multiplied by the configured reward/risk ratio.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an eligible stored confirmed swing low is newly penetrated (current low < level and prior low >= level), sweep depth is within configured bounds, sweep type passes its configured mode, and first-penetration and dual-side sweep rules pass; within the configured reclaim window from that sweep, close >= swept level + ATR close buffer AND directional close location and body strength meet their thresholds AND any enabled bullish-body and same-bar lower-wick requirements pass AND setup quality score >= minimum AND enabled direction, EMA-bias, market, cooldown, same-direction distance, duplicate-bar, active-position, and opposite-signal conflict filters pass.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an eligible stored confirmed swing high is newly penetrated (current high > level and prior high <= level), sweep depth is within configured bounds, sweep type passes its configured mode, and first-penetration and dual-side sweep rules pass; within the configured reclaim window from that sweep, close <= swept level − ATR close buffer AND directional close location and body strength meet their thresholds AND any enabled bearish-body and same-bar upper-wick requirements pass AND setup quality score >= minimum AND enabled direction, EMA-bias, market, cooldown, same-direction distance, duplicate-bar, active-position, and opposite-signal conflict filters pass.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= target.
