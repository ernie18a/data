Calculation: ZLEMA = EMA of close plus the difference between close and close from a lagged bar; compare the current value with its previous value.
Long: (close crosses above any prior completed daily, weekly, or monthly high/low AND close is above that level by more than an ATR-scaled threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND current ZLEMA > previous ZLEMA) OR (at any prior completed daily, weekly, or monthly low, low <= level AND close > level AND lower wick > candle body times a ratio AND volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND current ZLEMA > previous ZLEMA)
Short: (close crosses below any prior completed daily, weekly, or monthly high/low AND close is below that level by more than an ATR-scaled threshold AND volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND current ZLEMA <= previous ZLEMA) OR (at any prior completed daily, weekly, or monthly high, high >= level AND close < level AND upper wick > candle body times a ratio AND volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier AND current ZLEMA <= previous ZLEMA)
