Calculation: Developing volume profile = aggregate lower-timeframe bars from the current profile anchor through the current forming bar; sign each bar’s volume by whether its close is above or below its previous close, and distribute volume evenly across the price rows from its low through its high. Use row height set by a tick size or by ATR(14) divided by a row-count parameter.
Calculation: POC = center of the highest-volume profile row; VAH and VAL = upper and lower edges of the value area formed by expanding from the POC toward the adjacent row with greater volume until the configured share of profile volume is reached; POV = center of the lowest-volume row inside the value area, excluding the POC. Use the developing profile.
Calculation: Bar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume signed by close versus previous close within the current chart bar; CVD = cumulative bar delta since the current profile anchor, including the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; EMA = exponential moving average; SMA = simple moving average.
Long: (low < VAL AND close > VAL AND close > open) OR (low <= POC AND close > POC AND close > open AND low > VAL) OR (close > POV AND previous close <= POV AND close > open), with the selected signal model enabled; require positive bar delta if delta confirmation is enabled; if divergence is enabled, low < the lowest prior low over the divergence lookback AND CVD > the lowest prior CVD over that lookback; if the momentum guard is enabled, high - low <= ATR × its multiplier; for sweep or POC bounce, if the rejection-wick filter is enabled, (min(open, close) - low) / (high - low) × 100 >= its threshold; if the EMA filter is enabled, close > EMA; if the session filter is enabled, bar time is inside its session; long direction is enabled; not on a new profile bar; and no same-direction signal at a node within 1 ATR occurred inside the cooldown.
Short: (high > VAH AND close < VAH AND close < open) OR (high >= POC AND close < POC AND close < open AND high < VAH) OR (close < POV AND previous close >= POV AND close < open), with the selected signal model enabled; require negative bar delta if delta confirmation is enabled; if divergence is enabled, high > the highest prior high over the divergence lookback AND CVD < the highest prior CVD over that lookback; if the momentum guard is enabled, high - low <= ATR × its multiplier; for sweep or POC bounce, if the rejection-wick filter is enabled, (high - max(open, close)) / (high - low) × 100 >= its threshold; if the EMA filter is enabled, close < EMA; if the session filter is enabled, bar time is inside its session; short direction is enabled; not on a new profile bar; and no same-direction signal at a node within 1 ATR occurred inside the cooldown.
Long: bar delta / volume × 100 >= the whale-delta threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, 20) × 1.5.
Short: bar delta / volume × 100 <= negative the whale-delta threshold AND volume > SMA(volume, 20) × 1.5.
Long: bar delta / volume × 100 > 12.
Short: bar delta / volume × 100 < -12.
Long: CVD > CVD 10 bars ago.
Short: CVD <= CVD 10 bars ago.
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR high >= target price; if both occur on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR low <= target price; if both occur on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, Long condition occurs.
Exit: long, Short condition occurs.
