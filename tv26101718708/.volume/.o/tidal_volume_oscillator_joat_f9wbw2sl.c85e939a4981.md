Calculation: VZO = clamp(blend EMA-smoothed VZO with its exponential-decay weighted average over a lookback window, then multiply by an adaptive factor and clamp to [-100,100]. EMA-smoothed VZO is 100*(R-1)/(R+1), where R is smoothed positive momentum divided by smoothed negative momentum (use 100 if negative momentum is near zero and positive momentum is nonzero; otherwise use 1). Momentum is EMA of EMA(close-close[1]) times EMA(volume/SMA(volume)); smooth each positive and absolute-negative part of that momentum with an EMA. The adaptive factor is 1 plus 0.2 times the short-minus-long SMA of EMA-smoothed VZO divided by its matching rolling standard deviation, clipped to [0.9,1.1] (use zero for the ratio if standard deviation is zero). The short SMA lookback is about one third of the long lookback. The weighted average uses prior VZO values with weights exp(-bars_back/(window*0.3)).
Calculation: Signal = simple moving average of VZO.
Long: On a completed bar, VZO crosses above Signal (current VZO > Signal and previous VZO <= previous Signal), OR a newly confirmed VZO pivot low has a lower price low and higher VZO low than the preceding confirmed VZO pivot low, OR a newly confirmed VZO pivot low has a higher price low and lower VZO low than the preceding confirmed VZO pivot low. Compare price and VZO at each pivot bar; confirm pivots using bars on both sides.
Short: On a completed bar, VZO crosses below Signal (current VZO < Signal and previous VZO >= previous Signal), OR a newly confirmed VZO pivot high has a higher price high and lower VZO high than the preceding confirmed VZO pivot high, OR a newly confirmed VZO pivot high has a lower price high and higher VZO high than the preceding confirmed VZO pivot high. Compare price and VZO at each pivot bar; confirm pivots using bars on both sides.
