Calculation: CETP score = Over the current-timeframe bars, form each candle’s body ratio (close−open)/(high−low+ε) and upper/lower wick ratios, weighted by their respective parameters. Keep the latest CETP-window ratios, multiplying older stored ratios by the decay parameter each bar. Bin each ratio into CETP-bins categories using bin width (2/CETP-bins)×(1+ATR/14-bar SMA of ATR); compute Shannon entropy of the resulting 3D histogram and divide by ln(CETP-bins³). Multiply the mean stored body ratio by (1−normalized entropy) and [momentum weight + (CETP-window close momentum / lagged close)×(1+RMA gains/RMA losses)×momentum scale], then multiply by 1.2 when RMA(max(high−prior high,0))/ATR exceeds RMA(max(prior low−low,0))/ATR or vice versa, otherwise by 1.0. Use current and prior completed bars.
Calculation: Long threshold = long base threshold / 1.5 when CETP score > 0; otherwise long base threshold × 1.5.
Calculation: Short threshold = short base threshold / 1.5 when CETP score < 0; otherwise short base threshold × 1.5.
Calculation: Entry filters = absolute CETP score > minimum score strength AND standard deviation of close over 20 bars / 20-bar SMA of close × 100 < volatility limit AND current high−low > ATR × minimum price-move multiplier AND absolute momentum adjustment > 0.05 AND volume > 20-bar SMA of volume × minimum volume multiplier.
Long: CETP score > long threshold AND entry filters
Short: CETP score < short threshold AND entry filters
Exit: Long, CETP score < short threshold AND entry filters OR close <= entry price × (1−long stop-loss percent/100)−ATR × ATR multiplier OR trailing stop is active and close is at or below the highest high since entry minus ATR × trailing ATR multiplier OR bars held > maximum hold bars
Exit: Short, CETP score > long threshold AND entry filters OR close >= entry price × (1+short stop-loss percent/100)+ATR × ATR multiplier OR trailing stop is active and close is at or above the lowest low since entry plus ATR × trailing ATR multiplier OR bars held > maximum hold bars
