Calculation: Estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) ÷ (high − low), with zero-range protection, on each configured timeframe’s last completed bar
Calculation: Net delta = 2 × estimated buy volume − volume
Long: On any configured timeframe, net delta > 0 OR RSI of close > its upper threshold
Short: On any configured timeframe, net delta ≤ 0 OR RSI of close < its lower threshold
