Calculation: Fast ribbon average = average of two moving averages of close using the same type, with a shorter and a longer lookback.
Calculation: Slow ribbon average = average of two moving averages of close using the same type, with a shorter and a longer lookback; both lookbacks are longer than the corresponding fast ribbon lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish spread count = consecutive bars on which the fast ribbon average is above the slow ribbon average and the difference is greater than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Bearish spread count = consecutive bars on which the fast ribbon average is below the slow ribbon average and the difference is less than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Fast bullish spread count = consecutive bars on which the shorter-lookback fast moving average is above the longer-lookback fast moving average and their difference is greater than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Fast bearish spread count = consecutive bars on which the shorter-lookback fast moving average is below the longer-lookback fast moving average and their difference is less than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Slow bullish spread count = consecutive bars on which the shorter-lookback slow moving average is above the longer-lookback slow moving average and their difference is greater than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Slow bearish spread count = consecutive bars on which the shorter-lookback slow moving average is below the longer-lookback slow moving average and their difference is less than on the prior bar; otherwise reset to zero.
Calculation: Bar high = max(open, close) in Renko mode, otherwise high.
Calculation: Bar low = min(open, close) in Renko mode, otherwise low.
Long: bullish spread count >= 2 AND fast bullish spread count >= 2 AND slow bullish spread count >= 2
Short: bearish spread count >= 2 AND fast bearish spread count >= 2 AND slow bearish spread count >= 2
Exit: long, bar low <= highest bar high since entry * (1 - trailing-stop distance) OR highest bar high since entry >= entry price * (1 + target-profit distance) AND bar low <= highest bar high since entry * (1 - trailing-profit distance) OR bar low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss distance) OR (opposite-signal exit enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss disabled) AND fast ribbon average on prior bar < slow ribbon average on prior bar AND current open < slow ribbon average on prior bar AND that opposite-MA exit condition was false on the prior bar
Exit: short, bar high >= lowest bar low since entry * (1 + trailing-stop distance) OR lowest bar low since entry <= entry price * (1 - target-profit distance) AND bar high >= lowest bar low since entry * (1 + trailing-profit distance) OR bar high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss distance) OR (opposite-signal exit enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss disabled) AND fast ribbon average on prior bar > slow ribbon average on prior bar AND current open > slow ribbon average on prior bar AND that opposite-MA exit condition was false on the prior bar
