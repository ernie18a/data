Long: On the current chart timeframe, for any of the plotted moving-average lookbacks (from shorter to longer), the highest minus lowest of the moving average over its channel lookback is greater than the high-minus-low price range over its lookback multiplied by a rate, AND the current moving average is greater than the channel low plus that price-range threshold; the moving average is calculated from close using the selected MA type and may be linear-regression-smoothed.
Short: On the current chart timeframe, for any of the plotted moving-average lookbacks (from shorter to longer), the highest minus lowest of the moving average over its channel lookback is greater than the high-minus-low price range over its lookback multiplied by a rate, AND the current moving average is less than the channel high minus that price-range threshold; the moving average is calculated from close using the selected MA type and may be linear-regression-smoothed.
