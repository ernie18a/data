Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the opening-range formation session; use the completed range after that session ends.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close minus open.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open, open <= previous close, and close >= previous open; bearish engulfing = close < open, open >= previous close, and close <= previous open.
Long: During the signal window after the opening range completes, (an unswept confirmed swing low below the opening-range low is swept with low < swing low, then a later bullish candle closes strictly inside the opening range) OR (low <= opening-range low, close strictly inside the opening range, close > open, current candle body > previous candle body, no earlier close below the opening-range low this session, and no external swing low was swept this session) OR (after a close above the opening-range high arms an upside breakout, a later candle retests within the configured tolerance and closes above the opening-range high, and either it is bullish and meets the configured breakout candle pattern or the immediately previous candle was a valid retest that closed within the configured tolerance and was bearish; current body > previous candle body; the breakout has not failed or been locked for the session) OR (EMA was above the opening-range high when the range completed, price first closes above the opening-range high, then crosses above the EMA while still above the range high, and after that cross an EMA retest within configured tolerance has occurred, followed by a bullish engulfing candle or an immediately subsequent bullish candle after a bearish retest; the signal candle closes above both EMA and range high, has body > previous candle body, and the EMA setup has not been locked for the session). Apply the configured minimum body-to-range filter when enabled.
Short: During the signal window after the opening range completes, (an unswept confirmed swing high above the opening-range high is swept with high > swing high, then a later bearish candle closes strictly inside the opening range) OR (high >= opening-range high, close strictly inside the opening range, close < open, current candle body > previous candle body, no earlier close above the opening-range high this session, and no external swing high was swept this session) OR (after a close below the opening-range low arms a downside breakout, a later candle retests within the configured tolerance and closes below the opening-range low, and either it is bearish and meets the configured breakout candle pattern or the immediately previous candle was a valid retest that closed within the configured tolerance and was bullish; current body > previous candle body; the breakout has not failed or been locked for the session) OR (EMA was below the opening-range low when the range completed, price first closes below the opening-range low, then crosses below the EMA while still below the range low, and after that cross an EMA retest within configured tolerance has occurred, followed by a bearish engulfing candle or an immediately subsequent bearish candle after a bullish retest; the signal candle closes below both EMA and range low, has body > previous candle body, and the EMA setup has not been locked for the session). Apply the configured minimum body-to-range filter when enabled.
