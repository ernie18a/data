Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range window; use only after that window completes, resetting at its next start.
Calculation: Retest-zone width = ATR × a multiplier, clamped between configured minimum and maximum point widths; ATR is calculated from the chart bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by its configured-length average.
Long: Opening range is complete, the bar is in the configured active session and outside the opening-range window, no upward breakout has occurred since the range reset, and close > opening range high OR an upward breakout has occurred and has not failed, the bar's low is at most opening range high + retest-zone width, its high is at least opening range high − retest-zone width, close > opening range high, and close > open OR the prior bar confirmed a long retest, no upward breakout failure has occurred, the current bar is in the configured active session and outside the opening-range window, current high > prior confirmation bar high, and (volume is not required or relative volume ≥ the configured elevated-volume threshold).
Short: Opening range is complete, the bar is in the configured active session and outside the opening-range window, no downward breakout has occurred since the range reset, and close < opening range low OR a downward breakout has occurred and has not failed, the bar's high is at least opening range low − retest-zone width, its low is at most opening range low + retest-zone width, close < opening range low, and close < open OR the prior bar confirmed a short retest, no downward breakout failure has occurred, the current bar is in the configured active session and outside the opening-range window, current low < prior confirmation bar low, and (volume is not required or relative volume ≥ the configured elevated-volume threshold).
