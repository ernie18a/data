Calculation: Fast/slow/trend EMA = EMAs of the selected source on the chart timeframe, with fast shorter than slow and trend longer than both
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast and slow EMAs on the configured higher timeframe’s last completed bar
Calculation: Bull score = add one for fast EMA > slow EMA, close > trend EMA, RSI above its midpoint but below its upper bound, close > open, close > VWAP, ADX above its strength threshold, and volume > its moving average; add two for higher-timeframe trend bullish
Calculation: Bear score = add one for fast EMA < slow EMA, close < trend EMA, RSI below its midpoint but above its lower bound, close < open, close < VWAP, ADX above its strength threshold, and volume > its moving average; add two for higher-timeframe trend bearish
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured opening-range lookback of chart-timeframe bars; ready after that many bars have elapsed since the start of the day. The range includes the current bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = ATR-based stop from entry; when structure stops are enabled, use the closer of that stop and the recent swing low/high buffered by ATR, enforcing a minimum ATR distance from entry. Swing extrema use the current and preceding chart-timeframe bars over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry and initial stop
Calculation: First/second targets = entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk multiplied by the configured first/second reward-to-risk factors
Calculation: Active stop = initial stop; after first target is reached, move it to entry; after second target is reached, move it to the first target. For a long, targets are reached when high >= target; for a short, when low <= target.
Long: confirmed bar AND within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe trend bullish AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA AND close > both fast and slow EMAs AND RSI above its midpoint but below its upper bound AND bull score >= the configured minimum score and grade threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (opening-range filter disabled OR opening range ready AND close > opening-range high)
Short: confirmed bar AND within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled AND higher-timeframe trend bearish AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend EMA AND close < both fast and slow EMAs AND RSI below its midpoint but above its lower bound AND bear score >= the configured minimum score and grade threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (opening-range filter disabled OR opening range ready AND close < opening-range low)
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND low <= active stop
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND high >= active stop
