Calculation: Nearest support/resistance = Build a volume-by-price profile over the rolling lookback window on the chart timeframe, including the current bar: divide the window’s high-low range into bins and distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the bins touched by its low-high range. Repeatedly select the largest remaining bin whose volume meets the configured fraction of the highest-volume bin; suppress nearby bins using the configured spacing, classify each selected bin midpoint below the last confirmed close as support and above it as resistance, and retain the configured number per side. Nearest support/resistance is the closest retained level below/above that close.
Calculation: Volume moving average = Simple moving average of volume.
Long: (Close crosses above nearest resistance AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume moving average)) OR (low <= nearest support AND close > nearest support)
Short: (Close crosses below nearest support AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume moving average)) OR (high >= nearest resistance AND close < nearest resistance)
