Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively higher/lower than the highs/lows of the chosen lookback bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed swing.
Calculation: Reversal probability = successful outcomes divided by eligible outcomes among the first K completed stored sweeps of the same direction, scanning oldest first (use fewer than K if fewer are eligible); use 0 if the stored-sweep count is below K or there are no eligible outcomes. Label each sweep once at least 10 bars later: an above-swing-high sweep succeeds if the later close is more than the evaluation bar's ATR above its sweep-bar close; a below-swing-low sweep succeeds if the later close is more than the evaluation bar's ATR below its sweep-bar close. Keep sweeps in insertion order, removing the oldest when the memory limit is exceeded.
Long: Low < latest confirmed swing low AND Close > latest confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing low - Low > sweep buffer × ATR AND reversal probability for below-swing-low sweeps ≥ probability threshold
Short: High > latest confirmed swing high AND Close < latest confirmed swing high AND High - latest confirmed swing high > sweep buffer × ATR AND reversal probability for above-swing-high sweeps ≥ probability threshold
