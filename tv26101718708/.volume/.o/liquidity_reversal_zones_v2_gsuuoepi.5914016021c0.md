Calculation: Reversal score = smoothly saturated signed weighted composite of oversold/overbought extremity, reversal flow, CVD divergence, liquidity sweep, candle rejection, higher-timeframe bias, funding, and spot-perpetual basis, multiplied by event, volume, open-interest, volatility, VWAP/session, and chop factors
Calculation: Medium threshold = configured absolute score threshold, or the configured rolling percentile of absolute score with an absolute floor
Calculation: Confluence count = number of enabled, available source groups aligned with the score direction: extremity; flow or CVD divergence; sweep; rejection; open interest; higher-timeframe bias; funding; basis
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = a confirmed pivot low makes a lower low in price and a higher low in cumulative volume delta
Calculation: Bearish CVD divergence = a confirmed pivot high makes a higher high in price and a lower high in cumulative volume delta
Calculation: Spring = after a tight, low-efficiency box formed from earlier bars, the current low breaks below its low and the close reclaims it
Calculation: Upthrust = after a tight, low-efficiency box formed from earlier bars, the current high breaks above its high and the close falls back below it
Long: (upward sweep is enabled or a downward-liquidity sweep occurred) AND reversal score direction is positive AND absolute reversal score meets the medium threshold AND bullish reversal-candle filter passes when enabled AND confluence count meets the enabled-and-available minimum AND market is not filtered as choppy; in delayed-confirmation mode, these setup conditions held on the previous bar and current close is above previous close; subject to the configured signal cooldown OR bullish CVD divergence OR spring OR a long-zone retest with a sufficiently large lower wick and close above the zone bottom OR a completed higher-timeframe bar sweeps below the previous higher-timeframe low, closes above it, and has a lower wick at least the configured fraction of its range
Short: (upward sweep is enabled or an upward-liquidity sweep occurred) AND reversal score direction is negative AND absolute reversal score meets the medium threshold AND bearish reversal-candle filter passes when enabled AND confluence count meets the enabled-and-available minimum AND market is not filtered as choppy; in delayed-confirmation mode, these setup conditions held on the previous bar and current close is below previous close; subject to the configured signal cooldown OR bearish CVD divergence OR upthrust OR a short-zone retest with a sufficiently large upper wick and close below the zone top OR a completed higher-timeframe bar sweeps above the previous higher-timeframe high, closes below it, and has an upper wick at least the configured fraction of its range
