Calculation: Trendline = apply a finite-window Hodrick–Prescott smoother to close, then compute its volume-weighted average, resetting at each selected timeframe boundary using chart-bar volume; use the selected timeframe’s last completed bar to detect each boundary.
Calculation: Volume-difference BB%B = Bollinger %B of the EMA-smoothed, historically normalized one-bar change in (OBV − PVT), where OBV uses close direction and volume, and PVT is the price-volume trend.
Calculation: Difference between BB%B series = close BB%B minus Volume-difference BB%B.
Long: datetime is within the configured date range AND (close < Trendline AND Trendline is rising OR close BB%B < 0 OR Volume-difference BB%B < 0) AND (high < previous high AND low > previous low OR low equals open AND open is below the prior 10-bar lowest low AND open < close AND close is above the midpoint of the previous bar’s range OR high exceeds the highest hl2 of the preceding lookback bars).
Short: datetime is within the configured date range AND ((close BB%B >= 1 OR (close BB%B >= 0.7 AND close BB%B < 1) OR Volume-difference BB%B >= 1 OR close > Trendline) AND (Trendline crosses above 0 OR Difference between BB%B series crosses above 0) AND high < previous high AND low > previous low).
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured date range AND ( ((close BB%B >= 1 OR Volume-difference BB%B >= 1 OR (Trendline crosses above 0 AND Difference between BB%B series crosses above 0)) AND (high < previous high AND low > previous low OR low < the lowest hl2 of the preceding lookback bars) AND (Trendline >= 1 OR Difference between BB%B series >= 1)) OR open >= previous high).
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured date range AND (close BB%B < 0 OR (close BB%B >= 0 AND close BB%B < 0.3) OR Volume-difference BB%B < 0 OR (close < Trendline AND Trendline is rising)) AND (high < previous high AND low > previous low OR low < the lowest hl2 of the preceding lookback bars).
