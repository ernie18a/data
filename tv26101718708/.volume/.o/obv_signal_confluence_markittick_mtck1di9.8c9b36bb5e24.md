Calculation: On-balance volume = cumulative volume signed positive when close rises, negative when close falls, and zero when unchanged.
Calculation: OBV histogram = on-balance volume minus its moving average.
Calculation: Price pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of an equal number of bars on each side; recognize it after the right-side bars complete.
Long: OBV crosses above its moving average OR a new price pivot low is lower than the previous pivot low while OBV histogram at that pivot is higher than at the previous pivot low OR a new price pivot low is higher than the previous pivot low while OBV histogram at that pivot is lower than at the previous pivot low.
Short: OBV crosses below its moving average OR a new price pivot high is higher than the previous pivot high while OBV histogram at that pivot is lower than at the previous pivot high OR a new price pivot high is lower than the previous pivot high while OBV histogram at that pivot is higher than at the previous pivot high.
Exit: Long, OBV crosses below its moving average.
Exit: Short, OBV crosses above its moving average.
