Calculation: Normalized close = apply the logistic function to the rolling Z-score of the fractionally differenced close, scaled by the configured sigmoid compression scale; use close instead of the differenced close when differencing is disabled or not warmed up. Fractional differencing uses weights w₀ = 1 and wₖ = −wₖ₋₁(d − k + 1)/k, stopping before the first weight below the configured threshold or at the configured maximum window; sum each weight times the corresponding lagged close. The Z-score uses the configured lookback mean and standard deviation, floored at 0.0001.
Long: Normalized close crosses above 50 OR, at a confirmed RSI pivot low, price makes a lower low and RSI makes a higher low than at the prior qualifying pivot low within the configured pivot-spacing bounds.
Short: Normalized close crosses below 50 OR, at a confirmed RSI pivot high, price makes a higher high and RSI makes a lower high than at the prior qualifying pivot high within the configured pivot-spacing bounds.
