Calculation: Alligator lines = smoothed moving averages of (high+low)/2; lips uses the shortest lookback, teeth the middle, jaw the longest
Calculation: ATR trailing line = close trailing stop at a multiple of ATR: when close is above the prior line, use the greater of the prior line and close−multiple×ATR; otherwise use the lesser of the prior line and close+multiple×ATR
Calculation: ATR trail state = bullish when fast line > medium line > slow line; bearish when fast line < medium line < slow line; otherwise neutral. Fast, medium, and slow use progressively longer ATR lookbacks and larger multipliers. Trail direction changes only when the bullish or bearish state is entered from a nonmatching state
Calculation: Dow direction = bullish when current-timeframe close > SMA(close, shorter lookback) > SMA(close, longer lookback), bearish when close < the shorter-lookback SMA < the longer-lookback SMA, and neutral otherwise; require current-timeframe volume > its 20-bar SMA × volume threshold and ATR(14)/close × 100 > volatility threshold
Calculation: GST bands = apply DEMA to close, a Gaussian weighted moving average to that result, then an SMMA; upper band = SMMA + standard deviation(SMMA) × upper multiplier, lower band = SMMA − standard deviation(SMMA) × lower multiplier
Calculation: Directional confirmations = Dow direction (weight 2), ATR trail direction (weight 2), GST direction (weight 1), KAMA direction (weight 1), VWMA direction (weight 1), and Alligator direction (weight 1); a component contributes only when its direction-specific CCI, ATR/close, distance-from-lips, and Alligator-structure filters pass. Dow also requires its volume and volatility conditions. CCI long: CCI > oversold threshold and rising; short: CCI < overbought threshold and falling. ATR/close must meet its minimum; long distance: close−lips meets minimum; short distance: lips−close meets minimum. Long structure: lips > teeth > jaw and close > lips; short structure: lips < teeth < jaw and close < lips
Long: flat position AND (long confirmations ≥ 3 AND ADX, RSI, and NATR pass their enabled direction-specific ranges OR KAMA(shorter-lookback) crosses above KAMA(longer-lookback) OR close crosses above VWMA OR Alligator direction changes to bullish OR Dow direction changes to bullish OR ATR trail direction changes to bullish OR close > GST upper band); for standalone indicator markings, require the relevant direction-specific filters, including ADX/RSI/NATR only when enabled
Short: flat position AND (short confirmations ≥ 3 AND ADX, RSI, and NATR pass their enabled direction-specific ranges OR KAMA(shorter-lookback) crosses below KAMA(longer-lookback) OR close crosses below VWMA OR Alligator direction changes to bearish OR Dow direction changes to bearish OR ATR trail direction changes to bearish OR close < GST lower band); for standalone indicator markings, require the relevant direction-specific filters, including ADX/RSI/NATR only when enabled
Exit: long, short confirmations ≥ 3 OR close ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: short, long confirmations ≥ 3 OR close ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
