Calculation: Session and range value-area high/low = For each profile, divide its low-to-high price range into configurable buckets and assign each bar’s volume to the bucket containing its (high + low) / 2. The session profile uses bars in the current session through the current, possibly forming bar; the range profile uses a configurable rolling lookback through the current, possibly forming bar. Start at the highest-volume bucket (lowest bucket on ties), then add adjacent buckets in descending volume order, choosing the upper bucket on ties, until the configured share of total volume is included. Value-area high is the upper edge and value-area low the lower edge of the included buckets.
Long: close > session value-area high AND close > range value-area high OR (session value-area midpoint − range value-area midpoint) / range value-area width × 100 >= configurable divergence threshold
Short: close < session value-area low AND close < range value-area low OR (session value-area midpoint − range value-area midpoint) / range value-area width × 100 <= −configurable divergence threshold
