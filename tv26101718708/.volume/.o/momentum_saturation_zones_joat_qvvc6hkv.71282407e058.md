Calculation: Momentum = selected RSI, ROC, MFI, Stochastic %D, or their composite average; composite = (RSI + normalized ROC + MFI + Stochastic %D) / 4, where normalized ROC = clamp(50 + 50 × ROC / (2 × 100-bar standard deviation of ROC + 0.0001), 0, 100)
Calculation: Gate momentum = selected RSI, normalized ROC, MFI, or Stochastic %D; for composite, use the same composite average as Momentum
Calculation: Adaptive pullback threshold = configured pullback percentage × clamp(current ATR as a percentage of close / its 50-bar SMA, 0.6, 1.8); use the configured percentage alone when adaptation is disabled
Calculation: Bear strength = rounded sum of clamp((peak gate momentum − 50) × 40 / 50, 0, 40), clamp((bear pullback percentage − adaptive pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20), clamp((peak-bar volume / current 20-bar SMA volume − 1) × 20, 0, 20), and 20 when enabled bearish divergence exists
Calculation: Bull strength = rounded sum of clamp((50 − trough gate momentum) × 40 / 50, 0, 40), clamp((bull pullback percentage − adaptive pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20), clamp((trough-bar volume / current 20-bar SMA volume − 1) × 20, 0, 20), and 20 when enabled bullish divergence exists
Long: On a confirmed bar, Momentum is at least the rolling Momentum low plus the absolute rolling Momentum low times the adaptive pullback threshold; the rolling low’s gate momentum is at or below the oversold level if the absolute-extreme gate is enabled; the low’s bar volume is greater than the current 20-bar SMA volume times the volume multiplier if the volume filter is enabled; the low is at least the configured confirmation bars old; Bull strength is at least the minimum strength; the rolling-low bar differs from the last accepted bull trough; and bars since the last accepted zone are at least the cooldown
Short: On a confirmed bar, Momentum is at most the rolling Momentum high times (1 − adaptive pullback threshold); the rolling high’s gate momentum is at or above the overbought level if the absolute-extreme gate is enabled; the high’s bar volume is greater than the current 20-bar SMA volume times the volume multiplier if the volume filter is enabled; the high is at least the configured confirmation bars old; Bear strength is at least the minimum strength; the rolling-high bar differs from the last accepted bear peak; and bars since the last accepted zone are at least the cooldown
