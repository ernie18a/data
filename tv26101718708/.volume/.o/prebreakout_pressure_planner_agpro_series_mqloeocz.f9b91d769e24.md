Calculation: Pressure range = highest high and lowest low over the pressure-range lookback, excluding the current bar; valid when upper > lower.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the current timeframe; use the current bar.
Calculation: Range height = upper range boundary − lower range boundary; range ATR = range height / ATR.
Calculation: Compression score = clamp(100 − ((range ATR − 0.55) / (compression ceiling − 0.55)) × 100, 0, 100), where the ceiling depends on sensitivity.
Calculation: Bull slope score = clamp(50 + 650 × (current linear-regression value of low − its value one bar earlier) / ATR, 0, 100); bear slope score = clamp(50 − 650 × (current linear-regression value of high − its value one bar earlier) / ATR, 0, 100). Both regressions use the buildup-slope lookback.
Calculation: Close position in range = clamp((close − lower boundary) / range height, 0, 1); bull close score = close position × 100; bear close score = (1 − close position) × 100.
Calculation: Bull candle score = if close ≥ open, 70 × abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) + 30 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); otherwise 35 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment). Bear candle score = if close ≤ open, 70 × abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) + 30 × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment); otherwise 35 × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Bull raw pressure = 0.42 × bull slope score + 0.38 × bull close score + 0.20 × bull candle score; bear raw pressure = 0.42 × bear slope score + 0.38 × bear close score + 0.20 × bear candle score. Active side is bull when bull raw pressure ≥ bear raw pressure; side is neutral when their absolute difference < 8.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average of volume over the volume-baseline lookback. Relative-volume slope = relative volume − its value five bars earlier. Volume score = clamp(clamp((relative volume − 0.70) / max(volume-support level − 0.70, 0.10) × 70, 0, 85) + clamp(relative-volume slope × 120, −25, 25) + 15, 0, 100).
Calculation: Pressure score = clamp(0.30 × compression score + 0.28 × max(bull raw pressure, bear raw pressure) + 0.18 × volume score + 0.12 × active-side candle score + 0.12 × active-side close score, 0, 100).
Calculation: Fakeout risk score = clamp(0.42 × (100 − active-side candle score) + 0.34 × (100 − volume score) + 14 if range ATR exceeds the compression ceiling, otherwise 0, + 20 if either probe condition holds, otherwise 0, 0, 100). Upper probe: high > upper boundary + trigger width and close ≤ upper boundary. Lower probe: low < lower boundary − trigger width and close ≥ lower boundary. Trigger width = ATR × trigger-corridor width parameter.
Long: valid pressure range AND close is within its boundaries AND NOT (upper probe OR lower probe with fakeout risk score ≥ fakeout threshold) AND bull raw pressure ≥ bear raw pressure AND bull raw pressure − bear raw pressure ≥ 8 AND pressure score ≥ minimum pressure score.
Short: valid pressure range AND close is within its boundaries AND NOT (upper probe OR lower probe with fakeout risk score ≥ fakeout threshold) AND bear raw pressure > bull raw pressure AND bear raw pressure − bull raw pressure ≥ 8 AND pressure score ≥ minimum pressure score.
