Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the opening-range window; use after the window is complete.
Calculation: 9 EMA trend = 9 EMA is higher (lower) than each of its values on the prior two bars.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = sum of volume signed positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and zero when equal; reset each day.
Calculation: Extended = absolute distance from close to 9 EMA, as a percentage of close, is within its configured maximum.
Calculation: VWAP separation = absolute distance from close to VWAP, as a percentage of close, meets or exceeds its configured minimum.
Calculation: Long confirmation score = count of: close > VWAP; close > 9 EMA; bullish 9 EMA trend; 9 EMA > 21 EMA; valid ORB retest; bullish EMA pullback rejection; 5-minute RSI > 55; entry-timeframe RSI > 55; cumulative volume delta rising; not extended. Use the last completed 5-minute bar for 5-minute RSI and the last completed bar of the configured entry timeframe for entry RSI.
Calculation: Short confirmation score = count of: close < VWAP; close < 9 EMA; bearish 9 EMA trend; 9 EMA < 21 EMA; valid ORB retest; bearish EMA pullback rejection; 5-minute RSI < 45; entry-timeframe RSI < 45; cumulative volume delta falling; not extended. Use the last completed 5-minute bar for 5-minute RSI and the last completed bar of the configured entry timeframe for entry RSI.
Long: After a completed opening range, close crosses above its high while close > VWAP, close > 9 EMA, 9 EMA trend is bullish, 9 EMA > 21 EMA, not extended, and VWAP separation meets its minimum; then, while armed, low <= opening-range high AND close > opening-range high AND low <= 9 EMA AND close > 9 EMA AND close > open AND close > VWAP AND 9 EMA trend is bullish AND 9 EMA > 21 EMA AND not extended AND VWAP separation meets its minimum AND long confirmation score >= configured minimum AND no long signal has already occurred that day.
Short: After a completed opening range, close crosses below its low while close < VWAP, close < 9 EMA, 9 EMA trend is bearish, 9 EMA < 21 EMA, not extended, and VWAP separation meets its minimum; then, while armed, high >= opening-range low AND close < opening-range low AND high >= 9 EMA AND close < 9 EMA AND close < open AND close < VWAP AND 9 EMA trend is bearish AND 9 EMA < 21 EMA AND not extended AND VWAP separation meets its minimum AND short confirmation score >= configured minimum AND no short signal has already occurred that day.
Exit: Long, close < 9 EMA OR close < VWAP.
Exit: Short, close > 9 EMA OR close > VWAP.
