Calculation: Prior range = highest high and lowest low over the preceding sweep lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (current volume − rolling volume mean) / rolling volume standard deviation
Calculation: Void probability = sigmoid(1.8 × sweep rejection distance / ATR + 0.9 × max(volume Z-score, 0) + 0.6 × absolute 3-bar close change / ATR + 0.4 × absolute current open-to-previous close gap / ATR − 1.2)
Calculation: Volume anomaly = volume Z-score magnitude exceeds its threshold OR current volume exceeds rolling volume mean × burst multiplier
Calculation: Downward sweep = current low < prior range low AND current close > prior range low AND lower wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick ratio AND (current close − current low) ≥ ATR × minimum rejection distance AND current close > current open
Calculation: Upward sweep = current high > prior range high AND current close < prior range high AND upper wick / candle range ≥ minimum wick ratio AND (current high − current close) ≥ ATR × minimum rejection distance AND current close < current open
Calculation: New downward-target void = on a confirmed downward sweep with probability ≥ minimum probability, volume anomaly, and zone size ≥ ATR × minimum size, create a bullish void from prior range low − ATR to min(current close, prior range low + ATR × 0.5)
Calculation: New upward-target void = on a confirmed upward sweep with probability ≥ minimum probability, volume anomaly, and zone size ≥ ATR × minimum size, create a bearish void from max(current close, prior range high − ATR × 0.5) to prior range high + ATR
Calculation: Active voids = created zones not yet reached by price and not older than the maximum lifetime; merge same-direction zones when they overlap or their corresponding edges are closer than ATR × merge distance, retaining the outer boundaries and higher probability
Long: Downward sweep OR the nearest active void is above the close, its midpoint is within the maximum fill distance in ATR units, and its urgency exceeds the minimum urgency
Short: Upward sweep OR the nearest active void is below the close, its midpoint is within the maximum fill distance in ATR units, and its urgency exceeds the minimum urgency
