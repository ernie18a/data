Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of the selected price source over a lookback
Calculation: Local RSI peak = RSI at offset s is greater than the next s newer RSI values and greater than or equal to the s older values; local RSI bottom reverses these comparisons, where s is the pivot strength
Calculation: Body above line % = 100 × portion of the candle body above the confirmation line ÷ body size; body below line % = 100 × portion below the line ÷ body size; both are 0 for a zero-size body
Long: After RSI ≤ lower extreme, record its lowest value until RSI ≥ lower cooling level AND RSI > second-bottom re-entry ceiling; then a confirmed local RSI bottom ≤ that ceiling AND ≥ recorded first bottom + minimum gap; after that pivot, RSI rises for the configured consecutive bars AND has not fallen below the first bottom by more than the reset tolerance; mark the second bullish RSI signal; within the entry search window after that signal, body above line % ≥ required percentage; enter at the next bar's open
Short: After RSI ≥ upper extreme, record its highest value until RSI ≤ upper cooling level AND RSI < second-peak re-entry floor; then a confirmed local RSI peak ≥ that floor AND ≤ recorded first peak − minimum gap; after that pivot, RSI falls for the configured consecutive bars AND has not risen above the first peak by more than the reset tolerance; mark the second bearish RSI signal; within the entry search window after that signal, body below line % ≥ required percentage; enter at the next bar's open
