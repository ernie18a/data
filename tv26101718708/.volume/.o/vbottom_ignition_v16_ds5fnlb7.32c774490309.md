Calculation: EMA fast/slow = exponential moving averages of close; fast uses a shorter lookback than slow
Calculation: Market trend EMAs = exponential moving averages of close; fast uses a shorter lookback than slow
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / simple moving average of volume
Calculation: ATR compression ratio = ATR / simple moving average of ATR using the same lookback
Calculation: Bollinger width compression ratio = current Bollinger Band width / its simple moving average; width is (upper band − lower band) / basis, with bands at basis ± a parameterized standard deviation multiple
Calculation: Base range % = (highest high − lowest low) / lowest low × 100 over the base lookback
Calculation: Shelf variation % = (highest − lowest) / highest over the shelf lookback, using prior-bar highs for resistance or prior-bar lows for support
Calculation: Higher lows = lowest low in the oldest third of the lookback < lowest low in the middle third ≤ lowest low in the newest third
Calculation: Lower highs = highest high in the oldest third of the lookback > highest high in the middle third ≥ highest high in the newest third
Calculation: EMA distance % = (close − fast EMA) / fast EMA × 100 for long checks; (fast EMA − close) / fast EMA × 100 for short checks
Calculation: VCP score = count of: trend condition, flat relevant shelf, base range within limit, higher lows (long) or lower highs (short), ATR compression within limit, Bollinger width compression within limit, relative volume above its threshold, RSI above its long threshold or below its short threshold
Calculation: Continuation score = count of: close above (long) or below (short) fast EMA, flat relevant shelf, range within limit, higher lows (long) or lower highs (short), relative volume above threshold, RSI above long or below short threshold, and either ATR or Bollinger width compression within limit
Calculation: Context score = count of: prior impulse range % above minimum; pullback depth within maximum (long: (impulse high − recent low) / impulse range; short: (recent high − impulse low) / impulse range); close above/below fast EMA; close above/below slow EMA; fast EMA above/below slow EMA; RSI at least/at most 50; relative volume above the context minimum; relative volume above the context strong threshold; close within the configured percentage of the impulse high/low
Calculation: Bottom score = count of: close within configured percentage above the major low; bottom base range within limit; bottom breakout; close above fast EMA; fast EMA rising; RSI above minimum; RSI crosses above 50; volume condition passes (volume filter disabled, volume unavailable, or relative volume above breakout threshold)
Calculation: Initial long stop = lower of entry-bar close minus ATR × stop multiple and entry-bar low; initial short stop = higher of entry-bar close plus ATR × stop multiple and entry-bar high
Calculation: Trailing long stop = greatest of its prior value and each newly confirmed swing low; trailing short stop = least of its prior value and each newly confirmed swing high. Swing points use left/right confirmation bars.
Long: long trading enabled AND cooldown elapsed AND not in a short position AND (not already long OR pyramiding enabled) AND ((market trend filter disabled OR close > slow market-trend EMA AND fast market-trend EMA > slow market-trend EMA) AND (consolidation filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum AND absolute fast-EMA percentage change over its slope lookback ≥ minimum) AND (VCP breakout with VCP score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum OR continuation breakout with continuation score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum OR contextual continuation breakout with context score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum) OR bottom ignition breakout with bottom score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum AND (bottom trend-filter bypass enabled OR market trend allows long) AND (bottom consolidation-filter bypass enabled OR consolidation filter passes))
Short: short trading enabled AND cooldown elapsed AND not in a long position AND (not already short OR pyramiding enabled) AND market trend filter disabled OR (close < slow market-trend EMA AND fast market-trend EMA < slow market-trend EMA) AND consolidation filter disabled OR (ADX ≥ minimum AND absolute fast-EMA percentage change over its slope lookback ≥ minimum) AND (VCP breakdown with VCP score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum OR continuation breakdown with continuation score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum OR contextual continuation breakdown with context score ≥ minimum AND EMA distance ≤ maximum)
Exit: long, in a long position AND trailing long stop exists AND (close < trailing long stop OR low < trailing long stop when close-only exit is disabled)
Exit: short, in a short position AND trailing short stop exists AND (close > trailing short stop OR high > trailing short stop when close-only exit is disabled)
