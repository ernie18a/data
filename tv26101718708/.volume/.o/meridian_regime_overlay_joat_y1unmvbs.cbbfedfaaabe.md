Calculation: Composite score = clamp from −8 to 8 of the sum of: +1/−1/0 for fast EMA above/below/equal to slow EMA; +1/−1/0 for slow EMA above/below/equal to anchor EMA; the same two comparisons for fast/slow and slow/anchor EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; +1/−1/0 for close above/below/equal to the current day’s cumulative volume-weighted average of bar typical prices; +1/−1/0 for the fast EMA’s change over the slope lookback divided by ATR, above/below/within the positive/negative slope threshold; current structure state (+1 after an upward break of the latest confirmed pivot high, −1 after a downward break of the latest confirmed pivot low, otherwise 0; a break requires a close crossing the pivot and the pivot must be within the structure memory); +1/−1/0 for positive/negative directional index dominance with ADX at or above its floor; +1/−1/0 for a bullish/bearish impulse bar (body larger than 1.1 times the 20-bar average body, and volume divided by its 20-bar average at or above the relative-volume threshold); +1/−1/0 for close above/below/inside the current day’s opening-range high/low. Equal or unmet comparisons contribute 0.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger upper and lower bands are both inside the Keltner upper and lower bands. Bollinger bands use an EMA basis plus/minus the standard deviation of close times the band multiplier; Keltner bands use an EMA basis plus/minus an EMA of true range times the Keltner multiplier.
Calculation: Expansion = Bollinger band width (upper minus lower, divided by basis) is greater than its own lookback average times the expansion factor and greater than its prior-bar width.
Calculation: Regime code = 0 during squeeze; otherwise +3 if composite score ≥6 and expansion, +2 if score ≥3, +1 if score >0, −3 if score ≤−6 and expansion, −2 if score ≤−3, −1 if score <0, and 0 otherwise. The directional stack comparisons use current-bar EMAs locally and EMAs on the last completed higher-timeframe bar for higher-timeframe bias.
Long: Bar is completed AND regime code ≥2 AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA > anchor EMA AND close > current-day VWAP AND NOT squeeze
Long: Bar is completed AND expansion AND positive directional index > negative directional index AND ADX ≥ its floor AND composite score ≥4
Short: Bar is completed AND regime code ≤−2 AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA < anchor EMA AND close < current-day VWAP AND NOT squeeze
Short: Bar is completed AND expansion AND negative directional index > positive directional index AND ADX ≥ its floor AND composite score ≤−4
