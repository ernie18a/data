Calculation: Directional Change event = In up mode, a down DC occurs when low <= the tracked high extreme × (1 − threshold); otherwise an up OS occurs when high > that extreme. In down mode, an up DC occurs when high >= the tracked low extreme × (1 + threshold); otherwise a down OS occurs when low < that extreme. A DC switches mode and sets the new extreme to the current low or high, respectively; an OS keeps the mode and updates its corresponding extreme. Initialize in up mode with both extremes at the first close.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Chop = neither Trend Up nor Trend Down, and the 20-bar simple moving average of Body ratio < 0.35
Calculation: Trend Up = fast EMA of close > slow EMA of close and ADX >= trend threshold; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Trend Down = fast EMA of close < slow EMA of close and ADX >= trend threshold; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Range = ADX <= range threshold
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume > simple moving average of volume × volume multiplier
Long: selected signal agent is enabled AND current bar is in regular session or the session gate is disabled AND volume spike or the volume gate is disabled AND current bar is confirmed or close-only confirmation is disabled AND NOT Chop AND ((action filter is Trend only AND Trend Up AND (up OS or up DC event)) OR (action filter is Range only AND Range AND up DC event) OR (action filter is Off/map only AND (up OS or up DC event)))
