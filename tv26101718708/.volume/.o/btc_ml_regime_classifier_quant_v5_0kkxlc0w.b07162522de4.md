Calculation: Smoothed bullish probability = weighted percentage of bullish labels among the K nearest historical bars, smoothed with a weighted moving average. Compare the six current features with historical features normalized to their rolling min–max ranges: Choppiness Index; close-to-close price change plus half its change from the prior bar; close’s z-score from VWAP using the standard deviation of close; 14-period RSI; ATR’s z-score against its longer-term mean and standard deviation; and volume divided by its longer-term average. Each historical feature vector is paired with the direction of the close’s change over the following four bars, labeled bullish or bearish only when that change exceeds half an ATR in the corresponding direction. Find neighbors by the sum of log(1 + absolute feature differences), weighting each label by a Gaussian of its distance; omit neutral labels from the bullish and bearish vote totals.
