Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that same lookback; optionally smooth with an EMA.
Calculation: Adaptive average = recursively update the prior value by the squared efficiency-ratio-weighted blend of fast and slow smoothing constants, initialized at close; use its value from the prior bar as the anchor.
Calculation: Dead-zone width = ATR × dead-zone multiplier × (2 − efficiency ratio).
Calculation: Buy and sell bands = blend (1 − learning weight) × [anchor ∓ dead-zone width × the respective band multiplier] with learning weight × [anchor ∓ safe ATR × the respective learned multiplier]. Each learned multiplier is the sensitivity-scaled mean of recent wick penetrations beyond the anchor, measured in safe ATR units, clamped to its minimum and maximum; initialize it at 0.5 and update it after the minimum sample count. Keep separate buy-side low-wick and sell-side high-wick histories.
Calculation: Confirmed bear regime = close below anchor − dead-zone width for the required consecutive bars; confirmed bull regime = close above anchor + dead-zone width for the required consecutive bars. Each confirmed regime persists until the opposite regime is confirmed.
Long: Confirmed bear regime AND low ≤ buy band AND (buy throttle disabled OR no prior buy since the last bull-regime transition OR |low − last buy-signal close| ≥ ATR × buy separation) AND (LL filter disabled OR no prior buy low since the last bull-regime transition OR low < the lowest low at prior buy signals) AND bar is confirmed.
Exit: Long, confirmed bull regime AND high ≥ sell band AND (sell throttle disabled OR no prior sell in the current sell sequence OR no prior sell bar OR bars since last sell ≥ rounded [base cooldown × (1 + min(maximum multiplier − 1, max(0, (close − anchor) / safe ATR) × cooldown scale))]) AND (HH filter disabled OR no prior sell high since the last bear-regime transition OR high > the highest high at prior sell signals) AND bar is confirmed AND long position quantity > 0.
