Calculation: Previous range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the selected lookback, excluding the current bar; previous range width percentage = (high − low) / low × 100
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trend = close > fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA / close < fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = count of these six conditions: bullish/bearish trend; RSI between 45 and 72 / between 28 and 55; MACD line above/below signal with positive/negative histogram; close above/below Bollinger basis; usable high volume with OBV above/below its moving average; bullish/bearish candle close above previous swing high / below previous swing low
Calculation: Usable high volume = current volume is available and at least the selected multiplier times its moving average; when volume filtering is enabled, missing volume passes the volume gate
Calculation: Higher-timeframe permission = close above/below an EMA calculated on the selected higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Lower/upper reversion zone = previous range low + / high − 20% of previous range width
Calculation: Bullish/bearish rejection wick ratio = lower/upper wick length divided by current candle high − low
Calculation: Long stop = selected method: entry minus ATR multiple; entry × (1 − fixed stop percentage); or previous swing low minus ATR buffer, falling back to entry minus ATR multiple if invalid
Calculation: Short stop = selected method: entry plus ATR multiple; entry × (1 + fixed stop percentage); or previous swing high plus ATR buffer, falling back to entry plus ATR multiple if invalid
Calculation: Long target = selected method: entry plus ATR multiple; entry × (1 + fixed target percentage); or entry plus risk × reward ratio
Calculation: Short target = selected method: entry minus ATR multiple; entry × (1 − fixed target percentage); or entry minus risk × reward ratio
Long: bullish trend, bullish score ≥ minimum confluence, higher-timeframe permission if enabled, and close above session VWAP if enabled; OR close > fast EMA, RSI > 52, MACD histogram > 0 and greater than prior histogram, OBV > its moving average, volume gate, higher-timeframe permission if enabled, and close above session VWAP if enabled; OR previous range qualifies, close > previous range high + ATR buffer, prior close ≤ prior range high, candle close position ≥ selected minimum, RSI > 50, MACD line > signal, volume gate, breakout volume permission, and close above session VWAP if enabled; OR mean reversion enabled, sideways market (previous range within width threshold and neither trend aligned), range width ≥ selected ATR multiple, low ≤ lower reversion zone, close > lower reversion zone, bullish candle, lower wick ratio ≥ selected minimum, RSI < 38 and rising if RSI-turn filter enabled, and volume gate; AND confirmed bar, sufficient history, cooldown passed, long side allowed by trade/exit settings, fresh setup if enabled, and (no short candidate or bullish score > bearish score)
Short: bearish trend, bearish score ≥ minimum confluence, higher-timeframe permission if enabled, and close below session VWAP if enabled; OR close < fast EMA, RSI < 48, MACD histogram < 0 and less than prior histogram, OBV < its moving average, volume gate, higher-timeframe permission if enabled, and close below session VWAP if enabled; OR previous range qualifies, close < previous range low − ATR buffer, prior close ≥ prior range low, candle close position ≤ 1 − selected minimum, RSI < 50, MACD line < signal, volume gate, breakout volume permission, and close below session VWAP if enabled; OR mean reversion enabled, sideways market, range width ≥ selected ATR multiple, high ≥ upper reversion zone, close < upper reversion zone, bearish candle, upper wick ratio ≥ selected minimum, RSI > 62 and falling if RSI-turn filter enabled, and volume gate; AND confirmed bar, sufficient history, cooldown passed, short side allowed by trade/exit settings, fresh setup if enabled, and (no long candidate or bearish score > bullish score)
Exit: Long, low ≤ active long stop OR high ≥ active long target
Exit: Short, high ≥ active short stop OR low ≤ active short target
