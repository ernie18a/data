Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = confirmed pivot lows/highs on the chart timeframe, plus confirmed pivot lows/highs from a configurable higher timeframe; merge each pivot into the nearest same-side level within a configurable ATR-based tolerance, updating its touch count, weighted midpoint, and zone bounds, or create a new zone around it. Prune levels untouched for a configurable number of chart bars and cap the number per side.
Calculation: Level strength = pivot touch count + 2 for a level with a higher-timeframe pivot contribution + 1 if created within the last 80 chart bars.
Calculation: Zone boundaries and midpoint = the level's bottom, top, and midpoint; each new level begins with configurable ATR-based half-height.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when the higher-timeframe fast EMA is above the slow EMA and that bar's close is above the fast EMA; bearish when the fast EMA is below the slow EMA and close is below the fast EMA. Use the configured higher-timeframe bars; values are from the last completed bar historically and may use the forming bar in real time.
Calculation: Volume expansion = chart-bar volume ≥ its configurable moving average × a configurable multiplier.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true ranges over the lookback / highest high-to-lowest low range over the lookback) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Active session = chart bar is within the configured session, or true when the session filter is disabled.
Calculation: Signal layer count = 1 each for level strength meeting its minimum, aligned higher-timeframe trend (or 1 if that filter is disabled), volume expansion (or 1 if disabled), Choppiness Index below its maximum (or 1 if disabled), bar range ≥ 0.35 × ATR, and active session; add 1 for SWEEP or RETEST.
Long: At least one support level has strength ≥ its minimum, chart-bar low is within the configured ATR proximity of its midpoint or inside its zone, at least one enabled playbook applies (DEFEND: lower wick / bar range ≥ its minimum, close > body bottom + 0.25 × bar range, and close > zone bottom; SWEEP: low < zone bottom − configured ATR sweep depth, close > midpoint, and close > open; RETEST: within the configured prior-bar lookback some close < zone bottom, and current low ≤ zone top, close > midpoint, and close > open; COIL: each of the configured most recent bars has true range < 0.75 × ATR, close is within configured ATR proximity of the midpoint or inside the zone, and close > midpoint), Choppiness Index is below its maximum if that filter is enabled, the session is active if that filter is enabled, higher-timeframe trend is bullish if that filter is enabled, signal layer count ≥ its minimum, chart history exceeds 100 bars, cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is confirmed.
Short: At least one resistance level has strength ≥ its minimum, chart-bar high is within the configured ATR proximity of its midpoint or inside its zone, at least one enabled playbook applies (DEFEND: upper wick / bar range ≥ its minimum, close < body top − 0.25 × bar range, and close < zone top; SWEEP: high > zone top + configured ATR sweep depth, close < midpoint, and close < open; RETEST: within the configured prior-bar lookback some close > zone top, and current high ≥ zone bottom, close < midpoint, and close < open; COIL: each of the configured most recent bars has true range < 0.75 × ATR, close is within configured ATR proximity of the midpoint or inside the zone, and close < midpoint), Choppiness Index is below its maximum if that filter is enabled, the session is active if that filter is enabled, higher-timeframe trend is bearish if that filter is enabled, signal layer count ≥ its minimum, chart history exceeds 100 bars, cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is confirmed.
Exit: Long, close ≥ the nearest resistance level above entry price OR close ≤ the entry support zone bottom minus a configurable ATR buffer.
Exit: Short, close ≤ the nearest support level below entry price OR close ≥ the entry resistance zone top plus a configurable ATR buffer.
