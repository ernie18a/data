Calculation: Active trendline = project the line through the latest two confirmed swing lows for support or swing highs for resistance; ignore lines older than the maximum line age. In Auto mode, choose the eligible side nearest the close in ATR units; a support line is eligible when close ≥ line − ATR × invalidation distance, a resistance line when close ≤ line + ATR × invalidation distance. If both are eligible, choose the nearer; if neither is eligible but a support line exists, choose support.
Calculation: Retest score = 18% line-age score + 20% touch-count score + 22% depth score + 25% close-response score + 15% relative-volume score. Normalize age and touches upward, depth to peak at the preferred probe depth and decline toward the deep-probe limit, close response upward from the pocket edge to the response threshold, and relative volume upward from its weak to strong threshold; clamp each component to 0–100. Touch count starts at two anchors and adds post-anchor bars in the touch lookback whose high/low intersects the ATR-width pocket and whose close is within the invalidation distance on the defended side. Depth uses probe depth while a pocket touch occurred within the response window; otherwise it uses closeness to the line. Cap score at 44 when neither near nor recently touched, and at 24 when invalidated.
Calculation: Retest state = INVALIDATED if close crosses the invalidation edge; otherwise READY if pocket was touched within the response window, score ≥ ready threshold, and close is on the defended side; otherwise MONITOR if recently touched and score ≥ monitor threshold; otherwise FAILING if recently touched and close is on the wrong side or score < monitor threshold; otherwise WATCH if near the pocket; otherwise WAIT.
Long: Active side is support AND (price touches the retest pocket OR retest state is READY OR MONITOR OR close ≥ trendline + 0.70 ATR OR retest state is FAILING OR INVALIDATED on resistance).
Short: Active side is resistance AND (price touches the retest pocket OR retest state is READY OR MONITOR OR close ≤ trendline − 0.70 ATR OR retest state is FAILING OR INVALIDATED on support).
Exit: long, support-side retest state is INVALIDATED.
Exit: short, resistance-side retest state is INVALIDATED.
