Calculation: Matching-timeframe TOTAL and BTC dominance bars = aggregate their OHLCV from the backtest bars at the chart timeframe; use the bar currently forming.
Calculation: For each series, a swing-break event is bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high and bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low. A trend-break event is bullish when close first rises above the descending pivot-high line and bearish when close first falls below the ascending pivot-low line; pivot lines use the same swing lookback and slope method as the swing-break calculation.
Calculation: For each series, a long swing-pair event occurs when bullish swing-break and trend-break events occur in either order within the swing-pair window; a short swing-pair event is the corresponding bearish pair. An event not paired within the window expires.
Calculation: For each series, a reversal event is formed by pairing a macro reversal with the corresponding swing-pair event, in either order within the reversal-pair window; unmatched events expire. A macro reversal follows a pump event and alternates between the two reversal directions. Pump is local volume above its threshold, or (for TOTAL and BTC dominance) true range above its simple moving average over the swing lookback. A reversal candidate requires either a close crossing the Gaussian-weighted close average's lower envelope downward while the trailing-stop state is bullish, or a bullish trailing-stop flip while the last envelope-cross direction was downward; the short candidate is the mirrored condition. The envelope is the weighted close average plus or minus a multiple of the simple moving average of absolute close-to-average error. The trailing stop uses ATR for the chart series and simple-average true range for TOTAL and BTC dominance. Apply the optional recent-liquidity-sweep filter to all candidates; apply the optional ADX threshold filter to chart-series candidates.
Calculation: A recent liquidity sweep is a low below the prior lookback lows followed by a close back above them, or a high above the prior lookback highs followed by a close back below them; the corresponding filter passes when disabled or when a same-direction sweep occurred within its recency window.
Calculation: The local long signal is the local short reversal-pair event; the local short signal is the local long reversal-pair event. If enabled, block long when RSI's recent minimum is below its lower boundary, MACD histogram is below zero and falling, or a recent bearish candle body exceeds the ATR expansion threshold. If enabled, block short when RSI's recent maximum is above its upper boundary, MACD histogram is above zero and rising, or a recent bullish candle body exceeds the ATR expansion threshold.
Calculation: TOTAL-long-active and dominance-long-active mean the corresponding long signal occurred within the macro alignment window; TOTAL-short-active and dominance-short-active are defined analogously.
Long: Position side is not long AND (unblocked local long signal OR (TOTAL-long-active AND dominance-long-active) OR (TOTAL-long-active AND dominance-short-active) OR (TOTAL-long-active AND dominance-long-active first became true on this bar AND unblocked local long signal is false)).
Short: Position side is not short AND (unblocked local short signal OR (TOTAL-short-active AND dominance-short-active) OR (TOTAL-short-active AND dominance-long-active) OR (TOTAL-short-active AND dominance-short-active first became true on this bar AND unblocked local short signal is false)).
