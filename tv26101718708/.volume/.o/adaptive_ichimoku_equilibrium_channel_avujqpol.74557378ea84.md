Calculation: Equilibrium spine = efficiency ratio × fast range midpoint + (1 − efficiency ratio) × slow range midpoint; fast lookback is shorter than slow. If adaptation is disabled, use the slow midpoint.
Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the lookback; channel width = ATR × width multiplier × volatility factor. Channel bounds are spine ± width; position in channel = (close − lower bound) / (upper bound − lower bound), clamped to [0, 1].
Calculation: Trend state = bullish when close is above the cloud, cloud A is above cloud B, and spine slope is positive; classify as strong bullish only when efficiency ratio and ADX exceed their thresholds. Bearish is the mirror condition below the cloud; otherwise state is neutral. Cloud A is the mean of the fast and slow range midpoints; cloud B is the longer-lookback range midpoint.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = the same trend-state calculation on the configured higher-timeframe bars, using the latest available bar. Lower-timeframe momentum = close relative to its EMA on the configured lower-timeframe bars, using the latest available bar.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in price over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar price changes over that lookback. ATR uses true range. Volume average = simple average volume over its lookback.
Calculation: Long target = nearest unswept equal-high liquidity pool above entry, if enabled and available; otherwise upper channel bound. Short target = nearest unswept equal-low liquidity pool below entry, if enabled and available; otherwise lower channel bound. Equal-level pools pair pivot highs or lows within an ATR-based tolerance; discard a high pool when a later high exceeds it, and a low pool when a later low falls below it. Use the nearest qualifying pool to close.
Calculation: Divergence = at confirmed pivot highs, price makes a higher high while high minus equilibrium spine is less than at the previous pivot high; at confirmed pivot lows, price makes a lower low while spine minus low is less than at the previous pivot low. Pivot lookback is the same on both sides.
Calculation: Validated upward breakout = close crosses above the upper channel bound, displacement beyond it is at least the ATR-based threshold, candle body / bar range meets the minimum, volume / average volume meets the minimum, and close is in the upper half of the bar. Downward breakout is the mirror condition.
Long: (trend state > 0 AND position in channel < 0.25 AND close > open AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend > 0) AND (lower-timeframe filter disabled OR lower-timeframe close > EMA) AND (aligned-trend filter disabled OR efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter enabled AND higher-timeframe trend > 0 OR higher-timeframe filter disabled AND trend state is strong bullish)) AND long target − close ≥ minimum reward:risk × (close − cloud bottom + stop buffer × ATR) AND close > cloud bottom + stop buffer × ATR AND no setup is active) OR (trend state > 0 AND position in channel < 0.25 AND previous position in channel ≥ 0.25) OR bullish divergence OR validated upward breakout OR cloud A crosses above cloud B.
Short: (trend state < 0 AND position in channel > 0.75 AND close < open AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe trend < 0) AND (lower-timeframe filter disabled OR lower-timeframe close < EMA) AND (aligned-trend filter disabled OR efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter enabled AND higher-timeframe trend < 0 OR higher-timeframe filter disabled AND trend state is strong bearish)) AND close − short target ≥ minimum reward:risk × (cloud top + stop buffer × ATR − close) AND cloud top + stop buffer × ATR > close AND no setup is active) OR (trend state < 0 AND position in channel > 0.75 AND previous position in channel ≤ 0.75) OR bearish divergence OR validated downward breakout OR cloud A crosses below cloud B.
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR (high < target AND trend state < 0)
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR (low > target AND trend state > 0)
