Calculation: Predicted price = intercept + slope × current volume, where slope and intercept come from linear regression of close against volume over a lookback.
Calculation: MACD line of predicted price = difference between the short-term and long-term exponential moving averages of predicted price; MACD signal line = an exponential moving average of the MACD line.
Long: Predicted price crosses above its weighted moving average, AND MACD line is above its signal line, AND MACD line is rising over a lookback.
Short: MACD line is below its signal line, AND MACD line is falling over a shorter lookback, AND low is falling over a lookback.
Exit: long, MACD line is below its signal line AND MACD line is falling over a shorter lookback AND low is falling over a lookback.
