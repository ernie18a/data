Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume × signed close-position, where signed close-position = 2 × (close − low) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: CVD slope = cumulative volume delta − its EMA.
Calculation: Confirmed price or CVD pivot low/high = a low/high with the selected pivot lookback number of bars on both sides; price and CVD use the same lookback.
Long: A confirmed CVD pivot low occurs while the most recent confirmed price pivot is a low and occurred within the selected alignment window; OR the close crosses above its EMA AND CVD slope > 0 AND (confluence-only mode is off OR the bullish pivot condition holds) AND (session restriction is off OR the bar is in session) AND the bar is confirmed AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND the position is not already long.
Short: A confirmed CVD pivot high occurs while the most recent confirmed price pivot is a high and occurred within the selected alignment window; OR the close crosses below its EMA AND CVD slope < 0 AND (confluence-only mode is off OR the bearish pivot condition holds) AND (session restriction is off OR the bar is in session) AND the bar is confirmed AND the signal cooldown has elapsed AND the position is not already short.
