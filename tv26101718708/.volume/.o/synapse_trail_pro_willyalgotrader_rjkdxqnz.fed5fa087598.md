Calculation: ATR bands = EMA(close, trail lookback) ± ATR × volatility multiplier; optionally ratchet the lower band upward while direction is long and upper band downward while short, resetting the active band on a direction flip
Calculation: Regime score = 0.40 × min(ADX / ADX cap, 1) × 100 + 0.35 × clamp(100 − Choppiness Index, 0, 100) + 0.25 × 100 × correlation(close, bar index, long lookback)²
Calculation: Signal quality score = 100 × (HTF bias points + volume points + RSI points + 0.20 × regime score + 0.10 × break-strength points) / (50 + 30 when HTF data is valid + 20 when volume data is valid); HTF bias points are 30 for aligned, 15 for neutral, 0 for opposed; volume points are 20 when volume exceeds its longer-lookback SMA times the volume threshold, otherwise 0; RSI points are 20 when RSI is above its midpoint for long or below for short, otherwise 0; break-strength points are 100 × min(distance beyond the prior band / ATR, cap) / cap
Calculation: HTF bias = previous completed higher-timeframe close compared with the EMA on that same previous completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Entry stop = entry price − position side × ATR × stop multiplier
Calculation: Targets = entry price + position side × entry risk × each target R-multiple; entry risk is the distance from entry price to the entry stop
Long: close > prior upper ATR band AND prior direction was short AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND signal quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND (choppy-skip disabled OR regime score ≥ choppy threshold) AND (HTF alignment not required OR HTF bias is bullish) AND (range limit disabled OR current high − current low ≤ prior completed bar ATR × range limit) AND (extension limit disabled OR |close − EMA trail center| ≤ prior completed bar ATR × extension limit)
Short: close < prior lower ATR band AND prior direction was long AND bar is confirmed AND ATR > 0 AND signal quality score ≥ minimum quality threshold AND (choppy-skip disabled OR regime score ≥ choppy threshold) AND (HTF alignment not required OR HTF bias is bearish) AND (range limit disabled OR current high − current low ≤ prior completed bar ATR × range limit) AND (extension limit disabled OR |close − EMA trail center| ≤ prior completed bar ATR × extension limit)
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ third target OR a qualifying short-entry condition
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ third target OR a qualifying long-entry condition
