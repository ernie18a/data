Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; available after the right-side bars complete. Retain the most recent configured number of each.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick fraction = (high − max(open, close))/(high − low) / (min(open, close) − low)/(high − low), respectively; undefined when high = low.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low − low two bars ago’s comparison high (high two bars ago), when current low > high two bars ago; bearish FVG = low two bars ago − current high, when current high < low two bars ago. The middle bar is one bar ago.
Calculation: FVG score = round(clamp(clamp(gap size/current ATR × 3.2, 0, 3) + clamp(displacement body/current ATR × 2, 0, 3) + clamp(3 − age in bars/40, 0, 2) + clamp(current volume / its moving average × 1.2, 0, 2), 0, 10)); displacement body is the absolute open-close difference of the middle bar. Recalculate for each live gap on each bar.
Long: (low < any retained confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND close > open AND lower wick fraction ≥ sweep wick threshold) OR (a bullish FVG is live, its score ≥ minimum score, current bar overlaps its gap [low ≤ gap top AND high ≥ gap bottom], close > open, a bullish sweep occurred between the configured confirmation delay and window after the latest bullish sweep, inclusive, and more than the configured window bars have elapsed since the prior long magnet signal).
Short: (high > any retained confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND close < open AND upper wick fraction ≥ sweep wick threshold) OR (a bearish FVG is live, its score ≥ minimum score, current bar overlaps its gap [low ≤ gap top AND high ≥ gap bottom], close < open, a bearish sweep occurred between the configured confirmation delay and window after the latest bearish sweep, inclusive, and more than the configured window bars have elapsed since the prior short magnet signal).
