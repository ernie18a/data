Calculation: Volume profile = Using a configurable lookback of current-timeframe bars, distribute each bar’s volume evenly across price bins touched from low to high; optionally weight older bars by an exponential decay. Recompute the bins each bar.
Calculation: Low-volume pocket = A local-minimum bin whose volume is below a configurable percentile and below a configurable fraction of the mean volume of its adjacent bins; expand to contiguous bins below that percentile. Keep pockets meeting configurable minimum bin-count and ATR-width filters. Pocket bounds are the outer bin prices; midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Pocket walls = Mean bin volume over a configurable scan depth immediately above and below the pocket. Depth is mean volume within the pocket. For an above-price pocket, the above wall is far and below wall near; reverse these for a below-price pocket.
Calculation: Touch density = Number of closes in the pocket’s price bins over a configurable recent current-timeframe window, divided by that window’s bar count.
Calculation: Hurst proxy = Over a configurable current-timeframe close lookback, calculate the cumulative deviations from their mean, then (maximum cumulative deviation − minimum cumulative deviation) / standard deviation; take log of this ratio divided by log(half the lookback length), clamped to (0,1).
Calculation: HTF confluence = Pocket midpoint is within a configurable ATR distance of the high or low of any enabled higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Reversal score = Weighted mean of: sigmoid-normalized far-wall-to-depth ratio; 1 − vacuum; 1 − Hurst proxy; HTF confluence (1 if present, else 0); and 1 − min(10 × touch density, 1). Vacuum = clamp(1 − min(far-wall / near-wall, 1), 0, 1), using a small positive denominator when near-wall is zero.
Calculation: Breakthrough score = Weighted mean, using the same total weight, of: 1 − sigmoid-normalized far-wall-to-depth ratio; vacuum; Hurst proxy; and min(10 × touch density, 1). The sigmoid-normalized ratio is 2 / (1 + exp(−0.5 × far-wall / depth)) − 1. Classify as reversal when its score is greater than or equal to the breakthrough score; otherwise classify as breakthrough.
Calculation: Candidate pockets = For each of the four classes (above/below price × reversal/breakthrough), select the qualifying pocket with midpoint nearest the current close.
Calculation: Rising/falling volume = Current volume divided by its configurable short-term simple moving average exceeds the configured rising-volume threshold / is below the configured falling-volume threshold.
Calculation: Momentum and velocity filters = Bullish momentum is close above configurable EMA (or momentum filter disabled); bearish momentum is close below it (or disabled). Upward velocity is configurable ROC above zero (or velocity filter disabled); downward velocity is ROC below zero (or disabled).
Long: Nearest reversal pocket below price is selected AND (close crosses below its upper bound on this bar, or, in wick-confirmation mode, high reaches or exceeds its upper bound and close is below it) AND (falling volume OR upward velocity is false).
Long: Nearest breakthrough pocket above price is selected AND bullish momentum AND (close crosses above its lower bound on this bar, or, in wick-confirmation mode, low reaches or falls below its lower bound and close is above it) AND (rising volume OR upward velocity).
Short: Nearest reversal pocket above price is selected AND (close crosses above its lower bound on this bar, or, in wick-confirmation mode, low reaches or falls below its lower bound and close is above it) AND (falling volume OR upward velocity is false).
Short: Nearest breakthrough pocket below price is selected AND bearish momentum AND (close crosses below its upper bound on this bar, or, in wick-confirmation mode, high reaches or exceeds its upper bound and close is below it) AND (rising volume OR downward velocity).
