Calculation: Compression score = 10 × weighted mean of (1 − rolling percentile rank of ADX), normalized inverse Bollinger-width range, (1 − rolling percentile rank of N-bar high-low range), (1 − rolling percentile rank of volume), and clamp(1 − |RSI − 50|/25, 0, 1); use the same lookback for percentile ranks and the same N for Bollinger length and range.
Calculation: Buy-stop level = consolidation high + buffer × consolidation height; sell-stop level = consolidation low − buffer × consolidation height.
Calculation: Risk distance = the greater of the configured distance from entry to the selected stop basis (opposite range edge, range midpoint, or ATR multiple) and 0.25 × ATR(14); long stop = entry − risk distance, short stop = entry + risk distance; targets are entry ± risk distance × each configured R multiple.
Long: Confirmed bar in an active consolidation formed by at least the configured minimum consecutive bars with compression score ≥ configured minimum; when the consolidation is no longer tight or reaches its maximum span, configured breakout confirmation (close or high) > buy-stop level; no prior long breakout from this consolidation.
Short: Confirmed bar in an active consolidation formed by at least the configured minimum consecutive bars with compression score ≥ configured minimum; when the consolidation is no longer tight or reaches its maximum span, configured breakout confirmation (close or low) < sell-stop level; no prior short breakout from this consolidation.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop-loss level OR high ≥ selected take-profit level OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum tracking bars.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop-loss level OR low ≤ selected take-profit level OR bars since entry ≥ configured maximum tracking bars.
