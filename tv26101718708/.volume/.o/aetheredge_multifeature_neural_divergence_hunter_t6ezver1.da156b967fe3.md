Calculation: Normalized MACD histogram, CCI, and momentum = (value − lowest(value, 100 bars)) / (highest(value, 100 bars) − lowest(value, 100 bars)) × 100; use 50 when the range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish divergence count = across enabled RSI, MACD histogram, Stochastic, CCI, MFI, and momentum, count each regular or hidden bullish divergence between consecutive confirmed price pivot lows whose pivot-bar gap is within the minimum and maximum bounds. Regular: current pivot low < previous pivot low and oscillator at current pivot > oscillator at previous pivot. Hidden: current pivot low > previous pivot low and oscillator at current pivot < oscillator at previous pivot. Confirm each pivot after the configured right-side pivot bars; compare oscillator values on the pivot bars.
Calculation: Bearish divergence count = across enabled RSI, MACD histogram, Stochastic, CCI, MFI, and momentum, count each regular or hidden bearish divergence between consecutive confirmed price pivot highs whose pivot-bar gap is within the minimum and maximum bounds. Regular: current pivot high > previous pivot high and oscillator at current pivot < oscillator at previous pivot. Hidden: current pivot high < previous pivot high and oscillator at current pivot > oscillator at previous pivot. Confirm each pivot after the configured right-side pivot bars; compare oscillator values on the pivot bars.
Long: bullish divergence count ≥ minimum confluence OR regular bullish divergence count ≥ 3 OR hidden bullish divergence count ≥ 3
Short: bearish divergence count ≥ minimum confluence OR regular bearish divergence count ≥ 3 OR hidden bearish divergence count ≥ 3
