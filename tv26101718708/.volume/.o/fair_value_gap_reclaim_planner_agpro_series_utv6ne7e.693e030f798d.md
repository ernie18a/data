Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier, with gap size at least the ATR-based minimum and current volume meeting the minimum volume filter.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier, with gap size at least the ATR-based minimum and current volume meeting the minimum volume filter.
Calculation: Fair value gap reference = the selected midpoint or outer edge of the active gap.
Calculation: Reclaim quality score = a weighted normalized sum of reclaim-close distance from the reference in the gap direction, favorable wick share of candle range, candle body share of range, closeness of current fill progress to 50%, gap size relative to ATR, gap freshness, and relative volume (or a fixed contribution when relative volume scoring is disabled).
Long: On a confirmed bar, a valid bullish fair value gap is accepted when fresh gaps replace the active gap or no gap is active; OR an active bullish gap has not expired, price intersected its zone within the touch lookback, current close > the gap reference, previous close <= the gap reference, reclaim quality score >= the minimum ready score adjusted by confirmation mode, and no reclaim is already active.
Short: On a confirmed bar, a valid bearish fair value gap is accepted when fresh gaps replace the active gap or no gap is active; OR an active bearish gap has not expired, price intersected its zone within the touch lookback, current close < the gap reference, previous close >= the gap reference, reclaim quality score >= the minimum ready score adjusted by confirmation mode, and no reclaim is already active.
Exit: long, an active bullish gap exists and current close < its lower edge minus the ATR-based invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, an active bearish gap exists and current close > its upper edge plus the ATR-based invalidation buffer.
