Calculation: decision score = +2 if RSI is at or above its bull-bias level, otherwise −2 if at or below its bear-bias level; +1 for an RSI cross above its buy level, otherwise −1 for a cross below its sell level; +2 if stochastic %K is above %D and above 50, otherwise −2 if %K is below %D and below 50; +1 if %K crosses above %D while below its sell level, otherwise −1 if %K crosses below %D while above its buy level; +1 for a %K cross above its buy level, otherwise −1 for a cross below its sell level; +2 if MACD is above its signal line and its histogram is positive, otherwise −2 if MACD is below its signal line and its histogram is negative; +1 for a MACD cross above its signal line, otherwise −1 for a cross below; add +1 for a bullish EMA trend filter or −1 for a bearish one when enabled; add +1 for bullish volume confirmation or −1 for bearish volume confirmation when enabled. Bullish EMA trend: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > long-term EMA; bearish: fast EMA < slow EMA and close < long-term EMA. Bullish volume confirmation: volume > its average and close > open; bearish: volume > its average and close < open.
Long: decision score >= 3 AND previous-bar score < 3 OR decision score >= 6 AND previous-bar score < 6 OR RSI crosses above its buy level OR stochastic %K crosses above %D OR stochastic %K crosses above its buy level OR MACD crosses above its signal line
Short: decision score <= −3 AND previous-bar score > −3 OR decision score <= −6 AND previous-bar score > −6 OR RSI crosses below its sell level OR stochastic %K crosses below %D OR stochastic %K crosses below its sell level OR MACD crosses below its signal line
