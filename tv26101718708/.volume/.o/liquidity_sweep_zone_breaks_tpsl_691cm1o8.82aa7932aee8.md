Calculation: Swing high = a bar’s high greater than the highs of N bars before and N bars after it; confirmed N bars after the pivot bar. Swing low = a bar’s low lower than the lows of N bars before and N bars after it; confirmed N bars after the pivot bar. Use four independent N values in increasing order from shortest to longest.
Calculation: Sweep zone = the price range between a swept pivot and the high or low extreme of the breach bar. Create it after a confirmed sweep only if no close between breach and confirmation crossed that extreme; keep it active until broken. Ignore a duplicate zone with the same direction and pivot price within 10 minimum price increments.
Calculation: Risk distance = current ATR times a configurable multiplier, or entry price times a configurable percentage. Stop = entry price minus risk distance for long, plus it for short. Each target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk distance times its configurable risk-reward ratio.
Long: For any of the four pivot lookbacks, either (1) after price first breaches the latest confirmed unswept swing low (low < pivot or close < pivot), within the next confirmation-window bars and not on another low-breach bar, close > pivot and close > open; or (2) on a bar after an active buy-sweep zone formed, close < its sweep-bar low extreme; OR price closes above the sweep-bar high extreme of an active sell-sweep zone.
Short: For any of the four pivot lookbacks, either (1) after price first breaches the latest confirmed unswept swing high (high > pivot or close > pivot), within the next confirmation-window bars and not on another high-breach bar, close < pivot and close < open; or (2) on a bar after an active buy-sweep zone formed, close < its sweep-bar low extreme; OR price closes above the sweep-bar high extreme of an active sell-sweep zone.
Exit: Long, on a bar after entry, low <= stop OR high >= the farthest enabled target (TP3, else TP2, else TP1).
Exit: Short, on a bar after entry, high >= stop OR low <= the farthest enabled target (TP3, else TP2, else TP1).
