Calculation: Mapped levels = confirmed external swing highs/lows; repeated pivots within an ATR-based merge tolerance; midpoints of unfilled three-bar gaps; the prior opposite candle’s low before bullish displacement or high before bearish displacement; and previous completed daily/weekly highs and lows. Merge nearby factors into one persistent level. A displacement bar has a body in the top percentile of recent bodies and closes beyond the latest confirmed internal pivot.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick = high − max(open, close) / min(open, close) − low; body = |close − open|; range = high − low.
Calculation: Signed flow = (close − open) / range × volume when volume is available; otherwise (close − open) / range. Cumulative flow = running sum of signed flow.
Calculation: Squeeze bands = Bollinger Bands and Keltner Channels using the same lookback; the Bollinger envelope is inside the Keltner envelope for at least a minimum consecutive run, followed by expansion outside it.
Long: At bar close, after calibration is ready, ATR is positive, and this event class has not fired within the outcome horizon: (low < nearest mapped level below close within three ATR AND close > that level AND lower wick ≥ its rolling upper-quartile threshold) OR (confirmed external pivot low < prior confirmed external pivot low AND cumulative flow at the newer pivot > cumulative flow at the prior pivot) OR (close > latest confirmed external swing high) OR (volume is in the rolling upper 5th percentile AND range is in the rolling upper 10th percentile; when volume is unavailable, range is in its rolling upper 10th percentile AND body is in its rolling upper 15th percentile; direction requires close > open) OR (squeeze releases AND close > Bollinger basis) OR (bullish engulfing or bullish pin bar at a mapped level). A bullish pin bar has lower wick in its rolling upper quartile, lower wick > 2 × body, and close above the bar midpoint. Resolve simultaneous events in this priority order: sweep, divergence, shift, climax, squeeze, rejection; within sweep, divergence, or shift, choose the upward condition first.
Short: At bar close, after calibration is ready, ATR is positive, and this event class has not fired within the outcome horizon: (high > nearest mapped level above close within three ATR AND close < that level AND upper wick ≥ its rolling upper-quartile threshold) OR (confirmed external pivot high > prior confirmed external pivot high AND cumulative flow at the newer pivot < cumulative flow at the prior pivot) OR (close < latest confirmed external swing low) OR (volume is in the rolling upper 5th percentile AND range is in the rolling upper 10th percentile; when volume is unavailable, range is in its rolling upper 10th percentile AND body is in its rolling upper 15th percentile; direction requires close ≤ open) OR (squeeze releases AND close ≤ Bollinger basis) OR (bearish engulfing or bearish pin bar at a mapped level). A bearish pin bar has upper wick in its rolling upper quartile, upper wick > 2 × body, and close below the bar midpoint. Resolve simultaneous events in this priority order: sweep, divergence, shift, climax, squeeze, rejection; within sweep, divergence, or shift, choose the upward condition first.
