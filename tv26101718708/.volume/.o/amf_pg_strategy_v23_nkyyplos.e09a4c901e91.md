Calculation: Entry trend filter = ADX above a threshold AND, for long, +DI > −DI, or, for short, −DI > +DI; uses current chart bars and its own lookback parameters.
Calculation: Entry flow confirmation = ADX(5,10) > 30 AND +DI > −DI for long, or ADX(5,10) > 30 AND −DI > +DI for short.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); uses current chart bars.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = log(rescaled range of log-close deviations from their lookback mean) / log(lookback), where rescaled range is cumulative-deviation range divided by the standard deviation of those deviations; uses current chart bars and updates every fifth bar.
Calculation: Volatility expansion = short-lookback SMA of ATR(1) / longer-lookback SMA of ATR(1) > a threshold; uses current chart bars.
Calculation: Adaptive trend trail = recursively updated upper/lower bands. Set a base lookback by scaling current close-return volatility within its rolling range between a shorter and a longer bound; use about two thirds of that lookback as the secondary lookback. Compute volatility as an exponentially smoothed true range, initialized with an SMA; smooth it again with a 3-bar SMA (short) and compare with its SMA over the secondary lookback (long) to obtain a capped volatility ratio. Smooth close with a double EMA using the base lookback, combine that with the midpoint of the lookback high/low range, and weight the two by a capped ratio of smoothed recent volume to its longer smooth. Set normal bands around this center using the volatility ratio, smoothed volatility, and half of a 14-bar smoothed true range. When the flow confirmation is active in either direction, instead use close ± the volatility SMA × a volatility-ratio multiplier. In neutral trail state, enter its internal long/short state on a close beyond the corresponding band only with matching flow and entry filters; set the trail to the opposite band. In long state, raise the trail to the greater of its prior value and the lower band; close below it with short flow flips state short and resets trail to the upper band. In short state, lower the trail to the lesser of its prior value and the upper band; close above it with long flow flips state long and resets trail to the lower band. Use the current chart bars.
Long: close > adaptive trend trail AND long entry flow confirmation AND ADX above its threshold AND +DI > −DI AND Choppiness Index below its threshold AND Hurst exponent above its threshold AND volatility expansion is false; suppress if maximum drawdown protection has activated, and do not repeat while the last marked entry direction is long.
Short: close < adaptive trend trail AND short entry flow confirmation AND ADX above its threshold AND −DI > +DI AND Choppiness Index below its threshold AND Hurst exponent above its threshold AND volatility expansion is false; suppress if maximum drawdown protection has activated, and do not repeat while the last marked entry direction is short.
Exit: long, short-entry condition OR strategy equity drawdown from its peak reaches the configured limit.
Exit: short, long-entry condition OR strategy equity drawdown from its peak reaches the configured limit.
