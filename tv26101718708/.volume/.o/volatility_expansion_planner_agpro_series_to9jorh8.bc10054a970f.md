Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: ATR ratio = current ATR / its longer-lookback simple moving average; ATR slope = (current ATR − ATR from the slope lookback bars ago) / that earlier ATR.
Calculation: Range break = distance of the close beyond the prior range edge, divided by ATR; count it only when this is at least the minimum break threshold.
Calculation: Automatic direction = long if only the upper-edge break qualifies, short if only the lower-edge break qualifies; otherwise use the aligned trend (close and fast EMA above slow EMA for long, or both below for short); if neither aligns, choose long when close ≥ slow EMA, otherwise short.
Calculation: Close efficiency score = clamp(100 × (0.62 × directional close location within the bar range + 0.38 × absolute body / bar range), 0, 100). Directional close location is (close − low) / range for long and (high − close) / range for short.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(100 × (volume / its moving average − 0.75) / (1.55 − 0.75), 0, 100); use 55 if volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Compression = prior range width / its longer-lookback moving average ≤ its compression threshold AND ATR ratio ≤ its compression threshold. Squeeze age is consecutive compressed bars; for a range break use the age from the preceding bar.
Calculation: Risk shelf = the lower of prior range low and the lowest low over the shelf lookback for long; the higher of prior range high and the highest high over that lookback for short. Shelf distance = distance from close to shelf / ATR.
Calculation: Expansion score = rounded clamp to 0–100 of 0.24 × ATR expansion score + 0.22 × range break score + 0.18 × close efficiency score + 0.12 × volume score + 0.14 × trend score + 0.06 × shelf score + 0.04 × squeeze age score. ATR expansion score combines 72% peak score of ATR ratio (weak 0.82, ideal 1.38, bad above hot threshold) and 28% normalized ATR slope (−0.02 to 0.18). Range break score normalizes break distance from 0.35 × minimum break threshold to minimum break threshold + 0.95. Trend score: 55 when close is on the correct side of slow EMA, otherwise 18; add 28 when fast and slow EMAs are ordered in the correct direction; add up to 17 for a correctly directed slow EMA slope, normalized by its absolute slope / ATR from 0.01 to 0.32; clamp to 0–100. Shelf score is a peak score of shelf distance, with minimum shelf distance as weak, 42% of the interval from minimum to maximum as ideal, and maximum as bad. Squeeze age score normalizes age from 0.45× to 2.15× minimum squeeze age. Normalized scores rise linearly from 0 to 100 between their stated bounds and clamp outside; peak scores rise to the ideal and then fall to 0 at the bad bound.
Calculation: Too hot = ATR ratio > hot threshold AND close efficiency score < 58. Confirmation is confirmed when there is a range break, it is not too hot, close efficiency ≥ 62, volume score ≥ 52, and trend score ≥ 55. Shelf is weak when shelf distance is below the minimum or above the maximum.
Calculation: Structured state = range break AND expansion score ≥ structured threshold AND confirmed AND shelf is not weak.
Long: automatic direction is long AND the structured state begins on this bar AND the bar is confirmed.
Short: automatic direction is short AND the structured state begins on this bar AND the bar is confirmed.
