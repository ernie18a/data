Calculation: Adaptive trend direction = Use ATR, close-to-close absolute price change divided by prior close, and their weighted sum with a base factor; smooth that factor with an SMA. Form bands around the high-low midpoint at factor × ATR. Initialize the trend line at the upper band; when close is above the prior trend line, set direction bullish and the line to the greater of the lower band and prior line; otherwise set direction bearish and the line to the lesser of the upper band and prior line.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = A low/high that is lower/higher than the corresponding number of bars on both sides; becomes available only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Sellside sweep = Low below an unswept confirmed pivot low and close above that level; mark that level swept. Buyside sweep = High above an unswept confirmed pivot high and close below that level; mark that level swept.
Long: On a confirmed bar, adaptive trend direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (the prior completed higher-timeframe adaptive trend direction is bullish, if that filter is enabled) AND (ADX exceeds its threshold, if enabled) AND (volume exceeds its moving average × multiplier, if enabled) AND (RSI exceeds 50, if enabled) AND (absolute candle body is at least ATR × minimum body ratio, if enabled) AND a sellside sweep occurred within the sweep-memory lookback.
Short: On a confirmed bar, adaptive trend direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (the prior completed higher-timeframe adaptive trend direction is bearish, if that filter is enabled) AND (ADX exceeds its threshold, if enabled) AND (volume exceeds its moving average × multiplier, if enabled) AND (RSI is below 50, if enabled) AND (absolute candle body is at least ATR × minimum body ratio, if enabled) AND a buyside sweep occurred within the sweep-memory lookback.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar the adaptive trend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar the adaptive trend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
