Calculation: Internal pivot high/low = a high/low confirmed after the same lookback on each side; external pivots use a longer lookback, and minor pivots use a shorter lookback. A pivot level remains the latest level until broken.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken internal pivot high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken internal pivot low.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken external pivot high.
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken external pivot low.
Long: Low <= a sell-side liquidity pivot level AND close > that level.
Short: High >= a buy-side liquidity pivot level AND close < that level.
Long: Internal trend is bullish AND low <= the latest confirmed minor pivot low.
Short: Internal trend is bearish AND high >= the latest confirmed minor pivot high.
Long: The prior bar's low > the high three bars earlier AND the close two bars earlier > the low two bars earlier + ATR(200) two bars earlier * FVG threshold.
Short: The low three bars earlier > the prior bar's high AND the close two bars earlier < the high two bars earlier - ATR(200) two bars earlier * FVG threshold.
Long: Low < the top of an active, unmitigated bullish FVG AND close > its top.
Short: High > the bottom of an active, unmitigated bearish FVG AND close < its bottom.
Long: Low > high two bars earlier AND close one bar earlier > high two bars earlier AND close two bars earlier < open two bars earlier.
Short: High < low two bars earlier AND close one bar earlier < low two bars earlier AND close two bars earlier > open two bars earlier.
Long: A confirmed pivot low AND the confirmation bar's lower wick > half its high-low range.
Short: A confirmed pivot high AND the confirmation bar's upper wick > half its high-low range.
Long: Price overlaps an unmitigated bullish order or rejection block after its creation, before it has been retested.
Short: Price overlaps an unmitigated bearish order or rejection block after its creation, before it has been retested.
