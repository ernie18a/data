Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme across the configured left and right lookbacks; available only after the right-lookback bars complete.
Long: EMA9 crosses above EMA50 (mark the cross); enter on the first later confirmed bar while EMA9 > EMA50, the configured session and enabled filters pass, and the bullish cross remains armed; an opposite cross replaces the arm. OR close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high plus the configured ATR break buffer (mark the structure break), then enter either on that break or, in pullback mode, on a later bar with low <= swing high + configured ATR retest buffer and close > swing high; require enabled EMA, VWAP, volume, and session filters, and no open CHoCH position.
Short: EMA9 crosses below EMA50 (mark the cross); enter on the first later confirmed bar while EMA9 < EMA50, the configured session and enabled filters pass, and the bearish cross remains armed; an opposite cross replaces the arm. OR close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low minus the configured ATR break buffer (mark the structure break), then enter either on that break or, in pullback mode, on a later bar with high >= swing low - configured ATR retest buffer and close < swing low; require enabled EMA, VWAP, volume, and session filters, and no open CHoCH position.
Exit: long, EMA9 crosses below the configured-period SMA OR low <= active CHoCH stop OR (runner mode disabled AND low <= CHoCH TP2)
Exit: short, EMA9 crosses above the configured-period SMA OR high >= active CHoCH stop OR (runner mode disabled AND high >= CHoCH TP2)
