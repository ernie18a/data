Calculation: Confirmed swing range = latest confirmed pivot high minus latest confirmed pivot low; fib levels = swing low + range × selected fib fraction × volatility adjustment (bull) and swing high − range × selected fib fraction × volatility adjustment (bear), where the fraction is selected by trail mode and, in auto mode, by regime; volatility adjustment = clamp(1 / ATR-to-average-ATR ratio, 0.7, 1.5)
Calculation: Trail = ratcheting selected Fibonacci level: while long-biased, max(previous trail, bull level); while short-biased, min(previous trail, bear level); after a confirmed flip, reset to the opposite selected level
Calculation: Break confirmation = absolute(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ 0.45 and close’s penetration beyond the trail in the flip direction > 0.2 × ATR
Calculation: Confidence score = 100 × (0.20 × swing-structure score + 0.20 × regime-alignment score + 0.15 × ADX score + 0.15 × volume score + 0.15 × volatility-favorability score + 0.15 × trail/SMA confluence score); structure score uses swing range / ATR, regime alignment uses trend direction and trail direction, ADX score is ADX / its saturation level, volume score is current volume / its average, volatility favorability declines with distance of ATR ratio from 1 and is zero outside its allowed range, and trail/SMA confluence declines with distance between trail and SMA divided by 2 × ATR; clamp component scores to [0,1]
Long: previous trail direction is short AND close > previous trail AND break confirmation in the long direction AND (confidence filter disabled OR confidence grade meets configured minimum)
Short: previous trail direction is long AND close < previous trail AND break confirmation in the short direction AND (confidence filter disabled OR confidence grade meets configured minimum)
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + stop distance × configured second-target multiple OR low ≤ entry − stop distance
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − stop distance × configured second-target multiple OR high ≥ entry + stop distance
