Calculation: Prior candle body = absolute difference between its open and close
Calculation: Prior candle upper wick = prior high minus the greater of prior open and close
Calculation: Prior candle lower wick = the lesser of prior open and close minus prior low, floored at zero
Calculation: Wick symmetry = smaller prior wick divided by larger prior wick, with a small positive denominator floor
Calculation: Long stop = prior low minus a fixed tick offset; long target = signal-bar close plus 1.5 times the distance from signal-bar close to long stop
Calculation: Short stop = prior high plus a fixed tick offset; short target = signal-bar close minus 1.5 times the distance from short stop to signal-bar close
Long: PA mode enabled AND prior lower wick / prior candle body >= configured hammer wick ratio AND prior upper wick / prior candle body <= configured hammer upper-wick ratio AND prior candle body > 0 AND signal-bar close > prior close AND prior volume > the volume moving average on the prior completed bar × configured volume multiplier AND (long target − signal-bar close) / (signal-bar close − long stop) >= configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio OR POC mode enabled AND prior upper wick / prior candle body >= configured minimum POC wick ratio AND prior lower wick / prior candle body >= configured minimum POC wick ratio AND wick symmetry >= configured minimum symmetry ratio AND prior candle body > 0 AND prior volume > the volume moving average on the prior completed bar × configured volume multiplier AND signal-bar close > prior high AND (long target − signal-bar close) / (signal-bar close − long stop) >= configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio
Short: PA mode enabled AND prior upper wick / prior candle body >= configured star wick ratio AND prior lower wick / prior candle body <= configured star lower-wick ratio AND prior candle body > 0 AND signal-bar close < prior close AND prior volume > the volume moving average on the prior completed bar × configured volume multiplier AND (signal-bar close − short target) / (short stop − signal-bar close) >= configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio OR POC mode enabled AND prior upper wick / prior candle body >= configured minimum POC wick ratio AND prior lower wick / prior candle body >= configured minimum POC wick ratio AND wick symmetry >= configured minimum symmetry ratio AND prior candle body > 0 AND prior volume > the volume moving average on the prior completed bar × configured volume multiplier AND signal-bar close < prior low AND (signal-bar close − short target) / (short stop − signal-bar close) >= configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio
Exit: Long, bar low <= long stop OR bar high >= long target
Exit: Short, bar high >= short stop OR bar low <= short target
