Calculation: Volume-pressure-weighted MACD = EMA((EMA(close, longer lookback) − EMA(close, shorter lookback)) × (volume / SMA(volume, lookback))^power × (1 + clamp(0.1 × pressure score, −0.3, 0.3))); pressure score = (volume × (close − low)/(high − low + 0.0001) − volume × (high − close)/(high − low + 0.0001)) / SMA(absolute net pressure, same lookback)
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of volume-pressure-weighted MACD
Calculation: Histogram = volume-pressure-weighted MACD − signal line
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = low/high pivot with left bars = max(divergence lookback / 2, 2) and right bars = divergence lookback
Long: Volume-pressure-weighted MACD crosses above its signal line OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed price pivot low while the corresponding volume-pressure-weighted MACD pivot low is higher than its previous confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed price pivot low while the corresponding histogram pivot low is higher than its previous confirmed pivot low
Short: Volume-pressure-weighted MACD crosses below its signal line OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed price pivot high while the corresponding volume-pressure-weighted MACD pivot high is lower than its previous confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed price pivot high while the corresponding histogram pivot high is lower than its previous confirmed pivot high
