Calculation: Internal and swing pivot highs/lows = a bar’s high/low is the highest/lowest across equal-length bars on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars complete and update the latest level. Internal lookback is shorter than swing lookback; a new pivot resets that level’s taken flag.
Calculation: Structure trend = track separately for internal and swing structure, initially neutral; after an untaken upward break set bullish, and after an untaken downward break set bearish.
Calculation: Upward/downward break = either the current close crosses above/below the latest pivot level (current close above/below it and previous close at/below or at/above it), or the current high/low crosses beyond that level (current high/low above/below it and previous high/low at/below or at/above it), according to the selected close or wick confirmation mode.
Long: Internal upward break AND internal pivot high not yet taken AND internal structure trend = bearish, OR swing upward break AND swing pivot high not yet taken AND swing structure trend = bearish
Short: Internal downward break AND internal pivot low not yet taken AND internal structure trend = bullish, OR swing downward break AND swing pivot low not yet taken AND swing structure trend = bullish
