Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of bars in the session opening-range window, through the last bar of that window
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low of bars in the session opening-range window, through the last bar of that window
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) since session start, divided by cumulative volume since session start
Long: Opening range is locked AND (range-size filter disabled OR (opening-range high − opening-range low is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of current-timeframe ATR)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its current-timeframe moving average × configured multiplier) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP) AND (false-breakout filter disabled OR close has remained above opening-range high for at least the configured number of bars) AND within session AND daily trade count below its cap AND close > opening-range high; OR opening range is locked AND close[1] < opening-range low AND close > opening-range low
Short: Opening range is locked AND close[1] > opening-range high AND close < opening-range high
Exit: Long, high ≥ close + (close − opening-range low) × configured partial-profit multiple
Exit: Short, low ≤ close − (opening-range high − close) × configured partial-profit multiple
Exit: Long, low ≤ (opening-range low if range-based stop is selected, otherwise close − current-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiple) OR high ≥ close + (close − (opening-range low if range-based stop is selected, otherwise close − current-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiple)) × configured target multiple OR session just ended
Exit: Short, high ≥ (opening-range high if range-based stop is selected, otherwise close + current-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiple) OR low ≤ close − ((opening-range high if range-based stop is selected, otherwise close + current-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiple) − close) × configured target multiple OR session just ended
