Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed swing high and low, and retain the previous swing highs and lows.
Calculation: Uptrend = latest swing low > previous swing low; if no previous swing low, close > latest swing low. If neither previous swing low nor previous swing high exists, use close > EMA.
Calculation: Downtrend = latest swing high < previous swing high; if no previous swing high, close < latest swing high. If neither previous swing low nor previous swing high exists, use close < EMA.
Calculation: Uptrend Fibonacci retracement level = latest swing high − (latest swing high − latest swing low) × configured retracement fraction.
Calculation: Downtrend Fibonacci retracement level = latest swing low + (latest swing high − latest swing low) × configured retracement fraction.
Long: Uptrend AND close is within the configured percentage tolerance of any enabled uptrend Fibonacci retracement level AND (RSI is below its configured threshold or RSI confirmation is disabled) AND (volume is at least the configured multiple of its moving average or volume confirmation is disabled) AND trend mode permits uptrend signals
Short: Downtrend AND close is within the configured percentage tolerance of any enabled downtrend Fibonacci retracement level AND (RSI is above its configured threshold or RSI confirmation is disabled) AND (volume is at least the configured multiple of its moving average or volume confirmation is disabled) AND trend mode permits downtrend signals
