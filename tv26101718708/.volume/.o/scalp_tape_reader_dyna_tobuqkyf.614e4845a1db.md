Calculation: Buy pressure percentage = EMA over a smoothing lookback of the sum of buy-weighted volume over an accumulation lookback divided by max(1, summed buy-weighted plus sell-weighted volume); per bar, buy-weighted volume is volume × |close − open| / (high − low) when close > open, otherwise 0, and sell-weighted volume is the same when close < open, otherwise 0; use 0 weight for a zero-range bar
Long: Buy pressure percentage > dominance threshold OR buy pressure percentage > extreme threshold AND previous bar's buy pressure percentage <= extreme threshold OR previous bar's buy pressure percentage > dominance threshold
Short: Buy pressure percentage < 1 − dominance threshold OR buy pressure percentage < 1 − extreme threshold AND previous bar's buy pressure percentage >= 1 − extreme threshold OR previous bar's buy pressure percentage < 1 − dominance threshold
