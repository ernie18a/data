Long: On the source timeframe’s last completed bar, close crosses above the high of the latest confirmed pivot-low candle OR (in ICT Candle Run mode) close is above the selected MSS swing high, the MSS direction is not already bullish, and the surrounding swing sequence is valid (the next older swing low is below the intervening swing high and their timestamps differ); OR close is below the latest confirmed pivot-low candle high but no farther than ATR × the proximity multiplier below it; OR low > high[2] (bullish FVG; on the current timeframe require a confirmed bar and pass the enabled time-of-day gap and volume ranking filters); OR, for an active bullish breaker before it is retest-signaled or broken, open is between its midpoint and top and close > top. Apply the New York session filter to source-timeframe conditions when enabled.
Short: On the source timeframe’s last completed bar, close crosses below the low of the latest confirmed pivot-high candle OR (in ICT Candle Run mode) close is below the selected MSS swing low, the MSS direction is not already bearish, and the surrounding swing sequence is valid (the next older swing high is above the intervening swing low and their timestamps differ); OR close is above the latest confirmed pivot-high candle low but no farther than ATR × the proximity multiplier above it; OR high < low[2] (bearish FVG; on the current timeframe require a confirmed bar and pass the enabled time-of-day gap and volume ranking filters); OR, for an active bearish breaker before it is retest-signaled or broken, open is between its bottom and midpoint and close < bottom. Apply the New York session filter to source-timeframe conditions when enabled.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the configured number of bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot candle’s high and low. Source-timeframe pivots use completed source bars.
Calculation: Time-of-day gap/volume ranking = z-score of the three-candle FVG height and middle-candle volume against prior observations in the same time-of-day bucket; each must meet its configured threshold when ranking is enabled. Source FVGs use completed source bars; current-timeframe FVGs use confirmed current bars.
Calculation: MSS swing sequence = alternating confirmed swing highs and lows; for a bullish break use the selected swing high and its surrounding older low and intervening high, and for a bearish break use the selected swing low and its surrounding older high and intervening low. MSS pivots use the configured left lookback and one right bar.
