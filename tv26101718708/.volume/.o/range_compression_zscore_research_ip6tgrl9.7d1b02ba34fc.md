Calculation: Research range = highest high minus lowest low over the selected research-timeframe window of completed bars.
Calculation: Range percentage = research range divided by the last completed research-timeframe close, multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Range percentile = percentile rank of the range percentage against its historical sample of completed research-timeframe windows.
Calculation: Range z-score = (range percentage − historical mean range percentage) / historical standard deviation of range percentage; the historical sample is longer than the range window.
Calculation: Drift ratio = absolute change from the first to last close in the completed research-timeframe window / research range.
Calculation: Regression slope % = absolute one-bar change in the linear regression of closes over the completed research-timeframe window × window length / last completed research-timeframe close × 100.
Calculation: Daily-range CV = standard deviation / mean of individual high-low ranges in the completed research-timeframe window.
Calculation: Largest-day ratio = largest individual high-low range in the completed research-timeframe window / research range.
Calculation: Upper trigger = research-range high + configured buffer; lower trigger = research-range low − configured buffer. The buffer is configured as a fraction of ATR from the last completed research-timeframe bar, a percentage of its close, a tick count, or zero.
Long: Completed execution-bar close >= upper trigger AND compression qualifies: research range > 0 AND configured percentile/z-score tightness rule passes AND (sideways filter disabled OR drift ratio <= configured maximum AND regression slope % <= configured maximum) AND (range-stability filter disabled OR daily-range CV <= configured maximum AND largest-day ratio <= configured maximum) AND configured breakout-bar range percentage, execution-timeframe ATR ratio, and volume/SMA filters pass.
Short: Completed execution-bar close <= lower trigger AND compression qualifies: research range > 0 AND configured percentile/z-score tightness rule passes AND (sideways filter disabled OR drift ratio <= configured maximum AND regression slope % <= configured maximum) AND (range-stability filter disabled OR daily-range CV <= configured maximum AND largest-day ratio <= configured maximum) AND configured breakout-bar range percentage, execution-timeframe ATR ratio, and volume/SMA filters pass.
