Calculation: Reference line = a moving average of the selected price or volume source.
Calculation: Weighted line = the same moving average and lookback applied to the source adjusted by the oscillator weight; the adjustment is capped by a selected multiple of the reference source's standard deviation or ATR.
Calculation: Oscillator weight = the selected oscillator's distance from its centerline divided by its greatest historical distance on that side of the centerline; average weights when multiple oscillators are selected, then optionally multiply by relative-volume percentile rank.
Calculation: Divergence = either the reference and weighted lines change in opposite directions, or the oscillator weight and close change in opposite directions, according to the selected detection mode.
Calculation: Divergence channel = set its upper and lower levels from the selected price sources when a divergence occurs; while unbreached, expand levels on later divergences that extend them. Set its state bullish when the selected breach price exceeds the upper level, bearish when it falls below the lower level, and neutral until either breach; start a new channel after a breach when a later divergence occurs.
Long: Weighted line > reference line OR divergence channel is bullish; strong bullish state when the weighted line > reference line, close > reference line, and both lines rise from the previous bar, or when the divergence channel is bullish and the oscillator channel is strongly bullish.
Short: Weighted line < reference line OR divergence channel is bearish; strong bearish state when the weighted line < reference line, close < reference line, and both lines fall from the previous bar, or when the divergence channel is bearish and the oscillator channel is strongly bearish.
