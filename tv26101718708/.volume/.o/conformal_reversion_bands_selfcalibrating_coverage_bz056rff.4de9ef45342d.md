Calculation: Fair-value center = selected session VWAP, EMA, or rolling median; VWAP falls back to rolling median when volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Outer band = fair-value center ± the outer target-coverage quantile of absolute price deviations, normalized by lagged EMA absolute deviation and floored by an ATR-based minimum width. Seed the quantile from a rolling score window, then update it on confirmed bars with an online quantile tracker; use the prior-bar calibrated width to test the current bar.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = selected RSI or price deviation from the fair-value center divided by lagged local deviation scale. Compare consecutive confirmed pivot highs or lows, using configured left and right pivot lookbacks.
Long: Price < lower outer band OR (previous bar price < its lower outer band AND current price >= current lower outer band, if close-back-inside confirmation is enabled) OR (price at latest pivot low < price at previous pivot low AND oscillator at latest pivot low > oscillator at previous pivot low).
Short: Price > upper outer band OR (previous bar price > its upper outer band AND current price <= current upper outer band, if close-back-inside confirmation is enabled) OR (price at latest pivot high > price at previous pivot high AND oscillator at latest pivot high < oscillator at previous pivot high).
