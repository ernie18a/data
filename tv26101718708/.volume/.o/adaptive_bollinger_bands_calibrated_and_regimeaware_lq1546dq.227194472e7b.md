Calculation: Adaptive length = configured fixed length or rounded dominant-cycle period × multiplier, clipped to configured bounds. Main bands = selected rolling basis (SMA, EMA, volume-weighted average, or median) plus/minus selected deviation (standard deviation, median absolute deviation, asymmetric semideviation, empirical quantile, or EWMA volatility), adjusted by the tail and containment multipliers; inner and outer bands use shorter and longer multipliers than the main bands. Use current chart bars.
Calculation: %B = (close − lower main band) / (upper main band − lower main band); band z = distance from basis divided by the corresponding upward or downward deviation.
Calculation: Range regime = ADX is not above its threshold, or efficiency ratio is not above its threshold, and volatility-cluster z is not above its threshold. Trend regime = ADX and efficiency ratio are both above their thresholds; bullish trend if close is above the regime EMA, bearish otherwise. Volatility-cluster z uses an exponentially decaying sum of absolute log returns, normalized against its rolling mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Band-walk = consecutive closes above the main upper band or below the main lower band for at least the configured streak length.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = current volume exceeds 1.2 times its 20-bar average; if volume confirmation is disabled, or volume is unavailable, it passes.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = sign of %B − 0.5, using 20-bar SMA and standard-deviation bands. Calculate on each of four configured timeframes and on the configured higher timeframe, using lookahead-off values (the higher-timeframe bar still forming in real time).
Calculation: Squeeze fire = bandwidth rises after a squeeze ended within the configured expansion window; direction is long when close is above the basis and short otherwise. Squeeze uses the rolling percentile rank of bandwidth.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the latest confirmed %B pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed %B pivot low while its corresponding close is lower. Bearish divergence = the latest confirmed %B pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed %B pivot high while its corresponding close is higher.
Long: (range regime AND high pierces or touches the main upper band and closes below it AND prior %B ≥ 1 or prior band z ≥ the main-band multiplier OR range regime AND low pierces or touches the main lower band and closes above it AND prior %B ≤ 0 or prior band z ≤ negative main-band multiplier) AND volume confirmation; OR trend regime is bullish AND (upward band-walk OR close is above the inner upper band and above prior close) AND volume confirmation AND higher-timeframe direction ≥ 0; OR squeeze fire is upward; OR bullish divergence.
Short: (range regime AND high pierces or touches the main upper band and closes below it AND prior %B ≥ 1 or prior band z ≥ the main-band multiplier OR range regime AND low pierces or touches the main lower band and closes above it AND prior %B ≤ 0 or prior band z ≤ negative main-band multiplier) AND volume confirmation; OR trend regime is bearish AND (downward band-walk OR close is below the inner lower band and below prior close) AND volume confirmation AND higher-timeframe direction ≤ 0; OR squeeze fire is downward; OR bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop or high ≥ target 2. Initialize stop by the selected mode: below the outer lower band by the configured ATR pad; below entry by ATR × (1 + pad); or below entry by the distance from close to basis (falling back to 2 ATR when half-life is unavailable) plus the ATR pad. For fade entries target 1 is basis and target 2 is inner upper band; otherwise target 1 and target 2 are entry plus 1.5 and 3 times initial risk. Once high ≥ target 1 while stop is below entry, move stop to entry.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop or low ≤ target 2. Initialize stop by the selected mode: above the outer upper band by the configured ATR pad; above entry by ATR × (1 + pad); or above entry by the distance from close to basis (falling back to 2 ATR when half-life is unavailable) plus the ATR pad. For fade entries target 1 is basis and target 2 is inner lower band; otherwise target 1 and target 2 are entry minus 1.5 and 3 times initial risk. Once low ≤ target 1 while stop is above entry, move stop to entry.
