Calculation: Bullish order blocks = Track a state initialized to 0. On each bar, set it to 0 if the high from the lookback bars ago is greater than the highest high over the current lookback bars; otherwise set it to 1 if the low from the lookback bars ago is less than the lowest low over the current lookback bars; otherwise keep its prior value. When volume at the lookback bars ago is a pivot high confirmed by the lookback bars on both sides, store a bullish zone if the state is 1, with bottom equal to that bar’s low and top equal to the average of its high and low. Keep zones newest first.
Calculation: Bearish order blocks = Using the same lookback as bullish order blocks, store a bearish zone when a confirmed volume pivot high occurs while the state is 0, with top equal to that bar’s high and bottom equal to the average of its high and low. Keep zones newest first.
Calculation: Mitigation target = Lowest low over the lookback bars for bullish zones and highest high over the lookback bars for bearish zones; if mitigation uses closes, use lowest close and highest close instead.
Long: The bullish mitigation target is below the bottom of at least one stored bullish zone AND close > open AND close > low + (high - low) * 0.5; remove every bullish zone whose bottom is above the bullish mitigation target.
Short: The bearish mitigation target is above the top of at least one stored bearish zone AND close < open AND close < high - (high - low) * 0.5; remove every bearish zone whose top is below the bearish mitigation target.
