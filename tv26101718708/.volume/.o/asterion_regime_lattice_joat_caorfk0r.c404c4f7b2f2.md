Calculation: Trend displacement = +1 when close > fast EMA > slow EMA and the close’s linear-regression slope is positive; -1 for the inverse; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Momentum direction = +1 when ROC > 0, RSI is above its midpoint, and efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; -1 when ROC < 0, RSI is below its midpoint, and efficiency ratio exceeds its threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Structure direction = +1 when close is above the upper threshold of its position in the recent high-low range; -1 when below the lower threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volume direction = +1 when standardized volume impulse exceeds its positive threshold and OBV slope is positive; -1 when impulse is below its negative threshold and OBV slope is negative; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Volatility composite = the mean of ATR divided by its moving average and Garman-Klass realized volatility divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = the mean of trend, momentum, volatility, directional-movement, and ADX biases from each of two higher timeframes, using their bars still forming. Trend bias is positive when close > fast EMA > slow EMA and slope is positive, negative for the inverse, otherwise zero; momentum bias follows ROC sign; volatility bias is positive above its expansion threshold, negative below its compression threshold, otherwise zero; directional-movement bias follows the sign of +DI minus -DI; ADX bias is positive above its strong threshold, negative below its weak threshold, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Regime score = a weighted composite of trend displacement, momentum direction, structure direction, volume direction, volatility direction, and higher-timeframe bias, scaled to a signed score. Volatility direction follows the majority direction of those other components when volatility expands, opposes it when volatility contracts, and is zero otherwise.
Long: Regime score >= soft bullish threshold
Short: Regime score <= soft bearish threshold
