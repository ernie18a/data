Calculation: Trend rating = mean of +1 when close is above, -1 when below, and 0 when equal to each of six progressively longer simple moving averages, six progressively longer exponential moving averages, a short Hull moving average, a volume weighted moving average, and an Ichimoku component; the Ichimoku component is +1 when the cloud is bullish and its stated close/conversion/base-line conditions are met, -1 for the inverse bearish conditions, otherwise 0. Use the current bar and its preceding bar. For requested higher-timeframe ratings, use the current forming bar in real time and the most recently completed bar in historical data.
Calculation: Bollinger %B = (close - (SMA - 2 × standard deviation)) / (4 × standard deviation); use the relevant series’ daily bars, including the current forming daily bar in real time.
Calculation: Breadth sell filter = (front VIX futures daily close - VIX daily close) / VIX daily close ≤ threshold AND the three-bar average change in the daily SMA of the market-breadth series is negative AND (VVIX daily %B ≥ threshold OR VXX daily %B ≥ threshold) AND neither the current nor prior daily SKEW %B is above its threshold. Use the current forming daily bar in real time.
Calculation: Overbought adjustment = when daily MFI and RSI of the lead equity-index futures series both exceed their thresholds, use the adjusted rating threshold for each of the four trend ratings; otherwise use each rating’s regular threshold. Use the current forming daily bar in real time.
Long: In MA Rating mode, NOT (any of the four current 2-hour trend ratings ≤ its applicable threshold OR the breadth sell filter) AND, for each rating, ((current rating > its applicable threshold AND preceding rating > its applicable threshold) OR current rating ≥ 0). In EMA Trend mode, each of the four current 1-hour closes is above its 1-hour 171-period EMA.
Short: In MA Rating mode, any of the four current 2-hour trend ratings ≤ its applicable threshold OR the breadth sell filter. In EMA Trend mode, any of the four current 1-hour closes is at or below its 1-hour 171-period EMA.
Exit: Long, short condition when short entries are disabled.
Exit: Long, end datetime reached.
Exit: Short, long condition.
Exit: Short, end datetime reached.
