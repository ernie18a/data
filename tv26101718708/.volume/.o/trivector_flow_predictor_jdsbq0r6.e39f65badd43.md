Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute source change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback; clamp to [0,1]. Source is HLC3.
Calculation: Volatility unit = the largest of ATR, 0.70 × mean Parkinson volatility, and 0.70 × mean Garman–Klass volatility, multiplied by the market-profile volatility factor.
Calculation: Fused core = a short EMA of a weighted blend of ZLEMA, DEMA, HMA, KAMA, and a two-pole smoother of HLC3; slow core = EMA of the fused core over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Pressure = short EMA of the fused core's one-bar change divided by volatility unit. Phase = short EMA of (close − fused core) / volatility unit; phase velocity = phase − prior phase. Extension = absolute phase.
Calculation: Pulse rail = volatility-adjusted trailing line around the fused core. In bullish pulse, rail trails upward at the greater of the current lower band and prior rail, flipping bearish when close falls below prior rail; in bearish pulse, it trails downward at the lesser of the current upper band and prior rail, flipping bullish when close rises above prior rail. Band distance adjusts with ADX and efficiency ratio.
Calculation: Bullish candle = close > open, body / range ≥ minimum body ratio, and (close − low) / range ≥ minimum close position. Bearish candle = close < open, body / range ≥ minimum body ratio, and (high − close) / range ≥ minimum close position.
Calculation: Displacement = candle body / volatility unit meets the minimum displacement, with close in the directional 36% of the range.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low breaks below the prior lookback low by a volatility buffer, close reclaims that low, bullish candle, positive phase velocity, and extension within the sweep limit (unless late-entry filtering is off). Bearish sweep = high breaks above the prior lookback high by a volatility buffer, close falls back below that high, bearish candle, negative phase velocity, and extension within the sweep limit (unless late-entry filtering is off).
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high by a volatility buffer; bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low by a volatility buffer. Swing points use equal left/right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish retest = within the retest memory after a bullish structure break, low reaches the broken high within volatility tolerance and close is above that level and open. Bearish retest = within the retest memory after a bearish structure break, high reaches the broken low within volatility tolerance and close is below that level and open.
Calculation: Bullish FVG impulse = low > high two bars earlier, bullish candle, and body / volatility unit meets the FVG displacement threshold. Bearish FVG impulse = high < low two bars earlier, bearish candle, and body / volatility unit meets that threshold.
Calculation: Long trigger = any of: (phase reached below negative pullback threshold during setup memory, phase crosses above zero, close > fused core, phase velocity > 0); (close breaks above prior compression range plus volatility buffer, prior bar was compressed, bullish displacement, phase velocity > 0); bullish sweep; bullish pulse-rail flip with close > fused core, positive phase velocity, and bullish close position; bullish structure break with close > fused core and positive phase velocity; bullish FVG impulse with close > fused core and positive phase velocity; bullish retest with close > fused core and nonnegative phase velocity. Apply the corresponding extension limit to each trigger when late-entry filtering is on.
Calculation: Short trigger = directional inverse of Long trigger: phase reached above positive pullback threshold and crosses below zero with close < fused core and negative phase velocity; break below prior compressed range with bearish displacement; bearish sweep; bearish pulse-rail flip with close < fused core and negative phase velocity; bearish structure break, FVG impulse, or retest with close < fused core and nonpositive phase velocity. Apply the corresponding extension limit to each trigger when late-entry filtering is on.
Calculation: Long and short scores = capped 0–100 confluence scores. Each adds directional EMA, core/pressure, rail, directional movement, higher-timeframe EMA agreement, trigger, phase, candle, displacement, delta-volume, ADX, efficiency, fractal-energy, volume, extension, and volatility-expansion contributions, and subtracts chop/fractal-energy penalties. Base scores omit trigger, displacement, and expansion contributions. Dynamic minimum score adjusts the configured minimum for chop, impulse, and compression. Higher-timeframe agreement uses two higher timeframes, one shorter and one longer, comparing each current (potentially still-forming) bar's close with its EMA of the same lookback.
Calculation: Entry filters = candle-quality filter (allowing specified strong triggers), efficiency threshold or any raw trigger, optional close vs EMA, optional close vs VWAP, optional volume / average-volume threshold, optional higher-timeframe agreement minimum, optional prediction direction and confidence (sweeps exempt), noise floor, and optional confirmed-bar requirement.
Long: Bullish sweep OR (long trigger AND entry filters AND cooldown and minimum price movement since the prior major signal AND same-direction restriction passes AND long score ≥ dynamic minimum AND long score ≥ short score + dominance threshold AND no higher-scoring simultaneous short candidate).
Short: Bearish sweep OR (short trigger AND entry filters AND cooldown and minimum price movement since the prior major signal AND same-direction restriction passes AND short score ≥ dynamic minimum AND short score ≥ long score + dominance threshold AND no higher-scoring simultaneous long candidate).
Calculation: On entry, set entry price to close. Long initial stop = lower of pulse rail and entry minus minimum stop distance; short initial stop = higher of pulse rail and entry plus minimum stop distance. Risk = greater of stop distance and minimum stop distance. Targets are entry plus (long) or minus (short) risk multiplied by the TP1 and TP2 reward/risk parameters. While active, trail the long stop upward using the greater of its prior value and the lower of rail or close minus a volatility buffer; trail the short stop downward using the lesser of its prior value and the higher of rail or close plus a volatility buffer. After TP1 is touched, optionally move stop at least to entry.
Exit: Long, confirmed bar AND (short signal OR close ≤ active stop OR (confidence-loss exit enabled AND long score < confidence threshold AND short score > long score) OR (TP2 exit enabled AND close ≥ long TP2)).
Exit: Short, confirmed bar AND (long signal OR close ≥ active stop OR (confidence-loss exit enabled AND short score < confidence threshold AND long score > short score) OR (TP2 exit enabled AND close ≤ short TP2)).
