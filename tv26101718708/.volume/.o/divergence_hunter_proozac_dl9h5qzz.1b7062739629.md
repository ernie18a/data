Calculation: Confirmed price pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookback bars; confirm after the right-lookback bars close.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookback bars; confirm after the right-lookback bars close.
Long: At each newly confirmed price pivot low, compared with the preceding confirmed price pivot low, at least one enabled oscillator (RSI, MACD line, or OBV) has either (current pivot low < preceding pivot low AND its value at the current pivot bar > its value at the preceding pivot bar) OR (current pivot low > preceding pivot low AND its value at the current pivot bar < its value at the preceding pivot bar)
