Calculation: Volatility Z-score = (ATR(1) − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bar delta ratio = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, a small positive value).
Calculation: Prior session levels = high, low, and their midpoint from the last completed relevant session: Asia during London, London during NY pre-market and regular hours, or NY regular during after-hours.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low two bars earlier that is higher/lower than the two preceding and two following bars; use the most recent confirmed swing.
Calculation: Bull pre-signal score = weighted sum of bullish spring, cumulative-delta divergence, RSI divergence, stop hunt, no-supply, positive delta imbalance, elevated volume ratio, bullish VWAP/EMA/optional higher-timeframe alignment, proximity to a prior rolling high, up bar, prior-session-low fade, prior-session-high break, and bullish session bias conditions.
Calculation: Bear pre-signal score = weighted sum of bearish upthrust, cumulative-delta divergence, RSI divergence, stop hunt, no-demand, negative delta imbalance, elevated volume ratio, bearish VWAP/EMA/optional higher-timeframe alignment, proximity to a prior rolling low, down bar, prior-session-high fade, prior-session-low break, and bearish session bias conditions.
