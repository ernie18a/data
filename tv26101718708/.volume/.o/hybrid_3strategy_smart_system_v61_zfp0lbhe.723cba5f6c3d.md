Calculation: Bull FVG = current low > high two bars ago; bear FVG = current high < low two bars ago. Arm the zone only when its gap meets the configured ATR size threshold and its matching trend and market-strength filters pass; keep it armed until touched, used, or older than its configured maximum age.
Calculation: Bull FVG touch = an armed bull zone from an earlier bar overlaps the current bar; bear FVG touch = an armed bear zone from an earlier bar overlaps the current bar.
Calculation: High volume = volume > volume moving average × configured volume multiplier.
Calculation: Market trending = ADX > configured minimum AND absolute difference between the EMA and its configured earlier value >= ATR × configured slope minimum.
Calculation: Bull trend = close > EMA AND EMA > its configured earlier value AND (higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA OR higher-timeframe filter disabled) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in session) AND absolute distance from close to EMA <= ATR × configured maximum.
Calculation: Bear trend = close < EMA AND EMA < its configured earlier value AND (higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA OR higher-timeframe filter disabled) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is in session) AND absolute distance from close to EMA <= ATR × configured maximum. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in backtests.
Calculation: Bull rejection = close > open AND lower wick >= body size × configured fraction; bear rejection = close < open AND upper wick >= body size × configured fraction.
Calculation: FVG long score = 20 if bull trend + 15 if higher-timeframe bullish filter passes + 15 if market trending + 15 if bull FVG touched + 10 if close > open + 10 if high volume + 15 × min(current bull FVG gap size / ATR, 2). Current bull FVG gap size is zero unless a bull FVG forms on this bar. FVG short score is the mirrored calculation using bear conditions and current bear FVG gap size.
Calculation: Volume long score = 15 if close > EMA + 20 if RSI < configured oversold level, otherwise 10 + 20 if high volume + 15 if lower wick > body size, otherwise 8 + 15 if close > armed trigger + 10 if close > open + 5 × min(volume / volume moving average, 2). Volume short score mirrors this using close < EMA, RSI above configured overbought level, upper wick, and close < armed trigger/open.
Calculation: Trend retest long score = 20 if bull trend + 20 if market trending + 15 if higher-timeframe bullish filter passes + 15 if low <= armed breakout level + 15 if close > that level + 10 if close > open + 5 if high volume. Trend retest short score mirrors this using bear conditions, high >= level, close < level, and close < open.
Long: On a confirmed bar, after the configured cooldown since the last executed trade, (bull FVG touched AND bull trend AND close > open AND close > zone bottom AND FVG long score >= configured threshold) OR (a bullish exhaustion bar occurred within its configured validity window and close > its high trigger AND close > open AND volume long score >= configured threshold) OR (a bullish swing-high breakout occurred with bull trend and market trending, and within its configured retest window low <= breakout level AND close > breakout level AND close > open AND trend retest long score >= configured threshold). Bullish exhaustion bar = close < EMA AND high volume AND RSI below configured level AND close > open AND lower wick > body size × configured fraction. Bullish swing-high breakout = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high. Executed long entry requires flat position and either a new long setup or a pending long setup still within its validity window, with absolute distance from close to pending entry <= ATR × configured maximum and close >= EMA × configured factor.
Short: On a confirmed bar, after the configured cooldown since the last executed trade, (bear FVG touched AND bear trend AND close < open AND close < zone top AND FVG short score >= configured threshold) OR (a bearish exhaustion bar occurred within its configured validity window and close < its low trigger AND close < open AND volume short score >= configured threshold) OR (a bearish swing-low breakout occurred with bear trend and market trending, and within its configured retest window high >= breakout level AND close < breakout level AND close < open AND trend retest short score >= configured threshold). Bearish exhaustion bar = close > EMA AND high volume AND RSI above configured level AND close < open AND upper wick > body size × configured fraction. Bearish swing-low breakout = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. Executed short entry requires flat position and either a new short setup or a pending short setup still within its validity window, with absolute distance from close to pending entry <= ATR × configured maximum and close <= EMA × configured factor.
Exit: long, price <= stop loss or price >= take profit. Exit: short, price >= stop loss or price <= take profit.
