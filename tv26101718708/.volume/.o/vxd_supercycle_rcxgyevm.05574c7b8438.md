Calculation: Long trend zone = 2-bar EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend is bullish AND close > the smoothed linear regression of close; the trend line is rolling VWAP when selected and otherwise the short EMA.
Calculation: Long filtered trend zone = 2-bar EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend is bullish AND the smoothed linear regression of close is rising over three bars AND the smoothed average of (open + prior close)/2 is rising.
Calculation: Short trend zone = 2-bar EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend is bearish AND close < the smoothed linear regression of close; the trend line is rolling VWAP when selected and otherwise the short EMA.
Calculation: Short filtered trend zone = 2-bar EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend is bearish AND the smoothed linear regression of close is falling over three bars AND the smoothed average of (open + prior close)/2 is falling.
Calculation: Long PPDD marking = current candle closes above the prior candle's high after the prior candle closed below its open, AND the lower of the current and prior lows is below the latest confirmed pivot low (or the prior bar's pivot-low level), AND current close is above that pivot-low level.
Calculation: Short PPDD marking = current candle closes below the prior candle's low after the prior candle closed above its open, AND the higher of the current and prior highs is above the latest confirmed pivot high (or the prior bar's pivot-high level), AND current close is below that pivot-high level.
Long: close > latest confirmed pivot high AND close > Supertrend AND (long trend zone OR long filtered trend zone) AND (RSI MA crosses above 50 while RSI > RSI MA OR long PPDD marking)
Short: close < latest confirmed pivot low AND close < Supertrend AND (short trend zone OR short filtered trend zone) AND (RSI MA crosses below 50 while RSI < RSI MA OR short PPDD marking)
Exit: long, (2-bar EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend is bearish AND close < latest confirmed pivot low AND close < smoothed linear regression of close) OR (low <= stop at the lowest pivot low over a lookback longer than the pivot confirmation lookback) OR (high >= entry price + entry price × (entry price − stop) / entry price × reward multiple, while low > entry price)
Exit: short, (2-bar EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend is bullish AND close > latest confirmed pivot high AND close > smoothed linear regression of close) OR (high >= stop at the highest pivot high over a lookback longer than the pivot confirmation lookback) OR (low <= entry price − entry price × (stop − entry price) / entry price × reward multiple, while high < entry price)
