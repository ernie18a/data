Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted mean of the configured price source since the selected anchor, including the current bar; reset at a confirmed swing high or low, daily session start, or configured manual date, according to the selected anchor mode.
Calculation: Reclaim follow-through probability = online logistic probability from six cross-side features: clamp(side × (close − close 3 bars ago) / ATR(14), −3, 3); clamp(side × (fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR(14), −3, 3), with fast EMA shorter than slow EMA; clamp((RSI − 50) / 50 × side, −1, 1); clamp(volume-weighted VWAP standard deviation / its 50-bar SMA − 1, −1, 3); clamp(volume / its 20-bar SMA − 1, −1, 3); and clamp(bars since anchor / 50, 0, 3). Initialize logistic weights at zero. On each confirmed bar, update each open reclaim setup's age; resolve it as success if its close-based favorable excursion reaches the configured ATR multiple, otherwise as failure if close crosses VWAP against its side, otherwise as failure upon reaching the configured horizon. Train once per resolved setup by one logistic gradient step on its stored features and outcome, using the configured learning rate. At a VWAP cross, calculate probability using current weights and that cross's features.
Long: close crosses above Anchored VWAP AND bar index exceeds the configured warmup count AND Reclaim follow-through probability is at least the configured threshold.
Short: close crosses below Anchored VWAP AND bar index exceeds the configured warmup count AND Reclaim follow-through probability is at least the configured threshold.
