Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its moving average
Calculation: Candle body share = absolute(close − open) divided by max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: ATR expansion = max(high − low, minimum price increment) divided by ATR
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND candle body share ≥ its parameter AND ATR expansion ≥ its parameter AND relative volume ≥ its parameter
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND candle body share ≥ its parameter AND ATR expansion ≥ its parameter AND relative volume ≥ its parameter
Calculation: Bullish imbalance zone = on a confirmed bar, low > high two bars earlier, bullish displacement, and zone height ≥ its parameter times minimum price increment; zone bounds are high two bars earlier to current low
Calculation: Bearish imbalance zone = on a confirmed bar, high < low two bars earlier, bearish displacement, and zone height ≥ its parameter times minimum price increment; zone bounds are current high to low two bars earlier
Calculation: Delta pressure = exponential moving average of ((2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) × volume / max(volume moving average, 1) × 100; bullish delta = delta pressure > 5 and rising, bearish delta = delta pressure < −5 and falling
Calculation: A zone is touched when current high ≥ its bottom and current low ≤ its top; its midpoint is the average of its bounds. A bullish zone is failed when close < its bottom − 0.05 × ATR; a bearish zone is failed when close > its top + 0.05 × ATR. Failure remains true once reached.
Calculation: Rejection points = 10 when a bullish zone is touched, close > midpoint, close > open, and lower wick / bar range > 0.18 (bullish); or when a bearish zone is touched, close < midpoint, close < open, and upper wick / bar range > 0.18 (bearish). Otherwise points = 6 when a bullish zone is touched, close > bottom, close > open, and (close > midpoint or low ≤ midpoint), or when a bearish zone is touched, close < top, close < open, and (close < midpoint or high ≥ midpoint); otherwise 0.
Calculation: Zone score = clamp((top − bottom) / max(ATR, minimum price increment), 0.05, 1.20) / 1.20 × 14 + clamp((ATR expansion − displacement ATR parameter) / 1.50, 0, 1) × 13 + clamp((relative volume − 1) / 1.75, 0, 1) × 16 + freshness × 18 + VWAP score × 16 + 13 if matching delta is true + rejection points. Freshness = clamp(1 − zone age / maximum zone age, 0, 1). VWAP score for long = 0.85 if close ≥ VWAP, else 0.70 if low ≤ lower VWAP deviation band, else 0.35; for short = 0.85 if close ≤ VWAP, else 0.70 if high ≥ upper VWAP deviation band, else 0.35. If VWAP scoring is disabled, use 0.55 for either side.
Long: A touched, nonfailed bearish imbalance zone has a long setup and score ≥ signal threshold; the setup requires close < zone top, close < open, and (close < midpoint or high ≥ midpoint), OR the bearish-zone rejection condition; relative to all qualifying long, short, and failure candidates, this zone has the highest score. Also require signal cooldown: no earlier signal, or current bar index − last signal bar > cooldown parameter.
Short: A touched, nonfailed bullish imbalance zone has a short setup and score ≥ signal threshold; the setup requires close > zone bottom, close > open, and (close > midpoint or low ≤ midpoint), OR the bullish-zone rejection condition; relative to all qualifying long, short, and failure candidates, this zone has the highest score. Also require signal cooldown: no earlier signal, or current bar index − last signal bar > cooldown parameter.
Short: A bullish imbalance zone fails for the first time, zone age > 5 bars, and relative volume ≥ its parameter; its zone score (using the bullish score) is ≥ signal threshold or is the highest-scoring qualifying candidate after flooring its comparison score at the signal threshold. Also require signal cooldown.
Long: A bearish imbalance zone fails for the first time, zone age > 5 bars, and relative volume ≥ its parameter; its zone score (using the bearish score) is ≥ signal threshold or is the highest-scoring qualifying candidate after flooring its comparison score at the signal threshold. Also require signal cooldown.
