Calculation: Cloud high = maximum of the fast EMA, the EMA-smoothed linear regression of close, and the AlphaTrend value from two bars ago; Cloud low = their minimum. AlphaTrend is the Supertrend when the selected order condition is either cloud-and-pivot mode; in Cloud-MA mode it is a trailing line updated from ATR bands according to whether RSI is at least 50.
Calculation: Andean bull and bear = square roots of, respectively, the smoothed lower and upper second moments of open and close minus the square of the corresponding smoothed first moment; use the same lookback for both components.
Calculation: Cloud-MA = a trailing line based on Cloud low when Andean bull exceeds Andean bear, or Cloud high when Andean bear exceeds Andean bull; it cannot move against the selected direction. Cloud trend is bullish when Cloud-MA rises for two bars, bearish when it falls for two bars, and otherwise retains its previous direction.
Calculation: Pivot top and pivot bottom = the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, using the same left and right lookback.
Calculation: Swing high = highest pivot top over the sizing lookback; swing low = lowest pivot bottom over the sizing lookback.
Long: During the configured date range, when the selected order condition is cloud-and-pivot, close > pivot top AND close > Cloud high AND long entries are enabled AND no long position is open; OR, when it is cloud-and-pivot-with-linear-regression-filter, close > pivot top AND close > Cloud high AND the smoothed linear regression is rising for two bars AND long entries are enabled AND no long position is open; OR, when it is Cloud-MA, Cloud trend is bullish AND close > Cloud high AND long entries are enabled AND no long position is open. Enter only when this condition changes from false to true and the long re-entry gate is open.
Short: During the configured date range, when the selected order condition is cloud-and-pivot, close < pivot bottom AND close < Cloud low AND short entries are enabled AND no short position is open; OR, when it is cloud-and-pivot-with-linear-regression-filter, close < pivot bottom AND close < Cloud low AND the smoothed linear regression is falling for two bars AND short entries are enabled AND no short position is open; OR, when it is Cloud-MA, Cloud trend is bearish AND close < Cloud low AND short entries are enabled AND no short position is open. Enter only when this condition changes from false to true and the short re-entry gate is open.
Exit: Long, when Cloud trend changes to bearish in Cloud-MA mode, OR close > Cloud high AND close > pivot top in either pivot mode, OR low <= the long stop, OR high >= either long profit target. The initial long stop is the swing low; after the highest high since entry exceeds entry price plus the initial risk, move the stop to entry price. Profit targets are entry price plus initial risk multiplied by each target risk-reward parameter.
Exit: Short, when Cloud trend changes to bullish in Cloud-MA mode, OR close < Cloud low AND close < pivot bottom in either pivot mode, OR high >= the short stop, OR low <= either short profit target. The initial short stop is the swing high; after the lowest low since entry falls below entry price minus the initial risk, move the stop to entry price. Profit targets are entry price minus initial risk multiplied by each target risk-reward parameter.
