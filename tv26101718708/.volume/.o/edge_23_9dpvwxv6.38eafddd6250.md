Calculation: RSI bullish divergence = RSI is below its oversold threshold, current low is below the stored low, and current RSI is above the stored RSI. Store the first low and RSI observed below the threshold; update them only when a later below-threshold RSI is lower. Clear the stored pair when its age exceeds the divergence lookback.
Calculation: RSI bearish divergence = RSI is above its overbought threshold, current high is above the stored high, and current RSI is below the stored RSI. Store the first high and RSI observed above the threshold; update them only when a later above-threshold RSI is higher. Clear the stored pair when its age exceeds the divergence lookback.
Calculation: MFI bullish divergence = MFI is below its oversold threshold, current low is below the stored low, and current MFI is above the stored MFI. Store and update the low and MFI using the same rules as RSI bullish divergence, and clear them after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: MFI bearish divergence = MFI is above its overbought threshold, current high is above the stored high, and current MFI is below the stored MFI. Store and update the high and MFI using the same rules as RSI bearish divergence, and clear them after the divergence lookback.
Calculation: Bullish volume exhaustion = volume is at least the volume-average times its multiplier, the lower wick is at least the specified fraction of the bar's high-low range, and RSI is below its oversold threshold.
Calculation: Bearish volume exhaustion = volume is at least the volume-average times its multiplier, the upper wick is at least the specified fraction of the bar's high-low range, and RSI is above its overbought threshold.
Calculation: Extreme volatility = ATR divided by its moving average, using a longer period for the average than for ATR, exceeds the extreme-volatility threshold.

Use the current bar of each timeframe (forming in realtime; finalized in historical data).

Use the current bar of each timeframe (forming in realtime; finalized in historical data).
