Calculation: Confirmed swing lows/highs = pivot lows/highs with the chosen number of bars on both sides, available only after the right-side bars complete; retain the newest configured maximum per side and discard levels older than the configured maximum age.
Calculation: Sweep level = for a bullish sweep, the highest retained swing low strictly above the bar’s low; for a bearish sweep, the lowest retained swing high strictly below the bar’s high.
Calculation: Sweep depth = swing low − sweep-bar low for bullish sweeps; sweep-bar high − swing high for bearish sweeps.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on the selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Trade risk = clamp the distance from entry close to the swept extreme plus the configured ATR stop buffer, between the configured minimum and maximum ATR risk.
Calculation: Targets = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) trade risk multiplied by each configured target R multiple.
Long: A retained swing low is pierced (low < level, high ≥ level) with sweep depth between the configured ATR minimum and maximum, and price closes above that level on the sweep bar or within the configured reclaim window; AND volume meets the configured spike threshold (unless volume is unavailable or the filter is disabled); AND close is above the last completed higher-timeframe EMA (unless the filter is disabled or unavailable); AND long direction is allowed; AND no bullish and bearish sweep occur together; AND cooldown has elapsed; AND ATR is available; AND no trade is active and no trade exits on this bar.
Short: A retained swing high is pierced (high > level, low ≤ level) with sweep depth between the configured ATR minimum and maximum, and price closes below that level on the sweep bar or within the configured reclaim window; AND volume meets the configured spike threshold (unless volume is unavailable or the filter is disabled); AND close is below the last completed higher-timeframe EMA (unless the filter is disabled or unavailable); AND short direction is allowed; AND no bullish and bearish sweep occur together; AND cooldown has elapsed; AND ATR is available; AND no trade is active and no trade exits on this bar.
Exit: long, high ≥ TP3; or low ≤ stop, unless a target and stop are both touched on this bar and target-first priority applies; or low ≤ stop after TP1 or TP2 was previously reached, closing at the highest reached target; or the configured trade timeout is reached, closing at close.
Exit: short, low ≤ TP3; or high ≥ stop, unless a target and stop are both touched on this bar and target-first priority applies; or high ≥ stop after TP1 or TP2 was previously reached, closing at the highest reached target; or the configured trade timeout is reached, closing at close.
