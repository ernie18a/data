Calculation: Senkou B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback longer than Kijun, shifted forward by the Kijun lookback
Calculation: Cloud top/bottom = greater/lesser of the shifted Senkou A and Senkou B

Calculation: Fractal breakout levels = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low
Calculation: Repeating grid level = close when high or low first moves beyond the prior grid level by the grid gap; otherwise the prior grid level
