Calculation: Volume delta = volume / 2 when close > open, −volume / 2 when close < open, and 0 when close = open
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = running sum of volume delta
Long: Volume delta > 0 AND previous bar’s volume delta <= 0 AND cumulative volume delta > its simple moving average
Short: Volume delta < 0 AND previous bar’s volume delta >= 0 AND cumulative volume delta < its simple moving average
