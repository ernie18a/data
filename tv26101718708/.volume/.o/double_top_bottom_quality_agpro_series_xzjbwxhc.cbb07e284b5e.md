Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the extreme across the pivot lookback bars on both sides; usable only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Double Top neckline = lowest low over the distance between the latest two confirmed pivot highs, measured through the current candidate-detection bar; Double Bottom neckline = highest high over the distance between the latest two confirmed pivot lows, measured through the current candidate-detection bar
Calculation: Pattern quality score = sum of four rounded 0–25 components: equality (max(0, 1 − absolute pivot-price difference / (equality tolerance × ATR)) × 25); break volume (clamp((break-bar volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) / (volume multiplier − 1), 0, 1) × 25); symmetry (shorter pattern leg / longer pattern leg × 25); depth (min(1, pattern depth / ATR / 3) × 25)
Long: latest two confirmed pivot lows are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum allowed bars AND their price difference is at most the equality tolerance times ATR AND the neckline minus the higher pivot low is at least the minimum depth times ATR AND no active Double Bottom is within 2 ATR of the latest pivot low AND (no prior confirmed Double Bottom, or bars since its confirmation exceed cooldown, or its neckline differs from the current neckline by more than 2 ATR) AND before invalidation by a low below the lower pivot low minus 0.1 ATR, close > neckline AND pattern quality score >= minimum score
Short: latest two confirmed pivot highs are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum allowed bars AND their price difference is at most the equality tolerance times ATR AND the lower pivot high minus the neckline is at least the minimum depth times ATR AND no active Double Top is within 2 ATR of the latest pivot high AND (no prior confirmed Double Top, or bars since its confirmation exceed cooldown, or its neckline differs from the current neckline by more than 2 ATR) AND before invalidation by a high above the higher pivot high plus 0.1 ATR, close < neckline AND pattern quality score >= minimum score
Exit: long, high >= Double Bottom measured-move target (neckline + pattern depth)
Exit: short, low <= Double Top measured-move target (neckline − pattern depth)
