Calculation: Williams Alligator Jaw, Teeth, Lips = SMMA of (high + low) / 2 over respectively longer, intermediate, and shorter lookbacks, using each line’s respective backward shift.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator (AO) = shorter-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2 minus longer-lookback SMA of (high + low) / 2; AO falling = current AO < previous AO.
Calculation: Squat bar = (high − low) / volume < previous bar’s (high − low) / volume AND volume > previous bar’s volume.
Long: Within the trading window AND bullish reversal bar (close > (high + low) / 2 AND low equals the lowest low over the lookback AND high < each Williams Alligator line) AND (AO filter disabled OR AO falling) AND (MFI filter disabled OR current or either of the previous two bars is a squat bar) AND ((flat AND high >= bullish reversal bar’s high) OR (one entry open AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 2 threshold) AND high >= bullish reversal bar’s high) OR (two entries open AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 3 threshold) AND high >= bullish reversal bar’s high) OR (three entries open AND low < first entry price × (1 − layer 4 threshold) AND high >= bullish reversal bar’s high)); maximum four entries.
Exit: long, high >= volume-weighted average entry price + ATR × take-profit factor
