Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low from the session start through the opening-range period; use these levels after that period is complete.
Calculation: Bias score = 100 × clamp(0.55 × trend-slope score + 0.25 × trend-stack score + 0.20 × clamp((RSI − 50)/20, −1, 1), −1, 1). Trend-slope score = 0.40 × clamp((fast EMA change / close)/0.0020, −1, 1) + 0.35 × clamp((mid EMA change / close)/0.0015, −1, 1) + 0.25 × clamp((slow EMA change / close)/0.0010, −1, 1). Trend-stack score is 1 when fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA, −1 when fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA, and 0 otherwise. Fast, mid, and slow EMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Tension score = 100 × clamp(0.30 × compression + 0.25 × coupling + 0.20 × persistence + 0.15 × expansion impulse + 0.10 × volume score, 0, 1). Compression = 1 − (0.6 × 100-bar percentile rank of Bollinger-band width + 0.4 × 100-bar percentile rank of ATR/close). Coupling = clamp((|30-bar correlation(1-bar close return, RSI change)| + |30-bar correlation(3-bar close return, (fast EMA − slow EMA)/close)| + |30-bar correlation(1-bar close return, ATR/close)|)/3, 0, 1). Persistence = fraction of the persistence-lookback bars, including the current bar, whose bias score is on the same nonzero side of ±10 as the current bias score; 0 if current bias is between −10 and 10. Expansion impulse = clamp(max(5-bar ATR rate of change, 0)/20, 0, 1). Volume score = clamp(max(volume z-score, 0)/2.5, 0, 1) when the volume filter is enabled; otherwise 0. Volume z-score uses its own lookback. The lookbacks are unspecified.
Long: opening range is complete AND close > opening-range high AND tension score ≥ threshold AND bias score ≥ bull-bias threshold AND fast EMA > mid EMA AND the same qualifying condition was false on the previous bar.
Short: opening range is complete AND close < opening-range low AND tension score ≥ threshold AND bias score ≤ bear-bias threshold AND fast EMA < mid EMA AND the same qualifying condition was false on the previous bar.
Exit: long, (close > opening-range high AND tension score ≥ threshold AND bias score ≥ bull-bias threshold AND fast EMA > mid EMA) AND (bias score < 0 OR close < fast EMA).
Exit: short, (close < opening-range low AND tension score ≥ threshold AND bias score ≤ bear-bias threshold AND fast EMA < mid EMA) AND (bias score > 0 OR close > fast EMA).
