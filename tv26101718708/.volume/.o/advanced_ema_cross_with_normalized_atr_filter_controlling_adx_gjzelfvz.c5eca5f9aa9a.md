Calculation: Normalized ATR = (current ATR − lowest ATR over the rolling ATR lookback) ÷ (highest ATR over the rolling ATR lookback − lowest ATR over the rolling ATR lookback), using current-chart bars.
Calculation: Smoothed close = RMA of close over its lookback, using current-chart bars.
Calculation: Long stop level = entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage); use a lower stop-loss percentage when close > EMA(close, longer lookback), otherwise use a higher percentage.
Calculation: Long target level = entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage); use a lower take-profit percentage when close > EMA(close, longer lookback), otherwise use a higher percentage; the take-profit percentage is twice the stop-loss percentage.
Long: Supertrend direction is bullish AND the short-lookback EMA crosses above the longer-lookback EMA AND normalized ATR > its lower threshold, OR Supertrend direction is bearish AND the short-lookback EMA crosses above the longer-lookback EMA AND normalized ATR > its higher threshold. A cross above means the current short-lookback EMA > the current longer-lookback EMA AND the previous short-lookback EMA ≤ the previous longer-lookback EMA.
Exit: Long, (the short-lookback EMA crosses below the longer-lookback EMA OR smoothed close > its threshold) AND bars since entry ≥ the minimum holding period AND volume > its current-chart-bar SMA × its multiplier, OR low ≤ long stop level, OR high ≥ long target level.
