Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume, adding volume when close > previous close, subtracting it when close < previous close, and otherwise unchanged
Calculation: Scaled OBV = (OBV − its rolling low) / (its rolling high − its rolling low) × rolling maximum volume; use 0 when the rolling high equals the rolling low
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across equal lookbacks on each side; it is available after the following-side bars complete. Use the same lookbacks for price and Scaled OBV pivots.
Calculation: Fast and slow ribbon = short-lookback and longer-lookback EMAs of Scaled OBV
Long: Scaled OBV crosses above its selected moving average OR Fast ribbon > Slow ribbon OR a confirmed Scaled OBV pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed Scaled OBV pivot low is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed price pivot low AND the corresponding Scaled OBV pivot low is higher than the preceding Scaled OBV pivot low within the divergence window
Short: Scaled OBV crosses below its selected moving average OR Fast ribbon < Slow ribbon OR a confirmed Scaled OBV pivot high is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed Scaled OBV pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed price pivot high AND the corresponding Scaled OBV pivot high is lower than the preceding Scaled OBV pivot high within the divergence window
