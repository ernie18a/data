Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset at the selected anchor; use the current forming bar.
Calculation: VWAP standard-deviation bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus one volume-weighted standard deviation; the outer band extensions are 0.25 and 0.5 standard deviations beyond the upper or lower one-standard-deviation band.
Long: current anchored VWAP is available AND previous completed bar close is between 0.25 standard deviations below and above the previous upper band AND current bar volume × close meets the minimum dollar-volume threshold (scaled to bar duration) AND current developing POC (default: close) is between those same previous upper-band bounds AND current developing VAH (default: close) is between the previous upper band and 0.5 standard deviations above it AND the long conditions were false on the previous bar.
Short: current anchored VWAP is available AND previous completed bar close is between 0.25 standard deviations below and above the previous lower band AND current bar volume × close meets the minimum dollar-volume threshold (scaled to bar duration) AND current developing POC (default: close) is between those same previous lower-band bounds AND current developing VAL (default: close) is between the previous lower band and 0.5 standard deviations below it AND the short conditions were false on the previous bar.
