Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) = cumulative signed volume delta from lower-timeframe bars since the current anchor period began, including the current chart bar (which may still be forming)
Calculation: Buy volume = volume × (close − low) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Sell volume = volume × (high − close) ÷ (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Long: current chart-bar CVD close ≥ current chart-bar CVD open OR buy volume > sell volume
Short: current chart-bar CVD close < current chart-bar CVD open OR buy volume ≤ sell volume
