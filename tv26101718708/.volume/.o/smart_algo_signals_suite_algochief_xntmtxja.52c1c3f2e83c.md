Calculation: Swing direction = recursively smoothed close with adaptive alpha from the ratio of net price change to summed absolute changes over a configurable lookback; flip long when close exceeds the baseline plus an ATR-based band, flip short when it falls below the baseline minus that band, otherwise retain the prior direction
Calculation: Scalp direction = flip long when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA and RSI is above its midpoint; flip short when the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA and RSI is below its midpoint; otherwise retain the prior direction
Calculation: Trend trigger = selected swing or scalp direction changes to long or short
Calculation: Reversal trigger = a bullish candle closing above the prior high when its low is below the lower deviation band or RSI is oversold, or a bearish candle closing below the prior low when its high is above the upper deviation band or RSI is overbought; trigger only on the condition turning true
Calculation: Fair value trail = VWMA of typical price with ATR-width bands; in an up state, trail the lower band upward and flip down when close crosses below it; in a down state, trail the upper band downward and flip up when close crosses above it
Calculation: Retest long = up trail state and low touches the upper band while staying above the lower band with a small ATR-width allowance, and close > open and no trend or reversal long trigger; retest short = down trail state and high touches the lower band while staying below the upper band with a small ATR-width allowance, and close < open and no trend or reversal short trigger; each side fires only after its prior retest signal is outside a configurable cooldown
Calculation: Trade direction = latest trend or reversal trigger side, reset to flat after a momentum exit
Calculation: Momentum exit long = RSI crosses down through its upper exhaustion level, or a bearish candle makes a lookback high and its upper wick exceeds a configurable multiple of its lower wick; momentum exit short = RSI crosses up through its lower exhaustion level, or a bullish candle makes a lookback low and its lower wick exceeds a configurable multiple of its upper wick; exit markers are subject to a configurable cooldown
Calculation: Stop and target levels = entry price plus or minus configurable multiples of ATR or configurable percentages of entry price, with signs reversed for short positions
Long: trend trigger is long OR reversal trigger is long OR retest long
Short: trend trigger is short OR reversal trigger is short OR retest short
Exit: Long, low <= stop loss OR high >= any configured take-profit level OR (trade direction is long AND momentum exit long)
Exit: Short, high >= stop loss OR low <= any configured take-profit level OR (trade direction is short AND momentum exit short)
