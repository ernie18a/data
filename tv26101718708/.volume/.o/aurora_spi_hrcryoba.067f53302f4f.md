Calculation: Modeled buy fraction = for each analysis candle, either the normal CDF of its close change divided by the standard deviation of preceding same-session analysis-candle close changes, clamped to a configurable range (neutral if not warmed up); its candle direction (up = 1, down = 0, unchanged = 0.5); or its last nonzero intrabar close-change direction (neutral if none). Mode is configurable.
Calculation: Buy share = volume allocated to a price row from each analysis candle, weighted by its modeled buy fraction, divided by total volume allocated to that row. Allocate candle volume across the overlap of its high-low range with equally sized rows spanning the running session low-high range; use the analysis timeframe when smaller-timeframe bars are available, otherwise chart bars.
Long: On a completed analysis candle, for a price row with nonzero volume in each of the latest confirmation candles, current-window buy share >= dominance threshold AND previous-window buy share <= 1 − dominance threshold AND each of the latest confirmation candles' buy share >= dominance threshold AND current- and previous-window row volumes >= minimum row-volume fraction × the strongest current-window row volume; require at least the greater of minimum session candles, two comparison windows, and the confirmation count in the session.
Short: On a completed analysis candle, for a price row with nonzero volume in each of the latest confirmation candles, current-window buy share <= 1 − dominance threshold AND previous-window buy share >= dominance threshold AND each of the latest confirmation candles' buy share <= 1 − dominance threshold AND current- and previous-window row volumes >= minimum row-volume fraction × the strongest current-window row volume; require at least the greater of minimum session candles, two comparison windows, and the confirmation count in the session.
