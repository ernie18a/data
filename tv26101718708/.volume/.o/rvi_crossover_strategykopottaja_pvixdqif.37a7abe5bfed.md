Calculation: RVI = sum of the smoothed weighted moving average of (close − open) divided by the sum of the smoothed weighted moving average of (high − low), over the same lookback
Calculation: RVI signal = smoothed weighted moving average of RVI
Long: RVI crosses above RVI signal AND EMA(close) > VWMA(close)
Short: RVI crosses below RVI signal AND EMA(close) < VWMA(close)
