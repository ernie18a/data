Calculation: Latest confirmed down-swing = A→B, where A is a ZigZag pivot high and B the subsequent pivot low; before entry, if a bar closes below B, update B to that bar’s low
Calculation: Swing range = A − B
Calculation: Fib level at retracement ratio r = B + r × swing range
Calculation: Short-term EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback
Long: confirmed bar, waiting for Trade 1, close > upper Trade 1 entry level AND previous close ≤ upper Trade 1 entry level AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND volume > volume average × threshold
Long: confirmed bar, (waiting for Trade 1 and close > upper Trade 2 entry level AND previous close ≤ upper Trade 2 entry level OR waiting for Trade 2 after Trade 1 take-profit and close > upper Trade 2 entry level AND previous close ≤ upper Trade 2 entry level) AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND volume > volume average × threshold
Exit: long, Trade 1 active and (high ≥ lower Trade 1 take-profit level OR close < Trade 1 stop level) OR Trade 2 active and (high ≥ lower Trade 2 take-profit level OR close < Trade 2 stop level)
