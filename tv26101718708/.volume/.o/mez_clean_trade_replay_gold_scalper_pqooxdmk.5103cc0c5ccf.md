Calculation: Bearish momentum = close < fast EMA AND close < VWAP AND RSI < 50 AND MACD line < MACD signal line AND ADX > minimum
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low < lowest low over the liquidity lookback, excluding the current bar, AND close > that lowest low
Calculation: Bearish liquidity sweep = high > highest high over the liquidity lookback, excluding the current bar, AND close < that highest high
Calculation: Bullish trend stack = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA
Calculation: Bearish trend stack = fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend EMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish trend = last completed higher-timeframe close > its 200-period EMA; bearish trend = last completed higher-timeframe close < its 200-period EMA
Calculation: Buy quality score = weighted sum of bullish higher-timeframe trend, bullish liquidity sweep (or if not required), bullish Fibonacci zone (or if not required), bearish DXY trend or unavailable DXY (or DXY confirmation off), bullish trend stack, bullish momentum, and session eligibility
Calculation: Sell quality score = weighted sum of bearish higher-timeframe trend, bearish liquidity sweep (or if not required), bearish Fibonacci zone (or if not required), bullish DXY trend or unavailable DXY (or DXY confirmation off), bearish trend stack, bearish momentum, and session eligibility
Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci zone = close is within the 61.8%–65% retracement zone below the highest high over the Fibonacci lookback, with an ATR-based buffer; bearish Fibonacci zone = close is within the 61.8%–65% retracement zone above the lowest low over that lookback, with the same buffer
Long: flat position AND confirmed bar AND not near a configured news time AND session eligible AND (higher-timeframe bullish trend when strict trend filtering is enabled) AND (DXY bearish, unavailable, or confirmation is soft/off) AND (bullish liquidity sweep when required) AND (bullish Fibonacci zone when required) AND close is within the configured ATR distance of the fast EMA when chase filtering is enabled AND close is not within the configured buffer of the previous completed daily high when that filter is enabled AND buy quality score meets the threshold AND buy quality score > sell quality score AND (bullish liquidity sweep OR close crosses above fast EMA while above mid EMA OR close exceeds the highest high of the previous 10 bars with bullish momentum)
Short: flat position AND confirmed bar AND not near a configured news time AND session eligible AND (higher-timeframe bearish trend when strict trend filtering is enabled) AND (DXY bullish, unavailable, or confirmation is soft/off) AND (bearish liquidity sweep when required) AND (bearish Fibonacci zone when required) AND close is within the configured ATR distance of the fast EMA when chase filtering is enabled AND close is not within the configured buffer of the previous completed daily low when that filter is enabled AND sell quality score meets the threshold AND sell quality score > buy quality score AND (bearish liquidity sweep OR close crosses below fast EMA while below mid EMA OR close falls below the lowest low of the previous 10 bars with bearish momentum)
Exit: long, after entry bar, high reaches third profit target OR low reaches stop-loss OR (after first target is reached, close falls at least the configured pullback from the best high since entry OR on a confirmed bar close is below fast EMA OR RSI < 50 OR MACD line < MACD signal line); the early exit is suppressed if the third target or stop-loss is reached
Exit: short, after entry bar, low reaches third profit target OR high reaches stop-loss OR (after first target is reached, close rises at least the configured pullback from the best low since entry OR on a confirmed bar close is above fast EMA OR RSI > 50 OR MACD line > MACD signal line); the early exit is suppressed if the third target or stop-loss is reached
