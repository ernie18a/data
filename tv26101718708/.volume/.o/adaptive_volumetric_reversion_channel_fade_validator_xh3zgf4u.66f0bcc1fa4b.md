Calculation: Tactical regression channel = volume-weighted polynomial regression of the selected price source from the selected rolling or swing anchor through the current bar; its residual standard deviation defines the bands. Upper/lower outer bands = centerline ± selected multiple of residual standard deviation.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high or low higher or lower than the selected number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of k-bar log returns divided by k times the variance of one-bar log returns, using the selected sample window.
Calculation: Reversion correlation = correlation over the selected window between prior-bar deviations from the selected moving average and current-bar price changes.
Long: A confirmed pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low AND its oscillator value is higher than the prior pivot's oscillator value AND the pivot low reaches the selected lower channel band
Long: Low ≤ lower outer band AND (close > lower outer band OR a bullish divergence printed within the selected recent-bar window OR a liquidity sweep of a prior/equal swing low printed within 3 bars OR the selected density tell qualifies) AND the selected regime gate passes AND any enabled density, band-walk, and ride-risk gates pass AND the fade cooldown has elapsed
Short: A confirmed pivot high is higher than the prior confirmed pivot high AND its oscillator value is lower than the prior pivot's oscillator value AND the pivot high reaches the selected upper channel band
Short: High ≥ upper outer band AND (close < upper outer band OR a bearish divergence printed within the selected recent-bar window OR a liquidity sweep of a prior/equal swing high printed within 3 bars OR the selected density tell qualifies) AND the selected regime gate passes AND any enabled density, band-walk, and ride-risk gates pass AND the fade cooldown has elapsed
