Calculation: Opening Range High/Low = highest high / lowest low during the first opening-range window of the session; use only after that window completes.
Calculation: Acceptance = every close in the acceptance lookback is above the Opening Range High for long acceptance, or below the Opening Range Low for short acceptance.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over the session-length lookback; require it to meet the minimum threshold.
Calculation: Strong volume = current volume ≥ volume average + standard-deviation multiplier × volume standard deviation, using the same lookback as Relative volume.
Calculation: ORB touch volume = most recent volume when close is within the retest percentage of the relevant completed Opening Range level; score 2 if ≥ volume average + standard-deviation multiplier × volume standard deviation, 1 if ≥ 1.2 × volume average, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR on daily bars, using the latest value available; daily range context = previous completed daily high and low.
Calculation: Bull context = close > previous completed daily high; bear context = close < previous completed daily low; neutral context = neither.
Calculation: Effective bull context = bull context OR (neutral context AND close above every available current-session VWAP, current-session POC, and previous-session POC). Effective bear context = bear context OR (neutral context AND close below every available one of those levels).
Calculation: Current-session VWAP = cumulative typical-price × volume / cumulative volume during the current session; previous-session VWAP = the completed prior session's VWAP.
Calculation: Session POC = price bucket with the greatest accumulated volume during the session, assigning each bar's volume to the bucket containing its typical price; use the fixed 50-bucket grid spanning 1.5 × the previous completed daily range, beginning 10% of that span below the previous daily low. Previous-session POC = the completed prior session's POC.
Calculation: Tape ratio = bullish volume / (bearish volume + absorbed volume) if bullish volume exceeds bearish volume; otherwise the negative of bearish volume / (bullish volume + absorbed volume) if bearish volume exceeds bullish volume; otherwise zero. On 1-minute bars, classify each lower-timeframe bar's volume by whether its close is above or below the next bar's close (equal closes are absorbed); if there are fewer than two lower-timeframe bars, classify the chart bar by close versus open. Tape defended = absorbed volume is at least the absorption percentage of total classified volume.
Calculation: Long ORB score = 1 for long acceptance + long ORB touch-volume score + 1 if relative volume meets its minimum + 1 if absolute move from session open is less than the maximum percentage of daily ATR + 1 for bull context.
Calculation: Short ORB score = 1 for short acceptance + short ORB touch-volume score + 1 if relative volume meets its minimum + 1 if absolute move from session open is less than the maximum percentage of daily ATR + 1 for bear context.
Calculation: Breakout quality score (long) = tape points (2 if tape ratio ≥ 3, 1 if ≥ minimum tape ratio, else 0) + 1 if 1-minute fast EMA minus slow EMA > 0 + 1 if 1-minute RSI ≥ overbought threshold + 1 if long ORB touch volume is strong + 1 if close is above both available previous-session POC and previous-session VWAP.
Calculation: Breakout quality score (short) = tape points (2 if tape ratio ≤ −3, 1 if ≤ negative minimum tape ratio, else 0) + 1 if 1-minute fast EMA minus slow EMA < 0 + 1 if 1-minute RSI ≤ oversold threshold + 1 if short ORB touch volume is strong + 1 if close is below both available previous-session POC and previous-session VWAP.
Calculation: Tight balance = the prior lookback bars' highest high minus lowest low is positive and no greater than the configured percentage of current close.
Calculation: Dynamic bearish invalidation = after short acceptance, close > Opening Range High; dynamic bullish invalidation = after long acceptance, close < Opening Range Low. Each remains true for the rest of the session once triggered.
Long: In session AND bull context AND long ORB score = 5 AND long breakout quality score ≥ 3 AND long tape points > 0 AND previous-session POC is not above close by less than 0.3% AND long acceptance AND no prior long signal for this acceptance.
Long: In session AND long acceptance AND no dynamic bullish invalidation AND close ≥ Opening Range High AND (close is within the retest percentage above Opening Range High) AND (strong volume OR tape defended) AND relative volume meets its minimum AND daily ATR usage is below its maximum AND NOT bear context AND long breakout quality score ≥ 2 AND (long ORB score minus its context point ≥ 3) AND effective bull context AND (long tape points > 0 OR tape defended); signal only when this condition turns true.
Long: In session AND short acceptance AND no dynamic bearish invalidation AND tight balance AND close > prior balance high AND strong volume AND relative volume meets its minimum AND daily ATR usage is below its maximum AND NOT bear context AND long breakout quality score ≥ 2 AND effective bull context; signal only when this condition turns true.
Long: In session AND long acceptance AND no dynamic bullish invalidation AND tight balance AND close > prior balance high AND strong volume AND relative volume meets its minimum AND daily ATR usage is below its maximum AND NOT bear context AND long breakout quality score ≥ 2 AND effective bull context; signal only when this condition turns true.
Short: In session AND bear context AND short ORB score = 5 AND short breakout quality score ≥ 3 AND short tape points > 0 AND previous-session POC is not below close by less than 0.3% AND short acceptance AND no prior short signal for this acceptance.
Short: In session AND short acceptance AND no dynamic bearish invalidation AND close ≤ Opening Range Low AND (close is within the retest percentage below Opening Range Low) AND (strong volume OR tape defended) AND relative volume meets its minimum AND daily ATR usage is below its maximum AND NOT bull context AND short breakout quality score ≥ 2 AND (short ORB score minus its context point ≥ 3) AND effective bear context AND (short tape points > 0 OR tape defended); signal only when this condition turns true.

Short: In session AND short acceptance AND no dynamic bearish invalidation AND tight balance AND close < prior balance low AND strong volume AND relative volume meets its minimum AND daily ATR usage is below its maximum AND NOT bull context AND short breakout quality score ≥ 2 AND effective bear context; signal only when this condition turns true.
