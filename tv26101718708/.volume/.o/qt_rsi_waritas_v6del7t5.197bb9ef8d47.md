Calculation: r = ln(close / previous close); σ = rolling standard deviation of r; normalized(x,L,g) = sigmoid((x − EMA(x,L)) / max(STDEV(x,L), ε) × g), clamped to [0,1]; signed(x) = tanh(x), clamped to [−1,1]. Use symbolic lookbacks and tunable parameters; U is the union percentile lookback, L is adaptive model memory.
Calculation: Kalman estimate = recursive 1D filter of close with process variance 1e−4 and measurement variance 1e−2; innovation = close − prior estimate; Kalman lock = 1 − normalized(abs(innovation / close), L, 1).
Calculation: Markov transition = SMA(abs(state − prior state), 50), where state is 1 when rolling 100-bar STDEV(r) exceeds its rolling 500-bar median, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Wavelet energies E2, E3, E4 = squared differences between successive log-close changes over spans 2, 4, 8, and 16 bars: E2=(Δ₂−Δ₂[2])², E3=(Δ₄−Δ₄[4])², E4=(Δ₈−Δ₈[8])², where Δₙ=log(close)−log(close[n]); wavelet energy = normalized(E3,L,1).
Calculation: Hawkes pressure = normalized(h, L, 1), where h = 0.90 × prior h + 0.01 + prior jump, jump = abs(r)/max(STDEV(r,50), ε) when abs(r)>1.5×STDEV(r,50), else 0.
Calculation: Spectral purity = normalized(max(abs(SMA(r,8)−SMA(r,16)), abs(SMA(r,16)−SMA(r,32)), abs(SMA(r,32)−SMA(r,64))) / max(STDEV(r,128), ε), L, 1).
Calculation: Long-memory strength = normalized(tanh(4 × drift) × normalized(DFA,L,1), L, 1), where drift = EMA(r, trend lookback)/max(STDEV(r,64), ε), DFA = STDEV(cumulative sum(r−SMA(r,64)) − linear regression of that cumulative series over 64 bars,64)/max(STDEV(r,64), ε).
Calculation: Subatomic mass = normalized(abs(r)×(1+0.5×E3),L,1); spin = tanh(sign(r)×min(h,5)×0.35); charge = tanh(0.25×SMA((r−SMA(r,50))³,50)/max(STDEV(r,50)³, ε)); lifetime = normalized(1/max(STDEV(r,20), ε),L,1).
Calculation: Kalman model = tanh((Kalman estimate − its value 3 bars earlier)/(max(close,ε)×max(STDEV(r,64),ε))×2) × Kalman lock × spectral purity; drift model = long-memory strength; wave model = tanh((log(close)−log(close[4]))/max(STDEV(r,64),ε)×0.125) × wavelet energy × spectral purity × (1−0.35×Hawkes pressure); RSI model = tanh((RSI(close, RSI lookback)−50)/18) × (0.35+0.65×spectral purity); spin model = spin × subatomic mass × (0.50+0.50×lifetime); charge model = charge × (1−Hawkes pressure) × (0.50+0.50×spectral purity).
Calculation: Shock risk = 0.55×Hawkes pressure + 0.35×Markov transition + 0.10×(1−lifetime); confidence = clamp(0.30×Kalman lock + 0.24×spectral purity + 0.22×long-memory strength + 0.14×subatomic mass + 0.10×lifetime − 0.25×shock risk, 0,1).
Calculation: Signal = tanh(2.20×weighted mean of spin, drift, wave, Kalman, charge, and RSI models) × (0.35+0.65×confidence); weights are their respective model weights divided by their sum. Signal threshold = base threshold, or the greater of base threshold and the rolling L-bar 78th percentile of abs(Signal), when adaptive thresholding is enabled; clamp to [0.03,0.65].
Calculation: Coordination = clamp(abs(spin model + drift model + wave model + Kalman model)/max(sum of their absolute values, ε),0,1); dispersion = 1−coordination; manifold quality = 0.35×coordination + 0.25×spectral purity + 0.20×long-memory strength + 0.20×lifetime; stack = tanh(0.65×(spin model + drift model + wave model + Kalman model)).
Calculation: Union pass is true if any of these holds. For direction d=sign(Signal), directional values are each model×d and aligned Signal=Signal×d. All percentiles below use the same rolling U-bar window: (Kalman>P80(Kalman) AND drift>P70(drift) AND manifold quality>P70(manifold quality)); OR (drift>P80(drift) AND spin>P75(spin) AND mass>P60(mass)); OR (Kalman>P75(Kalman) AND Kalman lock<P5(Kalman lock) AND directional spin>P25(directional spin)); OR (aligned Signal>P50(aligned Signal) AND Kalman lock<P20(Kalman lock) AND manifold quality>P70(manifold quality)); OR (directional Kalman>P40(directional Kalman) AND long-memory strength>P80(long-memory strength) AND spin>P80(spin)); OR (directional drift>P75(directional drift) AND Kalman lock<P20(Kalman lock) AND spin>P25(spin)); OR (directional Kalman>P30(directional Kalman) AND long-memory strength>P80(long-memory strength) AND directional spin>P80(directional spin)); OR (aligned Signal>P60(aligned Signal) AND spectral purity>P70(spectral purity) AND shock risk<P50(shock risk)); OR (manifold quality>P75(manifold quality) AND coordination>P60(coordination) AND dispersion<P40(dispersion) AND lifetime>P60(lifetime)); OR (directional wave>P60(directional wave) AND wavelet energy>P60(wavelet energy) AND Hawkes pressure<P50(Hawkes pressure) AND directional Kalman>P60(directional Kalman)); OR (directional charge>P60(directional charge) AND long-memory strength>P70(long-memory strength) AND confidence>P70(confidence) AND shock risk<P40(shock risk)); OR (directional drift>P70(directional drift) AND directional spin>P70(directional spin) AND wavelet energy>P70(wavelet energy) AND spectral purity>P70(spectral purity)).
Long: bar index exceeds max(L,U) AND long signals enabled AND confidence ≥ minimum confidence AND shock risk ≤ maximum shock risk AND dispersion ≤ maximum dispersion AND coordination ≥ minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union pass) AND (Signal crosses above threshold OR (continuation enabled AND Signal > threshold AND stack crosses above zero)).
Short: bar index exceeds max(L,U) AND short signals enabled AND confidence ≥ minimum confidence AND shock risk ≤ maximum shock risk AND dispersion ≤ maximum dispersion AND coordination ≥ minimum coordination AND (union filter disabled OR union pass) AND (Signal crosses below negative threshold OR (continuation enabled AND Signal < negative threshold AND stack crosses below zero)).
