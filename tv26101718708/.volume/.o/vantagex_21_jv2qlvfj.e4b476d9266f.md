Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative ((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) / cumulative volume since the chosen session start; use the current forming session (London 03:00–11:00 or New York 09:30–16:00).
Calculation: Rolling FLOW base = the latest lookback-bar high and low when (highest high − lowest low) < (lookback × average high−low range × compression factor) AND (lookback × average volume over the lookback) > (lookback × average volume over the longer volume lookback × volume factor).
Calculation: Previous New York session = the completed 09:30–16:00 New York session; its range is high−low, body is |close−open|, direction is close compared with open, and volume is summed across its bars. Its volume baseline is the 20-day simple average volume from the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Previous-session close position = (previous New York session close − its low) / its range.
Long: close > session VWAP OR close > EMA(9), EMA(20), EMA(50), or EMA(200) on the chart timeframe OR close > EMA(9), EMA(20), EMA(50), or EMA(200) on 15-minute, 1-hour, or 4-hour bars (higher-timeframe EMA uses the bar still forming) OR EMA(50) > EMA(200) OR EMA(20) > EMA(50) OR EMA(9) > EMA(21) OR EMA(5) > EMA(13) OR close > open OR rounded-to-1-decimal RSI(14) > 50 OR Stoch RSI %K > %D OR previous New York close position > its bullish threshold OR previous-session direction is up AND previous-session body / range > breakout factor / 2 AND previous session is not a base OR current high and close > rolling FLOW base high AND current range > average range over the FLOW lookback × breakout factor AND close > open AND close > previous close.
Short: close < session VWAP OR close < EMA(9), EMA(20), EMA(50), or EMA(200) on the chart timeframe OR close < EMA(9), EMA(20), EMA(50), or EMA(200) on 15-minute, 1-hour, or 4-hour bars (higher-timeframe EMA uses the bar still forming) OR EMA(50) < EMA(200) OR EMA(20) < EMA(50) OR EMA(9) < EMA(21) OR EMA(5) < EMA(13) OR close < open OR rounded-to-1-decimal RSI(14) < 50 OR Stoch RSI %K < %D OR previous New York close position < its bearish threshold OR previous-session direction is down AND previous-session body / range > breakout factor / 2 AND previous session is not a base OR current low and close < rolling FLOW base low AND current range > average range over the FLOW lookback × breakout factor AND close < open AND close < previous close.
