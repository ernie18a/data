Calculation: Trend Magic = if CCI(close, longer lookback) is nonnegative, the greater of the prior Trend Magic and low minus an ATR-based offset; otherwise, the lesser of the prior Trend Magic and high plus an ATR-based offset. ATR is a moving average of true range.
Calculation: Squeeze Momentum histogram = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint between the highest high and lowest low and the moving average of close, using the same shorter lookback for these components.
Calculation: Cumulative Delta Volume candle = cumulative sum of signed volume, where each bar's volume is weighted by its candle body and wicks; the candle is up when its close is at least its open, otherwise down. Use the configured Heikin Ashi or regular candle values.
Long: low crosses above Trend Magic OR CCI is nonnegative AND close is above Trend Magic plus the configured crypto offset (zero otherwise) AND no short Trend Magic condition has occurred within the configured lookback AND Squeeze Momentum histogram is positive AND no negative histogram value has occurred within its configured lookback AND Cumulative Delta Volume candle is up AND no down candle has occurred within its configured lookback AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX exceeds its configured threshold)
Short: high crosses below Trend Magic OR CCI is negative AND close is below Trend Magic minus the configured crypto offset (zero otherwise) AND no long Trend Magic condition has occurred within the configured lookback AND Squeeze Momentum histogram is negative AND no positive histogram value has occurred within its configured lookback AND Cumulative Delta Volume candle is down AND no up candle has occurred within its configured lookback AND (ADX filter is disabled OR ADX exceeds its configured threshold)
Exit: long, high reaches a configured take-profit target above entry price OR close is below entry price by the configured stop-loss percentage
Exit: short, low reaches a configured take-profit target below entry price OR close is above entry price by the configured stop-loss percentage
