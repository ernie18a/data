Calculation: Bullish/bearish 15-minute trend = last completed 15-minute close above/below its EMA; use a configurable EMA lookback.
Calculation: VWAP reclaim/rejection = close crosses above/below session VWAP from the prior bar's opposite side.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish candle confirmation = either a same-direction candle body larger than 60% of its range, or a same-direction engulfing candle that closes on the matching side of VWAP. Bullish engulfing requires the prior candle bearish, current candle bullish, current open below prior close, and current close above prior open; bearish engulfing is the inverse.
Calculation: Volume surge = volume exceeds 1.5 times its 20-bar simple moving average.
Calculation: Daily pivot levels = calculate P=(prior completed day's high+low+close)/3, R1=2P−low, R2=P+high−low, S1=2P−high, S2=P−high+low. Support proximity is within 8% of the prior day's range of P, S1, or S2; resistance proximity is within that distance of P, R1, or R2.
Calculation: Psychological level proximity = close is within 0.2% of the nearest whole-number, half-number, or corresponding decade-scaled whole/half-number level.
Calculation: Long score = 3 if short-term EMA > longer-term EMA, 3 if close > VWAP, 2 if VWAP reclaim, 2 if bullish candle confirmation, 2 if near daily pivot support, 2 if near psychological level, 1 if 15-minute trend is bullish, and 1 if volume surge; otherwise each contribution is zero. Short score uses the inverse EMA, VWAP, reclaim, candle, pivot resistance, and 15-minute trend conditions, plus the same psychological-level and volume conditions.
Calculation: Long event = within the configurable confluence window, both a short-term EMA cross above the longer-term EMA and a VWAP reclaim occurred, if the require-both setting is enabled; otherwise either event occurred on the current bar. Short event is the inverse. A new event is true only when the corresponding event is true now and was false on the prior bar.
Calculation: In-session = time in either configured trading window in New York time, or the time filter is disabled. Trend filter passes when disabled or the last completed 15-minute close is above its EMA for longs and below for shorts. Cooldown passes when bars since the last same-side trigger exceed the configured cooldown.
Calculation: Long location = bullish 15-minute trend and (near daily pivot support or near psychological level). Short location = bearish 15-minute trend and (near daily pivot resistance or near psychological level).
Long: new long event AND long score ≥ minimum score AND trend filter passes AND in-session AND long cooldown passes AND (long score > minimum score OR (long score = minimum score AND long location)).
Short: new short event AND short score ≥ minimum score AND trend filter passes AND in-session AND short cooldown passes AND (short score > minimum score OR (short score = minimum score AND short location)).
