Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low of bars in the configured opening-range window, finalized when that window ends
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening range high − opening range low
Calculation: VWAP deviation = square root of cumulative volume-weighted squared differences between each bar’s (high + low + close) / 3 and the current session VWAP, divided by cumulative volume; reset each day
Long: (Breakout mode AND opening range finalized AND within configured trading hours AND daily trade count below its cap AND flat AND close crosses above opening range high AND shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND close > medium-lookback SMA) OR (Mean-reversion mode AND opening range finalized AND within configured trading hours AND daily trade count below its cap AND flat AND close < VWAP − 2 × VWAP deviation AND close crosses above the previous bar’s low AND close > medium-lookback SMA)
Short: (Breakout mode AND opening range finalized AND within configured trading hours AND daily trade count below its cap AND flat AND close crosses below opening range low AND shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND close < medium-lookback SMA) OR (Mean-reversion mode AND opening range finalized AND within configured trading hours AND daily trade count below its cap AND flat AND close > VWAP + 2 × VWAP deviation AND close crosses below the previous bar’s high AND close < medium-lookback SMA)
Exit: Long, before first target, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × nearer-target multiplier
Exit: Long, before first target, low ≤ opening range low in Breakout mode OR low ≤ entry price − opening-range size in Mean-reversion mode
Exit: Long, after first target, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × farther-target multiplier OR low ≤ entry price
Exit: Short, before first target, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × nearer-target multiplier
Exit: Short, before first target, high ≥ opening range high in Breakout mode OR high ≥ entry price + opening-range size in Mean-reversion mode
Exit: Short, after first target, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × farther-target multiplier OR high ≥ entry price
