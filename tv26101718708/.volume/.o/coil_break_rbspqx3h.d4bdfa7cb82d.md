Calculation: Inside-bar setup = A bar whose range is inside the prior bar’s range, whose body is inside the prior body, or whose range is inside the prior body, according to the selected definition; equality is allowed unless strict edges are selected. Store its high, the prior bar’s high, and its low. It remains valid for the selected number of bars after formation unless a bar closes below its stored low.
Calculation: Bull-flag setup = A pole qualifies when current high minus the rolling lowest low over the pole lookback meets the selected minimum measured in ATR or percent, and current high is at least the rolling highest high over that lookback. Store current high, the rolling low, and average volume over the pole lookback; a new qualifying high restarts the pole. After the pole, track the lowest low, highest high, and average volume of completed flag bars. Invalidate if the flag low falls below pole high minus the selected retracement fraction of the pole, or its age exceeds the selected maximum. The breakout reference is either the stored pole high or the flag high from bars before the current bar. If volume drying is required and measurable, flag average volume must be below pole average volume; unavailable volume passes this check.
Calculation: Tight-range box = A rolling window whose highest high minus lowest low is no greater than the selected threshold measured in ATR or percent. A box forms only when this condition becomes true after being false, and, if enabled, its high must be within the selected tolerance of the rolling high over the maximum-box-length lookback. Store that window’s high and low. Invalidate if a bar closes below the stored low or box age plus window length exceeds the selected maximum.
Long: A valid inside-bar setup is at least one bar old and within its breakout window, and (close > its selected stored high, or, if touch breakouts are selected, high > that level); OR a valid bull-flag setup has reached its minimum age and (close > its selected breakout reference, or, if touch breakouts are selected, high > that reference), with the required volume-drying condition; OR a tight-range box is valid and (close > its stored high, or, if touch breakouts are selected, high > that high); AND the trend filter is disabled, the trend moving average is unavailable, or close is above the selected simple or exponential moving average. If closed-bar triggers are selected, the bar must be complete.
