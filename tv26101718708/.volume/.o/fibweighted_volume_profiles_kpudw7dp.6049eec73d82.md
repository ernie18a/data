Calculation: Active swing = range between the latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low; extend the endpoint to the running high or low from the leg start.
Calculation: Swing volume profile = distribute each completed chart bar’s volume across the price bins touched by its range, over the active swing.
Calculation: Value area = contiguous price bins around the highest-volume bin that contain 70% of swing volume; value area high/low are the outer bin boundaries.
Long: completed-bar close > value area high AND swing volume profile is valid
Short: completed-bar close < value area low AND swing volume profile is valid
