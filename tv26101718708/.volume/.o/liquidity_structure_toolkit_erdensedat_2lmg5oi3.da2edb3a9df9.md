Calculation: Major BSL/SSL levels = highs/lows of confirmed 60-minute swing pivots, each requiring the sensitivity number of bars on both sides; use completed 60-minute bars.
Calculation: Fractal pivot high/low = a chart-timeframe high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; use confirmed pivots.
Long: A valid bullish candle (close > open and close − open > 0.15 × (high − low)) touches an active major SSL (low ≤ level and close > level), or is the first valid bullish candle within the next 10 bars after such a touch; OR an active major SSL is touched (low ≤ level) and, before any active major BSL touch, price breaks above the latest unbroken confirmed fractal pivot high (close > pivot high).
Short: A valid bearish candle (close < open and open − close > 0.15 × (high − low)) touches an active major BSL (high ≥ level and close < level), or is the first valid bearish candle within the next 10 bars after such a touch; OR an active major BSL is touched (high ≥ level) and, before any active major SSL touch, price breaks below the latest unbroken confirmed fractal pivot low (close < pivot low).
