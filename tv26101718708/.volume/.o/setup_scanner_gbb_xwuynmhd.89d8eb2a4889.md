Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme versus the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes available only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the first configured duration of each session; use only after that duration has elapsed.
Long: On a confirmed bar, session VWAP is available, the close crosses above it after at least the configured consecutive closes below it, and volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Short: On a confirmed bar, session VWAP is available, the close crosses below it after at least the configured consecutive closes above it, and volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Long: On a confirmed bar, the medium-term EMA is above the long-term EMA, the long-term EMA is rising over its configured lookback, price touched or fell below the medium-term EMA within the configured recent-bar window, and close > short-term EMA AND close > open AND close > previous high AND volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Short: On a confirmed bar, the medium-term EMA is below the long-term EMA, the long-term EMA is falling over its configured lookback, price touched or rose above the medium-term EMA within the configured recent-bar window, and close < short-term EMA AND close < open AND close < previous low AND volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Long: On a confirmed bar within the configured retest window after a close above the latest confirmed swing high, low <= broken level + ATR × retest tolerance AND close > broken level AND close > open. Defer newly confirmed swing highs while that break is live; use the queued pivot after the live break ends.
Short: On a confirmed bar within the configured retest window after a close below the latest confirmed swing low, high >= broken level − ATR × retest tolerance AND close < broken level AND close < open. Defer newly confirmed swing lows while that break is live; use the queued pivot after the live break ends.
Long: On a confirmed bar, low < the lowest low of the configured preceding bars AND close > that low AND close > open AND (close − low) > (high − close) AND volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Short: On a confirmed bar, high > the highest high of the configured preceding bars AND close < that high AND close < open AND (high − close) > (close − low) AND volume meets the volume-SMA filter.
Long: On a confirmed bar when a new confirmed swing low is available, its price is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low, its RSI value at the pivot is higher than the preceding pivot's RSI, and the pivot gap is within the configured maximum.
Short: On a confirmed bar when a new confirmed swing high is available, its price is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high, its RSI value at the pivot is lower than the preceding pivot's RSI, and the pivot gap is within the configured maximum.
Long: On a confirmed bar during the session, after the opening range is complete and before either side has broken that session, close > opening range high.
Short: On a confirmed bar during the session, after the opening range is complete and before either side has broken that session, close < opening range low.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple OR high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple × third-target multiple OR bars since entry >= expiry; if the bar touches both stop and third target, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple OR low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple × third-target multiple OR bars since entry >= expiry; if the bar touches both stop and third target, exit at the stop.
