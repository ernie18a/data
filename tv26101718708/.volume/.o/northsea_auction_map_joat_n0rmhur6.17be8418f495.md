Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 over the current anchor bars, including the current bar; anchor is the configured session or rolling window.
Calculation: Anchor location = (close − anchor low) / (anchor high − anchor low), using the current anchor bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Value-area ratio = volume in the contiguous profile rows selected around the highest-volume row until the configured value-area share is reached, divided by total profile volume. Allocate each anchor bar’s volume to rows in proportion to its high-low overlap; use the current anchor bars, including the current bar.
Calculation: Profile balance = mean row volume / sample standard deviation of row volumes in the current anchor profile.
Calculation: Delta bias = sum of per-bar delta estimates in the current anchor profile / total profile volume. Estimate each bar’s delta as volume times [0.45 × (2 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) − 1) + 0.35 × ((buy wick − sell wick) / (buy wick + sell wick)) + 0.20 × signed body ratio], where buy wick = min(open, close) − low plus the body if close > open, sell wick = high − max(open, close) plus the body if close < open, body ratio = abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick), and the signed body ratio is positive when close ≥ open and negative otherwise. Use the current anchor bars, including the current bar.
Long: Delta bias > its positive neutral threshold OR close is below Anchored VWAP by more than its neutral distance threshold relative to anchor range OR anchor location is in the upper range OR value-area ratio meets its high acceptance threshold OR profile balance meets its high threshold.
Short: Delta bias < its negative neutral threshold OR close is above Anchored VWAP by more than its neutral distance threshold relative to anchor range OR anchor location is in the lower range OR value-area ratio is below its low acceptance threshold OR profile balance meets its low threshold.
