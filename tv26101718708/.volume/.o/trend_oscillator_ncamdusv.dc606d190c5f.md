Calculation: Trend oscillator = 100 × (moving average of current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close − moving average of current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi open) / (moving average of current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi high − moving average of current-timeframe Heikin-Ashi low), using the same moving-average type and length for all four prices
Calculation: Normalized adaptive slope = blend the fast and slow slopes of the moving-averaged Heikin-Ashi body (close − open), weighting fast by the efficiency ratio and slow by 1 − efficiency ratio; each slope uses the selected method (smoothed derivative, linear-regression slope, or momentum), with the fast lookback shorter than or equal to the slow lookback; normalize the blend by its rolling maximum absolute value over the normalization lookback and multiply by 100
Long: Trend oscillator > 0 OR normalized adaptive slope > deadband and its prior value was ≤ deadband OR, within a positive-trend cycle, a later positive slope peak is lower than the stored strong positive slope peak, its close is higher than the stored peak close, and the previous bar's trend oscillator > 0
Short: Trend oscillator < 0 OR normalized adaptive slope < −deadband and its prior value was ≥ −deadband OR, within a negative-trend cycle, a later negative slope trough is higher than the stored strong negative slope trough, its close is lower than the stored trough close, and the previous bar's trend oscillator < 0
