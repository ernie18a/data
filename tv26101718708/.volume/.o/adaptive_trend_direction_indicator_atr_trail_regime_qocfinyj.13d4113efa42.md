Calculation: ATR trailing stop = initialize at close − ATR × multiplier; while bullish, ratchet to max(previous stop, close − ATR × multiplier), flipping bearish when close < stop and resetting stop to close + ATR × multiplier; while bearish, ratchet to min(previous stop, close + ATR × multiplier), flipping bullish when close > stop and resetting stop to close − ATR × multiplier
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = abs(close − close N bars ago) / sum(abs(close − previous close) over the same N bars)
Calculation: Regime = trending after ADX > threshold AND efficiency ratio > threshold for N consecutive bars; ranging after either condition fails for N consecutive bars; initial regime is ranging
Calculation: EMA slope = EMA(close, period) − EMA(close, period, slope lookback bars ago)
Calculation: Anchored VWAP standard deviation = sqrt(volume-weighted mean of source squared − VWAP squared), accumulated from the current anchor through the current forming anchor period
Long: flat or short AND date filter disabled or datetime >= start time AND ((trending regime AND bullish trailing-stop flip AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > long threshold) AND (VWAP side filter disabled OR close > anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard disabled OR anchored VWAP standard-deviation distance <= maximum) ) OR (ranging regime AND ranging mode = mean reversion AND (RSI-trigger mode uses RSI < oversold threshold OR VWAP-band-trigger mode uses close <= anchored VWAP − band distance × anchored VWAP standard deviation OR confirm mode uses both) ))
Short: flat or long AND shorts allowed AND date filter disabled or datetime >= start time AND ((trending regime AND bearish trailing-stop flip AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < short threshold) AND (VWAP side filter disabled OR close < anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard disabled OR anchored VWAP standard-deviation distance <= maximum) ) OR (ranging regime AND ranging mode = mean reversion AND (RSI-trigger mode uses RSI > overbought threshold OR VWAP-band-trigger mode uses close >= anchored VWAP + band distance × anchored VWAP standard deviation OR confirm mode uses both) ))
Exit: long, opposing bearish trailing-stop flip while the position is a trend trade OR EMA exit enabled AND held bars >= grace bars AND EMA slope < 0 OR time exit enabled AND held bars >= maximum bars OR ranging mode = mean reversion AND ranging regime AND (high >= take-profit level OR low <= stop level OR RSI > 50) OR short entry
Exit: short, opposing bullish trailing-stop flip while the position is a trend trade OR EMA exit enabled AND held bars >= grace bars AND EMA slope > 0 OR time exit enabled AND held bars >= maximum bars OR ranging mode = mean reversion AND ranging regime AND (low <= take-profit level OR high >= stop level OR RSI < 50) OR long entry
