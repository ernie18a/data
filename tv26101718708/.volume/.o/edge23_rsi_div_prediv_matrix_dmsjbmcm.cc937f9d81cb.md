Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index; calculate separately on each scanned timeframe using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = RSI is below the oversold threshold, the current low is below the stored low, and current RSI is above the RSI stored with that low. Initialize the stored low and RSI on the first oversold bar; while oversold, replace them when RSI makes a lower low; reset them after the divergence lookback expires. Count bullish divergence events in the rolling divergence lookback; confirmed bullish divergence requires at least 3 events. Bearish divergence is the inverse: RSI above the overbought threshold, current high above the stored high, current RSI below its stored RSI; initialize on the first overbought bar, replace when RSI makes a higher high, expire after the lookback, and require at least 3 events in the rolling lookback.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish exhaustion scores = sum the following points independently for each direction: 10 if price made a lower low (bullish) or higher high (bearish) versus the pre-divergence lookback and the 3-bar average absolute candle body is below that average shifted back by the pre-divergence lookback; 15 if that price move occurred while volume is below volume at the shifted bar; 15 if RSI is below 35 and its current one-bar change exceeds its prior one-bar change (bullish), or RSI is above 65 and its current change is below its prior change (bearish); 15 if MACD histogram rises (bullish) or falls (bearish) versus the prior bar; 10 if that price move occurred and ATR is no greater than prior-bar ATR; 10 if price is below (bullish) or above (bearish) the EMA, its absolute distance from EMA exceeds 1.5 ATR, and progress over the pre-divergence lookback is positive but less than current ATR; 10 if current volume divided by its longer volume-average lookback is below 1 and that ratio exceeded 1.8 within the pre-divergence lookback; 10 if, before the pre-divergence lookback, price moved down (bullish) or up (bearish) by more than 2 times ATR at that shifted bar, and current low is at least the lowest low (bullish) or current high is no greater than the highest high (bearish) over the pre-divergence lookback. Use the same pre-divergence lookback for shifted comparisons; the volume-average lookback is longer.
Long: bullish confirmed divergence OR bullish exhaustion score >= pre-divergence watch threshold AND bullish score > bearish score OR RSI < oversold threshold.
Short: bearish confirmed divergence OR bearish exhaustion score >= pre-divergence watch threshold AND bearish score > bullish score OR RSI > overbought threshold.
