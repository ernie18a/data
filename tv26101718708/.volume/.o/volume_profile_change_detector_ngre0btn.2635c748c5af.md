Calculation: Recent and baseline volume shares = Divide the price range from the lowest low to the highest high over the longer lookback by the chosen number of equal-width bins. Assign each bar’s close to a bin and sum its volume. For the bin containing the current close, divide its volume by total volume over the shorter lookback for the recent share, and by total volume over the longer lookback for the baseline share. Both windows include the current chart-timeframe bar; the shorter lookback is shorter than the longer one.
Calculation: Cluster score = Recent volume share ÷ (baseline volume share + 0.001).
Long: Price range over the longer lookback > 0 AND recent volume share × 100 ≥ minimum share threshold AND cluster score ≥ moderate threshold AND current close > close at the shorter-lookback offset.
Short: Price range over the longer lookback > 0 AND recent volume share × 100 ≥ minimum share threshold AND cluster score ≥ moderate threshold AND current close < close at the shorter-lookback offset.
