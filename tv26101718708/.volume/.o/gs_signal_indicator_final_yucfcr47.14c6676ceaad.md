Calculation: Relative strength = close divided by the benchmark close; compare its moving average with its prior-bar value and compare the ratio with its prior lookback high.
Calculation: XGS score = weighted sum of trend, range structure, momentum, relative strength, and risk conditions: close above medium EMA; fast EMA above medium EMA; medium EMA above base EMA; base EMA above long EMA; healthy trend (close above medium EMA, medium EMA above base EMA and not falling); supported range midpoint (close above midpoint and midpoint not falling); close above prior-bar range high; close above range midpoint; higher-timeframe close above its medium EMA and that EMA at or above its base EMA; RSI above bull floor and at or above strength level; MACD histogram positive and not falling; relative strength above its EMA with rising EMA and RSI above bull floor; relative strength above prior-bar range high; no trap; and safe price stretch. Higher-timeframe values use the current higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: DTS score = weighted sum of location, trigger, participation, and context conditions: close above medium EMA; close above VWAP (or medium EMA if VWAP is disabled); liquidity reclaim; close above prior-day pivot; strong bullish candle; A- or B-grade candle; stochastic K at or above D; bullish candle closing in the upper part of its range; relative volume at or above required volume; OBV above its average; CMF positive; in session; bullish phase; and no trap. Prior-day pivot uses the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Apex layer count = count of these true conditions: XGS score at or above its buy threshold; DTS score at or above its buy threshold; healthy trend with medium EMA above base EMA; RSI at or above strength level and below 68 with positive MACD histogram; MACD histogram not falling and MACD line at or above signal line; required relative volume with participation; CMF positive or MFI at or above 55; relative strength leadership; higher-timeframe support; strong A-grade candle; no trap and no distribution; bullish phase, safe price stretch, and not overextended.
Long: Apex layer count at or above the configured strict-layer threshold AND confirmed bar close AND (buy cooldown elapsed OR no prior major signal) AND (trade-state blocking disabled OR not in trade)
Long: XGS score at or above its buy threshold AND healthy trend AND higher-timeframe support AND no trap AND Apex layer count = 11 AND confirmed bar close AND (buy cooldown elapsed OR no prior major signal) AND (trade-state blocking disabled OR not in trade)

Long: XGS buy AND not DTS buy AND Apex layer count at or above the configured close-layer threshold AND this condition became true on this bar
Long: Apex layer count at or above the configured early-layer threshold AND below the configured close-layer threshold AND confirmed bar close AND this condition became true on this bar
Long: Apex layer count at or above the configured close-layer threshold AND below 11 AND confirmed bar close AND this condition became true on this bar

Exit: Long, in trade AND confirmed bar close AND (close below active stop OR (close below base EMA AND MACD histogram negative) OR (RSI below bull floor AND close below medium EMA)) AND this exit condition was false on the prior bar
Exit: Long, close below active stop; active stop is the lower of entry-bar close minus ATR times the stop multiplier and the entry-bar swing low minus ATR times the swing-stop buffer
