Calculation: Recent ATR ratio = short-window simple moving average of fast-length ATR divided by slow-length ATR
Calculation: Accumulation range = highest high and lowest low over the seed window, frozen when a range starts
Calculation: Cycle volume baseline = average volume over the seed window, frozen when a range starts
Long: On a confirmed bar, an active accumulation range has a low sweep at least the minimum ATR distance below its low on volume above the cycle volume baseline times the sweep-volume threshold, and within the return window price closes strictly inside the range; OR an active range with a confirmed low sweep closes above the range high plus the breakout ATR distance on volume above the cycle volume baseline times the breakout-volume threshold
Short: On a confirmed bar, an active accumulation range has a high sweep at least the minimum ATR distance above its high on volume above the cycle volume baseline times the sweep-volume threshold, and within the return window price closes strictly inside the range; OR an active range with a confirmed high sweep closes below the range low minus the breakout ATR distance on volume above the cycle volume baseline times the breakout-volume threshold
