Calculation: HTF support/resistance shelves = confirmed pivot lows/highs from the primary and enabled secondary higher timeframes, grouped by same-side price proximity using each timeframe’s ATR-scaled tolerance; retain shelves meeting the configured minimum touch count, flip their side when a confirmed close crosses their midpoint, and select the active shelves by configured proximity/scoring. Use confirmed HTF pivots; HTF bias, when enabled, uses the still-forming primary HTF close and EMA.
Calculation: Key sweep levels = previous completed daily high/low and weekly high/low, plus completed Asia and London session highs/lows.
Calculation: Tap arm = an active support/resistance shelf is intersected by the bar’s high-low range within the configured tick threshold, with the preceding bar not intersecting it; alternatively, a key-level sweep is a confirmed bar whose wick crosses a key level and whose close returns inside it. Asia/London levels qualify only after their session ends. An arm remains valid through the configured maximum number of bars, including the arm bar.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = chart-timeframe high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides; each becomes available after its right-side bars complete. Use the latest available pivot of each side.
Calculation: Synthetic delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1); use zero when volume is zero and a close position of 0.5 when high equals low. Delta ratio = synthetic delta / volume, or zero when volume is zero. Cumulative delta = sum of synthetic delta from the arm bar through the current bar, capped by the configured maximum lookback.
Calculation: Displacement = current bar range / chart-timeframe ATR(14), and absolute body / range; body ratio is zero when range is zero.
Long: Confirmed close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high plus the configured buffer AND a support tap or low-level sweep arm is valid AND within either configured trading session AND bullish displacement passes (range/ATR and body ratio meet their configured minima and close > open when directional close is required) AND bullish delta passes (delta ratio meets its configured minimum, volume meets its configured average-volume multiple when required, and cumulative delta > 0 when required) AND, when enabled, primary HTF close > its EMA AND the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Confirmed close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low minus the configured buffer AND a resistance tap or high-level sweep arm is valid AND within either configured trading session AND bearish displacement passes (range/ATR and body ratio meet their configured minima and close < open when directional close is required) AND bearish delta passes (delta ratio is at or below the negative configured minimum, volume meets its configured average-volume multiple when required, and cumulative delta < 0 when required) AND, when enabled, primary HTF close < its EMA AND the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop or high >= target; stop is the configured prior-structure low or latest pivot low, adjusted below entry with a fallback below entry if needed and the configured buffer, and target = entry price + (entry price − stop) × configured reward/risk multiple.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop or low <= target; stop is the configured prior-structure high or latest pivot high, adjusted above entry with a fallback above entry if needed and the configured buffer, and target = entry price − (stop − entry price) × configured reward/risk multiple.
