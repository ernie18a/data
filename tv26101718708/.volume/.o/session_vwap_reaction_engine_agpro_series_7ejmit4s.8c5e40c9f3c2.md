Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of each in-session bar’s typical price ((high + low + close) / 3), including the current bar
Calculation: VWAP corridor = session VWAP ± ATR-scaled half-width
Calculation: VWAP slope = current session VWAP minus session VWAP from a shorter lookback ago
Long: In session AND at least the minimum prior-side bars closed below their respective session VWAPs AND (close crosses above session VWAP and the cross occurred the required confirmation bars ago with every close since it above session VWAP OR low <= lower VWAP corridor and close > session VWAP and close > open and VWAP slope >= 0 OR (not reclaim and not bounce) and low <= session VWAP and close > session VWAP and close > open) AND event cooldown elapsed
Short: In session AND at least the minimum prior-side bars closed above their respective session VWAPs AND (close crosses below session VWAP and the cross occurred the required confirmation bars ago with every close since it below session VWAP OR low? no: high >= upper VWAP corridor and close < session VWAP and close < open and VWAP slope <= 0 OR (not reclaim and not bounce) and high >= session VWAP and close < session VWAP and close < open) AND event cooldown elapsed
