Calculation: Bullish score = average over the signal window of the sum of positive directional-indicator scores and positive volatility/volume-indicator scores; directional scores: ADX +15 when ADX exceeds its minimum and +DI > −DI, otherwise −15 when ADX exceeds its minimum and −DI > +DI, otherwise 0; Supertrend +13 when bullish, −13 when bearish, otherwise 0; close vs EMA +12 above, −12 below, otherwise 0; MACD +6 when MACD line > signal line and > 0, −6 when MACD line < signal line and < 0, otherwise 0; Ichimoku +4 when close > both spans and conversion line > base line, −4 when close < both spans and conversion line < base line, otherwise 0. Volatility/volume scores: ATR +15 when ATR > 1.1 × its moving average, −15 when ATR < 0.9 × its moving average, otherwise 0; close vs VWAP +13 above, −13 below, otherwise 0; volume +12 when volume > 1.2 × its moving average, −12 when volume < 0.8 × its moving average, otherwise 0; OBV +6 above its EMA, −6 below, otherwise 0; CMF +4 when > 0.05, −4 when < −0.05, otherwise 0. Each moving average and indicator uses its own lookback; the signal window includes the current bar and preceding bars.
Calculation: Bearish score = average over the signal window of the absolute values of negative directional-indicator scores and negative volatility/volume-indicator scores, summed.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over 5 bars if the impulse filter is enabled and the prior bar's absolute close−open exceeds 2 × current ATR; otherwise lowest low over 3 bars; subtract ATR × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over the same lookback; add ATR × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × risk/reward ratio.
Calculation: Short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × risk/reward ratio.
Long: bullish score >= threshold AND session filter disabled or datetime is in the London or New York session AND open trades < maximum AND no prior close or bars since last close >= cooldown AND anti-pullback filter disabled or prior bar close > prior bar open AND entry price > long stop AND entry price − long stop <= current ATR × maximum-stop ATR.
Short: bearish score >= threshold AND session filter disabled or datetime is in the London or New York session AND open trades < maximum AND no prior close or bars since last close >= cooldown AND anti-pullback filter disabled or prior bar close < prior bar open AND short stop > entry price AND short stop − entry price <= current ATR × maximum-stop ATR.
Exit: Long, bearish score >= threshold OR low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: Short, bullish score >= threshold OR high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
