Calculation: Bullish pattern = bullish engulfing (previous candle bearish; current candle bullish, closes above previous open, opens at or below previous close, and has a larger body); hammer (lower wick > 2× body, upper wick < 0.5× body, body < 40% of range, close ≥ open); breakout (close above the highest high of the prior 10 bars, bullish body > 50% of range); bounce (bullish candle with body > 55% of range, close above candle midpoint, and low within configured proximity of any support level); or spring (low sweeps below the prior configured lookback low, then within 3 bars close above that level on a bullish candle with qualified volume).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = bearish engulfing (previous candle bullish; current candle bearish, closes below previous open, opens at or above previous close, and has a larger body); shooting star (upper wick > 2× body, lower wick < 0.5× body, body < 40% of range, close ≤ open); breakdown (close below the lowest low of the prior 10 bars, bearish body > 50% of range); rejection (bearish candle with body > 55% of range, close below candle midpoint, and high within configured proximity of any resistance level); or upthrust (high sweeps above the prior configured lookback high, then within 3 bars close below that level on a bearish candle with qualified volume).
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = prior completed day high, low, and close; prior completed week high and low; current day's evolving high and low; pre-session high and low; opening-range high and low; latest confirmed pivot low/high; EMA with the intermediate lookback; and current VWAP. A price is near a level when it is within configured percentage proximity.
Calculation: Qualified volume = current volume ≥ its 20-bar average × configured multiplier, and (when enabled) current volume > previous bar volume > volume two bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = count of these six conditions: fast/middle/slow EMAs in ascending/descending order (lookbacks progressively longer); close above/below VWAP with VWAP rising/falling versus 3 bars earlier; fast/middle/slow EMAs in the same order on the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; RSI in its configured bullish/bearish range; MACD line above/below signal line with histogram rising/falling; qualified volume.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trend agreement = at least 2 of: fast/middle/slow EMAs in ascending/descending order; close above/below VWAP; higher-timeframe EMAs in ascending/descending order on the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Long/short risk = entry price minus/plus ATR × configured stop multiplier (when ATR stops are disabled, long stop is 0.2% below the minimum of current low, prior low, and intermediate EMA; short stop is 0.2% above their maximum). First and second targets = entry price plus/minus risk × their configured reward multiples. After price reaches the first target (long: high ≥ target; short: low ≤ target), move stop to entry.
Long: bullish score = 6 AND bullish pattern AND bullish trend agreement AND at least the configured call cooldown bars since the previous call signal AND within the good window (not 08:30–09:00, 11:30–12:30, or 13:30–14:30 Chicago time) AND outside the 14:30–14:50 MOC window; OR bullish score is 4–5 AND bullish pattern AND bullish trend agreement AND call cooldown elapsed AND within the good window AND outside the MOC window; OR within the MOC window AND bullish score ≥ 4 AND bullish pattern AND bullish trend agreement AND qualified volume AND (prior completed day high is unavailable OR close < 0.998 × prior completed day high) AND configured MOC cooldown elapsed; OR spring condition; OR not in a position AND a short signal occurred within the prior 20 bars AND bullish score ≥ 4 AND bullish pattern AND bullish trend agreement AND within the good window.
Short: bearish score = 6 AND bearish pattern AND bearish trend agreement AND at least the configured put cooldown bars since the previous put signal AND within the good window AND outside the MOC window; OR bearish score is 4–5 AND bearish pattern AND bearish trend agreement AND put cooldown elapsed AND within the good window AND outside the MOC window; OR within the MOC window AND bearish score ≥ 4 AND bearish pattern AND bearish trend agreement AND qualified volume AND (prior completed day low is unavailable OR close > 1.002 × prior completed day low) AND configured MOC cooldown elapsed; OR upthrust condition; OR not in a position AND a long signal occurred within the prior 20 bars AND bearish score ≥ 4 AND bearish pattern AND bearish trend agreement AND within the good window.
Exit: long, high ≥ second target OR low ≤ current stop OR bearish score ≥ 4 AND bearish pattern AND (close < VWAP OR fast/middle/slow EMAs in descending order) OR configured time-stop bar count reached; also flag exit warning when bearish score ≥ 4 AND bearish pattern AND (close < VWAP OR fast/middle/slow EMAs in descending order).
Exit: short, low ≤ second target OR high ≥ current stop OR bullish score ≥ 4 AND bullish pattern AND (close > VWAP OR fast/middle/slow EMAs in ascending order) OR configured time-stop bar count reached; also flag exit warning when bullish score ≥ 4 AND bullish pattern AND (close > VWAP OR fast/middle/slow EMAs in ascending order).
