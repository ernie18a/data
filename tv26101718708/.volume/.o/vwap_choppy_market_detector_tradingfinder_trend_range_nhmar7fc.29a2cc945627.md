Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP bands = VWAP of open for the current, still-forming anchor period, with upper and lower bands offset by a volume-weighted standard deviation multiplier; the trend bands use a wider multiplier than the range bands.
Long: Upper trend band ≠ lower trend band AND close > upper range band AND (upper trend band − lower trend band) ≥ an ATR multiple.
Short: Upper trend band ≠ lower trend band AND close < lower range band AND (upper trend band − lower trend band) ≥ an ATR multiple.
