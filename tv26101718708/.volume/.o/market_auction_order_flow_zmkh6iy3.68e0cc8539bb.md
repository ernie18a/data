Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3, reset at the configured anchor period and including bars of the current, still-forming period
Calculation: First VWAP band = VWAP plus or minus the configured distance, using either VWAP standard deviation or a percentage of VWAP
Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume when subbar close >= open, otherwise negative volume; if lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use chart-bar volume with the same close/open sign rule. Use configured lower timeframe or chart-aware lower timeframe; include subbars of the current, still-forming chart bar
Calculation: Delta EMA = EMA of intrabar delta
Calculation: SSL channel = SMA of highs and SMA of lows over the same lookback; channel direction switches up when close exceeds the high SMA, down when close falls below the low SMA, and otherwise retains its previous direction
Long: low penetrates below the lower first VWAP band by at least the configured minimum percentage of that band AND close > lower first VWAP band AND the configured same-side cooldown has elapsed
Long: previous close <= previous VWAP AND close > VWAP
Long: Delta EMA crosses above zero AND its lowest value over the configured lookback through the prior bar <= negative configured exhaustion threshold
Long: SSL channel direction switches up AND (signal is pending within the configured expiry window AND the opposite SSL crossover has not occurred AND (delta confirmation is disabled, delta is disabled, or Delta EMA > 0; in exhaustion mode, Delta EMA crosses above zero and its prior-lookback minimum <= negative exhaustion threshold) OR a new pending long is allowed by the configured cooldown and alternating-direction rules)
Short: high penetrates above the upper first VWAP band by at least the configured minimum percentage of that band AND close < upper first VWAP band AND the configured same-side cooldown has elapsed
Short: previous close >= previous VWAP AND close < VWAP
Short: Delta EMA crosses below zero AND its highest value over the configured lookback through the prior bar >= configured exhaustion threshold
Short: SSL channel direction switches down AND (signal is pending within the configured expiry window AND the opposite SSL crossover has not occurred AND (delta confirmation is disabled, delta is disabled, or Delta EMA < 0; in exhaustion mode, Delta EMA crosses below zero and its prior-lookback maximum >= exhaustion threshold) OR a new pending short is allowed by the configured cooldown and alternating-direction rules)
