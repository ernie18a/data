Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured opening-range session
Calculation: Opening-range close position = (last opening-range bar close − opening-range low) / (opening-range high − opening-range low)
Calculation: 15-minute EMA = EMA of 15-minute closes, using the last completed 15-minute bar
Long: A close exceeds opening-range high by the acceptance distance, volume exceeds its same-timeframe SMA times the volume multiplier, close is above VWAP and the 15-minute EMA, opening-range close position is at least the bearish threshold, opening-range width is within its configured bounds, and trading is permitted; then, within the configured bar limit and before a close below opening-range high, low reaches opening-range high plus retest tolerance and close finishes above opening-range high while trading remains permitted and the VWAP, EMA, and opening-range position filters still pass
Short: A close falls below opening-range low by the acceptance distance, volume exceeds its same-timeframe SMA times the volume multiplier, close is below VWAP and the 15-minute EMA, opening-range close position is at most the bullish threshold, opening-range width is within its configured bounds, and trading is permitted; then, within the configured bar limit and before a close above opening-range low, high reaches opening-range low minus retest tolerance and close finishes below opening-range low while trading remains permitted and the VWAP, EMA, and opening-range position filters still pass
Exit: Long, high reaches entry plus target distance OR low reaches the base stop (entry minus stop distance), raised to the breakeven stop if high has reached the breakeven trigger; exit also when the active trailing stop is reached if trailing is enabled
Exit: Short, low reaches entry minus target distance OR high reaches the base stop (entry plus stop distance), lowered to the breakeven stop if low has reached the breakeven trigger; exit also when the active trailing stop is reached if trailing is enabled
