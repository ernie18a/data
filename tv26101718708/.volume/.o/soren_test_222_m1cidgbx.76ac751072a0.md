Calculation: Range filter = recursively clamp the source toward its current value by a smoothed range: smooth range is EMA(EMA(abs(source change), shorter lookback), 2 × shorter lookback − 1) × multiplier. When source is above the prior filter, set filter to max(prior filter, source − smooth range); otherwise set it to min(prior filter, source + smooth range). Track consecutive rising and falling filter bars, resetting each count when the filter moves in the opposite direction.
Calculation: Relative Momentum Index = 100 − 100 / (1 + EMA(max(close − close[momentum lookback], 0), lookback) / EMA(max(close[momentum lookback] − close, 0), lookback)); use 0 when the denominator is 0.
Calculation: Position price = mean of the current directional signal sequence’s signal-bar closes and modeled swap-stop prices; a long modeled swap-stop price is the last long signal close × (1 − swap percentage), and a short modeled swap-stop price is the last short signal close × (1 + swap percentage). Reset the opposite direction’s sequence when direction changes.
Long: RSI of VWAP(close) crosses above its oversold level AND high > range-filter upper band AND rising-filter-bar count > 0 AND negative DI is not greater than positive DI OR positive DI > negative DI AND ADX > threshold AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier AND RSI(low) < 70 AND KAMA(source) > its prior value OR Relative Momentum Index crosses above its oversold level AND high > range-filter upper band AND rising-filter-bar count > 0 AND negative DI is not greater than positive DI.
Short: RSI of VWAP(close) crosses below its overbought level AND low < range-filter lower band AND falling-filter-bar count > 0 AND positive DI is not greater than negative DI OR negative DI > positive DI AND ADX > threshold AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier AND RSI(low) > 30 AND KAMA(source) < its prior value OR Relative Momentum Index crosses below its overbought level AND low < range-filter lower band AND falling-filter-bar count > 0 AND positive DI is not greater than negative DI.
Exit: Long, high crosses above Position price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low crosses below Position price × (1 − stop-loss percentage).
Exit: Short, low crosses below Position price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high crosses above Position price × (1 + stop-loss percentage).
