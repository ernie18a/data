Calculation: Delta ratio = (buy volume − sell volume) / total volume; classify lower-timeframe subbar volume as buy when close > open, sell when close < open, and split equally when equal. Use the selected lower timeframe within the current chart bar; if candle-proxy mode is selected or lower-timeframe data is unavailable, use delta = volume × (2×close − high − low)/(high − low), with buy/sell volume = (volume ± delta)/2.
Calculation: Volume z-score = (volume − moving average(volume)) / standard deviation(volume), using the same lookback for both.
Calculation: Body ratio = abs(close − open)/(high − low); small body is below its rolling 30th percentile and large body is above its rolling 70th percentile, using the same adaptive lookback.
Calculation: Swing high/low = rolling highest high/lowest low. Near high/low means the close is within the configured ATR distance of that swing extreme.
Calculation: Chart trend state = positive when close is above the selected VWMA or anchored VWAP (or, in order-flow momentum mode, the candle is up and its body exceeds its rolling average); negative when correspondingly below (or down with a larger-than-average body). Apply the configured ATR-distance filter to VWMA/VWAP modes. State magnitude is 2 when delta ratio is above 0.1 for positive state or below −0.1 for negative state, otherwise 1; retain the prior state when neither side qualifies.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, compare close with its 50-bar VWMA and candle-proxy delta ratio (delta/volume): positive above VWMA, negative below; state magnitude is 2 when delta ratio is above 0.1 for positive state or below −0.1 for negative state, otherwise 1. Neutral when close equals VWMA.
Calculation: Confluence score = one point each for enabled higher-timeframe alignment, chart trend-state alignment, volume z-score > 1, directional delta ratio beyond ±0.1, and proximity to the directional swing extreme.
Long: Chart trend state changes from nonpositive to positive OR (near swing low AND delta ratio > max(70th percentile of absolute delta ratio over the adaptive lookback, 0.1) [or 0.2 when adaptive thresholds are disabled] AND volume z-score > 1 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not negative AND confluence score meets its configured minimum) OR (small body AND volume z-score > 1.5 AND near swing low AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not negative AND confluence score meets its configured minimum) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND up candle AND large body AND close in the top quarter of the bar range AND chart trend state > 0 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not negative) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND up candle AND large body AND near swing high AND chart trend state ≤ 0 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not positive) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND small body AND near swing low AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not positive) OR (up candle AND volume > its moving average AND volume z-score < configured spike threshold).
Short: Chart trend state changes from nonnegative to negative OR (near swing high AND delta ratio < −max(70th percentile of absolute delta ratio over the adaptive lookback, 0.1) [or −0.2 when adaptive thresholds are disabled] AND volume z-score > 1 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not positive AND confluence score meets its configured minimum) OR (small body AND volume z-score > 1.5 AND near swing high AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not positive AND confluence score meets its configured minimum) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND down candle AND large body AND close in the bottom quarter of the bar range AND chart trend state < 0 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not positive) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND down candle AND large body AND near swing low AND chart trend state ≥ 0 AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not negative) OR (volume z-score ≥ configured spike threshold AND small body AND near swing high AND strict higher-timeframe filtering is disabled or higher-timeframe state is not negative) OR (down candle AND volume > its moving average AND volume z-score < configured spike threshold).
