Calculation: Smart Money Index = HMA of the average of the lookback percentile ranks of MFI, CMF, and cumulative-volume-delta momentum; cumulative volume delta adds volume on up bars and subtracts volume on down bars, resets periodically, and its momentum is its change over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Retail Sentiment Index = HMA of the average of the lookback percentile ranks of 14-bar RSI and 14-bar close percentile rank
Calculation: Smart upper/lower zones = rolling 90th/10th percentiles of the Smart Money Index over the normalization lookback
Calculation: Retail upper/lower zones = rolling 90th/10th percentiles of the Retail Sentiment Index over the normalization lookback
Calculation: Stop volume = red candle AND volume above its moving average times a threshold AND candle spread below its moving average times a threshold
Calculation: Buying climax = green candle AND volume above its moving average times a higher threshold than the stop-volume threshold AND candle spread above its moving average times a threshold
Calculation: Bullish divergence = confirmed close pivot low below the preceding confirmed close pivot low AND Smart Money Index at the new pivot above its value at the preceding pivot AND Retail Sentiment Index at the new pivot below the current lower retail zone AND (CVD momentum at the pivot > 0 OR stop-volume events in the three bars ending at the pivot >= the required event count)
Calculation: Bearish divergence = confirmed close pivot high above the preceding confirmed close pivot high AND Smart Money Index at the new pivot below its value at the preceding pivot AND Retail Sentiment Index at the new pivot above the current upper retail zone AND (CVD momentum at the pivot < 0 OR buying-climax events in the three bars ending at the pivot >= the required event count)
Calculation: Bull exhaustion = Retail Sentiment Index above its upper zone AND Smart Money Index above its upper zone AND 5-bar close momentum below its prior value AND red candle
Calculation: Bear exhaustion = Retail Sentiment Index below its lower zone AND Smart Money Index below its lower zone AND 5-bar close momentum above its prior value AND green candle
Long: bullish divergence OR stop volume OR bear exhaustion OR (Smart Money Index below its lower zone AND Smart Money Index rising for two bars AND Retail Sentiment Index < 40 AND close > 200-bar EMA AND close > 800-bar EMA)
Short: bearish divergence OR buying climax OR bull exhaustion OR (Smart Money Index above its upper zone AND Smart Money Index falling for two bars AND Retail Sentiment Index > 60)
