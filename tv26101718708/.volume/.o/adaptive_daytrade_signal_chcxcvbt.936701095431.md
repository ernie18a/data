Calculation: Long stop = if swing stop is enabled, the lower of the lowest low over the swing lookback (including the current bar) and entry price minus ATR times the stop multiplier; otherwise entry price minus ATR times the stop multiplier
Calculation: Short stop = if swing stop is enabled, the higher of the highest high over the swing lookback (including the current bar) and entry price plus ATR times the stop multiplier; otherwise entry price plus ATR times the stop multiplier
Calculation: Long target 2 = entry price + (entry price - long stop) × target 2 risk multiple
Calculation: Short target 2 = entry price - (short stop - entry price) × target 2 risk multiple
Long: last signal side is not long AND ((ADX >= trend threshold AND fast EMA > slower EMA AND trend EMA > its value three bars ago AND close > trend EMA AND low is within the configured percentage zone around fast EMA AND close > open AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP)) OR (ADX < trend threshold AND close <= lower Bollinger Band AND RSI <= oversold threshold AND close > open)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its moving average × configured multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within the configured session)
Short: last signal side is not short AND ((ADX >= trend threshold AND fast EMA < slower EMA AND trend EMA < its value three bars ago AND close < trend EMA AND high is within the configured percentage zone around fast EMA AND close < open AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP)) OR (ADX < trend threshold AND close >= upper Bollinger Band AND RSI >= overbought threshold AND close < open)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume >= its moving average × configured multiplier) AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is within the configured session)
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target 2
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target 2
