Calculation: Major swing pivots = alternating running highs/lows confirmed when price reverses by an ATR-scaled threshold
Calculation: Minor swing pivots = highs/lows confirmed after the same number of bars on each side, using a shorter lookback than major pivots
Long: (close > latest unbroken confirmed major pivot high AND previous close <= that high) OR (close > latest unbroken confirmed minor pivot high AND previous close <= that high) OR (low < nearest eligible minor pivot low AND close > that low AND lower wick >= 0.8 × ATR AND lower wick / (high - low) >= 0.35 AND more than 8 bars since the prior bullish sweep) OR (low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago AND low - high two bars ago >= 0.8 × ATR)
Short: (close < latest unbroken confirmed major pivot low AND previous close >= that low) OR (close < latest unbroken confirmed minor pivot low AND previous close >= that low) OR (high > nearest eligible minor pivot high AND close < that high AND upper wick >= 0.8 × ATR AND upper wick / (high - low) >= 0.35 AND more than 8 bars since the prior bearish sweep) OR (high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago - high >= 0.8 × ATR)
