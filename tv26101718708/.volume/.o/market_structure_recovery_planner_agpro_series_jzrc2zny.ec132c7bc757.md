Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest and preceding confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Bull structure = latest confirmed swing high and low are both above their preceding pivots; bear structure = both are below.
Calculation: Active damage plan = after a permitted new plan, bullish structure damage sets direction long, rail to the latest swing low, and damage extreme to that bar's low; bearish structure damage sets direction short, rail to the latest swing high, and damage extreme to that bar's high. A new plan is permitted when no plan is active, the prior plan failed, an expired plan's wait has elapsed, or active plan age exceeds four recovery windows.
Calculation: Damage threshold = the greater of the configured minimum damage depth and the sensitivity damage buffer, multiplied by ATR; the sensitivity recovery buffer, recovery pocket, and risk-edge distances are configurable ATR multiples.
Calculation: Recovery = while a plan is active and unrecovered, a confirmed close above the rail plus the recovery buffer for a long plan, or below the rail minus the buffer for a short plan; set recovered and start the acceptance hold count at one.
Calculation: Acceptance hold count = after recovery, increment up to the configured acceptance bars plus five while each bar's low (long) stays at least 0.40 times the recovery-pocket distance below the rail, or each bar's high (short) stays at most that distance above it; otherwise decrement to zero. Do not update on the recovery bar.
Calculation: Risk edge = damage extreme minus the configured risk-edge ATR distance for long plans, or plus that distance for short plans.
Calculation: Damage-depth score = linearly score damage depth from 0 at the minimum threshold to 100 at the ideal depth, then decline linearly to 0 at the maximum depth; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Recovery-close score = linearly score close distance beyond the rail, in the recovery direction, from 0 to 100 between zero and the configured strong-recovery ATR distance; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Acceptance score = linearly score the acceptance hold count from 0 to 100 between zero and the configured acceptance bars; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Participation score = current volume divided by its configurable simple moving average; linearly score ratios from 0.70 to the configured volume-confirm ratio as 0 to 100, clamped. If the average is unavailable or nonpositive, use neutral score 50.
Calculation: Room reference = for long plans, the greater of the latest confirmed swing high and the highest high over the configured room lookback; for short plans, the lesser of the latest confirmed swing low and the lowest low over that lookback. Room target = for longs, the greater of this reference and rail plus minimum-room ATR distance; for shorts, the lesser of the reference and rail minus that distance.
Calculation: Room ATR = for longs, max((room target − close) / ATR, 0); for shorts, max((close − room target) / ATR, 0). Room score = linearly score room ATR from 0 at 0.35 times minimum-room ATR to 100 at 1.70 times minimum-room ATR; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Recovery score = clamp((0.20 × damage-depth score + 0.25 × recovery-close score + 0.20 × acceptance score + 0.15 × participation score + 0.20 × room score) × (0.70 + 0.003 × timeliness penalty), 0, 100), where timeliness penalty declines linearly from 100 at age equal to the recovery window to 0 at twice that age, and is clamped to 0–100.
Long: confirmed new-plan condition AND bull structure AND close < latest confirmed swing low − ATR × damage threshold OR active long plan recovers OR active recovered long plan has recovery score ≥ review threshold OR active unrecovered long plan is within 0.80 ATR of its rail OR active recovered long plan is planner-ready (score ≥ ready threshold, not failed or expired, and room ATR ≥ minimum-room ATR).
Short: confirmed new-plan condition AND bear structure AND close > latest confirmed swing high + ATR × damage threshold OR active short plan recovers OR active recovered short plan has recovery score ≥ review threshold OR active unrecovered short plan is within 0.80 ATR of its rail OR active recovered short plan is planner-ready (score ≥ ready threshold, not failed or expired, and room ATR ≥ minimum-room ATR).
Exit: long, active recovered long plan AND confirmed close < risk edge.
Exit: short, active recovered short plan AND confirmed close > risk edge.
