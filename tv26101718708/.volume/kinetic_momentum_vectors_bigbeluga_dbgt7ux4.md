## Description

[b]Kinetic Momentum Vectors [BigBeluga][/b] is a high-performance analytical framework that reimagines price action as a physical system. By calculating the "mass" (volume) and "velocity" (rate of change), the script identifies the [b]Kinetic Energy[/b] driving a trend. Instead of traditional lagging oscillators, this tool projects momentum as external "propulsion vectors" anchored to a trend baseline, allowing you to see exactly when a move is fueled by institutional conviction or retail exhaustion.

[b]🔵 THE PHYSICS OF MOMENTUM[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Energy Normalization (0-1 Engine):[/b] The indicator processes volume-weighted price movement through a Min-Max normalization scaler. This isolates the most explosive "Kinetic Spikes" within your chosen lookback period, ensuring you only react to the most significant institutional surges.
[*] [b]Propulsion Fuel Candles:[/b] Momentum is visually detached from price and plotted as "fuel candles" relative to a central EMA. Bullish energy accelerates downward from the baseline, while bearish energy expands upward, creating a "momentum vacuum" that reveals the true strength of a trend's engine.
[image]https://www.tradingview.com/x/YclyBZzH/[/image]
[*] [b]Kinetic Spike Anchors:[/b] When normalization hits the "Spike Threshold," the script locks in a structural vector. These are the exact price levels where maximum work was performed by the market, serving as high-probability pivots for future support and resistance.
[image]https://www.tradingview.com/x/DBRGBej7/[/image]
[/list]

[b]🔵 STRUCTURAL INTELLIGENCE[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Refining Vector Levels:[/b] Unlike static lines, these levels feature a [b]3-bar confirmation window[/b]. The script dynamically updates the vector to the absolute high or low of the momentum event, ensuring your structural anchors are pixel-perfect.
[*] [b]Adaptive Level States:[/b] Vectors transition from solid to dashed the moment they are breached. This "Style-Override" logic provides an instant visual alert that a momentum barrier has failed and a potential "S/R Flip" is in progress.
[image]https://www.tradingview.com/x/FiMHZ7S2/[/image]
[*] [b]Auto-Scaling ATR Visuals:[/b] The vertical height of the momentum candles is governed by current volatility (ATR). This ensures that the visual data remains legible and proportional, whether you are trading a low-volatility Asian session or a high-volatility New York open.
[/list]

[b]🔵 CORE UTILITY[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Identifying "Empty" Trends:[/b] A healthy trend requires increasing Kinetic Fuel. If price continues to climb while the fuel candles shrink back toward the baseline, you are looking at a "low-kinetic" divergence—a primary warning sign of a trend reversal.
[*] [b]Institutional S/R Mapping:[/b] Use the Kinetic Spike labels as "hard" targets. Because these levels are generated by high-volume price displacement, they represent areas where big players have already shown their hand.
[*] [b]Momentum Breakout Confirmation:[/b] Enter trades when a price breakout is accompanied by a full-sized kinetic vector. If the fuel candles remain small during a breakout, it lacks the "mass" required to sustain the move.
[*] [b]The Baseline Magnet:[/b] The trend baseline acts as the gravitational center. When price overextends and kinetic energy hits a 1.0 spike, look for a mean-reversion move back toward the EMA baseline.
[/list]

[b]Kinetic Momentum Vectors[/b] strips away the noise of standard technical analysis to reveal the raw energy moving the market. By monitoring the fuel behind the price, you can trade with the flow of institutional kinetic energy and avoid the traps of exhausted trends.

````pine
// This work is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International

//@version=6
indicator("Kinetic Momentum Vectors [BigBeluga]", overlay=true, max_bars_back=500)

// ＩＮＰＵＴＳ ――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――{

int   emaLen        = input.int(100, "Trend Baseline Length", minval=1, tooltip="The period for the EMA that defines the primary trend direction.")
int   normLookback  = input.int(100, "Normalization Lookback", minval=10, tooltip="The lookback period used to determine the Min/Max range for the 0-1 kinetic energy scaling.")
float spikeThresh   = input.float(1.0, "Spike Threshold (0-1)", minval=0.1, maxval=1.0, step = 0.1, tooltip="The sensitivity for level creation. 1.0 means levels only form on the highest kinetic energy bar of the lookback.")
float candleOffset  = input.float(0.5, "Offset from Price (ATR %)", minval=0, tooltip="Vertical distance between the baseline and the start of the fuel candles.")
color bullColor     = input.color(#DDA94B, "Bullish Fuel Color", tooltip="Color used for bullish momentum and upward breakouts.")
color bearColor     = input.color(#2b5ca5, "Bearish Fuel Color", tooltip="Color used for bearish momentum and downward breakdowns.")

bool  showFuel      = input.bool(true, "Show Kinetic Fuel Candles", group="Visibility", tooltip="Toggle the visibility of the volume-price momentum candles.")
bool  showLevels    = input.bool(true, "Show Spike Levels", group="Visibility", tooltip="Toggle the visibility of the extended horizontal levels generated by volume spikes.")
bool  showBaseLine  = input.bool(true, "Show Baseline", group="Visibility", tooltip="Toggle the visibility of the trend baseline")

// }

// ＣＡＬＣＵＬＡＴＩＯＮＳ――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――{

// 1. Trend & Baseline Logic
float baseline = ta.ema(close, emaLen)
bool isTrendUp = close > baseline
bool trendChanged = ta.change(isTrendUp)

// 2. Kinetic Energy (Mass x Velocity)
float priceChange = close - close[1]
float kineticFuel = volume * math.abs(priceChange)

// 3. Min-Max Normalization
float maxFuel = ta.highest(kineticFuel, normLookback)
float minFuel = ta.lowest(kineticFuel, normLookback)
float normalizedFuel = (maxFuel > minFuel) ? (kineticFuel - minFuel) / (maxFuel - minFuel) : 0

// 4. Visual Scaling (ATR based)
float atr = ta.atr(200)
float scaledHeight = normalizedFuel * atr * 2
float gap = atr * candleOffset

// 5. Level & Spike State Machine
var line spikeLine = na
var label spikeLbl = na
var int start = 0
var bool top = false

bool isSpike = normalizedFuel >= spikeThresh and barstate.isconfirmed
float rsi = ta.rsi(close, 14)
float src = rsi > 50 ? high : low

// Level Creation
if isSpike and not trendChanged and bar_index - start > 5 and showLevels
    start := bar_index
    top := rsi > 50
    
    spikeLine := line.new(bar_index, src, bar_index, src, 
         color=chart.fg_color, 
         width=2, 
         style=line.style_solid)

    string txt = top ? str.tostring(high, "#.##") + "\n▼" : "▲\n" + str.tostring(low, "#.##")
    spikeLbl := label.new(bar_index, src, txt, 
         style = top ? label.style_label_down : label.style_label_up, 
         color = color.new(chart.fg_color, 100), 
         textcolor = top ? bearColor : bullColor)

// Level Updating (3-bar confirmation window)
if bar_index - start < 3 and not na(spikeLine) and showLevels
    if top
        if high > line.get_y1(spikeLine)
            line.set_xy1(spikeLine, bar_index, high)
            line.set_y2(spikeLine, high)
            label.set_xy(spikeLbl, bar_index, high)
            label.set_text(spikeLbl, str.tostring(high, "#.##") + "\n▼")
    else 
        if low < line.get_y1(spikeLine)
            line.set_xy1(spikeLine, bar_index, low)
            line.set_y2(spikeLine, low)
            label.set_xy(spikeLbl, bar_index, low)
            label.set_text(spikeLbl, "▲\n" + str.tostring(low, "#.##"))

// Style Override on Breach
if not na(spikeLine) and showLevels
    if top and high > line.get_y1(spikeLine) 
        line.set_style(spikeLine, line.style_dashed)
        line.set_width(spikeLine, 1)
    else if not top and low < line.get_y1(spikeLine)
        line.set_style(spikeLine, line.style_dashed)
        line.set_width(spikeLine, 1)

// Level Extension & Color Logic
if not na(spikeLine) and not trendChanged and showLevels
    line.set_x2(spikeLine, bar_index)
    line.set_color(spikeLine, close > line.get_y1(spikeLine) ? bullColor : bearColor)

// }

// ＰＬＯＴ ――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――――{

// Baseline Plot
plot(showBaseLine ? baseline : na, "Baseline", color.new(chart.fg_color, 60), 1)

// Kinetic Fuel Candles (Conditional Visibility)
float bullOpen  = baseline - gap
float bullClose = bullOpen - scaledHeight
float bearOpen  = baseline + gap
float bearClose = bearOpen + scaledHeight

plotcandle(showFuel and isTrendUp and not trendChanged ? bullOpen : na, bullOpen, bullClose, bullClose, 
     title="Bullish Fuel", color=bullColor, wickcolor=bullColor, bordercolor=bullColor)

plotcandle(showFuel and not isTrendUp and not trendChanged ? bearOpen : na, bearOpen, bearClose, bearClose, 
     title="Bearish Fuel", color=bearColor, wickcolor=bearColor, bordercolor=bearColor)

// Spike Indicators (Shapes)
plotshape(showFuel and isSpike and not trendChanged and not isTrendUp ? bearClose : na, "Bear Spike", shape.square, location.absolute, color=bearColor, size=size.tiny)
plotshape(showFuel and isSpike and not trendChanged and isTrendUp ? bullClose : na, "Bull Spike", shape.square, location.absolute, color=bullColor, size=size.tiny)

// }
````
