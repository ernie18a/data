Calculation: H = highest open of completed higher-timeframe bars over the long-entry lookback
Calculation: L = lowest open of completed higher-timeframe bars over the short-entry lookback
Long: close two bars ago > H AND H two bars ago > H one bar ago AND current close > H
Short: close two bars ago < L AND L two bars ago > L one bar ago AND current close < L
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + profit distance OR low <= entry price - loss distance
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - profit distance OR high >= entry price + loss distance
