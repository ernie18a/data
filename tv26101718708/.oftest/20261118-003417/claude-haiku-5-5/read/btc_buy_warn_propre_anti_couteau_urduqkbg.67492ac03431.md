Calculation: POC = lower price edge of the bin holding the greatest volume accumulated over the last POC lookback bars, where the price range from the lowest low to the highest high of those bars is split into POC bins of equal width
Calculation: CumDelta = cumulative sum over all bars of (volume if close > open; -volume if close < open; 0 otherwise)
Long: ABS(close - POC) / close * 100 <= POC max distance % AND (POC >= POC[POC change lookback] OR close > POC) AND close > EMA(close, EMA shorter length) AND EMA(close, EMA shorter length) > EMA(close, EMA longer length) AND (close > high[1] OR close crosses above EMA(close, EMA shorter length)) AND CumDelta > SMA(CumDelta, CumDelta average length) AND ((RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI entry max AND LOWEST(RSI(close, RSI length), RSI low lookback) < RSI low level AND RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI(close, RSI length)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI length) > SMA(RSI(close, RSI length), RSI average length)) OR (volume > SMA(volume, volume average length) * Smart Money volume multiple AND close > open AND open - low >= (close - open) * lower wick ratio) OR (low < LOWEST(low[1], price low lookback) AND CumDelta > LOWEST(CumDelta[1], CumDelta low lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI length) < RSI mid level)) AND (no previous Long OR bars since previous Long > cooldown bars)
Short: ((volume > SMA(volume, volume average length) * Smart Money volume multiple AND close < open AND close < low + (high - low) * bear close ratio) OR (RSI(close, RSI length) > RSI overbought level AND CumDelta < SMA(CumDelta, CumDelta average length)) OR (close < EMA(close, EMA shorter length) AND EMA(close, EMA shorter length) < EMA(close, EMA longer length) AND CumDelta < SMA(CumDelta, CumDelta average length))) AND (no previous Short OR bars since previous Short > cooldown bars)
