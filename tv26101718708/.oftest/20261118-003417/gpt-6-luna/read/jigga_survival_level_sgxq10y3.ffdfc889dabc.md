Calculation: score = sum of: +1 when MACD(close, long-term lookback, short-term lookback, signal lookback) is above zero and above its signal line, 0 when it is above zero and at or below its signal line or at or below zero and above its signal line, otherwise -1; +1 when its histogram is nonnegative and greater than its prior value, 0 when its histogram is nonnegative and at most its prior value or is negative and greater than its prior value, otherwise -1; +1 when EMA(RSI(close, lookback), smoothing lookback) is above upper threshold, -1 when below lower threshold, otherwise 0; +1 when EMA(close, shorter lookback) is above EMA(close, longer lookback), otherwise -1; +1 when EMA(Williams %R(close, high, low, lookback), smoothing lookback) is above upper threshold, -1 when below lower threshold, otherwise 0; +1 when ADX(high, low, close, directional-movement lookback, smoothing lookback) is above EMA(ADX, smoothing lookback) and close is above EMA(close, short lookback), -1 when ADX is above that EMA and close is at or below that EMA, otherwise 0; +1 when Chaikin Money Flow(high, low, close, volume, lookback) is above upper threshold, -1 when below the negative upper threshold, otherwise 0; and Supertrend direction(high, low, close, ATR lookback, factor).
Calculation: adjusted score = 0 when score is nonnegative and its prior value is nonpositive, or score is nonpositive and its prior value is nonnegative; otherwise score.
Calculation: level = high multiplied by one plus an offset when adjusted score crosses from negative to nonnegative; low multiplied by one minus an offset when adjusted score crosses from positive to nonpositive; otherwise its prior value. Retain the level color as positive after the first crossing and negative after the second.
Long: high is above level and level color is positive OR adjusted score and its prior value are both positive.
Exit: long, low is below level and level color is negative OR adjusted score and its prior value are both negative.
