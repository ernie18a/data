Calculation: first-hour high strike = ceiling(first-hour high / P1) × P1 for Nifty, or ceiling(first-hour high / P2) × P2 for Bank Nifty
Calculation: first-hour low strike = floor(first-hour low / P1) × P1 for Nifty, or floor(first-hour low / P2) × P2 for Bank Nifty
Calculation: straddle strike = (first-hour high strike + first-hour low strike) / 2
Option: sell call, underlying price is near the straddle strike
Option: sell put, underlying price is near the straddle strike
Option: sell call, underlying price is near the straddle strike AND call strike is the first-hour high strike
Option: sell put, underlying price is near the straddle strike AND put strike is the first-hour low strike
