Calculation: Nifty call strike = (round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval < first-hour high of the underlying on T1 ? round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval + Nifty strike interval : round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval)
Calculation: Nifty put strike = (round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval > first-hour low of the underlying on T1 ? round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval − Nifty strike interval : round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval)
Calculation: Bank Nifty call strike = (round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval < first-hour high of the underlying on T1 ? round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval + Bank Nifty strike interval : round(first-hour high of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval)
Calculation: Bank Nifty put strike = (round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval > first-hour low of the underlying on T1 ? round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval − Bank Nifty strike interval : round(first-hour low of the underlying on T1 / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval)
Calculation: straddle strike = (Nifty call strike + Nifty put strike) / 2 for Nifty; (Bank Nifty call strike + Bank Nifty put strike) / 2 for Bank Nifty
Option: sell call, underlying price is near the straddle strike after the first hour is complete
Option: sell put, underlying price is near the straddle strike after the first hour is complete
Option: sell call at the money, underlying price is near the straddle strike after the first hour is complete
Option: sell put at the money, underlying price is near the straddle strike after the first hour is complete
