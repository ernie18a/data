Calculation: DMI strength = 100 × RMA(abs(+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI, or 1 when zero), shared lookback), where +DI and −DI are 100 × RMA(positive directional movement, shared lookback) / RMA(true range, shared lookback) and 100 × RMA(negative directional movement, shared lookback) / RMA(true range, shared lookback); positive directional movement is the high change when it is positive and greater than the absolute low change, otherwise zero; negative directional movement is the absolute low change when it is positive and greater than the high change, otherwise zero
Calculation: MACD line = EMA(close, fast lookback) − EMA(close, slow lookback)
Calculation: RSI = 100 − 100 / (1 + RMA(max(change(close), 0), shared lookback) / RMA(−min(change(close), 0), shared lookback)), using 100 when the latter RMA is zero and 0 when the former RMA is zero
Calculation: Williams %R = 100 × (close − highest(high, lookback)) / (highest(high, lookback) − lowest(low, lookback))
Calculation: Money flow ratio = sum(money flow, lookback) / sum(volume, lookback), where money flow is zero when close equals high and low or high equals low, otherwise (2 × close − low − high) / (high − low) × volume
Calculation: Trend direction = positive initially; thereafter negative when prior direction is positive and close is below the prior lower band, positive when prior direction is negative and close is above the prior upper band, otherwise prior direction. The lower band is the greater of (high + low) / 2 − ATR(lookback) × multiplier and its prior value when prior close is above that prior value; otherwise it is the former. The upper band is the lesser of (high + low) / 2 + ATR(lookback) × multiplier and its prior value when prior close is below that prior value; otherwise it is the former.
Calculation: Score = MACD score + histogram score + RSI score + EMA score + strength score + Williams %R score + money flow score + trend direction, where MACD score is 1 when MACD line is positive and above its EMA(signal lookback), −1 when MACD line is nonpositive and not above that EMA, otherwise 0; histogram is MACD line minus its EMA(signal lookback); histogram score is 1 when histogram is nonnegative and greater than its prior value, −1 when it is negative and not greater than its prior value, otherwise 0; RSI score is 1 above the upper threshold, −1 below the lower threshold, otherwise 0, using EMA(RSI, RSI smoothing lookback); EMA score is 1 when EMA(close, short lookback) is above EMA(close, long lookback), otherwise −1; strength score is 1 when DMI strength is above EMA(DMI strength, strength smoothing lookback) and close is above EMA(close, price lookback), −1 when it is above that EMA and close is not above EMA(close, price lookback), otherwise 0; Williams %R score is 1 when EMA(Williams %R, Williams %R smoothing lookback) is above the negative lower threshold, −1 when below the negative upper threshold, otherwise 0; money flow score is 1 above the positive threshold, −1 below the negative threshold, otherwise 0
Calculation: Filtered score = 0 when Score and its prior value cross or touch zero in either direction, otherwise Score
Calculation: Level = high × (1 + offset) when prior Filtered score is negative and current Filtered score is nonnegative; low × (1 − offset) when prior Filtered score is positive and current Filtered score is nonpositive; otherwise its prior value
Calculation: Level color = green when prior Filtered score is negative and current Filtered score is nonnegative; red when prior Filtered score is positive and current Filtered score is nonpositive; otherwise its prior color
Long: (high > Level AND Level color is green) OR (prior Filtered score > 0 AND Filtered score > 0)
Exit: long, (low < Level AND Level color is red) OR (prior Filtered score < 0 AND Filtered score < 0)
