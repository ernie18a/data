Calculation: EMA = EMA(close, lookback)
Calculation: RSI = RSI(close, lookback)
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of (volume when close > open, negative volume when close < open, otherwise zero)
Calculation: POC = price of the highest-volume bin among bins spanning the highest high to lowest low over the lookback
Calculation: SmartBuy = volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND close > open AND min(open, close) − low ≥ abs(close − open) × wick ratio
Calculation: SmartSell = volume > SMA(volume, volume lookback) × volume multiplier AND close < open AND close < low + (high − low) × close-range ratio
Long: more than cooldown bars since the previous Long signal AND abs(close − POC(lookback, bin count)) / close × 100 ≤ maximum POC distance AND (POC(lookback, bin count) ≥ POC(lookback, bin count)[POC comparison lookback] OR close > POC(lookback, bin count)) AND close > EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) > EMA(close, longer lookback) AND (close > high[1] OR close crosses above EMA(close, shorter lookback)) AND CVD > SMA(CVD, CVD lookback) AND (RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI entry threshold AND lowest(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI low lookback) < RSI low threshold AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI(close, RSI lookback)[1] AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) > SMA(RSI(close, RSI lookback), RSI smoothing lookback) OR SmartBuy OR (low < lowest(low[1], divergence lookback) AND CVD > lowest(CVD[1], divergence lookback) AND RSI(close, RSI lookback) < RSI divergence threshold))
Short: more than cooldown bars since the previous Short signal AND (SmartSell OR (RSI(close, RSI lookback) > RSI warning threshold AND CVD < SMA(CVD, CVD lookback)) OR (close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND CVD < SMA(CVD, CVD lookback)))
