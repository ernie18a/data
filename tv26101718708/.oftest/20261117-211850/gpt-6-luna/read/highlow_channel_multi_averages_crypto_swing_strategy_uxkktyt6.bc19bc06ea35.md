Calculation: High average = arithmetic mean of the simple, exponential, weighted, Arnaud Legoux, Wilder, linear regression, and volume-weighted moving averages of high, using the same lookback parameter
Calculation: Low average = arithmetic mean of the simple, exponential, weighted, Arnaud Legoux, Wilder, linear regression, and volume-weighted moving averages of low, using the same lookback parameter
Long: close > High average
Short: close < Low average
Exit: long, price >= entry price + entry price × long take-profit parameter OR price <= entry price − entry price × long stop-loss parameter OR low crosses below Low average OR close < Low average
Exit: short, price <= entry price − entry price × short take-profit parameter OR price >= entry price + entry price × short stop-loss parameter OR high crosses above High average OR close > High average
