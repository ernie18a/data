Calculation: htf_close = close of the higher_tf bar two bars before the higher_tf bar still forming
Calculation: htf_open = open of the higher_tf bar two bars before the higher_tf bar still forming
Calculation: hma(x, n) = WMA of (2 * WMA of x over n/2 chart bars - WMA of x over n chart bars) over round(sqrt(n)) chart bars
Calculation: thma(x, n) = 3 * (hma(x, n) - hma(hma(x, n), n)) + hma(hma(hma(x, n), n), n)
Calculation: smoothed_close = thma(htf_close, ma_length)
Calculation: smoothed_open = thma(htf_open, ma_length)
Calculation: ha_close = (open + high + low + close) / 4 of the current chart bar
Calculation: rsi = RSI of ha_close over rsi_length chart bars with Wilder smoothing
Calculation: short_ma = SMA or EMA (per ma_type) of close over short_length chart bars
Calculation: long_ma = SMA or EMA (per ma_type) of close over long_length chart bars, where long_length > short_length
Calculation: po = (short_ma - long_ma) / long_ma * 100
Long: smoothed_close > smoothed_open AND smoothed_close[1] <= smoothed_open[1] AND rsi > rsi_threshold AND (po > po_threshold OR po > po[1]) AND po < po_upper_limit
Exit: long, smoothed_close < smoothed_open AND smoothed_close[1] >= smoothed_open[1]
