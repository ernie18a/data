Calculation: HTFOpen = open of the bar still forming on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: HighestOpen = EMA of length N of the highest HTFOpen over the last N bars
Calculation: LowestOpen = EMA of length N of the lowest HTFOpen over the last N bars
Long: SMA of HighestOpen over a shorter length crosses below SMA of HighestOpen over a longer length
Short: SMA of LowestOpen over a shorter length crosses above SMA of LowestOpen over a longer length
