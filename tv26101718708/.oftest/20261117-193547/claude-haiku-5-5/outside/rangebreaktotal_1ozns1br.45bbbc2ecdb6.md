Go long on the traded instrument when the close of the total crypto market capitalization on the current bar is greater than the highest such close over the previous N bars, where N is a lookback-length parameter, and the current bar time is strictly after a window-start parameter and strictly before a window-end parameter.
Go short on the traded instrument when the close of the total crypto market capitalization on the current bar is less than the lowest such close over the previous N bars, where N is the lookback-length parameter, and the current bar time is strictly after the window-start parameter and strictly before the window-end parameter.
