Calculation: Highest daily open over the previous 16 bars = highest of the daily open from the previous chart bar over 16 chart bars, using the daily bar still forming.
Calculation: Lowest daily open over the previous 16 bars = lowest of the daily open from the previous chart bar over 16 chart bars, using the daily bar still forming.
Long: close > Highest daily open over the previous 16 bars AND the value of Highest daily open over the previous 16 bars two chart bars ago > its value one chart bar ago.
Short: close < Lowest daily open over the previous 16 bars AND the value of Lowest daily open over the previous 16 bars two chart bars ago > its value one chart bar ago.
Exit: long, price >= entry price + profit distance in ticks OR price <= entry price - loss distance in ticks.
Exit: short, price <= entry price - profit distance in ticks OR price >= entry price + loss distance in ticks.
