Calculation: Upper level = EMA of the rolling highest of opening prices from another timeframe, using separate parameters for the highest lookback and EMA length; use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Lower level = EMA of the rolling lowest of opening prices from another timeframe, using separate parameters for the lowest lookback and EMA length; use the last completed bar.
Long: The shorter moving average of the upper level crosses below its longer moving average.
Short: The shorter moving average of the lower level crosses above its longer moving average.
