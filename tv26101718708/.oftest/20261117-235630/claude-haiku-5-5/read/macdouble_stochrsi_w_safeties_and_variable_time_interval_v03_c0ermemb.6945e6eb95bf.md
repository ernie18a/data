Calculation: MACD histogram(fast, slow, signal) = MACD(close, fast, slow) - SMA(MACD(close, fast, slow), signal)
Calculation: StochRSI K = SMA(StochRSI(close, RSI lookback, stoch lookback), K smoothing lookback)
Calculation: StochRSI D = SMA(StochRSI K, D smoothing lookback)
Long: MACD histogram(fast lookback of shorter MACD, slow lookback of shorter MACD, signal lookback of shorter MACD) on chart bars > 0 AND YEAR(datetime) > start year AND MACD histogram(fast lookback of longer MACD, slow lookback of longer MACD, signal lookback of longer MACD) on T1 bars, bar still forming > 0 AND close + high > close[1] + high[2] AND StochRSI K on T2 bars, bar still forming ≠ 0 AND StochRSI D on T2 bars, bar still forming < buy threshold AND StochRSI K on T2 bars, bar still forming > StochRSI D on T2 bars, bar still forming
Short: MACD histogram(fast lookback of shorter MACD, slow lookback of shorter MACD, signal lookback of shorter MACD) on chart bars < 0 AND YEAR(datetime) > start year AND MACD histogram(fast lookback of longer MACD, slow lookback of longer MACD, signal lookback of longer MACD) on T1 bars, bar still forming < 0 AND close + low < close[1] + low[2] AND StochRSI K on T2 bars, bar still forming ≠ 0 AND StochRSI D on T2 bars, bar still forming > sell threshold AND StochRSI K on T2 bars, bar still forming < StochRSI D on T2 bars, bar still forming
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop loss distance
