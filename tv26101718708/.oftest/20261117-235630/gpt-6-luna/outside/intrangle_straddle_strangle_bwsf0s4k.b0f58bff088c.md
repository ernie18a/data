Calculation: Upper option strike = ceiling(T1 high during the first hour / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval for Nifty; ceiling(T1 high during the first hour / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval for Bank Nifty
Calculation: Lower option strike = floor(T1 low during the first hour / Nifty strike interval) × Nifty strike interval for Nifty; floor(T1 low during the first hour / Bank Nifty strike interval) × Bank Nifty strike interval for Bank Nifty
Calculation: Straddle level = (Upper option strike + Lower option strike) / 2
Option: sell call, underlying price is near Straddle level and call strike is at the money
Option: sell put, underlying price is near Straddle level and put strike is at the money
Option: sell call, underlying price is near Straddle level and call strike = Upper option strike
Option: sell put, underlying price is near Straddle level and put strike = Lower option strike
