Calculation: Up volume = on T1 bars, if weighted wicks are enabled, for an up bar allocate volume × (top wick + body + bottom wick) / (2 × top wick + body + 2 × bottom wick), and for a down bar allocate volume × (top wick + bottom wick) / (2 × top wick + body + 2 × bottom wick); otherwise allocate all volume to up volume when close ≥ open; a doji contributes zero when weighted wicks are enabled
Calculation: Down volume = on T1 bars, if weighted wicks are enabled, for an up bar allocate volume × (top wick + bottom wick) / (2 × top wick + body + 2 × bottom wick), and for a down bar allocate volume × (top wick + body + bottom wick) / (2 × top wick + body + 2 × bottom wick); otherwise allocate all volume to down volume when close < open; a doji contributes zero when weighted wicks are enabled
Calculation: Volume RSI = 100 × RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × mapped up volume when source > source[1], RSI lookback P2) / (RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × mapped up volume when source > source[1], P2) + RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × mapped down volume when source < source[1], P2)); map summed T1 up and down volumes to chart bars using the T1 bar still forming in real time and the last completed T1 bar historically
Calculation: Direction state = 1 when Volume RSI > 50 + 50 × threshold P3; otherwise 1 when Volume RSI > 50 − 50 × P3 and the prior chart bar's Direction state > 0; otherwise −1 when Volume RSI < 50 − 50 × P3; otherwise −1 when Volume RSI < 50 + 50 × P3 and the prior chart bar's Direction state < 0; otherwise 0
Long: Direction state = 1
Short: Direction state = −1
