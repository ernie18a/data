Enter long on the traded instrument when the close of the total crypto market capitalization series (data external to the traded instrument) is greater than the highest of that series' closes over the previous N bars, excluding the current bar, and the current bar time is between a start date and an end date.
Enter short on the traded instrument when the close of the total crypto market capitalization series (data external to the traded instrument) is less than the lowest of that series' closes over the previous N bars, excluding the current bar, and the current bar time is between a start date and an end date.
