Calculation: Relative Rotation Graph RS-Ratio = normalized EMA of sector close relative to benchmark close
Calculation: Relative Rotation Graph RS-Momentum = normalized rate of change of the Relative Rotation Graph RS-Ratio
Long: Relative Rotation Graph RS-Momentum >= 100
Short: Relative Rotation Graph RS-Ratio < 100 AND Relative Rotation Graph RS-Momentum < 100
Exit: long, Relative Rotation Graph RS-Ratio >= 100 AND Relative Rotation Graph RS-Momentum < 100
