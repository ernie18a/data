Calculation: UpVolume = T1-bar volume assigned to up moves; when weighted wicks are enabled, for an up bar assign volume × (high − low) / (2(high − close) + (close − open) + 2(open − low)), and for a down bar assign volume × ((high − open) + (close − low)) / (2(high − open) + (open − close) + 2(close − low)); otherwise assign full volume to up bars and zero to down bars
Calculation: DownVolume = T1-bar volume assigned to down moves; when weighted wicks are enabled, for an up bar assign volume × ((high − close) + (open − low)) / (2(high − close) + (close − open) + 2(open − low)), and for a down bar assign volume × (high − low) / (2(high − open) + (open − close) + 2(close − low)); otherwise assign full volume to down bars and zero to up bars
Calculation: VolumeRSI = 100 × RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × UpVolume when source > source[1], RSI lookback) / (RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × UpVolume when source > source[1], RSI lookback) + RMA(abs(source − source[1]) × DownVolume when source < source[1], RSI lookback)); calculate on chart bars using the T1 volume sums mapped to each chart bar
Long: VolumeRSI > 50 + 50 × threshold OR (VolumeRSI > 50 − 50 × threshold AND prior trend is long)
Short: VolumeRSI < 50 − 50 × threshold OR (VolumeRSI < 50 + 50 × threshold AND prior trend is short)
