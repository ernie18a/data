Long: close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND close > EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T3, last completed bar) AND StochRSI K (RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) crosses above StochRSI D (D smoothing)
Short: close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T1, last completed bar) AND close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T2, last completed bar) AND close < EMA(close, HTF EMA lookback, T3, last completed bar) AND StochRSI K (RSI lookback, stochastic lookback, K smoothing) crosses below StochRSI D (D smoothing)
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR (ATR lookback, chart timeframe, at entry) × stop multiplier OR high >= entry price + ATR (ATR lookback, chart timeframe, at entry) × take-profit multiplier
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR (ATR lookback, chart timeframe, at entry) × stop multiplier OR low <= entry price - ATR (ATR lookback, chart timeframe, at entry) × take-profit multiplier
