Calculation: Consensus score = sum of: +1 when MACD(fast lookback, longer lookback, signal lookback) is above zero and above its signal line, −1 when it is at or below zero and at or below its signal line, otherwise 0; +1 when its histogram is positive and rising, −1 when it is negative and not rising, otherwise 0; +1 when EMA(RSI(shorter lookback), smoothing lookback) is above the upper threshold, −1 when it is below the lower threshold, otherwise 0; +1 when EMA(close, shorter lookback) is above EMA(close, longer lookback), otherwise −1; +1 when EMA(ADX(DI lookback, smoothing lookback), lookback) is rising and close is above EMA(close, lookback), −1 when that ADX condition holds and close is at or below that EMA, otherwise 0; +1 when EMA(Williams %R(lookback), smoothing lookback) is above the negative lower threshold, −1 when it is below the negative upper threshold, otherwise 0; +1 when Chaikin Money Flow(lookback) is above its positive threshold, −1 when it is below its negative threshold, otherwise 0; and +1 or −1 according to Supertrend(ATR lookback, multiplier) direction. The upper and lower thresholds are shared by the RSI and Williams %R comparisons; the MACD fast lookback is shorter than its longer lookback.
Calculation: Filtered score = 0 when Consensus score crosses or touches zero from either side; otherwise Consensus score.
Calculation: Signal level and direction = start with level 0 and bullish direction; on a bar where Filtered score changes from negative to nonnegative, set level to high × (1 + offset) and direction bullish; where it changes from positive to nonpositive, set level to low × (1 − offset) and direction bearish; otherwise, if the previous level is nonzero, set level to 0, and retain the previous direction. On other bars retain the previous level and direction.
Long: high > Signal level and Signal direction is bullish OR Filtered score[1] > 0 AND Filtered score > 0
Exit: long, low < Signal level and Signal direction is bearish OR Filtered score[1] < 0 AND Filtered score < 0
