## Description

[i]Crypto Cycle Navigator! by Haramii[/i]

Crypto Cycle Navigator by Haramii is a market cycle and risk management indicator designed for crypto swing trading, position trading and longer term investing.

The purpose of this indicator is not to predict the exact bottom or the exact top. The purpose is to help the user identify market phases, accumulation areas, trend confirmation, risk zones and potential profit taking areas.

This indicator is built around the idea that crypto markets move in cycles. Strong entries usually happen when the market is fearful, compressed or recovering from a large decline. Strong exits usually happen when price becomes extended, momentum becomes overheated or the market starts showing weakness after a strong move.

The indicator is designed around partial position management. Instead of using all in and all out signals, it focuses on gradual buying and gradual selling.

Main concept

Buy in portions.
Hold through the stronger part of the trend.
Take profit gradually.
Do not trust any single signal alone.
Always confirm with market structure, volume, support, resistance and higher time frames.

Core features

[b]1. Market phase detection[/b]

The dashboard shows the current market phase.

Possible phases include:

BULL

This means the market structure is strong. Price and trend conditions are aligned in a positive direction. This phase is usually better for holding positions and buying healthy pullbacks.

EARLY BULL

This means the market is starting to recover, but the full bull structure is not fully confirmed yet. This can be an interesting area, but risk is still higher than in a confirmed bull phase.

ACCUMULATION

This means the market may be trading in a lower risk area after a large decline or extended weakness. It does not mean the bottom is guaranteed. It only means the risk reward may be improving.

NEUTRAL

This means the market does not have a clear direction. In this phase, signals should be treated with more caution.

BEAR

This means the market structure is weak. New long positions should be avoided unless there is a clear higher time frame reason for a recovery setup.

[b]2. Monthly filter[/b]

The monthly filter is used as the main market direction filter.

Monthly GREEN means the broader market structure is supportive.

Monthly EARLY means the market is improving, but not fully confirmed.

Monthly RED means the broader market structure is weak.

The lower time frame signals should not be used blindly without checking the monthly condition. A buy signal is stronger when the monthly filter is green or improving. A buy signal is weaker when the monthly filter is red.

[b]3. Weekly cycle filter[/b]

The weekly data is used to detect larger cycle movement and recovery conditions.

The weekly RSI helps identify whether the market is deeply depressed, recovering or already strong.

A weekly recovery condition can help confirm that the market is moving out of a weak phase.

This is important because many fake daily signals appear during bear markets. The weekly filter helps reduce some of that noise.

[b]4. Daily entries[/b]

The daily chart is mainly used for timing entries and exits.

The indicator may show several types of buy labels.

BOTTOM BUY 25 percent

This appears when the indicator detects a possible accumulation or lower risk area. This does not mean the bottom is confirmed. It means the market may be entering an area where a small partial position can be considered.

CONFIRM BUY 25 percent

This appears when price action and momentum begin to confirm a recovery.

DIP BUY 25 percent

This appears during stronger market phases when price pulls back into a healthier area and then starts to recover.

BREAKOUT BUY 25 percent

This appears when price breaks above a previous structure while the higher time frame conditions are supportive.

The 25 percent idea is important. The signal is not designed to mean buy your full position. It is designed to support gradual position building.

[b]5. Profit taking signals[/b]

The indicator may show several types of sell or profit taking labels.

TAKE 25 percent

This appears when the market enters a higher risk area or price becomes extended.

TAKE 25 percent MORE

This appears when the market becomes more overheated.

LOSE EMA21 SELL 25 percent

This appears when short term trend support is lost after risk has increased.

EXIT REST

This is the most defensive signal. It should be treated carefully. In strong bear markets it can help protect capital. In bottoming phases it can sometimes appear too late or too aggressively. It should not be followed blindly.

The best use of sell signals is gradual profit taking, not panic selling.

[b]6. EMA structure[/b]

The indicator uses three important moving averages.

EMA 21

This is the short term trend guide. It reacts faster and is useful for spotting momentum shifts.

EMA 50

This is the medium term trend guide. Losing this level can be a warning sign.

EMA 200

This is the long term trend guide. Price above EMA 200 usually suggests a stronger market environment. Price below EMA 200 usually means risk is higher.

A strong bull structure usually has price above EMA 21, EMA 21 above EMA 50 and EMA 50 above EMA 200.

[b]7. Risk zones[/b]

The indicator plots colored zones on the chart.

Lower green or teal zones

These areas represent potential accumulation zones. They may become interesting after large declines, but they are not automatic buy areas.

Blue zones

These areas represent more neutral market areas. Price may be moving between support and resistance.

Upper red zones

These areas represent higher risk or potential overextension. They may be useful for taking partial profit or avoiding late entries.

Trend cloud

The trend cloud helps show whether price is moving with or against the current trend structure.

8. Dashboard

The dashboard gives a quick summary of the market.

It shows:

Market phase

Monthly condition

Weekly RSI

Bottom score

Top score

Cycle risk

Suggested action

The dashboard should be used as a quick overview, not as a standalone trading decision system.

How to use the indicator

[b]Step 1[/b]

Start with the monthly filter.

If monthly is GREEN, long setups are more valid.

If monthly is EARLY, only use smaller position sizes.

If monthly is RED, be careful with long entries.

[b]Step 2[/b]

Check the weekly condition.

If weekly RSI is recovering from a low area, the market may be forming a larger bottoming structure.

If weekly RSI is already very high, risk may be increasing.

[b]Step 3[/b]

Use the daily chart for entries.

Look for BOTTOM BUY, CONFIRM BUY, DIP BUY or BREAKOUT BUY signals.

Do not buy full size on one signal. Use partial entries.

Example:

First signal, buy 25 percent.

Second confirmation, buy another 25 percent.

Healthy pullback in uptrend, buy another 25 percent.

Breakout confirmation, buy the last 25 percent.

[b]Step 4[/b]

Use profit taking signals gradually.

If the market becomes extended and TAKE 25 percent appears, consider taking partial profit.

If TAKE 25 percent MORE appears, risk is higher and more profit can be secured.

If trend breaks after a major move, reduce exposure.

[b]Step 5[/b]

Always check support and resistance manually.

The indicator does not know your personal portfolio, entry price, risk tolerance or time horizon.

Important warnings

This indicator can be wrong.

It can give false buy signals during bear markets.

It can give exit signals before a recovery bounce.

It can give profit taking signals too early in strong bull markets.

It can miss exact bottoms.

It can miss exact tops.

It should not be used as a standalone trading system.

No indicator can predict the future. This tool is designed to support decision making, not replace it.

Suggested time frames

Weekly chart for cycle confirmation.

Daily chart for entries and profit taking.

4 hour chart only for fine tuning entries. The 4 hour chart should not control the whole investment decision.

Risk management

Never risk too much on one signal.

Use position sizing.

Use partial entries.

Use partial exits.

Keep cash available for deeper corrections.

Do not use leverage without advanced risk management.

Crypto is volatile and can move violently in both directions.

How I personally read the signals

A buy signal in a monthly green environment is much stronger than a buy signal in a monthly red environment.

A bottom signal after a large drawdown is interesting, but not guaranteed.

A take profit signal after a strong rally is often more useful than a sell signal during a deep correction.

The best signals happen when the monthly, weekly and daily conditions align.

Final note

It is not financial advice.

It should be combined with other tools such as support and resistance, trendlines, volume, market structure, Bitcoin dominance, macro conditions and your own risk management rules.

Use it as a navigator, not as an autopilot.

## Source Code

````pine
//@version=5
indicator("Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3", overlay=true, max_labels_count=500, max_lines_count=500)

monthlyTf = input.timeframe("M", "Monthly filter timeframe")
weeklyTf = input.timeframe("W", "Weekly cycle timeframe")

cycleLen = input.int(730, "Cycle lookback", minval=100)
zoneLen = input.int(365, "Zone lookback", minval=100)
atrLen = input.int(14, "ATR length", minval=1)
trendLen = input.int(55, "Trend length", minval=10)
sens = input.float(2.0, "Sensitivity", step=0.1)
cooldown = input.int(10, "Signal cooldown", minval=1)

showZones = input.bool(true, "Show zones")
showEma = input.bool(true, "Show EMA")
showCandles = input.bool(true, "Color candles")
showPanel = input.bool(true, "Show dashboard")
showRiskMarks = input.bool(true, "Show risk marks")

ema21 = ta.ema(close, 21)
ema50 = ta.ema(close, 50)
ema200 = ta.ema(close, 200)
rsi = ta.rsi(close, 14)
atr = ta.atr(atrLen)
volMa = ta.sma(volume, 20)

mClose = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, monthlyTf, close, barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
mEma21 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, monthlyTf, ta.ema(close, 21), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
mEma50 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, monthlyTf, ta.ema(close, 50), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
mEma200 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, monthlyTf, ta.ema(close, 200), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
mRsi = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, monthlyTf, ta.rsi(close, 14), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)

wClose = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, weeklyTf, close, barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
wEma21 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, weeklyTf, ta.ema(close, 21), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
wEma50 = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, weeklyTf, ta.ema(close, 50), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)
wRsi = request.security(syminfo.tickerid, weeklyTf, ta.rsi(close, 14), barmerge.gaps_off, barmerge.lookahead_off)

monthlyGreen = mClose > mEma21 and mEma21 > mEma50
monthlyEarlyGreen = mClose > mEma21 and mRsi > 48
monthlyRed = mClose < mEma21 and mEma21 < mEma50
weeklyGreen = wClose > wEma21 and wRsi > 50
weeklyRecovery = wRsi > 40 and wRsi > wRsi[1]
weeklyDeep = wRsi < 40

cycleHigh = ta.highest(high, cycleLen)
cycleLow = ta.lowest(low, cycleLen)
cycleRange = math.max(cycleHigh - cycleLow, syminfo.mintick)
cyclePos = ((close - cycleLow) / cycleRange) * 100

rangeHigh = ta.highest(high, zoneLen)
rangeLow = ta.lowest(low, zoneLen)
rangeSize = math.max(rangeHigh - rangeLow, syminfo.mintick)

lowerOuter = rangeLow
lowerInner = rangeLow + rangeSize * 0.25
valueTop = rangeLow + rangeSize * 0.45
midLow = rangeLow + rangeSize * 0.45
midHigh = rangeLow + rangeSize * 0.70
upperInner = rangeLow + rangeSize * 0.78
upperOuter = rangeHigh

basis = ta.ema(close, trendLen)
trendTop = basis + atr * sens
trendBot = basis - atr * sens

var int trend = 1
trend := close > trendTop[1] ? 1 : close < trendBot[1] ? -1 : nz(trend[1], 1)

bullStructure = close > ema21 and ema21 > ema50 and ema50 > ema200
earlyBull = close > ema21 and ema21 > ema50 and close > ema200
bearStructure = close < ema21 and ema21 < ema50 and close < ema200

bottomScore = 0.0
bottomScore += cyclePos < 35 ? 25 : 0
bottomScore += cyclePos < 25 ? 20 : 0
bottomScore += weeklyDeep ? 20 : 0
bottomScore += weeklyRecovery ? 15 : 0
bottomScore += rsi < 40 ? 10 : 0
bottomScore += close < lowerInner ? 10 : 0
bottomScore := math.min(bottomScore, 100)

topScore = 0.0
topScore += cyclePos > 70 ? 20 : 0
topScore += cyclePos > 82 ? 25 : 0
topScore += rsi > 68 ? 15 : 0
topScore += rsi > 76 ? 20 : 0
topScore += close > upperInner ? 20 : 0
topScore := math.min(topScore, 100)

allowBuy = monthlyGreen or monthlyEarlyGreen
riskOff = monthlyRed and not weeklyRecovery

bottomBuyRaw = allowBuy and bottomScore >= 55 and ta.crossover(rsi, 40)
confirmBuyRaw = allowBuy and weeklyRecovery and ta.crossover(close, ema21) and rsi > 45
dipBuyRaw = monthlyGreen and weeklyGreen and low <= ema21 and close > ema21 and rsi < 62 and close > open
breakoutBuyRaw = monthlyGreen and weeklyGreen and close > ta.highest(high, 55)[1] and rsi < 72

takeProfitRaw = topScore >= 55 and high > upperInner
takeMoreRaw = topScore >= 75 and rsi > 72
trendSellRaw = ta.crossunder(close, ema21) and topScore >= 35
exitRaw = riskOff or ta.crossunder(close, ema50) and trend == -1

buyRaw = bottomBuyRaw or confirmBuyRaw or dipBuyRaw or breakoutBuyRaw
sellRaw = takeProfitRaw or takeMoreRaw or trendSellRaw or exitRaw

var int lastBuyBar = na
var int lastSellBar = na

buyOk = buyRaw and (na(lastBuyBar) or bar_index - lastBuyBar > cooldown)
sellOk = sellRaw and (na(lastSellBar) or bar_index - lastSellBar > cooldown)

if buyOk
    lastBuyBar := bar_index

if sellOk
    lastSellBar := bar_index

pLowerOuter = plot(showZones ? lowerOuter : na, "Bottom zone outer", color=color.new(color.teal, 100))
pLowerInner = plot(showZones ? lowerInner : na, "Bottom zone inner", color=color.new(color.teal, 100))
fill(pLowerOuter, pLowerInner, color=showZones ? color.new(color.teal, 82) : na)

pValueTop = plot(showZones ? valueTop : na, "Value zone top", color=color.new(color.green, 100))
fill(pLowerInner, pValueTop, color=showZones ? color.new(color.green, 86) : na)

pMidLow = plot(showZones ? midLow : na, "Middle zone low", color=color.new(color.blue, 100))
pMidHigh = plot(showZones ? midHigh : na, "Middle zone high", color=color.new(color.blue, 100))
fill(pMidLow, pMidHigh, color=showZones ? color.new(color.blue, 90) : na)

pUpperInner = plot(showZones ? upperInner : na, "Top zone inner", color=color.new(color.red, 100))
pUpperOuter = plot(showZones ? upperOuter : na, "Top zone outer", color=color.new(color.red, 100))
fill(pUpperInner, pUpperOuter, color=showZones ? color.new(color.red, 84) : na)

pTrendTop = plot(showZones ? trendTop : na, "Trend cloud top", color=color.new(color.blue, 100))
pTrendBot = plot(showZones ? trendBot : na, "Trend cloud bottom", color=color.new(color.blue, 100))
fill(pTrendTop, pTrendBot, color=showZones ? trend == 1 ? color.new(color.blue, 84) : color.new(color.red, 88) : na)

plot(showEma ? ema21 : na, "EMA 21 gul", color=color.yellow, linewidth=2)
plot(showEma ? ema50 : na, "EMA 50 orange", color=color.orange, linewidth=2)
plot(showEma ? ema200 : na, "EMA 200 rød", color=color.red, linewidth=2)

candleColor = bullStructure ? color.lime : earlyBull ? color.green : bearStructure ? color.red : color.purple
barcolor(showCandles ? candleColor : na)

plotshape(bottomBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Bottom buy 25 procent", style=shape.labelup, text="BOTTOM\nBUY 25%", location=location.belowbar, color=color.teal, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(confirmBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Confirm buy 25 procent", style=shape.labelup, text="CONFIRM\nBUY 25%", location=location.belowbar, color=color.green, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(dipBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Dip buy 25 procent", style=shape.labelup, text="DIP\nBUY 25%", location=location.belowbar, color=color.blue, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(breakoutBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Breakout buy 25 procent", style=shape.labelup, text="BREAKOUT\nBUY 25%", location=location.belowbar, color=color.purple, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)

plotshape(takeProfitRaw and sellOk, title="Take profit 25 procent", style=shape.labeldown, text="TAKE\n25%", location=location.abovebar, color=color.orange, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(takeMoreRaw and sellOk, title="Take more 25 procent", style=shape.labeldown, text="TAKE\n25% MORE", location=location.abovebar, color=color.red, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(trendSellRaw and sellOk, title="Trend sell 25 procent", style=shape.labeldown, text="LOSE EMA21\nSELL 25%", location=location.abovebar, color=color.orange, textcolor=color.white, size=size.small)
plotshape(exitRaw and sellOk, title="Exit rest", style=shape.labeldown, text="EXIT\nREST", location=location.abovebar, color=color.black, textcolor=color.white, size=size.normal)

topRiskMark = showRiskMarks and topScore >= 55
bottomRiskMark = showRiskMarks and bottomScore >= 55

plotshape(topRiskMark, title="Top risk mark", style=shape.xcross, location=location.abovebar, color=color.blue, size=size.tiny)
plotshape(bottomRiskMark, title="Bottom risk mark", style=shape.xcross, location=location.belowbar, color=color.orange, size=size.tiny)

phase = monthlyGreen and weeklyGreen ? "BULL" : monthlyEarlyGreen and weeklyRecovery ? "EARLY BULL" : monthlyRed ? "BEAR" : bottomScore >= 55 ? "ACCUMULATION" : "NEUTRAL"
phaseColor = phase == "BULL" ? color.green : phase == "EARLY BULL" ? color.lime : phase == "ACCUMULATION" ? color.teal : phase == "BEAR" ? color.red : color.gray

actionText = bottomBuyRaw and buyOk ? "BUY 25%" : confirmBuyRaw and buyOk ? "BUY 25%" : dipBuyRaw and buyOk ? "BUY DIP 25%" : breakoutBuyRaw and buyOk ? "BUY BREAKOUT 25%" : takeMoreRaw and sellOk ? "TAKE 25%" : takeProfitRaw and sellOk ? "TAKE 25%" : trendSellRaw and sellOk ? "SELL 25%" : exitRaw and sellOk ? "EXIT REST" : "WAIT"
actionColor = str.contains(actionText, "BUY") ? color.green : str.contains(actionText, "TAKE") ? color.orange : str.contains(actionText, "SELL") ? color.red : str.contains(actionText, "EXIT") ? color.black : color.gray

var table dash = table.new(position.top_right, 2, 7)

if barstate.islast and showPanel
    table.cell(dash, 0, 0, "Phase", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 0, phase, text_color=color.white, bgcolor=phaseColor)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 1, "Monthly", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 1, monthlyGreen ? "GREEN" : monthlyEarlyGreen ? "EARLY" : monthlyRed ? "RED" : "NEUTRAL", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=monthlyGreen ? color.green : monthlyEarlyGreen ? color.lime : monthlyRed ? color.red : color.gray)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 2, "Weekly RSI", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 2, str.tostring(math.round(wRsi)), text_color=color.white, bgcolor=wRsi > 55 ? color.green : wRsi < 40 ? color.teal : color.gray)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 3, "Bottom score", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 3, str.tostring(math.round(bottomScore)), text_color=color.white, bgcolor=bottomScore >= 55 ? color.teal : bottomScore >= 35 ? color.orange : color.gray)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 4, "Top score", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 4, str.tostring(math.round(topScore)), text_color=color.white, bgcolor=topScore >= 75 ? color.red : topScore >= 55 ? color.orange : color.gray)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 5, "Cycle risk", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 5, str.tostring(math.round(cyclePos)) + "%", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=cyclePos > 75 ? color.red : cyclePos < 35 ? color.teal : color.gray)

    table.cell(dash, 0, 6, "Action", text_color=color.white, bgcolor=color.black)
    table.cell(dash, 1, 6, actionText, text_color=color.white, bgcolor=actionColor)

alertcondition(bottomBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Bottom buy 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 bottom buy 25 procent")
alertcondition(confirmBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Confirm buy 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 confirmed buy 25 procent")
alertcondition(dipBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Dip buy 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 dip buy 25 procent")
alertcondition(breakoutBuyRaw and buyOk, title="Breakout buy 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 breakout buy 25 procent")

alertcondition(takeProfitRaw and sellOk, title="Take profit 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 take profit 25 procent")
alertcondition(takeMoreRaw and sellOk, title="Take profit more 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 take profit 25 procent more")
alertcondition(trendSellRaw and sellOk, title="Trend sell 25 procent", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 trend sell 25 procent")
alertcondition(exitRaw and sellOk, title="Exit rest", message="Crypto Cycle by Haramii V3 exit rest")
````
