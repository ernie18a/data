During each enabled session, build the traded instrument’s high and low from that session’s range, then evaluate signals only in its execution window. If one ATM boundary is swept (both swept on the same bar ends the session), seek a reversal: after a low sweep, go long when a recent, sufficiently large bearish fair value gap formed during the session is inverted by a confirmed close above its upper edge, crossing from at or below that edge; after a high sweep, go short on the mirrored inversion of a bullish gap. Enter at that close if the stop and target are on the valid sides of entry. Use the sweep extreme or gap boundary for the stop, and the opposing ATM boundary or a configured risk multiple for the target. A confirmed close reaching the opposing ATM boundary before entry ends the setup. If price reaches the configured extension threshold beyond the swept boundary, permanently disable that reversal direction for the session and switch to continuation in the expansion direction, even if a reversal trade is active. After the switch, wait for price to tap a valid, uninvalidated, recent fair value gap in that direction on either supported timeframe; then track the pullback extreme and enter in the expansion direction at a confirmed close beyond the pre-pullback impulse extreme. Place the stop beyond the pullback extreme and target equal to the entry-to-stop risk. End each setup or trade at its stop, target, or execution-window close; do not open another trade in that session.
