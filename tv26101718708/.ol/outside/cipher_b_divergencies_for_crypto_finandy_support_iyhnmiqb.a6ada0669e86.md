On each confirmed bar, enter only when flat. Define the local trend by comparing the instrument’s close with its moving average on a higher timeframe; define the global trend from Bitcoin’s price versus its moving average on a selected timeframe, treating either the current or previous bar above the average as bullish and either below it as bearish. For a long, require both trends bullish and either a WaveTrend bullish cross while oversold, a qualifying regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI, or a strong bullish recovery following a deeply oversold WaveTrend low with bullish divergence and low RSI at that pivot. For a short, require both trends bearish and either a WaveTrend bearish cross while overbought or a qualifying regular bearish divergence in those oscillators. When divergence-only mode is enabled, suppress the WaveTrend cross entries but retain divergence and special bullish recovery entries. Set a fixed percentage stop loss and take profit from the entry price. If optional local-trend protection is enabled, close a long when price falls below the local average and close a short when price rises above it.
