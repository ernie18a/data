Enter long when price sweeps below the prior swing low and closes back above it, with a volume spike or a small candle body relative to its range, falling stablecoin dominance, and aggregated open interest across several exchanges at or below its moving average. Enter short on the inverse setup: sweep above the prior swing high and close back below it, the same volume or candle confirmation, rising stablecoin dominance, and aggregated open interest above its moving average. Keep a sweep valid for a short confirmation window and process it only once per window. Add to a long only when a new long signal occurs below the position’s average entry; add to a short only when a new short signal occurs above its average entry. Reduce the next allocation after a loss using a capped martingale progression, reset it after a non-losing trade, and reduce it when the enabled market regime opposes the trade. For open positions, exit on a favorable unrealized gain when price crosses the short-term EMA against the position; on a close-based adverse loss threshold; or via a wick-based catastrophe stop, a profit-protecting stop that ratchets upward or downward after favorable price milestones (including a recovery-price level for accumulated losses), or a maximum-profit limit. Apply the stop and limit rules symmetrically to long and short positions.
