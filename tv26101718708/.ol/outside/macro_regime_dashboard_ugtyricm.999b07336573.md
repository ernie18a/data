Go long the broad US equity market when all of these hold: volatility is elevated; the policy rate is no higher than its value over a prior lookback period (unless overridden); margin-account debt is falling quarter over quarter; at least one leading sector outperforms the broad market by a minimum margin over a lookback period; and a majority of available bellwether earnings reports meet or exceed estimates (missing earnings data passes neutrally, and this condition can be overridden). Also require the high-yield credit spread to be below its ceiling when that safeguard is enabled, and inflation to be below its ceiling when its safeguard is enabled; missing safeguard data passes. No short or exit rule is specified. For stock selection when the market signal is active, use the leading sector and liquidity regime: classify liquidity using policy-rate direction and central-bank balance-sheet direction, then favor high-growth companies in maximum liquidity, moderate growth and leverage in mixed regimes, and profitable, low-valuation, low-debt companies in minimum liquidity.
