Enter long when the short-, medium-, and long-term price EMAs are ordered upward, cumulative volume delta is above its signal, and the higher-timeframe momentum wave is above its signal; enter short when all three conditions are reversed. While long, exit if the upward EMA ordering or wave-above-signal condition is lost; while short, exit if the downward EMA ordering or wave-below-signal condition is lost. Also signal a long reentry when trend strength is elevated and the wave crosses above zero or turns upward while positive; signal a short reentry on the inverse conditions. At confirmed wave peaks or troughs, advise exiting aligned long or short positions, respectively.
