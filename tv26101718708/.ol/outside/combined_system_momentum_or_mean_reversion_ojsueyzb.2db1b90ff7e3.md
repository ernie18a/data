On a one-minute NQ chart, generate signals only during the New York regular session from 9:45 a.m. through noon, using confirmed bars. Define bullish or bearish trend states by whether price is above or below a twin-range filter; use the prior completed bar for NQ higher-timeframe states and the current state for VIX. Define upside or downside overextension as price beyond the corresponding two-standard-deviation session VWAP band or having consumed the configured fraction of the expected daily range from the overnight open.

Momentum long: the one-minute price crosses above its range filter, the 15-minute NQ state is bullish, at least one of the hourly or four-hour NQ states is bullish, the 15-minute VIX state is bearish, and price is not upside-overextended. Momentum short: the one-minute price crosses below its range filter, the 15-minute NQ state is bearish, and the 15-minute VIX state is bullish.

Mean-reversion long: a bullish reversal pattern occurs at a downside VWAP stretch and the 15-minute VIX state is bearish. Qualifying patterns are outside–down-break–up-break, outside–inside–up-break, or down-break–up-break. For either three-bar pattern, at least one open or close across the pattern must reach or pass the one-standard-deviation lower band. For the two-bar pattern, at least one open or close must be beyond the two-standard-deviation lower band, and price must also be downside-overextended.

Mean-reversion short: a bearish reversal pattern occurs at an upside VWAP stretch, and either the 15-minute VIX state is bullish or price is upside-overextended. Qualifying patterns are outside–up-break–down-break, outside–inside–down-break, or up-break–down-break. For either three-bar pattern, at least one open or close across the pattern must reach or pass the one-standard-deviation upper band. For the two-bar pattern, at least one open or close must be beyond the two-standard-deviation upper band, and price must also be upside-overextended.

Attach an ATR-scaled protective stop below the signal bar’s low for longs or above its high for shorts. The indicator specifies no separate exit signal.
