Build a USD global money-supply series by converting the major economies’ M2 data with their exchange rates and summing it. Smooth the series, calculate its rate of change, and standardize that rate against its trailing history. Set a sticky liquidity regime bullish when the score crosses above an upper threshold and bearish when it crosses below a lower threshold; retain the prior regime between thresholds. Change the confirmed regime only after the new regime persists for the required confirmation period. Separately, mark the price trend bullish when a faster Wilder-smoothed price average is above a slower one; use either the charted asset or a selected market-leader price series. Select the holding rule: liquidity mode holds only in a bullish liquidity regime; trend mode holds only in a bullish price trend; combined mode holds when either is bullish and exits to cash only when both are bearish. Enforce a minimum holding period before allowing an opposite state change. Emit BUY when the resulting state changes from cash to holding, and SELL when it changes from holding to cash. Health metrics, early impulse direction, projection, and backtest calculations do not alter these decisions.
