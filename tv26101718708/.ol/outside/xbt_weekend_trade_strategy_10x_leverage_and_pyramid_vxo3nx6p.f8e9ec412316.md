During the backtest window, use the traded instrument’s Friday daily close as the reference. On Saturday and Sunday, if flat, go short when the close rises above the reference by the configured entry threshold, or go long when it falls below the reference by that threshold. While holding a short, add short when the close rises by that threshold above the position’s average entry price; while holding a long, add long when it falls by that threshold below the average entry price. Close all positions when open profit exceeds the configured profit target relative to initial capital, and close any remaining positions on Monday.
