Estimate realized volatility and classify it as expanding, contracting, or stable by comparing it with its recent average. Project symmetric log-normal volatility bands around the current close. In a stable regime, if price reaches an inner band and a rejection candle confirms, enter against the move toward the anchor; place the stop beyond the corresponding outer band and exit at the anchor. At an outer-band touch, fade toward the anchor in stable or contracting regimes; in an expanding regime, wait for candle confirmation and favor continuation beyond the band, or remain flat. In a contracting regime, watch for the first volatility-expansion candle and trade in the direction of its break. In an expanding regime, avoid countertrend trades and take only confirmed momentum trades in the move's direction, otherwise stay flat.
