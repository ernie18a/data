At each new quarter in the New York–anchored daily cycle, begin tracking each of three instruments’ highs and lows and retain the completed prior quarter’s extremes. On confirmed bars, once prior-quarter data exists, emit a bearish signal once per quarter if at least one but not all three instruments have exceeded their own prior-quarter high. Emit a bullish signal once per quarter if at least one but not all three have fallen below their own prior-quarter low. The signals mark divergence; no entry, exit, or trade execution rule is specified.
