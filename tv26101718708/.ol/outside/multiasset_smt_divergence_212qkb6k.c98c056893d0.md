At each confirmed swing high or low on the traded instrument, compare its structure with each configured correlated asset. In sweep mode, signal bearish when the traded instrument breaks a prior swing high but a comparison asset fails to exceed its corresponding prior high; signal bullish when it breaks a prior swing low but the comparison asset fails to make a corresponding lower low. For inverse-correlated comparisons, reverse the comparison asset’s expected direction. In legacy mode, signal bearish when the traded instrument and a comparison asset disagree on whether consecutive swing highs made a higher high, and bullish when they disagree on whether consecutive swing lows made a lower low; for inverse-correlated assets, signal when those directional changes agree instead. When candle validation is enabled, require a bearish candle at a bearish signal swing and a bullish candle at a bullish signal swing. Signals may be generated at the main or optional shorter swing scale. No exit rule is defined.
