Build a composite risk score from Bitcoin price behavior and external on-chain activity, stablecoin liquidity, and spot ETF volume. Normalize each input against historical and rolling statistical ranges, then combine them. Signal an exit when the score crosses below its smoothed signal while elevated, or when the smoothed signal turns down while the score is extremely high. Keep the exit state active until the score falls below a lower cooling threshold. Mark prolonged time in the high-risk zone as saturation. The indicator defines no long or short entry rules.
