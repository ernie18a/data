During the configured trading session, sell a put when price crosses above an ATR-based trailing stop, RSI is within the configured put-selling range, ADX exceeds its configured minimum, and the current candle range is no greater than a configured multiple of average true range. Sell a call when price crosses below the stop and RSI is within the configured call-selling range, with the same ADX and candle-spike filters. Close all positions outside the trading session.
