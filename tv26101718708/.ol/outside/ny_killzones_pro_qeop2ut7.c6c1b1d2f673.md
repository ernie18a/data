Generate bearish SMT signals when the traded instrument or a comparison market takes a prior range high by a minimum tick threshold and the other does not; generate bullish SMT signals analogously for prior range lows. Compare London with the preceding Asian session and New York with the preceding London session. Also compare each intraday cycle range with the nearest same-day completed range whose corresponding high or low remains unswept. Use the traded instrument, a primary comparison market, and an optional second comparison market. Signals may update while a range forms and are confirmed when it ends; suppress both directions if high-side and low-side SMT occur together. These are directional alerts, not entry or exit rules.
