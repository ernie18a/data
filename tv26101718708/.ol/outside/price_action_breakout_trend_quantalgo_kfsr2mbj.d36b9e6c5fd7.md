Establish a range from the recent prior bars, excluding the current bar: its highest high and lowest low. Initialize long with the stop at the range low. While long, raise the stop to the higher of its current level and the range low; if the chosen confirmation price (close or bar low) falls below the stop, reverse to short and reset the stop to the range high. While short, lower the stop to the lower of its current level and the range high; if the chosen confirmation price (close or bar high) rises above the stop, reverse to long and reset the stop to the range low. The directional flips signal long or short entries; no separate exit signal is defined.
