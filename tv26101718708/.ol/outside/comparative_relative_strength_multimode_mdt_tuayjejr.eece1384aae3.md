Compare the traded instrument’s selected price series with a chosen external benchmark on the same timeframe. Derive relative strength as either their price ratio, the difference between their returns over a lookback, or the ratio’s percentage deviation from its own moving average. The indicator describes relative performance and does not itself issue long, short, or exit decisions. Its usage guidance treats new relative-strength highs as long-side candidates and new lows as short-side candidates; in the normalized regime filter, consider long setups only while relative strength is above zero.
