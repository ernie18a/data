Calculate two configurable moving averages from the traded instrument’s selected timeframes and price sources. When a confirmed bar closes with no open position, go long if the faster average crosses above the slower average, or short if it crosses below. For a long position, set the stop at the low of the bar before entry; for a short, set it at that bar’s high. Set the profit target in the trade’s favor at a configurable risk-to-reward multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. Exit when either the stop or target is reached.
