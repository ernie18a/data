Using external Bitcoin miner revenue, hashrate, and network difficulty, form a network-value proxy from the square root of miner revenue times hashrate divided by difficulty. Compare Bitcoin’s market price with that proxy on a logarithmic scale, smooth it, then calculate its rolling z-score. At or below −2 standard deviations, signal strong accumulation; between −2 and −1, signal favorable accumulation; from −1 to +1, maintain ordinary scheduled accumulation; from +1 to +2, signal caution; at or above +2, pause accumulation or take profit. Emit entry alerts when the score first enters the strong-accumulation or pause/take-profit zone.
