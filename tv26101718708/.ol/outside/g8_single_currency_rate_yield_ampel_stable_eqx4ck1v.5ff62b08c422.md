For a selected currency, compare each enabled external series’ current value with its value at an earlier momentum lookback. Add a bullish point for each rising 2-year yield and 10-year yield, and a bearish point for each falling yield; add a bullish point when the rate future falls and a bearish point when it rises. For the currency proxy, rising prices are bullish and falling prices bearish, reversing this mapping for inverse-quoted proxies. Ignore unavailable or disabled components and sum the contributions. A total at or above the bullish threshold indicates currency strength; a total at or below the bearish threshold indicates weakness; otherwise the bias is neutral. For an FX pair, favor a long when the base currency is bullish and the quote currency bearish, and a short when the base is bearish and the quote bullish; otherwise there is no directional bias.
