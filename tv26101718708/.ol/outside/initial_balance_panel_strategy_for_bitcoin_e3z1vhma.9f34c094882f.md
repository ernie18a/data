For each monitored crypto instrument, track the high and low of the first hour of each of the Asia, London, and New York sessions. Classify it as bullish when its close is above all three session highs, bearish when below all three session lows, and neutral otherwise. Enter long when the bullish count meets the configured minimum and the volatility-stop trend is bullish; enter short when the bearish count meets its configured minimum and the volatility-stop trend is bearish. Optional Bitcoin filters can require Bitcoin to be bullish or bearish, respectively, or neutral. Exit a long or short when the volatility-stop trend flips against the position.
