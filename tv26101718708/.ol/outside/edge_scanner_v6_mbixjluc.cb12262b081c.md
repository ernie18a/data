For each scanned instrument, calculate the rolling range high and low, the close’s position as a percentage of that range, and whether it is in the configured upper or lower edge zone. Classify the close as bullish above the range midpoint, bearish below it, or neutral at the midpoint. Recommend a strong long when it is in the lower edge zone, bullish, and in the bottom 10% of the range; recommend a strong short when it is in the upper edge zone, bearish, and in the top 10%. Otherwise recommend looking long in the lower zone with bullish bias, looking short in the upper zone with bearish bias, caution in the upper zone, watching for a bounce in the lower zone, or waiting in all other cases.
