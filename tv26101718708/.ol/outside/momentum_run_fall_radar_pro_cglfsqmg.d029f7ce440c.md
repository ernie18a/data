Build separate bullish and bearish composite scores from trend structure and slope, recent fast/slow average crossovers, momentum level and direction, MACD structure, directional trend strength, directionally aligned relative volume, proximity to or breach of recent support/resistance, volatility compression or expansion, higher-timeframe trend agreement, and relative performance versus a benchmark. Raise bullish or bearish watch, early, and setup states as the corresponding score crosses progressively stronger thresholds and exceeds the opposing score. Treat a close beyond the recent range, with a volatility buffer, as a breakout or breakdown; promote to a confirmed run or fall only when the matching score is strong and volume is elevated in the same direction. Strongest states additionally require very strong score, volume, and directional trend strength. A held retest of the broken level and matching momentum divergence can provide separate directional signals. Confirm signals at bar close by default. No explicit exit rule is defined.
