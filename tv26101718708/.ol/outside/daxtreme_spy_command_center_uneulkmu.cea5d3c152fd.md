Build each session’s opening range from its initial trading window. After it is established, arm a bullish setup when price crosses above the range high while above VWAP and the fast moving average, with the fast average rising and above the slower average. Arm a bearish setup on the inverse cross below the range low, below VWAP and the fast average, with the fast average falling and below the slower average. When enabled, require price to retest the broken range boundary and close back beyond it; also require a pullback to the fast average followed by a close back in the trade direction. Block entries that exceed the contract-maturity-specific distance limit from the fast average or are too close to VWAP when those filters are enabled. Score each direction by counting these confirmations: VWAP position, price position versus the fast average, fast-average slope, moving-average alignment, range retest, pullback rejection, higher-timeframe RSI strength, entry-timeframe RSI strength, volume-delta direction, and acceptable extension. Buy calls for a bullish setup or puts for a bearish setup only when its score meets the configured minimum; optionally limit each direction to one entry per session. For an open call, issue an exit warning and clear the position state when price closes below the fast average or VWAP, subject to enabled exit checks. For an open put, do so when price closes above either level, subject to enabled exit checks.
