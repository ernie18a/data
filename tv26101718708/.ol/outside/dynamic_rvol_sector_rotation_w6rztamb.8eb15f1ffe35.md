For each major sector ETF, rank sectors by relative volume. Consider buying calls when relative volume is at least 1.5 times its average, price change is positive, price is above its macro-timeframe 21-period EMA, and the absolute macro-candle move is below 80% of its 14-period ATR. If volume is elevated and price is rising but price remains below the macro EMA, treat it as a countertrend bounce and consider shorting the intraday pop at resistance. When the macro move has reached at least 80% of ATR, avoid chasing long calls; wait for a pullback or consider a structured spread. A top ranking alone is not a trade signal.
