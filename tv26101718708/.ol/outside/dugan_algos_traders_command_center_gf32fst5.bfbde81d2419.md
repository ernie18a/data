Count six directional checks: price above session VWAP, fast EMA above slow EMA, price above each of two longer-term moving averages, price above the prior day’s close, and market breadth above zero. If at least five are bullish, signal long; if at least five are bearish, signal short; otherwise, no directional signal. Qualify a directional signal using cumulative session volume relative to its historical daily average and MACD-histogram slope normalized by ATR, with momentum oriented to the selected direction. Insufficient relative volume or momentum that fails the configured momentum rule downgrades the verdict; passing both yields the highest-quality long or short setup. Also flag upward or downward crossings of prior-day, premarket, or completed opening-range extremes, and crossings of session VWAP. No exit rule is defined.
