During each regular session, record the session open and wait until the first configured observation window has elapsed. Compare that window’s closing price with the session open: go long if it is higher, short if it is lower, and take no trade if equal. Enter no earlier than the bar after the window closes, and allow at most one entry per session. Derive a daily implied-move fraction from a daily volatility index reading, expressed as a percentage and divided by the square root of the annual number of trading days; use the selected short-term volatility index when available, otherwise the standard volatility index. For an open long, exit if the price closes at or above the session open plus one implied move, reaches a take-profit or stop level respectively above or below entry by the implied move scaled by its configured factor, or the session reaches its configured pre-close exit time. For an open short, exit if the price closes at or below the session open minus one implied move, reaches a take-profit or stop level respectively below or above entry by the implied move scaled by its configured factor, or the pre-close exit time. If stop and target are both reached within one bar, treat the stop as reached first.
