Go long on a bullish fast/medium moving-average crossover when the trend is not bearish, momentum is supportive, price is above a volatility trail, confidence is high, and at least three of four timeframe trends agree; go short on the mirrored bearish crossover. If the optional strict filter is enabled, also require the configured confidence, directional timeframe agreement, trend strength, trail alignment, and London or overlap session. Standard crossover signals require the minimum confidence and are used when the premium condition is absent. Reversal signals go long when price crosses back above the lower volatility band after an oversold reading, or short when it crosses back below the upper band after an overbought reading; both require minimum confidence and the optional strict filter. A qualifying signal sets the position and its volatility-based stop and profit targets. Exit/reset on the stop being hit when automatic stop cleanup is enabled; profit targets only mark progress. An opposite signal replaces the current position.
