On the traded instrument, when RSI is below an oversold threshold, place a long limit order below the current close, with the offset set as either a percentage of price or a percentage of ATR. Cancel pending orders when the open is not sufficiently below the previous close. If enabled, block new entries while the squared deviation of hourly VIX from its long-term average has recently exceeded a volatility threshold. Exit the long when RSI rises above an exit threshold or price reaches a fixed percentage stop below the average entry price. Close any open position outside the configured trading date range.
