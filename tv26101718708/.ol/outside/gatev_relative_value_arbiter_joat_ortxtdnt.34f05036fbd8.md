Estimate a rolling beta-adjusted log-price spread between the traded instrument and a selected peer, then standardize it as a z-score. Only act when return correlation with the peer meets a minimum quality gate and the spread has nonzero variability. On a confirmed bar, go long the traded instrument when the spread is below a negative entry threshold and starts rising; go short when it is above a positive entry threshold and starts falling. Enforce a cooldown between entry signals. Exit or reset when the spread returns inside a narrow band around its mean, detected on the first bar it enters that band.
