On confirmed bars, calculate a weighted directional score from RSI, MACD impulse, adaptive-average trend and price position, volume and OBV, candle-range and wick behavior, and sector ETF performance relative to a broad-market ETF. Give each component bullish, bearish, partial, or neutral credit; scale indicator lengths and component weights by chart timeframe. Normalize the total score. Suppress signals during abnormally low volume. Enter long when the score reaches the configured bullish threshold, volume confirmation passes, and RSI, MACD, and adaptive-average signals are all bullish. Enter short under the symmetric bearish conditions. Close a long on a strong short signal or when the score falls below a moderate bearish exit boundary; close a short on a strong long signal or when the score rises above a moderate bullish exit boundary. A strong opposite signal reverses the position.
