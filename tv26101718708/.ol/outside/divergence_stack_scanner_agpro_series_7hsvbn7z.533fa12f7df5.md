At confirmed chart swing highs, compare whether the chart instrument and a selected correlated instrument each made a higher high than their prior swing high. At confirmed swing lows, compare whether each made a lower low than its prior swing low. For normally positively correlated instruments, treat a mismatch in whether they made the new extreme as a directional divergence: bearish at highs, bullish at lows. For normally inversely correlated instruments, treat matching outcomes as the divergence: bearish at highs, bullish at lows. Emit the corresponding bearish or bullish cue for the chart instrument when detected.
