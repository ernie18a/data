At a key support or resistance level, go long on an upward break or bullish rejection when multi-timeframe pressure and the trend regime are bullish; go short on a downward break or bearish rejection when both are bearish. Favor a session high/low breakout while the session is active and average daily range remains available; target the next level. When the day's range is spent late in the session, consider fading toward the open only if the macro backdrop is neutral. Avoid trades when pressure and price conflict, timeframes are split, the regime is range-bound or squeezed with little directional pressure, there is no nearby level, or macro conditions oppose the trade. The indicator defines no exit rule.
