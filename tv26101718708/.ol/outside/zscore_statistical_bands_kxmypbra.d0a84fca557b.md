Form a rolling Z-score from the selected price series or, in pairs mode, from a hedge-adjusted log spread or price ratio. Estimate mean-reversion half-life from the series’ changes; allow entries only when the estimate indicates reversion and is positive and shorter than the lookback. When flat, enter long on a low-Z extreme: either as Z crosses into the entry zone (early mode), or after it has entered and then crosses back out toward the mean (confirmed mode). Optionally use the same arm-and-rebound logic at a nearer threshold. If shorts are enabled, mirror these rules at high Z. Exit a long when Z crosses back toward the mean through its normal exit threshold; exit a short on the mirrored crossing. Optional exits close a position if Z crosses back beyond its entry threshold, price closes beyond the entry candle’s adverse extreme, or Z extends beyond an emergency stop. Track one active direction at a time and reset to flat after an exit.
