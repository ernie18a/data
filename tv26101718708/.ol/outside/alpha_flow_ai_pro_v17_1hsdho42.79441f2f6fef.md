Generate a long signal on a bullish crossover of the fast and medium price averages when confidence meets its configured minimum. Classify it as premium when the trend is not bearish, RSI supports bullish momentum, price is above the trailing stop, confidence is high, and most monitored higher timeframes are bullish; otherwise classify it as standard. Generate the short signal symmetrically on a bearish crossover, with a non-bullish trend, bearish RSI, price below the trailing stop, high confidence, and most higher timeframes bearish. Also generate reversal longs when price re-enters the lower volatility band from below while RSI is oversold and confidence meets its minimum; generate reversal shorts symmetrically at the upper band with overbought RSI. Confidence combines trend alignment, trend strength, RSI, MACD, relative volume, money flow, commodity-channel position, and momentum. When the optional strict filter is enabled, require the signal-side confidence, higher-timeframe agreement, trend strength, trailing-stop direction, and permitted trading session to meet their configured criteria. On any long or short signal, set the tracked position in that direction and place an ATR-based stop and three ATR-based profit targets. Track target hits without exiting at targets; when the stop is hit, clear the tracked position if automatic stop cleanup is enabled. An opposing signal replaces the tracked direction.
