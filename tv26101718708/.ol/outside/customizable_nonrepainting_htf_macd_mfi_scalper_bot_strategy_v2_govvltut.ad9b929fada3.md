Use confirmed higher-timeframe price, volume, and indicator data. Open a long when the higher-timeframe EMA is above its SMA and either MACD crosses above its signal line, or MFI crosses above its lower threshold while that EMA/SMA trend is upward. Require any enabled filters to pass: price is at or above its selected moving average; RSI is within its configured band and rising; and ATR exceeds its minimum. Close the long if price falls below either a stop derived from the entry signal bar’s high and ATR or a fixed percentage stop from the average entry price. Once price reaches the profit target, activate the trailing profit exit and a trailing protective stop. The strategy does not open short positions.
