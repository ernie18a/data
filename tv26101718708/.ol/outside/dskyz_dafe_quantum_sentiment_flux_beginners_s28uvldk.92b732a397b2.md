Choose preset-dependent fast and slow exponential averages, ATR length, and ATR-scaled momentum threshold. Enter long when the fast average crosses above the slow average and their separation exceeds the threshold, provided the strategy is flat, the cooldown since the prior entry has elapsed, and the volatility index is below its limit. Enter short when the fast average crosses below the slow average and their separation exceeds the threshold in the bearish direction, provided the strategy is flat and the cooldown has elapsed; there is no external-data filter on shorts. While a position is open, set a stop and profit target from the current close using ATR-scaled distances, with the target twice the stop distance; update these levels as the close and ATR change. The S&P 500 trend and sector breadth are informational only and do not affect entries or exits.
