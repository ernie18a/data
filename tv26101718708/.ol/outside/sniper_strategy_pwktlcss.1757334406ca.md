On the traded instrument, use the close and open of a higher-timeframe series: go long when its close crosses above its open, and go short when its close crosses below its open. Also generate a buy signal when a triple-smoothed close exceeds a double-smoothed close, a derived price level is above the current low, and that level is accelerating upward; generate a sell signal when the triple-smoothed close is below the double-smoothed close, the derived level is below the current high, and it is accelerating downward. The strategy reverses position on opposite long/short entries; no separate exit rule is specified.
