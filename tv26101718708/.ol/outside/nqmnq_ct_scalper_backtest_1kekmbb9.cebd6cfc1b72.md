Trade counter-trend only when price is stretched away from its mean while directional movement is established: buy in a downtrend after downside exhaustion, or sell in an uptrend after upside exhaustion. Require adequate volume and volatility, sufficient trend strength, and a context-weighted overextension score built from distance to moving averages/VWAP, momentum or oscillator extremes, volatility bands, volume flow, rejection wicks, swing structure, and cross-market divergence. Reject trades when higher-timeframe direction strongly opposes the reversal, continuation-style divergence warns against fading, or the candle is indecisive. Require directional confirmation through at least one enabled setup: reversal evidence at the extreme; price/flow divergence; a false break of a recent high or low followed by a close back inside and flow confirmation; aggressive order flow against the prevailing move; or a retest and rejection of a previously established high-volume zone. Apply stricter confirmation in sessions and regimes where reversals are less reliable, especially for sells and strong trends. Enter at most once per direction per bar, prioritizing reversal, divergence, sweep, aggressive-flow, then volume-zone setups; absorption entries are disabled. Before entry, require the mean-reversion target to offer minimum profit and sufficient reward relative to risk. Set the target toward the nearest relevant mean or value reference, choosing a farther reference with stronger confidence and scaling for context. Place the stop beyond the recent structural extreme, with volatility-based distance limits. Exit on either the stop or target; the pattern-based early-exit threshold exceeds the maximum possible pattern score, so it does not produce exits.
