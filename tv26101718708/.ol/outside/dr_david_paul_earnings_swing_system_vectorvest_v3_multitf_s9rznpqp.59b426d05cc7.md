On each bar, calculate a bullish confluence score from: trend alignment (fast EMA above slow EMA and price above long-term EMA, weighted twice); accelerating price momentum; relative performance versus a market benchmark; volume above its recent average; bullish market regime (price above a regime EMA, optionally requiring that EMA to rise); RSI above its midpoint but below overbought; bullish stochastic alignment below its overbought zone; positive and rising MACD histogram; price above VWAP; and ADX above a strength threshold. Buy or enter long when the score meets its minimum, the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, the regime is bullish, a bullish EMA crossover occurred within a recent-bar window, and the buy cooldown has elapsed. Sell or exit when the score is at or below its maximum, the fast EMA is below the slow EMA, the regime is bearish (optionally requiring the regime EMA to fall), a bearish EMA crossunder occurred within a recent-bar window, and the sell cooldown has elapsed. There is no short-entry or options decision rule; the ATR-based stop is display-only.
