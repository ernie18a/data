Use external market-breadth data: subtract declining issues from advancing issues, and subtract declining volume from advancing volume. If either volume series is unavailable, use a neutral multiplier of one. Multiply the issue difference by the volume difference, then subtract a longer-period EMA of that weighted series from a shorter-period EMA to form an oscillator. When the oscillator crosses upward through a threshold after being below it, enter long if flat. Close the long after a fixed number of bars from entry.
