On each closed bar, choose one entry method. For FVG pullback: record a bullish gap when a candle’s low is above the high two bars earlier, or a bearish gap when its high is below the low two bars earlier; require gap size to meet a configurable ATR-based minimum. While the gap remains within its configurable lifetime, go long when price touches the bullish gap and a bullish candle closes above its lower boundary; go short on the mirrored bearish-gap condition. For EMA trend: go long on the first bar of a regime where the fast EMA is above the slow EMA, close is above the fast EMA, and RSI is above its midpoint; go short on the first bar of the inverse regime. Apply the selected sensitivity filter: the least restrictive setting adds no trend confirmation; the middle setting requires price to be on the corresponding side of the medium-term EMA; the strict setting also requires the prior completed higher-timeframe close to be on that side of its EMA and trend strength to meet a minimum. Optional enabled trading sessions and event blackout windows suppress entries. With one position at a time, ignore same-direction signals while a position is open and allow an opposite signal to reverse it. On entry, set fixed take-profit and stop-loss levels at configurable distances from the signal-bar close. Exit when either level is reached; if both are reached in the same bar, take profit has precedence.
