Add a weighted open-interest vote: rising price and rising open interest is bullish; rising price and falling open interest is mildly bullish; falling price and rising open interest is bearish; falling price and falling open interest is mildly bearish. Classify the score into strong bullish, bullish, sideways, bearish, or strong bearish; force sideways when trend strength is weak. For the market view, average the Nifty score with sector breadth (bullish minus bearish sectors) and selected-stock buildup breadth (bullish buildup minus bearish buildup). Map the resulting market score to strong bullish (“buy dips”), bullish (“prefer longs”), sideways (“trade levels”), bearish (“be cautious”), or strong bearish (“sell rallies”); weak Nifty trend can constrain a modest market score to sideways.
