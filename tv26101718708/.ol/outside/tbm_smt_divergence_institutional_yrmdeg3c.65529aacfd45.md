On each confirmed bar, identify confirmed swing highs and lows for the traded instrument and a correlated external instrument. Compare the latest two paired swings, requiring their pivot times to fall within a configurable synchronization window. Signal short when the traded instrument makes a higher high while the external instrument makes a lower high; signal long when the traded instrument makes a lower low while the external instrument makes a higher low. When the volatility filter is enabled, require the traded instrument’s current ATR to meet a configurable fraction of its ATR moving average. No exit rule is specified.
