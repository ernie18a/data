On confirmed bars, enter only when flat, after a post-trade cooldown, and during permitted trading hours. A volatility-event signal occurs when the volatility index rises sufficiently above its prior confirmed daily close or rises sufficiently over a short intraday lookback, provided it is above a minimum level; optionally require short-dated implied volatility to exceed the standard volatility index. This signal opens a position in the traded instrument, normally long. Optionally, a separate signal opens a short when the volatility index falls sharply from its prior daily close, but only when the event signal is absent. An optional reverse mode flips the direction of every entry. For either position, exit at a fixed adverse price move, a fixed favorable price move, or after a maximum holding period. For volatility-event positions, also close the full position when volatility returns near its prior daily close, or reduce it once volatility has eased below an intermediate threshold and close the remainder on full reversion. For positions opened after a sharp volatility decline, exit when the holding period expires or a volatility-event signal appears.
