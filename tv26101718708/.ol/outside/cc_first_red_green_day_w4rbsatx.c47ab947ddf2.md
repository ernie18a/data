On completed signal-period candles, identify a recent local high preceded by at least the configured minimum count of higher closes within the allowed pause window. A bearish setup arms on the first qualifying red candle on or after that high; cancel it if price closes above the setup high or the setup expires. Confirm a short when price closes below the configured moving average, provided its normalized slope is not too strongly upward. The moving-average break may occur before the red candle only if the candle follows within the allowed gap; otherwise it must occur after arming. Permit only one confirmed short per high. Mirror these rules for a recent local low preceded by enough lower closes: arm on the first qualifying green candle, cancel if price closes below the setup low or it expires, and confirm a long on a moving-average close above it when the slope is not too strongly downward; apply the same break-before-signal timing and one-signal-per-low rules. After confirmation, optionally enter on a pullback to the signal candle's range or moving-average retest zone. Alternatively, enter on a close beyond the signal candle's low for a short or high for a long, only within the entry window and, if required, before the zone has been touched. No position exit rule is specified.
