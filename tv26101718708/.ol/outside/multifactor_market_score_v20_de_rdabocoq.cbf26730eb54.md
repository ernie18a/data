Classify the instrument and select an appropriate benchmark. Build a normalized composite score from available components: relative strength versus the benchmark and the instrument’s own history (intermediate and long-term returns, proximity to its yearly high, and relative-strength trend); for equities, earnings, revenue growth, and return on equity; and trend quality (price versus its long-term average, short-term versus long-term averages, and drawdown from a recent high). Renormalize the component weights when data is unavailable. Use history length, data coverage, model confidence, benchmark availability, and an optional safety filter as eligibility gates; the filter requires a healthy long-term trend and acceptable recent drawdown, plus adequate trading liquidity where applicable. Signal a regular buy when the score is above its configured buy threshold on consecutive bars and all buy gates pass. Signal a strong buy when the stronger gates also pass: strong relative strength and trend, high coverage and confidence, limited drawdown, and, for equities, adequate fundamental quality; require the score to remain above its stronger threshold, rise over recent bars, have positive momentum, and coincide with a recent price high. Signal exit when the score crosses below the configured buy threshold, provided minimum history and watch-level coverage remain available. A separate sell alert fires when the score crosses below its configured weak-score threshold. The framework produces long/buy and exit or avoid decisions; it has no short-entry logic.
