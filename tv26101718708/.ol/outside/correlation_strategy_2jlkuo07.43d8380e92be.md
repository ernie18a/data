During the active date window, calculate a weighted moving average of the same-timeframe price source of a correlated external instrument. Enter long on the traded instrument when that average has risen on each of the last five bars and the current position is flat or short. When currently long, reverse to short if the average has fallen on each of the last five bars. Record the external instrument’s price at entry. While long, close if its price rises above the recorded price by the configured long take-profit percentage or falls below it by the configured long stop-loss percentage. While short, close if its price rises above the recorded price by the configured short take-profit percentage, or falls below it by the configured short stop-loss percentage. Apply entries and exits only within the active date window.
