At each bar, calculate session-reset VWAP and the high and low of the configured opening range. A long-side call signal requires the base instrument to close above VWAP, its MACD line to be above its signal line, and its distance from VWAP to exceed a minimum percentage of price. The inverse instrument must show weakness: its close is below its open or its prior close. Also require a bullish base candle, a close higher than the prior close, volume above a multiple of its moving average, and a close above the opening-range high; volume, inverse confirmation, and opening-range filters may be disabled. A short-side put signal uses the symmetric conditions: base close below VWAP, MACD below its signal, sufficient VWAP distance, inverse instrument showing strength (close above its open or prior close), a bearish base candle, a lower close than the prior close, qualifying volume, and a close below the opening-range low, with the same optional filters. Emit a signal only when its full setup becomes true after being false, and optionally limit each direction to one signal per session. The logic defines no exit signal.
