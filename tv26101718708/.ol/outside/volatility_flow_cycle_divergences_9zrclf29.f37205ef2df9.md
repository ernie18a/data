For the S&P 500, compare successive confirmed swing highs and lows in the VIX-to-S&P ratio with the S&P’s price swings. Signal a bearish setup when the S&P makes a higher swing high while the ratio makes a lower swing high, provided S&P momentum is strong. Signal a bullish setup when the S&P makes a lower swing low while the ratio makes a higher swing low, provided S&P momentum is weak. Otherwise, generate no directional signal.
