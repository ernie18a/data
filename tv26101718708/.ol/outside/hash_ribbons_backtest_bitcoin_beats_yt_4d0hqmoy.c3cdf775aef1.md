Trade long only. When flat and within the configured trading window, enter long if either: (1) the traded instrument’s daily short-term simple moving average crosses above its daily long-term average, and the latest hash-rate recovery cross is more recent than both the latest bearish price-average cross and the latest hash-rate capitulation cross; or (2) the instrument’s daily short-term average is above its long-term average and the short-term hash-rate average crosses above the long-term hash-rate average. Close the position when the short-term hash-rate average crosses below the long-term hash-rate average, or when the configured leveraged stop loss is reached. No short position is opened.
