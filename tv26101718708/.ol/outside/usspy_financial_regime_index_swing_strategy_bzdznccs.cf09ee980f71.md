Build a financial-conditions score from standardized, optionally winsorized data: equity returns for domestic and global markets; inverse-standardized investment-grade/high-yield performance; two-year yields with sign reversed; the ten-year minus two-year yield spread; the dollar index and volatility index with signs reversed; and Treasury-bill versus equity performance with sign reversed. Combine the components using normalized equal or inverse-volatility weights, then smooth the result with an exponential moving average. Go long when the score crosses above its long-entry threshold while rising and, if enabled, the traded instrument is above its simple moving average; also go long whenever the score is above its unconditional long floor. Go short when the score crosses below its short-entry threshold while falling and, if enabled, the instrument is below its simple moving average. Close a long when the score crosses below its long-exit level or a short signal occurs. Close a short when the score crosses above its short-exit level, a long signal occurs, or the score falls below its forced-short-exit guard. Apply the configured long-only, short-only, or two-sided trading restriction.
