For each fresh, untagged order-block zone formed after a validated market-structure break, consider a long when the zone is bullish, structure is not bearish, and price is in the lower half of the current swing range. Consider a short when the zone is bearish, structure is not bullish, and price is in the upper half. Validate breaks with displacement, a strong close beyond the pivot, and a recent, well-formed pivot; for a reversal, require the prior leg’s protected swing to break. Rank eligible zones by quality and confluence: favor a recent liquidity sweep, high-volume acceptance, nearby key levels, higher-timeframe agreement, reversal momentum confirmation, and alignment with a trending regime; penalize thin-volume gaps, conflicting higher-timeframe or structural bias, volatile conditions, age, and prior mitigation. Select the eligible zone with the highest resulting score as the setup direction. No exit decision is defined.
