During the configured morning trading window of the regular US session, calculate session-anchored VWAP and fast, medium, and slow EMAs. Permit new setups only while the session’s price/VWAP cross count is below its configured limit. A long setup requires price to reclaim VWAP from below (by the configured reclaim rule), then touch or dip near VWAP and close above it while all three EMAs are strictly ordered upward and rising; require rising volume when that filter is enabled. A short setup is the mirror image: reclaim from above, retest near VWAP, close below it, all EMAs strictly ordered downward and falling, and rising volume when enabled. A setup is invalidated by acceptance on the opposite side of VWAP, loss of EMA alignment or reclaim, leaving the trading window, or reaching the cross limit. When flat, place a stop entry just beyond the setup candle in the setup direction, only if the distance to the protective stop at the other side of that candle plus a buffer is within the configured risk cap. Once filled, exit at that protective stop or at the configured fixed profit target. Other-market and VWAP status displays do not affect trades.
