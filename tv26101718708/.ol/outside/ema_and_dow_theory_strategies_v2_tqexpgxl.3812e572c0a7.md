Track fast and slow exponential averages for the traded instrument and an external market index. The directional bias is long when the instrument’s fast average is above its slow average, or when they are equal and the index’s fast average is below its slow average; it is short when the instrument’s fast average is below its slow average. Separately, detect confirmed swing highs and lows. Set the structural trend upward when price breaks the latest swing high and downward when it breaks the latest swing low. Enter long only when flat, the bias and structural trend are long, RSI is below its overbought limit, and a post-stop long-entry lockout has cleared. Enter short only when flat, the bias and structural trend are short, RSI is above its oversold limit, and a post-stop short-entry lockout has cleared. Close an opposing position before entering in the new direction. Close a position on a break that reverses the structural trend. Also exit when unrealized loss crosses the configured stop threshold, unless the opposite directional bias is active; after a stop, suppress re-entry in that direction until the trend color improves. Scale out part of a profitable position when its trend color weakens. When enabled, take successive partial profits at volatility-based price targets derived from average true range.
