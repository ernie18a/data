Use the traded instrument’s signal from monthly Heikin Ashi candles of a broad-market ETF. Calculate candle balance as (close − open) ÷ (high − low), smooth it with an exponential moving average, and derive rolling upper- and lower-percentile levels. Enter long when the upper percentile is rising and the smoothed balance is positive or crosses upward through a lower oversold threshold. Enter short when the smoothed balance is negative and both percentile levels are falling, or when it crosses downward through an upper threshold. No separate exit rule is specified; an opposite entry can reverse the position.
