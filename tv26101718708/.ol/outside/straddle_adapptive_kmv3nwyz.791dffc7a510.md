Compute a required rise percentage from the external volatility index using a tiered multiplier that increases at higher volatility levels. Signal long on the straddle when its combined closing premium reaches or exceeds the session low increased by that percentage, then track the highest combined close since the signal. Signal exit when the combined close falls by the configured fraction of the rise from the session low to that tracked high. After exit, set the session low to the current combined close and resume tracking; reset the low and spike state each day.
