During the configured date window, form the ratio of the configured long asset’s price to the configured short asset’s price on the selected timeframe. If the ratio crosses the configured entry level in either direction, both entry and stop levels are nonzero, the chart’s instrument matches a configured side, and the strategy is flat, enter long on the long asset’s chart or short on the short asset’s chart. For an open long position, place an exit that closes the configured fraction when the chart instrument reaches the configured take-profit or stop level. The code does not place an exit for open short positions.
