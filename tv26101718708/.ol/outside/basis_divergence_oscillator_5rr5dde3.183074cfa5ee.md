Compare the traded instrument with its cash or spot reference by standardizing their price difference over a rolling window. At confirmed price pivots, signal bullish when price makes a lower low while the standardized basis makes a higher low, and bearish when price makes a higher high while the basis makes a lower high; require a meaningful oscillator gap, pivots within a maximum distance, and an oversold or overbought basis reading respectively. Also signal bullish continuation when price makes a higher low while the basis makes a lower low, and bearish continuation when price makes a lower high while the basis makes a higher high, subject to the gap and distance filters. Signal bullish on a confirmed basis-oscillator trough inside the lower extreme zone and bearish on a confirmed peak inside the upper extreme zone. These are directional signals; no exit condition is defined.
