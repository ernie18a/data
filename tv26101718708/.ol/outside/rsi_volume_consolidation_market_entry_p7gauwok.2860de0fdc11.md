For the traded instrument, emit a strong buy when its RSI is in the good or mildly bullish range and has risen for three consecutive readings, volume confirms recent buying activity, price has remained in a tight range for several bars and is in the upper half near the range high, the short-term average is above the medium-term average with price above the short-term average, and a broad market reference has its short-term average above its medium-term average and trades above its long-term average. Emit an early setup when RSI is in the good or recovering range, volume confirms, consolidation is present, and the instrument's short-term trend is bullish; this tier is excluded when a strong buy applies. Emit a watch signal when consolidation, good or mildly bullish RSI, rising RSI, and the bullish short-term trend apply, and neither higher tier applies. The script defines no short or exit decision.
