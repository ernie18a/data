For each scanned instrument, derive an adaptive price-flow baseline from fast and slow Kalman estimates, recent pivot-break direction and activity, and ATR. Form upper and lower ATR bands around the baseline. Set direction bullish when price closes above the upper band (and the baseline is rising if slope confirmation is enabled); set it bearish when price closes below the lower band (and the baseline is falling if enabled). Otherwise retain the prior direction; initialize direction from whether price is above or below the baseline. Confirm flips on closed bars when configured. A trade-ready bullish or bearish setup requires a fresh flip in that direction, relative volume above its baseline threshold, distance from the active trail within its allowed ATR limit, RSI not beyond the direction-specific extreme, no active opposite-flip warning, and no applicable stale-data, session-end, or market-close block. When enabled, require higher-timeframe direction agreement and/or an active session. Near the opposite band, issue a flip-risk warning rather than a confirmed flip. The indicator provides directional states and setup alerts; it does not define a separate position exit rule.
