For each related instrument, compare the current period’s high and low with the previous period’s high and low, first inverting prices for instruments quoted in the opposite direction. Repeat across five horizons: current week versus prior week, current day versus prior day, current New York six-hour session versus the prior session, current 90-minute segment versus the prior segment, and current quarter of a 90-minute segment versus the prior quarter. A bearish signal occurs when at least one instrument makes a higher high and at least one does not; a bullish signal occurs when at least one makes a lower low and at least one does not. Report the signal by horizon; the indicator specifies no entry, exit, or trade execution rules.
