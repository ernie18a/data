Go long when the instrument meets all of these trend and relative-strength conditions: its close is above its short-, medium-, and long-term simple moving averages; the short-term average is above both longer-term averages, and the medium-term average is above the long-term average; the long-term average is rising; price is sufficiently above its trailing-year low and sufficiently near its trailing-year high; and its performance relative to its own intermediate-term average is stronger than the broad-market ETF’s corresponding performance. Also require either a volatility contraction, where the recent trading range is substantially narrower than the preceding range and current volume is well below its recent average, or a breakout: the close crosses above the prior short-term high on volume above its recent average. No short or exit rule is defined.
