On daily data for the traded instrument, target long when its close is above its long-term simple moving average, or when it is below that average but above its short-term simple moving average; otherwise target flat. A bear regime begins when QQQ falls beyond a defined drawdown from its trailing multi-year high. At entry, classify it as grinding if year-over-year CPI is above a defined inflation gate, otherwise as fast-recovering. Keep the regime latched until QQQ recovers near its multi-year high; during the bear, a fast-recovering regime may upgrade to grinding if CPI rises above the gate, but never downgrade. In a grinding regime, target long only above the traded instrument’s long-term average; otherwise use the normal two-average rule. Buy when the target changes from flat to long and sell or exit when it changes from long to flat. The rule does not take short positions.
