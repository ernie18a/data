Build a smoothed, normalized sentiment score from weighted price momentum, volatility behavior, RSI, buying-volume share, position in the recent price range, the share of major crypto assets trading above their trend averages, inverse stablecoin-dominance change, TOTAL/TOTAL2 trend scores, and BTC-dominance context. Classify the market as bull/risk-on when the traded asset is above its long-term average and the aggregate market score is at least neutral; classify it as bear/risk-off when both are below neutral; otherwise classify it as mixed. Emit an accumulation-watch signal when sentiment is in extreme fear and the regime is bull/risk-on; emit a risk-off warning when sentiment is fearful and the regime is bear/risk-off; emit trend continuation when sentiment is greedy, the regime is bull/risk-on, and short-term momentum is not fading versus longer-term momentum; emit a trim/crowding-risk signal when sentiment is in extreme greed and short-term momentum is fading, only when that condition first becomes true. The script also flags bullish divergence when price makes a lower pivot low while sentiment makes a materially higher low, and bearish divergence when price makes a higher pivot high while sentiment makes a materially lower high, subject to zone, spacing, and intervening-path filters. These are indicator signals, not automatic trade orders.
