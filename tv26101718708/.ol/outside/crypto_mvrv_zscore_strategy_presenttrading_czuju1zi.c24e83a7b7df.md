Using external Bitcoin market capitalization and realized capitalization data, calculate a market z-score from their difference divided by the standard deviation of market capitalization; calculate the ratio of market to realized capitalization; then standardize the difference between the market z-score and that ratio by its rolling mean and standard deviation, and smooth it. When this signal crosses above the long-entry threshold, enter long and close any short; when it crosses below the short-entry threshold, enter short and close any long. Enable long entries and exits, short entries and exits, or both according to the selected trade direction.
