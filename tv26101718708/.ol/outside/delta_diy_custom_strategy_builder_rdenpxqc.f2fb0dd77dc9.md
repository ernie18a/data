On each bar, evaluate the selected leading indicator as bullish or bearish, then require every enabled confirmation filter to agree with that direction. The lead may be based on the selected indicator’s trend, crossover, threshold, or breakout rule; confirmation filters use their corresponding directional rules, such as price relative to a moving average, moving-average alignment or crossover, oscillator direction or threshold, trend state, volume condition, or price relative to VWAP. A range filter requires price to break out in the matching direction, and the choppiness filter permits either direction only while the market is considered trending. Emit a long signal when the bullish lead and all enabled long filters hold; emit a short signal when the bearish lead and all enabled short filters hold. A lead remains eligible only for the configured expiry window while its direction persists. Optionally suppress signals that do not alternate with the previous accepted direction. The indicator defines no exit rule.
