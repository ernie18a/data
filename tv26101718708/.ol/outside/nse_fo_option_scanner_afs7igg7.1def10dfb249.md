On each bar, score bullish and bearish setups separately using five checks: the fast average is above (bullish) or below (bearish) the slower average; volume-and-price behavior indicates bullish or bearish positioning; the sector index is above or below its trend average; RSI is not extreme against the proposed direction; and volume is unusually high relative to its recent average. Award one point per satisfied check. Buy a call when the bullish score is high and the fast average crosses above the slower average. Buy a put when the bearish score is high and the fast average crosses below the slower average. The dashboard may show a weak directional setup when its score is moderate and the averages align, but this does not trigger a strong entry signal. No exit decision is defined.
