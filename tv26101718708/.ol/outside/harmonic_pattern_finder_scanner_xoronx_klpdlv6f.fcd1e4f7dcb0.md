Identify completed Bat, Butterfly, Shark, Cypher, Crab, Deep Crab, or Gartley structures in alternating price swings. A valid structure must meet its pattern’s Fibonacci-ratio constraints, with the final swing reaching the completion zone. Treat a structure beginning at a swing low as bullish and one beginning at a swing high as bearish: bullish implies long, bearish implies short. The indicator defines no exit rule.
