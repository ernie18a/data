Sum volume from nine external BTC markets, calculate its moving average, and measure the sum’s percentage excess over that average. When the sum is above its average and its percentage excess exceeds a configured threshold, require the candle to satisfy all three price-distance tests: close minus low, high minus open, and open minus close are each below a configured threshold. Enter long on the traded instrument only if its close is also above the previous bar’s close. Do not enter short. Exit a long using a trailing stop, a loss limit, or a profit target.
