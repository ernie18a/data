Go long when the short moving average crosses above the medium moving average, or when price closes above a medium- or long-term moving average for three consecutive bars after the prior bar was below it. When the external volatility index filter is enabled, allow entries only while the index is below its threshold; exit any open position if it rises above the threshold. For an open long, set an initial stop below entry. When unrealized profit reaches the first trigger, replace it with a stop below the price at that trigger; at the second profit trigger, use a trailing stop below the highest high since entry. The stop may only move upward. Also exit if the short moving average crosses below the medium moving average.
