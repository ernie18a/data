When the current close is below the Bollinger basis, count these bullish conditions: Bollinger Bands extend outside the Keltner Channels; ADX rises; RSI rises; negative directional movement falls; the close is between the lower Bollinger Band and basis; the close is below the lower Keltner Channel; the lowest low over the recent five bars touched or broke the lower Bollinger Band; and RSI is below 30. Set the count to zero if RSI is above 70. Enter long when the count meets the configured strength threshold, subject to the configured date window and market filter: block entry when the selected external market’s daily percentage change is at or beyond either configured hot or cold limit. Submit a limit order at the signal bar’s close; cancel it if it has not filled by the next bar. Exit the long position once at least 30 bars have passed since entry and it is Thursday. The strategy generates no short entries.
