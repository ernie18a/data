When flat, score bullish and bearish evidence independently from: aligned fast, medium, and slow moving averages; rising or falling recent swing highs or lows; RSI above or below its midpoint but outside its corresponding extreme zone; MACD direction and zero-line position; a fresh MACD crossover; stochastic direction outside its extreme zone; unusually high volume accompanying a candle in the matching direction; matching reversal or breakout candlestick patterns; Supertrend direction; overlap with the corresponding order-block zone; and RSI divergence against price (bearish divergence also requires the fast average below the medium average). Enter long or short when that side's score reaches the minimum, the market is inside the permitted session and outside excluded opening and closing windows, volatility is below its cap, and the available reward-to-risk ratio meets its minimum. Set an ATR-based protective stop and profit target, with an optional ATR-based trailing stop. Close a long when the moving-average trend turns bearish, or a short when it turns bullish. Do not open another position while one is open.
