Generate long and short setup signals only; there is no exit rule. A confirmed long trigger is either (1) price sweeps below a prior confirmed swing low, closes back above it with a bullish candle and sufficient body displacement, or (2) price is inside a bullish inverted fair value gap and forms a bullish candle with sufficient body displacement. Reverse these conditions for shorts using a swept swing high or a bearish inverted gap. A gap forms when three-candle price action leaves an untraded interval; it inverts when price closes beyond its far boundary, and remains active until price invalidates it from the other side. Score each direction by adding one point for a confirmed trigger, a recent matching SMT divergence, matching trend change or trend state, overlap with a matching fair value gap or inverted gap, and matching consecutive-close confirmation. SMT is bearish when the instrument makes a higher swing high while at least one correlated instrument fails to make a higher high; it is bullish when the instrument makes a lower swing low while at least one correlated instrument fails to make a lower low. Consecutive-close confirmation requires at least two rising closes for bullishness or falling closes for bearishness. Emit a lower-grade setup when the score meets its configured minimum but remains below the higher-grade threshold; emit the higher-grade setup when it meets that threshold. Both require an enabled trading session and a volatility regime above the configured chop floor, and same-direction signals must respect a cooldown. Do not use volume as a gate: the volume condition is calculated but does not affect setup signals.
