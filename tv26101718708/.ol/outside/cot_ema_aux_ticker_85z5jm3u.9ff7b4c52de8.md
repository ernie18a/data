On each weekly bar after the configured start date, enter long when commercial net positioning change is positive for the first selected currency and negative for the second, the traded instrument’s EMA is rising, and each enabled auxiliary instrument’s weekly EMA is moving in its configured direction (rising normally, falling when inverted). Enter short when all these conditions are reversed. Each entry uses a stop at the current week’s low for longs or high for shorts. Close open positions once their open profit is positive.
