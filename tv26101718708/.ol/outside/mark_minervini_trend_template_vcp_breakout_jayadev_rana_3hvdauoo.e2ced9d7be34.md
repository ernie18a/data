Go long when at least the required number of trend conditions hold: price is above the medium- and long-term averages; the medium average is above the long average; the long average is rising; the short average is above both longer averages; price is above the short average; price is sufficiently above its yearly low and near its yearly high; and the symbol’s benchmark-relative return ranks sufficiently well against its own recent history. Also require a base whose high-low depth contracts across full, half, and quarter windows, whose shortest-window depth is below its limit, and whose recent volume is not expanding relative to its longer average. Enter at the close when price breaks above the prior base high, stays within the allowed extension above that pivot, and volume meets its average-volume requirement. Set the stop at the tighter of the maximum-loss price and just below the base low; set a profit target a fixed percentage above entry. Exit if the low touches the stop, the high touches the target, or—if neither occurs—the close falls below the short-term average. This logic opens long positions only.
