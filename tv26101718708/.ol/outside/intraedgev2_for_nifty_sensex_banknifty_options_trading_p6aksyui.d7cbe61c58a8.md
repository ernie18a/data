Generate directional signals on confirmed bars; reset session state daily. Use a correlated index for index instruments and a broad-market index for stocks and stock options. For options, obtain gap information from the underlying and VWAP direction from its stock or futures reference; retain the contract’s own opening range, momentum and price structure. Use routed underlying or futures volume where applicable.

Cross-market divergence: for index instruments, signal bullish divergence when the traded instrument reaches its rolling low while its peer does not, and bearish divergence when it reaches its rolling high while its peer does not. Reverse these interpretations for stocks and stock options: unmatched highs indicate relative strength and unmatched lows indicate relative weakness. A downside liquidity sweep combined with recent bullish divergence produces a long setup; an upside sweep combined with recent bearish divergence produces a short setup. Divergence may precede the sweep, or cross-market divergence may follow it within the permitted window. Synthetic cumulative-volume-delta divergence can supply the preceding confirmation: lower price lows with stronger delta and an upper-range close are bullish; higher highs with weaker delta and a lower-range close are bearish. This delta confirmation is disabled on stocks and option charts. A sweep requires penetration of an eligible opening-range, prior-session, VWAP, volume-profile or expected-range level, a close back through it, and a sufficiently large rejection wick.

Macro bias: compare the previous session’s extremes with the preceding session and make the same comparison for the benchmark. An unmatched higher high gives a short-rallies bias; otherwise, an unmatched lower low gives a buy-dips bias. After the opening range forms, include this bias in the trend vote.

Opening-range direction becomes bullish or bearish when a confirmed candle closes beyond the corresponding boundary with sufficient candle body outside it. Clear that direction after sustained re-entry into the range, or after an aged breakout closes beyond the midpoint against its direction. Build a signed trend score from VWAP behavior, confirmed swing progression, agreement between price and a sloping moving average, price relative to a completed higher-timeframe moving average, and macro divergence. Neutralize moving-average votes within volatility-based dead bands. On non-option charts, VWAP reclaim or trading above value supplies a strong bullish vote; rejection or trading below value supplies a strong bearish vote, with weaker votes for simple directional positioning. Near VWAP is neutral. On option charts, use a weaker vote from underlying price above or below its own VWAP.

Apply the following verdicts in order: WAIT while the opening range forms, during a detected chop regime or the applicable late-session entry cutoff, or after excessive daily-range consumption. Chop is a threshold count of low relative volume, prolonged trading inside value, persistently neutral momentum, absent directional structure, compressed value area and repeated point-of-control crossings. Next, flag AGAINST for an opening-gap reversal confirmed by opening-range-close momentum; WAIT if that warning remains pending without a breakout. Flag AGAINST for an unfilled large-gap magnet, trend score opposing the breakout, or a breakout opposing an established gap continuation that has avoided meaningful retracement. For an otherwise aligned breakout, WAIT if momentum latched at opening-range completion is exhausted in its direction. Otherwise signal long or short with the breakout when the trend score confirms it, enough non-macro components contribute, and VWAP velocity does not oppose it. WAIT in all remaining cases. AGAINST warns against following the breakout; it does not automatically reverse the position.

Separately, allow bullish opening-range breakout and retest alerts only without a bearish macro divergence or opposing gap continuation and below the weekly volatility-implied ceiling; apply the inverse filters to bearish alerts. No position-exit rule or option call/put, strike or buy-versus-sell selection is implemented.
