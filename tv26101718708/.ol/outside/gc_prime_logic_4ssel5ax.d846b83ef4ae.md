Compute a directional score by combining smoothed, normalized price change with smoothed, normalized cumulative volume delta. Enter long when the score crosses above a positive threshold, price is above an upper band around its long-term average, and daily price and open interest both rise or both fall. Enter short when the score crosses below the negative threshold, price is below the lower band, and daily price falls while open interest rises. Raise the entry threshold during the specified morning session. Exit a long when the score crosses below the negative base threshold, price crosses below the lower band, or unusually high open interest coincides with a negative score. Exit a short on the mirrored conditions: score crosses above the positive base threshold, price crosses above the upper band, or unusually high open interest coincides with a positive score.
