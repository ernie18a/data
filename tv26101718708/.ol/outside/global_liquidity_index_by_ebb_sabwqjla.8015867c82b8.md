Build a global liquidity series from central-bank balance sheets, subtracting reverse-repo balances and Treasury cash, and adding regional money supplies converted to a common currency. Optionally reduce the series as bond volatility rises. Smooth it and optionally express it as a rate of change, then compare fast and slow exponential averages: go long when the fast average crosses above the slow average, and go short when it crosses below. No separate exit rule is defined.
