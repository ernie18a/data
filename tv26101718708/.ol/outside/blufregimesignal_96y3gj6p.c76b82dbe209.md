Maintain a persistent bullish or bearish state. In a bullish state, raise an ATR-based trailing floor as price rises; switch to bearish when the close falls below the floor. In a bearish state, lower an ATR-based trailing ceiling as price falls; switch to bullish when the close rises above it. Each switch signals the new direction and can serve as an exit from the prior direction.

Also signal a directional trend entry when the efficiency-ratio regime changes into trend: choose long if price is above session VWAP, short otherwise. Score the direction on five checks: trend-level efficiency ratio, fast-versus-slow moving-average alignment, directional RSI thrust, histogram momentum that is beyond zero and accelerating for consecutive bars, and price on the corresponding side of VWAP. Apply the configured minimum signal tier, same-direction lockout, cooldown, and higher-timeframe trend filter; the higher-timeframe filter may require alignment, opposition, or be disabled. A transition into range is reported as a regime change, not a directional entry. Volume does not gate these decisions.
