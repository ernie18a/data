Build a volume-weighted regression centerline anchored to a recent swing pivot or a rolling window, and measure its residual volatility. A tag of the upper outer band can produce a short; a tag of the lower outer band can produce a long. Require at least one matching reversal tell: a close back inside the band, bearish or bullish momentum divergence at the stretch, a rejection sweep of equal or recent swing highs or lows, or a matching extreme consensus across normalized market factors. Permit the fade only when the configured regime test identifies mean reversion, using a variance-ratio test, a negative correlation between prior deviation from the mean and the next return, or both; an optional factor-consensus requirement can also veto it. Enforce a minimum interval between signals. The framework defines no exit decision.
