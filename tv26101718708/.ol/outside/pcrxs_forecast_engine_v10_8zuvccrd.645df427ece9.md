On each bar, compute a price oscillator as the difference between a short and a longer weighted moving average, scaled by ATR, and its rolling standard deviation. A long setup occurs when the oscillator crosses upward through one standard deviation below zero, provided the higher-timeframe close is above its medium and long EMAs; RSI is below overbought; MACD histogram is not falling; money flow is not materially negative; volume exceeds its recent average floor; the halving-cycle measure is not strongly bearish; stablecoin dominance is not bearish; dominance favors the traded instrument (rising for BTC, falling for other crypto); and futures basis does not indicate excessive long leverage. A short setup occurs when the oscillator crosses downward through one standard deviation above zero, provided the higher-timeframe close is below its medium and long EMAs; RSI is above oversold; MACD histogram is not rising; money flow is not materially positive; volume exceeds its recent average floor; the halving-cycle measure is not strongly bullish; stablecoin dominance is not bullish; dominance does not favor the traded instrument; and stablecoin dominance is not near a recent high while falling. Exit a long if the oscillator crosses below zero, or RSI crosses down from overbought while higher-timeframe structure is bearish, or a short setup occurs. Exit a short if the oscillator crosses above zero, or RSI crosses up from oversold while higher-timeframe structure is bullish, or a long setup occurs. Independently, construct a forward price projection from regression trend, detected swing cycles, the halving cycle, and macro, dominance, futures, and global-market adjustments. If projected prices at two forward horizons both exceed the current price, with the nearer projection clearing a small positive margin, recommend long when confidence is sufficient; if both are below current price and the nearer clears a small negative margin, recommend short when confidence is sufficient. Confidence is the proportion of 19 directional factors agreeing with the projection direction, plus bonuses for a strong trend, elevated volume, a volatility squeeze, or strong money flow, capped below certainty. Entry signals take precedence over exits, which take precedence over these forecast-only recommendations.
