Generate directional setup signals only on confirmed bars. A bullish trigger is any of: price sweeps below the latest confirmed swing low, closes back above it with a bullish candle, sufficient displacement, and without having moved too far from that swing; price is inside an inverted bearish fair value gap, closes bullish, and has sufficient displacement; or price is inside a bullish order block, closes bullish, and has sufficient displacement. A bearish trigger is the mirror: sweep above the latest swing high and close back below it, bearish close inside an inverted bullish fair value gap, or bearish close inside a bearish order block; apply the same displacement and distance requirements where specified.

For each direction, count confluences: a confirmed directional trigger; recent matching intermarket divergence, where the traded instrument makes a new swing extreme but at least one comparison instrument fails to confirm it; a recent matching liquidity sweep; matching market-structure break or established trend; presence in a matching fair value gap, inverted fair value gap, or order block; matching two- or three-bar consecutive-close confirmation; location in the directional range zone (discount for bullish, premium for bearish); and position relative to the New York open (above for bullish, below for bearish).

Issue a higher-grade long or short when a confirmed trigger meets the higher confluence threshold and passes the applicable session gate, volatility/chop filter, volume filter, premium/discount rule, and same-direction cooldown. The volume filter applies only when enabled. The session gate applies to higher-grade signals when strict session filtering is enabled. The premium/discount rule may be configured as a warning instead of a block. Issue a lower-grade long or short when a confirmed trigger meets the lower threshold but remains below the higher threshold, passes the chop filter, and is outside the same-direction cooldown; this grade does not require the higher-grade session, volume, or premium/discount gates. Thresholds, filters, sessions, and cooldown are configurable.

On a higher-grade long, set entry at the signal close, stop below entry by a volatility-based distance, and profit targets above entry at configurable multiples of the risk distance. For a higher-grade short, mirror these levels. Treat a stop hit or first target hit as the end of the active risk setup.
