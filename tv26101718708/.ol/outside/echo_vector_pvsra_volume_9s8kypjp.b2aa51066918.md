On each bar where confirmed price and volume swing lows coincide, go long if the price low is below the previous confirmed price low while the volume low is above the previous confirmed volume low. Go short when confirmed price and volume swing highs coincide, the price high is above the previous confirmed price high, and the volume high is below the previous confirmed volume high. Open a position only when no tracked position is active, using the signal bar’s close as entry. Exit a long at its percentage target above entry or stop below entry; exit a short at its percentage target below entry or stop above entry. If both levels are reached in one bar, count the target first. Use the traded instrument’s price data and the volume series being analyzed; the volume series may come from a separate configured symbol.
