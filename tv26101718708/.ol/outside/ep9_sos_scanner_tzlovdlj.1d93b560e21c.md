On a completed daily bar, flag a long setup when the prior bar’s close-to-close change is no greater than the allowed prior-up cap, the current close is in at least the required fraction of the day’s high-low range, the current real-body size exceeds the prior bar’s, the close meets the minimum price, and volume meets the minimum. Calculate average daily range percentage over the configured lookback. Set the stop at the day’s low if today’s range meets the configured fraction of average daily range; otherwise set it below the close by the configured fraction of average daily range. Accept the setup only when stop risk as a percentage of entry is no greater than average daily range percentage; this is the long/pass signal. Define the first and second profit targets at the configured risk multiples above entry. For trend context, assess whether price is above its long-term average, that average is rising, and the instrument’s ratio to the benchmark is above its own lookback average. A passing setup is GO when all three trend checks pass, and moderate when at least two pass. No short or exit decision is specified.
