On each confirmed bar, allow entries only when the regime is not avoid: directional strength must not be weak while the intermediate timeframe is mixed. For calls, require the higher timeframe to be bullish (price above its VWAP); for puts, require it bearish (price below its VWAP). Also block calls against bearish intermediate structure and puts against bullish structure. Require the lower-timeframe trend, VWAP side, candle quality, volume, and any enabled strength, chop, and RSI filters to pass.

Enter calls or puts through any qualifying path: (1) price crosses VWAP and, within a short window, closes on the new side with the corresponding lower-timeframe trend; (2) after the opening range is set, price breaks its high or low; (3) price pulls back to a short-term average or VWAP, then resumes in the trend direction without excessive extension; or (4) an optional momentum path produces a strong directional candle while aligned with the higher-timeframe trend and VWAP. There are also countertrend entries: buy calls below VWAP after oversold conditions turn upward, or buy puts above VWAP after overbought conditions turn downward, with confirming price action, volume, and non-opposing intermediate structure. These countertrend paths bypass the quality-score gate.

For other entry paths, score confluence using higher- and intermediate-timeframe alignment, volume, directional strength, RSI, position relative to a moving average, proximity to session value-area levels, setup type, time of day, and directional volume. Require the score to clear its entry threshold; a higher score marks a stronger signal. If both directions qualify on one bar, choose the direction supported by directional movement, otherwise issue no entry.

For a call trade, exit when price closes at or below the higher of its protective stop and the recent swing low, or when at least two of these occur after the minimum holding period: close below the entry-bar low, below the short-term average, or below VWAP. Mirror these rules for puts using the lower of the stop and recent swing high, and the entry-bar high, average, and VWAP. Initialize the stop beyond the entry bar's adverse wick, with an ATR-based distance floor; ratchet it toward the medium-term average after a delay, never loosening it. Ignore VWAP as a soft-exit trigger for countertrend entries.
