On each chart bar, calculate a short and long weighted moving average of the traded instrument’s close. Define an up signal when the short average crosses above the long average, and a down signal when it crosses below. Determine trend confirmation on a higher timeframe: set the trend bullish when that timeframe’s close is above the weighted moving average of its highs, bearish when below the weighted moving average of its lows, and otherwise retain the previous trend state; use the prior higher-timeframe bar’s state. When flat, enter long on an up signal with bullish confirmation, or short on a down signal with bearish confirmation. Close a long when confirmation turns bearish and close a short when it turns bullish. Take-profit and stop-loss levels are calculated and displayed but do not trigger orders.
