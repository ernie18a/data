When flat, enter long if the traded instrument reaches a specified rise above its daily open, its cumulative volume for the day meets or exceeds a benchmark based on prior daily volume, its money flow indicates buying pressure, and the external market index is above its daily moving average; only enter before the intraday cutoff. While long, trail a stop below the highest high using a multiple of the instrument’s volatility measure, and set a profit target above entry using a volatility-based distance. Exit if the close falls below the trailing stop or reaches the profit target. No short entries.
