On daily data, issue a long/buy signal when the close crosses above its 20-day moving average and all these conditions hold: close is above the 150-day and 200-day averages; the 150-day average is above the 200-day average; the 200-day average is rising over roughly 30 trading days; the 50-day average is above both the 150-day and 200-day averages; close is at least 30% above its 52-week low and no more than 25% below its 52-week high. No short or exit signal is defined.
