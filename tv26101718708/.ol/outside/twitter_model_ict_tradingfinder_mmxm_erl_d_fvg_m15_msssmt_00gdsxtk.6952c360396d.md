During an enabled trading session, look for a long setup when the close is below both the previous day’s low and the New York midnight open; look for a short setup when it is above both the previous day’s high and that open. For a long setup, find the nearest recent bearish candle and set a confirmation level to the higher open of the next two bars (or the next bar’s open when only one is available). For a short setup, use the nearest recent bullish candle and the lower of those opens (or the next bar’s open). Signal long when a later close crosses up through its level, or short when a later close crosses down through its level, provided the crossing occurs within the level’s validity period. The first-signal-per-day mode permits only one setup per direction until the daily reset; the all-day mode permits repeated setups. The code’s SMT divergence is display-only and does not gate signals; it contains no FVG or MSS trade condition.
