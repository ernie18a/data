Across eight configurable comparison assets, optionally reverse each asset’s price orientation, then compare the current period’s high and low with the prior period’s. Evaluate five horizons: current week vs prior week, current day vs prior day, current New York 6-hour session vs prior session, current New York 90-minute interval vs prior interval, and current 22.5-minute interval vs prior interval. At each horizon, signal bearish divergence when at least one asset makes a higher high and at least one does not; signal bullish divergence when at least one makes a lower low and at least one does not. In Breaker display mode, identify the last asset in the scan that made the breakout; in Failed / non-confirming mode, identify the last asset that did not. This produces directional labels only; it defines no entry, position management, or exit rules.
