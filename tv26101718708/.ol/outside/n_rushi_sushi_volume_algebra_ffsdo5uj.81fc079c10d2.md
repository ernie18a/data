Compute a score from volume percentage change, absolute price percentage change, and the volume ratio, with the ratio capped. Go long when volume increases by more than 5%, price rises, and the score meets the weak-signal threshold. Go short when volume increases by more than 5%, price falls, and the score meets that threshold. Otherwise, wait; label a sufficiently high score without matching direction as caution.
