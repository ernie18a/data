Build six normalized macro-health scores from volatility/safe-haven and currency signals, market breadth, equity trend and volume, Bitcoin trend, oil trend adjusted for its correlation with equities, and high-yield credit trend. Combine them using weights that adapt to each block’s correlation with the charted asset and its inverse volatility; smooth the composite and blend it with its normalized momentum. Signal TRADE when the resulting score rises above an upper threshold, and NO TRADE when it falls below a lower threshold; retain the prior state between thresholds. This is a trade/no-trade gate and does not specify long or short direction.
