For each holding, compute a daily technical strength score from its 10-, 20-, and 50-day returns and volatility-adjusted returns ranked against the other holdings, plus trend structure (price versus short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs; EMA ordering and slopes) and ATR-normalized distance below the medium- and long-term EMAs. Track score and portfolio-rank changes over recent sessions. Classify each holding in priority order: EXIT when it is in the portfolio’s weakest fifth and has at least one ATR of long-term EMA damage with a weak trend; otherwise TRIM when it is in the weakest quarter and either at least one ATR below the medium-term EMA or rapidly deteriorating while below that EMA; otherwise RECOVERING when its low score is improving materially; otherwise TRIM WATCH when it is in the weakest third and below the medium-term EMA or rapidly deteriorating; otherwise DETERIORATING when rapid score or rank loss is confirmed by a sub-healthy score, weakened trend, or price below the medium-term EMA; otherwise LEADER when it is in the strongest fifth with a strong trend; otherwise HOLD. Rank review priority first by EXIT, TRIM, then TRIM WATCH; within each tier, prioritize greater current weakness and recent score deterioration, with EMA damage and rank loss as tie-breakers. This produces portfolio trim/exit guidance; it does not generate long or short entries.
