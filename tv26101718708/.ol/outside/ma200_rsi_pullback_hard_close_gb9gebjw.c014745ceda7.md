Long-only. Enter when the instrument closes above its long-term moving average, its momentum oscillator is below the oversold entry threshold, the broad-market ETF is above its own long-term moving average, volatility index is below its ceiling, and the post-exit cooldown has elapsed. On entry, set a fixed stop below the entry price by a multiple of average true range. Exit when the oscillator exceeds its overbought threshold, the instrument closes below its moving average, the close reaches or falls below the stop, or the broad-market ETF falls below its moving average; the latter forces an exit of any open position. After an exit, wait the configured cooldown before entering again.
