Fit a mean-reverting process to recent closing prices by regressing each one-bar price decline on the prior close. Use the fitted relationship to estimate the equilibrium price and the standard deviation of its residuals. Measure the current close’s distance from equilibrium in residual standard deviations. Signal long when price is below the lower overshoot threshold and that distance is moving back toward equilibrium; signal short when price is above the upper threshold and moving back toward equilibrium. Emit a signal only on a confirmed bar, when it does not repeat the current position direction, and after the cooldown since the prior signal. Maintain a long or short position state; no separate exit signal is defined.
