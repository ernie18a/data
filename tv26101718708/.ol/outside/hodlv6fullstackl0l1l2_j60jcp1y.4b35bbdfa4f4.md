For the traded asset, combine three scores: a macro risk-appetite score from recent momentum in the dollar, gold, equities, volatility, copper, and oil; a trend score from short-, medium-, and long-term moving-average alignment, trend strength, recent asset momentum, and volatility rank; and an entry-value score that rewards deeper drawdowns from the asset’s all-time high, lower RSI, bearish or ranging regimes, and price below its long-term average, with additional reward for higher volatility and a penalty when RSI and price extension indicate overheating. Weight the entry-value score most, then macro and trend scores, to form a composite. Map higher composite levels to progressively larger periodic buys; middle levels to reduced or normal buys; and the lowest levels to pausing contributions or reducing the position. This is a long-only DCA sizing framework; it gives no short-entry signal.
