Use an exponential moving average of closing prices and Chaikin money flow. Go long when the instrument’s high crosses above the previous completed daily high, its close is above the moving average, and money flow is positive; submit the long entry as a stop order at the prior bar’s previous-daily-high level. Go short when its low crosses below the previous completed daily low, its close is below the moving average, and money flow is negative; submit the short entry as a stop order at the prior bar’s previous-daily-low level. No exit is effectively triggered: each close order is nested under its entry condition but requires the close to be on the opposite side of the moving average, making that condition unreachable.
