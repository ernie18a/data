When flat, build bullish and bearish evidence from weighted recent price changes, directional memory, trend and higher-timeframe EMA direction, VWAP, MACD, ADX, candle strength, relative volume, volatility, RSI, and optional structural-zone and candlestick signals. Score each side; enter the stronger side when its score and lead clear configured thresholds, the trading session and regime allow entries, cooldown and price-rearm conditions pass, and enabled directional gates do not oppose it. Gates may require bias and signal-cluster agreement, aligned multi-timeframe structure breaks, a volatility-stop crossover, adaptive-average position and slope, and stacked moving-average trend. Separate EMA-crossover and fast-entry triggers can also authorize an entry when their own directional conditions and applicable gates pass. If both directions trigger, keep only the higher-scoring side; discard a tie. After a loss, temporarily block that direction, extending the lockout after repeated or chop-related losses and blocking retests near the losing entry; a win can reset the loss streak. On an opposite signal, close the open position first, then enter the new direction once flat. Set an initial stop using the configured ATR and/or recent swing structure, and place a first profit-taking order plus a final target for any remainder. After the first target reduces the position, trail the stop behind the best price reached since activation. Close at the active stop or target.
