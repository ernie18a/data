For the traded instrument, calculate the squared ATR-normalized distances between price and its daily, weekly, and monthly anchored VWAPs, then rank their sum over a rolling window. Also calculate a robust median/MAD Z-score of close, close’s position within the bar, and an absolute opening-gap measure. For breadth, average the robust Z-scores of three broad-market ETFs; if breadth filtering is disabled, treat it as satisfied. Suppress signals when the gap exceeds its threshold. A long setup requires energy percentile above its threshold, close Z-score below the negative entry threshold, close near the bar low, and breadth Z-score below its negative confirmation threshold. A short setup requires energy percentile above threshold, close Z-score above the positive entry threshold, close near the bar high, and breadth Z-score above its positive confirmation threshold. On a long setup, close any open long or short; enter long when a long setup occurred ten bars earlier. On a short setup, close any open long and enter short. While short, optionally place an ATR-based stop above entry and take-profit below entry. No long brackets are used. The code does not actually gate short entries with its short-lock state.
