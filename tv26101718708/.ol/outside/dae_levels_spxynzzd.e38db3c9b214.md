During the morning entry window, go long after price closes above the opening range high and then retests that level from above while closing back above it with a bullish rejection pattern. Require bullish bias on at least three of the four higher timeframes, based on their latest sweep status, and require the breadth indicator not to be overbought. Go short on the mirror condition: a close below the opening range low followed by a retest from below with a bearish rejection pattern; require bearish bias on at least three timeframes and breadth not to be oversold. If enabled, also require volume above its recent average. A bullish sweep is a dip below the prior bar's low that closes back above it; a bearish sweep is a move above the prior bar's high that closes back below it. A sweep's bias is considered broken when price later closes beyond its sweep-candle close in the opposing direction.
