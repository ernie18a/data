Compare the traded instrument’s price with a selected sector benchmark by dividing its price by the benchmark’s price, then smooth that ratio with a moving average. Go long when the instrument’s faster price moving average is above its slower one and the ratio is above its smoothed value; enter or reverse to long when not already long. Go short when the faster price moving average is below the slower one and the ratio is below its smoothed value; enter or reverse to short when not already short. For either position, exit at a volatility-based stop or a profit target set at a fixed risk-to-reward multiple of the stop distance, using the position’s average entry price and current ATR.
