Within the configured trading window and permitted direction, enter long when the trend indicator flips bullish and the instrument closes above a higher-timeframe moving average of highs or closes, as configured. Enter short when it flips bearish and the close is below the corresponding higher-timeframe moving average of lows or closes. Close a position when the trend indicator flips against it, or use the trend indicator as a stop, according to the configured exit mode. Long and short positions are mutually exclusive.
