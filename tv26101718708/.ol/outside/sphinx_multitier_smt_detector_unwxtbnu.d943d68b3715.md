Compare the chart instrument with one or two correlated instruments. For each reference swing low, track whether each instrument later trades below its own low from the same anchor; signal bullish while at least one but not all tracked instruments have swept it. For each reference swing high, apply the symmetric rule above the highs and signal bearish. Use completed session extremes and prior nested intraday cycle extremes, plus confirmed swing pivots on enabled higher timeframes. Prefer a divergence against the first sister when both sister comparisons qualify. Keep each signal active until its divergent sister also sweeps the reference; then clear it. When directional gates are enabled, require the corresponding long or short gate and reject signals marked no-trade or below the permitted bias tier. Higher-timeframe pivot signals use the same sweep-disagreement rule.
