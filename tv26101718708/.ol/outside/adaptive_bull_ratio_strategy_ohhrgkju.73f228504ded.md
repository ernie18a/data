Trade calls only, once per listed expiry, while flat and before the day preceding expiry. Enter long when the trend indicator flips bullish, or when price crosses above an external trend line; for a delayed entry, require the trend to remain bullish, price to stay within the allowed entry band, and price not to have exceeded the invalidation level. Open a call ratio structure: buy two calls two strike steps below rounded at-the-money, sell two calls three steps above and two calls four steps above, buy four far out-of-the-money calls, buy one put at the four-step call strike, and sell one put at the two-step in-the-money call strike. Set an initial stop below entry; tighten it on later days. Exit all open legs if price falls below the stop or at the configured expiry-day square-off time.

While in the trade, if price first rises above the three-step call strike, roll the call position upward, add a farther out-of-the-money short call, and add a deep out-of-the-money long put. If price instead jumps directly above the four-step call strike, advance directly to the next stage: close the initial in-the-money call and short put, adjust the short calls, and add deep out-of-the-money long puts. After the intermediate stage, a rise above the four-step call strike advances to the uncapped stage: close the nearer short call and add a long call at that strike, while adding a long put there. In the uncapped stage, ratchet the stop upward as price makes new highs. Once price reaches the configured number of strike steps above the four-step call strike, optionally close the existing calls and buy replacement out-of-the-money calls, and optionally close all put legs.
