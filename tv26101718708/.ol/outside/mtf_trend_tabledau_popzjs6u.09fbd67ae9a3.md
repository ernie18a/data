For the traded instrument, classify its 15-minute, 1-hour, and 4-hour trends. A timeframe is bullish when price is above its EMA, the EMA is rising, price is sufficiently far from the EMA relative to ATR, and recent highs and lows are both higher than their counterparts in the prior structure window, allowing an ATR-based tolerance. Apply the inverse conditions for bearish. Otherwise classify it as transitional when price and EMA slope agree directionally but strength or structure does not, or when EMA slope and structure conflict; classify it as ranging in other cases. Signal long when at least two timeframes are bullish and the 4-hour trend is not bearish; signal short when at least two are bearish and the 4-hour trend is not bullish. Otherwise give no directional signal. The indicator defines no exit rule.
