Compare the traded market’s percentage change with the percentage change of its reference currency pair, then accumulate the difference over a rolling window. Positive accumulated relative momentum is bullish: allow or favor long positions; when it is nonpositive, do not take or exit a long. Mark a bullish divergence when the market rises while the currency pair falls, and a bearish divergence when the market falls while the pair rises; treat these as bullish and bearish warnings, respectively.
