Use participation breadth to filter and size trades on the reference instrument. When breadth is above the midpoint and rising, take existing trade signals at full size; in the middle range, use half size and closer targets; when breadth is low, stand aside or trade the reference alone. At an extreme high, do not open new positions; reduce exposure and tighten stops. If the reference rises while breadth falls, stop adding and protect open positions; treat this as a warning, not an exit. If the reference falls while breadth repairs from a deeply washed-out level, prepare for a reversal but wait for breadth to cross upward through the midpoint before entering long. Exit when breadth crosses downward through the midpoint. When an open disagreement ends because the reference and breadth move in the same direction again, lift the warning.
