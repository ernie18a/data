Calculate rolling correlation between the traded instrument’s percentage returns and an external broad altcoin-market index. Enter long when correlation is negative and the instrument’s Hull moving average is higher than it was two bars earlier; enter short when correlation is negative and the average is lower. Close a long when correlation rises above its target and the instrument’s price has reached the required profit above the position’s average entry price. Close a short when correlation rises above its target and price has fallen enough below the average entry price to meet the required profit.
