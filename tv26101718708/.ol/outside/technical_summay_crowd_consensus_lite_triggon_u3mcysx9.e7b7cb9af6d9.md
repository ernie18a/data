On intraday bars, give each enabled oscillator and moving average one buy, sell, or neutral vote using its conventional directional or overbought/oversold reading. Oscillators cover momentum, trend, and overbought/oversold measures; moving averages vote buy when price is above them and sell when below. Find the larger of the buy and sell vote totals and express it as a share of all enabled votes, including neutral votes. Treat the reading as neutral below the configured neutral cutoff; otherwise its dominant side sets the daily phase. Initialize the phase from the first non-neutral reading each day; thereafter retain it through neutral readings and update it when the dominant side reverses. On a confirmed bar where the phase changes, enter in the new phase direction only if that bar's consensus satisfies the configured minimum-or-maximum consensus condition and, when enabled, the full candle is above the selected EMA for a long or below it for a short. Skip candles that straddle the EMA. An opposite entry reverses the position. No entries are generated on daily or higher timeframes; there is no other exit rule. The contrarian display option does not reverse trade direction.
