Compare the previous two completed candles on the selected timeframe. Use the older candle’s high and low as the reference. Signal bullish when the newer candle’s low breaks below the reference low and it closes back at or above that low without closing above the reference high; or when it closes above the reference high without first breaking below the reference low; or when it breaks below the reference low and closes above the reference high. Signal bearish by reversing these conditions: break above the reference high and close back at or below it without closing below the reference low; close below the reference low without first breaking above the reference high; or break above the reference high and close below the reference low. These patterns indicate directional bias; the indicator does not specify entries or exits. It also reports historical probabilities of both sides being reached based on the completed Asian-session range and weekday, but these do not determine direction.
