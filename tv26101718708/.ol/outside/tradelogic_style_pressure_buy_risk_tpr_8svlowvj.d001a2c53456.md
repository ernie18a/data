For the traded stock, compare its price performance with a benchmark and smooth that relative-performance ratio. Classify trend pressure as favorable when most of these are true: price is above its short- and medium-term averages; the medium average is above the two long-term averages; the longest average is rising; and smoothed relative performance is rising. Classify it as intermediate when some, but not most, are true, and unfavorable otherwise. Separately classify buy risk as favorable when price is above its medium-term average but not excessively extended, intermediate when it is more extended, and unfavorable when below the average or excessively extended. Separately rate trend quality by counting whether price is above its short- and medium-term averages, the medium average is above both long-term averages, and smoothed relative performance is rising; a high count is favorable, a middling count intermediate, and a low count unfavorable. These are separate long-suitability and trend classifications; the indicator does not define a short-entry rule.
