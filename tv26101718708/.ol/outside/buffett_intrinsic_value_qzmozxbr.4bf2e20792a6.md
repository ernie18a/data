Estimate intrinsic value as trailing earnings per share multiplied by a base valuation multiple adjusted upward for expected growth, then normalized by the relevant regional 10-year government bond yield. Estimate growth from return on equity times the earnings retention ratio; if unavailable, use year-over-year annual earnings-per-share growth, then a manual fallback. Compare intrinsic value with market price: recommend BUY when the percentage discount meets or exceeds the configured safety margin, HOLD when intrinsic value is at or above price but the safety margin is not met, and SELL when intrinsic value is below price.
