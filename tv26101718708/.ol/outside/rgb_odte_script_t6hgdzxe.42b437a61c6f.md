Evaluate trades only when the average absolute daily open-to-close range over the selected lookback is available and positive, and the buffered range distance meets the configured minimum. Require every enabled risk and eligibility filter to pass: volatility index at or below its ceiling and, where enabled, no higher than its prior daily average; daily ATR as a percentage of price below its ceiling; daily ATR relative to average body range below its ceiling; the absolute spread between the instrument’s fast and slow EMAs no greater than the allowed multiple of daily ATR; any enabled external-market fast EMA at least as high as its slow EMA; and time within the permitted entry window. From the current price, add and subtract the buffered range distance, rounding outward to the configured price increment for options, to set upper and lower short strikes; place long hedges a configured number of increments beyond them. If all filters pass and VWAP trade typing is enabled, compare the confirmation-timeframe close with daily anchored VWAP using a neutral buffer: above it, sell a put spread at the lower strike with a lower protective put; below it, sell a call spread at the upper strike with an upper protective call; within the buffer, sell an iron condor using both spreads and both hedges. If VWAP trade typing is disabled, sell the put spread whenever all base filters pass. Otherwise, make no trade.
