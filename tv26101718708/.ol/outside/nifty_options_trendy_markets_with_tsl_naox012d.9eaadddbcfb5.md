Buy the call or go long when price crosses above the upper Bollinger Band while ADX exceeds its entry threshold, volume exceeds its moving average by a spike factor, and price is above the Supertrend. Buy the put or go short when price crosses below the lower Bollinger Band with the same ADX and volume conditions and price below the Supertrend. Exit a long on a bearish MACD crossover or when ADX falls below its exit threshold; exit a short on a bullish MACD crossover or when ADX falls below that threshold. The coded ATR stop comparisons cannot trigger because each stop is calculated from the current close and ATR is positive.
