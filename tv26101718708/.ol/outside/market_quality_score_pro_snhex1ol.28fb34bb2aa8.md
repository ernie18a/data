Using daily data, score relative strength from percentile ranks of 12-month return excluding the latest month, 6-month and 3-month returns, proximity to the 52-week high, and the slope of price relative to a market benchmark; use standalone returns for asset types without benchmark comparison. For equities, add a fundamentals score from earnings growth, revenue growth, and return on equity. Add trend quality from price versus its long-term moving average, the ordering of the medium- and long-term averages, and drawdown from the recent high. Combine available components using their configured weights, renormalizing over available data; smooth the total score for indices. Track data coverage, history sufficiency, benchmark availability, and a confidence score based on coverage and history.

Issue a regular buy when the total score is at or above the buy threshold for two consecutive bars and history, coverage, confidence, benchmark, and enabled safety checks pass. Safety checks require price above its long-term average and an acceptable recent drawdown; equities also require sufficient average dollar turnover. Issue a strong buy when the regular buy gates pass, the score remains above the strong threshold for two bars, is rising, has positive recent score momentum, and price makes a recent high; also require strong relative strength, trend, confidence, coverage, drawdown quality, and, for equities, fundamentals.

Issue an exit marker when the score crosses below the buy threshold, provided minimum history and watch-level coverage remain. Issue a sell alert when it crosses below the weak-score threshold. Recommend watch when the score reaches the watch threshold and minimum watch coverage, history, confidence, and benchmark conditions pass; classify inadequate coverage or history as weak data. A new-high divergence warning occurs when price makes a recent high while the score is below its prior lookback high and has declined over recent bars.
