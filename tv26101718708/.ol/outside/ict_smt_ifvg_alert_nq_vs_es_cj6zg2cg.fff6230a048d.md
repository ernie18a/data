Generate a bullish signal when either of two related index futures makes a lower low than its own prior swing low while the other holds above its prior swing low, and price retraces into a sufficiently large earlier bearish gap and closes at or above that gap’s lower boundary. Generate a bearish signal when either index makes a higher high while the other holds below its prior swing high, and price retraces into a sufficiently large earlier bullish gap and closes at or below that gap’s upper boundary. Optionally require the same directional divergence between the indices on a higher timeframe. No exit logic is defined.
