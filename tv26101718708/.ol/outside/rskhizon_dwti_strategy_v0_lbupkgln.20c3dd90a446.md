On the external instrument, compute a bullish signal separately on the daily and 4-hour timeframes. A timeframe is bullish when its MACD line is at least its signal line and its smoothed stochastic RSI is at least its signal line. Open long when both timeframe signals are false and long trading is enabled; open short when both are true and short trading is enabled. Mixed signals produce no entry. Close a short when both signals are false and long trading is disabled; close a long when both are true and short trading is disabled.
