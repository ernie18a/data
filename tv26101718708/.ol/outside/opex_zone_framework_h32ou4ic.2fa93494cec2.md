While the option-expiry levels are active, classify the instrument using its daily close: below the put floor, activate the short framework and target deep support; from the put floor up to but below the gamma flip, stand aside; from the gamma flip up to but below the pin, favor dip buying with a stop below the gamma flip; at or above the pin, use a long bias with the three call-resistance levels as successive targets. If the bar’s high exceeds the third call-resistance level, classify it as extended with no edge. Signal a bearish regime on a daily-close cross below the gamma flip, activate the short framework on a cross below the put floor, resume the bullish framework on a cross back above the gamma flip, and mark the bullish zone on a cross above the pin. Treat the levels as stale after the expiry Friday close.
