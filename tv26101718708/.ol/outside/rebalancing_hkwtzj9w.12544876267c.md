At the first scheduled rebalance after the start date, buy the traded instrument to reach its target portfolio allocation. On each later scheduled check, compare its current portfolio weight with the target: if the weight is below target by at least the configured deviation, buy toward target; if above by at least that deviation, sell toward target. In periodic mode, rebalance toward target at every scheduled check instead. Trade only when the required adjustment meets the exchange’s minimum trade value. No exit signal is defined.
