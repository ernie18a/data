Over a rolling window, compute realized variance from squared log returns and jump-robust bipower variation from adjacent absolute log returns. Standardize their difference as a jump-significance score. When the score exceeds its significance cutoff, take the direction of the largest-magnitude return in the window: go long if positive, short if negative. Enter only when the session filter passes, the bar is confirmed, the cooldown has elapsed, and the position is not already in that direction. No exit rule is defined.
