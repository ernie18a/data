During the active date window, compute rolling standardized deviations from their own rolling means for three external ratios: volatility index divided by volatility-of-volatility index; bond volatility index divided by the inverse of the equity volatility index; and oil price divided by the inverse of oil volatility. Also compute the traded instrument’s Bollinger %B and, when the EMA filter is enabled, its EMA. Use the enabled signal groups as follows: all three enabled requires all three deviations; two enabled requires both enabled deviations for short entry; one enabled uses that group’s deviation. Enter short when the selected deviations are positive and %B is below its midpoint; with the EMA filter enabled, also require the instrument below its EMA. Otherwise, enter long. Short entries are submitted only while currently long; long entries are submitted while flat or short. Close long when the relevant deviation or deviations are negative and %B is above its midpoint: use all three for the all-enabled case, the equity volatility deviation for the two-group cases containing it, and the relevant single deviation for the equity volatility-only or bond-volatility-only cases. Close short when the corresponding short condition is met, without the EMA filter. In the oil-only case, the script’s close conditions instead use the bond-volatility deviation.
