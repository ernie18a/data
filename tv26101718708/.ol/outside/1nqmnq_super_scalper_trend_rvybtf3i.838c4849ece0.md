Generate long and short entries from these setup families: (1) fade a failed break of the session’s opening-range or regular-session extreme when price sweeps beyond it and closes back inside on a clean, non-doji rejection candle; (2) enter a pullback rejection in the prevailing higher-timeframe direction, either at the directional retracement zone of the recent swing or on a retest of the short-term EMA; (3) enter a confirmed break and hold of a recent swing boundary or opening-range boundary, or its later retest with a confirming candle; (4) enter the first confirmed revisit of a valid, unmitigated order block; (5) fade a recent high or low sweep only when the wick, close location, relative volume, and range show rejection, with supporting order-flow divergence, a nearby higher-timeframe level, or repeated sweeps; (6) enter during designated opening or economic-release impulse windows when directional confidence is sufficient, with volume spike and candle direction strengthening or weakening that confidence; (7) enter a directional volatility-squeeze release while momentum remains aligned; (8) enter a rejection from a valid fair-value gap, or a retest of a violated gap in its inverse direction. For each setup, require its own confidence score to clear its configured floor, any setup-specific higher-timeframe conflict gate to pass, and a valid target and stop with sufficient reward distance. Confidence combines a direction score built from price trend, momentum, volume delta and aggressor flow, market internals, relative performance versus related instruments, volatility regime, higher-timeframe trend, and market structure, with setup-specific evidence such as candle confirmation, location near support or resistance, and liquidity. Set targets at the nearest relevant opposing structure or reference level, adjusted for nearby level clustering; place stops beyond the setup’s invalidation structure with volatility and wick buffers. Close each ticket when its target or stop is touched; stop checks begin after the entry bar.
