Estimate liquidation pressure as Binance futures volume minus Coinbase spot volume. Set that value to zero on an up candle of the traded instrument. Generate buy and sell signals from the configured method: crossing a threshold with the smoothed or fast-weighted value, crossing the threshold with the raw difference, or crossing the fast and slow weighted values; when multiple methods are enabled, the moving-average method takes precedence over the raw-difference method, which takes precedence over the smoothed method. Enter long on a buy signal only when the optional date filter passes and the traded instrument closes above its long-term weighted moving average. Enter short on a sell signal only when shorts are allowed and the optional date filter passes. Close an open position using a take-profit limit and stop-loss based on its average entry price.
