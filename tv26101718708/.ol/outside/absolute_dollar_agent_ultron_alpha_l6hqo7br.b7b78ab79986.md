On each confirmed bar, form separate bullish and bearish confluence counts from aligned macro and fast Hull slopes, trail direction, active CHoCH direction, proximity to its zone, ATR-normalized momentum, the regime gate, and confirming volume. A bullish setup qualifies when the trail is bullish, its count meets the configured minimum, the bullish regime gate passes, and the selected Fibonacci regime checks pass; apply the analogous bearish rules. Fibonacci checks may require selected lower- and/or higher-timeframe trend alignment, price on the matching side of the regime basis, and sufficient time since opposing flips. A strong directional count with matching trail direction can override Fibonacci blocks.

A confirmed trail flip or a new, direction-matched CHoCH can trigger a qualified setup. CHoCH zones are tradeable only if enabled quality checks pass: adequate ATR-relative width, required higher-timeframe alignment, and no excessive extension. CHoCH triggers also require sufficient zone confidence; trail-flip triggers do not. Enter only while flat, using the selected mode: on the qualified trigger, on a later retest of the flipped trail that closes back in the trade direction, or on a timely retrace into the matching CHoCH zone while the trail agrees and directional qualification still holds. For vanilla options, map bullish entries to calls and bearish entries to puts; choose strike placement from the selected strike policy, with the dynamic policy using directional score and regime-gate strength. 

Set the initial stop at the trail and profit targets at configured risk multiples. After the first target, move the stop to breakeven; after the second, protect the first target; after the third, manage the remaining position with the trail. Exit on a stop hit (by close or intrabar according to the selected rule) or a confirmed trail flip against the position. For tracked option positions, also exit on an opposing trail flip or opposing CHoCH. Optional option scale-ins require a fresh gated RSI momentum signal in the position direction and remaining scale-in capacity.
