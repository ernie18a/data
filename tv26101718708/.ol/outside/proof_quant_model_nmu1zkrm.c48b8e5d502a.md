Use an external reference instrument’s primary-timeframe price and long-term-timeframe price. When flat, skip entries if enabled filters flag excessive or insufficient volatility, or a price outside the Bollinger Bands. If volatility is at least the low-volatility threshold and the primary-timeframe short moving average is above both its long moving average and the long-term-timeframe moving average, go long. In the low-volatility regime, go long when price is more than one standard deviation below its moving average, or short when it is more than one standard deviation above it. Exit a trend long when price reaches the upper Bollinger Band, falls below its ATR-based trailing level, or reaches the maximum holding duration. Exit a trend short at the lower Bollinger Band, above its ATR-based trailing level, or at the maximum holding duration. Exit a mean-reversion long when price returns to its moving average or reaches the maximum holding duration; exit a mean-reversion short when price returns to its moving average or reaches that duration.
