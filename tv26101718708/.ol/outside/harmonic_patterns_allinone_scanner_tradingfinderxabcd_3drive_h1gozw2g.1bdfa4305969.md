On each closed bar, identify alternating confirmed swing pivots and evaluate a candidate completion point after the latest corrective swing. Emit a bullish signal when a downward completion forms the bullish geometry and the selected pattern’s ratios all fall within its ranges; emit a bearish signal for the mirrored upward completion. Use absolute price-leg ratios: AB/XA, BC/AB, CD/BC, and AD/XA. Accept these ranges: Gartley 0.58–0.63, 0.382–0.886, 1.13–1.618, 0.77–0.82; Butterfly 0.77–0.82, 0.382–0.886, 1.618–2.24, 1.27–1.41; Shark 0.10–0.90, 1.13–1.618, 1.618–2.24, 0.886–1.13; Bat 0.382–0.50, 0.382–0.886, 1.618–2.618, 0.86–0.899; Deep Crab 0.860–0.895, 0.382–0.886, 2.0–3.618, 1.58–1.63; Cypher 0.382–0.618, 1.13–1.414, 1.272–2.0, 0.756–0.796; Alternative Shark 0.382–0.618, 1.13–1.618, 1.618–2.24, 0.886–1.13; Alternate Bat 0.345–0.399, 0.382–0.886, 2.0–3.618, 1.00–1.20; Nen-Star 0.382–0.786, 1.13–1.272, 1.272–2.618, 1.13–1.414; Dragon 0.38–0.62, 0.80–1.10, 0.40–0.80, 0.20–0.40. For 5-0, require an extra earlier swing, AB/XA 1.13–1.618, BC/AB 1.618–2.24, and completion CD/BC 0.45–0.55. For ABCD, require BC/AB 0.382–0.886, CD/BC 1.13–2.618, and AB and CD lengths within 2.5% of each other. For 3 Drive, require three successive directional drives with each later drive 1.27–1.618 times the preceding drive and the corresponding three-drive swing geometry. The code’s Dragon classification uses the mirrored geometry opposite the other pattern labels: its bullish signal checks upward-completion geometry, and bearish checks downward-completion geometry. Optional structural validation rejects completions that extend beyond the pattern’s maximum stated XA or BC ratio. A signal is recorded at the completion bar’s close; separately, mark follow-through confirmation if a bullish close exceeds the prior bar’s high or a bearish close falls below the prior bar’s low, with candle body exceeding 60% of its range. No exit rule is defined.
