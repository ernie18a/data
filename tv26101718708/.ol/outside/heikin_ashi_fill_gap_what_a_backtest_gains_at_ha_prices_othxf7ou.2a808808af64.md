On each confirmed bar, classify the Heikin Ashi candle as green when its close is above its open, otherwise red. When its color differs from the previous confirmed bar, act at the next bar’s open: in long-only mode, a green flip opens a long and a red flip closes it; in long-and-short mode, a green flip closes any short and opens a long, while a red flip closes any long and opens a short. Do nothing before a prior candle establishes the color.
