Compute the growth-to-value fund price ratio, take its logarithm, and measure the current value’s deviation from a rolling linear trend in standard deviations. Use the configured lookback and threshold. When re-entry signals are enabled, signal value (long value, short growth) when the z-score crosses down below the positive threshold after being above it; signal growth (long growth, short value) when it crosses up above the negative threshold after being below it. When re-entry signals are disabled, signal value while the z-score is at or above the positive threshold, and growth while it is at or below the negative threshold.
