On the underlying, calculate a short-term exponential moving average and VWAP. When the average crosses above VWAP, exit any open put option and buy a call option one strike in the money from the nearest at-the-money strike, if its premium is available. When it crosses below VWAP, exit any open call option and buy the corresponding in-the-money put, if its premium is available. For each entry, set a profit target at the configured percentage above its entry premium; exit when the option’s high reaches that target. A crossover in the opposite direction exits the current position and initiates the opposite option position.
