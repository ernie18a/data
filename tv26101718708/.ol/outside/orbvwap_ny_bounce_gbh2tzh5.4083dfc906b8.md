At each session, record the opening-range high and low. After the range closes, a close above its high authorizes long entries for the session; a close below its low authorizes shorts. During the entry window, place a resting buy limit at the selected session-anchored VWAP after an upward break, only while price is above that VWAP; place a resting sell limit there after a downward break, only while price is below it. Cancel an unfilled order whenever its setup or any enabled gate ceases to qualify. Apply configured direction, session, weekday, month, trade-count, opening-range-size, and risk-size gates. Shorts may also be blocked by configured time, month, or weekday rules. Optional entry gates require the instrument's price-position score across VWAP and opening-range references to meet a directional threshold, and/or correlated instruments' scores to agree. On entry, set the stop using the selected fixed-distance, prior-day volatility, VWAP, opposite opening-range boundary, or recent swing rule; set the target using the selected risk multiple, opening-range extension, fixed distance, or no target. Move the stop to entry after the configured favorable risk move, and optionally trail it behind the best price after a favorable activation threshold, tightening only; the trail may replace the fixed target. Exit on stop or target, and optionally close any open position at the configured end-of-session time. Do not carry positions overnight.
