Build separate bullish and bearish confluence scores from higher-timeframe liquidity-sweep bias, session activity, volume expansion, a correlated market’s opposite-side liquidity sweep, relative strength versus that market, higher-timeframe fair-value gaps, proximity to relevant prior-day or open levels, volatility-index direction, repeated same-direction sweeps, and structure proximity. Add extra weight for an engulfing reversal at structure or an engulfing candle inside a recent fair-value gap; cap each score. A sweep below recent lows followed by a close back above them triggers a potential long, and a sweep above recent highs followed by a close back below them triggers a potential short. Favor the higher score when it clears the configured minimum and is sufficiently stronger than its opponent; allow a high score to qualify without that score gap. On a qualifying sweep, issue the setup only for the dominant direction; when the opposite direction dominates, flag a fade in that direction. Conflicting fair-value-gap and sweep evidence calls for waiting. Treat a confirmed close beyond the recent opposite swing after a sweep as break-of-structure confirmation. Also signal long or short on an engulfing reversal at aligned structure, an engulfing candle within a recent matching fair-value gap, or a range breakout with expanding volume. Invalidate an active long if price closes below the swept-low-based stop, and an active short if it closes above the swept-high-based stop; a new opposite sweep replaces the active direction. Attach a stop and projected targets to qualifying sweep setups.
