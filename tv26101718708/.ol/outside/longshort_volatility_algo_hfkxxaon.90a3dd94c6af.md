Enter short when any of these holds: the external equity-skew measure is unusually high; the fast average of the market-breadth oscillator crosses above or remains above its slow average; the market-breadth index rebounds from its lower range while the skew measure is rising; or the small-cap index is above its moving average while skew is rising. Enter long when either a periodic October calendar window occurs or all of these hold: volatility-futures roll yield is low, recent market breadth is weakening, the fast oscillator average crosses below or remains below its slow average, volatility-index or volatility-ETP readings are near the top of their recent ranges (with the volatility-ETP condition also requiring the fast average to be falling), skew is not unusually high, and the small-cap index is not above its moving average. Each entry reverses an opposing position; there are no separate exit rules.
