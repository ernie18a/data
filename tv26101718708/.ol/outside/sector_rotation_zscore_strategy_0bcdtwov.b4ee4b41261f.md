Compute the log price ratio of two reference assets, standardize it over a rolling window, and optionally smooth it. Go long the traded instrument when this signal crosses upward through a threshold below zero, provided the optional trend-strength and bullish pivot-position filters pass. A qualifying cross may remain armed until the bullish filter agrees within its configured window. Close the long when the signal crosses downward through a threshold above zero, when the optional trend-strength exit triggers, or when the optional bearish pivot-position exit triggers; in confirmation mode, wait for bearish pivot bias after the exit cross. Optionally go short when the signal crosses upward out of an extreme-low region, subject to corresponding trend-strength and bearish pivot filters and the same arming-window behavior. Close the short when the signal crosses upward through the long-entry threshold, when the optional trend-strength exit triggers, or when the optional bullish pivot-position exit triggers; in confirmation mode, wait for bullish pivot bias after that cross. Optional risk exits close either position at configured percentage profit or loss, and an enabled date window closes open positions when the window ends.
