At each confirmed weekly close, evaluate three external conditions: SPX is above its long-term moving average; short-term VIX is below three-month VIX; and the standardized HYG-to-IEF ratio is above its downside cutoff. At Monday’s open, target SPY exposure is full when all three conditions hold, half when exactly two hold, and zero when one or none hold. Buy or add shares to reach the target; sell or close shares to reduce exposure to it.
