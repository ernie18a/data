Long-only. Enter when the sector index’s prior daily close is above its prior daily EMA; the instrument touches or falls below its EMA minus a multiple of ATR, then closes up; its smoothed RSI is at or below an adaptive oversold threshold; and price has reset near its EMA since the previous entry. Block entry during a macro pause, when volatility index exceeds the buy ceiling, around the estimated quarterly earnings date (except after a small positive earnings surprise), or when the close is too far above the EMA. Require an open market session, confirmed bar, no existing position, adequate buying power, and sufficient expected movement relative to trading costs. Exit after a minimum holding period when smoothed RSI reaches its adaptive overbought threshold or price reaches the EMA plus an ATR-based extension. Also exit on the tighter protective level formed by the initial ATR-based (or fixed-percentage) stop and a ratcheting ATR trailing stop; after sufficient profit, raise the trail above entry.
