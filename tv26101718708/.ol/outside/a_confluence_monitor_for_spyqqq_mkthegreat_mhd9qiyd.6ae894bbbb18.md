During regular trading hours, evaluate bullish and bearish setups on the chart instrument. For each direction, award one point for each condition: price is on the corresponding side of VWAP; the fast and middle moving averages are ordered in that direction and price is beyond the fast average; RSI is beyond its directional threshold; MACD line is on the corresponding side of its signal and the histogram is strengthening in that direction; volume meets its average-volume requirement; price is on the corresponding side of the slow moving average; and price crosses beyond at least one directional level (recent swing high/low, premarket high/low, prior-day high/low, or opening-range high/low), with an ATR-based buffer. Signal a call setup when the bullish score reaches the configured minimum and, if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, higher-timeframe price versus VWAP, fast versus middle averages, RSI, and MACD all agree bullishly. Signal a put setup under the mirrored bearish conditions. Emit each setup only when its qualifying state first becomes true. The chop condition (price near VWAP, RSI near its midpoint, and fast and middle averages close together) produces a caution alert but does not suppress either setup. No exit decision is defined.
