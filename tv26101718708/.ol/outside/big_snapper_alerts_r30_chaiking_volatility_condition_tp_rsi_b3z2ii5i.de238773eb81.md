Track the latest Big Snapper directional signal as a pending setup, replacing it when an opposite signal appears. A signal can come from the configured Big Snapper trend filter or its unfiltered direction-change / fast-average crossover arrows. For a long, require rolling volume-weighted average price above the medium-term moving average; for a short, require it below. Also require Chaikin volatility rate of change above its lower cutoff, a current candle body larger than a volatility-scaled standard deviation in the trade direction, and the Super Oscillator at its corresponding extreme now or on the prior bar, with the opposite sign four bars earlier. Enter only when the candle closes in the signal direction; set a stop beyond that candle by a volatility-independent multiple of its full range. Exit a long if its stop is breached, or if RSI previously entered overbought territory and later falls below its exit threshold, or if the positive Super Oscillator/ adaptive-average exit condition is met. Exit a short on a stop breach, or if RSI previously entered oversold territory and later rises above its exit threshold, or if the negative Super Oscillator/adaptive-average exit condition is met. Clear the pending setup and exit state after closing.
