Compare the chart instrument with a reference instrument using a configurable price ratio or difference, then derive fast, normal, and slow oscillators from the spread’s normalized, smoothed changes. Signal bullish when the fast oscillator crosses above the normal oscillator while the normal oscillator is below zero; signal bearish when it crosses below while the normal oscillator is above zero. Also signal bullish or bearish when any oscillator crosses zero in the corresponding direction. Flag a bullish or bearish divergence when the corresponding fast/normal crossover occurs within a configured window of a prior same-direction crossover, the current normal-oscillator wave is smaller than the prior wave by a configured proportion, and a smoothed estimate of the emphasized oscillator is respectively above or below the slow oscillator. These are directional signals for the chart instrument relative to the reference; no exit rule is defined.
