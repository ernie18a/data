For silver and gold, compare the MCX price with its COMEX price converted to INR and adjusted for import duty. Signal long when the price difference crosses above a configured upside entry level; signal short when it crosses below a configured downside entry level; signal exit when it crosses the configured exit level in either direction. Also monitor the gold/silver, gold/platinum, and platinum/silver ratios: signal buy when a ratio crosses below its configured buy level, sell when it crosses above its configured sell level, and free/exit when it crosses its configured free level in either direction. Separately, alert when either metal’s COMEX-versus-MCX daily percentage-change divergence, or its duty-adjusted landed-price premium/discount percentage, remains continuously within a configured narrow band for a configured duration; these timer alerts do not specify a trade direction.
