During the opening-range period, track the high and low of a selected reference instrument, optionally using its Heikin Ashi candles. During the subsequent trading period, go long when the traded instrument’s close is above the reference range high and relative volume is above its baseline; go short when its close is below the range low and relative volume is above baseline. Do not open the opposite direction while a position-direction flag is active. At the designated session-end period, close all positions and reset the direction flags.
