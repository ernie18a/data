For each consecutive pair of higher-timeframe candles, compare the traded instrument’s highs and lows with those of enabled, distinct correlated instruments whose candles match the same time period. Signal bearish when the traded instrument makes a higher high but any reference does not, or a reference makes a higher high but the traded instrument does not. Signal bullish by the equivalent disagreement over whether a lower low was made. A pair may signal both directions. Ignore comparisons with missing or time-mismatched reference data.
