Use the USD-quoted and stablecoin-quoted prices of a reference instrument. Compute their difference as a fraction of the USD-quoted price, scaled by a constant. Enter long when this measure crosses above a configurable positive threshold, confirmed on the following bar, unless a bearish threshold cross occurs on that bar. Enter short when it crosses below a configurable negative threshold, confirmed on the following bar, unless a bullish threshold cross occurs on that bar. For each position, place side-specific percentage-based stop-loss and two take-profit orders; close a configurable portion at the first target and the remainder at the second. If enabled, move the remaining position’s stop to its entry price after the first target is reached.
