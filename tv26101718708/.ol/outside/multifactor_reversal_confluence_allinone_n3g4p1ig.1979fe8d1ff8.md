Generate reversal signals by combining five directional detectors: (1) a return-distribution shift points opposite the prevailing drift; (2) a detected mean or variance change points opposite that drift, unless the move is accelerating; (3) a strong but stretched price leg with declining short-term efficiency points against the leg; (4) an unusually high-volume, wide-range bar with rejection at the relevant extreme establishes a candidate, strengthened by a lower-volume retest that holds; and (5) a sweep beyond a confirmed swing pivot followed within a limited window by a displacement close back through the pivot confirms a turn. Arm when the required number of detectors exceed their respective thresholds in the same direction within the expiry window. Advance to extreme when the matching climax qualifies, and to confirmed when matching structure qualifies. Emit at the selected lifecycle stage, defaulting to confirmed, only when enabled regime and turbulence gates pass; these assess mean-reversion conditions and whether a volatility burst is intensifying. Also apply configured session, higher-timeframe, and lower-timeframe-flow filters. Optionally adjust conviction for detector redundancy, flow, benchmark direction, divergence, and price location; calibration and conviction do not determine direction.

In a trending regime, optionally generate continuation signals in the trend direction: require a pullback from a recent extreme, then a resumption trigger (trend-average recross, strong momentum close, or trend-direction flip, according to the selected method), with confirming lower-timeframe flow when required. Suppress continuation during a volatility burst when its gate fails. Apply the configured options-favorability filter to displayed or alerted signals when enabled. The indicator produces no exit signal; it reports a structural stop for confirmed reversals and a recent-extreme or volatility-based stop for continuation entries.
