Calculate the spread as the front contract’s price minus the back contract’s price, then standardize it against its rolling mean and standard deviation. Enter long when the standardized spread crosses upward through a negative entry threshold; enter short when it crosses downward through the corresponding positive threshold. Close a long when the standardized spread crosses downward through zero or falls below a more extreme negative stop threshold. Close a short when it crosses upward through zero or rises above a more extreme positive stop threshold. Mean exits and stop exits are independently configurable.
