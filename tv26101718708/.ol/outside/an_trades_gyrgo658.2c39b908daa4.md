Generate a long signal when a recent bullish liquidity sweep (price breaks below a confirmed swing low and closes back above it) coincides with a bullish inverse fair value gap forming or price entering an active bullish inverse gap. Require a bullish delivery-state shift if enabled: price closes above the open of the latest qualifying bearish candle. Require bullish divergence against a comparison instrument if enabled: the traded instrument makes a new lookback low while the comparison instrument does not. Block the signal when the downside swing-liquidity target is closer than the upside target, if that filter is enabled, or when the lower wick exceeds a configurable multiple of average true range, if that filter is enabled. Generate a short signal by reversing these conditions: a recent sweep above a confirmed swing high with a close back below, a bearish inverse gap forming or being entered, a required bearish delivery-state shift, any required bearish divergence (new high not matched by the comparison instrument), and no applicable opposing-liquidity or upper-wick block. The indicator defines no exit signal.
