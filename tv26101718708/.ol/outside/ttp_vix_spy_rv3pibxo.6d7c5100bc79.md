Use VIX data as the external signal. Compute its MACD histogram. Generate a long signal when the histogram is rising and either below zero (signal 1) or above zero (signal 2). Generate a short signal when it is falling and either above zero (signal 1) or below zero (signal 2). Require the applicable enabled filters: for longs, the traded instrument’s RSI average has a sufficiently positive slope, its Supertrend indicates a downtrend, its Bollinger Band width was wide recently, its price average has a negative slope, and—if enabled—VIX RSI is above its lower bound; for shorts, require a sufficiently negative RSI-average slope, an uptrend, recently wide band width, a positive price-average slope, and—if enabled—VIX RSI below its upper bound. If enabled, suppress signals outside weekdays. The filters are individually optional. The indicator defines no exit rule.
