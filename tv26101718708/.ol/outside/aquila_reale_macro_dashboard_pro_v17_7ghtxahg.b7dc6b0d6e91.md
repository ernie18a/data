For Gold, compute an equal-weight macro score by assigning each input +1, 0, or −1 when its daily or timeframe change is materially pro-Gold, neutral, or anti-Gold. Treat rising Treasury yields, dollar strength, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, equity breadth, Bitcoin, and usually rising equities as anti-Gold; treat rising inflation, VIX, silver, EUR/USD, and yuan stress as pro-Gold. Falling values reverse the direction. Equity declines count as pro-Gold only during elevated fear; ignore dollar moves during extreme fear. Oil’s direction is pro-Gold below the high-price regime and reverses above it. Add the daily-only inputs to each timeframe’s core score. Use the slow-timeframe score for the trade verdict. First classify the regime from oil level, Gold’s daily direction, and the 10-year yield: expensive oil with falling Gold vetoes longs and returns WAIT; expensive oil with rising Gold or cheaper oil with rising Gold and low yields permits a long; cheaper oil with rising Gold and high yields gives LONG CAUTIOUS; cheaper oil with falling Gold gives NO LONG. Otherwise, use the score: strongly positive means LONG STRONG, moderately positive LONG OK, moderately negative NO LONG, strongly negative STAY OUT / SHORT BIAS, and near-neutral WAIT. In permitted-long regimes, the score distinguishes strong from ordinary long. Separately, label multi-timeframe alignment long or short only when all three scores agree in direction; otherwise show conflict or neutral.
