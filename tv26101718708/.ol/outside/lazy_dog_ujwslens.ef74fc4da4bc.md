After the required cooldown, outside the daily blackout window, start a cycle by recording the underlying price, setting an upper and lower strike at a fixed offset from it, and opening short positions in both options. While active, if the underlying reaches or exceeds the upper strike, close the short call and open a larger long call position; if it reaches or falls below the lower strike, close the short put and open a larger long put position. Track each long repair option’s simulated premium peak and close that position if its premium falls sufficiently below its peak. Close all positions when open loss reaches the loss limit, or after open profit has reached its target and subsequently retreats by the configured trailing amount, or at the daily exit time. After closing, reset cycle state; start a fresh cycle only after the cooldown and outside the blackout window.
