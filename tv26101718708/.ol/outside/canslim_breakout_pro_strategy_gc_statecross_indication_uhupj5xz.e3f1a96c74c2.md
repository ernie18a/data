When flat, enter long if the instrument closes above the highest high of the preceding base lookback, remains within the allowed percentage above that pivot, and its volume meets the required multiple of its moving average. Also require each enabled external market or sector gate to pass: its shorter-term moving average must exceed its longer-term moving average; missing external data does not block entry. While long, exit if the close reaches the hard loss threshold below average entry, reaches the profit target above average entry, or—after reaching the trail activation gain—falls to the trailing threshold below the highest close since entry. No short-entry or time-based exit rule is implemented.
