Within the active trading period, optionally wait for price to cross a trigger level; an enabled upper or lower border can disable entries, pause trading, or close the whole position, and an enabled close-and-stop level closes the position. Open only in permitted directions. For each direction, enter when either selected entry signal fires and all selected directional filters pass; the two entry signals are OR-connected, while filters are AND-connected. Signals may be grid re-entry or counter-trend events, Bollinger Band reversals, fractal breakouts, reversal-fractal patterns, or pin bars. The grid reference moves to a new high or low after price travels the configured gap; re-entry follows that move and counter-trend signals oppose it. Filters can use trend, momentum, breakout, volatility, swing-pattern, or Ichimoku conditions, optionally sampled on higher timeframes. While a position is open, reduce or close it when its configured average-price profit or loss grid step occurs, subject to optional average-price profitability checks and event-count limits. Also close on any configured directional exit signal (the two exit signals are OR-connected), a break-even crossing after prior realized profit, or a repeated break-even crossing; exit signals may also suppress new entries depending on the selected mode.
