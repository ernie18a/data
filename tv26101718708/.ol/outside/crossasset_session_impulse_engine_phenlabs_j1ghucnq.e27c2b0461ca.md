During the chosen trading session, build a range from its opening bars and wait until the range is complete. A confirmed close above the range arms a long; a close below arms a short. Confirm the direction when at least the configured number of live correlated markets also close beyond their own completed opening ranges in the corresponding direction, reversing a market’s vote when configured as inverse. If no basket markets are live, use the chart break alone. When enabled and volume is available, require current volume to meet the configured relative-volume threshold. Issue at most one signal per session; after entry, exit or invalidate if a confirmed close crosses the opposite side of the chart’s opening range.
