Generate a long signal when the oscillator either crosses upward out of its oversold band or crosses above its smoothing average while below its midpoint, depending on the selected trigger mode. Generate a short signal when it crosses downward out of its overbought band or crosses below its smoothing average while above its midpoint. When enabled, require the oscillator to be on the matching side of its average and require the average to be on the matching side of the midpoint. When directional bias gating is enabled, allow longs only under bullish bias and shorts only under bearish bias; bias may be set manually or derived by locating an external series within its rolling range, classifying high positions as bullish, low positions as bearish, and intermediate positions as neutral. Neutral bias blocks both signals. The indicator defines no exit rule.
