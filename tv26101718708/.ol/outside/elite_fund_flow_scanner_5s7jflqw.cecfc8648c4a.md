Flag a bullish setup when at least four of these conditions hold: price and its shorter moving average are above the longer moving average; volume is elevated versus its recent average; momentum is positive and rising; the instrument has recently outperformed its benchmark; and the benchmark’s shorter moving average is above its longer moving average. Require price to be above but still near its longer moving average for the setup signal. Flag a bullish breakout when the same score threshold is met and price closes above the recent high with especially elevated volume. The indicator defines no short or exit signal.
