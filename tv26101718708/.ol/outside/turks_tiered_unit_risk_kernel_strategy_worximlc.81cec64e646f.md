On each confirmed bar, calculate a graded exposure score from how far price is above each of four configured moving averages, normalizing each distance by an ATR-scaled ramp, clipping each contribution between zero and full size, then averaging them. Round the score to the configured exposure grid and update the held grid level only when the raw score crosses its configured hysteresis threshold. Scale the result and add the configured minimum exposure floor to obtain the target long weight, capped at full exposure. Never open a short. In the default envelope-gated mode, estimate a causal Gaussian-smoothed price and its rolling mean absolute deviation. When price is at or above the smoother, allow the target weight to follow the graded score; below the smoother, prevent the target from falling below the prior held target, while still allowing it to increase. Buy or sell the instrument at the close to move the position to the target weight; close the position when the target reaches zero. No option decisions.
