Track the latest confirmed swing high and low. Set the market trend bullish when price closes above the latest swing high, and bearish when it closes below the latest swing low; retain the prior trend otherwise. Close a short when the trend turns bullish and close a long when it turns bearish. Enter long when the local fast EMA is at or above the slow EMA, the market trend is bullish, and RSI is below its overbought level. Enter short when the local fast EMA is below the slow EMA, the market trend is bearish, and RSI is above its oversold level. Close an opposing position before entering, and enter only when flat. After a stop-loss exit, block re-entry in that direction until the EMA-and-index trend color improves; clear the block on a new entry. Exit a losing long at the configured loss threshold only while the local EMA signal remains bullish; exit a losing short at that threshold only while it remains bearish. When profitable, reduce a long by half if the combined local-and-index EMA color changes away from fully bullish, or reduce a short by half if it changes away from fully bearish, provided the local EMA signal still agrees with the position. Higher-timeframe swing trend is visual guidance only.
