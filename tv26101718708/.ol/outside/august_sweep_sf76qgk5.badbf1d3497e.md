Track confirmed swing highs and lows. Signal a bearish sweep when price rises at least an ATR-scaled minimum above the latest swing high, the prior bar’s high did not exceed that level, and the current bar closes back below it; signal a bullish sweep under the inverse conditions at the latest swing low. After a sweep, retain the nearest recent opposite-side swing as the protected level. Within a limited post-sweep window, signal bearish confirmation if price closes below the protected low, or bullish confirmation if it closes above the protected high. Cancel a pending setup if price closes back through the swept level or the window expires; consume it after confirmation. For each scanned instrument and timeframe, report the direction of the more recent sweep only while it remains within a freshness window; report no direction if neither qualifies or their ages tie. These are directional signals; the indicator defines no order execution or separate exit rule.
