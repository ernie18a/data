Compare the instrument’s closes on two configurable timeframes by calculating their relative difference, then apply a configurable transformation. Go long when this transformed difference crosses above a positive threshold and a normalized price-derived oscillator is above zero. Go short when it crosses below the corresponding negative threshold and the oscillator is below zero. On a new long or short signal, close any opposing position and enter in the signal direction. Exit after a configurable number of bars in the position; optionally use a trailing stop based on the entry bar’s high for longs or low for shorts. Apply trades only within the configured date window.
