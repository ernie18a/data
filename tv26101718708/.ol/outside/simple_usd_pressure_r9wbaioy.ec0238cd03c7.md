Compare each enabled USD major pair’s current close with its earlier close over the configured lookback. A rise in EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, or NZDUSD votes for USD weakness; a rise in USDJPY, USDCHF, or USDCAD votes for USD strength. Normalize the net pair votes by the number of enabled pairs. Optionally add confirmation votes: rising DXY or US10Y yields supports USD strength, while rising gold or SPX supports USD weakness. Normalize the net confirmation votes by enabled confirmations, then combine the pair score and confirmation score, giving the pair score greater weight; if no confirmations are enabled, use the pair score alone. Smooth the resulting score. Above the configured positive trend threshold, take a long-USD bias; below its negative, take a short-USD bias; between them, exit or take no directional position. Reverse the bias when applying it to pairs quoted in USD.
