During the enabled date window, calculate the selected moving average of open and close on an alternate timeframe. Enter long when the close-based average crosses above the open-based average plus an ATR buffer; enter short when it crosses below the open-based average minus the buffer. A direction setting can allow longs, shorts, both, or neither. In long-only mode, a short signal closes the long; in short-only mode, a long signal closes the short. When enabled, a trailing stop for each open position follows the close-derived stop level, ratcheting only in the direction that protects more profit, and exits the position when hit.
