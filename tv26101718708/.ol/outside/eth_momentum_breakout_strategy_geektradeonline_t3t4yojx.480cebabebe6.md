When flat and the cooldown after the previous exit has elapsed, enter long if enabled and all of these hold: a volatility squeeze, defined by Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channels, lasted long enough and released recently; the close breaks above the upper Bollinger Band by an ATR-based margin; the candle body is sufficiently large relative to its range; momentum, measured by the linear regression of price minus its moving average, is positive and rising; volume exceeds its moving average by a spike threshold; RSI is below its overbought ceiling; ATR relative to price exceeds a minimum; the higher-timeframe trend is bullish, with price above its EMA by an ATR buffer and the EMA rising; and the daily regime filters pass, including bounded distance from the long-term EMA and ATR relative to its historical average. Enter short on the symmetric conditions: a recent valid squeeze release, close below the lower band by the ATR margin, sufficiently large candle body, negative and falling momentum, volume spike, RSI above its oversold floor, minimum volatility, bearish higher-timeframe trend with price below its buffered EMA and the EMA falling, and passing daily regime filters. Apply direction, session, and fundamental-data filters only when enabled; when enabled, fundamentals require active addresses and large transactions above their averages, plus bullish sentiment for longs or bearish sentiment for shorts. Optional ADX filtering applies only when enabled. At entry, classify volatility and trend strength as hot, normal, or cold and lock that classification for the trade; scale profit targets, stop loss, and trailing distances wider in hot conditions and tighter in cold conditions. For either direction, set an initial ATR-based stop and profit targets; if partial profit-taking is enabled, close a portion at the nearer target and manage the remainder toward the farther target. Ratchet a trailing stop only in the favorable direction, tighten it after partial profit, and, in runner mode after partial profit, also trail against recent market structure and remove the final target. After a sufficient favorable move, move the stop beyond entry to protect profit. Exit the remaining position on its stop, target, optional failed-breakout reversal condition, stagnation without sufficient favorable progress, or maximum holding duration. After exit, wait through the cooldown before considering another entry; optionally shorten that wait after a trade that reached partial profit.
