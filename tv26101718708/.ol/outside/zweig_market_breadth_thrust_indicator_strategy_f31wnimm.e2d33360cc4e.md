Compute a moving average of the selected exchange’s advancing issues divided by advancing plus declining issues (or use the average advance and decline counts across the supported exchanges in combined mode). Enter long when this breadth ratio crosses upward through a configurable lower threshold. While long and the instrument is above its average entry price, partially close when either the breadth ratio crosses downward through an upper threshold or a short-period RSI crosses downward through its configured high level, according to the selected exit mode. Close the entire position when the breadth ratio crosses downward through the lower threshold while the instrument is above its average entry price. Also close if price falls below the configured percentage stop level; when trailing-stop mode is enabled, use the trailing stop instead. No short entries.
