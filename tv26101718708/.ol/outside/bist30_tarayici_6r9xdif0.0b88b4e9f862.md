For each watched instrument, classify an uptrend when its fast, medium, and slow simple moving averages are strictly ordered from highest to lowest and price closes above the slow average. In an uptrend, mark a pullback zone when the bar’s low reaches the medium average plus a configurable volatility-based allowance, while its close remains no lower than the slow average minus that allowance. Signal long only when that zone coincides with an enabled bullish reversal or continuation candle pattern: a three-bar morning star (bearish first candle, smaller middle body, then bullish close above the first candle’s midpoint); bullish engulfing (a bullish candle whose body covers the prior bearish body); bullish inside bar (a bullish candle contained within the prior bearish candle’s high-low range); hammer-like pin bar (lower wick substantially longer than the body and a small upper wick); or doji (body small relative to the full range). If enabled, also require volume above its moving average. Confirm on bar close when configured. Optionally suppress the long signal unless the projected reward-to-risk ratio to the first target meets a configured minimum and the stop distance meets a configured minimum. Set the protective stop below the lower of the signal bar’s low and the slow average, offset by a volatility-based amount. Calculate ascending profit targets from the prior completed higher-timeframe range and pivot using Fibonacci range multiples; use these as staged profit-taking levels. A return to neutral after the uptrend ends is a warning to close or stop out; the indicator defines no short entry.
