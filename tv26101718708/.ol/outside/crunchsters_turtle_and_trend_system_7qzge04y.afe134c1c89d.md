Within a user-selected trading date range, run either or both selectable systems and directions. Trend system: calculate fast and slower EMAs from prior closes, with the slower period a fixed multiple of the faster; normalize their spread by recent price-change volatility. Enter long when this normalized spread crosses above a positive threshold, and short when it crosses below the corresponding negative threshold. Exit a long when the spread crosses back below its positive entry threshold or when price reaches an adverse trailing-range threshold; exit a short on the converse conditions. Breakout system: compare the current close with the midpoint and width of the prior rolling high-low range, and normalize its displacement by that width. Enter long when the normalized displacement exceeds a positive threshold and short when it falls below the corresponding negative threshold. Exit positions when price reaches an adverse trailing-range threshold. For either system, also place a protective stop from entry based on a multiple of recent average true range. Size positions inversely to recent annualized return volatility, scaled to a chosen volatility target and capped by a chosen leverage limit.
