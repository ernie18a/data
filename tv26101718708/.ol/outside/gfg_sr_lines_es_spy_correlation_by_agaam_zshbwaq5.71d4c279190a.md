Enter long on the first bar where all conditions hold: instrument price is above a rising EMA; price is near that EMA; smoothed stochastic lines are in the bullish pullback zone and cross upward; MACD histogram is rising; the candle closes up; ADX meets the minimum trend threshold; volume exceeds its average times a multiplier; and both ES and SPY are above rising EMAs. In stricter correlation mode, also require each market’s smoothed stochastic lines to be bullish. Enter short on the mirrored conditions: price below a falling EMA and near it, stochastic lines in the bearish pullback zone crossing downward, falling MACD histogram, down-closing candle, sufficient ADX and volume, and both ES and SPY below falling EMAs; stricter mode also requires bearish stochastic momentum in both. Apply enabled volume, ADX, and correlation filters. Set a long stop below the recent swing low with an ATR buffer, or a short stop above the recent swing high with an ATR buffer. Exit a long if its stop is touched or, when enabled, stochastic crosses downward in the overbought region; exit a short if its stop is touched or stochastic crosses upward in the oversold region. A new opposite entry signal replaces the active direction.
