Score the instrument by counting which conditions hold: price above its long-term daily average; medium-term average above long-term average; long-term average rising; price above its weekly long-term average; normalized performance stronger than a benchmark; momentum within a healthy, non-overheated range; and recent average volume higher than its earlier level. A long-entry signal occurs when price retests the daily long-term average from above, the score meets the configured qualification threshold, and (if enabled) retest volume exceeds its average by the configured factor; suppress repeat signals for several bars after a retest. Flag a weakening condition when the score falls below the qualification threshold, but this is a warning, not an exit instruction. No short or exit decision is defined.
