After a qualifying run makes a fresh rolling swing low, consider a long only if the move into the low swept a prior swing low and left a clean, adequately sized fair-value gap in the discount half of that move. Require a strong displacement candle or a sufficiently large gap, then a candle close that inverts the gap upward within the allowed window. The gap must also lie in the discount half of the applicable higher-timeframe dealing range, with untaken upside liquidity at least twice the entry-to-swept-low risk away. Reverse these conditions for a short: fresh swing high, swept prior swing high, gap in premium, downward inversion, premium-side higher-timeframe range, and sufficient untaken downside liquidity. Do not enter on formation; enter when price returns to the inverted gap. Place the stop beyond the swept extreme and target the qualifying liquidity. Exit or invalidate if price closes back through the gap or takes the swept extreme before reaching the target. Displacement confirmation and correlated-market divergence may help prioritize setups but are not required.
