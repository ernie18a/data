Go long when the instrument crosses above a macro reference built from the relative performance of oil and gold, adjusted inversely for the dollar, or bullish volume absorption occurs; require price above its long-term exponential trend average and money flow above its buy threshold. Go short when price crosses below the macro reference or bearish volume absorption occurs; require price below the trend average and money flow below its sell threshold. Bullish absorption means estimated buying volume exceeds a volume-based threshold and the close is above the candle midpoint; bearish absorption uses estimated selling volume and a close below the midpoint. No explicit exit decision is defined.
