Calculate each selected external asset’s percentage price change over a configurable lookback, average the changes, and smooth the result. Transform it using the selected oscillator method (rate of change, centered RSI, centered stochastic, centered Williams %R, or scaled CCI). In benchmark mode, apply the same calculation to the equal-weight market reference; in relative mode, subtract its oscillator from the selected-assets oscillator. Signal bullish when the active oscillator crosses above its configurable upper boundary and bearish when it crosses below its configurable lower boundary. In relative mode, signal outperformance or underperformance when the relative oscillator crosses its respective configurable boundary. These are directional signals; no exit rule is defined.
