For each of three correlated assets, retain its last directional bias. Set it bullish when its previous candle either swept below the candle before it and closed back above that candle’s low, or closed above that candle’s high. Set it bearish when it swept above the earlier high and closed back below it, or closed below the earlier low. If both directions qualify, bullish takes precedence; otherwise retain the prior bias. Optionally require each asset’s bias to match on the selected timeframe and the next higher timeframe, treating disagreement as neutral. Derive the group direction from the charted asset alone, a majority, or unanimous agreement, according to the selected consensus rule. A bullish or bearish group direction indicates directional bias for the traded instrument; neutral indicates no directional bias. The indicator provides no entry, exit, or options-trading rules.
