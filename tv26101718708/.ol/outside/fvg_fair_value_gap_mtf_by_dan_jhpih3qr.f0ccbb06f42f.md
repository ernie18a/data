On each monitored instrument and timeframe, identify the latest fair value gap from four consecutive candles: bullish when the low two bars ago is above the high four bars ago, bearish when the high two bars ago is below the low four bars ago. Track that gap until price mitigates it: a bullish gap is filled when any subsequent low falls below its upper boundary; a bearish gap is filled when any subsequent high rises above its lower boundary. Report gap creation and mitigation states and optionally alert on them. No long, short, exit, or options trading decisions are specified.
