Compute the instrument’s daily expected move from its daily open and the relevant external volatility index, scaled by annualized trading days. This defines upper and lower daily volatility walls. Enter long when price crosses above the selected moving average, volume exceeds its recent average by a configured multiplier, the trading window is active, trend strength exceeds its configured minimum, price is above the higher-timeframe trend average, momentum is below its long-side exhaustion limit, and price has sufficient room below the upper wall. Enter short on the corresponding downward moving-average cross when volume and session conditions pass, trend strength is sufficient, price is below the higher-timeframe trend average, momentum is above its short-side exhaustion limit, and price has sufficient room above the lower wall. Attach a profit target and a stop loss to either position; use an ATR-based stop capped by a hard maximum when enabled, otherwise use the fixed stop. When the time filter is enabled, close all positions outside the trading window.
