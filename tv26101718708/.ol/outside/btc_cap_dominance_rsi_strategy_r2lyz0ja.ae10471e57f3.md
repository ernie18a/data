Calculate Bitcoin market capitalization as total crypto market capitalization minus altcoin market capitalization, and Bitcoin dominance as Bitcoin market capitalization divided by total crypto market capitalization. Compute the RSI of each series; use their average when dominance data is enabled, otherwise use only the Bitcoin market capitalization RSI. Enter long when this signal crosses above the bull threshold, and enter short when it crosses below the bear threshold. An opposite entry reverses the position; no separate exit condition is defined.
