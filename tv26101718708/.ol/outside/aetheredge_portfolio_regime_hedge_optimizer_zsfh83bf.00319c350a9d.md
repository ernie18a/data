For the main instrument and up to two related instruments, estimate rolling correlations, relative performance, the main instrument’s drawdown from its recent high, and its volatility regime. Feed these features into an adaptive model that scores hedge, diversify, and hold actions, learning from subsequent returns: reward hedging when the main instrument falls and penalize it when it rises; reward diversification when correlations are low and an alternate instrument outperforms; reward holding when the main instrument holds up. Recommend a hedge when it scores highest, its normalized score dominance clears a confidence gate, and the main instrument is below its recent high; the hedge may be implemented by taking an offsetting position in a related instrument or options. Recommend diversification when it scores highest, average correlation is below the decoupling boundary, and the selected action has changed. Otherwise hold.
