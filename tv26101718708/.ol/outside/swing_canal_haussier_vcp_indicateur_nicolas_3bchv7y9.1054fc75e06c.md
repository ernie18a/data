Enter long when the capped quality score meets its configured minimum and all required gates pass: the broad market is above its long-term moving average; recent pivot-based lower and upper channel lines are both rising and sufficiently parallel, with the lower line below the upper; price is close to the lower channel boundary by a percentage or volatility-based distance test; RSI is within a middle range; volume is below its longer-term average and declining; volatility is below its average and declining; relative strength versus a benchmark is above its average and that average is rising; and the current candle closes above its open and the previous candle’s high. The score awards weighted credit for a bullish market, valid channel, lower-channel proximity, middle-range RSI, weak or declining volume, contracting volatility and bar ranges, and strong relative strength. If the optional earnings filter is enabled, also require the next earnings date to be sufficiently far away or already past. The indicator defines no short or exit decision.
