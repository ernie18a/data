Use the higher-timeframe slow EMA as a directional filter: consider longs only when price is above it and shorts only when price is below it. For mean reversion, after price touches or pierces the slow lower band, enter long at the next candle’s open after a bullish reversal candle; target the slow EMA and place a stop below the recent swing low. After price touches or pierces the slow upper band, enter short at the next candle’s open after a bearish reversal candle; target the slow EMA and place a stop above the recent swing high. For breakouts, enter long at the close of a candle that closes above the slow upper band; exit on a close back inside the fast upper band or trail using swing highs, with a stop below the fast upper band. Enter short at the close of a candle that closes below the slow lower band; exit on a close back inside the fast lower band or trail using swing lows, with a stop above the fast lower band.
