On each confirmed bar, evaluate bullish and bearish setups using three routes. For reversal, detect price sweeping below a prior daily liquidity low or lower expected-move band and closing back above it, or sweeping above a prior daily liquidity high or upper band and closing back below it. Arm the direction and require a confirming signal within a short window; invalidate it if price breaches the sweep extreme by a volatility-based buffer. Require a minimum confluence score from sweep significance, daily trend alignment, swing or internal structure break, reclaim of the daily open or intraday VWAP, strong relative volume, directional candle displacement, expanding realized volatility, and an applicable volatility-index condition. Countertrend setups need a higher score and may require a major or multiple-level sweep.

For continuation, require price to break a recent multi-bar high in a rising trend above the daily fast average, or break a recent low in a falling trend below it. Confirm with a structure break, sufficient score from trend, swing structure, displacement, volume, volatility expansion, and volatility-index context, and reject entries too extended from the daily average.

For transition, first require a reclaim across the daily fast average, then within a limited window require a compatible regime, a recent structure break, adequate confluence from regime, structure, displacement, volume, and volatility expansion, and acceptable distance from the average. Qualify the setup and allow execution for a short follow-up window.

Enter long on the first eligible bullish route signal and short on the first eligible bearish route signal, prioritizing reversal over transition over continuation; do not open the opposing side on the same bar. Enter only when flat, outside the post-exit cooldown, and when the route-specific structural stop gives positive risk within a daily-volatility risk cap. Place the initial stop beyond the sweep extreme, breakout structure, or transition structure as appropriate. While holding, exit at the stop; on a confirmed opposing swing-structure break beyond a buffer; or, after the minimum holding period, at an optional fixed risk-multiple target or an activated trailing stop based on favorable extreme and volatility, tightened by confirmed swing structure when available. No option purchase or sale logic is specified beyond using CALL/PUT labels to represent the directional entries and exits.
