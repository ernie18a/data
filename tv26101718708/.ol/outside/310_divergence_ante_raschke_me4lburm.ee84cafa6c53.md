Calculate a fast momentum line as the difference between short- and longer-period simple moving averages of price, and a slower signal line as a moving average of that momentum. Confirm price swing pivots before evaluating signals. Signal bullish divergence when two confirmed lows are within the configured spacing limits, the later price low is no higher than the earlier low while momentum makes a higher low, the earlier low is below the lower volatility channel, and momentum remains below its signal line throughout the interval. Signal bearish divergence by the mirror conditions: later price high is no lower, momentum makes a lower high, the earlier high is above the upper channel, and momentum remains above its signal line throughout. Add a higher-conviction bullish signal when the same bullish divergence coincides with the market breadth index making a higher low; add a higher-conviction bearish signal when the same bearish divergence coincides with breadth making a lower high. For trend-following longs, require a recent momentum high, momentum above its signal line, a rising signal line, a shallow pullback toward that line that stays on its positive side, and momentum turning upward from a local low. For shorts, require the mirror: a recent momentum low, momentum below its signal line, a falling signal line, a shallow pullback toward it that stays on its negative side, and momentum turning downward from a local high. These are directional setup signals; the divergence setups are countertrend and the continuation setups are with-trend.
