Score each asset by combining its relative rank versus the other two assets on each return horizon and on acceleration with RSI quality, EMA trend quality, and volume confirmation. RSI quality rises from weak momentum toward a healthy upper range and declines at extreme readings. Trend quality is strongest when price is above the fast EMA and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA; volume confirms positive returns and penalizes negative returns. Optionally subtract an exhaustion penalty when RSI or the price’s percentage extension above the slow EMA is excessive. Select the highest-scoring asset, breaking ties in favor of BTC, then ETH, then SOL. If enabled, choose cash instead when all three prices are below their slow EMAs and the top score is below a cash threshold. Infer the current holding from the chart symbol when set to automatic, otherwise use the selected holding; treat cash as having a score of zero. Signal a rotation only when the destination differs from the holding, the asset leader meets a minimum score, and its score advantage over the holding meets a minimum edge. For a cash destination, qualify using the cash regime instead. Require the configured cooldown since the previous signal and, when enabled, a confirmed candle close.
