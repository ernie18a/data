Include crude oil only when enabled, using a configurable direct or inverse relationship to the index. Issue long bias when the 10-year yield supports long, the combined score meets the configured positive minimum, and any enabled 2-year yield agreement and index-versus-EMA confirmation pass. Issue short bias under the corresponding inverse conditions and negative score minimum. Otherwise remain neutral or wait; a change to neutral ends the prior bias.
