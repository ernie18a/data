Use an external broad-market index and its advance/decline breadth data to form a market bias. Add a bullish vote when the index is above each of three moving averages, when the shorter averages are above the longer ones, and when the cumulative advance/decline line is above its moving average; add bearish votes for the opposite conditions. Sum and normalize the votes, then smooth the result. A positive result signals an uptrend and long bias for the traded instrument; a negative result signals a downtrend and short bias. No exit rule is specified.
