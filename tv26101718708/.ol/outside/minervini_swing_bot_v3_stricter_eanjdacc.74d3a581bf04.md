Go long when the instrument meets a momentum trend template: price is above its medium- and long-term moving averages, those averages are aligned upward, the longest-term average has been rising, and price is sufficiently above its trailing-year low and near its trailing-year high. Require relative performance versus a broad-market benchmark to be above its own average and improving, with the benchmark itself above its medium-term average. Enter only on a close above the recent swing-high pivot by a buffer, with volume expanded versus its average. Also require volatility and bar ranges to be contracting and recent volume to have dried up before the breakout, while price remains within a reasonable distance of its medium-term average. The script defines no short or exit decision.
