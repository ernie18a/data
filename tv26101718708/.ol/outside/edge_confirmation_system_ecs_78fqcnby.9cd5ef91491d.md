Go long when price is above the long-term EMA, the faster trend EMA is above the slower one, and a normalized fast-versus-slow EMA momentum measure is positive and above its smoothed signal, unless momentum is overbought. If volume confirmation is enabled, also require volume to exceed its moving average by a threshold and the candle to close up. Go short on the mirrored conditions: price below the long-term EMA, faster trend EMA below the slower one, normalized momentum negative and below its smoothed signal, not oversold, and—when enabled—a qualifying volume surge on a down candle. Emit a signal only when it differs from the most recent directional signal. No exit rule is defined.
