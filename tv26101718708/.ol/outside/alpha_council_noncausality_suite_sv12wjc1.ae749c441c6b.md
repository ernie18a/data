Block setups during adverse acceleration or cascade conditions; require a directional change-of-character close, deceleration, and any enabled time-exhaustion checks to pass. Then signal short or long only when a bearish or bullish candle, respectively, coincides with an enabled external-data trigger: premium index crossing down from an unusually high reading for short or up from an unusually low reading for long; open interest showing an unusually sharp decline for either direction; or stablecoin-dominance data indicating the relevant directional shift. External triggers may be evaluated as direct retail-style crossings or as a consensus of current changes versus prior volume-anomaly observations. There are no explicit exit rules.
