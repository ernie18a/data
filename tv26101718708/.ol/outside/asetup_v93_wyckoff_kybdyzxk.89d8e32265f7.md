Generate a call-entry signal when bullish conditions score fully, or meet the lower qualifying score tier, and a bullish candle pattern is present, the trend filter agrees, the time filter permits entry, and the direction's cooldown has elapsed. Bullish conditions are an upward fast-to-slow moving-average stack, price above a rising VWAP, bullish higher-timeframe moving-average alignment, RSI in its bullish range, bullish MACD momentum, and qualified expanding volume. Bullish patterns include bullish engulfing, hammer, upside breakout, support bounce, or a confirmed high-volume Wyckoff spring. Generate put-entry signals by applying the corresponding bearish conditions and patterns: downward moving-average stack, price below a falling VWAP, bearish higher-timeframe alignment, RSI in its bearish range, bearish MACD momentum, qualified volume, and bearish engulfing, shooting star, downside breakdown, resistance rejection, or confirmed high-volume upthrust. The trend filter requires agreement from at least two of the moving-average stack, price-versus-VWAP position, and higher-timeframe alignment. Exclude blocked open, lunch, and late-afternoon periods from ordinary entries. In the end-of-day MOC window, allow a call or put with a qualifying directional score and pattern, trend agreement, and qualified volume, provided price has room before the prior day's high for calls or prior day's low for puts; MOC signals do not duplicate ordinary signals, and have their own cooldown. For an open call, exit if price reaches the second risk-based target, falls to the active stop, bearish moving-average alignment and below-VWAP price coincide with a bearish pattern, or the time stop expires. For an open put, exit on the mirrored target, stop, bullish reversal, or time-stop conditions. On reaching the first risk-based target, move the stop to entry. Initially set stops at an ATR-based distance from entry (or a nearby structural fallback); set targets at risk-based multiples. Entry signals are suppressed while a position of that direction is already open.
