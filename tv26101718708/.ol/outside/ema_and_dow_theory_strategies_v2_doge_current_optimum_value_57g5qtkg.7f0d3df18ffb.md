Enter long when the instrument’s fast EMA is at or above its slow EMA, the swing-structure trend is bullish, and RSI is below its overbought threshold. Enter short when the fast EMA is below its slow EMA, the swing-structure trend is bearish, and RSI is above its oversold threshold. Close an opposing position when the swing-structure trend reverses; the EMA signal also closes an opposing position before a new entry. Close a position when its unrealized loss exceeds the configured stop threshold, provided the EMA signal has not turned against that position. When profitable, partially close a long if its trend color weakens from fully bullish, or a short if it weakens from fully bearish, provided the EMA signal has not turned against that position. If take profit is enabled, set three targets at successive ATR-based distances from entry and close a portion at each target when price reaches it. The external index affects trend color and thus the weakening exit, but does not filter entries.
