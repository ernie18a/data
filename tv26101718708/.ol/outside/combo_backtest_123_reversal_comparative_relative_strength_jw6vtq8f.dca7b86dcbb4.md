Maintain two directional signals. The reversal signal turns long after two consecutive higher closes when the fast stochastic is below its slower average but above its midpoint; it turns short after two consecutive lower closes when the fast stochastic is above its slower average but below its midpoint. Otherwise, retain its prior direction. The relative-strength signal compares a smoothed ratio of the traded instrument’s close to a benchmark’s close: turn long above an upper threshold, short below a lower threshold, and exit an existing position when the ratio crosses its corresponding exit threshold; otherwise retain its prior direction. Hold long only when both signals are long, short only when both are short, and exit when they disagree or either is neutral. An optional reversal setting swaps long and short.
