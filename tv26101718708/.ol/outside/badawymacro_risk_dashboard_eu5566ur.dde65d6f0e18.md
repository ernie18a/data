Use external volatility, dollar strength, Treasury yield, growth-equity, and credit-versus-bond data. Treat lower volatility, a weaker dollar, lower yields, growth equities above their moving average, and credit outperforming bonds as positive; treat the opposite states as negative. Combine available signals into a normalized macro score, weighting liquidity signals most heavily and volatility, growth, and credit less. Classify the result as risk-on above an upper cutoff, risk-off below a lower cutoff, and neutral between them. Use risk-on as a long-bias filter and risk-off as a short/hedge or exposure-reduction filter; neutral favors reduced conviction. This is a regime filter, not a direct entry or exit rule.
