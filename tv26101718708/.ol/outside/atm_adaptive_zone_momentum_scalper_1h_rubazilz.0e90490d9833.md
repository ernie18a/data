Create demand and supply zones from detected doji, rejection, strong momentum, and low-momentum cluster candles. Merge nearby same-side zones, and ignore zones invalidated by a close beyond their far boundary. When flat, go long if price tests an active demand zone within an ATR-scaled buffer and closes bullish; go short if it tests supply within the buffer and closes bearish. Optional breakout signals require three consecutive rising closes, a bullish close above the prior high for long, or three falling closes, a bearish close below the prior low for short. An entry is placed only when the same bar also provides the corresponding demand or supply zone boundary. Exit a long at a stop below the tested demand zone or at the next supply zone above price, falling back to an ATR-based target; exit a short at a stop above the tested supply zone or at the next demand zone below price, with the same ATR-based fallback.
