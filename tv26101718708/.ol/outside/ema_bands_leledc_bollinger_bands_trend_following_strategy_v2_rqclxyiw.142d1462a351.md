Compute a central EMA from closing prices and upper/lower trend bands from EMAs of highs/lows. Optionally compute a slower trend EMA, extended buy/sell zones, Bollinger Bands with RSI, Leledc exhaustion signals, and a volatility squeeze from Bollinger/Keltner channel containment.

When flat, enter long when price crosses above the upper trend band, or enter short when it crosses below the lower trend band. A crossing includes either an open-to-close move through the band or a close moving across it while the candle remains on the new side. Optionally require the close to be on the matching side of the slower EMA. Optional bounce re-entries require the candle to open and close beyond the relevant trend band while its wick touches back through that band. A selected squeeze filter blocks these breakout and bounce entries while a squeeze is active.

Optional countertrend entries are allowed only while flat and outside an active squeeze. Enter long on a bullish Leledc exhaustion signal whose low extends below the selected inner or outer buy zone, or on a bullish Bollinger exhaustion signal. Enter short on a bearish Leledc signal whose high extends above the selected sell zone, or on a bearish Bollinger exhaustion signal. A Leledc signal marks a reversal candle after a run of closes in one direction when it establishes a new extreme over a lookback; an optional volume condition requires elevated volume. A bullish Bollinger exhaustion signal occurs when price previously closed below the lower band and returns above it while RSI is oversold; the bearish version returns below the upper band while RSI is overbought. The selected zone-crossing requirement applies to Leledc countertrend entries; Bollinger countertrend entries use the candle's open beyond the corresponding selected zone. These opposing exhaustion signals may also open a countertrend position immediately after closing a basic position when their corresponding countertrend direction is enabled.

For a basic long, close on a downward crossing of the upper trend band (nearest-band mode) or lower trend band (opposite-band mode). For a basic short, close on an upward crossing of the lower band (nearest-band mode) or upper band (opposite-band mode). In delayed-inside mode, close longs on a downward crossing of the lower band and shorts on an upward crossing of the upper band; also close after price crosses back inside the bands and remains inside without a new breakout for the selected delay. Independently enabled early exits close a long on a selected occurrence of bearish Leledc exhaustion, bearish Bollinger exhaustion, or a downward failure back through the selected sell zone; close a short on the corresponding bullish signal or upward failure through the selected buy zone. The chosen Leledc or Bollinger occurrence is counted since the position opened. When an enabled opposing countertrend trigger coincides with such an exhaustion exit, enter that countertrend position if price is beyond the selected zone.

Countertrend positions close on a configurable opposing price crossing of a trend band or buy/sell zone, a touch of a selected target band or zone, or the first opposing Leledc/Bollinger exhaustion signal. Apply an optional fixed adverse price stop measured from entry. Optional default positioning enters long or short whenever flat, with an optional slower-EMA direction filter; it is enabled for at most one direction at a time.
