Enter long when a volatility-normalized difference between fast and slow weighted moving averages crosses upward through its lower one-standard-deviation band, and all long filters pass: higher-timeframe price and moving averages are bullishly ordered; RSI is below its configured overbought bound; MACD histogram is nondecreasing; money flow is not materially negative; volume exceeds its baseline; the halving-cycle measure is not strongly bearish; stablecoin dominance is bullish or not bearish; BTC dominance favors the instrument (rising for BTC, falling for other assets); and futures basis does not indicate crowded longs. Enter short on a downward cross through the upper one-standard-deviation band when the filters are bearish: higher-timeframe price and moving averages are bearishly ordered; RSI is above its configured oversold bound; MACD histogram is nonincreasing; money flow is not materially positive; volume exceeds its baseline; the halving-cycle measure is not strongly bullish; stablecoin dominance is bearish or not bullish; BTC dominance does not favor the instrument; and stablecoin dominance is not near its recent high while falling. Close a long if the oscillator crosses below zero, if RSI crosses down through its overbought bound while higher-timeframe structure is bearish, or if a short entry triggers. Close a short if the oscillator crosses above zero, if RSI crosses up through its oversold bound while higher-timeframe structure is bullish, or if a long entry triggers. Otherwise wait. An urgent entry is the corresponding valid entry with high aggregate directional agreement, a strong trend, elevated volume, and no extreme volatility fear for longs. The indicator also gives a non-entry directional forecast when its composite projection is sufficiently above or below current price at near and medium horizons and directional confidence is adequate.
