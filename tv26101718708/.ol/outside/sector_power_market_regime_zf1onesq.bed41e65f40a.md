For each asset, classify normalized momentum as positive or negative and normalized volatility disorder as positive or negative: positive momentum with negative disorder is Efficient; positive with positive disorder is Explosive; negative with positive disorder is Chaotic; negative with negative disorder is Weak. Go long a target when the benchmark is Stable/Weak and the target is Efficient with stronger momentum than the benchmark. Exit a held position if it shifts from Efficient to Chaotic, or remains Explosive for an extended period. No short-entry rule is specified.
