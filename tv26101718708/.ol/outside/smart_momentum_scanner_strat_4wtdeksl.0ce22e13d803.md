Long-only strategy. Track confirmed swing lows as support. After price tests support within a tolerance, wait for a bullish candle to close above the prior candle’s high. Then wait for a confirmed swing low above support, followed by a close above the reversal candle’s high. Enter long only if volume exceeds its recent average by a required margin and either (a) price is above the near-term trend average, the faster average is above it, and it is rising, or (b) price is above the long-term average, the faster averages are aligned upward, and the long-term average is rising. Require price above VWAP when that filter is enabled; when enabled, also require the selected broad-market and sector instruments to be above their respective near-term averages. Invalidate the setup if price closes below support. Size the position using account risk and stop distance, subject to a maximum allocation. While long, place a stop below support with an volatility-based buffer, capped by a maximum volatility distance; use a volatility-based stop if structure stops are disabled. Exit on a stop fill, or, when enabled, a close below the near-term average, a close below trade support, a volatility-based profit target, or the maximum holding period.
