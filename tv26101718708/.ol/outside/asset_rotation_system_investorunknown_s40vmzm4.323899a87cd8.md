For each of six assets, compare its price against each other asset and against USD. On every comparison, mark the numerator asset as outperforming when the selected trend test is bullish: RSI above 50, CCI above zero, SuperTrend bullish, positive directional movement greater than negative, or price above Parabolic SAR. If using the combined method, treat the comparison as bullish when any of those tests is bullish. Give each asset one point for each of the five other assets and USD it outperforms; give USD one point for each asset that is bearish against USD. Hold the asset with the highest score, switching when another asset becomes the leader. If USD has the highest score, hold cash. Resolve ties by choosing the first item in the fixed order of the six assets followed by USD. This is a long-or-cash rotation rule; it does not open short positions.
