Calculate fast and slow triple exponential moving averages (TEMA) of closing prices on both the chart timeframe and a configurable higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Within the configured backtest date window, enter long when chart-timeframe fast TEMA is above slow TEMA and higher-timeframe fast TEMA is above slow TEMA; close the long when chart-timeframe fast TEMA falls below slow TEMA. Enter short when chart-timeframe fast TEMA is below slow TEMA and higher-timeframe fast TEMA is below slow TEMA; close the short when chart-timeframe fast TEMA rises above slow TEMA. Long and short trading can each be enabled or disabled independently. Short exits are not restricted by the date window.
