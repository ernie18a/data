On the traded instrument, calculate RSI and the close’s standardized deviation from its recent moving average. Signal to buy a call when RSI is oversold and the deviation is sufficiently negative, provided the instrument is above its intraday trend average and a daily market reference is above its long-term average; signal to buy a put when RSI is overbought and the deviation is sufficiently positive, provided the instrument is below its intraday trend average and the daily reference is below its long-term average. Each trend filter can be disabled. Enforce a separate cooldown between repeated signals on each side. For a call, set a limit entry below the signal close; for a put, set it above. Consider the setup filled when the instrument’s low reaches the call entry or its high reaches the put entry. After a fill, exit at the configured profit target or stop level; also discard the setup after its maximum holding period.
