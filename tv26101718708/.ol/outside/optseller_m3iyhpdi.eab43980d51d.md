During the permitted trading session, while below the daily trade limit, calculate an upper and lower price level by offsetting the selected price center (VWAP, or a short-term moving average) by a volatility measure (ATR), then rounding outward to option strikes. When price reaches or exceeds the upper level and the RSI is above its oversold threshold (if that filter is enabled), sell a call at that level. When price reaches or falls below the lower level and RSI is below its overbought threshold (if enabled), sell a put at that level. The trend filter adds no effective restriction because each signal already requires the corresponding level to be touched. For a sold call, exit if price rises to its percentage-based stop or falls to its percentage-based target; for a sold put, exit if price falls to its stop or rises to its target. Close any remaining positions at the end of the session. Reset the trade count each day and limit entries to the configured daily maximum.
