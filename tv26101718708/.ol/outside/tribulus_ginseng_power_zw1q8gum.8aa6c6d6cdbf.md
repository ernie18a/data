For the chart instrument, total crypto market capitalization, and Bitcoin dominance, independently detect bullish and bearish structural confirmations: a breakout of the latest confirmed swing range paired, in either order within a limited recent window, with a break of the corresponding pivot-based trend boundary. Also detect hybrid confirmations by pairing, within a limited recent window, a direction-specific event from a volatility-triggered phase, kernel-envelope deviation, and ATR trailing-stop state. Optional liquidity-sweep and trend-strength checks gate these events. Invert each engine’s paired hybrid-and-structure directions: the inverted bullish signal is that engine’s long-direction event, and the inverted bearish signal its short-direction event. On the chart instrument, optionally suppress longs after recent oversold RSI, falling negative MACD momentum, or a large bearish candle; suppress shorts after recent overbought RSI, rising positive MACD momentum, or a large bullish candle. A chart long event occurs when its inverted bearish event fires and is not suppressed; a chart short event occurs when its inverted bullish event fires and is not suppressed. Also allow an early long when bullish-direction events from both macro engines are active within the recent alignment window, at the first bar of that alignment, while no chart long event fires; apply the symmetric rule for early shorts. On a long event, if not already long, close any short and enter long. On a short event, if not already short, close any long and enter short. Otherwise retain the current position; there is no rule that exits to flat.
