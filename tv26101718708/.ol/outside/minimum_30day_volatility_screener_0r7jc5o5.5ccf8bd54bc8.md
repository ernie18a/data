Set a bullish state when the fast moving average is above the medium moving average and price is above both longer moving averages. Set a sell state when the fast moving average is below the medium moving average and price is below the medium moving average; otherwise clear a prior bullish state to neutral. A sell state persists until the bullish condition returns. For instrument choice, recommend shares or a high-delta call for higher-priced assets when ATR as a share of price is below its cap; recommend shares and avoid options when it exceeds the cap. For lower-priced assets, recommend buying shares on margin; for the intermediate price tier, advise checking the spread.
