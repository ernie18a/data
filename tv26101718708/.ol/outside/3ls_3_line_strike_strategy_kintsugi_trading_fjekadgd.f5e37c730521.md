Enter long when the latest candle is bullish and its body is larger than the preceding bearish candle’s body, following three consecutive bullish candles; current volume exceeds its moving average multiplied by a threshold; and the fast simple moving average is above the slow one. Enter short on the mirror pattern: a larger bearish candle after three consecutive bearish candles, qualifying volume, and the fast average below the slow one. Only enter while flat, on a confirmed bar, and within the configured session if the optional session filter is enabled. For longs, set the initial stop below the signal candle’s low with an offset, or below entry by an ATR-based distance; for shorts, use the signal candle’s high with an offset, or above entry by an ATR-based distance. In fixed-stop and ATR trail-stop modes, set a profit target at the configured risk-to-reward multiple of the initial risk. In ATR trail mode, use no profit target. While a position is open in either ATR mode, trail its stop by an ATR-based distance, moving it only in the favorable direction. Exit when the stop or applicable profit target is reached.
