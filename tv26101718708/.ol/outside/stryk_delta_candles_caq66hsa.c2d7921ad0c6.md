Estimate volume delta by summing lower-timeframe volume as positive when an intrabar closes up and negative when it closes down; resolve ties by comparing its close with the prior close. Evaluate signals only after each higher-timeframe candle completes. Signal short when delta changes from positive to negative while price closes flat or up, or when price closes up and delta is negative. Signal long when delta changes from negative to positive while price closes flat or down, or when price closes down and delta is positive. Also signal short when unusually large positive delta coincides with flat price, and long when unusually large negative delta coincides with flat price; judge unusually large against the recent average absolute delta. Use a price-movement tolerance to define flat. The logic specifies no exit condition.
