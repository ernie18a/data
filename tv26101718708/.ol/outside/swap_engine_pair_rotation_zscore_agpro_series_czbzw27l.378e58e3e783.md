On each confirmed evaluation bar, compare the two assets’ closing prices by computing the log of their price ratio and its rolling z-score. When the absolute z-score reaches the stretch threshold for the required consecutive bars, signal rotation only if pair integrity passes: return correlation, ratio-mean drift, and spread-volatility expansion must yield an integrity score above its minimum, and the assets must be distinct with compatible quotes. If the ratio is positive, Asset A is relatively rich: rotate from A into B; if negative, rotate from B into A. A deeper stretch threshold marks a stronger stretch event. When the absolute z-score returns inside the reversion band after previously being outside it, mark the pair as reverted and end or reduce the rotation. This indicator emits analytical signals; it does not execute orders or specify position sizes.
