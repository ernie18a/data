On a confirmed bar, enter long when the current and previous bars both close above their opens and each has volume above the chosen cutoff; price is above VWAP, Parabolic SAR, Supertrend, and volume-weighted moving average; RSI is above 50 and at most 80; any enabled EMA filter favors long; and the bar is within the allowed date and session. Enter short when both bars close below their opens with the same volume condition; price is below those four indicators; RSI is below 40 and at least 20; any enabled EMA filter favors short; and date and session filters pass. The strategy also allows a long when Supertrend and the volume-weighted moving average both cross above VWAP together, with two bullish high-volume bars and RSI in the long range; the corresponding simultaneous downward crosses, two bearish high-volume bars, and short-range RSI allow a short. For the two-bar entries, set the stop at the previous bar's low for longs or high for shorts. Exit at that stop or at a profit target set to the configured risk-to-reward multiple of the entry-to-stop risk. These signals correspond to long futures or buying calls, and short futures or buying puts.
