Calculate RSI on five configured timeframes and combine them into a weighted average. Treat RSI at or below the oversold threshold as a buy bias and RSI at or above the overbought threshold as a sell bias. A stronger long setup occurs when the combined RSI reaches its extreme low, forms bullish divergence against price, and the fastest and slowest timeframe RSIs show strong opposing extremes. A stronger short setup occurs when the combined RSI reaches its extreme high, forms bearish divergence against price, and the fastest and slowest timeframe RSIs show strong opposing extremes. Divergence means price makes a lower low while RSI makes a higher low for bullish divergence, or price makes a higher high while RSI makes a lower high for bearish divergence. The indicator specifies no exit rule.
