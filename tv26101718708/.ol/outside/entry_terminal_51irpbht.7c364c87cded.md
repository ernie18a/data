On each confirmed bar, detect breaks of the latest confirmed swing high or low. A break opposite the prior structure direction establishes a bullish or bearish regime; a break in the same direction is continuation. In that regime, wait for a confirmed rejection pivot whose wick meets configured size filters, then require price to move clear of the rejection zone without closing through its invalidation edge and retest the zone. After that retest, require a same-direction structure continuation break and fair-value gap to occur within the configured association window. Mark the associated gap as final; if price invalidates it before retesting, resume waiting for another qualifying break-gap pair. Otherwise, on a later confirmed bar that retests the final gap, signal long for the bullish regime or short for the bearish regime. Signals are entries only; the logic specifies no trade exit.
