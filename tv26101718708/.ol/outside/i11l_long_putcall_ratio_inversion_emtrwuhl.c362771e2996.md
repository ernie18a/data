Use the daily close of an external put/call ratio, smoothed with a moving average. Enter long when the smoothed ratio crosses down through its upper band, defined as its moving average plus a multiple of its rolling standard deviation. Exit a short position when the ratio is above its moving average. Exit a long if the traded instrument’s low falls below its average entry price by a configured percentage; exit a short if its high rises above average entry price by that percentage. Exit when the traded instrument’s high exceeds average entry price by the configured percentage stop distance multiplied by a configured reward multiple. No short entry is defined.
