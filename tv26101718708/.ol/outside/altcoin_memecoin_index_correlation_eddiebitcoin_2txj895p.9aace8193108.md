Use the traded asset’s close and a selected external crypto-sector dominance series (either altcoin or memecoin market capitalization divided by total crypto capitalization); the external series may be disabled. Compute fast and slow exponential moving averages for the asset and, when enabled, the external series. Enter or reverse long when the asset’s fast average is above its slow average and, if enabled, the external series’ fast average is above its slow average. Enter or reverse short when both comparisons are below. Take no new positions outside an enabled trading date window or when equity is at or below one-thirtieth of initial capital. Close all positions outside the trading window. Close a position when its unrealized percentage loss exceeds the configured stop threshold only if the opposite-direction entry condition is not currently true; record that stopped direction as blocked from re-entry until its trend-alignment state improves. Close all positions when unrealized profit exceeds the configured take-profit threshold. Optionally scale out once per position while profitable when the trend state weakens: for a long, the previously fully bullish asset-and-index alignment ceases; for a short, the previously fully bearish alignment ceases. Do not scale out if the opposite-direction entry condition is true.
