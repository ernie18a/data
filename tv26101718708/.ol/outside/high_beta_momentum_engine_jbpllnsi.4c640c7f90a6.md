Generate a buy signal when price is above a rising faster EMA, the faster EMA is above the slower EMA, RSI is above its bullish threshold and rising, the instrument’s recent return exceeds the benchmark’s, volume exceeds its average by the required relative amount, ATR exceeds its average, and price closes above the prior lookback-period high. Generate a sell signal when the inverse bearish conditions align: price is below a falling faster EMA, the faster EMA is below the slower EMA, RSI is below its bearish threshold and falling, the instrument underperforms the benchmark, volume and ATR meet the same expansion conditions, and price closes below the prior lookback-period low. Allow a signal only if the required cooldown since the previous buy or sell signal has elapsed.
