When flat, go long if the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average and RSI is above its midpoint. Set a percentage-based stop below entry. Exit the long if price falls below that stop, or if the slow average is above the fast average while MACD is below its signal and the smoothed stochastic is above its upper threshold. If price reaches twice the initial profit distance while ADX is falling, exit part of the position and reset the stop and profit limit around the current price. If short trading is enabled, after a long exit enter short when the stochastic reading from six bars earlier was above its upper threshold. Set a percentage-based stop above short entry. If price reaches twice the initial profit distance while ADX is falling, exit part of the short and reset the stop and profit limit around the current price.
