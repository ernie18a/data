Use completed daily highs to set a weekday long bias: on Monday, if the prior Friday’s high exceeded Thursday’s high, mark the day bullish; on Thursday, if Wednesday’s high exceeded Monday’s high, mark the day bullish. Otherwise remain neutral. On a bullish day, the stated trade approaches are to go long on a break and hold above the reference high (Friday’s or Wednesday’s), or take long trades aligned with the bias and look for a close above the day’s open. The setup is counted as a win if price reaches the reference high or closes above the day’s open. No short or exit rule is specified.
