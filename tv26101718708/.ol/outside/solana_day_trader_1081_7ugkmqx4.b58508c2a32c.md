Long-only entries are evaluated independently on the 15-minute, 30-minute, and 1-hour charts. By default, only 15-minute signals may create entries; higher-timeframe signals can be enabled. A signal requires the capitulation trigger to be armed: a volatility-washout reading that was extreme recently and has begun to ease, alongside a short-term rebound and evidence of a prior decline. This is the only default hard entry gate. A recent lower Bollinger-band close can demote a qualifying tier by one level. An optional trend-quality dead-zone filter can block signals.

Build a confluence score from bullish short-term EMA alignment and price above an adaptive moving average, each confirmed within a short window after the capitulation trigger; rising volume balance; positive signed-volume pressure confirmed within its own window; a recent bullish three-bar price gap; trend quality above its qualifying level; and relative volatility in the high band. Subtract a configurable penalty for the low volatility band. Each enabled condition contributes its configured weight. A score meeting the base floor produces a BASE long signal; meeting the higher floor produces SCALE, or FULL when directional strength is below its configured ceiling. An optional setting can suppress SCALE signals. An optional Bitcoin-above-its-moving-average requirement downgrades FULL to SCALE when Bitcoin is not bullish. No short-entry rule is present.

While an entry is active, monitor all enabled evaluation timeframes for exit signals: a close at or below the lower Bollinger band, a volatility-top reversal, or a momentum-exhaustion crossing. A first distinct exit type produces a partial-exit warning; two distinct exit types within the escalation window produce a full exit. Any new exit signal produces a full exit when nearby overhead sell-side liquidity is detected. An optional trend-quality collapse can also trigger a full exit. Apply an entry cooldown before recognizing exits. After a full exit, watch for a fresh qualifying long setup within a limited window and emit a re-entry signal.
