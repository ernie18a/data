For each monitored currency pair, evaluate the most recently closed four-hour candle. Classify it as a bullish or bearish signal candle if it is an outside bar, engulfs the preceding opposite-color candle with a larger body, or forms a similarly sized opposite-color railroad pair with aligned opens and closes. Reject signals whose range touches at least two Bollinger Band lines, or whose close fails to retrace at least the configured fraction of its range in the signal direction. For a continuation buy, require a bullish signal and a close above the Bollinger middle band by a configured buffer; for a continuation sell, require a bearish signal and a close below it by that buffer. Otherwise, a reversal buy requires a bullish signal, a close below the middle band, and contact with the lower outer band on the signal candle or within the configured recent lookback. A reversal sell requires a bearish signal, a close above the middle band, and recent contact with the upper outer band. Report the matching buy or sell setup; otherwise report no setup. If multiple categories match, prioritize continuation over reversal.
