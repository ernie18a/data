On a daily bar, arm a short setup when the instrument opens below its prior close by at least a configured gap threshold, closes below its open by at least a configured amount, meets a minimum-price filter, and the optional broad-volatility gate passes (volatility index at or below its configured ceiling; unavailable readings do not block the setup). Record that bar’s low as the breakdown level and watch it for a configured number of following sessions; a newer qualifying setup replaces the prior level. During that period, if a later bar’s low reaches or falls below the level, enter short at the break and cover at that session’s close. If the level is not broken before its watch period expires, discard it.
