Choose reversal or trend-following mode. Calculate a stochastic index of the selected trader group’s net COT position over a lookback, and a smoothed Stochastic Momentum Index (SMI). A bullish trigger occurs when the SMI crosses up through its oversold level; a bearish trigger occurs when it crosses down through its overbought level. In reversal mode, with the COT filter enabled, go long on a bullish trigger only when the COT index is below its lower extreme threshold, and go short on a bearish trigger only when it is above its upper extreme threshold. Without the filter, use the SMI triggers alone. In trend-following mode, with the filter enabled, require the COT index to be above its midpoint for longs or below it for shorts; without the filter, use the SMI triggers alone. In reversal mode, allow at most one signal of each direction until the COT index retreats out of that direction’s extreme zone. Entries can be disabled by direction. Exit a long when the SMI crosses down through its overbought level, and exit a short when it crosses up through its oversold level, if signal exits are enabled. Optionally place take-profit and stop-loss orders at either volatility-based or fixed-percentage distances from entry. If opposite-signal exits are disabled, an opposite entry can close and reverse the position.
