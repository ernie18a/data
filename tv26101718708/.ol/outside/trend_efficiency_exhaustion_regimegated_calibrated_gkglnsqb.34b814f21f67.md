On each confirmed bar, consider an exhaustion reversal when the current price leg is unusually large relative to recent completed legs and short-horizon efficiency is falling. Require a trend regime, a variance ratio consistent with trending, a mean-reversion half-life short enough for the evaluation horizon, no implied-volatility spike, and adequate price-efficiency divergence quality. After an up leg, signal short; after a down leg, signal long. Consider a continuation signal when short-horizon efficiency crosses upward through its ignition threshold while the current leg is still young and the regime is not chaotic; signal in the leg’s direction. Apply a per-signal cooldown. The script defines no exit rule.
