Trade long only. While within the configured trading window, enter when flat, price is not below the slow trend average, both external stress series have been calm for the required consecutive bars, and flush recovery conditions are met. Exit an open long if price falls below the slow trend average, either external series signals stress for the required consecutive bars, or a sharp one- or two-period decline or unusually large bearish range triggers a flush. After a recent flush, allow re-entry only when price is above a recovery average and volatility has been calm for the required consecutive bars.
