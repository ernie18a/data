Track confirmed swing highs and lows, ignoring swings smaller than a configurable ATR-based minimum. For a long signal, price must sweep below a confirmed swing low after the prior close was at or above it, then close back above that low within the next five bars. For a short signal, mirror this at a confirmed swing high: price sweeps above it after the prior close was at or below it, then closes back below within five bars. After reclaim, signal long when price reaches the 0.618-to-1.0 retracement zone between the swept low and its paired swing high; signal short on the mirrored retracement between the swept high and its paired swing low. The signal must occur within a configurable post-sweep window, before the setup is invalidated by a close beyond the swept extreme, and after the configured cooldown. Optional filters require a bullish candle with lower-wick rejection for longs or a bearish candle with upper-wick rejection for shorts, and require longs above the long-term moving average or shorts below it. Classify reclaim on the first bar after the sweep as fast and reclaim on a later eligible bar as gradual. The indicator issues long and short entry signals; it has no exit logic.
