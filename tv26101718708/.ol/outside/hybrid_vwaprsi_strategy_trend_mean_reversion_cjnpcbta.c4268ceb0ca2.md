Use the external market’s daily trend, defined by its close relative to its 200-day moving average, as a regime filter. In an uptrend, go long when the traded instrument is below its session-anchored VWAP, its RSI is oversold, and its ATR relative to price indicates elevated volatility. In a downtrend, go short when it is above VWAP, its RSI is overbought, and volatility is elevated. Exit either position with an ATR-based trailing stop, wider in an uptrend than in a downtrend.
