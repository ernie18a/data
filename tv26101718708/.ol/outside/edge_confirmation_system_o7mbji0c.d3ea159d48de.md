Buy calls when the instrument closes above its long-term trend average, its fast trend average is above its slower trend average, normalized momentum is positive and above its smoothed signal but not overbought, and volume is surging on an up candle. Buy puts when the inverse trend conditions hold, normalized momentum is negative and below its smoothed signal but not oversold, and volume is surging on a down candle. Emit a signal only when it differs from the most recent signal. No exit rule is defined.
