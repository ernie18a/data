When the optional macro overlay is enabled, rising values in the US dollar index, US 10-year yield, or VIX each increase the composite bias toward a long in USD/ZAR; falling values decrease it toward a short. These macro changes adjust a score that also uses the traded pair’s own price indicators. A sufficiently high composite score signals bullish USD/ZAR bias, a sufficiently low score signals bearish USD/ZAR bias, and intermediate scores are neutral. This is a directional research signal, not an explicit entry or exit rule.
