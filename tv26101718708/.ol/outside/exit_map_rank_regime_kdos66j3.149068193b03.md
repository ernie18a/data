Measure whether recent relative rank predicts subsequent relative returns across the comparison universe. When the relationship is reliably negative, treat top-ranked instruments as extension warnings and bottom-ranked instruments as relative rebound candidates. When reliably positive, favor top-ranked instruments as leaders and avoid or short bottom-ranked laggards. If the relationship is unreliable or data is insufficient, make no directional decision. The stop/target simulation reports hypothetical outcomes and does not itself decide whether to enter or exit.
