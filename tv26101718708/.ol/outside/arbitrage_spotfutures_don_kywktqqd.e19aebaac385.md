Compute the futures premium as (futures price − spot price) / spot price. Set the long-entry threshold to the greater of the configured minimum spread and the recent mean spread plus a volatility-based margin; set the short-entry threshold to the lesser of the negative minimum spread and the recent mean spread minus that margin. If adaptive thresholds are disabled or unavailable, use the configured minimum spread and its negative. When no position is open, or the prior position has exceeded its maximum holding duration, enter long spot and short futures if the spread reaches the long threshold; enter short spot and long futures if it reaches the short threshold. Close all legs when the position reaches its maximum holding duration or the spread crosses the exit threshold, which is a fixed fraction of the applicable entry threshold. The exit direction check uses whether the average entry price is positive; for positive-priced instruments, this selects the long-side exit condition.
