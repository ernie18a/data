Use external 10-year and 2-year government yields, the dollar index, and volatility index. Score one bullish point when the yield spread is steepening beyond its positive threshold, the dollar is falling beyond its negative momentum threshold, or smoothed volatility is below its calm threshold. Score one bearish point for the opposite conditions: spread flattening, dollar rising, or volatility above its elevated threshold. Sum the signals: positive totals favor long positions in risk assets; negative totals favor short positions in risk assets (or long positions in safe havens); a zero total is neutral, so wait or reduce exposure. The bias is strong when at least two signals agree and weak when only one signal fires.
