Use the volatility index’s change as a directional signal for the DAX. From 08:00 to 09:00, a fall greater than 0.30 points signals bullish bias (long); a rise greater than 0.30 signals bearish bias (short); otherwise wait for confirmation. From 09:00 onward, classify the index change: below −1.50 = strong bullish, −1.50 to −0.70 = bullish trend, −0.70 to −0.35 = bullish bias; above +1.50 = strong bearish, +0.70 to +1.50 = bearish trend, +0.35 to +0.70 = bearish bias; between −0.35 and +0.35 = range, with no directional trade. Do not trade during the first 15 minutes after the cash open. No exit rule is specified.
