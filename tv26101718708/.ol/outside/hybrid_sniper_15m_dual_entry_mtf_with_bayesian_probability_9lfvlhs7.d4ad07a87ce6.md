On the 15-minute chart, allow entries only during the configured New York session and when the 2-hour and 4-hour trends agree: bullish when each timeframe closes above its 20-period EMA and above its open; bearish when each closes below its EMA and below its open. Enter long on either (1) a sweep below the previous 2-hour low that closes back above it with above-average volume, or (2) a bullish candle closing above both VWAP and the 1-hour 20-period EMA with relative volume above its configured threshold. Enter short on either (1) a sweep above the previous 2-hour high that closes back below it with above-average volume, or (2) a bearish candle closing below both VWAP and the 1-hour 20-period EMA with relative volume above its configured threshold. Only open a new position when flat. For a long, place the stop below the lower of the signal bar low and previous 2-hour low, with an ATR buffer, and set the profit target at the configured risk multiple. For a short, place the stop above the higher of the signal bar high and previous 2-hour high, with an ATR buffer, and set the target at the configured risk multiple. Exit when price reaches either stop or target.
