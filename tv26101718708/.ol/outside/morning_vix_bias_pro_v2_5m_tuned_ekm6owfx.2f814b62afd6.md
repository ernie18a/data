Use a smoothed composite of normalized changes in VIX and front-month VIX futures, plus normalized SPX absolute price movement, as a regime filter. When the composite is positive, allow long SPX setups; when negative, do not take long SPX setups. Mark volatility exhaustion when VIX is unusually elevated, its recent rise is decelerating, and SPX realized-volatility acceleration is below its recent average. After that exhaustion signal, a shift from negative to positive bias is a re-entry condition for long SVXY.
