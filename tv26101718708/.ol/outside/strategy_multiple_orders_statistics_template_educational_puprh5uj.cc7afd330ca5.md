On an allowed date, if long entries are enabled and a bullish candle closes above both the close and open from two bars earlier, while the previous candle’s low and high are each below those from two bars earlier, open a long in the next available trade slot. For that slot, set a stop below the signal candle’s low and a profit target above its high using the slot’s configurable percentage offsets. If the corresponding bearish condition holds—a bearish candle closes below both values from two bars earlier, and the previous candle’s low and high are each above those from two bars earlier—open a short in the next available short slot, with a stop above the signal candle’s high and a target below its low using that slot’s configurable offsets. Respect the enabled long/short direction. Close each slot independently when price reaches its stop or target; reuse the slot after it closes.
