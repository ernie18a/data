On a confirmed bar, enter long when the fast moving average crosses above the slow moving average, RSI and trend strength exceed their thresholds, either stochastic measure is below its ceiling, the close is above the prior close, and true range is below its limit; only enter when flat. Set an initial stop and profit target from entry price. While long, close if price falls below the stop, or if the slow average is above the fast average while MACD is below its signal, smoothed RSI is declining fast enough, and stochastic is above its oversold threshold. When external volatility index data exceeds its threshold, close only if price is below entry and falling versus the prior close; otherwise move the stop to entry. If price reaches twice the initial profit distance and is below its close from two bars earlier, place a partial exit with a tightened stop and a new profit target. No short-entry logic is implemented.
