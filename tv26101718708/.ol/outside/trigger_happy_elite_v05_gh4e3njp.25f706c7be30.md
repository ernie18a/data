When flat, enter long on a confirmed pivot low or short on a confirmed pivot high, only during the permitted session and while daily profit and loss limits remain unmet. Require volatility above its minimum; for longs require RSI below its oversold threshold and, if enabled, price above the selected timeframe’s moving average; for shorts require RSI above its overbought threshold and, if enabled, price below that average. If enabled, require the daily volatility index to remain within its allowed range for either direction. Exit an open position at its take-profit or stop-loss level, or when its trailing stop is triggered.
