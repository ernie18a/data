Enter long when the broad-market benchmark is above its long-term moving average and volatility is below its cutoff, if either a shorter moving average crosses above a longer one or price closes above a selected moving average for three consecutive bars after the prior bar closed below it. Exit all positions if volatility exceeds its cutoff or the shorter moving average crosses below the longer one. For an open long, place an initial stop below entry; after price reaches the first profit threshold, move the stop below the close that triggered it; after reaching a higher profit threshold, trail the stop below the highest high since entry. Keep the stop from moving lower.
