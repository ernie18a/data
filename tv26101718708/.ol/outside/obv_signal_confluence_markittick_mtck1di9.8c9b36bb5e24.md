On each confirmed bar, calculate cumulative volume flow by adding volume when the selected price source rises, subtracting it when it falls, and adding zero when unchanged. Compare this flow with a configurable moving average. Go long when the flow crosses above its average and go short when it crosses below. If enabled, require the higher-timeframe flow to agree with the trade direction, directional strength to meet its minimum, and current volume to meet its historical-average multiple. A new long signal closes any short; a new short signal closes any long. Set entry from the prior close, place the stop one configurable volatility multiple away, and derive tiered profit targets from the stop distance using configurable risk-to-reward ratios. Emit a target-hit event when price reaches each target; no stop-hit exit is defined.
