For each monitored instrument, signal long when its close is above the prior lookback period’s highest high, its volume exceeds the prior lookback period’s highest volume, and its close is above a simple moving average. Signal short when its close is below the prior lookback period’s lowest low, its volume exceeds the prior lookback period’s highest volume, and its close is below that moving average. Also signal bullish momentum when the close is above an exponential moving average of highs while each of the previous three closes was below that average on its corresponding bar. No exit rule is defined.
