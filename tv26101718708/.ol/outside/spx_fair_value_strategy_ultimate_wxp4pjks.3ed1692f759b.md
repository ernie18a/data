Calculate net liquidity as the central bank balance sheet minus the Treasury General Account and reverse repo balance. Derive the instrument’s fair value from net liquidity, then calculate the spread between the instrument’s daily close and fair value. When net liquidity is positive, an upward crossing of the overbought threshold signals a short, and a downward crossing of the oversold threshold signals a long. Apply the selected direction mode: short-only enters on the overbought signal and exits on the oversold signal; long-only enters on the oversold signal and exits on the overbought signal; long/short switches direction on each signal. Inverse mode swaps the entry directions. Begin taking signals on or after the configured start date; on the configured close date, close open positions, with the close-date signal treated as a short entry in long/short mode.
