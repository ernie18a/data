Form support pools at confirmed swing lows and resistance pools at confirmed swing highs, weighted by volume and price range; for Bitcoin, aggregate volume from multiple exchanges. Go long when a substantial support pool is swept below and price closes back above it within the reclaim period; go short when a substantial resistance pool is swept above and price closes back below it within the period. When momentum confirmation is enabled, require oversold conditions for longs and overbought conditions for shorts. Treat decisive acceptance beyond a pool, or failure to reclaim in time, as a genuine break rather than a reversal signal.
