During the configured trading window, go long when Bitcoin market capitalization divided by the 90-day average of daily on-chain transaction value falls below the oversold threshold; close the long when it rises above the overbought threshold. Go short when that ratio rises above the overbought threshold; close the short when it falls below the oversold threshold. Also exit positions at the configured loss limit or trailing profit stop. The NVT median and deviation from its smoothed trend do not affect trades.
