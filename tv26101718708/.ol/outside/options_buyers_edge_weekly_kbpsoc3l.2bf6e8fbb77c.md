Combine normalized recent underlying momentum, futures-basis change, skew-confirmed signed option buyer pressure, and lower-timeframe up-versus-down volume imbalance into a directional score. If the score exceeds the positive direction threshold and implied volatility is not contracting, signal buying calls; if it falls below the negative threshold and implied volatility is not contracting, signal buying puts. If volatility is contracting, mark a directional reading as caution and otherwise avoid buying options. If there is no directional reading and time to expiry is in the most severe decay zone, avoid; otherwise wait. No sell or exit decision is defined.
