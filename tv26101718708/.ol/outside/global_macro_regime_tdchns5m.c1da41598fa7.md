Each enabled external market produces a bullish or bearish trend signal from either a volatility-buffered fast-versus-slow moving-average comparison, an adaptive volatility trailing stop, or their combination. Map bullish and bearish signals to the four regimes according to each market’s role: growth-improving versus deteriorating and inflation-rising versus declining. Sum and normalize the supporting signals for Goldilocks (growth improving, inflation declining), Reflation (both improving and rising), Inflation (growth deteriorating, inflation rising), and Deflation (both deteriorating and declining); optionally smooth the scores. The regime with the highest score becomes dominant, retaining the prior regime when it remains tied for highest. Hold the asset assigned to the dominant regime long; when the regime changes, rotate to its assigned asset or exit to cash if none is assigned. With no custom assignments, hold the chart instrument in Goldilocks or Reflation and remain in cash in Inflation or Deflation. The logic does not open short positions.
