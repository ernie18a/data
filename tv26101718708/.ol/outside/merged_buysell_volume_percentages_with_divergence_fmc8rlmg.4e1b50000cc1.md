During each regular session (09:30–17:00) and extended session (18:00–09:29), accumulate that session’s high, low, close, and volume. Estimate buy volume as cumulative volume multiplied by the close’s fraction of the session range above the low; estimate sell volume as cumulative volume multiplied by the close’s fraction below the high. Convert both estimates to percentages of their sum. When buy percentage crosses above sell percentage, with both above 1% and positive total volume, signal buying a call option. When sell percentage crosses above buy percentage under the same filters, signal buying a put option. No exit rule is specified.
