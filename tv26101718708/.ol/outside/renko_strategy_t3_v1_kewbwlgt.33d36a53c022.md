Build Renko OHLC values from the traded instrument and smooth them with a Tilson T3 filter. Treat the trend as bullish when the smoothed close is above the smoothed open and the latest bullish state is more recent than the latest bearish state; treat it as bearish under the converse conditions. On a change to bullish, enter long if within the configured trading window. On a change to bearish, enter short if shorting is enabled and within the window. Close a long when the trend turns bearish and close a short when it turns bullish.
