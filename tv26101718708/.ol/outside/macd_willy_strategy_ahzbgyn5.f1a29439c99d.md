Go long when the instrument closes above its exponential moving average, the MACD histogram is positive, and the fast smoothed Williams %R line is above the slow line. Go short when the close is below the moving average, the histogram is negative, and the fast line is below the slow line. Exit a long when the histogram is negative, the fast line is below the slow line, and that line is below the overbought threshold. Exit a short when the histogram is positive, the fast line is above the slow line, and that line is above the oversold threshold.
