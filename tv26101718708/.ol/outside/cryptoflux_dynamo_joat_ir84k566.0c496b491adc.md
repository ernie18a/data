Weight the trend and momentum pathways most, the squeeze pathway next, and the pullback pathway least. Adjust each score for volume participation, volatility regime, and cycle direction. Enter long or short only when its score reaches the required threshold and session, directional cycle, volume surge, drawdown lockout, and optional external-market dominance filters permit it. If both directions qualify, keep only the direction indicated by the MACD histogram. On an opposing entry, close the existing position and reverse; permit same-direction re-entry according to the configured rule, and allow shorts only when enabled. Size positions from equity risk divided by an ATR-based stop distance, capped by an equity-based exposure limit. Set stop and profit target from ATR using volatility-regime-dependent distances; optionally trail with an ATR-based stop that tightens the initial stop.
