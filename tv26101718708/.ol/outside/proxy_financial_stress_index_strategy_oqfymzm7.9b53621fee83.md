For each external daily series—market volatility, the 10-year government bond yield, the broad dollar index, a broad equity index, the euro-to-dollar exchange rate, and a high-yield bond fund—calculate its deviation from its moving average divided by its standard deviation. Multiply each normalized value by its assigned weight and sum them into a composite stress measure. Enter long in the traded instrument when this measure crosses from above to below a chosen threshold, only if no position is open. Exit the long position after the chosen number of bars in the position.
