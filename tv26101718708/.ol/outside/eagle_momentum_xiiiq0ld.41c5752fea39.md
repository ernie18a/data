Issue a long entry when at least three quarters of the applicable bullish checks pass: price is above its medium-term EMA, which is above its long-term SMA; the MACD histogram is positive and rising; RSI is in its bullish range; price is above the Bollinger midline; volume exceeds its recent average; SuperTrend is bullish; and, when enabled, the instrument outperformed its benchmark over the lookback period. Also require the MACD line to have crossed above its signal line on the current or one of the previous three bars. No short or exit rule is specified.
