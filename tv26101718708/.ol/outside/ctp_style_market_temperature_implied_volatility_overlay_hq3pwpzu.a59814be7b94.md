For bullish options, look for setups when the market regime is strong, price is above key moving averages, and volatility risk is low or improving. When conditions are mixed or volatility is cautionary, be selective and avoid chasing new bullish entries. When the regime is weak or volatility risk is elevated, avoid aggressive bullish trades and prioritize capital preservation. A weakening regime is a reason to tighten risk or consider taking partial profits; it is not an automatic exit signal. No short, put-buying, or explicit sell signal is defined.
