Use a bounded flow oscillator derived from signed volume, with configurable alternatives based on momentum of detrended flow or volume-weighted momentum. Compare confirmed price swing pivots with nearby oscillator swings, subject to configurable separation and minimum-magnitude filters. A higher price high with a lower flow high is bearish; a lower price low with a higher flow low is bullish. Optionally require regular divergences to occur in overbought or oversold territory. Hidden bearish divergence is a lower price high with a higher flow high; hidden bullish divergence is a higher price low with a lower flow low. Treat bearish signals as short bias and bullish signals as long bias. An oscillator pivot in the upper extreme zone is bearish; a pivot in the lower extreme zone is bullish. Optional volume-event alerts add bearish bias for a high-volume new high rejected near the bar's low, and bullish bias for a high-volume new low reclaimed near the bar's high or a wide, high-volume down bar closing away from its low. There is no exit condition; forward calibration reports outcomes but does not gate signals.
