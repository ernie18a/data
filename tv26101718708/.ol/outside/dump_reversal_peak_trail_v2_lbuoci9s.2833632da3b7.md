Long-only strategy. Arm an entry after a down candle whose selected-source percentage drop exceeds a threshold, stays within a maximum drop cap, and makes a low below the prior completed daily bars’ lookback low (or skip this low-break test if disabled). While waiting, reset the reclaim reference when a lower low forms on a candle within the drop cap. Block entries near the prior completed daily lookback high. When enabled, also require the external BTC perpetual’s hourly close to have risen over the preceding four hours. Require cooldown to have expired; allow entries only when flat or when pyramiding is permitted. For reclaim entries, use the configured confirmation: enter on a close above the dump candle’s high; after the configured number of closes above it; on a cross above it by placing a limit near that level; or by placing an ATR-buffered stop above it. In automatic mode, try the limit after the cross, then use the buffered stop if the limit expires, with configured order expiries. Alternatively, in rebound mode, track the lowest low after the qualifying dump and enter on the first green close after price has rebounded enough from that low and the recent range is sufficiently flat. Once long, track the highest profit percentage relative to weighted average entry. After profit reaches the configured arming threshold, exit when profit retraces from its peak by the configured amount. If enabled, also exit at a stop-loss percentage below average entry; if the position remains below average entry beyond the configured time limit, exit. Start the cooldown after a position closes.
