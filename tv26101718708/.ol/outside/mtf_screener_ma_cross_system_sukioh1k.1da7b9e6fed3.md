Calculate a fast and medium exponential moving average and a slow Hull moving average. Select one pair to compare: fast versus medium, fast versus slow, or medium versus slow. Enter long when the selected first average crosses above the second while the instrument's close is above its higher-timeframe slow Hull moving average. Enter short when the first crosses below the second while the close is below that higher-timeframe average. Set protective stop and profit target either by configurable percentages of entry price or by configurable multiples of average true range, with the target distance based on a configurable risk/reward ratio in ATR mode. Under fixed management, exit at the stop or target. Under break-even management, move the stop to entry after the configured favorable risk/reward threshold. Under partial management, close a configured portion at its favorable threshold and move the stop to entry. Under advanced management, do the same partial close and then trail the stop at a configurable ATR distance, never placing a long stop below entry or a short stop above entry.
