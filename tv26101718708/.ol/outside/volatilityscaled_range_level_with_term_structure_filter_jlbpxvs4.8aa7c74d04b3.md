At the start of each new selected trading period, calculate a reference level as the prior completed period’s close minus its ATR; in live-preview mode, use the forming period’s close and ATR. Round the level down to the configured strike increment to choose the short put strike, then choose a long put strike a configured spread width below it. Recommend selling the short put and buying the long put only when the short-dated volatility index divided by the longer-dated volatility index is at or below its configured maximum, the short-dated volatility index is at or above its configured minimum, and, if enabled, the underlying price is above its 21-period EMA. Otherwise report no trade. The configured minimum credit is a ticket-level requirement, not part of the signal gate. No conditional exit rule is implemented.
