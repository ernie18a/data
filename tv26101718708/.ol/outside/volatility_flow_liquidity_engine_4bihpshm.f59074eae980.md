Generate a long or short signal when a high pressure percentile has occurred recently and the current bar shows a meaningful range expansion. Set direction by scoring a close beyond the prior lookback high or low more heavily than close location within the bar and recent momentum; choose the higher score, with ties bullish. Require the release to persist for the configured confirmation period and respect the signal cooldown. If enabled, require higher-timeframe trend alignment, skip non-expiry signals in a statistically significant mean-reverting regime, and veto signals only when reconstructed order flow strongly opposes the direction. By default these filters are informational or disabled. Suppress a long when nearby resistance leaves insufficient room, and a short when nearby support leaves insufficient room. Suppress signals in stressed liquidity by default. The script provides no exit rule and makes no options decisions.
