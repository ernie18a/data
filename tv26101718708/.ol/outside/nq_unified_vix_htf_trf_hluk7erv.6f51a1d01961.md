During the morning cash session, enter long when the traded instrument crosses above its range-based trend filter, its 15-minute trend is bullish, and the 15-minute VIX is below its own trend filter. Enter short when the instrument crosses below its trend filter and the 15-minute VIX is above its trend filter; no higher-timeframe trend agreement is required for shorts. Allow only one open position. Exit either direction on its ATR-based stop or fixed risk-multiple target; for shorts, also exit if the VIX falls back below its trend filter. When price reaches the relevant extension level derived from the prior day's range and the opening range, move the stop to entry.
