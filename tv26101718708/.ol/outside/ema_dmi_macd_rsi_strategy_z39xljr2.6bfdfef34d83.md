Go long when the short-, medium-, and longer-term trend averages are bullishly ordered, price is above the long-term trend average and the higher-timeframe trend average, momentum is bullish, relative strength is strong, positive directional movement exceeds negative directional movement with a strong trend, and volume exceeds its recent average. Go short on the inverse conditions: bearish ordering, price below both trend averages, bearish momentum, weak relative strength, negative directional movement dominant with a strong trend, and above-average volume. For either position, exit at an ATR-based protective stop or profit target, with the target farther from entry than the stop.
