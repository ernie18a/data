During the regular trading session, excluding the opening interval and respecting a cooldown between repeated signals, sell a cash-secured put when the underlying is above its daily moving average, sufficiently below intraday VWAP, and its RSI recently fell below an oversold threshold and is now rising. Buy to close a short put when RSI reaches a strength threshold and the underlying is above VWAP, subject to the same session, opening-interval, and cooldown filters.
