Compare the traded asset’s momentum with a benchmark’s momentum, normalize the difference by their average volatility, and smooth it. Go long when the smoothed relative strength crosses above zero; go short when it crosses below zero. If a second benchmark filter is enabled, require relative strength versus that benchmark to have the same sign. Enforce a minimum gap between confirmed cross signals when a cooldown is enabled. Also flag bullish divergence when price makes a lower pivot low while relative strength makes a higher pivot low, and bearish divergence when price makes a higher pivot high while relative strength makes a lower pivot high.
