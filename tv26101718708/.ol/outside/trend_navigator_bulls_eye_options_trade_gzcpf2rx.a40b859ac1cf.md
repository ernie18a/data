On each bar, compare the monitored underlying proxy, call option, and put option with their own fast and trend EMAs. Classify each as bullish when its close and fast EMA are above its trend EMA, or bearish when both are below. Measure momentum by whether each fast EMA is rising or falling versus the prior bar. Buy the call when the underlying and call are bullish, both fast EMAs are rising, and the put fast EMA is falling. Buy the put when the underlying and call are bearish, both fast EMAs are falling, and the put fast EMA is rising. Otherwise, wait. A bullish trend state occurs when the underlying and call are bullish and the put is bearish; a bearish trend state is the inverse. A reset state occurs when the underlying and call are bullish, their fast EMAs are falling, and the put fast EMA is rising; this state means wait.
