For SPY, compare its price with a trend EMA and a volatility-adjusted range filter. Classify the trend as bullish when price is above its EMA and bearish when below. Classify the range as rising when price is above the filter and the filter has risen; classify it as falling when price is below the filter and the filter has fallen. From external VVIX and VIX data, mark each as rising when above its EMA and above its prior value. VVIX is falling when below its EMA and below its prior value. Risk is calm when VVIX is below its caution threshold and VIX is not rising; risk is dangerous when VVIX reaches its danger threshold, and panic when it reaches its panic threshold. Confirmed risk expansion occurs when both VVIX and VIX are rising; treat that as panic. Set the state to long when SPY trend and range are bullish, risk is calm, and VVIX is falling. Otherwise set it to exit or defend when panic risk is present, when VVIX is dangerous and rising, or when SPY trend and range are bearish. Otherwise set it to hold when SPY trend and range are bullish and panic risk is absent. If none applies, retain the prior state. Evaluate long first, then exit/defend, then hold.
