At each completed higher-timeframe candle, compare its high and low with the prior candle’s range. A break above the prior high followed by a close back below signals bearish bias; a break below the prior low followed by a close back above signals bullish bias. If both sides are swept, use the candle’s direction to choose bullish or bearish bias. Ignore signals disallowed by the selected directional filter. In the faster mode, confirm a sweep on a chart-timeframe close back inside the prior range; invalidate it if price later breaks through the swept level. In the confirmation mode, after a sweep, confirm bearish bias when a chart-timeframe candle closes below the opening level of the consecutive bullish candles that formed the upward manipulation, or bullish bias when it closes above the corresponding level of the consecutive bearish candles forming the downward manipulation. Treat reaching the opposite side of the prior range as success and breaking beyond the swept side as invalidation.
