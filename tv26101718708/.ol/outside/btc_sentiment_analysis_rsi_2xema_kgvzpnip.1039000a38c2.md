Within the configured trading date window, use the external Bitfinex BTC long and short position ratios. Enter a long position when the RSI of the long ratio crosses above the RSI of the short ratio, the long ratio’s change is above its EMA, and the short ratio’s change is below its EMA. Exit the long position when the long-ratio RSI crosses below the short-ratio RSI, the long ratio’s change is below its EMA, and the short ratio’s change is above its EMA. The strategy has no short-entry rule.
