For each configured reference pair, enter long on the chart instrument when its shorter EMA exceeds the next EMA, its medium EMA exceeds the longest EMA, Supertrend is bullish, and smoothed stochastic %K crosses above %D. Enter short when both EMA comparisons are reversed, Supertrend is bearish, and %K crosses below %D. A signal from any reference pair triggers the corresponding entry and closes all open positions; each entry also has an attached exit with its limit and stop both set to the chart instrument’s current close.
