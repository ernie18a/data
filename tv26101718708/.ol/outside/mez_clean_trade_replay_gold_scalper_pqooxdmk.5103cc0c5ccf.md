On each confirmed bar, evaluate long and short setups. For a long, require the enabled session, higher-timeframe trend, dollar-index, liquidity-sweep, Fibonacci-zone, extension-distance, and prior-day-level filters to pass; for a short, apply their directional counterparts. Score each side using higher-timeframe trend, liquidity sweep, Fibonacci location, dollar-index support, aligned moving-average structure, momentum, and session status. Momentum requires price relative to the fast moving average and VWAP, RSI on the matching side of neutral, matching MACD direction, and sufficient trend strength. A long trigger is a bullish liquidity sweep, an upward fast-average reclaim while above the middle average, or a momentum-confirmed break above recent highs. A short trigger is the inverse. Enter only when news and other enabled filters permit, the side's score meets the selected mode's threshold, its score exceeds the opposite side's, and its trigger occurs; do not open a new trade while one is active. Set a protective stop using the relevant recent swing extreme with a buffer when valid, otherwise use a volatility-based or fixed-distance stop. Set three directional profit targets using the selected volatility-based or fixed-distance method. After entry, record target hits; exit at the stop or third target, or after the first target if price pulls back sufficiently from its best favorable price or confirmed momentum reverses. If stop and third target are both touched on the same bar, treat the third target as the exit.
