On each confirmed bar, enter only when flat. In the standard mode, a long signal is a bullish WaveTrend/RSI/Stochastic divergence (including the special bullish divergence setup), or—if divergence-only mode is off—a bullish WaveTrend cross while oversold. A short signal is a bearish divergence from the enabled divergence sources, or—if divergence-only mode is off—a bearish WaveTrend cross while overbought. Apply the corresponding optional filters: long entries require price above the local higher-timeframe EMA, and short entries require price below it; optional price-range, RSI trend, slope, and volume filters must also pass. The global benchmark trend is not applied in standard mode. In diamond mode, use higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candle directions plus a WaveTrend cross and its directional threshold to signal long or short; require the matching local EMA trend and global benchmark trend, and replace the standard signals and filters. Respect the long-only and short-only switches. Submit a limit entry at the signal close, or at a configured lower price for longs / higher price for shorts. Set a stop and profit target from the entry price; use the separate long target for divergence-based long entries. Close an open position at its stop or target. If enabled, cancel pending orders after the configured bar count and close positions once that count is exceeded. If enabled, also close longs when price falls below the local EMA and shorts when price rises above it.
