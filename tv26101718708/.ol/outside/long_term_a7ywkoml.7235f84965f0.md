Enter long when any of these holds: price is above a fast moving average with elevated volume; price is above a slower moving average, has outperformed a comparison instrument over a lookback, has elevated volume, and exceeds the prior lookback’s high; or price is above a shorter moving average with exceptionally elevated volume. Exit the corresponding long position if price falls sufficiently below the fast moving average, drops sharply from the prior close, spikes far above the average of recent closes, or falls below both the short and fast moving averages.
