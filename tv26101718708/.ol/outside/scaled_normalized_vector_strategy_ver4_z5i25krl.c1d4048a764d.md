Calculate the ratio of the traded instrument’s close on the shorter of two selected timeframes to its close on the longer timeframe, minus one. Apply the selected transform to this ratio (leaky ReLU, sign step, Swish, z-score, or no transform). Go long when the transformed ratio crosses upward through a positive sensitivity threshold while a normalized momentum oscillator derived from the instrument’s selected price data is above zero. Go short when the ratio crosses downward through the corresponding negative threshold while the oscillator is below zero. Suppress duplicate entries on the same bar close. On a new long or short entry, close any position in the opposite direction. Exit the active position after the configured holding period.
