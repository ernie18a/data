Build adaptive volatility bands around a rolling price basis using a selected deviation method, adjusting width for tail behavior and realized containment. Classify the market as trending when directional strength and path efficiency are both elevated; otherwise classify it as ranging unless volatility clustering indicates a volatile regime. In a range, signal a short when price previously closed beyond the upper main band and now pierces it but closes back below; signal a long on the corresponding lower-band rejection. In a trend, signal with the trend when price persists beyond the main band or closes beyond the inner band while continuing in that direction. Require the configured volume or order-flow confirmation when enabled, and confirm signals at bar close when enabled. For trend entries, reject signals opposed by the higher-timeframe bias. Score each candidate using band stretch, regime fit, multi-timeframe agreement, mean-reversion half-life, the mode’s forward-calibrated edge, volume thrust, and order-flow alignment. Act only when the score meets its configured minimum and no veto applies: a volatility-index spike vetoes fades; insufficient directional multi-timeframe agreement, opposing higher-timeframe bias, or opposing configured cross-asset momentum vetoes trend rides. Enter only when flat. Set a stop using the configured outer-band, volatility-based, or half-life-based method. For fades, take profits at the basis and opposite inner band; for rides, set successive targets from stop risk. After the first target is reached, move the stop to entry. Exit on the stop or second target.
