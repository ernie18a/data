Long-only. Enter after a setup where daily RSI crosses above its bullish threshold while weekly RSI is above it, or weekly RSI crosses above it while daily RSI is above it. Require monthly RSI above the threshold when available, positive relative performance versus a benchmark over a lookback, and—when enabled—the benchmark above its long-term moving average. The setup remains valid for a limited confirmation window: enter if price breaks above the setup candle’s high, provided the position is flat or eligible to add after a partial exit and the stop distance is within the risk limit. Initialize the stop at the setup candle’s low; on an added position, never lower an existing stop. While long, raise the stop to a new candle low when daily RSI is below its bullish threshold and relative performance is negative, except when RSI is in the support zone above the hard-breakdown boundary. RSI below that boundary always qualifies as weakness. Exit immediately if price falls sufficiently below the stop; otherwise, a close below the stop marks that candle’s low as a pending exit trigger, canceled if price recovers above the stop and executed if price subsequently breaks the marked low. When weekly RSI is overbought and the close breaks below the prior multi-day low, sell half the position once; this permits one later add if a fresh entry setup confirms.
