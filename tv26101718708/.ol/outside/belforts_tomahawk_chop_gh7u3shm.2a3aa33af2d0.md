Use confirmed bars. Permit a long signal only when the higher-timeframe gate is bullish, and a short signal only when it is bearish: two completed higher-timeframe candles must share the direction; the intermediate-timeframe candle can raise alignment strength when it agrees, and a strong candle with no opposing wick raises it further. Require the configured minimum strength. Optionally require four stochastic measures to agree in the signal direction. Fire when any enabled trigger agrees with the gate: a smoothed RSI crossover (also require RSI on the appropriate side of its midpoint); bullish or bearish RSI divergence against price; a wave-oscillator crossover from an extreme zone or its divergence; a trend-following indicator reversal; either of two stochastic-versus-RSI crossovers, with its configured zone filter; matching strong candle patterns across both timeframes; a stochastic divergence against price; a stochastic extreme-zone exit while all four stochastic measures slope in the same direction; a volatility-band breakout by the RSI signal line, excluding configured extreme readings; a completed run of same-direction candles whose bodies and strength wicks expand without opposing wicks; or the end of a four-stochastic extreme followed by a candle in the corresponding reversal direction. Apply each trigger's configured optional background, zone, and confluence filters. Suppress a repeat of the same trigger and direction while its previous tracked signal remains open and has not reached its first target; release the block on that target or on the configured stop. The indicator also tracks configured stop and profit targets for signal statistics, but defines no separate trade-exit instruction.
