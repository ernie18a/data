On the comparison timeframe, count enabled lead markets breaking above their own prior lookback highs; for an inverse market, count a break below its prior lookback low instead. Signal bullish leadership when this count meets the configured upside threshold and the NQ reference has not broken above its own prior lookback high. Signal bearish leadership when enabled leads breaking below prior lookback lows meet the configured downside threshold, counting an inverse market’s break above its prior lookback high instead, and NQ has not broken below its own prior lookback low. The breakout range uses the configured number of preceding bars, or only the immediately preceding bar when the lookback filter is disabled. These are directional lead signals; no exit condition is defined.
