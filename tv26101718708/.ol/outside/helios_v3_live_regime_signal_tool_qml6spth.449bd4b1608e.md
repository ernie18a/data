Score trend components as bullish, neutral, or bearish: MACD line and signal line both above or below zero; average close versus average open; current close versus its momentum lookback close; and current close versus a long-term average. Add participation from whether on-balance volume is above or below its own average, and relative strength from whether the traded instrument’s price ratio to a benchmark is above or below its own average. Disable the relative-strength component for Bitcoin-based instruments. Combine the votes. Enter long when the combined score meets the bullish threshold for the profile’s required consecutive bars, realized volatility is within its profile cap, price extension above its long-term average is below the buy-block level, and the system is flat. Exit the long when the score meets the bearish threshold for the same confirmation period, volatility is within its cap, extension is not below the sell-block level, and the system is long. Stay flat when entry conditions fail; do not open short positions. Classify a qualifying buy as full size when its score exceeds the entry threshold and extension is below a stricter level; otherwise classify it as half size.
