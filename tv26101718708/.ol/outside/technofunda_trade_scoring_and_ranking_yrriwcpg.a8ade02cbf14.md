Score nine binary conditions: (1) close above the 50-, 150-, and 200-period simple moving averages; (2) those averages are ordered 50-period above 150-period above 200-period; (3) the 200-period average is higher than 20 bars earlier; (4) close is at least 75% of the highest high over 252 bars; (5) EPS growth exceeds 20%; (6) revenue growth exceeds 15%; (7) return on equity exceeds 17%; (8) today closes at or above the prior close, volume exceeds the greatest volume on a down day in the preceding configurable lookback, and volume is at least twice its lookback average; (9) the instrument’s 21-bar percentage return exceeds the benchmark’s. Label scores of 7–9 Strong Buy, 5–6 Buy, 3–4 Neutral, and 0–2 Weak. Mark a Strong Buy entry only when the score is at least 7 and the current candle closes above its open. The indicator specifies no short or exit decision.
