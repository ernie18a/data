Build the high and low of the configured opening range. During the configured trading window, allow at most one signal per direction per day. A long setup requires a fresh close above the range high, still within the allowed bars after breakout and not invalidated by a close below the short-term moving average. Require price and that average above session VWAP, both sloping upward; sufficient relative and expanding volume; a bullish candle closing above the prior bar’s high; and the first pullback that touches the range high, moving average, or VWAP within an ATR-scaled buffer and closes back above it. Also require the instrument and relevant broad-market index or indices to be above their VWAPs; apply the additional index filter to designated technology instruments. Reverse these conditions for a short setup: fresh close below the range low, no invalidation by a close above the moving average, price and average below VWAP with downward slopes, sufficient volume, bearish candle closing below the prior bar’s low, first qualifying pullback closing back below its touched level, and instrument and relevant indices below VWAP. On a signal, use the signal bar’s close as the entry reference and provide an ATR-scaled stop and two profit targets. The script does not define conditional exit signals.
