On the 1-hour chart, evaluate the 09:00–10:00 New York candle once per day. If it closes above its open and long entries are enabled, place a limit buy at its close; if it closes below its open and short entries are enabled, place a limit sell there. Set the stop beyond the candle’s low for a long or high for a short, either at the wick or offset by a configurable fraction of the candle’s range. Set the profit target in the trade’s direction at a configurable risk/reward multiple of the entry-to-stop distance. Close any open position at 15:00 New York time.
