Form a consolidation range when the recent high–low span is unusually small, measured either against its recent range distribution or relative to volatility. While contained, expand the range to include new highs and lows. Signal long when price probes below the range, closes back inside with a sufficiently large lower wick, then a following bar closes higher than the prior bar. Signal short on the mirror condition above the range, followed by a lower close. Signal long on an upside breakout, or short on a downside breakout, when price closes beyond the corresponding range boundary by a volatility-scaled buffer for the required confirmation period, after the range has lasted long enough; optionally require a volume spike. After a breakout, stop tracking the range and wait through a cooldown before detecting another. No exit condition is defined.
