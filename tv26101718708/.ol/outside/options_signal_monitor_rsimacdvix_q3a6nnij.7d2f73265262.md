Buy a call when RSI is above its oversold level and below its overbought level, and either MACD crosses above its signal line or its histogram is positive and rising, while price is above the short EMA and the short EMA is above the long EMA. Buy a put when RSI is above its midpoint and no more than a small configurable margin above its overbought level, and either MACD crosses below its signal line or its histogram is negative and falling, while price is below the short EMA and the short EMA is below the long EMA. Exit after a call signal if, three bars later, RSI is below oversold; exit after a put signal if, three bars later, RSI is above overbought. The configured volatility index and premium-based stop level do not affect these decisions.
