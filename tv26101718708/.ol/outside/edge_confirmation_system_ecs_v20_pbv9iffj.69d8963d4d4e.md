On each confirmed bar, classify trend as bullish when price is above its long-term average and its fast average is above its slower average; classify it as bearish when price is below the long-term average and the fast average is below the slower average. Classify momentum as bullish when a normalized short-versus-long average spread is positive and above its smoothed value, and bearish when it is negative and below its smoothed value. Classify volume as surging when current volume exceeds a multiple of its recent average; a bullish surge also requires an up-close candle, and a bearish surge a down-close candle. Enter long when trend and momentum are bullish, momentum is not overextended upward, and—when volume confirmation is enabled—a bullish volume surge occurs. Enter short under the symmetric bearish conditions, excluding downward overextension and requiring a bearish volume surge when enabled. Suppress another entry in the same direction until an entry in the opposite direction resets the direction state. While long, signal an exit when momentum turns bearish or when upward overextension coincides with momentum falling below its smoothed value. While short, signal an exit when momentum turns bullish or when downward overextension coincides with momentum rising above its smoothed value.
