On each confirmed bar, calculate rolling correlation and beta between Gold and Silver returns, with beta derived from their correlation and volatility ratio. Trade only when correlation passes its entry filter and beta is within its valid range. Build a pair score by combining the standardized beta-adjusted Silver-minus-Gold return residual with the inverted standardized log Gold/Silver price ratio. When the score is at an upper extreme and turns downward, enter long Gold/short Silver; when it is at a lower extreme and turns upward, enter short Gold/long Silver. Size the legs using the price ratio and beta frozen at entry. Exit the pair when the score reverts past its mean-reversion boundary, moves adversely beyond a stop measured from the entry score, correlation or beta becomes invalid, the holding limit is reached, or leveraged loss reaches the allocated equity. After exit, wait through a cooldown before allowing another entry; halt trading after liquidation.
