During the configured backtest window, use the traded instrument’s daily close from Friday as the reference. On Saturday and Sunday, when flat, enter short if the current close is above the reference by the configured upper percentage, or enter long if it is below the reference by the configured lower percentage. When already short, add to the short if the current close is above the average entry price by the upper percentage. When already long, submit a long entry on each eligible bar without an additional price condition. Close all positions when open profit as a fraction of initial capital exceeds the configured profit target, and close all positions on Monday.
