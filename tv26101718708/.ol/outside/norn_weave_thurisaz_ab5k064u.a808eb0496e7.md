Enter long when the instrument’s EMA is rising, its swing-break trend is bullish, trend strength exceeds its adaptive or configured threshold, and the external footprint-delta signal is not bearish. Enter short under the inverse conditions, requiring the external signal not to be bullish. If the instrument trend agrees with the daily swing-break trend, allow the entry. If daily trend is unknown, also allow it. When they disagree, block entries only while price lies between the corresponding 38.2% and 61.8% retracement levels of the latest daily swing; allow entries outside that zone. For an allowed countertrend entry in the shallow retracement region, set the first profit target at the 38.2% level; otherwise use three progressively farther volatility-based targets. Reduce the position in stages at those targets. Close the full position if loss exceeds the calculated or configured stop, if price returns to entry after favorable movement activates a break-even stop, or if the swing-break trend reverses. Reverse into the opposite direction only when that direction’s entry conditions are met.
