After a configured start date, calculate a weighted moving average of the sum of two rate-of-change measurements of a configurable reference market series, which may differ from the traded instrument. Enter or remain long in the traded instrument while this measure is above zero; close the long position when it is below zero. While long, place a limit order to sell a configured portion of the position when the traded instrument reaches a configured percentage gain from the average entry price. No short entry is specified.
