For each scanned instrument, calculate its position within the recent high–low range and whether its close is above or below the range midpoint. Signal a long setup when price is near the range low and above the midpoint; mark it strong when it is also in the bottom 10% of the range. Signal a short setup when price is near the range high and below the midpoint; mark it strong when it is also in the top 10%. Otherwise, flag price near the high as caution, price near the low as a possible bounce, and wait in all other cases.
