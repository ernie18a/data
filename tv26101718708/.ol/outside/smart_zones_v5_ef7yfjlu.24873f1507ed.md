Score bullish and bearish confluence from directional higher-timeframe liquidity sweeps, session activity, volume expansion, relative strength versus a correlated market, higher-timeframe gaps, proximity to relevant prior-day/open/gap structure, volatility-index direction, and repeated liquidity sweeps; add extra weight for a strong engulfing reversal at structure or an engulfing candle inside a recent gap. Favor the higher-scoring side only when its score meets the qualifying threshold; skip weak scores and wait when a gap conflicts with a liquidity sweep. Signal long after a downside liquidity sweep when bullish score qualifies, and short after an upside sweep when bearish score qualifies. Also signal after a sweep when price breaks the corresponding recent structure level, on a strong engulfing reversal at support/resistance structure, on an engulfing candle within a recent directional gap, or on a volatility-confirmed breakout from a consolidation range. Invalidate a long if price closes below the swept-low reference minus a volatility buffer; invalidate a short if it closes above the swept-high reference plus a volatility buffer.
