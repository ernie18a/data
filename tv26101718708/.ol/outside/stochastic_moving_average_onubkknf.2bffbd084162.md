When flat, enter long at the next bar’s open if enabled and the fast moving average is above the slow one, the close is below the fast average and at or above the slow average, and the stochastic %K crosses above %D while %K meets the oversold lookback condition. Enter short under the inverse conditions: the fast average is below the slow one, the close is above the fast average and at or below the slow average, and %D crosses above %K while %K meets the overbought lookback condition. The lookback condition can accept an extreme reading within the scan; with the all-bars option enabled, the implemented check effectively uses the oldest scanned reading. If the optional reference-market filter is enabled, longs also require that market’s close to be above its moving average now or on the prior bar; shorts require it to be below. Exit an open position when price reaches its stop or profit limit: place the stop one volatility-based distance from the signal-bar close and the target the configured risk-to-reward multiple of that distance in the trade’s direction.
