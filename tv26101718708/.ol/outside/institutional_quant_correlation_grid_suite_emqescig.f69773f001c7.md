Using rolling data for the traded instrument and an external benchmark, calculate price correlation, volume-weighted return correlation, beta, relative-strength momentum (the instrument-to-benchmark price ratio compared with its rolling average), and a z-score of the log price ratio. Generate independent signals: BUY when price and volume-weighted correlations meet the configured correlation threshold, relative-strength momentum is positive, beta exceeds its configured floor, and the z-score is below the configured extreme threshold. HOLD when price correlation meets the threshold, absolute relative-strength momentum is within the configured neutral band, and absolute z-score is below the extreme threshold. SELL/AVOID when relative-strength momentum is negative, or price correlation is below its configured low-correlation cutoff, or z-score meets or exceeds the extreme threshold. Signals may overlap.
