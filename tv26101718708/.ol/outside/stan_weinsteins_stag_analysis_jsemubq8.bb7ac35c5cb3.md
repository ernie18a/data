Stay out during sideways accumulation or a falling market; avoid buying during distribution and reduce exposure, take partial profits, and tighten protection. Hold long positions while the instrument is in an advancing trend. Enter long when it transitions into an advancing trend, trading volume is unusually high versus its recent average, and both the broad market and its sector are advancing. While the trend advances, maintain a volatility-adjusted trailing stop that can only rise; exit the long if the instrument closes below it. No short-entry rule is specified.
