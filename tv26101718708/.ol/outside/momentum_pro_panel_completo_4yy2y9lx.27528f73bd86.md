For the traded instrument, count bullish confirmations: price above its medium-term average, short-term average above medium-term average, momentum line above its signal line, strength above its bullish threshold, and volume above its recent average. A buy requires the benchmark price above its medium-term average, the instrument price and short-term average above its medium-term average, and the selected aggressiveness mode’s minimum confirmation count and strength threshold. More aggressive modes require fewer confirmations and a lower strength threshold. Sell when the short-term average falls below the medium-term average or strength falls below its neutral threshold. Emit a signal only when the corresponding buy or sell condition changes from false to true.
