Build a directional score from the adaptive volatility-based trend trail, price relative to smoothed high/low structure gates, momentum, trend strength and direction, candle-position pressure, directional volume expansion, liquidity-sweep signals and recent sweep risk, volatility expansion after compression, higher- and middle-timeframe EMA slopes, and intraday price relative to session VWAP. Contributions favor the direction each measure indicates; neutral readings contribute no direction. Clamp the combined score to a bounded range. Block fresh direction changes during poor efficiency or weak trend strength, after a large opening gap, on a weak-bodied candle with a dominant wick, when price is too far from the adaptive trail, or when the relevant higher or middle timeframe opposes the proposed direction. Also block longs while a recent, sufficiently strong buy-side sweep remains unreclaimed, and shorts while a recent, sufficiently strong sell-side sweep remains unreclaimed; decay sweep risk over time. When confirmed, change the persistent state to long if the score reaches the bullish threshold and the long-side blocks are clear, or to short if it reaches the bearish threshold and the short-side blocks are clear. If neither direction qualifies, retain the existing state, except reset to neutral when the absolute score falls below a neutral threshold. Enforce a minimum interval between directional flips; neutral resets may occur without that delay. Emit a buy or sell on a transition into the corresponding state, and treat a transition to neutral as an exit. Optionally confirm decisions intrabar instead of at bar close.
