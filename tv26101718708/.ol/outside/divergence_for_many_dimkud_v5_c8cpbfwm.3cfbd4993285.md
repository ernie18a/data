Within the configured trading date window, detect regular bullish or bearish divergences between price and any enabled indicators (including an optional external series). A bullish divergence occurs when price makes a lower pivot low while an indicator makes a higher low; a bearish divergence occurs when price makes a higher pivot high while an indicator makes a lower high. Require at least one divergence. Enter long on a bullish divergence and short on a bearish divergence when that direction is enabled and every enabled filter for that direction passes; prevent duplicate entries on the same bar. Filters can require a selected channel excursion or re-entry pattern, higher-timeframe RSI/CCI/momentum/MFI to lie within direction-specific ranges, MFI to be rising or falling, fast ATR or volume average to be above or below its slow average, or Supertrend to agree with the direction (optionally inverted). When enabled, close a short on a bullish divergence or a long on a bearish divergence if the corresponding direction's filters pass. While a position is open, exit at its configured percentage take-profit or stop-loss price. Optionally set stop distance equal to target distance, and optionally open the opposite position after a trade closes.
