Issue an early long-reversal signal when a downside reversal trigger occurs—a swing-low sweep that closes back above the prior low, a deep downside ATR extension that closes back inside the band, or a failed breakdown—and the weighted reversal score reaches its threshold. Score evidence from RSI divergence, rejection wick, climax volume, ATR reclaim, short-term price confirmation, Williams %R hook, agreement across hourly, four-hour, and daily structure, proximity to a swing low, improving RSI, weak up/down volume, and exceptional volume during a downtrend. Require the trend-extension condition unless the ATR reclaim itself qualifies; suppress signals during the cooldown or after a prior long signal until a new swing low forms. After a bearish structure change, wait through the cooldown unless a valid downside sweep occurs. When a strong bearish trend is present, suppress the signal unless a valid sweep or ATR reclaim occurs. Mirror these rules for short reversals, using upside sweeps, failed breakouts, bearish evidence, and bullish-trend filters. Classify a qualifying reversal as strong when its score reaches a higher threshold. These are reversal alerts, not confirmed entries: the guidance says to avoid trading against aligned multi-timeframe trends until structure, volume, or trend alignment confirms a change. The script also alerts on bullish or bearish breaks of the latest confirmed swing high or low when that break changes the stored structural trend; it specifies no exit rule. Quality grades and other dashboard readings do not independently trigger trades.
