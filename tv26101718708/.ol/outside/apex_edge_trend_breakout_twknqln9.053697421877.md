Enter long when the instrument closes above the prior range’s highest high and is in an uptrend: price is above its long-term EMA and the EMA is rising. Enter short when it closes below the prior range’s lowest low and is in a downtrend: price is below the EMA and the EMA is falling. If enabled, require the last closed higher-timeframe bar to agree with the trade direction. Set an initial stop a volatility-based distance from entry. After entry, trail the stop from the best price reached, offset by a volatility-based distance; ratchet it only in the trade’s favor and exit when price touches it. Use no fixed profit target.
