Use daily data to assess a long swing setup. Trend passes when every enabled condition passes: price is above its intermediate EMA, the faster EMA is above the intermediate EMA, the intermediate EMA is rising, and, if required, price is above the longer-term average. Quality passes when relative-strength rating and relative volume meet their configured minimums, any enabled average share-volume and dollar-volume floors are met, and, if required, the stock’s sector ETF has a positive daily return. A trigger occurs if price trades at or below either short- or intermediate-term EMA and closes back above it, or taps just above either EMA and closes above it; alternatively, a pullback trigger occurs when the low is within the configured zone above either EMA, price closes above that EMA with a sufficiently strong close within the candle range, and, if required, the candle closes up. If trend and quality pass but neither trigger occurs, classify as Watch. If either trend or quality fails, classify as Not Ready. If both pass and a trigger occurs, classify as Ready. Upgrade to Ready+ when a confirmed bullish engulfing, dragonfly doji, morning star, or piercing-line pattern is present; apply any enabled EMA-proximity and relative-volume pattern filters. Patterns do not independently qualify a trade. The framework makes no short or exit decisions.
