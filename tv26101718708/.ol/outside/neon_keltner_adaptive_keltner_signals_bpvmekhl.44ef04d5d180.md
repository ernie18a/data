On a confirmed bar, generate reversal longs when price returns inside the lower deviation band after closing below it, and reversal shorts when it returns inside the upper band after closing above it. Reject reversals that conflict with the long-term EMA slope, fail the WaveTrend confirmation, exceed the permitted channel-touch strength, or violate cooldown. A recent matching WaveTrend divergence can mark a reversal; require it only if that filter is enabled. When enabled, use relative-strength momentum versus an external benchmark to confirm direction; apply this filter automatically only after its confirmed signals have enough resolved outcomes and outperform unfiltered signals.

Generate trend-continuation longs when the long-term trend is rising and price has pulled back to the selected support zone, then closes above the midline on an up bar; shorts use the inverse conditions. Require the configured WaveTrend direction, minimum trend strength, and cooldown. A recent opposite divergence can warn on or block the signal. Apply the external relative-strength confirmation under the same adaptive rule.

For breakouts, go long at the next bar's open after a confirmed close crosses above an EMA-plus-volatility band, provided that open remains above the band and above the stop. The stop is at the breakout bar's low (or open, if selected). Exit on a stop hit, a confirmed close below the EMA basis, or the trade time limit. Short breakouts follow the inverse rules only when enabled; they are disabled by default. Signals may also be hidden when their resolved historical performance falls below the strategy's adaptive acceptance rule.
