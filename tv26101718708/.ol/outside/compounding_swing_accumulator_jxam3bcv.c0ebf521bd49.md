Use an external broad-market index’s closing price to calculate its standardized deviation from a long-term average and its RSI. When both are deeply below their respective lower thresholds and the strategy is in cash, go long the traded instrument. After both measures reach their upper thresholds, exit the long when the index price crosses below its short-term exponential average, provided that crossing occurs within a few bars of the upper-threshold condition. Otherwise hold the current state.
