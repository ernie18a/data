Track the most recent fast/slow EMA crossover: an upward cross arms a long signal and clears the short signal; a downward cross arms a short signal and clears the long signal. Enter in the enabled direction when its signal is armed, RSI meets the configured directional threshold, volume is at least its moving average, and the time is outside blocked sessions. If the opening-range filter is enabled, also require the close to break above the opening range for a long or below it for a short. Clear the signal after entry. Set a stop and profit target from entry price using ATR-based distance and the configured reward-to-risk ratio. After entry, exit at either level, on a confirmed opposite EMA cross, or at the configured end-of-day close time when that close option is enabled.
