For each of several timeframes, estimate signed buying control by combining normalized buy-versus-sell volume, longer-term cumulative flow, price location and slope relative to session VWAP, moving-average alignment and slope, and volume-weighted close location. Reduce the score when strong flow produces little price progress, and scale it by how strongly flow explains price movement. Rank the absolute score against its own recent history. Combine timeframe scores, weighted by their strength, into a directional consensus; discount consensus when timeframe readings are highly correlated, reduce it in choppy regimes, and damp it during unusually turbulent conditions. Classify the path as up or down by the consensus sign when adjusted conviction clears a threshold; otherwise classify it as chop. Enter long when the path turns up and the chart-timeframe control is positive and sufficiently strong; enter short when the path turns down and chart-timeframe control is negative and sufficiently strong. Also signal a bullish reversal when price falls while control rises, a selling-exhaustion event occurs, and control momentum turns upward; signal a bearish reversal when price rises while control falls, a buying-exhaustion event occurs, and control momentum turns downward. Use the chart instrument's data, or optionally order-flow data from a related futures instrument. The benchmark-relative strength and session-volume readings affect signal grading only, not direction.
