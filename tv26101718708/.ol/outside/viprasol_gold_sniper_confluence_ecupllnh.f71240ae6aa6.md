On a confirmed fast/slow moving-average crossover, consider a long when the fast average crosses above the slow average, or a short when it crosses below. Score directional agreement from price versus VWAP, RSI versus its midpoint, MACD versus its signal, moving-average alignment, trend strength with price on the matching side of the fast average, above-average volume with a matching candle direction, and confirmation-timeframe RSI. Add a directional point when rolling correlation with the dollar index is sufficiently inverse and the dollar index is moving in the direction that supports the trade. Require the score to meet a configurable minimum. Then require the instrument to pass the gold-symbol guard, the time to fall within the permitted London or New York session (unless disabled or on daily-or-higher charts), today's range to remain below the configured fraction of its average completed-day range, and enough bars to have elapsed since the prior entry. These filters may be disabled where configurable. For an accepted entry, set a stop at a configurable ATR-based risk distance and profit targets at successive multiples of that distance. After the first target is reached, move the working stop to entry if enabled. Exit that trade when its working stop or final configured target is touched; check the stop first if both are touched on one bar. Opposite crossovers do not themselves close open trades.
