Using a selected OHLC price series from a correlated crypto instrument (or the traded instrument if correlation data is disabled), enter long when RSI crosses above its upper threshold and that price is above its simple moving average; enter short when RSI crosses below its lower threshold and that price is below its moving average. Apply entries only within the configured date window. Exit each position when its configured take-profit or stop-loss level is reached; an opposite entry signal reverses the position.
