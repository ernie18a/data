Short the traded instrument when either its session VWAP’s RSI crosses below the short-entry threshold while session VWAP is below the long-term EMA, or its weekly VWAP’s RSI crosses below the cover threshold while session VWAP is below the long-term EMA. In the alternate VIX-filtered mode, omit the EMA condition and require the VIX RSI to cross above its trigger threshold along with either VWAP RSI entry cross. While short, cover one-third when price is below the average short-entry price and session VWAP RSI crosses back above the short-entry threshold. Exit the remainder when session VWAP RSI crosses above the cover threshold or price rises to the stop-loss level above the average entry price.
