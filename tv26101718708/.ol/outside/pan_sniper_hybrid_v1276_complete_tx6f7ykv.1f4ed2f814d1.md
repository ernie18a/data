Go long when a bullish MACD crossover occurs while price is above its smoothed trend baseline and lower-timeframe buy-volume share is strong; or when price sweeps below the prior pivot low and closes back above it with strong buy-volume share. Also go long when price reaches the prior-day low or projected daily lower range, forms a hammer or bullish engulfing candle, and volume is unusually high, subject to a cooldown. Another long signal occurs when price breaks above a recent swing high or crosses above the latest bearish gap boundary while a bearish order-block target is active. Bullish intermarket divergence—price makes a lower pivot low while BTC makes a higher pivot low—also marks a bullish signal. Go short when a bearish MACD crossover occurs while price is below its trend baseline and lower-timeframe buy-volume share is weak; or when price sweeps above the prior pivot high, closes back below it, and buy-volume share is weak. Also go short when price reaches the prior-day high or projected daily upper range, forms a shooting star or bearish engulfing candle, and volume is unusually high, subject to the same cooldown. Bearish intermarket divergence—price makes a higher pivot high while BTC makes a lower pivot high—marks a bearish signal. No exit conditions are defined.
