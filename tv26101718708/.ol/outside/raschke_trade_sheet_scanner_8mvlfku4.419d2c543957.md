For each market, set an upward bias when the fast and slow momentum measures and short-term rate of change are all rising; set a downward bias when all are falling; otherwise remain neutral. In the bias direction, favor buying the first pullback after a fresh momentum extreme, especially when price also tests a channel extreme. After a prolonged run of closes on one side of a short moving average, treat the first close across it in the opposite direction as a reversal signal: buy after a run below, or sell after a run above; trend riders may use it to exit. Treat price compression as a breakout watch and let the breakout determine direction. These are directional setups and signals, not standalone entry rules.
