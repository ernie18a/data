Calculate Bitcoin’s valuation ratio as market price divided by the on-chain realized price (realized market capitalization divided by circulating supply). When the ratio crosses below the cost-basis threshold, signal a long/buy; crossing below a deeper capitulation threshold is a stronger buy signal. When it crosses above an overvaluation threshold, signal a sell/short; crossing above a higher euphoria threshold is a stronger sell signal. A move back above cost basis after being below it is a bottom-recovery confirmation. The indicator defines no exit rule.
