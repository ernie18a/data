On each completed bar, compare the traded market with a positively correlated market, using rolling prior-bar highs and lows as liquidity references. A bullish candidate occurs when exactly one market sweeps below its prior low and the sweeping market closes back above that reference; a bearish candidate mirrors this at prior highs. Require the chart candle to align directionally, meet preset-specific ATR-normalized sweep-depth, close-location, range-expansion, and quality-score criteria. Also require preset-specific minimum positive return correlation, favorable external-range location (bullish near the range low, bearish near the range high), sufficient divergence separation, and an available directional cooldown. An accepted candidate arms a temporary setup. Within its confirmation window, accumulate directional displacement, a close beyond the SMT candle’s opposite extreme, and the preset-required FVG and/or micro-structure-break evidence. Displacement requires a directional candle with sufficiently large ATR-relative body, strong body-to-range ratio, and limited opposing wick. Cancel the setup if price closes beyond its SMT extreme by the stored ATR buffer, if the window expires, or when replaced by a new accepted setup. Emit a long signal when a bullish setup remains valid and all required evidence is present; emit a short signal for the mirrored bearish setup. Require a confirmed bar and an available final-signal cooldown. For trade management, enter at the signal close, set a stop at an ATR-based distance and a take-profit at a fixed risk multiple, then exit when either level is reached; if both are touched on one bar, prioritize the stop.
