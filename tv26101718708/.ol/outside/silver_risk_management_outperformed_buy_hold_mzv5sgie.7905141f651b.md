Within the configured trading window, hold silver long by default when entry conditions are met; otherwise stay flat. Exit a long if silver closes below its slow trend average, a sharp downside move or bearish volatility shock occurs, or configured macro stress persists for consecutive bars. Macro stress occurs when an enabled external series (the dollar index or U.S. 10-year yield) is above its trend average by more than a threshold and breaks above its recent high. Re-enter only when silver is not below its slow trend average and enabled macro series have been calm for consecutive bars. After a recent flush, also wait until silver is above its recovery average and volatility has remained calm for consecutive bars. The strategy takes long positions only; it does not open shorts.
