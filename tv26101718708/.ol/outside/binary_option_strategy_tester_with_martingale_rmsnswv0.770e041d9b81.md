Compute a triple-smoothed close average and a two-stage close average. Build a signal line by smoothing rolling extrema of averages of the bar’s high, low, and close: use the higher of the two smoothed extrema when the first average is above the second, otherwise use the lower. Generate a CALL signal when the first average is above the second, the signal line is above the bar’s low, and its rise is accelerating. Generate a PUT signal when the first average is below the second, the signal line is below the bar’s high, and its fall is accelerating. Act only when the permitted direction is enabled; optionally require the signal to occur during the selected session. Enter at the next bar’s open, allow only one active trade, and ignore repeated signals until a new directional state begins. For a losing trade, optionally retry up to the configured limit using the selected rule: repeat the prior option direction, switch direction, follow the prior bar’s color, or take the opposite of the prior bar’s color. A CALL wins on an up bar and a PUT wins on a down bar.
