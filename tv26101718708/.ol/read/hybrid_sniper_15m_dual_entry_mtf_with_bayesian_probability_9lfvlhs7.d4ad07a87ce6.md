Calculation: 1H baseline = 20-period EMA of close on the last completed 1H bar
Calculation: Chart relative volume = chart volume / 20-bar simple average of chart volume
Calculation: Long stop = min(entry-bar low, previous completed 2H low) − chart ATR(14) × stop buffer; long target = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward/risk multiple
Calculation: Short stop = max(entry-bar high, previous completed 2H high) + chart ATR(14) × stop buffer; short target = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward/risk multiple
Long: flat AND during the configured New York session AND current forming 2H close > its 20-period EMA AND current forming 2H close > its open AND current forming 4H close > its 20-period EMA AND current forming 4H close > its open AND ((chart low < previous completed 2H low AND chart close > previous completed 2H low AND chart relative volume > its 20-bar average) OR (chart close > chart VWAP AND chart close > last completed 1H baseline AND chart relative volume > the trend-entry threshold AND chart close > chart open))
Short: flat AND during the configured New York session AND current forming 2H close < its 20-period EMA AND current forming 2H close < its open AND current forming 4H close < its 20-period EMA AND current forming 4H close < its open AND ((chart high > previous completed 2H high AND chart close < previous completed 2H high AND chart relative volume > its 20-bar average) OR (chart close < chart VWAP AND chart close < last completed 1H baseline AND chart relative volume > the trend-entry threshold AND chart close < chart open))
Exit: long, chart high ≥ long target AND long target > previous chart close OR chart low ≤ long target AND long target < previous chart close OR chart low ≤ long stop AND long stop < previous chart close OR chart high ≥ long stop AND long stop > previous chart close
Exit: short, chart high ≥ short target AND short target > previous chart close OR chart low ≤ short target AND short target < previous chart close OR chart low ≤ short stop AND short stop < previous chart close OR chart high ≥ short stop AND short stop > previous chart close
