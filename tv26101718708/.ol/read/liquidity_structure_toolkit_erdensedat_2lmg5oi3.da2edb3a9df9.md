Calculation: Active major BSL/SSL = confirmed pivot high/low of higher-timeframe highs/lows, using equal left and right lookbacks; a level remains active until swept, and a swept level is eligible only on its sweep bar.
Calculation: Fractal pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest among an equal number of bars before and after it, using a shorter lookback than the major pivots.
Calculation: Valid bullish/bearish candle = close > open / close < open AND abs(close - open) > 0.15 * (high - low).
Long: An active major SSL has low <= its level AND close > its level AND (the current candle is valid bullish OR it is the first valid bullish candle within the signal lookback after that touch); OR an active major SSL was touched (low <= its level) most recently, with BSL taking precedence if both were touched on the same bar, AND price breaks above the latest unbroken fractal pivot high (close > level when close-break mode is selected, otherwise high > level).
Short: An active major BSL has high >= its level AND close < its level AND (the current candle is valid bearish OR it is the first valid bearish candle within the signal lookback after that touch); OR an active major BSL was touched (high >= its level) most recently, with BSL taking precedence if both were touched on the same bar, AND price breaks below the latest unbroken fractal pivot low (close < level when close-break mode is selected, otherwise low < level).
