Calculation: Candle body percentage = 100 × abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low); use 0 when high = low
Calculation: Bullish pullback = at least one bar within the configured lookback, including the current bar, has low ≤ fast EMA + configured ATR distance OR low ≤ medium EMA + configured ATR distance
Calculation: Bearish pullback = at least one bar within the configured lookback, including the current bar, has high ≥ fast EMA − configured ATR distance OR high ≥ medium EMA − configured ATR distance
Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR ÷ its longer-lookback simple moving average; use 0 when the average is 0
Calculation: Bullish score = sum of 1 point for each true condition: fast EMA > medium EMA; both EMAs are rising (each current EMA is greater than at least one value in its configured prior-bar lookback); EMA 200 filter disabled or close > EMA 200; bullish pullback; close > open and candle body percentage ≥ configured minimum; RSI > configured bullish level; ADX ≥ configured minimum; +DI > −DI; ATR ratio is within configured minimum and maximum; higher-timeframe filter disabled or fast EMA > medium EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of 1 point for each true condition: fast EMA < medium EMA; both EMAs are falling (each current EMA is less than at least one value in its configured prior-bar lookback); EMA 200 filter disabled or close < EMA 200; bearish pullback; close < open and candle body percentage ≥ configured minimum; RSI < configured bearish level; ADX ≥ configured minimum; −DI > +DI; ATR ratio is within configured minimum and maximum; higher-timeframe filter disabled or fast EMA < medium EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Long: (strict mode enabled AND all ten bullish score conditions are true OR strict mode disabled AND bullish score ≥ configured minimum score) AND NOT (strict mode enabled AND all ten bearish score conditions are true OR strict mode disabled AND bearish score ≥ configured minimum score) AND within configured trading session if enabled AND outside configured blackout window if enabled AND (show-every-signal mode enabled OR (bars since last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND most recent bullish-pullback bar differs from the one used for the previous long signal))
Short: (strict mode enabled AND all ten bearish score conditions are true OR strict mode disabled AND bearish score ≥ configured minimum score) AND NOT (strict mode enabled AND all ten bullish score conditions are true OR strict mode disabled AND bullish score ≥ configured minimum score) AND within configured trading session if enabled AND outside configured blackout window if enabled AND (show-every-signal mode enabled OR (bars since last signal ≥ configured cooldown AND most recent bearish-pullback bar differs from the one used for the previous short signal))
