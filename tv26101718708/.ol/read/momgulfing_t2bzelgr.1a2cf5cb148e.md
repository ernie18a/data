Calculation: Long stop level = lowest low among the red candle and all green candles in the matching long pattern; on a new long signal while already long, add a configurable fraction of (signal close − this level)
Calculation: Long target level = signal close + configurable reward/risk multiple × (signal close − base long stop level); on a new long signal while already long, subtract a configurable fraction of (signal close − base long stop level)
Calculation: Short stop level = highest high among the green candle and all red candles in the matching short pattern; on a new short signal while already short, subtract a configurable fraction of (this level − signal close)
Calculation: Short target level = signal close − configurable reward/risk multiple × (base short stop level − signal close); on a new short signal while already short, add a configurable fraction of (base short stop level − signal close)
Long: datetime is within the configured date range AND ((previous candle is red AND current candle is green AND current close > previous red high AND current volume > previous red volume) OR (two candles ago is red AND previous candle and current candle are green AND previous red high > previous green close AND current close > previous red high AND current volume > two-candles-ago red volume) OR (three candles ago is red AND the next two candles and current candle are green AND three-candles-ago red high > each of the next two closes AND current close > three-candles-ago red high AND current volume > three-candles-ago red volume))
Short: datetime is within the configured date range AND ((previous candle is green AND current candle is red AND current close < previous green low AND current volume > previous green volume) OR (two candles ago is green AND previous candle and current candle are red AND two-candles-ago green low < previous red close AND current close < two-candles-ago green low AND current volume > two-candles-ago green volume) OR (three candles ago is green AND the next two candles and current candle are red AND three-candles-ago green low < each of the next two closes AND current close < three-candles-ago green low AND current volume > three-candles-ago green volume))
Exit: long, low <= long stop level OR high >= long target level
Exit: short, high >= short stop level OR low <= short target level
