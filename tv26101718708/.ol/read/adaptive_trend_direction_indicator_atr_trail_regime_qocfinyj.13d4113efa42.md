Calculation: ATR trailing stop = initialize direction as long and stop at close minus ATR × multiplier; while long, ratchet the stop to the greater of its prior value and close minus ATR × multiplier when close is above the prior stop, then flip short if close is below the stop and set stop to close plus ATR × multiplier; while short, ratchet to the lesser of its prior value and close plus ATR × multiplier when close is below the prior stop, then flip long if close is above the stop and set stop to close minus ATR × multiplier
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over the same lookback; use 0 if the denominator is 0
Calculation: Confirmed regime = switch from ranging to trending after the configured number of consecutive bars with ADX above its threshold AND efficiency ratio above its threshold; switch from trending to ranging after that many consecutive bars where either condition fails; otherwise retain the prior regime
Calculation: EMA slope = current EMA minus EMA from the configured slope lookback bars earlier
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of HLC3 since the selected anchor reset; volume-weighted standard deviation uses the same anchor and source
Long: confirmed regime is trending (or regime filter disabled) AND ATR trailing stop direction flips from short to long AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is above its long threshold) AND (VWAP side filter disabled OR close is above anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard disabled OR VWAP standard deviation is unavailable OR absolute distance from close to anchored VWAP divided by VWAP standard deviation is at most its limit) AND position is flat or short AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is on or after its start); OR confirmed regime is ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (VWAP mean-reversion mode replaces RSI and close is at or below anchored VWAP minus its configured standard-deviation multiple OR it confirms RSI and both RSI is below its oversold threshold and close is at or below that band OR it is off and RSI is below its oversold threshold) AND position is flat or short AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is on or after its start
Short: confirmed regime is trending (or regime filter disabled) AND ATR trailing stop direction flips from long to short AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is below its short threshold) AND (VWAP side filter disabled OR close is below anchored VWAP) AND (VWAP extension guard disabled OR VWAP standard deviation is unavailable OR absolute distance from close to anchored VWAP divided by VWAP standard deviation is at most its limit) AND shorting is allowed AND position is flat or long AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is on or after its start); OR confirmed regime is ranging AND mean-reversion mode is enabled AND (VWAP mean-reversion mode replaces RSI and close is at or above anchored VWAP plus its configured standard-deviation multiple OR it confirms RSI and both RSI is above its overbought threshold and close is at or above that band OR it is off and RSI is above its overbought threshold) AND shorting is allowed AND position is flat or long AND (date filter disabled OR datetime is on or after its start)
Exit: long, position is long and (the trade is a trend trade and ATR trailing stop direction flips from long to short OR EMA exit is enabled and bars held has reached the grace period and EMA slope is below 0 OR time exit is enabled and bars held reaches its limit OR mean-reversion mode is enabled and regime is ranging and the trade is a mean-reversion trade and (high reaches its take-profit level OR low reaches its stop-loss level OR RSI is above 50)); mean-reversion take-profit is entry price plus the configured ATR multiple captured at entry, or current anchored VWAP when VWAP take-profit is enabled and available; stop-loss is entry price minus the configured ATR multiple captured at entry
Exit: short, position is short and (the trade is a trend trade and ATR trailing stop direction flips from short to long OR EMA exit is enabled and bars held has reached the grace period and EMA slope is above 0 OR time exit is enabled and bars held reaches its limit OR mean-reversion mode is enabled and regime is ranging and the trade is a mean-reversion trade and (low reaches its take-profit level OR high reaches its stop-loss level OR RSI is below 50)); mean-reversion take-profit is entry price minus the configured ATR multiple captured at entry, or current anchored VWAP when VWAP take-profit is enabled and available; stop-loss is entry price plus the configured ATR multiple captured at entry
