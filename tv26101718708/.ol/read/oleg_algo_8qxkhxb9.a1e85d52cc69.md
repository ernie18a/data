Calculation: Baseline = selected SMA, EMA, or HMA of close, or daily-reset volume-weighted average of HLC3
Calculation: Entry unit = selected ATR, standard deviation of close, or daily-reset volume-weighted standard deviation of HLC3
Calculation: Lower band = Baseline − Entry unit × initial gap
Calculation: Upper band = Baseline + Entry unit × initial gap
Calculation: Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4
Calculation: Heikin Ashi open = previous Heikin Ashi open and close averaged; on the first bar, (open + close) / 2
Long: Flat, long trades allowed, bar confirmed, inside the configured trading window if that filter is enabled, and (in reversal mode, close ≤ Lower band for the configured consecutive-bar count; or in trending mode, close ≥ Upper band for that count); no long cooldown: either no prior exit, or (in reversal mode prior exit price > Lower band, or in trending mode prior exit price < Upper band, or close ≤ prior exit price − ATR × scale-in step)
Long: Long position open, bar confirmed, inside the configured trading window if enabled, and close ≤ last long entry price − ATR × scale-in step; if Heikin Ashi confirmation is enabled, require this distance to have armed the scale and a bullish Heikin Ashi flip
Short: Flat, short trades allowed, bar confirmed, inside the configured trading window if that filter is enabled, and (in reversal mode, close ≥ Upper band for the configured consecutive-bar count; or in trending mode, close ≤ Lower band for that count); no short cooldown: either no prior exit, or (in reversal mode prior exit price < Upper band, or in trending mode prior exit price > Lower band, or close ≥ prior exit price + ATR × scale-in step)
Short: Short position open, bar confirmed, inside the configured trading window if enabled, and close ≥ last short entry price + ATR × scale-in step; if Heikin Ashi confirmation is enabled, require this distance to have armed the scale and a bearish Heikin Ashi flip
Exit: Long, low ≤ initial entry price − ATR × stop-loss multiple, or high ≥ average entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple; if break-even trailing is enabled, activate trailing at the take-profit level, then exit when low ≤ the active stop, which is the greater of average entry price and the highest since activation minus ATR × take-profit multiple, ratcheted upward
Exit: Short, high ≥ initial entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiple, or low ≤ average entry price − ATR × take-profit multiple; if break-even trailing is enabled, activate trailing at the take-profit level, then exit when high ≥ the active stop, which is the lesser of average entry price and the lowest since activation plus ATR × take-profit multiple, ratcheted downward
Exit: Long or short, configured session window is closed and prop mode is enabled
