Calculation: Rolling resistance = highest high over the lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Rolling support = lowest low over the lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Long: shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND close > rolling resistance AND close > open AND volume > volume SMA × volume threshold multiplier
Short: shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND close < rolling support AND close < open AND volume > volume SMA × volume threshold multiplier
Exit: long, low <= close − ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: short, high >= close + ATR × stop multiplier
