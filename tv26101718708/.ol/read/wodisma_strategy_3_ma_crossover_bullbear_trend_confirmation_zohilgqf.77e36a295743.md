Calculation: Volatility = 100 × (highest close over the lookback / lowest close over the same lookback − 1)
Calculation: Short-, mid-, long-term, and bull-bear moving averages = independently configured moving averages of selected price sources and lengths, each on its configured timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe); use the current requested-timeframe value without a completed-bar offset, so a higher-timeframe value may still be forming
Long: long entries allowed AND Volatility > threshold AND (bull-bear filter disabled OR long-term moving average > bull-bear moving average) AND short-term moving average > long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses above long-term moving average
Short: short entries allowed AND Volatility > threshold AND (bull-bear filter disabled OR long-term moving average < bull-bear moving average) AND short-term moving average < long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses below long-term moving average
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR bull-bear filter enabled AND long-term moving average < bull-bear moving average OR bull-bear filter enabled AND long-term moving average > bull-bear moving average AND short-term moving average < long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses below long-term moving average AND Volatility > threshold OR bull-bear filter disabled AND NOT (both long and short entries allowed) AND short-term moving average < long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses below long-term moving average AND Volatility > threshold
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR bull-bear filter enabled AND long-term moving average > bull-bear moving average OR bull-bear filter enabled AND long-term moving average < bull-bear moving average AND short-term moving average > long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses above long-term moving average AND Volatility > threshold OR bull-bear filter disabled AND NOT (both long and short entries allowed) AND short-term moving average > long-term moving average AND mid-term moving average crosses above long-term moving average AND Volatility > threshold
