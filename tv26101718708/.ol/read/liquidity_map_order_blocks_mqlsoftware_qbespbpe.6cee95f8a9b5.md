Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the configured right-side bars have completed on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Structure state = bullish, bearish, or neutral, updated on each confirmed swing: in a bullish state, a higher swing high records an upward break and protects the latest swing low; in a bearish state, a lower swing low records a downward break and protects the latest swing high. A swing high above the protected high changes bearish to bullish; a swing low below the protected low changes bullish to bearish. In neutral, a swing high above the last swing high changes to bullish, and a swing low below the last swing low changes to bearish.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure state = the same structure state calculated on each configured higher timeframe, read from its last completed bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = at least the configured minimum number of confirmed swing highs or lows within the configured lookback, clustered separately by side when adjacent prices differ by no more than the configured ATR fraction; its level is the mean pivot price. Its score is the product of a saturating touch-count factor, age half-life decay from the oldest pivot, relative pivot volume (or 1 when disabled/unavailable), and average rejection-wick factor, scaled by 100. A pool is swept when a later matching-side sweep is within the cluster distance; a high sweep requires high above the last swing high plus the configured ATR penetration and close below it, and a low sweep requires low below the last swing low minus that penetration and close above it.
Calculation: Draw target = unswept pool with greatest pull, where pull is pool score times exp(−distance-in-ATR-units / proximity parameter) times a structural factor (1 with neutral structure, otherwise 1 plus the configured prior when the pool lies in the structure direction, or 1 minus it otherwise) times path clearance. Path clearance is the product, over other unswept pools strictly between close and the candidate, of (1 − configured clearance parameter × other pool score / 100).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = on an upward/downward BOS, respectively, the first prior opposite-color candle found within the configured scan window, with zone from its low to high.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair-value gap = on an upward/downward BOS, respectively, low > high two bars earlier / high < low two bars earlier; the gap zone is between those prices.
Long: confirmed swing high above the applicable structure reference (last swing high when bullish or neutral; protected high when bearish) OR all configured higher-timeframe structure states are bullish OR draw target exists above close OR bullish order block is created OR bullish fair-value gap is created.
Short: confirmed swing low below the applicable structure reference (last swing low when bearish or neutral; protected low when bullish) OR all configured higher-timeframe structure states are bearish OR draw target exists below close OR bearish order block is created OR bearish fair-value gap is created.
