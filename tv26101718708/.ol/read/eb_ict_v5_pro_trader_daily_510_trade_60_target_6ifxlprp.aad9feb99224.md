Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = last completed bar's close > its 50-bar EMA; bearish bias = close < its 50-bar EMA
Calculation: Bullish swing sweep = low < most recent confirmed pivot low AND close > that pivot low; bearish swing sweep = high > most recent confirmed pivot high AND close < that pivot high
Calculation: Bullish market structure shift = close > most recent confirmed pivot high; bearish market structure shift = close < most recent confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND absolute body size > its 20-bar average multiplied by a threshold; bearish displacement = close < open AND absolute body size > its 20-bar average multiplied by a threshold
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars ago; bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars ago
Calculation: Bullish gap retest = low <= the latest bullish gap's upper bound AND close > its lower bound; bearish gap retest = high >= the latest bearish gap's lower bound AND close < its upper bound
Calculation: Range position = (close - lowest low over a lookback) / max(highest high over that lookback - lowest low, minimum price increment) * 100
Calculation: Buy score = weighted sum of bullish higher-timeframe bias, bullish swing sweep, bullish displacement, bullish market structure shift, bullish gap retest (otherwise bullish gap), discount position, and session membership
Calculation: Sell score = weighted sum of bearish higher-timeframe bias, bearish swing sweep, bearish displacement, bearish market structure shift, bearish gap retest (otherwise bearish gap), premium position, and session membership
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND close > prior close; bearish rejection = close < open AND close < prior close
Calculation: Trend buy = shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA; trend sell = shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA
Long: bullish swing sweep OR bullish market structure shift OR (buy score >= minimum score AND session membership AND ATR >= its 50-bar average multiplied by a threshold when the ATR filter is enabled AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade limit not reached AND position is flat AND close - lowest low over a short lookback > minimum price increment AND ((bullish higher-timeframe bias AND discount position below its threshold AND bullish swing sweep AND bullish rejection) OR (bullish higher-timeframe bias AND trend buy AND discount position below its threshold AND bullish gap retest AND bullish rejection) OR (bullish higher-timeframe bias AND trend buy AND discount position below its threshold AND bullish displacement AND close > shorter-lookback EMA)))
Short: bearish swing sweep OR bearish market structure shift OR (sell score >= minimum score AND session membership AND ATR >= its 50-bar average multiplied by a threshold when the ATR filter is enabled AND cooldown elapsed AND daily trade limit not reached AND position is flat AND highest high over a short lookback - close > minimum price increment AND ((bearish higher-timeframe bias AND premium position above its threshold AND bearish swing sweep AND bearish rejection) OR (bearish higher-timeframe bias AND trend sell AND premium position above its threshold AND bearish gap retest AND bearish rejection) OR (bearish higher-timeframe bias AND trend sell AND premium position above its threshold AND bearish displacement AND close < shorter-lookback EMA)))
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target
