Calculation: Relative strength = (stock close / stock close N bars ago) / (index close / index close N bars ago) − 1, using equal lookbacks and chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Long stop = alert-candle low at entry; on a hard RSI breakdown or eligible weakness signal, raise it to the current bar’s low only if that low is higher. On a scale-in, keep the higher of the existing stop and the new alert-candle low.
Calculation: Crash level = long stop × (1 − crash-buffer percentage / 100).
Long: Create or reset a long alert when chart-timeframe RSI crosses above 60 while weekly RSI > 60, OR weekly RSI crosses above 60 while chart-timeframe RSI > 60; monthly RSI is unavailable or > 60; relative strength > 0; and, when enabled, index close > index EMA. Weekly and monthly RSI use the current still-forming higher-timeframe bar. Within the confirmation window after the alert, enter when current high > alert-candle high, the initial risk percentage ((current high − alert-candle low) / current high × 100) is within its maximum, and the position is flat or a partial exit has occurred without a prior scale-in. A new alert resets the prior alert.
Exit: Long, low <= crash level.
Exit: Long, after a bar closes below the long stop, low <= that breakdown bar’s low while the confirmation is pending; cancel the pending confirmation if a later close is above the long stop. The initial stop is the alert-candle low; weakness updates it only upward when chart-timeframe RSI < 50, or when RSI < 60 and relative strength < 0 while RSI is outside the support zone from 50 through 50 plus the support buffer.
Exit: Long, weekly RSI >= the overbought threshold AND close < the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND no partial exit has already been taken (exit half the position).
