Calculation: Trend regime = strong trend when slow efficiency ratio >= its strong-trend floor and ADX >= its strong-trend floor and rising; otherwise trend when slow efficiency ratio >= its trend floor and ADX >= its trend floor; otherwise non-trending. A strong-trend state persists until ADX falls below its strong floor by a margin and slow efficiency ratio falls below its strong floor by a margin. Slow efficiency-ratio lookback is longer than fast; only slow efficiency ratio gates the trend states.
Calculation: Confirmed swing flip = track rolling highest high and lowest low; set swing trend bullish when a bar makes the rolling high and bearish when it makes the rolling low; a flip occurs when that state changes. Confirm swing pivots when the prior bar was the rolling extreme and the current bar no longer is. Use the confirmed pivot in the direction of the reversal.
Calculation: Major-level proximity = close is within a volatility-scaled distance of any session, weekly, or monthly VWAP; volume-profile point of control, value-area high, or value-area low; swing-anchored VWAP using the medium or long swing lookback; or prior day/week high or low. The long swing lookback exceeds the medium, which exceeds the short.
Calculation: Flip grade = score the nearby level (highest for monthly VWAP, point of control, or long-swing VWAP; next for value-area bounds or weekly VWAP; next for medium-swing VWAP or prior-day extremes; lowest for session VWAP), pivot-leg extension, relative volume, recent matching flips on two lower timeframes, and pivot-leg speed; map the total score to descending S, A, B, C, D tiers. Pivot-leg extension is measured in ATR units from session VWAP at the pivot; relative volume is current volume divided by its moving average. Both lower timeframes are below the chart timeframe; use their completed bars. Require the pivot-leg extension to meet its minimum.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when close is above its EMA and the EMA is non-falling; bearish when close is below its EMA and the EMA is non-rising; otherwise neutral. Use two higher timeframes, with the second longer than the first, and their last completed bars.
Calculation: Signed flow direction = cumulative signed volume is at or above its EMA for bullish flow, otherwise bearish. Estimate signed volume from lower-timeframe candle close location within each candle, volume-weighted; use completed lower-timeframe bars, falling back to a return-based volume estimate when unavailable. The flow timeframe is below the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Toxic flow = moving average of absolute estimated buy/sell volume imbalance divided by moving average of total volume reaches its toxicity threshold.
Calculation: Cross-timeframe flow alignment = sum the bullish/neutral/bearish signs of robust-z candle-geometry buying pressure on the chart timeframe and two higher timeframes; the higher timeframes are longer than the chart timeframe and use their last completed bars. A sign is bullish or bearish only when its z-score exceeds the neutral threshold in magnitude.
Calculation: Qualified bullish hull break = a confirmed close above the rising upper convex-hull edge of recent confirmed pivot highs by a volatility-scaled buffer, with minimum displacement, body size, and close location near the bar high; the edge slope is within its permitted range and has the required number of touches. Qualified bearish hull break is the mirror condition below the falling lower convex-hull edge of confirmed pivot lows, with close near the bar low.
Calculation: Failed hull break = after a qualified bullish break, close back below its break level by the volatility-scaled buffer within the failure window; after a qualified bearish break, close back above its break level by that buffer within the window.
Long: confirmed bullish swing flip AND (major-level proximity OR flip grade is S or A) AND flip grade meets the configured minimum AND pivot-leg extension meets its minimum AND not strong-trend regime AND not killed by an extreme ATR expansion, a prohibited intraday session, or sharply rising ADX AND NOT (toxic flow AND bearish signed flow) AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bearish AND global signal cooldown has elapsed; OR failed bearish hull break AND non-strong-trend regime AND sufficient lower-edge touches AND not toxic flow AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bearish AND no recent bearish swing flip within the signal cooldown AND global signal cooldown has elapsed; OR qualified bullish hull break AND trending regime AND sufficient upper-edge touches AND higher-timeframe bias sum is bullish when that filter is required AND not toxic flow AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bearish AND no recent bearish swing flip within the signal cooldown AND global signal cooldown has elapsed. For continuation, use either failed-break reversal mode or direct-break mode as configured; direct-break mode additionally requires that price is not leading flow when that filter is enabled.
Short: confirmed bearish swing flip AND (major-level proximity OR flip grade is S or A) AND flip grade meets the configured minimum AND pivot-leg extension meets its minimum AND not strong-trend regime AND not killed by an extreme ATR expansion, a prohibited intraday session, or sharply rising ADX AND NOT (toxic flow AND bullish signed flow) AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bullish AND global signal cooldown has elapsed; OR failed bullish hull break AND non-strong-trend regime AND sufficient upper-edge touches AND not toxic flow AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bullish AND no recent bullish swing flip within the signal cooldown AND global signal cooldown has elapsed; OR qualified bearish hull break AND trending regime AND sufficient lower-edge touches AND higher-timeframe bias sum is bearish when that filter is required AND not toxic flow AND cross-timeframe flow alignment is not strongly bullish AND no recent bullish swing flip within the signal cooldown AND global signal cooldown has elapsed. For continuation, use either failed-break reversal mode or direct-break mode as configured; direct-break mode additionally requires that price is not leading flow when that filter is enabled.
Exit: long, low <= latched stop price OR high >= latched second-target price
Exit: short, high >= latched stop price OR low <= latched second-target price
