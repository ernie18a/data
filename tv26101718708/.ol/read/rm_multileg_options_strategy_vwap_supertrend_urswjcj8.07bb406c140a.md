Calculation: Supertrend trend = initialize trend to up; calculate ATR from chart-timeframe OHLC, basic bands as (selected source high + selected source low) / 2 ± factor × ATR, and carry forward the upper band unless the basic upper band is lower or the previous selected-source close is above the previous upper band; carry forward the lower band unless the basic lower band is higher or the previous selected-source close is below the previous lower band; flip down to up when the previous trend is down and the current selected-source close exceeds the current upper band, and flip up to down when the previous trend is up and the current selected-source close falls below the current lower band. The selected source is synthetic strategy OHLC or alternate source OHLC, as configured. All values use chart-timeframe bars, including the current bar.

Short: Supertrend trend changes from up to down
