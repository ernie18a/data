Calculation: Average volume = mean volume over a lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Volume spread = volume × (high − low)
Calculation: Highest prior volume spread = maximum volume spread over the same lookback as average volume, excluding the current bar
Long: close > open AND (volume >= a multiple of average volume OR volume spread >= highest prior volume spread)
Short: close <= open AND (volume >= a multiple of average volume OR volume spread >= highest prior volume spread)
