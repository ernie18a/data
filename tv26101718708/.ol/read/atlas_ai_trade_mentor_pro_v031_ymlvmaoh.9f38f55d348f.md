Calculation: Trend score = add points for EMA(20)>EMA(50), EMA(50)>EMA(200), close above each of EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200), and each EMA rising over its lookback; add volatility-adjusted separation points when the absolute EMA(20)–EMA(50) gap exceeds 0.30 ATR and the EMA(50)–EMA(200) gap exceeds 0.60 ATR; cap at 100. The EMA lookbacks are short-, medium-, and long-term, respectively.
Calculation: Structure score = +10 for higher high, +10 for higher low, −10 for lower high, and −10 for lower low, capped between −20 and 20. Compare the latest confirmed pivot high with the preceding confirmed pivot high, and likewise for pivot lows.
Calculation: Long score = capped at 100: trend points (25/20/15/10/0 as trend score is >=80/>=70/>=60/>=50/otherwise) + positive structure score +15 for bullish break of structure +15 for bullish change of character +5 for each of close above EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200) + momentum points.
Calculation: Short score = capped at 100: trend points (25/20/15/10/0 as trend score is <=20/<=30/<=40/<=50/otherwise) + absolute value of negative structure score +15 for bearish break of structure +15 for bearish change of character +5 for each of close below EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200) + momentum points.
Calculation: Momentum points = 15 when RSI is above 50 for long or below 50 for short, plus 15 when MACD line is above signal line and histogram positive for long, or below signal line and histogram negative for short; add volatility points (20 if ATR/close as a percentage >1.5, 10 if >0.8, otherwise 5) and 10 if volume exceeds its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = close above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish break of structure = close below the latest confirmed pivot low. A pivot uses the same lookback on each side and is confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish change of character = lower low AND close above EMA(20) AND EMA(20) rising; bearish change of character = higher high AND close below EMA(20) AND EMA(20) falling.
Calculation: Initial stop = entry price adjusted adversely by the stop-loss percentage; target 1 and target 2 = entry price adjusted favorably by their respective percentages. For long, trailing stop = greatest of prior trailing stop and close − ATR × trailing multiplier; for short, trailing stop = least of prior trailing stop and close + ATR × trailing multiplier. Once close reaches the break-even trigger percentage in favor, set stop to entry price. Active stop is trailing stop when trailing is enabled and initialized, otherwise the stop.
Long: (close > EMA(20) AND EMA(20) > EMA(50) AND EMA(50) > EMA(200) AND RSI > 50 AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND ADX > 20 AND long score >= minimum signal score) OR long score >= 70.
Short: (close < EMA(20) AND EMA(20) < EMA(50) AND EMA(50) < EMA(200) AND RSI < 50 AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND ADX > 20 AND short score >= minimum signal score) OR (long score < 70 AND short score >= 70).
Exit: long, close >= target 1 OR close >= target 2 OR close <= active stop.
Exit: short, close <= target 1 OR close <= target 2 OR close >= active stop.
