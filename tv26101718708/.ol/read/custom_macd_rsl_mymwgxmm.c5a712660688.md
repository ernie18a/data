Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA of close minus longer-lookback EMA of close
Calculation: MACD signal line = shorter-lookback EMA of MACD
Calculation: Adaptive neutral zone = 5 when the daily close is from 20000 through 50000 inclusive, 10 when it is above 50000, and otherwise daily close divided by 500; use the higher-timeframe daily bar still forming
Long: MACD crosses above MACD signal line AND MACD is greater than negative adaptive neutral zone
Short: MACD crosses below MACD signal line AND MACD is less than adaptive neutral zone
