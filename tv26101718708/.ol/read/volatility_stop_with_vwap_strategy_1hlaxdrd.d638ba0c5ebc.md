Calculation: Volatility stop = a stateful ATR trail: use close as the source and ATR (RMA of true range) times a factor as the offset; while rising, trail at the greater of the prior stop and the highest source since the last reversal minus the offset; while falling, trail at the lesser of the prior stop and the lowest source since the last reversal plus the offset. Set rising when close is at or above the stop; on reversal, reset the extrema to close and the stop to close minus the offset when rising or close plus the offset when falling.
Long: rising volatility-stop trend AND close > volatility stop AND close > weekly VWAP from the higher-timeframe bar still forming AND no long position is open AND the same long condition was false on the prior bar.
Short: falling volatility-stop trend AND close < volatility stop AND close < weekly VWAP from the higher-timeframe bar still forming AND no short position is open AND the same short condition was false on the prior bar.
Exit: Long, short condition OR close crosses above R OR high crosses above R OR close crosses below R OR low crosses above R, where R = 2 × entry-bar close − entry-bar volatility stop.
Exit: Short, long condition OR close crosses below R OR low crosses below R OR close crosses above R OR high crosses above R, where R = 2 × entry-bar close − entry-bar volatility stop.
