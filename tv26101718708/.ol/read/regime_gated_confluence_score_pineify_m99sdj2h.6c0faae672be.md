Calculation: Trend factor = clamp(0.65 × (shorter-lookback EMA − longer-lookback EMA) / ATR + 0.35 × (shorter-lookback EMA − its value n bars earlier) / (ATR × √n), −1, 1); the shorter EMA length is less than the longer EMA length.
Calculation: Path efficiency = clamp(|close − close n bars earlier| / (n × SMA of absolute one-bar close changes over n bars), 0, 1).
Calculation: Volume factor = clamp(EMA of [clamp((2 × close − high − low) / (high − low), −1, 1) × clamp(volume / volume baseline, 0, 2.5) / 2.5], −1, 1), where volume baseline is the SMA over n bars of volume when volume > 0 and zero otherwise; use this factor only when at least 80% of those bars have volume > 0 and the baseline is positive, otherwise omit volume and renormalize the other weights.
Calculation: Regime = VOLATILE if ATR / its longer-lookback SMA is at least the volatility entry threshold, or if the prior regime was VOLATILE and the ratio is above its lower hold threshold; otherwise TREND if absolute EMA spread / ATR and path efficiency both meet their entry thresholds, or if the prior regime was TREND and both meet their lower hold thresholds; otherwise RANGE if either measure is at or below its maximum threshold; otherwise TRANSITION. Require sufficient valid data and shorter EMA length < longer EMA length.
Calculation: Score = 100 × (weighted sum of trend factor, momentum factor, and volume factor) × (0.55 + 0.45 × agreement) × volatility gate. Momentum factor = clamp((RSI − 50) / 25, −1, 1), inverted in RANGE. Use trend/momentum/volume weights TREND 55/30/15, RANGE 15/60/25, VOLATILE 40/35/25, TRANSITION 35/40/25; renormalize weights over included factors. Agreement = |weighted sum before gating| / sum of absolute weighted factors. Volatility gate = clamp(volatility entry threshold / (ATR / its longer-lookback SMA), 0.45, 1) in VOLATILE, otherwise 1.
Long: Score crosses above the positive directional threshold on a confirmed bar.
Short: Score crosses below the negative directional threshold on a confirmed bar.
