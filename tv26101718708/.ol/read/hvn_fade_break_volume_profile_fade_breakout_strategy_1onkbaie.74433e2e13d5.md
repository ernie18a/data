Calculation: Rolling profile = At each new higher-timeframe session, use chart-timeframe bars from the last completed sessions; divide their total low-to-high range into equal-width rows, distribute each bar’s volume equally across the rows touched by its low-to-high range, and calculate POC as the midpoint of the highest-volume row. Expand the value area from the POC toward the adjacent row with greater volume (ties upward) until it contains the configured share of total volume; its outer row boundaries are VAH and VAL. Freeze these levels until the next session.
Calculation: HVN zones = In the rolling profile, identify local maximum rows above a configured share of the tallest row, comparing rows within a configured window and resolving equal-volume peaks in favor of the lower row. Keep peaks whose prominence (peak volume minus the greater of the minimum volumes on either side, each side searched until a higher row) meets a configured share of peak volume. Extend each zone across contiguous rows whose volume is at least peak volume minus a configured share of prominence; retain the configured number of highest-volume zones. A zone’s price is its midpoint.
Calculation: Naked POCs = The POC calculated from each completed higher-timeframe session alone; retain it until a chart bar’s high-low range includes its price.
Calculation: RSI = Standard RSI of chart-timeframe closes.
Calculation: Average volume = Simple moving average of chart-timeframe volume.
Calculation: ATR = Standard ATR of chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = Most recent pivot high/low confirmed using equal configured numbers of chart bars on both sides.
Calculation: Long stop = For a breakout, the lower of VAH minus configured ATR multiple and the latest confirmed swing low; if no swing low exists, use VAH minus the ATR distance. For a fade, the lower of signal-bar low minus half the configured ATR distance and the latest confirmed swing low; if no swing low exists, use signal-bar low minus half the ATR distance.
Calculation: Short stop = For a breakout, the higher of VAL plus configured ATR multiple and the latest confirmed swing high; if no swing high exists, use VAL plus the ATR distance. For a fade, the higher of signal-bar high plus half the configured ATR distance and the latest confirmed swing high; if no swing high exists, use signal-bar high plus half the ATR distance.
Calculation: Breakout target = Nearest HVN midpoint or untraded naked POC beyond signal-bar close, whichever is nearer; if neither exists, use signal-bar close plus/minus configured risk multiple times the distance from signal-bar close to the stop, above for long and below for short. Naked POCs are considered only when enabled.
Long: Profile levels exist AND ( (previous bar high > VAH AND close < VAH AND close > previous close AND RSI <= oversold threshold) OR (previous close <= VAH AND close > VAH AND volume > average volume × configured multiplier) ) AND (single-position restriction disabled OR flat) AND signal-bar close > long stop.
Short: Profile levels exist AND ( (previous bar low < VAL AND close > VAL AND close < previous close AND RSI >= overbought threshold) OR (previous close >= VAL AND close < VAL AND volume > average volume × configured multiplier) ) AND (single-position restriction disabled OR flat) AND signal-bar close < short stop.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= (profile POC for fade; breakout target for breakout).
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= (profile POC for fade; breakout target for breakout).
