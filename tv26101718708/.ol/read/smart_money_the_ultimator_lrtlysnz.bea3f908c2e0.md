Calculation: Average True Range = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Relative effort = current volume divided by its moving average; when volume is zero or unavailable, use bar range in place of volume.
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 when the bar range is zero.
Calculation: Bullish campaign = an active accumulation or re-accumulation structure; bearish campaign = an active distribution or re-distribution structure. Campaign structure is seeded by a climax after the corresponding prior trend, then requires absorption, an automatic rally/reaction, a secondary test, and a mature trading range. A spring/UTAD locks the campaign direction; a quiet terminal test may lock it when the range is mature and no probe occurs.
Calculation: Spring = in a bullish campaign, low penetrates below range support by between the minimum and maximum allowed ATR multiples, close returns inside the range, and at least two of these hold: close position is at least 0.5, relative effort is at most its limit, close is above open, or bar range is at most its ATR multiple.
Calculation: UTAD = in a bearish campaign, high penetrates above range resistance by between the minimum and maximum allowed ATR multiples, close returns inside the range, and at least two of these hold: close position is at most 0.5, relative effort is at most its limit, close is below open, or bar range is at most its ATR multiple.
Calculation: Sign of Strength/Weakness = after a direction is locked and the required bars have elapsed, validation and maturity meet their thresholds, and either close position favors the campaign direction or close breaks the corresponding range edge; additionally, the bar has sufficient spread and relative effort, or the move from several bars earlier exceeds an ATR multiple, with relative effort above its floor.
Calculation: Last Point of Support/Supply = during Phase D, at least the required bars have elapsed since Strength/Weakness, price is near and holds the broken range edge, and relative effort and spread are below their limits.
Calculation: Entry readiness = count of satisfied conditions: direction locked; Phase C or later; quiet retest completed; Strength/Weakness formed; Last Point formed; maturity meets the selected entry-mode threshold; validation meets its threshold; close is near the range midpoint; and a meaningful event occurred recently.
Long: bullish campaign AND (Spring OR Last Point of Support OR Sign of Strength when no Spring/UTAD occurred) [the last alternative applies only in the most aggressive entry mode]; OR bullish campaign AND entry readiness meets its minimum AND maturity meets the selected threshold AND (entry mode is conservative and Last Point of Support forms now OR entry mode is standard and Spring or Last Point of Support forms now OR entry mode is aggressive and Spring forms now or Sign of Strength forms now without a Spring/UTAD).
Short: bearish campaign AND (UTAD OR Last Point of Supply OR Sign of Weakness when no Spring/UTAD occurred) [the last alternative applies only in the most aggressive entry mode]; OR bearish campaign AND entry readiness meets its minimum AND maturity meets the selected threshold AND (entry mode is conservative and Last Point of Supply forms now OR entry mode is standard and UTAD or Last Point of Supply forms now OR entry mode is aggressive and UTAD forms now or Sign of Weakness forms now without a Spring/UTAD).
