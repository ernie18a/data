Calculation: ATR = Wilder average of true range over the ATR lookback
Calculation: Bullish readiness score = round(clamp(0,100, bullish wick score + bullish close-location score + bullish reference score + volume score + bullish follow-through score)); bullish wick score = clamp(0,30, (lower-wick/body ratio ÷ (minimum wick ratio × 2.20)) × 30); bullish close-location score = clamp(0,20, ((close location − 0.45) ÷ 0.55) × 20); bullish reference score = 18 if low is within 0.35 ATR above the lowest low of the reference lookback on prior bars, else 10 if low is below the lowest low of the shorter lookback on prior bars plus 0.30 ATR, else 4; volume score = clamp(0,15, ((current volume ÷ volume moving average) − 0.80) ÷ 1.20 × 15); bullish follow-through score = 17 if close > open, else 10 if close > prior close, else 4
Calculation: Bearish readiness score = round(clamp(0,100, bearish wick score + bearish close-location score + bearish reference score + volume score + bearish follow-through score)); bearish wick score = clamp(0,30, (upper-wick/body ratio ÷ (minimum wick ratio × 2.20)) × 30); bearish close-location score = clamp(0,20, ((0.55 − close location) ÷ 0.55) × 20); bearish reference score = 18 if high is within 0.35 ATR below the highest high of the reference lookback on prior bars, else 10 if high is above the highest high of the shorter lookback on prior bars minus 0.30 ATR, else 4; volume score = clamp(0,15, ((current volume ÷ volume moving average) − 0.80) ÷ 1.20 × 15); bearish follow-through score = 17 if close < open, else 10 if close < prior close, else 4
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) ÷ (high − low)
Calculation: Upper-wick/body ratio = (high − max(open, close)) ÷ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment); lower-wick/body ratio = (min(open, close) − low) ÷ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment)
Calculation: Upper-wick share = (high − max(open, close)) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment); lower-wick share = (min(open, close) − low) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Confirmation rail = bullish setup high + 0.05 ATR, or bearish setup low − 0.05 ATR
Calculation: Invalidation rail = bullish setup low − ATR × invalidation buffer, or bearish setup high + ATR × invalidation buffer
Calculation: Target review rail = setup close + (setup close − invalidation rail) × target multiple for bullish setups, or setup close − (invalidation rail − setup close) × target multiple for bearish setups
Long: lower-wick/body ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND lower-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close location ≥ minimum close location AND lower wick > upper wick AND bullish readiness score ≥ minimum readiness score OR active bullish setup AND confirmation condition is met (strict: close > confirmation rail; balanced: close > setup candle body top AND close > open; fast: high ≥ confirmation rail AND close > setup candle body top)
Short: upper-wick/body ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND upper-wick share ≥ minimum wick share AND close location ≤ 1 − minimum close location AND upper wick > lower wick AND bearish readiness score ≥ minimum readiness score OR active bearish setup AND confirmation condition is met (strict: close < confirmation rail; balanced: close < setup candle body bottom AND close < open; fast: low ≤ confirmation rail AND close < setup candle body bottom)
Exit: long, close < invalidation rail OR high ≥ target review rail
Exit: short, close > invalidation rail OR low ≤ target review rail
