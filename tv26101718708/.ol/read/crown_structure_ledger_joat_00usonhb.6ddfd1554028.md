Calculation: Active short, intermediate, and primary high/low rails = the latest confirmed pivot high/low at each respective horizon; each pivot is confirmed after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Adaptive ATR corridor width = ATR × corridor multiplier × max(floor, EMA of ATR / SMA of ATR); when adaptive depth is disabled, use ATR × corridor multiplier. Use the same ATR lookback for ATR and SMA; the EMA smooths the ratio.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND (close > active short high rail + corridor width × short allowance OR close > active intermediate high rail + corridor width × intermediate allowance OR close > active primary high rail + corridor width × primary allowance); allowances increase from short to intermediate to primary.
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND (close < active short low rail − corridor width × short allowance OR close < active intermediate low rail − corridor width × intermediate allowance OR close < active primary low rail − corridor width × primary allowance); allowances increase from short to intermediate to primary.
