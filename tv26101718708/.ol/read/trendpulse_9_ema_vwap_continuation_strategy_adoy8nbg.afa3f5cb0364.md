Calculation: Basis = EMA of (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Upper and lower ATR channels = Basis ± ATR × channel multiplier
Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = EMAs of close, with the fast EMA using a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish bias = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > Basis
Calculation: Bearish bias = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < Basis
Calculation: Chop = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the regime lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(regime lookback)
Calculation: ATR expansion ratio = current ATR / simple moving average of ATR over the regime lookback
Calculation: Regime is Trend when Chop < chop threshold AND |Basis − Basis from slope lookback bars ago| / ATR > 0.15 AND (fraction of closes above Basis over the slope lookback > 0.65 OR fraction below Basis > 0.65); otherwise Expand when ATR expansion ratio > expansion threshold AND Chop < chop threshold + 5; otherwise Quiet when Chop ≥ chop threshold AND ATR expansion ratio < 1; otherwise Noisy
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Average dollar volume = simple moving average of (close × volume) over the dollar-volume lookback
Calculation: Long score = 100 × (0.18 × scale(relative volume, 0.8, 2.2) + 0.18 × scale((close − upper channel) / ATR, −0.2, 1.5) + 0.18 × scale(5-bar rate of change, −1, 3.5) + 0.14 × scale(|close − open| / ATR, 0.1, 1.2) + 0.12 × scale((fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR, −0.1, 1.2) + 0.20 × regime weight); short score uses (lower channel − close) / ATR, the negated 5-bar rate of change, and (slow EMA − fast EMA) / ATR in the corresponding terms. Regime weight is 1 for Trend, 0.75 for Expand, 0.20 for Quiet, and 0.40 for Noisy. Scale(x, a, b) = max(0, min(1, (x − a) / (b − a))).
Calculation: Pullback seen for long = lowest low over pullback lookback ≤ fast EMA + ATR × pullback-zone multiplier OR ≤ Basis + ATR × pullback-zone multiplier; for short use highest high ≥ fast EMA − ATR × pullback-zone multiplier OR ≥ Basis − ATR × pullback-zone multiplier
Calculation: Initial long risk = close − (min(low, Basis) − ATR × stop multiplier); initial short risk = (max(high, Basis) + ATR × stop multiplier) − close
Long: within date window AND cooldown bars since last entry exceeded AND long entries allowed AND bullish bias AND (dollar-volume filter disabled OR average dollar volume ≥ minimum) AND (benchmark filter disabled OR benchmark close > benchmark EMA) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum) AND long score ≥ minimum AND ((breakout mode enabled AND close > upper channel AND close > prior high) OR (continuation mode enabled AND pullback seen for long AND (retest filter disabled OR lowest low over 3 bars > Basis − ATR × 0.45) AND prior close within prior bar's low-high range AND high > prior high AND close > prior high AND close > fast EMA))
Short: within date window AND cooldown bars since last entry exceeded AND short entries allowed AND bearish bias AND (dollar-volume filter disabled OR average dollar volume ≥ minimum) AND (benchmark filter disabled OR benchmark close < benchmark EMA) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ minimum) AND short score ≥ minimum AND ((breakout mode enabled AND close < lower channel AND close < prior low) OR (continuation mode enabled AND pullback seen for short AND (retest filter disabled OR highest high over 3 bars < Basis + ATR × 0.45) AND prior close within prior bar's low-high range AND low < prior low AND close < prior low AND close < fast EMA))
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − initial long risk OR high ≥ entry price + initial long risk × reward/risk multiple OR (break-even enabled AND high ≥ entry price + initial long risk AND low ≤ max(entry price − initial long risk, entry price × (1 + break-even buffer percent / 100))) OR time in position ≥ time-stop bars OR short-entry condition
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + initial short risk OR low ≤ entry price − initial short risk × reward/risk multiple OR (break-even enabled AND low ≤ entry price − initial short risk AND high ≥ min(entry price + initial short risk, entry price × (1 − break-even buffer percent / 100))) OR time in position ≥ time-stop bars OR long-entry condition
