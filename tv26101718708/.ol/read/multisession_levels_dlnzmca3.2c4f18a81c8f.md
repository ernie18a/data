Calculation: Previous day high/low = highest high / lowest low among chart bars from the bar opening at 22:00 GMT through the bar before the next 22:00 GMT bar; use the last completed period.
Calculation: Previous week high/low = highest high / lowest low among chart bars in the last completed week, ending when the chart bar’s week number or year changes.
Calculation: Previous month high/low = highest high / lowest low among chart bars in the last completed month, ending when the chart bar’s month or year changes.
Long: Close crosses above the previous day high OR previous week high OR previous month high.
Short: Close crosses below the previous day low OR previous week low OR previous month low.
