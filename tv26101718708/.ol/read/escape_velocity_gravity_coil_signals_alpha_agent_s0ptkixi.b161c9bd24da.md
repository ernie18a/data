Calculation: Price gravity = smoothed log(1 + average normalized effort) − log(volatility-scaled absolute net log displacement) + weighted log(1 − path efficiency); normalized effort is volume divided by its time-of-day baseline, or true range divided by its baseline when volume is unavailable.
Calculation: Path efficiency = absolute net log price change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar log changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Robust z-score = (value − rolling median) / (1.4826 × rolling median absolute deviation).
Calculation: Directional gravity spread = upside gravity − downside gravity; each side's gravity uses its direction's effort and displacement with the shared inefficiency term.
Calculation: Wedge edges = lines through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs and the two most recent confirmed pivot lows; edge slopes and widths are measured at the current bar and at the later of the two older pivots.
Calculation: Bullish structure regime = close above the Kaufman adaptive average, its slope over a lookback is sufficiently positive relative to ATR, and price efficiency exceeds a minimum; bearish regime is the inverse. A regime reverses only after consecutive closes beyond the opposite ATR-based band, and only if the opposite raw regime condition holds.
Calculation: Short gravity-release score = count of these nine conditions: net log move is downward; path efficiency exceeds a lower threshold; path efficiency is rising; gravity's robust-z change is falling; displacement's robust z-score is rising; effort's robust z-score exceeds a lower threshold; effort imbalance is negative; directional gravity spread is positive; and either recent gravity was high, recent pressure occurred, or gravity acceleration is nonpositive.
Calculation: Initial stop and targets = entry price plus or minus configured distances according to position side; distances use either ATR-scaled settings or fixed cash settings. After target 1, active stop becomes entry price plus or minus the configured profit-lock distance. Target 3 is the final target.
Long: A confirmed falling converging wedge with both edges narrowing qualifies for a bullish COIL marking after it persists for the required maturity; OR a bullish structure regime shift; OR close breaks above the upper edge of a matured falling wedge within the required post-COIL window; OR a confirmed cup-and-handle breakout: cup depth exceeds an ATR-based minimum, handle depth is within a maximum fraction of cup depth, close is near the cup rim, handle high is near the rim, the pattern has matured, and close exceeds the prior handle high; OR on a chart below the confirmation timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has close above its trend SMA, close crosses above its faster EMA, and volume exceeds its average by a minimum ratio; on that timeframe, use its own completed bars; OR on a chart above the confirmation timeframe, no such signal.
Long: On a standard-candle chart while flat, a confirmed bullish COIL occurred within the post-COIL window AND (a bullish structure regime shift OR close breaks above the current upper edge of the matured falling wedge).
Short: A confirmed rising converging wedge with both edges narrowing qualifies for a bearish COIL marking after it persists for the required maturity; OR a bearish structure regime shift; OR on a chart below the confirmation timeframe, the last completed higher-timeframe bar has close below its trend SMA, close crosses below its faster EMA, and volume exceeds its average by a minimum ratio; on that timeframe, use its own completed bars; OR on a chart above the confirmation timeframe, no such signal.
Short: On a confirmed bar, downside net log move is negative, path efficiency exceeds its high threshold, the short gravity-release score meets its minimum, the raw short condition was false on the prior bar, the cooldown has elapsed since the last short signal, and no cup breakout signal occurs on this bar.
Short: On a standard-candle chart while flat, a confirmed short signal occurs.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= active stop OR high >= target 3 OR holding bars reach the maximum.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= active stop OR low <= target 3 OR holding bars reach the maximum.
