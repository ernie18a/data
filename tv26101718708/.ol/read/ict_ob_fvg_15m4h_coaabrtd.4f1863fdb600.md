Calculation: Three-bar fair value gap = bullish when the high of the oldest bar is below the low of the newest bar; bearish when the low of the oldest bar is above the high of the newest bar. Use completed source-timeframe bars; when the chart timeframe is lower than both source timeframes, also evaluate the lower source timeframe’s still-forming bar in real time.
Long: Bullish three-bar fair value gap OR bullish order block marking (bullish fair value gap and the immediately preceding completed bar closes below its open, or that bar does not close below its open and the second preceding completed bar closes below its open).
Short: Bearish three-bar fair value gap OR bearish order block marking (bearish fair value gap and the immediately preceding completed bar closes above its open, or that bar does not close above its open and the second preceding completed bar closes above its open).
