Calculation: Bullish major signal = after more than the major threshold of consecutive closes above their values four bars earlier, the bar closes below its open and its high is at least the highest high over the major lookback; or after more than the major threshold of consecutive closes below their values four bars earlier, the bar closes above its open and its low is at most the lowest low over the major lookback. Reset the corresponding counter when its signal fires. The major threshold is higher and its lookback longer than for the minor signal.
Calculation: Bullish minor signal = same counter and reversal rules as the bullish major signal, using the minor threshold and lookback.
Calculation: Composite trend = (RSI of OHLC4 over a lookback + RSI of the 14-bar sum of volume×HLC3 on positive HLC3 changes, using the 14-bar sum of volume×HLC3 on negative HLC3 changes as its RSI length + Bollinger oscillator of OHLC4 over a lookback with bands two standard deviations from its simple moving average + smoothed stochastic of OHLC4 over a lookback divided by 3) / 2.
Calculation: Regression trend = rolling correlation(bar index, composite trend) × stdev(composite trend) / stdev(bar index) as slope; intercept = EMA(composite trend) − slope × EMA(bar index); value = slope × current bar index + intercept.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursive close-based trailing stop offset by ATR over a lookback; when current and prior closes are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and close minus ATR; when both are below, use the lesser of the prior stop and close plus ATR; otherwise use close minus ATR if close is above the prior stop, or close plus ATR if not.
Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR over a lookback on higher-timeframe daily bars, using the last completed daily bar.
Long: Within the configured backtest date window AND ((bullish minor signal OR bullish major signal) AND regression trend <= configured minimum level OR regression trend > its prior value AND its prior value > its value two bars ago AND regression trend <= configured minimum level OR composite trend crosses above configured minimum level OR regression trend crosses below composite trend AND regression trend <= configured minimum level AND composite trend <= configured minimum level OR regression trend is below configured minimum level on the current and prior two bars AND regression trend > its prior value AND composite trend <= configured minimum level OR (regression trend or composite trend is <= configured minimum level on the current or prior two bars) AND close > ATR trailing stop AND prior close <= prior ATR trailing stop).
Exit: Long, low <= 0 before the first profit target OR low <= entry price after the first profit target OR high >= entry price + configured first target percentage × daily ATR OR high >= entry price + configured second target percentage × daily ATR
