Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = either a swing extreme confirmed after price retraces by a configurable ATR multiple, or a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in a symmetric configurable bar window
Calculation: Bullish order-block origin = among the prior configurable number of bars, the qualifying bearish candle with the lowest low; bearish order-block origin = the qualifying bullish candle with the highest high. A qualifying candle has range below 2.5 × ATR and body at least 25% of its range; when volume filtering is enabled, its volume is at least a configurable multiple of its rolling average. Alternatively, select the most recent qualifying opposite-color candle.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = the current low is above the high two bars earlier, the previous close is above that earlier high, and the gap is at least a configurable ATR multiple; bearish fair value gap = the current high is below the low two bars earlier, the previous close is below that earlier low, and the gap is at least a configurable ATR multiple
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed major pivot high OR close crosses above the latest confirmed minor pivot high OR price sweeps below the latest confirmed minor pivot low older than two bars and closes above it, with the lower wick at least a configurable ATR multiple and a configurable fraction of the candle range and the same-direction sweep cooldown elapsed OR a bullish fair value gap forms OR a mitigated bearish fair value gap is crossed upward by the close within its configured flip window OR a mitigated bearish order block is retested from above, with the low inside the block and the close above its lower boundary OR a bullish major break creates a qualifying bullish order-block origin
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed major pivot low OR close crosses below the latest confirmed minor pivot low OR price sweeps above the latest confirmed minor pivot high older than two bars and closes below it, with the upper wick at least a configurable ATR multiple and a configurable fraction of the candle range and the same-direction sweep cooldown elapsed OR a bearish fair value gap forms OR a mitigated bullish fair value gap is crossed downward by the close within its configured flip window OR a mitigated bullish order block is retested from below, with the high inside the block and the close below its upper boundary OR a bearish major break creates a qualifying bearish order-block origin
Exit: none
