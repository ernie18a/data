Calculation: DipPoints = RSI: 3 if RSI <= oversold threshold - deep margin, 2 if <= oversold threshold, 1 if <= oversold threshold + warning margin; Stochastic: 3/2/1 on an upward %K/%D crossover when %K <= oversold threshold - deep margin / threshold / threshold + 15, respectively, otherwise 1 if %K <= threshold; Bollinger Bands: 3 if close <= lower band - deep offset, 2 if <= lower band, 1 if <= lower band + 30% of the lower-band-to-middle-band distance; CCI: 3/2/1 if <= -deep/mid/light threshold; Williams %R: 3/2/1 if <= -deep/mid/light threshold; MFI: 3/2/1 if <= deep/mid/light threshold; ADX/DI: 3 on an upward +DI/-DI crossover with ADX >= strong threshold, 2 if +DI > -DI and ADX > its value N bars earlier, 1 if +DI > -DI; bullish RSI-price divergence: 3 if fresh, 2 if active, 1 through five bars after expiry; otherwise 0. Each component uses its own parameter set; deep, mid, and light thresholds are ordered from most extreme to least extreme. Divergence compares consecutive confirmed pivot lows: lower price low, higher RSI low, and RSI at the newer pivot < 50.
Calculation: PeakPoints = RSI: 3 if RSI >= overbought threshold + deep margin, 2 if >= overbought threshold, 1 if >= overbought threshold - warning margin; Stochastic: 3/2/1 on a downward %K/%D crossover when %K >= overbought threshold + deep margin / threshold / threshold - 15, respectively, otherwise 1 if %K >= threshold; Bollinger Bands: 3 if close >= upper band + deep offset, 2 if >= upper band, 1 if >= upper band - 30% of the upper-band-to-middle-band distance; CCI: 3/2/1 if >= deep/mid/light threshold; Williams %R: 3/2/1 if >= -(100 - deep/mid/light threshold); MFI: 3/2/1 if >= 100 - deep/mid/light threshold; ADX/DI: 3 on a downward +DI/-DI crossover with ADX >= strong threshold, 2 if -DI > +DI and ADX > its value N bars earlier, 1 if -DI > +DI; bearish RSI-price divergence: 3 if fresh, 2 if active, 1 through five bars after expiry; otherwise 0. Divergence compares consecutive confirmed pivot highs: higher price high, lower RSI high, and RSI at the newer pivot > 50. Deep, mid, and light thresholds are ordered from most extreme to least extreme.
Calculation: DipScore = sum of the eight DipPoints; PeakScore = sum of the eight PeakPoints. DipVoters and PeakVoters = count of respective component points >= 1.
Calculation: Volume multiplier = strong-volume multiplier if volume / its simple moving average >= strong-spike threshold; else weak-volume multiplier if that ratio >= spike threshold; else 1. Effective DipScore and PeakScore = corresponding score × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Bullish divergence active/fresh = bullish divergence detected within expiry/freshness bars; bearish divergence active/fresh = bearish divergence detected within expiry/freshness bars. Divergence uses a pivot with equal left and right lookback; confirmation occurs after the right-side bars.
Calculation: RawDip = DipVoters >= minimum voters AND Effective DipScore >= minimum total score AND bar confirmed, vetoed when the optional trend filter is enabled and close < trend EMA. RawPeak = PeakVoters >= minimum voters AND Effective PeakScore >= minimum total score AND bar confirmed, vetoed when the optional trend filter is enabled and close > trend EMA.
Calculation: DipWarning = NOT RawDip AND DipVoters >= minimum voters - 1 AND Effective DipScore >= minimum total score - 3 AND bar confirmed. PeakWarning = NOT RawPeak AND PeakVoters >= minimum voters - 1 AND Effective PeakScore >= minimum total score - 3 AND bar confirmed.
Calculation: ReEntryLong = enabled AND last exit side long AND bars since exit >= cooldown AND re-entry count < maximum AND (highest high since exit - low) / highest high since exit >= pullback threshold AND close > fast EMA AND close > slow EMA AND DipVoters >= re-entry minimum AND bar confirmed. ReEntryShort = enabled AND last exit side short AND bars since exit >= cooldown AND re-entry count < maximum AND (high - lowest low since exit) / lowest low since exit >= pullback threshold AND close < fast EMA AND close < slow EMA AND PeakVoters >= re-entry minimum AND bar confirmed. Fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA; discard re-entry eligibility after its maximum-bar window.
Calculation: PullbackLong = enabled AND close > trend EMA AND +DI > -DI AND ADX >= strong threshold AND DipVoters >= pullback minimum AND an EMA-touch occurred within the last bounce-count + 3 bars AND the last bounce-count bars each close > open AND close > slow EMA AND |close - slow EMA| / slow EMA <= maximum EMA distance AND bar confirmed. PullbackShort = enabled AND close < trend EMA AND -DI > +DI AND ADX >= strong threshold AND PeakVoters >= pullback minimum AND an EMA-touch occurred within the last bounce-count + 3 bars AND the last bounce-count bars each close < open AND close < slow EMA AND |close - slow EMA| / slow EMA <= maximum EMA distance AND bar confirmed. EMA-touch means low or high is within 1.5 × maximum EMA distance of slow EMA.
Calculation: GoLong = (RawDip AND position side != long OR ReEntryLong OR PullbackLong) AND long trading enabled. GoShort = (RawPeak AND position side != short OR ReEntryShort OR PullbackShort) AND short trading enabled. In single-direction mode, an opposite raw signal closes the open position.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price × (1 - stop-loss percent); initial short stop = entry price × (1 + stop-loss percent). For pullback entries, use stop-loss percent × pullback stop ratio. When breakeven is enabled, raise a long stop to entry price after close profit reaches phase-2 activation, or to entry price × (1 + locked-profit percent) after phase-3 activation; lower a short stop to entry price or entry price × (1 - locked-profit percent), respectively. Stop updates occur while the position remains open.
Calculation: Trail activation percent and trail distance percent = base values × [1 + (clamp(ATR / 50-bar ATR average, 0.5, 2) - 1) × ATR weight]. Trail distance is additionally multiplied by clamp(1 - opposite-side raw score / 24 × tighten rate + own-side raw score / 24 × loosen rate, 0.15, 1.5). Activate after favorable extreme since entry reaches trail activation percent; long trail = highest high since entry × (1 - adjusted trail distance / 100), short trail = lowest low since entry × (1 + adjusted trail distance / 100). Trail stop updates while position remains open.
Long: RawDip AND position side != long OR ReEntryLong OR PullbackLong OR (bullish divergence active AND NOT GoLong) OR DipWarning
Short: RawPeak AND position side != short OR ReEntryShort OR PullbackShort OR (bearish divergence active AND NOT GoShort) OR PeakWarning
Exit: long, GoShort OR low <= long stop OR (trailing enabled AND trail active AND low <= long trail)
Exit: short, GoLong OR high >= short stop OR (trailing enabled AND trail active AND high >= short trail)
