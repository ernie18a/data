Calculation: OU mean, reversion speed, half-life, and stationary standard deviation = fit a lag-1 linear regression to the chart-timeframe price series over a configurable lookback; derive the equilibrium mean, reversion half-life, and stationary standard deviation from the regression.
Calculation: OU Z-score = (current price − OU mean) / stationary standard deviation.
Calculation: Hurst exponent = rescaled range of log returns over the OU lookback.
Calculation: ADF t-statistic = t-statistic for the lagged price coefficient in a regression of price changes on lagged prices over the OU lookback.
Calculation: Variance ratio at q = ratio of the variance of q-bar price changes to q times the variance of one-bar price changes, over the OU lookback.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP deviation Z-score = Z-score of (close − chart-timeframe VWAP) relative to its rolling mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Session VWAP deviation Z-score = (close − current-session volume-weighted mean of hlc3) / current-session volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3.
Calculation: L1 direction = −1 when OU Z-score is above the short-entry threshold, +1 when it is below the negative long-entry threshold, otherwise 0; L1 strength = min(abs(OU Z-score) / the swing Z threshold, cap).
Calculation: L5 direction and strength = combine the rolling VWAP deviation Z-score signal and session VWAP deviation Z-score signal using their configured relative weights; signals point short above their positive thresholds and long below their negative thresholds. When both signals agree, combine their strengths by the same weights; when only one is nonzero, use its weighted strength. Multiply strength by configured volume-spike and order-flow alignment factors and by the VPIN penalty factor.
Calculation: Composite score = direction-weighted sum of L1 strength and L5 strength using their configured weights, multiplied by the applicable regime and other active multiplier factors; unavailable external-data layers contribute zero signal. Composite direction is the sign of this score.
Calculation: Expected move in basis points = abs(close − OU mean) / close × basis-point scale.
Long: Composite score > 0 AND at least one configured horizon qualifies under the active fusion mode AND expected move in basis points exceeds the configured transaction-cost requirement for that horizon AND the bar is outside configured open, close, lunch, and session-warmup blocks AND the volatility regime is not the configured hard-block regime.
Short: Composite score < 0 AND at least one configured horizon qualifies under the active fusion mode AND expected move in basis points exceeds the configured transaction-cost requirement for that horizon AND the bar is outside configured open, close, lunch, and session-warmup blocks AND the volatility regime is not the configured hard-block regime.
