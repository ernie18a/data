Calculation: Flex VWAP = volume-weighted mean of the selected price source since the active anchor; reset at the selected bar-cycle, confirmed swing-pivot, cumulative-volume, or daily-session boundary; use volume 1 when volume is missing or nonpositive
Calculation: Flex VWAP standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted variance of the selected price source since the active anchor
Calculation: Upper and lower deviation bands = Flex VWAP plus or minus the standard deviation multiplied by the selected band multiplier
Long: close crosses above Flex VWAP OR low crosses below the lower second deviation band
Short: close crosses below Flex VWAP OR high crosses above the upper second deviation band
