Calculation: 9:30 opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the opening range; use the last completed bar of the higher timeframe range.
Calculation: 1:30 AM opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the 1:30 AM opening range; use the last completed bar of the higher timeframe range.
Calculation: Engulfing run = consecutive candles in the same direction (close >= open for an up candle); its cumulative body extreme is the highest body top for an up run or lowest body bottom for a down run. The base is the preceding opposite-direction candle.
Long: The first close above the applicable higher-timeframe 9:30 opening-range high, with no earlier breakout of that range OR an up engulfing run reaches or exceeds the base candle's high, the base candle is down, its range overlaps the applicable opening-range zone, the bar is at or after the session open, and the first breakout has not yet occurred OR, on a chart lower than the opening-range timeframe, the first engulfing run aligned with the breakout bias reaches or exceeds the base candle's high, the base candle is down, its range overlaps the zone, and no entry has yet been taken for that zone.
Short: The first close below the applicable higher-timeframe 9:30 opening-range low, with no earlier breakout of that range OR a down engulfing run reaches or falls below the base candle's low, the base candle is up, its range overlaps the applicable opening-range zone, the bar is at or after the session open, and the first breakout has not yet occurred OR, on a chart lower than the opening-range timeframe, the first engulfing run aligned with the breakout bias reaches or falls below the base candle's low, the base candle is up, its range overlaps the zone, and no entry has yet been taken for that zone OR the first close above the higher-timeframe 1:30 AM opening-range high, with no earlier breakout of that range OR the first close below the higher-timeframe 1:30 AM opening-range low, with no earlier breakout of that range.
Exit: Long, bar low <= stop price OR bar high >= target price.
Exit: Short, bar high >= stop price OR bar low <= target price.
