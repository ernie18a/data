Calculation: Internal and swing pivots = either symmetric pivots using their respective lookbacks on both sides, confirmed after the right-side bars, or leg-transition pivots: a high/low at the lookback offset when it exceeds the highest/lowest value in the current lookback window; internal lookback is shorter than swing lookback.
Calculation: Bullish structure level = latest confirmed pivot high; bearish structure level = latest confirmed pivot low. A level can be broken once; bullish/bearish structure direction becomes the direction of the latest break.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the latest equal-low level, or latest swing low if no equal-low level exists, and close above that level and above open; bearish sweep = high above the latest equal-high level, or latest swing high if no equal-high level exists, and close below that level and below open.
Calculation: Displacement = bullish (bearish) candle whose body exceeds both a multiple of the longer-lookback average body and a multiple of ATR; its close is in the upper (lower) quarter of its range. Recent displacement = displacement within a configurable recent-bar window.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity grab = sweep of a previously confirmed symmetric pivot low/high using a shorter, medium, or longer pivot lookback; the candle body remains inside the level, the prior close was on the inside, penetration meets an ATR-based minimum, and the rejection wick meets a minimum share of candle range. A pierced level is consumed even if rejection filters fail.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trendline break = close above/below a descending line through the latest two confirmed swing highs / ascending line through the latest two confirmed swing lows, respectively; the pivots are separated by at least the configured minimum.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish imbalance = selected imbalance type forms in that direction: three-bar range gap; inverse gap when a tracked gap is mitigated; overlap of a new gap with a recent opposite gap; adjacent candle bodies gap while wick ranges overlap; or adjacent candle ranges gap. Gap size must meet the volatility threshold; optional displacement filter applies. Higher-timeframe source uses its last completed bar; chart-timeframe source uses the completed current bar.
Calculation: EMA alignment = short-lookback EMA above/below medium-lookback EMA and close above/below the longer-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume exceeds a multiple of its moving average.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = last completed higher-timeframe close above/below its longer-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = 2 points for matching swing direction, 1 for matching internal direction, 2 for matching higher-timeframe trend (or disabled filter), 2 for matching BOS (including recent matching BOS), 1 for matching CHoCH (including recent matching CHoCH), 2 for matching recent sweep, 1 for price in matching order block, 1 for price in matching imbalance, 2 for matching-direction OTE, 1 for recent matching displacement, 1 for volume confirmation, and 1 for EMA alignment.
Calculation: Entry reference = close, configured Fibonacci retracement of the latest swing impulse, or midpoint of the newest active matching order block (otherwise matching imbalance; otherwise close), according to the selected entry method.
Calculation: Stop reference = entry offset by ATR for ATR mode; matching order-block edge offset by ATR for Zone mode when available; otherwise the farther adverse extreme of the current bar, latest matching sweep, and matching order-block edge, offset by ATR. For a long, risk is entry minus stop; for a short, stop minus entry.
Calculation: Targets = configured risk multiples, matching internal/swing/equal liquidity levels and impulse extension, or their configured hybrid; fall back to risk-multiple targets when a reference is unavailable or out of order. The second target must meet the configured minimum reward-to-risk ratio.
Long: Confirmed bullish pivot label condition: a newly confirmed pivot high exceeds the previous pivot high (HH) or a newly confirmed pivot low exceeds the previous pivot low (HL).
Short: Confirmed bearish pivot label condition: a newly confirmed pivot high is below the previous pivot high (LH) or a newly confirmed pivot low is below the previous pivot low (LL).
Long: Confirmed close (or configured wick source) crosses above an unbroken internal or swing pivot high; label is BOS if that structure was already bullish, otherwise CHoCH, with CHoCH+ when the latest confirmed intervening low is a higher low.
Short: Confirmed close (or configured wick source) crosses below an unbroken internal or swing pivot low; label is BOS if that structure was already bearish, otherwise CHoCH, with CHoCH+ when the latest confirmed intervening high is a lower high.
Long: Bullish liquidity grab occurs, or close crosses above a descending swing trendline, or a bullish imbalance forms.
Short: Bearish liquidity grab occurs, or close crosses below an ascending swing trendline, or a bearish imbalance forms.
Long: No active trade; signals are confirmed on bar close when enabled; selected signal mode passes (Strict: bullish swing direction, recent bullish structure, recent bullish displacement, bullish impulse direction, close in discount and OTE, price in bullish order block or imbalance, recent bullish sweep, matching higher-timeframe trend unless disabled, and bullish candle; Score: bullish score meets threshold, bullish impulse direction, close in OTE, and bullish candle); all enabled sweep, order-block, imbalance, and displacement requirements pass; RSI is within its configured long range unless disabled; valid positive risk is no greater than the configured ATR limit; and second-target reward-to-risk meets its minimum. If both sides qualify, long takes precedence.
Short: No active trade; signals are confirmed on bar close when enabled; selected signal mode passes (Strict: bearish swing direction, recent bearish structure, recent bearish displacement, bearish impulse direction, close in discount and OTE, price in bearish order block or imbalance, recent bearish sweep, matching higher-timeframe trend unless disabled, and bearish candle; Score: bearish score meets threshold, bearish impulse direction, close in OTE, and bearish candle); all enabled sweep, order-block, imbalance, and displacement requirements pass; RSI is within its configured short range unless disabled; valid positive risk is no greater than the configured ATR limit; and second-target reward-to-risk meets its minimum. A short signal is suppressed when a long qualifies on the same bar.
Exit: Long, low <= stop; otherwise high >= first target, then high >= second target, then high >= third target, in sequence. After first target, move stop to entry if enabled. Also exit when a bearish BOS or CHoCH occurs before the third target.
Exit: Short, high >= stop; otherwise low <= first target, then low <= second target, then low <= third target, in sequence. After first target, move stop to entry if enabled. Also exit when a bullish BOS or CHoCH occurs before the third target.
