Calculation: ALMA-smoothed percentage change = ALMA of (source change over the smoothing interval / current source × 100), using the ALMA lookback.
Calculation: Custom Gaussian adaptive moving average = EMA of the weighted mean of midpoint values. For each lag from zero through the Gaussian lookback minus one, the midpoint is the sum of the highest and lowest ALMA-smoothed percentage changes from the current bar through that lag. Weight each midpoint by exp(−0.5 × ((lag − (lookback − 1)) / (2 × σ))²), where σ is the close standard deviation over the volatility lookback when adaptive, otherwise the fixed sigma; divide the weighted sum by the sum of weights.
