Long: The shorter-lookback SMA crosses above the longer-lookback SMA OR the higher-timeframe weekly MACD crosses above its signal line OR chart-timeframe MACD crosses above its signal line AND ((shorter-lookback SMA > longer-lookback SMA AND weekly MACD < its signal line) OR (shorter-lookback SMA < longer-lookback SMA AND weekly MACD > its signal line)) OR position is long AND close < first entry price AND a bullish Heikin-Ashi bar follows a bearish bar AND (close is at least a fixed percentage below the first entry price if no prior add, otherwise at least a fixed percentage below the last add price)
Short: The shorter-lookback SMA crosses below the longer-lookback SMA OR the higher-timeframe weekly MACD crosses below its signal line OR position is short AND close > first entry price AND a bearish Heikin-Ashi bar follows a bullish bar AND (close is at least a fixed percentage above the first entry price if no prior add, otherwise at least a fixed percentage above the last add price)
Exit: short, the shorter-lookback SMA crosses above the longer-lookback SMA OR the higher-timeframe weekly MACD crosses above its signal line
Exit: long, the shorter-lookback SMA crosses below the longer-lookback SMA OR the higher-timeframe weekly MACD crosses below its signal line
