Calculation: Custom range-normalized RSI = 50 + 50 × RMA(momentum × weight, lookback) / RMA(|momentum| × weight, lookback), where momentum is the lookback high-low range if the high exceeds its prior value, otherwise the negative range if the low is below its prior value, otherwise the change in source; weight is volume when volume weighting is enabled and 1 otherwise. Use chart-timeframe bars, or a higher-timeframe RSI when that option is enabled (higher-timeframe request uses lookahead off).
Calculation: Macro moving averages = four configurable moving averages of close on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe; their lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming when macro-close confirmation is disabled, and the last completed bar when enabled.
Calculation: Qualifying pivot highs/lows = confirmed pivots of the RSI and source on the chart timeframe, requiring minimum RSI distance from 50 and minimum bar distance from the prior qualifying pivot.
