Calculation: Macro score = weighted blend of a liquidity score, an alignment score, and an inverse-volatility-level score; liquidity uses configurable lookback momentum of dollar index, gold, equity index, and volatility index, weighted toward weaker dollar, stronger gold and equity index, and weaker volatility index; alignment counts weaker dollar, stronger gold, stronger equity index, weaker volatility index, and stronger industrial metal; volatility-level score decreases as volatility index level rises. Use daily bars from the higher timeframe, using the bar exposed by lookahead-on (the current, potentially forming daily bar).
Calculation: Trend score = weighted blend of a regime score, bounded recent momentum score, and inverse volatility-percentile score. Regime is strong bull when short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs are ordered upward and ADX exceeds its strength threshold; weak bull when EMAs are ordered upward; strong bear when ordered downward and ADX exceeds its strength threshold; weak bear when ordered downward; otherwise range. Use shorter, intermediate, and longer EMA lookbacks; momentum and volatility rank use their own configurable lookbacks on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Asset score = sum of drawdown-band score, RSI-zone score, regime-context score, long-term EMA-distance score, and ATR-to-close score, less an overheat penalty, bounded to its score range. Drawdown is close relative to the running all-time high; assign increasing scores at successively deeper configurable drawdown bands. RSI score decreases across successively higher configurable RSI zones. Regime-context score is highest in strong bear and lowest in strong bull. EMA-distance score decreases across successively higher configurable distance zones above the long-term EMA and increases below it. ATR-to-close score increases across successively higher configurable volatility zones. Apply the penalty when RSI exceeds its overheat threshold AND close is above the long-term EMA by more than its distance threshold.
Calculation: Final score = weighted blend of macro score, trend score, and asset score.
Calculation: Action tier = tiered mapping of final score, from highest to lowest: heavy buy, increased DCA, normal DCA, light DCA, pause, reduce.
