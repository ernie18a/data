Calculation: Standard demand/supply POI = On a source bar, take the immediately preceding N bars as the base. The base qualifies when its average candle body-to-range ratio is at most the mode-adjusted limit, its total high-low range is at most the mode-adjusted ATR limit, and (except in Loose mode) neighboring range overlap or base-close drift is within its limit (Strict requires both). The first opposing candle in the base defines the zone; its boundaries use the selected boundary method. A demand candidate additionally requires a bullish departure whose body is at least the mode-adjusted ATR multiple, body-to-range ratio meets its limit, and close exceeds the base high plus an ATR buffer; supply is symmetric below the base low. The candidate must pass the optional width limit. Within the BOS window after the candidate, a confirmed close must break the selected structure level plus/minus an ATR buffer. The structure level is the latest confirmed swing high/low, a rolling high/low over a longer lookback than the base, or the stricter of those levels in Hybrid mode. A qualifying event must meet its mode's score threshold: BOS 30 points, base quality 10 or 15, sufficient retracement 15, strong departure 15 (otherwise 12), liquidity sweep 10, imbalance 10, and resting-liquidity proxy 5. Strict mode additionally requires retracement and either sweep or imbalance; Custom mode applies its configured retracement, imbalance, and sweep requirements. Daily and H4 source bars are higher than the chart timeframe; use only the last completed source bar. AM-session source bars use the M15 or M5 layer: use the last completed source bar, which is higher, lower, or same timeframe as the chart as applicable. For AM candidates, the base must lie within the session; the base, departure, BOS, retracement, scoring, and optional width rules use the AM-specific limits. AM Strict mode requires base overlap, retracement, and imbalance. AM demand/supply events also require the current bar's low/high to remain beyond the zone's distal boundary.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish breaker = A confirmed close breaks the latest confirmed pivot high/low plus/minus an ATR buffer. Record the first opposing candle among the preceding search bars as the block (optionally include the next same-direction candle); its full candle or body defines the boundaries. A later confirmed close through the opposite block boundary plus/minus an ATR buffer creates the bullish/bearish breaker. For AM breakers, the structure break must occur in session; the later break must occur in session or within the allowed post-session bars. Daily/H4 breaker calculations use higher-timeframe bars; AM breaker calculations use their M15/M5 source bars. Use only the last completed source bar.
Long: A new confirmed standard demand POI occurs on a Daily or H4 source bar, or an AM M15/M5 demand event occurs and the chart close is above its proximal boundary; OR a bullish breaker is confirmed and the chart close is above its upper boundary. Apply the configured history-retention limit and suppress a source event already published.
Short: A new confirmed standard supply POI occurs on a Daily or H4 source bar, or an AM M15/M5 supply event occurs and the chart close is below its proximal boundary; OR a bearish breaker is confirmed and the chart close is below its lower boundary. Apply the configured history-retention limit and suppress a source event already published.
