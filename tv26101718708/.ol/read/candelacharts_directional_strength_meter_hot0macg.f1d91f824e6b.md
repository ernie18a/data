Calculation: Directional oscillator = the mean of RSI, MFI, MACD, CCI, TSI, RVI, Stochastic %K, Williams %R, ROC, Momentum, and CMO components, each normalized as follows: RSI/MFI = (value − 50)/20; CCI = value/100; Stochastic %K = (3-bar SMA of %K − 50)/50; Williams %R = (value + 50)/50; CMO = value/100; RVI = ((upward-change standard deviation EMA / (upward-change standard deviation EMA + downward-change standard deviation EMA) × 100) − 50)/20; MACD, TSI, ROC, and Momentum = divide a positive value by its rolling lookback high, a negative value by the absolute rolling lookback low, and zero by zero. MACD and TSI use a slower component with twice the lookback of the faster component. Optionally EMA-smooth the mean; the alert source is either this result or its selected moving average.
Calculation: Environmental quality = normalize the equal-weight mean of enabled ATR, ADX, absolute linear-regression slope, and absolute price/time correlation components; each component is ((value / its EMA) − 1) × (1 + its rolling standard deviation), with an additional −1 for ATR, regression slope, and correlation; normalize the mean by dividing positive values by the absolute rolling high, negative values by the absolute rolling low, and zero by zero.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = the mean of the normalized RSI, MFI, MACD, CCI, TSI, and RVI components used in the Directional oscillator.
Long: Alert source crosses above the buy threshold AND environmental quality ≥ quality threshold.
Short: Alert source crosses below the sell threshold AND environmental quality ≥ quality threshold.
Long: A confirmed pivot low in price is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low AND the divergence oscillator at the current pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND the preceding pivot was 5–60 bars earlier AND at least one of the two oscillator pivot values is outside [−1, 1].
Short: A confirmed pivot high in price is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the divergence oscillator at the current pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND the preceding pivot was 5–60 bars earlier AND at least one of the two oscillator pivot values is outside [−1, 1].
