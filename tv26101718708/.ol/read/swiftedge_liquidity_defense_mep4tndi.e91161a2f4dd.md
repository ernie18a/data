Calculation: Buy-volume share = buy volume divided by total buy and sell volume, where buy volume sums volume from up candles and sell volume sums volume from down candles on lower-timeframe completed bars within the chart bar; if lower-timeframe data is unavailable or bar-close-location mode is used, estimate buy volume as chart-bar volume × (close − low) / (high − low), or half the volume for a zero-range bar, and assign the remainder to sell volume
Long: A confirmed swing-low pool is active before the bar, low < its swing low, close >= its swing low, AND delta confirmation is disabled OR buy-volume share >= the configured reversal-side threshold
Short: A confirmed swing-high pool is active before the bar, high > its swing high, close <= its swing high, AND delta confirmation is disabled OR (100% − buy-volume share) >= the configured reversal-side threshold
