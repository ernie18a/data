Calculation: Opening-range high = high of the chart-timeframe bar opening at 09:00 each trading day
Calculation: Opening-range low = low of the chart-timeframe bar opening at 09:00 each trading day
Long: Today's opening range is captured AND today's entry orders have not been placed AND high >= opening-range high + ATR × sensitivity OR opening-range high > opening-range low AND close > open
Short: Today's opening range is captured AND today's entry orders have not been placed AND low <= opening-range low − ATR × sensitivity OR opening-range high <= opening-range low OR close <= open
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price − stop-loss distance
Exit: Short, low <= entry price − take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance
