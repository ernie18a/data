Calculation: Momentum z-score = (current close change − mean close change over the lookback) / standard deviation of close changes over the same lookback
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two chart bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two chart bars earlier
Long: Close crosses above the most recent confirmed pivot high AND momentum z-score > the positive threshold
Long: Close > the upper boundary of an un-inverted bearish fair value gap formed within its allowed age AND volume percentile rank on the bar before the gap formed ≥ the configured threshold
Short: Close crosses below the most recent confirmed pivot low AND momentum z-score < the negative threshold
Short: Close < the lower boundary of an un-inverted bullish fair value gap formed within its allowed age AND volume percentile rank on the bar before the gap formed ≥ the configured threshold
