Calculation: Fast ribbon = average of the same-type moving averages of chart close at the shorter and longer fast lookbacks; slow ribbon = average of the same-type moving averages of chart close at the shorter and longer slow lookbacks, with both slow lookbacks longer than the corresponding fast lookbacks.
Calculation: Ribbon spread = fast ribbon − slow ribbon.
Calculation: Fast ribbon count = previous count + 1 when the fast ribbon’s shorter-lookback MA exceeds its longer-lookback MA and their spread is increasing; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Slow ribbon count = previous count + 1 when the slow ribbon’s shorter-lookback MA exceeds its longer-lookback MA and their spread is increasing; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Uptrend count = previous count + 1 when the ribbon spread is positive and increasing; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Downtrend count = previous count + 1 when the ribbon spread is negative and decreasing; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Long score = 0 unless all three uptrend counts are at least 2; when they are, it is 2 if the previous long score is 0, otherwise the previous long score + 1; add 1 when the long RSI-channel condition first becomes true, and add 1 when Parabolic SAR is above the bar high.
Calculation: Short score = 0 unless all three downtrend counts are at least 2; when they are, it is 2 if the previous short score is 0, otherwise the previous short score + 1; add 1 when the short RSI-channel condition first becomes true, and add 1 when Parabolic SAR is below the bar low.
Calculation: RSI-channel midpoint = average of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback; its long slope condition is midpoint above its value five bars earlier, and its short slope condition is midpoint below its value five bars earlier.
Calculation: RSI-channel long condition = long slope condition AND close above the lower channel band AND close below the channel midpoint AND 5-period RSI below 35; RSI-channel short condition = short slope condition AND close below the upper channel band AND close above the channel midpoint AND 5-period RSI above 65. The channel bands are a 50-period SMA of close ± 0.25 × 50-period EMA of true range.
Calculation: Position high watermark = highest bar high since long entry; position low watermark = lowest bar low since short entry. On Renko charts, bar high/low are max/min of open and close; otherwise they are high/low.
Long: Long score ≥ 3 AND flat AND bar confirmed or historical.
Short: Long score < 3 AND short score ≥ 3 AND flat AND bar confirmed or historical.
Exit: Long, (low ≤ position high watermark × (1 − trailing-stop percentage)) OR (position high watermark ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) AND low ≤ position high watermark × (1 − trailing-profit percentage)) OR (low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)) OR (opposite-trend exit condition AND (opposite-trade exit enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss disabled)).
Exit: Short, (high ≥ position low watermark × (1 + trailing-stop percentage)) OR (position low watermark ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) AND high ≥ position low watermark × (1 + trailing-profit percentage)) OR (high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)) OR (opposite-trend exit condition AND (opposite-trade exit enabled OR both trailing stop and stop loss disabled)).
Calculation: Opposite-trend long exit condition = fast ribbon below slow ribbon on the prior bar AND current open below the prior slow ribbon AND this condition was false on the prior bar; opposite-trend short exit condition = fast ribbon above slow ribbon on the prior bar AND current open above the prior slow ribbon AND this condition was false on the prior bar.
