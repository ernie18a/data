Calculation: eVWMA = blend of a recursive volume-weighted average and an EMA of hlc3; recursive weight = min(current volume × volume sensitivity / trailing volume sum, 1), and the blend uses the EMA floor mix.
Calculation: Coil ATR percentile = 100 × (current SMA of true range − lowest value over the percentile lookback) / (highest value over the percentile lookback − lowest value over the percentile lookback); use 50 when highest equals lowest.
Calculation: Master-ATR distance = (close − eVWMA) / master ATR.
Calculation: Trail stop = close minus (ATR multiplier × ATR) for long, or close plus (ATR multiplier × ATR) for short; ratchet the long stop only upward and the short stop only downward. When higher-timeframe ATR is enabled, use the last completed bar of a higher timeframe; otherwise use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the specified number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Band-opening gap = absolute change in the trailing-stop level when the stop side changes or the trail re-arms; average gap = mean of the most recent configured number of band-opening gaps.
Long: Master-ATR distance > flat-band threshold OR (coil was coiled on the prior bar and is not coiled now, fewer than the configured bars have elapsed since release, no fade has fired since that release, distance is between the negative maximum-stretch and negative minimum-stretch thresholds, and long bias is enabled) OR (since price last returned inside the reset distance, its most negative Master-ATR distance reached the leg-stretch threshold, no long reversal has fired in that leg, and at least the configured minimum number of these checks hold: prior RSI ≤ oversold threshold and current RSI > prior RSI; prior low ≤ prior lower band and current close > current lower band; a confirmed pivot low; current volume ≥ volume multiplier × volume SMA) OR (a coil long fade fired, the configured confirmation window has not expired, recent volume SMA ≥ pre-fire volume SMA × (1 + surge threshold)) OR (relative volume ≥ spike threshold and close ≥ open) OR (a new band opens on the long trailing-stop side, the warmup count of gaps is met, and its gap > expansion threshold × average gap).
Short: Master-ATR distance < negative flat-band threshold OR (coil was coiled on the prior bar and is not coiled now, fewer than the configured bars have elapsed since release, no fade has fired since that release, distance is between the positive minimum-stretch and positive maximum-stretch thresholds, and short bias is enabled) OR (since price last returned inside the reset distance, its most positive Master-ATR distance reached the leg-stretch threshold, no short reversal has fired in that leg, and at least the configured minimum number of these checks hold: prior RSI ≥ overbought threshold and current RSI < prior RSI; prior high ≥ prior upper band and current close < current upper band; a confirmed pivot high; current volume ≥ volume multiplier × volume SMA) OR (a coil short fade fired, the configured confirmation window has not expired, recent volume SMA ≥ pre-fire volume SMA × (1 + surge threshold)) OR (relative volume ≥ spike threshold and close < open) OR (a new band opens on the short trailing-stop side, the warmup count of gaps is met, and its gap > expansion threshold × average gap).
Exit: Long position, trail is armed in signal-only mode and current close < prior long stop.
Exit: Short position, trail is armed in signal-only mode and current close > prior short stop.
