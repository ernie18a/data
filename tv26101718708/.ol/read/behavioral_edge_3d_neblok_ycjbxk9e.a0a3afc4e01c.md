Calculation: Crowd Emotion Index = EMA of the weighted mean of five values, rescaled from [-1,1] to [0,100]: (close − fair value)/ATR divided by an extension cap and clipped to [-1,1]; (RSI − 50)/25 clipped to [-1,1]; EMA of candle direction (close > open: +1, close < open: −1, otherwise 0) × relative volume capped at 3, clipped to [-1,1]; EMA of (close − prior close)/ATR divided by 1.5 and clipped to [-1,1]; and close’s position between the lookback lowest low and highest high, scaled to [-1,1]. Fair value is volume-weighted moving average of close, falling back to EMA of close when volume is unavailable. Relative volume = volume / average volume.
Calculation: Rejection candle = range > 0 and (close is at least 60% up the bar and (lower wick is at least 35% of range or close > open)) for bullish rejection; close is at most 40% up the bar and (upper wick is at least 35% of range or close < open) for bearish rejection.
Calculation: Volume climax = relative volume ≥ climax threshold; recent climax = climax on this bar or either of the prior two bars.
Calculation: Bullish emotion divergence = the lowest low of the latest 3 bars ≤ the lookback lowest low, and the lowest Crowd Emotion Index of the latest 3 bars exceeds the lookback lowest index by more than 3. Bearish emotion divergence = the highest high of the latest 3 bars ≥ the lookback highest high, and the highest index of the latest 3 bars is more than 3 below the lookback highest index.
Calculation: Sellers trapped = price sweeps below the prior swing low or prior-day low and closes back above that level, with lower wick ≥ ATR × trap threshold. Buyers trapped = price sweeps above the prior swing high or prior-day high and closes back below that level, with upper wick ≥ ATR × trap threshold. Swing levels use the prior completed chart bars, excluding the current bar. Prior-day levels use the prior completed daily bar (higher timeframe) and apply on intraday charts.
Calculation: Long reversal quality = count of: panic reached within the emotion lookback; volume climax on this or either of the prior two bars; bullish rejection; sellers trapped on this or the prior bar; bullish emotion divergence. Short reversal quality = count of: euphoria reached within the emotion lookback; recent volume climax; bearish rejection; buyers trapped on this or the prior bar; bearish emotion divergence.
Calculation: Long trap quality = count of: sweep of prior-day low; sweep of prior swing low; bullish rejection; current volume climax; Crowd Emotion Index ≤ fear threshold. Short trap quality = count of: sweep of prior-day high; sweep of prior swing high; bearish rejection; current volume climax; Crowd Emotion Index ≥ greed threshold.
Calculation: Fair-value extension = (close − fair value)/ATR. Herding = EMA of candle direction × relative volume capped at 3. Range position = (close − lookback lowest low)/(lookback highest high − lookback lowest low), scaled to [-1,1].
Calculation: Long herd quality = count of: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fair value; relative volume ≥ 1; herding ≥ 0.3; fair-value extension < 2; range position > 0. Short herd quality = count of: fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fair value; relative volume ≥ 1; herding ≤ −0.3; fair-value extension > −2; range position < 0. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low − ATR × stop buffer; short stop = signal-bar high + ATR × stop buffer. Risk = absolute value of entry price − stop. Long target = entry price + risk × target multiple; short target = entry price − risk × target multiple.
Long: bar closes and more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous signal, AND ((Crowd Emotion Index reached the fear threshold within the emotion lookback AND is higher than on the prior bar AND long reversal quality ≥ minimum reversal quality) OR (sellers trapped AND bullish rejection AND long trap quality ≥ minimum trap quality) OR (fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fair value AND Crowd Emotion Index crosses above 50 AND long herd quality ≥ minimum herd quality)).
Short: bar closes and more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the previous signal, AND ((Crowd Emotion Index reached the greed threshold within the emotion lookback AND is lower than on the prior bar AND short reversal quality ≥ minimum reversal quality) OR (buyers trapped AND bearish rejection AND short trap quality ≥ minimum trap quality) OR (fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fair value AND Crowd Emotion Index crosses below 50 AND short herd quality ≥ minimum herd quality)).
Exit: long position, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target (if both occur on the same bar, stop takes precedence).
Exit: short position, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target (if both occur on the same bar, stop takes precedence).
