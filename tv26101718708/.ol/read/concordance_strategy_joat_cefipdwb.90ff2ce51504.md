Calculation: Local baseline trend = fast EMA of close > medium EMA of close > slow EMA of close for bullish alignment; reverse all inequalities for bearish alignment; fast EMA is shorter than medium, which is shorter than slow
Calculation: Higher-timeframe baselines = fast, medium, and slow EMAs of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe, with the same length ordering as local baselines
Calculation: Trend score = +2 for bullish local baseline trend or −2 for bearish; +2 for bullish higher-timeframe baseline trend or −2 for bearish; +1 for positive fast-baseline slope or −1 for negative; +1 for positive medium-baseline slope or −1 for negative; when ADX exceeds its gate, +1 if +DI > −DI or −1 if −DI > +DI; outside a squeeze, add +1 if volatility-band width exceeds 1.15 times its own moving average and close is above the slow baseline, or −1 if below
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands (EMA basis, standard-deviation width) are inside Keltner Channels (EMA basis and EMA true range width)
Calculation: Bullish regime = trend score ≥ 3 and ADX > 0.75 times its gate; bearish regime = trend score ≤ −3 and ADX > 0.75 times its gate
Calculation: Soft bullish state = trend score > 0, bullish local baseline trend, and higher-timeframe fast EMA ≥ higher-timeframe medium EMA; soft bearish state = trend score < 0, bearish local baseline trend, and higher-timeframe fast EMA ≤ higher-timeframe medium EMA
Calculation: Confirmed daily/weekly levels = high and low of the last completed daily/weekly bar
Calculation: Active bullish sweep = a confirmed bar's low falls below the prior daily or enabled weekly low by the ATR buffer and closes above that level, remaining active through the configured sweep window; bearish sweep = high exceeds the prior daily or enabled weekly high by the ATR buffer and closes below it, active for the same window
Calculation: Confirmed structure levels = latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, using equal left/right pivot lengths
Calculation: Bullish liquidity stack = active bullish sweep and close crosses above the latest confirmed structure high, with a bullish displacement candle if displacement is required; bearish liquidity stack = active bearish sweep and close crosses below the latest confirmed structure low, with a bearish displacement candle if displacement is required. Displacement means candle body exceeds its 20-bar average body times the displacement factor
Calculation: Local and higher-timeframe retracement levels = Fibonacci levels 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 70.5%, and 78.6% between the respective lookback high and low; use the same lookback length on both, and use only completed bars for the higher-timeframe lookback. Orient levels by whether the low occurred after the high
Calculation: Retracement confluence count = number of corresponding local and higher-timeframe levels whose distance is no greater than the configured percentage tolerance of close
Calculation: Bullish OTE = local retracement is oriented up and close lies between the 61.8% and 78.6% levels; bearish OTE = oriented down and close lies between those levels
Calculation: Higher-timeframe retracement agreement = local and higher-timeframe retracements have the same orientation
Calculation: Pressure composite = 0.55 × z-score of cumulative money flow (close-location value × volume, weighted by max(relative volume, 1)) + 0.25 × (RSI − 50) / 10 + 0.20 × z-score of volume × (close − open); z-score uses the indicated rolling lookback, and the RSI length is independent
Calculation: Bullish pressure = pressure composite > 0 and greater than its prior-bar value; bearish pressure = pressure composite < 0 and less than its prior-bar value
Calculation: Sigma channel = choose the longest tested close-price linear-regression window with R² ≥ 0.50 that has the highest R²; channel boundaries are regression endpoint ± 1.4 residual standard deviations
Calculation: Bullish sigma signal = close crosses above the lower sigma boundary and regression slope > 0; bearish sigma signal = close crosses below the upper sigma boundary and regression slope < 0
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average; bullish ledger = volume / its lookback highest volume > 0.70, delta z-score > 0, and relative volume ≥ its gate; bearish ledger uses delta z-score < 0 and the same other conditions
Calculation: Long score = 2×bullish regime + 2×bullish liquidity stack + 1 if OTE is not required or bullish OTE + 1 if higher-timeframe agreement is not required or present + 1 if retracement confluence count ≥ 2 (0.5 if exactly 1) + 1×bullish pressure + 1×bullish sigma signal + 1×bullish ledger + 1 if relative volume meets its gate
Calculation: Short score = 2×bearish regime + 2×bearish liquidity stack + 1 if OTE is not required or bearish OTE + 1 if higher-timeframe agreement is not required or present + 1 if retracement confluence count ≥ 2 (0.5 if exactly 1) + 1×bearish pressure + 1×bearish sigma signal + 1×bearish ledger + 1 if relative volume meets its gate
Calculation: Session pass = always true on non-intraday charts; otherwise true during an enabled London or New York session and outside the configured closing window, or always true if both sessions are disabled
Long: bar is confirmed AND session pass AND ((long score ≥ entry score gate AND bullish regime AND higher-timeframe retracement agreement) OR (long score ≥ max(entry score gate − 1.5, 3) AND soft bullish state AND bullish pressure AND bullish sigma signal AND (retracement confluence count ≥ 1 OR bullish ledger))) AND position side is flat or short
Short: bar is confirmed AND session pass AND ((short score ≥ entry score gate AND bearish regime AND higher-timeframe retracement agreement) OR (short score ≥ max(entry score gate − 1.5, 3) AND soft bearish state AND bearish pressure AND bearish sigma signal AND (retracement confluence count ≥ 1 OR bearish ledger))) AND position side is flat or long
Calculation: Initial long risk = signal-bar close minus (minimum of signal-bar low, prior daily low, prior weekly low, and latest confirmed structure low, using signal-bar low when a level is unavailable) minus ATR times the structure buffer and stop multiplier; initial short risk = (maximum of signal-bar high, prior daily high, prior weekly high, and latest confirmed structure high, using signal-bar high when unavailable) plus ATR times both buffers, minus signal-bar close
Calculation: Long live stop = maximum of entry price − initial long risk, entry price when close ≥ entry price + initial long risk × break-even trigger (otherwise entry price − initial long risk), and close − ATR × trail multiplier; short live stop = minimum of entry price + initial short risk, entry price when close ≤ entry price − initial short risk × break-even trigger (otherwise entry price + initial short risk), and close + ATR × trail multiplier
Calculation: Long profit targets = entry price + initial long risk × first or second profit multiple; short profit targets = entry price − initial short risk × first or second profit multiple
Exit: long, low ≤ long live stop OR high ≥ first long profit target OR high ≥ second long profit target OR (trend score ≤ 0 AND pressure composite < 0) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry ≥ maximum bars in trade)
Exit: short, high ≥ short live stop OR low ≤ first short profit target OR low ≤ second short profit target OR (trend score ≥ 0 AND pressure composite > 0) OR (time exit enabled AND bars since entry ≥ maximum bars in trade)
