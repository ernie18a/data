Calculation: Bullish structure = the latest confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high AND the latest confirmed swing low is higher than the preceding confirmed swing low; bearish structure = both are lower.
Calculation: Demand zone = a zone around a confirmed swing low, buffered by a percentage parameter; merge overlapping zones and count touches. A demand zone is valid after at least two swing-low touches. Supply zone = the same construction around confirmed swing highs.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = close crosses above the second-latest confirmed swing high; bearish break of structure = close crosses below the second-latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bullish retest = after a bullish break of structure, low reaches the broken swing-high level within a percentage buffer and close finishes above it; clear the retest flag after it occurs. Bearish retest = after a bearish break of structure, high reaches the broken swing-low level within a percentage buffer and close finishes below it; clear the retest flag after it occurs.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of these conditions: bullish structure, close inside a valid demand zone, bullish break of structure, bullish retest, close above daily VWAP, shorter-lookback EMA above longer-lookback EMA, RSI above its midpoint. Bearish score = count of the corresponding bearish conditions: bearish structure, close inside a valid supply zone, bearish break of structure, bearish retest, close below daily VWAP, shorter-lookback EMA below longer-lookback EMA, RSI below its midpoint.
Calculation: Cooldown = swing-length parameter multiplied by a larger factor on lower chart intervals and a smaller factor otherwise.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR multiplied by the stop multiplier, which is smaller on lower chart intervals.
Long: bullish score >= minimum score AND no active long or short signal state AND trading is not locked after the configured number of consecutive losses AND no prior long signal exists or bars since the prior long signal > cooldown.
Short: bearish score >= minimum score AND no active long or short signal state AND trading is not locked after the configured number of consecutive losses AND no prior short signal exists or bars since the prior short signal > cooldown.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - initial risk distance OR (high >= entry price + 3 × initial risk distance AND low > entry price - initial risk distance)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + initial risk distance OR (low <= entry price - 3 × initial risk distance AND high < entry price + initial risk distance)
