Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high / lowest low at a pivot confirmed by bars on both sides; track the latest of each.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = current high exceeds the latest confirmed swing high by a configurable percentage; bearish structure break = current low falls below the latest confirmed swing low by a configurable percentage. A new break event occurs when the corresponding break was false on the prior bar. Classify a bullish event as a change of character unless prior structure bias was bullish, when it is a break of structure; classify bearish events symmetrically. Structure pivots and breaks use a higher timeframe, using its current forming bar in realtime (higher-timeframe data uses lookahead off).
Calculation: MACD = fast EMA of price minus slow EMA of price; signal = EMA of MACD; histogram = MACD minus signal. Calculate classic and fusion MACDs on the chart timeframe unless configured otherwise; if using a higher timeframe, use its current forming bar (lookahead off). Classic lengths are configurable; fusion MACD uses a second, independently configurable set of lengths.
Calculation: ATR = average true range. Use the chart timeframe unless configured otherwise; if using a higher timeframe, use its current forming bar (lookahead off). If ATR is unavailable, use chart-timeframe ATR as fallback for risk levels, but volatility readiness is false.
Calculation: Quality score = mean of structure alignment, momentum strength, trend alignment, ATR volatility, and volume intensity scores. Structure, momentum, and trend scores are 1 for bullish, 0 for bearish, and 0.5 otherwise. ATR volatility score = clamp(ATR / 14-bar SMA of ATR, 0, 1.5) / 1.5. Volume intensity score = clamp((volume / volume SMA) / volume threshold, 0, 1.5) / 1.5 when volume gating is enabled, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Protective long stop = greater of (current close − ATR × stop multiple) and (highest high since entry − ATR × trail-distance multiple) when trailing is active; otherwise current close − ATR × stop multiple. Trailing is active once highest high since entry − entry price is at least ATR × trail-activation multiple. Protective short stop = lesser of (current close + ATR × stop multiple) and (lowest low since entry + ATR × trail-distance multiple) when trailing is active; otherwise current close + ATR × stop multiple. Trailing is active once entry price − lowest low since entry is at least ATR × trail-activation multiple.
Long: bullish structure-break event OR (ATR available AND ATR meets minimum volatility filter, or that filter is disabled, AND bullish structure-break event AND both MACDs are bullish [each MACD line > its signal line AND MACD line > 0 AND histogram > prior histogram] AND close > directional EMA AND close > trend-guide EMA AND (volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold) AND quality score meets threshold when quality gate is enabled AND daily trade budget available AND entry cooldown elapsed AND optional crypto impulse gate passes [fast EMA > slow EMA when enabled]).
Short: bearish structure-break event OR (ATR available AND ATR meets minimum volatility filter, or that filter is disabled, AND bearish structure-break event AND both MACDs are bearish [each MACD line < its signal line AND MACD line < 0 AND histogram < prior histogram] AND close < directional EMA AND close < trend-guide EMA AND (volume gate disabled OR volume > volume SMA × volume threshold) AND quality score meets threshold when quality gate is enabled AND daily trade budget available AND entry cooldown elapsed AND optional crypto impulse gate passes [fast EMA < slow EMA, or fast EMA is not above slow EMA, when enabled] AND short-only restriction is disabled).
Exit: Long, low <= protective long stop OR high >= current close + ATR × take-profit multiple OR short-entry condition.
Exit: Short, high >= protective short stop OR low <= current close − ATR × take-profit multiple OR long-entry condition.
