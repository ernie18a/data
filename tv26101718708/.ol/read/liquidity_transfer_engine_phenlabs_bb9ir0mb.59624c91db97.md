Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the bars on both sides, using equal lookbacks before and after; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Equal-high/low pool = two consecutive confirmed pivot highs/lows whose price difference is within an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Reclaim boundary = sweep candle’s open-close midpoint, prior confirmed pivot high-low midpoint, or last internal swing (the latest pivot low for a bullish sweep, latest pivot high for a bearish sweep), according to the selected mode; Auto uses the prior range midpoint if it lies beyond the sweep extreme in the reclaim direction, otherwise the sweep candle midpoint.
Long: low < a confirmed pivot low − ATR-based minimum penetration AND close > that pivot low AND close > reclaim boundary AND low < sweep extreme; OR an equal-low pool is detected AND low < the latest confirmed pivot low − ATR-based minimum penetration AND close > that pivot low AND close > reclaim boundary AND low < sweep extreme; OR at session end, low < session low − ATR-based minimum penetration AND close > session low AND close > reclaim boundary AND low < sweep extreme.
Short: high > a confirmed pivot high + ATR-based minimum penetration AND close < that pivot high AND close < reclaim boundary AND high > sweep extreme; OR an equal-high pool is detected AND high > the latest confirmed pivot high + ATR-based minimum penetration AND close < that pivot high AND close < reclaim boundary AND high > sweep extreme; OR at session end, high > session high + ATR-based minimum penetration AND close < session high AND close < reclaim boundary AND high > sweep extreme.
