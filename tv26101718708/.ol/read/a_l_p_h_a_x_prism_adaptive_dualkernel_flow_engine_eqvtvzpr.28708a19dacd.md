Calculation: Slow kernel = Nadaraya–Watson weighted estimate of close over a lookback, using the selected kernel and bandwidth, then EMA-smoothed.
Calculation: Fast kernel = Same estimate as the slow kernel, with a shorter bandwidth and less EMA smoothing.
Calculation: Residual Z-score = (close − slow kernel) / EMA of the rolling standard deviation of (close − slow kernel).
Calculation: Efficiency Ratio = absolute net close change over its lookback / sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over its lookback / high-low range over that lookback) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Regime = Chop if Choppiness Index ≥ its threshold; otherwise Trend if Efficiency Ratio ≥ its threshold and |fast kernel − slow kernel| > 0.15 × residual standard deviation; otherwise Balance. Trend is Bull when fast kernel > slow kernel, Bear when fast kernel < slow kernel.
Calculation: Bull rejection candle = close > open AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) > 0.52. Bear rejection candle = close < open AND (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) > 0.52.
Calculation: Kernel spread = fast kernel − slow kernel. Bull spread = spread > 0 AND spread > its prior-bar value. Bear spread = spread < 0 AND spread < its prior-bar value.
Calculation: Residual bands = slow kernel ± band multiplier × residual standard deviation. A crossover above the upper band or crossunder below the lower band compares the current close and band with their prior-bar values.
Calculation: Pivot divergence = Bull when a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed pivot low, the slow kernel at that pivot is higher than at the prior pivot, and close ≤ lower residual band + residual standard deviation; Bear when a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior confirmed pivot high, the slow kernel at that pivot is lower than at the prior pivot, and close ≥ upper residual band − residual standard deviation. Require the configured cooldown since the prior same-direction divergence.
Calculation: Bull confluence score = 1 each for (Bull Trend OR Bull spread), (Z-score > 0 OR kernel spread > 0), higher-timeframe bull bias, volume ≥ its moving average × configured multiplier, bull rejection candle, non-Chop regime, and any qualifying enabled long setup. Bear score uses the corresponding bearish conditions. Higher-timeframe bias uses the last completed bar of a higher timeframe: fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA for Bull; fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA for Bear.
Long: Bull divergence AND bull rejection candle AND (Balance OR Bear Trend).
Long: (Bull Trend AND crossover above upper residual band AND Bull spread AND bull rejection candle) OR (Bull Trend AND low ≤ slow kernel + 0.25 × ATR AND close > slow kernel AND Bull spread AND bull rejection candle) OR (Balance AND residual Z-score ≤ −fade threshold AND bull rejection candle) OR (Bull divergence AND bull rejection candle AND (Balance OR Bear Trend)); AND long confluence score ≥ its threshold AND non-Chop AND volume filter passes when enabled AND higher-timeframe bias is Bull when enabled AND session filter passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Bear divergence AND bear rejection candle AND (Balance OR Bull Trend).
Short: (Bear Trend AND crossunder below lower residual band AND Bear spread AND bear rejection candle) OR (Bear Trend AND high ≥ slow kernel − 0.25 × ATR AND close < slow kernel AND Bear spread AND bear rejection candle) OR (Balance AND residual Z-score ≥ fade threshold AND bear rejection candle) OR (Bear divergence AND bear rejection candle AND (Balance OR Bull Trend)); AND bear confluence score ≥ its threshold AND non-Chop AND volume filter passes when enabled AND higher-timeframe bias is Bear when enabled AND session filter passes when enabled AND signal cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: Long, regime is Chop.
Exit: Short, regime is Chop.
