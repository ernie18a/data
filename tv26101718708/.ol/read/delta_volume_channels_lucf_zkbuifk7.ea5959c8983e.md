Calculation: Volume delta = sum of intrabar volumes, positive when an intrabar closes above its open, negative when below; if open equals close, use the close’s change from the prior intrabar, and if unchanged, carry forward the last polarity. Intrabars use a lower timeframe and are evaluated when the chart bar closes.
Calculation: DV-weighted moving average = the same type and lookback as the reference moving average, applied to the price source adjusted by signed delta-volume percentage times its absolute value and relative-volume percentile weight; cap the adjustment by a configurable number of reference-source standard deviations. Relative volume percentile uses a configurable lookback.
Calculation: DV bull state = DV-weighted moving average > reference moving average; DV strong bull state = DV bull state AND close > reference moving average AND both moving averages are rising. DV bear state = DV-weighted moving average <= reference moving average; DV strong bear state = DV bear state AND close < reference moving average AND both moving averages are falling.
Calculation: Divergence = volume delta is nonzero AND the reference and DV-weighted moving averages have different slope directions.
Calculation: Divergence channel = track the high and low (or bar body extremes) of divergence bars; start a new channel after a prior channel is breached, otherwise expand its levels when a divergence bar extends them. Bull state begins when the selected breach price is above the channel top; bear state begins when the selected breach price is below the channel bottom. Strong states require the corresponding DV strong state.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, transition into DV bull or DV strong bull state OR transition into divergence-channel bull or strong bull state; apply any enabled filters: bullish bar polarity, rising close versus prior close, CCI above zero, rising volume, a divergence within the configured recent-bar lookback, and/or bullish Efficient Work.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, transition into DV bear or DV strong bear state OR transition into divergence-channel bear or strong bear state; apply any enabled filters: bearish bar polarity, falling close versus prior close, CCI below zero, rising volume, a divergence within the configured recent-bar lookback, and/or bearish Efficient Work.
