Calculation: Fast and slow trailing rails = linear regression of hl2 over shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively, offset by ATR × an adaptive multiplier; initialize on the side of the regression line where close lies, then ratchet the active rail toward price (maximum of prior rail and lower band in an up state; minimum of prior rail and upper band in a down state). Flip to up when a confirmed close crosses above the prior rail, and to down when it crosses below. The adaptive multiplier is increased for strong directional power or an active 5D box signal, reduced for weak directional power, and otherwise unchanged.
Long: Fast rail ≥ slow rail OR (fast rail ≥ slow rail AND close < fast rail) OR (fast rail ≥ slow rail AND prior close < prior fast rail AND close > fast rail)
Short: Fast rail < slow rail OR (fast rail < slow rail AND close > fast rail) OR (fast rail < slow rail AND prior close > prior fast rail AND close < fast rail)
