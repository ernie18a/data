Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of open; use separate shorter-lookback and longer-lookback EMAs. For each dashboard timeframe, calculate from its most recent completed bar (higher or lower than the chart timeframe); on the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Calculation: MACD line = 12-lookback EMA of open − 26-lookback EMA of open; signal line = 9-lookback EMA of the MACD line; histogram = MACD line − signal line. Calculate on each dashboard timeframe using its most recent completed bar (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), or the current bar on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: StochRSI %K = 3-lookback SMA of ((RSI of open − lowest RSI over the StochRSI lookback) / (highest RSI over the StochRSI lookback − lowest RSI over the StochRSI lookback) × 100); %D = 3-lookback SMA of %K. Calculate on each dashboard timeframe using its most recent completed bar (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), or the current bar on the chart timeframe.
Long: On any dashboard timeframe, open > shorter-lookback EMA OR open > longer-lookback EMA OR (MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD line > 0 AND signal line > 0) OR StochRSI %K > %D
Short: On any dashboard timeframe, open <= shorter-lookback EMA OR open <= longer-lookback EMA OR (MACD histogram <= 0 AND MACD line <= 0 AND signal line <= 0) OR StochRSI %K < %D
