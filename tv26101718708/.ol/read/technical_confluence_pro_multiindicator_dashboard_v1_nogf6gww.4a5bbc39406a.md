Calculation: Bullish EMA stack = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA > long EMA; bearish EMA stack is the reverse (four periods ordered shortest to longest)
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = stochastic of RSI; bullish when its 3-bar SMA > the 3-bar SMA of that line; otherwise bearish
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint of the lookback high/low range and the Bollinger Band midpoint, over a configurable momentum lookback; bullish when above zero and rising, bearish when below zero and falling. Bollinger Bands use a configurable lookback and standard-deviation multiplier; the Keltner Channel uses the same lookback and a configurable ATR multiplier.
Calculation: QQE trend = smoothed RSI compared with a trailing line initialized to smoothed RSI; the line moves to smoothed RSI minus/plus a configurable factor times an EMA of absolute RSI changes when smoothed RSI rises above/falls below the prior line by that band, and otherwise holds its prior value.
Calculation: Kalman trend = normalized difference of fast and slow EMAs of a price estimate updated each bar by prior estimate + gain × (close − prior estimate); normalize the difference to 0–100 over a lookback. Bullish when normalized value > 60 and the difference is rising; bearish when normalized value < 40 and the difference is falling. The slow EMA lookback is longer than the fast EMA lookback.
Calculation: Wave Trend = EMA of the normalized deviation of hlc3 from its EMA, where the deviation is scaled by 0.015 times the EMA of absolute deviation; compare this value with its 4-bar SMA. Bullish when greater than that SMA and below the configurable overbought level; bearish when less than that SMA and above the configurable oversold level.
Calculation: Weighted confluence score = sum of: EMA stack (+2 bullish, −2 bearish, else 0); Supertrend (+2 bullish, −2 bearish); RSI (+1 above bullish threshold, −1 below bearish threshold, else 0); Stochastic RSI (+1 bullish, else −1); MACD (+1 line > signal, else −1); ADX/DI (+1 when ADX > minimum and +DI > −DI, −1 when ADX > minimum and −DI > +DI, else 0); close/VWAP (+1 above, else −1); OBV/21-bar EMA of OBV (+1 above, else −1); volume/20-bar SMA of volume (+1 above the average times a configurable multiplier, else −1); 60-minute MACD component (+1 when MACD line minus signal > signal, else −1); squeeze momentum (+1 bullish, −1 bearish, else 0); QQE trend (+1 bullish, else −1); Kalman trend (+1 bullish, −1 bearish, else 0); Wave Trend (+1 bullish, −1 bearish, else 0); and EMA alignment on each requested timeframe (+2 bullish, −2 bearish, else 0 for the first three timeframes; +3/−3 for the last two). Requested-timeframe calculations use the requested bars, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; an unoffset request can use a still-forming bar.
Calculation: Normalized score = clamp(round((weighted confluence score + 31) / 62 × 100), 0, 100).
Long: Normalized score >= 58 OR fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA > long EMA OR Supertrend bullish OR RSI > bullish threshold OR Stochastic RSI K > D OR MACD line > signal OR (ADX > minimum AND +DI > −DI) OR close > VWAP OR OBV > its 21-bar EMA OR volume > its 20-bar average × multiplier OR 60-minute MACD line minus signal > signal OR squeeze momentum > 0 and rising OR smoothed RSI > QQE trailing line OR (normalized Kalman oscillator > 60 AND oscillator rising) OR (Wave Trend > its 4-bar SMA AND Wave Trend < overbought level) OR requested-timeframe EMA(21) > EMA(50) > EMA(200).
Short: Normalized score <= 42 OR fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA < long EMA OR Supertrend bearish OR RSI < bearish threshold OR Stochastic RSI K <= D OR MACD line <= signal OR (ADX > minimum AND −DI > +DI) OR close <= VWAP OR OBV <= its 21-bar EMA OR volume <= its 20-bar average × multiplier OR 60-minute MACD line minus signal <= signal OR squeeze momentum < 0 and falling OR smoothed RSI <= QQE trailing line OR (normalized Kalman oscillator < 40 AND oscillator falling) OR (Wave Trend < its 4-bar SMA AND Wave Trend > oversold level) OR requested-timeframe EMA(21) < EMA(50) < EMA(200).
