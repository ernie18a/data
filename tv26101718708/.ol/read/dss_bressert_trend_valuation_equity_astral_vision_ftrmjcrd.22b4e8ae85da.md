Calculation: DSS = EMA of the stochastic of the EMA-smoothed stochastic of close, high, and low; both stochastic stages use the same lookback, and both EMA stages use the same smoothing length. Calculated on the selected resolution timeframe, using its current bar, including while it is still forming; this timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Trigger = EMA of DSS using its own lookback.
Long: DSS crosses above Trigger OR DSS is at or above the overbought threshold
Short: DSS crosses below Trigger OR DSS is at or below the oversold threshold
Exit: long, DSS crosses below Trigger
