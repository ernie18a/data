Calculation: Bar delta = estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume; classify lower-timeframe bars as buying when close > open and selling when close < open, using comparison with the previous lower-timeframe close for ties (split volume equally if unchanged); if intrabar data is unavailable or Bar Geometry is selected, buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and sell volume = volume − buy volume (split equally when high = low). Lower-timeframe bars are lower than the chart timeframe and belong to the current chart bar; use the chart bar itself when the selected timeframe is not lower.
Calculation: Delta z-score = (bar delta − its moving average over a lookback) / the standard deviation of bar delta over the same lookback.
Calculation: Imbalance level = volume-weighted average price of the spike’s lower-timeframe bars; with Bar Geometry, use the chart bar’s typical price (high + low + close) / 3. A bullish or bearish level remains active until mitigated.
Long: Delta z-score ≥ spike threshold AND bar delta > 0 OR an active bullish level is mitigated (low < level price with Wick trigger, or close < level price with Close trigger).
Short: Delta z-score ≤ −spike threshold AND bar delta < 0 OR an active bearish level is mitigated (high > level price with Wick trigger, or close > level price with Close trigger).
