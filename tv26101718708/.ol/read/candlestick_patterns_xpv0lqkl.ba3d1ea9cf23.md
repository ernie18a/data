Calculation: Candle body = absolute difference between close and open; upper wick = high minus the greater of open and close; lower wick = the lesser of open and close minus low; range = high minus low.
Calculation: Significant body = candle body is at least the configured minimum size times ATR; significant range = candle range is at least the configured minimum size times ATR.
Calculation: Current-timeframe trend = EMA with the shorter lookback is above the EMA with the longer lookback (uptrend); otherwise downtrend.
Calculation: Hammer shape = nonzero body, significant range, lower wick at least the configured wick-to-body multiple times the body, and upper wick no more than half the body. Inverted hammer shape = same, with upper and lower wick conditions reversed.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior candle bearish, current candle bullish, significant body, current close at or above prior open, current open at or below prior close, and current body at least the configured multiple of prior body. Bearish engulfing = prior candle bullish, current candle bearish, significant body, current close at or below prior open, current open at or above prior close, and current body at least the configured multiple of prior body.
Calculation: Bullish harami = prior candle bearish, current candle bullish, prior range at least the configured minimum size times current ATR, current high below prior high, current low above prior low, and current body less than 60% of prior body. Bearish harami = same range and body conditions, with prior candle bullish and current candle bearish.
Calculation: Piercing line = prior candle bearish, current candle bullish with significant body, current open below the configured gap reference, and current close above prior close plus the configured penetration fraction of the prior body. Dark cloud cover = prior candle bullish, current candle bearish with significant body, current open above the configured gap reference, and current close below prior close minus the configured penetration fraction of the prior body. The gap reference is either the prior low/high (strict mode) or prior close (relaxed mode).
Calculation: Morning star = candle two bars ago bearish, prior candle bearish with body less than 30% of its range, current candle bullish with significant body, and current close above the midpoint of the candle two bars ago. Evening star = candle two bars ago bullish, prior candle bullish with body less than 30% of its range, current candle bearish with significant body, and current close below the midpoint of the candle two bars ago.
Calculation: Three white soldiers = current and prior two candles bullish, closes rise across the three candles, current open is between prior close and prior open, and current body is significant. Three black crows = current and prior two candles bearish, closes fall across the three candles, current open is between prior close and prior open, and current body is significant.
Long: (hammer shape AND downtrend OR inverted hammer shape AND downtrend OR bullish engulfing OR bullish harami OR piercing line OR morning star OR three white soldiers) AND (trend filter disabled OR uptrend) AND at least the configured minimum number of bars since the last bullish or bearish label.
Short: (hammer shape AND uptrend OR inverted hammer shape AND uptrend OR bearish engulfing OR bearish harami OR dark cloud cover OR evening star OR three black crows) AND (trend filter disabled OR downtrend) AND at least the configured minimum number of bars since the last bullish or bearish label.
