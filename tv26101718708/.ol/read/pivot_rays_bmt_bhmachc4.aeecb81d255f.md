Calculation: Previous-period high/low = high/low of the last completed bar on the corresponding higher timeframe; use the chart timeframe when same-timeframe levels are enabled.
Calculation: Session high/low = maximum high/minimum low of the last completed session, calculated from chart-timeframe bars.
Long: For an enabled previous-period or session low, low < level since the level was established AND confirmed close > level AND no earlier reclaim signal for that level in the current period or session; previous-period timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe unless same-timeframe levels are enabled, and session levels are used only on charts below the configured session timeframe.
Short: For an enabled previous-period or session high, high > level since the level was established AND confirmed close < level AND no earlier reclaim signal for that level in the current period or session; previous-period timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe unless same-timeframe levels are enabled, and session levels are used only on charts below the configured session timeframe.
