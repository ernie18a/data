Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Break attempt = current high above prior range high by at least the configured ATR multiple, or current low below prior range low by at least that multiple; if both occur, use the side with the larger extension beyond its edge.
Calculation: Active break = on a new break attempt, freeze the broken edge, opposite range edge, midpoint, and ATR; track the most extreme high/low and bars elapsed since the attempt. Keep the event unresolved until it is confirmed as a trap or accepted, or expires.
Calculation: Trap risk = a 0–100 weighted score of reclaim speed, close-back-inside distance normalized by ATR, rejection-wick proportion, relative volume, and whether price closed back inside or touched back across the broken edge. Reduce the score when the break is still holding and break quality is high.
Calculation: Break quality = a 0–100 weighted score of close distance beyond the broken edge normalized by ATR, maximum extension beyond the edge normalized by ATR, candle-body proportion, and relative volume.
Long: lower-edge break attempt OR (active lower-edge break AND (close back above the broken edge AND trap risk ≥ configured trap threshold AND event age ≤ configured acceptance window OR event is fresh AND no confirmed trap AND (price touched back across the broken edge OR close is above it OR trap risk ≥ configured watch threshold) OR closes remain below the broken edge for at least the configured acceptance bars AND break quality ≥ configured minimum))
