Calculation: Whale-holdings change = difference between the current and previous close of the whale-holdings data series on a higher timeframe, using the higher-timeframe bar as it forms.
Calculation: Upper/lower Bollinger bands = the moving average of whale-holdings change plus/minus the same deviation multiplier times its standard deviation over the same lookback.
Long: Whale-holdings change > upper Bollinger band AND whale-holdings change > 0
Short: Whale-holdings change < lower Bollinger band AND whale-holdings change < 0
