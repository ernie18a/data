Calculation: Technical state = breakout if close > the highest high over a lookback period excluding the current bar, volume > a multiplier of average volume, close > short-term EMA, and short-term EMA > long-term EMA; otherwise healthy pullback if the low reaches within a tolerance above either EMA, close > that EMA, short-term EMA > long-term EMA, close > SMA, and RSI > 50; otherwise bull trend if close > short-term EMA, short-term EMA > long-term EMA, close > SMA, and RSI > 55; otherwise distribution if close < long-term EMA and MACD histogram is lower than on the prior bar; otherwise bear/weak if close < SMA, short-term EMA < long-term EMA, and RSI < 45; otherwise accumulation if close is within a tolerance of SMA and RSI is between 45 and 55 inclusive; otherwise neutral. Apply these categories in the stated order.
Calculation: Probability of next bull trend from a state = completed transitions from that state to bull trend divided by all completed transitions from that state; count transitions between consecutive completed chart bars.
Long: technical state = breakout OR technical state = healthy pullback OR (technical state is breakout or healthy pullback AND its completed-transition sample count meets a minimum AND probability of next bull trend from that state meets a minimum)
