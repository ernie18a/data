Calculation: ATR-normalized SMA slope = (current SMA − SMA from the lookback bars earlier) ÷ lookback ÷ ATR, calculated separately on the chart timeframe and each configured timeframe. Use the last completed bar of each aggregated timeframe; each may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe.
Long: Any configured timeframe’s ATR-normalized SMA slope > flat threshold OR bullish configured timeframes > bearish configured timeframes in the scalp block OR bullish configured timeframes > bearish configured timeframes in the swing block.
Short: Any configured timeframe’s ATR-normalized SMA slope < −flat threshold OR bearish configured timeframes > bullish configured timeframes in the scalp block OR bearish configured timeframes > bullish configured timeframes in the swing block.
