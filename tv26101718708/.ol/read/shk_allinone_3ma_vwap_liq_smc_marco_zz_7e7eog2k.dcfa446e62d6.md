Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-term moving averages = the selected moving average of close, with strictly increasing lookbacks.
Calculation: Signal score = +1 for each enabled bullish Heikin-Ashi, Supertrend, or UT Bot condition; −1 for each enabled bearish condition; 0 for a neutral condition.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = the average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close, initialized as (open + close) / 2.
Calculation: UT Bot trailing stop = a stop on Heikin-Ashi close that trails by the key-value multiple of ATR; when close and the previous close are above the previous stop, raise the stop to at least close minus that distance; when both are below, lower it to at most close plus that distance; otherwise set it on the opposite side of close.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low at the lookback bars before the current bar that is strictly above/below the intervening bars; the new pivot is confirmed when the detected leg changes. Internal structure uses a shorter lookback than swing structure.
Calculation: Deviation-filtered pivot top/bottom = a rolling high/low candidate at the configured depth before the current bar; accept an opposite-side candidate only when its percentage move from the prior accepted pivot exceeds ATR(10) / close × 100 × the deviation parameter. Replace a same-side pivot only with a more extreme candidate.
Calculation: Zigzag pivot labels = track rolling highs and lows over the configured depth; update the active extreme while direction persists, and switch direction to a low/high after the configured backstep. Label a high HH if it is at least the previous high, otherwise LH; label a low LL if it is at most the previous low, otherwise HL. Internal Zigzag uses its own, shorter-depth settings.
Long: short-term moving average crosses above medium-term moving average OR medium-term moving average crosses above long-term moving average OR signal score > 0 OR close crosses above an unbroken internal or swing pivot high OR a bullish fair-value gap forms OR a bullish three-bar Bollinger reversal occurs (two bars ago close below lower band and close < open; previous bar close above lower band and close > open; current close > previous high) OR a deviation-filtered pivot bottom is accepted OR a Zigzag low is labeled HL or LL.
Short: short-term moving average crosses below medium-term moving average OR medium-term moving average crosses below long-term moving average OR signal score < 0 OR close crosses below an unbroken internal or swing pivot low OR a bearish fair-value gap forms OR a bearish three-bar Bollinger reversal occurs (two bars ago close above upper band and close > open; previous bar close below upper band and close < open; current close < previous low) OR a deviation-filtered pivot top is accepted OR a Zigzag high is labeled LH or HH.
Exit: No long or short exit decision is defined.
