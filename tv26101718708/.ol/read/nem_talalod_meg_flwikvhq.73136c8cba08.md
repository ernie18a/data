Calculation: UT trailing stop = let previous stop be the prior bar’s stop, or 0 initially; if close and prior close are both above the previous stop, use max(previous stop, close − key × ATR); if both are below it, use min(previous stop, close + key × ATR); otherwise use close − key × ATR when close is above the previous stop, else close + key × ATR
Calculation: Distance from fast EMA = abs(close − fast EMA) / close
Long: Close crosses above UT trailing stop AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND close > EMA on a lower timeframe (using its last completed bar) AND close > EMA on a higher timeframe (using its last completed bar) AND ADX > minimum ADX AND Distance from fast EMA > minimum distance AND bar timestamp hour >= start hour AND bar timestamp hour < end hour
Short: Close crosses below UT trailing stop AND close < short-term EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND close < EMA on a lower timeframe (using its last completed bar) AND close < EMA on a higher timeframe (using its last completed bar) AND ADX > minimum ADX AND Distance from fast EMA > minimum distance AND bar timestamp hour >= start hour AND bar timestamp hour < end hour
Exit: long, high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR short-term EMA crosses below long-term EMA OR bar timestamp hour >= end hour
Exit: short, low <= entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR short-term EMA crosses above long-term EMA OR bar timestamp hour >= end hour
