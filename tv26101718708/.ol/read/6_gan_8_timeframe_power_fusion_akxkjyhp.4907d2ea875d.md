Calculation: Eight-timeframe direction count = number of monitored timeframe sine curves whose current value is greater than their prior value; each curve is that timeframe's close plus chart-timeframe ATR times a common amplitude factor times a sine wave with its own period and phase. For timeframes higher than the chart, use the last completed bar; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar; for lower timeframes, use the available bar within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Composite sine = average of the eight timeframe sine curves.
Calculation: GAN direction count = number of linear regression lines of close whose current value is greater than their prior value; use progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: GAN bullish alignment = GAN direction count is at least the bullish alignment threshold; GAN bearish alignment = the number of lines not rising is at least the bearish alignment threshold.
Calculation: Volume fusion = average of the 0–1 normalized values of the smoothed merged-bar delta, smoothed cumulative delta, and smoothed OBV. Merged-bar delta is summed volume over the merge lookback, positive when the merged close exceeds its open, negative when it is below, and zero otherwise. Cumulative delta sums merged-bar delta; OBV cumulatively sums merged volume signed by whether the merged close rose or fell from the prior merged close. Normalize each smoothed series against its rolling high and low; use 0.5 when they are equal.
Calculation: Volume resistance break = volume fusion crosses above its prior-bar rolling high. Volume support break = volume fusion crosses below its prior-bar rolling low.
Calculation: Power score = weighted blend of the eight-timeframe direction percentage, GAN direction percentage, close-versus-EMA trend score, volume score, RSI momentum score, and ATR volatility score. Trend score is bullish above the EMA and bearish below it. RSI momentum score is bullish above its upper momentum threshold, bearish below its lower momentum threshold, and neutral between them. Volume score prioritizes a resistance break, then a support break, then whether volume fusion is above or below its EMA, and is neutral otherwise. ATR volatility score is bullish when ATR exceeds its longer-term average and the bar closes up, bearish when ATR exceeds that average and the bar closes down, and neutral otherwise. Round the blended score to the nearest integer.
Long: power score >= bullish score threshold AND composite sine is rising AND close > EMA(close) AND GAN bullish alignment AND NOT volume support break AND previous signal != long
Short: power score <= bearish score threshold AND composite sine is not rising AND close < EMA(close) AND GAN bearish alignment AND NOT volume resistance break AND previous signal != short
