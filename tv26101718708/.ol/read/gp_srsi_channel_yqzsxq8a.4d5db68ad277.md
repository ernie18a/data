Calculation: Smoothed Stochastic RSI sum = K + D, calculated separately for open, high, low, and close on each higher-timeframe bar; K is a short-lookback SMA of Stochastic RSI, and D is a longer-lookback SMA of K. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Channel minimum = 100/800 times the sum across four higher timeframes of each timeframe’s minimum Smoothed Stochastic RSI sum across open, high, low, and close.
Calculation: Channel maximum = 100/800 times the sum across four higher timeframes of each timeframe’s maximum Smoothed Stochastic RSI sum across open, high, low, and close.
Long: Channel minimum <= 5
Short: Channel maximum >= 95
