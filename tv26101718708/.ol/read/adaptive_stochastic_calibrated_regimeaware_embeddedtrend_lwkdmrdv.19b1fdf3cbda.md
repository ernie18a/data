Calculation: Calibrated overbought and oversold levels = rolling upper and lower percentiles of smoothed stochastic %K over the same lookback.
Calculation: Embedded uptrend and downtrend = %K stays at or above the overbought level, or at or below the oversold level, respectively, for a consecutive-bar streak.
Calculation: Range regime = neither (ADX above its trend threshold AND efficiency ratio above its trend threshold) nor (volatility-cluster z-score above its threshold). Trend regime = ADX above its trend threshold AND efficiency ratio above its trend threshold; bullish when close is above its trend EMA, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Cycle phase = detrended price from the dominant-cycle calculation; long phase is at or below zero and rising, short phase is at or above zero and falling.
Calculation: Adaptive oscillator trail = a volatility-scaled trailing band around a CMO-adaptive moving average of %K; bullish when the moving average is above the trail, bearish otherwise.
Calculation: MTF stochastic direction = sign of the 3-period smoothed 14-period stochastic relative to 50, calculated on each configured timeframe using its last completed bar; higher-timeframe bias uses the same calculation on the configured higher timeframe's last completed bar.
Calculation: Conviction = weighted average, scaled to 0–100, of calibrated stretch, regime fit, embedment/trail alignment, MTF agreement, divergence, volume thrust, and optional cross-asset alignment. Apply the optional variance-ratio adjustment to regime fit when enabled.
Long: Range regime AND NOT embedded downtrend AND previous %K at or below previous oversold level AND %K crosses above %D AND long cycle phase (if enabled) AND SNR passes (if enabled) OR Trend regime AND bullish price regime AND (adaptive oscillator moving average crosses above trail OR bullish trail AND %K crosses above %D while %K < 50) AND SNR passes (if enabled) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bearish (if enabled) OR bullish regular divergence (price makes a lower low while %K makes a higher low at confirmed pivots) OR bullish hidden divergence (price makes a higher low while %K makes a lower low at confirmed pivots) OR %K crosses above %D OR adaptive oscillator moving average crosses above trail.
Short: Range regime AND NOT embedded uptrend AND previous %K at or above previous overbought level AND %K crosses below %D AND short cycle phase (if enabled) AND SNR passes (if enabled) OR Trend regime AND bearish price regime AND (adaptive oscillator moving average crosses below trail OR bearish trail AND %K crosses below %D while %K > 50) AND SNR passes (if enabled) AND higher-timeframe bias is not bullish (if enabled) OR bearish regular divergence (price makes a higher high while %K makes a lower high at confirmed pivots) OR bearish hidden divergence (price makes a lower high while %K makes a higher high at confirmed pivots) OR %K crosses below %D OR adaptive oscillator moving average crosses below trail.
Long: Long fade or ride condition AND conviction is at least the minimum threshold AND no active veto: volatility-index spike for fades; insufficient aligned configured timeframes, opposing higher-timeframe bias, opposing cross-asset momentum, or failed SNR for rides (each when enabled).
Short: Short fade or ride condition AND conviction is at least the minimum threshold AND no active veto: volatility-index spike for fades; insufficient aligned configured timeframes, opposing higher-timeframe bias, opposing cross-asset momentum, or failed SNR for rides (each when enabled).
