Calculation: EMA trend alignment = green when the close is above the chart-timeframe EMA and the close was above that EMA at every configured lookback; red when the close is below the EMA and was below it at every configured lookback
Calculation: Multi-timeframe trend count = number of the chart timeframe and configured other timeframes whose close is above their EMA; use the last completed bar for higher timeframes and the last available intrabar for lower timeframes
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback)
Calculation: Initial stop = the lower of the current and previous low minus prior-bar ATR × stop multiplier for a long; the higher of the current and previous high plus prior-bar ATR × stop multiplier for a short
Calculation: Take-profit price = entry price plus the initial stop distance × reward multiple for a long, or minus that distance for a short
Calculation: Breakeven trigger = entry price plus the initial stop distance × trigger multiple for a long, or minus that distance for a short
Long: MACD line crosses above its signal line AND MACD line < 0 AND EMA trend alignment is green AND close > chart-timeframe EMA AND multi-timeframe trend count ≥ alignment threshold AND Choppiness Index is within its configured lower and upper bounds AND flat AND within configured trading hours AND outside the configured entry-skip hour and calendar blackout periods AND cooldown after the previous exit has elapsed AND no stop loss has occurred that day AND initial stop distance ≥ minimum stop distance
Short: MACD line crosses below its signal line AND MACD line > 0 AND EMA trend alignment is red AND close < chart-timeframe EMA AND number of timeframes below their EMA ≥ alignment threshold AND Choppiness Index is within its configured lower and upper bounds AND flat AND within configured trading hours AND outside the configured entry-skip hour and calendar blackout periods AND cooldown after the previous exit has elapsed AND no stop loss has occurred that day AND initial stop distance ≥ minimum stop distance
Exit: Long, low ≤ initial stop + spread offset OR high ≥ take-profit price OR (breakeven is enabled AND high ≥ breakeven trigger AND low ≤ entry price + breakeven stop offset) OR configured end-of-day close condition
Exit: Short, high ≥ initial stop − spread offset OR low ≤ take-profit price OR (breakeven is enabled AND low ≤ breakeven trigger AND high ≥ entry price − breakeven stop offset) OR configured end-of-day close condition
