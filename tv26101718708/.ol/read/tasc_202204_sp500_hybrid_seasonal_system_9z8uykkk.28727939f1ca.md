Calculation: VIX rise (%) = (VIX close / lowest VIX close over the volatility lookback, excluding the current bar − 1) × 100; use the VIX bar mapped to each chart bar, using the last completed bar when the VIX timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: ATR rise (%) = (ATR / lowest ATR over the same volatility lookback, excluding the current bar − 1) × 100; ATR uses a shorter lookback than the volatility lookback, on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: VFI = EMA of the sum of signed volume-flow contributions over a long lookback divided by average volume over that lookback (average volume shifted back one bar), with short EMA smoothing. For each bar, flow is current HLC3 minus prior HLC3; volatility is the standard deviation of the change in log HLC3 over a shorter lookback; cutoff is a coefficient times close times volatility; volume is capped at average volume times a coefficient; contribution is capped volume if flow exceeds cutoff, negative capped volume if flow is below negative cutoff, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: VFI moving average = simple moving average of VFI over a shorter lookback.
Long: (calendar month is October or later OR before the configured sell month) AND (prior bar VFI > its critical threshold) AND (prior bar VIX rise (%) < its maximum threshold OR prior bar ATR rise (%) < a coefficient times that maximum threshold)
Short: calendar month = configured sell month OR prior bar VIX rise (%) > twice its maximum threshold OR (VFI crossed its critical threshold in either direction on the prior bar AND current VFI moving average < prior VFI moving average)
Exit: long, calendar month = configured sell month OR prior bar VIX rise (%) > twice its maximum threshold OR (VFI crossed its critical threshold in either direction on the prior bar AND current VFI moving average < prior VFI moving average)
