Calculation: Tenkan-sen = midpoint of the rolling high and low over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Kijun-sen = midpoint of the rolling high and low over a longer lookback than Tenkan-sen
Calculation: Leading Span A = average of Tenkan-sen and Kijun-sen, displaced forward by the Ichimoku displacement
Calculation: Leading Span B = midpoint of the rolling high and low over a lookback longer than Kijun-sen, displaced forward by the Ichimoku displacement
Calculation: Current cloud top and bottom = higher and lower of Leading Span A and B at the current chart position
Calculation: Candle direction = bullish if close > open and bearish if close < open, except a doji (absolute close-open <= candle range times the doji body ratio), which is neutral
Long: (Tenkan-sen > Kijun-sen OR (Tenkan-sen = Kijun-sen AND Kijun-sen > its prior-bar value)) AND close > close at the Ichimoku displacement bars ago AND close > current cloud top AND Kijun-sen >= its prior-bar value OR current candle-direction streak is bullish and has length >= 5, counting neutral dojis and ending at the most recent bearish candle OR the current 6-bar window has bullish first and last candles and exactly 1 interior candle is not bullish OR the current 8-bar window has bullish first and last candles and exactly 1 interior candle is not bullish OR the current 11-bar window has bullish first and last candles and exactly 2 interior candles are not bullish
Short: (Tenkan-sen < Kijun-sen OR (Tenkan-sen = Kijun-sen AND Kijun-sen < its prior-bar value)) AND close < close at the Ichimoku displacement bars ago AND close < current cloud bottom AND Kijun-sen <= its prior-bar value OR current candle-direction streak is bearish and has length >= 5, counting neutral dojis and ending at the most recent bullish candle OR the current 6-bar window has bearish first and last candles and exactly 1 interior candle is not bearish OR the current 8-bar window has bearish first and last candles and exactly 1 interior candle is not bearish OR the current 11-bar window has bearish first and last candles and exactly 2 interior candles are not bearish
