Calculation: Volume oscillator = either candle volume delta, where buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and sell volume = volume × (high − close) / (high − low), with both zero when high = low, and delta = buy volume − sell volume; or OBV, the cumulative sum of sign(close − previous close) × volume.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across the configured left and right bars; use it only after all right-side bars have completed. Compare each pivot with the preceding pivot of the same type.
Calculation: Minimum-move filter = price pivots pass if the minimum price change is disabled or their absolute percentage change from the preceding pivot is at least that threshold; volume oscillator pivots pass if the minimum volume change is disabled, or their absolute percentage change from the preceding pivot is at least that threshold (when the previous value is zero, pass only if the values differ).
Long: A current price pivot low is within the maximum pivot lookback of the preceding price pivot low AND the minimum-move filter passes AND ((current price low < preceding price low AND volume oscillator at current price pivot > volume oscillator at preceding price pivot) OR (current price low > preceding price low AND volume oscillator at current price pivot < volume oscillator at preceding price pivot)) OR a current volume oscillator pivot low is within the maximum pivot lookback of the preceding volume oscillator pivot low AND the minimum-move filter passes AND ((price low at current volume oscillator pivot < price low at preceding volume oscillator pivot AND current volume oscillator pivot low > preceding volume oscillator pivot low) OR (price low at current volume oscillator pivot > price low at preceding volume oscillator pivot AND current volume oscillator pivot low < preceding volume oscillator pivot low)).
Short: A current price pivot high is within the maximum pivot lookback of the preceding price pivot high AND the minimum-move filter passes AND ((current price high > preceding price high AND volume oscillator at current price pivot < volume oscillator at preceding price pivot) OR (current price high < preceding price high AND volume oscillator at current price pivot > volume oscillator at preceding price pivot)) OR a current volume oscillator pivot high is within the maximum pivot lookback of the preceding volume oscillator pivot high AND the minimum-move filter passes AND ((price high at current volume oscillator pivot > price high at preceding volume oscillator pivot AND current volume oscillator pivot high < preceding volume oscillator pivot high) OR (price high at current volume oscillator pivot < price high at preceding volume oscillator pivot AND current volume oscillator pivot high > preceding volume oscillator pivot high)).
