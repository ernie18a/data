Calculation: Fast, mid, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks; mid-EMA slope = (current mid EMA − mid EMA from a longer lookback) / ATR
Calculation: Relative volume (RVOL) = volume / longer-lookback average volume
Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA AND close > mid EMA AND mid-EMA slope ≥ minimum positive slope AND ADX ≥ minimum strength AND positive DI ≥ negative DI; bear trend = inverse EMA order and slope, close < mid EMA, ADX ≥ minimum strength, and negative DI ≥ positive DI
Calculation: Bull watch trend = NOT bull trend AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND mid-EMA slope > −minimum slope AND positive DI ≥ 0.85 × negative DI; bear watch trend = inverse EMA and close conditions AND mid-EMA slope < minimum slope AND negative DI ≥ 0.85 × positive DI
Calculation: Prior impulse high/low = highest high/lowest low over the impulse lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Pullback start = no active pocket AND matching bull/bear trend or watch trend AND price touches the fast- or mid-EMA lane within an ATR-based tolerance AND close remains on the trend side of the slow EMA AND RVOL ≤ start limit AND (bull: close ≤ max(open, prior close); bear: close ≥ min(open, prior close))
Calculation: Active pocket = starts with current high/low as its top/bottom and prior impulse high/low as anchors; while active, top/bottom expand to the highest high/lowest low and pocket age and RVOL sum accumulate each bar
Calculation: Pullback depth = bull: (anchor high − pocket bottom) / (anchor high − anchor low) × 100; bear: (pocket top − anchor low) / (anchor high − anchor low) × 100
Calculation: Average pullback RVOL = accumulated RVOL / pocket age; dry-up score = 100 when average RVOL ≤ preferred dry-up RVOL, 0 when ≥ hard RVOL, otherwise linear between them
Calculation: Depth score = 100 when depth ≤ preferred depth, 0 when ≥ hard depth, otherwise linear between them; duration score = 55 × pocket age / minimum age before minimum age, 100 through maximum age, then 0
Calculation: Trigger level = bull: pocket top + ATR buffer; bear: pocket bottom − ATR buffer; invalidation level = bull: pocket bottom − ATR buffer; bear: pocket top + ATR buffer
Calculation: Trigger pressure score = clamp(100 − 45 × ATR-normalized distance from close to trigger level, 0, 100)
Calculation: Quality score = 20% trend score + 28% dry-up score + 24% depth score + 12% duration score + 16% trigger pressure score; trend score combines normalized absolute mid-EMA slope (42%), normalized ADX excess (33%), and a trend confirmation bonus (25 for confirmed trend, otherwise 12)
Long: bull pocket is active AND (once per pocket, pocket age ≥ minimum age AND dry-up score ≥ minimum dry-up score AND depth ≤ hard depth AND quality score ≥ minimum watch quality AND trigger and invalidation conditions are false; OR pocket age ≥ minimum age AND dry-up score ≥ minimum dry-up score AND depth ≤ trigger depth AND quality score ≥ minimum trigger quality AND current RVOL ≥ average pullback RVOL × recovery ratio AND close > trigger level AND close > open)
Short: bear pocket is active AND (once per pocket, pocket age ≥ minimum age AND dry-up score ≥ minimum dry-up score AND depth ≤ hard depth AND quality score ≥ minimum watch quality AND trigger and invalidation conditions are false; OR pocket age ≥ minimum age AND dry-up score ≥ minimum dry-up score AND depth ≤ trigger depth AND quality score ≥ minimum trigger quality AND current RVOL ≥ average pullback RVOL × recovery ratio AND close < trigger level AND close < open)
