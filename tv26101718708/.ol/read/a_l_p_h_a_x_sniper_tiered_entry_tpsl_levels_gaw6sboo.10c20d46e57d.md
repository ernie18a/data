Calculation: Trend score = +1 if fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, otherwise −1 if fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA, otherwise 0; plus +1 if fast EMA rises on each of the last two bars, otherwise −1 if it falls on each; plus +1 if medium EMA rises from the prior bar, otherwise −1 if it falls; plus +1 if close > anchor EMA, otherwise −1 if close < anchor EMA. All EMAs use the chart timeframe; fast, medium, slow, and anchor lookbacks increase in that order.
Calculation: Bull score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: trend points (30 if trend score ≥3, 20 if ≥2, 10 if ≥1, else 0); RSI points (20 on recovery above the bull threshold, else 12 if RSI is at or below that threshold and >20, else 0); stochastic points (15 if %K crosses above %D while below the overbought threshold and %K has just risen from below the oversold threshold; else 12 for that crossover; else 8 if %K has just risen above the oversold threshold; else 0); MACD points (10 if MACD filtering is off, else 10 if MACD is above its signal and its histogram rises, else 5 if MACD is above its signal, else 0); volume points (10 if volume >1.5× its 20-bar SMA, else 7 if >1×, else 4 if the volume filter passes, else 0); candle points (5 for an up candle with body/range ≥ the minimum, 2 for any other up candle, else 0); +5 if the longer-lookback EMA is above the shorter-lookback EMA; session points (5 in London/NY overlap, 3 in London or NY alone, else 0); minus 15 if RSI >75, else 8 if RSI >68; minus 10 if the enabled ATR filter fails; minus 8 in Asia; minus 20 if close is more than 3% from the anchor EMA, else 10 if more than 1.5% away; minus 18 if either directional fast/medium/slow EMA alignment has lasted more than 150 bars, else 10 if more than 80 bars.
Calculation: Bear score = mirror of bull score: trend points are 30 if trend score ≤−3, 20 if ≤−2, 10 if ≤−1; RSI recovery is below the bear threshold, its pullback zone is RSI ≥ that threshold and <80, and penalties are 15 if RSI <25, else 8 if RSI <32. Use bearish stochastic crosses/exits from overbought, bearish MACD alignment, down candles, the longer-lookback EMA below the shorter-lookback EMA, and the same volume, session, ATR, extension, and trend-age points and penalties.
Calculation: RSI recovery up/down = RSI crosses above/below its respective pullback threshold. Stochastic %K = stochastic of close/high/low; %D = SMA of %K. A stochastic up/down cross is %K crossing above/below %D while below the overbought/above the oversold threshold; an exit from an extreme is %K rising above oversold/falling below overbought from beyond that threshold on the prior bar.
Calculation: HTF confirmation proxy = EMA(close, 5 × fast EMA lookback) >/< EMA(close, 5 × slow EMA lookback). Both are calculated on the chart timeframe, not aggregated higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trailing stop = close −/+ ATR × trail multiplier, ratcheted upward/downward respectively by taking the maximum/minimum of this value and the prior active trail.
Long: (RSI recovery up OR stochastic up cross OR stochastic exit from oversold) AND close > open AND trend score ≥1 AND volume filter passes AND ATR filter passes AND candle-strength filter passes AND session filter passes AND (MACD filter off OR MACD > signal) AND bull score ≥30 AND bars since prior long signal ≥ cooldown.
Short: (RSI recovery down OR stochastic down cross OR stochastic exit from overbought) AND close < open AND trend score ≤−1 AND volume filter passes AND ATR filter passes AND candle-strength filter passes AND session filter passes AND (MACD filter off OR MACD < signal) AND bear score ≥30 AND bars since prior short signal ≥ cooldown.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop-loss multiple OR low < active bullish trailing stop.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop-loss multiple OR high > active bearish trailing stop.
