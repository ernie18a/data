Calculation: Support and resistance zones = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot lows and highs, respectively, plus confirmed pivots from a higher timeframe using its last completed bar; merge a pivot into a same-side zone when its price is within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the zone midpoint, updating the midpoint by touch-count-weighted average and expanding the zone to include the pivot with an ATR-scaled half-height; otherwise create a zone. Prune zones not touched within the configured age and keep no more than the configured maximum per side.
Calculation: Zone strength = touch count + higher-timeframe contribution bonus + recency bonus.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true ranges over the lookback / (highest high − lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Confluence score = one point if zone strength meets its minimum; one point if higher-timeframe trend bias is disabled or aligned; one point if volume meets its expansion threshold; one point if the choppiness gate passes; one bonus point for SWEEP or RETEST; one point if current bar range meets its ATR threshold; and one point if the session filter is disabled or the bar is in session.
Long: A qualified support zone has strength at least the minimum; current low is within the ATR-scaled proximity of its midpoint or inside its zone; choppiness and session gates pass; higher-timeframe bias is disabled or bullish (higher-timeframe close above its fast EMA and fast EMA above its slower EMA, using the higher-timeframe bar available at the chart bar close); confluence score meets its minimum; the chart bar is confirmed; and the signal cooldown has elapsed; AND at least one of: DEFEND (lower wick / bar range meets its minimum, close > lower of open and close + 25% of bar range, and close > zone bottom); SWEEP (low < zone bottom − ATR-scaled sweep depth, close > zone midpoint, and close > open); RETEST (within the lookback, a prior close < zone bottom, and current low ≤ zone top, close > zone midpoint, and close > open); COIL (each of the configured consecutive bars has true range < 0.75 × ATR, close is within ATR-scaled proximity of zone midpoint, and close > zone midpoint).
Short: A qualified resistance zone has strength at least the minimum; current high is within the ATR-scaled proximity of its midpoint or inside its zone; choppiness and session gates pass; higher-timeframe bias is disabled or bearish (higher-timeframe close below its fast EMA and fast EMA below its slower EMA, using the higher-timeframe bar available at the chart bar close); confluence score meets its minimum; the chart bar is confirmed; and the signal cooldown has elapsed; AND at least one of: DEFEND (upper wick / bar range meets its minimum, close < higher of open and close − 25% of bar range, and close < zone top); SWEEP (high > zone top + ATR-scaled sweep depth, close < zone midpoint, and close < open); RETEST (within the lookback, a prior close > zone top, and current high ≥ zone bottom, close < zone midpoint, and close < open); COIL (each of the configured consecutive bars has true range < 0.75 × ATR, close is within ATR-scaled proximity of zone midpoint, and close < zone midpoint).
Exit: Long, close ≥ the nearest resistance-zone midpoint above entry price, or a short signal fires.
Exit: Short, close ≤ the nearest support-zone midpoint below entry price, or a long signal fires.
