Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages with the fast EMA using a shorter lookback than the slow EMA; the trend EMA uses a longer lookback.
Calculation: Hull MA = weighted moving average of (2 × weighted moving average of close over half the lookback − weighted moving average of close over the full lookback), over the square root of the lookback.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe buy volume = sum of intrabar volume where intrabar close > open, plus half the volume where close = open; sell volume uses close < open plus the other half of flat intrabars. Use lower-timeframe intrabars within the chart bar; at chart-bar close they are completed.
Calculation: Volume ratio = total intrabar volume divided by its rolling median or average over a lookback.
Calculation: Buy bubble = volume ratio ≥ threshold AND buy volume / total volume ≥ dominance threshold; sell bubble = volume ratio ≥ threshold AND sell volume / total volume ≥ dominance threshold. Keep only the strongest qualifying bubble per side in the lookback window.
Calculation: Candle delta = lower-timeframe buy volume − sell volume; three-bar delta = sum of candle delta over the latest three chart bars.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative candle delta, reset at the start of each day.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low in the same CVD period AND CVD at the newer pivot is higher; bearish divergence is a confirmed price pivot high higher than the previous confirmed pivot high in the same period AND CVD at the newer pivot is lower. Pivots require the configured number of bars on both sides and are recognized after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Market structure = confirmed swing highs and lows using the configured number of bars on both sides; a break occurs when price crosses the latest unused swing level, using either close or wick as configured. A break against the prior structure trend is a change of character; otherwise it is a break of structure. Track whether the latest swing low is higher than its predecessor and whether the latest swing high is lower than its predecessor.
Calculation: Equal-high and equal-low zones = two or more confirmed swing highs or lows within a tolerance based on ATR; a zone is swept when price moves beyond its outer edge. A zone touch occurs when the chart bar overlaps the zone, a latest broken structure level, or the trend EMA, within an ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bias = sum of bullish (+1) or bearish (−1) EMA-cloud trends across the configured timeframes above and below the chart timeframe. Use the current requested-timeframe bar (last completed historically and forming in real time).
Calculation: A long score = count of: bias ≥ threshold; close above trend EMA; bullish structure; a zone touch within lookback; a buy bubble within lookback; a bullish candle during a downtrend within lookback; positive three-bar delta or bullish CVD divergence within lookback; rising Hull MA.
Calculation: A short score = count of the mirrored conditions: bias ≤ −threshold; close below trend EMA; bearish structure; a zone touch within lookback; a sell bubble within lookback; a bearish candle during an uptrend within lookback; negative three-bar delta or bearish CVD divergence within lookback; falling Hull MA.
Calculation: B long score = count of: buy bubble within lookback; bullish CVD divergence within lookback; bullish change of character within lookback; rising Hull MA; positive three-bar delta. B short score counts the mirrored sell-side conditions.
Calculation: Sideways range = prior lookback high minus low ≤ ATR × threshold OR cloud flips during the lookback ≥ threshold. Range breakout = the prior bar was sideways AND current close is above the prior lookback high (long) or below the prior lookback low (short).
Calculation: C long score = count of: bias ≥ threshold; bullish structure; zone touch within lookback; buy bubble within lookback; positive three-bar delta or bullish CVD divergence within lookback. C short score counts the mirrored sell-side conditions.
Calculation: S long score = count of: positive bias; buy bubble within lookback; positive three-bar delta or bullish CVD divergence within lookback; rising Hull MA; break-bar volume ratio ≥ threshold with buy share ≥ threshold. S short score counts the mirrored sell-side conditions.

Short: range breakout down AND bias < 0 AND close < open AND (sell bubble OR sell share ≥ threshold with volume ratio ≥ threshold) AND no competing A, B, C, or S short signal.
