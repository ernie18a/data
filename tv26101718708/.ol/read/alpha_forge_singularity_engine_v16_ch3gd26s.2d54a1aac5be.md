Calculation: Energy score = current raw energy scaled to 0–100 within its rolling normalization range; raw energy is clipped relative volume × (0.60 × absolute body percent of close + 0.40 × candle range percent of close) × clipped candle-range-to-ATR ratio × (1 + 0.35 × twice the absolute distance of close’s candle-range location from 0.5)
Calculation: Directional force = smoothed signed force scaled to −100–100 by the rolling maximum absolute smoothed force; signed force is relative volume × (0.65 × signed close-to-open percent + 0.20 × signed candle-range percent + 0.15 × close-location bias × ATR percent)
Calculation: Singularity = raw energy > rolling mean(raw energy) + rolling standard deviation(raw energy) × sensitivity AND energy score ≥ minimum singularity score
Calculation: Bullish force = directional force ≥ directional confirmation threshold
Calculation: Bearish force = directional force ≤ −directional confirmation threshold
Calculation: Bullish force burst = directional force ≥ force-burst threshold AND energy score < minimum singularity score
Calculation: Bearish force burst = directional force ≤ −force-burst threshold AND energy score < minimum singularity score
Calculation: Charging = NOT singularity AND ATR percent < its rolling mean × compression factor AND (relative volume > 0.95 OR relative volume has risen for 3 bars) AND (absolute directional force > directional confirmation threshold OR absolute directional force has risen for 3 bars) AND energy score is between the charging minimum and maximum, inclusive
Calculation: Bullish recovery = most recent singularity within the impulse-memory lookback was bearish AND directional force ≥ force-burst threshold
Calculation: Bearish recovery = most recent singularity within the impulse-memory lookback was bullish AND directional force ≤ −force-burst threshold
Calculation: Take-profit and stop-loss levels = levels set from entry price using the selected percent-based or ATR-based distances, on the favorable and adverse sides respectively
Long: Singularity AND bullish force OR bullish force burst OR bullish recovery OR (charging AND directional force > 0), subject to the selected overlay mode (Conservative: singularity only; Standard: singularity, force burst, or recovery; Aggressive: any of these including charging), minimum-grade filter, enabled EMA trend filter (shorter-lookback EMA ≥ longer-lookback EMA), enabled higher-timeframe bias filter (shorter-lookback EMA ≥ longer-lookback EMA on a higher timeframe, using the last completed bar), enabled session filter, and signal cooldown; OR a short-trap marking condition (price sweeps below the lowest low of prior bars and closes back above it with a lower rejection wick, or, in confirmed mode, this occurred on the prior bar and current close > prior high)
Short: Singularity AND bearish force OR bearish force burst OR bearish recovery OR (charging AND directional force < 0), subject to the selected overlay mode (Conservative: singularity only; Standard: singularity, force burst, or recovery; Aggressive: any of these including charging), minimum-grade filter, enabled EMA trend filter (shorter-lookback EMA ≤ longer-lookback EMA), enabled higher-timeframe bias filter (shorter-lookback EMA ≤ longer-lookback EMA on a higher timeframe, using the last completed bar), enabled session filter, and signal cooldown; OR a long-trap marking condition (price sweeps above the highest high of prior bars and closes back below it with an upper rejection wick, or, in confirmed mode, this occurred on the prior bar and current close < prior low)
Exit: long, high ≥ take-profit level OR low ≤ stop-loss level
Exit: short, low ≤ take-profit level OR high ≥ stop-loss level
