Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the corresponding highs/lows of the pivot-length bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish 3-C target = P3 + (P2 − P1); bearish 3-C target = P3 − (P1 − P2).
Calculation: P1–P2 midpoint = (P1 + P2) / 2.
Long: On higher-timeframe bars (lookahead off: the forming bar in realtime, the last completed bar historically), a confirmed pivot low initializes bullish P1; while in phase 1, a lower confirmed pivot low replaces P1; while in phase 1, a confirmed pivot high above P1 confirms bullish P2; while in phase 2, a higher confirmed pivot high replaces P2; while in phase 2, a confirmed pivot low above P1 confirms bullish P3; OR the bullish 3-C target differs from the bearish P1–P2 midpoint by less than the intersection tolerance as a percentage of that midpoint; OR a new bullish P1 differs from the prior bullish 3-C target by less than twice the intersection tolerance as a percentage of that target.
Short: On higher-timeframe bars (lookahead off: the forming bar in realtime, the last completed bar historically), a confirmed pivot high initializes bearish P1; while in phase 1, a higher confirmed pivot high replaces P1; while in phase 1, a confirmed pivot low below P1 confirms bearish P2; while in phase 2, a lower confirmed pivot low replaces P2; while in phase 2, a confirmed pivot high below P1 confirms bearish P3; OR the bearish 3-C target differs from the bullish P1–P2 midpoint by less than the intersection tolerance as a percentage of that midpoint; OR a new bearish P1 differs from the prior bearish 3-C target by less than twice the intersection tolerance as a percentage of that target.
Exit: Long, during bullish phase 3, close < P1 OR high ≥ bullish 3-C target.
Exit: Short, during bearish phase 3, close > P1 OR low ≤ bearish 3-C target.
