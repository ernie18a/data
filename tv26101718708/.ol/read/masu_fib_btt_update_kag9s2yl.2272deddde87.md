Calculation: Bullish Fibonacci bounce = the latest qualifying swing high is later than the latest qualifying swing low; compute retracements at 38.2%, 50%, and 61.8% from that swing. At least one enabled level is within zone-width × ATR of the bar low, both swing anchors are at least two bars old, and either the lower wick is at least the configured fraction of the bar range or the bar closes bullish above the nearest touched level.
Calculation: Bearish Fibonacci rejection = the latest qualifying swing low is later than the latest qualifying swing high; use the same retracement levels and anchor-age rule. At least one enabled level is within zone-width × ATR of the bar high, and either the upper wick is at least the configured fraction of the bar range or the bar closes bearish below the nearest touched level.
Calculation: Qualifying swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the swing lookback plus the current bar, and whose distance from the opposite extreme in that window is at least the configured minimum ATR multiple. Retain the latest qualifying high and low and their bar indices.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume on bullish bars minus volume on bearish bars; volume on doji bars contributes zero.
Calculation: Buy/sell volume ratio = volume on bullish bars divided by the greater of volume on bearish bars and 1.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = (cumulative volume delta above its moving average and rising while close falls, or buy/sell volume ratio above its configured threshold) and (close crosses above VWAP or closes above VWAP).
Calculation: Bearish order flow = (cumulative volume delta below its moving average and falling while close rises, or buy/sell volume ratio below its configured threshold) and (close crosses below VWAP or closes below VWAP).
Calculation: Bullish ribbon alignment = at least the configured threshold minus one of the seven adjacent comparisons in an eight-average ribbon are bullish; each shorter-lookback average is above the next longer-lookback average. Use the configured average type.
Calculation: Bearish ribbon alignment = at least the configured threshold minus one of the seven adjacent comparisons in an eight-average ribbon are bearish; each shorter-lookback average is below the next longer-lookback average. Use the configured average type.
Long: (ADX above threshold AND short-lookback EMA crosses above longer-lookback EMA AND short-lookback EMA above longer-lookback EMA AND close above long-lookback EMA AND volume above its moving average × volume multiplier) OR (close above the previous bar's Donchian upper boundary AND volume above its moving average × volume multiplier AND close above long-lookback EMA) OR (ADX at or below threshold AND close below the lower Bollinger Band AND RSI below oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above oversold threshold); AND (higher-timeframe fast EMA above slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close above fast EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) when the MTF filter is enabled; AND bullish ribbon alignment when the ribbon filter is enabled; AND (order-flow filter disabled OR bullish order flow OR close above VWAP); AND (Fibonacci filter disabled OR bullish Fibonacci bounce OR latest qualifying swing high is later than latest qualifying swing low); AND within the configured trading session when the time filter is enabled; AND the chart bar is confirmed and there is no open position. Also long when a bullish Fibonacci bounce occurs on a confirmed chart bar.
Short: (ADX above threshold AND short-lookback EMA crosses below longer-lookback EMA AND short-lookback EMA below longer-lookback EMA AND close below long-lookback EMA AND volume above its moving average × volume multiplier) OR (close below the previous bar's Donchian lower boundary AND volume above its moving average × volume multiplier AND close below long-lookback EMA) OR (ADX at or below threshold AND close above the upper Bollinger Band AND RSI above overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below overbought threshold); AND (higher-timeframe fast EMA below slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close below fast EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) when the MTF filter is enabled; AND bearish ribbon alignment when the ribbon filter is enabled; AND (order-flow filter disabled OR bearish order flow OR close below VWAP); AND (Fibonacci filter disabled OR bearish Fibonacci rejection OR latest qualifying swing high is not later than latest qualifying swing low); AND within the configured trading session when the time filter is enabled; AND the chart bar is confirmed and there is no open position. Also short when a bearish Fibonacci rejection occurs on a confirmed chart bar.
Exit: long, low at or below signal-bar close minus ATR × stop multiple; OR, when trailing is enabled, price reaches the trailing stop after rising by ATR × trail multiple from entry; OR, when trailing is disabled, high at or above signal-bar close plus ATR × take-profit multiple.
Exit: short, high at or above signal-bar close plus ATR × stop multiple; OR, when trailing is enabled, price reaches the trailing stop after falling by ATR × trail multiple from entry; OR, when trailing is disabled, low at or below signal-bar close minus ATR × take-profit multiple.
