Calculation: Pair flow score = 100 × EMA of the weighted average of: ATR-normalized momentum ((close − close from the momentum lookback) / ATR, scaled and clamped to [−1,1]); signed relative volume ((volume / average volume − 1) × sign of momentum, scaled and clamped to [−1,1]); EMA trend ((fast EMA − slow EMA) / ATR, scaled and clamped to [−1,1]); volatility expansion ((ATR / close) / its moving average − 1), clamped to [−1,1] and multiplied by momentum sign; and directional breadth (+1 when close > fast EMA, otherwise −1). Fast trend lookback is shorter than slow trend lookback. Calculation bars use the configured calculation timeframe (which may equal the chart timeframe); use its last completed bar in confirmed mode or its still-forming bar in live mode.
Calculation: Currency flow score = the average of connected pair flow scores, adding a pair score for its base currency and subtracting it for its quote currency; clamp to [−100,100].
Long: pair flow score ≥ 10 OR pair connects the strongest-flow and weakest-flow currencies and the strongest-flow currency is the pair's base currency
Short: pair flow score ≤ −10 OR pair connects the strongest-flow and weakest-flow currencies and the strongest-flow currency is the pair's quote currency
