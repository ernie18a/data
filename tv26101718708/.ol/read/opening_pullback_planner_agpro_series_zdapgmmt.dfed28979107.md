Calculation: Opening drive = high, low, open, close, and average volume of the configured opening-reference bars, locked when that reference ends.
Calculation: Drive side = bullish when opening-drive close is at or above its high-low midpoint; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Drive quality = weighted score of opening-drive range divided by ATR, close location in the drive in the drive direction, and average opening-drive volume divided by the recent volume average; higher values indicate stronger drive quality.
Calculation: Pullback zone = retracement band between the configured shallow and deep fractions of the opening-drive range, measured back from its high for a bullish drive or its low for a bearish drive.
Calculation: Continuation trigger = opening-drive extreme plus an ATR buffer in the drive direction.
Calculation: Failure line = far edge of the pullback zone plus an ATR buffer beyond that edge, opposite the drive direction.
Calculation: Pullback score = weighted score of drive quality, pullback depth (best near the configured ideal depth), close recovery within the zone, current volume relative to its recent average, ATR relative to its longer-term average (best near its configured ideal), and target room relative to risk.
Long: Drive side is bullish AND drive quality meets the configured minimum AND (drive quality meets the configured retain-label threshold OR (pullback zone has been touched AND close is at or above its midpoint AND configured volume and room confirmations pass AND pullback score meets its configured minimum) OR (pullback zone has been touched AND close is above the continuation trigger AND pullback score meets the configured alert threshold)).
Short: Drive side is bearish AND drive quality meets the configured minimum AND (drive quality meets the configured retain-label threshold OR (pullback zone has been touched AND close is at or below its midpoint AND configured volume and room confirmations pass AND pullback score meets its configured minimum) OR (pullback zone has been touched AND close is below the continuation trigger AND pullback score meets the configured alert threshold)).
Exit: long, low is below the failure line when realtime confirmation is selected, otherwise close is below the failure line.
Exit: short, high is above the failure line when realtime confirmation is selected, otherwise close is above the failure line.
