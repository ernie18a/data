Calculation: Prior channel high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding lookback bars, excluding the current bar; optionally apply the selected moving average to each.
Calculation: Extreme age = bars since the raw channel extreme was set.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar low (or the lower low of the signal and preceding bars for the next-bar setup) − ATR × buffer; short stop = signal-bar high (or the higher high of those bars for the next-bar setup) + ATR × buffer.
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry price and stop; long target = entry price + risk × target multiple; short target = entry price − risk × target multiple.
Long: (low < prior channel low AND close > prior channel low AND prior channel low age ≥ minimum age) OR (previous bar low < its prior channel low AND previous bar close < its prior channel low AND previous channel low age ≥ minimum age AND close > previous bar's prior channel low), AND (higher-timeframe last completed close > its EMA when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled) AND (previous chart bar ADX ≥ minimum when the ADX filter is enabled) AND short condition is false.
Short: (high > prior channel high AND close < prior channel high AND prior channel high age ≥ minimum age) OR (previous bar high > its prior channel high AND previous bar close > its prior channel high AND previous channel high age ≥ minimum age AND close < previous bar's prior channel high), AND (higher-timeframe last completed close < its EMA when the higher-timeframe filter is enabled) AND (previous chart bar ADX ≥ minimum when the ADX filter is enabled) AND long condition is false.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long third target OR short-entry condition.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short third target OR long-entry condition.
