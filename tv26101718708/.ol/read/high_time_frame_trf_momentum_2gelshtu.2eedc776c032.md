Calculation: Twin Range Filter (TRF) = For each of two lookbacks, smooth absolute close-to-close changes with EMA(p), then smooth that result with EMA(2p−1) and multiply by its range multiplier; average the two ranges. The fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback. Recursively filter close by holding the prior filter when close is within the range, otherwise moving it to close minus the range when rising or close plus the range when falling. Set upper and lower bands to filter ± range, each held at its prior value unless the filter moves beyond it or the prior close crosses it. The TRF line switches between these bands according to whether close crosses the active band, and otherwise follows the active band.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close crosses above the chart-timeframe TRF AND the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its TRF AND New York time is 09:45 through 12:00 inclusive.
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, close crosses below the chart-timeframe TRF AND the last completed higher-timeframe close is at or below its TRF AND New York time is 09:45 through 12:00 inclusive.
