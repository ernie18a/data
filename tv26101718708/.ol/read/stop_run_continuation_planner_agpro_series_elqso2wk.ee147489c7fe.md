Calculation: Upper and lower stop-run references = highest high and lowest low over the prior lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / moving average of volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Continuation score = a 0–100 weighted score of normalized sweep depth, close acceptance and close location, candle body efficiency, volume participation, trend support, target room, and reversal risk; reversal risk increases with rejection, weak close acceptance, trend conflict, and low volume.
Long: Bar is confirmed AND an upper sweep (high exceeds the upper reference by the minimum ATR-scaled sweep depth) is selected (no lower sweep, or the bullish continuation score is at least the bearish score) AND ((continuation score is at least the watch threshold but below the ready threshold) OR (continuation score is at least the ready threshold AND close is above the upper reference by the ATR-scaled close buffer AND the configured confirmation mode is satisfied: close continuation, or additionally volume ratio meets its threshold, or additionally close location meets its threshold and trend is neutral or supportive).
Short: Bar is confirmed AND a lower sweep (low falls below the lower reference by the minimum ATR-scaled sweep depth) is selected (no upper sweep, or the bullish continuation score is below the bearish score) AND ((continuation score is at least the watch threshold but below the ready threshold) OR (continuation score is at least the ready threshold AND close is below the lower reference by the ATR-scaled close buffer AND the configured confirmation mode is satisfied: close continuation, or additionally volume ratio meets its threshold, or additionally close location meets its threshold and trend is neutral or supportive).
