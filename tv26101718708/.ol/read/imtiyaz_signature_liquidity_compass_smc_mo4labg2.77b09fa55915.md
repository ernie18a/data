Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low greater/less than the corresponding values in the preceding and following swing lookback bars; confirmed after the following bars complete.
Calculation: Active liquidity pool = confirmed swing high (above price) or low (below price), retained until close passes it by the ATR touch buffer or its age exceeds twice the freshness window; discard oldest pools above the per-side pool limit. Count a touch on each active bar whose high/low comes within the ATR touch buffer. A pool sweep is one-time: for a high pool, high > pool price AND close < pool price in close-back-inside mode, or high >= pool price in wick-touch mode; for a low pool, low < pool price AND close > pool price in close-back-inside mode, or low <= pool price in wick-touch mode.
Calculation: HTF trend = bullish when chart close > the EMA of close on a higher timeframe AND that EMA > its value 3 higher-timeframe bars earlier; bearish when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pool score = 25 × max(0, 1 − distance-in-ATR / maximum-scored-reach-in-ATR) + 20 × min(swing-bar volume / its longer-lookback volume average / full-volume-score ratio, 1) + 15 × max(0, 1 − pool age / freshness window) + 15 × min(touches, 3) / 3 + 10 if the pool remains on its original side of price + 15 if HTF trend aligns with that side, or 7.5 if HTF trend is neutral. Distance is absolute pool-price-to-close distance divided by ATR, floored at the minimum price increment. For each side, strongest pool is the active pool on that side with the highest score; missing score is zero.
Calculation: Net liquidity pull = strongest active above-price pool score − strongest active below-price pool score.
Long: A one-time sweep of an active below-price pool OR close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high (previous close <= that high) OR low > high two bars earlier OR net liquidity pull >= dominance threshold.
Short: A one-time sweep of an active above-price pool OR close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low (previous close >= that low) OR high < low two bars earlier OR net liquidity pull <= −dominance threshold.
