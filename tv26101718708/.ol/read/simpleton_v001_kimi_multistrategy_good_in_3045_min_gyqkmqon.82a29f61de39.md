Short: (RSI > overbought AND prior-bar RSI >= overbought AND (trend filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA)) OR (SuperTrend direction is bearish AND prior-bar direction was bullish) OR (MACD line crosses below signal line) OR (fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA AND (prior-bar fast EMA >= medium EMA OR two-bars-prior fast EMA >= medium EMA)) OR (close > upper Bollinger Band AND prior-bar close >= prior-bar upper Bollinger Band)

Exit: short, (low <= prior-bar close × (1 - take-profit %) OR high >= prior-bar close × (1 + stop-loss %))
