Long: A confirmed pivot low is swept (low < level and close >= level), then within the CSD lookback a later bar closes above the sweep bar's high; create a bullish zone from either (a) the bar immediately before the sweep, if its close > open, using that bar's high and low as zone bounds, or (b) a later bullish FVG, where low > high two bars earlier, using those values as bounds. On or after the CSD wait period, enter when a bar's low <= the zone top; if OTE-only mode is enabled, also require the zone to overlap a bullish FVG and close > the highest high from the CSD bar through the first zone-touch bar. If the HTF order-block filter is enabled, require the sweep low to be within the current bullish HTF order block.
Short: A confirmed pivot high is swept (high > level and close <= level), then within the CSD lookback a later bar closes below the sweep bar's low; create a bearish zone from either (a) the bar immediately before the sweep, if its close < open, using that bar's high and low as zone bounds, or (b) a later bearish FVG, where high < low two bars earlier, using those values as bounds. On or after the CSD wait period, enter when a bar's high >= the zone bottom; if OTE-only mode is enabled, also require the zone to overlap a bearish FVG and close < the lowest low from the CSD bar through the first zone-touch bar. If the HTF order-block filter is enabled, require the sweep high to be within the current bearish HTF order block.
