Calculation: Previous upper channel = previous bar’s highest high over the longer of the two configured lookbacks (the longer lookback is the larger of the two); Previous lower channel = previous bar’s lowest low over those lookbacks.
Calculation: Channel midpoint = (previous upper channel + previous lower channel) / 2.
Calculation: Bullish trend = selected high source is above a recursively range-filtered value and that filter has risen for at least one consecutive bar; bearish trend = selected high source is below the filter and the filter has fallen for at least one consecutive bar. The range filter uses an EMA of absolute close changes, smoothed by a second EMA with twice the slow lookback minus one, multiplied by the price lookback; it ratchets by that range and resets its rise/fall count when direction changes.
Calculation: Bullish volume = current up-bar volume is at least the average volume of up bars over the price lookback and cumulative up-bar volume exceeds cumulative down-bar volume, unless cumulative volume flow has been positive on both the current and prior bar and the current bar is a down bar whose volume is at least the average down-bar volume. Bearish volume is the converse: current down-bar volume is at least the average down-bar volume and cumulative down-bar volume exceeds cumulative up-bar volume, unless cumulative volume flow has been negative on both bars and the current bar is an up bar whose volume is at least the average up-bar volume. A bar is an up bar when close > open; otherwise it is a down bar.
Calculation: Momentum oscillator = 100 × (fast stochastic − slow stochastic), where fast stochastic = (short-period moving average of close − highest high over the short period) / (highest high − lowest low over the short period), and slow stochastic = (long-period moving average of close − lowest low over the long period) / (highest high − lowest low over the long period). Its signal line is a moving average over the configured trend lookback. The short period is shorter than the long period; use the selected moving-average type.
Calculation: Center price = channel midpoint. For the Moving Average mode, upper and lower trigger bands are center price plus/minus the selected percentage band. For the Fibonacci mode, upper and lower bands are center price plus/minus ATR over the price lookback multiplied by each of the two selected Fibonacci levels; the second level is the more extreme level.
Calculation: Price breakout = momentum fast line is above its slow line, below zero, and bullish trend holds. Price dumpout = momentum fast line is below its slow line, above zero, and bearish trend holds. Momentum fast line is 10 times the one-bar percentage change of a triple EMA of (2 × close + high + low) / 4; momentum slow line is 10 times an ALMA of the two-bar SMA of that percentage change. Fakeout = prior-bar (high − close) / close exceeds the configured threshold, or current high is at/above the extreme upper Moving Average band.
Long: (Average Price Channel mode AND ((low < previous lower channel AND bullish trend) OR price breakout)) OR (Moving Average mode AND low < selected lower band AND (bullish trend OR momentum oscillator crosses above its signal line)) OR (Fibonacci mode AND ((current and prior three lows are each at/below the extreme lower band) OR (low <= extreme lower band AND bearish volume) OR (low <= nearer lower band AND momentum oscillator crosses above its signal line))) OR (Peaks and Valleys mode AND (low < previous lower channel AND (momentum oscillator crosses above its signal line OR bearish volume))).
Short: (Average Price Channel mode AND ((high >= previous upper channel AND bearish trend) OR price dumpout OR fakeout)) OR (Moving Average mode AND high > selected upper band AND (bearish trend OR momentum oscillator crosses below its signal line)) OR (Fibonacci mode AND ((prior high >= nearer upper band AND current high >= extreme upper band) OR (prior high >= nearer upper band AND close < extreme upper band) OR (high >= nearer upper band AND bearish volume) OR high > extreme upper band)) OR (Peaks and Valleys mode AND (high >= previous upper channel AND (momentum oscillator crosses below its signal line OR bearish volume))).
Exit: Long, short condition OR low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price × (1 + target-profit fraction).
Exit: Short, long condition OR high >= entry price × (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price × (1 − target-profit fraction).
