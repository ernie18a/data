Calculation: Bullish FVG reference = on a confirmed chart bar, the high two bars earlier when current low > that high and the middle bar's close > that high; keep until a confirmed close falls below it, subject to a maximum stored-level cap that removes the oldest level.
Calculation: Bearish FVG reference = on a confirmed chart bar, the low two bars earlier when current high < that low and the middle bar's close < that low; keep until a confirmed close rises above it, subject to a maximum stored-level cap that removes the oldest level.
Calculation: Upper and lower channel boundaries = average active bullish and bearish FVG references separately (use the chart-timeframe close simple moving average when a side has no active references); apply two consecutive simple moving averages with the same lookback to each average; upper is the higher result and lower is the lower result.
Calculation: Super Bull wick extension = max((lower boundary − low) / abs(lower boundary), 0); Super Bear wick extension = max((high − upper boundary) / abs(upper boundary), 0).
Calculation: Volume threshold = average chart-timeframe volume over the selected lookback × multiplier.
Calculation: Target and stop = from signal-bar close: in percentage mode, long target and short stop use the applicable standard or Super percentage above close, and short target and long stop use the applicable percentage below close; in internal-level target mode, use the selected channel internal level as target only if it is beyond close in the trade direction. Stop uses the applicable standard or Super stop percentage.
Long: confirmed close crosses above lower channel boundary after at least the minimum number of consecutive confirmed closes below it AND bullish cooldown has elapsed AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume threshold)
Short: confirmed close crosses below upper channel boundary after at least the minimum number of consecutive confirmed closes above it AND bearish cooldown has elapsed AND (volume confirmation is disabled OR volume > volume threshold)
Exit: long, a later bar's high >= target OR low <= stop
Exit: short, a later bar's low <= target OR high >= stop
