Calculation: Combined premium = close of the two configured instruments added together; treat an unavailable close as zero. Calculate on each indicator timeframe’s current bar (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe); use the still-forming bar in real time and completed bars historically.
Calculation: Custom SuperTrend = apply an upper and lower band to combined premium, each offset by a configurable multiplier times the RMA of absolute bar-to-bar premium changes over a configurable lookback. Ratchet the upper band down to the lower of its raw and prior values and the lower band up to the higher of its raw and prior values. Start bearish; switch to bullish when premium exceeds the ratcheted upper band, resetting the lower band to its raw value. Switch to bearish when premium falls below the ratcheted lower band, resetting the upper band to its raw value. Otherwise retain the prior direction.
Long: On any indicator timeframe, combined premium > any of its five simple moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks OR RSI > 50 OR MACD histogram > 0 OR custom SuperTrend direction is bullish
Short: On any indicator timeframe, combined premium <= any of its five simple moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks OR RSI < 50 OR MACD histogram < 0 OR custom SuperTrend direction is bearish
