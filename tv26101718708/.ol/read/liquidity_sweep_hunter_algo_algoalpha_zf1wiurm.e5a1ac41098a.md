Calculation: Upper/lower liquidity levels = rolling highest high / lowest low over short-, medium-, and long-term lookbacks; record a new upper level when the rolling high is higher than it was several bars earlier and equals its prior-bar value, and a new lower level when the rolling low is lower than it was several bars earlier and equals its prior-bar value. Merge levels within a small price tolerance; retain until mitigated or expired.
Calculation: Visible swept bands = active levels with recently drawn heatmap cells, grouped into bands when their price intervals overlap. Upper levels are swept when high reaches or exceeds them; lower levels are swept when low reaches or falls below them. The upper reclaim level is the lowest swept upper-level price; the lower reclaim level is the highest swept lower-level price.
Calculation: Normalized liquidity strength = each active level's accumulated directional volume contribution divided by the maximum strength among the nearest active upper and lower levels, capped at the maximum. Use volume relative to its moving average, capped per bar; bearish bars add strength to the nearest upper levels and bullish bars to the nearest lower levels, with contributions decreasing for farther levels. A band's strength is its strongest constituent level's normalized strength; sweep strength is the average across swept bands.
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over a multi-bar lookback before the entry bar; short stop = highest high over that lookback. Long target = entry price plus the configured reward multiple of entry price minus long stop; short target = entry price minus the configured reward multiple of short stop minus entry price.
Long: On a confirmed bar, at least the configured minimum number of distinct visible lower bands are swept, and within the configured reclaim window a confirmed close is above the lower reclaim level; if strength filtering is enabled, average swept-band strength meets its minimum.
Short: On a confirmed bar, at least the configured minimum number of distinct visible upper bands are swept, and within the configured reclaim window a confirmed close is below the upper reclaim level; if strength filtering is enabled, average swept-band strength meets its minimum.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR bars since entry > configured expiry.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR bars since entry > configured expiry.
