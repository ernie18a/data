Calculation: Williams Alligator = long-, medium-, and short-lookback smoothed moving averages of median price, each sampled from its corresponding number of earlier bars.
Calculation: AO change = current AO minus prior-bar AO, where AO = short-lookback SMA of median price minus long-lookback SMA of median price.
Calculation: MFI = (high − low) / volume
Calculation: Squat bar = MFI is lower than on the preceding bar AND volume is higher than on the preceding bar.
Long: Within the configured trading window AND a stop entry is triggered above the high of a bar whose close is above its midpoint, whose low equals the lowest low over the lookback window, and whose high is below all three Williams Alligator lines AND (AO confirmation is disabled OR AO change < 0) AND (MFI confirmation is disabled OR the signal bar or either of the two preceding bars is a squat bar) AND (position is flat OR exactly one layer is open and low is below the first entry price by the second-layer drawdown threshold OR exactly two layers are open and low is below the first entry price by the third-layer drawdown threshold OR exactly three layers are open and low is below the first entry price by the fourth-layer drawdown threshold); the layer drawdown thresholds increase from layer two through layer four.
Exit: Long position, high >= volume-weighted average entry price + ATR × take-profit multiplier
