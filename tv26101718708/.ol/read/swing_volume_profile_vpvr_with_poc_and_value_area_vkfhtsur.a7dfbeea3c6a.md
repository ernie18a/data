Calculation: Swing-anchored volume profile levels = From the latest confirmed swing pivot to the current chart-timeframe bar, divide the full high-low range into price rows; allocate each bar’s volume evenly among the rows its high-low range touches. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume row; VAH and VAL bound the contiguous range grown from the POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent row until the selected share of total volume is included. A pivot is confirmed when its high or low is an extreme across equal left and right lookbacks.
Long: Confirmed bar close crosses above POC OR prior confirmed close was inside the value area and current confirmed close is above VAH
Short: Confirmed bar close crosses below POC OR prior confirmed close was inside the value area and current confirmed close is below VAL
