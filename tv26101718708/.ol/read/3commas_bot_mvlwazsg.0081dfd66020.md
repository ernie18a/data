Calculation: Fast MA and slow MA = user-selected moving average types (EMA, HEMA, SMA, HMA, WMA, VWMA, VWAP, DEMA, or T3) on chart-timeframe bars; fast MA uses a shorter lookback than slow MA. HEMA is an EMA of recursively calculated Heikin-Ashi open, where Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4, initial Heikin-Ashi open = (open + close) / 2, and later Heikin-Ashi open = (prior Heikin-Ashi open + prior Heikin-Ashi close) / 2.
Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Swing low/high = lowest low/highest high over the lookback on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Fast MA > slow MA AND prior fast MA <= prior slow MA AND ATR is available AND current bar is confirmed AND inside the configured date range AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is outside the ignored session) AND long trades enabled AND (flat position OR short position with reversal enabled).
Short: Fast MA < slow MA AND prior fast MA >= prior slow MA AND ATR is available AND current bar is confirmed AND inside the configured date range AND (session filter disabled OR current bar is outside the ignored session) AND short trades enabled AND (flat position OR long position with reversal enabled).
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR (limit exit enabled AND high >= long target). Initial long stop = signal-bar close - (signal-bar swing low distance from signal-bar close + ATR × risk adjustment); equivalently, swing low - ATR × risk adjustment. Long target = signal-bar close + reward-to-risk ratio × (signal-bar close - initial long stop). If trailing stop is enabled, it replaces the initial stop: it becomes active immediately when trigger fraction is zero, otherwise when high reaches signal-bar close + trigger fraction × (long target - signal-bar close); once active, raise it only, using (current swing low, or prior close, or prior open) - current ATR × trail multiplier, according to the selected source.
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR (limit exit enabled AND low <= short target). Initial short stop = signal-bar swing high + ATR × risk adjustment. Short target = signal-bar close - reward-to-risk ratio × (initial short stop - signal-bar close). If trailing stop is enabled, it replaces the initial stop: it becomes active immediately when trigger fraction is zero, otherwise when low reaches signal-bar close - trigger fraction × (signal-bar close - short target); once active, lower it only, using (current swing high, or prior close, or prior open) + current ATR × trail multiplier, according to the selected source.
