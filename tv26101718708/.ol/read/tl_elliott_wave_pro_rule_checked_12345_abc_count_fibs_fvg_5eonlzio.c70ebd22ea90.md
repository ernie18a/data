Calculation: ATR = average true range over a parameterized lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme over the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Wave pivots = alternating confirmed swing highs and lows, accepting a new pivot only when its distance from the preceding pivot meets a configurable ATR-based minimum; replace the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type.
Calculation: Impulse landmarks = the latest six alternating pivots forming origin, wave 1 end, wave 2 end, wave 3 end, wave 4 end, and wave 5 end; for a rising impulse, wave 1 rises, wave 2 retraces without passing the origin, wave 3 rises past wave 1 end, and wave 4 retraces without entering wave 1 territory; reverse these inequalities for a falling impulse. A wave 3 setup uses the first three landmarks; a wave 5 setup uses the first five. Require wave 3 to be at least as long as wave 1 while wave 5 is unformed; when wave 5 is formed, require wave 3 to be at least as long as both other motive waves in strict mode, or at least as long as the shorter of them in relaxed mode. A diagonal may overlap wave 1 in wave 4, but wave 4 must not pass the wave 2 endpoint and actionary waves must form a contracting wedge within the configured tolerance.
Calculation: Wave 2 retracement (%) = distance from wave 1 end to wave 2 end divided by wave 1 length, times 100.
Calculation: Wave 4 retracement (%) = distance from wave 3 end to wave 4 end divided by wave 3 length, times 100.
Calculation: Wave 2 zone = prices at the configured lower and upper retracement percentages of wave 1, measured from its end toward its origin.
Calculation: Wave 4 zone = prices at the configured lower and upper retracement percentages of wave 3, measured from its end toward its origin.
Calculation: Directional fair value gap = bullish when the current low is above the high two bars earlier, or bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier, with gap size at least a configurable ATR multiple; qualify only when the entire gap lies inside the corresponding entry zone and its starting bar is on or after the correction began.
Calculation: Long/short stop = selected stop mode: beyond the correction pivot, at the applicable count invalidation level, or the configured ATR distance from entry; then place it beyond the current bar's low/high by the configured ATR buffer. The wave 3 invalidation level is the impulse origin; the wave 5 level is the wave 1 endpoint.
Calculation: Wave 3 target 1/2 = wave 2 endpoint plus/minus the configured multiples of wave 1 length in the impulse direction. Wave 5 target 1 = wave 4 endpoint plus/minus wave 1 length; target 2 = wave 4 endpoint plus/minus the configured multiple of wave 4 length.
Calculation: Reward/risk = absolute distance from entry to target 1 divided by absolute distance from entry to stop.
Calculation: Last minor swing high/low = latest confirmed swing high/low using the minor swing pivot setting.
Long: rising impulse with wave 1 and wave 2 landmarks; wave 2 retracement is at least 0.75 times the lower configured zone percentage and no greater than the configured maximum acceptable retracement; wave 2 has not passed the impulse origin; any configured required guideline count is met; required fair value gap is present if enabled; no position is open; and, within the configured bar limit after wave 2, the selected trigger occurs (zone touch, close above the prior bar high and above the open, or close crossing above the last minor swing high), with reward/risk at least the configured minimum.
Short: falling impulse with wave 1 and wave 2 landmarks; wave 2 retracement is at least 0.75 times the lower configured zone percentage and no greater than the configured maximum acceptable retracement; wave 2 has not passed the impulse origin; any configured required guideline count is met; required fair value gap is present if enabled; no position is open; and, within the configured bar limit after wave 2, the selected trigger occurs (zone touch, close below the prior bar low and below the open, or close crossing below the last minor swing low), with reward/risk at least the configured minimum.
Long: rising impulse with wave 1 through wave 4 landmarks; wave 4 retracement is at least 0.7 times the lower configured zone percentage and no greater than 1.25 times the upper configured zone percentage; wave 3 is not the shortest motive wave; wave 2 has not passed the impulse origin; wave 4 has not invalidated the count; any configured required guideline count is met; required fair value gap is present if enabled; no position is open; and, within the configured bar limit after wave 4, the selected trigger occurs (zone touch, close above the prior bar high and above the open, or close crossing above the last minor swing high), with reward/risk at least the configured minimum.
Short: falling impulse with wave 1 through wave 4 landmarks; wave 4 retracement is at least 0.7 times the lower configured zone percentage and no greater than 1.25 times the upper configured zone percentage; wave 3 is not the shortest motive wave; wave 2 has not passed the impulse origin; wave 4 has not invalidated the count; any configured required guideline count is met; required fair value gap is present if enabled; no position is open; and, within the configured bar limit after wave 4, the selected trigger occurs (zone touch, close below the prior bar low and below the open, or close crossing below the last minor swing low), with reward/risk at least the configured minimum.
Long: falling impulse with a confirmed A and B correction pivot and countertrend signals enabled; no position is open; within the configured time limit after B, close is above the prior bar high and above the open.
Short: rising impulse with a confirmed A and B correction pivot and countertrend signals enabled; no position is open; within the configured time limit after B, close is below the prior bar low and below the open.
Exit: long, high >= target 1 OR low <= active stop (configured stop until break-even is reached, then entry price) OR close < impulse origin during wave 2 OR close < wave 1 endpoint during wave 4 (except diagonal counts, where close < wave 2 endpoint invalidates).
Exit: short, low <= target 1 OR high >= active stop (configured stop until break-even is reached, then entry price) OR close > impulse origin during wave 2 OR close > wave 1 endpoint during wave 4 (except diagonal counts, where close > wave 2 endpoint invalidates).
