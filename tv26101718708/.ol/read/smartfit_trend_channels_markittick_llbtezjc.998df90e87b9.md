Calculation: Adaptive source = selected smoother (SMA, EMA, RMA, double WMA, triple VWMA, HMA, LLAMA, or recursive Kalman filter) applied to the previous completed bar’s close; use that close directly if smoothing is disabled.
Calculation: Regression channel = OLS regression of the adaptive source over bars since the current anchor, using completed bars; calculate residual standard deviation and Pearson correlation of source with time. The upper and lower bands at the latest completed bar are the regression value plus or minus the configured multiple of residual standard deviation.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a high greater than all highs, or a low less than all lows, in a symmetric window; recognize it only after the window’s bars on both sides have closed.
Calculation: Channel anchor = initially the starting bar; after a breakout, use the latest opposite-type confirmed pivot if its regression fit meets the fit threshold and it lies between the prior anchor and breakout, otherwise use the breakout bar. Calculate the pivot’s fit using regression from that pivot through the latest completed bar.
Calculation: ADX = Average Directional Index; when its optional filter is enabled, require ADX to meet its configured minimum.
Long: regression slope < 0 AND absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold OR previous completed close > upper band AND previous regression length >= minimum bars AND absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold AND NOT (close before that > its then-current upper band AND its regression length >= minimum bars AND its absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX meets minimum)
Short: regression slope > 0 AND absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold OR previous completed close < lower band AND previous regression length >= minimum bars AND absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold AND NOT (close before that < its then-current lower band AND its regression length >= minimum bars AND its absolute Pearson correlation >= fit threshold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX meets minimum)
Exit: long, short breakout condition
