Calculation: LSMA = linear regression value of close over a lookback
Calculation: Pivot low = confirmed local minimum of close using left- and right-side lookbacks
Calculation: Renko rate of change = rate of change of the close of an ATR-based Renko series
Long: LSMA crosses below Pivot low AND close > VWMA AND Renko rate of change < 0
Short: VWAP crosses below LSMA
Exit: long, LSMA crosses Pivot low OR close < VWMA
