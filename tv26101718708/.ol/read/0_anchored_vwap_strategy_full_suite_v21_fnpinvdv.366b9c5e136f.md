Calculation: Stochastic RSI = apply stochastic calculation to RSI; smooth its %K with a selectable moving average to get K, then take a short-term SMA of K to get D
Calculation: High anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 (or high) from the most recent anchor reset; reset the candidate anchor on D < lower band or a new candidate high, and promote it to the active anchor when the high exceeds the prior active high, the state is overbought, K < D, and K < lower reversal level
Calculation: Low anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of HLC3 (or low) from the most recent anchor reset; reset the candidate anchor on D > upper band or a new candidate low, and promote it to the active anchor when the low falls below the prior active low, the state is oversold, K > D, and K > upper reversal level
Calculation: Alert price = open or close
Long: Alert price > High anchored VWAP AND (trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier) AND cooldown inactive AND within backtest date range
Short: Alert price < Low anchored VWAP AND (trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier) AND shorts enabled AND cooldown inactive AND within backtest date range
Exit: long, Alert price < Low anchored VWAP after any minimum hold requirement; or low <= entry price − ATR × stop multiplier when ATR stop is enabled; or high >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) when partial take profit is enabled
Exit: short, Alert price > High anchored VWAP after any minimum hold requirement; or high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier when ATR stop is enabled; or low <= average entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) when partial take profit is enabled
