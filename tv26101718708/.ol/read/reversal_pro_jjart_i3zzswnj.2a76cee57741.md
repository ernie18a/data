Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by its moving average over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Price Z-score = (close − moving average of close over the price lookback) / standard deviation of close over that lookback.
Calculation: Long/short classifier score = gradient-boosting classifier probability using RSI, relative volume, price Z-score, and ADX; train separate directional models on whether price moves at least 1 ATR in that direction within the forward-label horizon, using only completed outcomes.
Calculation: Long/short predicted excursion = separate directional gradient-boosting regressor output in ATR units, trained on the corresponding forward excursion.
Calculation: Initial stop = beyond the signal bar's low for a long or high for a short by an ATR buffer, with minimum distance of a fraction of ATR from entry.
Calculation: TP3 distance = greater of the directional predicted excursion in ATR units and initial risk multiplied by the TP3 risk floor.
Long: On bar close, low < lowest low of the prior trigger lookback AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND long classifier score ≥ score threshold AND long classifier score − short classifier score ≥ minimum directional edge AND ADX is within the allowed regime AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND (CCI filter disabled OR CCI reached its oversold threshold within its recent lookback) AND (EMA filter disabled OR the selected with-trend/counter-trend close-to-EMA condition holds) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ its floor).
Short: On bar close, high > highest high of the prior trigger lookback AND close < (high + low) / 2 AND short classifier score ≥ score threshold AND short classifier score − long classifier score ≥ minimum directional edge AND ADX is within the allowed regime AND cooldown since the previous signal has elapsed AND (CCI filter disabled OR CCI reached its overbought threshold within its recent lookback) AND (EMA filter disabled OR the selected with-trend/counter-trend close-to-EMA condition holds) AND (relative-volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ its floor).
Exit: long, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, high ≥ TP3 OR low ≤ current stop; after TP1 is reached, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled.
Exit: short, on a confirmed bar after the entry bar, low ≤ TP3 OR high ≥ current stop; after TP1 is reached, move the stop to entry when break-even is enabled.
