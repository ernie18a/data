Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3, reset at each new daily session
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = session VWAP plus or minus the configured number of standard deviations
Calculation: Previous-session-high and previous-session-low anchored VWAPs = volume-weighted average of hlc3 from the respective extreme bar of the previous session through the current bar
Calculation: A/B/D risk = max(distance from entry close to the signal candle's low minus the ATR buffer for a long, or to its high plus the buffer for a short, minimum ATR risk); stop = entry close minus risk for a long or plus risk for a short; target = entry close plus or minus the configured risk multiple
Calculation: Band-reversion stop = beyond the furthest high or low of the outside-band excursion by the ATR buffer, with risk floored at the minimum ATR risk; target = session VWAP at entry
Long: On a completed bar with volume and ATR available, no open position for this setup, and not the first bar of a session: for A, after at least the configured consecutive closes above session VWAP this session, the first subsequent touch (low <= session VWAP) that closes above it; OR for B, after at least the configured consecutive closes below session VWAP this session, a close above session VWAP followed within the configured retest-bar limit by a later touch (low <= session VWAP) closing above it, with no intervening close below session VWAP; OR for C, after the session has reached the configured minimum age, a close below the lower band followed by the first close back inside the band that is below session VWAP; OR for D, after at least the configured consecutive closes above the previous-session-low anchored VWAP, the first subsequent touch (low <= that VWAP) closing above it
Short: On a completed bar with volume and ATR available, no open position for this setup, and not the first bar of a session: for A, after at least the configured consecutive closes below session VWAP this session, the first subsequent touch (high >= session VWAP) that closes below it; OR for B, after at least the configured consecutive closes above session VWAP this session, a close below session VWAP followed within the configured retest-bar limit by a later touch (high >= session VWAP) closing below it, with no intervening close above session VWAP; OR for C, after the session has reached the configured minimum age, a close above the upper band followed by the first close back inside the band that is above session VWAP; OR for D, after at least the configured consecutive closes below the previous-session-high anchored VWAP, the first subsequent touch (high >= that VWAP) closing below it
Exit: long, low <= stored stop OR high >= stored target; if both are touched on one bar, exit at the stop, and if the open gaps through the stop, exit at the open
Exit: short, high >= stored stop OR low <= stored target; if both are touched on one bar, exit at the stop, and if the open gaps through the stop, exit at the open
Exit: long, current bar is the first bar of a new daily session; exit at its open
Exit: short, current bar is the first bar of a new daily session; exit at its open
