Calculation: confirmed swing-high pool = latest high that exceeds the highs of the preceding and following N bars, available after the following N bars complete; retain until replaced by a newer confirmed swing high
Calculation: confirmed swing-low pool = latest low that is below the lows of the preceding and following N bars, available after the following N bars complete; retain until replaced by a newer confirmed swing low
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close); body = abs(close − open)
Calculation: signed volume proxy = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume, lookback), or 0 when the SMA is not positive
Calculation: wick/body ratio = wick / body when body > 0; when body = 0, use 5 if wick > 0, otherwise 0
Calculation: delta ratio = signed volume proxy / abs(SMA(signed volume proxy, same lookback)), or 0 when the absolute SMA is 0
Calculation: absorption score = weighted average of volume, wick, and delta components, each clamped to [0,100]; volume component = 100 × volume ratio / (volume multiplier × 1.8); wick component = 100 × wick/body ratio / (minimum wick/body ratio × 2); long delta component = 100 × max(delta ratio, 0) / 2; short delta component = 100 × max(−delta ratio, 0) / 2
Long: a confirmed swing-low pool exists AND ATR > 0 AND low < pool − ATR × sweep buffer AND lower wick ≥ ATR × minimum sweep wick AND (close > pool, if close-back-inside is enabled) AND low < prior bar low AND (ATR ≥ SMA(ATR, longer lookback) × volatility floor, if volatility filter is enabled and the SMA is positive) AND (close > EMA(close, lookback), if EMA filter is enabled) AND volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier AND lower wick/body ratio ≥ minimum wick/body ratio AND long absorption score ≥ minimum score
Short: a confirmed swing-high pool exists AND ATR > 0 AND high > pool + ATR × sweep buffer AND upper wick ≥ ATR × minimum sweep wick AND (close < pool, if close-back-inside is enabled) AND high > prior bar high AND (ATR ≥ SMA(ATR, longer lookback) × volatility floor, if volatility filter is enabled and the SMA is positive) AND (close < EMA(close, lookback), if EMA filter is enabled) AND volume ratio ≥ volume multiplier AND upper wick/body ratio ≥ minimum wick/body ratio AND short absorption score ≥ minimum score
