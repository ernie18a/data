Calculation: Buy volume = volume × (|close − open| + min(open, close) − low) ÷ (high − low), using 1 as the divisor when high = low
Calculation: Sell volume = volume × (|close − open| + high − max(open, close)) ÷ (high − low), using 1 as the divisor when high = low
Calculation: Buy:sell ratio = buy volume ÷ sell volume, or buy volume when sell volume = 0
Long: Buy:sell ratio ≥ 1
Short: Buy:sell ratio < 1
