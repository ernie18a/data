Calculation: Bull score = count of: close > VWAP; fast EMA > slow EMA; RSI within the bullish range; volume >= volume moving average × volume multiplier; close > fast SMA > slow SMA. Fast averages use shorter lookbacks than slow averages.
Calculation: Bear score = count of: close < VWAP; fast EMA < slow EMA; RSI within the bearish range; volume >= volume moving average × volume multiplier; close < fast SMA < slow SMA. Fast averages use shorter lookbacks than slow averages.
Long: (EMA-cross requirement enabled AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bull score >= minimum score) OR (EMA-cross requirement disabled AND bull score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR close > close at both shorter and longer momentum lookbacks)); AND at least the signal cooldown has elapsed since the previous long signal; AND NOT (the prior bar met the applicable long trigger and the cooldown was elapsed then); OR fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND bull score >= strong-signal threshold.
Short: (EMA-cross requirement enabled AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bear score >= minimum score) OR (EMA-cross requirement disabled AND bear score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR close < close at both shorter and longer momentum lookbacks)); AND at least the signal cooldown has elapsed since the previous short signal; AND NOT (the prior bar met the applicable short trigger and the cooldown was elapsed then); OR fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND bear score >= strong-signal threshold.
Exit: Long, trim signals enabled AND bull score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR close > close at both shorter and longer momentum lookbacks) AND (RSI > overbought threshold OR close >= upper Bollinger Band × trim proximity factor) AND at least the trim cooldown has elapsed since either prior trim signal.
Exit: Short, trim signals enabled AND bear score >= minimum score AND (momentum filter disabled OR close < close at both shorter and longer momentum lookbacks) AND (RSI < oversold threshold OR close <= lower Bollinger Band × trim proximity factor) AND at least the trim cooldown has elapsed since either prior trim signal.
