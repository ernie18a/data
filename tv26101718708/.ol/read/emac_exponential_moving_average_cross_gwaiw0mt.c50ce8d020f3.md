Calculation: Configured moving average = simple, exponential, or weighted moving average of close over its configured lookback; the first and second short-lookback averages are shorter than the medium-lookback average, and the two long-lookback averages have longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend state = compare the latest available higher-timeframe close with the close the configured number of higher-timeframe bars earlier: up if current close >= past close × (1 + growth threshold); down if current close <= past close × (1 − growth threshold); sideways up if past close < current close < past close × (1 + growth threshold); sideways down if past close × (1 − growth threshold) < current close < past close. Use the latest available higher-timeframe bar (completed in historical bars; forming in real time).
Calculation: Initial stop price = the signal bar's high minus ATR × stop multiple if that is below the signal bar's low; otherwise the signal bar's low.
Calculation: Profit target price = entry price + (entry price − initial stop price) × target multiple.
Long: Previous close < previous fast moving average AND current close > fast moving average AND current close > second-shortest and medium-lookback moving averages AND (short moving-average filter disabled OR fast moving average > second-shortest moving average) AND fast moving average > medium-lookback moving average AND (long moving-average filter disabled OR shorter of the two long-lookback moving averages >= longer of the two long-lookback moving averages) AND the configured higher-timeframe trend requirement is met.
Exit: Long, (early exit enabled AND close < fast moving average) OR (stops enabled AND low <= initial stop price) OR (targets enabled AND high >= profit target price)
