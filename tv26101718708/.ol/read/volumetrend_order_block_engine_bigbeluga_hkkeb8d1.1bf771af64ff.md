Calculation: Custom volatility = simple moving average of high minus low over the lookback
Calculation: Custom Supertrend direction = start bullish when the prior custom volatility is unavailable; otherwise, if the prior trend stop equals the prior trailing upper band, bullish when close is above the current trailing upper band and bearish otherwise; in all other cases, bearish when close is below the current trailing lower band and bullish otherwise. Initial bands are hl2 plus or minus multiplier times custom volatility. Current trailing lower band equals the initial lower band when it exceeds the prior trailing lower band or prior close is below the prior trailing lower band, and equals the prior trailing lower band otherwise. Current trailing upper band equals the initial upper band when it is below the prior trailing upper band or prior close is above the prior trailing upper band, and equals the prior trailing upper band otherwise. The trend stop is the trailing lower band when bullish and trailing upper band when bearish.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low lower than the lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; confirmed swing high = a high higher than the highs of the specified number of bars on both sides. Each pivot becomes available only after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Buy volume percentage = sum of volume for bars with close at or above open, divided by total volume, over the pivot confirmation bar and the specified number of preceding bars; use 50% when total volume is zero. Sell volume percentage = 100% minus buy volume percentage.
Calculation: Active bullish order block = on a bullish trend bar confirming a swing low, set the zone top to the lower of open and close on the pivot bar and the zone bottom to top minus current custom volatility, if the zone does not overlap the active zone. Active bearish order block = on a bearish trend bar confirming a swing high, set the zone bottom to the higher of open and close on the pivot bar and the zone top to bottom plus current custom volatility, if the zone does not overlap the active zone. Zones overlap when their price intervals intersect, including touching. A newly accepted zone replaces the active zone and stores its buy and sell volume percentages. Clear the active zone when its trend is bullish and the bar high is below its bottom, or its trend is bearish and the bar low is above its top.
Long: current low crosses above the active zone top AND active zone exists AND active buy volume percentage is at least the minimum buy percentage AND bullish retest signals are enabled AND no swing low is confirmed on this bar AND custom Supertrend direction did not change on this bar AND this bar is confirmed
Short: current high crosses below the active zone bottom AND active zone exists AND active sell volume percentage is at least the minimum sell percentage AND bearish retest signals are enabled AND no swing high is confirmed on this bar AND custom Supertrend direction did not change on this bar AND this bar is confirmed
