Calculation: Risk-adjusted momentum score = For each of three horizons with strictly increasing lookbacks, divide the log return over that lookback by the rolling standard deviation of one-bar log returns multiplied by the square root of the lookback. Normalize the configured horizon weights to sum to 1; average available horizon values using their renormalized weights. Multiply that average by 0.35 + 0.65 × (absolute sum of available horizon signs / number of available horizons), then transform x to 100 × x / (2 + |x|). Use the last completed bar of the comparison timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. A horizon is unavailable when its required prices or volatility are unavailable or volatility is zero.
Calculation: Valid score = risk-adjusted momentum score when at least the configured minimum share of horizons is available and the source bar is no more than the configured lag behind the newest enabled source bar; otherwise unavailable.
Calculation: Chart rank = 1 + the number of enabled valid scores strictly greater than the chart symbol's valid score.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar with a new reference source time, chart score is valid AND current chart rank <= configured top-rank threshold AND previous chart rank > configured top-rank threshold.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar with a new reference source time, chart score is valid AND |previous chart rank − current chart rank| >= configured rank-change threshold AND current chart rank > previous chart rank.
