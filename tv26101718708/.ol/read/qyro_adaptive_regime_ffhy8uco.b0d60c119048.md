Calculation: Signed efficiency = net close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; retain the net change's sign.
Calculation: Adaptive trend = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close, using a shorter fast smoothing length and a longer slow smoothing length, adapted by the efficiency ratio.
Calculation: Regime score = weighted average of signed efficiency, normalized adaptive-trend slope, close location relative to the adaptive trend, volatility state, relative volume, and higher-timeframe alignment; clamp to its score range. Normalize slope by ATR; measure close location in ATR units. Volatility state compares ATR with its longer-lookback average: expanded above its expansion threshold, contracted below its contraction threshold, otherwise normal. Its reading depends on volatility state and whether close and adaptive-trend slope indicate upside momentum, downside momentum, or neither. Relative volume compares current volume with its moving average, or is neutral when volume is unavailable. Higher-timeframe alignment uses the last completed bar of a higher timeframe: bullish when its close is above its adaptive trend and that trend is rising, bearish when below and falling, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Regime state = range until the score reaches the uptrend-entry threshold or downtrend-entry threshold; enter uptrend at or above its entry threshold and downtrend at or below its entry threshold. While in uptrend, remain there until the score falls below its uptrend-exit threshold; while in downtrend, remain there until the score rises above its downtrend-exit threshold. Otherwise the state is range. Do not change state until the current state has held for the minimum number of bars. Before all score components have sufficient history, state is range.
Long: Regime state = uptrend AND previous bar's regime state != uptrend
Short: Regime state = downtrend AND previous bar's regime state != downtrend
