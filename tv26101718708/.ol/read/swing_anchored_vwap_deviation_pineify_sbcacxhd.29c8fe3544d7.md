Calculation: Swing-anchored VWAP = volume-weighted mean of HLC3 from the latest accepted confirmed swing pivot through the current bar; accept a pivot when its distance from the latest opposing confirmed pivot divided by ATR at the pivot meets a parameterized threshold, and if high and low pivots both qualify, accept the one with the larger ratio. Start the calculation at the pivot and process through its confirmation bar; reset on each accepted pivot. Valid only when accumulated volume is positive.
Long: Current close > current swing-anchored VWAP AND previous close <= previous swing-anchored VWAP AND current bar is confirmed AND no new pivot was accepted on the current bar AND accumulated volume is positive.
Short: Current close < current swing-anchored VWAP AND previous close >= previous swing-anchored VWAP AND current bar is confirmed AND no new pivot was accepted on the current bar AND accumulated volume is positive.
