Calculation: Simple moving average = arithmetic mean of closing prices over its lookback; the filter moving average uses a longer lookback than the entry moving average.
Calculation: Rising = current value is greater than every value in the preceding lookback bars; falling = current value is less than every value in the preceding lookback bars.
Calculation: True range = max(high − low, abs(high − previous close), abs(low − previous close)); ATR = average true range over its lookback.
Calculation: +DI and −DI = 100 × Wilder-smoothed positive and negative directional movement, respectively, divided by Wilder-smoothed true range. Positive movement is high − previous high when it exceeds previous low − low and is positive, otherwise zero; negative movement is previous low − low when it exceeds high − previous high and is positive, otherwise zero. ADX = simple moving average of 100 × abs(+DI − −DI) / (+DI + −DI).
Calculation: Initial stop = selected ATR multiple, percentage, or fixed price distance from the signal bar's low for a long or high for a short; ATR distance uses the signal bar's ATR.
Calculation: In-trade stop = selected ATR multiple below the average of the two preceding bars' lower open/close values for a long or above the average of their higher open/close values for a short; or a trailing stop offset from the highest high reached in a long or lowest low reached in a short by a multiple of the entry-stop distance as a percentage of entry price or by a fixed percentage. Keep the stop at least as favorable as its preceding value; reset it to the initial stop after a stop breach.
Long: (entry moving average is rising over its lookback OR +DI crosses above −DI AND ADX > threshold) AND (if enabled, close > open) AND (if enabled, volume > previous volume) AND (if enabled, filter moving average is rising over its lookback)
Short: (entry moving average is falling over its lookback OR −DI crosses above +DI AND ADX > threshold) AND (if enabled, close < open) AND (if enabled, volume > previous volume) AND (if enabled, filter moving average is falling over its lookback)
Exit: long, close < preceding bar's active stop
Exit: short, close > preceding bar's active stop
