Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = for any enabled indicator, a confirmed price pivot low paired with a later lower price low and higher indicator value; regular bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high paired with a later higher price high and lower indicator value. Compare against the most recent qualifying pivot among the configured pivot search limits, require the pivot separation to exceed five bars, and require the indicator and close to stay on or above their straight lines between lows for bullish divergence, or on or below their straight lines between highs for bearish divergence. With confirmation enabled, evaluate the current bar against the previous bar and exclude the current bar as the endpoint; otherwise use the current bar. Pivot source is close, or high/low for pivots and divergence price endpoints when configured.
Calculation: Divergence count = number of enabled indicators producing regular bullish divergences minus number producing regular bearish divergences; discard all divergences when this count is below the configured minimum.
Calculation: LazyBear volume-weighted MACD histogram = (short-lookback EMA of volume × close divided by short-lookback EMA of volume, minus long-lookback EMA of volume × close divided by long-lookback EMA of volume) minus its signal-length EMA; the long lookback exceeds the short lookback.
Calculation: Williams Vix Fix = (highest close over its lookback − low) / highest close over its lookback × −100.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = smoothed stochastic of RSI, using the same RSI series for stochastic high, low, and close.
Calculation: SMI Ergodic oscillator = TSI(close, short length, long length) minus its signal EMA; the long length exceeds the short length.
Calculation: Bull Bear Power = (high − EMA(close, length)) + (low − EMA(close, length)).
Calculation: Smoothed Balance of Power = triple EMA of EMA-smoothed Balance of Power, where Balance of Power is the mean of the normalized bullish open, close, and candle-body components minus the mean of the normalized bearish open, close, and candle-body components; normalize by high − low, using 0.01 when high equals low.
Calculation: Relative Volatility Index moving average = selected moving average of RVI; RVI = EMA of positive close changes weighted by close standard deviation divided by the sum of that EMA and EMA of standard deviation on non-positive close changes, multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Logistic dominance = sign of the average of the 30-step logistic map sequence multiplied by that sequence's standard deviation; map input is close and its return input is the negative sum of the logistic maps of positive and negative close, using the configured dominance lookback.
Long: positive regular divergence detected AND (divergence count is at least the configured minimum) AND within the configured test date range AND not already entered on the most recent closed trade's entry bar AND (long entries enabled) AND all enabled long filters pass: selected Keltner Channel or Bollinger Band entry condition (low below lower band; or prior low below lower band and current close above it; or close below lower band; or prior close below lower band and current close above it, according to selected mode); ATR short-lookback average true range < or > its longer-lookback counterpart as selected; smoothed short-lookback volume < or > its longer-lookback counterpart as selected; selected configured-timeframe RSI, CCI, and Chande Momentum Oscillator each within its configured long range (RSI/CCI/Momentum use the last completed bar on the configured timeframe when it differs from chart timeframe); configured-timeframe MFI within its strict long range and any enabled MFI rising/falling requirement passes (MFI uses the bar still forming on its configured timeframe when it differs from chart timeframe); SuperTrend uptrend, or downtrend when opposite mode is selected. An enabled second RSI range must also pass. Disabled filters impose no condition.
Short: negative regular divergence detected AND (divergence count is at least the configured minimum) AND within the configured test date range AND not already entered on the most recent closed trade's entry bar AND (short entries enabled) AND all enabled short filters pass: selected Keltner Channel or Bollinger Band entry condition (high above upper band; or prior high above upper band and current close below it; or close above upper band; or prior close above upper band and current close below it, according to selected mode); ATR short-lookback average true range < or > its longer-lookback counterpart as selected; smoothed short-lookback volume < or > its longer-lookback counterpart as selected; selected configured-timeframe RSI, CCI, and Chande Momentum Oscillator each within its configured short range (RSI/CCI/Momentum use the last completed bar on the configured timeframe when it differs from chart timeframe); configured-timeframe MFI within its strict short range and any enabled MFI rising/falling requirement passes (MFI uses the bar still forming on its configured timeframe when it differs from chart timeframe); SuperTrend downtrend, or uptrend when opposite mode is selected. An enabled second RSI range must also pass. Disabled filters impose no condition.
Exit: long, close >= entry price × (1 + configured take-profit fraction) OR close <= entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss fraction) OR, when opposite-signal stop is enabled, negative regular divergence detected and all enabled short filters pass.
Exit: short, close <= entry price × (1 − configured take-profit fraction) OR close >= entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss fraction) OR, when opposite-signal stop is enabled, positive regular divergence detected and all enabled long filters pass.
