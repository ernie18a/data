Calculation: MACD = EMA of close with a shorter lookback minus EMA of close with a longer lookback.
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of MACD.
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD minus MACD signal line.
Calculation: Histogram squeeze threshold = the selected percentile of absolute MACD histogram values over a rolling lookback.
Long: (MACD > 0 AND previous MACD <= 0 AND bars since any signal >= cooldown) OR (MACD > MACD signal line AND previous MACD <= previous MACD signal line AND MACD < 0 AND bars since any signal >= cooldown) OR (a confirmed histogram pivot low is higher than the previous histogram pivot low AND its corresponding price low is lower than the previous price low AND the histogram pivot is below zero) OR (all four higher-timeframe MACDs > 0 AND at least three of their histograms > 0 AND the prior chart bar did not meet both conditions; use each timeframe's current bar, including while it is still forming) OR (absolute histogram exceeds the squeeze threshold after at least four consecutive coiled bars AND histogram > 0).
Short: (MACD < 0 AND previous MACD >= 0 AND bars since any signal >= cooldown) OR (MACD < MACD signal line AND previous MACD >= previous MACD signal line AND MACD > 0 AND bars since any signal >= cooldown) OR (a confirmed histogram pivot high is lower than the previous histogram pivot high AND its corresponding price high is higher than the previous price high AND the histogram pivot is above zero) OR (all four higher-timeframe MACDs <= 0 AND at most one of their histograms > 0 AND the prior chart bar did not meet both conditions; use each timeframe's current bar, including while it is still forming) OR (absolute histogram exceeds the squeeze threshold after at least four consecutive coiled bars AND histogram < 0).
