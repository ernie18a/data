Calculation: Volume spike = volume >= average volume over the configured lookback × configured multiplier
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = bullish candle with body >= ATR × configured displacement multiplier, close in the upper configured fraction of its range, close > prior open, open < prior close, and a volume spike
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = bearish candle with body >= ATR × configured displacement multiplier, close in the lower configured fraction of its range, close < prior open, open > prior close, and a volume spike
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Downside sweep = prior bar low <= prior range low AND current close > prior range low
Calculation: Upside sweep = prior bar high >= prior range high AND current close < prior range high
Calculation: Structure flip long/short = bullish/bearish engulfing AND price is within the configured ATR distance of a qualifying support/resistance level (prior-day low/high, weekly open, daily open, or matching 1-hour/daily fair-value-gap midpoint)
Calculation: Fair-value gap = three-bar gap where current low > high two bars ago (bullish) or current high < low two bars ago (bearish), and gap size >= configured minimum; chart-timeframe gaps use chart bars, while 1-hour and daily gaps use the last completed bars of those higher timeframes
Calculation: Fair-value-gap entry long/short = matching chart-timeframe gap exists, is within its configured age limit, close is inside its gap, and bullish/bearish engulfing is true
Calculation: Box break long/short = close above stored range high / below stored range low, open below / above that boundary, and candle body >= configured fraction of ATR; the range begins when the configured lookback range first qualifies as narrow and updates while it remains narrow
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = +1 after a higher-timeframe downside sweep, -1 after an upside sweep, otherwise retain the prior value; evaluate the higher-timeframe bar data returned while that bar may still be forming
Calculation: Long score = capped sum of configured weights for positive higher-timeframe bias, configured session active, volume spike, correlated-symbol downside sweep, positive relative return versus the correlated symbol, qualifying 1-hour bullish gap not overridden by a sufficiently larger nearby daily bearish gap, daily bullish gap, proximity to prior-day low, falling daily VIX, at least two downside sweeps since reset, qualifying support proximity, structure flip long, and fair-value-gap entry long
Calculation: Short score = capped sum of configured weights for negative higher-timeframe bias, configured session active, volume spike, correlated-symbol upside sweep, negative relative return versus the correlated symbol, qualifying 1-hour bearish gap not overridden by a sufficiently larger nearby daily bullish gap, daily bearish gap, proximity to prior-day high, rising daily VIX, at least two upside sweeps since reset, qualifying resistance proximity, structure flip short, and fair-value-gap entry short
Calculation: Long/short dominance = its score is greater than the opposing score; score gap = absolute difference between the scores; worthwhile score = dominant score meets the configured higher threshold OR meets the configured lower threshold with at least the configured score gap
Calculation: Long stop = prior range low minus ATR × configured stop multiplier, captured on a downside sweep; short stop = prior range high plus ATR × configured stop multiplier, captured on an upside sweep
Long: downside sweep AND long score >= configured minimum score OR long score >= configured maximum-alert threshold OR short score <= configured weak-score threshold AND long score >= configured contrarian threshold OR downside sweep AND long score dominates AND worthwhile score OR upside sweep AND long score dominates AND long score >= configured fade threshold OR structure flip long OR fair-value-gap entry long OR box break long AND volume spike OR downside sweep AND close > highest high over the configured breakout lookback
Short: upside sweep AND short score >= configured minimum score OR short score >= configured maximum-alert threshold OR long score <= configured weak-score threshold AND short score >= configured contrarian threshold OR upside sweep AND short score dominates AND worthwhile score OR downside sweep AND short score dominates AND short score >= configured fade threshold OR structure flip short OR fair-value-gap entry short OR box break short AND volume spike OR upside sweep AND close < lowest low over the configured breakout lookback
Exit: long, close < captured long stop
Exit: short, close > captured short stop
