Calculation: Resistance = latest confirmed pivot high of max(open, close), available one bar after confirmation and carried forward
Calculation: Support = latest confirmed pivot low of min(open, close), available one bar after confirmation and carried forward
Calculation: Active resistance/support = the latest respective pivot level, active when established and deactivated after the first close above resistance or below support
Long: close crosses above resistance OR active resistance and close > active resistance
Short: close crosses below support OR active support and close < active support
