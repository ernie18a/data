Calculation: Session references = current session open; high, low and close of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar; high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe weekly and monthly bars; previous session HLC3 VWAP; and ADR calculated from completed higher-timeframe daily ranges.
Calculation: Session VWAP and deviation bands = volume-weighted mean and volume-weighted standard deviation of HLC3 accumulated during the current session.
Calculation: Risk ATR = chart ATR, optionally reduced after an afternoon cutoff when expiry mode is enabled.
Calculation: Opening range = high and low accumulated over a configured opening-session window using completed lower-timeframe bars, then fixed for the session; midpoint = average of its boundaries; width = high minus low.
Calculation: Body fraction above a level = portion of the open-close interval above that level divided by absolute candle body; body fraction below is symmetric; both are zero for a zero-size body.
Calculation: RMI = RSI applied to close changes over a momentum lag, using Wilder smoothing with a longer lookback than the momentum lag; opening-range RMI = last completed chart-bar RMI immediately before the opening range becomes fixed.
Calculation: Profile levels = developing session volume-profile POC, VAH and VAL calculated from completed lower-timeframe bars, sampled before incorporating the current chart bar; prior-session profile references are sampled before session rotation.
Calculation: Opening-range breakout state = upward when close exceeds the upper boundary and body fraction above it reaches the confirmation threshold; downward is symmetric. Reset to neutral after a required consecutive count of closes inside both boundaries by a width-based margin, or after a breakout-age threshold when close crosses the midpoint against the breakout. Reset breakout-age counters on neutralization; reset the directional counter on a new breakout; otherwise increment counters each completed chart bar. Reset session state daily.
Calculation: Breakout score = equally weighted signed votes: positive votes for volume exceeding its SMA by a multiplier, sufficient directional body extension beyond the broken boundary, close extending beyond that boundary by an ATR buffer, and close on the breakout side of VWAP; an optional positive RMI vote for remaining directional room or negative vote for exhaustion, using opening-range RMI; a negative vote when last-completed higher-timeframe daily RSI is exhausted in the breakout direction unless the opening gap sufficiently opposes that direction; and an optional positive POC vote when developing POC lies beyond the opposite opening-range boundary, or negative vote when it lies beyond the broken boundary. Upward body extension is max(close minus upper boundary, zero) divided by body size; downward extension is max(lower boundary minus max(open,close), zero) divided by body size. Store the score at each new breakout.
Calculation: Opening-range retest buffer = maximum of an ATR multiple and an opening-range-width multiple; retest close buffer = this buffer when stricter confirmation is enabled, otherwise zero.
Calculation: Synthetic CVD = session cumulative sum of volume × (2×close−high−low)/(high−low); for a zero-range candle use volume times the sign of close minus previous close. At each completed rolling-low or rolling-high bar, store current CVD as the corresponding anchor. The comparison lag equals the rolling-extreme lookback.
Calculation: RMI divergence references = latest independently confirmed price-high, price-low, RMI-high and RMI-low pivots using common left/right confirmation lengths. Also retain the latest overbought RMI-high pivot with the price high at its pivot bar, and latest oversold RMI-low pivot with the price low at its pivot bar. Update references before testing divergence; reset them daily. Recent extrema use the current bar plus the right-confirmation lookback, with price and RMI extrema calculated independently.
Calculation: Upper wick = high minus max(open,close); lower wick = min(open,close) minus low; range = high minus low.
Calculation: Chart sweep levels = enabled prior-session high, low, close, VWAP and POC; fixed opening-range boundaries; prior-week high and low; session open plus or minus an ADR multiple; and developing POC after a session-age warmup. Upper sweeps use prior-session high, upper opening-range boundary, prior-week high and upper ADR boundary; lower sweeps use their lower counterparts; prior close, VWAP and POC apply to both sides.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sweep levels = the corresponding chart sweep levels excluding POC. Use two higher timeframes, one longer than the other, evaluating each only when its bar has just completed.
Calculation: Structural levels = available opening-range high, low and midpoint, current and previous session VWAP, and prior-session high and low.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = previous bar low below both neighboring lows AND current close within an ATR tolerance of any structural level AND previous low within that tolerance of any structural level. Confirmed swing high is symmetric. Require an in-session bar and, when enabled, a fixed opening range.
Calculation: Relative session volume = accumulated volume in the current intraday time bucket divided by the mean volume of the same bucket in prior sessions, prorated to elapsed bucket time using the current bar closing timestamp. Without bucket history, use volume SMA multiplied by the number of chart bars per bucket as denominator.
Calculation: Structure vote = initialize prior swing high and low to opening-range boundaries; subsequently compare confirmed price pivots with their respective stored predecessors, assigning an upward vote for a higher pivot and downward otherwise. When high and low pivots confirm together, use their rounded mean vote. Use a shorter right-confirmation lookback early in the session and a longer one afterward. Reset daily.
Calculation: Activity filter = opening range fixed AND before the enabled late-entry cutoff AND the count of these conditions reaches a threshold: historical-bucket relative volume below its minimum; sustained closes inside developing value area, falling back to VWAP deviation bands; sustained neutral RMI; sustained unchanged neutral structure vote; developing value-area width below an ATR threshold after warmup. Use a higher activation threshold in expiry mode and a lower threshold before the prior-session profile is available.
Calculation: POC tolerance = ATR multiplied by a session-fixed coefficient selected from opening-range-completion ADR usage; use a smaller coefficient for greater ADR usage and a larger coefficient for lower usage.
Calculation: POC crossing state = upward after an in-session close above developing POC with sufficient body above it, following consecutive closes below their contemporaneous POCs; downward is symmetric. Require a fixed opening range, session-age warmup, and an inactive activity filter unless the afternoon bypass applies. Store POC at the crossing and reset that side's retest flag. Clear crossing state if POC migrates farther than a tolerance multiple from its stored crossing value; reset daily.
