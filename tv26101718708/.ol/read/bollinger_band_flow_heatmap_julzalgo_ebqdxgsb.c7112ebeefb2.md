Calculation: Buy strength = ((close − low) / max(true range, minimum tick)) × 0.6 + (lower wick / total candle range) × 0.25 + max((close − open) / max(true range, minimum tick), 0) × 0.35; sell strength = (1 − (close − low) / max(true range, minimum tick)) × 0.6 + (upper wick / total candle range) × 0.25 + max((open − close) / max(true range, minimum tick), 0) × 0.35; total candle range = absolute body + upper wick + lower wick, using minimum tick if zero.
Calculation: SmartFlow = weighted delta × (1 + min(abs(flow power), 5)^1.4) × agreement + delta volume × 0.6 + flow acceleration × 0.4 × acceleration weight. Buy and sell volumes are raw volume × their respective strengths, normalized so their sum equals raw volume; delta volume = buy volume − sell volume. Weighted delta = delta volume × (1 + clamp(volume Z-score, −4, 4) × 0.6), where volume Z-score uses raw volume's moving average and standard deviation. Flow acceleration = weighted delta − previous bar's weighted delta; flow power = weighted delta / (10-bar moving average of absolute weighted delta + 0.000001). Agreement = 1.2 when weighted delta and delta volume have the same sign, otherwise 0.7. Acceleration weight = 1.0 when flow acceleration and weighted delta have the same sign, otherwise 0.5.
Calculation: Lower and upper pressure = For each bar in the scan, compute its Bollinger channel as the moving average of HLC3 plus or minus its standard deviation times a multiplier, divide the channel into 30 equal layers, and assign that bar's SmartFlow to the layer containing HLC3. Add 18% of the assigned flow to each adjacent layer that exists. Sum the absolute accumulated layer values whose current layer midpoint is below the current close (lower pressure) or at/above it (upper pressure). The scan uses the current bar and preceding bars; each scanned bar uses its own channel.
Long: Lower pressure > upper pressure
Short: Upper pressure > lower pressure
