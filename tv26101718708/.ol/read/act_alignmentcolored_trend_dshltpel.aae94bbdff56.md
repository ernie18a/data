Calculation: Rung bars = use the current forming bar’s high, low, close, and open, and the previous completed bar’s high and low; State 1, State 2, Direction 1, and Direction 2 are successively higher timeframes than the chart timeframe, with Direction 2 higher than Direction 1.
Calculation: Strat bar type = inside if current high ≤ prior high and current low ≥ prior low; 2u/2d if only the prior high/low, respectively, is broken and the close holds beyond that extreme; 3u/3d if both extremes are broken and the close is above/below the prior range; failed 2u/2d if the respective single extreme is broken but the close returns within the prior range; failed 3 if both extremes are broken but the close returns within the prior range, classified up when close > open and down otherwise.
Long: chart timeframe ≤ State 1 timeframe AND ((close > both Direction 1 and Direction 2 opens AND NOT (Direction 1 is inside with neither prior extreme touched AND Direction 2 is inside) AND NOT ((Direction 1 is failed 2u or failed 3 up AND Direction 2 is 2u or 3u) OR (Direction 1 is 2u or 3u AND Direction 2 is failed 2u or failed 3 up))) OR (Direction 1 and Direction 2 closes are on opposite sides of their opens AND State 2 is 2u)).
Short: chart timeframe ≤ State 1 timeframe AND ((close < both Direction 1 and Direction 2 opens AND NOT (Direction 1 is inside with neither prior extreme touched AND Direction 2 is inside) AND NOT ((Direction 1 is failed 2d or failed 3 down AND Direction 2 is 2d or 3d) OR (Direction 1 is 2d or 3d AND Direction 2 is failed 2d or failed 3 down))) OR (Direction 1 and Direction 2 closes are on opposite sides of their opens AND State 2 is 2d)).
Exit: Long, previous bar met the Long condition AND current bar does not meet the Long condition.
Exit: Short, previous bar met the Short condition AND current bar does not meet the Short condition.
