Calculation: EMA bias = bullish when short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs are ordered from highest to lowest; bearish when ordered from lowest to highest; otherwise neutral. Calculate on daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute bars, using the still-forming bar of each higher timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = chart-timeframe close > highest high of prior swing-lookback bars. Bearish break of structure = chart-timeframe close < lowest low of prior swing-lookback bars.
Calculation: 1-hour sell-side liquidity sweep = still-forming 1-hour low < lowest low of prior swing-lookback 1-hour bars AND still-forming 1-hour close > that prior-low level. Buy-side liquidity sweep = still-forming 1-hour high > highest high of prior swing-lookback 1-hour bars AND still-forming 1-hour close < that prior-high level.
Calculation: Score = weighted sum of non-neutral EMA biases on daily, 4-hour, 1-hour, and 15-minute bars; presence of either chart-timeframe break of structure; presence of either chart-timeframe change of character (low < prior low AND close > prior high, or high > prior high AND close < prior low); and presence of either 1-hour liquidity sweep. Use the still-forming bar of each higher timeframe.
Long: daily EMA bias is bullish AND 4-hour EMA bias is bullish AND 1-hour EMA bias is bullish AND bullish break of structure AND 1-hour sell-side liquidity sweep AND score meets the minimum threshold.
Short: daily EMA bias is bearish AND 4-hour EMA bias is bearish AND 1-hour EMA bias is bearish AND bearish break of structure AND 1-hour buy-side liquidity sweep AND score meets the minimum threshold.
