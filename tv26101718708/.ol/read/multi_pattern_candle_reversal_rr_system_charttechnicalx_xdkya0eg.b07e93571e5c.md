Calculation: Fresh low = current or specified pattern-span low is below the lowest low in the preceding pivot-lookback bars; fresh high = corresponding high is above the highest high.
Calculation: Candle body = |close − open|; lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close).
Calculation: Strong close up = high − close ≤ (high − low) × fraction; strong close down = close − low ≤ (high − low) × fraction.
Calculation: Bullish engulf = prior candle red, current candle green, current open ≤ prior close, and current close ≥ prior open; bearish engulf is the reverse inequalities and colors.
Calculation: Compressed base = high − low across the specified bars immediately before the signal candle.
Calculation: Flag pole = largest-body candle in the specified search window before the immediately preceding flag-consolidation bars; pole direction is its candle direction. Flag range = high − low across those consolidation bars.
Calculation: Three-candle span low/high = minimum low / maximum high across the three candles.
Calculation: ADX rising = current ADX > ADX at the specified earlier lookback bar. ATR expansion = current ATR ≥ its simple moving average.
Calculation: Stop = for small-candle or engulfing entries, the two-candle low (long) or high (short); for compression breakout, the base low (long) or base high (short); for flag breakout, the flag low (long) or flag high (short); for three-candle patterns, the three-candle span low (long) or high (short). Target = entry price + risk distance × reward:risk ratio (long), or entry price − risk distance × reward:risk ratio (short), where risk distance = |entry price − stop|.
Long: (two consecutive green candles, both bodies ≤ ATR × small-body limit, first candle lower wick > 0 and ≥ its body × wick/body limit, and first candle makes a fresh low) OR (bullish engulf and its two-candle span makes a fresh low, optionally requiring current body ≥ prior body × size multiple, current volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple, and a strong close up) OR (prior-base range ≤ ATR × compression limit, current body ≥ ATR × breakout-body limit, current candle green, close > base high + ATR × buffer, optionally requiring current volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple, strong close up, and close above the wider lookback high) OR (flag pole is large enough, flag range ≤ ATR × flag-range limit, current body ≥ ATR × flag-breakout-body limit, current candle green, close > flag high, and, if reversal-only is required, pole is red) OR (three consecutive green candles, each body ≥ ATR × body limit, closes strictly rising, optionally requiring minimum close progress and strong closes on all three, opens within the prior candle bodies, and the three-candle span makes a fresh low); AND (ADX ≥ threshold OR, if enabled, ADX rising OR, if enabled, current candle range ≥ ATR × volatility-breakout limit, unless the ADX filter is disabled); AND (if enabled, ATR ≥ its moving average); AND (if enabled, bars since the last accepted signal ≥ cooldown); AND stop < entry price. Entry is at the signal bar close, on a confirmed bar. Apply the optional engulf and compression filters only when enabled.
Short: (two consecutive red candles, both bodies ≤ ATR × small-body limit, first candle upper wick > 0 and ≥ its body × wick/body limit, and first candle makes a fresh high) OR (bearish engulf and its two-candle span makes a fresh high, optionally requiring current body ≥ prior body × size multiple, current volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple, and a strong close down) OR (prior-base range ≤ ATR × compression limit, current body ≥ ATR × breakout-body limit, current candle red, close < base low − ATR × buffer, optionally requiring current volume ≥ its moving average × volume multiple, strong close down, and close below the wider lookback low) OR (flag pole is large enough, flag range ≤ ATR × flag-range limit, current body ≥ ATR × flag-breakout-body limit, current candle red, close < flag low, and, if reversal-only is required, pole is green) OR (three consecutive red candles, each body ≥ ATR × body limit, closes strictly falling, optionally requiring minimum close progress and strong closes on all three, opens within the prior candle bodies, and the three-candle span makes a fresh high); AND (ADX ≥ threshold OR, if enabled, ADX rising OR, if enabled, current candle range ≥ ATR × volatility-breakout limit, unless the ADX filter is disabled); AND (if enabled, ATR ≥ its moving average); AND (if enabled, bars since the last accepted signal ≥ cooldown); AND stop > entry price. Entry is at the signal bar close, on a confirmed bar. Apply the optional engulf and compression filters only when enabled.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target (if both occur on one bar, stop takes precedence).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target (if both occur on one bar, stop takes precedence).
