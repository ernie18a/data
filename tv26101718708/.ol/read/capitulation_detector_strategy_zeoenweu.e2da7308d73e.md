Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close; fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over its configured lookback.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume; body average = simple moving average of absolute close minus open.
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume >= volume average × configured multiplier.
Calculation: Body expansion = absolute close minus open >= body average × configured multiplier.
Calculation: Long trend persistence = bars since close was above the slow EMA >= configured minimum; short trend persistence = bars since close was below the slow EMA >= configured minimum.
Calculation: Long acceleration = bars since close was above the fast EMA >= configured minimum AND long trend persistence; short acceleration = bars since close was below the fast EMA >= configured minimum AND short trend persistence.
Calculation: Long extension = close < slow EMA AND (slow EMA - close) / ATR >= configured multiplier; short extension = close > slow EMA AND (close - slow EMA) / ATR >= configured multiplier.
Calculation: A lower-low swing occurs when low < the prior bar's low and the low two bars ago, and the prior bar's low < the low two and three bars ago. Count such swings when each swing low is below the last counted swing low; otherwise reset the count to one. Reset the count when close crosses above the slow EMA. Long multi-leg condition = count >= configured minimum.
Calculation: A higher-high swing occurs when high > the prior bar's high and the high two bars ago, and the prior bar's high > the high two and three bars ago. Count such swings when each swing high is above the last counted swing high; otherwise reset the count to one. Reset the count when close crosses below the slow EMA. Short multi-leg condition = count >= configured minimum.
Calculation: Long factor score = number of true conditions among long trend persistence, long acceleration, volume spike, body expansion, long extension, and long multi-leg condition. Short factor score = number of true conditions among short trend persistence, short acceleration, volume spike, body expansion, short extension, and short multi-leg condition.
Long: flat position AND long factor score >= configured minimum score AND close < open AND (volume spike required is disabled OR volume spike) AND close < slow EMA AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured window)
Short: flat position AND short factor score >= configured minimum score AND close > open AND (volume spike required is disabled OR volume spike) AND close > slow EMA AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured window)
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close - ATR at entry × configured stop multiplier (if stop loss enabled) OR high >= signal-bar close + ATR at entry × configured target multiplier (if take profit enabled) OR trailing stop is enabled and the trailing stop is reached.
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + ATR at entry × configured stop multiplier (if stop loss enabled) OR low <= signal-bar close - ATR at entry × configured target multiplier (if take profit enabled) OR trailing stop is enabled and the trailing stop is reached.
