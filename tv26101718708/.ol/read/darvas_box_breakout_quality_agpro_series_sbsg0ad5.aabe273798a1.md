Calculation: Active Darvas box = Start a candidate when the current high exceeds the highest high of the preceding seed-lookback bars; set its top and bottom to that bar’s high and low. While forming, a higher high resets the candidate top, bottom, and age to the current bar; otherwise keep the top and lower the bottom to the lowest low so far. Activate it once its age is at least the minimum age and its height divided by ATR is between the minimum and maximum height limits. Discard candidates older than the maximum age. Keep the active boundaries fixed until a breakout or age expiration.
Calculation: Compression score = 70% × tightness score + 30% × age score. Tightness score is 100 − 100 × (box height / ATR − minimum box height in ATR) / (maximum − minimum box height in ATR), clamped to 0–100. Age score rises linearly to 100 at three times the minimum box age (or minimum age + 1, whichever is greater), then declines as age increases, clamped to 30–100.
Calculation: Breakout quality score = 30% × compression score + 25% × volume score + 20% × breakout-distance score + 15% × directional close-location score + 10% × candle-body score. Volume score is relative volume divided by its support threshold × 100, clamped to 0–100; if volume data or its moving average is unavailable, use 50. Breakout-distance score is distance beyond the broken boundary in ATR divided by the target distance in ATR × 100, clamped to 0–100. Candle-body score is absolute close − open divided by high − low × 100, clamped to 0–100. Directional close-location score is (close − low) / (high − low) × 100 for an upward break, or (high − close) / (high − low) × 100 for a downward break, clamped to 0–100. Use at least the minimum tick as the range and ATR denominator.
Long: Active Darvas box AND (close > box top when close confirmation is required, otherwise high > box top) AND breakout quality score ≥ minimum score AND (volume support is not required OR volume ≥ volume moving average × support threshold).
Short: Active Darvas box AND (close < box bottom when close confirmation is required, otherwise low < box bottom) AND breakout quality score ≥ minimum score AND (volume support is not required OR volume ≥ volume moving average × support threshold).
Exit: Long, within the failure window after an upward box break and close < broken box top.
Exit: Short, within the failure window after a downward box break and close > broken box bottom.
