Calculation: Long stop level = low of the entry-signal bar × (1 − that trade’s stop-loss percentage)
Calculation: Long target level = high of the entry-signal bar × (1 + that trade’s take-profit percentage)
Calculation: Short stop level = high of the entry-signal bar × (1 + that trade’s stop-loss percentage)
Calculation: Short target level = low of the entry-signal bar × (1 − that trade’s take-profit percentage)
Long: close > open AND close > max(close two bars ago, open two bars ago) AND low one bar ago < low two bars ago AND high one bar ago < high two bars ago
Short: close < open AND close < min(close two bars ago, open two bars ago) AND low one bar ago > low two bars ago AND high one bar ago > high two bars ago
Exit: long, low < that trade’s long stop level OR high > that trade’s long target level
Exit: short, high > that trade’s short stop level OR low < that trade’s short target level
