Calculation: ORMA bands = Apply the selected moving average to sqrt(close); transform it as baseMA × (baseMA × coefficient + previous baseMA × (1 − coefficient)), where coefficient = 2 / (1 + MA lookback); blend this value with its previous value using 1 − (ATR(full lookback) / ATR(half lookback)) and that ratio, respectively. Upper/lower bands = blended value ± ATR(full lookback) × band factor. Trend state becomes bullish when close > upper band, bearish when close < lower band, and otherwise retains its previous state.
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger Band width < its longer-lookback average × compression threshold; Bollinger Band width = 2 × multiplier × standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Displacement = EMA of (0.7 × mean of normalized Bollinger %B, CCI, and ROC + 0.3 × signed candle body fraction × volume intensity). Normalize %B to [-1,1], CCI by dividing by 200 and clamping to [-1,1], and ROC by twice its longer-lookback standard deviation and clamping to [-1,1]. Volume intensity = clamp(volume / 20-bar average volume − 1, -1, 1). Strong bullish displacement = displacement > 1.5 × its 100-bar standard deviation; strong bearish displacement = displacement < −1.5 × its 100-bar standard deviation.
Calculation: Confluence score = map the weighted average of current-timeframe and higher-timeframe scores from [-1,1] to [0,100], clamped to that range; the higher-timeframe score has more weight. Current-timeframe score = mean of: +1 if close > EMA, otherwise −1; normalized oscillator composite; normalized Bollinger Band width deviation from its 50-bar average; structure score; and signed volume conviction. Structure score is +1 when the persistent structure state is bullish, otherwise −1; set that state bullish if the low from the swing lookback bars ago is below the lowest low over the more recent swing lookback bars, bearish if the high from the swing lookback bars ago is above the highest high over those more recent bars, otherwise retain its prior state. Signed volume conviction = candle direction × min(1, volume intensity). Higher-timeframe score = mean of: +1 if higher-timeframe close > its EMA, otherwise −1; and +1 if its %B > 0.5, −1 if its %B < 0, otherwise 0. Use the higher timeframe's still-forming bar.
Calculation: Recent bullish gap = a prior bar's low > the high two bars before it, with the intervening close > that earlier high; recent bearish gap = a prior bar's high < the low two bars before it, with the intervening close < that earlier low. Search a configurable recent-bar lookback, excluding the current bar.
Long: trend state is bullish AND NOT compressed AND confluence score >= long threshold AND strong bullish displacement AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour is within a configured active session) AND (gap filter disabled OR recent bullish gap) AND current setup was false on the previous bar AND position is flat.
Short: trend state is bearish AND NOT compressed AND confluence score <= short threshold AND strong bearish displacement AND (session filter disabled OR UTC hour is within a configured active session) AND (gap filter disabled OR recent bearish gap) AND current setup was false on the previous bar AND position is flat.
Exit: Long, low <= current stop OR high >= take-profit OR trend state is bearish OR compressed OR strong bearish displacement.
Exit: Short, high >= current stop OR low <= take-profit OR trend state is bullish OR compressed OR strong bullish displacement.
