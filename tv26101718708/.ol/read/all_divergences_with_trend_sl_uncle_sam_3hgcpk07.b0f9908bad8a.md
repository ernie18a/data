Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = for any enabled indicator, the indicator at the previous bar is higher than at a confirmed earlier close pivot low and the previous close is below that pivot low; the pivot is more than 5 bars and at most 100 bars back, among the 10 most recent pivots, and neither the indicator nor close falls below the straight line joining the compared points at intervening bars. Require the current indicator to be higher than its previous value OR the current close to be higher than its previous close.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = for any enabled indicator, the indicator at the previous bar is lower than at a confirmed earlier close pivot high and the previous close is above that pivot high; the pivot is more than 5 bars and at most 100 bars back, among the 10 most recent pivots, and neither the indicator nor close rises above the straight line joining the compared points at intervening bars. Require the current indicator to be lower than its previous value OR the current close to be lower than its previous close.
Long: Regular bullish divergence AND close > selected moving average
Short: Regular bearish divergence AND close < selected moving average
Exit: Long, low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss fraction) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit fraction) OR each of the configured consecutive closes through the current bar < current selected moving average
Exit: Short, high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit fraction) OR each of the configured consecutive closes through the current bar > current selected moving average
