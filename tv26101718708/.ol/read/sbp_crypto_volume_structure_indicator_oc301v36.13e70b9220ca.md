Calculation: True range average = average true range; true range uses the current high and low and the previous close.
Calculation: Volume center = volume-weighted average of typical price over a rolling window, falling back to an exponential moving average of typical price when unavailable; typical price = (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Volatility deviation = greater of the rolling standard deviation of typical price and a volatility floor proportional to the true range average.
Calculation: Directional participation = the exponentially smoothed signed volume divided by exponentially smoothed effective volume, clamped to [-1, 1]; signed volume = effective volume × (2 × close location − 1); close location = (close − low) / (high − low), clamped to [0, 1]. Effective volume is volume when positive, otherwise 1.
Calculation: Direction = 0.45 × directional participation + 0.30 × normalized volume-center slope + 0.25 × normalized close displacement from the volume center; slope and displacement are normalized by the true range average and clamped to [-1, 1].
Calculation: Control center = volume center + volatility deviation × directional participation × a configurable fraction.
Calculation: Control boundaries = control center ± the greater of a configurable fraction of volatility deviation and a configurable volatility floor.
Calculation: Value boundaries = volume center ± the greater of a configurable fraction of volatility deviation and a configurable volatility floor.
Calculation: Session control boundaries = control boundaries captured on the first confirmed chart bar of each day and held until the next day.
Calculation: Outer boundaries = volume center ± volatility deviation multiplied by a configurable scale.
Calculation: Bullish acceptance = close above the upper value boundary and upper session control boundary, direction above a positive threshold, candle body/range above a threshold, and close location above a threshold.
Calculation: Bearish acceptance = close below the lower value boundary and lower session control boundary, direction below a negative threshold, candle body/range above a threshold, and close location below a threshold.
Calculation: Bullish reclaim = low at or below the lower session control boundary, close above the upper session control boundary, direction above a positive threshold, lower wick longer than upper wick, and candle body/range above a threshold.
Calculation: Bearish reclaim = high at or above the upper session control boundary, close below the lower session control boundary, direction below a negative threshold, upper wick longer than lower wick, and candle body/range above a threshold.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the lowest low of the preceding short lookback and close back above that low. Bearish sweep = high above the highest high of the preceding short lookback and close back below that high.
Calculation: Bullish break = close above the highest high of the preceding shorter lookback and above open. Bearish break = close below the lowest low of the preceding shorter lookback and below open.
Calculation: Participation pass = current effective volume divided by its short-term average meets a configurable threshold.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity setup = bullish sweep and bullish break and participation pass and direction above a positive threshold and close above the session control center. Bearish liquidity setup = bearish sweep and bearish break and participation pass and direction below a negative threshold and close below the session control center.
Calculation: Buy rejection = high reaches or exceeds the upper outer boundary and close finishes below it. Sale rejection = low reaches or falls below the lower outer boundary and close finishes above it.
Calculation: Fast ribbon = double-smoothed typical price with a shorter lookback than the slow ribbon. Slow ribbon = double-smoothed typical price with a longer lookback than the fast ribbon.
Calculation: Momentum = short-term exponential moving average of close minus a longer-term exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: Bullish ribbon = direction meets a positive threshold, the short-smoothed typical-price slope is not materially negative, and close is at or above the volume center, on each of two consecutive confirmed bars. Bearish ribbon = direction meets a negative threshold, that slope is not materially positive, and close is at or below the volume center, on each of two consecutive confirmed bars.
Calculation: Bullish ribbon transition = bullish ribbon is true now but was false on the preceding bar. Bearish ribbon transition = bearish ribbon is true now but was false on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Trend line = a short exponential moving average of an exponentially smoothed, weighted average of current and preceding closes, with higher weights on more recent closes.
Calculation: Trend state = initially bullish; changes to bearish after bearish trend evidence on two consecutive confirmed bars, and to bullish after bullish trend evidence on two consecutive confirmed bars. Bullish evidence is close above trend line by a volatility-adjusted margin with positive normalized trend-line slope; bearish evidence is the inverse. Otherwise the state persists.
Calculation: Bullish vote count = number of true conditions among close above open, close above prior close, momentum rising, fast ribbon above slow ribbon, bullish ribbon, bullish trend state, lower wick longer than body, and positive direction.
Calculation: Bearish vote count = number of true conditions among close below open, close below prior close, momentum falling, fast ribbon below slow ribbon, bearish ribbon, bearish trend state, upper wick longer than body, and negative direction.
Calculation: Structure buy = (bullish acceptance or bullish reclaim or bullish liquidity setup) and not buy rejection and bullish vote count meets a threshold. Structure sale = (bearish acceptance or bearish reclaim or bearish liquidity setup) and not sale rejection and bearish vote count meets a threshold.
Calculation: Turning buy = the high-low range of the recent lookback relative to the true range average meets a threshold, the lowest low occurred within a recent subwindow but not on the current bar, close has risen from that low by a volatility-adjusted threshold, bullish vote count meets a threshold, a bullish break or sweep occurred, and the value boundaries are not compressed. It must hold for two consecutive confirmed bars, without turning sale also being true.
Calculation: Turning sale = the high-low range of the recent lookback relative to the true range average meets a threshold, the highest high occurred within a recent subwindow but not on the current bar, close has fallen from that high by a volatility-adjusted threshold, bearish vote count meets a threshold, a bearish break or sweep occurred, and the value boundaries are not compressed. It must hold for two consecutive confirmed bars, without turning buy also being true.
Calculation: Ribbon buy = bullish ribbon transition and bullish trend state and direction above a positive threshold and close above volume center. Ribbon sale = bearish ribbon transition and bearish trend state and direction below a negative threshold and close below volume center.
Calculation: Trend buy = trend state changed from bearish to bullish and bullish ribbon is true and direction above a positive threshold. Trend sale = trend state changed from bullish to bearish and bearish ribbon is true and direction below a negative threshold.
Calculation: Integrated buy = close above open, trend line, and volume center; fast ribbon above slow ribbon; momentum rising; bullish vote count meets a threshold; and value boundaries are not compressed. Integrated sale = close below open, trend line, and volume center; fast ribbon below slow ribbon; momentum falling; bearish vote count meets a threshold; and value boundaries are not compressed.
Calculation: Buy request = confirmed bar, close above open, and at least one of structure buy, turning buy, ribbon buy, trend buy, or integrated buy. Sale request = confirmed bar, close below open, and at least one of structure sale, turning sale, ribbon sale, trend sale, or integrated sale. If both requests occur, keep only the request with the higher vote count; discard both on a tie.
Long: buy request and position is not long.
Short: sale request and position is not short.
Exit: Long, sale request.
Exit: Short, buy request.
