Calculation: Valid descending trendline = the longest-span line through two confirmed pivot highs, optionally using the current rolling highest high as the right anchor; require a lower right anchor, minimum span and anchor drop, permitted pierces, and a permitted number of pivot-high touches within the line’s tolerance.
Calculation: Quality score = touch-count score + touch-spacing score (based on spacing variability relative to line span) + recency score (based on age of latest touch) + higher-low score (based on latest confirmed pivot low versus the preceding one).
Calculation: Confirmation count = one bar when the quality score reaches the automatic high-quality threshold; otherwise two bars, or the configured manual count when automatic confirmation is off.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial stop; after price reaches target 1, move it to entry price; after price reaches target 2, move it to target 1.
Long: A valid line has not fired, its right anchor is within the permitted age, close is above the latest confirmed pivot low within the allowed tolerance, quality score meets its minimum, volume is at least its moving average times the surge multiplier, and the current and required preceding bars each close above the line by the break buffer and close above their opens; and these conditions become true on this bar after being false on the prior bar.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, high reaches target 3 OR low reaches the trailing stop. Define initial stop and targets from entry price using either configured ATR multiples or fixed percentages.
