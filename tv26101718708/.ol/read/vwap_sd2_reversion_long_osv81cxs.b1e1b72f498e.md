Calculation: Lower 2-SD band = daily-reset session VWAP minus the configured multiple of volume-weighted standard deviation.
Calculation: StochRSI %K = smoothed stochastic of RSI over its lookback; %D = smoothed %K.
Long: Flat, and (if enabled) the last completed higher-timeframe ADX is above or below its threshold as configured, and the lower-band-to-VWAP distance meets its minimum, and a close below the lower 2-SD band is followed within the confirmation window by an RSI cross above its level (or, if selected, StochRSI %K crossing above its threshold or %D) and a MACD cross above its signal line (or, if selected, above zero); both crosses must be at or after the first qualifying dip, may occur in either order or on the same bar, and MACD must be bullish when confirmed. In strict mode, require fresh crosses after the preceding setup step.
Exit: long, high >= target OR low <= stop; target is the greater of session VWAP minus its buffer and entry plus the minimum-profit floor (or, in fixed-target mode, entry plus the fixed pip target; in ATR mode, also require entry plus the configured ATR target). Stop is entry minus the configured fixed-pip or ATR distance until high reaches the breakeven trigger, then the greater of that stop and entry plus the breakeven lock.
