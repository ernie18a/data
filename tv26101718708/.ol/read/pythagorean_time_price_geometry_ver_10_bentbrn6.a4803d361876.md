Calculation: Anchor swing = a selected confirmed pivot high and pivot low, with swing duration equal to the absolute bar-index difference and range equal to high minus low; auto-detection uses pivot lookback, expected duration, minimum ATR-scaled range, swing-quality and stability filters
Calculation: Pythagorean time node = for each multiplier 1 and 2, let D be anchor swing duration; period = round(√2 × D × multiplier), half-square offset = round(√1.25 × D × multiplier), and half-hypotenuse offset = round(√2 × D × multiplier ÷ 2); current bar is at a node when its bar-index distance from the earlier anchor bar modulo period equals the half-square offset, half-hypotenuse offset, period, or D × multiplier modulo period
Calculation: Grid level = with R as anchor swing range and B/A as the selected Pythagorean triple ratio, cell height = (B/A) × R × square scale; grid origin is −cell height for options, otherwise max(min(anchor low, lowest low over 500 chart bars) − 2 × cell height, 0); the levels are cell boundaries and cell midpoints
Long: close > open AND abs(close − open) ≥ 0.3 × ATR AND (at a Pythagorean time node OR (within 15% of a grid cell height from a grid boundary or midpoint AND not at a Pythagorean time node AND within 15% of a grid cell height from the lower boundary))
