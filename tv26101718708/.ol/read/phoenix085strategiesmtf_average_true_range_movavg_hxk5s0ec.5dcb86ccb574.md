Calculation: ATR = selected moving average of the higher-timeframe close from its last completed bar, smoothed on the chart timeframe
Long: datetime is within the configured backtest date range AND ATR > prior ATR AND prior ATR < ATR two bars ago AND ATR two bars ago < ATR three bars ago AND the shorter SMA of lower-timeframe closes crosses above the longer SMA AND the longer SMA < shorter SMA AND shorter SMA < the prior lower-timeframe close (lower-timeframe close uses the latest available bar, which can still be forming)
Short: datetime is within the configured backtest date range AND ATR > prior ATR AND prior ATR < ATR two bars ago AND ATR two bars ago < ATR three bars ago AND the shorter SMA of lower-timeframe closes crosses below the longer SMA AND the longer SMA > shorter SMA (lower-timeframe close uses the latest available bar, which can still be forming)
Exit: long, the short-entry condition occurs OR low <= entry price minus the configured stop-loss distance OR the trailing stop is active and low <= its trailing stop price
Exit: short, the long-entry condition occurs OR high >= entry price plus the configured stop-loss distance OR the trailing stop is active and high >= its trailing stop price
