Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than the highs of the same number of bars before and after it; confirmed only after those later bars close
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar low lower than the lows of the same number of bars before and after it; confirmed only after those later bars close
Long: low < an unswept confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low
Short: high > an unswept confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high
