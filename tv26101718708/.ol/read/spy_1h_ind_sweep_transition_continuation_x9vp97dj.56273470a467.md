Calculation: Daily ATR = average true range from higher-timeframe daily bars, using the last completed bar.
Calculation: Daily fast EMA and daily slow EMA = exponential moving averages of daily closes, using the last completed daily bar; the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Daily liquidity levels = highest high and lowest low over a longer daily lookback and a shorter daily lookback, each excluding the current, still-forming daily bar.
Calculation: Expected-move bands = current daily open ± (current daily open × standard deviation of daily log close-to-close returns over a daily lookback, using the last completed daily bar × square root of the configured horizon × configured multiplier).
Calculation: Daily volatility expansion = the standard deviation of daily log close-to-close returns over a longer lookback, from the last completed daily bar, is greater than its average over a shorter daily lookback.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average price of the current chart-timeframe session bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = current chart-bar volume divided by the EMA of volume for the same intraday time slot across prior sessions; if that average is unavailable, use volume divided by its chart-bar simple moving average.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ minimum body size relative to daily ATR AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body-to-range ratio AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ minimum close-location ratio.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND absolute(close − open) ≥ minimum body size relative to daily ATR AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) ≥ minimum body-to-range ratio AND (high − close) / (high − low) ≥ minimum close-location ratio.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = most recent pivot high/low confirmed using the configured left and right chart-bar lookbacks; internal high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured chart-bar lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the confirmed swing high; bearish structure break = close crosses below the confirmed swing low. Bullish/bearish internal structure break = close crosses above/below the internal high/low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trend alignment = daily fast EMA >/< daily slow EMA AND daily slow EMA is rising/falling versus the prior completed daily bar.
Calculation: Strong relative volume = relative volume ≥ configured threshold. Bullish/bearish reclaim = close >/< current daily open OR, on an intraday chart, close >/< session VWAP.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = low/high crosses below/above and closes back above/below any corresponding daily liquidity level or expected-move band. Major-level sweeps receive a higher base score than tactical-level or expected-move sweeps; each additional swept level adds score.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish reversal score = sweep score + trend-alignment score + structure-break score (a break forming a higher-high/lower-low swing sequence scores more than another swing break; an internal break scores less) + one score each for reclaim, strong relative volume, same-direction displacement, daily volatility expansion, and the applicable volatility-index Bollinger condition (upper-band spike for bullish; below-basis compression for bearish). The volatility-index Bollinger calculation uses same-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish reversal setup = sweep is armed; at least one and no more than the configured confirmation-window bars have elapsed since the sweep; reversal score meets the configured minimum, increased by the configured countertrend extra when trend alignment fails; countertrend sweep eligibility is satisfied; and, if swing-break requirement is enabled, the corresponding configured structure condition is met.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish continuation setup = close crosses above/below the prior configured chart-bar highest high/lowest low; continuation regime holds (daily fast EMA rising/falling and close above/below it); corresponding swing or internal structure break holds; extension from daily fast EMA is within the configured daily-ATR ceiling; and continuation score meets its configured minimum. Score adds regime, swing-sequence, internal break, swing break (more for a higher-high/lower-low sequence), displacement, strong relative volume, daily volatility expansion, and the bullish volatility-index compression condition.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish transition setup = a close-cross reclaim above/below the daily fast EMA occurred within the configured transition window; transition regime holds (close above/below daily fast EMA, its daily-ATR-normalized slope is not too negative/positive, and opposing daily trend alignment does not hold); a corresponding swing or internal structure break occurred within the configured structure-memory window; extension is within the configured daily-ATR ceiling; and transition score meets its configured minimum. Score adds regime, recent structure, displacement, strong relative volume, and daily volatility expansion.
Calculation: Reversal stop = swept-bar low/high buffered by configured daily ATR. Continuation stop = the tighter of the corresponding prior breakout boundary buffered by configured daily ATR and the latest opposite confirmed swing level, when available. Transition stop = the tighter of the daily fast EMA, prior transition breakout boundary buffered by configured daily ATR, and latest opposite confirmed swing level, when available.
Calculation: Valid entry risk = entry close minus long stop, or short stop minus entry close; it must be positive and no greater than the configured maximum daily-ATR risk.
Calculation: Active long trail = the higher of highest high since entry minus configured daily-ATR trail distance and, when enabled and available, locked long structure minus its configured daily-ATR buffer. Active short trail = the lower of lowest low since entry plus configured daily-ATR trail distance and, when enabled and available, locked short structure plus its configured daily-ATR buffer.
Long: bullish sweep AND no bullish sweep already armed (intrabar armed-call marking); OR flat and past cooldown AND daily ATR available AND confirmed chart bar AND valid risk AND (bullish reversal setup with valid reversal risk OR bullish transition setup qualified and within its execution window with valid transition risk OR bullish continuation setup with valid continuation risk).
Short: bearish sweep AND no bearish sweep already armed (intrabar armed-put marking); OR flat and past cooldown AND daily ATR available AND confirmed chart bar AND NOT long entry AND valid risk AND (bearish reversal setup with valid reversal risk OR bearish transition setup qualified and within its execution window with valid transition risk OR bearish continuation setup with valid continuation risk). Reversal has priority over transition, which has priority over continuation; a long entry has priority over a short entry.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ initial stop OR (confirmed chart bar AND an opposing swing break AND close < locked long structure − configured daily-ATR buffer) OR (minimum holding period elapsed AND trailing enabled AND runner activated at the configured gain in initial-risk multiples AND low ≤ active long trail) OR (minimum holding period elapsed AND take-profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + configured initial-risk multiple).
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ initial stop OR (confirmed chart bar AND an opposing swing break AND close > locked short structure + configured daily-ATR buffer) OR (minimum holding period elapsed AND trailing enabled AND runner activated at the configured gain in initial-risk multiples AND high ≥ active short trail) OR (minimum holding period elapsed AND take-profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − configured initial-risk multiple).
