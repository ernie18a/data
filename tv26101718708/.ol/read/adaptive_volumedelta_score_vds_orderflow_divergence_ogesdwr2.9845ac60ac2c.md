Calculation: Adaptive volume score = map the percentile rank of volume to −4.5…+4.5 as 0.09 × percentile rank − 4.5. When the chart timeframe is lower than the main timeframe, rank the rolling sum of chart-bar volume over a main-timeframe-length window; otherwise rank chart-bar volume. In the rolling case, include the bar still forming.
Calculation: Adaptive delta score = sign of summed wick-weighted delta × 0.045 × percentile rank of the absolute summed delta, where each bar’s wick-weighted delta is (close − open) ÷ max(high − low, minimum price increment) × volume. When the chart timeframe is lower than the main timeframe, sum chart-bar deltas over a main-timeframe-length rolling window; otherwise sum lower-timeframe deltas within the chart bar. The lower timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe; use its available bars within the current chart bar. In the rolling case, include the bar still forming.
Long: Adaptive volume score > high-volume threshold AND (adaptive delta score > dominance threshold OR (adaptive delta score > 0 AND price-window open ≥ price-window close))
Short: Adaptive volume score > high-volume threshold AND (adaptive delta score < −dominance threshold OR (adaptive delta score < 0 AND price-window open < price-window close))
