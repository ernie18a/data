Calculation: Composite score = clamp to [-8, 8] the sum of: +1 when fast EMA > slow EMA (−1 when <); +1 when slow EMA > anchor EMA (−1 when <); the same two comparisons for fast, slow, and anchor EMAs of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; +1 when close > session VWAP (−1 when <); +1 when the fast EMA’s change over its lookback divided by ATR exceeds the positive slope threshold (−1 when below the negative threshold); the persistent structure state (+1 after a confirmed close crossing above a confirmed pivot high, −1 after a confirmed close crossing below a confirmed pivot low, otherwise retain the prior state; ignore breaks older than the configured memory); +1 when +DI > −DI and ADX meets its floor (−1 for the inverse); +1 for a bullish impulse bar or −1 for a bearish impulse bar (directional close, body larger than its average, and relative volume meeting its threshold); +1 when close > current day’s opening-range high (−1 when close < its low). Ties or unmet thresholds contribute 0. Use shorter, longer, and anchor EMA lengths in that order; compute higher-timeframe EMAs from the prior completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands lie inside the Keltner Channel. Expansion = Bollinger Band width exceeds its longer-term mean by a multiplier and is greater than on the prior bar.
Long: Current chart bar is confirmed AND ((composite score >= 3 AND NOT squeeze) OR (expansion AND +DI > −DI AND ADX meets its floor AND composite score >= 4)) AND, for the first branch, fast higher-timeframe EMA > slow higher-timeframe EMA > anchor higher-timeframe EMA AND close > session VWAP.
Short: Current chart bar is confirmed AND ((composite score <= −3 AND NOT squeeze) OR (expansion AND −DI > +DI AND ADX meets its floor AND composite score <= −4)) AND, for the first branch, fast higher-timeframe EMA < slow higher-timeframe EMA < anchor higher-timeframe EMA AND close < session VWAP.
