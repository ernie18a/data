Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in source over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar source changes over that lookback; use 0 if the sum is 0. Smooth it with a 3-bar EMA.
Calculation: Adaptive regime filter and raw trend = use ATR times an efficiency-adjusted range multiplier as the buffer around the prior filter. Set the filter to source minus buffer when source exceeds prior filter plus buffer; set it to source plus buffer when source is below prior filter minus buffer; otherwise retain prior filter. Raw trend is +1 if the filter rises, -1 if it falls, otherwise retain prior trend.
Calculation: Locked trend = update to raw trend only after raw trend differs from locked trend for the adaptive confirmation count of consecutive bars and the current bar is confirmed. Adaptive confirmation count is base count + 2 (minimum 4) when efficiency < 0.22; base + 1 when < 0.42; base when < 0.68; otherwise base - 1 (minimum 1). Adjust the range multiplier by 1.15 when efficiency < 0.25, by 0.85 when efficiency > 0.70, otherwise leave it unchanged.
Calculation: Regime bands = adaptive regime filter plus or minus ATR times the adjusted range multiplier at fractions 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, and 1.00; the second upper/lower bands use fraction 0.50.
Calculation: Gate = ADX is at least its floor AND choppiness is at most its ceiling AND momentum agrees with locked trend AND higher-timeframe bias agrees with locked trend. ADX and choppiness conditions are omitted when their respective filters are disabled; momentum and higher-timeframe conditions are omitted when their respective filters are disabled.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over its lookback / (highest high minus lowest low over its lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 50 if the high-low range is 0.
Calculation: Momentum = EMA of (close minus close at the momentum lookback bars ago), with EMA length equal to the greater of 3 and the floored momentum lookback divided by 3.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = +1 when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above the EMA of that higher-timeframe close; otherwise -1. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar for both values.
Calculation: EMA alignment = +1 when the shorter-lookback chart-timeframe close EMA is above the longer-lookback chart-timeframe close EMA; otherwise -1.
Long: raw trend differs from locked trend and is +1 for at least the adaptive confirmation count of consecutive bars, and the current bar is confirmed
Long: locked trend = +1 AND gate is open AND low <= adaptive regime filter AND close > adaptive regime filter AND EMA alignment = +1 AND current bar is confirmed
Long: gate is open AND locked trend = +1 AND close > second upper regime band AND prior close <= prior second upper regime band
Short: raw trend differs from locked trend and is -1 for at least the adaptive confirmation count of consecutive bars, and the current bar is confirmed
Short: locked trend = -1 AND gate is open AND high >= adaptive regime filter AND close < adaptive regime filter AND EMA alignment = -1 AND current bar is confirmed
Short: gate is open AND locked trend = -1 AND close < second lower regime band AND prior close >= prior second lower regime band
