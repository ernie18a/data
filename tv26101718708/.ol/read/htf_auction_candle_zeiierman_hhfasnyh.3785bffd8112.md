Calculation: Estimated buy share = 0.5 for a zero-range lower-timeframe candle; otherwise clamp 0.5 + 0.43 × [0.48 × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1) + 0.32 × (close − open)/(high − low) + 0.20 × (lower wick − upper wick)/(high − low)] to [0.025, 0.975]. Estimated buy volume = volume × estimated buy share; estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume. Use lower-timeframe candles within the currently forming higher-timeframe candle; the lower timeframe is at or below the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Battle score for each of 20 equal price segments of the currently forming higher-timeframe candle = 0.70 × (segment estimated buy volume − segment estimated sell volume)/segment total estimated volume + 0.30 × (buy directional win evidence − sell directional win evidence)/total directional win evidence. Allocate estimated buy/sell volume across touched price cells, weighted toward separate buy and sell activity centers, then aggregate cell volumes by segment overlap. Distribute each lower-timeframe candle’s directional win evidence across segments in proportion to its range overlap; for a zero-range candle, assign it to the segment containing its close. Win evidence is the absolute difference between twice its estimated buy share and one. The higher-timeframe candle uses chart-bar open, running high and low, and current close.
Long: battle score > 0 and segment total estimated volume > 0 and absolute battle strength > 0.0001.
Short: battle score < 0 and segment total estimated volume > 0 and absolute battle strength > 0.0001.
