Calculation: RSI = RSI of high on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Composite RSI = EMA of the weighted average of RSI on three higher timeframes, each using the same RSI source and lookback as chart-timeframe RSI; use each higher timeframe’s last completed bar
Calculation: RSI slope = current chart-timeframe RSI − prior chart-timeframe RSI
Calculation: Confirmed tint state = switch to bullish after the configured number of consecutive bars with RSI above Composite RSI by at least the deadband and RSI slope at least the bullish slope threshold; switch to bearish after the configured number of consecutive bars with RSI below Composite RSI by at least the deadband and RSI slope at most the negative bearish slope threshold; otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: RSI pivot low/high = a chart-timeframe RSI low/high with the configured number of bars on each side; confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Price pivot low/high = the chart-timeframe low/high at the bar of the corresponding RSI pivot
Calculation: Pivot separation = bars since the prior pivot of the same type
Long: Confirmed tint state is bullish OR confirmed tint state changes from bearish to bullish OR an RSI pivot low is higher than its previous RSI pivot low AND its price pivot low is lower than its previous price pivot low OR an RSI pivot low is lower than its previous RSI pivot low AND its price pivot low is higher than its previous price pivot low AND pivot separation is within the configured minimum and maximum OR a chart-timeframe RSI pivot high is higher than the previous RSI pivot high and the highest RSI pivot high so far in the current bullish tint season OR a chart-timeframe RSI pivot low is at least as high as the previous RSI pivot low during a bullish tint season
Short: Confirmed tint state is bearish OR confirmed tint state changes from bullish to bearish OR an RSI pivot high is lower than its previous RSI pivot high AND its price pivot high is higher than its previous price pivot high OR an RSI pivot high is higher than its previous RSI pivot high AND its price pivot high is lower than its previous price pivot high OR a chart-timeframe RSI pivot high is no higher than the previous RSI pivot high during a bearish tint season OR a chart-timeframe RSI pivot low is lower than the previous RSI pivot low and the lowest RSI pivot low so far in the current bearish tint season
