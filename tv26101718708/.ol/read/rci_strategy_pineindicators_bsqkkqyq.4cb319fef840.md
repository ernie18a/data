Calculation: Rank Correlation Index (RCI) = Spearman rank correlation between bar order and source-price ranks over a lookback, scaled by 100
Calculation: RCI moving average = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or VWMA of RCI over a lookback
Long: trade direction ≠ Short Only AND current RCI > current RCI moving average AND previous RCI ≤ previous RCI moving average
Short: trade direction ≠ Long Only AND current RCI < current RCI moving average AND previous RCI ≥ previous RCI moving average
Exit: Long, current RCI < current RCI moving average AND previous RCI ≥ previous RCI moving average
Exit: Short, current RCI > current RCI moving average AND previous RCI ≤ previous RCI moving average
