Calculation: MFI = RSI of the sum of volume × source over the lookback when source rises and the sum of volume × source when source falls
Long: RSI < oversold level AND Stochastic RSI < oversold level AND MFI < oversold level AND Williams %R < oversold level
Short: RSI > overbought level AND Stochastic RSI > overbought level AND MFI > overbought level AND Williams %R > overbought level
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + take-profit distance OR low <= entry price - stop-loss distance
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - take-profit distance OR high >= entry price + stop-loss distance
