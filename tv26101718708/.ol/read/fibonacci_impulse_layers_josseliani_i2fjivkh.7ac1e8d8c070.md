Calculation: Active impulse = Track confirmed pivot highs and lows using the same lookback on each side. Keep the first confirmed high and low as structural boundaries. Track the lowest confirmed pivot low and highest confirmed pivot high since the last break. A break occurs when high (wick mode) or close (close mode) exceeds the structural high, or low (wick mode) or close (close mode) falls below the structural low; bullish break takes precedence if both occur on one bar. On a bullish break, set trend bullish and structural high to the break price; if trend was already bullish, first set structural low to the tracked candidate low. On a bearish break, set trend bearish and structural low to the break price; if trend was already bearish, first set structural high to the tracked candidate high. Clear both candidates after a break. Macro uses a longer pivot lookback than Local, both on the chart timeframe. Bias uses its own pivot lookback on a higher timeframe and the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Golden Zone = The price interval between the 50% and 61.8% retracement levels of the active impulse, measured from its end toward its origin.
Long: All three active impulses are valid and bullish, and this bullish alignment becomes true at chart-bar close; OR a valid bullish Local, Macro, or Bias impulse has not changed on this bar, its retracement event for this impulse has not already occurred, the previous chart-bar close is above that impulse's Golden Zone, and the current chart-bar high-low range overlaps the zone.
Short: All three active impulses are valid and bearish, and this bearish alignment becomes true at chart-bar close; OR a valid bearish Local, Macro, or Bias impulse has not changed on this bar, its retracement event for this impulse has not already occurred, the previous chart-bar close is below that impulse's Golden Zone, and the current chart-bar high-low range overlaps the zone.
