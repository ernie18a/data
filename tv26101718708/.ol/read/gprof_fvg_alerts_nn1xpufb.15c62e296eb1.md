Calculation: Daily ATR = average true range calculated on higher-timeframe daily bars, using the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Minimum gap threshold = configured fixed-point threshold, or the greater of configured percentage of Daily ATR and configured tick floor, according to the selected threshold mode.
Calculation: Room overlap count = number of bars in the configured lookback immediately before the three-bar gap whose open-close body overlaps the candle-1 extreme within a configured band of Daily ATR; use candle 1's low for bullish gaps and high for bearish gaps.
Long: On the confirmed chart-timeframe bar close, low > high two bars ago AND low - high two bars ago >= minimum gap threshold AND (room overlap count <= configured maximum OR (room overlap count > configured maximum AND the highest body top in the configured pre-gap lookback < current low)) AND no session break across either of the two intervals immediately preceding the current bar.
Short: On the confirmed chart-timeframe bar close, high < low two bars ago AND low two bars ago - high >= minimum gap threshold AND (room overlap count <= configured maximum OR (room overlap count > configured maximum AND the lowest body bottom in the configured pre-gap lookback > current high)) AND no session break across either of the two intervals immediately preceding the current bar.
