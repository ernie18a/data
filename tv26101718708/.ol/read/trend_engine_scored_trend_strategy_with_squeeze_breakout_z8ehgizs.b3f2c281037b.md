Calculation: Trend tracker = a stepped line using a multiple of the weighted moving average of true range: in an uptrend, take the greater of its prior value and low minus that range; in a downtrend, take the lesser of its prior value and high plus that range. Flip to down when close falls below the line, resetting it to high plus the range; flip to up when close rises above the line, resetting it to low minus the range.
Calculation: Equilibrium = Hull moving average of the linear regression of close.
Calculation: Trend score = 100 × the sum of enabled votes divided by the number of enabled votes. Votes are +1 or −1 for tracker direction, close above/below equilibrium, rising/falling equilibrium, RSI above/below 50, RSI above/below its own simple moving average, and +DI above/below −DI.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe agreement = count of enabled higher timeframes whose trend tracker direction matches the proposed trade direction, using each higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = selected tracker line, entry price adjusted against the position by an ATR multiple, entry price adjusted against the position by a percentage, or the recent swing low/high buffered by ATR; if this stop is on the profitable side of the signal close, use the ATR stop instead.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price adjusted in the position direction by a multiple of initial risk, an ATR multiple, or a percentage, according to the selected target mode.
Calculation: Trailing stop = either the trend tracker when it matches the position direction, or the best high/low since entry adjusted against the position by an ATR multiple; ratchet the stop only toward profit.
Long: tracker direction flips from down to up OR tracker direction is up, no long signal has occurred in that trend, and the bar is within the delayed-entry window; AND all enabled entry filters pass: trend score ≥ minimum; ADX ≥ minimum; RSI ≥ long minimum; close > equilibrium; volume > its average × multiplier (or volume is unavailable or zero); higher-timeframe agreement ≥ required count; cooldown has elapsed; consolidation filter is off, no active zone exists, or a matching up breakout occurred within its window; AND date is in range; AND long trades are allowed.
Short: tracker direction flips from up to down OR tracker direction is down, no short signal has occurred in that trend, and the bar is within the delayed-entry window; AND all enabled entry filters pass: trend score ≤ negative minimum; ADX ≥ minimum; RSI ≤ short maximum; close < equilibrium; volume > its average × multiplier (or volume is unavailable or zero); higher-timeframe agreement ≥ required count; cooldown has elapsed; consolidation filter is off, no active zone exists, or a matching down breakout occurred within its window; AND date is in range; AND short trades are allowed.
Long: prior RSI is below the RSI value two bars earlier AND current RSI is above prior RSI AND prior RSI ≤ oversold level.
Short: prior RSI is above the RSI value two bars earlier AND current RSI is below prior RSI AND prior RSI ≥ overbought level.
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ an enabled profit target OR an opposite downtrend flip when enabled OR maximum bars in trade reached when enabled OR backtest end passed when enabled.
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ an enabled profit target OR an opposite uptrend flip when enabled OR maximum bars in trade reached when enabled OR backtest end passed when enabled.
