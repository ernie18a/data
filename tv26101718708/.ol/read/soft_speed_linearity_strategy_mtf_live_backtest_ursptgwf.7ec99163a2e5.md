Calculation: Analysis bar = bar from the configured analysis timeframe, which may be the chart timeframe or higher or lower; use its still-forming values in live execution and its latest completed values in historical execution
Calculation: Backtest speed = (analysis bar close − open) / analysis bar duration in seconds
Calculation: Smoothed speed = EMA of execution speed
Calculation: Linearity score = 1 if |close − open| / max(ATR, minimum tick) is below minimum progress; otherwise assign the highest score from 5 down to 2 whose adverse excursion / max(ATR, minimum tick) is at or below that score's threshold. Adverse excursion is max(0, open − low) when close ≥ open, otherwise max(0, high − open)
Calculation: Weighted speed = smoothed speed × linearity score / 5
Long: confirmed backtest bar AND cooldown satisfied (if enabled) AND long direction allowed AND close > open AND linearity score ≥ entry minimum AND selected speed metric ≥ mode-specific minimum, where selected speed metric is either |smoothed speed| or |weighted speed|
Short: confirmed backtest bar AND cooldown satisfied (if enabled) AND short direction allowed AND close < open AND linearity score ≥ entry minimum AND selected speed metric ≥ mode-specific minimum, where selected speed metric is either |smoothed speed| or |weighted speed|
Exit: long, confirmed backtest bar AND (selected symmetric mode AND (linearity score < entry minimum OR selected speed metric < mode-specific minimum) OR selected hysteresis mode AND (linearity score ≤ lower exit threshold OR |smoothed speed| ≤ lower speed threshold) OR selected momentum mode AND smoothed speed ≤ 0 OR enabled opposite-signal exit AND short signal OR enabled channel-reentry exit AND speed is within the enabled speed channel)
Exit: short, confirmed backtest bar AND (selected symmetric mode AND (linearity score < entry minimum OR selected speed metric < mode-specific minimum) OR selected hysteresis mode AND (linearity score ≤ lower exit threshold OR |smoothed speed| ≤ lower speed threshold) OR selected momentum mode AND smoothed speed ≥ 0 OR enabled opposite-signal exit AND long signal OR enabled channel-reentry exit AND speed is within the enabled speed channel)
