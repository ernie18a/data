Calculation: conviction score = sum of enabled checks: absolute MACD histogram > its 50-bar simple moving average; for a long, fast EMA > its value 2 bars ago, or for a short, fast EMA < its value 2 bars ago; higher-timeframe trend agrees; absolute distance from close to slow EMA < maximum ATR multiple; for a long, RSI < overbought threshold, or for a short, RSI > oversold threshold. Higher-timeframe trend uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar: for bullish, its close > its 200-period EMA and its 50-period EMA > its 200-period EMA; for bearish, its close < its 200-period EMA and its 50-period EMA < its 200-period EMA.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND MACD histogram > 0 AND conviction score >= minimum score
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND MACD histogram < 0 AND conviction score >= minimum score
