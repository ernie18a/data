Calculation: Global sentiment ratio = fixed BTC supply divided by ((DAI market capitalization + USDT market capitalization + USDC market capitalization) / BTC price)
Calculation: Sentiment oscillator = RSI of the global sentiment ratio, smoothed by a weighted moving average; calculate on the configured analysis timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), using the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the latest available lower-timeframe bar
Calculation: Sentiment reference = weighted moving average of the sentiment oscillator, with a longer lookback than the oscillator's smoothing
Long: Within the configured test date range AND long direction enabled AND flat position AND sentiment oscillator crosses above sentiment reference AND sentiment oscillator is below the oversold threshold
Short: Within the configured test date range AND short direction enabled AND flat position AND sentiment oscillator crosses below sentiment reference AND sentiment oscillator is above the overbought threshold
Exit: Long, stop loss enabled AND low <= configured long stop level OR take profit enabled AND high >= configured long take-profit level OR both stop loss and take profit disabled AND within the configured test date range AND long direction enabled AND sentiment oscillator crosses below sentiment reference AND sentiment oscillator is above the overbought threshold
Exit: Short, stop loss enabled AND high >= configured short stop level OR take profit enabled AND low <= configured short take-profit level OR both stop loss and take profit disabled AND within the configured test date range AND short direction enabled AND sentiment oscillator crosses above sentiment reference AND sentiment oscillator is below the oversold threshold
