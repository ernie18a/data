Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on each side; use it only after the bars on both sides have completed.
Long: close > latest unbroken confirmed pivot high OR high > latest unbroken confirmed pivot high
Short: close < latest unbroken confirmed pivot low OR low < latest unbroken confirmed pivot low
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = a gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier and the prior close is above the prior low plus the prior 200-bar ATR multiplied by the gap threshold; its zone is bounded by the current low and the high two bars earlier. Bearish fair value gap = a gap where the current high is below the low two bars earlier and the prior close is below the prior high minus the prior 200-bar ATR multiplied by the gap threshold; its zone is bounded by the low two bars earlier and the current high.
Long: price raids an unmitigated bullish fair value gap when low < its upper boundary AND close > its upper boundary
Short: price raids an unmitigated bearish fair value gap when high > its lower boundary AND close < its lower boundary
Calculation: Active Fibonacci liquidity zone = the price interval between the -0.21 and -0.29 projections of the active leg. For a bullish leg, project from the leg low toward its high; for a bearish leg, project from the leg high toward its low. The leg is the latest qualifying structure-break swing in Scalp mode, the latest CHoCH swing in Intraday mode, the previous completed higher-timeframe candle’s range in HTF mode, or the completed session/first-four-hour range in Session/First 4H mode; use the higher-timeframe candle only after it completes. Session and First 4H leg direction follows whether close is above or below the Kinetic MA, defined as the selected moving average of close trailed by the running maximum during Supertrend uptrends and running minimum during Supertrend downtrends.
Long: active Fibonacci leg is bullish AND low < upper boundary of its liquidity zone AND close >= upper boundary of its liquidity zone
Short: active Fibonacci leg is bearish AND high > lower boundary of its liquidity zone AND close <= lower boundary of its liquidity zone
