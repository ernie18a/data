Calculation: Trend-adjusted 61.8% retracement level = if close is above the 200-bar simple moving average of close, the lowest low over the lookback plus 61.8% of the range between the highest high and lowest low over the lookback; otherwise, the highest high over the lookback minus 61.8% of that range
Calculation: Volume spike = volume exceeds the 20-bar simple moving average of volume multiplied by the configured volume threshold
Long: close > 200-bar simple moving average of close AND low <= trend-adjusted 61.8% retracement level plus the configured touch tolerance AND close > open AND volume spike AND, when the one-hour trend filter is enabled, the one-hour close > its 200-bar simple moving average (use the last completed one-hour bar when one hour is higher than the chart timeframe; use the available one-hour bar when one hour is lower)
Short: close <= 200-bar simple moving average of close AND high >= trend-adjusted 61.8% retracement level minus the configured touch tolerance AND close < open AND volume spike AND, when the one-hour trend filter is enabled, the one-hour close < its 200-bar simple moving average (use the last completed one-hour bar when one hour is higher than the chart timeframe; use the available one-hour bar when one hour is lower)
