Calculation: Cumulative safety-order deviation = first safety-order step × sum of step-scale powers from 0 through the number of safety orders already filled
Long: Position is flat AND the longer-lookback RSI on a higher-than-chart timeframe crosses above its threshold AND the date-window filter is disabled or the bar datetime is within the configured window
Long: Position is long AND fewer than the maximum safety orders have been filled AND the bar low is at or below the base entry price × (1 − cumulative safety-order deviation for the next order) AND (the same longer-lookback RSI on a higher-than-chart timeframe OR a shorter-lookback RSI on a higher-than-chart timeframe crosses above its threshold) AND the date-window filter is disabled or the bar datetime is within the configured window
Exit: Long, bar high is at or above the average entry price × (1 + take-profit fraction) AND the date-window filter is disabled or the bar datetime is within the configured window
