Calculation: Bullish candle strength = (close - low) / (high - low)
Calculation: Bearish candle strength = (high - close) / (high - low)
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume > its moving average × threshold
Calculation: Trend separation = absolute difference between the short-term EMA and long-term EMA
Long: Bullish candle strength > threshold AND Volume spike AND (Trend separation < ATR × threshold OR short-term EMA > long-term EMA)
Short: Bearish candle strength > threshold AND Volume spike AND (Trend separation < ATR × threshold OR short-term EMA < long-term EMA)
