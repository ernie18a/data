Calculation: Bullish FVG = A gap whose lower boundary is the high of the third-most-recent completed source-timeframe bar and whose upper boundary is the low of the latest completed source-timeframe bar, when the latter is higher than the former
Calculation: Bearish FVG = A gap whose lower boundary is the high of the latest completed source-timeframe bar and whose upper boundary is the low of the third-most-recent completed source-timeframe bar, when the latter is higher than the former
Calculation: Safety boundary = The FVG boundary extended outward by an adjustable percentage of the FVG height
Long: An active, not-yet-retested bullish FVG exists AND the current chart bar's low is at or below its upper boundary AND its high is at or above its lower boundary AND its low is above its safety boundary; source timeframe is equal to or higher than the intraday chart timeframe and only completed source-timeframe bars are used
Short: An active, not-yet-retested bearish FVG exists AND the current chart bar's low is at or below its upper boundary AND its high is at or above its lower boundary AND its high is below its safety boundary; source timeframe is equal to or higher than the intraday chart timeframe and only completed source-timeframe bars are used
