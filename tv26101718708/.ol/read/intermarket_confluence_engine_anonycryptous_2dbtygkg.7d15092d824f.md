Calculation: Relative-strength score = +1 when the Z-score of the difference between Asset A and Asset B rate of change is above its positive threshold and the ratio is above its EMA; −1 when below the negative threshold and the ratio is below its EMA; otherwise 0. The Z-score lookback is longer than the rate-of-change lookback.
Calculation: Trend score = +1 when the ratio’s short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs are bullishly aligned, or the ratio is above its medium- and long-term EMAs, and its linear-regression slope is positive; −1 for the inverse conditions and negative slope; otherwise 0. If enabled, require Asset A’s close to be above (bullish) or below (bearish) its EMA on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Momentum score = +1 when volume-weighted RSI is above its bullish threshold and MACD histogram is positive; −1 when RSI is below its bearish threshold and histogram is negative; otherwise 0. MACD uses shorter fast and longer slow EMA lengths.
Calculation: Volatility score = +1 when Bollinger width exceeds its longer-term average, ATR percentile is above its threshold, and ratio is above the Bollinger basis, or when a squeeze has just ended and ratio is above the Keltner basis; −1 for the corresponding below-basis conditions; otherwise 0. A squeeze is Bollinger Bands contained within the Keltner Channel.
Calculation: Statistical-extremes score = +1 when ratio Z-score is below its negative extreme threshold and its percentile rank is below its low threshold; −1 when Z-score is above its positive extreme threshold and percentile rank is above its high threshold; otherwise 0. The percentile lookback is longer than the Z-score lookback.
Calculation: Macro-regime score = the asset-class-specific score from DXY, VIX, and 10-year yield comparisons: Gold/Silver favors risk-off and opposes risk-on; Crypto scores bullish or bearish when at least two of its three corresponding conditions align; Forex and Indices favor risk-on and oppose risk-off; Commodities favor a falling dollar when VIX is not high and oppose a rising dollar.
Calculation: Liquidity score = +1 when the smoothed 10-year-yield rate of change indicates expanding liquidity, −1 when it indicates contracting liquidity, otherwise 0; reverse these directions for Forex. Smooth the yield rate of change with a shorter EMA than its rate-of-change lookback.
Calculation: Correlation score = +1 or −1 when rolling Pearson correlations with macro feeds exceed the configured directional thresholds for the selected asset class; otherwise 0. Gold/Silver uses VIX and DXY correlations, Crypto uses DXY correlation, Indices uses VIX correlation, and other classes score 0.
Calculation: Volume-participation score = +1 when OBV slope is positive, ratio is above its short-term EMA, and Asset A relative volume exceeds Asset B relative volume; −1 when all three conditions are reversed; otherwise 0. OBV slope uses linear regression.
Calculation: Net confluence score = 10 times the sum of the nine engine scores multiplied by their asset-class or custom weights, divided by the sum of those weights.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed ratio pivot low, the ratio pivot is higher than the previous pivot low while volume-weighted RSI at the pivot is lower than at the previous pivot low. Bearish divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, the ratio pivot is lower than the previous pivot high while RSI at the pivot is higher than at the previous pivot high.
Long: Net confluence score is at least the configured moderate-signal threshold OR (bullish divergence AND net confluence score > 0).
Short: Net confluence score is at most the negative configured moderate-signal threshold OR (bearish divergence AND net confluence score < 0).
