Calculation: Prior 10-bar price low/high = lowest low/highest high over the preceding 10 bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Prior 10-bar comparison-asset low/high = lowest low/highest high over the preceding 10 bars, excluding the current bar; use the requested asset timeframe (same as chart timeframe by default), using its current bar
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of bar delta; bar delta is the sum of lower-timeframe intrabar volume signed positive when intrabar close >= open and negative otherwise, or chart-bar volume signed by chart-bar close >= open when bar-direction mode is selected
Calculation: Active bullish swing order block = the latest unmitigated zone from the high and low of the bar at a confirmed swing low; a zone is mitigated when a bar closes below its low
Calculation: Active bearish swing order block = the latest unmitigated zone from the high and low of the bar at a confirmed swing high; a zone is mitigated when a bar closes above its high
Calculation: Previous completed higher-timeframe high/low = high/low of the last completed bar on the SFP reference timeframe
Long: current low < prior 10-bar price low AND (current VIX high < prior 10-bar VIX high OR current PCC high < prior 10-bar PCC high OR current comparison-asset low > prior 10-bar comparison-asset low) AND (RSI/BB confirmation disabled OR (RSI < oversold threshold AND current low < lower Bollinger Band)) AND (order-block confirmation disabled OR (current bar range overlaps active bullish swing order block)) AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR (current low < lowest low of the preceding 5 bars AND cumulative volume delta > lowest cumulative volume delta of the preceding 5 bars)) AND (SFP confirmation disabled OR (current low < previous completed higher-timeframe low AND current close > previous completed higher-timeframe low))
Short: current high > prior 10-bar price high AND (current VIX low > prior 10-bar VIX low OR current PCC low > prior 10-bar PCC low OR current comparison-asset high < prior 10-bar comparison-asset high) AND (RSI/BB confirmation disabled OR (RSI > overbought threshold AND current high > upper Bollinger Band)) AND (order-block confirmation disabled OR (current bar range overlaps active bearish swing order block)) AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR (current high > highest high of the preceding 5 bars AND cumulative volume delta < highest cumulative volume delta of the preceding 5 bars)) AND (SFP confirmation disabled OR (current high > previous completed higher-timeframe high AND current close < previous completed higher-timeframe high))
Exit: long, current low <= configured long stop price OR current high >= configured long target price OR short condition
Exit: short, current high >= configured short stop price OR current low <= configured short target price OR long condition
