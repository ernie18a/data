Calculation: High-participation bar = volume > simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback × a configurable multiplier
Calculation: Participation zones = on each high-participation bar, create a zone from its high and low, or extend the first active zone whose bounds expanded by ATR × a configurable tolerance overlap the bar’s range; extend zone bounds to include the bar. Add volume to bullish ownership by volume × bullish share and to bearish ownership by the remainder, where bullish share is max(0.55, (max(close, open) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick)) when close ≥ open, otherwise min(0.45, (max(close, open) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick)). Expire a zone after a configurable number of bars from its start. Deactivate it if close is above its top and its balance is ≥ 0, or below its bottom and its balance is ≤ 0.
Calculation: Zone balance = (bullish ownership − bearish ownership) / total ownership
Calculation: Zone weighted equilibrium = volume-weighted average of HLC3 for its high-participation bars
Calculation: Dominant active zone = active zone with greatest absolute balance; dominant top, bottom, balance, and weighted equilibrium are that zone’s values
Long: A dominant active zone exists AND ( (balance > 0.08 AND close > weighted equilibrium AND close ≤ top) OR close > top OR (close > weighted equilibrium AND previous close ≤ previous weighted equilibrium) )
Short: A dominant active zone exists AND ( (balance < −0.08 AND close < weighted equilibrium AND close ≥ bottom) OR close < bottom OR (close < weighted equilibrium AND previous close ≥ previous weighted equilibrium) )
