Calculation: Psychological level = an integer multiple of the configured grid step; zone = level ± configured half-width.
Calculation: Reversal distance = the greater of the configured grid-step fraction and configured ATR multiple, floored above the zone half-width plus minimum tick; ATR uses the last completed bar on the fixed stats timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Reversal probability = resolved reversals / (resolved reversals + resolved breakouts); breakout probability = resolved breakouts / the same total. Support hold rate = reversals on tests from above / (those reversals + breakouts downward); resistance hold rate = reversals on tests from below / (those reversals + breakouts upward). Use outcomes from the configured UTC calendar window on the fixed stats timeframe.
Calculation: A breakout fakeout is a breakout followed within the configured watch period by a close back through the opposite zone edge. Strength = scaled lower confidence bound of the dominant resolved outcome. A significant tier edge means the reversal rate differs from the same-tier reversal rate by at least the configured significance threshold.
Long: On a confirmed fixed-stats-timeframe bar (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), an active support test that began with the prior close above the zone closes at or above the level plus reversal distance, with no breakout on that bar; OR an active resistance test closes above the zone top plus the configured ATR breakout buffer, with no reversal on that bar; OR a new support test begins and its historical support hold rate meets the configured high-hold threshold with at least the configured minimum resolved tests; OR a reversal-zone fade trigger occurs with sufficient strength, significant positive tier edge, and high support hold rate; OR a breakout-zone continuation trigger occurs with high breakout probability, low fakeout rate, and sufficient breakout follow-through.
Short: On a confirmed fixed-stats-timeframe bar (higher or lower than the chart timeframe), an active resistance test that began with the prior close below the zone closes at or below the level minus reversal distance, with no breakout on that bar; OR an active support test closes below the zone bottom minus the configured ATR breakout buffer, with no reversal on that bar; OR a new resistance test begins and its historical resistance hold rate meets the configured high-hold threshold with at least the configured minimum resolved tests; OR a reversal-zone fade trigger occurs with sufficient strength, significant positive tier edge, and high resistance hold rate; OR a breakout-zone continuation trigger occurs with high breakout probability, low fakeout rate, and sufficient breakout follow-through.
Exit: Long, a close below the zone bottom minus the configured ATR breakout buffer during a support test being faded, OR a close back through the zone bottom within the breakout watch period after an upward breakout.
Exit: Short, a close above the zone top plus the configured ATR breakout buffer during a resistance test being faded, OR a close back through the zone top within the breakout watch period after a downward breakout.
