Calculation: Cumulative averaging-order deviation(k) = sum of the first k ladder steps; the first step is the configured initial deviation, and each later step is the previous step multiplied by the configured step multiplier.
Short: Flat, no open trades, within the configured date window, and the last completed higher-timeframe RSI crosses below its configured level (previous RSI ≥ level AND current RSI < level) OR short position open, within the configured date window, averaging orders remain, and close ≥ base entry price × (1 + cumulative deviation for the next order / 100).
Exit: Short, close ≥ average entry price × (1 + configured stop-loss percentage / 100) OR take-profit trailing is armed (close ≤ average entry price × (1 − configured take-profit percentage / 100) has occurred) AND close ≥ lowest close since trailing was armed × (1 + configured trailing percentage / 100).
