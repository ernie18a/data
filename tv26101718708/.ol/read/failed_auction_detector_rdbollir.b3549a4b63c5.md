Calculation: Swing high/low = most recent confirmed pivot high/low, using equal left and right lookbacks on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Session high/low = highest high / lowest low from earlier bars in the current session, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe (daily)
Calculation: Fresh high/low = current high/low exceeds the highest / is below the lowest chart-timeframe value over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Reference high/low = highest swept enabled high level / lowest swept enabled low level among swing, session, and prior-day levels
Calculation: Footprint top/bottom statistics = buy volume, sell volume, and total volume summed over the configured number of highest/lowest traded-price rows; also determine whether any such row has the respective sell/buy imbalance
Calculation: POC-row buy/sell volume = buy/sell volume at the footprint row with the greatest total volume
Calculation: Average row volume = total footprint volume divided by the number of traded-price rows
Calculation: Top/bottom delta = top/bottom buy volume minus top/bottom sell volume
Calculation: Short rejection evidence = top sell imbalance OR top delta <= 0 OR (top buy volume <= exhaustion factor × POC-row buy volume) OR (top total volume >= absorption factor × average row volume AND top delta > 0)
Calculation: Long rejection evidence = bottom buy imbalance OR bottom delta >= 0 OR (bottom sell volume <= exhaustion factor × POC-row sell volume) OR (bottom total volume >= absorption factor × average row volume AND bottom delta < 0)
Calculation: Short score = sum of: reclaimed distance from reference high >= configured ATR fraction; (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum price tick) >= configured wick fraction; top sell imbalance; any non-imbalance short rejection evidence; volume >= configured factor × chart-timeframe volume SMA; and high is within configured tolerance of prior-day high
Calculation: Long score = sum of: reclaimed distance from reference low >= configured ATR fraction; (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum price tick) >= configured wick fraction; bottom buy imbalance; any non-imbalance long rejection evidence; volume >= configured factor × chart-timeframe volume SMA; and low is within configured tolerance of prior-day low
Short: footprint available AND current high exceeds an enabled swing high, prior session high, or prior-day high by the configured sweep depth AND close is below the highest swept enabled high AND (session level was swept OR fresh-high filter is disabled OR Fresh high) AND (orderflow rejection filter is disabled OR Short rejection evidence) AND Short score >= configured minimum AND bar is completed AND one-signal-per-level filter is disabled OR the reference high differs from the last fired reference high
Long: footprint available AND current low falls below an enabled swing low, prior session low, or prior-day low by the configured sweep depth AND close is above the lowest swept enabled low AND (session level was swept OR fresh-low filter is disabled OR Fresh low) AND (orderflow rejection filter is disabled OR Long rejection evidence) AND Long score >= configured minimum AND bar is completed AND one-signal-per-level filter is disabled OR the reference low differs from the last fired reference low
