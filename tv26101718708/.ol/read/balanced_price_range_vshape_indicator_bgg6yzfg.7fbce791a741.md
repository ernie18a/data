Calculation: Fair Value Gap = bullish when a bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish when its high is below the low two bars earlier. The middle bar is the displacement bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Fair Value Gap = bullish when the last completed higher-timeframe bar’s low is above the high three higher-timeframe bars earlier; bearish when its high is below the low three higher-timeframe bars earlier.
Calculation: Liquidity levels = confirmed swing highs/lows; equal highs/lows from consecutive same-side swing pivots within an ATR-based tolerance; previous-day high/low; and configured session highs/lows.
Calculation: Displacement-body reclaim fraction = for a long, (inversion close − displacement close) / (displacement open − displacement close); for a short, (displacement close − inversion close) / (displacement close − displacement open).
Long: A bearish Fair Value Gap formed within the configured lookback AND the current bar closes above its upper boundary while the preceding bar’s close was at or below that boundary AND the displacement-body reclaim fraction meets the configured minimum AND (the preceding bar closed below its open, if the clean-tip filter is enabled) AND (a matching, unmitigated higher-timeframe bullish Fair Value Gap was tapped within the configured window and the inverted gap overlaps or is within the configured ATR buffer of it, OR a liquidity low was swept within the configured window) when the context gate is enabled AND (no un-swept, unbroken liquidity low is below the current bar’s low and within the configured ATR danger distance, if the clear-path filter is enabled).
Short: A bullish Fair Value Gap formed within the configured lookback AND the current bar closes below its lower boundary while the preceding bar’s close was at or above that boundary AND the displacement-body reclaim fraction meets the configured minimum AND (the preceding bar closed above its open, if the clean-tip filter is enabled) AND (a matching, unmitigated higher-timeframe bearish Fair Value Gap was tapped within the configured window and the inverted gap overlaps or is within the configured ATR buffer of it, OR a liquidity high was swept within the configured window) when the context gate is enabled AND (no un-swept, unbroken liquidity high is above the current bar’s high and within the configured ATR danger distance, if the clear-path filter is enabled).
