Calculation: Momentum oscillator = EMA((close − moving average of close) / ATR, using the moving average lookback for ATR), with a separate smoothing lookback
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the pivot lookback bars before and after it; becomes available only after the bars after it complete
Long: Supertrend direction flips from down to up on a completed chart-timeframe bar OR momentum oscillator crosses above its value two bars earlier while below its negative threshold on a completed chart-timeframe bar OR close > the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high OR low > high two bars earlier AND the middle bar's close > high two bars earlier AND the gap is at least the minimum ATR-scaled size
Short: Supertrend direction flips from up to down on a completed chart-timeframe bar OR momentum oscillator crosses below its value two bars earlier while above its positive threshold on a completed chart-timeframe bar OR close < the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low OR high < low two bars earlier AND the middle bar's close < low two bars earlier AND the gap is at least the minimum ATR-scaled size
