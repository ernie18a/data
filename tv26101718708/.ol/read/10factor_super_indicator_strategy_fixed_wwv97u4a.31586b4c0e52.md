Calculation: Composite score = 100 × the weighted sum of ten votes divided by their total weights. Votes are +weight for: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; Supertrend indicates uptrend; MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0; RSI > its bullish threshold; ADX > its minimum and +DI > −DI; close > VWAP; close > Bollinger basis and < upper band; Stoch RSI %K > %D and between its lower and upper bounds; OBV > its EMA. Votes are −weight for the corresponding bearish conditions: fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA; Supertrend indicates downtrend; MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; RSI < its bearish threshold; ADX > its minimum and −DI > +DI; close < VWAP; close < Bollinger basis and > lower band; %K < %D and between the bounds; OBV < its EMA. Otherwise those votes are zero. The ATR regime vote is its positive weight when ATR as a percentage of close exceeds its minimum, and zero otherwise. The fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Stoch RSI %K = a moving average of the RSI’s position within its rolling low-to-high range; %D = a moving average of %K.
Long: Composite score ≥ long threshold AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND Supertrend indicates uptrend AND ADX > its minimum AND ATR as a percentage of close > its minimum AND position is flat or short.
Short: Composite score ≤ short threshold AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND Supertrend indicates downtrend AND ADX > its minimum AND ATR as a percentage of close > its minimum AND position is flat or long.
Exit: long, composite score < exit threshold OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × take-profit multiple
Exit: short, composite score > negative exit threshold OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × take-profit multiple
