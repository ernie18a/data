Calculation: Buying volume = Sum volume of lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar where close > open, plus half the volume of bars where close = open; include the lower-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Selling volume = Sum volume of lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar where close < open, plus half the volume of bars where close = open; include the lower-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: Buying volume > Selling volume × imbalance ratio
Short: Selling volume > Buying volume × imbalance ratio
