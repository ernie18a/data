Calculation: Previous weekly high/low = highest high / lowest low of the preceding week
Calculation: Previous daily high/low = highest high / lowest low of the preceding day
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low of the candle after the middle candle > high of the candle before the middle candle, with gap at least an ATR-based minimum
Calculation: Bearish FVG = high of the candle after the middle candle < low of the candle before the middle candle, with gap at least an ATR-based minimum
Calculation: Bullish volume imbalance = lower body boundary of the later candle > upper body boundary of the earlier candle, with gap at least an ATR-based minimum
Calculation: Bearish volume imbalance = upper body boundary of the later candle < lower body boundary of the earlier candle, with gap at least an ATR-based minimum
Calculation: Structure direction = on each scanned timeframe, direction switches up when close breaks above the latest qualifying confirmed swing high and switches down when close breaks below the latest qualifying confirmed swing low; a switch against the prior direction is CHoCH. Swings use symmetric left/right lookbacks and an ATR-based minimum separation. Use the latest available bars from aggregated timeframes higher or lower than the chart timeframe (forming in real time; completed in historical data).
Calculation: Recent weekly-level sweep = the previous weekly high/low was swept within a recent-bar lookback; a high sweep has high > level and close <= level, and a low sweep has low < level and close >= level
Calculation: Near weekly level = close within an ATR-based tolerance of the previous weekly high/low, or price crossed that level and close remains within the tolerance
Calculation: Near daily level = close within a smaller ATR-based tolerance of the previous daily high/low
Calculation: Bullish MSS = an up CHoCH on either of the two highest scanned timeframes AND (recent sweep of previous weekly low OR near previous weekly low OR near previous daily low)
Calculation: Bearish MSS = a down CHoCH on either of the two highest scanned timeframes AND (recent sweep of previous weekly high OR near previous weekly high OR near previous daily high)
Calculation: IFVG near above/below = nearest mitigated FVG or volume-imbalance midpoint above/below close within an ATR-based tolerance
Long: bullish FVG OR bullish volume imbalance OR (bullish MSS AND IFVG near below AND NOT (bearish MSS AND IFVG near above)) OR (bullish MSS AND NOT (bearish MSS AND IFVG near above) AND NOT (bearish MSS AND NOT IFVG near above)) OR (up CHoCH on any scanned timeframe AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS)) OR (at least three scanned timeframe structure directions are up AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS OR any CHoCH)) OR (more scanned timeframe structure directions are up than down, at least one is up, AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS OR any CHoCH OR at least three directions are down))
Short: bearish FVG OR bearish volume imbalance OR (bearish MSS AND IFVG near above) OR (bearish MSS AND NOT IFVG near above AND NOT (bullish MSS AND IFVG near below)) OR (down CHoCH on any scanned timeframe AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS)) OR (at least three scanned timeframe structure directions are down AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS OR any CHoCH)) OR (more scanned timeframe structure directions are down than up, at least one is down, AND NOT (bearish MSS OR bullish MSS OR any CHoCH OR at least three directions are up))
