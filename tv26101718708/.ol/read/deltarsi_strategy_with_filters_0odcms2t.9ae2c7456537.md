Calculation: Delta-RSI = the derivative at the newest bar of a least-squares polynomial fit to RSI(close, RSI lookback) over a rolling lookback window; assign x = 0 to the oldest sample and x = window length − 1 to the newest
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of Delta-RSI
Long: (selected long trigger is zero-crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= 0 to > 0) OR (selected long trigger is signal-line crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= signal line to > signal line) OR (selected long trigger is direction change AND Delta-RSI > its previous value AND its previous value < the value two bars ago AND its previous value < 0); AND each enabled filter applied to long entries passes: volume > multiplier × its moving average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, RSI(close) > lower bound AND RSI(close) < upper bound; AND each enabled backtest date bound passes
Short: (selected short trigger is zero-crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from >= 0 to < 0) OR (selected short trigger is signal-line crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from >= signal line to < signal line) OR (selected short trigger is direction change AND Delta-RSI < its previous value AND its previous value > the value two bars ago AND its previous value > 0); AND each enabled filter applied to short entries passes: volume > multiplier × its moving average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, RSI(close) > lower bound AND RSI(close) < upper bound; AND each enabled backtest date bound passes
Exit: long, (selected exit trigger is zero-crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= 0 to > 0) OR (selected exit trigger is signal-line crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= signal line to > signal line) OR (selected exit trigger is direction change AND Delta-RSI > its previous value AND its previous value < the value two bars ago AND its previous value < 0); AND each enabled filter applied to exits passes: volume > multiplier × its moving average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, RSI(close) > lower bound AND RSI(close) < upper bound
Exit: short, (selected exit trigger is zero-crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= 0 to > 0) OR (selected exit trigger is signal-line crossing AND Delta-RSI crosses from <= signal line to > signal line) OR (selected exit trigger is direction change AND Delta-RSI > its previous value AND its previous value < the value two bars ago AND its previous value < 0); AND each enabled filter applied to exits passes: volume > multiplier × its moving average over a lookback, shorter-lookback ATR > longer-lookback ATR, RSI(close) > lower bound AND RSI(close) < upper bound
Exit: long, take-profit enabled AND high >= signal-close entry reference × (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, take-profit enabled AND low <= signal-close entry reference × (1 − take-profit percentage)
Exit: long, stop-loss enabled AND low <= stop level, where stop level is entry reference × (1 − stop-loss percentage), or, when trailing is enabled, the greater of that initial stop and the prior stop level and close × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: short, stop-loss enabled AND high >= stop level, where stop level is entry reference × (1 + stop-loss percentage), or, when trailing is enabled, the lesser of that initial stop and the prior stop level and close × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
Exit: long, enabled backtest date range is violated
Exit: short, enabled backtest date range is violated
