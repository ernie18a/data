Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 multiplied by volume, divided by cumulative volume since the current session began
Calculation: Session meridian = (current session high + current session low) / 2
Calculation: Prior session high and low = high and low of the most recently completed session
Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening period of the current London or New York session; fixed once that period ends
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND (close - low) / (high - low) > 0.5
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND (high - close) / (high - low) > 0.5
Calculation: Session ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs = EMAs with shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively, on a higher timeframe; use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming
Long: (prior session high is crossed upward by the close AND, within the retest window, low <= prior session high + ATR tolerance AND close > prior session high AND bullish rejection) OR (opening range is set AND close crosses above its high AND bullish rejection) OR (close > session VWAP AND close > session meridian AND low <= session meridian + ATR tolerance AND close > session meridian AND bullish rejection); AND the selected setup is enabled; AND bullish confluence score meets the configured minimum, where the score counts: close > session VWAP and close > session meridian, close > session VWAP, higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA and higher-timeframe close > fast EMA (or filter disabled), current session enabled for trading, volume >= volume average times its configured multiplier (or filter disabled), bullish rejection, and a qualifying enabled setup; AND signal cooldown has elapsed; AND bar is confirmed
Short: (prior session low is crossed downward by the close AND, within the retest window, high >= prior session low - ATR tolerance AND close < prior session low AND bearish rejection) OR (opening range is set AND close crosses below its low AND bearish rejection) OR (close < session VWAP AND close < session meridian AND high >= session meridian - ATR tolerance AND close < session meridian AND bearish rejection); AND the selected setup is enabled; AND bearish confluence score meets the configured minimum, where the score counts: close < session VWAP and close < session meridian, close < session VWAP, higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA and higher-timeframe close < fast EMA (or filter disabled), current session enabled for trading, volume >= volume average times its configured multiplier (or filter disabled), bearish rejection, and a qualifying enabled setup; AND signal cooldown has elapsed; AND bar is confirmed
