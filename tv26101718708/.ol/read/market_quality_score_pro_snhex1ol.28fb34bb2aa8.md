Calculation: Relative strength score = weighted mean of the available percentiles of (instrument return over the past year excluding the most recent month minus benchmark return over that period), six-month excess return, three-month excess return, current close divided by the highest close over the past year, and the 50-bar change in the instrument-to-benchmark close ratio; higher-timeframe daily bars, including the bar still forming; clamp to [0,1].
Calculation: Trend score = weighted mean of the available scores for close relative to the 200-bar SMA, 50-bar SMA relative to 200-bar SMA, and drawdown from the 60-bar highest close relative to the allowed drawdown; higher-timeframe daily bars, including the bar still forming; clamp to [0,1].
Calculation: Total score = weighted mean of available relative strength, fundamental quality (when available), and trend scores using their configured weights, renormalized over available components and clamped to [0,1]; smooth with a 3-bar EMA for index mode.
Calculation: Coverage = weighted proportion of available relative-strength, fundamental (equity mode only), and trend inputs; confidence = mean of coverage and history score, where full required history scores 1, minimum history scores 0.6, and less history scores 0.2.
Calculation: Drawdown pass = drawdown from the 60-bar highest close is at least the stricter of the configured maximum drawdown and −2.5 times 14-bar ATR as a percentage of close; higher-timeframe daily bars, including the bar still forming.
Long: Total score is at or above the buy threshold on this and the previous bar AND history is full AND coverage and confidence meet the buy minimums AND the required benchmark exists AND (safety filter is disabled OR drawdown pass and close is above the 200-bar SMA and, for equities, 20-bar average close-times-volume meets the turnover minimum; for ETFs, either volume is unavailable or that turnover minimum is met) AND this condition was false on the previous bar AND the strong-buy condition is false.
Long: Total score is at or above the strong-buy threshold on this and the previous bar AND all normal buy conditions hold AND relative-strength score meets its strong minimum AND trend score meets its strong minimum AND coverage and confidence meet their strong minimums AND drawdown meets its stricter strong minimum AND, in equity mode, fundamental quality meets its strong minimum AND total score is higher than 3 bars ago AND 3-bar-smoothed total-score change versus 20 bars ago is positive AND close is at or above the highest close of the previous 20 bars AND this condition was false on the previous bar.
Exit: Long, total score falls below the buy threshold after being at or above it on the previous bar AND minimum history is available AND coverage meets the watch minimum.
Exit: Long, total score crosses below the weak-score threshold.
