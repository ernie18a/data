Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago, the previous bar closed above its open, and (current low − high two bars ago) / current close × 100 ≥ minimum gap size
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago, the previous bar closed below its open, and (low two bars ago − current high) / current close × 100 ≥ minimum gap size
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume > simple moving average of volume × spike multiplier
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = current close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; the pivot uses equal left and right lookback lengths
Calculation: Bearish break of structure = current close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low; the pivot uses equal left and right lookback lengths
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current close > current open, previous close < previous open, current close > previous open, current open < previous close, and current body size > previous body size
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current close < current open, previous close > previous open, current close < previous open, current open > previous close, and current body size > previous body size

Long: Bullish break of structure
Long: Bullish engulfing
Short: Bearish fair value gap AND volume spike AND current close < current-bar exponential moving average of close
Short: Bearish break of structure

Exit: Long, current low ≤ long entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR current high ≥ long entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR bearish break of structure
Exit: Short, current high ≥ short entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR current low ≤ short entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR bullish break of structure
