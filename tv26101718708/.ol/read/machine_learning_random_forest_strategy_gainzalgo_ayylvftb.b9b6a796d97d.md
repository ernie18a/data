Calculation: Bull probability = average across classifier stumps of each stump’s training-sample bullish fraction on the side containing the current feature vector; train on the rolling window of lagged features and next-bar up/down outcomes, choosing each stump’s feature randomly and its threshold from random training-sample values to maximize weighted Gini information gain. Features: selected RSI, MFI, stochastic, or close z-score; close/time correlation; and close momentum divided by ATR, each normalized as in the source.
Calculation: Effective probability threshold = base threshold + adaptive boost; when enabled, boost is zero unless the mean PnL of the most recent resolved signals is negative, then it is the lesser of half the absolute mean PnL and the maximum boost. Resolve signals at target, stop, or the maximum holding period; if target and stop are both touched in one bar, count the stop.
Calculation: Target and stop = entry price plus/minus the configured multiple of ATR, with signs reversed for shorts; use ATR from the chart timeframe.
Long: Bull probability >= effective threshold AND previous bar’s bull probability was below its effective threshold AND bars since the previous long signal >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold).
Short: 1 − bull probability >= effective threshold AND previous bar’s bear probability was below its effective threshold AND bars since the previous short signal >= cooldown AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= its threshold).
Exit: long, high >= target OR low <= stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
Exit: short, low <= target OR high >= stop OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period.
