Calculation: Trend score = 0.65 × (short-term EMA − long-term EMA) / close + 0.35 × (long-term EMA − its earlier value) / close; the earlier value uses a longer lookback than the EMA calculation
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / short-term average volume
Calculation: Market state = cluster the recent trend score, trend slope, and relative volume observations; classify the dominant cluster as bullish when its trend score exceeds a positive ATR-based threshold, bearish when below the corresponding negative threshold, otherwise as a range; bullish or bearish states are active when relative volume exceeds its threshold, otherwise soft
Calculation: Nearest K-means price zone = cluster recent confirmed pivot highs and lows and sampled bar highs and lows, then select the center nearest to close
Calculation: Body, upper-wick, and lower-wick ratios = the respective candle body or wick length divided by high − low
Long: bullish market state OR bullish candle with body ratio above its threshold, opening below and closing above the nearest K-means price zone OR bullish candle with lower-wick ratio above its threshold and greater than both upper-wick ratio and body ratio times its threshold, whose low touches the nearest K-means price zone OR bullish candle with body ratio above its threshold closing above the previous bar's high
Short: bearish market state OR bearish candle with body ratio above its threshold, opening above and closing below the nearest K-means price zone OR bearish candle with upper-wick ratio above its threshold and greater than both lower-wick ratio and body ratio times its threshold, whose high touches the nearest K-means price zone OR bearish candle with body ratio above its threshold closing below the previous bar's low
