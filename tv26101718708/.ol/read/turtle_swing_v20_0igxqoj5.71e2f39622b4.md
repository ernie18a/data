Calculation: Short-term Donchian upper/lower bands = highest high / lowest low over the short-term lookback of completed chart bars; long-term bands use a longer lookback. Entry bands are from the previous completed bar; exit stops use the last completed bar’s band.
Long: Flat AND ((close > short-term Donchian upper band AND close > EMA AND RSI > threshold AND volume >= its 20-bar average × volume multiplier AND most recent System 1 closed-trade profit <= 0) OR (close > long-term Donchian upper band AND close > EMA AND RSI > threshold AND volume >= its 20-bar average × volume multiplier)) OR (already long AND close >= initial entry price + existing unit count × pyramid step AND EMA > its value at the configured slope lookback AND existing unit count < maximum units)
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR low <= short-term Donchian lower band OR low <= long-term Donchian lower band OR close < EMA
