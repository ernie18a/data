Calculation: Overnight POC, value area high, and value area low = build a price profile from completed overnight-session bars by dividing the overnight high-low range into rows, allocating each bar’s volume to rows in proportion to the bar’s price overlap (assign the full volume to the touched row when the bar has no range), and selecting the highest-volume row as POC; expand the value area from the POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent row until the target value-area volume is reached; POC is the center of its row, and value area high/low are the upper/lower boundaries of the outer included rows.
Long: During the New York session, the first bar since the latest completed overnight profile whose high >= overnight value area low - touch tolerance and low <= overnight value area low + touch tolerance
Long: During the New York session, the first bar since the latest completed overnight profile whose high >= overnight POC - touch tolerance and low <= overnight POC + touch tolerance AND close >= overnight POC
Long: During the New York session, after a close crosses above an overnight profile level (overnight value area high, POC, or value area low), the first later bar whose low <= that level + touch tolerance and close >= that level, provided no retest of that level has completed since the latest cross
Short: During the New York session, the first bar since the latest completed overnight profile whose high >= overnight value area high - touch tolerance and low <= overnight value area high + touch tolerance
Short: During the New York session, the first bar since the latest completed overnight profile whose high >= overnight POC - touch tolerance and low <= overnight POC + touch tolerance AND close < overnight POC
Short: During the New York session, after a close crosses below an overnight profile level (overnight value area high, POC, or value area low), the first later bar whose high >= that level - touch tolerance and close <= that level, provided no retest of that level has completed since the latest cross
