Calculation: XTL value = (source − selected moving average of source) divided by 0.015 times the mean absolute deviation of source from that moving average over the same lookback; the traditional option uses the mean absolute deviation of the absolute source-minus-average values.
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression of (source − average of the midpoint between the lookback highest high and lowest low, and the selected moving average of source) over the same lookback.
Long: +DI ≥ −DI OR +DI ≥ −DI AND ADX ≥ its trend threshold and the bullish trend state has just changed to true OR Hull average crosses above its value two bars ago OR XTL value > positive threshold OR regular bullish divergence (price makes a lower pivot low while Momentum makes a higher pivot low, with Momentum < 0) OR hidden bullish divergence (price makes a higher pivot low while Momentum makes a lower pivot low, with Momentum < 0)
Short: +DI < −DI OR +DI < −DI AND ADX ≥ its trend threshold and the bearish trend state has just changed to true OR Hull average crosses below its value two bars ago OR XTL value < negative threshold OR regular bearish divergence (price makes a higher pivot high while Momentum makes a lower pivot high, with Momentum > 0) OR hidden bearish divergence (price makes a lower pivot high while Momentum makes a higher pivot high, with Momentum > 0)
