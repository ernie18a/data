Calculation: Bull trend = shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND the longer-lookback EMA's change over 5 bars is no less than the negative configured ATR slope tolerance
Calculation: Bear trend = shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND the longer-lookback EMA's change over 5 bars is no greater than the configured ATR slope tolerance
Calculation: Automatic side = long when Bull trend is true and Bear trend is false; short when Bear trend is true and Bull trend is false; otherwise long when close >= longer-lookback EMA, short otherwise
Calculation: Invalidation reference = for a long, the lower of the lowest low over the configured invalidation lookback and the anchor price minus an ATR-based fallback distance; for a short, the higher of the highest high over that lookback and the anchor price plus an ATR-based fallback distance
Long: On a completed bar, close > the highest high of the preceding configured activation lookback AND management side is forced long or Automatic side is long AND activation confirmation is Close Only OR Bull trend is true (and, if confirmation also requires volume, volume >= its configured moving-average baseline when volume data is available) AND no map is active or the configured map cooldown has elapsed
Short: On a completed bar, close < the lowest low of the preceding configured activation lookback AND management side is forced short or Automatic side is short AND activation confirmation is Close Only OR Bear trend is true (and, if confirmation also requires volume, volume >= its configured moving-average baseline when volume data is available) AND no map is active or the configured map cooldown has elapsed
Exit: Long, close < the long invalidation reference
Exit: Short, close > the short invalidation reference
