Calculation: Macro bullish = completed higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA AND that EMA > its value one completed higher-timeframe bar earlier; macro bearish = completed higher-timeframe close < its long-term EMA AND that EMA < its value one completed higher-timeframe bar earlier
Calculation: Structural support = the nearest level below current close among the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low, the last completed higher-timeframe low, the previous completed daily low and close, current VWAP, and current long-term chart-timeframe EMA; structural resistance = the nearest level above current close among the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high, the last completed higher-timeframe high, the previous completed daily high and close, current VWAP, and current long-term chart-timeframe EMA
Calculation: Support zone = structural support ± an ATR-based width; resistance zone = structural resistance ± an ATR-based width
Calculation: Near support = structural support exists AND current low <= support zone's upper edge AND current close >= its lower edge; near resistance = structural resistance exists AND current high >= resistance zone's lower edge AND current close <= its upper edge
Calculation: Near VWAP = absolute distance between close and VWAP <= ATR-based zone width; near EMA = absolute distance between close and long-term chart-timeframe EMA <= ATR-based zone width
Calculation: Strong-body candle = absolute close-minus-open divided by high-minus-low >= a minimum fraction
Calculation: Bullish breakout = prior-bar resistance exists AND close > prior-bar resistance AND prior close <= prior-bar resistance; bearish breakout = prior-bar support exists AND close < prior-bar support AND prior close >= prior-bar support
Calculation: Bullish rejection = near support AND close > prior high AND close > open; bearish rejection = near resistance AND close < prior low AND close < open
Calculation: Rising MACD histogram = current MACD histogram > prior MACD histogram; falling MACD histogram = current MACD histogram < prior MACD histogram
Calculation: Bullish trigger = (bullish breakout OR bullish rejection) AND strong-body candle AND rising MACD histogram AND +DI > -DI; bearish trigger = (bearish breakout OR bearish rejection) AND strong-body candle AND falling MACD histogram AND -DI > +DI
Calculation: Bullish trend-strength condition = +DI > -DI AND ADX >= a minimum threshold AND ADX > prior ADX; bearish trend-strength condition = -DI > +DI AND ADX >= a minimum threshold AND ADX > prior ADX
Calculation: Bullish risk = close minus (the lower of low and support, if support exists, otherwise low, less an ATR-based buffer); bearish risk = (the higher of high and resistance, if resistance exists, otherwise high, plus an ATR-based buffer) minus close
Calculation: Bullish room = resistance minus close if resistance exists, otherwise an ATR-based fallback; bearish room = close minus support if support exists, otherwise an ATR-based fallback; room is sufficient when risk exceeds minimum tick size AND room >= risk times a minimum room-to-risk ratio
Calculation: Bullish score = 2 points if macro bullish, 1 if neither macro bullish nor bearish, otherwise 0; plus 2 if near support or bullish breakout; plus 1 if near VWAP or near EMA; plus 1 if RSI > midpoint; plus 1 if rising MACD histogram and MACD line > signal line; plus 1 if bullish trend-strength condition; plus 1 if bullish trigger; plus 1 if room is sufficient
Calculation: Bearish score = 2 points if macro bearish, 1 if neither macro bullish nor bearish, otherwise 0; plus 2 if near resistance or bearish breakout; plus 1 if near VWAP or near EMA; plus 1 if RSI < midpoint; plus 1 if falling MACD histogram and MACD line < signal line; plus 1 if bearish trend-strength condition; plus 1 if bearish trigger; plus 1 if room is sufficient
Calculation: Long target = the closer of close plus bullish risk times a target multiple and resistance, if resistance exists above close; otherwise close plus bullish risk times the target multiple; short target = the closer of close minus bearish risk times a target multiple and support, if support exists below close; otherwise close minus bearish risk times the target multiple
Long: completed bar AND within allowed session AND flat AND current bar index minus last signal bar > cooldown period AND bullish trigger AND bullish room is sufficient AND bullish score >= minimum score AND NOT bearish trigger
Short: completed bar AND within allowed session AND flat AND current bar index minus last signal bar > cooldown period AND bearish trigger AND bearish room is sufficient AND bearish score >= minimum score AND NOT bullish trigger
Exit: long, completed bar after entry bar AND (low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target OR (close < entry price AND falling MACD histogram) OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period OR outside allowed session)
Exit: short, completed bar after entry bar AND (high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target OR (close > entry price AND rising MACD histogram) OR bars since entry >= maximum holding period OR outside allowed session)
