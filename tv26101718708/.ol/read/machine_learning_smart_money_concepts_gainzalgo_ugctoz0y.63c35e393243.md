Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar high greater than the highs of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; usable after those later bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar low lower than the lows of the configured number of chart bars before and after it; usable after those later bars complete
Long: close > last confirmed swing high AND trend state is neutral or bearish
Short: close < last confirmed swing low AND trend state is neutral or bullish
