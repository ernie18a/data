Calculation: OFPI = T3-smoothed value of sum(volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low + ε) − 1)) / sum(volume), summed over a configurable lookback; use configurable T3 length and volume factor.
Calculation: Momentum shift = (previous OFPI > OFPI two bars ago AND current OFPI < previous OFPI) OR (previous OFPI < OFPI two bars ago AND current OFPI > previous OFPI).
Calculation: Harmonic price score = tanh(weighted sum of close-to-close returns over a configurable lookback, weighted by the Möbius function μ(n)/n^s, divided by the sum of absolute weights); return at lag n is (close[n−1] − close[n])/close[n].
Calculation: Strategy agreement = count among the first configurable number of strategies with signal > 0.2 or signal < −0.2; bullish agreement if bullish count reaches the required count, bearish agreement if bearish count reaches it, otherwise neutral. Strategies in order: (1) (RSI(14) − 50)/50; (2) −(close − SMA(close,20))/(2 × standard deviation(close,20)); (3) OFPI × 1.5 when momentum shifts, otherwise OFPI; (4) (ADX(14) − 25)/25; (5) (higher-timeframe RSI(14) − 50)/50. Strategy confidence is the absolute signal, except flow confidence is min(1, 1.3 × abs(OFPI)) during a momentum shift. The composite signal is the confidence-weighted mean of the selected strategies, multiplied by (1 + local trend direction × trend-mixing weight) and (1 + higher-timeframe trend direction × the same weight), then clamped to [−1,1].
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend direction = 1 when higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe SMA(50), −1 when below, otherwise 0; higher-timeframe RSI(14) and ATR(14) are also calculated on that higher timeframe. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Unified score = 10 × clamp(asymmetrically amplified weighted score, −1, 1). Before amplification, weighted score = harmonic price score × its weight + structure score × its weight + composite signal × its weight + correspondence score × its weight. Structure score is percentile rank of ATR(14) over the price-volume correlation lookback, divided by 100; negate it when ATR(14) > SMA(ATR(14),20) × 1.2, and make it negative absolute when higher-timeframe trend is bearish. Correspondence score = (1 − abs(harmonic price score − correlation(close, volume, lookback))) × sign(composite signal). For positive weighted score, multiply by 1.5, additionally by 1.2 when there is no volatility stress, by 1.3 when OFPI > 0.1, and by 1.15 when higher-timeframe trend is bullish. For negative weighted score, multiply by 1.5, additionally by 1.2 during volatility stress, by 1.3 when OFPI < −0.1, and by 1.15 when higher-timeframe trend is bearish. The four score weights and the price-volume correlation lookback are configurable.
Calculation: Local trend direction = 1 when close > SMA(close,50), −1 when below, otherwise 0.
Long: Flat position AND more than 10 chart bars since the previous entry AND ((ATR(14) > SMA(ATR(14),50) × 0.8 AND higher-timeframe ATR(14) > higher-timeframe SMA(ATR(14),20) × 0.8 AND (local trend direction ≠ 0 OR higher-timeframe trend direction ≠ 0) AND unified score > 0.6 × score threshold AND (strategy agreement is bullish OR neutral)) OR (OFPI > 0.15 AND composite signal > 0.2) OR (bullish engulfing OR morning star OR three white soldiers) AND unified score > 0.3 × score threshold AND OFPI > 0 OR (higher-timeframe trend direction > 0 AND momentum shift AND OFPI > 0) OR (OFPI > 0.3 AND unified score > 0).
Short: Flat position AND more than 10 chart bars since the previous entry AND ((ATR(14) > SMA(ATR(14),50) × 0.8 AND higher-timeframe ATR(14) > higher-timeframe SMA(ATR(14),20) × 0.8 AND (local trend direction ≠ 0 OR higher-timeframe trend direction ≠ 0) AND unified score < −0.8 × score threshold AND (strategy agreement is bearish OR neutral)) OR (OFPI < −0.15 AND composite signal < −0.2) OR (bearish engulfing OR three black crows OR evening star) AND unified score < −0.4 × score threshold AND OFPI < 0 OR (higher-timeframe trend direction < 0 AND momentum shift AND OFPI < 0) OR (OFPI < −0.3 AND unified score < 0).
Exit: Long, unified score < 1 OR NOT (ATR(14) > SMA(ATR(14),50) × 0.8 AND higher-timeframe ATR(14) > higher-timeframe SMA(ATR(14),20) × 0.8 AND (local trend direction ≠ 0 OR higher-timeframe trend direction ≠ 0)) OR (OFPI < −0.15 AND abs(OFPI) > abs(previous OFPI)).
Exit: Short, unified score > −1 OR NOT (ATR(14) > SMA(ATR(14),50) × 0.8 AND higher-timeframe ATR(14) > higher-timeframe SMA(ATR(14),20) × 0.8 AND (local trend direction ≠ 0 OR higher-timeframe trend direction ≠ 0)) OR (OFPI > 0.15 AND abs(OFPI) > abs(previous OFPI)).
