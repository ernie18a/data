Calculation: Bull/bear probability = corresponding directional points divided by the sum of bull and bear points, expressed as a percentage and rounded; points increase for aligned local, higher-timeframe, and intermediate-timeframe EMA trends; recent same-direction sweep, BOS, CHOCH, internal shift, displacement, IFVG, Fibonacci pullback zone, acceptance/rejection, EMA expansion, nearby opposing-side liquidity, session boost, and aligned market matrix; points decrease for weak momentum, opposing RSI divergence, opposing matrix, and weak matrix trend. Confirmed momentum requires a recent displacement candle, fast EMA slope in the same direction, and close on the same side of the fast EMA. The market matrix compares 9/21, 21/50, and 50/200 EMAs; RSI to 50; MACD to its signal and histogram to zero; directional indicators to each other; MFI to 50; OBV to its prior value; and Supertrend direction, then divides directional counts by their sum. Use a higher timeframe's last completed bar for the higher-timeframe and intermediate-timeframe trends and market matrix.
Calculation: Bull/bear A+ score = sum of weighted directional confirmations: intermediate-timeframe trend, recent same-direction sweep, any recent same-direction BOS/CHOCH/internal shift, same-direction EMA expansion or confirmed momentum, same-direction acceptance or rejection, recent same-direction displacement or confirmed momentum, and a held EMA retest; add aligned matrix and strong-trend boosts and subtract opposing-matrix and weak-trend penalties. Clamp to 0–100; cap at the no-sweep limit when required and no same-direction sweep is recent; cap at the divergence and chase limits when applicable.
Calculation: Recent sweep, BOS, CHOCH, internal shift, momentum, IFVG, Fibonacci-zone touch, EMA reclaim/rejection, and divergence = that event occurred within its respective validity lookback. Bullish/bearish sweep = price trades below the last confirmed pivot low/high and closes back above/below it. BOS = close breaks the last confirmed pivot high/low; CHOCH = BOS in the direction opposite the prior structure direction. Internal shift = close breaks the last confirmed shorter-lookback pivot high/low. Displacement = candle body exceeds ATR times its displacement threshold in the matching direction. Bullish/bearish IFVG = close crosses above the top of the latest active bearish gap / below the bottom of the latest active bullish gap; a bullish gap has current low above the high two bars earlier, and a bearish gap has current high below the low two bars earlier, each wider than the ATR gap threshold. A bullish/bearish Fibonacci zone is the overlap of the 50% and 75% retracement levels of the latest same-direction BOS leg. Divergence = price makes a higher high/lower low while RSI makes a lower high/higher low, using confirmed pivots. Held bullish/bearish EMA retest = a recent close crossing above/below the fast EMA, followed by a bar whose low/high touches the EMA and closes above/below it with bullish/bearish candle body.
