Calculation: Mapped level = confirmed external swing high/low, equal pivot level, unfilled three-bar fair value gap midpoint, preceding opposite-colour candle low/high before a displacement bar that breaks the latest internal pivot, or previous day/week high/low from the last completed higher-timeframe bar; merge nearby factors using a tolerance based on chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Bullish flow = cumulative sum of signed candle body position within its range, multiplied by volume when available; bearish/bullish divergence compares flow at successive confirmed external pivots.
Calculation: Wick threshold = 75th percentile of the corresponding wick over a rolling lookback; displacement threshold = 85th percentile of candle body over a rolling lookback; volume and range thresholds = 95th and 90th percentiles over rolling lookbacks.
Calculation: Squeeze = Bollinger Bands wholly inside Keltner Channels; squeeze release = bands leave the channels after at least five consecutive squeeze bars.
Long: confirmed bar close AND sufficient history for regime calibration AND chart-timeframe ATR > 0 AND the same event class has not fired within the outcome horizon AND ((price sweeps below the nearest mapped level below close within three ATR and closes above it with lower wick at or above its threshold) OR (price makes a lower confirmed external pivot low while cumulative flow makes a higher low) OR (close breaks above the latest confirmed external swing high) OR (volume and range meet their rolling percentile thresholds, or when volume is unavailable range and body meet their thresholds, AND close > open) OR (squeeze releases AND close > Bollinger basis) OR ((bullish engulfing candle OR bullish pin bar) AND close is within the mapped-level merge tolerance of a mapped level)).
Short: confirmed bar close AND sufficient history for regime calibration AND chart-timeframe ATR > 0 AND the same event class has not fired within the outcome horizon AND ((price sweeps above the nearest mapped level above close within three ATR and closes below it with upper wick at or above its threshold) OR (price makes a higher confirmed external pivot high while cumulative flow makes a lower high) OR (close breaks below the latest confirmed external swing low) OR (volume and range meet their rolling percentile thresholds, or when volume is unavailable range and body meet their thresholds, AND close <= open) OR (squeeze releases AND close <= Bollinger basis) OR ((bearish engulfing candle OR bearish pin bar) AND close is within the mapped-level merge tolerance of a mapped level)).
