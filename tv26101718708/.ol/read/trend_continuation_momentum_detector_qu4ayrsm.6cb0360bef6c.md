Calculation: Heiken Ashi OHLC = HA close is (open+high+low+close)/4; initial HA open is (open+close)/2, later HA open is the prior HA open and close average; HA high/low are the maximum/minimum of real high/low and HA open/close.
Calculation: Momentum score = the weighted average of five 0–100 scores: HA body divided by its average over a lookback, ×50 and capped at 100; opposing wick quality, 100−100×(opposing wick/HA body), floored at 0; absolute HA-close EMA slope over a lookback, divided by ATR and multiplied by sensitivity, capped at 100; volume divided by its average, ×50 and capped at 100; and an EMA-distance z-score score that rises linearly from 0 at |z|=0 to 100 at |z|=2, falls to 60 at |z|=3, then falls by 30 per additional z-score unit to a floor of 0. Normalize by the sum of the configurable weights.
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of the volume-weighted variance of typical price (high+low+close)/3, accumulated from the current session start.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe delta = sum of volume for lower-timeframe bars closing above their open minus sum for bars closing below their open, using completed bars within the current chart bar; if unavailable, use +1 when chart close>open, −1 when close<open, otherwise 0. The lower timeframe is shorter than the chart timeframe.

Long: session bars≥the minimum AND VWAP standard deviation/close×100≥the minimum AND momentum score<the maximum AND (score-fading filter disabled OR current score<prior score) AND (close−VWAP)/VWAP standard deviation≤−the threshold AND current HA body<its average over a lookback AND prior HA candle was bearish AND current real high≥prior HA body high AND current real low≤prior HA body low
Short: HA close<HA open AND HA close<EMA of HA close AND close<VWAP AND momentum score≥the bias threshold
Short: momentum score>the strong-signal threshold AND HA close<HA open AND upper HA wick≤HA body×wick tolerance AND HA body>its average over a lookback AND HA close<EMA of HA close AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close<VWAP) AND (delta filter disabled OR lower-timeframe delta<0) AND prior state was not strong sell AND sell-side EMA reset is ready
Short: session bars≥the minimum AND VWAP standard deviation/close×100≥the minimum AND momentum score<the maximum AND (score-fading filter disabled OR current score<prior score) AND (close−VWAP)/VWAP standard deviation≥the threshold AND current HA body<its average over a lookback AND prior HA candle was bullish AND current real high≥prior HA body high AND current real low≤prior HA body low
Exit: long, prior state was strong buy AND current state is neutral or sell
Exit: short, prior state was strong sell AND current state is neutral or buy
Exit: long, close<active long stop (set to next bar open minus the signal bar HA range times the stop multiplier)
Exit: short, close>active short stop (set to next bar open plus the signal bar HA range times the stop multiplier)
