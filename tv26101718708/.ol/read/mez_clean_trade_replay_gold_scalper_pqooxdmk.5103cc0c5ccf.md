Calculation: Liquidity sweep = low below the previous lookback-period low and close back above it (bullish); high above the previous lookback-period high and close back below it (bearish)
Calculation: Golden-pocket zone = close within the 61.8%–65% retracement zone of the lookback-period high–low range, widened by the configured ATR buffer
Calculation: Bullish momentum = close above fast EMA and VWAP, RSI above 50, MACD line above signal line, and ADX above its configured minimum; bearish momentum is the inverse
Calculation: Quality score = weighted sum of higher-timeframe trend, liquidity sweep or its disabled requirement, golden-pocket zone or its disabled requirement, DXY support, EMA stack, momentum, and session eligibility; use the configured direction-specific weights and mode-specific minimum threshold
Calculation: Previous-day levels = high and low of the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Stop = configured fixed or ATR-based distance from entry, unless the prior lookback-period low (long) or high (short), adjusted by the configured buffer, is beyond entry; in that case use that adjusted swing level
Calculation: Targets = configured fixed or ATR-based distances from entry, in the position direction, for TP1, TP2, and TP3
Calculation: Best price = highest high since entry for a long position or lowest low since entry for a short position
Long: bullish liquidity sweep OR (flat position AND bar closed AND outside configured UTC news windows AND session filter disabled or in a configured session AND higher-timeframe filter disabled or last completed higher-timeframe close above its 200-bar EMA AND DXY filter disabled, soft, unavailable, or last completed DXY close below its EMA AND liquidity requirement disabled or bullish liquidity sweep AND Fibonacci requirement disabled or close in bullish golden-pocket zone AND chase filter disabled or distance from close to fast EMA no greater than configured ATR multiple AND previous-day filter disabled or close outside the configured buffer around previous-day high AND bullish quality score at or above its mode-specific threshold AND bullish quality score greater than bearish quality score AND (bullish liquidity sweep OR close crosses above fast EMA while above mid EMA OR close above the prior 10-bar high with bullish momentum))
Short: bearish liquidity sweep OR (flat position AND bar closed AND outside configured UTC news windows AND session filter disabled or in a configured session AND higher-timeframe filter disabled or last completed higher-timeframe close below its 200-bar EMA AND DXY filter disabled, soft, unavailable, or last completed DXY close above its EMA AND liquidity requirement disabled or bearish liquidity sweep AND Fibonacci requirement disabled or close in bearish golden-pocket zone AND chase filter disabled or distance from close to fast EMA no greater than configured ATR multiple AND previous-day filter disabled or close outside the configured buffer around previous-day low AND bearish quality score at or above its mode-specific threshold AND bearish quality score greater than bullish quality score AND (bearish liquidity sweep OR close crosses below fast EMA while below mid EMA OR close below the prior 10-bar low with bearish momentum))
Exit: long, after entry bar, low at or below stop OR high at or above TP3 OR (TP1 reached AND close at or below best price minus configured pullback OR bar closed and (close below fast EMA OR RSI below 50 OR MACD line below signal line))
Exit: short, after entry bar, high at or above stop OR low at or below TP3 OR (TP1 reached AND close at or above best price plus configured pullback OR bar closed and (close above fast EMA OR RSI above 50 OR MACD line above signal line))
