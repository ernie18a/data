Calculation: ORAN close = For each of four higher timeframes, calculate K as the short-term SMA of Stochastic RSI (RSI lookback, then Stochastic lookback); calculate D as the longer-term SMA of K; sum K + D for the timeframe’s close; average the four sums and divide by 8. Use each higher timeframe’s bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Filtered local low/high = A pivot low/high in ORAN close, confirmed after the same lookback number of bars on the right as on the left.
Long: Filtered local low AND previous accepted pivot is a high AND previous high − local low ≥ minimum difference
Short: Filtered local high AND previous accepted pivot is a low AND local high − previous low ≥ minimum difference
Long: The last three filtered pivots are low, high, low AND the first-to-third confirmation-bar span ≤ cluster window
Short: The last three filtered pivots are high, low, high AND the first-to-third confirmation-bar span ≤ cluster window
