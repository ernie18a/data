Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-period centers = average of EMA(close) and SMA(close) at each period, with short < medium < long
Calculation: Squeeze = abs(short-period EMA − short-period SMA) / short-period center × 100 ≤ short-band threshold AND abs(medium-period center − short-period center) / short-period center × 100 ≤ medium-distance threshold AND abs(long-period center − short-period center) / short-period center × 100 ≤ long-distance threshold
Calculation: Recent squeeze = a squeeze occurred on the current bar or within the configured monitoring-bar count before it
Calculation: Volume spike = current volume ≥ configured multiplier × the average volume of the configured number of immediately preceding bars AND current volume ≥ configured minimum volume
Long: Recent squeeze AND Volume spike AND close > the maximum of the short-, medium-, and long-period EMA and SMA values AND close > open AND abs(close − open) ≥ configured body-to-shadow multiplier × (high − max(close, open)) AND current bar index − prior accepted long-signal bar index > configured same-direction ignore-bar count
Short: Recent squeeze AND Volume spike AND close < the minimum of the short-, medium-, and long-period EMA and SMA values AND close < open AND abs(close − open) ≥ configured body-to-shadow multiplier × (min(close, open) − low) AND current bar index − prior accepted short-signal bar index > configured same-direction ignore-bar count
