Calculation: Swing high = higher high of an adjacent bullish bar followed by a bearish bar on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Swing low = lower low of an adjacent bearish bar followed by a bullish bar on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Liquidity levels = highs and lows of opposite-color candle pairs on same-or-higher timeframes, using completed bars; highs use bullish-then-bearish pairs and lows bearish-then-bullish pairs. Also include completed configured session highs and lows, defined by session extrema or the same swing-pair rules, according to the selected session mode.
Calculation: 1H/4H bias = persistent state, initially neutral; bullish when the latest completed bar close on that timeframe is above its latest swing high, bearish when below its latest swing low, otherwise unchanged. Use completed 1H and 4H bars, whether each is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Combined bias = 1H bias if 1H and 4H biases agree; otherwise 4H bias if non-neutral, else 1H bias.
Long: A sell-side liquidity level is swept (low < level OR close returns above the level after a prior close below it within the double-close window) AND a chart-timeframe swing high exists AND close > that swing high within the ChoCh window after the sweep AND (invalidation is disabled OR close is at least the sweep-bar low) OR 1H bias is bullish OR 4H bias is bullish OR combined bias is bullish
Short: A buy-side liquidity level is swept (high > level OR close returns below the level after a prior close above it within the double-close window) AND a chart-timeframe swing low exists AND close < that swing low within the ChoCh window after the sweep AND (invalidation is disabled OR close is at most the sweep-bar high) OR 1H bias is bearish OR 4H bias is bearish OR combined bias is bearish
