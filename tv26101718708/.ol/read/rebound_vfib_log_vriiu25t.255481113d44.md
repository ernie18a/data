Calculation: V-shape = highest high within the lookback window (top), the lowest low from that top to the current bar (bottom), and the highest high above the log-price line from bottom to top (middle top); require minimum top-to-bottom and bottom-to-current bar counts
Calculation: Uptrend rate = the minimum log-price slope from the V-bottom low to any later low, annualized to percent per trading day; downtrend rate = the log-price slope from the V-bottom to the middle top, annualized to percent per trading day; V-trend = uptrend rate + downtrend rate
Calculation: V-Fib ratio = (log(price) − log-price base line from V-bottom to top) / (maximum log-price distance of highs above that base line / 0.236)
Calculation: Lead gain = (log(price) − log-price uptrend line from V-bottom using the uptrend rate) / uptrend rate per day × uptrend rate
Calculation: Technical Rating = TradingView aggregate Technical Rating
Calculation: Support line = the steepest log-price line through two lows after the V-bottom, with slope greater than the uptrend slope and no subsequent low below the line within the lookback window; support trail gap = the greater of the maximum peak-to-low drawdown since support began and the minimum support trail; support trail stop = support line price × (1 − support trail gap)
Calculation: Support rebound armed = after V-Fib falls from its peak by at least peak × minimum support pullback, V-Fib rises from the resulting trough by at least minimum support rebound; reset peak/trough tracking when the V-bottom or top changes
Calculation: Trend entry lock = set on trend entry; clear when V-Fib reaches the unlock ratio or the rate-based exit condition is met
Calculation: Active trailing stop = highest price since entry × (1 − entry trail gap); entry trail gap is the greater of the distance from entry to the V-bottom (trend entry) or support trail stop (support entry), capped by the corresponding maximum trail
Long: No open position AND trend entry lock is clear AND valid V-shape AND uptrend rate ≥ minimum uptrend rate AND downtrend rate ≤ maximum downtrend rate AND V-trend ≥ minimum V-trend AND Technical Rating ≥ minimum entry rating AND a lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar has high V-Fib ratio between minimum and maximum entry ratios inclusive AND lead gain computed from that bar’s low ≥ minimum entry lead gain
Long: No open position AND no qualifying trend-entry lower-timeframe bar in the current chart bar AND support entry enabled AND Technical Rating ≥ minimum entry rating AND support trail stop exists AND support rebound armed AND a lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar has high V-Fib ratio > maximum entry ratio
Long: No entry on the current chart bar AND support trail stop exists AND current-bar V-Fib ratio > maximum entry V-Fib ratio AND support rebound armed AND Technical Rating ≥ minimum entry rating
Exit: Long, low ≤ active trailing stop OR uptrend rate < keep uptrend rate AND uptrend rate < entry uptrend rate + exit uptrend difference OR V-Fib ratio ≥ exit V-Fib ratio when V-Fib exits are enabled
