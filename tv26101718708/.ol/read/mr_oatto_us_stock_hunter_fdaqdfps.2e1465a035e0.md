Calculation: EMA fast, medium, slow, filter, major = exponential moving averages with configured lookbacks; fast < medium < slow < major, and filter is separately configured and may equal slow. Use Daily bars: the current still-forming Daily bar when Daily is higher than the chart timeframe; otherwise the latest available completed Daily bar.
Calculation: Trend regime = classify in this order: Super Bullish if close > fast EMA and fast ≥ medium ≥ slow ≥ filter ≥ major; Bullish if fast ≥ medium ≥ slow ≥ filter ≥ major; Sideway Up if (fast ≥ medium ≥ slow ≥ filter and close > major) or (close > major and close ≥ filter); Sideway Down if close < filter and close > major and not (fast ≤ medium ≤ slow ≤ filter); Super Bear if close < major; Downtrend if close < filter; otherwise Sideway. Bullish regime = Super Bullish, Bullish, or Sideway Up. Bearish regime = Downtrend, Super Bear, or Sideway Down.
Calculation: Stock type = 1 if close > major and no Daily close below major within the configured reversal lookback; 2 if close > filter and a Daily close was below major within that lookback; 4 if close < filter and a new low occurred within the configured new-low lookback; 3 if close < filter otherwise; 0 otherwise. New low = Daily low below the lowest low of the preceding configured new-low lookback bars.
Calculation: Chase risk = any of: in a bullish regime, (close − slow EMA) / Daily ATR exceeds its configured threshold (or (close − slow EMA) / slow EMA exceeds its configured percentage threshold, according to the selected extension method); in a bearish regime, (slow EMA − close) / Daily ATR exceeds its configured threshold (or (slow EMA − close) / slow EMA exceeds its configured percentage threshold); trend-regime age reaches its configured threshold; or swing-leg count reaches its configured threshold. Swing-leg count resets when the regime changes, then counts confirmed Daily pivot highs in bullish regimes or confirmed Daily pivot lows in bearish regimes, using configured left and right pivot bars.
Long: bullish regime AND not below filter EMA AND not below major EMA AND not chase risk AND stock type is not 3 or 4 AND not cut warning; OR bullish regime now and not bullish regime on the prior bar; OR bullish regime AND close ≥ filter EMA AND Daily low ≤ fast EMA AND Daily high ≥ fast EMA.
Short: bearish regime AND close < filter EMA AND not chase risk AND stock type is not 3 or 4 AND not cut warning; OR bearish regime now and not bearish regime on the prior bar.
Exit: long, Daily close below filter EMA for at least the configured consecutive-day threshold.
Exit: short, Daily close below filter EMA for at least the configured consecutive-day threshold.
