Calculation: Gap state = At the first chart bar of each new calendar day, if the absolute percentage difference between its open and the previous chart bar's close meets the minimum gap size, set an active gap: a gap up has top = open and bottom = previous close; a gap down has top = previous close and bottom = open. For a gap up, fill depth = (top − current low) / (top − bottom) × 100; for a gap down, fill depth = (current high − bottom) / (top − bottom) × 100. Mark partial fill seen once depth reaches the partial-fill threshold; deactivate the gap once depth reaches 99%. A new qualifying daily gap replaces the previous gap.
Long: (previous bar is inside the bar two bars ago AND current close > previous bar high AND current close > EMA) OR (current close > highest high over the longer lookback excluding the current bar AND previous close <= that highest high AND current volume / SMA(volume, lookback) >= relative-volume threshold) OR (active gap is down AND partial fill has been seen AND current close > current open AND current close > gap bottom) OR (current bar high > previous bar high AND current bar low < previous bar low AND abs(current close − current open) / (current high − current low) × 100 >= minimum body percentage AND current close > current open AND current close >= previous bar high); AND volume >= minimum volume AND current volume / SMA(volume, lookback) >= relative-volume threshold AND current high − current low >= ATR × minimum range multiplier AND current close >= minimum price.
Short: (previous bar is inside the bar two bars ago AND current close < previous bar low AND current close < EMA) OR (active gap is up AND current close < current open AND current high < gap top AND current low > gap bottom) OR (current bar high > previous bar high AND current bar low < previous bar low AND abs(current close − current open) / (current high − current low) × 100 >= minimum body percentage AND current close < current open AND current close <= previous bar low); AND volume >= minimum volume AND current volume / SMA(volume, lookback) >= relative-volume threshold AND current high − current low >= ATR × minimum range multiplier AND current close >= minimum price AND current close < EMA
