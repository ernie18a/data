Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume multiplied by a per-bar sign: either 2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1 when high > low, or 0 when high = low; alternatively, +1 when close > prior close, −1 when close < prior close, or 0 when equal, according to the sign mode.
Calculation: CVD slope = CVD − EMA(CVD).
Calculation: Bullish pivot confluence = a confirmed CVD pivot low and the latest confirmed price pivot is a low whose confirmation was no more than the alignment window bars ago; bearish pivot confluence = a confirmed CVD pivot high and the latest confirmed price pivot is a high whose confirmation was no more than the alignment window bars ago. Price and CVD pivots use the same lookback on each side and are confirmed on the current chart timeframe.
Long: CVD slope > 0 AND close crosses above the reclaim EMA AND (confluence-only mode is disabled OR bullish pivot confluence) AND position side is not long AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown bars AND the bar is confirmed AND (session restriction is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session).
Short: CVD slope < 0 AND close crosses below the reclaim EMA AND (confluence-only mode is disabled OR bearish pivot confluence) AND position side is not short AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown bars AND the bar is confirmed AND (session restriction is disabled OR datetime is within the configured session).
