Calculation: Chaikin Oscillator = short-term EMA of the cumulative Accumulation/Distribution Line minus its long-term EMA; the line adds volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low) each bar
Long: Chaikin Oscillator crosses above 0 AND close > long-term SMA of close
Short: Chaikin Oscillator crosses below 0 AND close < long-term SMA of close
Exit: Long, low <= signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= signal-bar close + ATR × target multiplier
Exit: Short, high >= signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= signal-bar close − ATR × target multiplier
