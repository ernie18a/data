Calculation: Model probability = percentage of positive labels among the K nearest historical bars in six dimensions (Choppiness Index, price velocity plus half its acceleration, VWAP deviation divided by close-price standard deviation, RSI, ATR deviation from its moving average divided by ATR standard deviation, and volume divided by its moving average); min-max normalize each dimension over the model lookback; label each bar positive if its close change over the label horizon exceeds the ATR noise threshold, negative if below the negative threshold, otherwise neutral; compare current dimensions with historical dimensions using the sum of log(1 + absolute difference) across dimensions; weight each neighbor by exp(−distance² / bandwidth), and calculate positive weight as a percentage of positive and negative weights.
Calculation: Smoothed model probability = 3-bar weighted moving average of model probability.
Long: Smoothed model probability is at or above the bullish threshold and at or below the upper exhaustion threshold AND the shorter-period EMA of close crosses above the longer-period EMA of close.
Short: Smoothed model probability is at or below the bearish threshold and at or above the lower exhaustion threshold AND the shorter-period EMA of close crosses below the longer-period EMA of close.
Exit: Long, smoothed model probability crosses above the upper exhaustion threshold.
Exit: Short, smoothed model probability crosses below the lower exhaustion threshold.
