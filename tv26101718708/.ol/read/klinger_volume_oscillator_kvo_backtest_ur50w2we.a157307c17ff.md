Calculation: Klinger volume oscillator = EMA of signed volume with a shorter lookback minus EMA of signed volume with a longer lookback; signed volume = volume when current (high + low + close) / 3 > previous bar's (high + low + close) / 3, otherwise −volume
Calculation: Trigger = EMA of the Klinger volume oscillator
Long: Klinger volume oscillator > Trigger
Short: Klinger volume oscillator < Trigger
