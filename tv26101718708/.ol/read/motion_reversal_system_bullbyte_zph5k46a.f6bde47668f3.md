Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute net close displacement over the directional lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback; retain its sign.
Calculation: Adaptive efficiency threshold = the greater of a base floor and the rolling mean of absolute efficiency ratio plus a scaled rolling standard deviation.
Calculation: Confirmed direction = bullish when efficiency ratio exceeds the adaptive threshold, bearish when it is below the negative threshold, and neutral otherwise; require absolute net displacement to meet a minimum ATR multiple.
Calculation: Commitment = signed candle body fraction of true range multiplied by capped relative volume when usable volume is selected; otherwise, candle direction multiplied by twice the absolute distance from close to bar midpoint divided by true range. Relative volume is volume divided by its rolling average.
Calculation: Trend commitment baseline = mean and standard deviation of prior commitment observations aligned with the confirmed trend direction; include only positive aligned observations.
Calculation: Failure conditions = (1) close is against the confirmed trend versus the previous close; (2) trend-side wick divided by absolute body is at least a threshold; (3) current commitment, signed by trend direction, is at least a threshold number of standard deviations below the trend commitment baseline; (4) true range is at least a threshold ATR multiple.
Calculation: Failure score = normalized velocity reversal strength multiplied by one plus normalized wick strength, one plus normalized commitment anomaly strength, and one plus normalized range strength.
Calculation: Target 2 = entry price plus (for long) or minus (for short) an ATR-scaled target distance multiplied by a score-based target multiplier. Stop = entry price minus (for long) or plus (for short) an ATR-scaled stop distance.
Long: On a confirmed bar, enter at close when confirmed direction is bearish, the selected failure gate is met (all four conditions in Conservative mode, any three in Balanced mode, or condition 1 plus at least one other in Aggressive mode), there is no active trade, and cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a confirmed bar, enter at close when confirmed direction is bullish, the selected failure gate is met (all four conditions in Conservative mode, any three in Balanced mode, or condition 1 plus at least one other in Aggressive mode), there is no active trade, and cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, after the entry bar on a confirmed bar, high reaches Target 2 OR low reaches Stop; if both are reached on the same bar, exit at Stop.
Exit: short, after the entry bar on a confirmed bar, low reaches Target 2 OR high reaches Stop; if both are reached on the same bar, exit at Stop.
