Calculation: Rounded cup candidate = On each of several progressively longer lookbacks, find a central low and the highest price on each side; require valid depth, central position, recovery, both arms rising from the center, a rounded bottom, a U-shaped close-price fit, and acceptable rim matching. The inverted cup mirrors prices and directions. A handle is a shallow, upper-range pullback after a completed cup.
Calculation: Double-bottom/top candidate = The latest two confirmed low/high pivots have similar prices, sufficient separation and neckline height, a fresh intact second pivot, and price has moved toward the neckline by the required completion fraction. Classify each pivot as narrow or broad from the number of nearby bars close to its extreme.
Calculation: Selected candidate = The highest-scoring eligible candidate across cup lookbacks and the double-pivot families, subject to minimum score and direction-context filters. Prefer larger cup lookbacks with a scale bonus; admit short candidates only in net bearish context and reject a selected short in bullish context. On daily charts, a strong completed bullish cup from a higher timeframe may substitute when no local candidate qualifies. Higher-timeframe context uses requested bars without lookahead (last completed historically; possibly still forming in real time).
Calculation: Candidate entry and stop = For bullish cups, consider a fresh right-arm higher-low pivot, a non-breakout trend-support retest, a valid handle low, a bullish liquidity sweep below the prior rolling low that closes back above it, a neckline retest after a fresh breakout, and a fresh neckline breakout. Set each entry and its structural/ATR stop from that setup; keep only pairs with the stop on the losing side and risk within both percentage and ATR limits. Select the eligible pair with the lowest risk percentage plus its setup-specific ranking offset. Mirror the conditions and levels for bearish cups. Double-bottom/top candidates use the second pivot as an early entry before breakout or the neckline on retest, with a risk-valid stop.
Calculation: Displayed entry = For an unmissed formation-stage candidate, use its stored original pair. For a completed-or-later candidate, use neckline plus/minus an ATR offset, with the stop beyond both an ATR buffer and the recent swing extreme; require a correctly sided stop and risk within percentage and ATR limits. A triggered safe entry requires close at or beyond its entry in the trade direction. A later retest entry is allowed only for the same structure, within the distance limit, and with a risk-valid pair. The independent handle entry is valid on mini-pivot breakout or subsequent retest, provided its state and risk-valid pair qualify.
Calculation: Pattern target = Bullish neckline plus pattern depth times the target factor; bearish neckline minus pattern depth times the target factor.
Long: A selected active bullish cup, cup-with-handle, or double-bottom candidate meets the minimum score and remains valid with its target ahead.
Short: A selected active bearish inverted cup, inverted cup-with-handle, or double-top candidate meets the minimum score and remains valid with its target ahead and bearish context; reject it in bullish context.
Long: A bullish candidate has a valid unmissed early pair, a triggered safe entry, a valid same-structure retest entry, or a valid handle breakout/retest entry.
Short: A bearish candidate has a valid unmissed early pair, a triggered safe entry, a valid same-structure retest entry, or a valid handle breakout/retest entry.
Exit: long position, close <= applicable stop OR close >= pattern target.
Exit: short position, close >= applicable stop OR close <= pattern target.
