Calculation: Bullish CISD level = the highest high of the sweep bar and the consecutive bearish bars immediately before it, scanned back over the configured lookback
Calculation: Bearish CISD level = the lowest low of the sweep bar and the consecutive bullish bars immediately before it, scanned back over the configured lookback
Calculation: Equilibrium = the midpoint between the opposing confirmed pivot and the most extreme sweep price
Calculation: Long risk = long entry price − sweep low; short risk = sweep high − short entry price
Calculation: Long target = long entry price + long risk × configured risk multiple; short target = short entry price − short risk × configured risk multiple
Long: A chart-timeframe low < the latest confirmed pivot low from the chart timeframe (Tier 3), the higher mid timeframe (Tier 2), or the higher HTF (Tier 1; each uses its own pivot lookback), with no active hunt for that tier; after the sweep, close > the captured bullish CISD level with candle range ≥ the configured displacement multiple of 14-bar ATR; within the configured CISD timeout, a bullish FVG forms (current low > high two chart bars earlier) with its midpoint < Equilibrium; and, for Tier 3, a later retest has low ≤ FVG top and close ≥ FVG bottom (or a bullish retest of an inverted bearish FVG, after price closed above its top); long risk > 0 and opposing pivot high − long entry ≥ long risk × configured risk multiple; T1 has no parent gate, T2 additionally requires an unconsumed completed T1 long and an enabled session window, and T3 additionally requires an unconsumed completed T2 long and an enabled session window
Short: A chart-timeframe high > the latest confirmed pivot high from the chart timeframe (Tier 3), the higher mid timeframe (Tier 2), or the higher HTF (Tier 1; each uses its own pivot lookback), with no active hunt for that tier; after the sweep, close < the captured bearish CISD level with candle range ≥ the configured displacement multiple of 14-bar ATR; within the configured CISD timeout, a bearish FVG forms (current high < low two chart bars earlier) with its midpoint > Equilibrium; and, for Tier 3, a later retest has high ≥ FVG bottom and close ≤ FVG top (or a bearish retest of an inverted bullish FVG, after price closed below its bottom); short risk > 0 and short entry − opposing pivot low ≥ short risk × configured risk multiple; T1 has no parent gate, T2 additionally requires an unconsumed completed T1 short and an enabled session window, and T3 additionally requires an unconsumed completed T2 short and an enabled session window
Exit: long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ target price
Exit: short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ target price
