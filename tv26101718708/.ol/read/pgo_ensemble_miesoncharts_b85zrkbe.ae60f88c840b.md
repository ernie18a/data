Calculation: Member PGO state = For each length in the ensemble, calculate (close − selected moving average) / Wilder average true range over that same length. The moving average is the selected SMA, EMA, WMA, or volume-weighted moving average (use SMA when volume is unavailable). A member switches to long when its PGO exceeds the upper threshold, switches to short when it falls below the lower threshold, and otherwise holds its prior state; it starts neutral.
Calculation: Ensemble score = Sum of member states divided by the number of members; evaluate only after twice the longest member length in chart bars. Regime switches to up when the score reaches the positive trend threshold, switches to down when it reaches the negative trend threshold, and otherwise holds its prior state or becomes neutral if configured to go flat when split.
Long: Ensemble is ready AND score ≥ positive trend threshold AND prior regime ≠ up
Short: Ensemble is ready AND score ≤ negative trend threshold AND prior regime ≠ down
