Calculation: Swing highs and lows = highs and lows that are respectively higher and lower than the highs and lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; compare each new swing with the previous swing of the same kind to classify it as higher or lower.
Calculation: Structure direction = bullish after the upper break source exceeds the latest swing high, bearish after the lower break source falls below the latest swing low, and otherwise retains its prior direction; the upper break source is close when close-only breaks are enabled, otherwise high, and the lower break source is close when enabled, otherwise low. Each swing level can trigger a break only once, until a new swing of that kind is confirmed.
Calculation: Filtered structure direction = structure direction, except it is neutral when the direction is unknown or, if the mixed-swing filter is enabled, the latest swing high and low classifications conflict (higher high with lower low, or lower high with higher low).
Long: Filtered structure direction = bullish on any displayed timeframe OR the sum of filtered structure directions on the four configured higher timeframes > 0
Short: Filtered structure direction = bearish on any displayed timeframe OR the sum of filtered structure directions on the four configured higher timeframes < 0
