Calculation: Primary anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of the configured price source since each primary-anchor reset; use the source’s lower-timeframe volume-weighted average within each chart bar when lower-timeframe precision is selected.
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation = square root of the cumulative volume-weighted mean of squared price minus the square of the primary anchored VWAP.
Calculation: Deviation bands = primary anchored VWAP plus or minus each configured multiplier times VWAP standard deviation; band 1 uses the shorter multiplier and band 2 the longer multiplier.
Calculation: VWAP slope state = change in primary anchored VWAP over a configurable lookback, normalized by ATR; rising when above a positive threshold, falling when below its negative, otherwise flat. Limit the lookback to bars since the primary-anchor reset.
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of the configured price source since the start of the current week, using chart-timeframe bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses above primary anchored VWAP.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses below primary anchored VWAP.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close outside the primary-anchor reset and after the configured warm-up, with positive VWAP standard deviation and, if enabled, close above Weekly VWAP or Weekly VWAP unavailable; either (VWAP slope rising AND within the setup lookback a bar has low at or below primary anchored VWAP and above lower band 2 AND no close in the lookback is below lower band 1 AND current close is above primary anchored VWAP and open AND (lower wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range OR prior close is at or below primary anchored VWAP) AND this pullback setup is armed AND (upper band 2 minus close) / (close minus setup-window swing low minus ATR buffer) meets the configured minimum reward-to-risk, with both distances positive) OR (VWAP slope flat AND within the setup lookback a bar has low at or below lower band 2 AND current close is above lower band 2, below primary anchored VWAP, and above open AND (lower wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range OR prior close is at or below lower band 2) AND this mean-reversion setup is armed AND (primary anchored VWAP minus close) / (close minus setup-window swing low minus ATR buffer) meets the configured minimum reward-to-risk, with both distances positive). Apply the optional Weekly VWAP filter only when enabled. Rearm pullback after a close above upper band 1; rearm mean reversion after a close at or above primary anchored VWAP; reset both setups at each primary-anchor reset.
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close outside the primary-anchor reset and after the configured warm-up, with positive VWAP standard deviation and, if enabled, close below Weekly VWAP or Weekly VWAP unavailable; either (VWAP slope falling AND within the setup lookback a bar has high at or above primary anchored VWAP and below upper band 2 AND no close in the lookback is above upper band 1 AND current close is below primary anchored VWAP and open AND (upper wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range OR prior close is at or above primary anchored VWAP) AND this pullback setup is armed AND (close minus lower band 2) / (setup-window swing high plus ATR buffer minus close) meets the configured minimum reward-to-risk, with both distances positive) OR (VWAP slope flat AND within the setup lookback a bar has high at or above upper band 2 AND current close is below upper band 2, above primary anchored VWAP, and below open AND (upper wick is at least the configured fraction of bar range OR prior close is at or above upper band 2) AND this mean-reversion setup is armed AND (close minus primary anchored VWAP) / (setup-window swing high plus ATR buffer minus close) meets the configured minimum reward-to-risk, with both distances positive). Apply the optional Weekly VWAP filter only when enabled. Rearm pullback after a close below lower band 1; rearm mean reversion after a close at or below primary anchored VWAP; reset both setups at each primary-anchor reset.
