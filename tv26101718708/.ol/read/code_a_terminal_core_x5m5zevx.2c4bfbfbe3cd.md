Calculation: Bullish HMA condition = the Hull moving average is rising and is above its prior value plus the prior value’s change over the lookback
Calculation: Bearish HMA condition = the Hull moving average is falling and is below its prior value plus the prior value’s change over the lookback
Calculation: Wave oscillator and signal = apply an EMA channel oscillator to typical price, then compare it with its 3-bar simple moving average
Calculation: Wave difference = the wave oscillator minus its signal, rounded to one decimal place
Calculation: Money flow = the 60-bar simple moving average of ((close − open) / (high − low)) × 200, minus 2.25
Calculation: Short-lookback and long-lookback stochastic = 2-bar simple moving averages of the 40-bar and 80-bar price stochastic, respectively
Calculation: Trend score = smooth a score that adds 1 for each close above its close 11–20 bars ago and subtracts 1 otherwise
Calculation: Upward run = consecutive bars whose close exceeds the close 4 bars earlier; downward run = consecutive bars whose close is below the close 4 bars earlier
Long: current high > prior high AND (EMA(11) crosses above EMA(33) OR the wave oscillator crosses above its signal now or two bars ago) AND upward run ≤ its limit AND close > Parabolic SAR AND open > prior open AND close > prior close AND min(open, close) = low AND wave difference ≥ its positive threshold AND bullish HMA condition AND ((wave signal > 0 AND money flow > 0 AND wave signal < its upper band) OR (wave signal < 0 AND short-lookback stochastic > long-lookback stochastic AND wave signal < negative lower band)) AND (trend score is rising OR trend score is at or beyond either extreme) AND volume ≥ its minimum AND no prior long signal since the bullish HMA condition last became false
Short: current low < prior low AND (EMA(5) crosses below EMA(11) OR the wave oscillator crosses below its signal now or two bars ago) AND downward run ≤ its limit AND close < Parabolic SAR AND open < prior open AND close < prior close AND max(open, close) = high AND wave difference ≤ its negative threshold AND bearish HMA condition AND ((wave signal < 0 AND money flow ≤ 0 AND wave signal > its negative band) OR (wave signal > 0 AND short-lookback stochastic ≤ long-lookback stochastic AND wave signal > its upper band)) AND (trend score is falling OR trend score is at or beyond either extreme) AND volume ≥ its minimum AND no prior short signal since the bearish HMA condition last became false
Exit: long, close < open AND wave difference ≤ 0.2
Exit: short, close > open AND wave difference ≥ 0.2
