Calculation: Reference price = prior week's Wednesday 9:15 IST bar close, or prior week's Tuesday 9:15 IST bar close if the Wednesday price is unavailable
Long: Wednesday 9:15 IST AND Reference price is available AND close > Reference price
Short: Wednesday 9:15 IST AND Reference price is available AND close < Reference price
Exit: Long, low <= entry price minus a fixed stop distance OR day is not Wednesday AND hour = 15 AND minute >= 15
Exit: Short, high >= entry price plus a fixed stop distance OR day is not Wednesday AND hour = 15 AND minute >= 15
