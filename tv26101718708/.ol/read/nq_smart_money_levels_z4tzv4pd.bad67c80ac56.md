Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low confirmed after the same lookback length on both sides; each confirmed swing can be broken once
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, with the gap at least the minimum size
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, with the gap at least the minimum size
Long: close > VWAP AND close > EMA(200) AND EMA(50) > EMA(200) OR close > the last unbroken confirmed swing high OR bullish FVG
Short: close < VWAP AND close < EMA(200) AND EMA(50) < EMA(200) OR close < the last unbroken confirmed swing low OR bearish FVG
