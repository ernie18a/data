Calculation: Internal order block = On a close crossing above the current internal pivot high, create a bullish zone using the bar with the lowest parsed low since that pivot; on a close crossing below the current internal pivot low, create a bearish zone using the bar with the highest parsed high since that pivot. For bars whose range is at least twice the selected volatility measure (ATR or cumulative mean true range), parsed high is the bar low and parsed low is the bar high; otherwise they are the bar high and low. Remove a bearish zone when its mitigation price exceeds its high, and a bullish zone when its mitigation price falls below its low; mitigation price is the close or, respectively, the high or low. Internal pivots use the leg-change calculation. Bias is bullish when at least two of the latest three active zones are bullish, bearish when at least two are bearish, and neutral otherwise.
Long: close crosses above the current internal pivot high OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bullish on the chart timeframe OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bullish on 1H bars OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bullish on 5M bars
Short: close crosses below the current internal pivot low OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bearish on the chart timeframe OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bearish on 1H bars OR at least two of the latest three active internal order blocks are bearish on 5M bars
