Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the extreme across the configured number of bars before and after it. Chart swings use chart-timeframe bars; higher-timeframe swings use higher-timeframe bars and are available only after confirmation from completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Merged equal-high/low level = swings on the same side whose prices differ by no more than the configured tolerance times chart-timeframe ATR(14); use the higher price for equal highs and the lower price for equal lows.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an unhit sellside level from a confirmed chart swing low within its maximum age, or from a confirmed higher-timeframe swing low, has low < level and close > level (wick confirmation); OR, after that sweep, a later confirmed bar within the configured confirmation window has close > the sweep bar's high, with no intervening close < the sweep bar's low (candle-break confirmation).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an unhit buyside level from a confirmed chart swing high within its maximum age, or from a confirmed higher-timeframe swing high, has high > level and close < level (wick confirmation); OR, after that sweep, a later confirmed bar within the configured confirmation window has close < the sweep bar's low, with no intervening close > the sweep bar's high (candle-break confirmation).
