Calculation: Previous-bar highest high = highest high over the longer lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Previous-bar lowest low = lowest low over the same lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Trend Surfers trailing stop line = while in a long state, the greater of the current close minus ATR times a multiplier and the prior long trailing stop; while in a short state, the lesser of the current close plus ATR times a multiplier and the prior short trailing stop; undefined when flat. Enter the long state when close crosses above the previous-bar highest high, or the short state when close crosses below the previous-bar lowest low. Reset the state when the opposite breakout occurs or price touches the tighter of the fixed ATR stop and trailing stop.
Calculation: Smoothed +DI and -DI = apply Wilder smoothing of length L to positive and negative directional movement, respectively, divided by Wilder-smoothed true range of length L; apply the indicator's lag-free smoothing of length 3 to each result. Positive directional movement is the current high increase when it exceeds both zero and the magnitude of the low decrease, otherwise zero; negative directional movement is the magnitude of the low decrease when it exceeds both zero and the high increase, otherwise zero. Lag-free smoothing applies three successive pairs of EMAs of the same length, each pair transformed as 1.5 times the first EMA minus 0.5 times the second.
Long: Trend Surfers trailing stop line < previous-bar lowest low AND close > prior close AND smoothed +DI > smoothed -DI
Short: Trend Surfers trailing stop line > previous-bar highest high AND close < prior close AND smoothed +DI < smoothed -DI
Exit: Long, close < entry price times (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high reaches entry price plus a take-profit percentage of entry price
Exit: Short, close > entry price times (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low reaches entry price minus a take-profit percentage of entry price
