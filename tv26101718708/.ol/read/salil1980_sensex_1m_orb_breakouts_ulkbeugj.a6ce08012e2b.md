Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low of the first one-minute bar of each day
Calculation: Hourly RSI = RSI calculated on higher-timeframe bars, using the bar still forming
Long: Close crosses above the opening range high OR (close crosses above VWAP AND RSI > 50) OR (RSI crosses above 50 AND close > VWAP) OR hourly RSI > 50
Short: Close crosses below the opening range low OR (close crosses below VWAP AND RSI < 50) OR hourly RSI < 50
