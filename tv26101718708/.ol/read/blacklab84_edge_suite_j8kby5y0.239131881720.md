Calculation: Hull MA = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half-length) − WMA(close, full-length), square-root-length)
Calculation: RSI divergence = On each confirmed price pivot, compare it with the preceding price pivot of the same type and compare RSI at those pivot bars; bullish when price makes a lower pivot low and RSI a higher pivot low, bearish when price makes a higher pivot high and RSI a lower pivot high. Confirm pivots using the same lookback on both sides.
Calculation: RSI(9) divergence = RSI divergence using RSI length 9 instead of RSI length 14.
Calculation: CMO divergence = CMO = 100 × (sum of positive close changes over the lookback − sum of absolute negative close changes over the lookback) / (sum of positive close changes + sum of absolute negative close changes). On confirmed price pivots, compare the current pivot with any of the three preceding same-type pivots; bullish when price makes a lower pivot low and CMO a higher pivot low, bearish when price makes a higher pivot high and CMO a lower pivot high. Use a shorter pivot lookback than for RSI divergence.
Calculation: Bullish hidden RSI divergence = On confirmed pivot lows, current price pivot low is higher than the preceding pivot low and RSI at the current pivot is lower than RSI at the preceding pivot.
Calculation: Bearish hidden RSI divergence = On confirmed pivot highs, current price pivot high is lower than the preceding pivot high and RSI at the current pivot is higher than RSI at the preceding pivot.
Calculation: VWAP bands = Daily-reset VWAP of hlc3, with upper and lower bands at VWAP plus or minus a parameterized multiple of ATR.
Calculation: Volume climax = Volume is at least a parameterized multiple of its 20-bar average, and the bar makes a new low or high over a parameterized lookback; bullish requires close above the bar midpoint, bearish requires close below it.
Calculation: Hull slope acceleration = Hull MA minus its value three bars earlier; acceleration occurs when this slope crosses above the positive ATR-scaled threshold or below the negative ATR-scaled threshold.
Long: close > Hull MA OR Hull MA > its value two bars earlier OR bullish RSI divergence OR bullish RSI(9) divergence OR bullish CMO divergence OR bullish hidden RSI divergence OR volume climax with a new lookback low and close above the bar midpoint OR low <= lower VWAP band + ATR-scaled buffer AND close > lower VWAP band OR Hull slope crosses above the positive ATR-scaled threshold OR (Hull MA > its value two bars earlier AND close > VWAP AND bullish RSI divergence AND RSI(14) < 75 AND volume > 0.8 × its 20-bar average)
Short: close <= Hull MA OR Hull MA <= its value two bars earlier OR bearish RSI divergence OR bearish RSI(9) divergence OR bearish CMO divergence OR bearish hidden RSI divergence OR volume climax with a new lookback high and close below the bar midpoint OR high >= upper VWAP band − ATR-scaled buffer AND close < upper VWAP band OR Hull slope crosses below the negative ATR-scaled threshold OR (Hull MA <= its value two bars earlier AND close < VWAP AND bearish RSI divergence AND RSI(14) > 25 AND volume > 0.8 × its 20-bar average)
