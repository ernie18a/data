Calculation: Prior confirmed swing high = the most recent high that is higher than the highs in the preceding and following equal-length lookback windows; confirm it only after the following window is complete.
Calculation: Prior confirmed swing low = the most recent low that is lower than the lows in the preceding and following equal-length lookback windows; confirm it only after the following window is complete.
Calculation: POC = midpoint of the price bin with the greatest volume, assigning each bar’s volume to the bin containing its high-low midpoint over a lookback of chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Value area high and value area low = upper and lower edges of the smallest contiguous range of those bins that includes the POC bin and reaches the target share of total volume; expand one adjacent bin at a time, choosing the higher-volume adjacent bin first and choosing the upper bin on ties.
Long: low < prior confirmed swing low AND close > prior confirmed swing low OR close > prior confirmed swing high OR low > high two chart-timeframe bars earlier AND close one chart-timeframe bar earlier > high two chart-timeframe bars earlier OR close crosses above POC OR close crosses above value area low from below
Short: high > prior confirmed swing high AND close < prior confirmed swing high OR close < prior confirmed swing low OR high < low two chart-timeframe bars earlier AND close one chart-timeframe bar earlier < low two chart-timeframe bars earlier OR close crosses below POC OR close crosses below value area high from above
