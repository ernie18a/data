Calculation: Order-block zone = the last opposite candle’s full high–low range, or its open–close range when wick use is off.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = current low − high two bars earlier; bearish fair-value gap = low two bars earlier − current high.
Calculation: High sweep = current high exceeds the highest high of the preceding 10 bars; low sweep = current low is below the lowest low of the preceding 10 bars.
Long: On each enabled timeframe, using completed bars, current low > high two bars earlier AND bullish fair-value gap ≥ minimum gap size AND (middle candle range ≥ displacement threshold OR small gaps are enabled); OR a valid bearish fair-value gap closes below its lower boundary and flips bullish; OR a bullish bar closes above the top of the most recent bearish order-block zone AND that zone meets the minimum size; OR a low sweep sets a pending bullish zone from the current bar or first bearish bar among it and the preceding 8 bars, and a later bullish bar closes above that zone’s top AND its size meets the minimum.
Short: On each enabled timeframe, using completed bars, low two bars earlier > current high AND bearish fair-value gap ≥ minimum gap size AND (middle candle range ≥ displacement threshold OR small gaps are enabled); OR a valid bullish fair-value gap closes above its upper boundary and flips bearish; OR a bearish bar closes below the bottom of the most recent bullish order-block zone AND that zone meets the minimum size; OR a high sweep sets a pending bearish zone from the current bar or first bullish bar among it and the preceding 8 bars, and a later bearish bar closes below that zone’s bottom AND its size meets the minimum.
