Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the pivot left/right lookbacks; use only after the right-lookback bars have completed.
Calculation: Double top setup = a new confirmed swing high matched against a prior confirmed swing high among the recent same-side pivots, within the minimum/maximum bar span and ATR-based peak tolerance; between the peaks, the lowest low is the neckline, and no intervening high exceeds the higher peak; pattern height (higher peak minus neckline) meets the minimum ATR height. Double bottom is the inverse: matched swing lows, highest intervening high as neckline, no intervening low below the lower trough, and height (neckline minus lower trough) meets the minimum ATR height.
Calculation: Pattern quality score = rounded sum of peak match (25 × max(0, 1 − peak difference / ATR tolerance)), RSI divergence (20 if second peak RSI is lower for a top or higher for a bottom, else 0), volume divergence (15 if second peak's centered 3-bar volume sum is lower; 7.5 if either volume is unavailable; else 0), height (15 × min(1, height / (3 × ATR))), symmetry (10 × shorter neckline leg / longer neckline leg), and prior trend (15 × min(1, prior trend move / height)); prior trend move is the rise from the lowest low before the first peak for a top, or the fall from the highest high before the first trough for a bottom, over the prior-trend lookback.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; RSI = relative strength index; EMA = exponential moving average; 20-bar average volume = simple average of volume over 20 bars.
Calculation: Pattern stop = above the higher top peak or below the lower bottom trough by an ATR buffer. TP1/TP2 = neckline projected away from the pattern by the pattern height times their respective multipliers (TP1 uses the shorter multiplier; TP2 the longer multiplier).
Long: A valid, uninvalidated double bottom setup breaks upward when close > neckline AND quality score >= minimum score AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR 20-bar average volume <= 0 OR volume >= volume multiplier × 20-bar average volume) AND long signals enabled.
Short: A valid, uninvalidated double top setup breaks downward when close < neckline AND quality score >= minimum score AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (volume filter disabled OR 20-bar average volume <= 0 OR volume >= volume multiplier × 20-bar average volume) AND short signals enabled.
Exit: Long, low <= pattern stop (or entry price after TP1 if breakeven tracking is enabled) OR high >= TP2; if stop and target are touched on the same bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: Short, high >= pattern stop (or entry price after TP1 if breakeven tracking is enabled) OR low <= TP2; if stop and target are touched on the same bar, exit at the stop.
