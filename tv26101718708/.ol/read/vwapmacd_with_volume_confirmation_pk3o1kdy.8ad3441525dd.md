Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume since the start of the selected session, week, or month, divided by cumulative volume over that period
Calculation: VWAP-MACD line = shorter-lookback EMA of Anchored VWAP − longer-lookback EMA of Anchored VWAP
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of VWAP-MACD line
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its moving average
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a low/high that is lower/higher than the specified number of bars on both its left and right; compare its price and VWAP-MACD histogram value with those at the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type
Long: VWAP-MACD line crosses above Signal line AND Relative volume ≥ 1
Long: Confirmed pivot low price < preceding confirmed pivot low price AND histogram at the confirmed pivot low > histogram at the preceding pivot low
Short: VWAP-MACD line crosses below Signal line AND Relative volume ≥ 1
Short: Confirmed pivot high price > preceding confirmed pivot high price AND histogram at the confirmed pivot high < histogram at the preceding pivot high
