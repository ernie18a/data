Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA of (open + high + low + close) / 4, with a shorter lookback than Slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = EMA of (open + high + low + close) / 4, with a longer lookback than Fast EMA
Long: Fast EMA > Fast EMA one bar ago OR Slow EMA > Slow EMA two bars ago OR Fast EMA > Slow EMA
Short: Fast EMA < Fast EMA one bar ago OR Slow EMA < Slow EMA two bars ago OR Fast EMA <= Slow EMA
