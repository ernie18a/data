Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the swing lookback before and after it; confirm after the following swing-lookback bars.
Calculation: Rising support line = line through two higher confirmed swing lows separated by at least twice the swing lookback; require no close since the first point below the line by more than ATR × touch tolerance. Count swing lows within ATR × touch tolerance as touches; choose the valid candidate with most touches, then longest span. Extend through the current bar.
Calculation: Falling resistance line = line through two lower confirmed swing highs separated by at least twice the swing lookback; require no close since the first point above the line by more than ATR × touch tolerance. Count swing highs within ATR × touch tolerance as touches; choose the valid candidate with most touches, then longest span. Extend through the current bar.
Long: Flat AND rising support line age ≥ minimum line age AND low ≤ support line + ATR × touch tolerance AND close > support line AND close > open AND previous completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA
Long: Flat AND falling resistance line age ≥ minimum line age AND close > resistance line AND most recent confirmed swing low < close AND close − swing low ≤ ATR × break-distance multiplier AND previous completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA
Short: Flat AND falling resistance line age ≥ minimum line age AND high ≥ resistance line − ATR × touch tolerance AND close < resistance line AND close < open AND previous completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA
Short: Flat AND rising support line age ≥ minimum line age AND close < support line AND most recent confirmed swing high > close AND swing high − close ≤ ATR × break-distance multiplier AND previous completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA
Calculation: Bounce safety stop = for a long, the greater of the prior stop and rising support line − ATR × stop buffer; for a short, the lesser of the prior stop and falling resistance line + ATR × stop buffer. Initialize at entry to the corresponding line value with the same buffer.
Calculation: Break safety stop = for a long, update to each new confirmed swing low above the prior stop and below close; for a short, update to each new confirmed swing high below the prior stop and above close. Initialize at entry to the most recent confirmed swing low for a long or swing high for a short.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, close < safety stop
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, close > safety stop
