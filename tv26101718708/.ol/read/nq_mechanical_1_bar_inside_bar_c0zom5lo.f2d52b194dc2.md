Calculation: Inside-bar breakout = An inside bar has high ≤ prior high and low ≥ prior low. On the chart timeframe, only the immediately following bar can trigger: its completed close must exceed the inside bar’s high or fall below its low; otherwise the setup expires. On a higher timeframe, classify the last completed bar against the preceding completed bar; during the following, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, a completed chart-timeframe close beyond the inside bar’s high or low triggers once.
Calculation: Volatility trend = Apply a twin-range filter to the volatility-index series: calculate two smoothed ranges from absolute close changes using shorter and longer lookbacks, each multiplied by its range multiplier, and average them. Smooth each absolute-change series with an EMA over its lookback, then with an EMA over twice that lookback minus one. Move a range filter upward when close rises, but no farther than close minus the averaged range; move it downward when close falls, but no farther than close plus the averaged range. Maintain upper and lower trailing bands at filter ± averaged range, ratcheting each until the filter switches sides on a close crossing its active band. Trend is up when close is above the filter, otherwise down. Use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Timeframe continuity = For the chart timeframe and each configured timeframe equal to or higher than it, compare the chart close with that timeframe’s current-period open; higher-timeframe periods are still forming. Net continuity is the count of closes above their period opens minus the count below. Green means net > 0, middle means net = 0, red means net < 0.
Calculation: Recent key-level cross = Within the configured lookback, the close, high, or low crosses any of the prior day’s high or low, a completed Asia or London session’s high or low, or the session open.
Long: Chart bar confirmed AND within the entry window AND ((close < VWAP AND volatility trend is down AND net continuity ≥ 0 AND an inside-bar breakout is up) OR (close > VWAP AND net continuity > 0 AND an inside-bar breakout is up))
Short: Chart bar confirmed AND within the entry window AND ((close < VWAP AND volatility trend is up AND an inside-bar breakout is down) OR (close > VWAP AND recent key-level cross AND net continuity ≤ 0 AND an inside-bar breakout is down))
