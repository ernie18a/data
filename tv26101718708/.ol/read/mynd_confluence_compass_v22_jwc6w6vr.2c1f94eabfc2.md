Calculation: Per-timeframe state = bullish if close > fast EMA > slow EMA, Supertrend is bullish, RSI is above its midpoint, MACD histogram is rising, and ADX meets that timeframe’s minimum; bearish if the inverse conditions hold; otherwise neutral. Use the still-forming chart-timeframe bar and the last completed bar of each higher timeframe.
Calculation: Equal-weight alignment = count of enabled timeframes in the direction. With an adaptive denominator, required alignment is the greater of active timeframes minus allowed disagreements and the lesser of enabled timeframes and two; otherwise it is the greater of enabled timeframes minus allowed disagreements and one. An active timeframe has a non-neutral state.
Calculation: Weighted confluence percentage = weight of enabled timeframes in the direction divided by the denominator weight, times 100. With an adaptive denominator and at least two active timeframes, denominator weight is the weight of active timeframes; otherwise it is the weight of all enabled timeframes.
Calculation: Average ADX = mean ADX across the chart timeframe and enabled higher timeframes.
Calculation: Trailing exit line = Supertrend on the chart timeframe using the exit factor and ATR lookback; if progressive stop tightening is enabled, reduce the factor per reached profit tier, with a floor of 25% of its configured value.
Long: Long confluence gate is open (in equal-weight mode, bullish count meets required alignment and is greater than zero; in weighted mode, bullish confluence percentage meets its minimum) AND any enabled higher-timeframe opposition veto is clear AND any enabled average-ADX gate passes AND any enabled chart-timeframe ADX-rising condition passes AND the confluence gate has remained open for the configured number of consecutive chart bars and is newly confirmed AND current position is not long AND any enabled volume confirmation passes AND any enabled cooldown since the most recent long trailing-stop exit has elapsed.
Short: Short confluence gate is open (in equal-weight mode, bearish count meets required alignment and is greater than zero; in weighted mode, bearish confluence percentage meets its minimum) AND any enabled higher-timeframe opposition veto is clear AND any enabled average-ADX gate passes AND any enabled chart-timeframe ADX-rising condition passes AND the confluence gate has remained open for the configured number of consecutive chart bars and is newly confirmed AND current position is not short AND any enabled volume confirmation passes AND any enabled cooldown since the most recent short trailing-stop exit has elapsed.
Exit: Long, chart-timeframe trailing Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, chart-timeframe trailing Supertrend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
