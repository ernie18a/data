Calculation: HMA = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half lookback) − WMA(close, full lookback), square-root lookback)
Calculation: Inner cloud width = ATR × cloud-width multiplier; upper boundary = HMA + inner cloud width; lower boundary = HMA − inner cloud width
Calculation: ADX = RMA(DX, ADX lookback), where +DM = max(high − prior high, 0) when the upward high move exceeds the downward low move, otherwise 0; −DM = max(prior low − low, 0) when the downward low move exceeds the upward high move, otherwise 0; +DI and −DI = their respective RMA(DM, ADX lookback) divided by RMA(true range, ADX lookback), × 100; DX = |+DI − −DI| / (+DI + −DI) × 100, or 0 if the sum is 0
Calculation: Long retest score = integer truncation of min(proximity score + volume score + RSI score + ADX score, 100), where proximity score = min(max(upper boundary − low, 0) / inner cloud width × 50, 50); volume score = min(volume / max(volume SMA, 1) × 15, 25); RSI score = min(max(55 − RSI, 0) × 0.5, 15); ADX score = min(ADX × 0.4, 10)
Calculation: Short retest score = integer truncation of min(proximity score + volume score + RSI score + ADX score, 100), where proximity score = min(max(high − lower boundary, 0) / inner cloud width × 50, 50); volume score = min(volume / max(volume SMA, 1) × 15, 25); RSI score = min(max(RSI − 45, 0) × 0.5, 15); ADX score = min(ADX × 0.4, 10)
Long: low <= upper cloud boundary AND close > HMA AND close > open AND fast EMA (half HMA lookback) > slow EMA (full HMA lookback) AND bar index >= max(3 × HMA lookback, 50) AND bar is confirmed AND long retest score >= minimum score
Short: high >= lower cloud boundary AND close < HMA AND close < open AND fast EMA (half HMA lookback) < slow EMA (full HMA lookback) AND bar index >= max(3 × HMA lookback, 50) AND bar is confirmed AND short retest score >= minimum score
