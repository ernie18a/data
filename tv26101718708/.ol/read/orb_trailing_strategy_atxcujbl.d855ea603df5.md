Calculation: London opening range = highest high and lowest low from 03:00 to 03:15 ET; NY opening range = highest high and lowest low from 09:30 to 09:45 ET.
Calculation: Asia range = highest high and lowest low from 18:00 to midnight ET; Asia range is broken if, after midnight and before 03:00 ET, any high exceeds its high or any low falls below its low.
Calculation: Initial risk (1R) = long entry close minus entry bar low; short entry bar high minus short entry close.
Calculation: R level = greatest integer reached by favorable excursion from entry divided by 1R, ratcheted upward; for longs use the highest high, for shorts the lowest low.
Calculation: Trailing stop = initial entry bar low for longs or entry bar high for shorts until R level reaches 1; at level 1 use entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the break-even offset; at level 2 or higher use entry price plus (long) or minus (short) (R level − 1) × 1R.
Long: (03:15 ≤ New York time < 06:00 AND London session selected AND London opening range complete AND close ≥ London range high + minimum breakout AND low < close OR 09:45 ≤ New York time < 15:00 AND NY session selected AND NY opening range complete AND close ≥ NY range high + minimum breakout AND low < close) AND flat AND the selected session has fewer than two entries and no prior trade reaching at least 2R AND re-entry is enabled (initially enabled; after a trade closes, close must return strictly inside that session's range during its entry window) AND (Asia filter disabled OR Asia range broken) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > the EMA of close with a longer lookback on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar).
Short: (03:15 ≤ New York time < 06:00 AND London session selected AND London opening range complete AND close ≤ London range low − minimum breakout AND high > close OR 09:45 ≤ New York time < 15:00 AND NY session selected AND NY opening range complete AND close ≤ NY range low − minimum breakout AND high > close) AND flat AND the selected session has fewer than two entries and no prior trade reaching at least 2R AND re-entry is enabled (initially enabled; after a trade closes, close must return strictly inside that session's range during its entry window) AND (Asia filter disabled OR Asia range broken) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < the EMA of close with a longer lookback on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar).
Exit: Long, low ≤ trailing stop OR (London trade AND 06:00 ≤ New York time < 06:05) OR (NY trade AND 15:00 ≤ New York time < 15:05).
Exit: Short, high ≥ trailing stop OR (London trade AND 06:00 ≤ New York time < 06:05) OR (NY trade AND 15:00 ≤ New York time < 15:05).
