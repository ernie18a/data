Calculation: Basis = EMA of HLC3 using the longer basis lookback, optionally smoothed by a second EMA of the same lookback; cloud bounds = max/min(Basis, EMA of HLC3 using the shorter reference lookback)
Calculation: Ribbon expansion = absolute difference between the shortest and longest active EMAs
Calculation: Stop distance = configured ATR multiple times ATR, or configured percentage of entry price
Long: Fast-to-slow active EMAs are strictly descending in value (using the configured 2, 3, or 4 EMAs) AND (entry mode is Trend Flip and this alignment has just begun OR entry mode includes retest and a post-flip low touched the selected reference within the configured wait limit and the bar closes above that reference and above its open OR entry mode includes pullback and this is not a new flip and the bar's low touched the selected reference and the bar closes above it and above its open) AND (quality filter is disabled OR close > open AND close > Basis AND ribbon expansion > ATR times the configured minimum AND (HTF agreement is not required OR the higher-timeframe active EMAs align bullish using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is not required OR ADX >= configured minimum)) AND same-direction re-entry is allowed or no long plan is active
Short: Fast-to-slow active EMAs are strictly ascending in value (using the configured 2, 3, or 4 EMAs) AND (entry mode is Trend Flip and this alignment has just begun OR entry mode includes retest and a post-flip high touched the selected reference within the configured wait limit and the bar closes below that reference and below its open OR entry mode includes pullback and this is not a new flip and the bar's high touched the selected reference and the bar closes below it and below its open) AND (quality filter is disabled OR close < open AND close < Basis AND ribbon expansion > ATR times the configured minimum AND (HTF agreement is not required OR the higher-timeframe active EMAs align bearish using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADX filter is not required OR ADX >= configured minimum)) AND same-direction re-entry is allowed or no short plan is active
Exit: long, low <= entry price - stop distance OR high >= entry price + stop distance times configured take-profit-3 multiple OR (selected exit mode is Active Trend Lost and bullish active-EMA alignment was true on the prior bar and is false now) OR (selected exit mode is Full Ribbon Lost and all four bullish EMA alignments were true on the prior bar and are false now) OR (selected exit mode is Opposite Trend and bearish active-EMA alignment has just begun)
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR low <= entry price - stop distance times configured take-profit-3 multiple OR (selected exit mode is Active Trend Lost and bearish active-EMA alignment was true on the prior bar and is false now) OR (selected exit mode is Full Ribbon Lost and all four bearish EMA alignments were true on the prior bar and are false now) OR (selected exit mode is Opposite Trend and bullish active-EMA alignment has just begun)
