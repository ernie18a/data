Calculation: Active resistance/support trendline = highest-ranked valid line through two confirmed pivot highs/lows, respectively; candidate lines must meet configurable minimum pivot spacing, slope, and touch requirements, and are ranked by touches, span, violations, slope quality, and proximity to price. Micro pivots use shorter sensitivity/lookback than meso pivots. Mega pivots use a higher timeframe and only confirmed pivots from completed bars.
Calculation: Trendline level = price on the active line at the current bar, interpolated between its two pivot anchors in the configured price geometry.
Long: An active micro, meso, or mega resistance line is crossed upward by the configured break price beyond its break tolerance, the line was not already broken on the prior bar, the bar is confirmed, and (for micro/meso only) line quality meets the minimum signal quality; OR, within the configured retest window after such an upward break and before any earlier retest, low <= resistance trendline level plus retest tolerance AND close > resistance trendline level, the bar is confirmed, and (for micro/meso only) line quality meets the minimum signal quality.
Short: An active micro, meso, or mega support line is crossed downward by the configured break price beyond its break tolerance, the line was not already broken on the prior bar, the bar is confirmed, and (for micro/meso only) line quality meets the minimum signal quality; OR, within the configured retest window after such a downward break and before any earlier retest, high >= support trendline level minus retest tolerance AND close < support trendline level, the bar is confirmed, and (for micro/meso only) line quality meets the minimum signal quality.
