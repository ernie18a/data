Calculation: Adaptive Supertrend direction = For each ATR-length/multiplier pair, calculate ATR and trailing upper/lower bands; direction turns long when close crosses above the upper band and short when close crosses below the lower band. Use the configured manual pair, the highest-scoring pair, or the center pair of the 3×3 neighborhood with the highest average score; scores use closed trades from direction flips and the configured performance metric.
Calculation: Grid agreement = percentage of tested Supertrend pairs whose direction matches the active direction.
Calculation: Long momentum score = sum of five true/false conditions: close > fast EMA, fast EMA > slow EMA, RSI ≥ long threshold, close > prior close, and (volume ≥ its moving average when volume confirmation is enabled, otherwise true).
Calculation: Short momentum score = sum of five true/false conditions: close < fast EMA, fast EMA < slow EMA, RSI ≤ short threshold, close < prior close, and (volume ≥ its moving average when volume confirmation is enabled, otherwise true).
