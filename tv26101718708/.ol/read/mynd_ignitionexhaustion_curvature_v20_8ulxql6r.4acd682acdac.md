Calculation: Smoothed momentum = EMA of RSI(close) using a short smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Smoothed velocity = EMA of the one-bar change in smoothed momentum.
Calculation: Acceleration z-score = (EMA of the one-bar change in smoothed velocity − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation; use 0 when standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe smoothed momentum = smoothed momentum on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Volume climax = current volume ≥ a multiplier × its rolling average volume.
Long: (acceleration z-score crosses above the positive ignition threshold AND smoothed velocity > 0 AND oversold level < smoothed momentum < overbought level AND (HTF filter off OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum < overbought level)) OR (acceleration z-score crosses above the positive exhaustion threshold AND smoothed momentum ≤ oversold level AND smoothed momentum was falling over the required preceding bars, excluding the signal bar, AND (HTF filter off OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum ≤ oversold level) AND (volume-climax filter off OR volume climax)
Short: (acceleration z-score crosses below the negative ignition threshold AND smoothed velocity < 0 AND oversold level < smoothed momentum < overbought level AND (HTF filter off OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum > oversold level)) OR (acceleration z-score crosses below the negative exhaustion threshold AND smoothed momentum ≥ overbought level AND smoothed momentum was rising over the required preceding bars, excluding the signal bar, AND (HTF filter off OR higher-timeframe smoothed momentum ≥ overbought level) AND (volume-climax filter off OR volume climax)
