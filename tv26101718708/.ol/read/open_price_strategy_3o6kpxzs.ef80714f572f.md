Calculation: Open reference = selected price source on the first chart bar when the configured open-price session begins; retain it until the next session begins
Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = sum(volume × money flow multiplier, lookback) / sum(volume, lookback), where money flow multiplier = 0 when high = low, otherwise (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low)
Long: Within the backtest date range, either (during the ASAP entry window, regular mode enabled, entry price > open reference, and each enabled ASAP filter passes: close > ASAP EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow > 0) OR (during the ASAP entry window, contrarian mode enabled, entry price < open reference, and each enabled ASAP filter passes: close < ASAP EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow < 0) OR (during the wait-entry window, regular mode enabled, wait EMA crosses above open reference, and each enabled wait filter passes: close > wait EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow > 0) OR (during the wait-entry window, contrarian mode enabled, wait EMA crosses below open reference, and each enabled wait filter passes: close < wait EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow < 0)
Short: Within the backtest date range, either (during the ASAP entry window, regular mode enabled, entry price < open reference, and each enabled ASAP filter passes: close < ASAP EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow < 0) OR (during the ASAP entry window, contrarian mode enabled, entry price > open reference, and each enabled ASAP filter passes: close > ASAP EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow > 0) OR (during the wait-entry window, regular mode enabled, wait EMA crosses below open reference, and each enabled wait filter passes: close < wait EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow < 0) OR (during the wait-entry window, contrarian mode enabled, wait EMA crosses above open reference, and each enabled wait filter passes: close > wait EMA and/or Chaikin Money Flow > 0)
Exit: Long, outside the configured exit-time window while end-of-window closing is enabled OR stop EMA crosses below the open reference while open-line exit is enabled OR (stop/take-profit is enabled and within the backtest date range and low <= average entry price × (1 − stop-loss %) OR high >= average entry price × (1 + take-profit %))
Exit: Short, outside the configured exit-time window while end-of-window closing is enabled OR stop EMA crosses above the open reference while open-line exit is enabled OR (stop/take-profit is enabled and within the backtest date range and high >= average entry price × (1 + stop-loss %) OR low <= average entry price × (1 − take-profit %))
