Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest swing high / below the latest swing low while structure direction is not already bullish / bearish; a change of character also occurs when close breaks the previous swing high / low against the current structure direction. Swings use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = current low > high two bars ago / current high < low two bars ago, with the middle bar closing up / down; gap size must be at least a fraction of ATR. Price is in an aligned FVG when the current bar’s range intersects any retained FVG zone.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish momentum = 3-bar EMA of RSI above / below 50, its 3-bar change positive / negative, and the smoothed RSI below the overbought / above the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish volume = volume at least a multiple of its moving average, estimated buy volume greater / less than estimated sell volume, and the 10-bar sum of volume delta positive / negative. Estimate buy volume as full volume on an up close, otherwise volume × (close − low) / (high − low); estimate sell volume as full volume on a down close, otherwise volume × (high − close) / (high − low). Volume anomaly = absolute difference between volume and its moving average divided by volume standard deviation exceeds a threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = higher-timeframe close above / below its EMA and higher-timeframe RSI above / below 50; use the last available higher-timeframe values with lookahead off.
Calculation: Regime direction = +1 when +DI > −DI, otherwise −1; trending when ADX exceeds a threshold. Regime permits either direction while ranging, and only its direction while trending.
Calculation: Liquidity score = 1 if absolute volume delta / volume moving average exceeds a threshold, plus 1 if absolute estimated buy-sell volume difference / volume exceeds a threshold; otherwise 0.
Calculation: Quantum superposition = (absolute value of (structure direction + momentum signal + volume signal + higher-timeframe signal) / 4 + entanglement) / 2, where entanglement is +0.5 if higher-timeframe trend equals structure direction, otherwise −0.5, plus +0.5 if higher-timeframe signal equals momentum signal, otherwise −0.5. Collapsed when superposition exceeds a threshold; coherence is the absolute value term above.
Calculation: Smart-money score = 10 × institutional delta / volume moving average, signed positive for accumulation and negative for distribution, multiplied by 1.5 during a volume anomaly and clamped to [−100, 100]. Institutional delta = volume delta × volume / volume moving average. Accumulation requires 5-bar close change > 0, 5-bar volume change > 0, and institutional delta > 0; distribution requires the first two changes < 0 and institutional delta < 0.
Calculation: Total confluence = structure points (1 for a current bullish or bearish structure break when required, otherwise 1 for nonzero structure direction; plus 1 for price in an FVG aligned with structure when required) + momentum point (1 for nonzero momentum signal when alignment is required, otherwise 1) + volume points (1 for nonzero volume signal and 1 for volume anomaly when volume filter is enabled, otherwise 1) + higher-timeframe point (1 for nonzero signal when enabled, otherwise 1) + session point (1 in active session when enabled, otherwise 1) + liquidity score when enabled, otherwise 1 + quantum points (1 if collapsed, plus 1 if coherence exceeds a threshold, when enabled, otherwise 1) + smart-money points (1 if score > 50, plus 1 for accumulation or distribution, when enabled, otherwise 1) + harmonic point (1 when sin((bar index modulo 8) × 45°) × structure direction > 0.5, when enabled, otherwise 1).
Long: bullish structure direction AND total confluence >= minimum threshold AND (momentum alignment disabled OR momentum signal >= 0) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe signal >= 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume signal >= 0) AND (session filter disabled OR in active session) AND regime permits long AND (quantum filter disabled OR quantum superposition > 0) AND (smart-money filter disabled OR smart-money score >= 0) AND (FVG requirement disabled OR price in bullish FVG) AND (structure-break requirement disabled OR bullish structure break) AND flat; OR bullish structure break.
Short: bearish structure direction AND total confluence >= minimum threshold AND (momentum alignment disabled OR momentum signal <= 0) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe signal <= 0) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume signal <= 0) AND (session filter disabled OR in active session) AND regime permits short AND (quantum filter disabled OR quantum superposition < 0) AND (smart-money filter disabled OR smart-money score <= 0) AND (FVG requirement disabled OR price in bearish FVG) AND (structure-break requirement disabled OR bearish structure break) AND flat; OR bearish structure break.
Exit: long, low <= the active stop OR high >= entry price + risk distance × reward-risk ratio, where risk distance is entry price minus the initial stop; initial stop is the lower of close − ATR × stop multiplier and lowest low over twice the swing lookback minus an ATR buffer. If trailing is enabled, active stop is the greatest of initial stop and prior trailing stop and high − ATR × trail multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= the active stop OR low <= entry price − risk distance × reward-risk ratio, where risk distance is initial stop minus entry price; initial stop is the higher of close + ATR × stop multiplier and highest high over twice the swing lookback plus an ATR buffer. If trailing is enabled, active stop is the least of initial stop and prior trailing stop and low + ATR × trail multiplier.
