Calculation: Fast, medium, and trend EMAs = close EMAs with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar close minus ATR times a configurable multiplier, or signal-bar close minus a configurable percentage if ATR stops are disabled; short stop = signal-bar close plus ATR times a configurable multiplier, or signal-bar close plus a configurable percentage if ATR stops are disabled.
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar close plus the long stop distance from signal-bar close times a configurable risk/reward ratio; short target = signal-bar close minus the short stop distance from signal-bar close times a configurable risk/reward ratio.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above medium EMA AND close > trend EMA OR (fast EMA > medium EMA AND medium EMA > trend EMA AND close > trend EMA AND close > medium EMA AND close < medium EMA plus a configurable percentage AND (MACD line > signal line OR MACD line crosses above signal line) AND RSI is between configurable lower and upper thresholds) AND (volume > its moving average OR volume confirmation is disabled)
Short: Fast EMA crosses below medium EMA AND close < trend EMA OR (fast EMA < medium EMA AND medium EMA < trend EMA AND close < trend EMA AND close < medium EMA AND close > medium EMA minus a configurable percentage AND (MACD line < signal line OR MACD line crosses below signal line) AND RSI is between configurable lower and upper thresholds) AND (volume > its moving average OR volume confirmation is disabled)
Exit: Long, fast EMA crosses below medium EMA OR close < trend EMA OR RSI > its upper exit threshold OR low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: Short, fast EMA crosses above medium EMA OR close > trend EMA OR RSI < its lower exit threshold OR high >= short stop OR low <= short target
