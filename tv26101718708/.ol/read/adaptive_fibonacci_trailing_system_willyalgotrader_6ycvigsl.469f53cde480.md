Calculation: Valid swing = a pivot high or low confirmed after an equal left/right lookback, whose distance from the latest opposing valid swing divided by ATR, multiplied by volume divided by its average (or 1 when volume is unavailable), meets a minimum threshold; use the latest valid high and low as Fibonacci anchors.
Calculation: Fibonacci 0.5 = latest valid swing high − 0.5 × (swing high − swing low); other Fibonacci levels use the same formula with 0.382 and 0.618.
Calculation: Market regime = stateful classification using ADX and the ratio of ATR to its longer-term average: enter volatile when the ratio reaches its threshold and remain volatile until it falls below a lower exit threshold; otherwise enter trending when ADX reaches its threshold and leave trending when ADX falls below a lower exit threshold; otherwise range.
Calculation: Adaptive trailing stop = derive a bullish and bearish stop from the latest swing range using a Fibonacci retracement fraction (fixed by trailing mode, or wider in trending, intermediate in volatile, tighter in ranging) and adjust its distance inversely to ATR divided by its longer-term average; ratchet bullish stops upward only and bearish stops downward only.
Calculation: Confidence score = weighted combination of (1) the swing score of the latest low for long or latest high for short, normalized by a maximum; (2) regime, trail-direction, and SMA alignment; (3) ADX normalized to a maximum; (4) volume relative to its average, neutral when unavailable; (5) ATR-to-longer-term-average ratio, favoring a ratio near 1; and (6) proximity of the signal reference level to SMA, measured in ATR units.
Calculation: Trade levels = at entry close, stop distance is ATR × stop multiplier; for a long, stop = entry − stop distance and take-profits = entry + stop distance × each target multiple; for a short, reverse the signs. After the first take-profit is touched without the stop being touched on that bar, set the stop to entry for subsequent bars.
Long: confirmed bar close AND no position active AND (in trail-flip mode, trailing direction changes to bullish after a bullish breakout confirmation (body-to-range ratio meets its minimum, close exceeds the prior stop by more than the ATR penetration threshold, and the regime gate passes) OR after the configured number of consecutive closes below the stop; in 0.5-cross mode, prior close ≤ current Fibonacci 0.5 AND close > current Fibonacci 0.5 AND (strictness is none OR body ratio and ATR penetration pass, with the regime gate also required at full strictness))) AND (confidence filtering disabled OR long confidence score ≥ minimum confidence.
Short: confirmed bar close AND no position active AND (in trail-flip mode, trailing direction changes to bearish after a bearish breakout confirmation (body-to-range ratio meets its minimum, close is below the prior stop by more than the ATR penetration threshold, and the regime gate passes) OR after the configured number of consecutive closes above the stop; in 0.5-cross mode, prior close ≥ current Fibonacci 0.5 AND close < current Fibonacci 0.5 AND (strictness is none OR body ratio and ATR penetration pass, with the regime gate also required at full strictness))) AND (confidence filtering disabled OR short confidence score ≥ minimum confidence.
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ third take-profit.
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ third take-profit.
