Calculation: Currency strength = On the configured strength timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), use the current bar, including a still-forming bar when applicable. For each of these pairs—GBP/USD, USD/CHF, EUR/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CAD, NZD/USD, AUD/USD, AUD/NZD, AUD/CAD, AUD/CHF, AUD/JPY, CHF/JPY, GBP/CHF, GBP/AUD, GBP/CAD, GBP/JPY, GBP/NZD, EUR/JPY, EUR/CAD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CHF, EUR/AUD, EUR/NZD, CAD/JPY, NZD/JPY, CAD/CHF—calculate the close's position in the high-low range over a configurable lookback. Without smoothing, use the full lookback range; with smoothing, average the ranges for lookbacks 1 through the configured lookback, using equal or triangular weights that favor shorter lookbacks. A zero-width range gives ratio 0. Convert each ratio to score: 9 at ≥0.97, 8 at ≥0.90, 7 at ≥0.75, 6 at ≥0.60, 5 at ≥0.50, 4 at ≥0.40, 3 at ≥0.25, 2 at ≥0.10, 1 at ≥0.03, otherwise 0. Each pair contributes its score to its base currency and 9 minus its score to its quote currency. A currency's strength is the average of its contributions across these pairs.
Long: Chart base-currency strength ≥ upper threshold AND chart quote-currency strength ≤ lower threshold
Short: Chart base-currency strength ≤ lower threshold AND chart quote-currency strength ≥ upper threshold
