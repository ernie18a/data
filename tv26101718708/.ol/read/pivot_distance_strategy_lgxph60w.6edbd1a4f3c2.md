Calculation: Pivot distance = 100 × (close / most recent confirmed pivot high − most recent confirmed pivot low / close), using chart-timeframe bars; a pivot is confirmed after the configured number of bars to its right.
Calculation: Dynamic squeeze threshold = selected threshold method applied to pivot distance (fixed level, ATR as a percentage of close, Bollinger Band width, or Keltner Channel width); breakout threshold is calculated by the same method with its separate setting.
Long: (pivot distance > dynamic squeeze threshold OR fast moving average of pivot distance crosses above zero OR shorter-lookback moving average of pivot distance crosses above longer-lookback moving average) AND long entries enabled AND cooldown inactive AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe state is Long)
Short: (pivot distance < −dynamic squeeze threshold OR fast moving average of pivot distance crosses below zero OR shorter-lookback moving average of pivot distance crosses below longer-lookback moving average) AND short entries enabled AND cooldown inactive AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe state is Short)
Exit: Long, higher-timeframe filter enabled AND higher-timeframe state is Short or Squeeze
Exit: Short, higher-timeframe filter enabled AND higher-timeframe state is Long or Squeeze
Exit: Long, short-entry condition occurs while both directions are enabled OR (only long entries enabled AND (pivot distance < 0 for breakout mode OR moving average crosses below zero for zero-cross mode OR shorter-lookback moving average crosses below longer-lookback moving average for dual-average mode))
Exit: Short, long-entry condition occurs while both directions are enabled OR (only short entries enabled AND (pivot distance > 0 for breakout mode OR moving average crosses above zero for zero-cross mode OR shorter-lookback moving average crosses above longer-lookback moving average for dual-average mode))
Exit: Long, stop loss enabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR × stop multiplier OR take profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR × target multiplier
Exit: Short, stop loss enabled AND high ≥ entry price + ATR × stop multiplier OR take profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − ATR × target multiplier
Exit: Long, trailing stop enabled AND close ≤ trailing stop; after profit reaches ATR × activation multiplier, trailing stop is the highest of its prior value and close − ATR × trailing multiplier
Exit: Short, trailing stop enabled AND close ≥ trailing stop; after profit reaches ATR × activation multiplier, trailing stop is the lowest of its prior value and close + ATR × trailing multiplier
Exit: Long, breakout-cross exit enabled AND pivot distance crosses below the positive dynamic breakout threshold
Exit: Short, breakout-cross exit enabled AND pivot distance crosses above the negative dynamic breakout threshold
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot distance = 100 × (higher-timeframe close / most recent confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high − most recent confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low / higher-timeframe close); use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe state = Long when higher-timeframe pivot distance exceeds its dynamic breakout threshold and is outside its squeeze threshold; Short when below the negative breakout threshold and outside the squeeze threshold; otherwise Squeeze. In MA-zero mode, state is Long when its higher-timeframe moving average of pivot distance > 0 and Short when < 0, otherwise Squeeze. In dual-average mode, state is Long when the shorter-lookback higher-timeframe moving average of pivot distance > the longer-lookback one and Short when < it, otherwise Squeeze.
