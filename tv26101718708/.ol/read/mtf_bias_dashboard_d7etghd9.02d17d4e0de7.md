Calculation: Trend score = bounded combination of EMA stack alignment (fast > mid > slow bullish; fast < mid < slow bearish), slow EMA slope normalized by ATR, smoothed RSI level and direction, close location relative to the fast/mid/slow EMA band, VWAP location, and ATR relative to its longer-term baseline; use shorter lookbacks for fast/mid EMAs than slow/structure EMAs, and smooth RSI over a shorter lookback than the ATR baseline. For each stream, use its last completed bar; streams may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Confluence score = weighted sum of confirmed trend scores across the intraday timeframe streams, multiplied by session and low-volatility adjustment factors.
Long: Bull regime: in the conservative preset, 1H and 4H trend scores are both above the trend threshold; in the aggressive preset, 1H score is above threshold, 15m score is positive, and 5m score is above negative trend threshold; in the balanced preset, 1H score is above threshold, 15m score is at least the trend threshold, and 30m score is above negative trend threshold. Use the last completed bar for each stream; streams may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: Bear regime: in the conservative preset, 1H and 4H trend scores are both below negative trend threshold; in the aggressive preset, 1H score is below negative threshold, 15m score is negative, and 5m score is below trend threshold; in the balanced preset, 1H score is below negative threshold, 15m score is at most negative trend threshold, and 30m score is below trend threshold. Use the last completed bar for each stream; streams may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
