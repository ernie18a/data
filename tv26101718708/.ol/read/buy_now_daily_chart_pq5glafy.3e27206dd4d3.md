Calculation: WaveTrend signal line = SMA(WaveTrend fast line, signal smoothing length), where signal smoothing length is shorter than oscillator length
Long: lowest 14-period RSI of close over the setup window <= oversold threshold AND a WaveTrend fast-line crossover above its signal line occurred within the setup window AND volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier AND (close <= lowest low over the liquidity lookback × (1 + liquidity zone percentage) OR close >= highest high over the liquidity lookback × (1 − liquidity zone percentage)) AND (if EMA-cross filter is enabled, close crosses above its 9-period EMA; otherwise, a WaveTrend fast-line crossover above its signal line OR volume > 20-bar average volume × volume multiplier) AND (no prior long signal OR current bar index − prior long-signal bar index > cooldown length)
