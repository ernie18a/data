Calculation: BSSC = sum of bullish/bearish bucket scores from MFI, the A/D line’s position within its rolling range, and Williams %R
Calculation: Trend score = sum of +1 when BSSC > 0, OBV > its EMA, or StochRSI > its midpoint; −1 when the corresponding value is below its comparison level; and 0 when equal
Long: Trend score crosses above 0
Short: Trend score crosses below 0
