Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago AND previous close > high two bars ago
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago AND previous close < low two bars ago
Calculation: Low sweep = current low < the lowest low of the preceding swing-period bars AND current close > that lowest low
Calculation: High sweep = current high > the highest high of the preceding swing-period bars AND current close < that highest high
Calculation: Bullish trend = short-term EMA > medium-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND higher-timeframe short-term EMA > higher-timeframe medium-term EMA AND chart-timeframe close > higher-timeframe medium-term EMA; higher-timeframe EMAs use the higher-timeframe bar currently forming
Calculation: Bearish trend = short-term EMA < medium-term EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND higher-timeframe short-term EMA < higher-timeframe medium-term EMA AND chart-timeframe close < higher-timeframe medium-term EMA; higher-timeframe EMAs use the higher-timeframe bar currently forming
Calculation: Long confluence score = 15 if bullish trend + 10 if ADX > its threshold + 10 if positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator + 10 if RSI is between its lower and upper bounds + 15 if MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 + 10 if volume > its moving average × its threshold + 5 if ATR > its moving average × its threshold + 10 if low sweep + 10 if bullish fair value gap + 5 if close > open AND candle body > ATR × its threshold; otherwise each component contributes 0
Calculation: Short confluence score = 15 if bearish trend + 10 if ADX > its threshold + 10 if negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator + 10 if RSI is between its lower and upper bounds + 15 if MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 + 10 if volume > its moving average × its threshold + 5 if ATR > its moving average × its threshold + 10 if high sweep + 10 if bearish fair value gap + 5 if close < open AND candle body > ATR × its threshold; otherwise each component contributes 0
Calculation: Long stop distance = absolute value of close − (low − adaptive ATR); short stop distance = absolute value of close − (high + adaptive ATR); adaptive ATR = Wilder moving average of true range × volatility multiplier, where the multiplier is the configured ATR multiplier × 1.2 when ADX > its upper threshold, × 1 when ADX > its lower threshold, and × 0.8 otherwise
Calculation: Active target = entry price + (configured risk/reward ratio × long stop distance × selected target multiple) for long positions, or entry price − (configured risk/reward ratio × short stop distance × selected target multiple) for short positions; selected target multiple is one of four successive multiples
Long: selected high-or-close source > most recent confirmed pivot high AND long confluence score ≥ its threshold AND bullish trend AND ADX > its threshold AND no position is open
Short: selected low-or-close source < most recent confirmed pivot low AND short confluence score ≥ its threshold AND bearish trend AND ADX > its threshold AND no position is open
Exit: long, high ≥ active target OR (configured stop trigger is close and close ≤ entry price − long stop distance) OR (configured stop trigger is high/low and low ≤ entry price − long stop distance)
Exit: short, low ≤ active target OR (configured stop trigger is close and close ≥ entry price + short stop distance) OR (configured stop trigger is high/low and high ≥ entry price + short stop distance)
