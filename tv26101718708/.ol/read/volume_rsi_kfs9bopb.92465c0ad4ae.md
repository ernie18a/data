Calculation: Volume Power = Smooth the chart-bar RMA of the scaled volume-direction score. For each bar, let direction be (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 if high = low; let volume weight be volume^(1 − boost) × factor + (1 − factor), using 0 for volume^(...) when volume ≤ 0; and let raw score be direction × volume weight. Divide raw score by the long-term RMA of its absolute value, multiply by 100, and clamp to [−100, 100] (use 0 if the baseline is 0). Apply a shorter chart-bar RMA, then the selected chart-bar smoothing method (none, SMA, EMA, RMA, HMA, or WMA). The baseline lookback is specified in days and converted to chart bars; the subsequent RMA and smoothing use chart-bar lookbacks.
Long: Volume Power ≥ long threshold OR (RSI ≥ midline AND Volume Power < long threshold AND Volume Power > short threshold)
Short: Volume Power ≤ short threshold OR (RSI < midline AND Volume Power < long threshold AND Volume Power > short threshold)
