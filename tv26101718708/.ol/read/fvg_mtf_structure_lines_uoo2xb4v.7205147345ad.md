Calculation: Short-term and long-term structure pivots = For each lookback, a pivot high is the high from that many bars ago when it exceeds the highest high of the intervening bars and the structure leg changes to bearish; a pivot low is the low from that many bars ago when it is below the lowest low of the intervening bars and the leg changes to bullish. The long-term lookback is longer than the short-term lookback.
Long: Close crosses above an unbroken most recent pivot high, either short-term (if its level differs from the long-term pivot high) or long-term; OR, at the start of a higher-timeframe bar while the chart timeframe is lower, the still-forming higher-timeframe low is above the high from two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the last completed higher-timeframe close is above that same high.
Short: Close crosses below an unbroken most recent pivot low, either short-term (if its level differs from the long-term pivot low) or long-term; OR, at the start of a higher-timeframe bar while the chart timeframe is lower, the still-forming higher-timeframe high is below the low from two higher-timeframe bars earlier AND the last completed higher-timeframe close is below that same low.
