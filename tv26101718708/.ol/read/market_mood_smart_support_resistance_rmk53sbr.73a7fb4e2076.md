Calculation: Confirmed swing sequence = pivot highs/lows confirmed by the chosen number of bars on both sides; keep alternating swing types, replacing consecutive same-type swings with the more extreme one.
Calculation: Bullish ABCD structure = latest four swings are high A, low B, high C, low D; A > C, D < B, BC/AB is within the retracement bounds, and CD/BC is within the extension bounds. Bearish ABCD structure = low A, high B, low C, high D; A < C, D > B, BC/AB is within the retracement bounds, and CD/BC is within the extension bounds.
Calculation: Daily range percentage = average of completed daily high-minus-low ranges over the chosen lookback, divided by the previous completed daily close, expressed as a percentage; use completed bars from a higher timeframe than the chart.
Calculation: Required impulse = daily range percentage multiplied by the selected risk-profile factor.
Calculation: Bullish impulse = a bearish origin candle followed immediately by at least the selected minimum streak of consecutive bullish candles; move percentage = (close of the farthest candle in the streak − low of the nearest candle in the streak) / low of the nearest candle in the streak × 100. Search origin candles backward from breakout to D, capped by the maximum scan length; qualify when move percentage meets the required impulse. If several qualify, use the origin with the greatest move percentage.
Calculation: Bearish impulse = a bullish origin candle followed immediately by at least the selected minimum streak of consecutive bearish candles; move percentage = (high of the nearest candle in the streak − close of the farthest candle in the streak) / high of the nearest candle in the streak × 100. Search origin candles backward from breakout to D, capped by the maximum scan length; qualify when move percentage meets the required impulse. If several qualify, use the origin with the greatest move percentage.
Calculation: Active demand zone = high and low of the selected bullish impulse origin candle; invalidation level = zone low − ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Active supply zone = high and low of the selected bearish impulse origin candle; invalidation level = zone high + ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: First zone retest = first bar after zone creation whose high-low range overlaps the active zone.
Long: Confirmed, unexpired bullish ABCD structure AND selected breakout source (close, or high in wick mode) > A + ATR × breakout buffer AND (previous breakout source <= threshold OR current bar is candidate activation bar) AND qualified bullish impulse found OR first retest of an active demand zone.
Short: Confirmed, unexpired bearish ABCD structure AND selected breakout source (close, or low in wick mode) < A − ATR × breakout buffer AND (previous breakout source >= threshold OR current bar is candidate activation bar) AND qualified bearish impulse found OR first retest of an active supply zone.
Exit: Long, confirmed close < invalidation level of an active demand zone.
Exit: Short, confirmed close > invalidation level of an active supply zone.
