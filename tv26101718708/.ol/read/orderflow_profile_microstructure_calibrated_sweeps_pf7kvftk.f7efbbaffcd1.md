Calculation: Lower-timeframe order-flow delta = sum of intrabar volume weighted by (2 × Student-t CDF((intrabar close − previous intrabar close) / within-chart-bar intrabar-change standard deviation) − 1); for the first intrabar, use its close − open. Use confirmed bars from a timeframe lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Absorption ratio = (chart-bar volume / chart-bar volume simple moving average) / max(abs(close − open) / ATR, 0.05), with ATR and volume average calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Prior high/low = highest high/lowest low over the preceding lookback of chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a swing high/low confirmed by bars on both sides; use the most recent such pivot within the search window.
Long: (low < prior low AND high <= prior high AND ((absorption ratio >= threshold AND close > prior low) OR (lower wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) >= wick threshold AND close > open) OR (lower-timeframe order-flow delta > 0 AND volume >= volume simple moving average × exhaustion threshold) OR (volume < volume simple moving average × exhaustion threshold))) OR (volume > 20-chart-bar volume simple moving average × volume-spike threshold AND low < most recent confirmed pivot low AND close > most recent confirmed pivot low AND close − low >= (most recent confirmed pivot low − low) × (1 + reclaim threshold)).
Short: (high > prior high AND low >= prior low AND ((absorption ratio >= threshold AND close < prior high) OR (upper wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) >= wick threshold AND close < open) OR (lower-timeframe order-flow delta < 0 AND volume >= volume simple moving average × exhaustion threshold) OR (volume < volume simple moving average × exhaustion threshold))) OR (volume > 20-chart-bar volume simple moving average × volume-spike threshold AND high > most recent confirmed pivot high AND close < most recent confirmed pivot high AND high − close >= (high − most recent confirmed pivot high) × (1 + reclaim threshold)).
