Calculation: Buy Score = weighted average of available RSI Score, Trend Score, and Support Score, rescaling weights over available components. RSI Score = clamp((overbought level − analysis-timeframe RSI) / (overbought level − oversold level) × 100, 0, 100). Trend Score = 50 if fast EMA > slow EMA, plus 25 if chart close > slow EMA, plus 25 if slow EMA > its value at the longer slope lookback; available only when both EMAs exist. Support Score = the greater of the fast-EMA and slow-EMA proximity scores. Each proximity score is 100 at its EMA and falls linearly with percentage distance, using separate configurable limits above and below; the slow-EMA score is multiplied by its configurable cap. If both EMAs exist and fast EMA ≤ slow EMA, cap Support Score at its configurable non-uptrend limit. Use the shorter configured fallback EMA when a full-length EMA is unavailable and fallback is enabled; omit unavailable score components. The RSI and EMAs use higher-timeframe bars: the still-forming bar in real time and the last completed bar on historical bars. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Long: Buy Score > alert threshold AND previous bar's Buy Score ≤ alert threshold
