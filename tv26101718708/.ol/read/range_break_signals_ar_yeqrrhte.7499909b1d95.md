Calculation: Range bounds = Over the lookback bars, take the highest and lowest candle-body prices (max(open, close) and min(open, close)) or wicks (high and low), according to the selected bounds mode. Accept a range when its width is at most the configured multiple of ATR, and, if enabled, at least the configured minimum multiple of ATR; freeze its bounds when accepted.
Calculation: Active range = An accepted range remains active until a breakout; after a breakout, it becomes active again if price closes beyond the configured invalidation fraction of the range, or a new range may be accepted after the configured wait of lookback bars.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, an active range exists AND close > range top (or the entire candle body is above range top if that trigger option is selected) AND, if enabled, bars since range start meet the minimum age AND, if enabled, candle-body size ≥ configured multiple of ATR AND, if enabled, volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average (or volume or its average is unavailable) AND, if enabled, close is above the selected trend measure (EMA or Ichimoku cloud top) AND, if enabled, the buy confirmation value is nonzero on the current bar or within the configured preceding-bar window.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, an active range exists AND close < range bottom (or the entire candle body is below range bottom if that trigger option is selected) AND, if enabled, bars since range start meet the minimum age AND, if enabled, candle-body size ≥ configured multiple of ATR AND, if enabled, volume ≥ configured multiple of its moving average (or volume or its average is unavailable) AND, if enabled, close is below the selected trend measure (EMA or Ichimoku cloud bottom) AND, if enabled, the sell confirmation value is nonzero on the current bar or within the configured preceding-bar window.
