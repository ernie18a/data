Calculation: RSI = Wilder-smoothed RSI of the selected price source over the selected lookback
Calculation: RSI pivot low/high = an RSI low/high confirmed by the selected number of bars on both sides; use the price low/high and RSI value at the pivot bar
Long: (RSI crosses above the oversold level AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current bar is closed)) OR (divergence calculation is enabled AND the current confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the previous confirmed RSI pivot low AND the price low at the current pivot is lower than at the previous pivot AND the previous pivot is within the allowed bar range)
Short: (RSI crosses below the overbought level AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR the current bar is closed)) OR (divergence calculation is enabled AND the current confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the previous confirmed RSI pivot high AND the price high at the current pivot is higher than at the previous pivot AND the previous pivot is within the allowed bar range)
