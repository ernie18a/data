Calculation: All-time high = highest high over a long lookback of chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: 52-week high = highest high over 52 weekly bars; weekly is higher than the chart timeframe and uses the last completed weekly bar
Calculation: Long trailing ATR stop = highest high since long entry − ATR × multiplier
Long: (All-time-high breakout enabled AND high > prior chart bar's all-time high) OR (52-week breakout enabled AND high > last completed weekly 52-week high)
Exit: Long, (EMA exit selected AND close < EMA) OR (ATR exit selected AND low <= long trailing ATR stop)
