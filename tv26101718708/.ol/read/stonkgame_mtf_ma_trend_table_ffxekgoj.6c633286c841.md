Calculation: State = For each configured horizon, calculate a selected-type moving average of source close; standardize log(close / moving average) and log(moving average / its value at a configurable shorter lookback), with adaptive z-scores over up to the configured maximum valid source bars and requiring the configured minimum sample. Clip both z-scores to the configured limit; multiply the slope z-score by a path-efficiency adjustment when enabled; combine them using normalized configurable weights and optionally smooth with an EMA. Use the configured source timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed source bar when completed-bar mode is enabled, otherwise the bar still forming. The chart-timeframe state uses the current chart bar.
Calculation: Direction-shift long = On a confirmed chart bar, State for the selected marker horizon crosses above its configurable positive shift threshold while armed; arm after State reaches the opposite reset level (half the threshold, capped at a configured maximum), and disarm after the event.
Calculation: Direction-shift short = On a confirmed chart bar, State for the selected marker horizon crosses below the negative shift threshold while armed; arm after State reaches the positive reset level, and disarm after the event.
Calculation: Recovery long = On a confirmed chart bar, State for the selected marker horizon crosses back above the negative recovery threshold while armed; arm after State reaches at least 5% beyond the negative threshold, and disarm after the event.
Calculation: Recovery short = On a confirmed chart bar, State for the selected marker horizon crosses back below the positive recovery threshold while armed; arm after State reaches at least 5% beyond the positive threshold, and disarm after the event.
Long: Any configured horizon State is at or above the bullish-state threshold OR Direction-shift long OR Recovery long
Short: Any configured horizon State is at or below the bearish-state threshold OR Direction-shift short OR Recovery short
