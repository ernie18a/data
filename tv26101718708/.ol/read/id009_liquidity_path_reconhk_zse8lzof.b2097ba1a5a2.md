Calculation: Liquidity Pivot = Over the profile lookback, divide the range from the lowest low to highest high into equal price bins; add each bar’s volume, weighted more for newer bars, to the bin containing its HLC3. Smooth bin activity using neighboring bins weighted inversely by distance. Take the center of the highest-activity bin, smooth it with an EMA, then clamp it to the value area. The value area expands from that bin, adding the more active adjacent bin each step until it contains the selected share of total smoothed activity.
Calculation: Structural liquidity qualified = average daily dollar volume over a shorter daily lookback is at least its minimum; the 10th percentile of daily dollar volume over that same lookback is at least its minimum; and the percentage of zero-volume bars over a recent window on the configured continuity timeframe is at most its maximum. Daily values use the last completed daily bar; continuity values use the last completed bar of that timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Relative volume = current chart-timeframe volume divided by its chart-timeframe moving average.
Calculation: Close location value = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high equals low.
Calculation: Buy-share average = average close location value over a short lookback.
Calculation: Delta improving = EMA of smoothed signed volume is greater than a second, longer-smoothed EMA of that same signed volume; signed volume is positive when close > open, negative when close < open, and zero otherwise.
Long: Close crosses above the Liquidity Pivot OR (Structural liquidity qualified AND close > Liquidity Pivot AND close is more than one minimum price increment above the Liquidity Pivot AND close is no farther above the Liquidity Pivot than the configured maximum percentage AND close is within the mode-dependent maximum percentage distance of the fast EMA AND (the close crossed above the Liquidity Pivot within the mode-dependent reclaim window OR a recent low was within the configured retest band around the Liquidity Pivot and close is above it) AND (path mode is Early OR the Liquidity Pivot is at least as high as it was two bars ago; in Strict mode it must be higher than one bar ago and at least as high as two bars ago) AND (trend filter is disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA is not falling) AND Relative volume >= its configured minimum AND volume >= its moving average AND close > open AND Close location value >= its bullish threshold AND (Delta improving OR Buy-share average >= its threshold OR close > prior bar high) AND more bars have elapsed since the previous path signal than the mode-dependent cooldown).
Short: Close crosses below the Liquidity Pivot
