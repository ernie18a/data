Calculation: Confidence score = clamp(SMA of raw confidence over a smoothing lookback, 0, 1); raw confidence = 0.45 × ((direction score + 1) / 2) + 0.35 × quality score + 0.20 × absolute Bollinger position extremity. Direction score = 0.22 × centered RSI + 0.22 × signed ADX strength + 0.18 × normalized close-to-fast-SMA distance + 0.15 × normalized fast-minus-slow-SMA distance + 0.13 × normalized ROC + 0.10 × close-direction consistency. Quality score = 0.35 × normalized slow-to-fast ATR ratio + 0.25 × normalized volume-to-volume-SMA ratio + 0.25 × normalized Bollinger width + 0.15 × absolute signed ADX strength. Use lookback N; slow ATR uses 3N, slow SMA uses 2N, and ROC standard deviation uses the N-period standard deviation of 2N-period ROC. Normalize features as in their bounded ranges: centered RSI and ROC and SMA distances to [-1,1]; signed ADX is min(ADX/50,1), positive when +DI ≥ -DI and negative otherwise; Bollinger position is (close - lower band)/(upper band - lower band), defaulting to 0.5 when width is zero; Bollinger width is min(width/(4 × ATR),2)/2; slow-to-fast ATR ratio is min(slow ATR/ATR/1.5,1); volume ratio is min(volume/volume SMA/2,1), defaulting to 0.5 if volume SMA is zero; consistency is (2 × count of close[i] > close[i+1] over i=1..N - N)/N. Bollinger bands use 2 standard deviations. Smooth over max(round(N × (1 - prediction weight) × 0.5 + 2), 2) bars.
Calculation: Adaptive trend direction = stateful ratcheting band direction. Each bar, adaptive multiplier = threshold multiplier × (1.40 - 0.80 × confidence score); raw upper/lower bands = (high+low)/2 ± ATR × adaptive multiplier. If previous direction is bearish, ratchet upper band to min(current raw upper, previous upper); otherwise use current raw upper. If previous direction is bullish, ratchet lower band to max(current raw lower, previous lower); otherwise use current raw lower. Regime shift = ATR > 1.5 × slow ATR AND the sign of centered RSI differs from its prior-bar sign AND absolute centered RSI > 0.40. Confidence-adjusted flip threshold = ATR × 0.04 × (1 - 0.5 × confidence score). From bullish state, turn bearish when close < ratcheted lower band - flip threshold OR regime shift and direction score ≥ 0. From bearish state, turn bullish when close > ratcheted upper band + flip threshold OR regime shift and direction score < 0. Initialize direction bullish.
Long: adaptive trend direction on the prior bar = bullish AND adaptive trend direction two bars prior = bearish
Short: adaptive trend direction on the prior bar = bearish AND adaptive trend direction two bars prior = bullish
Exit: Long, (adaptive trend direction on the prior bar = bullish AND adaptive trend direction two bars prior = bearish) OR (adaptive trend direction on the prior bar = bearish AND adaptive trend direction two bars prior = bullish)
