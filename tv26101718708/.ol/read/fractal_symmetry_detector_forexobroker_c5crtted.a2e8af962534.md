Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is the highest within the pivot lookback on both sides, known only after the right-side bars complete; confirmed pivot low = the corresponding lowest low. Keep the latest alternating high/low pivots, replacing the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type.
Calculation: ABC = the latest three alternating pivots A, B, C; retracement ratio = |C − B| / |B − A|. Valid when the ratio is within the configured tolerance of any enabled level: 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, or 0.786.
Long: A and C are pivot lows and B is a pivot high AND a new C pivot completes a valid ABC AND ((close > open AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) OR (a bullish ABC setup was created within the previous 5 bars, inclusive, AND close > open AND close > (high + low) / 2 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier)) AND the chart bar is complete AND bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown bars.
Short: A and C are pivot highs and B is a pivot low AND a new C pivot completes a valid ABC AND ((close < open AND close < (high + low) / 2 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier) OR (a bearish ABC setup was created within the previous 5 bars, inclusive, AND close < open AND close < (high + low) / 2 AND volume >= volume SMA × volume multiplier)) AND the chart bar is complete AND bars since the last long or short signal >= cooldown bars.
