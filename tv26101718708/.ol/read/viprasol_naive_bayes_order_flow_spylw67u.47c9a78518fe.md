Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD) = Sum each bar's volume-weighted close position within its range: volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low + minimum price increment).
Calculation: Normalized CVD momentum = Z-score of (CVD − CVD from the momentum lookback bars ago) / (absolute value of that earlier CVD + minimum price increment), using a rolling normalization window.
Calculation: Normalized price-flow divergence = Z-score, using the same rolling normalization window as normalized CVD momentum, of price rate of change over the momentum lookback minus CVD rate of change over that same lookback.
Calculation: Normalized CVD slope = Z-score, using the same rolling normalization window as the other features, of the first difference between current and prior-offset linear regressions of CVD over the slope lookback.
Calculation: Bull/Bear/Diverged posterior probabilities = Online Gaussian Naive Bayes probabilities for the three classes using the three normalized features as independent inputs; train each bar on the prior bar's features and class, where Bull means price and CVD momentum are both positive, Bear means both are negative, and Diverged means neither class applies. Use observed class frequencies as priors after training, otherwise use a configurable bull prior and split the remainder equally between Bear and Diverged. A class likelihood is the product of the Gaussian densities of its three features using that class's running means and standard deviations; normalize prior × likelihood across classes. Signals are allowed after the accumulated training count reaches the warmup threshold.
Calculation: Trend EMA = Exponential moving average of close.
Calculation: Volume surge = Current volume > rolling average volume × configurable multiplier.
Long: (Bull posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close > Trend EMA) OR (Bull posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close > Trend EMA AND CVD > prior-bar CVD AND prior-bar long state was false AND cooldown has elapsed since any prior signal AND (volume-surge filter is disabled OR Volume surge) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR current bar is confirmed))
Short: (Bear posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close < Trend EMA) OR (Bear posterior ≥ entry threshold AND close < Trend EMA AND CVD < prior-bar CVD AND prior-bar short state was false AND cooldown has elapsed since any prior signal AND (volume-surge filter is disabled OR Volume surge) AND (bar-close confirmation is disabled OR current bar is confirmed))
