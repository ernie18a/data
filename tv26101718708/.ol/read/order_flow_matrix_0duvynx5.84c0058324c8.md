Calculation: Estimated buy/sell volume = split each bar’s volume using the configured method: candle position (volume × (close − low)/(high − low) for buys and volume × (high − close)/(high − low) for sells; split equally if high = low), candle direction (all volume to the side of the close, split equally if close = open), or lower-timeframe intrabars classified by close vs. open, falling back to candle position if unavailable.
Calculation: Volume delta = sum of estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Delta score = clamp(50 + 25 × volume delta / the longer-lookback average absolute volume delta, 0, 100); use 50 if that average is zero.
Calculation: CVD = cumulative estimated buy volume minus estimated sell volume. CVD state is bullish when CVD is above its EMA and its short-term slope is positive, bearish when below its EMA and its short-term slope is negative, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: EMA state = bullish when close > short-term EMA > long-term EMA; bearish when close < short-term EMA < long-term EMA; neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Structure state = compare the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows; bullish when both latest pivots are higher, bearish when both are lower, and neutral otherwise.
Calculation: Timeframe bull score = weighted mean of delta score, CVD state score, EMA state score, and structure state score; bullish component states score 100, bearish 0, and neutral 50.
Calculation: Overall bull score = weighted mean of the available bull scores for visible configured timeframes.
Long: On each configured dashboard timeframe, volume delta > 0 OR CVD > its EMA and CVD short-term slope > 0 OR close > short-term EMA and short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows are both higher OR RSI > 50 OR RSI >= its bullish-color threshold OR the most recent fair value gap is bullish OR the most recent order block is bullish OR timeframe bull score >= its bull threshold.
Short: On each configured dashboard timeframe, volume delta < 0 OR CVD < its EMA and CVD short-term slope < 0 OR close < short-term EMA and short-term EMA < long-term EMA OR the latest two confirmed pivot highs and lows are both lower OR RSI < 50 OR RSI <= its bearish-color threshold OR the most recent fair value gap is bearish OR the most recent order block is bearish OR timeframe bull score <= its bear threshold.
Long: Overall bull score crosses above its bull threshold OR all visible configured timeframes become bullish.
Short: Overall bull score crosses below its bear threshold OR all visible configured timeframes become bearish OR the third configured timeframe changes from bearish to bullish (Long) or bullish to bearish (Short).
Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier, bearish when current high < low two bars earlier; use the most recently formed gap.
Calculation: Order block = the prior opposite-direction candle before an impulse candle whose body is at least the configured multiple of ATR; use the most recent bullish or bearish impulse.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
For calculations on configured dashboard timeframes, use those aggregated bars, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe. Use the still-forming timeframe bar in live mode or the last completed bar in confirmed mode. For the intrabar volume method, use bars aggregated to the configured timeframe lower than the dashboard timeframe.
