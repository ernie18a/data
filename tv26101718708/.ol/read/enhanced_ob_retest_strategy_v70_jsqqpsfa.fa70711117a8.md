Calculation: Swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the configured swing lookback on the higher timeframe, using prior completed bars.
Calculation: BOS up/down = still-forming higher-timeframe close is above/below the swing high/low by at least the configured breakout percentage.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish OB zone = on BOS up/down, the open-close range of the most recent opposing candle among the configured lookback of prior completed higher-timeframe bars, provided its size is at least the configured ATR multiple; measure size against chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Bullish OB depth = (OB top − close) / (OB top − OB bottom); bearish OB depth = (close − OB bottom) / (OB top − OB bottom).
Calculation: High/low wick zone = on a chart bar making the highest high / lowest low in the configured lookback, the range from that high to max(open, close) / from min(open, close) to that low.
Calculation: Long/short overlap trigger = the high-wick zone intersects the bearish OB zone / the low-wick zone intersects the bullish OB zone, and the respective intersection was absent on the prior chart bar.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = for a long, initialize at entry-signal close minus configured ATR multiple × chart-timeframe ATR, then ratchet upward to the greater of its prior value and close minus that amount; for a short, initialize at entry-signal close plus that amount, then ratchet downward to the lesser of its prior value and close plus that amount. If breakeven is enabled, ratchet to at least/at most the entry price after close reaches the respective 1R threshold.
Long: Within the configured date range AND flat AND no overlap between bullish and bearish OB zones AND configured minimum bars have elapsed since the prior trade AND ((bullish OB is enabled AND close is inside the bullish OB AND bullish OB depth is at least the configured minimum retrace and at most the configured maximum entry depth) OR (low < low-wick-zone top AND close crosses above that top) OR long overlap trigger).
Short: Within the configured date range AND flat AND no overlap between bullish and bearish OB zones AND configured minimum bars have elapsed since the prior trade AND ((bearish OB is enabled AND close is inside the bearish OB AND bearish OB depth is at least the configured minimum retrace and at most the configured maximum entry depth) OR (high > high-wick-zone bottom AND close crosses below that bottom) OR short overlap trigger).
Exit: long, low <= long ATR trailing stop
Exit: short, high >= short ATR trailing stop
