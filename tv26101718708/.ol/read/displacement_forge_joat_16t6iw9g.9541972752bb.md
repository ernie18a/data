Calculation: Price-change Z-score = (current close − previous close change − rolling mean of close changes) / rolling standard deviation of close changes; use 0 when standard deviation is 0.
Calculation: Bullish order block = high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle among the preceding impulse-lookback bars, created on a bullish displacement if it does not overlap an existing unbroken zone. Bearish order block is the range of the most recent bullish candle among those bars, created on a bearish displacement under the same overlap rule. Track zones until broken, older than the maximum zone age multiplied by two, or removed to enforce the active-zone limit.
Long: On a completed bar, price-change Z-score > threshold AND close > open AND close > highest close of the preceding impulse-lookback bars AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) OR a tracked bullish order block has low within its range AND NOT (min(open, close) > zone high) AND close > open AND close − low > 0.4 × zone height.
Short: On a completed bar, price-change Z-score < −threshold AND close < open AND close < lowest close of the preceding impulse-lookback bars AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) OR a tracked bearish order block has high within its range AND NOT (max(open, close) < zone low) AND close < open AND high − close > 0.4 × zone height.
