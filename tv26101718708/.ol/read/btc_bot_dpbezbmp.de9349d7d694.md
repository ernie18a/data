Calculation: Pivot point = (high + low + close) / 3, using the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: Third resistance = higher-timeframe high + factor × (pivot point − higher-timeframe low), using the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: Momentum oscillator = 3 × RSI(close, lookback) − 2 × EMA(RSI(close, same lookback), same lookback)
Long: momentum oscillator > 0 AND prior-bar momentum oscillator ≤ 0
Short: prior-bar close > third resistance AND close two bars ago ≤ prior-bar third resistance
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + any of four take-profit percentages, ordered from lower to higher) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − any of four take-profit percentages, ordered from lower to higher) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
