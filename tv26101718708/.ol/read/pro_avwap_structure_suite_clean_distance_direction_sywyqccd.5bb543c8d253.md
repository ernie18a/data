Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (hlc3 × volume) since the anchor divided by cumulative volume since the anchor; use 1 as volume when volume is missing or nonpositive
Calculation: AVWAP touch = low ≤ AVWAP × (1 + sensitivity%) AND high ≥ AVWAP × (1 − sensitivity%)
Long: AVWAP touch AND close ≥ AVWAP for the year-to-date AVWAP, the AVWAP anchored at the first available bar, or the AVWAP anchored when price makes a 252-bar low
Short: AVWAP touch AND close < AVWAP for the year-to-date AVWAP, the AVWAP anchored at the first available bar, or the AVWAP anchored when price makes a 252-bar low
