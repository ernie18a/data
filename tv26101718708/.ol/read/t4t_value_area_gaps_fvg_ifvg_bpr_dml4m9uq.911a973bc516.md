Calculation: Previous-session VAH, VAL, and POC = build a volume profile from the completed session’s lower-timeframe bars (fall back to chart bars if unavailable); distribute each bar’s volume across price rows in proportion to overlap, or count each bar equally if volume is unavailable; POC is the highest-volume row, and the value area expands from POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent row until it contains the selected share of total volume. Use these levels through the next session.
Calculation: Previous-week VAH and VAL = the corresponding value-area boundaries from the completed week’s profile, calculated by the same method; use them through the next week.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current gap-timeframe low is above the high two gap-timeframe bars earlier, and the gap is at least the selected minimum multiple of gap-timeframe ATR(14). Bearish FVG = current gap-timeframe high is below the low two gap-timeframe bars earlier, and the gap meets the same minimum-size condition. For a higher gap timeframe, evaluate on its last completed bar; otherwise use the chart bar.
Calculation: Aligned zone = at least one selected prior-session or prior-week value-area level lies inside the zone or within the selected ATR(14)-based tolerance of either edge. The ATR and gap bars use the gap timeframe; prior-session and prior-week levels use completed sessions and weeks.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = a bearish FVG whose gap-timeframe close crosses above its upper edge; bearish IFVG = a bullish FVG whose gap-timeframe close crosses below its lower edge. The zone takes the opposite direction on inversion and is invalidated by a later close through its other edge.
Calculation: Bullish BPR = the overlap between a newly formed bullish FVG and an opposing gap formed within the selected pairing window; bearish BPR = the overlap formed by a newly formed bearish FVG and an opposing gap within that window. A BPR is invalidated by a close through its opposite edge.
Calculation: First test of a bullish zone = the first chart bar since formation or inversion with low at or below the zone’s upper edge. First test of a bearish zone = the first chart bar since formation or inversion with high at or above the zone’s lower edge.
Long: aligned bullish FVG forms OR aligned bullish IFVG forms OR aligned bullish BPR forms OR first test of an aligned bullish zone.
Short: aligned bearish FVG forms OR aligned bearish IFVG forms OR aligned bearish BPR forms OR first test of an aligned bearish zone.
