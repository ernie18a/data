Calculation: Basis = EMA of (close + (close − close lagged by half the trend lookback)) over the trend lookback
Calculation: Volatility band distance = highest ATR over three trend lookbacks × band multiplier; upper/lower bands = basis ± volatility band distance
Calculation: Trend state = bullish after close crosses above the upper band, bearish after close crosses below the lower band, otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: Momentum oscillator = EMA of (close − basis) / volatility band distance; signal = EMA of oscillator; histogram = oscillator − signal
Calculation: Bullish momentum confirmation = oscillator > signal AND histogram > its prior-bar value; bearish momentum confirmation = oscillator < signal AND histogram < its prior-bar value
Calculation: Bullish momentum = oscillator > minimum momentum AND bullish momentum confirmation; bearish momentum = oscillator < −minimum momentum AND bearish momentum confirmation
Calculation: Price structure = latest confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each using equal left/right pivot lookbacks; bullish structure after a qualifying close break above the pivot high, bearish structure after a qualifying close break below the pivot low
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = trend calculation on a higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar by default or its last completed bar when confirmed higher-timeframe mode is selected; when higher-timeframe influence is disabled, treat bias as neutral
Calculation: Trend regime = ADX at or above its threshold AND absolute normalized price distance from basis at or above its threshold, confirmed for the regime hold lookback; range regime = ADX below 85% of its trend threshold OR low-momentum chop, likewise confirmed for the hold lookback; otherwise transition regime
Calculation: Signal score = weighted sum of trend alignment, higher-timeframe alignment, structure alignment, momentum confirmation, non-extension, trend regime, and breakout/impulse/reclaim/reversal confirmation, normalized to 100 (omit and renormalize the higher-timeframe weight when disabled)
Calculation: Strong bullish reversal = prior oscillator below the negative reversal extreme AND current oscillator > signal AND histogram rising AND close reclaims basis AND strong bullish momentum AND bullish candle body > 40% of candle range AND no strong bearish trend; require bullish structure change as well in stock mode
Calculation: Strong bearish reversal = prior oscillator above the reversal extreme AND current oscillator < signal AND histogram falling AND close rejects basis AND strong bearish momentum AND bearish candle body > 30% of candle range AND no strong bullish trend; require bearish structure change as well in stock mode
Calculation: Breakout = trend and structure agree directionally AND close beyond the corresponding outer band and prior bar high/low AND momentum agrees AND normalized distance is below the breakout extension cap AND market is not chop; higher-timeframe bias must not oppose
Calculation: Impulse = trend and structure agree directionally AND close is on the matching side of basis AND momentum confirms AND histogram has matching sign AND price and histogram advance together or directional expansion is hot AND extension is below the breakout cap AND market is not chop; higher-timeframe bias must not oppose
Calculation: Early reclaim = trend and structure agree directionally AND close crosses basis in the matching direction AND oscillator and histogram improve in that direction AND oscillator has matching sign AND extension is below the entry cap AND market is not chop; higher-timeframe bias must not oppose
Calculation: A continuation = trend, trend slope, mature momentum, higher-timeframe permission, and structure agree directionally AND close crosses basis in the matching direction AND oscillator has matching sign AND extension is below the entry cap; block in range when range blocking is enabled
Calculation: C reversal = countertrend setup where trend is opposite the signal, or directional expansion is hot in the opposite trend, AND momentum confirms the signal direction AND close is on its side of basis AND market is not chop AND histogram magnitude exceeds twice minimum momentum AND basis slope magnitude exceeds 0.3% of ATR
Calculation: B setup arm = qualifying B signal AND a fresh matching trend transition, basis reclaim/rejection, momentum turn, pullback turn, micro-structure event, or C-to-B follow-through; re-entry settings may exclude pullback-turn and micro-structure arm events
Calculation: B breakout entry = after a long/short B setup is armed, close breaks its setup-bar high/low, candle body agrees, and histogram advances in that direction
Calculation: B pullback entry = after a long/short B setup is armed, price revisits its stored basis within an ATR buffer and closes back across it with an agreeing candle and improving oscillator and histogram
Calculation: B qualification = trend context and slope agree, close is on the matching side of basis, higher-timeframe bias is not strongly opposed, strong opposing structure/trend/HTF is absent, and either trend regime or the configured B eligibility rule passes
Calculation: Entry quality = B entry candle range / ATR is below its maximum AND distance from basis / ATR is below its maximum
Calculation: Long target levels = entry price + ATR at entry × each configured target multiple; short target levels = entry price − ATR at entry × each configured target multiple
Calculation: Initial protective stop = configured structure-plus-ATR, ATR-distance, percentage, or disabled stop; structure stop uses the latest protected swing with an ATR buffer and maximum ATR-distance cap
Calculation: Protective stop = initial stop tightened to breakeven after the configured target; after TP2, optionally tighten it with a one-way ATR trail from the highest high/close for longs or lowest low/close for shorts; optionally schedule risk-reduction stop after favorable movement reaches its configured ATR trigger
Long: (A continuation OR strong bullish reversal OR bullish breakout OR bullish impulse OR early bullish reclaim) AND score ≥ A threshold OR (B qualification AND score ≥ B threshold AND B setup arm AND (B breakout entry OR B pullback entry) AND entry quality) OR C reversal, subject to enabled grade selection, long risk/reward filter, signal cooldown/lock, rearm state, flat position, and configured candle-close and directional-close confirmation
Short: (A continuation OR strong bearish reversal OR bearish breakout OR bearish impulse OR early bearish reclaim) AND score ≥ A threshold OR (B qualification AND score ≥ B threshold AND B setup arm AND (B breakout entry OR B pullback entry) AND entry quality) OR C reversal, subject to enabled grade selection, short risk/reward filter, signal cooldown/lock, rearm state, flat position, configured short-entry permission, and configured candle-close and directional-close confirmation
Long: close > latest pivot high AND prior close ≤ that pivot high AND break distance > ATR × structure-break threshold AND structure cooldown passed
Short: close < latest pivot low AND prior close ≥ that pivot low AND break distance > ATR × structure-break threshold AND structure cooldown passed
Exit: long, low ≤ protective stop OR selected target is reached (high ≥ its target) OR configured opposite-signal exit is triggered by a confirmed short A/B signal or any short A/B/C signal
Exit: short, high ≥ protective stop OR selected target is reached (low ≤ its target) OR configured opposite-signal exit is triggered by a confirmed long A/B signal or any long A/B/C signal
