Calculation: Range high/low = prior completed day or week high/low, or the high/low accumulated from chart-timeframe bars in the selected session and locked when that session ends.
Calculation: Average daily range = moving average of daily high minus low over a configurable lookback, using completed daily bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a configurable higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Breakout strength = weighted score from candle body/range, candle range/ATR, volume/average volume, 3- and 5-bar momentum agreement, penetration beyond the range edge/ATR, RSI posture, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, first-break status, and range/average-daily-range; use the configured component tiers and weights.
Calculation: Stop and targets = entry close; stop is configured from the opposite range edge, range midpoint, or ATR distance, with ATR buffer and minimum/maximum ATR risk clamps; targets are configured R multiples of the resulting risk.
Long: range is ready AND (direct mode: close > range high AND prior close <= range high AND close - range high >= configured ATR buffer; retest mode: close first crosses above range high, price subsequently reaches within configured ATR tolerance of that level, and close > that level before a close below it by the tolerance invalidates the setup) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled or close > higher-timeframe EMA or EMA unavailable) AND (ADR filter disabled or range size is within configured minimum and maximum percentages of average daily range) AND configured per-period signal and direction limits permit a long AND breakout strength meets the configured minimum and, if enabled, strong-only threshold AND no active trade and no exit is triggered on this bar.
Short: range is ready AND (direct mode: close < range low AND prior close >= range low AND range low - close >= configured ATR buffer; retest mode: close first crosses below range low, price subsequently reaches within configured ATR tolerance of that level, and close < that level before a close above it by the tolerance invalidates the setup) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter disabled or close < higher-timeframe EMA or EMA unavailable) AND (ADR filter disabled or range size is within configured minimum and maximum percentages of average daily range) AND configured per-period signal and direction limits permit a short AND breakout strength meets the configured minimum and, if enabled, strong-only threshold AND no active trade and no exit is triggered on this bar.
Exit: long, high >= target 3 OR low <= stop OR period boundary.
Exit: short, low <= target 3 OR high >= stop OR period boundary.
