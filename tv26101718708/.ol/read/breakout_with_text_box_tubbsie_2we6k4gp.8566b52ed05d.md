Calculation: Relative distance to medium-long-lookback EMA = |close - medium-long-lookback EMA| / medium-long-lookback EMA
Long: During regular US market hours AND ((short-lookback EMA > medium-short-lookback EMA AND medium-short-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA AND close < open AND relative distance to short-lookback EMA <= the looser of the short-lookback EMA thresholds) OR (medium-short-lookback EMA > medium-long-lookback EMA AND close < open AND medium-long-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA AND relative distance to medium-long-lookback EMA <= its threshold)) OR (medium-short-lookback EMA > medium-long-lookback EMA AND medium-short-lookback EMA >= long-lookback EMA)
Short: During regular US market hours AND ((short-lookback EMA < medium-short-lookback EMA AND medium-short-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA AND close > open AND relative distance to short-lookback EMA <= the looser of the short-lookback EMA thresholds) OR (medium-short-lookback EMA < medium-long-lookback EMA AND close > open AND medium-long-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA AND relative distance to medium-long-lookback EMA <= its threshold)) OR (medium-short-lookback EMA < medium-long-lookback EMA AND medium-short-lookback EMA <= long-lookback EMA) OR medium-short-lookback EMA = medium-long-lookback EMA
