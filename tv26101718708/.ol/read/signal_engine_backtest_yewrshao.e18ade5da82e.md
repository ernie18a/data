Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by its own simple moving average, multiplied by 100
Calculation: Previous day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar, from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous week high/low = high/low of the last completed weekly bar, from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Long: configured long signal condition (long signal crosses above paired series OR crosses below paired series OR crosses above level OR crosses below level OR changes from zero to nonzero) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close > selected moving average) AND (date filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured date range) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio is within configured minimum and maximum) AND (S/R filter disabled OR close is farther than ATR times the configured buffer from every enabled previous day/week high/low) AND no open position
Short: configured short signal condition (short signal crosses above paired series OR crosses below paired series OR crosses above level OR crosses below level OR changes from zero to nonzero) AND (baseline filter disabled OR close < selected moving average) AND (date filter disabled OR bar time is within the configured date range) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= threshold) AND (volatility filter disabled OR volatility ratio is within configured minimum and maximum) AND (S/R filter disabled OR close is farther than ATR times the configured buffer from every enabled previous day/week high/low) AND no open position
Exit: long, low <= stop price AND high < TP1 price OR high >= TP2 price OR high >= TP1 price
Exit: short, high >= stop price AND low > TP1 price OR low <= TP2 price OR low <= TP1 price
