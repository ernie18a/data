Calculation: Opening Range High and Low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, during the configured opening-range period; lock both when that period ends.
Long: During the configured entry window after the opening range ends, close crosses above Opening Range High AND no entry has been taken that day AND the opening range is valid (if range filtering is enabled, its size is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of 14-period ATR) AND (daily close > daily 200-period EMA, if trend filtering is enabled; daily is a higher timeframe, using the non-lookahead daily value) AND (volume > 20-period average volume × configured multiplier, if volume filtering is enabled); if pullback limit entry is enabled, enter at Opening Range High when price reaches that level after the breakout.
Short: During the configured entry window after the opening range ends, close crosses below Opening Range Low AND no entry has been taken that day AND the opening range is valid (if range filtering is enabled, its size is between the configured minimum and maximum multiples of 14-period ATR) AND (daily close < daily 200-period EMA, if trend filtering is enabled; daily is a higher timeframe, using the non-lookahead daily value) AND (volume > 20-period average volume × configured multiplier, if volume filtering is enabled); if pullback limit entry is enabled, enter at Opening Range Low when price reaches that level after the breakout.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= profit target OR current time is at or past the configured cutoff before market close; when breakeven is enabled, move the active stop to entry price once close >= entry price + initial risk.
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= profit target OR current time is at or past the configured cutoff before market close; when breakeven is enabled, move the active stop to entry price once close <= entry price - initial risk.
