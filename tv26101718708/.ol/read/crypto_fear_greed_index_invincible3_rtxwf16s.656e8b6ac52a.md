Calculation: Fear & Greed Index = smoothed, weight-normalized average of: momentum score (50 + 15 times the z-score of close rate-of-change); volatility score (if short-term close rate-of-change is negative, 100 minus the percentile rank of ATR/close, otherwise 50 plus half that rank); RSI; volume pressure (up-volume average divided by the average of up-volume plus down-volume); range position (close within the rolling high-low range); crypto breadth (percentage of major crypto symbols above their EMAs); stablecoin dominance score (50 minus its change times 20); market trend score (average of clamped scores 50 + 250 times each market-cap symbol's percentage distance above its EMA); and BTC dominance context (75 when total-market rate-of-change is positive and BTC dominance falls, 60 when positive and dominance is unchanged or rises, 25 when negative and dominance rises, 35 when negative and dominance is unchanged or falls, otherwise 50). Clamp component scores and final index to 0–100. Use the same chart timeframe for all component data.
Calculation: Bull regime = close > asset EMA AND market trend score >= 50.
Calculation: Bear regime = close < asset EMA AND market trend score < 50.
Calculation: Momentum fading = Fear & Greed Index > 75 AND short-term close rate-of-change < longer-term close rate-of-change.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than a prior pivot low while the corresponding index pivot low is more than 6 points higher; both index pivots <= 45; pivot separation is at least max(3 × pivot length, 12 bars) and no more than the maximum divergence lookback; intervening price lows and index values stay above the straight lines between pivots.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than a prior pivot high while the corresponding index pivot high is more than 6 points lower; both index pivots >= 55; pivot separation is at least max(3 × pivot length, 12 bars) and no more than the maximum divergence lookback; intervening price highs and index values stay below the straight lines between pivots.
Long: Fear & Greed Index crosses above 25 AND bull regime OR Fear & Greed Index crosses above 55 AND bull regime OR regular bullish divergence.
Short: regular bearish divergence.
Exit: long, Fear & Greed Index > 75 AND short-term close rate-of-change < longer-term close rate-of-change AND momentum fading was false on the prior bar.
