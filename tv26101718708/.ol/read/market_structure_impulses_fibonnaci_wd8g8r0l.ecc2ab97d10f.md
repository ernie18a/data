Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = alternating highs and lows confirmed on bar close after price reverses from the running extreme by a configurable ATR-based or percentage threshold; the extreme-making bar cannot also confirm the pivot.
Calculation: Internal impulse = group of alternating pivot legs in one direction, extending its extreme when a pivot makes a new extreme; end it when retracement from the extreme reaches the internal threshold, the counter-leg breaks the previous pullback swing, or price breaks the origin. A broken origin immediately starts an opposite impulse from the prior extreme.
Calculation: External impulse = same pivot-leg grouping, using a separate, generally deeper retracement threshold; end it on retracement depth or origin break, without the internal structure-break rule.
Calculation: Impulse retracement = distance from its extreme to the furthest adverse price since that extreme, divided by the absolute distance from origin to extreme.
Calculation: Fast MACD = MACD line and signal line on the chart timeframe; bullish when line > signal, bearish when line < signal.
Calculation: Slow MACD = MACD line and signal line using longer-term parameters on a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Bullish/bearish permission is respectively line > 0 / < 0, line > / < signal, or both, according to the selected rule.
Calculation: Swept pivot = a confirmed pivot whose level is pierced by a later bar's wick and whose close returns across the level within the configured decision window; if it does not return within that window, treat it as broken, not swept.
Calculation: Sweep divergence = for a high pivot, price's highest high from piercing through sweep exceeds the pivot while the fast MACD maximum in that interval is below its value at the pivot; for a low pivot, price's lowest low is below the pivot while the fast MACD minimum is above its value at the pivot.
Long: On a confirmed bar, external impulse is bullish AND slow MACD is bullish AND external impulse retracement is at least the internal threshold and less than the external threshold AND price traded into the pullback band within the trigger window AND fast MACD line > signal line AND no prior trend signal has fired for this external impulse extreme.
Short: On a confirmed bar, external impulse is bearish AND slow MACD is bearish AND external impulse retracement is at least the internal threshold and less than the external threshold AND price traded into the pullback band within the trigger window AND fast MACD line < signal line AND no prior trend signal has fired for this external impulse extreme.
Long: On a confirmed bar within the trigger window after a low pivot is swept, price has not since traded below the sweep wick AND (divergence is not required OR sweep divergence holds) AND fast MACD line > signal line AND close > swept pivot level.
Short: On a confirmed bar within the trigger window after a high pivot is swept, price has not since traded above the sweep wick AND (divergence is not required OR sweep divergence holds) AND fast MACD line < signal line AND close < swept pivot level.
