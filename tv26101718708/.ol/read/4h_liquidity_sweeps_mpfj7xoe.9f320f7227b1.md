Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a pivot high on the sweep timeframe (higher than or equal to the chart timeframe), with each of the preceding pivot-strength bars having a lower high and each of the following pivot-strength bars having a high less than or equal to it; use only after those following bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a pivot low on the sweep timeframe (higher than or equal to the chart timeframe), with each of the preceding pivot-strength bars having a higher low and each of the following pivot-strength bars having a low greater than or equal to it; use only after those following bars complete.
Calculation: ATR(14) = 14-period average true range on the sweep timeframe, using the last completed bar.
Long: On the last completed sweep-timeframe bar, low < an unswept confirmed swing low − (minimum penetration setting × ATR(14)), AND (close > that swing low if close-back is required), AND (close ≥ the previous sweep-timeframe bar's low in Loose displacement mode, or close ≥ its midpoint in Strict mode), AND (the bar's open time is in a selected killzone if Filter out mode is active)
Short: On the last completed sweep-timeframe bar, high > an unswept confirmed swing high + (minimum penetration setting × ATR(14)), AND (close < that swing high if close-back is required), AND (close ≤ the previous sweep-timeframe bar's high in Loose displacement mode, or close ≤ its midpoint in Strict mode), AND (the bar's open time is in a selected killzone if Filter out mode is active)
