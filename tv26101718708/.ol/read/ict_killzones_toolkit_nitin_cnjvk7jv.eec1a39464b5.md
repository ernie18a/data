Calculation: Swing high/low = A swing high is recorded at the bar `L` bars ago when its high exceeds the highest high of the latest `L` bars and the swing state changes to high; a swing low is recorded at the bar `L` bars ago when its low is below the lowest low of the latest `L` bars and the state changes to low. The latest swing of each kind remains active until broken.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = A high/low with `L` bars on both sides; it becomes available after the `L` bars to its right have completed.
Long: Inside an enabled killzone AND (the prior close is above the latest unbroken swing high formed since the current killzone began OR the prior close is above the latest confirmed pivot high, that pivot has not already been broken, and the current shift state is neutral or short OR the low is above the high two bars ago, the prior close is above that high, the gap exceeds ATR(144), and neither of the two intervening bars forms a direct bullish gap)
Short: Inside an enabled killzone AND (the prior close is below the latest unbroken swing low formed since the current killzone began OR the prior close is below the latest confirmed pivot low, that pivot has not already been broken, and the current shift state is neutral or long OR the high is below the low two bars ago, the prior close is below that low, the gap exceeds ATR(144), and neither of the two intervening bars forms a direct bearish gap)
