Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs = exponential moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Upper/lower price bands = prior close plus/minus ATR multiplied by a parameter.
Calculation: PAC upper/lower = highest high / lowest low over a lookback, expanded outward by a parameter times the standard deviation of close.
Calculation: Bollinger width = (upper band − lower band) / basis, using a simple moving average basis and standard-deviation bands.
Calculation: Manual ADX = Wilder-smoothed directional movement and true range combined into the standard ADX calculation.
Calculation: OBV bullish = OBV above its simple moving average, OBV slope over a lookback positive, moving-average slope positive, and close above its lookback close; OBV bearish reverses all comparisons.
Calculation: Trend validation = ADX score (higher ADX gives a higher score) + directional MACD, RSI, EMA, and volume scores; valid when total score reaches a threshold and short-term EMA is above (up) or below (down) long-term EMA. Up scores MACD positive, RSI above its upper threshold, and short-term EMA above long-term EMA; down scores the inverse. Volume scores when volume exceeds its moving average.
Calculation: Trend direction = short-term EMA above long-term EMA with close above short-term EMA (up), or both below (down); ranging means neither.
Calculation: Entry confidence = absolute MACD difference divided by its moving average of absolute values; confident when it meets a threshold.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume divided by its moving average exceeds a threshold; volume increasing = that moving average exceeds its prior value.
Calculation: Confidence weight = 0.30 if either trend is validated + 0.25 if volume spikes + 0.20 if either OBV direction is confirmed + 0.25 if higher-timeframe MACD is bullish or bearish; block entries below 0.5.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = trend direction calculated on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA and MACD filters = EMA and MACD calculated on higher timeframes, using their last completed bars.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe volatility mode = ATR compared with its moving average on a higher timeframe, using the last completed bar; above 1.2 times the average selects High strictness, above the average selects Medium, otherwise Low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish reversal = RSI in its oversold/overbought zone and MACD crossing above/below its signal line; approaching reversal uses the same RSI zone with MACD already above/below its signal line. Reversal cooldown starts after an opposite confirmed reversal and counts down for a parameterized number of bars.
Calculation: Strong trend score = ADX score + higher-timeframe MACD direction score + RSI direction score + EMA direction score + volume score; EMA contributes two when short-term EMA is above/below long-term EMA, volume contributes one when above its moving average.
Calculation: Accurate long stop = the greater of entry price minus ATR times a parameter and the lowest low over a lookback; accurate short stop = the lesser of entry price plus ATR times a parameter and the highest high over a lookback. Accurate targets are entry price plus/minus stop risk times a parameter.
Calculation: Long trailing stop = after price advances from entry by ATR times a parameter, the highest high since activation minus ATR times a parameter; short trailing stop is the lowest low since activation plus the offset.
Long: bullish reversal and not in cooldown OR oversold RSI with MACD above its signal line and not in cooldown OR the applicable strictness entry condition below, with confidence weight at least 0.5, a new signal close different from the prior signal close, and higher-timeframe trend up OR the accurate execution condition below OR trend-validated up, confidence at least 0.85, volume spike, bullish higher-timeframe MACD, strong trend score above 2, and close above the prior lookback highs.
Long: High strictness: trend validation up AND not ranging AND bullish higher-timeframe MACD AND close above prior high AND close above open AND close above upper price band; Medium: trend validation up AND (bullish higher-timeframe MACD OR MACD positive) AND (close above prior high OR short-term EMA) AND (close above open OR RSI above 50) AND close above 98% of upper price band; Low: trend validation up AND (bullish higher-timeframe MACD OR MACD positive) AND (close above open OR RSI above 50) AND close above 98% of upper price band. Low strictness also allows ranging when MACD is positive, RSI above 55, volume spikes, and close is above short-term EMA and upper price band.
Long: accurate execution condition = higher-strictness entry signal AND bullish candle body greater than half the bar range AND Bollinger width above its threshold AND ADX above its threshold AND confidence at least 85%.
Short: bearish reversal and not in cooldown OR overbought RSI with MACD below its signal line and not in cooldown OR the applicable strictness entry condition below, with confidence weight at least 0.5, a new signal close different from the prior signal close, and higher-timeframe trend down OR the accurate execution condition below OR trend-validated down, confidence at least 0.85, volume spike, bearish higher-timeframe MACD, strong trend score above 2, and close below the prior lookback lows.
Short: High strictness: trend validation down AND not ranging AND bearish higher-timeframe MACD AND close below prior low AND close below open AND close below lower price band; Medium: trend validation down AND (bearish higher-timeframe MACD OR MACD negative) AND (close below prior low OR short-term EMA) AND (close below open OR RSI below 50) AND close below 102% of lower price band; Low: trend validation down AND (bearish higher-timeframe MACD OR MACD negative) AND (close below open OR RSI below 50) AND close below 102% of lower price band. Low strictness also allows ranging when MACD is negative, RSI below 45, volume spikes, and close is below short-term EMA and lower price band.
Short: accurate execution condition = higher-strictness entry signal AND bearish candle body greater than half the bar range AND Bollinger width above its threshold AND ADX above its threshold AND confidence at least 85%.
Exit: long, higher-timeframe trend down OR low <= active stop OR high >= active limit.
Exit: short, higher-timeframe trend up OR high >= active stop OR low <= active limit.
