Calculation: Trend regime = ADX is at or above its trend threshold AND current ATR divided by its longer-lookback average is at or above its minimum ratio
Calculation: Range regime = trend regime is false AND the absolute change in the long-term EMA over its slope lookback is less than ATR times its flatness threshold
Calculation: Directional bias = in trend regime, higher-timeframe close above its long-term EMA gives long bias and below gives short bias, only when higher-timeframe ADX meets the trend threshold; in range regime, chart close at or below the rolling lower close percentile gives long bias and at or above the rolling upper close percentile gives short bias; otherwise bias is neutral. Use the higher timeframe’s last completed bar.
Calculation: Long pullback state = while bias is long, a bar with low at or below the short-term EMA plus ATR times the pullback-zone width and close above the EMA activates the state and stores that bar’s high; each qualifying bar refreshes the stored high and resets its age; deactivate when age exceeds the pullback-memory limit.
Calculation: Short pullback state = while bias is short, a bar with high at or above the short-term EMA minus ATR times the pullback-zone width and close below the EMA activates the state and stores that bar’s low; each qualifying bar refreshes the stored low and resets its age; deactivate when age exceeds the pullback-memory limit.
Calculation: Setup invalidation = for long bias, close at or below the reference stop invalidates the setup until close recovers above the stop by ATR times the recovery threshold; for short bias, close at or above the reference stop invalidates it until close recovers below the stop by that amount; reset invalidation when bias changes.
Calculation: Reference stop = for long bias, the nearer of the structural floor minus an ATR buffer and the short-term EMA minus ATR times the stop multiple; for short bias, the nearer of the structural ceiling plus an ATR buffer and the short-term EMA plus ATR times the stop multiple. Ensure the stop is at least one ATR from the EMA, then cap its distance from the EMA at ATR times the maximum-stop multiple.
Calculation: Initial target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the initial stop distance times the target-to-risk multiple; snapshot the reference stop and target when the trade opens.
Calculation: Trailing stop = after close-based unrealized profit reaches the configured multiple of initial risk, calculate a long candidate as the highest high over the trailing lookback minus ATR times the trailing multiple, or a short candidate as the lowest low over that lookback plus that amount; thereafter only raise the long trail or lower the short trail.
Long: long bias AND long pullback state active AND close > stored pullback high AND setup not invalidated
Short: short bias AND short pullback state active AND close < stored pullback low AND setup not invalidated
Exit: long, close <= the greater of initial stop and armed trailing stop OR close >= initial target OR directional bias is neutral or short
Exit: short, close >= the lesser of initial stop and armed trailing stop OR close <= initial target OR directional bias is neutral or long
