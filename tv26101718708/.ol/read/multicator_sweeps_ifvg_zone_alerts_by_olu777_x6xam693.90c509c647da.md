Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = price wicks beyond an unmitigated confirmed swing low/high and closes back above/below it; also allow a close-break followed by a wick beyond and close back across the level when the sweep mode permits it.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = a three-bar gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier, and the gap exceeds the ATR-based volatility threshold. An inverse FVG completes when close crosses the far edge of an active opposite FVG; it begins when price enters that gap while close remains within it.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = previous candle is bearish/bullish and extends below/above the candle before it; current candle is bullish/bearish with body at least the ATR-based displacement threshold, forms a same-direction FVG at least the ATR-based size threshold, and closes beyond the latest confirmed swing high/low. A tap occurs on the first bar whose range overlaps an active block before mitigation. A strong tap additionally requires the block quality score to be at least 75; score adds 40/30/20 for displacement body at least 1.5/1.0/<1.0 ATR, 30/20/10 for FVG size at least 1.0/0.6/<0.6 ATR, and 30/20/10 for volume at least 1.25/1.0/<1.0 times its 20-bar average.
Calculation: Support/resistance rejection = an active zone centered on a confirmed swing pivot, initially extending by ATR at the pivot times a width multiplier on both sides; merge overlapping or nearby zones. A zone is support when close is at or above its midpoint, resistance otherwise. Rejection requires the bar range to overlap the zone and close above its top for support or below its bottom for resistance.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CISD = after a bullish/bearish sweep and a close above/below the swept level, save that sweep bar's high/low; within the configured validity bars, a later close above/below that saved high/low triggers the signal.
Calculation: Zero-lag trend on each configured timeframe = retain bullish state after close crosses above an EMA of close plus its deviation from close half the EMA length earlier, plus highest ATR over three times the EMA length times a multiplier; retain bearish state after close crosses below the corresponding lower band. Use that timeframe's still-forming bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe, and its current bar otherwise.
Long: bullish sweep OR bullish inverse FVG begins OR bullish inverse FVG completes OR bullish sweep zone is first touched at its top OR bullish order block is created OR bullish order block is first tapped OR strong bullish order block is first tapped OR support rejection OR bullish CISD OR zero-lag trend state is bullish.
Short: bearish sweep OR bearish inverse FVG begins OR bearish inverse FVG completes OR bearish sweep zone is first touched at its top OR bearish order block is created OR bearish order block is first tapped OR strong bearish order block is first tapped OR resistance rejection OR bearish CISD OR zero-lag trend state is bearish.
