Calculation: Support and resistance = cluster confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows whose prices are within the configured tolerance; count closes within tolerance of each cluster over the lookback, keep clusters with at least the minimum touches, and select the nearest qualifying level below close as support and above close as resistance. Tolerance is the configured ATR-based or percentage-based amount, whichever mode is selected, with a floor of two minimum price ticks.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its moving average over a lookback shorter than or equal to the configured volume window.
Long: Close crosses above resistance plus its tolerance (current close above the current threshold and prior close at or below the prior threshold) AND ADX is at least its threshold AND relative volume is at least its surge threshold AND the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA OR low is at or below support plus its tolerance AND close is above support plus its tolerance AND close is above open AND NOT (close crosses above resistance plus its tolerance AND ADX is at least its threshold AND relative volume is at least its surge threshold AND the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA)
Short: Close crosses below support minus its tolerance (current close below the current threshold and prior close at or above the prior threshold) AND relative volume is at least its surge threshold
