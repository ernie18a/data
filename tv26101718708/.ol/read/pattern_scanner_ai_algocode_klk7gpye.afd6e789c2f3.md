Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured bars on both sides; use only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: H0, H1, H2 / L0, L1, L2 = latest, preceding, and second-preceding confirmed pivot highs / lows, with their prices and bar indices
Calculation: Equality tolerance = ATR × tolerance factor or close × percentage factor
Calculation: Near(a,b) = |a − b| ≤ equality tolerance
Calculation: Break below/above level = close crosses below/above level −/+ ATR × breakout factor, previous close is at/above/below that threshold, and (body filter disabled or min(open,close) < level / max(open,close) > level), and (volume filter disabled, volume unavailable, or volume > 20-bar volume SMA × volume factor)
Calculation: Valid(entry, stop, target, start) = current bar − start is within minimum and maximum pattern width, |entry − stop| ≥ ATR × minimum-height factor × 0.5, |target − entry| ≥ ATR × minimum-height factor, |target − entry| / max(|entry − stop|, minimum tick) + 0.15 ≥ minimum reward-to-risk, and current bar − last signal bar ≥ cooldown
Long: (Near(L0,L1) AND H0 > max(L0,L1) AND L1 bar index < H0 bar index < L0 bar index AND Break above H0 AND Valid(H0,min(L0,L1),H0 + (H0 − average(L0,L1)),L1 bar index)) OR (Near(L0,L1) AND Near(L1,L2) AND H0 and H1 exist AND Break above max(H0,H1) AND Valid(max(H0,H1),min(L0,L1,L2),max(H0,H1) + (max(H0,H1) − average(L0,L1)),L2 bar index)) OR (L1 < L0 − tolerance AND L1 < L2 − tolerance AND Near(L0,L2) AND Break above average(H0,H1) AND Valid(average(H0,H1),L1,average(H0,H1) + (average(H0,H1) − L1),L2 bar index)) OR (H0 < H1 < H2 AND L0 < L1 < L2 AND H0 − L0 < 0.7 × (H2 − L2) AND H2 − L2 > ATR × minimum-height factor AND Break above H0 AND Valid(H0,L0,H0 + (H2 − L2),min(H2 bar index,L2 bar index)))
Short: (Near(H0,H1) AND L0 < min(H0,H1) AND H1 bar index < L0 bar index < H0 bar index AND Break below L0 AND Valid(L0,max(H0,H1),L0 − (average(H0,H1) − L0),H1 bar index)) OR (Near(H0,H1) AND Near(H1,H2) AND L0 and L1 exist AND Break below min(L0,L1) AND Valid(min(L0,L1),max(H0,H1,H2),min(L0,L1) − (average(H0,H1) − min(L0,L1)),H2 bar index)) OR (H1 > H0 + tolerance AND H1 > H2 + tolerance AND Near(H0,H2) AND Break below average(L0,L1) AND Valid(average(L0,L1),H1,average(L0,L1) − (H1 − average(L0,L1)),H2 bar index)) OR (H0 > H1 > H2 AND L0 > L1 > L2 AND H0 − L0 < 0.7 × (H2 − L2) AND H2 − L2 > ATR × minimum-height factor AND Break below L0 AND Valid(L0,H0,L0 − (H2 − L2),min(H2 bar index,L2 bar index)))
