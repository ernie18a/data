Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides, calculated on a higher timeframe using its still-forming bar.
Calculation: Resistance/support trendline = the line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs/lows, projected to the current chart bar.
Long: chart close crosses above the resistance trendline
Short: chart close crosses below the support trendline
