Calculation: Buyer-volume share = when volume > 0 and high > low, 100 × (close − low) / (high − low); otherwise 0
Long: (shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close) AND close > long-lookback SMA(close)) OR close > long-lookback SMA(close) OR Buyer-volume share > 60 OR (volume > 2 × SMA(volume, lookback equal to the shorter-lookback EMA) AND high > low AND abs(close − open) / (high − low) > 0.6 AND close > shorter-lookback EMA(close) AND close > open)
