Calculation: Previous-day high/low = highest high/lowest low of the last completed session; previous-week high/low and previous-month high/low = highest high/lowest low of the last completed week and month, using the same reset boundaries.
Calculation: ATR high/low = current session open ± ATR from the last completed bar of the selected timeframe × a distance multiplier; fixed for the session.
Calculation: Confirmation ATR = ATR from the last completed bar of its selected timeframe; if that timeframe is the chart timeframe, use the current chart timeframe.
Long: After a prior bar's high exceeds any previous-day, previous-week, previous-month, or ATR high, within the confirmation window on a later bar: close > that level AND close > open AND (distance from close to level ≥ minimum ATR distance OR the minimum distance is zero).
Long: After a prior bar's low falls below any previous-day, previous-week, previous-month, or ATR low, within the confirmation window on a later bar: close > that level AND close > open AND (distance from close to level ≥ minimum ATR distance OR the minimum distance is zero).
Short: After a prior bar's high exceeds any previous-day, previous-week, previous-month, or ATR high, within the confirmation window on a later bar: close < that level AND close < open AND (distance from close to level ≥ minimum ATR distance OR the minimum distance is zero).
Short: After a prior bar's low falls below any previous-day, previous-week, previous-month, or ATR low, within the confirmation window on a later bar: close < that level AND close < open AND (distance from close to level ≥ minimum ATR distance OR the minimum distance is zero).
