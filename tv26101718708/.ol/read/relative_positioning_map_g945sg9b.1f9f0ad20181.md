Calculation: Relative Strength = (instrument momentum return / instrument EWMA return volatility − benchmark momentum return / benchmark EWMA return volatility) / rolling standard deviation of that difference; use the same momentum lookback and EWMA span for both, with EWMA span shorter than the momentum lookback. Calculate on the user-selected analysis timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe, using its currently forming bar.
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured pre-market session, accumulated on the user-selected analysis timeframe.
Calculation: Relative-strength leader/laggard = the instrument with the highest/lowest Relative Strength among the configured instruments.
Long: The opening-range session has ended AND breakout direction is unset AND close > opening-range high OR the Relative-strength leader has held the highest rank for the configured number of consecutive confirmed bars AND its absolute Relative Strength is at least the configured threshold AND this leader has not already been labeled.
Short: The opening-range session has ended AND breakout direction is unset AND close < opening-range low OR the Relative-strength laggard has held the lowest rank for the configured number of consecutive confirmed bars AND its absolute Relative Strength is at least the configured threshold AND this laggard has not already been labeled.
