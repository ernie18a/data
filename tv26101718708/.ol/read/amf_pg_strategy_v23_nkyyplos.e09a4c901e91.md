Calculation: Hurst exponent = For log(close) over a lookback, take the cumulative sum of each observation's deviation from its lookback SMA; divide the range of that cumulative sum by the standard deviation of the deviations, then take the logarithm of the ratio divided by the logarithm of the lookback.
Calculation: AMF PG trail = On chart bars, adapt a lookback between a shorter and longer period using the current standard deviation of close-to-close returns normalized within its rolling range. Let the volatility length be that adaptive lookback and its shorter companion be about two-thirds of it. Smooth true range with an EMA of the volatility length to get N; let K be SMA(N, 3), L be SMA(N, companion), and A=min(K/L, 3). Let V be the double EMA of close over the volatility length, E=(highest high + lowest low)/2 over that length, G be the average of SMA(volume,3), SMA(volume,5), and SMA(volume,8), smoothed by EMA(5), and F=min(((SMA(volume,3)+volume)/2)/G,3). Set center=(V*F+E*(2-F))/2; raw bands=center ± K*A*F ± half an EMA of true range over 14, using plus for the upper and minus for the lower band. A flow impulse exists when the 3-bar SMA of close changes exceeds the 8-bar SMA by more than a volatility-scaled threshold, or is below it by more than that threshold; the threshold is the 8-bar standard deviation of close changes times a multiplier clamped from 1.5 to 3.5, based on the ratio of 20-bar to 100-bar Bollinger bandwidths. Confirm an impulse when either the 3-bar vs. 8-bar ATR comparison or 3-bar vs. 8-bar volume comparison exceeds its corresponding 8-bar standard deviation times that multiplier. On an impulse, use close ± K times a configurable multiplier as the bands; otherwise use the raw bands. The trail is stateful: initialize its direction from the first impulse; in neutral direction, close above the upper band with upward flow starts long and close below the lower band with downward flow starts short. In long direction, trail the lower band upward using its maximum with the prior trail; close below it with downward flow flips short and resets the trail to the upper band. In short direction, trail the upper band downward using its minimum with the prior trail; close above it with upward flow flips long and resets the trail to the lower band. Upward/downward flow means the shorter DMI has ADX above its threshold and +DI/-DI, respectively, above the other.
Long: close > AMF PG trail AND shorter DMI ADX > its threshold AND +DI > -DI AND (long-term ADX > its threshold when enabled) AND (Choppiness Index < its threshold when enabled) AND (long-term +DI > -DI when enabled) AND (Hurst exponent > its threshold AND ATR volatility expansion is false when enabled) AND last emitted signal side is not long AND drawdown protection is inactive
Short: close < AMF PG trail AND shorter DMI ADX > its threshold AND -DI > +DI AND (long-term ADX > its threshold when enabled) AND (Choppiness Index < its threshold when enabled) AND (long-term -DI > +DI when enabled) AND (Hurst exponent > its threshold AND ATR volatility expansion is false when enabled) AND last emitted signal side is not short AND drawdown protection is inactive
Exit: long, short decision condition
Exit: short, long decision condition
