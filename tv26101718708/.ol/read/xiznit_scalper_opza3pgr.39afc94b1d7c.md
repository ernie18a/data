Calculation: Efficiency Ratio = absolute(close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute close-to-prior-close changes over the same N bars
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted HLC3 since the start of the active session
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier; long target = signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio; keep both levels fixed for the trade
Calculation: Short stop = signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier; short target = signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier × reward-to-risk ratio; keep both levels fixed for the trade
Long: position is flat AND within an enabled session with that session’s trade count below its maximum AND bars since the prior entry ≥ cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR current close > EMA calculated from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (VWAP-bias filter disabled or the selected mode is VWAP Deviation OR current close > session VWAP) AND (ROC filter disabled OR ROC > 0) AND ((selected mode is EMA Cross AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ trending threshold) OR (selected mode is VWAP Deviation AND (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP < −deviation threshold AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ ranging threshold) OR (selected mode is Heikin Ashi + RSI AND current and prior Heikin Ashi candles are bullish AND RSI > long threshold AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ trending threshold) OR (selected mode is Price Structure AND, over the selected 2- or 3-bar lookback, highs and lows each rise consecutively AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ ranging threshold))
Short: position is flat AND within an enabled session with that session’s trade count below its maximum AND bars since the prior entry ≥ cooldown AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average × multiplier) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR current close < EMA calculated from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (VWAP-bias filter disabled or the selected mode is VWAP Deviation OR current close < session VWAP) AND (ROC filter disabled OR ROC < 0) AND ((selected mode is EMA Cross AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ trending threshold) OR (selected mode is VWAP Deviation AND (close − session VWAP) / session VWAP > deviation threshold AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ ranging threshold) OR (selected mode is Heikin Ashi + RSI AND current and prior Heikin Ashi candles are bearish AND RSI < short threshold AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ trending threshold) OR (selected mode is Price Structure AND, over the selected 2- or 3-bar lookback, highs and lows each fall consecutively AND Efficiency Ratio ≥ ranging threshold))
Exit: Long, low ≤ fixed long stop OR high ≥ fixed long target
Exit: Short, high ≥ fixed short stop OR low ≤ fixed short target
