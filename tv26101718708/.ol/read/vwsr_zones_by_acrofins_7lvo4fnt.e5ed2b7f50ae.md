Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configurable number of bars on each side; confirmed after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Resistance/support zone = an unbroken zone created from a confirmed pivot high/low, with bounds padded by ATR; merge same-type zones within a configurable ATR distance, expanding bounds and updating score and touch count; decay scores over time and remove zones exceeding the age limit or falling below the minimum viable score; if above the zone-count cap, remove the lowest-scored zones
Calculation: Zone score = base score plus a volume score based on pivot-bar volume relative to its prior-volume average and a reaction score based on the greatest price move away from the pivot during the reaction window, measured in ATR; cap at the score maximum
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = bullish when higher-timeframe close and fast EMA are both above the slow EMA; bearish when both are below; otherwise neutral; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Long stop and targets = stop is the farther-from-entry of entry minus ATR-based stop distance and the latest broken resistance zone's bottom minus an ATR buffer; targets are entry plus stop distance multiplied by the configured target risk multiples
Calculation: Short stop and targets = stop is the farther-from-entry of entry plus ATR-based stop distance and the latest broken support zone's top plus an ATR buffer; targets are entry minus stop distance multiplied by the configured target risk multiples
Long: On a confirmed bar, close crosses above an unbroken resistance zone's top by the configured ATR clearance AND the zone has the highest score among eligible breaks that bar AND enabled volume and candle-range filters pass; OR, within the configured bar window after a bullish break, low touches the broken zone's top AND close is above that top AND close minus low meets the configured ATR reaction threshold; AND enabled session, expiry, intraday-entry-time, daily-loss-cap, and counter-trend filters pass
Short: On a confirmed bar, close crosses below an unbroken support zone's bottom by the configured ATR clearance AND the zone has the highest score among eligible breaks that bar AND enabled volume and candle-range filters pass; OR, within the configured bar window after a bearish break, high touches the broken zone's bottom AND close is below that bottom AND high minus close meets the configured ATR reaction threshold; AND enabled session, expiry, intraday-entry-time, daily-loss-cap, and counter-trend filters pass
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR high >= third profit target OR intraday force-close time is reached
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR low <= third profit target OR intraday force-close time is reached
