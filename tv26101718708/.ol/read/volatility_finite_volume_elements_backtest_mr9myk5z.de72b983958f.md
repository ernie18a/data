Calculation: FVE = 100 × the sum over the same lookback of volume × flow direction, divided by the sum of volume over that lookback; flow direction is +1 when close − (high + low)/2 + typical price − prior bar’s typical price exceeds the cutoff × close, −1 when it is below the negative of that threshold, and 0 otherwise; typical price = (high + low + close)/3; cutoff = intra-period coefficient × intra-period volatility + inter-period coefficient × inter-period volatility; intra-period volatility = standard deviation over the same lookback of the simple moving average over that lookback of log(high) − log(low); inter-period volatility = standard deviation over the same lookback of the simple moving average over that lookback of log(typical price / prior bar’s typical price)
Calculation: FVE EMA = exponential moving average of FVE
Long: (FVE > FVE EMA AND reverse is false) OR (FVE < FVE EMA AND reverse is true)
