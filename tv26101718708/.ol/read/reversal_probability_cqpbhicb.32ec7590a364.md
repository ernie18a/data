Calculation: Bullish probability = 100 × the number of enabled bullish factors met divided by the number of enabled factors, rounded to the nearest integer; bullish factors are RSI below its oversold threshold, volume above 1.5 times its 20-bar average with close > open, close below the midpoint of the lookback high-low range, lower wick at least the configured fraction of the bar’s high-low range, and close above the EMA.
Calculation: Bearish probability = 100 × the number of enabled bearish factors met divided by the number of enabled factors, rounded to the nearest integer; bearish factors are RSI above its overbought threshold, volume above 1.5 times its 20-bar average with close < open, close above the midpoint of the lookback high-low range, upper wick at least the configured fraction of the bar’s high-low range, and close below the EMA.
Long: Close is below every low in the preceding lookback bars, then within the confirmation window close is above that setup bar’s high while not below its low, and (probability filter is disabled OR bullish probability is at least the minimum threshold)
Short: Close is above every high in the preceding lookback bars, then within the confirmation window close is below that setup bar’s low while not above its high, and (probability filter is disabled OR bearish probability is at least the minimum threshold)
