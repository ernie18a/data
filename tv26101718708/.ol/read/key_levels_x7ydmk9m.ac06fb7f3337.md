Calculation: Initial session range = highest high and lowest low from the start of an enabled session through its initial range period; lock these levels when that period ends.
Long: The locked range is active for the current session AND, since that session began, price traded above its high AND a bullish wick retest occurred after that trade (low <= range high AND close > range high) AND, since the most recent bullish retest and before an opposite retest, fewer than two breakout FVGs have occurred AND low > high two bars ago AND (the bullish FVG touches the range high within the configured tick tolerance OR the bullish FVG is fully above the range high within that tolerance)
Short: The locked range is active for the current session AND, since that session began, price traded below its low AND a bearish wick retest occurred after that trade (high >= range low AND close < range low) AND, since the most recent bearish retest and before an opposite retest, fewer than two breakout FVGs have occurred AND high < low two bars ago AND (the bearish FVG touches the range low within the configured tick tolerance OR the bearish FVG is fully below the range low within that tolerance)
