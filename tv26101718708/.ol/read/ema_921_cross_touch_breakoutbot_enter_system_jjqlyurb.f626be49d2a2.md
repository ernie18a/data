Calculation: Bullish swing level = highest high over the lookback bars completed before the bullish EMA cross; store at the cross
Calculation: Bearish swing level = lowest low over the lookback bars completed before the bearish EMA cross; store at the cross
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA
Long: After a bullish cross, high > the high of the first subsequent candle whose range includes the fast EMA
Short: After a bearish cross, low < the low of the first subsequent candle whose range includes the fast EMA
Long: After the bullish touch-candle high is broken, close > the stored bullish swing level
Short: After the bearish touch-candle low is broken, close < the stored bearish swing level
