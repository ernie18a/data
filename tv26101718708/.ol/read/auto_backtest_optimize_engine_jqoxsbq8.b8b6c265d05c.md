Long: First bar in the backtest window OR a bar whose low pivot (or close pivot) is newly confirmed, while flat and with no long entry in the prior 10 × pivot lookback bars OR a positive external signal OR the example MA crossunder (two same-type moving averages of close, with the first using the shorter lookback) OR MACD line crosses above its signal line OR RSI crosses above its oversold threshold OR manual start time is reached and start price < close OR, in cyclical stop mode, the first eligible bar or the bar after a short position has ended
Short: Bar whose high pivot (or close pivot) is newly confirmed, while flat and with no short entry in the prior 10 × pivot lookback bars OR a negative external signal OR the example MA crossover (two same-type moving averages of close, with the first using the shorter lookback) OR MACD line crosses below its signal line OR RSI crosses below its overbought threshold OR manual start time is reached and start price > close OR, in cyclical stop mode, the bar after the first eligible bar or the bar after a long position has ended
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR (take profit enabled AND high >= take-profit price) OR (close-on-opposite enabled AND short-entry signal) OR (stop loss disabled AND long-entry signal)
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR (take profit enabled AND low <= take-profit price) OR (close-on-opposite enabled AND long-entry signal) OR (stop loss disabled AND short-entry signal)
