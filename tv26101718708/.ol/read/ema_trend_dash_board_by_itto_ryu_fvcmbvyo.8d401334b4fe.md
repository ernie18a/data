Calculation: EMA stack = five EMAs of close with strictly increasing lookback lengths: fast, second, trend, fourth, and long-term.
Calculation: Bullish stack count = number of adjacent EMA comparisons in the EMA stack where the shorter-lookback EMA is greater than the next EMA; bearish stack count uses less-than comparisons.
Calculation: Above-EMA count = number of the five EMAs below the current close; below-EMA count = 5 minus the above-EMA count.
Calculation: Trend EMA slope = percentage change in the trend EMA from its value at a lookback shorter than the long-term EMA lookback; slope is up or down only when its absolute value meets or exceeds the flat threshold.
Calculation: Fast-trend gap expansion = the current absolute percentage gap between fast and trend EMAs minus that gap at a lookback ago exceeds an expansion threshold.
Calculation: Bull score = 8 × above-EMA count + 10 × bullish stack count + 10 if trend EMA slope is up + 10 if bullish stack count is at least 3 and fast-trend gap is expanding.
Calculation: Bear score = 8 × below-EMA count + 10 × bearish stack count + 10 if trend EMA slope is down + 10 if bearish stack count is at least 3 and fast-trend gap is expanding.
Calculation: Bullish bias = bull score is greater than or equal to bear score; bearish bias = bull score is less than bear score. Bias score is the score for the current bias.
Calculation: Bull pullback touch = bullish stack count is at least 3 and the bar low is at or below the second EMA; bear pullback touch = bearish stack count is at least 3 and the bar high is at or above the second EMA.
Long: bull score >= 80 AND bullish stack count >= 3 OR bull score >= 60 AND above-EMA count >= 4 AND NOT (bull score >= 80 AND bullish stack count >= 3) OR bull score > bear score AND bull score >= 40 AND NOT (bull score >= 80 AND bullish stack count >= 3) AND NOT (bull score >= 60 AND above-EMA count >= 4) OR bullish stack count >= 3 AND ((close crosses above the fast EMA within the configured maximum bars after a bullish pullback touch) OR (bullish pullback touch AND close > fast EMA AND close >= open)) OR trend EMA crosses above long-term EMA.
Short: bear score >= 80 AND bearish stack count >= 3 OR bear score >= 60 AND below-EMA count >= 4 AND NOT (bear score >= 80 AND bearish stack count >= 3) OR bear score > bull score AND bear score >= 40 AND NOT (bear score >= 80 AND bearish stack count >= 3) AND NOT (bear score >= 60 AND below-EMA count >= 4) OR bearish stack count >= 3 AND ((close crosses below the fast EMA within the configured maximum bars after a bearish pullback touch) OR (bearish pullback touch AND close < fast EMA AND close <= open)) OR trend EMA crosses below long-term EMA.
Exit: long, the current bullish bias matches the last aligned stack direction AND neither bullish nor bearish stack count is at least 3 AND 40 <= bias score < 60
Exit: short, the current bearish bias matches the last aligned stack direction AND neither bullish nor bearish stack count is at least 3 AND 40 <= bias score < 60
