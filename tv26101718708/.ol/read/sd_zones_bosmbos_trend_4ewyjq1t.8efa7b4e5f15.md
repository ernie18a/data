Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low confirmed as a pivot using equal lookbacks on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot of each kind
Calculation: Demand zone = from the latest swing-low candle’s low to the higher of its open and close; Supply zone = from the latest swing-high candle’s high to the lower of its open and close
Long: Close > 200-period EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA (fast EMA lookback shorter than slow EMA)
Short: Close < 200-period EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA (fast EMA lookback shorter than slow EMA)
Long: Close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= that swing high
Short: Close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= that swing low
Long: High > latest confirmed swing high AND close <= that swing high
Short: Low < latest confirmed swing low AND close >= that swing low
Long: Close > latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing high AND previous close <= that swing high; swing pivots use completed higher-timeframe bars
Short: Close < latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing low AND previous close >= that swing low; swing pivots use completed higher-timeframe bars
Long: Bullish trend AND bullish break of structure AND a confirmed swing low exists
Short: Bearish trend AND bearish break of structure AND a confirmed swing high exists
Exit: Long, bullish trend AND close < active demand zone bottom
Exit: Short, bearish trend AND close > active supply zone top
