Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high during the configured opening-range window; opening-range low = lowest low during that window
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = (opening-range high + opening-range low) / 2
Calculation: Initial stop = configured stop level: opening-range midpoint, opposite opening-range boundary, or entry price offset by the configured fixed distance
Calculation: Target levels = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the initial entry-to-stop distance multiplied by each configured risk/reward multiple; when adaptive targets are enabled, multiply each risk/reward multiple by the configured-clamped ratio of the average of recent completed opening-range sizes to the current opening-range size
Long: opening range complete AND within configured trading session AND allowed trading day AND outside flat window AND configured direction allows long AND (close > opening-range high + entry buffer AND previous close <= opening-range high + entry buffer [breakout mode] OR close crosses above opening-range high + entry buffer AND, on a later bar before retest timeout, low touches the configured retest level (opening-range high or midpoint) AND close > opening-range high + entry buffer [retest mode])
Short: opening range complete AND within configured trading session AND allowed trading day AND outside flat window AND configured direction allows short AND (close < opening-range low - entry buffer AND previous close >= opening-range low - entry buffer [breakout mode] OR close crosses below opening-range low - entry buffer AND, on a later bar before retest timeout, high touches the configured retest level (opening-range low or midpoint) AND close < opening-range low - entry buffer [retest mode])
Exit: long, low <= current stop OR high >= an active target level OR configured session ends OR flat window starts
Exit: short, high >= current stop OR low <= an active target level OR configured session ends OR flat window starts
