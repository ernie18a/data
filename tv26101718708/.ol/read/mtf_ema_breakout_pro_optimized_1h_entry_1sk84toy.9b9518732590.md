Calculation: Strong candle = abs(close − open) > max(high − low, minimum price increment) × body-fraction parameter
Calculation: Bull trend = close > EMA on the chart timeframe and on each of two higher timeframes, using their last completed bars
Calculation: Bear trend = close < EMA on the chart timeframe and on each of two higher timeframes, using their last completed bars
Long: When flat, after a bar with bull trend, RSI > 50, a strong candle, close > EMA, and prior close ≤ prior EMA, enter when a later bar's high > the recorded setup bar's high; initial stop = setup bar's prior low
Short: When flat, after a bar with bear trend, RSI < 50, a strong candle, close < EMA, and prior close ≥ prior EMA, enter when a later bar's low < the recorded setup bar's low; initial stop = setup bar's prior high
Exit: long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ entry + initial risk × risk-reward parameter; move stop to entry once high ≥ entry + initial risk
Exit: short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ entry − initial risk × risk-reward parameter; move stop to entry once low ≤ entry − initial risk
