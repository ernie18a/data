Calculation: Williams %R = 100 × (close − highest high over the lookback) ÷ (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback)
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR Williams %R crosses below the oversold threshold
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold
Exit: long, Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold OR low ≤ entry price − stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + profit distance
Exit: short, Williams %R crosses above the overbought threshold OR high ≥ entry price + stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − profit distance
