Calculation: Hurst exponent = clamp(log(variance of one-bar close changes over a lookback ÷ variance of lagged close changes over the same lookback) ÷ (2 × log(lag)), 0, 1)
Calculation: Adaptive Kalman price = recursively smooth the volume-weighted moving average of close, using gain = base gain × (0.5 + Hurst exponent), clamped to [0.01, 0.99]
Calculation: Adaptive SuperTrend = form bands around the adaptive Kalman price at ATR × (base multiplier + scale × (1 − Hurst exponent)); carry the upper band forward as the maximum of its prior value and the current lower band when the prior trend is bullish, and otherwise use the current lower band; carry the lower band forward as the minimum of its prior value and the current upper band when the prior trend is bearish, and otherwise use the current upper band. Trend becomes bullish when the adaptive Kalman price exceeds the prior lower band, bearish when it falls below the prior upper band, and otherwise retains the prior trend. The SuperTrend line is the carried upper band in a bullish trend and the carried lower band in a bearish trend.
Calculation: AI score = for each bar, label its SuperTrend value 1 if the short-lookback WMA of close exceeds the longer-lookback WMA of the SuperTrend line, otherwise 0. Compare the current SuperTrend value with the current and most recent historical SuperTrend values, select the k closest from the n most recent values, and compute the inverse-distance-weighted average of their labels.
Calculation: Last signal direction = direction of the most recent AI signal, initially neutral; update it to long on a long signal and short on a short signal.
Long: AI score = 1 AND prior-bar AI score < 1 AND last signal direction ≠ long
Short: AI score = 0 AND prior-bar AI score > 0 AND last signal direction ≠ short
