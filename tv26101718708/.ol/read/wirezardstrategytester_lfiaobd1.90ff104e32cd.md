Calculation: RSI score = positive when RSI is above 50 or at/below its oversold threshold: use full, 70%, 80%, or 50% of its maximum positive weight when respectively oversold and rising, oversold and not rising, above 50 and rising, or above 50 and not rising; otherwise use the corresponding negative weights when RSI is below 50 or at/above its overbought threshold, with rising/falling and 70%/80%/50% rules mirrored. A bullish divergence (current low below the prior five-bar low and RSI above its prior five-bar low, with RSI below 50) scales a positive score by 1.2, capped at the positive maximum; bearish divergence mirrors this above 50 and is capped at the negative maximum. RSI uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: MACD score = positive maximum weight when the Elder EMA rises and MACD histogram rises, otherwise positive half weight when histogram is positive and Elder EMA rises, otherwise positive 30% weight when histogram is negative, rising, and above its value two bars ago; use the mirrored negative rules for falling Elder EMA/histogram, negative histogram with falling Elder EMA, or positive histogram that is falling and below its value two bars ago; otherwise zero. MACD uses the chart timeframe; its fast EMA lookback is shorter than its slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: VIDYA = recursively update from the selected price source using alpha = 2/(VIDYA lookback + 1) × absolute(CMO/100), where CMO is 100 × (sum of positive source changes − sum of negative source changes)/(sum of positive and negative source changes). VIDYA uses the chart timeframe.
Calculation: VIDYA score = positive maximum weight when VIDYA rises and close is above it, or 70% of that weight if its slope is not accelerating; positive 30% when VIDYA rises and close is below it. Mirror these rules negatively when VIDYA falls, using close below or above it respectively; otherwise zero.
Calculation: Volume score = zero when volume is below 10% of its 50-bar SMA. Otherwise, when volume is above its chart-timeframe volume SMA and OBV is above its chart-timeframe SMA and rising, score positive 100% of its maximum weight if close is in the upper half of the bar and volume exceeds 1.5 times the volume SMA, positive 70% if in the upper half without that spike, or positive 50% otherwise. Mirror for OBV below its SMA and falling, using close in the lower half; otherwise zero. OBV adds volume on an up-close bar, subtracts it on a down-close bar, and is unchanged on an equal close.
Calculation: Market-depth score = positive maximum weight for a bullish candle (close > open and body > half the bar range) when bar range is below the ATR multiple threshold, otherwise positive 60% of maximum; multiply by 1.2, capped at maximum, if its lower wick exceeds the bar-range fraction threshold. Mirror negatively for a bearish candle and upper wick. For other candles, assign positive 30% when only the lower wick exceeds the threshold, negative 30% when only the upper wick does, otherwise zero. ATR and all inputs use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Sector relative strength = EMA(3) of the sector ETF percentage change minus the benchmark ETF percentage change over the same lookback, using same-chart-timeframe bars. Sector score = positive maximum weight above +1%, negative maximum below −1%, otherwise relative strength × maximum weight / 2, capped at either maximum; zero if data is unavailable or the ticker is unmapped and neutral-unmapped is enabled.
Calculation: Normalized score = clamp(100 × (RSI score + MACD score + VIDYA score + Volume score + Market-depth score + Sector score) / total component weights, −100, +100). Component weights vary by chart timeframe category; use the same category-specific weights in numerator and denominator.
Long: normalized score ≥ buy threshold AND volume > volume SMA AND volume ≥ 10% of 50-bar SMA AND RSI score > 0 AND MACD score > 0 AND VIDYA score > 0 AND bar is closed.
Short: normalized score ≤ sell threshold AND volume > volume SMA AND volume ≥ 10% of 50-bar SMA AND RSI score < 0 AND MACD score < 0 AND VIDYA score < 0 AND bar is closed.
Exit: long, short signal OR normalized score < −20 on a closed bar.
Exit: short, long signal OR normalized score > 20 on a closed bar.
