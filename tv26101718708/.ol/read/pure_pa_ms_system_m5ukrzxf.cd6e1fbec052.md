Calculation: Bullish structure = current bar low > most recent confirmed pivot low; a pivot low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it. Evaluate on chart bars and on the last completed bars of each higher timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish structure = current bar high < most recent confirmed pivot high; a pivot high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars before and after it. Evaluate on chart bars and on the last completed bars of each higher timeframe.
Calculation: Aligned bullish structure = bullish structure on the chart timeframe and on both higher timeframes.
Calculation: Aligned bearish structure = bearish structure on the chart timeframe and on both higher timeframes.
Calculation: Consolidating = count of higher highs and lower lows across the configured recent chart bars is below a configured threshold.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = lower wick / (high - low) meets a configured minimum; bearish rejection = upper wick / (high - low) meets a configured minimum. Require high > low.
Calculation: Strong bullish candle = close > open and (close - open) / (high - low) meets a configured minimum; strong bearish candle = close < open and (open - close) / (high - low) meets a configured minimum. Require high > low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its configured moving average; require the average to be positive.
Calculation: Valid pullback = recent configured high minus recent configured low is positive, and the pullback from that high for a long or from that low for a short is no greater than a configured fraction of that range.
Long: Not in a trade AND NOT consolidating AND higher-timeframe bullish structure AND aligned bullish structure AND previous chart bar had a bullish rejection AND current chart bar is a strong bullish candle AND relative volume meets a configured minimum; OR in a long trade AND NOT consolidating AND valid pullback AND close > previous bar high.
Short: Not in a trade AND NOT consolidating AND higher-timeframe bearish structure AND aligned bearish structure AND previous chart bar had a bearish rejection AND current chart bar is a strong bearish candle AND relative volume meets a configured minimum; OR in a short trade AND NOT consolidating AND valid pullback AND close < previous bar low.
Exit: long, in a long trade AND NOT consolidating AND (no continuation has occurred and bars since entry meet a configured duration and (current high - previous close) is less than a configured fraction of ATR; OR continuation has occurred and bars since the last continuation meet a configured duration; OR a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low forms and close < that pivot low).
Exit: short, in a short trade AND NOT consolidating AND (no continuation has occurred and bars since entry meet a configured duration and (previous close - current low) is less than a configured fraction of ATR; OR continuation has occurred and bars since the last continuation meet a configured duration; OR a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high forms and close > that pivot high).
