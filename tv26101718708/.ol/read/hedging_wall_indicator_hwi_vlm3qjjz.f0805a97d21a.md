Calculation: Nearest wall above/below = the closest level on that side of the close among positive manual options levels, the point of control from a volume profile, qualifying pivot clusters, and qualifying high-volume nodes. Build the profile over a lookback of chart-timeframe bars, splitting each bar’s volume equally across the price rows its high-low range touches; a high-volume node is a local row-volume peak above a multiple of average row volume. A pivot cluster groups confirmed pivot highs and lows within an ATR-based tolerance and qualifies when it reaches a minimum touch count; its level is the average of its grouped pivots.
Long: Close > previous bar’s nearest wall above AND previous close <= that wall OR low <= previous bar’s nearest wall below AND close > that wall
Short: Close < previous bar’s nearest wall below AND previous close >= that wall OR high >= previous bar’s nearest wall above AND close < that wall
