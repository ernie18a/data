Calculation: VPOC, consensus area bounds, and boundary bounds = Build a rolling volume profile on log-price rows over chart-timeframe bars. For each bar, add its volume, multiplied by a recency weight that decreases with age, to every row between its log low and log high. The profile canvas spans the rolling highest high and lowest low, expanded to include their EMAs (EMA length is the rounded square root of the profile lookback). VPOC is the peak row’s price, interpolated with adjacent rows. The consensus area bounds are at the volume-weighted row-index mean ±1 standard deviation; boundary bounds are at the mean ±2 standard deviations. Smooth each extracted price level with an EMA.
Calculation: Consensus area midpoint = geometric mean of the consensus area bounds.
Calculation: Consensus anchor = the configured choice of VPOC, consensus area midpoint, or their geometric mean.
Calculation: VPOC position and midpoint position = for each anchor, (close − anchor) divided by the distance from that anchor to the upper boundary if close ≥ anchor, otherwise divided by the distance to the lower boundary; clamp each result to [−1.5, 1.5].
Calculation: Coherence = 2 × VPOC position × midpoint position ÷ (VPOC position² + midpoint position²), or 0 when the denominator is 0.
Calculation: Consensus score = clamp(abs((VPOC position + midpoint position) ÷ 2) × abs(coherence), 0, 1.5).
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND close crosses above the consensus anchor (current close > current anchor AND previous close ≤ previous anchor) AND abs(coherence) ≥ coherence floor AND consensus score ≥ signal threshold AND close > consensus area low.
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND close crosses below the consensus anchor (current close < current anchor AND previous close ≥ previous anchor) AND abs(coherence) ≥ coherence floor AND consensus score ≥ signal threshold AND close < consensus area high.
