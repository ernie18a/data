Calculation: Broad consensus direction = bullish when at least the required number of six votes are positive and close is above the weighted blend of a short-term consensus (Hull MA, faster Hull variant, and KAMA) and a long-term consensus (VWAP, ALMA, Heikin-Ashi Hull MA, and the average of long-term ALMA, VWAP, and KAMA); bearish when at least the required number are negative and close is below that blend; otherwise neutral. The six votes compare close with Hull MA, faster Hull variant, KAMA, VWAP, ALMA, and Heikin-Ashi Hull MA: each votes by whether close is above or below its average, with the average's direction used to resolve ties; the Heikin-Ashi vote is positive when Heikin-Ashi close is above Heikin-Ashi open and close is above its Hull MA, negative for both opposite comparisons, and otherwise uses the Hull MA vote. The fast averages use shorter lookbacks than the base averages; long-term averages use longer lookbacks than the base averages.
Calculation: Trend-switch direction = bullish when at least the required number of six votes are positive and close is above the adaptive trend-switch line; bearish when at least the required number are negative and close is below that line; otherwise neutral. The votes are: base Hull MA rising/falling, close above/below base KAMA, Heikin-Ashi close above/below Heikin-Ashi open, close above/below base VWAP, adaptive trend-switch line rising/falling, and short-term EMA above/below the adaptive trend-switch line. The adaptive trend-switch line is a recursive smoothing of a weighted average of base Hull MA, shorter-lookback Hull variant, base KAMA, Heikin-Ashi Hull MA, and base VWAP; its smoothing responds to efficiency, price pressure, and EMA crossings, with prior trend direction retained by trend memory.
Calculation: Aligned trend direction = bullish when broad consensus and trend-switch directions are both bullish; bearish when both are bearish; otherwise neutral.
