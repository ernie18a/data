Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Long: On a requested timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using its last completed bar, or lower than the chart timeframe, using its current bar, fast EMA crosses above slow EMA OR fast EMA > slow EMA
Short: On a requested timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using its last completed bar, or lower than the chart timeframe, using its current bar, fast EMA crosses below slow EMA OR fast EMA <= slow EMA OR VIX close > VIX EMA
