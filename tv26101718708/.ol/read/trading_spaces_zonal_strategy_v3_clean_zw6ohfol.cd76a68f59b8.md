Calculation: Bullish zone = retained, noninvalidated zone whose price range intersects the current bar; created by an up bar on a higher timeframe that closes above the preceding lookback highs and has body size or full range above its ATR-scaled threshold. Its bounds come from the nearest preceding bearish base candle or configured bearish base cluster, using body or wick bounds. Invalidate when the configured close or low falls below the zone bottom. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bearish zone = retained, noninvalidated zone whose price range intersects the current bar; created by a down bar on a higher timeframe that closes below the preceding lookback lows and has body size or full range above its ATR-scaled threshold. Its bounds come from the nearest preceding bullish base candle or configured bullish base cluster, using body or wick bounds. Invalidate when the configured close or high rises above the zone top. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the lowest low of preceding bars AND close > open.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = high above the highest high of preceding bars AND close < open.
Long: A bullish zone intersects the current bar AND the most recent bullish sweep within the confirmation lookback occurred on an earlier bar AND close > previous high AND close > open
Short: A bearish zone intersects the current bar AND the most recent bearish sweep within the confirmation lookback occurred on an earlier bar AND close < previous low AND close < open
