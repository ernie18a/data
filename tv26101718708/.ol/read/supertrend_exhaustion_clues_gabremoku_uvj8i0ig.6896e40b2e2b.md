Calculation: Directional clue score = count of four true comparisons: directional DI is greater than opposite DI; close is above (bullish) or below (bearish) VWAP; close is above (bullish) or below (bearish) the prior close; normalized distance from SuperTrend is greater than on the prior bar. Normalized distance = clamp((clamp(abs(close − SuperTrend) / ATR, 0, 1.5) / 1.5) raised to a configurable power, 0, 1).
Calculation: Directional quality score = count of four true conditions: ADX/DI strength (when enabled, ADX >= threshold, directional DI > opposite DI, and, when required, ADX > prior ADX); VWAP acceptance (when enabled, close is on the directional side of both VWAP and SuperTrend); distance expansion (when enabled, normalized distance > prior normalized distance); and compression release in the trend direction.
Calculation: Compression = SuperTrend upper-minus-lower band width normalized to its rolling minimum and maximum over a lookback (0 if equal); squeeze when normalized width <= a configurable threshold; release when the current bar is not a squeeze and the prior bar was. Recent compression includes a squeeze or release within a configurable bar window.
Long: signals allowed on the chart timeframe AND (bullish exhaustion setup on the current bar AND not on the prior bar AND no active long sequence OR active long sequence AND bullish exhaustion setup); bullish exhaustion setup = directional clue score >= configurable threshold AND [counter-trend enabled AND SuperTrend trend is down AND active state is Exhausted OR continuation enabled AND SuperTrend trend is up AND active state is Exhausted]. Active state is Exhausted when the trend is up, bullish quality score <= configurable exhaustion limit, and neither a qualifying compressed break nor a raw trend flip applies.
Short: signals allowed on the chart timeframe AND (bearish exhaustion setup on the current bar AND not on the prior bar AND no active short sequence OR active short sequence AND bearish exhaustion setup); bearish exhaustion setup = directional clue score >= configurable threshold AND [counter-trend enabled AND SuperTrend trend is up AND active state is Exhausted OR continuation enabled AND SuperTrend trend is down AND active state is Exhausted]. Active state is Exhausted when the trend is down, bearish quality score <= configurable exhaustion limit, and neither a qualifying compressed break nor a raw trend flip applies.
