Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest pivot high/low using the same left/right lookback, computed on a configured timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe and taken from its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = a three-bar gap where the current low is above the high two bars earlier / the current high is below the low two bars earlier, with the middle bar bullish / bearish; apply the configured size filter.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the nearest bearish / bullish candle in the configured lookback before a bullish / bearish structure break; if none exists and fallback is enabled, use the bar with the lowest low / highest high in that lookback. In legacy mode, use the bearish / bullish candle from five bars earlier when the current bar gaps above its high / below its low.
Calculation: Active OTE zone = the 0.62–0.79 retracement of the latest TF1 swing pair, anchored from the latest high for an uptrend or latest low for a downtrend; TF1 is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe and uses its last completed bar. The trend direction is the direction of the latest TF1 structure break.
Long: A confirmed swing high is broken upward on TF1 or TF2, or a newly confirmed swing high is already below the close (retro break), using close or high as the configured break reference; OR a bullish fair value gap forms; OR a bullish order block is created; OR price sweeps below the latest confirmed liquidity pivot low by at least the configured ATR penetration and closes back above it when required; OR the bar range intersects the active bullish OTE zone.
Short: A confirmed swing low is broken downward on TF1 or TF2, or a newly confirmed swing low is already above the close (retro break), using close or low as the configured break reference; OR a bearish fair value gap forms; OR a bearish order block is created; OR price sweeps above the latest confirmed liquidity pivot high by at least the configured ATR penetration and closes back below it when required; OR the bar range intersects the active bearish OTE zone.
