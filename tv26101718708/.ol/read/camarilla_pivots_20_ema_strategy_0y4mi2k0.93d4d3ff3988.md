Calculation: Camarilla H4 = previous completed higher-timeframe bar close + 1.1 × (previous completed higher-timeframe bar high − low) / 2; H3 = close + 1.1 × (high − low) / 4; L3 = close − 1.1 × (high − low) / 4; L4 = close − 1.1 × (high − low) / 2
Long: close > H4 AND close > EMA AND previous chart-timeframe bar close ≤ H4 AND flat position
Short: close < L4 AND close < EMA AND previous chart-timeframe bar close ≥ L4 AND flat position
Exit: long, low ≤ H3 OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − H3) × risk/reward multiple
Exit: short, high ≥ L3 OR low ≤ entry price − (L3 − entry price) × risk/reward multiple
