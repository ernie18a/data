Long: On the first chart-timeframe bar of a Monday, Thursday, or Friday, the previous completed higher-timeframe daily bar is an inside day (its high is below the high and its low is above the low of the daily bar before it), the daily bar before the inside day closed above its open, and the current higher-timeframe daily bar’s open is below both the inside day’s high and the higher of the two daily highs before the inside day; then enter long at that daily open if it is at or above the highest high of the previous three completed daily bars, otherwise enter long when the chart-timeframe high reaches that level during the day.
Calculation: Stop price = entry price minus the configured fixed point distance or configured percentage of entry price.
Exit: Long, chart-timeframe low <= stop price OR, on a higher-timeframe daily bar strictly after the entry day, daily open > entry price.
