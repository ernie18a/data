Calculation: Trend score = weighted combination of (a) the count of adjacent short-to-long EMAs in bullish order minus the count in bearish order, (b) higher-high/higher-low versus lower-high/lower-low comparisons between the latest two confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows, and (c) DMI direction signed by ADX strength.
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted combination of smoothed RSI centered on 50 and MACD histogram normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Volatility score = candle direction when Bollinger-band width is expanding and ATR is within a healthy range and candle body exceeds half its range, plus close-to-close price slope over a short lookback normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe verdict = bullish when the shorter EMA is above the longer EMA and RSI is above 50; bearish when the shorter EMA is below the longer EMA and RSI is below 50; otherwise neutral. Use two higher timeframes and their last completed bars.
Calculation: Adaptive axis weights = each enabled axis's reliability, updated by EWMA after a fixed outcome horizon according to whether its directional prediction achieved a required ATR move, mapped to a weight range and adjusted by the current regime. Trend, momentum, and volatility regimes use different axis weight multipliers; quiet regime reduces all weights.
Calculation: Final score = weighted mean of the three axis scores, scaled to the score range, plus a bonus for each higher-timeframe verdict matching its sign and a penalty for each non-neutral verdict opposing it, clamped to the score range.
Calculation: Axis consensus = ignore enabled axis scores within the neutral band; the greater count of positive or negative leaning axes determines the agreed direction, requiring at least one leaning axis. The range between the highest and lowest leaning scores must not exceed twice the cross-axis tolerance.
Calculation: Active threshold = selected from four evenly spaced candidates in the preset threshold range; select the candidate with the highest learned reward value, with epsilon exploration. Update each candidate's reward by EWMA after its signal reaches the outcome horizon, rewarding it when the signal direction achieves the required ATR move.
Long: axis consensus is valid and positive AND trend score leans positive AND final score is at least the active threshold AND the previous signal state is not long
Short: axis consensus is valid and negative AND trend score leans negative AND final score is at most the negative active threshold AND the previous signal state is not short
Calculation: Signal state resets to neutral when neither strong entry condition holds and the absolute final score is below the active threshold minus a hysteresis buffer; otherwise retain the most recent strong signal direction.
Exit: long, close is at or below entry price minus an ATR-based stop distance OR close is at or above entry price plus an ATR-based profit distance
Exit: short, close is at or above entry price plus an ATR-based stop distance OR close is at or below entry price minus an ATR-based profit distance
