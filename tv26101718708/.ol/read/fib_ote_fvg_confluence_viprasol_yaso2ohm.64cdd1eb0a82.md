Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; optionally require the middle bar's close > high two bars earlier
Calculation: Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; optionally require the middle bar's close < low two bars earlier
Calculation: OTE band = Fibonacci retracement band of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's range; for bullish bias, top = high − range × start and bottom = high − range × end; for bearish bias, bottom = low + range × start and top = low + range × end
Calculation: Equilibrium = midpoint of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high and low
Calculation: Confluent FVG = gap overlaps the OTE band, or under the favored-half rule its midpoint is below equilibrium for a bullish gap or above equilibrium for a bearish gap
Long: low > high two bars earlier
Short: high < low two bars earlier
