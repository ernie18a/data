Calculation: demand setup = a bearish origin candle followed by a configured multi-bar sequence of bullish or doji candles; the final close is above the origin open, and no earlier close in the sequence is above the origin open
Calculation: supply setup = a bullish origin candle followed by a configured multi-bar sequence of bearish or doji candles; the final close is below the origin open, and no earlier close in the sequence is below the origin open
Calculation: bullish gap score = (current low − low two chart bars earlier’s corresponding high) / the standard deviation of bullish raw gaps over a longer chart-timeframe lookback
Calculation: bearish gap score = (low two chart bars earlier − current high) / the standard deviation of bearish raw gaps over a longer chart-timeframe lookback
Long: demand setup is confirmed at chart-bar close OR demand setup is confirmed using completed higher-timeframe bars while the chart timeframe is lower OR current low > high two chart bars earlier AND the middle chart bar’s high > high two chart bars earlier AND bullish gap score > minimum gap threshold
Short: supply setup is confirmed at chart-bar close OR supply setup is confirmed using completed higher-timeframe bars while the chart timeframe is lower OR current high < low two chart bars earlier AND the middle chart bar’s low < low two chart bars earlier AND bearish gap score > minimum gap threshold
