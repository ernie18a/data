Calculation: Anchored range = on a higher timeframe, confirm swing highs/lows using configurable left/right bars; update the high to a confirmed pivot high above the current low and the low to a confirmed pivot low below the current high. Use lookahead-off values (last completed higher-timeframe bar historically; developing higher-timeframe bar in realtime).
Calculation: Equilibrium band = midpoint of the anchored range ± a configurable fraction of its size; discount is at or below its lower edge, premium at or above its upper edge, and the interval between is neutral.
Calculation: Position feature = close’s location from range low to range high, clamped to [0,1], then z-score standardized over a configurable window.
Calculation: Dwell feature = fraction of the configurable recent chart bars whose closes are within range size / configurable band count of the current close; z-score standardize over the same window as the position feature.
Calculation: Momentum feature = close’s rate of change over a configurable chart-bar lookback; z-score standardize over the same window as the other features.
Calculation: Bull probability = sigmoid of a learned linear combination of the three current standardized features and an unregularized bias. After sufficient history, update weights once per chart bar using the previous bar’s features and whether its forward return over a configurable horizon was positive; apply configurable L2 weight decay to feature weights. Bear probability = 1 − bull probability.
Calculation: A long ML zone exists when any of the configurable evenly spaced price levels from range low through the discount edge has bull probability at or above the entry threshold, holding current dwell and momentum features fixed and recalculating position at each level. A short ML zone exists when any evenly spaced level from the premium edge through range high has bear probability at or above the entry threshold, using the same fixed features and recalculation.
Long: warmed up AND close is in discount AND close is not below range low AND bull probability ≥ entry threshold AND this condition was false on the previous chart bar; also mark a long ML zone when a long ML zone exists.
Short: warmed up AND close is in premium AND close is not above range high AND bear probability ≥ entry threshold AND this condition was false on the previous chart bar; also mark a short ML zone when a short ML zone exists.
