Calculation: Anchored linear regression channel = Fit a linear regression to closes from the most recently confirmed chart-timeframe pivot (using highs for pivot highs and lows for pivot lows) through the current bar; channel boundaries are the fitted value plus or minus a standard-deviation multiple of the regression residuals, with residual standard deviation measured since the anchor.
Calculation: ANOVA Z-score = For the preceding chart-timeframe bars, calculate an ANOVA F-statistic from close, low, and high: use their lookback means and variances, between-group mean square = the sum of each group’s lookback length times its squared difference from the mean of the three group means, divided by 2; within-group mean square = the sum of each group’s variance times (lookback length − 1), divided by (3 × lookback length − 6). Standardize this F-statistic by subtracting its lookback SMA and dividing by its lookback standard deviation.
Calculation: Trend correlation = Correlation between chart-timeframe close and bar index over a lookback.
Long: Close crosses above the upper anchored linear regression channel AND absolute ANOVA Z-score is at least its threshold AND RSI is greater than 50 AND trend correlation is positive AND volume is greater than its chart-timeframe SMA AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar OR (close crosses above or below either anchored channel boundary AND RSI is greater than 50 AND trend correlation is positive AND volume is greater than its chart-timeframe SMA AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Short: Close crosses below the lower anchored linear regression channel AND absolute ANOVA Z-score is at least its threshold AND RSI is at most 50 AND trend correlation is at most zero AND volume is greater than its chart-timeframe SMA AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar OR (close crosses above or below either anchored channel boundary AND RSI is at most 50 AND trend correlation is at most zero AND volume is greater than its chart-timeframe SMA AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
