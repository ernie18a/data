Calculation: Confirmed swing low = the latest pivot low confirmed after the configured number of right-side bars; confirmed swing high = the latest pivot high confirmed after the configured number of right-side bars
Calculation: Buy pressure = (close - low) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low; sell pressure = (high - close) / (high - low), or 0 when high = low
Calculation: Long stop = initially the entry signal bar's low minus one minimum tick; when close reaches average entry price + initial risk × the configured R threshold, raise it to at least average entry price and, if ATR trailing is enabled, close minus ATR × the configured multiplier; when the configured capitulation mode tightens the stop, raise it to at least close minus ATR
Calculation: Short stop = initially the entry signal bar's high plus one minimum tick; when close reaches average entry price - initial risk × the configured R threshold, lower it to at most average entry price and, if ATR trailing is enabled, close plus ATR × the configured multiplier; when the configured capitulation mode tightens the stop, lower it to at most close plus ATR
Calculation: Long capitulation = volume > configured multiple × moving average volume AND upper wick >= configured multiple × candle body; Short capitulation = volume > configured multiple × moving average volume AND lower wick >= configured multiple × candle body
Long: position is flat AND confirmed swing low exists AND low < confirmed swing low AND close > confirmed swing low AND close > open AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured entry session) AND volume > configured multiple × moving average volume AND buy pressure >= configured threshold
Short: position is flat AND confirmed swing high exists AND high > confirmed swing high AND close < confirmed swing high AND close < open AND (session filter is disabled OR datetime is within the configured entry session) AND volume > configured multiple × moving average volume AND sell pressure >= configured threshold
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR (configured capitulation mode is direct close AND long capitulation AND (close - average entry price) / initial risk >= configured minimum profit in R) OR (time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= configured limit AND close < average entry price)
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR (configured capitulation mode is direct close AND short capitulation AND (average entry price - close) / initial risk >= configured minimum profit in R) OR (time stop is enabled AND bars since entry >= configured limit AND close > average entry price)
