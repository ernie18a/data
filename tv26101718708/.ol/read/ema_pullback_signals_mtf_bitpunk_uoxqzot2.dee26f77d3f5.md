Calculation: Short-term EMA = exponential moving average of higher-timeframe closes; long-term EMA = exponential moving average of higher-timeframe closes with a longer lookback than the short-term EMA
Calculation: Fast zone = short-term EMA ± zone width × higher-timeframe ATR
Calculation: Trend zone = long-term EMA ± zone width × higher-timeframe ATR
Calculation: Departure efficiency = absolute change in close over the short-term EMA lookback ÷ sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback
Long: After higher-timeframe direction is valid (last completed higher-timeframe close > long-term EMA and long-term EMA is rising over its slope lookback), departure efficiency meets its minimum and a chart bar's low is above both zones; then, within the confirmation window measured in higher-timeframe intervals from first touch, a chart bar overlaps either zone and closes above the active zone's upper edge, above its open, and above the prior close; and either its body is at least half its range and it closes in the outer quarter at the high, or the MACD histogram is rising and Heikin-Ashi direction is up; and its close is no farther than the maximum extension above the active edge measured in completed higher-timeframe ATR. A Trend-zone overlap selects the Trend zone, including as an upgrade from a Fast-zone touch. After a signal or extension skip, require a fresh zone contact (the preceding chart bar did not overlap that zone), a pullback from the highest confirmed close since consumption of at least the rearm distance measured in the frozen higher-timeframe ATR, and qualifying departure efficiency before another attempt.
Short: After higher-timeframe direction is valid (last completed higher-timeframe close < long-term EMA and long-term EMA is falling over its slope lookback), departure efficiency meets its minimum and a chart bar's high is below both zones; then, within the confirmation window measured in higher-timeframe intervals from first touch, a chart bar overlaps either zone and closes below the active zone's lower edge, below its open, and below the prior close; and either its body is at least half its range and it closes in the outer quarter at the low, or the MACD histogram is falling and Heikin-Ashi direction is down; and its close is no farther than the maximum extension below the active edge measured in completed higher-timeframe ATR. A Trend-zone overlap selects the Trend zone, including as an upgrade from a Fast-zone touch. After a signal or extension skip, require a fresh zone contact (the preceding chart bar did not overlap that zone), a pullback from the lowest confirmed close since consumption of at least the rearm distance measured in the frozen higher-timeframe ATR, and qualifying departure efficiency before another attempt.
