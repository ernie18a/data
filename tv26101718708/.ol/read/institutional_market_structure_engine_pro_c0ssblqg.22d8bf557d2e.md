Calculation: Macro swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low with a longer lookback than the micro swing; a pivot is usable after its right-side lookback bars complete.
Calculation: Micro swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low with a shorter lookback than the macro swing; a pivot is usable after its right-side lookback bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish FVG setup = current low > high two bars ago AND the previous bar closes above its open with body > half its range AND previous-bar range > current ATR AND current low - high two bars ago > ATR × minimum FVG size AND current volume > 20-bar volume SMA AND the nearest bearish candle among bars 1–4 ago has low below the preceding bar's low.
Calculation: Bearish FVG setup = current high < low two bars ago AND the previous bar closes below its open with body > half its range AND previous-bar range > current ATR AND low two bars ago - current high > ATR × minimum FVG size AND current volume > 20-bar volume SMA AND the nearest bullish candle among bars 1–4 ago has high above the preceding bar's high.
Long: current high > latest unbroken macro swing high OR current high > latest unbroken micro swing high OR current low < second-latest unbroken macro swing low AND current close > that swing low OR Bullish FVG setup = true OR a bearish FVG's close is above its upper boundary.
Short: current low < latest unbroken macro swing low OR current low < latest unbroken micro swing low OR current high > second-latest unbroken macro swing high AND current close < that swing high OR Bearish FVG setup = true OR a bullish FVG's close is below its lower boundary.
