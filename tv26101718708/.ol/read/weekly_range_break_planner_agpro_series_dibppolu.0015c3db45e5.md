Calculation: Previous weekly high/low = high/low of the last completed weekly bar, a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe; relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Valid range = previous weekly high − previous weekly low is positive and at least the minimum range multiple of ATR, with ATR positive.
Calculation: Break context = first close beyond the valid prior-week boundary by at least the break-buffer multiple of ATR; retain the direction, boundary, prior-week range, and chart bar index until resolved or the next week.
Calculation: Retest shelf = broken boundary ± the shelf-depth multiple of ATR. Shelf touched when the bar's high/low intersects the shelf; bullish retest held when touched and close ≥ boundary; bearish retest held when touched and close ≤ boundary.
Calculation: Failed break = bullish close < boundary − invalidation-buffer multiple of ATR; bearish close > boundary + that buffer.
Calculation: Break score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of: prior-week range fit (range / 4 ATR, capped at 1, ×16), close distance (absolute close-to-boundary distance / 1.4 ATR, capped at 1, ×24), retest points (22 if held, else 12 if shelf touched, else 7), time quality (16 × [1 − bars since break / break window], bounded 0–16), volume participation (relative volume / 1.6, capped at 1, ×12; or 9 if disabled), and target room (room to first target / 2 ATR, capped at 1, ×14). First target is the broken boundary plus/minus the configured fraction of the prior-week range. Required score is the minimum ready score adjusted down in Fast mode, unchanged in Balanced mode, and up in Strict mode, bounded 1–100.
Long: valid range AND no active break AND close > previous weekly high + break-buffer multiple of ATR OR bullish break context unresolved AND break score ≥ required score AND (bullish retest held OR close distance from boundary ≥ 0.45 ATR) AND NOT failed break.
Short: valid range AND no active break AND close < previous weekly low − break-buffer multiple of ATR OR bearish break context unresolved AND break score ≥ required score AND (bearish retest held OR close distance from boundary ≥ 0.45 ATR) AND NOT failed break.
Exit: Long, bullish break context AND close < broken weekly high − invalidation-buffer multiple of ATR.
Exit: Short, bearish break context AND close > broken weekly low + invalidation-buffer multiple of ATR.
