Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low among bars whose opening times fall within the configured opening interval of the configured session; levels become defined after that interval.
Calculation: Bias score = 100 × clamp(weighted sum of (a) normalized fast, medium, and slow EMA slopes (each EMA minus its prior-bar value, divided by close), (b) trend-stack score (+1 when fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA, −1 when fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA, otherwise 0), and (c) normalized RSI deviation from 50, to [−1, 1]).
Calculation: Tension score = 100 × clamp(weighted sum of compression, coupling, persistence, expansion impulse, and volume score to [0, 1]). Compression is 1 minus a weighted sum of the rolling percent ranks of Bollinger Band width and ATR divided by close. Coupling is the mean absolute rolling correlation of 1-bar close return with RSI change, 3-bar close return with (fast EMA − slow EMA) / close, and 1-bar close return with ATR / close. Persistence is the fraction of the persistence lookback with the same nonzero bias state as the current bar, where bias state is +1 above a configurable positive threshold, −1 below its negative, and 0 otherwise. Expansion impulse is the unsigned normalization of the positive part of the ATR rate of change. Volume score is the unsigned normalization of the positive part of volume's rolling z-score when the volume filter is enabled, otherwise zero. Normalization clamps signed values to [−1, 1] and unsigned values to [0, 1].
Long: Current bar is in session and after the opening interval, close > opening range high (or high > opening range high when close confirmation is disabled), tension score ≥ its threshold, bias score ≥ its positive threshold, fast EMA > medium EMA, and the preceding bar did not meet these same long conditions.
Short: Current bar is in session and after the opening interval, close < opening range low (or low < opening range low when close confirmation is disabled), tension score ≥ its threshold, bias score ≤ its negative threshold, fast EMA < medium EMA, and the preceding bar did not meet these same short conditions.
Exit: Long, a long-entry condition is met and (bias score < 0 OR close < fast EMA).
Exit: Short, a short-entry condition is met and (bias score > 0 OR close > fast EMA).
