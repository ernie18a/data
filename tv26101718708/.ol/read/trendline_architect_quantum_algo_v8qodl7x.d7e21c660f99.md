Calculation: Support trendline = line joining consecutive confirmed swing-low pivots; retain only if its anchor span is allowed and no intervening close falls below it beyond the validity tolerance; reject near-duplicates and enforce the configured active-line cap. Start with two touches; confirm after the configured number of touches, each counted when the low enters the ATR-based tolerance band and the close is above the line. Expire after the configured age.
Calculation: Resistance trendline = line joining consecutive confirmed swing-high pivots; retain only if its anchor span is allowed and no intervening close rises above it beyond the validity tolerance; reject near-duplicates and enforce the configured active-line cap. Start with two touches; confirm after the configured number of touches, each counted when the high enters the ATR-based tolerance band and the close is below the line. Expire after the configured age.
Calculation: Breakout grade = grade from the sum of a base point and one point for each condition met: volume z-score exceeds its cutoff, close-to-line penetration in ATR units exceeds its cutoff, and candle body divided by candle range exceeds its cutoff; map the score to grades C, B, or A.
Long: close > resistance trendline + ATR-based breakout buffer AND (confirmed-line-only filter is disabled OR resistance trendline is confirmed) AND breakout grade >= configured minimum grade OR resistance trendline is broken and within its configured retest window AND low <= resistance trendline + ATR-based touch tolerance AND close > resistance trendline.
