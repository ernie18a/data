Calculation: Trend strength = 2 × absolute value of (Hurst exponent − 0.5)
Calculation: Volatility expansion = 20-bar ATR is greater than its 50-bar simple moving average multiplied by a threshold that increases with cascade depth
Calculation: Vortex spiral = close + sin(bar index × rotation speed ÷ period) × ATR(period) × volume factor; volume factor is 1 when volume amplification is disabled, otherwise 1 + 0.5 × (volume ÷ 50-bar volume SMA − 1). The signal uses the two shortest spirals, with the second period longer than the first.
Long: More than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior long signal AND ((Hurst exponent > strong-trend threshold AND volatility expansion) OR (Hurst exponent < mean-reversion threshold AND RSI < oversold threshold) OR (trend strength > moderate-strength threshold AND both shortest vortex spirals > close AND RSI < upper RSI threshold))
Short: More than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the prior short signal AND ((Hurst exponent > trend threshold AND close > 50-bar SMA AND RSI > overbought threshold AND both shortest vortex spirals < close) OR (Hurst exponent < mean-reversion threshold AND close < 50-bar SMA AND RSI < oversold threshold AND both shortest vortex spirals > close) OR (volatility expansion AND trend strength > strong-strength threshold AND absolute value of (close − 50-bar SMA) ÷ 50-bar SMA > distance threshold))
