Calculation: normalized pressure = 100 × EMA(estimated buy volume − estimated sell volume) / SMA(volume), where estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), estimated sell volume = volume − estimated buy volume, and the close position defaults to 0.5 when high = low
Calculation: close EMA = EMA(close)
Long: (zero-cross-only mode and normalized pressure crosses above 0 OR reclaim mode and normalized pressure > 0 and close crosses above close EMA) AND (strong-pressure-only mode is off OR |normalized pressure| ≥ threshold) AND (session filter is off OR datetime is within the configured session) AND position is not long AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown AND bar is complete; OR normalized pressure crosses above 0; OR normalized pressure crosses up through the strong-pressure threshold; OR close crosses above close EMA; OR normalized pressure becomes positive
Short: (zero-cross-only mode and normalized pressure crosses below 0 OR reclaim mode and normalized pressure < 0 and close crosses below close EMA) AND (strong-pressure-only mode is off OR |normalized pressure| ≥ threshold) AND (session filter is off OR datetime is within the configured session) AND position is not short AND bars since last signal ≥ cooldown AND bar is complete; OR normalized pressure crosses below 0; OR normalized pressure crosses down through the negative strong-pressure threshold; OR close crosses below close EMA; OR normalized pressure becomes negative
