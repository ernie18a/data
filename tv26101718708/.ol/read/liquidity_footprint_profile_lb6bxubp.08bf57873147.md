Calculation: Active liquidity levels = highs and lows of completed Asia and London sessions on the chart timeframe, plus the previous day’s high and low from the higher daily timeframe’s last completed bar; a high remains active until price trades above it, and a low until price trades below it.
Calculation: Buy-imbalance row = buy volume at a footprint price row is at least the configured imbalance multiple of sell volume at the adjacent lower-price row.
Calculation: Sell-imbalance row = sell volume at a footprint price row is at least the configured imbalance multiple of buy volume at the adjacent higher-price row.
Calculation: Delta percentage = 100 × (buy volume − sell volume) / (buy volume + sell volume).
Long: Footprint data is available AND bar close is confirmed AND at least the configured minimum number of consecutive buy-imbalance rows occur in the bar OR footprint data is available AND bar close is confirmed AND the bar low is below an active liquidity low AND close is above the highest swept liquidity low AND delta percentage is at or below the negative configured absorption threshold.
Short: Footprint data is available AND bar close is confirmed AND at least the configured minimum number of consecutive sell-imbalance rows occur in the bar OR footprint data is available AND bar close is confirmed AND the bar high is above an active liquidity high AND close is below the lowest swept liquidity high AND delta percentage is at or above the configured absorption threshold.
