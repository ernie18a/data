Calculation: Significant counter-direction candle = close < open for a downswing candidate, or close > open for an upswing candidate, and absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × its minimum-candle-size multiplier; ATR is calculated on the bars being evaluated.
Calculation: Confirmed bearish swing = while swing direction is up, at least two significant bearish candles have occurred since the most recent new high, and the high-to-low range accumulated during candidate tracking is ≥ ATR × the range multiplier. A new high resets the candle count. On confirmation, create a resistance at the highest close recorded during the up swing and change direction to down.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish swing = while swing direction is down, at least two significant bullish candles have occurred since the most recent new low, and the high-to-low range accumulated during candidate tracking is ≥ ATR × the range multiplier. A new low resets the candle count. On confirmation, create a support at the lowest close recorded during the down swing and change direction to up.
Calculation: Active support = a support created by a confirmed bullish swing that has not subsequently been broken by a close below it. Active resistance = a resistance created by a confirmed bearish swing that has not subsequently been broken by a close above it.
Calculation: Timeframe structure score = initialize to a configurable score of −2, 0, or +2. When one or more supports break, set the pending score to −2 if no supports remain or at least two supports broke; otherwise set it to 0 if the current score is +2, and −2 otherwise. When one or more resistances break, set the pending score to +2 if no resistances remain or at least two resistances broke; otherwise set it to 0 if the current score is −2, and +2 otherwise. Apply the pending score on the next evaluation bar before calculating a new pending score. Calculate independently on chart-timeframe bars and on higher-timeframe bars; the higher-timeframe calculation uses the currently available bar, which may still be forming. Each calculation has its own ATR length, minimum-candle-size multiplier, and range multiplier.
Long: Confirmed bullish swing on chart-timeframe bars OR chart-timeframe structure score = +2 OR higher-timeframe structure score = +2.
Short: Confirmed bearish swing on chart-timeframe bars OR chart-timeframe structure score = −2 OR higher-timeframe structure score = −2.
