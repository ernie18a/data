Long: Fast HMA crosses above slow HMA AND current-timeframe ATR > its minimum threshold AND ATR from the higher-timeframe bar still forming > its minimum threshold AND flat
Short: Fast HMA crosses below slow HMA AND current-timeframe ATR > its minimum threshold AND ATR from the higher-timeframe bar still forming > its minimum threshold AND flat
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar hl2 − current-timeframe ATR × stop-risk multiplier
Calculation: Long target = signal-bar hl2 + current-timeframe ATR × take-profit multiplier
Calculation: Short stop = signal-bar hl2 + current-timeframe ATR × stop-risk multiplier
Calculation: Short target = signal-bar hl2 − current-timeframe ATR × take-profit multiplier
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target
