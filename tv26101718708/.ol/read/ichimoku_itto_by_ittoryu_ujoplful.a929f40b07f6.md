Calculation: Ichimoku score = sum of five checks: +1 if close is above the current cloud, -1 if below, otherwise 0; +1 if Tenkan is above Kijun, -1 if below, otherwise 0; +1 if close is above the high of the bar at the Ichimoku displacement offset, -1 if below that bar’s low, otherwise 0; +1 if current Senkou A is above Senkou B, -1 if below, otherwise 0; +1 if Kijun is above its value at the slope lookback, -1 if below, otherwise 0. Tenkan, Kijun, and Senkou B are the midpoints of the highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks; Senkou A is the midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun. The current cloud uses Senkou A and B shifted back by the displacement offset.
Long: (Ichimoku score >= strong threshold AND previous Ichimoku score < strong threshold AND close is outside the current cloud AND absolute Ichimoku score > chop score band) OR (close is above the current cloud AND Tenkan > Kijun AND close > the high of the bar at the Ichimoku displacement offset AND at least one of those three conditions was false on the previous bar)
Short: (Ichimoku score <= -strong threshold AND previous Ichimoku score > -strong threshold AND close is outside the current cloud AND absolute Ichimoku score > chop score band) OR (close is below the current cloud AND Tenkan < Kijun AND close < the low of the bar at the Ichimoku displacement offset AND at least one of those three conditions was false on the previous bar)
