Calculation: Heikin Ashi OHLC = close (O+H+L+C)/4; open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close)/2; high = max(H, open, close); low = min(L, open, close). If double smoothing is selected, apply the same formulas to the first-pass Heikin Ashi OHLC.
Calculation: Strong bullish Heikin Ashi candle = Heikin Ashi close ≥ open and lower wick < half the minimum price tick; strong bearish Heikin Ashi candle = Heikin Ashi close < open and upper wick < half the minimum price tick.
Calculation: Baseline = first chart bar's (high+low)/2 or the configured manual level.
Calculation: Trend EMA = exponential moving average of chart close, or of the displayed overlay close when overlay source is selected; its lookback is configurable. If the overlay uses a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar or last completed bar according to the confirmation setting.
Calculation: Pattern OHLC = configured standard or displayed candle OHLC. If using a higher timeframe, use its still-forming bar or last completed bar according to the confirmation setting. Body = |close−open|; upper wick = high−max(open,close); lower wick = min(open,close)−low; range = high−low. Pattern context EMA uses the pattern close and a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bullish candlestick pattern = any of: hammer (lower wick ≥ 2×body, upper wick ≤ body, close below context EMA); inverted hammer (upper wick ≥ 2×body, lower wick ≤ body, close below context EMA); dragonfly doji (body ≤ range×doji threshold, lower wick ≥ 0.6×range, upper wick ≤ 0.1×range); tweezer bottom (previous candle bearish, current bullish, lows differ by no more than max(minimum tick, current range×0.05)); bullish marubozu (bullish candle, body ≥ 0.95×range); bullish engulfing (current bullish, previous bearish, current close ≥ previous open, current open ≤ previous close); bullish harami (previous bearish, current bullish, current open ≥ previous close, current close ≤ previous open, current body < previous body); morning star (two-back candle bearish, previous body < half the two-back body, current bullish and close > midpoint of the two-back body); three white soldiers (three bullish candles with successively higher closes, and each later open lies inside the preceding body); piercing line (previous bearish, current bullish, current open < previous close, current close > previous body midpoint and < previous open).
Calculation: Bearish candlestick pattern = any of: hanging man (lower wick ≥ 2×body, upper wick ≤ body, close above context EMA); shooting star (upper wick ≥ 2×body, lower wick ≤ body, close above context EMA); gravestone doji (body ≤ range×doji threshold, upper wick ≥ 0.6×range, lower wick ≤ 0.1×range); tweezer top (previous candle bullish, current bearish, highs differ by no more than max(minimum tick, current range×0.05)); bearish marubozu (bearish candle, body ≥ 0.95×range); bearish engulfing (current bearish, previous bullish, current open ≥ previous close, current close ≤ previous open); bearish harami (previous bullish, current bearish, current open ≤ previous close, current close ≥ previous open, current body < previous body); evening star (two-back candle bullish, previous body < half the two-back body, current bearish and close < midpoint of the two-back body); three black crows (three bearish candles with successively lower closes, and each later open lies inside the preceding body); dark cloud cover (previous bullish, current bearish, current open > previous close, current close < previous body midpoint and > previous open).
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, the latest pivot low in price is lower than the preceding pivot low while the oscillator pivot low is higher (regular), or price is higher while the oscillator is lower (hidden). Bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, the latest pivot high in price is higher than the preceding pivot high while the oscillator pivot high is lower (regular), or price is lower while the oscillator is higher (hidden). Oscillator is selected from RSI, MACD line, Stochastic, CCI, or MFI; its lookback is configurable. Pivots require configurable left and right bars. When using a higher timeframe, calculations use its still-forming bars or last completed bars according to the confirmation setting.
Long: Heikin Ashi close ≥ open AND previous Heikin Ashi close < previous Heikin Ashi open OR strong bullish Heikin Ashi candle OR selected coloring mode is Direction AND close ≥ open OR selected coloring mode is Momentum AND close ≥ previous close OR selected coloring mode is Trend AND selected price source > Trend EMA OR baseline source crosses above baseline OR source is above baseline OR Bullish candlestick pattern OR Bullish divergence.
Short: Heikin Ashi close < open AND previous Heikin Ashi close ≥ previous Heikin Ashi open OR strong bearish Heikin Ashi candle OR selected coloring mode is Direction AND close < open OR selected coloring mode is Momentum AND close < previous close OR selected coloring mode is Trend AND selected price source < Trend EMA OR baseline source crosses below baseline OR source is below baseline OR Bearish candlestick pattern OR Bearish divergence.
