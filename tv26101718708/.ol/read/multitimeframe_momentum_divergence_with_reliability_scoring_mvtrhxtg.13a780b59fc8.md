Calculation: Momentum oscillator = z-score of RSI(price source) minus 50, optionally smoothed; use the selected normalization and smoothing settings
Calculation: Higher-timeframe momentum direction = sign of RSI(close, momentum lookback) minus its value two bars earlier, calculated on each of three higher timeframes using the still-forming bar
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot = a local high or low confirmed after the selected number of chart-timeframe bars to its right
Calculation: Internal trend efficiency = absolute price change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar price changes over that lookback
Long: A confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than the preceding pivot low AND price at the newer pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND the pivot gap is within the maximum AND the oscillator-pivot difference meets the minimum volatility-scaled gap; OR a confirmed oscillator pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND price at the newer pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND the pivot gap is within the maximum AND the oscillator-pivot difference meets the minimum volatility-scaled gap; when triple-pivot mode is selected, the same divergence relationship also holds with the pivot before the preceding one; AND the required number of the three higher-timeframe momentum directions are positive; AND any enabled regime and time-slot significance filters pass
Short: A confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than the preceding pivot high AND price at the newer pivot is higher than at the preceding pivot AND the pivot gap is within the maximum AND the oscillator-pivot difference meets the minimum volatility-scaled gap; OR a confirmed oscillator pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND price at the newer pivot is lower than at the preceding pivot AND the pivot gap is within the maximum AND the oscillator-pivot difference meets the minimum volatility-scaled gap; when triple-pivot mode is selected, the same divergence relationship also holds with the pivot before the preceding one; AND the required number of the three higher-timeframe momentum directions are negative; AND any enabled regime and time-slot significance filters pass
