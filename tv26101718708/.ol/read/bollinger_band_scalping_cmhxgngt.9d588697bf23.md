Calculation: Candle wick-to-body ratio = (upper wick + lower wick) / absolute(open − close), where upper wick = high − max(open, close) and lower wick = min(open, close) − low; undefined when open = close
Long: open < lower Bollinger Band AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average × volume multiplier) AND candle body is nonzero AND candle wick-to-body ratio ≤ maximum wick-to-body ratio + 1 AND (trend filter disabled OR close > long-term EMA)
Short: open > upper Bollinger Band AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > its moving average × volume multiplier) AND candle body is nonzero AND candle wick-to-body ratio ≤ maximum wick-to-body ratio + 1 AND (trend filter disabled OR close < long-term EMA)
