Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the first configured number of bars of each entry session; freeze these levels after the range completes for that session.
Calculation: Candle close location = (close − low) / (high − low); use 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Long stop candidate = stored breakout level − current ATR × configured stop buffer; short stop candidate = stored breakout level + current ATR × configured stop buffer.
Calculation: Long target distance = min(current risk distance × configured target R multiple, configured dollar profit cap / (quantity × point value)); short target distance is the same. Target price is entry-bar close plus the long distance or minus the short distance. Require target distance ≥ current risk distance × configured minimum R multiple.
Calculation: Long live stop = the highest of the original stop and each reached profit-lock level: entry price plus the configured breakeven offset after a 1R favorable excursion, entry plus 1R after 2R, entry plus 2R after 3R, and entry plus each configured dollar lock amount divided by point value × live quantity after its corresponding dollar profit trigger. Short live stop = the lowest of the original stop and the mirrored levels below entry.
Long: Entry-session bar AND opening range complete AND long breakout armed AND close > opening range high AND close > open AND close location ≥ configured breakout strength AND (high − low > 0 OR close location fallback = 0.5) AND abs(close − open) ≥ ATR × configured minimum body threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP).
Short: Entry-session bar AND opening range complete AND short breakout armed AND close < opening range low AND close < open AND close location ≤ 1 − configured breakout strength AND (high − low > 0 OR close location fallback = 0.5) AND abs(close − open) ≥ ATR × configured minimum body threshold AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP).
Long: Flat AND trading not locked AND remaining drawdown > configured hard maximum risk + configured drawdown buffer AND a long breakout signal was recorded while in an entry session AND exactly the configured delay number of bars have elapsed AND current close > stored breakout level AND current close > long stop candidate AND configured minimum stop distance ≤ close − long stop candidate ≤ configured maximum stop distance AND quantity = floor(min(configured risk budget, configured hard maximum risk) / ((close − long stop candidate) × point value)) ≥ 1 AND quantity × (close − long stop candidate) × point value ≤ configured hard maximum risk AND quantity × (close − long stop candidate) × point value < remaining drawdown − configured drawdown buffer AND close ≤ recorded signal close + (recorded signal close − long stop candidate) × configured maximum chase R AND target distance ≥ current risk distance × configured minimum R multiple AND entry-session bar.
Short: Flat AND trading not locked AND remaining drawdown > configured hard maximum risk + configured drawdown buffer AND a short breakout signal was recorded while in an entry session AND exactly the configured delay number of bars have elapsed AND current close < stored breakout level AND current close < short stop candidate AND configured minimum stop distance ≤ short stop candidate − close ≤ configured maximum stop distance AND quantity = floor(min(configured risk budget, configured hard maximum risk) / ((short stop candidate − close) × point value)) ≥ 1 AND quantity × (short stop candidate − close) × point value ≤ configured hard maximum risk AND quantity × (short stop candidate − close) × point value < remaining drawdown − configured drawdown buffer AND close ≥ recorded signal close − (short stop candidate − recorded signal close) × configured maximum chase R AND target distance ≥ current risk distance × configured minimum R multiple AND entry-session bar.
Exit: Long, low ≤ long live stop OR high ≥ long target OR current evaluation PnL ≥ configured profit target OR drawdown from peak ≥ configured trailing draw limit − configured safety buffer OR outside configured manage sessions.
Exit: Short, high ≥ short live stop OR low ≤ short target OR current evaluation PnL ≥ configured profit target OR drawdown from peak ≥ configured trailing draw limit − configured safety buffer OR outside configured manage sessions.
