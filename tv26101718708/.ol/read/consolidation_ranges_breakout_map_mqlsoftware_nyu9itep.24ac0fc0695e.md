Calculation: Range Compression Index = weighted composite of movement inefficiency (1 − min(net close displacement over the evaluation window ÷ sum of absolute close-to-close changes, 1)), volatility compression (short-term ATR relative to longer-term ATR, rescaled to 0–1), and containment (share of closes inside the central portion of the candidate high-low range, scaled to the containment threshold and capped at 1)
Calculation: Live consolidation range = candidate whose Range Compression Index meets its threshold and whose high-low width is within the ATR-based width limit for the required consecutive confirmed bars; freeze its boundaries when confirmed, widen a boundary only for a wick within the ATR-based edge tolerance, and invalidate the range if its width limit or edge-update budget is exceeded
Long: confirmed bar AND live consolidation range AND close > upper boundary + ATR-based breakout buffer AND (volume gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR volume >= volume-expansion multiple × median volume)
Short: confirmed bar AND live consolidation range AND close < lower boundary − ATR-based breakout buffer AND (volume gate disabled OR volume unavailable OR volume >= volume-expansion multiple × median volume)
