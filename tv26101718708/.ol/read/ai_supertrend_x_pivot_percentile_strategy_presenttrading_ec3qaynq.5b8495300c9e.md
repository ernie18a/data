Calculation: First and second SuperTrends = on their respective higher timeframes, calculate a moving average of close × volume divided by the moving average of volume, then add/subtract the ATR multiplied by a factor; carry the bands forward unless the current band rises above the prior lower band, falls below the prior upper band, or the prior chart close crosses the prior band. Direction is bullish when price crosses above the upper band or remains above the lower band; bearish when price crosses below the lower band or remains below the upper band. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Each AI trend label = for each historical bar, label it bullish if the shorter-lookback WMA of close exceeds the longer-lookback WMA of that SuperTrend, otherwise bearish. For the current SuperTrend value, find the k historical SuperTrend values with the smallest absolute distance; weight their labels by the inverse of distance plus a small constant. The label is bullish only if the weighted average equals 1, bearish only if it equals 0. Apply separately to each SuperTrend.
Calculation: Pivot trend score = for each of the seven configured lookbacks (multiples of one base lookback), calculate the nearest-rank 75th percentile of highs and 25th percentile of lows. Compare these with the 144-bar 75th percentile of highs and 25th percentile of lows. Add one bullish count for each percentile above the high threshold and one bearish count for each below the low threshold; add one weak-bull count for each low percentile between thresholds and one weak-bear count for each high percentile between thresholds. Score = (bullish count + 0.5 × weak-bull count − 0.5 × weak-bear count − bearish count) − (bearish count + 0.5 × weak-bear count − 0.5 × weak-bull count − bullish count).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe ADX and DI = calculate directional movement from changes in higher-timeframe highs and lows; smooth directional movement and true range with weighted moving averages, calculate +DI and −DI, then smooth their normalized absolute difference into ADX. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: AI bullish label changes from non-bullish to bullish on either SuperTrend, OR its direction changes from bearish to bullish while its AI label is bullish, OR pivot trend score > 0, OR both SuperTrend directions are bullish and both AI labels are bullish and ADX > threshold and +DI > −DI and pivot trend score > 0.
Short: AI bearish label changes from non-bearish to bearish on either SuperTrend, OR its direction changes from bullish to bearish while its AI label is bearish, OR pivot trend score < 0, OR both SuperTrend directions are bearish and both AI labels are bearish and ADX > threshold and −DI > +DI and pivot trend score < 0.
Exit: long, low <= first SuperTrend − first ATR × first factor AND (first or second SuperTrend direction changes from bearish to bullish with its AI label bullish, OR pivot trend score < 0)
Exit: short, high >= first SuperTrend + first ATR × first factor AND (first or second SuperTrend direction changes from bullish to bearish with its AI label bearish, OR pivot trend score > 0)
