Calculation: Daily bias = bullish when the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows on higher-timeframe daily bars (using the last completed daily bar) are both rising; bearish when both are falling; otherwise neutral. A pivot is confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Qualifying raid = during the New York execution session, price first breaches an enabled, unconsumed pool against the bias by at least the configured tick penetration: for bullish bias, a low pool (latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low, fresh Asian low, fresh premarket low, or prior-day low) has current low at least the penetration below it and prior-bar low above that threshold; for bearish bias, apply the symmetric test to a high pool. If close-reclaim mode is selected, the raid bar must also close back across the pool. Asian and premarket pools are fresh for 24 hours after their session completes. The optional weekly-open filter uses the open of the higher-timeframe weekly bar still forming (fixed at its start): bullish raids require low below it; bearish raids require high above it.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low above high two chart-timeframe bars earlier, with gap size at least the configured fraction of ATR(14), and, when enabled, the middle bar closes above its open with body/range at least the configured minimum. Bearish fair value gap is the inverse: current high below low two bars earlier, with the same size test, and optionally a bearish middle bar meeting the body/range test.
Long: bullish daily bias AND New York session AND optional weekly-open filter passes AND daily entry count below limit AND a qualifying bullish raid occurs AND a bullish fair value gap forms within the configured bars after the raid while bias remains bullish and the session remains open AND the selected entry level (gap upper edge or midpoint) has positive risk to the stop (sweep low minus configured buffer), positive reward to the prior-day high, and reward/risk at least the configured minimum AND on a later bar than the gap, low <= entry level, with bias unchanged, session not ended, and prior-day high not already reached.
Short: bearish daily bias AND New York session AND optional weekly-open filter passes AND daily entry count below limit AND a qualifying bearish raid occurs AND a bearish fair value gap forms within the configured bars after the raid while bias remains bearish and the session remains open AND the selected entry level (gap lower edge or midpoint) has positive risk to the stop (sweep high plus configured buffer), positive reward from the prior-day low, and reward/risk at least the configured minimum AND on a later bar than the gap, high >= entry level, with bias unchanged, session not ended, and prior-day low not already reached.
Exit: long, low <= sweep-low stop OR high >= prior-day high target OR session ends while session-end flattening is enabled (exit at the prior in-session close); if stop and target are both touched on one bar, stop takes precedence.
Exit: short, high >= sweep-high stop OR low <= prior-day low target OR session ends while session-end flattening is enabled (exit at the prior in-session close); if stop and target are both touched on one bar, stop takes precedence.
