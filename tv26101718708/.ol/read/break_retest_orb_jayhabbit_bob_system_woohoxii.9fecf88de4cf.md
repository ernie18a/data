Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low from the regular-session open through the configured opening-range period.
Calculation: Long retest low = lowest low from the first close above the opening-range high through the confirming close back above it; short retest high = highest high from the first close below the opening-range low through the confirming close back below it.
Calculation: Long 1R target = entry price + (entry price − long retest low); short 1R target = entry price − (short retest high − entry price).
Long: After the opening range is set, close > opening range high starts a long setup; while in that setup, close < opening range high starts the retest, unless close < opening range low resets the setup; after the retest starts, close > opening range high confirms the long entry, unless close < opening range low resets the setup. Mark the retest when the close first falls below the opening-range high during the setup.
Short: After the opening range is set, close < opening range low starts a short setup; while in that setup, close > opening range low starts the retest, unless close > opening range high resets the setup; after the retest starts, close < opening range low confirms the short entry, unless close > opening range high resets the setup. Mark the retest when the close first rises above the opening-range low during the setup.
Exit: Long, after close >= long 1R target, raise the stop to the moving average of close when it is above the current stop; exit when close < current stop. Before that target is reached, the current stop is the long retest low.
Exit: Short, after close <= short 1R target, lower the stop to the moving average of close when it is below the current stop; exit when close > current stop. Before that target is reached, the current stop is the short retest high.
