Calculation: Active moving average = selected moving average of close over its lookback
Calculation: ATR = average true range over its lookback
Calculation: Retest band = ATR × selected band width, centered on the active moving average
Calculation: Moving-average slope = active moving average − its value at the slope lookback
Calculation: Reaction score = clamp(0.22 × slope score + 0.24 × depth score + 0.18 × wick score + 0.20 × close score + 0.16 × volume score, 0, 100); slope score = clamp(abs(moving-average slope / ATR) × 85, 0, 100); volume score = clamp((current volume / its simple moving average over the volume lookback − 0.65) / 1.05 × 100, 0, 100)
Calculation: Bullish depth score = clamp(100 − distance from the low to the moving average / retest band × 42 − max(moving average − low, 0) / retest band × 18, 0, 100); bearish depth score = clamp(100 − distance from the high to the moving average / retest band × 42 − max(high − moving average, 0) / retest band × 18, 0, 100)
Calculation: Close location = clamp((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment), 0, 1)
Calculation: Bullish wick score = clamp(lower wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × 160, 0, 100); bearish wick score = clamp(upper wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) × 160, 0, 100)
Calculation: Bullish close score = clamp(close location × 100 + (close above moving average ? 10 : −25), 0, 100); bearish close score = clamp((1 − close location) × 100 + (close below moving average ? 10 : −25), 0, 100)
