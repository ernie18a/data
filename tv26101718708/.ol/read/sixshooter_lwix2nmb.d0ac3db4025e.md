Calculation: dPOC = current-day VWAP of (high + low + close) / 3; pdPOC = prior completed day’s final VWAP. Daily periods are higher than the chart timeframe; current-day VWAP uses the developing day, prior-day VWAP uses the completed day.
Calculation: wPOC and mPOC = price-bin centers with the greatest volume in the current week or month’s profile; distribute each chart bar’s volume evenly across bins touched by its low–high range, optionally decay older bin volume. pwPOC and pmPOC = the prior completed week’s and month’s final POCs. Weekly and monthly periods are higher than the chart timeframe; current profiles use the developing period, prior profiles use completed periods.
Calculation: POC direction score = sum across the six POCs of +1 if a POC is above its value at the shorter lookback, −1 if below, and 0 if equal or unavailable.
Calculation: POC compression percentile = 100 minus the rolling percentile rank of (highest POC − lowest POC) / ATR; higher means tighter. Use a longer ranking window than the price tightness range lookback.
Calculation: Price tightness percentile = 100 minus the rolling percentile rank of (highest high − lowest low over the shorter price range lookback) / ATR; higher means tighter.
Calculation: Compression rate = prior POC width / ATR minus current POC width / ATR, comparing values at the POC direction lookback.
Calculation: Chop = absolute close change over the chop lookback / ATR; chop is present when below its minimum. Range breakout = high above the prior chop-lookback highest high, or low below the prior chop-lookback lowest low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe PSAR regime = compare higher-timeframe close with higher-timeframe PSAR; use the timeframe stepped above the chart timeframe. The higher-timeframe bar is developing in realtime and last completed historically. Chart PSAR proximity = absolute distance from chart-timeframe close to chart-timeframe PSAR, divided by ATR.
Calculation: Strong bull lockout = close above the long-term SMA, close within the configured fraction of the long-term highest high, and POC direction score strongly positive. Strong bear lockout = close below the long-term SMA, close within the configured fraction of the long-term lowest low, and POC direction score strongly negative. The high/low and SMA use the same long lookback.
Calculation: ATR expansion = short-term ATR at least the configured multiple of longer-term ATR. Thrust momentum = close minus close at the shorter thrust lookback.
Calculation: Rollover down = armed by a new rolling swing high, then at least the minimum age since the rolling high, drawdown from that high by at least the configured ATR multiple, close below its shorter slope-lookback close, and current high and low each below the prior bar’s. Rearm on a new rolling swing high; disarm only when a short signal fires from rollover.
Calculation: Canonical stacked support = all six POCs at or below close; mPOC < wPOC, wPOC ≤ dPOC, pmPOC < mPOC; close is within the configured ATR distance above dPOC and at least the configured ATR distance above pmPOC.
Long: ((early compression AND POC direction score ≥ positive minimum AND price tightness ≥ minimum AND higher-timeframe PSAR is not down AND (not chop OR range breakout up)) OR (POC compression ≥ READY threshold AND POC direction score ≥ positive minimum AND price tightness ≥ minimum AND higher-timeframe PSAR is not down AND (not chop OR range breakout up)) OR (short-term ATR expansion AND close is in the upper configured fraction of bar range AND thrust momentum ≥ configured ATR multiple AND POC direction score ≥ 1) OR (canonical stacked support AND not canonical stacked support on prior bar AND close > prior bar high)) AND rising edge of the applicable early-compression, READY, or thrust condition AND same-side cooldown elapsed AND opposite-side anti-flip interval elapsed AND chart PSAR distance / ATR ≥ minimum (or proximity gate disabled) AND not strong bear lockout.
Short: ((early compression AND POC direction score ≤ negative minimum AND price tightness ≥ minimum AND higher-timeframe PSAR is not up AND (not chop OR range breakout down)) OR (POC compression ≥ READY threshold AND POC direction score ≤ negative minimum AND price tightness ≥ minimum AND higher-timeframe PSAR is not up AND (not chop OR range breakout down)) OR rollover down OR (short-term ATR expansion AND close is in the lower configured fraction of bar range AND thrust momentum ≤ negative configured ATR multiple AND POC direction score ≤ −1)) AND rising edge of the applicable early-compression, READY, or thrust condition AND same-side cooldown elapsed AND opposite-side anti-flip interval elapsed AND chart PSAR distance / ATR ≥ minimum (or proximity gate disabled) AND not strong bull lockout. Rollover bypasses the higher-timeframe PSAR regime filter; coil conditions do not. Rollover remains armed until a short fires from rollover.
