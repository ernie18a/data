Calculation: Upper and lower volatility envelope = EMA-centered edges at equal distances, where the distance is the smaller of an ATR-based width and a percentage-of-price cap; the ATR-based width expands immediately with ATR and contracts gradually when ATR falls.
Long: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses above the upper volatility envelope
Short: On a confirmed chart-bar close, close crosses below the lower volatility envelope
