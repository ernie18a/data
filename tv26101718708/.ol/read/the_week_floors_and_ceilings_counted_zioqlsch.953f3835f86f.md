Calculation: Previous week's high = high of the last completed bar on the higher weekly timeframe
Calculation: Previous week's low = low of the last completed bar on the higher weekly timeframe
Long: chart bar closes above previous week's high AND the prior chart bar closed at or below it
Long: first confirmed chart bar of the week with high above previous week's high
Long: completed weekly close above the previous week's low
Short: chart bar closes below previous week's low AND the prior chart bar closed at or above it
Short: first confirmed chart bar of the week with low below previous week's low
Short: completed weekly close below the previous week's high
