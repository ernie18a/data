Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a long lookback
Calculation: Average candle range = simple moving average of high − low over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Midrange = midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low over the long lookback
Calculation: Recent Spring or Secondary Test low = low on the most recent bar where a Spring or Secondary Test condition was true
Calculation: Secondary Test = low is within 0.5% above or below the low on the most recent Selling Climax bar, volume is below average volume, and close > open
Long: volume > average volume × (volume-spike multiplier + 0.5) AND close < open AND low ≤ lowest low over 30 bars AND close < prior close OR close > open AND volume > average volume AND low on the prior bar = lowest low over the 30 bars ending on that prior bar AND close > prior close × 1.01 OR low < lowest low over the prior 20 bars AND close > open AND volume > average volume AND close > low × 1.01 OR close > prior close AND volume > average volume × 1.3 AND close − open > average candle range AND close > midrange OR low > recent Spring or Secondary Test low AND close > open AND volume < average volume AND close > midrange
Short: volume > average volume × (volume-spike multiplier + 0.5) AND close > open AND high ≥ highest high over 30 bars OR close > open AND volume > average volume AND high on the prior bar = highest high over the 30 bars ending on that prior bar AND close < prior close × 0.99 OR high > highest high over the prior 20 bars AND close < open AND volume > average volume AND close < high × 0.99 OR high < high on the most recent UTAD or Distribution Secondary Test bar AND close < open AND volume < average volume AND close < midrange
