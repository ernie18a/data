Calculation: MACD histogram acceleration = current MACD histogram is greater than each of its two immediately preceding values for bullish acceleration; less than each for bearish acceleration
Calculation: Candle close location = close is in the upper portion of the bar’s low-to-high range for bullish confirmation, or the lower portion for bearish confirmation
Calculation: Overextension = percentage distance between close and the long-term EMA, when close is above it for bullish overextension or below it for bearish overextension
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = close is above its value at an earlier lookback while RSI is below its value then and above a configured threshold
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = close is below its value at an earlier lookback while RSI is above its value then and below a configured threshold
Calculation: Bull bonus score = 2 points if fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA; +1 if close > long-term EMA; +1 if RSI is above its midpoint and rising versus an earlier lookback; +1 if stochastic %K > %D and %K at an earlier lookback is below a configured bound; +1 if +DI > -DI
Calculation: Bear bonus score = 2 points if fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA; +1 if close < long-term EMA; +1 if RSI is below its midpoint and falling versus an earlier lookback; +1 if stochastic %K < %D and %K at an earlier lookback is above a configured bound; +1 if -DI > +DI
Long: ADX > configured minimum AND fast and mid EMAs are each above their values at an earlier lookback AND RSI is within its configured buy range AND MACD histogram acceleration is bullish AND candle close location is bullish AND NOT (close > long-term EMA AND overextension exceeds its configured maximum) AND NOT bearish RSI divergence AND bull bonus score >= configured minimum AND (MACD line crosses above signal line while prior MACD histogram < 0 OR stochastic %K crosses above %D while prior %K is below a configured oversold bound OR RSI crosses above its midpoint after being below a configured bound at an earlier lookback OR fast EMA crosses above mid EMA while mid EMA is rising and ADX > a configured threshold) AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior long signal
Short: ADX > configured minimum AND fast and mid EMAs are each below their values at an earlier lookback AND RSI is within its configured sell range AND MACD histogram acceleration is bearish AND candle close location is bearish AND NOT (close < long-term EMA AND overextension exceeds its configured maximum) AND NOT bullish RSI divergence AND bear bonus score >= configured minimum AND (MACD line crosses below signal line while prior MACD histogram > 0 OR stochastic %K crosses below %D while prior %K is above a configured overbought bound OR RSI crosses below its midpoint after being above a configured bound at an earlier lookback OR fast EMA crosses below mid EMA while mid EMA is falling and ADX > a configured threshold) AND more than the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since the prior short signal
