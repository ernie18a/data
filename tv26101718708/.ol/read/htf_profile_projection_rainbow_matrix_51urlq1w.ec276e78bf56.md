Calculation: HTF price-bucket delta = Divide the still-forming higher-timeframe candle’s range into buckets, with bucket count equal to the higher-timeframe range divided by chart-timeframe ATR, rounded and bounded to 4–50. For each bucket, sum signed volume from lower-timeframe chart bars since the HTF candle opened, including the currently forming chart bar; allocate each bar’s signed volume equally across every bucket its low–high range touches. Signed volume is +volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, and 0 when close = open. Imbalance threshold = mean absolute bucket delta × the configured imbalance fraction.
Long: Still-forming higher-timeframe close >= its open OR, in Delta/Bull-Bear profile mode, a bucket’s delta > 0 and (imbalance display is off OR the bucket’s absolute delta > the imbalance threshold)
Short: Still-forming higher-timeframe close < its open OR, in Delta/Bull-Bear profile mode, a bucket’s delta < 0 and (imbalance display is off OR the bucket’s absolute delta > the imbalance threshold)
