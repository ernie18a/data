Calculation: Bar delta = 0 when high = low; otherwise volume × (close − open) / (high − low)
Calculation: Liquidity zones = create a buy-side zone at each confirmed pivot high, spanning from the pivot high to the higher of the pivot bar’s open and close; create a sell-side zone at each confirmed pivot low, spanning from the pivot low to the lower of the pivot bar’s open and close. If a zone is thinner than 0.1 × ATR(14) on the pivot bar, extend its inner boundary to that minimum thickness. A buy-side zone is swept when high > its top; a sell-side zone is swept when low < its bottom.
Calculation: Zone quadrant deltas = for each active zone, divide its height into four equal slices; accumulate bar delta in each slice in proportion to the overlap of the candle’s high-low range with that slice. The outer slice is the highest slice for a buy-side zone and the lowest for a sell-side zone.
Calculation: Outer-delta ratio = absolute outer-slice delta / (sum of the absolute deltas of all four slices + 0.0001)
Calculation: Bar-delta ratio = absolute bar delta / (volume + 0.0001)
Long: For an un-signaled sell-side zone touched by the candle or swept: (low < zone bottom AND outer-slice delta > 0 AND outer-delta ratio > 0.2) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer-delta ratio < 0.1) OR (close is within the zone AND outer-slice delta < 0 AND outer-delta ratio > 0.6) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND bar-delta ratio > 0.2)
Short: For an un-signaled buy-side zone touched by the candle or swept: (high > zone top AND outer-slice delta < 0 AND outer-delta ratio > 0.2) OR (high > zone top AND outer-delta ratio < 0.1) OR (close is within the zone AND outer-slice delta > 0 AND outer-delta ratio > 0.6) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND bar-delta ratio > 0.2)
