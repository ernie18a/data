Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low L bars ago that is respectively higher/lower than the highs/lows of the preceding L bars, confirmed when the leg changes; swing pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots.
Calculation: FVG bar change = the configured FVG timeframe advances; if it differs from the chart timeframe it may be higher or lower, and the current bar is still forming. FVG close/open use the last completed bar; FVG high/low use the current forming bar and the bar two bars earlier.
Calculation: FVG threshold = 0 when auto threshold is disabled; otherwise, twice the cumulative mean of absolute bar percentage changes, counted only at FVG timeframe changes.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed swing pivot high OR close crosses above the latest confirmed internal pivot high that differs from the swing pivot high OR (current FVG-timeframe low > FVG-timeframe high two bars earlier AND last completed FVG-timeframe close > FVG-timeframe high two bars earlier AND last completed FVG-timeframe percentage change > FVG threshold AND FVG timeframe changes)
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed swing pivot low OR close crosses below the latest confirmed internal pivot low that differs from the swing pivot low OR (current FVG-timeframe high < FVG-timeframe low two bars earlier AND last completed FVG-timeframe close < FVG-timeframe low two bars earlier AND negative last completed FVG-timeframe percentage change > FVG threshold AND FVG timeframe changes)
