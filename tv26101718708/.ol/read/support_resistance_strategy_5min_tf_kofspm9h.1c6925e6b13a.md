Calculation: Resistance = most recent confirmed pivot high, using a longer lookback before the pivot and a shorter lookahead after it; use the value from the prior chart bar and carry it forward until a new value is available
Calculation: Support = most recent confirmed pivot low, using a longer lookback before the pivot and a shorter lookahead after it; use the value from the prior chart bar and carry it forward until a new value is available
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (short-lookback EMA of volume − long-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ long-lookback EMA of volume
Long: close crosses above Resistance AND Volume oscillator > threshold AND open − low ≤ close − open AND close ≥ EMA(close) AND no open position AND bar is confirmed AND chart-bar time is within the entry session
Short: close crosses below Support AND Volume oscillator > threshold AND open − close ≥ high − open AND close ≤ EMA(close) AND no open position AND bar is confirmed AND chart-bar time is within the entry session
Exit: long, low ≤ close − ATR × multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ close + ATR × multiplier
Exit: long or short, chart-bar time is 14:55
