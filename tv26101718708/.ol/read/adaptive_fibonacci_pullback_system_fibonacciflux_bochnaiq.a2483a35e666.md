Calculation: Average Supertrend = arithmetic mean of three Supertrend lines using the same ATR lookback and different volatility factors; smoothed Supertrend = EMA of that average.
Calculation: AMA midline = mean of two adaptive bands. For lookback N, set h = max(sign(change(highest high over N bars)), 0), l = max(sign(-change(lowest low over N bars)), 0), and c = SMA(N, 1 if h > 0 or l > 0 else 0)^2. Each bar, upper band = prior upper band + c × (high − prior upper band), seeded with high; lower band = prior lower band + c × (low − prior lower band), seeded with low.
Long: low < average Supertrend AND close > smoothed Supertrend AND previous close > AMA midline AND close > AMA midline AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe RSI > its threshold AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe MACD line > its signal line AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe volume > its moving average × multiplier
Short: high > average Supertrend AND close < smoothed Supertrend AND previous close < AMA midline AND close < AMA midline AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe RSI < its threshold AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe MACD line < its signal line AND (if enabled) lower-timeframe volume > its moving average × multiplier
Exit: Long, previous bar's close crosses below the smoothed Supertrend OR low reaches the long stop price OR high reaches the long take-profit price OR price retraces from the take-profit level by the configured trailing offset
Exit: Short, previous bar's close crosses above the smoothed Supertrend OR high reaches the short stop price OR low reaches the short take-profit price OR price retraces from the take-profit level by the configured trailing offset
