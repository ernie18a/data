Calculation: Delta proxy = volume × (close − open) / (high − low)
Long: volume > its longer-term SMA × volume multiplier AND close > open AND Delta proxy > its shorter-term SMA AND prior bar Delta proxy < its prior shorter-term SMA
Short: volume > its longer-term SMA × volume multiplier AND close < open AND Delta proxy < its shorter-term SMA AND prior bar Delta proxy > its prior shorter-term SMA
Exit: long, low ≤ prior bar low × (1 − risk percentage) OR high ≥ prior bar close × (1 + risk percentage × reward-to-risk target)
Exit: short, high ≥ prior bar high × (1 + risk percentage) OR low ≤ prior bar close × (1 − risk percentage × reward-to-risk target)
