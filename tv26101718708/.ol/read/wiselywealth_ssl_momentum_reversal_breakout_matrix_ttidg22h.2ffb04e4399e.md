Calculation: High and low channel averages = selected moving average of high and low over the same lookback
Calculation: SSL state = 1 when close > high channel average; −1 when close < low channel average; otherwise retain the previous state, initially 1
Calculation: Channel expansion = absolute difference between the channel averages / ATR
Calculation: Long score = round(clamp(0–100, price-action score + RSI-momentum score + ADX/directional-dominance score + relative-volume score + channel-expansion score − upper-wick and SSL-overextension penalties)); price action uses SSL flip, close distance above the high channel average / ATR, candle body / range, and close location in range; RSI momentum uses RSI relative to 50 and its change; ADX/directional dominance uses ADX relative to its threshold and the difference between positive and negative directional indicators; relative volume = volume / its 20-bar simple moving average; channel expansion uses normalized channel width and its change; penalties use upper-wick / range and absolute close-to-SSL-line distance / ATR
Calculation: Short score = round(clamp(0–100, price-action score + RSI-momentum score + ADX/directional-dominance score + relative-volume score + channel-expansion score − lower-wick and SSL-overextension penalties)); price action uses SSL flip, close distance below the low channel average / ATR, candle body / range, and close location in range; RSI momentum uses RSI relative to 50 and its change; ADX/directional dominance uses ADX relative to its threshold and the difference between negative and positive directional indicators; relative volume = volume / its 20-bar simple moving average; channel expansion uses normalized channel width and its change; penalties use lower-wick / range and absolute close-to-SSL-line distance / ATR
Long: chart bar is closed AND SSL state changes from −1 to 1 AND RSI > 50 AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator AND ADX > its threshold AND channel expansion >= its threshold AND long score >= its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe SSL state = 1, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar)
Short: chart bar is closed AND SSL state changes from 1 to −1 AND RSI < 50 AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator AND ADX > its threshold AND channel expansion >= its threshold AND short score >= its threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe SSL state = −1, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar)
