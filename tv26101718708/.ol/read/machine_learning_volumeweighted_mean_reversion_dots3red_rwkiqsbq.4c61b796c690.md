Calculation: Volume-weighted kernel regression = weighted mean of prior chart-bar closes, with each weight equal to a volume weight times a selected time-kernel weight; the volume weight is the bar’s volume divided by the peak volume in its normalization lookback, raised to a power and bounded below by a floor. Exclude the still-forming bar. The selected kernel is Gaussian, rational quadratic, or Epanechnikov.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = regression plus and minus a band half-width; the half-width is a selected multiple of ATR, a selected multiple of the standard deviation of closes, or a selected percentage of the regression.
Long: In extension mode, close < lower band; or, in reversion mode, previous close <= previous lower band AND close > lower band AND (slope filter disabled OR regression > its prior value). Require signals enabled, the minimum gap since the previous signal, and no prior long signal still active (reset that direction when close is inside both bands).
Short: In extension mode, close > upper band; or, in reversion mode, previous close >= previous upper band AND close < upper band AND (slope filter disabled OR regression <= its prior value). Require signals enabled, the minimum gap since the previous signal, and no prior short signal still active (reset that direction when close is inside both bands).
Exit: long, close >= volume-weighted kernel regression.
Exit: short, close <= volume-weighted kernel regression.
