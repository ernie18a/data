Calculation: Upper-wick share = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low)
Calculation: Lower-wick share = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low)
Calculation: Formation box top = highest high in the wick-sequence window; formation box bottom = lowest low in that window
Calculation: Buy volume = sum, for each candle overlapping the formation box, of volume × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Sell volume = sum, for each candle overlapping the formation box, of volume × (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)
Calculation: Risk distance = ATR × stop-loss multiplier when ATR stop is selected; otherwise entry price × stop-loss percentage
Calculation: Take-profit level = entry price + risk distance × reward ratio for a long, or entry price − risk distance × reward ratio for a short
Long: no formation is active AND the current bar newly completes a window with at least the required number of bars whose lower-wick share is at least the bullish minimum and upper-wick share is at most the bullish maximum (and whose range is at least ATR × multiplier when the candle ATR filter is enabled), while fewer than the required number meet the bearish wick requirements; after the formation bar, breakout price (close when close-based breakout is selected, otherwise high) > formation box top AND buy volume ≥ sell volume AND (breakout candle size filter disabled OR current high − low ≥ prior-bar average candle range × size multiplier)
Short: no formation is active AND the current bar newly completes a window with at least the required number of bars whose upper-wick share is at least the bearish minimum and lower-wick share is at most the bearish maximum (and whose range is at least ATR × multiplier when the candle ATR filter is enabled), while fewer than the required number meet the bullish wick requirements; after the formation bar, breakout price (close when close-based breakout is selected, otherwise low) < formation box bottom AND sell volume > buy volume AND (breakout candle size filter disabled OR current high − low ≥ prior-bar average candle range × size multiplier)
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ stop-loss level OR (an enabled take-profit level exists and high ≥ the furthest enabled take-profit level)
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ stop-loss level OR (an enabled take-profit level exists and low ≤ the furthest enabled take-profit level)
