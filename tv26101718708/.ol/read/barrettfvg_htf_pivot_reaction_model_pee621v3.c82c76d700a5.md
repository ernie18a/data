Calculation: Qualified support/resistance level = a confirmed swing low/high from a higher timeframe, merged with pivots within the merge tolerance; it has at least the minimum reaction count and source-timeframe count, and is not expired. A reaction occurs when price enters the level’s touch band and then moves away in the expected direction by at least the required distance before the reaction window expires. Use confirmed higher-timeframe bars; pivot lookback is the same for all source timeframes.
Calculation: Tap setup = a qualified level is touched without closing beyond its touch band plus the overshoot allowance; direction is long when the previous close was above the level, short when it was below.
Calculation: Reclaim setup = price closes beyond a qualified level’s touch band plus the overshoot allowance, a confirmed chart-timeframe swing forms beyond the level (swing low above a broken resistance, swing high below a broken support), then price returns to touch the level within the retest window; direction is long when the return approaches from above, short when it approaches from below. After reclaim, reverse the level’s support/resistance polarity.
Calculation: Break of structure = close crosses above the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high plus its buffer, or below the latest confirmed swing low minus its buffer. Use the same pivot lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Accumulation range = after a tap or reclaim setup, the running high-low range stays within the maximum width for at least the minimum duration; freeze the range then, and discard it if it exceeds the width or the total setup window expires.
Calculation: Bearish/bullish channel break = close crosses above the far edge of a valid descending channel formed from chart-timeframe swing highs, or below the far edge of a valid ascending channel formed from chart-timeframe swing lows. Channel pivot lookback is the same for both directions; channel padding uses chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Recent major level = previous completed day/week high or low touched within the configured bar window; use completed higher-timeframe bars. The true day open is excluded.
Long: A qualified tap or reclaim setup arms within its direction-specific cooldown, then a break of structure up occurs before the setup window expires; AND the bar is confirmed, within an enabled session, and outside the long signal cooldown. OR an accumulation range breaks with close above its high and breakout volume meets the configured volume filter; AND the bar is confirmed, within an enabled session, and outside the long signal cooldown. OR a long entry occurs with a recent bearish channel break up or a recent major level touch and close above that level; OR a bearish channel breaks up within the forward window after a long entry.
Short: A qualified tap or reclaim setup arms within its direction-specific cooldown, then a break of structure down occurs before the setup window expires; AND the bar is confirmed, within an enabled session, and outside the short signal cooldown. OR an accumulation range breaks with close below its low and breakout volume meets the configured volume filter; AND the bar is confirmed, within an enabled session, and outside the short signal cooldown. OR a short entry occurs with a recent bullish channel break down or a recent major level touch and close below that level; OR a bullish channel breaks down within the forward window after a short entry.
