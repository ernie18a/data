Calculation: Supertrend = ATR-based trailing band used to identify direction changes
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = price pivot high above the previous price pivot high while RSI pivot high is below the previous RSI pivot high; bullish divergence = price pivot low below the previous price pivot low while RSI pivot low is above the previous RSI pivot low
Calculation: Bullish retest setup = after a bullish signal during an active London or New York session, with the higher-timeframe close above its EMA and ADX above the retest threshold, save a zone from the signal close minus an ATR-based zone width to the signal close, a structural stop below the recent lowest low by an ATR-based buffer, and the setup bar; clear the setup when its wait limit expires, the close falls below the structural stop, or a retest signal fires
Calculation: Bearish retest setup = after a bearish signal during an active London or New York session, with the higher-timeframe close below its EMA and ADX above the retest threshold, save a zone from the signal close to the signal close plus an ATR-based zone width, a structural stop above the recent highest high by an ATR-based buffer, and the setup bar; clear the setup when its wait limit expires, the close rises above the structural stop, or a retest signal fires
Long: current close crosses above Supertrend OR (bullish retest setup is pending AND bar is more than one bar after the setup bar AND low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom AND close > open AND candle body / candle range >= the timeframe-dependent minimum)
Short: current close crosses below Supertrend OR (bearish retest setup is pending AND bar is more than one bar after the setup bar AND high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND close < open AND candle body / candle range >= the timeframe-dependent minimum)
