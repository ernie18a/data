Calculation: Confluence score = weighted average of (1) structure: 65 + 35 × candle-body/range when candle direction agrees with the higher-timeframe EMA(20) direction, otherwise 5 + 25 × candle-body/range; (2) proximity: 100 − 18 × ATR distance to the nearest confirmed pivot high or low, clamped to 0–100; (3) session quality by hour; (4) candle-body/range; and (5) ATR divided by its longer-lookback average, scoring highest within a middle range and decreasing below or above it. The EMA uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; pivots and other calculations use chart-timeframe bars.
Long: higher-timeframe close > its EMA(20) AND (confluence score ≥ signal threshold OR (confluence score ≥ 80 AND previous score < 80) OR (confluence score in the 65–79 tier AND previous score < 40))
Short: higher-timeframe close ≤ its EMA(20) AND (confluence score ≥ signal threshold OR (confluence score ≥ 80 AND previous score < 80) OR (confluence score in the 65–79 tier AND previous score < 40))
