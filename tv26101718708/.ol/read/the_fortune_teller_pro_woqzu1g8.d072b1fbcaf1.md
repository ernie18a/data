Calculation: Current-day running high/low = highest high and lowest low among chart-timeframe bars since the daily boundary, including the current bar
Calculation: Volume moving average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback period
Long: (close crosses above current-day running high OR close crosses above short-term EMA) AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND volume > volume moving average
Short: (close crosses below current-day running low OR close crosses below short-term EMA) AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND volume > volume moving average
