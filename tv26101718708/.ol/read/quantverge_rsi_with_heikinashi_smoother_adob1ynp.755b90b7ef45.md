Calculation: HA-RSI candle = Compute RSI separately on chart-timeframe open, high, low, and close using the same lookback; HA-RSI close is their average. HA-RSI open is the average of the current and previous HA-RSI open and close, initialized as the average of RSI(open) and RSI(close). HA-RSI high is the maximum of RSI(high), HA-RSI open, and HA-RSI close; HA-RSI low is the minimum of RSI(low), HA-RSI open, and HA-RSI close.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe HA-RSI close = Average the RSI values of higher-timeframe open, high, low, and close using the same lookback as on the chart timeframe; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: HA-RSI close crosses above HA-RSI open AND HA-RSI open = HA-RSI low AND (strict mode is off OR previous HA-RSI close < oversold level) AND (price-action filter is off OR close > open) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe HA-RSI close > RSI midpoint) AND bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown.
Short: HA-RSI close crosses below HA-RSI open AND HA-RSI open = HA-RSI high AND (strict mode is off OR previous HA-RSI close > overbought level) AND (price-action filter is off OR close < open) AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR higher-timeframe HA-RSI close < RSI midpoint) AND bars since the most recent long or short signal >= cooldown.
