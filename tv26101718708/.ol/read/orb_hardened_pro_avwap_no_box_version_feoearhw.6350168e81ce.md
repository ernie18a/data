Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high among chart-timeframe bars during the opening interval
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low among chart-timeframe bars during the opening interval
Calculation: Session-anchored VWAP = cumulative (hlc3 × volume) since session open ÷ cumulative volume since session open, where hlc3 = (high + low + close) ÷ 3
Long: Opening range is ready AND close crosses above opening-range high AND close > session-anchored VWAP AND volume > volume simple moving average × a multiplier
Short: Opening range is ready AND close crosses below opening-range low AND close < session-anchored VWAP AND volume > volume simple moving average × a multiplier
