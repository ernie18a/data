Calculation: Normalized MACD = 10,000 × (fast EMA(close) − slow EMA(close)) / slow EMA(close), with the fast lookback shorter than the slow; for volume-weighted MACD, use volume-weighted EMAs instead.
Calculation: DRM = EMA of 50 + 50 × smoothed range force / smoothed absolute range force; range force is the rolling close range when its high or low extends, otherwise the close change. Use the same lookback for the rolling range and RMA smoothing, then apply the oscillator smoothing.
Calculation: Selected oscillator = RSI, MFI, CCI, or DRM with EMA smoothing; Stochastic %K or Stoch RSI %K with SMA smoothing. Its basis is an EMA for RSI, MFI, CCI, or DRM, and a SMA of %K for Stochastic or Stoch RSI. MACD uses its signal EMA as basis.
Long: MACD crosses above its signal line AND (MACD < 0 OR prior MACD < 0) AND hlc3 > trend line; OR selected oscillator is enabled and trend mode is not Off AND ((oscillator crosses above basis while below OS AND fast cooldown elapsed) OR (oscillator crosses above OS − Extreme AND fast cooldown elapsed) OR (oscillator crosses above OS AND slow cooldown elapsed AND no fast signal) OR bullish divergence (price makes a lower low and oscillator makes a higher low versus their prior confirmed pivot lows)), with oscillator signals additionally requiring hl2 and close above the trend line unless trend mode is Range
Short: MACD crosses below its signal line AND (MACD > 0 OR prior MACD > 0) AND hlc3 < trend line; OR selected oscillator is enabled and trend mode is not Off AND ((oscillator crosses below basis while above OB AND fast cooldown elapsed) OR (oscillator crosses below OB + Extreme AND fast cooldown elapsed) OR (oscillator crosses below OB AND slow cooldown elapsed AND no fast signal) OR bearish divergence (price makes a higher high and oscillator makes a lower high versus their prior confirmed pivot highs)), with oscillator signals additionally requiring hl2 and close below the trend line unless trend mode is Range
