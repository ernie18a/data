Calculation: Opening range high/low = maximum high/minimum low from the start of the session (or configured opening window) until that window ends; freeze both values at its end
Calculation: Opening-range size = opening range high − opening range low
Calculation: Breakout strength = sum: candle body/range scores 0.2/0.6/1.1/1.5 at ratios <0.55/≥0.55/≥0.70/≥0.85; candle range/ATR scores 0.2/0.6/1.0/1.5 at ratios <1.0/≥1.0/≥1.4/≥2.0; volume/20-bar average scores 0/0.5/1.0/1.5 at ratios <1.2/≥1.2/≥1.5/≥2.0; momentum alignment over 3 and 5 bars scores −0.4 if neither aligns, 0.7 if one aligns, 1.5 if both align; breakout penetration/ATR scores 0/0.3/0.6/1.0 at ratios <0.1/≥0.1/≥0.25/≥0.5; RSI alignment scores 0.5 (long above 52, short below 48); higher-timeframe EMA alignment scores 1.0; close versus session open alignment scores 0.5; add 1.0, round to one decimal, and clamp to 0–10
Calculation: Breakout penetration = (close − opening range high)/ATR for long, or (opening range low − close)/ATR for short
Calculation: ADR percentage = opening-range size / average daily range from the selected lookback of completed daily bars × 100
Calculation: Initial stop and targets = choose ATR distance, opposite opening-range edge, or opening-range midpoint as the raw stop; clamp its distance from entry to the configured minimum and maximum ATR distances; place three targets at configured R multiples of that risk, in the position direction
Long: opening range is complete AND trade window is active AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit is off OR no trade has been taken today) AND (close > opening range high OR high > opening range high, according to the configured close-or-wick trigger) AND NOT (close < opening range low OR low < opening range low, using the same trigger) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is off OR close > EMA calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADR filter is off OR ADR percentage is within its configured bounds) AND breakout strength ≥ configured minimum AND ATR is available
Short: opening range is complete AND trade window is active AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit is off OR no trade has been taken today) AND (close < opening range low OR low < opening range low, according to the configured close-or-wick trigger) AND NOT (close > opening range high OR high > opening range high, using the same trigger) AND (higher-timeframe EMA filter is off OR close < EMA calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ADR filter is off OR ADR percentage is within its configured bounds) AND breakout strength ≥ configured minimum AND ATR is available
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ target 1 OR high ≥ target 2 OR high ≥ target 3 OR trade window closes
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ target 1 OR low ≤ target 2 OR low ≤ target 3 OR trade window closes
