Calculation: VoVix = (short-lookback ATR − long-lookback ATR) / standard deviation of short-lookback ATR
Calculation: Regime index = count, over the regime lookback, of bars where the absolute VoVix value one chart bar earlier exceeds the spike threshold
Calculation: Decay confirmed = if the decay-persistence parameter is ≤ 1: regime index two chart bars earlier ≥ event-count threshold AND regime index one chart bar earlier ≤ event-count threshold − decay buffer; otherwise: regime index one chart bar earlier < event-count threshold − decay buffer AND the number of chart bars since regime index one chart bar earlier exceeded that cutoff ≥ the decay-persistence parameter
Long: flat AND |VoVix one chart bar earlier| > spike threshold AND its absolute value equals the highest absolute VoVix over the local-extreme lookback ending one chart bar earlier AND |VoVix one chart bar earlier| > the prior value of its moving average (lookback four times the local-extreme lookback) × super-spike multiplier AND regime index one chart bar earlier ≥ event-count threshold AND VoVix one chart bar earlier > 0 AND close one chart bar earlier equals the lowest close over the local-extreme lookback ending one chart bar earlier AND higher-timeframe VoVix on the last completed higher-timeframe bar > spike threshold
Short: flat AND |VoVix one chart bar earlier| > spike threshold AND its absolute value equals the highest absolute VoVix over the local-extreme lookback ending one chart bar earlier AND |VoVix one chart bar earlier| > the prior value of its moving average (lookback four times the local-extreme lookback) × super-spike multiplier AND regime index one chart bar earlier ≥ event-count threshold AND VoVix one chart bar earlier < 0 AND close one chart bar earlier equals the highest close over the local-extreme lookback ending one chart bar earlier AND higher-timeframe VoVix on the last completed higher-timeframe bar < −spike threshold
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop percentage) OR decay confirmed
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop percentage) OR decay confirmed
