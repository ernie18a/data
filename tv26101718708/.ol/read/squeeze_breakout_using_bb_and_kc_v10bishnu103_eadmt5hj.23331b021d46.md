Calculation: Keltner Channel = EMA of close ± a configurable multiple of ATR
Long: close > upper Bollinger Band AND previous completed bar’s Bollinger Band width < ATR × configured squeeze width AND (for each of the six previous completed bars, upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel OR, consistently on the lower side, lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel) when the BB-within-KC filter is enabled AND short-to-long EMAs are in descending order when the EMA trend filter is enabled AND volume > its longer-term average when the volume filter is enabled AND previous completed bar’s close > its long-term SMA AND long-term SMA / previous completed bar’s close ≥ configured ratio when the 200MA filter is enabled
Short: close < lower Bollinger Band AND previous completed bar’s Bollinger Band width < ATR × configured squeeze width AND (for each of the six previous completed bars, upper Bollinger Band < upper Keltner Channel OR, consistently on the lower side, lower Bollinger Band > lower Keltner Channel) when the BB-within-KC filter is enabled AND short-to-long EMAs are in ascending order when the EMA trend filter is enabled AND volume > its longer-term average when the volume filter is enabled AND previous completed bar’s close < its long-term SMA AND previous completed bar’s close / long-term SMA ≥ configured ratio when the 200MA filter is enabled
Exit: long, close < Bollinger Band middle line OR close crosses below the short-term EMA and price ≤ the low of that signal bar OR intraday bar close reaches the configured exit time
Exit: short, close > Bollinger Band middle line OR close crosses above the short-term EMA and price ≥ the high of that signal bar OR intraday bar close reaches the configured exit time
