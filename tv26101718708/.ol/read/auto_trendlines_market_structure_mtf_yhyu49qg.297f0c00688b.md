Calculation: confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest within the configurable lookback on each side; confirmed only after the bars on the right are complete
Calculation: active resistance/support = line through the two newest confirmed swing highs/lows, respectively, if no checked older swing high is above the resistance line, or older swing low below the support line, by more than an ATR-based tolerance; inactive after a close breaches the line by more than an ATR-based tolerance
Calculation: normalized line slope = active line's price change per chart bar divided by ATR
Long: confirmed swing low OR close above active resistance by more than an ATR-based tolerance OR both active line slopes exceed the positive flatness threshold OR only one line is active and its normalized slope exceeds the positive flatness threshold OR, on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, both active line slopes exceed the positive flatness threshold OR only one line is active and its normalized slope exceeds the positive flatness threshold
Short: confirmed swing high OR close below active support by more than an ATR-based tolerance OR both active line slopes are below the negative flatness threshold OR only one line is active and its normalized slope is below the negative flatness threshold OR, on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, both active line slopes are below the negative flatness threshold OR only one line is active and its normalized slope is below the negative flatness threshold
