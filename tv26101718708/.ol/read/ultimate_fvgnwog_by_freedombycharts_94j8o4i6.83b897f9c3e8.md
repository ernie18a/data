Calculation: Bullish FVG = the high of a bar two bars before is below the low of the current bar; bearish FVG = the low of a bar two bars before is above the high of the current bar. The intervening bar is unrestricted.
Calculation: FVG mitigation = for a bullish FVG, the low reaches the zone’s bottom or midpoint, or the close reaches the zone’s bottom or midpoint; for a bearish FVG, the high reaches the zone’s top or midpoint, or the close reaches the zone’s top or midpoint, according to the selected mitigation method.
Long: A bullish FVG forms on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar OR on the last completed higher-timeframe bar of a three-bar pattern, detected on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe period OR the Sunday weekend-opening gap is bullish OR a bearish FVG is mitigated and marked as an inverse FVG.
Short: A bearish FVG forms on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar OR on the last completed higher-timeframe bar of a three-bar pattern, detected on the first chart bar of the next higher-timeframe period OR the Sunday weekend-opening gap is bearish OR a bullish FVG is mitigated and marked as an inverse FVG.
