Calculation: Long score = capped weighted sum of bullish EMA alignment, bullish fast-EMA slope, oversold RSI and stochastic, bullish structure break, bullish candle pattern, volume above its moving average, proximity to recent swing support, ADX above its minimum, close near and above daily VWAP, bullish vector candle, and bullish multi-timeframe count; omit components whose filters are disabled.
Calculation: Short score = capped weighted sum of bearish EMA alignment, bearish fast-EMA slope, overbought RSI and stochastic, bearish structure break, bearish candle pattern, volume above its moving average, proximity to recent swing resistance, ADX above its minimum, close near and below daily VWAP, bearish vector candle, and bearish multi-timeframe count; omit components whose filters are disabled.
Calculation: Fast-EMA slope = (fast EMA now − fast EMA three chart bars ago) / ATR × 100; bullish/bearish slope requires it above/below the configured positive/negative minimum.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low; a change-of-character is such a break when the prior structure trend was bearish / bullish. Pivots use a configurable lookback and one right-side bar for confirmation.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish vector candle = close above / at or below open, body divided by high−low meets the configured minimum, and volume × (high−low) divided by its longer-lookback moving average meets the configured vector threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish multi-timeframe direction = at least two of these agree: fast EMA > / < mid EMA, close > / < slow EMA, RSI > 55 / < 45. Count bullish and bearish directions across enabled timeframes; use the latest available bar without lookahead on each requested timeframe, whether that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Near swing support/resistance = close is in the bottom/top fifth of the range between the recent lowest low and highest high over twice the structure-pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish candle pattern = bullish/bearish engulfing, hammer/shooting star, or a same-direction candle whose body exceeds half its range.
Long: Confirmed bar AND long score ≥ configured score threshold AND long score > short score AND absolute score difference ≥ configured minimum gap AND bullish slope filter passes AND bullish multi-timeframe count reaches its configured minimum AND ADX is not below its configured minimum when the ADX filter is enabled AND no long cooldown, opposing-trade lock, or same-direction active-trade lock applies.
Short: Confirmed bar AND short score ≥ configured score threshold AND short score > long score AND absolute score difference ≥ configured minimum gap AND bearish slope filter passes AND bearish multi-timeframe count reaches its configured minimum AND ADX is not below its configured minimum when the ADX filter is enabled AND no short cooldown, opposing-trade lock, or same-direction active-trade lock applies.
Long: Bullish vector candle.
Short: Bearish vector candle.
Long: Bullish structure break or bullish change-of-character.
Short: Bearish structure break or bearish change-of-character.
Exit: Long, low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop buffer OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop buffer × configured first-target risk/reward multiple OR more than 100 bars since entry.
Exit: Short, high ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop buffer OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop buffer × configured first-target risk/reward multiple OR more than 100 bars since entry.
