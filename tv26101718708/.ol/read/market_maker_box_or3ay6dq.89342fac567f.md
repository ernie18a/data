Calculation: Previous higher-timeframe range = high and low of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; midpoint = (high + low) / 2
Calculation: Direction score = sum of ATR-normalized trend components (fast EMA vs. slow EMA, fast EMA slope, current higher-timeframe close vs. slow EMA, last completed 6-hour candle body and whether it agrees with the preceding 6-hour body, and last completed daily candle body) and current-window components (previous range candle body and close location, sweep-and-reclaim, and forming higher-timeframe candle body). Use a longer lookback for the slow EMA than the fast EMA. The 6-hour and daily inputs use last completed bars; the current-window inputs use the forming higher-timeframe bar. Weight the forming candle more as the window progresses, and weight an opposing live candle only late in the window. If trend is strong, lock the score to its direction unless an opposing sweep-and-reclaim is confirmed by the live candle after the window has progressed; for a weaker trend, neutralize a score that opposes the trend when no such reversal is confirmed.
Long: Direction score >= bullish conviction threshold AND close is within the previous completed higher-timeframe range AND range width as a percentage of close >= minimum width OR low <= previous range low AND low[1] > previous range low OR higher-timeframe bar closes above the previous completed higher-timeframe high
Short: Direction score <= negative bearish conviction threshold AND close is within the previous completed higher-timeframe range AND range width as a percentage of close >= minimum width OR high >= previous range high AND high[1] < previous range high OR higher-timeframe bar closes below the previous completed higher-timeframe low
