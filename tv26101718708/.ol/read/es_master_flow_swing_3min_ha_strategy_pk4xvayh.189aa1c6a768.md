Calculation: Trailing stop = while long, initialize at entry-bar low minus ATR times the initial-stop multiplier, then each bar take the maximum of the previous stop and current low minus ATR times the trailing multiplier; while short, initialize at entry-bar high plus ATR times the initial-stop multiplier, then each bar take the minimum of the previous stop and current high plus ATR times the trailing multiplier
Long: current bar is complete AND close is above both the 50-period and 200-period EMAs AND ADX is above its threshold AND current close crosses above the upper Keltner Channel band (previous close was at or below the previous upper band) AND time is within the entry session AND daily loss limit and consecutive-loss limit are not halted AND position is flat
Short: current bar is complete AND close is below both the 50-period and 200-period EMAs AND ADX is above its threshold AND current close crosses below the lower Keltner Channel band (previous close was at or above the previous lower band) AND time is within the entry session AND daily loss limit and consecutive-loss limit are not halted AND position is flat
Exit: long, current bar is complete AND low is at or below the trailing stop OR time is within the end-of-day exit window OR daily loss limit or consecutive-loss limit is halted
Exit: short, current bar is complete AND high is at or above the trailing stop OR time is within the end-of-day exit window OR daily loss limit or consecutive-loss limit is halted
