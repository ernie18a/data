Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low; a change of character is such a break opposite the previous structure direction.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback.
Calculation: Displacement gap = low above the high two bars earlier / high below the low two bars earlier, respectively, with a same-direction candle and volume above the volume average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed after equal numbers of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Active liquidity pool = a pair of confirmed swing highs/lows within a configurable ATR distance; its level is the higher high / lower low, respectively.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = midpoint between the latest confirmed swing high and swing low.
Calculation: Long/short stop = beyond the entry zone low/high by an ATR buffer; for momentum entries, use the lowest/highest low/high over a configurable lookback as the zone low/high.
Calculation: Profit targets = the first three distinct levels beyond entry in the trade direction, each farther than an ATR-based buffer, from confirmed and prior swing highs/lows, higher-timeframe equilibrium levels, point of control, prior day/week/month highs/lows, and active liquidity pools; if fewer than three exist, fill missing targets successively one risk distance apart, starting from entry for target 1 or the prior target for later targets.
Long: On a confirmed bar, after sufficient history and cooldown and within the selected session if enabled, when flat: (momentum engine enabled AND selected trend filter passes (close above EMA, bullish Supertrend, at least three of five timeframe EMA trends bullish, or no filter) AND any of stochastic K crossing above D, RSI crossing above its configured long trigger, MACD histogram crossing above zero, or close crossing above the fast EMA) OR (zone engine enabled AND a bullish zone was tapped AND within its confirmation window close > open and close >= zone midpoint, with enabled structure, location, setup-class, and volume filters satisfied) OR (sweep entries enabled AND an active sell-side liquidity pool is swept AND within its confirmation window a bullish structure change occurs with close > open and enabled setup-class and volume filters satisfied).
Short: On a confirmed bar, after sufficient history and cooldown and within the selected session if enabled, when flat and no long signal: (momentum engine enabled AND selected trend filter passes (close below EMA, bearish Supertrend, at most two of five timeframe EMA trends bullish, or no filter) AND any of stochastic K crossing below D, RSI crossing below its configured short trigger, MACD histogram crossing below zero, or close crossing below the fast EMA) OR (zone engine enabled AND a bearish zone was tapped AND within its confirmation window close < open and close <= zone midpoint, with enabled structure, location, setup-class, and volume filters satisfied) OR (sweep entries enabled AND an active buy-side liquidity pool is swept AND within its confirmation window a bearish structure change occurs with close < open and enabled setup-class and volume filters satisfied).
Exit: Long, low <= stop (moved to entry after target 1 is reached) OR high >= target 3 OR the trade exceeds its configured lifetime OR bearish structure change OR, when smart exit is enabled, MACD histogram crosses below zero OR bearish displacement gap occurs OR two consecutive below-average-volume bars close below entry with a bearish candle OR, when opposite-signal flipping is enabled, a short signal occurs. Also mark a momentum long as ended when MACD histogram crosses below zero OR close crosses below the fast EMA OR the selected trend filter flips bearish.
Exit: Short, high >= stop (moved to entry after target 1 is reached) OR low <= target 3 OR the trade exceeds its configured lifetime OR bullish structure change OR, when smart exit is enabled, MACD histogram crosses above zero OR bullish displacement gap occurs OR two consecutive below-average-volume bars close above entry with a bullish candle OR, when opposite-signal flipping is enabled, a long signal occurs. Also mark a momentum short as ended when MACD histogram crosses above zero OR close crosses above the fast EMA OR the selected trend filter flips bullish.
