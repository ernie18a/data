Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Accumulation range = highest high minus lowest low over the accumulation lookback; qualify when below the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Sweep = price exceeds one, but not both, accumulation boundaries by the configured ATR multiple, then closes back from the breached side by at least the configured wick fraction; if volume filtering is enabled, volume must exceed its moving average
Calculation: Structure-break level = selected accumulation boundary or range midpoint; after a sweep, the break is a close above this level for a bullish setup or below it for a bearish setup
Calculation: Bullish gap = current low minus high two bars earlier; bearish gap = low two bars earlier minus current high; each must meet the configured ATR-based minimum. If displacement is enabled, the current or prior candle must close in the setup direction with body-to-range ratio meeting the configured minimum
Calculation: Trend filter = higher-timeframe close above all three EMAs (short-, medium-, and long-lookback) for longs, or below all three for shorts; use the current higher-timeframe bar, which may still be forming
Calculation: VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3
Long: An active accumulation has a qualifying downside sweep, followed within the configured timeout by a structure break; a bullish gap forms at least one bar after the break and within its configured window; then, within the retest window from the latest qualifying gap (updating to any wider bullish gap), the previous close is at or above the gap bottom, the current low touches or penetrates the gap top, and the current close is at or above the gap bottom; AND, if enabled, the trend filter is bullish; AND, if VWAP filtering is enabled, the gap bottom and current close are above VWAP. If gap-break mode is enabled instead, after price has touched the gap, signal when the current close is above the gap top within the retest window, subject to the same enabled filters.
Short: An active accumulation has a qualifying upside sweep, followed within the configured timeout by a structure break; a bearish gap forms at least one bar after the break and within its configured window; then, within the retest window from the latest qualifying gap (updating to any wider bearish gap), the previous close is at or below the gap top, the current high touches or penetrates the gap bottom, and the current close is at or below the gap top; AND, if enabled, the trend filter is bearish; AND, if VWAP filtering is enabled, the gap top and current close are below VWAP. If gap-break mode is enabled instead, after price has touched the gap, signal when the current close is below the gap bottom within the retest window, subject to the same enabled filters.
