Calculation: Bullish BOS = On the selected OB timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured), the last completed bar closes above the most recent confirmed swing high, having opened below or at it, and its close minus open exceeds the displacement multiple times ATR.
Calculation: Bearish BOS = On the selected OB timeframe, the last completed bar closes below the most recent confirmed swing low, having opened above or at it, and its open minus close exceeds the displacement multiple times ATR.
Calculation: Bullish order block = On a bullish BOS, the most recent bearish candle within the preceding lookback bars; its high is the top and its low is the bottom. It is active until an OB-timeframe close falls below its bottom.
Calculation: Bearish order block = On a bearish BOS, the most recent bullish candle within the preceding lookback bars; its high is the top and its low is the bottom. It is active until an OB-timeframe close rises above its top.
Long: An active bullish order block exists AND (no active bearish order block exists OR the absolute distance from close to the bullish order block bottom is less than or equal to the absolute distance from close to the bearish order block top).
Short: An active bearish order block exists AND (no active bullish order block exists OR the absolute distance from close to the bearish order block top is less than the absolute distance from close to the bullish order block bottom).
