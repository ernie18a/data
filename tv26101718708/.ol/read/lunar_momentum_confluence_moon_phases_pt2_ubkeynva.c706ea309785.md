Calculation: Lunar bias = -cos(2π × lunar-cycle fraction), where cycle age is datetime elapsed since the reference new moon modulo the synodic month; fraction = cycle age / synodic month
Calculation: New/full moon event = lunar phase bucket changes into bucket 0/4; bucket = floor((lunar-cycle fraction + 0.0625) × 8) modulo 8
Calculation: Bull trend = close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA > its prior-bar value; EMA lookbacks are short < medium < long
Calculation: Bear trend = close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA < its prior-bar value; EMA lookbacks are short < medium < long
Calculation: Bull participation = relative volume >= threshold AND close > open AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= 0.60 AND abs(close - open) >= 0.22 × ATR
Calculation: Bear participation = relative volume >= threshold AND close < open AND (high - close) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= 0.60 AND abs(close - open) >= 0.22 × ATR
Calculation: Bull momentum score = sum of weights 24 for bull trend, 8 for close > medium-term EMA, 16 for RSI >= bull RSI threshold, 5 for RSI > bull RSI threshold + 4, 16 for MACD histogram > 0 and >= prior-bar value, 10 for close > session VWAP, 11 for bull participation, and 10 if higher-timeframe filter is disabled or higher-timeframe close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA; each weight contributes only when its condition is true; clamp total to 0–100
Calculation: Bear momentum score = sum of weights 24 for bear trend, 8 for close < medium-term EMA, 16 for RSI <= bear RSI threshold, 5 for RSI < bear RSI threshold - 4, 16 for MACD histogram < 0 and <= prior-bar value, 10 for close < session VWAP, 11 for bear participation, and 10 if higher-timeframe filter is disabled or higher-timeframe close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA; each weight contributes only when its condition is true; clamp total to 0–100
Calculation: Long confluence = clamp(0.74 × bull momentum score + 0.26 × max(lunar bias, 0) × 100 + 6 if a full-moon event else 0, 0–100)
Calculation: Short confluence = clamp(0.74 × bear momentum score + 0.26 × max(-lunar bias, 0) × 100 + 6 if a new-moon event else 0, 0–100)
Calculation: Long trigger = (close > highest high over the prior breakout-lookback bars AND close > open) OR (lowest low over the pullback-lookback bars <= short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > open AND close > prior close)
Calculation: Short trigger = (close < lowest low over the prior breakout-lookback bars AND close < open) OR (highest high over the pullback-lookback bars >= short-term EMA AND close < short-term EMA AND close < open AND close < prior close)
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over the swing-lookback bars at entry minus ATR × stop buffer; long risk = max(entry price - long stop, 10 × minimum tick); long target 2 = entry price + long risk × target-2 risk multiple
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over the swing-lookback bars at entry plus ATR × stop buffer; short risk = max(short stop - entry price, 10 × minimum tick); short target 2 = entry price - short risk × target-2 risk multiple
Long: confirmed chart-timeframe bar AND long direction enabled AND more than cooldown bars since prior long signal AND lunar bias > long lunar threshold AND bull momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND long confluence >= minimum confluence score AND long trigger AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA); higher-timeframe calculations use the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Short: confirmed chart-timeframe bar AND short direction enabled AND more than cooldown bars since prior short signal AND lunar bias < -short lunar threshold AND bear momentum score >= minimum momentum score AND short confluence >= minimum confluence score AND short trigger AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA); higher-timeframe calculations use the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target 2 OR short decision
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target 2 OR long decision
