Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA pair = fast and slow EMAs calculated on bars higher than the chart timeframe; use the current higher-timeframe bar as it forms in real time, with lookahead off. The fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low, while RSI at the latest pivot is higher than RSI at the preceding pivot. Confirm pivots using bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = the latest confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high, while RSI at the latest pivot is lower than RSI at the preceding pivot. Confirm pivots using bars on both sides.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / moving average of volume.
Calculation: Extension z-score = (close - moving average of close) / standard deviation of close; use zero if the standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Continuation score = add points for each true condition: ADX above the strong threshold (20 points) or above the weak threshold (10 points); aligned trend with close beyond the fast EMA (15); aligned trend with MACD histogram moving farther from zero and on the trend side of zero (15); RSI between 50 and 70 in an uptrend or between 30 and 50 in a downtrend (10); volume ratio above its threshold (10); trend agrees with the higher-timeframe EMA pair (10). The strong ADX threshold exceeds the weak threshold.
Calculation: Reversal score = add points for each true condition: ADX below the weak threshold (15); ADX below its value three bars earlier while above the strong threshold (10); aligned trend with close on the opposite side of the fast EMA (10); trend-side MACD histogram weakening for two consecutive bars (10); RSI above its upper extreme in an uptrend or below its lower extreme in a downtrend (15); volume ratio above its higher threshold with a candle against the trend (10); absolute extension z-score above its higher threshold (15), or above its lower threshold (8); trend conflicts with the higher-timeframe EMA pair (10); divergence against the trend (15). The higher volume-ratio threshold exceeds the lower one, and the higher z-score threshold exceeds the lower one.
Calculation: Continuation percentage = continuation score / (continuation score + reversal score) * 100; use 50 when the score total is zero.
Calculation: Reversal percentage = 100 - continuation percentage.
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND continuation percentage >= signal-confidence threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA.
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND continuation percentage >= signal-confidence threshold AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA.
Exit: long, fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND reversal percentage >= signal-confidence threshold AND (bearish divergence OR RSI above its higher overbought threshold OR absolute extension z-score above its extreme threshold).
Exit: short, fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND reversal percentage >= signal-confidence threshold AND (bullish divergence OR RSI below its lower oversold threshold OR absolute extension z-score above its extreme threshold).
