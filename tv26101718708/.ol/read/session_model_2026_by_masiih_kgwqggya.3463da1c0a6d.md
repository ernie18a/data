Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the same number of bars on both sides; the pivot is known only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the same number of bars on both sides; the pivot is known only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = price makes a lower confirmed swing low than a prior swing low, while the comparison instrument does not make a lower low at the corresponding pivot; include the source’s alternate pivot matching cases.
Calculation: Bearish SMT divergence = price makes a higher confirmed swing high than a prior swing high, while the comparison instrument does not make a higher high at the corresponding pivot; include the source’s alternate pivot matching cases.
