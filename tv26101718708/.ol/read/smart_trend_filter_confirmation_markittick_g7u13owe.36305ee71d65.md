Calculation: Displayed trend direction = On the chart timeframe, smooth completed-bar HL2 using either a Kalman filter or an efficiency-ratio adaptive average (net displacement divided by summed absolute movement, controlling smoothing between faster and slower EMA constants). Compute a band half-width as an EMA of ATR multiplied by [a base multiplier + ATR/previous close × an ATR weight + the absolute close-to-close fractional move, capped at its 95th percentile, × a move weight]. Center upper and lower bands on the smoothed source. Initialize the trailing line at the upper band; if the last completed close is above the prior line, set raw direction bullish and line to the greater of the lower band and prior line; otherwise set raw direction bearish and line to the lesser of the upper band and prior line. Hold displayed direction at its prior value when any enabled test is true and no breakout override applies: absolute change in trailing line < ATR × threshold; absolute short-lookback linear-regression slope of trailing line / ATR < threshold; completed-bar volume ≤ its moving average; completed-bar high-low range ≤ its historical percentile; absolute trailing-line change / prior close × 10,000 < threshold; or ADX < threshold. The breakout override applies when absolute change in completed-bar closes > ATR × threshold. ATR, prior closes, filter inputs, and bands use completed chart-timeframe bars; signals are evaluated at bar close.
Long: Displayed trend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
Short: Displayed trend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Long, displayed trend direction changes from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, displayed trend direction changes from bearish to bullish.
