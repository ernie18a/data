Calculation: Liquidity pools = previous completed daily-bar high and low, plus highs and lows of completed enabled sessions; a pool is retired after a close through it or a breach that is not reclaimed within the reclaim window.
Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on the chart timeframe or on a higher daily/weekly timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Extension = max(0, (reference open − raided extreme) × trade direction) / ATR; reference open is the configured active-session, midnight, or daily open.
Calculation: Penetration = absolute distance from the raided pool level to the raid extreme / ATR.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier, the intervening bar closes above that earlier high, and its body change exceeds the configured threshold; bearish fair value gap is the inverse. Entry level is the configured fraction into the gap from the side price enters.
Calculation: Initial stop = raid extreme minus trade direction × configured ATR buffer; risk = absolute distance from entry to initial stop.
Calculation: Target = configured R multiple of risk, or the nearest unraided opposite-side pool at least the configured minimum R from entry.
Long: A live low-side pool is breached (low < pool level) and price closes back above it within the reclaim window; OR, for an eligible reclaimed low-side raid, the entry trigger is met: reclaim-bar close (no confirmation), a later close above the reclaim bar high (displacement), or a later low reaches the configured entry level of a bullish fair value gap formed after that displacement. Reject confirmation if price breaches the raid extreme again or its configured time window expires. Require valid stop geometry and any enabled minimum-risk rule; pass all enabled signal gates (extension and penetration buckets, direction, session, weekday, raid count, adaptive bucket record, higher-timeframe bias, premium/discount, SMT, session position, and one-trade-at-a-time).
Short: A live high-side pool is breached (high > pool level) and price closes back below it within the reclaim window; OR, for an eligible reclaimed high-side raid, the entry trigger is met: reclaim-bar close (no confirmation), a later close below the reclaim bar low (displacement), or a later high reaches the configured entry level of a bearish fair value gap formed after that displacement. Reject confirmation if price breaches the raid extreme again or its configured time window expires. Require valid stop geometry and any enabled minimum-risk rule; pass all enabled signal gates (extension and penetration buckets, direction, session, weekday, raid count, adaptive bucket record, higher-timeframe bias, premium/discount, SMT, session position, and one-trade-at-a-time).
Exit: Long, low <= current stop OR high >= target OR the resolution horizon expires; if a bar touches both stop and target, count the stop first. Optional management: move stop to entry after configured favorable R; after first target, bank configured fraction, move stop to entry, and target the runner at its second target; after configured favorable R, trail stop by configured percentage behind the best high.
Exit: Short, high >= current stop OR low <= target OR the resolution horizon expires; if a bar touches both stop and target, count the stop first. Optional management: move stop to entry after configured favorable R; after first target, bank configured fraction, move stop to entry, and target the runner at its second target; after configured favorable R, trail stop by configured percentage behind the best low.
