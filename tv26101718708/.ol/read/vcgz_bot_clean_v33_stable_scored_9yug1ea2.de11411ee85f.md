Calculation: Impulse range = highest high minus lowest low over the configured lookback; a long impulse qualifies when this is at least ATR times the minimum impulse threshold and the lowest-low bar is older than the highest-high bar; a short impulse qualifies when the range meets the same threshold and the highest-high bar is older than the lowest-low bar.
Calculation: Retracement zone = the configured lower and upper retracement fractions of the qualifying impulse range measured back from its extreme; zone midpoint = average of its boundaries. Keep the impulse and zone until the extreme exceeds the configured freshness age, the post-touch confirmation window expires, the corresponding higher-timeframe bias or session condition fails, or a position opens. Record the first bar whose range overlaps the zone as the touch bar; a reaction requires a later or same bar within the confirmation window to overlap the zone again and close beyond the midpoint in the trade direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = higher-timeframe close above (long) or below (short) its EMA, EMA rising (long) or falling (short) versus the prior higher-timeframe bar, and RSI at or beyond the configured bullish or bearish threshold. Use the current higher-timeframe bar (still forming in real time; historical values become available without lookahead).
Calculation: Confirmation candle = bullish (long) or bearish (short) candle, body at least the configured minimum ATR fraction, close in the configured strong-close portion of its range, and either a sweep beyond the prior configured local-lookback low (long) or high (short), or a corresponding wick larger than the configured fraction of the body.
Calculation: Local trend = close above (long) or below (short) the chart-timeframe EMA, with that EMA rising (long) or falling (short) versus the prior chart bar.
Calculation: Directional volume = volume above its chart-timeframe moving average times the configured multiplier, and close above open (long) or below open (short).
Calculation: Setup score = count of satisfied conditions: corresponding higher-timeframe bias, local trend, impulse size at least ATR times the configured strong-impulse threshold, zone reaction plus confirmation candle, and directional volume.
Calculation: Entry stop = for long, the lower of the signal-bar low and the lowest low over the recent three bars, minus the configured ATR buffer; for short, the higher of the signal-bar high and the highest high over the recent three bars, plus the configured ATR buffer. Risk = entry-to-stop distance. Target = entry plus risk times the configured reward multiple (long), or entry minus risk times that multiple (short). Fix stop and target at entry.
Long: bar closes confirmed AND no open position AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within configured session) AND a fresh locked long impulse has been touched within the confirmation window AND current bar overlaps its zone and closes above its midpoint AND confirmation candle AND local trend AND setup score at least the configured minimum AND risk > 0 AND no long signal fired on the prior bar.
Short: bar closes confirmed AND no open position AND (session filter disabled OR bar is within configured session) AND a fresh locked short impulse has been touched within the confirmation window AND current bar overlaps its zone and closes below its midpoint AND confirmation candle AND local trend AND setup score at least the configured minimum AND risk > 0 AND no short signal fired on the prior bar.
Exit: Long, low <= fixed entry stop OR high >= fixed entry target
Exit: Short, high >= fixed entry stop OR low <= fixed entry target
