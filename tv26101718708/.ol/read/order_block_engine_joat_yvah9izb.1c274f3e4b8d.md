Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low confirmed by the chosen number of bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = on an up-close bar that closes more than the chosen ATR multiple above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high, with volume above its chosen moving-average multiple when volume filtering is enabled and available, take the high/low of the most recent down-close bar within the chosen search depth. Bearish order-block zone is the mirror: a down-close bar closes more than the chosen ATR multiple below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low, and the zone is the high/low of the most recent up-close bar within the search depth.
Calculation: Order-block grade = sum of displacement-in-ATR score (1.0 if <0.6, 1.7 if ≥0.6, 2.5 if ≥1.0, 3.2 if ≥1.4, 4.0 if ≥2.0), volume-ratio score (0.4 if <0.9, 1.0 if ≥0.9, 1.8 if ≥1.2, 2.7 if ≥1.6, 3.5 if ≥2.2), and origin-candle body/range score (0.4 if <0.4, 1.0 if ≥0.4, 1.8 if ≥0.6, 2.5 if ≥0.8); cap at 10. Displacement is the close beyond the broken swing divided by ATR; volume ratio is breaking-bar volume divided by its moving average; body/range is absolute open-close divided by high-low.
Long: On a confirmed bar later than the zone’s creation, for any unmitigated bullish order-block zone, low ≤ zone high AND close > zone low AND close > open AND grade ≥ chosen minimum
Short: On a confirmed bar later than the zone’s creation, for any unmitigated bearish order-block zone, high ≥ zone low AND close < zone high AND close < open AND grade ≥ chosen minimum AND no long signal on that bar
Calculation: Long stop = bullish zone low minus chosen ATR buffer; short stop = bearish zone high plus chosen ATR buffer. Risk = absolute difference between entry price and stop. Long target 2 = entry price + risk × chosen target-2 R multiple; short target 2 = entry price − risk × chosen target-2 R multiple.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target 2
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target 2
