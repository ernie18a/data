Calculation: Weighted bullish/bearish probabilities = over the lookback bars, sum weights (1 + fraction of lookback remaining) for positive/negative close-to-close changes, respectively, then divide each sum by their combined sum.
Calculation: Directional memory = clamp(previous memory × decay + direction × (0.18 + 0.30 × absolute probability difference), −1, 1), where direction is the sign of the larger weighted probability; memory bias is positive/negative only when memory exceeds the positive/negative threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sniper score = count of these seven conditions: close above/below VWAP; chart-timeframe RSI above/below 50; MACD main above/below signal; EMA(9) above/below EMA(21); ADX above 25 and close above/below EMA(9); volume above its 20-bar SMA and candle bullish/bearish; RSI above/below 50 on a lower-timeframe bar. Sniper percentage = score / 7 × 100.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish cluster votes and weight = add one vote and the associated weight for each matching condition: weighted-probability direction (12); memory bias (8); close above/below trend EMA with EMA rising/falling (8); higher-timeframe close above/below its trend EMA with EMA rising/falling (10); close above/below VWAP (8); MACD main above/below signal (8); ADX above 20 and EMA(9) above/below EMA(21) (6); bullish/bearish candle (6); volume filter passes and candle bullish/bearish (6); sniper bias (10); sniper trigger (10); enabled bullish/bearish order-block touch or fair-value-gap touch (configured bonus weight). Consensus is directional votes divided by all votes; strength is the greater directional weight divided by 108 × 100; conflict is the lesser directional vote count divided by all votes × 100. Cluster direction is the side meeting the consensus threshold.
Calculation: Long/short score = 2 for matching preferred direction, plus 1 each for matching weighted-probability direction, memory bias, confidence threshold met, momentum threshold met, enabled EMA trend filter passing, enabled higher-timeframe EMA trend filter passing, enabled volume filter passing, ATR filter passing, enabled candle-body filter passing, matching sniper score greater than the opposite score (2), matching strong-bias threshold, close on the matching side of VWAP, MACD main on the matching side of signal, ADX above 25, lower-timeframe RSI on the matching side of 50, matching enabled TMA pattern bonus, and matching cluster direction bonus. Long/short edge = that side's score minus the opposite score.
Calculation: CISD direction = bullish when close crosses above the latest target set to the open of a prior bearish non-inside candle; bearish when close crosses below the latest target set to the open of a prior bullish non-inside candle. Direction persists until the opposite cross. Aligned CISD requires the same direction on chart, mid, and higher timeframe bars, confirmed for the configured number of bars; higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = recursively trail source by key value × ATR, moving it below a rising source or above a falling source; UT long/short trigger occurs when source crosses above/below that stop.
Calculation: Entry stop = ATR-based stop at entry close ± ATR distance, or valid swing low/high ± ATR buffer, or the closer of those valid stops in combined mode; offset outward by stop slippage. Risk = distance from entry close to stop. Long/short target 1 and target 5 = entry price ± risk × their respective target multiples.
Calculation: Trailing stop after target 1 = highest high since activation minus ATR × trail fraction for longs; lowest low since activation plus ATR × trail fraction for shorts. It only tightens the active stop.
Long: flat and entry guards pass AND at least one of: (session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND cooldown and price rearm pass AND long score and edge meet thresholds AND long entry gate passes); (session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND bullish EMA(9)/EMA(21) cross AND bullish sniper bias AND close above VWAP AND MACD main above signal AND lower-timeframe RSI above 50 AND long entry gate passes); (HFT layer enabled AND session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND HFT cooldown/rearm pass when enabled AND HFT long score/edge meet thresholds AND close above HFT KAMA when its filter is enabled AND bullish CISD alignment when its gate applies AND bullish TMA trend when its gate applies). Long entry gate is either strict: long bias gate AND bullish cluster gate AND bullish aligned CISD gate AND UT long trigger gate AND close above rising KAMA gate AND bullish TMA trend; or balanced: bullish sniper-score direction AND cluster is not bearish AND CISD state is not bearish AND no UT short trigger AND KAMA state is not bearish AND TMA state is not bearish, with each condition applied only when its filter is enabled. Also require long error cooldown and bad-entry-zone guards to pass.
Short: flat and entry guards pass AND at least one of: (session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND cooldown and price rearm pass AND short score and edge meet thresholds AND short entry gate passes); (session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND bearish EMA(9)/EMA(21) cross AND bearish sniper bias AND close below VWAP AND MACD main below signal AND lower-timeframe RSI below 50 AND short entry gate passes); (HFT layer enabled AND session allowed AND chop regime not blocking AND HFT cooldown/rearm pass when enabled AND HFT short score/edge meet thresholds AND close below HFT KAMA when its filter is enabled AND bearish CISD alignment when its gate applies AND bearish TMA trend when its gate applies). Short entry gate is either strict: short bias gate AND bearish cluster gate AND bearish aligned CISD gate AND UT short trigger gate AND close below falling KAMA gate AND bearish TMA trend; or balanced: bearish sniper-score direction AND cluster is not bullish AND CISD state is not bullish AND no UT long trigger AND KAMA state is not bullish AND TMA state is not bullish, with each condition applied only when its filter is enabled. Also require short error cooldown and bad-entry-zone guards to pass.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= target 1 OR high >= target 5 OR an opposite short signal occurs while reverse-close is enabled. Target 1 closes the configured portion; the remainder exits at target 5 or its active stop, which trails after target 1.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= target 1 OR low <= target 5 OR an opposite long signal occurs while reverse-close is enabled. Target 1 closes the configured portion; the remainder exits at target 5 or its active stop, which trails after target 1.
