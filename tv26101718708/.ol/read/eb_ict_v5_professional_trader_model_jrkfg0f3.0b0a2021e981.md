Calculation: Prior liquidity high = highest high over a configurable lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Prior liquidity low = lowest low over a configurable lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Long stop = lowest low over a configurable lookback ending on the signal bar
Calculation: Short stop = highest high over a configurable lookback ending on the signal bar
Long: low < prior liquidity low AND close > prior liquidity low AND close − low > ATR × configurable threshold
Long: close > open AND lower wick > candle body × configurable factor AND close is in the upper portion of the candle
Long: low < prior liquidity low AND close > prior liquidity low AND close − low > ATR × configurable threshold AND close > open AND lower wick > candle body × configurable factor AND close is in the upper portion of the candle AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND (fast EMA > its value several bars ago when slope is required) AND (RSI > 50 AND RSI < 75 when the RSI filter is enabled) AND (ADX >= minimum when the ADX filter is enabled) AND (previous completed higher-timeframe daily close > midpoint of its high and low when daily bias filter is enabled) AND (bar is in configured session when session filter is enabled) AND no long signal occurred within the configurable cooldown
Short: high > prior liquidity high AND close < prior liquidity high AND high − close > ATR × configurable threshold
Short: close < open AND upper wick > candle body × configurable factor AND close is in the lower portion of the candle
Short: high > prior liquidity high AND close < prior liquidity high AND high − close > ATR × configurable threshold AND close < open AND upper wick > candle body × configurable factor AND close is in the lower portion of the candle AND close < fast EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND (fast EMA < its value several bars ago when slope is required) AND (RSI < 50 AND RSI > 25 when the RSI filter is enabled) AND (ADX >= minimum when the ADX filter is enabled) AND (previous completed higher-timeframe daily close < midpoint of its high and low when daily bias filter is enabled) AND (bar is in configured session when session filter is enabled) AND no short signal occurred within the configurable cooldown OR high > prior liquidity high AND close < prior liquidity high AND high − close > ATR × configurable threshold AND close < open AND upper wick > candle body × configurable factor AND close is in the lower portion of the candle AND close > fast EMA AND fast EMA is lower than its value several bars ago AND no short signal occurred within the configurable cooldown AND premium-sell option is enabled
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + (entry price − long stop) × configurable risk/reward
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price − (short stop − entry price) × configurable risk/reward
