Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot whose high/low is greater/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; available only after right-side confirmation.
Calculation: Reclaimed swing low/high = price first trades below/above the latest confirmed swing low/high, then closes back above/below that level; reset when a new corresponding pivot forms.
Calculation: Swing-range position = (close − latest confirmed swing low) / (latest confirmed swing high − latest confirmed swing low), expressed as a percentage.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when the latest high and low comparisons are both higher; bearish when both are lower; otherwise neutral. A close above the previous swing high sets the high comparison higher, and a close below the previous swing low sets the low comparison lower.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = zone spanning the low-to-high of the lowest-low/highest-high candle, respectively, between a confirmed swing pivot and a close crossing above that pivot high/below that pivot low. Remove bullish zones when close is below their bottom and bearish zones when close is above their top. Nearest zone direction is the direction of the unmitigated zone whose bullish top or bearish bottom is closest to close; ties select bearish.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = gap between the high three bars earlier and the current low / between the current high and the low three bars earlier, respectively, with the intervening displacement candle bullish (close > open or close > prior close) / bearish (close < open or close < prior close), and the additional gap bounds not overlapping the middle candle. Remove bullish gaps when low falls below the gap bottom and bearish gaps when high rises above the gap top. Nearest gap direction is selected by the same closest-zone rule as order blocks.
Calculation: Weighted bias percentage = 100 × sum of enabled factor scores times their timeframe weights / (sum of active timeframe weights × number of enabled factors). Factor scores are +1 for bullish, −1 for bearish, and 0 for neutral. Factors are structure bias, nearest order-block direction, nearest gap direction, price relative to EMA, and swing position: below 0% = −1, above 100% = +1, below 30% = +1, above 70% = −1, otherwise 0. Analyze enabled timeframes that are equal to or higher than the chart timeframe; higher-timeframe calculations use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Previous day/week/month high/low = high/low of the last completed daily/weekly/monthly bar.
Long: Close > EMA OR close above latest confirmed swing high OR reclaimed swing low OR swing-range position < 50% OR both latest high and low comparisons are higher OR nearest unmitigated order block is bullish OR nearest unmitigated fair value gap is bullish OR weighted bias percentage > 20% OR a new swing high is above the prior swing high OR a new swing low is above the prior swing low OR close crosses above a previous day/week/month high OR a previous day/week/month low is swept and reclaimed.
Short: Close < EMA OR close below latest confirmed swing low OR reclaimed swing high OR swing-range position >= 50% OR both latest high and low comparisons are lower OR nearest unmitigated order block is bearish OR nearest unmitigated fair value gap is bearish OR weighted bias percentage < −20% OR a new swing high is below the prior swing high OR a new swing low is below the prior swing low OR close crosses below a previous day/week/month low OR a previous day/week/month high is swept and reclaimed.
