Calculation: H1 moving-average bias = using the visible moving averages ordered from shortest to longest lookback, bullish when the third-longest > longest, second-longest > third-longest, and close > second-longest; also bullish when those first two ordering conditions hold and close > third-shortest. Bearish conditions are the inverse. Set neutral when any pair of visible averages is closer than ATR × a spacing parameter.
Calculation: Ladder bias = compare rolling lowest lows at five lookbacks ordered shortest to longest (support levels) and rolling highest highs at the same lookbacks (resistance levels). Add one bullish point for each support level that is higher than the next longer-lookback support, and one bearish point for each resistance level that is lower than the next longer-lookback resistance. Bias is bullish when bullish points minus bearish points ≥ 1, bearish when ≤ −1, otherwise neutral; strong bias when the difference is ≥ 2 or ≤ −2.
Calculation: H1 composite bias = sum H1 moving-average bias and Ladder bias; set to neutral if H1 moving averages are too close. Classify as strong bullish at a sum ≥ 3, bullish at > 0, strong bearish at ≤ −3, bearish at < 0, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = for each of the two higher timeframes, bullish when chart close > EMA(shorter lookback) > EMA(longer lookback) > SMA(longest lookback), bearish when all inequalities are reversed, otherwise neutral. Sum the two timeframe biases; classify bullish when sum > 0 and bearish when sum < 0. Use higher-timeframe bars still forming.
Calculation: Engulfing bar = on the last completed chart bar, the candle range is at least ATR × a size parameter, body is at least half the candle range, and its body strictly engulfs the preceding candle's body; bullish if it closes above its open, bearish if it closes below its open. Use ATR from the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: Long-tail bar = on the last completed chart bar, the candle range is at least ATR × a size parameter, its body passes a configurable minimum body-to-range filter, and it closes in its direction. Bullish when the lower wick is at least 55% of range and the body is in the upper 45% of the range; bearish when the upper wick is at least 55% of range and the body is in the lower 45% of the range. Use ATR from the preceding chart bar.
Calculation: RSI retracement = RSI crosses back above its lower extreme after being below it, RSI is at or above its lower RSI moving-average threshold, and RSI crosses above its RSI moving average; bearish when RSI crosses back below its upper extreme after being above it, RSI is at or above its upper RSI moving-average threshold, and RSI crosses below its RSI moving average.
Long: H1 moving-average bias > 0 OR Ladder bias > 0 OR H1 composite bias > 0 OR higher-timeframe bias > 0 OR close > VWAP OR bullish engulfing bar OR bullish long-tail bar OR RSI crosses up through its upper breakout threshold OR bullish RSI retracement
Short: H1 moving-average bias < 0 OR Ladder bias < 0 OR H1 composite bias < 0 OR higher-timeframe bias < 0 OR close < VWAP OR bearish engulfing bar OR bearish long-tail bar OR RSI crosses down through its lower breakout threshold OR bearish RSI retracement

Short: higher-timeframe bias + H1 composite bias = −4
