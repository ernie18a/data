Calculation: VWAP deviation z-score = (close − VWAP − the longer-lookback moving average of close − VWAP) / the longer-lookback standard deviation of close − VWAP; use 0 when the standard deviation is 0
Long: within the configured session AND cooldown has elapsed since the last exit AND ATR is greater than its longer-lookback moving average times the configured volatility multiplier AND close is at or above the EMA of the higher timeframe's last completed bar AND VWAP deviation z-score is at or below the negative configured deviation threshold AND RSI is at or below the configured oversold threshold
Short: within the configured session AND cooldown has elapsed since the last exit AND ATR is greater than its longer-lookback moving average times the configured volatility multiplier AND close is at or below the EMA of the higher timeframe's last completed bar AND VWAP deviation z-score is at or above the configured deviation threshold plus the configured extra short deviation when extra short strictness is enabled AND RSI is at or above the configured overbought threshold AND shorts are enabled
Exit: Long, low is at or below entry price minus ATR times the configured stop multiplier OR high is at or above the configured profit limit (VWAP or, when VWAP targeting is enabled, the higher of VWAP and entry price plus risk distance times the configured target multiple) OR elapsed bars since entry reach the configured time limit when time stop is enabled
Exit: Short, high is at or above entry price plus ATR times the configured stop multiplier OR low is at or below the configured profit limit (VWAP or, when VWAP targeting is enabled, the lower of VWAP and entry price minus risk distance times the configured target multiple) OR elapsed bars since entry reach the configured time limit when time stop is enabled
