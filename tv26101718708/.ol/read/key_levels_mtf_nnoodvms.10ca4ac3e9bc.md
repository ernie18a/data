Calculation: Bias 0.618 = highest high over the lookback on a higher timeframe’s last completed bar minus 0.618 times the difference between that highest high and the lowest low over the same lookback
Calculation: ATR upper/lower bands = SMA of close ± ATR × multiplier, calculated on the selected timeframe’s most recently completed bar (chart timeframe by default)
Long: close crosses above Bias 0.618 OR close crosses below the ATR lower band
Short: close crosses below Bias 0.618 OR close crosses above the ATR upper band
