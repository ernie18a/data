Calculation: Standard pivot levels = using the previous completed higher-timeframe bar’s high, low, and close: PP = (high + low + close) / 3; R1 = 2 × PP − low; R2 = PP + (high − low); R3 = high + 2 × (PP − low); S1 = 2 × PP − high; S2 = PP − (high − low); S3 = low − 2 × (high − PP)
Calculation: Pivot zone boundaries = each pivot level ± zone width
Calculation: Zone width = current chart-timeframe ATR × multiplier
Long: close crosses above the upper boundary of the PP zone
Long: low tags the S1 or S2 zone AND close is above that zone AND broader trend is up
Long: low tags the S1 zone AND close is back inside or above the S1 zone
Short: close crosses below the lower boundary of the PP zone
Short: high tags the R1 or R2 zone AND close is below that zone AND broader trend is down
Short: high tags the R1 zone AND close is back inside or below the R1 zone
Exit: long, close is below the lower boundary of the PP zone
Exit: short, close is above the upper boundary of the PP zone
