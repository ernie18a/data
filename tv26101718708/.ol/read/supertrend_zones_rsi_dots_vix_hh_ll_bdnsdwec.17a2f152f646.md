Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the highs/lows over the configured left and right lookbacks; it becomes available after the right lookback completes
Calculation: RSI pivot spacing = number of bars between consecutive same-side RSI pivots, within the configured minimum and maximum range
Long: Supertrend direction is uptrend OR Supertrend direction changes from downtrend to uptrend OR Supertrend is in uptrend AND the selected signal (raw RSI or its selected moving average) crosses above 50 OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low AND the corresponding RSI pivot low is above the preceding RSI pivot low AND RSI pivot spacing is within range OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed pivot low exists and is not below the preceding confirmed pivot low (or no preceding pivot low exists)
Short: Supertrend direction is downtrend OR Supertrend direction changes from uptrend to downtrend OR Supertrend is in downtrend AND the selected signal (raw RSI or its selected moving average) crosses below 50 OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the preceding confirmed pivot high AND the corresponding RSI pivot high is below the preceding RSI pivot high AND RSI pivot spacing is within range OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the preceding confirmed pivot low OR a confirmed pivot high exists and is not above the preceding confirmed pivot high (or no preceding pivot high exists)
