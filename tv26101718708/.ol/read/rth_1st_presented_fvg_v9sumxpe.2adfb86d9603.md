Calculation: Three-candle FVG = bullish when the third bar’s low is above the first bar’s high by at least the minimum gap size; bearish when the third bar’s high is below the first bar’s low by at least the minimum gap size
Calculation: Swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater than/less than the highs/lows of the immediately preceding and following bars
Long: The first bullish three-candle FVG of the RTH session, with its middle bar at or after 09:31, OR a bullish three-candle FVG whose middle bar closes above the highest prior swing high (or whose high exceeds it when close-through is disabled) and meets the minimum middle-bar body size, OR the first bearish-reference-opposite bullish three-candle FVG after the session reference FVG; the reference is the displacement FVG if one occurs, otherwise the first RTH FVG
Short: The first bearish three-candle FVG of the RTH session, with its middle bar at or after 09:31, OR a bearish three-candle FVG whose middle bar closes below the lowest prior swing low (or whose low falls below it when close-through is disabled) and meets the minimum middle-bar body size, OR the first bullish-reference-opposite bearish three-candle FVG after the session reference FVG; the reference is the displacement FVG if one occurs, otherwise the first RTH FVG
