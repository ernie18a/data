Calculation: Pivot = (previous completed bar’s high + low + close) / 3, using the selected pivot timeframe (which may be higher, lower, or equal to the chart timeframe)
Calculation: Range = previous completed bar’s high − low on the selected pivot timeframe
Calculation: Fibonacci levels = Pivot, Pivot ± Range × 0.382, Pivot ± Range × 0.618, and Pivot ± Range × 1.000
Long: close > open AND high − close > 2 × |close − open| AND |close − any Fibonacci level| ≤ close × 0.002
Short: close < open AND open − low > 2 × |close − open| AND |close − any Fibonacci level| ≤ close × 0.002
