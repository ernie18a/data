Calculation: PDH/PDL = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Daily ATR = average daily high−low over a lookback; use completed higher-timeframe daily bars. Sweep and breakout levels = PDH +/− their respective ATR offsets above PDH, or PDL −/+ those offsets below PDL.
Calculation: Level strength score = weighted average, scaled 0–10, of capped prior-day volume versus its longer-term average, range versus ADR, close rejection from the level, distance from a round number, and isolation from recent daily highs/lows; calculate from completed higher-timeframe daily bars.
Long: (PDL strength score ≥ 7 AND low ≤ PDL sweep level AND close > PDL) OR (PDH strength score < 6.5 AND close > PDH breakout level AND volume > its longer-lookback average).
Short: (PDH strength score ≥ 7 AND high ≥ PDH sweep level AND close < PDH) OR (PDL strength score < 6.5 AND close < PDL breakout level AND volume > its longer-lookback average).
Exit: long, low ≤ PDL breakout level OR high ≥ Golden Pocket OR high ≥ PDH.
Exit: short, high ≥ PDH breakout level OR low ≤ Golden Pocket OR low ≤ PDL.
