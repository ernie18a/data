Calculation: Volume confirmation = a volume bar within the recent confirmation lookback exceeds its volume simple moving average times a multiplier, OR current volume exceeds its volume simple moving average AND the shorter-lookback volume simple moving average exceeds the longer-lookback volume simple moving average.
Calculation: Consolidating = the consecutive count of bars whose highest-high to lowest-low range over the consolidation lookback, as a percentage of the lowest low, is at most a threshold, is at least a minimum count; reset the count to zero when the range exceeds the threshold.
Calculation: Strong-buy condition = (RSI is within the good zone OR RSI is above 50 and no higher than the good-zone maximum) AND RSI is higher than its values on each of the prior two bars AND Volume confirmation AND Consolidating AND close is above the midpoint of the consolidation range AND close is no more than a threshold above its top AND close is above the shorter-lookback EMA AND the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA AND the reference market's shorter-lookback EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA AND its close is above its long-term EMA.
Calculation: Early-setup condition = (RSI is within the good zone OR RSI is at least the oversold level and below 55) AND Volume confirmation AND Consolidating AND (the stock's shorter-lookback EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA and close is above its long-term EMA OR close is above its shorter-lookback EMA and that EMA is above its longer-lookback EMA) AND NOT Strong-buy condition.
Calculation: Watch condition = Consolidating AND (RSI is within the good zone OR RSI is above 50 and no higher than the good-zone maximum) AND RSI is higher than its values on each of the prior two bars AND close is above the shorter-lookback EMA AND that EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA AND NOT Strong-buy condition AND NOT Early-setup condition.
Long: Strong-buy condition OR Early-setup condition OR Watch condition
