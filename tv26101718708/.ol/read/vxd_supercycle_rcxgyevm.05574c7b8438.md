Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = most recent swing high/low confirmed using equal left and right lookbacks
Calculation: Bull zone = 2-bar EMA of close > trend line, Supertrend is bullish, and close > smoothed linear regression of close; bullish body trend = EMA of (open + prior close)/2 is rising
Calculation: Bear zone = 2-bar EMA of close < trend line, Supertrend is bearish, and close < smoothed linear regression of close; bearish body trend = EMA of (open + prior close)/2 is falling
Calculation: Bull zone B = 2-bar EMA of close > trend line, Supertrend is bullish, regression line is rising versus 3 bars ago, and bullish body trend; bear zone B = corresponding bearish comparisons
Calculation: Bullish PPDD circle = bullish engulfing candle and current/prior candle low sweeps below the latest or previous confirmed pivot low, then close > that pivot low; bearish PPDD circle = bearish engulfing candle and current/prior candle high sweeps above the latest or previous confirmed pivot high, then close < that pivot high
Calculation: Bullish PPDD cross = prior bearish candle followed by bullish candle closing above prior open, with the bullish PPDD pivot sweep-and-close condition, and no bullish PPDD circle; bearish PPDD cross = prior bullish candle followed by bearish candle closing below prior open, with the bearish PPDD pivot sweep-and-close condition, and no bearish PPDD circle
Long: Bullish PPDD circle OR bullish PPDD cross
Short: Bearish PPDD circle OR bearish PPDD cross
Long: Bullish action state changes AND long entries enabled AND (no open trade OR open trade is short) AND prior one-trade flag is not long AND time is within the configured range AND ((filter enabled AND (RSI-MA crosses above 50 AND RSI > RSI-MA AND RSI condition enabled AND close > confirmed pivot high AND bull zone B OR close > Supertrend AND close > confirmed pivot high AND bull zone B)) OR (filter disabled AND (RSI-MA crosses above 50 AND RSI > RSI-MA AND RSI condition enabled AND close > confirmed pivot high AND bull zone AND time is in range OR close > Supertrend AND close > confirmed pivot high AND bull zone AND time is in range)))
Short: Bearish action state changes AND short entries enabled AND (no open trade OR open trade is long) AND prior one-trade flag is not short AND time is within the configured range AND ((filter enabled AND (RSI-MA crosses below 50 AND RSI < RSI-MA AND RSI condition enabled AND close < confirmed pivot low AND bear zone B OR close < Supertrend AND close < confirmed pivot low AND bear zone B)) OR (filter disabled AND (RSI-MA crosses below 50 AND RSI < RSI-MA AND RSI condition enabled AND close < confirmed pivot low AND bear zone AND time is in range OR close < Supertrend AND close < confirmed pivot low AND bear zone AND time is in range)))
Exit: Long, 2-bar EMA of close < trend line AND Supertrend is bearish AND close < confirmed pivot low AND close < smoothed linear regression of close
Exit: Short, 2-bar EMA of close > trend line AND Supertrend is bullish AND close > confirmed pivot high AND close > smoothed linear regression of close
Exit: Long, stop order at the lowest confirmed pivot low over a lookback when the bar low < entry price; otherwise take-profit limit at entry price + (entry price - that pivot low) × risk/reward when bar low > entry price
Exit: Short, stop order at the highest confirmed pivot high over a lookback when the bar high > entry price; otherwise take-profit limit at entry price - (that pivot high - entry price) × risk/reward when bar high < entry price
