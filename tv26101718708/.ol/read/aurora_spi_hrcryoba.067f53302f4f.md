Calculation: Price-row volume = allocate each analysis candle’s volume across equal rows spanning the session range, in proportion to the candle’s high-low overlap with each row; use lower-timeframe candles when enabled and available, otherwise chart candles. Decisions use completed analysis candles.
Calculation: Estimated buy share = estimated buy volume / row volume. Estimate each candle’s buy fraction from the normal CDF of its close-to-previous-close change divided by the standard deviation of preceding analysis-candle changes, clamped to the configured maximum; use a neutral fraction during warmup. If configured, use candle direction or the latest nonzero intrabar direction instead.
Long: For a price row, current-window buy share ≥ the dominance threshold AND immediately preceding, equal-length window buy share ≤ 1 − the threshold AND each of the latest confirmation analysis candles has buy share ≥ the threshold in that row AND both windows’ row volumes ≥ the configured minimum share of the strongest current-window row volume.
Short: For a price row, current-window buy share ≤ 1 − the dominance threshold AND immediately preceding, equal-length window buy share ≥ the threshold AND each of the latest confirmation analysis candles has buy share ≤ 1 − the threshold in that row AND both windows’ row volumes ≥ the configured minimum share of the strongest current-window row volume.
