Calculation: Pivot levels = use the selected pivot method on the previous completed higher-timeframe bar’s open, high, low, and close; derive R1–R4 and S1–S4 by that method’s formulas, with P as its pivot.
Calculation: Zones = each pivot level expanded by the selected width above and below; width is a percentage of price, an ATR multiple, or a fixed distance. Breakout threshold = a percentage of zone width.
Calculation: Zone state = locate the close in the gap between adjacent zones, excluding prices beyond R4 or S4. Compare the prior close with the relevant adjacent zone, expanded by the breakout threshold: a touch or retreat from the upper zone allows short; a touch or retreat from the lower zone allows long; a transition upward from the upper zone allows long, and a transition downward from the lower zone allows short. Require the relevant zone to be enabled; update the gap state when price transitions.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current candle bullish and at least one of the prior two candles bearish; with body-only mode, current open is no higher than the prior close plus the allowed gap and current close exceeds the prior candle’s open if bearish, otherwise its high; with full-range mode, current low is below prior low and current high above prior high.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current candle bearish and at least one of the prior two candles bullish; with body-only mode, current open is no lower than the prior close minus the allowed gap and current close is below the prior candle’s open if bullish, otherwise its low; with full-range mode, current low is below prior low and current high above prior high.
Calculation: Valid engulfing = engulfing candle body size is within the selected minimum and maximum range, defined by percentage, ATR multiple, or fixed distance.
Calculation: Bullish pin-and-engulf = prior candle has a lower wick longer than its upper wick, with body no more than the selected fraction of range, longer wick at least the selected fraction of range and at least the selected multiple of body; its low is below the lowest low of the earlier lookback bars; current candle is bullish, closes above prior high, and its body size is within the selected engulfing range.
Calculation: Bearish pin-and-engulf = prior candle has an upper wick at least as long as its lower wick, with body no more than the selected fraction of range, longer wick at least the selected fraction of range and at least the selected multiple of body; its high is above the highest high of the earlier lookback bars; current candle is bearish, closes below prior low, and its body size is within the selected engulfing range.
Calculation: Current-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of close. Higher-timeframe EMA = exponential moving average of close on the selected higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close. Bollinger width = (upper band − lower band) / band basis, using a simple moving average and standard deviation.
Long: confirmed bar AND (valid bullish engulfing OR bullish pin-and-engulf) AND zone state allows long AND current open is inside a relevant zone or crosses it AND (close > current-timeframe EMA OR bullish pin-and-engulf) AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI <= overbought limit AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR Bollinger width >= threshold) AND outside enabled no-trade window AND session trade limit not reached AND entry spacing condition met AND (flat OR pyramiding enabled with an existing long position and remaining position slot).
Short: confirmed bar AND (valid bearish engulfing OR bearish pin-and-engulf) AND zone state allows short AND current open is inside a relevant zone or crosses it AND (close < current-timeframe EMA OR bearish pin-and-engulf) AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI >= oversold limit AND (Bollinger filter disabled OR Bollinger width >= threshold) AND outside enabled no-trade window AND session trade limit not reached AND entry spacing condition met AND (flat OR pyramiding enabled with an existing short position and remaining position slot).
Exit: Long, low <= selected long stop price OR high >= long target price OR enabled session-close time condition; after enabled breakeven trigger, low <= entry price also exits.
Exit: Short, high >= selected short stop price OR low <= short target price OR enabled session-close time condition; after enabled breakeven trigger, high >= entry price also exits.
Exit: Long, high >= partial-profit target price (when partial take-profit is enabled).
Exit: Short, low <= partial-profit target price (when partial take-profit is enabled).
