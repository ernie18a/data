Calculation: Heiken-Ashi body ratio = absolute difference between Heiken-Ashi close and open divided by Heiken-Ashi range, multiplied by 100; use 0 when range is zero
Calculation: Heiken-Ashi lower-wick ratio = lower wick length divided by Heiken-Ashi range, multiplied by 100; use 0 when range is zero
Calculation: Heiken-Ashi upper-wick ratio = upper wick length divided by Heiken-Ashi range, multiplied by 100; use 0 when range is zero
Calculation: ATR wave direction = update the wave close to the selected upper endpoint when it exceeds the prior wave close by more than ATR, or to the selected lower endpoint when it falls below the prior wave close by more than ATR; otherwise carry the prior wave close. Direction is up if the updated close is higher than the prior wave close, down if lower, and otherwise carries the prior direction.
Calculation: Trend wave direction = update the one-bar close-movement trend when its nonzero direction differs from the prior one-bar movement; otherwise carry the prior trend. Change the wave direction to that trend when it differs from the current wave and close is strictly rising or falling over the configured lookback; otherwise carry the prior wave direction.
Long: Heiken-Ashi candle is bullish, its body ratio is at least the configured minimum, its lower-wick ratio is at most the configured maximum, and its body size is at least the configured minimum; this is the first such candle since Heiken-Ashi changed from bearish to bullish and no such signal has occurred since that change OR Heiken-Ashi candle is bullish, ATR wave direction is up, trend wave direction is up, MACD histogram is nonnegative, and at least one of these conditions was false on the prior chart bar
Short: Heiken-Ashi candle is bearish, its body ratio is at least the configured minimum, its upper-wick ratio is at most the configured maximum, and its body size is at least the configured minimum; this is the first such candle since Heiken-Ashi changed from bullish to bearish and no such signal has occurred since that change OR Heiken-Ashi candle is bearish, ATR wave direction is down, trend wave direction is down, MACD histogram is negative, and at least one of these conditions was false on the prior chart bar
