Calculation: Smoothed RSI = 3-bar EMA of RSI(close, RSI lookback)
Calculation: Short and long volume averages = simple moving averages of volume, with the short lookback shorter than the long lookback
Long: (Volume component enabled AND Divergence component enabled AND ((volume-buy event AND a divergence-buy event occurred within the confirmation window) OR (divergence-buy event AND a volume-buy event occurred within the confirmation window)) OR (Volume component enabled AND Divergence component disabled AND volume-buy event) OR (Divergence component enabled AND Volume component disabled AND divergence-buy event), with at least the minimum overall spacing since the previous long signal
Short: (Volume component enabled AND Divergence component enabled AND ((volume-sell event AND a divergence-sell event occurred within the confirmation window) OR (divergence-sell event AND a volume-sell event occurred within the confirmation window)) OR (Volume component enabled AND Divergence component disabled AND volume-sell event) OR (Divergence component enabled AND Volume component disabled AND divergence-sell event), with at least the minimum overall spacing since the previous short signal
Calculation: Volume-buy event = volume exceeds the long volume average times the buy multiplier AND smoothed RSI is below the buy threshold AND (volume 1 bar ago OR volume 2 bars ago exceeds the current short volume average), with at least the minimum volume-buy spacing since the previous volume-buy event
Calculation: Volume-sell event = volume exceeds the long volume average times the sell multiplier AND smoothed RSI is above the sell threshold, with at least the minimum volume-sell spacing since the previous volume-sell event
Calculation: Divergence-buy event = smoothed RSI is below the buy threshold AND close is above the lowest close over the lookback AND close is below that lowest close times the buy proximity multiplier AND smoothed RSI is above the lowest smoothed RSI over the lookback AND smoothed RSI is above its value 1 bar ago, with at least the minimum divergence-buy spacing since the previous divergence-buy event
Calculation: Divergence-sell event = smoothed RSI is above the sell threshold AND close is below the highest close over the lookback AND close is above that highest close times the sell proximity multiplier AND smoothed RSI is below the highest smoothed RSI over the lookback, with at least the minimum divergence-sell spacing since the previous divergence-sell event
