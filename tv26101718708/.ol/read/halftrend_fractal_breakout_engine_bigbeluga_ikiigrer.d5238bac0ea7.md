Calculation: HalfTrend uptrend flag = Initialize trend=0, nextTrend=0, maxLow=low, minHigh=high, upperLine=0, lowerLine=0, and flag=false. Each bar, using the amplitude lookback, set highPrice/lowPrice to the high/low at the highest/lowest bar, and highSMA/lowSMA to the means of highs/lows. If nextTrend=1, maxLow=max(lowPrice,maxLow); when highSMA<maxLow and close<previous low, set trend=1, nextTrend=0, minHigh=highPrice. Otherwise, minHigh=min(highPrice,minHigh); when lowSMA>minHigh and close>previous high, set trend=0, nextTrend=1, maxLow=lowPrice. When trend=0, on a flip into 0 set upperLine to the prior lowerLine; otherwise upperLine=max(maxLow, prior upperLine). When trend=1, on a flip into 1 set lowerLine to the prior upperLine; otherwise lowerLine=min(minHigh, prior lowerLine). Baseline is upperLine when trend=0, else lowerLine. Bands are baseline ± (ATR(100)/2 × channel-deviation multiplier). Set flag=true when close crosses above the upper band; set flag=false when close crosses below the lower band; otherwise retain its prior value.
Calculation: Top/bottom fractal = a bar whose high is strictly greater than the highs of the two bars before and after it / whose low is strictly less than the lows of the two bars before and after it; the pivot level is its high / low, confirmed two bars after the pivot.
Calculation: Pending top/bottom fractal level = the latest top/bottom fractal confirmed while the HalfTrend uptrend flag is true/false; clear it on a flag change and after its breakout.
Long: HalfTrend uptrend flag is true AND a pending top fractal level exists AND close is greater than that level AND the bar is confirmed.
Short: HalfTrend uptrend flag is false AND a pending bottom fractal level exists AND close is less than that level AND the bar is confirmed.
