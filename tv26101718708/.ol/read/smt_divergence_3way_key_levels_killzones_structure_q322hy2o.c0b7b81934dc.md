Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme over the parameterized bars on both sides; available after the right-side bars complete. Internal pivots use shorter left/right lookbacks than external pivots.
Calculation: First bearish-run open = open of the first candle in a consecutive run of candles where close < open.
Calculation: First bullish-run open = open of the first candle in a consecutive run of candles where close > open.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a parameterized lookback.
Long: A confirmed chart pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low, while at least one configured comparison market's corresponding pivot low is not lower, OR price reaches or falls below a prior completed higher-timeframe low while at least one comparison market's low stays above its corresponding level, OR after a completed session price reaches or falls below that session's low while at least one comparison market's low stays above its session low.
Short: A confirmed chart pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high, while at least one configured comparison market's corresponding pivot high is not higher, OR price reaches or exceeds a prior completed higher-timeframe high while at least one comparison market's high stays below its corresponding level, OR after a completed session price reaches or exceeds that session's high while at least one comparison market's high stays below its session high.
Long: After a low liquidity sweep, a bearish candle run of at least the minimum length has at least the required number of consecutive closes above the first bearish-run open, the first such close occurs within the sweep window, the sweep occurs during or after that run, at least one confirming candle has body size >= the configured ATR multiple, and any configured session and cooldown restrictions pass; if configured, require a close above the run open by the ATR margin.
Short: After a high liquidity sweep, a bullish candle run of at least the minimum length has at least the required number of consecutive closes below the first bullish-run open, the first such close occurs within the sweep window, the sweep occurs during or after that run, at least one confirming candle has body size >= the configured ATR multiple, and any configured session and cooldown restrictions pass; if configured, require a close below the run open by the ATR margin.
Long: Price breaks above the latest confirmed swing high using the configured close-or-wick test, the break candle meets the configured ATR body-size filter, and the prior structure bias is bearish (bullish CHoCH) OR bullish (bullish BOS).
Short: Price breaks below the latest confirmed swing low using the configured close-or-wick test, the break candle meets the configured ATR body-size filter, and the prior structure bias is bullish (bearish CHoCH) OR bearish (bearish BOS).
