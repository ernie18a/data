Calculation: Current-timeframe uptrend = shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close) AND close > shorter-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: Current-timeframe downtrend = shorter-lookback EMA(close) < longer-lookback EMA(close) AND close < shorter-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe uptrend = on a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's close > shorter-lookback EMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe downtrend = on a higher timeframe, the last completed bar's close < shorter-lookback EMA(close) < longer-lookback EMA(close)
Calculation: Mean = session VWAP on intraday charts; otherwise EMA(close)
Calculation: Major swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low, requiring the same number of chart bars to its left and right
Calculation: Bullish structure direction = close breaks above the latest unbroken major swing high by an ATR-based buffer while the regime is directional, not range-bound, and not compressed
Calculation: Bearish structure direction = close breaks below the latest unbroken major swing low by an ATR-based buffer while the regime is directional, not range-bound, and not compressed
Calculation: Bull score = points for current-timeframe uptrend, higher-timeframe uptrend, bullish structure direction, RSI above its midpoint threshold, positive MACD histogram, close above Mean, bullish volume participation (or neutral volume points when volume is unusable), and ADX above its trend threshold with +DI > -DI
Calculation: Bear score = points for current-timeframe downtrend, higher-timeframe downtrend, bearish structure direction, RSI below its midpoint threshold, negative MACD histogram, close below Mean, bearish volume participation (or neutral volume points when volume is unusable), and ADX above its trend threshold with -DI > +DI
Calculation: Bull/bear score difference = Bull score - Bear score
Calculation: Range-bound = Bollinger bandwidth below its longer-lookback average times a compression threshold OR (ADX below its range threshold AND absolute EMA separation divided by ATR below a minimum)
Calculation: Compression = Bollinger bandwidth below its longer-lookback average times a compression threshold
Calculation: Directional regime = not range-bound AND (ADX above its trend threshold OR EMA separation divided by ATR above its structure threshold OR ATR above its longer-lookback average times an expansion threshold)
Calculation: Confirmed bullish trend = completed chart bar AND directional regime AND not range-bound AND not compressed AND current-timeframe uptrend AND close above Mean AND required higher-timeframe alignment (if enabled) AND required bullish structure alignment (if enabled) AND required bullish ADX/DMI (if enabled) AND required bullish participation (if enabled) AND Bull score meets its threshold AND Bull/bear score difference meets its bullish advantage threshold
Calculation: Confirmed bearish trend = completed chart bar AND directional regime AND not range-bound AND not compressed AND current-timeframe downtrend AND close below Mean AND required higher-timeframe alignment (if enabled) AND required bearish structure alignment (if enabled) AND required bearish ADX/DMI (if enabled) AND required bearish participation (if enabled) AND Bear score meets its threshold AND Bull/bear score difference meets its bearish advantage threshold
Calculation: New bullish trend = confirmed bullish trend when neutral, or when confirmed bullish trend replaces an active bearish trend; otherwise a neutral state is restored after the active trend fails its health condition for the required consecutive completed bars. An active bullish trend is healthy when current-timeframe uptrend AND not range-bound AND not compressed AND Bull/bear score difference > 0.
Calculation: New bearish trend = confirmed bearish trend when neutral, or when confirmed bearish trend replaces an active bullish trend; otherwise a neutral state is restored after the active trend fails its health condition for the required consecutive completed bars. An active bearish trend is healthy when current-timeframe downtrend AND not range-bound AND not compressed AND Bull/bear score difference < 0.
