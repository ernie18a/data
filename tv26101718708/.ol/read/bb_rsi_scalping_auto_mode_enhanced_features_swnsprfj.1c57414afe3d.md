Calculation: Dynamic RSI oversold threshold = lowest RSI over a configurable lookback + configurable buffer; dynamic RSI overbought threshold = highest RSI over the same lookback − the buffer.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed lower price pivot low with a higher RSI at that pivot than the preceding price pivot low, within a configurable maximum bar gap; bearish divergence = a confirmed higher price pivot high with a lower RSI at that pivot than the preceding price pivot high, within the same maximum gap. Confirm pivots only after the configured number of bars to their right has completed.
Calculation: Recent bullish MACD inflection = MACD histogram turns upward from a lower prior value within the configured confirmation window; recent bearish MACD inflection = it turns downward from a higher prior value within that window.
Calculation: Recent volume confirmation = volume exceeds its moving average times a configurable multiplier (and, when enabled, exceeds prior-bar volume) within the configured confirmation window.
Long: No open position AND session filter is disabled or the current bar is in the configured session AND (reversal entry: in Conservative mode, low < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold threshold AND recent bullish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close > prior close, OR bullish divergence with the pivot low below the lower Bollinger Band and pivot RSI <= the dynamic oversold threshold plus recent bullish MACD inflection and recent volume confirmation; in Balanced mode, low is within a configurable fraction of Bollinger Band width from the lower band AND RSI <= dynamic oversold threshold + configurable near-entry buffer AND close > open; in Aggressive mode, the same band and RSI conditions without the candle condition) OR continuation entry: continuation mode enabled AND low <= Bollinger basis AND close > basis AND close > open AND RSI > its midpoint AND MACD histogram > prior histogram AND MACD line >= signal line AND recent volume confirmation). Auto mode selects Aggressive when Bollinger Band width and ATR are both above their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its moving average times the auto high-volume multiplier; selects Conservative when band width is not above its moving average and volume is not above that auto threshold; otherwise selects Balanced. A manually selected entry mode overrides Auto.
Long: No open position AND low is within a configurable fraction of Bollinger Band width from the lower band AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold threshold + configurable near-entry buffer (near-long marking; subject to its configured repeat cooldown).
Long: No open position AND continuation mode and continuation warnings are enabled AND the continuation long score is at least its configured threshold AND the full continuation long entry condition is false (continuation warning marking; subject to its configured repeat cooldown). The score counts one point each for low <= basis, close > basis, close > open, RSI above its midpoint, histogram > prior histogram, MACD line >= signal line, and recent volume confirmation.
Short: No open position AND session filter is disabled or the current bar is in the configured session AND (reversal entry: in Conservative mode, high > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought threshold AND recent bearish MACD inflection AND recent volume confirmation AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close < prior close, OR bearish divergence with the pivot high above the upper Bollinger Band and pivot RSI >= the dynamic overbought threshold plus recent bearish MACD inflection and recent volume confirmation; in Balanced mode, high is within a configurable fraction of Bollinger Band width from the upper band AND RSI >= dynamic overbought threshold − configurable near-entry buffer AND close < open; in Aggressive mode, the same band and RSI conditions without the candle condition) OR continuation entry: continuation mode enabled AND high >= Bollinger basis AND close < basis AND close < open AND RSI < its midpoint AND MACD histogram < prior histogram AND MACD line <= signal line AND recent volume confirmation). Auto mode selects Aggressive when Bollinger Band width and ATR are both above their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its moving average times the auto high-volume multiplier; selects Conservative when band width is not above its moving average and volume is not above that auto threshold; otherwise selects Balanced. A manually selected entry mode overrides Auto.
Short: No open position AND high is within a configurable fraction of Bollinger Band width from the upper band AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought threshold − configurable near-entry buffer (near-short marking; subject to its configured repeat cooldown).
Short: No open position AND continuation mode and continuation warnings are enabled AND the continuation short score is at least its configured threshold AND the full continuation short entry condition is false (continuation warning marking; subject to its configured repeat cooldown). The score counts one point each for high >= basis, close < basis, close < open, RSI below its midpoint, histogram < prior histogram, MACD line <= signal line, and recent volume confirmation.
Exit: Long position, if its type is Reversal and the selected reversal exit mode condition holds (Momentum Reversal: enabled bearish histogram inflection OR enabled bearish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross below its midpoint; First Retrace: close < open; Retrace + MACD Weakness: close < open AND histogram < prior histogram), OR its type is Continuation and the selected continuation exit mode condition holds (Momentum Reversal as above; MACD Cross Only: bearish MACD cross; Basis Break + MACD Weakness: close < Bollinger basis AND histogram < prior histogram), OR low <= initial invalidation level, OR dynamic exit is enabled and low <= its current level, OR enabled trailing stop has activated and low <= its current level, OR enabled break-even stop has activated and low <= entry price, OR enabled take-profit rules reach a full-exit condition, OR enabled maximum holding time has elapsed, OR the exit-soon marking condition holds (histogram < prior histogram).
Exit: Short position, if its type is Reversal and the selected reversal exit mode condition holds (Momentum Reversal: enabled bullish histogram inflection OR enabled bullish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross above its midpoint; First Retrace: close > open; Retrace + MACD Weakness: close > open AND histogram > prior histogram), OR its type is Continuation and the selected continuation exit mode condition holds (Momentum Reversal as above; MACD Cross Only: bullish MACD cross; Basis Break + MACD Weakness: close > Bollinger basis AND histogram > prior histogram), OR high >= initial invalidation level, OR dynamic exit is enabled and high >= its current level, OR enabled trailing stop has activated and high >= its current level, OR enabled break-even stop has activated and high >= entry price, OR enabled take-profit rules reach a full-exit condition, OR enabled maximum holding time has elapsed, OR the exit-soon marking condition holds (histogram > prior histogram).
