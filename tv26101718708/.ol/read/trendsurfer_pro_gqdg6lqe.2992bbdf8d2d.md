Calculation: Butterworth filter = the 2-pole Butterworth smoothing of close.
Calculation: VIDYA = close smoothed by an efficiency factor equal to the absolute 20-bar CMO, with smoothing length 10, then SMA-smoothed over 15 bars; its bands equal VIDYA because band distance is zero.
Calculation: Master Trend candle state = bullish when EMA(4) > EMA(5), RSI(14) > 50, and close > EMA(21), SMA(50), EMA(55), EMA(89), and EMA(750); bearish when all inequalities are reversed.
Calculation: Scaled volume = round(5 × volume / SMA(volume, 20)), clamped to 1–10.
Calculation: Impulse Z-score = (close change − its SMA over the longer lookback) / its standard deviation over that same lookback.
Calculation: Order block zone = for a bullish impulse, the high-low range of the lowest-low candle among the preceding shorter lookback bars; for a bearish impulse, the high-low range of the highest-high candle among those bars. The zone remains active until close passes beyond its far edge or its age exceeds the configured maximum.
Calculation: Session levels = high, low, and midpoint of each completed Tokyo, London, or New York session range.
