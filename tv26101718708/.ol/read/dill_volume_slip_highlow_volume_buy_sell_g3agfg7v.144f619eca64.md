Calculation: EMA trend stack = short-period EMA of close > medium-period EMA of close > long-period EMA of close (reverse the comparisons for a bearish stack)
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a lookback
Calculation: High volume = current volume > average volume × a threshold
Long: (chart is in intraday mode AND EMA trend stack is bullish AND close > VWAP AND high volume AND close > open AND RSI > a bullish threshold) OR (chart is in intraday mode AND EMA trend stack is bullish AND close > VWAP AND low <= short-period EMA of close AND close > short-period EMA of close AND volume > average volume AND RSI > a threshold) OR (chart is in swing mode AND EMA trend stack is bullish AND close > short-period EMA of close AND high volume AND close > open AND RSI > a threshold) OR (chart is in swing mode AND EMA trend stack is bullish AND low <= short-period EMA of close AND close > short-period EMA of close AND volume > average volume AND RSI > a threshold) OR (chart is in intraday mode AND close > highest high over a lookback of prior bars AND high volume AND close > VWAP) OR (high volume AND close > open)
Short: (chart is in intraday mode AND EMA trend stack is bearish AND close < VWAP AND high volume AND close < open AND RSI < a bearish threshold) OR (chart is in intraday mode AND EMA trend stack is bearish AND close < VWAP AND high >= short-period EMA of close AND close < short-period EMA of close AND volume > average volume AND RSI < a threshold) OR (chart is in swing mode AND EMA trend stack is bearish AND close < short-period EMA of close AND high volume AND close < open AND RSI < a threshold) OR (chart is in swing mode AND EMA trend stack is bearish AND high >= short-period EMA of close AND close < short-period EMA of close AND volume > average volume AND RSI < a threshold) OR (chart is in intraday mode AND close < lowest low over a lookback of prior bars AND high volume AND close < VWAP) OR (high volume AND close < open)
