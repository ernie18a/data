Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = on the last completed bars of each higher timeframe, EMA(9) > EMA(21) for bullish and EMA(9) < EMA(21) for bearish; the 4-hour trend veto is neutral when its EMA separation is below its threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive efficiency ratio = absolute close change over a lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback; classify trending when it exceeds an ATR-normalized threshold, then classify trend direction by current close versus the lookback close.
Calculation: Swing structure = compare the latest two confirmed swing highs and lows; bullish structure requires higher highs and higher lows, bearish structure requires lower highs and lower lows.
Calculation: Long signal score = weighted sum of the source's directional factors; short signal score is the corresponding bearish-weighted sum. Compare each score with its dynamic direction-veto threshold.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume. Liquidity passes when relative volume and ATR exceed their thresholds.
Calculation: Opening range = high and low accumulated during its build window, then fixed after that window. Pre-market range and session highs/lows are accumulated from their respective bars.
Calculation: Long stop and target = the entry-time long structural/ATR stop and adjusted profit target. Short stop and target = the entry-time short structural/ATR stop and adjusted profit target.
Long: (liquidity passes AND bullish trend (higher-timeframe EMA alignment, EMA(9) > EMA(21) > EMA(100), close > VWAP, ADX above threshold) AND bullish RSI (within its dynamic band and rising) AND two consecutive structure-break closes AND current close exceeds the prior swing high by its ATR buffer AND (confirmed opening-range retest OR failed-opening-range low rejection with a clean candle OR price is in the long OTE zone with sufficient swing range)) OR (bullish BOS with sufficient confluence AND long trend alignment AND enough optional gates pass) OR (bullish order-block displacement with enough confluence rules) OR (bullish EMA(21) pullback retest with sufficient score) OR (sweep of prior lows that closes back above them, passes wick/volume/range filters, and has bullish flow) OR (opening-drive window AND volume spike AND strong bullish candle AND sufficient confidence AND no HTF or pre-market veto); apply the path's session, position-side, direction-score, structural, trend-alignment, overextension, confidence, minimum-target-distance, and other path-specific vetoes.
Short: (liquidity passes AND bearish trend (higher-timeframe EMA alignment, EMA(9) < EMA(21) < EMA(100), close < VWAP, ADX above threshold) AND bearish RSI (within its dynamic band and falling) AND two consecutive structure-break closes AND current close is below the prior swing low AND (confirmed opening-range retest OR failed-opening-range high rejection with a clean candle OR price is in the short OTE zone with sufficient swing range)) OR (bearish BOS with sufficient confluence AND short trend alignment AND short volume/structural gates pass) OR (bearish order-block displacement with enough confluence rules) OR (bearish EMA(21) pullback retest with sufficient score) OR (sweep of prior highs that closes back below them, passes wick/volume/range filters, and has bearish flow) OR (opening-drive window AND volume spike AND strong bearish candle AND sufficient confidence AND no HTF or pre-market veto); apply the path's session, position-side, direction-score, structural, trend-alignment, overextension, confidence, minimum-target-distance, and other path-specific vetoes.
Long: Higher high and higher low confirmed by the latest swing pivots, subject to the configured swing-size and label-mode filters.
Short: Lower low and lower high confirmed by the latest swing pivots, subject to the configured swing-size and label-mode filters.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
