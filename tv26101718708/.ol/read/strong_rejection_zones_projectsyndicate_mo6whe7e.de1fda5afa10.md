Calculation: Bullish rejection zone = a support band centered on a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low, a chart-timeframe candle low whose lower wick is at least the configured share of its range, or the low of an up candle with volume at least the configured multiple of its chart-timeframe volume moving average; merge same-side detections within the configured ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Bearish rejection zone = a resistance band centered on a confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high, a chart-timeframe candle high whose upper wick is at least the configured share of its range, or the high of a down candle with volume at least the configured multiple of its chart-timeframe volume moving average; merge same-side detections within the configured ATR-based tolerance.
Calculation: Zone score = 10 times the weighted average of normalized confluence, detection-type diversity, average detection strength, and recency; normalize confluence by the configured full-credit detection count, diversity by the three possible detection types, strength from volume ratio, wick share, and range relative to ATR, and recency by elapsed bars since the last detection and the configured decay floor.
Long: A bullish rejection-zone detection occurs (confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low OR lower-wick share ≥ configured threshold OR up candle with volume ≥ configured multiple of its chart-timeframe volume moving average) OR price taps a bullish zone (low ≤ zone top) and closes above its top with score ≥ 7 OR price closes above a bearish zone's top on expanding volume and the zone is breached (score < 7, or the zone was strong before breach).
Short: A bearish rejection-zone detection occurs (confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high OR upper-wick share ≥ configured threshold OR down candle with volume ≥ configured multiple of its chart-timeframe volume moving average) OR price taps a bearish zone (high ≥ zone bottom) and closes below its bottom with score ≥ 7 OR price closes below a bullish zone's bottom on expanding volume and the zone is breached (score < 7, or the zone was strong before breach).
Exit: Long, close < bullish zone bottom (or low < bullish zone bottom when breach method is wick) OR close < top of a broken bearish zone.
Exit: Short, close > bearish zone top (or high > bearish zone top when breach method is wick) OR close > bottom of a broken bullish zone.
