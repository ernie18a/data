Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = prior Heikin-Ashi open and close average, or (open + close) / 2 on the first bar; Heikin-Ashi high/low = max/min of high/low and Heikin-Ashi open/close
Calculation: Rising(x,n) = x is greater than its value n bars ago; Falling(x,n) = x is less than its value n bars ago
Calculation: Donchian midpoint(n) = average of the highest high and lowest low over n bars
Calculation: Ichimoku leading spans = Donchian midpoint of the conversion and base lines, and Donchian midpoint of the longer span lookback, shifted forward by the displacement; use the chart-timeframe bars and the values plotted at the current bar
Calculation: KDJ = smoothed stochastic K and D; J = 3 × K − 2 × D
Calculation: M0 = Hull moving average of Heikin-Ashi close over the chop lookback
Calculation: Relative strength ratio = 100 − 100 / (1 + smoothed gains / smoothed losses), using price changes and the code's recursive smoothing
Calculation: Bollinger %B = (Heikin-Ashi close − lower Bollinger band) / (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band)
Calculation: Wave-trend lines = EMA-smoothed price deviation channel; WT1 is the smoothed channel index and WT2 is its short SMA
Calculation: STC = twice-smoothed stochastic normalization of the MACD line
Calculation: CCI = (Heikin-Ashi close − 20-period SMA) / (0.015 × 20-period mean absolute deviation)
