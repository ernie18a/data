Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Structure bias = direction of the latest confirmed close above the latest unbroken swing high or below the latest unbroken swing low; upward breaks set long bias and downward breaks set short bias. A break against or from neutral bias is CHoCH; a break with the bias is BOS.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = long when close is above its EMA and the EMA is rising; short when close is below its EMA and the EMA is falling; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bar on each of two higher timeframes, with the same EMA lookback.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier and the middle bar closes above that earlier high; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier and the middle bar closes below that earlier low. Keep only gaps whose size meets an ATR-based minimum; an FVG remains open until close passes its far boundary.
Calculation: FVG retest = the first later bar whose high/low range intersects an open FVG.
Calculation: Reversal confirmation = bullish engulfing OR bullish pin bar OR upward CHoCH/BOS OR (when enabled) the last lower-timeframe close exceeds the highest high of the preceding lower-timeframe lookback bars; bearish confirmation is the mirrored condition. Use lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including its latest available bar.
Calculation: Premium/discount = close above/below the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the range lookback; long alignment is below the midpoint and short alignment is above it.
Calculation: Setup score = min(100, structure points + FVG points + premium/discount points): structure points are awarded when the latest structure event is within the setup window and points in the bias direction; FVG points depend on distance to the nearest same-direction open FVG normalized by ATR (zero distance scores more, distance within the configured proximity threshold scores less, otherwise zero; at zero distance the points are higher for daily-FVG overlap, then premium/discount alignment, then neither); premium/discount points are awarded for directional alignment.
Calculation: Execution score = min(100, event-recency points + retest-proximity points), where each component awards more points for a more recent structure event or closer same-direction open FVG, respectively.
Calculation: Confluence score = rounded weighted sum of setup score and execution score.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, structure bias is long AND both higher-timeframe directions are long in strict mode (or either is long in soft mode) AND (a bullish FVG has been retested and bullish reversal confirmation occurs within the setup window OR a recent upward structure event is within the setup window, points in the bias direction, a bullish FVG is within the configured ATR proximity, and setup score meets its configured minimum) AND confluence score meets its configured threshold AND session filter passes when enabled AND the same direction cooldown has elapsed AND close is not inside an open bearish FVG.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, structure bias is short AND both higher-timeframe directions are short in strict mode (or either is short in soft mode) AND (a bearish FVG has been retested and bearish reversal confirmation occurs within the setup window OR a recent downward structure event is within the setup window, points in the bias direction, a bearish FVG is within the configured ATR proximity, and setup score meets its configured minimum) AND confluence score meets its configured threshold AND session filter passes when enabled AND the same direction cooldown has elapsed AND close is not inside an open bullish FVG.
Long: Confirmed close breaks above the latest unbroken swing high.
Short: Confirmed close breaks below the latest unbroken swing low.
