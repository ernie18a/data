Calculation: Volume DNA score = clamp to 0–100 the EMA-smoothed sum of four 0–25 scores: (1) OBV slope score = clamp((linear-regression slope of OBV / ATR) × 50 + 12.5) to 0–25; OBV adds volume when close rises, subtracts it when close falls, and is unchanged when close is unchanged. (2) CMF score = clamp((CMF + 0.5) × 25) to 0–25, where CMF is the volume-weighted average of ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), divided by average volume. (3) Relative volume score = clamp((volume / average volume − 0.5) × 12.5) to 0–25. (4) Volume-price alignment score: 25 if close rose and the short-term average volume rose over the lookback; 5 if exactly one rose; 15 if both fell; otherwise 12.5. Each score uses its own lookback; the alignment calculation compares price and short-term average volume over the same lookback.
