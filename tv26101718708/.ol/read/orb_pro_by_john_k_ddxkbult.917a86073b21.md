Calculation: Opening Range High/Low = highest high / lowest low during the opening-range formation session
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar's high/low is greater/less than those of the configured number of bars before and after it; confirmed only after those following bars complete
Calculation: Bullish/bearish body engulf = a bullish/bearish candle whose body covers the previous candle's body
Calculation: Candle body = absolute difference between close and open; body-to-range = candle body divided by high minus low
Long: During the signal window, after a confirmed swing low below the Opening Range Low is swept (low < swing low), a later bullish candle closes strictly inside the Opening Range and meets the configured minimum body-to-range filter; the sweep remains pending until confirmation or a close at/above the Opening Range High
Long: During the signal window, the low touches or falls below the Opening Range Low, the candle closes strictly inside the Opening Range, is bullish, has a larger body than the previous candle, and meets the configured minimum body-to-range filter; no earlier close was below the Opening Range Low and no external swing low below the range was swept that session
Long: During the signal window, after a close above the Opening Range High arms an upside breakout, a later candle touches within the configured retest tolerance of the Opening Range High, closes above it, is bullish, has a larger body than the previous candle, and meets the configured minimum body-to-range filter; either that candle is a bullish body engulf, or the immediately preceding candle was a valid retest candle that closed above the Opening Range High or within the configured BO close tolerance inside it, and was bearish
Long: During the signal window, the EMA is above the Opening Range High when the range forms if the outside-range filter is enabled; price closes above the Opening Range High and crosses above the EMA, then after that cross an EMA retest occurs and a bullish confirmation closes above both the EMA and Opening Range High, has a larger body than the previous candle, and meets the configured minimum body-to-range filter; either the confirmation candle touches the EMA within the configured retest tolerance and bullish-body-engulfs a bearish previous candle, or the immediately preceding candle touched the EMA within tolerance, closed at/above the EMA and above the Opening Range High, and was followed by a bullish candle
Short: During the signal window, after a confirmed swing high above the Opening Range High is swept (high > swing high), a later bearish candle closes strictly inside the Opening Range and meets the configured minimum body-to-range filter; the sweep remains pending until confirmation or a close at/below the Opening Range Low
