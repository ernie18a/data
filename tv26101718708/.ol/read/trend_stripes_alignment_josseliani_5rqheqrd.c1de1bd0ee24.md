Calculation: Current-timeframe stripe state = on each confirmed chart bar, set bullish when the faster MA is above the slower MA, their separation is at least a configurable fraction of ATR, and the slower MA is above its value several bars earlier; set bearish on the inverse comparisons; otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe stripe state = apply the same rule using the last completed higher-timeframe bar and a faster/shorter-lookback MA than the slower/longer-lookback MA; where the stripe uses one MA, set bullish when the last completed higher-timeframe close is above it and bearish when below it. Retain the prior state when neither direction qualifies.
Calculation: Entry EMA = EMA of chart-timeframe close.
Calculation: Bullish body fraction = share of the candle body above the Entry EMA; bearish body fraction = share below it. A zero-size body has fraction zero.
Calculation: Extension percentile = percentile rank over a recent lookback of the absolute distance between chart close and Reference MA, divided by chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Activity percentile = percentile rank over a recent lookback of chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Trend separation = absolute separation of the higher-timeframe stripe's MA pair divided by higher-timeframe ATR; when that stripe uses one MA, use the current-timeframe stripe's MA pair and ATR.
Calculation: Setup state = arm a first-signal setup when the higher-timeframe stripe is bullish and the current-timeframe stripe turns bullish, or bearish and the current-timeframe stripe turns bearish. After a higher-timeframe direction change, allow a setup only after the current-timeframe stripe aligns, turns opposite, then realigns; if it is already aligned at the higher-timeframe change, wait for the opposite turn and realignment. Clear the setup when stripe alignment is lost or its first signal occurs. A retest becomes ready after price moves at least a configurable ATR distance away from the Entry EMA in the aligned direction; it is consumed by a qualifying touch signal and can be rearmed after another move away.
Long: current-timeframe stripe state = bullish AND higher-timeframe stripe state = bullish AND (bullish alignment has just begun OR (bullish setup is armed AND close > open AND bullish body fraction >= configurable threshold AND long filters pass) OR (bullish retest is ready AND low <= Entry EMA AND close > open AND bullish body fraction >= configurable threshold AND long filters pass)); long filters pass when (close <= Reference MA OR extension percentile < configurable maximum) AND activity percentile >= configurable minimum AND trend separation >= configurable minimum.
Short: current-timeframe stripe state = bearish AND higher-timeframe stripe state = bearish AND (bearish alignment has just begun OR (bearish setup is armed AND close < open AND bearish body fraction >= configurable threshold AND short filters pass) OR (bearish retest is ready AND high >= Entry EMA AND close < open AND bearish body fraction >= configurable threshold AND short filters pass)); short filters pass when (close >= Reference MA OR extension percentile < configurable maximum) AND activity percentile >= configurable minimum AND trend separation >= configurable minimum.
