Calculation: Relative volume = current chart-bar volume divided by the average volume of the preceding chart bars.
Calculation: Estimated up volume and estimated down volume = sum volume from lower-timeframe bars whose close is respectively above or below their open; split doji volume equally between the two. If lower-timeframe bars are unavailable, use chart-bar volume, assigning it by chart-bar close versus open and splitting doji volume equally. Use lower-timeframe bars completed by the chart-bar close.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute difference between open and close divided by high minus low; lower-wick fraction = (minimum of open and close minus low) divided by high minus low; upper-wick fraction = (high minus maximum of open and close) divided by high minus low. When the range is zero, body fraction is 1 and both wick fractions are 0.
Long: One or two chart bars earlier, relative volume >= a threshold AND body fraction <= a threshold AND estimated down volume > estimated up volume AND lower-wick fraction >= a threshold AND lower-wick fraction >= upper-wick fraction multiplied by a dominance factor; on the confirmation bar, estimated up volume >= estimated down volume multiplied by a switch factor AND estimated up volume > 0 AND close > open AND close >= the average of the candidate bar's open and close. For a candidate two bars earlier, no one-bar-earlier candidate was confirmed on the preceding bar. Evaluate after the confirmation chart bar closes.
