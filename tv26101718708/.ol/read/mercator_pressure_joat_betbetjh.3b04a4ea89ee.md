Calculation: Composite pressure = clamp(22 × z-score of the EMA of weighted candle pressure, −100, 100), blended with smoothed volatility-channel position; apply a shorter EMA pre-smoothing and then a main EMA smoothing.
Calculation: Weighted candle pressure = weighted sum of (close−open)/EMA(abs(close−open)), (close−low)/(high−low)×2−1, ((high−low)−EMA(high−low))/EMA(high−low), and (volume−EMA(volume))/EMA(volume); volume component may be disabled. Each EMA and the z-score use configurable lookbacks.
Calculation: Smoothed volatility-channel position = EMA of the close’s position within an envelope centered on EMA((high+low)/2); envelope half-width is EMA(high−low) scaled by a configurable factor and adjusted by EMA(abs(close−open))/EMA(high−low). Clamp position to −100…100.
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of composite pressure, using a longer smoothing length than the composite pressure’s main EMA smoothing length.
Calculation: Bull regime = persistent state entered when composite pressure > upper threshold + hysteresis and exited to neutral when it falls below upper threshold − hysteresis; bear regime uses the corresponding lower threshold and reversed inequalities. Otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed price and composite-pressure pivot low, current pivot low price is lower and composite-pressure pivot value is higher than the prior pivot low; bearish divergence = at confirmed pivot highs, current pivot high price is higher and composite-pressure pivot value is lower than the prior pivot high. Require pivot spacing within configurable minimum and maximum bars. Hidden divergences reverse both comparisons.
Long: Bull regime begins OR composite pressure crosses above signal line OR bull regime and composite pressure > upper extreme threshold and absolute one-bar change in composite pressure > expansion threshold OR bullish regular or hidden divergence is confirmed.
Short: Bear regime begins OR composite pressure crosses below signal line OR bear regime and composite pressure < lower extreme threshold and absolute one-bar change in composite pressure > expansion threshold OR bearish regular or hidden divergence is confirmed.
