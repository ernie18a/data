Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset at each selected anchor period
Calculation: VWAP standard deviation bands = anchored VWAP ± volume-weighted standard deviation of typical price, multiplied by the selected band multiplier
Calculation: Band 1, Band 2, and Band 3 = standard deviation bands using successively wider multipliers
Long: Position is flat AND (Trend mode: selected trigger is close crossing above VWAP, Upper Band 1, or Upper Band 2 AND base filters pass AND (slope filter disabled OR VWAP percentage change over its lookback > minimum slope) AND selected confluence passes; OR Mean Reversion mode: selected trigger is close below the selected lower band, close crossing below it, or low below it with close above it AND base filters pass AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX < maximum) AND (ATR = 0 OR |close − VWAP| / ATR ≤ maximum deviation) AND selected confluence passes)
Short: Position is flat AND (Trend mode: selected trigger is close crossing below VWAP, Lower Band 1, or Lower Band 2 AND base filters pass AND (slope filter disabled OR VWAP percentage change over its lookback < negative minimum slope) AND selected confluence passes; OR Mean Reversion mode: selected trigger is close above the selected upper band, close crossing above it, or high above it with close below it AND base filters pass AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX < maximum) AND (ATR = 0 OR |close − VWAP| / ATR ≤ maximum deviation) AND selected confluence passes)
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ take-profit price; stop is entry-bar close minus the selected stop distance (ATR × selected multiple, or distance to Lower Band 1 in Trend mode / Lower Band 3 in Mean Reversion mode), raised to at least entry price if break-even is enabled and high reaches entry price + entry ATR × trigger multiple; take profit is VWAP or Upper Band 2 in Trend mode, VWAP in Mean Reversion mode, optionally fixed at entry
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ take-profit price; stop is entry-bar close plus the selected stop distance (ATR × selected multiple, or distance to Upper Band 1 in Trend mode / Upper Band 3 in Mean Reversion mode), lowered to at most entry price if break-even is enabled and low reaches entry price − entry ATR × trigger multiple; take profit is VWAP or Lower Band 2 in Trend mode, VWAP in Mean Reversion mode, optionally fixed at entry
