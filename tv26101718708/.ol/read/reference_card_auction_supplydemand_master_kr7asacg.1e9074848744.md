Calculation: Higher-timeframe curve = over the last curve lookback on a higher timeframe, divide the chart close’s distance above the range low by the range width; classify as high at or above 0.66, low at or below 0.33, otherwise middle. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA and slower EMA of close on a higher timeframe; bullish when the fast EMA is above the slow EMA and rising, bearish when below and falling, otherwise neutral. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Value bands = VWAP ± the standard deviation of close minus VWAP over the value lookback, multiplied by a width factor.
Calculation: Participation = volume is at least its moving average times a threshold.
Calculation: Demand and supply zones = identify a compact base of candles followed by a qualifying directional departure; demand bounds use the base’s lower price and upper body edge, and supply bounds use the base’s upper price and lower body edge. A zone becomes active on the bar after detection; invalidate demand when price consumes its lower portion or closes below it, and invalidate supply when price consumes its upper portion or closes above it. Track zone age and tests; select the best visible zone on each side, preferring untested, unbroken zones, then higher rank.
Calculation: Near demand = close is within an ATR-scaled distance of the active demand zone; near supply = close is within that distance of the active supply zone. A matching zone also qualifies when a bar sweeps beyond the zone and closes back across it.
Calculation: Bullish auction trigger = (low below the lower value band AND close above it AND close in the upper portion of the bar AND participation) OR (low below demand zone bottom AND close above demand zone top AND close in the upper portion of the bar AND participation) OR (close above the completed initial range high for the required consecutive bars AND participation).
Calculation: Bearish auction trigger = (high above the upper value band AND close below it AND close in the lower portion of the bar AND participation) OR (high above supply zone top AND close below supply zone bottom AND close in the lower portion of the bar AND participation) OR (close below the completed initial range low for the required consecutive bars AND participation).
Calculation: Long score = cap at 10 the sum of demand-zone strength (0–2), time at zone (0–1), freshness (2 if untested, 1 if tested once, otherwise 0), trend alignment (2 bullish, 1 neutral, otherwise 0), curve location (1 low, 0.5 middle, otherwise 0), reward-to-risk points (2 at 5R or more, 1 at the minimum R threshold, otherwise 0), and demand rejection points (clamp((rejection score − 0.55) × 0.75, 0, 0.35)).
Calculation: Short score = cap at 10 the sum of supply-zone strength (0–2), time at zone (0–1), freshness (2 if untested, 1 if tested once, otherwise 0), trend alignment (2 bearish, 1 neutral, otherwise 0), curve location (1 high, 0.5 middle, otherwise 0), reward-to-risk points (2 at 5R or more, 1 at the minimum R threshold, otherwise 0), and supply rejection points (clamp((rejection score − 0.55) × 0.75, 0, 0.35)).
Calculation: Long reward-to-risk = distance from close to the farther of (VWAP if above close, otherwise close plus minimum-R risk) and (supply-zone bottom if above close, otherwise higher-timeframe range high), divided by long risk; long risk is close minus demand-zone bottom buffered by ATR, or close minus the bar low buffered by ATR when no fresh demand zone exists.
Calculation: Short reward-to-risk = distance from close to the farther of (VWAP if below close, otherwise close minus minimum-R risk) and (demand-zone top if below close, otherwise lower-timeframe range low), divided by short risk; short risk is supply-zone top buffered by ATR minus close, or the bar high buffered by ATR minus close when no fresh supply zone exists.
Calculation: Middle chop = curve is middle AND close is between the value bands AND price is neither near demand nor near supply.
Long: long score ≥ required score AND (curve is not high AND (near demand OR failed lower-value rejection OR accepted range breakout)) AND at least one bullish auction trigger qualifies (a failed lower-value rejection requires near demand when the matching-zone filter is enabled) AND close > previous bar high when structure-break filter is enabled AND (near demand OR failed demand sweep when matching-zone filter is enabled) AND NOT middle chop AND long reward-to-risk ≥ minimum R threshold AND NOT (bearish higher-timeframe trend when trend alignment is enabled) AND NOT (opposing supply is within the ATR distance) AND bar range ≤ ATR × maximum chase factor AND candle is confirmed when closed-candle filter is enabled.
Short: short score ≥ required score AND (curve is not low AND (near supply OR failed upper-value rejection OR accepted range breakdown)) AND at least one bearish auction trigger qualifies (a failed upper-value rejection requires near supply when the matching-zone filter is enabled) AND close < previous bar low when structure-break filter is enabled AND (near supply OR failed supply sweep when matching-zone filter is enabled) AND NOT middle chop AND short reward-to-risk ≥ minimum R threshold AND NOT (bullish higher-timeframe trend when trend alignment is enabled) AND NOT (opposing demand is within the ATR distance) AND bar range ≤ ATR × maximum chase factor AND candle is confirmed when closed-candle filter is enabled.
