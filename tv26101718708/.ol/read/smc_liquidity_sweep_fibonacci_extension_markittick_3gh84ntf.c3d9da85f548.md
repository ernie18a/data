Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is strictly greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on each side; it becomes confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Active structural pivot = the latest confirmed pivot high or low not yet crossed by a qualifying structural break.
Calculation: Active bullish sweep = the prior chart bar’s low fell below the active pivot low while its close remained at or above that pivot low; it remains active until a bullish break resets it. Active bearish sweep = the prior chart bar’s high rose above the active pivot high while its close remained at or below that pivot high; it remains active until a bearish break resets it.
Calculation: HTF bias = on a higher timeframe, compare the last completed bar’s close with the simple moving average of its closes; bullish when close is above the average, bearish when below.
Calculation: Structural range midpoint = the midpoint of the range set by the most recent bullish or bearish structural break. A bullish break sets the range low to the recorded sweep low when the sweep filter is enabled, otherwise the active pivot low, and the range high to the broken pivot high. A bearish break sets the range high to the recorded sweep high when the sweep filter is enabled, otherwise the active pivot high, and the range low to the broken pivot low.
Calculation: ATR-based swing threshold = ATR times a volatility multiplier; alternatively, the threshold is close times a deviation percentage.
Calculation: Zigzag points = confirmed pivot highs and lows; replace the latest point when a more extreme pivot of the same type occurs, and append an opposite-type pivot only when its absolute price distance from the latest point meets or exceeds the swing threshold.
Long: Prior chart bar’s close is above the active pivot high (or its entire candle body is above it when full-body confirmation is enabled) AND, when enabled, an active bullish sweep exists AND, when enabled, higher-timeframe close is above its moving average AND, when enabled, prior chart bar’s close is below the structural range midpoint.
Short: Prior chart bar’s close is below the active pivot low (or its entire candle body is below it when full-body confirmation is enabled) AND, when enabled, an active bearish sweep exists AND, when enabled, higher-timeframe close is below its moving average AND, when enabled, prior chart bar’s close is above the structural range midpoint.
Long: The latest three zigzag points alternate low, high, low, and the third point’s price is above the first point’s price and below the second point’s price; the three points have not already generated a structure.
Short: The latest three zigzag points alternate high, low, high, and the third point’s price is below the first point’s price and above the second point’s price; the three points have not already generated a structure.
Exit: Long, prior chart bar’s close is below the first point’s price of an active bullish three-point structure.
Exit: Short, prior chart bar’s close is above the first point’s price of an active bearish three-point structure.
