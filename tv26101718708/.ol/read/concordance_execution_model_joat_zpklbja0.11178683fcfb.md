Calculation: ATR = Average True Range of the chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume.
Calculation: Delta pressure = EMA of (((close − low) / (high − low)) − ((high − close) / (high − low))) × volume, divided by EMA of volume.
Calculation: Volume pressure score = 50, adjusted upward or downward according to delta-pressure sign, whether OBV is above its moving average, whether close is above VWAP, and whether relative volume exceeds its gate; clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: ALMA direction = state that turns bullish when close exceeds the upper band and bearish when close falls below the lower band; the bands are ALMA of close plus or minus a configurable multiple of close’s standard deviation, with the upper band constrained not to fall and the lower band constrained not to rise unless the prior close crossed the prior band. Sum directions from four fixed aggregation timeframes, using each last completed bar, including timeframes lower and higher than the chart timeframe, to form the ALMA vote.
Calculation: Structure state = bullish after a confirmed close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high by an ATR buffer, bearish after a confirmed close falls below the latest confirmed pivot low by an ATR buffer; clear the broken pivot and retain the state until the opposite break.
Calculation: Kinetic trend = velocity divided by ATR from a second-order price estimator that predicts price, velocity, and acceleration, then corrects each estimate using the close-minus-predicted-price residual and gains derived from a smoothing parameter; increase correction gain when the absolute residual divided by ATR exceeds its configured threshold.
Calculation: Regression trend = linear regression of close over a lookback, with confidence equal to 1 minus the variance of close-minus-regression divided by close variance, clamped to 0–1; direction is the sign of the regression value’s change over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Long score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of configured weights for bullish structure, higher-timeframe bias, bullish ALMA vote, bullish kinetic trend, bullish regression trend, volume pressure score above its bullish threshold, and bullish EMA stack. Higher-timeframe bias compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with its last completed EMA. Bullish EMA stack means close > shorter EMA > longer EMA.
Calculation: Short score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of configured weights for bearish structure, higher-timeframe bias, bearish ALMA vote, bearish kinetic trend, bearish regression trend, volume pressure score below its bearish threshold, and bearish EMA stack. Bearish EMA stack means close < shorter EMA < longer EMA.
Long: current bar is confirmed AND cooldown bars since the last entry >= configured cooldown AND ATR / close > configured volatility threshold AND long score >= configured minimum AND delta pressure > configured bullish gate AND relative volume >= configured gate.
Short: current bar is confirmed AND cooldown bars since the last entry >= configured cooldown AND ATR / close > configured volatility threshold AND short score >= configured minimum AND delta pressure < negative configured bearish gate AND relative volume >= configured gate.
Exit: long, low <= max(entry price − ATR × configured stop multiple, ratcheting trailing stop based on the highest close since entry minus ATR × configured trail multiple) OR high >= entry price + ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry >= configured time limit OR structure state is bearish OR short score > long score + configured margin.
Exit: short, high >= min(entry price + ATR × configured stop multiple, ratcheting trailing stop based on the lowest close since entry plus ATR × configured trail multiple) OR low <= entry price − ATR × configured target multiple OR bars since entry >= configured time limit OR structure state is bullish OR long score > short score + configured margin.
