Calculation: Squeeze release = the first bar outside the Keltner Channel after the Bollinger Bands were fully inside it for at least the minimum consecutive number of bars.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = count of passes among RSI, optional MACD histogram confirmation, and optional volume confirmation; disabled optional confirmations count as passes. Volume passes when volume is unavailable, its moving average is zero, or volume is at least its moving average multiplied by a threshold.
Calculation: RSI hook = RSI crosses above the bullish level or is above it and has risen for two consecutive bars; bearish hook is the inverse.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe regime = on the higher timeframe's last completed bar, close is above its EMA and the EMA is above its value at a longer lookback for bullish regime; bearish regime is the inverse.
Long: Squeeze release AND close >= Keltner basis AND (session gate disabled OR UTC time is in an enabled session window) AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe regime is bullish) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (DI bias disabled OR +DI > −DI) AND RSI > bullish level AND (RSI hook disabled OR bullish RSI hook) AND (directional confluence score >= minimum) AND cooldown elapsed AND NOT short condition
Short: Squeeze release AND close <= Keltner basis AND (session gate disabled OR UTC time is in an enabled session window) AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe regime is bearish) AND (ADX gate disabled OR ADX >= minimum) AND (DI bias disabled OR −DI > +DI) AND RSI < bearish level AND (RSI hook disabled OR bearish RSI hook) AND (directional confluence score >= minimum) AND cooldown elapsed AND NOT long condition
