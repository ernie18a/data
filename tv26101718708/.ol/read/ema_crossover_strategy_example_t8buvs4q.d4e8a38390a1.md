Calculation: Short moving average = configured moving average of (high + low + close) / 3, using a shorter lookback than the long moving average
Calculation: Long moving average = configured moving average of (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Exit moving average = configured moving average of (high + low + close) / 3
Long: Short moving average crosses above long moving average AND bar time is within the configured date range
Exit: Long, close crosses below exit moving average AND bar time is within the configured date range OR bar time is outside the configured date range
