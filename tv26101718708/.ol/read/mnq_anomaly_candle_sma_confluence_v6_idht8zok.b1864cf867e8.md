Calculation: average candle size = simple moving average of true range over a lookback
Calculation: average volume = simple moving average of volume over the same lookback as average candle size
Calculation: short-, medium-, and long-term SMAs = simple moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks
Long: true range > average candle size × size multiplier AND volume > average volume AND close > open AND short-term SMA > medium-term SMA AND medium-term SMA > long-term SMA
Short: true range > average candle size × size multiplier AND volume > average volume AND close < open AND short-term SMA < medium-term SMA AND medium-term SMA < long-term SMA
