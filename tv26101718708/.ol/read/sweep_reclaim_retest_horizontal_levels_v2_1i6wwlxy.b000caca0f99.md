Calculation: Previous day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe).
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high/lowest low during the configured premarket session, fixed when that session ends.
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low with equal lookbacks on both sides; use only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Mid-range band = configured percentage of the previous day's range, centered on its midpoint.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on the configured higher timeframe; compare with that timeframe's bar still forming.
Long: During a configured entry session, low < a support level − sweep buffer and close > that level + sweep buffer, where the support level is chosen by priority: previous day low, premarket low, then swing low. Mark a bullish sweep.
Short: During a configured entry session, high > a resistance level + sweep buffer and close < that level − sweep buffer, where the resistance level is chosen by priority: previous day high, premarket high, then swing high. Mark a bearish sweep.
Long: A bullish sweep has armed a pending level; within the configured bar limit, before close invalidates the setup, and on a later bar during an entry session: low <= pending level + retest tolerance AND close > pending level AND (directional body filter disabled OR close > open) AND (prior-bar break filter disabled OR high > prior bar high) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR pending level is outside the mid-range band) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA).
Short: A bearish sweep has armed a pending level; within the configured bar limit, before close invalidates the setup, and on a later bar during an entry session: high >= pending level − retest tolerance AND close < pending level AND (directional body filter disabled OR close < open) AND (prior-bar break filter disabled OR low < prior bar low) AND (mid-range filter disabled OR pending level is outside the mid-range band) AND (higher-timeframe bias filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA).
