Calculation: Relative volume = current bar volume divided by the simple moving average of volume over a lookback
Calculation: Bullish close strength = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high equals low
Calculation: Bearish close strength = (high − close) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high equals low
Long: close > the highest high of the prior breakout-lookback bars AND close > open AND bullish close strength ≥ the required close strength AND relative volume ≥ the strong-volume multiplier
Short: close < the lowest low of the prior breakout-lookback bars AND close < open AND bearish close strength ≥ the required close strength AND relative volume ≥ the strong-volume multiplier
