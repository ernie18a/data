Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume when close >= open, otherwise negative volume.
Calculation: Divergence pressure = (z-score of EMA(cumulative volume delta, smoothing length) − z-score of EMA(close, smoothing length)) × (volume / SMA(volume, longer reference lookback)); each z-score uses its EMA series’ mean and standard deviation over the smoothing length.
Calculation: Effort = rounded 500-bar percentile rank of |bar delta| × (volume / SMA(volume, lookback)).
Calculation: Efficiency = rounded 100 × percentile rank of |close − prior close| / percentile rank of effort, positive when delta and close change have the same direction and negative otherwise; zero when effort percentile rank is zero.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe bin delta = net sum of lower-timeframe volume in each of 10 equal price bins across the chart bar’s high-low range, signed positive when the lower-timeframe close >= open and negative otherwise; use completed lower-timeframe bars within the completed chart bar. Largest-bin delta is the bin delta with greatest absolute value; its sign determines buy-side or sell-side.
Calculation: Climax = effort >= climax threshold AND divergence spike AND efficiency > climax efficiency threshold.
Calculation: Absorption = NOT climax AND effort >= absorption threshold AND efficiency <= absorption efficiency threshold AND chart-bar high-low range >= absorption range multiple × ATR(14).
Long: divergence pressure − SMA(divergence pressure, reference lookback) > spike threshold × stdev(divergence pressure, reference lookback) OR volume >= SMA(volume, lookback) × minimum-volume percentage AND largest-bin delta >= percentile(largest-bin absolute delta, 500 bars, 100 − top-percent threshold) AND percentile threshold > 0 OR (climax OR absorption) AND bar delta > 0
Short: SMA(divergence pressure, reference lookback) − divergence pressure > spike threshold × stdev(divergence pressure, reference lookback) OR volume >= SMA(volume, lookback) × minimum-volume percentage AND largest-bin delta <= −percentile(largest-bin absolute delta, 500 bars, 100 − top-percent threshold) AND percentile threshold > 0 OR (climax OR absorption) AND bar delta <= 0
