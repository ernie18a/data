Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low, using the configured number of chart bars on each side; available after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback of prior chart bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average volume over the configured lookback
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (current volume − moving average volume) / standard deviation of volume over the configured lookback
Calculation: Upper/lower wick fraction = upper/lower wick length / (high − low)
Calculation: Body fraction = abs(close − open) / (high − low)
Calculation: Bullish absorption zone = zone created by a bullish absorption; bottom = signal-bar low, top = min(bullish reference level, signal-bar low + ATR × configured zone thickness); remains active until a bar closes below its bottom
Calculation: Bearish absorption zone = zone created by a bearish absorption; top = signal-bar high, bottom = max(bearish reference level, signal-bar high − ATR × configured zone thickness); remains active until a bar closes above its top
Long: (confirmation enabled implies chart bar is closed) AND ((swing-break detection enabled AND a confirmed swing low exists AND low < confirmed swing low) OR (range-break detection enabled AND low < prior range low)) AND (close-back-inside enabled implies close > bullish reference level, which is the breached swing low when a swing break occurred, otherwise the prior range low) AND (bullish reference level − low) >= ATR × configured minimum failed-break distance AND lower wick fraction >= configured minimum rejection fraction AND relative volume >= configured minimum AND (volume Z-score filter disabled OR volume Z-score >= configured minimum) AND (effort-versus-result filter disabled OR body fraction <= configured maximum) AND (opposite-close filter disabled OR close > open), OR (zone generation and retest marking enabled AND an active bullish absorption zone exists AND low <= its top AND close > its bottom)
Short: (confirmation enabled implies chart bar is closed) AND ((swing-break detection enabled AND a confirmed swing high exists AND high > confirmed swing high) OR (range-break detection enabled AND high > prior range high)) AND (close-back-inside enabled implies close < bearish reference level, which is the breached swing high when a swing break occurred, otherwise the prior range high) AND (high − bearish reference level) >= ATR × configured minimum failed-break distance AND upper wick fraction >= configured minimum rejection fraction AND relative volume >= configured minimum AND (volume Z-score filter disabled OR volume Z-score >= configured minimum) AND (effort-versus-result filter disabled OR body fraction <= configured maximum) AND (opposite-close filter disabled OR close < open), OR (zone generation and retest marking enabled AND an active bearish absorption zone exists AND high >= its bottom AND close < its top)
