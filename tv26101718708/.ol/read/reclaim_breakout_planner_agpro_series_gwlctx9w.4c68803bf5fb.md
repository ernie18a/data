Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that is the highest/lowest across the configured bars on both sides; usable only after the right-side bars complete on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: ATR = Wilder average of true range on the chart timeframe; use the greater of ATR and minimum tick size.
Calculation: Fast/slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close on the chart timeframe, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Volume ratio = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a longer lookback; use 1 when volume is unavailable or the average is nonpositive.
Calculation: Acceptance score = clamp(0–100, 0.28 × reclaim-close score + 0.24 × retest-hold score + 0.18 × volume score + 0.18 × trend score + 0.12 × wick score). Reclaim-close score linearly scales the reclaim close distance in ATR from 0 to max(break threshold, reclaim threshold + 0.18), clamped to 0–100. Retest-hold score peaks at 100 when retest depth is 0.55 × pocket size in ATR, scales linearly from 0 at depth 0 to 100 at that peak, then falls linearly to 0 at 1.75 × pocket size, clamped to 0–100. Volume score linearly scales volume ratio from 0.75 to 1.55, clamped to 0–100; use 55 if volume is unavailable. Trend score is 100 when close and fast EMA are both on the trend side of slow EMA, 68 when close is on that side but fast EMA is not, and 32 otherwise. Wick score = clamp(0–100, 180 × rejection-wick share + 36 × close-location share + 20 × body share); for bullish reclaim use lower-wick share and (close−low)/range, and for bearish reclaim use upper-wick share and (high−close)/range. Range is max(high−low, minimum tick).
Calculation: Reclaim risk edge = for a long, the lower of reclaim-bar low and reclaimed level minus ATR × risk buffer; for a short, the higher of reclaim-bar high and reclaimed level plus ATR × risk buffer.
Calculation: Reclaim target = reclaimed level + position direction × max(distance from reclaimed level to risk edge, 0.20 × ATR) × target multiple.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses above the most recent usable pivot high plus ATR × minimum break distance; then, within the retest window and after the breakout bar, low reaches at least ATR × minimum level loss below that pivot high, and close is at least ATR × minimum reclaim distance above it; the watch has not expired or been invalidated by a close below the pivot high minus ATR × pocket size.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close crosses below the most recent usable pivot low minus ATR × minimum break distance; then, within the retest window and after the breakout bar, high reaches at least ATR × minimum level loss above that pivot low, and close is at least ATR × minimum reclaim distance below it; the watch has not expired or been invalidated by a close above the pivot low plus ATR × pocket size.
Exit: long, while an accepted reclaim plan is active and within its expiry window, a confirmed close is below its risk edge OR at or above its target.
Exit: short, while an accepted reclaim plan is active and within its expiry window, a confirmed close is above its risk edge OR at or below its target.
