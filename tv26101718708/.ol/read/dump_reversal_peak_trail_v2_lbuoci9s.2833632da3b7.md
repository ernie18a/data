Calculation: Dump change (%) = (selected end price / selected start price − 1) × 100
Calculation: N-day low = lowest low across the N most recent completed bars of the higher-timeframe daily series
Calculation: Resistance = highest high across the configured lookback of completed bars of the higher-timeframe daily series
Calculation: Resistance proximity = (resistance − close) / resistance × 100
Calculation: Hourly trend change = current close minus the close four hourly bars earlier, using the still-forming bar of the higher-timeframe hourly series
Calculation: Flat range (%) = (highest high − lowest low) / lowest low over the configured chart-bar lookback × 100
Calculation: Rebound (%) = (close − lowest low since the dump was armed) / lowest low since the dump was armed × 100
Calculation: Peak profit (%) = highest value since entry of (close − average entry price) / average entry price × 100
Long: A dump is armed when absolute dump change > anomaly threshold AND absolute dump change <= dump cap (or dump cap is zero) AND close < open AND low < N-day low; AND the selected entry mode is Classic AND close > armed dump high; OR the selected entry mode is TwoCloses AND the lowest close over the configured chart-bar lookback > armed dump high; OR the selected entry mode is BreakRetest AND close crosses above armed dump high and the pending buy limit at armed dump high plus the configured ATR offset is reached; OR the selected entry mode is ATRStop AND the pending buy stop at armed dump high plus the configured ATR buffer is reached; OR the selected entry mode is Auto AND either close crosses above armed dump high and the pending buy limit at armed dump high plus the configured ATR offset is reached, or the lowest close over the sensitivity-configured chart-bar lookback > armed dump high; OR the selected entry mode is Rebound AND close > open AND flat range <= maximum flat range AND rebound >= minimum rebound; AND no cooldown is active AND close is not within the configured percentage of resistance below it AND the BTC hourly trend change is positive (unless that filter is disabled) AND the position is flat or fewer than five trades are open. While waiting for reclaim, a lower low on a bar within the dump cap replaces the armed dump high with that bar's high. Pending entry orders expire after their configured number of chart bars; Auto places its ATR stop if its retest order expires.
Exit: Long, peak profit >= configured arming profit AND peak profit − current profit >= configured retrace OR stop loss is enabled AND low <= average entry price × (1 − configured stop loss percentage / 100) OR maximum loss duration is enabled AND close < average entry price AND elapsed time since entry >= configured maximum loss duration
