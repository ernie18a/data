Calculation: Normalized factor value = (factor − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation, using the same normalization lookback for all factors.
Calculation: Factor values = z-scored price minus session VWAP; z-scored high-low range signed by the direction of price change over a lookback; z-scored Kaufman efficiency ratio (net price change divided by summed absolute bar changes); z-scored chart close minus same-timeframe reference close; z-scored signed SAR distance from price in ATR units; z-scored RSI; z-scored linear-regression deviation in ATR units; z-scored KST (weighted sum of smoothed 10-, 15-, 20-, and 30-bar rates of change); and z-scored detrended cumulative volume delta, where each bar's signed volume is volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1), using 0.5 for the fraction when high equals low.
Calculation: Composite = arithmetic mean of the available, enabled factor values.
Calculation: Composite overbought/oversold threshold = configured multiplier × rolling standard deviation of the composite; use the configured fixed sigma threshold when adaptive scaling is disabled or unavailable.
Calculation: Composite extreme threshold = configured multiplier × rolling standard deviation of the composite; use the configured fixed extreme sigma threshold when adaptive scaling is disabled or unavailable.
Calculation: Minimum divergence gap = configured gap multiplier × rolling standard deviation of the composite.
Long: At least the required number of available, enabled factor values are ≤ −extreme sigma OR (at a confirmed price-pivot low, price is lower than at the preceding confirmed pivot low within the maximum pivot gap, the composite swing low is higher by at least the minimum divergence gap, and that swing low is at or below the negative composite overbought/oversold threshold) OR (at a confirmed price-pivot low, price is higher than at the preceding confirmed pivot low within the maximum pivot gap and the composite swing low is lower by at least the minimum divergence gap) OR (at a confirmed composite pivot low, the composite is at or below the negative composite extreme threshold).
Short: At least the required number of available, enabled factor values are ≥ extreme sigma OR (at a confirmed price-pivot high, price is higher than at the preceding confirmed pivot high within the maximum pivot gap, the composite swing high is lower by at least the minimum divergence gap, and that swing high is at or above the composite overbought/oversold threshold) OR (at a confirmed price-pivot high, price is lower than at the preceding confirmed pivot high within the maximum pivot gap and the composite swing high is higher by at least the minimum divergence gap) OR (at a confirmed composite pivot high, the composite is at or above the composite extreme threshold).
