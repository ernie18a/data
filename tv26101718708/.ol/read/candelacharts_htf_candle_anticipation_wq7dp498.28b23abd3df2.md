Calculation: Current HTF candle = aggregate chart bars in the active higher timeframe; use its still-forming OHLC. Previous HTF candle = immediately preceding completed candle in that same higher timeframe.
Calculation: Previous HTF midpoint = previous HTF low + (previous HTF high − previous HTF low) / 2.
Calculation: Previous HTF candle is bullish = its close > open, or its close = open and its open is closer to its high than to its low; otherwise it is bearish.
Calculation: High sweep = current HTF high > previous HTF high. Low sweep = current HTF low < previous HTF low. Close in previous range = current HTF close is between the previous HTF low and high, inclusive. Body in previous range = current HTF open and close are both between the previous HTF low and high, inclusive.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CHoCH = on the specified lower timeframe, close above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low formed during the current HTF candle, where that swing level is in the lower / upper half of the current HTF candle’s range. Swing highs/lows use equal left and right pivot lookbacks.
Long: chart timeframe < HTF timeframe AND (high sweep AND low sweep AND body in previous range AND current HTF close <= current HTF open OR low sweep AND close in previous range AND NOT (high sweep AND low sweep AND body in previous range) OR current HTF close > previous HTF high OR NOT high sweep AND NOT low sweep AND bullish CHoCH AND NOT bearish CHoCH OR no earlier condition applies AND (previous HTF candle bullish AND current HTF close >= previous HTF midpoint OR previous HTF candle bearish AND current HTF close > previous HTF midpoint))
Short: chart timeframe < HTF timeframe AND (high sweep AND low sweep AND body in previous range AND current HTF close > current HTF open OR high sweep AND close in previous range AND NOT (high sweep AND low sweep AND body in previous range) OR current HTF close < previous HTF low OR NOT high sweep AND NOT low sweep AND bearish CHoCH OR no earlier condition applies AND (previous HTF candle bullish AND current HTF close < previous HTF midpoint OR previous HTF candle bearish AND current HTF close <= previous HTF midpoint))
