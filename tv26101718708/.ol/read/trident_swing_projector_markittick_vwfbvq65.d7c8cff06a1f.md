Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is strictly above/below the highs/lows of the specified number of preceding and following chart bars; confirm only after the following bars have completed.
Calculation: Pullback depth = (B − C) / (B − A) × 100 for a bullish A-low, B-high, C-higher-low structure; (C − B) / (A − B) × 100 for a bearish A-high, B-low, C-lower-high structure.
Calculation: Projected leg = absolute A–B price distance, or C × (absolute A–B distance / A) when using percentage units; optionally multiply by the ratio of a selected moving average of recent absolute distances between the latest confirmed swing high and low to the current raw distance.
Calculation: Entry level = C plus one quarter of the projected leg for bullish setups, or minus one quarter for bearish setups. Targets = C plus one half, three quarters, or all of the projected leg for bullish setups; subtract those distances for bearish setups.
Calculation: Stop level = C minus a configurable buffer for bullish setups, or C plus a configurable buffer for bearish setups; the buffer may be zero, a tick amount, or a fraction of chart-timeframe ATR. After target 1, the managed stop may move to the entry level.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = compare the last completed higher-timeframe close with the preceding completed higher-timeframe close.
Long: A, B, C are consecutive confirmed chart-timeframe pivots forming a low, then high, then higher low AND A < B AND pullback depth is within the configured minimum and maximum AND, if the trend filter is enabled, B exceeds the preceding confirmed swing high AND, if the ADX filter is enabled, chart-timeframe DMI-derived ADX is at least its threshold AND, if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, higher-timeframe bias is up AND the setup is armed, not expired, and not invalidated by the prior completed chart-timeframe bar's low falling below C AND the prior completed chart-timeframe bar's close is at or above the entry level.
Short: A, B, C are consecutive confirmed chart-timeframe pivots forming a high, then low, then lower high AND A > B AND pullback depth is within the configured minimum and maximum AND, if the trend filter is enabled, B is below the preceding confirmed swing low AND, if the ADX filter is enabled, chart-timeframe DMI-derived ADX is at least its threshold AND, if higher-timeframe confirmation is enabled, higher-timeframe bias is down AND the setup is armed, not expired, and not invalidated by the prior completed chart-timeframe bar's high rising above C AND the prior completed chart-timeframe bar's close is at or below the entry level.
Exit: long, current chart-timeframe low <= managed stop OR current chart-timeframe high >= any of targets 1, 2, or 3
Exit: short, current chart-timeframe high >= managed stop OR current chart-timeframe low <= any of targets 1, 2, or 3
