Calculation: TPO value area = Divide the active profile high–low range into equal price rows; add one touch to each row crossed by each bar’s high–low. Starting at the row with the most touches, include adjacent rows with more touches (ties upward) until the configured share of all touches is reached; the outer row boundaries are VAL and VAH.
Calculation: Buffered VAH = TPO VAH + ATR × configured multiplier; Buffered VAL = TPO VAL − ATR × configured multiplier.
Calculation: Out-of-balance phase = Begin in balance with a profile anchored at its start that expands to include new highs and lows. Enter out of balance when close is above Buffered VAH or below Buffered VAL, and save those boundaries. While out of balance, return to balance if close is within the saved boundaries; also return to balance when the configured minimum breakout duration is reached and the recent-window TPO value-area spread is within its configured minimum and maximum percentages of the recent high–low range. Otherwise remain out of balance. On return to balance, anchor a new profile to the confirmed recent range.
Long: Higher-timeframe phase is out of balance AND chart close > that timeframe’s Buffered VAH.
Short: Higher-timeframe phase is out of balance AND chart close < that timeframe’s Buffered VAL.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe phase and buffered VA levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
