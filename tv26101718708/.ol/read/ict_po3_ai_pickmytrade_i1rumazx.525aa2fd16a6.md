Calculation: Compression score = weighted score of compression-window range relative to ATR, close dispersion relative to ATR, short-average drift relative to ATR, range compression versus a longer window, and largest true range relative to the window range.
Calculation: Accumulation score = weighted score of relative compression, range width versus ATR, minimum of defended upper and lower boundary touch counts, similarity of repeated upper and lower extremes, and accumulation duration.
Calculation: Liquidity-pool score = weighted score of the raided boundary’s proximity to prior-week, prior-day, Asia-session, or last confirmed swing liquidity; count of those levels clustered nearby; and defended boundary touches. Swing levels use only pivots confirmed after subsequent bars.
Calculation: Acceptance beyond level = weighted score of volume beyond the raided level relative to average volume (or fraction of excursion bars beyond it when volume is unavailable), consecutive closes beyond it, and total closes beyond it.
Calculation: Manipulation score = weighted score of sweep depth relative to locked ATR, inverse acceptance beyond level, reclaim close distance beyond the raided level relative to locked ATR, rejection-wick fraction, liquidity-pool score, and excursion duration.
Calculation: Distribution score = weighted score of close displacement from the raided level relative to locked ATR, presence and size of the first qualifying directional fair-value gap, close distance beyond the opposite accumulation boundary, largest expansion-leg body fraction, and elapsed bars since manipulation confirmation.
Calculation: Structural score = weighted geometric mean of accumulation, manipulation, and distribution scores, reduced for recent opposing raids; multiplied by a context factor from higher-timeframe trend and premium/discount, session fit when enabled, volatility/structure regime, target room, and position within the range-to-sweep leg.
Long: On confirmed bars, after a qualified accumulation range, its lower boundary is swept by at least the regime-based minimum penetration and passes the liquidity-pool and fresh-raid checks; price reclaims above that boundary before the manipulation timeout, with manipulation score above its floor, acceptance and sweep-depth below their veto limits; then a bullish displacement bar occurs, followed within the distribution window by a close above the accumulation range high; accumulation, manipulation, and distribution scores pass their floors, structural score meets the minimum, stop distance is within its risk limit, and adaptive gate does not block the signal when gate mode is enabled.
Short: On confirmed bars, after a qualified accumulation range, its upper boundary is swept by at least the regime-based minimum penetration and passes the liquidity-pool and fresh-raid checks; price reclaims below that boundary before the manipulation timeout, with manipulation score above its floor, acceptance and sweep-depth below their veto limits; then a bearish displacement bar occurs, followed within the distribution window by a close below the accumulation range low; accumulation, manipulation, and distribution scores pass their floors, structural score meets the minimum, stop distance is within its risk limit, and adaptive gate does not block the signal when gate mode is enabled.
