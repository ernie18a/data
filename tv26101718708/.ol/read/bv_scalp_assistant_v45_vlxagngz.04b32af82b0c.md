Calculation: EMA slope = chart-timeframe EMA minus its value three chart bars earlier; bullish if above an ATR-scaled positive threshold, bearish if below the corresponding negative threshold, otherwise flat.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = same-period EMA of closes on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Build-up score = weighted sum of: narrow recent high-low range; weighted overlap of adjacent recent bar ranges; enough recent bars with small ranges; EMA within the recent range plus an ATR margin; progressive compression or arch compression with declining average range (otherwise partial credit for rising lows, falling highs, arch compression, or range contraction); proximity to a barrier; and acceptable noise quality. Build-up is present when this score reaches its configured minimum. Progressive compression means successive thirds of the recent range are narrower; arch compression means the latest half-range is narrower than the earlier half-range. Noise quality is acceptable when wick-dominated or in-range large bars outnumber hard out-of-range large bars, or there are no hard bars.
Calculation: Barriers = the nearest levels above and below price among the two latest confirmed swing highs or lows, the highest high or lowest low over the configured lookback excluding the build-up bars, and (when enabled) adjacent configured round-number levels. A barrier is near when it is within the configured distance of the recent build-up high or low.
Calculation: Direction scores = weighted checklist scores. Long checklist: EMA slope bullish or flat; close at/above EMA or EMA near the build-up range; rising lows or arch compression; close in the upper part of the build-up range; summed lower wicks at least summed upper wicks; and (room to the nearest upper barrier or an upper barrier nearby) plus weak opposing sell pressure. Short checklist mirrors this: EMA slope bearish or flat; close at/below EMA or EMA near the range; falling highs or arch compression; close in the lower part; summed upper wicks at least summed lower wicks; and (room to the nearest lower barrier or a lower barrier nearby) plus weak opposing buy pressure. Subtract the configured penalty from each score when its direction is opposed by the higher-timeframe EMA relative to the chart EMA, if that filter is enabled; floor scores at zero. Weak opposing pressure means the average opposing candle body is no more than the configured fraction of average candle body.
Calculation: Range regime = recent range width is within its ATR-scaled limit, chart EMA displacement over the same lookback is within its ATR-scaled limit, and close/EMA crosses meet the configured minimum. Range position = close's position within that range. In a range, long is allowed only in the upper zone and short only in the lower zone.
Long: build-up is present AND long score reaches its configured minimum AND long score exceeds short score by the configured normal minimum gap (and by the larger configured range gap when in a range) AND EMA is near the build-up range or an upper barrier is near AND close position in the build-up range meets the configured long minimum AND upper-barrier room meets its minimum or an upper barrier is near AND close is within its ATR-scaled EMA distance limit AND recent rise is within its ATR-scaled limit AND no configured-lookback bullish power bar occurred AND range allows long AND, if enabled, current time is in an allowed session AND the configured long-signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: build-up is present AND short score reaches its configured minimum AND short score exceeds long score by the configured normal minimum gap (and by the larger configured range gap when in a range) AND EMA is near the build-up range or a lower barrier is near AND close position in the build-up range meets the configured short maximum AND lower-barrier room meets its minimum or a lower barrier is near AND close is within its ATR-scaled EMA distance limit AND recent drop is within its ATR-scaled limit AND no configured-lookback bearish power bar occurred AND range allows short AND, if enabled, current time is in an allowed session AND the configured short-signal cooldown has elapsed.
