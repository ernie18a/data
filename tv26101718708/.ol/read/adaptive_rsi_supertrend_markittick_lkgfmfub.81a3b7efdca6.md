Calculation: Adaptive RSI = let U be the highest close and L the lowest close over the lookback; let R=U−L and D=current close−previous close. Set X=R if U>previous U, −R if L<previous L, otherwise D. Adaptive RSI = 50 + 50×RMA(X, lookback)/RMA(|X|, lookback), or 50 when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Oscillator Supertrend = ATR is RMA of the absolute bar-to-bar change in Adaptive RSI. Form upper and lower bands as Adaptive RSI ± factor×ATR. Initialize the trend line to the upper band and direction bullish. Thereafter, direction is bullish when Adaptive RSI is above the previous trend line, bearish when below it, otherwise unchanged. In a bullish direction, the line is the greater of the lower band and previous line; in a bearish direction, it is the lesser of the upper band and previous line.
Calculation: Merged line = average of the RMA of Adaptive RSI and the Oscillator Supertrend line.
Long: Adaptive RSI crossed above the Merged line (current Adaptive RSI > current Merged line AND previous Adaptive RSI <= previous Merged line) AND (volume >= its SMA × volume multiplier if the volume filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe Adaptive RSI from the last completed higher-timeframe bar >= 50 if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled)
Short: Adaptive RSI crossed below the Merged line (current Adaptive RSI < current Merged line AND previous Adaptive RSI >= previous Merged line) AND (volume >= its SMA × volume multiplier if the volume filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe Adaptive RSI from the last completed higher-timeframe bar < 50 if the higher-timeframe filter is enabled)
