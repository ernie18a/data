Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over a longer lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; optionally smooth with an EMA.
Calculation: KAMA = recursively update the prior KAMA by the squared adaptive smoothing constant times the difference between close and prior KAMA; the constant interpolates between fast and slow smoothing constants using the efficiency ratio.
Calculation: Anchored KAMA = prior bar's KAMA.
Calculation: Dead-zone width = ATR × dead-zone multiplier × (2 − efficiency ratio).
Calculation: Confirmed bearish regime = close remains below anchored KAMA − dead-zone width for the required consecutive bars; confirmed bullish regime = close remains above anchored KAMA + dead-zone width for the required consecutive bars. Each confirmed regime replaces the other; otherwise retain the current regime.
Calculation: Buy band = blend of (anchored KAMA − dead-zone width × buy-band multiplier) and (anchored KAMA − ATR × learned buy multiplier), weighted by the learning weight. Learned buy multiplier = initial value until enough samples; thereafter, clamp the sensitivity-scaled average of the most recent normalized lower-wick penetrations below anchored KAMA to the configured minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Sell band = blend of (anchored KAMA + dead-zone width × sell-band multiplier) and (anchored KAMA + ATR × learned sell multiplier), weighted by the learning weight. Learned sell multiplier = initial value until enough samples; thereafter, clamp the sensitivity-scaled average of the most recent normalized upper-wick penetrations above anchored KAMA to the configured minimum and maximum.
Long: low <= buy band AND confirmed bearish regime AND bar is complete AND (no prior buy price in the current phase OR absolute difference between low and prior buy price >= ATR × buy separation) AND (no prior buy low in the current phase OR low < prior buy low)
Exit: long, high >= sell band AND confirmed bullish regime AND bar is complete AND (no prior sell OR no sell cooldown applies OR bars since prior sell >= base cooldown × [1 + min(maximum multiplier − 1, max(0, anchored-KAMA distance in ATR units) × cooldown scale)]) AND (no prior sell high in the current phase OR high > prior sell high)
