Calculation: Previous swing high/low = latest tracked confirmed pivot high/low, using a pivot lookback that decreases as ATR(14)/its 50-bar average increases and increases as that ratio decreases.
Calculation: Golden-pocket bounds = the two retracement prices at 0.500 and 0.618 of the distance between the latest tracked swing high and low, measured from the swing high in an upswing or from the swing low in a downswing.
Calculation: TP1 reward/risk ratio = absolute distance from close to the opposite swing boundary divided by absolute distance from close to the 0.786 retracement price.
Long: Current low < previous swing low AND current close > previous swing low AND ADX(14,14) >= its threshold AND current close > 200-period SMA
Short: Current high > previous swing high AND current close < previous swing high AND ADX(14,14) >= its threshold AND current close < 200-period SMA
Long: Upswing AND daily close > daily 200-period SMA AND 4-hour candle range overlaps the golden pocket AND a 4-hour golden-pocket overlap with volume > 1.5 × its 20-bar average, or a 4-hour sweep of either swing boundary, occurred within the current or previous chart bar AND TP1 reward/risk ratio >= 1.5 AND these conditions became true on the current chart bar
Short: Downswing AND daily close < daily 200-period SMA AND 4-hour candle range overlaps the golden pocket AND a 4-hour golden-pocket overlap with volume > 1.5 × its 20-bar average, or a 4-hour sweep of either swing boundary, occurred within the current or previous chart bar AND TP1 reward/risk ratio >= 1.5 AND these conditions became true on the current chart bar
