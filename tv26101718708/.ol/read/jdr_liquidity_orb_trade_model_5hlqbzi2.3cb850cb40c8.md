Calculation: Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe).
Calculation: Overnight high/low = highest/lowest price during the overnight session.
Calculation: Session opening-range high/low = highest/lowest price during the opening period of the regular session, locked when that period ends; prior-session opening range = the prior regular session's opening range.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR × body-size threshold AND absolute(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= candle-body threshold AND close > prior chart bar high.
Calculation: Bearish displacement = close < open AND absolute(close - open) >= ATR × body-size threshold AND absolute(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) >= candle-body threshold AND close < prior chart bar low.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = low > high two chart bars ago; bearish fair-value gap = high < low two chart bars ago.
Calculation: Long stop = low of the long signal bar; short stop = high of the short signal bar.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price - long stop) × risk/reward multiple; short target = entry price - (short stop - entry price) × risk/reward multiple.
Long: During the regular session, low < prior-day low OR low < overnight low OR (opening range locked AND low < current-session opening-range low) OR low < prior-session opening-range low; within the confirmation window after the latest such sweep, close > the selected swept level (if multiple levels are swept together, priority is prior-session opening range, current-session opening range, overnight, then prior-day); AND cooldown elapsed AND daily entry limit not reached AND (displacement filter disabled OR bullish displacement) AND (trend filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA, with fast EMA using the shorter lookback) AND (fair-value-gap filter disabled OR bullish fair-value gap).
Short: During the regular session, high > prior-day high OR high > overnight high OR (opening range locked AND high > current-session opening-range high) OR high > prior-session opening-range high; within the confirmation window after the latest such sweep, close < the selected swept level (if multiple levels are swept together, priority is prior-session opening range, current-session opening range, overnight, then prior-day); AND cooldown elapsed AND daily entry limit not reached AND (displacement filter disabled OR bearish displacement) AND (trend filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA, with fast EMA using the shorter lookback) AND (fair-value-gap filter disabled OR bearish fair-value gap).
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target.
