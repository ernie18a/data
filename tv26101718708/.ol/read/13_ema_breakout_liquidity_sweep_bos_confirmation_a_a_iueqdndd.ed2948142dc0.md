Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar, aggregated from chart bars; previous-session high/low = high/low of the last completed selected session; first-N session range = high/low of the first N chart bars in the current trade session, usable once those bars are complete.
Calculation: Sweep distance = greater of a configurable minimum tick distance and a configurable multiple of ATR; a high sweep occurs when high exceeds the selected liquidity high plus this distance and close returns below that level within a configurable number of chart bars; a low sweep occurs when low falls below the selected liquidity low minus this distance and close returns above that level within that many bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low formed using configurable left and right chart-bar lookbacks, available only after the right-side bars complete; use the latest confirmed value.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish BOS = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish EMA break = close crosses above/below EMA13, optionally requiring EMA13 to be greater/less than its value on the previous chart bar.
Calculation: Double bottom/top = the latest two confirmed swing lows/highs are separated by at least a configurable number of chart bars and differ by no more than a configurable multiple of ATR. Range breakout = the preceding configurable number of chart bars have a high-low width no greater than a configurable multiple of ATR, and close breaks above/below that range. Wedge breakout = the latest two confirmed swing highs are descending and latest two confirmed swing lows are ascending, and close breaks above the latest swing high/below the latest swing low.
Calculation: Qualified setup = bullish/bearish EMA break and BOS occur in the configured order within a configurable number of chart bars, or either order occurs within that window; optionally require a retest of the broken BOS level within a configurable number of bars (low touches or crosses the bullish level and close finishes above it / high touches or crosses the bearish level and close finishes below it). A qualified setup remains valid for a configurable number of chart bars.
Calculation: Initial stop = selected method: latest confirmed opposite swing point adjusted by a configurable tick buffer; entry close minus/plus a configurable ATR multiple; or entry close minus/plus a configurable tick distance. Risk = entry close minus the long stop / short stop minus entry close. A valid stop has risk greater than one tick. First target = entry close plus/minus one risk unit; runner target = entry close plus/minus two or three risk units, or the selected opposite liquidity level, when that target mode is chosen.
Long: bullish sweep within its configured validity window OR no valid bullish sweep and a selected bullish pattern (double bottom, range breakout, or wedge breakout) within its validity window; AND qualified bullish setup; AND selected entry mode is breakout close, or low <= EMA13 AND close > EMA13, or low <= bullish BOS level AND close > bullish BOS level; AND enabled filters pass (close above enabled EMA50 and EMA200, enabled stacked alignment EMA13 > EMA50 > EMA200, enabled volume > its moving average, enabled ATR > its moving average times a configurable multiple, and enabled trade-session filter); AND daily loss kill switch is inactive; AND position is flat if one-trade-at-a-time is enabled; AND selected long stop is valid.
Short: bearish sweep within its configured validity window OR no valid bearish sweep and a selected bearish pattern (double top, range breakout, or wedge breakout) within its validity window; AND qualified bearish setup; AND selected entry mode is breakout close, or high >= EMA13 AND close < EMA13, or high >= bearish BOS level AND close < bearish BOS level; AND enabled filters pass (close below enabled EMA50 and EMA200, enabled stacked alignment EMA13 < EMA50 < EMA200, enabled volume > its moving average, enabled ATR > its moving average times a configurable multiple, and enabled trade-session filter); AND daily loss kill switch is inactive; AND position is flat if one-trade-at-a-time is enabled; AND selected short stop is valid.
Exit: Long, close >= first target (close configurable portion of position; apply once).
Exit: Short, close <= first target (close configurable portion of position; apply once).
Exit: Long, close <= effective stop; before first target use initial stop, afterward use the greater of initial stop and entry price, further adjusted upward by the selected ATR or EMA13 trailing stop.
Exit: Short, close >= effective stop; before first target use initial stop, afterward use the lesser of initial stop and entry price, further adjusted downward by the selected ATR or EMA13 trailing stop.
Exit: Long, after first target, close >= selected runner target (two or three risk units or opposite liquidity high).
Exit: Short, after first target, close <= selected runner target (two or three risk units or opposite liquidity low).
