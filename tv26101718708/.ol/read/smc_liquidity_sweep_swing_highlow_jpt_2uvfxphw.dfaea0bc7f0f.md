Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookahead bars; it becomes available after the right-lookahead bars complete. Track the latest confirmed swing high until swept or replaced.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low lower than the lows of the preceding left-lookback bars and following right-lookahead bars; it becomes available after the right-lookahead bars complete. Track the latest confirmed swing low until swept or replaced.
Calculation: Upper-wick percentage = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum tick) × 100; lower-wick percentage = (min(open, close) − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) × 100.
Calculation: Long stop = min(signal-bar low, entry price − ATR × stop multiple); short stop = max(signal-bar high, entry price + ATR × stop multiple). Long target 3 = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × target-3 risk/reward multiple; short target 3 = entry price − (short stop − entry price) × target-3 risk/reward multiple.
Long: latest confirmed swing low exists and is unswept AND current low < swing low − ATR × sweep buffer AND (close-back filter disabled OR close > swing low) AND (rejection-body filter disabled OR close > open AND lower-wick percentage ≥ minimum wick percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND (no prior signal OR bars since last signal ≥ cooldown period)
Short: latest confirmed swing high exists and is unswept AND current high > swing high + ATR × sweep buffer AND (close-back filter disabled OR close < swing high) AND (rejection-body filter disabled OR close < open AND upper-wick percentage ≥ minimum wick percentage) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ average volume × volume multiplier) AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND (no prior signal OR bars since last signal ≥ cooldown period)
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target 3
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target 3
