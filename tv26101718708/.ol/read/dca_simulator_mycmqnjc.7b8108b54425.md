Calculation: Profit = position size × close − open trade count × single operation size
Long: DCA direction is long AND bar time > start time AND bar time < end time AND (selected period is daily OR selected period is weekly and weekday = selected weekday OR selected period is monthly and day of month = selected month day) AND weekday differs from the preceding chart bar
Short: DCA direction is short AND bar time > start time AND bar time < end time AND (selected period is daily OR selected period is weekly and weekday = selected weekday OR selected period is monthly and day of month = selected month day) AND weekday differs from the preceding chart bar
Exit: long, gain-threshold exit is selected AND current profit > gain threshold AND previous profit ≤ gain threshold
Exit: short, gain-threshold exit is selected AND current profit > gain threshold AND previous profit ≤ gain threshold
Exit: long, bar time > end time OR bar date = current date
Exit: short, bar time > end time OR bar date = current date
