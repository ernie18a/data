Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback of completed bars on the gauge timeframe (same as or higher than the chart timeframe)
Long: On an enabled gauge with buy signals, |close − period open| ≥ body threshold × ATR AND (min(close, period open) − period low) > 0 AND (min(close, period open) − period low) ≥ wick threshold × ATR AND (min(close, period open) − period low) / (period high − period low) ≥ range threshold AND elapsed fraction of the current period ≥ elapsed threshold; use the current still-forming gauge-period bar
Short: On an enabled gauge with sell signals, |close − period open| ≥ body threshold × ATR AND (period high − max(close, period open)) > 0 AND (period high − max(close, period open)) ≥ wick threshold × ATR AND (period high − max(close, period open)) / (period high − period low) ≥ range threshold AND elapsed fraction of the current period ≥ elapsed threshold; use the current still-forming gauge-period bar
