Calculation: ATR = Wilder average true range over the volatility lookback.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute price change over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute price changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Pressure = weighted average of the percentile ranks of ATR compression, absolute return acceleration, trend inefficiency, absolute volatility-index/forecast dislocation, and absolute companion-basis momentum; omit unavailable volatility-index and companion terms and renormalize the weights.
Calculation: Loaded = the highest pressure percentile in the recent spring-memory window.
Calculation: Range release = current high-low range divided by ATR, normalized so 0.8 ATR is 0 and 2.0 ATR or more is 1.
Calculation: Direction score = 2 points for closing above the prior-bar rolling trigger high or below the prior-bar rolling trigger low, 1 point for closing in the upper or lower half of the bar, and 1 point for price momentum over the direction lookback being nonnegative or negative, respectively; ties are bullish.
Calculation: Order-flow bias = positive or negative only when the standardized signed-volume imbalance exceeds its confirmation threshold in the same direction; otherwise neutral. Use reconstructed intrabar tick-rule volume from a lower timeframe when selected, or the selected bar-based volume classification; lower-timeframe bars are still forming within the current chart bar.
Calculation: Resistance and support = nearest opposing levels among prior-session value-area edges and point of control, live untested prior-session point-of-control levels, and anchored VWAP.
Long: Loaded reaches the high-pressure percentile threshold AND range release reaches its threshold for the configured confirmation-bar count AND the minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed AND bullish direction score AND liquidity is not stressed AND no resistance level is within the configured ATR clearance above the close.
Short: Loaded reaches the high-pressure percentile threshold AND range release reaches its threshold for the configured confirmation-bar count AND the minimum-bar cooldown has elapsed AND bearish direction score AND liquidity is not stressed AND no support level is within the configured ATR clearance below the close.
