Calculation: HLC3 = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Net behavioural pressure = 100 × (demand − supply) / (demand + supply), where each chart bar in the cohort lookback contributes weight = volume × 0.5^(bar age / recency half-life); demand sums weight × |(close − HLC3) / HLC3|^gain curvature for bars whose HLC3 is below close; supply sums weight × loss aversion × |(close − HLC3) / HLC3|^loss curvature for bars whose HLC3 is above close
Calculation: Support and resistance nodes = Divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high in the cohort lookback into equal price bins; sum each cohort's recency-weighted volume in its HLC3 bin; the support node is the center of the highest-weight bin below close, and the resistance node is the center of the highest-weight bin above close
Long: low ≤ support node + proximity × ATR AND close > support node AND net behavioural pressure > pressure threshold AND current bar is confirmed AND no signal occurred within the minimum signal gap
Short: high ≥ resistance node − proximity × ATR AND close < resistance node AND net behavioural pressure < −pressure threshold AND current bar is confirmed AND no signal occurred within the minimum signal gap
