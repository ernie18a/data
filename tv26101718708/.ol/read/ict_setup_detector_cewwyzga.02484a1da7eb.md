Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier / current high < low two bars earlier; gap bounds are the current low and the earlier high / the earlier low and the current high, and midpoint is their average.
Calculation: Displacement = the middle candle of the three-bar FVG has the matching direction and absolute close-open range >= ATR(20) × displacement multiplier; optionally its volume >= its 20-bar volume average × volume multiplier.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = outside overnight hours, a tracked level (prior completed daily high/low or the current overnight high/low from 18:00 to 09:30 New York time) was breached within the breach window and the current close crossed back through it; high breach and close below is a buy-side sweep, low breach and close above is a sell-side sweep. Each level can signal only once until its reset (overnight start for overnight levels; New York day change for daily levels).
Calculation: Recent sweep = the latest buy-side or sell-side sweep occurred within the sweep lookback; a newer sweep replaces the prior sweep direction.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure bias = the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows are both rising / both falling; pivots use equal left and right lookback lengths. For this and all bias calculations, use higher-timeframe bars and their last completed values.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish momentum bias = fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA / fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA; the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA. Calculate on each of three higher timeframes using their last completed bars.
Calculation: Bias gate allows long/short = the selected bias mode (Structure, Momentum, or Hybrid: Structure on the highest timeframe and Momentum on the other two) has at least one bullish/bearish timeframe, none opposing, and the opening-range-gap state is not bearish/bullish when gap alignment is required; the gap state is bullish for an unfilled positive gap, bearish for an unfilled negative gap, and neutral for a filled or zero gap, determined during the first RTH hour and locked afterward. If the bias gate is disabled, setup directions are unrestricted.
Calculation: Opening-range gap = today's 09:30 New York RTH open minus the prior RTH close captured at 16:00; it is filled during 09:30–10:00 if price reaches the prior RTH close.
Calculation: Silver Bullet stop/target = bullish: middle FVG candle low minus stop buffer / FVG midpoint plus target distance; bearish: middle candle high plus stop buffer / FVG midpoint minus target distance. Require positive stop distance from midpoint no greater than the maximum stop.
Calculation: OTE zone = for a bullish leg from the latest pivot low to a newly confirmed pivot high after a recent sell-side sweep, bounds are high minus 62% and 79% of leg range; midpoint entry reference is high minus 70.5% of range. For a bearish leg from latest pivot high to a newly confirmed pivot low after a recent buy-side sweep, bounds are low plus 62% and 79% of range; midpoint reference is low plus 70.5% of range. Stop is beyond the leg extreme by the stop buffer; require positive distance from the 70.5% reference no greater than the OTE maximum stop. Pivots use equal three-bar left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Order-block midpoint = midpoint of the body of the candle two bars before the current bar, which must close opposite to the displacement direction; stop is beyond that candle's extreme by the stop buffer. Target is midpoint plus/minus target distance. Require positive stop distance from midpoint no greater than the maximum stop.
Calculation: Sweep Reversal stop/target = for a sell-side sweep, current low minus stop buffer / current close plus target distance; for a buy-side sweep, current high plus stop buffer / current close minus target distance. Require positive stop distance from close no greater than the sweep-reversal maximum stop.
Long: A sell-side sweep reversal occurs and its stop distance passes the maximum, the bias gate allows long if enabled, and the killzone condition passes if required; OR a bullish Silver Bullet setup arms when a recent sweep is sell-side, a bullish FVG meets the minimum size, its middle candle is bullish displacement, current time is in a killzone, bias allows long, and its stop passes the maximum, then enters within the next 10 bars when low <= the stored FVG midpoint; OR a bullish OTE setup arms on the qualifying bullish pivot leg when bias allows long, then enters within 30 bars when low <= zone high AND close >= zone low AND close > open; OR a bullish order-block/FVG setup arms when a bullish FVG forms with bullish displacement and the opposing candle two bars earlier is bearish, bias allows long, and its stop passes the maximum, then enters within 20 bars when low <= the stored order-block midpoint. Expire unentered setups after their stated windows; cancel an armed setup if price reaches its stop before entry.
Short: A buy-side sweep reversal occurs and its stop distance passes the maximum, the bias gate allows short if enabled, and the killzone condition passes if required; OR a bearish Silver Bullet setup arms when a recent sweep is buy-side, a bearish FVG meets the minimum size, its middle candle is bearish displacement, current time is in a killzone, bias allows short, and its stop passes the maximum, then enters within the next 10 bars when high >= the stored FVG midpoint; OR a bearish OTE setup arms on the qualifying bearish pivot leg when bias allows short, then enters within 30 bars when high >= zone low AND close <= zone high AND close < open; OR a bearish order-block/FVG setup arms when a bearish FVG forms with bearish displacement and the opposing candle two bars earlier is bullish, bias allows short, and its stop passes the maximum, then enters within 20 bars when high >= the stored order-block midpoint. Expire unentered setups after their stated windows; cancel an armed setup if price reaches its stop before entry.
Exit: long, high >= stored target OR low <= stored stop OR bars since entry >= time-stop length.
Exit: short, low <= stored target OR high >= stored stop OR bars since entry >= time-stop length.
