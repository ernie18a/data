Calculation: Heavy volume = volume > simple moving average of volume × multiplier
Calculation: Long breakout level = high of the most recent heavy-volume bar where close < open
Calculation: Short breakout level = low of the most recent heavy-volume bar where close > open
Long: Heavy volume AND close < open
Long: Long breakout level > 0 AND previous close >= long breakout level AND previous low <= long breakout level AND close >= previous close AND previous close >= previous open AND close >= previous open
Short: Heavy volume AND close > open
Short: Short breakout level > 0 AND previous close <= short breakout level AND previous high >= short breakout level AND close <= previous close AND previous close <= previous open AND close <= previous open
Exit: Long, low <= low of the candle immediately preceding the long-entry signal
Exit: Short, high >= high of the candle immediately preceding the short-entry signal
