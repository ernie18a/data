Long: OBV crosses above its long-entry simple moving average (shorter lookback than the long-exit moving average) AND direction is set to long AND position is flat or short
Short: OBV crosses below its short-entry simple moving average (longer lookback than the short-exit moving average) AND direction is set to short AND position is flat or long
Exit: Long, OBV crosses below its long-exit simple moving average
Exit: Short, OBV crosses above its short-exit simple moving average
