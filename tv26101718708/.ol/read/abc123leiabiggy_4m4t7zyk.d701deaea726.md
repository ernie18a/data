Calculation: Swept level = the first strict cross of a level from one of the five most recent completed higher-timeframe bars, provided the previous chart bar did not cross it and it was not crossed during the preceding freshness lookback; if both a high and low qualify on the same bar, use the high.
Calculation: Impulse base = for a swept high, the lower low of the nearest prior bearish chart bar within the scan lookback and the chart bar immediately after it; for a swept low, the higher high of the nearest prior bullish chart bar within the scan lookback and the chart bar immediately after it.
Calculation: Post-sweep extreme = the highest high after a swept high, or lowest low after a swept low, from the impulse-base candle through the bar that breaks the impulse base.
Calculation: Retracement midpoint = the midpoint between the impulse base and post-sweep extreme.
Calculation: Entry level = after the impulse base breaks, track chart bars whose high-low range touches the retracement midpoint. For a swept high, use the lowest low of those bars; for a swept low, use the highest high. If a midpoint-touching bar also breaks the impulse base, set the entry level to the impulse base and wait for a later bar to touch it.
Long: A fresh sweep of a low from the five most recent completed higher-timeframe bars AND a valid impulse base is found AND price later breaks above the impulse base AND, before entry, price does not rise above the impulse base by three times the impulse-base-to-post-sweep-extreme range AND price touches the entry level (the retracement-derived level, or the impulse base after a retest is required).
Short: A fresh sweep of a high from the five most recent completed higher-timeframe bars AND a valid impulse base is found AND price later breaks below the impulse base AND, before entry, price does not fall below the impulse base by three times the post-sweep-extreme-to-impulse-base range AND price touches the entry level (the retracement-derived level, or the impulse base after a retest is required).
