Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages of close; the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fast and slow EMAs = the same EMAs on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: UT trailing stop = ATR-based trailing stop: initialize to close minus key × ATR; if current and previous closes are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and close minus key × ATR; if both are below, use the lesser of the prior stop and close plus key × ATR; otherwise use close minus key × ATR when close is above the prior stop, or close plus key × ATR otherwise.
Calculation: Profit targets = five successively farther levels from the average entry price, spaced by a configured increment; long targets are above entry and short targets are below entry.
Calculation: Initial stop = average entry price offset by a configured loss distance, below entry for longs and above entry for shorts.
Calculation: Dynamic stop = initial stop until the first target is hit; then average entry; after the second target, the first target; after the third target, the second target; after the fourth target, the third target. A long target is hit when high reaches it; a short target is hit when low reaches it. Reset target-hit state when flat.
Long: close crosses above the UT trailing stop AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > higher-timeframe slow EMA AND ADX > its configured minimum AND Supertrend is bullish AND volume > its 20-bar simple moving average AND the bar is within either configured trading session AND bars since the last entry > the configured cooldown AND position side is flat or short.
Short: close crosses below the UT trailing stop AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < higher-timeframe slow EMA AND ADX > its configured minimum AND Supertrend is bearish AND volume > its 20-bar simple moving average AND the bar is within either configured trading session AND bars since the last entry > the configured cooldown AND position side is flat or long.
Exit: long, high reaches any profit target OR low reaches the dynamic stop.
Exit: short, low reaches any profit target OR high reaches the dynamic stop.
