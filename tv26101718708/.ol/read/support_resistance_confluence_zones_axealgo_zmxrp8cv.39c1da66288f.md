Calculation: Active support/resistance zones = same-side swing highs/lows or later retests clustered within an ATR-based tolerance; require at least two swing points. Confirm pivots only after the configured number of bars to their right. Zone bounds expand to include clustered pivots, subject to configured ATR-based minimum and maximum heights. A zone is resistance until a confirmed close above its top flips it to support; support flips to resistance on a close below its bottom. Count confirmed retests no more often than the configured minimum bar spacing; remove zones beyond the configured ATR distance.
Calculation: Zone score = integer min(100, 80 − 40 / (1 + (max(total touches, 2) − 2) × 0.15) + 8 if aligned with the latest confirmed higher-timeframe swing of the same role within an ATR tolerance + 6 if near a psychological round number within an ATR tolerance + 6 if formed or retested on volume above 1.5 times its 20-bar average). Higher-timeframe swing alignment uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Active support zone has score >= 60 AND low <= zone top AND close > zone top AND close > open
Short: Active resistance zone has score >= 60 AND high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom AND close < open
