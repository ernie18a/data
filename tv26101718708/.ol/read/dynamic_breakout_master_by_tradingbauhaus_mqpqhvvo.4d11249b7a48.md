Calculation: Support/resistance zones = cluster confirmed pivot highs and lows (using highs/lows or candle body extremes) within a maximum width based on the recent price range; retain pivots within a lookback, require minimum pivot strength, and rank qualifying zones by pivot strength plus the number of recent bars whose highs or lows fall within each zone.
Calculation: Short-term moving average = configured SMA or EMA of close with a shorter lookback than the long-term moving average.
Calculation: Long-term moving average = configured SMA or EMA of close with a longer lookback than the short-term moving average.
Long: A resistance zone is broken (previous close ≤ its upper boundary, current close > its upper boundary, and current close is outside all zones) OR (current close crosses below the most recently recorded breakout level after a breakout and the short-term moving average is above the long-term moving average) AND the short-term moving average is above the long-term moving average AND RSI is between the configured oversold and overbought thresholds AND ATR exceeds ATR multiplied by the configured volatility multiplier AND the bar hour is within configured trading hours AND volume exceeds its 20-bar SMA multiplied by 1.5.
Short: A support zone is broken (previous close ≥ its lower boundary, current close < its lower boundary, and current close is outside all zones) OR (current close crosses above the most recently recorded breakout level after a breakout and the short-term moving average is below the long-term moving average) AND the short-term moving average is below the long-term moving average AND RSI is between the configured oversold and overbought thresholds AND ATR exceeds ATR multiplied by the configured volatility multiplier AND the bar hour is within configured trading hours AND volume exceeds its 20-bar SMA multiplied by 1.5.
Exit: long, RSI > 80 OR the short-term moving average crosses below the long-term moving average OR the stop, target, or trailing stop is hit (stop distance = ATR × 1.5; target distance = stop distance × configured reward ratio; trailing distance = half the stop distance).
Exit: short, RSI < 20 OR the short-term moving average crosses above the long-term moving average OR the stop, target, or trailing stop is hit (stop distance = ATR × 1.5; target distance = stop distance × configured reward ratio; trailing distance = half the stop distance).
