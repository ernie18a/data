Calculation: Williams flow = smoothed 0–100 close position within the lookback high-low range, (close − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low) × 100
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of Williams flow
Calculation: Bullish trend = close above the chart-timeframe EMA and EMA at least as high as its value several bars earlier
Calculation: Bearish trend = close below the chart-timeframe EMA and EMA at most as high as its value several bars earlier
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = bullish when higher-timeframe close is above its EMA and that EMA is non-falling, and its Williams flow is above both its signal line and the midline; bearish when the inverse conditions hold; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Precision score = weighted average of extreme reclaim, trend alignment, flow slope, ADX strength, candle impulse, and flow-signal cross scores; each component scores stronger when its corresponding bullish or bearish condition holds, and weaker or zero otherwise.
Long: Williams flow crosses above the oversold level OR (Williams flow was below the oversold level within the recent lookback AND Williams flow crosses above its signal line AND Williams flow is above the oversold level) OR Williams flow crosses above the midline OR (Williams flow is above the midline AND crosses above its signal line); AND Williams flow is above its signal line; AND Williams flow is rising and its signal line is non-falling; AND bullish trend; AND close > open AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio; AND bullish precision score >= minimum score; AND higher-timeframe direction is bullish; AND current bar is confirmed; AND raw long condition was false on the previous bar; AND the configured cooldown since the last long signal has elapsed
Short: Williams flow crosses below the overbought level OR (Williams flow was above the overbought level within the recent lookback AND Williams flow crosses below its signal line AND Williams flow is below the overbought level) OR Williams flow crosses below the midline OR (Williams flow is below the midline AND crosses below its signal line); AND Williams flow is below its signal line; AND Williams flow is falling and its signal line is non-rising; AND bearish trend; AND close < open AND absolute(close − open) / (high − low) >= minimum body ratio; AND bearish precision score >= minimum score; AND higher-timeframe direction is bearish; AND current bar is confirmed; AND raw short condition was false on the previous bar; AND the configured cooldown since the last short signal has elapsed
