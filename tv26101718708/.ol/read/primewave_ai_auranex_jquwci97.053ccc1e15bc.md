Calculation: Ichimoku conversion line = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the shorter adaptive lookback; base line uses a longer adaptive lookback, and Span B uses the longest. Adaptive lookbacks scale inversely with ATR’s position within its recent range, bounded by configured stretch limits.
Calculation: Current cloud top/bottom = max/min of Span A and Span B shifted forward by the displacement; Span A is the midpoint of the conversion and base lines.
Calculation: Chikou confirmation = current close compared with the close one displacement earlier.
Calculation: Confluence score = points for price vs. cloud (bull: 20 above, 8 inside, 0 below; bear: reverse); conversion/base alignment (15); Chikou confirmation (15); future-cloud direction from the relative slopes of Span A and Span B (10); cloud thickness (8 when at least 30% of its 50-bar average, otherwise 2); volume confirmation (10); momentum alignment (12 when above 20 in the trade direction, 6 when positive in that direction, otherwise 0); and momentum acceleration (10 when rising for bull or falling for bear). Cap at 100. Volume confirmation is volume above its 20-bar average times a configured multiplier, or true when volume is absent. Momentum is the 5-bar EMA of a clipped combination of normalized conversion/base spread, its 3-bar change, and normalized cloud slope; cloud slope compares each span with its value 5 bars earlier.
Calculation: Risk distance = max(distance from entry price to configured stop level, half the ATR). The stop level is selected as the relevant cloud edge plus/minus an ATR buffer, the base line plus/minus an ATR buffer, or entry price plus/minus a configured ATR multiple. Take-profit levels are entry price plus/minus risk distance times configured reward multiples; the third target uses the largest multiple.
Long: On a completed chart bar after warm-up, conversion line crosses above base line AND cloud thickness is at least 30% of its 50-bar average when the thickness filter is enabled AND volume confirmation holds when the volume filter is enabled AND Chikou is above the earlier close when the Chikou filter is enabled AND bullish confluence score meets the configured minimum; OR close crosses above current cloud top from at or below the prior bar's cloud top AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the configured breakout multiplier (or volume is absent), on a completed bar after warm-up; OR within 2–10 chart bars after an upward cloud breakout, low touches or falls below current cloud top AND close is above it, on a completed bar after warm-up.
Short: On a completed chart bar after warm-up, conversion line crosses below base line AND cloud thickness is at least 30% of its 50-bar average when the thickness filter is enabled AND volume confirmation holds when the volume filter is enabled AND Chikou is below the earlier close when the Chikou filter is enabled AND bearish confluence score meets the configured minimum; OR close crosses below current cloud bottom from at or above the prior bar's cloud bottom AND volume exceeds its 20-bar average times the configured breakout multiplier (or volume is absent), on a completed bar after warm-up; OR within 2–10 chart bars after a downward cloud breakout, high touches or rises above current cloud bottom AND close is below it, on a completed bar after warm-up.
Exit: Long, low <= stop level OR high >= third take-profit level.
Exit: Short, high >= stop level OR low <= third take-profit level.
