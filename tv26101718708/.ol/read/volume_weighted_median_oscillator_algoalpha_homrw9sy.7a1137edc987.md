Calculation: Volume-weighted median = the close whose cumulative volume, after sorting closes in ascending order over a lookback, first reaches at least half the total volume.
Calculation: Momentum histogram = (close − volume-weighted median) ÷ EMA of high − low; optionally WMA-smoothed.
Calculation: Median oscillator = (volume-weighted median − its EMA with a longer lookback) ÷ EMA of high − low; optionally WMA-smoothed.
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of the median oscillator.
Long: Momentum histogram crosses above zero OR median oscillator crosses above zero OR median oscillator crosses above its signal line OR median oscillator becomes greater than or equal to its signal line after previously being below it.
Short: Momentum histogram crosses below zero OR median oscillator crosses below zero OR median oscillator crosses below its signal line OR median oscillator becomes less than its signal line after previously being greater than or equal to it.
