Calculation: HTF VWAP = sum of (hlc3 × volume) divided by sum of volume from chart bars in the current higher-timeframe period, including the bar still forming
Calculation: HTF CVD = sum of volume for chart bars in the current higher-timeframe period where close ≥ open, minus volume where close < open, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a chart-bar high greater than the highs of an equal number of bars before and after it
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a chart-bar low less than the lows of an equal number of bars before and after it
Long: close > HTF VWAP OR (close > 0? no)
