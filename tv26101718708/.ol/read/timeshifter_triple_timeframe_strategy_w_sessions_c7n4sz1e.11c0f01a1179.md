Calculation: RMI = average of (an RSI-style value computed from RMA-smoothed positive and negative close changes, with 100 when smoothed losses are zero and 0 when smoothed gains are zero) and MFI of close
Calculation: TWAP = simple moving average of (high + low + close) / 3
Long: long trading is enabled AND within any enabled configured session AND higher-timeframe close > its simple moving average (use the last available higher-timeframe bar: completed historically, forming in realtime) AND chart-timeframe close crosses above its simple moving average AND (selected indicator is RMI and RMI > its overbought threshold OR selected indicator is TWAP and chart close > TWAP OR selected indicator is TEMA and chart close > TEMA OR selected indicator is DEMA and chart close > DEMA OR selected indicator is MA and chart close > its simple moving average OR selected indicator is MFI and MFI > its overbought threshold OR selected indicator is VWMA and chart close > VWMA OR selected indicator is PSAR and chart close > PSAR) AND (ADX confirmation is disabled OR ADX > its threshold)
Short: short trading is enabled AND within any enabled configured session AND higher-timeframe close < its simple moving average (use the last available higher-timeframe bar: completed historically, forming in realtime) AND chart-timeframe close crosses below its simple moving average AND (selected indicator is RMI and RMI < its oversold threshold OR selected indicator is TWAP and chart close < TWAP OR selected indicator is TEMA and chart close < TEMA OR selected indicator is DEMA and chart close < DEMA OR selected indicator is MA and chart close < its simple moving average OR selected indicator is MFI and MFI < its oversold threshold OR selected indicator is VWMA and chart close < VWMA OR selected indicator is PSAR and chart close < PSAR) AND (ADX confirmation is disabled OR ADX > its threshold)
Exit: long, long trading is enabled AND within any enabled configured session AND lower-timeframe close crosses below its simple moving average (use the last available lower-timeframe bar: completed historically, forming in realtime)
Exit: short, short trading is enabled AND within any enabled configured session AND lower-timeframe close crosses above its simple moving average (use the last available lower-timeframe bar: completed historically, forming in realtime)
