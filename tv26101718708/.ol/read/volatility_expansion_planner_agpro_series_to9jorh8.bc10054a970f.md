Calculation: ATR ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average
Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Range breakout = close is at least the minimum ATR-normalized distance above the prior range high (long) or below the prior range low (short)
Calculation: Expansion direction = configured long/short direction; in automatic mode, use the sole direction with a range breakout, otherwise the sole direction with trend alignment (close and fast EMA on the same side of the slow EMA), otherwise long when close is at or above the slow EMA and short otherwise
Calculation: Risk shelf = lower of prior range low and the lowest low over the shelf lookback (long); higher of prior range high and the highest high over that lookback (short)
Calculation: Shelf distance = distance from close to the risk shelf in ATR units, measured in the expansion direction
Calculation: Close efficiency = clamp to 0–100: 62% times the close’s location in the bar’s range toward the expansion direction plus 38% times absolute candle body divided by bar range
Calculation: Volume support = clamp to 0–100 the volume/current-volume-baseline ratio, mapping 0.75 to 0 and 1.55 to 100; use 55 when volume is unavailable
Calculation: Trend alignment score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 55 when close is on the expansion side of the slow EMA (otherwise 18), 28 when the fast EMA is on the expansion side of the slow EMA (otherwise 0), and up to 17 for the slow EMA’s ATR-normalized slope in the expansion direction, linearly scaled from 0 at 0.01 to 17 at 0.32
Calculation: Compression = prior range width divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average is at or below the compression range threshold AND ATR ratio is at or below the compression ATR threshold; compression age counts consecutive compressed bars
Calculation: Active compression age = prior compression age on a breakout bar; otherwise current compression age
Calculation: Risk shelf score = triangular score peaking at the configured ideal shelf distance, reaching 0 at the configured minimum and maximum shelf distances, linearly scaled between those points
Calculation: ATR expansion score = clamp to 0–100: 72% of a triangular score of ATR ratio (0 at 0.82, peak at 1.38, 0 at the configured hot ATR ratio) plus 28% of ATR-ratio slope score (0 at slope −0.02, 100 at 0.18)
Calculation: Range breakout score = clamp to 0–100 the ATR-normalized close distance beyond the prior range edge, linearly scaled from 35% of the minimum breakout distance to the minimum breakout distance plus 0.95 ATR
Calculation: Expansion score = round and clamp to 0–100 the weighted sum: ATR expansion score 24%, range breakout score 22%, close efficiency 18%, volume support 12%, trend alignment score 14%, risk shelf score 6%, and active compression age score 4%; age score scales linearly from 0 at 45% of minimum squeeze age to 100 at 215% of minimum squeeze age. If ATR ratio exceeds the hot threshold and close efficiency is below 58, cap the weighted sum at 48 before rounding
Calculation: Confirmation = range breakout AND NOT noisy; confirmed when close efficiency ≥ 62 AND volume support ≥ 52 AND trend alignment score ≥ 55
Long: expansion direction is long AND expansion state changes to structured: range breakout AND expansion score ≥ configured structured threshold AND confirmation AND shelf distance is within configured minimum and maximum AND current bar is confirmed AND previous bar’s state was not structured
Short: expansion direction is short AND expansion state changes to structured: range breakout AND expansion score ≥ configured structured threshold AND confirmation AND shelf distance is within configured minimum and maximum AND current bar is confirmed AND previous bar’s state was not structured
