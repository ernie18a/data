Long: RSI crosses above the upper threshold AND no trade is active AND the strategy start time has been reached AND more than the ATR lookback bars have elapsed
Short: RSI crosses below the lower threshold AND no trade is active AND the strategy start time has been reached AND more than the ATR lookback bars have elapsed

Exit: Short, high > trailing stop OR low reaches the entry price − ATR × take-profit multiplier
