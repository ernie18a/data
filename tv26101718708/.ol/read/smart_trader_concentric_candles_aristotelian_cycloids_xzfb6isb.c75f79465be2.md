Calculation: Maxima candles = the two most recent candles found by scanning backward from the last completed bar; select a candle when its high-low range is greater than the range of the immediately older candle. Also include the largest-range candle in a lookback window if it is not already selected.
Calculation: Cycloid price = for each selected candle, map its outer circle (radius = log(high/low) divided by twice that candle’s Yang-Zhang volatility), upper-wick circle (radius = log(high/max(open,close)) divided by twice that volatility), and lower-wick circle (radius = log(min(open,close)/low) divided by twice that volatility) onto a curve anchored at the candle’s low. Generate each curve from x(θ)=Rθ−r sin(θ+π) and y(θ)=R−r cos(θ+π), then convert its normalized height y to price as exp(log(low)+y×volatility). Use the curve price at the current bar.
Calculation: Contact = current bar’s close is within the contact tolerance of a cycloid price OR the current bar’s high is at or above it and low is at or below it; contact tolerance scales with current close and Yang-Zhang volatility.
Long: Contact AND (open < cycloid price AND close > cycloid price OR low ≤ cycloid price AND close > cycloid price) AND absolute(close − cycloid price) is not within the tighter stopped-at-contact tolerance.
Short: Contact AND (open > cycloid price AND close < cycloid price OR high ≥ cycloid price AND close < cycloid price) AND absolute(close − cycloid price) is not within the tighter stopped-at-contact tolerance.
