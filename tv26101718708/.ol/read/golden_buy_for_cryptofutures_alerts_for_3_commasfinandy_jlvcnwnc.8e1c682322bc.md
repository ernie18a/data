Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a confirmed pivot low, price makes a lower low and the indicator makes a higher low than at the preceding pivot low.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a confirmed pivot high, price makes a higher high and the indicator makes a lower high than at the preceding pivot high.
Calculation: Gold-buy condition = bullish WaveTrend or RSI divergence, and the preceding WaveTrend pivot low is at or below the oversold-level-3 threshold, current WaveTrend is above that threshold, the preceding pivot value minus current WaveTrend is at most −5, and RSI at the preceding WaveTrend pivot is below 30.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of the signal bar low and preceding bar low, minus ATR(14) × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Long target = signal bar close + (signal bar close − long stop) × reward/risk ratio.
Calculation: Short stop = the higher of the signal bar high and preceding bar high, plus ATR(14) × stop multiplier.
Calculation: Short target = signal bar close − (short stop − signal bar close) × reward/risk ratio.
Long: Flat AND (gold-buy condition OR divergence entries enabled) AND (bullish WaveTrend divergence with pivot oscillator at or below the bearish-divergence minimum OR bullish WaveTrend divergence with pivot oscillator at or below the second bullish-divergence threshold OR bullish RSI divergence with pivot RSI at or below the bullish-divergence minimum OR bullish Stochastic RSI divergence)
Short: Flat AND short entries enabled AND (bearish WaveTrend divergence with pivot oscillator at or above the bearish-divergence minimum OR bearish WaveTrend divergence with pivot oscillator at or above the second bearish-divergence threshold OR bearish RSI divergence with pivot RSI at or above the bearish-divergence minimum OR bearish Stochastic RSI divergence)
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
