Calculation: Support zone = for each confirmed swing low (its low is below the lows of the same number of bars on both sides), the range from the swing candle’s low to the lower of its open and close; pad to a minimum height and cap at a maximum height, both measured in ATR of the swing candle. Merge overlapping or nearby active zones.
Calculation: Resistance zone = for each confirmed swing high (its high is above the highs of the same number of bars on both sides), the range from the higher of the swing candle’s open and close to its high; pad to a minimum height and cap at a maximum height, both measured in ATR of the swing candle. Merge overlapping or nearby active zones.
Long: bar overlaps an active support zone AND close > its upper edge
Long: close > the upper edge of an active resistance zone
Short: bar overlaps an active resistance zone AND close < its lower edge
Short: close < the lower edge of an active support zone
