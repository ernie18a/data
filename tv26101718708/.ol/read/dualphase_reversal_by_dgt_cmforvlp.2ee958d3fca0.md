Calculation: Sell Momentum count = starts at 1 when close > close 4 bars ago and the prior close < close 5 bars ago; while active, increments on each bar with close > close 4 bars ago, and resets when that condition is false. At count 0 or 9, it restarts only on the stated start condition.
Calculation: Buy Terminal count = starts after Buy Momentum reaches 9, provided close <= low 2 bars ago; while active, increments when close <= low 2 bars ago and otherwise holds its count. It resets when Sell Momentum reaches 9, after reaching 13, or when close exceeds the highest high of the 9-bar Buy Momentum completion window. If it reaches 13 and the current low is at least the close recorded at count 8, it returns to 12.
Calculation: Sell Terminal count = starts after Sell Momentum reaches 9, provided close >= high 2 bars ago; while active, increments when close >= high 2 bars ago and otherwise holds its count. It resets when Buy Momentum reaches 9, after reaching 13, or when close falls below the lowest low of the 9-bar Sell Momentum completion window. If it reaches 13 and the current high is at most the close recorded at count 8, it returns to 12.
Long: Buy Momentum count is 1 through 9, or Buy Terminal count changes, or Buy Terminal count is 12 and its count-13 failure condition is met.
Short: Sell Momentum count is 1 through 9, or Sell Terminal count changes, or Sell Terminal count is 12 and its count-13 failure condition is met.
