Calculation: RSI moving average = simple moving average of RSI
Long: Enabled entry conditions: RSI < or > its threshold; RSI moving average < or > its threshold; MFI < or > its threshold; MACD line crosses above signal, histogram < threshold, or MACD line < threshold; close below MA, at least configured % below MA, above MA, or at least configured % above MA; shorter-lookback MA crosses above or below longer-lookback MA; Supertrend is uptrend or downtrend, or changes into uptrend or downtrend. Combine enabled conditions with AND in require-all mode, otherwise OR; no enabled conditions means no entry.
Short: Same configurable conditions as Long, with each condition inverted when short inversion is enabled; otherwise use the Long conditions. Inversion swaps RSI, RSI moving average, and MFI thresholds to 100 minus their configured thresholds; reverses MACD cross direction and negates its histogram/line threshold; reverses the price-vs-MA and MA-cross directions; and reverses Supertrend direction or change direction. Combine enabled conditions with AND in require-all mode, otherwise OR; no enabled conditions means no entry.
Exit: long position, enabled exit conditions: RSI > or < its threshold; RSI moving average > or < its threshold; MFI > or < its threshold; MACD line crosses below signal, histogram > threshold, or MACD line > threshold; shorter-lookback MA crosses below or above longer-lookback MA; Supertrend is downtrend or uptrend, or changes into downtrend or uptrend; close below MA, at least configured % below MA, above MA, or at least configured % above MA. Invert each condition for short positions when short inversion is enabled, using 100 minus the threshold for RSI, RSI moving average, and MFI, negating MACD thresholds and reversing cross/direction conditions. Combine enabled indicator conditions with AND in require-all mode, otherwise OR; no enabled conditions means no indicator exit. Also exit when low <= entry price minus configured stop amount or entry price × (1 − configured stop percent), or high >= entry price plus configured take-profit amount or entry price × (1 + configured take-profit percent), when those respective stop/take-profit options are enabled. If take-profit overrides indicators, use only enabled stop-loss and take-profit conditions; otherwise any enabled stop-loss, take-profit, or indicator exit condition exits.
Exit: short position, enabled exit conditions as above with short-position inversion when enabled. Also exit when high >= entry price plus configured stop amount or entry price × (1 + configured stop percent), or low <= entry price minus configured take-profit amount or entry price × (1 − configured take-profit percent), when those respective stop/take-profit options are enabled. If take-profit overrides indicators, use only enabled stop-loss and take-profit conditions; otherwise any enabled stop-loss, take-profit, or indicator exit condition exits.
