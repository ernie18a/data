Calculation: RSI = Wilder RSI of close, calculated on each enabled monitored timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, using the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of enabled monitored timeframes with RSI at or above the midline; bearish count = total enabled timeframes minus bullish count.
Long: every enabled monitored-timeframe RSI is at or below the oversold threshold OR bullish count crosses above bearish count
Short: every enabled monitored-timeframe RSI is at or above the overbought threshold OR bullish count crosses below bearish count
