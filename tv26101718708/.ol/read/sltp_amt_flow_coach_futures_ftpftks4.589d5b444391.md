Calculation: Footprint delta = volume traded at ask minus volume traded at bid within the bar
Calculation: Footprint volume = total volume across the bar’s footprint rows
Calculation: Delta share = footprint delta / footprint volume
Calculation: Session profile = accumulate footprint row volume by price from the daily session boundary; POC = price of the highest-volume row; value area = expand outward from POC, adding the higher-volume adjacent row first, until the configured fraction of session volume is included; VAL and VAH = lower and upper bounds of that area
Calculation: Nearest LVN = local minimum-volume session-profile row nearest the current close
Long: Footprint data is available AND footprint volume >= its moving average × the high-volume multiplier AND delta share <= negative delta threshold AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick) >= bullish close-location threshold AND (low <= VAL + ATR × near-level multiplier OR the bar range intersects the nearest LVN ± ATR × near-level multiplier OR low < the lowest low of the preceding swing lookback bars AND close > that preceding low)
Long: After a bullish absorption bar, within the confirmation-bar limit and before any bar trades below that absorption bar’s low, close > that absorption bar’s high AND (session POC if above close, otherwise VAH) > close
Short: Footprint data is available AND footprint volume >= its moving average × the high-volume multiplier AND delta share >= the delta threshold AND (close - low) / max(high - low, minimum tick) <= bearish close-location threshold AND (high >= VAH - ATR × near-level multiplier OR the bar range intersects the session POC ± ATR × near-level multiplier OR high > the highest high of the preceding swing lookback bars AND close < that preceding high)
Short: After a bearish absorption bar, within the confirmation-bar limit and before any bar trades above that absorption bar’s high, close < that absorption bar’s low AND (session POC if below close, otherwise VAL) < close
