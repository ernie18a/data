Calculation: Pivot high = a high greater than the highs of an equal number of bars on both sides; confirmed after the bars on the right complete. Pivot low = a low lower than the lows of an equal number of bars on both sides; confirmed after the bars on the right complete.
Calculation: Average true range = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Resistance zone = zone created around a confirmed pivot high, initially extending above and below the pivot by a fraction of ATR; merge same-type zones whose centers are within an ATR-based distance, widening their bounds to include the new pivot. Support zone = same construction around a confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Zone strength = volume score + reaction score + base bonus; volume score increases with pivot-bar volume relative to the prior-bar average volume, and reaction score increases with the largest move away from the pivot during a forward reaction window relative to ATR at the pivot. Merged zones also gain strength and a touch bonus. Strength decays each bar; discard zones that exceed their age limit, fall below minimum viable strength, or are stale after a break; if over the active-zone limit, discard the weakest.
Calculation: Break volume condition = when enabled, current volume is at least a multiplier of the prior-bar average; if the volume cap is also enabled, current volume must not exceed its multiplier of that average. Break momentum condition = when enabled, current high minus low is at least a multiplier of ATR.
Long: No position is open AND (an unbroken resistance zone is crossed upward, with close above its top and prior close at or below its top, AND break volume and momentum conditions pass; OR a prior upward break is within the configured retest bar window, low is at or below the broken zone top, close is above that top, and close minus low is at least a minimum ATR multiple). Apply the optional first-retest-only filter to the retest condition.
Short: No position is open AND (an unbroken support zone is crossed downward, with close below its bottom and prior close at or above its bottom, AND break volume and momentum conditions pass; OR a prior downward break is within the configured retest bar window, high is at or above the broken zone bottom, close is below that bottom, and high minus close is at least a minimum ATR multiple). Apply the optional first-retest-only filter to the retest condition.
Exit: Long, after the entry bar, low <= active stop OR high >= third profit target; after the first profit target is reached without a stop hit on that bar, move the stop to entry.
Exit: Short, after the entry bar, high >= active stop OR low <= third profit target; after the first profit target is reached without a stop hit on that bar, move the stop to entry.
