Calculation: ATR = average true range; pool tolerance and minimum sweep depth are ATR multiples.
Calculation: Active BSL = nearest level above close selected from (a) a pivot-high cluster with at least the minimum touches and age, merging nearby pivot highs by touch-weighted average, and (b) the highest high in the lookback range if enough highs are within pool tolerance. Active SSL is the corresponding nearest level below close from pivot-low clusters and the lowest low in the range.
Calculation: SSL sweep = low < active SSL − minimum sweep depth; BSL sweep = high > active BSL + minimum sweep depth. Retain the latest swept level and bar; a sweep is recent through the reclaim window, inclusive.
Calculation: Bull trigger = close > open AND body ≥ minimum body size AND (close − low) / (high − low) > 0.50. Bear trigger = close < open AND body ≥ minimum body size AND (high − close) / (high − low) > 0.50.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low > high two bars earlier; bearish FVG = high < low two bars earlier. The gap zone is [high two bars earlier, current low] for bullish and [current high, low two bars earlier] for bearish.
Calculation: Bull/bear score = setup points (2 for either sweep/reclaim setup; otherwise 1 for either absorption/FVG setup; otherwise 0) + 1 each for valid directional pool, aligned higher-timeframe bias, volume ≥ volume average × threshold, structural bias, RSI exhaustion after a recent matching sweep, non-chop market, and price at the matching pool zone. Structural bias is close above structure EMA OR RSI > 45 for long, and close below structure EMA OR RSI < 55 for short. Exhaustion is RSI < 38 with a recent SSL sweep for long, or RSI > 62 with a recent BSL sweep for short. Higher-timeframe bias uses fast and slow EMAs with the fast EMA longer-term counterpart relationship: bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close < fast EMA. Use the higher timeframe’s still-forming bar.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); non-chop means it is below the threshold.
Long: (SSL sweep AND close > active SSL AND bull trigger AND NOT (close < open AND body > 0.45 × ATR AND close < structure EMA AND structure EMA < 50-period EMA)) OR (recent SSL sweep at least one bar ago AND close > swept SSL AND low ≤ swept SSL + pool tolerance AND bull trigger) OR (low ≤ active SSL + pool tolerance AND lower wick / range > 0.42 AND body / range < 0.38 AND close > open AND volume ≥ volume average × threshold AND close > active SSL) OR (bullish FVG overlaps active SSL within pool tolerance AND bull trigger); AND bull score ≥ minimum score AND non-chop AND cooldown elapsed.
Short: (BSL sweep AND close < active BSL AND bear trigger AND NOT (close > open AND body > 0.45 × ATR AND close > structure EMA AND structure EMA > 50-period EMA)) OR (recent BSL sweep at least one bar ago AND close < swept BSL AND high ≥ swept BSL − pool tolerance AND bear trigger) OR (high ≥ active BSL − pool tolerance AND upper wick / range > 0.42 AND body / range < 0.38 AND close < open AND volume ≥ volume average × threshold AND close < active BSL) OR (bearish FVG overlaps active BSL within pool tolerance AND bear trigger); AND bear score ≥ minimum score AND non-chop AND cooldown elapsed.
