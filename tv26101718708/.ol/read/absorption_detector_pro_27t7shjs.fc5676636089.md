Calculation: Absorption score = (current volume / average volume over the lookback) / max((high − low) / average (high − low) over the same lookback, 0.05)
Calculation: Absorption score percentile = percentile rank of the current absorption score among scores over the ranking lookback
Calculation: Climax volume = current volume ≥ average volume over the PVSRA lookback of prior bars × climax multiplier OR current volume × (high − low) ≥ the highest prior-bar volume × (high − low) over that lookback
Calculation: Intrabar delta = sum of lower-timeframe volume from bars in the current chart bar that close above their open, minus sum from bars that close below their open; split volume equally for bars that close at their open. Use lower-timeframe bars, completed when the chart bar closes.
