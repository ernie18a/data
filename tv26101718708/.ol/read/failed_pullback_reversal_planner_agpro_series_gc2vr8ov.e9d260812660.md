Calculation: ATR = average true range; EMA = exponential moving average.
Calculation: Former bullish trend = fast EMA > base EMA > slow EMA and (base EMA − base EMA 5 bars earlier) / ATR ≥ minimum slope threshold; former bearish trend = fast EMA < base EMA < slow EMA and that slope ≤ negative minimum slope threshold.
Calculation: Bullish-trend pullback touch = former bullish trend and low ≤ fast EMA + ATR × reach threshold and close < fast EMA + ATR × 0.30; bearish-trend pullback touch = former bearish trend and high ≥ fast EMA − ATR × reach threshold and close > fast EMA − ATR × 0.30.
Calculation: Active reversal direction = bearish if a bullish-trend touch occurred within the failure window and is at least as recent as any bearish-trend touch within that window; otherwise bullish if a bearish-trend touch occurred within the window; otherwise none.
Calculation: Control rail = base EMA − ATR × control buffer for bearish direction, or base EMA + ATR × control buffer for bullish direction. Invalidation rail = fast EMA + ATR × invalidation buffer for bearish direction, or fast EMA − ATR × invalidation buffer for bullish direction.
Calculation: Failure score = rounded value clamped to 0–100 of 0.23 × pullback-depth score + 0.27 × control-break score + 0.20 × against-trend close score + 0.15 × volume score + 0.15 × follow-through score. Pullback-depth score linearly rises from 0 at depth 0.10 ATR to 100 at ideal depth, then linearly falls to 0 at excessive depth; depth is the distance from fast EMA to the lowest low in the failure window for bearish direction, or from fast EMA to the highest high for bullish direction, divided by ATR. Control-break score rises linearly from 0 to 100 as close moves beyond the control rail from 0 to the control-break threshold in ATR units. Against-trend close score is 100 × (1 − close location) / close-location threshold for bearish direction, or 100 × close location / threshold for bullish direction, clamped to 0–100; multiply by 1 when the candle closes against the former trend (close < open for bearish, close > open for bullish), otherwise by 0.55. Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment). Volume score = 100 × current volume / (volume moving average × volume threshold), clamped to 0–100. Follow-through score rises linearly from 0 to 100 as close extends beyond the lowest low of the prior 3 bars for bearish direction, or the highest high of the prior 3 bars for bullish direction, from 0 to the follow-through threshold in ATR units, clamped to 0–100.
Calculation: Pullback watch = active reversal direction exists, no control loss, close has not crossed the invalidation rail, and failure score ≥ watch threshold. Control loss = close is beyond the control rail in the reversal direction; control-loss review = control loss, no reversal warning, no invalidation, and failure score ≥ watch threshold. Reversal warning = confirmed bar, control loss, candle closes against former trend, volume meets the selected confirmation mode, strict-mode requirements are met when selected, close has not crossed invalidation rail, and failure score ≥ minimum threshold. Strict-mode requirements = follow-through score ≥ 45, target room ≥ 0.60 ATR, and pullback-depth score ≥ 45. Close-only mode skips volume confirmation; other modes require volume ratio ≥ volume threshold. Bar confirmation uses the completed bar when close confirmation is enabled.
Calculation: Follow-through = close is below the lowest low of the prior 3 bars for bearish direction, or above the highest high of the prior 3 bars for bullish direction. Follow-through alert condition = confirmed bar, follow-through, control loss, and failure score ≥ alert threshold.
Long: active reversal direction is bullish AND (pullback watch OR control-loss review OR reversal warning OR follow-through alert condition)
Short: active reversal direction is bearish AND (pullback watch OR control-loss review OR reversal warning OR follow-through alert condition)
Exit: long, active reversal direction is bullish AND close < invalidation rail
Exit: short, active reversal direction is bearish AND close > invalidation rail
