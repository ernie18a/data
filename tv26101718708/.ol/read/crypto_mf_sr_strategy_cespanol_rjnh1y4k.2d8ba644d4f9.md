Calculation: Support/resistance levels = Up to 15 levels, each created at a close that is the highest or lowest close over a centered window of 2 × pivot lookback + 1 bars, using the close pivot lookback bars ago. Exclude a pivot if any active level is crossed and closed back across by a bar at offset pivot lookback or pivot lookback + 1, or if a bar at offset pivot lookback or pivot lookback − 1 approaches within the frontrun threshold while closing on the same side. Count each open/close cross of an active level; remove it after the configured maximum breaks or when its percentage distance from close reaches 30 × the frontrun threshold. The frontrun threshold is half of ATR(20) as a percentage of close.
Long: Within the configured time window, with no open trade, positive close, and positive equity: open > support level AND low < support level + (10 × minimum tick × test sensitivity) AND close > support level; OR open < resistance level AND close > resistance level.
Short: open < resistance level AND high > resistance level − (10 × minimum tick × test sensitivity) AND close < resistance level; OR open > support level AND close < support level.
Exit: Long, resistance test OR (stop loss enabled and greater than maximum safety-order deviation and trailing take profit inactive AND low < stop level) OR (trailing take profit disabled AND high reaches take-profit level) OR (trailing take profit active AND low <= trailing level)
Exit: Short, (stop loss enabled and greater than maximum safety-order deviation and trailing take profit inactive AND high > stop level) OR (trailing take profit disabled AND low reaches take-profit level) OR (trailing take profit active AND high >= trailing level)
