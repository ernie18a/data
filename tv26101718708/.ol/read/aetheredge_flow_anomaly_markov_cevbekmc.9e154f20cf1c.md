Calculation: Anomaly event = squared Mahalanobis distance of (log(volume + 1), absolute close-to-close change divided by ATR) from its online exponentially weighted mean and covariance exceeds the anomaly threshold AND log(volume + 1) exceeds its prior mean; update the mean and covariance on each completed chart bar.
Calculation: Momentum state = one of five ordered buckets of the chart-bar close-to-close change standardized by its moving average and standard deviation.
Calculation: Expected-direction score = sum of each possible next momentum state’s value (state index centered on flat) multiplied by its probability in the first-order transition row for the current momentum state and current anomaly status. Initialize transition counts with a Laplace prior; on each completed chart bar, decay all counts and add the transition from the previous state using the previous bar’s anomaly status, then normalize the current row. Use completed chart bars only.
Long: Completed chart bar AND after the warmup period AND anomaly event AND expected-direction score exceeds the minimum score threshold
Short: Completed chart bar AND after the warmup period AND anomaly event AND expected-direction score is below the negative minimum score threshold
