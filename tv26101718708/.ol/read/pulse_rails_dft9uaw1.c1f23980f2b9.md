Calculation: Energy Wave = Compute OBV by cumulatively adding volume when close rises, subtracting volume when close falls, and adding zero when unchanged; compute OBV pressure as the 8-bar rate of change of the 13-bar EMA of OBV, momentum as the 8-bar rate of change of close, MACD histogram as the difference between the MACD line and its signal EMA, and ATR energy as π × ATR². Normalize each of OBV pressure, momentum, MACD histogram, and ATR energy over the energy lookback to 0–100 using 100 × (value − rolling low) / (rolling high − rolling low), or 50 when rolling high equals rolling low. Energy Wave = EMA(0.35 × normalized OBV pressure + 0.35 × normalized momentum + 0.20 × normalized MACD histogram + 0.10 × normalized ATR energy, wave smoothing length) − 50.
Calculation: Wave Signal = EMA(Energy Wave, wave smoothing length).
Long: close > Supertrend AND close > EMA OR (close > Supertrend AND close > EMA AND Energy Wave > Wave Signal AND previous Energy Wave <= previous Wave Signal AND Energy Wave > -15) OR (close > Supertrend AND close > EMA AND Energy Wave > 15)
Short: close < Supertrend AND close < EMA OR (close < Supertrend AND close < EMA AND Energy Wave < Wave Signal AND previous Energy Wave >= previous Wave Signal AND Energy Wave < 15) OR (close < Supertrend AND close < EMA AND Energy Wave < -15)
