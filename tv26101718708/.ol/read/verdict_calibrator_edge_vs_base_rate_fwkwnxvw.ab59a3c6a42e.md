Calculation: MACD = difference between the shorter-lookback and longer-lookback EMAs of close; MACD signal line = EMA of MACD
Calculation: Smoothed stochastic %K = smoothed stochastic of close, high, and low; %D = moving average of smoothed %K
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = moving average of close ± a multiple of close's standard deviation over the same lookback
Calculation: Breakout direction state = +1 when close exceeds the prior bar's highest high over the lookback, −1 when close falls below the prior bar's lowest low over the lookback, otherwise retain the previous state
Calculation: Bollinger fade direction state = +1 after close falls below the lower band, −1 after close rises above the upper band, otherwise retain the previous state
Calculation: RSI reversal direction state = +1 when RSI crosses up out of the oversold region, −1 when RSI crosses down out of the overbought region, otherwise retain the previous state
Long: current signal direction is +1 AND previous signal direction was not +1 AND the minimum bar gap since the prior signal is met; signal direction is +1 when the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA OR MACD is above its signal line OR Supertrend is bullish OR RSI is above its midpoint OR RSI reversal direction state is +1 OR smoothed stochastic %K is above %D OR close exceeds the prior lookback high OR close is above VWAP OR Bollinger fade direction state is +1
Short: current signal direction is −1 AND previous signal direction was not −1 AND the minimum bar gap since the prior signal is met; signal direction is −1 when the shorter-lookback EMA is below the longer-lookback EMA OR MACD is below its signal line OR Supertrend is bearish OR RSI is below its midpoint OR RSI reversal direction state is −1 OR smoothed stochastic %K is below %D OR close falls below the prior lookback low OR close is below VWAP OR Bollinger fade direction state is −1
