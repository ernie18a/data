Calculation: Trend score = weighted sum of directional states (+1 when a timeframe's close is above an EMA, −1 otherwise) across multiple timeframes and EMA lengths; weights increase with timeframe length and EMA length. Higher-timeframe bars use the still-forming bar; lower-timeframe bars use the latest bar available within the chart bar.
Long: Trend score > 0 AND selected main-timeframe close > its 20-period EMA AND selected main-timeframe close > its 50-period EMA AND the close on the trigger timeframe one step lower than the selected main timeframe > its 20-period EMA OR any EMA crosses above another EMA within the recent-cross lookback on any timeframe
Short: Trend score < 0 AND selected main-timeframe close < its 20-period EMA AND selected main-timeframe close < its 50-period EMA AND the close on the trigger timeframe one step lower than the selected main timeframe < its 20-period EMA OR any EMA crosses below another EMA within the recent-cross lookback on any timeframe
