Calculation: Previous-day lower threshold = previous completed daily bar low + (previous completed daily bar high − low) × zone-threshold parameter; daily bars are higher timeframe bars aggregated from the chart bars.
Calculation: Previous-day upper threshold = previous completed daily bar high − (previous completed daily bar high − low) × zone-threshold parameter; daily bars are higher timeframe bars aggregated from the chart bars.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current chart bar closes above its open, previous chart bar closes below its open, current open ≤ previous close, and current close ≥ previous open.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = current chart bar closes below its open, previous chart bar closes above its open, current open ≥ previous close, and current close ≤ previous open.
Calculation: Hammer = current chart bar’s lower wick > 2 × its body size and upper wick < 0.3 × its lower wick; body size is |close − open|, lower wick is low-to-body-low, and upper wick is body-high-to-high.
Calculation: Shooting star = current chart bar’s upper wick > 2 × its body size and lower wick < 0.3 × its upper wick; body size is |close − open|, lower wick is low-to-body-low, and upper wick is body-high-to-high.
Long: (bullish engulfing OR hammer) AND close ≤ previous-day lower threshold AND volume > its chart-bar SMA × volume-multiplier parameter AND (current bar index − last signal bar index) ≥ cooldown parameter; initialize last signal bar index to 0 and update it on either long or short signal.
Short: (bearish engulfing OR shooting star) AND close ≥ previous-day upper threshold AND volume > its chart-bar SMA × volume-multiplier parameter AND (current bar index − last signal bar index) ≥ cooldown parameter; initialize last signal bar index to 0 and update it on either long or short signal.
