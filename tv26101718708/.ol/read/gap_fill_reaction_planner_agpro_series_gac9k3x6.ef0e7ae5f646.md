Calculation: ATR = average true range of the chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Minimum gap distance = ATR × the configured minimum-gap factor.
Calculation: Upward gap zone = the first qualifying enabled pattern in this priority order: open − previous close ≥ minimum gap distance; low − previous high ≥ minimum gap distance; min(open, close) − max(previous open, previous close) ≥ minimum gap distance; or close > previous high and close − previous close ≥ ATR × the greater of four times the minimum-gap factor and the configured displacement floor. The wick, body, and displacement patterns are used only when enabled by the detection mode; the classic open gap is always eligible. Zone boundaries are respectively [open, previous close], [low, previous high], [min(open, close), max(previous open, previous close)], or [previous close + displacement height, previous close], where displacement height is the greater of minimum gap distance and the lesser of 35% of the absolute close change and 65% of ATR.
Calculation: Downward gap zone = the first qualifying enabled pattern in this priority order: previous close − open ≥ minimum gap distance; previous low − high ≥ minimum gap distance; min(previous open, previous close) − max(open, close) ≥ minimum gap distance; or close < previous low and previous close − close ≥ ATR × the greater of four times the minimum-gap factor and the configured displacement floor. The wick, body, and displacement patterns are used only when enabled by the detection mode; the classic open gap is always eligible. Zone boundaries are respectively [open, previous close], [previous low, high], [min(previous open, previous close), max(open, close)], or [previous close, previous close − displacement height], using the displacement-height formula above.
Calculation: Zone fill percentage = clamp(maximum fill reached so far, current fill, 0, 100), where current fill for an upward zone is 100 × clamp((zone top − low) / zone height, 0, 1), and for a downward zone is 100 × clamp((high − zone bottom) / zone height, 0, 1). Update fill and state only on a confirmed chart-timeframe bar after zone creation and within its configured projection window.
Calculation: Zone state = accepted if close is at or beyond the far boundary (upward: close ≤ zone bottom; downward: close ≥ zone top); otherwise rejected if fill percentage ≥ the configured rejection minimum, fill percentage is below the configured full-fill threshold, and close is beyond the near boundary away from the zone (upward: close > zone top; downward: close < zone bottom); otherwise fully filled if fill percentage ≥ the full-fill threshold; otherwise partial if fill percentage ≥ the rejection minimum; otherwise fresh. A rejected state remains rejected when the next calculated state would be partial. An accepted state remains accepted.
Calculation: Readiness score = round(clamp(size score + fill score + reaction score + participation score + recency score, 0, 100)), where size score = 20 × clamp((zone height / ATR) / 0.85, 0, 1); fill score = clamp(20 − abs(fill percentage − 58) × 0.25, 0, 20) when fill percentage ≥ rejection minimum, otherwise 5; reaction score = 30 for rejected state, otherwise 18 if the bar range intersects the zone, otherwise 14 for partial state, otherwise 6; participation score = 15 × clamp(0.60 × clamp(((high − low) / ATR) / 1.35, 0, 1) + 0.40 × volume factor, 0, 1), where volume factor = clamp(volume / 20-bar average volume, 0, 2) / 2, or 0.50 if unavailable; recency score = 15 × clamp(1 − zone age / configured projection length, 0, 1).
Long: An upward gap zone is created after a qualifying upward-gap pattern, subject to configured bar-spacing and confirmation rules, OR a tracked upward zone changes to rejected state with readiness score ≥ configured readiness threshold.
Short: A downward gap zone is created after a qualifying downward-gap pattern, subject to configured bar-spacing and confirmation rules, OR a tracked downward zone changes to rejected state with readiness score ≥ configured readiness threshold.
