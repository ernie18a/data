Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low with the configured number of lower/higher bars on both sides; use it only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low (use high/low instead of close in wick-break mode); it is a change of character if the prior structure was bearish/bullish, otherwise a break of structure.
Calculation: Structure leg = from the prior swing low to the bullish break price, or from the prior swing high to the bearish break price; extend its high/low with subsequent bars. Equilibrium = leg midpoint.
Calculation: OTE band = the configured retracement band of the active leg: for a bull leg, leg high minus the leg range times each configured retracement ratio; for a bear leg, leg low plus the leg range times each ratio.
Calculation: Bull/bear OTE touch = bull leg and current low within the bull OTE band / bear leg and current high within the bear OTE band.
Calculation: Bull/bear liquidity sweep = current low below the prior configured-lookback lowest low by at least the configured ATR depth, then close above that reference and above open / current high above the prior configured-lookback highest high by at least that depth, then close below that reference and below open. Recent sweep = this or either of the prior two bars.
Calculation: Bull/bear rejection = bullish/bearish close and (close−low)/(high−low) > 0.55 / (high−close)/(high−low) > 0.55; alternatively, a bullish/bearish close engulfs the prior bearish/bullish bar by closing beyond its open.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish/bearish bias = fast EMA above/below slow EMA and close above/below fast EMA, calculated from a higher timeframe using its current, possibly still-forming bar; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Confluence score = count of seven votes: structure agrees with direction; OTE touch; price close is below/above leg equilibrium; higher-timeframe bias agrees (or the filter is disabled); recent sweep agrees (when enabled); rejection agrees; and both ADX passes its configured minimum (or is disabled) and volume passes its configured moving-average expansion threshold (or is disabled).
Calculation: Bull/bear entry stop = the lower of swing low and leg low minus configured ATR buffer / the higher of swing high and leg high plus configured ATR buffer. Entry target = entry price plus/minus the stop risk times the configured reward multiple.
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish liquidity sweep OR ((bullish structure and bull leg and bull OTE touch and bull rejection) OR (bullish CHoCH within the configured window and recent bullish sweep and bull OTE touch and bull rejection)) AND close below equilibrium when the premium/discount filter is enabled AND confluence score meets its configured minimum AND session is active when enabled AND cooldown has elapsed.
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish liquidity sweep OR ((bearish structure and bear leg and bear OTE touch and bear rejection) OR (bearish CHoCH within the configured window and recent bearish sweep and bear OTE touch and bear rejection)) AND close above equilibrium when the premium/discount filter is enabled AND confluence score meets its configured minimum AND session is active when enabled AND cooldown has elapsed.
Exit: long, close at or below the bull entry stop OR high reaches the bull entry target OR bearish CHoCH.
Exit: short, close at or above the bear entry stop OR low reaches the bear entry target OR bullish CHoCH.
