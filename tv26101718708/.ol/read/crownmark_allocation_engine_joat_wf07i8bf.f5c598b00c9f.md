Calculation: Regime score = signed trend displacement (fast EMA minus slow EMA, scaled over slow lookback) × 35 + clamped z-score of the fast/slow EMA spread’s fast-lookback linear-regression slope × 10 + clamped ((ADX−20)/25) × 25 × spread sign + clamped (2×efficiency ratio−1) × 20 × spread sign + clamped z-score of volatility expansion × 5 × spread sign; efficiency ratio = absolute net close change / sum of absolute close changes; volatility expansion = close standard deviation / close SMA.
Calculation: Adaptive baseline = KAMA of close; corridor width = (0.65 × ATR(width lookback) + 0.35 × close standard deviation(width lookback)) × width multiplier; inner bands are halfway from baseline to outer bands.
Calculation: Rolling VWAP = sum(typical price × volume, auction lookback) / sum(volume, auction lookback); value area bands = rolling VWAP ± typical-price standard deviation × value-area width.
Calculation: ATR = average true range.
Calculation: Pressure = EMA of 100 × relative volume × (0.40 × clamped candle body/range + 0.35 × tanh(4 × body/ATR) + 0.25 × clamped close location); relative volume = volume / volume SMA. Pressure signal = EMA of pressure, with a longer smoothing length than pressure.
Calculation: Divergence oscillator = EMA over 3 bars of (1.2 × (14-bar RSI − 50) + 8-bar rate of change). Bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high higher than the prior confirmed price pivot high while its oscillator pivot high is lower; bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low lower than the prior confirmed price pivot low while its oscillator pivot low is higher. Recent divergence means the corresponding event occurred within the suppression lookback.
Long: regime score > bull threshold AND close > baseline AND baseline > prior baseline AND low <= inner upper band AND close > baseline − ATR × pullback tolerance AND close <= upper corridor AND close within value area (when acceptance is required) AND close >= rolling VWAP (when VWAP alignment is required) AND pressure > pressure threshold AND pressure > pressure signal AND (volatility expansion filter disabled OR range z-score > −0.10) AND (divergence guard disabled OR no recent bearish divergence) AND bar is confirmed AND position is flat or short.
Short: regime score < bear threshold AND close < baseline AND baseline < prior baseline AND high >= inner lower band AND close < baseline + ATR × pullback tolerance AND close >= lower corridor AND close within value area (when acceptance is required) AND close <= rolling VWAP (when VWAP alignment is required) AND pressure < −pressure threshold AND pressure < pressure signal AND (volatility expansion filter disabled OR range z-score > −0.10) AND (divergence guard disabled OR no recent bullish divergence) AND bar is confirmed AND position is flat or long.
Exit: Long, low <= max(entry price − ATR × stop multiple, lower corridor − ATR × 0.25, baseline − ATR × trail multiple) OR high >= entry price + ATR × target multiple OR regime score < 0 OR pressure < 0 OR close < baseline OR bars in trade >= maximum duration OR recent bearish divergence OR opposite short signal (when enabled).
Exit: Short, high >= min(entry price + ATR × stop multiple, upper corridor + ATR × 0.25, baseline + ATR × trail multiple) OR low <= entry price − ATR × target multiple OR regime score > 0 OR pressure > 0 OR close > baseline OR bars in trade >= maximum duration OR recent bullish divergence OR opposite long signal (when enabled).
