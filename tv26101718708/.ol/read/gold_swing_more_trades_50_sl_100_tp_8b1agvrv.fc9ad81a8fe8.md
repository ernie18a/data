Short: shorts enabled AND datetime is within the configured weekday trading session AND current bar index − last entry bar index > configured cooldown length (or no prior entry) AND close < VWAP AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < EMA(close, longer lookback) AND EMA(close, shorter lookback) < its value two bars earlier AND high ≥ EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close < EMA(close, shorter lookback) AND close < open AND RSI(close, configured lookback) ≤ configured short threshold AND position is flat or long
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured take-profit distance
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured take-profit distance
