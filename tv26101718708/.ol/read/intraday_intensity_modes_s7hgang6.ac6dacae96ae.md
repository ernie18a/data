Calculation: Color intensity = sum of the latest Osc Length values of ((2 × close − H − L) ÷ (H − L)) × V; on intraday charts H and L are the session high and low through the current bar and V is session volume through the current bar, while on non-intraday charts H, L, and V are the current bar’s high, low, and volume.
Long: Color intensity ≥ 0
Short: Color intensity < 0
