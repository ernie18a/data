Calculation: Estimated Delta = volume * ((close - low) - (high - close)) / (high - low) when high > low, otherwise 0
Calculation: Aggression Ratio = absolute Estimated Delta / moving average of absolute Estimated Delta over its configured lookback
Calculation: Delta Change = current Estimated Delta - previous chart bar's Estimated Delta
Calculation: Shock Ratio = absolute Delta Change / moving average of absolute Delta Change over its configured lookback
