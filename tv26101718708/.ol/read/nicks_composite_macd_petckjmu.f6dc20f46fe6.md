Calculation: Composite MACD histogram = average, across the chart timeframe and enabled higher timeframes, of (EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback)), minus a moving average of that composite MACD; use each higher timeframe’s bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a local high/low in the composite MACD histogram or price (high for highs, low for lows), confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides.
Long: On a confirmed histogram pivot low, current price pivot low < most recent earlier price pivot low within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot low > most recent earlier histogram pivot low within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot low < 0 OR current price pivot low > most recent earlier price pivot low within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot low < most recent earlier histogram pivot low within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot low < 0
Short: On a confirmed histogram pivot high, current price pivot high > most recent earlier price pivot high within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot high < most recent earlier histogram pivot high within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot high > 0 OR current price pivot high < most recent earlier price pivot high within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot high > most recent earlier histogram pivot high within the divergence lookback AND current histogram pivot high > 0
