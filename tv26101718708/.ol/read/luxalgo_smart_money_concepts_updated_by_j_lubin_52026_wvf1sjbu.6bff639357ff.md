Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = when the leg state switches, the pivot is the high/low n bars earlier; a bearish leg begins when that earlier high exceeds the highest high of the latest n bars, and a bullish leg begins when that earlier low is below the lowest low of the latest n bars. Swing pivots use a longer lookback than internal pivots.
Calculation: Auto FVG threshold = twice the cumulative mean of absolute completed FVG-timeframe candle percentage changes, sampled when a new FVG-timeframe bar begins.
Long: Chart close crosses above an unbroken swing pivot high OR chart close crosses above an unbroken internal pivot high that differs from the swing pivot high OR a newly confirmed pivot low is within the equality threshold of the prior pivot low OR the current FVG-timeframe bar's low is above the high from two FVG-timeframe bars earlier AND the previous completed FVG-timeframe close is above that high AND its percentage change exceeds the auto FVG threshold AND a new FVG-timeframe bar begins
Short: Chart close crosses below an unbroken swing pivot low OR chart close crosses below an unbroken internal pivot low that differs from the swing pivot low OR a newly confirmed pivot high is within the equality threshold of the prior pivot high OR the current FVG-timeframe bar's high is below the low from two FVG-timeframe bars earlier AND the previous completed FVG-timeframe close is below that low AND the negative of its percentage change exceeds the auto FVG threshold AND a new FVG-timeframe bar begins
