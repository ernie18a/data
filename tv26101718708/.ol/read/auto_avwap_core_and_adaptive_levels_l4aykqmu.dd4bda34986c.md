Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI %K = configurable moving average of the stochastic value of RSI(HLC3); %D = simple moving average of %K over a shorter smoothing lookback. The overbought threshold is above the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (anchor price × volume) / cumulative volume from the confirmed anchor; use High for resistance and Low for support.
Calculation: Local structure low/high = lowest Low / highest High over a configurable lookback of prior bars.
Long: A support Adaptive AVWAP is created after price closes at least a configured ATR distance above its candidate low following a bullish %K/%D crossover, with the candidate low remaining unbroken for the configured bars and, when selected by the confirmation mode, a close above its captured local structure high; the candidate episode began with %D below the oversold threshold, has not expired, price moved the required ATR distance below the newest valid support AVWAP, the minimum bars since the newest anchor elapsed, an Adaptive slot is available, and the candidate low meets the configured spacing rule (progressively higher than the newest support anchor when progressive anchors are enabled).
Long: Any active support Core or Adaptive AVWAP is interacted with (Low ≤ AVWAP + configured ATR touch tolerance) AND Close ≥ AVWAP + configured ATR rejection distance.
Long: For any active support Core or Adaptive AVWAP, its violation source (Close, Open, or Low) was below the AVWAP for at least the configured partial-violation bars but fewer than the full invalidation bars, and the current violation source is at or above the AVWAP.
Short: A resistance Adaptive AVWAP is created after price closes at least a configured ATR distance below its candidate high following a bearish %K/%D crossover, with the candidate high remaining unbroken for the configured bars and, when selected by the confirmation mode, a close below its captured local structure low; the candidate episode began with %D above the overbought threshold, has not expired, price moved the required ATR distance above the newest valid resistance AVWAP, the minimum bars since the newest anchor elapsed, an Adaptive slot is available, and the candidate high meets the configured spacing rule (progressively lower than the newest resistance anchor when progressive anchors are enabled).
Short: Any active resistance Core or Adaptive AVWAP is interacted with (High ≥ AVWAP − configured ATR touch tolerance) AND Close ≤ AVWAP − configured ATR rejection distance.
Short: For any active resistance Core or Adaptive AVWAP, its violation source (Close, Open, or High) was above the AVWAP for at least the configured partial-violation bars but fewer than the full invalidation bars, and the current violation source is at or below the AVWAP.
Exit: long, an active support Core or Adaptive AVWAP is below its violation source (Close, Open, or Low) for the configured number of consecutive bars; reset the count when this condition is false.
Exit: short, an active resistance Core or Adaptive AVWAP is above its violation source (Close, Open, or High) for the configured number of consecutive bars; reset the count when this condition is false.
