Calculation: Volume baseline = simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Current-price volume-profile POC = center of the highest-volume price bin across a configurable lookback of chart-timeframe bars; divide the highest high to lowest low into configurable equal-width bins and allocate each bar’s volume to each bin in proportion to the overlap of its high-low range with that bin.
Long: (datetime is within the configured recent lookback window AND volume > configured minimum actual volume AND volume >= volume baseline × configured high-volume multiplier AND (low-wick length / (high - low) >= configured minimum wick fraction) AND (abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= configured maximum body fraction OR low-wick length / (high - low) >= configured strong-wick fraction)) OR (enough chart-timeframe bars exist for the configured volume-profile lookback AND close <= current-price volume-profile POC)
Short: (datetime is within the configured recent lookback window AND volume > configured minimum actual volume AND volume >= volume baseline × configured high-volume multiplier AND (upper-wick length / (high - low) >= configured minimum wick fraction) AND (abs(close - open) / (high - low) <= configured maximum body fraction OR upper-wick length / (high - low) >= configured strong-wick fraction)) OR (enough chart-timeframe bars exist for the configured volume-profile lookback AND close > current-price volume-profile POC)
