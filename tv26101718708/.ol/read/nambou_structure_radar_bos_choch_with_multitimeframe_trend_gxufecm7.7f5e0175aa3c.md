Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar’s high is higher than the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after those bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar’s low is lower than the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after those bars complete.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, the configured break source (close or high) > the latest unbroken confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing high > the previous confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low > the previous confirmed swing low OR chart-timeframe structure trend = bullish OR, on a higher timeframe’s last completed bar, structure trend changes to bullish.
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, the configured break source (close or low) < the latest unbroken confirmed swing low OR a newly confirmed swing high < the previous confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low < the previous confirmed swing low OR chart-timeframe structure trend = bearish OR, on a higher timeframe’s last completed bar, structure trend changes to bearish.
