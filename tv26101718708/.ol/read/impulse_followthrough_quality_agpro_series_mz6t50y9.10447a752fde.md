Calculation: Impulse range = high - low; body efficiency = abs(close - open) / Impulse range; bullish edge close = (close - low) / Impulse range; bearish edge close = 1 - bullish edge close
Calculation: Impulse ATR ratio = Impulse range / ATR; ATR = average true range
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Normalized rising score(x, weak, strong) = clamp(100 * (x - weak) / (strong - weak), 0, 100); normalized falling score(x, good, bad) = clamp(100 * (bad - x) / (bad - good), 0, 100); peak score(x, low, ideal, high) = normalized rising score(x, low, ideal) when x <= ideal, otherwise normalized falling score(x, ideal, high)
Calculation: Impulse size score = peak score(Impulse ATR ratio, 0.70 * minimum impulse ATR, minimum impulse ATR + 0.75, oversized impulse ATR); body score = normalized rising score(body efficiency, 0.85 * minimum body efficiency, 0.92); edge-close score = normalized rising score(directional edge close, 0.85 * minimum close location, 0.95); volume score = peak score(volume ratio, 0.65, 1.35, climax volume ratio)
Calculation: Directional trend score = clamp(price score + EMA stack score + slope score, 0, 100). For bullish direction, price score is 42 when close >= slow EMA, otherwise 16; stack score is 34 when fast EMA >= slow EMA, otherwise 0; slope score is 24 * normalized rising score(abs(slow EMA - its value slope-bars ago) / ATR, 0.01, 0.30) when that slope is nonnegative, otherwise 0. For bearish direction, use close <= slow EMA, fast EMA <= slow EMA, and nonpositive slope respectively. Fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Directional impulse quality score = clamp(0.30 * impulse size score + 0.24 * body score + 0.22 * directional edge-close score + 0.10 * volume score + 0.14 * directional trend score, 0, 100)
Calculation: Follow-through progress = best favorable price since impulse / impulse ATR, measured from impulse close; follow-through closes = count within the follow-through window closing beyond the impulse close in the impulse direction; pullback depth = greatest retracement from impulse close against the impulse direction / impulse range
Calculation: Follow-through score = clamp(0.50 * normalized rising score(follow-through progress, 0.25 * required progress ATR, 1.65 * required progress ATR) + 0.30 * normalized rising score(follow-through closes, 1, minimum follow-through closes) + 0.20 * normalized falling score(pullback depth, 0.08, maximum pullback ratio), 0, 100)
Calculation: Target guide = impulse close + direction * impulse range * target range multiple; failure rail = bullish impulse low - impulse ATR * failure buffer ATR, or bearish impulse high + impulse ATR * failure buffer ATR
Calculation: Quality score = rounded clamp(0.36 * impulse quality score + 0.38 * follow-through score + 0.10 * best volume score + 0.08 * pullback score + 0.08 * target-room score, 0, 100), where pullback score = normalized falling score(pullback depth, 0.08, maximum pullback ratio), target-room score = normalized rising score((target guide - evaluation close) * direction / impulse ATR, 0.10, 1.40), and evaluation close is the failure/target resolution close after resolution, otherwise current close. Cap at 38 after failure; cap at 52 when past the follow-through window and follow-through score < 45.
Long: bullish impulse event: close > open AND Impulse ATR ratio >= minimum impulse ATR AND body efficiency >= minimum body efficiency AND bullish edge close >= minimum close location AND bullish impulse quality score >= minimum impulse score AND at least the new-impulse cooldown bars since the prior impulse (or no prior impulse); OR a new transition to Valid FT for an active bullish impulse: within tracking bars AND not failed or target-reached AND pullback depth < maximum pullback ratio AND quality score >= valid threshold AND follow-through score >= 55 AND follow-through closes >= minimum follow-through closes
