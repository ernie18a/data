Calculation: Bullish engulfing = latest completed higher-timeframe candle closes above its open, preceding completed higher-timeframe candle closes below its open, latest close >= preceding open, and latest open <= preceding close.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = latest completed higher-timeframe candle closes below its open, preceding completed higher-timeframe candle closes above its open, latest close <= preceding open, and latest open >= preceding close.
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = lower wick > 0.58 of the latest completed higher-timeframe candle's range and body < 0.28 of its range.
Calculation: Bearish pin bar = upper wick > 0.58 of the latest completed higher-timeframe candle's range and body < 0.28 of its range.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = at a confirmed local price/RSI pivot low, the current pivot low is lower than the preceding confirmed pivot low while its RSI value is higher than at that preceding pivot. Confirm pivots using configurable left and right chart bars; recognize divergence when the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = at a confirmed local price/RSI pivot high, the current pivot high is higher than the preceding confirmed pivot high while its RSI value is lower than at that preceding pivot. Confirm pivots using configurable left and right chart bars; recognize divergence when the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = bullish-pattern weight when a bullish engulfing or bullish pin bar occurs, plus bullish-divergence weight when bullish RSI divergence occurs, plus distance weight × max(0, 1 − (current low − lowest low over a configurable lookback) / (a configurable multiple of ATR)).
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = bearish-pattern weight when a bearish engulfing or bearish pin bar occurs, plus bearish-divergence weight when bearish RSI divergence occurs, plus distance weight × max(0, 1 − (highest high over a configurable lookback − current high) / (a configurable multiple of ATR)).
Long: At the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period, bullish engulfing OR bullish pin bar on the latest completed higher-timeframe candle; OR bullish RSI divergence; OR bullish RSI divergence AND bullish confluence score >= bearish confluence score; OR bullish confluence score − bearish confluence score >= 0.
Short: At the first chart bar of a new higher-timeframe period, bearish engulfing OR bearish pin bar on the latest completed higher-timeframe candle; OR bearish RSI divergence; OR bearish RSI divergence AND bearish confluence score >= bullish confluence score; OR bullish confluence score − bearish confluence score < 0.
