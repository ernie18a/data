Calculation: Supertrend = initialize trend bullish and stop at lower band; each bar, ATR bands are midpoint (high + low)/2 ± factor × ATR. Trail the upper band to the lower of its current value and prior upper band unless prior close exceeds prior upper band; trail the lower band to the higher of its current value and prior lower band unless prior close falls below prior lower band. In bullish trend, switch bearish if close falls below lower band, otherwise remain bullish; in bearish trend, switch bullish if close exceeds upper band, otherwise remain bearish. The stop is the lower band in bullish trend and upper band in bearish trend.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Supertrend = Supertrend calculated on aggregated bars of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe Supertrend = Supertrend calculated on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: Not already in a trade AND higher-timeframe Supertrend is bullish AND chart-timeframe Supertrend has just changed from bearish to bullish.
Exit: Long, low ≤ the chart-timeframe Supertrend value recorded at entry OR high ≥ entry price + (entry price − recorded chart-timeframe Supertrend value) × reward-to-risk ratio OR chart-timeframe Supertrend has just changed from bullish to bearish.
