Long: low > high two bars earlier OR a confirmed swing low forms using the order-block lookback OR low < the latest confirmed sweep-lookback swing low and close > that low OR close > the latest confirmed MSS-lookback swing high
Short: high < low two bars earlier OR a confirmed swing high forms using the order-block lookback OR high > the latest confirmed sweep-lookback swing high and close < that high OR close < the latest confirmed MSS-lookback swing low
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low (order-block lookback) = a high/low strictly greater/less than all highs/lows across the lookback bars on both sides; confirmed after the bars on the right have completed. This lookback is longer than the sweep lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low (sweep lookback) = a high/low strictly greater/less than all highs/lows across the lookback bars on both sides; track the latest confirmed high and low, replacing each with a newer one and clearing it after its sweep signal. This lookback is shorter than the order-block lookback and longer than the MSS lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low (MSS lookback) = a high/low strictly greater/less than all highs/lows across the lookback bars on both sides; track the latest confirmed high and low, replacing each with a newer one and clearing it after its MSS signal. This is the shortest of these lookbacks.
