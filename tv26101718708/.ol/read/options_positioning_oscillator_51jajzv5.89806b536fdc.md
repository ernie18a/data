Calculation: Positioning oscillator = EMA of the weighted mean of active sign-aligned z-scores: subtract implied-volatility rank centered at 50 and scaled by 28.8675; subtract the z-score of implied volatility minus its EMA; add the z-score of futures basis ((futures close − spot close) / spot close × 100); optionally add the z-score of put-call ratio (or subtract it, depending on its direction setting) and subtract the z-score of option skew. Normalize basis, optional legs, and the term deviation with a rolling mean and standard deviation; normalize term deviation on the higher volatility-leg timeframe using its own rolling mean and standard deviation. Calculate implied-volatility rank and term deviation on that higher timeframe, using its still-forming bar; use chart-timeframe spot and futures closes. Weights determine the mean; output smoothing uses an EMA.
Long: Positioning oscillator crosses above the negative lean threshold (current > −threshold AND previous ≤ −threshold) OR, at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, oscillator pivot value > preceding oscillator pivot low AND price low at that pivot < preceding price pivot low OR oscillator pivot value < preceding oscillator pivot low AND price low at that pivot > preceding price pivot low.
Short: Positioning oscillator crosses below the positive lean threshold (current < threshold AND previous ≥ threshold) OR, at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, oscillator pivot value < preceding oscillator pivot high AND price high at that pivot > preceding price pivot high OR oscillator pivot value > preceding oscillator pivot high AND price high at that pivot < preceding price pivot high.
