Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 multiplied by volume since the start of the current day, divided by cumulative volume since that start
Calculation: Weekly VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 multiplied by volume since the start of the current week, divided by cumulative volume since that start
Calculation: Trend bias = bullish if at least 3 trend votes are positive, bearish if at least 3 are negative, otherwise neutral; votes are +1 or -1 for fast EMA above/below intermediate EMA, intermediate EMA above/below slow EMA, fast EMA rising/falling over a lookback, intermediate EMA rising/falling over that lookback, and close above/below session VWAP; optionally add a vote for close above/below weekly VWAP
Calculation: Expansion regime = ADX at least its floor AND choppiness below its ceiling AND compression ratio not below its threshold
Calculation: Compression ratio = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback, divided by ATR multiplied by that lookback
Calculation: Bullish structure break = confirmed close above the latest confirmed pivot high, with previous close at or below that pivot high
Calculation: Bearish structure break = confirmed close below the latest confirmed pivot low, with previous close at or above that pivot low
Calculation: Sell-side sweep = confirmed low below the latest confirmed pivot low AND close above that pivot low
Calculation: Buy-side sweep = confirmed high above the latest confirmed pivot high AND close below that pivot high
Calculation: Structural bias = +1 after a bullish structure break; -1 after a bearish structure break; otherwise retain its prior value
Calculation: Momentum spread = average of the three shortest-period RSI values after lag-reduced EMA smoothing and subtracting 50, minus the average of the three longer-period RSI values after the same smoothing and subtraction; the longer periods exceed the shorter periods
Calculation: Momentum bias = bullish when momentum spread exceeds its positive threshold, bearish when below its negative threshold, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Volume delta proxy = (close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment) multiplied by volume
Calculation: Pressure composite = 0.35 × z-score of volume delta proxy + 0.25 × z-score of close minus session VWAP + 0.20 × z-score of close minus average of fast, intermediate, and slow EMAs - 0.20 × z-score of ATR
Calculation: Crowding composite = 0.50 × z-score of RSI(14) minus 50 + 0.50 × z-score of close minus weekly VWAP
Calculation: Liquidation risk = clamp(50 + 14 × absolute crowding composite + 10 × max(z-score of ATR, 0) + 8 when either side is swept, to 0–100
Calculation: Long score = weighted sum of points for expansion regime, bullish trend bias, positive structural bias, bullish structure break, sell-side sweep, bullish momentum bias, positive pressure bias, close above session VWAP, and (when enabled) close above weekly VWAP; subtract a penalty when liquidation risk is not allowed
Calculation: Short score = weighted sum of points for expansion regime, bearish trend bias, negative structural bias, bearish structure break, buy-side sweep, bearish momentum bias, negative pressure bias, close below session VWAP, and (when enabled) close below weekly VWAP; subtract a penalty when liquidation risk is not allowed
Calculation: Soft expansion = expansion regime OR ADX at least 60% of its floor
Calculation: Effective long stop = the higher of entry price minus ATR times stop multiple and, after trailing activates, highest high over the last 5 bars minus ATR times trail multiple; trailing activates when close reaches entry price plus ATR times stop multiple times trail trigger
Calculation: Effective short stop = the lower of entry price plus ATR times stop multiple and, after trailing activates, lowest low over the last 5 bars plus ATR times trail multiple; trailing activates when close reaches entry price minus ATR times stop multiple times trail trigger
Long: Confirmed bar AND cooldown ready AND long score at least entry threshold AND long score at least short score minus 8 AND soft expansion AND trend bias is neutral or bullish AND momentum bias is neutral or bullish AND pressure composite > -0.50 AND (bullish structure break OR sell-side sweep OR fast ribbon crosses above slow ribbon OR close crosses above fast EMA) AND liquidation risk < 95 AND position is flat or short
Short: Confirmed bar AND cooldown ready AND short score at least entry threshold AND short score at least long score minus 8 AND soft expansion AND trend bias is neutral or bearish AND momentum bias is neutral or bearish AND pressure composite < 0.50 AND (bearish structure break OR buy-side sweep OR fast ribbon crosses below slow ribbon OR close crosses below fast EMA) AND liquidation risk < 95 AND position is flat or long
Exit: Long, low <= effective long stop OR high >= entry price + ATR times target multiple OR (confirmed bar AND trend bias is not bullish OR momentum bias is bearish OR pressure bias is bearish OR liquidation risk is not allowed)
Exit: Short, high >= effective short stop OR low <= entry price - ATR times target multiple OR (confirmed bar AND trend bias is not bearish OR momentum bias is bullish OR pressure bias is bullish OR liquidation risk is not allowed)
