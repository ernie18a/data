Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average
Calculation: ATR compression = ATR / its moving average
Calculation: Bollinger width compression = current Bollinger Band width / its moving average
Calculation: Base range % = (highest high − lowest low) / lowest low × 100 over the base lookback
Calculation: Shelf variation % = (highest − lowest) / highest × 100 over the prior shelf lookback; use highs for resistance and lows for support
Calculation: Higher lows = the lowest low in the oldest third < the lowest low in the middle third ≤ the lowest low in the most recent third; for continuation setups, replace < with ≤
Calculation: Lower highs = the highest high in the oldest third > the highest high in the middle third ≥ the highest high in the most recent third; for continuation setups, replace > with ≥
Calculation: Long quality score = count of true conditions: trend is valid (close > fast EMA, or, in strict mode, fast EMA > slow EMA); resistance shelf variation ≤ its limit; base range ≤ its limit; higher lows; ATR compression ≤ its limit; Bollinger width compression ≤ its limit; relative volume ≥ its limit; RSI ≥ its minimum
Calculation: Short quality score = count of true conditions: trend is valid (close < fast EMA, or, in strict mode, fast EMA < slow EMA); support shelf variation ≤ its limit; base range ≤ its limit; lower highs; ATR compression ≤ its limit; Bollinger width compression ≤ its limit; relative volume ≥ its limit; RSI ≤ its maximum
Calculation: Continuation quality score = count of true conditions: close on the directional side of the fast EMA; corresponding resistance or support shelf variation ≤ its limit; continuation base range ≤ its limit; continuation higher lows or lower highs; relative volume ≥ its limit; RSI meets the directional minimum or maximum; ATR compression or Bollinger width compression ≤ its limit
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of the prior stop and each confirmed swing low, initialized to the lower of the entry bar low and close minus an ATR multiple
Calculation: Short trailing stop = minimum of the prior stop and each confirmed swing high, initialized to the higher of the entry bar high and close plus an ATR multiple
Long: Long direction is enabled AND (market trend filter disabled OR close > selected slow EMA AND selected fast EMA > selected slow EMA) AND cooldown elapsed AND not in a short position AND (not already long OR continuation adds enabled) AND ((close > prior resistance shelf × (1 + buffer) AND close > open AND long quality score ≥ its minimum AND close is within its maximum allowed distance above the fast EMA) OR (continuation scanner enabled AND close > prior continuation resistance × (1 + buffer) AND close > open AND continuation quality score ≥ its minimum AND close is within its maximum allowed distance above the fast EMA))
Short: Short direction is enabled AND (market trend filter disabled OR close < selected slow EMA AND selected fast EMA < selected slow EMA) AND cooldown elapsed AND not in a long position AND (not already short OR continuation adds enabled) AND ((close < prior support shelf × (1 − buffer) AND close < open AND short quality score ≥ its minimum AND close is within its maximum allowed distance below the fast EMA) OR (continuation scanner enabled AND close < prior continuation support × (1 − buffer) AND close < open AND continuation quality score ≥ its minimum AND close is within its maximum allowed distance below the fast EMA))
Exit: long, close < long trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled; otherwise low < long trailing stop
Exit: short, close > short trailing stop when close-only exits are enabled; otherwise high > short trailing stop
