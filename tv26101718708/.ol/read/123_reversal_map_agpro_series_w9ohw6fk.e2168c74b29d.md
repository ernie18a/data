Calculation: Confirmed pivot = a chart-timeframe high or low that is higher or lower than the highs or lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish 1-2-3 structure = the latest three alternating confirmed pivots are low P1, high P2, low P3; P2−P1 ≥ ATR × minimum leg threshold; P3−P1 ≥ ATR × minimum hold threshold; and (P2−P3)/(P2−P1) is within the configured minimum and maximum retracement thresholds.
Calculation: Bearish 1-2-3 structure = the latest three alternating confirmed pivots are high P1, low P2, high P3; P1−P2 ≥ ATR × minimum leg threshold; P1−P3 ≥ ATR × minimum hold threshold; and (P3−P2)/(P1−P2) is within the configured minimum and maximum retracement thresholds.
Long: bullish 1-2-3 structure is newly confirmed OR an active bullish structure is unbroken and close > P2 + ATR × neckline buffer OR an active broken bullish structure is within the retest window, low ≤ P2 + ATR × retest pocket width, close ≥ P2 − ATR × retest pocket width, and close > P2.
Short: bearish 1-2-3 structure is newly confirmed OR an active bearish structure is unbroken and close < P2 − ATR × neckline buffer OR an active broken bearish structure is within the retest window, high ≥ P2 − ATR × retest pocket width, close ≤ P2 + ATR × retest pocket width, and close < P2.
Exit: long, active bullish structure is unbroken and low < P1 − ATR × invalidation buffer OR active broken bullish structure and close < P3 − ATR × invalidation buffer.
Exit: short, active bearish structure is unbroken and high > P1 + ATR × invalidation buffer OR active broken bearish structure and close > P3 + ATR × invalidation buffer.
