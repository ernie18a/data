Calculation: HTF trend = on the higher timeframe (use the configured timeframe if higher than the chart timeframe; otherwise use the chart timeframe), EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200), and close from the last completed bar; bullish when EMA(20) > EMA(50) > EMA(200) and close > EMA(20), bearish when EMA(20) < EMA(50) < EMA(200) and close < EMA(20).
Calculation: HTF demand/supply zone = on each of two higher anchor timeframes, the latest confirmed 5-left/5-right swing low/high, respectively, with a band of 0.20 × HTF ATR(14) and a trade reach of 0.85 × HTF ATR(14); a chart bar is in a zone when its range overlaps the band extended by the trade reach. Use last completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Strong pivot low/high = confirmed chart-timeframe 10-left/10-right pivot low/high.
Calculation: Demand/supply regime zone = in either HTF demand/supply zone OR within chart-timeframe ATR(14) of the latest strong pivot low/high OR at a valid or magnet approach to demand/supply OR a strong key demand/supply magnet exists below/above price.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish divergence = at a confirmed chart-timeframe 5-left/5-right pivot low/high, respectively, the latest pivot price is lower/higher than the previous pivot price while RSI(14) at the pivot is higher/lower.
Calculation: Key demand/supply magnet = nearest chart-timeframe pivot-cluster level below/above close with at least the minimum reaction strength; cluster 5-left/5-right and 10-left/10-right pivot lows/highs of the same type within 1.25 × ATR(14), incrementing reaction strength on each match (initial strengths 1 and 2, capped at 6), and use the stronger confirmed cluster. A magnet is strong at strength ≥ 4, or strength ≥ 3 with an order-block score ≥ 2; it is elite at strength ≥ 5, or strength ≥ 4 with order-block score ≥ 2. Order-block score is 1 for overlap with the latest matching displacement candle body, plus 1 if its stored relative volume ≥ the volume threshold, plus 1 if that displacement is at most 20 bars old; bullish displacement is a bullish close at least 1.6 × ATR above the lowest low of the last 6 bars, bearish displacement is a bearish close at least 1.6 × ATR below the highest high of the last 6 bars, and either requires relative volume ≥ 1.1 or a volume spike. The matching body is the most recent opposite-color candle among the prior 10 bars; overlap tolerance is the greater of half that body or 0.35 × ATR. Keep magnets until pruned after 6 × zone lookback bars without a zone touch; use sticky displayed magnets when enabled, removing demand on a close below its band and supply on a close above its band.
Calculation: At-key-demand/supply = demand/supply regime zone (including its value 5 bars earlier) AND the nearest matching key magnet is strong AND the current low/high is within 0.65/0.85 × ATR of that magnet.
Calculation: Bull/bear SFP at strong zone = low/high sweeps below/above the latest strong pivot low/high, then closes above/below it, closes bullish/bearish, and volume / its lookback SMA exceeds the volume threshold.
Calculation: Abnormal volume spike = volume / its lookback SMA ≥ spike multiple OR volume z-score over that lookback ≥ spike z-score; directional bull/bear spike additionally requires a bullish/bearish candle whose body exceeds the wick on the close side.
Calculation: Bull/bear wick rejection = (close − low)/(high − low) > 0.65 and close > open / (high − close)/(high − low) > 0.60 and close < open.
Calculation: Bull/bear liquidity sweep = bullish/bearish wick rejection AND abnormal volume spike AND demand/supply regime zone.
Calculation: Major bull/bear zone hit = at demand/supply regime zone AND (nearest key magnet strength ≥ 5 OR strength ≥ 4 with order-block score ≥ 2) AND both higher anchor timeframes are in demand/supply zones.
Calculation: Bull/bear hard structure = matching SFP OR matching liquidity sweep OR matching divergence at demand/supply regime zone with an elite magnet OR matching ultimate bottom/top at a key magnet with order-block score ≥ 1 or strength ≥ 4 OR (when vector reversals are enabled) matching vector climax at the key magnet OR (when liquidity-reclaim reversals are enabled) matching liquidity reclaim at the key magnet.
Calculation: Bull/bear structural reversal = at-key-demand/supply AND matching hard structure.
Calculation: Bull reversal score = 2 × (bull liquidity sweep + bull SFP + bullish divergence at zone with elite magnet + major bull zone hit + bull regime flip + ultimate bottom + enabled bearish vector climax at demand + enabled liquidity reclaim at demand) + (in HTF demand zone + demand order-block score ≥ 1 + nearest demand order-block score ≥ 2 + enabled bullish vector climax/active at demand + enabled liquidity overlap at demand). Bullish divergence at zone means bullish divergence and demand regime zone; liquidity overlap means an untapped sell-side pool within max(0.25 × ATR, liquidity-pool cluster distance) of the demand magnet.
Calculation: Bear reversal score = 2 × (bear liquidity sweep + bear SFP + bearish divergence at zone with elite magnet + major bear zone hit + bear regime flip + ultimate top + enabled bullish vector climax at supply + enabled liquidity reclaim at supply) + (in HTF supply zone + supply order-block score ≥ 1 + nearest supply order-block score ≥ 2 + enabled bearish vector climax/active at supply + enabled liquidity overlap at supply). Bearish divergence at zone means bearish divergence and supply regime zone; liquidity overlap means an untapped buy-side pool within max(0.25 × ATR, liquidity-pool cluster distance) of the supply magnet.
Calculation: Major bull/bear reversal = matching structural reversal first becomes true on this bar AND matching hard structure AND its reversal score ≥ the major-reversal threshold AND its score ≥ / > the opposite score.
Calculation: Bull/bear regime score = 2 each for matching SFP, divergence reversal, volume-divergence reversal, liquidity sweep, ultimate candle, and enabled liquidity reclaim; 1 each for a directional volume spike at the matching regime zone, any volume spike with matching candle direction there, divergence at zone, matching MA setup there, matching MACD confirmation with matching candle direction there, matching candlestick pattern with matching candle direction there, order-block-boosted matching key magnet with matching candle direction, and RSI oversold/overbought with matching candle direction there; 2 for each enabled matching vector climax/active contribution specified for that side. Volume-divergence reversal requires enabled volume-divergence confluence, approach/magnet/strong-pivot context on that side, (matching directional volume spike OR matching divergence), and (RSI oversold/overbought OR matching divergence OR matching candle direction). Matching MA setup uses EMA(20), EMA(50), EMA(200): long is close crossing/reclaiming EMA(200), bullish EMA(20)>EMA(50) with low touching EMA(200) and close above it, or bullish EMA(20)>EMA(50) with low touching EMA(20), close above it, and EMA(20) nondecreasing; short is bearish regime (close<EMA(200) and EMA(20)<EMA(200)) with close crossing above EMA(200), rejection of EMA(200), close above both EMA(20)/EMA(200), or close above EMA(20) while EMA(20) is nonincreasing. MACD confirmation is MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0 / line < signal line and histogram < 0. Candlestick pattern is bullish/bearish engulfing, hammer/shooting star, pin bar, or three-candle morning/evening star as defined by their standard OHLC comparisons.
Calculation: Bull/bear regime reversal = matching demand/supply regime zone AND matching regime score ≥ the regime-flip threshold AND score ≥ / > the opposite score.
Calculation: Liquidity reclaim = confirmed-bar sweep and reclaim of a tracked untapped liquidity pool with impact ≥ the effective impact threshold, or (above the lower-timeframe cutoff) a matching abnormal-volume sweep extreme with a matching directional close; suppress the opposing reclaim on lower timeframes when regime is directional, keep only the higher-impact direction if both qualify, and require the effective cooldown since the previous reclaim. Pool impact is pool score + min(18, volume z-score × 4) + 10 for at least two touches, adjusted +16/−22 for alignment/opposition with the persistent regime on a reclaim close in the outer 40% of the bar. Pool score is min(100, max(0, 0.6 × base score + 0.4 × max(35, 100 − age × decay) + 15 if pool side aligns with regime)); pivot-pool base score is min(80, 18 + 10 × nearby pivot touches + 12 if touches meet the minimum + 15 for abnormal volume), and sweep-pool base score is 40 + min(30, volume z-score × 5). Buy-side pools come from confirmed pivot highs / high sweeps; sell-side pools from confirmed pivot lows / low sweeps. A pool is swept when high > its buy-side level or low < its sell-side level; a buy-side reclaim closes below its level in the bottom 40% of the bar, and a sell-side reclaim closes above its level in the top 40%. Use the configured pivot, cluster, score, volume-z, decay, age, impact, and cooldown parameters; adjust impact/cooldown upward on chart timeframes ≤ 5 minutes.
Calculation: Ultimate bottom = prior regime bearish AND at-key-demand AND bullish wick rejection AND (abnormal volume spike OR bullish spike OR relative volume ≥ threshold) AND (RSI oversold OR bullish divergence at zone OR body/range ≤ 0.35) AND close > open. Ultimate top = (prior regime bullish OR close > close 5 bars ago OR close ≥ prior 8-bar highest close OR high ≥ prior 12-bar highest high while at supply regime zone) AND (at-key-supply OR in HTF supply zone with nearest supply magnet within 1.1 × ATR) AND (bearish wick rejection OR upper wick/range ≥ 0.50 with upper wick ≥ 1.4 × lower wick and close ≤ 0.55 × (high + low) OR shooting star OR bearish pin bar) AND (abnormal volume spike OR bearish spike OR relative volume ≥ threshold OR relative volume ≥ 1.15 with that upper-wick rejection) AND (RSI > 65 OR RSI ≥ 58 OR bearish divergence at zone OR body/range ≤ 0.35 OR upper-wick/range ≥ 0.60).
Calculation: Long stop/target = if swing stop selected, lowest low over the swing lookback − 0.2 × ATR / entry + (entry − stop) × risk/reward; otherwise entry − ATR × stop multiplier / entry + (entry − stop) × risk/reward. Short stop/target = if swing stop selected, highest high over the swing lookback + 0.2 × ATR / entry − (stop − entry) × risk/reward; otherwise entry + ATR × stop multiplier / entry − (stop − entry) × risk/reward.
Long: bull regime reversal AND prior regime was not bullish.
Short: bear regime reversal AND prior regime was not bearish.
Long: major bull reversal.
Short: major bear reversal.
Long: bullish liquidity reclaim.
Short: bearish liquidity reclaim.
Long: ultimate bottom is true and was false on the prior bar.
Short: ultimate top is true and was false on the prior bar.
Long: flat position AND major bull reversal; enter at close and set the long stop/target.
Short: flat position AND major bear reversal; enter at close and set the short stop/target.
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target OR major bear reversal AND bear reversal score ≥ bull reversal score.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target OR major bull reversal AND bull reversal score ≥ bear reversal score.
