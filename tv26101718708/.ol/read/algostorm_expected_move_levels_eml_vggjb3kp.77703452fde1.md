Calculation: daily expected-move bands = daily anchor ± daily expected-move distance; anchor is the prior daily close, current daily open, or supplied spot, locked at the daily open. Distance uses the supplied daily straddle × multiplier; otherwise, in volatility mode, anchor × annualized implied volatility × √(1/252) × √(2/π) × multiplier; otherwise, the average high−low range of completed daily bars over a longer lookback ÷ 2 × multiplier. Completed daily bars are higher timeframe than an intraday chart; use the last completed bar.
Calculation: weekly expected-move bands = weekly anchor ± weekly expected-move distance; anchor is the prior weekly close, current weekly open, or supplied spot, locked at the weekly open. Distance uses the supplied weekly straddle × multiplier; otherwise, in straddle mode, supplied daily straddle × multiplier × √5; otherwise, in volatility mode, anchor × annualized implied volatility × √(5/252) × √(2/π) × multiplier; otherwise, the average high−low range of completed weekly bars over a longer lookback ÷ 2 × multiplier. Completed weekly bars are higher timeframe than an intraday or daily chart; use the last completed bar.
Long: current close > daily +1 expected-move band AND previous close ≤ previous daily +1 band OR current close > daily +2 expected-move band AND previous close ≤ previous daily +2 band OR current close > weekly +1 expected-move band AND previous close ≤ previous weekly +1 band
Short: current close < daily −1 expected-move band AND previous close ≥ previous daily −1 band OR current close < daily −2 expected-move band AND previous close ≥ previous daily −2 band OR current close < weekly −1 expected-move band AND previous close ≥ previous weekly −1 band
