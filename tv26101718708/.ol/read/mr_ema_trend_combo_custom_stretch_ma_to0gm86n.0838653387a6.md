Calculation: Z-score = smoothed ((close − EMA(close, longer mean lookback)) / EWMA standard deviation of close deviations), where the EWMA standard deviation uses a recursively updated EWMA mean and variance.
Long: previous-bar Z-score is at or below the negative long weak threshold AND that bar is a confirmed pivot low in Z-score; OR the selected long stretch price is at least the long stretch percentage below the selected long stretch moving average; OR an armed long run has an upward crossover of the shorter-period EMA over the longer-period EMA; OR an armed long run with no prior stretch signal in that run has the long stretch condition. A long run is armed by the long Z-score pivot condition while the shorter-period EMA is below the longer-period EMA, and stays armed until the upward EMA crossover.
Short: previous-bar Z-score is at or above the short weak threshold AND that bar is a confirmed pivot high in Z-score; OR the selected short stretch price is at least the short stretch percentage above the selected short stretch moving average; OR an armed short run has a downward crossover of the shorter-period EMA below the longer-period EMA; OR an armed short run with no prior stretch signal in that run has the short stretch condition. A short run is armed by the short Z-score pivot condition while the shorter-period EMA is at or above the longer-period EMA, and stays armed until the downward EMA crossover.
