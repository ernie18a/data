Calculation: Key liquidity levels = completed Asia-session high/low; current UTC day’s previous day high/low; latest confirmed swing pivot high/low; and round-number levels immediately above/below the close, rounded using the configured step.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = a bar’s low is below any key low and its close is above that level, with lower wick > (high − low) × minimum wick ratio × 0.5. Bearish sweep = a bar’s high is above any key high and its close is below that level, with upper wick > (high − low) × minimum wick ratio × 0.5.
Calculation: Bullish displacement = close > open, absolute body / (high − low) > minimum body ratio, and range > ATR × minimum range multiple. Bearish displacement uses close < open with the same size conditions. ATR uses chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Bullish bias = (shorter-period EMA > longer-period EMA > longest-period EMA and the shorter-period EMA is rising) OR (latest confirmed pivot high and low are each above their preceding confirmed pivots and close is above the longest-period EMA). Bearish bias reverses these comparisons. EMAs and pivots use chart-timeframe bars; the EMA slope compares the shorter-period EMA with its value a configured lookback earlier.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two chart-timeframe bars earlier and gap size as a percentage of close exceeds the configured minimum. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier and the gap size as a percentage of close exceeds the configured minimum.
Calculation: Bullish order block = bullish displacement after a bullish sweep and the preceding bar closes below its open; zone bounds are that bar’s high and low. Bearish order block = bearish displacement after a bearish sweep and the preceding bar closes above its open; bounds are that bar’s high and low.
Calculation: Zone score = liquidity weight × liquidity quality + displacement weight + inefficiency weight if a zone exists + session weight during a killzone + bias weight when bias aligns − noise penalty during dead-zone hours. Liquidity quality is 2 for an Asia or previous-day level sweep and 1 for other key-level sweeps. A killzone is 07:00–10:00 UTC or 12:00–15:00 UTC; dead-zone hours are 20:00–02:00 UTC.
Long: bullish sweep and not dead-zone OR bullish displacement within 0–3 bars of the latest bullish sweep OR a bullish zone candidate with bullish displacement within 0–3 bars of a bullish sweep, not dead-zone, an enabled bullish FVG or order block, zone score ≥ configured draw minimum, and at least 3 bars since the previous zone was created OR an active candidate bullish zone is retested (low ≤ zone top and low ≥ zone bottom, then close > zone top) before expiry, mitigation, or invalidation, and its score after adding the retest-confirmation weight ≥ configured alert threshold.
Short: bearish sweep and not dead-zone OR bearish displacement within 0–3 bars of the latest bearish sweep OR a bearish zone candidate with bearish displacement within 0–3 bars of a bearish sweep, not dead-zone, an enabled bearish FVG or order block, zone score ≥ configured draw minimum, and at least 3 bars since the previous zone was created OR an active candidate bearish zone is retested (high ≥ zone bottom and high ≤ zone top, then close < zone bottom) before expiry, mitigation, or invalidation, and its score after adding the retest-confirmation weight ≥ configured alert threshold.
