Calculation: Liquidity pools = prior completed day/week highs and lows; completed Asia, London, and New York session highs and lows; and equal swing highs/lows whose difference is within an ATR-based tolerance. A pool is swept when price exceeds a high or low by a minimum ATR-based penetration; a high sweep is rejected when close < pool price, and a low sweep when close > pool price.
Calculation: Swing high/low = a pivot whose high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot for structure breaks. The MSS pivot lookback is shorter than the display/equal-level pivot lookback.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier; its zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier; its zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. Entry level = selected FVG edge or midpoint.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = current-chart close compared with the EMA of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe. Bullish when close > EMA; bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Stop boundary = the most extreme high/low from the sweep through the MSS, offset beyond that extreme by a stop buffer.
Calculation: Exit target = nearest unswept opposing liquidity pool from entry if it offers at least 1R; otherwise the latest display swing in the trade direction if it offers at least 1R; otherwise entry plus/minus the configured fallback R multiple.
Calculation: Confluence score = one point each for a PD/PW/EQ pool sweep, a sweep rejection close, enabled SMT divergence, aligned higher-timeframe bias, midnight-open alignment, and killzone timing; after MSS add one point each for MSS confirmation, displacement where absolute(close − open) > half the bar range, a bound FVG zone, and a target at least the configured minimum R multiple. Compare with the configured minimum score.
Long: Sell-side liquidity pool swept AND, within the configured sweep-to-MSS window, close > latest confirmed swing high AND a bullish FVG formed on or after the sweep AND, after that FVG forms and within the configured MSS-to-entry window, low ≤ selected FVG entry level AND close > FVG lower bound AND confluence score ≥ minimum AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND no two-sided-raid cooldown AND (killzone filter disabled OR within the killzone) AND outside enabled news blackout AND within enabled date range AND flat position AND longs enabled.
Short: Buy-side liquidity pool swept AND, within the configured sweep-to-MSS window, close < latest confirmed swing low AND a bearish FVG formed on or after the sweep AND, after that FVG forms and within the configured MSS-to-entry window, high ≥ selected FVG entry level AND close < FVG upper bound AND confluence score ≥ minimum AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum) AND no two-sided-raid cooldown AND (killzone filter disabled OR within the killzone) AND outside enabled news blackout AND within enabled date range AND flat position AND shorts enabled.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop boundary OR high ≥ exit target.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop boundary OR low ≤ exit target.
