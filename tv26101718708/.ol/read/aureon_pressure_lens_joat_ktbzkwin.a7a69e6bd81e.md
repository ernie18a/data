Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × tanh(1.6 × pressure blend), where pressure blend is 0.28 × normalized impulse + 0.24 × normalized structure + 0.18 × normalized momentum + 0.18 × normalized range position + 0.12 × normalized candle body + 0.10 × (participation − 0.5). Each normalized component is tanh(raw component × sensitivity). Impulse = (close − close from the core lookback ago) / (ATR over the shorter lookback × √core lookback); structure = (EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback)) / ATR over the shorter lookback; momentum = ROC(close, shorter lookback) / standard deviation of that ROC over the core lookback; range position = clamp((2 × close − highest high − lowest low) / (highest high − lowest low), −1, 1) over the core lookback; candle body = (close − open) / (high − low). Denominators use their absolute value with a minimum of one tick.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, core lookback), or 1 if the average is unavailable or nonpositive; participation = clamp((relative volume − 0.70) / 2, 0, 1).
Calculation: Consensus = clamp(1 − (|normalized impulse − normalized structure| + |normalized structure − normalized momentum| + |normalized momentum − normalized range position| + |normalized range position − normalized candle body|) / 8, 0, 1).
Calculation: Signal line = EMA of pressure score over the signal lookback.
Long: Pressure score > 12 OR (pressure score > signal line AND pressure score > 0) OR (pressure score crosses above signal line on a completed bar AND pressure score > 0 AND consensus ≥ minimum conviction AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND pressure score > 15)
Short: Pressure score < −12 OR (pressure score < signal line AND pressure score < 0) OR (pressure score crosses below signal line on a completed bar AND pressure score < 0 AND consensus ≥ minimum conviction AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume AND pressure score < −15)
