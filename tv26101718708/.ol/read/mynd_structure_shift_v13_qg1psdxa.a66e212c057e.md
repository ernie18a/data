Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the configured number of bars before and after it; becomes known after those following bars complete
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low less than the lows of the configured number of bars before and after it; becomes known after those following bars complete
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume over a configurable lookback
Calculation: Bullish displacement = high − low ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ 1 − configurable close-position fraction AND close > open
Calculation: Bearish displacement = high − low ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND (close − low) / (high − low) ≤ configurable close-position fraction AND close < open
Calculation: Pending bullish sweep = starts when low < latest confirmed swing low − configurable ATR pierce tolerance AND close > latest confirmed swing low; records that bar's low and remains pending for a configurable confirmation window, extended once by a configurable grace period; ends on confirmation, close below the recorded low, or expiry; confirmation takes priority over invalidation on the same bar
Calculation: Pending bearish sweep = starts when high > latest confirmed swing high + configurable ATR pierce tolerance AND close < latest confirmed swing high; records that bar's high and remains pending for a configurable confirmation window, extended once by a configurable grace period; ends on confirmation, close above the recorded high, or expiry; confirmation takes priority over invalidation on the same bar
Long: newly confirmed swing high > previous confirmed swing high OR newly confirmed swing low > previous confirmed swing low OR close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close ≤ that swing high OR pending bullish sweep AND bullish displacement AND volume ≥ configurable multiple of average volume AND no bullish reversal signal within the configurable cooldown
Short: newly confirmed swing high < previous confirmed swing high OR newly confirmed swing low < previous confirmed swing low OR close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close ≥ that swing low OR pending bearish sweep AND bearish displacement AND volume ≥ configurable multiple of average volume AND no bearish reversal signal within the configurable cooldown
