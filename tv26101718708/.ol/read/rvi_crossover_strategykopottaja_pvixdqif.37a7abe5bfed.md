Calculation: RVI = sum of the 4-bar smoothed weighted moving average of close − open, over a lookback, divided by the sum of the 4-bar smoothed weighted moving average of high − low over the same lookback
Calculation: Smoothed RVI = 4-bar smoothed weighted moving average of RVI
Long: RVI crosses above Smoothed RVI AND EMA(close) > VWMA(close)
Short: RVI crosses below Smoothed RVI AND EMA(close) < VWMA(close)
