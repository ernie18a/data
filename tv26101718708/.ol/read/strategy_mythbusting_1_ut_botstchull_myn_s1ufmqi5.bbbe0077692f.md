Calculation: UT ATR trailing stop = recursively trail close by a configurable multiple of ATR: when current and previous close are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and current close minus ATR distance; when both are below, use the lesser of the prior stop and current close plus ATR distance; otherwise use current close minus ATR distance if above the prior stop, or plus ATR distance if below it.
Calculation: UT buy signal = close crosses above the UT ATR trailing stop; UT sell signal = close crosses below it.
Calculation: Schaff Trend Cycle = apply a stochastic normalization over a configurable lookback to MACD (fast EMA minus slow EMA), smooth recursively with alpha 0.5, normalize that result over the same lookback, then smooth recursively with alpha 0.5; carry forward the prior normalized value when the normalization range is zero.
Calculation: Hull moving average = WMA(2 × WMA(close, half-length) − WMA(close, length), rounded square root of length).
Long: datetime is within the configured trading date range AND the latest UT buy signal is later than the latest UT sell signal AND Schaff Trend Cycle is below 25 and greater than its prior value AND Hull moving average is greater than its value two bars earlier.
Short: datetime is within the configured trading date range AND the latest UT sell signal is later than the latest UT buy signal AND Schaff Trend Cycle is above 75 and less than its prior value AND Hull moving average is less than its value two bars earlier.
Exit: long, datetime is within the configured trading date range AND (close < entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured take-profit percentage)).
Exit: short, datetime is within the configured trading date range AND (close > entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured take-profit percentage)).
