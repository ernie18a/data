Calculation: Unweighted Currency Index open = seventh root of the product of the selected currency’s opening exchange rates against each of the other seven major currencies, inverting pair rates as needed
Calculation: Unweighted Currency Index close = seventh root of the product of the selected currency’s closing exchange rates against each of the other seven major currencies, inverting pair rates as needed
Long: Unweighted Currency Index close >= Unweighted Currency Index open
Short: Unweighted Currency Index close < Unweighted Currency Index open
