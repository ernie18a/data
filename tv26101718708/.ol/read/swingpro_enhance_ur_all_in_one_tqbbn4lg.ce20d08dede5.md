Calculation: Daily trend = Daily close above 21 EMA AND 8 EMA above 21 EMA AND 21 EMA rising; optionally Daily close above 50 SMA. Daily bars are higher timeframe on intraday charts; use the current bar, which may still be forming. The 8 EMA is shorter-term than the 21 EMA; the 50 SMA is longer-term.
Calculation: Daily U&R = any of the last N Daily lows below the corresponding 8 EMA or 21 EMA, AND current Daily close above that EMA, AND (if required) current Daily close above open. Daily bars are higher timeframe on intraday charts; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Weekly U&R = any of the last M Weekly lows below the 9 EMA, AND current Weekly close above the 9 EMA, AND (if required) current Weekly close above open. Weekly bars are higher timeframe than the chart when chart timeframe is Daily or lower; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Daily EMA tap = no low in the Daily U&R lookback below the corresponding 8 EMA or 21 EMA, AND current Daily low is above that EMA but within the configured percentage above it, AND current Daily close is above it. Daily bars are higher timeframe on intraday charts; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Weekly EMA tap = no low in the Weekly U&R lookback below the 9 EMA, AND current Weekly low is above it but within the configured percentage above it, AND current Weekly close is above it. Weekly bars are higher timeframe than the chart when chart timeframe is Daily or lower; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Daily pullback = current Daily low is at or above the 8 EMA or 21 EMA and within the configured percentage above it, AND Daily close is above it, AND close is at least the configured percentage up from low to high, AND (if required) close is at or above open. Daily bars are higher timeframe on intraday charts; use the current bar, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = current Daily candle is bullish, prior candle bearish with body at least 35% of its range, current body fully contains the prior body and is at least 110% of its size, and current body is at least 45% of its range.
Calculation: Dragonfly doji = current Daily range is positive, body is at most 5% of range, lower wick at least 65% of range, and upper wick at most 5% of range.
Calculation: Morning star = candle two bars ago is bearish with body at least 35% of its range; intervening body is at most 25% of that bearish body; current candle is bullish, closes at or above 55% up that body, and its body is at least 45% of its range.
Calculation: Piercing line = prior Daily candle is bearish with body at least 35% of its range; current open is below prior low; current candle is bullish and closes at or above 50% up the prior body but below prior open; current body is at least 45% of its range.
Calculation: Hammer = current Daily range is positive, body at most 28% of range, lower wick at least twice body, upper wick at most 8% of range, body lies in the upper 45% of range, and prior candle is bearish.
Calculation: Bullish harami = prior Daily candle is bearish with body at least 35% of its range; current candle is bullish, its body lies strictly inside the prior body, and its body is at most 60% of the prior body.
Calculation: Three white soldiers = three consecutive bullish Daily candles; each body is at least 55% of its range and upper wick at most 20%; each later candle opens strictly inside the prior body and closes above the prior close.
Calculation: Quality = RS Rating and RVol meet their configured minimums, average Daily volume and average Daily dollar volume meet their configured minimums when enabled, and sector ETF Daily close is above open when required. When a Daily or Weekly U&R or tap fires and relaxed quality is enabled, use the relaxed RS and RVol minimums and waive the sector condition; liquidity minimums still apply. RVol compares current or pace-projected Daily volume with the average volume of prior completed Daily bars; its lookback is shorter than the average-volume lookback. RS Rating is 12-month relative strength versus SPX, weighted 40%, 20%, 20%, 20% over 63, 126, 189, 252 Daily bars and scaled to a 1–99 rating.
