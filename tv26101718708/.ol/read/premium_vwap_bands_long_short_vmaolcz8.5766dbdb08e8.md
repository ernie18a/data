Calculation: VWAP slope = current session VWAP minus session VWAP on the previous chart bar
Calculation: Neutral zone = 5% of the absolute distance between session VWAP and the upper standard-deviation band
Long: bar is confirmed AND close > session VWAP + neutral zone AND VWAP slope > 0
Short: bar is confirmed AND close < session VWAP - neutral zone AND VWAP slope < 0
