Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = high/low that remains the extreme across the configured swing lookback on both sides; becomes known only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish order block = after close exceeds the latest uncrossed confirmed swing high, select the prior candidate candle with the lowest low since that swing that swept below a prior confirmed swing low; its high and low define the zone. Reject the zone if its height exceeds the configured ATR-based limit. Detect on the chart timeframe or configured order-block timeframe; for a different timeframe, use its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bearish order block = after close falls below the latest uncrossed confirmed swing low, select the prior candidate candle with the highest high since that swing that swept above a prior confirmed swing high; its high and low define the zone. Reject the zone if its height exceeds the configured ATR-based limit. Detect on the chart timeframe or configured order-block timeframe; for a different timeframe, use its last completed bar.
Long: An active, unbroken bullish order block exists and, on a later chart bar, low <= the zone's lower boundary plus 75% of its height AND high >= the zone's lower boundary plus 25% of its height
Short: An active, unbroken bearish order block exists and, on a later chart bar, low <= the zone's lower boundary plus 75% of its height AND high >= the zone's lower boundary plus 25% of its height
