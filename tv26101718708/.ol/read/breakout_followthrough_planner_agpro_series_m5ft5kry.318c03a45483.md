Calculation: ATR = average true range; normalized values use the current bar’s ATR.
Calculation: Upper/lower structure edge = highest prior high / lowest prior low over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar; structure height = upper edge − lower edge.
Calculation: Structure is valid when structure height / ATR is between the minimum and maximum structure-height parameters.
Calculation: Break quality = 20% break-distance score + 18% body-size score + 18% directional close-location score + 12% relative-volume score + 18% trend score + 14% structure score. Each score is linearly scaled to 0–100 and clamped; break distance is normalized from the break-buffer threshold to 0.90 ATR beyond it, body share from 0.28 to 0.82, directional close location from 0.55 to 0.95, and relative volume from 0.75 to the full-volume-support parameter. Trend score gives 36 points when close is on the directional side of the slow EMA, 34 when the fast and slow EMAs are directionally stacked, and up to 30 for the slow EMA’s directional slope over the slope lookback, normalized by ATR from 0.01 to 0.28. Structure score rises linearly from the minimum structure-height parameter to 1.80 ATR above it when structure height is at or below the midpoint of the minimum and maximum parameters; above the midpoint it falls linearly from 100 at 2.25 ATR below the maximum to 0 at the maximum.
Calculation: Follow-through score = 24% breakout quality + 26% best post-break travel score + 16% follow-through-close-count score + 18% clean-retest score + 8% target-room score + 8% current relative-volume score. Scores are linearly scaled to 0–100 and clamped: travel from 0 to 1.35 times required-travel ATR; close count from 0 to the minimum required follow-through closes; retest depth from 0 to maximum clean retest depth (inversely); remaining target room from 0 to twice required-travel ATR. Target-room score is 100 once the target is reached. Best travel is the maximum favorable post-break high/low excursion from breakout close divided by breakout ATR; retest depth is the maximum move back past the breakout level divided by breakout ATR.
Long: Close > upper structure edge + ATR × break-buffer parameter AND valid structure AND bullish break quality ≥ minimum break-quality parameter AND new-breakout cooldown elapsed OR active bullish plan AND follow-through score ≥ minimum planner-score parameter AND best post-break travel ≥ required-travel ATR AND follow-through closes ≥ minimum required closes AND retest depth < maximum clean retest depth AND target not reached AND failure rail not hit AND follow-through window has not matured with insufficient travel or closes AND tracking period not expired.
