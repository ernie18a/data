Calculation: Vortex positive = sum of absolute differences between each bar’s high and the previous bar’s low, divided by the sum of true range over the same lookback
Calculation: Vortex negative = sum of absolute differences between each bar’s low and the previous bar’s high, divided by the sum of true range over the same lookback
Calculation: Smoothing line = selected moving average (SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, VWMA, ALMA, or HMA) of the simple moving average of close
Long: Vortex positive crosses above Vortex negative AND close > smoothing line
Short: Vortex positive crosses below Vortex negative AND close < smoothing line
