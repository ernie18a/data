Calculation: Bullish CISD setup = a confirmed swing low (the low is below the adjacent bars’ lows); scan backward from it to the nearest bearish candle and then through the contiguous bearish run preceding that candle. The level is the open of the oldest candle in the run; the stretch size is the absolute difference between that open and the nearest bearish candle’s close. While pending, extend the stretch when a bearish candle closes below its prior end close; its level stays fixed.
Calculation: Bearish CISD setup = a confirmed swing high (the high is above the adjacent bars’ highs); scan backward from it to the nearest bullish candle and then through the contiguous bullish run preceding that candle. The level is the open of the oldest candle in the run; the stretch size is the absolute difference between that open and the nearest bullish candle’s close. While pending, extend the stretch when a bullish candle closes above its prior end close; its level stays fixed.
Long: Bullish CISD setup passes the enabled minimum stretch-size filter (stretch size >= ATR-based threshold) and, if the purge filter is enabled, a low pivot was swept within its configured tolerance; then a chart close crosses above its level before the configured pending timeout, OR the same setup on a higher timeframe passes its enabled size filter and a last completed higher-timeframe bar closes above its level before the configured higher-timeframe timeout, OR a bullish CISD setup on the selected symbol timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart) passes its enabled size and purge filters and a last completed symbol bar closes above its level before the configured pending timeout.
Short: Bearish CISD setup passes the enabled minimum stretch-size filter (stretch size >= ATR-based threshold) and, if the purge filter is enabled, a high pivot was swept within its configured tolerance; then a chart close crosses below its level before the configured pending timeout, OR the same setup on a higher timeframe passes its enabled size filter and a last completed higher-timeframe bar closes below its level before the configured higher-timeframe timeout, OR a bearish CISD setup on the selected symbol timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart) passes its enabled size and purge filters and a last completed symbol bar closes below its level before the configured pending timeout.
