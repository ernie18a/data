Calculation: Key levels = short-lookback and long-lookback SMAs on higher-timeframe bars; the prior completed day’s high and low; the prior completed configured session’s high and low; Fibonacci retracements between the latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high and pivot low; and unmitigated higher-timeframe pivot highs and lows formed when pivot-bar volume exceeds both its longer-lookback average and the preceding bar’s volume. Remove swing highs when close exceeds them and swing lows when close falls below them; remove either when older than the configured age limit. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar or the bar still forming as each source calculation provides it.
Calculation: Strong candle = absolute(close − open) divided by (high − low) meets the configured minimum; when high = low, body percentage is zero.
Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3 for the current session.
Long: At least one positive key level has low ≤ level + configured touch buffer AND close > level AND close > open, OR prior close < level AND close > level AND close > open; enabled strength and volume filters pass (strong candle and/or volume > its configured moving average); the configured VWAP bias passes (disabled, close > VWAP, VWAP rising over its configured lookback, or both price and slope); AND no short reaction at any key level passes its corresponding reaction, enabled filters, and configured VWAP bias.
Short: At least one positive key level has high ≥ level − configured touch buffer AND close < level AND close < open, OR prior close > level AND close < level AND close < open; enabled strength and volume filters pass (strong candle and/or volume > its configured moving average); the configured VWAP bias passes (disabled, close < VWAP, VWAP falling over its configured lookback, or both price and slope); AND no long reaction at any key level passes its corresponding reaction, enabled filters, and configured VWAP bias.
