Calculation: Bullish StochRSI confirmation = %K crosses above %D after %K was below the oversold threshold within a shorter recent lookback
Calculation: Bearish StochRSI confirmation = %K crosses below %D after %K was above the overbought threshold within a shorter recent lookback
Calculation: Confirmed cycle low = a pivot low candidate whose low is the first candidate, or is at least the minimum spacing after the prior candidate, or is lower than the prior candidate; after that pivot, both close > EMA and a bullish StochRSI confirmation have occurred
Calculation: Confirmed cycle high = a pivot high candidate whose high is the first candidate, or is at least the minimum spacing after the prior candidate, or is higher than the prior candidate; after that pivot, both close < EMA and a bearish StochRSI confirmation have occurred
Long: Confirmed cycle low using a shorter pivot lookback and shorter minimum spacing (DCL) OR confirmed cycle low using a longer pivot lookback and longer minimum spacing (WCL) OR low is the running lowest low of the current calendar year OR low is the lowest low within the current projected 4-year-low window OR a confirmed weekly-cycle low is nearest the 4-year target and is the lowest confirmed weekly-cycle low within the snap window
Short: Confirmed cycle high using a shorter pivot lookback and shorter minimum spacing (DCH) OR confirmed cycle high using a longer pivot lookback and longer minimum spacing (WCH)
