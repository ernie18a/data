Calculation: EMA regime = the fast, middle, and slow EMAs of close are strictly ordered in the same direction; fast has a shorter lookback than middle, which has a shorter lookback than slow. For requested-timeframe EMAs, use the still-forming requested bar; when that timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use the sampled intrabar.
Calculation: Bullish count = number of bullish regimes; bearish count = number of bearish regimes.
Long: any chart-timeframe EMA pair has its shorter-lookback EMA > its longer-lookback EMA OR any requested-timeframe EMA regime is bullish OR bullish count across the four requested-timeframe regimes >= 3 OR chart-timeframe bullish EMA-pair count > chart-timeframe bearish EMA-pair count OR bullish count across the four requested-timeframe regimes > bearish count across those regimes
Short: any chart-timeframe EMA pair has its shorter-lookback EMA < its longer-lookback EMA OR any requested-timeframe EMA regime is bearish OR bearish count across the four requested-timeframe regimes >= 3 OR chart-timeframe bearish EMA-pair count > chart-timeframe bullish EMA-pair count OR bearish count across the four requested-timeframe regimes > bullish count across those regimes
