Calculation: Heikin-Ashi candle direction = bullish when (open + high + low + close) / 4 is at least the Heikin-Ashi open; otherwise bearish. The first Heikin-Ashi open is (open + close) / 2; each later open is the average of the previous Heikin-Ashi open and close.
Calculation: ATR health = previous completed bar’s ATR is at least its 20-bar simple average multiplied by the configured health multiplier.
Calculation: Box range = highest high minus lowest low over the configured lookback, using completed bars before the current bar.
Calculation: EMA slope = absolute difference between EMA on the previous completed bar and EMA the configured lookback earlier; pass when that difference is at least previous-bar ATR times the configured slope multiplier and EMA is rising for long or falling for short.
Calculation: EMA cross count = number of changes in whether close is above EMA during the configured lookback, counting completed bars before the current bar.
Calculation: Structure stop = for long, low of the nearest prior bearish Heikin-Ashi candle within the configured lookback minus the configured buffer; for short, high of the nearest prior bullish Heikin-Ashi candle plus the configured buffer.
Calculation: Fixed stop = signal-bar close minus the configured distance for long, or plus the distance for short.
Calculation: Break-even stop = entry price plus the configured offset for long, or minus the offset for short; activate when favorable close-to-entry movement reaches the configured trigger, and tighten the active stop only.
Calculation: Take-profit level = signal-bar close plus the configured distance for long, or minus the distance for short.
Long: Flat position AND two previous completed Heikin-Ashi candles are bullish AND previous completed close > previous completed EMA AND inside the configured exchange-time session AND daily trade count < configured maximum AND daily losing-trade count < configured maximum AND ATR health passes AND box range >= previous completed ATR times the configured minimum AND EMA slope passes for long AND EMA cross count <= configured maximum.
Short: Flat position AND two previous completed Heikin-Ashi candles are bearish AND previous completed close < previous completed EMA AND inside the configured exchange-time session AND daily trade count < configured maximum AND daily losing-trade count < configured maximum AND ATR health passes AND box range >= previous completed ATR times the configured minimum AND EMA slope passes for short AND EMA cross count <= configured maximum.
Exit: Long, current bar low <= active stop OR current bar high >= active take-profit level OR (Heikin-Ashi candle is bearish and exit-on-flip is enabled).
Exit: Short, current bar high >= active stop OR current bar low <= active take-profit level OR (Heikin-Ashi candle is bullish and exit-on-flip is enabled).
