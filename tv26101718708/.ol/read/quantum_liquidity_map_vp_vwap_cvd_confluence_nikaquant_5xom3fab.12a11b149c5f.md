Calculation: Cumulative Volume Delta = session-anchored sum of per-bar volume multiplied by (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low); use zero delta when high = low.
Calculation: Relative Volume = current volume / moving average of volume over a longer lookback.
Calculation: Initial Balance High/Low = highest high and lowest low during the opening session interval; mark the range complete when that interval ends.
Calculation: Confluence Score = sum of directional points for close vs. anchored VWAP, close vs. rolling Volume Profile value area, session delta percentage, CVD vs. its shorter-term EMA, and Relative Volume; positive inputs add points, negative inputs subtract points, and delta points are capped at either extreme.
Long: A confirmed price swing low is below the previous confirmed swing low AND Cumulative Volume Delta at the new swing is above Cumulative Volume Delta at the previous swing AND the swing distance exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold OR low < the lowest low of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close > that low AND Relative Volume meets its threshold AND close > open OR volume meets its spike threshold relative to its moving average AND close > open OR the Initial Balance is complete AND close > Initial Balance High AND the upside break has not already occurred this session OR Confluence Score is positive AND its absolute value meets the configured alert threshold.
Short: A confirmed price swing high is above the previous confirmed swing high AND Cumulative Volume Delta at the new swing is below Cumulative Volume Delta at the previous swing AND the swing distance exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold OR high > the highest high of the preceding swing-lookback bars AND close < that high AND Relative Volume meets its threshold AND close < open OR volume meets its spike threshold relative to its moving average AND close < open OR the Initial Balance is complete AND close < Initial Balance Low AND the downside break has not already occurred this session OR Confluence Score is negative AND its absolute value meets the configured alert threshold.
