Calculation: WaveTrend 1 = EMA of the normalized difference between typical price and its EMA; normalize by 0.015 times the EMA of the absolute difference, then take an EMA of the result
Calculation: WaveTrend 2 = 3-bar SMA of WaveTrend 1
Calculation: Stochastic RSI K = SMA of the stochastic value of RSI applied to close; optionally use the mean of K and its SMA when configured
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator pivot high = value two bars ago is greater than the values one, three, and four bars ago and the current value; confirmed pivot low = value two bars ago is less than those four values
Calculation: RSI = RSI of close
Calculation: RSI running maximum close and RSI = initialize to the current close and RSI when the respective series is at its highest in the lookback window, otherwise carry forward the prior value; update each independently whenever its current value exceeds its running maximum
Calculation: RSI running minimum close and RSI = initialize to the current close and RSI when the respective series is at its lowest in the lookback window, otherwise carry forward the prior value; update each independently whenever its current value is below its running minimum
Long: WaveTrend 1 crosses above WaveTrend 2 OR WaveTrend 1 has a confirmed pivot low, its low is below the prior confirmed pivot low, and its value is above the prior confirmed pivot value OR Stochastic RSI K has a confirmed pivot low, its low is below the prior confirmed pivot low, and its value is above the prior confirmed pivot value OR RSI running minimum close[1] < RSI running minimum close[2] AND RSI[1] > RSI running minimum RSI AND RSI >= RSI[1] AND RSI[1] <= the configured maximum bullish RSI OR RSI running minimum close[1] > RSI running minimum close[2] AND RSI[1] > RSI running minimum RSI
Short: WaveTrend 2 crosses above WaveTrend 1 OR WaveTrend 1 has a confirmed pivot high, its high is above the prior confirmed pivot high, and its value is below the prior confirmed pivot value OR Stochastic RSI K has a confirmed pivot high, its high is above the prior confirmed pivot high, and its value is below the prior confirmed pivot value OR RSI running maximum close[1] > RSI running maximum close[2] AND RSI[1] < RSI running maximum RSI AND RSI <= RSI[1] AND RSI[1] >= the configured minimum bearish RSI OR RSI running maximum close[1] < RSI running maximum close[2] AND RSI[1] < RSI running maximum RSI
Exit: long, WaveTrend 2 crosses above WaveTrend 1
Exit: short, WaveTrend 1 crosses above WaveTrend 2
