Calculation: EMA cross count for each of three EMA lookbacks = number of close crossovers above (for long signals) or crossunders below (for short signals) that EMA during the same lookback window; the three EMA lookbacks are short, medium, and long, in increasing order
Calculation: ATR = average true range on a higher timeframe, using the bar still forming
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier; long target = signal-bar close + ATR × target multiplier; retain both levels until a short signal resets them
Calculation: Short stop = signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier; short target = signal-bar close − ATR × target multiplier; retain both levels until a long signal resets them
Long: close crosses above the short EMA AND short-EMA bullish cross count < medium-EMA bullish cross count AND short-EMA bullish cross count < long-EMA bullish cross count AND short-EMA bullish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > its lower threshold OR close crosses above the medium EMA AND medium-EMA bullish cross count < short-EMA bullish cross count AND medium-EMA bullish cross count < long-EMA bullish cross count AND medium-EMA bullish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND no short-EMA long signal AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > its lower threshold OR close crosses above the long EMA AND long-EMA bullish cross count < short-EMA bullish cross count AND long-EMA bullish cross count < medium-EMA bullish cross count AND long-EMA bullish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND no short-EMA or medium-EMA long signal AND MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > its lower threshold
Short: close crosses below the short EMA AND short-EMA bearish cross count < medium-EMA bearish cross count AND short-EMA bearish cross count < long-EMA bearish cross count AND short-EMA bearish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < its upper threshold OR close crosses below the medium EMA AND medium-EMA bearish cross count < short-EMA bearish cross count AND medium-EMA bearish cross count < long-EMA bullish cross count AND medium-EMA bearish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND no short-EMA short signal AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < its upper threshold OR close crosses below the long EMA AND long-EMA bearish cross count < short-EMA bearish cross count AND long-EMA bearish cross count < medium-EMA bearish cross count AND long-EMA bearish cross count < its threshold AND RSI is between its lower and upper bounds AND no short-EMA or medium-EMA short signal AND MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < its upper threshold
Exit: long position, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short position, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
