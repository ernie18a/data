Calculation: Current and prior profile levels = For each daily, weekly, and monthly period, distribute each chart-timeframe bar's volume evenly across price bins spanning its high to low; POC is the bin with greatest accumulated volume, and VAH/VAL are the contiguous range grown from POC toward the larger adjacent bin volume until the configured fraction of total volume is reached. Current-period levels use the still-forming higher-timeframe period; prior-period levels use the last completed higher-timeframe period.
Calculation: Level touch = A bar's high is at least a configured tolerance below a level and its low is at least that tolerance above it. A level is any current or enabled prior daily, weekly, or monthly POC/VAH/VAL; daily or weekly VWAP; in-session VWAP; or completed initial-balance high/low.
Calculation: Daily VWAP and deviation = Volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of chart-bar typical price (high + low + close)/3 since the current daily period began. Weekly VWAP is calculated likewise since the current weekly period began; session VWAP since the current session began.
Calculation: ATR = Average true range over a configurable lookback of chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current volume / simple moving average of volume over a configurable chart-bar lookback.
Calculation: CVD direction = Sign of the sum over the current day of each chart bar's volume multiplied by ((close − low) − (high − close))/(high − low), compared with that cumulative sum the configured number of chart bars earlier; zero range contributes zero. Reset at each new day.
Calculation: Prior-day value score = +2 if close is above prior daily VAH, +1 if above prior daily POC, −2 if below prior daily VAL, −1 if below prior daily POC, otherwise 0.
Calculation: HTF directional bias = Bullish if prior-day value score is positive, or if it is zero and CVD direction is positive; bearish if the score is negative, or if zero and CVD direction is negative.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish bias scores = Start each at 0. Add respectively 3 or 1 for prior-day value score ≥2 or =1; add 1 to bearish or bullish respectively for score ≤−2 or =−1. Add 1 to the side of CVD direction. Add 2 to bullish when close is above current weekly VAH, or to bearish when below current weekly VAL. Add 1 to bullish/bearish when close is above/below current daily POC by more than the configured level tolerance fraction. Add 2 bullish for a failed breakdown (close above current daily VAL after either of the prior two closes was below it); add 2 bearish for a failed breakout (close below current daily VAH after either of the prior two closes was above it). Add 1 bullish/bearish for two-bar acceptance above current daily VAH/below current daily VAL, where current close is beyond the level by the configured tolerance fraction and prior close is beyond it within the tolerance. The opposing score is unchanged by each addition.
Calculation: Strong-body bullish/bearish commit = Current candle is respectively bullish/bearish, its body/range meets the configured minimum, and close is above the prior high/below the prior low.
Calculation: Prior level interaction = The prior chart bar touched any eligible level. Sweep below support = Prior low is below current daily VAL or POC, or current weekly VAL, by more than half the configured level tolerance. Sweep above resistance = Prior high is above current daily VAH or POC, or current weekly VAH, by more than half the configured level tolerance.
Calculation: Pattern score = Breakout long: close above current daily VAH by the tolerance fraction (2); add 2 for two-bar acceptance, 1 for body/range at least configured fraction, 1 for body at least configured ATR fraction, 1 for relative volume above its configured threshold, 1 for positive CVD, 1 for close above current weekly VAH; subtract 2 if HTF bias is bearish. Breakout short is symmetric below daily VAL/weekly VAL with negative CVD and subtract 2 for bullish HTF bias. Continuation long: bullish HTF bias and prior level interaction (2); add 2 for bullish commit, otherwise 1 for bullish candle closing in the upper half; 2 for sweep below support; 1 for close within configured tolerance above daily VAL, nonnegative CVD, relative volume above its configured minimum, kill-zone time, and close inside a profile-level cluster. Continuation short is symmetric with bearish HTF bias, bearish commit or bearish candle closing in the lower half, sweep above resistance, proximity below daily VAH, nonpositive CVD, and the same volume/time/cluster additions. Reversal long: both prior closes below current daily VAL and prior level interaction (2); add 2 for sweep below support, 2 for bullish commit, 1 for close above daily VAL, 1 for positive CVD, 1 for kill-zone time, and 1 for cluster; subtract 1 for bearish HTF bias. Reversal short is symmetric: both prior closes above daily VAH, sweep above resistance, bearish commit, close below daily VAH, negative CVD, kill zone, cluster; subtract 1 for bullish HTF bias. Mean-reversion long: close at least configured ATR distance below daily POC or at least configured standard-deviation distance below daily VWAP (2); add 2 for bullish candle closing in upper configured fraction of range, 1 for bullish commit, 2 for relative-volume spike, 1 for positive CVD, and 1 for greater VWAP deviation. Mean-reversion short is symmetric above POC/VWAP with bearish candle, commit, negative CVD. Failed-breakout long: close above daily VAL after either of the prior two closes was below it (3); add 2 for sweep below support, 2 for bullish commit, and 1 each for body/range, relative volume, positive CVD, and kill-zone time. Failed-breakout short is symmetric for recovery above daily VAH after a prior close above it, sweep above resistance, bearish commit, body/range, relative volume, negative CVD, and kill zone. Sweep-reclaim long: sweep below support and prior level interaction (4); add 2 for bullish commit, otherwise 1 for bullish candle closing in the upper configured fraction; add 1 each for nonnegative CVD and sufficient relative volume. Short is symmetric. Candle-structure displacement long: prior candle bearish and current close above its open (score 4), or above the midpoint of prior candle body (score 2); add 1 for prior level interaction and 1 for positive CVD. Short is symmetric from a bullish prior candle and current close below its open or body midpoint, adding for prior level interaction and negative CVD. Inverse-gap long: a bullish three-bar gap exists (current low above high two bars earlier), the prior bar lies inside the latest such gap, and its age is within a configured limit (score 3); add 2 if current close is above the gap top and 1 for positive CVD. Short is symmetric for a bearish gap and close below its bottom. Acceptance long: current and prior closes meet the two-bar acceptance condition above daily VAH (3); add 1 if the close two bars earlier is above VAH within configured tolerance, and 1 for positive CVD. Short is symmetric below daily VAL with negative CVD. Rejection long: prior bar touched a level, its close is in the lower configured fraction of its range, and current candle is bullish (2); add 1 if current close is in the upper configured fraction and 1 for positive CVD. Short is symmetric for prior upper-fraction rejection and current bearish candle. A detector qualifies when its score is at least the greater of its configured minimum pattern score and its detector-specific floor (4 for breakout/continuation, 5 for reversal/mean-reversion/failed-breakout/sweep-reclaim; configured minimum for displacement/gap/acceptance/rejection). Only enabled breakout, continuation, reversal, mean-reversion, and failed-breakout detectors are eligible; sweep-reclaim, displacement, gap, acceptance, and rejection detectors are always eligible. The best eligible score per direction is that direction's engine score.
Calculation: Confirmation count = Count one each for sweep plus qualifying sweep-reclaim detector, strong-body commit, displacement (confirmed or forming), qualifying gap, qualifying acceptance, qualifying rejection, and failed break in that direction.
Calculation: Final signal gate = Engine score × 10 meets configured setup-score minimum; confirmation count meets the greater of configured minimum confirmations and the grade-based requirement (3 for grade A, 2 for B, otherwise 1), plus 1 when the opposing bias score exceeds the directional score by more than 1 and is at least 4. Grade is the sum of: min(directional bias score × 2.5, 20), location points (30 for top-priority level, 20 for medium, 10 for any touched level, otherwise 0), min(confirmation count/4 × 30, 30), and entry points (20 at/below preferred stop distance, linearly decreasing to 0 at maximum stop distance; 0 if nonpositive or above maximum). Grade A/B/C thresholds are configured score cutoffs. Also require valid location (prior level interaction, sweep, close within 0.7 ATR of daily VWAP, or the corresponding value-area/POC extreme), medium-or-higher prior-level quality, no same-direction cooldown, and structural stop distance within its configured positive maximum. Suppress signals inside daily value area away from VAH, VAL, POC, and touched levels, except mean-reversion setups. Require entry extension from prior high/low (or reclaimed daily VAL/VAH after a sweep) within its configured maximum. If enabled, require reward/risk to the nearest eligible structural level in the trade direction to meet its configured minimum. A-grade candidates require at least three confirmations. A signal fires only once per direction until two consecutive bars are away from all levels.
Calculation: Early signal eligibility = Current bar touches a daily, weekly, or monthly profile level if higher-timeframe-zone-only is enabled; otherwise it touches any eligible level. Directional body fraction is (close−low)/range for long and (high−close)/range for short. Require it to meet the early minimum; optionally require sweep, midpoint reclaim, and directional body at least configured ATR fraction. Conservative sensitivity requires aligned prior-day value score and kill-zone time; normal requires score nonnegative for long/nonpositive for short; aggressive imposes neither. Unless aggressive, CVD must not oppose. Require configured minimum early grade, one signal per direction until two consecutive bars away from all levels, and no same-direction confirmed signal on the bar.
Long: Final signal gate passes for the long side and no long signal is latched; OR early signal eligibility passes for the long side and no early long signal is latched.
Short: Final signal gate passes for the short side and no short signal is latched; OR early signal eligibility passes for the short side and no early short signal is latched.
