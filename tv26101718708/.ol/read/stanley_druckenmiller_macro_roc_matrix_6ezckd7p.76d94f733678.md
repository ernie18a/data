Calculation: Monthly ROC = percentage change in source price from the prior monthly bar, optionally smoothed with a moving average
Calculation: Monthly ROC velocity = current monthly ROC − prior monthly ROC
Calculation: Monthly ROC acceleration = current monthly ROC velocity − prior monthly ROC velocity
Long: Current monthly ROC ≥ 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC ≥ 0 OR current monthly ROC velocity ≥ 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC velocity ≥ 0 OR current monthly ROC acceleration ≥ 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC acceleration ≥ 0
Short: Current monthly ROC < 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC < 0 OR current monthly ROC velocity < 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC velocity < 0 OR current monthly ROC acceleration < 0 OR prior completed monthly ROC acceleration < 0
