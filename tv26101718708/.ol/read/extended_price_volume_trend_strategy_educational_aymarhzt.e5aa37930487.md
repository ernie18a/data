Calculation: Extended PVT line = the close on the most recent bar where the cumulative sum of ((close − previous close) / previous close × volume), minus the midpoint of its highest and lowest values over the lookback, crossed zero
Long: close crosses above the Extended PVT line
Short: close crosses below the Extended PVT line
Exit: long, close crosses below the Extended PVT line OR the 2-period EMA of close crosses below any of three levels, each equal to the Extended PVT line × (1 + its TP percentage)
Exit: short, close crosses above the Extended PVT line OR the 2-period EMA of close crosses above any of three levels, each equal to the Extended PVT line × (1 − its TP percentage)
