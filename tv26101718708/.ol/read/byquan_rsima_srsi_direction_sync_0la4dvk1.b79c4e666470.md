Calculation: RSI-MA = simple moving average of RSI, calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Smoothed SRSI average = EMA of the average of four higher-timeframe values, each value being the sum of the K-smoothed stochastic RSI and its D-smoothed value, calculated using the last completed bar of that higher timeframe.
Long: RSI-MA > its previous value AND Smoothed SRSI average > its previous value
Short: RSI-MA < its previous value AND Smoothed SRSI average < its previous value
