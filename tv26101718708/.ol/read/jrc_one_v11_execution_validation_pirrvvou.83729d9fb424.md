Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = last completed higher-timeframe close and short-lookback EMA are both above the longer-lookback EMA for bullish bias, or both below it for bearish bias
Calculation: Session levels = previous regular-session high/low, premarket high/low, opening-range high/low, and confirmed swing high/low; use lower-timeframe bars through the latest completed bar for session levels; a swing level becomes available only after its right-side confirmation bars
Calculation: Relative volume = current-bar volume divided by the average volume of preceding regular-session bars
Calculation: Retest band = mapped level plus or minus a configured fraction of ATR frozen when the setup is armed
Calculation: Stop = setup's tracked extreme, buffered outward by a configured fraction of frozen ATR
Calculation: Target = nearest mapped level or opposing supply/demand zone, with an ATR clearance buffer, capped by a configured maximum reward-to-risk multiple
Long: bullish higher-timeframe bias AND within a configured signal window AND eligible data/session levels are available AND no active position or pending fill AND daily plan cap and cooldown permit AND mapped level has not already been used in this direction today AND either (close crosses above mapped level from at-or-below it AND relative volume meets its threshold AND breakout extension is within its limit AND a later bar intersects the retest band, closes above the level, and has lower volume than the breakout bar when quiet retests are enabled AND a still later bar closes above the retest bar's high with a bullish body AND close is above VWAP and the shorter-lookback EMA is above the longer-lookback EMA) OR (low penetrates below mapped level by at least a configured ATR fraction AND close reclaims above the level with a bullish body in the upper half of the bar AND a later bar intersects the retest band and closes above the level AND a still later bar closes above the retest bar's high with a bullish body); setup remains within its lifetime and confirmation window, has not failed its level/ATR invalidation or lost bias, and entry extension, stop distance, modeled costs, and net reward-to-total-stop-risk gates pass; confirm at bar close, then accept a later open fill only if price remains on the valid side of stop and target, extension and stop-distance gates pass, costs and net reward gates pass, and higher-timeframe bias remains bullish
Short: bearish higher-timeframe bias AND within a configured signal window AND eligible data/session levels are available AND no active position or pending fill AND daily plan cap and cooldown permit AND mapped level has not already been used in this direction today AND either (close crosses below mapped level from at-or-above it AND relative volume meets its threshold AND breakout extension is within its limit AND a later bar intersects the retest band, closes below the level, and has lower volume than the breakout bar when quiet retests are enabled AND a still later bar closes below the retest bar's low with a bearish body AND close is below VWAP and the shorter-lookback EMA is below the longer-lookback EMA) OR (high penetrates above mapped level by at least a configured ATR fraction AND close reclaims below the level with a bearish body in the lower half of the bar AND a later bar intersects the retest band and closes below the level AND a still later bar closes below the retest bar's low with a bearish body); setup remains within its lifetime and confirmation window, has not failed its level/ATR invalidation or lost bias, and entry extension, stop distance, modeled costs, and net reward-to-total-stop-risk gates pass; confirm at bar close, then accept a later open fill only if price remains on the valid side of stop and target, extension and stop-distance gates pass, costs and net reward gates pass, and higher-timeframe bias remains bearish
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below stop OR high reaches or exceeds target OR regular session ends OR test date ends; if stop and target are both reached in one bar, exit at stop
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds stop OR low reaches or falls below target OR regular session ends OR test date ends; if stop and target are both reached in one bar, exit at stop
