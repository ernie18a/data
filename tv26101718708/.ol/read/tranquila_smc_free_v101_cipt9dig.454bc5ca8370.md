Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a chart-timeframe high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of equal-length windows before and after it; available after the following window completes. Use the latest pivot not yet crossed.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = low of the current FVG-timeframe bar is above the high two FVG-timeframe bars earlier, and the gap is at least the configured minimum size relative to chart-timeframe ATR. Bearish FVG = high of the current FVG-timeframe bar is below the low two FVG-timeframe bars earlier, and the gap is at least the configured minimum size relative to chart-timeframe ATR. The FVG timeframe may equal, exceed, or be below the chart timeframe; evaluate at a new FVG-timeframe bar using its last completed bar and the two earlier completed bars.
Long: Chart-timeframe close crosses above the latest unbroken confirmed pivot high OR a bullish FVG forms.
Short: Chart-timeframe close crosses below the latest unbroken confirmed pivot low OR a bearish FVG forms.
