Calculation: Rolling volume profile = Over the lookback bars, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into price buckets; distribute each bar’s volume equally among the buckets its high-low range spans. POC is the highest-volume bucket’s midpoint. Starting at POC, expand the value area toward whichever adjacent bucket has more volume (ties upward) until it contains the configured fraction of total volume; VAH and VAL are its upper and lower edges.
Calculation: Smoothed value levels = EMA of POC, VAH, and VAL, each using the same smoothing length.
Calculation: ATR bands = Smoothed VAL minus ATR times the buffer for the lower band; smoothed VAH plus ATR times the buffer for the upper band. Trail the lower band to the maximum of its raw value and prior lower band when prior close is above the prior lower band; otherwise use its raw value. Trail the upper band to the minimum of its raw value and prior upper band when prior close is below the prior upper band; otherwise use its raw value.
Calculation: Trend state = Initialize bullish. On each bar, switch from bearish to bullish when the flip-test price exceeds the prior upper band; switch from bullish to bearish when the flip-test price is below the prior lower band; otherwise retain the prior state. The flip-test price is close in Close mode, or high for bullish flips and low for bearish flips in Wick mode. Use prior confirmed bands.
Long: prior trend state is bearish AND flip-test price > prior upper band
Short: prior trend state is bullish AND flip-test price < prior lower band
