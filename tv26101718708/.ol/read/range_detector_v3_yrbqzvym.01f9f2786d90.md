Calculation: Range factor score = Count of these conditions: ADX is below its ceiling; Choppiness Index is above its floor; Bollinger bandwidth’s position within its recent range is below its ceiling; Bollinger Bands are inside the Keltner Channel; absolute linear-regression slope, normalized by ATR per bar, is below its ceiling. Use the same lookback for the Bollinger Bands and Keltner Channel.
Calculation: Confirmed range and rails = On a completed bar, start a candidate when the range factor score meets the required count. While consecutive completed bars meet that count, update the upper and lower rails to the highest high and lowest low since the candidate began. Confirm the range when both the required consecutive-bar count and minimum range age are met; cancel the candidate if a bar fails the factor count. While confirmed, expand a rail to a new high or low only when the penetration is less than the configured ATR fraction. End the range on a breakout or when its maximum age is exceeded.
Calculation: Relative volume = Current volume divided by its moving average over a longer lookback.
Long: Confirmed range AND high > upper rail AND close > upper rail AND absolute(close - open) >= configured ATR fraction AND relative volume >= configured minimum AND close > open
Long: Upward breakout occurred 2 through the configured retest-lookback bars ago AND low <= broken upper rail AND close > broken upper rail AND close > open
Short: Confirmed range AND low < lower rail AND close < lower rail AND absolute(close - open) >= configured ATR fraction AND relative volume >= configured minimum AND close < open
Short: Downward breakout occurred 2 through the configured retest-lookback bars ago AND high >= broken lower rail AND close < broken lower rail AND close < open
