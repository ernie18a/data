Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close, using a shorter lookback than the long lookback used for the TV preset rating
Calculation: TV preset rating = average of the available moving-average rating and other-indicator rating; each rating contribution is +1 for bullish, -1 for bearish, and 0 for neutral. Moving-average rating averages comparisons of close with short-, medium-, and long-lookback simple and exponential moving averages, a short-lookback Hull moving average, a medium-lookback volume-weighted moving average, and the Ichimoku rating. Ichimoku rating is +1 when the shorter cloud lead is above the longer lead, close is above the shorter lead and below the base line, and prior close was below the conversion line while current close is above it; it is -1 for the inverse conditions. Other-indicator rating averages: RSI (+1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling); stochastic (+1 both lines below their lower threshold with %K crossing above %D, -1 both above their upper threshold with %K crossing below %D); CCI (+1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling); ADX/DMI (+1 ADX above its threshold and +DI crossing above -DI, -1 ADX above its threshold and +DI crossing below -DI); Awesome Oscillator (+1 crossing above zero or positive and rising after a prior dip, -1 crossing below zero or negative and falling after a prior peak); momentum (+1 rising, -1 falling); MACD (+1 MACD above signal, -1 below); stochastic RSI (+1 in a downtrend, both lines below their lower threshold, and %K crossing above %D; -1 in an uptrend, both above their upper threshold, and %K crossing below %D); Williams %R (+1 below its lower threshold and rising, -1 above its upper threshold and falling); bull/bear power (+1 in an uptrend with bear power below zero and rising, -1 in a downtrend with bull power above zero and falling); ultimate oscillator (+1 above its upper threshold, -1 below its lower threshold). Trend for stochastic RSI and bull/bear power is close above/below the medium-lookback EMA. Ignore unavailable contributions. Strong strength uses a wider rating threshold than weak strength.
