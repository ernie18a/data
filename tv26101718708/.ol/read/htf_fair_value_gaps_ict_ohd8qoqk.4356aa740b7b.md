Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG zones = using the last three completed higher-timeframe bars (A, B, C), create a bullish zone when B closes above its open and C’s low is above A’s high; create a bearish zone when B closes below its open and C’s high is below A’s low. If the configured type is inverted FVG, reverse each gap comparison and use the gap between those same boundaries as the zone. When the displacement filter is enabled, B’s body must exceed the higher-timeframe simple moving average of absolute candle bodies, and each of B’s upper and lower wicks must be less than 0.36 times its body. A bullish zone remains active until a bar’s low is below its lower boundary; a bearish zone remains active until a bar’s high is above its upper boundary.
Long: current bar’s low is at or below an active bullish higher-timeframe FVG’s upper boundary AND current bar’s high is at or above its lower boundary
Short: current bar’s high is at or above an active bearish higher-timeframe FVG’s lower boundary AND current bar’s low is at or below its upper boundary
