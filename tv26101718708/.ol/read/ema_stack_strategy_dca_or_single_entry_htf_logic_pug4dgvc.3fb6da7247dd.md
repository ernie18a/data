Calculation: MA1–MA4 = configurable moving averages of price, with progressively longer lookbacks (MA1 shortest; MA4 longest); calculated on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe using its last completed bar, and decisions update when that logic bar closes
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on the MA logic timeframe, using its last completed bar when that timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Long stack = MA1 > MA2 AND (MA3 disabled OR MA2 > MA3) AND (MA4 disabled OR MA3 > MA4); MA4 is disabled when MA3 is disabled
Calculation: Short stack = MA1 < MA2 AND (MA3 disabled OR MA2 < MA3) AND (MA4 disabled OR MA3 < MA4); MA4 is disabled when MA3 is disabled
Calculation: Stack ready = the corresponding stack has held for at least the configured number of consecutive logic bars plus one
Calculation: DCA level = anchor price minus (long) or plus (short) the cumulative configured entry spacing; anchor is the first entry price or current average entry price, and each successive interval is the base interval multiplied by the configured spacing scale to the power of the prior add count
Calculation: DCA take-profit level = average entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the configured offset, measured in units, percent of average entry, or ATR multiple
Calculation: Last-DCA take-profit level = last entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the configured offset, measured in units or percent of last entry; used only after the configured maximum entry count is reached
Calculation: Trailing stop = highest price since position opened minus (long) or plus (short) the configured trail distance; activates after the favorable extreme moves the configured activation distance from average entry
Calculation: ATR bracket levels = average entry minus (long) or plus (short) the configured stop ATR multiple, and average entry plus (long) or minus (short) the configured target ATR multiple
Calculation: Risk-reward bracket levels = stop at the configured ATR distance from average entry or beyond the lowest low (long) / highest high (short) over the configured lookback; target is the configured risk-reward multiple of that risk distance from average entry
Calculation: Percent bracket levels = average entry decreased (long) or increased (short) by the configured stop percentage, and increased (long) or decreased (short) by the configured target percentage
Calculation: Break-even stop = average entry plus (long) or minus (short) the configured unit offset, armed after favorable movement from average entry reaches the configured unit, percent, or ATR trigger

Short: short position AND DCA enabled AND adds allowed AND entry count below maximum AND planned cash used below cash cap AND (DCA window restriction disabled OR outside enabled no-new-trade time windows) AND high >= DCA level
Exit: long, low <= selected take-profit level OR low <= active trailing stop OR (DCA MA kill enabled AND after-last-DCA exit is not selected AND MA1 < MA2)
Exit: short, high >= selected take-profit level OR high >= active trailing stop OR (DCA MA kill enabled AND after-last-DCA exit is not selected AND MA1 > MA2)
Exit: long, (selected single-entry exit is MA cross AND MA1 crosses below MA2) OR (ATR bracket selected AND (low <= ATR stop OR high >= ATR target)) OR (risk-reward bracket selected AND (low <= risk-reward stop OR high >= risk-reward target)) OR (percent bracket selected AND (low <= percent stop OR high >= percent target)) OR (time stop selected AND completed bars in position >= configured limit) OR (single-entry trailing enabled AND low <= active trailing stop)
