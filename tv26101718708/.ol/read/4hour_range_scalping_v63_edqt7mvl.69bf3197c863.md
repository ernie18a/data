Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the opening range for each session day
Calculation: Opening range low = lowest low during the opening range for each session day
Long: The range is set AND a close has been below the opening range low since it was set AND close crosses above the opening range low AND position is flat
Short: The range is set AND a close has been above the opening range high since it was set AND close crosses below the opening range high AND position is flat
Exit: long, low <= the lowest low recorded while closes were below the opening range low OR high >= entry price + (entry price − that lowest low) × risk/reward ratio
Exit: short, high >= the highest high recorded while closes were above the opening range high OR low <= entry price − (that highest high − entry price) × risk/reward ratio
