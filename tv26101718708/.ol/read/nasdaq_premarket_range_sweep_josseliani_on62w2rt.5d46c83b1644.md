Calculation: Range high/low = highest high and lowest low across bars in the configured session; range width = range high − range low; midpoint = (range high + range low) / 2
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; available only after the bars on the right have completed
Calculation: Body inside range (%) = 100 × overlap of the candle body with the range / candle body size
Long: After the range is complete and during its signal window, a bar with low > range high takes the nearest confirmed pivot high (when that filter is enabled), AND either (close of the previous completed signal-engine bar is inside the range AND body inside range (%) meets the configured minimum AND current high > that bar’s high AND (that bar’s high − range low) / range width ≤ 0.75) OR (price has moved at least one range width below range low, a confirmed pivot high formed after the sweep below range low, and current high breaks that pivot); also require the per-range signal limit not to be reached, the opposite range boundary not yet reached, and no prior long signal cycle unless current low is below that prior cycle’s outside-excursion low
Short: After the range is complete and during its signal window, a bar with high < range low takes the nearest confirmed pivot low (when that filter is enabled), AND either (close of the previous completed signal-engine bar is inside the range AND body inside range (%) meets the configured minimum AND current low < that bar’s low AND (range high − that bar’s low) / range width ≤ 0.75) OR (price has moved at least one range width above range high, a confirmed pivot low formed after the sweep above range high, and current low breaks that pivot); also require the per-range signal limit not to be reached, the opposite range boundary not yet reached, and no prior short signal cycle unless current high is above that prior cycle’s outside-excursion high
Exit: Long position, high ≥ target, where target is the midpoint after a reclaim at or within 25% of range low, range high after a deeper reclaim, or range low after a pivot entry
Exit: Short position, low ≤ target, where target is the midpoint after a reclaim at or within 25% of range high, range low after a deeper reclaim, or range high after a pivot entry
