Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively higher/lower than the highs/lows of the selected number of bars on both sides; it becomes available only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Prior local structure high/low at sweep = the highest high/lowest low of the selected number of bars immediately before the sweep bar.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar or a higher timeframe's last completed bar, an available confirmed swing low is swept when low <= swing low − ATR × minimum sweep penetration; the setup is confirmed within the maximum confirmation bars when a close since the sweep is above the swept swing low, close > the prior local structure high saved at the sweep, and |close − open| >= ATR × minimum displacement body.
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar or a higher timeframe's last completed bar, an available confirmed swing high is swept when high >= swing high + ATR × minimum sweep penetration; the setup is confirmed within the maximum confirmation bars when a close since the sweep is below the swept swing high, close < the prior local structure low saved at the sweep, and |close − open| >= ATR × minimum displacement body.
