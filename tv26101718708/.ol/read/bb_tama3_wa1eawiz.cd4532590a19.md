Calculation: Box upper/lower = linear regression over the box lookback of the most recent breakout high and the lowest low, respectively. A breakout is a high above the prior bar’s highest high over the box lookback; capture the upper and lower values when the bars since the latest breakout equal the box lookback minus two and the highest high over the lookback minus two bars is below that over the lookback minus one bar.
Calculation: Normalized box level = normalize each box upper/lower value to [-1, 1] using the rolling highest box upper and lowest box lower over the same lookback; use 0 when the range is zero. Normalized box midpoint = average of the normalized upper and lower levels.
Calculation: Normalized trend level = average of (1) normalized trend angle and (2) normalized trend line. The trend line is the linear regression of hlc3 plus its difference from a second linear regression of that first regression, using the same lookback. The angle is the lookback-period simple moving average of the arctangent, in degrees, of the trend line’s one-bar change divided by its prior value, multiplied by 10. Normalize the angle and trend line separately to [-1, 1] using their respective rolling highest and lowest values over the same lookback; use 0 when a range is zero.
Calculation: Normalized directional movement = the exponential moving average of (positive directional index minus negative directional index) divided by their sum, using the same lookback for the directional movement smoothing and exponential average. The positive and negative directional indices use smoothed true range and smoothed positive and negative directional movement, respectively; use 0 when a denominator is zero.
Long: Normalized trend level crosses above normalized box midpoint AND (normalized directional movement is greater than or equal to zero OR its value two bars ago is below zero and strictly below its value one bar ago, which is strictly below its current value).
Short: Normalized trend level crosses below normalized box midpoint AND (normalized directional movement is less than or equal to zero OR its value two bars ago is above zero and strictly above its value one bar ago, which is strictly above its current value).
