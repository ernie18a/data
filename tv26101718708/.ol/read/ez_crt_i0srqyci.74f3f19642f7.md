Calculation: Bullish CRT = On a higher timeframe, the last completed C2 bar has a low below the preceding C1 bar’s low, closes within C1’s high–low range, and does not also sweep C1’s high (unless dual sweeps are allowed). Bearish CRT = the inverse.
Calculation: Automatic trend bias = On a higher timeframe, the latest two confirmed swing highs are higher and latest two confirmed swing lows are higher (bullish), or both are lower (bearish). A recent confirmed higher-timeframe swing-high sweep and bearish rejection overrides to bearish; a recent swing-low sweep and bullish rejection overrides to bullish. Rejection requires the close to reclaim the swing level, a directional candle, rejection wick at least the configured multiple of body size, and close in the configured strong-close portion of the bar. The override uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; automatic structure uses the higher-timeframe value available with lookahead off.
Calculation: Bullish execution shift = Execution-chart close exceeds the highest high of the preceding configured lookback bars; bearish execution shift = close is below the lowest low of those bars. A qualified shift also requires a directional candle whose range and body-to-range ratio meet their configured ATR thresholds when displacement is required.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = Current execution-chart low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish fair-value gap = current high is below the low two bars earlier. Gap size must meet the configured ATR threshold.
Calculation: Severe chop = Absolute net close change over the configured lookback divided by the sum of successive absolute close changes is at or below its threshold, AND candle-direction alternation ratio and two-sided-wick ratio are at or above their thresholds. A two-sided wick has each wick at least the configured fraction of that bar’s range.
Long: A bullish CRT is aligned with the permitted bias, is not already at its C1-high target or invalidated below the C2 sweep low, is within the entry window, and is selected for execution; simultaneous candidates select the smallest C1 range, with the shorter source timeframe breaking ties. OR, for the configured entry model: CRT Shift + FVG: after the execution-chart price first breaks above C2 high, the first qualifying bullish gap forms within the configured search window and its first retest occurs within the configured retest window, overlaps the gap, closes at or above its lower edge and bullish when directional close is required; OR Video Adaptive: in clean price action, price first breaks above C2 high, or in non-severe chop it first breaks above C2 high and then retests within the configured window, touching within ATR tolerance, closing at/above the trigger and bullish; OR Exact C2 Break: price crosses above C2 high by the configured wick or close trigger; OR Aggressive Structure Break: bullish execution shift; OR TBS + Structure Shift: a close below the preceding local lows occurred within the configured bars before a bullish execution shift; OR Break + Retest: after a qualified bullish execution shift, price retests its broken prior-high level within the configured window, touches within ATR tolerance, closes at/above that level and bullish. Entry requires the configured execution timeframe, confirmed execution-bar close if enabled, no prior session entry if limited to one, no severe chop if blocked, and the configured midpoint filter if enabled.
Short: A bearish CRT is aligned with the permitted bias, is not already at its C1-low target or invalidated above the C2 sweep high, is within the entry window, and is selected for execution; simultaneous candidates select the smallest C1 range, with the shorter source timeframe breaking ties. OR, for the configured entry model: CRT Shift + FVG: after the execution-chart price first breaks below C2 low, the first qualifying bearish gap forms within the configured search window and its first retest occurs within the configured retest window, overlaps the gap, closes at or below its upper edge and bearish when directional close is required; OR Video Adaptive: in clean price action, price first breaks below C2 low, or in non-severe chop it first breaks below C2 low and then retests within the configured window, touching within ATR tolerance, closing at/below the trigger and bearish; OR Exact C2 Break: price crosses below C2 low by the configured wick or close trigger; OR Aggressive Structure Break: bearish execution shift; OR TBS + Structure Shift: a close above the preceding local highs occurred within the configured bars before a bearish execution shift; OR Break + Retest: after a qualified bearish execution shift, price retests its broken prior-low level within the configured window, touches within ATR tolerance, closes at/below that level and bearish. Entry requires the configured execution timeframe, confirmed execution-bar close if enabled, no prior session entry if limited to one, no severe chop if blocked, and the configured midpoint filter if enabled.
