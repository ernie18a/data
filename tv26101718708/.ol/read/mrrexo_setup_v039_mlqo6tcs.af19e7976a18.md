Calculation: Fast band = moving averages of high and low using the shorter lookback; slow band = moving averages of high and low using the longer lookback. The average type is configurable.
Calculation: Fast/slow band midpoint = average of that band's high and low averages.
Long: Entry signals are enabled, the selected band and signal type are enabled, signal spacing has elapsed, the bands do not overlap and their gap is at least the configured minimum, the fast midpoint is above the slow midpoint, and both midpoints exceed their values at the trend lookback; AND at least one of: (close was below the selected band's lower edge within the cross lookback and close is now above its upper edge); (after that upward cross, within the return lookback, low reaches or falls below the upper edge and close is at or above the lower edge); (low reaches or falls below the upper edge and close is above it).
Short: Entry signals are enabled, the selected band and signal type are enabled, signal spacing has elapsed, the bands do not overlap and their gap is at least the configured minimum, the fast midpoint is below the slow midpoint, and both midpoints are below their values at the trend lookback; AND at least one of: (close was above the selected band's upper edge within the cross lookback and close is now below its lower edge); (after that downward cross, within the return lookback, high reaches or exceeds the lower edge and close is at or below the upper edge); (high reaches or exceeds the lower edge and close is below it). If both bands qualify for the same direction and signal type on a bar, use the configured fast-or-slow priority.
