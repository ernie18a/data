Calculation: Momentum = 7-period EMA of (0.20×VFI + 0.20×Laguerre RSI + 0.15×20×EMA(Fisher transform, 7) + 0.15×TSI/2 + 0.10×(MFI−50)/2 + 0.10×normalized OBV + 0.05×normalized 14-period EMA of the A/D line + 0.05×50×CMF); VFI uses hlc3 log-return standard deviation over 30 bars, a cutoff of 0.2×that deviation×close, prior-bar 14-period average volume capped at 2.5×average, and a 14-bar sum of signed capped volume divided by average volume and multiplied by 4; Laguerre RSI uses gamma 0.4 and four recursive stages, then 100×(CU/(CU+CD)−0.5); Fisher transform input is 0.1×((VFI+Laguerre RSI)/2)/1.5; normalized OBV and A/D each map their rolling 100-bar range to −50…50.
Calculation: Bullish system signals = count of: Momentum > 0 and rising; (active bullish order block or low > high two bars ago) and bullish market structure and close below the midpoint of the rolling 50-bar high-low range; velocity conditions (basis1 > basis2 and basis1 rising over 2 bars and basis1−basis5 > 5); a sweep below the latest 10-left/10-right pivot low with volume > 2.25×45-bar average volume; trend conditions (Hull MA rising over 3 bars, SuperTrend bullish, ADX > 20, EMA(9)>EMA(21)>EMA(50)); bullish RSI divergence with at least 2-of-4 oscillator confirmations and composite oscillator < −20; squeeze breakout upward with volatility index < 40; or institutional flow conditions (flow index > 10, CMF > 0, MFI > 50). Bullish market structure changes to bullish when a confirmed pivot high exceeds the prior highest pivot high or a confirmed pivot low is not lower than the prior lowest pivot low; a bullish order block activates on a confirmed pivot low when close exceeds the high 10 bars earlier, spans the prior bar's low-high, and stays active until close falls below its bottom. Bullish RSI divergence is a confirmed price pivot low below its previous confirmed pivot low while the corresponding RSI pivot low is higher; confirmations are RSI higher, MACD line higher than 5 bars ago, TSI higher than 5 bars ago, and smoothed stochastic higher than 5 bars ago. A squeeze breakout is a bar after squeeze-on (20-period, 2-deviation Bollinger Bands inside 20-period EMA ± 1.5 ATR Keltner Channels) when squeeze is off; upward means close > prior close. Volatility index is the mean of 0–100 rolling 100-bar percentiles of EMA(ATR(14),7), Bollinger width, and 20-bar log-return standard deviation×√252×100. Flow index = (50×CMF + MFI−50 + normalized OBV−50 + normalized A/D−50)/4; normalized OBV and A/D map their rolling 100-bar ranges to 0…100.
Calculation: Bearish system signals = count of: Momentum < 0 and falling; (active bearish order block or high < low two bars ago) and bearish market structure and close above the midpoint of the rolling 50-bar high-low range; velocity conditions (basis1 < basis2 and basis1 falling over 2 bars and basis1−basis5 < −5); a sweep above the latest 10-left/10-right pivot high with volume > 2.25×45-bar average volume; trend conditions (Hull MA falling over 3 bars, SuperTrend bearish, ADX > 20, EMA(9)<EMA(21)<EMA(50)); bearish RSI divergence with at least 2-of-4 oscillator confirmations and composite oscillator > 20; squeeze breakout downward with volatility index < 40; or institutional flow conditions (flow index < −10, CMF < 0, MFI < 50). Bearish market structure changes to bearish when a confirmed pivot low falls below the prior lowest pivot low or a confirmed pivot high is not higher than the prior highest pivot high; a bearish order block activates on a confirmed pivot high when close is below the low 10 bars earlier, spans the prior bar's low-high, and stays active until close rises above its top. Bearish RSI divergence is a confirmed price pivot high above its previous confirmed pivot high while the corresponding RSI pivot high is lower; confirmations are RSI lower, MACD line lower than 5 bars ago, TSI lower than 5 bars ago, and smoothed stochastic lower than 5 bars ago. A squeeze breakout is a bar after squeeze-on (20-period, 2-deviation Bollinger Bands inside 20-period EMA ± 1.5 ATR Keltner Channels) when squeeze is off; downward means close < prior close.
Calculation: Velocity bases = averages of EMA(momentum(21),9) and EMA(momentum(21),21) for basis1; EMA(21) and EMA(55) for basis2; EMA(55) and EMA(100) for basis3; EMA(100) and EMA(200) for basis4; EMA(200) and EMA(9) for basis5. All bullish velocity layers means momentum(21) is above each basis; all bearish means it is at or below each basis and at or below ALMA(close,9,0.85,6)−(ALMA−momentum(21)).
Calculation: Bullish and bearish confluence scores = respective system-signal count + respective bonus count + the positive or absolute negative part, respectively, of the sum of eight signed system scores (each system contributes +1 for its bullish condition, −1 for its bearish condition, otherwise 0). Bullish bonuses are: extreme bullish velocity (basis1 and adjusted ALMA above the 25-bar mean of the five bases plus 6 standard deviations, and adjusted ALMA higher than 3 bars ago); all bullish velocity layers; divergence confirmation count equals 4; and volume surge with flow index > 20. Bearish bonuses use the corresponding bearish conditions, including volume surge with flow index < −20. The divergence bonus is added to both sides when the confirmation count equals 4. The velocity system score is bullish for the bullish velocity conditions or all bullish layers, and bearish for the bearish velocity conditions or all bearish layers. Other system scores use their respective system conditions above.
Long: If confluence scoring is enabled, bullish confluence score ≥ minimum signals AND bullish confluence score > bearish confluence score; otherwise, bullish system signals ≥ minimum signals AND bullish system signals > bearish system signals.
Short: If confluence scoring is enabled, bearish confluence score ≥ minimum signals AND bearish confluence score > bullish confluence score; otherwise, bearish system signals ≥ minimum signals AND bearish system signals > bullish system signals.
Exit: Long, low ≤ prior-bar close − ATR(10)×stop-loss multiplier OR high ≥ prior-bar close + ATR(10)×stop-loss multiplier×risk-reward ratio OR (trailing enabled AND low ≤ prior-bar close − ATR(10)×trailing multiplier).
Exit: Short, high ≥ prior-bar close + ATR(10)×stop-loss multiplier OR low ≤ prior-bar close − ATR(10)×stop-loss multiplier×risk-reward ratio OR (trailing enabled AND high ≥ prior-bar close + ATR(10)×trailing multiplier).
