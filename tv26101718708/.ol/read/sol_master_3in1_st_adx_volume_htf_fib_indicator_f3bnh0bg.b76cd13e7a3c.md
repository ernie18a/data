Calculation: Higher-timeframe swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the longer lookback, using completed higher-timeframe bars only; swing range = max(high − low, minimum price increment). Bullish retracement zone = [swing high − range × longer retracement fraction, swing high − range × shorter retracement fraction]; bearish retracement zone = [swing low + range × shorter retracement fraction, swing low + range × longer retracement fraction]. Fib touch = chart close was inside the relevant zone within the configured recent-bar lookback.
Long: Confirmed chart bar AND bullish Supertrend (if enabled) AND ADX above threshold (and rising if required) AND ATR above its moving average AND ATR / close above minimum (if ATR filter enabled) AND volume above its moving average × multiplier AND volume above prior bar if required AND close > open (if volume filter enabled) AND fib touch (if enabled) AND close > prior bar high (if break filter enabled) AND absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × minimum body multiplier (if body filter enabled) AND the selected trigger is true: close > prior rolling Donchian high (or high > that level if close-break requirement is disabled); OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND lowest low over the pullback lookback ≤ fast EMA AND close > prior bar high; OR close > Keltner upper band AND close > prior bar high. If one signal per side is enabled, the raw long condition must be false on the prior bar.
Short: Confirmed chart bar AND bearish Supertrend (if enabled) AND ADX above threshold (and rising if required) AND ATR above its moving average AND ATR / close above minimum (if ATR filter enabled) AND volume above its moving average × multiplier AND volume above prior bar if required AND close < open (if volume filter enabled) AND fib touch (if enabled) AND close < prior bar low (if break filter enabled) AND absolute(close − open) ≥ ATR × minimum body multiplier (if body filter enabled) AND the selected trigger is true: close < prior rolling Donchian low (or low < that level if close-break requirement is disabled); OR fast EMA < slow EMA AND highest high over the pullback lookback ≥ fast EMA AND close < prior bar low; OR close < Keltner lower band AND close < prior bar low. If one signal per side is enabled, the raw short condition must be false on the prior bar.
