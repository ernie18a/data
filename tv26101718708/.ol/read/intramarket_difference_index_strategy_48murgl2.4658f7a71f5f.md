Calculation: Intramarket Difference Index (IDI) = For the chart market and base market, subtract the base-market difference series from the chart-market difference series. In MA mode, each difference series is (close − simple moving average) / √(ATR × MA lookback); in RSI mode, it is RSI. Use chart-timeframe bars for both markets. The trend threshold is the MA threshold in MA mode and the RSI lookback in RSI mode.
Calculation: Fisher-transformed IDI = Normalize IDI to its rolling range, smooth the normalized value recursively with weights 0.33 current and 0.67 previous, clamp it to [−0.99, 0.99], apply 0.5 × ln((1+x)/(1−x)), then smooth recursively with weights 0.5 current and 0.5 previous.
Long: In TREND mode, IDI[1] ≥ threshold AND IDI[2] < threshold AND (Hurst regime filter disabled OR smoothed Hurst exponent ≥ threshold); OR in MEAN REVERSION mode, Fisher-transformed IDI[1] ≥ 2.5 AND Fisher-transformed IDI[2] < 2.5 AND (Hurst regime filter disabled OR smoothed Hurst exponent < threshold). For an actual entry, also require prior-bar position flat AND close between the Donchian lower and upper channels.
Short: In TREND mode, IDI[1] ≤ −threshold AND IDI[2] > −threshold AND (Hurst regime filter disabled OR smoothed Hurst exponent ≥ threshold); OR in MEAN REVERSION mode, Fisher-transformed IDI[1] ≤ −2.5 AND Fisher-transformed IDI[2] > −2.5 AND (Hurst regime filter disabled OR smoothed Hurst exponent < threshold). For an actual entry, also require prior-bar position flat AND close between the Donchian lower and upper channels.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − chart-timeframe ATR × stop multiplier OR close ≤ Donchian lower channel OR low ≤ Donchian lower channel OR (ATR take-profit enabled AND high ≥ entry price + chart-timeframe ATR × take-profit multiplier)
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + chart-timeframe ATR × stop multiplier OR close ≥ Donchian upper channel OR high ≥ Donchian upper channel OR (ATR take-profit enabled AND low ≤ entry price − chart-timeframe ATR × take-profit multiplier)
Calculation: Donchian upper/lower channel = highest/lowest close over their respective lookbacks, excluding the current bar. Calculate on chart timeframe or the selected higher timeframe; use completed bars of that timeframe.
