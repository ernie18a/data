Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low. Structure state starts neutral and changes to bullish/bearish on the corresponding break; BOS is a break when the prior state is not the opposite direction, and CHoCH is a break when it is.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity sweep = high above the latest confirmed swing high and close below it / low below the latest confirmed swing low and close above it.
Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (daily timeframe, higher than the chart timeframe when the chart is intraday).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low above the high two bars earlier / current high below the low two bars earlier, with gap size greater than ATR times the configured minimum-size multiple.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish institutional zone = after bullish/bearish displacement (close above prior high / below prior low and candle body larger than ATR times the configured displacement multiple), the prior candle is bearish/bullish; zone bounds are prior candle low to body top / prior candle high to body bottom.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = average of the highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback; discount is close below it and premium is close above it.
Long: Bullish structure break with prior bearish state (CHoCH) OR bullish structure break with prior state not bearish (BOS) OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish fair value gap OR bullish institutional zone OR (a bullish liquidity sweep or a low below previous-day low with close above it occurred within the configured sweep lookback, and price range intersects the latest bullish institutional zone or bullish fair value gap, and close > open and close > prior high, and (premium/discount filter disabled or close < premium/discount midpoint), and (trend filter disabled or short-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA), and (one-trade-at-a-time disabled or position is flat)).
Short: Bearish structure break with prior bullish state (CHoCH) OR bearish structure break with prior state not bullish (BOS) OR bearish liquidity sweep OR bearish fair value gap OR bearish institutional zone OR (a bearish liquidity sweep or a high above previous-day high with close below it occurred within the configured sweep lookback, and price range intersects the latest bearish institutional zone or bearish fair value gap, and close < open and close < prior low, and (premium/discount filter disabled or close > premium/discount midpoint), and (trend filter disabled or short-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA), and (one-trade-at-a-time disabled or position is flat)).
Exit: long, low <= the greater of the initial stop and (after high reaches entry plus initial risk, entry price; if trailing is enabled, close minus ATR times the configured trail multiple) OR high >= entry plus initial risk times the configured reward/risk multiple
Exit: short, high >= the lesser of the initial stop and (after low reaches entry minus initial risk, entry price; if trailing is enabled, close plus ATR times the configured trail multiple) OR low <= entry minus initial risk times the configured reward/risk multiple
