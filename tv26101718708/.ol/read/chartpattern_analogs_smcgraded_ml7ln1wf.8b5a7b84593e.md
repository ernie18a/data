Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; trend regime if > trend threshold, chop regime if < chop threshold, otherwise mixed.
Calculation: Pattern candidate = extract 7 perceptually important points from the lookback window (start and end, then repeatedly insert the point with greatest vertical deviation from the line between its surrounding selected points); normalize prices to the window's low–high range. Select the best-fitting family from double top/bottom, head-and-shoulders/inverse, and triple top/bottom templates by normalized RMSE, if fit meets its threshold. Also fit regression lines to at least two interior PIP highs and lows; if the lower of their R² values meets its threshold, classify by line slopes and channel-width change: widening = neutral broadening; flat highs with rising lows = long ascending triangle; flat lows with falling highs = short descending triangle; falling highs with rising lows = neutral symmetrical triangle; both lines rising = short rising wedge if width contracts enough, otherwise long ascending channel; both falling = long falling wedge if width contracts enough, otherwise short descending channel. Keep whichever template or geometric candidate has greater fit; reject if neither qualifies.
Calculation: Candidate direction = long for double bottom, inverse head-and-shoulders, triple bottom, ascending triangle, and falling wedge; short for double top, head-and-shoulders, triple top, descending triangle, rising wedge, and descending channel; neutral for symmetrical triangle and broadening.
Calculation: Analog direction and confidence = when the resolved-pattern memory has at least its minimum size, find the k nearest prior normalized PIP shapes by dynamic time warping; direction is the sign of their mean signed forward close change at the resolution horizon, measured in entry ATRs. Confidence is the fraction of those neighbors whose signed change agrees with that direction and reaches the follow-through ATR threshold. Otherwise use candidate direction and candidate fit as confidence.
Calculation: Motif strength = 1 − nearest-neighbor DTW distance divided by the mean DTW distance to the memory.
Calculation: Swing levels = confirmed pivot highs and lows using equal left and right swing lookbacks. Equal-high/low pools form from consecutive same-side pivots within an ATR-scaled tolerance; sweep low/high when price wicks beyond the corresponding pool or latest swing and closes back across it (a latest-swing sweep also requires a bullish/bearish candle, respectively).
Calculation: Dealing-range position = (close − latest swing low) / (latest swing high − latest swing low). Favorable location is below 0.5 for long and above 0.5 for short.
Calculation: Setup quality = 0.34 × min(1, true range / ATR / 2) + 0.20 × min(1, candle body / candle range / 0.8) + 0.20 × min(1, max(0, (volume z-score over 20 bars + 0.5) / 2.5)) + 0.26 × sweep indicator.
Calculation: Setup grade = grade of the weighted score 100 × (0.50 × confidence + 0.30 × setup quality when quality is enabled + 0.10 × sweep indicator + 0.10 × favorable-location indicator) / sum of enabled weights; grades are A+ at ≥80, A at ≥68, B at ≥55, C at ≥45, otherwise below C.
Long: confirmed bar AND candidate exists AND candidate's final pivot is within the completion-recency lookback AND bars since prior emission ≥ minimum gap AND candidate height ≥ minimum ATR multiple AND NOT (regime veto enabled AND ((candidate is reversal AND trend regime) OR (candidate is continuation AND chop regime) OR (range-pattern veto enabled AND candidate is range pattern AND chop regime))) AND ((analog memory is mature AND analog direction > 0) OR (analog memory is not mature AND candidate direction > 0)) AND (motif threshold disabled OR analog memory is not mature OR motif strength ≥ motif threshold) AND NOT (location veto enabled AND dealing-range position is known AND position > premium threshold) AND (SMC disabled OR minimum grade disabled OR setup grade meets minimum grade).
Short: confirmed bar AND candidate exists AND candidate's final pivot is within the completion-recency lookback AND bars since prior emission ≥ minimum gap AND candidate height ≥ minimum ATR multiple AND NOT (regime veto enabled AND ((candidate is reversal AND trend regime) OR (candidate is continuation AND chop regime) OR (range-pattern veto enabled AND candidate is range pattern AND chop regime))) AND ((analog memory is mature AND analog direction < 0) OR (analog memory is not mature AND candidate direction < 0)) AND (motif threshold disabled OR analog memory is not mature OR motif strength ≥ motif threshold) AND NOT (location veto enabled AND dealing-range position is known AND position < 1 − premium threshold) AND (SMC disabled OR minimum grade disabled OR setup grade meets minimum grade).
