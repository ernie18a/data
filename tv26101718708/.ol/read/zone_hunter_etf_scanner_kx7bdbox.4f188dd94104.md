Calculation: Impulse percentage = (highest high over the impulse lookback − lowest low over the impulse lookback) / lowest low × 100
Calculation: Bull retracement = (highest high over the impulse lookback − close) / (highest high − lowest low) × 100; bear retracement = (close − lowest low over the impulse lookback) / (highest high − lowest low) × 100
Calculation: Flag range percentage = (highest high over the flag lookback − lowest low over the flag lookback) / close × 100
Calculation: Flag slope = arctangent of (linear regression of close over the flag lookback at offset 0 − the same regression at offset 1), in degrees
Calculation: Demand zone = lowest low over 5 bars through that low plus ATR × zone-thickness multiplier; supply zone = highest high over 5 bars minus ATR × zone-thickness multiplier through that high
Calculation: Demand freshness = offset of the lowest low in the 5-bar window ≤ freshness lookback; supply freshness = offset of the highest high in the 5-bar window ≤ freshness lookback
Calculation: Bull flag found = impulse percentage ≥ minimum impulse percentage AND bull retracement ≤ maximum retracement percentage AND flag range percentage ≤ maximum flag range percentage AND flag slope ≤ 5°; bear flag found = impulse percentage ≥ minimum impulse percentage AND bear retracement ≤ maximum retracement percentage AND flag range percentage ≤ maximum flag range percentage AND flag slope ≥ −5°
Calculation: Demand active = demand freshness AND close above the demand zone's upper boundary when close-out is required, otherwise above its lower boundary; supply active = supply freshness AND close below the supply zone's lower boundary when close-out is required, otherwise below its upper boundary
Calculation: Bull flag active = bull flag found AND close above the flag's lowest low; bear flag active = bear flag found AND close below the flag's highest high
Calculation: Bull flag score = clamp01(0.34 × clamp01((impulse percentage − minimum impulse percentage) / (3 × minimum impulse percentage)) + 0.26 × clamp01(1 − bull retracement / (1.10 × maximum retracement percentage)) + 0.20 × clamp01(1 − flag range percentage / (1.15 × maximum flag range percentage)) + 0.20 × (1 − clamp01(abs(max(flag slope, 0)) / 25)))^1.55; bear flag score uses bear retracement and abs(min(flag slope, 0)) instead
Calculation: Demand score = clamp01(0.58 × clamp01(1 − demand-low offset / (1.25 × freshness lookback)) + 0.22 × clamp01((close − demand-zone upper boundary) / (2.5 × ATR)) + 0.20 × clamp01((close − demand-zone lower boundary) / (3 × ATR)))^1.75; supply score mirrors this using the supply-high offset and distances from close to the supply-zone boundaries
Calculation: Candidate score = its score × 0.55 if inactive, otherwise its score; selected setup = highest-scoring found candidate among demand, supply, bull flag, and bear flag, with ties resolved in that order
Long: selected setup is demand or bull flag
Short: selected setup is supply or bear flag
