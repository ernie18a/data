Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the chosen lookback number of bars on both sides; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago, with (current low − high two bars ago) / high two bars ago × 100 ≥ the minimum gap size. Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago, with (low two bars ago − current high) / current high × 100 ≥ the minimum gap size.
Long: Close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high AND close > EMA(200) AND label cooldown has elapsed; OR a bullish fair value gap is awaited after this condition, price retests its zone (low ≤ gap top AND close ≥ gap bottom), close > the highest high of the preceding breakout lookback bars, signal cooldown has elapsed, and the daily signal limit is not reached; OR close crosses above the most recently confirmed swing high AND close < EMA(200) AND label cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Close crosses below the most recently confirmed swing low AND close < EMA(200) AND label cooldown has elapsed; OR a bearish fair value gap is awaited after this condition, price retests its zone (high ≥ gap bottom AND close ≤ gap top), close < the lowest low of the preceding breakout lookback bars, signal cooldown has elapsed, and the daily signal limit is not reached; OR close crosses below the most recently confirmed swing low AND close > EMA(200) AND label cooldown has elapsed.
