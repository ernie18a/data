Calculation: Heikin-Ashi candles = Heikin-Ashi open, high, low, and close
Calculation: %K = 3-period SMA of 10-period stochastic of close, high, and low
Calculation: %D = 3-period SMA of %K
Calculation: fast Heikin-Ashi open and close = 5-period EMA of Heikin-Ashi open and close from a lower timeframe
Calculation: major Heikin-Ashi open and close = 8-period EMA of Heikin-Ashi open and close from a higher timeframe
Calculation: EMA9 = 9-period EMA of close
Calculation: EMA18 = 18-period EMA of close
Long: %K crosses above %D AND %K < 30 AND fast Heikin-Ashi close > fast Heikin-Ashi open AND major Heikin-Ashi close > major Heikin-Ashi open
Long: %K crosses above %D AND %K < 30 AND fast Heikin-Ashi close > fast Heikin-Ashi open AND major Heikin-Ashi close > major Heikin-Ashi open AND close > EMA9
