Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average.
Calculation: Money flow = EMA of 0.30 × centered MFI + 0.30 × CMF × 100 + 0.25 × clipped OBV deviation z-score × 28 + 0.15 × clipped price-volume pressure; centered MFI = (MFI − 50) × 2; CMF = volume-weighted close-location pressure over its lookback; OBV deviation z-score = (OBV − its EMA) / its standard deviation over the OBV trend lookback; price-volume pressure = 30 × min(relative volume, 3) × (0.65 × close location in bar range mapped to [−1, 1] + 0.35 × candle body direction), where body direction is 1 for up, −1 for down, and 0 for unchanged. Clip the OBV and price-volume components to [−100, 100].
Calculation: Flow signal = EMA of money flow with a longer smoothing lookback than the money-flow smoothing lookback.
Calculation: Smart money slope = current smart money line minus its prior-bar value; smart money line is an EMA of money flow.
Calculation: Relative flow strength score = min(100, 0.8 × |money flow| + 10 × min(relative volume, 3) + 2 × |smart money slope|).
Calculation: Divergence score = min(100, 1.5 × |money flow − money flow at the divergence lookback| + 8 × |smart money slope| + 40).
Calculation: Accumulation score = min(100, accumulation base + clipped accumulation flow component + accumulation turn component + clipped volume component); base is 48 in an active accumulation zone, 30 for an accumulation candidate, otherwise 0; flow component is clip((smart money + 28) × 0.65, 0, 30); turn component is 12 if the smart money line has risen for two consecutive bars or money flow turns up, otherwise 0; volume component is clip(relative volume × 6, 0, 10).
Calculation: Distribution score = min(100, distribution base + clipped distribution flow component + distribution turn component + clipped volume component); base is 48 in an active distribution zone, 30 for a distribution candidate, otherwise 0; flow component is clip((28 − smart money) × 0.65, 0, 30); turn component is 12 if the smart money line has fallen for two consecutive bars or money flow turns down, otherwise 0; volume component is clip(relative volume × 6, 0, 10).
Calculation: Bullish divergence = current low is below the lowest low over the preceding divergence lookback, money flow is above its preceding lookback low, money flow turns up, smart money slope is positive, and the bullish flow-confirm condition is false.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = current high is above the highest high over the preceding divergence lookback, money flow is below its preceding lookback high, money flow turns down, smart money slope is negative, and the bearish flow-out condition is false.
Long: Money flow > its positive confirmation threshold AND smart money slope > 0 AND money flow > flow signal AND relative volume meets its confirmation threshold AND relative flow strength score meets its minimum threshold AND no bullish flow-confirm signal occurred within its cooldown AND, if the short-term-low filter is enabled, the close is not within its configured fraction of the lowest low over the divergence lookback.
Short: Money flow < the negative confirmation threshold AND smart money slope < 0 AND money flow < flow signal AND relative volume meets its confirmation threshold AND relative flow strength score meets its minimum threshold AND no bearish flow-out signal occurred within its cooldown AND, if the short-term-low filter is enabled, the close is not within its configured fraction of the lowest low over the divergence lookback.
Long: For the configured minimum number of consecutive bars, close is at or below its accumulation-lookback prior close OR the high-low range over the accumulation lookback is no greater than ATR times its configured flat-range factor; close is in the lower part of that lookback range; smart money is rising; money flow > flow signal + its configured margin; smart money > its configured lower bound; relative volume meets its minimum; accumulation score meets its minimum; the accumulation zone has just started; and no accumulation signal occurred within its cooldown.
Short: For the configured minimum number of consecutive bars, close is at or above its distribution-lookback prior close OR the high-low range over the distribution lookback is no greater than ATR times its configured flat-range factor; close is in the upper part of that lookback range; smart money is falling; money flow < flow signal − its configured margin; smart money < its configured upper bound; relative volume meets its minimum; distribution score meets its minimum; the distribution zone has just started; and no distribution signal occurred within its cooldown.
Long: Bullish divergence is true AND divergence score meets its minimum AND no divergence signal of either direction occurred within its cooldown.
Short: Bearish divergence is true AND divergence score meets its minimum AND no divergence signal of either direction occurred within its cooldown.
