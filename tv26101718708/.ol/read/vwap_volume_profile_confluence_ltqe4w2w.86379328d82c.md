Calculation: Anchored VWAP = within each daily or weekly anchor period, cumulative volume-weighted average of each chart bar’s (high + low + close) / 3, including the current forming bar; use the selected anchor period, which is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: VWAP bands = anchored VWAP plus or minus a parameterized multiple of the cumulative volume-weighted standard deviation of chart-bar typical prices from anchored VWAP, including the current forming bar.
Calculation: Session volume profile = divide the current anchor period’s expanding high-low range into 50 equal price buckets; add each chart bar’s volume to the bucket nearest its typical price. POC is the midpoint of the highest-volume bucket. Value Area High and Low are the outer bucket midpoints after expanding from the POC toward the adjacent bucket with more volume until at least 70% of total volume is covered. Use the current forming anchor period, which is higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: VWAP slope = (current anchored VWAP − anchored VWAP from a parameterized lookback of chart bars) / current band width × 100; rising means this exceeds a positive sensitivity threshold, falling means it is below the negative threshold.
Calculation: Confluence tolerance = current anchor period high-low range × a parameterized percentage.
Calculation: Upper-band confluence = upper band is within the confluence tolerance of the POC or Value Area High.
Calculation: Lower-band confluence = lower band is within the confluence tolerance of the POC or Value Area Low.
Calculation: At upper band = close ≥ upper band × (1 − confluence percentage); at lower band = close ≤ lower band × (1 + confluence percentage).
Calculation: Near VWAP = absolute distance between close and anchored VWAP < 15% of the distance between the upper and lower bands.
Calculation: Sustained trend = (close > VWAP and close from 5 bars ago > VWAP from 5 bars ago and close from 10 bars ago > VWAP from 10 bars ago) OR (close < VWAP and close from 5 bars ago < VWAP from 5 bars ago and close from 10 bars ago < VWAP from 10 bars ago).
Long: (at lower band AND lower-band confluence) OR (VWAP rising AND near VWAP AND sustained trend AND NOT (at lower band AND lower-band confluence)) OR (VWAP rising AND close > VWAP AND NOT at upper band AND NOT (at lower band AND lower-band confluence) AND NOT (VWAP rising AND near VWAP AND sustained trend AND NOT (at lower band AND lower-band confluence)))
Short: (at upper band AND upper-band confluence) OR (VWAP falling AND near VWAP AND sustained trend AND NOT (at upper band AND upper-band confluence)) OR (VWAP falling AND close < VWAP AND NOT at lower band AND NOT (at upper band AND upper-band confluence) AND NOT (VWAP falling AND near VWAP AND sustained trend AND NOT (at upper band AND upper-band confluence)))
