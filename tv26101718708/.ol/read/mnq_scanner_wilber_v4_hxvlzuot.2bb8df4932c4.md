Calculation: Long pullback = on the higher-timeframe bar immediately before the current one, low <= the shorter-term higher-timeframe EMA and close > that EMA; on the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, close > that EMA and close > the previous bar’s close
Calculation: Short pullback = on the higher-timeframe bar immediately before the current one, high >= the shorter-term higher-timeframe EMA and close < that EMA; on the current, still-forming higher-timeframe bar, close < that EMA and close < the previous bar’s close
Calculation: Momentum candle = previous chart bar has a body-to-range ratio >= the configured minimum, body size >= the configured minimum, and closes in the top quarter for bullish momentum or bottom quarter for bearish momentum
