Calculation: Delta = sum of volume from lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, signed positive when a bar closes above its open and negative when it closes below; use chart bars when the chart timeframe is at or below one minute, and estimate as volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) when lower-timeframe data is unavailable. Evaluate signals at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Delta percentile thresholds = rolling percentiles of absolute Delta over the configured lookback; the strong-bubble threshold is higher than the bubble threshold.
Calculation: Absorption = nonzero Delta whose absolute value reaches its configured percentile threshold, and whose candle body is within its configured ATR fraction or whose candle closes against Delta, and whose high (positive Delta) or low (negative Delta) reaches the recent lookback extreme, and whose rejection wick on that side reaches its configured fraction of the candle range; enforce the configured minimum spacing between absorption signals.
Calculation: Bubble level = bubble close minus ATR times the configured buffer for positive Delta, or plus ATR times the configured buffer for negative Delta.
Calculation: Strong bubble = bubble whose absolute Delta reaches the strong-bubble percentile threshold and whose absolute Delta as a percentage of volume reaches the configured dominance threshold.
Long: Delta < 0 AND (Absorption at a recent low OR (absolute Delta reaches the bubble percentile threshold AND close < open AND not Absorption AND (Strong bubble OR enough bars since the previous bubble)) OR a sell bubble closes above its Bubble level within the configured window after the bubble OR a strong sell bubble's ray is broken by a close above its upper boundary).
Short: Delta > 0 AND (Absorption at a recent high OR (absolute Delta reaches the bubble percentile threshold AND close > open AND not Absorption AND (Strong bubble OR enough bars since the previous bubble)) OR a buy bubble closes below its Bubble level within the configured window after the bubble OR a strong buy bubble's ray is broken by a close below its lower boundary).
