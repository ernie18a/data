Calculation: Selected moving average = ALMA, EMA, VWMA, Hull moving average, or EMA(close) × (1 + True Strength Index of close)
Calculation: Moving-average slope = selected moving average − its value at the longer lookback
Calculation: KSI = true range² ÷ (volume × EMA(volume)) × its multiplier; KSI signal = EMA(KSI)
Long: ADX ≥ its threshold AND (ADX is rising if required) AND KSI filter passes AND cooldown since last exit has passed AND (moving-average slope crosses above zero OR mid-trend re-entry is enabled AND moving-average slope > 0 AND moving-average slope > slope at last exit AND moving-average slope is rising for the required consecutive bars) OR ADX ≥ its threshold AND (ADX is rising if required) AND KSI filter passes AND cooldown since last exit has passed AND (close crosses above selected moving average OR the same mid-trend re-entry condition)
Short: ADX ≥ its threshold AND (ADX is rising if required) AND KSI filter passes AND cooldown since last exit has passed AND (moving-average slope crosses below zero OR mid-trend re-entry is enabled AND moving-average slope < 0 AND moving-average slope < slope at last exit AND moving-average slope is falling for the required consecutive bars) OR ADX ≥ its threshold AND (ADX is rising if required) AND KSI filter passes AND cooldown since last exit has passed AND (close crosses below selected moving average OR the same mid-trend re-entry condition)
Exit: Long, close crosses below selected moving average OR close crosses below VWMA OR moving-average slope has faded while positive for the required consecutive bars OR moving-average slope crosses below zero OR KSI exceeds its spike threshold OR low ≤ dynamic stop price OR high ≥ take-profit price OR trailing stop is triggered
Exit: Short, close crosses above selected moving average OR close crosses above VWMA OR moving-average slope has strengthened while negative for the required consecutive bars OR moving-average slope crosses above zero OR KSI exceeds its spike threshold OR high ≥ dynamic stop price OR low ≤ take-profit price OR trailing stop is triggered
