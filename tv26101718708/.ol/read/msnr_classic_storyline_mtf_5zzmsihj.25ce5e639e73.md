Calculation: Structural levels = At the close of each completed bar, each consecutive non-doji candle pair creates a level at the earlier candle's close: green-red creates an A resistance; red-green creates a V support; green-green creates a bullish-gap support; red-red creates a bearish-gap resistance. A close through a level flips its side and makes it fresh again. A level is fresh until first rejected on its current side; thereafter it cannot trigger another rejection until flipped. Process only completed bars. The higher timeframe is higher than its paired lower timeframe; the lower timeframe is lower than its paired higher timeframe.
Long: On a completed higher-timeframe bar, a fresh support level has low <= level and close > level; if several qualify, use the lowest. Starting with the lower-timeframe bar opening at the rejection bar's open, wait for the first bar whose low <= rejected level. At that touch, lock the nearest earlier A resistance level on the lower timeframe; then, on a later lower-timeframe bar, close > locked A level. The earliest completing setup determines the bullish marking; a same-bar tie between setups favors the newer rejection. Invalidate a pending setup if a completed higher-timeframe bar closes below its rejected support.
Short: On a completed higher-timeframe bar, a fresh resistance level has high >= level and close < level; if several qualify, use the highest. Starting with the lower-timeframe bar opening at the rejection bar's open, wait for the first bar whose high >= rejected level. At that touch, lock the nearest earlier V support level on the lower timeframe; then, on a later lower-timeframe bar, close < locked V level. The earliest completing setup determines the bearish marking; a same-bar tie between setups favors the newer rejection. Invalidate a pending setup if a completed higher-timeframe bar closes above its rejected resistance.
