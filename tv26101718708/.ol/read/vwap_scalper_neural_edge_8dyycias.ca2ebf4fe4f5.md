Calculation: Volume-weighted delta = EMA of ((close − low) − (high − close)) / bar range, multiplied by volume divided by its recent average and capped at a configurable maximum; use 1 when volume or its average is unavailable or the average is zero.
Calculation: Velocity = EMA of (close − close from a configurable number of bars earlier) / ATR.
Calculation: Acceleration = current velocity − velocity from the same configurable number of bars earlier.
Long: (close ≤ VWAP − upper-band ATR multiple × ATR + reversal-zone ATR multiple × ATR AND volume-weighted delta > its previous value AND acceleration > 0 AND at least the configured minimum number of these hold: volume-weighted delta > its positive threshold, close > VWAP, velocity > its positive threshold) OR (close crosses above VWAP OR (close > VWAP AND volume-weighted delta crosses above its positive momentum threshold), AND at least the configured minimum number of the same bullish conditions hold) OR (close > VWAP − upper-band ATR multiple × ATR AND close < VWAP + bounce-zone ATR multiple × ATR AND volume-weighted delta > its previous value > its value two bars earlier AND volume-weighted delta > its positive bounce threshold AND at least the configured minimum number of the same bullish conditions hold); AND current time is within the London or New York session on a weekday (UTC) AND the higher-timeframe close is above its VWAP (use the bar still forming when available) AND the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since either signal.
Short: (close ≥ VWAP + upper-band ATR multiple × ATR − reversal-zone ATR multiple × ATR AND volume-weighted delta < its previous value AND acceleration < 0 AND at least the configured minimum number of these hold: volume-weighted delta < its negative threshold, close < VWAP, velocity < its negative threshold) OR (close crosses below VWAP OR (close < VWAP AND volume-weighted delta crosses below its negative momentum threshold), AND at least the configured minimum number of the same bearish conditions hold) OR (close < VWAP + upper-band ATR multiple × ATR AND close > VWAP − bounce-zone ATR multiple × ATR AND volume-weighted delta < its previous value < its value two bars earlier AND volume-weighted delta < its negative bounce threshold AND at least the configured minimum number of the same bearish conditions hold); AND current time is within the London or New York session on a weekday (UTC) AND the higher-timeframe close is below its VWAP (use the bar still forming when available) AND the configured minimum number of bars has elapsed since either signal.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − entry-bar ATR × configured stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + 3 × entry-bar ATR × configured stop multiplier
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + entry-bar ATR × configured stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − 3 × entry-bar ATR × configured stop multiplier
