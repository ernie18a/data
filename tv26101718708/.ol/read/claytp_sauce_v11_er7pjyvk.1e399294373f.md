Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a chart-timeframe high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; usable only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Short-, medium-, and long-lookback SMA slopes = each SMA of close minus its value one chart bar earlier; lookbacks increase from short to medium to long.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of OHLC4 since the configured daily anchor time; calculate separately for each enabled anchor.
Calculation: Long stop = selected prior swing low (optionally a configured fraction of the entry-to-swing distance), entry-bar low, or entry price minus a configured percentage, then subtract configured point and percentage buffers.
Calculation: Short stop = selected prior swing high (optionally a configured fraction of the entry-to-swing distance), entry-bar high, or entry price plus a configured percentage, then add configured point and percentage buffers.
Calculation: Long target = entry price + (entry price - long stop) × configured reward-to-risk ratio.
Calculation: Short target = entry price - (short stop - entry price) × configured reward-to-risk ratio.
Long: latest confirmed pivot high is active AND latest confirmed pivot high > latest confirmed pivot low AND (configured tick entry and pivot high > current high and price reaches pivot high OR configured close entry and close > pivot high) AND selected signal type permits the break (BOS when the prior break direction is not down, CHoCH when it is down, or either when both types are selected) AND long direction is allowed AND position is flat or short AND the pivot has not already been used for a long entry today AND daily trade limit is not reached AND current time is inside the configured trading window if enabled AND selected SMA slope filter passes: all three slopes > 0, or at least two > 0, or short- and medium-lookback slopes > 0, or short-lookback slope > 0, or close > all three SMAs, according to the selected mode; AND each enabled stacking filter has short-lookback SMA > medium-lookback SMA > long-lookback SMA AND each enabled divergence filter has short-lookback slope > medium-lookback slope > long-lookback slope AND close > each enabled anchored VWAP AND, for close entries when enabled, close > open AND abs(close - open) >= max(high - low - abs(close - open), 0) × configured body-to-wick factor AND long stop < entry price < long target.
Short: latest confirmed pivot low is active AND latest confirmed pivot low < latest confirmed pivot high AND (configured tick entry and pivot low < current low and price reaches pivot low OR configured close entry and close < pivot low) AND selected signal type permits the break (BOS when the prior break direction is not up, CHoCH when it is up, or either when both types are selected) AND short direction is allowed AND position is flat or long AND the pivot has not already been used for a short entry today AND daily trade limit is not reached AND current time is inside the configured trading window if enabled AND selected SMA slope filter passes: all three slopes < 0, or at least two < 0, or short- and medium-lookback slopes < 0, or short-lookback slope < 0, or close < all three SMAs, according to the selected mode; AND each enabled stacking filter has short-lookback SMA < medium-lookback SMA < long-lookback SMA AND each enabled divergence filter has short-lookback slope < medium-lookback slope < long-lookback slope AND close < each enabled anchored VWAP AND, for close entries when enabled, close < open AND abs(close - open) >= max(high - low - abs(close - open), 0) × configured body-to-wick factor AND short target < entry price < short stop.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR a new day starts when end-of-day closing is enabled OR long stop >= long target OR long stop >= average entry price OR long target <= average entry price.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR a new day starts when end-of-day closing is enabled OR short stop <= short target OR short stop <= average entry price OR short target >= average entry price.
