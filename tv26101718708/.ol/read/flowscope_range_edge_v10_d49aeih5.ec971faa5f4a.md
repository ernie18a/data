Calculation: Edge-zone width = ATR over a lookback, multiplied by a parameter and bounded by minimum and maximum widths.
Calculation: Range high and range low = the higher and lower of the two configured manual range levels.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average over a lookback.
Long: During the configured active session with a valid range, (the bar overlaps the lower edge zone AND low <= range low AND close > range low AND close > open) OR (close crosses above range high plus a configured buffer) OR (after an upside breakout, the required number of recent closes are above range high, and the bar overlaps the upper edge zone, closes above range high, and closes above open) OR (after a lower-edge rejection confirmation with no intervening opposite confirmation or trigger, current high > that confirmation bar's high) OR (after a qualifying upper-edge retest with no intervening trigger, current high > that retest bar's high and, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its configured threshold).
Short: During the configured active session with a valid range, (the bar overlaps the upper edge zone AND high >= range high AND close < range high AND close < open) OR (close crosses below range low minus a configured buffer) OR (after a downside breakout, the required number of recent closes are below range low, and the bar overlaps the lower edge zone, closes below range low, and closes below open) OR (after an upper-edge rejection confirmation with no intervening opposite confirmation or trigger, current low < that confirmation bar's low) OR (after a qualifying lower-edge retest with no intervening trigger, current low < that retest bar's low and, if the volume filter is enabled, relative volume >= its configured threshold).
