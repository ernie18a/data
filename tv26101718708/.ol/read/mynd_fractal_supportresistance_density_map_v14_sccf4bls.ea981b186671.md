Calculation: Fractal S/R zones = assign price bins scores when symmetric swing highs or lows confirm at short-, medium-, and long-term lookbacks; longer-term pivots have greater weights, and each pivot’s contribution is scaled by its relative volume (capped). Multiply all scores by a decay factor each bar. Rank bins by score, merge nearby candidates within a configurable distance, and track the highest-ranked zones. Bin width is manual or ATR-based; reset scores when the map is periodically re-anchored or price moves outside its range.
