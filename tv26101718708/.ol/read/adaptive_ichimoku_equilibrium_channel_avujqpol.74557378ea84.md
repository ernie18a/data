Calculation: Fast and slow range midpoints = (highest high + lowest low) over the respective lookbacks; the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute change in the selected price source over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive spine = efficiency ratio × fast midpoint + (1 − efficiency ratio) × slow midpoint.
Calculation: Cloud = the area between the average of the fast and slow midpoints and the midpoint of the high-low range over the cloud lookback; cloud top and bottom are the higher and lower boundaries.
Calculation: Trend state = bullish when close is above cloud top and the cloud is bullish, spine slope is positive, efficiency ratio > 0.4, and ADX > 22; otherwise bullish when close is above cloud top and either the cloud is bullish or spine slope is positive. Bearish is the mirror condition below cloud bottom; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Range position = (close − (spine − channel width)) / (2 × channel width), clamped to [0, 1]; channel width is ATR-based and adapts to ATR relative to its longer-term average.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = the trend state calculated on a higher timeframe, using that timeframe's close, cloud, kijun slope, efficiency ratio, and ADX; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Lower-timeframe momentum = close relative to its 9-bar EMA on a lower timeframe; use the latest completed lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Breakout conditions = close crosses beyond the channel rail, displacement beyond it is at least the configured ATR fraction, candle body/range and volume/20-bar average meet their configured minimums, and the close holds in the breakout candle's outer half.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous pivot low while its distance below the spine is smaller; bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous pivot high while its distance above the spine is smaller.
Calculation: Liquidity targets = nearest unswept equal-high pool above price for longs or equal-low pool below price for shorts; pools form from confirmed pivots within the configured ATR tolerance. If absent, use the upper or lower channel rail, respectively.
Long: Trend state > 0 AND range position < 0.25 AND close > open AND higher-timeframe trend > 0 AND lower-timeframe close > its EMA AND efficiency ratio > the clarity threshold AND close > stop AND (target − close) >= minimum reward-risk ratio × (close − stop), where stop is cloud bottom minus the configured ATR buffer and target is the bullish liquidity target or upper channel rail; OR trend state > 0 AND range position < 0.25 AND previous range position >= 0.25; OR bullish divergence; OR bullish breakout; OR cloud has just turned bullish; OR trend state has just changed from non-bullish to bullish.
Short: Trend state < 0 AND range position > 0.75 AND close < open AND higher-timeframe trend < 0 AND lower-timeframe close < its EMA AND efficiency ratio > the clarity threshold AND stop > close AND (close − target) >= minimum reward-risk ratio × (stop − close), where stop is cloud top plus the configured ATR buffer and target is the bearish liquidity target or lower channel rail; OR trend state < 0 AND range position > 0.75 AND previous range position <= 0.75; OR bearish divergence; OR bearish breakout; OR cloud has just turned bearish; OR trend state has just changed from non-bearish to bearish.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target OR trend state < 0.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target OR trend state > 0.
