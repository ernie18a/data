Calculation: Swing high = a bar’s high, confirmed after an equal-length window of following bars, that is strictly higher than the highs of the equal-length preceding and following windows; Swing low = the corresponding strictly lower low.
Long: The last completed chart-timeframe bar closes above the latest active confirmed swing high (or its entire open-close body is above it when body confirmation is enabled) AND, if the liquidity-sweep filter is enabled, a bullish sweep is active (a prior bar’s low fell below the latest swing low and no subsequent close fell below that swing low) AND, if the higher-timeframe bias filter is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close is above its 50-period SMA AND, if the premium/discount filter is enabled and the range high differs from the range low, the last completed chart-timeframe close is at or below the range midpoint.
Short: The last completed chart-timeframe bar closes below the latest active confirmed swing low (or its entire open-close body is below it when body confirmation is enabled) AND, if the liquidity-sweep filter is enabled, a bearish sweep is active (a prior bar’s high rose above the latest swing high and no subsequent close rose above that swing high) AND, if the higher-timeframe bias filter is enabled, the last completed higher-timeframe close is below its 50-period SMA AND, if the premium/discount filter is enabled and the range high differs from the range low, the last completed chart-timeframe close is at or above the range midpoint.
