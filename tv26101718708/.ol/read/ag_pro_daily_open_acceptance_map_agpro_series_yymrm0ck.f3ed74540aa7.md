Calculation: Daily open = open of the current higher-timeframe bar while it is still forming.
Calculation: Tolerance = the greater of two minimum price ticks and chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by the selected tolerance parameter; strict mode uses a larger tolerance than normal mode.
Long: On an intraday chart, on a confirmed bar, either (close crosses above the daily open AND close > daily open + tolerance AND a low has been below daily open − 0.25 × tolerance today AND no bullish reclaim has yet occurred today) OR (close > daily open + tolerance for the required consecutive bars and bullish acceptance begins) OR (close crosses above the daily open AND close > daily open AND a low was below daily open − tolerance within the selected rejection lookback AND bullish acceptance is inactive AND this is not a first bullish reclaim AND the down-rejection state begins). The required hold is reduced by one, to a minimum of one bar, after a first bullish reclaim if the reclaim bonus is enabled. Strict mode uses a longer hold and rejection lookback than normal mode.
Short: On an intraday chart, on a confirmed bar, either (close crosses below the daily open AND close < daily open − tolerance AND a high has been above daily open + 0.25 × tolerance today AND no bearish reclaim has yet occurred today) OR (close < daily open − tolerance for the required consecutive bars and bearish acceptance begins) OR (close crosses below the daily open AND close < daily open AND a high was above daily open + tolerance within the selected rejection lookback AND bearish acceptance is inactive AND this is not a first bearish reclaim AND the up-rejection state begins). The required hold is reduced by one, to a minimum of one bar, after a first bearish reclaim if the reclaim bonus is enabled. Strict mode uses a longer hold and rejection lookback than normal mode.
