Calculation: Prior-week value area = value area from the last completed weekly profile, built from chart-timeframe bars by dividing the period high–low into price rows, allocating each bar’s volume equally across rows touched, selecting the highest-volume row as VPOC, then expanding from VPOC toward the larger adjacent row volume until the configured share of total volume is included.
Calculation: Prior-day value area and VPOC = same profile calculation over the last completed daily period; these are higher-timeframe values than the chart bars.
Calculation: Reference high/low = prior-day value-area high/low, prior-week value-area high/low, or the higher high / lower low of those levels, according to the selected reference mode.
Calculation: Daily VPOC trend = compare the last configured number of completed daily VPOCs (higher timeframe than chart bars); up/down when every step has that direction in strict mode, otherwise when at least 66% of steps have that direction.
Calculation: Micro-profile shape = profile the impulse-plus-balance window using chart bars and the same volume-row method; classify P when its VPOC is in the upper configured fraction of the window, b when in the lower configured fraction, otherwise D.
Calculation: Single-print rows = price rows in the impulse range touched by no more than the configured number of chart bars in the running session profile.
Calculation: Impulse = highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback; impulse range is their difference.
Calculation: Initial stop = selected structural level (long: balance low, impulse low, or reference high; short: balance high, impulse high, or reference low) offset by the configured ATR buffer; initial risk = absolute distance from trigger close to initial stop; targets are trigger close plus/minus configured risk multiples.
Long: an upward impulse (range ≥ configured ATR multiple and close ≥ impulse low + 65% of impulse range) crosses the reference high (lookback low < reference high and close > reference high), and weekly location permits longs (close above prior-week value-area high; if required, current week opened above it), and weekly test/rejection permits longs (if required, after a prior close above the weekly value area, a bar tests within 0.15 ATR of its high edge and later closes above it, above its open, with range ≥ configured ATR multiple), and daily VPOC trend permits longs (if required, rising), and balance is contained, valid, and at least the configured minimum bars old, and single-print row count meets its configured minimum (if required), and micro-profile shape is P, and no position is open and the session signal limit is not reached, and either close > balance high and close > open OR low ≤ balance low + 25% of balance range and close > balance low + 50% of balance range and close > open and close > reference high.
Short: a downward impulse (range ≥ configured ATR multiple and close ≤ impulse low + 35% of impulse range) crosses the reference low (lookback high > reference low and close < reference low), and weekly location permits shorts (close below prior-week value-area low; if required, current week opened below it), and weekly test/rejection permits shorts (if required, after a prior close below the weekly value area, a bar tests within 0.15 ATR of its low edge and later closes below it, below its open, with range ≥ configured ATR multiple), and daily VPOC trend permits shorts (if required, falling), and balance is contained, valid, and at least the configured minimum bars old, and single-print row count meets its configured minimum (if required), and micro-profile shape is b, and no position is open and the session signal limit is not reached, and either close < balance low and close < open OR high ≥ balance high − 25% of balance range and close < balance high − 50% of balance range and close < open and close < reference low.
Exit: long, high ≥ second profit target OR low ≤ current stop OR (first profit target has not been reached and elapsed chart bars since entry ≥ configured time limit).
Exit: short, low ≤ second profit target OR high ≥ current stop OR (first profit target has not been reached and elapsed chart bars since entry ≥ configured time limit).
