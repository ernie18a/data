Calculation: Market structure trend = bullish when close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish when close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the prior trend. Pivots are confirmed on the chart timeframe after the pivot-length bars on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish inducement = in a bullish structure trend, low is below the preceding short-window low and close is above that low; bearish inducement = in a bearish structure trend, high is above the preceding short-window high and close is below that high.
Calculation: Confluence score = sum of these eight checks: structure trend agrees with close relative to the trend EMA; RSI is within the allowed band for that trend; MACD line is above/below its signal line for bullish/bearish trend; volume exceeds its moving average times the configured volume multiplier; close agrees with trend relative to the trend EMA (count this as a separate check); the configured weekend filter allows the bar; ATR is greater than its prior-bar value; close is at or above/below the higher-timeframe EMA for bullish/bearish trend. The higher-timeframe EMA uses the latest value available from the higher timeframe, including its still-forming bar.
Calculation: ATR stop and target = stop is an ATR-multiple from entry price against the position; target is an ATR-multiple times the configured risk-to-reward ratio from entry price in the position direction.
Long: close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high OR (bullish structure trend AND low < preceding short-window low AND close > that low) OR (bullish structure trend AND (bullish break or change of character OR bullish inducement is enabled) AND confluence score >= configured threshold AND close >= higher-timeframe EMA AND position is flat AND daily and weekly risk budgets permit entry AND global risk limit permits entry AND emergency kill switch is off AND no same-direction cooldown is active)
Short: close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low OR (bearish structure trend AND high > preceding short-window high AND close < that high) OR (bearish structure trend AND (bearish break or change of character OR bearish inducement is enabled) AND confluence score >= configured threshold AND close <= higher-timeframe EMA AND position is flat AND daily and weekly risk budgets permit entry AND global risk limit permits entry AND emergency kill switch is off AND no same-direction cooldown is active)
Exit: long, close <= entry price - ATR stop multiple OR close >= entry price + ATR stop multiple * configured risk-to-reward ratio
Exit: short, close >= entry price + ATR stop multiple OR close <= entry price - ATR stop multiple * configured risk-to-reward ratio
