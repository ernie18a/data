Calculation: Supertrend direction = calculate true range ATR and, when adaptive multipliers are enabled, multiply ATR by max multiplier − (max multiplier − min multiplier) × efficiency ratio, where efficiency ratio = |close − close[n]| / sum of |close − prior close| over the same n-bar lookback; form upper and lower bands around hl2, ratchet the lower band upward to the greater of its raw value and prior lower band when prior close is above the prior lower band, and ratchet the upper band downward to the lesser of its raw value and prior upper band when prior close is below the prior upper band; initialize direction bullish, switch bearish to bullish when close exceeds the prior upper band, and switch bullish to bearish when close falls below the prior lower band; otherwise retain prior direction. ATR uses the selected RMA or SMA of true range.
Long: Supertrend direction changes from bearish to bullish
Short: Supertrend direction changes from bullish to bearish
