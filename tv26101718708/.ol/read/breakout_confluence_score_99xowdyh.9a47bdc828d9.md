Calculation: Consolidation box = after the minimum configured number of consecutive bars have candle bodies (max(open, close) to min(open, close)) within the high-low range of the first bar, set the box boundaries to that first bar’s high and low; reject the candidate if any bar’s high-low range exceeds the configured multiple of ATR calculated before that bar, when the oversized-candle filter is enabled. The box ends on a close outside either boundary or when its configured maximum duration is reached; a duration end produces no signal.
Calculation: Average candle body = simple moving average of |close − open|.
Calculation: Long score = sum of the configured weights for each true condition: breakout candle body > average candle body × configured multiplier; ADX > configured minimum; close > trend EMA; slope EMA > its value on the previous bar; RSI > 50; volume > average volume × configured multiplier; box width / ATR < configured maximum; and box duration ≥ minimum consolidation length + configured premium.
Calculation: Short score = sum of the configured weights for each true condition: breakout candle body > average candle body × configured multiplier; ADX > configured minimum; close < trend EMA; slope EMA < its value on the previous bar; RSI < 50; volume > average volume × configured multiplier; box width / ATR < configured maximum; and box duration ≥ minimum consolidation length + configured premium. Use separate lookbacks for the trend EMA and slope EMA.
Long: A consolidation box is active AND close > its upper boundary AND long score ≥ configured minimum score.
Short: A consolidation box is active AND close < its lower boundary AND short score ≥ configured minimum score.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × configured stop-loss multiple OR high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × configured take-profit multiple OR holding duration exceeds its configured limit.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + entry ATR × configured stop-loss multiple OR low ≤ entry price − entry ATR × configured take-profit multiple OR holding duration exceeds its configured limit.
