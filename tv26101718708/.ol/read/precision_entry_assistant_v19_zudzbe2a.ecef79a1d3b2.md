Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the opening-range session, locked after that session ends and reset each day
Calculation: VWAP slope = absolute difference between current VWAP and VWAP five chart bars earlier
Calculation: 8-bar range = highest high minus lowest low over the last 8 chart bars
Calculation: Candle body = absolute difference between close and open
Calculation: Close position = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100; if high equals low, use 50
Calculation: VWAP crosses = count over the chop lookback of close crossing VWAP between adjacent chart bars
Calculation: EMA crosses = count over the chop lookback of the fast EMA crossing the slow EMA between adjacent chart bars
Calculation: Low sweep = current low is below the lowest low of the prior 8 chart bars and close is above current low
Calculation: High sweep = current high is above the highest high of the prior 8 chart bars and close is below current high
Long: (during NY AM session AND NY signal count is below its limit AND bars since last signal exceed the cooldown AND (market state filter disabled OR (VWAP slope > ATR × slope threshold AND 8-bar range > ATR × expansion threshold AND absolute distance from close to VWAP ≥ ATR × VWAP avoidance threshold AND VWAP crosses ≤ chop limit AND EMA crosses ≤ chop limit)) AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND candle body > ATR × displacement threshold AND close position ≥ directional-close threshold AND (low ≤ opening-range high AND close > opening-range high OR low ≤ VWAP AND close > VWAP OR close > opening-range high)) OR (during Asia or London session AND off-session signal count is below its limit AND bars since last signal exceed the cooldown AND same market state filter AND close > open AND candle body > ATR × displacement threshold AND close position ≥ directional-close threshold AND close > VWAP AND (low sweep OR low ≤ VWAP AND close > VWAP))
