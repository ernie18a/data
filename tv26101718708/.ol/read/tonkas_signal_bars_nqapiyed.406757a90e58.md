Calculation: Candle body size = absolute value of close minus open
Calculation: Average body size = rolling average of candle body size over a lookback
Calculation: Candle body fraction = candle body size divided by candle high minus low
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close above the highest high of the prior structure lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bearish structure break = close below the lowest low of the prior structure lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Long: For any enabled positive manual level, (close > open AND close > level AND candle body crosses the level AND (close - level) / candle body size >= configured minimum body fraction beyond the level OR previous bar's high >= level - configured tolerance AND previous bar's low <= level + configured tolerance AND close > open AND close > level AND previous bar's close < previous bar's open AND close > max(previous bar's open, previous bar's close)) AND candle body size > average body size × configured multiplier AND candle body fraction >= configured minimum AND bullish structure break
