Calculation: Rectangle high/low = highest selected-source high / lowest selected-source low over the configured lookback; selected source is Heikin Ashi OHLC or chart OHLC
Calculation: Rectangle range percent = 100 × (rectangle high − rectangle low) / selected-source close
Calculation: Accumulation bias = configured measure: wick bias (sum lower wicks − sum upper wicks) / total wicks; body bias (sum bullish body sizes − sum bearish body sizes) / total body sizes; signed RSI bias (sum RSI on bullish bars − sum RSI on bearish bars) / sum RSI on non-doji bars; TSI bias (double-smoothed close change / double-smoothed absolute close change); CMF (sum money-flow multiplier × volume / sum volume); or the average of these measures, including CMF only when volume exists. Wick, body, RSI, and CMF use the prior configured rectangle-lookback bars; TSI uses its prior value. RSI and TSI use their configured lengths, with TSI's first smoothing length longer than its second.
Long: prior bar's rectangle range percent < configured maximum AND selected-source close > prior rectangle high AND prior selected-source close ≤ prior rectangle high AND the last signal was not long AND session filter passes when enabled AND the accumulation-strength band is enabled AND the conflict-only filter passes when enabled
Short: prior bar's rectangle range percent < configured maximum AND selected-source close < prior rectangle low AND prior selected-source close ≥ prior rectangle low AND the last signal was not short AND session filter passes when enabled AND the accumulation-strength band is enabled AND the conflict-only filter passes when enabled
Exit: long, selected-source low ≤ stop OR selected-source high ≥ second profit target; if both occur on the same bar, stop takes priority
Exit: short, selected-source high ≥ stop OR selected-source low ≤ second profit target; if both occur on the same bar, stop takes priority
