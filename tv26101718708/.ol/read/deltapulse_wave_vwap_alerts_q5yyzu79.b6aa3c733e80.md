Calculation: Delta wave = EMA of 100 × (EMA(volume when close > open, longer lookback) − EMA(volume when close < open, same lookback)) / EMA(volume when volume > 0, otherwise 1, same lookback), using a shorter lookback for the final EMA.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3 since the start of the day; reset at each new day.
Calculation: Session VWAP standard deviation = square root of the nonnegative difference between the cumulative volume-weighted average of ((high + low + close) / 3)² and Session VWAP².
Calculation: Bullish divergence = at a Delta wave trough on the previous bar (wave rises from that bar to the current bar, and the trough is below 20% of the lower threshold), the previous bar's low is lower than the low at the last such trough, its Delta wave is higher than the wave at that trough, and the troughs are separated by at least the minimum gap; update the stored trough after each qualifying trough. Initialize stored low, wave, and index to zero.
Calculation: Bearish divergence = at a Delta wave peak on the previous bar (wave falls from that bar to the current bar, and the peak is above 20% of the upper threshold), the previous bar's high is higher than the high at the last such peak, its Delta wave is lower than the wave at that peak, and the peaks are separated by at least the minimum gap; update the stored peak after each qualifying peak. Initialize stored high, wave, and index to zero.
Long: bullish divergence OR (bullish divergence AND close > Session VWAP AND close < Session VWAP + Session VWAP standard deviation)
Short: bearish divergence OR (bearish divergence AND close < Session VWAP AND close > Session VWAP − Session VWAP standard deviation)
