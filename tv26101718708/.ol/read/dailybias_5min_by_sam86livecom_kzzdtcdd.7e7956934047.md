Calculation: Fast and slow DEMA = 2 × EMA(close, lookback) − EMA(EMA(close, lookback), lookback); the fast lookback is shorter than the slow lookback.
Calculation: Q-Trend state = adaptive midpoint of the highest and lowest source values over its lookback, shifted by an ATR-based offset when the source crosses the midpoint; a fresh bullish/bearish signal occurs when that state changes to bullish/bearish. Strong bullish/bearish means the open is in the lowest/highest one-eighth of the lookback range now or within the prior four bars.
Calculation: UT trailing level = recursively trailed close (or same-timeframe Heikin-Ashi close when selected), offset by a configurable multiple of ATR; it trails below price while price is above the prior trail and above price while below it.
Calculation: Momentum = EMA(close − close from the momentum lookback bars ago, smoothing lookback); bullish/bearish when above/below zero.
Calculation: Daily trend = higher-timeframe close compared with its EMA, plus optional ADX strength gate; use the higher-timeframe bar supplied by the data, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Demand/supply zones = zones around the latest confirmed pivot low/high, with depth equal to a configurable ATR multiple; use two pivot configurations, A with longer left/right lookbacks than B. A bar is in a zone when its high-low range overlaps it.
Calculation: Delta = signed volume using the selected proxy: volume × (close−open)/(high−low), signed volume by close versus prior close, or signed volume by close versus open; use a minimum range of one tick for the first proxy. Strong positive/negative delta means delta has that sign and its absolute value is at least the configured percentile of its rolling lookback.
Calculation: Volume spike = volume greater than its longer-lookback simple moving average multiplied by a configurable factor.
Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low of the first configured number of bars in each day; opening-range breakout is only evaluated after those bars are complete.
Calculation: Market sentiment = average of normalized RSI, stochastic, stochastic RSI, CCI, bull-bear power, SMA position, VWAP-band position, Bollinger position, Supertrend position, linear-regression correlation, and pivot-based market-structure position; bullish above 60 and bearish below 40.
Long: fast DEMA crosses above slow DEMA AND inside the configured session OR momentum crosses above zero OR fresh Q-Trend bullish signal OR close crosses above the UT trailing level AND close is above it OR in demand AND delta is positive and strong AND (volume-spike filter disabled OR volume spike) OR market sentiment > 60 OR session allowed AND cooldown satisfied AND (fresh Q-Trend bullish signal OR UT buy signal OR fast DEMA crosses above slow DEMA) AND long score ≥ configured minimum AND required volume confirmation AND daily trend bullish AND RSI below its configured upper gate AND position is flat or short. Long score counts: daily trend bullish, fast DEMA > slow DEMA, Q-Trend bullish state, close > UT trail, in demand, strong positive delta, VWAP confirmation (if enabled), opening-range breakout above the range (if enabled), and bullish momentum.
Short: fast DEMA crosses below slow DEMA AND inside the configured session OR momentum crosses below zero OR fresh Q-Trend bearish signal OR close crosses below the UT trailing level AND close is below it OR in supply AND delta is negative and strong AND (volume-spike filter disabled OR volume spike) OR market sentiment < 40 OR session allowed AND cooldown satisfied AND (fresh Q-Trend bearish signal OR UT sell signal OR fast DEMA crosses below slow DEMA) AND short score ≥ configured minimum AND required volume confirmation AND daily trend bearish AND RSI above its configured lower gate AND position is flat or long. Short score counts: daily trend bearish, fast DEMA < slow DEMA, Q-Trend bearish state, close < UT trail, in supply, strong negative delta, VWAP confirmation (if enabled), opening-range breakout below the range (if enabled), and bearish momentum.
Exit: long, opposite final short entry signal OR low ≤ prior bar's long stop level OR high ≥ prior bar's long target level.
Exit: short, opposite final long entry signal OR high ≥ prior bar's short stop level OR low ≤ prior bar's short target level.
