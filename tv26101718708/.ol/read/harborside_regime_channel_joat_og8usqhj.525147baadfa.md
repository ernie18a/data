Calculation: Structural center = smoothed midpoint of the latest confirmed swing high and swing low; each pivot requires the same lookback on both sides, falling back to the current high or low before a pivot is confirmed
Calculation: Upper and lower channel rails = smoothed structural center plus or minus ATR times a width multiplier
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = on the higher bias timeframe’s last completed bar, shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA and close > shorter-lookback EMA; bearish bias is the reverse
Calculation: Volume impulse = volume / its longer-lookback EMA
Long: Confirmed close crossover above the upper rail AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish bias), causing a change into bullish regime; OR the initial regime is bullish (close >= structural center); OR confirmed close crossover above structural center; OR higher-timeframe bullish bias; OR compression AND confirmed close crossover above the upper rail; OR bullish regime AND volume impulse >= threshold; OR bullish regime held for at least the configured confirmation bars
Short: Confirmed close crossunder below the lower rail AND (HTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish bias), causing a change into bearish regime; OR the initial regime is bearish (close < structural center); OR confirmed close crossunder below structural center; OR higher-timeframe bearish bias; OR compression AND confirmed close crossunder below the lower rail; OR bearish regime AND volume impulse >= threshold; OR bearish regime held for at least the configured confirmation bars
