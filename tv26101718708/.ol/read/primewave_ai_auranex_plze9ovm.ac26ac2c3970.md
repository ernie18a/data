Calculation: Adaptive Tenkan, Kijun, and Span B = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over their respective lookbacks, each scaled by the same ATR-based volatility ratio; their lookbacks retain the short, medium, and long ordering. In classic mode, use their respective fixed lookbacks.
Calculation: Current cloud top and bottom = maximum and minimum of Senkou A and Senkou B from the configured displacement ago.
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = weighted points for close above the cloud (partial points inside), Tenkan above Kijun, close above its displacement-lagged value, Senkou A's recent rise exceeding Senkou B's, cloud thickness passing its average-thickness filter, volume passing its average-volume filter, positive momentum (more points above its stronger threshold), and rising positive momentum. Bearish confluence score uses the mirrored cloud, Tenkan/Kijun, lagged-price, momentum, and momentum-acceleration conditions; cloud slope awards points when Senkou A's rise does not exceed Senkou B's. Both scores are capped at their maximum.
Calculation: Momentum = smoothed, bounded combination of normalized Tenkan–Kijun spread, its change over a shorter lookback, and the difference between Senkou A and B slopes over a longer lookback; spread and slope are normalized by ATR.
Calculation: Stop-loss = selected configured level: long, cloud bottom or Kijun minus an ATR buffer, or entry price minus an ATR multiple; short, cloud top or Kijun plus an ATR buffer, or entry price plus an ATR multiple.
Calculation: TP3 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the greater of the absolute entry-to-stop distance and an ATR-based minimum risk distance, multiplied by the configured TP3 risk multiple.
Long: Tenkan crosses above Kijun AND cloud thickness passes its configured filter AND volume passes its configured average-volume filter AND close exceeds its displacement-lagged value if Chikou confirmation is enabled AND bullish confluence score meets its configured minimum AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up OR close is above current cloud top AND previous close was at or below previous cloud top AND volume passes the configured breakout-volume threshold AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up OR a prior upward cloud breakout occurred more than one and within the configured maximum number of chart bars ago AND current low is at or below current cloud top AND current close is above current cloud top AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up.
Short: Tenkan crosses below Kijun AND cloud thickness passes its configured filter AND volume passes its configured average-volume filter AND close is below its displacement-lagged value if Chikou confirmation is enabled AND bearish confluence score meets its configured minimum AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up OR close is below current cloud bottom AND previous close was at or above previous cloud bottom AND volume passes the configured breakout-volume threshold AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up OR a prior downward cloud breakout occurred more than one and within the configured maximum number of chart bars ago AND current high is at or above current cloud bottom AND current close is below current cloud bottom AND bar is confirmed and indicator is warmed up.
Exit: long, current low ≤ stop-loss OR current high ≥ TP3.
Exit: short, current high ≥ stop-loss OR current low ≤ TP3.
