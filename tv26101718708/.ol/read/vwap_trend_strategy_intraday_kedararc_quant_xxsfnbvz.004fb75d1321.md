Calculation: Candle body percentage = abs(close − open) / open × 100
Calculation: Higher-timeframe close and EMA = close and EMA of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Long: (close crosses above session VWAP OR (retest mode is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND the lowest low over the retest lookback ≤ session VWAP)) AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close > session VWAP AND (open ≤ session VWAP OR low ≤ session VWAP) AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume ≥ volume SMA × momentum volume multiplier AND candle body percentage ≥ body threshold); apply the configured same-side daily trade limit, minimum bars between same-side entries, position eligibility (position size ≤ 0), and market-open cooldown
Short: (close crosses below session VWAP OR (retest mode is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND the highest high over the retest lookback ≥ session VWAP)) AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume ≥ volume SMA × volume multiplier OR (momentum exception is enabled AND close < session VWAP AND (open ≥ session VWAP OR high ≥ session VWAP) AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume ≥ volume SMA × momentum volume multiplier AND candle body percentage ≥ body threshold); apply the configured same-side daily trade limit, minimum bars between same-side entries, position eligibility (position size ≥ 0), and market-open cooldown
Exit: long, close < session VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA OR ATR stop is enabled AND low ≤ close − ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: short, close > session VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA OR ATR stop is enabled AND high ≥ close + ATR × stop multiplier
