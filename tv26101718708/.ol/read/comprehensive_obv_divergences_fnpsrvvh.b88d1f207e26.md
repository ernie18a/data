Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of sign(close change) × volume
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a close lower/higher than the preceding and following pivot-lookback bars; confirmed after the following bars complete
Long: latest confirmed pivot low in close < preceding confirmed pivot low in close AND OBV at latest confirmed pivot low > OBV at preceding confirmed pivot low
Short: latest confirmed pivot high in close > preceding confirmed pivot high in close AND OBV at latest confirmed pivot high < OBV at preceding confirmed pivot high
