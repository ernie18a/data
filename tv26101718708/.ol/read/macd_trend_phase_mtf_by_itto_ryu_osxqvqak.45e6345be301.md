Calculation: PPO = (short-term EMA(close) − long-term EMA(close)) / long-term EMA(close) × 100; use the same EMA lengths on every timeframe.
Calculation: Signal = EMA(PPO) using a shorter lookback than the long-term EMA used for PPO.
Calculation: Histogram = PPO − Signal.
Calculation: Trend phase = on each phase timeframe, use PPO and Signal from its last completed bar. If |PPO| is below a threshold, phase is chop. Otherwise, bullish means PPO > 0 and bearish means PPO < 0; upward impulse means PPO > Signal, downward impulse means PPO < Signal. Bullish with upward impulse is established bull if the slow regime is bullish, otherwise emerging bull. Bullish with downward impulse is weakening bull if the latest completed upward-impulse leg's PPO peak is lower than the previous such leg's peak, otherwise bull pullback. Bearish with downward impulse is established bear if the slow regime is bearish, otherwise emerging bear. Bearish with upward impulse is weakening bear if the latest completed downward-impulse leg's PPO trough is higher than the previous such leg's trough, otherwise bear rally. The slow regime is bullish when PPO > 0 and bearish otherwise, using the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the phase timeframe. Commit a new phase only after it persists for the configured number of chart bars.
Calculation: Weighted phase score = established bull/bear ±1, pullback/rally ±0.75, emerging bull/bear ±0.5, weakening bull/bear ±0.25, and chop 0; positive for bull and negative for bear. Calculate each dashboard phase from its timeframe's last completed bar (dashboard timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe), then divide the sum of each phase score multiplied by its weight by the sum of weights and multiply by 100.
Long: Committed phase is bull pullback AND current chart-timeframe Histogram > previous Histogram AND previous Histogram < Histogram two chart bars ago OR a dashboard phase is established bull, bull pullback, or emerging bull OR weighted phase score percentage ≥ the positive major-bias threshold.
Short: Committed phase is bear rally AND current chart-timeframe Histogram < previous Histogram AND previous Histogram > Histogram two chart bars ago OR a dashboard phase is established bear, bear rally, or emerging bear OR weighted phase score percentage ≤ the negative major-bias threshold.
