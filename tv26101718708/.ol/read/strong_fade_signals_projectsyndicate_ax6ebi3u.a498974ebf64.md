Calculation: Basis = selected moving average of the selected price source
Calculation: Upper band = basis + band width × ATR; lower band = basis − band width × ATR
Calculation: Up-burst magnitude = (high − upper band) / ATR when high exceeds the upper band, otherwise 0; down-burst magnitude = (lower band − low) / ATR when low is below the lower band, otherwise 0. If closing-burst filtering is enabled, count a burst only when close is also beyond its band.
Calculation: Stack height = capped 1 + floor(burst magnitude / ATR per stack step) when burst magnitude is positive, otherwise 0
Calculation: Peak rejection = upper-wick share of the peak bar's range for an up-burst; lower-wick share for a down-burst
Calculation: Setup score = 10 × weighted mean of the clamped burst-magnitude/heat-saturation ratio, clamped stack-height/max-stack ratio, directional RSI exhaustion ((50 − RSI)/30 for long; (RSI − 50)/30 for short), peak rejection/0.6, directional 3-bar basis slope normalized by ATR ((side × slope/ATR + 1)/2), 1 − 100-bar ATR percentile/100, and absolute entry-to-basis distance/(1.5 × band width × ATR)
Calculation: Stop = selected stop basis (beyond the burst extreme, an ATR multiple from entry, or beyond the lookback swing extreme), with ATR buffer where applicable; clamp entry-to-stop risk between the configured ATR floor and ceiling
Calculation: Targets = either configured R multiples from entry using stop risk, or (when the basis lies in the reversion direction) halfway from entry to basis, at basis, and half a band beyond basis
Long: Previous bar has a positive down-burst magnitude that is at least the magnitude two bars ago and greater than the current bar's magnitude AND previous down-burst stack height meets the minimum AND previous down-burst magnitude meets the minimum AND (rejection filter disabled OR peak rejection meets its minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR peak RSI is at or below its oversold threshold) AND long side is allowed AND no trade is active AND no trade closed on this bar AND ATR is available AND bars since the last signal meet the minimum spacing AND setup score tier meets the minimum stars
Short: Previous bar has a positive up-burst magnitude that is at least the magnitude two bars ago and greater than the current bar's magnitude AND previous up-burst stack height meets the minimum AND previous up-burst magnitude meets the minimum AND (rejection filter disabled OR peak rejection meets its minimum) AND (RSI filter disabled OR peak RSI is at or above its overbought threshold) AND short side is allowed AND no trade is active AND no trade closed on this bar AND ATR is available AND bars since the last signal meet the minimum spacing AND setup score tier meets the minimum stars
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target 3 OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, resolve as stop, otherwise a stop after target 1 or 2 was previously reached exits at that highest reached target
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target 3 OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, resolve as stop, otherwise a stop after target 1 or 2 was previously reached exits at that highest reached target
