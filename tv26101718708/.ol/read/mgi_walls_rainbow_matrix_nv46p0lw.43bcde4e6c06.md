Calculation: Radar POC = hlc3 at the most recent bar with the highest volume in the configured radar timeframe’s lookback window. Radar VAH/VAL = POC plus/minus the volume-weighted standard deviation of hlc3 around the POC over that lookback window. Radar VWMA = volume-weighted average of hlc3 over its lookback window. Radar SAR = Parabolic SAR. Radar timeframes may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; request.security uses lookahead_off, so historical higher-timeframe values use the latest completed bar, while realtime values may use the forming bar.
Calculation: Global radar level = arithmetic mean of the available corresponding levels across the configured radar timeframes.
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = volume-weighted average of hlc3 since the start of the current day, week, or month, calculated from chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Fused wall = levels from different timeframes within the configured merge margin, combined into one price zone. Its direction is support when the summed support weights exceed resistance weights, resistance when resistance weights exceed support weights; ties are support when close is at or above the zone midpoint, otherwise resistance.
Long: close >= any radar VWMA, POC, VAH, VAL, SAR, global radar level, anchored VWAP, or fused wall level
Short: close < any radar VWMA, POC, VAH, VAL, SAR, global radar level, anchored VWAP, or fused wall level
