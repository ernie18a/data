Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the opening-range session; opening range size = high − low
Calculation: Long Fibonacci targets = opening range high + opening range size × 0.216, 0.618, and 1.0, respectively; short targets = opening range low − opening range size × 0.216, 0.618, and 1.0, respectively
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest high since the setup or entry minus an ATR-based distance; use the initial ATR distance until price exceeds the first target, then use 0.236 × opening range size, and after price exceeds the second target use 0.382 × opening range size. Short trailing stop = lowest low since setup or entry plus the corresponding distance.
Long: Close crosses above opening range high AND within the trading session OR (the four moving averages are ordered from shortest-lookback highest to longest-lookback lowest AND high is below the first long target AND close crosses above opening range high AND within the trading session; enter at opening range high, then wait for low below opening range high while high remains at or below the first long target)
Short: Close crosses below opening range low AND within the trading session OR (the four moving averages are ordered from shortest-lookback lowest to longest-lookback highest AND low is above the first short target AND close crosses below opening range low AND within the trading session; enter at opening range low, then wait for high above opening range low while low remains at or above the first short target)
Exit: Long, low < prior-bar trailing stop OR high > prior-bar third long target OR within the exit session
Exit: Short, high > prior-bar trailing stop OR low < prior-bar third short target OR within the exit session
