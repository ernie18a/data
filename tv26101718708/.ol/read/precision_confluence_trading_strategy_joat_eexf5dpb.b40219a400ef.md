Calculation: Daily CPR = from the previous completed higher-timeframe daily bar: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; bottom = (high + low) / 2; top = 2 × pivot − bottom
Calculation: Weekly CPR = from the previous completed higher-timeframe weekly bar: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; bottom = (high + low) / 2; top = 2 × pivot − bottom
Calculation: Narrow daily CPR = ((daily top − daily bottom) / daily pivot) × 100 is below its narrowness threshold
Calculation: Hull moving average (HMA) = weighted moving average of (2 × WMA(source, half lookback) − WMA(source, lookback)), using the square root of the lookback as the final WMA lookback
Calculation: WaveTrend = first line is EMA((HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, channel lookback)), channel lookback)), average lookback); second line is the 4-bar SMA of the first line
Calculation: RSI bullish divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while RSI makes a higher pivot low; compare successive pivot lows confirmed with equal left/right lookbacks
Calculation: RSI bearish divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while RSI makes a lower pivot high; compare successive pivot highs confirmed with equal left/right lookbacks
Calculation: WaveTrend bullish divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while the first WaveTrend line makes a higher pivot low; compare successive pivot lows confirmed with equal left/right lookbacks
Calculation: WaveTrend bearish divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while the first WaveTrend line makes a lower pivot high; compare successive pivot highs confirmed with equal left/right lookbacks
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = sum of enabled component scores: CPR (10 if close is above both daily and weekly pivots, plus 3 if above daily top, plus 2 if daily CPR is narrow); HMA (5 if close is above rising medium HMA, plus 7 if short-to-long HMAs are strictly descending by lookback, plus 3 if short EMA is above long EMA); WaveTrend (8 for an upward cross below its oversold threshold, otherwise 5 for any upward cross, plus 5 for WaveTrend bullish divergence, plus 2 if WaveTrend momentum is positive and rising); divergence (5 for RSI bullish divergence plus 5 if RSI and WaveTrend bullish divergences both hold); ADX (10 if ADX exceeds its strength threshold and +DI exceeds −DI, otherwise 5 if +DI exceeds −DI); volume (7 if bullish candle, volume exceeds its moving average multiple, and cumulative volume delta exceeds its moving average, otherwise 5 if bullish candle and volume exceeds its moving average multiple, plus 3 if volume exceeds its climax multiple and candle is bullish); SMC (2 for significant bullish FVG, 2 for bullish order block, 1 for bullish breaker, 2 for volume-confirmed low sweep, 3 for bullish displacement); MTF (15 if all three higher-timeframe directions and strength checks are bullish, 10 if all three directions are bullish, otherwise 5 if the 1-hour direction is bullish); moving averages (3 if close exceeds medium SMA, 4 if close exceeds long SMA, 3 if medium SMA exceeds long SMA)
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = sum of enabled component scores: CPR (10 if close is below both daily and weekly pivots, plus 3 if below daily bottom, plus 2 if daily CPR is narrow); HMA (5 if close is below falling medium HMA, plus 7 if short-to-long HMAs are strictly ascending by lookback, plus 3 if short EMA is below long EMA); WaveTrend (8 for a downward cross above its overbought threshold, otherwise 5 for any downward cross, plus 5 for WaveTrend bearish divergence, plus 2 if WaveTrend momentum is negative and falling); divergence (5 for RSI bearish divergence plus 5 if RSI and WaveTrend bearish divergences both hold); ADX (10 if ADX exceeds its strength threshold and −DI exceeds +DI, otherwise 5 if −DI exceeds +DI); volume (7 if bearish candle, volume exceeds its moving average multiple, and cumulative volume delta is below its moving average, otherwise 5 if bearish candle and volume exceeds its moving average multiple, plus 3 if volume exceeds its climax multiple and candle is bearish); SMC (2 for significant bearish FVG, 2 for bearish order block, 1 for bearish breaker, 2 for volume-confirmed high sweep, 3 for bearish displacement); MTF (15 if all three higher-timeframe directions and strength checks are bearish, 10 if all three directions are bearish, otherwise 5 if the 1-hour direction is bearish); moving averages (3 if close is below medium SMA, 4 if close is below long SMA, 3 if medium SMA is below long SMA)
Calculation: Significant bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, previous close > that earlier high, and gap size as a percentage of that earlier high exceeds its threshold
Calculation: Significant bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, previous close < that earlier low, and gap size as a percentage of that earlier low exceeds its threshold
Calculation: Bullish order block = two bars earlier and previous bars are bearish, current bar is bullish, and volume exceeds its moving average multiple
Calculation: Bearish order block = two bars earlier and previous bars are bullish, current bar is bearish, and volume exceeds its moving average multiple
Calculation: Bullish breaker = close crosses above the low of the latest bearish order block
Calculation: Bearish breaker = close crosses below the high of the latest bullish order block
Calculation: Volume-confirmed low sweep = current low is below the prior bar's rolling lookback low, current close is above that low, and volume exceeds its moving average multiple
Calculation: Volume-confirmed high sweep = current high is above the prior bar's rolling lookback high, current close is below that high, and volume exceeds its moving average multiple
Calculation: Bullish displacement = bullish candle body exceeds the ATR multiple and volume exceeds its climax multiple
Calculation: Bearish displacement = bearish candle body exceeds the ATR multiple and volume exceeds its climax multiple
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = close above HMA is bullish; otherwise bearish. Strength = ADX exceeds its threshold. Use the last completed bar from each higher timeframe; the three requested timeframes are all higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long initial stop = the lower of close − ATR × dynamic stop multiplier and rolling swing low − 0.2 × ATR; dynamic stop multiplier is 0.8 × ATR multiplier for an ultra signal, the ATR multiplier for a strong signal, and 1.2 × ATR multiplier otherwise
Calculation: Short initial stop = the higher of close + ATR × dynamic stop multiplier and rolling swing high + 0.2 × ATR; dynamic stop multiplier is 0.8 × ATR multiplier for an ultra signal, the ATR multiplier for a strong signal, and 1.2 × ATR multiplier otherwise
Long: position is flat AND bullish confluence score ≥ minimum score AND ((bullish score ≥ strong threshold AND bearish score < conflict threshold) OR (bullish score ≥ medium threshold AND bullish score > bearish score + score margin AND not strong-long)) AND session filter passes when enabled
Short: position is flat AND bearish confluence score ≥ minimum score AND ((bearish score ≥ strong threshold AND bullish score < conflict threshold) OR (bearish score ≥ medium threshold AND bearish score > bullish score + score margin AND not strong-short)) AND session filter passes when enabled
Long: bullish confluence score ≥ ultra threshold AND bearish confluence score < ultra conflict threshold AND (WaveTrend crosses upward OR RSI bullish divergence OR bullish displacement)
Short: bearish confluence score ≥ ultra threshold AND bullish confluence score < ultra conflict threshold AND (WaveTrend crosses downward OR RSI bearish divergence OR bearish displacement)
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ take-profit price; initial active stop is the long initial stop and take-profit is close + (close − initial stop) × reward ratio; after breakeven trigger, active stop becomes entry price + 0.1 × (entry price − long initial stop) and take-profit becomes entry price + (entry price − long initial stop) × reward ratio
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ take-profit price; initial active stop is the short initial stop and take-profit is close − (initial stop − close) × reward ratio; after breakeven trigger, active stop becomes entry price − 0.1 × (short initial stop − entry price) and take-profit becomes entry price − (short initial stop − entry price) × reward ratio
