Calculation: upward CHoCH = close crosses above the last confirmed swing high after at least the required number of downward continuation breaks; downward CHoCH = close crosses below the last confirmed swing low after at least the required number of upward continuation breaks; continuation breaks count only while structure trend matches their direction; reset both counts on a CHoCH
Calculation: market score = 30% direction score + 30% efficiency score + 25% participation score + 15% cost score; direction score is 100 when structure trend agrees with close relative to KAMA, 50 when structure trend is set but disagrees, otherwise 30; efficiency score is the smoothed Kaufman Efficiency Ratio divided by its trending threshold, capped at 1, times 100; participation score is 100 for high relative volume and high ATR-normalized close-to-close move, 10 for high relative volume and low move, 35 for low relative volume and high move, otherwise 60; cost score is 100 minus the percentile rank of the EDGE effective bid-ask spread estimate over its longer lookback, or 50 when unavailable
