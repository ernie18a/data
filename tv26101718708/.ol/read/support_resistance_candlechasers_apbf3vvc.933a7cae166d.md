Calculation: Support/resistance levels = confirmed swing lows/highs using separate configurable left lookbacks and a shared right confirmation window; accept at detection only if the close has moved away from the pivot by at least the configured fraction of pivot ATR, the level is not already broken, and any enabled pivot-volume filter passes. Optionally detect on a higher timeframe using completed higher-timeframe bars. Merge qualifying same-role pivots within configured ATR spacing into the existing fixed-price level. A level remains active until strictly broken beyond its creation-time buffer, or until its configured age limit if enabled. Use the configured close- or wick-based break test; decisions commit at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Eligible level = active level meeting configured minimum grade and score and the configured notification scope (displayed levels selected using the previous close, or all qualifying levels). Grade is 1 plus 1 reaction point at 1 ATR or 2 at 2 ATR, plus up to 2 points for additional qualifying separated swing evidence. Score is the clamped 0–100 sum: base 10, up to 30 reaction points, 20 swing-evidence points, 15 higher-timeframe source/confluence points, 10 pivot-relative-volume points, and 15 rejection points, less up to 15 repeated-visit points and 10 age points.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the average of the preceding configured volume lookback bars; unavailable if volume or its baseline is unusable.
