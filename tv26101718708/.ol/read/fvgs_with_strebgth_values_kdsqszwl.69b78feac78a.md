Calculation: FVG strength (%) = round(100 × points earned / points available). Base points: 1 each if gap size ≥ ATR × small-gap threshold, gap size ≥ ATR × large-gap threshold, and the previous bar’s range ≥ ATR × displacement threshold; the large-gap threshold is greater than the small-gap threshold. Add one available point for each enabled optional score: volume (previous-bar volume > previous-bar volume SMA × volume multiplier); trend (bullish: close > fast EMA > slow EMA; bearish: close < fast EMA < slow EMA, with the fast EMA shorter than the slow EMA); structure (bullish: close > prior lookback highest high; bearish: close < prior lookback lowest low); sweep (bullish: previous-bar low < the lowest low in the lookback window ending two bars ago AND previous bar closed above its open; bearish: previous-bar high > the highest high in that window AND previous bar closed below its open). An optional score earns one point when its condition holds.
Long: low > high two bars ago AND bullish FVG strength ≥ minimum strength
Short: high < low two bars ago AND bearish FVG strength ≥ minimum strength
