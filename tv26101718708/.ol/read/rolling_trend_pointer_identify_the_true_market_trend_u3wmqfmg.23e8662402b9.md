Calculation: Positive and negative deviation boundaries = independently computed from the volume-weighted variance of positive deviations and absolute negative deviations over a longer lookback, multiplied by a band-width parameter; the negative boundary is below zero
Calculation: Anchored VWAP slope = current calendar-period anchored VWAP minus its value at a shorter lookback; calculate separately for monthly and optionally longer quarterly, semiannual, and annual calendar periods
