Calculation: Evaluation bars = chart-timeframe bars; evaluate the last completed bar and its preceding completed bars.
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = a simple moving average of close plus and minus a standard-deviation multiple, using the same lookback and multiplier for both bands.
Calculation: Bullish signal candle = bullish outside bar (high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close > open) OR bullish engulfing (previous close < previous open AND close > open AND open <= previous close AND close >= previous open AND current body > previous body) OR bullish railroad (previous close < previous open AND close > open AND both bodies > 0 AND absolute body-size difference / previous body size <= body tolerance AND absolute(current open - previous close) <= price tolerance AND absolute(current close - previous open) <= price tolerance).
Calculation: Bearish signal candle = bearish outside bar (high > previous high AND low < previous low AND close < open) OR bearish engulfing (previous close > previous open AND close < open AND open >= previous close AND close <= previous open AND current body > previous body) OR bearish railroad (previous close > previous open AND close < open AND both bodies > 0 AND absolute body-size difference / previous body size <= body tolerance AND absolute(current open - previous close) <= price tolerance AND absolute(current close - previous open) <= price tolerance).
Calculation: Signal-candle close threshold = for buys, low + (high - low) × retracement fraction; for sells, high - (high - low) × retracement fraction.
Calculation: Two Bollinger lines touched = at least two of the upper, middle, and lower bands lie within the signal candle's high-low range, inclusive.
Long: Bullish signal candle AND high > low AND NOT two Bollinger lines touched AND close >= buy signal-candle close threshold AND (close > middle band + continuation buffer OR (high < middle band AND low <= lower band on the signal candle or a selected number of preceding bars, comparing each bar with its own lower band)).
Short: Bearish signal candle AND high > low AND NOT two Bollinger lines touched AND close <= sell signal-candle close threshold AND (close < middle band - continuation buffer OR (low > middle band AND high >= upper band on the signal candle or a selected number of preceding bars, comparing each bar with its own upper band)).
