Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low from the session open through the configured opening-range window; afternoon range high/low = highest high/lowest low during its configured window.
Calculation: Swing pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the configured number of bars on each side; use only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Equal-high/low liquidity level = the higher/lower of the latest two consecutive swing pivot highs/lows whose difference is within the configured ATR-based or percentage tolerance; retain until a close passes through it.
Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high/lowest low during the configured premarket window.
Calculation: Previous-day high/low/close = high/low/close of the last completed daily bar. Weekly high/low = current weekly bar's high/low, except on Monday use the previous completed weekly bar.
Long: During the active session, after the opening or afternoon range is locked, close crosses from at-or-below to above its high, then a later green bar closes above the crossing bar's high (or merely closes green if beyond-cross confirmation is disabled); apply configured confluence, grade-suppression, and breakout-cooldown filters. OR signals are active until the afternoon range locks; afternoon-range signals are active after it locks.
Short: During the active session, after the opening or afternoon range is locked, close crosses from at-or-above to below its low, then a later red bar closes below the crossing bar's low (or merely closes red if beyond-cross confirmation is disabled); apply configured confluence, grade-suppression, and breakout-cooldown filters. OR signals are active until the afternoon range locks; afternoon-range signals are active after it locks.
Long: During the active session, a green bar has its low within the configured band above the opening- or afternoon-range low; then a later green bar closes above the trigger bar's high, provided no breakout signal occurs on that bar. Use each reversal setup once until price closes below the range low.
Short: During the active session, a red bar has its high within the configured band around the opening- or afternoon-range high; then a later red bar closes below the trigger bar's low, provided no breakout signal occurs on that bar. Use each reversal setup once until price closes above the range high.
Long: Fast EMA > mid EMA and close > slow EMA, with fast > mid > slow when stack filtering is enabled; after a bar's low reaches or falls below the fast EMA and a confirmed swing pivot high exists, close above that pivot high, with close above VWAP and within session when those filters are enabled.
Short: Fast EMA < mid EMA and close < slow EMA, with fast < mid < slow when stack filtering is enabled; after a bar's high reaches or exceeds the fast EMA and a confirmed swing pivot low exists, close below that pivot low, with close below VWAP and within session when those filters are enabled.
Long: After a bar touches VWAP within the configured ATR-based band while closing above the lower band edge, a green reversal bar occurs; within the configured confirmation scope, a later green bar closes above the reversal reference close, with required volume surge and rising RSI below its overbought threshold when enabled. Apply the session and same-direction signal-spacing filters. If an intervening green bar fails the enabled confirmation filters, raise the reference close to that bar's close.
Short: After a bar touches VWAP within the configured ATR-based band while closing below the upper band edge, a red reversal bar occurs; within the configured confirmation scope, a later red bar closes below the reversal reference close, with required volume surge and falling RSI above its oversold threshold when enabled. Apply the session and same-direction signal-spacing filters. If an intervening red bar fails the enabled confirmation filters, lower the reference close to that bar's close.
Long: Within the configured session, the low pierces below and the open and close remain above any enabled sell-side level: previous-day low, premarket low, opening/afternoon-range low, weekly low, equal-low liquidity level, or previous-day close.
Short: Within the configured session, the high pierces above and the open and close remain below any enabled buy-side level: previous-day high, premarket high, opening/afternoon-range high, weekly high, equal-high liquidity level, or previous-day close.
