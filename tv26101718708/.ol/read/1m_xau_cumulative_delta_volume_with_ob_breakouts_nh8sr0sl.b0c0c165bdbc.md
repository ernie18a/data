Calculation: Daily profile high/low = highest high and lowest low during the daily profile session, available after that session ends
Calculation: Support zone = daily profile low ± ATR-based zone width
Calculation: Resistance zone = daily profile high ± ATR-based zone width
Calculation: Recent delta = rolling sum over a lookback of chart bars of each bar’s delta; bar delta is lower-timeframe up-volume minus down-volume, classifying volume by close versus open, then close versus prior close for doji bars, then carrying forward the prior intrabar classification
Long: Flat AND (support-zone pending armed by a prior close above the support zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close below the zone bottom, and close > support-zone top AND recent delta ≥ positive threshold AND its long-signal cooldown has elapsed) OR (support-bounce pending armed by a prior close above the support zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close below the zone bottom, and close crosses above support-zone top AND its support-bounce cooldown has elapsed) OR (resistance-bounce pending armed by a prior close above the resistance zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close below the zone bottom, and close crosses above resistance-zone top AND its resistance-bounce cooldown has elapsed) OR (close is above the resistance-zone top by at least a breakout-percentage threshold) OR (an earlier upward breakout occurred since the current profile began AND a later bar touches the resistance-zone top and closes above it)
Short: Flat AND (resistance-zone pending armed by a prior close below the resistance zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close above the zone top, and close < resistance-zone bottom AND recent delta ≤ negative threshold AND its short-signal cooldown has elapsed) OR (support-rejection pending armed by a prior close below the support zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close above the zone top, and close crosses below support-zone bottom AND its support-rejection cooldown has elapsed) OR (resistance-rejection pending armed by a prior close below the resistance zone followed by a touch, remains valid within its timeout without a close above the zone top, and close crosses below resistance-zone bottom AND its resistance-rejection cooldown has elapsed) OR (close is below the support-zone bottom by at least a breakout-percentage threshold) OR (an earlier downward breakout occurred since the current profile began AND a later bar touches the support-zone bottom and closes below it)
Exit: Long, high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)
Exit: Short, low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage)
