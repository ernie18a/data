Calculation: Bullish signal count = number of bullish conditions met, one point each: multi-timeframe absorption below VWAP; directional bias above its positive threshold; close within 1% of any of the five most recent confirmed pivot lows (from up to ten stored); bullish RSI divergence count at its minimum; close below the lower Bollinger Band AND RSI below its oversold threshold; bullish absorption AND order-flow confidence at its minimum; negative multi-timeframe VWAP deviation on at least two intervals AND oscillator-gap confirmation on at least two intervals; close below the moving average by at least the configured first deviation; zero-lag trend score above its threshold AND close above its upper volatility band.
Calculation: Bearish signal count = number of bearish conditions met, one point each: multi-timeframe absorption above VWAP; directional bias below its negative threshold; close within 1% of any of the five most recent confirmed pivot highs (from up to ten stored); bearish RSI divergence count at its minimum; close above the upper Bollinger Band AND RSI above its overbought threshold; bearish absorption AND order-flow confidence at its minimum; positive multi-timeframe VWAP deviation on at least two intervals AND oscillator-gap confirmation on at least two intervals; close above the moving average by at least the configured first deviation; zero-lag trend score below its threshold AND close below its lower volatility band.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe absorption = on at least two configured intervals, absolute VWAP deviation meets that interval's threshold, volume exceeds its lookback average times its surge multiplier, relative volume meets its minimum, and deviation has the stated direction. VWAP deviation uses the configured method: (close − VWAP) / (1.5 × price standard deviation), (close − VWAP) / price standard deviation, or (close − VWAP) / standard deviation of close − VWAP. Use each interval's requested bar: last completed bar in historical backtests; still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Directional bias = (bullish bars − bearish bars) / lookback × (1 − momentum weight) + mean((close − prior close) / prior close) over the same lookback × momentum weight; bullish bars close above open, bearish bars close below open.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish RSI divergence count = count since the most recent reset every 50 chart bars of confirmed pivot pairs where price makes a lower low / higher high and RSI makes a higher low / lower high, respectively; reset both counts at each reset.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish absorption = current volume / 20-bar average volume × (1 − absolute candle body / candle range) exceeds the absorption minimum, with close above open / below open, respectively.
Calculation: Order-flow confidence = 40 points if absorption exceeds its minimum, plus 30 if either toxicity ratio exceeds its threshold, plus 30 if either ratio exceeds 0.5; each toxicity ratio is the corresponding aggressive-buy or aggressive-sell volume divided by total volume over its lookback. Aggressive buy bars close above open and in the top quarter of their range; aggressive sell bars close below open and in the bottom quarter.
Calculation: Oscillator-gap confirmation = absolute difference between VWAP deviation and the z-score of the 14-bar stochastic value, meeting its interval-specific threshold. VWAP deviation uses the configured method defined above. Use each interval's requested bar: last completed bar in historical backtests; still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Zero-lag trend score = sum over the configured shorter-to-longer lag range of +1 when zero-lag EMA is above its lagged value, otherwise −1; zero-lag EMA is EMA(close + (close − close[ floor((length − 1) / 2) ]), length). Upper/lower volatility bands are zero-lag EMA plus/minus the configured multiplier times the highest ATR(length) over 3 × length bars.
Long: long trades enabled AND bullish signal count meets the configured minimum when confluence is enabled (otherwise > 0) AND (bearish signal count does not qualify OR bullish signal count > bearish signal count).
Short: short trades enabled AND bearish signal count meets the configured minimum when confluence is enabled (otherwise > 0) AND (bullish signal count does not qualify OR bearish signal count > bullish signal count).
Exit: Long, opposite short-entry condition OR close <= close × (1 − stop-loss percentage) OR close >= close × (1 + take-profit percentage).
Exit: Short, opposite long-entry condition OR close >= close × (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR close <= close × (1 − take-profit percentage).
