Calculation: Band position = (last completed higher-timeframe close − (last completed higher-timeframe EMA(close) − band width × ATR)) / (2 × band width × ATR); use the same higher-timeframe ATR in the band and position calculation.
Calculation: Box high/low = highest high / lowest low across the selected number of last completed higher-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Target = broken box edge ± target multiple × ATR; use chart-timeframe ATR or, if selected, the last completed higher-timeframe ATR.
Long: higher-timeframe band position ≤ bottom threshold AND last completed higher-timeframe volume ≥ volume multiple × its moving average OR box is armed or consolidating AND close > box high AND volume ≥ volume multiple × chart-timeframe moving average volume
Short: higher-timeframe band position ≥ top threshold AND last completed higher-timeframe volume ≥ volume multiple × its moving average OR box is armed or consolidating AND close < box low AND volume ≥ volume multiple × chart-timeframe moving average volume
Exit: long, high ≥ long target
Exit: short, low ≤ short target
