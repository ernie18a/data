Calculation: HLC3 = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Stop distance = ATR × ATR multiplier in ATR mode; otherwise signal-bar close × maximum-loss percentage
Calculation: Profit target distance = stop distance × risk/reward ratio
Long: When SMA-cross mode is selected, the simple moving average of close crosses above the simple moving average of open; OR, when squeeze mode is selected, HLC3 crosses above the upper Bollinger Band with the narrower width parameter while HLC3 is between the upper and lower Bollinger Bands with the wider width parameter; apply the configured trade-side and inverse-side settings, and require the bar to be in the configured session, date range, and enabled trading day, outside the daily exit period, and outside any enabled news window
Short: When SMA-cross mode is selected, the simple moving average of close crosses below the simple moving average of open; OR, when squeeze mode is selected, HLC3 crosses below the lower Bollinger Band with the narrower width parameter while HLC3 is between the upper and lower Bollinger Bands with the wider width parameter; apply the configured trade-side and inverse-side settings, and require the bar to be in the configured session, date range, and enabled trading day, outside the daily exit period, and outside any enabled news window
Exit: Long, price reaches the stop level at signal-bar close minus stop distance OR reaches the target level at signal-bar close plus profit target distance OR the configured trailing stop is triggered OR the bar is in the daily exit period OR the bar is in an enabled news window
Exit: Short, price reaches the stop level at signal-bar close plus stop distance OR reaches the target level at signal-bar close minus profit target distance OR the configured trailing stop is triggered OR the bar is in the daily exit period OR the bar is in an enabled news window
