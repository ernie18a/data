Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = Track the running high and low while the swing direction is up/down. Confirm a high on a wick pullback (high below the prior analysis bar’s high), a close below that bar’s low, a break below the previous confirmed swing low, or a bearish same-bar reversal at the running high; confirm a low by the mirrored conditions. A confirmed high is valid if it exceeds the preceding confirmed high, otherwise it is containment; a confirmed low is valid if it is below the preceding confirmed low, otherwise it is containment. When analyzing a higher timeframe, use its last completed bar for swing detection and regime updates.
Calculation: Bullish regime = a confirmed analysis-bar close above the previous confirmed swing high; confirmed when the latest swing high is valid and the latest swing low is containment.
Calculation: Bearish regime = a confirmed analysis-bar close below the previous confirmed swing low; confirmed when the latest swing high is containment and the latest swing low is valid.
Long: The still-forming analysis bar’s high exceeds the previous confirmed swing high while the regime is not bullish OR the bullish regime is active.
Short: The still-forming analysis bar’s low is below the previous confirmed swing low while the regime is not bearish OR the bearish regime is active.
