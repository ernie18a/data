Calculation: Wave C active = a confirmed pivot high, followed by a confirmed pivot low, followed by another confirmed pivot high; remains active until the next confirmed pivot low. A pivot high/low is a bar whose high/low equals the highest/lowest value in a symmetric window around it, confirmed after the window’s right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Capitulation candle = open > close AND (high - low - abs(close - open)) > abs(close - open) AND volume > a parameter multiple of its shorter-lookback SMA.
