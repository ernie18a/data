Calculation: Aggregated volume = sum of volume across the configured source markets
Long: close crosses above the short-term EMA AND close is above the long-term EMA
Short: close crosses below the short-term EMA AND close is below the long-term EMA
Exit: long, aggregated volume > (the shorter-lookback or longer-lookback SMA of aggregated volume, as selected) × volume multiplier
Exit: short, aggregated volume > (the shorter-lookback or longer-lookback SMA of aggregated volume, as selected) × volume multiplier
Exit: long, close crosses below the long-term EMA
Exit: short, close crosses above the long-term EMA
