Long: Within the configured datetime window, while flat, all enabled entry conditions are true and at least one is enabled: RSI, Ultimate Oscillator, Bollinger %B, ADX, MFI, or CCI crosses above/below or is above/below its configured level; chart close crosses above/below or is above/below Parabolic SAR; fast moving average crosses above/below or is above/below the slower moving average; Stochastic %K crosses above/below or is above/below its configured level, or crosses above/below its %D; MACD is above/below zero or crosses above/below its signal line; or the configured consecutive Heikin Ashi bars are bullish/bearish. Any selected entry predicates are combined with AND.
Exit: Long, within the configured datetime window, while long, either all enabled exit conditions are true and at least one is enabled, or low <= entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage), or high >= entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage). Exit conditions use the same configurable comparisons as entry conditions; any selected exit predicates are combined with AND.
Calculation: Bollinger %B = (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band), where the bands are the close SMA plus/minus the configured multiple of close standard deviation.
Calculation: Ultimate Oscillator = 100 × (4 × fast buying-pressure/true-range ratio + 2 × medium ratio + slow ratio) / 7, where buying pressure is close minus min(low, previous close), and true range is max(high, previous close) minus min(low, previous close).
Calculation: Stochastic %K = the close's position within the configured lookback high-low range; %D = a one-bar SMA of %K (therefore equal to %K in this script).
Calculation: Heikin Ashi bullish/bearish streak = all configured consecutive chart-timeframe Heikin Ashi bars have close > open / close < open.
