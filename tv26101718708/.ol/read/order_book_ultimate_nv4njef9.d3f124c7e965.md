Calculation: Synthetic bid/ask depths = for each of a parameterized set of levels above/below close, multiply a Gaussian distance-from-close weight, average-volume baseline, depth multiplier, round-number weight, side-adjusted pressure weight, side-adjusted OBV weight, regime weight, and volume-ratio adjustment; add volume-weighted historical candle rejections at that level. Level spacing increases with distance from close. Best bid/ask wall = level with greatest depth on that side; wall ratio = greatest depth / average depth on that side; bid/ask depth ratio = total bid depth / total ask depth. A wall is confirmed when its ratio exceeds the adjusted wall threshold and is at least 0.85 times the opposing wall ratio; it is stable when confirmed for the required consecutive bars at approximately the same price.
Calculation: Historical bid/ask zones = stable bid/ask walls recorded on completed chart bars, respectively merged with nearby zones on the same side; their strengths decay each completed chart bar. Nearest historical bid/ask = strongest recorded bid zone at/below close or ask zone at/above close, respectively.
Calculation: Bid/ask absorption = low/high is within an ATR-based zone of the best or nearest historical wall, volume exceeds an average-volume multiple, the corresponding lower/upper wick exceeds 0.85 times candle body, and close is above low + 0.45 candle ranges / below high - 0.45 candle ranges, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = low/high moves beyond the best or nearest historical bid/ask wall by an ATR-based distance and close returns above/below that wall, respectively.
Calculation: Pressure = smoothed sum of volume-weighted signed candle body divided by summed volume over a lookback; in intrabar mode use the equivalent unsmoothed lower-timeframe flow pressure, and in hybrid mode blend both using the intrabar weight multiplied by intrabar quality. Lower-timeframe bars are aggregated within the current chart bar, including any bar still forming.
Calculation: Pro score = 50 + 50 times the clamp to [-1,1] of 0.30 pressure + 0.20 normalized bid/ask depth ratio + 0.18 normalized bid/ask wall-ratio difference + 0.08 recent bullish-minus-bearish candle proportion + 0.06 OBV direction (+0.35/-0.35) + 0.03 RSI contribution (+0.20 below 35, -0.20 above 65, otherwise 0) + 0.08 absorption contribution (+0.35 bid, -0.35 ask) + 0.05 sweep contribution (+0.30 bullish, -0.30 bearish) + 0.02 trend-regime contribution (+0.12/-0.12 by directional DI). Normalized depth ratio is log(bid/ask) / log(imbalance threshold), clamped to [-1,1]; normalized wall difference is (bid wall ratio - ask wall ratio) / max(bid wall ratio, ask wall ratio, 1), clamped to [-1,1].
Calculation: Confidence = clamp to [0,100] the sum of agreement points: 15 if pressure sign matches score direction, 13 each if normalized depth ratio and wall difference signs match, 8 if candle-trend score sign matches, 8 if OBV direction matches, 10 times intrabar quality when lower-timeframe bars exist (otherwise 3), 8 if either absorption occurs, 6 if either sweep occurs, and 19 times regime quality (0.55 low liquidity, 0.75 high volatility, 0.80 ranging, 0.88 trending, otherwise 0.78). Low liquidity means volume / 20-bar average volume < 0.65.
Long: bid absorption OR bullish sweep OR (confirmed chart bar if confirmation is enabled; pro score >= long-bias threshold; confidence >= minimum; and, if anti-noise filtering is enabled, volume >= minimum-volume multiple of 20-bar average, pressure > -0.10, (stable bid wall OR bid absorption OR bullish sweep), and not low liquidity).
Short: ask absorption OR bearish sweep OR (confirmed chart bar if confirmation is enabled; pro score <= short-bias threshold; confidence >= minimum; and, if anti-noise filtering is enabled, volume >= minimum-volume multiple of 20-bar average, pressure < 0.10, (stable ask wall OR ask absorption OR bearish sweep), and not low liquidity).
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + ATR × target distance OR low <= entry price - ATR × invalidation distance OR elapsed bars >= maximum bars in signal OR (close < best bid wall - ATR × invalidation distance AND bid wall confirmed).
Exit: Short, low <= entry price - ATR × target distance OR high >= entry price + ATR × invalidation distance OR elapsed bars >= maximum bars in signal OR (close > best ask wall + ATR × invalidation distance AND ask wall confirmed).
