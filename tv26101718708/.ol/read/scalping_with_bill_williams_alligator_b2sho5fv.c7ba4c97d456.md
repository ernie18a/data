Calculation: Jaw, teeth, lips = selected moving average of Heikin Ashi (high + low) / 2, using respectively the longest, intermediate, and shortest lookbacks
Long: Heikin Ashi close > Heikin Ashi open on the current and preceding bars AND jaw < teeth AND teeth < lips
Short: Heikin Ashi close < Heikin Ashi open on the current and preceding bars AND jaw > teeth AND teeth > lips
Exit: long, jaw > teeth OR jaw > lips OR teeth > lips
Exit: short, jaw < teeth OR jaw < lips OR teeth < lips
