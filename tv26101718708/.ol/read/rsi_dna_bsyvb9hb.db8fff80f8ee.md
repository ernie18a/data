Calculation: RSI = standard relative strength index of close
Calculation: Smoothed RSI = exponential moving average of RSI; use as the displayed RSI only when HA smoothing is enabled
Calculation: RSI Heiken-Ashi direction = bullish when Smoothed RSI > prior HA RSI open, bearish when Smoothed RSI < prior HA RSI open; prior HA RSI open = average of the prior HA RSI open and prior Smoothed RSI
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed chart-timeframe low pivot, price makes a lower low and displayed RSI makes a higher low than at the preceding low pivot; regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed high pivot, price makes a higher high and displayed RSI makes a lower high than at the preceding high pivot. Pivot left and right lookbacks are equal
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = at a confirmed chart-timeframe low pivot, price makes a higher low and displayed RSI makes a lower low than at the preceding low pivot; hidden bearish divergence = at a confirmed high pivot, price makes a lower high and displayed RSI makes a higher high than at the preceding high pivot. Pivot left and right lookbacks are equal
Calculation: Bullish failure swing = displayed RSI forms a pivot low at or below the oversold level, then a pivot high above the oversold level (the break level), then a higher pivot low above both the first low and oversold level; signal when RSI crosses above the break level. Bearish failure swing = displayed RSI forms a pivot high at or above the overbought level, then a pivot low below the overbought level (the break level), then a lower pivot high below both the first high and overbought level; signal when RSI crosses below the break level. Pivot left and right lookbacks are equal
Calculation: RSI squeeze = absolute distance of displayed RSI from the midline is within the configured compression distance and its lookback standard deviation is within the configured maximum
Calculation: Volume-confirmed divergence = selected condition on the divergence pivot bar: volume exceeds its volume moving average by the configured multiplier (spike); or spike plus lower wick/range for bullish divergence or upper wick/range for bearish divergence meets the configured ratio (absorption); or pivot-bar volume is lower than each of the two preceding bars (fade). Apply the configured mode: spike, absorption, fade, spike or absorption, or any
Long: RSI crosses above the midline OR RSI crosses below the oversold level OR regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence OR bullish failure swing OR RSI crosses above the midline plus the configured release distance after a squeeze within the configured post-squeeze window OR HA RSI direction turns bullish OR displayed RSI is above the midline OR (HA RSI direction is bullish AND displayed RSI is below the midline) OR (candle color mode is high/low structure AND high > prior high AND low > prior low) OR (candle color mode is RSI midline AND displayed RSI > midline) OR (candle color mode is HTF bias AND higher-timeframe bias is bullish); divergence signals require the configured volume confirmation when enabled and require the HTF filter to be disabled or bullish when enabled; failure swings and squeeze releases require the HTF filter to be disabled or bullish when enabled
Short: RSI crosses below the midline OR RSI crosses above the overbought level OR regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence OR bearish failure swing OR RSI crosses below the midline minus the configured release distance after a squeeze within the configured post-squeeze window OR HA RSI direction turns bearish OR displayed RSI < midline OR (HA RSI direction is bearish AND displayed RSI > midline) OR (candle color mode is high/low structure AND high < prior high AND low < prior low) OR (candle color mode is RSI midline AND displayed RSI < midline) OR (candle color mode is HTF bias AND higher-timeframe bias is bearish); divergence signals require the configured volume confirmation when enabled and require the HTF filter to be disabled or bearish when enabled; failure swings and squeeze releases require the HTF filter to be disabled or bearish when enabled. HTF bias uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar: close > EMA for bullish / close < EMA for bearish, RSI >= configured bull level for bullish / RSI <= configured bear level for bearish, or both, according to the selected mode
