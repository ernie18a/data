Calculation: WaveTrend fast and signal lines = On higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed bar: the fast line is an EMA of HLC3's deviation from its EMA, normalized by 0.015 times the EMA of the absolute deviation; the signal line is a shorter-lookback SMA of the fast line.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = On higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed bar: the three preceding bars are bearish; the current bar is bullish; its close is at or above the previous bar's open; the previous bar's close is at or above the current open; and the current real body is larger than the previous real body.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = On higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed bar: the two bars before the previous bar and the previous bar are bullish; the current bar is bearish; its open is at or above the previous bar's close; the previous bar's open is at or above the current close; and the current real body is larger than the previous real body.
Long: Bullish engulfing OR WaveTrend fast line crosses above signal line OR (Bullish engulfing AND (WaveTrend fast line is below the oversold threshold OR crosses above the signal line))
Short: Bearish engulfing OR WaveTrend fast line crosses below signal line OR (Bearish engulfing AND (WaveTrend fast line is above the overbought threshold OR crosses below the signal line))
