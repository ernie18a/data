Calculation: Upper rail = highest high over an adaptive lookback of chart bars; lower rail = lowest low over the same lookback
Calculation: Bullish break = close crosses above the upper rail from the previous bar; bearish break = close crosses below the lower rail from the previous bar
Calculation: Trend state = +1 after a bullish break, −1 after a bearish break, otherwise the prior state
Calculation: Adaptive equilibrium = lower rail + rail range × clamp(0.5 + (average(EMA, VWMA-or-WMA) − 0.5) × shift, lower bound, upper bound), where EMA and VWMA-or-WMA smooth the normalized position of HLC3 within the rails; use WMA when VWMA is unavailable
Calculation: Reversal pressure score = weighted sum of opposing close location, wick rejection, opposing body strength, relative volume participation, equilibrium slope or curl, and position within the rail range
Long: Bullish break with prior trend state +1 or −1 OR trend state −1 AND close at or below adaptive equilibrium AND close in the lower rail zone AND (equilibrium turns up or bullish rejection) AND bullish reversal pressure score meets its threshold OR bullish retest entry: a pending bullish break level is touched or swept on a later confirmed bar, close is above that level and equilibrium, the bar closes up with close location in the upper portion of its range, trend state is +1, the break level is above equilibrium, equilibrium rises if its slope filter is enabled, and the configured stop and rail-width filters pass
Short: Bearish break with prior trend state +1 or −1 OR trend state +1 AND close at or above adaptive equilibrium AND close in the upper rail zone AND (equilibrium turns down or bearish rejection) AND bearish reversal pressure score meets its threshold OR bearish retest entry: a pending bearish break level is touched or swept on a later confirmed bar, close is below that level and equilibrium, the bar closes down with close location in the lower portion of its range, trend state is −1, the break level is below equilibrium, equilibrium falls if its slope filter is enabled, and the configured stop and rail-width filters pass
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop price OR high ≥ active target price OR a valid short retest entry replaces the unresolved plan
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop price OR low ≤ active target price OR a valid long retest entry replaces the unresolved plan
