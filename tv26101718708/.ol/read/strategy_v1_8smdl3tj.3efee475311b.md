Calculation: Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe).
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal configurable left- and right-side lookbacks.
Calculation: Break of structure up/down = close crosses above the latest swing high / below the latest swing low, with a configurable price buffer.
Calculation: Opposite-candle zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle before an upward break of structure, or bullish candle before a downward break, within a configurable lookback; expire after a configurable number of chart bars or when close breaks beyond the zone by a configurable buffer.
Calculation: Fast/slow higher-timeframe swing levels = latest confirmed pivot highs/lows on two higher timeframes, using the same pivot lookback; compare chart close with both levels. Use the current higher-timeframe bars, which may still be forming.
Calculation: Fast/slow higher-timeframe moving averages = configured moving average of close on two higher timeframes, using the same lookback; compare each current higher-timeframe close with its average. The higher-timeframe bars may still be forming.
Calculation: Bullish bias = prior-day low sweep (low below prior-day low and close above it); else prior-day high confirmed break (close above it by a configurable distance); else, according to the configured bias method, chart close above both higher-timeframe swing highs or both higher-timeframe moving averages.
Calculation: Bearish bias = prior-day high sweep (high above prior-day high and close below it); else prior-day low confirmed break (close below it by a configurable distance); else, according to the configured bias method, chart close below both higher-timeframe swing lows or both higher-timeframe moving averages.
Calculation: Local high/low sweep = high above the highest prior high / low below the lowest prior low over a configurable chart-bar lookback, with close back below / above that level.
Calculation: Moving average rejection = low at or below the configured chart-timeframe moving average and close above it for long; high at or above it and close below it for short.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish external zone = corresponding zone formed within a configurable number of chart bars after a prior-day or local liquidity sweep, or a confirmed break of the opposite prior-day level.
Calculation: Zone touch = current bar range overlaps the active zone; touched zone remains eligible for a configurable number of chart bars.
Calculation: Long stop = lower of the bullish zone bottom minus a configurable buffer and entry reference minus a configurable fraction of zone height. Short stop = higher of the bearish zone top plus a configurable buffer and entry reference plus that fraction of zone height.
Calculation: Long target = nearer of entry reference plus configured risk multiple times stop distance, and the nearest higher of prior-day high, latest swing high, or bearish zone bottom, each offset by a configurable buffer. Short target = nearer of entry reference minus that risk multiple times stop distance, and the nearest lower of prior-day low, latest swing low, or bullish zone top, each offset by the buffer.
Long: bullish bias AND bullish zone touch AND (zone is external OR internal zones are allowed) AND no configured progression-cancel condition AND (anticipated entry enabled OR bullish zone was touched within the confirmation window AND (BOS confirmation is disabled OR upward BOS/CHoCH)) AND (MA confluence is disabled OR long MA rejection) AND entry reference is inside the active bullish zone AND stop distance is within the configured maximum AND target risk/reward meets the configured minimum AND no active no-trade filter.
Short: bearish bias AND bearish zone touch AND (zone is external OR internal zones are allowed) AND no configured progression-cancel condition AND (anticipated entry enabled OR bearish zone was touched within the confirmation window AND (BOS confirmation is disabled OR downward BOS/CHoCH)) AND (MA confluence is disabled OR short MA rejection) AND entry reference is inside the active bearish zone AND stop distance is within the configured maximum AND target risk/reward meets the configured minimum AND no active no-trade filter.
Long: prior-day high sweep OR local high sweep OR upward BOS OR upward CHoCH.
Short: prior-day low sweep OR local low sweep OR downward BOS OR downward CHoCH.
Exit: long, low <= active stop OR high >= first, second, or final target; after price reaches the first swing-high break level, raise stop by a configurable amount; after price reaches configured progress toward final target, raise stop to entry plus a configurable amount.
Exit: short, high >= active stop OR low <= first, second, or final target; after price reaches the first swing-low break level, lower stop by a configurable amount; after price reaches configured progress toward final target, lower stop to entry minus a configurable amount.
