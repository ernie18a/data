Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range session; retain those levels after the session ends. Up breakout = opening range established AND current close > range high AND previous close <= range high. Down breakout = opening range established AND current close < range low AND previous close >= range low.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars ago; bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars ago. Retain the latest qualifying gap of each direction. Bullish retest = current low <= gap top AND current close >= gap bottom. Bearish retest = current high >= gap bottom AND current close <= gap top.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = a newly confirmed pivot high exceeds the previous confirmed pivot high, or a newly confirmed pivot low is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low, while structure is bullish. Bearish structure break uses the same events while structure is bearish. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish order block = previous bar bearish AND current bar bullish AND current high-low range exceeds ATR times the configured volatility threshold AND current volume exceeds average volume times the configured volume threshold. Bearish order block is the inverse. Retain the latest block of each direction; a block is strong when the sum of its volume-strength and range-strength classifications reaches the configured strength threshold.
Calculation: Premium/discount range = highest high and lowest low over the configured lookback; equilibrium = their midpoint. Premium = close above equilibrium and above the midpoint between equilibrium and range high. Discount = close below equilibrium and below the midpoint between range low and equilibrium.
Calculation: Golden zone = close lies between 0.618 retracement and 1.052 times that level, inclusive, using the primary swing range. Primary swing lookback is shorter than the secondary swing lookback; each range runs from its earlier extreme to its later extreme and uses retracement levels measured from the earlier extreme.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the previous price pivot low while the corresponding RSI pivot low is higher. Bearish divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the previous price pivot high while the corresponding RSI pivot high is lower.
Calculation: Bullish mean-reversion signal = short-lookback RSI below its configured oversold threshold AND close above the long-lookback EMA. Bearish mean-reversion signal = short-lookback RSI above its configured overbought threshold AND close below the long-lookback EMA.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = volume-confirmed hammer OR volume-confirmed bullish engulfing OR morning star. Hammer: lower wick > 2 × body, upper wick < 0.3 × body, body < 0.4 × range, close > open, and volume > average volume × configured multiplier. Bullish engulfing: current candle bullish, previous candle bearish, current open < previous close, current close > previous open, and current volume > previous volume × configured multiplier. Morning star: first candle bearish, middle candle body < 0.3 × its range, third candle bullish, and third close above the midpoint of the first candle's range. Bearish pattern is the mirrored shooting star OR bearish engulfing OR evening star.
Calculation: Liquidity below = three consecutive equal lows that are the lowest of the three bars OR two confirmed pivot lows within the configured percentage tolerance. Liquidity above = three consecutive equal highs that are the highest of the three bars OR two confirmed pivot highs within that tolerance.
Calculation: Long confluence score = configured weighted sum of: up breakout, bullish structure break when required, bullish gap retest, existence of a bullish order block, discount, golden zone, bullish mean-reversion signal, bullish divergence, liquidity below, volume spike, bullish momentum, bullish pattern, and strong bullish order block. Short confluence score = corresponding down breakout, bearish structure break when required, bearish gap retest, bearish order block, premium, golden zone, bearish mean-reversion signal, bearish divergence, liquidity above, volume spike, bearish momentum, bearish pattern, and strong bearish order block. Volume spike = volume > average volume × configured multiplier. Bullish momentum = RSI change over its lookback > 0 AND MACD histogram > 0. Bearish momentum = RSI change over its lookback < 0 AND MACD histogram < 0. Use the same-chart-timeframe bars for all calculations.
Calculation: Regime filter passes when disabled, or when choppy conditions are allowed, or when ATR divided by its moving average is not above the configured threshold. Otherwise classify as choppy. Kill-zone filter passes when disabled or when datetime is in a configured trading session. Volume confirmation = volume > average volume × configured multiplier. Trend confirmation for long = bullish EMA ordering OR close above long-lookback EMA and short EMA above medium EMA; for short, reverse these comparisons.
Calculation: Long stop = established opening-range low when the opening-range filter is enabled; otherwise entry-bar low minus ATR times configured multiplier. Short stop = established opening-range high when enabled; otherwise entry-bar high plus ATR times configured multiplier. Risk = entry price minus long stop, or short stop minus entry price. Target distance = risk times configured reward multiple, increased by the configured volatility adjustment when ATR is above its moving average times the configured threshold.
Long: close > opening range high AND previous close <= opening range high.
Short: close < opening range low AND previous close >= opening range low.
Long: bullish fair value gap retest.
Short: bearish fair value gap retest.
Long: bullish structure break.
Short: bearish structure break.
Long: bullish divergence.
Short: bearish divergence.
Long: bullish pattern.
Short: bearish pattern.
Long: liquidity below.
Short: liquidity above.
Long: long confluence score >= configured mode threshold AND regime filter passes AND datetime is in a kill zone if enabled AND daily trade count is below its configured limit AND position is flat AND volume confirmation passes AND (partial setups are allowed OR long trend confirmation passes) AND risk > 0.
Short: short confluence score >= configured mode threshold AND regime filter passes AND datetime is in a kill zone if enabled AND daily trade count is below its configured limit AND position is flat AND volume confirmation passes AND (partial setups are allowed OR short trend confirmation passes) AND risk > 0.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR datetime is in the configured end-of-day exit session.
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR datetime is in the configured end-of-day exit session.
