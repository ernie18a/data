Calculation: Ribbon tension = Sum of the positive gaps between each pair of adjacent moving averages, where the gap direction is set by whether the fastest moving average is at least the slowest moving average; reversed pairs contribute zero.
Long: Fastest moving average >= slowest moving average OR, at a confirmed ribbon-tension peak in an uptrend, the price high over the peak window is lower than at the previous peak in the same trend leg within the maximum pivot distance and ribbon tension is higher OR, at a confirmed ribbon-tension peak in an uptrend, the price high over the peak window is higher than at the previous peak in the same trend leg within the maximum pivot distance and ribbon tension is lower
Short: Fastest moving average < slowest moving average OR, at a confirmed ribbon-tension peak in a downtrend, the price low over the peak window is higher than at the previous peak in the same trend leg within the maximum pivot distance and ribbon tension is higher OR, at a confirmed ribbon-tension peak in a downtrend, the price low over the peak window is lower than at the previous peak in the same trend leg within the maximum pivot distance and ribbon tension is lower
