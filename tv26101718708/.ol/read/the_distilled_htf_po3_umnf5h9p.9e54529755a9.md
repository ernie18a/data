Calculation: CISD origin = open of the oldest chart-timeframe bar in the consecutive bearish run immediately preceding a low sweep, or the consecutive bullish run immediately preceding a high sweep.
Calculation: Three-bar fair value gap = a gap between the first and third chart-timeframe bars, with displacement between their candle bodies as well as their wicks.
Long: A completed higher-timeframe candle has a low below the previous completed higher-timeframe candle’s low and closes at or above that low, AND a chart-timeframe bar closes above the CISD origin before the end of the next higher-timeframe candle, AND, if a three-bar fair value gap exists in the run, a chart-timeframe bar closes above its inversion threshold before that deadline.
Short: A completed higher-timeframe candle has a high above the previous completed higher-timeframe candle’s high and closes at or below that high, AND a chart-timeframe bar closes below the CISD origin before the end of the next higher-timeframe candle, AND, if a three-bar fair value gap exists in the run, a chart-timeframe bar closes below its inversion threshold before that deadline.
