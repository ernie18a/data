Calculation: Opening-range high, low, and average volume = highest high, lowest low, and average volume over the opening-range lookback, captured on the bar whose zero-based index equals that lookback and held constant thereafter
Calculation: Long initial stop = lowest low over the stop lookback minus an ATR multiple; short initial stop = highest high over the stop lookback plus an ATR multiple
Calculation: Long targets = entry price plus the initial risk distance multiplied by the first and second reward multiples; short targets = entry price minus the initial risk distance multiplied by those multiples, where long risk distance is entry price minus long initial stop and short risk distance is short initial stop minus entry price
Long: close crosses above SMA50 AND RSI is below its oversold threshold AND close is below VWAP AND close is above SMA200 OR EMA9 crosses above EMA20 AND close is above VWAP AND close is above SMA200 OR opening-range high is defined AND close crosses above opening-range high AND volume exceeds opening-range average volume multiplied by its threshold
Short: close crosses below SMA50 AND RSI is above its overbought threshold AND close is above VWAP AND close is below SMA200 OR EMA9 crosses below EMA20 AND close is below VWAP AND close is below SMA200 OR opening-range low is defined AND close crosses below opening-range low AND volume exceeds opening-range average volume multiplied by its threshold
Exit: long, low is at or below the active stop OR high is at or above the first target OR high is at or above the second target; after a partial exit at the first target, the active stop is the entry price
Exit: short, high is at or above the active stop OR low is at or below the first target OR low is at or below the second target; after a partial exit at the first target, the active stop is the entry price
