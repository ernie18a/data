Calculation: 12 moving averages = the same selected moving-average type applied to close with progressively longer lookbacks; the mean is their arithmetic average.
Calculation: Bullish MA touch = low lies between any adjacent pair of the 12 moving averages (including the upper boundary and excluding the lower), while close > their mean and MA5 > MA6 > MA7 > MA8.
Calculation: Bearish MA touch = high lies between any adjacent pair of the 12 moving averages (including the lower boundary and excluding the upper), while close < their mean and MA5 < MA6 < MA7 < MA8.
Long: position is flat AND close > mean of 12 moving averages AND MA5 > MA6 > MA7 > MA8 AND high > previous high AND previous bar had a bullish MA touch
Short: position is flat AND close < mean of 12 moving averages AND MA5 < MA6 < MA7 < MA8 AND low < previous low AND previous bar had a bearish MA touch
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target
