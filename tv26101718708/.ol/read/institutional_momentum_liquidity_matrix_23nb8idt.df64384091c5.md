Calculation: Reverse RSI price for target r = previous completed chart-bar close + max((r/(100-r) × previous average loss − previous average gain) × (length−1), 0) when that value is positive; otherwise previous close − max((previous average gain ÷ (r/(100-r)) − previous average loss) × (length−1), 0) when positive; otherwise previous close. Average gains and losses are Wilder-smoothed from chart-bar close changes. Use the configured overbought and oversold targets for their respective prices.
Calculation: Current-profile POC = midpoint of the highest-volume price bin in the selected current session/profile. Build bins over that profile's chart-bar high-to-low range; distribute each chart bar's total lower-timeframe volume evenly across the bins it spans. Use lower-timeframe bars (lower than the chart timeframe) completed by the completed chart bar; use the selected daily, weekly, monthly, or custom-session profile.
Calculation: RSI-profile bin = volume-weighted mean of lower-timeframe RSI values assigned to the bin; weight by lower-timeframe volume or absolute open-to-close change, according to the selected liquidity source. For each chart bar, distribute its lower-timeframe weighted total evenly across bins it spans. Also retain the maximum and minimum of these chart-bar RSI values within the profile.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe. POC protection for a projected price = its absolute distance from the current-profile POC is no greater than ATR multiplied by the configured proximity tolerance.
Long: chart bar is complete AND ((low <= oversold reverse RSI price AND (POC proximity filter is disabled OR oversold reverse RSI price is protected by the POC)) OR (a non-POC profile bin meets the configured volume significance threshold AND ((peak-retention mode AND bin minimum RSI <= oversold threshold AND bin maximum RSI < overbought threshold) OR (volume-weighted mode AND bin mean RSI <= 50 − (50 − oversold threshold) × configured sensitivity)))
Short: chart bar is complete AND ((high >= overbought reverse RSI price AND (POC proximity filter is disabled OR overbought reverse RSI price is protected by the POC)) OR (a non-POC profile bin meets the configured volume significance threshold AND ((peak-retention mode AND bin maximum RSI >= overbought threshold AND bin minimum RSI > oversold threshold) OR (volume-weighted mode AND bin mean RSI >= 50 + (overbought threshold − 50) × configured sensitivity)))
Exit: long, low <= entry price − chart-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiplier AND chart bar is complete OR high >= entry price + chart-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiplier × configured reward multiple
Exit: short, high >= entry price + chart-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiplier AND chart bar is complete OR low <= entry price − chart-timeframe ATR × configured stop multiplier × configured reward multiple
