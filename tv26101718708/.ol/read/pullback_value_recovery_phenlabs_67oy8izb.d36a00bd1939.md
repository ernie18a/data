Calculation: Prior range = highest high and lowest low over a rolling lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Advance mean = episode-anchored mean of HLC3 for bars whose close-to-close change follows the seed direction; use volume weighting when valid volume mode applies, otherwise equal-bar weighting
Calculation: Retracement mean = episode-anchored mean of HLC3 for bars whose close-to-close change opposes the seed direction, with the same weighting mode as the advance mean
Calculation: Outer mean = the higher of the advance and retracement means for a long setup, or the lower for a short setup
Calculation: Fresh bullish seed = current confirmed close exceeds the prior range high, its upward body meets the impulse threshold times ATR from before this bar, it closes in the candle's upper quarter, and the same breakout condition was false on the prior bar; the bearish seed is the mirrored condition
Calculation: Pullback depth = distance from the local pullback's starting peak to its lowest low for a long, or from its starting trough to its highest high for a short; reset pullback tracking after a close beyond the prior running directional extreme
Long: A fresh bullish seed occurs while warmed up, idle, and outside cooldown; later, before setup expiry or origin invalidation, an earlier bar has established the required adverse-close count and pullback depth and armed the setup with close at or below the outer mean plus the recovery buffer; on a subsequent confirmed bar, close is above the prior-bar outer mean plus buffer and above the previous bar's high, stop distance from close to the signal-inclusive pullback low plus padding is positive and within its maximum, and distance from close to the prior outer mean is within its maximum chase
Short: A fresh bearish seed occurs while warmed up, idle, and outside cooldown; later, before setup expiry or origin invalidation, an earlier bar has established the required adverse-close count and pullback depth and armed the setup with close at or above the outer mean minus the recovery buffer; on a subsequent confirmed bar, close is below the prior-bar outer mean minus buffer and below the previous bar's low, stop distance from the signal-inclusive pullback high plus padding is positive and within its maximum, and distance from the prior outer mean to close is within its maximum chase
