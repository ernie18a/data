Calculation: Test band = eligible pre-existing level ± max(minimum tick, ATR-scaled tolerance); levels are current auction VAL/VAH/POC, completed auction-profile VAL/VAH/POC, active DPOCs, and earlier POCs. Use only levels present in the prior-close area registry; completed profiles become available when closed.
Calculation: Eligible test = current bar range overlaps the band; VAL is a long-test edge, VAH a short-test edge, and POC/DPOC a long test only after the prior close was above the band or a short test only after it was below.
Calculation: Localized absorption = unusually high bar volume versus its prior average, with at least a minimum share of volume inside the band and signed band delta opposing the test side; require insufficient price progress from the prior close or a close beyond the response threshold.
Calculation: Material CVD divergence = price extends beyond the preceding same-side pivot while cumulative footprint delta moves oppositely, with sufficient price extension, prominence, delta separation, and pivot spacing; formal pivots use equal left and right lookbacks. Early tests compare current extremes with the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Response buffer = max(minimum tick, ATR-scaled buffer). A pending test is cancelled by loss of its source, invalid or missing footprint totals, a contradictory accepted/migrated auction event, an adverse extreme beyond its frozen threshold, or expiry of the configured response deadline.
Long: eligible long test AND (localized absorption OR material bullish CVD divergence) AND source remains valid AND close > test-band upper edge + response buffer, on the evidence bar or within the pending response deadline.
Short: eligible short test AND (localized absorption OR material bearish CVD divergence) AND source remains valid AND close < test-band lower edge - response buffer, on the evidence bar or within the pending response deadline.
Exit: long, close < frozen test-band lower edge - response buffer.
Exit: short, close > frozen test-band upper edge + response buffer.
