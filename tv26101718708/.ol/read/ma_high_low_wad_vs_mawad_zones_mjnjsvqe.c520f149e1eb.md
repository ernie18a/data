Calculation: Williams Accumulation/Distribution (WAD) = cumulative sum, starting at 0, of close − min(low, previous close) when close > previous close; close − max(high, previous close) when close < previous close; and 0 when close = previous close.
Calculation: MA(WAD) = moving average of WAD.
Long: WAD ≥ MA(WAD)
Short: WAD < MA(WAD)
