Calculation: Confirmation score = weighted points for close beyond entry, close beyond setup level, candle direction, close position within the bar, close relative to VWAP, and close relative to prior close; use long-direction comparisons for longs and inverse comparisons for shorts.
Calculation: Volatility ratio = current ATR / its longer-lookback average; volatile and extreme-volatility states are reached when this ratio exceeds their respective thresholds.
Long: Valid configured long setup AND (close is within the configured priority range of the long level OR close crosses above the long level OR, after that cross, low touches the long level and close remains above it); then, while armed, a confirmed bar has low ≤ entry and close ≥ entry − 0.5 × |entry − stop-loss|; then, before timeout or invalidation, a confirmed bar outside the US open window has long confirmation score ≥ minimum score, where the minimum rises in volatile conditions, rises further in extreme volatility, and rises after a stop-loss that day.
Short: Valid configured short setup AND (close is within the configured priority range of the short level OR close crosses below the short level OR, after that cross, high touches the short level and close remains below it); then, while armed, a confirmed bar has high ≥ entry and close ≤ entry + 0.5 × |entry − stop-loss|; then, before timeout or invalidation, a confirmed bar outside the US open window has short confirmation score ≥ minimum score, where the minimum rises in volatile conditions, rises further in extreme volatility, and rises after a stop-loss that day.
Long: When no A+ level is in reach and no trade is active, a valid B long level crosses upward on close.
Short: When no A+ level is in reach and no trade is active, a valid B short level crosses downward on close.
Exit: Long, after entry bar, high ≥ first target (close half and activate break-even stop) OR high ≥ second target OR low ≤ active stop OR, before first target in automatic paper management, any health hard-exit rule is met or health status is below its critical threshold.
Exit: Short, after entry bar, low ≤ first target (close half and activate break-even stop) OR low ≤ second target OR high ≥ active stop OR, before first target in automatic paper management, any health hard-exit rule is met or health status is below its critical threshold.
