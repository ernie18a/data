Calculation: Active COF = high and low of the latest completed bar from the selected higher or lower timeframe; initialize from its first available completed bar and replace it with a completed bar whose close is above the active high or below the active low
Calculation: COF entry level = active COF low + (active COF high − active COF low) × the configured discount percentage for bullish COFs or premium percentage for bearish COFs
Long: For either the higher-timeframe or lower-timeframe COF, its latest completed bar closed at or above its open AND, after a chart bar high first exceeded the bullish COF entry level, a later chart bar low reached or fell below that level AND a later completed lower-timeframe confirmation bar closed within the active COF range AND the current chart close is at least the confirmation close plus the configured momentum distance AND no long signal has already triggered for this COF
Short: For either the higher-timeframe or lower-timeframe COF, its latest completed bar closed below its open AND, after a chart bar low first fell below the bearish COF entry level, a later chart bar high reached or exceeded that level AND a later completed lower-timeframe confirmation bar closed within the active COF range AND the current chart close is at most the confirmation close minus the configured momentum distance AND no short signal has already triggered for this COF
