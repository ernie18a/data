Calculation: Bullish FVG = on a confirmed bar of the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe, low > high two bars earlier; zone is between that earlier high and the current low. Bearish FVG = high < low two bars earlier; zone is between the current high and that earlier low.
Calculation: Bullish order block = after a close above the latest confirmed pivot high, the most recent bearish candle within the longer order-block search lookback; bearish order block = after a close below the latest confirmed pivot low, the most recent bullish candle within that lookback. The pivot uses equal lookbacks on both sides. Zones are created and invalidated only on confirmed bars of the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe; invalidate a bullish zone when close is below its low, or a bearish zone when close is above its high.
Calculation: Timeframe bias = for each enabled chart-timeframe-or-higher series, use the direction of the newest order-block zone containing close, if any; otherwise the newest fair-value-gap zone containing close, if any; otherwise the newest order-block direction within its calculation depth, if any; otherwise the newest fair-value-gap direction within its calculation depth, or neutral if neither exists. Use only last completed bars of higher timeframes.
Calculation: Global bias score = sum of each enabled timeframe bias multiplied by its fixed weight, with successive timeframe weights increasing from 1 through 4.
Long: Any enabled timeframe bias > 0 OR global bias score > 0 OR a bullish fair-value gap forms OR a bullish order block forms
Short: Any enabled timeframe bias < 0 OR global bias score < 0 OR a bearish fair-value gap forms OR a bearish order block forms
