Calculation: Candle fingerprint = [absolute(open−close)/max(high−low, minimum price increment), (high−max(open,close))/range, (min(open,close)−low)/range, (min(open,close)−low)/range, min(volume/average volume over a lookback, 3)/3]; optionally include min(range/ATR, 2)/2, (clamp((close−EMA)/ATR, −2, 2)+2)/4, and candle context (inside previous bar range = 0, outside = 1, otherwise = 0.5). Use same dimensions and parameters for current and historical candles; averages and indicators use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Similarity score = 100 × (1 − min(Euclidean distance between current and historical fingerprints, √number of active dimensions)/√number of active dimensions).
Calculation: Historical outcome = compare (high of the bar N bars after the candidate close − candidate close)/ATR at candidate bar with (candidate close − low of that bar)/ATR at candidate bar; outcome is the larger magnitude, positive for the first and negative for the second (negative on a tie).
Calculation: Best qualifying match = the historical candidate with the highest similarity score among candidates in the lookback window that are at least N+2 chart bars old and whose absolute historical outcome meets the minimum outcome threshold; N is the forward-bar parameter. If scores tie, use the first candidate encountered in the scan, which is the oldest. Its outcome sign sets direction.
Long: Best qualifying match outcome > 0 AND its similarity score ≥ minimum score AND at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the previous signal.
Short: Best qualifying match outcome < 0 AND its similarity score ≥ minimum score AND at least the cooldown number of chart bars have elapsed since the previous signal.
