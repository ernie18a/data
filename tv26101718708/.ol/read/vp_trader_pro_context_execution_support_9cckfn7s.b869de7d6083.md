Calculation: Signal volume profile = On each chart bar, select the configured rolling lookback, session range, or higher-timeframe range through the current bar. For a higher-timeframe range, include the still-forming bar and optionally preceding completed bars. Divide the range from its lowest low to highest high into equal price bins; distribute each chart bar's nonnegative volume equally among the bins touched by its low-to-high range. The point of control is the center of the first maximum-volume bin. Starting there, add the larger adjacent bin, choosing the upper bin on ties, until the selected volume fraction is reached. Value-area low and high are the lower and upper edges of the selected bins, rounded to the price increment.
Calculation: Reference profile = Save the signal profile at a configurable bar interval; at a session-range boundary, save the preceding bar's profile. Initialize it with the first available signal profile.
Calculation: Directional bias = Round and clamp a weighted sum of the point-of-control shift divided by current value-area width, value-area overlap transformed to a signed scale, close minus point of control divided by value-area width, and the current acceptance direction. Clamp the shift and location components to a symmetric unit range. Overlap is the intersection of current and reference value areas divided by their union; its signed component is twice one minus overlap, minus one. Acceptance direction is positive on an upward acceptance event, negative on a downward acceptance event, and zero otherwise.
Calculation: Session cumulative volume delta = Reset at each configured session start; during the session add volume when close is above open, subtract volume when close is below open, and add zero otherwise.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = A pivot high or low with the same configurable number of chart bars on each side; evaluate it only after the right-hand bars have completed. Compare each pivot with the preceding confirmed pivot of the same type and use cumulative volume delta at each pivot. Require the current confirmation bar to be more than a configurable cooldown after the pivot bar of the last bullish or bearish divergence.
Long: Close is above signal value-area high OR directional bias is positive OR the close returns inside the signal value area after fewer than the required consecutive closes below value-area low OR a confirmed pivot low is lower than the preceding pivot low AND its session cumulative volume delta is higher than at the preceding pivot low AND the divergence cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Close is below signal value-area low OR directional bias is negative OR the close returns inside the signal value area after fewer than the required consecutive closes above value-area high OR a confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND its session cumulative volume delta is lower than at the preceding pivot high AND the divergence cooldown has elapsed.
