Calculation: Opening-range high = high of the first bar of each trading day
Calculation: Opening-range low = low of the first bar of each trading day
Long: Opening range is set AND (close crosses above opening-range high OR (close crosses above VWAP AND RSI > 50) OR (RSI crosses above 50 AND close > VWAP))
Short: Opening range is set AND (close crosses below opening-range low OR (close crosses below VWAP AND RSI < 50))
