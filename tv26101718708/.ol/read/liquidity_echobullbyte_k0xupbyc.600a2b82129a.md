Calculation: HTF compression zone = on a higher timeframe, the current bar’s high-low range is below a calibrated fraction of the mean range of that bar and the prior two bars; zone boundaries are that bar’s high and low. Use the higher-timeframe bar value available on each chart bar (the current forming bar in real time; the completed bar value historically when it closes).
Calculation: Eligible support/resistance = nearest active, unexpired compression-zone low below close / high above close, subject to the minimum touch count; expire zones after their configured lifetime, and increment a zone’s touch count when a chart bar reaches within 0.25 ATR of either boundary and overlaps the zone.
Calculation: Pressure gradient = [(2×close−high−low)/max(high−low, minimum price increment) now − the same value two chart bars earlier] / 2.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / a rolling volume average; use the longer averaging window when volume’s coefficient of variation exceeds its threshold, otherwise the shorter window.
Calculation: Calibrated thresholds = derive compression, volume-vacuum, minimum-wick, and pressure-gradient thresholds from the calibration-window mean ATR percentile, volume coefficient of variation, and mean wick-to-body ratio; apply the configured sensitivity bias and threshold clamps.
Calculation: Bull spike sweep = eligible support exists AND volume vacuum is active AND low < support AND close ≥ support − ATR×maximum body distance AND lower wick ≥ ATR×minimum wick AND pressure gradient > its threshold.
Calculation: Bear spike sweep = eligible resistance exists AND volume vacuum is active AND high > resistance AND close ≤ resistance + ATR×maximum body distance AND upper wick ≥ ATR×minimum wick AND pressure gradient < −its threshold.
Calculation: Bull grind sweep = grind path enabled by setting or calibration AND eligible support exists AND any of the preceding configured lookback bars had low < support − 0.10 ATR AND close > support AND close < support + ATR×maximum grind-close distance AND close > open AND pressure gradient > its threshold.
Calculation: Bear grind sweep = grind path enabled by setting or calibration AND eligible resistance exists AND any of the preceding configured lookback bars had high > resistance + 0.10 ATR AND close < resistance AND close > resistance − ATR×maximum grind-close distance AND close < open AND pressure gradient < −its threshold.
Long: Bull spike sweep OR bull grind sweep OR, while idle and within the configured sweep window after a confirmed bull sweep, confirmed bar closes above open, body size / ATR ≥ minimum commitment-body ratio, volume is valid and volume ratio ≥ minimum commitment-volume ratio, close > swept-zone boundary when close-crossing is enabled, close > EMA when EMA filter is enabled, and the prior bar closes above open and above swept-zone boundary when two-bar confirmation is enabled; also require readiness and minimum signal spacing for entry.
Short: Bear spike sweep OR bear grind sweep OR, while idle and within the configured sweep window after a confirmed bear sweep, confirmed bar closes below open, body size / ATR ≥ minimum commitment-body ratio, volume is valid and volume ratio ≥ minimum commitment-volume ratio, close < swept-zone boundary when close-crossing is enabled, close < EMA when EMA filter is enabled, and the prior bar closes below open and below swept-zone boundary when two-bar confirmation is enabled; also require readiness and minimum signal spacing for entry.
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + ATR×Target 1 distance (partial close); high ≥ entry + ATR×Target 2 distance; low ≤ current stop; or Target 1 has not been hit and bars since entry > timeout. If enabled, move stop to entry after Target 1; if trailing is enabled and its start condition is met, ratchet stop to the greater of its prior value and the lowest low of the last four bars minus ATR×trailing offset.
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − ATR×Target 1 distance (partial close); low ≤ entry − ATR×Target 2 distance; high ≥ current stop; or Target 1 has not been hit and bars since entry > timeout. If enabled, move stop to entry after Target 1; if trailing is enabled and its start condition is met, ratchet stop to the lesser of its prior value and the highest high of the last four bars plus ATR×trailing offset.
