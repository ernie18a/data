Calculation: Volume rank = percentile rank of current volume among the latest 100 chart bars
Calculation: Spread rank = percentile rank of current high − low among the latest 100 chart bars
Calculation: Low effort = volume rank below the configured low-volume threshold; high effort = volume rank at or above the configured high-volume threshold
Calculation: Narrow spread = spread rank below the configured narrow-spread threshold; normal, wide, and ultra-wide spreads are the configured successive higher spread-rank bands
Calculation: Close position = ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low); high, middle, and low positions are determined by the configured close-position threshold
Calculation: Upper rejection = upper wick / range at or above the configured wick threshold, greater than the lower-wick ratio, and body / range at or below the configured maximum; lower rejection is the inverse
Calculation: Background = strong when the rolling sum of bullish VSA scores minus bearish VSA scores exceeds the configured positive threshold; weak when it is below the corresponding negative threshold. Per-bar scores: bullish efficiency 3, easy upward movement 1, constructive upward 2; bearish efficiency 3, easy downward movement 1, constructive downward 2; all other cases 0. When trend influence is enabled, add 5 to the score matching the current trend direction.
Calculation: Down-move z-score = z-score of ln(close / close three chart bars earlier), using its 20-bar mean and population standard deviation
Long: Prior bar is a No Supply setup and current close > prior close. Setup: prior bar closed below the close before it, has low effort or volume below each of the prior two bars, has narrow spread, and has middle or high close position. If traditional No Supply mode is enabled, require volume below each of the prior two bars and omit the close-position condition.
Long: Prior bar is a lower rejection with middle or high close position, normal, wide, or ultra-wide spread, and strong background; current close > prior close. If the clear-uptrend filter is enabled, require bullish trend direction on the setup bar.
Long: Current bar is a stopping-volume bar and background is strong. Stopping-volume bar: close < prior close, high effort, narrow or normal spread, middle close position, and stopping-volume state active. The state activates when down-move z-score ≤ −2 and deactivates five bars after its most recent activation.
Short: Prior bar is a No Demand setup and current close < prior close. Setup: prior bar closed above the close before it, has low effort or volume below each of the prior two bars, has narrow spread, and middle close position. If traditional No Demand mode is enabled, require volume below each of the prior two bars and omit the close-position condition.
Short: Prior bar is an upper rejection with middle close position, normal, wide, or ultra-wide spread, and weak background; current close < prior close. If the clear-downtrend filter is enabled, require bearish trend direction on the setup bar.
Short: Current bar is a stopping-volume bar and background is not strong. Stopping-volume bar: close < prior close, high effort, narrow or normal spread, middle close position, and stopping-volume state active.
