Calculation: ALMA = Arnaud Legoux moving average of close; the fast set uses three shorter lookbacks and the slow set uses three longer lookbacks, each optionally smoothed by a second ALMA.
Long: (fast set enabled AND open < all three fast ALMAs AND close > all three fast ALMAs AND no fast-set bullish or bearish engulf signal occurred within the configured cooldown) OR (slow set enabled AND open < all three slow ALMAs AND close > all three slow ALMAs AND no slow-set bullish or bearish engulf signal occurred within the configured cooldown)
Short: (fast set enabled AND open > all three fast ALMAs AND close < all three fast ALMAs AND no fast-set bullish or bearish engulf signal occurred within the configured cooldown) OR (slow set enabled AND open > all three slow ALMAs AND close < all three slow ALMAs AND no slow-set bullish or bearish engulf signal occurred within the configured cooldown)
