Calculation: Bullish FVG = a bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier; store its upper boundary as that bar’s low and lower boundary as the earlier high until invalidated.
Calculation: Bearish FVG = a bar’s high is below the low two bars earlier; store its upper boundary as the earlier low and lower boundary as that bar’s high until invalidated.
Long: low is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars AND close is above that low OR low is below the low of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar AND close is above that low OR close is above the upper boundary of an un-inverted bearish FVG OR close is above the low of the most recent earlier down candle AND the previous close is at or below that candle’s low.
Short: high is above the highest high of the preceding lookback bars AND close is below that high OR high is above the high of the previous completed higher-timeframe bar AND close is below that high OR close is below the lower boundary of an un-inverted bullish FVG OR close is below the high of the most recent earlier up candle AND the previous close is at or above that candle’s high.
