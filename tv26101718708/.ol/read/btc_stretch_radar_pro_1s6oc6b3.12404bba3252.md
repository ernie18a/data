Calculation: EMA stretch (%) = 100 × (close − EMA(close)) / EMA(close)
Calculation: Upper and lower stretch extremes = rolling mean of EMA stretch ± multiplier × rolling standard deviation of EMA stretch, using the same longer lookback
Calculation: ATR baseline = simple average of ATR over a longer lookback than the ATR lookback
Long: EMA stretch < lower stretch extreme AND ATR > ATR baseline
Short: EMA stretch > upper stretch extreme AND ATR > ATR baseline
