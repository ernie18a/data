Calculation: ATR exceedance = current high > previous bar's high + previous ATR × multiplier OR current low < previous bar's low - previous ATR × multiplier
Calculation: Exceedance frequency = percentage of ATR-exceedance bars over a short lookback
Calculation: Exceedance z-score = (current exceedance frequency − its long-lookback mean) / its long-lookback standard deviation
Long: Exceedance z-score > sensitivity threshold
Short: Exceedance z-score < −sensitivity threshold
