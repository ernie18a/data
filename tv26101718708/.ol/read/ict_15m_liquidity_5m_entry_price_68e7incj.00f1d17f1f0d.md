Calculation: Sell-side liquidity levels = enabled previous-day low from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar, completed Asia and London session lows, and latest confirmed enabled 15-minute and 4-hour pivot lows; buy-side levels use the corresponding highs.
Calculation: Bullish 15-minute displacement = completed 15-minute close > open AND absolute body ≥ the average body of the five 15-minute bars ending two bars before the completed bar × the configured strength multiplier; bearish displacement reverses the close/open comparison.
Calculation: Bullish 15-minute FVG = completed 15-minute low > high from two bars before it; bearish 15-minute FVG = completed 15-minute high < low from two bars before it.
Calculation: Confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high/low = the latest pivot high/low confirmed using the configured lookback on chart-timeframe bars.
Long: On a completed higher-timeframe 15-minute bar, its low < an enabled sell-side liquidity level, selecting the highest level swept; within the configured reclaim limit, a completed 15-minute close > that level; within the configured confirmation limit after reclaim, bullish 15-minute displacement; AND on a later chart-timeframe bar within the configured setup limit, no low < the sweep-period low, a pullback has occurred (close < open OR low < prior bar low OR price overlaps the bullish confirmation zone when one exists), and within the configured pullback-to-MSS limit a later close > the chart-timeframe pivot high recorded at the pullback, with close > open; AND any configured displacement requirement is met; AND any enabled New York session filter is satisfied; AND the configured signal cooldown has elapsed.
Short: On a completed higher-timeframe 15-minute bar, its high > an enabled buy-side liquidity level, selecting the lowest level swept; within the configured reclaim limit, a completed 15-minute close < that level; within the configured confirmation limit after reclaim, bearish 15-minute displacement; AND on a later chart-timeframe bar within the configured setup limit, no high > the sweep-period high, a pullback has occurred (close > open OR high > prior bar high OR price overlaps the bearish confirmation zone when one exists), and within the configured pullback-to-MSS limit a later close < the chart-timeframe pivot low recorded at the pullback, with close < open; AND any configured displacement requirement is met; AND any enabled New York session filter is satisfied; AND the configured signal cooldown has elapsed.
