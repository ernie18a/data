Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = short-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over its lookback.
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high / lowest low during the opening-range session.
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed local high/low using the same lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle = hammer (lower wick exceeds a body-size threshold, upper wick is below a body-size threshold, and close is in the upper half of the bar) OR bullish engulfing (current bullish body engulfs the prior bearish body and is larger). Bearish reversal candle is the inverse.
Calculation: Initial risk = ATR at entry × stop multiple; initial stop = entry price − initial risk for long, entry price + initial risk for short; first and second targets are entry price ± initial risk × their respective reward multiples, with plus for long and minus for short.
Long: cooldown elapsed AND (trend-pullback conditions OR opening-range breakout conditions OR mean-reversion conditions OR (no primary long signal this bar AND (VWAP reclaim conditions OR VWAP rejection conditions OR liquidity-sweep conditions))); enter only when flat. Trend-pullback conditions: ADX above trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI AND higher-timeframe trend is up AND (low <= short-lookback EMA OR distance from close to that EMA is below an ATR-scaled threshold) AND close > that EMA AND body size exceeds an ATR-scaled minimum AND close > open AND relative volume >= threshold AND longer-lookback RSI below its upper limit AND close < long-lookback EMA + ATR-scaled cap. Opening-range breakout conditions: opening range complete AND current bar is outside the range session and within its validity period AND no opening-range signal has fired today AND close > opening-range high AND close > open AND relative volume >= threshold AND higher-timeframe trend is up. Mean-reversion conditions: close > long-lookback SMA AND short-lookback RSI below its lower limit AND close < short-lookback EMA AND bullish reversal candle AND volume > its average. VWAP reclaim conditions: ADX above trend threshold AND positive DI > negative DI AND higher-timeframe trend is up AND prior close <= prior VWAP AND close > VWAP AND close > open AND volume > its average AND longer-lookback RSI below its upper limit. VWAP rejection conditions: ADX below range threshold AND close < VWAP − ATR-scaled distance AND bullish reversal candle AND volume > its average. Liquidity-sweep conditions: prior bar swept below the latest confirmed swing low (low < swing low AND close > swing low) AND current close > open AND close > swing low AND volume > its average.
Short: cooldown elapsed AND (trend-pullback conditions OR opening-range breakout conditions OR mean-reversion conditions OR (no primary short signal this bar AND (VWAP reclaim conditions OR VWAP rejection conditions OR liquidity-sweep conditions))); enter only when flat. Trend-pullback conditions: ADX above trend threshold AND negative DI > positive DI AND higher-timeframe trend is down AND (high >= short-lookback EMA OR distance from close to that EMA is below an ATR-scaled threshold) AND close < that EMA AND body size exceeds an ATR-scaled minimum AND close < open AND relative volume >= threshold AND longer-lookback RSI above its lower limit AND close > long-lookback EMA − ATR-scaled cap. Opening-range breakout conditions: opening range complete AND current bar is outside the range session and within its validity period AND no opening-range signal has fired today AND close < opening-range low AND close < open AND relative volume >= threshold AND higher-timeframe trend is down. Mean-reversion conditions: close < long-lookback SMA AND short-lookback RSI above its upper limit AND close > short-lookback EMA AND bearish reversal candle AND volume > its average. VWAP reclaim conditions: ADX above trend threshold AND negative DI > positive DI AND higher-timeframe trend is down AND prior close >= prior VWAP AND close < VWAP AND close < open AND volume > its average AND longer-lookback RSI above its lower limit. VWAP rejection conditions: ADX below range threshold AND close > VWAP + ATR-scaled distance AND bearish reversal candle AND volume > its average. Liquidity-sweep conditions: prior bar swept above the latest confirmed swing high (high > swing high AND close < swing high) AND current close < open AND close < swing high AND volume > its average.
Exit: long, high >= second target OR low <= current stop OR bars held >= time limit. After high reaches the first target, move the stop to entry price.
Exit: short, low <= second target OR high >= current stop OR bars held >= time limit. After low reaches the first target, move the stop to entry price.
