Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close.
Calculation: Bullish trend count = number of sampled timeframes where that timeframe’s close is above its 200-period EMA; bearish trend count = number where it is below.
Long: RSI has crossed below 30 at least twice since the most recent cross above 70 or long signal, AND RSI crosses above 50, AND bullish trend count ≥ 5
Short: RSI has crossed above 70 at least twice since the most recent cross below 30 or short signal, AND RSI crosses below 50, AND bearish trend count ≥ 5
