Calculation: Bull trend = fast EMA > base EMA AND base EMA > slow EMA
Calculation: Bear trend = fast EMA < base EMA AND base EMA < slow EMA
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Pullback depth (bull) = (highest high over the pullback lookback − low) / ATR; pullback depth (bear) = (high − lowest low over the pullback lookback) / ATR
Calculation: Normalized trend slope = (base EMA − base EMA from the slope lookback bars ago) / ATR, multiplied by +1 for bull trend or −1 for bear trend
Calculation: Target room = distance in the trend direction from close to the prior trend-side extreme over the target lookback, divided by ATR; if that reference is behind price or leaves less than 0.30 ATR, use the configured open-room distance instead
Calculation: Continuation score = clamp to 0–100 of 24% trend score + 22% pullback-depth score + 16% volume score + 22% defense-response score + 16% target-room score. Trend score combines EMA-stack score (100 for a complete trend stack) and normalized-slope score, weighted 62% and 38%. Pullback-depth score peaks at the configured ideal depth between the configured minimum useful and deep depths. Volume score rates lower volume / its moving average as better. Defense-response score combines trend-side close location, trend-side wick response, and close-back-to-trend score, weighted 38%, 30%, and 32%; close-back score is 100 when close is beyond the base EMA in the trend direction, 62 when close is inside the pocket, otherwise 34. Target-room score increases with target room up to the configured clean-room distance.
Long: bull trend AND price range touches the pocket between the base EMA plus configured ATR width and the slow EMA minus configured ATR width AND invalidation has not occurred AND risk-watch condition is false AND continuation score >= effective ready threshold AND defense-response score >= configured minimum AND target room >= the greater of 45% of configured clean-room distance or configured follow-through distance; OR a qualified bull continuation review reaches its frozen favorable target before its frozen invalidation within the configured proof window
Short: bear trend AND price range touches the pocket between the slow EMA plus configured ATR width and the base EMA minus configured ATR width AND invalidation has not occurred AND risk-watch condition is false AND continuation score >= effective ready threshold AND defense-response score >= configured minimum AND target room >= the greater of 45% of configured clean-room distance or configured follow-through distance; OR a qualified bear continuation review reaches its frozen favorable target before its frozen invalidation within the configured proof window
Exit: long, close < slow EMA − configured ATR invalidation buffer
Exit: short, close > slow EMA + configured ATR invalidation buffer
