Calculation: EMA alignment = the five chart-timeframe EMAs, ordered from shortest to longest lookback, are strictly increasing for bullish alignment or strictly decreasing for bearish alignment
Calculation: SMA trend = the shorter-lookback SMA is above the longer-lookback SMA for bullish trend, or below it for bearish trend
Calculation: Stochastic %K = stochastic of close within high-low, smoothed by a moving average
Calculation: RSI trigger = the short-lookback RSI crosses above its trigger threshold
Calculation: Bullish warning = a bearish candle whose body exceeds an ATR-based size threshold, or a downward gap exceeding an ATR-based threshold
Calculation: Bearish warning = a bullish candle whose body exceeds an ATR-based size threshold, or an upward gap exceeding an ATR-based threshold
Calculation: Near support/resistance = close is within a percentage threshold of any of the three shortest-lookback EMAs or the shorter-lookback SMA
Calculation: Touching longer-lookback SMA = high >= the longer-lookback SMA AND low <= the longer-lookback SMA
Long: bullish EMA alignment AND stochastic %K <= bullish retracement threshold AND ADX >= minimum threshold AND near support/resistance AND NOT bullish warning; OR touching longer-lookback SMA AND bullish SMA trend AND stochastic %K <= bullish retracement threshold AND NOT bullish warning; OR (bullish EMA alignment AND stochastic %K <= bullish retracement threshold AND ADX >= minimum threshold AND near support/resistance AND NOT bullish warning OR touching longer-lookback SMA AND bullish SMA trend AND stochastic %K <= bullish retracement threshold AND NOT bullish warning) AND (close crosses above the high of the most recent bar with bullish retracement OR RSI trigger)
Short: bearish EMA alignment AND stochastic %K >= bearish retracement threshold AND ADX >= minimum threshold AND near support/resistance AND NOT bearish warning; OR touching longer-lookback SMA AND bearish SMA trend AND stochastic %K >= bearish retracement threshold AND NOT bearish warning; OR (bearish EMA alignment AND stochastic %K >= bearish retracement threshold AND ADX >= minimum threshold AND near support/resistance AND NOT bearish warning OR touching longer-lookback SMA AND bearish SMA trend AND stochastic %K >= bearish retracement threshold AND NOT bearish warning) AND close crosses below the low of the most recent bar with bearish retracement
