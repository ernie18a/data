Calculation: Double top = two successive confirmed pivot highs within a configured relative tolerance; the second peak must be within a configured percentage distance of either the chart-timeframe 200-period EMA or the higher-timeframe 200-period EMA. The neckline is the lowest low between the peaks. Use the higher-timeframe EMA value available on the current chart bar (the forming higher-timeframe bar in real time; the latest completed value on historical bars).
Calculation: Candle range percentage = (high − low) / entry price × 100; largest wick ratio = the larger upper or lower wick divided by high − low.
Calculation: Initial short stop = entry plus the configured stop distance (selected by the configured points, ATR, or ticks mode); short target = entry minus the configured target distance (selected by the same mode), or entry minus configured risk-reward multiple × stop distance in test mode. Partial target = entry minus the configured fraction of the distance to the full target.
Calculation: For a short position, favorable excursion = entry price − bar low. At the configured break-even fraction of target distance, move the stop to entry. At the configured trailing fraction, trail the stop at the lower of its prior value and the shorter-period EMA. Sky-cap price = entry minus the configured percentage of entry.
Short: A double top is set up AND close < neckline AND close < chart-timeframe 200-period EMA (unless the trend filter is disabled) AND within the configured trading session, outside the session gap, on an allowed weekday, within the configured date range, below the daily trade cap, outside the cooldown, and consecutive losses < configured limit (unless that limiter is disabled) AND candle range percentage <= configured maximum (unless enabled filter is disabled) AND absolute distance from close to neckline / close × 100 <= configured maximum (unless enabled filter is disabled) AND close < open (unless enabled filter is disabled) AND ADX >= configured minimum (unless enabled filter is disabled) AND largest wick ratio <= configured maximum (unless enabled filter is disabled) AND initial stop distance / close × 100 <= 2.25%.
Exit: short, bar high >= active stop OR bar low <= partial target (close configured partial quantity, if enabled) OR bar low <= full target OR bar low <= sky-cap price OR current time is outside the configured trading session when session-end closing is enabled.
