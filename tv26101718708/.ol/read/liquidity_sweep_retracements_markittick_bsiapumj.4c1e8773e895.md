Calculation: Recent high = highest high of the preceding lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Recent low = lowest low of the preceding lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar
Long: low < Recent low AND close > Recent low AND close > open
Short: high > Recent high AND close < Recent high AND close < open
Long: close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar > 20-period SMA of closes on that higher timeframe
Short: close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar <= 20-period SMA of closes on that higher timeframe
