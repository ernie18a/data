Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot zones = On completed bars of a higher timeframe, find swing highs and lows that are respectively the highest highs and lowest lows across a symmetric pivot-strength window. Scan the rolling lookback; process newest pivots first. Merge each pivot into the first existing cluster whose center is within the clustering tolerance (a fraction of higher-timeframe ATR), updating the touch-count-weighted center and the zone's highest and lowest prices; otherwise start a cluster. Keep zones meeting the minimum touch count. Extend each zone to at least the center plus or minus 20% of the clustering tolerance.
Calculation: Key levels = Previous completed daily high, low, and close; previous completed weekly high and low; and the enabled daily simple moving averages, all from completed higher-timeframe bars. The daily moving averages use progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Nearest opposing level = Among qualifying zones and enabled key levels, the nearest level above the current close is resistance (use the zone's lower edge); the nearest level below the close is support (use the zone's upper edge). A close within any qualifying zone is inside a zone.
Calculation: Danger band = Higher-timeframe ATR multiplied by a proximity parameter; use ATR from the higher timeframe's last completed bar.
Calculation: Stop size = Either a fixed-points parameter or a multiple of ATR calculated on the chart timeframe.
Long: Close is outside every qualifying zone AND (no resistance exists OR (resistance minus close >= danger band AND (resistance minus close) / stop size >= minimum room in R))
Short: Close is outside every qualifying zone AND (no support exists OR (close minus support >= danger band AND (close minus support) / stop size >= minimum room in R))
