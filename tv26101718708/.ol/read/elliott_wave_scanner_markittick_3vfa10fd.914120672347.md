Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the specified number of bars on both sides; confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the specified number of bars on both sides. Use only after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Wave length = absolute price distance between consecutive pivots; retracement or extension ratio = retraced or extended distance divided by the preceding wave length.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; volume average = simple moving average of volume; market midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the market-structure lookback.
Long: confirmed pivots form, in chronological order, low P0, high P1, low P2, high P3, low P4, high P5 AND P1>P0 AND P2>P0 AND P3>P1 AND P2>P0 AND (P1-P2)/(P1-P0) is within 0.382–0.786 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance AND (P3-P4)/(P3-P2) is within 0.236–0.500 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance AND P4>P1 when the no-overlap rule is enabled AND P3-P2 is at least P1-P0 and P5-P4 when the not-shortest rule is enabled AND P3-P2>P1-P0 when the wave-3-extension rule is enabled AND each of P5-P4, P1-P0, and P3-P2 exceeds ATR times the minimum-size multiplier AND P5-P4/(P1-P0) is within 0.618–1.618 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance when the wave-5 ratio filter is enabled AND volume>volume average when volume confirmation is enabled AND close>market midpoint when market-structure filter is enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close>its 50-period simple moving average when higher-timeframe bias is enabled AND (P5+0.618*(P3-P2) - entry price)/(entry price - (P4 - ATR buffer)) meets the minimum risk/reward ratio, with entry price=P5.
Long: confirmed pivots form, in chronological order, low A, high B, low C AND C<=A AND C-wave length/A-wave length is within 0.618–1.618 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance AND A-wave length and C-wave length each exceed ATR times the minimum-size multiplier AND close>market midpoint when market-structure filter is enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close>its 50-period simple moving average when higher-timeframe bias is enabled AND (C+1.618*A-wave length - C)/(C - (C - ATR buffer)) meets the minimum risk/reward ratio, with entry price=C.
Short: confirmed pivots form, in chronological order, high P0, low P1, high P2, low P3, high P4, low P5 AND P1<P0 AND P2<P0 AND P3<P1 AND P2<P0 AND (P2-P1)/(P0-P1) is within 0.382–0.786 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance AND (P4-P3)/(P2-P3) is within 0.236–0.500 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance AND P4<P1 when the no-overlap rule is enabled AND P2-P3 is at least P0-P1 and P4-P5 when the not-shortest rule is enabled AND P2-P3>P0-P1 when the wave-3-extension rule is enabled AND each of P4-P5, P0-P1, and P2-P3 exceeds ATR times the minimum-size multiplier AND (P4-P5)/(P0-P1) is within 0.618–1.618 adjusted by Fibonacci tolerance when the wave-5 ratio filter is enabled AND volume>volume average when volume confirmation is enabled AND close<market midpoint when market-structure filter is enabled AND last completed higher-timeframe close<its 50-period simple moving average when higher-timeframe bias is enabled AND (entry price-(P5-0.618*(P2-P3)))/(P4+ATR buffer-entry price) meets the minimum risk/reward ratio, with entry price=P5.
