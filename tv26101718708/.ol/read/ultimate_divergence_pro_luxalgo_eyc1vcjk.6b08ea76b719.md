Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close
Calculation: RSI pivot low = a local low in RSI confirmed after the configured number of bars to its right; RSI pivot high = a local high confirmed the same way
Long: Two consecutive RSI pivot lows are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation AND the newer pivot-bar close > EMA of close AND the newer pivot-bar volume > short-term SMA of volume AND ((newer pivot-bar low < prior pivot-bar low AND newer pivot RSI > prior pivot RSI AND (newer pivot RSI <= oversold threshold OR prior pivot RSI <= oversold threshold)) OR (newer pivot-bar low > prior pivot-bar low AND newer pivot RSI < prior pivot RSI) OR (absolute difference between newer and prior pivot-bar lows <= 0.15 × ATR on the newer pivot bar AND newer pivot RSI > prior pivot RSI))
Short: Two consecutive RSI pivot highs are within the configured minimum and maximum bar separation AND the newer pivot-bar close < EMA of close AND the newer pivot-bar volume > short-term SMA of volume AND ((newer pivot-bar high > prior pivot-bar high AND newer pivot RSI < prior pivot RSI AND (newer pivot RSI >= overbought threshold OR prior pivot RSI >= overbought threshold)) OR (newer pivot-bar high < prior pivot-bar high AND newer pivot RSI > prior pivot RSI) OR (absolute difference between newer and prior pivot-bar highs <= 0.15 × ATR on the newer pivot bar AND newer pivot RSI < prior pivot RSI))
