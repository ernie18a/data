Calculation: Signed lower-timeframe volume = +volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, +volume when close = open and close > the prior lower-timeframe close, −volume when close = open and close < the prior lower-timeframe close; otherwise retain the prior lower-timeframe buy/sell classification. Uses lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the forming lower-timeframe bar in real time.
Calculation: Per-price-row buy/sell volume = sum of each lower-timeframe bar’s classified buy/sell volume multiplied by the fraction of its high-low range overlapping that row; rows span the chart bar’s low to high.
Calculation: Anchored cumulative volume delta = running sum of signed lower-timeframe volume, reset to zero at each anchor-period change. Uses lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, including the forming lower-timeframe bar in real time.
Long: Anchored cumulative volume delta close > its open OR per-price-row buy volume > 0 AND row is not the lowest row AND row buy volume >= absolute sell volume of the immediately lower row × imbalance ratio
Short: Anchored cumulative volume delta close <= its open OR per-price-row sell volume < 0 AND an immediately higher row exists AND absolute row sell volume >= buy volume of the immediately higher row × imbalance ratio
