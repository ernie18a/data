Calculation: Volume delta = volume × (2 × close − high − low) / (high − low) on the higher-timeframe bar; use 0 when high = low. Use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Delta HMA = WMA(2 × WMA(volume delta, half the HMA lookback) − WMA(volume delta, full HMA lookback), square root of the HMA lookback) on higher-timeframe bars; use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Average close-location percentage = simple moving average of (close − low) / (high − low) × 100 over the volume-ratio lookback on higher-timeframe bars; use 50 when high = low. Use the bar still forming.
Long: (volume delta > 0 AND current delta HMA > previous higher-timeframe bar's delta HMA) OR (volume delta > 0 AND close > close from the volume-ratio lookback bars ago AND average close-location percentage > 60) OR (volume delta > 0 AND close < close from the volume-ratio lookback bars ago AND average close-location percentage > 60)
Short: (volume delta < 0 AND current delta HMA < previous higher-timeframe bar's delta HMA) OR (volume delta < 0 AND close < close from the volume-ratio lookback bars ago AND average close-location percentage < 40) OR (volume delta < 0 AND close > close from the volume-ratio lookback bars ago AND average close-location percentage < 40)
