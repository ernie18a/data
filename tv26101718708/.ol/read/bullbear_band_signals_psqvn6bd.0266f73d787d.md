Calculation: Selected source = selected OHLC input (open, high, low, close, HL2, HLC3, or OHLC4)
Calculation: Upper band = higher of the selected-source SMA and EMA; the SMA lookback is shorter than the EMA lookback
Calculation: Lower band = lower of the selected-source SMA and EMA; the SMA lookback is shorter than the EMA lookback
Long: Selected source > upper band AND previous bar's selected source <= previous bar's upper band
Short: Selected source < lower band AND previous bar's selected source >= previous bar's lower band
