Calculation: Trifecta bullish = RSI is above 50 AND MACD line is above zero AND Chaikin Money Flow is above zero; bearish = all three are below their respective thresholds.
Calculation: MT-Stoch score = sum of five bullish votes minus five bearish votes: three stochastic K/D comparisons using long-, medium-, and short-lookback settings; a smoothed stochastic RSI value above 20 (bearish vote below 80); and the 100-bar percentile rank of the stochastic RSI K/D ratio at least 50 (bearish vote below 50). Each comparison contributes one vote.
Calculation: MCU score = bullish points minus bearish points across three groups. Structure group: one point for close above the cloud formed from conversion/base midpoints and the long-lookback midpoint, with both cloud values taken from 30 bars earlier; one for conversion line above base line; one for close above close 30 bars earlier. Reverse each comparison for bearish points. Volatility group: one point for ATR as a percentage of close divided by its longer-lookback average above its threshold, when close is above the previous close; one for that ratio above a higher threshold when close is above the previous close; one for close above its EMA. Reverse the price comparisons for bearish points. Squeeze/momentum group: one point for linear-regression momentum above zero; one if it is above zero and rising; one if it is above zero and a squeeze ended within the recent lookback. Reverse for bearish points, using below zero and falling. The conversion lookback is shorter than the base lookback, which is shorter than the cloud's long lookback; the volatility ratio uses a shorter ATR lookback than its averaging lookback.
Calculation: MA/ADX bullish state = short-lookback SMA above long-lookback SMA AND ADX above its threshold; bearish state reverses the SMA comparison. MA triggers are the corresponding SMA crossovers, with ADX above its threshold and RSI below its upper bound for long, above its lower bound for short.
Long: MA/ADX bullish crossover trigger OR first bar Trifecta becomes bullish OR first bar MT-Stoch score reaches its positive threshold OR first bar MCU score reaches its positive threshold; AND every enabled filter is bullish (Trifecta, MT-Stoch, MCU, or MA/ADX state, as selected).
Short: MA/ADX bearish crossover trigger OR first bar Trifecta becomes bearish OR first bar MT-Stoch score reaches its negative threshold OR first bar MCU score reaches its negative threshold; AND every enabled filter is bearish (Trifecta, MT-Stoch, MCU, or MA/ADX state, as selected).
Exit: Long, opposing short trigger occurs OR bars since entry reach the configured holding period OR low reaches the entry price minus the configured ATR stop multiple times ATR OR high reaches the entry price plus the configured ATR target multiple times ATR OR RSI crosses above its upper extreme OR datetime is outside the configured date range.
Exit: Short, opposing long trigger occurs OR bars since entry reach the configured holding period OR high reaches the entry price plus the configured ATR stop multiple times ATR OR low reaches the entry price minus the configured ATR target multiple times ATR OR RSI crosses below its lower extreme OR datetime is outside the configured date range.
