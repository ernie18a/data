Calculation: ATR band stack = Four price trails at proportional multiples of ATR; each trail ratchets upward only during an uptrend and downward only during a downtrend, and reseeds on a trend flip. The selected flip trail is Band 2, 3, or 4.
Calculation: Trend flip = In an uptrend, source closes below the selected trail's previous-bar value; in a downtrend, source closes above it.
Calculation: Retest state = During an uptrend, a low touching a band arms a long retest for the retest window; during a downtrend, a high touching a band arms a short retest. Keep the greatest depth touched (Band 1–4) while armed; clear on expiry or trend flip.
Calculation: Retest score = Depth points (Band 1: 15, Band 2: 25, Band 3: 18, Band 4: 10) + candle points (long close location (close−low)/(high−low), short mirrored: >0.7 gives 20, >0.5 gives 12, otherwise 5) + volume points (volume > 1.2 × previous-bar 20-period volume SMA gives 20, > SMA gives 12, otherwise 5; use 12 if volume is unavailable) + higher-timeframe bias points (20 when aligned with the previous completed higher-timeframe close relative to its EMA-50, 10 when unavailable, otherwise 0) + trend-age points (15 at 10–150 bars since flip, 8 below 10, otherwise 5).
Calculation: Risk levels = At entry, use close as entry. Stop distance is the greater of signal-bar wick-to-close distance plus 0.25 × risk ATR and 0.5 × risk ATR (long stop below the low; short stop above the high); alternatively use the configured ATR multiple. Targets 1–3 are entry plus (long) or minus (short) stop distance multiplied by their respective risk multiples. After Target 1 is touched, move stop to entry if break-even is enabled.
Long: Confirmed bullish trend flip after warm-up OR (uptrend, armed long retest, close > Band 1, close > open, retest score ≥ minimum, and retest cooldown elapsed).
Short: Confirmed bearish trend flip after warm-up OR (downtrend, armed short retest, close < Band 1, close < open, retest score ≥ minimum, and retest cooldown elapsed).
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ Target 3 OR a confirmed short signal; if a bar touches both stop and target, stop takes precedence. After Target 1, the active stop is entry when break-even is enabled.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ Target 3 OR a confirmed long signal; if a bar touches both stop and target, stop takes precedence. After Target 1, the active stop is entry when break-even is enabled.
