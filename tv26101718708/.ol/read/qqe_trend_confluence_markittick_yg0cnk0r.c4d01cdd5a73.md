Calculation: QQE pair = calculate RSI from the selected price source (optionally pre-smoothed by the selected filter), smooth RSI with an EMA, then form a trailing level by multiplying a double EMA of absolute bar-to-bar changes in smoothed RSI by a factor and ratcheting the level in the RSI's direction.
Calculation: ADX = Wilder's Average Directional Index.
Calculation: Trade levels = use the prior chart bar's close as entry; risk distance is current chart bar ATR(14) times a selected multiple; place the stop one risk distance against the position and targets at selected risk:reward multiples in its direction.
Long: Primary QQE smoothed RSI crosses above its trailing level on the last completed chart bar AND (secondary QQE confirmation disabled OR its smoothed RSI is above its trailing level on the last completed chart bar; use a longer RSI EMA smoothing than the primary) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX on the last completed chart bar is at least its threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR the QQE smoothed RSI is above its trailing level on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Short: Primary QQE smoothed RSI crosses below its trailing level on the last completed chart bar AND (secondary QQE confirmation disabled OR its smoothed RSI is below its trailing level on the last completed chart bar; use a longer RSI EMA smoothing than the primary) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX on the last completed chart bar is at least its threshold) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR the QQE smoothed RSI is below its trailing level on the last completed higher-timeframe bar).
Exit: long, Short condition OR (trade levels enabled AND high reaches or exceeds any active long target OR low reaches or falls below the active long stop).
Exit: short, Long condition OR (trade levels enabled AND low reaches or falls below any active short target OR high reaches or exceeds the active short stop).
