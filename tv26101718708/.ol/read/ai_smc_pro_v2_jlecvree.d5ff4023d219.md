Calculation: Bullish trend = short-, medium-, and long-lookback EMAs are in descending lookback order by value, and close is above the medium-lookback EMA; bearish trend is the reverse.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order flow = smooth the buy/sell pressure derived from close location within the bar, lower/upper wick share, candle direction and size, and a volume-above-average multiplier; bullish requires buy pressure above its threshold, positive estimated delta above its threshold, and positive smoothed delta; bearish is the inverse. Sell pressure = 100 − buy pressure; estimated delta = buy pressure − sell pressure.
Calculation: Normal volatility = ATR divided by close is between the high- and low-volatility cutoffs.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish swing break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low. A bullish/bearish CHoCH marking also occurs when the latest two pivot highs form a higher high / latest two pivot lows form a lower low and close is beyond the latest pivot. Pivots require bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block marking = the preceding bar is bearish/bullish, current close breaks above its high/below its low, current candle is directionally strong relative to short-lookback ATR, and volume exceeds its average. The zone remains active until a close breaks its far boundary.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair-value-gap marking = current low is above the high two bars earlier / current high is below the low two bars earlier, and the gap exceeds an ATR-based cutoff. Remove the zone when close fills it.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a lookback; premium means close above it, discount means close below it.
Calculation: MTF bias = majority direction across a chart-timeframe close versus its EMA and two higher-timeframe close-versus-EMA checks; the middle higher-timeframe check also requires RSI above/below its midpoint. Use the latest returned bar for each timeframe with lookahead off; higher-timeframe values may use a still-forming bar.
Calculation: AI confidence = normalized weighted score of trend, momentum, MACD, volume, structure, order flow, MTF agreement, session, and premium/discount alignment. Momentum scores RSI ranges; MACD scores line/signal and histogram direction or histogram contraction; volume scores volume versus its average and spike level; structure scores a current break, existing directional structure, or neutral; MTF scores directional agreement; session scores London/New York, Asia, or other; zone alignment scores discount with bullish trend or premium with bearish trend. Weights, score cutoffs, and mode thresholds are parameters.
Calculation: Long/short stop distance = ATR × stop multiplier. Target distance = stop distance × minimum reward/risk parameter. Optional trailing stop follows close by the stop distance; optional breakeven moves stop to entry after price advances one initial stop distance.
