Calculation: Confirmed pivot highs/lows = swing highs/lows requiring configurable bars on both sides; available only after the right-side bars close.
Calculation: Valid support/resistance trendline = line through two confirmed pivot lows/highs, respectively, rising/falling, meeting configurable minimum span and slope bounds (slope measured absolutely or relative to ATR); between anchors, no more than the allowed number of candle bodies cross the line beyond ATR tolerance; at least two confirmed pivots touch within ATR/tick tolerance, separated by the minimum spacing.
Calculation: Active trendline = valid line not invalidated by the configured break rule (a confirmed price basis crosses the line by more than the greater of the ATR buffer and minimum tick buffer; optionally retain it until price reclaims the original side); rank eligible lines by touch count, then recency of last touch, then span, and select the best and second-best. Chart lines use chart-timeframe bars; optional higher-timeframe lines use only the last completed higher-timeframe bars and select the best line.
Long: an active rising support trendline exists (primary or second-best chart line, or primary optional higher-timeframe line)
Short: an active falling resistance trendline exists (primary or second-best chart line, or primary optional higher-timeframe line)
