Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average of the selected price source, reset at the selected session, day, or week boundary
Calculation: Upper band = VWAP + standard deviation of the selected price source over the lookback × multiplier
Calculation: Lower band = VWAP − standard deviation of the selected price source over the lookback × multiplier
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the lookback
Calculation: SMMA confirmation line = EMA of close
Calculation: MACD = difference between a shorter-lookback and longer-lookback EMA; signal line = EMA of MACD; histogram = MACD − signal line
Long: (configured entry is Price Over VWAP AND close crosses above VWAP OR configured entry is Price Over Upper Band AND close crosses above upper band OR configured entry is Price Under Upper Band AND close crosses below upper band) AND entry time is within the configured session AND weekday is enabled AND daily limit is not reached AND daily trade limit is not reached AND position size is nonnegative and below the pyramiding limit AND (long-only or both directions enabled) AND (confirmation mode is AND and every enabled confirmation is true OR confirmation mode is OR and at least one enabled confirmation is true OR no confirmations are enabled); long confirmations are RSI below its long threshold, close above the EMA confirmation line, and MACD above signal line with positive histogram
Short: (configured entry is Price Under VWAP AND close crosses below VWAP OR configured entry is Price Under Lower Band AND close crosses below lower band OR configured entry is Price Over Lower Band AND close crosses above lower band) AND entry time is within the configured session AND weekday is enabled AND daily limit is not reached AND daily trade limit is not reached AND position size is nonpositive and absolute position size is below the pyramiding limit AND (short-only or both directions enabled) AND (confirmation mode is AND and every enabled confirmation is true OR confirmation mode is OR and at least one enabled confirmation is true OR no confirmations are enabled); short confirmations are RSI above its short threshold, close below the EMA confirmation line, and MACD below signal line with negative histogram
Exit: Long, close <= average entry price − fixed stop ticks × minimum tick OR close >= average entry price + fixed target ticks × minimum tick OR close <= average entry price − ATR × multiplier OR close >= average entry price + ATR × multiplier OR for capped ATR, close <= the higher of (average entry price − ATR × multiplier) and (average entry price × (1 − cap percentage)) OR close >= the lower of (average entry price + ATR × multiplier) and (average entry price × (1 + cap percentage)) OR close crosses below VWAP OR close crosses below upper band OR close crosses above lower band OR the trailing stop is hit after price advances by the configured trigger from entry, with the stop trailing the favorable extreme by the configured distance OR daily PnL reaches its take-profit or stop-loss limit OR bar close time matches the configured close-all time
Exit: Short, close >= average entry price + fixed stop ticks × minimum tick OR close <= average entry price − fixed target ticks × minimum tick OR close >= average entry price + ATR × multiplier OR close <= average entry price − ATR × multiplier OR for capped ATR, close >= the lower of (average entry price + ATR × multiplier) and (average entry price × (1 + cap percentage)) OR close <= the higher of (average entry price − ATR × multiplier) and (average entry price × (1 − cap percentage)) OR close crosses above VWAP OR close crosses below upper band OR close crosses above lower band OR the trailing stop is hit after price advances by the configured trigger from entry, with the stop trailing the favorable extreme by the configured distance OR daily PnL reaches its take-profit or stop-loss limit OR bar close time matches the configured close-all time
