Calculation: Lunar momentum = sin(2π × fraction of the synodic month elapsed since a reference new moon); long lunar score = max(lunar momentum, 0) × 100; short lunar score = max(−lunar momentum, 0) × 100
Calculation: Bull trend = close > long-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA > its prior value; bear trend = close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA < its prior value. The three EMA lookbacks are ordered short, medium, long.
Calculation: Bull participation = relative volume above its threshold AND close > open AND (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ 0.60 AND |close − open| ≥ 0.22 × ATR; bear participation = relative volume above its threshold AND close < open AND (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) ≥ 0.60 AND |close − open| ≥ 0.22 × ATR. Relative volume = volume / max(SMA(volume), 1).
Calculation: Bull momentum score = weighted sum of: bull trend, close > medium-term EMA, RSI ≥ bull RSI threshold, RSI > bull RSI threshold + 4, MACD histogram > 0 and ≥ its prior value, close > session VWAP, bull participation, and (HTF filter disabled OR bullish HTF filter). Bear momentum score uses the corresponding bearish conditions: bear trend, close < medium-term EMA, RSI ≤ bear RSI threshold, RSI < bear RSI threshold − 4, MACD histogram < 0 and ≤ its prior value, close < session VWAP, bear participation, and (HTF filter disabled OR bearish HTF filter). Weights in that order: 24, 8, 16, 5, 16, 10, 11, 10; clamp each score to 0–100.
Calculation: Bullish HTF filter = last completed higher-timeframe close > its long-term EMA AND its short-term EMA > its medium-term EMA; bearish HTF filter = last completed higher-timeframe close < its long-term EMA AND its short-term EMA < its medium-term EMA. Use the same respective EMA lookbacks as on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long trigger = (close > highest high over the prior breakout lookback bars AND close > open) OR (lowest low over the pullback lookback bars ≤ short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > open AND close > prior close). Short trigger = (close < lowest low over the prior breakout lookback bars AND close < open) OR (highest high over the pullback lookback bars ≥ short-term EMA AND close < short-term EMA AND close < open AND close < prior close).
Calculation: Long confluence = clamp(0.74 × bull momentum score + 0.26 × long lunar score + 5 when a new moon or first-quarter phase begins while lunar momentum > 0, otherwise no bonus, 0, 100). Short confluence = clamp(0.74 × bear momentum score + 0.26 × short lunar score + 5 when a full-moon or last-quarter phase begins while lunar momentum < 0, otherwise no bonus, 0, 100). Lunar phase is divided into eight equal buckets; a phase begins when the bucket changes.
Long: Chart bar is confirmed AND long direction is enabled AND no long signal occurred within the cooldown lookback AND lunar momentum > its positive bias threshold AND bull momentum score ≥ its minimum AND long confluence ≥ its minimum AND long trigger AND (HTF filter disabled OR bullish HTF filter).
Short: Chart bar is confirmed AND short direction is enabled AND no short signal occurred within the cooldown lookback AND lunar momentum < the negative bias threshold AND bear momentum score ≥ its minimum AND short confluence ≥ its minimum AND short trigger AND (HTF filter disabled OR bearish HTF filter).
Calculation: At long entry, save stop = lowest low over the swing lookback − ATR × stop buffer and target 2 = entry price + (entry price − stop) × target 2 risk multiple. At short entry, save stop = highest high over the swing lookback + ATR × stop buffer and target 2 = entry price − (stop − entry price) × target 2 risk multiple. Keep these levels fixed until exit.
Exit: Long, low ≤ saved stop OR high ≥ saved target 2 OR short signal.
Exit: Short, high ≥ saved stop OR low ≤ saved target 2 OR long signal.
