Calculation: Candle body = |close − open|; upper wick = high − max(open, close); lower wick = min(open, close) − low; range = high − low.
Calculation: Hammer = body ≤ body-size fraction × range AND lower wick ≥ dominant-wick/body multiple × max(body, minimum-body floor) AND lower wick ≥ dominant-wick/range fraction × range AND upper wick ≤ opposite-wick/body multiple × max(body, minimum-body floor).
Calculation: Shooting star/inverted hammer = Hammer with upper and lower wick roles reversed.
Calculation: Spinning top = body ≤ body-size fraction × range AND each wick ≥ minimum-wick/body multiple × max(body, minimum-body floor).
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND previous close < previous open AND close ≥ previous open AND open ≤ previous close.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close ≤ previous open AND open ≥ previous close.
Calculation: Entry level = reversal candle high for long or low for short, or preceding candle high for long or low for short, according to the selected reference.
Calculation: Trend EMA = EMA(close, trend lookback); falling/rising EMA context compares it with its value at the older slope-lookback bar.
Calculation: Swing extreme = lowest low / highest high over the swing lookback, including the current bar.
Calculation: Breakout structure = highest high / lowest low over the structure lookback, shifted back by the excluded-recent-bars count.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = fast EMA compared with slow EMA on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar; fast lookback is shorter than slow lookback.
Calculation: Stretch distance = distance from the selected SMA or EMA to price, measured in ATR units; ATR uses the last completed daily bar, a higher timeframe than the chart.
Long: At bar close, bullish close is required if enabled AND (enabled Hammer OR enabled spinning top OR enabled bullish engulfing) AND not also a qualified short reversal AND the selected context is downtrend (close < trend EMA AND trend EMA is below its older value), fresh swing low (low ≤ swing extreme), either, or off AND (if enabled) higher-timeframe trend agrees with trade (fast EMA > slow EMA) or reversal (fast EMA < slow EMA) AND (if enabled) entry level > breakout structure AND (if enabled) RSI ≤ oversold threshold AND (if enabled) close ≤ selected moving average − minimum stretch × daily ATR AND entry/time/session/trade-limit/cooldown gates pass AND no active position or pending order when one-at-a-time is enabled AND no active post-win no-chase block; arm a buy stop at the entry level. Enter long when a subsequent bar reaches that level (high ≥ entry level; resolve using chronological completed lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart OHLC).
Short: At bar close, bearish close is required if enabled AND (enabled shooting star/inverted hammer OR enabled spinning top OR enabled bearish engulfing) AND not also a qualified long reversal AND the selected context is uptrend (close > trend EMA AND trend EMA is above its older value), fresh swing high (high ≥ swing extreme), either, or off AND (if enabled) higher-timeframe trend agrees with trade (fast EMA < slow EMA) or reversal (fast EMA > slow EMA) AND (if enabled) entry level < breakout structure AND (if enabled) RSI ≥ overbought threshold AND (if enabled) close ≥ selected moving average + minimum stretch × daily ATR AND entry/time/session/trade-limit/cooldown gates pass AND no active position or pending order when one-at-a-time is enabled AND no active post-win no-chase block; arm a sell stop at the entry level. Enter short when a subsequent bar reaches that level (low ≤ entry level; resolve using chronological completed lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart OHLC).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ target OR (enabled force-flat window is active) OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars; resolve stop/target hits in chronological completed lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart OHLC, with configurable stop-first or target-first handling if both are touched in one sub-bar.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ target OR (enabled force-flat window is active) OR bars held ≥ maximum hold bars; resolve stop/target hits in chronological completed lower-timeframe bars when available, otherwise chart OHLC, with configurable stop-first or target-first handling if both are touched in one sub-bar.
