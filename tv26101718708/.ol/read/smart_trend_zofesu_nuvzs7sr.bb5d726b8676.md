Calculation: ATR trend direction and trailing line = Initialize bullish with the upper band (high − ATR × volatility multiplier). While bullish, if close < prior trailing line and (volume confirmation is disabled or volume percentile rank > its threshold), switch bearish and set the line to the lower band (low + ATR × volatility multiplier); otherwise keep bullish and set the line to max(prior line, upper band). While bearish, if close > prior trailing line and (volume confirmation is disabled or volume percentile rank > its threshold), switch bullish and set the line to the upper band; otherwise keep bearish and set the line to min(prior line, lower band).
Long: prior ATR trend direction is bearish AND current ATR trend direction is bullish AND (main trend filter is disabled OR close > HMA of close)
Short: prior ATR trend direction is bullish AND current ATR trend direction is bearish AND (main trend filter is disabled OR close < HMA of close)
