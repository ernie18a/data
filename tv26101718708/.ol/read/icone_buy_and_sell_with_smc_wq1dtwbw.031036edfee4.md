Calculation: EMA = exponential moving average of close over a lookback.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Candle body ratio = abs(close - open) / max(high - low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over a lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Valid compression structure = contracting structure range AND contracting ATR AND structure-range-to-ATR ratio within the configured bounds AND (converging highs and lows OR either converging highs or rising lows).
Calculation: Setup score = capped sum of trend points (price side of long-term EMA, short-term EMA slope, long-term EMA slope), impulse points (directional move from the lookback extreme relative to ATR, plus path efficiency), compression points (range contraction, ATR contraction, and range width), structure points (converging highs/lows and valid compression structure), breakout points (raw breakout, strong directional candle, and extra breakout distance), momentum points (directional RSI and volume filters), and liquidity/context points (higher-timeframe trend, structure sweep, discount/premium, and proximity to range midpoint). Use direction-specific conditions and configured weights.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = prior close above/below the EMA on a higher timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Daily trend = prior daily close above/below its EMA; use the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Multi-timeframe bullish count = number of five fixed aggregation intervals whose last completed close is above its EMA; use the last completed bar of each interval.
Calculation: Impulse = close is at least the configured ATR multiple above the lookback low and above the short-term EMA (bullish), or the lookback high is at least that ATR multiple above close and close is below the short-term EMA (bearish).
Calculation: Strong bullish candle = bullish candle, body ratio meets its configured minimum, and close is near the bar high. Strong bearish candle = bearish candle, body ratio meets its configured minimum, and close is near the bar low.
Calculation: Raw bullish breakout = prior bar had a valid compression structure AND close crosses above structure high plus an ATR buffer. Raw bearish breakout = prior bar had a valid compression structure AND close crosses below structure low minus an ATR buffer.
Calculation: Bullish fakeout = prior bar had a valid compression structure AND current high exceeds structure high by a minimum ATR fraction AND close is below structure high AND candle is bearish. Bearish fakeout = prior bar had a valid compression structure AND current low falls below structure low by a minimum ATR fraction AND close is above structure low AND candle is bullish. Apply a configured cooldown between fakeout markings.
Calculation: Bullish trend resumption = a low touched the short-term EMA within the configured recent-bar window, then close is above that EMA, candle is bullish, and close exceeds prior high. Bearish trend resumption = a high touched the short-term EMA within that window, then close is below that EMA, candle is bearish, and close is below prior low.
Calculation: Approved breakout = confirmed bar AND raw breakout in that direction AND direction permitted AND corresponding setup score meets its threshold AND (not strict-filter mode OR directional trend, impulse, volume, RSI, candle, and higher-timeframe filters all pass).
Calculation: Approved retest = within the configured bars after an approved breakout, price touches its breakout level within ATR tolerance without exceeding the maximum adverse ATR distance, then closes beyond that level in the breakout direction with a matching candle; require direction permitted and, in strict-filter mode, matching trend and higher-timeframe filters. Cancel the pending retest after expiry or a close beyond the level by the configured failure distance.
Calculation: Sweep-long = bearish fakeout with long direction permitted and (trend alignment disabled OR regime is not bearish). Sweep-short = bullish fakeout with short direction permitted and (trend alignment disabled OR regime is not bullish). Regime is bullish when close is above the long-term EMA and higher-timeframe trend is bullish, bearish when both are bearish, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Long protective stop = selected ATR stop, structure stop, or the lower of both; ATR stop is close minus configured ATR multiple, structure stop is the lower of recent lookback low and pending breakout level (or structure low) minus an ATR buffer. Short protective stop = selected ATR stop, structure stop, or the higher of both; ATR stop is close plus configured ATR multiple, structure stop is the higher of recent lookback high and pending breakout level (or structure high) plus an ATR buffer.
Calculation: Trade targets = entry price plus/minus initial risk distance multiplied by configured target multiples, in position direction. After target one, optionally move stop to at least entry plus/minus an ATR buffer; after target two, optionally trail at close minus/plus an ATR multiple.
Long: approved long breakout OR approved long retest OR bullish trend resumption OR sweep-long; for the actual strategy entry also require timing/session/history/cooldown/daily trade-count gates, macro gate if enabled, daily bullish gate if enabled (sweep-long bypasses it), precision headroom gate if enabled, flat position (or permitted flip from short), and positive size.
Short: approved short breakout OR approved short retest OR bearish trend resumption OR sweep-short; for the actual strategy entry also require timing/session/history/cooldown/daily trade-count gates, macro gate if enabled, daily bearish gate if enabled (sweep-short bypasses it), precision headroom gate if enabled, flat position (or permitted flip from long), and positive size.
Long: raw bullish breakout when approved; approved bullish retest; bullish trend resumption; bearish fakeout when its cooldown permits (marked as a long reversal); sweep-long.
Short: raw bearish breakout when approved; approved bearish retest; bearish trend resumption; bullish fakeout when its cooldown permits (marked as a short reversal); sweep-short.
Exit: Long, low <= current protective stop OR high >= target one OR high >= target two OR high >= target three OR high >= target four; reduce configured portions at targets one through three and close the remainder at target four or stop.
Exit: Short, high >= current protective stop OR low <= target one OR low <= target two OR low <= target three OR low <= target four; reduce configured portions at targets one through three and close the remainder at target four or stop.
Exit: Long or Short, live guide escalates on each newly occurring warning (opposing fakeout, close crossing the short-term EMA against position, declining volume on a counter-direction candle while profitable, opposing candle-volume imbalance, nearby structure/key/liquidity level while profitable, or macro score turning against position): advise selling 25%, then 75%, then closing all; do not escalate ordinary warnings before target one for automatic trades when that guard is enabled. A hard close beyond the opposing structure boundary advises closing all immediately; sufficiently adverse macro score advises selling 75% immediately.
