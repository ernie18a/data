Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low with configurable numbers of lower highs/lows on its left and right; use only after the right-side source bars have closed and only within the same context segment.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on the source timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish manipulation candidate = after price sweeps below the latest unused confirmed swing low by at least the minimum penetration, pair the sweep low (B) with the latest eligible confirmed swing high before it (A). Update B on each lower low; replace A with the latest eligible confirmed swing high strictly between A and B. Reject if A is not above the swept low, the sweep is no longer between A and B by the minimum penetration, A is too far from B, or B−A is less than the larger of the minimum tick range and the configured ATR multiple. Expire if confirmation does not occur within the configured wait.
Calculation: Bearish manipulation candidate = mirror the bullish candidate: sweep above the latest unused confirmed swing high, pair the sweep high (B) with the latest eligible confirmed swing low before it (A), update B on each higher high and replace A with the latest eligible confirmed swing low strictly between A and B. Apply the mirrored ordering, distance, size, and expiry checks.
Calculation: Displacement candle = a closed source candle whose body points in the candidate direction, whose body is at least the configured ATR multiple, and whose body/range is at least the configured fraction.
Calculation: Source timeframe = each independent engine's native timeframe; use closed bars only. Relative to the chart timeframe, use higher-timeframe bars when the engine timeframe is higher, lower-timeframe bars when it is lower, and chart bars when equal.
Long: A bullish manipulation candidate is valid AND a later closed source bar closes above A by at least the configured break buffer AND closes back above the swept swing low AND is a displacement candle.
Short: A bearish manipulation candidate is valid AND a later closed source bar closes below A by at least the configured break buffer AND closes back below the swept swing high AND is a displacement candle.
