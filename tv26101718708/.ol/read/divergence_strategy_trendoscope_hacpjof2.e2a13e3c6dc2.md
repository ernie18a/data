Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = price makes a lower low while the oscillator makes a higher low; regular bearish divergence = price makes a higher high while the oscillator makes a lower high.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = price makes a higher low while the oscillator makes a lower low; hidden bearish divergence = price makes a lower high while the oscillator makes a higher high.
Calculation: Valid divergence = a divergence between the latest and third latest alternating zigzag pivots, with no intervening price or oscillator values beyond their respective pivot-to-pivot lines, and with the selected trend filter met (zigzag pivot trend, moving-average change between pivots, or external trend signal).
Calculation: Entry price = for regular divergences, the middle pivot in cautious or mixed mode and the third latest pivot in confident mode; for hidden divergences, the middle pivot.
Calculation: Stop price = for regular divergences, the third latest pivot in cautious mode or when using a market order, and the latest pivot otherwise; for hidden divergences, the latest pivot in cautious mode when not using a market order, and the third latest pivot otherwise.
Calculation: Target price = entry price plus the risk/reward multiple times the distance from entry to stop for longs, or minus that amount for shorts.
Long: Valid bullish regular or hidden divergence AND (market order at the signal close OR bar high reaches the entry price)
Short: Valid bearish regular or hidden divergence AND (market order at the signal close OR bar low reaches the entry price)
Exit: Long, bar low <= stop price OR bar high >= target price OR close < the divergence endpoint
Exit: Short, bar high >= stop price OR bar low <= target price OR close > the divergence endpoint
