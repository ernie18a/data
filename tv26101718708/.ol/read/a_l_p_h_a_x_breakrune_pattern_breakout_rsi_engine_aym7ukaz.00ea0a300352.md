Calculation: Pattern boundaries = From the two latest confirmed pivot highs and lows, qualify a pattern only when pattern height (highest of the two highs minus lowest of the two lows) is at least a configurable ATR multiple and the latest contributing high and low pivots are each within a configurable age limit. Classify as ascending triangle when highs are flat within an ATR tolerance and lows rise beyond an ATR tolerance (bullish bias); descending triangle when lows are flat and highs fall beyond tolerance (bearish bias); range when both are flat (neutral); bull channel when highs and lows both rise beyond tolerance (bullish bias); bear channel when both fall beyond tolerance (bearish bias). Resistance and support are the respective higher and lower pivots for triangles/ranges, and the latest high and low for channels.
Calculation: Pending break = On a qualifying pattern, a raw long break occurs when close crosses above resistance plus an ATR-based buffer from at or below it, provided pattern bias is non-bearish; a raw short break occurs when close crosses below support minus the buffer from at or above it, provided pattern bias is non-bullish. Store direction, broken boundary, and break bar. Within a configurable window, classify a long break as fake when close is below its stored boundary and upper wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) meets a configurable threshold; classify a short break as fake when close is above its boundary and lower wick / max(high − low, minimum price increment) meets the threshold. Classify as true when the required consecutive closes are all beyond the stored boundary and no fake has been classified. Expire unresolved pending breaks after the window.
Calculation: Fast RSI and Core RSI = RSI of close using separate periods, with Fast RSI using the shorter period. RSI slopes = each RSI minus its value at a configurable number of bars earlier.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = Fast EMA above Slow EMA and close above Fast EMA for bullish trend; Fast EMA below Slow EMA and close below Fast EMA for bearish trend. Use a higher timeframe; historical calculations use the last completed higher-timeframe bar, while realtime calculations can use the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of ATR(1) over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 50 when the denominator is not positive.
Calculation: Signal quality score = 2 points if pattern size qualifies; 2 if the true-break RSI conditions for the signal direction pass; 2 for a true break or 1 for a fakeout reversal; 1 if volume meets its moving average times a configurable multiplier (or volume confirmation is disabled); 1 if choppiness is below its threshold (or chop blocking is disabled); and, when higher-timeframe bias is enabled, 2 if it agrees with direction and 0 otherwise, or 1 if disabled. Cap at 10.
Long: A true-break long: pending direction is long AND true break is classified AND Fast RSI is above the long trigger AND Core RSI is above 50 AND Fast RSI slope is positive AND Core RSI slope is non-negative; OR a fakeout reversal long: pending direction is short AND a fakeout is classified AND Fast RSI crosses above 50 AND Fast RSI slope is positive. For either entry, require the signal cooldown to have elapsed, choppiness below its threshold when chop blocking is enabled, higher-timeframe bullish alignment when hard alignment is enabled, and quality score at least the configured minimum.
Short: A true-break short: pending direction is short AND true break is classified AND Fast RSI is below the short trigger AND Core RSI is below 50 AND Fast RSI slope is negative AND Core RSI slope is non-positive; OR a fakeout reversal short: pending direction is long AND a fakeout is classified AND Fast RSI crosses below 50 AND Fast RSI slope is negative. For either entry, require the signal cooldown to have elapsed, choppiness below its threshold when chop blocking is enabled, higher-timeframe bearish alignment when hard alignment is enabled, and quality score at least the configured minimum.
Long: Short-break fakeout is classified (failed short-break mark) OR active directional bias is bullish (pending direction is long, otherwise pattern bias is bullish).
Short: Long-break fakeout is classified (failed long-break mark) OR active directional bias is bearish (pending direction is short, otherwise pattern bias is bearish).
