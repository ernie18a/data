Calculation: Reversal score = 10 times the weighted mean of five components, each clamped to [0,1]: directional fast/slow EMA separation normalized as clamp((separation/ATR + 1)/2); RSI distance from 50 normalized as clamp(abs(RSI−50)/50 × 1.6); calmness as clamp(1 − the 100-bar ATR percentile/100); proximity as clamp(1 − abs(entry−fast EMA)/(2×ATR)); directional fast EMA change over 3 bars normalized as clamp((directional change/ATR + 1)/2). Use the configured weights; if their sum is zero, score is zero.
Calculation: Star tier = 4 at score ≥ 7.5, 3 at ≥ 6.5, 2 at ≥ 5.5, 1 at ≥ 4.5, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Stop distance = clamp the absolute distance from entry to the configured raw stop between the configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples. The raw stop is either the recent swing low minus an ATR buffer for longs / swing high plus an ATR buffer for shorts, or entry minus / plus a configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Targets = entry plus (long) or minus (short) stop distance multiplied by each configured R target; stop = entry minus (long) or plus (short) stop distance.
Long: On a confirmed bar, RSI crosses above the configured midline or oversold threshold AND (trend filter is disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA) AND star tier ≥ configured minimum AND no trade is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND ATR is available.
Short: On a confirmed bar, RSI crosses below the configured midline or overbought threshold AND (trend filter is disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA) AND star tier ≥ configured minimum AND no trade is active AND cooldown has elapsed AND ATR is available.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR high ≥ third target OR (low ≤ stop after an earlier bar reached the first or second target) OR bars since entry ≥ configured time limit.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR low ≤ third target OR (high ≥ stop after an earlier bar reached the first or second target) OR bars since entry ≥ configured time limit.
