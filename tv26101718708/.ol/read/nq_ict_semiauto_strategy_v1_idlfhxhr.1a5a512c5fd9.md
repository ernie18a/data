Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe).
Calculation: Dealing-range midpoint = (previous-day high + previous-day low) / 2.
Calculation: Premium/discount band = dealing-range midpoint ± a configured fraction of the previous-day range; premium is above the upper edge and discount is below the lower edge.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal configured numbers of chart bars on each side; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Sweep reference high/low = the greater of previous-day high and the latest confirmed swing high / the lesser of previous-day low and the latest confirmed swing low; use the previous-day level if no pivot is available.
Calculation: Bearish/bullish displacement = bar range > ATR × configured multiplier, body/range ≥ configured minimum, and close within a configured fraction of the low/high, respectively.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = current low > high two chart bars earlier / current high < low two chart bars earlier; gap size must meet a configured minimum.
Calculation: Long/short setup stop = the lower of current low and sweep reference low minus a configured buffer / the greater of current high and sweep reference high plus a configured buffer, respectively.
Long: low < sweep reference low AND close > sweep reference low OR close > latest confirmed swing high OR configured NY session is active AND price is below the premium/discount band AND a bullish liquidity sweep, bullish displacement, bullish MSS, and bullish fair value gap have each occurred within the configured setup lookback AND current bar overlaps that gap within twice the setup lookback AND entry-to-setup-stop distance is greater than one tick and no greater than the configured maximum stop distance.
Short: high > sweep reference high AND close < sweep reference high OR close < latest confirmed swing low OR configured NY session is active AND price is above the premium/discount band AND a bearish liquidity sweep, bearish displacement, bearish MSS, and bearish fair value gap have each occurred within the configured setup lookback AND current bar overlaps that gap within twice the setup lookback AND setup-stop-to-entry distance is greater than one tick and no greater than the configured maximum stop distance.
Exit: long, low ≤ current stop OR high ≥ entry price + initial risk × configured target multiple; once high ≥ entry price + initial risk × configured break-even multiple, current stop is at least entry price.
Exit: short, high ≥ current stop OR low ≤ entry price − initial risk × configured target multiple; once low ≤ entry price − initial risk × configured break-even multiple, current stop is at most entry price.
