Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range session, reset each session
Calculation: Estimated delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / candle range; strong positive/negative delta = delta > 0 / delta < 0 and absolute delta ≥ moving average of absolute delta × its strength multiplier
Calculation: Displacement = candle body ≥ ATR × its threshold and body / candle range ≥ its minimum fraction
Calculation: Primary bullish/bearish FVG = among valid gaps found during the configured scan after setup confirmation, the gap with the lowest/highest midpoint; a bullish gap has low > high two bars earlier, and a bearish gap has high < low two bars earlier; each gap must meet the configured minimum ATR size
Calculation: Primary FVG retest = bar range overlaps the selected gap; midpoint = average of its top and bottom
