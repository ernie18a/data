Calculation: Support/resistance zones = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows, using equal left and right lookbacks, clustered into ranges no wider than ATR times a width parameter; retain qualifying ranges by strength, where each clustered pivot adds strength and each bar in the lookback window whose high or low falls inside the range adds strength. Permanently remove a resistance range after a close above its top and a support range after a close below its bottom.
Long: The previous chart bar closed above an active support-zone top and its low was at or below that top but at or above the zone bottom; the current close is above that same top and the previous close was at or below it; DXY close is below its simple moving average on both the higher-timeframe daily and 4-hour bars, using the latest completed bars.
Short: The previous chart bar closed below an active resistance-zone bottom and its high was at or above that bottom but at or below the zone top; the current close is below that same bottom and the previous close was at or above it; DXY close is above its simple moving average on both the higher-timeframe daily and 4-hour bars, using the latest completed bars.
