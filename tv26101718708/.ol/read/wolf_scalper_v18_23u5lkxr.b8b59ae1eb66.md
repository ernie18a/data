Calculation: ATR = average true range of chart bars; use the 14-bar value for displacement, entry distance, and risk, and the 50-bar value for the chop filter.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = price takes out the latest confirmed pivot low, closes back above it, and the lower wick exceeds the configured fraction of the bar range. Bearish liquidity sweep = price takes out the latest confirmed pivot high, closes back below it, and the upper wick exceeds that fraction.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the configured minimum gap; bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least that gap. An FVG becomes an inverted FVG when price closes beyond its far boundary: a bearish FVG inverts bullish above its top, and a bullish FVG inverts bearish below its bottom. Remove an inverted gap after price crosses its opposite boundary.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the prior bar's body after a bullish displacement bar that closes above the prior high; bearish order block = the prior bar's body after a bearish displacement bar that closes below the prior low. Keep only blocks whose body is within the configured ATR size cap; remove a bullish block when close falls below its configured mitigation level, and a bearish block when close rises above its mitigation level.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = candle body exceeds the configured multiple of ATR(14).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CISD = two or three consecutive rising/falling closes, respectively; emit only when direction differs from the last CISD and the configured minimum bars have elapsed since its last label.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish CHoCH = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high / below the latest confirmed pivot low, respectively, while the stored trend direction is not already bullish / bearish; update trend direction when triggered.
Calculation: Premium/discount = rolling high/low midpoint over the configured lookback; premium is close above the midpoint between the range high and midpoint, and discount is close below the midpoint between the range low and midpoint.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish confluence score = 1 each for a confirmed pattern in that direction; a same-direction sweep within the recent window; a same-direction CHoCH or stored trend; a same-direction FVG, inverted FVG, or order block containing the bar; a same-direction CISD on this bar; the corresponding discount/premium zone; and close above/below the current New York session open. Also add 1 for a same-direction SMT divergence within the recent window when comparison-symbol OHLC data is available.
Long: bullish liquidity sweep AND close > open AND bullish displacement AND close is no farther than the configured ATR limit above the latest pivot low AND bullish confluence score >= B minimum AND bullish confluence score < A++ minimum AND ATR(14) > ATR(50) × configured chop minimum AND no bullish signal within the configured cooldown
Long: (bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish inverted FVG containing the bar OR bullish order block containing the bar) AND close > open AND bullish displacement AND (bullish confluence score >= A++ minimum OR (bullish confluence score >= B minimum AND bullish confluence score < A++ minimum)) AND ATR(14) > ATR(50) × configured chop minimum AND bar is in an enabled NY AM, NY PM, or London session AND no bullish signal within the configured cooldown
Long: bullish CISD condition
Long: bullish CHoCH condition
Short: bearish liquidity sweep AND close < open AND bearish displacement AND close is no farther than the configured ATR limit below the latest pivot high AND bearish confluence score >= B minimum AND bearish confluence score < A++ minimum AND ATR(14) > ATR(50) × configured chop minimum AND no bearish signal within the configured cooldown
Short: (bearish liquidity sweep OR bearish inverted FVG containing the bar OR bearish order block containing the bar) AND close < open AND bearish displacement AND (bearish confluence score >= A++ minimum OR (bearish confluence score >= B minimum AND bearish confluence score < A++ minimum)) AND ATR(14) > ATR(50) × configured chop minimum AND bar is in an enabled NY AM, NY PM, or London session AND no bearish signal within the configured cooldown
Short: bearish CISD condition
Short: bearish CHoCH condition
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR(14) at entry × configured stop multiple OR high >= entry price + (ATR(14) at entry × configured stop multiple) × configured first-target multiple
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR(14) at entry × configured stop multiple OR low <= entry price - (ATR(14) at entry × configured stop multiple) × configured first-target multiple
