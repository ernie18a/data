Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot low (2-left/1-right or 3-left/1-right) = a bar whose low is below the lows of the specified number of preceding bars and the following bar; confirm using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot high (2-left/1-right or 3-left/1-right) = a bar whose high is above the highs of the specified number of preceding bars and the following bar; confirm using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe band midpoint = average of the simple moving average and exponential moving average of close, each with its own configurable lookback; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe band distance = 100 × (close − band midpoint) / band midpoint, evaluated at each pivot bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe band momentum = 100 × (band midpoint − its value at a configurable earlier lookback) / that earlier value, evaluated at each pivot bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe band width = 100 × absolute difference between the simple and exponential moving averages / band midpoint, evaluated at each pivot bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe RSI = RSI of close over a configurable lookback, evaluated at each pivot bar.
Long: A confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low (2-left/1-right or 3-left/1-right) and its previous pivot low of the same structure are within the configured minimum and maximum separation, AND, for any one of RSI, band distance, band momentum, or band width, (current pivot low < previous pivot low AND current measure value > previous measure value) OR (current pivot low > previous pivot low AND current measure value < previous measure value).
Short: A confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high (2-left/1-right or 3-left/1-right) and its previous pivot high of the same structure are within the configured minimum and maximum separation, AND, for any one of RSI, band distance, band momentum, or band width, (current pivot high > previous pivot high AND current measure value < previous measure value) OR (current pivot high < previous pivot high AND current measure value > previous measure value).
