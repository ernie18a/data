Calculation: Previous RTH close = close of the last bar in the previous 09:30–16:00 New York session.
Calculation: Current RTH open = open of the first bar at 09:30 New York.
Calculation: Gap size in ATR = absolute difference between the current RTH open and previous RTH close divided by ATR from the last completed daily bar, using completed daily bars on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Opening location = current RTH open is above the previous RTH high, below the previous RTH low, or inside the previous RTH range, including its boundaries.
Calculation: Opening bias = for a tiny gap, mean reversion if the open is inside the previous RTH range and otherwise mean reversion; for a small/medium gap, moderate mean reversion if inside and otherwise balanced; for a large gap, mixed if inside and otherwise continuation favored; for an extreme gap, strong continuation. The ordered gap-size classes use three increasing thresholds.
Calculation: First-interval direction = compare the close of the bar ending at 09:45 New York with the current RTH open; it moves toward the gap fill if a gap up closes below the open or a gap down closes above it, and otherwise moves away.
Long: (gap up AND opening bias is continuation favored or strong continuation) OR (gap down AND opening bias is strong mean reversion, mean reversion, or moderate mean reversion) OR (gap down AND first-interval direction moves toward the gap fill) OR (gap up AND first-interval direction moves away from the gap fill)
Short: (gap up AND opening bias is strong mean reversion, mean reversion, or moderate mean reversion) OR (gap down AND opening bias is continuation favored or strong continuation) OR (gap up AND first-interval direction moves toward the gap fill) OR (gap down AND first-interval direction moves away from the gap fill)
