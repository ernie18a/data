Calculation: Previous-bar high = high of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Previous-bar low = low of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe
Calculation: Long take-profit price = entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage / 100)
Calculation: Long stop-loss price = entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage / 100)
Calculation: Short take-profit price = entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage / 100)
Calculation: Short stop-loss price = entry price × (1 + stop-loss percentage / 100)
Long: Close crosses above previous-bar high
Short: Close crosses below previous-bar low
Exit: Long, high ≥ long take-profit price OR low ≤ long stop-loss price
Exit: Short, low ≤ short take-profit price OR high ≥ short stop-loss price
