Calculation: Selected volume measure = volume, close × volume, or ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume, according to the selected measure
Calculation: Volume anomaly score = (ln(current selected volume measure) − mean(log selected volume measure over preceding positive-volume lower-timeframe bars)) / population standard deviation of those preceding log measures; calculate before adding the current bar and only after the warmup minimum
Calculation: Print direction = buy if close > open, sell if close < open; if equal, buy if close is above the midpoint of the bar's high-low range, sell if below; if still unresolved, compare close with the previous positive-volume lower-timeframe bar's close; otherwise neutral. Use lower-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Cluster count = number of distinct symbols with qualifying prints within the cluster window ending at the newest qualifying print; cluster net = sum of each such print's anomaly score, positive for buy or neutral and negative for sell
Long: Print direction is buy AND the volume anomaly score passes the selected filter (at or above the lower threshold; at or above the higher threshold in surge-only mode; or at/above the lower and below the higher threshold in lower-tier-only mode) OR cluster count is at least the minimum AND cluster net is at least zero
Short: Print direction is sell AND the volume anomaly score passes the selected filter (at or above the lower threshold; at or above the higher threshold in surge-only mode; or at/above the lower and below the higher threshold in lower-tier-only mode) OR cluster count is at least the minimum AND cluster net is below zero
