Calculation: Fractal pivot low = a low lower than the surrounding bars over its pivot lookback, confirmed only after the same number of later bars; the weak-fractal lookback is shorter than the major-fractal lookback. Detection uses the selected timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe when different) and only its last completed bar.
Calculation: Fractal pivot high = a high higher than the surrounding bars over its pivot lookback, confirmed only after the same number of later bars; the weak-fractal lookback is shorter than the major-fractal lookback. Detection uses the selected timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe when different) and only its last completed bar.
Calculation: Price-change Z-score = current close-to-close change minus the recent average close-to-close change, divided by the standard deviation of recent close-to-close changes.
Long: A new confirmed major or weak fractal pivot low forms AND its pivot-bar volume is at least the configured minimum volume.
Long: The close crosses above the latest confirmed structural pivot high AND the price-change Z-score exceeds the configured positive threshold AND this pivot high has not previously produced a bullish break.
Long: A broken resistance zone has first moved below the zone by at least the configured ATR distance, then retests the zone from below AND the configured flip confirmation is satisfied (zone retest; OR close at least the configured ATR distance above the zone; OR an upper-wick rejection, bullish engulfing candle, or such a close).
Short: A new confirmed major or weak fractal pivot high forms AND its pivot-bar volume is at least the configured minimum volume.
Short: The close crosses below the latest confirmed structural pivot low AND the price-change Z-score is below the negative configured threshold AND this pivot low has not previously produced a bearish break.
Short: A broken support zone has first moved above the zone by at least the configured ATR distance, then retests the zone from above AND the configured flip confirmation is satisfied (zone retest; OR close at least the configured ATR distance below the zone; OR a lower-wick rejection, bearish engulfing candle, or such a close).
