Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute close change over its lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive trend basis = Kaufman adaptive moving average of close; channel width = ATR multiplied by a base width plus a noise expansion multiplied by (1 − trend efficiency).
Calculation: Bullish raw trend = close above adaptive trend basis AND normalized basis slope over its lookback at least the positive minimum AND trend efficiency at least its minimum. Bearish raw trend = close below basis AND normalized slope at most the negative minimum AND efficiency at least its minimum.
Calculation: Trend state = bullish or bearish raw trend when prior state is neutral; retain a bullish state until the close is below the lower channel for the required consecutive bars, then switch to bearish only if bearish raw trend holds, otherwise neutral; retain a bearish state until the close is above the upper channel for the required consecutive bars, then switch to bullish only if bullish raw trend holds, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Nearest resistance/support = nearest distinct higher/lower value, respectively, among prior completed daily high/low, prior completed 2-day and 3-day high/low, and prior completed weekly, monthly, and yearly high/low. Zone half-width = daily ATR multiplied by a width factor.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low pivot with left and right lookbacks. Wedge boundaries = lines through the two latest pivot highs and two latest pivot lows, projected to the current bar.
Calculation: Pattern confirmation = candidate condition holds for the required consecutive bars before the break, and the same pattern type is outside its cooldown; wedge breaks additionally require a new wedge instance, and head-and-shoulders breaks require a new head pivot.
Long: Trend state changes to bullish from any non-bullish state OR a bearish trend breaks (prior state bearish AND close above upper channel for the required consecutive bars) OR close crosses above nearest resistance + zone half-width from at or below it OR a mature inverse head-and-shoulders candidate crosses above the mean of the two latest pivot highs OR a mature descending wedge closes above its projected upper boundary OR a mature bull flag closes above the prior flag-range high OR a mature cup-and-handle closes above the prior handle high.
Short: Trend state changes to bearish from any non-bearish state OR a bullish trend breaks (prior state bullish AND close below lower channel for the required consecutive bars) OR close crosses below nearest support − zone half-width from at or above it OR a mature head-and-shoulders candidate crosses below the mean of the two latest pivot lows OR a mature ascending wedge closes below its projected lower boundary.
