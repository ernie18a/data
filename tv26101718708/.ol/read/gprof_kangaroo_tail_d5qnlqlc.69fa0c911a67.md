Calculation: Daily ATR = ATR on higher-timeframe daily bars, using the last completed daily bar.
Calculation: Camarilla R3/R4 = C + (H − L) × 1.1/4 and C + (H − L) × 1.1/2; S3/S4 = C − (H − L) × 1.1/4 and C − (H − L) × 1.1/2; R6 = C × H/L; S6 = 2C − R6. Use the prior session’s RTH or ETH H/L/C according to the configured source; in Auto mode use ETH when the post-market or current premarket extends the prior RTH range by more than the configured percentage of Daily ATR.
Calculation: Qualified confluence levels = premarket high/low, prior-session high/low, prior regular-session close, current-week high/low, previous completed overnight high/low, applicable Camarilla R3/R4/R6 for shorts and S3/S4/S6 for longs, and enabled user-supplied higher-timeframe levels. A live premarket high/low counts only after remaining unchanged for the configured maturity period while premarket is open; current-week high/low counts only after remaining unchanged for that period. Other listed levels are frozen. A qualifying level is within the sweep-wick span expanded by the greater of the configured percentage of Daily ATR or the configured tick floor.
Long: chart bar is confirmed AND chart timeframe is intraday AND low < the lowest low of the longer lookback’s preceding chart bars AND body bottom ≥ high − configured body fraction × (high − low) AND high − body top ≤ max(configured percentage × (high − low), one tick) AND body bottom − low ≥ max(configured percentage × Daily ATR, configured tick floor) AND body lies within the previous chart bar’s low-to-high range AND at least one applicable long confluence level qualifies.
Short: chart bar is confirmed AND chart timeframe is intraday AND high > the highest high of the longer lookback’s preceding chart bars AND body top ≤ low + configured body fraction × (high − low) AND body bottom − low ≤ max(configured percentage × (high − low), one tick) AND high − body top ≥ max(configured percentage × Daily ATR, configured tick floor) AND body lies within the previous chart bar’s low-to-high range AND at least one applicable short confluence level qualifies.
