Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the extreme within an equal lookback on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Descending resistance line = line through consecutive confirmed pivot highs where the newer high is lower; before a break, require the latest two highs each below the prior two highs. Rising support line = line through consecutive confirmed pivot lows where the newer low is higher; before a break, require the latest two lows each above the prior two lows. Extend each line by its slope each bar; offset resistance below and support above by a price buffer proportional to ATR(200) times the padding parameter.
Calculation: Displacement = confirming bar range is at least a parameterized multiple of ATR(14), its body/range is at least a parameterized ratio, and its close is in the signal direction when directional close is required.
Calculation: Estimated bar delta = volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1); use 0 when range is zero. Cumulative delta = sum of estimated bar delta from the trendline break through the confirming bar, capped by a parameterized lookback.
Calculation: Stop and target = entry is the signal bar close. Stop is beyond either the trendline break level or the lowest low / highest high over a parameterized lookback of prior bars, with a tick buffer; target is entry plus/minus the stop distance times the selected risk multiple.
Long: (break above descending resistance, when the bar's low is above the active resistance line OR within the configured BOS wait, a close above the latest confirmed pivot high) AND displacement passes when enabled AND, when order-flow confirmation is enabled, estimated bar delta ratio is at least its minimum, volume meets its configured average-volume threshold when required, and cumulative delta is positive when required.
Short: (break below rising support, when the bar's high is below the active support line OR within the configured BOS wait, a close below the latest confirmed pivot low) AND displacement passes when enabled AND, when order-flow confirmation is enabled, estimated bar delta ratio is no greater than the negative minimum, volume meets its configured average-volume threshold when required, and cumulative delta is negative when required.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target
