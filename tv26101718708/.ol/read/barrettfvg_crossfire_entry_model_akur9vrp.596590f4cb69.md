Calculation: Fair Value Gap = bullish when the current low is above the high two bars earlier; bearish when the current high is below the low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Crossfire zone = the overlap of an unfilled Fair Value Gap and a newer Fair Value Gap of the opposite direction; its direction is the newer gap’s direction. Each subsequent overlapping opposite-direction gap flips the zone, narrows it to the overlap, and increases its star count.
Calculation: Zone retest = a completed chart-timeframe bar overlaps a live zone after the preceding bar did not overlap it; the zone must have existed before the current bar.
Calculation: BOS pivot = the latest confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high or pivot low, using the same configurable left and right lookback.
Calculation: Active VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset at the configured day-open anchor; use the session VWAP during its session after its grace period, and otherwise use the day-open VWAP.
Calculation: Displacement = trigger-bar range is at least the configured multiple of ATR, and absolute body divided by range is at least the configured minimum; when directional close is required, close is above open for long or below open for short.
Calculation: Stop and target = stop is beyond either the lowest low or highest high over the configured lookback excluding the entry bar, or beyond the retested zone’s far edge, according to the selected stop method; apply the configured tick buffer and ensure the stop is beyond entry. Target is entry plus (or minus) the stop distance multiplied by the configured reward-to-risk multiple.
Long: bullish zone retest OR (a pending bullish retest meets the minimum star count and, if BOS is required, close crosses above the latest pivot high plus the configured buffer; displacement passes if enabled; close is above the active VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled; the bar is in an allowed session if session gating is enabled; and the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed); evaluate on bar close.
Short: bearish zone retest OR (a pending bearish retest meets the minimum star count and, if BOS is required, close crosses below the latest pivot low minus the configured buffer; displacement passes if enabled; close is below the active VWAP if the VWAP filter is enabled; the bar is in an allowed session if session gating is enabled; and the configured same-direction cooldown has elapsed); evaluate on bar close.
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop OR low <= target.
