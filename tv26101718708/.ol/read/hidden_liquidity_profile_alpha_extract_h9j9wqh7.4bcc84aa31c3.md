Calculation: Supply liquidity by price bin = sum of volume × candle range × age decay × (upper-wick size × wick weight + candle-body size × body weight × sell bias), distributed around the bar’s supply price; sell bias is 1 for a down or flat bar and 0.55 for an up bar. Supply price is 45% of the upper wick below the high when an upper wick exists, otherwise 20% of the candle range below the high.
Calculation: Demand liquidity by price bin = sum of volume × candle range × age decay × (lower-wick size × wick weight + candle-body size × body weight × buy bias), distributed around the bar’s demand price; buy bias is 1 for an up or flat bar and 0.55 for a down bar. Demand price is 45% of the lower wick above the low when a lower wick exists, otherwise 20% of the candle range above the low.
Calculation: Age decay = (bar age within the analyzed lookback, with the current bar age 1, divided by the lookback length) raised to the decay power.
Calculation: Normalized supply strength = supply liquidity in a bin divided by the maximum supply liquidity of any bin. Normalized demand strength = demand liquidity in a bin divided by the maximum demand liquidity of any bin.
Calculation: Combined normalized strength = the bin’s supply plus demand liquidity divided by the maximum combined liquidity of any bin.
Calculation: Candidate score = 70% of the greater normalized supply or demand strength plus 30% of combined normalized strength.
Long: A selected candidate bin has normalized demand strength > normalized supply strength AND its greater normalized strength meets the key-level trigger AND it is a local peak when local-peak preference is enabled AND its candidate score is the highest among eligible bins not within the minimum spacing of an already selected bin.
Short: A selected candidate bin has normalized supply strength >= normalized demand strength AND its greater normalized strength meets the key-level trigger AND it is a local peak when local-peak preference is enabled AND its candidate score is the highest among eligible bins not within the minimum spacing of an already selected bin.
