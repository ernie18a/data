Calculation: MACD = EMA(close, shorter lookback) − EMA(close, longer lookback), calculated on the chart timeframe or selected timeframe; if the selected timeframe differs, use its still-forming bar (it may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe)
Calculation: Signal line = SMA(MACD, signal lookback), calculated on the same timeframe and bar as MACD
Long: MACD crosses above Signal line
Short: MACD crosses below Signal line
