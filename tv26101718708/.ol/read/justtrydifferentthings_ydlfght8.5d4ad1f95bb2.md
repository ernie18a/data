Calculation: Signal open = open of the requested timeframe bar, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the current bar, whether forming or completed
Calculation: H = EMA of the longer-lookback highest Signal open
Calculation: L = EMA of the longer-lookback lowest Signal open
Long: short-lookback SMA of H crosses below long-lookback SMA of H
Short: short-lookback SMA of L crosses above long-lookback SMA of L
