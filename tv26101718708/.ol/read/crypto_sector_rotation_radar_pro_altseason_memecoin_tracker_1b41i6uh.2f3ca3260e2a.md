Calculation: Relative strength momentum = weighted average of the rate of change in a sector’s market-cap-to-TOTAL ratio over short-, medium-, and long-term lookbacks, from shortest to longest, using completed bars on the selected calculation timeframe (which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe)
Calculation: Sector rank = 1 plus the number of the other six sectors with higher relative strength momentum
Long: Sector rank ≤ 3 AND relative strength momentum > 0
Short: Sector rank ≥ 5 AND relative strength momentum < 0
