Calculation: Value-area high and low = Over the configured lookback window, divide the range from its lowest low to highest high into equal price bins. For each bin, count the profile-timeframe bars whose high-low range overlaps it. Start at the bin with the greatest count and expand toward the adjacent bin with the greater count until the configured fraction of total counts is included; ties expand upward. The value-area high is the top of the uppermost included bin; the value-area low is the bottom of the lowermost included bin. Profile bars use the configured timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar historically and the still-forming bar in realtime.
Calculation: Value-area position (%) = (chart-bar close − value-area low) / (value-area high − value-area low) × 100
Long: value-area high > value-area low AND value-area position ≥ 0 AND value-area position ≤ the configured long-zone maximum
Short: value-area high > value-area low AND value-area position ≤ 100 AND value-area position ≥ the configured short-zone minimum
