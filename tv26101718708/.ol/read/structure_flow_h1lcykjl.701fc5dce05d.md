Calculation: Yearly VWAP = cumulative((high + low + close) / 3 × volume) / cumulative(volume), reset at each calendar year
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal configurable numbers of bars on both sides; retain the latest two confirmed pivots of each type
Calculation: ATR buffer = ATR × configurable buffer multiplier
Calculation: Bullish BOS = latest swing high > preceding swing high AND close > latest swing high + ATR buffer AND this condition was false on the prior bar AND latest swing high differs from the last bullish BOS level; bearish BOS is the inverse using swing lows
Calculation: Normalized flow = EMA( ((close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment) − (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum price increment)) × volume, short lookback) / max(EMA(volume, same short lookback), 1)
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, longer lookback)
Calculation: Bullish stack = EMA(close, intermediate lookback) > Yearly VWAP AND close > both; bearish stack is the inverse
Calculation: Active market = ATR / close ≥ configurable minimum; reduce the minimum by a configurable factor on chart timeframes of one hour or less
Calculation: Valid long location = location gate disabled OR price range is within ATR-based tolerance of an unmitigated bullish order-block zone, latest swing high, lower support band, or Yearly VWAP; valid short location uses an unmitigated bearish order-block zone, latest swing low, upper resistance band, or Yearly VWAP
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = highest-scoring qualifying bearish candle among a configurable recent-bar lookback at bullish BOS; bearish order-block zone is the highest-scoring qualifying bullish candle at bearish BOS. A candidate qualifies when body/range meets a configurable minimum; score by proximity to the broken swing level, range, wick, body, and departure as specified by the candidate-score formula. A zone is invalid after mitigation, expiry, or excessive ATR distance.
Calculation: Lower support and upper resistance bands = adaptively smoothed bands around a smoothed trail; trail follows the higher of EMA and Yearly VWAP in a bullish stack, the lower in a bearish stack, otherwise their midpoint (or EMA when their gap exceeds the ATR cap on chart timeframes of one hour or less). Band width uses smoothed ATR plus capped, smoothed EMA–VWAP gap, with a minimum width and limited per-bar compression; support/resistance edges are offset from the trail by configurable inner and outer width multipliers.

Short: Bearish BOS OR (Active market AND valid short location AND bearish stack AND Bearish BOS AND Normalized flow < configurable negative threshold AND Relative volume ≥ configurable threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA)) OR (Active market AND B-tier enabled AND valid short location AND bearish stack AND Bearish BOS AND ((Normalized flow < negative threshold AND Relative volume < threshold) OR (Normalized flow < 70% of negative threshold AND Relative volume ≥ threshold)) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR B-tier higher-timeframe requirement disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND NOT (Active market AND valid short location AND bearish stack AND Bearish BOS AND Normalized flow < negative threshold AND Relative volume ≥ threshold AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA)))
