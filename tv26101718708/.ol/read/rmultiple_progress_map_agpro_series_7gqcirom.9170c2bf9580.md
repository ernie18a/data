Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the structure lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Recent swing low/high = lowest low / highest high over the invalidation lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Trend = bullish when fast EMA > slow EMA, slow EMA is at least its value five bars earlier, and close >= fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA < slow EMA, slow EMA is at most its value five bars earlier, and close <= fast EMA
Calculation: Candle direction score = 45% × abs(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) + 55% × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) for long; replace the last fraction with (high − close) / max(high − low, minimum tick) for short
Calculation: Range-break activation = close > prior range high AND close > open for long; close < prior range low AND close < open for short
Calculation: Trend-pulse activation = bullish trend AND low <= fast EMA AND long candle direction score >= its threshold for long; bearish trend AND high >= fast EMA AND short candle direction score >= its threshold for short
Calculation: Invalidation proxy = recent swing low − ATR × buffer for long; recent swing high + ATR × buffer for short
Calculation: Risk unit = absolute difference between close and the invalidation proxy
Calculation: Activation score = weighted combination of trend quality, candle direction score, risk-fit score, and volume score; trend quality is strongest when trend agrees with direction, risk-fit favors ATR-normalized risk within configured bounds, and volume score rises with volume relative to its 20-bar average
Long: Bar is confirmed AND no progress cycle is active AND bar index exceeds the structure lookback AND the selected activation mode (range break, trend pulse, or either) is true AND long invalidation proxy < close AND long risk unit / ATR is within configured bounds AND long activation score >= its threshold AND (short candidate is invalid OR long activation score >= short activation score)
Short: Bar is confirmed AND no progress cycle is active AND bar index exceeds the structure lookback AND the selected activation mode (range break, trend pulse, or either) is true AND short invalidation proxy > close AND short risk unit / ATR is within configured bounds AND short activation score >= its threshold AND (long candidate is invalid OR short activation score > long activation score)
Exit: Long, bar is confirmed AND active bars > 0 AND low <= stored invalidation proxy OR bar is confirmed AND active bars >= maximum tracking bars AND invalidation proxy was not touched
Exit: Short, bar is confirmed AND active bars > 0 AND high >= stored invalidation proxy OR bar is confirmed AND active bars >= maximum tracking bars AND invalidation proxy was not touched
