Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is above the highs of the preceding and following equal-length pivot windows; confirmed only after the following window closes
Long: Current confirmed pivot low < previous confirmed pivot low AND at least one enabled reference asset's low at the current pivot time > its low at the previous chart pivot low AND (correlation filter disabled OR that reference's rolling close correlation with the chart >= minimum) AND (confirmation mode is none OR RSI is rising OR close > open OR RSI is rising AND close > open, according to the selected mode) AND bars since the previous long signal >= cooldown AND no short signal on this bar
Short: Current confirmed pivot high > previous confirmed pivot high AND at least one enabled reference asset's high at the current pivot time < its high at the previous chart pivot high AND (correlation filter disabled OR that reference's rolling close correlation with the chart >= minimum) AND (confirmation mode is none OR RSI is falling OR close < open OR RSI is falling AND close < open, according to the selected mode) AND bars since the previous short signal >= cooldown AND no long signal on this bar
