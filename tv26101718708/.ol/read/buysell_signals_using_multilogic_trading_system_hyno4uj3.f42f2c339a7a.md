Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the range lookback, excluding the current bar; prior range width = high − low; prior range width % = width / low × 100
Calculation: Prior swing high/low = highest high / lowest low over the swing lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA; bearish trend = close < fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish confluence score = count of: bullish trend; RSI > 45 and RSI < 72; MACD line > MACD signal and MACD histogram > 0; close > Bollinger basis; usable volume is high and OBV > its moving average; close > open and close > prior swing high
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = count of: bearish trend; RSI > 28 and RSI < 55; MACD line < MACD signal and MACD histogram < 0; close < Bollinger basis; usable volume is high and OBV < its moving average; close < open and close < prior swing low
Calculation: High volume = current volume >= volume multiplier × its moving average; volume permission = volume filter disabled OR volume unavailable OR high volume
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: OBV = cumulative volume signed positive when close rises from the previous bar, negative when it falls, and zero when unchanged
Calculation: Long stop = configured stop method: prior swing low − ATR buffer; or entry price − ATR multiple; or entry price × (1 − fixed stop percentage); if the result is unavailable or >= entry price, use entry price − ATR multiple
Calculation: Short stop = configured stop method: prior swing high + ATR buffer; or entry price + ATR multiple; or entry price × (1 + fixed stop percentage); if the result is unavailable or <= entry price, use entry price + ATR multiple
Calculation: Long target = configured target method: entry price + ATR multiple; or entry price × (1 + fixed target percentage); or entry price + (entry price − long stop) × risk/reward ratio
Calculation: Short target = configured target method: entry price − ATR multiple; or entry price × (1 − fixed target percentage); or entry price − (short stop − entry price) × risk/reward ratio
Long: (bullish trend AND bullish confluence score >= minimum trend confluence AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (close > fast EMA AND RSI > 52 AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > previous bar's histogram AND OBV > its moving average AND volume permission AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (prior range width % <= range threshold AND close > prior range high + ATR × breakout buffer AND previous close <= previous bar's prior range high AND (high − low) > 0 AND (close − low) / (high − low) >= minimum breakout close position AND RSI > 50 AND MACD line > MACD signal AND volume permission AND (breakout volume requirement disabled OR volume unavailable OR high volume) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > session VWAP)) OR (prior range width % <= range threshold AND NOT bullish trend AND NOT bearish trend AND prior range width >= ATR × minimum range-to-ATR ratio AND low <= prior range low + 0.20 × prior range width AND close > prior range low + 0.20 × prior range width AND close > open AND (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low) >= minimum rejection wick ratio AND RSI < 38 AND (RSI-turn filter disabled OR RSI > previous bar's RSI) AND volume permission); signal only on a confirmed chart bar; apply configured fresh-setup and cooldown requirements and the configured open-trade/opposite-signal gate; if both long and short candidates occur, require bullish confluence score > bearish confluence score
Short: (bearish trend AND bearish confluence score >= minimum trend confluence AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (close < fast EMA AND RSI < 48 AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < previous bar's histogram AND OBV < its moving average AND volume permission AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (prior range width % <= range threshold AND close < prior range low − ATR × breakout buffer AND previous close >= previous bar's prior range low AND (high − low) > 0 AND (close − low) / (high − low) <= 1 − minimum breakout close position AND RSI < 50 AND MACD line < MACD signal AND volume permission AND (breakout volume requirement disabled OR volume unavailable OR high volume) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < session VWAP)) OR (prior range width % <= range threshold AND NOT bullish trend AND NOT bearish trend AND prior range width >= ATR × minimum range-to-ATR ratio AND high >= prior range high − 0.20 × prior range width AND close < prior range high − 0.20 × prior range width AND close < open AND (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) >= minimum rejection wick ratio AND RSI > 62 AND (RSI-turn filter disabled OR RSI < previous bar's RSI) AND volume permission); signal only on a confirmed chart bar; apply configured fresh-setup and cooldown requirements and the configured open-trade/opposite-signal gate; if both long and short candidates occur, require bearish confluence score > bullish confluence score
Exit: Long, low <= active long stop OR high >= active long target
Exit: Short, high >= active short stop OR low <= active short target
