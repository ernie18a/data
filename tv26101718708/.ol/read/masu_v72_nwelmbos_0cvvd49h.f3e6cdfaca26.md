Calculation: NW Kernel Ribbon = eight Gaussian-kernel weighted averages of close, with bandwidths increasing from short-term to long-term; bullish when configured ribbon ordering/expansion test is met, bearish when its inverse is met.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish BOS = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Order-flow bullish = (cumulative volume delta is above its moving average while close falls and delta rises, OR up-bar volume / max(down-bar volume, 1) exceeds its threshold) AND (close crosses above VWAP or is above VWAP); bearish is the inverse, using down-bar volume / max(up-bar volume, 1), and VWAP below/crossdown.
Calculation: Smart-money bullish = bullish engulfing order block OR bullish fair value gap OR low-side liquidity sweep OR bullish BOS; bearish = bearish engulfing order block OR bearish fair value gap OR high-side liquidity sweep OR bearish BOS.
Calculation: Long confluence score = sum of the configured weights for bullish higher-timeframe EMA alignment, bullish NW ribbon, bullish order-flow filter, bullish smart-money filter, ADX above its trend threshold, bullish ELM vote weighted by its calibrated accuracy, and rising shortest-term NW line. Short score uses the corresponding bearish filters, vote, and falling NW line.
Calculation: Volatility-adjusted stop and target distances = ATR multiplied by regime-dependent factors; regime is determined by ATR percentile (high/low volatility) and ADX (trending/ranging).
Calculation: Cost filter = ATR in ticks divided by estimated spread plus commission; require the ratio to meet its configured minimum.
Long: ((ADX > trend threshold AND short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND close > long-term trend EMA AND short-term EMA crosses above long-term EMA AND volume > its moving average × volume threshold) OR (close > the prior-bar rolling highest high AND volume > its moving average × volume threshold AND close > long-term trend EMA) OR (ADX <= trend threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold threshold AND RSI crosses above oversold threshold)) AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA (use the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND bullish NW ribbon AND (order flow disabled OR bullish order-flow condition OR close > VWAP) AND (smart-money filter disabled OR bullish smart-money condition) AND (ELM blocking disabled OR ELM probability >= 1 − hard-block threshold) AND datetime within the configured session (or session filter disabled) AND long confluence score >= minimum AND long reward/risk >= minimum AND cost filter passes (or disabled) AND equity guard inactive AND position is flat.
Long: bullish BOS AND session condition passes AND cost filter passes AND equity guard inactive AND position is flat AND long confluence score >= minimum.
Short: ((ADX > trend threshold AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND close < long-term trend EMA AND short-term EMA crosses below long-term EMA AND volume > its moving average × volume threshold) OR (close < the prior-bar rolling lowest low AND volume > its moving average × volume threshold AND close < long-term trend EMA) OR (ADX <= trend threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought threshold AND RSI crosses below overbought threshold)) AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close < fast EMA (use the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND bearish NW ribbon AND (order flow disabled OR bearish order-flow condition OR close < VWAP) AND (smart-money filter disabled OR bearish smart-money condition) AND (ELM blocking disabled OR ELM probability <= hard-block threshold) AND datetime within the configured session (or session filter disabled) AND short confluence score >= minimum AND short reward/risk >= minimum AND cost filter passes (or disabled) AND equity guard inactive AND position is flat.
Short: bearish BOS AND session condition passes AND cost filter passes AND equity guard inactive AND position is flat AND short confluence score >= minimum.
Exit: long, low <= entry price − applicable ATR stop distance OR high >= entry price + applicable ATR target distance OR (trailing stop activated after the applicable favorable-price move AND low <= highest price since entry − trailing offset).
Exit: short, high >= entry price + applicable ATR stop distance OR low <= entry price − applicable ATR target distance OR (trailing stop activated after the applicable favorable-price move AND high >= lowest price since entry + trailing offset).
