Calculation: VROC = ((open − open n bars ago) / open n bars ago) × 100 / n × (volume / n-bar average volume)
Calculation: Smoothed VROC = simple moving average of VROC over a smoothing period
Calculation: Previous-peak VROC = Smoothed VROC at the most recent prior bar where high equaled the highest high in the lookback window
Short: high ≥ the highest high over the lookback window ending on the previous bar AND Smoothed VROC < Previous-peak VROC AND Smoothed VROC > 0
