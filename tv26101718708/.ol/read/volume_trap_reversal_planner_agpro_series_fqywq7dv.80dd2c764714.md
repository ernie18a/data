Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / simple moving average of volume over the volume-baseline lookback
Calculation: Upper reference = highest high over the trap-reference lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Lower reference = lowest low over the trap-reference lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Base score = clamp(30×clamp((relative volume − 0.70×spike threshold)/(max(full-volume threshold, effective spike threshold + 0.10) − 0.70×spike threshold), 0, 1) + 22×clamp((swept-side wick share − 0.25)/0.45, 0, 1) + 24×clamp((recovery-side close location − 0.52)/0.36, 0, 1) + 14×clamp((sweep penetration in ATRs − effective sweep buffer)/(0.85 − effective sweep buffer), 0, 1) + (relative volume ≥ effective spike threshold ? 10 : clamp((relative volume − 1)/(effective spike threshold − 1), 0, 1)), 0, 70)
Calculation: Confirmation score = clamp(10×clamp((body share − 0.18)/0.44, 0, 1) + 12×clamp((directional close location − 0.55)/0.35, 0, 1) + 8×clamp((directional follow-through in ATRs − 0.05)/1.20, 0, 1), 0, 30)
Long: On a completed chart bar, (low < lower reference − ATR × effective sweep buffer AND close > lower reference + ATR × effective recovery buffer AND lower wick share ≥ effective wick threshold AND relative volume ≥ effective spike threshold AND downside base score ≥ watch threshold AND no simultaneous upside trap AND enough bars since the previous trap event) OR (downside probe AND relative volume ≥ effective spike threshold AND downside base score ≥ watch threshold AND enough bars since the previous event label) OR (an active downside trap has a confirmation bar within the confirmation window: close > open AND body share ≥ confirmation body threshold AND bull close location ≥ confirmation close threshold AND close > trap confirmation level + ATR × confirmation buffer AND base score + confirmation score ≥ minimum reversal score)
Short: On a completed chart bar, (high > upper reference + ATR × effective sweep buffer AND close < upper reference − ATR × effective recovery buffer AND upper wick share ≥ effective wick threshold AND relative volume ≥ effective spike threshold AND upside base score ≥ watch threshold AND no simultaneous downside trap AND enough bars since the previous trap event) OR (upside probe AND relative volume ≥ effective spike threshold AND upside base score ≥ watch threshold AND enough bars since the previous event label) OR (an active upside trap has a confirmation bar within the confirmation window: close < open AND body share ≥ confirmation body threshold AND bear close location ≥ confirmation close threshold AND close < trap confirmation level − ATR × confirmation buffer AND base score + confirmation score ≥ minimum reversal score)
Exit: long, close < active downside trap risk edge
Exit: short, close > active upside trap risk edge
