Calculation: Confirmed swings = strict pivot highs/lows with separate left and right confirmation windows; accept a pivot only when its absolute distance from the preceding accepted pivot of the same kind exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold, using current chart ATR. Signals occur on the confirmation bar, without backdating.
Calculation: Chart structure = bullish when the latest two accepted highs and latest two accepted lows both rise; bearish when both fall; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Context bias = persistent direction of the last completed higher-timeframe close minus that timeframe's close a lookback earlier: bullish above a positive chart-ATR tolerance, bearish below its negative, otherwise retain the previous direction; initialize neutral. Structural bias uses context bias when known, otherwise chart structure.
Calculation: Structural events = bullish BOS when structural bias is bullish and close > latest accepted high AND previous close <= that same high; bearish BOS is symmetric below the latest accepted low. Bullish CHoCH uses the bullish BOS price comparison with bearish structural bias; bearish CHoCH is symmetric with bullish structural bias. Bullish MSS requires bearish structural bias AND close > second-latest accepted high AND previous close <= that high AND absolute candle body >= displacement multiplier × chart ATR; bearish MSS is symmetric below the second-latest accepted low. A BOS is displaced when its body meets the same displacement threshold.
Calculation: Validation event = retain the latest event's direction, type, anchor and age. On each bar select MSS before CHoCH before displaced BOS before ordinary BOS. Maintain separate last-occurrence ages for these winning structure events. A fresh breaker retest subsequently replaces the validation event only when no structure event fired or the winner was ordinary BOS. Freshness expires when age exceeds its limit.
Calculation: Liquidity pools = create a high-side pool at each accepted swing high and a low-side pool at each accepted swing low, dated at the pivot. Merge into the first unswept same-side pool within an ATR-scaled tolerance by averaging the two prices; retain its original date. Remove pools beyond maximum age, swept pools beyond a shorter retention age, and oldest pools exceeding capacity.
Calculation: Sweep = inspect unswept pools oldest first and select only the first qualifying pool. A bullish sweep requires low < low-side pool price AND close > pool price AND high - pool price >= minimum-distance multiplier × chart ATR. A bearish sweep requires high > high-side pool price AND close < pool price AND pool price - low >= the same minimum distance. Mark the selected pool swept. Quality = capped sum of penetration depth / ATR × depth weight and absolute candle body / ATR × rejection weight, with rejection weight greater than depth weight. Remember the latest sweep's direction, price, quality and date.
Calculation: Sweep confirmation = each new sweep resets and arms a confirmation window. Before the expiry bar, an armed bullish sweep advances when close > open AND close > swept price; bearish is symmetric. On a later bar before expiry, the bullish confirming state becomes confirmed when close > swept price AND close > previous close; bearish is symmetric. Unfulfilled states persist until expiry. Confirmed state persists for a longer retention window unless replaced by a new sweep.
Calculation: Order blocks = for each pre-breaker validation event, find the nearest preceding opposite-body candle within a search window; its entire high-low range becomes a block in the event direction, dated at that candle. A formed block becomes tested when a later-dated bar overlaps it. On a subsequent bar, a tested bullish block becomes mitigated when close < bottom; bearish when close > top. Expire by age and remove oldest blocks above capacity. Breaker confirmation can also mitigate a block.
Calculation: Fair value gaps = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier AND the gap size >= minimum-gap multiplier × chart ATR, with bounds [earlier high, current low]; bearish when current high < low two bars earlier with the same size filter, with bounds [current high, earlier low]. A formed gap becomes tested on an overlapping bar after formation. On a subsequent bar, a tested bullish gap inverts when close < bottom; bearish when close > top. Inversion reverses its effective direction. Expire gaps by age, allowing inverted gaps a longer lifetime, and remove oldest gaps above capacity.
Calculation: Breaker blocks = a sufficiently high-quality bullish sweep nominates each active bullish order block whose expanded bounds contain the swept price; a bearish sweep similarly nominates bearish blocks. The proposed breaker direction is opposite the order block. Store the order block's current array index and do not nominate an already tracked index. Starting on the following bar, confirm the candidate if a winning CHoCH in its proposed direction occurs within the confirmation window; optionally accept a displaced BOS instead. Confirmation mitigates the order block then occupying the stored index. Unconfirmed candidates expire after a longer failure window. A confirmed breaker becomes active on the next bar. Its first overlapping bar with close > open AND close > bottom produces a bullish retest; bearish requires close < open AND close < top. Active or tested bullish breakers expire when close < bottom; bearish when close > top; both also expire by age. Retain records subject to expiry retention and capacity.
Calculation: Row samples = independently evaluate every configured structure-row timeframe, whether higher than, equal to, or lower than the chart timeframe. Sample the last completed bar; for a lower timeframe use the last completed intrabar in each chart bar. Whenever the sampled close changes, append the previous chart bar's sampled high and low to that row's bounded buffers. Thus buffer updates depend on close changes, not merely timeframe boundaries.
Calculation: Row structure = bullish when the latest two buffered highs and latest two buffered lows both rise; bearish when both fall; otherwise neutral. Weighted structure = weighted average of row directions, assigning bullish +1, bearish -1 and neutral 0; earlier rows receive heavier weights, with equal weights for the final shorter-horizon rows. Weights remain attached to row positions when timeframes are reassigned.
Calculation: Row range = maximum buffered high and minimum buffered low. With sufficient samples and a positive range, row position = (chart close - range low) / (range high - range low). Chart position uses the latest accepted chart swing high and low in the same formula. Discount lies below the lower equilibrium boundary; premium lies above the upper equilibrium boundary. Lower OTE and low-proximity regions lie within discount; upper OTE and high-proximity regions lie within premium.
Calculation: Row reclaim = compare chart close with the current row range each bar. Transition from below the range into the inclusive range sets bullish reclaim; transition from above into the range sets bearish reclaim. Clear both flags whenever close leaves the range; otherwise retain them.
Calculation: Row support/resistance = order-block support exists when a buffered low < chart close; resistance exists when a buffered high > chart close. A bullish row gap exists when the newest buffered low > the third-newest buffered high AND newest low < chart close; bearish requires newest high < third-newest low AND newest high > chart close. Breaker support is the nearest buffered high below close; breaker resistance is the nearest buffered low above close. Select the nearer breaker side; an equal-distance tie selects resistance for bearish context bias, otherwise support.
Calculation: Row trend direction = chart structure direction when either recent winning BOS/MSS exists AND the sampled row close is displaced from its own high-low midpoint by at least an ATR-scaled tolerance in the chart-structure direction, OR a winning BOS/CHoCH exists within a shorter recency window AND the sampled close lies strictly on the opposite side of its midpoint; otherwise neutral. Use current chart ATR for the tolerance.
Calculation: Continuation direction = context bias when known, otherwise the sign of weighted structure. Its direction is independent of score tier.
Calculation: Reversal direction = the latest validation direction when it is CHoCH, MSS or breaker retest, is opposite a known context bias, and remains within both validation freshness and an independent reversal-age limit; additionally the latest sweep must have that same opposite-bias direction, sufficient quality, and age within a sweep-freshness window longer than the sweep confirmation window. Otherwise neutral. Its direction while these gates pass is independent of score components.
Calculation: Body fraction = absolute(close - open) / (high - low), or zero for a zero-range bar.
Calculation: Active major session = chart-bar datetime falls within an enabled major-session window in its configured timezone, excluding the lunch window.
