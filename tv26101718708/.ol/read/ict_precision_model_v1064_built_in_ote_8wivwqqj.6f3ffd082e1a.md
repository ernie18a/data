Calculation: Prior-day high/low and prior-week high/low = high/low of the last completed daily or weekly bar (higher timeframe than chart); compare current chart-bar extremes and close against these levels.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks; retain the latest. Relative equal highs/lows = mean of the latest two pivot highs/lows when their difference is within a tick tolerance; reset equal levels each day. A sweep requires the chart-bar high/low to exceed a level by a tick buffer and the same bar to close back below/above it.
Calculation: 15-minute swing highs/lows and relative equal highs/lows = the same pivot and equal-level calculations on 15-minute bars (higher or lower than chart as applicable); request the pivot series with lookahead enabled, so the 15-minute bar may still be forming. Retain latest levels and reset equal levels each day.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two bars earlier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two bars earlier; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. Apply the same gap test to 15-minute, 60-minute, and 240-minute bars (higher or lower than chart as applicable), using the requested bar that may still be forming. Retain the latest gap per direction, preferring 240-minute, then 60-minute, then 15-minute when multiple gaps appear. A gap is tapped when the chart bar overlaps its zone; invalidate a bullish gap when close is below its lower bound and a bearish gap when close is above its upper bound.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity = a sweep of prior-day low, prior-week low, latest chart-timeframe swing low, prior session low, relative equal low, latest 15-minute swing low, or 15-minute relative equal low, OR a tap of an active bullish higher-timeframe gap. Bearish liquidity = the corresponding high-side sweeps or a tap of an active bearish higher-timeframe gap. Major bullish/bearish liquidity is the same test excluding chart-timeframe swing, session, and equal-level sweeps.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish trend = EMA(20) >/< EMA(50) and close above/below EMA(20). Strong body = absolute close-open > average absolute body over 20 bars times a multiplier. Bullish/bearish structure = close above/below the highest high/lowest low of the preceding swing-length bars, and low/high above/below the preceding lowest low/highest high. Bullish/bearish impulse = matching trend and structure, strong body, and 10-bar high-low range > 1.8 ATR(14). Bullish/bearish correction = matching trend, not matching impulse, close above/below EMA(50), and body < average body. Chop state means neither impulse nor correction.
Calculation: Deep chop = 12-bar high-low range < average 30-bar candle range times a multiplier, ATR(14) < its 50-bar average times 0.85, absolute close minus close five bars ago < ATR(14) times 0.6, and current body < average body times 0.75, while in chop state. Light chop uses the same consolidation test while not in chop state.
Calculation: Setup state sequence = bullish/bearish manipulation after matching liquidity, then a body shift, displacement, gap, and retracement. Bullish manipulation stores that bar's high/low when bullish liquidity occurs with market state <= 0; bearish manipulation stores them when bearish liquidity occurs with market state >= 0. Allow manipulation only during the stated trade windows, below the daily signal limit, outside deep chop, and outside the manipulation cooldown. In bullish manipulation, store the open of a bearish candle whose low is at least the manipulation low and whose high is at most 1.01 times the manipulation high; bullish shift is a later bullish close above that open. Bearish manipulation stores the open of a bullish candle whose high is at most the manipulation high and whose low is at least 0.99 times the manipulation low; bearish shift is a later bearish close below that open. Displacement then requires matching candle direction, strong body, and close beyond the prior swing extreme. Store displacement high/low. After displacement, wait for a matching chart-timeframe fair value gap and store its bounds.
Calculation: Bullish retracement levels = displacement high minus (displacement high minus manipulation low) times 0.391, 0.500, 0.659, 0.689, 0.705, and 0.790; bearish levels = displacement low plus (manipulation high minus displacement low) times those factors. Bullish OTE zone spans levels at 0.689 and 0.790; bearish OTE zone spans levels at 0.689 and 0.790. A level is hit when the bar range contains it. Fib tapped means any of the 0.391, 0.659, or OTE-zone levels was hit since setup formation.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish micro confirmation = bullish/bearish candle close beyond the 0.500 retracement level. Bullish/bearish rejection = body <= 45% of candle range, corresponding lower/upper wick >= 1.5 times body, bullish/bearish candle, and close above prior high/below prior low. Bullish/bearish BOS = close above/below the prior swing extreme with a strong body. True bullish/bearish reversal = matching liquidity, candle direction, BOS, and strong body.
Calculation: Setup-zone retest count increments on each new entry into the stored gap zone. B+ retest = first retest while in the B+ time window and before any signal that day; A+ retest = second or later retest. Zone tapped means either retest condition.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish score = 3 for matching liquidity + 3 for major matching liquidity + 2 for matching equal-level sweep + 2 during A+ time windows (otherwise 1 during B+ window, else 0) + 2 when matching setup state has reached its gap + 3 for OTE hit (otherwise 2 for 0.659 hit, otherwise 1 for 0.391 hit) + 2 for matching micro confirmation or rejection + 1 for matching trend + 2 for matching impulse (-2 for opposite impulse; otherwise 0) + 1 on 1- or 3-minute chart (0 on chart timeframes of at least 5 minutes) - 5 for deep chop (-2 for light chop; otherwise 0).
Calculation: Pivot score = 3 + 3 for major matching liquidity + 2 for matching equal-level sweep + 2 during A+ time (otherwise 1) + 1 for matching trend + 1 on 1- or 3-minute chart.
Calculation: Recent opposite signal blocks a signal for the flip cooldown. A blocked long is allowed when close exceeds the high of the most recent short signal, body > 1.2 times average body, and candle is bullish; a blocked short is allowed when close is below the low of the most recent long signal, body > 1.2 times average body, and candle is bearish.
Calculation: Daily/session midpoint = midpoint of current day's running high/low; both running ranges initialize on a new day. Trade windows use New York time: Asia 19:30–23:00, London 02:00–05:00, Judas 09:00–09:30, NY open 09:30–10:05, ten-AM 09:50–10:15, continuation 10:30–11:30, B+ 10:30–14:30; cutoff is before 14:30. A+ time is Asia, London, Judas, NY open, or ten-AM. Key trade time is cutoff and any listed window. Reset daily signal count; maximum signals per day applies. A setup expires after the maximum wait; manipulation cooldown applies after each manipulation.
Long: key trade time AND daily signal count below maximum AND NOT deep chop AND ( (setup state is bullish gap AND stored manipulation low < daily midpoint OR session midpoint AND fib tapped AND bullish rejection AND true bullish reversal AND bullish score >= 7) OR (setup state is bullish gap AND stored manipulation low < daily midpoint OR session midpoint AND bullish gap-zone retest AND bullish micro confirmation AND bullish score >= 7) OR (market state is neutral AND bullish liquidity AND (low < daily midpoint OR session midpoint) AND close > open AND close > prior high AND lower wick >= 1.2 times body AND NOT deep chop AND pivot score >= 7) OR (market state is neutral AND NOT deep chop AND NOT in correction AND bullish impulse AND bullish trend AND bullish structure AND bullish continuation-zone retest after its displacement bar AND close > open AND close > continuation-zone midpoint AND close > daily midpoint) ) AND (no recent short signal OR bullish flip allowed). For bullish setup confirmation, precedence is reversal, then regular confirmation; pivot has precedence over both, and continuation follows them.
Short: key trade time AND daily signal count below maximum AND NOT deep chop AND ( (setup state is bearish gap AND stored manipulation high > daily midpoint OR session midpoint AND fib tapped AND bearish rejection AND true bearish reversal AND bearish score >= 7) OR (setup state is bearish gap AND stored manipulation high > daily midpoint OR session midpoint AND bearish gap-zone retest AND bearish micro confirmation AND bearish score >= 7) OR (market state is neutral AND bearish liquidity AND (high > daily midpoint OR session midpoint) AND close < open AND close < prior low AND upper wick >= 1.2 times body AND NOT deep chop AND pivot score >= 7) OR (market state is neutral AND NOT deep chop AND NOT in correction AND bearish impulse AND bearish trend AND bearish structure AND bearish continuation-zone retest after its displacement bar AND close < open AND close < continuation-zone midpoint AND close < daily midpoint) ) AND (no recent long signal OR bearish flip allowed). Continuation displacement is recorded only during the continuation window, with matching impulse/trend/structure, candle direction, body > 2 times average body, and close above/below daily midpoint; its zone spans the displacement candle open and low (bullish) or high and open (bearish). Limit the B+ retest path to the first daily signal.
