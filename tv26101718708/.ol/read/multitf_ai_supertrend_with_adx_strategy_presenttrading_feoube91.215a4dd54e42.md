Calculation: First and second SuperTrend direction = on each chart bar, form bands around a selectable moving average of higher-timeframe close × chart volume divided by the same moving average of chart volume, using independently configured lengths; add/subtract the higher-timeframe ATR × its multiplier. Ratchet the lower band upward unless the prior chart close is below the prior lower band, and ratchet the upper band downward unless the prior chart close is above the prior upper band. If prior ATR is unavailable, direction = 1; otherwise, if prior SuperTrend equals the prior upper band, direction = -1 when chart close > upper band, else 1; otherwise direction = 1 when chart close < lower band, else -1. SuperTrend = lower band when direction = -1, otherwise upper band. Both source timeframes are higher than the chart timeframe; use the latest completed higher-timeframe bar (the value updates on the closing chart bar of that interval). The two instances use separate settings.
Calculation: First and second KNN trend label = for each instance, label each of the n most recent SuperTrend samples 1 when WMA(chart close, price lookback) > WMA(that SuperTrend, prediction lookback), else 0. Rank samples by absolute distance from the current SuperTrend, take the k nearest, and compute the inverse-distance-weighted mean of their labels. The label is 1 if that mean equals 1, and 0 if it equals 0. Each instance has independently configured k, n, and lookbacks.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe +DI, -DI, and ADX = from higher-timeframe high, low, and close, compute positive and negative directional movement by comparing the current high rise with the prior low fall (retain the larger positive movement, else zero); divide each movement's WMA by the WMA of true range and multiply by 100. DX = 100 × abs(+DI - -DI) / (+DI + -DI); ADX = WMA(DX). Use the latest completed higher-timeframe bar (the value updates on the closing chart bar of that interval).
Calculation: Bullish SuperTrend signal = that instance's direction changes from 1 to -1 while its KNN trend label is 1. Bearish SuperTrend signal = direction changes from -1 to 1 while its KNN trend label is 0.
Calculation: First SuperTrend trailing stop = first SuperTrend - its higher-timeframe ATR × its multiplier. Short trailing stop = first SuperTrend + its higher-timeframe ATR × its multiplier.
Long: First SuperTrend direction = -1 AND first KNN trend label = 1 AND second SuperTrend direction = -1 AND second KNN trend label = 1 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > its threshold AND +DI > -DI)
Long: First KNN trend label = 1 AND previous first KNN trend label != 1 OR second KNN trend label = 1 AND previous second KNN trend label != 1 OR first SuperTrend bullish signal OR second SuperTrend bullish signal
Short: First SuperTrend direction = 1 AND first KNN trend label = 0 AND second SuperTrend direction = 1 AND second KNN trend label = 0 AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > its threshold AND -DI > +DI)
Short: First KNN trend label = 0 AND previous first KNN trend label != 0 OR second KNN trend label = 0 AND previous second KNN trend label != 0 OR first SuperTrend bearish signal OR second SuperTrend bearish signal
Exit: long, neither SuperTrend has a bullish signal AND chart low <= first SuperTrend trailing stop
Exit: short, neither SuperTrend has a bearish signal AND chart high >= short trailing stop
