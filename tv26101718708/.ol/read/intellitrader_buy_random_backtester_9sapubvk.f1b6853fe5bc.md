Calculation: Highest high since long entry = maximum high from the entry bar through the current bar.
Calculation: Configured-aggregation price-change value = current close − (previous close / previous close) × 100, calculated on the latest available bar of each aggregation above or below the chart timeframe; use the still-forming bar when available, otherwise the last completed bar.
Long: Datetime is within the configured backtest window AND, if the price-change filter is enabled, each configured-aggregation price-change value is within its configured minimum and maximum AND, for each enabled indicator filter on each configured aggregation above or below the chart timeframe, the indicator is strictly between its configured minimum and maximum; use the latest available bar (still-forming when available, otherwise the last completed bar), and if that filter's rising option is enabled, its current value is greater than its previous value. Indicators are RSI with shorter and longer lookbacks, Stochastic, StochRSI, and CCI. All enabled filters must pass.
Exit: Long, datetime is within the configured backtest window AND ((take-profit is enabled AND highest high since long entry is at least entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) AND low is at most highest high since long entry × (1 − trailing-profit percentage capped at take-profit percentage)) OR (stop-loss is enabled AND low is at most entry price × (1 − stop-loss percentage)) OR (trailing stop is enabled AND low is at most highest high since long entry × (1 − trailing-stop percentage))).
