Calculation: ATR = average true range on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured number of bars on both sides; use the most recently confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Coil range = highest high minus lowest low over the configured coil lookback, excluding the current bar; compressed when coil range / ATR is at or below the coil threshold.
Calculation: Propulsion strength = round to 0.1 and clamp to 0–10 the sum of: range/ATR points (2.5 at ≥2.5, 1.9 at ≥1.8, 1.3 at ≥1.3, 0.8 at ≥1.0, otherwise 0.3); body/range points (1.5 at ≥0.85, 1.1 at ≥0.70, 0.7 at ≥0.55, otherwise 0.3); oriented close-in-range points (1.0 at ≥0.85, 0.7 at ≥0.70, 0.4 at ≥0.60, otherwise 0); absolute net close displacement over the momentum window, in ATR, points (1.0 at ≥3, 0.7 at ≥2, 0.4 at ≥1.2, otherwise 0); consecutive same-direction close points (0.5 at ≥4, 0.3 at ≥3, otherwise 0); volume / volume moving-average points (1.5 at ≥2.5, 1.1 at ≥2, 0.7 at ≥1.5, 0.4 at ≥1.3, otherwise 0); 0.5 if directional acceleration is positive; 0.8 if compressed; 0.7 if the corresponding swing is broken; and trend-alignment points (0.5 if both chart EMA slope and higher-timeframe alignment agree, 0.25 if one agrees, otherwise 0). Directional acceleration means the 3-bar close change has the signal direction and exceeds the prior 3-bar close change in that direction. The chart EMA slope compares its current value with its value two bars earlier.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = current close above (bullish) or below (bearish) the EMA computed on the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Risk and targets at entry = entry is signal-bar close; choose the launch low/high over the momentum window as structural stop anchor, or the signal bar low/high as ignition-bar anchor; offset the anchor by an ATR buffer, then clamp entry-to-stop distance between an ATR floor and ceiling. Place three targets in the trade direction at configured R multiples of that distance.
Long: bullish candle AND signal-bar range / ATR ≥ displacement threshold AND body / range ≥ minimum body ratio AND (close − low) / range ≥ close-location threshold AND (structure break not required OR close > last confirmed swing high) AND (coil release not required OR coil is compressed) AND (volume thrust not required OR volume / its moving average ≥ volume multiplier) AND bullish propulsion strength ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only filter off OR bullish strength ≥ strong threshold) AND higher-timeframe alignment bullish AND (cooldown off OR bars since prior entry ≥ cooldown) AND no active trade AND (short not eligible OR bullish strength ≥ bearish strength).
Short: bearish candle AND signal-bar range / ATR ≥ displacement threshold AND body / range ≥ minimum body ratio AND (high − close) / range ≥ close-location threshold AND (structure break not required OR close < last confirmed swing low) AND (coil release not required OR coil is compressed) AND (volume thrust not required OR volume / its moving average ≥ volume multiplier) AND bearish propulsion strength ≥ minimum strength AND (strong-only filter off OR bearish strength ≥ strong threshold) AND higher-timeframe alignment bearish AND (cooldown off OR bars since prior entry ≥ cooldown) AND no active trade AND (long not eligible OR bearish strength > bullish strength).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop OR (high ≥ target 3 AND low > stop) OR (stop is hit after target 1 or target 2 was reached on an earlier bar).
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop OR (low ≤ target 3 AND high < stop) OR (stop is hit after target 1 or target 2 was reached on an earlier bar).
