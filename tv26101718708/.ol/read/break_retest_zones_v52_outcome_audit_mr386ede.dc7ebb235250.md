Calculation: Support/resistance zones = confirmed swing-high zones span from the pivot high down by the smaller of the pivot candle’s high-low range and a configured ATR-based maximum height; swing-low zones span from the pivot low up by the smaller of the pivot candle’s high-low range and that maximum. Use configurable left/right pivot lookbacks. Reject overlapping zones; initialize each zone with one touch. If a newly created zone is already crossed by the close, arm it as broken in that direction.
Calculation: Zone state = each zone is live, armed long after a close crosses above its top, armed short after a close crosses below its bottom, failed, or consumed. A live-zone touch increments its touch count when the bar overlaps the zone, closes outside it, and is sufficiently separated from the previous touch. A break records its bar, breakout volume relative to the longer-lookback average volume, whether a qualifying opposite-side liquidity sweep occurred within its lookback and age limits, whether the breakout body meets its ATR threshold, and whether a directional fair-value gap occurred. While armed and before confirmation, accumulate pullback volume and record any directional fair-value gap. Fail an armed long if close falls below the zone bottom; fail an armed short if close rises above its top. Expire an armed setup after its retest window or if price closes beyond the zone by its configured ATR-based runaway distance. A failed zone returns to live after its cooldown. Enforce the configured maximum zones per side, removing the oldest live zone first.
Calculation: Previous higher-timeframe and daily highs/lows = highs and lows of the last completed bar on each respective higher timeframe; use the daily levels only where specified below.
Calculation: Breakout grade = 2 points if any previous higher-timeframe or daily high/low lies within the zone expanded by an ATR-based tolerance; 1 point if breakout volume is at least a configured multiple of the longer-lookback average volume; 1 point if average pullback volume is below breakout volume; and 1 point each for a qualifying liquidity sweep, ATR-sized breakout body, and directional fair-value gap during the breakout leg or retest. Map the total to configured grade ranks and require the configured minimum rank.
Calculation: Stop = beyond the far edge of the zone by a configured tick offset. Target 1 = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the entry-to-stop risk multiplied by a configured reward multiple.
Long: A live resistance zone’s close crosses above its top (current close > zone top AND previous close <= zone top) OR (a long-armed zone has elapsed more than the configured minimum gap after its break AND current low <= zone top AND current close > zone top AND touch count >= configured minimum AND current time is in an allowed session AND elapsed bars <= the retest window AND breakout grade rank >= configured minimum AND (room filter disabled OR previous higher-timeframe high is absent OR is at/below entry OR is at least configured risk distance above entry)). For the armed-zone entry condition, entry is current close; reject if the zone has failed or expired or the runaway condition is met.
Short: A live support zone’s close crosses below its bottom (current close < zone bottom AND previous close >= zone bottom) OR (a short-armed zone has elapsed more than the configured minimum gap after its break AND current high >= zone bottom AND current close < zone bottom AND touch count >= configured minimum AND current time is in an allowed session AND elapsed bars <= the retest window AND breakout grade rank >= configured minimum AND (room filter disabled OR previous higher-timeframe low is absent OR is at/above entry OR is at least configured risk distance below entry)). For the armed-zone entry condition, entry is current close; reject if the zone has failed or expired or the runaway condition is met.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR (high >= target 1 AND low > stop)
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR (low <= target 1 AND high < stop)
