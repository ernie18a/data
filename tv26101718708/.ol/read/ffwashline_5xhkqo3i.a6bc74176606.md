Calculation: Confirmed swing levels = chart-timeframe pivot highs and lows confirmed after the pivot-length bars to the right; discard levels older than the configured maximum age or closed through (high level: close > level; low level: close < level). Track each level’s touches when the bar’s extreme comes within the configured ATR touch tolerance, and its obviousness as 0.40 × clamp(touches/3, 0, 1) + 0.35 × (1 − clamp(age/maximum level age, 0, 1)) + 0.25 if it is the newest level, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Poke = for a tracked high, high − level > ATR × minimum pierce depth, close ≤ level, and upper-tail/range ≥ minimum tail ratio; for a tracked low, level − low > ATR × minimum pierce depth, close ≥ level, and lower-tail/range ≥ minimum tail ratio. If multiple levels on a side qualify, use the highest high level or lowest low level and count all qualifying levels as the cluster. Use confirmed bars only.
Calculation: Wash line = a qualifying high poke creates a bearish line at its selected level, with spike at that bar’s high; a qualifying low poke creates a bullish line at its selected level, with spike at that bar’s low. Store poke ATR, tail ratio, cluster count, origin level’s obviousness, and poke bar. On later bars, retire the line if its configured stop is hit (wick or close, as configured), if closes beyond its level reach the configured breach limit, or if its age exceeds its lifetime. A bearish line’s stop is spike + current ATR × stop buffer; a bullish line’s stop is spike − current ATR × stop buffer. Count a retest only after maximum favorable departure from the level reaches the configured minimum departure × poke ATR; retest is high ≥ level − current ATR × touch tolerance for bearish lines, or low ≤ level + current ATR × touch tolerance for bullish lines, provided close has not passed beyond the level. Track retest count, most recent retest bar, maximum favorable departure, and breach count.
Calculation: Wash-line score = 100 × (weighted mean of: tail score clamp((tail ratio − minimum tail ratio)/(full-tail ratio − minimum tail ratio), 0, 1); stored level obviousness; cluster score 1 for ≥3, 0.7 for 2, else 0.4; departure score clamp(maximum departure/(configured full-departure ATR × poke ATR), 0, 1); retest score 1 for ≥2, 0.7 for 1, else 0.25; and room score clamp(room in risk units/minimum target R, 0, 1)) × freshness multiplier × breach multiplier. Room is the farthest favorable distance to a tracked opposing swing divided by risk; risk is max(abs(level − stop), minimum tick). Freshness multiplier decreases linearly from 1 to the configured freshness floor over line lifetime; breach multiplier is configured breach penalty raised to breach count. Use the configured weights, with a zero-weight-sum denominator floored above zero.
Calculation: Trigger pattern = inside bar (high ≤ prior high and low ≥ prior low), or rejection tail (upper-tail/range for bearish, lower-tail/range for bullish ≥ configured trigger tail ratio), or outside bar (high > prior high and low < prior low) closing in the trade direction (close < open bearish; close > open bullish). Inside-bar and rejection-tail patterns require a retest on this bar; outside-bar pattern requires a retest on this bar or the prior bar.
Long: qualifying low poke OR an active bullish wash line has a trigger pattern, retest count ≥ configured minimum, score ≥ configured minimum, and trigger cooldown elapsed.
Short: qualifying high poke OR an active bearish wash line has a trigger pattern, retest count ≥ configured minimum, score ≥ configured minimum, and trigger cooldown elapsed.
