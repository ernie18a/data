Calculation: Session VWAP = session-anchored volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3
Long: Close crosses above session VWAP AND, within the configured bar window from that cross, at least one low is at or below current session VWAP AND close > session VWAP AND close > open AND (rejection filter disabled OR lower wick > candle body × configured ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its configurable-lookback simple moving average × configured factor) AND (distance filter disabled OR |close − session VWAP| > configured multiple × ATR) AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close > session VWAP from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND position is flat AND daily trade limit is not reached AND entry is within the configured session and before its end AND no short signal is simultaneously true
Short: Close crosses below session VWAP AND, within the configured bar window from that cross, at least one high is at or above current session VWAP AND close < session VWAP AND close < open AND (rejection filter disabled OR upper wick > candle body × configured ratio) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ its configurable-lookback simple moving average × configured factor) AND (distance filter disabled OR |close − session VWAP| > configured multiple × ATR) AND (higher-timeframe bias disabled OR close < session VWAP from the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND position is flat AND daily trade limit is not reached AND entry is within the configured session and before its end AND no long signal is simultaneously true
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − configured stop distance OR high ≥ entry price + configured target distance OR bar crosses the configured session close time
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + configured stop distance OR low ≤ entry price − configured target distance OR bar crosses the configured session close time
