Calculation: Previous-day high = highest high of the previous trading day, including extended-hours bars
Calculation: Previous-day low = lowest low of the previous trading day, including extended-hours bars
Calculation: Premarket high = highest high of the current premarket session, fixed at market open
Calculation: Premarket low = lowest low of the current premarket session, fixed at market open
Long: close >= previous-day high OR close >= premarket high
Short: close <= previous-day low OR close <= premarket low
