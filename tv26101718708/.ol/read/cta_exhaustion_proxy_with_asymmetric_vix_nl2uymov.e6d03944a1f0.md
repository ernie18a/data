Calculation: Fast, medium, and slow moving averages = moving averages of close with progressively longer lookbacks
Calculation: CTA score = sum of +1 for each moving average below close and -1 for each moving average at or above close
Calculation: Extreme duration = consecutive chart bars with CTA score equal to +3 or -3; reset to zero otherwise
Calculation: Two-bar score change = current CTA score minus CTA score two chart bars earlier
Calculation: Strong bullish candle = close > open, high > low, and (close - open) / (high - low) >= body threshold
Calculation: Strong bearish candle = close < open, high > low, and (open - close) / (high - low) >= body threshold
Calculation: Volatility index = close of VIX, or front-month VIX futures close if VIX is unavailable, from higher-timeframe bars using the still-forming bar
Long: (CTA score one chart bar earlier = -3 AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier >= long-duration threshold AND close > fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier <= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND volatility index > long threshold) OR (CTA score one or two chart bars earlier = -3 AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier >= short-duration threshold AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier < long-duration threshold AND two-bar score change >= 2 AND close > fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier <= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND strong bullish candle AND volatility index > long threshold) OR (CTA score one chart bar earlier <= -2 AND two-bar score change >= 2 AND close > fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier <= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND volatility index > long threshold)
Short: (CTA score one chart bar earlier = +3 AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier >= long-duration threshold AND close < fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier >= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND volatility index < short threshold) OR (CTA score one or two chart bars earlier = +3 AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier >= short-duration threshold AND extreme duration one chart bar earlier < long-duration threshold AND two-bar score change <= -2 AND close < fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier >= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND strong bearish candle AND volatility index < short threshold) OR (CTA score one chart bar earlier >= +2 AND two-bar score change <= -2 AND close < fast moving average AND close one chart bar earlier >= fast moving average one chart bar earlier AND volatility index < short threshold)
