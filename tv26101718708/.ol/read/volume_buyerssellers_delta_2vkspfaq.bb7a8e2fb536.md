Calculation: Buyer percentage = 100 × rolling sum of buyer-attributed volume / rolling sum of buyer-attributed plus seller-attributed volume over the same lookback; per bar, buyer-attributed volume = volume × (bullish body fraction + 0.5 × upper-wick fraction), where bullish body fraction = abs(close − open)/(high − low) when close ≥ open, otherwise 0, and upper-wick fraction = (high − max(open, close))/(high − low); when high = low, bullish body fraction = 0.5 and upper-wick fraction = 0.25
Calculation: Seller percentage = 100 × rolling sum of seller-attributed volume / rolling sum of buyer-attributed plus seller-attributed volume over the same lookback; per bar, seller-attributed volume = volume × (bearish body fraction + 0.5 × lower-wick fraction), where bearish body fraction = abs(close − open)/(high − low) when close < open, otherwise 0, and lower-wick fraction = (min(open, close) − low)/(high − low); when high = low, bearish body fraction = 0 and lower-wick fraction = 0.25
Calculation: Delta percentage = buyer percentage − seller percentage
Calculation: Consecutive bullish-bar count = number of consecutive bars with close ≥ open, reset by a bar with close < open or at session start
Calculation: Consecutive bearish-bar count = number of consecutive bars with close < open, reset by a bar with close ≥ open or at session start
Long: buyer percentage > strong-buying threshold OR delta percentage > bullish-delta threshold OR buyer percentage ≥ P1 threshold OR low < lowest low over the divergence lookback AND delta percentage > delta percentage from the divergence lookback bars ago AND delta percentage < 0 OR consecutive bullish-bar count > 2 OR consecutive bullish-bar count ≥ sustained-buying threshold
Short: seller percentage > strong-selling threshold OR delta percentage < bearish-delta threshold OR seller percentage ≥ P1 threshold OR high > highest high over the divergence lookback AND delta percentage < delta percentage from the divergence lookback bars ago AND delta percentage > 0 OR consecutive bearish-bar count > 2 OR consecutive bearish-bar count ≥ sustained-selling threshold
