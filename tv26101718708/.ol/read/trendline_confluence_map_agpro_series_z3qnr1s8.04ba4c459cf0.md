Calculation: ATR = average true range.
Calculation: Qualifying confluence zone = on a confirmed bar, cluster projected, touch-validated trendlines from consecutive major or minor swing pivots with recent major-pivot horizontal support/resistance levels whose prices are within zone-width × ATR; require at least the minimum source count and one trendline. Score each cluster as the average across its sources of touch score (40 for ≥4 touches, 25 for 3, 10 for 2, otherwise 0) + age score (min(20, age/8)) + angle score (5 if |slope|/ATR < 0.05, 10 if < 0.1, 15 if ≤ 0.6, 10 if ≤ 1, otherwise 5), plus volume score (15 if volume/20-bar volume average ≥1.8, 12 if ≥1.4, 9 if ≥1.1, 6 if ≥0.8, otherwise 3; default 7.5 when unavailable) and 10 if a horizontal level is within 0.5 × ATR of the cluster midpoint. The highest-scoring eligible cluster is support-biased when upward sources outnumber downward sources, resistance-biased when downward sources outnumber upward sources, and otherwise resistance-biased when close is below its midpoint.
Long: confirmed bar AND highest-scoring eligible cluster score ≥ minimum zone score AND support-biased AND no active support-biased zone within 1.2 × ATR.
Short: confirmed bar AND highest-scoring eligible cluster score ≥ minimum zone score AND resistance-biased AND no active resistance-biased zone within 1.2 × ATR.
