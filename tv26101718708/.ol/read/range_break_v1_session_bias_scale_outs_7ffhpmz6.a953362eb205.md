Calculation: Asia range = highest high minus lowest low during the Asia session
Calculation: London move = London session close minus London session open
Calculation: London range = highest high minus lowest low during the London session
Calculation: Average Asia range = average of the longer-lookback completed Asia-session ranges
Calculation: Active range = high and low of the selected completed Asia, London, or New York opening range
Calculation: Regime score = count of: current Asia range / average Asia range <= compression threshold; absolute London move / Asia range >= minimum move threshold; London range / Asia range <= maximum range threshold
Long: Entry window is open AND (session bias is disabled OR (Asia and London sessions are complete AND regime filter is disabled OR regime score >= minimum score) AND London move > 0 AND London move > Asia range * dominance threshold AND (outside-close requirement is disabled OR London close > Asia high) OR neutral-bias longs are enabled and neither bullish nor bearish bias applies) AND (range-size filter is disabled OR active range size is within configured minimum and maximum) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > VWAP) AND position is flat AND (daily trade limit is disabled OR no trade has occurred today) AND selected entry mode condition: (direct break: high > active range high + buffer) OR (retest: a long break has occurred AND low <= active range high + retest tolerance AND high > active range high + buffer) OR (sweep: low has crossed below active range low AND high > active range high + buffer)
Short: Entry window is open AND (session bias is disabled OR (Asia and London sessions are complete AND regime filter is disabled OR regime score >= minimum score) AND London move < 0 AND absolute London move > Asia range * dominance threshold AND (outside-close requirement is disabled OR London close < Asia low) OR neutral-bias shorts are enabled and neither bullish nor bearish bias applies) AND (range-size filter is disabled OR active range size is within configured minimum and maximum) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < VWAP) AND position is flat AND (daily trade limit is disabled OR no trade has occurred today) AND selected entry mode condition: (direct break: low < active range low - buffer) OR (retest: a short break has occurred AND high >= active range low - retest tolerance AND low < active range low - buffer) OR (sweep: high has crossed above active range high AND low < active range low - buffer)
Calculation: Long stop reference = selected active-range low, active-range midpoint, London low, Asia low, or entry price minus configured fixed distance
Calculation: Short stop reference = selected active-range high, active-range midpoint, London high, Asia high, or entry price plus configured fixed distance
Calculation: Long risk = entry price minus long stop reference, valid only when long stop reference < entry price
Calculation: Short risk = short stop reference minus entry price, valid only when short stop reference > entry price
Exit: Long, low <= active long stop (or entry price after TP1 when break-even movement is enabled) OR high >= entry price + long risk * configured target multiple OR high >= entry price + configured fixed target distance OR current bar time equals configured forced-flat time
Exit: Short, high >= active short stop (or entry price after TP1 when break-even movement is enabled) OR low <= entry price - short risk * configured target multiple OR low <= entry price - configured fixed target distance OR current bar time equals configured forced-flat time
