Calculation: CTA score = for each of the fast, medium, and slow moving averages, +1 if close is above the average and -1 otherwise, summed; the fast lookback is shorter than the medium lookback, which is shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Extreme streak = consecutive bars with CTA score equal to +3 or -3; reset to zero otherwise
Calculation: Bullish candle body ratio = (close - open) / (high - low), when close > open and high > low
Calculation: Bearish candle body ratio = (open - close) / (high - low), when close < open and high > low
