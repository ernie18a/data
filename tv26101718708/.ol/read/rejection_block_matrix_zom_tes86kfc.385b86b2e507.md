Calculation: Rejection block = A demand block is created at a confirmed pivot low when its lower wick share of the candle range meets the configured minimum, or on a failed sweep when low is below the last confirmed pivot low, close is above that pivot low, and lower wick share meets the minimum. A supply block is created symmetrically at a confirmed pivot high or on a failed sweep above the last confirmed pivot high. Pivot confirmation uses the configured number of bars on both sides; the block uses the pivot candle, while a failed-sweep block uses the current candle. Block height is the extreme-to-body-edge distance clamped between configured minimum and maximum ATR multiples; demand spans from the low to low plus height, supply from high minus height to high.
Calculation: Live block = A rejection block retained within the configured age and block-count limits, unless invalidated by a close below a demand block's bottom or above a supply block's top. A block is touched when high is at least its bottom and low is at most its top, excluding its creation bar.
Long: A live demand block is touched AND close > its top AND close > open
Short: A live supply block is touched AND close < its bottom AND close < open
