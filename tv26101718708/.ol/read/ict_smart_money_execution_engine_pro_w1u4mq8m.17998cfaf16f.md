Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed when the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed when the bars on the right have completed.
Long: Current low > high two bars earlier OR a confirmed pivot low forms using the intermediate-swing or order-block lookback OR current low < the most recently confirmed, unswept pivot low using the liquidity lookback AND current close > that pivot low OR current close crosses above the most recently confirmed pivot high using the structure lookback
