Calculation: Anchored VWAP and volume-weighted standard deviation = since the selected anchor, sum(source × volume)/sum(volume) and sqrt(max(sum(source² × volume)/sum(volume) − VWAP², 0)); source is a selected bar price (close, hlc3, or ohlc4). The anchor is selected by confirmed swing pivots, daily session, or a configured date.
Calculation: Reclaim follow-through probability = logistic probability from a linear model with an intercept and six side-oriented features: clipped (close minus close at a shorter lookback) / ATR; clipped (shorter-lookback EMA minus longer-lookback EMA) / ATR; clipped (RSI minus its midpoint) / its scale; clipped (volume-weighted standard deviation / its moving average minus one); clipped (volume / its moving average minus one); clipped anchor age. Train online on each confirmed VWAP cross: success if the close moves the configured ATR multiple in the cross direction before closing back across VWAP; otherwise failure on that recross or at the configured horizon. Update logistic weights by gradient descent using the saved cross-bar features. Predict using current features and weights.
Long: close > anchored VWAP AND previous close <= previous anchored VWAP AND bars since start exceed the configured warmup AND reclaim follow-through probability >= configured minimum.
Short: close < anchored VWAP AND previous close >= previous anchored VWAP AND bars since start exceed the configured warmup AND reclaim follow-through probability >= configured minimum.
