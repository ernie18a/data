Calculation: 47-period simple moving average = simple moving average of close, using the same completed calculation bars as the 9-period exponential moving average; use lower, equal, or higher bars according to their relationship to the chart timeframe.
Calculation: 9-period exponential moving average = exponential moving average of close over a shorter lookback than the 47-period simple moving average; use the same completed calculation bars as the 47-period simple moving average.
Calculation: 3 AM anchored VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset on the first calculation bar in the 3 AM hour and carried forward until the next reset; use completed calculation bars.
Calculation: Cross-anchored VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset on each 47-period simple moving average / 3 AM anchored VWAP cross and carried forward until the next cross; use completed calculation bars.
Calculation: Prior lookback high/low = highest high and lowest low over the configured number of bars before the current bar.
Long: 47-period simple moving average crosses above 3 AM anchored VWAP OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses above 47-period simple moving average OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses above 3 AM anchored VWAP OR low < prior lookback low AND close > prior lookback low OR, after a cross arms a setup, close > 47-period simple moving average AND close > cross-anchored VWAP on the same completed bar; enter at the next bar's open only when flat. For a stop-out, re-arm that cross once in the opposite direction. A new cross replaces the armed setup; the 3 AM reset clears it. Do not queue a trigger while a trade is open.
Short: 47-period simple moving average crosses below 3 AM anchored VWAP OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses below 47-period simple moving average OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses below 3 AM anchored VWAP OR high > prior lookback high AND close < prior lookback high OR, after a cross arms a setup, close < 47-period simple moving average AND close < cross-anchored VWAP on the same completed bar; enter at the next bar's open only when flat. For a stop-out, re-arm that cross once in the opposite direction. A new cross replaces the armed setup; the 3 AM reset clears it. Do not queue a trigger while a trade is open.
Long: 9-period exponential moving average crosses above 47-period simple moving average OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses above 3 AM anchored VWAP on a higher timeframe's last completed bar.
Short: 9-period exponential moving average crosses below 47-period simple moving average OR 9-period exponential moving average crosses below 3 AM anchored VWAP on a higher timeframe's last completed bar.
Exit: long, low <= cross candle low OR high >= entry price + configured profit distance.
Exit: short, high >= cross candle high OR low <= entry price - configured profit distance.
Exit: long or short, if stop and profit levels are both touched in the same bar, exit at the stop level.
