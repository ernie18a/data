Calculation: Bullish score = sum of configured points for each true condition: close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high; MFI is above its threshold; smoothed Stoch RSI is below its threshold; short-term SMA of close is above long-term SMA of close.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of configured points for each true condition: close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low; MFI is below its threshold; smoothed Stoch RSI is above its threshold; short-term SMA of close is below long-term SMA of close.
Calculation: Stoch RSI = a short-term SMA of the stochastic of RSI.
Long: bar is confirmed AND bullish score is at least the configured minimum score.
Short: bar is confirmed AND bearish score is at least the configured minimum score.
