Calculation: Pivot = (higher-timeframe still-forming bar high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: First support = 2 × Pivot − higher-timeframe still-forming bar high; first resistance = 2 × Pivot − higher-timeframe still-forming bar low
Calculation: Second support = Pivot − (first resistance − first support); second resistance = Pivot + (first resistance − first support)
Calculation: Third support = higher-timeframe still-forming bar low − 2 × (higher-timeframe still-forming bar high − Pivot); third resistance = higher-timeframe still-forming bar high + 2 × (Pivot − higher-timeframe still-forming bar low)
Long: close > selected resistance level (first, second, or third)
Short: close < selected support level (first, second, or third)
