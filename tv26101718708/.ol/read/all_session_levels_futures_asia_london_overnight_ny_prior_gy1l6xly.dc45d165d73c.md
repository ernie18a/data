Calculation: Asia session high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured Asia session
Calculation: London session high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured London session
Calculation: Overnight high/low = highest high / lowest low during the configured overnight session
Calculation: Average range = simple moving average of high minus low over a lookback
Calculation: Demand zone = prior bar's low to the higher of its open and close, created when the prior bar's absolute body is below the average range times the base-size parameter and the current bar closes above its open, has a range above the average range times the impulse-strength parameter, and its body exceeds half its range
Calculation: Supply zone = the lower of the prior bar's open and close to the prior bar's high, created when the prior bar's absolute body is below the average range times the base-size parameter and the current bar closes below its open, has a range above the average range times the impulse-strength parameter, and its body exceeds half its range
Calculation: An unmitigated demand zone remains active until a bar closes below its bottom; an unmitigated supply zone remains active until a bar closes above its top
Long: (close > London session high AND previous close <= London session high) OR (close < London session low AND previous close >= London session low) OR (close > Asia session high AND previous close <= Asia session high) OR (close < Asia session low AND previous close >= Asia session low) OR (close > Overnight high AND previous close <= Overnight high) OR (close < Overnight low AND previous close >= Overnight low) OR (zones enabled AND the bar's low <= the latest unmitigated demand zone's top AND the bar's high >= its bottom)
Short: (close < London session low AND previous close >= London session low) OR (close > London session high AND previous close <= London session high) OR (close < Asia session low AND previous close >= Asia session low) OR (close > Asia session high AND previous close <= Asia session high) OR (close < Overnight low AND previous close >= Overnight low) OR (close > Overnight high AND previous close <= Overnight high) OR (zones enabled AND the bar's high >= the latest unmitigated supply zone's bottom AND the bar's low <= its top)
