Calculation: Ergotic MACD = EMA(close, longer lookback) − EMA(close, shorter lookback); signal = EMA(Ergotic MACD, signal lookback)
Calculation: Money flow index = RSI(positive money flow sum, negative money flow sum), where positive money flow sum is the lookback sum of volume × close when close increased, otherwise 0; negative money flow sum is the lookback sum of volume × close when close decreased, otherwise 0
Calculation: Stop price = (high + low + close) / 3 on the configured stop timeframe (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as configured), using the most recent available bar, which may still be forming
Long: datetime is within the configured backtest window AND ((Ergotic MACD < its signal AND direction is not reversed) OR (Ergotic MACD > its signal AND direction is reversed)) AND RSI is not above its configured buy cutoff AND money flow index is not above its configured upper cutoff AND (if enabled, close or its configured EMA is greater than every value in the preceding configured price lookback) AND (if enabled, RSI is greater than every value in the preceding configured RSI lookback) AND (if enabled, money flow index is greater than every value in the preceding configured money flow lookback)
Short: datetime is within the configured backtest window AND ((Ergotic MACD > its signal AND direction is not reversed) OR (Ergotic MACD < its signal AND direction is reversed)) AND RSI is not below its configured sell cutoff AND money flow index is not below its configured lower cutoff AND (if enabled, close or its configured EMA is less than every value in the preceding configured price lookback)
Short: a long position is open AND Stop price has reached the configured activation threshold above entry price AND Stop price has made a new high since activation AND Stop price <= the tracked high × (1 − configured trailing stop percentage)
Exit: long, low <= average entry price × (1 − configured stop-loss percentage) OR high >= average entry price × (1 + configured take-profit percentage)
