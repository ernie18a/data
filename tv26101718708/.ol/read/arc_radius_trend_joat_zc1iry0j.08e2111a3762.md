Calculation: Curved radius = ATR × base multiplier × (1 + min(|acceleration / ATR|, 2))^curve strength, where acceleration is the EMA of the change in the EMA of close changes; both EMAs use the same lookback.
Calculation: Upper/lower bands = ratcheted bands initialized at hl2 ± curved radius; thereafter the upper band is max(hl2 + curved radius, prior upper band) if prior close > prior upper band, otherwise hl2 + curved radius; the lower band is min(hl2 − curved radius, prior lower band) if prior close < prior lower band, otherwise hl2 − curved radius.
Calculation: Trend state = bullish if close > prior upper band, bearish if close < prior lower band, otherwise the prior trend state (initially bullish).
Calculation: JOAT expansion score = EMA over 5 bars of (45 × signed efficiency + 25 × (1 − binary up/down close-change entropy) + clamped participation score − clamped liquidity penalty). Signed efficiency = (close − close N bars ago) / sum of absolute close changes over N bars. Participation score = clamp((volume / max(SMA(volume, impact length), 1) − 1) × 15 + (|close − open| / max(high − low, minimum tick)) × (2 × (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick) − 1) × 15, −25, 25). Liquidity penalty = clamp((impact ratio − 1) × 15, −20, 20); impact ratio = impact / SMA(impact, impact length), or 1 if the SMA is zero; impact = EMA over impact length of (absolute log close return / max(close × volume, 1)) × 100000000.
Long: Trend state is bullish OR JOAT expansion score > 12 OR (JOAT expansion score > 12 AND prior JOAT expansion score ≤ 12).
Short: Trend state is bearish OR JOAT expansion score < −12 OR (JOAT expansion score < −12 AND prior JOAT expansion score ≥ −12).
