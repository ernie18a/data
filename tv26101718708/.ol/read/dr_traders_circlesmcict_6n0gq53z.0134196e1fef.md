Calculation: Displacement candle = real body exceeds the average real body over a lookback, and each wick is less than a fraction of the body
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is an extreme over a lookback to its left and one bar to its right
Calculation: Bullish/bearish fair value gap = the current low is above the high two bars earlier / the current high is below the low two bars earlier, with the immediately preceding bar a bullish/bearish displacement candle; inverse-gap mode reverses the gap comparisons
Calculation: Bullish volume imbalance = current open > prior close, prior high > current low, current close > prior close, current open > prior open, and prior high < current candle body low
Calculation: Bearish volume imbalance = current open < prior close, prior low < current high, current close < prior close, current open < prior open, and prior low > current candle body high
Long: close breaks above the latest confirmed swing high while structure direction is not bullish (bullish MSS) OR structure direction is bullish and close breaks above a new, not previously marked swing high (bullish BOS) OR close breaks above a confirmed swing high and forms a bullish order block from the lowest candle body/low since that swing OR a bearish order block is invalidated by a confirmed swing low inside it (bullish polarity-change marking) OR bullish fair value gap OR bullish volume imbalance OR bullish displacement candle
Short: close breaks below the latest confirmed swing low while structure direction is not bearish (bearish MSS) OR structure direction is bearish and close breaks below a new, not previously marked swing low (bearish BOS) OR close breaks below a confirmed swing low and forms a bearish order block from the highest candle body/high since that swing OR a bullish order block is invalidated by a confirmed swing high inside it (bearish polarity-change marking) OR bearish fair value gap OR bearish volume imbalance OR bearish displacement candle
