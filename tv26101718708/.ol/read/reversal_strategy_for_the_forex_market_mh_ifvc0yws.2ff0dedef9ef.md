Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a higher-timeframe bar’s high/low is higher/lower than the configured lookback bars on both sides; use the latest such pivot available from the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Apply the same lookbacks on all higher timeframes.
Calculation: Support/resistance = latest confirmed pivot low/high on the higher timeframe; H4 support/resistance is confirmed when within twice the confirmation tolerance of either the corresponding daily or weekly support/resistance.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND position is flat AND support is confirmed AND (current low OR previous bar’s low) <= H4 support + entry buffer AND current close > current open AND current close > previous close AND current close > H4 support AND current close > H4 support - stop buffer
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND position is flat AND resistance is confirmed AND (current high OR previous bar’s high) >= H4 resistance - entry buffer AND current close < current open AND current close < previous close AND current close < H4 resistance AND current close < H4 resistance + stop buffer
Exit: long, current low <= H4 support at entry - stop buffer OR current high >= entry price + (entry price - (H4 support at entry - stop buffer)) × reward/risk ratio OR bar time’s hour and minute equal the configured daily close time
Exit: short, current high >= H4 resistance at entry + stop buffer OR current low <= entry price - ((H4 resistance at entry + stop buffer) - entry price) × reward/risk ratio OR bar time’s hour and minute equal the configured daily close time
