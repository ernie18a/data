Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(0.54×close position in bar range + 0.30×body direction + 0.32×body fraction×(body direction−0.5) + 0.14, 0, 1), where close position=(close−low)/max(high−low, minimum tick), body direction=1 for close>open, 0 for close<open, otherwise 0.5, and body fraction=abs(close−open)/max(high−low, minimum tick)
Calculation: Pressure delta = 100×sum(volume×(2×estimated buy share−1), pressure lookback)/sum(volume, pressure lookback)
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative sum of volume×(2×estimated buy share−1)
Calculation: Long score = clamp(weighted sum of: long trend, close at/above VWAP, minimum ATR percentage, ADX above its threshold with +DI>−DI, RSI above 50 and nondecreasing, volume expansion, positive pressure delta (larger contribution above its threshold), bullish CVD (CVD above its EMA and EMA nondecreasing), discount reclaim, bullish wick rejection, downside sweep, compression breakout upward with volume expansion, and bullish regime continuation or flat-regime bullish break; 0, 100)
Calculation: Short score = clamp(weighted sum of: short trend, close at/below VWAP, minimum ATR percentage, ADX above its threshold with −DI>+DI, RSI below 50 and nonincreasing, volume expansion, negative pressure delta (larger contribution below its negative threshold), bearish CVD (CVD below its EMA and EMA nonincreasing), premium rejection, bearish wick rejection, upside sweep, compression breakout downward with volume expansion, and bearish regime continuation or flat-regime bearish break; 0, 100)
Calculation: Long trend = fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA; short trend = fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA. The fast EMA uses a shorter length than the slow EMA.
Calculation: Volume expansion = volume > volume baseline × expansion factor OR volume z-score > its threshold; volume baseline and standard deviation use the same lookback.
Calculation: Discount reclaim = (close−VWAP)/ATR < negative VWAP-band threshold AND close>open; premium rejection = (close−VWAP)/ATR > VWAP-band threshold AND close<open
Calculation: Bullish wick rejection = lower wick > max(body, minimum tick) × wick threshold AND close>open AND close > low + bar range × close-location threshold; bearish wick rejection = upper wick > max(body, minimum tick) × wick threshold AND close<open AND close < low + bar range × (1−close-location threshold)
Calculation: Downside sweep = low < lowest low over the prior structure lookback AND close > that prior lowest low AND close>open; upside sweep = high > highest high over the prior structure lookback AND close < that prior highest high AND close<open
Calculation: Compression = 24-bar range as a percentage of close < ATR as a percentage of close × compression factor AND abs(fast EMA−slow EMA) < ATR × separation factor. Upward breakout = prior bar was compressed AND close > highest high of the prior 8 bars AND volume expansion; downward breakout = prior bar was compressed AND close < lowest low of the prior 8 bars AND volume expansion.
Calculation: Regime = bullish when long trend and close>=VWAP, bearish when short trend and close<=VWAP, otherwise flat. On each confirmed bar, increment the hold counter for the previous regime when the regime persists and its turn counter when it changes to the opposite regime; in flat regime increment the corresponding bullish or bearish break counter. Initialize all counters to 1. Bullish continuation probability = bullish hold/(bullish hold+bullish turn); bearish continuation probability = bearish hold/(bearish hold+bearish turn); flat break probability = max(flat bullish, flat bearish)/(flat bullish+flat bearish).
Long: long score >= setup threshold AND long score > short score + separation threshold AND ATR percentage >= minimum AND (trend filter disabled OR long trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND (confirmation disabled OR current bar confirmed) AND no signal within cooldown; OR long score > short score + separation threshold; OR discount reclaim; OR bullish wick rejection; OR downside sweep; OR upward breakout with volume expansion; OR pressure delta > bullish pressure threshold
Short: short score >= setup threshold AND short score > long score + separation threshold AND ATR percentage >= minimum AND (trend filter disabled OR short trend) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume expansion) AND (confirmation disabled OR current bar confirmed) AND no signal within cooldown; OR short score > long score + separation threshold; OR premium rejection; OR bearish wick rejection; OR upside sweep; OR downward breakout with volume expansion; OR pressure delta < negative bearish pressure threshold
