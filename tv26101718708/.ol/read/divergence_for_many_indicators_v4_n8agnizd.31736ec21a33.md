Calculation: Positive regular divergence = For a selected indicator (MACD, MACD histogram, RSI, Stochastic, CCI, Momentum, OBV, VW-MACD, CMF, or MFI), compare its value and price at the current decision bar with each of the most recent configured number of confirmed pivot lows within the configured lookback. Pivot lows use configured Close or Low source; require a separation greater than five bars. Regular: indicator is higher and comparison price is lower than at the pivot. Hidden: indicator is lower and comparison price is higher. Comparison price is Close when Close is the pivot source, otherwise Low. Unless unconfirmed signals are enabled, use the prior completed chart bar for the current endpoint; otherwise use the forming bar. Unless unconfirmed signals are enabled, also require current indicator > prior indicator OR current close > prior close. For both indicator and close, every intervening value must be at or above the straight line between the endpoints.
Calculation: Negative regular or hidden divergence = Use the same pivot search and endpoint rules, comparing against confirmed pivot highs. Pivot highs use configured Close or High source. Regular: indicator is lower and comparison price is higher than at the pivot. Hidden: indicator is higher and comparison price is lower. Comparison price is Close when Close is the pivot source, otherwise High. Unless unconfirmed signals are enabled, also require current indicator < prior indicator OR current close < prior close. For both indicator and close, every intervening value must be at or below the straight line between the endpoints.
Long: At least one enabled built-in indicator has a positive regular or hidden divergence, the configured divergence-type selection permits it, and the total count of detected divergences across enabled indicators and permitted types meets the configured minimum.
Short: At least one enabled built-in indicator has a negative regular or hidden divergence, the configured divergence-type selection permits it, and the total count of detected divergences across enabled indicators and permitted types meets the configured minimum.
