Calculation: Stochastic RSI %K = moving average of the stochastic of RSI values
Long: no open position AND within configured backtest date range AND long trades enabled AND short-term and long-term Supertrend directions are up AND Stochastic RSI %K crossed above the oversold threshold within the preceding lookback bars
Short: no open position AND within configured backtest date range AND short trades enabled AND short-term and long-term Supertrend directions are down AND Stochastic RSI %K crossed below the overbought threshold within the preceding lookback bars
Exit: long, close is below the short-term Supertrend line
Exit: short, close is above the short-term Supertrend line
