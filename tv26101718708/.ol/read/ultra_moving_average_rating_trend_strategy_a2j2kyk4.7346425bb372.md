Calculation: aggregate rating = arithmetic mean of 82 moving-average ratings and one Ichimoku rating. For each of SMA, EMA, ALMA, SMMA, LSMA, VWMA, DEMA, HMA, KAMA, FRAMA, VIDYA, JMA, TEMA, ZLEMA, TRIMA, and T3, calculate multiple lookbacks from short to long (six for SMA and EMA; five for each other type). Each moving-average rating is +1 when close is above the average, -1 when below, and 0 when equal. Ichimoku rating is +1 when the leading span A is above span B, close is above span A and below the base line, and the prior close was below the conversion line while the current close is above it; -1 when span B is above span A, close is below span B and above the base line, and the prior close was above the conversion line while the current close is below it; otherwise 0. The conversion line is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a shorter lookback; the base line uses a longer lookback; span A is their midpoint; span B is the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over a still longer lookback. Divide the sum of the ratings by the number of valid ratings.
Long: aggregate rating >= long-entry threshold
Short: aggregate rating <= short-entry threshold
Exit: Long, aggregate rating <= short-entry threshold
Exit: Short, aggregate rating >= long-entry threshold
