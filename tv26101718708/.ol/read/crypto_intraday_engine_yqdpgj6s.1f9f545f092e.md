Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: UTC Daily VWAP = cumulative sum of ((high + low + close) / 3) × volume since 00:00 UTC divided by cumulative volume since 00:00 UTC.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low extreme relative to configurable numbers of chart bars on both sides; available after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Support zone = [confirmed pivot low, pivot low + ATR at pivot × configurable zone depth]; resistance zone = [confirmed pivot high − ATR at pivot × configurable zone depth].
Calculation: Valid support/resistance zones are removed when a completed close is below the support bottom − current ATR × configurable invalidation buffer, or above the resistance top + current ATR × configurable invalidation buffer, respectively. Among remaining zones, nearest support is the one with the smallest distance below or overlapping the close; nearest resistance is the one with the smallest distance above or overlapping the close.
Calculation: Compressed area = nearest resistance bottom − nearest support top ≤ current ATR × configurable minimum gap; overlapping zones also count as compressed.
Long: close > UTC Daily VWAP + ATR × configurable neutral distance AND a support-zone observation was started on an earlier completed bar when close > UTC Daily VWAP + ATR × configurable neutral distance, that bar intersected the nearest support zone, the zones were not compressed, and that support zone had not been evaluated AND the observation age is at least 1 and no greater than the configurable observation window AND close > observed support-zone top AND close > UTC Daily VWAP.
Short: close < UTC Daily VWAP − ATR × configurable neutral distance AND a resistance-zone observation was started on an earlier completed bar when close < UTC Daily VWAP − ATR × configurable neutral distance, that bar intersected the nearest resistance zone, the zones were not compressed, and that resistance zone had not been evaluated AND the observation age is at least 1 and no greater than the configurable observation window AND close < observed resistance-zone bottom AND close < UTC Daily VWAP.
