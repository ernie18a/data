Calculation: Cumulative intraday-to-EOD volume percentage = cumulative intraday-feed volume divided by cumulative EOD-feed volume, adding daily volumes only when both are available; use completed daily EOD bars and intraday bars aggregated over each day.
Long: Cumulative intraday-to-EOD volume percentage >= 50%
Short: Cumulative intraday-to-EOD volume percentage < 50%
