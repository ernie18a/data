Calculation: Major swing pivots = alternating highs and lows confirmed when price retraces from the running extreme by an ATR-scaled threshold.
Calculation: Minor swing pivots = local highs and lows confirmed by a fixed symmetric bar window.
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed major pivot high (current close > pivot high AND previous close ≤ pivot high) OR close crosses above the latest confirmed minor pivot high (current close > pivot high AND previous close ≤ pivot high) OR low < the nearest recent confirmed minor pivot low AND close > that pivot low AND lower wick ≥ the configured ATR-scaled minimum AND lower wick / (high − low) ≥ the configured minimum fraction AND the configured same-direction sweep cooldown has elapsed OR low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > that earlier high AND the gap is at least the configured ATR-scaled minimum.
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed major pivot low (current close < pivot low AND previous close ≥ pivot low) OR close crosses below the latest confirmed minor pivot low (current close < pivot low AND previous close ≥ pivot low) OR high > the nearest recent confirmed minor pivot high AND close < that pivot high AND upper wick ≥ the configured ATR-scaled minimum AND upper wick / (high − low) ≥ the configured minimum fraction AND the configured same-direction sweep cooldown has elapsed OR high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < that earlier low AND the gap is at least the configured ATR-scaled minimum.
