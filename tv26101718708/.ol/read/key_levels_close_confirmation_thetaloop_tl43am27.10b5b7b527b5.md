Calculation: Prior-day high, low, and close = high, low, and close of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than an intraday chart).
Calculation: Prior-week high and low = high and low of the last completed weekly bar (higher timeframe than an intraday chart).
Calculation: Current-week open = open of the current weekly bar (higher timeframe than an intraday chart; bar may still be forming).
Calculation: Current-month open = open of the current monthly bar (higher timeframe than an intraday chart; bar may still be forming).
Calculation: Opening-range high and low = highest high and lowest low of the first configured interval of regular-session chart bars; use only after that interval ends.
Calculation: Pre-market high and low = highest high and lowest low of pre-market chart bars; use after pre-market ends.
Long: For any available level, the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar (higher than the chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe if the configured confirmation timeframe is lower) closes above the level, and the prior counted confirmation bar closed at or below the level; OR chart-bar low < level AND chart-bar close >= level AND chart-bar open >= level. For intraday confirmation bars, count breaks only when the confirming bar ended in the regular session; count probes only during the regular session. Emit at most the first break or probe per level and direction per session.
Short: For any available level, the last completed confirmation-timeframe bar (higher than the chart timeframe, or the chart timeframe if the configured confirmation timeframe is lower) closes below the level, and the prior counted confirmation bar closed at or above the level; OR chart-bar high > level AND chart-bar close <= level AND chart-bar open <= level. For intraday confirmation bars, count breaks only when the confirming bar ended in the regular session; count probes only during the regular session. Emit at most the first break or probe per level and direction per session.
