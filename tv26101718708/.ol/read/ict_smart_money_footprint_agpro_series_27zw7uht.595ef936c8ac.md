Calculation: Confirmed chart-timeframe swing high/low = pivot high/low with the configured number of chart bars on each side; use the latest confirmed pivot of each type.
Calculation: Bullish sequence score = round(clamp(45 + 25 × clamp((candle range / chart-timeframe ATR − displacement threshold) / (1.4 × displacement threshold), 0, 1) + 20 × bullish close position + 10 if the bar overlaps an active bullish higher-timeframe zone, otherwise 0, 0, 100)); bearish score uses bearish close position and an active bearish higher-timeframe zone. Higher-timeframe zones use confirmed pivot highs/lows and higher-timeframe ATR; use confirmed higher-timeframe data.
Long: low < latest confirmed swing low − sweep buffer × chart-timeframe ATR AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND close > open OR close > open AND candle range > displacement threshold × chart-timeframe ATR AND close > prior high AND (close − low) / candle range ≥ displacement close threshold OR a prior close was at or above the latest confirmed swing low and a later close crossed below it, then within the reclaim window a close crosses back above that stored level OR a bullish sweep was followed within the sequence window by a bullish displacement AND bullish sequence score ≥ minimum sequence score AND bullish footprint range top > bottom OR an active bullish expansion sequence is reclaimed within the reclaim window.
Short: high > latest confirmed swing high + sweep buffer × chart-timeframe ATR AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND close < open OR close < open AND candle range > displacement threshold × chart-timeframe ATR AND close < prior low AND (high − close) / candle range ≥ displacement close threshold OR a prior close was at or below the latest confirmed swing high and a later close crossed above it, then within the reclaim window a close crosses back below that stored level OR a bearish sweep was followed within the sequence window by a bearish displacement AND bearish sequence score ≥ minimum sequence score AND bearish footprint range top > bottom OR an active bearish expansion sequence is reclaimed within the reclaim window.
