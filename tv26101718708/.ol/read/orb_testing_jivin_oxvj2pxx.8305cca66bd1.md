Calculation: Previous-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart timeframe).
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the opening window; opening-range range = opening-range high − opening-range low. The range must be within its minimum and maximum bounds.
Calculation: Active long/short arm = reset before the session; during the trade window, a sweep arms a retest at the previous-day low/high and overrides any existing arm. A low below the previous-day low followed by a close above it arms long; a high above the previous-day high followed by a close below it arms short. Otherwise, when no arm is active, a close above/below the opening-range high/low arms long/short if the opening-range range is valid. A long ORB arm is cleared by a close below the opening-range low; a short ORB arm by a close above the opening-range high. A long sweep arm is cleared by a close below the previous-day low − 1.5 × ATR; a short sweep arm by a close above the previous-day high + 1.5 × ATR. ATR is calculated on the chart timeframe.
Long: Flat position AND no trade yet today AND daily loss limit not reached AND within the trade window AND an ORB long arm is active AND low ≤ armed level + 0.4 × ATR AND close ≥ armed level − 0.4 × ATR AND close > opening-range low OR flat position AND no trade yet today AND daily loss limit not reached AND within the trade window AND a long sweep arm is active AND low ≤ armed level + 0.4 × ATR AND close ≥ armed level − 0.4 × ATR
Short: Flat position AND no trade yet today AND daily loss limit not reached AND within the trade window AND an ORB short arm is active AND high ≥ armed level − 0.4 × ATR AND close ≤ armed level + 0.4 × ATR AND close < opening-range high OR flat position AND no trade yet today AND daily loss limit not reached AND within the trade window AND a short sweep arm is active AND high ≥ armed level − 0.4 × ATR AND close ≤ armed level + 0.4 × ATR
Exit: Long, close ≤ entry-signal close − ATR × stop multiplier OR close ≥ entry-signal close + ATR × target multiplier OR time ≥ 15:50 New York time OR daily loss limit reached
Exit: Short, close ≥ entry-signal close + ATR × stop multiplier OR close ≤ entry-signal close − ATR × target multiplier OR time ≥ 15:50 New York time OR daily loss limit reached
