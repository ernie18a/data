Calculation: Short-lookback moving average = selected moving-average method applied to selected regular or Heikin-Ashi close on its selected timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe), using that timeframe's current bar while it is forming; its lookback is shorter than the long-lookback moving average's.
Calculation: Long-lookback moving average = selected moving-average method applied to selected regular or Heikin-Ashi close on its selected timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe), using that timeframe's current bar while it is forming.
Calculation: Trend-confirmation moving average = selected moving-average method applied to selected regular or Heikin-Ashi close on its selected timeframe (same, higher, or lower than the chart timeframe), using that timeframe's current bar while it is forming.
Long: Short-lookback moving average crosses above long-lookback moving average AND (trend confirmation is disabled OR chart close > trend-confirmation moving average) AND bar datetime is within the configured backtest window.
Short: Short-lookback moving average crosses below long-lookback moving average OR (trend confirmation is enabled AND chart close < trend-confirmation moving average), AND bar datetime is within the configured backtest window.
Exit: Long, short condition AND bar datetime is within the configured backtest window.
