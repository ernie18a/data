Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = tracked highest high/lowest low, confirmed after three qualifying bearish/bullish bars respectively, each closing below the prior low/above the prior high; reset the count when a new extreme occurs. Other bars do not reset the count.
Calculation: Confirmed internal high = tracked high within the active confirmed swing range, confirmed by the same bearish-bar rule; reset its count on a new high or swing break.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish if the current higher-timeframe close exceeds the previous completed higher-timeframe high, or the chart bar's low is below that high-timeframe low and the current higher-timeframe close is above it; bearish if the current higher-timeframe close is below the previous completed higher-timeframe low, or the chart bar's high is above that high-timeframe high and the current higher-timeframe close is below it. The current higher-timeframe bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure state = expansion if the current higher-timeframe close exceeds the previous completed higher-timeframe high; shift if it is below the previous completed higher-timeframe low; otherwise continuation. Its direction is bullish when the current higher-timeframe close exceeds the previous completed higher-timeframe close, and bearish otherwise. The current higher-timeframe bar may still be forming.
Long: close > confirmed swing high OR close > confirmed internal high OR weekly bias is bullish OR weekly bias is bullish sweep OR daily bias is bullish OR daily bias is bullish sweep OR a higher-timeframe structure state is expansion or continuation and its direction is bullish
Short: close < confirmed swing low OR weekly bias is bearish OR weekly bias is bearish sweep OR daily bias is bearish OR daily bias is bearish sweep OR a higher-timeframe structure state is shift or continuation and its direction is bearish
