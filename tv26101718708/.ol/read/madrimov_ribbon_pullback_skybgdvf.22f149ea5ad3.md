Calculation: Ribbon MAs = same-type moving averages of close at shorter and longer lookbacks; use the shorter- and longer-lookback averages throughout.
Calculation: Clean bullish ribbon = shorter-lookback MA > longer-lookback MA AND shorter-lookback MA minus its value at a longer lookback ago > ATR × slope threshold AND the absolute difference between the two MAs > ATR × separation threshold. Clean bearish ribbon = the inverse MA ordering AND its value at that lookback ago minus its current value > ATR × slope threshold AND the same separation condition.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe MAs = the same-type moving averages of close at the ribbon lookbacks, calculated on a higher timeframe using the HTF value available on each chart bar (the current HTF bar as it forms).
Calculation: Ribbon touch = a bar whose high is at or above the lower edge of the range between the ribbon MAs AND whose low is at or below its upper edge. A touch remains recorded until a bullish or bearish internal structure break; the break bar resets it.
Calculation: Bullish internal structure break = close crosses above the latest unbroken internal pivot high. A pivot high is high five bars ago when the internal leg switches to a high leg after that high exceeds the highest high of the latest five bars; each pivot is eligible until crossed or replaced by a new pivot high.
Calculation: Bearish internal structure break = close crosses below the latest unbroken internal pivot low. A pivot low is low five bars ago when the internal leg switches to a low leg after that low falls below the lowest low of the latest five bars; each pivot is eligible until crossed or replaced by a new pivot low.
Long: higher-timeframe shorter-lookback MA > higher-timeframe longer-lookback MA AND clean bullish ribbon AND a ribbon touch was recorded on the prior bar AND bullish internal structure break
Short: higher-timeframe shorter-lookback MA < higher-timeframe longer-lookback MA AND clean bearish ribbon AND a ribbon touch was recorded on the prior bar AND bearish internal structure break
