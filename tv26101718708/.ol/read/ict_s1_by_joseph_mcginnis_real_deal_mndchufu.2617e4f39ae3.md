Calculation: Previous-day midpoint = midpoint of the high and low of the last completed daily bar, from a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe midpoints = midpoint of the high and low of the last completed bar on each of two higher timeframes, with one longer-term and one shorter-term.
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing high and low = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, respectively, using equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Confidence score = weighted sum of whether the bar is in a New York AM or PM session, higher-timeframe bullish/bearish alignment, a directional retest, a directional break of the latest swing level, and a same-direction fair value gap. A bullish fair value gap has current low above high two bars earlier; a bearish fair value gap has current high below low two bars earlier.
Long: Close > previous-day midpoint AND confidence score >= its threshold AND bars since the previous long signal > its cooldown AND (previous bar low <= previous-day midpoint AND close > previous-day midpoint OR previous bar low <= latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low OR close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= latest confirmed swing high) AND in a New York AM or PM session.
Short: Close <= previous-day midpoint AND confidence score >= its threshold AND bars since the previous short signal > its cooldown AND (previous bar high >= previous-day midpoint AND close < previous-day midpoint OR previous bar high >= latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high OR close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= latest confirmed swing low) AND in a New York AM or PM session.
