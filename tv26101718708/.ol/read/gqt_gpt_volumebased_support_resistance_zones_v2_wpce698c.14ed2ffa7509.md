Calculation: Volume moving average = simple moving average of volume over a lookback, calculated on the signal timeframe (equal to or higher than the chart timeframe); use the current signal-timeframe bar, which is still forming until it closes.
Calculation: Support zone = for the latest qualifying fractal low, the pivot candle’s open if its close is at or above its open, otherwise its close. The pivot is the low three signal-timeframe bars ago when its low is below the lows five and four bars ago, the lows two and one bars ago rise successively, and its volume exceeds the volume moving average from the pivot bar.
Calculation: Resistance zone = for the latest qualifying fractal high, the pivot candle’s close if its close is at or above its open, otherwise its open. The pivot is the high three signal-timeframe bars ago when its high is above the highs five and four bars ago, the highs two and one bars ago fall successively, and its volume exceeds the volume moving average from the pivot bar.
Long: Current close < current support zone AND previous close >= previous support zone AND current datetime is within the configured start and end dates AND position size <= 0
Exit: Long, current close > current resistance zone AND previous close <= previous resistance zone AND current datetime is within the configured start and end dates
