Calculation: MACD state = bullish when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0; bearish when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; otherwise neutral
Calculation: KDJ state = calculate RSV from close within the rolling lowest low and highest high; K = short-smoothed RSV, D = short-smoothed K, J = 3K − 2D; bullish when K > D and J > K; bearish when K < D and J < K; otherwise neutral
Calculation: RSI state = neutral when RSI >= overbought threshold or RSI <= oversold threshold; otherwise bullish when RSI > 50, bearish when RSI < 50, and neutral when RSI = 50
Calculation: CCI state = neutral when CCI >= overbought threshold or CCI <= oversold threshold; otherwise bullish when CCI > 0, bearish when CCI < 0, and neutral when CCI = 0
Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative selected price source × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each selected anchor; state is bullish when close > VWAP, bearish when close < VWAP, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Moving average state = bullish when close > selected moving average and its current value > its value two bars earlier; bearish when close < the moving average and its current value <= its value two bars earlier; otherwise neutral
Calculation: Bollinger %B state = %B is 100 × (close − lower band) / (upper band − lower band); bullish when %B > 60, bearish when %B < 40, otherwise neutral
Calculation: Volume state = bullish when close > open and volume >= 0.8 × its moving average; bearish when close < open and volume >= 0.8 × its moving average; otherwise neutral
Calculation: Bullish percentage = 100 × sum of weights for bullish component states / sum of active component weights; bearish percentage = 100 × sum of weights for bearish component states / sum of active component weights; components are MACD, KDJ, RSI, CCI, anchored VWAP, moving average, volume, and optionally Bollinger %B
Calculation: ATR trail = on a long candidate, initialize to low − ATR × multiplier and thereafter use the greater of the prior trail and low − ATR × multiplier; on a short candidate, initialize to high + ATR × multiplier and thereafter use the lesser of the prior trail and high + ATR × multiplier
Long: bullish percentage >= its threshold AND prior bar bullish percentage < its threshold AND (bar is confirmed when close confirmation is enabled) AND (MA filter is disabled OR close > moving average) AND previous candidate signal is not long AND (price gate is disabled OR gate reference is unavailable OR close > selected gate reference); OR close > prior high AND close > open; OR close is not > prior high AND not < prior low AND low < prior low AND close >= open; OR none of those bullish candle conditions apply and close >= open
Short: bearish percentage >= its threshold AND prior bar bearish percentage < its threshold AND (bar is confirmed when close confirmation is enabled) AND (MA filter is disabled OR close < moving average) AND previous candidate signal is not short AND (price gate is disabled OR gate reference is unavailable OR close < selected gate reference); OR close < prior low AND close < open; OR close is not > prior high AND not < prior low AND high > prior high AND close <= open; OR none of those bearish candle conditions apply and close < open
