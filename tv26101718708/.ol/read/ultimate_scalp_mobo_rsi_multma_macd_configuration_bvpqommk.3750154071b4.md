Calculation: Upper and lower volatility bands = SMA(close, lookback) ± multiplier × standard deviation(close, lookback)
Calculation: Bullish candle pattern = (lower wick > 1.5 × body and upper wick < 0.5 × body) OR (lower wick > 0.6 × range) OR (high < prior high and low > prior low) OR (absolute difference between current and prior low ≤ 0.1 × current range and current low < min(open, close)) OR (prior close < prior open and current open < prior low and current close > midpoint of prior body and current close < prior open) OR (close two bars ago < open two bars ago and prior body < 0.3 × prior range and current close > current open and current close > midpoint of the body two bars ago)
Calculation: Bearish candle pattern = (upper wick > 1.5 × body and lower wick < 0.5 × body) OR (upper wick > 0.6 × range) OR (high < prior high and low > prior low) OR (absolute difference between current and prior high ≤ 0.1 × current range and current high > max(open, close)) OR (prior close > prior open and current open > prior high and current close < midpoint of prior body and current close > prior open) OR (close two bars ago > open two bars ago and prior body < 0.3 × prior range and current close < current open and current close < midpoint of the body two bars ago)
