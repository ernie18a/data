Calculation: Prior low = lowest low over the preceding sweep-lookback bars; prior high = highest high over the preceding sweep-lookback bars
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, with zone [high two bars earlier, current low]; bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, with zone [current high, low two bars earlier]
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = latest bearish FVG zone after close crosses above its upper boundary; bearish IFVG = latest bullish FVG zone after close crosses below its lower boundary. A broken bullish FVG may also become a bearish IFVG when low < its lower boundary; a broken bearish FVG may become a bullish IFVG when high > its upper boundary.
Calculation: Bullish order block = prior bearish candle's [low, open] zone when a bullish displacement candle closes above the prior high; bearish order block = prior bullish candle's [open, high] zone when a bearish displacement candle closes below the prior low. Displacement body must exceed the average body size over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Bullish breaker = latest bearish order block zone after close crosses above its upper boundary; bearish breaker = latest bullish order block zone after close crosses below its lower boundary.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < prior low; bearish sweep = high > prior high
Calculation: Bullish three-bar recovery = within the recovery window after a bullish sweep, close > the sweep bar's high-low midpoint and current body size >= ATR × recovery multiplier; bearish three-bar recovery = within the recovery window after a bearish sweep, close < the sweep bar's high-low midpoint and current body size >= ATR × recovery multiplier
Calculation: Bullish two-bar snapback = prior candle closes below open, current candle closes above open, current low < prior low, current close > prior open, and current body size >= ATR × recovery multiplier; bearish two-bar snapback = prior candle closes above open, current candle closes below open, current high > prior high, current close < prior open, and current body size >= ATR × recovery multiplier
Calculation: Bullish recovery = bullish three-bar recovery OR bullish two-bar snapback within the recovery window after a bullish sweep; bearish recovery = bearish three-bar recovery OR bearish two-bar snapback within the recovery window after a bearish sweep. Recovery remains active through the retest window plus recovery window after its sweep.
Calculation: Zone touch = current bar's [low, high] overlaps the zone
Calculation: Valid bullish IFVG = bullish IFVG size >= minimum size and its creation is no more than the maximum bar interval after its source FVG; valid bearish IFVG uses the same filters
Calculation: BPR = bullish and bearish FVG zones overlap OR bullish and bearish IFVG zones overlap, allowing the configured overlap tolerance
Long: bullish recovery is active AND current bar touches a valid bullish IFVG OR bullish order block OR bullish breaker AND close > open AND (the touched zone is an order block or breaker OR the touched zone is a valid bullish IFVG) AND (BPR blocking is disabled OR current bar is not in a BPR) AND no long signal has triggered since the latest bullish sweep
Short: bearish recovery is active AND current bar touches a valid bearish IFVG OR bearish order block OR bearish breaker AND close < open AND (the touched zone is an order block or breaker OR the touched zone is a valid bearish IFVG) AND (BPR blocking is disabled OR current bar is not in a BPR) AND no short signal has triggered since the latest bearish sweep
