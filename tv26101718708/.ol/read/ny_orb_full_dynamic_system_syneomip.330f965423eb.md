Calculation: Opening range high = highest high during the configured opening-range window; opening range low = lowest low during that window.
Calculation: Opening-range range = opening range high − opening range low.
Calculation: Simple moving average (SMA) = arithmetic mean of close over the configured lookback.
Calculation: Stop distance = selected method: fixed tick distance, ATR × multiplier, the lesser of ATR × multiplier and a maximum tick distance, or opening-range range × multiplier.
Calculation: Profit target distance = selected source (ATR or opening-range range) × multiplier.
Calculation: Daily PnL = realized PnL for the day + unrealized PnL, with unrealized PnL calculated from close, entry price, and position side.
Long: close > opening range high AND (confirmation disabled OR close at the configured prior-bar lookback <= opening range high) AND (second-break filter disabled OR an earlier close > opening range high was followed by a close <= opening range high and close is now > opening range high) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < overbought threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line > signal line) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (trend filter disabled OR close > SMA) AND (direction allows long) AND (entry time is within the configured window AND trading day is enabled AND daily entry lockout is inactive for an executed entry).
Short: close < opening range low AND (confirmation disabled OR close at the configured prior-bar lookback >= opening range low) AND (second-break filter disabled OR an earlier close < opening range low was followed by a close >= opening range low and close is now < opening range low) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > oversold threshold) AND (MACD filter disabled OR MACD line < signal line) AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (trend filter disabled OR close < SMA) AND (direction allows short) AND (entry time is within the configured window AND trading day is enabled AND daily entry lockout is inactive for an executed entry).
Exit: long, low <= entry price − stop distance OR low <= active trailing stop OR high >= profit target OR (breakeven enabled AND close >= entry price + breakeven trigger AND low <= entry price) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND close crosses below the selected SMA or VWAP AND (target is unset OR close < target)) OR daily PnL >= daily profit limit OR daily PnL <= daily loss limit OR time >= configured hard exit time.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + stop distance OR high >= active trailing stop OR low <= profit target OR (breakeven enabled AND close <= entry price − breakeven trigger AND high >= entry price) OR (MA-cross exit enabled AND close crosses above the selected SMA or VWAP AND (target is unset OR close > target)) OR daily PnL >= daily profit limit OR daily PnL <= daily loss limit OR time >= configured hard exit time.
