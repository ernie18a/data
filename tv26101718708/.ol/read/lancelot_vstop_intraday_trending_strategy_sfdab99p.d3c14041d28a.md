Calculation: Three OHLC4 volatility stops = ATR trailing stops using the same ATR lookback and progressively longer stop distances. For each, track the highest OHLC4 in an uptrend or lowest OHLC4 in a downtrend; trail the stop at that extreme minus or plus the stop distance times ATR, respectively. The stop can only move toward price within a trend. Trend direction changes when OHLC4 crosses the stop; reset the extreme to OHLC4 on a change. ATR and OHLC4 use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Renko UP/DN = persistent upper and lower rails initialized around the midpoint of the configured Renko-timeframe high-low range using half that timeframe's ATR; thereafter, rail height is the prior upper-minus-lower distance. UP occurs when chart close reaches or exceeds the prior upper rail plus one, two, or three rail heights; DN occurs when chart close reaches or falls below the prior lower rail minus one, two, or three rail heights. Check three heights first, then two, then one. Advance both rails in the corresponding direction by the same number of Renko-timeframe ATRs. The configured timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the latest available bar, which is the last completed bar in historical backtests.
Long: close > all three volatility stops AND the shortest-distance stop > the medium-distance stop > the longest-distance stop AND Renko UP
Short: close < all three volatility stops AND the shortest-distance stop < the medium-distance stop < the longest-distance stop AND Renko DN
Exit: long, close crosses below the shortest-distance volatility stop OR Renko DN
Exit: short, close crosses above the shortest-distance volatility stop OR Renko UP
