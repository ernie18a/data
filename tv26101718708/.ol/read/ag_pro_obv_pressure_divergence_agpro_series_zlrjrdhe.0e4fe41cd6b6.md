Calculation: Smoothed OBV = EMA of cumulative signed volume, adding volume when close rises from the previous bar, subtracting it when close falls, and adding zero when unchanged; its smoothing lookback is shorter than the slow OBV EMA lookback.
Calculation: Bullish divergence score = clamp(price swing / ATR × 12, 0, 25) + clamp(OBV rise / 50-bar OBV standard deviation × 14, 0, 25) + clamp(pivot gap / minimum pivot gap × 8, 0, 10) + (12 if fast OBV EMA > slow OBV EMA, otherwise 4) + (10 if the 5-bar EMA of smoothed OBV change > 0, otherwise 2) + (15 if the current pivot low is within 0.25 ATR of the lowest low over the context lookback, otherwise 8) + (12 if the shorter-lookback price EMA < the longer-lookback price EMA, 7 if it is greater, otherwise 9), clamped to 0–100.
Calculation: Bearish divergence score = clamp(price swing / ATR × 12, 0, 25) + clamp(OBV fall / 50-bar OBV standard deviation × 14, 0, 25) + clamp(pivot gap / minimum pivot gap × 8, 0, 10) + (12 if fast OBV EMA < slow OBV EMA, otherwise 4) + (10 if the 5-bar EMA of smoothed OBV change < 0, otherwise 2) + (15 if the current pivot high is within 0.25 ATR of the highest high over the context lookback, otherwise 8) + (12 if the shorter-lookback price EMA > the longer-lookback price EMA, 7 if it is less, otherwise 9), clamped to 0–100.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot low forms lower than the previous confirmed pivot low, with smoothed OBV at the new pivot higher than at the previous pivot, pivot gap ≥ minimum pivot gap, price swing ≥ ATR at the new pivot × minimum price swing, (bullish divergence score ≥ quality threshold OR weak signals are enabled), and (no prior bullish signal OR pivot gap from the prior bullish signal ≥ cooldown OR bullish divergence score ≥ premium threshold); OR a bullish setup is pending, its confirmation window has not expired, and (close > the high of its pivot bar OR, when close-based confirmation is disabled, high > that level) AND fast OBV EMA > slow OBV EMA.
Short: A newly confirmed pivot high forms higher than the previous confirmed pivot high, with smoothed OBV at the new pivot lower than at the previous pivot, pivot gap ≥ minimum pivot gap, price swing ≥ ATR at the new pivot × minimum price swing, (bearish divergence score ≥ quality threshold OR weak signals are enabled), and (no prior bearish signal OR pivot gap from the prior bearish signal ≥ cooldown OR bearish divergence score ≥ premium threshold); OR a bearish setup is pending, its confirmation window has not expired, and (close < the low of its pivot bar OR, when close-based confirmation is disabled, low < that level) AND fast OBV EMA < slow OBV EMA.
