Calculation: Breakout setup = close > the highest high over the selected lookback on prior bars AND volume > average volume × the selected spike multiplier AND ((the prior bar’s highest high minus lowest low over the selected lookback) / its previous close < the selected range threshold AND (OBV > its moving average over the selected lookback, when the OBV filter is enabled)) OR (close > open × (1 + the selected big-move threshold), when the momentum override is enabled)
Calculation: Pending confirmation = set by a breakout setup, storing that bar’s high and the lowest low used by the setup; remains pending until entry or until close < the current lowest low over the selected lookback
Long: A pending confirmation exists AND high > the stored setup-bar high AND no breakout setup occurs on this bar
Exit: Long, close crosses below the selected trend line (EMA or SMA of close) OR (close < open AND volume > average volume × the selected dump multiplier) OR (close < open AND previous close > previous open AND close < previous low AND open > previous high) OR (initial stop is enabled AND low <= the stored setup low)
