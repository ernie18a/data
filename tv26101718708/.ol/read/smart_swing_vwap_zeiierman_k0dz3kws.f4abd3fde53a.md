Calculation: Swing candidate = for each enabled lookback, use the most recent rolling high and low; bullish if the high is newer than the low, anchored at that low, otherwise bearish, anchored at that high.
Calculation: Move strength = geometric mean of normalized directional progress, volatility expansion, and relative volume participation, reduced when any component is weak.
Calculation: Anchor score = weighted combination of agreement with other valid same-direction candidates, normalized swing range, and move strength; agreement increases when candidate pivots are close in price or bar position.
Long: a bullish candidate has the highest score among valid candidates, meets minimum score and move-strength thresholds, is not the same as the active anchor, satisfies the minimum bars-between-anchors rule, and either no anchor exists, direction differs, active score is unavailable, or candidate score exceeds the active score by the required margin OR close crosses above the active structural level while bearish.
Short: a bearish candidate has the highest score among valid candidates, meets minimum score and move-strength thresholds, is not the same as the active anchor, satisfies the minimum bars-between-anchors rule, and either no anchor exists, direction differs, active score is unavailable, or candidate score exceeds the active score by the required margin OR close crosses below the active structural level while bullish.
