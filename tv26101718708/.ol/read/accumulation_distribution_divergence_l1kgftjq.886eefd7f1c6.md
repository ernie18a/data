Calculation: A/D oscillator = Let CLV = 0 when high = low, otherwise ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low); A/D line = cumulative sum of CLV × volume; detrend = A/D line − EMA(A/D line); normalize detrended values over a lookback window with a robust z-score (subtract rolling median and divide by 1.4826 × rolling median absolute deviation; classic mean/stdev z-score is selectable); oscillator = 100 × tanh(z-score / 3). A selectable three-window mode uses the median of z-scores from half, the full, and double the normalization lookback; otherwise use the full-window z-score.
Long: At a confirmed pivot low (using low by default), its price is lower than the previous confirmed pivot low and its A/D oscillator value is higher than at that previous pivot low, OR its price is higher and its oscillator value is lower; the two pivot lows are no more than the configured maximum bars apart.
Short: At a confirmed pivot high (using high by default), its price is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high and its A/D oscillator value is lower than at that previous pivot high, OR its price is lower and its oscillator value is higher; the two pivot highs are no more than the configured maximum bars apart.
