Calculation: Coral direction = direction of a six-stage smoothed price filter; bullish when its value rises, bearish when it falls. Fast and slow versions use the same lookback, with the slow version smoother.
Calculation: Pivot percentages = EMA of (close / latest confirmed pivot high × 100) and (latest confirmed pivot low / close × 100), using the same pivot and smoothing lookbacks. Bullish when the high percentage exceeds the low percentage; bearish when the low percentage exceeds the high percentage. Strong bullish/bearish when the respective percentage exceeds its threshold.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot percentages = the same pivot percentages calculated from a higher timeframe, using its latest available bar (developing in realtime; completed historical value as mapped to the chart).
Calculation: MACD alignment = fast EMA minus slow EMA compared with its signal SMA; bullish when MACD is above its signal and bearish when below. A bullish/bearish cross is a cross above/below the signal.
Calculation: Order blocks = on a close crossing above/below a confirmed swing or internal pivot, create a bullish/bearish block from the respective extreme candle between the pivot and crossing bar; remove it when close crosses beyond its far edge. Internal and swing pivots use shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively. A nearby bullish block has close at or above its lower edge and no farther than an ATR-based distance above its upper edge; a nearby bearish block has close at or below its upper edge and no farther than an ATR-based distance below its lower edge.
Calculation: Directional confluence score = sum of points for matching fast Coral direction, fast Coral flip, slow Coral direction, pivot direction, strong pivot percentage, higher-timeframe pivot direction, MACD alignment, MACD cross, nearby matching order block, price on the matching side of session VWAP, and matching VWAP slope. The flip and MACD cross points are one-bar bonuses; other components persist while true.
Calculation: Maintenance score = directional confluence score excluding the fast Coral flip and MACD cross bonuses.
Long: fast Coral is bullish (or flips bullish if flip-only entry is required) AND high pivot percentage > low pivot percentage AND MACD is bullish AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (slow Coral bullish if required) AND (higher-timeframe pivot bullish if that filter is enabled and required) AND (nearby bullish order block if required) AND (close > VWAP if VWAP alignment is required and enabled) AND last entry direction is not long AND bar is confirmed and warmed up.
Short: fast Coral is bearish (or flips bearish if flip-only entry is required) AND low pivot percentage > high pivot percentage AND MACD is bearish AND directional confluence score >= minimum score AND (slow Coral bearish if required) AND (higher-timeframe pivot bearish if that filter is enabled and required) AND (nearby bearish order block if required) AND (close < VWAP if VWAP alignment is required and enabled) AND last entry direction is not short AND bar is confirmed and warmed up.
Exit: long, low <= stop price (initial stop or stepped trailing stop) OR high >= selected take-profit target when using a fixed target OR maintenance score < decay threshold for the required consecutive bars and any required take-profit gate is met OR a confirmed short signal occurs.
Exit: short, high >= stop price (initial stop or stepped trailing stop) OR low <= selected take-profit target when using a fixed target OR maintenance score < decay threshold for the required consecutive bars and any required take-profit gate is met OR a confirmed long signal occurs.
