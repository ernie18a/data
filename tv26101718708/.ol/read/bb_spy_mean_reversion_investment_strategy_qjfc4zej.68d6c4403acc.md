Calculation: ATR = Average True Range on higher-timeframe bars, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Adaptive Bollinger bands = Bollinger bands whose standard-deviation multiplier is scaled by volatility-of-volatility: stdev of one-bar close percentage changes divided by its moving average, transformed by a gain and clamped to configured minimum and maximum scale
Calculation: Regime gate = disabled, or (band-width percentile exceeds its threshold AND normalized absolute basis slope exceeds its threshold) for trend mode, or band-width percentile is below 100 minus that threshold for mean-reversion mode
Calculation: Bias = most recent unfiltered long or short setup direction; initialize on the first setup and change only when an opposite setup occurs
Long: close crosses above the selected lower Bollinger band AND close > open AND close > long EMA AND (regime filter is not applied to longs OR regime gate is true), OR flat AND Bias is long AND re-arming is enabled (or the position just exited long)
Short: close crosses below the selected upper Bollinger band AND close < open AND close < short EMA AND ADX > minimum AND (regime filter is not applied to shorts OR regime gate is true), OR flat AND Bias is short AND re-arming is enabled (or the position just exited short)
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= entry price + take-profit multiple × ATR OR (time exit enabled AND ((bars since entry >= minimum hold AND close - entry price < profit-gate multiple × ATR stop multiple × ATR) OR bars since entry >= minimum hold + extension))
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= entry price - take-profit multiple × ATR OR (time exit enabled AND ((bars since entry >= minimum hold AND entry price - close < profit-gate multiple × ATR stop multiple × ATR) OR bars since entry >= minimum hold + extension))
