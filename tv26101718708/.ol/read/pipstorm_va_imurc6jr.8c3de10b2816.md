Calculation: ADR high/low = highest high/lowest low during the after-range session; when the chart timeframe is lower than 5 minutes, use the higher, still-forming 5-minute bar values.
Calculation: ODR high/low = highest high/lowest low during the overnight-range session; when the chart timeframe is lower than 5 minutes, use the higher, still-forming 5-minute bar values.
Calculation: RDR high/low = highest high/lowest low during the regular-range session, stored when that session ends; when the chart timeframe is lower than 5 minutes, use the higher, still-forming 5-minute bar values.
Long: During the after-range extension, close > ADR high AND previous close <= ADR high AND ADR high is available AND the upward break has not already occurred in this extension; OR during the overnight-range extension, close > ODR high AND previous close <= ODR high AND ODR high is available AND the upward break has not already occurred in this extension; OR during the regular-range extension, close > RDR high AND previous close <= RDR high AND RDR high is available AND the upward break has not already occurred in this extension.
Short: During the after-range extension, close < ADR low AND previous close >= ADR low AND ADR low is available AND the downward break has not already occurred in this extension; OR during the overnight-range extension, close < ODR low AND previous close >= ODR low AND ODR low is available AND the downward break has not already occurred in this extension; OR during the regular-range extension, close < RDR low AND previous close >= RDR low AND RDR low is available AND the downward break has not already occurred in this extension.
