Calculation: Aroon Up = 100 × (lookback length − bars since highest high in the lookback) / lookback length; Aroon Down uses the lowest low instead.
Calculation: Awesome Oscillator change = current Awesome Oscillator − previous Awesome Oscillator; Awesome Oscillator = 5-bar SMA of (high + low)/2 − 34-bar SMA of (high + low)/2.
Calculation: RSI average = SMA of RSI over the RSI lookback.
Calculation: VWAP stop distance = multiplier × absolute value of the 14-bar volume-weighted average of session VWAP of true range.
Long: While flat and within the configured date range, enter when either (internal mode and every enabled internal condition holds) or (external mode and the selected external series equals the configured signal value). Internal conditions: Aroon Up crosses above Aroon Down, or Aroon Up > configured threshold, or Aroon Up < configured threshold; Awesome Oscillator change crosses above zero, or is > configured threshold, or is < configured threshold; RSI crosses above its RSI average, or RSI > configured threshold, or RSI < configured threshold; MACD line crosses above signal line, or MACD line > configured threshold, or MACD line < configured threshold; Stochastic %K crosses above %D, or %K > configured threshold, or %K < configured threshold; Stochastic RSI %K crosses above %D, or %K > configured threshold, or %K < configured threshold; the configured moving average with the first length crosses above the configured moving average with the second length; Supertrend direction crosses below zero.
Exit: Long, high >= entry price + configured risk/reward multiple × absolute(entry price − stop price), or (high < that target and low <= stop price). Stop price is selected from: current low − ATR × configured multiplier; lowest low over configured lookback; or current low − VWAP stop distance.
