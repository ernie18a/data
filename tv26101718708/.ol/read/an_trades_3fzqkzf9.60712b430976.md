Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot with equal left and right lookbacks, known after the right-side bars complete; retain the latest confirmed high and low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = current low below the latest confirmed swing low and close back above it / current high above the latest confirmed swing high and close back below it. A new sweep resets its side’s invalidation; a later close below the swept low / above the swept high invalidates that side until another sweep.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fair value gap (FVG) = using completed bars aggregated to a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe, bullish when the high two bars earlier is below the latest completed bar’s low (zone from that high to that low), bearish when the low two bars earlier is above the latest completed bar’s high (zone from that bar’s high to the earlier low). Retain the latest zone of each direction per higher timeframe; discard a bullish zone when chart close exceeds its upper bound, or a bearish zone when chart close falls below its lower bound.
Calculation: Delivery candle = a bar with nonzero high-low range and absolute close-open divided by high-low at least the delivery threshold; retain the latest bullish and bearish delivery candle opens.
Calculation: Nearest opposing liquidity = nearest level above and below the current close among the last five confirmed swing highs and lows; compare their distances from the close.
Long: Current bar overlaps an active bullish higher-timeframe FVG AND close is above the latest bearish delivery candle’s open AND the nearest swing low is not closer than the nearest swing high (or either level is unavailable) AND the lower wick is not greater than ATR(20) times the wick threshold AND the latest bullish sweep has not been invalidated.
Short: Current bar overlaps an active bearish higher-timeframe FVG AND close is below the latest bullish delivery candle’s open AND the nearest swing high is not closer than the nearest swing low (or either level is unavailable) AND the upper wick is not greater than ATR(20) times the wick threshold AND the latest bearish sweep has not been invalidated.
