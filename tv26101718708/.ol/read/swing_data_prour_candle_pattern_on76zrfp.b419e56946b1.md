Calculation: 8-period EMA = exponential moving average of close on daily bars; 21-period EMA = same, with a longer lookback than the 8-period EMA. Daily metrics use the current daily bar, which is still forming on lower-timeframe charts.
Calculation: Average daily volume = simple moving average of daily volume over a longer lookback; relative volume = current daily volume divided by average daily volume, multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Candle range = high − low; candle body = absolute value of close − open; lower wick = min(open, close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open, close).
Calculation: Close position in range = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100.
Calculation: Relative strength rating = a 1–99 rating of the instrument’s weighted 63-, 126-, 189-, and 252-bar daily performance relative to the S&P 500, mapped using the rating reference distribution.
Calculation: Sector ETF change = (current daily close − current daily open) / current daily open × 100; uses the current daily bar, which is still forming on lower-timeframe charts.
Long: Daily low ≤ 8-period EMA AND daily close > 8-period EMA, OR daily low ≤ 21-period EMA AND daily close > 21-period EMA.
Long: Daily low > 8-period EMA AND daily low ≤ 8-period EMA × (1 + tap-zone percentage) AND daily close > 8-period EMA, OR the same conditions for the 21-period EMA.
Long: Daily low is within the configured proximity percentage of the 8-period or 21-period EMA AND relative volume ≥ the configured pattern minimum AND (current candle is bullish and its body fully engulfs the prior bearish candle’s body and is at least the configured percentage larger, OR candle body ≤ range × configured doji tolerance AND lower wick ≥ body × configured wick ratio AND upper wick ≤ body × 0.5, OR the candle two bars earlier is bearish AND the intervening candle body ≤ its body × configured maximum AND the current candle is bullish and closes at or above the configured percentage of the earlier bearish body, OR the prior candle is bearish AND current open < prior low AND current candle is bullish AND current close is at or above the configured percentage into the prior body but below prior open).
Long: Daily low is within the configured proximity percentage of the 8-period or 21-period EMA AND relative volume ≥ the configured pattern minimum AND one of the bullish candle-pattern conditions above holds; the pattern-timeframe bar is still forming.
Long: Daily close > 21-period EMA AND 8-period EMA > 21-period EMA AND 21-period EMA > its prior daily value AND (daily close > 50-period SMA if that filter is enabled) AND relative strength rating ≥ its minimum AND relative volume ≥ its minimum AND (average daily volume ≥ its minimum if enabled) AND (daily close × average daily volume ≥ its minimum dollar volume if enabled) AND (sector ETF change > 0 if required) AND (an 8- or 21-period EMA undercut/reclaim or tap/reclaim occurs OR a qualifying pullback occurs).
Long: Daily low ≥ 8-period EMA AND daily low ≤ 8-period EMA × (1 + pullback-zone percentage) AND daily close > 8-period EMA AND close position in range ≥ its configured minimum AND (daily close ≥ daily open if required), OR the same conditions for the 21-period EMA.
