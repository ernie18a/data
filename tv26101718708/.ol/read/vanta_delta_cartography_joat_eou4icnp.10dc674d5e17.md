Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative (HLC3 × volume) divided by cumulative volume, reset at each active session
Calculation: Volume-profile POC = midpoint of the highest-volume price row among rows assigning each bar’s volume to the row containing its HLC3; value area = contiguous rows around POC accumulating the configured fraction of total volume, expanding toward the higher-volume adjacent row first; VAH and VAL are the outer row boundaries
Calculation: Estimated buy share = clamp(0.17 + 0.43 × (close − low)/max(high − low, minimum tick) + 0.20 × abs(close − open)/max(high − low, minimum tick) + 0.20 × (close ≥ open ? 1 : 0), 0.05, 0.95)
Calculation: CVD = cumulative volume × (2 × estimated buy share − 1), reset at each active session
Calculation: CVD impulse = (fast EMA of CVD − slow EMA of CVD) / max(abs(slow EMA of CVD), volume); the fast EMA has a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the prior confirmed price pivot low AND its corresponding confirmed CVD pivot low is higher than the prior one; bearish CVD divergence = a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the prior confirmed price pivot high AND its corresponding confirmed CVD pivot high is lower than the prior one
Calculation: Profile skew = (total estimated buy pressure − total estimated sell pressure) / total profile volume
Calculation: Bid/ask shelf power = maximum across profile rows of, respectively, positive/negative (buy fraction − 0.5) × row volume / maximum row volume × 2
Calculation: Bid/ask quality = clamp(32 + location score + shelf score + delta score + divergence score + compression score + favorable profile skew × 18, 0, 100); bid location score is 18 if close > Session VWAP, else 10 if close > POC, else 0; ask location score is 18 if close < Session VWAP, else 10 if close < POC, else 0; shelf score is clamp(shelf power × 34, 0, 18); delta score is clamp((directional CVD impulse − impulse threshold) × 85, 0, 20); divergence score is 14 when the corresponding divergence occurs, else 0; compression score is 8 when value-area width / ATR ≤ 2.6, 4 when ≤ 4, else 0
Calculation: Bid/ask auction bias = clamp(50 ± profile skew × 55 ± clamp((close − Session VWAP) / ATR, −2, 2) × 8, 0, 100), using plus signs for bid and minus signs for ask
Long: bullish CVD divergence OR bid auction bias ≥ 58 OR (confirmed bar AND active session AND price above Session VWAP, at or above VAL, and at or below VWAP + 2 standard deviations AND fast CVD EMA > slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse > impulse threshold AND bid quality ≥ quality gate AND bid quality > ask quality AND (close crosses above POC from at/below it OR low ≤ VAL + max(0.20 × ATR, 5 minimum ticks) AND close > VAL AND close > open OR bullish CVD divergence OR bid shelf power > shelf threshold) AND signal cooldown has elapsed
Short: bearish CVD divergence OR ask auction bias ≥ 58 OR (confirmed bar AND active session AND price below Session VWAP, at or below VAH, and at or above VWAP − 2 standard deviations AND fast CVD EMA < slow CVD EMA AND CVD impulse < −impulse threshold AND ask quality ≥ quality gate AND ask quality > bid quality AND (close crosses below POC from at/above it OR high ≥ VAH − max(0.20 × ATR, 5 minimum ticks) AND close < VAH AND close < open OR bearish CVD divergence OR ask shelf power > shelf threshold) AND signal cooldown has elapsed)
Exit: long, confirmed bar AND (bearish CVD divergence OR close < Session VWAP OR close < POC)
Exit: short, confirmed bar AND (bullish CVD divergence OR close > Session VWAP OR close > POC)
