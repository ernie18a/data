Calculation: Range Filter = Let raw range be |close − prior close|; smooth it with an EMA over a lookback, then smooth that result with an EMA over a longer lookback of 2 × the first lookback − 1 and multiply by a range factor. Initialize the filter at close. On each bar, if close is above the prior filter, set the filter to max(prior filter, close − smoothed range); otherwise set it to min(prior filter, close + smoothed range). Its direction is rising when it increases and falling when it decreases; otherwise retain the previous direction.
Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi = EMA-smooth chart-timeframe OHLC, calculate Heikin-Ashi from the smoothed OHLC (HA close is their average; initial HA open is the average of smoothed open and close; subsequent HA open is the average of prior HA open and prior HA close), then EMA-smooth the resulting HA open and close. Apply the same calculation to the higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Stochastic Momentum Index = Let highest high and lowest low use the same lookback. Double-EMA-smooth close − (highest high + lowest low)/2 and highest high − lowest low, using the two smoothing lengths. Divide the smoothed first value by half the smoothed second value and multiply by 100; use 0 if the denominator is 0.
Calculation: Trailing stop = At entry, long: entry price − trailing distance; short: entry price + trailing distance. Each subsequent bar, long: max(previous stop, close − trailing distance); short: min(previous stop, close + trailing distance). Trailing distance is a multiplier of chart-timeframe ATR.
Calculation: Take-profit price = If enabled, long: entry price + reward multiple × initial trailing distance; short: entry price − reward multiple × initial trailing distance.
Long: Flat AND warmed up (bar index > 4 × Range Filter lookback, smoothed Range Filter range available, and ATR available) AND bars since last exit ≥ cooldown AND long direction enabled AND Range Filter direction is rising AND chart-timeframe smoothed Heikin-Ashi close ≥ open AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Heikin-Ashi close ≥ open, if higher-timeframe agreement is enabled) AND current bullish agreement was absent on the prior bar AND (Stochastic Momentum Index < upper limit, if its filter is enabled).
Short: Flat AND warmed up (bar index > 4 × Range Filter lookback, smoothed Range Filter range available, and ATR available) AND bars since last exit ≥ cooldown AND short direction enabled AND Range Filter direction is falling AND chart-timeframe smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < open AND (higher-timeframe smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < open, if higher-timeframe agreement is enabled) AND current bearish agreement was absent on the prior bar AND (Stochastic Momentum Index > negative upper limit, if its filter is enabled).
Exit: Long, low ≤ trailing stop OR (take-profit enabled AND high ≥ take-profit price) OR (Range Filter flip exit enabled AND Range Filter direction is falling).
Exit: Short, high ≥ trailing stop OR (take-profit enabled AND low ≤ take-profit price) OR (Range Filter flip exit enabled AND Range Filter direction is rising).
