Calculation: KAMA-smoothed ATR = Average True Range recursively smoothed using the Kaufman efficiency ratio of close changes: ER = absolute close change over the efficiency lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback; smoothing coefficient = (ER × (2/3 − 2/31) + 2/31)².
Calculation: Volume Z-score = (volume − its rolling mean) / its rolling standard deviation; Stage 2 ATR factor multiplier = clamp(1 + 0.4 × Volume Z-score, 0.5, 2.5).
Calculation: Hurst estimate = clamp(log(R/S over the longer return window ÷ R/S over the shorter return window) / log(longer window ÷ shorter window), 0.3, 0.7), defaulting to 0.5 if unavailable; R/S is the range of cumulative deviations of log returns from their rolling mean divided by their rolling standard deviation. The longer window exceeds the shorter window. Stage 3 ATR factor multiplier = clamp(1 + (Hurst estimate − 0.5), 0.5, 1.8).
Calculation: Stage 1 source = midpoint of the rolling highest high and lowest low.
Calculation: Cascaded Supertrend stages = for each stage, form upper/lower bands as source ± (stage ATR factor × KAMA-smoothed ATR); ratchet the lower band up to the prior lower band unless the current lower band is higher or the prior source was below the prior lower band, and ratchet the upper band down to the prior upper band unless the current upper band is lower or the prior source was above the prior upper band. Initialize direction bullish when prior ATR is unavailable; thereafter, if prior Supertrend equals prior upper band, direction is bullish when source exceeds the current upper band and bearish otherwise; otherwise direction is bearish when source is at least the current lower band and bullish otherwise. Output the lower band in bullish direction and upper band in bearish direction. Stage 1 uses the range midpoint as source and its base factor; Stage 2 uses Stage 1 output as source and its base factor multiplied by the volume multiplier; Stage 3 uses Stage 2 output as source and its base factor multiplied by the Hurst multiplier. Direction is bullish for the lower-band output and bearish for the upper-band output.
Long: Stage 3 changes to bullish direction on a confirmed bar OR (Stage 3 is bullish AND open < Stage 2 output AND close > Stage 2 output AND the same bounce condition was false on the prior bar) OR all three stage directions are bullish OR Stage 3 changes to bullish direction on a confirmed bar AND Stage 2 direction is bullish on the current bar or either of the two preceding bars.
Short: Stage 3 changes to bearish direction on a confirmed bar OR (Stage 3 is bearish AND open > Stage 2 output AND close < Stage 2 output AND the same bounce condition was false on the prior bar) OR all three stage directions are bearish OR Stage 3 changes to bearish direction on a confirmed bar AND Stage 2 direction is bearish on the current bar or either of the two preceding bars.
