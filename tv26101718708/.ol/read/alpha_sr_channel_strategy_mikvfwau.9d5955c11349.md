Calculation: Channel = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using that timeframe’s still-forming bar, plus/minus half the configured width; width is configured as a percentage of current close, a multiple of higher-timeframe ATR, or a fixed price distance.
Calculation: Engulfing range bounds = configured minimum and maximum, each calculated as a percentage of current close, a multiple of chart-timeframe ATR, or a fixed price distance.
Calculation: Bullish engulf = current candle is green; previous candle is red or is green with body no larger than a configured fraction of the minimum engulfing range; and, in body-only mode, current close exceeds the previous open (or previous high for a red previous candle) while current open is no more than the allowed gap above previous close; otherwise current low is below previous low and current high above previous high.
Calculation: Bearish engulf = current candle is red; previous candle is green or is red with body no larger than a configured fraction of the minimum engulfing range; and, in body-only mode, current close is below the previous open (or previous low for a green previous candle) while current open is no more than the allowed gap below previous close; otherwise current low is below previous low and current high above previous high.
Calculation: Bullish pin-and-engulf = previous candle has a lower wick longer than its upper wick, with the longer wick meeting configured wick-to-body and wick-to-range minimums, body-to-range maximum, and minimum range; its low is below the lowest low of the configured number of bars preceding it; current candle is green and closes above previous high; and current body is within the configured pin-pattern minimum and engulfing maximum ranges.
Calculation: Bearish pin-and-engulf = previous candle has an upper wick at least as long as its lower wick, with the longer wick meeting configured wick-to-body and wick-to-range minimums, body-to-range maximum, and minimum range; its high is above the highest high of the configured number of bars preceding it; current candle is red and closes below previous low; and current body is within the configured pin-pattern minimum and engulfing maximum ranges.
Calculation: Channel trend state turns long when current and previous closes are above the upper channel band, and turns short when current and previous closes are below the lower channel band; otherwise it retains its prior state.
Long: chart timeframe equals the configured execution timeframe AND execution timeframe <= channel timeframe AND channel timeframe <= separate trend-filter timeframe when that filter is enabled AND channel trend state is long AND current open is within the channel AND (bullish engulf within configured range bounds OR bullish pin-and-engulf) AND trade/session limits permit entry AND not in configured no-trade window AND Friday trading is enabled or current day is not Friday AND RSI is at or below its configured upper limit when enabled AND (close is above the shorter-lookback chart-timeframe EMA when enabled OR bullish pin-and-engulf) AND squeeze momentum value is <= 0 when enabled AND Bollinger Band width is at or above its configured minimum when enabled AND close is above the separate higher-timeframe EMA when enabled.
Short: chart timeframe equals the configured execution timeframe AND execution timeframe <= channel timeframe AND channel timeframe <= separate trend-filter timeframe when that filter is enabled AND channel trend state is short AND current open is within the channel AND (bearish engulf within configured range bounds OR bearish pin-and-engulf) AND trade/session limits permit entry AND not in configured no-trade window AND Friday trading is enabled or current day is not Friday AND RSI is at or above its configured lower limit when enabled AND (close is below the shorter-lookback chart-timeframe EMA when enabled OR bearish pin-and-engulf) AND squeeze momentum value is >= 0 when enabled AND Bollinger Band width is at or above its configured minimum when enabled AND close is below the separate higher-timeframe EMA when enabled.
Exit: Long, low <= configured stop (entry-bar low minus buffer, configured lookback low minus buffer, lower channel band minus buffer, or entry price minus fixed distance) OR high >= entry price + (entry price - stop) × configured reward multiple OR configured day/week-end close time occurs.
Exit: Short, high >= configured stop (entry-bar high plus buffer, configured lookback high plus buffer, upper channel band plus buffer, or entry price plus fixed distance) OR low <= entry price - (stop - entry price) × configured reward multiple OR configured day/week-end close time occurs.
