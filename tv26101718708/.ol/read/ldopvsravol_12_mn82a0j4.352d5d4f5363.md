Calculation: Prior 10-bar average volume = mean volume of the ten bars before the current bar
Calculation: Volume-spread = (high − low) × volume
Calculation: Vector volume = volume ≥ 1.5 × prior 10-bar average volume OR current volume-spread ≥ the highest volume-spread of the ten bars before the current bar
Long: close ≥ open AND vector volume
Short: close < open AND vector volume
