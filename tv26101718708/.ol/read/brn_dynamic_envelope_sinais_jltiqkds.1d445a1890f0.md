Calculation: Moving average = selected SMA, EMA, WMA, RMA, or VWMA of the source over one lookback
Calculation: Upper band = moving average of close + (moving average of high − moving average of close) × distance multiplier
Calculation: Lower band = moving average of close − (moving average of close − moving average of low) × distance multiplier
Long: After close crosses above the upper band, enter when close crosses above that breakout bar’s high or open > that high; keep the trigger active until entry or close crosses below the lower band
Short: After close crosses below the lower band, enter when close crosses below that breakout bar’s low or open < that low; keep the trigger active until entry or close crosses above the upper band
