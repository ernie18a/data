Calculation: WaveTrend = WT1 is EMA of (source − EMA(source, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel length)), channel length)); WT2 is a moving average of WT1; source is typical price.
Calculation: Regular divergence = compare oscillator values at successive confirmed local highs or lows (each pivot is the center of a five-bar window): bullish when price makes a lower pivot low and oscillator a higher pivot low; bearish when price makes a higher pivot high and oscillator a lower pivot high. For WaveTrend, also evaluate the same comparison with the second set of pivot limits.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = smoothed stochastic of RSI calculated from log(close).
Calculation: Money-flow area = moving average of ((close − open) / (high − low)) × volume multiplier, minus its vertical offset.
Long: WT2 < WT1 OR WT1 crosses above WT2 while WT2 is at or below the oversold level OR regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR regular bullish WaveTrend or RSI divergence AND the prior WaveTrend pivot low is at or below the deeper oversold level AND current WT2 is above that level AND the prior pivot low minus current WT2 is at most −5 AND RSI at the prior WaveTrend pivot is below 30 OR money-flow area is above its bullish level AND WT2 is below its bullish level AND WT1 crosses above WT2 AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend VWAP is above its bullish level OR WT2 is at or below its bullish level AND WT1 crosses above WT2 AND both higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles are bullish.
Short: WT2 > WT1 OR WT1 crosses below WT2 while WT2 is at or above the overbought level OR regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI OR money-flow area is below its bearish level AND WT2 is above its bearish level AND WT1 crosses below WT2 AND higher-timeframe WaveTrend VWAP is below its bearish level OR WT2 is at or above its bearish level AND WT1 crosses below WT2 AND both higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi candles are bearish.
Exit: long, WT2 > WT1 OR WT1 crosses below WT2 while WT2 is at or above the overbought level OR regular bearish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI.
Exit: short, WT2 ≤ WT1 OR WT1 crosses above WT2 while WT2 is at or below the oversold level OR regular bullish divergence in WaveTrend, RSI, or Stochastic RSI.
