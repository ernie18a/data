Calculation: Deviation oscillator = selected price minus its EMA or linear-regression equilibrium, divided by ATR; clamp the result, then use it directly, standardize it by a longer-window mean and standard deviation, or robust-standardize it by the longer-window median and median absolute deviation.
Calculation: Adaptive band = selected base threshold × volatility ratio, where volatility ratio is ATR divided by its longer-window average and is capped at selected minimum and maximum multipliers; the lower band is the negative of the upper band.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = sign of the last completed higher-timeframe close relative to its EMA.
Calculation: Confluence = count of matching higher-timeframe bias, ranging ADX regime, recent same-direction divergence, calm volatility (volatility ratio ≤ 1), and healthy spread when enabled.
Long: Deviation oscillator crosses above the lower band AND remains at least the selected clearance above it AND higher-timeframe bias is bullish when that gate is enabled AND ADX is below its trend threshold when that gate is enabled AND the intraday session skip has elapsed when enabled AND oscillator warm-up is complete AND the chart bar is confirmed when enabled AND long confluence meets its minimum AND bullish agreement across the three higher timeframes meets its minimum.
Long: At a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price makes a lower low than at the previous pivot low AND oscillator makes a higher low by at least the selected minimum gap (regular bullish divergence); OR price makes a higher low AND oscillator makes a lower low by at least the selected minimum gap (hidden bullish divergence). Pivot spacing and divergence cooldown must be within their selected limits.
Short: Deviation oscillator crosses below the upper band AND remains at least the selected clearance below it AND higher-timeframe bias is bearish when that gate is enabled AND ADX is below its trend threshold when that gate is enabled AND the intraday session skip has elapsed when enabled AND oscillator warm-up is complete AND the chart bar is confirmed when enabled AND short confluence meets its minimum AND bearish agreement across the three higher timeframes meets its minimum.
Short: At a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price makes a higher high than at the previous pivot high AND oscillator makes a lower high by at least the selected minimum gap (regular bearish divergence); OR price makes a lower high AND oscillator makes a higher high by at least the selected minimum gap (hidden bearish divergence). Pivot spacing and divergence cooldown must be within their selected limits.
