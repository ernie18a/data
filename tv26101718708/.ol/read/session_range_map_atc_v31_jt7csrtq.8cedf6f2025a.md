Calculation: Prior-session high = highest high during the previous regular session
Calculation: Prior-session low = lowest low during the previous regular session
Calculation: Pre-market high = highest high during the current pre-market session
Calculation: Pre-market low = lowest low during the current pre-market session
Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high from the regular-session open through the end of the selected opening-range window
Calculation: Opening-range low = lowest low from the regular-session open through the end of the selected opening-range window
Long: previous close ≤ prior-session high AND current close > prior-session high OR previous close ≤ pre-market high AND current close > pre-market high OR opening range is locked AND current close > opening-range high + minimum price increment
Short: previous close ≥ prior-session low AND current close < prior-session low OR previous close ≥ pre-market low AND current close < pre-market low OR opening range is locked AND current close < opening-range low − minimum price increment
