Calculation: EMA momentum score = +2 when the short EMA > the medium EMA and close > the longer trend EMA, −2 when the short EMA < the medium EMA and close < the longer trend EMA; +1 when close > the longest EMA, −1 when close < it; +2 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, −2 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; +1 on a MACD bullish crossover, −1 on a bearish crossover; +1 when RSI > its bullish level, −1 when RSI < its bearish level; +1 when ADX ≥ its minimum and +DI > −DI, −1 when ADX ≥ its minimum and −DI > +DI; +1 when volume > its moving average and close > open, −1 when volume > its moving average and close < open; plus +1 when the higher-timeframe close > its EMA or −1 when it is < its EMA, if higher-timeframe trend scoring is enabled. The short, medium, trend, and longest EMA lookbacks increase in that order. The higher-timeframe close and EMA use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Oscillator score = +2 when RSI ≥ its bullish level, −2 when RSI ≤ its bearish level; +1 when RSI crosses up through its buy level, −1 when it crosses down through its sell level; +2 when smoothed Stochastic %K > %D and %K > 50, −2 when %K < %D and %K < 50; +1 on a %K/%D bullish crossover while %K < the sell level, −1 on a bearish crossover while %K > the buy level; +1 when MACD line > signal line and histogram > 0, −2 when MACD line < signal line and histogram < 0; +1 on a MACD bullish crossover, −1 on a bearish crossover; plus +1 when the fast EMA > the slow EMA and close > the trend EMA or −1 when fast EMA < slow EMA and close < trend EMA, if the trend filter is enabled; plus +1 when volume > its moving average and close > open or −1 when volume > its moving average and close < open, if the volume filter is enabled. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Mean = EMA of close.
Calculation: Mean-reversion stretch = ATR-based distance between close and Mean.
Calculation: Entry stop distance = ATR × the configured stop multiplier.
Long: In Auto mode on a lower chart timeframe, or in Mean Reversion mode, close < Mean − the configured ATR stretch AND short-period RSI < its oversold level AND the long trend gate passes (if required, close > the longest EMA and, when enabled and available, higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA). In momentum modes, use the selected signal rule: EMA-only, EMA momentum score ≥ its threshold and previous score < threshold; oscillator-only, oscillator score ≥ its threshold and previous score < threshold; Either, either of those first-crossing conditions; Both Agree, both scores meet their thresholds AND at least one did not meet its threshold on the previous bar. Require the long trend gate AND, when enabled, ADX ≥ its minimum AND, when enabled, close − shortest EMA ≤ ATR × maximum extension AND RSI < its maximum.
Short: In Auto mode on a lower chart timeframe, or in Mean Reversion mode, close > Mean + the configured ATR stretch AND short-period RSI > its overbought level AND short trading is enabled AND the short trend gate passes (if required, close < the longest EMA and, when enabled and available, higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA). In momentum modes, use the selected signal rule: EMA-only, EMA momentum score ≤ −threshold and previous score > −threshold; oscillator-only, oscillator score ≤ −threshold and previous score > −threshold; Either, either of those first-crossing conditions; Both Agree, both scores meet their negative thresholds AND at least one did not meet its threshold on the previous bar. Require short trading enabled AND the short trend gate AND, when enabled, ADX ≥ its minimum AND, when enabled, shortest EMA − close ≤ ATR × maximum extension AND RSI > its minimum.
Long: EMA momentum score crosses from below its strong-buy threshold to at or above it (BUY marking).
Short: EMA momentum score crosses from above its negative strong-sell threshold to at or below it (SELL marking).
Long: EMA momentum score ≥ its strong-buy threshold AND oscillator score ≥ its strong-buy threshold (strong bullish bar marking).
Short: EMA momentum score ≤ its negative strong-sell threshold AND oscillator score ≤ its negative strong-sell threshold (strong bearish bar marking).
Long: EMA momentum score ≥ its buy level OR oscillator score ≥ its buy level (bullish bar marking).
Short: EMA momentum score ≤ its negative sell level OR oscillator score ≤ its negative sell level (bearish bar marking).
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot low, price makes a lower low and the oscillator a higher low than at the prior pivot low; regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed oscillator pivot high, price makes a higher high and the oscillator a lower high than at the prior pivot high. Hidden bullish divergence = pivot low price makes a higher low and oscillator a lower low; hidden bearish divergence = pivot high price makes a lower high and oscillator a higher high. Compare pivots separated by the configured minimum and maximum bars; confirm each pivot after the configured right-side bars. Oscillator is selected from RSI, MACD histogram, or Stochastic %K.
Long: Regular bullish divergence OR enabled hidden bullish divergence.
Short: Regular bearish divergence OR enabled hidden bearish divergence.
Exit: Long, close ≤ entry price − ATR × configured stop multiplier OR (in mean-reversion mode, close ≥ Mean) OR (in momentum mode, close ≥ entry price + entry stop distance × reward/risk target) OR the configured maximum holding bars have elapsed OR (outside mean-reversion mode, an opposite sell signal occurs when flip exits are enabled) OR (outside mean-reversion mode, both scores equal zero when neutral exits are enabled) OR an enabled session-flatten condition occurs.
Exit: Short, close ≥ entry price + ATR × configured stop multiplier OR (in mean-reversion mode, close ≤ Mean) OR (in momentum mode, close ≤ entry price − entry stop distance × reward/risk target) OR the configured maximum holding bars have elapsed OR (outside mean-reversion mode, an opposite buy signal occurs when flip exits are enabled) OR (outside mean-reversion mode, both scores equal zero when neutral exits are enabled) OR an enabled session-flatten condition occurs.
