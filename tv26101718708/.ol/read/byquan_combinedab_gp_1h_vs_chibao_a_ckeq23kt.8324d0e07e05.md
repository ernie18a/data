Calculation: Long retest setup = After the fast EMA crosses above either the mid or slow EMA, record the fast EMA as the impulse start and peak. While the fast EMA rises, update the peak; after two consecutive declines, lock the peak. Once the fast EMA has pulled back from the peak by at least the configured fraction of the rise from impulse start to peak, the setup is ready. The retest level is peak minus that rise times (1 − configured early-retest ratio). Fast, mid, and slow EMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Short retest setup = After the fast EMA crosses below either the mid or slow EMA, record the fast EMA as the impulse start and bottom. While the fast EMA falls, update the bottom; after two consecutive rises, lock the bottom. Once the fast EMA has pulled back from the bottom by at least the configured fraction of the drop from impulse start to bottom, the setup is ready. The retest level is bottom plus that drop times (1 − configured early-retest ratio). Fast, mid, and slow EMA lookbacks are ordered shortest to longest.
Calculation: Normalized SRSI = For each of four higher timeframes, calculate the close-based SRSI on the last completed bar: RSI of close, stochastic of that RSI over its lookback, smoothed by the configured K average, then smoothed by the configured D average; add K and D. Sum the four timeframe values and divide by 8.
Calculation: Recent confirmed momentum low = When normalized SRSI is at or above its configured high watch level and neither watch state is active, start high-watch; trigger when it falls to or below 50. Track the lowest value from that trigger, resetting the confirmation count whenever a lower value occurs; otherwise increment the count. Confirm the low after the configured number of consecutive bars without a new low. True while a confirmed low occurred within the configured recent-bar window.
Calculation: Recent confirmed momentum high = When normalized SRSI is at or below its configured low watch level and neither watch state is active, start low-watch; trigger when it rises to or above 50. Track the highest value from that trigger, resetting the confirmation count whenever a higher value occurs; otherwise increment the count. Confirm the high after the configured number of consecutive bars without a new high. True while a confirmed high occurred within the configured recent-bar window.
Long: Long retest setup is ready AND fast EMA ≥ retest level AND mid EMA > slow EMA AND current chart bar is closed AND recent confirmed momentum low
Short: Short retest setup is ready AND fast EMA ≤ retest level AND mid EMA < slow EMA AND current chart bar is closed AND recent confirmed momentum high
