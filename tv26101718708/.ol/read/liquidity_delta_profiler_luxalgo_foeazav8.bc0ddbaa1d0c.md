Calculation: BSL/SSL zones = Confirmed swing-high/swing-low zones: a swing is the highest/lowest high/low over the pivot lookback on both sides. A BSL zone spans from the pivot high to max(open, close) on the pivot bar; an SSL zone spans from min(open, close) to the pivot low. If a zone is narrower than 0.1 ATR on the pivot bar, extend it to that minimum width. Divide each zone into four equal price bands, numbered from bottom to top.
Calculation: Bar delta = (high − low = 0 ? 0 : volume × (close − open) / (high − low)). Allocate each bar's delta to each zone band in proportion to the bar's price-range overlap with that band; accumulate these allocations while the zone is active.
Calculation: Outer-delta share = abs(outer-band accumulated delta) / (sum of absolute accumulated deltas across all four bands + 0.0001). The outer band is the top band for BSL and bottom band for SSL.
Calculation: Zone midpoint = (zone top + zone bottom) / 2.
Long: An active, not-yet-signaled SSL zone is hit or swept, and total absolute accumulated band delta > 0, and at least one holds: (low < zone bottom AND outer-band accumulated delta > 0 AND outer-delta share > 0.2) OR (low < zone bottom AND outer-delta share < 0.1) OR (close is within the zone AND outer-band accumulated delta < 0 AND outer-delta share > 0.6) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND abs(bar delta) / (volume + 0.0001) > 0.2 AND none of the preceding three signal conditions holds).
Short: An active, not-yet-signaled BSL zone is hit or swept, and total absolute accumulated band delta > 0, and at least one holds: (high > zone top AND outer-band accumulated delta < 0 AND outer-delta share > 0.2) OR (high > zone top AND outer-delta share < 0.1) OR (close is within the zone AND outer-band accumulated delta > 0 AND outer-delta share > 0.6) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND abs(bar delta) / (volume + 0.0001) > 0.2 AND none of the preceding three signal conditions holds).
