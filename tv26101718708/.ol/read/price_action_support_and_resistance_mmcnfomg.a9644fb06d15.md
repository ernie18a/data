Calculation: Support/resistance = confirmed pivot low/high using pivot-strength bars on each side; merge a new pivot into the existing same-side level when within the ATR-based zone by averaging it into the level, otherwise reset the level and touch count; count distinct reactions in the zone only when separated by the minimum touch spacing; level is valid at the minimum signal-touch count.
Calculation: ATR-based zone = ATR × zone-width factor.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) × rejection ratio AND lower wick > upper wick.
Calculation: Bearish rejection = close < open AND upper wick ≥ max(abs(close − open), minimum price increment) × rejection ratio AND upper wick > lower wick.
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = close > open AND prior close < prior open AND open ≤ prior close AND close ≥ prior open.
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = close < open AND prior close > prior open AND open ≥ prior close AND close ≤ prior open.
Calculation: Previous-bar low = lowest low of the configured lookback over completed chart-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar.
Long: Flat AND normal-entry cooldown elapsed AND ((valid support AND price intersects support zone AND (low < support − 0.2 × zone AND close > support OR bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing)) OR (valid resistance breakout closes above resistance + 0.2 × zone after the prior close was at or below that threshold, followed within the configured retest window by a later bar intersecting the stored breakout level, closing at or above it, and showing bullish rejection or bullish engulfing)); OR flat AND re-entry armed, unused, and within its post-exit window AND close crosses above saved setup level + ATR × re-entry buffer from a prior close at or below that threshold AND (bullish rejection OR bullish engulfing OR close > open AND close > prior close).
Exit: Long, (before a qualifying resistance warning, close < the previous-bar low captured at entry) OR (after a qualifying resistance warning, close < the rolling previous-bar low); evaluate only after the entry bar or warning-activation bar, respectively. A qualifying resistance warning is a valid resistance-zone bearish rejection or bearish engulfing, or a valid resistance failed breakout (high > resistance + 0.2 × zone AND close < resistance); warning cooldown applies.
