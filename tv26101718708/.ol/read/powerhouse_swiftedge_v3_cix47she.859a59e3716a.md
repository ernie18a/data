Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a pivot high/low with the structure lookback bars on each side; usable only after confirmation.
Calculation: Trend score = EMA-smoothed, clipped combination of normalized adaptive moving-average slope and normalized close-to-average distance; the adaptive average is Kaufman’s adaptive moving average. Use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative lower-timeframe volume classified as buying when candle close ≥ open and selling otherwise; when lower-timeframe candles are unavailable, estimate delta by allocating chart-bar volume according to close’s position within its high-low range.
Calculation: Stop level = entry price minus (long) or plus (short) risk; risk is either an ATR multiple or the distance to the opposite swing plus an ATR buffer, constrained to configured minimum and maximum ATR distances.
Calculation: Target level = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) a configured distance, measured in risk multiples, ATR multiples, or points.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, close > the latest unbroken confirmed swing high AND the selected structure-break type permits the break (CHoCH, BOS, or both) AND every enabled filter agrees AND the session filter passes if enabled AND, when one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last signal was not long; OR close is below that swing high and within the configured ATR distance of it AND every enabled filter except momentum and breakout agrees AND the session filter passes if enabled AND, when one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last signal was not long (Get Ready long pre-signal).
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, close < the latest unbroken confirmed swing low AND the selected structure-break type permits the break (CHoCH, BOS, or both) AND every enabled filter agrees AND the session filter passes if enabled AND, when one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last signal was not short; OR close is above that swing low and within the configured ATR distance of it AND every enabled filter except momentum and breakout agrees AND the session filter passes if enabled AND, when one-signal-per-direction is enabled, the last signal was not short (Get Ready short pre-signal).
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop level OR high ≥ any enabled target level OR a short signal occurs.
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop level OR low ≤ any enabled target level OR a long signal occurs.
