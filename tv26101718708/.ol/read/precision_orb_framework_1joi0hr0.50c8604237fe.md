Calculation: Opening range high/low = opening bar's body high/low, or its high/low if full-range mode is selected; reset each day.
Calculation: Close position (%) = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low); use 50 when high = low.
Calculation: Bull acceptance = consecutive closes above opening range high during the trade window reach the configured count, or acceptance is disabled.
Calculation: Bear acceptance = consecutive closes below opening range low during the trade window reach the configured count, or acceptance is disabled.
Calculation: VWAP = session volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3.
Calculation: Bias EMA = EMA of close; compare its current value and VWAP with their respective values at the configured lookback on the same chart timeframe.
Calculation: Strong bullish bias = close > VWAP AND close > Bias EMA AND VWAP rising AND Bias EMA rising.
Calculation: Strong bearish bias = close < VWAP AND close < Bias EMA AND VWAP falling AND Bias EMA falling.
Calculation: Up REV warning = first warning of the day AND bullish breakout AND high exceeds the latest confirmed swing high by at least the configured sweep distance AND (upper wick is at least the configured share of bar range OR close position is below the configured bullish strength threshold).
Calculation: Down REV warning = first warning of the day AND bearish breakout AND low is below the latest confirmed swing low by at least the configured sweep distance AND (lower wick is at least the configured share of bar range OR close position is above the configured bearish strength threshold).
Long: confirmed bar AND opening range is set AND within the configured trade window AND close > opening range high AND close − opening range high ≥ configured break distance AND (acceptance is disabled OR bull acceptance) AND no earlier entry signal that day AND Thursday is not disabled by configuration AND close position ≥ configured bullish strength threshold AND (bias filter is disabled OR countertrend blocking is disabled OR NOT strong bearish bias) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (REV suppression is disabled OR NOT up REV warning)
Short: confirmed bar AND opening range is set AND within the configured trade window AND close < opening range low AND opening range low − close ≥ configured break distance AND (acceptance is disabled OR bear acceptance) AND no earlier entry signal that day AND Thursday is not disabled by configuration AND close position ≤ 100 − configured bearish strength threshold AND (bias filter is disabled OR countertrend blocking is disabled OR NOT strong bullish bias) AND (VWAP filter is disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (REV suppression is disabled OR NOT down REV warning)
