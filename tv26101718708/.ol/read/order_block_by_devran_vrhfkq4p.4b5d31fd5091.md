Calculation: Normalized range percentile = (current bar’s high−low − lowest high−low over the lookback) / (highest high−low − lowest high−low over the lookback) × 100; use 50 if the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low; bearish sweep = high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high. The swing uses equal left and right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Bullish MSS = a bullish sweep followed within the confirmation window by close > the swing high captured at the sweep AND close−open > ATR-scaled displacement; bearish MSS = a bearish sweep followed within the window by close < the swing low captured at the sweep AND open−close > ATR-scaled displacement. Use chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Order-block score = base score plus points for a recent same-direction sweep, strong displacement, recent same-direction MSS, a same-direction fair value gap, aligned higher-timeframe EMA bias, and high normalized range percentile. The score threshold is set by the selected minimum grade.
Long: Bullish MSS OR (prior bar closes below its open AND current bar closes above its open AND prior bearish body exceeds ATR-scaled minimum AND current bullish body exceeds ATR-scaled displacement AND prior normalized range percentile meets the minimum AND order-block score meets the grade threshold AND no live bullish zone overlaps the candidate zone with a score greater than or equal to the candidate score)
Short: Bearish MSS OR (prior bar closes above its open AND current bar closes below its open AND prior bullish body exceeds ATR-scaled minimum AND current bearish body exceeds ATR-scaled displacement AND prior normalized range percentile meets the minimum AND order-block score meets the grade threshold AND no live bearish zone overlaps the candidate zone with a score greater than or equal to the candidate score)
