Calculation: Baseline = adaptive moving average of close, using the chart-timeframe profile’s smoother and lookback.
Calculation: Z-score = the selected close-to-baseline residual (absolute, percentage, or logarithmic) divided by its rolling standard deviation; apply the chart-timeframe profile’s Z-score smoother.
Calculation: Hurst value = EMA-smoothed, clipped log ratio of the rolling variance of the lagged close change to the rolling variance of the one-bar close change, divided by twice the log of the lag.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence = at a confirmed Z-score pivot low, the latest price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while the latest Z-score pivot low is higher than the preceding Z-score pivot low; pivot spacing is within a maximum lookback and the latest Z-score pivot low is below the oversold threshold.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence = at a confirmed Z-score pivot high, the latest price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high while the latest Z-score pivot high is lower than the preceding Z-score pivot high; pivot spacing is within a maximum lookback and the latest Z-score pivot high is above the overbought threshold.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = at a confirmed Z-score pivot low, the latest price pivot low is higher than the preceding price pivot low while the latest Z-score pivot low is lower than the preceding Z-score pivot low; pivot spacing is within a maximum lookback.
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence = at a confirmed Z-score pivot high, the latest price pivot high is lower than the preceding price pivot high while the latest Z-score pivot high is higher than the preceding Z-score pivot high; pivot spacing is within a maximum lookback.
Calculation: Bullish regime agreement = in a mean-reverting regime, regular bullish divergence or Z-score crossing above the negative extreme threshold; in a trending regime, hidden bullish divergence or rising baseline with Z-score below zero; otherwise false.
Calculation: Bearish regime agreement = in a mean-reverting regime, regular bearish divergence or Z-score crossing below the positive extreme threshold; in a trending regime, hidden bearish divergence or falling baseline with Z-score above zero; otherwise false.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish confirmation = smoothed Z-score on a higher timeframe’s last completed bar is below the inner negative threshold, or is positive and falling.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish confirmation = smoothed Z-score on a higher timeframe’s last completed bar is above the inner positive threshold, or is negative and rising.
Calculation: Volume confirmation = current volume is positive and its rolling Z-score is above the positive volume threshold.
Calculation: Bull score = sum of the configured weights for: Z-score crossing above the negative extreme threshold; either bullish divergence; rising baseline; enabled Hurst bullish regime agreement; enabled higher-timeframe bullish confirmation; enabled volume confirmation. Bull percentage = bull score divided by the sum of all component weights, multiplied by 100.
Calculation: Bear score = sum of the configured weights for: Z-score crossing below the positive extreme threshold; either bearish divergence; falling baseline; enabled Hurst bearish regime agreement; enabled higher-timeframe bearish confirmation; enabled volume confirmation. Bear percentage = bear score divided by the sum of all component weights, multiplied by 100.
Long: Regular bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence OR (bull percentage >= conviction threshold AND bull percentage > bear percentage) OR (bull percentage >= weak-signal threshold AND bull percentage > bear percentage AND bull percentage < conviction threshold).
Short: Regular bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence OR (bear percentage >= conviction threshold AND bear percentage > bull percentage) OR (bear percentage >= weak-signal threshold AND bear percentage > bull percentage AND bear percentage < conviction threshold).
