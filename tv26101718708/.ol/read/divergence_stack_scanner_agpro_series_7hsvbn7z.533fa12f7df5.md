Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a high or low greater than or less than the highs or lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; it is usable only after the bars on both sides have completed.
Calculation: Bullish stack = count of RSI, MACD histogram, CCI, MFI, and OBV with regular bullish divergence at confirmed price lows, where the current low is below the previous confirmed low and the oscillator value is above its value at that previous low. Count divergences at the current pivot, or within the configured bar tolerance if near-stacks are enabled; use the exact-pivot count when it meets the minimum stack, otherwise use the tolerance-window count.
Calculation: Bearish stack = count of RSI, MACD histogram, CCI, MFI, and OBV with regular bearish divergence at confirmed price highs, where the current high is above the previous confirmed high and the oscillator value is below its value at that previous high. Count divergences at the current pivot, or within the configured bar tolerance if near-stacks are enabled; use the exact-pivot count when it meets the minimum stack, otherwise use the tolerance-window count.
Calculation: Readiness score = round(clamp to 0–100 of 0.34 × (20 × stack) + 0.20 × swing score + 0.15 × trend score + 0.20 × confirmation score + 0.11 × distance score + sensitivity adjustment). Swing score is 100 × clamp((absolute price change between the current and previous same-direction pivot ÷ ATR at the pivot − 0.25) ÷ (1.80 − 0.25), 0, 1). Distance score is 100 − 100 × clamp((absolute distance from current close to pivot ÷ current ATR − 0.35) ÷ (2.70 − 0.35), 0, 1). For bullish events, trend score is 100 if the shorter-period EMA is below the longer-period EMA, else 68 if close is below the shorter-period EMA, else 42; for bearish events reverse these comparisons. Confirmation is true in pivot-only mode, otherwise bullish confirmation requires close above the pivot bar's high and bearish confirmation requires close below its low; strict-close mode additionally requires bullish close above, or bearish close below, the shorter-period EMA. Confirmation score is 100 when confirmed, 78 in pivot-only mode when unconfirmed, and 38 otherwise. Sensitivity adjustment is a configurable score offset.
Long: Confirmed price pivot low AND bullish stack ≥ minimum stack AND readiness score ≥ max(0, minimum ready score − 14).
Short: Confirmed price pivot high AND bearish stack ≥ minimum stack AND readiness score ≥ max(0, minimum ready score − 14).
