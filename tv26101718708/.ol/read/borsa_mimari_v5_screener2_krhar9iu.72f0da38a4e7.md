Calculation: Trend score = +1 for each true condition and −1 for each false condition: MACD line above signal line; RSI above neutral and not above overbought; ADX above its strength threshold and positive directional indicator above negative directional indicator; close above shorter EMA; close above longer EMA; SuperTrend bullish; volume above its moving average times the high-volume threshold.
Calculation: Dip-zone upper boundary = longer EMA − ATR × dip-zone multiplier adjusted by half an ATR.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = first bar's (open + close) / 2, then (prior Heikin-Ashi open + prior Heikin-Ashi close) / 2; Heikin-Ashi high = maximum of high, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close; Heikin-Ashi low = minimum of low, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close.
Calculation: Falling resistance line = line through the two latest confirmed pivot highs when the earlier pivot high is higher than the later pivot high; extend the line forward using its slope.
Calculation: Dip targets = lowest low over a short lookback plus ATR times each target multiplier; on a fresh full-strength long signal, targets = signal close plus ATR times each target multiplier.
Calculation: Long stop = SuperTrend value at a fresh full-strength long signal, thereafter ratcheted upward to the maximum of its prior value and current SuperTrend value while SuperTrend is bullish; reset when close falls below it.
Long: SuperTrend bullish AND close below dip-zone upper boundary AND MACD line above signal line AND RSI above neutral AND RSI not above overbought AND trend score at least the dip threshold OR SuperTrend bullish AND MACD line above signal line AND RSI above neutral AND RSI not above overbought AND ADX above its strength threshold AND positive directional indicator above negative directional indicator AND close above both the shorter and longer EMAs AND trend score at least the strong-long threshold OR prior close below the lower Bollinger band and current close inside the Bollinger bands OR close crosses above the falling resistance line OR SuperTrend changes from bearish to bullish OR Heikin-Ashi changes from bearish to bullish OR Heikin-Ashi is bullish and its low equals its open.
Short: SuperTrend bearish AND close above the EMA-based peak-zone lower boundary AND MACD line at or below signal line AND RSI not above overbought AND trend score at most the peak threshold OR SuperTrend bearish AND close above the EMA-based peak-zone lower boundary AND MACD line at or below signal line AND RSI below neutral AND volume above its moving average times the high-volume threshold AND trend score at most the strong-peak threshold OR SuperTrend bearish AND MACD line at or below signal line AND RSI below neutral AND ADX above its strength threshold AND positive directional indicator not above negative directional indicator AND close below both the shorter and longer EMAs AND trend score at most the strong-short threshold OR SuperTrend changes from bullish to bearish OR Heikin-Ashi changes from bullish to bearish OR Heikin-Ashi is bearish and its high equals its open OR Heikin-Ashi is bearish and Heikin-Ashi close is greater than the average of Heikin-Ashi open and close OR Heikin-Ashi is bullish and Heikin-Ashi close is less than the average of Heikin-Ashi open and close.
Exit: long, close below the long stop OR high reaches any active dip target or full-strength-long target.
