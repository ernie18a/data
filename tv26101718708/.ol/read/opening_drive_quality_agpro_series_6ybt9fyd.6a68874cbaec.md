Calculation: Opening-drive direction = bullish if the opening-drive close is above its open, bearish if below; if equal, bullish when the close is at or above the drive midpoint, otherwise bearish
Calculation: Opening-drive quality score = weighted score from drive-body efficiency, direction-adjusted close location within the drive range, drive range divided by ATR, and (when available and enabled) average drive volume divided by its recent volume average; clamp component scores and total to 0–100
Calculation: Drive follow-through = maximum favorable price extension beyond the locked opening-drive high (bullish) or low (bearish), divided by ATR
Calculation: Pullback zone = the portion of the locked drive range extending inward from its high (bullish) or low (bearish); risk line = beyond the drive low (bullish) or high (bearish), at least as far as the outer edge of the pullback zone plus an ATR buffer
Calculation: Readiness score = 45% of opening-drive quality score + 32% of follow-through score + 23% of pullback score; follow-through score scales extension relative to the required ATR extension and is capped at 100; pullback score is based on whether price overlaps and holds the pullback zone, whether the risk line is broken, and whether follow-through is confirmed
Long: a bullish opening drive locks and its quality score is at least the minimum quality threshold or minimum label quality; OR a bullish locked drive's favorable extension reaches the required ATR extension and readiness score is at least the minimum quality threshold or minimum label quality; OR those follow-through and readiness conditions hold and price overlaps the pullback zone, closes at or above its lower edge, and has not broken the risk line
Short: a bearish opening drive locks and its quality score is at least the minimum quality threshold or minimum label quality; OR a bearish locked drive's favorable extension reaches the required ATR extension and readiness score is at least the minimum quality threshold or minimum label quality; OR those follow-through and readiness conditions hold and price overlaps the pullback zone, closes at or below its upper edge, and has not broken the risk line
Exit: long, a bullish locked drive's close is below its risk line
Exit: short, a bearish locked drive's close is above its risk line
