Calculation: Swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low on chart-timeframe bars, requiring bars on both sides of the pivot.
Calculation: Average daily range = mean of (high − low) / abs(low) over daily bars; daily bars are higher timeframe bars when the chart timeframe is lower, using the still-forming bar.
Calculation: Selected trailing moving average = one of two shorter-lookback moving averages, calculated from close on the selected higher timeframe using its still-forming bar, or on chart-timeframe bars if selected.
Long: chart-bar high crosses above the latest confirmed swing high AND, when the slowest moving-average filter is enabled, swing high > slowest moving average AND, when the range filter is enabled, swing high − prior-bar swing low < the configured fraction of average daily range.
Exit: long, chart-bar low <= stop, where stop is the latest swing low until the prior swing low exceeds the entry price, after which that swing-low stop is held; use the low of the latest bar whose low crossed below the selected trailing moving average instead when that crossing occurred after entry, the prior close is above that crossing low, and the prior close is above the selected trailing moving average.
