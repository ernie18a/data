Calculation: Opening-range high = highest high of chart-timeframe bars in the configured opening-range session; opening-range low = lowest low of those bars; opening-range midpoint = their average. Use these levels after that session ends.
Calculation: Initial stop = configured stop level: opening-range midpoint, opposite opening-range boundary, or configured distance from entry price.
Calculation: Risk distance = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. Profit targets = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) risk distance multiplied by each configured target multiple. Current stop = initial stop, moved to entry price after the first target is reached when break-even movement is enabled.
Long: Opening range is complete AND no ORB signal has fired that day AND ((breakout mode AND current close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high) OR (retest mode AND a bullish breakout is pending, the current bar is before its timeout, current low <= the configured retest level (opening-range high or midpoint), and current close > opening-range high)). A pending bullish breakout is canceled if a later close <= opening-range high or its timeout expires.
Short: Opening range is complete AND no ORB signal has fired that day AND ((breakout mode AND current close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low) OR (retest mode AND a bearish breakout is pending, the current bar is before its timeout, current high >= the configured retest level (opening-range low or midpoint), and current close < opening-range low)). A pending bearish breakout is canceled if a later close >= opening-range low or its timeout expires.
Exit: long, current low <= current stop OR current high >= an enabled profit target
Exit: short, current high >= current stop OR current low <= an enabled profit target
