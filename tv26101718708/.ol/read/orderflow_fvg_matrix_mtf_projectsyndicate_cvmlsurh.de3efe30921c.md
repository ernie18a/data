Calculation: Estimated delta = sum over the three FVG candles of [2 × volume × (close − low) / (high − low) − volume], using half volume as buying volume when high = low.
Calculation: Wick absorption volume = sum over the three FVG candles of [volume × (upper wick + lower wick) / (2 × range)], with zero for a zero range.
Calculation: FVG strength = 10 × clamp(weighted mean, 0, 1), where the configurable weights apply to: gap size / (1.5 × higher-timeframe ATR); direction-aligned delta / total volume; total volume / (6 × higher-timeframe average volume); absolute delta / total volume; absolute middle-candle body / its range; and 2 × wick absorption volume / total volume. Clamp each factor to [0, 1]; use zero for a denominator that is not positive.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, a new bullish FVG on any enabled higher timeframe, using completed higher-timeframe bars, where the last completed bar's low > the low from three bars back's high and the gap meets the configured minimum gap filter (ATR-based or percent-of-price); OR a bullish zone is created with strength at least the configured minimum.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, a new bearish FVG on any enabled higher timeframe, using completed higher-timeframe bars, where the last completed bar's high < the low from three bars back and the gap meets the configured minimum gap filter (ATR-based or percent-of-price); OR a bearish zone is created with strength at least the configured minimum.
Exit: Long, current chart bar low <= the active bullish zone's mitigation boundary (selected touch, half-fill, or full-fill rule).
Exit: Short, current chart bar high >= the active bearish zone's mitigation boundary (selected touch, half-fill, or full-fill rule).
