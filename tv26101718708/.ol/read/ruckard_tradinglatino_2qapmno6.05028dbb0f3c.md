Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close minus the average of the midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the lookback and the lookback SMA of close
Calculation: Session VPOC = price of the highest-volume row in a volume profile built by dividing the rolling high-low range into price rows and allocating each bar’s volume in proportion to its overlap with each row
Long: Squeeze momentum < 0 AND current squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum OR Squeeze momentum > 0 AND current squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum
Short: Squeeze momentum > 0 AND current squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum OR Squeeze momentum < 0 AND current squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum
Long: Fast EMA > slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars AND (Squeeze momentum < 0 AND current squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum OR Squeeze momentum > 0 AND current squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum) AND entry price > the highest session VPOC below the current bar low AND the resulting stop distance is below the maximum allowed distance
Short: Fast EMA < slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars AND (Squeeze momentum > 0 AND current squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum OR Squeeze momentum < 0 AND current squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum) AND entry price < the lowest session VPOC above the current bar high AND the resulting stop distance is below the maximum allowed distance
Exit: Long, Squeeze momentum > 0 AND current squeeze momentum < previous squeeze momentum OR Fast EMA < slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars OR close is below the entry price by the minimum loss percentage after the order has aged the required number of bars OR low reaches the stop price OR price reaches the trailing-profit trigger and then retraces by the trailing offset
Exit: Short, Squeeze momentum < 0 AND current squeeze momentum > previous squeeze momentum OR Fast EMA > slow EMA on each of the last two completed higher-timeframe bars OR close is above the entry price by the minimum loss percentage after the order has aged the required number of bars OR high reaches the stop price OR price reaches the trailing-profit trigger and then retraces by the trailing offset
