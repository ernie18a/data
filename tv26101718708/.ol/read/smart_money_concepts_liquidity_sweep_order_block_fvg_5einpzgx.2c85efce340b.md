Calculation: Liquidity pool = confirmed swing highs or lows clustered within an ATR-based tolerance; a pool remains eligible until swept or removed.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = price trades below an eligible low pool and closes back above it; bearish sweep = price trades above an eligible high pool and closes back below it.
Calculation: Displacement = candle body size divided by ATR; bullish displacement is an up-close meeting the threshold, bearish displacement a down-close meeting it. A displacement is sweep-born when it follows a sweep in its direction within the lookback window.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the ATR-based minimum, with bullish displacement; bearish FVG = current high is below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum, with bearish displacement.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the prior candle is bearish and current bar has bullish displacement; bearish order block = the prior candle is bullish and current bar has bearish displacement. Use the prior candle's high and low as the zone boundaries. If an FVG is contained in its matching order block, use the FVG zone only; otherwise each qualifies as its own zone.
Calculation: Zone score = weighted normalized sum of displacement strength, relative volume, sweep origin, FVG size, higher-timeframe directional alignment, premium/discount alignment, volume-profile location, and directional order-flow delta. Normalize the sum by total component weights. Relative volume compares current volume with its recent average; premium/discount compares close with the midpoint of the recent high-low range; profile location rewards overlap with the POC, value area, or an untested prior POC. Higher-timeframe bias compares the last completed higher-timeframe close with its EMA. Order-flow delta signs lower-timeframe intrabar volume by each intrabar's close versus open and sums it; use close-location-signed chart-bar volume when intrabar data is unavailable.
Calculation: Fresh zone = an unspent bullish or bearish zone created on an earlier bar. It is spent when price closes through its far boundary, or when its wick crosses that boundary if wick mitigation is selected.
Calculation: Armed zone entry = first touch of a fresh zone, with zone score meeting the entry threshold; entry is the zone's upper boundary for bullish zones and lower boundary for bearish zones. Stop is beyond the opposite boundary by an ATR-based buffer. Target is the nearest eligible opposing liquidity pool, selected naked POC, or fixed risk multiple according to target mode; cap the reward-to-risk ratio and require it to meet the minimum.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = current low is at the rolling lookback low and cumulative delta is above its rolling lookback low; bearish CVD divergence = current high is at the rolling lookback high and cumulative delta is below its rolling lookback high.
Long: sell-side liquidity sweep OR bullish CVD divergence OR armed bullish zone entry.
Short: buy-side liquidity sweep OR bearish CVD divergence OR armed bearish zone entry.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= target.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= target.
