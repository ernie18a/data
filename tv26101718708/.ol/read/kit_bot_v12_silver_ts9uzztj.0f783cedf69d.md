Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = price forms a lower pivot low while RSI at that pivot forms a higher low and is below its lower threshold.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = price forms a higher pivot high while RSI at that pivot forms a lower high and is above its upper threshold.
Long: RSI is rising, below its midpoint threshold, and above its lower threshold AND close > short-term EMA AND the lowest low over a short lookback <= lower Bollinger Band AND at least the configured minimum number of these are true: close > lower Bollinger Band AND previous close <= previous lower Bollinger Band; hammer OR bullish engulfing OR (doji AND close > open AND lower wick > upper wick); bullish RSI divergence; volume > its longer-term average × configured volume multiple; close > longer-term EMA AND within an enabled trading session AND daily trade count < configured maximum AND position is flat.
Short: RSI is falling, above its upper threshold, and below its higher threshold AND close < short-term EMA AND the highest high over a short lookback >= upper Bollinger Band AND at least the configured minimum number of these are true: close < upper Bollinger Band AND previous close >= previous upper Bollinger Band; gravestone OR bearish engulfing OR (doji AND close < open AND upper wick > lower wick); bearish RSI divergence; volume > its longer-term average × configured volume multiple; close < longer-term EMA AND within an enabled trading session AND daily trade count < configured maximum AND position is flat.
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close − signal-bar ATR × configured stop multiple OR high >= signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR × configured stop multiple × configured reward multiple.
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + signal-bar ATR × configured stop multiple OR low <= signal-bar close − signal-bar ATR × configured stop multiple × configured reward multiple.
