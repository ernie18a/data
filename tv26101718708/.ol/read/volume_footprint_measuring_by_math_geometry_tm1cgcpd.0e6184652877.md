Calculation: Buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low); if high = low, buy volume = volume / 2. Sell volume = volume − buy volume.
Calculation: Buy/sell volume at a price row = that side’s bar volume distributed over the bar’s low-to-high range by a truncated Gaussian; buy center = (low + close) / 2, sell center = (close + high) / 2, and standard deviation = (high − low) / concentration parameter. Normalize each side’s distribution over the bar range so its row volumes sum to that side’s bar volume.
Calculation: Window row volume = sum of buy and sell row volumes across qualifying bars in the footprint window. Profile buy/sell intensity = sum of each side’s Gaussian volume distributions across bars in the profile period, sampled across the period’s low-to-high range.
Calculation: Point of control (POC) = price row or profile sample with the greatest combined buy and sell volume or intensity, respectively.
Long: buy volume at a price exceeds sell volume at the immediately lower price × imbalance threshold, on a candle row OR on a footprint-window row OR at a profile sample. For window and profile signals, the marked level is the first qualifying imbalance above or below that domain’s POC.
Short: sell volume at a price exceeds buy volume at the immediately higher price × imbalance threshold, on a candle row OR on a footprint-window row OR at a profile sample. For window and profile signals, the marked level is the first qualifying imbalance above or below that domain’s POC.
