Calculation: VWMA = volume-weighted moving average of close
Long: (close > VWAP AND RSI > 50 AND RSI <= 80 AND close > Supertrend long stop AND close > VWMA AND close > Parabolic SAR AND volume > volume threshold AND previous bar volume > volume threshold AND close > open AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > EMA) AND flat) OR (Supertrend long stop crosses above VWAP AND VWMA crosses above VWAP AND RSI > 50 AND RSI <= 80 AND volume > volume threshold AND previous bar volume > volume threshold AND close > open AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND position is flat or short)
Short: (close < VWAP AND RSI < 40 AND RSI >= 20 AND close < Supertrend short stop AND close < VWMA AND close < Parabolic SAR AND volume > volume threshold AND previous bar volume > volume threshold AND close < open AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < EMA) AND flat) OR (Supertrend short stop crosses below VWAP AND VWMA crosses below VWAP AND RSI < 40 AND RSI >= 20 AND volume > volume threshold AND previous bar volume > volume threshold AND close < open AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND position is flat or long)
Exit: long, low <= stop (the low of the bar before entry) OR high >= entry price + (entry price - stop) * reward multiple
Exit: short, high >= stop (the high of the bar before entry) OR low <= entry price - (stop - entry price) * reward multiple
