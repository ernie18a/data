Calculation: Aggregated intrabar delta = sum across exchanges of each lower-timeframe bar’s volume × (2 × buy ratio − 1), where buy ratio is 0.5 if high = low; otherwise, if close ≥ open, 0.5 + 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low), else 0.5 × (close − low) / (high − low). Use lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar, including the bar still forming. Always include Binance; include Bybit, OKX, and Bitget when enabled.
Calculation: Daily CVD = cumulative aggregated intrabar delta, reset at the start of each UTC day when daily reset is enabled.
Calculation: Bull session percentile = percentage of stored positive prior-session bull peaks less than or equal to the current session’s highest Daily CVD; bear session percentile = percentage of stored negative prior-session bear peaks greater than or equal to the current session’s lowest Daily CVD. Store up to the percentile lookback number of prior qualifying session peaks per direction; calculate each percentile only when at least five peaks are stored.
Calculation: Price SMA = simple moving average of close.
Long: (lowest close over the swing lookback < lowest close over the preceding, nonoverlapping swing-lookback window AND lowest Daily CVD over the swing lookback > lowest Daily CVD over the preceding, nonoverlapping swing-lookback window AND bull session percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close > price SMA) AND bullish divergence condition was false on the prior chart bar) OR (close < prior close AND Daily CVD > prior Daily CVD AND volume > volume SMA over the absorption lookback × absorption volume multiplier AND bull session percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND bullish absorption condition was false on the prior chart bar)
Short: (highest close over the swing lookback > highest close over the preceding, nonoverlapping swing-lookback window AND highest Daily CVD over the swing lookback < highest Daily CVD over the preceding, nonoverlapping swing-lookback window AND bear session percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND (trend filter disabled OR close < price SMA) AND bearish divergence condition was false on the prior chart bar) OR (close > prior close AND Daily CVD < prior Daily CVD AND volume > volume SMA over the absorption lookback × absorption volume multiplier AND bear session percentile ≥ divergence threshold AND bearish absorption condition was false on the prior chart bar)
