Calculation: Smoothed RSI = EMA of RSI
Long: fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND close > longer-term EMA AND smoothed RSI > midpoint AND MACD line > MACD signal line AND volume > volume moving average × multiplier AND within the configured trading session
Short: fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND close < longer-term EMA AND smoothed RSI < midpoint AND MACD line < MACD signal line AND volume > volume moving average × multiplier AND within the configured trading session
Exit: Long, short condition OR high reaches entry price + profit target OR configured end-of-day flatten time
Exit: Short, long condition OR low reaches entry price − profit target OR configured end-of-day flatten time
