Calculation: Adaptive Fourier forecast = linearly detrend log close over candidate lookbacks, rank DFT cycles by power, frequency, stability, phase consistency, and directional agreement; select the best cycle, reconstruct it from the strongest harmonics, and add a damped trend projection.
Calculation: Forecast direction = projected log-price change over roughly one-sixth of the dominant cycle, normalized by ATR and the square root of the horizon; classify as up, down, or neutral using a dead-zone threshold.
Calculation: Cycle phase = selected cycle angle; bullish when near a trough or rising, bearish when near a peak or falling.
Calculation: Confidence = weighted score of cycle stability, forecast direction, relative amplitude, phase, forecast quality, ZLEMA confirmation, volume, and support/resistance room, adjusted for stability and higher-timeframe agreement.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = sum of selected Fourier-bin projections compared with an ATR-scaled threshold; use only the last completed bar of each enabled timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Projected buy level = forecast price at the next cycle trough within the effective forecast horizon, averaged with nearby support when close to it; valid only below the current close.
Calculation: Projected sell level = forecast price at the next cycle peak within the effective forecast horizon, averaged with nearby resistance when close to it; valid only above the current close.
Long: forecast direction is up AND projected change is positive AND cycle phase is bullish AND (weak cycles are allowed OR cycle stability >= minimum stability) AND (ZLEMA confirmation is disabled OR close > ZLEMA) AND volume confirmation passes AND close is not overextended above ZLEMA AND cycle value is not near its peak AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND long confidence >= entry threshold AND current signal was not already long on the previous bar AND bars since the previous signal >= cooldown AND current position is not long.
Short: forecast direction is down AND projected change is negative AND cycle phase is bearish AND (weak cycles are allowed OR cycle stability >= minimum stability) AND (ZLEMA confirmation is disabled OR close < ZLEMA) AND volume confirmation passes AND close is not overextended below ZLEMA AND cycle value is not near its trough AND higher-timeframe filter passes AND short confidence >= entry threshold AND current signal was not already short on the previous bar AND bars since the previous signal >= cooldown AND current position is not short.
Long: next cycle trough is within the effective forecast horizon AND projected buy level < close.
Short: next cycle peak is within the effective forecast horizon AND projected sell level > close.
Exit: long, low <= the stop-loss level OR high >= the third target OR bars since entry > trade-expiry limit.
Exit: short, high >= the stop-loss level OR low <= the third target OR bars since entry > trade-expiry limit.
