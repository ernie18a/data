Calculation: Oscillator = selected oscillator calculated from chart data (e.g., RSI, smoothed Stochastic %K, MACD histogram, CCI, MFI, rate of change, Williams %R, OBV, or an external oscillator)
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low/high = a low/high with the configured number of lower lows/higher highs on each side; usable only after the configured right-side bars have closed
Calculation: Oscillator range = highest oscillator value minus lowest oscillator value over the normalization lookback
Calculation: Oscillator percentile rank = current oscillator's percentile rank over the normalization lookback
Calculation: ATR = average true range over its configured lookback
Calculation: Divergence quality score = 0–100 weighted score of oscillator divergence size, zone depth for regular divergence, intervening price swing, and pivot price separation; hidden divergence omits zone depth
Long: On the close confirming a pivot low, a prior confirmed pivot low is within the configured minimum and maximum bar span, current pivot price < prior pivot price AND current pivot oscillator > prior pivot oscillator (regular), OR current pivot price > prior pivot price AND current pivot oscillator < prior pivot oscillator (hidden); absolute oscillator difference >= configured minimum percentage of oscillator range; pivot price separation >= configured ATR multiple; and the pair passes all applicable gates: for regular divergence, zone filter is off OR both pivots meet the selected oversold condition (adaptive: prior rank <= outer-percentile threshold and current rank <= its outer-percentile threshold; fixed: prior and current oscillator are below their respective thresholds); every intervening low and oscillator value stays on or above the straight line between pivots when line-of-sight filtering is enabled; intervening highest high minus the greater pivot low >= configured ATR multiple; quality score >= configured minimum. Emit at most one regular and one hidden signal, selecting the qualifying prior pivot with highest score or the most recent qualifying prior pivot according to the configured selection mode.
Short: On the close confirming a pivot high, a prior confirmed pivot high is within the configured minimum and maximum bar span, current pivot price > prior pivot price AND current pivot oscillator < prior pivot oscillator (regular), OR current pivot price < prior pivot price AND current pivot oscillator > prior pivot oscillator (hidden); absolute oscillator difference >= configured minimum percentage of oscillator range; pivot price separation >= configured ATR multiple; and the pair passes all applicable gates: for regular divergence, zone filter is off OR both pivots meet the selected overbought condition (adaptive: prior rank >= 100 minus the outer-percentile threshold and current rank >= 100 minus its outer-percentile threshold; fixed: prior and current oscillator are above their respective thresholds); every intervening high and oscillator value stays on or below the straight line between pivots when line-of-sight filtering is enabled; the lesser pivot high minus intervening lowest low >= configured ATR multiple; quality score >= configured minimum. Emit at most one regular and one hidden signal, selecting the qualifying prior pivot with highest score or the most recent qualifying prior pivot according to the configured selection mode.
