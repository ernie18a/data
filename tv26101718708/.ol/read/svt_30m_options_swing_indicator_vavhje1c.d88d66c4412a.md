Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA of normalized deviation of typical price from its EMA, with a shorter smoothing EMA; compare it with its 4-bar SMA.
Calculation: Bullish score = count of these seven conditions: RSI(14) > 50; RSI(9) > RSI(14); WaveTrend > its SMA; CCI > 0; ADX >= minimum and +DI > -DI; MACD line > signal line and MACD histogram > its prior value; close > EMA(20) and EMA(50). Bearish score = count of the corresponding inverse conditions. A score qualifies when it meets the configured threshold.
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, position is flat or short, entry and exit cooldowns have elapsed, bullish setup or bullish continuation is true, and the 2H trend is not strongly bearish. Bullish setup: (Daily close > Daily EMA(50) AND Daily RSI(14) > 50 OR 2H close > 2H EMA(20) AND 2H RSI(14) > 50 AND 2H MACD line > signal line OR 2H bullish recovery [2H MACD histogram > its prior value AND 2H RSI(14) > 45 AND 2H close > 2H EMA(20)]); a pullback occurred within the configured lookback [low <= EMA(20) OR low <= EMA(50) OR RSI(14) < 45]; a bullish MACD cross occurred within the configured active-bar window; MACD line > signal line AND MACD histogram > its prior value; bullish score qualifies; close > EMA(20) AND close > prior close AND close > open; close > EMA(20) AND EMA(20) >= EMA(50); close <= EMA(20) + ATR × configured extension limit; and chop filter permits trading. Bullish continuation: Daily close > Daily EMA(50) AND Daily EMA(50) > Daily EMA(200) AND Daily RSI(14) > 52; 2H close > 2H EMA(50) AND 2H EMA(20) > 2H EMA(50) AND 2H RSI(14) > 50 AND 2H MACD line > signal line; chart close > EMA(20) AND EMA(20) >= EMA(50); bullish MACD cross occurred within the configured active-bar window; bullish score qualifies; close > EMA(20); close <= EMA(20) + ATR × configured extension limit; and chop filter permits trading. Chop filter permits trading when chop filtering is disabled, or the chop zone is false, or a bullish breakout from chop occurs (close > prior bar's highest high over the chop lookback AND bullish MACD momentum AND RSI(14) > 50). Chop zone: ADX < configured chop threshold AND abs(EMA(20) − EMA(50)) < ATR × configured compression limit AND highest high minus lowest low over the chop lookback < ATR × configured range limit AND close is strictly inside that range. In Safe mode, bullish setup requires Daily bullish AND (2H bullish OR bullish recovery); otherwise it requires Daily bullish OR 2H bullish OR bullish recovery.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, position is flat or long, entry and exit cooldowns have elapsed, bearish setup or bearish continuation is true, and the 2H trend is not strongly bullish. Bearish setup: (Daily close < Daily EMA(50) AND Daily RSI(14) < 50 OR 2H close < 2H EMA(20) AND 2H RSI(14) < 50 AND 2H MACD line < signal line OR 2H bearish recovery [2H MACD histogram < its prior value AND 2H RSI(14) < 55 AND 2H close < 2H EMA(20)]); a pullback occurred within the configured lookback [high >= EMA(20) OR high >= EMA(50) OR RSI(14) > 55]; a bearish MACD cross occurred within the configured active-bar window; MACD line < signal line AND MACD histogram < its prior value; bearish score qualifies; close < EMA(20) AND close < prior close AND close < open; close < EMA(20) AND EMA(20) <= EMA(50); close >= EMA(20) − ATR × configured extension limit; and chop filter permits trading. Bearish continuation: Daily close < Daily EMA(50) AND Daily EMA(50) < Daily EMA(200) AND Daily RSI(14) < 48; 2H close < 2H EMA(50) AND 2H EMA(20) < 2H EMA(50) AND 2H RSI(14) < 50 AND 2H MACD line < signal line; chart close < EMA(20) AND EMA(20) <= EMA(50); bearish MACD cross occurred within the configured active-bar window; bearish score qualifies; close < EMA(20); close >= EMA(20) − ATR × configured extension limit; and chop filter permits trading. Chop filter permits trading when chop filtering is disabled, or the chop zone is false, or a bearish breakout from chop occurs (close < prior bar's lowest low over the chop lookback AND bearish MACD momentum AND RSI(14) < 50). Chop zone: ADX < configured chop threshold AND abs(EMA(20) − EMA(50)) < ATR × configured compression limit AND highest high minus lowest low over the chop lookback < ATR × configured range limit AND close is strictly inside that range. In Safe mode, bearish setup requires Daily bearish AND (2H bearish OR bearish recovery); otherwise it requires Daily bearish OR 2H bearish OR bearish recovery.
Exit: Long, low <= active stop OR bars held >= configured maximum hold OR (bars held >= configured minimum hold AND [(MACD crosses below signal AND RSI(14) < its SMA AND close < EMA(20) AND bearish score >= 4) OR (2H strongly bearish: close < EMA(50) AND EMA(20) < EMA(50) AND RSI(14) < 50 AND MACD line < signal line) OR (close < EMA(50) AND MACD line < signal line AND RSI(14) < 45) OR (theta protection enabled AND bars held >= configured theta-check bars AND highest high since entry < entry price + entry ATR × configured minimum-move limit AND 2H is not strongly bullish)]. Active stop is entry price − entry ATR × configured emergency-stop multiple until highest high since entry >= entry price + entry ATR × configured trail-start multiple; thereafter it is max(entry price + entry ATR × configured breakeven offset, highest high since entry − entry ATR × configured trail multiple).
Exit: Short, high >= active stop OR bars held >= configured maximum hold OR (bars held >= configured minimum hold AND [(MACD crosses above signal AND RSI(14) > its SMA AND close > EMA(20) AND bullish score >= 4) OR (2H strongly bullish: close > EMA(50) AND EMA(20) > EMA(50) AND RSI(14) > 50 AND MACD line > signal line) OR (close > EMA(50) AND MACD line > signal line AND RSI(14) > 55) OR (theta protection enabled AND bars held >= configured theta-check bars AND lowest low since entry > entry price − entry ATR × configured minimum-move limit AND 2H is not strongly bearish)]. Active stop is entry price + entry ATR × configured emergency-stop multiple until lowest low since entry <= entry price − entry ATR × configured trail-start multiple; thereafter it is min(entry price − entry ATR × configured breakeven offset, lowest low since entry + entry ATR × configured trail multiple).
Calculation: Daily and 2H values use higher-timeframe bars, taking the last completed bar in the backtest. All other calculations use chart-timeframe bars.
