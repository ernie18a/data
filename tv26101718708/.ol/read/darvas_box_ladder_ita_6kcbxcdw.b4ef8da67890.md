Calculation: Active Darvas box = Start at a bar whose high is the highest high over a lookback window. Confirm its high as the ceiling after it remains unbeaten for a confirmation count; any higher high resets the ceiling and count. The floor is the lowest low since the ceiling was set; confirm it after it remains unbroken for the same confirmation count, resetting the floor and count whenever a lower low occurs. Discard boxes whose height as a percentage of the floor is outside minimum and maximum limits. If a live box closes above its ceiling plus a breakout buffer, start building the next box from that bar’s high. A confirmed box whose floor is at or below the previous rung’s floor starts a new ladder; otherwise it continues the ladder.
Long: Active Darvas box AND close > ceiling × (1 + breakout buffer)
Exit: Long, active Darvas box AND close < floor
