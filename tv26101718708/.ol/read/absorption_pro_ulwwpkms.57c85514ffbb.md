Calculation: Base score = sum of the Bollinger, RSI, delta-streak, daily/weekly value-area, absorption, stochastic, CCI, MACD, ADX, key-level, RSI-divergence, and correlated-market-divergence scores below.
Calculation: Bollinger score = add a positive step for each configured upper standard-deviation band the close exceeds; add a negative step for each lower band it falls below. Bands use an EMA and standard deviation over the same lookback.
Calculation: RSI score = signed number of configured RSI steps from the midpoint.
Calculation: Delta-streak score = positive and increasing for consecutive bars with positive close-location delta and a down close; negative and decreasing for consecutive bars with negative close-location delta and an up close; otherwise zero. Close-location delta = volume × (2 × (close − low)/(high − low) − 1).
Calculation: Value-area score = add signed points when the close is within a configured tolerance of the prior completed daily or weekly value-area boundary, with resistance boundaries positive and support boundaries negative. Derive each value area from the period’s volume-at-price profile around its point of control, expanding toward the side with more volume until the configured fraction of period volume is included.
Calculation: Absorption score = positive when volume meets its minimum, volume × (close − open)/(high − low) exceeds its minimum, close > open, absolute open-to-close percentage move is below its maximum, close is in the upper portion of the bar, close exceeds its EMA, and the bar intersects the upper VWAP-deviation zone; negative when the mirrored delta, candle direction, lower close position, EMA, and lower VWAP-deviation-zone conditions hold; otherwise zero. Scale the signed score by the configured delta intensity, capped at its configured maximum. VWAP and deviation use volume-weighted typical price and its running standard deviation since the configured VWAP period began.
Calculation: Stochastic score = signed score for configured extreme %K values; adjust it on %K/%D crosses in the configured extreme zones. %K uses the close relative to the rolling low and high; %K smoothing and %D smoothing use configured lookbacks.
Calculation: CCI score = signed, stepwise score when CCI exceeds either configured extreme; CCI uses typical price, its moving average, and mean absolute deviation.
Calculation: MACD score = signed score when the MACD histogram exceeds a configured number of its rolling standard deviations; increase bearish score when price rises while histogram falls and score is positive, or bullish score when price falls while histogram rises and score is negative. Cap the score at configured bounds.
Calculation: ADX score = directional-indicator spread scaled by ADX strength above its configured threshold, capped at configured bounds.
Calculation: Key-level score = add positive points when a bar approaches from below and closes near prior daily/weekly resistance, or a neutral pivot/close/round number; add negative points when it approaches support from above, or a neutral level from above. Include prior daily/weekly pivot levels and the configured Fibonacci retracement zone: negative for an up swing closing in its retracement zone, positive for a down swing closing in its retracement zone.
Calculation: RSI-divergence score = positive when a confirmed higher price swing high has a lower RSI than the prior swing high; negative when a confirmed lower price swing low has a higher RSI than the prior swing low. Confirm swings using the configured percentage reversal.
Calculation: Correlated-market-divergence score = positive when a confirmed higher price swing high is accompanied by a lower correlated-market swing high; negative when a confirmed lower price swing low is accompanied by a higher correlated-market swing low. Use the same reversal setting.
Calculation: Total score = base score on the configured calculation timeframe, plus optional base scores from the configured higher and extra timeframes multiplied by their respective scales. Apply a trend multiplier: amplify scores aligned with the trend EMA slope and reduce scores against it, with strength based on absolute EMA slope divided by ATR. Multiply by a configured SMMA distance multiplier when score direction agrees with price’s side of the SMMA; then multiply by an ADX adjustment that reduces scores at high ADX and increases them at low ADX. Round to the configured score increment. For any requested timeframe above the chart timeframe, use its still-forming bar; for a timeframe below the chart, use the requested lower-timeframe value; for the chart timeframe, use the current bar.
Long: total score < the configured long threshold selected by whether close is above or at/below the absorption EMA.
Short: total score > the configured short threshold selected by whether close is above or at/below the absorption EMA.
Exit: Long, total score > the configured short threshold selected by whether close is above or at/below the absorption EMA OR low <= entry price − configured stop distance OR high >= entry price + configured target distance.
Exit: Short, total score < the configured long threshold selected by whether close is above or at/below the absorption EMA OR high >= entry price + configured stop distance OR low <= entry price − configured target distance.
