Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the unique maximum/minimum over equal lookbacks on both sides; use only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Validated pattern = one of: Head and Shoulders (high-low-high-low-high, center high above both shoulders, shoulder and trough pairs within ATR-scaled tolerances); inverse Head and Shoulders (the inverse); Double Top/Bottom (high-low-high or low-high-low, paired extrema within ATR-scaled tolerance and separated by a minimum interval); Triangle, Wedge, or Rectangle (four alternating pivots whose two boundary slopes satisfy the corresponding converging, directional, or flat geometry); Flag/Pennant (a strong preceding move followed by a short, narrow consolidation). Require the pattern's weighted shape, symmetry, volume, time, and trend-context score to meet its minimum threshold.
Calculation: Pattern neckline and measured-move target = for Head and Shoulders, the mean of the two troughs and that level minus the head height; inverse Head and Shoulders uses the two peaks and adds the head depth. For Double Top/Bottom, use the intervening pivot and subtract/add the extremum-to-neckline height. For Triangle/Wedge/Rectangle, use the direction-selected projected boundary and add/subtract the projected range. For Flag/Pennant, use the consolidation extreme in the pole direction and add/subtract the pole length.
Long: A validated bullish pattern is detected OR close > its neckline + ATR(14) × breakout-confirmation factor OR a validated bearish pattern fails (close > its stop).
Short: A validated bearish pattern is detected OR close < its neckline − ATR(14) × breakout-confirmation factor OR a validated bullish pattern fails (close < its stop).
Exit: Long, close < the active bullish pattern stop OR close >= its measured-move target.
Exit: Short, close > the active bearish pattern stop OR close <= its measured-move target.
