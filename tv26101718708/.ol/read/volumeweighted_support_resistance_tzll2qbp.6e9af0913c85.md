Calculation: Qualified swing high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the configured number of bars on both sides, and whose volume exceeds its volume SMA times a multiplier; confirm it after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Qualified swing low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the configured number of bars on both sides, and whose volume exceeds its volume SMA times a multiplier; confirm it after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Resistance zone = from a qualified swing high down by a multiple of ATR(14), using ATR when the swing is confirmed; keep only the most recent configured number of unbroken resistance zones.
Calculation: Support zone = from a qualified swing low up by a multiple of ATR(14), using ATR when the swing is confirmed; keep only the most recent configured number of unbroken support zones.
Long: confirmed close > the top of any active resistance zone
Short: confirmed close < the bottom of any active support zone
