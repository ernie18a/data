Calculation: Long H2 trigger = Reset the long count and waiting state when chart-timeframe close < chart-timeframe EMA or high equals the highest high over the long-count lookback. Otherwise, when close > EMA and the bar is not inside the prior bar, high > prior high starts H1 if the count is zero, or triggers H2 if the count is one and waiting for H2; high < prior high while the count is one sets waiting for H2. An inside bar has high <= prior high and low >= prior low.
Calculation: Short L2 trigger = Reset the short count and waiting state when chart-timeframe close > chart-timeframe EMA or low equals the lowest low over the short-count lookback. Otherwise, when close < EMA and the bar is not inside the prior bar, low < prior low starts L1 if the count is zero, or triggers L2 if the count is one and waiting for L2; low > prior low while the count is one sets waiting for L2.
Long: Long H2 trigger AND high - low > 0 AND (close - low) / (high - low) >= two-thirds AND low <= chart-timeframe EMA plus the parameterized proximity tolerance AND last completed higher-timeframe close > its EMA
Short: Short L2 trigger AND high - low > 0 AND (high - close) / (high - low) >= two-thirds AND high >= chart-timeframe EMA minus the parameterized proximity tolerance AND last completed higher-timeframe close < its EMA
