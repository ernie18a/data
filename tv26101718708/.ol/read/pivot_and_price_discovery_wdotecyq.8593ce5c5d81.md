Calculation: Source = (open + high + low + close) / 4
Calculation: Regression channel = least-squares linear regression of Source over a lookback; its current endpoint is the center, and its residual standard deviation is the population standard deviation of Source from the fitted line. Upper/lower boundaries = center ± residual standard deviation × deviation parameter.
Calculation: Micro trend rising/falling = Source rising/falling over a shorter lookback than the regression channel.
Calculation: Skewness = unbiased sample skewness of Source over the lookback, centered on its volume-weighted moving average and scaled by sample standard deviation. Excess kurtosis = the corresponding unbiased sample excess kurtosis.
Calculation: Critical skewness/kurtosis = the respective critical value based on lookback length and standard error; use the fixed reference value for lookbacks above the large-sample cutoff.
Long: Previous candle does not contain the current candle's range AND close > open AND ((skewness > 2 × critical skewness AND excess kurtosis > critical kurtosis) OR micro trend rising OR (close > previous close AND Source > previous Source AND close > (previous close + previous open) / 2 AND current hl2 > hl2 from two bars ago))
Long: (close > lower regression boundary AND previous hl2 < previous lower regression boundary AND previous candle does not contain current candle's range) OR (open > previous close AND low > previous low AND previous close < close from two bars ago AND previous candle does not contain current candle's range AND absolute skewness < critical skewness AND excess kurtosis > −critical kurtosis)
