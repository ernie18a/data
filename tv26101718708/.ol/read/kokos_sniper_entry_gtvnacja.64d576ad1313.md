Calculation: EMA channel = EMA of high (upper boundary) and EMA of low (lower boundary), using the same lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe rejection bias = on the last completed higher-timeframe bar, bullish when lower wick / range meets the configured rejection ratio and close is in the upper half of the range; bearish when upper wick / range meets the ratio and close is in the lower half
Long: close crosses above the upper EMA channel (or close > upper EMA channel in over/under mode) AND the configured ATR filter passes AND session is allowed AND higher-timeframe bullish rejection bias is enabled or present; OR a long clearing signal is pending AND the candle body or full candle is inside the EMA channel AND close > open or close > the previous high or close > the previous bar midpoint
Short: close crosses below the lower EMA channel (or close < lower EMA channel in over/under mode) AND the configured ATR filter passes AND session is allowed AND higher-timeframe bearish rejection bias is enabled or present; OR a short clearing signal is pending AND the candle body or full candle is inside the EMA channel AND close < open or close < the previous low or close < the previous bar midpoint
