Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the median volume at the same time of day over recent sessions when enough history exists; otherwise divide by the average of prior bars.
Calculation: Spread rank = percentile rank of the current bar's high-low range divided by the prior bar's ATR, compared with the preceding lookback window.
Calculation: Relative-volume rank = percentile rank of relative volume compared with the preceding lookback window.
Calculation: Normalized leg = (current close minus the close a lookback ago) divided by the ATR of the preceding bar.
Long: (Close < previous close AND Spread rank <= low threshold AND Relative-volume rank <= low threshold AND current volume < each of the two preceding volumes) OR (current low < the lowest low over the preceding lookback AND close is in the upper portion of the bar AND Relative-volume rank >= high threshold) OR (Relative-volume rank >= very high threshold AND Spread rank <= midpoint threshold AND close is in the upper half of the bar AND Normalized leg <= negative threshold) OR (Relative-volume rank >= extreme threshold AND Spread rank >= high threshold AND Close < previous close AND close is in the upper portion of the bar AND Normalized leg <= negative threshold)
Short: (Close > previous close AND Spread rank <= low threshold AND Relative-volume rank <= low threshold AND current volume < each of the two preceding volumes) OR (current high > the highest high over the preceding lookback AND close is in the lower portion of the bar AND Relative-volume rank >= high threshold) OR (Relative-volume rank >= very high threshold AND Spread rank <= midpoint threshold AND close is in the lower half of the bar AND Normalized leg >= positive threshold) OR (Relative-volume rank >= extreme threshold AND Spread rank >= high threshold AND Close > previous close AND close is in the lower portion of the bar AND Normalized leg >= positive threshold)
