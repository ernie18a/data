Calculation: VWAP = session cumulative volume-weighted average of each bar’s typical price, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Long: (current bar is in regular trading hours AND time is at or after the post-open entry time AND time is at or before the entry cutoff AND current open >= prior completed daily close (higher timeframe, last completed bar) * (1 + gap threshold) AND current close > VWAP AND current low <= VWAP) OR (current bar is in regular trading hours AND time is at or after the post-open entry time AND time is at or before the entry cutoff AND current close < VWAP * (1 - VWAP deviation))
Short: (current bar is in regular trading hours AND time is at or after the post-open entry time AND time is at or before the entry cutoff AND current open <= prior completed daily close (higher timeframe, last completed bar) * (1 - gap threshold) AND current close < VWAP AND current high >= VWAP) OR (current bar is in regular trading hours AND time is at or after the post-open entry time AND time is at or before the entry cutoff AND current close > VWAP * (1 + VWAP deviation))
Exit: long, current low <= entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage) OR current high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage) OR time is at or after the end-of-day flatten time
Exit: short, current high >= entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR current low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage) OR time is at or after the end-of-day flatten time
