Calculation: candle force = volume × (high − low)
Calculation: average force = simple moving average of candle force over the lookback
Calculation: relative force = candle force / average force
Calculation: body ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low)
Long: relative force ≥ vector threshold AND body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND close > open
Short: relative force ≥ vector threshold AND body ratio ≥ minimum body ratio AND close < open
