Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a local high/low over the pivot-strength number of bars on each side, available after the bars on the right have completed.
Calculation: Neural oscillator = the weighted sum of the 50-bar z-scores of RSI(14), MACD line(12,26,9), Stochastic(14), MFI(14), and CCI(20). With adaptive weighting, each weight is that oscillator’s positive 100-bar correlation with the 5-bar return, using the oscillator value from 5 bars earlier, divided by the sum of positive correlations; use equal weights if adaptive weighting is off or that sum is zero.
Calculation: Divergence confidence = 0.5 × min(1, 50 × abs(change in price pivot) / max(previous price pivot, 0.0001) + 0.5 × abs(change in neural-oscillator pivot)) + 0.5 × (number of the five normalized oscillators moving in the confirming direction between the corresponding price-pivot bars / 5). For bullish confirmation count oscillators that rose; for bearish confirmation count oscillators that fell.
Long: A new confirmed price pivot low occurs AND the two latest price pivot lows are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum pivot-gap bars AND ((the latest price pivot low is lower than the previous one AND the latest neural-oscillator pivot low is higher than the previous one AND regular divergence is enabled) OR (the latest price pivot low is higher than the previous one AND the latest neural-oscillator pivot low is lower than the previous one AND hidden divergence is enabled)) AND divergence confidence ≥ its threshold AND no bullish signal occurred within the cooldown period.
Short: A new confirmed price pivot high occurs AND the two latest price pivot highs are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum pivot-gap bars AND ((the latest price pivot high is higher than the previous one AND the latest neural-oscillator pivot high is lower than the previous one AND regular divergence is enabled) OR (the latest price pivot high is lower than the previous one AND the latest neural-oscillator pivot high is higher than the previous one AND hidden divergence is enabled)) AND divergence confidence ≥ its threshold AND no bearish signal occurred within the cooldown period.
