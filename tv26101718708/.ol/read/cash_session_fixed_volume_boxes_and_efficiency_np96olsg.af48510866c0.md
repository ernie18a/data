Calculation: Current-box volume = sum of volume from the first bar of the current box through the current bar; reset at cash-session open and after a box reaches the volume target
Calculation: Current-box open = open of the first bar of the current box; reset at cash-session open and after a box reaches the volume target
Long: current bar is within the cash session AND current-box volume >= volume target AND close > current-box open
Short: current bar is within the cash session AND current-box volume >= volume target AND close < current-box open
