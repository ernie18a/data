Calculation: 16-bar highest prior daily open = highest open among the last 16 completed bars of a higher timeframe
Calculation: 16-bar lowest prior daily open = lowest open among the last 16 completed bars of a higher timeframe
Long: close two chart bars ago > 16-bar highest prior daily open AND 16-bar highest prior daily open two chart bars ago > 16-bar highest prior daily open one chart bar ago AND close > 16-bar highest prior daily open
Short: close two chart bars ago < 16-bar lowest prior daily open AND 16-bar lowest prior daily open two chart bars ago > 16-bar lowest prior daily open one chart bar ago AND close < 16-bar lowest prior daily open
Exit: long, high >= take-profit price OR low <= stop-loss price OR low <= trailing-stop price
Exit: short, low <= take-profit price OR high >= stop-loss price OR high >= trailing-stop price
