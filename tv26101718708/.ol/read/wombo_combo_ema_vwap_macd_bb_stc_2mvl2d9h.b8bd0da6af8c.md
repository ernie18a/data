Calculation: STC = clamp to 0–100 the EMA-smoothed, twice-normalized stochastic of the MACD line; MACD is the difference between a shorter-lookback and longer-lookback EMA of close, and both stochastic normalizations use the same cycle lookback.
Long: Uptrend mode enabled AND (if STC filter enabled, STC crosses above its lower threshold) AND (if EMA filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback is above the EMA) AND (if VWAP filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback is above VWAP) AND (if MACD filter enabled, MACD crosses above its signal line AND MACD is below zero) AND (if Bollinger Band filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback crosses below the lower band)
Short: Downtrend mode enabled AND (if STC filter enabled, STC crosses below its upper threshold) AND (if EMA filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback is below the EMA) AND (if VWAP filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback is below VWAP) AND (if MACD filter enabled, MACD crosses below its signal line AND the signal line is above zero) AND (if Bollinger Band filter enabled, selected price at the configured countback crosses above the upper band)
Exit: long, low is at or below average entry price times (1 − stop-loss fraction) OR high is at or above average entry price times (1 + profit-target fraction) OR (if trailing stop enabled, low is at or below the greater of the prior trailing stop and close times (1 − trailing-loss fraction))
Exit: short, high is at or above average entry price times (1 + stop-loss fraction) OR low is at or below average entry price times (1 − profit-target fraction) OR (if trailing stop enabled, high is at or above the lesser of the prior trailing stop and close times (1 + trailing-loss fraction))
