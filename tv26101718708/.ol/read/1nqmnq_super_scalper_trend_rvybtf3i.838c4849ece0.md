Calculation: Bullish / bearish rejection = price trades beyond the active range low / high and closes back above / below it; the candle has range > 0.3 ATR, body > 30% of its range, and is not a doji. Use the opening range after it is locked; otherwise use the prior completed regular-session range.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish OTE rejection = close is in the 50–79% retracement zone of the recent 20-bar high–low range, the bar reaches the zone midpoint and closes back above / below it, the range exceeds the minimum ATR multiple, and the bar is not a doji.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish structure break = a bullish / bearish candle closes beyond the prior completed lookback high / low, remains beyond it on the next bar, and has body at least 40% of its range. Opening-range breaks qualify when a close crosses the locked opening-range high / low for the first time that day.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish break retest = after a structure or opening-range break, price retests within the ATR tolerance and closes back beyond the broken level, with a confirming engulfing, hammer / shooting-star, sufficiently large directional candle, or (for opening-range breaks) qualifying wick rejection or directional volume increase; exclude dojis.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish order-block rejection = after a strong directional move, identify the most recent opposite-color candle within the lookback as the zone; on its first touch, close beyond its far edge with a directional commitment candle and confirming directional candle pattern; formation rule score meets its minimum. Invalidate the zone on a close through its far edge.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish trend retest = in an efficiency-ratio trend, price crosses the 21-period EMA intrabar and closes back on the trend side, without an opposing higher low / lower high structure.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price exceeds the prior lookback high / low but closes back below / above it, with a qualifying wick, wick-to-body ratio, relative volume, bar range, wick standard-deviation, and close-rejection fraction.
Calculation: Opening-drive signal = during one of the configured opening, economic-release, or second-wave time windows, the directional confidence threshold is met; adjust the threshold for a volume spike and strong directional candle.
Calculation: Squeeze continuation = a volatility squeeze released within the recent-bar window, with directional momentum and normalized momentum above its side-specific minimum.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish FVG rejection = a valid, unexpired three-bar fair-value gap is touched and rejected in its directional continuation direction; require directional close confirmation.
Calculation: Bullish / bearish inverse-FVG retest = a previously violated opposite-direction fair-value gap is retested at its midpoint and closed back through it by a directional candle.
Calculation: Setup confidence = the setup-specific composite of directional, mean-distance, range-band, key-level, volatility, candle, liquidity, trend/context, and regime factors; compare it with that setup's minimum threshold.
