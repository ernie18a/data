Calculation: Whittaker upper/lower bounds = asymmetric Whittaker smoothing of chart-timeframe highs/lows over the full configured window; the bounds are recalculated using the full dataset and repaint historically.
Calculation: Polynomial regression channel = least-squares polynomial fit of the configured chart-timeframe source over its lookback, with upper/lower bands offset by the configured channel distance.
Calculation: Absorption candle = volume percentile is high AND (real body is small relative to bar range OR the relevant rejection wick is large relative to bar range).
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = cumulative lower-timeframe volume, added for up-closing bars and subtracted for down-closing bars; use the latest available lower-timeframe values (the bar still forming in real time).
Calculation: Volume vacuum = the prior chart-timeframe bar has very high volume percentile AND the current bar has very low volume percentile.
Calculation: Causal volume-weighted anomaly z-score = (close − anomaly-anchor meta-mean) / standard deviation of close; the meta-mean averages volume-weighted prices anchored at prior high-volume anomalies.
Long: A lower Whittaker bound or lower polynomial channel was touched within the recent configured window OR a bullish liquidation-wick shock or bullish liquidity sweep occurred; AND the current close is at or below the midpoint of the Whittaker bounds; AND an absorption candle with a lower rejection wick or small body occurred; AND the current regime is not bearish. Also mark long when a bullish absorption base has been cleared and either cumulative volume delta is at least its prior value or a volume vacuum occurs. A bullish absorption base remains cleared until the causal volume-weighted anomaly z-score becomes positive.
Short: An upper Whittaker bound or upper polynomial channel was touched within the recent configured window OR a bearish liquidation-wick shock or bearish liquidity sweep occurred; AND the current close is at or above the midpoint of the Whittaker bounds; AND an absorption candle with an upper rejection wick or small body occurred; AND the current regime is not bullish. Also mark short when a bearish absorption base has been cleared and either cumulative volume delta is at most its prior value or a volume vacuum occurs. A bearish absorption base remains cleared until the causal volume-weighted anomaly z-score becomes negative.
Exit: long, price reaches the nearest active bearish absorption-star wick level above the entry price.
Exit: short, price reaches the nearest active bullish absorption-star wick level below the entry price.
