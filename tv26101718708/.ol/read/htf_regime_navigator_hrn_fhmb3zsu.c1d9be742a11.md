Calculation: Higher-timeframe series = aggregate chart data into the selected higher timeframe and use the bar still forming; confirmed pivots become available only after their right-hand confirmation bars. EMAs may instead use the chart timeframe. EMA slope is the percentage change from its value a selected number of bars earlier on that EMA's own timeframe.
Calculation: Range = the latest confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high and low with equal left and right wings. If the high is not above the low, optionally substitute the highest high and lowest low over a higher-timeframe lookback. Range position is (chart close - range low) / range height. An upside or downside breakout occurs when the forming higher-timeframe close exceeds the corresponding range edge by the breakout buffer.
Calculation: Spring = the latest chart-bar low below the range low penetrates no more than a selected fraction of range height, its volume passes the selected volume qualification, the current close is above the current range low within the reclaim window, the downside breakout is false, and more than the cooldown has elapsed since the previous spring. Upthrust mirrors this at the range high. Volume qualification accepts any volume, volume below its average, or volume above a multiple of its average; missing volume or average passes. The average is either a chart-volume SMA or, for intraday data, the prior exponentially updated average for the same session bar, falling back to the SMA when unavailable. Each new qualifying penetration replaces the previous one.
Calculation: Regular divergence = on the forming higher-timeframe series, the latest newly confirmed price pivot is lower than the preceding low pivot while its RSI is higher, or higher than the preceding high pivot while its RSI is lower. RSI is measured at the price pivots. Suppress consecutive repeated pivot values; retain each event for a selected chart-bar memory.
Calculation: Regime = upward trend when there is an upside breakout and, if confirmation is enabled, at least the selected number of active EMAs are below close and at least one fewer of them, with a minimum of one, have positive slope above the slope threshold; cap the required count at the active EMA count and require at least one active EMA. Downward trend mirrors this. Otherwise, with a valid range, accumulation or distribution is the side with more evidence: respectively, the earlier higher-timeframe close above or below the buffered range midpoint, chart-bar up-volume share above or below its symmetric thresholds, a recent spring or upthrust, and recent bullish or bearish divergence. Ties or an invalid range are neutral. Up-volume share is up-bar volume divided by up- plus down-bar volume over a chart-bar lookback, with unchanged bars excluded and a neutral share when the denominator is zero.
Calculation: Forecast = the weighted average of bounded components: EMA structure is the difference between counts of active EMAs below and above close divided by their count; regime assigns positive or negative scores of greater magnitude to trends than to accumulation or distribution; entry value is distance from the range midpoint, reversed for a bearish regime and doubled for a directional regime; divergence adds or subtracts a fixed score for each recent bullish or bearish event; Wyckoff adds or subtracts an event score for recent springs or upthrusts and adds the centered up-volume share times its weight. Include a fixed discretionary component as a parameter. Bound each component and the weighted result symmetrically. Base probability is the greater of its floor and its prior minus sensitivity times absolute forecast; split the remainder between bullish and bearish probabilities in proportion to the signed forecast, round bullish and base probabilities, and calculate bearish probability as the remainder.
Calculation: Fibonacci anchors = optionally the range, the latest confirmed swing high and low on the chart or a forming higher timeframe, or the highest high and lowest low over a chart-bar lookback. Missing swing anchors fall back individually to the range; swap inverted anchors. Retracement is measured from high toward low in upward orientation and from low toward high in downward orientation. Display orientation is optionally forced; otherwise it follows directional regime, then forecast thresholds with hysteresis, retaining its previous direction inside the thresholds. Signal orientation optionally follows display orientation; otherwise it follows directional regime, then the order of anchor bars, then swing-anchor order, then whether range position is below its midpoint.
Calculation: Fibonacci confluence = at least one, or optionally all, selected active EMAs lie within the golden retracement zone extended by a tolerance proportional to anchor range; optionally require close inside the unextended zone and any or all selected EMA changes over a chart-bar slope lookback to have the selected positive, negative, or nonzero sign. Its direction is selected from the majority sign of included EMA changes, the majority of active EMAs below versus above close, both of those bullish tests together, nonnegative regime direction, or display orientation with the selected slope direction taking precedence. Ties in the first two tests are bearish.
Calculation: EMA status = above when close exceeds the EMA by the test tolerance and the bar does not straddle it; below when close is below the EMA by the wider below tolerance; otherwise testing. Test tolerance is a fraction of close or an ATR multiple divided by close. Long structure requires at least one signal EMA and none below. Short structure optionally requires none above; otherwise all must be below, except the designated fast EMA may be testing if at least one other EMA is below. A reclaim is the transition into the corresponding structure.
Calculation: Ordered stack = selected signal EMAs ranked by EMA length multiplied by their timeframe duration; collapse equal effective lengths using the first configured EMA, then require the fastest selected depth to be strictly decreasing for longs or increasing for shorts. Fewer than two distinct members passes the order gate. A fresh stack license starts when the ordered stack forms after a configurable consecutive broken period, after a recent opposite stack, or after any break, and lasts for its memory while order persists.
Calculation: Fresh license = an enabled reclaim within its memory or while fewer than the permitted number of post-reclaim test episodes have been consumed and structure remains intact, an enabled fresh stack license, or an enabled recent spring for longs or upthrust for shorts. A test episode starts when the designated fast EMA test becomes true after being false. A long test is absolute close-to-EMA distance divided by close within tolerance, or low at or below EMA and close at or above it; a short test mirrors the latter. Consume a post-reclaim episode only when structure and all candidate gates pass, the side is not confirmed, holding, or invalidated, and cooldown permits action; do not consume the reclaim bar.
Calculation: Candidate gates = corresponding structure; selected all-or-majority slope alignment, optionally required to have begun within its freshness window; optional ordered stack; close or designated fast EMA inside the selected signal retracement zone, with price substituted if that EMA is disabled; optional close inside that zone; optional Fibonacci confluence with matching direction, except either-slope mode accepts either direction; optional veto against the opposite trending regime; optional requirement for matching directional regime; and optional minimum signal score. If license bypass is enabled, a fresh license can satisfy the slope gate and, in reclaim-and-test mode, the zone gate. The score sums EMA-structure points, slope-alignment and freshness points, signal-zone and deeper golden-pocket points, and volume-versus-average points, capped at its maximum.
Calculation: Signal trigger = optional volume above a multiple of its chart-volume average, with missing data passing, AND one selected trigger: an enabled reclaim bar or the first bar of a fast-EMA test episode with a fresh license; a fast-EMA test on any bar; any signal EMA testing; or a reclaim bar. In reclaim-and-test mode the test also requires the corresponding structure. Each side has its own state and cooldown: when idle, candidate gates and trigger start confirmation; the trigger bar counts as the first confirmation bar, subsequent bars require only the corresponding structure, and loss of structure cancels confirmation and starts cooldown. A fresh reclaim may clear cooldown. After the required consecutive closes, the side is confirmed; it remains active until invalidation or structure loss. Invalidation starts a configurable inactive interval and cooldown; structure loss starts cooldown.
Calculation: Invalidation level = optionally the lowest signal EMA for a long or highest for a short, the designated fast EMA with fallback to that extreme, or the selected stop reference. EMA references include a percentage buffer below for longs or above for shorts. Stop reference optionally uses that buffered EMA, the latest spring low or upthrust high, a selected signal-oriented Fibonacci retracement, or the lower of two protective retracements for longs and higher for shorts; Fibonacci and event extremes are unbuffered.

Short: Candidate gates change from false to true while the short signal state is idle AND no trigger starts confirmation on that bar
Long: Long signal confirmation count >= required confirmation closes
Short: Short signal confirmation count >= required confirmation closes

Exit: short, close > short invalidation level OR short structure = false
