Calculation: Confirmed price pivot = a pivot high/low in high/low, confirmed after the configured right-side bars; compare each new pivot P2 with prior same-type pivots P1 whose separation is within the configured minimum/maximum bar window.
Calculation: RSI, Stochastic RSI, MFI, and ROC = the standard indicators calculated from chart-timeframe close (MFI from hlc3); Stochastic RSI is the smoothed stochastic of RSI.
Calculation: Oscillator concordance = count of enabled oscillators meeting the applicable divergence comparison, divided by the number of enabled oscillators. For regular bullish/bearish divergences, RSI, Stochastic RSI, and MFI count only when P1 is below their oversold/above their overbought threshold, respectively; ROC has no zone condition.
Calculation: Clean pivot slope = true when intermediate lows (for a low-pivot pair) or highs (for a high-pivot pair) do not cross the linear interpolation between P1 and P2, as determined by a change in sign of their deviation from that line.
Calculation: Elliott-wave context = classify the latest confirmed swings independently at Small, Medium, and Large degrees. Swings are alternating confirmed highs/lows meeting each degree's minimum percentage move. Classify the latest six swings as impulse W5 or Diagonal when their respective impulse/diagonal structure tests pass; otherwise test, in order, W3, W2, W4, Corrective C, Triangle E, or neutral. Scale each degree's position score by its pattern quality; use the configured proximity window around the divergence pivot, scaled longer for Medium and Large. Combine degree scores with Small weighted most, then Medium, then Large; apply agreement/conflict adjustments. For hidden divergences use the hidden-context position scores; for regular divergences use regular-context scores. Also calculate trend agreement from the Medium trend, or Large if Medium is unavailable, with a same-direction Medium/Large adjustment when both agree.
Calculation: Hurst probability = clamp(0.5 + (0.5 − Hurst) × configured mean-reversion weight, 0.05, 0.95), where Hurst is the clamped log rescaled-range/log lookback ratio of cumulative close log returns. Entropy probability = normalized Shannon entropy of close log returns binned over the configured lookback, clamped to [0.05, 0.95].
Calculation: Divergence posterior = logistic(prior log-odds + sum of each configured weight times the logit of its component probability). Components are oscillator concordance; RSI divergence magnitude divided by RSI standard deviation, scaled by the configured z-score threshold and clamped to [0.05, 0.95]; Hurst probability; entropy probability; Elliott-wave context; overextension probability; and trend-agreement probability. Overextension is the ratio of the current pivot leg length to the previous similar pivot leg length, mapped as clamp((ratio − 1)/3, 0.05, 0.95).
Long: Confirmed low-pivot P2 is lower than P1 AND at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators show regular bullish divergence (oscillator P2 > P1; for RSI, Stochastic RSI, and MFI, P1 is below its oversold threshold) AND clean pivot slope passes when required AND the regular wave filter passes AND divergence posterior meets the configured minimum score AND the configured bullish cooldown has elapsed OR confirmed low-pivot P2 is higher than P1 AND at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators show hidden bullish divergence (oscillator P2 < P1) AND clean pivot slope passes when required AND the hidden wave filter passes AND divergence posterior meets the configured minimum score AND the configured bullish cooldown has elapsed. For either case, P1-to-P2 separation must satisfy the configured bar limits.
Short: Confirmed high-pivot P2 is higher than P1 AND at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators show regular bearish divergence (oscillator P2 < P1; for RSI, Stochastic RSI, and MFI, P1 is above its overbought threshold) AND clean pivot slope passes when required AND the regular wave filter passes AND divergence posterior meets the configured minimum score AND the configured bearish cooldown has elapsed OR confirmed high-pivot P2 is lower than P1 AND at least the configured minimum number of enabled oscillators show hidden bearish divergence (oscillator P2 > P1) AND clean pivot slope passes when required AND the hidden wave filter passes AND divergence posterior meets the configured minimum score AND the configured bearish cooldown has elapsed. For either case, P1-to-P2 separation must satisfy the configured bar limits.
