Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression, at the current bar, of close minus the average of (highest high and lowest low over the lookback) and the simple moving average of close over that same lookback.
Calculation: Volume histogram green = close > previous bar midpoint OR (current high-low range > its lookback average AND close > previous midpoint + previous range / divisor AND volume > its lookback average) OR (high > previous high AND current range < range average / 1.5 AND volume < volume average) OR (low < previous low AND current range < range average / 1.5 AND volume > volume average); red = (current range > its lookback average AND close < previous midpoint - previous range / divisor AND volume > its lookback average) OR close < previous bar midpoint. Previous midpoint = (previous high + previous low) / 2.
Long: close > PSAR AND previous close <= previous PSAR AND close > long-term EMA AND close > short-term SMA AND short-term SMA > long-term EMA AND squeeze momentum > 0 AND volume histogram green
Short: close < PSAR AND previous close >= previous PSAR AND close < long-term EMA AND close < short-term SMA AND short-term SMA < long-term EMA AND squeeze momentum < 0 AND volume histogram red
Exit: long, close < entry price * (1 - stop-loss percentage) OR high >= entry price * (1 + take-profit percentage)
Exit: short, close > entry price * (1 + stop-loss percentage) OR low <= entry price * (1 - take-profit percentage)
