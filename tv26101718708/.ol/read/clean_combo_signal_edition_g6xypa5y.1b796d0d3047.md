Calculation: Demand and supply zones = zones centered on confirmed swing lows and highs whose pivot-bar volume exceeds its volume moving average; use ATR-based zone widths. A zone is near when close is within the zone plus an ATR-based distance; invalidate demand below its bottom by an ATR-based margin and supply above its top by an ATR-based margin.
Calculation: POC = midpoint of the price bucket with the greatest volume, using a fixed number of buckets across the highest-high to lowest-low range of a lookback window; allocate each bar’s volume among buckets in proportion to price-range overlap. VAH and VAL = expand upward and downward from the POC bucket until the accumulated bucket volume reaches 70% of total volume.
Calculation: Hurst estimate = 1 − [100 × log10(sum of true range / (highest high − lowest low)) / log10(lookback)] / 100.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish confirmation = faster EMA > medium EMA and RSI > 50; bearish confirmation = faster EMA < medium EMA and RSI < 50. Use the higher-timeframe bar value returned with lookahead off.
