Calculation: Dynamic RSI oversold threshold = lowest RSI over the longer lookback + buffer; overbought threshold = highest RSI over that lookback − buffer
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish MACD inflection = within the confirmation lookback, MACD histogram turned upward from a local trough / downward from a local peak
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier, optionally also exceeds prior-bar volume; recent volume confirmation means it occurred within the confirmation lookback
Calculation: Bullish/bearish RSI divergence = price makes a lower low / higher high while RSI makes a higher low / lower high, with the two confirmed pivots no farther apart than the maximum pivot gap
Calculation: Auto entry mode = aggressive when Bollinger Band width and ATR exceed their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its higher threshold; conservative when band width is not expanding and volume is not above that threshold; balanced otherwise. A manually selected mode overrides this
Calculation: Trend mode gate = trend mode is active when enabled and ADX is at or above its threshold; strong trend means ADX is at or above its higher threshold
Calculation: Trend entry ADX baseline = ADX on the entry bar
Calculation: Initial invalidation level = lowest low / highest high over the invalidation lookback at entry, minus / plus a tick buffer for long / short
Calculation: Dynamic invalidation level = while holding, the greater / lesser of the prior level and the lowest low / highest high over the dynamic lookback, minus / plus a tick buffer for long / short
Calculation: Trailing stop = after price moves favorably by the start ATR multiple from entry, highest high since activation minus ATR multiple / lowest low since activation plus ATR multiple for long / short; update only in the favorable direction
Calculation: Break-even stop = entry price, activated after favorable movement by the configured ATR multiple
Calculation: Take-profit levels = entry price plus / minus ATR times each configured target multiple for long / short
Long: no position AND in session AND ((trend mode active AND ADX below the strong-trend threshold AND confirmed uptrend pullback) OR (trend mode active AND ADX below the strong-trend threshold AND (reversal long OR midline continuation long)) OR (trend mode inactive AND (reversal long OR midline continuation long)))
Long: no position AND lower band proximity AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold threshold + near-entry buffer
Long: no position AND continuation enabled AND continuation score >= warning threshold AND continuation score < 7
Long: no position AND trend warnings enabled AND trend mode enabled AND confirmed uptrend AND not confirmed trend long pullback AND RSI <= trend long RSI threshold
Short: no position AND in session AND ((trend mode active AND ADX below the strong-trend threshold AND confirmed downtrend pullback) OR (trend mode active AND ADX below the strong-trend threshold AND (reversal short OR midline continuation short)) OR (trend mode inactive AND (reversal short OR midline continuation short)))
Short: no position AND upper band proximity AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought threshold − near-entry buffer
Short: no position AND continuation enabled AND continuation score >= warning threshold AND continuation score < 7
Short: no position AND trend warnings enabled AND trend mode enabled AND confirmed downtrend AND not confirmed trend short pullback AND RSI >= trend short RSI threshold
Exit: long, low <= initial invalidation level OR low <= dynamic invalidation level OR enabled trailing stop is activated and low <= trailing stop OR enabled break-even stop is activated and low <= break-even stop OR enabled take-profit exit condition OR enabled maximum holding time has elapsed OR (trade type is reversal AND selected reversal exit condition) OR (trade type is continuation AND selected continuation exit condition) OR (trade type is trend long AND trend mode enabled AND (ADX < trend entry ADX baseline − drop threshold OR (−DI > +DI AND ADX > reversal threshold)))
Exit: short, high >= initial invalidation level OR high >= dynamic invalidation level OR enabled trailing stop is activated and high >= trailing stop OR enabled break-even stop is activated and high >= break-even stop OR enabled take-profit exit condition OR enabled maximum holding time has elapsed OR (trade type is reversal AND selected reversal exit condition) OR (trade type is continuation AND selected continuation exit condition) OR (trade type is trend short AND trend mode enabled AND (ADX < trend entry ADX baseline − drop threshold OR (+DI > −DI AND ADX > reversal threshold)))
