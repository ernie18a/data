Calculation: Bollinger %B = (close − lower Bollinger band) / (upper Bollinger band − lower Bollinger band).
Calculation: Regression channel position = (close − lower regression band) / (upper regression band − lower regression band); use shorter- and longer-lookback channels where both are used.
Calculation: Regression slope = change in the regression-line value per bar, divided by its current value.
Calculation: Lower-band condition = longer-lookback regression position ≤ its low threshold OR shorter-lookback regression position ≤ its low threshold OR 50-bar Bollinger %B ≤ its low threshold OR shorter-lookback Bollinger %B ≤ its low threshold.
Calculation: Upper-band condition = Bollinger %B ≥ its high threshold OR shorter-lookback regression position ≥ its high threshold.
Calculation: Stochastic K(3) = 3-bar simple average of 14-bar stochastic %K.
Calculation: Volatility panic ratio = highest volatility-index close over a short lookback / 20-bar simple average of volatility-index closes.
Calculation: MACD histogram improving = current MACD histogram > previous bar's histogram; MACD histogram = MACD(12,26,9) line − signal line.
Calculation: Crash-recovery lockout = highest volatility-index close over a longer lookback ≥ its lockout threshold.
Calculation: Long conviction = count of these true conditions: volatility panic ratio ≥ its threshold; both longer-lookback regression position and 50-bar Bollinger %B ≤ their low thresholds; MACD histogram improving; volatility-index close ≥ its threshold; longer-lookback regression slope > its threshold; price is at the lower band of the regression channel anchored at the most recently confirmed pivot low; close is within the configured tolerance of the 61.8% or 100% retracement of the rise from the lowest low in the configured lookback before the highest high in its configured lookback.
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = close > close from the configured lookback bars ago AND 14-bar RSI < its value that many bars ago.
Calculation: Bars above moving average = consecutive bars with close ≥ its 20-bar simple moving average, resetting to zero when close < that average.
Calculation: Drawdown from recent high = (close − highest close over the configured lookback) / that highest close.
Calculation: Volatility-index change = (current close − close from the configured lookback bars ago) / that earlier close.
Calculation: Underlying-index long-average slope = (current 200-bar simple moving average − its value 30 bars ago) / its value 30 bars ago.
Calculation: New 20-bar closing low = close ≤ the lowest close over the prior 20 bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Day change = (close − previous close) / previous close.
