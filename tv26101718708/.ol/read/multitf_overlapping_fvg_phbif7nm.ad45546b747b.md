Calculation: Bullish FVG = on a watched timeframe, current low > high two bars earlier AND previous close > high two bars earlier; its zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND previous close < low two bars earlier; its zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. For timeframe-based calculations, use the requested timeframe’s current bar (forming in realtime, last completed historically); it may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish cluster = an unmitigated bullish 1-minute FVG seed plus at least the configured minimum number of distinct-timeframe bullish FVGs in total, each unmitigated and overlapping the seed’s price zone; use at most one higher-timeframe FVG per timeframe. Bearish cluster = the equivalent using bearish FVGs. A member is mitigated when price fills the configured percentage of its zone, using either the wick or close-based price; clusters remain active while at least two members are unmitigated and for one additional bar after that count falls below two.
