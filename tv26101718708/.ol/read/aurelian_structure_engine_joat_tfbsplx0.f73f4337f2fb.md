Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Yearly VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of (high + low + close) / 3, reset at each calendar year
Calculation: Bullish value stack = close > 50-period EMA, close > Yearly VWAP, and 50-period EMA > Yearly VWAP
Calculation: Bearish value stack = close < 50-period EMA, close < Yearly VWAP, and 50-period EMA < Yearly VWAP
Calculation: Bullish structure break = on a completed chart bar, close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high plus an ATR buffer; at least two confirmed pivot highs must exist
Calculation: Bearish structure break = on a completed chart bar, close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low minus an ATR buffer; at least two confirmed pivot lows must exist
Calculation: Pressure = 7-period EMA of volume-weighted candle close-position imbalance, divided by 7-period EMA of volume; candle imbalance is ((close − low) / (high − low)) − ((high − close) / (high − low))
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-period simple moving average of volume
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = last completed higher-timeframe close > its 50-period EMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish bias = last completed higher-timeframe close < its 50-period EMA
Calculation: Bid-block touch = price range intersects the active bid block, which is created at a bullish structure break from the best-scoring qualifying prior down candle; offer-block touch = price range intersects the active offer block, created at a bearish structure break from the best-scoring qualifying prior up candle. Qualifying candles are within the configured prior-bar search, on the appropriate side of the break close by an ATR margin, and separated from the opposing active block by an ATR margin. Select the qualifying candle minimizing distance of its block edge to the value spine plus 0.35 × candle range plus 0.015 × bars-back × ATR minus 0.25 × body size. A bid block spans low to max(open, close); an offer block spans min(open, close) to high. Remove a block when close invalidates its far edge by 0.10 ATR; when blocks overlap, retain the bid block if structure bias is nonnegative, otherwise retain the offer block.
Calculation: Near bid = bid-block touch, or close within 0.45 ATR of the lower inner value band, or close within 0.45 ATR of Yearly VWAP
Calculation: Near offer = offer-block touch, or close within 0.45 ATR of the upper inner value band, or close within 0.45 ATR of Yearly VWAP
Calculation: Bull score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 18 for bullish value stack, 18 for near bid, 18 for bullish structure break, 8 for bullish break while prior structure bias is bearish, up to 18 from positive pressure scaled by the pressure threshold, up to 14 from relative volume scaled by its threshold, and 12 for bullish higher-timeframe bias
Calculation: Bear score = clamp to 0–100 the sum of 18 for bearish value stack, 18 for near offer, 18 for bearish structure break, 8 for bearish break while prior structure bias is bullish, up to 18 from absolute negative pressure scaled by the pressure threshold, up to 14 from relative volume scaled by its threshold, and 12 for bearish higher-timeframe bias
Long: bars since the prior long or short signal >= cooldown AND bullish structure break AND bullish value stack AND bullish higher-timeframe bias AND (pressure > pressure threshold OR relative volume >= relative-volume threshold)
Long: bars since the prior long or short signal >= cooldown AND bullish structure break AND bullish value stack AND bullish higher-timeframe bias AND pressure > pressure threshold AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND near bid
Short: bars since the prior long or short signal >= cooldown AND bearish structure break AND bearish value stack AND bearish higher-timeframe bias AND (pressure < −pressure threshold OR relative volume >= relative-volume threshold)
Short: bars since the prior long or short signal >= cooldown AND bearish structure break AND bearish value stack AND bearish higher-timeframe bias AND pressure < −pressure threshold AND relative volume >= relative-volume threshold AND near offer
