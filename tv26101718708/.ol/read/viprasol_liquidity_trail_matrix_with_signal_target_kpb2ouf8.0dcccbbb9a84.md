Calculation: Trailing liquidity bands = four ATR-spaced bands around the selected price source; in an uptrend each band trails upward using the greater of its current offset and prior band, and in a downtrend each trails downward using the lesser. Trend flips down when the source falls below the prior bar’s selected flip band and flips up when it rises above it.
Calculation: Retest score = weighted score for pullback depth, reclaim-candle close location, trend age, volume versus its prior-bar average, and higher-timeframe bias; add the RSI regime component when scoring mode is enabled, then normalize to the base score scale.
Calculation: RSI regime = bullish when price is above its trend moving average and RSI is in the bullish range for the required consecutive bars; bearish when price is below its trend moving average and RSI is in the bearish range for the required consecutive bars; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = prior completed higher-timeframe close compared with its 50-period EMA calculated on that higher timeframe.
Calculation: Trade stop and targets = stop is either an ATR-distance from entry or beyond the signal-bar wick with an ATR buffer and minimum ATR distance; targets are configured risk multiples from entry. After target 1 is reached, move the stop to entry when break-even management is enabled.
Long: confirmed upward trend flip OR confirmed uptrend retest (price touched a trailing band within the retest window, then closes above the inner band and above its open, and the retest score meets its minimum); apply the RSI regime and ADX strength filters when hard filtering is enabled. Open only when flat, or when reversal is enabled and the current position is short; also require any enabled session and post-stop cooldown filters to pass.
Short: confirmed downward trend flip OR confirmed downtrend retest (price touched a trailing band within the retest window, then closes below the inner band and below its open, and the retest score meets its minimum); apply the RSI regime and ADX strength filters when hard filtering is enabled. Open only when flat, or when reversal is enabled and the current position is long; also require any enabled session and post-stop cooldown filters to pass.
Exit: long, a confirmed bar after entry reaches the active stop or final target, the configured trade timeout expires, or an opposite short signal occurs while reversal is enabled; if stop and final target are both reached in one bar, prioritize the stop.
Exit: short, a confirmed bar after entry reaches the active stop or final target, the configured trade timeout expires, or an opposite long signal occurs while reversal is enabled; if stop and final target are both reached in one bar, prioritize the stop.
