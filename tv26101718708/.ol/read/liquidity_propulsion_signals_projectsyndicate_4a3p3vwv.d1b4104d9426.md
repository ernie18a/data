Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with the same lookback on each side; use the latest confirmed low as sell-side liquidity and latest confirmed high as buy-side liquidity.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; use it to normalize grab depth and charge components and to set stop/target distances.
Calculation: Failed sell-side grab = price trades below the latest unused confirmed swing low, then closes above it within the reclaim window; grab depth is (swing low − lowest low since first pierce) / ATR and must meet the minimum depth.
Calculation: Failed buy-side grab = price trades above the latest unused confirmed swing high, then closes below it within the reclaim window; grab depth is (highest high since first pierce − swing high) / ATR and must meet the minimum depth.
Calculation: Propulsion charge = 100 × weighted mean of six components, each clipped to [0,1]: grab depth / depth reference; 1 − bars to reclaim / reclaim window; rejection wick fraction of bar range; (volume / volume average − 1) / volume surge reference above 1; (coil looseness reference − pre-grab coil range / ATR) / (coil looseness reference − 1); reclaim displacement / (0.6 × ATR). Pre-grab coil range uses the same lookback before the first pierce. Weights and references are configurable.
Calculation: Loaded long spring = failed sell-side grab with charge at or above the arm threshold and, if enabled, the strong threshold; ignition level is the high of its reclaim bar and invalidation level is the lowest low since the pierce.
Calculation: Loaded short spring = failed buy-side grab with charge at or above the arm threshold and, if enabled, the strong threshold; ignition level is the low of its reclaim bar and invalidation level is the highest high since the pierce.
Long: Loaded long spring AND close > its ignition level before the ignition window expires AND close >= its invalidation level
Short: Loaded short spring AND close < its ignition level before the ignition window expires AND close <= its invalidation level
Calculation: Entry price = close on discharge; risk distance = ATR × risk-distance parameter; long stop = entry − risk distance and targets = entry + risk distance × each target-R parameter; short stop = entry + risk distance and targets = entry − risk distance × each target-R parameter.
Exit: long, low <= stop OR high >= final target OR stop is hit after an earlier target was reached; if stop and any target are hit on the same bar, count the stop first.
Exit: short, high >= stop OR low <= final target OR stop is hit after an earlier target was reached; if stop and any target are hit on the same bar, count the stop first.
