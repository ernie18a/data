Calculation: Shooting star = Upper wick ≥ 2 × body, lower wick ≤ 30% of range, high ≥ prior 20-bar high × 0.99, prior candle closes above its open, 7-bar RSI is falling and > 50, volume < 1.1 × 5-bar average volume, and body meets the configured minimum size.
Calculation: Morning star = Two bars ago closes below its open, its body > 0.5 × 5-bar ATR, and 14-bar RSI two bars ago < 35; prior bar low < low two bars ago and its volume < 0.7 × 20-bar average volume; current volume > 1.2 × 20-bar average volume, current close > prior high, current return from two bars ago > 1.5%, and either MACD crosses above its signal or close > medium moving average.
Calculation: Evening star = Two bars ago closes above its open, its body > 0.5 × 5-bar ATR, and 14-bar RSI two bars ago > 65; prior bar high > high two bars ago and its volume < 0.7 × 20-bar average volume; current volume > 1.2 × 20-bar average volume, current close < prior low, current return from two bars ago < −1.5%, and either MACD crosses below its signal or close < medium moving average.
Calculation: Tweezer top = Current and prior highs differ by no more than the smaller of configured ATR(5) tolerance and 0.05% of close; close[1] > close[3], close > close[2], prior candle closes above its open, current candle has an upper wick and closes ≤ open, high ≥ prior 10-bar high × 0.995, and body meets the configured minimum size.
Calculation: Tweezer bottom = Current and prior lows differ by no more than the smaller of configured ATR(5) tolerance and 0.05% of close; close[1] < close[3], close < close[2], prior candle closes below its open, current candle has a lower wick and closes ≥ open, low ≤ prior 10-bar low × 1.005, and body meets the configured minimum size.
