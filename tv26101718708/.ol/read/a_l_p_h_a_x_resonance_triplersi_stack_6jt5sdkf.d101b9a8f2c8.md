Calculation: Pulse, Core, and Anchor RSI = RSI of close using progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Dynamic overbought/oversold = upper/lower configured percentiles of Core RSI over a lookback.
Calculation: RSI bandwidth = highest Core RSI minus lowest Core RSI over a lookback; average bandwidth = moving average of RSI bandwidth over that same lookback.
Calculation: Coil release = prior bar bandwidth below average bandwidth × compression multiplier, current bar bandwidth no longer below it, and absolute one-bar Pulse RSI change at least the configured minimum.
Calculation: RSI slopes = current RSI minus its value three bars earlier for Pulse, Core, and Anchor.
Calculation: Bull stack = Pulse RSI above midline, Core RSI above midline minus its tolerance, and Anchor RSI above midline minus its tolerance; bear stack = Pulse RSI below midline, Core RSI below midline plus its tolerance, and Anchor RSI below midline plus its tolerance.
Calculation: Bull slope alignment = Pulse and Core RSI slopes positive and Anchor RSI slope nonnegative; bear slope alignment = Pulse and Core RSI slopes negative and Anchor RSI slope nonpositive.
Calculation: Bullish divergence = confirmed price pivot low below the prior confirmed price pivot low, with Core RSI at the corresponding pivot above Core RSI at the prior pivot and no more than its configured tolerance above dynamic oversold; bearish divergence = confirmed price pivot high above the prior confirmed price pivot high, with Core RSI at the corresponding pivot below Core RSI at the prior pivot and no more than its configured tolerance below dynamic overbought.
Calculation: Divergence validity = a bullish or bearish divergence remains valid for up to twice the pivot confirmation lookback after its pivot.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish when higher-timeframe fast EMA is above slow EMA and higher-timeframe close is above fast EMA; bearish when fast EMA is below slow EMA and close is below fast EMA. Use the latest available higher-timeframe bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over the lookback / (highest high minus lowest low over the lookback)) / log10(lookback).
Calculation: Directional resonance score = capped-at-10 sum of (2 for full directional RSI stack, otherwise 1 if Pulse and Core RSI are on that side of the midline, otherwise 0); (2 for full directional slope alignment, otherwise 1 if Pulse and Core slopes agree directionally, otherwise 0); (1 if Pulse RSI leads Core RSI and Core RSI is within 3 points of Anchor RSI in the directional order); (if higher-timeframe bias is enabled, 2 when aligned and 0 otherwise, else 1); (1 when volume confirmation is disabled or volume meets its configured moving-average threshold); (1 when chop blocking is disabled or Choppiness Index is below its configured threshold); and (1 when the optional price EMA filter is disabled or close is on the directional side of that EMA).
Long: Confirmed bar close AND cooldown elapsed since the prior signal AND chop gate passes AND hard higher-timeframe alignment is disabled or higher-timeframe bias is bullish AND optional EMA filter is disabled or close is above its EMA AND bullish resonance score meets its configured minimum AND at least one of: Pulse RSI crosses above dynamic oversold AND Core RSI rises AND Anchor RSI is above midline minus its tolerance AND close > open AND close > prior close; Pulse RSI crosses above midline AND bull stack AND bull slope alignment; valid bullish divergence AND close > open AND close > prior close AND Pulse RSI rises AND close > the low from the pivot-lookback bars ago; coil release AND Pulse RSI > dynamic oversold AND bull slope alignment AND close > open.
Short: Confirmed bar close AND cooldown elapsed since the prior signal AND chop gate passes AND hard higher-timeframe alignment is disabled or higher-timeframe bias is bearish AND optional EMA filter is disabled or close is below its EMA AND bearish resonance score meets its configured minimum AND at least one of: Pulse RSI crosses below dynamic overbought AND Core RSI falls AND Anchor RSI is below midline plus its tolerance AND close < open AND close < prior close; Pulse RSI crosses below midline AND bear stack AND bear slope alignment; valid bearish divergence AND close < open AND close < prior close AND Pulse RSI falls AND close < the high from the pivot-lookback bars ago; coil release AND Pulse RSI < dynamic overbought AND bear slope alignment AND close < open.
