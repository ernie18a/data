Calculation: Bullish structure = each of the three most recent confirmed swing highs is higher than the previous one AND each of the three most recent confirmed swing lows is higher than the previous one; bearish structure uses lower highs AND lower lows. Swing points are confirmed when a bar is a pivot with the swing lookback on both sides.
Calculation: Upward structure break = close crosses above the second most recent confirmed swing high; downward structure break = close crosses below the second most recent confirmed swing low.
Calculation: Confirmation score = count of these nine true conditions: bullish or bearish structure; sweep above the prior lookback-period high and close back below it OR sweep below the prior lookback-period low and close back above it; absolute candle body exceeds a multiple of its longer-lookback average; bullish gap (low exceeds the high two bars earlier and the intervening close exceeds that high) OR bearish gap (high is below the low two bars earlier and the intervening close is below that low); upward or downward structure break; bearish retest (after a downward break, high returns near the broken low and close is below it) OR bullish retest (after an upward break, low returns near the broken high and close is above it); RSI above its upper threshold with bullish structure OR below its lower threshold with bearish structure; close inside a confirmed demand or supply zone; ATR times the stop multiplier is positive. A confirmed zone is a pivot-high or pivot-low price expanded by a proportional buffer on both sides, with overlapping same-type zones merged and a minimum number of pivot touches; close must lie within the zone.
Long: bullish structure AND upward structure break AND confirmation score >= minimum score AND no long or short signal is active AND trading is not locked AND session filter passes AND no prior long signal is within the cooldown period.
Short: bearish structure AND downward structure break AND confirmation score >= minimum score AND no long or short signal is active AND trading is not locked AND session filter passes AND no prior short signal is within the cooldown period.
Long: bullish structure.
Short: bearish structure.
Exit: long, low <= entry price - ATR times stop multiplier.
Exit: short, high >= entry price + ATR times stop multiplier.
Exit: long, high >= entry price + 3 times the stop distance.
Exit: short, low <= entry price - 3 times the stop distance.
