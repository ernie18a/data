Calculation: Support and resistance levels = most recent confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed using configurable left and right bar counts
Calculation: Volume oscillator = 100 × (short-lookback EMA of volume − longer-lookback EMA of volume) ÷ longer-lookback EMA of volume
Calculation: Internal and swing structure levels = latest confirmed pivot highs and lows; a pivot at n bars ago is a high above the highs of the following n bars or a low below the lows of the following n bars. Internal pivots use a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken internal pivot high, which differs from the latest swing pivot high
Long: Close crosses above the latest unbroken swing pivot high
Long: Close crosses above the resistance level AND the volume oscillator exceeds its threshold AND open − low ≤ close − open
Long: Close crosses above the resistance level AND open − low > close − open
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken internal pivot low, which differs from the latest swing pivot low
Short: Close crosses below the latest unbroken swing pivot low
Short: Close crosses below the support level AND the volume oscillator exceeds its threshold AND open − close ≥ high − open
Short: Close crosses below the support level AND open − close < high − open
