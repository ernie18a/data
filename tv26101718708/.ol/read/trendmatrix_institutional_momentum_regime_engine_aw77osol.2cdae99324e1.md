Calculation: Price source = close, or the exponential moving average of close over a smoothing lookback, according to the smoothing setting.
Calculation: Trend midpoint = linear regression of the price source over the trend-anchor lookback.
Calculation: Z-score = (price source − exponential moving average of price source over the extension lookback) / standard deviation of price source over that same lookback; use 0 if standard deviation is zero.
Calculation: Volatility percentile = percentile rank of the exponential moving average of the higher-timeframe close standard deviation over the volatility-regime lookback, smoothed over 20 higher-timeframe bars, ranked over 252 higher-timeframe bars. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Upper and lower anchors = highest and lowest price source over the trend-anchor lookback.
Calculation: Dynamic upper multiplier = base upper multiplier × (1 − logistic( Z-score) × upper compression factor); dynamic lower multiplier = base lower multiplier × (1 − logistic(−Z-score) × lower compression factor), where logistic(x) = 1/(1+e^(−x)).
Calculation: Local volatility = average true range over the trend-anchor lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower bands = max and min, respectively, of [(0.7 × upper anchor + 0.3 × trend midpoint − dynamic upper multiplier × local volatility × (1 + 0.5 × volatility percentile/100))] and [(0.7 × lower anchor + 0.3 × trend midpoint + dynamic lower multiplier × local volatility × (1 + 0.5 × volatility percentile/100)].
Calculation: Adaptive centerline = 10-period exponential moving average of the midpoint between the upper and lower bands.
Calculation: Bullish slope = trend midpoint is greater than its value one bar earlier; bearish slope = trend midpoint is less than its value one bar earlier.
Long: (price source crosses above adaptive centerline AND prior state is not bullish) OR (price source is above adaptive centerline AND bullish slope AND prior state is not bullish AND the centerline crossover occurred within the last 2 bars) OR (price source crosses above lower band AND prior state is not bullish); require the current bar to be confirmed if close confirmation is enabled.
Short: (price source crosses below adaptive centerline AND prior state is bullish) OR (price source is below adaptive centerline AND bearish slope AND prior state is bullish AND the centerline crossunder occurred within the last 2 bars) OR (price source crosses below upper band AND prior state is bullish); require the current bar to be confirmed if close confirmation is enabled.
