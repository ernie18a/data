Calculation: Fast/slow trend alignment = the short-term pair of close-based moving averages are ordered upward, each rising over its lookback, and close is above both; the long-term pair is ordered upward, each rising over its lookback, and close is above at least one.
Calculation: Fast bear = the short-term pair of close-based moving averages are ordered downward, each falling over its lookback, and close is below both.
Calculation: Upper-wick fraction = (high − max(open, close)) / max(high − low, minimum range); wick passes if this is at most the configured maximum, or the wick filter is disabled.
Calculation: Demand dominant = count of up bars exceeds count of down bars over the configured lookback, or the demand requirement is disabled.
Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-previous-close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Fast extension in ATR = (close − short-term moving average 1) / ATR.
Calculation: Strong alignment = fast and slow trend alignment and close above both short-term moving averages.
Calculation: Structural deterioration = fast bear, or close below the lower short-term moving average, or close below the lower long-term moving average, or trend efficiency at most its low threshold while close is below the higher short-term moving average.
Calculation: Trend maturity score = 0.40 × min(bars since latest TS/TS+ turn / maturity duration, 1) + 0.40 × min(max(fast extension in ATR, 0) / maturity extension, 1) + 0.20 × min(trend efficiency / high-efficiency threshold, 1).
Calculation: Exhaustion risk = fast extension in ATR at least the exhaustion threshold, or maturity score at least the exhaustion threshold and extension at least the extended threshold, or trend efficiency at most its low threshold while extended, or positive fast-average slope with declining slope while extended and beyond the extended threshold.
Calculation: Recent slow bear = a slow bear occurred within the configured lookback.
Calculation: Prior-bar breakout = current high exceeds the highest high of the configured number of preceding bars.
Calculation: Long-entry wick and risk gate = wick passes; and, for RA entries, structural deterioration is false, high risk is false, and portfolio trim/review are false; for TS/CS entries, portfolio trim/review are false.
Long: TS+ conditions = no TS/TS+ already occurred since the latest slow bear; TS+ cooldown has elapsed; recent slow bear; prior-bar breakout; up bar closing in the configured upper portion of its range; range exceeds its moving average by the configured multiplier; wick passes; and (close crosses above short-term average 1, or close is above the fast pair) when required, and (short-term average 1 rises and average 2 is non-falling) when required, and close is at least the lower long-term average when required; and no TS signal.
Long: TS conditions = recent slow bear and the fast pair is not wholly above the long-term pair; both short-term averages rise and the upper fast average is at least the lower long-term average; up bar closes in the configured upper portion of its range and above the upper fast average; wick passes; close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high recorded in the turn phase; and no TS/TS+ has occurred since the latest slow bear.
Long: RA conditions = fast pair is not bearish and close is above the upper fast average; required count of lower-or-equal-low events in the pullback window; prior-bar breakout over the configured higher-high lookback; up bar; required pause since prior breakout; wick passes; reset has occurred if required; signal cooldown and post-turn suppression have elapsed; trend-lock suppression is false; and long-entry wick and risk gate.
Long: CS standard conditions = fast trend alignment and close above both long-term averages; lower-low event count since the latest confirmed anchor pivot meets the requirement; no higher-low cooldown is pending; current high (or close, if required) exceeds the anchor high; up bar if required; wick passes; fast-ribbon touch during the prior pullback window if required; demand dominant; and portfolio trim/review are false.
Long: CS rotational conditions = short-term average 1 above average 2; close above the lower fast average and upper long-term average; fast pair is not bearish; prior pullback touches the upper fast average; current high exceeds the highest high over the configured preceding-bar lookback; up bar if required; wick passes; demand dominant; rotational cooldown elapsed; and portfolio trim/review are false.
Long: Ideal marker = fast and slow trend alignment, fast pair above slow pair, close above upper fast average, maturity score within configured bounds, trend efficiency at least its threshold, fast extension in ATR at most its threshold, opportunity is compression setup, optimal pullback, add on pullback, or re-acceleration (or core trend hold if enabled), risk is low (or normal if enabled), exhaustion and extended chase risk are false, and fresh RA/CS signal if required; when fresh signal is not required, this condition has just changed from false to true.
Long: Portfolio BUILD marker = ideal marker, or strong alignment and fresh RA/CS momentum, maturity score within build bounds, efficiency at least its threshold, fast extension at most its threshold, not high risk, not extended chase risk, and opportunity is neither Avoid/Reduce nor Chase Risk; emit only on transition into BUILD and when portfolio cooldown permits.
Long: Portfolio SMALL marker = strong alignment and fresh RA/CS momentum, not BUILD or EXTENDED, not high risk, and (elevated risk or Chase Risk opportunity or maturity above its threshold or fast extension above its threshold); emit only on transition into SMALL and when portfolio cooldown permits.
Exit: Long, confirmed portfolio trim = (Avoid/Reduce opportunity and structural deterioration) or (late-stage extension and structural deterioration) or (exhaustion reversal) or (high risk and structural deterioration); condition must persist for the configured trim confirmation bars.
Exit: Long, confirmed portfolio review = exhaustion-risk stage, or fast bear with close below upper fast average, or close below upper long-term average and below long-term average 3, or transition with weak alignment, close below lower fast average and low efficiency; deterioration must have persisted for the configured review confirmation bars; exclude confirmed trim and extended hold.
