Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the configured number of bars on each side; use its values and volume checks only after the required right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume over one lookback.
Calculation: Pivot-bar close position = (close − low) / (high − low), evaluated on the pivot bar; use 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Bar move (%) = 100 × (close − open) / open; use 0 when open ≤ 0.
Calculation: Prior trend = rising when the current simple moving average of close is above its value one equal-length lookback earlier; otherwise falling.
Long: In idle state, a confirmed pivot low has volume above the volume average × climax threshold AND pivot-bar close position ≥ 0.40 AND prior trend is falling (Selling Climax); OR volume is above 0.85 × the climax threshold but not above the climax threshold AND pivot-bar close position ≥ 0.30 AND prior trend is falling (Preliminary Support).
Short: In idle state, a confirmed pivot high has volume above the volume average × climax threshold AND pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.60 AND prior trend is rising (Buying Climax); OR volume is above 0.85 × the climax threshold but not above the climax threshold AND pivot-bar close position ≤ 0.70 AND prior trend is rising (Preliminary Supply).
Long: After a Selling Climax, a confirmed pivot high has (pivot high − support) / support × 100 ≥ minimum range (Automatic Rally); OR in accumulation state after the rally, a confirmed pivot low is between support × (1 − spring tolerance/100) and support × (1 + test tolerance/100) AND pivot-bar volume < pivot-bar volume average × low-volume threshold (Secondary Test); OR in accumulation state before a Secondary Test, close > resistance AND bar move ≥ minimum move AND volume > volume average × strength-volume threshold (Sign of Strength).
Short: After a Buying Climax, a confirmed pivot low has (resistance − pivot low) / resistance × 100 ≥ minimum range (Automatic Reaction); OR in BC-started state after the reaction, a confirmed pivot high is between resistance × (1 − 4 × test tolerance/100) and resistance × (1 + spring tolerance/100) AND pivot-bar volume < pivot-bar volume average × low-volume threshold (Secondary Test).
Long: After a Secondary Test, low ≤ support AND low ≥ support × (1 − spring tolerance/100) AND close ≥ support × 0.995 AND close position ≥ 0.55 (Spring); OR low < support × (1 − spring tolerance/100) AND close position ≥ 0.40 (deep Spring); OR after Spring, a confirmed pivot low has pivot-bar volume < pivot-bar volume average × low-volume threshold AND pivot low ≥ support × 0.98 (Test); OR after Spring or Test, close > resistance AND bar move ≥ minimum move AND volume > volume average × strength-volume threshold (Sign of Strength).
Short: After a Secondary Test in a BC-started state, high ≥ resistance AND high ≤ resistance × (1 + spring tolerance/100) AND close ≤ resistance × 1.005 AND close position ≤ 0.45 (UTAD); OR low < support × (1 − spring tolerance/100) AND close position < 0.40 AND bar move ≤ −minimum move AND volume > volume average × strength-volume threshold (Sign of Weakness).
Long: After Sign of Strength, a confirmed pivot low has pivot-bar volume < pivot-bar volume average × low-volume threshold AND pivot low > support (Last Point of Support); OR after Markdown, a confirmed pivot high is later than the Markdown entry bar AND is at least 3 × test tolerance percent above the lowest close since Markdown began (Automatic Rally); OR after Last Point of Support, close > resistance (Markup).
Short: After UTAD, close < support AND bar move ≤ −minimum move AND volume > volume average × strength-volume threshold (Sign of Weakness); OR after Sign of Weakness, a confirmed pivot high has pivot-bar volume < pivot-bar volume average × low-volume threshold AND pivot high < resistance (Last Point of Supply); OR after Last Point of Supply, close < support AND volume > volume average × strength-volume threshold (Markdown); OR during Markup, close < the highest close since Markup began × (1 − 5 × test tolerance/100) (failed Markup to Markdown).
Exit: none
