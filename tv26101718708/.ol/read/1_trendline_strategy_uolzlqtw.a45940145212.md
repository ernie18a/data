Calculation: Upper pool boundary = the raw descending line through two consecutive confirmed pivot highs (equal left and right lookbacks), formed when the newer high is lower than the older high and either a prior pool has broken or both highs are below each of the two preceding pivot highs; keep it only if, while extending the line from the older pivot to the current bar, every intervening low is at or above the parallel line offset below it by padding × min(ATR(200) × 0.1, close × 0.001).
Calculation: Lower pool boundary = the raw ascending line through two consecutive confirmed pivot lows (equal left and right lookbacks), formed when the newer low is higher than the older low and either a prior pool has broken or both lows are above each of the two preceding pivot lows; keep it only if, while extending the line from the older pivot to the current bar, every intervening high is at or below the parallel line offset above it by padding × min(ATR(200) × 0.1, close × 0.001).
Calculation: Long stop = signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier; long target = signal-bar close + ATR × target multiplier.
Calculation: Short stop = signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier; short target = signal-bar close − ATR × target multiplier.
Long: flat position AND active upper pool boundary exists AND low > upper pool boundary AND (trend filter disabled OR close > EMA calculated on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar AND that EMA > its value on the previous chart bar).
Short: flat position AND active lower pool boundary exists AND high < lower pool boundary AND (trend filter disabled OR close < EMA calculated on a higher timeframe using its last completed bar AND that EMA < its value on the previous chart bar).
Exit: long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
