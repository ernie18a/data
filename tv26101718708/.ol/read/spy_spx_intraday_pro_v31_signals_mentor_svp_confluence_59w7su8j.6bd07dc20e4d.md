Calculation: Fast EMA = exponential moving average of close with a shorter lookback than the slow EMA
Calculation: Slow EMA = exponential moving average of close with a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: VWAP = volume-weighted average price
Calculation: Shorter higher-timeframe trend = close above (bullish) or below (bearish) VWAP on the shorter higher timeframe, using its last completed bar
Calculation: Longer higher-timeframe trend = close above (bullish) or below (bearish) VWAP on the longer higher timeframe, using its last completed bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure = bullish when close is above VWAP and fast EMA is above slow EMA; bearish when close is below VWAP and fast EMA is below slow EMA, using the last completed bar of a higher timeframe shorter than the longer higher timeframe
Calculation: Candle body percentage = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low
Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the opening-range window, fixed after that window ends
Calculation: Recent pullback = a bar within the pullback lookback touched or crossed the fast EMA, slow EMA, or VWAP
Calculation: Confluence score = weighted sum of directional higher-timeframe trend, higher-timeframe structure, strong volume, ADX, directional RSI range, close relative to slow EMA, proximity to supportive or opposing session-volume-profile levels, qualifying entry path, session timing, and directional high volume; cap the result at the score maximum
