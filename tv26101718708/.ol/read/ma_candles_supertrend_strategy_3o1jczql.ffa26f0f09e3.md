Calculation: Smoothed OHLC = apply the selected moving average, using the same type and lookback, separately to each chart-timeframe open, high, low, and close series
Calculation: Custom supertrend direction and stops = true range is max(smoothed high, prior smoothed close) − min(smoothed low, prior smoothed close); ATR is the selected moving average of true range times its multiplier. Start long stop at smoothed close − ATR and short stop at smoothed close + ATR. Ratchet the long stop to max(current long stop, prior long stop) when the prior selected wick or close is above the prior long stop; otherwise use the current long stop. Ratchet the short stop to min(current short stop, prior short stop) when the prior selected wick or close is below the prior short stop; otherwise use the current short stop. Start direction at +1; change −1 to +1 when the current selected wick or close is above the prior short stop; change +1 to −1 when the prior selected wick or close is below the prior long stop. Use the configured wick mode for wick-versus-close selections.
Calculation: Prior annual high and low = high and low of the last completed annual bar, from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe
Long: direction = +1 AND prior direction = +1 AND (close > prior close OR close > short stop) AND (close > prior annual high × (1 − downward threshold) OR close > prior annual low × (1 + rebound threshold)) AND chart bar time is within the configured date range
Short: direction = −1 AND (prior direction = −1 AND close < prior close OR close < long stop) AND (close < prior annual low × (1 + downward threshold) OR close < prior annual high × (1 − rebound threshold)) AND chart bar time is within the configured date range
Exit: Long, direction = −1 AND (prior direction = −1 AND close < prior close OR close < long stop)
Exit: Short, direction = +1 AND (prior direction = +1 AND close > prior close OR close > short stop)
