Calculation: Moving average = selected moving average type and lookback applied to the selected price source; use its value from the previous chart bar.
Calculation: ATR bands = previous chart bar's selected moving average of true range, plus or minus ATR multiplied by a multiplier.
Calculation: Confirmed uptrend = selected confirmation price (close, or high for uptrend and low for downtrend) is above the upper band for the configured number of consecutive bars; confirmed downtrend = it is below the lower band for that many consecutive bars. A trend changes to up or down only when its confirmed state differs from the current trend.
Calculation: Cross below moving average = current low crosses from at or above to below the previous-bar moving average; cross above moving average = current high crosses from at or below to above the previous-bar moving average.
Calculation: Take-profit percentage = either a fixed percentage or the 70th percentile of percentage maximum favorable excursions from completed confirmed trends; stop-loss percentage = either a fixed percentage or the 70th percentile of percentage maximum adverse excursions from completed confirmed trends.
Long: Position is flat AND (ATR filter is disabled OR previous-bar ATR is greater than its simple moving average) AND ((entry mode is breakout AND a confirmed uptrend has just begun) OR (entry mode is moving-average cross AND current trend is up AND current low crosses below the previous-bar moving average)).
Short: Position is flat AND (ATR filter is disabled OR previous-bar ATR is greater than its simple moving average) AND ((entry mode is breakout AND a confirmed downtrend has just begun) OR (entry mode is moving-average cross AND current trend is down AND current high crosses above the previous-bar moving average)).
Exit: Long, current low is at or below entry price times (1 minus stop-loss percentage / 100) OR current high is at or above entry price times (1 plus take-profit percentage / 100).
Exit: Short, current high is at or above entry price times (1 plus stop-loss percentage / 100) OR current low is at or below entry price times (1 minus take-profit percentage / 100).
