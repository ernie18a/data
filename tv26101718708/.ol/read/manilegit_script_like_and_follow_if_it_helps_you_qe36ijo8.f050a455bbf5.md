Calculation: Fast/slow trend = fast EMA > slow EMA; main trend = close > longer-term EMA; bearish versions reverse both comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use only after the bars on both sides have completed.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = high > latest confirmed pivot high AND close < latest confirmed pivot high; bearish sweep = low < latest confirmed pivot low AND close > latest confirmed pivot low.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars earlier.
Calculation: Bullish order block = bullish structure break AND previous bar closed below its open; bearish order block = bearish structure break AND previous bar closed above its open.
Calculation: Bullish demand zone = volume exceeds its moving average by a high-volume threshold AND close > open AND lower wick exceeds body by a threshold; bearish supply zone = volume exceeds its moving average by the same threshold AND close < open AND upper wick exceeds body by that threshold.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = close > open AND lower wick exceeds body by a rejection threshold; bearish rejection = close < open AND upper wick exceeds body by the same threshold.
Calculation: Wave = difference between a shorter-period and a longer-period EMA of close; its signal is an EMA of the wave. Bull wave = wave > signal AND wave > zero; bear wave = wave < signal AND wave < zero. Bull wave flip = wave crosses above signal; bear wave flip = wave crosses below signal.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = fast EMA > slow EMA, or fast EMA < slow EMA, on a higher timeframe, using its last completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish trend regime = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > the longer-term EMA; bearish trend regime = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < the longer-term EMA.
Calculation: Bullish pressure = close > open AND volume > its moving average; bearish pressure = close < open AND volume > its moving average.
Calculation: Volatility expansion = ATR > its moving average; strong candle = body / (high - low) exceeds a strength threshold AND high - low > ATR.
Calculation: Bullish heatmap = wave momentum > zero AND bullish pressure AND volatility expansion; bearish heatmap = wave momentum < zero AND bearish pressure AND volatility expansion.
Calculation: Premium/discount midpoint = midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the same lookback; premium = close > midpoint; discount = close < midpoint.
Calculation: AI confluence score = weighted sum of trend presence, strong ADX, aligned higher-timeframe trend, volatility expansion, directional pressure, session overlap, structure break, sweep, fair value gap, order block, supply/demand zone, equal swing level, aligned wave and trend, aligned Supertrend and trend, strong candle, and news allowance.
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish CHoCH OR bullish sweep OR bullish fair value gap OR bullish order block OR bullish demand zone OR bullish rejection OR bull wave flip OR bullish heatmap OR (bullish trend regime AND close > open) OR (close > Supertrend) OR (bullish trend regime AND RSI > its midpoint AND close > VWAP AND higher-timeframe bias is bullish AND ADX exceeds its strong-trend threshold AND discount AND close > Supertrend AND bull wave AND (bullish structure break OR bullish sweep OR bullish rejection OR bullish demand zone) AND news is allowed AND AI confluence score reaches its high threshold).
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish CHoCH OR bearish sweep OR bearish fair value gap OR bearish order block OR bearish supply zone OR bearish rejection OR bear wave flip OR bearish heatmap OR (bearish trend regime AND close < open) OR (close < Supertrend) OR (bearish trend regime AND RSI < its midpoint AND close < VWAP AND higher-timeframe bias is bearish AND ADX exceeds its strong-trend threshold AND premium AND close < Supertrend AND bear wave AND (bearish structure break OR bearish sweep OR bearish rejection OR bearish supply zone) AND news is allowed AND AI confluence score reaches its high threshold).
