Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest over the configured number of chart bars on each side; confirm only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Wedge boundaries = straight lines through two confirmed pivot highs (upper) and two confirmed pivot lows (lower), using overlapping pivot pairs
Calculation: Strict rising wedge = both boundary slopes are positive and the lower slope is steeper; strict falling wedge = both slopes are negative and the upper slope is steeper; for either, the initial width is positive and meets its ATR-based minimum, the final width is positive and within the configured maximum fraction of initial width, the pattern span and midpoint travel meet their configured ATR-based minima, and at least one additional confirmed pivot lies within the configured ATR tolerance of a boundary
Long: an active strict falling wedge remains unbroken and not invalidated, the bar is after its last defining pivot, the break cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is confirmed AND (in close-break mode, close > upper boundary + ATR-based break buffer; otherwise, high > upper boundary + ATR-based break buffer)
Short: an active strict rising wedge remains unbroken and not invalidated, the bar is after its last defining pivot, the break cooldown has elapsed, and the bar is confirmed AND (in close-break mode, close < lower boundary - ATR-based break buffer; otherwise, low < lower boundary - ATR-based break buffer)
Exit: long, high >= break close + initial wedge width × target-width multiplier - ATR-based target half-width OR close < lower boundary at break - ATR-based invalidation buffer
Exit: short, low <= break close - initial wedge width × target-width multiplier + ATR-based target half-width OR close > upper boundary at break + ATR-based invalidation buffer
