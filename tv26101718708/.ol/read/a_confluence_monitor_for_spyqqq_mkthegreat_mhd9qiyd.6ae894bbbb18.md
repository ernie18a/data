Calculation: Bull score = count of these seven conditions that are true: close > VWAP; short-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA AND close > short-lookback EMA; RSI > its bullish threshold; MACD line > MACD signal AND MACD histogram > its prior-bar value; volume >= its longer-lookback average volume × volume multiplier; close > long-lookback EMA; bullish breakout trigger.
Calculation: Bear score = count of these seven conditions that are true: close < VWAP; short-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA AND close < short-lookback EMA; RSI < its bearish threshold; MACD line < MACD signal AND MACD histogram < its prior-bar value; volume >= its longer-lookback average volume × volume multiplier; close < long-lookback EMA; bearish breakdown trigger. The fast, medium, and slow EMA lookbacks are progressively longer; MACD fast lookback is shorter than its slow lookback.
Calculation: Bullish breakout trigger = close crosses above any of: the highest high of the preceding breakout-lookback bars, premarket high, previous completed daily high, or opening-range high; crossing level is increased by ATR × buffer multiplier. Premarket and opening-range levels are the running session high, available only after at least one bar in that session.
Calculation: Bearish breakdown trigger = close crosses below any of: the lowest low of the preceding breakout-lookback bars, premarket low, previous completed daily low, or opening-range low; crossing level is decreased by ATR × buffer multiplier. Premarket and opening-range levels are the running session low, available only after at least one bar in that session.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish confirmation = higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe VWAP AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA AND higher-timeframe RSI > bullish threshold AND higher-timeframe MACD line > MACD signal. These values use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish confirmation = higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe VWAP AND higher-timeframe short-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA AND higher-timeframe RSI < bearish threshold AND higher-timeframe MACD line < MACD signal. These values use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Long: regular session AND bull score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe bullish confirmation) AND (prior bar did not meet the same long conditions).
Short: regular session AND bear score >= minimum score AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR higher-timeframe bearish confirmation) AND (prior bar did not meet the same short conditions).
