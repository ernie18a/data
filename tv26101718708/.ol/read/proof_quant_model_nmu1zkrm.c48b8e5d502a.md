Calculation: Historical volatility = moving average of the standard deviation of one-bar percentage changes in primary-timeframe price, over a longer lookback than the smoothing lookback; primary timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and uses its last completed bar
Calculation: Z-score = (primary-timeframe price − its moving average) ÷ its standard deviation, using the same lookback for the moving average and standard deviation; primary timeframe is higher than the chart timeframe and uses its last completed bar
Long: Flat AND historical volatility ≤ high-volatility threshold AND historical volatility ≥ low-volatility threshold AND primary-timeframe price is between the lower and upper Bollinger Bands, inclusive AND ((historical volatility ≥ low-volatility threshold AND short-lookback primary-timeframe SMA > longer-lookback primary-timeframe SMA AND short-lookback primary-timeframe SMA > longer-lookback SMA of the higher long-term-timeframe price) OR (historical volatility < low-volatility threshold AND Z-score < −1))
Short: Flat AND historical volatility ≤ high-volatility threshold AND historical volatility ≥ low-volatility threshold AND primary-timeframe price is between the lower and upper Bollinger Bands, inclusive AND historical volatility < low-volatility threshold AND Z-score > 1
Exit: Long, primary-timeframe price ≥ upper Bollinger Band OR primary-timeframe price < primary-timeframe price − ATR multiplier × 14-bar ATR on the chart timeframe OR primary-timeframe price ≥ Bollinger Band basis OR bars since entry ≥ maximum holding bars
Exit: Short, primary-timeframe price ≤ lower Bollinger Band OR primary-timeframe price > primary-timeframe price + ATR multiplier × 14-bar ATR on the chart timeframe OR primary-timeframe price ≤ Bollinger Band basis OR bars since entry ≥ maximum holding bars
