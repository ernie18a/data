Calculation: Volume profile resistance = highest high over the lookback minus (highest high over the lookback minus lowest low over the same lookback) divided by the number of profile rows
Calculation: Volume profile support = lowest low over the lookback plus (highest high over the lookback minus lowest low over the same lookback) divided by the number of profile rows
Long: Current close > current volume profile resistance AND previous close <= previous volume profile resistance
Short: Current close < current volume profile support AND previous close >= previous volume profile support
