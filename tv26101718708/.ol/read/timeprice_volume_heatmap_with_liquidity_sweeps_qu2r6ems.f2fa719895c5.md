Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar’s high greater than the highs of the preceding and following pivot-strength bars; available once the following bars are complete.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar’s low lower than the lows of the preceding and following pivot-strength bars; available once the following bars are complete.
Long: Current low < an unswept confirmed pivot low that has not exceeded its maximum age
Short: Current high > an unswept confirmed pivot high that has not exceeded its maximum age
