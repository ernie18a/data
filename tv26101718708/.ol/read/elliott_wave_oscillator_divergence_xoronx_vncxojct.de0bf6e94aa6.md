Calculation: Elliott Wave Oscillator (EWO) = short-lookback moving average of the selected OHLC price source minus long-lookback moving average of the same source, calculated on the signal timeframe. For higher-timeframe signals use the last completed bar; for chart-timeframe signals use the last completed bar; for lower-timeframe signals use completed aggregated bars.
Long: A confirmed negative EWO lobe has a trough at least the minimum closed-bar count behind the confirmation bar and has crossed zero; its price extreme is the lobe’s lowest low. Compare it with the eligible prior negative-lobe anchor having the lowest EWO trough, whose price extreme is that lobe’s lowest low: the pivot separation is within the minimum and maximum lookback bounds, the anchor is a strong lobe (absolute EWO trough is at least the configured fraction of the rolling maximum negative EWO amplitude), and no intervening EWO value is lower than the anchor trough; AND (current low < anchor low AND current EWO trough >= anchor EWO trough [regular bullish divergence] OR current low > anchor low AND current EWO trough < anchor EWO trough [hidden bullish divergence]). If the largest-divergence filter is enabled, the absolute price gap exceeds the previous selected gap for this anchor and divergence type, or ties it with a later pivot.
Short: A confirmed positive EWO lobe has a peak at least the minimum closed-bar count behind the confirmation bar and has crossed zero; its price extreme is the lobe’s highest high. Compare it with the eligible prior positive-lobe anchor having the highest EWO peak, whose price extreme is that lobe’s highest high: the pivot separation is within the minimum and maximum lookback bounds, the anchor is a strong lobe (absolute EWO peak is at least the configured fraction of the rolling maximum positive EWO amplitude), and no intervening EWO value is higher than the anchor peak; AND (current high > anchor high AND current EWO peak <= anchor EWO peak [regular bearish divergence] OR current high < anchor high AND current EWO peak > anchor EWO peak [hidden bearish divergence]). If the largest-divergence filter is enabled, the absolute price gap exceeds the previous selected gap for this anchor and divergence type, or ties it with a later pivot.
