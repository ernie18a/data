Calculation: Bullish candle candidates = confirmed bullish engulfing, hammer, inverted hammer, morning star, piercing line, bullish harami, inside-bar break upward, bullish marubozu, three advancing bullish candles, tweezer low, bullish rail, or bullish sweep-and-reclaim (bar low below the last confirmed pivot low and close back above it with bullish lower-wick rejection).
Calculation: Bearish candle candidates = confirmed bearish engulfing, shooting star, hanging man, evening star, dark cloud cover, bearish harami, inside-bar break downward, bearish marubozu, three declining bearish candles, tweezer high, bearish rail, or bearish sweep-and-reject (bar high above the last confirmed pivot high and close back below it with bearish upper-wick rejection).
Calculation: Candle candidate score = pattern-specific base score plus context contributions for trend alignment, location alignment, structure alignment, momentum alignment, volume expansion, wick/body quality, and normalized absolute regression slope; structure alignment has a configurable weight. Bullish and bearish candidates use the corresponding directional tests. Choose the single highest score across both directions; ties go to the candidate encountered first in this order: bullish engulfing, bearish engulfing, hammer, inverted hammer, shooting star, hanging man, morning star, evening star, piercing line, dark cloud cover, bullish harami, bearish harami, upward inside break, downward inside break, bullish marubozu, bearish marubozu, three advancing bullish candles, three declining bearish candles, tweezer low, tweezer high, bullish rail, bearish rail, bullish sweep-and-reclaim, bearish sweep-and-reject.
Calculation: Bullish context = fast EMA is above slow EMA and linear-regression slope is nonnegative. Bearish context = fast EMA is below slow EMA and linear-regression slope is nonpositive. Regression slope is the change in the linear-regression value from the prior bar.
Calculation: Bullish location alignment = close is in or below the lower regression field, or bullish context and close is above the fast EMA. Bearish location alignment = close is in or above the upper regression field, or bearish context and close is below the fast EMA. The regression field uses the regression value plus/minus its standard deviation, with inner and outer bands.
Calculation: Bullish structure alignment = close is above the last confirmed pivot high. Bearish structure alignment = close is below the last confirmed pivot low. Confirm pivots using bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Bullish momentum alignment = RSI is above its midpoint and rising. Bearish momentum alignment = RSI is below its midpoint and falling. Volume expansion = volume is above its moving average. Wick rejection = lower wick is at least a multiple of the body and the close is in the upper part of the bar for bullish candidates; upper wick is at least a multiple of the body and the close is in the lower part for bearish candidates. Strong body = body is a sufficient fraction of the bar range.
Calculation: Candidate patterns = engulfing requires opposite-colored prior bar, current body engulfs the prior body, and current body is sufficiently large relative to the prior body; hammer and shooting star require a long lower or upper wick respectively, a limited opposite wick, and a close near the corresponding end of the range; inverted hammer requires a bullish body, long upper wick, close in the upper part of the range, and bullish location; hanging man requires a bearish body, long lower wick, bearish location, and close in the lower part; morning/evening star requires an opposite-colored bar two bars earlier, a small middle bar, and a current close beyond the midpoint of the first bar's body in the reversal direction; piercing/dark cloud requires an opposite-colored prior bar, a gap beyond its close, and a current close past the midpoint but still within its body; harami requires opposite-colored bars, current range inside prior range, and close beyond the prior open in the current bar's direction; inside break requires the prior bar inside the bar before it and current close beyond the prior range in the breakout direction; marubozu requires a directional body occupying most of the range, close near the directional extreme, and a small opposing wick; three-candle sequence requires three same-colored bars with successively higher closes for bullish or lower closes for bearish, and a sufficiently large current body; tweezer requires adjacent lows or highs within an ATR-based tolerance, opposite-colored bars, and close near the directional extreme; rail requires opposite-colored bars, current body occupying a sufficient range fraction, similar adjacent body sizes, and close beyond the prior bar's high or low in the current direction. All multi-bar patterns use completed bars.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND the highest-scoring candidate is bullish AND its score meets the quality threshold AND no accepted signal occurred within the cooldown interval.
Short: Current bar is confirmed AND the highest-scoring candidate is bearish AND its score meets the quality threshold AND no accepted signal occurred within the cooldown interval.
