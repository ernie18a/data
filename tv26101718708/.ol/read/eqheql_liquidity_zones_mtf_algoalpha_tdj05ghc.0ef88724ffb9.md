Calculation: Volatility tolerance = longer-lookback EMA of the average of absolute high-to-prior-high and low-to-prior-low changes, multiplied by the equal-level tolerance
Calculation: Equal-high match = absolute difference between highs < volatility tolerance
Calculation: Equal-low match = absolute difference between lows < volatility tolerance
Calculation: EQH zone = [maximum of the two highs, maximum of their opens and closes]; EQL zone = [minimum of the two lows, minimum of their opens and closes]
Long: On a chart-timeframe bar close or on processing a last completed bar from a selected higher timeframe, adjacent equal lows form a new EQL when the prior bar did not already match and (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI > neutral + strength); OR a confirmed local trough matches the nearest valid older local trough within the longer lookback, intervening lows stay above both troughs, the prior bar did not already form an EQL, and (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI > neutral + strength); OR a known EQL zone is active or swept on this chart bar, this chart bar touches the zone, (its open or previous close is at or above the zone's lower edge), and its close is above the zone's upper edge
Short: On a chart-timeframe bar close or on processing a last completed bar from a selected higher timeframe, adjacent equal highs form a new EQH when the prior bar did not already match and (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI < neutral − strength); OR a confirmed local peak matches the nearest valid older local peak within the longer lookback, intervening highs stay below both peaks, the prior bar did not already form an EQH, and (RSI filter is disabled OR RSI < neutral − strength); OR a known EQH zone is active or swept on this chart bar, this chart bar touches the zone, (its open or previous close is at or below the zone's upper edge), and its close is below the zone's lower edge
