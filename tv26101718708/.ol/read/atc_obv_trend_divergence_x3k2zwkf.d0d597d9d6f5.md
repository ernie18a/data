Calculation: OBV = cumulative sum of volume when close rises, minus volume when close falls; unchanged close adds zero
Calculation: Smoothed OBV = Hull moving average of OBV
Calculation: OBV slope Z-score = (current smoothed OBV minus smoothed OBV from the slope lookback, minus its rolling mean) divided by its rolling standard deviation over the longer Z-score window; use zero when standard deviation is zero
Calculation: OBV pivot high/low = confirmed swing high/low in smoothed OBV, using the configured left and right pivot lookbacks
Calculation: Price pivot high/low = confirmed swing high/low in high/low, using the same left and right pivot lookbacks as OBV pivots
Calculation: OBV structure = compare the latest and preceding confirmed OBV pivot highs and lows: higher highs plus higher lows is Uptrend; lower highs plus lower lows is Downtrend; higher highs plus lower lows is Expanding; lower highs plus higher lows is Contracting; otherwise classify the most recent pivot side as Higher High, Lower High, Higher Low, or Lower Low when its corresponding comparison holds
Long: (OBV structure is Uptrend, Higher High, or Higher Low) OR (OBV slope Z-score crosses above the bullish threshold) OR (smoothed OBV crosses above its rolling mean baseline) OR (a new confirmed OBV pivot high exceeds the preceding confirmed OBV pivot high) OR (bullish divergence: latest price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low AND latest OBV pivot low is higher than the preceding OBV pivot low AND the price pivot lows are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum divergence bars AND latest price and OBV pivot lows are no more than twice the right pivot lookback apart AND, if the same-side slope filter is enabled, OBV slope Z-score is above the negative bearish threshold; trigger on confirmation of a new price pivot low and only if more than the right pivot lookback plus one bars have passed since the preceding bullish divergence)
Short: (OBV structure is Downtrend, Lower High, or Lower Low) OR (OBV slope Z-score crosses below the negative bearish threshold) OR (smoothed OBV crosses below its rolling mean baseline) OR (a new confirmed OBV pivot low is below the preceding confirmed OBV pivot low) OR (bearish divergence: latest price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high AND latest OBV pivot high is lower than the preceding OBV pivot high AND the price pivot highs are separated by at least the minimum and at most the maximum divergence bars AND latest price and OBV pivot highs are no more than twice the right pivot lookback apart AND, if the same-side slope filter is enabled, OBV slope Z-score is below the bullish threshold; trigger on confirmation of a new price pivot high and only if more than the right pivot lookback plus one bars have passed since the preceding bearish divergence)
