Calculation: Bull score = count of these six conditions: close > short-term EMA and short-term EMA > long-term EMA; Supertrend is bullish; ADX >= trend-strength threshold and +DI > -DI; RSI >= bullish threshold; linear-regression slope > 0; higher-timeframe close > short-term EMA > long-term EMA. The higher timeframe is above the chart timeframe and uses its still-forming bar; its EMA lookbacks correspond to the chart's short-term and long-term EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: Bear score = count of these six conditions: close < short-term EMA and short-term EMA < long-term EMA; Supertrend is bearish; ADX >= trend-strength threshold and -DI > +DI; RSI <= bearish threshold; linear-regression slope < 0; higher-timeframe close < short-term EMA < long-term EMA. The higher timeframe is above the chart timeframe and uses its still-forming bar; its EMA lookbacks correspond to the chart's short-term and long-term EMA lookbacks.
Calculation: Linear-regression slope = current linear-regression value of close minus its value one chart bar earlier.
Calculation: Trend score = bull score - bear score.
Long: (bull score >= strong-signal score threshold AND bull score > bear score AND the prior chart bar did not meet this same strong-bull condition) OR (bull score >= 3 AND trend score > prior chart bar's trend score AND prior chart bar did not meet the strong-bull condition AND bull score, bear score do not meet the strong-bull condition)
Short: (bear score >= strong-signal score threshold AND bear score > bull score AND the prior chart bar did not meet this same strong-bear condition) OR (bear score >= 3 AND trend score < prior chart bar's trend score AND prior chart bar did not meet the strong-bear condition AND bear score, bull score do not meet the strong-bear condition)
