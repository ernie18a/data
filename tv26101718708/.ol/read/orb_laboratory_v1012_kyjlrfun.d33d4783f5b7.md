Calculation: Breakout buffer = selected buffer amount in ticks × minimum tick size, opening range range × selected percentage, or ATR × selected multiplier; ATR uses chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two chart-timeframe bars earlier, with gap size ≥ ATR × selected minimum; bearish FVG = current high < low two chart-timeframe bars earlier, with gap size ≥ ATR × selected minimum

Calculation: Short stop = selected model: current bar high plus buffer; reference price plus ATR × multiplier; opening range midpoint; opening range high plus buffer; breakout extreme plus buffer; reference price plus opening range range × selected percentage; or FVG top plus buffer
Calculation: Long target = selected model: entry price + original risk × selected multiple; opening range high + opening range range × selected multiple; entry price + ATR × selected multiple; opening range midpoint; or opening range high

Calculation: Fade target = selected model: opening range midpoint; opposite opening range boundary; or entry price ± original risk × selected multiple (plus for long, minus for short)
