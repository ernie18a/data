Calculation: Session range high/low = the highest high and lowest low of a session, updated as its source bars form; compare the current London session with the last completed Asia session, or the current New York session with the last completed London session.
Calculation: Cycle range high/low = the highest high and lowest low within an aligned intraday cycle; compare the current cycle with the nearest earlier completed cycle of the same duration on the same day whose corresponding liquidity level has not been swept by an intervening completed cycle. Update from source bars as the current cycle forms.
Long: For any session or cycle comparison, the primary market or an enabled comparison market makes a lower low than its reference range low by a configurable tick buffer AND at least one other compared market does not; do not signal if both bullish and bearish divergence occur in the same comparison.
Short: For any session or cycle comparison, the primary market or an enabled comparison market makes a higher high than its reference range high by a configurable tick buffer AND at least one other compared market does not; do not signal if both bullish and bearish divergence occur in the same comparison.
