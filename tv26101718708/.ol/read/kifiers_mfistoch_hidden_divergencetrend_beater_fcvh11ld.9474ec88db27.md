Calculation: Normalized ATR regime = EMA of ATR divided by close with a shorter lookback compared with EMA of ATR divided by close with a longer lookback
Calculation: Bullish MFI divergence = a higher price pivot low AND a higher MFI pivot low than their respective preceding pivot lows, detected on the same chart bar
Calculation: Entry support = the latest tracked pivot low at entry
Long: VWMA crosses above SMA OR (Stochastic is at least 50 AND MFI crosses above 50 AND VWMA is above SMA AND close is at least VWMA AND OBV is above its EMA AND normalized ATR regime is not ranging) OR (bullish MFI divergence AND VWMA is above SMA AND close is at least VWMA AND Stochastic is at least 50 AND normalized ATR regime is ranging, within the configured divergence validity period, and position is flat)
Short: VWMA crosses below SMA
Exit: long, high reaches entry price multiplied by one plus the configured take profit percentage OR close is at or below entry support
