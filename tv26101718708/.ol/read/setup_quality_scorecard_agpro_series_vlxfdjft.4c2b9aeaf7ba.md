Calculation: Bullish bias = bullish votes exceed bearish votes; each side gets one vote for its EMA stack, higher-timeframe EMA alignment, fast EMA slope over a longer lookback, close relative to the fast EMA, and candle direction. Use short-, medium-, and long-term EMAs; higher-timeframe alignment uses the higher timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Active pivot = most recent confirmed pivot high or low within the context lookback; confirm pivots using equal left and right lookbacks. For bullish bias, use the pivot low as the reference, falling back to the fast EMA; for bearish bias, use the pivot high, falling back to the fast EMA.
Calculation: Readiness score = 24% location score + 22% trend-support score + 22% momentum score + 16% risk-fit score + 16% target-room score. Location score decreases with ATR-normalized distance from the active pivot reference and is capped when price is beyond that reference on the invalid side. Trend-support score combines EMA-stack support, higher-timeframe EMA support, and fast EMA slope. Momentum score combines directional RSI level, RSI change over a short lookback, candle body-to-range quality, and relative volume versus a longer-lookback average. Risk-fit score peaks when ATR-normalized distance to the invalidation edge is near its preferred distance and is zero when invalidated. Target-room score increases with ATR-normalized distance to the nearer valid pivot target, or a risk-distance-based projected target when no valid pivot target exists.
Calculation: Checklist count = number of true conditions among valid bias with trend support above its minimum, location score above its minimum, momentum score above its minimum, risk-fit score above its minimum, and target-room score above its minimum with room above its minimum.
Calculation: Invalidation edge = for bullish bias, active pivot low minus an ATR buffer, or low minus an ATR-based fallback risk distance if no active pivot low; for bearish bias, active pivot high plus an ATR buffer, or high plus the fallback risk distance if no active pivot high.
Calculation: Target = for bullish bias, active pivot high if above close, otherwise close plus risk distance times a target multiple; for bearish bias, active pivot low if below close, otherwise close minus risk distance times a target multiple.
Long: bullish votes exceed bearish votes AND close is above the invalidation edge AND ATR-normalized risk distance is no greater than its maximum AND target room is at least its minimum AND readiness score is at least the READY threshold AND checklist count is at least four; the READY state has just begun OR the MONITOR state has just begun.
Long: bullish votes exceed bearish votes AND close is above the invalidation edge AND checklist count is at least three AND (state is READY or MONITOR OR readiness score is within a small margin below the MONITOR threshold) AND the context-label spacing interval has elapsed.
Short: bearish votes exceed bullish votes AND close is below the invalidation edge AND ATR-normalized risk distance is no greater than its maximum AND target room is at least its minimum AND readiness score is at least the READY threshold AND checklist count is at least four; the READY state has just begun OR the MONITOR state has just begun.
Short: bearish votes exceed bullish votes AND close is below the invalidation edge AND checklist count is at least three AND (state is READY or MONITOR OR readiness score is within a small margin below the MONITOR threshold) AND the context-label spacing interval has elapsed.
Exit: long, close <= invalidation edge OR (a prior READY or MONITOR state with prior readiness score at least the MONITOR threshold AND high >= target).
Exit: short, close >= invalidation edge OR (a prior READY or MONITOR state with prior readiness score at least the MONITOR threshold AND low <= target).
