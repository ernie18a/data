Calculation: Sushi bullish/bearish event = close crosses above the most recent validated swing high / below the most recent validated swing low, respectively; a swing is validated when the highest high and lowest low over the short swing window equal those over twice that window, with either extreme at the window’s oldest bar, and the same validation has not occurred during the intervening short window.
Calculation: Trendline bullish/bearish event = after a confirmed pivot high/low, close crosses above the descending / below the ascending projected pivot trendline; its slope is based on ATR, standard deviation, or linear regression as selected.
Calculation: Sushi bullish/bearish sequence = a Sushi event and a trendline event in either order, with the second occurring within the short sequence window; bullish uses the two bullish events, bearish uses the two bearish events.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = low falls below the preceding lookback’s lowest low and closes back above it / high rises above the preceding lookback’s highest high and closes back below it, respectively.
Calculation: Mean-deviation reversal = close crosses below the Gaussian-weighted close average minus the average absolute deviation from that average times a multiplier / crosses above the average plus that deviation band, respectively.
Calculation: Trailing-stop direction change = close crosses above / below the prior value of a trailing stop offset by ATR times a multiplier, respectively.
Calculation: Reversal event = mean-deviation reversal occurs while trailing-stop direction agrees, or trailing-stop direction changes to a direction matching the most recent mean-deviation reversal; when liquidity confirmation is enabled, a same-direction liquidity sweep must have occurred within the recent-sweep window. For the local asset only, the ADX filter must also pass when enabled.
Calculation: Pump event = local volume exceeds a threshold, or (for TOTAL and BTC dominance) true range exceeds its moving average.
Calculation: Pump-phase bullish/bearish event = after a pump event, a bearish/bullish reversal event occurs first, or after that event a bullish/bearish reversal event occurs; the phase alternates between these two events and resets after the second.
Calculation: Engine bullish/bearish event = a Pump-phase bullish/bearish event and a Sushi bullish/bearish sequence occur in either order, with the second within the engine sequence window.
Calculation: Macro bullish/bearish active = the corresponding BTC dominance / TOTAL bearish / bullish engine event occurred within the macro lookback window, respectively.
Calculation: Local long / short trigger = local bearish / bullish engine event, respectively, subject to enabled anomaly filters. The long trigger is blocked if the recent RSI high exceeds its upper boundary, MACD histogram is positive and rising, or a recent bullish candle body exceeds ATR times a multiplier. The short trigger is blocked if the recent RSI low is below its lower boundary, MACD histogram is negative and falling, or a recent bearish candle body exceeds ATR times a multiplier.
Long: position side is not long AND (local long trigger OR (TOTAL macro bullish active AND BTC dominance macro bullish active AND alignment first became active this bar AND NOT local long trigger))
Short: position side is not short AND (local short trigger OR (TOTAL macro bearish active AND BTC dominance macro bearish active AND alignment first became active this bar AND NOT local short trigger))
Exit: long, short trigger OR (TOTAL macro bearish active AND BTC dominance macro bearish active AND alignment first became active this bar AND NOT local short trigger)
Exit: short, long trigger OR (TOTAL macro bullish active AND BTC dominance macro bullish active AND alignment first became active this bar AND NOT local long trigger)
