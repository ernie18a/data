Calculation: Wave = the smoothed stochastic of a zero-lag EMA of close, using the bar lows and highs for its range.
Calculation: Signal line = a moving average of Wave.
Calculation: Money Flow = the EMA of volume × (close − open) / (high − low), divided by the EMA of volume and scaled by 100.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by the previous bar’s moving average volume.
Long: Wave crosses above the oversold level AND Money Flow > 0 AND close > open
Long: Wave crosses above 50 AND Money Flow > 0 AND Relative volume ≥ its threshold
Long: At a confirmed pivot low, price makes a lower low than at the previous pivot low AND Wave makes a higher low AND Wave < 50 AND the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum interval
Short: Wave crosses below the overbought level AND Money Flow ≤ 0 AND close < open
Short: Wave crosses below 50 AND Money Flow ≤ 0 AND Relative volume ≥ its threshold
Short: At a confirmed pivot high, price makes a higher high than at the previous pivot high AND Wave makes a lower high AND Wave > 50 AND the pivots are no farther apart than the maximum interval
