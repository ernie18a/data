Calculation: Buy volume = sum of lower-timeframe bar volume within the current chart bar when lower-timeframe close > open, plus half the volume when close = open; lower timeframe is below the chart timeframe and uses bars within the current chart bar, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Sell volume = sum of lower-timeframe bar volume within the current chart bar when lower-timeframe close < open, plus half the volume when close = open; lower timeframe is below the chart timeframe and uses bars within the current chart bar, including the bar still forming
Calculation: Total volume = buy volume + sell volume
Long: (total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × absorption volume threshold AND sell volume > buy volume AND close − low > high − close AND close >= open) OR (total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × imbalance volume threshold AND close > open AND buy volume / (sell volume if nonzero, otherwise 1) >= imbalance ratio threshold) OR (close = high AND buy volume > 0 AND sell volume > 0 AND total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × missed-auction volume threshold)
Short: (total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × absorption volume threshold AND buy volume > sell volume AND high − close > close − low AND close <= open) OR (total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × imbalance volume threshold AND close < open AND sell volume / (buy volume if nonzero, otherwise 1) >= imbalance ratio threshold) OR (close = low AND sell volume > 0 AND buy volume > 0 AND total volume > its 20-chart-bar simple moving average × missed-auction volume threshold)
