Calculation: HiLo Activator = upper threshold is the simple moving average of highs over its lookback and lower threshold is the simple moving average of lows over the same lookback; direction is bullish when close is above the upper threshold, bearish when close is below the lower threshold, and neutral otherwise; its stop is the lowest low over the lookback when bullish, the highest high when bearish, and otherwise the previous stop
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = ATR multiplied by a configurable factor is the stop distance; if current and previous closes are above the previous stop, the stop is the greater of the previous stop and current close minus the distance; if both are below it, the stop is the lesser of the previous stop and current close plus the distance; otherwise set it to current close minus the distance when current close is above the previous stop, or current close plus the distance when it is not
Long: At least one entry indicator is enabled AND every enabled entry indicator is bullish: moving averages use either short-period average crossing above or staying above the long-period average; HiLo Activator uses either direction changing from bearish to bullish or its stop below the bar low; MACD uses either MACD line crossing above or staying above its signal line, subject to the optional filter requiring MACD line at or above its threshold; ADX uses either positive directional indicator crossing above or staying above the negative directional indicator, with ADX within its configured minimum and maximum and optionally rising or falling as configured; Stochastic uses either %K crossing above or staying above %D, with %K at or below its configured maximum threshold; VWAP uses either close crossing above VWAP or staying above it, or the inverse configured direction; RSI uses either crossing above its oversold threshold or staying below it; Bollinger Bands use either source crossing above the lower band or staying below it; ATR trailing stop and Parabolic SAR each use either close crossing above the indicator or staying above it. Require the configured long-enabled trading mode, permitted entry date/session, confirmed bar, and—if same-direction re-entry prevention is enabled—not all enabled entry indicators were bullish on the previous bar
Short: At least one entry indicator is enabled AND every enabled entry indicator is bearish: moving averages use either short-period average crossing below or staying below the long-period average; HiLo Activator uses either direction changing from bullish to bearish or its stop at or above the bar low; MACD uses either MACD line crossing below or staying below its signal line, subject to the optional filter requiring MACD line at or below its threshold; ADX uses either negative directional indicator crossing above or staying above the positive directional indicator, with ADX within its configured minimum and maximum and optionally rising or falling as configured; Stochastic uses either %K crossing below or staying below %D, with %K at or above its configured minimum threshold; VWAP uses either close crossing below VWAP or staying below it, or the inverse configured direction; RSI uses either crossing below its overbought threshold or staying above it; Bollinger Bands use either source crossing below the upper band or staying above it; ATR trailing stop and Parabolic SAR each use either close crossing below the indicator or staying below it. Require the configured short-enabled trading mode, permitted entry date/session, confirmed bar, and—if same-direction re-entry prevention is enabled—not all enabled entry indicators were bearish on the previous bar
Exit: Long position, during the permitted exit date/session, at least one enabled exit indicator is bearish (using its configured entry-indicator bearish rule) or all enabled exit indicators are bearish if the require-all-exits option is enabled; OR close is at or below the fixed stop price, at or above the fixed take-profit price, or at/below the active trailing stop; OR the daily profit reaches its configured gain or loss limit, or the exit session ends
Exit: Short position, during the permitted exit date/session, at least one enabled exit indicator is bullish (using its configured entry-indicator bullish rule) or all enabled exit indicators are bullish if the require-all-exits option is enabled; OR close is at or above the fixed stop price, at or below the fixed take-profit price, or at/above the active trailing stop; OR the daily profit reaches its configured gain or loss limit, or the exit session ends
Exit: Long position, price reaches the configured partial-profit distance above entry price; if configured, move the stop to entry price after this partial exit
Exit: Short position, price reaches the configured partial-profit distance below entry price; if configured, move the stop to entry price after this partial exit
