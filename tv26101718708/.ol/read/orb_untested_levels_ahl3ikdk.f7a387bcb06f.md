Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the configured opening-range window
Calculation: Opening-range midpoint = average of the opening-range high and low
Calculation: Untested session high/low = the high/low of a completed session that has not subsequently been touched by a bar whose high is at or above it and low is at or below it; a level touched on the current bar remains eligible for that bar
Long: During the configured trading session, with no long signal already taken that day, the previous bar swept an untested session low (low at or below the level, open and close above it, previous close above it) OR swept the running low of the active London session after the configured minimum elapsed time using the same comparisons; AND the current bar closes above its open; AND the current bar does not overlap the opening range
Short: During the configured trading session, with no short signal already taken that day, the previous bar swept an untested session high (high at or above the level, open and close below it, previous close below it) OR swept the running high of the active London session after the configured minimum elapsed time using the same comparisons; AND the current bar closes below its open; AND the current bar does not overlap the opening range
Long: During the configured ORB trade window, with the opening-range size below its configured maximum and no ORB signal already taken that day: a bar closes above the opening-range high and above its open; a later bar has low at or below the midpoint and closes above the midpoint but below its open; the next bar closes above the midpoint and above its open
Short: During the configured ORB trade window, with the opening-range size below its configured maximum and no ORB signal already taken that day: a bar closes below the opening-range low and below its open; a later bar has high at or above the midpoint and closes below the midpoint but above its open; the next bar closes below the midpoint and below its open
