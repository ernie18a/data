Calculation: Composite baseline = arithmetic mean of the selected moving averages of close using ascending Fibonacci lookbacks and the selected moving average type
Calculation: Oscillator = (close² − composite baseline²) / composite baseline²
Calculation: Fast signal line = short-lookback simple moving average of the oscillator
Calculation: Slow signal line = longer-lookback simple moving average of the oscillator
Long: Fast signal line > slow signal line
Short: Fast signal line < slow signal line
Long: At a confirmed oscillator pivot low, oscillator value > previous oscillator pivot-low value AND corresponding price low < previous corresponding price low OR oscillator value < previous oscillator pivot-low value AND corresponding price low > previous corresponding price low
Short: At a confirmed oscillator pivot high, oscillator value < previous oscillator pivot-high value AND corresponding price high > previous corresponding price high OR oscillator value > previous oscillator pivot-high value AND corresponding price high < previous corresponding price high
