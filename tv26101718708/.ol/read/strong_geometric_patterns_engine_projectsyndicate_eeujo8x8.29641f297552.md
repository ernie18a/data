Calculation: ADR = mean of high − low over the last 10 completed daily bars; use the chart bar’s value from the last completed daily bar
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a chart-timeframe high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same parameterized number of bars on both sides; record it only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: Pattern candidates = use the latest three confirmed pivot highs (H1,H2,H3) and lows (L1,L2,L3), in chronological order; require at least three of each and a span from the earliest first pivot to the latest third pivot within the parameterized minimum and maximum. Let tol = ADR × equal-level tolerance, stol = ADR × swing-slope threshold, top = max(H1,H2,H3), bottom = min(L1,L2,L3), oldRange = H1−L1, newRange = H3−L3, and converging = oldRange > 0 AND newRange ≤ oldRange × convergence fraction. A bearish head-and-shoulders candidate exists when H2 > H1+tol AND H2 > H3+tol AND abs(H1−H3) ≤ 1.6×tol; its trigger is (L2+L3)/2 and invalidation is H3. A bullish inverse head-and-shoulders candidate exists when L2 < L1−tol AND L2 < L3−tol AND abs(L1−L3) ≤ 1.6×tol; its trigger is (H2+H3)/2 and invalidation is L3. A bearish double-top candidate exists when abs(H2−H3) ≤ tol AND H2 > L3+tol AND ( (H2+H3)/2−L3 ) ≥ tol×minimum-depth multiple; its trigger is L3 and invalidation is max(H2,H3). A bullish double-bottom candidate exists when abs(L2−L3) ≤ tol AND H3 > L2+tol AND ( H3−(L2+L3)/2 ) ≥ tol×minimum-depth multiple; its trigger is H3 and invalidation is min(L2,L3). Classify highs as flat when abs(H3−H1) ≤ stol, rising when H3 > H1+stol, or falling when H3 < H1−stol; classify lows the same way using L3 and L1. A bullish ascending-triangle candidate has flat highs and rising lows; trigger top, invalidation L3. A bearish descending-triangle candidate has flat lows and falling highs; trigger bottom, invalidation H3. A converging symmetrical-triangle candidate has falling highs and rising lows; its direction is bullish when H1+L1 < H3+L3, otherwise bearish; trigger top if bullish or bottom if bearish, and invalidation bottom if bullish or top if bearish. A bearish rising-wedge candidate has rising highs and rising lows and is converging; trigger L3, invalidation H3. A bullish falling-wedge candidate has falling highs and falling lows and is converging; trigger H3, invalidation L3. For triangles and wedges, measured height is top−bottom. For head-and-shoulders patterns, height is head minus trigger for bearish and trigger minus head for bullish. For double patterns, height is the distance between the average equal-level pivots and the intervening pivot. If multiple candidates qualify, select in this priority order: head-and-shoulders/inverse, double top/bottom, ascending/descending triangle, symmetrical triangle, rising/falling wedge; within a priority, choose the highest score. Candidate score for head-and-shoulders and double patterns = round to one decimal of 10×(0.65×max(0,1−pivot-level difference/tol)+0.35×min(1,height/(ADR×0.6))). For triangles and wedges = round to one decimal of 10×(0.55×convergence score+0.45×min(1,height/(ADR×0.7))), where convergence score is 1 if converging, otherwise 0.4.
Calculation: Pending setup = selected candidate recorded on a bar close that confirms a new pivot, only when flat, no setup is pending, and the minimum signal spacing has elapsed. It stores direction, trigger, invalidation, height, and score; expires after the parameterized pending duration. Cancel a bullish setup if low < invalidation, or a bearish setup if high > invalidation, or when it expires. Permit at most one active trade or pending setup; require the minimum signal spacing between entries.
Calculation: Entry levels = entry is the closing price on the breakout bar. Clamp candidate height to at least ADR×measured-move floor and at most ADR×measured-move cap. For bullish entries, stop = invalidation−ADR×stop buffer, but if stop ≥ entry use entry−2×ADR×stop buffer; targets are entry plus clamped height times the shorter and longer target multiples. For bearish entries, stop = invalidation+ADR×stop buffer, but if stop ≤ entry use entry+2×ADR×stop buffer; targets are entry minus clamped height times the shorter and longer target multiples.
Long: A bullish pending setup exists AND close > its trigger AND the minimum signal spacing has elapsed AND no trade is active
Short: A bearish pending setup exists AND close < its trigger AND the minimum signal spacing has elapsed AND no trade is active
Exit: Long, high ≥ longer target OR high ≥ shorter target OR low ≤ stop
Exit: Short, low ≤ longer target OR low ≤ shorter target OR high ≥ stop
