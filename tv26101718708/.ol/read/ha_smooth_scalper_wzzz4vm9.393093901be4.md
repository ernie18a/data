Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi OHLC = EMA-smooth each chart-bar OHLC value, then calculate Heikin-Ashi close as their average; initialize Heikin-Ashi open as the average of smoothed open and close, then recursively average prior Heikin-Ashi open and close; set high/low to the maximum/minimum of smoothed high/low, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close; EMA-smooth each resulting Heikin-Ashi OHLC series again.
Calculation: Smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction = bullish when smoothed Heikin-Ashi close >= smoothed Heikin-Ashi open; otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Active direction = smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction when structure locking is off. When on, initialize from that direction; while bullish, protect each newly confirmed pivot low, and while bearish, protect each newly confirmed pivot high. Detect pivots from either chart highs/lows or smoothed Heikin-Ashi highs/lows, using equal configurable left and right pivot strengths; a pivot is confirmed after the right-side bars complete. Flip bullish structure to bearish when close is below the protected low minus the configurable ATR buffer, or when low is below it in wick mode. Flip bearish structure to bullish when close is above the protected high plus the buffer, or when high is above it in wick mode. On a flip, protect the latest confirmed swing high or low for the new direction.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi direction = smoothed Heikin-Ashi direction calculated on each enabled higher timeframe, using either its last completed bar or its still-forming bar according to the confirmation setting.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA confirmation = bullish when higher-timeframe close > its EMA and the EMA >= its prior value; bearish when close < its EMA and the EMA <= its prior value; otherwise neutral. Use either the last completed or still-forming higher-timeframe bar according to the confirmation setting. The configured confirmation method may instead use higher-timeframe Heikin-Ashi direction.
Calculation: Timeframe agreement = count enabled higher-timeframe directions matching the proposed side; required agreement is the lesser of the configured minimum and enabled timeframe count. If no timeframes are enabled, agreement passes.
Long: Active direction = bullish AND prior active direction = bearish AND (reversal mode OR slow EMA of smoothed Heikin-Ashi close > its prior value) AND (impulse filter disabled OR current absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi body > prior body) AND (no enabled higher timeframes OR bullish timeframe agreement >= required agreement) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR confirmation is bullish).
Short: Active direction = bearish AND prior active direction = bullish AND (reversal mode OR slow EMA of smoothed Heikin-Ashi close < its prior value) AND (impulse filter disabled OR current absolute smoothed Heikin-Ashi body > prior body) AND (no enabled higher timeframes OR bearish timeframe agreement >= required agreement) AND (higher-timeframe confirmation disabled OR confirmation is bearish).
