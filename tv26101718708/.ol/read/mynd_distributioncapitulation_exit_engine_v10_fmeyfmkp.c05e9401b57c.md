Calculation: Distribution score = near-high factor × weighted average of (distribution-day score, down-volume-ratio score, momentum-rollover score); distribution-day score = min(100, distribution-day count / critical count × 100), where a distribution day is a close decline meeting the minimum-decline threshold with volume at least the prior bar's volume times the volume multiplier; down-volume-ratio score = max(0, (down-volume percentage − 50) × 2), where down-volume percentage is volume on bars closing below the prior close divided by total volume; momentum-rollover score = min(100, (RSI at the rollover lookback − current RSI) × 10) when the earlier RSI is at least 55 and current RSI is lower, otherwise 0; near-high factor is 1 when close is within the near-high percentage of the highest high over the near-high lookback, otherwise 0.5.
Calculation: Capitulation score = weighted average of volume-climax score, rejection-candle score, and momentum-exhaustion score; volume-climax score = clamp((volume z-score / z-score threshold) × 100, 0, 100); rejection-candle score = average of min(100, (bar range / ATR / minimum range-to-ATR ratio) × 100) and min(100, (close position within bar range / minimum close-position ratio) × 100); momentum-exhaustion score = min(100, max(0, oversold level − min(current RSI, RSI at uptick lookback)) × 5 + max(0, current RSI − RSI at uptick lookback) × 5) when current RSI exceeds RSI at the uptick lookback, otherwise 0.
Exit: Long, distribution score crosses above the long warning threshold AND remains below the long exit threshold OR distribution score crosses above the long exit threshold
Exit: Short, capitulation score crosses above the short warning threshold AND remains below the short exit threshold OR capitulation score crosses above the short exit threshold
