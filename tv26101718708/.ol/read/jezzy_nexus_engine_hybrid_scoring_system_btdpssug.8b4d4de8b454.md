Calculation: Bull/bear score = count of 7 conditions: MACD histogram and MACD line both above/below the signal line; at least 2 of 3 slow, moderate, and fast Parabolic SAR values below/above close; all of the last N closes above/below the fast EMA and fast EMA above/below slow EMA; fast EMA above/below slow EMA on the higher timeframe when the chart timeframe is at or below it (use the unoffset higher-timeframe value, including its forming bar in realtime); directional CISD + FVG; touch of a directionally flipped FVG within the last five bars; and close above the regression of lows with that regression rising / close below the regression of highs with that regression falling.
Calculation: Bullish CISD + FVG = close above the lowest low of the preceding swing lookback and current low above the high two bars earlier by at least the minimum ATR-scaled gap. Bearish CISD + FVG = close below the highest high of the preceding swing lookback and current high below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum ATR-scaled gap.
Calculation: Bullish flipped-FVG touch = a qualifying bearish FVG (formed with bearish CISD) was flipped when close rose above its midpoint, remains valid until close rises above its top, and any of the last five bars overlaps its zone. Bearish flipped-FVG touch = a qualifying bullish FVG (formed with bullish CISD) was flipped when close fell below its midpoint, remains valid until close falls below its bottom, and any of the last five bars overlaps its zone.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish wick rejection = lower/upper wick at least the configured multiple of candle body, body at least the configured ATR fraction, and wick at least the configured ATR fraction.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish engulfing candle = current candle closes up/down, previous candle closes down/up, current candle body engulfs the previous candle body, and current body is at least the configured ATR multiple.
Long: (bullish wick rejection OR bullish CISD + FVG) AND bullish score >= minimum score AND ADX >= minimum AND RSI < overbought threshold AND volume >= volume moving average × multiplier AND no bearish engulfing candle in the recent lookback AND (daily chart OR UTC time in an enabled session) AND bullish-signal cooldown satisfied; OR fast EMA > slow EMA AND previous bar low <= previous fast EMA AND previous bar close > previous fast EMA AND current close > current open AND (daily chart OR UTC time in an enabled session).
Short: (bearish wick rejection OR bearish CISD + FVG) AND bearish score >= minimum score AND ADX >= minimum AND RSI > oversold threshold AND volume >= volume moving average × multiplier AND no bullish engulfing candle in the recent lookback AND (daily chart OR UTC time in an enabled session) AND bearish-signal cooldown satisfied; OR fast EMA < slow EMA AND previous bar high >= previous fast EMA AND previous bar close < previous fast EMA AND current close < current open AND (daily chart OR UTC time in an enabled session).
