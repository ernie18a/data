Calculation: Hull moving average = weighted moving average of 2 × half-length weighted moving average of close − full-length weighted moving average of close, smoothed with a weighted moving average of rounded square root of the length; the fast length is shorter than the slow length
Calculation: Bollinger upper/lower bands = selected moving average of close ± selected multiplier × standard deviation of close over the band lookback
Long: Fast Hull moving average crosses above slow Hull moving average OR open < short-period EMA AND open < longer-period EMA AND close > short-period EMA AND close > longer-period EMA, followed on a subsequent bar by a close of a lower-timeframe bar > setup-bar high before any such close < setup-bar low OR open < Bollinger upper band AND close > Bollinger upper band, followed within the configured wait limit by a bar whose low is within the configured percentage tolerance of the EMA and whose index is after the setup bar, followed by a lower-timeframe close > retest-bar high (including on the retest bar if its close > setup-bar high); cancel if close < EMA before retest or a lower-timeframe close < the low of a bar that closed below EMA before confirmation
Short: Fast Hull moving average crosses below slow Hull moving average OR open > short-period EMA AND open > longer-period EMA AND close < short-period EMA AND close < longer-period EMA, followed on a subsequent bar by a close of a lower-timeframe bar < setup-bar low before any such close > setup-bar high OR open > Bollinger lower band AND close < Bollinger lower band, followed within the configured wait limit by a bar whose high is within the configured percentage tolerance of the EMA, followed by a lower-timeframe close < retest-bar low; cancel if close < setup-bar low, the wait limit expires, or a lower-timeframe close > the high of a bar that closed above EMA before confirmation
