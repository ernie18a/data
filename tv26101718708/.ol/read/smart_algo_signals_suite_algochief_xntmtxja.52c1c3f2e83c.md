Calculation: Swing direction = maintain the prior direction unless close > adaptive baseline + ATR × threshold (set direction long) or close < adaptive baseline − ATR × threshold (set direction short); adaptive baseline uses an efficiency-ratio-smoothed update of close, and threshold is ATR divided by sensitivity.
Calculation: Scalp direction = long when the shorter-lookback EMA of close > the longer-lookback EMA and RSI > 50; short when the shorter-lookback EMA < the longer-lookback EMA and RSI < 50; otherwise retain prior direction.
Calculation: Active engine direction = swing direction or scalp direction, according to the selected engine mode.
Calculation: Fair value trail = VWMA of hlc3 with ATR-width bands; in long mode, trail the lower band upward as the maximum of its prior value and VWMA − band width, with upper band = lower band + band width; switch to short mode when close < lower band. In short mode, trail the upper band downward as the minimum of its prior value and VWMA + band width, with lower band = upper band − band width; switch to long mode when close > upper band.
Long: (Trend mode AND active engine direction = long AND prior active engine direction ≠ long) OR (Reversal mode AND ((low ≤ 20-bar SMA of close − 2.1 × 20-bar standard deviation of close OR 9-bar RSI < 30) AND close > open AND close > prior high) AND the bullish reversal condition was false on the prior bar) OR (fair value trail direction = long AND low ≤ upper trail band AND low ≥ lower trail band − 0.2 × band width AND close > open AND no raw bullish signal on this bar AND more than 4 bars since the prior long retest signal).
Short: (Trend mode AND active engine direction = short AND prior active engine direction ≠ short) OR (Reversal mode AND ((high ≥ 20-bar SMA of close + 2.1 × 20-bar standard deviation of close OR 9-bar RSI > 70) AND close < open AND close < prior low) AND the bearish reversal condition was false on the prior bar) OR (fair value trail direction = short AND high ≥ lower trail band AND high ≤ upper trail band + 0.2 × band width AND close < open AND no raw bearish signal on this bar AND more than 4 bars since the prior short retest signal).
Exit: Long, last raw entry direction is long AND (14-bar RSI crosses below 68 OR (high = highest high over 14 bars AND close < open AND high − close > 1.5 × (close − low))) OR (low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ final enabled target price).
Exit: Short, last raw entry direction is short AND (14-bar RSI crosses above 32 OR (low = lowest low over 14 bars AND close > open AND close − low > 1.5 × (high − close))) OR (high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ final enabled target price).
