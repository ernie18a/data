Calculation: ATR bands = chart-timeframe SMA ± long-lookback chart-timeframe ATR multiplied by three ascending multipliers; the first and second bands use the lower multipliers, the third the highest.
Calculation: MACD histogram = the difference between a shorter-lookback and a longer-lookback chart-timeframe EMA of close, minus a signal EMA of that difference with a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Heikin-Ashi open and close = open and close of Heikin-Ashi bars on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Support/resistance position = (chart close − support) / (resistance − support), using high and low from the configured higher timeframe, aligned to the chart bar and offset back by the configured timeframe value in chart bars; the higher-timeframe bar may still be forming.
Long: SMA is above the 200-period EMA AND prior chart bar’s Heikin-Ashi open is below its close AND current SMA is above prior SMA AND close is below the first upper ATR band AND MACD histogram is above zero AND (support/resistance filter is disabled OR support/resistance position is above 100)
Short: SMA is below the 200-period EMA AND prior chart bar’s Heikin-Ashi open is above its close AND current SMA is below prior SMA AND close is above the first lower ATR band AND MACD histogram is below zero AND (support/resistance filter is disabled OR support/resistance position is below zero)
Exit: long, close is above the third upper ATR band OR close is below the second lower ATR band OR (take-profit is enabled AND high reaches entry price plus the configured percentage of the entry-signal bar’s close)
Exit: short, close is below the third lower ATR band OR close is above the second upper ATR band OR (take-profit is enabled AND low reaches entry price minus the configured percentage of the entry-signal bar’s close)
