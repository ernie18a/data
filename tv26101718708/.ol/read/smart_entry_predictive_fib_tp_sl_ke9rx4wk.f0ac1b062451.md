Calculation: Swing range = highest high minus lowest low over a lookback; use long-, medium-, and short-term lookbacks, with long > medium > short.
Calculation: Long zone = swing high minus 0.618 to 0.786 of the swing range; short zone = swing high minus 0.236 to 0.382 of the swing range. For each direction, use the intersection of all three zones if nonempty; otherwise the first nonempty pair in long-medium, long-short, medium-short order; otherwise use the long-term zone.
Calculation: Bias score = clamp to [-90, 90] the sum of: -(RSI - 50) / 50 * 30; +20 if short-term EMA > long-term EMA, else -20; +15 if close > SMA, else -15; +5 if short-term EMA is higher than 3 bars ago, else -5; +5 if RSI is higher than 3 bars ago, else -5; (0.5 - (close - long-term swing low) / long-term swing range) * 20; and +15 if volume > its moving average and close >= open, -15 if volume > its moving average and close < open, otherwise 0. Long percentage = clamp(round(50 + bias score / 2), 5, 95); short percentage = clamp(round(50 - bias score / 2), 5, 95).
Calculation: Entry price = volume point of control if it lies within the corresponding zone, otherwise the zone's lower boundary for long or upper boundary for short. Volume point of control = midpoint of the highest-volume of 20 equal price bins spanning the long-term swing range; bin volume is the sum of volume for bars whose high-low range overlaps the bin.
Calculation: Risk-reward ratio = round(abs(target - entry price) / abs(entry price - stop price), 1). For long, target is the long-term 0.236 retracement and stop price is entry price * (1 - stop buffer). For short, target is the long-term 0.786 retracement and stop price is entry price * (1 + stop buffer).
Long: close is within the long zone AND long percentage >= 60 AND RSI <= 50 AND short-term EMA >= long-term EMA AND long risk-reward ratio >= minimum risk-reward ratio.
Short: close is within the short zone AND short percentage >= 60 AND RSI >= 50 AND short-term EMA <= long-term EMA AND short risk-reward ratio >= minimum risk-reward ratio.
