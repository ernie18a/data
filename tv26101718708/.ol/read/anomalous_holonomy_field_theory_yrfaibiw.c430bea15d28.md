Calculation: Signal strength = SCORE AMPLIFIER / 10 times a score initialized to zero: if close is above both the 20-period EMA and the EMA of close on the higher timeframe (last completed bar), add 1.5 if close is also above the 50-period EMA, otherwise add 1; add 10-bar rate of change divided by 10 when positive; add 0.5 when volume exceeds 1.2 times its 20-bar average. If close is below both EMAs, subtract 1.5 if also below the 50-period EMA, otherwise subtract 1; add 10-bar rate of change divided by 10 when negative; subtract 0.5 when volume exceeds 1.2 times its 20-bar average. Add VWAP distance as a percentage of close, capped to the range -2 to 2. If the score is positive, add 1.5 when RSI(14) < 30 or 0.5 when RSI(14) < 40; if negative, subtract 1.5 when RSI(14) > 70 or 0.5 when RSI(14) > 60. Add 0.5 when MACD(12,26,9) line is above its signal line and the score is positive; subtract 0.5 when it is below and the score is negative.
Calculation: Signal threshold = signal sensitivity times execution-mode multiplier, adjusted by volatility regime: multiply by 0.7 when ATR(14)/close is below its 30th percentile over a 100-bar lookback, by 1.3 when above its 70th percentile, otherwise leave unchanged. Execution-mode multiplier is mode-dependent.
Calculation: Long stop = the greater of close minus 1.5 times ATR(14) times the mode stop multiplier, and the lesser of the 5-bar low and prior-bar typical price minus 0.382 times the 10-bar high-low range.
Calculation: Short stop = the lesser of close plus 2 times ATR(14) times the mode stop multiplier, and the greater of the 5-bar high and prior-bar typical price plus 0.382 times the 10-bar high-low range.
Long: Signal strength >= signal threshold AND can trade AND position is flat.
Long: Signal strength >= 1.2 times signal threshold AND can trade AND position is flat; in conservative mode with signal confirmation enabled, close > 20-period EMA AND RSI(14) > 30 AND RSI(14) < 70.
Short: Signal strength <= -signal threshold AND can trade AND position is flat.
Short: Signal strength <= -1.2 times signal threshold AND can trade AND position is flat; in conservative mode with signal confirmation enabled, close < 20-period EMA AND RSI(14) > 30 AND RSI(14) < 70.
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= take-profit price OR trailing stop is hit OR bars in trade exceed the mode-dependent maximum. Take-profit price is close plus the entry-time distance from close to long stop multiplied by the mode target multiplier; trailing stop activates and offsets at mode-dependent multiples of ATR(14).
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= take-profit price OR trailing stop is hit OR bars in trade exceed the mode-dependent maximum. Take-profit price is close minus the entry-time distance from short stop to close multiplied by the mode target multiplier; trailing stop activates and offsets at mode-dependent multiples of ATR(14).
