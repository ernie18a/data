Calculation: Moving average = selected EMA, SMA, WMA, RMA, or HMA of close; the signal uses one selected moving average, and its slope compares the current value with its value at a longer bar lookback.
Calculation: Delta ratio = 100 × (sum of lower-timeframe up-candle volume minus down-candle volume) / chart-bar volume; use lower-timeframe candles within the chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low minus the high two chart bars earlier; bearish FVG = the low two chart bars earlier minus current high. A gap is active when its size meets the configured minimum; it ends when a chart-bar close passes through it.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe FVG = the same three-bar gap calculation on completed higher-timeframe bars; it ends when a chart-bar close passes through it.
Calculation: Bullish absorption = chart-bar volume exceeds its volume SMA times a configured multiplier, delta ratio is at or below a configured negative threshold, and close is at or above the bar midpoint. Bearish absorption uses a positive threshold and close at or below the midpoint.
Long: Bullish absorption OR a qualifying bullish chart-timeframe FVG forms OR a qualifying bullish higher-timeframe FVG forms OR an enabled signal condition holds: the low touches or crosses the selected moving average and close finishes above it, with its slope rising if the slope filter is enabled; OR an active bullish FVG is retested (low reaches its upper boundary, only its first retest if configured); OR an active bullish higher-timeframe FVG is retested (low reaches its upper boundary, first retest only).
Short: Bearish absorption OR a qualifying bearish chart-timeframe FVG forms OR a qualifying bearish higher-timeframe FVG forms OR an enabled signal condition holds: the high touches or crosses the selected moving average and close finishes below it, with its slope falling if the slope filter is enabled; OR an active bearish FVG is retested (high reaches its lower boundary, only its first retest if configured); OR an active bearish higher-timeframe FVG is retested (high reaches its lower boundary, first retest only).
Long: On a confirmed chart bar, emit a buy signal if the signal system is enabled, the bar is beyond the configured cooldown from the prior signal, the buy trigger count meets the configured minimum and exceeds the sell trigger count, the buy score passes the configured minimum grade, and any enabled session, VWAP, and aligned-bias filters pass. Buy triggers are the enabled MA-touch, chart-FVG-retest, higher-timeframe-FVG-retest, and bullish-absorption conditions. Buy score is buy trigger count plus one if close is above VWAP plus one if both higher-timeframe biases are bullish. Minimum grade C/B/A/A+ requires score rank 1/2/3/4 respectively, where rank 2/3/4 requires score at least 3/4/5.
Short: On a confirmed chart bar, emit a sell signal if the signal system is enabled, the bar is beyond the configured cooldown from the prior signal, the sell trigger count meets the configured minimum and exceeds the buy trigger count, the sell score passes the configured minimum grade, and any enabled session, VWAP, and aligned-bias filters pass. Sell triggers are the enabled MA-touch, chart-FVG-retest, higher-timeframe-FVG-retest, and bearish-absorption conditions. Sell score is sell trigger count plus one if close is below VWAP plus one if both higher-timeframe biases are bearish. Each bias is close above its EMA with the EMA rising, or close below its EMA with the EMA falling; the two higher timeframes use the same EMA lookback, with one shorter and one longer. Use completed higher-timeframe bars when confirmed-bias mode is enabled; otherwise use the developing bars.
Exit: long, low reaches or falls below entry price minus ATR times the configured stop multiplier OR high reaches or exceeds entry price plus ATR times the stop multiplier times the configured first-target R multiple.
Exit: short, high reaches or exceeds entry price plus ATR times the configured stop multiplier OR low reaches or falls below entry price minus ATR times the stop multiplier times the configured first-target R multiple.
