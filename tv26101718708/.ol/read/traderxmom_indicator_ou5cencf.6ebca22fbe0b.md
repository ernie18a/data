Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = (previous Heikin-Ashi open + previous Heikin-Ashi close) / 2, initialized as (open + close) / 2; Heikin-Ashi high/low = maximum/minimum of high/low, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close
Calculation: Strong bullish Heikin-Ashi candle = Heikin-Ashi close > Heikin-Ashi open AND Heikin-Ashi open − Heikin-Ashi low ≤ minimum tick; strong bearish Heikin-Ashi candle = Heikin-Ashi close < Heikin-Ashi open AND Heikin-Ashi high − Heikin-Ashi open ≤ minimum tick
Long: (strong bullish Heikin-Ashi candle AND close > short EMA AND short EMA > long EMA OR short EMA > long EMA AND both EMAs are rising over the trend lookback AND their separation meets the minimum threshold AND previous bar's Heikin-Ashi candle was bearish AND strong bullish Heikin-Ashi candle AND close > short EMA OR short EMA crosses above long EMA AND strong bullish Heikin-Ashi candle) AND close > short EMA AND short EMA > long EMA AND close > VWAP AND cooldown has elapsed AND outside the kill zone AND (time filter disabled OR current time is within an enabled session)
