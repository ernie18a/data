Calculation: Each of three independently configured moving averages = selected moving-average type applied to its selected source over its lookback, on its selected timeframe (chart timeframe or higher/lower; use the value returned for that timeframe, which may be from a still-forming higher-timeframe bar in real time).
Long: For any pair of moving averages, the first crosses above the second (first > second now AND first <= second on the previous bar), AND both are visible AND trading that pair is enabled.
Exit: Long, for any pair, the first crosses below the second (first < second now AND first >= second on the previous bar), OR price reaches the take-profit level above entry or stop-loss level below entry, each offset by the configured percentage of the signal-bar close.
