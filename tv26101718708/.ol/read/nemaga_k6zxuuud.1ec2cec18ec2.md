Calculation: ALMA-smoothed percentage change = ALMA of the percentage change in close over the smoothing interval.
Calculation: Gaussian adaptive moving average = 7-bar EMA of a weighted average where each term is the sum of the highest and lowest ALMA-smoothed percentage change over the current and preceding bars up to that term’s lookback; weight each term by exp(-0.5 × ((lookback offset − last offset) / (2 × sigma))²), with sigma from the standard deviation of close over a volatility lookback when adaptive, otherwise fixed.
Long: ALMA-smoothed percentage change >= Gaussian adaptive moving average AND (RSI rising AND Chande Momentum Oscillator rising, when that filter is enabled) AND (close > EMA AND EMA rising, when that filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA AND higher-timeframe EMA rising, when that filter is enabled; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND long conditions true on this confirmed chart bar and false on the prior chart bar AND last signal was not long AND bars since last signal > cooldown.
Short: ALMA-smoothed percentage change < Gaussian adaptive moving average AND (RSI falling AND Chande Momentum Oscillator falling, when that filter is enabled) AND (close < EMA AND EMA falling, when that filter is enabled) AND (higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA AND higher-timeframe EMA falling, when that filter is enabled; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND short conditions true on this confirmed chart bar and false on the prior chart bar AND last signal was not short AND bars since last signal > cooldown.
