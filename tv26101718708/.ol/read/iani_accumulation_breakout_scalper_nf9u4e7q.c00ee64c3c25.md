Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low in the selected source (close by default; high/low in wick mode) that is higher/lower than the preceding and following pivot-sensitivity bars; confirm it after the following bars complete.
Calculation: Resistance/support trendline = line through the two most recent confirmed pivot highs/lows, extended forward.
Calculation: Accumulation = close lies strictly between the resistance and support trendlines, and each trendline has at least the minimum number of touches within the touch-count lookback; a touch is when the selected touch source (close by default; high/low in wick mode) is within the touch tolerance, counting touches only when separated by at least the minimum bar spacing.
Calculation: Accumulation-armed state = becomes true when accumulation is detected and remains true until an upward or downward breakout.
Calculation: Breakout state = upward when close crosses above resistance plus the breakout buffer while accumulation-armed; downward when close crosses below support minus the breakout buffer while accumulation-armed. Clear the opposite state on a breakout; clear the active state after the maximum wait or its retest entry.
Calculation: Confirmed-pivot sequence = alternating pivot highs and lows; replace the latest pivot of the same type if a more extreme one confirms.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders setup = last five pivots are high-low-high-low-high, outer highs differ by no more than the shoulder tolerance, the middle high exceeds both outer highs by more than that tolerance, and the first-to-last span meets the minimum pattern spacing. Neckline joins the two intervening lows.
Calculation: Inverse head-and-shoulders setup = last five pivots are low-high-low-high-low, outer lows differ by no more than the shoulder tolerance, the middle low is below both outer lows by more than that tolerance, and the first-to-last span meets the minimum pattern spacing. Neckline joins the two intervening highs.
Calculation: Double-top setup = last three pivots are high-low-high, the highs differ by no more than the pattern tolerance, and the first-to-last span meets the minimum pattern spacing. Neckline is the intervening low.
Calculation: Double-bottom setup = last three pivots are low-high-low, the lows differ by no more than the pattern tolerance, and the first-to-last span meets the minimum pattern spacing. Neckline is the intervening high.
Long: close crosses above resistance trendline plus breakout buffer while accumulation-armed OR upward breakout state is active and low <= resistance trendline + retest tolerance and close > resistance trendline OR an active inverse head-and-shoulders setup has a later close > its neckline + neckline buffer before its wait expires OR an active double-bottom setup has a later close > its neckline + neckline buffer before its wait expires.
Short: close crosses below support trendline minus breakout buffer while accumulation-armed OR downward breakout state is active and high >= support trendline - retest tolerance and close < support trendline OR an active head-and-shoulders setup has a later close < its neckline - neckline buffer before its wait expires OR an active double-top setup has a later close < its neckline - neckline buffer before its wait expires.
