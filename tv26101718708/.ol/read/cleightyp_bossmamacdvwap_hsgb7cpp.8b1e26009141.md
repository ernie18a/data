Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low that is the highest/lowest across the same configurable number of chart bars on both sides; use the most recently confirmed pivot as the active level until it is broken.
Calculation: SMA slope = current chart-bar SMA minus prior chart-bar SMA; calculate three SMAs with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: NY VWAP and daily VWAP = volume-weighted average of OHLC4, each reset at its configured daily anchor time.
Calculation: Stop loss = selected level among the last opposite pivot, breakout-bar low for longs or high for shorts, or entry price adjusted by the configured percentage; apply configured point and percentage buffers away from entry.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the stop-loss distance multiplied by the configured risk-reward factor.
Long: active pivot high AND (intrabar high >= pivot high OR confirmed close > pivot high) AND configured BOS/CHoCH mode matches whether the prior break direction is not down/up respectively AND long trend filters pass AND enabled VWAP filters have close above their VWAPs AND position is flat or short AND daily trade limit permits entry AND pivot level has not already been used for a long AND stop-loss distance > 0 AND (confirmed-bar mode only: strong-candle filter disabled OR close > open AND body size >= wick size multiplied by configured factor).
Short: active pivot low AND (intrabar low <= pivot low OR confirmed close < pivot low) AND configured BOS/CHoCH mode matches whether the prior break direction is not up/down respectively AND short trend filters pass AND enabled VWAP filters have close below their VWAPs AND position is flat or long AND daily trade limit permits entry AND pivot level has not already been used for a short AND stop-loss distance > 0 AND (confirmed-bar mode only: strong-candle filter disabled OR close < open AND body size >= wick size multiplied by configured factor).
Exit: long, low <= stop loss OR high >= profit target OR a new day begins with end-of-day closing enabled.
Exit: short, high >= stop loss OR low <= profit target OR a new day begins with end-of-day closing enabled.
