Calculation: Relative strength rank = percentile rank over the 52-week window of (asset return over the relative-strength lookback − benchmark return over the same lookback)
Calculation: Contraction depth for each window = (highest high − lowest low) / highest high × 100, using completed bars through the prior bar; windows are the full base lookback, about half that lookback, and about one quarter that lookback
Long: Flat position AND at least the required number of these eight conditions hold: close > medium-term SMA; close > long-term SMA; medium-term SMA > long-term SMA; long-term SMA > its value the rising-average lookback ago; short-term SMA > medium-term SMA; short-term SMA > long-term SMA; close > short-term SMA; close ≥ 52-week low × (1 + minimum distance above low); close ≥ 52-week high × (1 − maximum distance below high); relative strength rank ≥ minimum rank; AND full-window contraction depth > half-window depth > quarter-window depth AND quarter-window depth ≤ maximum depth AND prior-bar 5-bar average volume ≤ prior-bar 50-bar average volume × maximum dry-up ratio; AND close > base pivot (prior-bar full-window highest high) AND close ≤ pivot × (1 + maximum extension) AND volume ≥ 50-bar average volume × minimum breakout multiplier; AND bar index > 52-week lookback
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ target price OR (close < short-term SMA AND moving-average exit is enabled); stop price = the higher of entry price × (1 − maximum stop distance) and prior-bar quarter-window lowest low × 0.995 when base-low stops are enabled, otherwise entry price × (1 − maximum stop distance); target price = entry price × (1 + target distance)
