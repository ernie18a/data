Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA gap expanding = current absolute difference between EMA 93 and EMA 240 is greater than their absolute difference at the longer gap lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA 93 slope = EMA 93 on the last completed higher-timeframe bar compared with EMA 93 from the longer slope lookback earlier; rising means greater and falling means lower.
Calculation: Move since latest 4H EMA 93/EMA 240 cross = percent change in the last completed 4H close from the last completed 4H close at the latest cross, measured in the cross direction.
