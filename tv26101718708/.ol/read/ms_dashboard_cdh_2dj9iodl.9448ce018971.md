Calculation: Heikin-Ashi money flow = simple moving average over a lookback of ((Heikin-Ashi close − Heikin-Ashi open) / (Heikin-Ashi high − Heikin-Ashi low)) × a positive scale factor
Long: Heikin-Ashi money flow > 0
Long: Heikin-Ashi money flow > 0 AND previous bar’s Heikin-Ashi money flow < 0
Short: Heikin-Ashi money flow ≤ 0
Short: Heikin-Ashi money flow < 0 AND previous bar’s Heikin-Ashi money flow > 0
