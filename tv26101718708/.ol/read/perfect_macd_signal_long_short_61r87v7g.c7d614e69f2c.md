Calculation: Support level = latest confirmed pivot low, using left and right bars on the chart timeframe

Calculation: Near support = support level exists and absolute(close − support level) / close is within the proximity tolerance
Calculation: Near resistance = resistance level exists and absolute(close − resistance level) / close is within the proximity tolerance
