Calculation: Higher-timeframe VWAP = VWAP on the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: MACD bullish/bearish = fast EMA crosses above/below slow EMA; the fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than the slow EMA.
Calculation: High-volume bar = volume is at least the volume-spike threshold times its moving average over the volume lookback.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low falls below the prior bar’s lowest low over the sweep lookback, and close exceeds the prior bar’s midpoint by the sweep-strength fraction of its range. Bearish liquidity sweep = high exceeds the prior bar’s highest high over the sweep lookback, and close falls below the prior bar’s midpoint by that fraction of its range.
Calculation: Bullish pin bar = low falls below the prior bars’ lowest low over the pin-bar lookback, close > open, and close − open > 2 × (open − low). Bearish pin bar = high exceeds the prior bars’ highest high over that lookback, close < open, and open − close > 2 × (high − open).
Calculation: Delta = volume when close > open, otherwise −volume; cumulative delta = running sum of delta.
Calculation: Trend alignment = TEMA with a shorter lookback is above the smoothed moving average for bullish alignment, and below it for bearish alignment.
Calculation: Long trade score = trend points (strong if ADX > trend threshold, medium if ADX > chop threshold, otherwise zero; awarded when +DI > −DI) + momentum points (strong when bullish MACD and bullish trend alignment, medium when either holds, otherwise zero) + volatility points (strong when ADX > trend threshold, low when ADX < chop threshold, otherwise medium) + volume points (strong for a high-volume bar, medium when volume > its average, otherwise zero) + pattern points (strong when both bullish sweep and bullish pin bar hold, medium when either holds, otherwise zero).
Calculation: Short trade score = trend points (strong if ADX > trend threshold, medium if ADX > chop threshold, otherwise zero; awarded when −DI > +DI) + momentum points (strong when bearish MACD and bearish trend alignment, medium when either holds, otherwise zero) + volatility points (strong when ADX > trend threshold, low when ADX < chop threshold, otherwise medium) + volume points (strong for a high-volume bar, medium when volume > its average, otherwise zero) + pattern points (strong when both bearish sweep and bearish pin bar hold, medium when either holds, otherwise zero).
Calculation: Long confluence count = count of: close > higher-timeframe VWAP, high-volume bar, bullish MACD, bullish liquidity sweep, cumulative delta > prior cumulative delta.
Calculation: Short confluence count = count of: close < higher-timeframe VWAP, high-volume bar, bearish MACD, bearish liquidity sweep, cumulative delta < prior cumulative delta.
Calculation: Scaled profit distance = side-specific profit target × clamp(1 + (ATR / its moving average − 1) × volatility factor, profit lower bound, profit upper bound).
Calculation: Scaled stop distance = min(ATR × stop factor / tick size, stop cap) × tick size × clamp(1 + (ATR / its moving average − 1) × volatility factor, stop lower bound, stop upper bound).
Long: long confluence count ≥ minimum confluences AND daily trade count < daily limit AND daily VIX close < VIX pause level AND bullish trend alignment AND ADX > chop threshold AND (ADX ≤ trend threshold OR (+DI > −DI AND close > EMA)) AND long trade score ≥ long score threshold.
Short: short confluence count ≥ minimum confluences AND daily trade count < daily limit AND daily VIX close < VIX pause level AND bearish trend alignment AND ADX > chop threshold AND (ADX ≤ trend threshold OR (−DI > +DI AND close < EMA)) AND short trade score ≥ short score threshold AND ATR ≤ 2 × its moving average.
Exit: long, close ≤ entry price − scaled stop distance / point value OR high ≥ entry price + scaled profit distance / point value OR high ≥ entry price + maximum profit distance / point value OR the long trailing stop is reached after close ≥ entry price + trail activation distance / point value OR bars in trade > time-stop limit.
Exit: short, close ≥ entry price + scaled stop distance / point value OR low ≤ entry price − scaled profit distance / point value OR low ≤ entry price − maximum profit distance / point value OR the short trailing stop is reached after close ≤ entry price − trail activation threshold / point value OR bars in trade > time-stop limit.
