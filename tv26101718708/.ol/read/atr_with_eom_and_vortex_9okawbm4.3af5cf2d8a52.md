Calculation: Ease of Movement = moving average of midpoint price change × (high − low) ÷ volume
Calculation: Vortex average = average of the positive and negative Vortex indicators, each equal to a rolling sum of absolute high − prior low or absolute low − prior high, respectively, divided by the rolling sum of ATR(1)
Long: ATR > EMA of ATR AND Ease of Movement > 0 AND Vortex average > 1
Short: ATR < EMA of ATR AND Ease of Movement < 0 AND Vortex average < 1
Exit: long, ATR < EMA of ATR AND Ease of Movement < 0 AND Vortex average < 1
