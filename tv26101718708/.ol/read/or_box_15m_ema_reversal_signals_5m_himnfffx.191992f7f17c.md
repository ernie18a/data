Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low of lower-timeframe bars during the configured opening window; fixed after the window ends.
Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Live higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of higher-timeframe closes, including the bar still forming.
Long: After the opening range is fixed, the confirmed higher-timeframe EMA is above its high; the setup remains armed until the close is within the opening range or the EMA is at or below its high; while armed, close > opening-range high AND low <= confirmed higher-timeframe EMA AND close > confirmed higher-timeframe EMA AND no bullish touch is pending AND the chart bar is complete AND the containing higher-timeframe bar has low < live higher-timeframe EMA AND close > live higher-timeframe EMA. A bullish touch becomes pending on this signal and is cleared when low > confirmed higher-timeframe EMA.
Short: After the opening range is fixed, the confirmed higher-timeframe EMA is below its low; the setup remains armed until the close is within the opening range or the EMA is at or above its low; while armed, close < opening-range low AND high >= confirmed higher-timeframe EMA AND close < confirmed higher-timeframe EMA AND no bearish touch is pending AND the chart bar is complete AND the containing higher-timeframe bar has high > live higher-timeframe EMA AND close < live higher-timeframe EMA. A bearish touch becomes pending on this signal and is cleared when high < confirmed higher-timeframe EMA.
