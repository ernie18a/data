Calculation: Hann FIR filter(length) = sum of close from the current bar through length−1 prior bars, weighted at offset i by 1−cos(2π(i+1)/(length+1)), divided by the sum of those weights
Calculation: Swing high = the prior bar's high is the highest high in the lookback window ending on that bar, and the current high is below the current lookback-window highest high; swing low is the inverse using lows
Calculation: Structure trend = initialize neutral; on a new swing high, set bullish if its price exceeds the previous swing high; on a new swing low, set bearish if its price is below the previous swing low; a bullish break of structure sets bullish and a bearish break of structure sets bearish; otherwise retain the prior state
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = on a completed bar, close exceeds the latest swing high and that swing high has not previously triggered a bullish break; bearish break of structure = inverse using the latest swing low
Calculation: Bullish FVG = middle bar closes above its open and the high two bars ago is below the current low; zone is between that earlier high and current low. Bearish FVG = middle bar closes below its open and the low two bars ago is above the current high; zone is between current high and that earlier low. Use the latest zone of each direction
Calculation: Near bullish FVG = close is above the latest bullish zone's lower boundary minus ATR times the proximity parameter and below its upper boundary plus that amount; near bearish FVG = close is below the latest bearish zone's upper boundary plus that amount and above its lower boundary minus that amount
Calculation: Long stop distance = max(close − (lookback lowest low − ATR × stop buffer), ATR × minimum distance); short stop distance = max((lookback highest high + ATR × stop buffer) − close, ATR × minimum distance)
Calculation: Long take-profit price = entry price + long stop distance × reward-risk parameter; short take-profit price = entry price − short stop distance × reward-risk parameter
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest high since entry − ATR × trailing parameter; short trailing stop = lowest low since entry + ATR × trailing parameter
Calculation: Enabled session = weekday UTC hour in an enabled session window: Asia 01:00–09:00, London 07:00–16:00, or New York 13:00–22:00
Long: completed bar AND flat position AND (Hann FIR filter(shorter lookback) crosses above Hann FIR filter(next longer lookback) OR bullish break of structure AND Hann FIR filter(shorter lookback) > Hann FIR filter(longest lookback)) AND structure trend >= neutral AND ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds AND (session filter disabled OR current time is in an enabled session) AND (FVG filter disabled OR near bullish FVG)
Short: completed bar AND flat position AND (Hann FIR filter(shorter lookback) crosses below Hann FIR filter(next longer lookback) OR bearish break of structure AND Hann FIR filter(shorter lookback) < Hann FIR filter(longest lookback)) AND structure trend <= neutral AND ADX is between its minimum and maximum thresholds AND (session filter disabled OR current time is in an enabled session) AND (FVG filter disabled OR near bearish FVG)
Exit: Long, low <= long stop price OR high >= long take-profit price OR low <= long trailing stop OR bearish break of structure
Exit: Short, high >= short stop price OR low <= short take-profit price OR high >= short trailing stop OR bullish break of structure
