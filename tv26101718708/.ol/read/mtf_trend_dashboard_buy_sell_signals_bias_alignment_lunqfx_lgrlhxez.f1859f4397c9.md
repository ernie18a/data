Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with a lookback of the same length on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish after a close above the latest confirmed swing high; bearish after a close below the latest confirmed swing low; otherwise retain the previous bias, initially neutral. On higher timeframes, update only from the last completed bar.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of (the latest chart swing low or current low, whichever is lower) minus an ATR buffer, and the close minus a minimum ATR distance, whichever is lower; if no swing low exists, use the lowest low over a lookback instead. Short stop = the higher of (the latest chart swing high or current high, whichever is higher) plus an ATR buffer, and the close plus a minimum ATR distance, whichever is higher; if no swing high exists, use the highest high over a lookback instead. Use a shorter lookback for chart swings than for higher-timeframe bias swings.
Long: Chart close > latest confirmed chart swing high not already broken AND at least the required number of strictly higher timeframes have bullish bias on their last completed bars AND bullish higher-timeframe count > bearish count AND close − long stop <= ATR × maximum stop distance AND no long position is open.
Short: Chart close < latest confirmed chart swing low not already broken AND at least the required number of strictly higher timeframes have bearish bias on their last completed bars AND bearish higher-timeframe count > bullish count AND short stop − close <= ATR × maximum stop distance AND no short position is open.
Exit: Long, low <= stop price OR high >= entry price + (entry price − stop price) × second profit-target multiple OR short-entry condition.
Exit: Short, high >= stop price OR low <= entry price − (stop price − entry price) × second profit-target multiple OR long-entry condition.
