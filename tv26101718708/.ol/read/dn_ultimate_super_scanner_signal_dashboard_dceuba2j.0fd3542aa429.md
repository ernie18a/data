Calculation: High volume = current volume > average volume × configured high-volume multiplier
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume
Calculation: Long ATR trail = max(previous long trail, close − ATR × configured trail multiplier), initialized at entry close − ATR × configured trail multiplier
Calculation: Short ATR trail = min(previous short trail, close + ATR × configured trail multiplier), initialized at entry close + ATR × configured trail multiplier
Long: (intraday chart AND fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA AND close > VWAP AND high volume AND close > open AND RSI > upper threshold) OR (non-intraday chart AND fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND high volume AND close > open AND RSI > threshold) OR (intraday or non-intraday chart AND fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA AND low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND volume > average volume AND RSI > threshold) OR (shorter-lookback EMA > medium-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA AND close > longer-lookback SMA AND this bullish stack is newly true AND optional relative-volume filter passes) OR (intraday chart AND close > highest high of the configured lookback over prior completed chart bars AND high volume AND close > VWAP) OR (high volume AND close > open)
Short: (intraday chart AND fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA AND close < VWAP AND high volume AND close < open AND RSI < lower threshold) OR (non-intraday chart AND fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND high volume AND close < open AND RSI < threshold) OR (intraday or non-intraday chart AND fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA AND high >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND volume > average volume AND RSI < threshold) OR (shorter-lookback EMA < medium-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA AND close < longer-lookback SMA AND this bearish stack is newly true AND optional relative-volume filter passes) OR (intraday chart AND close < lowest low of the configured lookback over prior completed chart bars AND high volume AND close < VWAP) OR (high volume AND close < open)
Exit: long, close < long ATR trail
Exit: short, close > short ATR trail
