Calculation: Bullish order block = range [low[1], high[1]] of a bearish higher-timeframe bar when the still-forming higher-timeframe bar closes above high[1]; bearish order block = range [low[1], high[1]] of a bullish higher-timeframe bar when the still-forming higher-timeframe bar closes below low[1].
Calculation: Active zones = unmitigated order blocks and breakers; an order block becomes a breaker when a chart bar closes below a bullish zone's bottom or above a bearish zone's top, flips direction, and remains active. Mitigation occurs on wick contact or a close inside the zone, according to the selected mitigation mode; mitigated zones that have not become breakers are inactive.
Long: A bullish order block is detected on a higher timeframe OR the chart bar's high-low range overlaps any active bullish zone and the preceding chart bar did not overlap any active bullish zone.
Short: A bearish order block is detected on a higher timeframe OR the chart bar's high-low range overlaps any active bearish zone and the preceding chart bar did not overlap any active bearish zone.
