Calculation: Donchian center = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the lookback.
Calculation: Chandelier long stop = highest high over the lookback − ATR × multiplier; Chandelier short stop = lowest low over the lookback + ATR × multiplier. Each stop carries forward toward the trade direction and resets to its current value when the prior close crosses its prior stop.
Calculation: Ichimoku filter lines = Donchian centers with short, medium, and long lookbacks; cloud line A = average of the short- and medium-lookback centers; cloud line B = the long-lookback center. Compare current lines and close with cloud lines displaced into the past.
Long: While flat, enter when an enabled entry trigger is true: random trigger; external signal > 0 and not equal to +1 or +3; an enabled long filter changes from false to true; long filters are true; RSI crosses above its selected long level; or the shorter-lookback Donchian center crosses above the longer-lookback center. AND long direction is allowed, all enabled long filters are true, date and data-length filters pass, and capital remains above zero. For pyramiding, while already long, enter on an enabled entry trigger when pyramiding is on, the entry limit is not reached, and its selected price-increment, same/different-entry-signal, or every-opportunity rule passes; apply enabled long filters if required.
Short: While flat, enter when an enabled entry trigger is true: random trigger; external signal < 0 and not equal to −1 or −3; an enabled short filter changes from false to true; short filters are true; RSI crosses below its selected short level; or the shorter-lookback Donchian center crosses below the longer-lookback center. AND short direction is allowed, all enabled short filters are true, date and data-length filters pass, and capital remains above zero. For pyramiding, while already short, enter on an enabled entry trigger when pyramiding is on, the entry limit is not reached, and its selected price-increment, same/different-entry-signal, or every-opportunity rule passes; apply enabled short filters if required.
Exit: Long, close < prior in-trade stop OR an enabled hard exit: random exit; external signal = +3; short filters change from false to true; RSI crosses below its selected long-exit level; the shorter-lookback Donchian center crosses below the longer-lookback center; or close exceeds the fixed target above entry by the selected multiple of initial stop distance.
Exit: Short, close > prior in-trade stop OR an enabled hard exit: random exit; external signal = −3; long filters change from false to true; RSI crosses above its selected short-exit level; the shorter-lookback Donchian center crosses above the longer-lookback center; or close falls below the fixed target below entry by the selected multiple of initial stop distance.
