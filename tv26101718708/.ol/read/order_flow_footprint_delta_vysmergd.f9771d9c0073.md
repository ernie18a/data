Calculation: Bar range = high − low, with a minimum of one tick
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute(close − open) / bar range
Calculation: Volume average = moving average of volume over a lookback
Calculation: Positive delta proxy = volume when close > open; negative delta proxy = −volume when close < open; otherwise zero
Calculation: Bullish imbalance = close > open, body ratio at least the strong-body threshold, and volume at least the volume average times the impulse multiplier
Calculation: Bearish imbalance = close < open, body ratio at least the strong-body threshold, and volume at least the volume average times the impulse multiplier
Calculation: Bullish absorption = close ≥ open, body ratio at most the absorption-body threshold, and volume at least the volume average times the absorption-volume multiplier
Calculation: Bearish absorption = close ≤ open, body ratio at most the absorption-body threshold, and volume at least the volume average times the absorption-volume multiplier
Calculation: Bullish structure = the last two confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs and the last two confirmed chart-timeframe pivot lows are both rising; bearish structure = both are falling
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = last completed higher-timeframe close above its 50-period EMA for bullish bias, or below it for bearish bias
Calculation: Bullish stack = consecutive bullish imbalances for the configured stack length; bearish stack = consecutive bearish imbalances for that length
Calculation: Bull impulse zone = highest high and lowest low across the bullish stack; bear impulse zone = highest high and lowest low across the bearish stack
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: OF2 upper level = the greater of the prior chart-timeframe lookback high and the prior completed daily high; OF2 lower level = the lesser of the prior chart-timeframe lookback low and the prior completed daily low
Calculation: Bull sweep = high above the OF2 upper level and close below it; bear sweep = low below the OF2 lower level and close above it
Calculation: OF3 upper range = highest high over the chart-timeframe range lookback, excluding the current bar; OF3 lower range = lowest low over that lookback, excluding the current bar
Long: bullish structure and (higher-timeframe bias is bullish when its filter is enabled) AND a bullish stack occurred within the pullback window AND the current bar is 1 or more bars after that stack AND low ≤ bull impulse zone high + ATR × zone padding AND low ≥ bull impulse zone low − ATR × zone padding AND (bullish imbalance OR (positive delta proxy and close > open and volume > volume average)) AND the prior same-direction OF1 signal is outside its cooldown
Short: bearish structure and (higher-timeframe bias is bearish when its filter is enabled) AND a bearish stack occurred within the pullback window AND the current bar is 1 or more bars after that stack AND high ≥ bear impulse zone low − ATR × zone padding AND high ≤ bear impulse zone high + ATR × zone padding AND (bearish imbalance OR (negative delta proxy and close < open and volume > volume average)) AND the prior same-direction OF1 signal is outside its cooldown
Long: a bear sweep occurred 1 to the configured confirmation-window bars ago AND close > the current OF2 lower level AND (bullish absorption OR bullish imbalance) AND low ≥ the low of the sweep bar AND the prior same-direction OF2 signal is outside its cooldown
Short: a bull sweep occurred 1 to the configured confirmation-window bars ago AND close < the current OF2 upper level AND (bearish absorption OR bearish imbalance) AND high ≤ the high of the sweep bar AND the prior same-direction OF2 signal is outside its cooldown
Long: a bullish break occurred 2 to the configured retest-window bars ago and has not been used AND the current bar retests its broken upper-range level (low ≤ level + ATR × retest padding) and closes above that level AND volume > volume average AND (bullish imbalance OR (positive delta proxy and body ratio ≥ the retest-body threshold)) AND the prior same-direction OF3 signal is outside its cooldown
Short: a bearish break occurred 2 to the configured retest-window bars ago and has not been used AND the current bar retests its broken lower-range level (high ≥ level − ATR × retest padding) and closes below that level AND volume > volume average AND (bearish imbalance OR (negative delta proxy and body ratio ≥ the retest-body threshold)) AND the prior same-direction OF3 signal is outside its cooldown
Calculation: Bullish break = close above the OF3 upper range by the ATR break-padding threshold, volume at least the volume average times the break-volume multiplier, close > open, and body ratio at least the break-body threshold; bearish break = close below the OF3 lower range by that ATR padding, with corresponding bearish conditions
