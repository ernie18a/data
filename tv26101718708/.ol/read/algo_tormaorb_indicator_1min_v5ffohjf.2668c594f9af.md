Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, during the configured opening-range bars of the current day; finalize when that window ends.
Long: Opening range is formed AND current time is within the configured trading window AND no breakout has occurred today AND (close > opening-range high AND previous close <= opening-range high OR a long breakout has occurred and no retest has occurred since AND low <= opening-range high AND low >= opening-range low)
Short: Opening range is formed AND current time is within the configured trading window AND no breakout has occurred today AND (close < opening-range low AND previous close >= opening-range low OR a short breakout has occurred and no retest has occurred since AND high >= opening-range low AND high <= opening-range high)
