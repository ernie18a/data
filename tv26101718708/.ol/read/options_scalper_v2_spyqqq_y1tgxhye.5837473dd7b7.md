Calculation: Long score = 2 if the short-term EMA crossed above the long-term EMA within the recent lookback, plus 1 each if the short-term EMA is above the long-term EMA; RSI is above its lower bullish threshold and below its upper bullish threshold; close is above VWAP while the VWAP filter is enabled; MACD line is above its signal line and its histogram is rising; volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier and close is above open with the candle body greater than half its range; stochastic %K is below its oversold threshold and %D is below its oversold threshold, or %K is above %D; close is above the long-term EMA; and high exceeds the highest high of the preceding lookback bars.
Calculation: Short score = 2 if the short-term EMA crossed below the long-term EMA within the recent lookback, plus 1 each if the short-term EMA is below the long-term EMA; RSI is below its upper bearish threshold and above its lower bearish threshold; close is below VWAP while the VWAP filter is enabled; MACD line is below its signal line and its histogram is falling; volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier and close is below open with the candle body greater than half its range; stochastic %K is above its overbought threshold and %D is above its overbought threshold, or %K is below %D; close is below the long-term EMA; and low is below the lowest low of the preceding lookback bars.
Long: The short-term EMA crossed above the long-term EMA within the recent lookback AND long score is at least the minimum score AND long score is greater than short score AND the bar is within an enabled trading session (morning or afternoon) AND the minimum bars since the last signal have elapsed.
Short: The short-term EMA crossed below the long-term EMA within the recent lookback AND short score is at least the minimum score AND short score is greater than long score AND the bar is within an enabled trading session (morning or afternoon) AND the minimum bars since the last signal have elapsed.
Exit: Long, low is at or below entry price minus the stop ATR multiple times ATR OR high is at or above entry price plus the target ATR multiple times ATR OR a short signal occurs.
Exit: Short, high is at or above entry price plus the stop ATR multiple times ATR OR low is at or below entry price minus the target ATR multiple times ATR OR a long signal occurs.
