Calculation: Active zones = Every refresh interval after the lookback is available, cluster the highs, lows, and closes of the preceding lookback bars using volume weights (volume divided by its moving average, capped at 3; multiply close weights by 1.2). Initialize cluster centers at evenly spaced quantiles of the sorted prices, then iterate nearest-center assignment and volume-weighted mean updates. Keep clusters with at least the minimum density, excluding centers within 0.7 ATR of an active zone. At each refresh, replace all unbroken zones; retain broken zones. A center above the current close is resistance; otherwise it is support. Each zone's upper and lower bounds are its center plus or minus half the zone-width ATR multiple. Newly created zones are active and unbroken.
Long: An active, unbroken resistance zone exists AND close > its upper bound + ATR × break buffer AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier
Short: An active, unbroken support zone exists AND close < its lower bound − ATR × break buffer AND volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier
