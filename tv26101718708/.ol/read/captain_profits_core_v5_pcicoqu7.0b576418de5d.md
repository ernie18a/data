Calculation: ATR trailing stop = on the chart timeframe, let source be close or Heikin-Ashi close; initialize the stop to the prior source. If current and prior source are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and current source minus ATR; if both are below, use the lesser of the prior stop and current source plus ATR; otherwise use current source minus ATR when current source is above the prior stop, or current source plus ATR when it is not. ATR uses a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Buy candle dominance = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Sell candle dominance = (high − close) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / moving average of volume over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Volume fade = volume is lower on each of the last N bars than on the immediately preceding bar.
Long: position is not long AND source > ATR trailing stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close > longer-lookback EMA AND shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA) AND (candle dominance filter disabled OR buy candle dominance ≥ threshold) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ threshold) AND (breakout filter disabled OR close > prior bar high AND close > open OR strict breakout: low > prior bar open AND close > prior bar high AND close > open)
Short: position is not short AND source < ATR trailing stop AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR close < longer-lookback EMA AND shorter-lookback EMA < longer-lookback EMA) AND (candle dominance filter disabled OR sell candle dominance ≥ threshold) AND (relative volume filter disabled OR relative volume ≥ threshold) AND (breakout filter disabled OR close < prior bar low AND close < open OR strict breakout: high < prior bar open AND close < prior bar low AND close < open)
Exit: long, source < ATR trailing stop OR (volume fade exit enabled AND volume fade AND close < open)
Exit: short, source > ATR trailing stop OR (volume fade exit enabled AND volume fade AND close > open)
