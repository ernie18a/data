Calculation: Camarilla levels = higher-timeframe close plus or minus the range multiplied by 0.0916, 0.183, 0.275, or 0.55; plus gives H1–H4 respectively, and minus gives L1–L4 respectively. Use the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
