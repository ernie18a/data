Calculation: Normalized score = position of the current value within its lookback range, scaled from 0 to 100; invert the result for inverse scores
Calculation: Gold breadth = percentage of the tracked gold currency pairs whose close is above the long-term EMA and whose short-term EMA is above the long-term EMA
Calculation: Macro score = weighted average of inverse real-yield, inverse dollar, gold-liquidity, inverse short-rate, risk-off, and gold-breadth scores
Long: Macro score >= 60
Short: Macro score < 40
