Calculation: New highs percentage = NYSE new 52-week highs / (NYSE advances + declines) × 100; use the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
Calculation: New lows percentage = NYSE new 52-week lows / (NYSE advances + declines) × 100; use the higher-timeframe bar while it is forming.
Calculation: McClellan Oscillator = shorter-lookback EMA minus longer-lookback EMA of 1000 × (NYSE advances − NYSE declines) / (NYSE advances + declines); use higher-timeframe bars while they are forming.
Short: New highs percentage ≥ threshold AND new lows percentage ≥ threshold AND NYSE Composite close ≥ its close at the longer lookback
Short: Bars since the most recent trigger ≤ signal-window length AND McClellan Oscillator < 0
Exit: long, Bars since the most recent trigger ≤ signal-window length AND McClellan Oscillator < 0
