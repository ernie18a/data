Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / its moving average over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe proximity = 1 − min(nearest distance from close to the last completed higher-timeframe bar’s open, high, low, or close, divided by ATR and capped at 3, 1) / 3.
Calculation: Displacement strength = min(abs(close − open) / ATR, capped at 3, 1) / 3.
Calculation: Confidence = percentage of the k nearest prior samples voting high probability, using Euclidean distance across relative volume capped at 3 and scaled to 0–1, higher-timeframe proximity, and displacement strength. A prior sample votes high probability when the absolute close change over a configurable evaluation window exceeds its ATR at the sample bar times a configurable threshold. Use only labeled prior samples and retain a configurable rolling training window.
Calculation: Equal-high candidate = a newly confirmed pivot high within an ATR-scaled tolerance of the preceding confirmed pivot high; equal-low candidate = the corresponding condition for pivot lows.
Calculation: Bullish order-block candidate = current candle body is upward and exceeds a configurable ATR multiple, and the preceding candle is bearish. Its zone spans the preceding candle’s high to low. Bearish order-block candidate = current candle body is downward and exceeds that ATR multiple, and the preceding candle is bullish; its zone also spans the preceding candle’s high to low.
Calculation: Volume-node candidate = current volume exceeds a configurable multiple of its moving average and is at least the highest volume in a configurable lookback. It is bullish when close ≥ open and bearish otherwise; its zone spans current high to low.
Long: confidence ≥ minimum confidence AND cooldown since the last placed zone has elapsed AND the candidate zone does not overlap an existing zone within an ATR-scaled merge distance AND the first eligible candidate in the per-bar priority order (bullish order block, bearish order block, equal high, equal low, volume node) is a bullish order block, an equal low, or a bullish volume node.
Short: confidence ≥ minimum confidence AND cooldown since the last placed zone has elapsed AND the candidate zone does not overlap an existing zone within an ATR-scaled merge distance AND the first eligible candidate in the per-bar priority order (bullish order block, bearish order block, equal high, equal low, volume node) is a bearish order block, an equal high, or a bearish volume node.
