Calculation: Active liquidity levels = volume-qualified pivot highs and lows from enabled same or higher timeframe bars, using the last completed bar; include chart-timeframe equal-high and equal-low clusters. A pivot is confirmed by bars on both sides and passes when its pivot-bar volume meets the volume filter relative to its timeframe’s longer-lookback volume SMA. Equal-pivot clusters use the shorter pivot lookback and average levels within the percentage tolerance. Remove swept levels after a close beyond the zone; merge nearby levels, preferring the higher timeframe. Each zone extends the configured percentage above and below its level.
Long: Close >= active liquidity level OR (low < pivot-low zone bottom AND min(open, close) >= pivot-low zone bottom AND the sellside-grab cooldown has elapsed)
Short: Close < active liquidity level OR (high > pivot-high zone top AND max(open, close) <= pivot-high zone top AND the buyside-grab cooldown has elapsed)
