Calculation: MACD score = Add a direction score based on whether the MACD line is rising, falling, or unchanged and whether the histogram is rising, falling, or unchanged; add a histogram direction score when the MACD line is unchanged. For a rising MACD line, the first direction term is the direction weight plus the histogram weight if the histogram rises, otherwise the direction weight; for a non-rising line, it is the direction weight if the histogram is unchanged, otherwise the direction weight minus the histogram weight. The second direction term is the negative direction weight minus the histogram weight if the MACD line and histogram both fall; if the MACD line falls but the histogram does not, it is the negative direction weight; otherwise it is the negative direction weight if the histogram is unchanged, or the negative of the direction weight minus the histogram weight. When the MACD line is unchanged, add the histogram weight if the histogram rises or subtract it if the histogram falls. Also add a crossover score: positive on an upward MACD-line/signal-line crossover, negative on a downward crossover, otherwise zero; add 0.6 times the prior bar's crossover score accumulator.
Calculation: Combined score = Weighted average of the chart-timeframe MACD score and the higher-timeframe MACD score, with the higher-timeframe score weighted more heavily; use the still-forming higher-timeframe bar.
Long: Combined score changes and is greater than zero
Short: Combined score changes and is less than zero
