Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = most recent pivot high/low with the swing lookback bars on both sides; use only pivots confirmed on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Lower/upper wick ratio = lower/upper wick length divided by high − low.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Prior down context = close below the close from the momentum lookback bars ago OR below the longer-period EMA; prior up context = close above that prior close OR above the longer-period EMA. The fast EMA period is shorter than the slow EMA period.
Calculation: Volume score = clamp(((current volume / its longer-lookback simple moving average − 0.65) / 0.85 × 75) + 15 + 35 × ((current volume / its moving average) − its value three bars ago), 0, 100); unavailable volume is treated as zero.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = low below the last confirmed swing low by at least the minimum ATR multiple AND close above that swing low AND close location at least the sensitivity threshold; bearish sweep = high above the last confirmed swing high by at least the minimum ATR multiple AND close below that swing high AND close location at most one minus that threshold.
Calculation: Bullish rejection = rejection-only reactions enabled AND prior down context AND lower wick ratio at least the sensitivity-adjusted wick threshold AND close location at least the sensitivity threshold AND close > open. Bearish rejection = rejection-only reactions enabled AND prior up context AND upper wick ratio at least that wick threshold AND close location at most one minus the sensitivity threshold AND close < open.
Calculation: Bullish reaction score = clamp(0.68 × max(bullish sweep score if swept else 0, bullish rejection score if rejected else 0) + 0.20 × clamp((slow EMA − low) / ATR / 1.75 × 100, 0, 100) + 0.12 × volume score, 0, 100). Bullish sweep score = clamp(0.55 × clamp((low-sweep depth in ATR units − minimum sweep depth) / (sensitivity-specific ideal sweep depth − minimum sweep depth) × 100, 0, 100) + 45 × close location, 0, 100). Bullish rejection score = clamp(95 × lower wick ratio + 30 × close location + 18 × body/range when close > open, 0, 100).
Calculation: Bearish reaction score = clamp(0.68 × max(bearish sweep score if swept else 0, bearish rejection score if rejected else 0) + 0.20 × clamp((high − slow EMA) / ATR / 1.75 × 100, 0, 100) + 0.12 × volume score, 0, 100). Bearish sweep score = clamp(0.55 × clamp((high-sweep depth in ATR units − minimum sweep depth) / (sensitivity-specific ideal sweep depth − minimum sweep depth) × 100, 0, 100) + 45 × (1 − close location), 0, 100). Bearish rejection score = clamp(95 × upper wick ratio + 30 × (1 − close location) + 18 × body/range when close < open, 0, 100).
Calculation: Candidate side = bullish when bullish reaction exists and bearish reaction does not, or when both exist and bullish score is at least bearish score; otherwise bearish when bearish reaction exists. Candidate score is the chosen side's reaction score.
Calculation: Candidate may start or replace the active setup when there is no active setup, the active setup failed or expired, the candidate side differs and its score is at least 6 above the active reaction score, or the setup is not yet confirmed, the candidate score is at least 10 above the active reaction score, and it is more than one bar after setup start.
Calculation: Confirmation line = reaction bar high + ATR buffer for bullish setup, or reaction bar low − ATR buffer for bearish setup. Invalidation level = reaction bar low − ATR buffer for bullish setup, or reaction bar high + ATR buffer for bearish setup. Target = confirmation line plus (confirmation line − invalidation level) × target-room multiple for bullish setup, or minus that distance × target-room multiple for bearish setup.
Calculation: Confirmation score = clamp(0.30 × reaction score + 0.25 × confirmation progress + 0.20 × momentum score + 0.12 × volume score + 0.13 × room score, 0, 100). Confirmation progress is the clamped percentage of the distance from reaction reference to confirmation line traversed by close in the setup direction. Momentum score uses the fast EMA change over the momentum lookback, normalized by ATR, with a close-above-fast-EMA bonus for bullish setups and a close-below-fast-EMA bonus for bearish setups; reverse the momentum sign for bearish setups. Room score is the clamped score of available room divided by risk distance; available room is the smaller of distance to the next confirmed swing in the setup direction and distance to target, if such a swing exists, otherwise distance to target.
Long: (candidate side is bullish AND candidate score ≥ minimum reaction score AND candidate may start or replace the active setup) OR (active setup is bullish AND close > confirmation line AND confirmation score ≥ confirmed threshold AND setup has not already been confirmed, failed, or expired).
Short: (candidate side is bearish AND candidate score ≥ minimum reaction score AND candidate may start or replace the active setup) OR (active setup is bearish AND close < confirmation line AND confirmation score ≥ confirmed threshold AND setup has not already been confirmed, failed, or expired).
Exit: long, active bullish setup AND close < invalidation level.
Exit: short, active bearish setup AND close > invalidation level.
