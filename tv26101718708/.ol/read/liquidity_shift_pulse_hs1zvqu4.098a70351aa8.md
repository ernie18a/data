Calculation: Confirmed swing low/high = a bar’s low/high is below/above the lows/highs of the configured number of bars on both sides; available only after the right-side bars complete. Use the latest confirmed pivot available before the current bar.
Calculation: Previous-range high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Candle range = high − low; body ratio = |close − open| / range; bullish close location = (close − low) / range; bearish close location = (high − close) / range; lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) − low) / range; upper wick ratio = (high − max(open, close)) / range.
Calculation: ATR-normalized range = candle range / ATR.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / average volume over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar; if usable volume data or baseline is unavailable, the volume filter passes.
Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log(sum of true range over the configured lookback / (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) / log(lookback).
Calculation: Directional regime = Choppiness Index ≤ directional threshold OR (Choppiness Index ≤ transition threshold AND below its previous-bar value).
Calculation: Internal structure high/low = highest high/lowest low over the configured lookback, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Bullish setup = created on a confirmed bar when low < latest confirmed swing low − ATR × sweep buffer, close > that swing low, and lower wick ratio ≥ minimum; store that bar’s low and its prior internal structure high. It remains active through the setup window and is invalidated if age exceeds the window or close < stored sweep low − ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Bearish reversal setup = while long and maximum high since entry − entry price ≥ entry ATR × reversal activation, created on a confirmed bar when high > latest confirmed swing high + ATR × sweep buffer, close < that swing high, and upper wick ratio ≥ minimum; store that bar’s high and its prior internal structure low. It remains active through the reversal window and is invalidated if age exceeds the window or close > stored sweep high + ATR × invalidation buffer.
Calculation: Protected structure = on entry, stored bullish sweep low minus ATR buffer for a liquidity entry, or prior internal structure low minus ATR buffer for an expansion entry. After entry, each confirmed pivot low whose pivot bar is later than the entry bar may raise it to pivot low minus ATR at the pivot bar × buffer, only when that candidate is below current close and above the existing protected structure.
Calculation: Maximum favorable move = highest high since entry − entry price; current open profit = max(close − entry price, 0); giveback percentage = 100 × (maximum favorable move − current open profit) / maximum favorable move.
Long: Current bar is confirmed AND position is flat AND ((bullish setup is active, age ≤ setup window, close > open, ATR-normalized range ≥ minimum, body ratio ≥ minimum, bullish close location ≥ minimum, close > stored internal structure high + ATR × break buffer, and enabled relative-volume and directional-regime filters pass) OR (expansion entry is enabled, close > previous-range high + ATR × breakout buffer, close > open, ATR-normalized range ≥ minimum, body ratio ≥ minimum, bullish close location ≥ minimum, and enabled relative-volume and regime filters pass; when the choppiness filter is enabled, also require Choppiness Index < its previous-bar value).
Short: 
Exit: Long, current bar is confirmed AND ((bar index > entry bar AND close < protected structure) OR (bearish reversal setup is active, age ≤ reversal window, close < open, ATR-normalized range ≥ minimum, body ratio ≥ minimum, bearish close location ≥ minimum, and close < stored internal structure low − ATR × break buffer) OR (large-trend protection is enabled, maximum favorable move ≥ entry ATR × activation, giveback percentage ≥ maximum, close < open, and close < previous close)).
