Calculation: Macro Hull = selected Hull moving average (HMA, exponential HMA, or triple HMA) of close; compare its current value with its value two chart bars earlier. If configured on a higher timeframe, use that timeframe’s bar still forming.
Calculation: Fast Hull = selected Hull moving average of close with a shorter lookback than Macro Hull; compare its current value with its value two chart bars earlier.
Calculation: Trail = recursive stop based on EMA(close, trail lookback) ± ATR × distance multiplier; in an uptrend use the greater of EMA − ATR distance and the prior trail, and flip down when close falls below the trail and the fast EMA is below the slow EMA. In a downtrend use the lesser of EMA + ATR distance and the prior trail, and flip up when close rises above the trail and the fast EMA is above the slow EMA. The fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Fib regime = direction of change in a double EMA of hlc3; bullish when it rises, bearish when it falls, otherwise retain the prior direction. Higher-timeframe Fib regime uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar; compare close with its double-EMA basis when the band filter is enabled.
Calculation: Swing break state = retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low (pivot lookback on both sides); a bullish break occurs when close (or high, if close confirmation is disabled) exceeds the pivot high, and a bearish break when close (or low) falls below the pivot low. A CHoCH occurs when the break direction reverses the prior break direction.
Calculation: Zone quality = current bar range ≥ ATR × minimum width, the higher-timeframe Fib regime agrees with the break direction when required, and the close is within the extension limit in ATR from the recent opposite swing extreme.
Calculation: Confluence score = count enabled bullish or bearish conditions: Macro Hull slope agrees; Fast Hull slope agrees; trail direction agrees; active CHoCH direction agrees; close is within 1.5 active-zone ranges of either zone edge and zone direction agrees; normalized close change over the momentum lookback exceeds the positive threshold for bullish or negative threshold for bearish; Ultron Gate permits the direction; volume ≥ its longer-lookback average × volume multiplier. Ultron Gate permits bullish if Fib regime is bullish or close is above EMA(200), and bearish if Fib regime is bearish or close is below EMA(200). The minimum score is configurable.
Calculation: Fib gate = require configured LTF/HTF Fib direction agreement, close above/below the corresponding LTF and HTF double-EMA bases when band filters are enabled, and either direction agreement or elapsed bars since each relevant regime flip exceeds its cooldown. A soft override passes when the direction’s score ≥ its override threshold and the trail agrees. Higher-timeframe inputs use the last completed bar for Fib regime and basis.
Calculation: Zone confidence = fraction of these nine conditions true for the zone direction: higher-timeframe CHoCH agrees; Fib gate agrees; nearest unbroken low/high liquidity pivot is within 4 ATR; close is below 0.45/above 0.55 of the trailing swing range; volume confirms; normalized momentum confirms; session is NY AM or LDN-NY; higher-timeframe Fib agrees; close is in the active zone. Higher-timeframe CHoCH uses the configured confluence timeframe’s current bar (which may still be forming).
Calculation: RSI smart condition = RSI above bullish threshold and below overbought limit with rising RSI-momentum EMA, or RSI below bearish threshold and above oversold limit with falling RSI-momentum EMA. The RSI-momentum EMA is calculated from close.
Calculation: Neural trigger = trail direction agrees, direction score ≥ minimum, Ultron Gate permits direction, and Fib gate passes; trigger event is a trail flip or a same-direction CHoCH whose zone confidence meets its minimum. Zone confidence is bypassed for trail-flip triggers.
Long: bullish CHoCH zone when close (or high if close confirmation is disabled) breaks the latest pivot high after a bearish break, and zone quality passes.
Short: bearish CHoCH zone when close (or low if close confirmation is disabled) breaks the latest pivot low after a bullish break, and zone quality passes.
Long: trail flips from bearish to bullish.
Short: trail flips from bullish to bearish.
Long: bullish RSI smart condition becomes true after being false, with enabled trail, Hull, and Fib direction filters satisfied.
Short: bearish RSI smart condition becomes true after being false, with enabled trail, Hull, and Fib direction filters satisfied.
Long: bullish neural trigger.
Short: bearish neural trigger.
Long: position is flat and, on a confirmed chart bar, bullish entry mode condition holds: Signal Change uses bullish neural trigger; Trail Retest requires a pending bullish trail flip, low ≤ its trail level, close ≥ that level, and bullish neural trigger; Zone Retest requires a pending bullish zone, a later bar within the retest window, low ≤ zone high, close > zone low, bullish trail direction, and bullish qualification (trail direction, score, Ultron Gate, Fib gate).
Short: position is flat and, on a confirmed chart bar, bearish entry mode condition holds: Signal Change uses bearish neural trigger; Trail Retest requires a pending bearish trail flip, high ≥ its trail level, close ≤ that level, and bearish neural trigger; Zone Retest requires a pending bearish zone, a later bar within the retest window, high ≥ zone low, close < zone high, bearish trail direction, and bearish qualification (trail direction, score, Ultron Gate, Fib gate).
Exit: long, confirmed close ≤ active stop (or low ≤ active stop when close confirmation is disabled) OR confirmed bearish trail flip. Active stop follows trail initially; after TP1 it is the greater of entry plus breakeven buffer and trail; after TP2 it is the greater of the TP1 price (if TP locking is enabled) or entry plus buffer, and trail; after TP3 it trails upward, never decreasing.
Exit: short, confirmed close ≥ active stop (or high ≥ active stop when close confirmation is disabled) OR confirmed bullish trail flip. Active stop follows trail initially; after TP1 it is the lesser of entry minus breakeven buffer and trail; after TP2 it is the lesser of the TP1 price (if TP locking is enabled) or entry minus buffer, and trail; after TP3 it trails downward, never increasing.
