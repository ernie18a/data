Calculation: Stochastic %K = 100 × (close − lowest low over its lookback) / (highest high over its lookback − lowest low over its lookback); %D = SMA(%K, its lookback)
Calculation: Fast EMA = EMA(close, shorter lookback than the slow EMA); Slow EMA = EMA(close, longer lookback)
Long: Fast EMA > Slow EMA AND close < Fast EMA AND close >= Slow EMA AND %K > %D AND previous %K <= previous %D AND (at least one %K in the configured lookback is below the oversold threshold; if the all-must option is enabled, the oldest %K in that lookback is below the threshold) AND position is flat AND (reference-market filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe reference close > its EMA OR previous higher-timeframe reference close > its EMA)
Short: Fast EMA < Slow EMA AND close > Fast EMA AND close <= Slow EMA AND %D > %K AND previous %D <= previous %K AND (at least one %K in the configured lookback is above the overbought threshold; if the all-must option is enabled, the oldest %K in that lookback is above the threshold) AND position is flat AND (reference-market filter is disabled OR higher-timeframe reference close < its EMA OR previous higher-timeframe reference close < its EMA)
Exit: long, low <= signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier OR high >= signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio
Exit: short, high >= signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier OR low <= signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier × risk-reward ratio
