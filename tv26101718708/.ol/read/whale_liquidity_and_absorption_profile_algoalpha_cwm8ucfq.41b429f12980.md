Calculation: Strong-volume threshold = the volume at ascending index floor(N × configured strength percentile / 100), capped at N−1, among lower-timeframe volume samples from the rolling chart-bar lookback.
Calculation: Price-bin delta = total volume of lower-timeframe bars closing above their open minus total volume of lower-timeframe bars closing at or below their open, grouped by close into equal-width bins spanning the lookback’s minimum to maximum lower-timeframe close.
Long: At least one lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar closes above its open and has volume ≥ the strong-volume threshold; OR the chart close lies in a price bin whose delta is positive and whose absolute delta equals the maximum absolute delta across bins.
Short: At least one lower-timeframe bar within the current chart bar closes at or below its open and has volume ≥ the strong-volume threshold; OR the chart close lies in a price bin whose delta is negative and whose absolute delta equals the maximum absolute delta across bins.
