Calculation: Long score = sum the configured importance weights for: close above the long-term EMA; short-term EMA rising while medium-term EMA falls; Supertrend up or reverses up; Parabolic SAR below close or flips below close; close above HMA or HMA pivots up; bullish regular divergence in RSI, MACD, WaveTrend, Stochastic, RVI, or OBV; RSI below 30 or crosses above 50; MACD histogram pivots up below zero or MACD crosses above its signal below zero; WaveTrend crosses above its signal below −10; Stochastic crosses above its signal; close below the lower Bollinger Band; ATR stop direction flips up; RVI rises under the specified 25/40 comparison; HMA crosses above any support/resistance level while close exceeds its close two bars earlier; CMF crosses above zero or is above zero; HMA crosses below VWAP or the medium-term EMA is above VWAP; bullish engulfing pattern. Regular divergence compares a lower price low with a higher oscillator low, or a higher price high with a lower oscillator high, confirmed by a pivot and a prior pivot 5–60 bars earlier; MACD bullish divergence additionally requires the oscillator pivots and recent oscillator range below zero, and bearish divergence requires them above zero. ATR stop direction uses a one-bar ATR stop with multiplier 1.85. Support/resistance levels use close-price pivots with 200 left bars and either 5 or 20 right bars; the HMA crossing condition applies to any of the eight latest/prior pivot levels. Bullish engulfing requires a small bearish candle followed by a larger bullish candle whose body engulfs the prior body, with close below the 50-period SMA.
Calculation: Short score = sum the configured importance weights for the corresponding bearish conditions: close below the long-term EMA; short-term EMA falling while medium-term EMA rises; Supertrend down or reverses down; Parabolic SAR above close or flips above close; close below HMA or HMA pivots down; bearish regular divergence in RSI, MACD, WaveTrend, Stochastic, RVI, or OBV; RSI above 70 or crosses below 50; MACD histogram pivots down above zero or MACD crosses below its signal above zero; WaveTrend crosses below its signal above 10; Stochastic crosses below its signal; open above the upper Bollinger Band; ATR stop direction flips down; RVI falls under the specified 75/60 comparison; HMA crosses below any support/resistance level while close is below its close two bars earlier; CMF crosses below zero or is below zero; HMA crosses above VWAP or the medium-term EMA is below VWAP; bearish engulfing pattern. Bearish engulfing requires a small bullish candle followed by a larger bearish candle whose body engulfs the prior body, with close above the 50-period SMA. Divergence and support/resistance pivot conditions use the same rules as for the long score.
