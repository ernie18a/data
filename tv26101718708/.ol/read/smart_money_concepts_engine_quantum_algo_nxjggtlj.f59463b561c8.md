Calculation: Confirmed swing highs/lows = highs/lows at the center of symmetric pivot lookbacks, available after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed internal highs/lows = same pivot calculation with a shorter lookback than swing pivots.
Calculation: Range position = (close − latest confirmed swing low) / (latest confirmed swing high − latest confirmed swing low).
Calculation: Demand zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bearish candle before a bullish swing break; supply zone = high-to-low range of the most recent bullish candle before a bearish swing break.
Calculation: Equal highs/lows = consecutive confirmed swing highs/lows whose difference is within an ATR-scaled tolerance.
Long: On a confirmed bar, close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high OR close > latest unbroken confirmed internal high OR low < an unswept confirmed swing low AND close > that swing low AND at least 5 bars since the previous single-swing sweep marking OR low < an active equal-low level AND close > that level OR a fresh demand zone is tapped (low ≤ zone top) AND close ≥ zone bottom AND swing bias is bullish AND internal bias is bullish AND range position < 0.5.
Short: On a confirmed bar, close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low OR close < latest unbroken confirmed internal low OR high > an unswept confirmed swing high AND close < that swing high AND at least 5 bars since the previous single-swing sweep marking OR high > an active equal-high level AND close < that level OR a fresh supply zone is tapped (high ≥ zone bottom) AND close ≤ zone top AND swing bias is bearish AND internal bias is bearish AND range position > 0.5.
