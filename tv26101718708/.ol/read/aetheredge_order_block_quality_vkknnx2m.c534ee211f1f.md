Calculation: Bullish order block = the high-to-low zone of the most recent bearish candle within the origin lookback when the close crosses above the previous swing-length highest high; bearish order block = the zone of the most recent bullish candle when the close crosses below the previous swing-length lowest low. Create a block only when absolute candle body / ATR exceeds the minimum displacement threshold. Keep at most the configured maximum number of active blocks, removing the oldest when exceeded.
Calculation: Order-block quality = logistic prediction from five features captured at block creation: clamped zone size / ATR; clamped absolute candle body / ATR; clamped tanh((short-term EMA − long-term EMA) / ATR) × block direction; clamped ((RSI − 50) / 50) × block direction; and clamped origin-candle volume / average volume. Initialize logistic weights to zero. After each block resolves, update them online using logistic prediction error, learning rate, and L2 regularization on feature weights (not the bias). Use the resulting score captured at creation for that block.
Long: On a confirmed bar after warmup, an unresolved bullish block has not previously been retested, low ≤ its top, and its creation-time quality ≥ the quality threshold.
Short: On a confirmed bar after warmup, an unresolved bearish block has not previously been retested, high ≥ its bottom, and its creation-time quality ≥ the quality threshold.
Calculation: Resolve each unresolved bullish block as broken when close < bottom, or held when it has been retested and close > top; resolve each unresolved bearish block as broken when close > top, or held when it has been retested and close < bottom. If neither occurs by the resolve horizon, label it held if retested and broken otherwise. Train the quality model once at resolution with held = 1 and broken = 0.
