Calculation: Closed structure resistance/support = high/low of the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Closed bias close and slow EMA = close and slow EMA from the last completed bar on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe; prior slow EMA = that EMA from the preceding completed bar.
Calculation: Bullish bias = closed bias close > closed bias slow EMA AND closed bias slow EMA > prior closed bias slow EMA; bearish bias = closed bias close < closed bias slow EMA AND closed bias slow EMA < prior closed bias slow EMA.
Calculation: Bullish expansion = fast EMA > slow EMA AND slow EMA >= its value 3 bars ago AND regime strength >= expansion threshold; bearish expansion = fast EMA < slow EMA AND slow EMA <= its value 3 bars ago AND regime strength >= expansion threshold.
Calculation: Regime strength = 100 × (0.35 × clamp(ADX(14,14)/40,0,1) + 0.30 × efficiency ratio + 0.20 × clamp((|fast EMA − slow EMA|/ATR)/1.25,0,1) + 0.15 × clamp((|slow EMA − slow EMA 3 bars ago|/ATR)/0.75,0,1)); efficiency ratio = |close − close N bars ago| / sum of |close − prior close| over N bars.
Calculation: Reaction zone = ATR × zone multiplier; support/resistance touch = bar range intersects the corresponding closed structure level ± reaction zone.
Calculation: Bull reaction = support touch AND close > closed structure support AND lower wick / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND close > open; bear reaction = resistance touch AND close < closed structure resistance AND upper wick / candle range >= minimum wick ratio AND close < open. Candle range = max(high − low, minimum tick); lower wick = min(open,close) − low; upper wick = high − max(open,close).
Calculation: Recent bull/bear reaction = a bull/bear reaction occurred within the configured reaction memory bars.
Calculation: Equilibrium = VWAP of hlc3 in scalping mode, otherwise fast EMA; equilibrium touch = bar range intersects equilibrium ± ATR × pullback tolerance.
Calculation: Bull pullback = (support touch OR equilibrium touch) AND close > open AND close >= equilibrium; bear pullback = (resistance touch OR equilibrium touch) AND close < open AND close <= equilibrium. Recent pullback = corresponding pullback occurred within the configured reaction memory bars.
Calculation: Bull momentum score = (25 if RSI >=55, else 18 if >=50, else 10 if >=45, else 0) + (20 if RSI > prior RSI) + (20 if EMA-smoothed 3-bar close change > 0) + (20 if fast EMA > slow EMA) + (15 if 3-bar rate of change > 0). Bear momentum score = (25 if RSI <=45, else 18 if <=50, else 10 if <=55, else 0) + (20 if RSI < prior RSI) + (20 if EMA-smoothed 3-bar close change < 0) + (20 if fast EMA < slow EMA) + (15 if 3-bar rate of change < 0).
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / 20-bar simple moving average of volume when valid volume data exists; otherwise volume confirmation passes. Volume gate = volume confirmation disabled OR volume unavailable OR relative volume >= minimum relative volume. Strong volume = volume unavailable OR relative volume >= strong-volume threshold.
Calculation: Micro breakout long/short = close > highest high / < lowest low over the preceding configured microstructure lookback bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Long/short structural risk = max(distance from close to (lowest low / highest high over configured stop lookback ± ATR padding), minimum tick). Long/short reward-risk = distance from close to closed structure resistance above close / long risk, or closed structure support below close / short risk; if the level is not beyond close, use the minimum reward-risk threshold. Long/short reward-risk passes when its ratio >= minimum threshold.
Calculation: M1 pulse score (lower timeframe than the chart timeframe; uses available intrabars within the current chart bar, including the latest available bar) = RSI component + candle component + breakout component + EMA component + volume component + RSI-rise/fall component. Bull RSI component = 35 if prior RSI <=30 and lower than the RSI two bars ago and current RSI rises; else 25 if prior RSI <=35 and >30 and current RSI rises; else 12 if current RSI >50 and rising; else 0. Bear RSI component is the inverse: 35 if prior RSI >=70 and higher than two bars ago and current RSI falls; else 25 if prior RSI >=65 and <70 and current RSI falls; else 12 if current RSI <50 and falling; else 0. Candle component = 10 if candle direction agrees; breakout component = 20 if close exceeds prior high (bull) or falls below prior low (bear); EMA component = 15 if 9-period EMA is above/below 21-period EMA; volume component = 10 if relative volume >=1; RSI-rise/fall component = 10 if RSI moves in the same direction for two consecutive changes.
Calculation: M1 bull/bear pulse = corresponding score >= pulse threshold AND (corresponding extreme RSI turn OR momentum RSI turn OR prior-bar breakout); high-quality pulse = pulse AND score >= high-quality threshold. M1 directional evidence = inspector inactive OR data available, minimum pulse count met, and (last pulse direction agrees OR best directional score exceeds the opposite score). Inspector is active in scalping mode when enabled; if active, directional evidence is required when pulse requirement is enabled.
Calculation: Continuation long score = min(100, (25 if bullish expansion, else 12 if bullish trend direction in transition, else 0) + (15 if bullish bias) + (15 if recent bull reaction, else 12 if recent bull pullback, else 0) + 0.20 × bull momentum score + (10 if micro breakout long) + (5 if strong volume) + (10 if long reward-risk passes) + M1 long score addition). Continuation short score is the symmetric bearish version. M1 score addition = 0 when inspector inactive, 10 when a high-quality pulse exists, else 6 when directional evidence passes, else 0.
Calculation: Reversal long score = min(100, (20 if bearish expansion occurred within reversal memory and is no longer active) + (20 if recent bull reaction) + 0.20 × bull momentum score + (15 if micro breakout long) + (10 if close > fast EMA) + (5 if strong volume) + (10 if long reward-risk passes) + M1 long score addition). Reversal short score is the symmetric bullish-to-bearish version.
Calculation: Fresh ready signal = ready now AND not ready on the prior bar. Entry allowed only when flat and the configured cooldown since the prior entry has elapsed; if both directions are fresh, choose the higher execution score, with ties going long.
Long: bull reaction OR (allowed continuation family AND bullish expansion AND bullish bias AND (recent bull reaction OR recent bull pullback) AND micro breakout long AND bull momentum score >= continuation minimum AND volume gate AND long reward-risk passes AND required M1 long evidence AND continuation long score >= entry minimum) OR (allowed reversal family AND bearish expansion occurred within reversal memory and is no longer active AND recent bull reaction AND close > fast EMA AND micro breakout long AND bull momentum score >= reversal minimum AND volume gate AND long reward-risk passes AND required M1 long evidence AND reversal long score >= entry minimum); entry signal must be fresh, flat, cooldown-eligible, and win the direction selection.
Short: bear reaction OR (allowed continuation family AND bearish expansion AND bearish bias AND (recent bear reaction OR recent bear pullback) AND micro breakout short AND bear momentum score >= continuation minimum AND volume gate AND short reward-risk passes AND required M1 short evidence AND continuation short score >= entry minimum) OR (allowed reversal family AND bullish expansion occurred within reversal memory and is no longer active AND recent bear reaction AND close < fast EMA AND micro breakout short AND bear momentum score >= reversal minimum AND volume gate AND short reward-risk passes AND required M1 short evidence AND reversal short score >= entry minimum); entry signal must be fresh, flat, cooldown-eligible, and win the direction selection.
Long: in scalping mode with M1 inspector active, any high-quality M1 bull pulse within the chart bar.
Short: in scalping mode with M1 inspector active, any high-quality M1 bear pulse within the chart bar.
Exit: long, at least 2 of (close < fast EMA, RSI < 50, EMA-smoothed 3-bar close change < 0) AND bull momentum score < 50, OR low <= structural stop, OR high >= third profit target; only after the entry bar.
Exit: short, at least 2 of (close > fast EMA, RSI > 50, EMA-smoothed 3-bar close change > 0) AND bear momentum score < 50, OR high >= structural stop, OR low <= third profit target; only after the entry bar.
