Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; confirm after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = candle body size or full high-low range is at least a parameter fraction of the 14-bar average true range.
Calculation: Bullish retracement levels = logarithmic retracements from the active higher low to higher high, with level price = higher high × (higher low / higher high)^level; the active anchors are set after bullish CHoCH or BOS and updated from subsequent confirmed swings.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = three consecutive bullish candles where the current low exceeds the high two bars earlier by at least a parameter minimum gap, the middle bar's high exceeds that earlier high and its low is below the current low, and the current high exceeds the middle high; discard when price reaches or crosses the gap's lower boundary.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish FVG = the same three-candle structure and middle-bar conditions on bars aggregated to a higher timeframe, with gap size at least a parameter fraction of that timeframe's 14-bar ATR; use the higher-timeframe bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish structure state = initialize bullish when price breaks a confirmed swing high; while bullish, a displaced break below the latest eligible confirmed swing low changes state to bearish, and otherwise a break above the highest confirmed swing high is bullish BOS. While bearish, a displaced break above the latest eligible confirmed swing high changes state to bullish, and otherwise a break below the lowest confirmed swing low is bearish BOS. A break is close above/below the level or high/low above/below it.
Calculation: Bullish discount area = close is between the logarithmic 0.618 and 0.886 retracement prices, or close is inside an unfilled bullish FVG on the chart timeframe or higher timeframe.
Long: bullish CHoCH break OR bullish BOS break OR bullish structure state AND close > open AND (close > previous bar high OR close crosses above the 0.705 retracement) AND bullish discount area AND close >= active higher low
Short: bearish CHoCH break OR bearish BOS break
Exit: long, close < active higher low
