Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the pivot-length bars before and after it; it is available after those later bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the pivot-length bars before and after it; it is available after those later bars complete.
Long: A newly confirmed pivot high is higher than the previous confirmed pivot high OR a confirmed pivot low forms OR low > high two bars ago AND (low − high two bars ago) / close × 100 ≥ minimum gap percentage OR low one bar ago < the lowest low over the sweep lookback ending one bar ago AND close > that lowest low AND close one bar ago < that lowest low
