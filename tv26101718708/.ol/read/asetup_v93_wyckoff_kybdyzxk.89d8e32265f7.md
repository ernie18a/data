Calculation: Bull score = count of: EMA(9) > EMA(21) > EMA(50); close > VWAP and VWAP > its value 3 bars ago; higher-timeframe EMA(9) > EMA(21) > EMA(50) (or true if the higher-timeframe filter is disabled); RSI within its configured bullish range; MACD line > signal line and MACD histogram > its prior value; qualified volume.
Calculation: Bear score = count of the symmetric bearish conditions: EMA(9) < EMA(21) < EMA(50); close < VWAP and VWAP < its value 3 bars ago; higher-timeframe EMA(9) < EMA(21) < EMA(50) (or true if the higher-timeframe filter is disabled); RSI within its configured bearish range; MACD line < signal line and MACD histogram < its prior value; qualified volume. The higher-timeframe EMAs use the last completed higher-timeframe bar; the short-term and long-term EMA lookbacks are ordered 9, 21, 50.
Calculation: Qualified volume = volume >= its 20-bar SMA times the configured multiplier, and, when expanding-volume is enabled, volume > prior-bar volume > volume two bars ago.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = bullish engulfing OR hammer OR breakout OR support bounce OR Wyckoff spring. Bullish engulfing = prior candle bearish, current candle bullish, current close > prior open, current open <= prior close, and current body larger than prior body. Hammer = lower wick > 2 times body, upper wick < 0.5 times body, body/range < 0.4, and close >= open. Breakout = close above the highest high of the prior 10 bars, close > open, and body/range > 0.5. Support bounce = low within the configured proximity of any of prior-day low/close, current-day low, EMA(21), VWAP, prior-week low, pre-session low, opening-range low, or latest confirmed swing low; close > open, body/range > 0.55, and close above the candle midpoint. Wyckoff spring = a low below the lowest low of the prior configured lookback, followed within 3 bars by close above that swept level and open, with qualified volume.
Calculation: Bearish pattern = bearish engulfing OR shooting star OR breakdown OR resistance rejection OR Wyckoff upthrust. Bearish engulfing = prior candle bullish, current candle bearish, current close < prior open, current open >= prior close, and current body larger than prior body. Shooting star = upper wick > 2 times body, lower wick < 0.5 times body, body/range < 0.4, and close <= open. Breakdown = close below the lowest low of the prior 10 bars, close < open, and body/range > 0.5. Resistance rejection = high within the configured proximity of any of prior-day high/close, current-day high, EMA(21), VWAP, prior-week high, pre-session high, opening-range high, or latest confirmed swing high; close < open, body/range > 0.55, and close below the candle midpoint. Wyckoff upthrust = a high above the highest high of the prior configured lookback, followed within 3 bars by close below that swept level and open, with qualified volume.
Calculation: Bull trend = at least 2 of: bullish EMA stack; close > VWAP; bullish higher-timeframe EMA stack (or true if the higher-timeframe filter is disabled). Bear trend = at least 2 of the symmetric bearish conditions. Higher-timeframe values use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bull score conditions and bear score conditions are each 6-item counts as defined above. Bullish/bearish RSI ranges, volume multiplier, Wyckoff lookback, proximity, time windows, and cooldown are configurable parameters.
Calculation: Initial long stop = entry price minus ATR times configured stop multiplier when ATR stops are enabled; otherwise the lesser of current low, prior-bar low, and EMA(21), multiplied by one minus the configured stop buffer. Initial short stop = entry price plus ATR times configured stop multiplier when ATR stops are enabled; otherwise the greater of current high, prior-bar high, and EMA(21), multiplied by one plus the configured stop buffer. Initial risk = distance from entry price to initial stop. First and second targets are entry price plus (for long) or minus (for short) initial risk times their respective configured reward-to-risk parameters.
Long: Bull score = 6 AND bullish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bull trend) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior A/B long signal AND outside configured blocked time windows AND outside the MOC window.
Long: Bull score is 4 or 5 AND bullish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bull trend) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior A/B long signal AND outside configured blocked time windows AND outside the MOC window.
Long: In the MOC window AND bull score >= 4 AND bullish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bull trend) AND qualified volume AND (prior-day high unavailable OR close below prior-day high by the configured clearance threshold) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior MOC signal AND no A/B long signal on this bar.
Long: A bearish signal occurred within the prior 20 bars AND bull score >= 4 AND bullish pattern AND bull trend AND outside configured blocked time windows AND neither a long nor short position is open.
Long: Wyckoff spring condition is true.
Short: Bear score = 6 AND bearish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bear trend) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior A/B short signal AND outside configured blocked time windows AND outside the MOC window.
Short: Bear score is 4 or 5 AND bearish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bear trend) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior A/B short signal AND outside configured blocked time windows AND outside the MOC window.
Short: In the MOC window AND bear score >= 4 AND bearish pattern AND (strict trend filter disabled OR bear trend) AND qualified volume AND (prior-day low unavailable OR close above prior-day low by the configured clearance threshold) AND at least the configured cooldown bars since the prior MOC signal AND no A/B short signal on this bar.
Short: A bullish signal occurred within the prior 20 bars AND bear score >= 4 AND bearish pattern AND bear trend AND outside configured blocked time windows AND neither a long nor short position is open.
Short: Wyckoff upthrust condition is true.
Exit: Long, high >= first target (move stop to entry price) OR high >= second target OR low <= current stop OR (bearish EMA stack AND close < VWAP AND bearish pattern) OR configured time-stop bars held.
Exit: Short, low <= first target (move stop to entry price) OR low <= second target OR high >= current stop OR (bullish EMA stack AND close > VWAP AND bullish pattern) OR configured time-stop bars held.
