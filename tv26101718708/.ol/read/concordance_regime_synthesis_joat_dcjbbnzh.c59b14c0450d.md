Calculation: Regime state = Track the most recently confirmed pivot high and pivot low, each confirmed after the configured number of bars on both sides; use the current high or low, respectively, until a pivot exists. Compute the center as a smoothed moving average of the mean of those two levels. Compute upper and lower bands as smoothed moving averages of center plus or minus ATR times a configurable multiplier. When the close crosses above the upper band, queue bullish state; when it crosses below the lower band, queue bearish state. Apply a queued state after its configurable hold interval, on a confirmed bar. Until state is set, initialize it bullish when close is at or above center, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Normalized regime distance = (close - regime center) / ATR; regime strength = absolute value of normalized regime distance.
Calculation: Pressure signal = EMA of ((close - configurable moving average of close) / maximum of the highest absolute moving-average distance over a longer lookback and minimum tick size) × 100. The moving-average type is configurable; the normalization lookback is longer than the pressure-signal EMA lookback.
Calculation: Participation axis = Divide the high-low range over a configurable lookback into configurable equal price bins; add each bar's volume to the bin containing its close. The axis is the midpoint of the lowest-price bin with the greatest volume.
Calculation: Normalized axis deviation = (close - participation axis) / ATR.
Calculation: Delta wave = EMA, over a configurable smoothing lookback, of the EMA of (up-bar volume minus down-bar volume) / EMA of volume × 100. Up-bar volume is volume when close > open; down-bar volume is volume when close < open; otherwise each is zero. Delta slope = current delta wave minus prior bar's delta wave.
Calculation: Recent bullish structure = Within the configurable validity lookback, either a confirmed bullish impulse occurred (bar two bars ago closed below its open, prior bar closed above its open, and prior-bar body is at least prior-bar ATR times a configurable multiplier) or a bullish gap occurred (current low > high two bars ago and prior close > high two bars ago).
Calculation: Recent bearish structure = Within the configurable validity lookback, either a confirmed bearish impulse occurred (bar two bars ago closed above its open, prior bar closed below its open, and prior-bar body is at least prior-bar ATR times a configurable multiplier) or a bearish gap occurred (current high < low two bars ago and prior close < low two bars ago).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bullish bias = On the last completed bar of a valid timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, the 21-period EMA of close is above the 55-period EMA of close, and that bar's close is above the 55-period EMA.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bearish bias = On the last completed bar of a valid timeframe equal to or higher than the chart timeframe, the 21-period EMA of close is below the 55-period EMA of close, and that bar's close is below the 55-period EMA.
Calculation: Long score = 2 if regime state is bullish, plus 1 each if normalized regime distance >= 0, regime strength >= configurable threshold, pressure signal > 0, delta wave > 0, delta slope >= 0, recent bullish structure is true, close > participation axis, normalized axis deviation > 0, and higher-timeframe filter is disabled or timeframe invalid or higher-timeframe bullish bias is true.
Calculation: Short score = 2 if regime state is bearish, plus 1 each if normalized regime distance <= 0, regime strength >= configurable threshold, pressure signal < 0, delta wave < 0, delta slope <= 0, recent bearish structure is true, close < participation axis, normalized axis deviation < 0, and higher-timeframe filter is disabled or timeframe invalid or higher-timeframe bearish bias is true.
Calculation: Score spread = long score - short score.
Calculation: Long stop = Initially the lesser of entry price - ATR × configurable stop multiplier and regime center - ATR × 0.25; on each bar, if trailing is enabled, raise it to the greater of its prior value and close - ATR × configurable trail multiplier. If trailing is disabled, retain the prior stop.
Calculation: Short stop = Initially the greater of entry price + ATR × configurable stop multiplier and regime center + ATR × 0.25; on each bar, if trailing is enabled, lower it to the lesser of its prior value and close + ATR × configurable trail multiplier. If trailing is disabled, retain the prior stop.
Calculation: Long target = Entry price + ATR × configurable target multiplier.
Calculation: Short target = Entry price - ATR × configurable target multiplier.
Long: bar is confirmed AND (session filter is disabled OR bar is within configured session) AND (no prior entry OR bars since prior entry >= configurable cooldown) AND long score >= configurable confluence threshold AND score spread >= 2 AND position size <= 0
Short: bar is confirmed AND (session filter is disabled OR bar is within configured session) AND (no prior entry OR bars since prior entry >= configurable cooldown) AND short score >= configurable confluence threshold AND score spread <= -2 AND position size >= 0
Exit: long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR regime state is bearish OR score spread <= 0 OR pressure signal < 0 OR close < regime center
Exit: short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR regime state is bullish OR score spread >= 0 OR pressure signal > 0 OR close > regime center
