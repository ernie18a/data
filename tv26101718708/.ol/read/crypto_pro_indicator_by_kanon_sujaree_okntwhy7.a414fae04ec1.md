Calculation: Directional confidence = rounded weighted sum of VWAP, RSI, MACD, ADX, structure, EMA, volume, and divergence scores. VWAP, RSI, and MACD have equal major weights; structure, EMA, volume, and divergence have equal minor weights. For each direction, VWAP scores full weight when price is on that side of VWAP and the bar is near that side’s band, half weight when price is on that side only, otherwise zero. RSI scores full weight when the chart-timeframe RSI is beyond its directional extreme and the last completed higher-timeframe RSI is on that side of 50; half weight when chart-timeframe RSI is between 50 and its extreme and the higher-timeframe RSI is on that side of 50; otherwise zero. MACD scores full weight on a directional MACD/signal-line crossover, half weight when the MACD line is on that side of the signal line and the histogram is beyond zero and moving further in that direction, otherwise zero. ADX contributes a tiered trend-strength score, with stronger ADX tiers scoring more and the weakest tier scoring zero; award the full tier score when directional DI is greater than the opposite DI, otherwise half. Structure scores full minor weight on a close crossing the last confirmed swing high or low in that direction, half when the most recent such break state is in that direction, otherwise zero; initialize the break state bullish. EMA scores full minor weight when close is above (below) EMA and EMA is rising (falling) over a shorter lag, half when close is above (below) EMA only, otherwise zero. Volume scores full minor weight for a directional candle with volume in the configured high-volume band, half for a directional candle with volume in the configured normal-volume band, otherwise zero. Bullish divergence requires a lower price low than the prior lookback minimum, a higher RSI than its prior lookback minimum, and RSI below its midpoint; bearish divergence is the reverse using highs and RSI above its midpoint. Divergence scores full minor weight when present, otherwise zero. Use configurable thresholds and bands; within each indicator, full scores exceed half scores.
Calculation: VWAP bands = session VWAP plus or minus a configurable multiple of the standard deviation of HLC3 minus VWAP over a rolling lookback. A bar is near the lower band when its low is at or below the band with a configurable tolerance; it is near the upper band when its high is at or above the band with a configurable tolerance.
Calculation: Structure = the most recent confirmed swing high and swing low, each requiring the same lookback on both sides; a break occurs when close crosses the respective level.
Long: Buy confidence reaches or exceeds its threshold after being below it on the prior bar AND the previous signal was not buy.
Exit: long, Sell confidence reaches or exceeds its threshold after being below it on the prior bar AND the previous signal was buy.
