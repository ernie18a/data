Calculation: Fast, mid, and slow EMA = exponential moving averages of close, with progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: Normalized SRSI = for each of four higher timeframes, calculate Stochastic RSI on close as smoothed K plus smoothed D; average the four values and scale to 0–100. Use the current higher-timeframe bar while it is forming, and the latest available completed higher-timeframe bar in historical data.
Long: After fast EMA crosses above mid EMA or slow EMA, track its peak while it rises; after two consecutive declines, require peak minus fast EMA to reach the pullback fraction of peak minus the fast EMA at the cross; then, when fast EMA reaches peak minus that impulse height times (1 minus the early-retest fraction), require mid EMA > slow EMA. This is the original long marking.
Long: The original long condition AND a confirmed momentum low occurred within the last week of chart bars. A momentum low occurs after Normalized SRSI reaches the high watch level and then the midpoint, and is confirmed after the configured number of bars without a new lower value.
Short: After fast EMA crosses below mid EMA or slow EMA, track its trough while it falls; after two consecutive rises, require fast EMA minus trough to reach the pullback fraction of fast EMA at the cross minus trough; then, when fast EMA reaches trough plus that impulse height times (1 minus the early-retest fraction), require mid EMA < slow EMA. This is the original short marking.
Short: The original short condition AND a confirmed momentum high occurred within the last week of chart bars. A momentum high occurs after Normalized SRSI reaches the low watch level and then the midpoint, and is confirmed after the configured number of bars without a new higher value.
