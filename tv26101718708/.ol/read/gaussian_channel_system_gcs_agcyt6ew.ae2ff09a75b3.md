Calculation: Gaussian moving average = Gaussian-weighted average of the current and preceding source prices over a lookback window, with weights determined by a Gaussian kernel
Calculation: Upper channel = Gaussian moving average + residual standard deviation × channel-width multiplier, where residual = source price − Gaussian moving average
Calculation: Lower channel = Gaussian moving average − residual standard deviation × channel-width multiplier
Calculation: Mid-upper channel = Gaussian moving average + half of the channel-width multiplier × residual standard deviation
Calculation: Mid-lower channel = Gaussian moving average − half of the channel-width multiplier × residual standard deviation
Calculation: Normalized Gaussian slope = (current Gaussian moving average − Gaussian moving average 5 bars earlier) ÷ (5 × ATR)
Calculation: Z-score = (source price − lookback mean of source price) ÷ lookback standard deviation of source price
Long: (low ≤ mid-lower channel AND normalized Gaussian slope > positive slope threshold AND previous bar Z-score < lower Z-score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous long signal) OR (close crossed above upper channel AND ADX > trending threshold AND +DI > −DI AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous long signal) OR (fast EMA crossed above slow EMA AND close > Gaussian moving average AND ADX > trending threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous long signal)
Short: (high ≥ mid-upper channel AND normalized Gaussian slope < negative slope threshold AND previous bar Z-score > upper Z-score threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous short signal) OR (close crossed below lower channel AND ADX > trending threshold AND −DI > +DI AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous short signal) OR (fast EMA crossed below slow EMA AND close < Gaussian moving average AND ADX > trending threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > 1.1 × 20-bar average volume) AND more than the cooldown interval since the previous short signal)
