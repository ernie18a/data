Calculation: Mother-bar range = high and low of the bar immediately before the inside bar; during an active setup, extend its high when the current high exceeds it while max(open, close) remains at or below the boundary, and extend its low when the current low falls below it while min(open, close) remains at or above the boundary. The setup begins when current high <= prior high and current low >= prior low, and ends after a breakout signal.
Calculation: Breakout price = current high for Touch mode or current close for Bar Close mode; downside breakout price = current low for Touch mode or current close for Bar Close mode.
Calculation: Stop and target levels = entry price plus or minus ATR measured at entry times the configured stop multiple or target multiple; the seven target multiples increase from the shortest to the longest.
Long: active setup AND breakout price > mother-bar high AND (EMA filtering disabled OR selected EMA 9 filter with high > EMA 9 OR selected EMA 26 filter with high > EMA 26 OR selected EMA Cross (9/26) filter with EMA 9 > EMA 26)
Short: active setup AND downside breakout price < mother-bar low AND (EMA filtering disabled OR selected EMA 9 filter with low < EMA 9 OR selected EMA 26 filter with low < EMA 26 OR selected EMA Cross (9/26) filter with EMA 9 < EMA 26)
Exit: long, after the entry bar, low <= stop level OR high >= any target level OR (target 1 and stop not previously hit AND downside breakout price < mother-bar low)
Exit: short, after the entry bar, high >= stop level OR low <= any target level OR (target 1 and stop not previously hit AND breakout price > mother-bar high)
