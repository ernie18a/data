Calculation: ATR = average true range over its configured lookback.
Calculation: Box levels = collect confirmed pivot highs and lows; for each side, scan pivots newest to oldest within the configured lookback, use the newest as anchor, and pair it with the first older pivot at least the configured bar gap away and within ATR × touch tolerance; the level is their average. A candidate box requires both levels and top > bottom.
Calculation: Active box = create only when no box is active, candidate height is within configured ATR bounds, close is within 0.1 ATR of both bounds, and the fraction of closes inside the exact bounds over the configured lookback meets the configured minimum. Freeze its bounds until an upward breakout, downward breakdown, or age greater than the configured maximum.
Calculation: Upward breakout = on a completed chart bar, the configured number of latest closes are each above active-box top + current ATR × breakout buffer, and current close's position within its high-low range meets the configured minimum.
Calculation: Initial stop = select by configured stop mode: entry − ATR × volatility multiplier; box bottom − ATR × structural multiplier; box bottom reduced by the configured percentage; box midpoint; or, for the mixed mode, the higher of the volatility and structural ATR stops. The volatility multiplier is selected from four configured multipliers using entry ATR/close percent and three ascending thresholds.
Calculation: Risk = the greater of entry price − initial stop and ATR × configured minimum risk multiple.
Calculation: TP1 = box top + box height × configured multiple. TP2 = entry price + risk × configured multiple.
Calculation: Break-even stop = entry price + risk × configured protection multiple. Runner stop = initial stop before TP1 is hit; break-even stop after TP1 and before TP2; thereafter the greater of break-even stop and highest close since entry − ATR × configured trailing multiple.
Long: upward breakout AND flat position AND risk > 0 AND valid positive quantity that can be split into three configured portions of at least one unit each.
Exit: long, high >= TP1 OR low <= initial stop OR high >= TP2 OR low <= (TP1 hit ? break-even stop : initial stop) OR low <= runner stop.
