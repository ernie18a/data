Calculation: ATR regime score = normalize current ATR / its longer-term average over its own longer lookback to 0–100 using its rolling minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Bandwidth score = normalize Bollinger Band width to 0–100 using its rolling minimum and maximum; fast bandwidth score uses a shorter normalization lookback.
Calculation: Historical-volatility score = normalize the standard deviation of log close-to-close returns to 0–100 using its rolling minimum and maximum.
Calculation: Relative volume = volume / its moving average.
Calculation: ATR expansion = ATR / its moving average; ATR is rising when this ratio exceeds its prior-bar value and accelerating when its increase exceeds the prior increase. Bandwidth is rising or accelerating by the same prior-bar comparisons.
Calculation: Direction = bullish when close > fast EMA > slow EMA and ROC > 0; bearish when close < fast EMA < slow EMA and ROC < 0. The slow EMA uses a longer lookback than the fast EMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish impulse = candle body points in that direction, close is beyond the fast EMA in that direction, and body size / ATR exceeds an impulse threshold. Strong impulse additionally closes beyond the prior bar's high/low.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish micro-breakout = confirmed close exceeds the prior rolling high/low over a short lookback, is beyond the fast EMA in that direction, and the fast EMA is rising/falling. A breakout is recent while it occurred within the recent-bar window.
Calculation: Momentum acceleration = ROC is increasing and its current change exceeds its prior change for bullish acceleration; ROC is decreasing and its current change is more negative than its prior change for bearish acceleration.
Calculation: Fast score = weighted composite of fast ATR and bandwidth scores, impulse, momentum acceleration, breakout, and relative-volume scores. Confirmed score = weighted composite of ATR, bandwidth, historical-volatility, relative-volume, and ATR/bandwidth acceleration scores. Fast signal and confirmed signal are smoothed scores.
Calculation: Bullish and bearish setup scores = point totals for, respectively, close above/below fast EMA, fast EMA above/below slow EMA, fast EMA rising/falling, ROC positive/negative, directional momentum acceleration, directional impulse, recent directional breakout, directional setup when fast score is elevated, candle direction when relative volume is elevated, and directional momentum acceleration when ATR and bandwidth rise. A current breakout receives more points than a merely recent breakout.
Calculation: Directional dominance = bullish setup score exceeds bearish setup score by a required margin, or bearish setup score exceeds bullish setup score by that margin.
Calculation: Compression = any of: Bollinger Bands inside Keltner Channels; ATR and bandwidth scores both below their compression thresholds; confirmed score below its compression threshold. Compression exit = confirmed bar is no longer compressed, prior bar was compressed, and the compression lasted for a minimum number of bars.
Calculation: False-expansion risk = risk points for low relative volume, non-rising ATR, non-rising bandwidth, small body / ATR, confirmed score below compression threshold, and no recent directional breakout, reduced for higher-timeframe expansion, strong dominance, aligned direction and dominance, and aligned recent breakout. Higher-timeframe regime uses the last completed higher-timeframe bar. Risk is very high at the highest risk threshold; continuation is allowed only at low or medium risk.
Calculation: Ignition points = count of: ATR rising, bandwidth rising, directional impulse, directional momentum acceleration, recent directional breakout, fast score above its early threshold, close on the directional side of fast EMA, and compression exit.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish armed cycle = confirmed bar, neutral cycle, cooldown elapsed, fast score above its early threshold, corresponding setup score above its minimum, corresponding directional dominance, and at least one of corresponding momentum acceleration, recent breakout, or direction. Cooldown and minimum cycle age are measured in chart bars. Opposite-cycle invalidation resets the cycle when the opposing setup score exceeds its reversal threshold, has dominance and a recent breakout, fast score is above its early threshold, and risk is not very high.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish ignition = confirmed bar in the corresponding armed cycle, cooldown elapsed, directional dominance, risk not very high, and either (directional ignition points meet their minimum and a recent directional breakout or compression exit or upward cross of the early fast-score threshold occurs) OR (fast score meets the fast-breakout threshold, directional setup score meets its fast minimum, relative volume meets its fast threshold, and a recent directional breakout exists). An ignition starts the corresponding directional cycle and cooldown.
Calculation: Release quality = capped base quality from points for fast and confirmed scores above their expansion thresholds, ATR and bandwidth acceleration, elevated relative volume, recent directional breakout, directional direction, and directional dominance, adjusted by the last completed higher-timeframe regime (bonus for expansion/extreme, penalty for compression).
Long: bullish ignition OR confirmed bullish release (bullish cycle is in ignition state, confirmed score >= expansion threshold, bullish setup score >= bearish setup score, and bullish direction or recent bullish breakout) OR bullish continuation OR bullish invalidation of a bearish cycle.
Short: bearish ignition OR confirmed bearish release (bearish cycle is in ignition state, confirmed score >= expansion threshold, bearish setup score >= bullish setup score, and bearish direction or recent bearish breakout) OR bearish continuation OR bearish invalidation of a bullish cycle.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish continuation = confirmed bar, corresponding cycle direction, continuation has not already fired, corresponding macro lock is clear, bars since ignition meet a minimum, fast score and corresponding setup score meet their minimums, directional dominance meets its minimum, corresponding direction holds, close is beyond fast EMA, fast score is reaccelerating and corresponding setup score is rising, release quality meets its minimum, risk allows continuation, and no same-side ignition or release occurs on this bar. A continuation sets the corresponding macro lock until opposite direction and dominance occur.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish invalidation = confirmed bar in the opposite cycle, opposing setup score meets its reversal minimum, opposing dominance and recent breakout hold, fast score meets its early threshold, and risk is not very high. It resets the cycle and invalidates the prior opposite-direction cycle.
