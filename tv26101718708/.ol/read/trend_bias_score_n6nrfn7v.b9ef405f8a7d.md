Calculation: Trend Bias Score = weighted average across six configured timeframes, ordered from shorter to longer; for each timeframe, use its current bar, including a still-forming bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe. Each timeframe score is a weighted average of: trend score (weighted signs of close above the short-term moving average, short-term above medium-term, and medium-term above long-term); RSI momentum normalized around 50; MACD histogram acceleration (+1 when the histogram increases, otherwise −1); and energy score. Energy score is a weighted average of volume bias (signed volume is positive for close ≥ open and negative otherwise; if signed volume exceeds its longer-term EMA, use the candle direction as +1/−1, otherwise 0) and ATR bias (+1 when ATR exceeds its EMA, otherwise −1). Omit open-interest behavior because it is not available in the specified backtest data.
