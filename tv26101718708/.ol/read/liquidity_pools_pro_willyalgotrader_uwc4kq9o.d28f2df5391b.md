Calculation: Adaptive pivot lookback = configured manual lookback, or a rounded, bounded inverse function of the ratio of short-term ATR to long-term ATR; confirm pivots using this lookback to the left and the configured confirmation lookback to the right.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = confirmed pivots of the same type (highs or lows) whose prices are within ATR × equality tolerance and whose pivot ages are within the maximum matching lookback; pool level is the average of its matched pivot prices, updated as new pivots match; pool zone bounds are the level ± half the current ATR-based equality tolerance. Add volume from each subsequent bar that touches or crosses the zone.
Calculation: Pool strength = clamp to 0–100 the sum of a capped touch-count score, a recency score that decays by half-life, a capped logarithmic score of pool cumulative volume divided by the median cumulative volume of tracked pools, and an optional bonus when a higher-timeframe pivot is within 1.5 × equality tolerance. Use only the last completed higher-timeframe bar for pivots; compare pivots of the same type.
Calculation: Sweep stop distance = ATR × configured stop multiplier, or, when sweep-wick placement is selected and its distance is at least 0.1 × ATR, the distance from signal close beyond the signal-bar wick plus an ATR buffer; otherwise use the ATR-based distance. Entry = signal-bar close. Initial stop = entry minus (long) or plus (short) the stop distance. TP1/TP2/TP3 = entry plus (long) or minus (short) stop distance × the respective target multiplier.
Long: confirmed bar AND warmed up AND active low-side pool AND bar index ≥ last pool touch index + pivot confirmation lookback + 1 AND low < pool zone bottom AND close > pool level AND pool strength ≥ signal threshold AND (no active position OR current position's stop or TP3 is reached).
Short: confirmed bar AND warmed up AND active high-side pool AND bar index ≥ last pool touch index + pivot confirmation lookback + 1 AND high > pool zone top AND close < pool level AND pool strength ≥ signal threshold AND (no active position OR current position's stop or TP3 is reached).
Exit: long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ TP3.
Exit: short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ TP3.
