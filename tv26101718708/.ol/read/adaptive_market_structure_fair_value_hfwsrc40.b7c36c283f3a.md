Calculation: Price efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute close changes over that lookback; smooth it with an exponential moving average.
Calculation: Adaptive pivot confirmation delay = interpolate between the minimum and maximum right-side bar counts, decreasing as smoothed price efficiency rises.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a bar whose high exceeds the highs of the preceding left-side lookback and is not exceeded by any high during its adaptive right-side confirmation bars.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a bar whose low is below the lows of the preceding left-side lookback and is not undercut by any low during its adaptive right-side confirmation bars.
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative price-volume product divided by cumulative volume since the selected session anchor, using the selected price source.
Calculation: VWAP displacement = current session VWAP minus its value at a lookback; normalize by a noise gate based on smoothed true range, with the shorter chart timeframe using a longer displacement lookback than the higher chart timeframe.
Calculation: VWAP trend = before sufficient session history, the sign of VWAP displacement; afterward, positive or negative displacement exceeding half its rolling standard deviation, respectively, with smoothed VWAP efficiency above its qualification threshold.
Calculation: Adaptive KAMA = recursive adaptive moving average of close, with responsiveness controlled by smoothed close efficiency, using a shorter efficiency lookback than its slow smoothing length.
Calculation: Normalized KAMA slope = the five-bar linear-regression progression of adaptive KAMA divided by a noise gate based on smoothed true range; qualify its direction by multiplying by smoothed close efficiency.
Long: A confirmed pivot low forms OR a confirmed close exceeds an unbroken confirmed pivot high at any selected structural depth OR the KAMA state is bullish (qualified KAMA slope positive and efficiency at least the transition threshold), subject to the price-acceptance filter on entry from range or reversal from bearish state, OR during the active VWAP session on a chart timeframe lower than the selected structure timeframe, adaptive KAMA crosses above session VWAP on a confirmed bar, OR VWAP has a bullish trend and close is above session VWAP.
Short: A confirmed pivot high forms OR a confirmed close falls below an unbroken confirmed pivot low at any selected structural depth OR the KAMA state is bearish (qualified KAMA slope negative and efficiency at least the transition threshold), subject to the price-acceptance filter on entry from range or reversal from bullish state, OR during the active VWAP session on a chart timeframe lower than the selected structure timeframe, adaptive KAMA crosses below session VWAP on a confirmed bar, OR VWAP has a bearish trend and close is below session VWAP.
