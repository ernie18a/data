Calculation: Long WT = EMA(EMA((selected OHLC source − EMA(source, channel length)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(source − EMA(source, channel length)), channel length)), average length)); its signal is the 4-bar SMA of Long WT.
Calculation: Short WT = the same WaveTrend calculation with its own selected source and channel and average lengths; its signal is the 4-bar SMA of Short WT.
Calculation: Hull A = Kahlman-smoothed HMA of the selected source, where HMA(x,L) = WMA(2×WMA(x,L/2) − WMA(x,L), round(sqrt(L))). Hull B = Kahlman-smoothed WMA(3×WMA(close,L/6) − WMA(close,L/4) − WMA(close,L/2), L/2). For Kahlman smoothing, with input x, gain g, prior output k and prior velocity v: d=x−k; output k+d×sqrt(2g/10000)+v+gd/10000; new velocity v+gd/10000. Initialize k=x and v=0. Use the same length and gain for Hull A and Hull B.
Calculation: Selected OHLC source = the configured choice of open, high, low, close, or their standard bar averages.
Long: Hull B > Hull A AND Hull B[1] < Hull A[1] AND (Long WT crosses above its signal now OR Long WT[1] crosses above its signal[1] OR Long WT[2] crosses above its signal) AND (Stochastic RSI K > D when enabled, otherwise the configured fallback) AND (volume > the configured multiplier × the mean of volume SMA, volume VWMA, and volume WMA when enabled, otherwise the configured fallback) AND (ADX > configured level when enabled, otherwise ADX < 100) AND within the configured date/session/weekday filter.
Short: Hull A > Hull B AND Hull A[1] < Hull B[1] AND Short WT crosses below its signal AND (Stochastic RSI K < D when enabled, otherwise the configured fallback) AND (volume > the configured multiplier × the mean of volume SMA, volume VWMA, and volume WMA when enabled, otherwise the configured fallback) AND (ADX > configured level when enabled, otherwise ADX < 100) AND within the configured date/session/weekday filter.
Exit: long, low <= the active long stop OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured long take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured long second take-profit percentage) OR high >= entry price × (1 + configured long third take-profit percentage) OR (high <= entry price OR high >= long position-check level) AND (Short entry condition OR long position-check condition) OR long take-profit source <= first take-profit level after high reaches that level OR long second take-profit source <= second take-profit level after high reaches that level OR Hull B > Hull A after high reaches the second take-profit level.
Exit: short, high >= the active short stop OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured short take-profit percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured short second take-profit percentage) OR low <= entry price × (1 − configured short third take-profit percentage) OR (low >= entry price OR low <= short position-check level) AND (Long entry condition OR short position-check condition) OR short take-profit source >= first take-profit level after low reaches that level OR short second take-profit source >= second take-profit level after low reaches that level OR Hull A > Hull B after low reaches the second take-profit level.
