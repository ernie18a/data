Calculation: Bullish confluence score = sum of 1 for each true condition: fast EMA > slow EMA and close > slow EMA; MACD line > MACD signal; RSI > 50 and < its upper threshold; ADX > its minimum and +DI > -DI; volume > volume SMA × its multiplier; higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA
Calculation: Bearish confluence score = sum of 1 for each true condition: fast EMA < slow EMA and close < slow EMA; MACD line < MACD signal; RSI < 50 and > its lower threshold; ADX > its minimum and -DI > +DI; volume > volume SMA × its multiplier; higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment score = sum of 1 for each true condition: higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA (bullish) or < (bearish); higher-timeframe close > its EMA (bullish) or < (bearish); higher-timeframe RSI > 50 (bullish) or < 50 (bearish). Use a higher timeframe's still-forming bar; fast EMA has a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Bullish phase = bullish score ≥ entry threshold and > bearish score; absolute difference between scores ≥ neutral-gap threshold; close > VWAP when VWAP filter is enabled; datetime is within configured session when session filter is enabled; bars since last exit > cooldown when cooldown is enabled; absolute fast/slow EMA difference > ATR × minimum gap when chop filter is enabled; higher-timeframe bullish alignment score ≥ 2 when that filter is enabled; when structure filter is enabled, the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows are each rising; when liquidity filter is enabled, current low < lowest low over the prior lookback and close > that prior low, or close > prior lookback high; when candle filter is enabled, close > open, body/range ≥ minimum body percentage, and close ≥ high − 0.30 × range.
Calculation: Bearish phase = bearish score ≥ entry threshold and > bullish score; absolute difference between scores ≥ neutral-gap threshold; close < VWAP when VWAP filter is enabled; datetime is within configured session when session filter is enabled; bars since last exit > cooldown when cooldown is enabled; absolute fast/slow EMA difference > ATR × minimum gap when chop filter is enabled; higher-timeframe bearish alignment score ≥ 2 when that filter is enabled; when structure filter is enabled, the latest two confirmed pivot highs and latest two confirmed pivot lows are each falling; when liquidity filter is enabled, current high > prior lookback high and close < that high, or close < prior lookback low; when candle filter is enabled, close < open, body/range ≥ minimum body percentage, and close ≤ low + 0.30 × range.
Calculation: Trailing stop = on long entry, entry close − ATR × trailing multiplier; on each subsequent long bar, maximum of prior trailing stop and close − ATR × trailing multiplier. On short entry, entry close + ATR × trailing multiplier; on each subsequent short bar, minimum of prior trailing stop and close + ATR × trailing multiplier.
Long: bar is confirmed and bullish phase and position phase is not long
Short: bar is confirmed and bearish phase and position phase is not short
Exit: long, bar is confirmed and (bearish phase or bullish score ≤ exit threshold or close < VWAP or close < trailing stop)
Exit: short, bar is confirmed and (bullish phase or bearish score ≤ exit threshold or close > VWAP or close > trailing stop)
