Calculation: Momentum = EMA of the close rate of change; optionally multiply by min(volume / average volume, 2)
Calculation: Confirmed positive momentum peak = a momentum pivot high above the minimum strength, separated from the prior accepted positive peak by at least the minimum spacing; confirmed negative momentum trough = a momentum pivot low below the negative minimum strength, with the same spacing rule
Calculation: Positive and negative momentum levels = most recent confirmed positive peak and negative trough, respectively; corresponding price levels = high and low of each peak/trough bar
Calculation: Sweep threshold = configured percentage of the absolute momentum level or configured absolute distance
Calculation: First EMA = EMA of close on the chart timeframe; second EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Long stop = second EMA at entry; long target = entry + configured reward-risk multiple × (entry − long stop); short stop = second EMA at entry; short target = entry − configured reward-risk multiple × (short stop − entry)
Long: Momentum > 0 AND momentum crosses above the previous positive momentum level AND close > its corresponding price level AND first EMA > second EMA AND (momentum expansion filter disabled OR momentum exceeds the previous positive level by at least its configured percentage) AND (breakout displacement filter disabled OR (close − corresponding price level) / ATR meets its configured minimum) AND (efficiency filter disabled OR efficiency ratio meets its configured minimum) AND cooldown elapsed AND same-direction spacing elapsed AND opposite-direction spacing elapsed AND no live trade AND (close > second EMA for a trade to open)
Short: Momentum < 0 AND momentum crosses below the previous negative momentum level AND close < its corresponding price level AND first EMA < second EMA AND (momentum expansion filter disabled OR previous negative level exceeds momentum by at least its configured percentage) AND (breakout displacement filter disabled OR (corresponding price level − close) / ATR meets its configured minimum) AND (efficiency filter disabled OR efficiency ratio meets its configured minimum) AND cooldown elapsed AND no live trade AND no long signal on this bar AND (close < second EMA for a trade to open)
Long: Momentum > positive momentum level + sweep threshold AND no positive-level sweep is already active
Short: Momentum < negative momentum level − sweep threshold AND no negative-level sweep is already active
Long: A confirmed negative momentum trough has a lower corresponding price low and a higher momentum value than the prior confirmed negative trough
Short: A confirmed positive momentum peak has a higher corresponding price high and a lower momentum value than the prior confirmed positive peak
Exit: long, on a bar after entry, low <= long stop OR high >= long target; if both occur before any target was reached, exit at the stop
Exit: short, on a bar after entry, high >= short stop OR low <= short target; if both occur before any target was reached, exit at the stop
