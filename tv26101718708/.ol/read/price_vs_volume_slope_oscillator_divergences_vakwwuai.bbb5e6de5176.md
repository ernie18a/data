Calculation: Normalized price slope = clamp(percentage change in the price moving average over the slope lookback ÷ its long-term EMA of absolute slope, −3, 3) ÷ 3; smooth with an EMA.
Calculation: Normalized volume slope = clamp(percentage change in the volume moving average over the same slope lookback ÷ its long-term EMA of absolute slope, −3, 3) ÷ 3; smooth with an EMA. Use the same moving-average type and smoothing settings as for price.
Calculation: Price efficiency = absolute normalized price slope ÷ (absolute normalized volume slope + a constant).
Calculation: Pivot volume = either volume on the confirmed pivot bar or the average volume over a shorter lookback centered on that bar, as selected.
