Calculation: Session volume profile = distribute each bar’s volume equally among the price bins its high-low range touches, over the current daily session (capped at a rolling lookback); control level is the highest-volume bin midpoint, and value bounds enclose the specified fraction of volume by expanding from that bin toward the higher-volume adjacent bin. Smooth the midpoint and half-width toward the current profile’s bounds with ATR-limited changes to obtain the value center and upper/lower value boundaries.
Calculation: Direction score = 0.30 × normalized directional-index difference + 0.25 × sign of fast EMA vs. slower EMA + 0.20 × ATR-normalized smoothed close change + 0.15 × ATR-normalized close displacement from an HLC3 EMA + 0.10 × close position relative to the smoothed value boundaries; clamp normalized terms to their specified ranges.
Calculation: Bullish response = any of (low sweeps below the prior multi-bar low and closes back above it on an up bar; close breaks above the prior two-bar high on an up bar; two consecutive closes above the upper value boundary; or a bullish test of the dynamic trendline with larger lower wick, non-declining trend slope, and positive direction score), and lower wick is at least 70% of upper wick.
Calculation: Bearish response = any of (high sweeps above the prior multi-bar high and closes back below it on a down bar; close breaks below the prior two-bar low on a down bar; two consecutive closes below the lower value boundary; or a bearish test of the dynamic trendline with larger upper wick, non-rising trend slope, and negative direction score), and upper wick is at least 70% of lower wick.
Calculation: Dynamic trendline = EMA of a weighted moving average of twice the shorter WMA minus the longer WMA; trend slope is its one-bar change divided by ATR.
Calculation: Bullish votes = count of: close > open; close > prior close; smoothed close change rising; fast EMA > slower EMA; positive directional index > negative directional index; direction score > 0; close > session profile control; and body/range efficiency with close in the upper part of the candle range.
Calculation: Bearish votes = count of the corresponding bearish conditions: close < open; close < prior close; smoothed close change falling; fast EMA < slower EMA; negative directional index > positive directional index; direction score < 0; close < session profile control; and body/range efficiency with close in the lower part of the candle range.
Calculation: Compressed = recent multi-bar high-low range is small relative to ATR and net close displacement divided by the sum of absolute close changes is low.
Calculation: Participation expanded = volume is unavailable or current volume is at least its moving average.
Long: confirmed bar AND value boundaries available AND NOT compressed AND participation expanded AND bullish response AND bullish votes meet the required threshold AND direction score is positive beyond its threshold AND bullish votes > bearish votes AND (no prior signal and no simultaneous short candidate OR prior signal was short).
Short: confirmed bar AND value boundaries available AND NOT compressed AND participation expanded AND bearish response AND bearish votes meet the required threshold AND direction score is negative beyond its threshold AND bearish votes > bullish votes AND (no prior signal and no simultaneous long candidate OR prior signal was long).
Long: bullish participation flow, trend, and displacement alignment persists for two consecutive confirmed bars.
Short: bearish participation flow, trend, and displacement alignment persists for two consecutive confirmed bars.
