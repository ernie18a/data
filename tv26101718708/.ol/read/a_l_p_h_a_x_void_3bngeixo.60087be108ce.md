Calculation: Valid bull FVG = current low > high two bars earlier, middle-bar body > its 14-bar average body × displacement multiplier, and gap width ≥ ATR × minimum-gap multiplier; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Valid bear FVG = current high < low two bars earlier, same displacement condition, and gap width ≥ ATR × minimum-gap multiplier; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier].
Calculation: Bull volume estimate = volume × (close − low) / (high − low); bear volume estimate = volume − bull volume estimate (when high = low, each estimate = volume / 2). Smooth each estimate with an EMA; volume delta = bull EMA − bear EMA; smooth delta with a 5-bar EMA.
Calculation: Momentum = linear regression, over a lookback, of close minus the average of (highest high + lowest low) / 2 and the simple moving average of close.
Calculation: Bull FVG midpoint = (zone top + zone bottom) / 2; bear FVG midpoint = (zone top + zone bottom) / 2.
Long: A valid, unfilled bull FVG no older than the maximum allowed age overlaps the bar (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom), and close > open AND close ≥ zone midpoint; AND at least the minimum number of these five votes are bullish: fast EMA > slow EMA > signal EMA AND close > fast EMA; momentum > 0 AND momentum > prior-bar momentum; volume delta > 0 AND smoothed volume delta > 0 AND OBV > OBV from the configured lookback bars earlier; close > session VWAP; ADX ≥ minimum AND +DI > −DI; AND ADX ≥ minimum; AND within the configured active session; AND fast EMA > slow EMA; AND bars since last long signal ≥ cooldown; AND this setup was false on the prior bar. When multiple FVGs qualify, use the most recently formed one.
Short: A valid, unfilled bear FVG no older than the maximum allowed age overlaps the bar (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom), and close < open AND close ≤ zone midpoint; AND at least the minimum number of these five votes are bearish: fast EMA < slow EMA < signal EMA AND close < fast EMA; momentum < 0 AND momentum < prior-bar momentum; volume delta < 0 AND smoothed volume delta < 0 AND OBV < OBV from the configured lookback bars earlier; close < session VWAP; ADX ≥ minimum AND −DI > +DI; AND ADX ≥ minimum; AND within the configured active session; AND fast EMA < slow EMA; AND bars since last short signal ≥ cooldown; AND this setup was false on the prior bar. When multiple FVGs qualify, use the most recently formed one.
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + ATR × first-target multiplier (partial 50%); OR low ≤ long stop; OR high ≥ entry price + ATR × full-target multiplier; OR low ≤ long trailing stop. Long stop = retested FVG bottom − ATR × stop multiplier (or signal-bar low − ATR × stop multiplier if FVG-edge stops are disabled). Long trailing stop starts at the same base minus ATR × trailing multiplier, then advances to the maximum of its prior value and current low − ATR × trailing multiplier; active after entry bar.
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − ATR × first-target multiplier (partial 50%); OR high ≥ short stop; OR low ≤ entry price − ATR × full-target multiplier; OR high ≥ short trailing stop. Short stop = retested FVG top + ATR × stop multiplier (or signal-bar high + ATR × stop multiplier if FVG-edge stops are disabled). Short trailing stop starts at the same base plus ATR × trailing multiplier, then advances to the minimum of its prior value and current high + ATR × trailing multiplier; active after entry bar.
