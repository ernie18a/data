Calculation: Effort = square root of (true range / ATR × volume / average volume); use 1 for relative volume when volume is unavailable; map to a bounded 0–100 level.
Calculation: Response = 0.55 × (absolute candle body / ATR) + 0.45 × (absolute close change / ATR); map to a bounded 0–100 level.
Calculation: Path efficiency = absolute close change over the context lookback / sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback, clamped to 0–1; map to a bounded 0–100 level.
Calculation: Direction = 0.60 × close location within true range + 0.40 × candle body direction × body fraction; set to zero inside a direction dead zone. Direction conviction is the bounded level of its absolute value.
Calculation: Bull/Bear Drive evidence = weighted geometric mean of effort, response, path efficiency, and conviction in the matching direction. Bull/Bear Absorption evidence = weighted geometric mean of effort, inverse response, matching one-sided wick rejection, and inverse path efficiency. Churn evidence = weighted geometric mean of effort, inverse response, two-sided wick rejection, and direction neutrality. Balance evidence = weighted geometric mean of inverse effort, inverse response, and inverse path efficiency. Convert each component to a 0–1 fraction before the geometric mean; clamp inputs above zero.
Calculation: Response Field = EMA of (response level × direction × (0.35 + 0.65 × path efficiency)).
Calculation: Active candidate = highest-evidence state among Bull Drive, Bear Drive, Bull Absorption, Bear Absorption, and Churn; also track the second-highest evidence. An active candidate qualifies when its evidence meets the active-entry threshold and exceeds the second-highest by the required separation.
Calculation: Confirmed-bar regime state starts in Transition. In Transition, enter the qualifying active candidate, or enter Balance when balance evidence meets its entry threshold and the top active evidence is below the active-entry threshold. In Balance, enter a qualifying active candidate only after it remains the candidate for the balance-escape confirmation count; otherwise hold while balance evidence meets its hold threshold, and return to Transition when it does not. In an active state, switch to a different qualifying candidate only after its evidence exceeds the current state's evidence by the switch margin for the switch confirmation count. Otherwise hold while current-state evidence meets the active-hold threshold. Below that threshold, after the active-exit confirmation count, enter Balance if balance evidence meets the active-to-balance threshold and top active evidence is below the active-entry threshold for the required confirmation count; otherwise enter Transition. For Bull Drive, two consecutive confirmed bars with Response Field <= 0 release the state to Balance if the active-to-balance conditions hold, otherwise to Transition. For Bear Drive, use Response Field >= 0. Active-state switching has priority over this guard.
Calculation: Bull/Bear Vacuum evidence = weighted geometric mean of displacement-leverage evidence, response, path efficiency, and conviction in the matching direction. Displacement leverage is the bounded level of the positive log of (response level + epsilon) / (effort level + epsilon), normalized to its reference ratio.
Long: On a confirmed bar, enter when the regime changes to Bull Drive or Bull Absorption, or when Bull Vacuum evidence meets its threshold with positive direction and the condition was false on the previous bar.
Short: On a confirmed bar, enter when the regime changes to Bear Drive or Bear Absorption, or when Bear Vacuum evidence meets its threshold with negative direction and the condition was false on the previous bar.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar when Bull Drive ownership is released after two consecutive bars with Response Field <= 0.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar when Bear Drive ownership is released after two consecutive bars with Response Field >= 0.
