Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than its previous confirmed pivot low while price is lower; bearish divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than its previous confirmed pivot high while price is higher. The two pivots must be separated by a configurable minimum and maximum number of bars. Confirm pivots using configurable left and right bars; evaluate the signal on confirmation, referring to the pivot bar and previous pivot.
Calculation: Oscillators used for divergence = RSI, OBV, MACD histogram, and momentum; momentum is the change in close over a configurable lookback. Each oscillator can contribute one divergence to the score.
Calculation: Relative volume at a bar = volume divided by its configurable moving average (SMA, EMA, WMA, or RMA). Divergence-window relative volume = the maximum volume from the current bar through the bar two bars after the confirmed pivot, divided by the volume moving average at the pivot bar.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish candlestick pattern = any enabled matching pattern in the configurable window beginning at the confirmed pivot: hammer/inverted hammer, engulfing, pin bar, doji/dragonfly/gravestone, morning/evening star, shooting star/hanging man, or key reversal bar. Use the source's pattern comparisons, with configurable wick/body and doji-body thresholds; key reversal requires taking the previous bar's low/high, closing above/below its close, and meeting configurable rejection-wick and close-location thresholds.
Calculation: Price near a Fibonacci level = the pivot-bar low/high is within a configurable percentage of the latest confirmed swing low/high or one of the 23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, or 78.6% retracement levels between them. Swing pivots use equal configurable left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Divergence confluence score = count of enabled oscillator divergences meeting the configurable minimum divergence count, plus one each for the matching candle-pattern filter, a volume-above-moving-average occurrence in the pivot window, the Fibonacci filter (or high divergence-window relative volume), and high divergence-window relative volume. Require the configurable minimum total score.
Calculation: Standalone bullish key reversal = current low below previous low, current close above previous close, lower-wick fraction and close-above-low fraction each meet configurable thresholds, and current relative volume meets its configurable minimum. Standalone bearish key reversal uses current high above previous high, close below previous close, upper-wick fraction and close-below-high fraction meeting configurable thresholds, and the same relative-volume requirement.
Long: RSI bullish divergence OR bullish divergence confluence score meets its minimum OR standalone bullish key reversal
Short: RSI bearish divergence OR bearish divergence confluence score meets its minimum OR standalone bearish key reversal
