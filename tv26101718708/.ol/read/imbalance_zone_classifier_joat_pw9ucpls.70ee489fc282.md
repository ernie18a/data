Calculation: Gap size score = For a bullish gap, (current low − high two bars ago) / current low × 100; for a bearish gap, (low two bars ago − current high) / current high × 100. Use the larger gap size, rank it against the 1000-bar chart-timeframe history using the nearest-rank 100th percentile, then divide by that percentile and multiply by 100, capped at 100 (0 if the percentile is 0).
Calculation: Directional strength score = 50% gap size score + 50% directional volume share. Directional volume share is bullish volume divided by bullish plus bearish volume for a bullish gap, and bearish volume divided by that total for a bearish gap. Sum volume from lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar; evaluate at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Composite strength score = integer part of directional strength score.
Long: After the first 50 chart bars, current low > high two bars ago AND previous bar close > previous bar open AND gap size score > 5 AND composite strength score for a bullish gap ≥ minimum score.
Short: After the first 50 chart bars, current high < low two bars ago AND previous bar close < previous bar open AND gap size score > 5 AND composite strength score for a bearish gap ≥ minimum score.
