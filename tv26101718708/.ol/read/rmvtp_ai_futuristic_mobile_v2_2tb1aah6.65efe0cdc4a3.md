Calculation: Timeframe score = 0.35 × trend score + 0.25 × momentum score + 0.20 × candle score + 0.20 × (trend score × volume score), where trend score is 1 when close is above the medium-term EMA, −1 when below, otherwise 0; momentum score is 1 when close exceeds its longer-lookback close, −1 when below, otherwise 0; candle score is 1 when (close − open)/(high − low) exceeds a positive threshold, −1 when below its negative, otherwise 0 (use 0 when high = low); volume score is 1 when volume / its average over a longer lookback exceeds a high threshold, 0.5 when it exceeds a lower threshold, otherwise 0.
Calculation: Final score = weighted sum of the five timeframe scores, with greater weights on the longer timeframes; compute each score from that timeframe’s bars. For timeframes higher than the chart, use the last completed bar; for timeframes lower than the chart, use the latest lower-timeframe bar available for the chart bar.
Long: Final score ≥ bullish threshold AND close > medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA > medium-term EMA
Short: Final score ≤ negative bearish threshold AND close < medium-term EMA AND short-term EMA < medium-term EMA
