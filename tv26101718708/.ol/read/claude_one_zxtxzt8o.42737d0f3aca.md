Short: position is flat AND timestamp is Monday–Friday 07:00–16:00 GMT AND close < long-term EMA AND short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND high >= short-term EMA − ATR × pullback-distance multiplier AND maximum RSI over the short lookback >= overbought threshold AND current RSI < short-entry threshold AND previous RSI >= short-entry threshold AND close < open AND ADX >= minimum threshold
Calculation: Long Chandelier stop = highest high over its lookback − ATR over the same lookback × multiplier; if previous close > previous long Chandelier stop, use the greater of this value and the previous long stop, otherwise use this value
Calculation: Short Chandelier stop = lowest low over its lookback + ATR over the same lookback × multiplier; if previous close < previous short Chandelier stop, use the lesser of this value and the previous short stop, otherwise use this value
Exit: long, low <= the greater of entry price − ATR × initial-stop multiplier and the long Chandelier stop OR RSI crosses below 50 OR timestamp hour is >= force-flat hour GMT
Exit: short, high >= the lesser of entry price + ATR × initial-stop multiplier and the short Chandelier stop OR RSI crosses above 50 OR timestamp hour is >= force-flat hour GMT
