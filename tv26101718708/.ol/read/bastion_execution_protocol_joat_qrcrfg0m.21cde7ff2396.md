Calculation: Normalized VWAP slope = (current VWAP − VWAP from the configured lookback ago) / (ATR × square root of the lookback); positive/negative trend means slope exceeds/falls below configured thresholds.
Calculation: Trend regime = squeeze if Bollinger Band width is in its configured low percentile; otherwise trend up if short-, medium-, and long-term SMAs align upward with positive VWAP slope, or if either upward alignment or positive slope holds; trend down analogously, with upward conditions taking priority; otherwise range.
Calculation: Market structure = bullish after a confirmed close above the latest pivot high while neutral or bearish; bearish after a confirmed close below the latest pivot low while neutral or bullish; otherwise retain the prior state.
Calculation: Displacement candle = body/range meets its configured minimum and body is at least the configured multiple of the 20-bar average body; bullish/bearish displacement additionally requires close > open / close < open and a confirmed bar.
Calculation: Stochastic Momentum Index = 100 × double-smoothed EMA of (close − midpoint of the recent high/low range) / (one-half of the double-smoothed EMA of that range).
Calculation: CVD = cumulative estimated buy volume minus sell volume; estimated buy volume is full volume when close ≥ open, otherwise volume × (close − low)/(high − low); sell volume is remaining volume.
Calculation: Engulfing pattern = confirmed bullish/bearish candle whose body engulfs the prior bearish/bullish candle body, is larger than that body, and has volume above its configured multiple of the 20-bar average volume. Pin bar = confirmed candle with the directional wick greater than twice its body and the opposite wick less than half its body.
Calculation: Allowed trading time = configured session is allowed when session filtering is enabled, the weekday is enabled, and the daily trade count is below its configured limit.
Long: Confirmed bar AND (trend regime is up AND market structure is bullish AND RSI is above its configured bull threshold AND SMI > 0 AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR CVD > its configured moving average) AND allowed trading time AND (squeeze avoidance disabled OR not squeeze) AND (trend-regime requirement disabled OR regime is trending) AND (bullish displacement OR enabled bullish pattern OR (close > short-term SMA AND close > VWAP))) OR bullish displacement OR enabled bullish pattern.
Short: Confirmed bar AND (trend regime is down AND market structure is bearish AND RSI is below its configured bear threshold AND SMI < 0 AND (CVD confirmation disabled OR CVD < its configured moving average) AND allowed trading time AND (squeeze avoidance disabled OR not squeeze) AND (trend-regime requirement disabled OR regime is trending) AND (bearish displacement OR enabled bearish pattern OR (close < short-term SMA AND close < VWAP))) OR bearish displacement OR enabled bearish pattern.
Exit: Long, low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ take-profit OR configured end-of-day close time reached; initial stop = entry price − ATR-based risk distance, take-profit = entry price + risk distance × configured reward/risk, and after close profit reaches the configured R threshold the stop ratchets upward to close − ATR-based trail distance.
Exit: Short, high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ take-profit OR configured end-of-day close time reached; initial stop = entry price + ATR-based risk distance, take-profit = entry price − risk distance × configured reward/risk, and after close profit reaches the configured R threshold the stop ratchets downward to close + ATR-based trail distance.
