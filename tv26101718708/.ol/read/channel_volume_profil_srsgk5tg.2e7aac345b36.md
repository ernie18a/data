Calculation: Upper channel rail = linear regression of close + residual width, where residual width is the highest high-minus-regression residual over the channel lookback, multiplied by a width factor.
Calculation: Lower channel rail = linear regression of close + residual width, where residual width is the lowest low-minus-regression residual over the channel lookback, multiplied by a width factor.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = confirmed bar with low at or below the lower channel rail plus a range-based tolerance, and close above that rail, and lower wick at least a set multiple of the candle body; OR low at or below VAL and close above VAL; OR low below the latest liquidity pivot low by an ATR-based tolerance and close above that pivot; in all cases, lower-wick/body ratio must meet a set fraction of the wick threshold.
Calculation: Bearish sweep = confirmed bar with high at or above the upper channel rail minus a range-based tolerance, and close below that rail, and upper wick at least a set multiple of the candle body; OR high at or above VAH and close below VAH; OR high above the latest liquidity pivot high by an ATR-based tolerance and close below that pivot; in all cases, upper-wick/body ratio must meet a set fraction of the wick threshold.
Calculation: A confluence score = 20 for a channel-rail sweep + 20 for a value-area sweep + 20 if wick/body meets the wick threshold (10 if it meets 70% of that threshold) + 10 if volume exceeds its moving average times a volume factor + 15 for reclaiming the POC + 15 if a validated IDM label is within a set bar window; calculate bullish and bearish scores using their respective sweeps and reclaim conditions.
Long: Bullish sweep; bullish A confluence score at least the minimum score, and (candle-direction filter disabled OR close >= open), and (A volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average times a volume factor).
Short: Bearish sweep; bearish A confluence score at least the minimum score, and (candle-direction filter disabled OR close <= open), and (A volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average times a volume factor).
Long: During rising simulated channel direction, a confirmed swing-low candidate is swept below by an ATR-based tolerance and the bar closes back above the candidate; in strict BOS mode, price must also break above the latest swing high, while early-candidate mode marks the swing-low candidate when confirmed.
Short: During falling simulated channel direction, a confirmed swing-high candidate is swept above by an ATR-based tolerance and the bar closes back below the candidate; in strict BOS mode, price must also break below the latest swing low, while early-candidate mode marks the swing-high candidate when confirmed.
