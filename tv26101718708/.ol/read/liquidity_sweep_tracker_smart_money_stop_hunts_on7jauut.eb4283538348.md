Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low is the highest/lowest across the specified lookback bars on both sides; it becomes available after the bars on the right complete.
Calculation: Unfilled fair-value gap = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier, or bearish when current high < low two bars earlier; midpoint = average of the gap boundaries. A gap is filled when price reaches its near boundary.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low while structure bias is not already bullish / bearish; the break changes the bias.
Calculation: Confidence score = weighted mean of seven 0–100 scores: sweep depth (triangular score, ideal interval set by parameters), touched-level count (linear to its cap), structure alignment (100 aligned, 55 neutral, 25 opposed), ATR regime (linear between configured low/high references), post-sweep displacement (linear to its ideal ATR distance, capped at 100), volume (50 if volume filter is off; otherwise volume-ratio score, clipped to 0–100), and HTF proximity (50 if HTF filter is off or no HTF swing exists; otherwise 100 minus distance/proximity reference × 100, floored at 0). Weights and score parameters are configurable.
Calculation: HTF proximity = absolute distance from the swept level to the latest confirmed HTF swing high (short) or low (long), divided by chart-timeframe ATR; use higher-timeframe bars and the last completed bar.
Calculation: Initial stop = beyond the sweep extreme by ATR × stop-buffer × clipped ATR-regime ratio, capped at the configured maximum stop distance. Long stop uses the sweep low and is capped below entry; short stop uses the sweep high and is capped above entry.
Calculation: Target 2 = nearest unswept opposing confirmed swing (below entry for short, above for long); if absent, use the configured ATR fallback unless an opposing swing is required. Target 1 = nearest midpoint of an unfilled gap between entry and Target 2; if none, Target 1 = Target 2.
Calculation: Active stop = the tighter of initial stop and the post-entry confirmed swing trail (long: higher swing low minus ATR buffer; short: lower swing high plus ATR buffer); when ATR backstop is enabled, also apply the close-based ATR trail.
Short: An unbroken confirmed swing high is exceeded by high > swing high + ATR × sweep buffer, and within the configured confirmation window a close < that level; confidence score ≥ minimum; each enabled optional filter passes: volume at sweep > its moving average × multiplier, HTF proximity ≤ its reference, at least one enabled bearish trigger passes (bearish engulfing OR bearish displacement OR close below sweep-bar low), and reward/risk ≥ minimum. Target 2 must exist.
Long: An unbroken confirmed swing low is exceeded downward by low < swing low − ATR × sweep buffer, and within the configured confirmation window a close > that level; confidence score ≥ minimum; each enabled optional filter passes: volume at sweep > its moving average × multiplier, HTF proximity ≤ its reference, at least one enabled bullish trigger passes (bullish engulfing OR bullish displacement OR close above sweep-bar high), and reward/risk ≥ minimum. Target 2 must exist.
Exit: Long, close < latest confirmed swing low while structure bias is not already bearish (if opposing-structure exit enabled) OR low ≤ active stop OR high ≥ Target 1 OR elapsed bars since entry ≥ time limit AND no favorable bullish structure break since entry AND open profit ≤ 0.
Exit: Short, close > latest confirmed swing high while structure bias is not already bullish (if opposing-structure exit enabled) OR high ≥ active stop OR low ≤ Target 1 OR elapsed bars since entry ≥ time limit AND no favorable bearish structure break since entry AND open profit ≤ 0.
