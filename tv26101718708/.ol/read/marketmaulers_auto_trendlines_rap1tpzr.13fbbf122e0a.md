Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe
Calculation: Trendline = straight line through two consecutive confirmed pivot lows (rising support, later low higher) or pivot highs (falling resistance, later high lower); pivots are strict extrema over equal lookback windows before and after the pivot. Reject lines that exceed the slope limit, are crossed by an intervening close beyond the touch tolerance, or duplicate an existing line. Count the anchors as touches; count later touches when price reaches within the ATR-scaled touch tolerance and closes on the line’s valid side, no more often than the touch spacing. A line is validated when its touch count reaches the minimum.
Long: a validated resistance line closes above the line by more than the break threshold, OR a broken support line has first moved clear of the line and then returns within the touch tolerance and closes below it, OR a broken support line closes back above the line by more than the break threshold
Short: a validated support line closes below the line by more than the break threshold, OR a broken resistance line has first moved clear of the line and then returns within the touch tolerance and closes above it, OR a broken resistance line closes back below the line by more than the break threshold
