Calculation: Volume delta = volume × absolute(close − open) ÷ (high − low) × sign(close − open), or zero when high = low
Calculation: EMA ribbon score = sum of +1 when each successive faster EMA is above the next slower EMA and +1 when close is above the fastest EMA; otherwise each comparison contributes −1. Use five EMAs in descending lookback order.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe confluence = 1.0 × daily score + 1.5 × weekly score + 0.75 × custom higher-timeframe score; calculate each from the last completed bar. Each daily/weekly score is the sign of the sum of: +1 if HMA is rising, otherwise −1; +1 if Supertrend is bullish, otherwise −1; +1 if RSI is above its upper threshold, −1 if below its lower threshold, otherwise 0. The custom higher-timeframe score uses the same HMA and RSI comparisons, without Supertrend.
Calculation: Market regime score = 0.4 × scaled ADX + 0.4 × (100 − Choppiness Index, floored at zero) + 0.2 × normalized Bollinger Band width percentile. Trending means score above its threshold; ranging means score below its threshold.
Calculation: Confidence score = points for higher-timeframe confluence magnitude, market regime, EMA ribbon magnitude, RSI distance from its midpoint, volume-delta magnitude versus its moving average, squeeze release, matching confirmed CVD divergence, and matching liquidity sweep; use the indicator’s configured point tiers and weights.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while its corresponding RSI pivot low is higher. Bearish RSI divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high while its corresponding RSI pivot high is lower. Use confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Bullish CVD divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while its corresponding CVD pivot low is higher. Bearish CVD divergence = a newly confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high while its corresponding CVD pivot high is lower. Use confirmed pivots.
Long: bullish RSI divergence OR bullish CVD divergence OR (HMA rising AND RSI above midpoint AND volume delta positive AND EMA ribbon score at least the bullish threshold AND Supertrend bullish AND higher-timeframe confluence at least its bullish threshold when conflict suppression is enabled AND confidence score at least its configured minimum AND not in strong ranging conditions (when strict range filtering is enabled) AND distance from the 20-bar high plus ATR resistance zone exceeds its configured ATR distance AND candle body exceeds its configured fraction of the bar range when enabled AND minimum spacing since the previous signal has elapsed AND the conditions hold for the required confirmation bars when enabled AND not inside an opposing fair-value gap when enabled AND no nearby qualifying pivot resistance when enabled AND no nearby volume-profile resistance when enabled)
Short: bearish RSI divergence OR bearish CVD divergence OR (HMA falling AND RSI below midpoint AND volume delta negative AND EMA ribbon score at most the bearish threshold AND Supertrend bearish AND higher-timeframe confluence at most its bearish threshold when conflict suppression is enabled AND confidence score at least its configured minimum AND not in strong ranging conditions (when strict range filtering is enabled) AND distance from the 20-bar low minus ATR support zone exceeds its configured ATR distance AND candle body exceeds its configured fraction of the bar range when enabled AND minimum spacing since the previous signal has elapsed AND the conditions hold for the required confirmation bars when enabled AND not inside an opposing fair-value gap when enabled AND no nearby qualifying pivot support when enabled AND no nearby volume-profile support when enabled)
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + ATR × configured first target multiple
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + ATR × configured second target multiple
Exit: long, high ≥ entry + ATR × configured third target multiple OR low ≤ effective stop, where the initial stop is entry − ATR × regime-adjusted stop multiple; after the first target, the effective stop is the greater of entry and the ratcheting trail, with each bar’s trail candidate equal to high − ATR × configured trail multiple
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − ATR × configured first target multiple
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − ATR × configured second target multiple
Exit: short, low ≤ entry − ATR × configured third target multiple OR high ≥ effective stop, where the initial stop is entry + ATR × regime-adjusted stop multiple; after the first target, the effective stop is the lesser of entry and the ratcheting trail, with each bar’s trail candidate equal to low + ATR × configured trail multiple
