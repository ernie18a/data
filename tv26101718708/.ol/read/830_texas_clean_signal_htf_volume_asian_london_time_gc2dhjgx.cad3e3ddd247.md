Calculation: Opening range = highest high and lowest low from the 8:30, 8:35, and 8:40 Texas-time bars; opening close and VWAP = their values from the 8:40 bar
Calculation: Opening bias = long if opening close > opening VWAP; short if opening close < opening VWAP; neutral otherwise
Calculation: Long-term and short-term HTF trend = on each of two higher timeframes, compare the longer-lookback EMA of close with the shorter-lookback EMA of close, using the latest value available with lookahead off
Calculation: RSI/MACD confluence = RSI above 50 OR MACD histogram greater than its prior value for long; RSI below 50 OR MACD histogram less than its prior value for short
Calculation: Volume confirmation = volume > its moving average × volume multiplier
Long: opening range is complete AND opening bias is long OR (opening range is complete AND opening bias is short AND close > opening range high AND close > VWAP) OR close crosses above opening range high (prior close <= opening range high) AND close > VWAP AND close > the EMA of high AND close > the EMA of close AND RSI/MACD confluence is long AND both higher-timeframe trends are long (when enabled) AND volume confirmation (when enabled)
Short: opening range is complete AND opening bias is short OR (opening range is complete AND opening bias is long AND close < opening range low AND close < VWAP) OR close crosses below opening range low (prior close >= opening range low) AND close < VWAP AND close < the EMA of low AND close < the EMA of close AND RSI/MACD confluence is short AND both higher-timeframe trends are short (when enabled) AND volume confirmation (when enabled)
