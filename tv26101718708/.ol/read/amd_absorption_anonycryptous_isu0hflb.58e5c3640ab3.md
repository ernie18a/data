Calculation: Accumulation range = rolling highest high and lowest low over the accumulation lookback; it qualifies when (highest high − lowest low) / lowest low × 100 ≤ maximum range percentage.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low − high two bars earlier; bearish fair value gap = low two bars earlier − current high.
Calculation: Absorption = previous bar volume > its rolling average volume × minimum volume multiplier AND abs(previous close − previous open) / (previous high − previous low) ≤ maximum body-to-range ratio; a zero-range bar does not qualify.
Long: Within an enabled session, a qualifying accumulation exists and, within the manipulation search window, (current low < accumulation low OR a bullish manipulation is active AND current bar qualifies as absorption OR a bullish fair value gap > ATR × minimum gap multiplier AND current close > accumulation low); the fair value gap setup must follow a low sweep below the accumulation range.
Short: Within an enabled session, a qualifying accumulation exists and, within the manipulation search window, (current high > accumulation high OR a bearish manipulation is active AND current bar qualifies as absorption OR a bearish fair value gap > ATR × minimum gap multiplier AND current close < accumulation high); the fair value gap setup must follow a high sweep above the accumulation range.
