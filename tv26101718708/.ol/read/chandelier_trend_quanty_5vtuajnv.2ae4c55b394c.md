Calculation: Uptrend trailing level = the greater of the previous uptrend level and the highest high (or close, if selected) since the last turn minus ATR × multiplier; reset to the current high (or close) minus ATR × multiplier when an uptrend starts.
Calculation: Downtrend trailing level = the lesser of the previous downtrend level and the lowest low (or close, if selected) since the last turn plus ATR × multiplier; reset to the current low (or close) plus ATR × multiplier when a downtrend starts.
Long: On a completed bar, the previous trend was down and close > the downtrend trailing level.
Short: On a completed bar, the previous trend was up and close < the uptrend trailing level.
Exit: long, on a completed bar, close < the uptrend trailing level.
Exit: short, on a completed bar, close > the downtrend trailing level.
