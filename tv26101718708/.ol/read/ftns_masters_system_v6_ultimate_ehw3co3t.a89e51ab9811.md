Calculation: Smoothed Heiken-Ashi = apply a longer-lookback RMA to each OHLC series; set HA close to their mean, initialize HA open to the mean of smoothed open and close, then recursively set HA open to the mean of its prior value and prior HA close; apply a shorter-lookback WMA to HA open and close. Bullish means smoothed HA close ≥ smoothed HA open.
Calculation: Classic pivot levels = from the previous completed higher-timeframe bar, P = (high + low + close) / 3; R1 = 2P − low; R2 = P + (high − low); S1 = 2P − high; S2 = P − (high − low).
Calculation: Triple-smoothed EMA rate of change = percentage change of the third EMA in a three-EMA cascade, scaled by a constant; compare a shorter-lookback value with a longer-lookback value.
Long: (mode is Trend Follow AND EMA(shorter) > EMA(longer) AND EMA(longer) > SMA(shorter) AND SMA(shorter) > SMA(longer) AND smoothed Heiken-Ashi changes from bearish to bullish AND ADX > its prior value AND ADX > threshold AND +DI > −DI AND volume exceeded its longer-lookback average times a configurable multiplier within the current or prior three bars) OR (mode is Counter Trend AND (low or close is within an ATR-scaled threshold of S1 OR low is within an ATR-scaled threshold of S2) AND smoothed Heiken-Ashi changes from bearish to bullish AND shorter-lookback triple-smoothed EMA rate of change > longer-lookback triple-smoothed EMA rate of change).
Short: (mode is Trend Follow AND EMA(shorter) < EMA(longer) AND EMA(longer) < SMA(shorter) AND SMA(shorter) < SMA(longer) AND smoothed Heiken-Ashi changes from bullish to bearish AND ADX > its prior value AND ADX > threshold AND −DI > +DI AND volume exceeded its longer-lookback average times a configurable multiplier within the current or prior three bars) OR (mode is Counter Trend AND (high or close is within an ATR-scaled threshold of R1 OR high is within an ATR-scaled threshold of R2) AND smoothed Heiken-Ashi changes from bullish to bearish AND shorter-lookback triple-smoothed EMA rate of change ≤ longer-lookback triple-smoothed EMA rate of change).
