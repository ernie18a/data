Calculation: IBI zone = On a configured lower, intermediate, or higher timeframe, using only completed bars, a base candle has (high − low − |close − open|) / (high − low) at least a threshold. An impulse is either a non-base candle or, in strict mode, a candle whose |close − open| / (high − low) meets a threshold. After an impulse, collect one or more consecutive base candles, up to a limit; the next impulse completes the zone. A bullish completing impulse makes a demand zone; a bearish one makes a supply zone. Demand zone entry = lowest base-candle body price; its height = highest base-candle body price − lowest base-candle wick price. Supply zone entry = highest base-candle body price; its height = highest base-candle wick price − lowest base-candle body price. The zone remains active until a confirmed chart-bar body closes below its lowest wick (demand) or above its highest wick (supply).
Calculation: Stop = demand zone lowest wick minus a buffer, or supply zone highest wick plus a buffer. Target levels = entry plus (demand) or minus (supply) zone height multiplied by a step and each successive target number.
Long: An active demand zone has positive height; close > entry; |close − entry| ≤ proximity × zone height; cooldown since its prior arm has elapsed; the zone has no armed or active trade; rearming is allowed by the loss setting; and calculated contract count > 0. Enter when a subsequently armed zone's bar low ≤ entry.
Short: An active supply zone has positive height; close < entry; |close − entry| ≤ proximity × zone height; cooldown since its prior arm has elapsed; the zone has no armed or active trade; rearming is allowed by the loss setting; and calculated contract count > 0. Enter when a subsequently armed zone's bar high ≥ entry.
Exit: Long, confirmed bar body closes below stop OR high reaches the final enabled target.
Exit: Short, confirmed bar body closes above stop OR low reaches the final enabled target. If stop and target are hit on the same bar, exit at the stop.
