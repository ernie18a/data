Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low pivot confirmed after an equal lookback on both sides; compare each new pivot with the second preceding pivot of the same kind.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure bias = bullish after a higher high or higher low pivot; bearish after a lower high or lower low pivot; retain the bias until the opposite condition occurs.
Calculation: Daily VWAP = cumulative typical price × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at each new day.
Calculation: Key-level proximity = the bar’s low/high is within a configurable percentage of its price of any corresponding low/high from the previous day’s completed higher-timeframe bar, a completed Asia or Europe session, the previous completed higher-timeframe bar used for the 4-hour levels, or the current week’s running range.
Long: a higher high pivot OR a higher low pivot OR a bullish engulfing candle (current candle bullish, previous candle bearish, current body engulfs the previous body and is larger) OR a low sweep (low below the prior lookback-bar lowest low and close above it) OR a hammer (lower wick at least the configured multiple of the body, upper wick no larger than the body, close above open, and—when enabled—low near a key level AND RSI below its configured threshold AND a swing low pivot was detected the configured pivot lookback bars earlier or a low sweep occurred on this or either of the prior two bars) OR a double bottom (new swing low within the configured percentage tolerance of the second preceding swing low) OR bullish RSI divergence (new swing low below the second preceding swing low and RSI at the new pivot above RSI at the preceding pivot) OR bullish trend (close above the shorter EMA, shorter EMA above the longer EMA, close above daily VWAP, and bullish structure bias).
Short: a lower high pivot OR a lower low pivot OR a bearish engulfing candle (current candle bearish, previous candle bullish, current body engulfs the previous body and is larger) OR a high sweep (high above the prior lookback-bar highest high and close below it) OR a shooting-star pin (upper wick at least the configured multiple of the body, lower wick no larger than the body, close below open, and—when enabled—high near a key level AND RSI above its configured threshold AND a swing high pivot was detected the configured pivot lookback bars earlier or a high sweep occurred on this or either of the prior two bars) OR a double top (new swing high within the configured percentage tolerance of the second preceding swing high) OR bearish RSI divergence (new swing high above the second preceding swing high and RSI at the new pivot below RSI at the preceding pivot) OR bearish trend (close below the shorter EMA, shorter EMA below the longer EMA, close below daily VWAP, and bearish structure bias).
