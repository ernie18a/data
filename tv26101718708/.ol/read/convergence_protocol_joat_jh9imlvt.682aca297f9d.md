Calculation: Baseline = SMA of EMA(close) using the same lookback twice; step band = SMA(high − low)
Calculation: Bullish regime = baseline rising and bullish structure score ≥ 2; bearish regime = baseline falling and bearish structure score ≥ 2; otherwise neutral. Bullish structure score counts close above baseline + step and baseline + 2 × step, higher latest confirmed pivot high than the preceding one, and higher latest confirmed pivot low than the preceding one. Bearish score counts the symmetric below-band comparisons and lower latest pivot high and low.
Calculation: Bullish structure = latest confirmed pivot high is higher than the preceding pivot high AND latest confirmed pivot low is higher than the preceding pivot low; bearish structure = both are lower.
Calculation: Volatility expansion = short-period ATR / long-period ATR > threshold AND EMA of that ratio minus 1 > its SMA signal; squeeze = short-period ATR < long-period ATR × threshold AND short-period ATR < its own SMA; squeeze release = squeeze is false and was true on the preceding bar.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume when close > open, −volume when close < open, otherwise 0. Fast and slow delta = EMAs of bar delta, with the fast lookback shorter. Delta oscillator = (fast delta − slow delta) / max(rolling 100th percentile of absolute fast-minus-slow delta, 1); delta signal = EMA of the oscillator. Bullish delta = oscillator > threshold AND oscillator > signal; bearish delta = oscillator < negative threshold AND oscillator < signal.
Calculation: Bullish BOS = confirmed close crosses above latest confirmed pivot high; bearish BOS = confirmed close crosses below latest confirmed pivot low. Each remains true until the opposite BOS.
Calculation: Bullish sweep = confirmed low < latest confirmed pivot low − ATR × sweep factor AND close > that pivot low; bearish sweep = confirmed high > latest confirmed pivot high + ATR × sweep factor AND close < that pivot high.
Calculation: Long score = sum of bullish regime, bullish structure, volatility expansion, no squeeze, bullish delta, bullish BOS, and bullish sweep. Short score = sum of bearish regime, bearish structure, volatility expansion, no squeeze, bearish delta, and bearish sweep.
Calculation: Initial risk distance = ATR at entry × stop multiplier; first and second profit distances = initial risk distance × their respective target multiples.
Long: confirmed bar AND flat position AND (volatility gate disabled OR volatility expansion) AND ((bullish regime AND long score ≥ minimum score) OR (bullish regime AND within half a step of baseline AND low ≤ baseline + 0.3 × step AND close > baseline AND bullish delta) OR (squeeze release AND bullish regime AND bullish delta) OR (fast delta crosses above slow delta AND bullish regime AND no squeeze AND (volatility gate disabled OR volatility expansion)) OR (bullish sweep AND bullish delta) OR (fast delta crosses above slow delta AND bullish regime))
Short: confirmed bar AND flat position AND (volatility gate disabled OR volatility expansion) AND ((bearish regime AND short score ≥ minimum score) OR (bearish regime AND within half a step of baseline AND high ≥ baseline − 0.3 × step AND close < baseline AND bearish delta) OR (squeeze release AND bearish regime AND bearish delta) OR (fast delta crosses below slow delta AND bearish regime AND no squeeze AND (volatility gate disabled OR volatility expansion)) OR (bearish sweep AND bearish delta) OR (fast delta crosses below slow delta AND bearish regime))
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − initial risk distance
Exit: long, high ≥ entry price + first profit distance OR high ≥ entry price + second profit distance
Exit: long, confirmed bearish regime OR, after first target is hit, price falls to the trailing stop at the highest high since activation minus the ATR trailing offset
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + initial risk distance
Exit: short, low ≤ entry price − first profit distance OR low ≤ entry price − second profit distance
Exit: short, confirmed bullish regime OR, after first target is hit, price rises to the trailing stop at the lowest low since activation plus the ATR trailing offset
