Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = low > high two bars ago and low − high two bars ago ≥ minimum gap size × ATR; store the gap from high two bars ago to low and its creation bar
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = high < low two bars ago and low two bars ago − high ≥ minimum gap size × ATR; store the gap from high to low two bars ago and its creation bar
Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = EMAs of close, with the fast lookback shorter than the slow lookback
Calculation: Trend-up state = fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND RSI > 50
Calculation: Trend-down state = fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND RSI < 50
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = last completed higher-timeframe close > its 50-period EMA for uptrend, or < its 50-period EMA for downtrend
Long: (selected entry method is FVG Pullback AND a bullish fair value gap is 1 to its configured maximum age in chart bars AND low ≤ gap top AND close > open AND close > gap bottom OR selected entry method is EMA Trend AND trend-up state is true now AND was false on the previous chart bar) AND (sensitivity is Aggressive OR close > 50-period EMA AND (sensitivity is Balanced OR higher-timeframe trend is up AND ADX ≥ configured threshold)) AND chart timeframe matches configured lock if enabled AND confirmed bar closes within an enabled session with sufficient time remaining if session-end closing is enabled AND not in a news blackout AND position is flat or short
Short: (selected entry method is FVG Pullback AND a bearish fair value gap is 1 to its configured maximum age in chart bars AND high ≥ gap bottom AND close < open AND close < gap top OR selected entry method is EMA Trend AND trend-down state is true now AND was false on the previous chart bar) AND (sensitivity is Aggressive OR close < 50-period EMA AND (sensitivity is Balanced OR higher-timeframe trend is down AND ADX ≥ configured threshold)) AND NOT a simultaneous long signal AND chart timeframe matches configured lock if enabled AND confirmed bar closes within an enabled session with sufficient time remaining if session-end closing is enabled AND not in a news blackout AND position is flat or long
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − stop-loss distance OR high ≥ entry price + take-profit distance OR an opposite short entry signal occurs OR a news blackout starts OR an enabled session ends
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + stop-loss distance OR low ≤ entry price − take-profit distance OR an opposite long entry signal occurs OR a news blackout starts OR an enabled session ends
