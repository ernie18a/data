Calculation: Bull score = add 1 each for fast EMA > slow EMA, close > trend EMA, RSI > 50 and < 68, close > open, close > VWAP, ADX > 18, and volume > its 20-bar simple moving average; add 2 if the fast EMA > slow EMA on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Bear score = add 1 each for fast EMA < slow EMA, close < trend EMA, RSI < 50 and > 32, close < open, close < VWAP, ADX > 18, and volume > its 20-bar simple moving average; add 2 if the fast EMA < slow EMA on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe.
Calculation: Long initial stop = the greater of entry price minus ATR times the configured stop multiplier and the lowest low over the configured swing lookback (including the current bar) minus 0.2 ATR; if its distance from entry is less than 0.5 ATR, use entry price minus 0.5 ATR.
Calculation: Short initial stop = the lesser of entry price plus ATR times the configured stop multiplier and the highest high over the configured swing lookback (including the current bar) plus 0.2 ATR; if its distance from entry is less than 0.5 ATR, use entry price plus 0.5 ATR.
Calculation: Risk = absolute difference between entry price and initial stop. Long target 1 = entry price + risk × configured target 1 multiple; long target 2 = entry price + risk × configured target 2 multiple. Short targets are entry price minus risk × the respective configured target multiples.
Long: chart bar is completed AND bar time is within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND RSI > 50 AND RSI < 68 AND bull score >= configured minimum score AND bull score >= 5 AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close > VWAP) AND (opening-range filter disabled OR opening range is ready and close > rolling opening-range high).
Short: chart bar is completed AND bar time is within the configured trading session when the session filter is enabled AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < trend EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA using the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND RSI < 50 AND RSI > 32 AND bear score >= configured minimum score AND bear score >= 5 AND (VWAP filter disabled OR close < VWAP) AND (opening-range filter disabled OR opening range is ready and close < rolling opening-range low).
Exit: Long, chart bar is completed AND low <= active stop; active stop starts at the long initial stop, moves to entry price when high >= target 1, and moves to target 1 when high >= target 2.
Exit: Short, chart bar is completed AND high >= active stop; active stop starts at the short initial stop, moves to entry price when low <= target 1, and moves to target 1 when low <= target 2.
