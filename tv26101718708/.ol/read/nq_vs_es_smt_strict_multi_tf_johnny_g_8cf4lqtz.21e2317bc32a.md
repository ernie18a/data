Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a high greater than the highs of the specified number of bars before and after it; it becomes available after the bars after it complete.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a low lower than the lows of the specified number of bars before and after it; it becomes available after the bars after it complete.
Long: At a confirmed pivot low, the chart market's pivot low is lower than its previous confirmed pivot low AND the comparison market's pivot low is greater than or equal to its previous confirmed pivot low; this condition holds on the chart timeframe OR on any configured requested timeframe. Requested timeframe bars use the last completed bar historically and the bar still forming in real time; relative to the chart timeframe, each requested timeframe may be higher or lower.
Short: At a confirmed pivot high, the chart market's pivot high is higher than its previous confirmed pivot high AND the comparison market's pivot high is lower than or equal to its previous confirmed pivot high; this condition holds on the chart timeframe OR on any configured requested timeframe. Requested timeframe bars use the last completed bar historically and the bar still forming in real time; relative to the chart timeframe, each requested timeframe may be higher or lower.
