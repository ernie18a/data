Calculation: Bar delta = lower-timeframe buyer-initiated volume minus seller-initiated volume, summed within the chart bar; use lower-timeframe bars contained in the current chart bar, including any still forming. If unavailable, use (close − open) / (high − low) × volume, or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Cumulative volume delta = previous cumulative volume delta + bar delta, starting at 0.
Long: On confirmation of a pivot low (with the configured number of bars on each side), its low is below the previous confirmed pivot low and its cumulative volume delta is higher, AND the pivot lows are separated by at least the pivot length and no more than the maximum pivot distance, AND the absolute difference between their lows is at least ATR(14) × the minimum swing multiplier, AND the same bullish exhaustion signal has not occurred within the cooldown; OR its low is above the previous confirmed pivot low and its cumulative volume delta is lower, with the same distance, swing, and cooldown conditions for bullish absorption; OR volume is at least the configured multiple of its 20-bar SMA, abs(close − open) / (high − low) is at most the configured maximum body ratio, close is in the upper 35% of the bar's range, and bar delta is negative.
Short: On confirmation of a pivot high (with the configured number of bars on each side), its high is above the previous confirmed pivot high and its cumulative volume delta is lower, AND the pivot highs are separated by at least the pivot length and no more than the maximum pivot distance, AND the absolute difference between their highs is at least ATR(14) × the minimum swing multiplier, AND the same bearish exhaustion signal has not occurred within the cooldown; OR its high is below the previous confirmed pivot high and its cumulative volume delta is higher, with the same distance, swing, and cooldown conditions for bearish absorption; OR volume is at least the configured multiple of its 20-bar SMA, abs(close − open) / (high − low) is at most the configured maximum body ratio, close is in the lower 35% of the bar's range, and bar delta is positive.
