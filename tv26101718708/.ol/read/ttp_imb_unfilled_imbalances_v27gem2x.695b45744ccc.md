Calculation: Three-bar fair value gap = bullish when the first bar’s high is below the third bar’s low; bearish when the first bar’s low is above the third bar’s high.
Long: A bullish fair value gap forms on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe’s completed third bar, its height meets the minimum-size filter, it is within the multiplicative price band, and no later bar—including a still-forming higher-timeframe bar—has a low at or below the gap’s top minus the fill-threshold fraction of its original height; it is among the nearest eligible unfilled zones below price or contains price.
Short: A bearish fair value gap forms on the chart timeframe or a higher timeframe’s completed third bar, its height meets the minimum-size filter, it is within the multiplicative price band, and no later bar—including a still-forming higher-timeframe bar—has a high at or above the gap’s bottom plus the fill-threshold fraction of its original height; it is among the nearest eligible unfilled zones above price or contains price.
