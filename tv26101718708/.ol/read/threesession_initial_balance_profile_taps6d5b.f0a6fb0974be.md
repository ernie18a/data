Calculation: Initial Balance high/low = highest high and lowest low of the bars in each configured session window, finalized when that window ends; range = high − low.
Calculation: Long OTE zone = [Initial Balance low + 0.21 × range, Initial Balance low + 0.382 × range].
Calculation: Short OTE zone = [Initial Balance low + 0.618 × range, Initial Balance low + 0.79 × range].
Long: close is within a session’s Long OTE zone.
Short: close is within a session’s Short OTE zone.
