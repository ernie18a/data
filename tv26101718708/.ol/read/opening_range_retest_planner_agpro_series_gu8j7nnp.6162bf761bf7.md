Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low over the configured opening-range period; range is locked when that period ends.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Retest quality score = weighted score for retest-pocket touch, close holding the broken boundary, retest timing, current volume relative to its recent average, opening-range size relative to ATR, and room to the first projected target; weights and thresholds are configurable.
Long: A valid locked opening range exists AND tracking is active AND no breakout has occurred for this range AND close > opening-range high + ATR-scaled breakout buffer; then, on a later bar within the retest window, price touches the pocket around the opening-range high AND close >= opening-range high AND retest quality score >= configured minimum.
Short: A valid locked opening range exists AND tracking is active AND no breakout has occurred for this range AND close < opening-range low - ATR-scaled breakout buffer; then, on a later bar within the retest window, price touches the pocket around the opening-range low AND close <= opening-range low AND retest quality score >= configured minimum.
