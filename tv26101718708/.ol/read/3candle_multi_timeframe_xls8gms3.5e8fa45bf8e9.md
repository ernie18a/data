Calculation: Level zone = configured level ± configured half-width, where half-width is either a configured tick count × minimum tick or a configured percentage of the level
Calculation: Candle close position = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low), or 50 when high = low
Calculation: Long body confirmation = close position ≥ 75, or close position ≥ 50 and close > open
Calculation: Short body confirmation = close position ≤ 25, or close position ≤ 50 and close < open
Calculation: Long close-position check = close position ≥ 50; short close-position check = close position ≤ 50
Calculation: Arrival candle = previous bar; confirmation candle = current bar; pre-arrival close = close two bars ago
Calculation: Merged candle = previous and current bars combined: open from previous bar, close from current bar, highest high and lowest low across both
Calculation: Developing delta = sum of 5-minute bar volume since the current higher-timeframe bar began, positive for up bars, negative for down bars, zero for unchanged bars; compare with the current higher-timeframe bar's volume
Calculation: Developing POC = close of the 5-minute bar with greatest volume since the current higher-timeframe bar began, with the latest bar winning ties
Calculation: Optional context score = sum of session allowed, higher-timeframe candle direction allowed, developing delta allowed, and close allowed relative to developing POC; each allowed condition contributes 1. The higher-timeframe candle and developing POC/delta use the current still-forming higher-timeframe bar (higher than the 5-minute entry chart); POC and delta use 5-minute bars
Calculation: Signal spacing = current bar index − prior same-direction signal bar index > configured minimum spacing, or the sweep exception: for long, current low < prior long-signal low and current close > prior long-signal close; for short, current high > prior short-signal high and current close < prior short-signal close
