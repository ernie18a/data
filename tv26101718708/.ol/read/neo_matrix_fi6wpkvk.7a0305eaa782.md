Calculation: Neo Wall = trailing lower ATR band while bullish; trailing upper ATR band while bearish; switch bearish after two consecutive closes below the wall and bullish after two consecutive closes above it
Calculation: Trend Guide direction = direction of change in a held HLC3 guide; update the guide only when HLC3 exceeds the prior guide by more than its held ATR range, using current-bar data
Calculation: Core = two-pole super smoother of HLC3; Core slope = Core minus its value at a shorter lag; slope threshold = the range of that slope over a longer lookback multiplied by a positive fraction
Calculation: Flow = smoothed money flow index of HL2, centered at 50; bullish flow = Flow exceeds the greater of its average over recent positive Flow observations and a positive threshold; bearish flow = Flow is below the lesser of its average over recent negative Flow observations and a negative threshold
Calculation: MACD = difference between a short-period and a longer-period exponential average; histogram = MACD minus its signal average; bullish candle state = MACD > 0 and histogram > 0; bearish candle state = MACD < 0 and histogram < 0
Calculation: Upper/lower stretch touch = high reaches or exceeds / low reaches or falls below Core plus/minus a smoothed true-range distance
Calculation: Wall setup touch = in the prior Wall regime, low reaches the Wall or its lower defense buffer when bullish, or high reaches the Wall or its upper defense buffer when bearish
Calculation: Setup touch = Wall setup touch OR either stretch touch
Calculation: Long/short context score = average over recent bars, excluding the current bar, of weighted bullish/bearish Flow bias, Core slope bias, and MACD histogram bias; each bias compares its change or magnitude against a threshold derived from its longer-lookback range
Calculation: Long/short watch allowed = no bearish/bullish strong Flow observation, respectively, during the recent context lookback, and no upper/lower stretch touch, respectively
Calculation: Long/short watch score = corresponding context score plus a positive weight for a lower/upper stretch touch, respectively
Long: Neo Wall is bullish OR (Neo Wall is bullish AND Trend Guide direction is rising) OR bullish Flow OR (Flow > 0 AND Flow >= its average over recent positive Flow observations) OR bullish candle state OR (Setup touch AND long watch allowed AND (NOT short watch allowed OR long watch score > short watch score))
Short: Neo Wall is bearish OR (Neo Wall is bearish AND Trend Guide direction is falling) OR bearish Flow OR (Flow < 0 AND Flow <= its average over recent negative Flow observations) OR bearish candle state OR (Setup touch AND short watch allowed AND (NOT long watch allowed OR short watch score > long watch score))
