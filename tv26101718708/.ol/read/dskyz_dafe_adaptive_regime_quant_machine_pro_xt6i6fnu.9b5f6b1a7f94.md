Calculation: Bullish pattern score = 0.5 × bullish engulfing + (0.4 if range regime, otherwise 0.2) × hammer + 0.2 × morning star + 0.2 × piercing + (0.3 if volatile regime, otherwise 0.15) × double bottom, where each pattern contributes 1 when true and 0 otherwise
Calculation: Bearish pattern score = 0.5 × bearish engulfing + (0.4 if range regime, otherwise 0.2) × shooting star + 0.2 × evening star + 0.2 × dark cloud + (0.3 if volatile regime, otherwise 0.15) × double top, where each pattern contributes 1 when true and 0 otherwise
Calculation: Bull score = bullish pattern score + 0.2 if fast EMA > slow EMA + 0.15 if RSI < 30 + 0.15 if MACD line > MACD signal + 0.1 if ATR > its longer-lookback simple average + 0.2 if higher-timeframe MACD line > signal, higher-timeframe RSI > 50, and higher-timeframe RSI < 70
Calculation: Bear score = bearish pattern score + 0.2 if fast EMA < slow EMA + 0.15 if RSI > 70 + 0.15 if MACD line < MACD signal + 0.1 if ATR > its longer-lookback simple average + 0.2 if higher-timeframe MACD line < signal, higher-timeframe RSI < 50, and higher-timeframe RSI > 30
Calculation: Bullish engulfing = prior close < prior open AND close > open AND close > prior open AND open < prior close AND low <= 1.01 × lowest low over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Hammer = high − low > 3 × absolute(open − close) AND (close − low) / (0.001 + high − low) > 0.6 AND low <= 1.01 × lowest low over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Morning star = close two bars ago < open two bars ago AND absolute(prior close − prior open) < 0.2 × (prior high − prior low) AND close > open AND close > midpoint of the open and close two bars ago AND low <= 1.01 × lowest low over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Piercing = prior close < prior open AND close > open AND close > midpoint of prior open and close AND open < prior close AND low <= 1.01 × lowest low over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Double bottom = low < prior low AND prior low > low two bars ago AND low two bars ago < low three bars ago AND close > open AND low <= 1.01 × lowest low over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = prior close > prior open AND close < open AND close < prior open AND open > prior close AND high >= 0.99 × highest high over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Shooting star = high − low > 3 × absolute(open − close) AND (high − close) / (0.001 + high − low) > 0.6 AND high >= 0.99 × highest high over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Evening star = close two bars ago > open two bars ago AND absolute(prior close − prior open) < 0.2 × (prior high − prior low) AND close < open AND close < midpoint of the open and close two bars ago AND high >= 0.99 × highest high over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Dark cloud = prior close > prior open AND close < open AND close < midpoint of prior open and close AND open > prior close AND high >= 0.99 × highest high over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Double top = high > prior high AND prior high < high two bars ago AND high two bars ago > high three bars ago AND close < open AND high >= 0.99 × highest high over the recent lookback AND volume > 1.5 × its lookback average
Calculation: Range regime = ADX < 25 AND (highest high − lowest low over the recent lookback) / close < 0.03 AND NOT (higher-timeframe MACD line > signal AND higher-timeframe RSI > 50) AND NOT (higher-timeframe MACD line < signal AND higher-timeframe RSI < 50)
Calculation: Volatile regime = Bollinger Band width > 1.5 × its longer-lookback average AND ATR > 1.2 × its longer-lookback average AND (higher-timeframe RSI > 70 OR higher-timeframe RSI < 30)
Calculation: Initial long stop = lowest low over the recent short lookback − stop distance; initial short stop = highest high over the recent short lookback + stop distance; stop distance is either the ATR-based stop distance or the configured fixed distance
Calculation: Initial target = entry-bar close + 2 × (entry-bar close − initial long stop) for longs; entry-bar close − 2 × (initial short stop − entry-bar close) for shorts
Calculation: Long trailing stop = maximum of prior long trailing stop and close − ATR × trailing multiplier once close > half the long target price; short trailing stop = minimum of prior short trailing stop and close + ATR × trailing multiplier once close < half the short target price
Long: bull score >= 1 AND drawdown from peak equity < 5% AND position size <= 0
Short: bear score >= 1 AND drawdown from peak equity < 5% AND position size >= 0
Exit: Long, low <= initial long stop OR low <= long trailing stop OR high >= long target OR close < initial long stop
Exit: Short, high >= initial short stop OR high >= short trailing stop OR low <= short target OR close > initial short stop
