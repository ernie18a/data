Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a higher-timeframe bar high/low that is the highest/lowest across equal lookbacks on both sides; confirm only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Daily range budget = median high-minus-low range of preceding completed higher-timeframe daily bars.
Calculation: Upper/lower budget boundary = session low plus the daily range budget / session high minus the daily range budget, respectively; session high and low update from chart bars.
Long: A confirmed chart bar closes above a confirmed swing low after price traded below it within the rejection window AND the setup remains valid (not past its validity period and price has not traded below the sweep extreme since rejection) AND the distance from the sweep low to the lower budget boundary is at or below the configured room limit.
Short: A confirmed chart bar closes below a confirmed swing high after price traded above it within the rejection window AND the setup remains valid (not past its validity period and price has not traded above the sweep extreme since rejection) AND the distance from the upper budget boundary to the sweep high is at or below the configured room limit.
