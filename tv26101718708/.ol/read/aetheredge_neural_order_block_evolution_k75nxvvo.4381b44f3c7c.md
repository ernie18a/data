Calculation: ATR = average true range over a lookback
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in the same-length windows before and after it
Calculation: Volume anomaly score = sigmoid(0.7 × (volume − 20-bar volume mean) / 20-bar volume standard deviation), using 0 when standard deviation is zero
Calculation: Liquidity score = sigmoid(1.5 × min(cluster count / 8, 1) + 1.2 × sweep flag − 1); cluster count is the number of bars in the 30-bar window from the candidate candle through the following bars whose low (bullish) or high (bearish) is within 0.5 × ATR of the OB boundary; bullish sweep flag is 1 when the candidate low is within 0.1 × ATR of the 30-bar lowest low and its close is above the candle midpoint, bearish sweep flag is 1 when its high is within 0.1 × ATR of the 30-bar highest high and its close is below the midpoint, otherwise 0
Calculation: MTF consensus score = sigmoid(weighted agreeing timeframe biases − 1.5); each bias is bullish when that timeframe's close is above its 21-period EMA and bearish otherwise; use three timeframes higher than the chart timeframe, with the highest timeframe receiving the greatest weight, and use each timeframe's last completed bar
Calculation: Displacement score = sigmoid(displacement / ATR − displacement threshold)
Calculation: OB quality score = (volume anomaly score × volume weight + liquidity score × liquidity weight + MTF consensus score × MTF weight + displacement score × displacement weight) / sum of the four weights; use 0.5 if the weight sum is zero
Long: Close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high AND the nearest preceding bearish candle within the OB search window has displacement (highest high from the candidate candle to the current bar − OB top) greater than the displacement threshold × ATR AND OB quality score ≥ minimum quality AND no unbroken bullish OB overlaps more than half the candidate OB's height
Short: Close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low AND the nearest preceding bullish candle within the OB search window has displacement (OB bottom − lowest low from the candidate candle to the current bar) greater than the displacement threshold × ATR AND OB quality score ≥ minimum quality AND no unbroken bearish OB overlaps more than half the candidate OB's height
Exit: Long, low (wick mitigation mode) or close (close mitigation mode) < bullish OB bottom − ATR × break buffer
Exit: Short, high (wick mitigation mode) or close (close mitigation mode) > bearish OB top + ATR × break buffer
