Calculation: Previous-day high, low, and close = high, low, and close of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar.
Calculation: Average Daily Range (ADR) = mean high-minus-low range of prior completed higher-timeframe daily bars over a lookback; use only completed bars.
Calculation: Day type = expansion if completed daily range ≥ ADR × expansion threshold; consolidation if ≤ ADR × consolidation threshold; otherwise reversal.
Calculation: Session high/low = highest high / lowest low of completed chart-timeframe bars in the configured Asian or London session so far that day.
Calculation: Candle body fraction = abs(close − open) ÷ (high − low).
Calculation: Bullish Fair Value Gap = low of the prior completed chart bar > high from two chart bars earlier; bearish Fair Value Gap = high of the prior completed chart bar < low from two chart bars earlier.
Calculation: ADR zone = current higher-timeframe daily open ± ADR × the zone's multiplier; use the 95% or 99% zone.
Long: The latest configured number of completed higher-timeframe daily bars are all consolidation AND current daily open < midpoint of previous-day high and low OR the latest configured number are all reversal AND current daily open < previous-day close OR prior completed chart bar low < previous-day low AND its close > previous-day low OR prior completed chart bar low < Asian or London session low AND its close > that level AND its candle body fraction ≥ the configured minimum OR a bullish Fair Value Gap exists AND the prior bar's low or high is within either ADR zone.
Short: The latest configured number of completed higher-timeframe daily bars are all consolidation AND current daily open ≥ midpoint of previous-day high and low OR the latest configured number are all reversal AND current daily open ≥ previous-day close OR prior completed chart bar high > previous-day high AND its close < previous-day high OR prior completed chart bar high > Asian or London session high AND its close < that level AND its candle body fraction ≥ the configured minimum OR a bearish Fair Value Gap exists AND the prior bar's low or high is within either ADR zone.
