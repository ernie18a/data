Calculation: Volatility cutoff = intra-bar coefficient × standard deviation over a lookback of log(high) − log(low) + inter-bar coefficient × standard deviation over the same lookback of log((high + low + close) / 3) − log(previous bar’s (high + low + close) / 3)
Long: Money flow > Volatility cutoff × close
Short: Money flow < −Volatility cutoff × close
