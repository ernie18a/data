Calculation: Estimated delta = sum lower-timeframe intrabar volume, signed positive when the intrabar close is above its open, negative when below; for ties, sign by comparison with the prior intrabar close, or zero if equal. Intrabar timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe; aggregate into delta-timeframe candles, whose timeframe is equal to or higher than the chart timeframe. Use only completed delta-timeframe candles for signals.
Calculation: Average absolute delta = mean of the absolute estimated deltas of the last N completed delta-timeframe candles, including the signal candle.
Long: On a completed delta-timeframe candle, (current estimated delta > 0 AND previous completed estimated delta < 0 AND candle close − open ≤ flat tolerance) OR (candle close − open < −flat tolerance AND current estimated delta > 0) OR (absolute current estimated delta ≥ absorption threshold × average absolute delta AND |candle close − open| ≤ flat tolerance AND current estimated delta < 0).
Short: On a completed delta-timeframe candle, (current estimated delta < 0 AND previous completed estimated delta > 0 AND candle close − open ≥ −flat tolerance) OR (candle close − open > flat tolerance AND current estimated delta < 0) OR (absolute current estimated delta ≥ absorption threshold × average absolute delta AND |candle close − open| ≤ flat tolerance AND current estimated delta > 0).
