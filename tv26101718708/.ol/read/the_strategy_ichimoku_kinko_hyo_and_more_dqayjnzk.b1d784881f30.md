Calculation: Ichimoku lines = Tenkan (midpoint of highest high and lowest low over its lookback), Kijun (same over its longer lookback), Senkou A (midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun), Senkou B (same midpoint over a still longer lookback); cloud bounds are Senkou A and B shifted forward by the Kijun lookback, and Chikou comparisons use current close against prior highs/lows and prior cloud bounds at the Kijun lookback.
Calculation: TRIX = change in triple EMA of log(close); MACD = difference of shorter- and longer-lookback EMAs, with its signal line an EMA of MACD.
Calculation: RSI stochastic = smoothed stochastic of RSI; K is shorter-smoothed than D.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe sampling = chart timeframe or a configured higher timeframe; on a higher timeframe use its forming bar when repainting is allowed, otherwise its previous completed bar. Signals and filters marked as having no timeframe filter use the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Long/short confluence score = sum of bullish/bearish state scores from SMA(shorter lookback) vs SMA(longer lookback), EMA(shorter) vs EMA(longer), price vs Kijun, Tenkan vs Kijun, strong Ichimoku B1 and B2 states, price vs Parabolic SAR, price vs latest confirmed fractal bounds, MACD vs signal, RSI vs 50, DMI direction, Klinger signal vs zero, Vortex positive vs negative, price vs SuperTrend, price vs Tenkan, EMA(21) vs SMA(20), and rising HMA(15), ALMA(9), and MACD. Each ordinary state scores 1 bullish or 0 bearish; rising HMA and ALMA score 2, otherwise -1. State scores retain their last value when neither comparison is true.
Long: Any enabled entry signal is true, all enabled long entry filters are true, and no enabled bearish exit filter is true. Entry signals are OR-connected; the two entry signal groups are OR-connected. Entry filters are AND-connected. Entry signals: TRIX turns rising; short-lookback EMA crosses above long-lookback EMA; HMA, ALMA, or MACD turns rising; +DI crosses above -DI with ADX below +DI and above -DI (modified DMI), or +DI is above -DI and ADX is above its threshold (classic DMI), or ADX turns upward while +DI exceeds -DI; bullish pin bar; prior bar is inside the bar before it and current close exceeds prior high; prior bar is an outside bar and current high exceeds prior high; prior bar is inside and the next bar is outside and current high exceeds that bar's high (sandwich); current close exceeds prior high (breakout 1); current high exceeds prior high (breakout 2); current low exceeds prior low; close is nonzero (every-bar entry); prior close exceeds SMA; current high exceeds latest confirmed fractal high; reverse bullish fractal (confirmed pivot low with current up bar and prior down bar, or its stated higher-low variant); RSI crosses above 50; current high exceeds Parabolic SAR or SuperTrend; close crosses above Kijun, cloud upper bound, Tenkan, or Chikou comparison level; Senkou A crosses above Senkou B; Tenkan crosses above cloud upper bound or Kijun; B1 state (Tenkan >= Kijun, high above cloud, close above prior high and prior cloud upper bound, Senkou A > Senkou B); B2 state (Tenkan >= Kijun, high above cloud, close above prior high); grid countertrend/reentry trigger; upper fractal trendline break; three-segment higher-low pattern followed by current high above prior high; long confluence score exceeds its threshold; or Ichimoku confluence score exceeds its threshold. For non-pyramiding entries, conditions stated as crosses use the corresponding level/state comparison where applicable: rising/falling slope entries use rising/falling state, and DMI entries use the stated directional and ADX comparisons. With pyramiding enabled, slope entries require a turn into the slope direction and level/state entries require a cross.

Short: Any enabled entry signal is true, all enabled short entry filters are true, and no enabled bullish exit filter is true. Entry signals are OR-connected; the two entry signal groups are OR-connected. Entry filters are AND-connected.
