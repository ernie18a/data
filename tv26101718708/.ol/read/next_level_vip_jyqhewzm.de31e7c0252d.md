Calculation: Rolling VWAP = rolling sum of typical price × volume divided by rolling sum of volume
Calculation: Trend confirmation = positive when smoothed breakout direction, close change over a short lookback, and EMA change over a shorter lookback are all positive; negative when all are negative; otherwise zero. Breakout direction is positive when close exceeds the prior bar’s highest high over a long lookback, negative when it falls below the prior bar’s lowest low over that lookback, and zero otherwise; smooth breakout direction with a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Whale-volume event = volume exceeds average volume multiplied by a threshold, ATR divided by its longer-lookback average, and a chart-timeframe scaling factor. Sustained whale volume = no non-event within a short lookback.
Calculation: Whale-volume up = whale-volume event AND sustained whale volume AND close above rolling VWAP AND close rises AND volume exceeds its shorter-lookback average by a threshold. Whale-volume down = same conditions with close below rolling VWAP and falling.
Calculation: Signal score = weighted sum of: ADX above its threshold or minimum; volume above its average by a threshold; whale-volume event; RSI on the corresponding side of its midpoint and not beyond the corresponding extreme; breakout direction and close on the same side of EMA; whale-volume up (positive) or down (negative); and sustained whale volume.
Calculation: Volatility filter = ATR below its average by an upper threshold AND above its average by a lower threshold AND its one-bar change is below a fraction of that average.
Long: (close crosses above EMA OR whale-volume up AND close above prior EMA OR lowest low over a short lookback is below EMA AND close above EMA AND RSI is between lower and upper bounds AND range over that lookback exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold AND close above rolling VWAP AND volume above its average AND trend confirmation positive) AND signal score meets its threshold AND volatility filter
Short: (close crosses below EMA OR whale-volume down AND close below prior EMA OR highest high over a short lookback is above EMA AND close below EMA AND RSI is between lower and upper bounds AND range over that lookback exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold AND close below rolling VWAP AND volume above its average AND trend confirmation negative) AND signal score meets its threshold AND volatility filter
Exit: long, close is below entry price by a maximum-loss percentage OR close reaches the take-profit level set at entry from the recent highest high plus an ATR-scaled distance OR close reaches the trailing stop OR trend confirmation is negative
Exit: short, close is above entry price by a maximum-loss percentage OR close reaches the take-profit level set at entry from the recent lowest low minus an ATR-scaled distance OR close reaches the trailing stop OR trend confirmation is positive
