Calculation: Volume-profile zones = Divide the range from the lowest low to the highest high in the profile into equal price bins. Allocate each bar’s volume to bins in proportion to the overlap of its high-low range with each bin. A low-volume node is a bin whose volume is at or below the configured percentage of the maximum bin volume and is strictly lower than every bin within the configured neighboring-bin lookback on both sides. Join adjacent node bins into zones and expand each zone by the configured number of bins. A zone below the current close is green; a zone above it is red.
Long: Green zone, low <= zone top AND close > zone top OR red zone, close > zone top AND volume > 0
Short: Red zone, high >= zone bottom AND close < zone bottom OR green zone, close < zone bottom AND volume > 0
