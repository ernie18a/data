Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = a current low above the high two bars earlier, with gap size at least the configured fraction of ATR; retain the gap until used, replaced, or expired by its configured lifetime.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = a current high below the low two bars earlier, with gap size at least the configured fraction of ATR; retain the gap until used, replaced, or expired by its configured lifetime.
Calculation: Bullish FVG pullback = a later bar within the gap's configured lifetime has low at or below the gap top, closes above its open, and closes above the gap bottom.
Calculation: Bearish FVG pullback = a later bar within the gap's configured lifetime has high at or above the gap bottom, closes below its open, and closes below the gap top.
Calculation: EMA trend state = fast EMA (shorter lookback) above slow EMA (longer lookback), close above fast EMA, and RSI above its midpoint; bearish state is the reverse.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe trend = previous completed bar's close above its EMA; bearish trend is the reverse. Use a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe.
Long: position is not long AND signal bar is inside any enabled session (or no sessions are enabled) AND signal bar close is outside every enabled news window AND ((entry method is FVG Pullback AND Bullish FVG pullback OR entry method is EMA Trend AND EMA trend state began on this bar) AND sensitivity is Aggressive OR close is above the long-term EMA AND (sensitivity is Balanced OR higher-timeframe trend is bullish AND ADX meets the configured strength threshold)).
Short: position is not short AND signal bar is inside any enabled session (or no sessions are enabled) AND signal bar close is outside every enabled news window AND ((entry method is FVG Pullback AND Bearish FVG pullback OR entry method is EMA Trend AND bearish EMA trend state began on this bar) AND sensitivity is Aggressive OR close is below the long-term EMA AND (sensitivity is Balanced OR higher-timeframe trend is bearish AND ADX meets the configured strength threshold)).
Exit: long, a bar after entry reaches take-profit (high >= entry price + configured target) OR stop-loss (low <= entry price - configured stop) OR a short signal occurs.
Exit: short, a bar after entry reaches take-profit (low <= entry price - configured target) OR stop-loss (high >= entry price + configured stop) OR a long signal occurs.
Calculation: If one chart bar reaches both target and stop, use its lower-timeframe intrabars in chronological order to determine which level was reached first.
