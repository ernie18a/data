Calculation: Structure direction (on chart timeframe and a higher timeframe) = score bullish: +3 if the latest confirmed pivot low is above the prior confirmed pivot low by more than the timeframe's ATR times a buffer; +2 if the latest confirmed pivot high is above the prior confirmed pivot high by more than that buffer; +3 if the previous bar's close is above the latest confirmed pivot high by more than ATR times a breakout buffer; +1 if the previous bar's close is above the midpoint of the latest confirmed pivot high and low. Score bearish symmetrically for lower highs (+3), lower lows (+2), a previous close below the latest pivot low by more than the breakout buffer (+3), and a previous close below the pivot midpoint (+1). Direction is bullish if bullish score >= 5 and >= bearish score + 2; bearish if bearish score >= 5 and >= bullish score + 2; otherwise neutral. Use longer pivot lookback on the chart timeframe than on the higher timeframe. Higher-timeframe calculations use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Daily context direction = score bullish for the last completed daily bar: +1 if its close > open, +1 if its close > (high + low)/2, +1 if its close > the close five daily bars earlier, +1 if its close > the 5-period daily EMA evaluated on that completed bar. Score bearish for each symmetric less-than comparison. Direction is bullish if bullish score >= 3 and > bearish score; bearish if bearish score >= 3 and > bullish score; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Previous London range = the high and low of the most recently completed London session on chart-timeframe bars; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: London direction = bullish if current close > previous London high, or otherwise if current close > previous London midpoint; bearish if current close < previous London low, or otherwise if current close < previous London midpoint; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Base direction = assign 5 points to the side indicated by higher-timeframe structure direction, 3 points to the side indicated by chart-timeframe structure direction, 2 points to the side indicated by daily context direction, and 1 point to the side indicated by London direction. Bullish if bullish points >= 6 and >= bearish points + 2; bearish if bearish points >= 6 and >= bullish points + 2; otherwise neutral. Daily and London context may be disabled, in which case their direction is neutral.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish reversal = higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND chart-timeframe structure has a higher low and an upward break of structure AND (current close > latest higher-timeframe pivot high OR current close > previous London high OR daily context is not bearish).
Calculation: Confirmed bearish reversal = higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND chart-timeframe structure has a lower high and a downward break of structure AND (current close < latest higher-timeframe pivot low OR current close < previous London low OR daily context is not bullish).
Calculation: Reversal warning = (higher-timeframe structure is bearish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a higher low without an upward break of structure OR has a higher low with an upward break of structure OR chart-timeframe structure is bullish OR current close > previous London high), unless confirmed bullish reversal) OR (higher-timeframe structure is bullish AND (chart-timeframe structure has a lower high without a downward break of structure OR has a lower high with a downward break of structure OR chart-timeframe structure is bearish OR current close < previous London low), unless confirmed bearish reversal).
Calculation: Final direction = bullish if confirmed bullish reversal; otherwise bearish if confirmed bearish reversal; otherwise neutral if reversal warning; otherwise base direction.
Long: Final direction is bullish AND current bar is within the configured trading session.
Short: Final direction is bearish AND current bar is within the configured trading session.
