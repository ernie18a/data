Calculation: Normalized volume = volume / the 100th percentile of volume over the 500-bar lookback × 100
Calculation: Resistance level = the prior bar’s highest high over the lookback, set when that prior high equals the prior lookback highest high and the current high is lower; retain until updated
Calculation: Support level = the prior bar’s lowest low over the lookback, set when that prior low equals the prior lookback lowest low and the current low is higher; retain until updated
Long: Normalized volume > support volume threshold AND a support level has been established
Short: Normalized volume > resistance volume threshold AND a resistance level has been established
