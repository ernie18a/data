Calculation: Ease of Movement = SMA(divisor × (current HL2 − previous HL2) × (high − low) / volume, lookback), where HL2 = (high + low) / 2
Long: close > shortest-period EMA AND shortest-period EMA > second-shortest-period EMA AND second-shortest-period EMA > third-shortest-period EMA AND third-shortest-period EMA > longest-period EMA AND Ease of Movement > 0 OR shortest-period EMA crosses above both second-shortest-period EMA and third-shortest-period EMA on the current bar AND Ease of Movement > 0
Exit: long, close < shortest-period EMA AND shortest-period EMA < second-shortest-period EMA AND second-shortest-period EMA < third-shortest-period EMA AND third-shortest-period EMA < longest-period EMA AND Ease of Movement < 0 OR shortest-period EMA crosses below both second-shortest-period EMA and third-shortest-period EMA on the current bar AND Ease of Movement < 0
