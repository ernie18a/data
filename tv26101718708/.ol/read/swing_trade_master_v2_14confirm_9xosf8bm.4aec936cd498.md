Calculation: Choppiness Index = 100 × log10(sum of true range over its lookback / (highest high − lowest low over its lookback)) / log10(lookback); use 50 if the high-low range is zero.
Calculation: Bull score = sum of 14 votes: fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA; RSI > its midpoint and below overbought; MACD bullish crossover or MACD line > signal line and > zero; Stochastic RSI K crosses above D and K is below overbought; ADX meets its minimum and +DI > −DI; CCI crosses above oversold or is > 0 and below overbought; close > Bollinger middle band and <= upper band; Williams %R crosses above oversold; volume > its moving average; Supertrend is bullish; close > rolling VWAP; Squeeze momentum is positive and rising or a squeeze has just released; close > Parabolic SAR; MFI is below oversold or crosses above oversold.
Calculation: Bear score = sum of 14 votes: fast EMA < mid EMA < slow EMA; RSI < its midpoint and above oversold; MACD bearish crossover or MACD line < signal line and < zero; Stochastic RSI K crosses below D and K is above oversold; ADX meets its minimum and −DI > +DI; CCI crosses below overbought or is < 0 and above oversold; close < Bollinger middle band and >= lower band; Williams %R crosses below overbought; volume > its moving average; Supertrend is bearish; close < rolling VWAP; Squeeze momentum is negative and falling or a squeeze has just released; close < Parabolic SAR; MFI is above overbought or crosses below overbought.
Calculation: Squeeze momentum = linear regression of close − ((highest high + lowest low)/2 + SMA(close)) / 2, using the squeeze lookback; a squeeze releases when Bollinger Bands are no longer inside the Keltner Channel after being inside on the prior bar.
Long: Choppiness filter is disabled or Choppiness Index <= its configured maximum AND bull score >= its configured minimum AND (MACD line crosses above signal line OR RSI crosses above oversold OR Stochastic RSI K crosses above D while K < overbought OR Williams %R crosses above oversold OR Supertrend flips bullish OR close flips above Parabolic SAR) AND bear score <= bull score AND the prior signal was not long.
Short: Choppiness filter is disabled or Choppiness Index <= its configured maximum AND bear score >= its configured minimum AND (MACD line crosses below signal line OR RSI crosses below overbought OR Stochastic RSI K crosses below D while K > oversold OR Williams %R crosses below overbought OR Supertrend flips bearish OR close flips below Parabolic SAR) AND bull score <= bear score AND the prior signal was not short.
Exit: long, at least three bars have elapsed since entry AND (Supertrend flips bearish OR close flips below Parabolic SAR OR at least two of: RSI crosses below overbought; MACD line crosses below signal line; Stochastic RSI K > overbought and crosses below D; close >= upper Bollinger Band and bear score meets the exit minimum; CCI crosses below overbought; Williams %R crosses below overbought; MFI crosses below overbought; close crosses below rolling VWAP) AND no long exit has already fired for this trade.
Exit: short, at least three bars have elapsed since entry AND (Supertrend flips bullish OR close flips above Parabolic SAR OR at least two of: RSI crosses above oversold; MACD line crosses above signal line; Stochastic RSI K < oversold and crosses above D; close <= lower Bollinger Band and bull score meets the exit minimum; CCI crosses above oversold; Williams %R crosses above oversold; MFI crosses above oversold; close crosses above rolling VWAP) AND no short exit has already fired for this trade.
