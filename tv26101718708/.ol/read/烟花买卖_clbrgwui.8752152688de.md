Calculation: First fractal adaptive moving average = Recursively smooth its input price, initialized from zero, using alpha derived from the fractal dimension D = log((R1 + R2)/R3)/log(2), where R1 is the high-low range of the first half-window divided by that half-window, R2 is the high-low range of the full window shifted back by half a window divided by half a window, and R3 is the full-window high-low range divided by the full window.
