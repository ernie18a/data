Calculation: SuperTrend direction = set lower band to source − ATR × multiplier and upper band to source + ATR × multiplier; carry the lower band forward as the maximum of its current and prior values when the prior close is above the prior lower band, and carry the upper band forward as the minimum of its current and prior values when the prior close is below the prior upper band; direction flips up when prior direction is down and close exceeds the prior upper band, and flips down when prior direction is up and close falls below the prior lower band
Calculation: ATR = Wilder moving average of true range
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = simple moving average of close ± standard deviation of close × deviation multiplier
Long: SuperTrend direction flips up AND MACD line crosses above its signal line AND RSI > 50 AND close crosses above the upper Bollinger Band AND volume > simple moving average of volume × spike multiplier AND ATR / close > minimum volatility threshold
Short: SuperTrend direction flips down AND MACD line crosses below its signal line AND RSI < 50 AND close crosses below the lower Bollinger Band AND volume > simple moving average of volume × spike multiplier AND ATR / close > minimum volatility threshold
Exit: Long, low <= entry price − ATR × stop multiplier
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR × stop multiplier

Exit: Short, low <= entry price − ATR × first target multiplier
