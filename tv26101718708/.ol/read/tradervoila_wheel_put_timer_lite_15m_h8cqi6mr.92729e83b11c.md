Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close) / 3, reset each trading session
Short: The lowest RSI over a short lookback is below a threshold AND current RSI is above previous-bar RSI AND close is at least a threshold percentage below session VWAP AND close is above the simple moving average of daily closes over a longer lookback (daily timeframe, higher than the chart timeframe, using the last completed daily bar) AND during the regular market session after the opening skip interval AND more than a cooldown interval of chart bars has elapsed since the previous short signal
Exit: short, RSI is at or above a strength threshold AND close is above session VWAP AND during the regular market session after the opening skip interval AND more than a cooldown interval of chart bars has elapsed since the previous exit signal
