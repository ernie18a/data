Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close
Calculation: RSI average = simple moving average of RSI
Calculation: Short-term highest volume = highest volume over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Long-term highest volume = highest volume over a longer lookback
Long: RSI > 30 AND previous RSI < 30 AND the short-term highest volume equals the long-term highest volume on any bar in the current consecutive RSI-below-30 stretch
Short: RSI < 70 AND previous RSI > 70 AND the short-term highest volume equals the long-term highest volume on any bar in the current consecutive RSI-above-70 stretch
Exit: Long, low reaches the stop price below entry price OR, after RSI first recovers above 30, RSI average crosses above RSI after having been below RSI
Exit: Short, high reaches the stop price above entry price OR, after RSI first recovers above 30, RSI average crosses below RSI after having been above RSI
