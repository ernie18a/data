Calculation: CVD balance = cumulative sum of lower-timeframe completed-bar volume multiplied by the sign of each bar’s close minus its previous close.
Calculation: Retention candle = selected CVD series as close, previous close as open, with high and low extended by a smoothed candle body; its EMA uses a shorter lookback than the macro CVD EMA.
Calculation: Pivot low/high = a bar whose low/high is lower/higher than the preceding and following bars over the respective left and right lookbacks; confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Long: CVD above its EMA AND EMA rising beyond its slope threshold AND CVD above its value at the momentum lookback bars ago OR CVD above its EMA AND EMA not rising beyond its slope threshold OR price forms a lower pivot low while CVD at that pivot is higher than at the previous price pivot low OR CVD is below its EMA while retention-candle high and low are both higher than at the structure lookback bars ago
Short: CVD below its EMA AND EMA falling beyond its slope threshold AND CVD below its value at the momentum lookback bars ago OR CVD below its EMA AND EMA not falling beyond its slope threshold OR price forms a higher pivot high while CVD at that pivot is lower than at the previous price pivot high OR CVD is above its EMA while retention-candle high and low are both lower than at the structure lookback bars ago
