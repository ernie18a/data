Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low/high = a bar whose low/high is lower/higher than the lows/highs of the same number of higher-timeframe bars on each side; use it only after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Secondary low/high at a primary pivot = the secondary market's low/high on the same higher-timeframe bar as that primary pivot.
Calculation: Bearish origin open = the open of the first chart bar with close < open after a bar with close >= open; retain it through consecutive bearish bars until a bullish signal, then clear it.
Calculation: Bullish origin open = the open of the first chart bar with close > open after a bar with close <= open; retain it through consecutive bullish bars until a bearish signal, then clear it.
Long: At each new confirmed higher-timeframe pivot low, if a prior confirmed pivot low and its secondary low exist, (current primary pivot low < prior primary pivot low AND current secondary low >= prior secondary low) OR (current primary pivot low >= prior primary pivot low AND current secondary low < prior secondary low); OR current chart close > bearish origin open AND prior chart close <= bearish origin open.
Short: At each new confirmed higher-timeframe pivot high, if a prior confirmed pivot high and its secondary high exist, (current primary pivot high > prior primary pivot high AND current secondary high <= prior secondary high) OR (current primary pivot high <= prior primary pivot high AND current secondary pivot high > prior secondary high); OR current chart close < bullish origin open AND prior chart close >= bullish origin open.
