Calculation: Short-term pivot lookback = max(3, round((11 − structure-sensitivity parameter) × smoothed-volatility ratio^1.5)), where smoothed-volatility ratio is EMA(ATR(200) / SMA(ATR(200), 200), 50); use 1.0 for the ratio when unavailable, or 1.0 as the multiplier when auto-adjustment is off.
Calculation: Active short-term high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low using that lookback, if its kNN confidence meets the confidence threshold; a pivot is confirmed after the lookback number of bars on both sides. kNN confidence is the mean score of the k nearest prior same-side pivots by Manhattan distance in relative ATR and relative volume; relative ATR = ATR(14) / SMA(ATR(14), 100), relative volume = volume / SMA(volume, 100), every pivot score is 1, and confidence is 0.5 when the history has no more than k pivots. The active level becomes inactive when a close crosses it.
Calculation: Bullish close position = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low); bearish close position = 100 × (high − close) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Structure bias = bullish when close is above the selected structure high or above the midpoint of its high-low range; bearish when close is below the selected structure low or below that midpoint; otherwise neutral. The selected structure uses the configured bias source; in Auto mode use the highest available of long-, medium-, then short-term structure.
Long: close > active short-term high AND bullish close position ≥ minimum-close-position parameter AND (maximum-volume filter disabled OR volume / lowest volume among the preceding lookback bars × 100 ≤ maximum-volume parameter) AND (prior-volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ previous bar volume) AND (prior-bias filter disabled OR previous bar's structure bias is bearish or neutral) AND NOT (close > open AND each of the previous three bars closed above its open) AND the full condition was false on the previous bar.
Short: close < active short-term low AND bearish close position ≥ minimum-close-position parameter AND (maximum-volume filter disabled OR volume / lowest volume among the preceding lookback bars × 100 ≤ maximum-volume parameter) AND (prior-volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ previous bar volume) AND (prior-bias filter disabled OR previous bar's structure bias is bullish or neutral) AND NOT (close < open AND each of the previous three bars closed below its open) AND the full condition was false on the previous bar.
