Calculation: PDH/PDL = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar
Calculation: PWH/PWL = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe weekly bar
Calculation: PMH/PML = high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe monthly bar
Calculation: Session VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 × volume since the current session began, divided by cumulative volume
Calculation: Monthly anchored VWAP = cumulative sum of (high + low + close) / 3 × volume since the current month began, divided by cumulative volume
Calculation: Uptrend filter = current close is above each of the last completed higher-timeframe daily EMA(8), EMA(21), EMA(50), EMA(100), EMA(200), SMA(50), SMA(100), and SMA(200
Long: (close > any of PDL, PWL, PML AND previous close <= that level) OR (close > any of PDH, PWH, PMH AND previous close > that level AND close two bars ago <= that level) OR (previous close > any of PDH, PWH, PMH, PDL, PWL, PML AND low <= that level AND close >= that level AND (uptrend filter is false OR time since the latest session high is within the configured limit)) OR (close was above a monthly anchored VWAP on the previous bar AND low <= that VWAP AND close >= that VWAP OR previous close was below that VWAP AND close >= that VWAP) AND (uptrend filter is false OR time since the latest session high is within the configured limit) OR (session open was previously lost AND current and previous closes are above it) OR (price was previously above session open AND low >= session open AND low <= session open plus the configured tolerance AND close > session open AND close > open AND session open has not been wick-crossed) OR (price was previously above session open AND low < session open AND close > session open AND close > open AND session open has not been lost) OR (the configured number of previous closes were below Session VWAP AND close > Session VWAP AND close > open AND close < PDH) OR (the configured number of previous closes were above Session VWAP AND low <= Session VWAP plus the configured tolerance AND close > Session VWAP AND close > open) OR (a weekly or monthly level was previously closed above during the session AND the current bar tests and closes above it green, with the level not previously lost; for an unbroken test, low is at or above the level and within the configured tolerance, and for a wick reclaim, low is below the level; no staged level event occurs on this bar)
Short: (high >= any of PDH, PWH, PMH AND close < that level AND previous high < that level) OR (close < any of PDL, PWL, PML AND previous close >= that level) OR (previous close > any of PDH, PWH, PMH AND close < that level AND the prior-high break occurred within the configured sweep window) OR (the configured number of previous closes were above Session VWAP AND close < Session VWAP AND close < open AND close > PDL)
Exit: long, close > PDH AND Session VWAP slope is flat or falling
