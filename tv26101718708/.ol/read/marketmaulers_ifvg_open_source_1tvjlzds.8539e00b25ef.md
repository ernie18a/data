Calculation: Fair value gap = bullish gap when current low > high two bars earlier; bearish gap when current high < low two bars earlier; record after the third bar closes.
Calculation: Displacement = within the last five chart bars, absolute candle body ≥ 1.2 × ATR(14).
Calculation: HTF fair value gap = bullish when low > high two HTF bars earlier and the middle candle closes above its open; bearish when high < low two HTF bars earlier and the middle candle closes below its open. Use higher-timeframe bars; the Delivery check uses a tapped live gap, with historical evaluation able to include a still-forming HTF bar.
Calculation: Clear target = an unswept liquidity level on the profit side at least 1.5 × ATR away; equal-high/low targets require at least two unswept swing levels within 0.1 × ATR.
Calculation: Setup grade = 1 for the inversion, plus 1 each for a direction-matched liquidity sweep, HTF-gap Delivery, Displacement, Clear target, and SMT; a sweep is a raid of a swing, data-candle, session, or prior day/week/month high or low followed by a close back inside within three bars. Sweep credit expires when price trades beyond the raid extreme; minor sweeps expire sooner than major sweeps.
Long: A bearish fair value gap closes above its upper boundary within the inversion window AND the setup grade meets the configured minimum AND (sweep is required and a sellside sweep sponsors the gap within the pairing window OR sweep is graded and the grade is at least two) AND any active opposite-side setup passes reversal proof AND the entry-economy rules permit the setup.
Short: A bullish fair value gap closes below its lower boundary within the inversion window AND the setup grade meets the configured minimum AND (sweep is required and a buyside sweep sponsors the gap within the pairing window OR sweep is graded and the grade is at least two) AND any active opposite-side setup passes reversal proof AND the entry-economy rules permit the setup.
Exit: long, close below the IFVG lower boundary OR the configured completion level is reached (selected SD rung by high, or TP by close).
Exit: short, close above the IFVG upper boundary OR the configured completion level is reached (selected SD rung by low, or TP by close).
