Calculation: Overall direction = majority direction across the chart timeframe and automatically selected higher timeframes; ties follow the chart timeframe. Each timeframe is bullish when its fast EMA is above its slow EMA, otherwise bearish.
Calculation: ADX-weighted consensus score = sum of ADX values for timeframes agreeing with the overall direction divided by the sum of ADX values across all included timeframes, multiplied by 100; use the same ADX lookback on each timeframe.
Long: Fast EMA > slow EMA on the chart timeframe's last completed bar OR fast EMA > slow EMA on a higher timeframe's last completed bar OR close > slow EMA on the chart timeframe's last completed bar OR close > slow EMA on a higher timeframe's last completed bar OR overall direction is bullish AND ADX-weighted consensus score >= 50% OR all included timeframes are bullish OR chart-timeframe direction changes from bearish to bullish
Short: Fast EMA <= slow EMA on the chart timeframe's last completed bar OR fast EMA <= slow EMA on a higher timeframe's last completed bar OR close <= slow EMA on the chart timeframe's last completed bar OR close <= slow EMA on a higher timeframe's last completed bar OR overall direction is bearish AND ADX-weighted consensus score >= 50% OR all included timeframes are bearish OR chart-timeframe direction changes from bullish to bearish
