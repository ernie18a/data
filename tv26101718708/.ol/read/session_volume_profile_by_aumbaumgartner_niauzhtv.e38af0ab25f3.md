Calculation: Session VAL and VAH = For each completed session, distribute each bar’s volume evenly across price buckets from its low to high, or assign it to the close bucket according to the selected distribution mode. Find the highest-volume bucket, then expand the value area from it one bucket at a time toward the side with greater combined volume in its next two buckets, preferring the upper side on ties, until it contains the configured share of session volume; VAL and VAH are its lower and upper boundaries.
Long: close < most recently completed session’s VAL AND price has remained outside the value area continuously for less than the configured balance duration
Short: close > most recently completed session’s VAH AND price has remained outside the value area continuously for less than the configured balance duration
