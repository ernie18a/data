Calculation: Swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than both adjacent bars on the same timeframe.
Calculation: True swing high/low = a swing high/low at a bullish-to-bearish / bearish-to-bullish candle turn: for a high, the older candle is bullish and the pivot bearish, or the pivot bullish and newer candle bearish; for a low, the older candle is bearish and the pivot bullish, or the pivot bearish and newer candle bullish.
Calculation: Average range = mean high minus low of the visible completed candles on the relevant timeframe.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fair value gap = on completed bars of a timeframe higher than the chart, current low above the high two bars earlier, or current high below the low two bars earlier; the intervening candle's body points toward the gap and is at least a configurable fraction of average range, and gap height is at least a configurable fraction of average range. A close through the far edge inverts the gap's direction; a later close back through the near edge spends it.
Calculation: Lower-layer fair value gap = the same three-bar gap definition on completed bars of timeframes higher than the chart but lower than the PO3 timeframe.
Calculation: Suspension block = a run of at least three same-direction higher-timeframe candles with each candle opening beyond the previous candle's close in the run's direction, with no gap spanning more than one timeframe period; the span from the oldest close to newest open meets a configurable minimum fraction of average range. A close through the far edge inverts its direction; a later close back through the near edge spends it.
Long: A higher-timeframe or lower-layer swing low is first raided (bar low < swing low and close > swing low) OR a true swing low is first broken by a later bar's close above its full wick extreme, at least two bars after the swing, with the configured displacement condition satisfied (bar range meets its configurable average-range threshold OR a fresh gap opens upward) OR a bullish higher-timeframe or lower-layer fair value gap is live or inverted from a bearish gap OR a bullish suspension block is live or inverted from a bearish block.
Short: A higher-timeframe or lower-layer swing high is first raided (bar high > swing high and close < swing high) OR a true swing high is first broken by a later bar's close below its full wick extreme, at least two bars after the swing, with the configured displacement condition satisfied (bar range meets its configurable average-range threshold OR a fresh gap opens downward) OR a bearish higher-timeframe or lower-layer fair value gap is live or inverted from a bullish gap OR a bearish suspension block is live or inverted from a bullish block.
