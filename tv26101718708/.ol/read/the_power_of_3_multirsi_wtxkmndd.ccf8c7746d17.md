Calculation: RSI fast, RSI medium, RSI slow = RSI of close on the chart timeframe, using progressively longer lookbacks.
Calculation: RSI 4 = RSI of close on an optional timeframe; if that timeframe is lower than the chart timeframe, use the chart timeframe. On a higher timeframe, use its last completed bar in confirmed mode or its still-forming bar otherwise.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot high/low = a local RSI high/low using configurable left and right lookbacks; confirm it only after the right lookback bars have completed.
Calculation: Pivot distance = number of bars between consecutive same-kind RSI pivots, within configurable minimum and maximum bounds.
Long: At a confirmed RSI pivot low, price low < preceding pivot low AND RSI pivot value > preceding pivot value AND pivot distance is in bounds (regular bullish divergence), OR price low > preceding pivot low AND RSI pivot value < preceding pivot value AND pivot distance is in bounds (hidden bullish divergence), on any of RSI fast, RSI medium, RSI slow, or RSI 4.
Long: RSI fast, RSI medium, and RSI slow are all <= the oversold level AND (RSI 4 alignment is not required OR RSI 4 <= its configured oversold level AND (turning filter is off OR RSI 4 > its previous value)) AND (first-alignment mode is off OR the preceding bar was not oversold-aligned).
Short: At a confirmed RSI pivot high, price high > preceding pivot high AND RSI pivot value < preceding pivot value AND pivot distance is in bounds (regular bearish divergence), OR price high < preceding pivot high AND RSI pivot value > preceding pivot value AND pivot distance is in bounds (hidden bearish divergence), on any of RSI fast, RSI medium, RSI slow, or RSI 4.
Short: RSI fast, RSI medium, and RSI slow are all >= the overbought level AND (RSI 4 alignment is not required OR RSI 4 >= its configured overbought level AND (turning filter is off OR RSI 4 < its previous value)) AND (first-alignment mode is off OR the preceding bar was not overbought-aligned).
