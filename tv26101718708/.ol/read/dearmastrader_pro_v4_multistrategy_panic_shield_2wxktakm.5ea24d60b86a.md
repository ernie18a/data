Calculation: ZLEMA = 2 × EMA(source, length) − EMA(EMA(source, length), length)
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the prior RSI pivot low AND source at the confirmed pivot is lower than at the prior pivot; confirm after the right-pivot lookback
Calculation: Bearish RSI divergence = confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the prior RSI pivot high AND source at the confirmed pivot is higher than at the prior pivot; confirm after the right-pivot lookback
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of MACD, where MACD = fast EMA(source) − slow EMA(source)
Calculation: Long trailing stop = highest value of source − ATR × multiplier since entering long
Calculation: Short trailing stop = lowest value of source + ATR × multiplier since entering short
Long: (Zero Lag entry selected AND source crosses above ZLEMA + ATR × multiplier AND ZLEMA > prior ZLEMA) OR (Bollinger + ATR entry selected AND source crosses above upper Bollinger Band) OR (RSI Divergence entry selected AND bullish RSI divergence) OR (MACD / SMA Cross entry selected AND MACD crosses above its signal line) OR (Magical DCA Shield entry selected AND source > macro EMA AND source crosses above ZLEMA AND RSI < overbought threshold); AND trading direction permits long; AND (trend filter disabled OR source > trend EMA); AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume EMA × minimum-volume multiplier)
Short: (Zero Lag entry selected AND source crosses below ZLEMA − ATR × multiplier AND ZLEMA < prior ZLEMA) OR (Bollinger + ATR entry selected AND source crosses below lower Bollinger Band) OR (RSI Divergence entry selected AND bearish RSI divergence) OR (MACD / SMA Cross entry selected AND MACD crosses below its signal line) OR (Magical DCA Shield entry selected AND source < macro EMA AND source crosses below ZLEMA AND RSI > oversold threshold); AND trading direction permits short; AND (trend filter disabled OR source < trend EMA); AND (volume filter disabled OR volume ≥ volume EMA × minimum-volume multiplier)
Exit: Long, (exit mode is Zero Lag AND source crosses below ZLEMA) OR (exit mode is Bollinger + ATR AND trailing stop enabled AND source crosses below long trailing stop) OR (exit mode is RSI Divergence AND RSI crosses above 70) OR (exit mode is MACD / SMA Cross AND MACD crosses below its signal line) OR (exit mode is Magical DCA Shield AND source crosses below the lowest low over the structural lookback OR source crosses below macro EMA) OR (stop-loss enabled AND low ≤ stop level, where stop level is signal-bar close − ATR × stop multiplier for ATR mode or signal-bar close × (1 − stop percentage) for percentage mode) OR (take-profit enabled AND high ≥ target level, where target level is signal-bar close + ATR × target multiplier for ATR mode or signal-bar close × (1 + target percentage) for percentage mode)
Exit: Short, (exit mode is Zero Lag AND source crosses above ZLEMA) OR (exit mode is Bollinger + ATR AND trailing stop enabled AND source crosses above short trailing stop) OR (exit mode is RSI Divergence AND RSI crosses below 30) OR (exit mode is MACD / SMA Cross AND MACD crosses above its signal line) OR (exit mode is Magical DCA Shield AND source crosses above the highest high over the structural lookback OR source crosses above macro EMA) OR (stop-loss enabled AND high ≥ stop level, where stop level is signal-bar close + ATR × stop multiplier for ATR mode or signal-bar close × (1 + stop percentage) for percentage mode) OR (take-profit enabled AND low ≤ target level, where target level is signal-bar close − ATR × target multiplier for ATR mode or signal-bar close × (1 − target percentage) for percentage mode)
