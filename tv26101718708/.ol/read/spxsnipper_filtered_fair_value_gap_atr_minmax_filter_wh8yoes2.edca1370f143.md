Calculation: Evaluation timeframe = chart timeframe, or the selected higher timeframe when the higher-timeframe lock is enabled; locked-timeframe decisions use the current bar while it is forming
Calculation: Bullish gap size = current bar low minus the high two bars earlier
Calculation: Bearish gap size = the low two bars earlier minus current bar high
Calculation: ATR gap bounds = ATR on the evaluation timeframe over the configured lookback multiplied by the lower and higher of the configured minimum and maximum gap percentages, respectively, divided by 100
Long: bar two bars earlier is the first candle, the prior bar is the second candle, and the current bar is the third candle; second candle close > second candle open AND current close > current open AND second candle high > second candle low AND second candle close >= second candle high - (second candle high - second candle low) * configured second-candle edge percentage / 100 AND current high > current low AND current close >= current high - (current high - current low) * configured third-candle edge percentage / 100 AND current close > (second candle high + second candle low) / 2 AND current low > high two bars earlier AND bullish gap size >= minimum ATR gap bound AND bullish gap size <= maximum ATR gap bound
Short: bar two bars earlier is the first candle, the prior bar is the second candle, and the current bar is the third candle; second candle close < second candle open AND current close < current open AND second candle high > second candle low AND second candle close <= second candle low + (second candle high - second candle low) * configured second-candle edge percentage / 100 AND current high > current low AND current close <= current low + (current high - current low) * configured third-candle edge percentage / 100 AND current close < (second candle high + second candle low) / 2 AND current high < low two bars earlier AND bearish gap size >= minimum ATR gap bound AND bearish gap size <= maximum ATR gap bound
