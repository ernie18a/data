Calculation: Bullish engulfing = previous bar closes below its open, current bar closes above its open, current close >= previous open, current open <= previous close, current body > previous body, current high > previous high, current low < previous low, and current close > previous high
Calculation: Bearish engulfing = previous bar closes above its open, current bar closes below its open, current open >= previous close, current close <= previous open, current body > previous body, current high > previous high, current low < previous low, and current close < previous low
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Bollinger Bands = close-based moving average plus/minus a multiple of close standard deviation; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA crossover = the shorter-lookback EMA crosses above the longer-lookback EMA; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA((hlc3 - EMA(hlc3, short lookback)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(hlc3 - EMA(hlc3, short lookback)), same lookback)), longer-lookback)
Calculation: WaveTrend alignment = WaveTrend is above its overbought threshold on the chart and higher timeframes, or below its oversold threshold on the chart and higher timeframes; chart-timeframe value uses the requested bar with lookahead off, higher-timeframe values use the still-forming bar
Calculation: Active bullish gap = the lowest low in a third 15-bar block is above the highest high in the first of three consecutive 15-bar blocks; active bearish gap = the highest high in the third block is below the lowest low in the first block; blocks advance in 15-bar offsets, and a gap becomes inactive when a later 15-bar close crosses its far boundary
Calculation: In-gap bar = the bar's open, high, low, and close are all within an active gap
Calculation: Entry risk = absolute distance from entry price to the initial stop price
Long: Bullish engulfing OR shorter-lookback EMA crosses above longer-lookback EMA OR (bullish engulfing AND a last-completed higher-timeframe close was below its lower Bollinger Band within the prior 6 chart bars AND current open is below the chart-timeframe lower Bollinger Band) OR (bullish engulfing within 15 chart bars after a last-completed higher-timeframe bullish EMA crossover AND Supertrend is bullish) OR (chart-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA crosses above longer-lookback EMA within 18 chart bars after a bullish higher-timeframe engulfing) OR (bullish engulfing within 9 chart bars of a cross into oversold while WaveTrend is aligned oversold) OR (bullish engulfing AND in-gap bar in an active bullish gap AND Supertrend is bullish); for strategy entries, also require Monday through Friday after 00:00 or Saturday at or before 05:55, Shanghai time
Short: Bearish engulfing OR shorter-lookback EMA crosses below longer-lookback EMA OR (bearish engulfing AND a last-completed higher-timeframe close was above its upper Bollinger Band within the prior 6 chart bars AND current open is above the chart-timeframe upper Bollinger Band) OR (bearish engulfing within 15 chart bars after a last-completed higher-timeframe bullish EMA crossover AND Supertrend is bearish) OR (chart-timeframe shorter-lookback EMA crosses below longer-lookback EMA within 18 chart bars after a bearish higher-timeframe engulfing) OR (bearish engulfing within 9 chart bars of a cross into overbought while WaveTrend is aligned overbought) OR (bearish engulfing AND in-gap bar in an active bearish gap AND Supertrend is bearish); for strategy entries, also require Monday through Friday after 00:00 or Saturday at or before 05:55, Shanghai time
Exit: Long, shorter-lookback EMA crosses below longer-lookback EMA before the first target, price reaches the initial stop, high reaches entry + 1 × risk (close half), high reaches entry + 2 × risk (close remainder), remaining position reaches entry after the first target, or the allowed trading window ends
Exit: Short, shorter-lookback EMA crosses above longer-lookback EMA before the first target, price reaches the initial stop, low reaches entry - 1 × risk (close half), low reaches entry - 2 × risk (close remainder), remaining position reaches entry after the first target, or the allowed trading window ends
