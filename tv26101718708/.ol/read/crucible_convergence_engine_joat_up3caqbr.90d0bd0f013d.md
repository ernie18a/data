Calculation: Directional bias = (EMA of positive directional movement − EMA of negative directional movement) / their sum, using a zero value when the sum is zero; positive and negative movement are the respective up and down changes in highs and lows when that change exceeds the other.
Calculation: Normalized EMA spread = (shorter-lookback EMA of close − longer-lookback EMA of close) / ATR.
Calculation: Confirmed regime = raw regime after the same raw state occurs on two consecutive bars; raw regime is bullish when ATR percentile rank is above the kinetic threshold and not above the turbulence threshold, directional bias is above its positive threshold, and normalized EMA spread is above its positive threshold; bearish uses the corresponding negative thresholds; volatile when ATR percentile rank is above the turbulence threshold; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Volume-weighted momentum = EMA of (EMA of (close change × log(1 + short-to-long average volume ratio × sensitivity), shorter lookback) − EMA of the same input, longer lookback), then smoothed with a shorter EMA; momentum signal = EMA of the momentum index with a longer lookback.
Calculation: Upper and lower statistical bands = SMA of close ± standard deviation of close × multiplier, using the same lookback.
Calculation: Short-to-long average volume ratio = shorter-lookback SMA of volume / longer-lookback SMA of volume.
Calculation: Grid rails = the ceiling and floor multiples of a grid increment around EMA of close; proximity tolerance = distance between rails × proximity fraction.
Calculation: Trailing stop = for a long, the highest value so far of close − ATR × trail multiple; for a short, the lowest value so far of close + ATR × trail multiple.
Long: confirmed regime is bullish AND shorter-lookback trend moving average of close > longer-lookback trend moving average of close AND volume-weighted momentum index > momentum signal AND close < upper statistical band AND short-to-long average volume ratio ≥ volume threshold AND these conditions were false on the previous bar.
Short: confirmed regime is bearish AND shorter-lookback trend moving average of close < longer-lookback trend moving average of close AND volume-weighted momentum index < momentum signal AND close > lower statistical band AND short-to-long average volume ratio ≥ volume threshold AND these conditions were false on the previous bar.
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiple OR high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × target multiple OR low ≤ trailing stop OR confirmed regime is bearish OR close ≥ upper statistical band OR high is within proximity tolerance of the upper grid rail.
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiple OR low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × target multiple OR high ≥ trailing stop OR confirmed regime is bullish OR close ≤ lower statistical band OR low is within proximity tolerance of the lower grid rail.
