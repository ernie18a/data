Calculation: Money flow = moving average of (close − low − (high − close)) over its lookback
Calculation: Strong candle = close > open AND close − low > high − close
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = moving average of RSI’s normalized position between its lowest and highest values over the lookback
Long: Close crosses above WMA AND ADX > its threshold AND close > EMA AND RSI > its threshold AND Money flow > 0 AND Strong candle AND Stochastic RSI < its upper threshold
Short: Close crosses below WMA AND negative directional indicator > positive directional indicator
Exit: Long, RSI crosses above either profit-taking threshold OR close crosses below WMA reduced by the stop offset while close > EMA
