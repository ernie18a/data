Calculation: Buyer and seller volume = either lower-timeframe intrabar up/down volume aggregated within each chart bar, or, in geometry mode, volume × (close − low)/(high − low) and volume × (high − close)/(high − low), respectively; when high = low, each is volume/2.
Calculation: Highest Buyers (HB), Highest Sellers (HS), Lowest Buyers (LB), Lowest Sellers (LS) = bars with the greatest buyer volume, greatest seller volume, least positive buyer volume, and least positive seller volume, respectively, within the lookback window; ties select the most recent bar. HB price is its high; HS price is its low.
Calculation: HB remaining power (%) = 100 × (HB buyer volume − seller volume accumulated from bars after the HB bar through the current bar) / HB buyer volume.
Calculation: Cumulative delta = buyer volume − seller volume accumulated from bars after the HB bar through the current bar.
Calculation: Trend = uptrend if both upper and lower slopes of linear regressions through the five segment highs and lows over the same lookback exceed ATR × 0.001; downtrend if both are below −ATR × 0.001; otherwise ranging. The high and low regressions use the same lookback.
Calculation: HB zone = HB high’s position between the upper and lower regression lines projected to the HB bar: top at ≥100%, upper at ≥75%, middle at ≥25%, lower at >0%, otherwise bottom.
Calculation: Nearest event and post-event momentum = the most recent of HB, HS, LB, LS, or their 100%/200% depletion events; depletion occurs when opposing volume accumulated after the event reaches 100%/200% of its event volume. Classify HB/LB/HS-depletion as bullish events and HS/LS/HB-depletion as bearish events. Count up to five bars after the nearest event as bullish when close > prior close and bearish when close < prior close; momentum is bullish or bearish only when its count exceeds the other.
Long: (nearest event is at most 2 bars old AND ((it is bearish AND post-event momentum is bullish AND bullish count ≥ 3) OR (it is bullish AND post-event momentum is bullish))) OR (nearest event is not at most 2 bars old AND ((trend is uptrend AND cumulative delta > 0) OR (trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta > 0 AND HB remaining power ≥ long threshold AND post-event momentum is bullish) OR (trend is ranging AND cumulative delta > 0 AND post-event momentum is bullish))); AND (close > HB high OR close > HS low).
Short: (nearest event is at most 2 bars old AND ((it is bearish AND post-event momentum is bearish) OR (it is bullish AND post-event momentum is bearish AND bearish count ≥ 3))) OR (nearest event is not at most 2 bars old AND ((trend is uptrend AND HB remaining power < opponent-dominance threshold AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish) OR (trend is downtrend AND cumulative delta < 0) OR (trend is ranging AND cumulative delta < 0 AND post-event momentum is bearish))); AND NOT (close > HB high AND close > HS low).
Exit: long, current signal is short OR low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target.
Exit: short, current signal is long OR high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target.
Calculation: Long stop = HS low − buffer; long target = HB high − buffer; short stop = HB high + buffer; short target = HS low + buffer. Buffers are percentages of close; place/update these orders only when both stop and target are respectively below/above close for long, or above/below close for short.
Long: current signal is long AND both HB and HS prices exist AND stop < close AND target > close AND (flat or already long with room to add under the pyramid limit); evaluate on confirmed bars, at most one entry per bar.
Short: current signal is short AND both HB and HS prices exist AND stop > close AND target < close AND (flat or already short with room to add under the pyramid limit); evaluate on confirmed bars, at most one entry per bar.
