Calculation: Bullish score = count of the nine comparisons where chart close is above the short-, medium-, or long-lookback EMA on each of three configured timeframes; use the last completed bar on any timeframe higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bearish score = nine minus the bullish score.
Calculation: VWAP = session VWAP calculated from (high + low + close) / 3 on its configured timeframe; use the last completed bar if that timeframe is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Previous daily close = close of the last completed daily bar.
Long: (Trend Change mode: bullish score >= configured bullish threshold AND prior bar bullish score < that threshold) OR (Strong Trend Only mode: all but at most one of the nine EMA comparisons are bullish AND prior bar did not meet that condition) OR (Breakout Confirmed mode: bullish score >= configured bullish threshold AND close >= previous daily close plus the configured percentage threshold AND prior bar did not meet both conditions); AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR close > VWAP); AND (close is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled); AND (strict alternation disabled OR last signal was not long).
Short: (Trend Change mode: bearish score >= configured bearish threshold AND prior bar bearish score < that threshold) OR (Strong Trend Only mode: all but at most one of the nine EMA comparisons are bearish AND prior bar did not meet that condition) OR (Breakout Confirmed mode: bearish score >= configured bearish threshold AND close <= previous daily close minus the configured percentage threshold AND prior bar did not meet both conditions); AND (VWAP confirmation disabled OR close < VWAP); AND (close is confirmed if close confirmation is enabled); AND (strict alternation disabled OR last signal was not short).
