Calculation: Previous completed higher-timeframe bar high/low = the high/low of the last completed bar on a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low > high two chart bars earlier; gap zone is from that earlier high to current low.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current high < low two chart bars earlier; gap zone is from current high to that earlier low.
Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is respectively greater/less than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on each side; confirmed after the following bars complete.
Long: Chart timeframe is intraday AND current low < previous completed higher-timeframe bar low AND current close > previous completed higher-timeframe bar low OR a bullish fair value gap forms OR an active, unexpired bearish fair value gap closes above its upper boundary OR at a confirmed chart pivot low, the latest chart pivot low < the prior chart pivot low AND the latest two correlated-symbol pivot lows on the chart timeframe satisfy latest > prior AND the latest correlated pivot is no more than the specified maximum bars old.
Short: Chart timeframe is intraday AND current high > previous completed higher-timeframe bar high AND current close < previous completed higher-timeframe bar high OR a bearish fair value gap forms OR an active, unexpired bullish fair value gap closes below its lower boundary OR at a confirmed chart pivot high, the latest chart pivot high > the prior chart pivot high AND the latest two correlated-symbol pivot highs on the chart timeframe satisfy latest < prior AND the latest correlated pivot is no more than the specified maximum bars old.
