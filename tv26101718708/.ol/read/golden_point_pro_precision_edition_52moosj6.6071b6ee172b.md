Calculation: Golden Buy Score = round(100 × bullish points / available points), where bullish points are 10 for close > prior close and close > open, plus 20 for fast EMA > slow EMA and close > fast EMA when EMA filter is enabled, 20 for close > VWAP when VWAP filter is enabled, 15 for RSI ≥ buy threshold when RSI filter is enabled, 15 for ADX ≥ minimum and +DI > −DI when ADX filter is enabled, 10 for volume ≥ volume moving average × volume multiplier when volume filter is enabled, and 10 for ATR ≥ its moving average × ATR multiplier when ATR filter is enabled; available points are 10 plus the weights of enabled filters
Calculation: Golden Sell Score = round(100 × bearish points / available points), where bearish points are 10 for close < prior close and close < open, plus 20 for fast EMA < slow EMA and close < fast EMA when EMA filter is enabled, 20 for close < VWAP when VWAP filter is enabled, 15 for RSI ≤ sell threshold when RSI filter is enabled, 15 for ADX ≥ minimum and −DI > +DI when ADX filter is enabled, 10 for volume ≥ volume moving average × volume multiplier when volume filter is enabled, and 10 for ATR ≥ its moving average × ATR multiplier when ATR filter is enabled; available points are 10 plus the weights of enabled filters
