Calculation: Simple moving average = arithmetic mean of the chart-timeframe closes over a lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the highest high/lowest low on the chart timeframe between the prior and current completed higher-timeframe structure bars, recorded when a completed higher-timeframe close breaks the active structure high/low in the opposite direction to the prior structure direction. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Long: The previous chart-timeframe close > the latest confirmed swing high AND the prior structure direction ≤ 0.
Short: The previous chart-timeframe close < the latest confirmed swing low AND the prior structure direction ≥ 0.
Long: The configured third event occurs after the configured first and second events in sequence, and the resulting direction is bullish.
Short: The configured third event occurs after the configured first and second events in sequence, and the resulting direction is bearish.
