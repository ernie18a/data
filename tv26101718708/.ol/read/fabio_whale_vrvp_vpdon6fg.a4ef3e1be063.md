Calculation: Trend efficiency = absolute change in close over the lookback divided by the highest high minus lowest low over that lookback
Calculation: Volume profile levels = over the lookback, divide the highest-high-to-lowest-low range into rows; allocate each bar's volume to each row in proportion to the price-range overlap; POC is the row with the most volume; expand from POC by adding the higher-volume adjacent row until the configured share of total volume is reached; VAH and VAL are the outer row prices
Calculation: Near a volume profile level = absolute distance from close to that level divided by close is less than the configured tolerance
Calculation: Aggressive buy volume = buy volume, estimated as volume times (close − low) / (high − low), exceeds its prior-lookback average plus the configured minimum-tier multiple of its prior-lookback standard deviation
Calculation: Aggressive sell volume = sell volume, estimated as volume times (high − close) / (high − low), exceeds its prior-lookback average plus the configured minimum-tier multiple of its prior-lookback standard deviation
Long: (trend efficiency exceeds its configured threshold AND close exceeds its lookback-ago close AND close is near POC, VAH, or VAL AND aggressive buy volume) OR (trend efficiency does not exceed its configured threshold AND close is near VAL or POC AND aggressive buy volume) OR (close is below VAL AND close is near VAL AND aggressive buy volume); AND within the configured session; AND cooldown elapsed; AND flat position
Short: (trend efficiency exceeds its configured threshold AND close is below its lookback-ago close AND close is near POC, VAH, or VAL AND aggressive sell volume) OR (trend efficiency does not exceed its configured threshold AND close is near VAH or POC AND aggressive sell volume) OR (close is above VAH AND close is near VAH AND aggressive sell volume); AND within the configured session; AND cooldown elapsed; AND flat position
Exit: long, low is at or below the stop (signal-bar low minus configured ATR multiple) OR high is at or above the target (entry price plus configured risk/reward multiple of entry-to-stop risk) OR bars since entry reach the configured maximum OR outside the configured session
Exit: short, high is at or above the stop (signal-bar high plus configured ATR multiple) OR low is at or below the target (entry price minus configured risk/reward multiple of stop-to-entry risk) OR bars since entry reach the configured maximum OR outside the configured session
