Calculation: Valid support rejection = On the detection timeframe (equal to or higher than the chart timeframe), a confirmed pivot low whose pivot-bar close is at least the rejection-wick threshold above the pivot low; the close before the pivot is above the pivot low plus zone thickness; and the highest high among the first N completed bars after the pivot is at least the required displacement above the pivot low. N is the smaller of the displacement window and pivot-right lookback. Required displacement is either a tick distance or ATR at the pivot multiplied by a factor. Evaluate only after the pivot-right bars complete.
Calculation: Valid resistance rejection = On the detection timeframe (equal to or higher than the chart timeframe), a confirmed pivot high whose pivot-bar close is at least the rejection-wick threshold below the pivot high; the close before the pivot is below the pivot high minus zone thickness; and the lowest low among the first N completed bars after the pivot is at least the required displacement below the pivot high. N is the smaller of the displacement window and pivot-right lookback. Required displacement is either a tick distance or ATR at the pivot multiplied by a factor. Evaluate only after the pivot-right bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed support zone = A valid support rejection starts a candidate zone extending upward from its pivot low by the zone thickness. A later valid support rejection counts toward the same zone when its proposed zone overlaps the candidate within the merge tolerance and occurs at least the minimum spacing after the candidate's last counted rejection. The zone is confirmed when its count reaches the minimum rejection threshold.
Calculation: Confirmed resistance zone = A valid resistance rejection starts a candidate zone extending downward from its pivot high by the zone thickness. A later valid resistance rejection counts toward the same zone when its proposed zone overlaps the candidate within the merge tolerance and occurs at least the minimum spacing after the candidate's last counted rejection. The zone is confirmed when its count reaches the minimum rejection threshold.
Long: Valid support rejection occurs and its support zone's rejection count reaches the minimum rejection threshold.
Short: Valid resistance rejection occurs and its resistance zone's rejection count reaches the minimum rejection threshold.
