Calculation: Upper wick = high − max(open, close) on the last completed signal-timeframe bar; the signal timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Lower wick = min(open, close) − low on the last completed signal-timeframe bar; the signal timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Lower wick > its simple moving average × multiplier AND low < previous low
Short: Upper wick > its simple moving average × multiplier AND high > previous high
