Calculation: Chart EMAs = exponential moving averages of close, ordered from shorter to longer lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMAs = exponential moving averages of close on higher-timeframe bars, ordered from shorter to longer lookback; use the bar still forming
Calculation: Bullish golden candle = chart EMAs are ordered short-to-long from highest to lowest; body size meets its configured minimum; and either the candle body or wick touches or approaches one of the two shortest EMAs within its configured tolerance, with close above that EMA; when enabled, reject if low extends beyond the configured wick-overdrive threshold below the second-shortest EMA
Calculation: Bearish golden candle = chart EMAs are ordered short-to-long from lowest to highest; body size meets its configured minimum; and either the candle body or wick touches or approaches one of the two shortest EMAs within its configured tolerance, with close below that EMA; when enabled, reject if high extends beyond the configured wick-overdrive threshold above the second-shortest EMA
Calculation: Bullish stop = at signal, the second-longest chart EMA if the longest EMA is above it, otherwise the longest chart EMA
Calculation: Bearish stop = at signal, the second-longest chart EMA if the longest EMA is below it, otherwise the longest chart EMA
Calculation: Target = signal close plus (long) or minus (short) configured reward-to-risk multiple times the distance from signal close to the stop
Long: A bullish golden candle on the setup bar preceding the confirmed pivot-low bar AND the confirmed pivot low occurs in a bullish chart-EMA ordering AND the prior candle's body high is at least the body high of the candle before it AND close is outside the configured proximity band around the higher-timeframe EMA AND NOT (higher-timeframe bearish EMA ordering AND close above the higher-timeframe second-shortest EMA) AND within the configured active session when the session filter is enabled AND bullish signals in the current bullish EMA bubble are below the configured limit AND position is flat or short AND bullish stop is below signal close by more than one minimum tick
Short: A bearish golden candle on the setup bar preceding the confirmed pivot-high bar AND the confirmed pivot high occurs in a bearish chart-EMA ordering AND the prior candle's body low is at most the body low of the candle before it AND close is outside the configured proximity band around the higher-timeframe EMA AND NOT (higher-timeframe bullish EMA ordering AND close below the higher-timeframe second-shortest EMA) AND within the configured active session when the session filter is enabled AND bearish signals in the current bearish EMA bubble are below the configured limit AND position is flat or long AND bearish stop is above signal close by more than one minimum tick
Exit: Long, low <= long stop OR high >= long target OR a valid short entry signal occurs
Exit: Short, high >= short stop OR low <= short target OR a valid long entry signal occurs
