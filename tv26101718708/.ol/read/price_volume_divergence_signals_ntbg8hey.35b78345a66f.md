Calculation: Smoothed volume = raw volume when the volume smoothing lookback is 1; otherwise the simple moving average of volume over that lookback.
Calculation: Bullish phase = close is at least its value at the price direction lookback ago and smoothed volume is at least its value at the volume direction lookback ago, OR close is below its value at the price direction lookback ago and smoothed volume is below its value at the volume direction lookback ago. Before both references are available, classify using close >= open and volume >= previous-bar volume (treat missing previous volume as rising); the first combination is bullish rally and the second bullish pullback.
Calculation: Bearish phase = close is below its value at the price direction lookback ago and smoothed volume is at least its value at the volume direction lookback ago, OR close is at least its value at the price direction lookback ago and smoothed volume is below its value at the volume direction lookback ago. Before both references are available, use the complementary fallback combinations; the first is bearish pullback and the second bearish rally.
Calculation: Confirmed price pivots = a high or low that is the highest high or lowest low over the pivot left lookback and pivot right confirmation lookback, respectively; detect it only after the right lookback bars have closed.
Calculation: Pivot volume = either volume on the pivot bar or the simple moving average of volume over the volume average lookback ending on the pivot bar, according to the selected comparison mode.
Long: Bullish phase OR a confirmed price pivot low is below the previous confirmed price pivot low by at least the minimum price difference percentage, its pivot volume is below the previous pivot low's volume by at least the minimum volume difference percentage, and the distance between those pivots is positive and no greater than the maximum pivot distance.
Short: Bearish phase OR a confirmed price pivot high is above the previous confirmed price pivot high by at least the minimum price difference percentage, its pivot volume is below the previous pivot high's volume by at least the minimum volume difference percentage, and the distance between those pivots is positive and no greater than the maximum pivot distance.
