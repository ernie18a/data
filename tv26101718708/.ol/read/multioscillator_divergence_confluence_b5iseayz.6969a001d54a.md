Calculation: Bullish/bearish lean count = count of enabled RSI, MACD line, smoothed Stochastic, CCI, MFI, and smoothed OBV that rise/fall over the same lookback while close falls/rises over that lookback.
Calculation: Pressure wave = EMA of 50 + (bullish lean count − bearish lean count) × 50/6.
Calculation: Regular bullish divergence votes = count of enabled oscillators where, at the same confirmed price-low pivot pair separated by the configured minimum and maximum bars, the newer low is lower and the oscillator value is higher.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence votes = count of enabled oscillators where, at the same confirmed price-low pivot pair in that spacing range, the newer low is higher and the oscillator value is lower.
Calculation: Regular bearish divergence votes = count of enabled oscillators where, at the same confirmed price-high pivot pair in that spacing range, the newer high is higher and the oscillator value is lower.
Calculation: Hidden bearish divergence votes = count of enabled oscillators where, at the same confirmed price-high pivot pair in that spacing range, the newer high is lower and the oscillator value is higher.
Calculation: Signal score = clamp(10 × divergence votes + 10 if the pivot has the required EMA context + 10 if an enabled RSI, Stochastic, CCI, or MFI is in the corresponding exhaustion zone + 6 if the volume bonus is enabled and pivot volume exceeds its SMA + round(6 × pivot-spacing quality) + 8 if the regime aligns with the divergence type − 10 if fewer than four oscillators are enabled, to 0–100). Pivot-spacing quality is max(0, 1 − distance from the midpoint of the configured spacing range divided by half that range); regular divergences align with Ranging or Quiet regimes, hidden divergences with Trending.
Calculation: Regime = Trending if ADX ≥ its threshold; otherwise Volatile if ATR/close percentile rank over its lookback ≥ its upper threshold, Quiet if that rank ≤ its lower threshold, and Ranging otherwise.
Calculation: Setup proximity = current low is within configured ATR distance of the lowest low over the setup lookback, or current high is within that distance of the highest high.
Long: (Pressure wave crosses above its configured threshold AND bullish lean count ≥ 2 AND Setup proximity is near the swing low) OR (confirmed price-low pivot AND regular bullish divergence votes ≥ configured minimum confluence AND regular bullish score ≥ configured minimum score) OR (confirmed price-low pivot AND hidden bullish divergence votes ≥ configured minimum confluence AND hidden bullish score ≥ configured minimum score AND pivot close > EMA).
Short: (Pressure wave crosses below 100 minus its configured threshold AND bearish lean count ≥ 2 AND Setup proximity is near the swing high) OR (confirmed price-high pivot AND regular bearish divergence votes ≥ configured minimum confluence AND regular bearish score ≥ configured minimum score) OR (confirmed price-high pivot AND hidden bearish divergence votes ≥ configured minimum confluence AND hidden bearish score ≥ configured minimum score AND pivot close < EMA).
