Calculation: TSI = 100 × EMA(EMA(change in close, longer lookback), shorter lookback) / EMA(EMA(absolute change in close, longer lookback), shorter lookback); the shorter lookback is less than the longer lookback
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume
Long: TSI > 0 AND ADX > minimum threshold AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > trend EMA AND higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe trend EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar AND volume > volume average AND the same full condition was false on the previous chart bar; the fast EMA lookback is shorter than the slow EMA lookback, and the trend EMA lookback is longer than both
