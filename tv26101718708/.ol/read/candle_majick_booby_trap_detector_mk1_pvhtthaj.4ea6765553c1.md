Calculation: Bullish reversal pattern = any of: lower wick ≥ body × primary wick/body ratio AND upper wick ≤ body × opposite-wick/body limit AND close location within bar ≥ 0.55; bullish engulfing (current bar up, prior bar down, current open ≤ prior close, current close ≥ prior open); bullish pin bar (lower wick ≥ 55% of range, close location ≥ 0.60, body ≤ 35% of range); bullish outside reversal (current high > prior high, current low < prior low, up close above bar midpoint); or morning star (bar two bars ago down, prior body ≤ 35% of prior range, current bar up and closes at or above the midpoint of the bar two bars ago).
Calculation: Bearish reversal pattern = any of the mirrored bearish forms: upper-wick hammer/star geometry with close location ≤ 0.45; bearish engulfing (current bar down, prior bar up, current open ≥ prior close, current close ≤ prior open); bearish pin bar (upper wick ≥ 55% of range, close location ≤ 0.40, body ≤ 35% of range); bearish outside reversal (current high > prior high, current low < prior low, down close below bar midpoint); or evening star (bar two bars ago up, prior body ≤ 35% of prior range, current bar down and closes at or below the midpoint of the bar two bars ago).
Calculation: Pattern quality = clamp(45 × dominant wick/range + 35 × directional close quality + 20 × (1 − body/range), 0, 100); directional close quality is close location for bullish patterns and 1 − close location for bearish patterns.
Calculation: Adjusted setup quality = clamp(pattern quality × (1 − away-from-key-level penalty percentage) + key-level confluence bonus × max(number of enabled relevant key levels within the ATR-based proximity distance − 1, 0), 0, 100); apply the penalty only when no relevant enabled key level is within that distance. Relevant bullish levels are previous-day low, current-session low, confirmed swing low, session VWAP, nearest round number, and enabled manual levels; bearish levels use the corresponding highs plus VWAP, nearest round number, and enabled manual levels.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe direction = bullish when the shorter-lookback EMA > longer-lookback EMA and close > shorter-lookback EMA; bearish when both inequalities are reversed; otherwise neutral. Use three higher timeframes and the last completed bar historically (the requested bar may still be forming in real time).
Calculation: Trap risk score = clamp(sum of configured weights for: close's position within the rolling high-low range being in the middle zone; nearest confirmed swing low below a bullish candidate or swing high above a bearish candidate within the ATR distance; round-number surface bounce; news-like event; enough higher-timeframe directions opposing the pattern; repeated tests of the candidate low/high; and no enabled relevant key level within the ATR proximity distance, plus extra weight for each additional opposing higher timeframe), 0, 100. A news-like event requires relative volume, volume z-score, and true-range z-score all to meet their respective thresholds. Repeated tests count prior bars whose range touches the candidate low/high within ATR tolerance, counting only touches separated by at least the configured minimum bars.
Long: bullish reversal pattern AND adjusted setup quality ≥ minimum pattern quality AND no active candidate AND chart bar confirmed.
Short: bearish reversal pattern AND adjusted setup quality ≥ minimum pattern quality AND no active candidate AND chart bar confirmed.
Exit: Long, an active bullish candidate is within its evaluation window AND close < candidate low − ATR × failure buffer AND its trap risk score at creation ≥ confirmed-trap threshold.
Exit: Short, an active bearish candidate is within its evaluation window AND close > candidate high + ATR × failure buffer AND its trap risk score at creation ≥ confirmed-trap threshold.
