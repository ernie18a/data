Calculation: Contested volume = sum, across lower-timeframe bars within the current chart bar, of min(estimated buy volume, estimated sell volume), where estimated buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low) and estimated sell volume = volume × (high − close) / (high − low); ignore zero-range bars, and use the chart bar's OHLCV split if lower-timeframe data is unavailable.
Calculation: Time-of-day surprise = log(contested volume + 1) minus the mean of the log contested volume + 1 for the same and adjacent clock slots, estimated from prior completed chart bars; subtract the session adjustment, calculated from the clipped running mean of the current session's residuals, scaled by the session normalization setting and the fraction of the session observed.
Long: net delta ≥ 0 AND (lower-timeframe data available OR lower-timeframe data not required) AND ((time-of-day normalization is active AND contested volume > 0 AND the clock slot has sufficient prior-session history AND the time-of-day baseline is available for the trigger window AND (activity floor disabled OR contested volume ≥ activity floor × time-of-day baseline) AND percentile rank of time-of-day surprise over the trigger lookback ≥ trigger percentile) OR (time-of-day normalization unavailable or inactive AND EMA of contested volume > 0 AND highest EMA over the prior trigger lookback > lowest EMA over the prior trigger lookback AND (activity floor disabled OR EMA of contested volume ≥ activity floor × SMA of contested volume over the size lookback) AND percentile rank of EMA of contested volume over the trigger lookback ≥ trigger percentile))
