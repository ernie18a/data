Calculation: High volume = volume > volume moving average × volume multiplier
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price tick)
Calculation: Delta proxy = volume × (2 × close location − 1)
Calculation: Delta strength = delta proxy / exponential moving average of absolute delta proxy
Calculation: Bullish order-flow impulse = delta strength ≥ threshold AND cumulative delta proxy ≥ its exponential moving average AND close location ≥ 0.60
Calculation: Bearish order-flow impulse = delta strength ≤ −threshold AND cumulative delta proxy ≤ its exponential moving average AND close location ≤ 0.40
Calculation: Bullish absorption = high volume AND lower wick > candle body AND close location ≥ 0.55
Calculation: Bearish absorption = high volume AND upper wick > candle body AND close location ≤ 0.45
Calculation: Failed-break long trap = low < the prior lookback lowest low AND close > that low AND delta strength < 0 AND close location ≥ 0.55
Calculation: Failed-break short trap = high > the prior lookback highest high AND close < that high AND delta strength > 0 AND close location ≤ 0.45
Calculation: Bullish impulse tag = candle body ≥ 1.4 × its exponential moving average AND bullish order-flow impulse AND close > prior high
Calculation: Bearish impulse tag = candle body ≥ 1.4 × its exponential moving average AND bearish order-flow impulse AND close < prior low
Calculation: Bullish trend = close > slow EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA
Calculation: Bearish trend = close < slow EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA
Calculation: VWAP reclaim = close > VWAP AND prior close ≤ prior VWAP
Calculation: VWAP rejection = close < VWAP AND prior close ≥ prior VWAP
Calculation: Near EMA = distance from close to fast EMA or slow EMA ≤ ATR × proximity threshold
Calculation: Structure break up = close > highest high over the lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Structure break down = close < lowest low over the lookback, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bullish rejection candle = lower wick ≥ candle body × wick-ratio threshold AND close location ≥ 0.60 AND close > open
Calculation: Bearish rejection candle = upper wick ≥ candle body × wick-ratio threshold AND close location ≤ 0.40 AND close < open
Calculation: Long thesis invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP-flip invalidation enabled AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA) OR (opposite-order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR bearish absorption OR failed-break short trap))) AND NOT (bullish trend AND close > VWAP AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR trigger break up OR structure break up)
Calculation: Short thesis invalidation = invalidation enabled AND ((VWAP-flip invalidation enabled AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA) OR (opposite-order-flow invalidation enabled AND (bullish order-flow impulse OR bullish absorption OR failed-break long trap))) AND NOT (bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR trigger break down OR structure break down))
Calculation: Entry confidence = clamp to 0–100 of the maximum of trend, pullback, and reversal long/short scores, less conflict penalties; each trend score adds 25 for aligned trend, 20 for aligned VWAP side, 15 for high volume, 15 for structure break, 10 for near EMA, 10 for allowed session, and order-flow score; each pullback score adds 25 for aligned trend, 20 for aligned VWAP side, 20 for near EMA, 20 for pullback structure break, 10 for pullback volume pass, and order-flow score; each reversal score adds 20 for its starting VWAP side, 25 for VWAP reclaim/rejection, 20 for fast/slow EMA crossover/crossunder, 20 for reversal structure break, 10 for reversal volume pass, and order-flow score. Order-flow score is 15 for aligned impulse, else 12 for aligned absorption, else −8 for aligned exhaustion, plus configured aligned trap and impulse bonuses. Subtract 15 for trend conflict, 10 for VWAP-side conflict, 15 for opposite order-flow conflict, 5 when no modifier is present, and 8 when trigger confirmation is required but absent.

Short: (continuation enabled AND bearish trend AND (VWAP filter disabled OR VWAP rejection) AND (volume filter disabled OR high volume) AND structure break down AND (order-flow filter disabled OR bearish order-flow pass) AND session pass AND (trigger requirement disabled OR short trigger confirmation) AND NOT short thesis invalidation) OR (pullback enabled AND ((bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND near EMA AND pullback structure break down AND (pullback volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR bearish order-flow pass) AND session pass AND (trigger requirement disabled OR short trigger confirmation) AND NOT short thesis invalidation) OR (lower-timeframe execution enabled AND bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND near EMA AND trigger break down AND NOT short thesis invalidation)) AND (NOT lower-timeframe execution base OR entry confidence ≥ entry-score threshold) OR (reversal enabled AND close > VWAP AND VWAP rejection AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND reversal structure break down AND (reversal volume requirement disabled OR high volume) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR bearish order-flow pass) AND session pass AND (trigger requirement disabled OR short trigger confirmation) AND NOT short thesis invalidation) OR (momentum enabled AND bearish trend AND close < VWAP AND session pass AND (bearish order-flow impulse OR structure break down OR trigger break down) AND NOT short thesis invalidation AND entry confidence ≥ entry-score threshold) OR (session pass AND (short watch OR pullback-short watch OR reversal-short watch) AND (bearish rejection candle OR trigger break down OR failed-break short trap OR bearish impulse tag)) OR bearish absorption
