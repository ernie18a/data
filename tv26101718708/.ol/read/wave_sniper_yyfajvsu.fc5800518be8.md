Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = local highs/lows confirmed after a configurable right-side lookback; keep pivots in alternating order and replace a pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same type.
Calculation: A/B/C wave ratios = B/A = |B−A| ÷ |A−origin| × 100; C/A = |C−B| ÷ |A−origin| × 100.
Calculation: Flat pattern = four consecutive pivots origin→A→B→C, with A and C pivot types matching, B opposite, and A opposite the origin pivot. Classify in order: regular if B/A is within the configured regular range and C/A is within the configured regular range; expanded if B/A and C/A are within their configured expanded ranges; running if B/A > 100 and C/A < 100 minus the effective tolerance. Effective tolerance is the configured tolerance, increased by its coded multiplier during a valid truncated fifth wave.
Calculation: Valid impulse = five alternating waves from six consecutive pivots; W2 retraces at least its configured minimum of W1 but less than all of W1 and does not cross the origin; W3 is at least the configured minimum length relative to W1; W4 does not overlap W1 beyond the configured tolerance. A truncated fifth wave fails to extend beyond the end of W3.
Long: Confirmed flat pattern is regular or expanded and C is a low pivot OR confirmed running flat and C is a high pivot OR valid 3-wave pullback with C a low pivot OR bullish 5-wave retest: after a flat pattern with A at a low, subsequent price moves above the fifth-wave pivot, then the bar overlaps the A/B/C price range and closes above its open OR valid truncated fifth wave is downward.
Short: Confirmed flat pattern is regular or expanded and C is a high pivot OR confirmed running flat and C is a low pivot OR valid 3-wave pullback with C a high pivot OR bearish 5-wave retest: after a flat pattern with A at a high, subsequent price moves below the fifth-wave pivot, then the bar overlaps the A/B/C price range and closes below its open OR valid truncated fifth wave is upward.
Calculation: Valid 3-wave pullback = four consecutive alternating pivots origin→A→B→C, with A and C pivot types matching and B opposite; |C−B|/|A−origin| is within the configured tolerance of 100%; for an uptrend, C is above origin × (1−tolerance) for continuation or below it for reversal; for a downtrend, C is below origin × (1+tolerance) for continuation or above it for reversal.
