Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high and lowest low during the first configured N minutes of each configured session; use only after that window is complete.
Calculation: VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 weighted by volume, divided by cumulative volume since the start of the current ET day.
Long: During a configured session after its opening range is complete and before the session ends (for NY, before its configured signal cutoff), close >= opening-range high + minimum breakout distance AND previous close < opening-range high + minimum breakout distance; OR low <= opening-range low AND close >= opening-range low + rejection buffer AND close <= opening-range high. If the optional rejection-wick filter is enabled, the rejection condition also requires close - low >= minimum wick size. If the optional VWAP filter is enabled, close > VWAP. Apply the configured per-session first-breakout and first-rejection limits; a rejection is disallowed on a bar that breaks out.
Short: During a configured session after its opening range is complete and before the session ends (for NY, before its configured signal cutoff), close <= opening-range low - minimum breakout distance AND previous close > opening-range low - minimum breakout distance; OR high >= opening-range high AND close <= opening-range high - rejection buffer AND close >= opening-range low. If the optional rejection-wick filter is enabled, the rejection condition also requires high - close >= minimum wick size. If the optional VWAP filter is enabled, close < VWAP. Apply the configured per-session first-breakout and first-rejection limits; a rejection is disallowed on a bar that breaks out.
