Calculation: Organ Flow = EMA of the EMA-smoothed sum of four rolling Z-scores: (1) change in a long-lookback EMA of close, (2) rate of change of close over a shorter lookback, (3) one-bar change in On-Balance Volume, and (4) one-bar change in ATR multiplied by +1 when close is above a shorter-lookback EMA of close and −1 otherwise. Each Z-score uses its own rolling mean and standard deviation.
Calculation: Organ Signal = EMA of Organ Flow, using the same lookback as the final EMA in Organ Flow.
Calculation: Chaos = EMA of the mean of the absolute pairwise differences among the trend, momentum, and volume Z-scores, using the same smoothing lookback as the Z-score sum in Organ Flow.
Calculation: Timeframe Force = EMA of the weighted sum of Organ Flow from three selected timeframes. For each selected timeframe, use its last completed bar; a selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: current Organ Flow crosses above the positive sync-flip threshold AND current Chaos is below the low-chaos threshold AND Timeframe Force is positive AND Organ Flow is positive on at least two of the three selected timeframes; OR current Organ Flow is above Organ Signal AND Timeframe Force is positive AND Organ Flow is positive on at least two of the three selected timeframes AND the absolute difference between Organ Flow and Organ Signal is greater than on the prior bar; OR current Chaos crosses below the chaos-collapse threshold AND its highest value over the recent lookback exceeds the high-chaos threshold AND current Organ Flow is above the positive sync-flip threshold AND Timeframe Force is positive AND Organ Flow is positive on at least two of the three selected timeframes. Each signal requires the current bar to be confirmed when close confirmation is enabled.
Short: current Organ Flow crosses below the negative sync-flip threshold AND current Chaos is below the low-chaos threshold AND Timeframe Force is negative AND Organ Flow is negative on at least two of the three selected timeframes; OR current Organ Flow is below Organ Signal AND Timeframe Force is negative AND Organ Flow is negative on at least two of the three selected timeframes AND the absolute difference between Organ Flow and Organ Signal is greater than on the prior bar; OR current Chaos crosses below the chaos-collapse threshold AND its highest value over the recent lookback exceeds the high-chaos threshold AND current Organ Flow is below the negative sync-flip threshold AND Timeframe Force is negative AND Organ Flow is negative on at least two of the three selected timeframes. Each signal requires the current bar to be confirmed when close confirmation is enabled.
