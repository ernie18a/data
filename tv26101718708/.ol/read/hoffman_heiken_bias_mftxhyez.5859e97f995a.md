Calculation: Net Heikin Ashi volume = volume × (up fraction − down fraction) × Heikin Ashi OHLC4; for bullish Heikin Ashi bars, up fraction = (top wick + bottom wick + 2 × body) / [2 × (top wick + bottom wick + body)] and down fraction = (top wick + bottom wick) / [2 × (top wick + bottom wick + body)]; for bearish bars, swap these fractions. Heikin Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin Ashi open = (previous Heikin Ashi open + previous Heikin Ashi close) / 2, initialized as (open + close) / 2; high and low are the maximum and minimum, respectively, of the bar's high or low and Heikin Ashi open and close. Body is the absolute difference between Heikin Ashi open and close; top wick and bottom wick are the distances from the Heikin Ashi high to the upper body edge and from the lower body edge to the Heikin Ashi low.
Calculation: Net volume projection = linear regression projection of net Heikin Ashi volume, extended forward by one regression lookback length.
Long: short-lookback SMA(close) > longer-lookback EMA(close) AND longer-lookback EMA(close) > each of the longer-lookback EMA(close), SMA(close), and EMA(close) regime averages and both bands formed by adding and subtracting half of a longer-lookback RMA(true range) from the longer-lookback EMA(close) AND net volume projection > 0
Short: short-lookback SMA(close) < longer-lookback EMA(close) AND longer-lookback EMA(close) < each of the longer-lookback EMA(close), SMA(close), and EMA(close) regime averages and both bands formed by adding and subtracting half of a longer-lookback RMA(true range) from the longer-lookback EMA(close) AND net volume projection < 0
