Calculation: Prior range high/low = highest high / lowest low over the prior range lookback bars, excluding the current bar; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; expansion size = (high − low) / ATR; volatility ratio = ATR / its longer-term simple moving average; volume ratio = volume / its simple moving average.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / max(high − low, minimum price increment).
Calculation: Expansion attempt = confirmed bar unless realtime confirmation is selected; candle range is at least the sensitivity-adjusted expansion threshold OR volatility ratio is at least 1.18; price probes beyond the prior range edge by at least the sensitivity-adjusted ATR edge threshold; close location is at least the sensitivity-adjusted close threshold for an upside attempt, or at most one minus that threshold for a downside attempt. If both sides qualify, choose upside when close is at or above the range midpoint, otherwise downside.
Calculation: Active expansion event = most recent qualifying attempt, retaining its direction, range edge, extreme, ATR, midpoint, expansion size, and volume ratio; update its extreme to the highest high for upside or lowest low for downside. Evaluate through the failure window, inclusive.
Calculation: Travel progress in ATR = distance from event edge to best extreme, in the event direction, divided by event ATR. Inside depth in ATR = distance of close back inside the event edge divided by event ATR. Edge distance in ATR = close's distance beyond the edge in the event direction divided by event ATR.
Calculation: Failure score = clamp(0–100, 18% × normalized expansion size + 29% × normalized inside depth + 22% × normalized low travel progress + 13% × normalized max(current, event) volume ratio + 18% × normalized inside depth). Normalize high-good components linearly from 0 at weak to 100 at strong; normalize low-good travel linearly from 100 at strong to 0 at weak; clamp each component to 0–100. Expansion size weak/strong = 0.85/1.65 × sensitivity-adjusted expansion threshold; inside-depth close-failure strong = max(0.70 × required travel, 0.20 ATR); travel strong/weak = 0.15/1 × required travel; volume weak/strong = 0.75 / 1.35 × volume-support threshold; inside-depth re-entry weak/strong = sensitivity-adjusted re-entry threshold / max(required travel, re-entry threshold + 0.35 ATR).
Calculation: Failure confirmation = realtime allowed, or bar confirmed for close-failure mode; for close-plus-volume mode, bar confirmed and current or event volume ratio meets support threshold; for strict mode, those conditions plus missing required travel, re-entry buffer met, and close below the context EMA after an upside attempt or above it after a downside attempt.
Calculation: Failure-ready = active event within its failure window AND failure confirmation AND inside depth meets the sensitivity-adjusted re-entry buffer AND travel progress is below required real travel AND failure score meets the review threshold.
Calculation: Failure-watch = active event within its failure window AND close is across the event edge toward the range AND travel progress is below required real travel AND failure score meets the watch threshold AND failure-ready is false.
Long: downside expansion attempt OR active downside failure-ready OR active downside failure-watch.
Short: upside expansion attempt OR active upside failure-ready OR active upside failure-watch.
