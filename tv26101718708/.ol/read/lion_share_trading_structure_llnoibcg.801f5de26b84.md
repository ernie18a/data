Calculation: Confirmed pivot high = a chart-bar high that is higher than the highs of the specified number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot low = a chart-bar low that is lower than the lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; it is confirmed after the bars on its right have completed.
Calculation: Daily bias = reset to neutral at each new day; while neutral, set to bullish if chart-bar high exceeds the previous completed higher-timeframe bar's high, otherwise set to bearish if chart-bar low is below that bar's low; retain the bias until the next day.
