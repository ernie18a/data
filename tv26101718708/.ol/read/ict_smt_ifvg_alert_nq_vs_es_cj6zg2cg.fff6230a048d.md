Calculation: Bullish SMT divergence = chart low below the lowest chart low in the preceding swing-lookback bars AND comparison-instrument low above its preceding swing-lookback low, OR comparison-instrument low below its preceding swing-lookback low AND chart low above its preceding swing-lookback low; use current chart-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar, and exclude the current bar from each lookback.
Calculation: Bearish SMT divergence = chart high above the highest chart high in the preceding swing-lookback bars AND comparison-instrument high below its preceding swing-lookback high, OR comparison-instrument high above its preceding swing-lookback high AND chart high below its preceding swing-lookback high; use current chart-timeframe bars, including the still-forming bar, and exclude the current bar from each lookback.
Calculation: Bullish 5-minute SMT divergence = chart 5-minute low below the lowest chart 5-minute low in the preceding HTF-lookback bars AND comparison-instrument 5-minute low above its preceding 5-minute low, OR comparison-instrument 5-minute low below its preceding 5-minute low AND chart 5-minute low above its preceding 5-minute low; use the higher 5-minute timeframe and its still-forming bar, excluding the current bar from each lookback.
Calculation: Bearish 5-minute SMT divergence = chart 5-minute high above the highest chart 5-minute high in the preceding HTF-lookback bars AND comparison-instrument 5-minute high below its preceding 5-minute high, OR comparison-instrument 5-minute high above its preceding 5-minute high AND chart 5-minute high below its preceding 5-minute high; use the higher 5-minute timeframe and its still-forming bar, excluding the current bar from each lookback.
Calculation: Bullish IFVG = low four bars ago minus high two bars ago is at least the minimum gap size AND low one bar ago is between high four bars ago and low two bars ago AND current close is at least high four bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish IFVG = low two bars ago minus high four bars ago is at least the minimum gap size AND high one bar ago is between high two bars ago and low four bars ago AND current close is at most low four bars ago.
Long: (Bullish SMT divergence AND Bullish IFVG) OR Bullish 5-minute SMT divergence
Short: (Bearish SMT divergence AND Bearish IFVG) OR Bearish 5-minute SMT divergence
