Calculation: Pivot = (high + low + close) / 3 of the last completed bar of a higher timeframe
Calculation: Resistance 1 = 2 × Pivot − low of the same bar
Calculation: Support 1 = 2 × Pivot − high of the same bar
Long: close > Resistance 1 when reverse is off OR close < Support 1 when reverse is on
Short: close < Support 1 when reverse is off OR close > Resistance 1 when reverse is on
