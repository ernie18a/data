Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot high = a bar high higher than the highs of the preceding and following pivot lookback bars; use higher-timeframe bars, with the source bar mapped using lookahead_on.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe pivot low = a bar low lower than the lows of the preceding and following pivot lookback bars; use higher-timeframe bars, with the source bar mapped using lookahead_on.
Calculation: BOS bias = starts neutral; becomes bullish when the higher-timeframe close crosses above the latest pivot high, bearish when it crosses below the latest pivot low, and persists until the opposite break. The higher-timeframe close is from the bar still forming (lookahead_on); clear the pivot level after its break.
Calculation: Protective stop = for a long, either the lower of the current and prior bar lows or the current bar open; for a short, either the higher of the current and prior bar highs or the current bar open, according to the selected stop source.
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus (long) or minus (short) the absolute distance from entry to protective stop multiplied by the configured risk/reward multiple.
Long: higher-timeframe close crosses above the latest pivot high OR (position is flat AND bars since last exit >= configured cooldown AND daily P&L < configured profit limit AND daily P&L > negative configured loss limit AND consecutive losses < configured maximum AND bar time is within the configured session if session filtering is enabled AND (BOS filtering is disabled OR BOS bias is bullish) AND prior bar close < prior bar open AND current bar close > current bar open AND current bar close > max(prior bar open, prior bar close) AND computed order quantity > 0)
Short: higher-timeframe close crosses below the latest pivot low OR (position is flat AND bars since last exit >= configured cooldown AND daily P&L < configured profit limit AND daily P&L > negative configured loss limit AND consecutive losses < configured maximum AND bar time is within the configured session if session filtering is enabled AND (BOS filtering is disabled OR BOS bias is bearish) AND prior bar close > prior bar open AND current bar close < current bar open AND current bar close < min(prior bar open, prior bar close) AND computed order quantity > 0)
Exit: long, current bar low <= protective stop OR current bar high >= profit target
Exit: short, current bar high >= protective stop OR current bar low <= profit target
