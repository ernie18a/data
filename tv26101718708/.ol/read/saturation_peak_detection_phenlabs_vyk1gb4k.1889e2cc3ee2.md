Calculation: Momentum = configured RSI(close), ROC(close), MFI(typical price, volume), or their average; normalize ROC to [0,100] as clamp(50 + 50 × ROC / (2 × 100-bar standard deviation of ROC + 0.0001)).
Calculation: RSI-space momentum = RSI for RSI mode, normalized ROC for ROC mode, MFI for MFI mode, or the average of RSI, normalized ROC, and MFI for composite mode.
Calculation: Rolling peak/trough = highest/lowest momentum over the configured peak lookback; anchor price = high/low of the bar containing that peak/trough.
Calculation: Adaptive pullback threshold = configured base percentage, optionally multiplied by clamp((ATR / close × 100) / (50-bar SMA of ATR / close × 100 + 0.0001), 0.6, 1.8).
Calculation: Bear saturation = confirmed bar AND momentum ≤ rolling peak × (1 − adaptive pullback threshold / 100) AND peak is unused AND cooldown elapsed AND (absolute-extreme gate disabled OR peak RSI-space momentum ≥ configured overbought threshold) AND (volume gate disabled OR peak-bar volume > 20-bar average volume × configured multiplier) AND (pivot-age gate disabled OR peak is at least the configured confirmation bars old) AND bear strength ≥ configured minimum.
Calculation: Bull saturation = confirmed bar AND momentum ≥ rolling trough + |rolling trough| × adaptive pullback threshold / 100 AND trough is unused AND cooldown elapsed AND (absolute-extreme gate disabled OR trough RSI-space momentum ≤ configured oversold threshold) AND (volume gate disabled OR trough-bar volume > 20-bar average volume × configured multiplier) AND (pivot-age gate disabled OR trough is at least the configured confirmation bars old) AND bull strength ≥ configured minimum.
Calculation: Bear strength = round(clamp((peak RSI-space momentum − 50) × 0.8, 0, 40) + clamp((actual percentage pullback − adaptive pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((peak-bar volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + (enabled bear-divergence bonus ? 20 : 0)); bear divergence means the current peak-bar high exceeds the previous signaled peak-bar high while the current rolling peak is lower than the previous signaled rolling peak.
Calculation: Bull strength = round(clamp((50 − trough RSI-space momentum) × 0.8, 0, 40) + clamp((actual percentage recovery − adaptive pullback threshold) × 2 + 10, 0, 20) + clamp((trough-bar volume / 20-bar average volume − 1) × 20, 0, 20) + (enabled bull-divergence bonus ? 20 : 0)); bull divergence means the current trough-bar low is below the previous signaled trough-bar low while the current rolling trough is higher than the previous signaled rolling trough.
Calculation: Zone bounds = anchor price ± (trigger-bar ATR × configured zone-width multiplier / 2); support invalidation = zone bottom × (1 − configured offset / 100); resistance invalidation = zone top × (1 + configured offset / 100).
Long: bull saturation OR a confirmed-bar touch of an active support zone (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom AND more than 2 bars since the previous zone touch).
Short: bear saturation OR a confirmed-bar touch of an active resistance zone (low ≤ zone top AND high ≥ zone bottom AND more than 2 bars since the previous zone touch).
Exit: long, close < active support-zone invalidation.
Exit: short, close > active resistance-zone invalidation.
