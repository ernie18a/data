Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar’s high/low that is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the specified number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot.
Calculation: Adaptive impulse threshold = base impulse × volatility ratio, where volatility ratio is ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average, bounded by lower and upper limits.
Long: low < latest confirmed swing low AND close > latest confirmed swing low AND close > open AND absolute(close − open) / ATR ≥ adaptive impulse threshold AND higher-timeframe close > its EMA (use the higher-timeframe bar still forming) AND latest confirmed swing low has not already produced a long signal AND close − low > 0
Short: high > latest confirmed swing high AND close < latest confirmed swing high AND close < open AND absolute(close − open) / ATR ≥ adaptive impulse threshold AND higher-timeframe close < its EMA (use the higher-timeframe bar still forming) AND latest confirmed swing high has not already produced a short signal AND high − close > 0
