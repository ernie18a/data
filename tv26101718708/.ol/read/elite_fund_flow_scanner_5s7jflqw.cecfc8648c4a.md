Calculation: Long-term and short-term simple moving averages = simple moving averages of close, with the long-term lookback longer than the short-term lookback
Calculation: Relative volume = volume divided by its average over a shorter lookback
Calculation: Relative strength = (close / close 10 bars ago) / (benchmark close / benchmark close 10 bars ago)
Calculation: Trend alignment = close above the long-term simple moving average AND the short-term simple moving average above the long-term simple moving average
Calculation: Momentum = RSI above its threshold AND RSI rising over three bars
Calculation: Bull market = benchmark short-term simple moving average above its long-term simple moving average
Calculation: Fund-flow score = count of true conditions among trend alignment, relative volume above its threshold, momentum, relative strength above its threshold, and bull market
Calculation: Accumulation = close above the long-term simple moving average AND close below the long-term simple moving average multiplied by (1 + its threshold)
Calculation: Strong breakout = close above the highest high over the lookback, including the current bar, AND relative volume above its threshold
Long: fund-flow score at least its threshold AND accumulation
Long: fund-flow score at least its threshold AND strong breakout
