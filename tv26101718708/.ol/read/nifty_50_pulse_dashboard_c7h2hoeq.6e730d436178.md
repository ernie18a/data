Calculation: Buy turnover percentage = 100 × Σ(volume × close × close-location-in-range) / Σ(volume × close) across tracked stocks, where close-location-in-range = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low; use each stock’s current still-forming bar on the selected data timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Long: Buy turnover percentage ≥ bullish threshold OR tracked stock or index close > its previous close on the selected data timeframe (current bar still forming; timeframe may be higher or lower than chart timeframe)
Short: Buy turnover percentage ≤ bearish threshold OR tracked stock or index close < its previous close on the selected data timeframe (current bar still forming; timeframe may be higher or lower than chart timeframe)
