Calculation: Volume threshold = 0 when volume filtering is disabled; otherwise, the selected manual threshold, the selected multiplier times average volume over the configured lookback, or the greater of those two thresholds when both are selected
Calculation: Lower ATR trailing stop = current low minus ATR times the configured multiplier; carry forward the previous stop when this value is lower, except reset to the current value when the configured price trigger is at or below the stop
Calculation: Upper ATR trailing stop = current high plus ATR times the configured multiplier; carry forward the previous stop when this value is higher, except reset to the current value when the configured price trigger is at or above the stop
Long: ADX > ADX from the longer entry lookback AND ADX > configured threshold AND positive directional indicator > negative directional indicator AND datetime is within the configured date range AND close >= configured moving average (or close when the moving average filter is disabled) AND volume > volume threshold
Short: ADX > ADX from the longer entry lookback AND ADX > configured threshold AND positive directional indicator < negative directional indicator AND datetime is within the configured date range AND close <= configured moving average (or close when the moving average filter is disabled) AND short trades are enabled AND volume > volume threshold
Exit: long, ADX < ADX from the shorter exit lookback OR (ATR trailing stop is enabled AND configured price trigger <= lower ATR trailing stop) OR (Parabolic SAR is enabled AND configured price trigger <= Parabolic SAR)
Exit: short, ADX < ADX from the shorter exit lookback OR (ATR trailing stop is enabled AND configured price trigger >= upper ATR trailing stop) OR (Parabolic SAR is enabled AND configured price trigger >= Parabolic SAR)
