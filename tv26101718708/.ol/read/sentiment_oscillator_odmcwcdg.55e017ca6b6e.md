Calculation: Market Sentiment = EMA(close − previous close) divided by EMA(volume), using the same lookback
Calculation: MACD = shorter-lookback EMA of Market Sentiment − longer-lookback EMA of Market Sentiment
Calculation: Momentum Histogram = MACD − EMA(MACD)
Long: Momentum Histogram crosses above zero
Short: Momentum Histogram crosses below zero
Exit: long, MACD crosses below zero
