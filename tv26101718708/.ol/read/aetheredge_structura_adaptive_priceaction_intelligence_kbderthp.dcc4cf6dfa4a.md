Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar whose high is higher than the highs of the N bars before and after it; it becomes available after those N later bars complete.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a bar whose low is lower than the lows of the N bars before and after it; it becomes available after those N later bars complete.
Long: close > most recent confirmed swing high AND that swing high has not previously been broken
Short: close < most recent confirmed swing low AND that swing low has not previously been broken
