Calculation: Cumulative safety-order deviation = sum of each step from 1 through the order number, where the first step is the initial deviation and each subsequent step is the previous step multiplied by the step multiplier
Calculation: Average entry = total quote cost of the base order and filled safety orders divided by total asset quantity acquired, with each fill’s quantity equal to its quote size divided by its fill price
Long: Flat AND lower-timeframe RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar is at or above its oversold level AND the previous sampled lower-timeframe RSI was below that level OR long position AND fewer than the maximum safety orders have filled AND close is at or below base entry × (1 − cumulative safety-order deviation for the next order / 100)
Exit: Long, close is at or above average entry × (1 + minimum profit threshold / 100) AND lower-timeframe RSI on the last completed lower-timeframe bar is at or below its overbought level AND the previous sampled lower-timeframe RSI was above that level
