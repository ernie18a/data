Calculation: Rolling return skewness = third central moment of returns divided by return standard deviation cubed; calculate on lower-timeframe close returns when realized estimation is enabled, otherwise on chart-timeframe price-source returns
Calculation: Normalized skew = robust median/MAD z-score or mean/stdev z-score of rolling return skewness, using either the medium lookback alone or the median of z-scores at half, medium, and double lookbacks
Long: A confirmed chart-timeframe pivot low has a lower price low and higher normalized skew than the preceding pivot low, OR a higher price low and lower normalized skew than the preceding pivot low; the pivot lows are no farther apart than the maximum pivot gap; and higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled or the latest higher-timeframe divergence state is bullish
Short: A confirmed chart-timeframe pivot high has a higher price high and lower normalized skew than the preceding pivot high, OR a lower price high and higher normalized skew than the preceding pivot high; the pivot highs are no farther apart than the maximum pivot gap; and higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled or the latest higher-timeframe divergence state is bearish
