Calculation: Recursive upper band = max(previous upper band, source) − 2/(length+1) × width; recursive lower band = min(previous lower band, source) + 2/(length+1) × width. Initialize previous bands to source.
Calculation: Width = selected method: ATR, standard deviation of source, average high−low range, recursive filtered volatility, or absolute difference between source and the previous band. Recursive filtered volatility = absolute source change when source has risen or fallen throughout the lookback; otherwise its previous value.
Calculation: Band-direction count = number of the last lookback comparisons where the lower band is greater than its prior value (long) or the upper band is less than its prior value (short).
Calculation: Entry stop = entry-bar low − ATR × multiplier (long); entry-bar high + ATR × multiplier (short). Take-profit = entry price + (entry price − long stop) × reward/risk ratio (long); entry price − (short stop − entry price) × reward/risk ratio (short).
Long: low crosses below the recursive lower band AND band-direction count = lookback AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI < oversold) AND (MFI filter disabled OR MFI < oversold) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (consolidation filter disabled OR consolidation count < consolidation lookback) AND (close-on-opposite enabled OR position is flat or long)
Short: high crosses above the recursive upper band AND band-direction count = lookback AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI > overbought) AND (MFI filter disabled OR MFI > overbought) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX > threshold) AND (consolidation filter disabled OR consolidation count < consolidation lookback) AND (close-on-opposite enabled OR position is flat or short)
Exit: long, low < entry stop OR high > take-profit
Exit: short, high > entry stop OR low < take-profit
