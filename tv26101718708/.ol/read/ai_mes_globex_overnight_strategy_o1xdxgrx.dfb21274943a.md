Calculation: Opening range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the configured session range-building window; lock these levels when that window ends. Opening range midpoint = (high + low) / 2; range size = high - low.
Calculation: Valid opening range = range size is between the configured minimum and maximum, inclusive.
Calculation: Breakout volume = current bar volume > its moving average over a longer lookback × breakout volume multiplier. Fade volume = current bar volume > the same moving average × fade volume multiplier.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar high/low that is higher/lower than the configured number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot of each type.
Calculation: Pivot break = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high (previous close at or below it), or crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low (previous close at or above it).
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Long stop = the lower of (latest confirmed pivot low minus stop buffer, or close minus ATR × stop multiplier if no pivot low exists) and close minus ATR × stop multiplier. Short stop = the higher of (latest confirmed pivot high plus stop buffer, or close plus ATR × stop multiplier if no pivot high exists) and close plus ATR × stop multiplier. Calculate at entry.
Calculation: Long target = opening range midpoint for a fade entry with no breakout entry; otherwise close + (close - long stop) × reward-to-risk ratio. Short target = opening range midpoint for a fade entry with no breakout entry; otherwise close - (short stop - close) × reward-to-risk ratio. Calculate at entry.
Long: In the configured overnight session AND trade count is below its configured limit AND flat AND outside the configured news blackout AND (valid opening range AND (close crosses above opening range high + breakout buffer AND RSI > breakout-long threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR breakout volume condition) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) OR latest confirmed pivot high is crossed upward AND close > higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI > breakout-long threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR breakout volume condition) OR valid opening range AND high >= opening range high - fade buffer AND close < opening range high AND previous close >= opening range high AND RSI < oversold threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR fade volume condition) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA)).
Short: In the configured overnight session AND trade count is below its configured limit AND flat AND outside the configured news blackout AND (valid opening range AND (close crosses below opening range low - breakout buffer AND RSI < breakout-short threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR breakout volume condition) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) OR latest confirmed pivot low is crossed downward AND close < higher-timeframe EMA AND RSI < breakout-short threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR breakout volume condition) OR valid opening range AND low <= opening range low + fade buffer AND close > opening range low AND previous close <= opening range low AND RSI > overbought threshold AND (volume filter disabled OR fade volume condition) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA)).
Exit: long, low <= entry stop OR high >= entry target OR the configured session ends.
Exit: short, high >= entry stop OR low <= entry target OR the configured session ends.
