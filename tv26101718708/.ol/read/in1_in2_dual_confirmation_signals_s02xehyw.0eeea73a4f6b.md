Calculation: Stochastic RSI = RSI of close on the selected In1 timeframe, then the stochastic of that RSI (using the RSI as high, low, and close), smoothed with a shorter-period SMA; use completed selected-timeframe bars when wait-for-close is on, or the still-forming bars when it is off. The selected timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI SuperTrend direction = form bands at Stochastic RSI ± a multiplier; trail the upper band downward unless the prior Stochastic RSI crossed above the prior upper band, and trail the lower band upward unless it crossed below the prior lower band. When the prior direction used the upper band, switch to bullish direction if Stochastic RSI crosses above the current upper band; otherwise remain bearish. When it used the lower band, switch to bearish direction if Stochastic RSI crosses below the current lower band; otherwise remain bullish.
Calculation: EVEREX oscillator = average six price-flow components: current close location within its bar range, close-open spread as a percent of bar range, that spread’s absolute size normalized against its longer-lookback SMA and signed by spread direction, current close location within the two-bar range, close change as a percent of the two-bar range, and that change’s absolute size normalized against its longer-lookback SMA and signed by change direction. Map each normalized magnitude ratio x to 1.00 if x>1.50, 0.90 if x>1.20, 0.80 if x>1.00, 0.70 if x>0.80, 0.60 if x>0.60, 0.50 if x>0.40, 0.25 if x>0.20, otherwise 0.10; multiply by 100. Multiply the six-component average by volume normalized to its longer-lookback SMA using the same mapping. Split positive and negative flow into bulls and bears, smooth each with a WMA, transform their ratio to a -100-to-100 oscillator, then smooth it with a shorter-period WMA.
Long: EVEREX oscillator is rising now and was not rising on the prior bar AND (Stochastic RSI SuperTrend direction just switched to bullish and Stochastic RSI < 50 OR a bullish switch occurred 1–2 bars ago and Stochastic RSI is rising).
Short: EVEREX oscillator is not rising now and was rising on the prior bar AND (Stochastic RSI SuperTrend direction just switched to bearish and Stochastic RSI > 50 OR a bearish switch occurred 1–2 bars ago and Stochastic RSI is falling).
