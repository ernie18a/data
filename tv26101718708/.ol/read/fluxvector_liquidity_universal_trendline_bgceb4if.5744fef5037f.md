Calculation: Adaptive trendline = initialize to close; thereafter previous trendline + gain × ((close − previous trendline) + 0.25 × flow velocity × current range). Current range = max(high − low, ε). Gain = minimum gain + (maximum gain − minimum gain) × min(1, sigmoid(energy) × (0.5 + 0.5 × sigmoid(impact efficiency))). Sigmoid(x) = 1 / (1 + exp(−x)). Energy = z-score of directional liquidity pulse + z-score of liquidity density − z-score of volatility curvature; each z-score uses the same energy lookback. Directional liquidity pulse = ((0.6 × (close − open) / range) − 0.4 × ((high − close − (close − low)) / range)) × square root of normalized volume. Normalized volume = volume / SMA(volume, flow lookback). Liquidity density = normalized volume / max(range / close, ε). Volatility curvature = current realized volatility − 2 × previous realized volatility + realized volatility from two bars ago; realized volatility = standard deviation of log(close / previous close) over the volatility lookback. Impact efficiency = absolute change in close / (range + ε) × square root of normalized volume. Flow velocity = EMA(directional liquidity pulse, max(5, flow lookback / 3)).
Calculation: One-bar projection = current adaptive trendline + (current adaptive trendline − previous adaptive trendline).
Long: close < adaptive trendline AND one-bar projection > adaptive trendline
Short: close > adaptive trendline AND one-bar projection < adaptive trendline
Exit: long, close > adaptive trendline AND one-bar projection < adaptive trendline
