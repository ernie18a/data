Calculation: Anchored VWAP = cumulative (high + low + close) / 3 × volume divided by cumulative volume, reset at the configured session, week, or month boundary
Calculation: Reclaim score = clamp(24 × clamp(directional close distance from VWAP / ATR / 0.70, 0, 1) + 18 × clamp(1 − bars since the most recent same-direction pullback / pullback lookback, 0, 1) + 18 × clamp(directional wick / bar range / 0.55, 0, 1) + 12 × clamp(abs(close − open) / bar range / 0.65, 0, 1) + relative-volume score + slope score + 8 × clamp(abs(close − VWAP) / max(1.2 × ATR, minimum tick), 0, 1), 0, 100); relative-volume score = 12 × clamp(current volume / average volume over a shorter lookback / 1.6, 0, 1) when enabled, otherwise 8; slope score = 8 when the VWAP change over three bars aligns with the direction, otherwise 2.8
Calculation: Pullback quality = 100 × (pullback freshness score + wick score + close-distance score) / 60, where the component scores are those used in the reclaim score
Long: VWAP and ATR are valid AND volume meets the configured minimum (or no minimum is configured) AND a low reached at least one pullback-pocket depth below VWAP within the configured lookback AND close > VWAP + reclaim buffer AND previous close <= current VWAP AND reclaim score >= configured ready threshold AND the bar is confirmed; OR a confirmed bar has low <= VWAP − pullback-pocket depth AND pullback quality >= both configured watch and historical-label thresholds AND no ready reclaim event occurs on that bar
Short: VWAP and ATR are valid AND volume meets the configured minimum (or no minimum is configured) AND a high reached at least one pullback-pocket depth above VWAP within the configured lookback AND close < VWAP − reclaim buffer AND previous close >= current VWAP AND reclaim score >= configured ready threshold AND the bar is confirmed; OR a confirmed bar has high >= VWAP + pullback-pocket depth AND pullback quality >= both configured watch and historical-label thresholds AND no ready reclaim event occurs on that bar AND the bar does not also meet the bullish pullback condition
Exit: Long, an active reclaim is within its configured bar window, T2 has not been reached, the bar is confirmed, and close < reclaim VWAP − reclaim ATR × invalidation shelf
Exit: Short, an active reclaim is within its configured bar window, T2 has not been reached, the bar is confirmed, and close > reclaim VWAP + reclaim ATR × invalidation shelf
