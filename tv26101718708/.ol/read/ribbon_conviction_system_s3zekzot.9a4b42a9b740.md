Calculation: Ribbon averages = a short HMA, then progressively longer EMA/KAMAs; crossover lines are any configured subset of the longer averages.
Calculation: Conviction score = a weighted average of (1) direction-aligned z-scored signed price-volume flow from lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar (or equal-weight price direction if volume is unavailable), (2) direction-aligned effort-versus-move quality, (3) Kaufman Efficiency Ratio, and (4) a weighted location score combining session VWAP slope, swing-anchored VWAP position and slope, session value-area position, and prior-session VWAP/POC acceptance or obstacle distance; multiply by a VPIN-style flow-toxicity dampener and a higher-timeframe stop-agreement factor. The three stop directions use bars 3×, 5×, and 15× the chart timeframe, with the current higher-timeframe bar when forming and the latest available value otherwise.
Long: the short HMA crosses above any configured longer crossover line AND conviction score >= configured minimum AND (if clearance filter is enabled, close > crossed line + configured ATR fraction × ATR) AND (if opening-session filter is enabled, the excluded opening bars have passed).
Short: the short HMA crosses below any configured longer crossover line AND conviction score >= configured minimum AND (if clearance filter is enabled, close < crossed line - configured ATR fraction × ATR) AND (if opening-session filter is enabled, the excluded opening bars have passed).
