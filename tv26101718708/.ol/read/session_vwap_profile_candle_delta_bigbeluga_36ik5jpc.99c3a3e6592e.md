Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of close since the start of the current higher-timeframe session, including the current chart bar
Calculation: Upper and lower session VWAP bands = Session VWAP plus or minus a configured multiple of the volume-weighted standard deviation of close within the current session
Long: low < lower session VWAP band AND close > lower session VWAP band
Short: high > upper session VWAP band AND close < upper session VWAP band
