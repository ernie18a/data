Calculation: Rolling VWAP and σ = volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of hlc3 over the lookback bars, using prior bars and excluding the current bar; calculate on chart bars or, when enabled, higher-timeframe bars (use the forming bar or last completed bar according to the confirmation setting).
Calculation: Deviation bands = VWAP ± kσ, or VWAP × exp(±kσ/VWAP) in geometric mode, for k = 1 through 6.
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bullish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot low is higher than the previous RSI pivot low while the corresponding price low is lower; bearish RSI divergence = a confirmed RSI pivot high is lower than the previous RSI pivot high while the corresponding price high is higher.
Long: close crosses above VWAP OR (a bar's low is within a configurable σ-based tolerance of any VWAP or deviation band, or is at/below that line and closes above it, OR close is below the lower 6σ band) AND, within a configurable momentum window, RSI reached the oversold level OR crossed back above it OR bullish RSI divergence occurred; emit only when this reversal condition changes from false to true OR close crosses above any lower 2σ–6σ band.
Short: close crosses below VWAP OR (a bar's high is within a configurable σ-based tolerance of any VWAP or deviation band, or is at/above that line and closes below it, OR close is above the upper 6σ band) AND, within a configurable momentum window, RSI reached the overbought level OR crossed back below it OR bearish RSI divergence occurred; emit only when this reversal condition changes from false to true OR close crosses below any upper 2σ–6σ band.
