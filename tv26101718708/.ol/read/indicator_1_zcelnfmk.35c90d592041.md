Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = pivot high/low with equal left and right lookbacks; each pivot becomes confirmed after the right-side bars complete. Track the two most recent confirmed highs and lows.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high / below the latest confirmed swing low, respectively (current close above/below the level and previous close at/below/at-or-above it). Set structure state to bullish/bearish on the respective break. Bullish/bearish change of character = bullish/bearish break when the prior structure state was bearish/bullish.
Calculation: Structural uptrend = latest swing high > prior swing high AND latest swing low > prior swing low. Structural downtrend = latest swing high < prior swing high AND latest swing low < prior swing low.
Calculation: Range midpoint = (highest high + lowest low) / 2 over the range lookback.
Calculation: Normalized return entropy = normalize over the 200-bar rolling min/max of [stdev(abs(log(close / prior close))) / SMA(abs(log(close / prior close)))], using the entropy lookback for stdev and SMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish phase score = count of these four true comparisons: close > / < fast EMA; fast EMA > / < next EMA; next EMA > / < next EMA; next EMA > / < slow EMA. Bullish/bearish phase lock = corresponding score >= 3 AND close > / < the second-fastest EMA.
Calculation: Volume ratio = volume / SMA(volume). Volume compression = volume ratio < compression threshold. Volume surge = volume ratio > surge threshold AND volume > 1.2 × prior volume. Bullish/bearish volume resonance = volume surge AND close > / < open AND the prior 5-bar minimum volume ratio < compression threshold.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish smart-money candle = abs(close − open)/(high − low) > body threshold AND close > / < open AND (close − low)/(high − low) > 0.6 / < 0.4 AND volume > SMA(volume).
Calculation: Bullish/bearish momentum acceleration = velocity > 0 / < 0 AND velocity > / < prior velocity AND prior velocity > / < velocity two bars ago, where velocity = (close − close three bars ago) / ATR.
Calculation: Choppiness = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATR over chop lookback / (highest high − lowest low over chop lookback)) / log10(chop lookback). Hard block = choppiness > hard threshold; soft warning = choppiness > soft threshold AND not hard-blocked.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish sweep = within the sweep-age bars, lowest low < / highest high > the prior sweep-lookback lowest low / highest high, AND current close > / < that prior level AND close > / < open.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = fast EMA > / < slow EMA calculated on the last completed higher-timeframe bar; fast EMA uses a shorter lookback than slow EMA.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order block = the most recent prior bearish/bullish candle within the order-block lookback followed by a bullish/bearish candle whose body is at least the impulse threshold × ATR and whose volume is >= its volume SMA. Bullish block bounds are that prior candle's low/high; bearish block bounds are its low/high. Bullish block is valid while close > its low; bearish block is valid while close < its high.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish FVG = low > high two bars ago AND close > open / high < low two bars ago AND close < open. Bullish FVG-in-block = bullish FVG AND valid bullish block AND high two bars ago lies within the block. Bearish FVG-in-block = bearish FVG AND valid bearish block AND low two bars ago lies within the block.
Calculation: Bullish risk = close − (bullish block low − stop buffer × ATR); bullish risk in ATR = bullish risk / ATR. Bullish geometry passes if the bullish block is valid AND risk in ATR is between minimum and maximum risk thresholds, inclusive; bypass this test when the geometry filter is disabled.
Calculation: Bull score = sum of: normalized return entropy below compression threshold; bullish phase lock; volume compression OR bullish volume resonance; bullish smart-money candle; bullish momentum acceleration; bullish sweep; bullish higher-timeframe bias; 2 × bullish change of character; bullish FVG-in-block.
Calculation: Exit score = sum of: normalized return entropy > 1 − compression threshold; bearish phase lock; volume compression OR bearish volume resonance; bearish smart-money candle; bearish momentum acceleration.
Calculation: Entry threshold = minimum score, reduced by 1 when bullish change of character AND not structural uptrend; add 1 when soft warning is active.
Calculation: Entry stop = bullish block low − stop buffer × ATR. Entry target 1/2 = entry close + bullish risk × target-1/target-2 R-multiple. After target 1 is reached, the stop moves to entry price and the remaining position has target 2.
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish change of character OR (bull score >= entry threshold AND prior bull score < prior entry threshold AND not hard-blocked AND (structure state is bullish OR bullish change of character) AND close < range midpoint AND bullish geometry passes AND RSI < overbought threshold AND bars since last signal >= cooldown AND position is flat).
Short: bearish structure break.
Exit: long, exit score >= 3 OR bearish change of character OR (close > range midpoint AND bearish smart-money candle).
Exit: long, low <= entry stop.
Exit: long, high >= target 1 (close half; move stop on remainder to entry price).
Exit: long, high >= target 2 OR low <= entry price after target 1.
Exit: long, bars held >= time-stop bars AND target 1 has not been reached.
