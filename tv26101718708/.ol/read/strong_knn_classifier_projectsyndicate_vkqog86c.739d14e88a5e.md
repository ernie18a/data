Calculation: Feature vector = z-scores over a rolling normalization window of (1) net delta divided by chart-bar volume plus one, (2) buy-minus-sell volume divided by total buy-and-sell volume, (3) absorption, (4) CVD slope, and (5) close-minus-VWAP divided by ATR. Reconstruct delta and buy/sell volume from successive lower-timeframe intrabar closes, weighting by intrabar volume; split unchanged closes evenly. If intrabar data is unavailable, use chart-bar volume signed by the chart close change from the previous close. Absorption = volume z-score divided by one plus absolute candle body/ATR when volume exceeds its average-volume threshold, otherwise zero. CVD slope = change in cumulative delta over its lookback divided by that lookback and average volume plus one. Use completed intrabars; evaluate signals at chart-bar close.
Calculation: Reversal probability = logistic of sharpness times a prior-shrunk, distance-weighted vote among the k nearest resolved historical feature vectors. Distance is the sum across features of ln(1 + absolute feature difference); neighbor weight is exp(−distance/temperature). The vote is (reversal weight − continuation weight)/total weight, shrunk by total weight/(total weight + prior strength). Label a historical vector continuation when the forward close move over the outcome horizon, signed by the trend direction over the trend-reference lookback, exceeds the ATR-based outcome threshold; label it reversal when the signed move is below the negative threshold. Store only decisive labels after their horizon has completed. Require the resolved sample pool to reach its minimum size.
Calculation: Confidence = 2 × absolute value of (reversal probability − 0.5).
Calculation: Structural zone = within the configured ATR band of VWAP or the latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high or low.
Long: Close > close at the trend-reference lookback AND reversal probability < 0.5 OR close < close at the trend-reference lookback AND reversal probability > 0.5; confidence >= minimum signal threshold; resolved sample pool is sufficient; chart bar is confirmed; structural zone condition is met when zone filtering is enabled; and bars since the prior signal > cooldown.
Short: Close < close at the trend-reference lookback AND reversal probability < 0.5 OR close > close at the trend-reference lookback AND reversal probability > 0.5; confidence >= minimum signal threshold; resolved sample pool is sufficient; chart bar is confirmed; structural zone condition is met when zone filtering is enabled; and bars since the prior signal > cooldown.
