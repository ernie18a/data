Calculation: CEI = (indecision + fear + greed) / 3; each component is the mean of its pattern rates over the preceding lookback bars. Indecision = mean(doji rate, spinning-top rate); fear = mean(shooting-star rate, hanging-man rate, bearish-engulfing rate); greed = mean(marubozu rate, hammer rate, bullish-engulfing rate, three-white-soldiers rate). Each rate is the count of matching patterns divided by lookback length. For a candle, body = |close − open|, range = high − low, upper wick = high − max(open, close), and lower wick = min(open, close) − low. Doji: body/range < doji threshold. Spinning top: body/range < spinning-top threshold and body/range ≥ doji threshold. Shooting star: upper wick/body > shooting-star threshold and lower wick < body. Hanging man: lower wick/body > hanging-man threshold and upper wick < body. Bearish engulfing: current close < previous open, current open > previous close, and current body/previous body > engulfing threshold. Marubozu: body/range > marubozu threshold. Hammer: lower wick/body > hammer threshold and upper wick < body. Bullish engulfing: current close > previous open, current open < previous close, and current body/previous body > engulfing threshold. Three white soldiers: three consecutive bullish candles with strictly ascending closes.
Long: CEI crosses above the lower threshold AND position is flat AND cooldown has expired.
Short: CEI crosses above the higher threshold AND position is flat AND cooldown has expired.
Exit: long, (close > open AND volume > average volume over the lookback × volume multiplier AND close > entry price) OR (bars held ≥ maximum holding period AND close ≥ entry price) OR (bars held ≥ loss holding period AND close < entry price × (1 − loss threshold)).
Exit: short, (close < open AND volume > average volume over the lookback × volume multiplier AND close < entry price) OR (bars held ≥ maximum holding period AND close ≤ entry price) OR (bars held ≥ loss holding period AND close > entry price × (1 + loss threshold)).
