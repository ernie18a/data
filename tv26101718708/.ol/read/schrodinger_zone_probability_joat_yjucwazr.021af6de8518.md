Calculation: Bullish FVG zone = [high two bars ago, current low] when current low > high two bars ago, prior close > high two bars ago, and gap ≥ ATR × configurable minimum.
Calculation: Bearish FVG zone = [current high, low two bars ago] when current high < low two bars ago, prior close < low two bars ago, and gap ≥ ATR × configurable minimum.
Calculation: Displacement candle = body ≥ ATR × configurable multiple and body/range ≥ 0.55; upward if close > open, downward if close < open.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = range of a qualifying bearish candle found within the configurable lookback before an upward displacement; bearish order-block zone = range of a qualifying bullish candle before a downward displacement. A candidate's range must be ≤ 2.5 × ATR and body/range ≥ 0.25. Use the most recent candidate, the candidate with the lowest low for bullish / highest high for bearish, or, in strict-volume mode, the most recent candidate whose volume ≥ 20-bar volume SMA.
Calculation: Support probability and resistance probability = (1 ± side score)/2, clamped to [0.01, 0.99], where side score = clamp((direction weight × direction bias + flow weight × flow bias) × (0.4 + 0.6 × (distance weight × distance pull + (1 − distance weight) × 0.5), −1, 1); weights are normalized by their sum. Direction bias = clamp(((close − zone midpoint) / zone height) / 4, −1, 1). Distance pull = exp(−ln(2) × (distance from close to zone midpoint / max(current ATR, half formation ATR)) / configurable ATR half-life). Flow bias = clamp(((EMA slope over one bar + ROC over half the configurable flow lookback) / 2 / ATR over max(14, flow lookback)) / 1.5, −1, 1); smooth flow bias with an EMA over max(5, flow lookback / 4). Use configurable distance, direction, and flow weights.
Calculation: Touch = high ≥ zone bottom − tolerance × ATR and low ≤ zone top + tolerance × ATR (wick mode), or close within those expanded bounds (close mode). Record the first touched bar. After the configurable hold-bar count, compare the mean close over those bars with the zone bounds; if it is inside or on a bound, use current close versus zone midpoint to choose the side.
Long: An uncollapsed zone exists and its support probability ≥ its resistance probability, OR a touched zone has reached the hold-bar count and (mean close over the hold bars > zone top OR mean close is between or on the zone bounds and current close ≥ zone midpoint).
Short: An uncollapsed zone exists and its support probability < its resistance probability, OR a touched zone has reached the hold-bar count and (mean close over the hold bars < zone bottom OR mean close is between or on the zone bounds and current close < zone midpoint).
