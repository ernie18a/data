Calculation: MACD line = fast EMA of close − slow EMA of close
Calculation: MACD signal line = EMA of the MACD line
Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line − MACD signal line
Calculation: Peak profit = highest close-based open-profit percentage since entry
Long: position is flat AND bars since last entry ≥ cooldown AND ADX > trend threshold AND volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier AND close > trend EMA AND trend EMA > its value at the longer slope lookback AND ((bullish MACD crossover occurred within the validity lookback AND MACD line > MACD signal line) OR (MACD line > MACD signal line AND MACD histogram > 0 AND close > highest high over the shorter continuation lookback, excluding the current bar))
Short: position is flat AND bars since last entry ≥ cooldown AND ADX > trend threshold AND volume > volume SMA × volume multiplier AND close < trend EMA AND trend EMA < its value at the longer slope lookback AND ((bearish MACD crossunder occurred within the validity lookback AND MACD line < MACD signal line) OR (MACD line < MACD signal line AND MACD histogram < 0 AND close < lowest low over the shorter continuation lookback, excluding the current bar))
Exit: long, current close-based open-profit percentage is at or below the trailing level after peak profit reaches the activation threshold (trailing level is peak profit × (1 − giveback percentage) in relative mode, or peak profit − giveback percentage points in points mode) OR current bar low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR bearish MACD crossover when opposite-cross exit is enabled
Exit: short, current close-based open-profit percentage is at or below the trailing level after peak profit reaches the activation threshold (trailing level is peak profit × (1 − giveback percentage) in relative mode, or peak profit − giveback percentage points in points mode) OR current bar high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR bullish MACD crossover when opposite-cross exit is enabled
