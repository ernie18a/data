Calculation: Candle body = abs(close − open); candle range = high − low.
Calculation: Bullish leg = consecutive bullish candles whose body / close × 100 meets the configured minimum move; bearish leg = consecutive bearish candles meeting the same threshold. Leg length is configurable.
Calculation: Base = consecutive candles immediately before the leg, with configurable maximum length; its average body is less than the leg’s average body divided by the configured leg/base ratio, and every base candle’s body is less than 0.65 × the leg’s average body.
Calculation: Demand zone = [lowest low of base, highest open or close of base]; supply zone = [lowest open or close of base, highest high of base].
Calculation: Zone tests = earlier bars intersecting the zone, counted from the leg’s first bar toward the present. A demand zone is mitigated if any such bar closes below its lower boundary; a supply zone is mitigated if any closes above its upper boundary.
Calculation: Zone score = tight-base points (2 for a base of at most 2 bars, 1 for at most 4, otherwise 0) + leg/base average-body ratio points (2 at least 4, 1 at least 2, otherwise 0) + freshness points (2 with 0 tests, 1 with 1 test, otherwise 0) + 1 if the most recent leg candle closes in the top 30% of its range for demand or bottom 30% for supply + 1 if its volume exceeds 1.5 × the average volume of the 20 bars before the leg + 1 if total leg range exceeds 1.5 × leg length × 14-bar ATR; cap at 10.
Long: A bullish leg follows a valid base, forming a demand zone with score at least the configured minimum, and the zone is not mitigated unless retaining mitigated zones is enabled, and has zero tests if fresh-only is enabled; OR the close is inside the live demand zone (at or below the highest open or close of the last 20 bars and at or above the lowest low of the last 20 bars) and the prior bar’s close was outside its live demand zone.
Short: A bearish leg follows a valid base, forming a supply zone with score at least the configured minimum, and the zone is not mitigated unless retaining mitigated zones is enabled, and has zero tests if fresh-only is enabled; OR the close is inside the live supply zone (at or above the lowest open or close of the last 20 bars and at or below the highest high of the last 20 bars) and the prior bar’s close was outside its live supply zone.
