Calculation: Chaikin Money Flow = sum over the lookback of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low), divided by summed volume; use zero for the multiplier when high = low.
Calculation: Stochastic %K = the smoothed moving average of 100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback); %D = a further smoothed moving average of %K.
Calculation: Stochastic crossover state = +1 after %K crosses above %D, −1 after it crosses below %D, and 0 until either crossover; retain the state until the next crossover.
Long: On any configured scan timeframe, Chaikin Money Flow ≤ its buy threshold OR RSI > 50 OR the latest two confirmed close pivot lows form a lower low while the latest two confirmed RSI pivot lows form a higher low OR stochastic crossover state = +1 OR (stochastic crossover state = 0 AND %K < 20). Use that scan timeframe’s latest completed bar, whether it is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
Short: On any configured scan timeframe, Chaikin Money Flow ≥ its sell threshold OR RSI ≤ 50 OR the latest two confirmed close pivot highs form a higher high while the latest two confirmed RSI pivot highs form a lower high OR stochastic crossover state = −1 OR (stochastic crossover state = 0 AND %K > 80). Use that scan timeframe’s latest completed bar, whether it is higher or lower than the chart timeframe.
