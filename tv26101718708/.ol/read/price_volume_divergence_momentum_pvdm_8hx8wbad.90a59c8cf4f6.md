Calculation: PVD momentum = normalize the change in an adaptively smoothed price-volume divergence by an adaptive average of its absolute change; apply optional smoothing.
Calculation: Pressure reserve = clip a decayed running sum of price-volume divergence changes, divided by a scaled adaptive average of their absolute changes; apply optional smoothing.
Long: PVD momentum >= 0 OR pressure reserve >= 0
Short: PVD momentum < 0 OR pressure reserve < 0
