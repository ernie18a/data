Calculation: Fast/slow Williams %R = Williams %R smoothed by EMA; fast uses a shorter lookback than slow. Bull pressure holds while both are above the upper threshold; bear pressure holds while both are below the lower threshold. A run completes on the first bar its pressure condition no longer holds. A qualifying event has a run high-low range at least the configured minimum ATR multiple and, when enabled, a run duration at least the configured minimum bars.
Calculation: Bull rail event = completion of a separate bull-pressure run; its rail is the run low. Bear rail event = completion of a separate bear-pressure run; its rail is the run high. The two %R lookbacks use the same fast-versus-slow relationship as above.
Calculation: Body-weighted midpoint = (high + low)/2 + abs(close - open)/(high - low) * (close - (high + low)/2), using zero for the fraction when high = low.
Calculation: Fast rail = adaptive follower of the body-weighted midpoint with a shorter base length; structure rail = same follower with a longer base length and step capped at 1.5 ATR. For each follower, adapt its length as max(base length/(1 + abs(midpoint - prior rail)/ATR), 1), then move the prior rail toward the midpoint by the difference divided by that length. Fast rail step is capped at 2.5 ATR. Fast rail direction turns up or down when its rail change exceeds the configured ATR dead zone; otherwise it retains its direction. Structure trend turns up when the midpoint crosses above the structure rail plus its configured half-width in longer-period ATR, and down when it crosses below the rail minus that half-width; otherwise it retains its direction.
Long: A qualifying bullish pressure event completes OR (trend state is unset AND a bull rail event completes) OR (trend state is bearish AND close > defended level AND close > lower bull rail) OR (trend state is bearish AND fast rail direction is up AND structure trend is up).
Short: A qualifying bearish pressure event completes OR (trend state is unset AND a bear rail event completes) OR (trend state is bullish AND close < defended level AND close < upper bear rail) OR (trend state is bullish AND a highest completed bull-event high exists AND fast rail direction is down) OR (trend state is bullish AND no highest completed bull-event high exists AND fast rail direction is down AND structure trend is down).
