Calculation: Early top/bottom confidence = 100 × the sum of enabled factor weights whose live-bar conditions are true divided by the sum of enabled weights for RSI extreme, volume spike, Bollinger-band pierce, mean extension, Stoch-RSI extreme, wick rejection, higher-timeframe RSI extreme, and liquidity sweep.
Calculation: Confirmed top/bottom confidence = clamp(100 × (matching enabled factor weights + regime boost − opposing-regime penalty − hidden-divergence penalty) / (sum of enabled factor weights + regime adjustment weight), 0, 100). Factors are RSI extreme, regular RSI divergence, volume spike, Bollinger-band pierce, mean extension, Stoch-RSI extreme, wick rejection, engulfing candle, higher-timeframe RSI extreme, and liquidity sweep. The regime adjustment boosts a reversal aligned with the trend and penalizes one against it; its magnitude is greater in a strong trend. Hidden RSI divergence penalizes the corresponding reversal.
Calculation: Regular bearish RSI divergence = price makes a higher pivot high while RSI at that pivot makes a lower high; regular bullish RSI divergence = price makes a lower pivot low while RSI at that pivot makes a higher low. Hidden bearish divergence = price makes a lower pivot high while RSI makes a higher high; hidden bullish divergence = price makes a higher pivot low while RSI makes a lower low.
Calculation: Mean extension = average of the signed distance from the EMA mean and session VWAP, each divided by ATR; positive measures extension above both means, negative measures extension below both means.
Calculation: Trend regime = fast EMA above/below slow EMA and EMA mean rising/falling over its lookback; strong trend additionally requires the absolute close change divided by the sum of absolute close changes over the same lookback to meet its threshold. Fast EMA is shorter-term than slow EMA.
Long: Live-bar bottom confidence crosses up through its threshold OR a confirmed pivot low has bottom confidence at least the minimum, with at least the minimum number of enabled bottom factors voting and enough bars since the prior confirmed bottom.
Long: At the confirmed pivot low, RSI or higher-timeframe RSI is oversold; OR bullish RSI divergence; OR volume exceeds its moving average by the configured multiplier; OR the low pierces the lower Bollinger band; OR mean extension below the means meets its threshold; OR Stoch-RSI is oversold; OR the lower wick is at least the configured multiple of the body and no shorter than the upper wick; OR a bullish engulfing candle; OR the low sweeps below prior lows and closes above the low. The pivot must be confirmed by left and right chart bars; sample factors at the pivot bar. The higher-timeframe RSI uses the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Short: Live-bar top confidence crosses up through its threshold OR a confirmed pivot high has top confidence at least the minimum, with at least the minimum number of enabled top factors voting and enough bars since the prior confirmed top.
Short: At the confirmed pivot high, RSI or higher-timeframe RSI is overbought; OR bearish RSI divergence; OR volume exceeds its moving average by the configured multiplier; OR the high pierces the upper Bollinger band; OR mean extension above the means meets its threshold; OR Stoch-RSI is overbought; OR the upper wick is at least the configured multiple of the body and no shorter than the lower wick; OR a bearish engulfing candle; OR the high sweeps above prior highs and closes below the high. The pivot must be confirmed by left and right chart bars; sample factors at the pivot bar. The higher-timeframe RSI uses the last completed bar of a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Exit: long, close is below the pivot low minus the configured ATR hard-invalidation buffer OR high reaches the mapped target.
Exit: short, close is above the pivot high plus the configured ATR hard-invalidation buffer OR low reaches the mapped target.
