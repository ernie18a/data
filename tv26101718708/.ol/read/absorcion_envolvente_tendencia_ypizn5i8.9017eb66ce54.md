Calculation: Buy volume fraction = (close − low) / (high − low) when high > low and volume > 0; otherwise 0
Calculation: Sell volume fraction = (high − close) / (high − low) when high > low and volume > 0; otherwise 0
Calculation: Body-to-range ratio = abs(close − open) / (high − low) when high > low
Long: (high > low AND body-to-range ratio < configured maximum absorption body fraction AND volume > SMA(volume, configured lookback) × configured minimum-volume multiplier AND buy volume fraction > configured minimum dominant fraction AND close > EMA(close, configured lookback)) OR (previous chart bar met the absorption conditions in the first group AND close > open AND open < previous close AND close > previous open AND abs(close − open) > abs(previous close − previous open) AND close > EMA(close, configured lookback))
Short: (high > low AND body-to-range ratio < configured maximum absorption body fraction AND volume > SMA(volume, configured lookback) × configured minimum-volume multiplier AND sell volume fraction > configured minimum dominant fraction AND close < EMA(close, configured lookback)) OR (previous chart bar met the absorption conditions in the first group with sell volume fraction and close below EMA AND close < open AND open > previous close AND close < previous open AND abs(close − open) > abs(previous close − previous open) AND close < EMA(close, configured lookback))
