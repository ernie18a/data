Calculation: Raw stochastic %K = 100 × (close − lowest low over the lookback) / (highest high over the lookback − lowest low over the lookback)
Calculation: Current-timeframe %K = SMA(raw stochastic %K, K smoothing lookback); %D = SMA(%K, D smoothing lookback)
Calculation: Higher-timeframe %K/%D = linear regression of the corresponding smoothed %K/%D over the stochastic lookback, using the last completed bar of the next higher timeframe
Calculation: ATR stop distance = ATR × initial-stop multiple; initialize the long stop at entry price − stop distance and the short stop at entry price + stop distance. When favorable movement from entry reaches the breakeven R threshold, move the stop to entry price. At the trailing R threshold, update the long stop to the greater of its prior value and close − ATR × trailing multiple; update the short stop to the lesser of its prior value and close + ATR × trailing multiple.
Long: Higher-timeframe %K > 50 AND its prior value ≤ 50 AND current-timeframe %K > 50 AND current-timeframe %K > its prior value AND current-timeframe %K > %D AND higher-timeframe %K > %D AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR fast EMA > slow EMA)
Short: Higher-timeframe %D < 50 AND its prior value ≥ 50 AND current-timeframe %K < 50 AND current-timeframe %K < its prior value AND current-timeframe %K < %D AND higher-timeframe %K < %D AND (EMA trend filter disabled OR fast EMA < slow EMA)
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop level OR higher-timeframe %D crosses below the upper stochastic threshold OR (trend-flip exit enabled AND fast EMA < slow EMA) OR (time stop enabled AND bars since entry ≥ time limit)
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop level OR higher-timeframe %K crosses above the lower stochastic threshold OR (trend-flip exit enabled AND fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (time stop enabled AND bars since entry ≥ time limit)
