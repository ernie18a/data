Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot = a bar whose high is greater than the highs of the preceding left-lookback bars and at least the highs of the following right-lookahead bars, or whose low is less than the preceding lows and at most the following lows; retain alternating pivots, replacing the latest pivot with a more extreme pivot of the same side
Calculation: Swing retracement = (B − C) / (B − A), for the latest three pivots A, B, C
Calculation: Entry level = B − 0.618 × (B − A) when swing retracement ≥ 0.618; otherwise B − 0.382 × (B − A)
Calculation: Displaced average = the simple moving average of close, referenced from a configurable number of bars earlier
Calculation: Secondary swing retracement levels = B − 0.382 × (B − A2) and B − 0.618 × (B − A2), where A2 is the older pivot before A
Calculation: Fibonacci confluence = the midpoint of the closest pair among the primary and secondary 0.382 and 0.618 retracement levels, when their distance is no greater than a configurable ATR multiple
Calculation: Smoothed close = a configurable moving-average type applied to close
Long: A, B, C are low, high, low respectively AND B > A AND C < B AND swing retracement ≥ 0.382 AND swing retracement ≤ configurable maximum AND B − A ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND (displaced-average filter disabled OR close > displaced average) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close > smoothed close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ configurable minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > the previous completed bar's close from a higher timeframe) AND (confluence requirement disabled OR Fibonacci confluence exists)
Short: A, B, C are high, low, high respectively AND B < A AND C > B AND swing retracement ≥ 0.382 AND swing retracement ≤ configurable maximum AND A − B ≥ configurable ATR multiple AND (displaced-average filter disabled OR close < displaced average) AND (smoothing filter disabled OR close < smoothed close) AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ configurable minimum) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < the previous completed bar's close from a higher timeframe) AND (confluence requirement disabled OR Fibonacci confluence exists)
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR high ≥ XOP price OR an opposite short signal occurs while a long position is open
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR low ≤ XOP price OR an opposite long signal occurs while a short position is open
