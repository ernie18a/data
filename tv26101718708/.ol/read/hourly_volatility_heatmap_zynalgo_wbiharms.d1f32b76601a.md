Calculation: Hot-hour rank = Rank each hour by the average of (its mean chart-bar high-low range divided by the maximum such mean across hours) and (its share of completed days in which it contained that day's high or low, divided by the maximum such share across hours); use the mean of those two percentages. Use the rolling lookback of completed days, grouped by hour in the selected timezone.
Calculation: Bull bias = Percentage of tracked hourly candles in that hour slot that closed above their open, using the rolling lookback of completed days.
Calculation: Recent high = Highest high of the previous swing-lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: Recent low = Lowest low of the previous swing-lookback chart bars, excluding the current bar.
Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close over the RSI lookback.
Long: On a confirmed bar, current hour is among the top-ranked hours AND low <= recent low AND close > open AND (directional-bias filter disabled OR bull bias >= 50%) AND (RSI-divergence filter disabled OR current RSI > RSI on the previous swing-lookback bar with the lowest low) AND signal cooldown elapsed AND (trade mode off OR stability filter disabled or no trade open) AND (trade mode off OR alternating-signal filter disabled or last signal was not long)
Short: On a confirmed bar, current hour is among the top-ranked hours AND high >= recent high AND close < open AND (directional-bias filter disabled OR bull bias <= 50%) AND (RSI-divergence filter disabled OR current RSI < RSI on the previous swing-lookback bar with the highest high) AND signal cooldown elapsed AND (trade mode off OR stability filter disabled or no trade open) AND (trade mode off OR alternating-signal filter disabled or last signal was not short)
Calculation: Initial stop = Low of the long sweep bar or high of the short sweep bar.
Calculation: Target levels = Entry price plus (long) or minus (short) each configured target multiple times the absolute distance between entry price and initial stop; final target is the highest enabled target level.
Calculation: Trailing stop = Initial stop before target 1 is reached; entry price after target 1 and before target 2; target 1 price after target 2.
Exit: Long, On a confirmed bar, low <= trailing stop OR high >= final target OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, stop takes precedence.
Exit: Short, On a confirmed bar, high >= trailing stop OR low <= final target OR bars since entry >= maximum trade duration; if stop and target are both reached on the same bar, stop takes precedence.
