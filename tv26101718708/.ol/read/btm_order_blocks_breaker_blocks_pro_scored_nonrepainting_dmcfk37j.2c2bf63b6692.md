Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; use only after the bars on both sides have completed
Calculation: Bullish/bearish structure break = a completed bar closes above the latest unbroken confirmed swing high / below the latest unbroken confirmed swing low
Calculation: Bullish/bearish order-block zone = on a bullish/bearish structure break, find the lowest low / highest high since the broken swing, searching back no farther than the configured limit; use that extreme candle, or the nearest opposite-colour candle within the next three older bars, as the origin. The zone spans the origin candle's full wick range, extended to the move's extreme. Keep it only if price has not closed through its far side since the origin, its height is within the configured ATR limit, and it passes enabled quality filters: at least one move candle has body >= configured ATR multiple (displacement), a fair-value gap exists in the move, the move's extreme exceeds a prior confirmed swing low/high (liquidity sweep), origin volume >= configured multiple of its 20-bar average, and score >= configured minimum. Score one point for each of displacement, fair-value gap, liquidity sweep, and above-average volume. Skip a zone overlapping an active same-direction order block when overlap filtering is enabled
Calculation: Breaker zone = an active bullish order block after a bar closes below its bottom, or an active bearish order block after a bar closes above its top; retain the same zone and reverse its direction. Require the breaking bar's body to meet the configured ATR displacement threshold if enabled
Calculation: Zone retest = for a support zone, current low <= zone top and prior low > zone top; for a resistance zone, current high >= zone bottom and prior high < zone bottom
Long: bullish structure break OR bullish order-block zone created OR bullish breaker zone created OR bullish order-block zone retested OR bullish breaker zone retested
Short: bearish structure break OR bearish order-block zone created OR bearish breaker zone created OR bearish order-block zone retested OR bearish breaker zone retested
