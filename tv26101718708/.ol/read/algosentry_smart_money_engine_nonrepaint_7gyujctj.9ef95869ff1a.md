Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a bar’s high that is higher than the highs of the same parameter-length number of bars before and after it; it becomes available only after those following bars close. Confirmed swing low = the inverse. Track the latest and preceding confirmed swing highs and lows.
Long: A newly confirmed swing high is higher than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low is higher than the preceding confirmed swing low OR the confirmed close is above the latest confirmed swing high that has not already been broken (once per swing) OR the low is below the latest confirmed swing low and the close is above it (once per swing) OR the low is above the high two bars earlier OR a bullish break occurs and at least one of the preceding parameter-limited bars is bearish. For breaks and sweeps, if EMA alignment is required, close > EMA.
Short: A newly confirmed swing high is lower than the preceding confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low is lower than the preceding confirmed swing low OR the confirmed close is below the latest confirmed swing low that has not already been broken (once per swing) OR the high is above the latest confirmed swing high and the close is below it (once per swing) OR the high is below the low two bars earlier OR a bearish break occurs and at least one of the preceding parameter-limited bars is bullish. For breaks and sweeps, if EMA alignment is required, close < EMA.
