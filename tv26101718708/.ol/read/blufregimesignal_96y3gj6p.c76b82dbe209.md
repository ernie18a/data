Calculation: Session VWAP = volume-weighted average of the typical price (high + low + close)/3, reset each day.
Calculation: Kaufman efficiency ratio = absolute change in close over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute bar-to-bar close changes over that lookback.
Calculation: Regime state = RANGE when efficiency ratio falls below the range-entry threshold; while RANGE, leave it when the ratio reaches the range-exit threshold, becoming TREND if it exceeds the trend-entry threshold and otherwise CAUTION. TREND becomes RANGE if the ratio falls below the range-entry threshold when it reaches the trend-exit threshold; otherwise it becomes CAUTION. While CAUTION, enter RANGE below the range-entry threshold or TREND above the trend-entry threshold.
Calculation: Directional confluence tier = score one point each for efficiency ratio at or above the trend-entry threshold, fast HMA above (bullish) or below (bearish) the slower HMA, RSI at or above (bullish) or at or below (bearish) its thrust threshold, MACD histogram positive and rising for two consecutive bars (bullish) or negative and falling for two consecutive bars (bearish), and close above (bullish) or below (bearish) session VWAP. PRIME requires all five points and the corresponding MACD acceleration; STRONG requires at least four points; WEAK requires at least three; otherwise NONE.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe HMA = HMA of higher-timeframe closes, using the still-forming higher-timeframe bar; higher-timeframe bullish means chart close > HMA and bearish means chart close < HMA.
Calculation: Bull flip stop = the greater of the prior bull flip stop and current close minus ATR times the outermost (largest) flip-stop multiple; bear flip stop = the lesser of the prior bear flip stop and current close plus ATR times the outermost (largest) flip-stop multiple. Initialize flip state as BULL; on a flip, rearm the opposing stop at its current candidate. The four flip-stop multiples increase from tightest to outermost.
Long: (Regime state has just entered TREND AND cooldown since any prior shift has elapsed AND close > session VWAP AND bullish confluence tier meets the configured minimum tier and any prime-only requirement AND same-direction lockout has elapsed AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR aligned mode and higher-timeframe bullish OR reversal mode and higher-timeframe bearish)) OR (flip state is BEAR AND close > prior bear flip stop).
Short: (Regime state has just entered TREND AND cooldown since any prior shift has elapsed AND close <= session VWAP AND bearish confluence tier meets the configured minimum tier and any prime-only requirement AND same-direction lockout has elapsed AND (higher-timeframe filter is off OR aligned mode and higher-timeframe bearish OR reversal mode and higher-timeframe bullish)) OR (flip state is BULL AND close < prior bull flip stop).
