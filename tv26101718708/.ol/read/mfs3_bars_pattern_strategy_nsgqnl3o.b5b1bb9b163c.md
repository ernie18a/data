Calculation: Candle body = absolute value of close − open
Calculation: Medium average body = simple moving average of candle body using a medium lookback
Calculation: Long, medium, and short SMA = simple moving averages of close using successively shorter lookbacks
Calculation: Ignite low = low of the Ignite bar in the pattern
Calculation: Long target = signal bar open + 2 × (signal bar open − Ignite low)
Long: current candle body ≥ 3 × medium average body AND close > open AND prior candle is a pullback AND prior candle body < Ignite candle body ÷ 3 AND prior candle high > Ignite close AND prior candle low < Ignite close AND current candle body > Ignite candle body ÷ 3 AND current open > prior close AND Ignite candle close > Ignite open AND Ignite candle body ≥ 3 × medium average body at the Ignite bar AND long SMA > medium SMA > short SMA at the Ignite bar AND Ignite candle close < short SMA at the Ignite bar AND position is flat
Exit: long, low ≤ Ignite low OR high ≥ Long target
