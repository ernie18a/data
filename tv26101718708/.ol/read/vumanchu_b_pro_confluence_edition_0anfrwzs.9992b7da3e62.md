Calculation: WaveTrend = ESA = EMA(source, channel length); deviation = EMA(abs(source − ESA), channel length); CI = (source − ESA)/(0.015 × deviation); WT1 = EMA(CI, average length); WT2 = SMA(WT1, smoothing length). Use WT2 for zones and divergences; WT1 and WT2 for crosses.
Calculation: WT divergence = compare confirmed WT2 pivot fractals (center value exceeds both two preceding and two following values for a top; is below all four for a bottom) with the previous qualifying pivot: regular bearish = price higher high and WT2 lower high; regular bullish = price lower low and WT2 higher low; hidden bearish = price lower high and WT2 higher high; hidden bullish = price higher low and WT2 lower low. Qualifying tops require WT2 ≥ the bearish threshold and bottoms WT2 ≤ the bullish threshold. Use regular and/or hidden divergences as enabled.
Calculation: RSI divergence = the same pivot and regular/hidden divergence comparisons, using RSI for the oscillator; qualifying tops require RSI ≥ the bearish threshold and bottoms RSI ≤ the bullish threshold.
Calculation: MFI area = SMA(((close − open)/(high − low)) × multiplier, lookback) − vertical offset.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = K = SMA(Stochastic(RSI(source), RSI(source), RSI(source), lookback), K smoothing); D = SMA(K, D smoothing). Apply the configured logarithmic transform to source before RSI when enabled.
Calculation: Buy score = points for WT1 crossing above WT2 while WT2 ≤ oversold level + bullish WT divergence + bullish RSI divergence + MFI area > 0 + K crossing above D while K < 30 + (when trend filter enabled) uptrend. Sell score = points for WT1 crossing below WT2 while WT2 ≥ overbought level + bearish WT divergence + bearish RSI divergence + MFI area < 0 + K crossing below D while K > 70 + (when trend filter enabled) downtrend. Each criterion contributes its configured points.
Calculation: Uptrend = close > slow EMA and fast EMA > slow EMA. Downtrend = close < slow EMA and fast EMA < slow EMA; fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback.
Long: Buy score ≥ minimum score AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ minimum and ≤ maximum) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > SMA(volume, lookback) × multiplier) AND (ATR filter disabled OR abs(close − open) ≥ ATR × multiplier) AND NOT (trend filter enabled AND downtrend AND ADX > maximum) AND no buy signal within the configured cooldown bars. In classic mode, replace the score and filters with WT1 crossing above WT2 while WT2 ≤ oversold level.
Long: When classic markings are enabled, WT1 crosses above WT2 while WT2 ≤ oversold level.
