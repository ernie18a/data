Calculation: Cumulative delta = cumulative sum of volume when close ≥ open, otherwise negative volume
Calculation: Volatility ratio = ATR divided by its longer-lookback simple moving average; LOW if below the lower threshold, HIGH if above the upper threshold, otherwise MEDIUM
Calculation: Trend score = 100 × (0.40 × min(|RSI change over its lookback| / scale, 1) + 0.35 × min(max(volume / its simple moving average − 1, 0) / scale, 1) + 0.25 × min(max(ATR / its shorter-lookback simple moving average − 1, 0) / scale, 1)); the ATR average lookback is shorter than the volatility-ratio average lookback
Calculation: Pressure score = 100 × (0.40 × |bar delta / volume| + 0.35 × min(max(volume / its simple moving average − 1, 0) / scale, 1) + 0.25 × min(|close − open| / (high − low), 1))
Calculation: ATR trail = close minus (ATR × active regime multiplier) while bullish, or close plus (ATR × active regime multiplier) while bearish; trail levels ratchet only toward price, and direction flips bullish when close crosses above the prior bearish trail or bearish when close crosses below the prior bullish trail
Calculation: Trail-engine long target = entry price + ATR × target multiplier, boosted when trend score is strong; short target = entry price − ATR × target multiplier, with the same boost; stop is the ATR trail at entry
Calculation: Pressure-engine long/short targets and stops = entry price adjusted by the respective configurable target/stop percentages in the position direction
Long: On a confirmed bar while flat and cooldown has elapsed, the selected engine mode enables the trend engine and its regime gate is armed (HIGH or MEDIUM in Regime Arms mode), and close crosses above the prior bearish ATR trail, and trend score ≥ its minimum; OR the selected mode enables the pressure engine and its regime gate is armed (LOW or MEDIUM in Regime Arms mode), and cumulative delta crosses above its simple moving average, and pressure score ≥ its minimum, and RSI < its overbought threshold, and close ≥ open; in Confluence Required mode both engines’ long conditions must hold on the same bar
Short: On a confirmed bar while flat and cooldown has elapsed, the selected engine mode enables the trend engine and its regime gate is armed (HIGH or MEDIUM in Regime Arms mode), and close crosses below the prior bullish ATR trail, and trend score ≥ its minimum; OR the selected mode enables the pressure engine and its regime gate is armed (LOW or MEDIUM in Regime Arms mode), and cumulative delta crosses below its simple moving average, and pressure score ≥ its minimum, and RSI > its oversold threshold, and close < open; in Confluence Required mode both engines’ short conditions must hold on the same bar
Exit: long, high ≥ target OR low ≤ stop OR bars since entry ≥ maximum duration OR (trail-flip exit enabled AND close crosses below the prior bullish ATR trail)
Exit: short, low ≤ target OR high ≥ stop OR bars since entry ≥ maximum duration OR (trail-flip exit enabled AND close crosses above the prior bearish ATR trail)
