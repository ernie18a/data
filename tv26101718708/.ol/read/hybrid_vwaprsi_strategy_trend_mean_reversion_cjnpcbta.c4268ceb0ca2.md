Calculation: VWAP = cumulative (midpoint of high and low × volume) / cumulative volume since the session reset
Calculation: Market trend = higher-timeframe, still-forming close > its longer-term simple moving average
Long: Market trend AND close < VWAP AND RSI < oversold threshold AND ATR / close > volatility threshold
Short: NOT Market trend AND close > VWAP AND RSI > overbought threshold AND ATR / close > volatility threshold
Exit: Long, after price rises from entry by an ATR-based activation distance, low <= highest high since activation minus an ATR-based trailing offset
Exit: Short, after price falls from entry by an ATR-based activation distance, high >= lowest low since activation plus an ATR-based trailing offset
