Calculation: EMA trend = Bullish when the short-term EMA is above the long-term EMA and the timeframe close is above the short-term EMA; bearish when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise neutral.
Calculation: Market structure = Bullish when the latest two confirmed swing highs and latest two confirmed swing lows are each higher than their predecessors; bearish when both are lower; otherwise neutral. Confirm pivots using the configured pivot lookback.
Calculation: Momentum slope = Change in linear-regression value over the configured lookback, divided by ATR over that lookback; classify positive values above its configured positive threshold as bullish, negative values below its configured negative threshold as bearish, otherwise neutral.
Calculation: MTF agreement = Across the four configured timeframes and, if enabled and not duplicative, the chart timeframe, count bullish and bearish votes from each enabled method (one vote per method per timeframe). Dominant side is the side with more votes; agreement is the dominant side's votes divided by all enabled method slots. Compute each method on its own timeframe. For timeframes higher than the chart, use the request.security value with lookahead off: the bar still forming in realtime, and the last available bar historically. For timeframes lower than the chart, use the request.security value with lookahead off: the available intrabar value, which can update while the chart bar is forming.
Long: (dominant side is bullish AND agreement crosses from below to at/above the configured strong threshold) OR (dominant side is bullish AND differs from the prior bar's dominant side).
Short: (dominant side is bearish AND agreement crosses from below to at/above the configured strong threshold) OR (dominant side is bearish AND differs from the prior bar's dominant side).
