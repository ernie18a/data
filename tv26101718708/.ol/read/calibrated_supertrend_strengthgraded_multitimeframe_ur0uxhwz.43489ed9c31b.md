Calculation: Primary Supertrend = Build a trailing lower and upper band around the selected price source using ATR and an adaptive multiplier; carry each band forward unless its current basic band tightens it or the prior close crosses the prior band. Direction is up until close falls below the prior lower band, then down until close rises above the prior upper band. The ATR length may adapt to the measured dominant cycle; the multiplier may scale with ATR’s historical rank, trend efficiency, and recent return-path volatility.
Calculation: Confirmed flip = A primary Supertrend direction change whose close breaches the prior opposite band by at least the configured ATR threshold, after the configured minimum bars since the preceding raw flip, and on a confirmed bar close.
Calculation: Flip strength = A 0–100 weighted score of breach depth, ADX, trend efficiency, higher-timeframe agreement, regime alignment, volume thrust, volume delta, and optional secondary Supertrend agreement.
Calculation: Conviction = A 0–100 weighted score of regime alignment, higher-timeframe agreement, distance beyond the primary Supertrend band, trend efficiency, volume delta, and optional cross-asset momentum.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe Supertrend directions = Supertrend directions from three timeframes higher than the chart timeframe, each read from its last completed bar.
Calculation: Secondary Supertrend = A second, slower Supertrend, using a longer ATR lookback and a wider ATR multiplier than the primary Supertrend.
Long: Primary Supertrend direction changes from down to up (raw flip) OR a confirmed flip is up AND conviction meets its threshold AND flip strength meets its threshold AND no active veto (volatility-index spike, all three higher-timeframe Supertrends opposing, Range or Volatile regime, or opposing cross-asset momentum) OR secondary Supertrend direction changes from down to up OR primary Supertrend direction is up AND close is above its line.
Short: Primary Supertrend direction changes from up to down (raw flip) OR a confirmed flip is down AND conviction meets its threshold AND flip strength meets its threshold AND no active veto (volatility-index spike, all three higher-timeframe Supertrends opposing, Range or Volatile regime, or opposing cross-asset momentum) OR secondary Supertrend direction changes from up to down OR primary Supertrend direction is down AND close is below its line.
Exit: long, close < primary Supertrend line
Exit: short, close > primary Supertrend line
