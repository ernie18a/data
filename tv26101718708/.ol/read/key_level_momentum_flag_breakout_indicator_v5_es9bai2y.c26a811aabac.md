Calculation: Premarket high/low = highest high and lowest low, respectively, of bars in the current configured premarket session
Calculation: Yesterday high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar (daily timeframe; higher than the chart timeframe; last completed bar)
Calculation: Recent bull key break = a close crossed above the premarket high or yesterday high within the configured setup lookback
Calculation: Recent bear key break = a close crossed below the premarket low or yesterday low within the configured setup lookback
Calculation: Near bull key = close is within configured ATR distance of premarket high or yesterday high, or close is above either level, or close is above VWAP
Calculation: Near bear key = close is within configured ATR distance of premarket low or yesterday low, or close is below either level, or close is below VWAP
Calculation: Bull flag breakout = close is in the configured signal window AND recent bull key break AND near bull key AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > close from the configured impulse lookback by at least the configured ATR distance AND close > open AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram > its prior value AND volume > its moving average times the configured breakout multiplier AND lowest low over the configured flag lookback (including current bar) > slow EMA AND close > VWAP AND close > highest high of the configured flag lookback excluding current bar
Calculation: Bear flag breakdown = close is in the configured signal window AND recent bear key break AND near bear key AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < close from the configured impulse lookback by at least the configured ATR distance AND close < open AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram < its prior value AND volume > its moving average times the configured breakout multiplier AND highest high over the configured flag lookback (including current bar) < slow EMA AND close < VWAP AND close < lowest low of the configured flag lookback excluding current bar
Calculation: Bull continuation breakout = close is in the configured signal window AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND near bull key AND within the configured pullback lookback a low came within the configured ATR distance of fast EMA, slow EMA, or VWAP AND close > open AND close > fast EMA AND low <= fast EMA AND close > prior close AND close > highest high of the configured microstructure lookback excluding current bar AND close > open AND MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram >= its prior value AND volume >= its moving average times the configured continuation multiplier
Calculation: Bear continuation breakdown = close is in the configured signal window AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND near bear key AND within the configured pullback lookback a high came within the configured ATR distance of fast EMA, slow EMA, or VWAP AND close < open AND close < fast EMA AND high >= fast EMA AND close < prior close AND close < lowest low of the configured microstructure lookback excluding current bar AND close < open AND MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram <= its prior value AND volume >= its moving average times the configured continuation multiplier
Calculation: Bull momentum ignition = close is in the configured signal window AND close > VWAP AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > highest high of the configured ignition lookback excluding current bar AND close > open AND absolute candle body >= ATR times the configured body multiplier AND volume >= its moving average times the configured continuation multiplier AND MACD histogram > its prior value
Calculation: Bear momentum ignition = close is in the configured signal window AND close < VWAP AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < lowest low of the configured ignition lookback excluding current bar AND close < open AND absolute candle body >= ATR times the configured body multiplier AND volume >= its moving average times the configured continuation multiplier AND MACD histogram < its prior value
Long: position is not long AND (bull flag breakout OR bull continuation breakout OR bull momentum ignition)
Short: position is not short AND (bear flag breakdown OR bear continuation breakdown OR bear momentum ignition)
Exit: long, close < fast EMA OR close < VWAP OR MACD histogram > 0 AND MACD histogram < its prior value OR short signal
Exit: short, close > fast EMA OR close > VWAP OR MACD histogram < 0 AND MACD histogram > its prior value OR long signal
