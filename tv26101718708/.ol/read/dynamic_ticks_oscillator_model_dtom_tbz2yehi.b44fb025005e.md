Calculation: Down Ticks = daily close of NYSE Down Ticks (higher timeframe than the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming)
Calculation: Down Ticks ROC = percentage change in Down Ticks over a lookback
Calculation: ROC volatility = standard deviation of Down Ticks ROC over a longer lookback
Long: Down Ticks ROC < −ROC volatility × entry multiplier
Exit: Long, Down Ticks ROC > ROC volatility × exit multiplier
