Calculation: RSI bearish divergence = highest high over the recent lookback > highest high over the equally long preceding window AND highest RSI over the recent lookback < highest RSI over the preceding window
Calculation: Smoothed volume delta = 5-period EMA of (2 × estimated buy volume − volume) / (volume + epsilon), where estimated buy volume is volume × (0.5 + 0.5 × abs(close − open) / (high − low + epsilon)) on up bars, and volume × (0.5 − 0.5 × abs(close − open) / (high − low + epsilon)) otherwise
Short: Not in a trade AND ((Tier 1 signal is true now and was false on the prior chart bar) OR (Tier 2 signal is true now and was false on the prior chart bar)), where Tier 1 = daily OBV < configured maximum AND chart-timeframe OBV < its value N chart bars ago AND 1H OBV < its value N 1H bars ago AND RSI ≥ configured minimum AND (RSI bearish divergence OR RSI < its same-length SMA) AND MFI ≥ Tier 1 threshold AND MFI < its value N bars ago AND (delta filter disabled OR smoothed volume delta < configured threshold); Tier 2 = daily OBV < configured maximum AND chart-timeframe OBV < its value N chart bars ago AND RSI ≥ configured minimum AND MFI ≥ Tier 2 threshold AND MFI < its value N bars ago AND Tier 1 is false. Use the last completed 1H bar (lower timeframe) for both 1H OBV values and the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe) for daily OBV; other values use chart-timeframe bars.
Exit: Short, high ≥ signal-bar high + ATR × configured stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − configured final take-profit fraction)
