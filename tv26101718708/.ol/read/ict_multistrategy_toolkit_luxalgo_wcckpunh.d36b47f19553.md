Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the maximum/minimum over the pivot lookback before and after it; it becomes available after the bars after it complete
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = current high above an unswept confirmed pivot high, or current low below an unswept confirmed pivot low; record the sweep bar’s high/low and pivot
Calculation: Market structure shift = after a low sweep, a bar closes above a confirmed pivot high while the previous close was at or below it; after a high sweep, a bar closes below a confirmed pivot low while the previous close was at or above it
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low above the high two bars earlier, with the middle bar closing above its open; zone is [high two bars earlier, current low]. Bearish FVG = current high below the low two bars earlier, with the middle bar closing below its open; zone is [current high, low two bars earlier]. A bullish FVG is unmitigated until a bar’s low falls below its lower boundary; a bearish FVG is unmitigated until a bar’s high rises above its upper boundary
Calculation: OTE zone = the area between the 62% and 79% retracements of the impulse range; for a bullish shift, range from the lowest low over the lookback through the shift bar’s high; for a bearish shift, range from the highest high over the lookback through the shift bar’s low
Calculation: Stop price = sweep-bar low for bullish sweep-and-shift entries and bullish Judas Swing; sweep-bar high for bearish sweep-and-shift entries and bearish Judas Swing; OTE impulse low for long OTE and impulse high for short OTE; sweep-bar low for long Turtle Soup and sweep-bar high for short Turtle Soup
Calculation: Profit target = entry price plus (entry price minus stop price) times the risk-reward multiplier for longs; entry price minus (stop price minus entry price) times the multiplier for shorts
Long: (a confirmed bar’s low < an unswept pivot low and close > that pivot low and pivot age > twice the pivot lookback and close != low) OR (a low sweep is followed by a bullish market structure shift and a confirmed bar touches the upper boundary of an unmitigated bullish FVG formed on or after the sweep bar and (the bar is in a configured Silver Bullet session or the 2022 model is enabled) and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active)) OR (daily close > daily open and a low sweep occurs during the early portion of the configured Judas Swing session and a bullish market structure shift occurs while waiting for that sweep and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active) and close != sweep-bar low) OR (a bullish market structure shift is followed by a confirmed bar intersecting the OTE zone and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active) and close != impulse low)
Short: (a confirmed bar’s high > an unswept pivot high and close < that pivot high and pivot age > twice the pivot lookback and close != high) OR (a high sweep is followed by a bearish market structure shift and a confirmed bar touches the lower boundary of an unmitigated bearish FVG formed on or after the sweep bar and (the bar is in a configured Silver Bullet session or the 2022 model is enabled) and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active)) OR (daily close < daily open and a high sweep occurs during the early portion of the configured Judas Swing session and a bearish market structure shift occurs while waiting for that sweep and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active) and close != sweep-bar high) OR (a bearish market structure shift is followed by a confirmed bar intersecting the OTE zone and (overlap prevention is disabled or no trade is active) and close != impulse high)
Exit: long, low <= stop price OR (low > stop price AND high >= profit target)
Exit: short, high >= stop price OR (high < stop price AND low <= profit target)
