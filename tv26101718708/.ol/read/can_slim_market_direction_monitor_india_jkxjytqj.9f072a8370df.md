Calculation: Rally-attempt day count = While close is below the 50-bar simple moving average or below 95% of the highest high over 50 bars, track the lowest low and its bar; reset the count to 0 whenever a lower low occurs. After that low, set the count to 1 on the first bar whose close exceeds the prior close, then increment it by 1 each bar while no lower low occurs. Reset the count and clear the tracked low after the count exceeds 18.
Calculation: Weighted distribution-day count = Sum weights of distribution days and stalling days from the prior 25 bars: a distribution day has close at least 0.2% below the prior close and volume above prior-bar volume (weight 1); a stalling day has a positive close change below 0.2%, volume above prior-bar volume, and close below the bar midpoint (weight 0.5). Remove a day when more than 25 bars have elapsed since it, or a later high reaches 5% above its low.
Long: rally-attempt day count is 4–13 AND close >= prior close * 1.015 AND volume > prior-bar volume
Exit: long, weighted distribution-day count >= red threshold OR (close < 200-bar simple moving average of close AND that moving average <= its value 20 bars earlier)
