Calculation: Bollinger basis = selected moving average of close over the shorter lookback
Calculation: Upper band = Bollinger basis + standard deviation of close over the shorter lookback × band multiplier
Calculation: Lower band = Bollinger basis − standard deviation of close over the shorter lookback × band multiplier
Calculation: Trend SMA = simple moving average of close over a longer lookback than the Bollinger basis
Long: close > upper band AND close > trend SMA
Short: close < lower band AND close < trend SMA
Exit: Long, minimum holding bars elapsed AND (high ≥ take-profit price OR low ≤ stop-loss price)
Exit: Short, minimum holding bars elapsed AND (low ≤ take-profit price OR high ≥ stop-loss price)
