Calculation: Rolling value area high/low = over a rolling lookback that includes the current chart bar, divide the highest-high to lowest-low range into price bins; assign each bar’s full volume to the bin containing its typical price (high + low + close) / 3; sum volume per bin; start at the highest-volume bin (ties choose the lowest bin), then expand one adjacent bin at a time toward the side with more volume (ties choose the lower side) until the included volume reaches the target fraction of total volume. Value Area High is the upper edge of the highest included bin; Value Area Low is the lower edge of the lowest included bin. If the range is zero, use the first bin.
Long: (close > current Value Area High AND previous close <= previous Value Area High AND volume >= simple moving average of volume over a lookback × multiplier) OR (close > current Value Area Low AND previous close <= previous Value Area Low)
Short: (close < current Value Area High AND previous close >= previous Value Area High) OR (close < current Value Area Low AND previous close >= previous Value Area Low AND volume >= simple moving average of volume over a lookback × multiplier)
