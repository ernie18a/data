Calculation: Long score = trend score + momentum score + efficiency score + volatility score + participation score; trend score is 10 points if close is above the slow EMA plus 10 if the short-lookback EMA is above the slow EMA; momentum score scales linearly from 0 at RSI 50 to 20 at RSI 70, clamped to 0–20; efficiency score is 0 unless the net close change over the lookback is positive, otherwise 20 times the efficiency ratio divided by its full-points threshold, clamped to 0–20; efficiency ratio = absolute net close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute close-to-close changes over that lookback; volatility score is 20 between the configured lower and upper ATR percentile-rank bounds, tapers linearly to 0 below the lower bound or above the upper bound; participation score is 10 if volume data is unavailable, otherwise 20 times (current volume / its moving average − 0.5), clamped to 0–20, when close > open, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Short score = trend score + momentum score + efficiency score + volatility score + participation score; trend score is 10 points if close is below the slow EMA plus 10 if the short-lookback EMA is below the slow EMA; momentum score scales linearly from 0 at RSI 50 to 20 at RSI 30, clamped to 0–20; efficiency score is 0 unless the net close change over the lookback is negative, otherwise 20 times the efficiency ratio divided by its full-points threshold, clamped to 0–20; volatility score is the same as for the long score; participation score is 10 if volume data is unavailable, otherwise 20 times (current volume / its moving average − 0.5), clamped to 0–20, when close < open, and 0 otherwise.
Calculation: Trailing stop = at entry, signal-bar close minus (ATR × stop multiple) for a long or plus (ATR × stop multiple) for a short; thereafter, update each bar to the greater of the prior stop and close minus (ATR × stop multiple) for a long, or the lesser of the prior stop and close plus (ATR × stop multiple) for a short.
Long: enough history to calculate the slow EMA and ATR percentile rank AND longs enabled AND flat AND current long score >= entry threshold AND previous-bar long score < entry threshold.
Short: enough history to calculate the slow EMA and ATR percentile rank AND shorts enabled AND flat AND current short score >= entry threshold AND previous-bar short score < entry threshold AND no long entry signal on this bar.
Exit: long, long score < exit threshold OR low <= trailing stop.
Exit: short, short score < exit threshold OR high >= trailing stop.
