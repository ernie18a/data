Calculation: Trend score = weighted sum of (the count of adjacent EMAs in bullish order minus the count in bearish order), (higher-high and higher-low indicators minus lower-high and lower-low indicators), and a directional DMI/ADX score; clamp to the score range.
Calculation: Momentum score = weighted sum of the smoothed RSI’s distance from its midpoint and MACD histogram normalized by ATR; clamp to the score range.
Calculation: Volatility score = directional candle score when Bollinger Band width is expanding, ATR is in its healthy range, and candle body exceeds its configured share of the bar range, plus close’s multi-bar change normalized by ATR; clamp to the score range.
Calculation: Final score = weighted average of Trend, Momentum, and Volatility scores, adjusted by higher-timeframe alignment bonuses or penalties, then clamped to the score range. Axis weights adapt from each axis’s prior directional outcomes and the current volatility regime.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe verdict = bullish when the faster EMA exceeds the slower EMA and RSI exceeds its midpoint; bearish when both comparisons are reversed; otherwise neutral. Use the last completed bars of two higher timeframes.
Calculation: Consensus = among enabled axes, count scores beyond the configured positive and negative lean thresholds; the majority leaning direction is agreed. Ignore neutral axes. Consensus is valid when the range of leaning scores is within the configured tolerance.
Calculation: Active threshold = selected from an adaptive set of thresholds spanning the configured preset range; threshold selection favors thresholds with better prior directional outcomes, with configured exploration.
Long: Strong bullish consensus is valid AND the Trend score leans bullish AND the Final score is at least the Active threshold AND the previous signal is not bullish AND no position is open AND absolute Final score meets the confidence floor AND the drawdown halt is inactive.
Short: Strong bearish consensus is valid AND the Trend score leans bearish AND the Final score is at most the negative Active threshold AND the previous signal is not bearish AND no position is open AND absolute Final score meets the confidence floor AND the drawdown halt is inactive.
Exit: long, price reaches the initial stop below entry by a configured ATR multiple OR reaches an enabled take-profit level above entry, with targets spaced by configured risk/reward multiples; after the first target, move the stop to entry when enabled, and while Momentum agrees with the long side, raise the stop to the greater of its prior level and close minus a configured ATR multiple.
Exit: short, price reaches the initial stop above entry by a configured ATR multiple OR reaches an enabled take-profit level below entry, with targets spaced by configured risk/reward multiples; after the first target, move the stop to entry when enabled, and while Momentum agrees with the short side, lower the stop to the lesser of its prior level and close plus a configured ATR multiple.
