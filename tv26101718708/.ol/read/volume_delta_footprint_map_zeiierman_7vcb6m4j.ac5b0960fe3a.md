Calculation: Lower-timeframe candle delta = candle volume signed positive when close > open and negative when close < open; if equal, use the sign of close versus the previous lower-timeframe close, or zero if equal or unavailable. Use volume 1 when volume is zero. If no lower-timeframe candles are available for a chart bar, apply the same rule to that chart bar.
Calculation: Price stripes = divide the range from the lowest low to highest high in the lookback chart bars, expanded above and below by a parameterized fraction of average ATR, into a parameterized number of equal price levels. Allocate each candle's delta and volume equally across the levels intersecting its high-low range.
Calculation: Persistent delta and volume per stripe = current stripe delta and volume plus the prior value multiplied by an exponential decay whose half-life is a parameter. Process chart bars oldest to newest.
Calculation: Stripe strength = 0.70 × (absolute persistent delta divided by the largest absolute persistent delta among stripes in that chart bar) + 0.30 × (absolute persistent delta divided by persistent volume).
Long: persistent volume > 0 AND stripe strength >= minimum stripe strength AND persistent delta >= 0 AND (price-touch gaps are disabled OR the chart bar's high-low does not intersect the stripe level)
Short: persistent volume > 0 AND stripe strength >= minimum stripe strength AND persistent delta < 0 AND (price-touch gaps are disabled OR the chart bar's high-low does not intersect the stripe level)
