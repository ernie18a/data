Calculation: Log-price RSI change = current RSI of the natural logarithm of the bar's high, low, or close minus its previous value; use the corresponding series for each signal.
Calculation: Modified MACD = change in RSI(100) of the difference between short- and long-lookback moving averages of the selected price series; signal line = change in RSI(100) of a moving average of the modified MACD; midpoint = average of the modified MACD and signal line.
Long: Log-price RSI change of close crosses above 14 OR log-price RSI change of close crosses above 10 OR log-price RSI change of low crosses above 20 OR modified MACD, signal line, or midpoint crosses above 2, 3, 4, or 5; AND close is above the open of every selected timeframe's current, still-forming bar (each timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe).
Short: Log-price RSI change of low crosses below -20 OR log-price RSI change of close crosses below -14 OR log-price RSI change of close crosses below -10 OR modified MACD, signal line, or midpoint crosses below -2, -3, -4, or -5; AND close is below the open of every selected timeframe's current, still-forming bar (each timeframe may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe).
