Calculation: Bull score = 1 each for (fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA), (latest confirmed swing high > prior confirmed swing high OR latest confirmed swing low > prior confirmed swing low), (50 < RSI < overbought threshold), (MACD line > signal line AND MACD line > 0), (MACD line crosses above signal line), (smoothed stochastic K > D AND K < 80), (high volume AND close > open: 3 points), (bullish engulfing OR bullish hammer/pin bar OR bullish inside-bar breakout OR doji after a down candle), (close above Supertrend: 2 points), (bar overlaps the latest bullish order-block zone: 2 points), and (14-bar lowest low < 28-bar lowest low AND 14-bar lowest RSI > 28-bar lowest RSI: 3 points); otherwise 0 for each component.
Calculation: Bear score = 1 each for (fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA), (latest confirmed swing high < prior confirmed swing high OR latest confirmed swing low < prior confirmed swing low), (oversold threshold < RSI < 50), (MACD line < signal line AND MACD line < 0), (MACD line crosses below signal line), (smoothed stochastic K < D AND K > 20), (high volume AND close < open: 3 points), (bearish engulfing OR bearish shooting star/pin bar OR bearish inside-bar breakout OR doji after an up candle), (close below Supertrend: 2 points), (bar overlaps the latest bearish order-block zone: 2 points), and (14-bar highest high > 28-bar highest high AND 14-bar highest RSI < 28-bar highest RSI AND fast EMA < medium EMA: 3 points); otherwise 0 for each component.
Calculation: Bullish order-block zone = high and low of the bar immediately before the latest bar whose close is more than 1% above the previous close.
Calculation: Bearish order-block zone = high and low of the bar immediately before the latest bar whose close is more than 1% below the previous close.
Calculation: Session filter = within the configured trading session AND outside the configured opening and closing exclusion windows.
Calculation: Volatility filter = ATR as a percentage of close is below its configured maximum.
Calculation: Risk/reward filter = configured ATR-multiple take-profit distance divided by configured ATR-multiple stop-loss distance is at least the configured minimum risk/reward ratio.
Long: bull score >= 8 AND session filter AND volatility filter AND risk/reward filter.
Long: 14-bar lowest low < 28-bar lowest low AND 14-bar lowest RSI > 28-bar lowest RSI.
Short: bear score >= 8 AND session filter AND volatility filter AND risk/reward filter.
Short: 14-bar highest high > 28-bar highest high AND 14-bar highest RSI < 28-bar highest RSI AND fast EMA < medium EMA.
Exit: long, low <= current close - ATR × configured stop multiple OR high >= current close + ATR × configured target multiple OR trailing stop is hit OR fast EMA < medium EMA < slow EMA.
Exit: short, high >= current close + ATR × configured stop multiple OR low <= current close - ATR × configured target multiple OR trailing stop is hit OR fast EMA > medium EMA > slow EMA.
