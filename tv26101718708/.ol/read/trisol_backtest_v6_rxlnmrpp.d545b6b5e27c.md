Calculation: Short HMA = Hull moving average of chart-timeframe close with a shorter lookback than the long HMA
Calculation: Long HMA = Hull moving average of chart-timeframe close with a longer lookback than the short HMA
Calculation: Short RSI average = short-lookback simple moving average of RSI
Calculation: Long RSI average = longer-lookback weighted moving average of RSI
Calculation: Latest confirmed swing low = most recent pivot low, confirmed only after the required bars to its right are complete
Calculation: Prior-high target = highest high over the lookback of completed chart-timeframe bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Lower-timeframe percentage change = 100 × (latest completed lower-timeframe close − preceding completed lower-timeframe close) / preceding completed lower-timeframe close
Long: Close is between the short and long HMAs, inclusive AND MACD histogram < 0 AND RSI crosses above the long RSI average AND short RSI average crosses above the long RSI average
Long: Lower-timeframe percentage change ≤ −re-entry threshold AND a partial exit has occurred in the current position AND re-entry has not occurred since that partial exit
Exit: long, low ≤ latest confirmed swing low OR high ≥ prior-high target OR lower-timeframe percentage change ≥ partial-exit threshold AND no partial exit has occurred in the current position
