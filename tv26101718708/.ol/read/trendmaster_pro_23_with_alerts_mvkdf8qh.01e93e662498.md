Long: short-term moving average crosses above long-term moving average AND Bollinger Band width > 2 × ATR(lookback equal to Bollinger length) AND close > Bollinger middle band AND RSI(14) > 55 AND MACD line > MACD signal line AND smoothed stochastic %K > 20 AND smoothed stochastic %K > smoothed %D AND ADX(14) > 25 AND (higher-trend filter disabled OR close > trend moving average) AND current bar is within an enabled trading session whose trade limit is not reached AND long-side stop-loss and take-profit limits are not reached AND position is flat or short
Short: short-term moving average crosses below long-term moving average AND Bollinger Band width > 2 × ATR(lookback equal to Bollinger length) AND close < Bollinger middle band AND RSI(14) < 45 AND MACD line < MACD signal line AND smoothed stochastic %K < 80 AND smoothed stochastic %K < smoothed %D AND ADX(14) > 25 AND (higher-trend filter disabled OR close < trend moving average) AND current bar is within an enabled trading session whose trade limit is not reached AND short-side stop-loss and take-profit limits are not reached AND position is flat or long
Calculation: Stop price = selected stop method: entry price adjusted against the position by the configured percentage; the short/long moving-average intersection value; or the moving average of low for longs / high for shorts
Calculation: Target price = entry price + position direction × configured risk/reward multiple × absolute difference between entry price and stop price
Exit: Long, short-entry condition OR low <= stop price OR high >= target price OR current bar time equals configured market-close time
Exit: Short, long-entry condition OR high >= stop price OR low <= target price OR current bar time equals configured market-close time
