Calculation: Upper level = the most recent maximum high of two consecutive completed higher-timeframe bars where the earlier bar closes above its open and the later bar closes below its open; carry this value forward until another qualifying pair occurs.
Calculation: Lower level = the most recent minimum low of two consecutive completed higher-timeframe bars where the earlier bar closes below its open and the later bar closes above its open; carry this value forward until another qualifying pair occurs.
Long: chart close > Upper level
Short: chart close < Lower level
Exit: Long, chart close < Lower level when short entries are disabled
