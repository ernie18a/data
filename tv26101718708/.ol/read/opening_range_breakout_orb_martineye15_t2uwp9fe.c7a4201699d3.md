Calculation: Opening range high and low = highest high and lowest low of bars in the configured session window, fixed when the window ends
Calculation: Opening range size = opening range high minus opening range low
Calculation: Stop price = configured opposite opening-range boundary, opening-range midpoint, entry price plus or minus ATR times a multiplier, entry price plus or minus a fixed tick distance, or entry price adjusted by a configured percentage; use the direction appropriate to the position
Calculation: Target price = entry price adjusted in the position direction by a configured risk multiple times the entry-to-stop distance, opening-range-size multiple, ATR multiple, fixed tick distance, or percentage; use the configured final target
Long: confirmed bar outside the opening-range session AND opening range is locked AND active day AND opening-range size is within enabled minimum and maximum limits AND (close > opening range high for close confirmation OR high > opening range high for wick confirmation) AND (retest disabled OR an earlier breakout above the opening range high was followed by low <= opening range high and this bar confirms another breakout above it) AND (first-breakout mode and no earlier long signal today OR every-breakout mode and this is a new breakout, following a return inside the boundary when retest is disabled) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average) AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no trade taken today)
Short: confirmed bar outside the opening-range session AND opening range is locked AND active day AND opening-range size is within enabled minimum and maximum limits AND (close < opening range low for close confirmation OR low < opening range low for wick confirmation) AND (retest disabled OR an earlier breakout below the opening range low was followed by high >= opening range low and this bar confirms another breakout below it) AND (first-breakout mode and no earlier short signal today OR every-breakout mode and this is a new breakout, following a return inside the boundary when retest is disabled) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume > volume moving average) AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day disabled OR no trade taken today)
Exit: long, confirmed bar low <= stop price OR high >= configured final target price
Exit: short, confirmed bar high >= stop price OR low <= configured final target price
