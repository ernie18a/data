Calculation: Confirmed swing/internal pivots = a high/low that is the highest/lowest among the parameterized number of bars on each side; the longer lookback is the swing pivot and the shorter is the internal pivot. A pivot becomes available only after its right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Protected structure = on a directional break, anchor the protected opposite level to the latest confirmed opposite pivot; while bias is bearish, a close above the protected high flips bias bullish, and while bias is bullish, a close below the protected low flips bias bearish. Otherwise, a close above the latest unbroken pivot high breaks upward, or below the latest unbroken pivot low breaks downward. A same-bias break is BOS; a bias-reversing break is CHoCH. Check breaks before adding pivots confirmed on that bar.
Calculation: Liquidity pool = an unswept confirmed swing high or low; merge same-side, same-source pivots within an ATR-based tolerance, retaining the higher high or lower low. Remove a pool after any wick takes it or it expires. A completed higher-timeframe bar is used for higher-timeframe data; no higher-timeframe calculation is required for chart-timeframe signals.
Calculation: Displacement = absolute candle body >= prior-bar ATR × threshold AND body / candle range >= threshold.
Calculation: Bullish FVG = current low > high two bars earlier AND middle candle closes above its open AND gap width >= prior-bar ATR × threshold. Bearish FVG = current high < low two bars earlier AND middle candle closes below its open AND gap width >= prior-bar ATR × threshold.
Calculation: Order block = on an upward/downward structure break, if the break candle is directionally displaced, find the nearest opposing candle within the lookback; its wick or body range is the candidate bullish/bearish zone. Require the configured recent same-direction FVG association when enabled; reject candidates breached at the distal edge, re-entered after departure before detection, or not cleanly closed beyond the zone at detection.
Long: Close > latest unbroken pivot high when bias is not bearish, or close > protected high when bias is bearish.
Short: Close < latest unbroken pivot low when bias is not bullish, or close < protected low when bias is bullish.
Long: Low < an unswept sell-side liquidity pool AND close > that pool.
Short: High > an unswept buy-side liquidity pool AND close < that pool.
Long: Bullish FVG condition.
Short: Bearish FVG condition.
Long: Bullish order block condition.
Short: Bearish order block condition.
Long: A bullish zone is overlapped (low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom), close > zone top AND close > open, low >= zone bottom, zone is active and unexpired, this is an allowed distinct encounter, and the bar is later than the zone's detection bar.
Short: A bearish zone is overlapped (low <= zone top AND high >= zone bottom), close < zone bottom AND close < open, high <= zone top, zone is active and unexpired, this is an allowed distinct encounter, and the bar is later than the zone's detection bar.
Long: Bullish zone rejection condition AND swing bias is bullish AND (sequence disabled OR a sell-side sweep occurred before an upward shift from the selected internal/swing engine, followed by bullish displacement on the shift bar or later, and this zone was detected at or after the shift and the rejection is later than the displacement) AND (discount filter disabled OR close is between latest confirmed swing low and the midpoint of latest confirmed swing high/low) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe swing bias is bullish) AND (session filter disabled OR bar meets selected session membership) AND setup cooldown has elapsed.
Short: Bearish zone rejection condition AND swing bias is bearish AND (sequence disabled OR a buy-side sweep occurred before a downward shift from the selected internal/swing engine, followed by bearish displacement on the shift bar or later, and this zone was detected at or after the shift and the rejection is later than the displacement) AND (premium filter disabled OR close is between the midpoint of latest confirmed swing high/low and latest confirmed swing high) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR completed higher-timeframe swing bias is bearish) AND (session filter disabled OR bar meets selected session membership) AND setup cooldown has elapsed.
