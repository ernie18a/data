Calculation: Forming-auction delta = 100 × (buy volume − sell volume) / total volume, accumulated from chart bars in the still-forming higher-timeframe auction; for each bar, buy volume = volume × (close − low) / (high − low), and sell volume = volume − buy volume (use an even split when high = low).
Calculation: Forming-auction control price = volume-weighted mean of each chart bar’s (high + low + close) / 3, using bars in the still-forming higher-timeframe auction.
Calculation: Settled POC = midpoint of the highest-volume price row of the last completed higher-timeframe auction; distribute each chart bar’s volume uniformly across its high-low range when accumulating row volume.
Calculation: Absorption = volume ≥ 2 × 30-bar average volume AND (high − low) ≤ 0.7 × 30-bar average range.
Calculation: Kinetic fuel = normalize volume × absolute change in close over the last 100 bars to the range from its 100-bar minimum to maximum.
Long: (close > settled POC AND previous close ≤ settled POC AND prior consecutive closes below settled POC ≥ acceptance-bars parameter OR forming-auction delta > delta-trigger parameter AND prior delta ≤ delta-trigger parameter AND close > forming-auction control price) AND signal filters (selected signal model permits the trigger; auction delta > 0 if alignment is required; kinetic fuel ≥ minimum fuel; win rate for the signal’s absolute-delta tier ≥ minimum probability when that tier has at least the minimum samples; cooldown elapsed; current bar confirmed; ATR available) OR absorption AND close-position fraction ≥ 0.5, where close-position fraction = (close − low) / (high − low), or 0.5 when high = low.
Short: (close < settled POC AND previous close ≥ settled POC AND prior consecutive closes above settled POC ≥ acceptance-bars parameter OR forming-auction delta < −delta-trigger parameter AND prior delta ≥ −delta-trigger parameter AND close < forming-auction control price) AND signal filters (selected signal model permits the trigger; auction delta < 0 if alignment is required; kinetic fuel ≥ minimum fuel; win rate for the signal’s absolute-delta tier ≥ minimum probability when that tier has at least the minimum samples; cooldown elapsed; current bar confirmed; ATR available; no long signal on this bar) OR absorption AND close-position fraction < 0.5.
Calculation: Long stop at entry = settled POC − ATR × stop-buffer parameter for a reclaim, otherwise forming-auction control price − ATR × stop-buffer parameter (or signal-bar low − ATR × stop-buffer parameter if control is unavailable); if this stop is at or above entry, use entry − ATR.
Calculation: Short stop at entry = settled POC + ATR × stop-buffer parameter for a reclaim, otherwise forming-auction control price + ATR × stop-buffer parameter (or signal-bar high + ATR × stop-buffer parameter if control is unavailable); if this stop is at or below entry, use entry + ATR.
Calculation: Long target at entry = settled auction high if target mode is auction magnet and its distance above entry is greater than 0.2 × ATR and no more than 3 × risk; otherwise entry + risk × reward-risk parameter.
Calculation: Short target at entry = settled auction low if target mode is auction magnet and its distance below entry is greater than 0.2 × ATR and no more than 3 × risk; otherwise entry − risk × reward-risk parameter.
Exit: long, opposite short signal OR low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long target; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, exit at the stop.
Exit: short, opposite long signal OR high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short target; if stop and target are both touched on one bar, exit at the stop.
