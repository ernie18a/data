Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = high/low that remains the extreme across a configurable number of bars before and after it; use the latest confirmed pivot of each kind, and clear the corresponding pivot after its level is broken.
Calculation: Fast and slow Kalman estimates = recursive Kalman filters of close, each updating its prior estimate by gain × (close − prior estimate), where gain = (prior error + process noise) / (prior error + process noise + measurement noise); the fast estimate uses a shorter lookback than the slow estimate.
Calculation: Structure impulse = +1 when close crosses above the latest pivot high (prior close ≤ that level), −1 when close crosses below the latest pivot low (prior close ≥ that level), otherwise 0.
Calculation: Structure activity and bias = RMA of absolute structure impulse and RMA of structure impulse, respectively, using the same lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive Kalman trail = prior trail + adaptive factor × (fast Kalman estimate − prior trail) + push factor × structure bias × agreement factor × ATR + anchor factor × (pivot midpoint − trail) when both pivots exist. The adaptive factor interpolates between fast and slow smoothing factors according to structure activity. The agreement factor is 1 when structure bias and the fast-versus-slow Kalman ordering agree in direction, otherwise a lower configurable factor. The pivot midpoint is the average of the latest pivot high and low.
Calculation: Trail boundaries = adaptive Kalman trail ± ATR × boundary multiplier.
Calculation: Trail direction = initialize bullish when close ≥ adaptive Kalman trail, otherwise bearish; switch bullish when close is above the upper boundary and the trail is rising, and switch bearish when close is below the lower boundary and the trail is falling; otherwise retain the prior direction.
Long: close > latest confirmed pivot high AND prior close ≤ latest confirmed pivot high OR prior trail direction is bearish AND current trail direction is bullish
Short: close < latest confirmed pivot low AND prior close ≥ latest confirmed pivot low OR prior trail direction is bullish AND current trail direction is bearish
