Calculation: Adaptive RSI = Calculate volatility as the standard deviation of close over the base RSI lookback, then average that volatility over twice the base lookback. Set the adaptive length to the base lookback multiplied by average volatility divided by current volatility and by adaptive sensitivity; use a ratio of 1 if either volatility is zero, round the result, and clamp it between 3 and three times the base lookback. For each bar, let up = max(change in close, 0), down = max(-change in close, 0), and alpha = 1 / adaptive length. Seed the up and down averages with their respective simple averages over the base lookback; thereafter update each as alpha times its current value plus (1 - alpha) times its previous value. RSI = 100 - 100 / (1 + up average / down average).
Calculation: RSI signal moving average = selected SMA, EMA, RMA, WMA, or HMA of the adaptive RSI.
Calculation: Confirmed RSI pivot high/low = an RSI high/low with the configured number of lower/higher RSI values on both its left and right; confirm after the right-side bars complete. Pair it with the price high/low from the same pivot bar.
Long: Adaptive RSI crosses above its signal moving average OR current confirmed RSI pivot low > previous confirmed RSI pivot low AND paired current price low < paired previous price low OR current confirmed RSI pivot low < previous confirmed RSI pivot low AND paired current price low > paired previous price low
Short: Adaptive RSI crosses below its signal moving average OR current confirmed RSI pivot high < previous confirmed RSI pivot high AND paired current price high > paired previous price high OR current confirmed RSI pivot high > previous confirmed RSI pivot high AND paired current price high < paired previous price high
