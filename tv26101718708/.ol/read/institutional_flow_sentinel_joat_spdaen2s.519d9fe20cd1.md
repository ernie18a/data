Calculation: Adaptive Supertrend direction = bullish when close > the prior trendline, otherwise bearish; initialize the trendline at the upper band, then in bullish direction set it to the greater of the lower band and prior trendline, and in bearish direction set it to the lesser of the upper band and prior trendline. Upper/lower bands = (high + low) / 2 ± ATR × smoothed dynamic factor; dynamic factor = base factor + ATR / close × volatility weight + |close − prior close| / prior close × price-change weight.
Calculation: Liquidity sweep = price crosses an unswept pivot level and closes back beyond it: low < pivot low and close > pivot low (bullish sweep), or high > pivot high and close < pivot high (bearish sweep); pivots require the configured number of bars on both sides for confirmation, and each level can be swept once.
Long: On a confirmed bar, adaptive Supertrend direction flips from bearish to bullish AND the last completed bar on a higher timeframe has bullish adaptive Supertrend direction AND ADX > its threshold AND volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND RSI > 50 AND |close − open| ≥ ATR × minimum body ratio AND a bullish sweep occurred within the recent-sweep lookback.
Short: On a confirmed bar, adaptive Supertrend direction flips from bullish to bearish AND the last completed bar on a higher timeframe has bearish adaptive Supertrend direction AND ADX > its threshold AND volume > its moving average × volume multiplier AND RSI < 50 AND |close − open| ≥ ATR × minimum body ratio AND a bearish sweep occurred within the recent-sweep lookback.
Exit: Long, on a confirmed bar adaptive Supertrend direction flips from bullish to bearish.
Exit: Short, on a confirmed bar adaptive Supertrend direction flips from bearish to bullish.
