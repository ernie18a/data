Calculation: Slow pivot high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using a longer lookback than fast pivots; confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Fast pivot high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using a shorter lookback than slow pivots; confirmed after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Bullish close percentage = 100 × (close − low) / (high − low), when high > low.
Calculation: Bearish close percentage = 100 × (high − close) / (high − low), when high > low.
Long: A slow pivot low has closed through its level from above (current close < level AND previous close >= level) while no break cycle is waiting and no fast swing has been taken since the latest slow pivot; then the first fast pivot high whose pivot bar is after the break forms; while its line is active and within its extension period, close > fast pivot high AND volume <= previous bar volume × volume limit AND bullish close percentage >= close percentage requirement.
Short: A slow pivot high has closed through its level from below (current close > level AND previous close <= level) while no break cycle is waiting and no fast swing has been taken since the latest slow pivot; then the first fast pivot low whose pivot bar is after the break forms; while its line is active and within its extension period, close < fast pivot low AND volume <= previous bar volume × volume limit AND bearish close percentage >= close percentage requirement.
