Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Bullish order block = the most recent down-close candle within a lookback when a close above the last unbroken confirmed swing high has a body at least a configured ATR multiple; bearish order block mirrors this at a close below the last unbroken confirmed swing low. The zone is the origin candle's body or full range.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current low minus the high two bars earlier, if positive and at least a configured ATR multiple; bearish fair value gap mirrors this. The middle candle's body must meet a configured ATR multiple when that filter is enabled.
Calculation: Active zone = a bullish or bearish order block or fair value gap not expired, closed through its far edge, or already used for a signal. It must have been created before the current bar. A bullish-zone trigger is current low at or below its top and close at or above its top; a bearish-zone trigger is current high at or above its bottom and close at or below its bottom. If multiple unused zones trigger on one side, use the one with the greatest effective mass.
Calculation: Effective zone mass = initial mass × 0.5^(zone age / configured half-life). Order-block initial mass = displacement candle body / ATR × (0.5 + its volume percentile / 100). Fair-value-gap initial mass = gap height / ATR × (0.5 + middle-candle volume percentile / 100).
Calculation: Gravity bias = 100 × (bullish field − bearish field) / (bullish field + bearish field), where each field is the sum over its active zones of effective mass / (distance from close to zone midpoint in ATR units squared + configured softening epsilon squared). Use zero if both fields are zero.
Calculation: Trend score = 100 × weighted average of (market-structure regime, signed efficiency ratio, and clamped ATR-normalized Hull moving-average slope). Market-structure regime is +1 after a bullish break of a confirmed swing high and −1 after a bearish break of a confirmed swing low; signed efficiency ratio is net close change / sum of absolute close changes over its lookback; slope is Hull moving average change over its lookback / (ATR × lookback), clamped to −1..+1. Trend state changes to bullish when score exceeds the entry threshold while state is neutral or bearish, to bearish when score is below the negative entry threshold while state is neutral or bullish, and to neutral when a non-neutral state's absolute score falls below the exit threshold.
Calculation: Long quality = 100 × (0.35 × trend alignment + 0.25 × max(0, min(1, gravity bias / 100)) + 0.20 × candidate effective mass / greatest active bullish-zone effective mass + 0.20 × (close − low) / bar range). Trend alignment is 1 in bullish state, 0.4 in neutral state, and 0 in bearish state. Short quality mirrors this: use bearish-zone mass, −gravity bias, and (high − close) / bar range; trend alignment is 1 in bearish state, 0.4 in neutral state, and 0 in bullish state. Clamp mass ratios to 0..1 and use zero if the side's greatest mass is zero.
Long: Trend state changes to bullish OR (bullish-zone trigger AND confirmed bar AND configured signal cooldown elapsed AND gravity bias > 0 AND trend state is bullish AND position is flat or short AND long quality meets the primary threshold) OR (bullish-zone trigger AND confirmed bar AND configured signal cooldown elapsed AND gravity bias > 0 AND trend state is bullish or neutral AND position is long AND triggered zone was created after the previous entry AND add count is below its cap AND long quality meets the re-add threshold).
Short: Trend state changes to bearish OR (bearish-zone trigger AND confirmed bar AND configured signal cooldown elapsed AND gravity bias < 0 AND trend state is bearish AND position is flat or long AND short quality meets the primary threshold) OR (bearish-zone trigger AND confirmed bar AND configured signal cooldown elapsed AND gravity bias < 0 AND trend state is bearish or neutral AND position is short AND triggered zone was created after the previous entry AND add count is below its cap AND short quality meets the re-add threshold).
Exit: long, trend state is bearish OR a close below the last unbroken confirmed swing low changes market-structure regime to bearish.
Exit: short, trend state is bullish OR a close above the last unbroken confirmed swing high changes market-structure regime to bullish.
