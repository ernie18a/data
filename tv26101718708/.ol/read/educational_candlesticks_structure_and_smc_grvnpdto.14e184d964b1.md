Calculation: ATR = 14-bar average true range.
Calculation: Swing high/low = confirmed pivot high/low using equal left and right lookbacks; retain the latest two of each. A pivot is confirmed only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Up/down break = close crosses above latest swing high + ATR × penetration factor / below latest swing low − ATR × penetration factor; trend changes to the break direction. CHoCH is a break against the prior trend; BOS is a break with or without a neutral prior trend.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish liquidity sweep = high above latest swing high and close below it, with previous close below it / low below latest swing low and close above it, with previous close above it.
Calculation: Double top/bottom neckline break = latest two swing highs/lows differ by no more than 0.6 ATR, with the latest intervening swing low/high between them; close crosses below that low / above that high.
Calculation: Ascending/descending triangle = latest two swing highs differ by no more than 0.6 ATR and latest swing low is higher than the prior low / latest two swing lows differ by no more than 0.6 ATR and latest swing high is lower than the prior high.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish engulfing = current candle closes up/down; prior candle closes down/up; current real body contains the prior real body and is larger.
Calculation: Piercing line / dark cloud cover = current candle closes up/down after opening below/above prior close; it closes above/below the midpoint of the prior down/up real body but below/above its open; current body exceeds 30% of prior body.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish harami = current real body is inside prior real body, prior candle closes down/up, and current body is less than 60% of prior body.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish belt hold = candle closes up/down, opens within 5% of its range from its low/high, body is at least 65% of range, and opposite wick is at most 30% of range.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish marubozu = candle closes up/down and body is at least 92% of its range.
Calculation: Hammer shape = lower wick at least twice body and upper wick at most 0.8 times body; shooting-star shape = upper wick at least twice body and lower wick at most 0.8 times body. Hammer is bullish unless structure is up (then bearish hanging man); shooting star is bullish when structure is down (inverted hammer), otherwise bearish.
Calculation: Tweezer top/bottom = current and prior highs/lows differ by at most 0.08 ATR, current candle closes down/up, and prior candle closes up/down.
Calculation: Morning/evening star = first candle closes down/up with body over 0.5 ATR; middle body is at most 35% of first body; third candle closes up/down beyond the midpoint of the first real body.
Calculation: Three white soldiers / three black crows = three consecutive up/down closes progressing in that direction; each later open lies inside the preceding real body; latest opposite wick is at most 35% of latest body.
Calculation: Outside bar = current high exceeds prior high and current low is below prior low; direction is up when close > open, otherwise down.
Long: bullish CHoCH or BOS break OR bullish liquidity sweep OR double-bottom neckline break OR ascending triangle OR bullish engulfing OR piercing line OR bullish harami OR bullish belt hold OR bullish marubozu OR hammer shape when structure is not up OR shooting-star shape when structure is down OR tweezer bottom OR morning star OR three white soldiers OR outside bar closing up.
Short: bearish CHoCH or BOS break OR bearish liquidity sweep OR double-top neckline break OR descending triangle OR bearish engulfing OR dark cloud cover OR bearish harami OR bearish belt hold OR bearish marubozu OR hammer shape when structure is up OR shooting-star shape when structure is not down OR tweezer top OR evening star OR three black crows OR outside bar closing down.
