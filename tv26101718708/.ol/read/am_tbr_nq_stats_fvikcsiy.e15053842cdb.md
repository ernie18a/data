Calculation: Prior-session percentage net change = (daily close − previous daily close) / previous daily close × 100; calculate its sample standard deviation over a rolling lookback of completed daily bars (higher timeframe, last completed bar).
Calculation: TBR upper/lower levels = 08:00 New York open ± the configured standard-deviation distance, with standard deviation converted to price units as open × percentage standard deviation / 100.
Long: During the 08:00–12:00 New York weekday window, the lower level is touched before the upper level; if both are touched in the same lower-timeframe bar, choose the nearer level to that bar's open, with ties assigned to the upper level. Use completed lower-timeframe bars to determine touch order; if unavailable, use chart-bar high/low and the same tie-break.
Short: During the 08:00–12:00 New York weekday window, the upper level is touched before the lower level; if both are touched in the same lower-timeframe bar, choose the nearer level to that bar's open, with ties assigned to the upper level. Use completed lower-timeframe bars to determine touch order; if unavailable, use chart-bar high/low and the same tie-break.
Exit: Long, after the lower-level touch, price reaches or crosses the TBR open before 12:00 New York.
Exit: Short, after the upper-level touch, price reaches or crosses the TBR open before 12:00 New York.
