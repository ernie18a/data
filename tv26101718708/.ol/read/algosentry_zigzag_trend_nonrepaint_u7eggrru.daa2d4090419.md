Calculation: Heikin-Ashi close = (open + high + low + close) / 4; Heikin-Ashi open = prior Heikin-Ashi open/close average, initialized as (open + close) / 2; Heikin-Ashi high/low = max/min of high/low, Heikin-Ashi open, and Heikin-Ashi close.
Calculation: ZigZag price = Heikin-Ashi close; ZigZag high/low = Heikin-Ashi high/low when wick mode is selected, otherwise ZigZag price.
Calculation: ZigZag deviation threshold = configured percentage, or the greater of (ATR / close × 100 × configured multiplier) and a positive minimum when adaptive mode is selected; use the configured ATR lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed ZigZag pivot reversal = while rising, bars since pivot ≥ configured minimum and the ZigZag low has fallen below the leg extreme by at least the deviation threshold as a percentage of the extreme; while falling, bars since pivot ≥ configured minimum and the ZigZag high has risen above the leg extreme by at least that threshold. On reversal, close the leg, save its length and regular high/low, set the pivot to its extreme, and switch direction. The first leg direction is set when price first moves by the deviation threshold from the initial pivot.
Calculation: Correction = ZigZag rising while Heikin-Ashi close < moving average, or ZigZag falling while Heikin-Ashi close > moving average. A correction starts when this becomes true after being false; its start price is the pivot price and its extreme tracks the highest price in a falling trend or the lowest price in a rising trend.
Calculation: Correction depth tier = 100 × current correction-leg length / prior closed ZigZag-leg length; green if below the configured green threshold, else orange if below the configured orange threshold, else red if below the configured red threshold, else ungraded. Apply the configured signal-tier selection to determine which tiers qualify.
Calculation: Directional gate = (SMA-200 filter disabled OR (moving average > SMA-200 AND close > SMA-200)) for up, or (moving average < SMA-200 AND close < SMA-200) for down; AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > / < the EMA on the higher timeframe's last completed bar, respectively); AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX ≥ configured minimum). Compute ADX using the configured lookback for both DMI and ADX.
Calculation: Moving average direction for signals 1–3 = moving average > / < its prior chart-timeframe value when its absolute one-bar percentage slope meets the configured threshold; otherwise use moving average > / < its prior value. If the slope-only-for-green option is disabled, signals 1–3 additionally require the slope threshold. For signal 4, require the slope threshold and use its up/down direction.
Calculation: Qualified red correction marker = correction starts AND signal-tier-qualified correction depth exists AND moving-average direction for signals 1–3 AND corresponding directional gate.
Calculation: Qualified magenta moving-average-touch marker = active correction AND qualified red correction marker has occurred in the correction AND no orange or prior magenta marker has occurred in it AND chart high ≥ moving average AND chart low ≤ moving average AND signal-tier-qualified correction depth exists AND moving-average direction for signals 1–3 AND corresponding directional gate.
Calculation: Qualified orange pivot-reversal marker = active correction AND confirmed ZigZag pivot reversal AND signal-tier-qualified correction depth exists AND moving-average direction for signals 1–3 AND corresponding directional gate.
Calculation: Green early-break condition = qualified red and orange markers have both occurred since the correction began AND the selected method is met: (a) during an active correction, in a falling trend, ZigZag price < lowest ZigZag price over the configured number of prior chart bars, or in a rising trend ZigZag price > highest over those bars; (b) during an active correction, price has retraced at least the configured percentage from correction extreme toward correction start; or (c) regular low/high, or close when confirmed-break mode is selected, is below/above the regular low/high saved at the correction start in a falling/rising trend, respectively. For (a) and (b), use Heikin-Ashi close as ZigZag price. The selected method is one of these three alternatives.
Long: qualified red correction marker with up direction OR qualified magenta moving-average-touch marker with up direction OR qualified orange pivot-reversal marker with up direction OR green early-break condition with up direction and up directional gate
Short: qualified red correction marker with down direction OR qualified magenta moving-average-touch marker with down direction OR qualified orange pivot-reversal marker with down direction OR green early-break condition with down direction and down directional gate
