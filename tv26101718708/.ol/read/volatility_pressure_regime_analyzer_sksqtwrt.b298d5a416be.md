Calculation: ATR = average true range
Calculation: Efficiency ratio = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback
Calculation: Log return = natural logarithm of close divided by prior close
Calculation: Pressure = (compression percentile + 1.5 × acceleration percentile + 100 × (1 − efficiency ratio)) / 3.5, where compression percentile = 100 − percentile rank of ATR and acceleration percentile = percentile rank of the absolute change in log return
Calculation: Loaded = highest pressure percentile within the lookback
Calculation: Release = clamp(((high − low) / ATR − 0.8) / 1.2, 0, 1)
Calculation: Direction scores = up score: 2 if close exceeds the prior-bar value of the rolling highest high, plus 1 if close is at least as near the high as the low, plus 1 if close change over the direction lookback is nonnegative; down score: 2 if close is below the prior-bar value of the rolling lowest low, plus 1 if close is nearer the low than the high, plus 1 if close change over the direction lookback is negative
Calculation: Ichimoku cloud bias = +1 when close is above both cloud spans displaced by the displacement lookback, −1 when below both, otherwise 0; each span uses the midpoint of its rolling highest high and lowest low, and span A is the midpoint of the conversion and base lines
Calculation: Variance ratio = variance of the k-bar log return divided by k times the variance of the one-bar log return; pin regime requires the ratio below 1 minus its neutral band and, when significance is enabled, its standardized distance below −z threshold
Long: (loaded ≥ pressure-percentile threshold AND release ≥ release threshold for the confirmation count AND signal debounce interval has elapsed AND (regime filter disabled OR not in pin regime) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR at least the required number of chart and higher-timeframe cloud biases agree with the up direction)) AND up score ≥ down score; OR loaded pressure percentile ≥ 50 AND close > EMA
Short: (loaded ≥ pressure-percentile threshold AND release ≥ release threshold for the confirmation count AND signal debounce interval has elapsed AND (regime filter disabled OR not in pin regime) AND (Ichimoku filter disabled OR at least the required number of chart and higher-timeframe cloud biases agree with the down direction)) AND down score > up score; OR loaded pressure percentile ≥ 50 AND close ≤ EMA
