Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest in an odd-length window centered on it; confirm after the same number of bars have completed to its right. Use the latest unbroken swing high/low as the break level; replace it when a newer same-side swing confirms.
Calculation: Bullish/bearish Pivot MA = the selected moving average of low/high, respectively, refreshed by qualifying confirmed low/high pivots. Both use the same lookback and MA type. The bullish MA is eligible when it exists, is unbroken, close is at or above it, and it is rising; the bearish MA is eligible when it exists, is unbroken, close is at or below it, and it is falling. Only one side is active: choose the sole eligible side; if both are eligible, choose bearish after a new bearish pivot or when close is below the prior close, otherwise bullish.
Calculation: Structure break = close above/below the latest unbroken swing high/low, or high/low above/below it when wick confirmation is used. If both directions break on the same bar, classify bullish when close is at or above open, otherwise bearish. Track the direction of the last structure break.
Long: Bullish structure break after the last structure break was bearish (Bullish Shift) OR bullish structure break when the last structure break was not bearish (bullish continuation) OR latest shift is bullish AND bullish Pivot MA is active OR, after a Bullish Shift, the first later bar intersects the existing bullish Pivot MA within the configured ATR-based tolerance (bullish retest).
Short: Bearish structure break after the last structure break was bullish (Bearish Shift) OR bearish structure break when the last structure break was not bullish (bearish continuation) OR latest shift is bearish AND bearish Pivot MA is active OR, after a Bearish Shift, the first later bar intersects the existing bearish Pivot MA within the configured ATR-based tolerance (bearish retest).
