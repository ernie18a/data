Calculation: Bullish trend regime = a persistent state initially bullish; set bullish when the smoothed slope of the shorter-term EMA is above a positive ATR-based threshold, set bearish when below its negative, and otherwise retain the prior state. The slope is the smoothed average of the EMA’s change over a longer lookback; the threshold is a small multiple of the average ATR.
Calculation: Bullish reversal candle pattern = any of: bullish engulfing (current bullish body engulfs the prior bearish body and is larger); hammer (lower wick ≥ 2× body, upper wick ≤ 0.5× body, range > 0.3× average ATR); bullish inverse hammer (bullish candle, upper wick ≥ 2× body, lower wick ≤ 0.5× body, range > 0.3× average ATR); bullish doji (body ≤ 0.05× average ATR, range > 0.3× average ATR, and low ≤ the lowest of the three higher-timeframe lows plus S/R tolerance); morning star (two bars ago bearish, prior body ≤ 0.3× the body two bars ago, current bullish, current close ≥ prior-bar midpoint + half the body two bars ago, and current body ≥ 0.4× average ATR); bullish harami (current bullish body contained within prior bearish body and current body < 0.6× prior body); or tweezer bottom (current and prior lows within three minimum ticks, prior bearish and current bullish, and current range > 0.3× average ATR). Body is absolute close minus open; wick and range are calculated from OHLC.
Calculation: Bearish reversal candle pattern = any of: bearish engulfing (current bearish body engulfs the prior bullish body and is larger); shooting star (upper wick ≥ 2× body, lower wick ≤ 0.5× body, range > 0.3× average ATR); hanging man (current bearish candle, lower wick ≥ 2× body, upper wick ≤ 0.5× body, range > 0.3× average ATR); bearish doji (body ≤ 0.05× average ATR, range > 0.3× average ATR, and high ≥ the highest of the three higher-timeframe highs minus S/R tolerance); evening star (two bars ago bullish, prior body ≤ 0.3× the body two bars ago, current bearish, current close ≤ prior-bar midpoint − half the body two bars ago, and current body ≥ 0.4× average ATR); bearish harami (current bearish body contained within prior bullish body and current body < 0.6× prior body); or tweezer top (current and prior highs within three minimum ticks, prior bullish and current bearish, and current range > 0.3× average ATR).
Calculation: Near support = the low or close is within S/R tolerance of any of the lows from three distinct higher timeframes or the previous completed daily low. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bars; use the previous completed daily bar.
Calculation: Near resistance = the high or close is within S/R tolerance of any of the highs from three distinct higher timeframes or the previous completed daily high. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bars; use the previous completed daily bar.
Long: Near support AND RSI below its oversold threshold AND stochastic %K crosses above %D AND bullish reversal candle pattern AND (a bullish trend flip occurred within the recent-flip window OR bullish trend regime) AND current bar index minus the bar index of the most recent long signal ≥ cooldown.
Short: Near resistance AND RSI above its overbought threshold AND stochastic %K crosses below %D AND bearish reversal candle pattern AND (a bearish trend flip occurred within the recent-flip window OR bearish trend regime) AND current bar index minus the bar index of the most recent short signal ≥ cooldown.
