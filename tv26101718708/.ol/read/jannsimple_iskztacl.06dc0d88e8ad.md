Calculation: Bullish candle confirmation = (close > open AND close - open > candle range × configured body fraction) OR (close > open AND close > previous open AND open < previous close AND previous close < previous open AND close > VWAP)
Calculation: Bearish candle confirmation = (close < open AND open - close > candle range × configured body fraction) OR (close < open AND close < previous open AND open > previous close AND previous close > previous open AND close < VWAP)
Calculation: Near psychological level = absolute distance from close to any strong or medium psychological price level is less than a configured fraction of close; each level is a whole-number or half-number increment at the corresponding price precision
Calculation: Near support = close is within a configured fraction of the previous completed higher-timeframe daily range from the daily pivot, first support, or second support; pivot = (previous daily high + low + close) / 3, first support = 2 × pivot − previous daily high, second support = pivot − (previous daily high − low)
Calculation: Near resistance = close is within a configured fraction of the previous completed higher-timeframe daily range from the daily pivot, first resistance, or second resistance; first resistance = 2 × pivot − previous daily low, second resistance = pivot + (previous daily high − low)
Calculation: Long score = weighted sum of: short-lookback EMA > long-lookback EMA; close > VWAP; VWAP reclaim (current close > VWAP and previous close < previous VWAP); bullish candle confirmation; near support; near psychological level; higher-timeframe close > its EMA; volume > its longer-lookback average multiplied by a configured surge factor
Calculation: Short score = weighted sum of: short-lookback EMA < long-lookback EMA; close < VWAP; VWAP rejection (current close < VWAP and previous close > previous VWAP); bearish candle confirmation; near resistance; near psychological level; higher-timeframe close < its EMA; volume > its longer-lookback average multiplied by a configured surge factor
Calculation: New long event = bullish EMA crossover and VWAP reclaim both occurred within the configured confluence window, or—if the dual-event requirement is disabled—a bullish EMA crossover or VWAP reclaim occurred on this bar; and the resulting event was false on the previous bar
Calculation: New short event = bearish EMA crossunder and VWAP rejection both occurred within the configured confluence window, or—if the dual-event requirement is disabled—a bearish EMA crossunder or VWAP rejection occurred on this bar; and the resulting event was false on the previous bar
Long: New long event AND (long score > configured minimum score OR (long score = configured minimum score AND higher-timeframe close > its EMA AND (near support OR near psychological level))) AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close > its EMA) AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within either configured session) AND bars since prior long signal > configured cooldown
Short: New short event AND (short score > configured minimum score OR (short score = configured minimum score AND higher-timeframe close < its EMA AND (near resistance OR near psychological level))) AND (trend filter disabled OR higher-timeframe close < its EMA) AND (time filter disabled OR bar time is within either configured session) AND bars since prior short signal > configured cooldown
