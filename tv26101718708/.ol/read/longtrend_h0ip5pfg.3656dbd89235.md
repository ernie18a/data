Calculation: Adaptive ATR trailing stop = maintain ATR-based upper and lower bands for each candidate multiplier in an increasing configured range; for each candidate, exponentially smooth (close − prior close) × sign(prior close − prior stop) as its performance score. Cluster scores into three groups by 3-means initialized at the 25th, 50th, and 75th score percentiles; use the mean multiplier of the configured best, average, or worst cluster. With that multiplier, update upper/lower bands from hl2 ± ATR × multiplier, ratcheting each band while the prior close remains beyond it; bullish state begins when close exceeds the upper band, bearish state when close falls below the lower band, and otherwise the prior state persists. The trailing stop is the lower band in bullish state and upper band in bearish state.
