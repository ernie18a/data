Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback on chart-timeframe bars
Calculation: Active high/low pools = confirmed chart-timeframe pivot highs/lows, retaining the configured number of most recent pools per side
Calculation: Sweep event = when idle and cooldown is zero, choose the qualifying pool sweep with greatest wick pierce divided by ATR; a low-pool sweep requires low < pool level − ATR × sweep buffer, pierce ≥ ATR × (minimum wick + sweep buffer), and, when reclaim is required, close > pool level; a high-pool sweep uses the mirrored comparisons and close < pool level. Store sweep direction, level, wick extreme as origin, and bar.
Calculation: Displacement event = within the configured bars after a sweep, on a later chart-timeframe bar: bullish if close > sweep level, close − origin ≥ ATR × minimum displacement, |close − open| ≥ ATR × minimum body, and close > open; bearish uses the mirrored comparisons. If no displacement occurs before the window expires, clear the event and start cooldown.
Calculation: Acceptance score = round to 0.1 and cap at 10 the sum of: min(2, sweep depth in ATR / 0.35), min(2.5, displacement leg in ATR / minimum displacement × 1.4), min(2, current resolve-bar body in ATR / minimum body × 1.2), volume pulse score (volume / configured-lookback volume SMA; if pulse ≥ configured volume multiplier, min(2, 1 + (pulse − multiplier) × 0.6), otherwise min(1, pulse / multiplier)), 1 if close breaks the prior 10 chart-timeframe bars' high for bullish or low for bearish, else 0, and HTF bias score (1 when event direction matches current HTF close versus prior completed HTF close, 0.4 when equal, otherwise 0; if HTF scoring is disabled, 0.5). The current HTF bar may still be forming.
Calculation: Resolve event = after displacement, hold line is origin plus (bullish) or minus (bearish) the displacement leg size × configured hold fraction. Rejected means bullish close < sweep level and close < open, or bearish close > sweep level and close > open. Accepted means not rejected and bullish close ≥ hold line and low > origin, or bearish close ≤ hold line and high < origin.
Calculation: Event timing = resolve on rejection, on acceptance at least one bar after displacement, or after the configured acceptance window. Emit rejection on rejection or when the window expires without acceptance; otherwise emit acceptance. After resolution start cooldown.
Long: bullish acceptance event AND acceptance score ≥ 0.7 × minimum acceptance score
Short: bearish acceptance event AND acceptance score ≥ 0.7 × minimum acceptance score
Exit: long, bullish rejection event
Exit: short, bearish rejection event
