Calculation: Technical rating = mean of the moving-average rating and oscillator rating. Each rating is (number of +1 actions − number of −1 actions) / number of indicators. Moving-average actions: +1 when close is above the average, −1 when below; include short-term and long-term SMA and EMA, VWMA, HMA, and Ichimoku. Ichimoku action is +1 when close is above both lagged spans and conversion line is above base line, −1 when close is below both lagged spans and conversion line is below base line, otherwise 0. Oscillator actions: RSI: +1 below oversold and rising, −1 above overbought and falling; Stochastic and Stoch RSI: +1 when both smoothed lines are below oversold and the faster line is above the slower, −1 when both are above overbought and the faster is below the slower; CCI: +1 below its negative threshold and rising, −1 above its positive threshold and falling; DMI/ADX: +1 when ADX is above its threshold, rising, and +DI > −DI; −1 when ADX is above its threshold, rising, and −DI > +DI; Awesome Oscillator: +1 when positive and rising, −1 when negative and falling; Momentum: +1 when rising, −1 when falling; MACD: +1 when MACD > signal, −1 when MACD < signal; Williams %R: +1 below oversold and rising, −1 above overbought and falling; Bull/Bear Power: +1 when positive and rising, −1 when negative and falling; Ultimate Oscillator: +1 above its upper threshold, −1 below its lower threshold. An unlisted or neutral condition contributes 0.
Calculation: Upper and lower magnets = the weighted-price center of the candidate cluster with greatest pull on that side, considering only candidates within the maximum search distance. Candidates are unbroken swing highs/lows (weight 2), untouched previous-day high/low (weight 2), and estimated liquidation levels from candles whose volume exceeds its longer-lookback average times a multiplier (weight 1). For liquidation candidates, use close × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance margin) below price and close × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance margin) above price, for the configured leverage levels. Merge candidates within the configured cluster-width percentage of a candidate level. Cluster strength is the sum of weights; pull is strength divided by distance from close in percent. A swing candidate is removed once price reaches it; a previous-day level is eligible only while the current day's high/low has not touched it.
Calculation: All-timeframe agreement = 100 × sum of enabled timeframe votes / maximum possible votes. A vote is +1 when upper pull exceeds lower pull, upper strength exceeds lower strength, or technical rating meets the positive threshold; it is −1 for the corresponding lower-side dominance or negative technical threshold; otherwise 0. In the Pull + Strength + Technicals mode, use the enabled vote types; in Technicals-only mode, use technical votes only. Use chart-timeframe values on the signal bar close and the last completed bar for any higher timeframe.
Long: Selected source is chart-timeframe technicals AND technical rating ≥ positive threshold AND last signal is not long OR selected source is all-timeframe technicals OR Pull + Strength + Technicals AND agreement ≥ positive agreement threshold AND last signal is not long.
Short: Selected source is chart-timeframe technicals AND technical rating ≤ negative threshold AND last signal is not short OR selected source is all-timeframe technicals OR Pull + Strength + Technicals AND agreement ≤ negative agreement threshold AND last signal is not short.
Exit: long, short condition.
Exit: short, long condition.
