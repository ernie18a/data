Calculation: Price efficiency = absolute close change over the lookback divided by the sum of absolute one-bar close changes over that lookback (0 if the sum is 0)
Calculation: Chaos = 100 × log10(sum of one-bar ATRs over the volatility lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) ÷ log10(lookback)
Calculation: Directional conviction, bull force, and bear force = ADX and +DI/−DI calculated with equal lookbacks
Calculation: Directional regime = directional score > 60, where directional score is directional power ÷ total power × 100; directional power = (100 × price efficiency + 100 − chaos + 20 if conviction > 25) ÷ 2.2; equilibrium power = (100 − volatility percentile rank + 100 × (1 − price efficiency) + 20 if conviction < 25) ÷ 2.2; chaos power = (chaos + 100 × (1 − price efficiency)) ÷ 2; total power is the sum of those three powers
Calculation: Bullish regime = directional regime AND bull force > bear force; bearish regime = directional regime AND bear force > bull force
Calculation: EMA ribbon = EMAs of close from the shortest to longest lookback; use the shortest, longest, and approximately two-thirds-through ribbon EMAs as fast, slow, and middle levels
Calculation: Ribbon aligned = (close > middle EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA) OR (close < middle EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA)
Calculation: Pressure index = 100 × 14-bar simple average of buy pressure minus sell pressure ÷ 14-bar simple average of volume; buy pressure on an up bar = volume × (body + 0.5 × upper wick) ÷ bar range, on a down bar = volume × (upper wick + 0.3 × body) ÷ bar range, and on a doji = half of volume; sell pressure = volume − buy pressure
Calculation: Fractal efficiency = clamp to [0,1] of (1 + log(range sum ÷ total span) ÷ log(50)) ÷ 2; range sum is the 50-bar sum of max(high, previous close) − min(low, previous close), and total span is highest high − lowest low over 50 bars
Calculation: Fractal momentum = clamp to [−100,100] of the 5-bar EMA of (Laguerre value − 50) × (1 + fractal efficiency); Laguerre value is 100 × up-movement sum ÷ (up-movement sum + down-movement sum) across four Laguerre stages, using gamma = 0.7 × (1 − fractal efficiency) + 0.1 × fractal efficiency and a 4th-order Gaussian-smoothed close; use 50 when both sums are zero
Calculation: Flow = EMA(close, flow lookback) − EMA(close, twice that lookback); flow direction is positive when flow > 0 and negative otherwise; flow magnitude = absolute flow ÷ ATR; flow acceleration = flow change over 3 bars
Calculation: Volume surge = volume > 1.5 × 20-bar average volume; strong bullish (bearish) candle = close > open (close < open) and body > 0.3 × ATR; order-block reversal = prior candle bearish (bullish), current candle bullish (bearish), and volume > 1.2 × 20-bar average volume
Calculation: Gap up = low > high two bars ago and gap size > 0.3 × ATR; gap down = high < low two bars ago and gap size > 0.3 × ATR
Calculation: Range breakout up (down) = close above the prior 20-bar highest high (below the prior 20-bar lowest low) AND volume surge
Calculation: Long entry score = count one point for each satisfied group: (1) any of: Laguerre value < 30 AND positive flow AND close > fast EMA; pressure index > 30 AND flow direction just reversed to positive; fractal momentum > 20 AND volume surge AND close > middle EMA; strong bullish candle AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND pressure index > 20; fractal momentum > 0 AND flow acceleration > 0 AND ribbon aligned; (2) any of: fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND bullish regime; bullish structure break AND fractal momentum > 20; bullish order-block reversal AND positive flow AND conviction > 20; gap up AND pressure index > 40 AND ribbon aligned; range breakout up AND cumulative buy-minus-sell pressure > 0 AND flow magnitude > 0.5; (3) any of: fractal momentum > 40 AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND conviction > 25; Laguerre value < 30 AND positive flow AND volume surge; absolute fractal momentum > 60 AND fractal momentum > 0 AND fast-to-slow EMA spread increased; pressure index > 70 AND flow acceleration > 0 AND bullish regime; bull force > bear force AND bull force > 25 AND ribbon aligned; (4) any of: bullish structure shift AND volume surge; bullish order-block reversal AND close > last confirmed pivot low AND fractal momentum > 20; gap up AND positive flow AND fast EMA > slow EMA; bullish structure break AND pressure index > 50; volume absorption (volume > 1.5 × its 20-bar average AND bar range < 0.5 × its 20-bar average) AND buy pressure > sell pressure AND close > middle EMA; (5) ribbon spread percentage < 0.7 × its 20-bar average AND spread increased AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND volume surge; (6) 10-bar EMA of up-bar volume minus down-bar volume > 0 AND temporal force > 0.5 AND bullish regime. Cumulative pressure is the running sum of buy pressure minus sell pressure; temporal force = (flow ÷ ATR) × (volume ÷ 10-bar EMA of volume − 1) × flow magnitude. A structure break/shift is detected when confirmed pivot highs/lows update structure direction: a higher pivot high in an up structure or lower pivot low in a down structure is a break; a higher pivot high in a down structure or lower pivot low in an up structure is a shift, changing direction accordingly.
Calculation: Short entry score = count one point for each satisfied group: (1) any of: Laguerre value > 70 AND negative flow AND close < fast EMA; pressure index < −30 AND flow direction just reversed to negative; fractal momentum < −20 AND volume surge AND close < middle EMA; strong bearish candle AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND pressure index < −20; fractal momentum < 0 AND flow acceleration < 0 AND ribbon aligned; (2) any of: fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND bearish regime; bearish structure break AND fractal momentum < −20; bearish order-block reversal AND negative flow AND conviction > 20; gap down AND pressure index < −40 AND ribbon aligned; range breakout down AND cumulative buy-minus-sell pressure < 0 AND flow magnitude > 0.5; (3) any of: fractal momentum < −40 AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND conviction > 25; Laguerre value > 70 AND negative flow AND volume surge; absolute fractal momentum > 60 AND fractal momentum < 0 AND fast-to-slow EMA spread increased; pressure index < −70 AND flow acceleration < 0 AND bearish regime; bear force > bull force AND bear force > 25 AND ribbon aligned; (4) any of: bearish structure shift AND volume surge; bearish order-block reversal AND close < last confirmed pivot high AND fractal momentum < −20; gap down AND negative flow AND fast EMA < slow EMA; bearish structure break AND pressure index < −50; volume absorption AND sell pressure > buy pressure AND close < middle EMA; (5) ribbon spread percentage < 0.7 × its 20-bar average AND spread increased AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND volume surge; (6) 10-bar EMA of up-bar volume minus down-bar volume < 0 AND temporal force < −0.5 AND bearish regime
Long: long entry score ≥ the configured minimum AND position is flat
Short: short entry score ≥ the configured minimum AND position is flat
Exit: long, low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier × profit multiplier OR low ≤ close − current ATR × trailing offset when trailing is enabled
Exit: short, high ≥ entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR low ≤ entry price − ATR at entry × stop multiplier × profit multiplier OR high ≥ close + current ATR × trailing offset when trailing is enabled
