Calculation: Long score = 2 if the higher-timeframe EMA stack is bullish and its close is above its middle EMA, plus 2 if the chart-timeframe EMAs are fast > middle > slow, plus 2 if close > VWAP, plus 2 if a long trigger occurs, plus 1 if a bullish structure break occurs, plus 1 if the higher-timeframe EMA stack is bullish and its close is above its VWAP; otherwise each component adds 0. Higher-timeframe values use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Short score = 2 if the higher-timeframe EMA stack is bearish and its close is below its middle EMA, plus 2 if the chart-timeframe EMAs are fast < middle < slow, plus 2 if close < VWAP, plus 2 if a short trigger occurs, plus 1 if a bearish structure break occurs, plus 1 if the higher-timeframe EMA stack is bearish and its close is below its VWAP; otherwise each component adds 0. Higher-timeframe values use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = current high exceeds the highest high of prior bars over the swing lookback, OR close exceeds the latest confirmed pivot high, OR current low is below the lowest low of prior bars over the sweep lookback and close > open.
Calculation: Bearish structure break = current low is below the lowest low of prior bars over the swing lookback, OR close is below the latest confirmed pivot low, OR current high exceeds the highest high of prior bars over the sweep lookback and close < open.
Calculation: Long trigger = EMA fast crosses above VWAP, OR (EMA fast > EMA middle > EMA slow AND close > VWAP AND low <= EMA fast AND close > open); if the trend-slope filter is enabled, EMA fast must also be higher than on the prior bar.
Calculation: Short trigger = EMA fast crosses below VWAP, OR (EMA fast < EMA middle < EMA slow AND close < VWAP AND high >= EMA fast AND close < open); if the trend-slope filter is enabled, EMA fast must also be lower than on the prior bar.
Long: within the configured session AND (long score >= minimum score threshold OR C trades are enabled AND long score < 7) AND ATR >= minimum ATR AND high - low <= average(high - low) over the candle-range lookback * maximum candle expansion multiple AND 0 < close - lowest low over the swing lookback <= maximum stop distance AND long trigger AND no long signal occurred within the cooldown bars.
Short: within the configured session AND (short score >= minimum score threshold OR C trades are enabled AND short score < 7) AND ATR >= minimum ATR AND high - low <= average(high - low) over the candle-range lookback * maximum candle expansion multiple AND 0 < highest high over the swing lookback - close <= maximum stop distance AND short trigger AND no short signal occurred within the cooldown bars.
