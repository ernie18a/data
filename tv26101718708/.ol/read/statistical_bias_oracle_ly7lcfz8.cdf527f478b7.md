Calculation: Bullish candle event = the previous completed bar’s low is below the bar before it’s low and its close is above that low, OR its close is above the bar before it’s high.
Calculation: Bearish candle event = the previous completed bar’s high is above the bar before it’s high and its close is below that high, OR its close is below the bar before it’s low.
Calculation: Asset bias = bullish or bearish on a bullish or bearish candle event, respectively; otherwise retain the prior bias. Evaluate on the selected bias timeframe and, when higher-timeframe validation is selected, independently on the next higher timeframe. Use completed bars on both timeframes.
Long: the configured validation rule confirms bullish bias for the selected asset or asset group (selected bias timeframe is bullish, or both it and the next higher timeframe are bullish) AND the configured consensus rule is met (chart asset bullish, at least two of three assets bullish, or all three assets bullish).
Short: the configured validation rule confirms bearish bias for the selected asset or asset group (selected bias timeframe is bearish, or both it and the next higher timeframe are bearish) AND the configured consensus rule is met (chart asset bearish, at least two of three assets bearish, or all three assets bearish).
