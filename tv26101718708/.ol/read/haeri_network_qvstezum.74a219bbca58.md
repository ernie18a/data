Calculation: Bullish score = sum of configured weights for these conditions: close > long-term EMA; shorter EMA rising while longer EMA falls; Supertrend bullish or reverses bullish; price above PSAR or PSAR reverses bullish; close above HMA or HMA turns up; bullish regular divergence in RSI, MACD, WaveTrend, Stochastic, RVI, or OBV; RSI oversold or crosses above 50 within the current or prior two bars; MACD histogram turns up below zero or MACD crosses above its signal below zero; WaveTrend crosses above its signal below -10; Stochastic crosses above its signal; close below the lower Bollinger Band; ATR trailing-stop direction reverses bullish; RVI volatility condition; HMA crosses above any support/resistance level with close > close two bars ago; CMF crosses above or is above zero; VWAP crosses above HMA or shorter EMA is above VWAP; bullish engulfing candle.
Calculation: Bearish score = sum of configured weights for these conditions: close < long-term EMA; shorter EMA falling while longer EMA rises; Supertrend bearish or reverses bearish; price below PSAR or PSAR reverses bearish; close below HMA or HMA turns down; bearish regular divergence in RSI, MACD, WaveTrend, Stochastic, RVI, or OBV; RSI overbought or crosses below 50 within the current or prior two bars; MACD histogram turns down above zero or MACD crosses below its signal above zero; WaveTrend crosses below its signal above 10; Stochastic crosses below its signal; open above the upper Bollinger Band; ATR trailing-stop direction reverses bearish; RVI volatility condition; HMA crosses below any support/resistance level with close < close two bars ago; CMF crosses below or is below zero; VWAP crosses below HMA or shorter EMA is below VWAP; bearish engulfing candle.
Calculation: Long-term and shorter EMA = EMAs of close, with the long-term EMA lookback longer than the shorter EMA lookback.
Calculation: HMA = Hull moving average of close.
Calculation: WaveTrend = EMA( (HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, n)) / (0.015 × EMA(abs(HLC3 − EMA(HLC3, n)), n)), m); its signal is the simple moving average of WaveTrend over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: ATR trailing-stop direction = bullish when close exceeds the prior short stop; bearish when close falls below the prior long stop; otherwise retain the prior direction. Stops use prior stop values and ATR, with the long stop ratcheting upward and the short stop ratcheting downward.
Calculation: Support/resistance levels = the latest three confirmed pivot highs and pivot lows of close using a longer left lookback and a right lookback, plus the latest pivot high and low using the same left lookback and a shorter right lookback.
Calculation: Bullish regular divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot low is higher than the prior confirmed pivot low while the corresponding price low is lower; the prior pivot is 5–60 bars earlier. Bearish regular divergence = a confirmed oscillator pivot high is lower than the prior confirmed pivot high while the corresponding price high is higher; the prior pivot is 5–60 bars earlier. Confirm pivots with 6 bars left and 2 bars right; use RSI, MACD, WaveTrend, Stochastic %K, RVI, or OBV oscillator respectively. MACD divergences additionally require both pivot values below zero for bullish divergence or above zero for bearish divergence.
Calculation: RVI volatility condition = RVI > 25 and prior RVI < 40 (bullish), or RVI < 75 and prior RVI > 60 (bearish).
Calculation: Long RSI filter = RSI(close, 20) > EMA(RSI(close, 20), 40) − 1.8.
Calculation: Short RSI filter = RSI(close, 20) < EMA(RSI(close, 20), 40) + 1.8.
Calculation: CCI trigger = CCI(close, 2) crosses above its EMA(2) (bullish) or below it (bearish).
Calculation: Adjusted CCI = CCI(close, 2), adjusted by −25 on a bullish candle with body > 0.2 or +25 on a bearish candle with body > 0.2; require adjusted CCI = unadjusted CCI.
Calculation: HalfTrend reversal = the custom HalfTrend direction changes to bullish or bearish; use the most recent reversal direction and bars since each reversal.
Calculation: Long setup 5 = bullish CCI trigger AND prior CCI < 35 AND bullish CCI trigger AND prior CCI < −55 AND adjusted CCI = CCI AND the most recent HalfTrend reversal is bullish and occurred more recently than the bearish reversal, with at least one bar since it.
Calculation: Short setup 5 = bearish CCI trigger AND prior CCI > 65 AND bearish CCI trigger AND prior CCI > 55 AND adjusted CCI = CCI AND the most recent HalfTrend reversal is bearish and occurred more recently than the bullish reversal, with at least one bar since it.
Calculation: Long setup 6 = prior RSI(close, 2) < 33 AND bullish CCI trigger AND prior CCI < −55 AND adjusted CCI = CCI.
Calculation: Short setup 6 = prior RSI(close, 2) > 67 AND bearish CCI trigger AND prior CCI > 55 AND adjusted CCI = CCI.
Calculation: Exit stochastic = EMA(Stochastic(RSI(close, 21), 21), 3); its signal is EMA of that value over 3 bars.
Long: (Long setup 5 OR Long RSI filter OR Long setup 6) OR bullish score ≥ weak-long threshold OR bullish score ≥ strong-long threshold and (alternating signals are disabled OR prior signal was not long) AND (trend filter is disabled OR long-term EMA is rising).
Short: (Short setup 5 AND Short RSI filter) OR Short setup 6 OR bearish score ≥ weak-short threshold OR bearish score ≥ strong-short threshold and (alternating signals are disabled OR prior signal was not short) AND (trend filter is disabled OR long-term EMA is falling).
Exit: Long, bearish score ≥ weak-short threshold AND exit stochastic < its signal AND NOT (Long setup 5 OR Long RSI filter OR Long setup 6).
Exit: Short, bullish score ≥ weak-long threshold AND exit stochastic > its signal AND NOT ((Short setup 5 AND Short RSI filter) OR Short setup 6).
