Calculation: Signed volume = sum over completed lower-timeframe sub-bars within the current chart bar of sub-bar volume × (2 × (close − low) / (high − low) − 1); use 0 for a zero-range sub-bar. If lower-timeframe data is unavailable or disabled, use chart-bar volume × (2 × Φ((close − previous close) / stdev(close − previous close, lookback)) − 1), where Φ is the standard normal CDF.
Calculation: Detrended cumulative signed volume = cumulative sum of signed volume − its EMA over the detrending lookback.
Calculation: Selected flow oscillator = detrended cumulative signed volume divided by its rolling standard deviation or its robust z-score using rolling median and median absolute deviation; alternatively, RSI of detrended cumulative signed volume; alternatively, MFI computed from hlc3 × volume, summing positive flow when hlc3 rises and negative flow when hlc3 falls over the MFI lookback. Apply the selected smoothing method and lookback if smoothing is enabled.
Calculation: Overbought/oversold thresholds = selected thresholds for normalized cumulative volume (positive/negative), RSI (70/30), or MFI (80/20); extreme thresholds = selected extreme levels for normalized cumulative volume (positive/negative), or 90/10 for RSI or MFI.
Calculation: Confirmed price swing high/low = high/low greater/less than the surrounding pivot-length chart bars on both sides; known after the right-side bars complete. For each price pivot, its flow peak/trough = highest/lowest selected flow oscillator in the 2 × pivot-length + 1 chart-bar window centered on that pivot. Compare with the previous confirmed pivot of the same type, provided the pivot separation is no greater than the maximum gap.
Calculation: Flow divergence minimum gap = magnitude threshold × rolling standard deviation of the selected flow oscillator over the divergence-gap lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed oscillator peak/trough = selected flow oscillator greater/less than the surrounding reversal-pivot-length chart bars on both sides; known after the right-side bars complete.
Long: confirmed price swing low < previous confirmed price swing low AND flow trough > previous flow trough AND flow trough − previous flow trough ≥ flow divergence minimum gap AND (extreme-zone gate disabled OR flow trough ≤ oversold threshold) OR hidden divergences enabled AND confirmed price swing low > previous confirmed price swing low AND flow trough < previous flow trough AND previous flow trough − flow trough ≥ flow divergence minimum gap OR confirmed oscillator trough ≤ lower extreme threshold.
Short: confirmed price swing high > previous confirmed price swing high AND flow peak < previous flow peak AND previous flow peak − flow peak ≥ flow divergence minimum gap AND (extreme-zone gate disabled OR flow peak ≥ overbought threshold) OR hidden divergences enabled AND confirmed price swing high < previous confirmed price swing high AND flow peak > previous flow peak AND flow peak − previous flow peak ≥ flow divergence minimum gap OR confirmed oscillator peak ≥ upper extreme threshold.
