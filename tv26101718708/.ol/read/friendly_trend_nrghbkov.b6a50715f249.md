Calculation: Normalized impulse = EMA(change in HLC3, shorter lookback) / EMA(absolute change in HLC3, same lookback).
Calculation: Adaptive basis = slow EMA of HLC3 + (fast EMA of HLC3 − slow EMA) × clamp(0.60 × directional efficiency + 0.40 × absolute normalized impulse, 0, 1); directional efficiency = absolute HLC3 change over the long lookback / (mean absolute HLC3 change over that lookback × lookback length). Fast EMA lookback is shorter than slow EMA lookback.
Calculation: Adaptive band = (0.65 × RMA(True Range, range lookback) + 0.35 × EMA(absolute HLC3 − adaptive basis, same range lookback)) × band multiplier × clamp(short-term EMA(True Range) / long-term EMA(True Range), 0.75, 1.35). Range and short-term noise lookbacks are shorter than the long-term noise lookback; short-term noise lookback is shorter than the range lookback.
Calculation: Confirmed trend state = bullish when close > adaptive basis + adaptive band × reversal buffer, normalized impulse > momentum threshold, and adaptive basis > its value at a shorter slope lookback; bearish when the corresponding opposite comparisons hold. The setup must hold for the required consecutive completed bars. State persists until the opposite setup confirms. Evaluate chart state on completed chart bars; evaluate other-timeframe states on the last completed bar, including the last completed lower-timeframe state when that timeframe is below the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Overall score = duration-weighted average of selected-timeframe states (+1 bullish, −1 bearish, 0 neutral), with each timeframe weighted by log(1 + its duration / the shortest selected duration).
Long: chart or any selected-timeframe confirmed trend state = +1 OR overall score > its threshold.
Short: chart or any selected-timeframe confirmed trend state = −1 OR overall score < the negative of its threshold.
