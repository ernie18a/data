Calculation: MACD histogram = MACD line (short-term EMA of close minus long-term EMA of close) minus its signal line (EMA of the MACD line); calculate on the configured MACD timeframe, using the last completed bar in realtime
Calculation: Negative histogram average = mean absolute value of negative MACD histogram bars over its lookback
Calculation: Positive histogram average = mean value of positive MACD histogram bars over its lookback
Calculation: RSI = relative strength index of close over its lookback, on the configured RSI timeframe
Calculation: Negative-candle count = count of bars with close < open over the pullback lookback
Calculation: Positive-candle count = count of bars with close > open over the pullback lookback
Long: (EMA mode is fast-versus-slow and fast EMA >= slow EMA and bars since fast EMA crossed above slow EMA <= cross-validity lookback OR EMA mode is close-versus-slow and close > slow EMA and bars since close crossed above slow EMA <= cross-validity lookback OR EMA mode is close-versus-fast and close > fast EMA and bars since close crossed above fast EMA <= cross-validity lookback) AND MACD histogram < 0 AND abs(MACD histogram) > minimum histogram magnitude AND (abs(MACD histogram) is the highest over its lookback now OR it was the lowest over its lookback on the prior bar) AND (histogram-average filter disabled OR abs(MACD histogram) >= configured multiplier × negative histogram average) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is within the configured long range) AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR RSI is rising over its lookback) AND (pullback filter disabled OR negative-candle count >= configured minimum)
Short: (EMA mode is fast-versus-slow and fast EMA < slow EMA and bars since fast EMA crossed below slow EMA <= cross-validity lookback OR EMA mode is close-versus-slow and close < slow EMA and bars since close crossed below slow EMA <= cross-validity lookback OR EMA mode is close-versus-fast and close < fast EMA and bars since close crossed below fast EMA <= cross-validity lookback) AND MACD histogram >= 0 AND MACD histogram > minimum histogram magnitude AND (MACD histogram is the highest over its lookback now OR it was the highest over its lookback on the prior bar) AND (histogram-average filter disabled OR MACD histogram >= configured multiplier × positive histogram average) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI is within the configured short range) AND (RSI direction filter disabled OR RSI is falling over its lookback) AND (pullback filter disabled OR positive-candle count >= configured minimum)
Exit: long, bar high >= entry price × (1 + configured profit fraction) OR bar low <= entry price × (1 − configured stop fraction)
Exit: short, bar low <= entry price × (1 − configured profit fraction) OR bar high >= entry price × (1 + configured stop fraction)
