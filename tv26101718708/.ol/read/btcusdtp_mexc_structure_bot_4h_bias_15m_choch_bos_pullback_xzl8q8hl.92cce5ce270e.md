Calculation: Confirmed higher-timeframe swing high/low = high/low pivot with the configured number of bars on each side; use higher-timeframe bars and only confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = bullish after a completed higher-timeframe close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high plus its BOS buffer times higher-timeframe ATR; bearish after a close falls below the latest confirmed swing low minus its BOS buffer times higher-timeframe ATR; otherwise retain the prior bias.
Calculation: Confirmed chart-timeframe swing high/low = high/low pivot with the configured number of bars on each side; use only confirmed pivots.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe break up/down = chart close exceeds the latest confirmed swing high plus its BOS buffer times chart ATR / falls below the latest confirmed swing low minus its BOS buffer times chart ATR.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe structure trend = direction of the most recent valid chart-timeframe break; initially neutral.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe change of character up/down = break up/down while the prior structure trend is bearish/bullish. Continuation break up/down = break up/down while the prior structure trend is bullish/bearish. Update the trend after evaluating these conditions.
Calculation: Long limit price = lowest low over the configured chart-bar lookback plus the configured fraction of the distance from that low to the current high.
Calculation: Short limit price = highest high over the configured chart-bar lookback minus the configured fraction of the distance from the current low to that high.
Calculation: Long stop = latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing low, or the lookback low if unavailable, minus the configured buffer times chart ATR. Short stop = latest confirmed chart-timeframe swing high, or the lookback high if unavailable, plus the configured buffer times chart ATR.
Long: Higher-timeframe bias is bullish AND a chart-timeframe change of character up has occurred while that bias is bullish AND a subsequent chart-timeframe continuation break up occurs AND no position or pending entry exists; place a limit entry at the long limit price.
Short: Higher-timeframe bias is bearish AND a chart-timeframe change of character down has occurred while that bias is bearish AND a subsequent chart-timeframe continuation break down occurs AND no position or pending entry exists; place a limit entry at the short limit price.
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop OR high >= entry price + first target R multiple * (entry price - initial stop) OR high >= entry price + second target R multiple * (entry price - initial stop).
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop OR low <= entry price - first target R multiple * (initial stop - entry price) OR low <= entry price - second target R multiple * (initial stop - entry price).
