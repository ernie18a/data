Calculation: Pivot high/low = a bar’s high/low is greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on both sides; use the latest confirmed pivot high/low.
Calculation: Momentum z-score = (current close change − 50-bar average close change) / 50-bar standard deviation of close change.
Calculation: Bullish order block = the high and low of the first bearish bar among the 10 bars before a bullish market-structure break; bearish order block = the high and low of the first bullish bar among the 10 bars before a bearish market-structure break. A bullish break occurs when close crosses above the latest pivot high and momentum z-score exceeds a positive threshold; a bearish break occurs when close crosses below the latest pivot low and momentum z-score is below the corresponding negative threshold. Clear the broken pivot after its break. An order block is active until a bullish block’s low falls below its bottom or a bearish block’s high rises above its top.
Calculation: Active stop for a long = the greater of entry price minus the hard-stop distance and the dynamic stop; for a short = the lesser of entry price plus the hard-stop distance and the dynamic stop. For a long, once close reaches entry price plus the break-even trigger, initialize the dynamic stop at entry price and thereafter raise it to the greater of its prior value and close minus 2.5 ATR. For a short, once close reaches entry price minus the break-even trigger, initialize it at entry price and thereafter lower it to the lesser of its prior value and close plus 2.5 ATR. ATR uses a 14-bar lookback.
Long: close crosses above the latest pivot high AND momentum z-score exceeds a positive threshold OR position size = 0 AND at least one active bullish order block exists AND low <= its top AND close > its bottom
Short: close crosses below the latest pivot low AND momentum z-score is below the corresponding negative threshold OR position size = 0 AND at least one active bearish order block exists AND high >= its bottom AND close < its top
Exit: long, high >= entry price + target-profit distance OR low <= active stop for a long
Exit: short, low <= entry price − target-profit distance OR high >= active stop for a short
