Calculation: USD liquidity index = daily close of USCBBS divided by 1 billion, minus daily close of RRPONTSYD, minus daily close of WTREGEN; use the last completed daily bar (higher timeframe than the chart timeframe)
Calculation: Top line = nearest-rank upper percentile of the USD liquidity index over a lookback; Bot line = nearest-rank lower percentile over the same lookback
Long: Bot line > its previous value OR Top line > its previous value
Short: USD liquidity index crosses below both the Top line and the Bot line
Exit: Long, USD liquidity index crosses below both the Top line and the Bot line OR Top line < its previous value
Exit: Short, USD liquidity index crosses above both the Top line and the Bot line
