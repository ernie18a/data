Calculation: Bullish breakout = After a candle with range > ATR × impulse-strength threshold, begin a compression at the first candle whose range < ATR × compression threshold; track the highest high across small-range compression candles. After at least the minimum stall count, a close above that high with body/range ≥ minimum body fraction is a bullish breakout. The breakout direction is independent of the impulse direction.
Calculation: Bearish breakout = Same impulse and compression sequence as for a bullish breakout, tracking the lowest low; after at least the minimum stall count, a close below that low with body/range ≥ minimum body fraction is a bearish breakout.
Long: Previous bar was a bullish breakout AND current close > current open AND current close > the longer-term EMA AND current close > VWAP AND position is flat
Short: Previous bar was a bearish breakout AND current close < current open AND current close < the longer-term EMA AND current close < VWAP AND position is flat
Exit: Long, close ≥ entry price + first profit distance OR close ≥ entry price + second profit distance OR price reaches the long trailing stop OR stop loss is enabled AND price ≤ entry price − stop distance
Exit: Short, close ≤ entry price − first profit distance OR close ≤ entry price − second profit distance OR price reaches the short trailing stop OR stop loss is enabled AND price ≥ entry price + stop distance
