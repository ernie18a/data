Calculation: EMA stack = fast EMA > mid EMA > slow EMA for bullish alignment, with periods ordered shortest to longest; reverse the inequalities for bearish alignment.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA stack = the same fast-to-slow EMA alignment on a higher timeframe, using that timeframe’s current bar (which may still be forming).
Calculation: Bull score = count of: bullish EMA stack; close above VWAP and VWAP above its value several chart bars earlier; bullish higher-timeframe EMA stack (or filter disabled); RSI within its configured bullish zone; MACD line above signal line with histogram rising; qualified volume.
Calculation: Bear score = count of the corresponding bearish conditions: bearish EMA stack; close below VWAP and VWAP below its value several chart bars earlier; bearish higher-timeframe EMA stack (or filter disabled); RSI within its configured bearish zone; MACD line below signal line with histogram falling; qualified volume.
Calculation: Qualified volume = volume at least its configured multiple of its longer-period average, and, when expansion is required, volume greater than each of the previous two chart bars.
Calculation: Trend agreement = at least two of EMA stack alignment, close on the corresponding side of VWAP, and higher-timeframe EMA stack alignment (or disabled higher-timeframe filter).
Calculation: Candle body fraction = absolute(close − open) / (high − low), or zero when high equals low.
Calculation: Bullish pattern = any enabled condition: bullish engulfing (previous candle bearish, current candle bullish, current body larger, current close above previous open, and current open at or below previous close); hammer (lower wick greater than configured multiple of body, upper wick less than configured fraction of body, body fraction below configured maximum, and close at or above open); breakout (close above the highest high of the configured number of preceding bars, close above open, and body fraction above configured minimum); support bounce (support touched within configured proximity, close above open, body fraction above configured minimum, and close above candle midpoint); or spring (low sweeps below the lowest low of a configured lookback, then within the configured bar limit close rises above the swept level, close above open, and volume is qualified).
Calculation: Bearish pattern = any enabled condition: bearish engulfing (previous candle bullish, current candle bearish, current body larger, current close below previous open, and current open at or above previous close); shooting star (upper wick greater than configured multiple of body, lower wick less than configured fraction of body, body fraction below configured maximum, and close at or below open); breakdown (close below the lowest low of the configured number of preceding bars, close below open, and body fraction above configured minimum); resistance rejection (resistance touched within configured proximity, close below open, body fraction above configured minimum, and close below candle midpoint); or upthrust (high sweeps above the highest high of a configured lookback, then within the configured bar limit close falls below the swept level, close below open, and volume is qualified).
Calculation: Support levels = previous day low and close, current-day low, mid EMA, VWAP, previous-week low, pre-session low, opening-range low, and last confirmed swing low. Resistance levels use the corresponding highs plus previous-day close, mid EMA, and VWAP. A level is touched when the bar’s low (support) or high (resistance) is within the configured percentage proximity of it. Current-day, session, and opening-range levels accumulate from their respective session bars; swing levels use configured left and right pivot lookbacks.
Calculation: Entry signal cooldown = the configured minimum number of chart bars since the previous signal of the same direction; MOC signals use a separate cooldown shared by both directions.
Calculation: Long entry signal = bull score at least the configured B-tier minimum and bullish pattern, trend agreement if strict filtering is enabled, cooldown satisfied, and either the allowed non-MOC trading window or the MOC conditions: MOC window, qualified volume, close clear of previous-day-high restriction, and no same-bar regular long signal.
Calculation: Short entry signal = bear score at least the configured B-tier minimum and bearish pattern, trend agreement if strict filtering is enabled, cooldown satisfied, and either the allowed non-MOC trading window or the MOC conditions: MOC window, qualified volume, close clear of previous-day-low restriction, and no same-bar regular short signal.
Calculation: Long reversal-zone marking = no position, a short signal occurred within the configured recent-signal lookback, bull score meets its configured minimum, bullish pattern, trend agreement, and allowed non-MOC trading window.
Calculation: Short reversal-zone marking = no position, a long signal occurred within the configured recent-signal lookback, bear score meets its configured minimum, bearish pattern, trend agreement, and allowed non-MOC trading window.
Calculation: Entry risk = entry price minus initial long stop for a long position, or initial short stop minus entry price for a short position. Initial stop uses entry price offset by configured ATR multiple when ATR stops are enabled; otherwise use the configured offset from the minimum of current low, previous low, and mid EMA for longs, or maximum of current high, previous high, and mid EMA for shorts. First and second targets are entry price plus configured risk multiples for longs and minus configured risk multiples for shorts.
Calculation: After the first target is reached, mark it hit and move the stop to entry price.
Long: Long entry signal OR spring condition OR long reversal-zone marking.
Short: Short entry signal OR upthrust condition OR short reversal-zone marking.
Exit: long position, high reaches or exceeds the first target before it has been hit (partial exit).
Exit: short position, low reaches or falls below the first target before it has been hit (partial exit).
Exit: long position, high reaches or exceeds the second target OR low reaches or falls below the current stop OR EMA stack is bearish AND close is below VWAP AND bearish pattern OR configured time stop is reached.
Exit: short position, low reaches or falls below the second target OR high reaches or exceeds the current stop OR EMA stack is bullish AND close is above VWAP AND bullish pattern OR configured time stop is reached.
Exit: long position, bear score meets the configured reversal-warning minimum AND bearish pattern AND (close below VWAP OR bearish EMA stack).
Exit: short position, bull score meets the configured reversal-warning minimum AND bullish pattern AND (close above VWAP OR bullish EMA stack).
