Calculation: Bollinger Band width = (upper band − lower band) / middle band
Calculation: Smoothed ROC = EMA of the percentage change in close over a shorter lookback
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = highest value of (high − ATR × multiplier) over a lookback; initialize to this value, then retain the prior stop unless close > prior stop AND close > prior close, when it updates to this value; reset to this value when close < stop
Long: (flat AND a qualifying signal occurred within the short eligibility window AND no close cross below the short-term EMA has reset it AND close > long-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND smoothed ROC >= 0 AND long-term EMA slope >= 0 AND close > ATR trailing stop) OR (long AND ((close is above average entry price by more than a gain threshold AND close > long-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND long-term EMA slope >= 0 AND close > ATR trailing stop AND open trades below the entry limit) OR (low crosses below short-term EMA AND close > short-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND long-term EMA slope >= 0 AND close > ATR trailing stop)), where a qualifying signal is (close crosses above short-term EMA OR (close > prior high AND close > short-term EMA)) AND volume > short-term average volume AND (Bollinger Band width < its longer-term average OR a trend-death exit occurred within the short Phoenix window)
Exit: long, close < ATR trailing stop OR close crosses below long-term EMA
