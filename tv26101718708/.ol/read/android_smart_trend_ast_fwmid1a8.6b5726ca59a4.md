Calculation: Z-score = (value − lookback mean) / lookback standard deviation; use 0 when standard deviation is 0
Calculation: Range-normalized value = 100 × (value − lookback low) / (lookback high − lookback low); use 50 when high equals low
Calculation: Current-timeframe trend score = 0.40 × organ score + 0.25 × band-position score + 0.20 × EMA-ribbon score + 0.15 × RSI(14), clamped to 0–100. Organ score = clamp(EMA-smoothed sum of 2 × Z-score of long-lookback EMA slope + 1.5 × Z-score of short-lookback close ROC + Z-score of OBV change + 0.5 × Z-score of signed ATR change, mapped by organ / 6 × 50 + 50, to 0–100). OBV change is the change in cumulative volume signed by the direction of close change. Signed ATR change is ATR change multiplied by +1 when close is above EMA(20), otherwise −1. Band-position score uses EMA-smoothed mirrored upper and lower bands around HL2: upper/lower = HL2 × (1 ± band percentage × mirror factor); score = clamp(50 + 50 × (close − band midpoint) / band range, 0–100), with band range floored at minimum tick. EMA-ribbon score is 65 when EMA(21) > EMA(55), 35 when EMA(21) < EMA(55), otherwise 50. RSI score is RSI(14).
Calculation: PVM score = 0.40 × EMA(9) of (55-bar correlation of close change and volume change + 1) × 50 + 0.35 × EMA(9) of the 100-bar range-normalized product of ATR(14)/EMA(ATR(14), 34) and volume/EMA(volume, 34) + 0.25 × EMA(9) of the 100-bar range-normalized close ROC(14)
Calculation: Power = clamp(blend(current-timeframe trend score and PVM score with their respective multi-timeframe scores when multi-timeframe power is enabled; otherwise use current-timeframe scores only). Each multi-timeframe score is 0.30 × fast + 0.35 × mid + 0.35 × macro. Each uses the last completed bar of its configured timeframe; relative to the chart timeframe, each may be higher or lower depending on configuration. When PVM boost is enabled, blend the trend and PVM results using the configured PVM weight; otherwise use the trend result. Clamp to 0–100.
Calculation: Trend direction = initially neutral; set to long on a long signal and short on a short signal, and retain the direction until the opposite signal.
Long: Power ≥ bullish power threshold AND trend direction ≠ long
Short: Power ≤ bearish power threshold AND trend direction ≠ short
