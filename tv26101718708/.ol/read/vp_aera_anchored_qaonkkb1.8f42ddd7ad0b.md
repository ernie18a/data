Calculation: Last confirmed swing high/low = latest confirmed pivot high/low using the same configurable left/right bar lookback on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Recent upward/downward structure break = a chart-timeframe close above the last confirmed swing high / below the last confirmed swing low within a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Anchored volume-profile POC = price row with the greatest volume in the selected profile window; VAH and VAL = upper and lower boundaries of the value area expanded from POC until it contains the selected share of window volume.
Calculation: Value-area height = VAH − VAL.
Calculation: Long level test = current low is within configurable tolerance × value-area height of VAL or POC.
Calculation: Short level test = current high is within configurable tolerance × value-area height of VAH or POC.
Long: Current chart bar is confirmed AND (if liquidity sweep is required: current low < last confirmed swing low AND current close > last confirmed swing low AND current close > current open; otherwise: current close > last confirmed swing low AND current close > current open AND current close > prior close) AND (if structure confirmation is required: an upward structure break occurred within the configurable recent-break lookback) AND (if the value-area test is required: the long level test is true, or profile levels are unavailable) AND (if the EMA trend filter is enabled: current close > EMA) AND enough chart bars have elapsed since the previous signal to satisfy the configurable signal cooldown.
Short: Current chart bar is confirmed AND (if liquidity sweep is required: current high > last confirmed swing high AND current close < last confirmed swing high AND current close < current open; otherwise: current close < last confirmed swing high AND current close < current open AND current close < prior close) AND (if structure confirmation is required: a downward structure break occurred within the configurable recent-break lookback) AND (if the value-area test is required: the short level test is true, or profile levels are unavailable) AND (if the EMA trend filter is enabled: current close < EMA) AND enough chart bars have elapsed since the previous signal to satisfy the configurable signal cooldown AND no long signal occurs on the same bar.
