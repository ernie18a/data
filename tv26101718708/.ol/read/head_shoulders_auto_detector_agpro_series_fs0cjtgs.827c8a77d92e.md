Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that remains the highest/lowest across the configured number of bars on both sides; it becomes available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: Neckline = the line interpolated through the two most recent opposing pivots between the left shoulder and head, and between the head and right shoulder.
Calculation: Pattern symmetry score = 50% × [100 × (1 − |left-to-head span − head-to-right span| / max(span lengths))] + 50% × clamp[100 × (1 − shoulder price difference / max(head extension beyond both shoulders, ATR)), 0, 100].
Calculation: TP1 distance = either the absolute distance from the head to the average of the two neckline pivots, or the absolute distance from the head to the interpolated neckline at the head; use the selected method.
Long: A new confirmed pivot allows a valid inverse head-and-shoulders pattern: the latest three confirmed pivot lows form left shoulder, head, and right shoulder in that order; the head is below both shoulders; its extension beyond each shoulder is at least the configured ATR multiple; shoulder price difference is within its configured ATR tolerance; each shoulder-to-head span is at least the pivot lookback and less than 200 bars; the nearest confirmed pivot highs in the two intervening spans both exist and differ by no more than the configured neckline ATR tolerance; and the pattern symmetry score meets its configured minimum. Emit no more than once per configured two pivot-lookback lengths since the prior pattern. OR, while this pattern is forming and not invalidated or expired, close > current interpolated neckline after the right-shoulder pivot.
Short: A new confirmed pivot allows a valid head-and-shoulders pattern: the latest three confirmed pivot highs form left shoulder, head, and right shoulder in that order; the head is above both shoulders; its extension beyond each shoulder is at least the configured ATR multiple; shoulder price difference is within its configured ATR tolerance; each shoulder-to-head span is at least the pivot lookback and less than 200 bars; the nearest confirmed pivot lows in the two intervening spans both exist and differ by no more than the configured neckline ATR tolerance; and the pattern symmetry score meets its configured minimum. Emit no more than once per configured two pivot-lookback lengths since the prior pattern. OR, while this pattern is forming and not invalidated or expired, close < current interpolated neckline after the right-shoulder pivot.
Exit: Long, low >= neckline at confirmation + TP1 distance OR low <= head price − configured ATR stop buffer × ATR at confirmation
Exit: Short, high <= neckline at confirmation − TP1 distance OR high >= head price + configured ATR stop buffer × ATR at confirmation
