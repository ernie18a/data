Calculation: Realized volatility = standard deviation of close-to-close log returns, annualized and expressed as a percentage, using the standard lookback
Calculation: Premium spread = smoothed implied volatility minus realized volatility
Calculation: IV rank = (smoothed implied volatility − its trailing lookback low) / (its trailing lookback high − its trailing lookback low) × 100
Short: An implied-volatility index is available AND premium spread is at or above the rich-premium threshold AND IV rank is above the high-rank cutoff
