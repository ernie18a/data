Calculation: Prior-day high/low = high/low of the last completed daily bar; daily ATR = average true range calculated from daily bars (higher timeframe, current bar still forming).
Calculation: Opening-range high/low = highest high/lowest low during the session’s opening range.
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume divided by its moving average over a shorter lookback.
Calculation: Close location = (close − low) / (high − low).
Calculation: Execution EMA pair = shorter-lookback and longer-lookback EMAs of the chart’s hl2; structure EMA pair = longer-lookback EMAs of hl2 than the execution pair.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA pairs = EMAs using the execution and structure lookbacks on timeframes higher than the chart timeframe; use the bar still forming.
Calculation: Relative strength = instrument return since prior daily close minus benchmark return since prior daily close.
Calculation: Mapped setup levels = configured long/short trigger, confirmation, invalidation, hard-invalidation, target, and action-zone levels; event setups may replace these with opening-range levels.
Long: A long setup is mapped AND the trigger is accepted by consecutive closes above it OR by a close above confirmation with sufficient relative volume and close location; AND the trigger is retested from above, the long action zone is touched and reclaimed, or a qualifying high-volume impulse/confirmation occurs; AND close is above VWAP; AND execution and structure EMA pairs are bullish and required higher-timeframe EMA pairs are bullish; AND RSI has bullish momentum without exhaustion; AND relative strength, volume, expected reward-to-risk, no-chase, Strat, and surface-direction filters pass; AND the session, map, operational-integrity, and market gates pass; AND the long quality score meets its threshold and cooldown/one-signal-per-session rules permit it.
Short: A short setup is mapped AND the trigger is accepted by consecutive closes below it OR by a close below confirmation with sufficient relative volume and close location; AND the trigger is retested from below, the short action zone is touched and rejected, or a qualifying high-volume impulse/confirmation occurs; AND close is below VWAP; AND execution and structure EMA pairs are bearish and required higher-timeframe EMA pairs are bearish; AND RSI has bearish momentum without exhaustion; AND relative weakness, volume, expected reward-to-risk, no-chase, Strat, and surface-direction filters pass; AND the session, map, operational-integrity, and market gates pass; AND the short quality score meets its threshold and cooldown/one-signal-per-session rules permit it. If both directions qualify, choose the higher score; ties choose long.
Exit: Long, low reaches the long invalidation before target 1 OR high reaches target 1 (partial, move stop to entry) OR high reaches target 2 (partial, trail remainder) OR high reaches target 3 after target 2 OR, after target 1 on an earlier bar and before target 3, low reaches entry price.
Exit: Short, high reaches the short invalidation before target 1 OR low reaches target 1 (partial, move stop to entry) OR low reaches target 2 (partial, trail remainder) OR low reaches target 3 after target 2 OR, after target 1 on an earlier bar and before target 3, high reaches entry price.
