Calculation: Structure high/low = highest high / lowest low over the preceding structure lookback bars, excluding the current bar
Calculation: Bullish/bearish BOS = confirmed close above structure high + ATR buffer / below structure low − ATR buffer, with the preceding close at or below / at or above the corresponding preceding-bar threshold
Calculation: Bullish/bearish displacement = either a same-direction candle with body at least the ATR body threshold, body-to-range ratio at least the minimum, and close in the outer 20% of its range; or at least two same-direction candles among the last three, current close beyond the close two bars ago, and movement from the three-bar low / to the three-bar high at least the ATR leg threshold
Calculation: Recent confirmation = the condition occurred within its configured lookback: bullish/bearish fair value gap (current low > high two bars ago / current high < low two bars ago), bullish/bearish candle pattern, volume expansion (volume divided by its moving average reaches the expansion threshold), or bullish/bearish liquidity sweep (low below structure low and close back above / high above structure high and close back below)
Calculation: Bullish/bearish quality = base quality plus configured bonuses for recent fair value gap, candle pattern, volume expansion, and same-direction liquidity sweep
Long: Confirmed bullish BOS AND bullish displacement occurred within the displacement memory AND quality meets the minimum AND each enabled confirmation filter has a recent bullish confirmation AND a bearish source candle exists within the opposite-candle search lookback AND its origin has not already produced a bullish zone AND the zone passes the enabled size filter
Short: Confirmed bearish BOS AND bearish displacement occurred within the displacement memory AND quality meets the minimum AND each enabled confirmation filter has a recent bearish confirmation (volume expansion may be shared) AND a bullish source candle exists within the opposite-candle search lookback AND its origin has not already produced a bearish zone AND the zone passes the enabled size filter
