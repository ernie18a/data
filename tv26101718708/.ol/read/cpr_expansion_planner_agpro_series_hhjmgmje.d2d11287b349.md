Calculation: CPR = from the last completed bar of the selected higher timeframe: pivot = (high + low + close) / 3; bottom/top = lower/higher of (high + low) / 2 and 2 × pivot − (high + low) / 2.
Calculation: Active side = selected bullish or bearish side; in automatic mode, bullish if close > CPR top, bearish if close < CPR bottom, otherwise bullish if current higher-timeframe anchor open > CPR midpoint, bearish if it is < midpoint, otherwise bullish if close ≥ midpoint and bearish otherwise.
Calculation: Expansion score = rounded 0–100 clipped weighted sum: 24% narrow-CPR-width score, 16% anchor-open proximity score, 25% acceptance score, 18% range-expansion score, and 17% target-room score. Each component is normalized to 0–100 using its configured thresholds; lower CPR width and open distance score higher, while accepted-close count, distance beyond CPR, range expansion, and target room score higher as they increase.
Calculation: Accepted closes = consecutive chart-timeframe closes beyond the CPR edge on the active side; acceptance score = 62% normalized accepted-close count plus 38% normalized distance beyond that edge.
Calculation: Range expansion = 72% normalized ratio of current anchor range to the greater of prior anchor range and ATR, plus 28% normalized current anchor range against the average range of prior higher-timeframe anchor bars.
Calculation: Target room = distance from close to target rail in the active direction, in ATR units; target rail is beyond the active CPR edge by the greater of configured ATR room, a configured multiple of prior anchor range, or five CPR widths.
Long: active side is bullish AND close > CPR top AND accepted closes meet the configured minimum AND confirmation-mode close, volume, and strict filters pass AND expansion score ≥ configured ready threshold AND close is not beyond the bullish invalidation rail AND target room ≥ 0.35 ATR.
Short: active side is bearish AND close < CPR bottom AND accepted closes meet the configured minimum AND confirmation-mode close, volume, and strict filters pass AND expansion score ≥ configured ready threshold AND close is not beyond the bearish invalidation rail AND target room ≥ 0.35 ATR.
Long: active side is bullish AND close > CPR top AND expansion score ≥ configured watch threshold AND the Long condition is false AND close is not beyond the bullish invalidation rail.
Short: active side is bearish AND close < CPR bottom AND expansion score ≥ configured watch threshold AND the Short condition is false AND close is not beyond the bearish invalidation rail.
