Calculation: Previous-day value profile = aggregate the last completed higher-timeframe daily session’s chart bars; distribute each bar’s volume evenly across the price bins touched by its low–high range; POC is the bin midpoint with greatest volume; expand the value area outward from POC, adding the higher-volume adjacent bin first, until the configured fraction of volume is included; VAH and VAL are the resulting upper and lower boundaries. Previous-day high/low = maximum high/minimum low of that same completed daily session.
Calculation: ATR = average true range; use the configured lookback.
Calculation: Volume percentile = current volume’s percentile rank over the configured lookback; ATR percentile = current ATR’s percentile rank over the same configured lookback.
Calculation: Buy/sell pressure ratio = (2 × close − high − low) / max(high − low, minimum tick); when volume is unavailable, the participation gate is bypassed.
Calculation: Full-body candle = absolute(close − open) / max(high − low, minimum tick) ≥ configured acceptance fraction.
Calculation: Reversal quality score = 15% volume percentile + 15% clamp(50 + 250 × buy/sell pressure ratio for longs, or 50 − 250 × ratio for shorts, to 0–100) + 20% rejection score + 10% location score + 10% higher-timeframe score + 10% ATR-percentile score + 10% reward-to-risk score + 10% session score. Clamp component scores to 0–100. For a long reversal, rejection score = 65% × 100 × (close − low)/range + 35% × clamp(20 + 60 × (VAL − sweep low)/ATR, 0, 100); location score = clamp(100 − 35 × (close − VAL)/ATR, 0, 100). For a short reversal, use 65% × 100 × (high − close)/range + 35% × clamp(20 + 60 × (sweep high − VAH)/ATR, 0, 100), and location score = clamp(100 − 35 × (VAH − close)/ATR, 0, 100). For a long squeeze, rejection score = 50% × 100 × (close − low)/range + 50% × 100 × (1 − min(1, abs(low − broken level)/max(retest tolerance × ATR, minimum tick))); for a short squeeze use (high − close)/range and abs(high − broken level). Squeeze location score = clamp(100 − 35 × abs(close − broken level)/ATR, 0, 100). Higher-timeframe score is 100 aligned, 55 neutral, 15 opposed; determine bias by comparing close with the 20-period EMA on the last completed higher-timeframe bar. ATR-percentile score rises linearly from 0 to 100 below percentile 25, is 100 from percentile 25 through 90, then falls linearly to 40 at percentile 100. Reward-to-risk score = clamp(40 × reward/risk to TP1, 0, 100). Session score follows the configured session schedule.
Calculation: Entry risk = entry-to-stop distance. For reversal stops, use the sweep extreme beyond the configured ATR buffer; for squeeze stops, use the more extreme of the signal bar and prior bar, beyond the configured ATR buffer. Enforce the configured minimum stop distance in classic stop mode. Skip entries whose stop distance exceeds the configured maximum ATR multiple.
Long: Confirmed intraday bar; previous-day value profile available; inside configured session and entry window, outside opening lockout; no active trade; cooldown elapsed; no prior long signal that day; bullish candle; and either (low < VAL AND close > VAL AND no preceding close below VAL) OR (close crosses upward through VAL after 1 to configured-maximum consecutive closes below VAL) OR (an accepted full-body bullish close above VAH, followed within the configured retest window by low ≤ broken VAH + retest tolerance × ATR and close > broken VAH, with no intervening invalidation); for squeeze setups, chop is absent; if participation is required and volume is available, volume percentile ≥ configured minimum and buy/sell pressure ratio > configured positive threshold; reversal quality score ≥ configured minimum plus the chop increment and, for a reversal opposed to higher-timeframe bias, the configured countertrend increment; and stop distance ≤ configured maximum ATR multiple.
Short: Confirmed intraday bar; previous-day value profile available; inside configured session and entry window, outside opening lockout; no active trade; cooldown elapsed; no prior short signal that day; bearish candle; and either (high > VAH AND close < VAH AND no preceding close above VAH) OR (close crosses downward through VAH after 1 to configured-maximum consecutive closes above VAH) OR (an accepted full-body bearish close below VAL, followed within the configured retest window by high ≥ broken VAL − retest tolerance × ATR and close < broken VAL, with no intervening invalidation); for squeeze setups, chop is absent; if participation is required and volume is available, volume percentile ≥ configured minimum and buy/sell pressure ratio < the configured negative threshold; reversal quality score ≥ configured minimum plus the chop increment and, for a reversal opposed to higher-timeframe bias, the configured countertrend increment; and stop distance ≤ configured maximum ATR multiple.
Exit: Long, low ≤ stop price OR (TP1 not yet reached AND high ≥ TP1) OR (TP1 reached AND high ≥ TP2).
Exit: Short, high ≥ stop price OR (TP1 not yet reached AND low ≤ TP1) OR (TP1 reached AND low ≤ TP2).
