Calculation: Regime = Assign the current bar to the nearest K-means centroid using Euclidean distance across four features, each min-max normalized over the training lookback: absolute separation of the 21-period and 50-period EMAs divided by ATR; 20-period close standard deviation divided by close; high-low range over the feature lookback divided by ATR; and absolute change in the 21-period EMA over the feature lookback divided by ATR times that lookback. Train centroids on the training window, initializing them at evenly spaced offsets, then iteratively assign observations and replace each centroid with its assigned observations’ mean. Classify each centroid as Trend if its normalized separation plus normalized EMA-change score is at least both other scores; otherwise Volatile if its normalized standard-deviation score is at least the Range score; otherwise Range. The Range score is 1 minus normalized range score minus normalized separation score.
Calculation: Channel upper = For Range, midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the Range channel lookback, plus the greater of 0.85 times half that high-low span and ATR times the Range width multiplier. For Trend, linear-regression value of close over the Trend channel lookback plus the greater of the regression residual standard deviation and ATR times the Trend width multiplier. For Volatile, EMA of close over the Volatile channel lookback plus ATR times the Volatile width multiplier. Select by current regime.
Calculation: Channel lower = For Range, midpoint of the highest high and lowest low over the Range channel lookback, minus the greater of 0.85 times half that high-low span and ATR times the Range width multiplier. For Trend, linear-regression value of close over the Trend channel lookback minus the greater of the regression residual standard deviation and ATR times the Trend width multiplier. For Volatile, EMA of close over the Volatile channel lookback minus ATR times the Volatile width multiplier. Select by current regime.
Long: Close > Channel upper + ATR × breakout threshold for the configured number of consecutive completed chart-timeframe bars
Short: Close < Channel lower − ATR × breakout threshold for the configured number of consecutive completed chart-timeframe bars
