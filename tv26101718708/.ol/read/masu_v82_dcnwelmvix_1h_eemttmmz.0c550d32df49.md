Calculation: Fast and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages with shorter and longer lookbacks, respectively; filter EMA = longer-lookback exponential moving average
Calculation: Bollinger Bands = 20-bar simple moving average of close ± 2 standard deviations of close
Calculation: Donchian upper/lower = highest high / lowest low over the lookback; midpoint = their average
Calculation: NW ribbon = eight Gaussian-weighted averages of close, with bandwidths ordered shortest to longest; bullish ordering is present when at least the configured number of adjacent pairs have the shorter-bandwidth average above the longer-bandwidth average; bearish ordering is the reverse
Calculation: Intraday VWAP = cumulative volume-weighted average of typical price (high + low + close)/3, resetting each day
Calculation: CVD = cumulative sum of volume on up-close bars minus volume on down-close bars
Calculation: MTF trend = on a higher timeframe, fast EMA above slow EMA and close above fast EMA for bullish; reverse for bearish; use the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: Daily VIX and daily SPX proxy values = use the last completed daily bar; SPX breadth is bullish when its close is above its EMA
Calculation: Long and short confluence scores = sum of configured weights for, respectively: matching MTF trend, matching NW ribbon ordering, matching order-flow filter, matching smart-money filter, ADX above threshold, matching ELM probability vote, positive/negative fastest NW-line slope, matching Donchian trend filter, breadth filter (long: SPX close above its EMA; short: always passes), and matching VIX sentiment vote. The ELM vote is probability above its confidence threshold for long or below one minus that threshold for short. Order-flow filter passes long when disabled or close is above VWAP and either CVD is above its moving average and rising or up-volume/down-volume ratio exceeds one plus threshold; it passes short when disabled or close is below VWAP and either CVD is below its moving average and falling or that ratio is below one minus threshold. Smart-money filter passes long when disabled or a bullish order block, bullish fair-value gap, low liquidity sweep, or bullish break of structure occurs; passes short on the corresponding bearish patterns. VIX favors long when enabled, sentiment is neutral or greed, and VIX is not rising; it favors short when enabled and sentiment is fear or VIX is rising.
Long: flat position AND in session (or session filter disabled) AND not equity-guarded AND not blocked by ELM or extreme-fear VIX filters AND long confluence score ≥ minimum AND MTF, NW ribbon, order-flow, smart-money, and Donchian filters pass AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > filter EMA AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction) OR (close > prior 20-bar highest high AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction AND close > filter EMA) OR (ADX ≤ threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < 30 AND RSI crosses above 30 AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction) OR (Donchian breakout enabled AND close > prior Donchian upper band AND prior close ≤ Donchian upper band from two bars ago AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction AND close > filter EMA))
Short: flat position AND in session (or session filter disabled) AND not equity-guarded AND not blocked by ELM AND short confluence score ≥ minimum AND MTF, NW ribbon, order-flow, smart-money, and Donchian filters pass AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < filter EMA AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction) OR (close < prior 20-bar lowest low AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction AND close < filter EMA) OR (ADX ≤ threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > 70 AND RSI crosses below 70 AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction) OR (Donchian breakout enabled AND close < prior Donchian lower band AND prior close ≥ Donchian lower band from two bars ago AND volume > volume average × spike multiplier AND volume ≥ volume average × floor fraction AND close < filter EMA))
Long: bullish order block (up-close current bar, down-close prior bar, and current close in the upper 30% of its range) OR bullish fair-value gap (current low > high two bars ago and gap > configured ATR fraction) OR low liquidity sweep (current low below prior lookback low, up-close, and close above that prior low) OR bullish break of structure (close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high)
Short: bearish order block (down-close current bar, up-close prior bar, and current close in the lower 30% of its range) OR bearish fair-value gap (current high < low two bars ago and gap > configured ATR fraction) OR high liquidity sweep (current high above prior lookback high, down-close, and close below that prior high) OR bearish break of structure (close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low)
Exit: long, low ≤ hard stop (opposite Donchian boundary if configured, otherwise entry price minus ATR × regime stop multiplier) OR high ≥ partial-profit limit (entry price plus ATR × partial-profit multiplier) OR trailing stop is activated after the configured fraction of full target distance and low ≤ highest price since activation minus ATR × trail offset; when trailing is disabled, also exit if high ≥ full target (entry price plus ATR × regime target multiplier)
Exit: short, high ≥ hard stop (opposite Donchian boundary if configured, otherwise entry price plus ATR × regime stop multiplier) OR low ≤ partial-profit limit (entry price minus ATR × partial-profit multiplier) OR trailing stop is activated after the configured fraction of full target distance and high ≥ lowest price since activation plus ATR × trail offset; when trailing is disabled, also exit if low ≤ full target (entry price minus ATR × regime target multiplier)
