Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a bar whose high/low is the highest/lowest across the configured left and right chart-bar lookbacks; confirm only after the right-side bars have completed.
Calculation: Upper/lower wick ratio = (high − max(open, close))/(high − low) / (min(open, close) − low)/(high − low), respectively, on the pivot bar.
Calculation: Close rejection ratio = (high − close)/(high − low) for a pivot high; (close − low)/(high − low) for a pivot low.
Calculation: Relative volume = pivot-bar volume / the pivot-bar SMA of volume over its configured lookback.
Calculation: Outer zone boundary = pivot price ± (ATR on the pivot bar × zone-width multiplier); add for a resistance pivot high and subtract for a support pivot low.
Long: Confirmed pivot low AND lower wick ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND close rejection ratio ≥ minimum close rejection AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume OR latest active, unbroken support zone touched (low ≤ pivot price) and close > pivot price, on a bar after zone creation, with no prior hold signal for that zone OR latest active, broken resistance zone retested (low ≤ its outer top boundary) and close > that boundary, on a bar after the break and before zone expiry, with no prior flip signal for that zone.
Short: Confirmed pivot high AND upper wick ratio ≥ minimum wick ratio AND close rejection ratio ≥ minimum close rejection AND relative volume ≥ minimum relative volume OR latest active, unbroken resistance zone touched (high ≥ pivot price) and close < pivot price, on a bar after zone creation, with no prior hold signal for that zone OR latest active, broken support zone retested (high ≥ its outer bottom boundary) and close < that boundary, on a bar after the break and before zone expiry, with no prior flip signal for that zone.
