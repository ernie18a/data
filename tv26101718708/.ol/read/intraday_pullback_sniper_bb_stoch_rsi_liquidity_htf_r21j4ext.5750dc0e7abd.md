Calculation: Bollinger Bands = simple moving average of close ± a multiplier of the close standard deviation, using the same lookback for both
Calculation: Stoch RSI K = smoothed stochastic of RSI(close); an extreme is K at/below the oversold threshold or at/above the overbought threshold at least once within a lookback
Calculation: Higher-timeframe bias = from the last completed higher-timeframe bar: bullish/bearish when close is above/below its EMA, when market structure is rising/falling, or by combining both; when combined sources disagree, bias is transitional toward the structure direction
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = highest high/lowest low in a symmetric window with equal lookbacks on each side; same-kind swings replace the current swing only when more extreme, and accepted highs and lows alternate
Calculation: Entry-timeframe structure = calculated from completed bars of a lower timeframe; rising sequence is a higher low followed by a higher high, falling sequence is a lower high followed by a lower low; a structure break is a close above the last confirmed swing high or below the last confirmed swing low
Long: low <= lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close <= lower Bollinger Band + (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) * rejection-zone fraction AND Stoch RSI K was at/below oversold threshold within its lookback AND long bias is allowed AND (zone filter disabled OR price is in an enabled bullish zone)
Long: an earlier long setup is still valid AND (entry structure is a higher low followed by a higher high OR close breaks above the last confirmed entry-timeframe swing high, according to the selected entry mode) AND (selected mode requires a fresh band rejection: low <= lower Bollinger Band AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close <= lower Bollinger Band + (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) * rejection-zone fraction) AND long bias is allowed AND (liquidity sweep requirement disabled OR a low liquidity level was pierced within its validity lookback and close reclaimed above it) AND an enabled session is open (or no sessions are enabled) AND cooldown has elapsed
Short: high >= upper Bollinger Band AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close >= upper Bollinger Band - (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) * rejection-zone fraction AND Stoch RSI K was at/above overbought threshold within its lookback AND short bias is allowed AND (zone filter disabled OR price is in an enabled bearish zone)
Short: an earlier short setup is still valid AND (entry structure is a lower high followed by a lower low OR close breaks below the last confirmed entry-timeframe swing low, according to the selected entry mode) AND (selected mode requires a fresh band rejection: high >= upper Bollinger Band AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close >= upper Bollinger Band - (upper Bollinger Band - lower Bollinger Band) * rejection-zone fraction) AND short bias is allowed AND (liquidity sweep requirement disabled OR a high liquidity level was pierced within its validity lookback and close reclaimed below it) AND an enabled session is open (or no sessions are enabled) AND cooldown has elapsed
Exit: long, no exit decision
Exit: short, no exit decision
