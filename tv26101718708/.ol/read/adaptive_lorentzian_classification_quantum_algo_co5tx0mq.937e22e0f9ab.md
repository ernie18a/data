Calculation: Features = configurable vector of up to six normalized RSI, WaveTrend, CCI, ADX, MFI, or Fisher Transform values; compare each current feature with the corresponding feature stored for each historical sample.
Calculation: Training label = LONG if source price change over the prediction horizon exceeds the neutral-zone multiple of ATR(20) measured at the sample bar; SHORT if it is below the negative of that threshold; otherwise NEUTRAL. Pair each label with the feature vector from the sample bar; use samples in the recent training window, spaced by the configured minimum number of bars.
Calculation: Prediction and confidence = find up to K approximate nearest samples by the configured weighted feature distance (Lorentzian, Manhattan, Euclidean, or their 50/50 hybrid); sum their labels using weights 1/(1 + distance), or equal weights if distance weighting is disabled. Prediction is the signed vote (scaled by K when distance-weighted); confidence is the absolute weighted vote divided by total vote weight, as a percentage. K may shrink as the percentile rank of ATR(10) over its recent lookback rises. Signal becomes LONG when prediction > 0, confidence meets its minimum, volatility and regime filters pass, and any enabled ADX filter passes; becomes SHORT when prediction < 0 under the same gates; otherwise it retains its prior value. Regime filter: |EMA(20) − EMA(50)| / ATR(20) exceeds its threshold. Volatility filter: ATR(10) > ATR(40). ADX filter: ADX(14) > its threshold.
Calculation: Bullish kernel = rational-quadratic kernel regression estimate is rising versus the prior bar, or, in smoothing mode, the Gaussian estimate is at least the rational-quadratic estimate. Bearish kernel = rational-quadratic estimate is falling versus the prior bar, or, in smoothing mode, the Gaussian estimate is at most the rational-quadratic estimate. In smoothing mode the Gaussian estimate uses a shorter lookback than the rational-quadratic estimate.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA of close on a higher timeframe than the chart timeframe, using the current higher-timeframe bar (forming in real time; last completed bar on historical bars).
Long: Signal changes to LONG AND (EMA filter disabled OR close > configured EMA) AND (SMA filter disabled OR close > configured SMA) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (kernel filter disabled OR bullish kernel).
Short: Signal changes to SHORT AND (EMA filter disabled OR close < configured EMA) AND (SMA filter disabled OR close < configured SMA) AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (kernel filter disabled OR bearish kernel).
Exit: Long, ATR trailing stop enabled AND close < long trailing stop; trailing stop starts at entry close − ATR(14) × configured multiplier and ratchets upward to the maximum of its prior value and close − ATR(14) × multiplier.
Exit: Short, ATR trailing stop enabled AND close > short trailing stop; trailing stop starts at entry close + ATR(14) × configured multiplier and ratchets downward to the minimum of its prior value and close + ATR(14) × multiplier.
Exit: Long, ATR trailing stop condition OR (dynamic exits enabled AND EMA and SMA filters disabled AND kernel smoothing disabled AND bearish kernel slope reversal AND the latest bullish kernel alert is later than the long entry) OR (strict exits selected AND the long entry was one prediction horizon ago AND ((bars held equals the horizon AND the signal from one horizon ago was LONG with its EMA and SMA filters passing) OR (0 < bars held < horizon AND a new SHORT signal occurs AND that prior LONG signal and its EMA and SMA filters passed)). Bars held resets when signal changes.
Exit: Short, ATR trailing stop condition OR (dynamic exits enabled AND EMA and SMA filters disabled AND kernel smoothing disabled AND bullish kernel slope reversal AND the latest bearish kernel alert is later than the short entry) OR (strict exits selected AND the short entry was one prediction horizon ago AND ((bars held equals the horizon AND the signal from one horizon ago was SHORT with its EMA and SMA filters passing) OR (0 < bars held < horizon AND a new LONG signal occurs AND that prior SHORT signal and its EMA and SMA filters passed)). Bars held resets when signal changes.
