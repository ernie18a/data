Calculation: Confirmed swing high = a high greater than the highs of the preceding and following swing-length bars
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = a low less than the lows of the preceding and following swing-length bars
Long: close > latest confirmed swing high AND previous close <= latest confirmed swing high AND latest confirmed swing high has not previously been broken
Short: close < latest confirmed swing low AND previous close >= latest confirmed swing low AND latest confirmed swing low has not previously been broken
