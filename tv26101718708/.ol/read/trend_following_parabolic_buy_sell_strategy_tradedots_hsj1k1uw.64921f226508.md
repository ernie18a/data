Long: close > long-term moving average AND fast moving average crosses above slow moving average AND close > Parabolic SAR AND position is flat
Short: close < long-term moving average AND slow moving average crosses above fast moving average AND close < Parabolic SAR AND position is flat
Calculation: Long stop loss = long-term moving average on the entry signal bar
Calculation: Long take profit = entry signal bar close + (entry signal bar close − long-term moving average on the entry signal bar) × risk-reward ratio
Calculation: Short stop loss = long-term moving average on the entry signal bar
Calculation: Short take profit = entry signal bar close − (long-term moving average on the entry signal bar − entry signal bar close) × risk-reward ratio
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop loss OR high ≥ long take profit
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop loss OR low ≤ short take profit
