Calculation: Confirmed swing pivots = local highs or lows confirmed after the configured right-side pivot bars, then eligible as trendline anchors after the configured maturity delay; use separate shorter-pivot and longer-pivot scales.
Calculation: Swing sequence = at each scale, alternate pivot highs and lows, accepting a reversal only when its price move exceeds the configured ATR multiple; replace the latest same-side swing only with a more extreme pivot.
Calculation: Active resistance/support trendlines = pair selected descending swing highs / ascending swing lows, respectively, with the first anchor inside the configured lookback and the second later than the first; require configured touch count within ATR-based tolerance, configured slope bounds, and no intervening candle body beyond the line. Project each qualifying line forward for an extension based on anchor spacing and touch count. Apply configured nesting and overlap selection, then retain no more than the configured active-line cap per side and scale; retire lines at the end of their projection.
Calculation: Break confirmation = consecutive closes beyond an active projected trendline; reset the count on any close that is not beyond it. A line breaks when the count reaches the configured threshold and all enabled filters pass: at least one counted bar has volume above the configured multiple of the chart-timeframe volume SMA; the confirming close is at least the configured ATR multiple beyond the line; and, if selected, the confirming close is above the selected moving average for an upward break or below it for a downward break.
Long: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, resistance trendline break confirmation.
Short: On a confirmed chart-timeframe bar, support trendline break confirmation.
Exit: Long, close below the line of an upward-broken trendline before the configured negation window expires.
Exit: Short, close above the line of a downward-broken trendline before the configured negation window expires.
