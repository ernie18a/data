Calculation: Key highs/lows = previous completed bars from higher daily and weekly timeframes, plus the current Asia-session, London-session, and opening-range highs/lows
Calculation: Highest key high = maximum of the key highs; lowest key low = minimum of the key lows
Calculation: Recent high/low touch = a bar’s high at or above the highest key high / low at or below the lowest key low within the configured lookback
Calculation: Level-cross direction = most recent close crossing above any key high (bullish) or below any key low (bearish)
Calculation: FVG = bullish when current low > high two bars earlier, with zone from that earlier high to current low; bearish when current high < low two bars earlier, with zone from current high to that earlier low
Calculation: Fast and slow EMA = EMAs of close, with the fast EMA using the shorter lookback
Long: After the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA (or, when a cross is not required, while fast EMA > slow EMA), low <= fast EMA and close > fast EMA; OR an active bearish FVG formed while a recent low touch was present and its top <= the lowest key low, with close > its top and a recent low touch; OR an active bullish FVG is touched after formation without being invalidated, then on a later bar closes above its top, while the most recent level-cross direction is bearish, the FVG is within its configured maximum age, and either that direction coincides with a recent low touch or current low <= the lowest key low
Short: After the fast EMA crosses below the slow EMA (or, when a cross is not required, while fast EMA < slow EMA), high >= fast EMA and close < fast EMA; OR an active bullish FVG formed while a recent high touch was present and its bottom >= the highest key high, with close < its bottom and a recent high touch; OR an active bearish FVG is touched after formation without being invalidated, then on a later bar closes below its bottom, while the most recent level-cross direction is bullish, the FVG is within its configured maximum age, and either that direction coincides with a recent high touch or current high >= the highest key high
