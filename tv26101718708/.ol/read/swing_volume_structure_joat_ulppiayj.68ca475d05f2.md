Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low that is the highest/lowest across configurable bars on both sides; becomes available after the right-side bars complete. Compare each new high (low) pivot with the previous high (low) pivot to classify it as HH/LH (HL/LL).
Calculation: Relative volume = current volume / moving average of volume over a configurable lookback; expansion means relative volume meets a configurable threshold.
Calculation: Breakout score = base score plus weighted contributions for a close crossing the latest confirmed pivot, a qualifying retest of that pivot, volume expansion, a directional expansion candle, the latest opposite pivot being HL (long) or LH (short), and prior swing volume exceeding its preceding value. A directional expansion candle has matching candle direction, a close near that direction’s end of the range, and body size large relative to the range.
Calculation: Sweep score = base score plus weighted contributions for a qualifying sweep, volume expansion, and a close near the reclaim/rejection end of the range.
Long: Bar is confirmed AND cooldown has elapsed since the last accepted long or short state AND ((close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high AND bullish breakout score meets the minimum) OR (low crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low, close reclaims above it, lower wick is larger than body by a configurable factor, and bullish sweep score meets the minimum)).
Short: Bar is confirmed AND cooldown has elapsed since the last accepted long or short state AND ((close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low AND bearish breakout score meets the minimum) OR (high crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high, close rejects below it, upper wick is larger than body by a configurable factor, and bearish sweep score meets the minimum)).
