Calculation: Bar delta = volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / (high − low); if high = low, use volume when close > prior close, −volume when close < prior close, otherwise 0
Calculation: CVD = anchor-reset cumulative sum of bar delta
Calculation: CVD slope = CVD minus CVD from a longer lookback; bullish acceleration = current CVD slope > its value three bars earlier; bearish acceleration = current CVD slope < its value three bars earlier
Calculation: Average volume = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: VWAP and standard deviation bands = volume-weighted mean and standard deviation of typical price (high + low + close) / 3 since the selected session, week, or month anchor; ±1.5σ and ±2σ bands are VWAP ± 1.5 or 2 standard deviations
Calculation: Body ratio = absolute close − open divided by high − low; lower/upper wick = distance from the lower/upper extreme to the nearer body edge; wick/body ratio = wick divided by body, or 2 when body size is at most close × 0.0003
Long: Bar delta ≥ 0 OR (close > VWAP AND CVD slope > 0) OR (at least the configured minimum number of prior bars closed below VWAP AND open < VWAP AND close > VWAP AND body ratio ≥ 0.5 AND bar delta > average volume × 0.6 AND CVD slope > 0 AND bullish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.3) OR (low < lower 2σ band − close × configured wick-extension percentage AND close > lower 1.5σ band AND lower-wick/body ratio > 1.5 AND bar delta > average volume × 0.5 AND bullish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.5) OR (a confirmed price pivot low is lower than the preceding price pivot low while its corresponding CVD pivot low is higher than the preceding CVD pivot low AND absolute close − VWAP ≤ 1.5 standard deviations AND bullish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.2)
Short: Bar delta < 0 OR (close < VWAP AND CVD slope ≤ 0) OR (at least the configured minimum number of prior bars closed above VWAP AND open > VWAP AND close < VWAP AND body ratio ≥ 0.5 AND bar delta < −average volume × 0.6 AND CVD slope ≤ 0 AND bearish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.3) OR (high > upper 2σ band + close × configured wick-extension percentage AND close < upper 1.5σ band AND upper-wick/body ratio > 1.5 AND bar delta < −average volume × 0.5 AND bearish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.5) OR (a confirmed price pivot high is higher than the preceding price pivot high while its corresponding CVD pivot high is lower than the preceding CVD pivot high AND absolute close − VWAP ≤ 1.5 standard deviations AND bearish acceleration AND volume > average volume × 1.2)
