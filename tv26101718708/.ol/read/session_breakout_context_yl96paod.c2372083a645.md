Calculation: Session range = highest high and lowest low during the named session; use the completed range in the following session.
Calculation: Bullish structure break = lower-timeframe close above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish structure break = lower-timeframe close below the latest confirmed pivot low. Use equal pivot lookbacks; use the last available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Net pressure = moving average of volume × ((close − low) − (high − close)) / max(high − low, minimum range); when volume is unavailable, use close − open instead. Its signal is a shorter moving average of net pressure.
Calculation: Wide-spread high-volume bullish VSA = relative volume above its threshold, body greater than three quarters of range, and close ≥ open; bearish VSA is the same with close < open. High-volume narrow-spread upper-wick rejection = relative volume above its threshold, body less than one quarter of range, upper wick greater than 1.5 times lower wick and greater than body; lower-wick rejection is the inverse. Relative volume is volume divided by its moving average.
Calculation: Exhaustion boundaries = chart-timeframe EMA of close ± ATR.
Long: During the session after Asia, London, or New York respectively, no signal has yet occurred in that session, and high > the completed prior session's high AND ((bullish structure break AND net pressure > 0 AND net pressure > its shorter signal average AND VSA is not wide-spread high-volume bullish) OR bearish structure break OR upper-wick rejection OR high > upper exhaustion boundary).
Long: During the session after Asia, London, or New York respectively, no signal has yet occurred in that session, and low < the completed prior session's low AND (bullish structure break OR lower-wick rejection OR low < lower exhaustion boundary).
Short: During the session after Asia, London, or New York respectively, no signal has yet occurred in that session, and high > the completed prior session's high AND (bearish structure break OR upper-wick rejection OR high > upper exhaustion boundary).
Short: During the session after Asia, London, or New York respectively, no signal has yet occurred in that session, and low < the completed prior session's low AND ((bearish structure break AND net pressure < 0 AND net pressure < its shorter signal average AND VSA is not wide-spread high-volume bearish) OR bullish structure break OR lower-wick rejection OR low < lower exhaustion boundary).
Exit: Long, low < the selected stop level (session midpoint or opposite range extreme) OR the session ends.
Exit: Short, high > the selected stop level (session midpoint or opposite range extreme) OR the session ends.
