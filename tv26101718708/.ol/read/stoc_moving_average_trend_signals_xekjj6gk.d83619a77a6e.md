Calculation: Signal MA = the selected 10 EMA, 20 EMA, 50 SMA, or 200 SMA
Calculation: Volume average = simple moving average of volume
Calculation: MA separation = absolute difference between 10 EMA and 200 SMA, divided by close, times 100
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = highest high since long entry minus ATR times a multiplier, updated each bar to the greater of its prior value and the newly calculated stop
Long: (selected entry mode is MA Stack AND 10 EMA > 20 EMA > 50 SMA > 200 SMA AND the preceding bar did not have this alignment OR selected entry mode is Price vs MA AND close crosses above Signal MA AND (slope filter disabled OR Signal MA > Signal MA at the configured number of bars ago) OR selected entry mode is Fast/Slow Crossover AND 10 EMA crosses above 20 EMA AND (slope filter disabled OR 20 EMA > 20 EMA at the configured number of bars ago)) AND (volume filter disabled OR volume or its average is unavailable OR volume >= Volume average times the configured multiplier) AND (choppy market filter disabled OR MA separation >= configured minimum) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar is completed)
Short: 10 EMA < 20 EMA < 50 SMA < 200 SMA AND the preceding bar did not have this alignment
Exit: long, (selected exit mode is MA Signal Exit AND ((selected entry mode is MA Stack AND bullish MA alignment is lost after being present on the preceding bar) OR (selected entry mode is Price vs MA AND close crosses below Signal MA) OR (selected entry mode is Fast/Slow Crossover AND 10 EMA crosses below 20 EMA)) OR selected exit mode is ATR Trailing Exit AND close < ATR trailing stop) AND (bar-close confirmation disabled OR current bar is completed)
