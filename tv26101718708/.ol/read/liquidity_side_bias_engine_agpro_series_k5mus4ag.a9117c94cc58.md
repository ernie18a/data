Calculation: Buy-side pool = nearest untaken pivot high above close or prior higher-timeframe high, whichever is closer; sell-side pool = nearest untaken pivot low below close or prior higher-timeframe low, whichever is closer. Pivot highs/lows use equal left/right lookbacks. Prior-period levels use the last completed higher-timeframe bar: prior day on intraday charts, prior week on daily charts, prior month on higher timeframes.
Calculation: Buy-side score = clamp(0.28 × proximity + 0.26 × adjusted strength + 0.26 × trend bias + 0.20 × range bias, 0, 100); sell-side score uses the corresponding sell-side factors. Proximity = clamp(100 − pool distance in ATRs / maximum distance in ATRs × 100, 0, 100). Strength = 0.7 × pool strength + 0.3 × freshness; pool strength = clamp(untaken swing pivots within ATR tolerance of the pool / 4 × 100, 0, 100), raised to at least 72 for a prior-period pool; freshness = clamp(100 − pivot age / freshness window × 100, 0, 100), or 62 for a prior-period pool. Trend bias: aligned trend = 100, price on the trend side of the EMA without aligned slope = 64, opposing trend = 28, otherwise 50; uptrend requires close above the EMA and EMA above its value the slope lookback bars ago, downtrend is the inverse. Range bias: buy = clamp(50 + (0.5 − range position) × 100, 0, 100); sell = clamp(50 + (range position − 0.5) × 100, 0, 100). Range position = clamp((close − lowest low) / max(highest high − lowest low, ATR), 0, 1) over the range lookback. A missing pool has score 0.
Long: buy-side pool exists AND buy-side score − sell-side score > balanced-gap threshold AND rounded buy-side score ≥ active-bias threshold.
Short: sell-side pool exists AND sell-side score − buy-side score > balanced-gap threshold AND rounded sell-side score ≥ active-bias threshold.
