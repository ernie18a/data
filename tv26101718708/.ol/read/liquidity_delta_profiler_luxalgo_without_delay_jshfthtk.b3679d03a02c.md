Calculation: Buy-side liquidity zone = for each confirmed pivot high, [max(open, close), high] on the pivot bar; if its height is less than 0.1 ATR, use [high − 0.1 ATR, high]. Confirm the pivot after the configured number of bars to its right.
Calculation: Sell-side liquidity zone = for each confirmed pivot low, [low, min(open, close)] on the pivot bar; if its height is less than 0.1 ATR, use [low, low + 0.1 ATR]. Confirm the pivot after the configured number of bars to its right.
Calculation: Bar delta = volume × (close − open) / (high − low), or 0 when high = low.
Calculation: Zone quadrant delta = cumulative sum, for each bar overlapping a zone quadrant, of bar delta × (overlap between the bar range and quadrant range / bar range); use 0 when the bar range is zero. Divide each zone into four equal price quadrants from bottom to top.
Calculation: Zone outer delta share = absolute cumulative delta in the outer quadrant / (sum of the absolute cumulative deltas in all four quadrants + 0.0001); the outer quadrant is the top one for a buy-side zone and the bottom one for a sell-side zone.
Calculation: Bar delta strength = absolute bar delta / (volume + 0.0001).
Long: At an unsignaled sell-side zone on a bar that overlaps or sweeps the zone, low < zone bottom AND outer delta > 0 AND zone outer delta share > 0.2 OR (low < zone bottom AND zone outer delta share >= 0.1 AND (outer delta <= 0 OR zone outer delta share >= 0.2) AND zone outer delta share < 0.1) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND zone outer delta share > 0.6 AND outer delta < 0) OR (low < zone bottom AND close > zone midpoint AND bar delta > 0 AND bar delta strength > 0.2 AND no earlier signal condition applies).
Short: At an unsignaled buy-side zone on a bar that overlaps or sweeps the zone, high > zone top AND outer delta < 0 AND zone outer delta share > 0.2 OR (high > zone top AND zone outer delta share >= 0.1 AND (outer delta >= 0 OR zone outer delta share >= 0.2) AND zone outer delta share < 0.1) OR (close >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top AND zone outer delta share > 0.6 AND outer delta > 0) OR (high > zone top AND close < zone midpoint AND bar delta < 0 AND bar delta strength > 0.2 AND no earlier signal condition applies).
