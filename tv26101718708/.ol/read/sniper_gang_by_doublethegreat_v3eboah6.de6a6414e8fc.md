Calculation: Smoothed stochastic lines = stochastic %K smoothed by a simple moving average; use progressively longer lookbacks for fast, medium, and slow lines, and a trend line with a longer lookback than the slow line. The trend line is calculated on 5-minute bars (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as applicable), using the currently forming bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe alignment = fast EMA above slow EMA and chart close above VWAP calculated on each higher timeframe for bullish alignment; reverse both comparisons for bearish alignment. Use the currently forming higher-timeframe bar. Alignment requires both reference timeframes when the second is enabled, otherwise only the first.
Calculation: Bull score = count of: close above chart VWAP; 14-period RSI above 50; MACD line above signal line; fast EMA above slow EMA; ADX above 25 and close above fast EMA; volume above its 20-bar average and close above open; 14-period RSI above 50 on 5-minute bars (higher or lower than the chart timeframe as applicable, using the currently forming bar). Bear score counts the corresponding reversed conditions. Active score is the bull score for a long position, bear score for a short position, or the larger score when flat.
Calculation: Market structure oscillator = weighted average of three 0–100 normalized close positions within ranges reset by bullish or bearish breaks of short-, intermediate-, and long-term swing levels, with the intermediate structure using longer-term swings than the short structure and the long structure using longer-term swings than the intermediate structure. Normalize each series with a 4-bar simple moving average; weights are configurable separately for short, intermediate, and long structure. A bullish break occurs when close exceeds the latest unbroken swing high, a bearish break when close falls below the latest unbroken swing low. Swing highs/lows are confirmed when the prior bar's high/low is respectively above/ below the bar before it and at least as high/low as the current bar.
Calculation: Cycle oscillator = market structure oscillator minus its configurable-length EMA, plus 50.
Calculation: ATR trailing stop = recursive stop based on close and configurable ATR lookback multiplied by a sensitivity factor: if current and prior closes are above the prior stop, use the greater of the prior stop and close minus the ATR distance; if both are below, use the lesser of the prior stop and close plus the ATR distance; otherwise use close minus the ATR distance when close is above the prior stop, and close plus the distance otherwise.
Calculation: Quad bullish divergence = all four smoothed stochastic lines first enter oversold together; save that bar's low and fast stochastic. After they leave oversold, a bar makes a low at or below the saved low while fast stochastic is above its saved value, rising, and still within 10 points above the oversold threshold. Quad bearish divergence is the inverse: all four first enter overbought together; save that bar's high and fast stochastic; after they leave overbought, a bar makes a high at or above the saved high while fast stochastic is below its saved value, falling, and within 10 points below the overbought threshold. Clear these states on a primary EMA-cross entry.
Calculation: Hidden bullish divergence = trend stochastic at or above its strong-bull threshold, medium and slow stochastic at or above 50, and fast stochastic oversold and rising. Hidden bearish divergence = trend stochastic at or below the inverse strong-bull threshold, medium and slow stochastic at or below 50, and fast stochastic overbought and falling.
Long: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND no prior active long signal AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bullish higher-timeframe alignment)
Long: Close crosses above ATR trailing stop
Long: Market structure oscillator crosses above 50
Long: Cycle oscillator crosses above 50
Long: Quad bullish divergence
Long: Hidden bullish divergence
Long: Active score is at least 5 AND (quad bullish divergence OR hidden bullish divergence OR trend is bullish, fast stochastic is oversold, and fast stochastic is rising) AND market structure oscillator is at least 50 AND close is above chart VWAP AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bullish higher-timeframe alignment) AND position side is long
Short: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND no prior active short signal AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bearish higher-timeframe alignment)
Short: Close crosses below ATR trailing stop
Short: Market structure oscillator crosses below 50
Short: Cycle oscillator crosses below 50
Short: Quad bearish divergence
Short: Hidden bearish divergence
Short: Active score is at least 5 AND (quad bearish divergence OR hidden bearish divergence OR trend is bearish, fast stochastic is overbought, and fast stochastic is falling) AND market structure oscillator is at most 50 AND close is below chart VWAP AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR bearish higher-timeframe alignment) AND position side is short
Exit: Long, fast stochastic is overbought AND trend stochastic is falling AND close is below slow EMA
Exit: Short, fast stochastic is oversold AND trend stochastic is rising AND close is above slow EMA
Exit: Long or short, active score is at most 1
