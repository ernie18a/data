Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the highest high/lowest low in a centered window; confirm after the required bars on the right close. Liquidity swings use a longer lookback than structure swings.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over a configurable lookback.
Calculation: Bullish breaker zone = after price trades below a confirmed liquidity swing low not previously swept, track the sweep’s lowest low and the latest applicable confirmed structure swing high. When a bar closes above that swing high with a bullish fair value gap (low > high two bars earlier) or a bullish body at least one ATR, create a zone from the body of the nearest bullish candle at or within five bars before the broken swing high; if none exists, use the broken swing-high candle. The zone is active until a bar closes below its bottom.
Calculation: Bearish breaker zone = after price trades above a confirmed liquidity swing high not previously swept, track the sweep’s highest high and the latest applicable confirmed structure swing low. When a bar closes below that swing low with a bearish fair value gap (high < low two bars earlier) or a bearish body at least one ATR, create a zone from the body of the nearest bearish candle at or within five bars before the broken swing low; if none exists, use the broken swing-low candle. The zone is active until a bar closes above its top.
Long: First touch of an active, not previously touched bullish breaker zone (low <= zone top AND close >= zone bottom).
Short: First touch of an active, not previously touched bearish breaker zone (high >= zone bottom AND close <= zone top).
