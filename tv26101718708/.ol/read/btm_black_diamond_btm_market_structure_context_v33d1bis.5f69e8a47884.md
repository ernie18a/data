Calculation: confirmed swing high/low = a bar high/low greater/less than the highs/lows of the same number of bars on each side, available only after the right-side bars complete
Calculation: retest zone = the broken swing level plus or minus a parameterized fraction of ATR
Calculation: previous-day high/low = the high/low of the last completed higher-timeframe day
Calculation: Asian range high/low = the highest high/lowest low during the 23:00–07:00 London session
Long: a newly confirmed swing high is higher than the prior confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low is higher than the prior confirmed swing low
Short: a newly confirmed swing high is lower than the prior confirmed swing high OR a newly confirmed swing low is lower than the prior confirmed swing low
Long: close > latest unbroken confirmed swing high
Short: close < latest unbroken confirmed swing low
Long: after a bullish break, while its retest zone is active and on a later bar, low <= the zone’s upper bound AND close > open AND close > the zone’s upper bound
Short: after a bearish break, while its retest zone is active and on a later bar, high >= the zone’s lower bound AND close < open AND close < the zone’s lower bound
Long: high > previous-day high AND close < previous-day high AND that level has not already been swept that day OR high > Asian range high AND close < Asian range high AND that level has not already been swept that session
Short: low < previous-day low AND close > previous-day low AND that level has not already been swept that day OR low < Asian range low AND close > Asian range low AND that level has not already been swept that session
Long: on each applicable higher timeframe, short-term EMA > long-term EMA AND close > long-term EMA; use the last completed bar
Short: on each applicable higher timeframe, short-term EMA < long-term EMA AND close < long-term EMA; use the last completed bar
Long: short-term EMA > long-term EMA OR close > long-lookback EMA
Short: short-term EMA < long-term EMA OR close < long-lookback EMA
