Calculation: Bullish FVG = a bar’s low is above the high two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size; bearish FVG = a bar’s high is below the low two bars earlier by at least the minimum gap size.
Calculation: Active FVG = a bullish or bearish FVG formed within the configured lookback on that timeframe. For multi-timeframe values, use the last available bar with lookahead off; higher-timeframe values use the last completed bar historically and may use the forming bar in real time, while lower-timeframe values use the last available lower-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Bullish alignment score = count of the ten sampled timeframes with an active bullish FVG; bearish alignment score = count with an active bearish FVG. The sampled timeframes include bars both lower and higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Bullish zone = the price range from the high two bars before the latest bullish FVG to that FVG bar’s low; bearish zone = the range from the latest bearish FVG bar’s high to the low two bars before it. Use the latest FVG from the entry-zone timeframe, which may be higher or lower than the chart timeframe; use the last available bar with lookahead off.
Calculation: Bullish retest = the chart close is within the bullish zone and an active bullish FVG exists on the entry-zone timeframe; bearish retest = the chart close is within the bearish zone and an active bearish FVG exists on that timeframe. A new retest is true when it is true now and was false on the prior chart bar.
Calculation: Bullish sequence = active bullish FVGs on both shorter- and longer-lookback sampled timeframes, with the shorter-lookback FVG no older than the longer-lookback FVG; bearish sequence is the equivalent for bearish FVGs. Use the last available bars with lookahead off.
Long: bullish retest is new AND bullish alignment score is at least the configured minimum AND bullish alignment score > bearish alignment score AND the kill-zone filter is disabled or the current time is in its allowed window AND the session filter is disabled or the current session is outside NY, or London manipulation is bullish, or both Asia extremes were swept in London, or neither exclusive bullish nor exclusive bearish London manipulation occurred AND the structure filter is disabled or market structure is neutral/bullish AND no long entry has occurred since the bullish zone changed or was invalidated.
Long: bullish alignment score is at least the configured minimum AND bullish alignment score > bearish alignment score AND bullish sequence AND the kill-zone filter is disabled or current time is in its allowed window AND the signal was not true on the prior chart bar AND no long entry has occurred since the bullish zone changed or was invalidated.
Long: a sell-side liquidity sweep occurs (low below the latest confirmed swing low and close back above it) OR bullish break of structure occurs (close crosses above the latest confirmed swing high) OR a bullish change of character occurs (bullish break of structure while prior market direction was bearish).
Short: bearish retest is new AND bearish alignment score is at least the configured minimum AND bearish alignment score > bullish alignment score AND the kill-zone filter is disabled or the current time is in its allowed window AND the session filter is disabled or the current session is outside NY, or London manipulation is bearish, or both Asia extremes were swept in London, or neither exclusive bullish nor exclusive bearish London manipulation occurred AND the structure filter is disabled or market structure is neutral/bearish AND no short entry has occurred since the bearish zone changed or was invalidated.
Short: bearish alignment score is at least the configured minimum AND bearish alignment score > bullish alignment score AND bearish sequence AND the kill-zone filter is disabled or current time is in its allowed window AND the signal was not true on the prior chart bar AND no short entry has occurred since the bearish zone changed or was invalidated.
Short: a buy-side liquidity sweep occurs (high above the latest confirmed swing high and close back below it) OR bearish break of structure occurs (close crosses below the latest confirmed swing low) OR a bearish change of character occurs (bearish break of structure while prior market direction was bullish).
Exit: long, an active bullish FVG exists on the entry-zone timeframe and close crosses below its lower boundary, with this condition false on the prior chart bar.
Exit: short, an active bearish FVG exists on the entry-zone timeframe and close crosses above its upper boundary, with this condition false on the prior chart bar.
