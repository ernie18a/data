Calculation: Prior-bar midpoint = (high + low) / 2 of the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe midpoint = (high + low) / 2 of the last completed higher-timeframe bar; use two higher timeframes, one longer than the other.
Calculation: Confirmed pivot high/low = a high/low greater/less than the highs/lows in the chosen number of bars before and after it; retain the latest confirmed pivot high and low.
Calculation: Bullish fair value gap = current bar low > high two bars ago.
Calculation: Bearish fair value gap = current bar high < low two bars ago.
Long: (in NY AM or NY PM session AND (close > either higher-timeframe midpoint) AND ((close > prior-bar midpoint AND previous bar low <= prior-bar midpoint) OR (close > latest confirmed pivot low AND previous bar low <= latest confirmed pivot low) OR (bullish fair value gap AND close > open))) OR (in NY AM or NY PM session AND close > either higher-timeframe midpoint AND close > prior-bar midpoint AND (absolute distance from close to prior-bar midpoint or latest confirmed pivot low, divided by that level, is less than the proximity threshold) AND the same warning condition was false on the previous bar AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the last long warning).
Short: (in NY AM or NY PM session AND (close < either higher-timeframe midpoint) AND ((close < prior-bar midpoint AND previous bar high >= prior-bar midpoint) OR (close < latest confirmed pivot high AND previous bar high >= latest confirmed pivot high) OR (bearish fair value gap AND close < open))) OR (in NY AM or NY PM session AND close < either higher-timeframe midpoint AND close < prior-bar midpoint AND (absolute distance from close to prior-bar midpoint or latest confirmed pivot high, divided by that level, is less than the proximity threshold) AND the same warning condition was false on the previous bar AND more than the cooldown interval has elapsed since the last short warning).
