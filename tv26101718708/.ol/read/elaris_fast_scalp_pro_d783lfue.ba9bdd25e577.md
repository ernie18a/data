Calculation: Fast, slow, and trend EMAs = EMAs of close; fast has a shorter lookback than slow, and trend has a longer lookback than both.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA and close = EMA and close on a higher-timeframe bar; the bar may still be forming in realtime, and with lookahead off its value becomes available historically at that bar’s close.
Calculation: Long score = 25 points if fast EMA > slow EMA and close > trend EMA; 20 if RSI > 50 and rising; 15 if the VWAP filter is disabled or close > VWAP; 15 if the volume filter is disabled or volume > volume SMA × configured multiplier; 15 if ATR as a percentage of close meets its configured minimum and high − low is no greater than ATR × configured maximum; 10 if higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled or higher-timeframe close > higher-timeframe EMA. Otherwise, award zero for each component.
Calculation: Short score = 25 points if fast EMA < slow EMA and close < trend EMA; 20 if RSI < 50 and falling; 15 if the VWAP filter is disabled or close < VWAP; 15 if the volume filter is disabled or volume > volume SMA × configured multiplier; 15 if ATR as a percentage of close meets its configured minimum and high − low is no greater than ATR × configured maximum; 10 if higher-timeframe confirmation is disabled or higher-timeframe close < higher-timeframe EMA. Otherwise, award zero for each component.
Long: low <= fast EMA AND close > fast EMA AND close > open AND close > previous close AND close > previous high AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= configured minimum) AND long score >= configured minimum AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (no prior signal OR current bar is more than the configured cooldown bars after the last signal)
Short: high >= fast EMA AND close < fast EMA AND close < open AND close < previous close AND close < previous low AND (ADX filter disabled OR ADX >= configured minimum) AND short score >= configured minimum AND (session filter disabled OR datetime is within the configured session) AND (no prior signal OR current bar is more than the configured cooldown bars after the last signal)
