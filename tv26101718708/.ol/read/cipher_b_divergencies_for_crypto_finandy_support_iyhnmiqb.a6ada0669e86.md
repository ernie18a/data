Calculation: WaveTrend = Let source be HLC3; compute ESA = EMA(source, channel length), deviation = EMA(abs(source − ESA), channel length), CI = (source − ESA) / (0.015 × deviation), WT1 = EMA(CI, average length), WT2 = SMA(WT1, smoothing length).
Calculation: Regular divergence = Compare successive oscillator pivots, each a five-bar fractal whose center is a high or low; bearish when price makes a higher pivot high while the oscillator makes a lower pivot high, bullish when price makes a lower pivot low while the oscillator makes a higher pivot low. Apply the relevant oscillator pivot threshold where specified.
Calculation: RSI = Relative Strength Index of close.
Calculation: Stochastic RSI = Stochastic of RSI, with logarithm of close as the RSI source.
Long: Chart bar is confirmed AND position is flat AND close is above the local SMA of closes on the higher-timeframe bar (latest available, including a still-forming bar) AND Bitcoin close is above its SMA on the higher-timeframe bar (latest available) or the prior Bitcoin higher-timeframe close is above its corresponding SMA AND (regular bullish WaveTrend divergence with oscillator pivot at or below the bullish threshold OR regular bullish Stochastic RSI divergence OR regular bullish RSI divergence OR WaveTrend crosses above its signal while WaveTrend is at or below the oversold threshold OR (regular bullish WaveTrend or RSI divergence AND previous WaveTrend low pivot is at or below the extreme oversold threshold AND current WaveTrend is above that threshold AND current WaveTrend is at least 5 points above the previous WaveTrend low pivot AND RSI at the previous WaveTrend low pivot is below 30)). The oversold WaveTrend cross condition and its divergence alternatives are restricted by the divergence-only setting; regular divergences and the final compound condition are not.
Short: Chart bar is confirmed AND position is flat AND close is below the local SMA of closes on the higher-timeframe bar (latest available, including a still-forming bar) AND Bitcoin close is below its SMA on the higher-timeframe bar (latest available) or the prior Bitcoin higher-timeframe close is below its corresponding SMA AND (regular bearish WaveTrend divergence with oscillator pivot at or above the bearish threshold OR regular bearish Stochastic RSI divergence OR regular bearish RSI divergence OR WaveTrend crosses below its signal while WaveTrend is at or above the overbought threshold). The overbought WaveTrend cross condition is restricted by the divergence-only setting; regular divergences are not.
Exit: Long, local close is below the local higher-timeframe SMA when the cancel-on-cross setting is enabled OR low reaches the stop set below the signal-bar close or high reaches the target set above it.
Exit: Short, local close is above the local higher-timeframe SMA when the cancel-on-cross setting is enabled OR high reaches the stop set above the signal-bar close or low reaches the target set below it.
