Calculation: Previous completed higher-timeframe high/low = high/low of the last completed bar from a timeframe higher than the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = the latest chart-timeframe pivot high/low, confirmed after the configured lookback bars on both sides.
Calculation: Long sweep low = lowest low since price first fell below the previous completed higher-timeframe low during the configured New York session; reset each New York day.
Calculation: Short sweep high = highest high since price first rose above the previous completed higher-timeframe high during the configured New York session; reset each New York day.
Long: During the configured New York session AND long entries enabled AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit disabled OR no trade yet today) AND a long sweep has occurred today AND latest confirmed swing high > long sweep low minus configured stop buffer AND previous completed higher-timeframe high > latest confirmed swing high AND high >= latest confirmed swing high.
Short: During the configured New York session AND short entries enabled AND flat AND (one-trade-per-day limit disabled OR no trade yet today) AND a short sweep has occurred today AND latest confirmed swing low < short sweep high plus configured stop buffer AND previous completed higher-timeframe low < latest confirmed swing low AND low <= latest confirmed swing low.
Exit: Long, low <= initial stop (long sweep low minus configured stop buffer), or high >= previous completed higher-timeframe high; when break-even management is enabled and high >= entry price + (entry price - initial stop), use entry price as the stop.
Exit: Short, high >= initial stop (short sweep high plus configured stop buffer), or low <= previous completed higher-timeframe low; when break-even management is enabled and low <= entry price - (initial stop - entry price), use entry price as the stop.
