Calculation: 2/20 EMA signal = Let threshold be the minimum of the current and previous lows if both are above the EMA of close; otherwise let it be the maximum of the current and previous highs. Signal is short if threshold > previous close, long if threshold < previous close, otherwise retain the prior signal.
Calculation: Daily 3-bar pattern signal = On daily bars, signal is long when the first bar closes below its open, the middle bar's high and low are each below the first bar's, and the last bar's high and low are each above the middle bar's. Signal is short when the first bar closes above its open, the middle bar's high and low are each above the first bar's, and the last bar's high and low are each below the middle bar's. Otherwise retain the prior signal.
Long: 2/20 EMA signal is long AND daily 3-bar pattern signal from the previous completed daily bar is long AND datetime is after the configured start date
Short: 2/20 EMA signal is short AND daily 3-bar pattern signal from the previous completed daily bar is short AND datetime is after the configured start date
Exit: long or short, 2/20 EMA signal is neither long nor short-matching the daily 3-bar pattern signal from the previous completed daily bar
