Calculation: Asian range = high and low of bars during the Asian session; midpoint = their average.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe fair value gap = a gap formed by three consecutive completed higher-timeframe bars; bearish when the first bar's low is above the third bar's high, bullish when the first bar's high is below the third bar's low.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe order block = the middle candle's range when the preceding candle is opposite-colored and the following candle closes beyond the middle candle's high or low in the reversal direction. If price later closes through the zone, it becomes a breaker block; a fair value gap closed through becomes an inverse fair value gap. Use completed higher-timeframe bars for these zones.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe structure bias = bullish when price closes above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish when it closes below the latest confirmed pivot low; otherwise retain the prior bias. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks. This calculation uses higher-timeframe bars, including the bar still forming.
Calculation: Swing break = close crosses beyond the latest confirmed pivot low (bearish) or pivot high (bullish), using equal left and right lookbacks on chart-timeframe bars.
Calculation: Chart-timeframe fair value gap = a bearish gap when the low two bars ago is above the current high, or a bullish gap when the high two bars ago is below the current low, subject to a minimum size relative to ATR. A tap occurs when a bar's range overlaps the gap.
Calculation: CISD level = open of the first candle in the latest consecutive run of opposite-colored candles; CISD occurs when a candle closes across that level in the reversal direction.
Calculation: Hidden order block = the opposite-colored candle between the latest two fair value gaps of the same direction, if the gaps are within the configured lookback; tap occurs when a bar's range overlaps that candle's body/range zone.
Calculation: OTE retracement level = London sweep extreme + (opposite-side range-break extreme − London sweep extreme) × selected retracement fraction.
Long: During the London session, after the Asian range is complete and absent a news-day filter, price trades below the Asian low and closes back above it; the low is not protected; higher-timeframe bias is bullish or neutral; and price is in a bullish higher-timeframe point of interest unless that filter is disabled. Within the configured confirmation window, a selected bullish trigger occurs (swing break followed by a bullish FVG tap, CISD followed by a retracement to its level, bullish FVG tap, or bullish hidden-order-block tap; AUTO accepts any). If FVG validation is enabled, price must also have traded into a bullish FVG since the sweep. Entry-to-stop risk is positive and reward to the Asian high divided by risk meets the minimum reward/risk.
Short: During the London session, after the Asian range is complete and absent a news-day filter, price trades above the Asian high and closes back below it; the high is not protected; higher-timeframe bias is bearish or neutral; and price is in a bearish higher-timeframe point of interest unless that filter is disabled. Within the configured confirmation window, a selected bearish trigger occurs (swing break followed by a bearish FVG tap, CISD followed by a retracement to its level, bearish FVG tap, or bearish hidden-order-block tap; AUTO accepts any). If FVG validation is enabled, price must also have traded into a bearish FVG since the sweep. Entry-to-stop risk is positive and reward to the Asian low divided by risk meets the minimum reward/risk.
Long: After a qualifying London sweep, price closes above the opposite Asian-range boundary; during the New York session, price reaches the selected OTE retracement level, unless the origin POI restriction or pre-session draw-reached skip filter blocks the setup. Enter at that OTE level.
Short: After a qualifying London sweep, price closes below the opposite Asian-range boundary; during the New York session, price reaches the selected OTE retracement level, unless the origin POI restriction or pre-session draw-reached skip filter blocks the setup. Enter at that OTE level.
Exit: Long, high reaches the position target or low reaches the stop; for London trades, move the stop to entry after the configured favorable-R threshold, and exit if price then reaches entry. If target and stop are reached in the same bar, resolve target first. New York continuation target is the draw beyond the range, or a projected extension if unavailable; its stop is beyond the selected OTE stop level.
Exit: Short, low reaches the position target or high reaches the stop; for London trades, move the stop to entry after the configured favorable-R threshold, and exit if price then reaches entry. If target and stop are reached in the same bar, resolve target first. New York continuation target is the draw beyond the range, or a projected extension if unavailable; its stop is beyond the selected OTE stop level.
