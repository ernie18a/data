Calculation: Confirmed swing high/low = a bar whose high/low is higher/lower than the highs/lows of the configured number of bars on both sides; available only after the right-side bars complete.
Calculation: ATR = average true range on the chart timeframe.
Calculation: Projected line = linear projection through two specified confirmed swings, evaluated at the current chart bar.
Calculation: Double-top setup = latest two swing highs have nearly equal prices (absolute difference ≤ their mean × symmetry tolerance), with the latest swing low between them; height (mean highs − intervening low) > ATR. Neckline is the intervening low. Double-bottom setup mirrors this using two nearly equal swing lows and the intervening high; height (intervening high − mean lows) > ATR.
Calculation: Head-and-shoulders setup = three swing highs in chronological order, with the middle higher than both outer highs and the outer highs nearly equal; two intervening swing lows define a projected neckline. Height (middle high − mean intervening lows) > ATR. Inverse setup mirrors this with three swing lows, a lower middle low, nearly equal outer lows, and intervening swing highs defining the projected neckline; height > ATR.
Calculation: Triangle setup = the projected line through the two latest swing highs is above the projected line through the two latest swing lows, and the vertical distance between those lines at the older of the latest two swing lows > ATR. Upper and lower line slopes are price change per bar. Ascending: absolute upper slope < close × 0.0003 and lower slope > 0. Descending: upper slope < 0 and absolute lower slope < close × 0.0003. Bull setup when close > upper line + breakout margin × ATR, or when that is false, close is not below lower line − breakout margin × ATR, and (ascending or upper slope < 0). Otherwise, bear setup when close < lower line − breakout margin × ATR or descending.
Calculation: Flag setup = pole height > 2.5 × ATR, where pole height is highest high − lowest low over bars from the older of the latest two swing highs/lows through the current bar (at most 120 bars; span must exceed 5 bars). Bull flag: both upper- and lower-line slopes < 0; boundary is the projected upper line. Bear flag: both slopes > 0; boundary is the projected lower line.
Calculation: Wedge setup = projected upper line > projected lower line. Falling wedge: both slopes < 0 and lower slope < upper slope; bullish boundary is the upper line. Rising wedge: both slopes > 0 and lower slope > upper slope; bearish boundary is the lower line.
Calculation: Pattern phase = FIRED when the close is beyond its directional boundary by more than breakout margin × ATR; otherwise ARMED when absolute distance from close to boundary ≤ armed tolerance × ATR; otherwise FORMING.
Calculation: HTF bullish = higher-timeframe close > its shorter-lookback EMA > its longer-lookback EMA. HTF bearish = higher-timeframe close < its shorter-lookback EMA < its longer-lookback EMA. Use the last completed higher-timeframe bar in historical evaluation (the source uses lookahead off).
Calculation: Confluence score = completion ≥ 85%: 2 points, otherwise completion ≥ minimum formation threshold: 1; add 2 for alignment with enabled HTF bias, 0 for enabled counter-bias, or 1 when HTF bias is disabled; add 1 when chop index is below its ceiling; add 2 for FIRED, 1 for ARMED, 0 for FORMING; add 1 when enabled volume-coil test passes (chart-timeframe volume ≤ configured fraction of its longer-lookback volume average); add 1 when reward/risk to target ≥ minimum; add 1 when pattern height ≥ 1.5 × ATR. Cap at 10. Chop index = 100 × log10(sum of true ranges over its lookback ÷ (highest high − lowest low over that lookback)) ÷ log10(lookback); use 50 if the range is zero.
Calculation: Selected setup = highest-ranked active setup among the five setup families, with rank = confluence score + 0.05 × completion percentage + 3 for FIRED, 2 for ARMED, or 0 for FORMING; ties keep the earlier family in this order: double top/bottom, head-and-shoulders/inverse, triangle, flag, wedge. Consider setups only when the chart has more than 200 bars, both swing lists have at least two entries, and the chop gate passes. Discard the selected setup if completion is below the minimum, confluence is below its minimum, or (for FIRED only) reward/risk is below its minimum; optionally discard FIRED setups counter to enabled HTF bias. Reward/risk = absolute target distance from entry ÷ absolute stop distance from entry; entry is current bar open. For double tops/bottoms, H&S/inverse, and triangles, target is one pattern height beyond the boundary; for flags, one pole height beyond the boundary; for wedges, target is the older of the latest two swing highs (falling) or lows (rising). Stop is the opposite triangle line, opposite flag/wedge line, or entry moved against the trade by configured stop fraction of target distance for double and H&S patterns.
Long: Selected setup is bullish AND phase is ARMED or FIRED AND the current chart bar is confirmed AND its setup-and-phase key differs from the previous event AND (no prior FIRED event exists or at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since it).
Short: Selected setup is bearish AND phase is ARMED or FIRED AND the current chart bar is confirmed AND its setup-and-phase key differs from the previous event AND (no prior FIRED event exists or at least the configured cooldown bars have elapsed since it).
