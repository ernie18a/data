Calculation: Most recent EMA crossover direction = whether the shorter-period EMA crossed above or below the longer-period EMA most recently
Long: The most recent EMA crossover direction is upward AND close > VWAP AND close > shorter-period EMA AND the previous bar was flat
Short: The most recent EMA crossover direction is downward AND close < VWAP AND close < shorter-period EMA AND the previous bar was flat
Exit: Long, close < VWAP OR (close < shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA crosses below longer-period EMA)
Exit: Short, close > VWAP OR (close > shorter-period EMA AND shorter-period EMA crosses above longer-period EMA)
