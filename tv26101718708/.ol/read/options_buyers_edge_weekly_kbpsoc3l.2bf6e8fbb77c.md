Calculation: ATM IV proxy = (ATM call close + ATM put close) / (0.8 × underlying close × √(time to expiry in years)) × 100
Calculation: IV slope = ATM IV proxy − its shorter-lookback exponential moving average
Calculation: Directional flow = (call buyer pressure − put buyer pressure) / (|call buyer pressure| + |put buyer pressure|), or 0 if the denominator is 0; each pressure is the sum across the seven strikes from three steps below to three steps above ATM of +volume × strike weight when option close rises from the prior chart bar, otherwise −volume × strike weight; strike weights are 0.3, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 0.8, 0.5, 0.3 from lowest to highest strike
Calculation: Skew-gated flow = directional flow when skew gating is disabled, skew is unavailable, or directional flow agrees with skew; otherwise 0. Skew = put close two strikes below ATM − call close two strikes above ATM; positive flow agrees when skew ≤ 0, negative flow agrees when skew ≥ 0
Calculation: Lower-timeframe volume imbalance = (up-volume − down-volume) / (up-volume + down-volume), using lower-timeframe bars within the chart bar; up-volume sums volume when close is at least the previous lower-timeframe close, and down-volume sums volume when close is lower; 0 if total volume is 0
Calculation: Direction score = 0.40 × sign(three-chart-bar close change / ATR) × min(|three-chart-bar close change / ATR|, 1) + 0.25 × clamp(change in futures basis over the longer lookback / ATR, −1, 1) + 0.20 × skew-gated flow + 0.15 × lower-timeframe volume imbalance; futures basis = futures close − underlying close
Long: Direction score > direction threshold AND IV slope ≥ 0 AND direction score on the prior chart bar ≤ direction threshold
Short: Direction score < −direction threshold AND IV slope ≥ 0 AND direction score on the prior chart bar ≥ −direction threshold
