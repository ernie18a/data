Calculation: ZigZag pivots x,a,b,c,d = the five latest alternating reversal pivots; a down-to-up candle reversal marks the lower of the two bars’ lows, and an up-to-down reversal marks the higher of their highs. Use chart-timeframe bars or, when selected, higher-timeframe bars sampled at the higher-timeframe boundary while that bar is forming.
Calculation: xab = |b−a|/|x−a|; xad = |a−d|/|x−a|; abc = |b−c|/|a−b|; bcd = |c−d|/|b−c|.
Calculation: Fib level(r) = d−|d−c|×r when d>c, otherwise d+|d−c|×r.
Calculation: Bull pattern = d<c AND any of: ABCD (abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 1.13–2.618); Bat (xab 0.382–0.5, abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 1.618–2.618, xad ≤0.618); AntiBat (xab 0.5–0.886, abc 1–2.618, bcd 1.618–2.618, xad 0.886–1); AltBat (xab ≤0.382, abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 2–3.618, xad ≤1.13); Butterfly (xab ≤0.786, abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 1.618–2.618, xad 1.27–1.618); AntiButterfly (xab 0.236–0.886, abc 1.13–2.618, bcd 1–1.382, xad 0.5–0.886); Gartley (xab 0.5–0.618, abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 1.13–2.618, xad 0.75–0.875); AntiGartley (xab 0.5–0.886, abc 1–2.618, bcd 1.5–5, xad 1–5); Crab (xab 0.5–0.875, abc 0.382–0.886, bcd 2–5, xad 1.382–5); AntiCrab (xab 0.25–0.5, abc 1.13–2.618, bcd 1.618–2.618, xad 0.5–0.75); Shark (xab 0.5–0.875, abc 1.13–1.618, bcd 1.27–2.24, xad 0.886–1.13); AntiShark (xab 0.382–0.875, abc 0.5–1, bcd 1.25–2.618, xad 0.5–1.25); 5-O (xab 1.13–1.618, abc 1.618–2.24, bcd 0.5–0.625, xad 0–0.236); Wolf (xab 1.27–1.618, abc 0–5, bcd 1.27–1.618, xad 0–5); Head and Shoulders (xab 2–10, abc 0.9–1.1, bcd 0.236–0.88, xad 0.9–1.1); Contracting Triangle (xab, abc, bcd each 0.382–0.618, xad 0.236–0.764); Expanding Triangle (xab 1.236–1.618, abc 1–1.618, bcd 1.236–2, xad 2–2.236).
Calculation: Bear pattern = the same pattern conditions as Bull pattern with d>c.
Calculation: Entry-eligible pattern = ABCD, Bat, AltBat, Butterfly, Gartley, Crab, Shark, 5-O, Wolf, Head and Shoulders, Contracting Triangle, Expanding Triangle, AntiBat, AntiButterfly, AntiGartley, AntiCrab, or AntiShark.
Long: (Bull pattern AND NOT Bull pattern on the previous bar) OR (d<c AND Entry-eligible pattern AND close ≤ Fib level(entry-window rate))
Short: (Bear pattern AND NOT Bear pattern on the previous bar) OR (d>c AND Entry-eligible pattern AND close ≥ Fib level(entry-window rate))
Exit: long, high ≥ Fib level(take-profit rate) OR low ≤ Fib level(stop-loss rate)
Exit: short, low ≤ Fib level(take-profit rate) OR high ≥ Fib level(stop-loss rate)
