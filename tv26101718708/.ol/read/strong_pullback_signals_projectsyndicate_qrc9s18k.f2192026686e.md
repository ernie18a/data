Calculation: Fast, pullback, and slow EMAs = exponential moving averages with progressively longer lookbacks, from fast to slow; calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Average true range = ATR calculated on chart bars.
Calculation: Pullback limit = pullback EMA minus entry depth times ATR for longs; pullback EMA plus entry depth times ATR for shorts.
Calculation: Pullback swing extreme = lowest low for a long setup or highest high for a short setup, from its breakout bar through the current bar.
Calculation: Entry price = the lower of the current open and pullback limit for longs; the higher for shorts.
Calculation: Stop = entry price minus the clamped risk distance for longs, or plus it for shorts. Risk distance is the absolute distance from entry price to the swing extreme adjusted outward by an ATR buffer, clamped between configured minimum and maximum ATR risk distances.
Calculation: Profit targets = entry price plus the stop risk distance times each configured R multiple for longs, or minus it for shorts; use progressively larger R multiples for targets 1, 2, and 3.
Calculation: Higher-timeframe EMA = EMA calculated on bars of a higher timeframe, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar.
Calculation: Setup score = 10 times the weighted average of six 0–1 components: higher-timeframe alignment (0.6 if its filter is off or its EMA is unavailable; otherwise 1 when aligned and 0 when not), session quality (0 during configured weak hours, otherwise 1), trend separation (absolute fast/slow EMA distance divided by ATR and a configured normalization distance, capped at 1), entry depth (depth divided by a configured reference depth, capped at 1), directional RSI momentum (the RSI distance beyond 50 in the trade direction divided by a configured normalization distance, bounded to 0–1), and volume (current volume divided by its chart-bar 20-period SMA, capped at 1). The six configured component weights define the weighted average; if their sum is zero, score is zero.
Long: no position is active AND no setup is armed AND cooldown permits a new setup AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > slow EMA AND fast EMA > its value at the configured slope lookback AND close > the highest high of the preceding configured breakout lookback bars AND close > open AND absolute(close − open) >= ATR × configured minimum breakout-body ratio; then, after the configured minimum wait from that breakout, while the setup remains armed, low <= pullback limit AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close > higher-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current exchange-time hour is not a configured weak hour) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI >= configured long minimum) AND close > slow EMA AND (fill-close confirmation disabled OR close > open) AND (strong-score-only filter disabled OR setup score >= configured threshold). The setup swing extreme includes the current bar; update it with each armed bar. The entry fill uses the entry price defined above.
Short: no position is active AND no setup is armed AND cooldown permits a new setup AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < slow EMA AND fast EMA < its value at the configured slope lookback AND close < the lowest low of the preceding configured breakout lookback bars AND close < open AND absolute(close − open) >= ATR × configured minimum breakout-body ratio; then, after the configured minimum wait from that breakout, while the setup remains armed, high >= pullback limit AND (higher-timeframe filter disabled OR higher-timeframe EMA unavailable OR close < higher-timeframe EMA) AND (session filter disabled OR current exchange-time hour is not a configured weak hour) AND (RSI filter disabled OR RSI <= configured short maximum) AND close < slow EMA AND (fill-close confirmation disabled OR close < open) AND (strong-score-only filter disabled OR setup score >= configured threshold). The setup swing extreme includes the current bar; update it with each armed bar. The entry fill uses the entry price defined above.
Exit: Long, low <= stop OR high >= target 3, while a long position is active. Track the highest target reached on each bar; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, count the stop and exit. If stop is reached after target 1 or 2 was reached earlier, exit at stop and record the highest prior target reached.
Exit: Short, high >= stop OR low <= target 3, while a short position is active. Track the highest target reached on each bar; if stop and any target are touched on the same bar, count the stop and exit. If stop is reached after target 1 or 2 was reached earlier, exit at stop and record the highest prior target reached.
