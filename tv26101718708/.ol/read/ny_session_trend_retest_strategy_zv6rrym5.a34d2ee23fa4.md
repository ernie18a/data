Calculation: Anchored session VWAP = cumulative (typical price × volume) / cumulative volume from the first regular-session bar, where typical price = (high + low + close) / 3
Calculation: Premarket high/low = running highest high / lowest low during the current day’s premarket session
Calculation: VWAP slope = (current anchored session VWAP − VWAP at the configured lookback) / minimum tick; price range = (highest high − lowest low) over the configured lookback, in minimum ticks
Calculation: Candle-body percentage above/below a level = the percentage of the open-close body above/below that level, provided the close is above/below the level; for a zero-size body, use 100% when the close is beyond the level, otherwise 0%
Long: During the regular session, flat, and outside the post-close cooldown; if the regime filter is enabled, absolute VWAP slope meets its minimum and price range meets its minimum; and at least one enabled entry applies: low ≤ VWAP + retest buffer AND close > VWAP AND close > higher-timeframe EMA of close (higher timeframe, last completed bar); OR, in premarket breakout close mode, premarket high exists AND candle-body percentage above premarket high meets its threshold AND close > higher-timeframe EMA; OR, in premarket breakout retest mode, low ≤ premarket high + retest buffer AND close > premarket high AND close > higher-timeframe EMA; OR, low ≤ entry EMA + retest buffer AND close > entry EMA AND close > higher-timeframe EMA; also require close > VWAP if that optional filter is enabled and close > entry EMA if that optional filter is enabled
Short: During the regular session, flat, and outside the post-close cooldown; if the regime filter is enabled, absolute VWAP slope meets its minimum and price range meets its minimum; and at least one enabled entry applies: high ≥ VWAP − retest buffer AND close < VWAP AND close ≤ higher-timeframe EMA of close (higher timeframe, last completed bar); OR, in premarket breakout close mode, premarket low exists AND candle-body percentage below premarket low meets its threshold AND close ≤ higher-timeframe EMA; OR, in premarket breakout retest mode, high ≥ premarket low − retest buffer AND close < premarket low AND close ≤ higher-timeframe EMA; OR, high ≥ entry EMA − retest buffer AND close < entry EMA AND close ≤ higher-timeframe EMA; also require close < VWAP if that optional filter is enabled and close < entry EMA if that optional filter is enabled
Exit: Long, low ≤ the fixed stop level set at entry (lowest low over the recent lookback minus stop buffer) OR outside the regular session OR high ≥ premarket high when entry price < premarket high OR high ≥ running regular-session high OR, after a partial target, low ≤ entry price if breakeven stop is enabled OR candle-body percentage below VWAP meets its threshold while VWAP-exit override is active OR candle-body percentage below exit EMA meets its threshold while the override is inactive. The override starts when enabled and entry price < exit EMA; it deactivates after close > exit EMA AND close > entry price + the configured profit distance.
Exit: Short, high ≥ the fixed stop level set at entry (highest high over the recent lookback plus stop buffer) OR outside the regular session OR low ≤ premarket low when entry price > premarket low OR low ≤ running regular-session low OR, after a partial target, high ≥ entry price if breakeven stop is enabled OR candle-body percentage above VWAP meets its threshold while VWAP-exit override is active OR candle-body percentage above exit EMA meets its threshold while the override is inactive. The override starts when enabled and entry price > exit EMA; it deactivates after close < exit EMA AND close < entry price − the configured profit distance.
