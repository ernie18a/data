Calculation: Liquidity Pressure = EMA(((close - open) / (high - low)) * volume, smoothing length) / EMA(volume, same length); when high = low, use 0 for the division.
Calculation: Adaptive EMA(source, length, lookback) = recursive EMA with alpha = 2 / (length + 1) * min(1, abs(source - source[lookback]) / highest(abs(source - source[lookback]), length)); use 0 for the ratio when its denominator is 0, and initialize with source.
Calculation: Cycle Line = EMA(adaptive EMA(z, slow length, fast lag), ribbon smoothing), where z = (source - baseline) / (0.015 * deviation), baseline = adaptive EMA(source, base length, adaptation lookback), and deviation = adaptive EMA(abs(source - baseline), base length, adaptation lookback).
Calculation: Signal Line = EMA(adaptive EMA(unsmoothed Cycle Line, max(2, round(slow length * 0.35)), slow lag), same ribbon smoothing). Fast lag is shorter than slow lag.
Calculation: Confirmed bullish/bearish divergence = compare confirmed price pivot lows/highs with the nearest confirmed Cycle Line pivot of the same type among the three most recent, if within the maximum matching gap. Compare each current matched pair with up to the three preceding matched pairs whose price pivots are separated by the permitted minimum and maximum bars. Bullish regular: lower price low and higher oscillator low; bullish hidden: higher price low and lower oscillator low. Bearish regular: higher price high and lower oscillator high; bearish hidden: lower price high and higher oscillator high. If zero-line context is required, both oscillator pivots must be at or below zero for bullish, or at or above zero for bearish divergences. Pivots and divergences become available only after pivot confirmation.
Long: On a completed bar, Cycle Line crosses above Signal Line AND (threshold filter disabled OR Cycle Line < negative threshold) AND (pressure confirmation off OR pressure confirmation is same direction and Liquidity Pressure > 0 OR pressure confirmation is zero-cross and Liquidity Pressure crosses above zero) OR Liquidity Pressure crosses above zero OR a confirmed bullish regular or enabled hidden divergence.
Short: On a completed bar, Cycle Line crosses below Signal Line AND (threshold filter disabled OR Cycle Line > positive threshold) AND (pressure confirmation off OR pressure confirmation is same direction and Liquidity Pressure < 0 OR pressure confirmation is zero-cross and Liquidity Pressure crosses below zero) OR Liquidity Pressure crosses below zero OR a confirmed bearish regular or enabled hidden divergence.
