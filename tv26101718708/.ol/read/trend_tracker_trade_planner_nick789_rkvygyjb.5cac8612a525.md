Calculation: Source = (open + high + low + close) / 4
Calculation: Basis = fast EMA of Source in fast mode, or the average of fast and slow EMAs in smooth mode; the slow EMA has a longer lookback than the fast EMA
Calculation: ATR bands = Basis ± ATR × band multiplier
Calculation: Tracker direction = bullish when Source exceeds the prior upper ATR band, bearish when Source falls below the prior lower ATR band, otherwise the prior direction; initialize bullish
Calculation: Tracker line = in bullish direction, the greater of the lower ATR band and the prior tracker line if the prior direction was bullish (otherwise the lower band); in bearish direction, the lesser of the upper ATR band and the prior tracker line if the prior direction was bearish (otherwise the upper band)
Calculation: Trend delta = cumulative buy volume minus cumulative sell volume since the latest tracker direction change; buy/sell volume is volume of up/down candles, summed from lower-timeframe bars when that optional source is enabled and available, otherwise from chart bars
Calculation: Trend delta percentage = Trend delta / cumulative buy and sell volume
Calculation: Risk = the greater of the distance from entry to the tracker line (or ATR × stop multiplier when fixed ATR stop is selected) and a minimum risk amount
Calculation: Targets = entry price plus risk × target ratio for a long, or minus risk × target ratio for a short; stop = entry price minus risk for a long, or plus risk for a short
Long: tracker direction changes from bearish to bullish AND (the optional higher-timeframe tracker direction agrees, or a previously blocked bullish flip is followed within the wait limit by higher-timeframe agreement while the local tracker remains bullish) OR a pending bullish flip remains valid and Trend delta ≥ 0 OR tracker direction is bearish AND (optional low-band breach condition is met) AND volume exceeds its moving average by a configured multiple AND close > low + (high − low) × wick sensitivity AND Trend delta percentage > its prior value
Short: tracker direction changes from bullish to bearish AND (the optional higher-timeframe tracker direction agrees, or a previously blocked bearish flip is followed within the wait limit by higher-timeframe agreement while the local tracker remains bearish) OR a pending bearish flip remains valid and Trend delta ≤ 0 OR tracker direction is bullish AND (optional upper-band breach condition is met) AND volume exceeds its moving average by a configured multiple AND close < high − (high − low) × wick sensitivity AND Trend delta percentage < its prior value
Exit: long, high ≥ first target OR high ≥ second target OR high ≥ third target OR low ≤ stop
Exit: short, low ≤ first target OR low ≤ second target OR low ≤ third target OR high ≥ stop
