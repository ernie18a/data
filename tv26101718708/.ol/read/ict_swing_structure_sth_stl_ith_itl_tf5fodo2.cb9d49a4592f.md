Calculation: Confirmed short-term swing high = On the configured structure timeframe (same as chart when chart-timeframe mode is enabled; otherwise the configured higher timeframe), process each bar using its high and low, including the still-forming structure-timeframe bar: ignore bars whose high is at or below the reference high and whose low is at or above the reference low; otherwise, if no high candidate exists and the bar high exceeds the reference high, start a candidate at that high; if a candidate exists, update it when the bar high is greater than or equal to the candidate, otherwise confirm the candidate. After each non-inside bar, set the reference high and low to that bar's high and low. Equal highs are allowed.
Calculation: Confirmed short-term swing low = Apply the same state process independently: ignore bars whose high is at or below the reference high and whose low is at or above the reference low; otherwise, if no low candidate exists and the bar low is below the reference low, start a candidate at that low; if a candidate exists, update it when the bar low is less than or equal to the candidate, otherwise confirm the candidate. After each non-inside bar, set the reference high and low to that bar's high and low. Equal lows are allowed.
Calculation: Intermediate-term swing high = The middle of the latest three confirmed short-term swing highs is higher than both adjacent swing highs.
Calculation: Intermediate-term swing low = The middle of the latest three confirmed short-term swing lows is lower than both adjacent swing lows.
Long: Confirmed short-term swing low OR intermediate-term swing low
Short: Confirmed short-term swing high OR intermediate-term swing high
