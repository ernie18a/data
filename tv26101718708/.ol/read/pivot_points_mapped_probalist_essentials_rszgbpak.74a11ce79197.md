Calculation: Pivot levels = from the last completed bar of a higher timeframe: pivot P is (high + low + close)/3, or (high + low + 2×close)/4 for the close-weighted method; for the range R = high − low, Classic levels are R1=2P−low, S1=2P−high, R2=P+R, S2=P−R, R3=high+2(P−low), S3=low−2(high−P); Fibonacci levels are P ± 0.382R, P ± 0.618R, P ± R; Camarilla levels are close ± (1.1/12)R, close ± (1.1/6)R, close ± (1.1/4)R, with positive offsets for R1–R3 and negative offsets for S1–S3.
Calculation: Touch tolerance = ATR × a configurable multiplier
Long: Previous completed bar low ≤ S1 + touch tolerance AND current completed bar close > S1 OR previous completed bar low ≤ S2 + touch tolerance AND current completed bar close > S2 OR previous completed bar low ≤ S3 + touch tolerance AND current completed bar close > S3
Short: Previous completed bar high ≥ R1 − touch tolerance AND current completed bar close < R1 OR previous completed bar high ≥ R2 − touch tolerance AND current completed bar close < R2 OR previous completed bar high ≥ R3 − touch tolerance AND current completed bar close < R3
