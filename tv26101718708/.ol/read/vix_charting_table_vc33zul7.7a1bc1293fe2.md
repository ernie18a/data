Calculation: Twin Range Filter = average of two smoothed ranges, one using a shorter lookback and one a longer lookback. Each smoothed range is EMA(EMA(abs(close − prior close), lookback), 2 × lookback − 1) × multiplier. Use their average as the range width. The range filter follows close by at most that width, moving upward when close rises and downward when close falls. The filter’s upper and lower bands are the filter plus and minus the range width; trail each band until the prior close crosses it, then switch the TRF line between the bands according to which band the prior TRF line followed and which side close occupies.
Long: volatility-index close <= TRF calculated on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart
Short: volatility-index close > TRF calculated on the last completed bar of a higher timeframe than the chart
