Calculation: MA1, MA2, MA3 = each enabled, independently configured moving average of close; when multiple are enabled, the code aligns them in slot order (MA1 above MA2 above MA3 for bullish, reverse for bearish), with no enforced lookback ordering. A configured timeframe may be the chart timeframe or another timeframe; requests use that timeframe’s current bar, including a still-forming bar when it is higher than the chart timeframe.
Long: Position is not long AND close is above every enabled MA AND (only MA1 enabled OR only MA2 enabled OR only MA3 enabled OR MA1 and MA2 enabled and MA1 > MA2 OR MA1 and MA3 enabled and MA1 > MA3 OR MA2 and MA3 enabled and MA2 > MA3 OR all three enabled and MA1 > MA2 AND MA2 > MA3) AND (DMI filter disabled OR +DI > -DI AND ADX > threshold) AND (higher-timeframe KAMA filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe KAMA) AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close > higher-timeframe VWAP). Higher-timeframe KAMA and VWAP use the current bar, including a still-forming bar.
Short: Position is not short AND close is below every enabled MA AND (only MA1 enabled OR only MA2 enabled OR only MA3 enabled OR MA1 and MA2 enabled and MA1 < MA2 OR MA1 and MA3 enabled and MA1 < MA3 OR MA2 and MA3 enabled and MA2 < MA3 OR all three enabled and MA1 < MA2 AND MA2 < MA3) AND (DMI filter disabled OR -DI > +DI AND ADX > threshold) AND (higher-timeframe KAMA filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe KAMA) AND (higher-timeframe VWAP filter disabled OR close < higher-timeframe VWAP). Higher-timeframe KAMA and VWAP use the current bar, including a still-forming bar.
Exit: long, bearish conditions for a short signal are true
Exit: short, bullish conditions for a long signal are true
