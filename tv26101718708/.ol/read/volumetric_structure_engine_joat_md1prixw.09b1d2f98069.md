Calculation: Confirmed swing high = previous bar's high equals the highest high in the lookback window ending on that bar, and current bar's high is below the highest high in the lookback window ending on the current bar; evaluate on bar close.
Calculation: Confirmed swing low = previous bar's low equals the lowest low in the lookback window ending on that bar, and current bar's low is above the lowest low in the lookback window ending on the current bar; evaluate on bar close.
Calculation: Structure trend = initially neutral; turns bullish when a confirmed swing high exceeds the previous confirmed swing high while trend is bearish, when a confirmed swing low is below the previous confirmed swing low while trend is bullish, or when a bullish break of structure occurs; turns bearish on the converse conditions; otherwise retains its prior value.
Long: confirmed swing high AND its price > previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low AND its price > previous confirmed swing low OR close > last confirmed swing high AND structure trend is bearish AND last confirmed swing high != the level of the previous bullish break of structure
Short: confirmed swing high AND its price < previous confirmed swing high OR confirmed swing low AND its price < previous confirmed swing low OR close < last confirmed swing low AND structure trend is bullish AND last confirmed swing low != the level of the previous bearish break of structure
