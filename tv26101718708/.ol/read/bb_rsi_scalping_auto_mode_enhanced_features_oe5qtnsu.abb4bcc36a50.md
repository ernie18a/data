Calculation: Dynamic RSI oversold = lowest RSI over a lookback + buffer; dynamic RSI overbought = highest RSI over a lookback − buffer
Calculation: MACD bullish inflection = histogram rises after falling; bearish inflection = histogram falls after rising
Calculation: Bullish divergence = a confirmed pivot low has lower price and higher RSI than the preceding pivot low within a maximum bar gap; bearish divergence = a confirmed pivot high has higher price and lower RSI than the preceding pivot high within a maximum bar gap
Calculation: Recent bullish/bearish MACD inflection = the corresponding inflection occurred within the confirmation window
Calculation: Recent strong volume = volume exceeds its moving average times a multiplier, optionally also exceeding the prior bar's volume
Calculation: Auto mode = aggressive when Bollinger Band width and ATR exceed their respective moving averages and volume exceeds its moving average times a higher multiplier; conservative when Bollinger Band width is not expanding and volume does not exceed that higher threshold; balanced otherwise
Long: flat, in session if the session filter is enabled, and (effective mode is conservative AND ((low < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold AND recent bullish MACD inflection AND recent strong volume AND close > lower Bollinger Band AND close > prior close) OR (bullish divergence AND pivot low < lower Bollinger Band at that pivot AND RSI at that pivot <= dynamic RSI oversold at that pivot AND recent bullish MACD inflection AND recent strong volume)) OR effective mode is balanced AND low <= lower Bollinger Band + Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry buffer AND close > open OR effective mode is aggressive AND low <= lower Bollinger Band + Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry buffer OR continuation mode is enabled AND low <= Bollinger Band basis AND close > basis AND close > open AND RSI > 50 AND MACD histogram > prior histogram AND MACD line >= signal line AND recent strong volume)
Long: flat AND low <= lower Bollinger Band + Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI <= dynamic RSI oversold + near-entry buffer (near-long marking, subject to its display setting and repeat cooldown)
Long: flat AND continuation mode is enabled AND the count of true conditions among low <= basis, close > basis, close > open, RSI > 50, MACD histogram > prior histogram, MACD line >= signal line, and recent strong volume meets the warning score (continuation warning marking, subject to its display setting and cooldown)
Short: flat, in session if the session filter is enabled, and (effective mode is conservative AND ((high > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought AND recent bearish MACD inflection AND recent strong volume AND close < upper Bollinger Band AND close < prior close) OR (bearish divergence AND pivot high > upper Bollinger Band at that pivot AND RSI at that pivot >= dynamic RSI overbought at that pivot AND recent bearish MACD inflection AND recent strong volume)) OR effective mode is balanced AND high >= upper Bollinger Band − Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry buffer AND close < open OR effective mode is aggressive AND high >= upper Bollinger Band − Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry buffer OR continuation mode is enabled AND high >= Bollinger Band basis AND close < basis AND close < open AND RSI < 50 AND MACD histogram < prior histogram AND MACD line <= signal line AND recent strong volume)
Short: flat AND high >= upper Bollinger Band − Bollinger Band width × near-band percentage AND RSI >= dynamic RSI overbought − near-entry buffer (near-short marking, subject to its display setting and repeat cooldown)
Short: flat AND continuation mode is enabled AND the count of true conditions among high >= basis, close < basis, close < open, RSI < 50, MACD histogram < prior histogram, MACD line <= signal line, and recent strong volume meets the warning score (continuation warning marking, subject to its display setting and cooldown)
Exit: Long, (trade is a reversal AND selected reversal exit condition is met, or trade is a continuation AND selected continuation exit condition is met) OR low <= initial invalidation level OR low <= ratcheting dynamic exit level OR enabled trailing stop is activated and low <= trailing stop OR enabled break-even stop is activated and low <= entry price OR enabled take-profit rules trigger a full exit OR enabled maximum holding time is reached
Exit: Short, (trade is a reversal AND selected reversal exit condition is met, or trade is a continuation AND selected continuation exit condition is met) OR high >= initial invalidation level OR high >= ratcheting dynamic exit level OR enabled trailing stop is activated and high >= trailing stop OR enabled break-even stop is activated and high >= entry price OR enabled take-profit rules trigger a full exit OR enabled maximum holding time is reached
Exit: Long, (exit-soon marking enabled AND MACD histogram < prior histogram AND no full or partial exit)
Exit: Short, (exit-soon marking enabled AND MACD histogram > prior histogram AND no full or partial exit)
Exit: Long, selected reversal exit = Momentum Reversal: enabled bearish MACD histogram inflection OR enabled bearish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross below 50; First Retrace: close < open; Retrace + MACD Weakness: close < open AND MACD histogram < prior histogram
Exit: Short, selected reversal exit = Momentum Reversal: enabled bullish MACD histogram inflection OR enabled bullish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross above 50; First Retrace: close > open; Retrace + MACD Weakness: close > open AND MACD histogram > prior histogram
Exit: Long, selected continuation exit = Momentum Reversal: enabled bearish MACD histogram inflection OR enabled bearish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross below 50; MACD Cross Only: bearish MACD cross; Basis Break + MACD Weakness: close < Bollinger Band basis AND MACD histogram < prior histogram
Exit: Short, selected continuation exit = Momentum Reversal: enabled bullish MACD histogram inflection OR enabled bullish MACD cross OR enabled RSI cross above 50; MACD Cross Only: bullish MACD cross; Basis Break + MACD Weakness: close > Bollinger Band basis AND MACD histogram > prior histogram
