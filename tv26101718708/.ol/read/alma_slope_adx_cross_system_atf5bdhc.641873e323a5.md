Calculation: Selected moving average = selected ALMA, EMA, VWMA, Hull moving average, or EMA(close, MA length) × (1 + TSI(close, short smoothing, long smoothing))
Calculation: MA slope = selected moving average − its value slope-lookback bars earlier
Calculation: ADX = average directional index, using the same lookback for directional movement and ADX smoothing
Calculation: ATR stop percentage = ATR × stop multiplier / close
Calculation: Volatility multiplier = current ATR / simple moving average of current ATR over a longer lookback
Long: ADX ≥ threshold AND (ADX rising or rising requirement disabled) AND cooldown elapsed AND ((slope-entry mode AND (MA slope crosses above zero OR mid-trend re-entry condition)) OR (price-cross-entry mode AND (close crosses above selected moving average OR mid-trend re-entry condition))); mid-trend re-entry condition = re-entry enabled AND MA slope > 0 AND MA slope > slope at last exit AND MA slope rising for the required consecutive bars
Short: ADX ≥ threshold AND (ADX rising or rising requirement disabled) AND cooldown elapsed AND ((slope-entry mode AND (MA slope crosses below zero OR mid-trend re-entry condition)) OR (price-cross-entry mode AND (close crosses below selected moving average OR mid-trend re-entry condition))); mid-trend re-entry condition = re-entry enabled AND MA slope < 0 AND MA slope < slope at last exit AND MA slope falling for the required consecutive bars
Exit: long, close crosses below selected moving average (if reverse MA exit enabled) OR close crosses below VWMA (if reverse VWMA exit enabled) OR MA slope stays positive and decreases for the required consecutive bars (if slope-deceleration exit enabled) OR MA slope crosses below zero (if zero-cross exit enabled) OR low ≤ the higher of the initial stop at entry price × (1 − stop percentage) and the highest high since entry × (1 − stop percentage) (if stop enabled) OR high ≥ entry price × (1 + take-profit percentage) (if take profit enabled) OR the trailing stop is activated when high reaches entry price × (1 + trailing start distance) and low reaches the highest high since entry × (1 − trailing follow distance)
Exit: short, close crosses above selected moving average (if reverse MA exit enabled) OR close crosses above VWMA (if reverse VWMA exit enabled) OR MA slope stays negative and increases for the required consecutive bars (if slope-deceleration exit enabled) OR MA slope crosses above zero (if zero-cross exit enabled) OR high ≥ the lower of the initial stop at entry price × (1 + stop percentage) and the lowest low since entry × (1 + stop percentage) (if stop enabled) OR low ≤ entry price × (1 − take-profit percentage) (if take profit enabled) OR the trailing stop is activated when low reaches entry price × (1 − trailing start distance) and high reaches the lowest low since entry × (1 + trailing follow distance)
