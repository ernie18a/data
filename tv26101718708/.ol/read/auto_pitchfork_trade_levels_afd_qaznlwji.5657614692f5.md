Calculation: Confirmed swing pivot = a bar whose high is strictly above (or low strictly below) the highs (or lows) of the swing-length bars on each side; confirm only after those later bars complete. Consecutive pivots must alternate high and low; replace a same-kind pivot only if the new one is more extreme.
Calculation: Pitchfork = construct the selected Standard, Schiff, or Modified Schiff median through the midpoint of P1–P2, with parallel lines through P1 and P2; warning lines are one median-to-parallel distance beyond each parallel. For Standard, the median starts at P0; for Schiff, it starts at P0’s bar with price halfway from P0 to P1; for Modified Schiff, both start bar and price are halfway from P0 to P1.
Calculation: Valid fork = three alternating confirmed pivots P0–P1–P2 pass all of these comparisons: both directional legs are at least the minimum ATR-scaled leg size; both pivot gaps are at least the swing length; P2 is beyond P0 in the setup direction; fork width is within the configured ATR-scaled bounds; price structure between pivots remains intact within the configured ATR tolerance; and the confirming close lies between the warning lines, allowing the configured ATR margin. Fork width is the distance between the two parallels.
Calculation: ATR = average true range over the configured lookback, using chart-timeframe bars. For intraday charts, the R cap uses ATR from the last completed higher-timeframe daily bar; otherwise it uses chart ATR.
Calculation: Stop = beyond P2 by the configured ATR margin, with a minimum distance of half the capped R; round outward to the tick grid. Capped R is the smaller of the absolute P1–P2 distance and the configured multiple of the ATR cap. Entry is the confirming close rounded to the tick grid.
Calculation: Targets = according to the selected basis: (a) actual-stop basis uses Entry-to-Stop distance as the unit; (b) ATR basis uses the fork’s ATR; each has three successive targets at configured unit multiples, rounded outward from Entry; (c) median basis uses the median as the sole target; (d) structure basis uses the median, then the parallel and warning line on the setup’s target side. Sloped targets already at or behind Entry when the fork forms are excluded.
Long: A valid fork forms with P0 and P2 confirmed low pivots and P1 a high pivot, with P2 above P0.
Short: A valid fork forms with P0 and P2 confirmed high pivots and P1 a low pivot, with P2 below P0.
Exit: long, low <= Stop OR high >= the last available target OR close > upper warning line + configured ATR margin OR fork age reaches its configured limit OR a newer valid fork replaces it.
Exit: short, high >= Stop OR low <= the last available target OR close < lower warning line - configured ATR margin OR fork age reaches its configured limit OR a newer valid fork replaces it.
