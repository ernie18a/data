Calculation: Momentum adjustment = (close − close N bars ago) / close N bars ago × (1 + Wilder-smoothed average of positive close changes over N bars / Wilder-smoothed average of absolute negative close changes over N bars); use 1 for the ratio when average loss is zero
Calculation: CETP score = mean of the decayed weighted candle body ratios over N bars × (1 − normalized Shannon entropy of the N-bar 3D histogram) × (momentum weight + momentum adjustment × momentum scale) × trend multiplier. For each bar, body ratio = (close − open) / (high − low + ε) × body weight; upper wick ratio = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low + ε) × upper-wick weight; lower wick ratio = (min(open, close) − low) / (high − low + ε) × lower-wick weight. Shift the prior N ratios one place per bar, multiplying shifted values by the decay factor, and append the current ratios. Bin each stored ratio using bin size 2 / bins per dimension × (1 + ATR(14) / SMA(ATR(14), 14)); clamp each bin index to [0, bins per dimension − 1], and count the 3D bin combinations. Normalized entropy = −sum(p × ln(p)) / ln((bins per dimension)^3), where p is each bin count / N. Trend multiplier = 1.2 when Wilder-smoothed positive high changes / ATR(14) differs from Wilder-smoothed negative low changes / ATR(14), otherwise 1.0
Long: CETP score > long threshold base / 1.5 AND abs(CETP score) > minimum score strength AND 20-bar close standard deviation / 20-bar close SMA × 100 < volatility limit AND high − low > max(ATR(14) × minimum price-move multiplier, 10 × minimum tick) AND abs(momentum adjustment) > 0.05 AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × minimum volume multiplier
Short: CETP score < short threshold base / 1.5 AND abs(CETP score) > minimum score strength AND 20-bar close standard deviation / 20-bar close SMA × 100 < volatility limit AND high − low > max(ATR(14) × minimum price-move multiplier, 10 × minimum tick) AND abs(momentum adjustment) > 0.05 AND volume > 20-bar volume SMA × minimum volume multiplier
Exit: Long, low <= entry price × (1 − long stop-loss percent / 100) − ATR(14) × ATR multiplier OR trailing stop is hit OR short condition is true OR bars held > maximum hold bars
Exit: Short, high >= entry price × (1 + short stop-loss percent / 100) + ATR(14) × ATR multiplier OR trailing stop is hit OR long condition is true OR bars held > maximum hold bars
