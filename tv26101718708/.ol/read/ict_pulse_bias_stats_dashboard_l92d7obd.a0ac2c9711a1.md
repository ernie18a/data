Calculation: Bullish score = count of these conditions: chart close is at or above the midpoint of the previous completed daily range (daily timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart close is at or above the midpoint of the previous completed weekly range (weekly timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart close is at or above the midpoint of the previous completed monthly range (monthly timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart low is below the low of at least one completed Asia, London, or New York session; chart close is at or above the midpoint of the active session's range so far
Calculation: Bearish score = count of these conditions: chart close is below the midpoint of the previous completed daily range (daily timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart close is below the midpoint of the previous completed weekly range (weekly timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart close is below the midpoint of the previous completed monthly range (monthly timeframe higher than chart timeframe); chart high is above the high of at least one completed Asia, London, or New York session; chart close is below the midpoint of the active session's range so far
Long: Bullish score > Bearish score
Short: Bearish score > Bullish score
