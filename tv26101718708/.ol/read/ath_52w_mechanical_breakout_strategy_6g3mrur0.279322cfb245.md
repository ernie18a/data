Calculation: All-time high = highest high over a rolling lookback on the chart timeframe
Calculation: 52-week high = highest high over 52 bars on a higher weekly timeframe, including the still-forming weekly bar
Calculation: Highest high since entry = highest high since entering the long position
Long: position is flat AND (high > prior chart bar's all-time high OR high > prior chart bar's 52-week high)
Exit: Long, low <= highest high since entry - ATR × multiplier
Exit: Long, close < EMA
