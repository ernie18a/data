Calculation: Bullish ribbon = at least the configured threshold minus one of the seven adjacent comparisons in an eight-MA ribbon, ordered shortest to longest, has each shorter-period MA above the next; bearish ribbon reverses the comparisons.
Calculation: Bullish order flow = (cumulative volume delta above its moving average while close is below the prior close and cumulative delta is rising, OR up-candle volume divided by max(down-candle volume, 1) exceeds its configured threshold) AND (close crosses above VWAP OR close is above VWAP); bearish order flow reverses the delta and volume comparisons and uses a VWAP cross below or close below VWAP.
Calculation: Bullish order block = close > open, prior close < prior open, and the current upper wick is less than 30% of the current high-low range; bearish order block = close < open, prior close > prior open, and the current lower wick is less than 30% of the current range.
Calculation: Bullish fair-value gap = low > high two bars ago and the gap exceeds the configured ATR multiple; bearish fair-value gap = high < low two bars ago and the gap exceeds the configured ATR multiple.
Calculation: Bullish liquidity sweep = low breaks below the prior rolling lookback low and close > open and close is above that prior low; bearish liquidity sweep = high breaks above the prior rolling lookback high and close < open and close is below that prior high.
Calculation: Bullish break of structure = close crosses above the latest confirmed pivot high; bearish break of structure = close crosses below the latest confirmed pivot low. Pivots use equal left and right lookbacks.
Calculation: Demand-zone marking = bullish candle body exceeds the configured ATR multiple and the prior candle is bearish; supply-zone marking = bearish candle body exceeds the configured ATR multiple and the prior candle is bullish.
Long: (position is flat AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA > slow EMA AND close > long-term EMA AND fast EMA crosses above slow EMA AND volume > its 20-bar average times the configured multiplier) OR (close > prior rolling highest high AND volume > its 20-bar average times the configured multiplier AND close > long-term EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close < lower Bollinger Band AND RSI < oversold level AND RSI crosses above oversold level))) AND (MTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA > slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close > fast EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bullish ribbon) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR close > VWAP OR ((bullish CVD condition OR strong-buying condition) AND close crosses above VWAP)) AND (SMC filter disabled OR bullish order block OR bullish fair-value gap OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish break of structure) AND (time is within the configured trading session); OR bullish break of structure OR bullish liquidity sweep OR bullish fair-value gap OR bullish order block OR demand-zone marking.
Short: (position is flat AND ((ADX > threshold AND fast EMA < slow EMA AND close < long-term EMA AND fast EMA crosses below slow EMA AND volume > its 20-bar average times the configured multiplier) OR (close < prior rolling lowest low AND volume > its 20-bar average times the configured multiplier AND close < long-term EMA) OR (ADX <= threshold AND close > upper Bollinger Band AND RSI > overbought level AND RSI crosses below overbought level))) AND (MTF filter disabled OR higher-timeframe fast EMA < slow EMA AND higher-timeframe close < fast EMA, using the last completed higher-timeframe bar) AND (ribbon filter disabled OR bearish ribbon) AND (order-flow filter disabled OR close < VWAP OR ((bearish CVD condition OR strong-selling condition) AND close crosses below VWAP)) AND (SMC filter disabled OR bearish order block OR bearish fair-value gap OR bearish liquidity sweep OR bearish break of structure) AND (time is within the configured trading session); OR bearish break of structure OR bearish liquidity sweep OR bearish fair-value gap OR bearish order block OR supply-zone marking.
Exit: Long, low <= entry price - ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR (trailing enabled AND high reaches entry price + ATR at entry × trailing multiplier AND low <= highest high since activation - ATR at entry × trailing multiplier) OR (trailing disabled AND high >= entry price + ATR at entry × take-profit multiplier)
Exit: Short, high >= entry price + ATR at entry × stop multiplier OR (trailing enabled AND low reaches entry price - ATR at entry × trailing multiplier AND high >= lowest low since activation + ATR at entry × trailing multiplier) OR (trailing disabled AND low <= entry price - ATR at entry × take-profit multiplier)
