Calculation: Current higher-timeframe high/low = high/low of the higher-timeframe bar still forming
Calculation: Previous higher-timeframe close = close of the last completed higher-timeframe bar
Calculation: ATR distance to current higher-timeframe high = (current higher-timeframe high − close) / ATR
Calculation: ATR distance to current higher-timeframe low = (close − current higher-timeframe low) / ATR
Long: close > current higher-timeframe high OR close crosses above previous higher-timeframe close OR ATR distance to current higher-timeframe low < threshold OR ATR distance to current higher-timeframe low < ATR distance to current higher-timeframe high
Short: close < current higher-timeframe low OR close crosses below previous higher-timeframe close OR ATR distance to current higher-timeframe high < threshold OR ATR distance to current higher-timeframe high < ATR distance to current higher-timeframe low
