Calculation: Exponential moving average = EMA of close over a lookback; compare the daily and 4-hour EMAs separately with their respective closes. Both timeframes are higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar.
Calculation: BOS range high/low = highest high / lowest low over a lookback on the 15-minute timeframe, which is higher than the chart timeframe; use the last completed bar.
Calculation: Long retracement level = impulse high − (impulse high − impulse low) × retracement fraction; short retracement level = impulse low + (impulse high − impulse low) × retracement fraction.
Long: Daily close > daily EMA AND 4-hour close > 4-hour EMA AND a bullish BOS has occurred since the daily reset (15-minute close crosses above the prior sampled 15-minute BOS range high) AND chart-bar low crosses below the long retracement level AND within the configured trading session AND daily and weekly loss/profit limits permit trading AND no trade has been taken that day.
Short: Daily close < daily EMA AND 4-hour close < 4-hour EMA AND a bearish BOS has occurred since the daily reset (15-minute close crosses below the prior sampled 15-minute BOS range low) AND chart-bar high crosses above the short retracement level AND within the configured trading session AND daily and weekly loss/profit limits permit trading AND no trade has been taken that day.
Exit: long, chart-bar low ≤ impulse low OR chart-bar high ≥ impulse high + (impulse high − impulse low) × target fraction
Exit: short, chart-bar high ≥ impulse high OR chart-bar low ≤ impulse low − (impulse high − impulse low) × target fraction
