Calculation: Option intrinsic value = max(0, call spot high − strike price for a call, or strike price − spot low for a put)
Calculation: Resistance level n = option intrinsic value + n × step size, for n = 1 through 7
Long: Current close > any resistance level 1–6 AND previous close ≤ that same level’s previous value
Exit: long, high ≥ the resistance level immediately above the level crossed at entry OR close ≤ the low of the bar immediately before entry
