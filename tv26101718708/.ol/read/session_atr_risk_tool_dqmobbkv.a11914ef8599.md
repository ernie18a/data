Calculation: Stop distance = selected volatility basis (chart-timeframe ATR or mean high-low range of the most recent completed sessions) × stop multiplier
Calculation: Long stop = entry price − stop distance
Calculation: Long 1:1 target = entry price + stop distance
Calculation: Short stop = entry price + stop distance
Calculation: Short 1:1 target = entry price − stop distance
Exit: Long, low ≤ long stop OR high ≥ long 1:1 target
Exit: Short, high ≥ short stop OR low ≤ short 1:1 target
