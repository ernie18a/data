Shiori Regime Map｜Trend & Range Visual Compass

Shiori Regime Map is a market regime visualization tool designed to help discretionary traders understand the current market environment before making trading decisions.

It is not a buy/sell signal system, not an automated strategy, and not a high/low prediction tool.

Instead of asking “Should I buy or sell right now?”, this indicator asks a more important question first:

Is the current market environment suitable for trend-following trades, or is it better to wait?

What this indicator does

Shiori Regime Map uses price structure, moving averages, ATR-based volatility, slope, range compression, trend extension, evidence strength, and chase-risk conditions to classify the current market posture.

The indicator visualizes whether the market is currently in a bullish trend, bearish trend, early trend development, range/chop, transition/waiting phase, no-chase condition, or neutral state.

Its goal is simple: help traders avoid forcing trades in noisy regimes and become more aware of when the market is actually offering a cleaner trend environment.

Main features

Regime Background

The background color gives a quick visual reading of the current market posture.

Green means a bullish trend environment.

Light green means early bullish development.

Red means a bearish trend environment.

Light red means early bearish development.

Orange means range, chop, or high-noise conditions.

Yellow means waiting or transition.

Purple means no-chase: the direction may still be valid, but chasing risk is high.

No background means neutral or no clear playbook.

F0–F7 Action Level

The dashboard translates the regime reading into a simple action framework.

F0: Observe.

F1: Wait for trigger.

F2: Early bullish watch.

F3: Bullish trend environment.

F4: Early bearish watch.

F5: Bearish trend environment.

F6: No chasing longs.

F7: No chasing shorts.

These levels are not trading commands. They are regime-based action hints designed to help traders think in terms of market environment rather than isolated signals.

Dashboard

The simplified dashboard shows the current regime, action level, chase risk, pace note, and bar status. More detailed diagnostic information can be enabled through Standard or Debug mode.

The default view is intentionally clean, with a light transparent dashboard and minimal visual clutter.

Moving Averages

The indicator includes short-term and long-term moving averages by default. These are used as part of the trend-state reading and can also help users visually understand whether the market is trending or transitioning.

Important notes

Green does not mean “buy now.”

Red does not mean “short now.”

No-Chase is not a reversal signal.

Range/Chop means trend-following conditions are poor.

This indicator should be used together with your own trading plan, timeframe selection, and risk management rules.

Shiori Regime Map is best understood as a market posture map. It helps you decide whether the environment deserves your attention before you think about execution.

Suggested use

Use it to identify whether the market is trending, ranging, transitioning, or too extended to chase.

Use it to avoid overtrading during noisy range conditions.

Use it as a regime filter before applying your own entry and exit methods.

Use it to develop better discipline around one of the most important trading questions:

Is this a market worth trading right now?

    wa + wb + wc == 0.0 ? na : (a * wa + b * wb + c * wc) / (wa + wb + wc)

    top = bull ? low : bear ? low[2] : na
    bottom = bull ? high[2] : bear ? high : na
    [bull, bear, top, bottom, time]
