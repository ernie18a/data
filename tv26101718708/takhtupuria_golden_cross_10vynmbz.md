## Description

TAKHTUPURIA - GOLDEN CROSS ULTIMATE (50/200 EMA)

This is a professional trend-following indicator based on the classic Golden Cross and Death Cross logic using 50 EMA and 200 EMA.
### Key Features:
* **Golden & Death Cross Signals:** Automatically detects when the 50 EMA crosses the 200 EMA for high-probability trend reversals.
* **Live Market Sessions:** Includes a luxury dashboard showing active trading sessions (London, New York, Tokyo, Sydney) and market overlaps.
* **Takhtupuria Branding:** Designed with a professional interface for clean and effective chart analysis.
* **Single Alert System:** One alert condition covers both Buy and Sell signals for easier management.
Best used on 1-minute to daily timeframes for Forex, Gold (XAUUSD), and Crypto.
