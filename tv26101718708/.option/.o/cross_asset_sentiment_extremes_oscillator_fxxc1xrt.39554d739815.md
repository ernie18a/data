Build a smoothed composite sentiment score from percentile-ranked external market data and the traded instrument’s own momentum, distance from its long-term average, and realized volatility. Select external inputs by asset class: for stocks and indices, combine volatility, selling pressure, put/call positioning, credit spreads, and market breadth; for crypto, combine perpetual-versus-spot premium, a US spot-demand premium, stablecoin dominance, ETH-versus-BTC momentum, and dollar strength. Orient each input so higher values indicate greater greed, reverse fear-oriented inputs, and average available inputs before blending the external score with the instrument-derived score. With zone-exit triggers, go long when the score crosses upward out of the fear zone and go short when it crosses downward out of the greed zone. With zone-entry triggers, go long when it crosses downward into the fear zone and short when it crosses upward into the greed zone. The fear and greed boundaries, ranking windows, smoothing, and blend weight are configurable.
