When the broad volatility index is below its allowed ceiling or unavailable, arm a short setup if the instrument opens sharply below the prior close, closes sufficiently below its open, and meets the minimum price filter. Set the level at that session’s low and watch it for a limited number of later sessions. If price reaches or falls below the level while it is live, short there and cover at that session’s close. A newer qualifying gap-down setup replaces the prior level.
