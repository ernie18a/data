On each confirmed bar, calculate two SuperTrend trend directions on the traded instrument and obtain daily VIX data. Enter long when both trends are bullish and the enabled long VIX filter passes; enter short when both are bearish and the enabled short VIX filter passes. The long filter is selectable among VIX below its long-term mean minus two standard deviations, below its long-term mean, falling below its short-term EMA, above its mean, rising above its EMA, or no filter; the default is above the mean. The short filter is selectable among VIX above its mean plus two standard deviations, above its mean, rising above its EMA, or no filter; the default is rising above its EMA. Close a long if either trend turns bearish, and close a short if either turns bullish. Trading direction can be restricted to long, short, or both.
