For each scanned stock, treat daily price and volume data as the signal. Mark it READY as a potential long candidate when all trend conditions pass, the volatility contraction score meets its minimum, and price is within the allowed zone below the recent pivot. Mark it BREAKOUT as a potential long candidate when those trend and contraction conditions pass, price is above the pivot but within the maximum chase zone, and relative volume meets its breakout threshold. Mark it EXTENDED when the trend conditions pass but price is beyond the maximum chase zone; do not chase. Otherwise classify it as developing or skip. The logic gives no short or exit signal; entries and risk management require separate review.
