After the configured start and before option expiry, at each scheduled hedge interval, either: (1) in simple mode, open a position when the traded instrument crosses the strike—above it for a long call or short put, below it for a short call or long put—and close that position on the reverse crossing; or (2) in synthetic-option mode, calculate the option delta from the instrument price, strike, time to expiry, volatility, and risk-free rate, then rebalance the instrument position toward delta times the configured hedge size, reversing trade direction for a short option position. Close any remaining hedge after expiry.
