Use a broad-market benchmark as a long-entry gate: when it is more than its configured drawdown threshold below its all-time high, allow a target-asset long entry only while the benchmark is above its long-term moving average; otherwise allow entries. When allowed, go long on an upward cross of any adjacent pair in the target asset’s short-, medium-, and long-term moving-average sequence, or when its price is above its long-term average and breaks above the high reached during the previous trade. Exit based on the target asset’s stop or moving-average crosses; the benchmark does not trigger exits. No short entries.
