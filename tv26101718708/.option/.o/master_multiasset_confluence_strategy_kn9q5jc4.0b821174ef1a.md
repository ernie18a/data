Go long when the traded instrument makes a lower low than its recent prior range, while at least one external market fails to confirm the weakness: volatility-index highs or put/call highs remain below their prior highs, or broad-market futures lows remain above their prior lows. Go short when the instrument makes a higher high while at least one external market fails to confirm the strength: volatility-index lows or put/call lows remain above their prior lows, or broad-market futures highs remain below their prior highs. Require every enabled confirmation: an oversold/oversold-band or overbought/overbought-band condition, a touch of an active swing order block, cumulative-volume-delta divergence, or a swing failure against the prior higher-timeframe range. An opposite qualifying signal can close the current position and open the other direction. Close positions at configured profit or loss limits.
