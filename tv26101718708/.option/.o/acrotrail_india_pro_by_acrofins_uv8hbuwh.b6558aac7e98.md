Maintain an ATR-width band around an EMA center, ratcheting the active side only tighter. A confirmed close crossing the prior band flips direction: consider long on a bearish-to-bullish flip and short on a bullish-to-bearish flip. Score each candidate using higher-timeframe trend alignment, volume confirmation when available, directional RSI momentum, a regime score combining trend strength, low choppiness, and price-fit strength, and the distance of the breakout beyond the band. Adjust component weights for instrument type, session context, volatility, and regime; reject candidates below the configured quality threshold and, when enabled, reject candidates in a choppy regime. For intraday charts, block entries outside the local exchange session; daily and higher-timeframe charts bypass this gate. Manage an open position with an ATR-based stop and risk-multiple profit targets; optionally move the stop to entry after the first target. Exit on stop or final target, and reverse when an opposite qualifying signal arrives.
