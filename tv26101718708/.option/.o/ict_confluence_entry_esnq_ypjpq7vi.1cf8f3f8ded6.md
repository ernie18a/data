Generate a long signal when price closes above the upper boundary of a previously formed bearish fair value gap; generate a short signal when it closes below the lower boundary of a previously formed bullish fair value gap. Require the selected minimum confluence grade: count a recent liquidity sweep, a recent rejection of a higher-timeframe fair value gap, and recent SMT divergence as supporting confluences. A bullish sweep takes a prior swing low and closes back above it; a bearish sweep takes a prior swing high and closes back below it. A bullish higher-timeframe gap rejection touches the gap and closes above its upper boundary; a bearish rejection touches it and closes below its lower boundary. Bullish SMT occurs when the traded instrument makes a rolling low while a correlated instrument fails to make a corresponding low; bearish SMT is the inverse at rolling highs. Grade the entry as highest when all three supporting confluences are present; next when sweep and higher-timeframe rejection are present; next when rejection and SMT are present; then when exactly one supporting confluence is present; lowest when none are present. Apply the configured direction, minimum-grade, and optional trading-session filters. Emit no exit signal; the indicator only marks entries.
