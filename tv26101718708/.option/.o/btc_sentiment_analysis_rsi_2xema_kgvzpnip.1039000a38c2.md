Within the configured trading date range, go long the chart instrument when the RSI of the external BTC long-position series crosses above the RSI of the external BTC short-position series, the long-position series’ change is above its own EMA, and the short-position series’ change is below its own EMA. Exit the long when the RSI of the long-position series crosses below the RSI of the short-position series, the long-position series’ change is below its own EMA, and the short-position series’ change is above its own EMA. No short entry is used.
