During the London session, go long when price sweeps below the Asian-session low and closes back above it, momentum is oversold, the market is stationary or compressed, and the dollar index is falling. Go short when price sweeps above the Asian-session high and closes back below it, momentum is overbought, the market is stationary or compressed, and the dollar index is rising; disable shorts in long-only mode. Exit positions after sufficient profit when price crosses the fast trend average against the position, or when the configured loss boundary is reached.
