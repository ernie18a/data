Use country-level and global cross-market data outside the traded instrument to build a composite crisis score from equity stress, currency and capital flows, inflation, sovereign and credit risk, external vulnerability, and sentiment. Increase the score for worsening stress in each category, amplify it when stress is broad across categories, and smooth it so crises rise faster than they recede. Map the resulting score from low to high risk to progressively reduce exposure: deploy, become selective, trade cautiously, hedge, reduce, then exit all. The framework gives no short signal.
