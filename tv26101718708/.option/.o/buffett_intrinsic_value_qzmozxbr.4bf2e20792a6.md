For the traded stock, estimate intrinsic value from positive trailing EPS, adjusted by expected growth (prefer ROE × earnings retention; otherwise use year-over-year EPS growth), and normalize the valuation for the relevant regional 10-year government bond yield. Compare intrinsic value with market price: go long when the discount meets the required margin of safety; hold when value is at or above market price but the safety margin is not met; exit or sell when market price exceeds intrinsic value.
