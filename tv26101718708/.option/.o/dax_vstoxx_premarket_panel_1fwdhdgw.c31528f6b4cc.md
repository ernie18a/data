For the DAX, use the external volatility index’s change since the Frankfurt pre-open as an early directional signal: a sufficiently large decline indicates long bias, and a sufficiently large rise indicates short bias. After the cash open, classify the change from that open using calibrated bands: a larger decline indicates stronger long bias, a smaller decline indicates mild long bias, a change near zero indicates range/no directional position, a smaller rise indicates mild short bias, and a larger rise indicates stronger short bias. The framework specifies no separate exit rule.
