For the traded instrument, track swing and session liquidity levels, including the Asia range and prior-day extremes. During the London or New York killzone, a sweep below a sellside level arms a long setup; a sweep above a buyside level arms a short setup. Within a limited bar window after the sweep, require a strong directional candle whose body exceeds the recent average, then require a matching fair-value-gap event: either a gap is inverted by a candle body closing through it, or price retraces into a valid matching gap. On a confirmed bar, signal long or short in the direction of that sequence. Place the stop beyond the most extreme price reached since the sweep, with a small tick buffer; skip the setup if the stop distance exceeds the applicable session or volatility-based cap. Grade setups using confluence: proximity to higher-timeframe gaps or key opens, recent SMT divergence against a correlated instrument, an equal-level sweep, nearby target-side liquidity, and whether the entry is in discount for longs or premium for shorts; subtract credit when multiple unswept pools remain close behind the entry. Apply the configured minimum grade to filter signals. Set the first target at the nearest unswept pool in the trade direction and the final target at the farther of that pool or the minimum risk multiple. The indicator defines no exit signal.
