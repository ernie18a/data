On the traded instrument, compute short-term returns for it and a correlated market instrument, plus short-term changes in a volatility index and a smoothed market-breadth measure. Cast one vote each: bullish when both markets rise, bearish when both fall, otherwise neutral; bullish when volatility falls sharply, bearish when it rises sharply, otherwise neutral; and bullish or bearish when breadth is sufficiently positive or negative, with extreme breadth optionally reversing the vote in exhaustion mode. Sum the votes. Treat a sufficiently positive total as long confirmation and a sufficiently negative total as short confirmation; mixed or neutral totals provide no directional confirmation. This is a confirmation filter for separate entry signals; no exit rule is defined.
