For the selected currency, compare current values of its 2-year yield, 10-year yield, short-rate future, and FX proxy with their values a fixed lookback ago. Add one point for each rising yield, falling rate future, and proxy move that indicates currency strength; subtract one for the opposite moves; unchanged, unavailable, or disabled inputs contribute zero. Reverse the proxy direction for currencies whose proxy quote strengthens when that currency weakens. Sum the contributions: a sufficiently positive score signals long bias in the currency, a sufficiently negative score signals short bias, and an intermediate score signals neutral/exit. For a currency pair, go long when the base currency is bullish and the quote currency bearish; go short for the reverse; otherwise exit or remain neutral.
