For each tracked instrument, divide its daily close by the selected external benchmark close, smooth the ratio, standardize it against its rolling historical mean and volatility, then smooth the resulting score. Go long when the score crosses upward through the configured lower extreme; go short when it crosses downward through the configured upper extreme. Treat the latest direction as active only during the configured signal-freshness period; otherwise issue no new direction. The cross-sectional rotation read is available only for a comparable universe and requires an active short signal on an instrument with overextension confirmed by at least two of the USD, broad-equity, and gold benchmarks, paired with an active long signal on an instrument with undervaluation confirmed by at least two of those benchmarks. No separate exit rule is defined.
