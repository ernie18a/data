Compare available external macro indicators with their moving averages: lower volatility, dollar strength, and government yields, higher growth-market prices, and credit outperforming government bonds each contribute positively; opposite conditions contribute negatively. Normalize the available signals into a macro risk score. A score above the upper regime threshold favors longs and blocks new shorts; a score below the lower threshold favors shorts or defensive exposure and blocks longs. Between thresholds, take no directional action. The regime is a directional filter, not an entry or exit signal.
