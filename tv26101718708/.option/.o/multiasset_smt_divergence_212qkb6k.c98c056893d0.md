Compare swing highs and lows on the traded instrument with corresponding swings in one or more correlated instruments. In sweep mode, signal short when the traded instrument takes a prior swing high but a comparison instrument fails to take its corresponding high; signal long when the traded instrument takes a prior swing low but a comparison instrument fails to take its corresponding low. For inverse-correlated comparisons, reverse the comparison. If candle validation is enabled, require a bearish candle at the high pivot for a short signal and a bullish candle at the low pivot for a long signal. In legacy mode, compare successive confirmed pivots: signal short when the instruments disagree on whether highs rose, and long when they disagree on whether lows fell, reversing the comparison for inverse-correlated pairs. Apply the same rules to any enabled shorter-swing pass. The indicator defines no exit decision.
