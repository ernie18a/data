Enter long when price crosses above the session opening-range high, the fast moving average is above the slow moving average, momentum is bullish, trend strength clears its minimum, the higher-timeframe price is above its trend average, and an external volatility index is below its safety ceiling. Enter short on the inverse conditions, crossing below the opening-range low. Allow only one open position at a time and block re-entry in the last trade’s direction until an opposite-direction trade occurs. Set an initial stop using current volatility and trail it behind price as the trade moves favorably; exit when the closing price crosses the stop. Profit targets do not independently close the position.
