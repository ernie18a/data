Use India VIX as an external risk gate: at its extreme level, issue no trade; rising into a shock condition while already elevated calls for exiting all positions. Otherwise, classify the traded index by its ATR-normalized distance from its EMA, using separate entry and reset thresholds to maintain bullish, bearish, or neutral bias. Compare short-horizon realized volatility with long-horizon realized volatility. Combine bias and volatility state: bullish bias with normal or expanding volatility favors a bullish put spread; bearish bias with normal or expanding volatility favors a bearish call spread. Fading directional bias, or neutral bias with normal volatility, favors a two-sided condor; neutral bias with compressed volatility calls for a wider condor; neutral bias with expanding volatility is unstable. Extreme VIX overrides these structures with no trade. For an active short put or call, exit that leg when the index reaches its corresponding stop, set by retracing three quarters of the distance from entry spot to that leg’s short strike.
