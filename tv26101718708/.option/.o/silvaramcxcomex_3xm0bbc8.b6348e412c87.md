For silver and gold, convert the COMEX price into local currency with the exchange rate and import duty, then subtract the corresponding local exchange price. Signal a long entry when this difference crosses above its user-set upside-entry level, a short entry when it crosses below its downside-entry level, and exit when it crosses its user-set exit level in either direction. Separately, for each gold/silver, gold/platinum, and platinum/silver ratio, signal buy when the ratio crosses down through its user-set buy level, sell when it crosses up through its sell level, and free or exit when it crosses its free level in either direction. The indicator also alerts when daily COMEX-versus-local percentage divergence or the converted-price gap stays within a configured narrow band for a configured duration; these alerts have no long, short, or exit direction.
