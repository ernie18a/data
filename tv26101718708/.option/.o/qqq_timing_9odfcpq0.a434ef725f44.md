Go long when the S&P 500’s daily low closes above its 21-day exponential moving average for three consecutive days. Exit the long if either the S&P 500’s 7-day simple moving average crosses below its 107-day simple moving average while the S&P 500 closes below both its 7-day average and 14-day exponential average, and the traded instrument closes below its 14-day exponential average; or the instrument’s low falls below both its entry-price stop level and its 50-day simple moving average. No short entry rule.
