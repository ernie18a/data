Build a smoothed composite sentiment score from the traded asset’s momentum, volatility, RSI, volume pressure, and range position, combined with external crypto breadth (share of major coins above their trend averages), inverse stablecoin-dominance change, broad crypto-market trend, and BTC-dominance context. Classify the regime as risk-on when the asset is above its long-term trend and the broad-market score is at least neutral; classify it as risk-off when both are below those conditions; otherwise treat it as mixed. In a risk-on regime, an upward recovery out of extreme fear signals accumulation, and an upward move into greed signals trend continuation. In a risk-off regime, worsening fear signals avoid or exit exposure, not a short entry. At extreme greed, fading asset momentum signals trim or exit exposure. The indicator has no explicit short-entry rule.
