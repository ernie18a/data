For a traded instrument A paired with external instrument B, form a relative-value series from A and B using the selected ratio or hedged log-spread method, then standardize it against its rolling mean and volatility. Allow mean-reversion entries only when the series has a valid positive, finite estimated half-life shorter than its estimation window. When flat, enter long A if the standardized spread is below its lower entry band and either the aggressive mode is active or, in conservative mode, it has turned upward through that band after previously crossing below it. Optionally, apply the same rebound rule at a nearer band. If shorting is enabled, enter short A symmetrically when the spread is above its upper band and turns downward through it, or immediately on reaching it in aggressive mode; optional nearer-band entries work symmetrically. Track one active direction at a time. Exit a long when the spread turns upward through its lower normal-exit band; exit a short when it turns downward through its upper normal-exit band. Optional risk exits close a long if the spread falls back through its lower entry band or price closes below the entry candle's low, and close a short if the spread rises back through its upper entry band or price closes above the entry candle's high. An optional emergency exit closes either position if the spread extends further against it beyond its emergency boundary. Clear pending entry setups when the spread crosses the mean, and reset position state after any exit.
