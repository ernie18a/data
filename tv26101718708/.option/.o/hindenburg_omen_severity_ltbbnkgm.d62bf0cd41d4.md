When both new 52-week highs and new 52-week lows exceed a threshold as a share of advancing plus declining issues, and the broad market index is at or above its value from a prior lookback period, open a signal window. While that window remains open, a negative McClellan Oscillator (the difference between short- and longer-term exponential averages of normalized net advances) activates the warning: consider shorting the traded instrument or exiting a long position. A nonnegative oscillator deactivates the warning until it turns negative again; when the window expires, there is no directive. No long-entry signal is defined.
