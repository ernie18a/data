Enter long when a bullish liquidity sweep occurred recently, price formed or revisited a bullish inverted fair value gap, and any required bullish delivery shift is present; block if nearby liquidity favors the downside or a large lower wick signals rejection. If enabled, also require bullish divergence: the traded instrument makes a new lookback low while the comparison instrument does not. Enter short on the inverse conditions: recent bearish sweep, bearish inverted fair value gap, any required bearish delivery shift, no nearby upside-favoring liquidity or large upper wick, and—if enabled—bearish divergence where the traded instrument makes a new lookback high without the comparison instrument doing so. The indicator defines no exit signal.
