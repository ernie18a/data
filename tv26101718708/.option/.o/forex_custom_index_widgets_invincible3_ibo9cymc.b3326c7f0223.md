For each major currency, build an index from its related FX crosses: use each pair’s price movement for its base currency and the inverse movement for its quote currency, aggregating the results geometrically. Derive a force score from normalized relative index strength, cross-pair breadth, index-move efficiency, current impulse, directional persistence, and change in strength; combine these factors with configurable weights. For the chart pair, compare the base currency’s force with the quote currency’s force: go long when the difference exceeds a configurable positive gap, short when it is below the corresponding negative gap, and otherwise remain neutral. The strongest-versus-weakest currencies may also identify a candidate pair direction when their force gap is large enough. No exit rule is specified.
