On daily data, evaluate each available selected cross-asset market independently using the chosen signal method: a fast/slow moving-average spread beyond a volatility-scaled threshold, a volatility-based adaptive trailing-stop direction, or both combined. Classify each market’s bullish or bearish direction as evidence for the macro regimes according to whether that market represents growth, inflation, or both; normalize the regime evidence into shares and select the regime with the largest share, retaining the current regime when it remains tied for the lead. When the dominant regime changes, go long the asset assigned to that regime; if no asset is assigned to the active regime, exit to cash. With no regime-specific assets configured, use the traded instrument for the two improving-growth regimes and remain in cash for the other two. The system does not open short positions.
