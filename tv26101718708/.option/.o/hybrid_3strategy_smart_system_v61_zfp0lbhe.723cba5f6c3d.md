Use higher-timeframe price relative to its moving average as a directional gate: allow longs when above and shorts when below. Require matching local trend (price and moving average aligned), sufficient trend strength, and any enabled session and price-distance filters. Generate a candidate from any enabled setup: (1) a sufficiently large bullish or bearish three-bar fair-value gap, then a later retest that closes back in the trend direction; (2) a high-volume, oversold bullish reversal below the local average or overbought bearish reversal above it, followed within a limited window by a close beyond the reversal bar’s extreme; or (3) a close through a confirmed swing high or low in the trend direction, followed within a limited window by a retest that closes back beyond the broken level. Require a confirming candle, a qualifying setup score, and completion of the trade cooldown. When multiple candidates occur, prioritize fair-value-gap pullbacks, then volume reversals, then swing-break retests; check long candidates before short candidates. With no open position, record the candidate’s close as entry and set a protective stop beyond the setup’s low or high, with volatility-based padding; set the target at a setup-specific reward multiple of the entry-to-stop risk. Enter on a confirmed bar while the candidate remains within its validity window, price has not moved too far from the recorded entry, and price remains on the permitted side of the local average. Exit at the protective stop or target.
