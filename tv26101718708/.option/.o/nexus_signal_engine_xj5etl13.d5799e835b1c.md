While flat, enter long or short on the traded instrument when either entry path qualifies. Grand entries require enough directional flips among pivot-based SuperTrend, classic SuperTrend, Chandelier Exit, and a range filter within a recent window, with at least one qualifying flip on the current bar. Also require a recent touch of the corresponding RSI entry band and a recent WaveTrend visit to the matching extreme zone; optional filters require price on the appropriate side of the RSI midline and the external reference trend to agree. The external trend is bullish when the reference symbol closes above its EMA on both selected timeframes, and bearish when below on both. Trend entries require that same external trend direction, a touch of the traded instrument’s corresponding RSI entry band, and any enabled local EMA and WaveTrend filters; suppress a Trend entry when a Grand entry occurs simultaneously. For an open position, exit at its fixed stop or the direction-appropriate dynamic RSI target, according to configured wick or close triggers. The Grand stop is based on a recent swing extreme; the Trend stop is volatility-based. If the target closes only part of the position, move the stop to entry and trail the remainder using volatility; exit the remainder at breakeven or the trailing stop.
