On confirmed daily bars, calculate cumulative US market breadth from the advance-minus-decline ratio, treating missing unchanged issues as zero. Enter long when breadth has a strong positive impulse versus its rolling baseline, is above its trend average and rising, the traded instrument closes above its own trend average, and the cooldown since the prior kickoff has elapsed. Enter short when a confirmed higher high in the instrument coincides with a lower breadth high, breadth is in its weak regime with a materially negative trend-spread score, and that divergence is recent. The indicator defines no exit condition.
