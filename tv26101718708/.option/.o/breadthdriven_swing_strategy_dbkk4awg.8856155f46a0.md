Trade the S&P 500 using the percentage of its constituent stocks trading above their own long-term moving averages as an external breadth series. Enter long when either (1) a smoothed long-term breadth series forms a sufficiently prominent local trough below a washed-out threshold, or (2) when enabled, a smoothed short-term breadth series forms a sufficiently prominent local trough and the raw breadth series has reached an extreme low within a recent lookback. Exit the long at the first sufficiently prominent local peak in the smoothed long-term breadth series that is above an overheated threshold. Never open short positions.
