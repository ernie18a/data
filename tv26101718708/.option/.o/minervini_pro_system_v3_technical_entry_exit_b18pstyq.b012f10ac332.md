Use the instrument’s price, volume, and a broad market index to identify long entries. Require a strong trend: price above its medium and long moving averages, the medium average above the long averages, and the long average rising; price must also be near its yearly high and well above its yearly low. Require the instrument to outperform the index over recent months, volatility and daily price changes to be contracting, price not substantially extended above its medium average, the index in an uptrend, and the instrument’s weekly trend positive. Enter long on any of: (1) a close above the recent short-term high with volume above average while the prior bar showed declining volume; (2) a close above the recent multiweek pivot, above the open, with strong volume and a close in the upper portion of the bar; or (3) within several bars of such a breakout, a bullish bar that tests and holds near the pivot while remaining above the medium average. While long, raise a trailing stop to the highest value of its prior stop and the recent swing low. Exit if the close falls below that stop, falls below both the breakout pivot and medium average, closes below a short-term average and below the prior close, or forms a bearish, high-volume bar with a lower close.
