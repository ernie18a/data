On a confirmed bar, consider a long when price is above VWAP and forms a bullish VWAP bounce, reclaim, or resistance-fractal break, with bullish momentum. Consider a short when price is below VWAP and forms a bearish VWAP rejection, breakdown, or support-fractal break, with bearish momentum. Require at least three of four checks to pass: trend direction, volume, candle strength, and the external macro check. The macro check compares the 10-year government bond yield with its recent average: a yield that is not rising supports longs, while a rising yield supports shorts.
