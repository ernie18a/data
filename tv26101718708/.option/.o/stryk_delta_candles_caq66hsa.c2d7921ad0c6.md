On a completed higher-timeframe candle, treat a bearish setup as higher-conviction when the candle’s delta flips from positive to negative while price holds flat or rises, or when price rises with negative delta, or unusually large positive delta meets flat price—and the external market-breadth reading reaches an extreme positive level during that candle. Treat the corresponding bullish setup as higher-conviction when delta flips from negative to positive while price holds flat or falls, or price falls with positive delta, or unusually large negative delta meets flat price—and breadth reaches an extreme negative level. The breadth extreme qualifies the directional setup; no exit rule is specified.
