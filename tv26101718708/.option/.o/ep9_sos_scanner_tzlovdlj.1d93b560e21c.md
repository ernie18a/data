On a daily bar, form a bullish setup when the prior bar did not exceed the allowed prior-up-move cap, the close is in the required upper portion of the day’s range, the current candle body is larger than the prior body, and price and volume meet their minimums. Accept the setup only if estimated stop risk as a percentage of entry is no greater than average daily range as a percentage. For the strict long decision, also require price above its long-term moving average, that average rising, and the stock’s price relative to the benchmark above its own moving average. A relaxed long decision requires any two of those three trend and relative-strength conditions. No short or exit decision is defined.
