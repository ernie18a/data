Create support zones at confirmed swing lows and resistance zones at confirmed swing highs, using each pivot bar’s traded volume multiplied by its price range as the zone’s pressure weight. For each zone, track its band, side, age, and whether it has been swept. A support zone is swept when price trades below its lower band by a minimum volatility-scaled depth; a resistance zone is swept when price trades above its upper band by that depth. Within a limited number of bars after a sweep, go long if price closes back above the support zone’s selected reclaim level, or go short if it closes back below the resistance zone’s selected reclaim level. Optionally require recent momentum to be oversold for the long or overbought for the short. Expire a pending sweep as a genuine break if price closes decisively beyond the zone or remains beyond its outer edge through the reclaim window; do not trade that failed reclaim. Optionally flip a broken zone’s side for subsequent tracking. Zone strength ranks its pressure weight against other active zones and blends that relative weight with defended retests, relative volume, rejection-wick depth, and successful prior sweep defenses; strength decays while ignored. Trade only reclaims at zones meeting any configured strength threshold.
