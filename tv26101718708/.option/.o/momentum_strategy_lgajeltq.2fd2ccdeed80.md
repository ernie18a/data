Go long when the instrument closes at a new high over a recent lookback, its fast momentum is bullish (RSI above a threshold), its trend average is rising, and the candle satisfies the strategy’s bullish close-versus-open/high-range condition. For instruments other than the designated benchmarks, also require the instrument’s price relative to its applicable market benchmark to make a recent high and have a rising trend average. Exit when the close falls below a short-term average; after the position has gained sufficiently relative to its entry price, use a longer-term average instead. Exit as well if relative performance falls below its own trend average. The strategy has no independent short-entry signal.
