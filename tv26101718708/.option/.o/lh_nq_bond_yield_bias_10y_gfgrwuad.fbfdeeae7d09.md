For the traded index, compare smoothed recent changes in the 10-year Treasury yield, 2-year yield, VIX, dollar index, and optionally crude oil against configurable minimum-move thresholds; ignore unavailable or stale quotes. A qualifying fall in the 10-year yield supports long bias and a qualifying rise supports short bias, with twice the score weight of each other enabled market. Falling VIX or dollar index supports long bias; rising values support short bias. Crude oil supports either direction according to a configurable direct or inverse relationship. Go long only when the 10-year yield supports long, the combined score reaches the positive minimum, and any enabled 2-year confirmation and traded-index EMA filters agree. Go short under the corresponding opposite conditions. Otherwise exit or remain neutral.
