On each completed weekly interval, calculate the percentage change over the chosen lookback in the ratio of Bitcoin’s price to gold’s price. Generate a long or risk-on signal when this momentum satisfies the selected bullish trigger: above the upper deadband, above its smoothed signal line, or above zero. Generate a sell or risk-off signal when it satisfies the corresponding bearish trigger: below the lower deadband, below the smoothed signal line, or below zero. Keep the active regime until the opposite trigger occurs, and emit signals only when the regime changes. A sell signal indicates exiting or avoiding long exposure; the indicator does not specify opening a short.
