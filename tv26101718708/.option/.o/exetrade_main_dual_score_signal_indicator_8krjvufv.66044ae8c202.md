Build and independently compare long and short scores from trend, support/resistance, momentum, higher-timeframe context, and reward/risk. When configured, volume from an external symbol adjusts pivot support/resistance strength, which feeds those scores. Add bonuses for confirmed support/resistance bounces and divergences; reduce scores for nearby opposing levels or broken support/resistance. Enter long or short when its score reaches the configured signal grade, exceeds the opposite score by the required margin, and is not blocked by nearby higher-timeframe resistance/support, a broken level, or a ranging market (normal signals only). Emit a range entry when reward/risk meets its configured minimum and a direction-specific setup condition holds. Exit an existing position on its trailing stop, when the opposing score reaches the normal signal threshold, or when price is below the cloud while the trend is down for a long, or above the cloud while the trend is up for a short.
