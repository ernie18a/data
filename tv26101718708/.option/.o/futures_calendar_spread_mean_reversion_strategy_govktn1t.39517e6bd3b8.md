For the spread between a nearer- and a deferred-month futures contract, calculate its rolling mean and standard deviation, then normalize the spread’s distance from its mean. Enter long when this normalized value crosses upward through a lower negative entry threshold; enter short when it crosses downward through an upper positive entry threshold. Close a long if the value crosses below the mean or falls beyond a lower stop threshold. Close a short if it crosses above the mean or rises beyond an upper stop threshold.
