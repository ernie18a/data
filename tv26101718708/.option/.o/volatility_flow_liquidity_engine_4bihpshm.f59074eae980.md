When configured companion and volatility-index data are available, build a pressure score from unusually low ATR, the volatility index’s divergence from forecast realized volatility, changes in the derivative–underlying basis, return acceleration, and price-trend inefficiency. After pressure has recently reached a high percentile, wait for a genuine range expansion. Set direction from a breakout of the recent high or low, close location within the bar, and recent price momentum. Confirm or veto that direction with reconstructed order flow; optionally require higher-timeframe trend agreement and a non-pinning volatility regime. Signal long or short only if liquidity is not stressed and nearby structure leaves room in that direction. The external feeds are optional; without them, omit their pressure factors and renormalize the score. No exit rule is defined.
