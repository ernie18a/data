Go long when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA, price is above the visible Ichimoku cloud, and either open-interest change is sufficiently positive or smoothed signed volume is positive. Go short on the opposite EMA cross when price is below the cloud and either open-interest change is sufficiently negative or smoothed signed volume is negative. Exit positions at the configured stop-loss or take-profit levels, calculated from entry price and volatility or a fixed percentage.
