Go long when either (1) the instrument’s daily short-term price average crosses above its longer-term price average, and the latest upward crossover in Bitcoin network hash-rate averages occurred more recently than both the latest downward price-average crossover and the latest downward hash-rate crossover; or (2) the instrument’s short-term price average is above its longer-term average and Bitcoin’s short-term hash-rate average crosses above its longer-term average. Exit the long if Bitcoin’s short-term hash-rate average crosses below its longer-term average or the configured protective stop is hit. The script does not place short trades.
