Smooth the instrument’s open, high, low, and close into synthetic candles, then compute a Supertrend-style direction and trailing stops from their true range. Use the prior completed year’s high and low as external reference levels. Enter long when the trend is up and either it was already up with the instrument closing higher than the previous bar, or the close is above the short trailing stop; require the close also to be near or above the prior-year high or sufficiently above its low. Enter short when the trend is down and either it was already down with the close lower than the previous bar, or the close is below the long trailing stop; require the close also to be near or below the prior-year low or sufficiently below its high. Close a long on the short-entry trend/price condition, and close a short on the long-entry trend/price condition. Apply the configured trading-date window and permitted trade direction to entries.
