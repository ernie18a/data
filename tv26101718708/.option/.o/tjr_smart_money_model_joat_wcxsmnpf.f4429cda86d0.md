For a long setup, wait for price to trade below a confirmed swing low and close back above it. Within a limited window, require a bullish displacement candle to close above the latest unbroken swing high. Then wait for a bullish candle to retrace into the displacement fair-value gap, or into the midpoint band of the reversal leg if no qualifying gap formed. For a short setup, mirror the sequence: trade above a confirmed swing high and close back below it; within a limited window, require bearish displacement closing below the latest unbroken swing low; then wait for a bearish retrace reaction into the bearish fair-value gap or reversal-leg midpoint band. An optional correlated-instrument filter requires bullish SMT for longs (the traded instrument makes a lower swing low while the correlated instrument does not) or bearish SMT for shorts (the traded instrument makes a higher swing high while the correlated instrument does not). An optional session filter restricts entries to configured trading windows. Expire setups if confirmation or retracement takes too long, and consume a setup after entry. Emit long or short on a qualifying entry; no exit signal is defined.
