For gold, score the intraday moves in the US 2-year yield and the dollar index against a configurable minimum-move threshold: each falling driver adds one bullish point, each rising driver subtracts one, and smaller moves add zero. A positive score favors long, a negative score favors short, and zero is neutral; larger absolute scores indicate stronger bias. A real yield below its configured threshold is supportive, while a higher real yield is hostile. No exit rule is defined.
