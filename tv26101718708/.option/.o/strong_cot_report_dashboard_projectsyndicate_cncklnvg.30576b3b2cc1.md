For each market, use weekly CFTC positioning data independently of the traded instrument’s price. Fade crowded extremes after reversal confirmation: go short when large speculators are near the top of their historical net-position range, are net long, and commercials are near the bottom of theirs; go long on the inverse configuration. Otherwise, follow a build: go long when speculator net positioning is rising, the index is in the upper half of its range, and open interest is expanding; go short when net positioning is falling, the index is in the lower half, and open interest is expanding. Treat falling open interest during a build as covering or liquidation rather than strong trend confirmation. Exit a build when positioning reaches a crowded extreme; the indicator gives no standalone exit rule for a fade.
