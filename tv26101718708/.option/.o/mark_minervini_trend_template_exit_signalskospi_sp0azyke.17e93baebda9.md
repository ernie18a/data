Go long when the instrument first satisfies a Stage 2 uptrend template: its price is above its medium- and long-term moving averages, those averages are properly ordered, the long-term average is rising, and price is sufficiently above its trailing-year low without being too far below its trailing-year high. Also require the instrument’s price relative to the selected benchmark to be above its own medium-term average. Exit on a downward cross below the medium-term or long-term average; a downward cross below the short-term average is a trailing exit signal when the uptrend template was not met on the prior bar. Flag an overextended move above the medium-term average as a climax warning. No short-entry rule is defined.
