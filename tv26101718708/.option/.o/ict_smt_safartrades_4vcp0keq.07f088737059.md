For each enabled correlated market, confirm swing highs and lows on both markets using the same swing definition and compare consecutive corresponding pivots. At a confirmed swing high on the traded instrument, signal short when its high rises but the correlated market’s corresponding high does not rise, or when its high falls but the correlated market’s high does not fall. At a confirmed swing low, signal long when its low rises but the correlated market’s corresponding low does not rise, or when its low falls but the correlated market’s low does not fall. Equal traded-market pivots signal when the correlated pivot changes. If needed, invert the correlated market’s prices before comparing. The indicator does not define trade exits; its invalidation rules only change or remove chart drawings.
