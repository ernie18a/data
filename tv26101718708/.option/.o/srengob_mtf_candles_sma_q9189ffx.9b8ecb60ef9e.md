Build a weighted confirmation score from: bullish or bearish swing structure; price inside the matching order block; order-block strength; rejection candle; liquidity sweep; price aligned with the simple moving average; active session; strong recent momentum; absence of unusually narrow ranges; and a quick reversal. Add momentum and volume bonuses for impulse, early momentum, divergence, or directional volume pressure. For volume confirmation, use volume from the gold futures instrument: award confirmation when it spikes, exceeds its average, or rises across recent bars. Subtract points for unusually narrow ranges. Go long when the score reaches its configured threshold and bullish structure is present, price is inside a bullish order block, and price is above its simple moving average. Go short when the score reaches the threshold and bearish structure is present, price is inside a bearish order block, and price is below its simple moving average. No exit rule is defined.
