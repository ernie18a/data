When the closing-price correlation between the traded instrument and a user-selected external instrument is below a threshold, enter according to the traded instrument’s direction filter: go long when its price is below its moving average, or when its RSI is below an oversold level; go short when its price is above its moving average, or when its RSI is above an overbought level. The direction filter can be configured to use either the moving-average or RSI rule, and an optional reverse setting swaps the long and short mappings.
