Track three-candle price gaps after formation. A close through the far edge of a bullish gap signals short; a close through the far edge of a bearish gap signals long. Ignore wick-only breaches and gaps that do not invert within their validity window. At inversion, score six checks: the inversion itself; a recent, direction-matched liquidity sweep that price rejects; a tap into a live higher-timeframe gap in the trade direction; recent strong price displacement; unswept profit-side liquidity with sufficient room; and cross-market divergence, where the traded market sweeps a recent high or low while its correlated market does not sweep its corresponding extreme. Print only when the configured grade floor is met and the configured sweep policy, trading-session filter, duplicate suppression, and active-trade rules permit it. For a counter-direction setup while a trade is live, require the configured reversal grade and displacement criteria, plus a close through the live setup's invalidation boundary; the stricter reversal policy also requires a break of recent structure. Exit or release the trade when its configured target or target ladder is reached, when a confirmed close invalidates its zone, or when its configured no-progress timeout expires. Treat completed trades as no longer active.
