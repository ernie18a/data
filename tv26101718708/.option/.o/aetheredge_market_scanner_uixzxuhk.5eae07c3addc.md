Train two online logistic classifiers on the chart instrument using lagged feature snapshots: one predicts whether its forward move exceeds an ATR-scaled threshold, and the other predicts whether that move is upward. For each candidate instrument, compute the same ATR-normalized trend, momentum, range-compression, volatility, volume, and price-in-range features; apply the shared classifiers. Rank candidates by predicted opportunity probability. Treat candidates with predicted upward probability at least one half as long candidates and the rest as short candidates, subject to the selected direction filter. There is no exit rule.
