On the traded instrument, issue a long signal when price is above its fast moving average, which is above its intermediate moving average; any enabled longer-term trend filters pass; its return relative to a broad-market benchmark is positive and improving; and trading volume exceeds its rolling average by the required threshold on an up bar. Signal only when this full setup first becomes true. Flag elevated risk when price is below the intermediate moving average, relative performance versus the benchmark is negative, and volume exceeds the threshold on a down bar; this is a warning, not an explicit short or exit instruction. No short signal is defined.
