Measure the share of a basket trading above each member’s own moving average. Use breadth to size or manage a position in the traded instrument: when breadth is rising above half, allow full size; when it is between roughly one-third and half, use reduced size; when it is below roughly one-third, stand aside. When breadth is very high, avoid new entries and reduce or protect positions. If the reference instrument rises while breadth falls materially, stop adding and protect open positions; this is a warning, not an exit by itself. If the reference falls while breadth rises from a washed-out level, prepare for a reversal but do not enter until breadth crosses above half. Exit or reduce when breadth crosses below half. Lift divergence-related caution when reference movement and breadth movement agree again. The indicator supplies no standalone short-entry rule.
