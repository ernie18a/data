Combine normalized, weighted directional signals from the traded instrument’s price and volume (VWAP location and slope, cumulative volume delta, MACD, money flow, and short-term RSI) with external NYSE TICK, advancing-minus-declining issues, up-minus-down volume, and VIX. Use the RSI mean-reversion signal only in the low-volatility regime. Divide the weighted sum by the weights of available signals to get a directional score. A score above the configured bullish threshold supports a put credit spread; below the bearish threshold supports a call credit spread; between the thresholds supports either side, allowing an iron condor if both qualify. For each side, estimate probability of profit from the short strike’s distance from price relative to expected move, adjusted by directional score. Emit a long-bias or short-bias spread signal only when its side is supported and its estimated probability meets the configured minimum; trigger new signals when the score crosses into the corresponding directional region. No exit decision is defined.
