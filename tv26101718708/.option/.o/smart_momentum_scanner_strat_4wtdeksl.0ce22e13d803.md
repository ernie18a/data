Track confirmed swing-low support. After price tests support, wait for a bullish candle to close above the prior candle’s high. Then require a confirmed swing low above that support, followed by a close above the reversal candle’s high. Enter long only when this sequence is complete and volume exceeds its recent average, price is above VWAP when that filter is enabled, and either (a) price is above its medium-term average, the short-term average is above the medium-term average, and the medium-term average is rising, or (b) price is above its long-term average, the short-term average is above the medium-term average, the medium-term average is above the intermediate average, and the long-term average is rising. When enabled, also require each external market or sector reference to be above its own medium-term average. Invalidate the setup if price closes below support. While long, exit on the protective stop; optionally exit on a close below the medium-term average, a close below the trade’s support, a profit target, or expiry of a maximum holding period. Do not open short positions.
