On the traded instrument, enter long when price is above a rising EMA, near that EMA relative to ATR, and the stochastic lines are in the bullish pullback zone with the faster line crossing above the slower one; also require a rising MACD histogram, a bullish candle, sufficient trend strength, and volume above its average. Require both ES and SPY to be above their own rising EMAs; if external momentum confirmation is enabled, require their stochastic lines to be bullish as well. Enter short on the mirrored conditions: price below a falling EMA and near it, stochastic lines in the bearish pullback zone with a downward cross, falling MACD histogram, bearish candle, sufficient trend strength and volume, and both ES and SPY below their falling EMAs (plus bearish external stochastic momentum when enabled). Signal an entry only when its full setup becomes true after being false. Exit a long if price reaches its protective stop below recent lows with an ATR buffer, or if stochastic is in the upper extreme and crosses downward. Exit a short if price reaches its protective stop above recent highs with an ATR buffer, or if stochastic is in the lower extreme and crosses upward.
