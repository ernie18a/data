Classify the traded market as ranging or trending. In a range, go long after price pierces the lower band and closes back above it, or short after it pierces the upper band and closes back below; require the prior bar to have been stretched beyond that band. In a trend, ride an upper-band walk or bullish continuation long, and a lower-band walk or bearish continuation short. Require the applicable signal to pass volume confirmation, conviction, and other enabled signal gates. For trend rides, block a trade when higher-timeframe or multi-timeframe direction conflicts; when a cross-asset feed is enabled, block a ride that conflicts with its signed momentum. For range fades, block trades during a volatility-index spike when that feed is enabled. Exit at the stop or final target; after the first target is reached, move the stop to entry.
