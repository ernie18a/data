Compare the same external reference asset’s USD-quoted and USDT-quoted prices. Normalize their difference by the USD-quoted price and scale it. When this measure crosses above its upper threshold, go long on the following bar unless a cross below the lower threshold occurs on that bar. When it crosses below its lower threshold, go short on the following bar unless a cross above the upper threshold occurs on that bar. Manage open positions with price-based stop-loss and take-profit orders; after the first take-profit is reached, optionally move the remaining position’s stop to breakeven.
