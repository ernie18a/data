During the regular trading session, go long when the market-breadth TICK trend turns bullish, its short-term average is positive, VIX is above its entry floor, the traded instrument is above its EMA, and its RSI confirms bullish momentum; use rising Stochastic RSI to time an entry after the setup appears. Reverse these conditions for shorts. Also allow long entries on regular bullish divergence between the instrument’s price lows and TICK lows, and short entries on regular bearish divergence between their highs, with the corresponding TICK trend, RSI, VIX, and EMA filters. Exit on a TICK trend reversal confirmed by RSI, opposite price–TICK divergence, or an extreme TICK reading; protect positions with a stop based on recent instrument prices and daily ATR, and optionally take profit at a daily-ATR-based target. Close positions near the end of the session, with an option to retain part of a position overnight on selected weekdays.
