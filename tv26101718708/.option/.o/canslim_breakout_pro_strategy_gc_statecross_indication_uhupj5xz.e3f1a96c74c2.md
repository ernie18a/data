On the traded instrument, enter long when its close breaks above the highest high of the preceding base period, remains within the permitted distance above that level, and volume meets the required multiple of its average, provided each enabled external gate passes: the market index, and optionally a selected sector index, must have its faster moving average above its slower moving average. An unavailable index passes its gate. While long, exit at the configured loss level, profit target, or—after the profit activation threshold is reached—when the close falls to the configured trailing distance below the highest close since entry. No short entry or time-based exit is coded.
