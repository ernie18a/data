Use a separate cryptocurrency benchmark’s data to generate signals for the traded instrument. Go long when the benchmark’s fast-minus-slow EMA spread crosses above its EMA signal line while the spread is below a scaled rolling-low threshold, its close is below its moving average and the midpoint of its recent high-low range, and its RSI is below its own moving average. Go short on the converse: the spread crosses below its signal line while above a scaled rolling-high threshold, with the close above its moving average and range midpoint and RSI above its own moving average. An optional reverse setting swaps the long and short actions. Close positions when the traded instrument reaches its profit target or stop-loss level, each measured from its entry price.
