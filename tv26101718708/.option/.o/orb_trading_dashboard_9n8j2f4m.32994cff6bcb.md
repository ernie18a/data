For the traded instrument, calculate a composite directional bias from its position and ordering relative to completed daily moving averages, opening-range breakout/retest state, session VWAP and volatility bands, prior-day and premarket levels, current session range position, week and month opens, ATR extension, and room to nearby support or resistance. Add a market-regime contribution from the external benchmark’s position relative to its VWAP and its change from the prior close; add relative strength from the instrument’s daily change minus the benchmark’s daily change, with a small extra adjustment when the instrument’s 15-minute opening-range direction opposes the benchmark’s VWAP direction. Positive evidence raises the bias score and negative evidence lowers it. Interpret a sufficiently positive score as long bias, a sufficiently negative score as short bias, and scores near zero as neutral; the dashboard provides no explicit entry, exit, or position-management rule.
