Use the underlying instrument’s prices during regular U.S. market hours. Set directional bias to calls when its 13-period EMA is above both its 48- and 200-period EMAs, puts when the 13-period EMA is below both, and no-trade when it lies between the 48- and 200-period EMAs. For a call entry, require the 9-period EMA above the 13- and 200-period EMAs, a down candle closing within a tight distance of the 9-period EMA, and call bias. For a put entry, require the 9-period EMA below the 13- and 200-period EMAs, an up candle closing within a tight distance of the 9-period EMA, and put bias. The indicator also marks secondary put setups when the 13-period EMA is below the 48-period EMA, the 48-period EMA is below the 200-period EMA, and an up candle closes near the 48-period EMA; secondary call setups use the inverse EMA ordering and a down candle near the 48-period EMA. Warn against trading while price is inside the premarket high-low range. It defines no exit rule.
