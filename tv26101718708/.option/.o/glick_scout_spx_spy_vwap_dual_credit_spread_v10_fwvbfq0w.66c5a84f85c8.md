During the configured regular market session, allow a signal only when the volatility index is within its configured bounds and the minimum spacing between signals has elapsed. For a bearish entry (sell a call credit spread), require price to cross below the current-session VWAP and the bearish score to meet the configured minimum; score one point each for a down candle, close below current VWAP, close below prior-session VWAP, and the downward cross. For a bullish entry (sell a put credit spread), require the upward cross and the bullish score to meet the minimum, scoring an up candle, close above each VWAP, and the upward cross. Emit at most one direction per bar. The put/call ratio is informational only; no exit condition is implemented.
