At each reset period, calculate each currency’s strength as the cumulative sum of its average percentage change against the other currencies in the basket, reversing the sign when it is the quote currency. Rank currency pairs by the absolute difference between their strengths; for each of the three largest gaps, recommend buying the stronger currency against the weaker one, orienting the pair according to standard market pair ordering. For gold, compare its basket strength with USD strength and include only the XAU/USD gap. These are ranked trade suggestions; no entry trigger or exit rule is defined.
