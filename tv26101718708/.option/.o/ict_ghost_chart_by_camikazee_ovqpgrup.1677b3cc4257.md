Enter long when price sweeps below the prior day’s low or a recent low and closes back above it, then within a limited window closes through a confirmed swing high with a sufficiently strong bullish candle. Require a bullish fair value gap, price below the midpoint of the recent higher-timeframe range, and an ICT trading window. Apply the configured setup-quality, trend, and probability filters; these score sweep, structure shift, displacement, range location, session timing, volume, and divergence. Divergence adds support when the traded instrument makes a new rolling low but a correlated instrument does not. Enter short symmetrically: sweep above the prior day’s high or a recent high and close back below, then confirm a strong break below a swing low, bearish fair value gap, price above the higher-timeframe range midpoint, and an ICT window, subject to the corresponding filters. The indicator defines no exit decision.
