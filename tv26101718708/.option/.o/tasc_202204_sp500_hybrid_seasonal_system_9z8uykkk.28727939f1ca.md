Use calendar month and volatility/money-flow data to control positions in the traded equity instrument. Enter long when the month is at or after October or before the configured seasonal exit month, and the previous bar passed a gate: money-flow indicator is above its critical level, and either the external volatility index’s rise from its prior-period high is below its limit or the traded instrument’s ATR rise from its prior-period high is below a scaled limit. Exit longs during the configured seasonal exit month; also exit when the external volatility index exceeds twice its rise limit, or when money flow crosses its critical level while its moving average is declining. Apply the volatility and money-flow exits on the bar after their conditions occur. If shorting is enabled, enter short on those same exit signals instead of closing the long. The seasonal exit is applied throughout its configured month.
