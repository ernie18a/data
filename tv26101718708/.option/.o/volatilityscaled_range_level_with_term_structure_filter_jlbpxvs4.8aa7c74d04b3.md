At the start of each selected trading period, sell a put credit spread on SPX only when the short-dated volatility index is in contango versus the longer-dated index and its level meets a minimum volatility floor. If the optional trend filter is enabled, also require SPX to be above its trend average. Place the short put strike at or below the prior period’s close minus one ATR, rounded down to a valid strike increment, and place the long put farther out of the money by the chosen spread width. Otherwise, do not open a position. No exit rule is implemented.
