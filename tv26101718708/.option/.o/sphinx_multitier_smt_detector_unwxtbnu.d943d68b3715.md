Compare the traded instrument with one or two correlated instruments. At each completed session or intraday-cycle high, and each confirmed higher-timeframe swing high, record all instruments’ values at the same anchor. If the traded instrument later exceeds its anchored high while at least one comparison instrument has not exceeded its own, signal short. At each corresponding low, signal long when the traded instrument or a comparison instrument breaks its anchored low but not all included instruments have done so. Keep each signal active until the traded instrument and the comparison instrument responsible for the divergence have both swept their respective levels; then exit. With an optional external filter enabled, allow a direction only when its gate permits it, the no-trade gate is inactive, and the bias gate qualifies. The indicator provides contextual signals, not a complete entry system.
