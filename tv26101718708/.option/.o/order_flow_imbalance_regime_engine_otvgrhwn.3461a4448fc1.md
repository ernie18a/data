On a confirmed bar, issue a long signal on the first bar when buy-side excitation dominates, its rolling percentile strength is high, standardized order-flow imbalance is materially positive, and the prior bar’s standardized imbalance on the correlated reference instrument is positive and strong. Issue a short signal under the mirrored conditions: sell-side excitation dominates, cascade strength is high, target-instrument imbalance is materially negative, and the lagged reference imbalance is negative and strong. The reference-asset check is required when cross-asset confirmation is enabled. No exit rule is defined.
