On weekly data, go long when either: (1) the instrument shows a Bollinger-band oversold rebound—its low breaks below the lower band, its close recovers above the band by an ATR-based margin and is above the week's midpoint—and either the Treasury yield has fallen substantially from its recent high while its correlation with the instrument is low, or the yield is low; or (2) the instrument closes above the prior Donchian-channel high, SPY is above its moving average, and either enough time has passed since the yield index's recent high, its correlation with the instrument is high, or the yield is low. Close the position on a yield-conditioned trailing stop when the instrument falls by an ATR-based margin from its recent closing high while the yield index has risen substantially from its recent low, or while the yield is elevated and above that low; also close on a stop-loss when the instrument is below its moving average after a substantial weekly loss and SPY has fallen substantially or its correlation with the instrument is negative. Close as overbought when the instrument has risen substantially from its long-term low, then falls by an ATR-based margin from its recent high while its stochastic oscillator has recently been elevated. The strategy does not open short positions.
