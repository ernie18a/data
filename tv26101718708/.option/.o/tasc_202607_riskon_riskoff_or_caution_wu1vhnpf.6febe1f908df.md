At each confirmed week-end, evaluate three conditions using external market data: the broad equity index is above its long-term trend average; short-term volatility is below three-month volatility; and the high-yield-to-intermediate-Treasury ratio has a rolling standardized value above its downside threshold. If all three conditions hold, target full long exposure in the traded instrument; if exactly two hold, target half exposure; otherwise exit to cash. Rebalance for execution at the next week's open. Never take a short position.
