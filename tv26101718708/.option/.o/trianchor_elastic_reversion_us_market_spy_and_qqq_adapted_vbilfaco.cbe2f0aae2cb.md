On the traded instrument, measure how far price is from its daily, weekly, and monthly anchored VWAPs, normalize those distances by ATR, combine them, and require the result to be unusually high versus its recent history. Also require the instrument’s close to have an unusually negative or positive robust Z-score and to finish near the corresponding bar extreme. Reject signals after an excessive session gap. Confirm direction with the average robust Z-score of SPY, QQQ, and IWM: require it to be strongly negative for a long fade and strongly positive for a short fade. On a long setup, close any open position and enter long after a fixed delay if the setup still qualifies from that many bars earlier. On a short setup, close any long and enter short immediately. Exit a short on a qualifying long setup or at its configured ATR stop or profit target; a long is closed by a qualifying long setup or a short setup.
