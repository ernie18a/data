Calculate daily Camarilla pivot levels from the instrument’s prior session data. Signal long when price sweeps below S3 and closes back above it, or closes across R4 on a bullish candle with elevated relative volume. Signal short when price sweeps above R3 and closes back below it, or closes across S4 on a bearish candle with elevated relative volume. For the suggested play, compare the current pivot width with the prior day’s and compare the prior session close with the close before it: in a higher-close context, wide pivots favor an S3-to-R3 long and otherwise an R4-to-R6 long; in a lower-close context, wide pivots favor an R3-to-S3 short and otherwise an S4-to-S6 short. These are entry signals and planned targets; the script defines no exit trigger.
