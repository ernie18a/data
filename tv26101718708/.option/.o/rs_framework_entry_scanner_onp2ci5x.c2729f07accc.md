For the traded instrument, compare its returns over medium and longer lookback periods with a benchmark’s returns. For a long setup, count one point for each condition: the instrument outperformed the benchmark over each period, its price is above its long-term moving average, its MACD histogram is positive, and its RSI is in a healthy midrange. For a short setup, count one point for each: it underperformed the benchmark over each period, its price is below its long-term moving average, its MACD histogram is negative, and its RSI is in a bearish midrange. Enter long when at least four long conditions hold and any bullish trigger occurs: price is near its fast moving average while above its intermediate moving average; the MACD histogram crosses above zero on elevated volume; or price breaks above a recent swing high on elevated volume. Enter short when at least four short conditions hold and any bearish trigger occurs: price is near its fast moving average while below its intermediate moving average; the MACD histogram crosses below zero on elevated volume; or price breaks below a recent swing low on elevated volume. The script defines no exit decision.
