For the traded instrument, build a normalized composite score from available relative strength, company earnings quality when applicable, and trend quality, renormalizing weights over available components. Relative strength ranks the instrument’s medium- and long-term returns, proximity to its yearly high, and change in its price ratio against a suitable external benchmark; benchmark data also determines whether required comparison-data gates pass. Trend quality reflects price versus its long-term average, the short-term average versus the long-term average, and drawdown from a recent high. For equities, earnings quality uses earnings growth, revenue growth, and return on equity. Require adequate history, data coverage, confidence, and any applicable safety checks before buying; these include price above its long-term average, acceptable recent drawdown, and sufficient trading liquidity for equities and, where volume data exists, funds. Signal a long when the score meets the ordinary buy threshold on consecutive bars and all buy gates pass. Signal a stronger long when the score meets the higher threshold on consecutive bars, is rising, has positive recent score momentum, makes a recent price high, and passes stricter relative-strength, trend, confidence, coverage, drawdown, and (for equities) earnings-quality checks. Signal an exit when the score crosses down through the ordinary buy threshold, provided minimum history and watch-level data coverage remain available. A separate sell alert occurs when the score crosses below the model’s weak-score threshold. The model does not signal short positions.
