Use the traded instrument’s fast-versus-slow EMA relation and swing-break trend to determine direction. A long is eligible when its fast EMA is above its slow EMA, or when they are equal and the external index’s fast EMA is below its slow EMA. A short is eligible when the instrument’s fast EMA is below its slow EMA; the index does not affect short eligibility. Enter only when the swing-break trend agrees, the RSI filter allows entry, and re-entry guards permit it. Track whether both markets’ EMAs are bullish or bearish: if a long is profitable and the instrument remains bullish while the combined state changes from both bullish to anything else, partially exit; if a short is profitable and the instrument remains bearish while the combined state changes from both bearish to anything else, partially exit. Apply the strategy’s stop, profit-taking, and opposing-signal exits.
