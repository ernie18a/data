Use the active instrument’s mapped long and/or short trigger, confirmation, invalidation, hard-fail, and target levels; where no map exists, use the prior-day range if generic mapping is enabled. Enter long after price accepts above its trigger through consecutive closes or a strong, high-volume close above confirmation, then meets the retest, controlled-pullback, or expansion-entry condition. Enter short symmetrically below its trigger or confirmation, with a retest or failed-rebound condition. Require all enabled filters to agree: session and fresh-map eligibility, adequate data integrity, aligned moving-average cloud and momentum, VWAP position, relative strength or weakness versus the relevant benchmark, volume, favorable expected reward versus risk, opening-range and event rules, and the mapped market gate. Market gates use relevant external indices, sector ETFs, volatility, breadth, and volatility-curve data; reject entries when those conditions oppose the trade. Do not enter watch-only setups, during blocked session windows, after a hard map failure, or beyond the permitted chase distance. If both sides qualify, take only the side with the higher quality score. Limit repeat entries by the configured cooldown and per-session rule. Exit an open trade at its mapped invalidation; after the first target, move the stop to entry, then manage successive targets and close at the final target. Close at entry if price returns there after the first target but before the final target. Mark the map broken after a hard-fail breach on a single-direction setup and wait for remapping.
