Build a fear series from an implied-volatility index, normalize it, and compare its confirmed swing pivots with the traded instrument’s price pivots. Go long when price makes a lower low while fear makes a higher low, or when price makes a higher low while fear makes a lower low. Go short when price makes a higher high while fear makes a lower high, or when price makes a lower high while fear makes a higher high. Require the divergence to be recent and meaningful; apply any enabled fear, market-regime, or time-of-day filters. No exit rule is defined.
