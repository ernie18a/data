On each completed bar, evaluate long and short entries from the traded instrument’s price, trend strength and direction, momentum, and volume. A long setup requires price above its volatility-buffered trend baseline, a strong trend with positive directional movement dominant, bullish momentum above its threshold, and volume above its rolling average. A short setup requires the corresponding bearish conditions. Require the configured minimum confluence score and, when enabled, reject entries if momentum is unusually large relative to its historical volatility-adjusted average or if the signal has decayed with age. When regime filtering is enabled, permit entries only in a trending regime. When cross-asset filtering is enabled, compare the leading asset’s lagged prices with the traded instrument over a rolling window: if the correlation magnitude reaches its threshold, allow longs only for positive correlation and shorts only for negative correlation; if it does not reach the threshold, allow either direction. Emit a signal only when the setup first becomes true and the minimum spacing since the prior signal has elapsed. Exit a long when the faster momentum oscillator crosses below zero, and exit a short when it crosses above zero.
