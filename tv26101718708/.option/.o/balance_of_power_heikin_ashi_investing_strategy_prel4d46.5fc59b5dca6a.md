Use the monthly Heikin-Ashi candles of SPY to calculate and smooth a balance-of-power measure, then track its rolling upper and lower percentile levels. Go long on the traded instrument when the upper percentile is rising and the measure is positive, or when the measure crosses upward through its lower trigger level. Go short when the measure is negative while both percentile levels are falling, or when the measure crosses downward through its upper trigger level. An opposite entry reverses the position; no separate exit rule is specified.
