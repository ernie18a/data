On the 15-minute timeframe, compare the Hull trend of the dollar index, gold priced in Australian dollars, and Bitcoin priced in US dollars. Enter long when the dollar index trend is down, gold and Bitcoin trends are up, and the traded instrument’s daily return is above a positive threshold; enter short when those three external trends are reversed and the traded instrument’s daily return is below that threshold. Enter only when flat. Close a long when the dollar index trend turns up and the traded instrument’s daily return is below the threshold; close a short when the dollar index trend turns down and the daily return is above it.
