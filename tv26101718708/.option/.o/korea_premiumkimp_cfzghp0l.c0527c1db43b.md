Calculate the Korean-market premium for the traded asset by comparing its Korean-won price with its foreign-market price converted using the won exchange rate; alternatively, use the premium between Korean-won Bitcoin and a dollar Bitcoin index converted to won. Compare the premium with lower and upper thresholds, using the configured method: fixed absolute levels, Bollinger bands, or levels positioned within the recent range of smoothed premium highs and lows. When the premium newly falls below the lower threshold, enter long, or close a short in short-only mode. When it newly rises above the upper threshold, enter short, or close a long in long-only mode. In both-directions mode, take the corresponding long or short entry at each threshold event. Act only within the configured trading date range.
