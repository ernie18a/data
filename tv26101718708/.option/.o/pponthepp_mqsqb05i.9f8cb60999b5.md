Across a basket of related instruments, compare each instrument’s current-period high and low with the preceding period’s high and low, using weekly and daily periods plus New York session-based daily and shorter intraday cycles. Optionally invert an instrument’s prices before comparing. If some instruments exceed the prior high and others do not, signal short; if some fall below the prior low and others do not, signal long. Otherwise, give no directional signal. The indicator defines no exit rule.
