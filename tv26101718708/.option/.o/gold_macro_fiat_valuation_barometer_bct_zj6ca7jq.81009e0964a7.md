For gold, compute a macro score from five factors: 10-year real yields (full bullish credit when low or below their recent average; none when high and above average; otherwise half), gold relative to broad money supply (full credit when its historical percentile is low, none when high, otherwise half), gold’s trend across five major fiat currencies (full credit when above trend in at least three, none when above in at most one, otherwise half), the gold-to-silver ratio and its direction (full credit when high or falling from an elevated level, none when low, otherwise half), and gold’s distance from a fair-value estimate based on money supply and real yields (full credit when substantially below value, none when substantially above, otherwise half). Weight these factors 30%, 25%, 15%, 15%, and 15%, respectively, and smooth the total. Go long/accumulate when the score crosses above 70%; exit or reduce when it crosses below 35%. For tactical timing, buy when gold is at least one standard deviation below fair value and its valuation distance turns upward; reduce when it is at least 1.35 standard deviations above fair value and turns downward. These tactical signals are one-shot until valuation returns near fair value. The framework has no short-entry signal.
