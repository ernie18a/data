Enter long when the instrument’s smoothed log-price-ratio change versus a benchmark is above its own moving average and an entry threshold, at a recent high, but below an overbought ceiling; its RSI recently reached an oversold level and is now rising; its short EMA exceeds a lagged longer SMA; average volume clears a minimum; and benchmark momentum agrees with the sign of the instrument’s correlation (positive correlation when the benchmark is not sharply falling, negative correlation when it is). While long, exit if relative strength falls below a negative threshold while correlation with the benchmark is strongly positive, the holding period reaches its limit, or the instrument reaches its profit target. No short entry.
