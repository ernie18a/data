For the traded instrument, use a configured basket of other instruments as external evidence. When enough basket data is available, calculate the share with positive one-bar returns and the share trading above their own trend averages. Combine these into a participation score, reducing its strength when coverage is thin, the two measures disagree, or return dispersion is unusually high. Go long when the score clears the positive broad-participation threshold and both shares indicate a majority; go short under the corresponding negative conditions. Treat insufficient data or fragmented evidence as neutral and exit any position. Retain a broad state while the score remains beyond its state-retention boundary; otherwise return to neutral.
