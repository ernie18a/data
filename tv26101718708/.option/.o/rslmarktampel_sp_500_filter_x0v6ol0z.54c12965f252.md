Use the S&P 500 as the market filter. If it closes below its long-term weekly average for consecutive weeks, or below its long-term daily average, exit all positions and stay in cash. Otherwise, if it closes below its long-term weekly average or its medium-term daily average, do not open positions; hold an existing instrument only while it remains above its own long-term weekly average, and exit it if it falls below. In the offensive regime, when the index is above its long-term weekly and daily averages, hold or select instruments by relative-strength ranking and exit those that fall in rank or below their relative-strength baseline. Resume buying after the index closes back above its long-term weekly average, selecting the highest-ranked instruments.
