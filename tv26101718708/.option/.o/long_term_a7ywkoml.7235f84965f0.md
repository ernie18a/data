Enter long when any of these hold: price is above the medium-term average on elevated volume; price is above the long-term average, has outperformed a comparison instrument over the lookback period, volume is elevated, and price breaks above the prior-period high; or price is above the short-term average on exceptionally elevated volume. Exit long positions if price falls materially below the medium-term average, drops substantially from the prior close, rises unusually far above recent closes, or falls below both the short-term and medium-term averages.
