For each candidate asset and each other candidate plus USD, evaluate the pair’s trend using the selected method: RSI above its midpoint, CCI above zero, SuperTrend bullish, positive directional movement exceeding negative, or price above Parabolic SAR. Award one point to the pair’s numerator asset when bullish, and one to the other asset when bearish. If combining all methods, treat the pair as bullish when any method is bullish. Sum each asset’s points, including USD’s points from the inverse asset/USD comparisons, and hold long the asset with the highest score. If USD has the highest score, exit to cash. Resolve ties by retaining the earliest asset in the candidate order, with USD last. The logic does not issue short entries.
