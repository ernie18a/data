Select an external benchmark appropriate to the traded market: a broad equity index for most markets, its inverse for USD currency pairs, or a broad cryptocurrency index for crypto. Over a rolling window, measure the adjusted strength of the relationship among the traded instrument’s closing price, its volume, and the benchmark. Use that score to adjust the bullish and bearish weights derived from either the instrument’s RSI or its money-flow index, then normalize the bullish weight against their combined total. Go long when the normalized bullish weight exceeds the midpoint; go short when it is below the midpoint. Reevaluate each bar, reversing the position when the signal changes.
