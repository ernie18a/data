For the traded instrument, enter long when its EMA is rising, its swing-breakout trend is bullish, and its trend strength exceeds the active threshold; if the external footprint filter is enabled and data is available, require the selected BTC or ETH footprint delta smoothed over recent bars to be positive. Enter short when the corresponding EMA and swing trend are bearish and trend strength exceeds the threshold; with the filter enabled and data available, require that external delta to be negative. If external delta data is unavailable, it does not block either entry.
