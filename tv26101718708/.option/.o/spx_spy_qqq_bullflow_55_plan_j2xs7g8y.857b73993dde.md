For the broad-market index, consider longs after price reclaims its bullish trigger only when the volatility index is below its risk threshold; favor shorts after price breaks its bearish trigger when volatility holds above that threshold and rises. Use the broad-market ETF as confirmation: if the index reclaims its bullish trigger but the ETF has not reclaimed its own bullish level, do not enter the index long; ETF breakdown supports the bearish case. For the technology ETF, favor a long after its stronger breakout level is reclaimed when volatility is low and the broad-market ETF is above its reclaim level. Exit or avoid entries when these external confirmations fail.
