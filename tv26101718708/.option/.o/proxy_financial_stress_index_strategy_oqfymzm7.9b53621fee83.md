For the traded instrument, calculate a composite stress score from daily closes of the VIX, US 10-year Treasury yield, dollar index, S&P 500, EUR/USD, and a high-yield corporate bond ETF. Standardize each series as its deviation from its moving average divided by its standard deviation, then combine the standardized values using configurable component weights. Enter long when the composite score crosses below a configurable threshold from above, provided no position is open. Close the long after a configurable number of bars. The strategy has no short-entry rule.
