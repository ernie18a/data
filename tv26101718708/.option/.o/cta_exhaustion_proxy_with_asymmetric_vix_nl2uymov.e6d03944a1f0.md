Estimate trend crowding by scoring the instrument bullish or bearish when its price is above or below each of its fast, medium, and slow moving averages. Consider a long reversal when prior readings show strong bearish crowding and price crosses above the fast average, provided daily VIX is elevated. This can trigger an early signal when the crowding score improves rapidly, a sharp reversal signal when extreme bearish crowding lasted for a shorter sustained period and the current bullish candle is strong, or an exhaustion signal when it lasted longer. Consider a short reversal symmetrically: prior strong bullish crowding, a rapid score decline or sustained extreme crowding, and a cross below the fast average, with daily VIX subdued; the sharp signal also requires a strong bearish candle. The logic defines no exit condition.
