On confirmed bars during the regular session, combine candle-location pressure from the index futures and tracking funds with the inverse fund’s pressure direction reversed. Normalize each leg by its own recent volume, weight valid legs, and standardize the aggregate against its recent history; require sufficient valid-leg coverage. Generate a long direction when the aggregate crosses above zero, or is positive and rising while a majority share of active weight is bullish; generate a short direction on the corresponding cross below zero, or when it is negative and falling while a majority share is bearish. Require the external filter instrument’s RSI to support that direction: for long, RSI is oversold, recovering after being oversold, or between neutral and overbought; for short, RSI is overbought, declining after being overbought, or between oversold and neutral. Apply the RSI requirement to both directions when enabled. Emit agreement signals only on a new qualifying state and respect a per-direction cooldown. The indicator defines no exit rule.
