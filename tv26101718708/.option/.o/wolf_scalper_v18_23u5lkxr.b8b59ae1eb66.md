Compare the traded instrument’s confirmed swing highs and lows with those of two related instruments. If it makes a higher swing high while either related instrument fails to make a higher high, record a recent bearish divergence; if it makes a lower swing low while either related instrument fails to make a lower low, record a recent bullish divergence. Each recent divergence adds one point to the corresponding side’s confluence score. Generate a short or long only when the traded instrument also has a confirmed bearish or bullish setup, respectively, and its total score meets the configured grade threshold and applicable filters. The divergence alone does not trigger a trade, and no exit decision uses the related instruments.
