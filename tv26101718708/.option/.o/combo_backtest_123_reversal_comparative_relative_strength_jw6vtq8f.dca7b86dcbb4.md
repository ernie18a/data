Compare the traded instrument’s close with a benchmark instrument’s close, and calculate a moving average of their price ratio. The relative-strength state turns long when the average ratio rises above an upper threshold and short when it falls below a lower threshold. Between those thresholds, retain the prior state until the ratio crosses a central exit threshold against that state, then become flat. Enter long only when this state is long and the traded instrument has risen over two consecutive comparisons while its fast stochastic is below its slow stochastic and above a midpoint. Enter short only when the state is short and the instrument has fallen over two consecutive comparisons while its fast stochastic is above its slow stochastic and below the midpoint. Otherwise, exit any open position. An optional reverse setting swaps long and short.
