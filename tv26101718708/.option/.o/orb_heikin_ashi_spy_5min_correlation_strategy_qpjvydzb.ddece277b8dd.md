Build a reference range from the high and low of a correlated asset during the opening-range period, optionally using its Heikin-Ashi candles. During the subsequent trading window, go long when the traded instrument closes above the reference high and its relative volume exceeds its baseline; go short when it closes below the reference low and relative volume exceeds its baseline. Do not activate the opposite direction while a position signal is active. Exit all positions at the session’s designated close time.
