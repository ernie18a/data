Use the yield curve, broad-equity drawdown, and gold momentum to rotate suggested exposure among bonds, equities, gold, and cash. For base allocation, prioritize an inverted curve; otherwise, a flat or inverted curve that is steepening calls for a bond-building stance; otherwise, a positively steep curve with falling short-term yields and long yields stable or falling calls for an equity-rotation stance; otherwise, rising long yields that drive bad steepening call for reduced duration and tighter risk; use a balanced stance when none apply. If broad-equity drawdown crosses a warning level, trim equities and add cash and gold; at a hard drawdown level, impose a risk-off allocation with reduced equities and increased gold and cash. When the curve is inverted or showing bad steepening, or the drawdown warning is active, positive gold momentum adds a further tilt from equities to gold. Normalize the resulting allocations. This logic specifies long exposure and reductions among the named assets; it does not specify short entries.
