Derive a fair-value estimate from external net liquidity (Federal Reserve assets minus Treasury cash balance minus reverse-repo balance), then compare daily SPX closing price with that estimate. With positive net liquidity, signal overbought when the spread crosses above its upper threshold and oversold when it crosses below its lower threshold. After the configured start date, trade overbought as short and oversold as long; in short-only or long-only mode, use the opposite signal to exit. Long/short mode reverses position on each signal. An inverse setting swaps long and short actions. Close open positions on the configured close date.
