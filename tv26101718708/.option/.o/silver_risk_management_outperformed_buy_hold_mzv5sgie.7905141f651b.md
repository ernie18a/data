Stay long the traded instrument by default. Exit when its price falls below a slow trend average, a major external risk indicator (such as a bond yield or dollar index) remains above its own trend average by an unusually large margin while breaking a recent high, or the instrument suffers a sharp downside or volatility shock. Re-enter only when the instrument is above its slow trend average, external risk indicators are calm for consecutive bars, and, after a recent shock, price has recovered above a faster average with volatility calm for consecutive bars.
