Use a configurable external market series as the signal input. Block entries whenever an enabled volatility or Bollinger-band filter rejects the market. When volatility qualifies for the trend regime, go long if the short-term average is above both the longer-term average and the long-term-context average. When volatility qualifies for the mean-reversion regime, go long below the lower z-score threshold and short above the upper threshold. Close a trend long when the external price reaches the upper Bollinger band; close a mean-reversion long or short when the external price returns to the Bollinger mean. Also close positions at the configured holding limit; the trend-long trailing stop additionally uses ATR from the traded chart instrument. The strategy has no external-market condition that opens a trend short.
