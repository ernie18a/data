On the traded instrument, consider a long after it sweeps recent lows and closes back above them, or a short after it sweeps recent highs and closes back below them. Score each direction by confluence: matching higher-timeframe sweep bias, active London or New York session, unusually high volume, a matching liquidity sweep in the correlated market, relative strength versus that market, aligned higher-timeframe fair-value gaps, proximity to relevant structure, supportive volatility-index direction, repeated same-direction sweeps, and reversal or gap-entry confirmation. Reversal and gap-entry confirmations carry extra weight. Signal only when the relevant score reaches the configured minimum; favor the higher-scoring side. Treat a conflicting gap and sweep as a wait condition. After a qualifying sweep, exit or invalidate a long if price closes below the swept-low setup's protective level, or a short if it closes above the swept-high setup's protective level; also mark a break of the opposite recent structure as entry confirmation.
