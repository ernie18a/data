Use the selected broad-market index as the signal source for the traded instrument, with the volatility index as confirmation. Calculate the index’s 200-bar simple average and standard deviation, its 14-bar RSI, its 10-bar exponential average, and its price-to-average ratio. Long signals: (1) RSI below 30 and price at least 2 standard deviations below its average, or RSI below 35 and price at least 1.8 standard deviations below average while volatility is at least 35; (2) price at least 2.8 standard deviations below average with volatility at least 35, or at least 2 deviations below with volatility at least 45; (3) price-to-average ratio at or below 0.85, or at or below 0.88 while volatility is at least 35. Sell or exit signals: (1) after RSI above 70 and price at least 2 standard deviations above average occurred within the past five bars, price crosses below its 10-bar exponential average; (2) price is at least 3 standard deviations above average, volatility is at or below 12, and price crosses below its 10-bar exponential average; (3) after the price-to-average ratio reached at least 1.15 within the past five bars, price crosses below its 10-bar exponential average. Suppress each individual tier’s repeated signal until more than five bars have passed since its previous signal. No separate short-entry rule is specified.
