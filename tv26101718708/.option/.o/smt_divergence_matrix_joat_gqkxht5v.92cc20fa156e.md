On the traded instrument, compare each confirmed swing low or high with the corresponding swing in either enabled reference instrument. Go long when the traded instrument makes a lower swing low while at least one valid reference makes a higher swing low; go short when it makes a higher swing high while at least one valid reference makes a lower swing high. When the correlation filter is enabled, the triggering reference must meet the configured minimum correlation. Apply the selected confirmation rule on the pivot-confirmation bar: none, RSI moving in the signal direction, a candle closing in that direction, or both. Enforce the configured minimum bar gap between signals of the same direction, and do not emit long and short signals on the same bar. The indicator defines no separate exit decision; its ATR-based stop and target are illustrative trade zones.
