During the active date window, calculate a weighted moving average of a selected external reference instrument’s price. Enter long when that average has risen for five consecutive bars and the traded instrument is flat or short. When currently long, switch to short if the average has fallen for five consecutive bars. Record the reference instrument’s price on entry. While long, exit if its price rises above the entry reference price by the configured profit threshold or falls below it by the configured loss threshold. While short, exit if its price rises above the entry reference price by the configured short profit threshold or falls below it by the configured short loss threshold.
