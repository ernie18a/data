During 9:30–10:30 ET, go long when price breaks above the opening 15-minute range, then retests its high from above while closing above it, and at least three of the 4-hour, daily, weekly, and monthly sweep biases are bullish; do not take the long if external market breadth is overbought. Go short on the mirrored break and retest below the range low when at least three timeframe biases are bearish, unless breadth is oversold. Sweep bias is bullish when the latest sweep of a prior bar’s low is followed by price holding at or above the sweep candle’s close; it is bearish when a sweep of a prior high is followed by price holding at or below that close. No exit rule is defined.
