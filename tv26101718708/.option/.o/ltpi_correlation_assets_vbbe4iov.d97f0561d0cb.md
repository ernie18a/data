For the traded asset, cast bullish or bearish votes from rate of change, RSI relative to its midpoint, fast versus slow EMA, and close versus a long-term SMA. Average the votes into a directional trend score. Compute the asset’s rolling Pearson correlation with Bitcoin across multiple lookback windows, exclude windows with insufficient aligned data, and combine valid correlations into a weighted average. Multiply the trend score by this correlation weight: go long when the result is above the neutral zone, short when below it, and exit or stay flat when it is inside the zone. A negative correlation can reverse the trend score’s direction.
