Using two external assets, calculate rolling statistics of their log prices: means, standard deviations, and correlation. Estimate the hedge ratio as correlation multiplied by the second asset’s standard deviation divided by the first’s; estimate the intercept from the rolling means. Compute the second asset’s log price minus its value predicted from the first asset. On the traded instrument, enter short when this residual exceeds the positive entry threshold, and exit that short when it falls below the positive exit threshold. Enter long when the residual falls below the negative entry threshold, and exit that long when it rises above the negative exit threshold.
