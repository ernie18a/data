Use the session-to-date percentage changes of the instrument’s sector peers to compare a cap-weighted basket with an equal-weighted basket. Favor a long when both averages are positive and moving together. Avoid longs when both are negative and moving together; treat a positive weighted average with weaker equal-weighted breadth as fragile and avoid chasing. A change in the gap between the averages signals possible rotation, but does not by itself trigger an entry, short, or exit.
