Compare the traded instrument’s confirmed swing highs and lows with a normalized series from a related external market, optionally inverted or time-shifted. Only act when the two markets’ recent correlation is strong enough. Go long when the instrument makes a lower low while the external series makes a higher low, or when the instrument makes a higher low while the external series makes a lower low. Go short when the instrument makes a higher high while the external series makes a lower high, or when the instrument makes a lower high while the external series makes a higher high. Require the compared swings to be sufficiently separated in magnitude and time; optionally require agreement across three successive swings and a statistically active time slot. The script defines no exit decision.
