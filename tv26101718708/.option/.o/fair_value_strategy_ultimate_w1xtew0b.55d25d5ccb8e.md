Use net liquidity, calculated as the central bank’s balance sheet minus the treasury’s cash account and reverse repo balance, to estimate fair value for a selected market index by scaling net liquidity and subtracting an offset. Compare the selected index’s daily close with that estimate. When net liquidity is positive, an upward crossing of the configurable upper threshold signals overvaluation; a downward crossing of the configurable lower threshold signals undervaluation. After the configured start date, in long/short mode, open short on overvaluation and long on undervaluation; in short-only mode, open short on overvaluation and exit on undervaluation; in long-only mode, open long on undervaluation and exit on overvaluation. An inverse setting swaps long and short directions. Close any open position on the configured close date.
