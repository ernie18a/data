For a ZAR pair, combine its trend, momentum, RSI, and position within its volatility bands into a directional score. When macro inputs are enabled, add bullish weight when the US dollar index, US yields, or volatility index have risen over the lookback period, and bearish weight when they have fallen. Go long when the capped score reaches the bullish zone, short when it reaches the bearish zone, and otherwise remain neutral. The indicator defines no exit rule.
