Trade the options position long-only from the underlying’s price and trend data. Enter when the trend turns bullish: either the trend indicator switches to its uptrend state, or a chosen support-like source crosses from above the underlying’s close to below it. A delayed entry is allowed while the bullish trend remains active and price stays within the configured entry tolerance of the signal price; cancel it if price rises beyond the invalidation level or the waiting window expires. Enter only when flat, during the permitted entry window, and if no trade has already been opened for that expiry. As the underlying rises through the position’s successive call-strike levels, adjust the position in stages: roll at the first level, then remove the short calls at the next; if price jumps past both, apply the final stage directly. After the final stage, ratchet the stop upward as price advances. At a configured further advance, optionally roll the calls higher and/or close the puts. Exit the position if the underlying falls below its current stop or the expiry square-off time is reached.
