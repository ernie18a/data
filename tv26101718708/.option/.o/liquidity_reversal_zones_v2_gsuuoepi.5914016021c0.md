On each confirmed bar, build a directional reversal score from oversold/overbought conditions, turning order flow, regular divergences, candle rejection, higher-timeframe bias, and optional funding or spot-perpetual basis. Give positive direction to potential longs and negative direction to shorts. Scale the score by aligned event evidence and context, including volume, open-interest/price behavior, volatility, VWAP, and trend or chop conditions. A long setup requires price to pierce a prior low liquidity level and close back above it; a short setup requires piercing a prior high level and closing back below it. Candidate levels include prior swing and rolling extremes and completed session extremes. Require the directional score to meet at least a medium-strength threshold, enough enabled and available evidence sources to agree, and no active chop block; apply any configured reversal-candle requirement and signal cooldown. Emit a long or short signal on the setup bar, or on the next bar if confirmation mode requires follow-through in the signal direction. Track each resulting zone: a return into it followed by a rejecting wick and close back out is a same-direction retest signal; a close through the far edge invalidates the zone, while sufficient favorable movement confirms it. The indicator defines no separate position exit rule.
