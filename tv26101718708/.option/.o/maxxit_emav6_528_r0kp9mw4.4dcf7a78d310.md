When trading a mirror instrument, use the configured source instrument’s price bars to generate signals for the traded instrument. On each confirmed bar, permit new signals only when trading is enabled, the session and daily loss limits allow trading, and that direction is below its position limit. Enter long when the slow trend EMA has risen over its lookback and the fast EMA crosses above the medium EMA; enter short on the mirrored conditions. If pullback-to-fast-EMA filtering is enabled, also require the prior bar to have crossed the fast EMA and the current close to reclaim it in the trend direction. In re-entry mode, instead require the position to be flat, the slow EMA, fast EMA, and recent highs (long) or lows (short) to be rising (falling), the fast EMA to remain on the trend side of the slow EMA now and several bars earlier, the prior bar to pull back through the fast EMA, and the current close to rebound across it; suppress re-entry when the fast and slow EMAs are excessively far apart relative to ATR. Close an open position when the source price reaches its configured take-profit or enabled fixed/trailing stop; a valid opposite-direction entry can also reverse the position unless directional-only mode prevents reversal. Apply the same directional rules with signs reversed for shorts.
