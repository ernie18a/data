In Grinder mode, mark a bullish long setup when the stock has low volatility, has stayed above its intermediate trend average for an extended period, and has outperformed a benchmark over the same recent period. Require the stock’s price to be at or between its fast and intermediate trend averages (or very near the fast average), with the fast, intermediate, and long-term averages stacked in bullish order. The benchmark comparison is the only input from outside the traded stock. No short or exit logic is defined.
