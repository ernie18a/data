On each confirmed bar, calculate a normalized weighted score from the instrument’s RSI state and divergence, MACD impulse and momentum, adaptive-average direction and price position, volume/OBV and candle pressure, candle body/wick and range-versus-ATR proxies, plus the mapped sector ETF’s return relative to a broad-market benchmark over a lookback. Give each component a positive or negative contribution according to its bullish or bearish conditions; neutral or mixed conditions contribute zero or partial weight. Enter or reverse long when the score reaches the bullish threshold, the core momentum, RSI, and adaptive-average signals all agree bullish, volume confirms, and volume is not abnormally low. Enter or reverse short under the mirrored bearish conditions. Close a long on a valid short signal or when the score falls below a negative exit boundary; close a short on a valid long signal or when the score rises above a positive exit boundary. Evaluate decisions at bar close.
