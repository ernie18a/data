Compare futures price with the corresponding spot price. Compute the relative spread as (futures − spot) / spot. Enter long when the spread reaches or exceeds the greater of a fixed positive minimum and its recent mean plus 1.5 standard deviations; enter short when it reaches or falls below the lesser of the negative minimum and its recent mean minus 1.5 standard deviations. The adaptive thresholds apply when recent spread statistics are available; otherwise use the fixed minimum. Allow entries when no position is open or the existing position has lasted longer than the maximum holding period. Close any open position when the spread falls below 60% of the long-entry threshold, or when the maximum holding period expires. Each signal represents a paired arbitrage: long spot and short futures for a long signal, short spot and long futures for a short signal.
