Use the underlying futures’ price, volume, volatility, expiry, and event status to choose an options-selling stance. First, switch to defensive mode and manage or exit existing positions if trend strength breaks upward through its no-trend boundary, volatility percentile jumps sharply, or price crosses an extreme Bollinger-band boundary. Otherwise, sell both sides at full size when trend strength indicates a range, volatility is in its comfortable-to-rich zone without expansion, price is mid-range, expiry is sufficiently distant, and no event is flagged. When a trend is present, sell a put spread in an uptrend or a call spread in a downtrend at full size if volatility is comfortable-to-rich without expansion, expiry is sufficiently distant, and there is no event combined with rich volatility. In all other conditions, use half size with wider strikes; do not open new positions in defensive mode. Close positions at the regime’s premium-decay target, if a sold option’s premium reaches the specified stop multiple of entry, or when expiry is near. After partial profit, trail the remainder to breakeven. When conditions turn defensive, close or adjust the tested side and manage the untested side for decay.
