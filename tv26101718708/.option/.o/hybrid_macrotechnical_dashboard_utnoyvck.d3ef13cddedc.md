On weekly data, build a macro score from global liquidity momentum, credit spreads, real yields, dollar strength, copper relative to gold, the 10-year/2-year yield spread, and volatility: improving liquidity, falling spreads and real yields, a weakening dollar, a rising copper/gold ratio, a positive yield spread, and calm volatility add bullish points; the opposite conditions subtract points. When the score is positive, classify the instrument as bullish whether its price is above or below its 40-week average (secure bull or buy-the-dip state). When the score is not positive, classify it as danger if price is above that average and crash if below. The indicator specifies no short entry or exit rule.
