Compare the instrument’s price with a benchmark selected for its asset class or specified by the user. Calculate the log of the instrument-to-benchmark price ratio. For several short-, medium-, and long-term horizons, measure the change in this relative-strength series and rank each change against its own recent history; average the ranks into a rotation score. Also calculate whether the price ratio is above its long-term average and whether the relative-strength trend’s recent slope is unusually strong or weak. Enter long when the score crosses above its upper threshold, the ratio is above its average, and the slope is sufficiently positive; optionally require the instrument’s price to be above its own trend average. Enter short on the mirrored lower-threshold crossing with the ratio below its average and slope sufficiently negative; optionally require price below its trend average. Close a long when the score falls below its exit threshold or the ratio drops below its average; close a short when the score rises above its mirrored exit threshold or the ratio rises above its average. Apply a volatility-based protective stop and wait through a cooldown after closing before allowing another entry.
