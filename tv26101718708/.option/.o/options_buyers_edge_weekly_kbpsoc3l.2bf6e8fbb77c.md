For the underlying, combine normalized recent price momentum, the change in futures basis, lower-timeframe up-versus-down volume imbalance, and signed option buyer pressure across nearby call and put strikes. Reduce the option-flow contribution when the corresponding out-of-the-money option skew disagrees. If the combined directional score exceeds its positive threshold and implied volatility is not falling, signal a long call; if it falls below the negative threshold under the same premium condition, signal a long put. Falling implied volatility suppresses new directional entries. When the score is neutral, avoid entry if expiry decay is extreme; otherwise wait. No explicit exit rule is defined.
