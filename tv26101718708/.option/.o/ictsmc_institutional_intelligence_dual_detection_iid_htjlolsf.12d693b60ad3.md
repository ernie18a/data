For a potential reversal, require a fresh structural break against the prevailing higher-timeframe bias and a recent, qualifying sweep of liquidity against that bias. Set the reversal direction from the structural break. Increase reversal conviction when a correlated instrument fails to confirm the traded instrument’s new swing extreme: at a new high, the correlated instrument fails to make its expected high (or low if inversely correlated), supporting a short; at a new low, it fails to make its expected low (or high if inversely correlated), supporting a long. The divergence adds conviction but does not independently trigger a trade; the indicator reports directional conditions and leaves trade decisions to the user.
