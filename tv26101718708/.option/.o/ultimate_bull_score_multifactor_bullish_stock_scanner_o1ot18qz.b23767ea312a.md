On completed daily data with sufficient history, compare the instrument’s momentum and price performance with a broad-market benchmark, and assess its reported revenue, earnings, cash flow, margins, and valuation. Combine these with the instrument’s trend, momentum, volume, price structure, and chart-pattern evidence into a bullish score; deduct for excessive extension, unusually rapid gains, weakening momentum or accumulation, deteriorating revenue or cash flow, and expensive valuation. Consider a long setup when the adjusted score and business-data coverage are sufficiently strong, risk deductions are limited, price is above its intermediate trend average, and volume data is available. Also consider an entry setup when the short-term trend has recently turned up, its slope is improving, price and relative strength have recently reclaimed key levels, accumulation and momentum are improving, and price remains near its intermediate trend average; apply the same coverage, risk, and volume filters. A pullback watch area is identified near rising trend support or recently broken resistance when the broader score and filters qualify. No short or exit rules are defined.
