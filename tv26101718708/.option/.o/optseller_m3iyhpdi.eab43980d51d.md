Using the underlying price, calculate a center from VWAP (or a short-term moving average if VWAP is disabled), then set upper and lower trigger levels one volatility range above and below it, rounded outward to option strikes. During the configured trading session, while below the daily trade limit, sell a call when the underlying’s high reaches the upper level if RSI is above its oversold threshold; sell a put when its low reaches the lower level if RSI is below its overbought threshold. The trend filter does not restrict entries because its condition is already satisfied whenever the corresponding level is touched. Exit each short option at its percentage stop or profit target measured from its trigger strike, or at the end-of-day cutoff.
