Use advancing/declining volume, market breadth, and uptick/downtick readings as external inputs for the traded instrument. Score bullish evidence across six checks: volume positive, volume above its configured strong threshold, breadth positive, breadth above its strong threshold, ticks positive, and ticks above their warning threshold. Score bearish evidence across the mirrored checks, counting nonpositive breadth and ticks as bearish. A side with at least five checks signals a trend in that direction; exactly four signals a high-confidence directional bias. When neither side scores at least three, classify conditions as choppy and take no directional position. Compare volume with its moving average and its previous reading: rising, strongly positive volume above the average forbids shorts; falling, strongly negative volume below the average forbids longs. If breadth and volume have opposite signs, flag divergence and treat the bias cautiously. The indicator provides no explicit exit rule.
