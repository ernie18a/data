Use NYSE breadth to identify a bearish market warning. A breadth trigger occurs when both new highs and new lows exceed a configured share of advancing plus declining issues while the traded instrument is at or above its close from a configured lookback. For a configured window after that trigger, the warning is active when the McClellan oscillator— the difference between short and long exponential averages of normalized net advances—is negative. Also require that NYSE new lows exceed new highs while the traded instrument has made a 52-week high within a recent configured period. If both conditions have occurred within a configured joint window, treat the composite as a short signal. Optionally require a third condition within that window: overbought momentum (RSI or volatility-normalized MACD) together with an elevated smoothed NYSE advances share. No exit or long rule is specified.
