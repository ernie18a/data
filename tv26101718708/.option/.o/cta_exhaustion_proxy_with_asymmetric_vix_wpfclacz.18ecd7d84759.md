Use the traded instrument’s position relative to short-, medium-, and long-term moving averages to form a bullish or bearish alignment score. Consider reversal signals only after the score reaches an extreme: for a long, bearish; for a short, bullish. Require daily volatility-index data to be elevated for longs and subdued for shorts. Signal a reversal when price crosses back through the short-term average in the opposite direction, with either a rapid score reversal after a recent extreme, a rapid reversal after a few bars of persistent extreme positioning (also requiring a strong reversal candle when enabled), or a crossing after prolonged extreme positioning. No exit condition is defined.
