Use the benchmark index as external data. Go long when the instrument’s monthly close is above a rising monthly moving average, monthly RSI is above a bullish threshold, and its price relative to the benchmark is above its own moving average and rising over the lookback period. Require the current bar to make both a higher high and a higher low than the prior lookback range. Mark the signal stronger when the bar closes up, its volume exceeds average volume, and recent up-bar volume exceeds down-bar volume. Otherwise, avoid a long entry. No short or exit rule is defined.
