For the traded asset, combine a macro risk score with trend and entry-condition scores. The macro score improves when the dollar weakens, gold and equities rise, volatility falls, more cross-market indicators align with risk-on conditions, and the volatility index signals less fear. The trend score favors bullish moving-average alignment and positive momentum, while weaker trend and unusually high volatility lower it. The entry score favors deeper drawdowns from the asset’s all-time high, oversold momentum, price below its long-term average, and bearish regimes; penalize overheated momentum when price is well above that average. Weight the macro, trend, and entry scores, then map the combined score to graduated actions: stronger scores increase buying, intermediate scores maintain or reduce buying, and the weakest scores pause contributions or reduce exposure.
