On each daily decision, compare daily Bitcoin MVRV and weekly Bitcoin RSI with their respective dynamic thresholds, calculated from each indicator’s long-term weekly mean and standard deviation. Buy when either indicator falls below its mean minus a volatility-scaled threshold; sell part of the existing long position when either rises above its mean plus a volatility-scaled threshold. For each active signal, scale the trade fraction from a minimum near the threshold to a maximum at the indicator’s extreme undervalued or overvalued level; average the fractions when both indicators signal in the same direction. Buys use available cash, and sells reduce the current position. The strategy does not open short positions.
