For each altcoin, compare its normalized momentum against Bitcoin with a significance threshold, and classify Bitcoin dominance momentum using symmetric regime thresholds. When dominance is rising and the altcoin is significantly underperforming Bitcoin, signal short; when dominance is falling and the altcoin is significantly outperforming Bitcoin, signal long. Otherwise issue no directional signal. There is no exit rule. Optionally flag a divergence setup when the altcoin’s dollar momentum is positive despite the directional signal; add a stronger timing flag when the relative-price RSI is above its upper timing level for a short signal or below its lower timing level for a long signal.
