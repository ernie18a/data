Calculate the external two-year government bond yield minus the central-bank policy rate, then compare that spread with its recent mean and standardized historical range. Go long when the spread exceeds its configured positive gap threshold, its standardized value exceeds the positive anomaly threshold, and it is above its recent average for the required consecutive period. Go short under the mirrored conditions: the spread is below the negative gap threshold and recent average, its standardized value is below the negative anomaly threshold, and this persists for the required period. Exit the corresponding position when its confirmed conditions cease to hold. The indicator provides directional pressure signals; it defines no separate position sizing or risk rules.
