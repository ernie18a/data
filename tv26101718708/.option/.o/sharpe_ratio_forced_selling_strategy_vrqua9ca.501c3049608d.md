Compute the traded instrument’s rolling annualized Sharpe ratio from its returns in excess of the per-period risk-free rate, using the external short-term government bill yield to derive that rate. When flat and entries are enabled, go long if the ratio falls below a low threshold; disable further entries. Exit the long if the ratio rises above a high threshold or the holding period reaches its maximum. Re-enable entries when the ratio rises above the high threshold. The strategy takes no short positions.
