For the S&P 500, compare successive confirmed swing highs and lows in the VIX-to-S&P 500 ratio with the S&P 500 price and its RSI at those pivots. Go short when the S&P 500 makes a higher swing high, the ratio makes a lower swing high, and the S&P 500 RSI indicates strength. Go long when the S&P 500 makes a lower swing low, the ratio makes a higher swing low, and the RSI indicates weakness. No exit rule is specified.
