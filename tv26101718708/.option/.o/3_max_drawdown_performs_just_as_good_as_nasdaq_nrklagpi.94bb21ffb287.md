Long-only. Enter when either: (1) the broad-market index’s daily lows stay above its intermediate EMA for several consecutive days, the growth-market ETF’s lows are above its fast and intermediate EMAs, its closes are not falling across several consecutive sessions, and broad-market ADX indicates a trend; or (2) Bitcoin is bullish by fast-versus-intermediate EMA, low-versus-intermediate EMA, and rising-close checks, while the broad-market index closes above its short SMA. Apply an entry filter: if the broad-market index has gone an extended period without a new all-time high, require its close to be sufficiently above its long-term EMA. Once a drawdown from the broad-market index’s all-time high reaches the configured crash threshold, suppress exits while holding until the index recovers the pre-drawdown high; this hold also applies to a later entry made while the crash state remains armed. Exit outside that recovery hold if either (a) the position is losing, the growth-market ETF closes below its intermediate EMA, and the broad-market index closes below its short SMA; or (b) the position is profitable and either the broad-market index’s short SMA falls below the prior session’s long SMA while the index is below its short SMA and the growth-market ETF is below its intermediate EMA, or the ETF’s low breaches the entry-price stop while also below its longer-term SMA. Never open short positions.
