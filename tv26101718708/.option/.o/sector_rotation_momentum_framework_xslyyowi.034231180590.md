Compare the traded instrument’s price with a configurable sector benchmark by dividing its price by the benchmark’s price, then smooth that ratio with a moving average. Enter long when the instrument’s faster trend average is above its slower trend average and the ratio is above its smoothing average; enter short when the faster average is below the slower average and the ratio is below its smoothing average. Allow an entry when flat or positioned in the opposite direction, reversing on the opposite signal. Exit each position at either a volatility-based stop or a profit target set at a fixed risk-to-reward multiple of the stop distance.
