On each confirmed bar, identify confirmed swing highs and lows in the traded instrument and a correlated external instrument, comparing each instrument’s two most recent pivots of the same type. If the traded instrument makes a higher high while the external instrument makes a lower high, and the latest high pivots are within the configured synchronization window, signal short. If the traded instrument makes a lower low while the external instrument makes a higher low, and the latest low pivots are within that window, signal long. Require volatility to meet the configured ATR-versus-ATR-average filter when enabled. No exit rule is specified.
