On each confirmed intraday bar, score enabled oscillators and moving averages on the selected data symbol as bullish, bearish, or neutral; identify the larger directional vote and its share of all enabled indicators. Maintain that direction as the daily phase, ignoring neutral readings and resetting the phase each day. Enter long or short when the phase flips, the consensus share passes the configured minimum-or-maximum threshold, and, if enabled, the entire candle is above the alignment EMA for a long or below it for a short. An opposite entry reverses the position.
