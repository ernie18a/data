On the traded instrument, calculate a confidence score from trend alignment, trend strength, momentum, volume, and oscillator agreement. Generate a long signal on a bullish fast/medium moving-average crossover when the broader moving-average trend is not bearish, momentum is supportive, price is above a volatility-based trailing level, confidence is high, and multiple chart timeframes agree on a direction. Generate the corresponding short signal on a bearish crossover with bearish momentum and price below the trailing level. Also allow a long reversal when price reclaims the lower volatility band after an oversold reading, or a short reversal when it falls back below the upper band after an overbought reading; these require adequate confidence. A stricter optional filter requires directional agreement across multiple timeframes, strong trend strength, trailing-level confirmation, and an eligible London trading session. Open or replace the tracked position on any enabled signal. Set a volatility-scaled stop and profit targets at entry; target hits are reported but do not close the position. Exit and clear the tracked position when its stop is reached.
