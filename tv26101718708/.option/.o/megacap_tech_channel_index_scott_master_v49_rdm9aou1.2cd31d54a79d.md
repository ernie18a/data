Open a long position only when the prior daily close of the relevant technology-sector index is above its short-term daily moving average, the traded instrument reaches its lower volatility channel and closes bullish, and its smoothed momentum reading is oversold under a threshold that adapts to recent history or uses a fallback. Require the setup to have reset after price returns near its moving average, volatility index conditions to permit buying, no earnings blackout, and price not to be too extended above its moving average. Exit after a minimum holding period when momentum becomes overbought or price reaches the upper volatility channel. Also exit if price hits the initial risk stop or a trailing stop that ratchets upward as price advances; tighten protection after a meaningful gain. The strategy takes no short positions.
