For each of the 38 tracked cryptocurrencies, derive a point-and-figure trend from its price history and mark it bullish after an upward breakout above a prior column high, or bearish after a downward breakout below a prior column low. Calculate the breadth index as the percentage of tracked assets marked bullish, then derive its own point-and-figure columns from that percentage. Emit a bullish alert when a new rising column begins after the index was at or below its oversold threshold; emit a bearish alert when a new falling column begins after it was at or above its overbought threshold. Emit bullish confirmation on an upward breakout above a prior index column high when the prior index was not oversold; emit bearish confirmation on a downward breakout below a prior column low when the prior index was not overbought. While confirmed bullish, a switch to a falling column marks an uptrend correction; while confirmed bearish, a switch to a rising column marks a downtrend correction. These are directional signals for the traded instrument; the indicator defines no exit rule.
