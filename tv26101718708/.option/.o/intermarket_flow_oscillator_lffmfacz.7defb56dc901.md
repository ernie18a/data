Use the relative price of a risk asset versus a safe-haven asset as the external signal. Normalize and smooth their log-price ratio. When it crosses above its rolling mean, favor long positions in the traded instrument; when it crosses below, favor short positions or exit longs. At a statistically extreme negative reading, a turn upward signals a bullish reversal and a potential long entry. At an extreme positive reading, a turn downward signals a bearish reversal and a potential short entry or exit from longs.
