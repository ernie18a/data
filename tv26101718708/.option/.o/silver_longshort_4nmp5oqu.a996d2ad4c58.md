Within the configured trading window, use daily data for the traded silver instrument and the external gold-to-silver price ratio. Go long when silver’s short-term moving average crosses above its long-term moving average, the ratio is above its upper threshold, and the ratio’s short-term moving average is below its long-term moving average. Otherwise, go short when the ratio is below its lower threshold and its short-term moving average crosses above its long-term moving average. On either signal, close any existing position before entering the indicated direction; no separate exit signal is defined.
