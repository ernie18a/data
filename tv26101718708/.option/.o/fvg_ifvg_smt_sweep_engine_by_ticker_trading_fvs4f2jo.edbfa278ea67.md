For each enabled reference level—previous bar, recent closed-bar extreme, confirmed swing pivot, or session extreme—compare the traded instrument with a correlated partner on matching, valid bars. A bullish signal (long) occurs when the traded instrument sweeps below its own low reference by the required margin while the partner stays sufficiently above its low reference; a bearish signal (short) occurs when it sweeps above its high reference while the partner stays sufficiently below its high reference. By default, only signals where the traded instrument is the sweeper count; the mirror case may be enabled. For a sweep one bar after its reference was set, keep the signal pending until a later bar reaches the sweep bar’s extreme, otherwise discard it after the confirmation window. Discard a signal if the partner subsequently sweeps its matching reference within the neutralization window. No exit logic is defined.
