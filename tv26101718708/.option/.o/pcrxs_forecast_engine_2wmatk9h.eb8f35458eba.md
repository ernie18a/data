For the traded instrument, enter long when its volatility-normalized fast-minus-slow weighted-average oscillator crosses upward through its one-standard-deviation lower band, and all these gates pass: higher-timeframe close and shorter EMA are above the longer EMA; RSI is below overbought; MACD histogram is not falling; money flow is not meaningfully negative; volume is sufficient; the four-year Bitcoin cycle is not strongly bearish; stablecoin dominance is falling or not rising; Bitcoin dominance favors the instrument (rising for Bitcoin, falling for other crypto); and CME futures basis does not indicate crowded longs. Enter short on the oscillator crossing downward through its upper one-standard-deviation band, with inverse higher-timeframe structure, non-oversold RSI, non-rising MACD histogram, non-positive money flow, sufficient volume, a cycle not strongly bullish, stablecoin dominance rising or not falling, Bitcoin dominance unfavorable to the instrument, and no falling-high stablecoin-dominance dry-powder condition. Exit a long when the oscillator crosses below zero, when RSI crosses down from overbought while higher-timeframe structure is bearish, or when a short entry occurs. Exit a short on the inverse conditions. If entry and exit signals conflict, the displayed recommendation prioritizes entries over exits.
