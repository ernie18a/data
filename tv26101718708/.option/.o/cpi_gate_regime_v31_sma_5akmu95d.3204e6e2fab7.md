On the traded instrument, target long when its daily close is above its long-term moving average, or when it is below that average but above its short-term moving average; otherwise target flat. Track the underlying index’s drawdown from its trailing multi-year high. When the drawdown reaches the bear trigger, latch a bear regime and classify it using current year-over-year inflation: high inflation sets a restrictive regime, otherwise a fast-recovery regime. Keep the regime latched until the index recovers near its prior high; while latched, the fast-recovery regime may upgrade to restrictive if inflation rises, but never downgrade. In the restrictive regime, disable the short-term-average re-entry and target long only above the long-term average; otherwise use the normal two-average rule. Generate a buy or exit only when this target changes. The system does not take short positions.
