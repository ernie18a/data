Signal long when the instrument closes above its long-term average while its medium-term average is also above that average, and its close is within 5% above the long-term average; additionally, at least four of these five conditions must hold: that trend alignment, above-average trading volume, rising momentum above its midpoint, outperformance of a broad-market benchmark over a recent period, and the benchmark’s medium-term average above its long-term average. A second long signal requires the same four-of-five confirmation plus unusually high volume and a close above the recent highest high. No short or exit conditions are defined.
