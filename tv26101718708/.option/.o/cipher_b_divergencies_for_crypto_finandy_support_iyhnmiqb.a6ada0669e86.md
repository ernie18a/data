On a confirmed bar, enter long only when flat, the instrument is above its local trend average, Bitcoin is above its trend average now or on the prior bar, and a bullish signal occurs: an oversold upward WaveTrend crossover, a configured bullish divergence, or the strong oversold bullish reversal. Enter short only when flat, the instrument is below its local trend average, Bitcoin is below its trend average now or on the prior bar, and a bearish signal occurs: an overbought downward WaveTrend crossover or a configured bearish divergence. When divergence-only mode is enabled, require a divergence signal instead of the ordinary crossover signal; the strong oversold bullish reversal remains eligible. Close a position at its percentage-based profit target or stop loss. If the optional trend-cross exit is enabled, also close a long when the instrument falls below its local trend average, or a short when it rises above it.
