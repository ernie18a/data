In pair mode, compare the traded asset’s price with a secondary asset’s price by calculating their ratio, then measure and smooth that ratio’s rolling Z-score. When the Z-score crosses up through the lower extreme and remains above it for the confirmation period, go long the traded asset. When it crosses down through the upper extreme and remains below it for the confirmation period, go short the traded asset. Exit a long when the Z-score returns to or above zero; exit a short when it returns to or below zero. Also exit either position if the deviation lasts longer than its historical average closure time or the position lasts longer than twice that average. The asset-versus-moving-average mode uses only the traded asset’s data, so it does not provide qualifying external-data decisions.
