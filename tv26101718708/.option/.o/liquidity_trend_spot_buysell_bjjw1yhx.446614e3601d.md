Build a USD global money-supply series by converting major economies’ M2 data with their exchange rates and summing it. Smooth the series, calculate its rate of change, and standardize that change against its trailing history. Set the liquidity regime bullish when the score crosses above an upper threshold and bearish when it crosses below a lower threshold; retain the prior regime between thresholds and require the new regime to persist for a confirmation period before switching. Optionally calculate a price trend from a market-leader asset using fast and slow smoothed averages; trend is bullish when the fast average is above the slow average. In Liquidity mode, hold the traded asset when liquidity is bullish. In Trend mode, hold when the selected price trend is bullish. In Combined mode, hold when either is bullish and move to cash only when both are bearish. Enforce a minimum holding period before allowing the opposite position change. Never short.
