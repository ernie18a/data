For the traded palm oil futures, compare the external USD/MYR rate with its 30-day average and calculate its 30-day correlation with the futures price. When the absolute correlation is at least 0.5, treat USD/MYR more than 0.5% above its average as a bullish bias and more than 0.5% below as a bearish bias; otherwise use a neutral bias. When absolute correlation is below 0.5, ignore the currency signal. This changes conviction or position sizing only; it does not define long, short, or exit entries.
