Trade long when the instrument closes above the high of its recent range, provided the range is within an acceptable volatility-scaled size, the instrument is above its trend average, the market benchmark is above its trend average, volatility is expanding, and volume is elevated versus its average. If short trading is enabled, apply the inverse conditions and enter on a close below the recent range low. Set the initial stop at the opposite side of the breakout range. Take partial profits at a risk-based target and trail the remainder; alternatively, configurable modes exit fully at a risk-based target or use only the trailing stop. The trailing stop follows a selected moving average or volatility-based distance, tightens in the trade’s favor, and cannot move past the initial range stop. Optionally exit on a close through the instrument’s trend average or on an opposite breakout signal.
