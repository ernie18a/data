On the traded instrument, use correlated instruments to detect divergence: their cycle highs or lows fail to confirm the traded instrument’s corresponding extreme, accounting for inverse correlations. Consider a short when a cycle high is swept and bearish divergence or a bearish fair-value-gap retest is present, or when bearish liquidity divergence is present; a correlated instrument’s sweep paired with divergence at the traded instrument’s high can also qualify. Confirm only after price closes below the relevant bullish-origin level (a bearish change in delivery). Consider a long symmetrically: after a cycle low sweep with bullish divergence or bullish fair-value-gap retest, or bullish liquidity divergence; a correlated sweep paired with divergence at the traded instrument’s low can also qualify. Confirm only after price closes above the relevant bearish-origin level. When directional bias filtering is enabled, require bearish bias for shorts and bullish bias for longs. The indicator defines no exit rule.
