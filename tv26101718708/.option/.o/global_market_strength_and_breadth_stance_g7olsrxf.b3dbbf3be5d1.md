Evaluate a selected market’s primary benchmark and breadth across its benchmark and sector universe using timeframe-appropriate moving-average structure and trend quality. Classify each series as constructive (bullish, very bullish, or pullback), bearish, or other. If the primary benchmark is bullish or very bullish, at least 70% are constructive, and at most 10% are bearish, apply a broad long bias to the traded instrument. Otherwise, if the primary is bullish, very bullish, or in pullback, at least half are constructive, and fewer than 30% are bearish, apply a selective long bias. If the primary is bearish or very bearish and at least 75% are bearish, stand aside; otherwise, if the primary is bearish or very bearish or at least half are bearish, apply a short bias. In all other conditions, remain neutral and limit exposure. This is a market regime filter, not an instrument-specific entry or exit trigger.
