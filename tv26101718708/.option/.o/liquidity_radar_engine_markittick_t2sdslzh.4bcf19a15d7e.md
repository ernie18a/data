When the cross-market filter is enabled, compare a reference market’s latest completed close with its long-term average. Allow a long setup only when the reference trend aligns with the traded instrument’s direction, accounting for whether the two markets are configured to move together or oppositely; apply the inverse alignment rule to shorts. The traded instrument generates a long setup after price sweeps below the prior day’s low, closes back above it, then crosses above its latest confirmed swing high within a limited window, with required displacement and enabled signal filters satisfied. Generate a short setup symmetrically after a sweep above the prior day’s high, a close back below it, and a cross below the latest confirmed swing low. Close an open position when price reaches its stop or final target; these exit conditions use the traded instrument.
