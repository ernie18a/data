Enter long when the traded instrument’s daily RSI crosses above its entry threshold while weekly RSI is above that threshold, or weekly RSI crosses above it while daily RSI is already above it. Require monthly RSI above the threshold when available, the instrument’s relative performance versus its benchmark to be positive, and the benchmark above its long-term moving average when that market filter is enabled. Treat a qualifying bar as a setup: enter if price breaks its high within the confirmation window and the risk from that breakout price to the setup bar’s low is acceptable. Set the initial stop at the setup bar’s low.

While long, raise the stop to a new candle low when daily RSI shows weakness: either RSI falls below its hard-breakdown level, or RSI falls below its trend threshold while relative performance is negative. Ignore the relative-performance weakness exit while RSI is in the support zone above the hard-breakdown level. Exit immediately if price reaches the configured emergency buffer below the stop. For a normal stop exit, a close below the stop arms an exit at that candle’s low; cancel it if price closes back above the stop, otherwise exit if price subsequently breaks the armed low. If weekly RSI reaches its overbought level and the close falls below the prior lookback-period low, take a partial profit. After that partial exit, allow one additional long entry using the same setup and confirmation rules.
