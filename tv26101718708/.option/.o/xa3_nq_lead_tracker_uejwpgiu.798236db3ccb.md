On the traded instrument, compare its high and low with their respective prior range extremes. Count enabled correlated markets that break above their own prior range highs for an upward signal, or below their prior range lows for a downward signal; for an inverse volatility market, reverse these directions. Signal long when the upward count meets its required threshold and the traded instrument has not also broken above its range high. Signal short when the downward count meets its required threshold and the traded instrument has not also broken below its range low. There is no exit rule.
