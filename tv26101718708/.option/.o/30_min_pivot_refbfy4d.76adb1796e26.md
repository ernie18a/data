On a 30-minute chart during the regular session, consider a long setup when the daily trend is constructive (daily 8-period EMA is at least approximately aligned with the daily 21-period EMA) and price tests or reclaims support near either daily EMA, trades within their band, or reclaims the prior day’s low after undercutting it. Require a green trigger candle; on the opening candle, also require a support bounce or a green candle following a red candle. Reject setups when price is trapped below the enabled moving-average resistance levels or overhead resistance leaves insufficient room for the minimum reward-to-risk requirement. Place a buy stop at the trigger candle’s high and an initial stop at its low; cancel the pending order if price invalidates the stop before entry, gaps too far above entry, or the order expires. There are no short signals. After entry, take the first target at two times the initial risk; then move the stop to breakeven and trail it upward with the selected short-term EMA, never below breakeven. Exit the remaining position if the stop is breached or the second target is reached, with the second target based on nearby daily swing or 52-week highs and a minimum reward distance. If enabled, close an open position at session end.
