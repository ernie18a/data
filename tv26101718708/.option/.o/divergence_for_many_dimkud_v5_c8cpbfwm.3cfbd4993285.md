On the traded instrument, compare its confirmed price pivots with each enabled indicator’s pivots. A bullish regular divergence occurs when price makes a lower low while an indicator makes a higher low; a bearish regular divergence occurs when price makes a higher high while an indicator makes a lower high. Enter long on a bullish divergence or short on a bearish divergence when that direction is enabled, the date window is open, no order has already been placed on the bar, and every enabled directional filter passes. Filters can include channel-band conditions, higher-timeframe RSI, money-flow, or momentum ranges and direction, volatility and volume comparisons, and trend direction. Optional opposite-divergence signals close an open position when their directional filters pass. Close positions at configured profit or stop levels; optionally open the opposite position after a stop-loss.
