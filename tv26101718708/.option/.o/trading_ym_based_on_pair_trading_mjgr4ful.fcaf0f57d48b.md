Use the traded instrument and an external comparison instrument. Compute their close-price ratio, smooth that ratio with a long-term moving average, then subtract the comparison instrument’s close multiplied by the smoothed ratio from the traded instrument’s close. Calculate Bollinger Bands around this spread. Enter long when the spread crosses below the lower band, or short when it crosses above the upper band, only while flat. Exit a long or short when the spread crosses the middle band in the corresponding direction.
