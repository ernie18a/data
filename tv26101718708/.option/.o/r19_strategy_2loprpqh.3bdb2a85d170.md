When flat, use the selected reference instrument on the strategy decision timeframe. Go long when its MACD spread crosses above its signal while below its scaled lookback low, ADX is above its threshold, and its close is below both its moving average and the midpoint of its recent high-low range, with RSI below its own moving average. Go short on the mirrored conditions: MACD spread crosses below its signal while above its scaled lookback high, ADX clears the threshold, and close is above its moving average and range midpoint, with RSI above its own moving average. Manage open positions using the traded instrument’s price: apply an initial maximum-loss stop, begin trailing after a configured favorable move and ratchet the stop in the profitable direction, and exit at the configured take-profit level.
