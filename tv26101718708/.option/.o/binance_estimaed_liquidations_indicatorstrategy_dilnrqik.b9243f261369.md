Estimate liquidation pressure as futures volume minus spot volume, setting it to zero on candles where the traded instrument closes above its open. Select one signal rule: upward or downward crossing of a configured threshold by the raw pressure, its fast weighted average, or a smoothed fast average; alternatively, use upward or downward crossing of the fast weighted average over the slow weighted average. If multiple rules are enabled, the moving-average crossover takes precedence over the raw-pressure rule, which takes precedence over the smoothed rule. Enter long on an upward crossing only when the traded instrument is above its long-term weighted average. Enter short on a downward crossing only when shorting is enabled. Apply any configured date restriction to entries. Exit an open position at its configured take-profit or stop-loss level relative to the average entry price.
