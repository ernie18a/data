Use the correlated reference instrument’s candles and daily moving average. Enter long when its current candle gaps upward from the prior candle’s range by at least a minimum gap size and its close is above the daily moving average. Enter short when it gaps downward by at least that size and closes below the moving average. While long, exit on a short-entry condition or when price reaches a target projected above the entry close by the entry close’s distance from the recent rolling low. While short, exit on a long-entry condition or when price reaches a target projected below the entry close by the recent rolling high’s distance from the entry close. Optionally reverse the resulting long and short directions on the traded instrument.
