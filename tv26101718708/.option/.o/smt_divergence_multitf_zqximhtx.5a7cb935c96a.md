On each enabled timeframe, compare confirmed swing pivots in the traded instrument with pivots in each enabled correlated instrument, matching each pair of pivots when their occurrence times are within a small tolerance. Signal long on the traded instrument when its latest swing low is lower than its previous swing low while the correlated instrument’s matched latest swing low is higher than its previous one. Signal short when the traded instrument’s latest swing high is higher than its previous swing high while the correlated instrument’s matched latest swing high is lower than its previous one. No exit rule is defined.
