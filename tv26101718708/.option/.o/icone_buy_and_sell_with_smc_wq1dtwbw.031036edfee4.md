For a gold trade, first require a valid technical entry setup on the traded chart: a scored compression breakout or retest, a trend pullback continuation, or a confirmed false breakout traded in reverse. Then use external market data to form a directional macro score: falling real yields and dollar, rising central-bank liquidity and gold ETF flows, and supportive gold-relative market trends favor longs; opposite conditions favor shorts. Block a long when the score is strongly bearish and block a short when it is strongly bullish; a neutral score allows either direction. The macro gate is disabled for the index profile. While a position is open, a strongly adverse macro shift escalates the exit advice; actual strategy exits also use the traded instrument’s stop and profit targets.
