For each horizon, combine directional composites from the instrument’s relevant timeframes with a directional context score drawn from external value/auction data, options or positioning data, a correlated market leader, and the implied-versus-realized volatility premium. The composites blend trend, trend quality, momentum, structure, volume flow, and mean reversion, with factor weights adapted to the market regime and adjusted for each factor’s historical follow-through on the instrument. Go long when the resulting horizon composite is above a neutral band; go short when it is below the negative band; otherwise take no action. Contextual stress reduces the confidence-weighted score but does not change the directional verdict. No exit rule is defined.
