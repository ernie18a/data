Build the instrument’s smoothed Heikin-Ashi SuperTrend direction. In the standard modes, trigger long or short when its direction flips; in the breakout mode, trigger in the direction of the trend only when price breaks the corresponding prior range, alternating signal direction. Optional continuation signals enter with the trend when a strong Heikin-Ashi candle appears early in a same-color run, has a short opposing wick, and is sufficiently separated from the previous confirmation. When enabled, suppress either signal around qualifying economic news. When the macro filter is enabled, block longs when the selected dollar-index and/or yield data are above their own moving averages, and block shorts when the selected data are below their averages. The script defines no explicit exit decision.
