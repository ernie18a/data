During the regular morning session after the opening range, signal long when either: (1) price crosses above a volatility-adaptive range filter, the traded market is bullish on the 15-minute timeframe and on at least one of the 1-hour or 4-hour timeframes, the volatility index is bearish on 15 minutes, and price is not overextended upward; or (2) a bullish outside-down-up, outside-inside-up, or down-up reversal pattern forms at a session VWAP downside stretch, with the two-bar reversal requiring a more extreme stretch and downside overextension, while the volatility index is bearish on 15 minutes. Signal short when either: (1) price crosses below the range filter, the market is bearish on 15 minutes, and the volatility index is bullish on 15 minutes; or (2) a bearish outside-up-down, outside-inside-down, or up-down reversal forms at a session VWAP upside stretch, with the two-bar reversal requiring a more extreme stretch and upside overextension, and the volatility index is bullish or price is overextended upward. Use a protective volatility-based stop beyond the signal bar's low for longs or high for shorts. No other exit rule is specified.
