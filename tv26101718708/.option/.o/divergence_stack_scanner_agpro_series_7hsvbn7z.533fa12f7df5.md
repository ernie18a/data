On each confirmed swing high or low, compare the traded instrument and an optional correlated instrument with their respective previous same-direction pivots. For normally positively correlated instruments, signal short at a swing high or long at a swing low when only one instrument makes a new high or low. For normally inversely correlated instruments, signal short or long when both instruments either make a new high or low, or neither does. These correlated-instrument signals are separate from the indicator’s oscillator-divergence stack.
