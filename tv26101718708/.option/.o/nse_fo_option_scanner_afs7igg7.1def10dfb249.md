On the traded instrument, score bullish and bearish conditions separately, one point each: bullish when its fast EMA is above its slow EMA (bearish when below), volume-and-price interpretation indicates bullish buildup or covering (bearish buildup or unwinding), the related sector index is above its 20-period EMA (bearish when below), RSI is below 65 for bullish or above 35 for bearish, and volume exceeds 1.5 times its 20-bar average. Signal long only when the bullish score is at least 4 and the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA; signal short only when the bearish score is at least 4 and it crosses below. No exit condition is defined.
