Estimate bullish or bearish conviction for the traded instrument by combining its order-flow evidence with a correlated paired instrument’s normalized short-term price momentum and volume-weighted candle direction. The paired instrument’s bullish or bearish reading shifts conviction in that direction; a neutral reading adds no directional confirmation. Apply higher-timeframe direction checks and adjust conviction for conflicting evidence, elevated flow risk, regime instability, and agreement across horizons and microstructure signals. Go long when the resulting direction is bullish and confidence clears the action threshold; go short when bearish and it clears the threshold; otherwise wait. The paired instrument provides confirmation, not an independent exit trigger.
