Build a smoothed composite from standardized external positioning data: implied-volatility level and stress contribute bearish weight when elevated, futures premium contributes bullish weight, and optional put-call ratio and option-skew data contribute according to their configured direction. When the composite crosses upward out of its bearish-lean zone, go long; when it crosses downward out of its bullish-lean zone, go short. An opposite turn exits the current position and signals the reverse direction. The indicator defines no separate exit condition.
