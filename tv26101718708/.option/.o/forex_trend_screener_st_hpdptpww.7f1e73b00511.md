For each non-DXY instrument, calculate a momentum oscillator on each configured timeframe by normalizing the instrument’s price deviation from an exponential average by its exponentially averaged absolute deviation, then smoothing the result. Use the configured live or last-closed reading. Set direction long when every enabled trend timeframe is positive, short when every one is negative, and neutral otherwise. A directional candidate qualifies only if every enabled checklist condition passes: the Dollar Index’s closed higher-timeframe readings agree in sign; its enabled higher-timeframe live readings do not conflict in sign with their last-closed readings; its fastest reading is not strongly opposed to the candidate direction while still building; the instrument’s trend and entry scores exceed their configured minimums; none of its trend timeframes has a live-versus-closed sign conflict; its correlation-adjusted volatility relative to the Dollar Index is below the configured maximum; and every trend-timeframe oscillator reading exceeds the configured minimum magnitude. The higher-timeframe synchronization checks for the Dollar Index and, optionally, EUR/USD are shared filters. Trend score combines slower-timeframe oscillator strength with absolute correlation to the Dollar Index; entry score combines fastest-timeframe oscillator strength with momentum building in the candidate direction, including any configured pause-and-recovery bonus. Rank fully qualified candidates by the geometric mean of those scores; the top three are highlighted. This is a screening verdict, not an order or position-management system: no explicit entry execution or exit rule is defined, and a candidate becomes neutral when timeframe direction consensus fails.
