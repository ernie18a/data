Track the trend of the traded instrument using three conditions: whether its EMA is rising or falling, whether price has closed beyond the latest confirmed swing high or low (upward or downward trend, respectively, retained until the opposite swing level is broken), and whether ADX is above its threshold. Enter long when all three indicate an uptrend and the external BTC or ETH footprint buy-minus-sell volume delta, smoothed over time, is positive; enter short when all three indicate a downtrend and that delta is negative. Missing external delta data does not block entry. Close an opposing position when the trend flips, then enter in the new direction when flat and its entry conditions still hold. Exit the full position when loss exceeds its percentage stop, or after profit reaches its break-even trigger if price then retraces to the entry price with a small offset in the trade's favor. Take partial profits at three successive targets spaced by one, two, and three times the entry-time ATR-based distance, closing 30% of the position at each target.
