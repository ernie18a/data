On a confirmed bar, compare the traded instrument with a correlated instrument. Go long when the traded instrument makes a lower low than its latest confirmed swing low while the correlated instrument holds above its previous confirmed swing low, provided the latest swing lows are sufficiently close in time. Go short when the traded instrument makes a higher high than its latest confirmed swing high while the correlated instrument remains below its previous confirmed swing high, with the swing highs sufficiently close in time. Signal each direction at most once per corresponding traded-instrument swing. No exit rule is defined.
