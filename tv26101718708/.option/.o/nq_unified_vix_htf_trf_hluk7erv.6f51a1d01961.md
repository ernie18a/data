During the morning cash session, enter long when the traded instrument crosses above its range-filter trend line, its 15-minute trend is bullish, and the 15-minute VIX is below its own trend filter. Enter short when the instrument crosses below its trend line and the 15-minute VIX is above its filter. Only enter while flat. Manage either position with an volatility-scaled stop and a fixed reward-to-risk target; move the stop to entry after price reaches the relevant extension level. Exit a short as well if the VIX falls back below its filter. Close positions at the session cutoff.
