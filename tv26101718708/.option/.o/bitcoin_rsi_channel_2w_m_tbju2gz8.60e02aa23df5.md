On the 2-week or monthly timeframe, calculate Bitcoin’s 14-period RSI and normalize it within a declining channel whose upper and lower boundaries are fitted separately for each timeframe. At a confirmed candle close, mark the traded instrument as a buy/long when normalized RSI is at or below the lower user-defined cutoff, and as a sell/short when it is at or above the upper cutoff; otherwise make no directional decision. The indicator defines no separate exit rule.
