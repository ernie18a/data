Choose either unfiltered or stochastic-filtered entry mode. Build a Renko series for the traded instrument. In unfiltered mode, go long when Renko close crosses above Renko open and go short when it crosses below; an opposite entry reverses the position. In filtered mode, go long only while Renko is bullish when the stochastic %K crosses above %D after %K was below the oversold threshold; go short only while Renko is bearish when %K crosses below %D after it was above the overbought threshold. There is no separate strategy exit rule.
