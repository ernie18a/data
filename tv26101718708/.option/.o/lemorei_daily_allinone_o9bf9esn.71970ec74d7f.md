Use the traded instrument’s return relative to a broad-market benchmark over a recent lookback as part of its daily long-context assessment. Favor long consideration only when relative performance is nonnegative and price is above its long-term trend average, with no breakdown and otherwise constructive daily price action; weak relative performance combined with price below that average is a hard veto. Relative strength also raises or lowers overnight and holding-context scores. The indicator provides long-context permission or rejection, not direct entries, shorts, or exits.
