For the Pakistan equity instrument, calculate a macro score from external policy-rate direction and its position versus its historical mean, inflation direction and level, and the real rate’s distance from a neutral level; smooth the score. A cross above the upper risk-on boundary signals a favorable equity bias, and a cross below the lower risk-off boundary signals a defensive bias. A policy rate above its historical mean and rising adds bearish pressure; above the mean and falling indicates easing relief. The indicator provides macro bias and alerts, but does not define explicit long, short, or exit orders.
