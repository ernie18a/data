Enter long after a valid volatility squeeze releases, if price closes decisively above the upper volatility band, the candle has a substantial body, momentum is positive and strengthening, and volume is elevated. Require the higher-timeframe trend to be rising with price above its buffered average, daily volatility and distance from its long-term average to be within acceptable ranges, volatility to exceed a minimum, and momentum not to be extremely overbought. Enter short on the inverse conditions, with momentum not extremely oversold. Allow entries only when flat and after a cooldown; optional fundamental and session filters can further restrict entries. On entry, set an initial volatility-based stop and profit target, scaled according to whether the market is trending strongly, ranging, or neither. While in a position, ratchet a volatility-based trailing stop in the favorable direction and move the stop beyond entry after sufficient favorable movement. Exit at the stop or target, or close a trade that fails to make sufficient progress within a time window or exceeds its maximum duration. Optional partial profit-taking can reduce the position at an earlier target; in runner mode, trail the remainder without a final target.
