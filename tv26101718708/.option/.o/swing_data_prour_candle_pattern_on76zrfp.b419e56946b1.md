On the traded instrument, signal a long-ready setup only when every enabled trend filter passes (price above its intermediate trend average, faster average above that average, and the intermediate average rising; an optional long-term average filter may also apply), every enabled quality filter passes (relative strength versus the broad market and relative volume meet their required floors, optional liquidity floors are met, and the instrument’s mapped sector ETF is up on the day), and an entry trigger occurs. A trigger is either a move below or near a trend average followed by a close back above it, or a pullback near an average that closes above it with the required candle strength. If trend and quality pass but no trigger occurs, mark it as watch; if either fails, mark it not ready. A confirmed bullish candle pattern can upgrade a ready setup, but cannot create one by itself. The logic provides no short or exit signal.
