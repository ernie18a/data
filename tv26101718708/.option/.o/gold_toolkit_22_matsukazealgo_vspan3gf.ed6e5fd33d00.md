Generate signals only for enabled modules. On intraday charts, apply the configured session filter to modules that use it; when enabled, signals are allowed only in selected sessions. Read the Dollar Index’s current daily close relative to its daily open: a rising Dollar is strong, a falling or unchanged Dollar is weak. For the trend-flip, stop-run reversal, coral-trend breakout, arc-momentum flip, and asymmetric-trend modules, allow longs only with a weak Dollar and shorts only with a strong Dollar; if the Dollar feed is disabled or unavailable, do not block these signals. The stop-run module also requires a range-boundary sweep, reversal confirmation, and a qualifying combined setup score. The other hard-filtered modules require, respectively: a Supertrend direction flip with recent oversold/overbought confirmation; a close crossing the trend line and breaking its risk band; an oscillator arc changing direction; or an asymmetric trend changing direction. Dollar alignment is a scoring bonus, not a hard gate, for harmonic-pattern and order-block signals. Those signals, and signals from the remaining modules, follow their own price-structure conditions without a Dollar-direction requirement: inverse-gap flips, Parabolic SAR flips, swing-structure changes, channel breakouts/pullbacks/continuations, support bounces or resistance rejections, completed harmonic patterns, fair-value-gap formation, institutional accumulation/distribution, order-block formation, liquidity sweeps, and price crossing session-anchored VWAP. The indicator has no separate exit signal driven by outside-instrument data.
