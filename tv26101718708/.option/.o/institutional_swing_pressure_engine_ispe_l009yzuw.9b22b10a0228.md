For the traded instrument, combine volatility compression, directional price-volume flow, market-relative strength, price structure, and confirmed higher-timeframe trend into bullish and bearish pressure assessments. Compare relative performance with a broad-market benchmark and, when available, a sector benchmark; stronger relative performance supports bullish pressure and weaker performance supports bearish pressure. Favor a long when bullish pressure exceeds bearish pressure and the factors align; favor a short when bearish pressure exceeds bullish pressure and the inverse factors align. A close beyond resistance or support in the matching pressure direction confirms a breakout. Rising directional pressure while price stays range-bound can signal a developing setup. Treat a failed breakout or weakening pressure after an extended move as an exit warning; no standalone exit rule is defined.
