Use the daily trend of a broad market benchmark as a regime filter. When the benchmark is above its long-term daily average, signal a long call on the traded instrument if its price is unusually far below its recent average, its momentum is oversold, and its price remains above its short-term trend baseline. When the benchmark is below its long-term daily average, signal a long put if the instrument is unusually far above its recent average, its momentum is overbought, and its price is below its short-term trend baseline. Suppress repeated signals during a cooldown period. For each signal, set a limit entry beyond the current price; after entry fills, exit at a profit target, a protective stop, or a maximum holding time.
